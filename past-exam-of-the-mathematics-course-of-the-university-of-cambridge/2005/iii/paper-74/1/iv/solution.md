<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

During [radiation domination](../../../../../../radiation-domination.md), [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives $\rho\propto a^{-4}$: dilution contributes $a^{-3}$ and the [cosmological redshift](../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) of each [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) contributes another $a^{-1}$. Thus $\alpha=4>2$ and $a=A\sqrt t$ in the flat radiation-dominated approximation. The [particle horizon](../../../../../../particle-horizon.md) measured as proper radial distance on a constant-time slice is

$$
\boxed{s_{\rm hor}(t)=a(t)c\int_0^t\frac{dt'}{a(t')}=A\sqrt t\frac{2c\sqrt t}{A}=2ct.}
$$

Every local [comoving observer](../../../../../../comoving-observer.md) measures the [speed of light](../../../../../../speed-of-light.md) to be $c$. However, the spatial separations traversed earlier have expanded by the time the final distance is measured; the present separation from the earliest accessible comoving worldline is not the integral of local path lengths measured at their different traversal times. Indeed $\dot s_{\rm hor}=Hs_{\rm hor}+c=2c$ in this era, explicitly separating [cosmic expansion](../../../../../../expansion-of-the-universe.md) from local propagation. Curvature or a later transition to [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md) changes the exact formula; $2ct$ is the radiation-dominated result.
