<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Here a smooth family is understood as a [smooth proper family of compact complex manifolds](../../../../../../smooth-proper-family-of-compact-complex-manifolds.md): $f:\mathcal X\to S$ is a proper holomorphic submersion, with compact Kähler fibres. Properness and compactness are essential for the usual statement. Indeed, the projection $(\mathbb C_t\times\mathbb C_z)\setminus\{(0,0)\}\to\mathbb C_t$ is a holomorphic submersion with Kähler fibres, but its fibres are $\mathbb C$ for $t\ne0$ and $\mathbb C^*$ for $t=0$. Their first [Betti numbers](../../../../../../betti-number.md) are zero and one, so their [cohomology](../../../../../../cohomology-split.md) cannot give a fixed-rank variation. This supplies a counterexample if “smooth family” is read without the usual proper compact-fibre hypothesis. The [Ehresmann fibration theorem](../../../../../../ehresmann-fibration-theorem.md) gives local differentiable trivializations, so

$$
\mathbb H_{\mathbb Z}=R^kf_*\mathbb Z/\text{torsion}
$$

is a [local system](../../../../../../local-system.md) with fibre $H^k(X_s,\mathbb Z)/\text{torsion}$. Its associated flat bundle has the [Gauss–Manin connection](../../../../../../gauss-manin-connection.md), given by differentiating [cohomology](../../../../../../cohomology-split.md) classes in topologically constant frames.

The [Kähler Hodge decomposition](../../../../../../hodge-decomposition-theorem-for-compact-kahler-manifolds.md) gives

$$
H^k(X_s,\mathbb C)=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}H^{p,q}(X_s).
$$

The [Betti numbers](../../../../../../betti-number.md) are locally constant by differentiable triviality. The dimensions of $H^q(X_s,\Omega^p_{X_s})$ are upper semicontinuous, and their sums for fixed $p+q=k$ equal the fixed [Betti number](../../../../../../betti-number.md). Near any fixed fibre, every summand can only decrease, while their sum cannot change; hence none decreases. Thus the [Hodge numbers](../../../../../../hodge-number.md) are locally constant. The [relative de Rham complex](../../../../../../relative-de-rham-complex.md) and the [Grauert base change theorem](../../../../../../grauert-base-change-theorem.md) then make

$$
F^p_s=\bigoplus_{r\ge p}H^{r,k-r}(X_s)
$$

the fibres of holomorphic subbundles $F^p\mathcal H$. More intrinsically these are the images of the [hypercohomology](../../../../../../hypercohomology.md) of the truncated [relative de Rham complex](../../../../../../relative-de-rham-complex.md) $\Omega_{\mathcal X/S}^{\ge p}$ in the full relative de Rham [cohomology](../../../../../../cohomology-split.md). Kähler degeneration of the spectral sequence ensures that these images have the required ranks.

For the transversality proof, let $A^2$ denote the ideal in the absolute holomorphic de Rham complex generated by two pulled-back base one-forms. Keeping zero or one base differential gives a [short exact sequence of cochain complexes](../../../../../../short-exact-sequence-of-cochain-complexes.md), with the usual shifted differential in the left-hand complex

$$
0\longrightarrow f^*\Omega_S^1\otimes
\Omega_{\mathcal X/S}^{\bullet-1}
\longrightarrow
\Omega_{\mathcal X}^{\bullet}/A^2
\longrightarrow
\Omega_{\mathcal X/S}^{\bullet}
\longrightarrow0.
$$

The connecting morphism in degree $k$ is the [Gauss–Manin connection](../../../../../../gauss-manin-connection.md)

$$
\nabla:\mathcal H\longrightarrow\mathcal H\otimes\Omega_S^1.
$$

To compute it, lift a relative de Rham cocycle to the middle complex, apply the absolute differential, and retain the terms with one base differential. A cocycle in filtration degree $p$ can be represented using absolute forms of degree at least $p$. In a term containing one base differential, removing that factor leaves a relative form of degree at least $p-1$. The connecting construction therefore lands in $F^{p-1}$:

$$
\boxed{\nabla F^p\subseteq F^{p-1}\otimes\Omega_S^1.}
$$

This is the filtration shift behind [Griffiths transversality](../../../../../../griffiths-transversality.md). It explains the inequality rather than merely naming a theorem. The unfiltered construction agrees with topological parallel transport by the de Rham comparison, and its square is zero, as required for the flat connection.

Consequently the fibre [cohomology](../../../../../../cohomology-split.md) gives a pure [variation of Hodge structure](../../../../../../variation-of-hodge-structure.md). To claim a polarized integral variation one needs the additional compatible flat polarization. In a smooth projective family, a relative ample [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) supplies a flat integral [Kähler class](../../../../../../kahler-class.md); its primitive pieces are polarized by the [Hodge–Riemann bilinear relations](../../../../../../hodge-riemann-bilinear-relations.md), and the full [cohomology](../../../../../../cohomology-split.md) is polarized using the appropriate primitive-piece signs. For a general compact Kähler family, the unpolarized variation above is automatic, but a flat integral polarization is not supplied merely by knowing that each fibre is Kähler. This distinction is particularly relevant to unrestricted K3 families.
