<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A pure integral [variation of Hodge structure](../../../../../../variation-of-hodge-structure.md) of weight $w$ on a complex manifold $S$ consists of a [local system](../../../../../../local-system.md) $\mathbb H_{\mathbb Z}$ of finite-rank free abelian groups and a decreasing filtration by holomorphic subbundles

$$
F^p\mathcal H\subseteq\mathcal H
=\mathbb H_{\mathbb Z}\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathcal O_S.
$$

Each fibre, with this filtration and the conjugation from its integral lattice, must be a [Hodge structure](../../../../../../hodge-structure.md) of weight $w$ with locally constant [Hodge numbers](../../../../../../hodge-number.md). The [local system](../../../../../../local-system.md) gives a flat connection $\nabla$: in a locally constant frame it differentiates the coefficient functions. The filtration must satisfy [Griffiths transversality](../../../../../../griffiths-transversality.md),

$$
\boxed{\nabla(F^p\mathcal H)\subseteq
F^{p-1}\mathcal H\otimes\Omega_S^1.}
$$

A polarized variation additionally has a locally constant integral [bilinear form](../../../../../../bilinear-form.md) $Q$ which polarizes every fibre. Flatness means $\nabla Q=0$. A collection of fibrewise Hodge decompositions without a holomorphic filtration or the transversality condition is not yet a [variation of Hodge structure](../../../../../../variation-of-hodge-structure.md).
