<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $f=kS^\alpha$, $Sf'=\alpha f$ and $S^2f''=\alpha(\alpha-1)f$. Thus $\lambda=\lambda_\alpha=\sigma^2\alpha(\alpha-1)/2+(r-q)\alpha$, and all values with this fixed spatial factor are

$$
\boxed{V(S,t)=A S^\alpha e^{(r-\lambda_\alpha)(t-T)}.}
$$

This [Power payoff in the Black-Scholes model](../../../../../../power-payoff-in-the-black-scholes-model.md) calculation remains valid for any real $\alpha$ on $S>0$; the specified nonzero exponent excludes the constant-payoff subpart. It also supplies the moment term in [power put-call parity](../../../../../../power-put-call-parity.md) above.
