Use the standard global convention that $\mathcal M_c^2$ consists of [L2-bounded continuous martingales](../../../../../../l2-bounded-continuous-martingale.md), here starting at zero. This matters for arbitrary unbounded [stopping times](../../../../../../stopping-time.md) in part (iv); square-integrability at each separate deterministic time alone is weaker. Such [martingales](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) have terminal $L^2$ limits and satisfy the [optional sampling theorem](../../../../../../optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) at arbitrary finite [stopping times](../../../../../../stopping-time.md). Infinite [stopping times](../../../../../../stopping-time.md) can also be included by evaluating at the terminal limit. The [weakly orthogonal continuous martingales](../../../../../../weakly-orthogonal-continuous-martingales.md) condition compares cross-time [expectations](../../../../../../expected-value.md). The following four parts prove its equivalence with the equal-time, bracket-mean and forward-stopping tests.
