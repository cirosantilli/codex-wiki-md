<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $M,N$ are [weakly orthogonal continuous martingales](../../../../../../../weakly-orthogonal-continuous-martingales.md), taking the two times equal gives $\mathbb E[M_sN_s]=0$. Conversely suppose this equal-time condition holds. For $s\le t$, the [martingale](../../../../../../../martingale-split.md) property and [conditional expectation](../../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) give

$$
\mathbb E[M_sN_t]=\mathbb E[M_s\mathbb E(N_t\mid\mathcal F_s)]
=\mathbb E[M_sN_s]=0.
$$

For $t\le s$, condition the other factor at time $t$ instead. Products are integrable by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Thus the equal-time condition implies the full cross-time definition, proving (i) equivalent to (ii).
