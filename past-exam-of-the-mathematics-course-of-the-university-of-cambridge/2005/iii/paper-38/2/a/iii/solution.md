↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [polarization identity](../../../../../../../polarization-identity.md) for [quadratic covariation](../../../../../../../quadratic-covariation.md), together with $\mathbb E[M\pm N]_s=\mathbb E(M_s\pm N_s)^2$, yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[M,N]_s=\mathbb E[M_sN_s]}.
$$

The bracket identity for each zero-starting square-integrable [martingale](../../../../../../../martingale-split.md) is the [Itô isometry](../../../../../../../ito-isometry.md) for integration of the constant integrand against that [martingale](../../../../../../../martingale-split.md). Polarizing both identities gives the displayed [covariance identity for continuous square-integrable martingales](../../../../../../../covariance-identity-for-continuous-square-integrable-martingales.md). The bracket is integrable: its total variation is at most $\sqrt{[M]_s[N]_s}$ by the [Kunita-Watanabe inequality](../../../../../../../kunita-watanabe-inequality.md), whose [expectation](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) is finite by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Therefore condition (iii) is equivalent to (ii).
