<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take $\Omega=[0,1)$ with its [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md) and [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md) of total mass one. Let $\mathcal F_n$ be the finite [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) generated by the half-open [dyadic intervals](../../../../../../dyadic-interval.md) of length $2^{-n}$. Set $X_0=f(1)-f(0)$ if the indexing begins at one. Each $X_n$ is [adapted](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) to this [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md), and if $L$ is a [Lipschitz constant](../../../../../../lipschitz-constant.md) of $f$, then $|X_n|\leq L$, so every coordinate is integrable.

On a parent interval $[u,u+2^{-n})$, [conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) averages the two equally likely child values. Their average is

$$
\frac12\,2^{n+1}\bigl(f(u+2^{-n-1})-f(u)+f(u+2^{-n})-f(u+2^{-n-1})\bigr)
=2^n\bigl(f(u+2^{-n})-f(u)\bigr)=X_n.
$$

Thus **$\mathbb E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=X_n$**, establishing the [dyadic slope martingale](../../../../../../dyadic-slope-martingale.md). One can equivalently include $1$ as a separate null atom without changing any [probability law](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md).
