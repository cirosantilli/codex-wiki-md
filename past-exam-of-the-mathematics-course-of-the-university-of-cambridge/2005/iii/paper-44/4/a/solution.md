<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A court-based [randomized controlled trial](../../../../../../randomized-controlled-trial.md) would assign eligible offenders between the two sentencing policies using [randomization](../../../../../../randomization.md), ideally stratified by court and important prespecified risk factors. With [allocation concealment](../../../../../../allocation-concealment.md), the next assignment cannot be selected to suit an offender's prognosis. Baseline characteristics, both recorded and unrecorded, are balanced in distribution rather than confounded with judges' sentencing preferences. This makes a comparison of outcomes interpretable as a [causal effect](../../../../../../causal-effect.md) of assignment, subject to complete and comparable follow-up, whereas separate historical cohorts can differ in severity, social circumstances or calendar period.

The commission should compare both specified outcomes over the same 360-day period from sentencing, using an [intention-to-treat analysis](../../../../../../intention-to-treat-analysis.md). An offender initially assigned community service remains in that group even if the order is later revoked and prison follows. Revocation is part of the policy's consequences; restricting the comparison to completers would select on a post-assignment outcome and lose the protection of [randomization](../../../../../../randomization.md).

Within-court individual [randomization](../../../../../../randomization.md) also provides an objective allocation procedure and valid uncertainty estimates. It does not guarantee identical characteristics in a particular finite sample or eliminate loss to follow-up, unequal outcome ascertainment or limited generalizability. Eligibility, genuine uncertainty about the two policies, and a feasible authorized allocation mechanism would need agreement before the trial. If whole courts rather than individuals were assigned, that would be a [cluster-randomized trial](../../../../../../cluster-randomized-trial.md) and its correlation structure would require a different [sample size](../../../../../../sample-size.md) calculation.
