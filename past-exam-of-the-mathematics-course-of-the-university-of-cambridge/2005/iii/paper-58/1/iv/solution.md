<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Without the upper-arm rotation, the [photon polarization](../../../../../../photon-polarization.md) still labels the path. Before BS2 the [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md) is

$$
\alpha|\ell\rangle|H\rangle+i\beta e^{i\theta}|u\rangle|V\rangle.
$$

After the balanced [beam splitter](../../../../../../beam-splitter.md), the polarization-valued output amplitudes are

$$
|\chi_1\rangle=\frac{i}{\sqrt2}\bigl(\alpha|H\rangle+\beta e^{i\theta}|V\rangle\bigr),\qquad |\chi_0\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2}\bigl(\alpha|H\rangle-\beta e^{i\theta}|V\rangle\bigr).
$$

The detectors do not resolve [photon polarization](../../../../../../photon-polarization.md). Since $\langle H|V\rangle=0$, the cross terms vanish in the [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md):

$$
\boxed{P(D_1)=\langle\chi_1|\chi_1\rangle=\frac{|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2}{2}=\frac12,\qquad P(D_0)=\frac12.}
$$

These [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) are independent of the tunable [quantum phase](../../../../../../quantum-phase.md) and the input [photon polarization](../../../../../../photon-polarization.md), so there is no usable [wave interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) fringe. Equivalently, taking the [partial trace](../../../../../../partial-trace.md) over [photon polarization](../../../../../../photon-polarization.md) removes the [quantum coherence](../../../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) between the two path [quantum states](../../../../../../quantum-state.md). The [which-path information](../../../../../../which-path-information.md) is retained in orthogonal [photon polarizations](../../../../../../photon-polarization.md) even without anybody measuring it. Rotating the two occupied [photon polarization](../../../../../../photon-polarization.md) [quantum states](../../../../../../quantum-state.md) to the same [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md) removes that distinction and allows the cross term in part (ii).
