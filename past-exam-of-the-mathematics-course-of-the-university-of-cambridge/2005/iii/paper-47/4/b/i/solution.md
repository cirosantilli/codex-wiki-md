↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $q^*_{.025}$ and $q^*_{.975}$ be the empirical [quantiles](../../../../../../../quantile-function.md) of the paired-bootstrap values $\widehat\theta^*$. The [percentile bootstrap confidence interval](../../../../../../../percentile-bootstrap-confidence-interval.md) is

$$
\boxed{[q^*_{.025},q^*_{.975}].}
$$

Its endpoints use the [bootstrap](../../../../../../../bootstrapping-statistics.md) distribution of the estimate itself. The regularity assumptions and resampling scheme are those in the parent solution.
