Small random-walk proposal [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) gives many accepted tiny moves and strong [autocorrelation](../../../../../../../autocorrelation.md); excessive [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) proposes mostly low-density points, giving low acceptance and long rejection plateaus. Tune each coordinate or block during a pilot run to balance movement and acceptance, and inspect effective sample size per unit computation. Rescale or transform parameters with very different units, and account for [posterior](../../../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) [correlation](../../../../../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) rather than adjusting only diagonal [variances](../../../../../../../variance-split.md).

There is no universal optimal acceptance percentage for every target or update dimension. Keep the chosen proposal fixed in the production run, or use an adaptive method with a proved diminishing-adaptation and containment justification. The direction of the trace-based changes for this particular run is given in the parent solution.
