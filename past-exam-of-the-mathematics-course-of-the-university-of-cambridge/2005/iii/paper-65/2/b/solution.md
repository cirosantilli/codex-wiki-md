<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of a real $2\times2$ [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is $p(z)=z^2-Tz+D$. Thus $p(1)=1-T+D$ vanishes exactly when $T=D+1$, and $p(-1)=1+T+D$ vanishes exactly when $T+D+1=0$. These are the required [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) conditions.

For $D=1$ and $|T|<2$, the [discriminant](../../../../../../discriminant.md) is negative, and

$$
z_\pm=\frac T2\pm i\sqrt{1-\frac{T^2}{4}}.
$$

Their moduli equal one. Taking $\alpha\in(0,\pi)$ with $\cos\alpha=T/2$ gives **$z_\pm=e^{\pm i\alpha}$**. The strict [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) inequalities exclude $\alpha=0,\pi$ and hence exclude the repeated real multipliers $+1,-1$.
