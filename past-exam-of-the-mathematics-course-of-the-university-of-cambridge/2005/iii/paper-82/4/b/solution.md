<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For downward-phase reversal, the [P-SV displacement potentials](../../../../../../p-sv-displacement-potentials.md) instead produce

$$
\mathbf v=\omega\begin{pmatrix}k&s\\-p&k\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\phi_-\\\psi_-\end{pmatrix},\qquad \mathbf t=\begin{pmatrix}2\mu kp&H\\-H&2\mu ks\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\phi_-\\\psi_-\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Eliminating the potentials as before gives

$$
\boxed{Z_-=\frac1{\omega D}\begin{pmatrix}-\rho\omega^2p&k(2\mu D-\rho\omega^2)\\-k(2\mu D-\rho\omega^2)&-\rho\omega^2s\end{pmatrix}=-Z_+^\dagger.}
$$

The [Hermitian part](../../../../../../hermitian-part-of-a-matrix.md) is negative definite, and consequently

$$
\boxed{\langle S_z\rangle_-=-\frac{\rho\omega}{2D}\left(p|v_x|^2+s|v_z|^2\right)<0}
$$

for a nonzero backward [displacement field](../../../../../../displacement-field-mechanics.md). The P potential must use vertical [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) $k_\alpha$ and the S potential $k_\beta$, as in the original PDF; the TeX incorrectly gives the shear [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) for both.
