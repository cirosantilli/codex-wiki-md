Take a stable isotropic elastic material with [mass density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho>0$ and [Lamé parameters](../../../../../lame-parameter.md) $\lambda,\mu$, where $\mu>0$ and $3\lambda+2\mu>0$. Its [elastic stiffness tensor](../../../../../elastic-stiffness-tensor.md) is

$$
c_{ijpq}=\lambda\delta_{ij}\delta_{pq}+\mu(\delta_{ip}\delta_{jq}+\delta_{iq}\delta_{jp}),\qquad \alpha^2=\frac{\lambda+2\mu}{\rho},\quad\beta^2=\frac\mu\rho.
$$

The [P wave](../../../../../p-wave.md) is longitudinal and the [S wave](../../../../../s-wave.md) transverse. The wavefront-transport alternative and all three steps of the [Love wave](../../../../../love-wave.md) alternative are given below.
