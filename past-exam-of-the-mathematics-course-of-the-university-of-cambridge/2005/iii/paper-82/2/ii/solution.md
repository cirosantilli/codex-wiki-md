<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Refer all layer [wave amplitudes](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) to the lower interface. Let a downward incident SH [displacement field](../../../../../../displacement-field-mechanics.md) have unit [wave amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) there, an upward reflected wave have [wave amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $R_h$, and the decaying half-space wave [wave amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $T_h$. Interface continuity gives

$$
1+R_h=T_h,\qquad i\mu_0q(1-R_h)=-\mu pT_h.
$$

Solving these equations proves the [Love-wave interface reflection phase](../../../../../../love-wave-interface-reflection-phase.md) and transmission [wave amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md):

$$
\boxed{R_h=\frac{\mu_0q-i\mu p}{\mu_0q+i\mu p}=e^{-2i\arctan(\mu p/(\mu_0q))},\quad T_h=\frac{2\mu_0q}{\mu_0q+i\mu p}.}
$$

Thus $|R_h|=1$: the [evanescent](../../../../../../evanescent-wave.md) substrate field has nonzero [wave amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) but no net downward mean flux. At the traction-free top, an upward incident wave and its downward reflection must have canceling [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md), so their [displacement field](../../../../../../displacement-field-mechanics.md) [reflection coefficient](../../../../../../reflection-coefficient.md) is $R_0=+1$.

A layer wave acquires [wave phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) $2qh$ during a complete round trip. [Constructive interference](../../../../../../constructive-interference.md) requires

$$
R_0R_he^{2iqh}=1,\qquad qh-\arctan\frac{\mu p}{\mu_0q}=n\pi.
$$

Taking the tangent gives again **$\tan(qh)=\mu p/(\mu_0q)$**. The reflection phases, rather than only geometric travel distance, are essential to the mode condition.
