<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Factor the coefficients into a [tensor product](../../../../../../../tensor-product.md):

$$
|\Psi\rangle
=\left(\frac{|0\rangle-|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}\right)_A
\otimes\left(\frac{|0\rangle-|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}\right)_B.
$$

This is already a [Schmidt decomposition](../../../../../../../schmidt-decomposition.md) with one term. Consequently

$$
\boxed{r=1,\qquad s_1=1,\qquad\lambda_1=1.}
$$

Each [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) is the rank-one [orthogonal projector](../../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto $(|0\rangle-|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, so its other [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is zero. The joint [pure state](../../../../../../../pure-state.md) is a [product state](../../../../../../../product-state.md), with no [entanglement](../../../../../../../entangled-state.md).
