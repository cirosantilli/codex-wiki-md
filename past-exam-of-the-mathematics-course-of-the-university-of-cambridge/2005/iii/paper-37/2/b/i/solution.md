<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The coefficient [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) in the computational [orthonormal bases](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), with Alice's index labelling rows, is

$$
C=\frac1{\sqrt3}\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Since the entries are real, the [reduced density matrices](../../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) are

$$
\rho_A=CC^\dagger=\frac13\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\rho_B=C^{\mathsf T}\overline C=\frac13\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Both have [trace](../../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) one and [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md) $1/9$. Their characteristic [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is $\lambda^2-\lambda+1/9$, so the nonzero [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are

$$
\lambda_\pm=\frac{3\pm\sqrt5}{6}.
$$

Both are positive. Thus the [Schmidt rank](../../../../../../../schmidt-rank.md), or the [Schmidt number](../../../../../../../schmidt-number.md) of this [pure state](../../../../../../../pure-state.md), is

$$
\boxed{r=2.}
$$

If “Schmidt numbers” denotes the individual positive [Schmidt coefficients](../../../../../../../schmidt-coefficient.md) instead, those are

$$
\boxed{s_+=\frac{\sqrt5+1}{2\sqrt3},\qquad s_-=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2\sqrt3}.}
$$

Their squares are $\lambda_\pm$; giving both the [Schmidt rank](../../../../../../../schmidt-rank.md) and the [Schmidt coefficients](../../../../../../../schmidt-coefficient.md) removes this terminology ambiguity. The [pure state](../../../../../../../pure-state.md) is [entangled](../../../../../../../entangled-state.md) because its [Schmidt rank](../../../../../../../schmidt-rank.md) exceeds one.
