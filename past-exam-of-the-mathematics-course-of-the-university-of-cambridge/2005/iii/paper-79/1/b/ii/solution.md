<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $r=\sqrt{\mu/\delta}$. At first order the amplitude-phase [perturbation](../../../../../../../perturbation.md) is $A=r+\varepsilon(A_1+ir\theta)+O(\varepsilon^2)$. Expanding the cubic term in the [focusing nonlinear Schrodinger equation](../../../../../../../focusing-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md) gives $A|A|^2=r^3+\varepsilon(3r^2A_1+ir^3\theta)+O(\varepsilon^2)$. The constant terms cancel because $\delta r^2=\mu$. Separating real and [imaginary parts](../../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) yields

$$
\boxed{(A_1)_t=\chi r\theta_{xx},\qquad r\theta_t+\chi(A_1)_{xx}+2\mu A_1=0.}
$$

For the Fourier [normal modes](../../../../../../../normal-mode.md), these equations give $-i\Omega\widehat A_1=-\chi rK^2\widehat\theta$ and $-ir\Omega\widehat\theta+(2\mu-\chi K^2)\widehat A_1=0$. Their [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md) vanishes exactly when

$$
\boxed{\Omega^2=\chi K^2(\chi K^2-2\mu).}
$$

The [focusing nonlinear Schrodinger modulation dispersion](../../../../../../../focusing-nonlinear-schrodinger-modulation-dispersion.md) is negative for $0<K^2<2\mu/\chi$. One of the two [frequencies](../../../../../../../frequency.md) then has positive imaginary part and grows exponentially: the constant state has [modulational instability](../../../../../../../modulational-instability.md). The [growth rate](../../../../../../../growth-rate.md) is $\sqrt{\chi K^2(2\mu-\chi K^2)}$, maximized at $K^2=\mu/\chi$ with value $\mu$. Modes beyond the unstable band have real [frequencies](../../../../../../../frequency.md). The zero-[wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) phase mode is neutral, with possible linear phase drift from a uniform amplitude [perturbation](../../../../../../../perturbation.md).
