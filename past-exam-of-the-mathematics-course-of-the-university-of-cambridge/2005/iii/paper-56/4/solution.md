<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Represent affine [complexified Minkowski space](../../../../../complexified-minkowski-spacetime.md) by complex $2\times2$ [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) $x^{AA'}$, with the conformal metric characterized by $\det(dx)$. Let an element of [twistor space](../../../../../twistor-space.md) be $Z=(\omega^A,\pi_{A'})\in\mathbb C^4$, and identify nonzero twistors under $Z\sim cZ$, $c\ne0$. The compact [projective twistor space](../../../../../projective-twistor-space.md) is $\mathbb{CP}^3$. For affine space-time the corresponding open domain is the [affine projective twistor space](../../../../../affine-projective-twistor-space.md)

$$
\boxed{PT_{\rm aff}=\{[\omega,\pi]:\pi\ne0\}
=\mathbb{CP}^3\setminus\mathbb{CP}^1_\infty
\cong\operatorname{Tot}(\mathcal O(1)\oplus\mathcal O(1)).}
$$

Use the [twistor incidence relation](../../../../../twistor-incidence-relation.md) in the convention $\omega^A=x^{AA'}\pi_{A'}$; the more usual factor $i$ can be absorbed into the complex coordinate $x$. A point $x$ determines a [twistor line](../../../../../twistor-line.md)

$$
L_x=\{[x\pi,\pi]:[\pi]\in\mathbb{CP}^1\}\cong\mathbb{CP}^1.
$$

In the two projective charts, fibre coordinates $\omega/\pi_{0'}$ and $\omega/\pi_{1'}$ differ by division by $\lambda=\pi_{1'}/\pi_{0'}$, giving the two $\mathcal O(1)$ summands. Two distinct space-time points have intersecting lines precisely when $(x-y)\pi=0$ for some nonzero $\pi$, equivalently $\det(x-y)=0$; this recovers conformal null separation.

The [twistor correspondence](../../../../../twistor-correspondence.md) is the double fibration

$$
M_{\mathbb C}\,\xleftarrow{p}\,
\mathcal F=M_{\mathbb C}\times\mathbb{CP}^1
\ \xrightarrow{\ q\ }\ PT_{\rm aff},
\qquad q(x,[\pi])=[x\pi,\pi].
$$

For a fixed twistor, the incidence solutions form an affine [alpha-plane](../../../../../alpha-plane.md), a two-dimensional totally null plane. Its tangent distribution on $\mathcal F$ is generated by

$$
V_A=\pi^{A'}\partial_{AA'},\qquad
\pi^{A'}=\epsilon^{A'B'}\pi_{B'}.
$$

These [vector fields](../../../../../vector-field.md) annihilate the incidence coordinates, since $\pi^{A'}\pi_{A'}=0$, and commute. This distribution is the geometric source of the linear system in the [Penrose-Ward correspondence](../../../../../penrose-ward-correspondence.md).

For the inverse construction, take a rank-$r$ [holomorphic vector bundle](../../../../../holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) $E$ on the twistor domain corresponding to a space-time open set $U$. The crucial hypothesis is

$$
\boxed{E|_{L_x}\cong\mathcal O^{\oplus r}\quad\text{for every }x\in U.}
$$

These are [line-trivial bundles in the Ward correspondence](../../../../../line-trivial-bundles-in-the-ward-correspondence.md). It is not enough to specify an arbitrary [holomorphic vector bundle](../../../../../holomorphic-vector-bundle.md), nor just zero [First Chern class](../../../../../first-chern-class.md) on its lines: $\mathcal O(1)\oplus\mathcal O(-1)$ has degree zero but is nontrivial. A line with a nontrivial splitting is a jumping line and does not support the regular linewise construction there. Thus a globally nonsingular field on all affine complexified space-time requires triviality on all its relevant lines; a local construction uses only the lines over $U$.

Choose [holomorphic vector bundle](../../../../../holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) [bundle frames](../../../../../frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) on two twistor patches, and let $P$ be the invertible transition [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) on their overlap. Pull it back to $\mathcal F$. Because it comes from [affine projective twistor space](../../../../../affine-projective-twistor-space.md), it is constant along the $q$-fibres:

$$
V_A P(x,\pi)=0.
$$

Triviality on $L_x$ supplies a global [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) [bundle frame](../../../../../frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) on that line. Comparing this global [bundle frame](../../../../../frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) with the two local [bundle frames](../../../../../frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) produces a [holomorphic splitting of a twistor patching matrix](../../../../../holomorphic-splitting-of-a-twistor-patching-matrix.md),

$$
P(x,\lambda)=\Psi_-(x,\lambda)^{-1}\Psi_+(x,\lambda),
$$

where $\Psi_+$ and $\Psi_-$ are [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) and invertible near the two affine patches of the [complex projective line](../../../../../complex-projective-line.md). The splitting can be chosen holomorphically in $x$ locally: the restrictions have $r$ independent global sections and $H^1(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O^{\oplus r})=0$, so these [bundle frames](../../../../../frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) extend through a neighborhood of the given line. This explains where regularity and linewise triviality enter the construction.

Differentiate the factorization using $V_AP=0$. Multiplication by the invertible factors gives

$$
(V_A\Psi_+)\Psi_+^{-1}
=(V_A\Psi_-)\Psi_-^{-1}.
$$

Define their common negative to be $\mathcal A_A$. It is regular on both projective patches and homogeneous of degree one in $\pi$, because $V_A$ has that degree while the [bundle frames](../../../../../frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) have degree zero. Consequently

$$
\mathcal A_A(x,\pi)=\pi^{A'}A_{AA'}(x).
$$

This last conclusion is elementary: on one affine chart its entries are entire in $\lambda$; regularity at infinity after dividing by the transition factor $\lambda$ allows at most linear growth, so each entry is affine in $\lambda$. Equivalently, a global section of $\mathcal O(1)$ is a linear homogeneous polynomial. We have thereby recovered a [gauge connection](../../../../../connection-vector-bundle.md) $D_{AA'}=\partial_{AA'}+A_{AA'}$ on the space-time bundle whose fibre is $H^0(L_x,E|_{L_x})$.

The splitting factors now obey

$$
\boxed{\pi^{A'}D_{AA'}\Psi_\pm=0.}
$$

Their invertibility implies the compatibility conditions

$$
\pi^{A'}\pi^{B'}F_{AA'BB'}=0\quad\text{for every }[\pi].
$$

In the [spinor decomposition of gauge curvature](../../../../../spinor-decomposition-of-gauge-curvature.md),

$$
F_{AA'BB'}=\epsilon_{AB}\Phi_{A'B'}+\epsilon_{A'B'}\Phi_{AB},
$$

the two spinors are symmetric. The antisymmetric primed factor vanishes when contracted with $\pi^{A'}\pi^{B'}$, leaving $\epsilon_{AB}\pi^{A'}\pi^{B'}\Phi_{A'B'}=0$. A quadratic polynomial that vanishes for every spinor has every coefficient zero, so $\Phi_{A'B'}=0$. With the orientation convention used here, this is exactly [anti-self-duality of gauge curvature](../../../../../anti-self-duality-of-gauge-curvature.md), hence the [ASDYM equations](../../../../../anti-self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md). This establishes the field equation from the [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) data, rather than assuming it as an extra input.

The construction naturally gives a gauge class. If another linewise splitting is chosen, $\Psi'_\pm\Psi_\pm^{-1}$ agrees on the overlap and is [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) on the entire compact [complex projective line](../../../../../complex-projective-line.md); its entries are therefore independent of $\lambda$. Write this common [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) as $g(x)^{-1}$. Then

$$
A'_{AA'}=g^{-1}A_{AA'}g+g^{-1}\partial_{AA'}g,
$$

the ordinary [Yang-Mills gauge transformation](../../../../../yang-mills-gauge-transformation.md). Changing the original [holomorphic vector bundle](../../../../../holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) [bundle frames](../../../../../frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) or replacing $E$ by an isomorphic bundle produces the same space-time gauge class. A determinant trivialization or another [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) structure-group reduction gives the corresponding restriction from $GL(r,\mathbb C)$ to $SL(r,\mathbb C)$ or another complex [gauge group](../../../../../gauge-group.md).

For perspective, the forward direction starts from an ASD [gauge connection](../../../../../connection-vector-bundle.md) and uses its flat [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) along each [alpha-plane](../../../../../alpha-plane.md) to descend parallel sections to [affine projective twistor space](../../../../../affine-projective-twistor-space.md). The reconstructed linear system is exactly that parallel-section system. Going back through it recovers the original line-trivial bundle, while changing space-time [bundle frames](../../../../../frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) amounts to [Yang-Mills gauge transformation](../../../../../yang-mills-gauge-transformation.md). Thus, locally and with the stated regularity and triviality conditions, **[holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) line-trivial bundles determine solutions of the [ASDYM equations](../../../../../anti-self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md) uniquely up to gauge**.

No unitary reality condition is imposed on [complexified Minkowski space](../../../../../complexified-minkowski-spacetime.md) in this question. Obtaining real Euclidean [gauge fields](../../../../../gauge-field.md) requires the compatible antiholomorphic twistor involution and a bundle reality structure; obtaining finite-action instantons requires further global and boundary conditions. Those are additional restrictions on the complex correspondence, not automatic consequences of having any [holomorphic vector bundle](../../../../../holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) on [projective twistor space](../../../../../projective-twistor-space.md).
