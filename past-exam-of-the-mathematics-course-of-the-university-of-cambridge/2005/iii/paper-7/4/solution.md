<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Apply the [spectral-radius maximum principle](../../../../../spectral-radius-maximum-principle.md) to the entire [Banach-space-valued holomorphic function](../../../../../banach-space-valued-holomorphic-function.md) $f(z)=a+zb$ on the [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) disk $|z|\le\varepsilon$. On its [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) the assumed estimate gives $r(f(z))\le C\varepsilon$. Therefore

$$
0\le r(a)=r(f(0))\le C\varepsilon
$$

for every $\varepsilon>0$. Letting $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ proves

$$
\boxed{r(a)=0.}
$$

The puncture at $z=0$ in the hypothesis causes no difficulty: the bound is used only on the nonzero [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) circle.
