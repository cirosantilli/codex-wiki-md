↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The domination hypothesis includes $r=0$. For any approximation error $h$, [linearity](../../../../../../../linearity.md) and the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) therefore give the uniform estimate

$$
|T_rh-T_0h|\leq|T_rh|+|T_0h|\leq2S(h).
$$

For a general input $g$ and an approximating $f$, this implies

$$
|T_rg-T_0g|
\leq|T_rf-T_0f|+2S(g-f).
$$

The first term converges on the dense class, while the [weak type (1,1)](../../../../../../../weak-type-1-1.md) estimate controls the exceptional set where the second is large.
