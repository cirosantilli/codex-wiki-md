<h1 id="3/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The printed symbol $E$ is not defined. Take $E=L^1(\mathbb R)$, or more generally an $L^1$ subspace with its induced $L^1$ [norm](../../../../../../../norm.md); density must be in that [norm](../../../../../../../norm.md). Given $g\in E$, choose a sequence $f_n\in F$ with

$$
\|g-f_n\|_1\leq2^{-2n}.
$$

By the assumed convergence on $F$, each $T_rf_n\to T_0f_n$ holds outside a [null set](../../../../../../../null-set.md) $N_n$. Their [countable union](../../../../../../../countable-union.md) $N=\bigcup_nN_n$ is still null. Thus outside $N$ the convergence statements for every member of this single approximation sequence hold simultaneously. No assertion of simultaneous convergence for the entire uncountable dense class is needed.
