<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A nonnegative [sublinear operator](../../../../../../sublinear-operator.md) satisfies $S(f+g)\leq S(f)+S(g)$ and $S(cf)=|c|S(f)$ [almost everywhere](../../../../../../almost-everywhere.md). It has [weak type (1,1)](../../../../../../weak-type-1-1.md) if there is a finite constant $C$, independent of the function and threshold, such that

$$
\boxed{\lambda\{x:S(f)(x)>\alpha\}\leq
\frac{C}{\alpha}\|f\|_{L^1(\mathbb R)}
\quad\text{for every }f\in L^1(\mathbb R),\ \alpha>0.}
$$

For a signed or complex output use $|S(f)|$ in the [level set](../../../../../../level-set.md). The bound controls the distribution of large values; it does not assert that $S(f)$ is integrable.
