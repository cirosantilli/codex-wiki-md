<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [Airy equation](../../../../../../airy-equation.md), let $P=e^{-ikx+\omega t}q$. Its [local relation](../../../../../../local-relation.md) follows by arranging the flux so that the unwanted spatial derivatives cancel:

$$
(\partial_x-ik)(-q_{xx}-ikq_x+k^2q)=-q_{xxx}-ik^3q.
$$

Consequently

$$
P_t-(e^{-ikx+\omega t}X)_x=e^{-ikx+\omega t}[q_t+q_{xxx}+(\omega+ik^3)q].
$$

Thus the [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) and the required flux are **given by**

$$
\boxed{\omega(k)=-ik^3,\qquad X=-q_{xx}-ikq_x+k^2q.}
$$

The calculation is polynomial in the complex [spectral parameter](../../../../../../spectral-parameter.md), so the [local relation](../../../../../../local-relation.md) holds for every $k\in\mathbb C$.
