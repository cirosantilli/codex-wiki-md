<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

A [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md) requires two independent even coefficients in the [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) to vanish: $r=u=0$, with $v>0$ providing stability. At $h=0$, the ordinary critical line $r=0,u>0$ meets the [tricritical three-phase line](../../../../../../tricritical-three-phase-line.md) $r=3u^2/(16v),u<0$. The [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) discontinuity along the first-order line is $\sqrt{-3u/(4v)}$, which goes to zero at their meeting.

The positive sextic coefficient makes this a different leading local potential from an ordinary quartic [critical point](../../../../../../critical-point.md). Consequently its [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md), crossover behavior and [upper critical dimension](../../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md) differ. There are two relevant even tuning directions near the [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md), in addition to the conjugate odd field. The root solution and labelled [phase diagram](../../../../../../phase-diagram.md) display the three controls and the [tricritical wings](../../../../../../tricritical-wing.md); varying $h$ can give a first-order reversal within the ordered coexistence sheet, a first-order weak/strong transition on a wing, or a continuous transition at a wing's ordinary critical edge.
