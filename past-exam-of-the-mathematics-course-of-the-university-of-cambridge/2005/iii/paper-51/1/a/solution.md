<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) distinguishes the equilibrium phases and captures the relevant broken [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md). For an [Ising model](../../../../../../ising-model.md), it is the spontaneous [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md): its sign changes under spin reversal, and it is zero in the symmetric phase. Spontaneous [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md) means taking the thermodynamic limit before sending the external field to zero; the finite-volume zero-field mean can vanish even in an ordered phase.

In [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md), a slowly varying real [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) $\phi(x)$ represents the coarse-grained local [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md). A useful local [free-energy density](../../../../../../free-energy-density.md) gives the functional

$$
\mathcal F[\phi]=\int d^Dx\left[\frac\kappa2(\nabla\phi)^2+\frac r2\phi^2+\frac u4\phi^4+\frac v6\phi^6-h\phi\right],\qquad\kappa>0,\ v>0.
$$

At zero field, spin-reversal [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) excludes odd powers. The gradient term penalizes rapid spatial variation. The field $h$ is the [field conjugate to an order parameter](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md). The [mean-field approximation](../../../../../../mean-field-approximation.md) replaces the fluctuating [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) by the equilibrium minimizer; a uniform minimizer has $\phi=M$ and obeys $rM+uM^3+vM^5=h$.
