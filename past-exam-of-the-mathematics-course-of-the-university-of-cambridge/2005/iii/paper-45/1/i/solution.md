[Parametric linkage analysis](../../../../../../parametric-linkage-analysis.md) specifies a disease-transmission model: the mode of inheritance, disease-[allele](../../../../../../allele.md) frequency and [penetrance](../../../../../../penetrance.md) of each [genotype](../../../../../../genotype.md). Combining these with [Mendelian segregation](../../../../../../mendelian-segregation.md) and the [recombination fraction](../../../../../../recombination-fraction.md) gives a [pedigree likelihood](../../../../../../pedigree-likelihood.md). One can estimate the [recombination fraction](../../../../../../recombination-fraction.md) or compare a linked model, $\theta<1/2$, with the unlinked model, $\theta=1/2$, using a [likelihood-ratio test](../../../../../../likelihood-ratio-test.md) or [LOD score](../../../../../../lod-score.md). Incomplete [penetrance](../../../../../../penetrance.md) and unobserved [genotypes](../../../../../../genotype.md) are accommodated by summing over the possible underlying inheritance states.

[Nonparametric linkage analysis](../../../../../../nonparametric-linkage-analysis.md) instead tests whether relatives with the same disease [phenotype](../../../../../../phenotype.md) share marker [alleles](../../../../../../allele.md) [identical by descent](../../../../../../identity-by-descent.md) more often than expected from their [pedigree](../../../../../../pedigree.md). For example, [affected-sib-pair analysis](../../../../../../affected-sib-pair-analysis.md) compares sharing among affected [full siblings](../../../../../../full-sibling.md) with its Mendelian null distribution. It avoids specifying a complete disease-[penetrance](../../../../../../penetrance.md) model and disease-[allele](../../../../../../allele.md) frequency. “Model-free” still assumes an inheritance model for the marker, correct family relationships and a justified treatment of [genetic-study ascertainment](../../../../../../genetic-study-ascertainment.md); it does not [mean](../../../../../../expected-value.md) assumption-free.

**Parametric analysis models the disease [likelihood](../../../../../../likelihood-function.md); nonparametric analysis tests departure from the marker's Mendelian sharing distribution.**
