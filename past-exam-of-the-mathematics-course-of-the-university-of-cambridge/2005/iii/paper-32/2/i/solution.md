<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [isogeny of elliptic curves](../../../../../../isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md) over $k$ is a nonconstant $k$-morphism $\varphi:E_1\to E_2$ which sends $O_1$ to $O_2$. A nonconstant morphism of smooth projective curves is finite and surjective. Its compatibility with the [elliptic curve group law from Riemann-Roch](../../../../../../elliptic-curve-group-law-from-riemann-roch.md) can be seen without assuming it in the definition.

Identify a point $P$ with the degree-zero [divisor class](../../../../../../divisor-class.md) $[P-O_1]$. A [finite morphism](../../../../../../finite-morphism.md) pushes forward divisors, and pushes a [principal divisor](../../../../../../principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) to the [principal divisor](../../../../../../principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) of the norm of its rational function. It therefore induces a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) on [degree-zero Picard groups of a curve](../../../../../../degree-zero-picard-group-of-a-curve.md). On geometric points, the pushforward of $[P-O_1]$ is $[\varphi(P)-O_2]$. Since point addition is addition of these [divisor classes](../../../../../../divisor-class.md), this proves

$$
\boxed{\varphi(P+Q)=\varphi(P)+\varphi(Q).}
$$

The equality is defined over $k$, so in particular it holds on the [groups](../../../../../../group-split.md) of $k$-rational points and after every [field](../../../../../../field.md) extension. The argument also covers inseparable [isogenies of elliptic curves](../../../../../../isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md); separability is not part of the definition.
