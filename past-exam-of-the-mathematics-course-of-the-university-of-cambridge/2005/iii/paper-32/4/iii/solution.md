<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a rank-zero example, take $E_0:y^2=x^3+x$, with rational two-torsion $(0,0)$. Its [two-isogeny formula](../../../../../../two-isogeny-formula.md) companion is $E'_0:y^2=x^3-4x$. The [prime-support bound in two-isogeny descent](../../../../../../prime-support-bound-in-two-isogeny-descent.md) allows only $\pm1$ on $E_0$; all nonzero affine $x$ are positive because $x(x^2+1)$ is a square. Hence $A_0=\{1\}$. For $E'_0$ the possible classes are $\{1,-1,2,-2\}$, all realized: $O$ gives $1$, $(0,0)$ gives $[-4]=-1$, and $(2,0),(-2,0)$ give $2,-2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{2^{g_{E_0}}=\frac{1\cdot4}{4}=1,\qquad g_{E_0}=0.}
$$

This is the [rank-zero elliptic curve with cubic x cubed plus x](../../../../../../rank-zero-elliptic-curve-with-cubic-x-cubed-plus-x.md) certificate; no unresolved locally soluble covering is being counted as soluble.

For a positive-rank example, take $E_1:y^2=x^3-2x$. Its companion is $E'_1:y^2=x^3+8x$. The first image is contained in $\{1,-1,2,-2\}$. The identity gives $1$, $(0,0)$ gives $-2$, and $(2,2)$ gives $2$; multiplicativity of the square-class [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) also gives $-1$. Thus $A_1$ has all four classes. On $E'_1$, the same prime-support bound allows $\pm1,\pm2$, but $x(x^2+8)$ is nonnegative only for $x\ge0$, eliminating the negative classes. The identity gives $1$ and $(0,0)$ gives $[8]=2$, so $B_1=\{1,2\}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{2^{g_{E_1}}=\frac{4\cdot2}{4}=2,\qquad g_{E_1}=1.}
$$

The [rank-one elliptic curve with cubic x cubed minus twice x](../../../../../../rank-one-elliptic-curve-with-cubic-x-cubed-minus-twice-x.md) therefore supplies the required positive-rank example. The exhibited point $(2,2)$ is itself nontorsion: doubling gives $(9/4,-21/8)$, whose nonintegral coordinates contradict the [Nagell–Lutz theorem](../../../../../../nagell-lutz-theorem.md) if it were torsion. No assertion that this point generates the full free part is needed for the rank calculation.
