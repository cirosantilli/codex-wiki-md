<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Over $\mathbb F_2$, both $x=0,1$ give $x^3-x=0$, and both $y=0,1$ give $y^2+y=0$. There are four affine [rational points](../../../../../../rational-point.md), together with the point $O$ at infinity. Over $\mathbb F_3$, each $x=0,1,2$ gives $x^3-x=0$, while precisely $y=0,2$ solve $y^2+y=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\#\widetilde E(\mathbb F_2)=5,\qquad\#\widetilde E(\mathbb F_3)=7.}
$$

Both reductions are nonsingular by the discriminant calculation, so these are the point counts of the reduced [elliptic curves](../../../../../../elliptic-curve.md), with their [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) laws.
