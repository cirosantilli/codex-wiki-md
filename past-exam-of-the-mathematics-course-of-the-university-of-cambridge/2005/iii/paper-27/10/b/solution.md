<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Idempotence gives the [join](../../../../../../least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) order $x\le y\iff x+y=y$. Reflexivity follows from $x+x=x$; antisymmetry follows from commutativity; if $x+y=y$ and $y+z=z$, associativity gives $x+z=x+(y+z)=(x+y)+z=z$. Thus it is a [partial order](../../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md).
