Distributivity makes multiplication [order-preserving](../../../../../../order-preserving-function.md) for the natural order: if $u+v=v$, then $xu+xv=x(u+v)=xv$, so $xu\le xv$. It also expands every generated expression into a finite join of monomials, as used in [solution](../solution.md).
