<h1 id="2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For the [theta pinch](../../../../../../theta-pinch.md), the [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) is azimuthal:

$$
\mathbf j_0=\frac c{4\pi}\nabla\times(B_0\hat{\mathbf z})=-\frac{cB_0'}{4\pi}\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}.
$$

Its [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) is $\mathbf j_0\times\mathbf B_0/c=-B_0B_0'\hat{\mathbf r}/(4\pi)$. Static radial balance with the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) gradient therefore gives

$$
\boxed{p_0'+\frac{B_0B_0'}{4\pi}=0,\qquad p_0(r)+\frac{B_0(r)^2}{8\pi}=\text{constant}.}
$$

The constant is the equilibrium [magnetohydrodynamic total pressure](../../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-total-pressure.md).
