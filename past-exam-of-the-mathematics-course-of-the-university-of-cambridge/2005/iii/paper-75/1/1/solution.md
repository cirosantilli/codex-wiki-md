<h1 id="1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Use [Gaussian units](../../../../../../gaussian-units.md), uniform [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) $n$, and [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) $\rho=nm_i$. Here $e>0$ is the magnitude of the elementary charge, as fixed by the printed current relation. Neglect [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) inertia and displacement current. Then

$$
\mathbf j=\frac c{4\pi}\nabla\times\mathbf B,\qquad
\mathbf u_e=\mathbf u-\frac c{4\pi en}\nabla\times\mathbf B.
$$

Freezing the [magnetic flux](../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) into this [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) velocity, while retaining the [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) momentum equation, gives the closed [Hall magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../../../hall-magnetohydrodynamics.md) system

$$
\boxed{\partial_t\mathbf B=\nabla\times\left[\left(\mathbf u-\frac c{4\pi en}\nabla\times\mathbf B\right)\times\mathbf B\right],}
$$



$$
\boxed{\partial_t\mathbf u+(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=-\frac{\nabla p}{\rho}+\frac{(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\times\mathbf B}{4\pi\rho},\qquad\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0.}
$$

The [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) enforces the [incompressible flow](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) constraint; taking the divergence of momentum determines it up to the usual boundary-dependent constant. The induction equation preserves the [solenoidal magnetic-field constraint](../../../../../../solenoidal-magnetic-field-constraint.md). One can absorb $B^2/(8\pi)$ into [magnetohydrodynamic total pressure](../../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-total-pressure.md), replacing the Lorentz term by [magnetic tension](../../../../../../magnetic-tension.md). Keeping [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) inertia here is important: [electron magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../../../electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md) with fixed [ions](../../../../../../ion.md) will be a later limit, not the complete two-field system.
