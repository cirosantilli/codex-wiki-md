<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A norm estimate controls the worst possible refined differences for every choice of the initial controls. Submultiplicativity turns a length-$p$ contraction into a bound for arbitrary long products, so it proves a certain number of continuous [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md). Its [absolute values](../../../../../../absolute-value.md) can lose cancellations; failure of the estimate is inconclusive. **It therefore gives a [lower bound](../../../../../../lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) on smoothness.**

Conversely, an observable eigenmode of a repeated local product exhibits a particular sequence of shrinking neighborhoods with inadequate decay. General-position controls have a nonzero coefficient in that mode, so greater [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) is impossible. Special symmetric or [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) data may suppress the bad mode; this is why the general-position qualification matters. **The obstruction gives an [upper bound](../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) on smoothness, while failure to find an obstruction proves nothing.** A [lower bound](../../../../../../lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) on spectral growth becomes an [upper bound](../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) on smoothness because large surviving differences correspond to rough behavior.

For example, the binary [Cardinal cubic B-spline](../../../../../../cardinal-cubic-b-spline.md) [subdivision mask](../../../../../../subdivision-mask.md) has symbol $M(z)=(1+z)^4/8$, up to an irrelevant index shift. At [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) order $r=2$, the first-difference [subdivision mask](../../../../../../subdivision-mask.md) is $B_2=(1+z)/2$, whose maximum parity sum is $1/2$: the limit is at least $C^2$. At $r=3$, $B_3=1$, and the surviving difference mode does not decay. For generic controls the third [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is piecewise constant with nonzero jumps, so the [subdivision curve](../../../../../../subdivision-curve.md) is not $C^3$. Here the lower and upper tests determine the exact [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md), but they need not coincide for an arbitrary [subdivision mask](../../../../../../subdivision-mask.md). Stability, sum rules and borderline modes must be handled explicitly before interpreting any numerical estimate as a [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) level.
