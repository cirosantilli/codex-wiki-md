<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If each univariate basis sums to one, distribute the finite sums, or use local finiteness when the basis is infinite:

$$
\boxed{\sum_j\sum_kb_{jk}(u,v)=\left(\sum_j\phi_j(u)\right)\left(\sum_k\psi_k(v)\right)=1.}
$$

Thus the [tensor-product surface basis](../../../../../../tensor-product-surface-basis.md) is a [partition of unity](../../../../../../partition-of-unity.md). In particular, translating every [control point](../../../../../../control-point.md) by a fixed vector translates the [parametric surface](../../../../../../parametric-surface.md) by precisely that vector, and constant control data produce a constant [parametric surface](../../../../../../parametric-surface.md).
