↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [tensor-product surface basis](../../../../../../tensor-product-surface-basis.md) is $b_{jk}(u,v)=\phi_j(u)\psi_k(v)$. If both univariate bases consist of [nonnegative functions](../../../../../../nonnegative-function.md), then

$$
\boxed{b_{jk}(u,v)\ge0.}
$$

If both factors are strictly positive at the chosen parameters, their product is strictly positive there as well. Its support is the [Cartesian product](../../../../../../cartesian-product.md) of the two factor supports. With [partition of unity](../../../../../../partition-of-unity.md), this nonnegativity additionally places the [parametric surface](../../../../../../parametric-surface.md) point in the [convex hull](../../../../../../convex-hull.md) of the active [control points](../../../../../../control-point.md).
