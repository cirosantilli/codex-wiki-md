<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Let the univariate [linear precision](../../../../../../linear-precision-of-a-geometric-basis.md) sites be $\xi_j,\eta_k$, meaning

$$
\sum_j\phi_j(u)=1,\quad\sum_j\xi_j\phi_j(u)=u,\qquad\sum_k\psi_k(v)=1,\quad\sum_k\eta_k\psi_k(v)=v.
$$

Then their Cartesian-product grid is a [linear precision](../../../../../../linear-precision-of-a-geometric-basis.md) set for the [tensor-product surface basis](../../../../../../tensor-product-surface-basis.md):

$$
\sum_{j,k}(\xi_j,\eta_k)\phi_j(u)\psi_k(v)=(u,v).
$$

Indeed, the first coordinate is $(\sum_j\xi_j\phi_j)(\sum_k\psi_k)=u$, and the second is $(\sum_j\phi_j)(\sum_k\eta_k\psi_k)=v$. More generally, take controls $P_{jk}=A+\xi_jB+\eta_kC$. The resulting [parametric surface](../../../../../../parametric-surface.md) is exactly

$$
\boxed{P(u,v)=A+uB+vC.}
$$

This proves [linear precision of a geometric basis](../../../../../../linear-precision-of-a-geometric-basis.md) and [affine map](../../../../../../affine-map.md) reproduction, including arbitrary changes of the embedding plane. In fact the products also reproduce the bilinear coordinate $uv$, since $\sum_{j,k}\xi_j\eta_k\phi_j\psi_k=uv$; affine precision is the requested consequence.
