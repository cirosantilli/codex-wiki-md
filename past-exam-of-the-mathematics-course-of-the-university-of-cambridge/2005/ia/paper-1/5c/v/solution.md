<h1 id="5c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The cubic terms are the expansion of $(z+\overline z)^3=(2x)^3$. Subtracting $\overline z=x-iy$ therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}=8x^3-x,\qquad \operatorname{Im}=y.}
$$

Here the expression is a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $z$ and its [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md), rather than a holomorphic polynomial in $z$ alone.
