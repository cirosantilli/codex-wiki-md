↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [complex exponential function](../../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) factorizes as $e^{x+iy}=e^xe^{iy}=e^x(\cos y+i\sin y)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}(e^z)=e^x\cos y,\qquad \operatorname{Im}(e^z)=e^x\sin y.}
$$

The real exponential multiplies both components; it does not change the [argument](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md).
