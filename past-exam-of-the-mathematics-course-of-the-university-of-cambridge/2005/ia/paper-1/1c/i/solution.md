<h1 id="1c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The two free indices are the row and column indices of a [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md). The [Kronecker delta](../../../../../../kronecker-delta.md) has value one on its diagonal and zero off it, so the answer is **the $3\times3$ [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md) $I$**.
