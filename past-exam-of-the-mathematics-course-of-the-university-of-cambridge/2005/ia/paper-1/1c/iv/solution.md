<h1 id="1c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Compare with $(\mathbf b\times\mathbf a)_i=\epsilon_{ijk}b_ja_k$. The scalar factors commute, so the answer is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf b\times\mathbf a=-\mathbf a\times\mathbf b.}
$$

The order of the contracted indices matters because the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) is antisymmetric.
