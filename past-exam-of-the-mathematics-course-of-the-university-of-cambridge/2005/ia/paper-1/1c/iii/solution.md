<h1 id="1c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The contracted [Kronecker delta](../../../../../../kronecker-delta.md) is $\delta_{ll}=3$. The other scalar contraction is $a_iC_{ij}b_j=\mathbf a^TC\mathbf b$, while $C_{ik}d_i$ is the $k$th component of the [matrix transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) applied to $\mathbf d$. Hence the resulting [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is

$$
\boxed{3(\mathbf a^TC\mathbf b)\mathbf d-C^T\mathbf d.}
$$

Both terms have the same single free index $k$, as required by the [Einstein summation convention](../../../../../../einstein-notation.md).
