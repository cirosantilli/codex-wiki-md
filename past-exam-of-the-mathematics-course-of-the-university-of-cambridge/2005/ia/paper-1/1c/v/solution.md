<h1 id="1c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Interchange the two dummy indices $j,k$. The product $a_ja_k$ is symmetric under this interchange, while the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) changes sign. Thus the contraction equals its negative and is **the zero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md)**, equivalently $\mathbf a\times\mathbf a=\mathbf0$.
