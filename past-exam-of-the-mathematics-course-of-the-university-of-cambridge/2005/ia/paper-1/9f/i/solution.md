<h1 id="9f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $n\ge2$, $0<n^{-2}\le[n(n-1)]^{-1}=1/(n-1)-1/n$. The upper bound has telescoping partial sums, bounded by one. The positive partial sums of the original [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) are consequently bounded by two and increasing, so they converge as a [monotone bounded sequence](../../../../../../monotone-bounded-sequence.md). **The series converges absolutely.** This also supplies the $p=2$ case of a [p-series](../../../../../../p-series.md) without requiring its exact sum.
