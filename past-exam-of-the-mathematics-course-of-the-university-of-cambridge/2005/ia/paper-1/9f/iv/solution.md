<h1 id="9f/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The PDF has the doubly exponential denominator $e^{e^n}$. For its positive summands, the successive-term ratio is

$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}=\left(1+\frac1n\right)^3\exp\bigl(e^n-e^{n+1}\bigr)=\left(1+\frac1n\right)^3e^{-(e-1)e^n}\longrightarrow0.
$$

Thus the [ratio test](../../../../../../ratio-test.md) proves **absolute convergence**. Concretely, the tail is eventually dominated by a [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) of ratio $1/2$; the polynomial numerator cannot offset the doubly exponential decay.
