↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The complex [Taylor theorem](../../../../../../taylor-theorem.md) says that an [analytic function](../../../../../../space-of-holomorphic-functions.md) has the expansion

$$
f(z)=\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(m)}(z_0)}{m!}(z-z_0)^m
$$

throughout every disk centred at $z_0$ contained in $D$. In particular it converges for $|z-z_0|<\operatorname{dist}(z_0,\mathbb C\setminus D)$, and uniformly on every smaller closed disk. The coefficients are uniquely its [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) divided by factorials. The convergence radius can be larger if the function extends beyond the boundary of $D$; the theorem does not say that the given domain determines its exact radius.
