At $B=K$, homogeneity gives

$$
\frac{S_0}{K}C(K^2/S_0,K)=C(K,S_0)=P(S_0,K).
$$

The price is therefore $C(S_0,K)-P(S_0,K)$. Zero-rate [put-call parity](../../../../../../../put-call-parity.md) yields

$$
\boxed{C_{\rm out}=S_0-K}.
$$
