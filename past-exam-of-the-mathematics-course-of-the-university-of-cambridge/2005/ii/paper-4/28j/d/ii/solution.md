↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [zero-rate barrier-strike call hedge](../../../../../../../zero-rate-barrier-strike-call-hedge.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\text{fair value}=100-80=20,\qquad\Delta=1}.
$$

Hold one share and borrow $80$. Stop and liquidate when the price first hits $80$: [continuity](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) makes the portfolio value exactly zero then, so it meets the knocked-out payoff. If no hit occurs before maturity, the stock remains above $80$ and the portfolio pays $S_T-80$, exactly the call payoff. There is no dependence on the stated volatility or maturity in this special contract.
