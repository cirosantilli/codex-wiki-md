<h1 id="1h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [prime factorization](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) is $561=3\cdot11\cdot17$, so it is composite and [squarefree](../../../../../../squarefree-integer.md). Moreover $3-1=2$, $11-1=10$ and $17-1=16$ all divide $560=561-1$. The [Korselt criterion](../../../../../../korselt-criterion.md) therefore proves that **561 is a [Carmichael number](../../../../../../carmichael-number.md)**.
