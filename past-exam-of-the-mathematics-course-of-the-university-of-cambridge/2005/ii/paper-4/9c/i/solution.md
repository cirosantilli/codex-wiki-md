<h1 id="9c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Poisson bracket](../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) is $\{Q,P\}=\alpha q^{\beta-2}$. For it to be identically one on an open region, the exponent must vanish and the coefficient must equal one:

$$
\boxed{\beta=2,\qquad\alpha=1}.
$$

The transformation is defined away from $q=0$, and its inverse there is $q=1/P$, $p=QP^2$.
