<h1 id="9c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiating gives

$$
\{Q,P\}=\alpha\beta q^{2\alpha-1}(\cos^2(\beta p)+\sin^2(\beta p))
=\alpha\beta q^{2\alpha-1}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac12,\qquad\beta=2}.
$$

For real coordinates take $q>0$ and a local branch of the angle. The transformation is locally [canonical transformation](../../../../../../canonical-transformation.md) because its determinant is one, but periodicity in $p$ prevents a globally one-to-one map if all real $p$ are retained.
