<h1 id="10d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), $H=\dot a/a=2/(3t)$. The flat matter-only [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) $H^2=8\pi G\rho_m/3$ gives $\rho_m=1/(6\pi Gt^2)$. With zero [sound speed](../../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) the perturbation equation is therefore

$$
\ddot\delta_k+\frac4{3t}\dot\delta_k-\frac2{3t^2}\delta_k=0.
$$

Substituting $t^s$ yields $s(s-1)+4s/3-2/3=0$, or $(3s-2)(s+1)=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\delta_k=A_kt^{2/3}+B_kt^{-1}},
$$

with growing mode proportional to the [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) and decaying mode proportional to $t^{-1}$.
