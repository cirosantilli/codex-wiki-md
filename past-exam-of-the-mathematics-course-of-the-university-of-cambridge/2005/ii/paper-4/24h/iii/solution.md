<h1 id="24h/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\zeta=u+iv$. Direct differentiation gives

$$
\boxed{\varphi_1=1-\zeta^2,\qquad
\varphi_2=i(1+\zeta^2),\qquad
\varphi_3=2\zeta}.
$$

These are holomorphic [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) and their squared sum is

$$
(1-\zeta^2)^2-(1+\zeta^2)^2+4\zeta^2=0.
$$

Moreover direct calculation of the metric gives $E=G=(1+u^2+v^2)^2>0$ and $F=0$, so the map is genuinely an [immersion](../../../../../../immersion.md) everywhere. The preceding characterizations prove that it is an **[isothermal parametrization](../../../../../../isothermal-coordinates.md) of a [minimal surface](../../../../../../minimal-surface.md)**, the [Enneper surface](../../../../../../enneper-surface.md).
