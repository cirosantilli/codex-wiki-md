<h1 id="31d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With $K=1$, the additive jump is $g(t)=1/[2(t^2-1)]$, analytic inside the contour. Its [Cauchy integral](../../../../../../cauchy-transform.md) has $D_+(z)=g(z)$ and $D_-(z)=0$. Therefore $C_+=(z^2-1)g=1/2$, $C_-=0$, and

$$
\boxed{\phi(t)=\tfrac12.}
$$

The moment is indeed $(\pi i)^{-1}\int_L(\tau+1/\tau)(1/2)d\tau=1$, as required. The principal-value Cauchy sum is $1/2$, and the original left side becomes $[(t^2+t-1)-(t^2-t-1)]/2-1=t-1$. Thus the recovered function satisfies both the equation and the self-consistency condition, and the homogeneous negative-index argument proves uniqueness.
