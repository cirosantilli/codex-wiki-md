<h1 id="24h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A point $x\in X$ is a [critical point of a smooth map](../../../../../../critical-point-of-a-smooth-map.md) if $df_x:T_xX\to T_{f(x)}Y$ is not surjective. A [critical value](../../../../../../critical-value.md) is the image of at least one critical point. A [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md) is a point $y\in Y$ for which every point of $f^{-1}(y)$ has surjective [differential](../../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md). A point outside the image is therefore a [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md) vacuously. These definitions use rank relative to $\dim Y$, not merely whether a [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) vanishes in chosen coordinates.
