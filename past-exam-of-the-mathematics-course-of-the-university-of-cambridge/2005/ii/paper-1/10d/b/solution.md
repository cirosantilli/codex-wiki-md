<h1 id="10d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The weak-conversion rate per expansion time falls as $T^3$. Below the stated threshold it becomes less than one, so neutron-proton interconversion cannot track the rapidly changing equilibrium ratio: [cosmological weak freeze-out](../../../../../../cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md) occurs. Free [neutrons](../../../../../../neutron.md) subsequently decay, reducing their abundance until [nuclei](../../../../../../atomic-nucleus.md) can survive photodissociation. Once the [deuterium bottleneck](../../../../../../deuterium-bottleneck.md) is passed, [nuclear reactions](../../../../../../nuclear-reaction.md) rapidly assemble [neutrons](../../../../../../neutron.md) into the strongly bound [helium-4](../../../../../../helium-4.md) [nucleus](../../../../../../atomic-nucleus.md). The short available time, low density and missing stable mass-five and mass-eight [nuclei](../../../../../../atomic-nucleus.md) impede extensive production of heavier [nuclei](../../../../../../atomic-nucleus.md), leaving nearly all surviving [neutrons](../../../../../../neutron.md) in helium.

If $r=n_n/n_p$ just before helium formation, each helium [nucleus](../../../../../../atomic-nucleus.md) uses two [neutrons](../../../../../../neutron.md) and two [protons](../../../../../../proton.md), so $n_{\rm He}=n_n/2$. Neglecting binding-energy corrections to nuclear [masses](../../../../../../mass.md),

$$
\boxed{Y_{4\rm He}=\frac{4(n_n/2)}{n_n+n_p}=\frac{2r}{1+r}=\frac29\simeq0.222\quad(r=1/8).}
$$

The remaining [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) mainly become [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md). Counting all four helium nucleons, rather than only the captured [neutrons](../../../../../../neutron.md), is essential to this [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) fraction.
