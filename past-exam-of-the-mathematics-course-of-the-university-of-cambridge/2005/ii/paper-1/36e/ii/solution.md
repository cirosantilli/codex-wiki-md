<h1 id="36e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\pi/2<2\alpha<3\pi/2$, $2/3<\lambda_0<2$ while $2<\lambda_1<6$. Thus **the first harmonic mode dominates the particular $r^2$ term**, and the remaining modes are higher order. Its coefficient is positive. The particular solution alone can be negative here and cannot describe the physical leading flow. Once $2\alpha>\pi$, $\lambda_0<1$ and the leading [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) gradient behaves as $r^{\lambda_0-1}$, singular at the corner although the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) itself tends to zero.
