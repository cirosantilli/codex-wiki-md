<h1 id="36e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $2\alpha<\pi/2$, $\lambda_0>2$ and $\lambda_1>6$. Every harmonic correction decays faster than $r^2$ at the corner, so **the $r^2f(\theta)$ particular solution is the leading corner [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md)**. It is positive inside this acute wedge and the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) gradient tends to zero at the vertex.
