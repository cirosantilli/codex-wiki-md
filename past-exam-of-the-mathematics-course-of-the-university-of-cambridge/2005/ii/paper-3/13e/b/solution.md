<h1 id="13e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If RNA concentration does not fluctuate, protein synthesis is an immigration-death process, so its intrinsic normalized [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is $\boxed{1/m_2}$. If protein birth/death noise is suppressed and protein responds deterministically to the fluctuating RNA input, the remaining normalized [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\eta_{22}^{\rm ext}=\frac{\beta_2}{\beta_1+\beta_2}\eta_{11}.}
$$

Writing $\tau_i=1/\beta_i$, its factor is $\tau_1/(\tau_1+\tau_2)$. Slow environmental changes are followed almost fully; rapid environmental fluctuations are averaged over the longer protein response time. Thus extrinsic noise is the magnitude of relative RNA fluctuations multiplied by a dynamical averaging factor.
