At gross rate $R=5/4$, the conditional up probabilities of the [equivalent martingale measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md) are

$$
\boxed{q_0=\frac{(5/4)15-12}{30-12}=\frac38,\quad
q_u=\frac{(5/4)30-36}{45-36}=\frac16,\quad
q_d=\frac{(5/4)12-10}{16-10}=\frac56.}
$$

All lie strictly between zero and one. The four terminal path probabilities are $1/16,5/16,25/48,5/48$ in the order good-good, good-bad, bad-good, bad-bad.
