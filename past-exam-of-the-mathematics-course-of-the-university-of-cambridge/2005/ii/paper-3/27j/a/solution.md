<h1 id="27j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At the node with stock price thirty, both possible next prices exceed thirty-three, the value of thirty invested at gross rate $11/10$. Take no position at time zero. If the first move is good, borrow thirty at time one and buy one share; if it is bad, do nothing. At time two sell the share for forty-five or thirty-six and repay thirty-three, leaving twelve or three. The strategy has zero initial cost, no possible loss, and positive profit on the good first move, so it is an [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md).
