Here the $L^1$ difference is $\int_n^m x^{-2}dx\leq1/n$, and the squared $L^2$ difference is $\int_n^m x^{-4}dx\leq1/(3n^3)$. Both vanish uniformly for $m>n$, so **the sequence is Cauchy in both spaces**, with limit $x^{-2}$ in each.
