<h1 id="23h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an oriented surface normal $N$ and a unit-speed curve tangent $T$, take signed [geodesic curvature](../../../../../../geodesic-curvature.md) $k_g=(dT/ds)\cdot(T\times N)$. This convention uses boundary orientation with the region on the right; reversing to the usual left-hand boundary convention reverses both orientation and the corresponding curvature convention. With positive orientation consistent this way, the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](../../../../../../gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) for a region $D$ with piecewise smooth boundary is

$$
\boxed{\int_DK\,dA+\int_{\partial D}k_g\,ds+\sum\text{exterior turning angles}=2\pi\chi(D).}
$$

For a disk with smooth boundary there are no corner terms and $\chi(D)=1$. Stating the signed convention is necessary to reproduce the sign in part (ii).
