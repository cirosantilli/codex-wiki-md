<h1 id="23h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $T,n,B$ be the [Frenet frame](../../../../../../frenet-frame.md) of the original space curve, with $T'=kn$, $n'=-kT+\tau B$, $B'=-\tau n$. Put $w=\sqrt{k^2+\tau^2}$. The normal-indicatrix arc-length satisfies $d\bar s/ds=w>0$, and its unit tangent is $\overline T=(-kT+\tau B)/w$. On the unit sphere its outward normal is $n$, and $\overline T\times n=(-kB-\tau T)/w$.

Differentiate $\overline T$ in $s$. The derivative of $1/w$ is parallel to $\overline T$ and contributes nothing to its projection onto $\overline T\times n$. The remaining derivative is $(-k'T+\tau'B-(k^2+\tau^2)n)/w$, giving

$$
k_g=\frac{\tau k'-k\tau'}{w^3}
=\boxed{-\frac{d}{ds}\arctan(\tau/k)\frac{ds}{d\bar s}.}
$$

With the alternative left-normal signed-curvature convention the displayed scalar changes sign, while the area conclusion is unchanged.
