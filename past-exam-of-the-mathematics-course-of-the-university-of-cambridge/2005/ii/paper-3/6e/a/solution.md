<h1 id="6e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The probability of an exact copy is $Q=q^L$. Incorrect copies contribute mutants at rate $a(1-Q)x$, giving $\boxed{\dot y=by+a(1-Q)x}$. For $y>0$, differentiating the ratio gives

$$
\boxed{\dot z=(aQ-b)z-a(1-Q)z^2.}
$$

This is a logistic-type equation for relative concentration; the absolute concentrations need not have a stationary value.
