<h1 id="19g/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $\chi$ of a [representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) of $H\leq K$, the [induced character](../../../../../../induced-character.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Ind}_H^K\chi(g)=\frac1{|H|}\sum_{x\in K\,;\,x^{-1}gx\in H}\chi(x^{-1}gx).}
$$

The sum is zero when no conjugate of $g$ meets $H$.
