<h1 id="15d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The fluid conservation equation is $\dot\rho+3H(\rho+P/c^2)=0$. With the radiation equation of state, it becomes $\dot\rho+4H\rho=0$, giving $\rho a^4=\rho_0$. Define the [critical density](../../../../../../critical-density.md) $\rho_{\rm crit}=3H^2/(8\pi G)$ and [cosmological density parameter](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md) $\Omega=\rho/\rho_{\rm crit}$. Since $a_0=1$,

$$
\boxed{\rho=\frac{3H_0^2\Omega_0}{8\pi G a^4}.}
$$

Evaluating the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) today gives $H_0^2+kc^2=H_0^2\Omega_0$, hence

$$
\boxed{kc^2=H_0^2(\Omega_0-1),\qquad
H^2=H_0^2\Omega_0\left(a^{-4}-\beta a^{-2}\right),
\quad\beta=\frac{\Omega_0-1}{\Omega_0}.}
$$
