<h1 id="10d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let the surface be at $R$ with vanishing external [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md). Multiply [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) by $4\pi r^3$ and integrate:

$$
\int_0^R4\pi r^3P'(r)\,dr
=[4\pi r^3P]_0^R-3\int_0^R4\pi r^2P\,dr
=-3\langle P\rangle V.
$$

The other side is $-\int_0^R4\pi Gm(r)\rho(r)r\,dr=-\int_0^M Gm/r(m)\,dm$. Building the star one shell at a time identifies this as its negative [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../../gravitational-energy.md) $E_{\rm grav}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{3\langle P\rangle V=-E_{\rm grav}.}
$$

If the star has nonzero surface [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) $P_s$, the integration instead gives $3\langle P\rangle V-3P_sV=-E_{\rm grav}$; the requested form assumes $P_s=0$.
