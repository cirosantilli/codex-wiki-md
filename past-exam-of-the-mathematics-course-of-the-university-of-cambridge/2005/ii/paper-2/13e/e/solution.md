<h1 id="13e/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

At the threshold $P_*=3+2\sqrt2$, the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) quadratic has a double zero at

$$
k_*^2=\frac{P_*-1}{2P_*}=P_*^{-1/2}=\sqrt2-1.
$$

Consequently the scaled disturbance length in $\cos(s/L)$ is

$$
\boxed{L_{\rm crit}=P_*^{1/4}=\sqrt{1+\sqrt2}.}
$$

The physical length is $\ell_uL_{\rm crit}=\sqrt{\ell_u\ell_v}$ at threshold; its full spatial [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) is $2\pi$ times this length, not this length itself. For much shorter lengths, the large negative [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) terms smooth both components. For much longer lengths, [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) contributes little and the stable homogeneous reaction feedback, especially the [inhibitor](../../../../../../inhibitor-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md) response, suppresses disturbances. The selected length lies between those two stabilization mechanisms.
