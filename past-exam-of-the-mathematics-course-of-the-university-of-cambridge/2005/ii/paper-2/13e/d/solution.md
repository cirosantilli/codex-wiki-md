<h1 id="13e/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Assume $Q>1$, so $T(k)<0$ for every [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md). A [Turing instability](../../../../../../turing-instability.md) therefore occurs exactly when $D(k)<0$ for some $k$. Put $z=k^2\geq0$. The upward quadratic $1+(1-P)z+Pz^2$ has a negative minimum on this half-line precisely when

$$
P>1,\qquad(P-1)^2>4P.
$$

Combining these gives

$$
\boxed{P>3+2\sqrt2.}
$$

The unstable band is $z_-<k^2<z_+$, where

$$
z_\pm=\frac{P-1\pm\sqrt{(P-1)^2-4P}}{2P}.
$$

The degradation-limited [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) distances are proportional to $\ell_u=\sqrt{D_u/\beta_u}$ and $\ell_v=\sqrt{D_v/\beta_v}$. Thus $P=(\ell_v/\ell_u)^2$ and the criterion says **the [inhibitor](../../../../../../inhibitor-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md) must travel more than $(1+\sqrt2)$ times as far as the [activator](../../../../../../activator-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md) before degradation**. Short-range activation and longer-range inhibition permit a local peak to reinforce itself. On a finite domain the allowed wavenumbers must also intersect the unstable band; the displayed condition assumes continuously available modes.
