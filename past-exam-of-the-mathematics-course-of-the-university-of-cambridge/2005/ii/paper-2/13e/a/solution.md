<h1 id="13e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose the [nondimensionalization](../../../../../../nondimensionalization.md)

$$
\boxed{t=\beta_u\tau,\qquad s=x\sqrt{\beta_u/D_u},\qquad
Q=\frac{\beta_v}{\beta_u},\quad P=\frac{D_v\beta_u}{D_u\beta_v}.}
$$

The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\partial_\tau=\beta_u\partial_t$ and $\partial_x^2=(\beta_u/D_u)\partial_s^2$. Dividing the first equation by $\beta_u$ and the second by $\beta_v$ gives the required scaled [reaction–diffusion system](../../../../../../reaction-diffusion-system.md). Positive degradation and [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) constants are assumed.
