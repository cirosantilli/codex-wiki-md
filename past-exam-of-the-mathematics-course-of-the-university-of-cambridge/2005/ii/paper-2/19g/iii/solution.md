<h1 id="19g/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $A$ is square by [completeness](../../../../../../completeness.md). Column [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) says $A^*A=\operatorname{diag}(|Z(g_j)|)$. Taking determinants gives

$$
\boxed{|\det A|^2=\det(A^*A)=\prod_j|Z(g_j)|.}
$$

In particular its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is nonzero.
