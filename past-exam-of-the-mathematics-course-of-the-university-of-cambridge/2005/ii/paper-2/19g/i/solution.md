<h1 id="19g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The irreducible [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) are an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of the space of complex class [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) for the [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md)

$$
\langle f,h\rangle=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}\overline{f(g)}h(g).
$$

Thus the row [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) relation and [completeness](../../../../../../completeness.md) statement are

$$
\boxed{\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}\overline{\chi_i(g)}\chi_k(g)=\delta_{ik},
\qquad f=\sum_i\langle\chi_i,f\rangle\chi_i.}
$$

In particular the number of irreducible [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) equals the number of conjugacy classes. In class-representative notation, the [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) relation is $\sum_j\overline{\chi_i(g_j)}\chi_k(g_j)/|Z(g_j)|=\delta_{ik}$.
