<h1 id="19g/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Complex conjugation permutes the irreducible [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md), because the conjugate of an [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) is irreducible. This involution fixes the real-valued [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) and interchanges the other [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) in pairs. If there are $r$ such pairs, then conjugating $A$ permutes its rows by $r$ transpositions. Therefore

$$
\overline{\det A}=(-1)^r\det A.
$$

It follows that **the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is real when $r$ is even and purely imaginary when $r$ is odd**. It never vanishes, by the preceding part. Real-valued [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) include both real and [quaternionic representation](../../../../../../pseudoreal-representation.md) types; the criterion concerns values of the [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md), not those two representation types separately.
