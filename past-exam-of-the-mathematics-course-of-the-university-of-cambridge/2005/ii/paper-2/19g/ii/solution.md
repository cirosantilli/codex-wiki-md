<h1 id="19g/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply [completeness](../../../../../../completeness.md) to the indicator $f_j$ of the [conjugacy class](../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) of $g_j$. Its expansion coefficient is $\langle\chi_i,f_j\rangle=\overline{\chi_i(g_j)}/|Z(g_j)|$, because that class has size $|G|/|Z(g_j)|$. Evaluating at $g_k$ gives

$$
\delta_{jk}=\sum_i\frac{\overline{\chi_i(g_j)}\chi_i(g_k)}{|Z(g_j)|},
\qquad\boxed{\sum_i\overline{\chi_i(g_j)}\chi_i(g_k)=\delta_{jk}|Z(g_j)|.}
$$

The complex conjugation is part of the printed formula; dropping it would not give the general column [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) relation.
