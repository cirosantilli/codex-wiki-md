<h1 id="18g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $a=0$, the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $K$ and its [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) is trivial. Otherwise choose a root $\alpha$ and a primitive $n$th root of unity $\zeta\in K$. Every root is $\zeta^j\alpha$, so $L=K(\alpha)$. Each automorphism is determined by $\sigma(\alpha)/\alpha\in\mu_n$. This is an injective group homomorphism, since the [roots of unity](../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) are fixed by every automorphism. A subgroup of the [cyclic group](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $\mu_n$ is cyclic. Thus **$\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ is cyclic**, with order dividing $n$.
