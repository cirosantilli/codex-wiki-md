↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This assertion is **false**. Set $\alpha=1$ and $\beta=\omega_1$. The limit recursion gives

$$
1+\omega_1=\sup_{\gamma<\omega_1}(1+\gamma)=\omega_1.
$$

Every term is [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md), and they are cofinal in the [first uncountable ordinal](../../../../../../first-uncountable-ordinal.md). Yet $\alpha=1$ is neither zero nor $\omega_1$.
