<h1 id="16f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

This assertion is **false**, even for nonzero limit [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal.md). Take $\alpha=\omega$ and $\beta=\omega^2$. Then $\omega+\omega^2=\omega^2$, while $\omega^2+\omega>\omega^2$. The first identity follows from the limit recursion, since $\sup_{n<\omega}(\omega+\omega n)=\omega^2$; the second sum strictly extends the initial block of type $\omega^2$.
