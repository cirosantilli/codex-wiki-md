<h1 id="16f/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

This assertion is **always true**. [Ordinal addition](../../../../../../ordinal-addition.md) obeys $\alpha+\beta\geq\alpha$ and $\alpha+\beta\geq\beta$. Thus both summands are at most $\omega_1$. If $0<\beta<\omega_1$ and $\alpha<\omega_1$, both [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal.md) are [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md), and their disjoint ordered union is [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md), so their sum cannot be $\omega_1$. If instead $\alpha=\omega_1$ and $\beta>0$, appending a nonempty second block gives a sum strictly larger than $\omega_1$. These exhaust the possibilities with $0<\beta<\omega_1$; hence **$\beta=0$ or $\beta=\omega_1$**.
