<h1 id="16f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

This assertion is **always true**. Synthetically, either bracketing places three disjoint blocks of order types $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ in that order. The identity on these blocks is an [order isomorphism](../../../../../../order-isomorphism.md), so both sums have the same [order type](../../../../../../order-type.md). This proves [associativity of ordinal addition](../../../../../../associativity-of-ordinal-addition.md) for successor and limit [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal.md) alike.
