<h1 id="11f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md) says that in a nonempty complete [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md), a [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) intersection of open dense subsets is dense. Equivalently, such a space cannot be a [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) union of [nowhere dense sets](../../../../../../nowhere-dense-set.md): a set is [nowhere dense](../../../../../../nowhere-dense-set.md) when the interior of its closure is empty. Indeed the complements of those closures are open dense; their intersection is dense and in particular nonempty, so a [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) union of the original sets cannot exhaust the space. The same argument shows that the complement of such a union is dense.
