<h1 id="5e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Integrating $\psi_y=x+\sin t$ and imposing $-\psi_x=-y$ gives the [streamfunction](../../../../../../stream-function.md)

$$
\boxed{\psi(x,y,t)=y(x+\sin t)}
$$

up to an arbitrary function of time. At the specified time, the [streamlines](../../../../../../streamline.md) are

$$
\boxed{y(x+1)=C}.
$$

For $C\ne0$, the connected branches are rectangular hyperbolas. The zero level consists of the lines $y=0$ and $x=-1$, with the [stagnation point](../../../../../../stagnation-point.md) $(-1,0)$ at their intersection; their half-lines and the stationary point are the distinct [streamlines](../../../../../../streamline.md).
