↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [least-squares estimator](../../../../../../ordinary-least-squares-estimators.md) satisfies

$$
\widehat\beta=\beta+\frac{\sum_i x_i\epsilon_i}{S_{xx}}.
$$

A linear combination of independent [normal random variables](../../../../../../gaussian-random-variable.md) is normal; its mean is $\beta$ and its variance is $\sigma^2\sum_i x_i^2/S_{xx}^2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\widehat\beta\sim N\left(\beta,\frac{\sigma^2}{S_{xx}}\right).}
$$
