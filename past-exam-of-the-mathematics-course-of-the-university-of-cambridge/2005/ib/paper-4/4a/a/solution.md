<h1 id="4a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Where both derivatives exist, the ordinary product rule gives

$$
\frac{(\gamma_1\gamma_2)'}{\gamma_1\gamma_2}=\frac{\gamma_1'}{\gamma_1}+\frac{\gamma_2'}{\gamma_2}.
$$

Neither denominator vanishes. Integrate over a common piecewise-smooth subdivision and use the contour characterization of the [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md):

$$
\boxed{n(\gamma_1\gamma_2,0)=n(\gamma_1,0)+n(\gamma_2,0).}
$$
