<h1 id="4a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On the right half-plane a single-valued [holomorphic logarithm](../../../../../../holomorphic-logarithm.md) is $\operatorname{Log}z=\log|z|+i\operatorname{Arg}z$, with $-\pi/2<\operatorname{Arg}z<\pi/2$, and its derivative is $1/z$. Thus

$$
\oint_\eta\frac{dz}{z}=\operatorname{Log}\eta(1)-\operatorname{Log}\eta(0)=0,
\qquad \boxed{n(\eta,0)=0.}
$$

This proves the conclusion directly from the integral definition of the [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md), without appealing to its homotopy invariance.
