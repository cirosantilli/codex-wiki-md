<h1 id="3b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the nonzero $x$ axis the function equals one, whereas its assigned value at the origin is zero. Therefore $f(x,0)$ does not tend to $f(0,0)$ as $x\to0$. It is not a [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) there, and [differentiability implies continuity](../../../../../../differentiability-implies-continuity.md) shows

$$
\boxed{f\text{ is not differentiable at }(0,0).}
$$
