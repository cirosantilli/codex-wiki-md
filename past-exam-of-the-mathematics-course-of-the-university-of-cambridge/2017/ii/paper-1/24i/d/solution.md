<h1 id="24i/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [semicubical parabola](../../../../../../semicubical-parabola.md) has [coordinate ring](../../../../../../coordinate-ring.md)

$$
A=k[x,y]/(x^2-y^3)\cong k[t^3,t^2]\subset k[t],
$$

under $x\mapsto t^3$, $y\mapsto t^2$. The isomorphism is valid in every characteristic: after division by the monic [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $x$, a remainder $P(y)+xQ(y)$ maps to a sum of distinct even powers and odd powers at least three, and can vanish only when both [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) are zero.

Its [fraction field](../../../../../../field-of-fractions.md) contains $t=t^3/t^2$, which is an [integral element](../../../../../../integral-element.md) over $A$ because it satisfies $z^2-y=0$. However $t\notin A$, since a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $t^2,t^3$ has no term of degree one. Thus $A$ is not an [integrally closed domain](../../../../../../integrally-closed-domain.md). The [ring](../../../../../../ring.md) $k[t]$ of the affine line is a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) and therefore an [integrally closed domain](../../../../../../integrally-closed-domain.md). This property is preserved by [ring isomorphism](../../../../../../ring-isomorphism.md). By coordinate-ring functoriality,

$$
\boxed{Z(x^2-y^3)\not\cong\mathbb A^1}.
$$

The parametrization is a bijection on points over an algebraically closed [field](../../../../../../field.md), but that does not make its inverse a morphism at the cusp.
