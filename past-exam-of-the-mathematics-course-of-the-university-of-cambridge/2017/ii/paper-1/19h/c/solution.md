<h1 id="19h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose an [integral basis](../../../../../../integral-basis.md) $u_1,\ldots,u_d$ of the nonzero [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) $\mathfrak a$. Its rational span is $L$, since it contains a nonzero integer multiple of $\mathcal O_L$. The condition $x\mathfrak a\subseteq\mathfrak a$ gives integers $m_{ij}$ with $xu_j=\sum_i m_{ij}u_i$. Thus multiplication by $x$ on $L$ has an integer [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $M$ in this basis.

Its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) $P(t)=\det(tI-M)$ is monic with integer coefficients. By the [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](../../../../../../cayley-hamilton-theorem.md), $P(M)=0$. On the [field](../../../../../../field.md) $L$, this is multiplication by $P(x)$, so $P(x)=0$. Hence $x$ is an [algebraic integer](../../../../../../algebraic-integer.md), and

$$
\boxed{x\in\mathcal O_L}.
$$

This is the [determinant trick](../../../../../../determinant-trick.md) for an [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) preserved by multiplication.
