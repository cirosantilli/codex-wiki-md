<h1 id="19h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a nonzero $\alpha\in\mathfrak a$. It is an [algebraic integer](../../../../../../algebraic-integer.md), with monic [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md)

$$
\alpha^d+c_{d-1}\alpha^{d-1}+\cdots+c_1\alpha+c_0=0,
\qquad c_j\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Its constant coefficient is nonzero, since otherwise the [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) would have the root zero and be divisible by its variable, contradicting irreducibility and $\alpha\ne0$. Rearranging gives $c_0=-\alpha(\alpha^{d-1}+\cdots+c_1)$. The expression in parentheses lies in the [ring of integers of a number field](../../../../../../ring-of-integers.md), so the [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) property gives $c_0\in\mathfrak a$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak a\cap\mathbb Z\ne\{0\}}.
$$
