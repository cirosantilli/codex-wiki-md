The [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$ is the smallest [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) containing all open subsets of $\mathbb R$. A real-valued [Borel measurable function](../../../../../../borel-measurable-function.md) on $(E,\mathcal E)$ is a map $f$ with $f^{-1}(B)\in\mathcal E$ for every $B\in\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$. It suffices to test the [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md) $(a,\infty)$ for rational $a$, because these generate the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md).

Here “Borel [function](../../../../../../function-split.md)” describes measurability into the Borel structure of the target; the domain need not itself be a topological space.
