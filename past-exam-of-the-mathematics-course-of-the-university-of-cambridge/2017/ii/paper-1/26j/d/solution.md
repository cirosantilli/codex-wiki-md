<h1 id="26j/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Each [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $(x,y)\mapsto|R_n(x)-R_n(y)|$ is Borel measurable on the product square, by composition with its coordinate projections and arithmetic of measurable real [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md). Its value is one exactly when the $n$th digits disagree. Therefore the partial disagreement counts

$$
f_N(x,y)=\sum_{n=1}^N|R_n(x)-R_n(y)|
$$

are nonnegative Borel [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) and increase to the extended-real [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $f$. For every finite $a$, $\{f>a\}=\bigcup_N\{f_N>a\}$ is Borel. Hence

$$
\boxed{f:[0,1)^2\to[0,\infty]\text{ is nonnegative measurable}}.
$$

Infinite disagreement is allowed, so restricting the target to finite real values would lose part of the stated [function](../../../../../../function-split.md).
