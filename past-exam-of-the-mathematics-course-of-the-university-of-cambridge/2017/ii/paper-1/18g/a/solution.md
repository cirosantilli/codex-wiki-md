<h1 id="18g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In an irreducible complex [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) $\rho$, every $\rho(z)$ for $z$ in the [center of a group](../../../../../../center-of-a-group.md) commutes with the entire representation. [Schur lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) therefore gives $\rho(z)=\lambda_z I$. Faithfulness makes $z\mapsto\lambda_z$ an injective homomorphism $Z(G)\to\mathbb C^\times$.

Its image is a finite subgroup. If its order is $m$, [Lagrange's theorem for finite groups](../../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) shows that every element is an $m$th root of unity. These roots form a [cyclic group](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md), and every subgroup of a [cyclic group](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) is cyclic. Hence

$$
\boxed{Z(G)\text{ is cyclic}}.
$$

Both irreducibility over the algebraically closed [field](../../../../../../field.md) and faithfulness are used: scalar action alone would not embed the centre.
