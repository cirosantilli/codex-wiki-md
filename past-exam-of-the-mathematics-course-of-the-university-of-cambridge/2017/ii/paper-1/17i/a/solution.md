<h1 id="17i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) of a nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $f$ over $K$ is an extension in which $f$ splits into linear factors and which is generated over $K$ by all its roots. For a constant nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $K$; the zero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) does not have a root-generated [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) in this standard definition.

Given two [splitting fields](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) $L,L'$, enumerate the roots generating $L$ and extend a $K$-embedding one root at a time. If an embedding is defined on $K(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_j)$, the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) of the next root divides $f$ over that [field](../../../../../../field.md). Its image under the embedding therefore divides $f$ over the corresponding subfield of $L'$, and has a root in $L'$. The quotient construction $K(\alpha)\cong K[t]/(m_\alpha)$ extends the embedding to that root. The [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) need not be a [separable polynomial](../../../../../../separable-polynomial.md).

At the end this gives a $K$-embedding $L\to L'$. It sends the distinct roots of $f$ injectively to its roots, hence permutes the finite [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) of distinct roots and contains every root in its image. Since these generate $L'$, the embedding is onto. Thus **the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is unique up to a (K)-isomorphism**.
