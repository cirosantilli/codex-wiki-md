<h1 id="17i/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The quartic is irreducible by [Eisenstein criterion](../../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at two, so its [Galois group](../../../../../../../galois-group.md) is transitive on its four roots. Its [discriminant](../../../../../../../discriminant.md) is $1616=16\cdot101$, so five and seven are unramified primes for the [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md). The squarefree factorizations are

$$
f(t)\equiv(t-1)(t^3+t^2+t-2)\pmod5,
\qquad
f(t)\equiv(t+2)(t+3)(t^2+2t-2)\pmod7.
$$

The cubic modulo five has no root in $\mathbb F_5$, hence is irreducible. The quadratic modulo seven has [discriminant](../../../../../../../discriminant.md) five, a nonsquare there. The [Frobenius cycle type](../../../../../../../frobenius-cycle-type.md) theorem therefore supplies a three-cycle and a transposition in the [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md).

Transitivity implies that its order is divisible by four, and the three-cycle implies divisibility by three. Its order divides $24$, so it is $12$ or $24$. The only subgroup of index two in $S_4$ is $A_4$: an index-two subgroup is the [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) of a nontrivial homomorphism to $C_2$, and all transpositions are conjugate and generate $S_4$, forcing this homomorphism to be the sign. The exhibited transposition excludes $A_4$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(t^4+2t+2/\mathbb Q)\cong S_4}.
$$
