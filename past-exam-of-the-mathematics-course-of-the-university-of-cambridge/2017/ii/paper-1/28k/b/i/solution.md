↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

for an estimator $\delta(X)$ and loss $L$, its [risk function](../../../../../../../risk-function.md) is $R(p,\delta)=\mathbb E_p L(\delta(X),p)$, where [expectation](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) is over repeated data at fixed $p$. Its [Bayes risk](../../../../../../../bayes-risk.md) under prior $\pi$ is $r(\pi,\delta)=\int R(p,\delta)\,\pi(dp)$, equivalently the joint [expectation](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) under the prior and sampling model.
