<h1 id="20i/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

View the [torus](../../../../../../torus.md) as the topological [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) $S^1\times S^1$ under coordinatewise multiplication, and let $x_0$ be the common basepoint. From the given free [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) construct

$$
\boxed{K(s,z)=H(s,z)\,H(s,1)^{-1}x_0}.
$$

This is continuous, has $K(s,1)=x_0$, and at $s=0,1$ agrees with $\alpha,\beta$ because $H(0,1)=H(1,1)=x_0$. Thus it exhibits the requested [based homotopy](../../../../../../based-homotopy.md) explicitly.

Algebraically $\pi_1(S^1\times S^1)\cong\mathbb Z^2$ is [Abelian](../../../../../../abelian-group.md), so conjugate elements are equal. Free and [based homotopy](../../../../../../based-homotopy.md) classes of loops with a specified common basepoint therefore coincide here.
