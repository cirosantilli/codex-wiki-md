<h1 id="20i/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $a,b$ be the two oriented circle generators of the [fundamental group of a bouquet of circles](../../../../../../fundamental-group-of-a-bouquet-of-circles.md), $\pi_1(S^1\vee S^1)\cong F(a,b)$. Take

$$
\boxed{[\alpha]=a,\qquad[\beta]=b^{-1}ab}.
$$

Their loops are freely homotopic: slide the basepoint of the $a$ loop along the path $b$, thereby introducing the route $b^{-1}$ to the original loop and $b$ back. Equivalently the change-of-basepoint construction along a loop realizes conjugation, and the retraced connecting portions can be contracted with a moving marked point.

They are not based homotopic, since their [reduced words](../../../../../../reduced-word-in-a-free-group.md) in the [free group](../../../../../../free-group.md) are distinct: $a$ has length one and $b^{-1}ab$ has length three with no cancellation. This illustrates that free [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) remembers conjugacy classes, whereas [based homotopy](../../../../../../based-homotopy.md) remembers actual elements of the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md).
