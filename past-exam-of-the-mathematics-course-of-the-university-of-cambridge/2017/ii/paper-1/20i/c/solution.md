<h1 id="20i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $H(s,z)$ be the [free homotopy](../../../../../../free-homotopy.md) from $\alpha$ to $\beta$ and put $v(s)=H(s,1)$. Because both endpoint loops are based at $x_0$, this is itself a loop based at $x_0$.

Cut the circle parameter at 1 and regard $H$ as a map on a square. Traversing its boundary gives the concatenation $\alpha*v*\bar\beta*\bar v$. The boundary is null-homotopic since the map extends over the square. Thus, in the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md),

$$
[\alpha][v][\beta]^{-1}[v]^{-1}=1,
\qquad \boxed{[\beta]=[v]^{-1}[\alpha][v]}.
$$

Hence the corresponding elements are [conjugate group elements](../../../../../../conjugate-group-elements.md). The moving basepoint path is precisely what distinguishes free [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) from [based homotopy](../../../../../../based-homotopy.md).
