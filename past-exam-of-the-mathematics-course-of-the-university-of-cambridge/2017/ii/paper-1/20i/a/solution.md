<h1 id="20i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use path concatenation in the order of traversal and let $\bar u(t)=u(1-t)$. A loop $\alpha$ based at $x_0$ determines the loop $\bar u*\alpha*u$ based at $x_1$. Thus the [change of basepoint isomorphism](../../../../../../change-of-basepoint-isomorphism.md) is

$$
\boxed{u_\#([\alpha])=[\bar u*\alpha*u]}.
$$

A [based homotopy](../../../../../../based-homotopy.md) of $\alpha$ can be concatenated with the fixed outer paths to give a [based homotopy](../../../../../../based-homotopy.md) of the resulting loops, so this map is well-defined on the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md). Different parenthesizations and affine reparametrizations of path concatenation are based homotopic.
