<h1 id="16h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $A$ be the [adjacency matrix of a graph](../../../../../../adjacency-matrix.md) and $Av=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$. Choose a [vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) $i$ maximizing $|v_i|>0$. Then

$$
|\lambda||v_i|=\left|\sum_{j\sim i}v_j\right|\leq\deg(i)|v_i|\leq\Delta|v_i|.
$$

Dividing gives

$$
\boxed{|\lambda|\leq\Delta.}
$$

This argument works even with a complex [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md); for a finite undirected [graph](../../../../../../graph-split.md) the matrix is real symmetric and all [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are real.
