<h1 id="16h/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If a [regular graph](../../../../../../regular-graph.md) has $c>1$ connected components, the [indicator vector](../../../../../../indicator-vector.md) of each component is a $\Delta$-[eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md). They are [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md), and the Laplacian argument shows that every such [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) is constant on each component. Therefore its [multiplicity](../../../../../../multiplicity-mathematics.md) is exactly $c$, in particular greater than one.
