<h1 id="9c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a thin spherical shell, the radial [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) force per unit [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) is $-dP/dr$. By the [shell theorem](../../../../../../spherical-shell-theorem.md), the inward [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md) is $GM(r)/r^2$, because exterior spherical shells contribute no force. Balancing force densities in [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{dP}{dr}=-\frac{GM(r)\rho(r)}{r^2},\qquad\frac{dM}{dr}=4\pi r^2\rho(r).}
$$

The second equation is [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md); it closes the pressure-support equation once an [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) is supplied.
