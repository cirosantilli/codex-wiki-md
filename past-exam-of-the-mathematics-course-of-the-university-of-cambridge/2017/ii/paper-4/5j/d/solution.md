↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The count weights correct the loss of frequencies for a deliberately coarsened binary response, but do not supply a third outcome category. Thus **this remains inappropriate for the full [contingency table](../../../../../../contingency-table.md)**. With the base R factor convention, it could fit the chosen first damage level versus the other two combined, using a [logistic regression](../../../../../../logistic-regression.md) and the frequencies as weights. That is a different scientific question. The supplied [log-linear model](../../../../../../log-linear-model.md) retains all three categories and their counts without this information loss.
