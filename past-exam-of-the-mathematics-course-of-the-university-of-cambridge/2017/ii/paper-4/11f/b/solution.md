<h1 id="11f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For each [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) $m$, take $\gamma_m(t)=e^{2\pi imt}$. It is a continuous closed curve avoiding zero and has the continuous argument $2\pi mt$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{w(\gamma_m)=m,\qquad m\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Positive and negative [integers](../../../../../../integer.md) correspond to opposite senses of traversal, and the constant curve gives zero.
