<h1 id="27k/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The pivot $Z=\sqrt n\,\Sigma^{-1/2}(\overline X-\theta)$ has independent standard [normal coordinates](../../../../../../normal-coordinates.md), so $Z^TZ$ has the [chi-squared distribution](../../../../../../chi-squared-distribution.md) with $d$ [degrees of freedom](../../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md). Let $q_{d,1-\alpha}$ be its $(1-\alpha)$ quantile. The [confidence ellipsoid](../../../../../../confidence-ellipsoid.md)

$$
\boxed{C_n^\alpha=\left\{v\in\mathbb R^d:n(\overline X-v)^T\Sigma^{-1}(\overline X-v)\leq q_{d,1-\alpha}\right\}}
$$

therefore has exactly $1-\alpha$ coverage for every $\theta$. No large-sample approximation is required when the covariance is known.
