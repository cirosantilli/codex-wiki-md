<h1 id="27k/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With $\Sigma=I_d$, constrained likelihood maximization minimizes $\|\theta-\overline X\|^2$. On the [unit sphere](../../../../../../../unit-sphere.md) this is $1+\|\overline X\|^2-2\theta^T\overline X$. By the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) its minimizer is

$$
\boxed{\widehat\theta=\frac{\overline X}{\|\overline X\|}\quad\text{if }\overline X\ne0.}
$$

If $\overline X=0$, every [unit vector](../../../../../../../unit-vector.md) is a [maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md); a specified measurable tie-breaking choice makes an estimator defined everywhere. This event has probability zero in the nonsingular normal model, but the optimization still has that exceptional case.
