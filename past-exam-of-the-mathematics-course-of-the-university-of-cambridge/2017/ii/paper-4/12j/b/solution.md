<h1 id="12j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two design matrices span the same six-dimensional space of arbitrary log means for the six periods. Fit.2 uses the first period as a baseline, whereas fit.3 uses cumulative indicator steps. If $a_j$ is the log mean for period $j$, fit.3 has intercept $a_1$ and jump coefficients $a_{j+1}-a_j$. Thus both fits give identical fitted means and identical maximized [likelihood functions](../../../../../../likelihood-function.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{D(\text{fit.2})-D(\text{fit.3})=0.}
$$

Different coefficient tests arise because they test different contrasts, not because one full fit explains the observations better.
