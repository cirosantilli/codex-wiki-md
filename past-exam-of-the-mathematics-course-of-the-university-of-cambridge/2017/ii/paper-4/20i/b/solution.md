<h1 id="20i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Triangulate the [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) by the boundary complex of the [cross-polytope](../../../../../../cross-polytope.md) with [simplicial vertices](../../../../../../vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) $\pm e_0,\ldots,\pm e_n$: a face contains at most one [simplicial vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) from each antipodal pair. Take its [barycentric subdivision](../../../../../../barycentric-subdivision.md). Its [simplicial vertices](../../../../../../vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) are the nonempty faces $F$ of the original complex, and its simplices are strictly nested chains $F_0\subset\cdots\subset F_k$.

Form a quotient complex whose [simplicial vertices](../../../../../../vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) are pairs $\{F,-F\}$, and whose simplices are the images of these chains. No chain contains both $F$ and $-F$. Moreover a chain of face-orbits lifts uniquely up to simultaneous negation: after choosing a largest face, every smaller face has at most one representative contained in it, because an original face contains no antipodal [simplicial vertices](../../../../../../vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md). Thus the quotient is an honest [simplicial complex](../../../../../../simplicial-complex.md), without two different simplices with the same [simplicial vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) set. Its realization is homeomorphic to the [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) modulo its antipodal action. This provides **a triangulation of [Real projective space](../../../../../../real-projective-space.md) $\mathbb{RP}^n$**. The subdivision avoids the duplicate-simplex problem of naively identifying [simplicial vertices](../../../../../../vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) in the unsubdivided cross-polytope boundary.
