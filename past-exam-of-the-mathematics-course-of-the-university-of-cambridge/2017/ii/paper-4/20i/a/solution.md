<h1 id="20i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $n=2m-1$. On $\mathbb R^{2m}$ define the orthogonal [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md)

$$
J(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_{2m-1},x_{2m})=(-x_2,x_1,\ldots,-x_{2m},x_{2m-1}).
$$

It satisfies $J^T=-J$ and $J^TJ=I$. Hence $\langle x,Jx\rangle=0$ and $\|Jx\|=\|x\|$. Restricting to the [unit sphere](../../../../../../unit-sphere.md) gives

$$
\boxed{f(x)=Jx\in S^{2m-1},\qquad f(x)\perp x.}
$$

This continuous unit tangent field is available exactly because the ambient dimension is even.
