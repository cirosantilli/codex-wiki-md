<h1 id="18g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In $V_n\otimes V_n$, interchange of the two factors commutes with SU2. On the [highest-weight vector](../../../../../../highest-weight-vector.md) $D^r x_1^{n-r}x_2^{n-r}$ it acts by $(-1)^r$. Each irreducible summand in the [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition](../../../../../../clebsch-gordan-decomposition.md) has [multiplicity](../../../../../../multiplicity-mathematics.md) one, so this sign is its scalar action on the entire summand. The [symmetric square](../../../../../../symmetric-square.md) is the $+1$ [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) and the [exterior square](../../../../../../exterior-square.md) the $-1$ [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{S^2V_n\cong\bigoplus_{j=0}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}V_{2n-4j},\qquad
\Lambda^2V_n\cong\bigoplus_{j=0}^{\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor}V_{2n-2-4j}.}
$$

For instance, $S^2V_1=V_2$ and $\Lambda^2V_1=V_0$. The dimensions agree respectively with $(n+1)(n+2)/2$ and $n(n+1)/2$.
