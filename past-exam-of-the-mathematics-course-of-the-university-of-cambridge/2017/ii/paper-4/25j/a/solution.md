<h1 id="25j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a measurable map, each preimage $\theta^{-1}(A)$ lies in $\mathcal E$, so $\mu_\theta$ is well-defined and nonnegative. The preimage of the [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) is empty, and preimages commute with disjoint countable [unions](../../../../../../set-union.md), giving

$$
\mu_\theta\!\left(\bigcup_{j=1}^\infty A_j\right)=\mu\!\left(\bigcup_{j=1}^\infty\theta^{-1}(A_j)\right)=\sum_{j=1}^\infty\mu_\theta(A_j)
$$

for pairwise disjoint measurable $A_j$. Thus **$\mu_\theta$ is a [measure](../../../../../../measure.md)**, the [pushforward measure](../../../../../../pushforward-measure.md) $\theta_*\mu$. Its total mass is $\mu(E)$, because $\theta^{-1}(E)=E$. Finiteness is not needed for this construction, but is used in the uniqueness argument next.
