↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Consider $A=\{x:\omega_1=\omega_2\}=[0,1/4)\cup[3/4,1)$. The first digit pair is swapped by the transformation, so the equality of its digits is unchanged: $\theta^{-1}(A)=A$. Yet $\mu(A)=1/2$. An [ergodic measure-preserving transformation](../../../../../../../ergodicity.md) has only invariant events of [measure](../../../../../../../measure.md) zero or one. Thus

$$
\boxed{\theta\text{ is not ergodic}.}
$$

Its order-two property also explains why averaging along orbits cannot in general sample the entire interval.
