<h1 id="34d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Substitute $v=3b\bar v$, $T=(8a/(27bk))\bar T$, and $p=(a/(27b^2))\bar p$ into the [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md). After cancellation of the material parameters,

$$
\boxed{\bar p=\frac{8\bar T}{3\bar v-1}-\frac3{\bar v^2}.}
$$

This is the [Van der Waals law of corresponding states](../../../../../../van-der-waals-law-of-corresponding-states.md): fluids described by this model share the same reduced equation, although real fluids need not obey it exactly.
