<h1 id="34d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a simple homogeneous thermodynamic system, [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy.md) is extensive: $E(\lambda S,\lambda V,\lambda N)=\lambda E(S,V,N)$. [Euler theorem for homogeneous functions](../../../../../../euler-theorem-for-homogeneous-functions.md) and $dE=T\,dS-p\,dV+\mu\,dN$ give $E=TS-pV+\mu N$, hence

$$
\boxed{G=E+pV-TS=\mu N.}
$$

Also $dG=-S\,dT+V\,dp+\mu\,dN$, so at fixed $T,N$,

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial\mu}{\partial p}\right)_{T,N}=\frac VN=v.}
$$

At coexistence gas and liquid have the same [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md), [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) and [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md). Integrating $d\mu=v\,dp$ along the intervening isotherm gives $\int v\,dp=0$. [Integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), with endpoints at the same coexistence [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) $p_*$, gives the [Maxwell construction](../../../../../../maxwell-construction.md)

$$
\boxed{\int_{v_l}^{v_g}[p(v,T)-p_*]\,dv=0.}
$$

The horizontal line at $p_*$ replaces the mechanically unstable loop of the van der Waals isotherm. The extensivity argument presupposes the ordinary [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md) and neglects macroscopic interface contributions.
