<h1 id="33c/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**An even potential $V(x)=V(-x)$ preserves [parity](../../../../../../../parity.md).** Its [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) commutes with the [parity](../../../../../../../parity.md) operator, so an even incoming solution produces only an even outgoing solution, and similarly for an odd one. Thus

$$
\boxed{S_{+-}=S_{-+}=0.}
$$

An asymmetric potential generically couples the [parity](../../../../../../../parity.md) channels; an accidental vanishing at one energy does not imply the symmetry for all energies.
