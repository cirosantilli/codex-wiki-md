<h1 id="4h/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fix an effective numbering of [Turing machines](../../../../../../../turing-machine.md) and use the [diagonal halting set](../../../../../../../diagonal-halting-set.md)

$$
H=\{e:M_e(e)\text{ halts}\}.
$$

It is [computably enumerable](../../../../../../../recursively-enumerable-set.md): dovetail all the computations and output each index when its computation halts. It is not a [recursive set](../../../../../../../computable-set.md). Otherwise a machine could, on input $e$, loop if the alleged decider says $e\in H$ and halt otherwise. Applying this machine to its own index contradicts either answer. Hence **$H$ is [computably enumerable](../../../../../../../recursively-enumerable-set.md) but not a [recursive set](../../../../../../../computable-set.md)**.
