<h1 id="4h/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use $L=\{a^nb^n:n\geq0\}$. The [context-free grammar](../../../../../../../context-free-grammar.md) $S\to aSb\mid\varepsilon$ generates exactly $L$, so it is a [context-free language](../../../../../../../context-free-language.md). It is not a [regular language](../../../../../../../regular-language.md): if $p$ is a pumping length, any allowed decomposition of $a^pb^p$ has a nonempty pumped segment consisting only of $a$'s. Pumping down leaves fewer $a$'s than $b$'s, contradicting the [pumping lemma for regular languages](../../../../../../../pumping-lemma-for-regular-languages.md). Thus **$L$ is a [context-free language](../../../../../../../context-free-language.md) but not a [regular language](../../../../../../../regular-language.md)**.
