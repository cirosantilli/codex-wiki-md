One form of the [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md) is: **a nonempty [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) cannot be a countable union of closed nowhere dense subsets**. Equivalently, a countable intersection of open dense subsets is dense. A [Banach space](../../../../../../banach-space-split.md) is a [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) for its norm metric, so this form applies directly to the next part.
