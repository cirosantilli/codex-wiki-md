↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** The valid [distributive law for ordinal multiplication](../../../../../../distributive-law-for-ordinal-multiplication.md) is

$$
\boxed{\alpha(\beta+\gamma)=\alpha\beta+\alpha\gamma.}
$$

The synthetic definition of [ordinal multiplication](../../../../../../ordinal-multiplication.md) takes a sequence of copies of the left factor, indexed by the right factor. A sequence indexed first by $\beta$ and then by $\gamma$ is exactly the concatenation of the two corresponding sequences. Taking [order types](../../../../../../order-type.md) gives the identity. Equivalently, use [transfinite induction](../../../../../../transfinite-induction.md) on $\gamma$, with the successor rule and the supremum rule at [limit ordinals](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md).
