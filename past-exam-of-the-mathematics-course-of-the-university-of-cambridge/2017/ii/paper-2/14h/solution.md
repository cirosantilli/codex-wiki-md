For [ordinals](../../../../../ordinal.md), the inductive definition of [ordinal addition](../../../../../ordinal-addition.md) is

$$
\alpha+0=\alpha,\quad\alpha+(\beta+1)=(\alpha+\beta)+1,\quad
\alpha+\lambda=\sup_{\beta<\lambda}(\alpha+\beta)\quad(\lambda\text{ a nonzero limit ordinal}).
$$

The synthetic definition takes a [well-order](../../../../../well-order.md) of type $\alpha$, followed by a disjoint [well-order](../../../../../well-order.md) of type $\beta$, with every point of the first preceding the second. They agree by [transfinite induction](../../../../../transfinite-induction.md) on $\beta$: the empty second order does nothing; adding its last point takes the successor; at a [limit ordinal](../../../../../limit-ordinal.md), the concatenated order is the union of its initial concatenations, whose types have the displayed supremum.
