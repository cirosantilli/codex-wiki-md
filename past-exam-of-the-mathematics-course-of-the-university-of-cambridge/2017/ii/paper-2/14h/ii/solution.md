**False.** Put $\alpha=\beta=1$ and $\gamma=\omega$. Then

$$
\boxed{(\alpha+\beta)\gamma=2\omega=\omega,\qquad\alpha\gamma+\beta\gamma=\omega+\omega=\omega\,2.}
$$

The two [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal.md) differ. The order of the factors matters in the [distributive law for ordinal multiplication](../../../../../../distributive-law-for-ordinal-multiplication.md).
