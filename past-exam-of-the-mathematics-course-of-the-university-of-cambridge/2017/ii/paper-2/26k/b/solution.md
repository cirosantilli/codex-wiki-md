↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The observations have finite mean and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md). The [strong law of large numbers](../../../../../../strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) gives $\overline X_n\to\mathbb EX_1$ almost surely, so $\widetilde\theta_n\to\theta$ almost surely and therefore is a [consistent estimator](../../../../../../consistency-statistics.md). The [central limit theorem](../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\sqrt n(\widetilde\theta_n-\theta)\ \xrightarrow{d}\ N\left(0,\frac{1-\alpha}{(3-\alpha)(2-\alpha)^2}\right).}
$$
