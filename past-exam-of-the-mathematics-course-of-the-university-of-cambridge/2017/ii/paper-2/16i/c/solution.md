<h1 id="16i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In characteristic $p$, let $K=\mathbb F_p(s,t)$ and $L=\mathbb F_p(s^{1/p},t^{1/p})$, with $s,t$ having [algebraic independence](../../../../../../algebraic-independence.md). The monomials $s^{i/p}t^{j/p}$, $0\leq i,j<p$, form a $K$-basis, so $[L:K]=p^2$. Their independence follows by grouping [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) monomials according to their exponents modulo $p$, after clearing rational-function denominators.

Every $\alpha\in L$ has $\alpha^p\in K$, so its [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) divides $T^p-\alpha^p$ and $[K(\alpha):K]\leq p$. Thus **this finite purely inseparable extension has no primitive element**. It demonstrates why the [separable field extension](../../../../../../separable-extension.md) hypothesis cannot simply be removed.
