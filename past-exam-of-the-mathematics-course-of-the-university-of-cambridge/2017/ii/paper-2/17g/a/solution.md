<h1 id="17g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $g$ have order $n$ in a finite group. In a complex [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md), its [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is diagonalizable with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\zeta_j$ satisfying $\zeta_j^n=1$. If each $g^i$ with $\gcd(i,n)=1$ is conjugate to $g$, the [character of a representation](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is constant on these powers. Hence

$$
\varphi(n)\chi(g)=\sum_j\sum_{\substack{1\leq i\leq n\\(i,n)=1}}\zeta_j^i\in\mathbb Z,
$$

using the stated integrality of each [Ramanujan sum](../../../../../../ramanujan-sum.md). Therefore $\chi(g)$ is rational. It is also an [algebraic integer](../../../../../../algebraic-integer.md), being a sum of [roots of unity](../../../../../../root-of-unity.md); a rational [algebraic integer](../../../../../../algebraic-integer.md) is an [integer](../../../../../../integer.md). Thus $\boxed{\chi(g)\in\mathbb Z}$.

In a [symmetric group](../../../../../../symmetric-group.md), raising a [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) to a power coprime to its order preserves each cycle length and therefore its entire cycle type. Since cycle type determines the [conjugacy class](../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md), the hypothesis holds, and **all character values of [symmetric groups](../../../../../../symmetric-group.md) are [integers](../../../../../../integer.md)**.
