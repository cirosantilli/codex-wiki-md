<h1 id="30a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Poincaré-Bendixson theorem](../../../../../../poincare-bendixson-theorem.md) says that a nonempty [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) omega-limit set of a smooth planar flow containing no equilibrium is a [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md); use its allowed cylinder version here. For $F>1$, choose $p_-=(F-1)/(2k)$ and $p_+=2(F+1)/k$. At the lower boundary $\dot p>0$ and at the upper boundary $\dot p<0$, so the [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) annulus $p_-\leq p\leq p_+$ is positively invariant. It has no equilibrium because $F-\sin\theta>0$. The theorem supplies a [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md), and $\dot\theta=p>0$ makes it encircle the cylinder.

Every [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) must lie in $p>0$: for $p\leq0$, $\dot p>0$, and a crossing of $p=0$ can only be upward. In $p>0$ a [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) is a [graph of a function](../../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) satisfying

$$
\frac{dp}{d\theta}=\frac{F-\sin\theta}{p}-k.
$$

Two distinct [function graphs](../../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) cannot cross by uniqueness of this scalar differential equation. If $p_1>p_2$, their difference obeys

$$
(p_1-p_2)'=-\frac{F-\sin\theta}{p_1p_2}(p_1-p_2).
$$

It contracts by a factor strictly less than $1$ during one circuit, contradicting periodicity of both [function graphs](../../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md). The scalar return map is order preserving, so a multi-circuit periodic [graph of a function](../../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) also cannot have a period larger than one circuit. Therefore **there is exactly one [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md), and it encircles the cylinder**. The standard auxiliary results used are existence/uniqueness of smooth differential equations and invariance at inward-pointing boundaries.
