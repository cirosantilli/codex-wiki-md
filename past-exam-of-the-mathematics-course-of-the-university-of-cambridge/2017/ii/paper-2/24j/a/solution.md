↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $f\in L^1(\mathbb R^d)$, use the [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) convention

$$
\boxed{\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R^d}e^{-i\xi\cdot x}f(x)\,dx.}
$$

The [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) is absolutely convergent for every $\xi$, and $|\widehat f(\xi)|\leq\|f\|_1$.
