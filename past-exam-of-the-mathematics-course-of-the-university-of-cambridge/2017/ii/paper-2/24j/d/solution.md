<h1 id="24j/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Define $g$ by the inverse [integral](../../../../../../integral.md). Its absolute convergence gives $|g(x)|\leq(2\pi)^{-d}\|\widehat f\|_1$. If $x_n\to x$, the exponential factors converge pointwise and their differences are bounded by $2|\widehat f|$. The [dominated convergence theorem](../../../../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md) proves $g(x_n)\to g(x)$. Therefore **$g$ is bounded and [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) and equals $f$ almost everywhere**.
