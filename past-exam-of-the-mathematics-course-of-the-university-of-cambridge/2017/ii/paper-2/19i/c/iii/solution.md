**No, it cannot deformation retract onto a [graph](../../../../../../../graph-split.md).** Each [sphere](../../../../../../../sphere.md) contributes a degree-$2$ [homology](../../../../../../../homology-split.md) class independent of the other two. Attaching [circles](../../../../../../../circle.md) at points introduces only vertices and edges, so

$$
\boxed{H_2(\widehat X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^3.}
$$

For example, the [cellular chain complex](../../../../../../../cellular-chain-complex.md) obtained by giving each [sphere](../../../../../../../sphere.md) one $2$-cell and a [tree](../../../../../../../tree-graph-theory.md) of arcs has three zero-boundary $2$-cells and no $3$-cells. Every [graph](../../../../../../../graph-split.md) has zero degree-$2$ [homology](../../../../../../../homology-split.md). A [deformation retraction](../../../../../../../deformation-retraction.md) would induce a [homotopy equivalence](../../../../../../../homotopy-equivalence.md) and an isomorphism on [homology](../../../../../../../homology-split.md), which is impossible. An ordinary [retraction](../../../../../../../retraction.md) has no such requirement.
