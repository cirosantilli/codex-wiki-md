<h1 id="19i/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [pushout of groups](../../../../../../../pushout-of-groups.md) is

$$
G=(G_1*G_2)/\left\langle\!\left\langle i_1(h)i_2(h)^{-1}:h\in H\right\rangle\!\right\rangle.
$$

Its maps from $G_1,G_2$ agree on $H$, and every other pair of [group homomorphisms](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) agreeing on $H$ factors through one unique homomorphism from $G$. This is the [universal property](../../../../../../../universal-property.md). When the $i_j$ are injective, it is the [amalgamated free product](../../../../../../../amalgamated-free-product.md), and the two factors and the identified copy of $H$ embed in it. Some conventions use this name for the quotient even without injectivity; the embedding assertion requires injectivity.
