The [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](../../../../../../../seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) states: if $X=U\cup V$, where $U,V$ are open and path connected, $U\cap V$ is path connected and contains a basepoint $x$, then $\pi_1(X,x)$ is the [pushout of groups](../../../../../../../pushout-of-groups.md) of $\pi_1(U,x)$ and $\pi_1(V,x)$ over $\pi_1(U\cap V,x)$. Thus **the [fundamental group](../../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) is obtained by amalgamating the overlap relations**. Openness can be replaced by standard neighborhood hypotheses, as in the wedge calculation.
