<h1 id="19i/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [real projective plane](../../../../../../../real-projective-plane.md) has [fundamental group](../../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) $C_2=\langle a\mid a^2=1\rangle$, while the [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) has [fundamental group](../../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) $\mathbb Z=\langle b\rangle$. Apply the [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](../../../../../../../seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) to open neighborhoods of the two wedge summands whose overlap is contractible. This gives

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(\mathbb {RP}^2\vee S^1,x)\cong C_2*\mathbb Z=\langle a,b\mid a^2=1\rangle.}
$$

Changing the basepoint within this path-connected space gives an isomorphic group.
