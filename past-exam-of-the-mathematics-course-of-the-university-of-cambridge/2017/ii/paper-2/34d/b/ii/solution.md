<h1 id="34d/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Replace the sum by the [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md) against the [density of states](../../../../../../../density-of-states.md) and set $x=\epsilon/(kT)$, $y=\mu/T$:

$$
\log\mathcal Z=AV(kT)^{a+1}\int_0^\infty x^a\log(1+e^{y/k-x})\,dx.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{b=a+1,\qquad f(y)=Ak^{a+1}\int_0^\infty x^a\log(1+e^{y/k-x})\,dx,\qquad\log\mathcal Z=VT^bf(\mu/T).}
$$

For the given $a>0$, the [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md) converges at zero and infinity for every finite $y$.
