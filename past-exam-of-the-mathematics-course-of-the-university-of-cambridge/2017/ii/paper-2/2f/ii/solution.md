<h1 id="2f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** By the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](../../../../../../weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md), choose a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $Q_n$ with $\sup_{|t|\leq n}|Q_n(t)-f(t)|<1/n$. For each fixed $t$, this bound applies once $n\geq|t|$, so $Q_n(t)\to f(t)$. This proves [pointwise convergence](../../../../../../pointwise-convergence.md); the same construction even gives [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) on every [closed interval](../../../../../../closed-real-interval.md).
