↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** If [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) held, some [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $P_n$ would satisfy $\sup_{t\in\mathbb R}|P_n(t)-\sin t|<1$. Such a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) would be bounded on $\mathbb R$, hence constant: a nonconstant [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) grows without bound in absolute value along a real half-line. For any constant $c$, however,

$$
\sup_{t\in\mathbb R}|c-\sin t|\geq\max\{|c-1|,|c+1|\}\geq1,
$$

a contradiction.
