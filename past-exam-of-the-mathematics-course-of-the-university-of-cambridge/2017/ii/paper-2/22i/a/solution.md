<h1 id="22i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A point $P$ belongs to the [smooth locus of a variety](../../../../../../smooth-locus-of-a-variety.md) if its [local ring](../../../../../../local-ring.md) $\mathcal O_{X,P}$ is a [regular local ring](../../../../../../regular-local-ring.md), meaning

$$
\boxed{\dim_k(\mathfrak m_P/\mathfrak m_P^2)=\dim\mathcal O_{X,P}.}
$$

Equivalently, the [Zariski tangent space](../../../../../../zariski-tangent-space.md) has the expected local dimension. Over the algebraically closed field, this agrees with smoothness for a reduced variety. In affine coordinates, the [Jacobian criterion](../../../../../../jacobian-criterion.md) expresses it by the rank of the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) of first [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) of defining equations.
