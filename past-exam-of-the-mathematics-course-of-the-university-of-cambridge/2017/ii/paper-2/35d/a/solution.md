<h1 id="35d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $k=1$, put $r=\sin\chi$. The spatial [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) becomes

$$
a^2\left[d\chi^2+\sin^2\chi(d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2)\right].
$$

The global closed spatial slice is a [3-sphere](../../../../../../three-sphere.md) with $0\leq\chi\leq\pi$; the original $r$ chart covers only a hemisphere, so two charts are needed. A [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) at fixed $\chi$ in an equatorial angular plane has circumference $\boxed{2\pi a\sin\chi}$. The full spatial volume is

$$
\boxed{4\pi a^3\int_0^\pi\sin^2\chi\,d\chi=2\pi^2a^3.}
$$

Integrating a single $r\in[0,1]$ chart without its second copy would give only half this volume.
