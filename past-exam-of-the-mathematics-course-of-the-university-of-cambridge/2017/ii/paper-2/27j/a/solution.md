A standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) is a real process $(B_t)_{t\geq0}$ with $B_0=0$ almost surely, [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) paths, [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) increments, and $B_t-B_s\sim N(0,t-s)$ for $0\leq s<t$. If a filtration is specified, require adaptedness and independence of each increment from the past sigma-algebra. Thus **the increments are centered Gaussian with [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) equal to elapsed time**.
