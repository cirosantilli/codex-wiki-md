<h1 id="38a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $\Delta f=0$, then $\Delta^2u=0$, so the $h^4$ term in the previous expansion vanishes. Therefore the normalized [local truncation error](../../../../../../local-truncation-error.md) is $\boxed{O(h^4)}$, and the unscaled residual is $O(h^6)$. This is generally fourth order under the given harmonic-source condition; special solutions can have additional cancellation, but that is not guaranteed by this argument.
