[Burnside's theorem](../../../../../../burnside-s-theorem.md) states that **every finite [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) of order $p^a q^b$ is solvable**, where $p,q$ are [primes](../../../../../../prime-number.md) and $a,b$ are nonnegative [integers](../../../../../../integer.md). In particular, a nonabelian [simple group](../../../../../../simple-group.md) cannot have order divisible by only one or two distinct [primes](../../../../../../prime-number.md).
