<h1 id="8e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8E](../8e.md)

[Liouville integrability](../../../../../integrable-hamiltonian-system.md) on a $2n$-dimensional [symplectic manifold](../../../../../symplectic-manifold.md) means that there are $n$ functionally independent [first integrals](../../../../../first-integral.md), including $H$, whose pairwise [Poisson brackets](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) vanish. Near a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) connected regular common level [set](../../../../../set-split.md), the [Arnold-Liouville theorem](../../../../../liouville-arnold-theorem.md) gives [action-angle variables](../../../../../action-angle-variables.md) $(J_i,\theta_i)$ with $J_i=(2\pi)^{-1}\oint p\,dq$, $\theta_i$ periodic modulo $2\pi$, $H=H(J)$, $\dot J_i=0$ and $\dot\theta_i=\partial H/\partial J_i$.

For a frozen [mass](../../../../../mass.md), the turning point is $q_*=(2E)^{1/(2k)}$. The [action variable](../../../../../action-variable.md) is

$$
 J=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_0^{q_*}\sqrt{2m(E-q^{2k}/2)}\,dq
 =C_k m^{1/2}E^{(k+1)/(2k)},\quad
 C_k=\frac{2\sqrt2\,2^{1/(2k)}}{\pi}\int_0^1\sqrt{1-s^{2k}}\,ds.
$$

[Adiabatic invariance of the action](../../../../../adiabatic-invariance-of-the-action.md) holds to leading order when the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) varies slowly compared with an orbital period, away from degenerating [orbits](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). Keeping $J$ fixed gives

$$
\boxed{E(t)\propto m(t)^{-k/(k+1)},\qquad c=-\frac{k}{k+1}.}
$$

For $k=1$ this agrees with $E=\omega J$ and $\omega\propto m^{-1/2}$.
