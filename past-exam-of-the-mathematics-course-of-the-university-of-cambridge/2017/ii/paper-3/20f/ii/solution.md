Integrating the exponential gives the [Fourier transform of an interval indicator](../../../../../../fourier-transform-of-an-interval-indicator.md)

$$
\boxed{\widehat{\chi_{[a,b]}}(\xi)=\frac{e^{-2\pi i a\xi}-e^{-2\pi i b\xi}}{2\pi i\xi}\quad(\xi\ne0),\qquad \widehat{\chi_{[a,b]}}(0)=b-a.}
$$

The value at zero is the removable [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md), for $a\leq b$.
