<h1 id="22i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [algebraic varieties](../../../../../../algebraic-variety.md) that are [irreducible topological spaces](../../../../../../irreducible-topological-space.md), a [rational map](../../../../../../rational-map-complex-analysis.md) $X\dashrightarrow Y$ is an equivalence class of [morphisms](../../../../../../morphism.md) $U\to Y$ on nonempty open [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) of $X$, with two representatives equivalent if they agree on a nonempty open [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md). Such [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) are dense. **A [birational map](../../../../../../birational-map.md) has a rational inverse**; equivalently it restricts to an [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md) between dense open [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md). In [function field](../../../../../../function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) language it induces an [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md) $k(Y)\cong k(X)$ over the ground [field](../../../../../../field.md).
