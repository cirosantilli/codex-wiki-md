<h1 id="22i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Work over the usual algebraically closed ground [field](../../../../../../field.md) of characteristic zero. Away from $x=0$, a line of slope $t=y/x$ through the singular point meets the [curve](../../../../../../curve.md) with $t^2=x-1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\phi(x,y)=y/x,\qquad \psi(t)=(t^2+1,\ t(t^2+1)).}
$$

Substitution verifies the defining equation. On $x\ne0$ and $t^2+1\ne0$, the two maps are inverse [morphisms](../../../../../../morphism.md), so this is a [birational map](../../../../../../birational-map.md) to $\mathbb A^1$. The two parameters $t=\pm i$ both map to the node $(0,0)$, explaining why the globally defined parametrization is not a global [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md).
