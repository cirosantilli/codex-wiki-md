<h1 id="19f/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $x\ne y$, $|(\delta_x-\delta_y)(f)|\leq2\|f\|_\infty$, giving an upper bound $2$ for the [operator norm](../../../../../../operator-norm.md). By [Urysohn lemma](../../../../../../urysohn-s-lemma.md), there is $h:K\to[0,1]$ with $h(x)=1,h(y)=0$. Then $f=2h-1$ has [uniform norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md) $1$ and $(\delta_x-\delta_y)(f)=2$. Thus every distinct pair has [operator norm](../../../../../../operator-norm.md) $2$, and

$$
\boxed{\inf_{x\ne y}\|\delta_x-\delta_y\|=2\quad\text{if }|K|\geq2.}
$$

The stated hypotheses also permit a singleton $K$. Then there are no distinct pairs, so the infimum is **$+\infty$ under the extended-real convention**, or undefined as a real number. This edge case cannot be assigned the value $2$.
