<h1 id="19f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The real [Stone-Weierstrass theorem](../../../../../../stone-weierstrass-theorem.md) says that a real [subalgebra](../../../../../../subalgebra.md) $A\subset C(K,\mathbb R)$ which contains the constants and separates points is dense in $C(K,\mathbb R)$ for the [uniform norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md), when $K$ is [compact Hausdorff space](../../../../../../compact-hausdorff-space.md). If $A$ is also closed, **$A=C(K,\mathbb R)$**. The complex version needs closure under [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md); that extra condition is unnecessary here.
