<h1 id="19f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since a [subalgebra](../../../../../../subalgebra.md) is closed under addition and scalar multiplication, the given absolute-value property yields

$$
 \boxed{\max(f,g)=\frac{f+g+|f-g|}{2}\in A.}
$$

The same identity with a minus sign gives $\min(f,g)\in A$. These are pointwise operations on real-valued [continuous functions](../../../../../../continuous-function.md).
