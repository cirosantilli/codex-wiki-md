<h1 id="19f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $F,G$ be disjoint closed [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) of $K$. They are [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md). For each $x\in F,y\in G$, the [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) condition gives disjoint open neighbourhoods $U_{xy},V_{xy}$. For fixed $x$, finitely many $V_{xy}$ cover $G$; intersect the corresponding $U_{xy}$ and take the [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) of those $V_{xy}$ to obtain disjoint open [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md) $U_x\ni x$ and $V_x\supset G$. Finitely many $U_x$ cover $F$. Their [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) and the [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) of the corresponding $V_x$ are disjoint open neighbourhoods of $F,G$. The case of an empty [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) is immediate. Hence **$K$ is a [normal topological space](../../../../../../normal-space.md)**.

[Urysohn lemma](../../../../../../urysohn-s-lemma.md) states that in a [normal topological space](../../../../../../normal-space.md) satisfying $T_1$, disjoint closed [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md) $F,G$ admit a [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $h:K\to[0,1]$ with $h=0$ on $F$ and $h=1$ on $G$.
