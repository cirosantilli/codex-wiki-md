<h1 id="34d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A clockwise engine [Carnot cycle](../../../../../../carnot-cycle.md) has four reversible steps. At hot [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) $T_h$, [isothermal expansion](../../../../../../isothermal-expansion.md) absorbs $Q_h=T_h\Delta S>0$ and does positive [work](../../../../../../work.md) by the gas. [Adiabatic expansion](../../../../../../adiabatic-expansion.md) then cools it to $T_c$, with zero [heat](../../../../../../heat.md) and positive [work](../../../../../../work.md) by the gas. [Isothermal compression](../../../../../../isothermal-compression.md) at $T_c$ emits $Q_c=-T_c\Delta S$ and requires [work](../../../../../../work.md) on the gas. Finally [adiabatic compression](../../../../../../adiabatic-compression.md) returns it to $T_h$, with zero [heat](../../../../../../heat.md) and [work](../../../../../../work.md) on the gas.

For an [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md), the $p,V$ isotherms obey $pV=\text{constant}$ and the adiabats $pV^\gamma=\text{constant}$, the latter being steeper. In the $T,S$ plane, the isotherms are horizontal and the adiabats vertical, forming a clockwise rectangle. [Heat](../../../../../../heat.md) occurs only on the horizontal legs, while [work](../../../../../../work.md) occurs on all four $p,V$ legs.

$$
\boxed{W=(T_h-T_c)\Delta S,\qquad\eta_{\rm Carnot}=1-T_c/T_h.}
$$

Reversing the arrows gives a refrigerator, reversing the [heat](../../../../../../heat.md) and [work](../../../../../../work.md) signs.
