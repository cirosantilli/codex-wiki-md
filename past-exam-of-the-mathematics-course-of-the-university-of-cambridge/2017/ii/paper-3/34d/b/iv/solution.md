<h1 id="34d/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The total [work](../../../../../../../work.md) by the gas is the enclosed triangular area, $W=\tfrac12p_0V_0$. The positive [heat](../../../../../../../heat.md) on the sloping leg is

$$
 Q_{BC,+}=p_0V_0\int_1^{15/8}(15/2-4v)dv=\frac{49}{32}p_0V_0.
$$

Add $Q_{AB}=48p_0V_0/32$, excluding the emitting part of $BC$ and all of $CA$. Hence

$$
\boxed{Q_{\rm in}=\frac{97}{32}p_0V_0,\qquad\eta=\frac{W}{Q_{\rm in}}=\frac{16}{97}.}
$$

As an independent [conservation of energy](../../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) check, the [heat](../../../../../../../heat.md) emitted on late $BC$ is $p_0V_0/32$ and on $CA$ is $80p_0V_0/32$; thus $Q_{\rm in}-Q_{\rm out}=16p_0V_0/32=W$. Using only the net [heat](../../../../../../../heat.md) on $BC$ would give an incorrect [thermal efficiency](../../../../../../../thermal-efficiency.md).
