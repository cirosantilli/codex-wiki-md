↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $N$ particles of a classical monatomic [ideal gas](../../../../../../../ideal-gas.md), the [equation of state](../../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) and [internal energy](../../../../../../../internal-energy.md) are

$$
\boxed{pV=Nk_BT,\qquad U=\frac32Nk_BT=\frac32pV.}
$$

Only translational degrees of freedom contribute; the particle number is fixed throughout the [thermodynamic cycle](../../../../../../../thermodynamic-cycle.md).
