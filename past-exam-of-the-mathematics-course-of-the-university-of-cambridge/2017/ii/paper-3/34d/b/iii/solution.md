<h1 id="34d/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

On the straight sloping leg, $p=p_0(3-V/V_0)$ and $dp=-p_0dV/V_0$. Put $v=V/V_0$, which increases from $1$ to $2$. The reversible [heat transfer](../../../../../../../heat-transfer.md) is

$$
 dQ=p_0V_0\left(\frac{15}{2}-4v\right)dv.
$$

Thus **[heat](../../../../../../../heat.md) is absorbed for $1<v<15/8$ and emitted for $15/8<v<2$**, with zero instantaneous [heat](../../../../../../../heat.md) at $v=15/8$. The switching point has $(p,V)=(9p_0/8,15V_0/8)$. In particular the net [heat](../../../../../../../heat.md) on this leg, $\tfrac32p_0V_0$, is not the total positive [heat](../../../../../../../heat.md) on it.
