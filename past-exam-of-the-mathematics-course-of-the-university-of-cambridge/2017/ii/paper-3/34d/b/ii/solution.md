<h1 id="34d/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a reversible change, the [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) is $T\,dS=dU+p\,dV$. Using the monatomic [internal energy](../../../../../../../internal-energy.md),

$$
\boxed{T\,dS=\frac32V\,dp+\frac52p\,dV.}
$$

Along the constant-[volume](../../../../../../../volume.md) leg, $Q_{AB}=\tfrac32V_0(2p_0-p_0)=\tfrac32p_0V_0>0$, so [heat](../../../../../../../heat.md) is absorbed. Along the constant-[pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) return leg, $Q_{CA}=\tfrac52p_0(V_0-2V_0)=-\tfrac52p_0V_0$, so [heat](../../../../../../../heat.md) is emitted. The [work](../../../../../../../work.md) signs are zero on $AB$ and negative, or [work](../../../../../../../work.md) on the gas, on $CA$.
