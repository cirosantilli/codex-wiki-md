↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

$L^p$ boundedness means $\sup_n\|X_n\|_p<\infty$, with the essential-supremum [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) for $p=\infty$. For finite $p>1$, let $C=\sup_n\mathbb E|X_n|^p<\infty$. On $|X_n|>M$, $|X_n|\leq M^{1-p}|X_n|^p$, so

$$
 \boxed{\sup_n\mathbb E[|X_n|\mathbf1_{|X_n|>M}]\leq C M^{1-p}\longrightarrow0.}
$$

This proves [uniform integrability](../../../../../../uniform-integrability.md). For an $L^\infty$ bounded family the tails are identically zero beyond the common essential bound.
