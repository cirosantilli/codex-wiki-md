<h1 id="32c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [32C](../32c.md)

Put $N_a=a^\dagger a$ and $N_b=b^\dagger b$. The [canonical commutation relations](../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) give $[N_a,a^\dagger]=a^\dagger$, $[N_a,a]=-a$, and the analogous relations for $b$, with all cross-mode operators commuting. Consequently $[K_3,K_+]=K_+$ and $[K_3,K_-]=-K_-$. Also

$$
 K_+K_-=N_a(N_b+1),\qquad K_-K_+=(N_a+1)N_b,
$$

so $[K_+,K_-]=N_a-N_b=2K_3$. This is the [Schwinger boson representation](../../../../../schwinger-boson-representation.md) of the [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) algebra, with $\hbar=1$ in the stated [commutators](../../../../../commutator.md).

The vacuum has $N_a=N_b=0$, and $(a^\dagger)^2|0\rangle$ has occupation numbers $(2,0)$, hence $K_3$ [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1$. With $N=N_a+N_b$,

$$
 K^2=N_aN_b+\frac12N+\frac14(N_a-N_b)^2
 =\frac N2\left(\frac N2+1\right).
$$

Thus $\boxed{K^2(a^\dagger)^2|0\rangle=2(a^\dagger)^2|0\rangle}$. It is the highest-weight state of the spin-one [irreducible representation](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md). There are **two other states in this multiplet**, $a^\dagger b^\dagger|0\rangle$ and $(b^\dagger)^2|0\rangle$, with $K_3$ [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $0,-1$. A normalized [basis](../../../../../basis.md) is $(a^\dagger)^2|0\rangle/\sqrt2$, $a^\dagger b^\dagger|0\rangle$, $(b^\dagger)^2|0\rangle/\sqrt2$. This count assumes the usual two-mode [bosonic Fock space](../../../../../bosonic-fock-space.md); an extra unmentioned vacuum multiplicity could replicate the multiplet.
