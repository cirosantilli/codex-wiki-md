<h1 id="3g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3G](../3g.md)

Identify a binary word with its coefficient [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $R=\mathbb F_2[X]/(X^7-1)$. A [cyclic code](../../../../../cyclic-code.md) is exactly an [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) of $R$: closure under multiplication by $X$ is closure under cyclic shift. Its inverse image in the [principal ideal domain](../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) $\mathbb F_2[X]$ has a unique monic [generator polynomial of a cyclic code](../../../../../generator-polynomial-of-a-cyclic-code.md) $g$ dividing $X^7-1$. Put

$$
 a=X+1,\quad b=X^3+X+1,\quad c=X^3+X^2+1;\qquad X^7-1=abc.
$$

The cubics have no root in $\mathbb F_2$, hence are [irreducible polynomials](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md). The eight monic [divisors](../../../../../divisor.md) yield all [binary cyclic codes of length seven](../../../../../binary-cyclic-codes-of-length-seven.md). A code generated by $g$ has [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $g,Xg,\ldots,X^{6-\deg g}g$ and [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $7-\deg g$, with the zero code treated separately.

For the [dual of a cyclic code](../../../../../dual-of-a-cyclic-code.md), let $h=(X^7-1)/g$. Orthogonality of coefficient [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) to all cyclic shifts gives the generator $h^*(X)=X^{\deg h}h(X^{-1})$. Here $a^*=a,b^*=c,c^*=b$. Thus the complete pairing, with the respective [dimensions](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md), is

$$
\boxed{1\ (7)\longleftrightarrow abc\ (0),\quad
 a\ (6)\longleftrightarrow bc\ (1),\quad
 b\ (4)\longleftrightarrow ab\ (3),\quad
 c\ (4)\longleftrightarrow ac\ (3).}
$$

These are respectively the whole-space/zero, even-weight/repetition, and two [Hamming code](../../../../../hamming-code.md)/[binary simplex code](../../../../../binary-simplex-code.md) pairs. The reciprocal formula follows because the product of a word [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) and the reversed [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of an orthogonal word has zero coefficients in all cyclic positions; the resulting inclusion has the correct complementary [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md), hence is equality.
