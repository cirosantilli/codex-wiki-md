<h1 id="36d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In [Minkowski spacetime](../../../../../../minkowski-spacetime.md) with metric $-dt^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2$, take

$$
\boxed{\gamma(t)=(t,\cos t,\sin t,0).}
$$

Its [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) is $(1,-\sin t,\cos t,0)$, with [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) square $-1+\sin^2t+\cos^2t=0$. Its [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) is $(0,-\cos t,-\sin t,0)$, nonzero and not proportional to the tangent because its time component is zero. Thus it is a regular [null curve](../../../../../../null-curve.md) which is **not a [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) under any reparametrization**.
