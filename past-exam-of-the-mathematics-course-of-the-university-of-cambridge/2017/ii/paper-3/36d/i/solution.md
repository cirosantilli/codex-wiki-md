<h1 id="36d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At $p$, choose local [normal coordinates](../../../../../../normal-coordinates.md) with $g_{\alpha\beta}(p)=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$. Let $e_0,e_1$ be their coordinate [basis](../../../../../../basis.md). The [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) $V=e_0+e_1$ and $W=e_0-e_1$ have zero [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) square, and their component [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $-2\ne0$. Thus **two independent [null vectors](../../../../../../null-vector.md) form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of the [tangent space](../../../../../../tangent-space.md)**. [Normal coordinates](../../../../../../normal-coordinates.md) are only needed at the chosen point, not on a whole neighbourhood with flat metric.
