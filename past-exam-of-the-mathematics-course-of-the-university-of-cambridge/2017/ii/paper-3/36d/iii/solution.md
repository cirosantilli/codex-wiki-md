<h1 id="36d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $U$ be the smooth nonzero tangent along a regular [null curve](../../../../../../null-curve.md), and $a=\nabla_UU$. Metric compatibility gives $g(U,a)=\tfrac12U[g(U,U)]=0$. In a two-dimensional [Lorentzian manifold](../../../../../../lorentzian-manifold.md), the [orthogonal complement](../../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) of a nonzero [null vector](../../../../../../null-vector.md) is its own span; this follows directly in the preceding null [basis](../../../../../../basis.md). Therefore $a=\kappa(t)U$.

Consequently **every regular [null curve](../../../../../../null-curve.md) is a [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) up to reparametrization**. To obtain an [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) $\lambda$, write $q=dt/d\lambda>0$ and solve $dq/dt+\kappa(t)q=0$. Then $\nabla_{qU}(qU)=q^2(\kappa+q^{-1}dq/dt)U=0$. A solution $q=C\exp(-\int\kappa dt)$ exists locally and along any nonsingular parameter interval. The original parameter need not be affine; the literal assertion of zero covariant [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) for every parameter would be false.
