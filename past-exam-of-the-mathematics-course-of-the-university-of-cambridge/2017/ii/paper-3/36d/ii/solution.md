<h1 id="36d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write the nonzero [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) as $U=aV+bW$. Since $g(V,V)=g(W,W)=0$ and $g(V,W)=-2$, nullness gives $g(U,U)=-4ab=0$. Thus exactly one coefficient can be nonzero, proving $\boxed{U\parallel V\text{ or }U\parallel W}$. This pointwise argument does not require the [curve](../../../../../../curve.md) to be a [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md).
