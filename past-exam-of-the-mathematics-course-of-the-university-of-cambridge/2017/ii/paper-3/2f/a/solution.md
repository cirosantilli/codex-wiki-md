<h1 id="2f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $D$ be the closed unit [disc](../../../../../../topological-disc.md) and set

$$
 f(x)=x-\frac{g(Kx)}K.
$$

The displacement bound gives $\|f(x)\|\leq1$, so $f:D\to D$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). The [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) applies because $D$ is nonempty, [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) and [convex](../../../../../../convex-function.md). At a [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) $x_*$, $x_*=x_*-g(Kx_*)/K$. Therefore $\boxed{t=Kx_*,\quad g(t)=0}$.
