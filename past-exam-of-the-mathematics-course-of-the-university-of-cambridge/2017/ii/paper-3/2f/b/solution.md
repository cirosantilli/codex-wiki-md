<h1 id="2f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For arbitrary $y\in\mathbb R^2$, the [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) $g_y(x)=g(x)-y$ satisfies $\|g_y(x)-x\|\leq K+\|y\|$. Applying the preceding [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) argument with this positive bound gives $g_y(t)=0$. Hence $g(t)=y$, and **$g$ is surjective**.
