<h1 id="2f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $e=(1,0)$ and define

$$
 g(x)=\begin{cases}e,&x=0,\\x,&x\ne0.\end{cases}
$$

Then $\|g(x)-x\|\leq1$ everywhere, but $0$ is not in the [image of a function](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md). The map is [discontinuous](../../../../../../discontinuity-of-a-function.md) at $0$, giving **a bounded-displacement map which is not surjective**.
