<h1 id="16i/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Over $\mathbb F_7$, the [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $t^4-1$ factors as $(t-1)(t+1)(t^2+1)$. Since $-1$ is not a square modulo $7$, the last factor is an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md). The [splitting field](../../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb F_{49}$, so $\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(t^4-1/\mathbb F_7)\cong C_2}$. The [finite-field Frobenius automorphism](../../../../../../../finite-field-frobenius-automorphism.md) fixes $1,-1$ and swaps the other two roots.
