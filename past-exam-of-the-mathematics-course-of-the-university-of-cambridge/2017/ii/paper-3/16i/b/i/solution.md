<h1 id="16i/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Over $\mathbb F_3$,

$$
 t^4+1=(t^2+t+2)(t^2+2t+2).
$$

Both quadratics have [discriminant](../../../../../../../discriminant.md) $2$, a nonsquare in $\mathbb F_3$, so are [irreducible polynomials](../../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md). Both split in the same quadratic [finite field](../../../../../../../finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_9$. Consequently $\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(t^4+1/\mathbb F_3)\cong C_2}$. Its generator is the [finite-field Frobenius automorphism](../../../../../../../finite-field-frobenius-automorphism.md) $x\mapsto x^3$, acting as a product of two transpositions on the four roots.
