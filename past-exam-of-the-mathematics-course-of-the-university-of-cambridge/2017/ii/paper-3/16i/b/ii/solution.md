<h1 id="16i/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $t^3-t-2$ has values $3,3,4,2,3$ at $0,1,2,3,4$ respectively, modulo $5$. Thus $t^3-t-2$ has no root and is an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) over $\mathbb F_5$. One root generates $\mathbb F_{125}$; all its [Frobenius conjugates](../../../../../../../frobenius-conjugate.md) are there, so this is its [splitting field](../../../../../../../splitting-field.md). Hence $\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(t^3-t-2/\mathbb F_5)\cong C_3}$, generated by $x\mapsto x^5$, a 3-[cycle](../../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) on the roots.
