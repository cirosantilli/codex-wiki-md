<h1 id="10g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $D_n=p_{n-1}q_{n-2}-p_{n-2}q_{n-1}=(-1)^{n-2}$. Solving the given [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) for the [complete quotient](../../../../../../complete-quotient.md) and rationalizing gives

$$
 \theta_n=\frac{p_{n-2}-\sqrt d\,q_{n-2}}{\sqrt d\,q_{n-1}-p_{n-1}}
 =\frac{D_n\sqrt d+dq_{n-1}q_{n-2}-p_{n-1}p_{n-2}}
 {p_{n-1}^2-dq_{n-1}^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{s_n=\frac{p_{n-1}^2-dq_{n-1}^2}{D_n},\qquad
 r_n=\frac{dq_{n-1}q_{n-2}-p_{n-1}p_{n-2}}{D_n}.}
$$

The denominator $D_n$ is $\pm1$, so both are [integers](../../../../../../integer.md). The [algebraic norm](../../../../../../algebraic-norm.md) denominator is nonzero because $\sqrt d$ is an [irrational number](../../../../../../irrational-number.md).
