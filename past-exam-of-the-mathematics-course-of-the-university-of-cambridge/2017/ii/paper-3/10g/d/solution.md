<h1 id="10g/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [continued fraction convergents](../../../../../../continued-fraction-convergent.md) of $\sqrt{67}$ begin $8,41/5,90/11,131/16,221/27$. The last one yields

$$
\boxed{x=221,\qquad y=27,\qquad 221^2-67\cdot27^2=48841-48843=-2.}
$$

Changing either sign gives another valid [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) solution.
