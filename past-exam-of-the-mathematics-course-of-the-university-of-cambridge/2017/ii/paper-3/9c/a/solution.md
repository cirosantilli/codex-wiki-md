<h1 id="9c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) imposes $\mu_e+\mu_p=\mu_H+\mu_\gamma$. For blackbody [photons](../../../../../../photon.md), $\mu_\gamma=0$. Define the positive [Hydrogen binding energy](../../../../../../hydrogen-binding-energy.md) by $I=(m_e+m_p-m_H)c^2$, approximately $13.6\,\mathrm{eV}$. Multiplying the two [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md) number densities and dividing by the neutral-hydrogen density gives

$$
 \frac{n_en_p}{n_H}=\frac{g_eg_p}{g_H}
 \left(\frac{2\pi kT}{h^2}\frac{m_em_p}{m_H}\right)^{3/2}e^{-I/(kT)}.
$$

Use $g_eg_p/g_H=1$ and $m_p/m_H\simeq1$ to obtain the [Saha ionization equation](../../../../../../saha-ionization-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{n_en_p}{n_H}=\left(\frac{2\pi m_e kT}{h^2}\right)^{3/2}e^{-I/(kT)}.}
$$

The assumptions are nonrelativistic, dilute, nondegenerate species at a common [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md), ground-state hydrogen with the stated [degeneracy factors](../../../../../../degeneracy-factor.md), negligible [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) correction in the prefactor, and sufficiently rapid reactions to maintain [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md).
