<h1 id="39a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With shift $1/2$, the unwanted [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) lie in $[-1/2,1/2]$, and both endpoints occur. The dominant shifted [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is $1/2+\epsilon$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\rho=\frac{1/2}{1/2+\epsilon}=1-2\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2).}
$$

The negative endpoint can alternate in sign, but the [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) error has this factor, and the even-iteration comparison removes the alternation.
