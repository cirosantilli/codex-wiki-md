<h1 id="11h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [many-one reduction](../../../../../../many-one-reduction.md) $A\leq_mB$ is a [total computable function](../../../../../../total-computable-function.md) $f:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ with $x\in A\iff f(x)\in B$. If $B$ is [recursively enumerable](../../../../../../recursively-enumerable-set.md), compute $f(x)$ and run a semidecision procedure for $B$ on that value. It halts exactly when $x\in A$, so **$A$ is [recursively enumerable](../../../../../../recursively-enumerable-set.md)**. Totality of $f$ is essential to this argument.
