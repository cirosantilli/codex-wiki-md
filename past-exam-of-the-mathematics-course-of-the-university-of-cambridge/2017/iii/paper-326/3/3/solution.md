<h1 id="3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

In an infinite-dimensional [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), every bounded [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) has a [weakly convergent](../../../../../../weak-convergence.md) subsequence, but it need not have a norm-convergent one. For example an orthonormal [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) $e_n$ has pairwise distance $\sqrt2$ and therefore no strongly convergent subsequence, although $e_n\rightharpoonup0$ by [Bessel inequality](../../../../../../bessel-s-inequality.md).

More generally, [weak sequential compactness of bounded sequences in a reflexive Banach space](../../../../../../weak-sequential-compactness-of-bounded-sequences-in-a-reflexive-banach-space.md) ensures a [weakly convergent](../../../../../../weak-convergence.md) subsequence with its limit in that space. Conversely, this property for all bounded sequences characterizes reflexivity, using the [Eberlein-Šmulian theorem](../../../../../../eberlein-smulian-theorem.md) and weak compactness of the closed unit ball. Thus **reflexivity replaces finite-dimensional [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) compactness by weak sequential compactness**.

In a nonreflexive [Banach space](../../../../../../banach-space-split.md) there is no such general weak-subsequence guarantee. The coordinate unit vectors in $\ell^1$ give an example: by the [Schur property](../../../../../../schur-property.md), [weak convergence](../../../../../../weak-convergence.md) would imply [norm convergence](../../../../../../norm-convergence.md), impossible for their pairwise distance two. [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](../../../../../../banach-alaoglu-theorem.md) supplies weak-star compactness of a dual unit ball, which gives convergent subnets, not automatically convergent subsequences. For a separable predual, the weak-star compact dual ball is metrizable and subsequences do suffice. For an arbitrary [Banach space](../../../../../../banach-space-split.md) one can also regard a bounded [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) in its bidual and extract a weak-star convergent subnet there; the limit need not belong to the original space. These qualifications distinguish weak from weak-star compactness.
