<h1 id="3/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a real [Banach space](../../../../../../banach-space-split.md) $X$ and a [proper convex function](../../../../../../proper-convex-function.md) $E:X\to\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\}$, the [convex conjugate](../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) is the extended-valued function on the [continuous dual space](../../../../../../continuous-dual-space-split.md)

$$
\boxed{E^*(p)=\sup_{x\in X}\{\langle x,p\rangle-E(x)\},\qquad p\in X^*.}
$$

Here proper means that $E$ never takes $-\infty$ and is finite somewhere. The pairing is the duality pairing, or the inner product after identification in a [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md). The conjugate is convex and lower semicontinuous because it is a supremum of continuous affine functions. Its definition gives the [Fenchel–Young inequality](../../../../../../fenchel-young-inequality.md) $E(x)+E^*(p)\geq\langle x,p\rangle$; the [Fenchel-Moreau theorem](../../../../../../fenchel-moreau-theorem.md) recovers $E$ as $E^{**}$ under the stated closed-convex hypotheses.
