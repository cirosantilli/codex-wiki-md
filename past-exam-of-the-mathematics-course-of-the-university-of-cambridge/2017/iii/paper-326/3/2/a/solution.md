<h1 id="3/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [absolute value function](../../../../../../../absolute-value.md), $px-|x|\leq0$ for all $x$ if $|p|\leq1$, with equality at $x=0$. If $|p|>1$, taking $x=t\operatorname{sign}(p)$ with $t\to\infty$ makes the expression diverge. Thus its [convex conjugate](../../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) is

$$
\boxed{E^*(p)=\begin{cases}0,&|p|\leq1,\\+\infty,&|p|>1.\end{cases}}
$$

This is the [indicator functional of a constraint set](../../../../../../../indicator-functional-of-a-constraint-set.md) for the dual unit interval, not a zero-one indicator function.
