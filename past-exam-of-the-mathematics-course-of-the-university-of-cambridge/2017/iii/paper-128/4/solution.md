<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Choose a finite-dimensional generating [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) $V\subseteq A$ containing $1$, and write $V^n$ for the [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) of products of $n$ elements of $V$. The [Gelfand–Kirillov dimension](../../../../../gelfand-kirillov-dimension.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{GKdim}A=\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log\dim_k V^n}{\log n}.}
$$

For a nonzero [finitely generated module](../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) $M$ considered as a right [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md), choose a finite-dimensional generating [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) $W\subseteq M$ and define the [Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of a module](../../../../../gelfand-kirillov-dimension-of-a-module.md) by

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{GKdim}_A M=\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log\dim_k(WV^n)}{\log n}.}
$$

For left [modules](../../../../../module-mathematics.md), replace $WV^n$ by $V^nW$. These values are independent of the choices: two algebra-generating [vector subspaces](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) satisfy $V\subseteq (V')^c$ and $V'\subseteq V^d$ for some positive integers $c,d$, and two module-generating [vector subspaces](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) are contained in bounded-degree translates of one another. The corresponding growth bounds differ only by a constant rescaling and shift of $n$, which do not change the [limit superior](../../../../../limit-superior.md).

Let $S$ be a nonzero [standard graded algebra](../../../../../standard-graded-algebra.md), generated by $r$ elements of degree one. It is a homogeneous [quotient ring](../../../../../quotient-ring.md) of the [polynomial ring](../../../../../polynomial-ring.md) $k[z_1,\ldots,z_r]$. By the [Hilbert-Serre theorem](../../../../../hilbert-serre-theorem.md), its [Hilbert series](../../../../../hilbert-series.md) is a rational function whose only possible pole is at $t=1$. After canceling, write it as $P(t)/(1-t)^d$ with $P(1)\ne0$, where $0\leq d\leq r$. The cumulative dimensions are the coefficients of $P(t)/(1-t)^{d+1}$ and are eventually a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $n$ of degree $d$, with positive leading coefficient. With $V=S_0\oplus S_1$, these cumulative dimensions equal $\dim V^n$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{GKdim}S=d\in\{0,1,\ldots,r\}.}
$$

For $d=0$ the [algebra](../../../../../algebra-split.md) is finite-dimensional and nonzero, so the cumulative dimension is eventually a positive constant. If the zero [algebra](../../../../../algebra-split.md) is allowed, the common convention $\operatorname{GKdim}0=-\infty$ is an exception to the printed assertion; the integer conclusion concerns nonzero unital [algebras](../../../../../algebra-split.md).

For the [Weyl algebra](../../../../../weyl-algebra.md) $A=A_1(k)=k\langle X,D\rangle/(DX-XD-1)$, the total-degree [filtered algebra](../../../../../filtered-algebra.md) structure has [associated graded ring](../../../../../associated-graded-ring.md) $\operatorname{gr}A=k[x,\xi]$. Its ordered [monomials](../../../../../monomial.md) $X^iD^j$ form a [basis](../../../../../basis.md). Every [finitely generated module](../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) has a [good filtration of a module](../../../../../good-filtration-of-a-module.md), and its [associated graded module](../../../../../associated-graded-module.md) is finitely generated over $k[x,\xi]$. The [Hilbert-Serre theorem](../../../../../hilbert-serre-theorem.md) therefore shows that its [Gelfand–Kirillov dimension](../../../../../gelfand-kirillov-dimension.md) is an integer in $\{0,1,2\}$.

A nonzero [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) of [Gelfand–Kirillov dimension](../../../../../gelfand-kirillov-dimension.md) zero here would have eventually constant cumulative dimension, hence be finite-dimensional. This is impossible in [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) zero: taking the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of the [endomorphisms](../../../../../endomorphism.md) representing $DX-XD=1$ gives $0=\dim_k M$. For right [modules](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) the right-action operators reverse composition and give the negative identity instead, with the same contradiction. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{GKdim}_A M\in\{1,2\}.}
$$

Both occur. The regular [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) $A$ has $\dim V^n=\binom{n+2}{2}$, hence [Gelfand–Kirillov dimension](../../../../../gelfand-kirillov-dimension.md) two. For a right [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) of [Gelfand–Kirillov dimension](../../../../../gelfand-kirillov-dimension.md) one, take $M=k[x]$ with $f\cdot X=xf$ and $f\cdot D=-f'$. These actions satisfy the defining relation because $(f\cdot D)\cdot X-(f\cdot X)\cdot D=f$. The [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is a [cyclic module](../../../../../cyclic-module.md) generated by $1$, and $\dim(1\cdot V^n)=n+1$. This also gives the lower bound for this particular [Weyl algebra](../../../../../weyl-algebra.md) without invoking a general inequality for higher [Weyl algebras](../../../../../weyl-algebra.md).
