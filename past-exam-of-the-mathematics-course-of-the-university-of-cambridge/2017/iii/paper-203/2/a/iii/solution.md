<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

With the normalization used in the [Chordal Loewner equation](../../../../../../../chordal-loewner-equation.md), [half-plane-capacity parameterization](../../../../../../../half-plane-capacity-parameterization.md) means

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{hcap}(K_t)=2t,\qquad g_t(z)=z+\frac{2t}{z}+O(z^{-2}).}
$$

Thus $K_0$ is empty and the [half-plane-capacity composition rule](../../../../../../../half-plane-capacity-composition-rule.md) gives $\operatorname{hcap}(K_{s,t})=2(t-s)$. The factor two produces $\partial_tg_t(z)=2/(g_t(z)-U_t)$. A convention using [half-plane capacity](../../../../../../../half-plane-capacity.md) $t$ instead changes that coefficient to one and rescales time; the convention must be kept consistent.
