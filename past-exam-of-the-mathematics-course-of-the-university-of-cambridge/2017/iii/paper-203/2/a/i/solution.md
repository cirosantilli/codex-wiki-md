↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A family $(K_t)_{t\geq0}$ of [compact H-hulls](../../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md) is non-decreasing when

$$
\boxed{K_s\subseteq K_t\quad\text{whenever }0\leq s\leq t.}
$$

This permits equal hulls at different times. A [Loewner chain](../../../../../../../loewner-chain.md) with strictly increasing [half-plane capacity](../../../../../../../half-plane-capacity.md) has strict inclusion for $s<t$. Usually its initial [compact H-hull](../../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md) is $K_0=\varnothing$.
