<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [chordal restriction property](../../../../../../chordal-restriction-property.md) concerns the unparameterized trace law. Let $A$ be a [compact H-hull](../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md) with $0\notin\overline A$, and put $\psi_A(z)=g_A(z)-g_A(0)$. This [conformal map](../../../../../../conformal-map.md) takes $\mathbb H\setminus A$ onto $\mathbb H$, fixes $0$ and infinity, and has [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) one at infinity. On the event that the curve avoids $A$, its image is a curve from $0$ to infinity in $\mathbb H$. Restriction requires positive avoidance probability for every admissible $A$, and the conditional identity

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L\bigl(\psi_A(\gamma)\mid\gamma\cap A=\varnothing\bigr)=\mathcal L(\gamma),}
$$

up to increasing changes of the curve parameter. Equivalently, conditioning an [SLE](../../../../../../schramm-loewner-evolution.md) in a marked [simply connected domain](../../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md) to stay in an admissible [simply connected domain](../../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md) gives the same [SLE](../../../../../../schramm-loewner-evolution.md) law in that subdomain. The property does not assert that the original [half-plane-capacity parameterization](../../../../../../half-plane-capacity-parameterization.md) survives an arbitrary [conformal map](../../../../../../conformal-map.md).
