↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Koebe quarter theorem](../../../../../../koebe-quarter-theorem.md) says that if $F:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C$ is a [univalent function](../../../../../../univalent-function.md) with $F(0)=0$ and $F'(0)=1$, then

$$
\boxed{\{w:|w|<1/4\}\subseteq F(\mathbb D).}
$$

Equivalently, an arbitrary [univalent function](../../../../../../univalent-function.md) $f$ on the [unit disc](../../../../../../unit-disc.md) has $B(f(0),|f'(0)|/4)\subseteq f(\mathbb D)$. The constant cannot be increased: the [Koebe function](../../../../../../koebe-function.md) $F(w)=w/(1-w)^2$ omits the ray $(-\infty,-1/4]$.
