<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\mathbb H=\{z:\operatorname{Im}z>0\}$. A [compact H-hull](../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md) is a bounded, relatively closed set $A\subseteq\mathbb H$ for which $D_A=\mathbb H\setminus A$ is a [simply connected domain](../../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md). “Compact” refers to its compact [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md) in $\overline{\mathbb H}$; [boundary](../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) points on $\mathbb R$ can be adjoined without changing the remaining [domain](../../../../../../domain-mathematical-analysis.md).

The [Riemann mapping theorem](../../../../../../riemann-mapping-theorem.md) and normalization at infinity give a unique [mapping-out function of a compact H-hull](../../../../../../mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md) $g_A:D_A\to\mathbb H$ with [hydrodynamic normalization at infinity](../../../../../../hydrodynamic-normalization-at-infinity.md). By the [Schwarz reflection principle](../../../../../../schwarz-reflection-principle.md) outside a large disc, it has the [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md)

$$
g_A(z)=z+\frac{a}{z}+O(|z|^{-2}),\qquad a\in\mathbb R.
$$

The [half-plane capacity](../../../../../../half-plane-capacity.md) is the coefficient

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{hcap}(A)=a.}
$$

The empty [compact H-hull](../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md) has $g_A(z)=z$ and [half-plane capacity](../../../../../../half-plane-capacity.md) zero. Nonnegativity follows from the [Brownian representation of half-plane capacity](../../../../../../brownian-representation-of-half-plane-capacity.md) proved next.
