<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose $A\subseteq B$. Map out $A$ and let $C$ be the relatively closed image hull corresponding to $g_A(B\setminus A)$, with bounded complementary components filled if needed. Then $g_A$ maps $\mathbb H\setminus B$ onto $\mathbb H\setminus C$. Uniqueness of the [mapping-out function of a compact H-hull](../../../../../../../mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md) gives $g_B=g_C\circ g_A$. Comparing their [Laurent series](../../../../../../../laurent-series.md) yields the [half-plane-capacity composition rule](../../../../../../../half-plane-capacity-composition-rule.md)

$$
\operatorname{hcap}(B)=\operatorname{hcap}(A)+\operatorname{hcap}(C).
$$

The [Brownian representation of half-plane capacity](../../../../../../../brownian-representation-of-half-plane-capacity.md) makes the last term nonnegative, proving [monotonicity of half-plane capacity](../../../../../../../monotonicity-of-half-plane-capacity.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{hcap}(A)\leq\operatorname{hcap}(B).}
$$
