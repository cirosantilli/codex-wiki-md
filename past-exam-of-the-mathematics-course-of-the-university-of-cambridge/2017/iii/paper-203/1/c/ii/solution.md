<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $r>0$, the [conformal map](../../../../../../../conformal-map.md) $h(z)=r g_A(z/r)$ maps $\mathbb H\setminus rA$ onto $\mathbb H$ and has [Laurent series](../../../../../../../laurent-series.md) $h(z)=z+r^2a/z+O(z^{-2})$. The uniqueness in [hydrodynamic normalization at infinity](../../../../../../../hydrodynamic-normalization-at-infinity.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{hcap}(rA)=r^2\operatorname{hcap}(A).}
$$

At $r=0$ the scaled [closure](../../../../../../../closure-topology.md) is contained in $\mathbb R$, so the [interior](../../../../../../../interior-topology.md) [compact H-hull](../../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md) is empty and both sides are zero. This is the scaling part of [scaling and translation of half-plane capacity](../../../../../../../scaling-and-translation-of-half-plane-capacity.md).
