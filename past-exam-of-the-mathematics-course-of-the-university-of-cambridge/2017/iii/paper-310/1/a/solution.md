<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use units $c=1$ and [cosmic time](../../../../../../cosmic-time.md) $t$. Define the [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md) $H=\dot a/a$, the [equation-of-state parameter](../../../../../../equation-of-state-parameter.md) $\omega=P/\rho$, and the [cosmological density parameter](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md)

$$
\Omega=\frac\rho{\rho_{\rm crit}},\qquad\rho_{\rm crit}=\frac{3H^2}{8\pi G}.
$$

Here $\rho_{\rm crit}$ is the [critical density](../../../../../../critical-density.md). The definition requires $H\ne0$; assume positive [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) and an expanding branch so that $\log a$ is a valid time coordinate. The [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $\Omega-1=K/(a^2H^2)$. The [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) and [Hubble parameter identity](../../../../../../hubble-parameter-identity.md) give

$$
\frac{\dot H}{H^2}=\frac{\ddot a}{aH^2}-1=-1-\frac12(1+3\omega)\Omega.
$$

Differentiating the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) and combining with this identity yields the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) $\dot\rho=-3H(\rho+P)$. Consequently

$$
\frac{d\log\Omega}{d\log a}=\frac{\dot\rho}{H\rho}-2\frac{\dot H}{H^2}
=-3(1+\omega)+2+(1+3\omega)\Omega.
$$

Thus the [cosmological density parameter flow](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter-flow.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\Omega}{d\log a}=(1+3\omega)\Omega(\Omega-1).}
$$

This identity allows time-dependent $\omega$; constancy is needed only for the simple power-law integration in the next part.
