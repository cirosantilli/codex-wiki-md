<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take a homogeneous comoving cell of physical [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) $V_c=a^3V_0$ and total [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) $S=sV_c$. At vanishing [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md), the reversible [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) reads

$$
T\,dS=d(\rho V_c)+P\,dV_c=V_c\,d\rho+(\rho+P)dV_c.
$$

For an extensive equilibrium system, the [thermodynamic Euler relation](../../../../../../euler-relation-in-thermodynamics.md) is $E=TS-PV_c+\mu N$. At $\mu=0$ this gives

$$
\boxed{s=\frac{\rho+P}{T}.}
$$

The supplied pressure identity verifies consistency directly: $dP=(\rho+P)dT/T$ implies

$$
d\left[\frac{(\rho+P)V_c}{T}\right]=\frac{V_c\,d\rho+(\rho+P)dV_c}{T}=dS.
$$

The [thermodynamic Euler relation](../../../../../../euler-relation-in-thermodynamics.md) fixes the additive normalization that a differential identity alone would leave undetermined.

For adiabatic cosmological expansion, the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) gives $\dot\rho+3H(\rho+P)=0$, while $\dot V_c=3HV_c$. Hence

$$
T\dot S=V_c\bigl[\dot\rho+3H(\rho+P)\bigr]=0,\qquad
\boxed{sa^3=\mathrm{constant}.}
$$

This proves [cosmological entropy conservation](../../../../../../cosmological-entropy-conservation.md) in the stated perfect-fluid approximation. [Kinetic equilibrium](../../../../../../kinetic-equilibrium.md) alone does not guarantee adiabatic evolution: entropy injection, dissipative stresses or out-of-equilibrium reactions would require additional source terms. Those effects are implicitly excluded in this calculation.
