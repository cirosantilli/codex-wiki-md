<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [Mészáros effect](../../../../../../meszaros-effect.md) suppresses the growth of modes which enter the horizon during [radiation domination](../../../../../../radiation-domination.md). Modes with comoving [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) $k\gg k_{\rm eq}$ enter earlier, but gain only a logarithmic growth factor until [matter-radiation equality](../../../../../../matter-radiation-equality.md); modes entering during [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md) grow as $a$ after entry. Consequently small-scale amplitudes are reduced relative to an extrapolation of the unsuppressed large-scale [cosmological density power spectrum](../../../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md), creating a turnover near the [matter-radiation equality scale](../../../../../../matter-radiation-equality-scale.md) $k_{\rm eq}=\mathcal H_{\rm eq}$ in units $c=1$.

More quantitatively, for adiabatic primordial fluctuations with [scalar spectral index](../../../../../../scalar-spectral-index.md) $n_s$, the late-time linear spectrum can be written, up to a time-dependent normalization,

$$
P_m(k)\propto k^{n_s}T^2(k).
$$

The [cold-dark-matter transfer function](../../../../../../cold-dark-matter-transfer-function.md) approaches a constant at $k\ll k_{\rm eq}$, whereas [logarithmic small-scale matter transfer](../../../../../../logarithmic-small-scale-matter-transfer.md) gives $T(k)\propto(k_{\rm eq}/k)^2\log(k/k_{\rm eq})$ at large $k$. Thus

$$
\boxed{P_m(k)\propto\begin{cases}k^{n_s},&k\ll k_{\rm eq},\\k^{n_s-4}\log^2(k/k_{\rm eq}),&k\gg k_{\rm eq}.\end{cases}}
$$

For approximately scale-invariant initial curvature fluctuations, $n_s\simeq1$, the large-$k$ scaling is $k^{-3}\log^2k$, rather than $k$. This discussion ignores baryonic acoustic structure and nonlinear clustering. The [Mészáros equation solution basis](../../../../../../meszaros-equation-solution-basis.md) includes both a constant and a logarithmic branch during radiation domination; the physical logarithmic growth is selected by matching the earlier radiation forcing, so not every mathematical solution grows logarithmically.
