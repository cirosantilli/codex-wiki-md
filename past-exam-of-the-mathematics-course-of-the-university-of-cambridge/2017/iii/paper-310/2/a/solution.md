<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Here primes mean [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md) derivatives, $d\tau=dt/a$, and spatial derivatives use comoving coordinates in [Newtonian gauge in cosmology](../../../../../../newtonian-gauge.md). Set $\theta_m=\nabla\cdot\mathbf v_m$. The linear [cosmological Euler equation](../../../../../../cosmological-euler-equation.md) gives $\theta_m'=-\mathcal H\theta_m-\nabla^2\Phi$, while the continuity equation gives $\theta_m=-\delta_m'+3\Phi'$.

Differentiate the continuity equation and substitute:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta_m''&=-\theta_m'+3\Phi''\\
&=\mathcal H\theta_m+\nabla^2\Phi+3\Phi''\\
&=-\mathcal H\delta_m'+\nabla^2\Phi+3\Phi''+3\mathcal H\Phi'.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the [linear matter density equation in Newtonian gauge](../../../../../../linear-matter-density-equation-in-newtonian-gauge.md) is

$$
\boxed{\delta_m''+\mathcal H\delta_m'=\nabla^2\Phi+3(\Phi''+\mathcal H\Phi').}
$$

The common potential in these supplied equations assumes that the relevant [scalar anisotropic stress](../../../../../../scalar-anisotropic-stress.md) is neglected. No Fourier-sign convention is needed for the derivation in position space.
