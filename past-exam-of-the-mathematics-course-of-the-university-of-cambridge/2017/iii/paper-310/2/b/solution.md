<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Normalize the [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) so that $a_0=1$. With separately conserved [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) and [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md), their [energy densities](../../../../../../energy-density.md) scale as $a^{-3}$ and $a^{-4}$. Hence the flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) is

$$
H^2=H_0^2\bigl(\Omega_{m,0}a^{-3}+\Omega_{r,0}a^{-4}\bigr),\qquad
\mathcal H^2=H_0^2\bigl(\Omega_{m,0}a^{-1}+\Omega_{r,0}a^{-2}\bigr).
$$

Equality of the two densities defines [matter-radiation equality](../../../../../../matter-radiation-equality.md), with $a_{\rm eq}=\Omega_{r,0}/\Omega_{m,0}$. Define the dimensionless variable

$$
\boxed{y=\frac a{a_{\rm eq}}=a\frac{\Omega_{m,0}}{\Omega_{r,0}}=\frac{\bar\rho_m}{\bar\rho_r}.}
$$

Substituting $a=a_{\rm eq}y$ gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H^2=C\left(\frac1y+\frac1{y^2}\right),\qquad C=\frac{H_0^2\Omega_{m,0}^2}{\Omega_{r,0}}.}
$$

Both reference density fractions are positive. In the two-component flat model their sum at the reference epoch is one; no dark-energy component is being included in this background calculation.
