<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The initial field values are $\phi_i/M_{\rm Pl}=\sqrt{122n}$, approximately $15.6$, $19.1$ and $22.1$ for the three powers. A simple [monomial inflation potential](../../../../../../monomial-inflation-potential.md) over this super-Planckian field range needs theoretical protection. Generic [Planck-suppressed corrections to inflaton potentials](../../../../../../planck-suppressed-corrections-to-inflaton-potentials.md), such as $c_p\phi^p/M_{\rm Pl}^{p-4}$, need not be small there; they can change the slope and curvature and spoil [slow-roll inflation](../../../../../../slow-roll-approximation.md). [Radiative corrections](../../../../../../radiative-correction.md) and possible couplings to other particles likewise require control. A symmetry, such as an approximate [scalar-field shift symmetry](../../../../../../scalar-field-shift-symmetry.md), or a specified [ultraviolet completion](../../../../../../ultraviolet-completion.md) could supply that protection, but it is not part of the bare monomial model.

A large field value is not by itself a proof that the [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) is Planckian: a sufficiently small $\lambda$ can keep $V\ll M_{\rm Pl}^4$. The issue is control of the [effective field theory](../../../../../../effective-field-theory.md) and stability of the flat potential over its field range, rather than simply comparing the field value with a mass scale.

There is also a global potential issue for $n=3$: $V=\lambda\phi^3$ continued over all real $\phi$ is unbounded below and has no stable minimum at zero. Restricting to $\phi>0$ does not specify what happens when the field reaches that boundary, so a completion is needed for post-inflationary evolution and [reheating](../../../../../../reheating.md). The even powers have a stable minimum but still need interactions that transfer the inflaton energy to a hot bath. These interactions and the resulting [reheating](../../../../../../reheating.md) history also affect the mapping between a pivot scale and the assumed 60 [number of e-folds](../../../../../../number-of-e-folds.md). Therefore the concise theoretical concerns are **control of large-field corrections, a consistent stable completion, and a specified reheating mechanism**.
