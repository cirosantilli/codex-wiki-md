<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The relevant [Penrose singularity theorem](../../../../../../penrose-singularity-theorem.md) states: a [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) time-oriented four-dimensional [globally hyperbolic spacetime](../../../../../../globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md) with a noncompact [Cauchy hypersurface](../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md), a nonempty [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) orientable boundaryless [trapped surface](../../../../../../trapped-surface.md), and the [null convergence condition](../../../../../../null-convergence-condition.md) is **future null-geodesically incomplete**. Under the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md), the [null energy condition](../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md) supplies the curvature hypothesis. A [trapped surface](../../../../../../trapped-surface.md) here is spacelike and has both future normal [null expansions](../../../../../../null-expansion.md) strictly negative. The conclusion is an incomplete [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md), not necessarily a divergent curvature invariant at an identifiable point.

Suppose, for contradiction, that every future [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) is complete. Write $S$ for the [trapped surface](../../../../../../trapped-surface.md) and

$$
E^+(S)=J^+(S)\setminus I^+(S)=\partial I^+(S).
$$

The equality follows because the [causal future](../../../../../../causal-future.md) of a [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) set is [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) in a [globally hyperbolic spacetime](../../../../../../globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md). This [future horismos](../../../../../../future-horismos.md) is a closed [achronal boundary](../../../../../../achronal-boundary.md). It is nonempty: the restriction of a Cauchy time [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) to [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) $S$ has a minimum, and a point at that minimum cannot be chronologically preceded by another point of $S$.

Normalize the two future null normal directions along $S$ against a smooth future timelike field, fixing the affine scale continuously. Their initial [null expansions](../../../../../../null-expansion.md) are continuous and strictly negative. [Compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) of the normalized normal bundle gives a uniform $c>0$ with $\theta_\pm\leq-c$ everywhere on $S$. The preceding [null focusing theorem](../../../../../../null-focusing-theorem.md) forces a [conjugate point to a spacelike surface](../../../../../../conjugate-point-to-a-spacelike-surface.md) along each normal generator within affine length $L=2/c$.

By the supplied boundary-generator result, every point of $E^+(S)$ is reached by an orthogonal future [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) which has no earlier conjugate point. No such boundary generator can remain on the boundary beyond its first focal point. Every point of $E^+(S)$ therefore lies in

$$
K=\left\{\exp_p(\lambda U):p\in S,\ U\text{ a normalized future null normal},\ 0\leq\lambda\leq L\right\}.
$$

The parameter set is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md). Future null completeness makes its [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) flow defined throughout this common finite interval, and smooth dependence on initial data makes its image $K$ [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md). Since $E^+(S)$ is closed and contained in $K$, it is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md). This is the [compactness of the future horismos of a trapped surface](../../../../../../compactness-of-the-future-horismos-of-a-trapped-surface.md) step; a merely pointwise finite bound would not suffice without [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) and uniform normalization.

Choose a smooth complete timelike vector field, obtained if necessary by positive rescaling against a complete auxiliary [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md). Its inextendible [integral curves of a vector field](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) meet a chosen smooth [Cauchy hypersurface](../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md) $C$ exactly once. Projection along those curves defines a [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) $\pi_C:E^+(S)\to C$. The [achronal set](../../../../../../achronal-set.md) property makes this map an [injective function](../../../../../../injective-function.md): two boundary points on the same timelike [integral curve of a vector field](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) would be timelike related.

An [achronal boundary](../../../../../../achronal-boundary.md) is a topological [hypersurface](../../../../../../hypersurface.md) without boundary, even at nonsmooth generator junctions; the submanifold property allowed in the question supplies this fact. Thus domain and codomain are both three-dimensional [topological manifolds](../../../../../../topological-manifold.md) without boundary. [Invariance of domain](../../../../../../invariance-of-domain.md) makes $\pi_C(E^+(S))$ [open](../../../../../../open-set.md) in $C$. [Compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) makes it [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md) in the [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) $C$, and it is nonempty. [Connectedness](../../../../../../connected-space.md) of the [spacetime](../../../../../../spacetime.md) gives [connectedness](../../../../../../connected-space.md) of $C$, so this image is all of $C$. That would make $C$ [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md), contradicting the noncompact [Cauchy hypersurface](../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md) hypothesis. The completeness assumption is false, proving the theorem.

The noncompactness hypothesis and strict trapping are essential to this version. [Global hyperbolicity](../../../../../../globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md) cannot simply be omitted in the [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) and projection steps, and nonpositive initial expansion with zeros does not supply the uniform focusing bound used here.
