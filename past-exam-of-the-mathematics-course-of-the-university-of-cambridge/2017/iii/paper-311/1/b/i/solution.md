<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The common axial [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) fixes the same sign convention for both spins. Initial rest and alignment of the separation with the symmetry axis give no orbital [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md). At large separation, the total axial charge is therefore $J_1+J_2$. Since [axisymmetric radiation carries no axial angular momentum](../../../../../../../axisymmetric-radiation-carries-no-axial-angular-momentum.md), all of this charge remains in the remnant after the radiation has escaped and the exterior has settled:

$$
\boxed{J_f=J_1+J_2.}
$$

Here the spins are signed quantities about the same axis, not their magnitudes. The conclusion assumes the exact [axisymmetry](../../../../../../../axisymmetric-vector-field.md) stipulated throughout the merger and no remaining exterior matter carrying [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md); the final stationary vacuum remnant is a [Kerr black hole](../../../../../../../kerr-black-hole.md) under the usual uniqueness hypotheses.
