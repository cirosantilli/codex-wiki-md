<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [gravitational wave](../../../../../../../gravitational-wave.md) can propagate through a region with vanishing [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md); its gravitational field is not a matter current $T_{ab}m^b$. Nevertheless, exact [axisymmetry](../../../../../../../axisymmetric-vector-field.md) gives an axial [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md), and the vacuum identity $d\star dm=0$ makes the [Komar angular momentum](../../../../../../../komar-angular-momentum.md) equal on enclosing [spacelike submanifolds](../../../../../../../spacelike-submanifold.md) in the same [homology class](../../../../../../../homology-class.md). Applying the same identity to the [spacetime](../../../../../../../spacetime.md) tube between surrounding [spacelike submanifolds](../../../../../../../spacelike-submanifold.md) at different times gives zero net flux of this charge through the tube.

Consequently **[gravitational waves](../../../../../../../gravitational-wave.md) in exact [axisymmetry](../../../../../../../axisymmetric-vector-field.md) carry no net [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) about the symmetry axis**. They can still carry [energy](../../../../../../../energy.md) and decrease the total [mass](../../../../../../../mass.md). In a mode description, rotationally invariant radiative data have zero azimuthal mode number; exact [axisymmetry](../../../../../../../axisymmetric-vector-field.md) therefore excludes the axial [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) flux present in nonaxisymmetric merger radiation. This does not mean there are no [gravitational waves](../../../../../../../gravitational-wave.md), or that radiation cannot transport [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) when the exact symmetry is absent. The relevant conserved charge includes any horizon contributions identified in the preceding [Komar angular momentum with inner boundaries](../../../../../../../komar-angular-momentum-with-inner-boundaries.md) identity.
