<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Minimality of the conjugacy representative implies that $w$ is freely reduced; otherwise [free reduction](../../../../../../free-reduction.md) produces a shorter representative of the same element. It is also a [cyclically reduced word](../../../../../../cyclically-reduced-word.md). If $w=svs^{-1}$ as a [freely reduced word](../../../../../../freely-reduced-word.md) with its first and last letters inverse, the shorter word $v$ represents a conjugate, contradicting minimality. This is the [cyclic reduction of a shortest conjugacy representative](../../../../../../cyclic-reduction-of-a-shortest-conjugacy-representative.md).

The word is nonempty because the element has order greater than one. Since it is cyclically reduced, no cancellation occurs between successive copies in $w^n$, and $w^n$ is a nonempty [freely reduced word](../../../../../../freely-reduced-word.md). It represents the identity because [conjugation](../../../../../../conjugation.md) preserves the order. Apply the defining property of the [Dehn presentation](../../../../../../dehn-presentation.md) to obtain

$$
\boxed{u\text{ is a consecutive segment of }w^n\text{ and of some }r\in R,
\qquad |u|>|r|/2.}
$$

The [relator](../../../../../../relator.md) belongs to the finite symmetrized set fixed in the definition. No assumption that the group itself is torsion-free is made.
