<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A subset is a [quasiconvex subset](../../../../../../quasiconvex-subset.md) with constant $C\geq0$ if every [metric geodesic](../../../../../../metric-geodesic.md) segment with endpoints in it lies within distance $C$ of it. Explicitly,

$$
\boxed{p,q\in N,\ z\in[p,q]\quad\Longrightarrow\quad d_M(z,N)\leq C,}
$$

for every choice of the [metric geodesic](../../../../../../metric-geodesic.md) $[p,q]$. The distance is computed in the ambient [geodesic metric space](../../../../../../geodesic-metric-space.md), not in the intrinsic path metric of $N$. One only asks the [metric geodesic](../../../../../../metric-geodesic.md) to stay close to $N$, rather than requiring it to lie in $N$; the latter makes $N$ a [convex subset of a geodesic metric space](../../../../../../convex-subset-of-a-geodesic-metric-space.md).

The usual convention takes $C\geq0$. If the printed allowance $C\in\mathbb R$ is interpreted literally using the distance inequality, a negative $C$ cannot work for a nonempty $N$, since the constant [metric geodesic](../../../../../../metric-geodesic.md) at a point of $N$ has distance zero. For the empty subset the endpoint condition is vacuous.
