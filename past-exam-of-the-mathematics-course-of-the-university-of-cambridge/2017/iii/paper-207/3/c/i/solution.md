<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a treated patient the recovery [transition intensity](../../../../../../../transition-intensity.md) is $\mu_T=0.25\times2=0.5$ per year. A symptoms spell is a [holding time](../../../../../../../holding-time.md) with an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) of rate $\mu_T$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(\text{length of a treated symptoms spell})=\frac1{0.5}=2\text{ years}.}
$$

The treatment multiplier for onset affects how often symptoms begin, not the duration of a spell once the patient is in state 2, under this homogeneous [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain.md).
