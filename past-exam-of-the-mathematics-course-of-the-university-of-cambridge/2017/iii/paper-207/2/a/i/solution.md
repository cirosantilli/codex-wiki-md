↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [Type I error](../../../../../../../type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) is rejecting a true [null hypothesis](../../../../../../../null-hypothesis.md). Here it means declaring a [statistical association](../../../../../../../statistical-association.md) between the biomarker and bowel cancer when, in the population and under the association model being tested, there is no [statistical association](../../../../../../../statistical-association.md). **It is a false positive association finding**, not merely a large estimation error. A test with level $0.05$ limits this [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) to $0.05$ under the [null hypothesis](../../../../../../../null-hypothesis.md), subject to valid calibration; the [p-value](../../../../../../../p-value.md) is not the [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) that the [null hypothesis](../../../../../../../null-hypothesis.md) is true.
