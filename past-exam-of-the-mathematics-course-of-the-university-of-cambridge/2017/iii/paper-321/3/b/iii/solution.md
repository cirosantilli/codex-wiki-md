<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\Sigma_{d0}$ be the uniform dust background and put $\delta_d=\Sigma_d'/\Sigma_{d0}$. The linearized [continuity equation](../../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) is $\partial_t\delta_d=-\partial_xv'_x$ because background shear has no [divergence](../../../../../../../divergence.md) or axisymmetric [advection](../../../../../../../advection.md). Using the late entrained velocity $v'_x=u\cos\theta$ gives

$$
\partial_t\delta_d=ku\sin\theta,\qquad \boxed{\delta_d=\frac{ku}{\Omega}\cos\theta+C(x).}
$$

Thus the coherent travelling wave has **maximum fractional excess $|ku|/\Omega$**, and its crest density is $\Sigma_{d0}[1+|ku|/\Omega]$ when the stationary part is absent. This is the [density contrast of an entrained dust wave](../../../../../../../density-contrast-of-an-entrained-dust-wave.md), and requires $|ku|/\Omega\ll1$ in the present linear approximation. Its fractional oscillating density amplitude is the same as the gas's long-wavelength amplitude, so the coherent wave produces no additional dust-to-gas concentration ratio at this order.

Velocity damping alone does not erase the time-independent $C(x)$; initial density and the history of the forcing are needed to fix it. The printed data therefore determine the travelling-wave contrast, rather than a unique total late-time concentration for arbitrary initial histories. As an explicit example, switch on the prescribed gas epicycle at $t=0$ with initially uniform dust and $\mathbf v'=0$. The corrected velocity pair gives $v'_x=u(1-e^{-\epsilon\Omega t})\cos\theta$. Integrating continuity then leaves

$$
C(x)=-\frac{ku}{\Omega}\frac{\epsilon^2\cos(kx)+\epsilon\sin(kx)}{1+\epsilon^2}
$$

at late times. It is smaller by order $\epsilon$ in the stipulated weak-coupling regime, and confirms the stated leading wave-crest estimate while showing why arbitrary stationary density structure is not removed by the velocity transient.
