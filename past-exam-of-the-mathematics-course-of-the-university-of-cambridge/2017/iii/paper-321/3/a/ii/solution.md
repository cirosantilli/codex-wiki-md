<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [normal mode](../../../../../../../normal-mode.md) proportional to $e^{ikx-i\omega t}$, the linear equations give

$$
-i\omega U_x=2\Omega U_y-ik\frac{c_s^2}{\Sigma_0}\hat\sigma,\qquad -i\omega U_y=-\frac\Omega2U_x,\qquad -i\omega\hat\sigma=-ik\Sigma_0U_x.
$$

For $\omega\ne0$, eliminate $U_y=-i\Omega U_x/(2\omega)$ and $\hat\sigma=k\Sigma_0U_x/\omega$. The [density-wave dispersion relation in a non-self-gravitating disk](../../../../../../../density-wave-dispersion-relation-in-a-non-self-gravitating-disk.md) is

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=\Omega^2+c_s^2k^2.}
$$

The $\Omega^2$ term is the [radial epicyclic frequency](../../../../../../../radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) squared for [Keplerian rotation](../../../../../../../keplerian-disk.md): [Coriolis force](../../../../../../../coriolis-force.md) and background shear restore an orbital displacement. The $c_s^2k^2$ term is acoustic restoration by a [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md). Long wavelengths are mainly [epicyclic motion](../../../../../../../epicyclic-motion.md); short wavelengths are mainly [acoustic waves](../../../../../../../acoustic-wave.md). The given model contains no disk self-gravity term.

For completeness, the full three-variable determinant is proportional to $\omega(\omega^2-\Omega^2-c_s^2k^2)$. Its additional $\omega=0$ branch has $U_x=0$ and $2\Omega U_y=ikc_s^2\hat\sigma/\Sigma_0$. This is a stationary [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md)/Coriolis balance, the [zero-frequency balanced mode of an axisymmetric disk](../../../../../../../zero-frequency-balanced-mode-of-an-axisymmetric-disk.md), rather than a propagating density wave. At $k=0$ it reduces to a uniform surface-density change. Dividing by $\omega$ is legitimate only for the wave branches just derived.
