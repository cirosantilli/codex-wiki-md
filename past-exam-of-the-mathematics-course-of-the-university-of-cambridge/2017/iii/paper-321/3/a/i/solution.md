<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [razor-thin disk approximation](../../../../../../../razor-thin-disk-approximation.md) uses vertically integrated [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) and [surface density of a disk](../../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md). To close the printed equations, interpret the [barotropic equation of state](../../../../../../../barotropic-equation-of-state.md) as $P=P(\Sigma)$, or supply an equivalent fixed vertical conversion from the printed $\rho$ to $\Sigma$. Without that conversion, the literal $P=P(\rho)$ does not close this two-dimensional system. Set $c_s^2=(dP/d\Sigma)_0>0$, and write $\Sigma=\Sigma_0+\sigma$, $\mathbf u=\mathbf u_0+\mathbf u'$, $h'=P'/\Sigma_0=c_s^2\sigma/\Sigma_0$. [Axisymmetric flow](../../../../../../../axisymmetric-flow.md) here means $\partial_y=0$, so background [advection](../../../../../../../advection.md) vanishes. The background shear enters through $\mathbf u'\cdot\nabla\mathbf u_0=-3\Omega u'_x\mathbf e_y/2$. The complete [linearized equations](../../../../../../../linearized-equation.md) are

$$
\boxed{\partial_tu'_x=2\Omega u'_y-\partial_xh',\qquad \partial_tu'_y=-\frac\Omega2u'_x,\qquad \partial_t\sigma=-\Sigma_0\partial_xu'_x,\qquad h'=\frac{c_s^2}{\Sigma_0}\sigma.}
$$

These are the compressible [Keplerian shearing sheet](../../../../../../../keplerian-shearing-sheet.md) equations for an [inertial-acoustic wave](../../../../../../../inertial-acoustic-wave.md). No perturbed self-gravity is present in the given force law, so no gravitational potential perturbation is to be added. This closure is the [barotropic closure of a razor-thin disk](../../../../../../../barotropic-closure-of-a-razor-thin-disk.md).
