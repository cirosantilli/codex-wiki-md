<h1 id="3/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Since the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) of $|z-k|$ is $\operatorname{sgn}(z-k)$ away from $k$, choose the [Kruzhkov entropy flux](../../../../../../../kruzhkov-entropy-flux.md)

$$
\boxed{\psi_k(z)=\operatorname{sgn}(z-k)\bigl(f(z)-f(k)\bigr),\qquad\psi_k(k)=0.}
$$

On either side of $k$ its [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) is $f'(z)\eta_k'(z)$. The factor $f(z)-f(k)$ vanishes at the change of sign, so the function is continuous and there is no extra delta contribution to its weak [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md). Adding a constant to the flux changes none of the test integrals. Substitution into the entropy inequality pairs $|u-k|$ with this flux, not with an arbitrary antiderivative unrelated to the chosen entropy.
