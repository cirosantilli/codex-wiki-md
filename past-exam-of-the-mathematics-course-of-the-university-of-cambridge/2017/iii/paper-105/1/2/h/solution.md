<h1 id="1/2/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

For each [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $g$, take the [real analytic](../../../../../../../real-analytic-function.md) adjoint solution on the common cap from the preceding part. On the flat boundary face the full [boundary jet](../../../../../../../boundary-jet.md) of $u$ vanishes; on the curved face that of $v$ vanishes. The [complete boundary-jet condition for formal adjoints](../../../../../../../complete-boundary-jet-condition-for-formal-adjoints.md), proved under its corrected hypothesis, therefore applies. The two corner points have zero boundary measure, and the cap is a bounded piecewise smooth domain. It follows that

$$
\int_{\omega_\varepsilon}ug=\int_{\omega_\varepsilon}uP^*v=\int_{\omega_\varepsilon}(Pu)v=0.
$$

By the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](../../../../../../../weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md), [polynomials](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) uniformly approximate $\bar u$ on the compact cap closure. Taking the limit gives $\int_{\omega_\varepsilon}|u|^2=0$. Continuity then gives $u=0$ pointwise in the cap. For real $u$, simply approximate $u$ itself.

A positive cap alone is not a neighborhood of the origin. Apply the same argument after reflecting $x\mapsto-x$; the reflected operator remains [real analytic](../../../../../../../real-analytic-function.md) and non-characteristic. Shrinking the two parameters to their minimum proves vanishing on

$$
\boxed{\{(x,y):|x|+y^2<\varepsilon_*,\ \varepsilon_*>0\}.}
$$

This set is open and contains the origin, including the flat face where the value data vanish. The argument is the [parabolic-cap proof of Holmgren uniqueness](../../../../../../../parabolic-cap-proof-of-holmgren-uniqueness.md), and it establishes [Holmgren uniqueness theorem](../../../../../../../holmgren-uniqueness-theorem.md) for nonanalytic $C^m$ solutions here.
