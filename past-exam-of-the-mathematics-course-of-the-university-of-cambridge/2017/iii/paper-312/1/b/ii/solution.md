The result above gives $K=-3H+\kappa$. Since the shear is trace-free and already first order, it cannot contribute linearly to the quadratic [tensor contraction](../../../../../../../tensor-contraction.md) $K_{ij}K^{ij}=\operatorname{tr}(K^2)$. Therefore

$$
K^2=9H^2-6H\kappa,\qquad K_{ij}K^{ij}=3H^2-2H\kappa,
$$

up to second-order [scalar cosmological perturbations](../../../../../../../scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md). Combining these with the supplied spatial [Ricci scalar](../../../../../../../ricci-scalar.md) gives the [Hamiltonian constraint](../../../../../../../hamiltonian-constraint.md)

$$
6H^2+4\Delta\Phi-4H\kappa=16\pi G(\bar\rho+\delta\rho).
$$

Its background part is the flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) $6H^2=16\pi G\bar\rho$. Subtracting that equation proves the [linearized cosmological Hamiltonian constraint](../../../../../../../linearized-cosmological-hamiltonian-constraint.md):

$$
\boxed{\Delta\Phi-H\kappa=4\pi G\delta\rho.}
$$

Here $\rho$ is the [energy density](../../../../../../../energy-density.md) measured by the hypersurface normal, as required by the normal-normal projection of the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md).
