<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using the normalization of the timelike [unit normal](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md), the [spatial projection tensor](../../../../../../../spatial-projection-tensor.md) satisfies

$$
P^\mu{}_\alpha n_\mu=n_\alpha+(n^\mu n_\mu)n_\alpha=0.
$$

Likewise $P^\mu{}_\alpha n^\alpha=0$ and $P^\mu{}_\alpha P^\alpha{}_\nu=P^\mu{}_\nu$. The [spacetime](../../../../../../../spacetime.md) form of the [induced metric](../../../../../../../induced-metric.md) is therefore

$$
h_{\alpha\beta}=P^\mu{}_\alpha P^\nu{}_\beta g_{\mu\nu}=g_{\alpha\beta}+n_\alpha n_\beta.
$$

Its restriction to the slice is the given three-metric. [Metric compatibility](../../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) of the four-dimensional [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) gives

$$
\nabla_\ell h_{\alpha\beta}=(\nabla_\ell n_\alpha)n_\beta+n_\alpha\nabla_\ell n_\beta.
$$

Apply the three projectors defining the [spatial covariant derivative](../../../../../../../spatial-covariant-derivative.md). In the first summand $P^\beta{}_j n_\beta=0$, and in the second $P^\alpha{}_i n_\alpha=0$. Both summands vanish, so

$$
\boxed{P^\mu{}_\alpha n_\mu=0,\qquad h_{ij|k}=0.}
$$

This proves the [metric compatibility of the spatial covariant derivative](../../../../../../../metric-compatibility-of-the-spatial-covariant-derivative.md) without treating the [spatial projection tensor](../../../../../../../spatial-projection-tensor.md) itself as covariantly constant.
