<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the local [orthonormal tetrad](../../../../../../orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md), the components of the [photon number current](../../../../../../photon-number-current.md) are

$$
J^{\hat0}=\int f\,d^3p=n_\gamma,\qquad J^{\hat i}=\int f e^i\,d^3p.
$$

They are respectively the [photon number density](../../../../../../photon-number-density.md) and the [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) number flux. Thus **$J^\mu$ is the [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) number four-current**, not an [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) current or an [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge.md) current. [Photons](../../../../../../photon.md) carry no [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge.md). For the isotropic background,

$$
\bar n_\gamma=\frac{4\pi}{a^3}\int_0^\infty\epsilon^2\bar f(\epsilon)\,d\epsilon.
$$

Integrating the covariant [Boltzmann equation](../../../../../../boltzmann-equation.md) over the invariant mass-shell measure, with vanishing momentum-boundary flux, gives

$$
\nabla_\mu J^\mu=\int\frac{d^3p}{E}\,C[f],
$$

where $C[f]$ is the collision operator in the covariant streaming equation. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\nabla_\mu J^\mu=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\int\frac{d^3p}{E}\,C[f]=0.}
$$

Collisionless [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) propagation satisfies this condition, as do [Thomson scattering](../../../../../../thomson-scattering.md) and ordinary [Compton scattering](../../../../../../compton-scattering.md) when all participating [photons](../../../../../../photon.md) are counted: each collision has one incoming and one outgoing [photon](../../../../../../photon.md). [Photon emission](../../../../../../photon-emission.md), [photon absorption](../../../../../../photon-absorption.md), [bremsstrahlung](../../../../../../bremsstrahlung.md), [double Compton scattering](../../../../../../double-compton-scattering.md) and [electron-positron annihilation in cosmology](../../../../../../electron-positron-annihilation-in-cosmology.md) or its inverse generally give a nonzero number source. A cancellation of net creation and destruction can nevertheless make the integrated source vanish even when individual number-changing reactions occur. [Cosmological redshift](../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) itself changes [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) energy, not [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) number; in an expanding homogeneous universe a conserved [photon number current](../../../../../../photon-number-current.md) gives $\bar n_\gamma\propto a^{-3}$.
