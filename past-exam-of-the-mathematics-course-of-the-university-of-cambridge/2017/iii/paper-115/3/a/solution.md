<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) of a smooth $n$-dimensional manifold can be specified by a nowhere-vanishing smooth $n$-form $\omega$: an ordered tangent basis is positive precisely when $\omega$ evaluates positively on it. Multiplication by a positive [smooth function](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) preserves the [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md). This is the differential-form formulation of the [equivalent formulations of orientability of a smooth manifold](../../../../../../equivalent-formulations-of-orientability-of-a-smooth-manifold.md).

Use the standard convention that the [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) connected manifold is nonempty and has no boundary. Choose a positive [volume form](../../../../../../volume-form.md) $\omega$ for its [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md). It is a [closed differential form](../../../../../../closed-differential-form.md), because there are no nonzero $(n+1)$-forms. For $n>0$, compactness makes its integral finite, and positivity on a coordinate neighborhood together with nonnegativity elsewhere gives

$$
\int_M\omega>0.
$$

If $\omega=d\eta$, the [Generalized Stokes theorem](../../../../../../generalized-stokes-theorem.md) would give $\int_M\omega=\int_{\partial M}\eta=0$, a contradiction. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[\omega]\ne0\text{ in }H^n_{\mathrm{dR}}(M).}
$$

For $n=0$, a nonempty connected manifold is a point, and $H^0_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)=\mathbb R$ directly. The boundary hypothesis matters: a closed interval is an oriented [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) connected [manifold with boundary](../../../../../../manifold-with-boundary.md) with $H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}=0$. Thus the result is about ordinary boundaryless manifolds, not an unrestricted version including boundary.
