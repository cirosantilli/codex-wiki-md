<h1 id="3/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $V=C$ for each integral [projective curve](../../../../../../../projective-curve.md) in $X$. The assumed positivity gives $D\cdot C>0$, hence certainly $D\cdot C\ge0$. By the definition of a [nef divisor](../../../../../../../nef-line-bundle.md),

$$
\boxed{D\in\operatorname{Nef}(X).}
$$

This is only the curve test. It does not already prove [ampleness](../../../../../../../ample-cartier-divisor.md): positivity against each individual curve can fail to be uniform on limiting classes in the [closed cone of curves](../../../../../../../closed-cone-of-curves.md). The hypotheses in higher dimensions, used in the next sections, are what rule out this failure.
