<h1 id="3/iii/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose $0<\epsilon\ll1$ as in (c). Then

$$
D=(D-\epsilon A)+\epsilon A
$$

is the sum of a [nef divisor](../../../../../../../nef-line-bundle.md) and an [ample real divisor](../../../../../../../ample-real-divisor.md). The [nef-plus-ample ampleness lemma](../../../../../../../nef-plus-ample-ampleness-lemma.md) gives

$$
\boxed{D\text{ is ample}.}
$$

For clarity, this last lemma follows from [Kleiman's criterion](../../../../../../../kleiman-s-criterion.md) and the [convex cone](../../../../../../../convex-cone.md) property: if $x$ is an interior point of the [nef cone](../../../../../../../nef-cone.md) and $y$ lies in that cone, translating a small neighbourhood of $x$ by $y$ stays in the cone. Thus $x+y$ remains in its interior, which is the [ample cone](../../../../../../../ample-cone.md) on a [projective scheme](../../../../../../../projective-scheme.md).

The complete argument proves the [real Nakai–Moishezon criterion](../../../../../../../real-nakai-moishezon-criterion.md) rather than assuming it: curve positivity gives nefness, rational approximation and a proved section-count inequality give bigness, induction and the finite-support argument give a uniform [ample](../../../../../../../ample-line-bundle.md) subtraction, and the nef-plus-ample lemma concludes [ampleness](../../../../../../../ample-cartier-divisor.md). The zero-dimensional case is automatic, and [ampleness on reduced components](../../../../../../../ampleness-on-reduced-components.md) handles reducibility and nilpotents.
