<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [ample real divisor](../../../../../../ample-real-divisor.md) is a finite positive real combination of [ample Cartier divisors](../../../../../../ample-cartier-divisor.md):

$$
D=\sum_{j=1}^r a_jA_j,\qquad a_j>0.
$$

Equivalently its [numerical class](../../../../../../real-numerical-divisor-classes.md) lies in the [ample cone](../../../../../../ample-cone.md). This is a numerical condition even when the coefficients are irrational; it does not mean that some integer multiple of $D$ must be an integral divisor.

On an integral [projective variety](../../../../../../projective-variety.md), a [big real divisor](../../../../../../big-real-divisor.md) is a finite positive real combination of [big Cartier divisors](../../../../../../big-divisor.md). Equivalently, by the real form of [Kodaira's lemma](../../../../../../kodaira-s-lemma.md),

$$
\boxed{D\sim_{\mathbb R}B+E,\quad B\text{ ample real},\quad E\text{ effective real Cartier}.}
$$

For an integral [Cartier divisor](../../../../../../cartier-divisor-split.md), bigness means maximal section-growth order $h^0(X,mD)\ge c m^{\dim X}$ along sufficiently divisible positive $m$, or [Iitaka dimension](../../../../../../iitaka-dimension.md) $\dim X$. The [real linear equivalence of divisors](../../../../../../real-linear-equivalence-of-divisors.md) formulation permits finite positive combinations of effective [Cartier divisors](../../../../../../cartier-divisor-split.md), whose supports are codimension one. The definitions and the ample-plus-effective formulation on integral varieties are discussed in [Fujino's notes on big real divisors](https://www.math.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~fujino/big-r-divisor5.pdf).

For the paper's assertions on a general [projective scheme](../../../../../../projective-scheme.md), use [componentwise bigness on a projective scheme](../../../../../../componentwise-bigness-on-a-projective-scheme.md): require bigness on every reduced irreducible component. All arguments below can then be carried out on those finitely many integral components; [ampleness](../../../../../../ample-cartier-divisor.md) is also detected there. On a reducible scheme, merely asking for maximal total section growth on one component is insufficient. For example $X=\mathbb P^2\amalg\mathbb P^2$, with $D$ restricting to $\mathcal O(1)$ on the first component and $\mathcal O(-1)$ on the second, has quadratic total section growth, but negative intersection with every line in the second component. No finite collection of codimension-one [subvarieties](../../../../../../closed-subvariety.md) can contain all those lines. Thus that weaker meaning would make part (ii) false. In dimension zero the positivity statements are vacuous and every [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) is [ample](../../../../../../ample-line-bundle.md); the compatible bigness convention also regards it as big.
