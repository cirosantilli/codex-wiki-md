<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Nakai–Moishezon criterion](../../../../../../nakai-moishezon-criterion.md) says that a [Cartier divisor](../../../../../../cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$ on a [projective scheme](../../../../../../projective-scheme.md) is [ample](../../../../../../ample-line-bundle.md) exactly when

$$
\boxed{D^{\dim V}\cdot[V]>0\quad\text{for every positive-dimensional integral closed subvariety }V.}
$$

In particular the test includes each positive-dimensional irreducible component. Here and below, “proper” means proper over the ground [field](../../../../../../field.md), not necessarily a strict subset of $X$. Interpreting it as a strict subset would make the later criteria false even for an integral [projective curve](../../../../../../projective-curve.md), whose strict closed [subvarieties](../../../../../../closed-subvariety.md) have dimension zero.

The [intersection product](../../../../../../intersection-product-of-cartier-divisors.md) of [Cartier divisors](../../../../../../cartier-divisor-split.md) with cycles depends only on their [numerical equivalence of divisors](../../../../../../numerical-equivalence-of-divisors.md) classes. One way to see this is to intersect all but one factor first, obtaining a one-cycle; replacing the remaining factor by a numerically equivalent divisor does not change its pairing with that cycle. Multilinearity then handles replacement of every factor. Consequently $D\equiv D'$ makes all the displayed numbers equal. Thus

$$
\boxed{D\equiv D'\Longrightarrow(D\text{ ample}\iff D'\text{ ample}).}
$$

The criterion applies to the integral reduced [subvarieties](../../../../../../closed-subvariety.md) of a possibly [nonreduced scheme](../../../../../../nonreduced-scheme.md); [ampleness on reduced components](../../../../../../ampleness-on-reduced-components.md) explains why the nilpotent structure does not change this condition.
