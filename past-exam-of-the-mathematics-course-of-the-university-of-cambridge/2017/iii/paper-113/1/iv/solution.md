<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use the [ring homomorphism](../../../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) $k[t]\to k[x]$ given by $t\mapsto x^2$. The source [scheme](../../../../../../scheme.md) $X=\operatorname{Spec}k[x]$ is an [integral scheme](../../../../../../integral-scheme.md). At the rational point $y=(t)$, its [residue field](../../../../../../residue-field.md) is $k$, and its [scheme-theoretic fibre](../../../../../../scheme-theoretic-fibre.md) is

$$
\boxed{X_y=\operatorname{Spec}(k[x]\otimes_{k[t]}k)
=\operatorname{Spec}(k[x]/(x^2)).}
$$

The class of $x$ is nonzero but has square zero, so this is not a [reduced scheme](../../../../../../reduced-scheme.md). This example works over every field. An integral total space therefore need not have reduced fibres.
