<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the [principal open subscheme](../../../../../../principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(a)\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}A$, the induced [morphism of sheaves](../../../../../../morphism-of-sheaves.md) is the [ring homomorphism](../../../../../../ring-homomorphism.md)

$$
A_a\longrightarrow B_{\alpha(a)},\qquad \frac{b}{a^n}\longmapsto\frac{\alpha(b)}{\alpha(a)^n}.
$$

If $\alpha$ is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md), this map is injective by [exactness of localization](../../../../../../exactness-of-localization.md): it is the localization of the injective $A$-module map $A\to B$. Explicitly, if the displayed fraction vanishes, some power of $\alpha(a)$ annihilates $\alpha(b)$; injectivity gives $a^mb=0$, so the original fraction also vanishes. The principal opens form an [open basis](../../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md), so the [kernel sheaf](../../../../../../kernel-sheaf.md) is zero on a basis and hence zero everywhere. Conversely, an injective [morphism of sheaves](../../../../../../morphism-of-sheaves.md) induces an injection on [global sections](../../../../../../global-section.md). On the whole [affine scheme](../../../../../../affine-scheme.md) these sections are $A\to B$, exactly $\alpha$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\alpha\text{ is injective}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \phi\text{ is injective}.}
$$
