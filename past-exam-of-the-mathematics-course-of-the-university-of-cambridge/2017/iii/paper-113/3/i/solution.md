<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $K$ be the function field of the [integral scheme](../../../../../../integral-scheme.md) $X$ and choose a nonzero [rational section of a line bundle](../../../../../../rational-section-of-a-line-bundle.md) $s\in\mathcal L_\eta$. Choose an open trivializing cover $U_i$ with generators $e_i$ for the [invertible sheaf](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) $\mathcal L$. On each nonempty $U_i$, write $s=a_ie_i$ with $a_i\in K^*$. If $e_i=u_{ij}e_j$ on an overlap, then $a_j=a_iu_{ij}$, so $a_i/a_j=u_{ij}^{-1}$ is a regular unit. Consequently the local rational functions $a_i$ define a [Cartier divisor](../../../../../../cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$.

The [divisor line bundle](../../../../../../divisor-line-bundle.md) $\mathcal O_X(D)$ restricts to $a_i^{-1}\mathcal O_{U_i}$. Define its local isomorphism from $\mathcal L$ by $e_i\mapsto a_i^{-1}$. On an overlap,

$$
u_{ij}a_j^{-1}=a_i^{-1},
$$

so the maps glue. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L\cong\mathcal O_X(D).}
$$

The rational section maps to the rational function $1$, which also fixes the sign convention for $D$. Choosing a different nonzero rational section changes $D$ by a [principal Cartier divisor](../../../../../../principal-cartier-divisor.md), so its [linear equivalence of Cartier divisors](../../../../../../linear-equivalence-of-cartier-divisors.md) class is unchanged. No projectivity or Noetherian hypothesis is required.
