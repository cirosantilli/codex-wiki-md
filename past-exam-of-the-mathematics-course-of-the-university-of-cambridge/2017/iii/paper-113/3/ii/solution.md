<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each point $x$, choose a [global section](../../../../../../global-section.md) $s$ whose [germ](../../../../../../germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) $s_x$ generates the rank-one free $\mathcal O_{X,x}$-module $\mathcal L_x$. The section $s\otimes s$ is global, and [tensor products](../../../../../../tensor-product.md) commute with taking [stalks](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md). Therefore

$$
(s\otimes s)_x=s_x\otimes s_x
$$

generates $(\mathcal L\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal L)_x$. Thus **the tensor square is globally generated**. More generally, the [tensor product of globally generated line bundles](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-globally-generated-line-bundles.md) is globally generated: choose a generator section for each factor at the point and tensor them. This works on every scheme, including nonreduced schemes.
