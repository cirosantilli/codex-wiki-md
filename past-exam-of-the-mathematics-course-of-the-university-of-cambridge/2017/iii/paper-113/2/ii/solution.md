<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

First suppose $X$ is a nonempty [integral scheme](../../../../../../integral-scheme.md), with [generic point](../../../../../../generic-point.md) $\eta$ and function field $K=\mathcal O_{X,\eta}$. Since taking [stalks](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) preserves the inclusion $\mathcal I\hookrightarrow\mathcal O_X$, local freeness of rank two would give an injective $K$-linear map

$$
K^2\cong\mathcal I_\eta\hookrightarrow\mathcal O_{X,\eta}=K,
$$

contradicting the [dimension of a vector space](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md).

For a nonempty [Noetherian scheme](../../../../../../noetherian-scheme.md) that is reduced, there are finitely many [irreducible components](../../../../../../irreducible-component.md). Choose one, and remove the union of the others. The resulting nonempty open subscheme is irreducible and reduced, hence integral. Restricting the proposed [locally free sheaf](../../../../../../locally-free-sheaf.md) to it gives the contradiction above. Therefore **no locally free ideal of rank two exists on a nonempty reduced Noetherian scheme**.

The [rank bound for locally free ideals on reduced schemes](../../../../../../rank-bound-for-locally-free-ideals-on-reduced-schemes.md) in fact removes the Noetherian assumption: on an affine open where the rank is fixed, localization at a [minimal prime ideal](../../../../../../minimal-prime-ideal.md) gives a field, so a free ideal has rank at most one. Nonemptiness is necessary for the literal statement: on the empty scheme the zero sheaf is vacuously locally free of every stipulated rank. The question is interpreted with this usual nonempty hypothesis.
