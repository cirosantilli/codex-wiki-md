↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first [Young–Jucys–Murphy element](../../../../../../../jucys-murphy-element.md) is the empty sum $X_1=0$. Applying it to the nonzero simultaneous [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) defining a point of the [joint spectrum](../../../../../../../joint-spectrum.md) gives $0=a_1v$. Hence

$$
\boxed{a_1=0.}
$$

For the remaining spectral arguments use the following local facts, without assuming the [restriction branching rule for a symmetric group](../../../../../../../restriction-branching-rule-for-a-symmetric-group.md): consecutive [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are distinct; a difference $a_{r+1}-a_r=\pm1$ makes $s_r=(r\ r+1)$ act on the corresponding line as $\pm1$; every other adjacent interchange gives another point of the [joint spectrum](../../../../../../../joint-spectrum.md) in the same [irreducible representation](../../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md). These are the [local spectral rules for Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](../../../../../../../local-spectral-rules-for-young-jucys-murphy-elements.md). They follow from $s_rX_rs_r+s_r=X_{r+1}$ and the one- or two-dimensional analysis of the two neighboring [eigenspaces](../../../../../../../eigenspace.md). They are the appropriate local results used below.
