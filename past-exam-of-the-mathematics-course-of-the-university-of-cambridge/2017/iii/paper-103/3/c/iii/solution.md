<h1 id="3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [hook partition](../../../../../../../hook-partition.md) $\lambda=(n-k,1^k)$ has cell contents

$$
0,1,\ldots,n-k-1,-1,-2,\ldots,-k.
$$

In a [standard Young tableau](../../../../../../../standard-young-tableau.md), the unique zero-content cell is $(1,1)$ and contains $1$. The other [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are precisely the remaining contents, in whatever order the tableau supplies. Their product is independent of that order:

$$
\boxed{X_2\cdots X_nw_T=(-1)^k k!(n-k-1)!\,w_T.}
$$

The scalar is nonzero over the [complex numbers](../../../../../../../complex-number.md). For the single row or single column, one of the factorials is $0!=1$; the formula still gives $(n-1)!$ or $(-1)^{n-1}(n-1)!$, respectively.
