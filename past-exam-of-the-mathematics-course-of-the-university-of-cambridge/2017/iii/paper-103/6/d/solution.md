<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Specht module](../../../../../../specht-module.md) construction gives a nonzero inclusion $V^\lambda\hookrightarrow M^\lambda$ over the [complex numbers](../../../../../../complex-number.md): a [polytabloid](../../../../../../polytabloid.md) has coefficient one at its original [tabloid](../../../../../../tabloid.md), because the row and column stabilizers have trivial intersection, and its span is the corresponding [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md). This establishes $a=M(\lambda,\lambda)\geq1$ without using the multiplicity-one conclusion. Similarly $a'=M(\lambda',\lambda')\geq1$.

By the corrected [tensor identity for induced representations](../../../../../../tensor-identity-for-induced-representations.md) and the conjugate-partition sign twist, the multiplicity of $V^\lambda$ in $\widetilde M^{\lambda'}$ is $a'$. The [character inner product](../../../../../../character-inner-product.md) already computed is the sum of products of multiplicities of common [irreducible representations](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md). Thus

$$
1=\langle\chi_{M^\lambda},\chi_{\widetilde M^{\lambda'}}\rangle\geq aa'\geq1.
$$

Both are positive [integers](../../../../../../integer.md), so $a=a'=1$. In particular

$$
\boxed{M(\lambda,\lambda)=1.}
$$

This deduction avoids assuming the full [Young's rule](../../../../../../young-s-rule.md), which would already contain the answer. Alternatively, the permitted one-dimensional [R-module homomorphism](../../../../../../module-homomorphism.md) space gives the same character inner product immediately.
