<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The projections $P_T$ in the [Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra](../../../../../../../gelfand-tsetlin-algebra.md) satisfy

$$
P_TP_R=\delta_{TR}P_T,\qquad \sum_TP_T=1.
$$

Consequently the map sending a diagonal element to its scalar on each [Gelfand–Tsetlin basis](../../../../../../../gelfand-tsetlin-basis.md) vector is a [ring isomorphism](../../../../../../../ring-isomorphism.md)

$$
\boxed{GZ_n\cong\prod_T\mathbb C.}
$$

Each factor is a [simple module](../../../../../../../irreducible-module.md) over this algebra, and the algebra is their [direct sum](../../../../../../../direct-sum.md) as a module over itself. This proves that $GZ_n$ is a [semisimple algebra](../../../../../../../semisimple-algebra.md). The argument uses its explicit diagonal structure; being a subalgebra of a [semisimple algebra](../../../../../../../semisimple-algebra.md) by itself would not suffice.
