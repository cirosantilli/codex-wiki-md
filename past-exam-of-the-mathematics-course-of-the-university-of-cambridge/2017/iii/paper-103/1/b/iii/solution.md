↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [primitive idempotent](../../../../../../../primitive-idempotent.md) products used above show that the [Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra](../../../../../../../gelfand-tsetlin-algebra.md) is generated by the centers at all levels: those products give every diagonal [matrix unit](../../../../../../../matrix-unit.md), whereas each center acts diagonally. Hence

$$
\boxed{GZ_n=\operatorname{alg}(Z_0,Z_1,\ldots,Z_n).}
$$
