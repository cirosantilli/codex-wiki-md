<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](../../../../../../jucys-murphy-element.md) are

$$
X_1=0,\qquad X_r=\sum_{a=1}^{r-1}(a\ r)\quad(r\geq2),
$$

with products of [permutations](../../../../../../permutation.md) composed from right to left. Fix $i<j$. In $[X_i,X_j]$, all pairs of disjoint [transpositions](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) commute. The only terms left, for each $a<i$, are

$$
[(a\ i),(i\ j)]+[(a\ i),(a\ j)]=0.
$$

Indeed $(a\ i)(i\ j)=(a\ j)(a\ i)$ and $(i\ j)(a\ i)=(a\ i)(a\ j)$. Summing directly proves

$$
\boxed{[X_i,X_j]=0\qquad(1\leq i,j\leq n).}
$$

Thus all the [Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](../../../../../../jucys-murphy-element.md) commute.
