<h1 id="1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the second generation assertion use the permitted [Olshanskii centralizer lemma](../../../../../../../olshanskii-centralizer-lemma.md), in the precise form

$$
C_{\mathbb C S_r}(\mathbb C S_{r-1})=\operatorname{alg}(Z_{r-1},X_r).
$$

In particular $Z_r\subseteq\operatorname{alg}(Z_{r-1},X_r)$. Induction puts every $Z_r$ in $\operatorname{alg}(1,X_1,\ldots,X_r)$. Conversely, if $A_r$ is the sum of all [transpositions](../../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) in $S_r$, then $A_r\in Z_r$ and $X_r=A_r-A_{r-1}\in GZ_n$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{GZ_n=\operatorname{alg}(1,X_1,\ldots,X_n).}
$$

Here generation is unital; the zero generator $X_1$ causes no problem.
