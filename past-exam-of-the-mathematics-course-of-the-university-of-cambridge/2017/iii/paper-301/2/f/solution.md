<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

In either the displayed [Dirac representation of the gamma matrices](../../../../../../dirac-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md) or the displayed [Weyl representation of the gamma matrices](../../../../../../weyl-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md), the spatial blocks give

$$
[\gamma^j,\gamma^k]=-\begin{pmatrix}[\sigma^j,\sigma^k]&0\\0&[\sigma^j,\sigma^k]\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The [Pauli matrix commutator identity](../../../../../../pauli-matrix-commutator-identity.md) $[\sigma^j,\sigma^k]=2i\epsilon^{jkl}\sigma^l$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{S^{jk}=-\frac i2\epsilon^{jkl}\begin{pmatrix}\sigma^l&0\\0&\sigma^l\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) is normalized by $\epsilon^{123}=1$. The constant is $-i/2$, consistent with rotation generators represented by [skew-Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md) under the chosen [Lorentz algebra](../../../../../../lorentz-algebra.md) convention. For $j=k$ both sides vanish automatically.
