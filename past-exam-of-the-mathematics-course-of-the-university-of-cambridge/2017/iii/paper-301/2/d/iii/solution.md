<h1 id="2/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply the [commutator derivation identity](../../../../../../../commutator-derivation-identity.md) $[S,AB]=[S,A]B+A[S,B]$ and the preceding [gamma matrix](../../../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) transformation identity. Keeping the matrix order unchanged gives

$$
[S^{\mu\nu},\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma]
=\gamma^\mu\eta^{\nu\rho}\gamma^\sigma-\gamma^\nu\eta^{\mu\rho}\gamma^\sigma+\gamma^\rho\gamma^\mu\eta^{\nu\sigma}-\gamma^\rho\gamma^\nu\eta^{\mu\sigma}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{E=1,\quad F=-1,\quad G=1,\quad H=-1.}
$$

The order of the [gamma matrices](../../../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) matters, since they generally do not commute. The PDF has the four ordinary metric contractions shown here; the conversion's stray powers such as $\eta^\nu\rho^\sigma$ are not mathematical expressions to retain.
