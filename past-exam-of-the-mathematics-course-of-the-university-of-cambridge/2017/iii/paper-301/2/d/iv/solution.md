<h1 id="2/d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Since $S^{\rho\sigma}=\frac14[\gamma^\rho,\gamma^\sigma]$, apply the [commutator derivation identity](../../../../../../../commutator-derivation-identity.md) to this [commutator](../../../../../../../commutator.md) and insert part (ii):

$$
\begin{aligned}
[S^{\mu\nu},S^{\rho\sigma}]
&=\frac14\left([[S^{\mu\nu},\gamma^\rho],\gamma^\sigma]+[\gamma^\rho,[S^{\mu\nu},\gamma^\sigma]]\right)\\
&=\eta^{\nu\rho}S^{\mu\sigma}-\eta^{\mu\rho}S^{\nu\sigma}+\eta^{\nu\sigma}S^{\rho\mu}-\eta^{\mu\sigma}S^{\rho\nu}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is exactly the printed [Lorentz algebra](../../../../../../../lorentz-algebra.md) relation, including its final two signs; their alternative appearance in other formulas comes from $S^{\rho\mu}=-S^{\mu\rho}$. Therefore **the matrices S form a representation of the Lorentz algebra**. Their definition contains no extra factor of $i$, so the spatial rotation generators below are represented by [skew-Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md). Adding a factor of $i$ would change the convention for the [Lie algebra](../../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) structure constants and cannot be done silently.
