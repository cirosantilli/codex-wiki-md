<h1 id="2/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [Clifford algebra](../../../../../../../clifford-algebra.md) to write $\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu=2\eta^{\mu\nu}I_4-\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu$. The [Lorentz generator from gamma-matrix commutators](../../../../../../../lorentz-generator-from-gamma-matrix-commutators.md) is therefore

$$
S^{\mu\nu}=\frac14[\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu]=\frac12\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu-\frac12\eta^{\mu\nu}I_4,
\qquad
\boxed{A=\frac12,\quad B=-\frac12.}
$$

This also checks the diagonal case $\mu=\nu$, where $S^{\mu\mu}=0$ because $(\gamma^\mu)^2=\eta^{\mu\mu}I_4$. The metric term in the printed expression is understood to multiply the [identity matrix](../../../../../../../identity-matrix.md). The generators are antisymmetric in their two spacetime indices.
