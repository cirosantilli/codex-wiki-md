↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [gamma matrices](../../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) represent the spacetime [Clifford algebra](../../../../../../clifford-algebra.md). With the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) convention above,

$$
\boxed{\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu+\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu=2\eta^{\mu\nu}I_4.}
$$

The braces denote the [anticommutator](../../../../../../anticommutator.md). Consequently $(\gamma^0)^2=I_4$, $(\gamma^i)^2=-I_4$, and distinct [gamma matrices](../../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) anticommute. The [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md) is essential: the right side is a matrix equality, not a [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md) equality. These signs agree with the explicit [Dirac representation of the gamma matrices](../../../../../../dirac-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md) in the next part.
