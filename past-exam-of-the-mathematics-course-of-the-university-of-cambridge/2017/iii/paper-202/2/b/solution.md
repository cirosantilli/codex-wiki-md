↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For [probability measures](../../../../../../probability-measure.md) on $(\Omega,\mathcal F)$, [absolute continuity of measures](../../../../../../absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) means preservation of null sets in one direction:

$$
\boxed{\widetilde{\mathbb P}\ll\mathbb P\iff\bigl(\mathbb P(A)=0\Longrightarrow\widetilde{\mathbb P}(A)=0\bigr)\quad\text{for every }A\in\mathcal F.}
$$

Equivalently, by the [Radon-Nikodym theorem](../../../../../../radon-nikodym-theorem.md), there is an $\mathcal F$-measurable [Radon-Nikodym derivative](../../../../../../radon-nikodym-derivative.md) $Z\geq0$ with $\mathbb E_{\mathbb P}Z=1$ and $\widetilde{\mathbb P}(A)=\mathbb E_{\mathbb P}[Z\mathbf1_A]$. The derivative may vanish, so [absolute continuity of measures](../../../../../../absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) does not require equivalence of the two [probability measures](../../../../../../probability-measure.md). In particular, the reverse null-set implication is not part of the definition.
