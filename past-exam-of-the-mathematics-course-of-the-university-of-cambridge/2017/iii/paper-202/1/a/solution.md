<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Work on a [filtered probability space](../../../../../../filtered-probability-space.md) with the [usual conditions for a filtration](../../../../../../usual-conditions-for-a-filtration.md). A continuous [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) belongs to the class of [L2-bounded continuous martingales](../../../../../../l2-bounded-continuous-martingale.md) when

$$
\boxed{\sup_{t\geq0}\mathbb E|M_t|^2<\infty.}
$$

This means a uniform bound over the entire time interval, not merely being [square-integrable](../../../../../../square-integrable-function.md) at each individual time. The [L2 martingale convergence theorem](../../../../../../l2-martingale-convergence-theorem.md) gives a terminal variable $M_\infty$ and [almost sure convergence](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md) and convergence in the [Lebesgue space](../../../../../../lp-space.md) $L^2$; moreover $M_t=\mathbb E[M_\infty\mid\mathcal F_t]$ by [conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md).

The [predictable sigma-algebra](../../../../../../predictable-sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal P$ on $\Omega\times[0,\infty)$ is the smallest [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) making every left-continuous [adapted process](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) measurable. Equivalently, it is generated by

$$
A\times\{0\},\quad A\in\mathcal F_0,
\qquad
A\times(s,t],\quad 0\leq s<t,\ A\in\mathcal F_s,
$$

where the sample coordinate comes first. A [previsible process](../../../../../../predictable-process.md) is precisely a process measurable for this [predictable sigma-algebra](../../../../../../predictable-sigma-algebra.md). Values at time zero matter for its definition, although they make no contribution to integration against a continuous [quadratic variation](../../../../../../quadratic-variation.md) starting at zero.
