<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md) specified in the PDF, a [Lie-invariant bilinear form](../../../../../../lie-invariant-bilinear-form.md) satisfies

$$
B(xv,w)+B(v,xw)=0\qquad(x\in\mathfrak g,\ v,w\in V).
$$

The [dual Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../dual-lie-algebra-representation.md) has action $(x\phi)(w)=-\phi(xw)$, so the map $T_B:V\to V^*$ defined by $T_B(v)=B(v,-)$ is an [intertwining operator](../../../../../../intertwining-operator.md). If $V$ is [irreducible](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) and $B\ne0$, its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is zero. Since $V,V^*$ have equal finite [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md), $T_B$ is an [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md), and $B$ is a [nondegenerate bilinear form](../../../../../../nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md).

For $B_1\ne0$, the composition $T_{B_1}^{-1}T_{B_2}$ is a representation endomorphism. Over an [algebraically closed field](../../../../../../algebraically-closed-field.md), the [Schur lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) makes it scalar, proving

$$
\boxed{B_2=\lambda B_1.}
$$

Transposing $B$ gives another [Lie-invariant bilinear form](../../../../../../lie-invariant-bilinear-form.md). For $B\ne0$, write $B^T=\mu B$; transposing twice gives $\mu^2=1$. In [field characteristic](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two, this means $\mu=1$ or $\mu=-1$. Thus the form is a [symmetric bilinear form](../../../../../../symmetric-bilinear-form.md) or an [alternating bilinear form](../../../../../../alternating-bilinear-form.md), respectively. The zero form has both properties. In the second case $B(v,v)=-B(v,v)$ forces $B(v,v)=0$, using the same characteristic assumption.
