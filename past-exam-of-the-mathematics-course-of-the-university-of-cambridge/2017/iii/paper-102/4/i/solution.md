<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a positive [root system](../../../../../../root-system.md) $R^+$ for a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), with [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) $W$ and [Weyl vector](../../../../../../half-sum-of-positive-roots.md) $\rho=\frac12\sum_{\alpha\in R^+}\alpha$. For a [dominant integral weight](../../../../../../dominant-integral-weight.md) $\lambda$, let $L(\lambda)$ denote its finite-dimensional [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md), and use formal symbols with $e^\mu e^\nu=e^{\mu+\nu}$. Its [formal character of a weight module](../../../../../../formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md) is $\operatorname{ch}L(\lambda)=\sum_\mu\dim L(\lambda)_\mu\,e^\mu$. The [Weyl character formula](../../../../../../weyl-character-formula.md) says

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ch}L(\lambda)
=\frac{\sum_{w\in W}\det(w)e^{w(\lambda+\rho)}}
{\sum_{w\in W}\det(w)e^{w\rho}}.}
$$

Here $\det(w)=(-1)^{\ell(w)}$, the sign of the product of simple [root reflections](../../../../../../root-reflection.md). The [Weyl denominator formula](../../../../../../weyl-denominator-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sum_{w\in W}\det(w)e^{w\rho}
=e^\rho\prod_{\alpha\in R^+}(1-e^{-\alpha}).}
$$

The quotient is initially interpreted in the fraction field of the group algebra of the [weight lattice](../../../../../../weight-lattice.md); the theorem makes it a finite character.
