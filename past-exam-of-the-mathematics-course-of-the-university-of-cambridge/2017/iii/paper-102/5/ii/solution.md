<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [G2 dimension polynomial](../../../../../../g2-dimension-polynomial.md) gives dimension seven at the short-root [fundamental weight](../../../../../../fundamental-weight.md), so

$$
\boxed{V=L(\omega_1),\qquad\omega_1=2\alpha_1+\alpha_2.}
$$

The [crystal of the seven-dimensional G2 representation](../../../../../../crystal-of-the-seven-dimensional-g2-representation.md) is a chain whose weights, in order, are

$$
2\alpha_1+\alpha_2,\quad\alpha_1+\alpha_2,\quad\alpha_1,\quad0,
\quad-\alpha_1,\quad-\alpha_1-\alpha_2,\quad-2\alpha_1-\alpha_2.
$$

Its successive arrow colors are $1,2,1,1,2,1$, as drawn above. Each arrow subtracts its indicated [simple root](../../../../../../simple-root.md); in particular the central three vertices form a length-two string of color one. The six nonzero weights are exactly the six short roots, each with [multiplicity](../../../../../../multiplicity-mathematics.md) one, and the zero weight also has [multiplicity](../../../../../../multiplicity-mathematics.md) one.
