<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose, towards a contradiction, that $G\in M$. Then $D=\mathbb P\setminus G$ is a ground-model [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) by [axiom schema of separation](../../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md). It is dense. If $p\notin G$, it already lies in $D$. If $p\in G$, [atomless forcing order](../../../../../../atomless-forcing-order.md) structure gives [incompatible forcing conditions](../../../../../../incompatible-forcing-conditions.md) $q,r\leq p$. Both cannot belong to the directed filter $G$, so at least one is a strengthening of $p$ in $D$.

All compatibility and extension quantifiers range over the same ground-model [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $\mathbb P$, so this density argument is also valid internally in the [transitive model](../../../../../../transitive-model.md) $M$. Genericity requires $G\cap D\ne\varnothing$, contradicting the definition of $D$. Therefore a [generic filter for an atomless order is new](../../../../../../generic-filter-for-an-atomless-order-is-new.md):

$$
\boxed{G\notin M.}
$$

The assumption is essential: [a forcing atom determines a ground-model generic filter](../../../../../../a-forcing-atom-determines-a-ground-model-generic-filter.md). For an atom $p$, the conditions compatible with $p$ form a [generic filter](../../../../../../generic-filter.md) $G_p\in M$: two such conditions have strengthenings below $p$, which have a common strengthening by the atom property; every dense [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) has a member below $p$. In a general [partial order](../../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md), $G_p$ need not be the [principal filter](../../../../../../principal-filter-in-an-ordered-set.md) above $p$.
