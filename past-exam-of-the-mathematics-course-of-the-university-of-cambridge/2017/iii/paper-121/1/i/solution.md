<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For parameters $a_1,\ldots,a_n\in M$, [set-theoretic absoluteness](../../../../../../set-theoretic-absoluteness.md) means agreement of the two membership [first-order structures](../../../../../../first-order-structure.md):

$$
\boxed{(M,\in)\models\varphi(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ (N,\in)\models\varphi(a_1,\ldots,a_n).}
$$

This must hold for every such parameter tuple. Equivalently, [formula relativization to a class](../../../../../../formula-relativization-to-a-class.md) produces two formulas $\varphi^M$ and $\varphi^N$ whose truth values agree on tuples from $M$. Agreement for parameters only in $N\setminus M$ is not part of the definition. For a proper [class in set theory](../../../../../../class-set-theory.md), this notation is understood separately for each fixed [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md), rather than as an unrestricted truth predicate for the entire universe.
