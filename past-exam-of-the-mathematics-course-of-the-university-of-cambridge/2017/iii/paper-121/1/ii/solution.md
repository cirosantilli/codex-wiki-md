<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A syntactically [bounded formula in set theory](../../../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md) is built from the atomic [first-order formulas](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $u=v$ and $u\in v$ using [Boolean operations](../../../../../../boolean-operation.md) and bounded quantifiers $\exists u\in v$ and $\forall u\in v$. These abbreviate $\exists u(u\in v\land\cdots)$ and $\forall u(u\in v\Rightarrow\cdots)$, with $u$ different from the bounding variable $v$.

The superscript in the question allows provable equivalence: a [ZF-equivalent bounded formula](../../../../../../zf-equivalent-bounded-formula.md) is any [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\varphi(\vec v)$ for which some syntactically [bounded formula in set theory](../../../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md) $\psi(\vec v)$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{\mathsf{ZF}\vdash\forall\vec v\,(\varphi(\vec v)\leftrightarrow\psi(\vec v)).}
$$

Thus $\Delta_0^{\mathrm{ZF}}$ is the class of [first-order formulas](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) equivalent over [ZF](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) to a syntactic $\Delta_0$ [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md). If a convention uses $\Delta_0^{\mathrm{ZF}}$ just for syntactically bounded set-theoretic [first-order formulas](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md), it is the smaller syntactic class; the subsequent [set-theoretic absoluteness](../../../../../../set-theoretic-absoluteness.md) proof works for both conventions.
