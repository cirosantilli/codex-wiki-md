<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The essential fact is [absoluteness of cardinalhood in limit ranks](../../../../../../absoluteness-of-cardinalhood-in-limit-ranks.md). Let $\alpha\leq\beta$ be [limit ordinals](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md) and $x\in V_\alpha$. Being an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) is absolute between these [transitive sets](../../../../../../transitive-set.md), so a non-ordinal is a [cardinal number](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) in neither structure. For an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\delta\in V_\alpha$, we have $\delta<\alpha$.

If $\delta$ is not an ambient [cardinal number](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md), there is an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\gamma<\delta$ and a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) $f:\gamma\to\delta$. With [Kuratowski ordered pairs](../../../../../../kuratowski-ordered-pair.md), its graph has [rank of a set](../../../../../../rank-of-a-set.md) at most $\delta+3$. Since $\alpha$ is a [limit ordinal](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md), $\delta+3<\alpha$, so $f\in V_\alpha$. The assertion that $f$ is such a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) is a [bounded formula in set theory](../../../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md) and is therefore evaluated correctly by each rank. Conversely, any internal [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) witnessing failure of [cardinal number](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) status is an actual one by the same [set-theoretic absoluteness](../../../../../../set-theoretic-absoluteness.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{V_\alpha\models\text{``}\delta\text{ is a cardinal''}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \delta\text{ is an ambient cardinal}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ V_\beta\models\text{``}\delta\text{ is a cardinal''.}}
$$

Unlike arbitrary [transitive models](../../../../../../transitive-model.md), these rank-initial structures contain every sufficiently low-rank witness. That witness availability is what upgrades the usual [downward absoluteness of cardinalhood](../../../../../../downward-absoluteness-of-cardinalhood.md) to agreement in both directions.
