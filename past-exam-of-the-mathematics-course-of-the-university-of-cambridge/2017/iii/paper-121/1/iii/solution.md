<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Von Neumann hierarchy](../../../../../../von-neumann-hierarchy.md) is the following [transfinite recursion](../../../../../../transfinite-recursion.md) on the [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal.md):

$$
\boxed{V_0=\varnothing,\qquad V_{\alpha+1}=\mathcal P(V_\alpha),\qquad V_\lambda=\bigcup_{\beta<\lambda}V_\beta\ \text{for nonzero limit }\lambda.}
$$

Here $\mathcal P$ denotes the full [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md). The levels are increasing [transitive sets](../../../../../../transitive-set.md), and the construction is continuous at [limit ordinals](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md). [Transfinite induction](../../../../../../transfinite-induction.md) proves these properties: transitivity at a successor follows because a member of a [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of $V_\alpha$ already belongs to $V_\alpha\subseteq\mathcal P(V_\alpha)$; at a limit it follows from the [set union](../../../../../../set-union.md) of increasing [transitive sets](../../../../../../transitive-set.md). In [ZF](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md), [Axiom of foundation](../../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) and [Axiom schema of replacement](../../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) ensure that every [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) belongs to some $V_\alpha$. In terms of the [rank of a set](../../../../../../rank-of-a-set.md), $x\in V_\alpha$ exactly when $\operatorname{rank}(x)<\alpha$.
