<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Starting from a matter-antimatter symmetric ensemble, exact [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) would pair every baryon-producing process with an equally probable antibaryon-producing process. Their contributions to the net [baryon number](../../../../../../baryon-number.md) cancel. Thus [C violation](../../../../../../violation-of-charge-conjugation.md) is necessary. Violating [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) alone is insufficient: if [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md) is exact, the parity-reflected conjugate channels still have equal probabilities and cancel in a [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md) invariant ensemble. One needs [CP violation](../../../../../../cp-violation.md) as well.

The remaining [Sakharov conditions](../../../../../../sakharov-conditions.md) are **[baryon number](../../../../../../baryon-number.md) violation and departure from [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md)**. Conserving [baryon number](../../../../../../baryon-number.md) cannot turn an initially zero value into a nonzero one. In [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md), under the usual [CPT theorem](../../../../../../cpt-theorem.md) hypotheses and with no imposed [chemical potentials](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) for charges odd under [CPT symmetry](../../../../../../cpt-symmetry.md), states and their conjugates have equal thermal weights, so the equilibrium expectation of net [baryon number](../../../../../../baryon-number.md) vanishes and thermal stationarity prevents its sustained production. A nonequilibrium history allows different forward and reverse populations to generate an asymmetry. These are the standard necessary conditions for dynamical [baryogenesis](../../../../../../baryogenesis.md) from symmetric initial conditions, not a guarantee of a sufficiently large asymmetry.
