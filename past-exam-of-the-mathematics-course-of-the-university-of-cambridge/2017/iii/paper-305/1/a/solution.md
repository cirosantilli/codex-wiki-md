<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set $t_0=1$ and $t_i=-1$ for spatial indices. The [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) of the scalar $i$ supplies a minus sign, while conjugating each of the three spatial [gamma matrices](../../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) supplies three more. In the original order,

$$
B^{-1}\gamma^{5*}B=-i(B^{-1}\gamma^{0*}B)(B^{-1}\gamma^{1*}B)(B^{-1}\gamma^{2*}B)(B^{-1}\gamma^{3*}B)=(-i)(-1)^3\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3.
$$

Consequently the [chirality matrix](../../../../../../chirality-matrix.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{B^{-1}\gamma^{5*}B=\gamma^5.}
$$

No rearrangement of the [gamma matrices](../../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) is needed, so no additional [anticommutator](../../../../../../anticommutator.md) sign occurs. The star is entrywise [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md), not [matrix transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) or [Hermitian conjugation](../../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md).
