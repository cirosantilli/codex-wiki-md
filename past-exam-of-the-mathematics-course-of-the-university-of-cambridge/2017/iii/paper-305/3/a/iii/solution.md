**Forbidden by electric-charge conservation**. The initial [bottom quark](../../../../../../../bottom-quark.md) has [electric charge](../../../../../../../electric-charge.md) $-1/3$, while the displayed final [electric charges](../../../../../../../electric-charge.md) sum to $2/3+1+0=5/3$. Therefore no [Standard Model](../../../../../../../standard-model-split.md) [Feynman diagram](../../../../../../../feynman-diagram.md), at tree level or any loop order, can realize this channel.

This is an intentionally disallowed process, not a transcription correction. The charge-conserving semileptonic bottom decay instead has an [Electron](../../../../../../../electron.md) and an [antineutrino](../../../../../../../antineutrino.md) of [Electron](../../../../../../../electron.md) flavour, while a [Positron](../../../../../../../positron.md) accompanies the conjugate antiquark decay. Replacing the displayed particles silently would change the requested classification.
