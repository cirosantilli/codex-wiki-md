**Allowed through a t-channel [W boson](../../../../../../../w-boson.md)**. Along one fermion line the [up quark](../../../../../../../up-quark.md) becomes a [down quark](../../../../../../../down-quark.md) by emitting a virtual $W^+$; along the other the [charm antiquark](../../../../../../../charm-antiquark.md) absorbs it and becomes a [strange antiquark](../../../../../../../strange-antiquark.md). The middle panel shows this [weak charged current](../../../../../../../charged-current.md) exchange. Both [electric charge](../../../../../../../electric-charge.md) and [baryon number](../../../../../../../baryon-number.md) are conserved at each vertex, and the relevant [CKM matrix](../../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) entries are nonzero.

This is the unique physical-particle [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md). A neutral exchange would require a [flavor-changing neutral current](../../../../../../../flavor-changing-neutral-current.md) on each line, absent at tree level in the [Standard Model](../../../../../../../standard-model-split.md). An annihilation into a neutral boson would likewise require an off-diagonal up-type neutral current. The different generations do not forbid the charged-current graph.
