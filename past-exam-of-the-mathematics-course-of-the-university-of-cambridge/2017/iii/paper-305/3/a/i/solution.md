**Allowed through an s-channel [W boson](../../../../../../../w-boson.md)**. The initial [up quark](../../../../../../../up-quark.md) and [down antiquark](../../../../../../../down-antiquark.md) annihilate into a virtual $W^+$, which produces the final [charm quark](../../../../../../../charm-quark.md) and [strange antiquark](../../../../../../../strange-antiquark.md). The two [weak charged current](../../../../../../../charged-current.md) vertices contain the nonzero [CKM matrix](../../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) elements $V_{ud}$ and $V_{cs}$, with [complex conjugations](../../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) determined by fermion-flow conventions.

The left panel shows the unique physical-particle [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md). A neutral exchanged [gauge boson](../../../../../../../gauge-boson.md) cannot connect these charged annihilation currents, and flavour-diagonal neutral vertices cannot turn an [up quark](../../../../../../../up-quark.md) into a [charm quark](../../../../../../../charm-quark.md). There is no additional elementary charged scalar in the minimal [Standard Model](../../../../../../../standard-model-split.md).
