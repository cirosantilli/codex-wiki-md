**Allowed through a virtual [W boson](../../../../../../../w-boson.md)**. The [charm quark](../../../../../../../charm-quark.md) emits a $W^+$ and becomes a [down quark](../../../../../../../down-quark.md) through the nonzero [CKM matrix](../../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) element $V_{cd}$. The virtual [W boson](../../../../../../../w-boson.md) produces a [positive muon](../../../../../../../antimuon.md) and a [muon neutrino](../../../../../../../muon-neutrino.md) through the leptonic [weak charged current](../../../../../../../charged-current.md). The right panel is the unique physical-particle [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md).

Its total [electric charge](../../../../../../../electric-charge.md) is $-1/3+1=2/3$, equal to the initial charge, and its final [lepton number](../../../../../../../lepton-number.md) is $-1+1=0$. The decay is [Cabibbo suppressed](../../../../../../../cabibbo-suppression.md), rather than forbidden. These diagram counts use [unitary gauge](../../../../../../../unitary-gauge.md): in covariant gauges charged unphysical [Goldstone bosons](../../../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) can supply gauge-dependent pieces of the same physical amplitudes, not additional physical channels.
