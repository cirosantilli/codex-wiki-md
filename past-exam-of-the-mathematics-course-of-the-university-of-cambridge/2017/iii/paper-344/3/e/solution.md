<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The total charge of the widely separated $+1,-1$ pair is zero, so a uniform planar [nematic director](../../../../../../nematic-director.md) is compatible with the far boundary if it imposes no additional winding. Each integer defect is susceptible to [energetic fission of an integer nematic disclination](../../../../../../energetic-fission-of-an-integer-nematic-disclination.md): the positive defect can split into two $+1/2$ defects and the negative defect into two $-1/2$ defects. Like-sign elementary defects repel, whereas opposite signs attract through their elastic distortion. This commonly produces subsequent encounters and annihilations of opposite half-integer defects.

The attraction can also be seen before fission. In the fixed-amplitude outer theory, a neutral pair of charges $q,-q$ separated by $R$ has

$$
F_{\rm pair}=2\pi K\lambda_0^2q^2\log(R/r_0)+\text{core terms},\qquad -\frac{dF_{\rm pair}}{dR}=-\frac{2\pi K\lambda_0^2q^2}{R}.
$$

Its energy therefore falls as the separation decreases. In [nematic defect-antidefect annihilation](../../../../../../nematic-defect-antidefect-annihilation.md), opposite defects meet, the amplitude can vanish in their merging cores, and the local winding disappears; the remaining smooth director distortion relaxes towards the uniform energy minimum.

**Expected route: integer-defect fission and attraction of opposite elementary defects, followed by annihilation and relaxation of the remaining texture.**

Direct approach and annihilation of the unsplit integer pair is also topologically allowed. Whether fission happens first, the paths of the four elementary defects, and the annihilation time require a specified dynamical law and core mobilities; the [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) alone does not fix them. Pinning, imposed boundary textures, or sustained thermal production of defect pairs can prevent a strictly defect-free final state. The stated final state presumes dissipative relaxation with no such obstruction.
