<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

These are [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md) at a [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md). Hold the other control at its tricritical value and take the coefficients to be [real analytic functions](../../../../../../real-analytic-function.md) of [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) along a thermal approach with $\mathcal A_2=a(T-T_c)+o(T-T_c)$, $a>0$, $\mathcal A_6\to s>0$, and $\mathcal A_4=0$ or $\mathcal A_4=o(|\mathcal A_2|^{1/2})$. At fixed tricritical $g$, a generic [real analytic](../../../../../../real-analytic-function.md) [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) path with nonzero thermal slope has $\mathcal A_4=O(T-T_c)$ and meets the last condition. Paths tangent to a scaling direction can have different apparent [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) powers.

At zero field on the ordered side with $\mathcal A_4=0$, stationarity gives $m^4=-\mathcal A_2/s$; under the more general approach $\mathcal A_4=o(|\mathcal A_2|^{1/2})$, this relation holds to leading order. Thus the [order-parameter critical exponent](../../../../../../order-parameter-critical-exponent.md) is

$$
\boxed{m\sim\left(\frac{a(T_c-T)}s\right)^{1/4},\qquad \beta=\frac14.}
$$

For the zero-quartic representative, putting this minimum into the [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) gives the singular order-parameter contribution (and the leading asymptotic contribution under the stated negligible-quartic approach)

$$
f_{\rm s}=-\frac{|\mathcal A_2|^{3/2}}{3\sqrt s}.
$$

Using $C_{\rm s}=-T\,\partial_T^2f_{\rm s}$ at fixed other control yields $C_{\rm s}\sim(T_c-T)^{-1/2}$, specifically $Ta^{3/2}/(4\sqrt{s(T_c-T)})$ to leading order. Hence

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac12.}
$$

This [heat-capacity critical exponent](../../../../../../heat-capacity-critical-exponent.md) refers to the nonzero ordered-side singular amplitude; in this [mean-field approximation](../../../../../../mean-field-approximation.md) to [Landau theory](../../../../../../landau-theory.md), the disordered [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md) contributes zero singular [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md), while any regular background remains. Fluctuation corrections lie outside the present approximation.

In the disordered phase, differentiating the equation of state at $m=h=0$ gives the [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) $\chi=1/\mathcal A_2$. On the ordered branch its curvature is $\mathcal A_2+5sm^4=4|\mathcal A_2|$, so $\chi=1/(4|\mathcal A_2|)$. Both give the [magnetic-susceptibility critical exponent](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility-critical-exponent.md)

$$
\boxed{\gamma=1.}
$$

At the tricritical [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) with both lower coefficients zero, $h=sm^5$, so $m=\operatorname{sgn}(h)(|h|/s)^{1/5}$ and the [critical-isotherm exponent](../../../../../../critical-isotherm-exponent.md) is

$$
\boxed{\delta=5,\qquad (\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)=\left(\frac12,\frac14,1,5\right).}
$$

The [Widom scaling relation](../../../../../../widom-scaling-relation.md) $\gamma=\beta(\delta-1)$ and [Rushbrooke scaling relation](../../../../../../rushbrooke-scaling-relation.md) $\alpha+2\beta+\gamma=2$ both check these powers. Their validity here is a mean-field conclusion, not a claim that fluctuations in every spatial dimension preserve them.
