<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Discuss the zero-field phase boundaries, taking $h=0$ and writing $r=\mathcal A_2$, $q=\mathcal A_4$, $s=\mathcal A_6>0$. This qualification matters because a conjugate field generally rounds the zero-field continuous transition. Nonzero [stationary points](../../../../../../stationary-point.md) satisfy $r+qm^2+sm^4=0$.

For $q>0$, the [continuous phase transition](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md) occurs at

$$
\boxed{r=0,\qquad q>0.}
$$

On the $r>0$ side the only stable phase is $m=0$; on the $r<0$ side the equilibrium [global minima](../../../../../../global-minimum.md) are $\pm m_0$ with $m_0^2\sim-r/q$. The [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md) therefore grows continuously from zero as $r$ changes sign.

For $q<0$, the [first-order phase transition](../../../../../../first-order-phase-transition.md) instead occurs while $r$ is positive. At [phase coexistence](../../../../../../phase-coexistence.md), $m=0$ and $m=\pm m_0$ are equally deep [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md) points. With $a=m_0^2>0$, stationarity and equality of [free energies](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) give

$$
r+qa+sa^2=0,\qquad \frac12ra+\frac14qa^2+\frac16sa^3=0.
$$

Eliminating $r$ gives $-qa^2/4-sa^3/3=0$, hence

$$
\boxed{m_0^2=-\frac{3\mathcal A_4}{4\mathcal A_6},\qquad \mathcal A_2=\frac{3\mathcal A_4^2}{16\mathcal A_6},\qquad \mathcal A_4<0.}
$$

At this coexistence value, the [sextic even Landau potential](../../../../../../sextic-even-landau-potential.md) factors as $f(m)=s m^2(m^2+3q/(4s))^2/6\geq0$. This verifies that the three equal stationary values are [global minima](../../../../../../global-minimum.md). Above this coexistence value of $r$ the disordered phase wins; below it the ordered phase wins, with a finite jump in the phase-selected [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md). The values $r=0$ and $r=q^2/(4s)$ are the disordered and ordered [spinodal points](../../../../../../spinodal-point.md), respectively, not the coexistence boundary: the latter follows from the repeated root of $r+qa+sa^2=0$.

The two transition loci meet at the [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A_2=\mathcal A_4=0,\qquad \mathcal A_6>0.}
$$

Two independent control parameters such as $T$ and $g$ can tune these two conditions. In a generic local phase diagram their coefficient map has a [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) of [rank](../../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md) two at the intersection; unspecified functions $\mathcal A_2(T,g)$ and $\mathcal A_4(T,g)$ do not by themselves determine the geometric orientation of the boundaries. The sextic term stabilizes this intersection and the negative-quartic first-order side.
