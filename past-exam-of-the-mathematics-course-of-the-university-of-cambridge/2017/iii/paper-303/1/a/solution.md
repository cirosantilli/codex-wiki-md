<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [Landau theory](../../../../../../landau-theory.md) approximation, choose the [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md) of the [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) as a function of the [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md). Since $\mathcal A_6>0$, the [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is a [coercive function](../../../../../../coercive-function.md) and a [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md) exists. The candidates are [stationary points](../../../../../../stationary-point.md) satisfying

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A_2m+\mathcal A_4m^3+\mathcal A_6m^5=h.}
$$

Check their values of the [free-energy density](../../../../../../free-energy-density.md); a [stationary point](../../../../../../stationary-point.md) or a [metastable](../../../../../../metastability.md) [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md) alone need not be the equilibrium state. Positive curvature $\mathcal A_2+3\mathcal A_4m^2+5\mathcal A_6m^4$ ensures local stability; when it vanishes, higher terms must be checked. The selected [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md) is the [equilibrium magnetization](../../../../../../equilibrium-magnetization.md) within this approximation.

At zero [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md), equal opposite minima describe distinct phases exhibiting [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md). A [spontaneous magnetization](../../../../../../spontaneous-magnetization.md) is obtained by taking the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md) before $h\to0^+$ or $h\to0^-$. A symmetric finite-volume average can instead have zero [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value.md) even when the minima are nonzero. Thus one must specify the phase when interpreting the minimizer as the equilibrium [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value.md).
