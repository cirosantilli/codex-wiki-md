<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Faddeev-Popov ghost fields](../../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) $c$ and $\bar c$ are spin-zero [Grassmann fields](../../../../../../grassmann-field.md) in the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of $G$; their statistics are anticommuting, despite their scalar transformation under spacetime rotations. The [Nakanishi-Lautrup field](../../../../../../nakanishi-lautrup-field.md) $h$ is an auxiliary commuting spin-zero field, also in the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{c,\bar c:\ (0,\ \text{Grassmann odd},\ \mathrm{ad}\,G),\qquad h:\ (0,\ \text{Grassmann even},\ \mathrm{ad}\,G).}
$$

These [Faddeev-Popov ghost fields](../../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) are not physical spin-zero fermion states; they represent the gauge-fixing Jacobian, so their scalar spin does not contradict the physical [Spin-statistics theorem](../../../../../../spin-statistics-theorem.md).
