<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take Euclidean [gamma matrices](../../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) with $\{\gamma_\mu,\gamma_\nu\}=2\delta_{\mu\nu}$, and independent [Grassmann fields](../../../../../../grassmann-field.md) $\psi,\bar\psi$. The minimally coupled [Dirac action](../../../../../../dirac-action.md) is

$$
\boxed{S_\psi=\int d^4x\,\bar\psi^a(\gamma_\mu D_\mu+m)_{ac}\psi^c.}
$$

Its mass term is the [Dirac mass term](../../../../../../dirac-mass-term.md), and the interaction is $-ig\bar\psi^a\gamma_\mu A_\mu^b(T^b_{\mathrm{ad}})_{ac}\psi^c$. With Fourier convention $e^{ip\cdot x}$, the free kinetic operator is $i\not p+m$. The [Euclidean adjoint Dirac Feynman rules](../../../../../../euclidean-adjoint-dirac-feynman-rules.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{S^{ac}(p)=\delta^{ac}\frac{-i\not p+m}{p^2+m^2},\qquad V_\mu^{b;ac}=+ig\gamma_\mu(T^b_{\mathrm{ad}})_{ac}=gf^{bac}\gamma_\mu.}
$$

The vertex sign follows by expanding $e^{-S_\psi}$, not $e^{iS_\psi}$. [Momentum conservation](../../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) supplies the usual [Dirac delta function](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) at each vertex. There is only a one-gluon fermion vertex, with no two-gluon seagull because the fermionic [covariant derivative](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) is first order. A closed fermion loop has the usual [fermionic sign](../../../../../../fermionic-sign.md) $-1$. The identity $(i\not p+m)(-i\not p+m)=(p^2+m^2)I$ checks the [Dirac propagator](../../../../../../dirac-propagator.md).
