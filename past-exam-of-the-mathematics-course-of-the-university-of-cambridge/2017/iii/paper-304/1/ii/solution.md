<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of the Euclidean evolution kernel identifies its initial and final positions. Thus the [Euclidean worldline path integral](../../../../../../euclidean-worldline-path-integral.md) is over periodic paths $x(\tau+\beta)=x(\tau)$, with $\tau\in[0,\beta]$:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal Z=\int_{x(0)=x(\beta)}\mathcal Dx\,e^{-S_E[x]},\qquad S_E[x]=\frac12\int_0^\beta\left[\dot x^2+\omega^2x^2\right]d\tau.}
$$

The circle has circumference $\beta$, not an arbitrary unit length. The [Euclidean action](../../../../../../euclidean-action.md) follows by [Wick rotation](../../../../../../wick-rotation.md) of the unit-mass oscillator action. Equivalently, [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) with [periodic boundary conditions](../../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) gives $S_E=\tfrac12\langle x,L_\omega x\rangle$ for $L_\omega=-\partial_\tau^2+\omega^2$. The [path integral](../../../../../../path-integral.md) measure is formal until a regularization and its normalization are specified; the next part supplies a finite-dimensional version.
