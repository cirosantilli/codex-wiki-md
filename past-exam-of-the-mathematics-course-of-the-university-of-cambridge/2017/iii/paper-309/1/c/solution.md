<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

On a branch where $(\rho,\phi)$ are valid [local coordinates](../../../../../../local-coordinate.md), the [inclusion map](../../../../../../inclusion-map.md) is $j(\rho,\phi)=(x(\rho),\rho\cos\phi,\rho\sin\phi)$, with $x^2=1-a^2\rho^2$. Its [differential](../../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) gives

$$
\boxed{j_*\partial_\phi=-z\partial_y+y\partial_z=:K.}
$$

Strictly this is first a [vector field along a map](../../../../../../vector-field-along-a-map.md) on the surface. The displayed formula extends it canonically to a [smooth](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) on all of $\mathbb R^3$. Its [integral curves of a vector field](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) through $(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ obey $\dot x=0$, $\dot y=-z$, $\dot z=y$, hence

$$
\boxed{x(s)=x_0,\qquad y(s)+iz(s)=(y_0+iz_0)e^{is}.}
$$

These are circles around the $x$-axis, traversed with angular speed one; points on the axis are stationary. Since $K(x^2+a^2(y^2+z^2))=0$, curves starting on the ellipsoid stay there. The local angular chart can expire while the ambient [flow map](../../../../../../flow-map.md) and the geometric curve remain well defined.
