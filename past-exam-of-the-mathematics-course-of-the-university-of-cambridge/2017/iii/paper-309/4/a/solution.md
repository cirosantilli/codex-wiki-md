<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose signature $(-,+,+,+)$, with $e^0$ timelike. The [orthonormal coframe in spacetime](../../../../../../orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) reconstructs the [Lorentzian metric](../../../../../../lorentzian-metric.md) as

$$
\boxed{g=-(e^0)^2+(e^1)^2+(e^2)^2+(e^3)^2=-dt^2+A(t-z)^2dx^2+B(t-z)^2dy^2+dz^2.}
$$

Because $A$ and $B$ are nowhere zero on the domain, the coframe is invertible and the metric nondegenerate; either sign of each function is allowed. In [retarded and advanced null coordinates](../../../../../../retarded-and-advanced-null-coordinates.md) $u=t-z$, $v=t+z$ the metric is $-du\,dv+A(u)^2dx^2+B(u)^2dy^2$, a diagonal example of [Rosen coordinates for a plane gravitational wave](../../../../../../rosen-coordinates-for-a-plane-gravitational-wave.md). Multiplication by $-1$ would give the opposite overall signature convention; all subsequent signs here use the displayed one.
