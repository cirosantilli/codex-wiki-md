<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use [conditional independence](../../../../../../conditional-independence.md) at the common age $Y=y$. A system with a [red giant](../../../../../../red-giant.md) and a second evolved component is either a pair of [red giants](../../../../../../red-giant.md), with [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) $g(y)^2$, or a [red giant](../../../../../../red-giant.md) and a [white dwarf](../../../../../../white-dwarf.md), with [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) $2g(y)w(y)$. The factor two counts which component is the [white dwarf](../../../../../../white-dwarf.md); a pair of [red giants](../../../../../../red-giant.md) is counted only once. Since $w=9g$, the unconditional [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of this event is

$$
p_{G+\mathrm{evolved}}=\int_0^1(g^2+2gw)dy=19\int_0^1\frac{y^2}{8100}dy=\frac{19}{24300}.
$$

This event is contained in the event of at least one [red giant](../../../../../../red-giant.md). Therefore the requested [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md), among systems selected for containing a [red giant](../../../../../../red-giant.md), is

$$
\boxed{\frac{p_{G+\mathrm{evolved}}}{p_{\geq1G}}=\frac{19}{269}\simeq0.07063.}
$$

In particular the denominator is a fraction of systems, not the individual-star fraction $1/180$.
