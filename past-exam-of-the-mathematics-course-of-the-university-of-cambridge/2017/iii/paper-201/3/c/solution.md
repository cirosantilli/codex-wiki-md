<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Work on $([0,1],\mathcal B,dt)$ as a [probability space](../../../../../../probability-space.md), with [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md) of total mass one. Let $\mathcal F_n$ be the [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) generated by the level-$n$ half-open dyadic cells together with the separate null cell $\{1\}$. Define the [dyadic slope martingale](../../../../../../dyadic-slope-martingale.md) by

$$
G_n(t)=2^n\bigl[f((k+1)2^{-n})-f(k2^{-n})\bigr]
\quad\text{on }[k2^{-n},(k+1)2^{-n}).
$$

The endpoint $t=1$ may be assigned any value, since it is a null set for [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md). The two child slopes average to their parent slope, by telescoping the two increments. Therefore $\mathbb E[G_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=G_n$ [almost everywhere](../../../../../../almost-everywhere.md), so this is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md). The [Lipschitz condition](../../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) gives $|G_n|\leq K$ everywhere except possibly at the freely chosen endpoint, where we take zero.

Apply the [Lp martingale convergence theorem](../../../../../../lp-martingale-convergence-theorem.md) with $p=2$. Its limit $g$ has $|g|\leq K$ [almost everywhere](../../../../../../almost-everywhere.md) and $G_n\to g$ in [L1 norm](../../../../../../l1-norm.md) as well, by [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Choose a measurable representative of $g$ and set it to zero on any exceptional null set; it is then a bounded [measurable function](../../../../../../measurable-function.md) on the entire interval.

Set $f_n(x)=f(0)+\int_0^xG_n(t)dt$. Telescoping at the grid points shows that $f_n$ is the [linear interpolation](../../../../../../linear-interpolation.md) of $f$ on the dyadic grid. The [Lipschitz condition](../../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) gives $\|f_n-f\|_\infty\leq K2^{-n}\to0$. Also

$$
\sup_{0\leq x\leq1}\left|\int_0^x(G_n-g)(t)dt\right|\leq\|G_n-g\|_1\to0.
$$

The two uniform limits coincide, giving the [absolutely continuous function](../../../../../../absolutely-continuous-function.md) representation

$$
\boxed{f(x)=f(0)+\int_0^xg(t)dt\quad(0\leq x\leq1),\qquad |g(t)|\leq K.}
$$

The chosen bound holds for every $t$ after the null-set modification; the [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) identity holds for every $x$ simultaneously.
