<h1 id="1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The odd-potential branch uses $s=\tanh(kL)$, whereas the even-potential branch uses $1/s$. The [imaginary parts](../../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) are identical because

$$
\frac{\sqrt{1/s}}{1+1/s}=\frac{\sqrt s}{1+s}.
$$

Consequently the [equality of top-hat jet parity growth rates](../../../../../../../equality-of-top-hat-jet-parity-growth-rates.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\gamma_{\mathrm{odd}}=\gamma_{\mathrm{even}}
=\frac{k|V|\sqrt{\tanh(kL)}}{1+\tanh(kL)}
=\frac{k|V|}{2}\sqrt{1-e^{-4kL}}.}
$$

Here $\gamma=kc_i$ is the positive [temporal growth rate](../../../../../../../growth-rate.md). The real [phase velocities](../../../../../../../phase-velocity.md) differ: $c_{r,\mathrm{odd}}=\tfrac V2(1-e^{-2kL})$ and $c_{r,\mathrm{even}}=\tfrac V2(1+e^{-2kL})$. Thus equal amplification does not imply equal propagation. As $kL\to\infty$, both approach the isolated-[vortex sheet](../../../../../../../vortex-sheet.md) [phase velocity](../../../../../../../phase-velocity.md) $V/2$ and [growth rate](../../../../../../../growth-rate.md) $k|V|/2$.
