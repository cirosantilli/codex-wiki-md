<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $\phi'=\Phi(z)e^{ik(x-ct)}$ and let $B_\pm$ be the displacement amplitudes at $z=\pm L$. Use subscripts $I$ for the jet interior and $O$ for the adjoining exterior. Each interface is a material [vortex sheet](../../../../../../../vortex-sheet.md), so its linearized [kinematic boundary conditions](../../../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition.md) are

$$
\Phi'_I(\pm L)=ik(V-c)B_\pm,\qquad
\Phi'_O(\pm L)=-ikcB_\pm.
$$

The equal [mass densities](../../../../../../../density.md) make the hydrostatic displacement terms cancel. The [dynamic boundary condition for an inviscid interface](../../../../../../../dynamic-boundary-condition-for-an-inviscid-interface.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{(V-c)\Phi_I(\pm L)=-c\Phi_O(\pm L),\qquad
\frac{\Phi'_I(\pm L)}{V-c}=\frac{\Phi'_O(\pm L)}{-c}=ikB_\pm.}
$$

The division form assumes $c\ne0,V$; the preceding undivided equations remain the proper conditions in those exceptional cases. Potentials themselves need not be continuous, and their vertical [derivatives](../../../../../../../derivative.md) need not be equal: the same material displacement is advected by different tangential base [velocities](../../../../../../../velocity.md). At infinity the exterior potentials decay. These displacement and [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) conditions, rather than continuity of $\Phi$ and $\Phi'$, determine the [equal-density top-hat planar-jet dispersion relation](../../../../../../../equal-density-top-hat-planar-jet-dispersion-relation.md).
