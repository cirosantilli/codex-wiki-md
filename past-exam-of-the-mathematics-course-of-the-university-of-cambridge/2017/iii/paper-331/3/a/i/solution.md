<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the physical coefficients $U,\mu,c_d$ real. Substitution of $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$ into the [linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation](../../../../../../../linear-complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) gives $-i\omega+ikU-\mu+(1+ic_d)k^2=0$, hence

$$
\omega=Uk+(c_d-i)k^2+i\mu.
$$

Let $A=c_d-i$. [Completing the square](../../../../../../../completing-the-square.md) yields $\omega=A(k+U/(2A))^2+i\mu-U^2/(4A)$. Therefore the [absolute wavenumber](../../../../../../../absolute-wavenumber.md) and [absolute frequency](../../../../../../../absolute-frequency.md) are

$$
\boxed{k_0=-\frac{U}{2(c_d-i)},\qquad
\omega_0=i\mu-\frac{U^2}{4(c_d-i)}.}
$$

Their explicitly separated parts are

$$
k_0=-\frac{U(c_d+i)}{2(1+c_d^2)},\qquad
\omega_0=-\frac{U^2c_d}{4(1+c_d^2)}
+i\left[\mu-\frac{U^2}{4(1+c_d^2)}\right].
$$

Thus $d\omega/dk=U+2(c_d-i)k$ vanishes at $k_0$. The complex saddle describes the localized [Green function](../../../../../../../green-s-function.md) response; it is different from selecting a real [wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) for a [Fourier mode](../../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) for temporal amplification.
