<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) in the [nonnegative orthant](../../../../../../nonnegative-orthant.md), the PDF's [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) gives the [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form.md)

$$
x^TAx=x_1^2+4x_1x_2+x_2^2\geq0.
$$

Thus it is a [copositive matrix](../../../../../../copositive-matrix.md). On the other hand, with $u=(1,-1)^T$,

$$
\boxed{u^TAu=-2<0},
$$

so it is not a [positive semidefinite matrix](../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). Equivalently, the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $3$ and $-1$, with [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $(1,1)^T$ and $(1,-1)^T$.

**The local TeX has corrupted the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) entries**. The PDF has diagonal entries one and off-diagonal entries two. The all-twos [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) in the TeX would instead have [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form.md) $2(x_1+x_2)^2$ and would be a [positive semidefinite matrix](../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md); it cannot demonstrate the requested distinction.
