<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take a singly [quantized vortex](../../../../../../quantum-vortex.md) of positive [circulation](../../../../../../circulation-physics.md). The [superfluid velocity](../../../../../../superfluid-velocity.md) is $\mathbf u=(\hbar/m)\nabla S$, where $S$ is the [complex argument](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) of the condensate. Since $S=\theta$, [quantized circulation](../../../../../../quantized-circulation.md) gives $u_\theta=\hbar/(mr)$. Insert the radial [Thomas–Fermi approximation for a condensate](../../../../../../thomas-fermi-approximation-for-a-condensate.md) into the cylindrical volume [integral](../../../../../../integral.md), excluding the core $r<a_0$:

$$
E_{\rm kin}=\frac{\pi H_0\hbar^2n_0}{m}\int_{a_0}^{R_r}\left(1-\frac{r^2}{R_r^2}\right)\frac{dr}{r}.
$$

Integrating the logarithmic and quadratic terms separately gives

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm kin}=\frac{\pi H_0\hbar^2n_0}{m}\left[\log\frac{R_r}{a_0}-\frac12+\frac{a_0^2}{2R_r^2}\right].}
$$

The [logarithm](../../../../../../logarithm.md) is the familiar long-range flow contribution of a [quantum vortex](../../../../../../quantum-vortex.md); the $-1/2$ comes from the declining trapped [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md). If the bucket truncates the cloud, put $R=R_0<R_r$ and the same integration gives $E_{\rm kin}=\pi H_0\hbar^2n_0[\log(R/a_0)-(R^2-a_0^2)/(2R_r^2)]/m$, provided $a_0<R$. A vortex of integer [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md) $q$ has $u_\theta=q\hbar/(mr)$ and multiplies this flow [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) by $q^2$. The core radius acts as a cutoff of order the local [healing length](../../../../../../healing-length.md); its internal [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) and interaction [energies](../../../../../../energy.md) are not computed by this shell estimate.
