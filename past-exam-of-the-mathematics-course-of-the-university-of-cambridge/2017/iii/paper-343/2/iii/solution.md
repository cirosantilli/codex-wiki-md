<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The axial [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) per unit volume is $mn(r)r u_\theta(r)=\hbar n(r)$ for a singly quantized positive [quantum vortex](../../../../../../quantum-vortex.md). Thus every particle in the prescribed shell contributes $\hbar$:

$$
L_z=2\pi H_0\hbar n_0\int_{a_0}^{R_r}\left(1-\frac{r^2}{R_r^2}\right)r\,dr.
$$

This yields

$$
\boxed{L_z=\pi H_0\hbar n_0\left[\frac{R_r^2}{2}-a_0^2+\frac{a_0^4}{2R_r^2}\right]
=\frac{\pi H_0\hbar n_0R_r^2}{2}\left(1-\frac{a_0^2}{R_r^2}\right)^2.}
$$

This is $\hbar$ times the shell particle number. For a wall-truncated cloud with $R=R_0<R_r$, the corresponding result is $L_z=\pi H_0\hbar n_0[R^2-a_0^2-(R^4-a_0^4)/(2R_r^2)]$. Reversing the [quantized circulation](../../../../../../quantized-circulation.md) reverses $L_z$ without changing the flow [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). For [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md) $q$, $L_z$ is multiplied by $q$.
