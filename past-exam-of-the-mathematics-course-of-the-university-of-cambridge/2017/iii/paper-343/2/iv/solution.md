<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In a [rotating reference frame](../../../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md) at angular frequency $\Omega$, the relevant [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) difference is approximately $E_{\rm kin}-\Omega L_z$. The vortex-free state has zero [circulation](../../../../../../circulation-physics.md) and zero corresponding [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md). For $a_0/R_r\ll1$, the preceding shell estimates reduce to

$$
E_{\rm kin}\simeq\frac{\pi H_0\hbar^2n_0}{m}\left[\log\frac{R_r}{a_0}-\frac12\right],\qquad
L_z\simeq\frac{\pi H_0\hbar n_0R_r^2}{2}.
$$

Therefore the [thermodynamic vortex-nucleation frequency](../../../../../../thermodynamic-vortex-nucleation-frequency.md) for the first positive [quantum vortex](../../../../../../quantum-vortex.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Omega_c\simeq\frac{2\hbar}{mR_r^2}\left[\log\frac{R_r}{a_0}-\frac12\right],\qquad
\Omega>\Omega_c\ \Rightarrow\ E_{\rm kin}-\Omega L_z<0.}
$$

The common factors $H_0,n_0$ cancel explicitly, although the trap radius and core scale still depend on [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md). In the strict leading-logarithm limit, $\Omega_c\sim2\hbar\log(R_r/a_0)/(mR_r^2)$. The finite constant $-1/2$ is the one obtained from the stated Thomas–Fermi shell [integral](../../../../../../integral.md); a resolved vortex core and readjustment of the [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) at fixed particle number can change the nonlogarithmic constant. This is an energetic threshold, not a dynamical guarantee of nucleation: a surface barrier can delay entry. Higher positive [winding numbers](../../../../../../winding-number.md) have flow [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) proportional to $q^2$ and [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) proportional to $q$, so their analogous threshold is larger in this approximation.
