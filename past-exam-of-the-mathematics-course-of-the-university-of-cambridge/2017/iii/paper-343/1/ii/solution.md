<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the nonzero homogeneous [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) $n_0=\mu/g$ from the previous part, with $g,\mu>0$. Set

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\psi=\frac\psi{\sqrt{n_0}},\qquad
\widetilde{\mathbf x}=\frac{\mathbf x}{\ell_h},\quad
\ell_h=\frac\hbar{\sqrt{2m\mu}},\qquad
\widetilde t=\frac{2\mu}{\hbar}t.}
$$

The length $\ell_h$ is the conventional [healing length](../../../../../../healing-length.md) in this normalization. In the dimensional [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](../../../../../../gross-pitaevskii-equation.md), $\hbar^2/(2m\ell_h^2)=\mu$ and $g n_0=\mu$. Substitution and division by $\mu\sqrt{n_0}$ therefore give

$$
2i\partial_{\widetilde t}\widetilde\psi=-\widetilde\nabla^2\widetilde\psi+(|\widetilde\psi|^2-1)\widetilde\psi,
$$

or equivalently the requested $-2i$ form. The factor two in the time scale is necessary: using $\mu t/\hbar$ instead would leave a coefficient $-i$. The normalized [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) is $|\widetilde\psi|^2=n/n_0$.
