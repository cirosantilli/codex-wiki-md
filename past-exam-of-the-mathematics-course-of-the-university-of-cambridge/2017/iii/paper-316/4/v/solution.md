<h1 id="4/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For a spherical [planet](../../../../../../planet.md) of [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) $\rho_p$, $R_p=(3M_p/4\pi\rho_p)^{1/3}$. The point-comet surface [escape velocity](../../../../../../escape-velocity.md) and circular [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md) speed therefore give

$$
\chi^2=\frac{2GM_p/R_p}{GM_\star/a_p}
=\frac{2M_pa_p}{M_\star}\left(\frac{4\pi\rho_p}{3M_p}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

Raise this equation to the power $3/2$ and solve for $M_p$:

$$
\boxed{M_p=\sqrt{\frac{3}{32\pi}}\,
\chi^3 M_\star^{3/2}a_p^{-3/2}\rho_p^{-1/2}}.
$$

Thus the constant of proportionality is $\boxed{\sqrt{3/(32\pi)}}$, and the [gravitational constant](../../../../../../gravitational-constant.md) cancels. Dimensional checking gives [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) on the right: $M_\star^{3/2}a_p^{-3/2}\rho_p^{-1/2}$ has that dimension.

This uses the [planet](../../../../../../planet.md)'s own [escape velocity](../../../../../../escape-velocity.md). If instead $\chi$ is defined at finite-comet contact, an additional factor $(1+R/R_p)^{3/2}$ appears on the right, and $R_p$ itself depends on $M_p$; the quoted pure-planet scaling is then only the $R/R_p\to0$ limit.
