<h1 id="4/viii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Viii](../viii.md)

The two-boundary map omits several effects that can alter [planetary scattering](../../../../../../planetary-scattering.md).

The initial [semi-major axis](../../../../../../semi-major-axis.md), [orbital eccentricity](../../../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md), [orbital inclination](../../../../../../orbital-inclination.md) and [orbital phase](../../../../../../orbital-phase.md) determine whether encounters occur and their relative [velocities](../../../../../../velocity.md). A strongly bound [comet](../../../../../../comet.md) needs more [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) to escape; a nearly parabolic one needs less. The planetary radius and [mass density](../../../../../../density.md), the finite [comet](../../../../../../comet.md) radius, and [gravitational focusing](../../../../../../gravitational-focusing.md) determine collision probabilities. [Tidal disruption](../../../../../../tidal-disruption.md), atmospheric [gas drag](../../../../../../gas-drag.md), [sublimation](../../../../../../sublimation.md) and physical fragmentation can destroy a body before a nominal point-particle scattering sequence is completed.

Other [planets](../../../../../../planet.md) can hand a [comet](../../../../../../comet.md) from one scatterer to another, eject it, or lift its [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md) clear of the original scatterer's [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). [Mean-motion resonances](../../../../../../mean-motion-resonance.md) and [secular perturbations](../../../../../../secular-perturbation.md) can protect objects from encounters or correlate kicks, contradicting the independent [random walk](../../../../../../random-walk.md) assumption. [Planetary migration](../../../../../../planetary-migration.md) changes the encounter geometry over time.

[Stellar flybys](../../../../../../stellar-flyby.md) and a [galactic tide](../../../../../../galactic-tide.md) can change distant [comet](../../../../../../comet.md) [periapses](../../../../../../periapsis.md), allowing new encounters or detaching an object from the planetary region. Stellar [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) evolution changes both binding and planetary [orbits](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). Finally, the age and the supply rate of new [comets](../../../../../../comet.md) determine whether the observed population is a residual one or continuously replenished. Thus the mass-radius map is a useful conditional classification, not a complete survival law.
