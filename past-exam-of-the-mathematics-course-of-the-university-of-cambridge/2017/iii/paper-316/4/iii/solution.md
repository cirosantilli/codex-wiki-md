<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The incoming and outgoing [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) vectors have the same magnitude $v_\infty$, by [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md). The chord between them has length

$$
|\Delta\boldsymbol v|=2v_\infty\sin\frac{\theta}{2}
 =\frac{2v_\infty}{1+r_{\min}v_\infty^2/\mu}.
$$

For fixed $r_{\min}$ put $z=v_\infty\sqrt{r_{\min}/\mu}$. Then $|\Delta\boldsymbol v|=\sqrt{\mu/r_{\min}}\;2z/(1+z^2)$, and $(z-1)^2\geq0$ proves $2z/(1+z^2)\leq1$. Equality occurs at $v_\infty=\sqrt{\mu/r_{\min}}$.

A non-colliding [comet](../../../../../../comet.md) must have $r_{\min}\geq r_0$, where $r_0=R_p$ in the point-comet approximation and $r_0=R_p+R$ for finite bodies. Thus the [non-colliding gravitational velocity-kick bound](../../../../../../non-colliding-gravitational-velocity-kick-bound.md) is

$$
\boxed{|\Delta\boldsymbol v|_{\max}=\sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r_0}}
=\frac{v_{\rm esc}(r_0)}{\sqrt2}}.
$$

The optimizing grazing [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) has $v_\infty=v_{\rm esc}/\sqrt2$, $e_h=2$ and $\theta=\pi/3$. If contact itself is excluded, this is a supremum approached from larger $r_{\min}$. The finite collision radius is essential: an ideal point [planet](../../../../../../planet.md) with unrestricted $r_{\min}\to0$ has no finite upper bound.

A constant [Galilean boost](../../../../../../galilean-transformation.md) by the [planet](../../../../../../planet.md)'s stellar orbital [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) leaves the vector difference unchanged. It changes the [comet](../../../../../../comet.md)'s incoming and outgoing heliocentric [energies](../../../../../../energy.md), which is the basis of a [gravitational assist](../../../../../../gravitational-assist.md).
