<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the separation vector $\boldsymbol r$ from the [star](../../../../../../star.md) to the [comet](../../../../../../comet.md), and let $m_{\rm red}=mM_\star/(m+M_\star)$. The [two-body problem](../../../../../../two-body-problem.md) separates into uniform [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) motion and

$$
\ddot{\boldsymbol r}=-\frac{\mu}{r^3}\boldsymbol r,\qquad \mu=G(M_\star+m).
$$

Taking a cross product with $\boldsymbol r$ proves [conservation of angular momentum](../../../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md); taking the scalar product with $\dot{\boldsymbol r}$ proves [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md). The relative constants are

$$
\boldsymbol h=\boldsymbol r\times\dot{\boldsymbol r},\qquad \varepsilon=\frac{v^2}{2}-\frac{\mu}{r}.
$$

The total relative [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) are $m_{\rm red}\boldsymbol h$ and $m_{\rm red}\varepsilon$, respectively. The conserved [eccentricity vector](../../../../../../eccentricity-vector.md) is $\boldsymbol e=\dot{\boldsymbol r}\times\boldsymbol h/\mu-\widehat{\boldsymbol r}$. Its scalar product with $\widehat{\boldsymbol r}$ gives $r=h^2/[\mu(1+e\cos f)]$, with $f$ the [true anomaly](../../../../../../true-anomaly.md). The [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md) and [apoapsis](../../../../../../apoapsis.md) distances are therefore $h^2/[\mu(1+e)]$ and $h^2/[\mu(1-e)]$. Their sum is twice the [semi-major axis](../../../../../../semi-major-axis.md), so

$$
\boxed{h=\sqrt{\mu a(1-e^2)}}.
$$

At either apsis $v=h/r$; substituting an apsidal radius gives $\varepsilon=-\mu/(2a)$. Thus the [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) implies the [vis-viva equation](../../../../../../vis-viva-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{v=\sqrt{\mu\left(\frac2r-\frac1a\right)}}.
$$

These are exact relative-coordinate formulas for an [elliptic orbit](../../../../../../elliptic-orbit.md), $0\leq e<1$. In the barycentric frame the [comet](../../../../../../comet.md)'s speed is $M_\star/(M_\star+m)$ times the relative speed; the printed identification with the [comet](../../../../../../comet.md)'s stellar orbital speed uses $M_\star\gg m$.
