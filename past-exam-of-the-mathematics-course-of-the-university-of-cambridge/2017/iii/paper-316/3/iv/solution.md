<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Now the eccentric [planet](../../../../../../planet.md) $c$ is inside the circular perturber $d$. The [interior eccentricity-type mean-motion resonance](../../../../../../interior-eccentricity-type-mean-motion-resonance.md) has

$$
\boxed{\phi_{cd}=(m+n)\lambda_d-m\lambda_c-n\varpi_c}.
$$

In the mean-conjunction approximation, $\phi_{cd}=n(\Lambda-\varpi_c)$, so $\Lambda_j=\varpi_c+(\phi_{cd}+2\pi j)/n$. Successive [conjunctions](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) advance by $2\pi m/n$; coprime $m,n$ visit $n$ distinct phases.

The near-conjunction tangential pulls are reversed for an eccentric body inside its perturber. Just before [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) the inner body is pulled forwards and afterwards backwards. Near [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md), an inward-moving inner body experiences the stronger earlier forward pull; its [semi-major axis](../../../../../../semi-major-axis.md) grows, its [mean motion](../../../../../../mean-motion.md) falls, and $\phi_{cd}$ increases because its coefficient of $\lambda_c$ is negative. On the outward side the feedback reverses, restoring the first-order centre zero. Higher-order feedback is strongest on the side closest to the external perturber, near [apoapsis](../../../../../../apoapsis.md); the leading coefficient alternates in sign with order.

Writing $R_{\rm res}=B_ne_c^n\cos\phi_{cd}$ gives $B_n<0$ for odd $n$ and $B_n>0$ for even $n$ in the leading non-crossing model. The same negative-curvature [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) argument therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\phi_{cd,0}=\begin{cases}0,&n=1,3,\\ \pi,&n=2.\end{cases}}
$$

Conjunction phases relative to [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md) are $\{0\}$, $\{\pi/2,3\pi/2\}$ and $\{0,2\pi/3,4\pi/3\}$, respectively, as shown in the lower row of the figure. As before, these are small-eccentricity symmetric predictions; additional resonant [Fourier harmonics](../../../../../../fourier-harmonic.md) and coupled secular dynamics can change the stable branches.

For actual alignment of the eccentric body at true longitude $\Lambda$, the exact phase relation is instead $\phi_{cd}=(m+n)x-my$, where $x=\Lambda-\varpi_c$ and $y=M_c(x)$. The simple equally spaced angles replace $y$ by $x$ at leading order.
