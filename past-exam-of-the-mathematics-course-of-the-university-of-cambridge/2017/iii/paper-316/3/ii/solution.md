<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the mean-conjunction approximation set $\lambda_b=\lambda_c=\Lambda$. The [resonant conjunction geometry](../../../../../../resonant-conjunction-geometry.md) is then

$$
\phi_{bc}=q(\Lambda-\varpi_c)\pmod{2\pi},\qquad
\boxed{\Lambda_j=\varpi_c+\frac{\phi_{bc}+2\pi j}{q},\quad j=0,\ldots,q-1}.
$$

There are $q$ phase directions spaced by $2\pi/q$. The integer $p$ determines their temporal order: at exact resonance the [synodic period](../../../../../../synodic-period.md) is $P_{\rm syn}=(p+q)P_b/q=pP_c/q$, and the [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) longitude advances by $2\pi p/q$ per [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md). For coprime $p,q$ this visits all $q$ branches; [resonant-argument libration](../../../../../../resonant-argument-libration.md) broadens each direction by about its [libration amplitude of a resonant argument](../../../../../../libration-amplitude-of-a-resonant-argument.md) divided by $q$.

**Mean [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) and actual alignment are different for an eccentric [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md)**. If $\Lambda$ is the true common longitude, write $x=\Lambda-\varpi_c=f_c$ and $y=M_c(f_c)$, with $M_c$ the [mean anomaly](../../../../../../mean-anomaly.md). Because $\lambda_c=\varpi_c+y$ while the circular [planet](../../../../../../planet.md) has $\lambda_b=\Lambda$, the exact geometric relation is

$$
\phi_{bc}=(p+q)y-px.
$$

The displayed equally spaced directions use $y=x+O(e_c)$, hence are leading-small-eccentricity geometry. With appreciable $e_c$, use [Kepler's equation](../../../../../../kepler-s-equation.md) to locate true [conjunctions](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md); equating true and [mean longitudes](../../../../../../mean-longitude.md) silently is not exact.
