<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use conventional [mean longitudes](../../../../../../mean-longitude.md) $\lambda$ and take the positive integers $p,q$ in lowest terms. Rotational invariance requires the angular coefficients of a [resonant argument](../../../../../../resonant-argument.md) to sum to zero. Since the inner [planet](../../../../../../planet.md) has zero [orbital eccentricity](../../../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md), the eccentricity-type term involving its undefined apsidal angle has zero amplitude. The surviving outer-eccentricity argument is

$$
\boxed{\phi_{bc}=(p+q)\lambda_c-p\lambda_b-q\varpi_c\pmod{2\pi}}.
$$

Its leading [disturbing function](../../../../../../disturbing-function.md) term is proportional to $e_c^q\cos\phi_{bc}$. Near the period commensurability, $(p+q)n_c-pn_b$ is small, so the [resonant argument](../../../../../../resonant-argument.md) is slow and repeated perturbations can add coherently. Trapping produces [resonant-argument libration](../../../../../../resonant-argument-libration.md), rather than circulation, with $(p+q)n_c-pn_b-q\dot\varpi_c\simeq0$.

A near-integer period ratio alone does not prove [mean-motion resonance](../../../../../../mean-motion-resonance.md): the diagnostic is bounded [resonant-argument libration](../../../../../../resonant-argument-libration.md). At $e_c=0$ this eccentric resonant term vanishes and $\varpi_c$ is undefined. If $p,q$ have a common factor, reduce the ratio first; the unreduced $q$ is not the actual lowest resonance order.
