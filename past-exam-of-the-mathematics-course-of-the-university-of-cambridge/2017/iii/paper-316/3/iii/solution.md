<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the weak, non-crossing, leading-eccentricity model the exterior resonant coefficient has positive sign: write its [disturbing function](../../../../../../disturbing-function.md) as $R_{\rm res}=B_qe_c^q\cos\phi_{bc}$, with $B_q>0$ for the orders considered. The physical feedback can be seen for first order. Before an outer [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) near [apoapsis](../../../../../../apoapsis.md), the faster inner [planet](../../../../../../planet.md) pulls the outer one backwards; after passing it pulls forwards. On the outward side, the nearer, stronger earlier pull wins, reducing the outer [semi-major axis](../../../../../../semi-major-axis.md) and increasing its [mean motion](../../../../../../mean-motion.md). Thus $\phi_{bc}<\pi$ is driven upward. On the inward side, the later forward pull wins and drives $\phi_{bc}>\pi$ downward. The paired impulses cancel at the symmetric centre.

For higher order, sum the feedback over all $q$ [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) phases. The first surviving angular [Fourier harmonic](../../../../../../fourier-harmonic.md) is $\sin\phi_{bc}$; the lower [Fourier harmonics](../../../../../../fourier-harmonic.md) cancel over the equally spaced directions. A local [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) reduction with canonical [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) $J$ has

$$
H_{\rm res}=-\frac{A_J}{2}J^2-B_qe_c^q\cos\phi_{bc},\quad A_J>0,
\qquad \ddot\phi_{bc}=A_JB_qe_c^q\sin\phi_{bc}.
$$

The negative curvature of the [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md) [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) gives the displayed sign. [Linear stability analysis](../../../../../../linear-stability.md) at $\pi$ gives a restoring [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md). The [symmetric libration centres of an eccentricity resonance](../../../../../../symmetric-libration-centres-of-an-eccentricity-resonance.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\phi_{bc,0}=\pi\quad(q=1,2,3)}.
$$

The corresponding [conjunction](../../../../../../conjunction-astronomy.md) phases relative to [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md) are $\{\pi\}$, $\{\pi/2,3\pi/2\}$ and $\{\pi/3,\pi,5\pi/3\}$. They suppress repeatedly close passages and provide [resonance protection](../../../../../../resonance-protection.md).

These are expected centres of the specified leading-harmonic model, not centres fixed solely by the integer order for arbitrary [orbital eccentricity](../../../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md) and masses. [Asymmetric resonant-argument libration](../../../../../../asymmetric-resonant-argument-libration.md) can occur when higher [Fourier harmonics](../../../../../../fourier-harmonic.md) matter. For example, if $R=A\cos\phi+B\cos2\phi$, $A>0$, $B>A/4$, the symmetric $\pi$ equilibrium loses stability and stable centres satisfy $\cos\phi=-A/(4B)$. Thus further dynamical information is needed beyond the PDF's unrestricted eccentric [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) to assert unique centres. Exterior asymmetric branches are documented by [Winter and Murray, “Resonance and chaos. II. Exterior resonances and asymmetric libration”](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=41311cac53950a41051c987026c8c0a9a3d2029c&repid=rep1&type=pdf).
