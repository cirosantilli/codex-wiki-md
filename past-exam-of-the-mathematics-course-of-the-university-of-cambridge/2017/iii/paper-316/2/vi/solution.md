<h1 id="2/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

**Radiative drag is only one of several loss mechanisms**. Around a [star](../../../../../../star.md), [radiation-pressure blowout](../../../../../../radiation-pressure-blowout-orbit.md) can eject small fragments; its $\beta=1/2$ threshold applies specifically to zero-kick release from a circular parent [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). [Stellar-wind drag](../../../../../../stellar-wind-drag.md) and [gas drag](../../../../../../gas-drag.md) can drive [planetary migration](../../../../../../planetary-migration.md), while [sublimation](../../../../../../sublimation.md) destroys grains approaching high-temperature regions. [Collisional cascades](../../../../../../collisional-cascade.md) destroy or fragment grains and can feed the unbound size range. [Planetary scattering](../../../../../../planetary-scattering.md) can cause ejection, collision with a [planet](../../../../../../planet.md), or a stellar impact; [resonant trapping of dust](../../../../../../resonant-trapping-of-dust.md) can instead delay [planetary migration](../../../../../../planetary-migration.md).

For [circumplanetary orbits](../../../../../../circumplanetary-orbit.md), collisions with the [planet](../../../../../../planet.md) or its satellites, disruption in collisions, and escape under stellar [tidal forces](../../../../../../tidal-force.md) are additional losses. Orbits near or outside the [Hill sphere](../../../../../../hill-sphere.md) need not remain planet-bound. [Radiation pressure on circumplanetary dust](../../../../../../radiation-pressure-on-circumplanetary-dust.md) can excite planetocentric [orbital eccentricity](../../../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md) or unbind very small grains; it need not act only through slow [Poynting–Robertson drag](../../../../../../poynting-robertson-drag.md). For charged grains, the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) in stellar or planetary [magnetic fields](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) can alter or destabilize an [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md). [Shadowing of circumplanetary dust](../../../../../../shadowing-of-circumplanetary-dust.md) changes the radiation-force average and can reduce the quoted decay rate. Which mechanism dominates depends on grain size and composition, environment, [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) orientation and the available collision or gas density.
