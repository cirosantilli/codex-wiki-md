<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At the $d$ line, the [focal lengths](../../../../../../../focal-length.md) obey $1/f_{id}=(n_{id}-1)\rho_i$. Thus

$$
\frac{f_{2d}}{f_{1d}}=\frac{(n_{1d}-1)\rho_1}{(n_{2d}-1)\rho_2}
=-\frac{(n_{1d}-1)(n_{2F}-n_{2C})}{(n_{2d}-1)(n_{1F}-n_{1C})}.
$$

Rearranging gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{f_{2d}}{f_{1d}}=-\frac{(n_{2F}-n_{2C})/(n_{2d}-1)}{(n_{1F}-n_{1C})/(n_{1d}-1)}=-\frac{V_{1d}}{V_{2d}}.}
$$

The last equality uses the [Abbe number](../../../../../../../abbe-number.md) definition. The minus sign signifies a converging and a diverging component, with the normal [optical glass](../../../../../../../optical-glass.md) convention and nonzero finite [focal lengths](../../../../../../../focal-length.md).
