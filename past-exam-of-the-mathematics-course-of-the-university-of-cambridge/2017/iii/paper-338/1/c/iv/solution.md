<h1 id="1/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

$V_d$ is the [Abbe number](../../../../../../../abbe-number.md), a dimensionless inverse measure of [optical dispersion](../../../../../../../dispersion-optics.md). Large $V_d$ means weak dispersion. The [Abbe diagram](../../../../../../../abbe-diagram.md) below spans a representative visible-light [optical glass](../../../../../../../optical-glass.md) range, roughly $1.45\lesssim n_d\lesssim2.0$ and $20\lesssim V_d\lesssim95$. The shaded families are schematic, not exact catalogue boundaries; modern compositions overlap and include high-index [crown glasses](../../../../../../../crown-glass-optics.md).

<a id="1/c/iv/image-an-abbe-diagram-of-representative-optical-glasses"></a>
![](../../../../../../../past-exam-of-the-mathematics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2017/iii/paper-338-glass-map.png)

**[Figure 2](#1/c/iv/image-an-abbe-diagram-of-representative-optical-glasses). An Abbe diagram of representative optical glasses**.

For a usual positive objective, choose component 1 as a higher-$V_d$ [crown glass](../../../../../../../crown-glass-optics.md) and component 2 as a lower-$V_d$ [flint glass](../../../../../../../flint-glass.md). Then $f_{1d}>0$, $f_{2d}<0$, and $|f_{2d}|/f_{1d}=V_{1d}/V_{2d}>1$: **the [crown glass](../../../../../../../crown-glass-optics.md) is converging, the [flint glass](../../../../../../../flint-glass.md) diverging, and their dispersive powers cancel while leaving positive net power**. The [Abbe numbers](../../../../../../../abbe-number.md) alone do not set their absolute [focal lengths](../../../../../../../focal-length.md); the specified total [focal length](../../../../../../../focal-length.md) does. As a representative pair, $V_{1d}=64.17$, $V_{2d}=36.37$ gives $f_{1d}\simeq0.4332f_d$, $f_{2d}\simeq-0.7644f_d$. Representative index and dispersion data are given in [SCHOTT's optical-glass data](https://www.schott.com/de-de/products/optical-glass-p1000267).
