↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Local [sidereal time](../../../../../../../sidereal-time.md) is the [right ascension](../../../../../../../right-ascension.md) on the upper [celestial meridian](../../../../../../../celestial-meridian.md). Since [right ascension](../../../../../../../right-ascension.md) increases eastward while [hour angle](../../../../../../../hour-angle.md) increases westward, their difference is

$$
\boxed{h=\mathrm{LST}-\alpha\pmod{24\ \mathrm{hours}}.}
$$

One hour of either coordinate is $15^\circ$. For a fixed stellar direction, $h$ increases at the sidereal rotation rate. Mean [sidereal time](../../../../../../../sidereal-time.md) should be used with mean [right ascension](../../../../../../../right-ascension.md), or apparent [sidereal time](../../../../../../../sidereal-time.md) with apparent [right ascension](../../../../../../../right-ascension.md), for the same reference equinox.
