<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The fundamental angular floor is the [diffraction limit of a telescope](../../../../../../../diffraction-limit-of-a-telescope.md), of order $\lambda/D$. The ideal [diffraction grating](../../../../../../../diffraction-grating.md) has $R_g=mW/d$, whereas its slit-limited resolving power has $R\theta D=mW\lambda/d$. Requiring $R\le R_g$ gives

$$
\boxed{\theta D\gtrsim\lambda.}
$$

The coefficient depends on the adopted line-resolution and aperture conventions. Below this scale, a narrower [spaxel](../../../../../../../spaxel.md) oversamples the same spatial mode rather than creating a new independently resolved element. Equivalently a spatial optical mode occupies [etendue](../../../../../../../etendue.md) of order $\lambda^2$.

There is also an engineering floor before arbitrary shrinkage: the [optical camera](../../../../../../../optical-camera.md) [focal length](../../../../../../../focal-length.md), [photodetector](../../../../../../../photodetector.md) size and support clearances do not shrink, while the number of [photodetectors](../../../../../../../photodetector.md) increases as $\theta^{-2}$. Thus the shrinking-beam model's constant combined [optical camera](../../../../../../../optical-camera.md) volume is eventually overtaken by fixed per-camera overheads. For fixed brightness, smaller [spaxels](../../../../../../../spaxel.md) receive fewer [photons](../../../../../../../photon.md), so [read noise](../../../../../../../read-noise.md) and [photon shot noise](../../../../../../../photon-shot-noise.md) can constrain useful sampling before the formal optical floor. **[Diffraction](../../../../../../../diffraction.md) limits independent spatial information; fixed [photodetector](../../../../../../../photodetector.md) and mechanical dimensions limit practical volume reduction.**
