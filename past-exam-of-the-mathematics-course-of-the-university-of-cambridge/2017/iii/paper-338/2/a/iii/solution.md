<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Insert the slit-limited [spectral resolving power](../../../../../../../spectral-resolving-power.md) $R=\lambda q/p$ into the invariant expression:

$$
L=\frac{RpA_{\rm cam}}{f_{\rm cam}}=\frac{(\lambda q/p)pA_{\rm cam}}{f_{\rm cam}},
\qquad
\boxed{L=\frac{\lambda qA_{\rm cam}}{f_{\rm cam}}.}
$$

Here $q=|dx/d\lambda|$ is a magnitude; a signed [linear spectral dispersion](../../../../../../../linear-spectral-dispersion.md) would require the corresponding absolute value. Since $q$ has units of [photodetector](../../../../../../../photodetector.md) length per unit [wavelength](../../../../../../../wavelength.md), $L$ has units of length.
