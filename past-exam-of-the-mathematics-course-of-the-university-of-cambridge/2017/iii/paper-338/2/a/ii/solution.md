<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $f_t$ be the [optical telescope](../../../../../../../optical-telescope.md) [focal length](../../../../../../../focal-length.md), $x=f_t\theta$ the physical [optical slit](../../../../../../../optical-slit.md) width, $f_{\rm coll}$ the [collimator](../../../../../../../collimator.md) [focal length](../../../../../../../focal-length.md), and $A_{\rm coll}=Df_{\rm coll}/f_t$ the incident collimated beam [diameter](../../../../../../../diameter.md). For the [grating equation](../../../../../../../grating-equation.md) $m\lambda=d(\sin\alpha+\sin\beta)$, differentiation at fixed [wavelength](../../../../../../../wavelength.md) gives $|d\beta/d\alpha|=\cos\alpha/\cos\beta$. Thus the monochromatic [optical slit](../../../../../../../optical-slit.md) image width is

$$
p=x\frac{f_{\rm cam}}{f_{\rm coll}}\frac{\cos\alpha}{\cos\beta}.
$$

The incident and emergent [optical pupil](../../../../../../../optical-pupil.md) widths are $A_{\rm coll}=W\cos\alpha$ and $A_{\rm cam}=W\cos\beta$. Multiplying cancels the [anamorphic magnification of a grating](../../../../../../../anamorphic-magnification-of-a-grating.md):

$$
\frac{pA_{\rm cam}}{f_{\rm cam}}=\frac{xA_{\rm coll}}{f_{\rm coll}}=\theta D,
\qquad
\boxed{L=R\theta D=\frac{RpA_{\rm cam}}{f_{\rm cam}}.}
$$

This is conservation of the [Lagrange optical invariant](../../../../../../../lagrange-optical-invariant.md) in the dispersion plane. It uses paraxial [optical slit](../../../../../../../optical-slit.md) [angles](../../../../../../../angle.md), an unvignetted [optical pupil](../../../../../../../optical-pupil.md) and matching full-width conventions for $\theta,p,D,A_{\rm cam}$.
