<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let the required [arithmetic progression](../../../../../../arithmetic-progression.md) have length $m\geq2$, let $k$ be the number of colors, and take $n=n(m,k)$ from the [Hales-Jewett theorem](../../../../../../hales-jewett-theorem.md). Given any [finite coloring](../../../../../../finite-coloring.md) $\chi$ of $[mn]$, color a [word over an alphabet](../../../../../../string.md) $w\in[m]^n$ by $\chi(w_1+\cdots+w_n)$. For a [monochromatic](../../../../../../monochromatic-set.md) [combinatorial line](../../../../../../combinatorial-line.md), let $A\ne\varnothing$ be its active coordinate set and let $C$ be the sum of its fixed coordinates. The sums of its $m$ words are

$$
C+|A|,\ C+2|A|,\ldots,\ C+m|A|.
$$

They all lie in $[mn]$, have one color, and form an [arithmetic progression](../../../../../../arithmetic-progression.md) with strictly positive [common difference](../../../../../../common-difference.md) $|A|$. Thus, writing $W(k,m)$ for the finite [Van der Waerden theorem](../../../../../../van-der-waerden-theorem.md) bound,

$$
\boxed{W(k,m)\leq m\,n(m,k).}
$$

The length-one case is immediate. Restricting a [finite coloring](../../../../../../finite-coloring.md) of the [positive integers](../../../../../../positive-integer.md) to this finite [integer interval](../../../../../../integer-interval.md) gives the infinite-domain formulation of the [Van der Waerden theorem](../../../../../../van-der-waerden-theorem.md) as well.
