<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a quasi-static self-gravitating object of fixed [mass](../../../../../../../mass.md), negligible surface [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md), and no accretion, nuclear source or additional deep heating, [conservation of energy](../../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives the [intrinsic planetary luminosity](../../../../../../../intrinsic-planetary-luminosity.md)

$$
L_{\rm int}=-\frac{d}{dt}(U+\Omega),\qquad
\Omega=-\int_0^M\frac{Gm}{r(m)}\,dm.
$$

Here $U$ is internal [energy](../../../../../../../energy.md), and $\Omega$ is negative gravitational [potential energy](../../../../../../../potential-energy.md). Gravitational contraction makes $\Omega$ more negative and releases power $L_{\rm grav}\equiv-d\Omega/dt>0$. Some of that release raises the internal [energy](../../../../../../../energy.md); it cannot all be radiated while the object maintains [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md).

To quantify the split assume thermal gas pressure with constant [specific-heat ratio](../../../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma>4/3$ and negligible rotation or magnetic support. This excludes the dynamically unstable gas-supported regime. The [stellar virial theorem](../../../../../../../stellar-virial-theorem.md) gives

$$
\Omega+3\int P\,dV=0,\qquad
U=\frac1{\gamma-1}\int P\,dV=-\frac{\Omega}{3(\gamma-1)}.
$$

It follows that

$$
\boxed{L_{\rm int}=\frac{3\gamma-4}{3(\gamma-1)}L_{\rm grav}.}
$$

For a monatomic [ideal gas](../../../../../../../ideal-gas.md), $\gamma=5/3$, so $L_{\rm int}=L_{\rm grav}/2$: half the released gravitational [energy](../../../../../../../energy.md) heats the gas and half escapes. If the density profile remains homologous, write $\Omega=-qGM^2/R$, with $q>0$ a fixed structure constant. Then

$$
L_{\rm grav}=-q\frac{GM^2}{R^2}\dot R,\qquad
L_{\rm int}\sim\frac{GM^2}{R\tau_{\rm KH}},
$$

up to the structure and virial coefficients, where $\tau_{\rm KH}$ is the [Kelvin-Helmholtz cooling time](../../../../../../../kelvin-helmholtz-cooling-time.md).

For a diatomic gas with $\gamma=7/5$ the idealized fraction is $1/6$, already demonstrating that the half-factor is not universal. Partial [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md), dissociation, changing structure, accretion and deep heating require the full energy equation. The general link between luminosity and [Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction](../../../../../../../kelvin-helmholtz-mechanism.md) is $L_{\rm int}=-d(U+\Omega)/dt$, with any genuine source terms added explicitly.
