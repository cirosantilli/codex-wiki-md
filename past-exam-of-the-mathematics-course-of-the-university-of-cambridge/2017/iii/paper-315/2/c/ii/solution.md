<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The two measurements sample different atmospheric regions and different [radiative transfer](../../../../../../../radiative-transfer.md) geometries. An [exoplanet emission spectrum](../../../../../../../exoplanet-emission-spectrum.md) probes the dayside; an [exoplanet transmission spectrum](../../../../../../../exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md) probes the morning and evening limbs of the [day-night terminator](../../../../../../../day-night-terminator.md). Several alternatives to an [exoplanet cloud deck](../../../../../../../exoplanet-cloud-deck.md) can therefore suppress the transmission feature.

- A cooler terminator has a smaller [atmospheric scale height](../../../../../../../atmospheric-scale-height.md) $H=k_BT/(\mu m_Hg)$, so the same change in slant [optical depth](../../../../../../../optical-depth.md) produces a smaller transit-area change. A larger [mean molecular weight](../../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight.md) has the same geometric effect. The assumption that the nightside [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md) sets both limbs may itself be wrong.
- Genuine chemical heterogeneity can reduce terminator [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md). At lower [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md), [thermochemical equilibrium](../../../../../../../thermochemical-equilibrium.md) can favour [methane](../../../../../../../methane.md) through $\mathrm{CO}+3\mathrm{H}_2\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{CH}_4+\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm O$, if local reaction times are sufficiently short. [Atmospheric photochemistry](../../../../../../../atmospheric-photochemistry.md), elemental abundance differences, and [horizontal chemical quenching](../../../../../../../horizontal-chemical-quenching.md) can modify this simple picture.
- The feature depends on a band-to-continuum [opacity](../../../../../../../opacity.md) ratio, not abundance alone. A continuum enhanced by other gas absorption or a partly covered heterogeneous limb can mute the feature; the [two-limb transmission spectrum](../../../../../../../two-limb-transmission-spectrum.md) illustrates this averaging. Stellar surface heterogeneity and measurement systematics can also bias an inferred feature amplitude.
- A strong CO emission feature can be amplified by an [atmospheric thermal inversion](../../../../../../../inversion-meteorology.md), because the line forms in hotter gas than the continuum. It is consequently not an unambiguous abundance measurement without a simultaneous [atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](../../../../../../../atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md) fit.

**Temperature, mean molecular weight, limb chemistry, continuum opacity and the dayside temperature gradient are alternatives or degeneracies to test.** In particular, the three-scale-height amplitude alone cannot establish different CO abundances between the hemispheres.
