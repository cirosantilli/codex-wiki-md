<h1 id="4/j/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [J](../j.md)

Three research directions natural in the 2017 setting are:

- Atmospheric characterization of small planets, extending beyond bright [hot Jupiters](../../../../../../hot-jupiter.md) to super-Earth-sized and terrestrial bodies. The aim is to determine whether they retain light envelopes or heavier secondary atmospheres and to understand [exoplanet habitability](../../../../../../exoplanet-habitability.md). Potential [exoplanet biosignatures](../../../../../../exoplanet-biosignature.md) require a chemical and stellar context because abiotic processes can mimic individual molecules; future infrared observatories such as the then-planned [James Webb Space Telescope](../../../../../../james-webb-space-telescope.md) offered a route to better spectra, without guaranteeing a biological interpretation.
- Connecting measured composition to [planet formation](../../../../../../planet-formation.md) and [planetary migration](../../../../../../planetary-migration.md). [Atmospheric metallicity of a giant planet](../../../../../../atmospheric-metallicity-of-a-giant-planet.md), [atmospheric carbon-to-oxygen ratio](../../../../../../atmospheric-carbon-to-oxygen-ratio.md), orbital architecture and bulk [mass](../../../../../../mass.md)-[radius](../../../../../../radius.md) measurements can constrain accretion of gas and solids. [Atmospheric condensate rainout](../../../../../../atmospheric-condensate-rainout.md), [disequilibrium chemistry in an exoplanet atmosphere](../../../../../../disequilibrium-chemistry-in-an-exoplanet-atmosphere.md) and [exoplanet interior-composition degeneracy](../../../../../../exoplanet-interior-composition-degeneracy.md) make a one-to-one mapping from a measured ratio to a birth location unreliable; comparative samples and coupled models are needed.
- Resolving atmospheric structure in space and time. [Exoplanet thermal phase curves](../../../../../../exoplanet-thermal-phase-curve.md), repeated spectra, high-resolution planetary spectroscopy and [exoplanet direct imaging](../../../../../../exoplanet-direct-imaging.md) can test [day-night heat redistribution](../../../../../../day-night-heat-redistribution.md), winds, patchy [exoplanet clouds](../../../../../../exoplanet-cloud.md), [atmospheric escape](../../../../../../atmospheric-escape.md) and chemical heterogeneity. The challenge is to distinguish actual variability or circulation from changes in stellar light and instrumental calibration.

**Small-planet atmospheres, formation through composition, and atmospheric dynamics/variability provide three concrete emerging directions.** The examples describe research aims as of the paper's date, rather than treating later discoveries or later operating missions as established in 2017.
