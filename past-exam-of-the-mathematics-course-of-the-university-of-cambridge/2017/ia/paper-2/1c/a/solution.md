<h1 id="1c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Subtract successive terms and use a [telescoping series](../../../../../../telescoping-series.md):

$$
z_{n+1}-z_1=\sum_{j=1}^n(z_{j+1}-z_j)=\sum_{j=1}^nc_j.
$$

Thus the [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md) has solution

$$
\boxed{z_{n+1}=z_1+\sum_{j=1}^nc_j.}
$$
