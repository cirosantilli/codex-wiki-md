<h1 id="11f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the positive exponent in the PDF. The TeX introduces a minus sign in the numerator while retaining the positive-sign normalizing constant, which would not define the stated [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md). The [Curie–Weiss model](../../../../../../curie-weiss-model.md) assigns equal weights to $s$ and $-s$, since $(\sum_js_j)^2$ is unchanged by simultaneous reversal. This [spin inversion symmetry](../../../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md) pairs every configuration with one having the opposite value of $S_i$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb ES_i=0,\qquad\mathbb P(S_i=1)=\mathbb P(S_i=-1)=\frac12.}
$$
