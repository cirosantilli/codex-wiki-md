<h1 id="11f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $C_{12}=\mathbb E[S_1S_2]\geq0$, using the supplied correlation property of the [Curie–Weiss model](../../../../../../curie-weiss-model.md). Since $\mathbf1_{\{S_i=1\}}=(1+S_i)/2$ and each spin has zero [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value.md),

$$
\mathbb P(S_1=1,S_2=1)
=\frac14\mathbb E[(1+S_1)(1+S_2)]=\frac{1+C_{12}}4.
$$

Divide by $\mathbb P(S_1=1)=1/2$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(S_2=1\mid S_1=1)=\frac{1+C_{12}}2
\geq\frac12=\mathbb P(S_2=1).}
$$

The expression assumes $n\geq2$, as required for $S_2$ to exist. It makes the connection between the spin correlation and the requested [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) explicit.
