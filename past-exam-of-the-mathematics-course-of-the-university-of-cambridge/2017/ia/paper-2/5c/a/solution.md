<h1 id="5c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With no [electrical resistance](../../../../../../electrical-resistance.md) or applied [electric potential difference](../../../../../../electric-potential-difference.md), divide the equation by the [inductance](../../../../../../inductance.md) $L$ to get a [simple harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) with angular frequency $\omega_0=(LC)^{-1/2}$. Its general real solution is

$$
\boxed{I(t)=A\cos(\omega_0t)+B\sin(\omega_0t),\qquad
\omega_0=\frac1{\sqrt{LC}}.}
$$

Each nonzero solution has period $2\pi\sqrt{LC}$. Here $\omega_0$ is an [angular frequency](../../../../../../angular-frequency.md); the ordinary frequency in cycles per unit time is $\omega_0/(2\pi)$.
