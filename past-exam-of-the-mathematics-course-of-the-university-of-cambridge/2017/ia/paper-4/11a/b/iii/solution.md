<h1 id="11a/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For nonzero $\omega$, the [equilibrium points](../../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) satisfy $\sin2\theta=0$. The [equilibrium point](../../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $\theta=0$ is stable: it puts the bead at the point farthest from the rotation axis. Writing $\theta=\eta$ small in the tangential [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) gives

$$
\ddot\eta=-\omega^2\eta+O(\eta^3),\qquad\boxed{\Omega_{\rm osc}=|\omega|,\quad f_{\rm osc}=\frac{|\omega|}{2\pi}.}
$$

Thus the [small oscillation](../../../../../../../small-oscillation.md) is [simple harmonic motion](../../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md), with [angular frequency](../../../../../../../angular-frequency.md) $|\omega|$ and ordinary [frequency](../../../../../../../frequency.md) $|\omega|/(2\pi)$.

The endpoints $\theta=\pm\pi/2$ represent the same rim point $O$. A perturbation there instead satisfies $\ddot\eta=+\omega^2\eta$ and is unstable. Equivalently, the [centrifugal potential](../../../../../../../centrifugal-potential.md) along the hoop is $-\tfrac12\omega^2|\mathbf r|^2=-2a^2\omega^2\cos^2\theta$, whose minimum is at zero and maximum at $O$. If $\omega=0$, every bead position with zero relative [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md) is an [equilibrium point](../../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), but perturbing that [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md) produces unrestored drift; there is no nonzero restoring [frequency](../../../../../../../frequency.md).
