<h1 id="11a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The original PDF measures $\theta$ at the rim point $O$, from the positive $x'$-axis to $OP$; it is not the angle at the centre of the circle. The converted TeX omits this entire subpart. In rotating [Cartesian coordinate system](../../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md), the centre is $(a,0)$, and the nonzero intersection of the ray $\mathbf r=\rho(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$ with $(x'-a)^2+y'^2=a^2$ has $\rho=2a\cos\theta$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r=2a\cos\theta(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0)=a(1+\cos2\theta,\sin2\theta,0).}
$$

The second form stays regular even at the rim point $O$, where the polar radius is zero. Define the outward [unit normal](../../../../../../../unit-normal.md) from the centre and the increasing-angle [unit tangent vector](../../../../../../../unit-tangent-vector.md) by

$$
\mathbf n=(\cos2\theta,\sin2\theta,0),\qquad\boldsymbol\tau=(-\sin2\theta,\cos2\theta,0).
$$

The first two [derivatives](../../../../../../../derivative.md) in the [rotating reference frame](../../../../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md) are $\mathbf v'=2a\dot\theta\boldsymbol\tau$ and $\mathbf a'=2a\ddot\theta\boldsymbol\tau-4a\dot\theta^2\mathbf n$. These describe a [bead on a horizontal hoop rotating about its rim](../../../../../../../bead-on-a-horizontal-hoop-rotating-about-its-rim.md).
