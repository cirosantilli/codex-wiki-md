<h1 id="11a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [rotating-frame derivative formula](../../../../../../rotating-frame-derivative-formula.md) is $(d\mathbf A/dt)_S=(d\mathbf A/dt)_{S'}+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf A$. Applying it twice to the [position](../../../../../../position.md) vector, with constant [angular velocity](../../../../../../angular-velocity.md), gives

$$
\mathbf a_S=\mathbf a_{S'}+2\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf v_{S'}+\boldsymbol\omega\times(\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r).
$$

Solving for $\mathbf a_{S'}$ identifies the displayed terms: its left side is the [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) relative to the [rotating reference frame](../../../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md), the first right-hand term is the inertial [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md), $-2\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf v_{S'}$ is the [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md), and $-\boldsymbol\omega\times(\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r)$ is the outward [centrifugal acceleration](../../../../../../centrifugal-acceleration.md).

For a particle of [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) $m$, replace $\mathbf a_S$ by the actual [force](../../../../../../force.md) divided by $m$ using [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md). The remaining terms then appear as apparent [forces](../../../../../../force.md) in $S'$. There is no [Euler acceleration](../../../../../../euler-acceleration.md) because $\boldsymbol\omega$ is constant and no translational acceleration term because the common origin is fixed in the [inertial frame](../../../../../../inertial-frame.md).
