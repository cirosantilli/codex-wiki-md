<h1 id="9a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [momentum conservation](../../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) and [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) in the laboratory [inertial frame](../../../../../../inertial-frame.md). The [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) has [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) magnitude $E/c$, while the outgoing [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) satisfies the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md). Its total [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) obey

$$
E_e=mc^2+E_1-E_2,\qquad c^2|\mathbf p_e|^2=E_1^2+E_2^2-2E_1E_2\cos\theta.
$$

Substitute these into $E_e^2=m^2c^4+c^2|\mathbf p_e|^2$. Cancelling $m^2c^4$ and $(E_1-E_2)^2$ gives $mc^2(E_1-E_2)=E_1E_2(1-\cos\theta)$. Both photon [energies](../../../../../../energy.md) are positive, so division yields

$$
\boxed{\frac1{E_2}-\frac1{E_1}=\frac{1-\cos\theta}{mc^2},\qquad E_2=\frac{E_1}{1+(E_1/mc^2)(1-\cos\theta)}.}
$$

This is [Compton scattering](../../../../../../compton-scattering.md). The energies include the [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md)'s [rest energy](../../../../../../rest-energy.md); using only its [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) in the mass-shell equation would give the wrong result. The outgoing [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) has unchanged [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) at $\theta=0$ and smaller [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) for a nonzero scattering angle.
