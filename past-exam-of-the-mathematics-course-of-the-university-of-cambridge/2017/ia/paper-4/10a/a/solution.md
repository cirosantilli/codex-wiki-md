<h1 id="10a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [central force](../../../../../../central-force.md) has no component in the angular direction. The angular component of the [acceleration in polar coordinates](../../../../../../acceleration-in-polar-coordinates.md) therefore gives $2\dot r\dot\theta+r\ddot\theta=0$, or

$$
\frac d{dt}(r^2\dot\theta)=0.
$$

For unit [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), $h=r^2\dot\theta$ is the signed [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) perpendicular to the orbital plane, so its magnitude $|h|$ is conserved. Equivalently, the [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) $\mathbf r\times\mathbf F$ vanishes, which also fixes that plane.

The given [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) is $k/r$, whose radial [force](../../../../../../force.md) is $k/r^2$: positive $k$ is repulsive and negative $k$ attractive. The [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) in [polar coordinates](../../../../../../polar-coordinates.md) is $(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2)/2$, so [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12\dot r^2+V_{\rm eff}(r),\qquad V_{\rm eff}(r)=\frac{h^2}{2r^2}+\frac kr.}
$$

For $h\ne0$ and $k>0$, the [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md) is positive, strictly decreasing from $+\infty$ to $0$, and has no stationary point. For $k=-\kappa<0$, it still tends to $+\infty$ at zero, crosses zero at $r=h^2/(2\kappa)$, and has its unique minimum at

$$
\boxed{r_c=\frac{h^2}{\kappa},\qquad V_{\rm eff}(r_c)=-\frac{\kappa^2}{2h^2}.}
$$

It then approaches zero from below. The minimum corresponds to a stable [circular orbit](../../../../../../circular-orbit.md). If $h=0$, the centrifugal barrier disappears; the attractive [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md) is simply $-\kappa/r$ and has no minimum.
