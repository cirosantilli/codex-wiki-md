<h1 id="11f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $s\leq0$, the terms $n^{-s}$ do not tend to zero, so the series diverges by the [term test for divergence](../../../../../../term-test-for-divergence.md). If $s>0$, the terms form a nonnegative decreasing sequence, and the [Cauchy condensation test](../../../../../../cauchy-condensation-test.md) gives the condensed series

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}2^k(2^k)^{-s}
=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}2^{k(1-s)}.
$$

This [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) converges exactly when $1-s<0$. Hence the [p-series](../../../../../../p-series.md) criterion is

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}\text{ converges}\iff s>1}.
$$
