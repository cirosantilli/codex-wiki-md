<h1 id="8c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

First compute

$$
AB=\begin{pmatrix}
-2&2&2\\
-3&-7&-1\\
-1&-4&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The vector $\ell=(1,-2,4)^T$ lies in the [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $(AB)^T$, since $\ell^TAB=0$. If $ABx=d$ is solvable, multiplying by $\ell^T$ gives the necessary compatibility condition

$$
0=\ell^Td=1-2+4k.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{4k=1}.
$$
