<h1 id="13a/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the open strip $-c<\operatorname{Im}\lambda<c$, neither pole of $g$ is present. The two evaluations therefore give

$$
\boxed{H(\lambda)+J(\lambda)=\left(g(\lambda)-\frac{r_+}{\lambda-\alpha_+}\right)+\frac{r_+}{\lambda-\alpha_+}=g(\lambda)}.
$$

Both summands are [holomorphic](../../../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) on this strip. This is a [half-plane decomposition by Cauchy integrals](../../../../../../../half-plane-decomposition-by-cauchy-integrals.md); the pole subtraction in $H$ is what makes its extension [holomorphic](../../../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) even at the upper pole of $g$.
