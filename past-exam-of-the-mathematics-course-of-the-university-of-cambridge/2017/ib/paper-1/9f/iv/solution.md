↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**False.** Take $U=\mathbb R^2$, $V=\mathbb R$, $\alpha(x,y)=x+y$, $X=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0)\}$, and $Y=\operatorname{span}\{(0,1)\}$. Then $U=X\oplus Y$ is a [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md), but $\alpha(X)=\alpha(Y)=\mathbb R$, so their intersection is not zero. Their ordinary sum is $V$, as always follows from [surjective function](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) and $U=X+Y$; the failure is precisely the [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md) assertion.
