↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** Use the lifted [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $u_1,\ldots,u_r$ above and put $W=\operatorname{span}\{u_1,\ldots,u_r\}$. If $\sum c_i u_i=0$, applying $\alpha$ gives $\sum c_i v_i=0$, so all $c_i=0$. Thus the $u_i$ form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of $W$, and $\alpha|_W$ takes this [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) to the [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of $V$. It is a [linear isomorphism](../../../../../../linear-isomorphism.md).

In fact the [right inverse](../../../../../../right-inverse.md) gives the stronger splitting

$$
\boxed{U=\ker\alpha\oplus W}.
$$

For every $u\in U$, write $u=(u-\beta\alpha u)+\beta\alpha u$; the first term is in the [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) and the second in $W$. Their intersection is zero because $\alpha|_W$ is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md). This is a [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md) decomposition.
