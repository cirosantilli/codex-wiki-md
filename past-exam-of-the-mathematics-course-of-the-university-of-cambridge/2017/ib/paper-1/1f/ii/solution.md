<h1 id="1f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $S$ is [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) and has $n$ elements, apply the [Steinitz exchange lemma](../../../../../../steinitz-exchange-lemma.md) with $S$ as the independent family and the [standard basis](../../../../../../standard-basis.md) as the spanning family. All $n$ members of the [standard basis](../../../../../../standard-basis.md) are replaced, leaving $S$ itself spanning. Thus $\boxed{\operatorname{span}S=\mathbb R^n}$, so $S$ is a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md).
