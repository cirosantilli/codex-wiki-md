<h1 id="17d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [volume flux](../../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit transverse width is

$$
Q=\int_0^h u(z)\,dz=\frac{\rho g\sin\alpha}{3\mu}h^3-\frac{S}{2\mu}h^2.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{Q=0\iff S=\frac23\rho gh\sin\alpha}.
$$

Assume a genuinely inclined plane, $0<\alpha<\pi/2$. With $Z=z/h$ and $U=\rho gh^2\sin\alpha/\mu$, the zero-flux [velocity profile](../../../../../../velocity-profile.md) is $u/U=Z/3-Z^2/2$. It is positive for $0<Z<2/3$, zero at $Z=2/3$, and negative above that level. Its maximum is $U/18$ at $Z=1/3$, and its surface value is $-U/6$. Thus equal integrated downslope and upslope transports cancel, rather than the fluid being motionless.
