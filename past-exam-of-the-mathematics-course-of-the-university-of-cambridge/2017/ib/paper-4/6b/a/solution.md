<h1 id="6b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md) is a [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) and a simultaneous formal [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) of the [momentum operator](../../../../../../momentum-operator.md) and the time-translation energy operator:

$$
-i\hbar\partial_x\phi=\hbar k\phi,\qquad i\hbar\partial_t\phi=E\phi.
$$

Its [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) and [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) are therefore definite, while its [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) is spatially uniform:

$$
\boxed{p=\hbar k,\qquad \mathcal E=E,\qquad |\phi|^2=A^2}.
$$

For a particle of mass $m$, the [probability current](../../../../../../probability-current.md) is $j=(\hbar/m)\operatorname{Im}(\overline\phi\,\partial_x\phi)=\hbar kA^2/m$, directed according to the sign of $k$.

On the whole [real line](../../../../../../real-line.md), a nonzero constant-amplitude [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) is not [square-integrable](../../../../../../square-integrable-function.md) and cannot be normalised to total [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) one. It represents an idealised [momentum eigenstate](../../../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md), interpreted through [generalized eigenfunctions](../../../../../../generalized-eigenfunction.md) or as a limit of [wave packets](../../../../../../wave-packet.md). Also, arbitrary $k,E$ do not automatically solve the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) for a specified [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md): for a free particle, $E=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$, or this value plus the constant [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) for a constant-potential region. If $A=0$, the zero function is not a physical quantum state.
