<h1 id="13e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\{O_i\}$ be an [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) of $Z$ in its [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md). Write $O_i=Z\cap U_i$ with each $U_i$ open in $X$. Since $Z$ is a [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md), $X\setminus Z$ is open, and $\{U_i\}\cup\{X\setminus Z\}$ is an [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) of the [compact space](../../../../../../compact-space.md) $X$. A finite subcover restricts to a finite subcover of $Z$. Hence **the closed subset $Z$ is compact**.

If $\{V_j\}$ is an [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) of $f(Z)$ in its [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md), the inverse images under the restricted [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) $f|_Z$ form an [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) of $Z$. Take a finite subcover; the corresponding $V_j$ cover $f(Z)$. Thus **the [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) image $f(Z)$ is compact**. Neither argument assumes a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) or asserts that a [compact set](../../../../../../compact-space.md) must be [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md) in an arbitrary [topological space](../../../../../../topological-space.md).
