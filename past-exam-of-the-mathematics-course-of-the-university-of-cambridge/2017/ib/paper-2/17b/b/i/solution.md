<h1 id="17b/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $d=qE$. With the stated unit mass and oscillator frequency, complete the square:

$$
U_0+U_1=\frac{x^2}{2}-qEx=\frac{(x-d)^2}{2}-\frac{d^2}{2}.
$$

Translation by $d$ preserves the spectrum of the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md), and the last term is a constant energy shift. The [displaced quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../../displaced-quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) therefore has

$$
\boxed{E_n=\hbar\left(n+\frac12\right)-\frac{q^2E^2}{2},\qquad n\ge0.}
$$

Its normalized eigenfunctions are the original oscillator eigenfunctions evaluated at $x-d$, in particular the [ground state](../../../../../../../ground-state.md) is $(\pi\hbar)^{-1/4}e^{-(x-d)^2/(2\hbar)}$. The energy gaps remain $\hbar$.
