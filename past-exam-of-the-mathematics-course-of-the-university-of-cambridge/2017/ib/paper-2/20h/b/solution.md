<h1 id="20h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Starting at zero, the next renewal is at time $Y_1$, and there are no intervening renewals because $Y_1\ge1$. Thus the [first return time](../../../../../../first-return-time.md) obeys $T_0=Y_1$ in distribution, indeed under the original renewal construction. Its [mean recurrence time](../../../../../../mean-recurrence-time.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E_0T_0=\mathbb EY_1=\mu.}
$$

The chain returns to zero almost surely and has a finite expected return time, so zero is a [positive recurrent state](../../../../../../positive-recurrent-state.md). The assumption $\mu<\infty$ is what upgrades recurrence to positive recurrence.
