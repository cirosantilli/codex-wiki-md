<h1 id="2e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $\boxed{R=\mathbb Q[X]}$ and, for the next clause, its subring $S=\mathbb Z[X]$. Division with remainder makes $R$ a [Euclidean domain](../../../../../../../euclidean-domain.md), with the degree as Euclidean size for nonzero polynomials. For completeness, a nonzero [ideal](../../../../../../../ideal.md) of $R$ contains a polynomial $g$ of least degree; division of any member by $g$ leaves a smaller-degree remainder in the ideal, so that remainder must vanish. The ideal is therefore $(g)$; the zero ideal is already principal. Thus $R$ is a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md).
