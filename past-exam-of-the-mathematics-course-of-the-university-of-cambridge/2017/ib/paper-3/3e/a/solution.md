<h1 id="3e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [product topology](../../../../../../product-topology.md) has as a [basis of a topology](../../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) all sets $U\times V$, where $U$ is open in $X$ and $V$ is open in $Y$. Open sets are arbitrary unions of these rectangles. The [projection maps](../../../../../../projection-map.md) are $p(x,y)=x$ and $q(x,y)=y$. For any open $U\subset X$ and $V\subset Y$,

$$
\boxed{p^{-1}(U)=U\times Y,\qquad q^{-1}(V)=X\times V.}
$$

These are open rectangles, including the empty case. Taking inverse images of open sets therefore proves that both [projection maps](../../../../../../projection-map.md) are [continuous functions](../../../../../../continuous-function.md).
