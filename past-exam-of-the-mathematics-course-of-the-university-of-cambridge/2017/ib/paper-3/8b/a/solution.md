<h1 id="8b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Inside the [infinite square well](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md), the time-independent [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) is $-(\hbar^2/(2m))\psi''=E\psi$. The infinite exterior potential imposes $\psi(0)=\psi(a)=0$. For $E>0$, the first condition leaves a sine and the second requires $ka=n\pi$. A zero or negative energy gives only the zero solution under these conditions. The normalized [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) and [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are

$$
\boxed{\psi_n(x)=\sqrt{\frac2a}\sin\frac{n\pi x}{a},\qquad E_n=\frac{\hbar^2\pi^2n^2}{2ma^2},\quad n=1,2,\ldots.}
$$

The [wavefunctions](../../../../../../wave-function.md) vanish outside the well, and a constant phase is immaterial. Normalization uses $\int_0^a\sin^2(n\pi x/a)\,dx=a/2$.
