<h1 id="8b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) gives the probability of $E_n$ as the squared coefficient of the initial state in the [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) basis. Direct integration gives

$$
c_n=\int_0^{a/2}\sqrt{\frac2a}\,\psi_n(x)\,dx
=\frac2{n\pi}\left(1-\cos\frac{n\pi}2\right).
$$

The strict energy cutoff corresponds to $n^2<5$, so only $n=1,2$ contribute. Both coefficients equal $2/\pi$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P\!\left(E<\frac{5\hbar^2\pi^2}{2ma^2}\right)=\frac8{\pi^2}.}
$$

The discontinuous initial [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md) is a legitimate normalized $L^2$ state for this spectral probability calculation, although it is not in the Hamiltonian's differentiability domain; finite mean energy is not required to compute this probability.
