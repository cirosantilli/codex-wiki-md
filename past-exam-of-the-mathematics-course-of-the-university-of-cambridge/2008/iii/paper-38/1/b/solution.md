<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let the finite primal supremum be $v$. First it is attained. The set

$$
K=\{(Ax+s,c^Tx-t):x,s,t\geq0\}
$$

is a [finitely generated cone](../../../../../../finitely-generated-cone.md), hence closed by the argument in the root Solution. For a sequence of primal [feasible points](../../../../../../feasible-point.md) whose values approach $v$, the pairs $(b,c^Tx)$ belong to $K$. Their limit $(b,v)$ therefore belongs to $K$. It gives a [feasible point](../../../../../../feasible-point.md) $x_*$ with $c^Tx_*\geq v$, so necessarily $c^Tx_*=v$.

Let $I$ index the tight primal inequalities at $x_*$ and let $Z$ index its zero coordinates. Consider the closed [convex cone](../../../../../../convex-cone.md) generated by $A_i^T$ for $i\in I$ and $-e_j$ for $j\in Z$. If $c$ were outside this cone, [separation from a closed convex cone](../../../../../../separation-from-a-closed-convex-cone.md) would give a direction $d$ with

$$
A_id\leq0\ (i\in I),\qquad d_j\geq0\ (j\in Z),\qquad c^Td>0.
$$

For sufficiently small positive $\delta$, $x_*+\delta d$ remains a [feasible point](../../../../../../feasible-point.md): the active constraints have the required signs, and every inactive constraint retains its positive slack. Its larger objective contradicts optimality. Therefore there are $y_i\geq0$, supported on $I$, and $\mu_j\geq0$, supported on $Z$, such that

$$
c=A^Ty-\mu.
$$

This makes $y$ a dual [feasible point](../../../../../../feasible-point.md). Moreover, the support restrictions give

$$
b^Ty=y^TAx_*=c^Tx_*+\mu^Tx_*=v.
$$

Together with [weak duality](../../../../../../weak-duality.md), this proves [strong duality](../../../../../../strong-duality.md) and dual attainment:

$$
\boxed{D\text{ is feasible, has minimum }v,\text{ and is bounded below}.}
$$
