<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fix a primal [feasible point](../../../../../../feasible-point.md) $x_0$. For every dual [feasible point](../../../../../../feasible-point.md) $y$, nonnegativity and the primal and dual inequalities give

$$
c^Tx_0\leq x_0^TA^Ty=y^TAx_0\leq y^Tb.
$$

Hence **the dual objective is bounded below by $c^Tx_0$ wherever it is feasible**, which is [weak duality](../../../../../../weak-duality.md). This is the meaning of “bounded” in this assertion; it does not say that the dual feasible set is bounded, or that it is nonempty. For example, with $A=0$, $b=0$, $c=1$ and a single variable, the primal is feasible and unbounded above, while its dual would require $0\geq1$ and is infeasible.
