<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The divisors have no common irreducible component, since a common curve would give infinitely many intersection points. The [Bézout theorem](../../../../../../bezout-s-theorem.md) therefore gives total [intersection multiplicity](../../../../../../intersection-multiplicity.md) $\deg C\deg D=3\cdot3=9$. As all intersection is supported at $p$,

$$
\boxed{I_p(C,D)=9.}
$$
