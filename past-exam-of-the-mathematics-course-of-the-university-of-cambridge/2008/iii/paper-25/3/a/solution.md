<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $D$ be an [effective divisor](../../../../../../effective-cartier-divisor.md) [linearly equivalent](../../../../../../linear-equivalence-of-weil-divisors.md) to $C$. If $C$ were not a component of $D$, all [intersection multiplicities](../../../../../../intersection-multiplicity.md) between its components and $C$ would be nonnegative, contradicting $D\cdot C=C^2<0$. Hence $D=C+D'$ with $D'$ effective and [linearly equivalent](../../../../../../linear-equivalence-of-weil-divisors.md) to zero. For an [ample divisor](../../../../../../ample-cartier-divisor.md) $H$, this gives $H\cdot D'=0$. Positivity of $H$ on every irreducible component of a nonzero effective divisor forces $D'=0$. Therefore $\boxed{D=C}$, illustrating the [rigidity of a negative curve](../../../../../../rigidity-of-a-negative-curve.md).
