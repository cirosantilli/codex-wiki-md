<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [adjunction formula](../../../../../../adjunction-formula.md) on the degree-$d$ [projective hypersurface](../../../../../../projective-hypersurface.md) gives $K_X=(d-4)H$. Since $C$ is a [smooth rational curve](../../../../../../smooth-rational-curve.md), adjunction on $C$ gives $(K_X+C)\cdot C=2g(C)-2=-2$. Its degree is $H\cdot C=a$, so the [smooth rational-curve self-intersection on a projective hypersurface](../../../../../../smooth-rational-curve-self-intersection-on-a-projective-hypersurface.md) formula is

$$
\boxed{C^2=-2-(d-4)a.}
$$
