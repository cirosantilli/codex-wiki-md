<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $F$ be the generator of the [smooth flow](../../../../../../smooth-flow.md). The [Anosov flow](../../../../../../anosov-flow.md) property requires $F$ to be nowhere zero and a continuous splitting of the [tangent bundle](../../../../../../tangent-bundle.md)

$$
TN=E^s\oplus\mathbb RF\oplus E^u
$$

which is invariant under $D\phi_t$. There must be constants $C\ge1$ and $\kappa>0$ such that, for every $x$, every $t\ge0$ and every vector in the indicated fibre,

$$
\boxed{\|D\phi_t\xi_s\|\le Ce^{-\kappa t}\|\xi_s\|,
\qquad
\|D\phi_{-t}\xi_u\|\le Ce^{-\kappa t}\|\xi_u\|.}
$$

The first inequality defines the [Anosov stable bundle](../../../../../../stable-bundle-of-an-anosov-flow.md); the second defines the [Anosov unstable bundle](../../../../../../unstable-bundle-of-an-anosov-flow.md) and is equivalent to uniform forward expansion there. The remaining one-dimensional direction is the flow direction. The [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) can come from any [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md), since norms are uniformly equivalent on a [closed manifold](../../../../../../closed-manifold.md). The essential requirements are a continuous invariant splitting and estimates uniform in both the base point and time, rather than separate exponential rates along individual orbits.
