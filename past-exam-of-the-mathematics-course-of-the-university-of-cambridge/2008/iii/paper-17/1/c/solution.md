<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) to represent a tangent vector to the [unit tangent bundle](../../../../../../unit-tangent-bundle.md) at $(x,v)$ by the initial data $(J(0),D_tJ(0))$ of the [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) generated by a variation of the unit-speed [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md). The unit-speed constraint gives $\langle D_tJ(0),v\rangle=0$. Along the [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md), write

$$
J(t)=a\dot\gamma(t)+y(t)i\dot\gamma(t),\qquad D_tJ(t)=\dot y(t)i\dot\gamma(t).
$$

Here $a$ is constant, while the perpendicular [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) equation is

$$
\ddot y+K(\gamma(t))y=0.
$$

The flow direction is represented by $(a,y,\dot y)=(1,0,0)$. Consequently $(y,z)=(y,\dot y)$ are coordinates on the transverse quotient. Define its nondegenerate [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form.md) by $Q(y,z)=yz$. Its [signature of a quadratic form](../../../../../../signature-of-a-quadratic-form.md) is $(1,1)$, and

$$
\frac{d}{dt}Q(y(t),z(t))=z(t)^2-K(\gamma(t))y(t)^2.
$$

Since the [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is strictly negative and the surface is a [closed manifold](../../../../../../closed-manifold.md), there is $k_0>0$ with $K\le-k_0$. Hence

$$
\dot Q\ge\min(1,k_0)(y^2+z^2).
$$

The [quadratic-form criterion for an Anosov flow](../../../../../../quadratic-form-criterion-for-an-anosov-flow.md) now proves **the geodesic flow is Anosov**. This is the [geodesic hyperbolicity from negative Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../geodesic-hyperbolicity-from-negative-gaussian-curvature.md) argument. If the surface is not oriented, the local choice of perpendicular unit vector changes both $y$ and $z$ in sign; their product is unchanged, so the [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form.md) still exists globally.

When $K=-1$, the perpendicular solutions are $y=Ae^t+Be^{-t}$. The pure contracting data have $(y,z)=(B,-B)$ and the pure expanding data have $(y,z)=(A,A)$. In the [canonical coframe of a surface unit tangent bundle](../../../../../../canonical-coframe-of-a-surface-unit-tangent-bundle.md), $H$ represents $(iv,0)$ and $V$ represents $(0,iv)$, so

$$
\boxed{E^s(x,v)=\mathbb R(H-V),\qquad E^u(x,v)=\mathbb R(H+V).}
$$

Equivalently the [Anosov stable bundle](../../../../../../stable-bundle-of-an-anosov-flow.md) consists of pairs $(\eta,-\eta)$ with $\eta\perp v$, and the [Anosov unstable bundle](../../../../../../unstable-bundle-of-an-anosov-flow.md) consists of $(\eta,\eta)$. The derivative multiplies these data by $e^{-t}$ and $e^t$, respectively, using parallel transport along the [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) to identify the perpendicular directions. Together with $\mathbb RX$ they give the required invariant splitting.
