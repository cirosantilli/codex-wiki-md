<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\pi:SM\to M$ be the [unit tangent bundle](../../../../../../unit-tangent-bundle.md) projection. Write $X$ for the [geodesic flow](../../../../../../geodesic-flow.md) generator and $V$ for the [vertical vector field of a surface unit tangent bundle](../../../../../../vertical-vector-field-of-a-surface-unit-tangent-bundle.md), positively rotating $v$ toward $iv$. The acceleration prescribed in the question changes only this velocity angle, so the [surface thermostat](../../../../../../generalized-thermostat-on-a-riemannian-surface.md) generator is

$$
F=X+\lambda V.
$$

It never vanishes: $d\pi(F)=v\ne0$. Also $F$ and $V$ are linearly independent, since $d\pi(V)=0$.

The [vertical Maslov cycle of a surface thermostat](../../../../../../vertical-maslov-cycle-of-a-surface-thermostat.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Lambda_V=\{((x,v),L):V(x,v)\in L\}
=\{((x,v),\mathbb RF(x,v)\oplus\mathbb RV(x,v))\}.}
$$

There is exactly one such plane in each fibre, so $\Lambda_V$ is the image of a smooth section. A two-plane containing $F$ corresponds to a line in the rank-two quotient $T(SM)/\mathbb RF$; thus $\Lambda(SM)$ is a [projective bundle](../../../../../../projective-bundle.md) with fibre $\mathbb{RP}^1$. Its total dimension is four and the cycle has dimension three, making it a smooth hypersurface. In this two-dimensional transverse space the [Maslov cycle](../../../../../../maslov-cycle.md) means the projective line meeting the distinguished vertical line, which here is just that distinguished line itself in each fibre.
