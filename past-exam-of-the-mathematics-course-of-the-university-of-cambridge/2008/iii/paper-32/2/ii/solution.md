<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $\alpha=\lambda^{1/5}$, $M=\mathbb Q(\alpha)$ and $E=\mathbb Q(\zeta_5)$. The [Eisenstein criterion](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at $\lambda$ gives $[M:\mathbb Q]=5$, whereas the [prime cyclotomic field degree](../../../../../../prime-cyclotomic-field-degree.md) gives $[E:\mathbb Q]=4$. The [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) $F=E(\alpha)$ is a [Galois extension](../../../../../../finite-galois-extension.md) of $E$, and an automorphism fixing $E$ sends $\alpha$ to $\zeta_5^b\alpha$. Thus its [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) embeds in $C_5$. It is nontrivial, since $M$ cannot be contained in the degree-four field $E$. Therefore $[F:E]=5$ and $[F:\mathbb Q]=20$.

Index the five roots by $j\in\mathbb F_5$, with root $\zeta_5^j\alpha$. Every automorphism has the form $\zeta_5\mapsto\zeta_5^u$, $\alpha\mapsto\zeta_5^b\alpha$ and acts on indices as $j\mapsto uj+b$, with $u\in\mathbb F_5^\times$. This is a faithful inclusion of $G=\operatorname{Gal}(F/\mathbb Q)$ into the [affine group over a finite field](../../../../../../affine-group-over-a-finite-field.md), and both groups have order $20$, so it is an isomorphism. This is the [affine Galois group of a prime-radical splitting field](../../../../../../affine-galois-group-of-a-prime-radical-splitting-field.md).

Let $D$ be the specified [decomposition group](../../../../../../decomposition-group.md). Its nonidentity element is an [involution](../../../../../../involution.md). If its linear coefficient were $1$, it would be a nontrivial translation, which has order $5$. Hence it acts as $j\mapsto-j+b$. There is exactly one fixed root, at $j=b/2$, and the other four roots form two pairs. By [involution orbits in an affine group of odd prime degree](../../../../../../involution-orbits-in-an-affine-group-of-odd-prime-degree.md), $D$ has three orbits on the five roots. Those roots identify with $G/\operatorname{Gal}(F/M)$, so the double-coset formula gives three primes in $M$.

For $E$, the embeddings identify with $G/\operatorname{Gal}(F/E)\simeq\mathbb F_5^\times$. The image of $D$ is $\{1,-1\}$ and acts by multiplication, giving the two orbits $\{1,-1\}$ and $\{2,-2\}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\#\{\mathfrak m\mid p\text{ in }M\}=3,\qquad\#\{\mathfrak e\mid p\text{ in }E\}=2.}
$$

The orbit argument counts primes whether or not $p$ is ramified; cyclicity of the given order-two [decomposition group](../../../../../../decomposition-group.md) does not require that it be generated by an unramified Frobenius element.
