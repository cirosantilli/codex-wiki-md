<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [polynomial discriminant](../../../../../../polynomial-discriminant.md) satisfies

$$
\operatorname{disc}(f)=(-1)^{n(n-1)/2}\operatorname{Res}(f,f'),\qquad n=\deg f.
$$

A polynomial has no repeated roots over the algebraic closure exactly when it is coprime to its derivative. Consequently the assumed squarefree reduction has nonzero [resultant](../../../../../../resultant.md) with its derivative, so $p\nmid\operatorname{disc}(f)$. On the other hand the power basis is a basis of the [order in a number field](../../../../../../order-in-a-number-field.md) $\mathbb Z[\alpha]$, and the [discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice](../../../../../../discriminant-index-formula-for-an-integral-lattice.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{disc}(f)=\operatorname{disc}(1,\alpha,\ldots,\alpha^{n-1})=[\mathcal O_L:\mathbb Z[\alpha]]^2d_L.
$$

Here the [field discriminant](../../../../../../field-discriminant.md) $d_L$ is an integer. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p\nmid[\mathcal O_L:\mathbb Z[\alpha]].}
$$

This is [squarefree reduction excludes a prime from the power-basis index](../../../../../../squarefree-reduction-excludes-a-prime-from-the-power-basis-index.md); its argument establishes the index condition before applying the [Kummer-Dedekind theorem](../../../../../../kummer-dedekind-theorem.md).
