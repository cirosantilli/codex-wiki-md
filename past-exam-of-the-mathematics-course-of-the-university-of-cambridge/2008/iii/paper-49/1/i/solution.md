<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the ordered basis $(K_1,K_2,J)$, the three [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) matrices are

$$
A_{K_1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\0&0&-\lambda\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
A_{K_2}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&\lambda\\0&0&0\\-1&0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
A_J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\lambda&0\\\lambda&0&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Their cross-product traces vanish, while their squared traces are $-2\lambda$, $-2\lambda$ and $-2\lambda^2$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\kappa=-2\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda,\lambda^2).}
$$

This is the [Killing form for cyclic three-generator brackets](../../../../../../killing-form-for-cyclic-three-generator-brackets.md) with constants $(1,\lambda,\lambda)$.

For $\lambda>0$, the form is nondegenerate and negative-definite. The algebra is **semisimple and compact**, isomorphic to the [SU(2) Lie algebra](../../../../../../su-2-lie-algebra.md). For $\lambda<0$, it is nondegenerate with signature $(2,1)$, so the algebra is **semisimple and noncompact**, isomorphic to $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb R)$.

For $\lambda=0$, the only nonzero bracket is $[K_1,K_2]=J$, with $J$ central. This is the [Heisenberg Lie algebra](../../../../../../heisenberg-lie-algebra.md), which is nilpotent and **neither semisimple nor compact**. Compactness is ruled out even though the Killing form is merely degenerate: $A_{K_1}$ is nonzero and nilpotent, whereas in a [compact Lie algebra](../../../../../../compact-lie-algebra.md) every adjoint map is skew-adjoint for a positive inner product. [Skew-adjoint nilpotent operators vanish](../../../../../../skew-adjoint-nilpotent-operators-vanish.md).
