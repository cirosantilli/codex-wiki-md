<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Separate a smooth large-scale [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $U_L$ from a remaining smaller-scale component $w_L$. If the large-scale variation length is much greater than $r$, their longitudinal increment has the form

$$
\Delta v=rA+\Delta w+O(r^2),\qquad A=\partial_LU_L.
$$

For a positive integer $p$, the leading expansion of its structure function is

$$
\langle(\Delta v)^p\rangle
=\sum_{j=0}^p\binom pj r^j\langle A^j(\Delta w)^{p-j}\rangle
+\text{higher Taylor terms}.
$$

Thus even a [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) component at scales much larger than $r$ enters through its [gradients](../../../../../../gradient.md) and through mixed moments with the finer field. It is not generally legitimate to discard those mixed moments: large-scale strain can organize and modulate the finer motions. For example, at fourth order the expansion contains

$$
6r^2\langle A^2(\Delta w)^2\rangle+r^4\langle A^4\rangle,
$$

in addition to the small-scale fourth moment and the generally present odd mixed terms. Even if the components were independent and symmetric, the displayed mixed second-moment term would survive. This explicitly exhibits [large-scale contributions to structure functions](../../../../../../large-scale-contributions-to-structure-functions.md) beyond second order.

The implication for [K62 refined similarity](../../../../../../kolmogorov-refined-similarity-hypothesis.md) is that an observed structure function need not measure a purely scale-local [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) fluctuation. Dividing it by $(\epsilon_r r)^{1/3}$ may still leave dependence on outer-scale [gradients](../../../../../../gradient.md), correlations or finite-range cutoffs. The universal conditional coefficients $\beta_p$ and the identification of a measured exponent with the [lognormal intermittency model](../../../../../../lognormal-intermittency-model.md) therefore require additional locality and scale-separation tests. **Finite-range mixed contributions can imitate or obscure anomalous scaling.** This is an objection to treating the 1962 dimensional argument as an unconditional law for measured increments; it is not, by itself, a proof that an asymptotic refined similarity regime cannot exist.
