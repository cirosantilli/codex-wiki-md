<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The conserved total [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $\mathbf J_t=(0,0,2)$, and the [black-hole spin](../../../../../../black-hole-spin.md) magnitude is

$$
J_h=\sqrt{\frac34+\frac94}=\sqrt3.
$$

The initial hole is noncollinear with the total vector, so persistent positive alignment [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) leads to the positive $z$ direction. The final vectors are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf J_{h,f}=(0,0,\sqrt3),\qquad
\mathbf J_{d,f}=(0,0,2-\sqrt3).}
$$

The final disk magnitude is $2-\sqrt3$, smaller than its initial magnitude one, and its direction is parallel to the hole. Their sum remains $(0,0,2)$.
