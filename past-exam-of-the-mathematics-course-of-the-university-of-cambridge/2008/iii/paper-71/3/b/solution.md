Here the conserved total [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $\mathbf J_t=(0,0,1)$, while the [black-hole spin](../../../../../../black-hole-spin.md) magnitude remains $\sqrt3$. The hole again aligns with the positive $z$ direction in the persistent-[torque](../../../../../../torque.md) regime. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf J_{h,f}=(0,0,\sqrt3),\qquad
\mathbf J_{d,f}=(0,0,1-\sqrt3).}
$$

Since $1-\sqrt3<0$, the disk points in the negative $z$ direction and has magnitude $\sqrt3-1$. This realizes [counteralignment of a black hole and accretion disk](../../../../../../counteralignment-of-a-black-hole-and-accretion-disk.md) even though the hole itself is aligned with the conserved total vector. **The black hole and disk do not always finish with aligned spins.**
