<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $F=\sup_\Omega f^+\geq0$ and $m=\inf_{\partial\Omega}u$, both finite by [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on the compact closure. Set $z=Fv+m-u$. Constants are annihilated by $L$, so

$$
Lz=F Lv-f\geq F-f\geq0.
$$

On the boundary, $z\leq m-u\leq0$, using $v\leq0$ and $F\geq0$. The [weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](../../../../../../weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) therefore gives $z\leq0$ inside as well. Rearrangement proves

$$
\boxed{u(x)\geq\left(\sup_\Omega f^+\right)v(x)+\inf_{\partial\Omega}u.}
$$

The nonnegative multiplier is essential when multiplying the barrier inequality.
