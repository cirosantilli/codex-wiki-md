<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The space $W_0^{1,2}(\Omega)$ is the closure of $C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ in the [Sobolev norm](../../../../../../sobolev-norm.md); membership imposes the homogeneous Dirichlet condition in the weak sense, without assuming that the boundary is smooth. A [weak solution](../../../../../../weak-solution.md) is an element of that space satisfying

$$
\boxed{\int_\Omega a_{ij}D_i uD_j\phi\,dx=-\int_\Omega f\phi\,dx\quad\text{for every }\phi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega).}
$$

Repeated indices are summed. The minus sign comes from integration by parts in the divergence equation. Boundedness of the coefficients, [Hölder's inequality](../../../../../../holder-s-inequality.md) and density extend this identity to every $\phi\in W_0^{1,2}(\Omega)$. Symmetry permits interchanging the derivative indices in the left side.
