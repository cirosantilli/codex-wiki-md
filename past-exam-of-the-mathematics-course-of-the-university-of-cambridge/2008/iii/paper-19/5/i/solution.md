<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use product coordinates $x^i$ on $M$ and $y^\alpha$ on $N$. The lifted [vector fields](../../../../../../vector-field.md) have the form $X=\sum_iX^i(x)\partial_{x^i}$ and $Y=\sum_\alpha Y^\alpha(y)\partial_{y^\alpha}$. The coefficients of each depend only on its own coordinate set, and the coordinate partial derivatives commute. The [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) formula therefore gives

$$
[X,Y]=\sum_{i,\alpha}X^i\partial_{x^i}Y^\alpha\,\partial_{y^\alpha}-Y^\alpha\partial_{y^\alpha}X^i\,\partial_{x^i}=0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{[X,Y]=0}$ for fields lifted separately from the two factors. The lift hypothesis matters: arbitrary product-manifold fields can have coefficients depending on both factors.
