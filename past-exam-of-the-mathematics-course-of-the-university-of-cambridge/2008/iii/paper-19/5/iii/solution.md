<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $Y$ is lifted from $N$, its covariant derivative $\nabla_YY$ is also lifted from $N$. Part (ii) therefore gives $\nabla_X(\nabla_YY)=0$. It also gives $\nabla_XY=0$, and part (i) gives $[X,Y]=0$. Substituting into the [Riemannian curvature two-form](../../../../../../riemannian-curvature-two-form.md) definition yields

$$
R(X,Y)Y=\nabla_X\nabla_YY-\nabla_Y\nabla_XY-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Y=0.
$$

With the four-slot convention fixed above, this proves

$$
\boxed{R(X,Y,X,Y)=g(R(X,Y)Y,X)=0.}
$$

For nonzero lifted vectors from different factors they are orthogonal, so the sectional-curvature denominator is positive. Hence the corresponding mixed plane has [sectional curvature](../../../../../../sectional-curvature.md) zero, providing the mixed-plane value in the $S^2\times S^2$ example.
