<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [connected correlation function](../../../../../../connected-correlation-function.md) $G(x)=\langle M(x)M(0)\rangle-\langle M\rangle^2$ in a selected [pure thermodynamic phase](../../../../../../pure-thermodynamic-phase.md). The exponential [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) is defined by $\xi^{-1}=-\lim_{|x|\to\infty}|x|^{-1}\log|G(x)|$, when this limit exists in the chosen direction. Linearizing the [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md) around a stable uniform minimum $M_0$ gives

$$
\mathcal F^{(2)}=\frac12\int d^Dx\,[K|\nabla\delta M|^2+V''(M_0)(\delta M)^2],\qquad
\widetilde G(q)=\frac{k_BT}{Kq^2+V''(M_0)}.
$$

Hence the [Landau scalar correlation length](../../../../../../landau-scalar-correlation-length.md) is $\boxed{\xi=\sqrt{K/V''(M_0)}}$. Away from criticality the [massive Gaussian field correlation tail](../../../../../../massive-gaussian-field-correlation-tail.md) behaves as $|x|^{-(D-1)/2}e^{-|x|/\xi}$, up to its amplitude. At a continuous transition $V''$ tends to zero, so $\xi$ diverges and fields vary on scales much longer than the lattice spacing. This motivates a [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) expansion, but the simultaneous growth of correlated fluctuations is precisely why the [Ginzburg criterion](../../../../../../ginzburg-criterion.md) must be checked.
