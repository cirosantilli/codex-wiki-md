[Universality classes](../../../../../../universality-class.md) identify systems with the same long-distance [renormalization-group fixed point](../../../../../../renormalization-group-fixed-point.md). Spatial dimension, [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) symmetry and number of components, and the range of interactions help determine the equilibrium class. Microscopic lattice details and many local interactions become [irrelevant operators](../../../../../../irrelevant-operator.md) under coarse-graining. Universal quantities include [critical exponents](../../../../../../critical-exponent.md), scaling functions after fixing metric normalizations, and suitable amplitude ratios. The critical temperature, bare couplings, absolute correlation-length and susceptibility amplitudes, analytic [free-energy density](../../../../../../free-energy-density.md) backgrounds, and microscopic length scales are generally not universal. Dynamic universality can additionally depend on conservation laws, so it should not be inferred solely from a static scalar potential.
