<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

At a [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md), both $r$ and $u$ vanish, while $v>0$ stabilizes the [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md). It separates ordinary continuous transitions for $u>0$ from first-order coexistence for $u<0$. The zero-field order-parameter jump shrinks to zero as $u\uparrow0$, and the [tricritical wings](../../../../../../tricritical-wing.md) terminate there in the full $(r,u,h)$ diagram. For $r<0$, $u=0$, minimizing gives $M^4=-r/v$ and

$$
V(M_0)=-\frac{|r|^{3/2}}{3\sqrt v}.
$$

Two thermal derivatives give the [heat-capacity critical exponent](../../../../../../heat-capacity-critical-exponent.md) $\alpha=1/2$, compared with the finite mean-field heat-capacity jump, $\alpha=0$, at an ordinary quartic transition. The remaining tricritical [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md) are $(\beta,\gamma,\delta)=(1/4,1,5)$, and the mean-field [correlation-length critical exponent](../../../../../../correlation-length-critical-exponent.md) is $\nu=1/2$. To observe tricritical scaling one must tune the quartic scaling field as well as the thermal field: a generic path with fixed $u>0$ instead has ordinary critical behavior. Its [upper critical dimension](../../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md) is three, as the [multicritical Ginzburg ratio](../../../../../../multicritical-ginzburg-ratio.md) shows.
