<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) distinguishes phases by an equilibrium expectation value. For an [Ising model](../../../../../../ising-model.md), the local field $M(x)$ is the coarse-grained [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md), and the uniform value is $M=N^{-1}\langle\sum_r\sigma_r\rangle$. The [spin inversion symmetry](../../../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md) maps $M$ to $-M$, so at zero field the [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) contains even powers. Its disordered minimum is $M=0$; ordered [pure thermodynamic phases](../../../../../../pure-thermodynamic-phase.md) have $M=\pm M_0$. Their sign is selected by taking the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md) before sending $h$ to zero. A symmetric mixture can have zero average without being a single disordered phase. The term $-hM$ identifies $h$ as the [field conjugate to an order parameter](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md).
