<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $\epsilon>0$, the quartic interaction has a nonzero limiting value $\lambda_*=O(\epsilon)$ instead of disappearing under coarse-graining. Long-wavelength [thermal fluctuations](../../../../../../thermal-fluctuation.md) are therefore interacting, so the fluctuation-free [Landau approximation](../../../../../../landau-approximation.md) cannot give the generic critical powers. For example, its ordinary [correlation-length critical exponent](../../../../../../correlation-length-critical-exponent.md) is $1/2$, whereas the relevant thermal [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) just derived gives

$$
\boxed{\nu=\frac1{y_t}=\frac12+\frac\epsilon{12}+O(\epsilon^2).}
$$

The tadpole is momentum independent, so the [anomalous dimension](../../../../../../anomalous-dimension.md) begins at $O(\epsilon^2)$. Combining this with the scaling relations yields $\gamma=1+\epsilon/6+O(\epsilon^2)$, $\beta=1/2-\epsilon/6+O(\epsilon^2)$, and $\alpha=\epsilon/6+O(\epsilon^2)$, exhibiting the deviation from ordinary mean-field indices. These are controlled statements for small positive $\epsilon$, not exact numerical exponents at arbitrary dimension. The [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md) itself is not invalid: integrating its fluctuations is precisely how the [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](../../../../../../wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md) was obtained. The invalid step is replacing that interacting long-distance theory by a uniform saddle and ignoring its fluctuation corrections.
