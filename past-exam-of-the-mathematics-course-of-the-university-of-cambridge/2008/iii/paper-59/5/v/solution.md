<h1 id="5/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

[Primitive causality](../../../../../../primitive-causality.md) is an initial-region completeness requirement. A state's full restriction to a suitable region $O$ determines expectations of [observables](../../../../../../observable.md) in its future [domain of dependence](../../../../../../domain-of-dependence.md). The [domain of dependence](../../../../../../domain-of-dependence.md) is not the whole causal future: it contains points whose past-inextendible causal curves all meet the specified initial data region. Merely being reachable by one causal curve from $O$ is not enough.

A standard local [time-slice axiom](../../../../../../time-slice-axiom.md) makes this precise when $O$ contains a neighbourhood of a [Cauchy slice](../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md) of its causal development:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A(O)=\mathcal A(D(O)),\qquad\mathcal A(D^+(O))\subseteq\mathcal A(O),}
$$

with the regions and algebra identifications understood in the specified local net. Definitions applying [primitive causality](../../../../../../primitive-causality.md) to all bounded open regions can be stronger than a time-slice property stated only for suitable causally convex Cauchy neighbourhoods; these formulations should not be conflated without extra hypotheses.

For a local hyperbolic Klein–Gordon equation, a later field is determined by the field and its normal derivative, or [canonical momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md), on a [Cauchy slice](../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md). Its value can be written using the causal fundamental solution, with integration supported where the past [light cone](../../../../../../light-cone.md) meets the initial slice. If that support lies in the initial patch, the later smeared field is generated by the patch's initial field and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) operators. Their algebra therefore generates the [observables](../../../../../../observable.md) of the patch's [domain of dependence](../../../../../../domain-of-dependence.md). This is the local differential-equation mechanism behind the time-slice property.

The required information is the entire restricted [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md): it assigns expectations to every [observable](../../../../../../observable.md) and product in the initial algebra. Mean values of the initial field and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) alone do not determine their fluctuations or higher correlations and thus are insufficient. Knowledge of the restricted state fixes later [probability distributions](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md) and correlations; it does not fix which individual outcome a future measurement will produce.

Finally, [primitive causality](../../../../../../primitive-causality.md) concerns propagation of information already encoded in initial data, while [microcausality](../../../../../../microcausality.md) controls compatibility of spacelike measurements and [quantum no-signalling](../../../../../../quantum-no-signalling.md) controls possible interventions. The [spectrum condition](../../../../../../spectrum-condition.md) adds a separate positivity restriction. **A causal initial-value evolution, positive energy and [no-signalling](../../../../../../quantum-no-signalling.md) are complementary requirements, not equivalent definitions of quantum locality.**
