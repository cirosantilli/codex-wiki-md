↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Locality in [quantum theory](../../../../../quantum-theory-split.md) is not one interchangeable condition. Spatial concentration of a [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) concerns a state description. [Quantum no-signalling](../../../../../quantum-no-signalling.md) restricts operational changes of remote statistics. [Microcausality](../../../../../microcausality.md) concerns commutation of spacelike [observable](../../../../../observable.md) algebras. The [spectrum condition](../../../../../spectrum-condition.md) constrains energy-momentum and stability, while [primitive causality](../../../../../primitive-causality.md) concerns determination of future-domain expectations by initial-region data. The distinctions are clearest when all five are discussed; none should be replaced silently by the [Bell factorization](../../../../../bell-local-causality.md) examined elsewhere.

**Wave-function tails and entangled correlations are not by themselves controllable signals.** Conversely, absence of signals does not by itself supply a local initial-value dynamics or positive-energy spectrum. Each claim must specify the [observable](../../../../../observable.md) algebras, allowed operations and relevant causal regions.
