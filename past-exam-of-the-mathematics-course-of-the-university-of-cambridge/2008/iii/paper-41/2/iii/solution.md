↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By [balanced factorial orthogonality](../../../../../../balanced-factorial-orthogonality.md), omitting B adds $SS_B$ to the full additive residual: **$\operatorname{RSS}_{A\text{-only}}=2.6867+0.4033=3.0900$**. This model has $3$ parameters and $9$ residual [degrees of freedom](../../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md).
