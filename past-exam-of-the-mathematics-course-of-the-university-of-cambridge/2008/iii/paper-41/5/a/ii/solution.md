↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With [right censoring](../../../../../../../right-censoring.md), an event has not occurred by the last observation time $C$, so the data establish only $T>C$. One records $Y=\min(T,C)$ and $\delta=\mathbf1_{\{T\le C\}}$, distinguishing an observed event from a censored exit. A business still operating at the follow-up deadline is administratively right-censored. **Its observed age is a lower bound on its lifetime, not its eventual closure age.** Standard [survival analysis](../../../../../../../survival-analysis-split.md) requires suitable [independent censoring](../../../../../../../independent-censoring.md), possibly conditional on the fitted [covariates](../../../../../../../covariate.md).
