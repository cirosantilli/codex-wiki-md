For these two simple hypotheses the [Bayes factor](../../../../../../bayes-factor.md) is the ratio of their [normal probability densities](../../../../../../normal-density.md):

$$
B_{10}(y)=\frac{\exp[-(y-2.49)^2/2]}{\exp[-y^2/2]}
=\exp\left(2.49y-\frac{2.49^2}{2}\right).
$$

Therefore $\boxed{B_{10}(y)=e^{2.49(y-1.245)}}$, and the [posterior odds](../../../../../../posterior-odds.md) are $RB_{10}(y)$.
