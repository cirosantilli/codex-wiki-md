↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Conditional on a false relationship, the independent studies are each negative with probability $1-\alpha$. The complement of at least one positive is that all are negative. Hence the [familywise error rate](../../../../../../familywise-error-rate.md) is $\boxed{1-(1-\alpha)^n}$.
