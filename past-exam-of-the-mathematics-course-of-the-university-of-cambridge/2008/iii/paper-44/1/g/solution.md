<h1 id="1/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Let $A_n$ denote the event that at least one study is positive. [Conditional independence](../../../../../../conditional-independence.md) gives $\mathbb P(A_n\mid T)=1-\beta^n$ and $\mathbb P(A_n\mid F)=1-(1-\alpha)^n$. Updating the [prior odds](../../../../../../prior-odds.md) by this event's [Bayes factor](../../../../../../bayes-factor.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\mathbb P(T\mid A_n)}{\mathbb P(F\mid A_n)}
=R\frac{1-\beta^n}{1-(1-\alpha)^n}.}
$$

These are the [odds under at-least-one-positive selection](../../../../../../odds-under-at-least-one-positive-selection.md), not the odds using every study's actual result.
