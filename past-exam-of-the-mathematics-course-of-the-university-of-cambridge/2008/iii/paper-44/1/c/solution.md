<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Under the null, $Y$ has the [standard normal distribution](../../../../../../standard-normal-distribution.md), so the [Type I error](../../../../../../type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) is $\alpha=\mathbb P_0(Y>1.65)=1-\Phi(1.65)\simeq0.05$. Under the alternative, the [Type II error](../../../../../../type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) is $\beta=\mathbb P_1(Y\leq1.65)=\Phi(1.65-2.49)=\Phi(-0.84)\simeq0.20$. Hence $\boxed{\alpha\simeq0.05,\quad\beta\simeq0.20}$, using the supplied rounded normal probabilities.
