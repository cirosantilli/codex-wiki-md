<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let the [prior odds](../../../../../../prior-odds.md) be $\mathbb P(T)/\mathbb P(F)=R$, so $\mathbb P(T)=R/(1+R)$. The [Type I error](../../../../../../type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) gives $\mathbb P(P\mid F)=\alpha$, while the [statistical power](../../../../../../statistical-power.md) gives $\mathbb P(P\mid T)=1-\beta$. By [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(F\mid P)=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+R(1-\beta)}.}
$$

This is a posterior probability of the null, not the false-positive probability $\alpha$ conditional on the null. The distinction is the [base-rate effect in a positive study](../../../../../../base-rate-effect-in-a-positive-study.md).
