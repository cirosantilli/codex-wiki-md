↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The transformation $t=(x-\overline x)/s_x$ centers day at zero and measures it in standard-deviation units. It reduces numerical scale differences and makes the intercept-like parameter refer to the middle of the observed time range. In [nonlinear regression](../../../../../../nonlinear-regression.md) this can reduce posterior parameter dependence and improve [Markov chain Monte Carlo](../../../../../../markov-chain-monte-carlo.md) mixing. It also makes priors on the growth coefficient easier to interpret; it does not change the curve family.
