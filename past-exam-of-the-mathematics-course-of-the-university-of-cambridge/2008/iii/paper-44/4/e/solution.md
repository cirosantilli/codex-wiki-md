<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A common second parameter means the trees reach the same fraction of their asymptotic sizes at the centered day; a common third parameter means they have the same standardized growth rate. **The trees may differ mainly in final size while sharing a growth shape.** Similar environment and the overlapping posterior estimates make this biologically plausible. Equality is a stronger modelling assumption than [partial pooling](../../../../../../partial-pooling.md), and should be checked predictively.
