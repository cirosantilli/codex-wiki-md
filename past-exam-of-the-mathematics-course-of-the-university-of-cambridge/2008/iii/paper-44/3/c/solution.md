By [normal-normal conjugacy](../../../../../../normal-normal-conjugacy-with-known-observation-variance.md), the [posterior distribution](../../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) is

$$
\boxed{\theta_i\mid y_{i1}\sim N(m_{i1},v_{i1}),\qquad
v_{i1}=\left(\tau^{-2}+\sigma_i^{-2}\right)^{-1},\qquad
m_{i1}=v_{i1}\left(\frac\mu{\tau^2}+\frac{y_{i1}}{\sigma_i^2}\right).}
$$

The [posterior mean](../../../../../../posterior-mean.md) is a precision-weighted average of the population mean and the observed personal measurement.
