<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the new measurement as $Y_{i1}=\theta_i+\varepsilon_{i1}$, with independent $\theta_i\sim N(\mu,\tau^2)$ and $\varepsilon_{i1}\sim N(0,\sigma_i^2)$. Adding independent [normal distributions](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) gives the [prior predictive distribution](../../../../../../bayesian-model-evidence.md)

$$
\boxed{Y_{i1}\sim N(\mu,\tau^2+\sigma_i^2).}
$$
