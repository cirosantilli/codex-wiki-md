<h1 id="3/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

After $k$ measurements the [normal-normal conjugacy](../../../../../../normal-normal-conjugacy-with-known-observation-variance.md) formulas are

$$
v_{ik}=(\tau^{-2}+k\sigma_i^{-2})^{-1},\qquad
m_{ik}=v_{ik}\left(\mu\tau^{-2}+\sigma_i^{-2}\sum_{j=1}^k y_{ij}\right).
$$

The upper monitoring limit is $m_{ik}+z_{0.999}\sqrt{v_{ik}+\sigma_i^2}$. Its center moves toward the athlete's own mean and the population's influence diminishes. Its predictive width decreases toward $z_{0.999}\sigma_i$, since uncertainty about the mean vanishes but the next measurement's error remains. The absolute upper limit need not decrease monotonically: its center can move upward or downward with the observations. This is [sequential normal predictive monitoring](../../../../../../sequential-normal-predictive-monitoring.md).
