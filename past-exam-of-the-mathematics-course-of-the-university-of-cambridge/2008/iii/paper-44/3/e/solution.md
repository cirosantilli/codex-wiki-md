<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Taking the upper $0.999$ [quantile](../../../../../../quantile-function.md) of the [posterior predictive distribution](../../../../../../posterior-predictive-distribution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{u_{i2}=m_{i1}+\Phi^{-1}(0.999)\sqrt{v_{i1}+\sigma_i^2}.}
$$

The same predictive false-alarm probability is retained after updating with the first measurement.
