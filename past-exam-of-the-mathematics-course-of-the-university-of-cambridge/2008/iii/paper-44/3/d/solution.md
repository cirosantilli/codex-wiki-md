<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Integrate the next independent measurement error over the [posterior distribution](../../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) of $\theta_i$. The [posterior predictive distribution](../../../../../../posterior-predictive-distribution.md) is

$$
\boxed{Y_{i2}\mid y_{i1}\sim N(m_{i1},v_{i1}+\sigma_i^2).}
$$

Both uncertainty about the personal mean and variation in a new measurement contribute.
