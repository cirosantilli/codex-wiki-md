↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $z_{0.999}=\Phi^{-1}(0.999)$ be the [standard normal distribution](../../../../../../standard-normal-distribution.md) quantile. Standardizing the prediction in part (a) gives

$$
\boxed{u_{i1}=\mu+z_{0.999}\sqrt{\tau^2+\sigma_i^2}.}
$$

This is a one-sided predictive limit; its upper-tail probability is $0.001$ under the specified model.
