The [Student t-distribution](../../../../../../student-s-t-distribution.md) has heavier tails than the [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md), so unusual personal means need not force the entire population's spread upward or be shrunk as strongly toward its center. The latent $\lambda_i$ allows a large local prior variance for an outlying individual. This [normal scale mixture representation of Student t](../../../../../../normal-scale-mixture-representation-of-student-t.md) is a useful robust version of the [hierarchical Bayesian model](../../../../../../hierarchical-bayesian-model.md). It still assumes a symmetric, unimodal population; a genuinely multimodal population may need a different model.
