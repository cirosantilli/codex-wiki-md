The [Bayesian network](../../../../../../bayesian-network.md) has a shared parent $\psi$ and, for each hospital, the chain $\psi\to\theta_i\to y_i$. A plate encloses the hospital-specific pair. Observed counts are shaded; the local rates are [latent variables](../../../../../../latent-variable.md). Conditional on $\psi$, the pairs are independent.
