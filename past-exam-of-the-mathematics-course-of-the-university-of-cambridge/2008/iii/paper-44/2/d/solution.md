<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Put $s=\sum_i y_i$. The independent-count [likelihood function](../../../../../../likelihood-function.md) is proportional to $(1-\phi)^n\phi^s$. Its [conjugate prior](../../../../../../conjugate-prior.md) is $\boxed{\phi\sim\operatorname{Beta}(a,b),\quad a,b>0}$, giving the [posterior distribution](../../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) $\operatorname{Beta}(a+s,b+n)$.
