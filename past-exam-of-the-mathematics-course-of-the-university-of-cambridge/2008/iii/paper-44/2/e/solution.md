<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A flat density in $\log\psi$ induces $p(\psi)\propto1/\psi$. Since $\psi=(1-\phi)/\phi$, the absolute [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is $|d\psi/d\phi|=\phi^{-2}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p(\phi)\propto\frac{1}{\phi(1-\phi)},\qquad0<\phi<1.}
$$

This is the [Haldane prior](../../../../../../haldane-prior.md), an [improper prior](../../../../../../improper-prior.md) with formal beta parameters $(0,0)$.
