<h1 id="5/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use the same nonsemisimple matrix from part (i) and the one-dimensional [Lie subalgebra](../../../../../../lie-subalgebra.md)

$$
\mathfrak g=\mathbb C X\subseteq\mathfrak{gl}_2,\qquad X=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

It is a [Lie subalgebra](../../../../../../lie-subalgebra.md) because $[aX,bX]=0$ for all scalars $a,b$. Its [Jordan–Chevalley decomposition](../../../../../../jordan-chevalley-decomposition.md) has $X_s=I_2$ and $X_n=X-I_2\ne0$. If $I_2=cX$, comparing off-diagonal entries forces $c=0$, which contradicts the diagonal entries. If $X_n=cX$, taking traces gives $0=2c$, again forcing $c=0$ although $X_n\ne0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{X_s\notin\mathfrak g,\qquad X_n\notin\mathfrak g.}
$$

The semisimplicity assumption on the [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) in part (ii) is therefore essential.
