<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply the [lowering operator](../../../../../../../lowering-operator.md) $F$ to the [highest-weight vector](../../../../../../../highest-weight-vector.md) from part (ii). Since $Fw_{03}=4w_{13}+w_{04}$ and $Fw_{12}=2w_{13}$, we obtain

$$
\boxed{Fv_+=w_{04}-2w_{13}\in W_0.}
$$

This is nonzero because $w_{04},w_{13}$ are distinct elements of the [exterior power](../../../../../../../exterior-power.md) basis. Both have weight zero. As a consistency check, the [raising operator](../../../../../../../raising-operator.md) sends this vector to $2v_+$, as required by the [sl2 highest-weight lowering formula](../../../../../../../sl2-highest-weight-lowering-formula.md).
