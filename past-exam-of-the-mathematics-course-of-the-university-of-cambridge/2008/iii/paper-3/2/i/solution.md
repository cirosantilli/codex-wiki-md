<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Work with a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) and its [root system](../../../../../../root-system.md) $R$ in the real span $E$ of the [roots of a root system](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md). The [Killing form](../../../../../../killing-form.md) induces a positive definite [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) on this real root space, after the usual identification with the dual of the real span of the [coroots](../../../../../../coroot.md). For each root $\alpha$, its [Weyl reflection](../../../../../../weyl-reflection.md) is

$$
s_\alpha(\lambda)=\lambda-\langle\lambda,\alpha^\vee\rangle\alpha,\qquad \alpha^\vee=\frac{2\alpha}{(\alpha,\alpha)}.
$$

The [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) is $\boxed{W=\langle s_\alpha:\alpha\in R\rangle}$. The structural facts being used are that these [reflections](../../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) preserve the finite [root system](../../../../../../root-system.md), that the roots span $E$, and that the reflections in the [simple roots](../../../../../../simple-root.md) already generate $W$. Thus $W$ is finite: it acts faithfully as permutations of $R$. Equivalently, it is the normalizer of the [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) modulo its centralizer in the associated adjoint group.
