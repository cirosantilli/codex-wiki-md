<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the [defining representation](../../../../../../defining-representation-of-a-matrix-lie-algebra.md), the standard coordinate vectors $e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4,e_5$ are [weight vectors](../../../../../../weight-vector.md) of weights $\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,-\varepsilon_1,-\varepsilon_2,0$, respectively. Thus the [weight diagram](../../../../../../weight-diagram.md) has four axial points and the origin, **each of multiplicity one**.

For the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), the eight nonzero [weights of a representation](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) are the roots from part (ii), each of multiplicity one, with the displayed $X_\alpha$ as basis. The zero [weight space](../../../../../../weight-space.md) is the [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md), with basis

$$
H_1=\operatorname{diag}(1,0,-1,0,0),\qquad H_2=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,0,-1,0).
$$

The [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) diagram therefore has the four axial points, four diagonal points, and **the origin of multiplicity two**. The multiplicities sum to ten, as they must.
