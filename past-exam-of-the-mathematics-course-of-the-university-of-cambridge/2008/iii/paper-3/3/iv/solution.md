<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md), the [Killing form](../../../../../../killing-form.md) is the symmetric [bilinear form](../../../../../../bilinear-form.md)

$$
\boxed{B(X,Y)=\operatorname{tr}_{\mathfrak g}(\operatorname{ad}X\,\operatorname{ad}Y),\qquad \operatorname{ad}X(Z)=[X,Z].}
$$

In the [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md) of this [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), $\operatorname{ad}H$ acts as zero on the abelian [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) and as multiplication by $\alpha(H)$ on $\mathfrak g_\alpha$. For $H,K\in\mathfrak h$, their product thus acts as multiplication by $\alpha(H)\alpha(K)$ on each [root space](../../../../../../root-space.md). Every root space here is one-dimensional. Taking the trace over the two-dimensional Cartan subalgebra and the eight root spaces proves

$$
\boxed{B(H,K)=\sum_{\alpha\in R}\alpha(H)\alpha(K).}
$$

For a general weight decomposition the trace would include the corresponding [weight multiplicities](../../../../../../weight-multiplicity.md); the root multiplicities are one in the semisimple setting here.
