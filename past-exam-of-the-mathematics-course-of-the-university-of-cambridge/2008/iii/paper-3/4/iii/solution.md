<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the finite-dimensional complex [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) setting implicit in this root-system construction, the given [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) and [root spaces](../../../../../../root-space.md) determine a reduced crystallographic [root system](../../../../../../root-system.md) $R$. The ordering divides $R$ into [positive roots](../../../../../../positive-root.md) and their negatives. The [simple roots](../../../../../../simple-root.md) are the positive roots that cannot be written as a sum of two positive roots; a standard root-system fact is that they form a basis and that every positive root is a nonnegative integral combination of them.

Index the [simple roots](../../../../../../simple-root.md) as $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_\ell$ and form the [Cartan matrix](../../../../../../cartan-matrix.md)

$$
A_{ij}=\langle\alpha_j,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle=\frac{2(\alpha_i,\alpha_j)}{(\alpha_i,\alpha_i)}.
$$

For $i\ne j$, these entries are nonpositive [integers](../../../../../../integer.md) and $A_{ij}A_{ji}\in\{0,1,2,3\}$. Construct one vertex per simple root and join distinct vertices by $A_{ij}A_{ji}$ lines. If root lengths differ, direct the arrow toward the shorter [simple root](../../../../../../simple-root.md). **This labelled and oriented graph is the [Dynkin diagram](../../../../../../dynkin-diagram.md).** Its unoriented [Coxeter graph](../../../../../../coxeter-graph.md) is admissible: normalizing the simple roots to unit length gives $4(v_i,v_j)^2=A_{ij}A_{ji}$. A general Lie algebra without the semisimple root-system hypotheses need not produce such a diagram.
