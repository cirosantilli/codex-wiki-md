<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the crystallographic convention for this course, an [admissible Coxeter graph](../../../../../../admissible-crystallographic-coxeter-graph.md) comes from linearly independent unit [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) $v_1,\ldots,v_r$ in a [Euclidean space](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md), such that for $i\ne j$,

$$
(v_i,v_j)\leq0,\qquad n_{ij}=4(v_i,v_j)^2\in\{0,1,2,3\}.
$$

The vertices $i,j$ have no connection when $n_{ij}=0$, and a single, double or triple connection when $n_{ij}=1,2,3$. Equivalently, the symmetric [Gram matrix](../../../../../../gram-matrix.md)

$$
\boxed{G_{ii}=1,\qquad G_{ij}=-\frac{\sqrt{n_{ij}}}{2}\ (i\ne j)}
$$

must be [positive-definite](../../../../../../positive-definite-bilinear-form.md). This equivalence follows because a positive definite [Gram matrix](../../../../../../gram-matrix.md) can be realized by linearly independent [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md), while such vectors necessarily have a positive definite Gram matrix. In the edge-labelled [Coxeter graph](../../../../../../coxeter-graph.md) convention, these connections have labels $3,4,6$, since the corresponding inner products are $-\cos(\pi/3)$, $-\cos(\pi/4)$ and $-\cos(\pi/6)$.
