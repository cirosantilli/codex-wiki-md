<h1 id="4/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

If the three [simple roots](../../../../../../simple-root.md) are distinct and every pair of root spaces had nonzero [Lie bracket](../../../../../../lie-bracket.md), part (iv) would put all three connections in the [Dynkin diagram](../../../../../../dynkin-diagram.md). Their underlying [Coxeter graph](../../../../../../coxeter-graph.md) would contain a triangle, contradicting part (ii). Hence **at least one pair satisfies $\boxed{[\mathfrak g_{\alpha_i},\mathfrak g_{\alpha_j}]=0}$.** If two of the listed roots coincide, their common [root space](../../../../../../root-space.md) is one-dimensional, so its bracket with itself is already zero. Thus the conclusion also covers that case.
