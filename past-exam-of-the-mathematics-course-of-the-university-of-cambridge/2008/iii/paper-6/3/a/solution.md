<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fix a [Borel subgroup](../../../../../../borel-subgroup.md) $B$ and a [maximal algebraic torus](../../../../../../maximal-algebraic-torus.md) $T\subset B$. Conjugacy of [Borel subgroups](../../../../../../borel-subgroup.md) and the equality $N_G(B)=B$ identify the [flag variety](../../../../../../generalized-flag-variety.md) with $G/B$, by $gB\mapsto{}^gB$.

Every simultaneous conjugacy orbit on pairs contains a pair $(B,{}^gB)$: first conjugate its first member to $B$. The remaining allowed conjugations are by $B$. Two such pairs are in the same orbit exactly when $g'$ belongs to $BgB$. Indeed ${}^{g'}B={} ^{bg}B$ is equivalent to $(bg)^{-1}g'\in N_G(B)=B$. Thus there is an explicit bijection

$$
\boxed{G\backslash(\mathcal B\times\mathcal B)\ \longleftrightarrow\ B\backslash G/B.}
$$

The [Bruhat decomposition](../../../../../../bruhat-decomposition.md) is the disjoint partition $G=\coprod_{w\in W}B\dot wB$, with $W=N_G(T)/T$. Under the displayed bijection its double coset $B\dot wB$ becomes the orbit

$$
O(w)=G\cdot(B,{}^{\dot w}B).
$$

This proves that the orbits are indexed by the [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md). Conversely, the assertion that these particular $O(w)$ exhaust the orbits without repetitions says precisely that the $B\dot wB$ exhaust $G$ without repetitions. Hence the orbit description and the [Bruhat decomposition](../../../../../../bruhat-decomposition.md) are equivalent, not merely two sets of the same cardinality. Their common index is the [relative position of Borel subgroups](../../../../../../relative-position-of-borel-subgroups.md).
