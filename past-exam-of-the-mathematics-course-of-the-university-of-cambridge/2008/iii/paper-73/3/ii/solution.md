<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the printed convention $\rho$ is energy density, since the gravitational source is $8\pi G\rho/(3c^2)$. For nonrelativistic [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md), $\rho_m=\rho_{m,0}(1+z)^3$. Define the [cosmological density parameters](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md) by

$$
\Omega_{m,0}=\frac{8\pi G\rho_{m,0}}{3c^2H_0^2},
\qquad \Omega_{k,0}=-\frac{kc^2}{a_0^2H_0^2},
\qquad \Omega_{\Lambda,0}=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3H_0^2}.
$$

The [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) then gives

$$
H(z)=H_0E(z),\qquad E(z)=\sqrt{\Omega_{m,0}(1+z)^3+
\Omega_{k,0}(1+z)^2+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}.
$$

Let $n_c$ be the constant [comoving number density](../../../../../../comoving-number-density.md) of absorbers, and let $\sigma_p$ be their fixed proper interception area. Their proper [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) is $n_p(z)=n_c(1+z)^3$, with $a_0=1$. The [redshift-time relation](../../../../../../redshift-time-relation.md) gives a local proper photon-path increment

$$
d\ell=c|dt|=\frac{c\,dz}{(1+z)H(z)}.
$$

For an infinitesimal path, the expected number of interceptions, and to first order the probability of an interception, is $d\mathcal N=n_p\sigma_p\,d\ell$. Thus [absorber incidence and comoving number density](../../../../../../absorber-incidence-and-comoving-number-density.md) imply

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\mathcal N}{dz}
=\frac{n_c\sigma_pc}{H_0}\frac{(1+z)^2}{E(z)}.}
$$

Two factors survive: proper density brings three powers of $1+z$, whereas proper path length brings the inverse of one power. A fixed comoving area instead of the assumed fixed proper area would change the result. The formula is an incidence rate, not a probability bounded by one over a finite redshift interval; multiple interceptions can occur.
