<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take $v>0$ for recession. By the definition of [kinematic redshift](../../../../../../kinematic-redshift.md), $1+z_{\rm kin}=\nu_e/\nu_0$. Inverting the supplied [relativistic Doppler effect](../../../../../../relativistic-doppler-effect.md) and expanding with $\beta=v/c$ gives

$$
1+z_{\rm kin}=\sqrt{\frac{1+\beta}{1-\beta}}
=(1+\tfrac12\beta+O(\beta^2))(1+\tfrac12\beta+O(\beta^2))
=1+\beta+O(\beta^2).
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{z_{\rm kin}\simeq v/c\quad\text{for }|v|\ll c.}
$$

A negative radial [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) gives a blueshift at this order.
