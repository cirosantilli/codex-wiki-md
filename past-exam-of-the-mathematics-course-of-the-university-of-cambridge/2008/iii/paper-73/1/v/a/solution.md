<h1 id="1/v/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\nu_c$ be the photon [frequency](../../../../../../../frequency.md) in the local comoving frame just after emission. The [kinematic redshift](../../../../../../../kinematic-redshift.md) gives $\nu_e/\nu_c=1+z_{\rm kin}$, and propagation in the [FRW metric](../../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) gives $\nu_c/\nu_0=1+z_{\rm cosm}$. Multiplication gives

$$
\boxed{1+z_{\rm tot}=(1+z_{\rm cosm})(1+z_{\rm kin}).}
$$

Equivalently, $z_{\rm tot}=z_{\rm cosm}+z_{\rm kin}+z_{\rm cosm}z_{\rm kin}$. Both redshifts being small is what permits their approximate addition. Here the observer's own [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) is assumed already corrected to the [Hubble flow](../../../../../../../hubble-flow.md) frame.
