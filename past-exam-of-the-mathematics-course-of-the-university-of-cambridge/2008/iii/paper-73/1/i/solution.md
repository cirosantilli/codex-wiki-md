<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [kinematic redshift](../../../../../../kinematic-redshift.md) is the [relativistic Doppler effect](../../../../../../relativistic-doppler-effect.md) arising from relative motion of emitter and receiver. In a local inertial frame, recession decreases the received [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) and approach increases it. A [cosmological redshift](../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) instead compares photons emitted and received by comoving observers at different [cosmic times](../../../../../../cosmic-time.md): expansion of the [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) stretches their [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md). It exists even when both observers have zero [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md). Both are frequency ratios measured by observers, but a large [cosmological redshift](../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) should not be inserted into a flat-spacetime radial-speed formula to define the galaxy's unique recession speed.
