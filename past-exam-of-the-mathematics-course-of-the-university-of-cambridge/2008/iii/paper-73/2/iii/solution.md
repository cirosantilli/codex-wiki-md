<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At $z=0$, spatial flatness gives $\tan\theta_0=\sqrt{(1-\Omega_{\Lambda,0})/\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}$, hence $\cos\theta_0=\sqrt{\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}$ and $\sin\theta_0=\sqrt{1-\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{t_0=\frac{2}{3H_0\sqrt{\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}}
\log\left[\frac{1+\sqrt{\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}}
{\sqrt{1-\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}}\right].}
$$

For $\Omega_{\Lambda,0}\to0$, $\operatorname{arsinh}u\sim u$ in the previous integral gives $t_0\to2/(3H_0)$, the [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) result. For $\Omega_{m,0}=0$, the positive-vacuum flat slicing has no finite-age [Big Bang](../../../../../../big-bang.md), explaining the other endpoint divergence.
