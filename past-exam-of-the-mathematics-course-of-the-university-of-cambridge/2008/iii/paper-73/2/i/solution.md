<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Divide the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) by $a^2$ and use $a=(1+z)^{-1}$. The [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md) therefore satisfies

$$
\boxed{\frac{H(z)}{H_0}
=\sqrt{\Omega_{m,0}(1+z)^3+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}.}
$$

The positive root describes the expanding branch. Spatial flatness and the neglect of [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md) imply $\Omega_{m,0}+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}=1$.
