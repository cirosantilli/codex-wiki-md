<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every real $r\ge0$,

$$
\log(1+r)=\int_0^r\frac{dt}{1+t}\le r.
$$

Taking $r=|w|$ proves the required [logarithm inequality](../../../../../../logarithm-inequality.md), even without the lower bound $|w|\ge1/2$. Hence **$C_2=1$ works**.
