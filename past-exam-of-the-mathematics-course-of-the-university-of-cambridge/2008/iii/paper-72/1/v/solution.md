<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The qualitative prediction of [galactic chemical evolution](../../../../../../galactic-chemical-evolution.md) is useful: later [stellar populations](../../../../../../stellar-population.md) tend to inherit more enriched gas, and old [stellar populations](../../../../../../stellar-population.md) can preserve low [stellar metallicity](../../../../../../stellar-metallicity.md). The [closed-box metallicity distribution](../../../../../../closed-box-metallicity-distribution.md), however, predicts many more metal-poor long-lived dwarfs in the solar neighbourhood than are observed. This is the [G-dwarf problem](../../../../../../g-dwarf-problem.md), also apparent with other long-lived tracers. A single well-mixed box cannot describe the different age and abundance distributions of the [galactic disk](../../../../../../galactic-disk.md), [galactic bulge](../../../../../../galactic-bulge.md), and [stellar halo](../../../../../../stellar-halo.md) of the [Milky Way](../../../../../../milky-way.md). Nor need its exponentially declining [star formation rate](../../../../../../star-formation-rate.md) match the history of ongoing disk [star formation](../../../../../../star-formation.md).

Prolonged relatively metal-poor [galactic gas inflow](../../../../../../galactic-gas-inflow.md) supplies fuel while diluting the enrichment, allowing many [stars](../../../../../../star.md) to form after the earliest enrichment stage; pre-enrichment of the initial reservoir also removes a pristine tail. [Galactic outflows](../../../../../../galactic-outflow.md) lower the [effective yield](../../../../../../effective-yield.md), but a constant well-mixed outflow alone still gives an exponential [metallicity distribution function](../../../../../../metallicity-distribution-function.md), and is not automatically a cure for the [G-dwarf problem](../../../../../../g-dwarf-problem.md). Spatially varying [star formation](../../../../../../star-formation.md), radial transport and migration, and selection of local survivors complicate a comparison with one global box. A varying [initial mass function](../../../../../../initial-mass-function.md) or [stellar yield](../../../../../../stellar-yield.md) changes the enrichment law. Finally, finite stellar lifetimes and [Type Ia supernovae](../../../../../../type-ia-supernova.md) make iron enrichment delayed, so an observed iron-abundance distribution is not interchangeable with the instantaneous total-metal distribution. **Gas exchange, spatially distinct populations, and delayed enrichment are essential qualifications to the closed-box prediction.**
