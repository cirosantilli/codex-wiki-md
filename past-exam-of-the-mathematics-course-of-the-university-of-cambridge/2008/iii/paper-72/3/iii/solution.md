<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Integrate the dwarf [singular isothermal sphere](../../../../../../singular-isothermal-sphere.md) only to its truncation radius:

$$
m_d=\frac{2\sigma_d^2r_t}{G}=\frac{2\sigma_d^2}{G}\frac{D\sigma_d}{\sqrt2\sigma_h}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{m_d=\frac{\sqrt2D\sigma_d^3}{G\sigma_h}.}
$$

The dimensions are $(\text{length})(\text{speed})^2/G$, as required for mass. At fixed host dispersion and separation the mass scales as $\sigma_d^3$, rather than the luminosity's fourth-power scaling used later. Under the rough equal-density [tidal radius](../../../../../../tidal-radius.md) convention, the corresponding mass is $2D\sigma_d^3/(G\sigma_h)$, larger by $\sqrt2$.
