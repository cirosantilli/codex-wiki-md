<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Use the classical [Faber-Jackson relation](../../../../../../faber-jackson-relation.md) $L\propto\sigma^4$ as the explicitly requested extrapolation. The supplied host fixes its normalization:

$$
L_d=10^{11}\left(\frac{10}{300}\right)^4L_\odot=1.23\times10^5L_\odot.
$$

With the mass derived using circular-orbit [Jacobi truncation of two singular isothermal spheres](../../../../../../jacobi-truncation-of-two-singular-isothermal-spheres.md), the dynamical [mass-to-light ratio](../../../../../../mass-to-light-ratio.md) is

$$
\Upsilon_d=\frac{5.05\times10^7}{1.23\times10^5}\frac{M_\odot}{L_\odot}=4.09\times10^2\frac{M_\odot}{L_\odot}.
$$

Hence to one significant figure

$$
\boxed{L_d\simeq10^5L_\odot,\qquad \Upsilon_d\simeq4\times10^2M_\odot/L_\odot.}
$$

The rough equal-density truncation convention gives $\Upsilon_d\simeq6\times10^2M_\odot/L_\odot$ instead. Both conclusions indicate a very large dynamical [mass-to-light ratio](../../../../../../mass-to-light-ratio.md) compared with a normal old stellar population.

Within their bright stellar bodies, typical giant [elliptical galaxies](../../../../../../elliptical-galaxy.md) have optical dynamical [mass-to-light ratios](../../../../../../mass-to-light-ratio.md) of order a few to ten in solar units, with variations in band, [initial mass function](../../../../../../initial-mass-function.md), and [dark matter](../../../../../../dark-matter.md). Large outer apertures can give substantially larger ratios. Many [dwarf spheroidal galaxies](../../../../../../dwarf-spheroidal-galaxy.md) have much larger inferred ratios, from tens to hundreds and, for some very faint systems, still larger values. Thus the computed ratio is plausible as the scale of a very dark-matter-dominated faint dwarf, not as a purely stellar ratio. Dynamical inference depends on member identification, binary motions, equilibrium, and the assumed mass profile.

The stipulated [Faber-Jackson relation](../../../../../../faber-jackson-relation.md) is not a reliable universal extrapolation down to every [dwarf spheroidal galaxy](../../../../../../dwarf-spheroidal-galaxy.md). Also, the [fundamental plane of elliptical galaxies](../../../../../../fundamental-plane-elliptical-galaxies.md) contains radius and [surface brightness](../../../../../../surface-brightness.md) as well as [velocity dispersion](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion.md); the two-variable [Faber-Jackson relation](../../../../../../faber-jackson-relation.md) is a projection of that information, not an interchangeable definition of the full plane.
