<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $a+b=0$, then $p_1=(a+b)p_0=0$. Applying the count recurrence successively gives $p_n=0$ for every $n\ge1$. Normalization forces

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(N=0)=1.}
$$

Thus the [claim count distribution](../../../../../../claim-count-distribution.md) is a point mass at zero, and the [aggregate claims model](../../../../../../aggregate-claims-model.md) also has $S=0$ almost surely. In particular the recursion starts at $g_0=1$ and gives no positive-loss probabilities.
