<h1 id="5/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $k>0$, the surface presentation

$$
\pi_1(\Sigma_2)=\langle a_1,b_1,a_2,b_2\mid[a_1,b_1][a_2,b_2]=1\rangle
$$

has an epimorphism to $\mathbb Z/k$ sending $a_1$ to $1$ and the other generators to zero. Its kernel has index $k$. By the [classification of connected covering spaces](../../../../../../classification-of-connected-covering-spaces.md), it determines a connected $k$-sheeted cover $p:\widetilde\Sigma\to\Sigma_2$. Pull back the orientation, so each local sheet has degree $+1$ and $\deg p=k$. Counting lifts of cells gives $\chi(\widetilde\Sigma)=k\chi(\Sigma_2)=-2k$, hence $2-2g=-2k$ and $g=k+1$.

For $k<0$, use the $|k|$-sheeted cover and compose with an [orientation-reversing diffeomorphism](../../../../../../orientation-reversing-diffeomorphism.md) of $\Sigma_2$, for instance reflection in a symmetric embedded genus-two surface. By [multiplicativity of mapping degree](../../../../../../multiplicativity-of-mapping-degree.md), the degree becomes $-|k|=k$. For $k=0$ take a constant map from the torus. Thus the [arbitrary-degree maps to a surface of genus two](../../../../../../arbitrary-degree-maps-to-a-surface-of-genus-two.md) can be chosen with

$$
\boxed{g(k)=|k|+1,\qquad\Sigma_{|k|+1}\longrightarrow\Sigma_2\text{ of degree }k\quad(k\in\mathbb Z).}
$$
