<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose an oriented embedded closed $d$-disc $D$ in $M$ and collapse $M\setminus\operatorname{int}D$ to a point. The quotient is $D/\partial D\cong S^d$, with orientation chosen to agree on the disc interior. The resulting [collapse map of degree one onto a sphere](../../../../../../collapse-map-of-degree-one-onto-a-sphere.md) is

$$
\boxed{q:M\longrightarrow M/(M\setminus\operatorname{int}D)\cong S^d,\qquad\deg q=1.}
$$

Indeed, any target point other than the collapsed point has exactly one preimage, where the map is an orientation-preserving homeomorphism and has local degree $+1$. The [degree as a sum of local degrees](../../../../../../degree-as-a-sum-of-local-degrees.md) therefore gives global degree one. This is the usual connected, positive-dimensional setting for mapping degree; extra components, if allowed, may all be collapsed.
