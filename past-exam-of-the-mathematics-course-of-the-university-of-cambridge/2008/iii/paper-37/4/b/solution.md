<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Translate the horizontal coordinate by $x$. Then $X_t+x$ is a standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) starting at zero, and continuity identifies $\tau$ with its [first-passage time](../../../../../../first-passage-time.md) to $x$. The vertical coordinate remains an independent standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) starting at zero. Part (a) therefore gives the [Cauchy exit law from a Brownian half-plane](../../../../../../cauchy-exit-law-from-a-brownian-half-plane.md):

$$
\boxed{Y_\tau\overset d=xC,\qquad f_{Y_\tau}(y)=\frac{x}{\pi(x^2+y^2)}.}
$$

The TeX transcription's initial value $-z$ and later $Y_T$ are both corrected here to the PDF's $-x$ and $Y_\tau$.
