<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $z_f(t)$ be the [filling-box first front](../../../../../../filling-box-first-front.md), with $z_f(0)=H$. Below it the original fluid has not yet been through the plume. In the [filling box model](../../../../../../filling-box-model.md), the [turbulent plume](../../../../../../turbulent-plume-split.md) adjusts rapidly and occupies a negligible fraction of the room's area. A point source with negligible source volume flux supplies $Q(z)=CF_s^{1/3}z^{5/3}$. All its volume crossing the front grows the processed region, so [volume conservation](../../../../../../volume-conservation.md) gives

$$
A_c\dot z_f=-\pi CF_s^{1/3}z_f^{5/3}.
$$

Put $a_p=\pi CF_s^{1/3}/A_c$. Integrating $d(z_f^{-2/3})/dt=2a_p/3$ yields

$$
\boxed{z_f(t)=\left(H^{-2/3}+\frac23a_pt\right)^{-3/2}
=H\left[1+\frac{2\pi CF_s^{1/3}H^{2/3}}{3A_c}t\right]^{-3/2}.}
$$

The first front approaches the floor only as $t\to\infty$. A nonzero source volume flux would require an equal exhaust to keep the room volume fixed; it is treated in the following parts.
