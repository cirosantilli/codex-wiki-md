<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $P$ denote total [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../../gravitational-energy.md), $P_b$ the [background potential energy](../../../../../../background-potential-energy.md) of the adiabatically sorted density field, and $A=P-P_b$ the [available potential energy](../../../../../../available-potential-energy.md). Define $B$ positive for reversible transfer from [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) to $P$, $\epsilon$ as viscous kinetic-energy loss, $F$ as imposed mechanical work, $W$ as molecular transfer from [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy.md) to potential energy, and $M$ as the irreversible conversion of $A$ to $P_b$. With boundary contributions removed, the [stratified mixing energy budget](../../../../../../stratified-mixing-energy-budget.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dot K=F-B-\epsilon,\qquad
\dot P=B+W,\qquad
\dot P_b=M+W,\qquad
\dot A=B-M.}
$$

For an unforced flow set $F=0$. In an alternative notation, if $\Phi_d=\dot P_b$ is the full diffusive increase of background energy and $\Phi_i=W$, then $M=\Phi_d-\Phi_i$ and $\dot A=B+\Phi_i-\Phi_d$. This distinguishes irreversible density mixing from reversible buoyancy exchange. The internal reservoir obeys $\dot I=\epsilon-W$, so summing the kinetic, potential and internal budgets gives $d(K+P+I)/dt=F$ as required by [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md).
