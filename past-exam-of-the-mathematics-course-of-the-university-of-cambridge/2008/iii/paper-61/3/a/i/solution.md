<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a normalized state $|\psi(t_F;f)\rangle$ driven by the admissible control $f$, maximize

$$
\boxed{J_A[f]=\langle\psi(t_F;f)|A|\psi(t_F;f)\rangle.}
$$

Here $A$ is the specified [Hermitian operator](../../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), $t_F$ the final time, and the dependence on $f$ is through the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md). The objective is real and is exactly the desired [expectation value](../../../../../../../expectation-value.md), so increasing it increases the requested observable. A control-resource penalty may be included when the admissible set does not already constrain field strength or fluence.
