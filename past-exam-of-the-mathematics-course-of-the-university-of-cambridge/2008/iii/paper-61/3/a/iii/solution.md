<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For actual final [density operator](../../../../../../../density-matrix.md) $\rho_f(t_F)$ and target $\rho_d$, minimize

$$
\boxed{J_
ho[f]=\frac12\|\rho_f(t_F)-\rho_d\|_{\rm HS}^2.}
$$

This [Hilbert-Schmidt distance](../../../../../../../hilbert-schmidt-distance.md) is nonnegative and is zero exactly at the target, for pure or mixed states. The subscript $f$ denotes evolution under the chosen control; $t_F$ is fixed. A simple overlap $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_d\rho_f)$ need not have its maximum at a general mixed target, whereas this distance has the required equality condition. For purely [Quantum Hamiltonian](../../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) dynamics, the target must lie on the initial state's [unitary orbit of a density operator](../../../../../../../unitary-orbit-of-a-density-operator.md) to attain zero error; otherwise the constrained optimization finds the closest accessible state instead.
