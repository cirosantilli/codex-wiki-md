<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [reachable set](../../../../../../reachable-set.md) contains all states attainable from a specified initial state by admissible [control inputs](../../../../../../control-input.md) in the allowed times, and [controllability](../../../../../../controllability.md) means that every target in the declared state space is reachable from every initial state. For a closed [quantum control](../../../../../../quantum-control.md) system, distinguish [pure-state controllability](../../../../../../pure-state-controllability.md) on state rays, [density operator controllability](../../../../../../density-operator-controllability.md) on fixed-spectrum unitary orbits, and [unitary operator controllability](../../../../../../unitary-operator-controllability.md) for entire propagators. State engineering asks for a particular state transfer; process engineering asks for an entire [quantum gate](../../../../../../quantum-logic-gate.md), which must act correctly on every input. A closed [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) evolution preserves density-operator [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), so arbitrary mixed states of different spectra cannot be connected even by a fully operator-controllable system.
