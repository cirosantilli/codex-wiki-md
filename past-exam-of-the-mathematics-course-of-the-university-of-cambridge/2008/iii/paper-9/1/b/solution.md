↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**True.** If $H$ is an [open subgroup](../../../../../../open-subgroup.md), every left [coset](../../../../../../coset.md) $gH$ is open because left translation is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md). The other cosets partition the complement:

$$
G\setminus H=\bigcup_{g\notin H}gH.
$$

That complement is open, so $H$ is a [closed subgroup](../../../../../../closed-subgroup.md).
