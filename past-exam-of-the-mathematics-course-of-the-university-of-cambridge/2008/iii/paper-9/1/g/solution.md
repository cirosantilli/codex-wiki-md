↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

**True.** Given a compatible [left-invariant group metric](../../../../../../left-invariant-group-metric.md) $d$, define

$$
\boxed{d_R(x,y)=d(x^{-1},y^{-1}).}
$$

Pullback by the bijection $x\mapsto x^{-1}$ preserves all metric axioms, and because inversion is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md), $d_R$ induces the same topology. For right translation by $a$, left invariance gives

$$
d_R(xa,ya)=d(a^{-1}x^{-1},a^{-1}y^{-1})=d(x^{-1},y^{-1})=d_R(x,y).
$$

Thus $d_R$ is the required compatible [right-invariant group metric](../../../../../../right-invariant-group-metric.md). No metrizability theorem is needed.
