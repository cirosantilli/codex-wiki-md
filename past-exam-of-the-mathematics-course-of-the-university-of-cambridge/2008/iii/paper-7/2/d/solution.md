<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

First show that [free modules are flat](../../../../../../free-modules-are-flat.md). If $F=\bigoplus_{j\in J}Ae_j$, distributivity of the [tensor product of modules](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-modules.md) gives

$$
U\otimes_AF\cong\bigoplus_{j\in J}U.
$$

Explicitly, $u\otimes\sum_j a_je_j$ maps to $(a_ju)_j$, and the inverse sends a finitely supported tuple $(u_j)_j$ to $\sum_j u_j\otimes e_j$. For an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) $u:U\hookrightarrow V$, the tensor map is therefore the coordinatewise [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) $\bigoplus_JU\hookrightarrow\bigoplus_JV$. This proves flatness for any free rank.

By part (c), a [projective module](../../../../../../projective-module.md) $P$ is a [direct summand](../../../../../../direct-summand.md) of a [free module](../../../../../../free-module.md) $F$. The latter is flat by the preceding argument, and part (b) proves that its summand $P$ is [flat](../../../../../../flat-module.md). Therefore $\boxed{\text{every projective }A\text{-module is flat}}$. This proves [projective modules are flat](../../../../../../projective-modules-are-flat.md) directly from the exactness and splitting definitions.
