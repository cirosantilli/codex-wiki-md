<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For any [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) $u:U\hookrightarrow V$, the [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md) decomposition $F=P\oplus Q$ gives natural [module isomorphisms](../../../../../../module-isomorphism.md)

$$
U\otimes_AF\cong(U\otimes_AP)\oplus(U\otimes_AQ),\qquad V\otimes_AF\cong(V\otimes_AP)\oplus(V\otimes_AQ).
$$

Under these, $u\otimes1_F$ is the block-diagonal map $(u\otimes1_P)\oplus(u\otimes1_Q)$. It is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) because $F$ is a [flat module](../../../../../../flat-module.md). If an element lies in the [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $u\otimes1_P$, insert zero in the other component; injectivity of the whole map forces that element to be zero. Hence $u\otimes1_P$ is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) for every $u$, which proves $\boxed{P\text{ is flat}}$. This establishes that [direct summands of flat modules are flat](../../../../../../direct-summands-of-flat-modules-are-flat.md), without a finite-generation assumption.
