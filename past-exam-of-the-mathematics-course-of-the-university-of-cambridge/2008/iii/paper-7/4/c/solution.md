<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose algebra generators $b_1,\ldots,b_n$ for $B$ over $A$. Because the extension is integral, each $b_i$ satisfies a monic equation

$$
b_i^{e_i}+a_{i,e_i-1}b_i^{e_i-1}+\cdots+a_{i,0}=0\qquad(a_{i,j}\in A).
$$

Repeatedly reduce any exponent at least $e_i$ using this equation. Since the generators commute, every polynomial in them becomes an $A$-linear combination of the finitely many monomials

$$
b_1^{\nu_1}\cdots b_n^{\nu_n},\qquad0\leq\nu_i<e_i.
$$

Thus $B$ is a [finitely generated module](../../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) over $A$, with at most $D=\prod_i e_i$ generators. This proves the relevant [subalgebra generated by finitely many integral elements](../../../../../../subalgebra-generated-by-finitely-many-integral-elements.md) assertion rather than merely invoking it.

For a fixed [prime ideal](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak p\subset A$, form the [fiber ring](../../../../../../fiber-ring.md) $C=B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)$. Tensoring the finite generating list shows $\dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}C\leq D$: its [pure tensors](../../../../../../pure-tensor.md) with one span the fiber over the [residue field](../../../../../../residue-field.md) $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$. Part (b) therefore gives only finitely many primes in $C$. By the canonical bijection in part (a), these are precisely the primes of $B$ contracting to $\mathfrak p$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\#\{\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Spec}B:A\cap\mathfrak q=\mathfrak p\}\leq\dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}(B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p))\leq D<\infty.}
$$

No [Noetherian](../../../../../../noetherian-ring.md) hypothesis on $A$ is needed. For an empty algebra-generating list, $B=A$ and the empty product $D=1$ gives the same conclusion.
