<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set $S=A\setminus\mathfrak m$, a [multiplicative subset](../../../../../../multiplicatively-closed-set.md) because $\mathfrak m$ is a [prime ideal](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md). The [quotient module](../../../../../../quotient-module.md) $B=A/\mathfrak a$ is a [cyclic module](../../../../../../cyclic-module.md) generated by $\bar1$, so its [localization of a module](../../../../../../localization-of-a-module.md) $S^{-1}B$ is zero precisely when $\bar1/1=0$. By the [vanishing criterion in a module localization](../../../../../../vanishing-criterion-in-a-module-localization.md), this is equivalent to the existence of $s\in S$ with $s\bar1=0$ in $B$. The latter condition says $s\in\mathfrak a$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{B_{\mathfrak m}=0\iff\mathfrak a\cap(A\setminus\mathfrak m)\ne\varnothing\iff\mathfrak a\not\subseteq\mathfrak m.}
$$

Explicitly, if $s\in\mathfrak a\setminus\mathfrak m$, multiplication by $s$ annihilates $B$ but becomes invertible after [localization](../../../../../../localization-of-a-ring.md), forcing every localized element to be zero.
