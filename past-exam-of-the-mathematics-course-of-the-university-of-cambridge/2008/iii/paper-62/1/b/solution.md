<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On the expanding branch of the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md), dropping the vacuum term gives the [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md)

$$
\tau(a)\simeq\frac1{H_0}\int_0^a\frac{du}{\sqrt{\Omega_r+\Omega_m u}}
=\frac{2}{H_0\Omega_m}\left(\sqrt{\Omega_r+\Omega_m a}-\sqrt{\Omega_r}\right).
$$

The lower limit sets $\tau=0$ at the [Big Bang](../../../../../../big-bang.md). The approximation requires $\Omega_\Lambda a^4\ll\Omega_m a$, and remains safe earlier when [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md) dominates. At [matter-radiation equality](../../../../../../matter-radiation-equality.md), $\rho_{m,0}a^{-3}=\rho_{r,0}a^{-4}$, so $a_{\rm eq}=\Omega_r/\Omega_m$. Substitution yields

$$
\boxed{\tau_{\rm eq}=\frac{2\sqrt{\Omega_r}}{H_0\Omega_m}(\sqrt2-1).}
$$

In conformal coordinates the radial [FRW metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) is $ds^2=a^2(-d\tau^2+dr^2)$. A radial light ray therefore travels a comoving distance $d r=d\tau$. The maximum distance traversed since the [Big Bang](../../../../../../big-bang.md) is $\int_0^{t_{\rm eq}}dt/a=\tau_{\rm eq}$: this is the [comoving particle horizon](../../../../../../comoving-particle-horizon.md), rather than the instantaneous [comoving Hubble radius](../../../../../../comoving-hubble-radius.md) or the acoustic horizon. Restoring $c$ in the numerical conversion gives

$$
\tau_{\rm eq}\simeq
\frac{2(3000/h)\sqrt{4.2\times10^{-5}/h^2}(\sqrt2-1)}{\Omega_m}\,\mathrm{Mpc}
=\frac{16.1}{\Omega_m h^2}\,\mathrm{Mpc}.
$$

For $\Omega_m=0.3$ and $h=0.7$, the [comoving particle horizon](../../../../../../comoving-particle-horizon.md) at [matter-radiation equality](../../../../../../matter-radiation-equality.md) is **about $110\,\mathrm{Mpc}$**. The corresponding [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) is $a_{\rm eq}\simeq2.9\times10^{-4}$.
