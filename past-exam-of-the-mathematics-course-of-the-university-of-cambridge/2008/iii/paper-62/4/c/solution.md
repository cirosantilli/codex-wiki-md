<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

During [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md), the radiation source can be neglected irrespective of whether the mode is inside or outside the horizon in the supplied [linear cosmological density perturbation](../../../../../../linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) equation. The [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $\mathcal H=2/\tau$ and $4\pi Ga^2\rho_C=6/\tau^2$, just as in part (a). Therefore

$$
\delta''+\frac2\tau\delta'-\frac6{\tau^2}\delta=0.
$$

Substituting $\tau^q$ gives $(q-2)(q+3)=0$, so for every [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md)

$$
\delta_{C,\mathbf k}=A_{\mathbf k}\tau^2+B_{\mathbf k}\tau^{-3}.
$$

Thus the [matter-era growing and decaying density modes](../../../../../../matter-era-growing-and-decaying-density-modes.md) have **growing behaviour $\delta_{C,\mathbf k}\propto\tau^2\propto a$ for all $k$**. There is no pressure-gradient term introducing a $k$-dependent growth rate for [cold dark matter](../../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md). The coefficients retain their different earlier histories; common growth does not erase the spectral shape. A specially chosen pure decaying solution is of course possible, so the growing-mode statement does not assert that every initial condition grows.
