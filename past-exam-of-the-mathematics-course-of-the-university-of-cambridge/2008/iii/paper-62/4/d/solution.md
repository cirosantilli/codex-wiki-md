<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $P(k,\tau)=\langle|\delta_{C,\mathbf k}|^2\rangle$ be the [matter power spectrum](../../../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md) in the normalization of the question. The [Harrison-Peebles-Zeldovich spectrum](../../../../../../harrison-peebles-zeldovich-spectrum.md) gives $P\sim\mathcal C\tau^4k$ before entry. Using the order-of-magnitude [cosmological horizon crossing](../../../../../../cosmological-horizon-crossing.md) criterion $k\tau_H\sim1$, rather than tracking a wavelength factor of $2\pi$, gives

$$
P(k,\tau_H)\sim\mathcal C k^{-3}.
$$

The mass-fluctuation [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) on radius $k^{-1}$ is therefore

$$
\boxed{\left\langle\left(\frac{\delta M}{M}\right)_k^2\right\rangle_H
\sim k^3P(k,\tau_H)\sim\mathcal C,}
$$

independent of [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md). Equal logarithmic ranges of scales enter with comparable dimensionless fluctuation strength. This is the [scale invariance](../../../../../../scale-invariance.md) encoded by the [HPZ spectrum](../../../../../../harrison-peebles-zeldovich-spectrum.md); it is not the claim that the dimensional [matter power spectrum](../../../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md) is independent of $k$ at a fixed time.
