<h1 id="3/v/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Extra relativistic [neutrino](../../../../../../../neutrino.md) species increase the [effective number of relativistic energy degrees of freedom](../../../../../../../effective-number-of-relativistic-energy-degrees-of-freedom.md). At fixed [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md) the [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md) rises as $g_*^{1/2}$, while the standard conversion rate is unchanged. Consequently [cosmological weak freeze-out](../../../../../../../cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md) moves to higher [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md), $T_F\propto g_*^{1/6}$, and the [neutron-to-proton ratio](../../../../../../../neutron-to-proton-ratio.md) $r_F=e^{-Q/T_F}$ increases. The initial [neutron](../../../../../../../neutron.md) fraction $r_F/(1+r_F)$ therefore increases as well.

The quicker expansion also reduces the cosmic time required to cool to the [deuterium bottleneck](../../../../../../../deuterium-bottleneck.md) [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md): during [radiation domination](../../../../../../../radiation-domination.md), $t\propto g_*^{-1/2}T^{-2}$ while the thermal count is roughly constant. Fewer [neutrons](../../../../../../../neutron.md) decay before burning. Both factors in the [neutron](../../../../../../../neutron.md) budget increase, giving **a larger final helium-4 mass fraction**. This is the [primordial helium response to extra relativistic species](../../../../../../../primordial-helium-response-to-extra-relativistic-species.md), under the assumption that the additional species contribute mainly to expansion rather than independently changing the conversion rates.
