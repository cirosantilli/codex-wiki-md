<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The small [baryon-to-photon ratio](../../../../../../baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) suppresses nuclear equilibrium abundances through the factor $\eta^{A-1}$. Cooling eventually makes the binding-energy exponential favourable, but the [deuterium bottleneck](../../../../../../deuterium-bottleneck.md) delays substantial nuclear burning until well after [cosmological weak freeze-out](../../../../../../cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md). In ordinary [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](../../../../../../big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md), nearly every [neutron](../../../../../../neutron.md) surviving that delay ends up in helium-4. This makes the final [primordial helium mass fraction](../../../../../../primordial-helium-mass-fraction.md) chiefly a [neutron](../../../../../../neutron.md) budget rather than an unrestricted equilibrium abundance.

Let $r_F$ be the [neutron-to-proton ratio](../../../../../../neutron-to-proton-ratio.md) at freeze-out, let $\Delta t$ be the delay until effective nuclear burning, and let $\tau_n$ be the [neutron](../../../../../../neutron.md) mean lifetime. The initial fraction of [baryons](../../../../../../baryon.md) which are [neutrons](../../../../../../neutron.md) is $x_{n,F}=r_F/(1+r_F)$. Because [neutron](../../../../../../neutron.md) decay converts a [neutron](../../../../../../neutron.md) into a [proton](../../../../../../proton.md), total [baryon number](../../../../../../baryon-number.md) remains constant, while the [neutron](../../../../../../neutron.md) fraction becomes $x_{n,B}=x_{n,F}e^{-\Delta t/\tau_n}$. Each helium nucleus uses two [neutrons](../../../../../../neutron.md) and four [baryons](../../../../../../baryon.md). Hence [neutron-limited helium synthesis](../../../../../../neutron-limited-helium-synthesis.md) gives

$$
\boxed{Y_4\simeq\frac{2r_F}{1+r_F}e^{-\Delta t/\tau_n},\qquad
\tau_n=\frac{\tau_{1/2}(n)}{\ln2}.}
$$

Equivalently, $Y_4=2r_B/(1+r_B)$ with $r_B=x_{n,B}/(1-x_{n,B})$. This accounts for the extra [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) produced by [neutron](../../../../../../neutron.md) decay; multiplying $r_F$ alone by the survival factor would miss their contribution. The following responses refer to ordinary neutron-limited burning, without exotic particle injection or a change to the nuclear network.
