<h1 id="3/v/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A larger [baryon-to-photon ratio](../../../../../../../baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) favours bound nuclei at a fixed [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md) through the equilibrium factor $\eta^{A-1}$. In particular it helps deuterium survive photodissociation sooner: there are fewer energetic [photons](../../../../../../../photon.md) per [baryon](../../../../../../../baryon.md), and nuclear reaction rates per [baryon](../../../../../../../baryon.md) also rise. A schematic [deuterium bottleneck](../../../../../../../deuterium-bottleneck.md) condition is $\eta e^{B_D/T_B}\sim\mathrm{constant}$, suppressing slowly varying powers of [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md). It gives

$$
\frac{B_D}{T_B}\simeq\mathrm{constant}-\ln\eta,\qquad
\frac{dT_B}{d\ln\eta}\simeq\frac{T_B^2}{B_D}>0.
$$

Thus higher $\eta$ permits burning at a higher [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md). During [radiation domination](../../../../../../../radiation-domination.md) that means earlier cosmic time, $t_B\propto T_B^{-2}$, leaving less time for [free-neutron decay after freeze-out](../../../../../../../free-neutron-decay-after-freeze-out.md). More [neutrons](../../../../../../../neutron.md) enter helium, so **the final helium-4 mass fraction increases**, ordinarily rather weakly. This is the [baryon-density effect on primordial helium](../../../../../../../baryon-density-effect-on-primordial-helium.md).

The equilibrium helium factor $\eta^3$ indicates the direction of the change but is not a prediction that the final helium abundance grows without bound as $\eta^3$. Once almost all surviving [neutrons](../../../../../../../neutron.md) are captured, the final [primordial helium mass fraction](../../../../../../../primordial-helium-mass-fraction.md) is limited by their supply, as the shared [neutron](../../../../../../../neutron.md) budget shows.
