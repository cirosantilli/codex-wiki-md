<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Work in [natural units](../../../../../../natural-units.md) $\hbar=c=k_B=1$, at [temperatures](../../../../../../temperature.md) far below the $W$-boson mass but of order or above the [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) mass. The [Fermi interaction](../../../../../../fermi-interaction.md) then supplies a local four-fermion amplitude. The [Fermi constant](../../../../../../fermi-constant.md) has dimension energy$^{-2}$. In scattering of a thermal lepton from a nonrelativistic nucleon, the nucleon current matrix element has size $m_N$, while the lepton current has size $T$. Thus $\mathcal M\sim G_Fm_NT$. The two-body [relativistic scattering cross-section](../../../../../../relativistic-scattering-cross-section.md) contains the kinematic factor $s^{-1}\sim m_N^{-2}$; when the final and initial lepton energies are comparable, their momentum ratio is of order one. Consequently $\sigma\sim|\mathcal M|^2/m_N^2\sim G_F^2T^2$. This estimates [temperatures](../../../../../../temperature.md) above the [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) mass and mass-splitting thresholds; those scales only affect the dimensionless rate function near freeze-out.

The light leptons in the thermal bath have [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) $n\sim T^3$ and speed of order unity. Their conversion rate per nucleon is therefore

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{n\leftrightarrow p}\sim n\langle\sigma v\rangle\sim G_F^2T^5.}
$$

For example, $n+\nu_e\leftrightarrow p+e^-$ and $n+e^+\leftrightarrow p+\bar\nu_e$ change the [neutron-to-proton ratio](../../../../../../neutron-to-proton-ratio.md). The bath density here is the lepton density, not the much smaller [baryon](../../../../../../baryon.md) density. Dimensionless weak couplings, phase-space integrals and the several reaction channels set a numerical coefficient. [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) masses and the neutron-proton splitting also become important for a precision rate near [cosmological weak freeze-out](../../../../../../cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md).
