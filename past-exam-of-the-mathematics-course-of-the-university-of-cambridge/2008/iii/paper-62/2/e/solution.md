<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

With zero [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) and a homogeneous [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md), the preceding [perfect fluid](../../../../../../perfect-fluid.md) identification gives

$$
\boxed{P=\rho=\frac12\dot\phi^2.}
$$

This [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) is that of a [stiff fluid](../../../../../../stiff-fluid.md). The field equation integrates to $a^3\dot\phi=K$, since $\partial_t(a^3\dot\phi)=0$. Hence $\rho=K^2/(2a^6)$, agreeing with the [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md) for $w=1$. If this nonzero component dominates a flat universe, the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives

$$
\left(\frac{\dot a}{a}\right)^2=\frac{4\pi G K^2}{3a^6},\qquad
\dot a=\sqrt{\frac{4\pi G}{3}}\,|K|a^{-2}
$$

on the expanding branch. Integrating once more, $a^3=3\sqrt{4\pi G/3}\,|K|(t-t_B)$. Choosing the [Big Bang](../../../../../../big-bang.md) at $t_B=0$ yields

$$
\boxed{a(t)\propto t^{1/3}.}
$$

The [homogeneous free scalar as a stiff fluid](../../../../../../homogeneous-free-scalar-as-a-stiff-fluid.md) construction requires $K\ne0$; a constant zero-potential field has zero [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) and cannot dominate this expansion.
