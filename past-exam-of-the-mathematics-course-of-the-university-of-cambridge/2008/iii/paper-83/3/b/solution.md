<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [overdamped filament bending equation](../../../../../../small-slope-elastohydrodynamic-filament-equation.md) is linear, so sinusoidal forcing has a sinusoidal steady response at the same frequency. Write $h=h_0\operatorname{Re}[e^{i\omega t}F(\eta)]$ with $\eta=x/\ell$. Substitution gives

$$
i\omega\zeta F=-\frac{A}{\ell^4}F^{(4)}.
$$

Using the [elastohydrodynamic penetration length](../../../../../../elastohydrodynamic-penetration-length.md) from the previous part reduces this [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) to

$$
\boxed{F^{(4)}+iF=0.}
$$

The boundary conditions become $F(0)=1$, $F''(0)=0$, and decay of $F$ and its derivatives at infinity. The derivative condition expresses zero [bending moment](../../../../../../bending-moment.md) at the driven end, rather than a clamped slope. This [separation of variables](../../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) constructs the periodic response; an unspecified initial shape can add a transient solution of the homogeneous evolution problem.
