<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [filament bending modulus](../../../../../../filament-bending-modulus.md) has units force times length squared. The perpendicular [drag coefficient](../../../../../../drag-coefficient.md) in this equation is drag per unit length divided by velocity, so its units are force times time per length squared. Hence $A/(\zeta\omega)$ has units length to the fourth power. Balancing oscillatory viscous drag $\zeta\omega h$ against elastic force density $Ah/\ell^4$ gives the [elastohydrodynamic penetration length](../../../../../../elastohydrodynamic-penetration-length.md)

$$
\boxed{\ell(\omega)=\left(\frac{A}{\zeta\omega}\right)^{1/4},\qquad\omega>0.}
$$

Increasing frequency confines the response nearer the actuator. The chosen coefficient one in this characteristic length is a convention; the exact decay lengths acquire the root-dependent factors found below.
