↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An [equivalent martingale measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md) is a [probability measure](../../../../../../probability-measure.md) $Q$ equivalent to the objective measure $P$, meaning the two have exactly the same null events, under which the discounted stock $\widetilde S_t=S_t/B_t$ is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md). In this one-period model the condition is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E_Q[S_1/B_1\mid\mathcal F_0]=S_0/B_0,\qquad Q\sim P.}
$$

The discounted [bank account](../../../../../../bank-account.md) is identically one. [Integrability](../../../../../../integrability.md) of the discounted stock is part of the [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) condition. In an augmented market the discounted price of every additional asset must satisfy the same condition.
