<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [European call option](../../../../../../european-call-option.md) pays one in the state $S_1=5$ and zero in the other two states. Short one call, buy one third of a stock, and borrow one half unit of cash. Writing the option quote as $c=1/2$, the holdings and initial value are

$$
\boxed{(h_B,h_S,h_C)=(-1/2,1/3,-1),\qquad V_0=-1/2+1-c=0.}
$$

The terminal value is $-1/2+S_1/3-(S_1-4)^+$. At stock prices $5,3,2$ it is respectively

$$
\boxed{(1/6,\ 1/2,\ 1/6).}
$$

It is strictly positive in every state, so this [portfolio](../../../../../../investment-portfolio.md) is an [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md).
