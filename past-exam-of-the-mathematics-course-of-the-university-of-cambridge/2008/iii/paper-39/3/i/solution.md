↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Relative to a [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_t)$, a [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md) is an [adapted process](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) $U_t$ with $\mathbb E|U_t|<\infty$ for every $t$ and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[U_{t+1}\mid\mathcal F_t]\leq U_t\quad\text{almost surely for every }t.}
$$

By the [tower property](../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) this also gives $\mathbb E[U_u\mid\mathcal F_t]\leq U_t$ for every $u\geq t$.
