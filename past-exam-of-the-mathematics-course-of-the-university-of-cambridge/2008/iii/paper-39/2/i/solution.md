↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [complete market](../../../../../../complete-market.md) is one in which every [contingent claim](../../../../../../contingent-claim.md) in the pricing class can be replicated: for each finite maturity $T$ and terminal $\mathcal F_T$-measurable payoff $H$, there are an initial value and an admissible [self-financing strategy](../../../../../../self-financing-portfolio.md) whose terminal value is $H$ almost surely. It is enough for the [complete market](../../../../../../complete-market.md) characterization to include all claims with bounded discounted payoffs. Thus **every such payoff is attainable by trading the existing assets**, rather than needing an additional independent security.
