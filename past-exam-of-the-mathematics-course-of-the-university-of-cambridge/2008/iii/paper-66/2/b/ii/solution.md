<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the screen trace of the [optical matrix of a null congruence](../../../../../../../optical-matrix-of-a-null-congruence.md) equation. The cross-contractions of symmetric [null shear](../../../../../../../null-shear.md) with antisymmetric [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md) vanish, and

$$
\operatorname{tr}(\widehat B^2)=\frac12\theta^2+\widehat\sigma_{ab}\widehat\sigma^{ab}-\widehat\omega_{ab}\widehat\omega^{ab}.
$$

The minus sign in the [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md) contribution follows from tracing the square of an [antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md). The tidal trace is $P^{ef}R_{cedf}U^cU^d=R_{cd}U^cU^d$: the additional null terms in $P^{ef}$ vanish by curvature antisymmetry. Thus the [Null Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../../null-raychaudhuri-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{U\cdot\nabla\theta=-\frac12\theta^2-\widehat\sigma_{ab}\widehat\sigma^{ab}+\widehat\omega_{ab}\widehat\omega^{ab}-R_{ab}U^aU^b.}
$$

In particular, [null shear](../../../../../../../null-shear.md) focuses the [null geodesic congruence](../../../../../../../null-geodesic-congruence.md) while [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md) enters with the opposite sign. With vanishing [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md) and the [null energy condition](../../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md), the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) imply $U\cdot\nabla\theta\le-\theta^2/2$. These signs and the factor $1/2$ depend on using the full-trace [null expansion](../../../../../../../null-expansion.md) in a two-dimensional screen.
