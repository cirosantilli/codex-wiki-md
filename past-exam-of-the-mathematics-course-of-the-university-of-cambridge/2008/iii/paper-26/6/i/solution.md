<h1 id="6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume [subobjects](../../../../../../subobject.md) of projectives are projective. For any object $X$, choose $0\to K\to P\to X\to0$ with $P$ projective. The [kernel in a category](../../../../../../kernel-in-a-category.md) $K$ is projective by the assumption. The derived long exact sequence gives a dimension-shifting [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md)

$$
L_2F(X)\cong L_1F(K),
$$

because $L_2F(P)=L_1F(P)=0$. The right side is zero because $K$ is projective. Since $X$ and the [right exact functor](../../../../../../right-exact-additive-functor.md) $F$ were arbitrary, $\boxed{\text{(i)}\Rightarrow\text{(ii)}}$.
