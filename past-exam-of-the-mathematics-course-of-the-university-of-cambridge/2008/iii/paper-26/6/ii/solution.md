<h1 id="6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume $L_2F=0$ for every additive [right exact functor](../../../../../../right-exact-additive-functor.md) into an [abelian category](../../../../../../abelian-category.md). Given a [subobject](../../../../../../subobject.md) $K\hookrightarrow P$ of a projective, let $X$ be its [cokernel in a category](../../../../../../cokernel-in-a-category.md). The sequence $0\to K\to P\to X\to0$ is exact, and dimension shifting gives $L_1F(K)\cong L_2F(X)=0$ for every $F$. The projectivity criterion proved in the root solution makes $K$ projective. This gives (ii)$\Rightarrow$(i).

For any short exact sequence $0\to X\to Y\to Z\to0$, the long exact sequence now starts at the relevant place with

$$
0\to L_1F(X)\to L_1F(Y)\to L_1F(Z).
$$

It is exact because the preceding term $L_2F(Z)$ vanishes. This is precisely left exactness of $L_1F$, proving $\boxed{\text{(ii)}\Rightarrow\text{(iii)}}$.
