<h1 id="6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Assume $L_1F$ is left exact for every additive [right exact](../../../../../../right-exact-additive-functor.md) $F$. For $0\to K\to P\to X\to0$ with $P$ projective, left exactness makes $L_1F(K)\to L_1F(P)=0$ a [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md). Hence $L_1F(K)=0$. Dimension shifting then gives $L_2F(X)=0$. As $X,F$ are arbitrary, (iii)$\Rightarrow$(ii), completing

$$
\boxed{\text{(i)}\iff\text{(ii)}\iff\text{(iii)}.}
$$

The argument also recovers projectivity of every such [kernel in a category](../../../../../../kernel-in-a-category.md) directly from the universal first-derived-functor criterion.
