**Theorems matter at least as much as definitions: they show when a categorical description captures actual mathematical structure.** Definitions in [category theory](../../../../../category-theory-split.md) are exceptionally economical, but economy alone does not explain their applications.

An [adjunction](../../../../../adjoint-functors.md) compresses a universal mapping property into natural hom-set bijections. The free-group [adjunction](../../../../../adjoint-functors.md), for example, records that assigning images to generators extends uniquely to a homomorphism. That is a useful definition, but proving that a free group exists and satisfies this property still requires mathematics. The definition organizes that proof rather than replaces it.

The [Yoneda lemma](../../../../../yoneda-lemma.md) offers a sharper example. A [representable functor](../../../../../representable-functor.md) is easy to define; the theorem identifying [natural transformations](../../../../../natural-transformation.md) out of it with elements of another [functor](../../../../../functor.md) gives the definition its computational force. The representation of a set-valued [functor](../../../../../functor.md) as a [colimit](../../../../../colimit.md) of representables is then a substantive consequence, and the theorem that [filtered colimits](../../../../../filtered-colimit-of-modules.md) commute with [finite limits](../../../../../finite-limit.md) turns it into a criterion for finite-limit preservation. None of these conclusions follows merely from choosing attractive terminology.

Likewise a [monad](../../../../../monad.md) packages operations and their compatibility, while the [Eilenberg-Moore category](../../../../../eilenberg-moore-category.md) defines the corresponding algebras. The [Beck monadicity theorem](../../../../../beck-s-monadicity-theorem.md) answers a different question: whether a given [category](../../../../../category-split.md) is really recovered by that algebraic description. Applying it to [compact Hausdorff spaces](../../../../../compact-hausdorff-space.md) needs a topological argument about closed quotient relations. The definitions would not by themselves predict or justify the result.

Finally, [abelian categories](../../../../../abelian-category.md) put [kernels in a category](../../../../../kernel-in-a-category.md), [cokernels in a category](../../../../../cokernel-in-a-category.md) and exactness into a setting where homological arguments can be reused. Some consequences, such as image factorization, become short because strong structural content has deliberately been included in the definition. Yet [left derived functors](../../../../../left-derived-functor.md), long exact sequences and the detection of projective objects supply information about obstructions to exactness which the terminology alone cannot provide.

Definitions and theorems therefore play complementary roles. A good definition states what should be invariant across examples; a theorem proves which constructions preserve it, which examples satisfy it, and what can be deduced from it. [Category theory](../../../../../category-theory-split.md) makes that partnership unusually visible, rather than making proofs less important.
