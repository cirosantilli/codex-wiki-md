<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take $A=1$. By assumption $E=(1\downarrow F)$ has [finite limits](../../../../../../finite-limit.md), so $E^{\mathrm{op}}$ has finite [colimits](../../../../../../colimit.md). A [filtered category](../../../../../../filtered-category.md) is nonempty, admits a cocone over every pair of objects, and admits a coequalizing arrow for every parallel pair. The [initial object](../../../../../../initial-object.md) of $E^{\mathrm{op}}$ gives nonemptiness, its binary [coproducts](../../../../../../coproduct.md) give the required two-object cocones, and its [coequalizers](../../../../../../coequalizer.md) equalize parallel arrows. Consequently $\boxed{\text{(ii)}\Rightarrow\text{(iii)}}$. These statements also handle the otherwise possible empty [category of elements](../../../../../../category-of-elements.md).
