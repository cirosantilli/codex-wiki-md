<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Pauli matrices](../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md) obey $X|0\rangle=|1\rangle$, $X|1\rangle=|0\rangle$, $Y|0\rangle=i|1\rangle$, and $Y|1\rangle=-i|0\rangle$. Thus $X\otimes X$ and $Y\otimes Y$ contribute opposite amplitudes on $|00\rangle,|11\rangle$ and equal amplitudes on $|01\rangle,|10\rangle$. For the [XY exchange interaction](../../../../../../xy-exchange-interaction.md) $E=(X\otimes X+Y\otimes Y)/2$,

$$
\boxed{E|00\rangle=0,\quad E|01\rangle=|10\rangle,\quad E|10\rangle=|01\rangle,\quad E|11\rangle=0.}
$$

Equivalently $E=|01\rangle\langle10|+|10\rangle\langle01|$. It transfers an excitation across a link without creating or destroying excitations, so a sum of such links preserves the [single-excitation subspace](../../../../../../single-excitation-subspace.md).
