A [Hadamard space](../../../../../../hadamard-space.md) is a complete [CAT(0) space](../../../../../../cat-0-space.md). A useful equivalent definition, which makes the remaining assertions consequences rather than assumptions, is a [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) in which every pair $x,y$ has a midpoint $m$, with $d(x,m)=d(y,m)=d(x,y)/2$, satisfying

$$
\boxed{d(z,m)^2\leq\frac12d(z,x)^2+\frac12d(z,y)^2-\frac14d(x,y)^2
\quad\text{for every }z.}
$$

This is the nonpositive-curvature midpoint inequality. Euclidean [Hilbert spaces](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) satisfy it with equality in the parallelogram formula. The comparison argument in (c) shows that this definition gives the usual CAT(0) triangle condition.
