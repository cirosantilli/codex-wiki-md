<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $p,q$ lie in the [closed ball](../../../../../../closed-ball.md) of center $z$ and radius $R$. On their unique [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md), the [Hadamard squared-distance inequality](../../../../../../hadamard-squared-distance-inequality.md) gives

$$
d(z,\gamma(t))^2\leq(1-t)d(z,p)^2+td(z,q)^2-t(1-t)d(p,q)^2
\leq R^2.
$$

Thus every point of that [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) lies in the ball. By uniqueness there are no other [geodesics](../../../../../../geodesic.md) to consider, so **every [closed ball](../../../../../../closed-ball.md) is geodesically convex**.
