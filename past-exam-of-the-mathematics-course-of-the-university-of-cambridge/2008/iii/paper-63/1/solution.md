<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Use [metric signature](../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(+---)$ throughout this solution. [Stress-energy conservation](../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) for the [perfect fluid](../../../../../perfect-fluid.md) is $\nabla_bT^{ab}=0$. Expanding it and using [metric compatibility](../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) gives

$$
0=U^a\nabla_b[(\rho+p)U^b]+(\rho+p)U^b\nabla_bU^a-\nabla^ap.
$$

The normalization of the [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) implies $U_aU^b\nabla_bU^a=0$. Contracting with $U_a$ therefore gives the [relativistic perfect-fluid energy equation](../../../../../relativistic-perfect-fluid-energy-equation.md),

$$
U^b\nabla_b\rho+(\rho+p)\nabla_bU^b=0,
$$

while subtracting the component parallel to $U$ gives the [relativistic Euler equation](../../../../../relativistic-euler-equation.md),

$$
\boxed{(\rho+p)U^b\nabla_bU_a=\nabla_ap-U_aU^b\nabla_bp.}
$$

Equivalently, apply the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) $\delta_a{}^c-U_aU^c$ to the conservation equation. This form makes clear that a [pressure](../../../../../pressure.md) gradient orthogonal to the [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) supplies the fluid's [four-acceleration](../../../../../four-acceleration.md).
