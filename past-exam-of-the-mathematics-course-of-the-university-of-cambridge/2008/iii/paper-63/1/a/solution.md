<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $N=\sqrt{K^aK_a}>0$ on the fluid region and choose the [Killing vector field](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) $K$ to be future-directed. The normalized [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md) is

$$
\boxed{U^a=\frac{K^a}{N}.}
$$

The [Killing equation](../../../../../../killing-equation.md) implies both $K^b\nabla_bN=0$ and

$$
K^b\nabla_bK_a=-K^b\nabla_aK_b=-\frac12\nabla_a(K^bK_b).
$$

Consequently its [Killing-flow four-acceleration](../../../../../../killing-flow-four-acceleration.md) is

$$
U^b\nabla_bU_a=\frac1{N^2}K^b\nabla_bK_a=-\nabla_a\log N.
$$

For a scalar, the [Lie derivative](../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) is $\mathcal L_Kp=K^b\nabla_bp$. Thus the assumed stationarity of the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) gives $U^b\nabla_bp=0$. These are the ingredients needed for [hydrostatic equilibrium along a Killing vector](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium-along-a-killing-vector.md); hypersurface orthogonality of $K$ is not needed.
