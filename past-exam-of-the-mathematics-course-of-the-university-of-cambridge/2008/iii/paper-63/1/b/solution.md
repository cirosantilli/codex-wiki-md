<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substitute the [Killing-flow four-acceleration](../../../../../../killing-flow-four-acceleration.md) and stationary [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) into the [relativistic Euler equation](../../../../../../relativistic-euler-equation.md). It gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla_ap=-(\rho+p)\nabla_a\log N,
\qquad N=\sqrt{K^cK_c}.}
$$

For the [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) of [blackbody radiation](../../../../../../black-body-radiation.md), $p=\rho/3$, this becomes $d\rho=-4\rho\,d\log N$. The [barotropic hydrostatic first integral](../../../../../../barotropic-hydrostatic-first-integral.md) therefore gives, on each connected nonvacuum fluid region,

$$
\boxed{\rho N^4=C>0,\qquad \rho=C(K^cK_c)^{-2}.}
$$

This is the [stationary radiation density](../../../../../../stationary-radiation-density.md) law. Combining it with the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) $\rho\propto T^4$ also gives the [Tolman–Ehrenfest relation](../../../../../../tolman-ehrenfest-relation.md) $TN=\mathrm{constant}$.

At a regular finite [free surface](../../../../../../free-surface.md), a smooth [Killing vector field](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) has finite norm. Hence $C/N^4$ cannot approach zero there. More globally, its norm has a finite upper bound on a [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) regular fluid closure, which bounds the density away from zero whenever $C>0$. If the timelike norm instead tends to zero, the density diverges. Thus a [radiation fluid has no regular free surface](../../../../../../radiation-fluid-has-no-regular-free-surface.md): a finite radiation-filled cavity needs a material wall supporting nonzero [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md), rather than an unsupported radiation-vacuum interface.
