<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $S,T$ be [module sources](../../../../../../source-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) at $D$. Since $S\mid M\downarrow_D$ and $M\mid T\uparrow^G$, [Mackey decomposition](../../../../../../mackey-restriction-formula.md) gives

$$
S\mid\operatorname{Res}_D^G\operatorname{Ind}_D^G T
\cong\bigoplus_{g\in D\backslash G/D}
\operatorname{Ind}_{D\cap{}^gD}^D
\operatorname{Res}_{D\cap{}^gD}^{{}^gD}({}^gT).
$$

The [module source](../../../../../../source-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) $S$ has [module vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) $D$. By [Krull–Schmidt theorem](../../../../../../krull-schmidt-theorem.md) it is a summand of one term, and minimality of its [module vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) forces $D\cap{}^gD=D$. As both [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) have order $|D|$, $g\in N_G(D)$. The corresponding term is simply ${}^gT$, which is indecomposable. Thus

$$
\boxed{S\cong{}^gT\quad\text{for some }g\in N_G(D).}
$$

This proves conjugacy of [sources of an indecomposable module](../../../../../../source-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) at the specified [module vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md).
