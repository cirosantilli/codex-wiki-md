<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $D,E$ be [module vertices](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) of $M$, and choose a [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) $T$ such that $M\mid\operatorname{Ind}_E^G T$. Relative $D$-projectivity then gives

$$
M\mid\operatorname{Ind}_D^G\operatorname{Res}_D^G\operatorname{Ind}_E^G T.
$$

Apply [Mackey decomposition](../../../../../../mackey-restriction-formula.md) to the inner restriction and use induction transitivity. The resulting [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) is a [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md) of [modules](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) induced to $G$ from [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $D\cap{}^gE$, for representatives $g\in D\backslash G/E$. By [Krull–Schmidt theorem](../../../../../../krull-schmidt-theorem.md), the [indecomposable module](../../../../../../indecomposable-module.md) $M$ is a summand of one of these [induced representations](../../../../../../induced-representation.md). It is therefore relatively projective for one intersection contained in $D$. Minimality of $D$ forces $D=D\cap{}^gE$, so $D\le{}^gE$.

Interchanging $D,E$ gives $E\le{}^hD$ for some $h$. The two inclusions force equal orders, and hence

$$
\boxed{D={}^gE.}
$$

Thus all [vertices of an indecomposable module](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) are conjugate in $G$.
