<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

If $N_G(D)\le H\le G$, then $N_H(D)=N_G(D)=N$ and $C_H(D)=C_G(D)=C$. Applying part (d) to $G$ and to $H$ therefore gives the same target [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md)

$$
J_D=\operatorname{Tr}_D^N(kC).
$$

Compose the two bijections. A block [idempotent](../../../../../../idempotent.md) $e$ of $G$ with defect $D$ corresponds to the unique block [idempotent](../../../../../../idempotent.md) $f$ of $H$ with defect $D$ satisfying

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Br}_D^G(e)=\operatorname{Br}_D^H(f).}
$$

This proves the requested form of [Brauer first main theorem](../../../../../../brauer-first-main-theorem.md), including uniqueness and the equality of the Brauer images.
