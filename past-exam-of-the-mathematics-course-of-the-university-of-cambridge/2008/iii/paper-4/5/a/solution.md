<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $D$ be a $p$-subgroup and let $N_G(D)\le H\le G$. Put

$$
\mathcal X=\{D\cap{}^gD:g\in G\setminus H\},\qquad
\mathcal Y=\{H\cap{}^gD:g\in G\setminus H\}.
$$

A [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) relatively projective for a family means a summand of a sum of [modules](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) induced from its members, allowing [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) and conjugates. The [Green correspondence](../../../../../../green-correspondence.md) gives inverse bijections $f,g$ between the indecomposable $RG$-modules and $RH$-modules with [module vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) $D$, up to isomorphism, such that

$$
\operatorname{Res}_H^G M\cong f(M)\oplus M_{\mathcal Y},\qquad
\operatorname{Ind}_H^G U\cong g(U)\oplus U_{\mathcal X}.
$$

The error terms are respectively relatively $\mathcal Y$-projective and $\mathcal X$-projective. In particular the displayed correspondents are the unique summands of [module vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) $D$, occur with multiplicity one, and have the same [module source](../../../../../../source-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) as the original [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md), up to [normalizer](../../../../../../normalizer.md) conjugacy.

We will also use the general form of [Green correspondence](../../../../../../green-correspondence.md): replace [module vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) $D$ by any [module vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) $Q\le D$ not conjugate into a member of $\mathcal X$. Restriction and induction again have unique summands with that [module vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md), and the same error-family description. This is important in the final induction argument: merely knowing that an error term has a proper [module vertex](../../../../../../vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) would not give the required intersection bound.
