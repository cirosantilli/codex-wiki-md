<h1 id="7e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The assertion is **true**. A [Möbius map](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) fixes infinity if and only if $c=0$: if $c\ne0$, its value there is the finite number $a/c$. For $c=0$, the decomposition above uses only a nonzero complex scaling and a translation. These maps form the [Affine subgroup of the Möbius group](../../../../../../affine-subgroup-of-the-mobius-group.md).
