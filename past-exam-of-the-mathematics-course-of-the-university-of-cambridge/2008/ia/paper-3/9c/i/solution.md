<h1 id="9c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [vector triple product](../../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) removes the nested [cross products](../../../../../../cross-product.md):

$$
F_i=\omega_i\omega_jx_j-|\boldsymbol\omega|^2x_i.
$$

Since $\boldsymbol\omega$ is constant, the [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) is

$$
\partial_iF_i=\omega_i\omega_i-3|\boldsymbol\omega|^2=-2|\boldsymbol\omega|^2.
$$

The cube has [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) $2^3=8$. With the outward [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md), the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\int_S\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=-16|\boldsymbol\omega|^2.}
$$
