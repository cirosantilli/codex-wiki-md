<h1 id="3c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate the parametrized [curve](../../../../../../curve.md) to obtain $\boldsymbol x'(t)=(1-t^2,2t,1+t^2)$. Its [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) obeys

$$
|\boldsymbol x'(t)|^2=(1-t^2)^2+4t^2+(1+t^2)^2=2(1+t^2)^2.
$$

Since $1+t^2>0$, the [arc length](../../../../../../arc-length.md) is

$$
\boxed{L=\int_0^1\sqrt2(1+t^2)\,dt=\frac{4\sqrt2}{3}.}
$$
