<h1 id="5e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume $H=\ker\theta$ for a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $\theta:G\to K$. For $h\in H$ and $g\in G$,

$$
\theta(ghg^{-1})=\theta(g)\theta(h)\theta(g)^{-1}=e_K.
$$

Thus $gHg^{-1}\subseteq H$. Apply the same inclusion with $g^{-1}$ to obtain the reverse inclusion, giving $gHg^{-1}=H$. Hence **every kernel is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md)**, proving the reverse implication and the required equivalence.
