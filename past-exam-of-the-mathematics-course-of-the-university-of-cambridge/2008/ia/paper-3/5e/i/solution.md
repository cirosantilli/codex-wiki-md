<h1 id="5e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume $H$ is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md). Take the [quotient group](../../../../../../quotient-group.md) $K=G/H$ and the natural projection $\theta(g)=gH$. The well-defined multiplication constructed above gives $\theta(gk)=\theta(g)\theta(k)$, so this is a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md). Its identity is the [coset](../../../../../../coset.md) $H$, and $\theta(g)=H$ exactly when $g\in H$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\ker\theta=H,}
$$

which proves the implication from normality to being the [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md).
