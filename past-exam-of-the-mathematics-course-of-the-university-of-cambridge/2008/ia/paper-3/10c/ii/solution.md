<h1 id="10c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [tensor contraction](../../../../../../tensor-contraction.md) $B_{kl}=\delta_{ij}T_{ijkl}$ is an [isotropic second-rank tensor](../../../../../../isotropic-second-rank-tensor.md), because the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../../kronecker-delta.md) and $T$ are invariant under [rotations](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md). The classification proved above yields **$B_{kl}=\mu\delta_{kl}$** for some scalar $\mu$.

For the given three-term [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md), contraction over $i,j$ gives

$$
\delta_{ij}T_{ijkl}=\alpha\delta_{ii}\delta_{kl}+\beta\delta_{kl}+\gamma\delta_{kl}
=\boxed{(3\alpha+\beta+\gamma)\delta_{kl}}.
$$

Thus **$\mu=3\alpha+\beta+\gamma$**.
