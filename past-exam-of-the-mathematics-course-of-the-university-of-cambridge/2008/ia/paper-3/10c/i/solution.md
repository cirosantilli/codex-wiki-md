<h1 id="10c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Both the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) and $T$ are invariant under proper [rotations](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md). Their [tensor contraction](../../../../../../tensor-contraction.md)

$$
A_l=\epsilon_{ijk}T_{ijkl}
$$

is consequently an isotropic [vector](../../../../../../vector.md). The introductory proof shows that it must vanish, so **$\epsilon_{ijk}T_{ijkl}=0$**.

For the displayed combination of [Kronecker deltas](../../../../../../kronecker-delta.md), the three terms are

$$
\alpha\epsilon_{ijk}\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl},\qquad
\beta\epsilon_{ijk}\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl},\qquad
\gamma\epsilon_{ijk}\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}.
$$

In each term the [tensor contraction](../../../../../../tensor-contraction.md) repeats two indices of the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md); each term is zero. This verifies the identity for arbitrary $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$.
