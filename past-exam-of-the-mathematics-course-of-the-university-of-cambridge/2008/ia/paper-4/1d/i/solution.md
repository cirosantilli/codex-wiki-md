**False.** Take $A=\{0\}$ and $B=C=\{0,1\}$, with $f(0)=0$ and $g=\operatorname{id}_B$. Then $f$ is an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) and $g$ is a [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), but the [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) $g\circ f$ has [image of a function](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) $\{0\}$, so it is not a [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) onto $C$.
