↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**True.** For each $a\in A$, the definition of [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md) gives

$$
a\in f^{-1}(Z\cap W)\iff f(a)\in Z\cap W\iff f(a)\in Z\text{ and }f(a)\in W\iff a\in f^{-1}(Z)\cap f^{-1}(W).
$$

Thus $\boxed{f^{-1}(Z\cap W)=f^{-1}(Z)\cap f^{-1}(W)}$. Unlike the corresponding statement for [images of a function](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md), this [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) identity requires no [injectivity](../../../../../../injective-function.md).
