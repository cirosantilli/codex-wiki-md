<h1 id="6d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [greatest common divisor](../../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) is

$$
\gcd(437,1012)=\gcd(19\cdot23,44\cdot23)=23,
$$

since $19$ and $44$ are [coprime integers](../../../../../../coprime-integers.md). Part (b) therefore gives $\boxed{1012/23=44}$ distinct $437$th powers in the nonzero [multiplicative group of a finite field](../../../../../../multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_{1013}^\times$. If “powers mod $1013$” includes all [residue classes](../../../../../../residue-class.md), the additional value $0=0^{437}$ gives **$\boxed{45}$ values including zero**. The distinction is relevant because part (b) explicitly works in the nonzero group.
