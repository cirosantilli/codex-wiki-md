<h1 id="5a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Before time one, both forcing terms vanish. The [separable differential equation](../../../../../../separable-differential-equation.md) $\theta_i'=-a\theta_i$ gives $\theta_i(t)=C_i e^{-at}$. The initial values give $C_1=C_2=T_0-T_\infty$, and hence

$$
\boxed{T_1(t)=T_2(t)=T_\infty+(T_0-T_\infty)e^{-at},\qquad0\leq t<1.}
$$

In particular the common left-hand limit at time one is $T_\infty+(T_0-T_\infty)e^{-a}$.
