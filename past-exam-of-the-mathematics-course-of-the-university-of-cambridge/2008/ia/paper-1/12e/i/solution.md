<h1 id="12e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $z\ne0$, the ratio of successive absolute terms is

$$
\frac{|z^{n+1}|/(n+1)^2}{|z^n|/n^2}
=|z|\left(\frac n{n+1}\right)^2\longrightarrow|z|.
$$

The [ratio test](../../../../../../ratio-test.md) therefore gives [absolute convergence](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) for $|z|<1$ and divergence for $|z|>1$, while at zero the series is trivially convergent. Hence $R=1$. On the boundary, $|z^n/n^2|=1/n^2$, and the convergent [p-series](../../../../../../p-series.md) proves

$$
\boxed{R=1;\quad\text{absolute convergence at every point of }|z|=1.}
$$
