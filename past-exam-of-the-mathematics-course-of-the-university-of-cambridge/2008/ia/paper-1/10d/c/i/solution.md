<h1 id="10d/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**No such continuous function exists.** The [extreme value theorem](../../../../../../../extreme-value-theorem.md) makes every [continuous function](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on the [closed interval](../../../../../../../closed-real-interval.md) $[0,1]$ bounded and gives it a finite maximum. Its image therefore cannot equal the unbounded [real interval](../../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $[0,\infty)$.
