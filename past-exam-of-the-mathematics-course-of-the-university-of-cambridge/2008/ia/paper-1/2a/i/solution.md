<h1 id="2a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By the [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md) identities,

$$
\begin{aligned}
U^\dagger U&=(A-iB)(A+iB)=A^2+B^2+i(AB-BA)=I,\\
UU^\dagger&=(A+iB)(A-iB)=A^2+B^2-i(AB-BA)=I.
\end{aligned}
$$

Subtracting the two equations yields $2i(AB-BA)=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{AB=BA.}
$$

The subtraction is important: entrywise real and imaginary parts cannot generally be used to separate these terms, since the [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) need not have real entries.
