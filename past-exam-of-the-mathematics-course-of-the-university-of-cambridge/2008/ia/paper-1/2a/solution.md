<h1 id="2a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2A](../2a.md)

Taking the [conjugate transpose](../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) of $U=A+iB$ and using that both parts are [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) gives $U^\dagger=A-iB$. Addition and subtraction recover the [Hermitian parts of a unitary matrix](../../../../../hermitian-parts-of-a-unitary-matrix.md):

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{U+U^\dagger}{2},\qquad
B=\frac{U-U^\dagger}{2i}.}
$$

These depend only on $U$, so any such decomposition has the same $A,B$ and is unique. Conversely the displayed expressions are [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and sum to $U$ in the form $A+iB$, establishing existence as well as uniqueness. Their commutation and quadratic relation are proved in the two labelled parts.
