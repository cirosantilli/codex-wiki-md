↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Adding the two expanded [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md) identities instead cancels the commutator and gives $2(A^2+B^2)=2I$. Hence

$$
\boxed{A^2+B^2=I.}
$$

Together with part (i), this characterizes the commuting [Hermitian parts of a unitary matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-parts-of-a-unitary-matrix.md).
