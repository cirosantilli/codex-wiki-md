<h1 id="5b/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $\tau=a\cdot(b\times c)$. The [vector triple product identity](../../../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) gives

$$
(b\times c)\times(c\times a)
=[(b\times c)\cdot a]c-[(b\times c)\cdot c]a=\tau c.
$$

Cyclic invariance of the [scalar triple product](../../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) gives $(a\times b)\cdot c=\tau$. Hence the [squared scalar triple product from cyclic cross products](../../../../../../../squared-scalar-triple-product-from-cyclic-cross-products.md) is

$$
\boxed{(a\times b)\cdot[(b\times c)\times(c\times a)]=[a\cdot(b\times c)]^2.}
$$

If an expansion entirely in pairwise [dot products](../../../../../../../dot-product.md) is desired, expand the [Gram determinant](../../../../../../../gram-determinant.md) of $a,b,c$:

$$
\boxed{|a|^2|b|^2|c|^2+2(a\cdot b)(b\cdot c)(c\cdot a)
-|a|^2(b\cdot c)^2-|b|^2(c\cdot a)^2-|c|^2(a\cdot b)^2.}
$$

Both expressions remain valid for degenerate, including linearly dependent, vectors.
