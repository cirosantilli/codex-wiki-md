<h1 id="5b/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) contraction $\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{i\ell m}=\delta_{j\ell}\delta_{km}-\delta_{jm}\delta_{k\ell}$ in [suffix notation](../../../../../../../einstein-notation.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
(a\times b)\cdot(c\times d)
&=\epsilon_{ijk}a_jb_k\epsilon_{i\ell m}c_\ell d_m\\
&=(\delta_{j\ell}\delta_{km}-\delta_{jm}\delta_{k\ell})a_jb_kc_\ell d_m.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md) contraction expands as

$$
\boxed{(a\times b)\cdot(c\times d)=(a\cdot c)(b\cdot d)-(a\cdot d)(b\cdot c).}
$$
