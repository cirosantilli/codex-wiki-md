<h1 id="11d/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $f$ is [differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) at $x$, the [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) applied to $f\cdot f$ gives

$$
\boxed{g'(x)=2f(x)f'(x).}
$$

This proves sufficiency of the first alternative. No additional growth condition is required; if $f(x)=0$, the derivative of the square is zero.
