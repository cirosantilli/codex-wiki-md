<h1 id="24h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Parametrize the regular curve by arclength $s$. Its unit tangent is $T=\gamma'$, and its [curvature](../../../../../../curvature.md) is $\kappa=|T'|$. Where $\kappa>0$, define the principal normal $N=T'/\kappa$ and binormal $B=T\times N$. The [torsion of a space curve](../../../../../../torsion-of-a-curve.md) is $\tau=-B'\cdot N$. Orthonormality and differentiation give the [Frenet-Serret formulas](../../../../../../frenet-serret-formulas.md)

$$
\boxed{T'=\kappa N,\qquad N'=-\kappa T+\tau B,\qquad B'=-\tau N.}
$$

For a general parameter, $\kappa=|\dot\gamma\times\ddot\gamma|/|\dot\gamma|^3$ and $\tau=\det(\dot\gamma,\ddot\gamma,\dddot\gamma)/|\dot\gamma\times\ddot\gamma|^2$. The normal and torsion formulas require nonzero [curvature](../../../../../../curvature.md); at zero-[curvature](../../../../../../curvature.md) points this particular Frenet frame need not be defined.
