<h1 id="28j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The Dollar bank account is a tradable asset whose Euro value is $Y_t e^{r_Dt}$. Under the Euro [equivalent martingale measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md), discount this value by $e^{r_Et}$. The result is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) precisely when $\mu+r_D-r_E=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{\mu=r_E-r_D\quad\text{under }Q_E.}
$$

When the rates coincide, $\mu=0$ and part (a) proves that the exchange rate itself is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) under $Q_E$.
