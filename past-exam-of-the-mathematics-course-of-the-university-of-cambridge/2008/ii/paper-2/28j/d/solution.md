<h1 id="28j/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For equal rates, the [change of numeraire](../../../../../../change-of-numeraire.md) from the Euro bank account to the Dollar account has density process $Z_t=Y_t/Y_0$, a strictly positive mean-one [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) under $Q_E$ by part (b). Conditional [change of measure](../../../../../../change-of-measure.md) gives

$$
E_{Q_D}\left[\frac1{Y_t}\mid\mathcal F_s\right]=\frac1{Z_s}E_{Q_E}\left[\frac{Z_t}{Y_t}\mid\mathcal F_s\right]=\frac1{Z_sY_0}=\boxed{\frac1{Y_s}}.
$$

Thus the reciprocal is a genuine [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) under $Q_D$, directly from the Euro-measure [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) property, without recalculating its stochastic differential equation.
