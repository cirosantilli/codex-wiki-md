<h1 id="24h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Parametrize a regular curve by arclength $s$, write its unit tangent as $T$, and choose an oriented surface unit normal $N$. Its signed [geodesic curvature](../../../../../../geodesic-curvature.md) is

$$
\boxed{k_g=\langle dT/ds,N\times T\rangle.}
$$

It vanishes exactly when the acceleration of the unit-speed curve is normal to the surface, equivalently when the curve is a geodesic with arclength parametrization. For an arbitrary regular parametrization, this means the curve becomes a geodesic after reparametrization.

The global [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](../../../../../../gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) for a compact oriented surface with smooth boundary, positively oriented with the surface on the left, is

$$
\boxed{\int_SK\,dA+\int_{\partial S}k_g\,ds=2\pi\chi(S).}
$$

For piecewise smooth boundary, add the signed exterior turning angles at its corners to the left side.
