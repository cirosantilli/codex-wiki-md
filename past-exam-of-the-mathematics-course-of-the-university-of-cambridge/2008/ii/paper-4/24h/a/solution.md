<h1 id="24h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a compact triangulated surface, the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) is $\chi=V-E+F$, [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the triangulation. For a connected closed orientable surface, the [genus](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$ is its number of handles, characterized by

$$
\boxed{\chi=2-2g.}
$$

A connected compact orientable surface with $b$ boundary components instead has $\chi=2-2g-b$. For multiple connected components the Euler characteristics add, with [genus](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) specified for each component. A compact boundaryless surface embedded in $\mathbb R^3$ is orientable, so the usual closed-surface formula applies to each connected component.
