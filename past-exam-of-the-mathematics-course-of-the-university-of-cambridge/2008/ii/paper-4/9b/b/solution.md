<h1 id="9b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $\omega_1=u$, $\omega_2=\Omega+v$, $\omega_3=w$ and retain terms linear in the perturbations in [Euler equations for a torque-free rigid body](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-a-torque-free-rigid-body.md). Using $I_3=I_1+I_2$ gives

$$
\dot u=-\Omega w,\qquad\dot v=0,\qquad
\dot w=-\frac{a^2-b^2}{a^2+b^2}\Omega u.
$$

Consequently $\ddot u=\beta^2\Omega^2u$, where $\beta=\sqrt{(a^2-b^2)/(a^2+b^2)}>0$. For $\Omega>0$ the growing solution is

$$
\boxed{u=C e^{\beta\Omega t},\qquad w=-\beta C e^{\beta\Omega t}.}
$$

There is also a decaying mode with the opposite exponent. For negative $\Omega$, select the exponent $+\beta|\Omega|$ as the growing one. This is the [intermediate axis theorem](../../../../../../intermediate-axis-theorem.md): steady rotation about the intermediate principal inertia axis is unstable whenever $\Omega\ne0$.
