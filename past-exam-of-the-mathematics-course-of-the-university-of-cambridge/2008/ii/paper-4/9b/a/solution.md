<h1 id="9b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the sheet to lie in the $x_1x_2$ plane, with lengths $a,b$ in those directions. Its surface density is $M/(ab)$. Reflection symmetry about the coordinate axes makes the off-diagonal entries of the [inertia tensor](../../../../../../inertia-tensor.md) zero. Its [principal moments of inertia](../../../../../../principal-moment-of-inertia.md) are

$$
I_1=\frac M{ab}\int_{-a/2}^{a/2}\int_{-b/2}^{b/2}x_2^2\,dx_2dx_1=\frac{Mb^2}{12},
$$

and similarly $I_2=Ma^2/12$. Since $I_3$ integrates $x_1^2+x_2^2$, the [perpendicular axis theorem](../../../../../../perpendicular-axis-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{I_1=Mb^2/12,\quad I_2=Ma^2/12,\quad I_3=M(a^2+b^2)/12.}
$$
