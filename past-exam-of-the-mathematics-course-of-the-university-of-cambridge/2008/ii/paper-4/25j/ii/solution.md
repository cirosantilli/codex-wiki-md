<h1 id="25j/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $t\ne0$, direct integration of each interval in the [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) gives

$$
\widehat s(t)=\sum_{k=1}^nc_k\frac{e^{itb_k}-e^{ita_k}}{it}.
$$

Hence $|\widehat s(t)|\leq2\sum_k|c_k|/|t|$, and

$$
\boxed{\widehat s(t)\longrightarrow0\quad\text{as }|t|\to\infty.}
$$

The finite value at zero is $\sum_kc_k(b_k-a_k)$ and does not affect this limit.
