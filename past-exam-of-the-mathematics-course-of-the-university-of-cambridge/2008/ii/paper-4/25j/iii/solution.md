<h1 id="25j/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose a [step function](../../../../../../step-function.md) $s$ with $\|f-s\|_1<\varepsilon$, by part (i). The [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) satisfies

$$
|\widehat f(t)-\widehat s(t)|
\leq\int|f(x)-s(x)|\,dx<\varepsilon.
$$

By part (ii), $\limsup_{|t|\to\infty}|\widehat f(t)|\leq\varepsilon$. Since $\varepsilon$ is arbitrary,

$$
\boxed{\widehat f(t)\to0\quad\text{as }|t|\to\infty.}
$$

This proves the [Riemann-Lebesgue lemma](../../../../../../riemann-lebesgue-lemma.md) with the transform convention in the question.
