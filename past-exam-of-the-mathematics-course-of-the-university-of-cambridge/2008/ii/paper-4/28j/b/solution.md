<h1 id="28j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $b=1-R>0$ and $m_b=\mathbb EX_0^b$. Homogeneity of the [constant relative risk aversion utility](../../../../../../constant-relative-risk-aversion-utility.md) gives $U(z_N)=U(z_n)\prod_{j=n}^{N-1}X_j^b$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[U(z_N)\mid z_n]=U(z_n)m_b^{N-n}.}
$$

As before this is an extended-valued [conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) if the required moment is infinite and days remain.
