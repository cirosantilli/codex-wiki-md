↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md),

$$
\|Uf\|^2=\langle U^*Uf,f\rangle=\|f\|^2.
$$

Thus it is an [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md), and every power has norm one. The triangle inequality gives

$$
\|A_nf\|\leq\frac1n\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\|U^kf\|=\|f\|,\qquad
\boxed{\|A_n\|\leq1.}
$$
