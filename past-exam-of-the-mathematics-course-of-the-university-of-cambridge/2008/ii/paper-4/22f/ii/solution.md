<h1 id="22f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The set $X$ is the range of the linear operator $I-U$, hence is a linear subspace. If $f=g-Ug$, the sum telescopes:

$$
A_nf=\frac1n\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(U^kg-U^{k+1}g)=\frac{g-U^ng}{n}.
$$

Since $U$ is an [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md), $\|A_nf\|\leq2\|g\|/n\to0$. Hence **the averages tend to zero on $X$**.
