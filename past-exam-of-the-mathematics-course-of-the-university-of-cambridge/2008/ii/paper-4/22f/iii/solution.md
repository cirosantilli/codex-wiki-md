<h1 id="22f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $v\in\overline X$, choose $x\in X$ with $\|v-x\|<\varepsilon$. By part (i),

$$
\|A_nv\|\leq\|A_n(v-x)\|+\|A_nx\|\leq\varepsilon+\|A_nx\|.
$$

The last term tends to zero by part (ii). Taking the limit superior and then $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ proves **$A_nv\to0$ for every $v\in\overline X$**.
