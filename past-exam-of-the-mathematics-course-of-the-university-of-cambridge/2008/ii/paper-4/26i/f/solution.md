↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For equilibrium, let $t\to\infty$ in part (e). Monotone convergence of the tail integral and the finite mean give

$$
\int_v^\infty\mathbb P(S>s)\,ds=\mathbb E(S-v)_+,
$$

and consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(V^E>v)=1-\exp[-\lambda\mathbb E(S-v)_+].}
$$

This limiting argument uses the equilibrium age and residual-service distribution produced by the queue, not just a fresh [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) copy of $S$ for each mosquito present. Replacing their residual service by fresh service would generally be wrong unless the service law is memoryless.
