<h1 id="26i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The tail integral identity gives $\int_0^\infty\mathbb P(S>v)\,dv=\mathbb ES$. Therefore the limiting and stationary occupancy distribution is

$$
\boxed{Q^E\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda\mathbb ES).}
$$

It is finite under the stated finite-mean assumption.
