<h1 id="30a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a simple stationary point at the endpoint $b$, only one half of the local quadratic neighborhood lies in the integration interval. The even Gaussian integrand therefore gives half the interior [stationary phase](../../../../../../stationary-phase-method.md) contribution:

$$
\boxed{I(\lambda)=f(b)\sqrt{\frac\pi{2\lambda|g''(b)|}}e^{i\lambda g(b)+i\pi\operatorname{sgn}(g''(b))/4}+O(\lambda^{-1}).}
$$
