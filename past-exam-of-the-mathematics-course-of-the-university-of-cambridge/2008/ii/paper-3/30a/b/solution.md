<h1 id="30a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use disjoint small neighborhoods of the finitely many nondegenerate stationary points $t_j$, apply the [stationary phase](../../../../../../stationary-phase-method.md) formula in each, and [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) elsewhere. The leading contributions add:

$$
\boxed{I(\lambda)=\sum_j f(t_j)\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{\lambda|g''(t_j)|}}e^{i\lambda g(t_j)+i\pi\operatorname{sgn}(g''(t_j))/4}+O(\lambda^{-1}).}
$$

Interference between these oscillatory contributions can cancel the leading coefficient at particular values of $\lambda$.
