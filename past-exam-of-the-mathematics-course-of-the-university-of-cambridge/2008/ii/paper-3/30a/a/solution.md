<h1 id="30a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Near the nondegenerate stationary point, write $g(t)=g(t_0)+g''(t_0)(t-t_0)^2/2+O((t-t_0)^3)$ and replace $f(t)$ by $f(t_0)$ to leading order. Scaling $t-t_0$ by $\lambda^{-1/2}$ reduces the local integral to the Fresnel integral, while the remaining nonstationary region contributes $O(\lambda^{-1})$. The [stationary phase](../../../../../../stationary-phase-method.md) result is

$$
\boxed{I(\lambda)=f(t_0)\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{\lambda|g''(t_0)|}}\exp\left(i\lambda g(t_0)+\frac{i\pi}4\operatorname{sgn}g''(t_0)\right)+O(\lambda^{-1}).}
$$
