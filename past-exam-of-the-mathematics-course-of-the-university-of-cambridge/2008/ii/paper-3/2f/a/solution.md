<h1 id="2f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The closed-set version of the [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md) says that if a nonempty [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) is a [countable union](../../../../../../countable-union.md) of [closed sets](../../../../../../closed-set.md), at least one of those [closed sets](../../../../../../closed-set.md) has nonempty [interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md). Equivalently, a nonempty [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) cannot be a [countable union](../../../../../../countable-union.md) of closed [nowhere dense sets](../../../../../../nowhere-dense-set.md).
