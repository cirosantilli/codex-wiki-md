<h1 id="18h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [separable algebraic element](../../../../../../separable-algebraic-element.md) has a [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) with distinct roots in an [algebraic closure](../../../../../../algebraic-closure.md). A [separable field extension](../../../../../../separable-extension.md) is an algebraic extension all of whose elements are separable. In characteristic $p$, the extension $\mathbb F_p(t)/\mathbb F_p(t^p)$ is inseparable: the minimal polynomial of $t$ is $T^p-t^p$, irreducible over the smaller field but having the single root $t$ with multiplicity $p$.

If $L$ is a [finite field](../../../../../../finite-field.md) of order $q$, every element of $L$ is a root of $T^q-T\in K[T]$, because $K$ contains its prime field. This polynomial has derivative $-1$, so it has no repeated roots. Every element's minimal polynomial divides it and is therefore separable. Hence **$L/K$ is separable**.
