[Convergence of a numerical method](../../../../../../convergence-of-a-numerical-method.md) means that, for every fixed final time $T$ and admissible initial-value problem, the maximum grid error tends to zero:

$$
\boxed{\max_{0\leq nh\leq T}|y_n-y(nh)|\longrightarrow0\quad\text{as }h\downarrow0.}
$$
