<h1 id="10e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10E](../10e.md)

Write the [photon energy density](../../../../../photon-energy-density.md) as $\epsilon=a_RT^4$, where $a_R=4\sigma/c$ by the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md). The [photon gas](../../../../../photon-gas.md) has [pressure](../../../../../pressure.md) $P=\epsilon/3$ and zero [photon chemical potential](../../../../../photon-chemical-potential.md). The [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) therefore gives

$$
TdS=d(\epsilon V)+P\,dV=4a_RT^3V\,dT+\frac43a_RT^4\,dV.
$$

Dividing by $T$ and integrating gives the [entropy density of a photon gas](../../../../../entropy-density-of-a-photon-gas.md):

$$
\boxed{S=\frac{4a_R}{3}T^3V,\qquad s=\frac SV=\frac{16\sigma}{3c}T^3.}
$$

The integration constant is fixed to zero at zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature.md), consistently with the [Third law of thermodynamics](../../../../../third-law-of-thermodynamics.md).

The inequality $\Gamma\gg H$ says interactions act much faster than the expansion time, maintaining local [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md). For adiabatic reversible expansion with no additional source of [entropy](../../../../../entropy.md), [cosmological entropy conservation](../../../../../cosmological-entropy-conservation.md) fixes the entropy in a comoving volume. Since that volume scales as $a^3$, and the relativistic degrees of freedom are fixed here, $T^3a^3$ is constant. Thus

$$
\boxed{T\propto a^{-1}.}
$$

During [radiation domination](../../../../../radiation-domination.md), the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $H^2\propto\rho\propto T^4$, and hence $\boxed{H\propto T^2}$. Fast interactions alone would not exclude an additional entropy-producing process; changing relativistic species likewise changes the simple temperature scaling.
