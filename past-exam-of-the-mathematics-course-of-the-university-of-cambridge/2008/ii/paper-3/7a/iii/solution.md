<h1 id="7a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a supercritical [pitchfork bifurcation](../../../../../../pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md), use $\boxed{x_{n+1}=x_n+\mu x_n-x_n^3}$. The symmetric stable branches $x=\pm\sqrt\mu$ appear for $\mu>0$ as the origin becomes unstable. Replacing the cubic minus sign by plus gives a subcritical [pitchfork bifurcation](../../../../../../pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md).
