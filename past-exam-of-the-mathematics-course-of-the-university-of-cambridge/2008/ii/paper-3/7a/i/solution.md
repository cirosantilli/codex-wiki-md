A representative [normal form](../../../../../../normal-form-dynamical-systems.md) for a [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) of a map is $\boxed{x_{n+1}=x_n+\mu-x_n^2}$. Two fixed points $x=\pm\sqrt\mu$ appear for $\mu>0$; near the bifurcation the positive branch is stable and the negative branch unstable.
