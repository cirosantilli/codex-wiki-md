↑ **Parent:** [25I](../25i.md)

An irreducible [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is [positive recurrent](../../../../../positive-recurrent-markov-chain.md) when the mean return time to a state is finite, and [null recurrent](../../../../../null-recurrent-state.md) when return occurs almost surely but the mean return time is infinite. For the nonexplosive chain here, whose rates are bounded, [positive recurrence](../../../../../positive-recurrent-markov-chain.md) is equivalent to existence of a normalized [stationary distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md). All rates are understood to be positive.
