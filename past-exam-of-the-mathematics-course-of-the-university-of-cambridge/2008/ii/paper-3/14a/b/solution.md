<h1 id="14a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take $H(x,y)=y^2/2+x^2/2-x^4/4$. Along a trajectory,

$$
\dot H=y(x^3-x)+(x-x^3)(y+ax-bx^3)=(x-x^3)(ax-bx^3).
$$

Integrating over a [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) returns $H$ to its starting value, and hence

$$
\boxed{\oint_\Gamma(x-x^3)(ax-bx^3)\,dt=0.}
$$

This is the [energy obstruction for a cubic planar oscillator](../../../../../../energy-obstruction-for-a-cubic-planar-oscillator.md).
