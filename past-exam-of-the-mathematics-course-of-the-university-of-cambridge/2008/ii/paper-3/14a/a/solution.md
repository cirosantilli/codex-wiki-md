<h1 id="14a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [equilibrium points](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are $(0,0)$ and $(\pm1,\pm(b-a))$, with correlated signs. The [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}a-3bx^2&1\\3x^2-1&0\end{pmatrix}$. At the origin its eigenvalues satisfy $\lambda^2-a\lambda+1=0$. It is a [focus](../../../../../../focus-dynamical-systems.md) for $0<|a|<2$, a repeated-eigenvalue node for $|a|=2$, and a [node](../../../../../../node-dynamical-systems.md) for $|a|>2$; stability is determined by the sign of $a$, with stability for $a<0$. Its [Poincaré index](../../../../../../poincare-index.md) is $+1$. At either other equilibrium the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is $-2$, so it is a [saddle point](../../../../../../saddle-point.md) with [Poincaré index](../../../../../../poincare-index.md) $-1$.
