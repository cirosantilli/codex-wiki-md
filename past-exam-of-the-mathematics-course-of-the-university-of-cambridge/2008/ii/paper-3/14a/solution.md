↑ **Parent:** [14A](../14a.md)

For a positively oriented [simple closed curve](../../../../../simple-closed-curve.md) on which the vector field is nonzero, its [Poincaré index](../../../../../poincare-index.md) is the total rotation of the vector field along that curve divided by $2\pi$, equivalently the [winding number](../../../../../winding-number.md) of its normalized direction. The [Poincaré index](../../../../../poincare-index.md) of an isolated [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) is that of a sufficiently small enclosing curve containing no other fixed point. The [index of a planar periodic orbit](../../../../../index-of-a-planar-periodic-orbit.md) is **$+1$**.
