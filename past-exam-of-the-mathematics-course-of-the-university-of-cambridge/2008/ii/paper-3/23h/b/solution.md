<h1 id="23h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [minimal surface](../../../../../../minimal-surface.md) has $H=0$, so $k_2=-k_1$ and $\boxed{K=-k_1^2\leq0}$. For a nonflat example take the [catenoid](../../../../../../catenoid.md) parametrization $X(u,v)=(\cosh v\cos u,\cosh v\sin u,v)$. Its [first fundamental form](../../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) has $E=G=\cosh^2v$ and $F=0$. Choosing the normal appropriately, its [second fundamental form](../../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) has coefficients $e=-1$, $f=0$, $g=1$. Thus its principal curvatures are $\pm\operatorname{sech}^2v$, proving $H=0$ and $\boxed{K=-\operatorname{sech}^4v<0}$.
