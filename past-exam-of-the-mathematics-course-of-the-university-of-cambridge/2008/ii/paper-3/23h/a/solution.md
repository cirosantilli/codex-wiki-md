<h1 id="23h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an oriented surface with unit normal $N$, the [Gauss map](../../../../../../gauss-map.md) sends $p$ to $N(p)\in S^2$. The [shape operator](../../../../../../shape-operator.md) is $-dN$ on the tangent plane; its eigenvalues are the [principal curvatures](../../../../../../principal-curvature.md) $k_1,k_2$. The [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is $K=k_1k_2$ and the [mean curvature](../../../../../../mean-curvature.md) is $H=(k_1+k_2)/2$. Reversing orientation reverses both principal curvatures and $H$, but preserves $K$. The [Theorema Egregium](../../../../../../theorema-egregium.md) says that [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is determined by the [first fundamental form](../../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md), so is invariant under local isometries.
