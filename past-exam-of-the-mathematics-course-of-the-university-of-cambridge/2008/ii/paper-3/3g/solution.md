<h1 id="3g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3G](../3g.md)

Suppose a [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) subset $C\subseteq F$ contains distinct points $a,b$. The distance function $d_a(x)=|x-a|$ is a [Lipschitz map](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) with constant one, by the [reverse triangle inequality](../../../../../reverse-triangle-inequality.md). Its image $d_a(C)$ is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) and contains both $0$ and $|b-a|$, so it contains the whole interval $[0,|b-a|]$. Monotonicity and the [Lipschitz](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) inequality for [Hausdorff dimension](../../../../../hausdorff-dimension.md) imply

$$
1=\dim_H[0,|b-a|]\leq\dim_H d_a(C)\leq\dim_H C\leq\dim_H F<1,
$$

a contradiction. Thus every nonempty [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) subset of $F$ is a single point: **$F$ is [totally disconnected](../../../../../totally-disconnected-space.md)**. The argument is the [Hausdorff dimension of a connected set](../../../../../hausdorff-dimension-of-a-connected-set.md) bound, and does not require $F$ to be [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md).
