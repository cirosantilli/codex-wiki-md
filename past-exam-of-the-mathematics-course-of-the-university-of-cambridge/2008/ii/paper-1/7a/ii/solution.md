<h1 id="7a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For general $k$, the same [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) gives $\dot E=-ky^2$. For $k=0.1$ the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) at the origin are $-0.05\pm i\sqrt{3.99}/2$, so it is a [stable focus](../../../../../../stable-spiral.md). At $(1,0)$ they are $(-0.1\pm\sqrt{4.01})/2$, so it remains a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md). The vector field has constant [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) $-0.1$: there are no [periodic orbits](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md), by the planar [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) criterion. Nor can the former [homoclinic orbit](../../../../../../homoclinic-orbit.md) survive, since $E$ strictly decreases along every nonconstant orbit except at isolated crossings of $y=0$.

The [stable manifold](../../../../../../stable-manifold.md) of the saddle separates escape to positive $x$ from convergence to the origin. Its two branches approach the saddle in forward time with tangent slope $(-0.1-\sqrt{4.01})/2$. The left unstable branch spirals into the origin, whereas the right unstable branch escapes. The shaded component on the origin's side of the [stable manifold](../../../../../../stable-manifold.md) in the right panel is the [basin of attraction](../../../../../../basin-of-attraction.md). The boundary itself is excluded: its trajectories converge to the saddle. This boundary is the damped [stable manifold](../../../../../../stable-manifold.md), rather than the conservative [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) contour. [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) decrease prevents bounded recurrent motion, and every bounded orbit off the saddle's [stable manifold](../../../../../../stable-manifold.md) consequently converges to the origin. The plotted manifold was obtained by integrating backwards from the saddle's stable eigendirection.
