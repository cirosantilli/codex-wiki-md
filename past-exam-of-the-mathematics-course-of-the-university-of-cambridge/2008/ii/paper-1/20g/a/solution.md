The [ideal class group](../../../../../../ideal-class-group.md) of a [number field](../../../../../../number-field.md) is the [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) of nonzero [fractional ideals](../../../../../../fractional-ideal.md) of its [ring of integers](../../../../../../ring-of-integers.md) under multiplication, modulo the subgroup of principal [fractional ideals](../../../../../../fractional-ideal.md). For degree $n$ and $r_2$ pairs of complex embeddings, the [Minkowski bound for ideal classes](../../../../../../minkowski-s-bound.md) states that each class contains an [integral ideal](../../../../../../integral-ideal.md) $I$ with

$$
N(I)\leq\left(\frac4\pi\right)^{r_2}\frac{n!}{n^n}\sqrt{|D_K|}.
$$

There are only finitely many [integral ideals](../../../../../../integral-ideal.md) of bounded [ideal norm](../../../../../../ideal-norm.md): as sublattices of the free abelian [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) of [integers](../../../../../../integer.md), they have bounded index, and finitely many subgroups have any fixed index. Thus the [ideal class group](../../../../../../ideal-class-group.md) is finite.
