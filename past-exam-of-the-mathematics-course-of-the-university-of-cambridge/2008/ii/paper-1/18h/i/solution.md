<h1 id="18h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Over a [finite field](../../../../../../finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_q$, the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is a finite extension $\mathbb F_{q^m}$, where $m$ is the least common multiple of the degrees of the distinct irreducible factors. Its [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) is cyclic of order $m$, generated by $x\mapsto x^q$.

Over $\mathbb F_2$, the [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has no linear factor: its values at zero and one are both one. The only irreducible quadratic is $x^2+x+1$; reduction modulo it gives $x$, not zero. A reducible quartic without linear factors would split into quadratics, so $f$ is irreducible. Thus its [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $\mathbb F_{16}$ and

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f/\mathbb F_2)\simeq C_4.}
$$
