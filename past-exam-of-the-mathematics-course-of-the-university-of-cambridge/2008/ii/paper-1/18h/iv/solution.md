<h1 id="18h/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Irreducibility modulo two implies irreducibility over $\mathbb Q$ by [Gauss lemma for polynomials](../../../../../../gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md), so the [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) embeds transitively in $S_4$. The [discriminant](../../../../../../discriminant.md) is $229$, hence primes two and three are unramified. The factor-degree theorem says that square-free reduction with factor degrees $d_1,\ldots,d_r$ provides an element of the [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) with cycle lengths $d_1,\ldots,d_r$. The reductions already found therefore give a 4-cycle and a 3-cycle.

The [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) order divides 24 and is divisible by four and three, so is 12 or 24. An order-12 subgroup of $S_4$ has index two and must be $A_4$: in its quotient every [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) has the same nontrivial image, since [transpositions](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) are conjugate and generate $S_4$, making the quotient map the sign. But a 4-cycle is odd. Therefore the order is 24 and

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f/\mathbb Q)=S_4.}
$$

The [discriminant](../../../../../../discriminant.md) can also be obtained from $f'=x^2(4x+3)$ and the [resultant](../../../../../../resultant.md), giving $256-27=229$.
