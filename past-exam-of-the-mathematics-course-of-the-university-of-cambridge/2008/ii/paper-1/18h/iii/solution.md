<h1 id="18h/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) over $\mathbb F_2$ is $\mathbb F_{16}$, and it contains the unique subfield $\mathbb F_4$. Over $\mathbb F_4$, Frobenius is the square of the order-four $\mathbb F_2$ Frobenius, so it has two orbits of length two on the four roots. Thus $f$ factors into two irreducible quadratics, and

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f/\mathbb F_4)\simeq C_2,}
$$

generated by $x\mapsto x^4$ on $\mathbb F_{16}$.
