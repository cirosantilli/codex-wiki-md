<h1 id="18h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Direct division gives $f=(x-1)(x^3+2x^2+2x+2)$ over $\mathbb F_3$. The cubic takes values $2,1,1$ at $0,1,2$, so is irreducible. Finite-field extensions are cyclic [Galois extensions](../../../../../../finite-galois-extension.md), and the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) degree is the least common multiple of these factor degrees, namely three. Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f/\mathbb F_3)\simeq C_3,\qquad\text{splitting field }\mathbb F_{27}.}
$$
