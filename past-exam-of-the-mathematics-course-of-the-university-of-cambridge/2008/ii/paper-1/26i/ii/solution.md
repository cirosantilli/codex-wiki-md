<h1 id="26i/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\pi$ be a [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) vector satisfying [detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md). Since each row of the [infinitesimal generator matrix](../../../../../../transition-intensity-matrix.md) sums to zero,

$$
(\pi Q)_j=\sum_i\pi_iq_{ij}=\pi_j\sum_iq_{ji}=0.
$$

Thus $\pi Q=0$, and $\pi e^{tQ}=\pi$ for all $t\geq0$. This proves that $\pi$ is an [stationary distribution](../../../../../../stationary-distribution.md). The diagonal term causes no problem because its [detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md) equation is an identity.
