↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain.md) started with distribution $\pi$ is [reversible](../../../../../../reversible-markov-chain.md) if its finite-dimensional distributions are unchanged by reversing time: for every $T>0$, $(X_t:0\leq t\leq T)$ and $(X_{T-t}:0\leq t\leq T)$ have the same law. In particular, reversibility includes [stationarity](../../../../../../stationary-process.md). The [detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md) equations are

$$
\boxed{\pi_iq_{ij}=\pi_jq_{ji}\quad(i,j\in I).}
$$

For a finite [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain.md), these equations are equivalent to reversibility with initial distribution $\pi$. Equivalently, they imply $\pi_iP_{ij}(t)=\pi_jP_{ji}(t)$ for every transition matrix $P(t)=e^{tQ}$.
