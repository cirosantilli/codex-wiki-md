<h1 id="26i/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Use the equilibrium correspondence from the preceding part. For $i\ne j$,

$$
\widehat\pi_i\widehat P_{ij}=\frac{\pi_iq_{ij}}C.
$$

Consequently the [detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md) equations for the [jump chain](../../../../../../jump-chain.md) hold exactly when those for the [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain.md) hold. The reversibility characterizations already given prove the equivalence. Both chains here are started in their respective [stationary distributions](../../../../../../stationary-distribution.md), which generally differ.
