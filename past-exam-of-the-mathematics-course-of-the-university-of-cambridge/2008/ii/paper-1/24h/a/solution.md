<h1 id="24h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is continuous and $GL(n)=\det^{-1}(\mathbb R\setminus\{0\})$ is open in $M(n)\simeq\mathbb R^{n^2}$. Every open subset inherits the ambient smooth charts, so it is a [submanifold](../../../../../../submanifold.md) and

$$
\boxed{\dim GL(n)=n^2.}
$$
