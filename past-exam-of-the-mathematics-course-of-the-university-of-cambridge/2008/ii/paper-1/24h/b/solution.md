<h1 id="24h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an invertible $A$, factor $\det(A+tB)=\det A\det(I+tA^{-1}B)$. The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md)'s permutation expansion gives $\det(I+tC)=1+t\operatorname{tr}C+O(t^2)$. Differentiating at zero yields

$$
\boxed{(d\det)_A(B)=\det A\,\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}B).}
$$

At a determinant-one matrix choose $B=A/n$; the derivative is one. Thus one is a [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md) of the smooth [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) map on $GL(n)$. The [regular level set theorem](../../../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md) makes $SL(n)$ a [submanifold](../../../../../../submanifold.md) of [codimension of a submanifold](../../../../../../codimension-of-a-submanifold.md) one, with

$$
\boxed{\dim SL(n)=n^2-1.}
$$

This includes $n=1$, when $SL(1)=\{1\}$ has dimension zero.
