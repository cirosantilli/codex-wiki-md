**False.** Attach 594 pendant vertices to one vertex of $K_{3,3}$. The [connected graph](../../../../../../connected-graph.md) still contains the nonplanar $K_{3,3}$, but has $v=600$, $e=603$, and [average degree of a graph](../../../../../../average-degree-of-a-graph.md) $2e/v=2.01$. Attaching more leaves lowers the average further toward two without making the graph planar.
