<h1 id="29c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Here the [characteristic curves](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) are straight lines $x=y+sa$, $y\cdot n=0$, along which $\dot u=c\cdot(y+sa)+d$. If $a\cdot n\ne0$, every nearby point determines a unique starting point and travel parameter:

$$
s=\frac{x\cdot n}{a\cdot n},\qquad y=x-sa.
$$

Integrating along the [Characteristic curve](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) gives

$$
\boxed{u(x)=u_0(y)+s(c\cdot y+d)+\frac{s^2}{2}\,c\cdot a.}
$$

The non-characteristic condition is exactly what permits division by $a\cdot n$ and ensures transversality to the data hyperplane. If $a\cdot n=0$, the [Characteristic curve](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) lines starting there never leave it. Moreover the prescribed data must satisfy $a\cdot\nabla_Su_0=c\cdot x+d$ on the hyperplane even to be compatible. If they do, the [Characteristic curve](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) construction still leaves off-hyperplane data undetermined; if they do not, no $C^1$ solution exists.
