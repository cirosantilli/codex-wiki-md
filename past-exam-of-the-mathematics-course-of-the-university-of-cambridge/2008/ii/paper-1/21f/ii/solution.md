<h1 id="21f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose open neighborhoods of the sphere and circle in the wedge, each enlarged by a small contractible neighborhood of the attaching point in the other piece. They [deformation retraction](../../../../../../deformation-retraction.md) to $S^2$ and $S^1$ and have contractible intersection. Van Kampen therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(S^2\vee S^1)=\pi_1(S^2)*\pi_1(S^1)=\mathbb Z.}
$$

The generator traverses the circle once; the sphere contributes no fundamental-group generator.
