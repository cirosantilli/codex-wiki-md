↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [van Kampen theorem](../../../../../../seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) says: if $X=U\cup V$ with $U,V$ open and path connected, $U\cap V$ path connected, and a base point in the intersection, then $\pi_1(X)$ is the [free product](../../../../../../free-product.md) $\pi_1(U)*\pi_1(V)$ modulo the [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) generated by identifying the two images of every element of $\pi_1(U\cap V)$. Equivalently it is the [pushout](../../../../../../pushout.md) of the two inclusion-induced fundamental-group maps.
