<h1 id="1h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1H](../1h.md)

For a [real number](../../../../../real-number.md) $\alpha$, set $\alpha_0=\alpha$ and $a_j=\lfloor\alpha_j\rfloor$. If $\alpha_j-a_j\ne0$, define $\alpha_{j+1}=(\alpha_j-a_j)^{-1}$. The resulting [simple continued fraction](../../../../../simple-continued-fraction.md) is $[a_0;a_1,a_2,\ldots]$, where $a_0$ is an [integer](../../../../../integer.md) and subsequent $a_j$ are [positive integers](../../../../../positive-integer.md). It terminates when a [complete quotient](../../../../../complete-quotient.md) is integral; otherwise its convergents tend to $\alpha$.

For the [continued fraction of the square root of nineteen](../../../../../continued-fraction-of-the-square-root-of-nineteen.md), the [complete quotients](../../../../../complete-quotient.md) after $a_0=4$ are

$$
\frac{\sqrt{19}+4}{3},\quad\frac{\sqrt{19}+2}{5},\quad\frac{\sqrt{19}+3}{2},\quad\frac{\sqrt{19}+3}{5},\quad\frac{\sqrt{19}+2}{3},\quad\sqrt{19}+4.
$$

Their [integer](../../../../../integer.md) parts are $2,1,3,1,2,8$. Taking the reciprocal after subtracting $8$ from the last quotient returns the first one, so

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{19}=[4;\overline{2,1,3,1,2,8}].}
$$
