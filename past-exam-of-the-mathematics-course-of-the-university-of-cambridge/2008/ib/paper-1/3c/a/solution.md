<h1 id="3c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a regular smooth curve $z=z_j(t)$ passing through $z_0$, the complex tangent of its image is $dw_j/dt=f'(z_0)z_j'(t)$. When $f'(z_0)\ne0$, both tangents are multiplied by the same nonzero complex number. Multiplication rescales lengths by $|f'(z_0)|$ and rotates every direction by $\arg f'(z_0)$, so the difference of tangent arguments is unchanged. Hence the [analytic function](../../../../../../space-of-holomorphic-functions.md) defines a [conformal map](../../../../../../conformal-map.md) at $z_0$: **oriented angles, and therefore ordinary angles between the curves, are preserved**. The nonzero derivative condition also ensures that the image tangents are nonzero.
