<h1 id="1e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1E](../1e.md)

For $n\geq1$, an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is a number $\lambda\in\mathbb C$ for which there is a nonzero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $v$ satisfying $Av=\lambda v$. Equivalently, $\ker(A-\lambda I)\ne\{0\}$, or $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$. The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) $\det(tI-A)$ has degree $n$ and leading coefficient one. By the [fundamental theorem of algebra](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-algebra.md) it has a complex root $\lambda$. At that root $A-\lambda I$ is a [singular matrix](../../../../../singular-matrix.md), so it has a nonzero [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) [vector](../../../../../vector.md), proving that $A$ has an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
