**False in general.** Take $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,6,6)$ and $B=\operatorname{diag}(2,2,9)$. Both have [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) $13$ and [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $36$, but their [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) multisets are different. For $n>3$, append equal identity blocks to preserve this counterexample. In dimensions one and two the assertion is true: in dimension two the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $t^2-(\operatorname{tr}A)t+\det A$, whose roots, with multiplicity, are determined by those two quantities.
