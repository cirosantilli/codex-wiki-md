**False.** Every entry of $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$ is strictly positive, yet $A(1,-1)^T=-(1,-1)^T$. Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $3$ and $-1$, so one has negative real part. Positivity of entries does not imply the [positive-definite matrix](../../../../../../positive-definite-matrix.md) property.
