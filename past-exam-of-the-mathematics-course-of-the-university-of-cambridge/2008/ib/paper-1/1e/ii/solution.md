**False.** The [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ has real [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1$ and $2$, because its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $(t-1)(t-2)$, but $A^*=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&2\end{pmatrix}\ne A$. Real spectrum alone does not imply that it is a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md).
