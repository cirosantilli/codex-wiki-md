<h1 id="1e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** If $Av=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$ and $A=A^*$, then

$$
\lambda v^*v=v^*Av=(v^*Av)^*=\bar\lambda v^*v.
$$

Since $v^*v>0$, $\lambda=\bar\lambda$. Thus every [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) is real.
