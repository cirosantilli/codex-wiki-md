<h1 id="17b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Without [surface tension](../../../../../../surface-tension.md), the exact [dynamic boundary condition for an inviscid interface](../../../../../../dynamic-boundary-condition-for-an-inviscid-interface.md) is equality of pressure, $p_1=p_2$, at $z=\zeta$. The [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md) in each irrotational fluid is

$$
\partial_t\Phi_i+\tfrac12|\nabla\Phi_i|^2+p_i/\rho_i+gz=C_i(t).
$$

Choose the potential gauge so that $C_i=p_0/\rho_i+U_i^2/2$, its unperturbed value. Expanding the kinetic term as $U_i^2/2+U_i\partial_x\varphi_i$ and evaluating gravity at the displaced interface gives

$$
p_i|_{z=\zeta}=p_0-\rho_i[\partial_t\varphi_i+U_i\partial_x\varphi_i+g\zeta]
$$

to first order. Equality of these pressures gives

$$
\boxed{\rho_1[(\partial_t+U_1\partial_x)\varphi_1+g\zeta]=\rho_2[(\partial_t+U_2\partial_x)\varphi_2+g\zeta]\qquad(z=0).}
$$
