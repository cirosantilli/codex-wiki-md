<h1 id="16b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Charge conservation gives the [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) $\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf J=0$. Since $\mathbf J$ is independent of time, so is $\partial_t\rho=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf J$. Integrating at each fixed position gives

$$
\boxed{\rho(\mathbf r,t)=\rho(\mathbf r,0)-t\nabla\cdot\mathbf J(\mathbf r)=\rho(\mathbf r,0)+t\dot\rho(\mathbf r,0).}
$$
