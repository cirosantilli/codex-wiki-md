<h1 id="16b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $s=|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|$ and introduce the [magnetic vector potential](../../../../../../magnetic-vector-potential.md) $\mathbf A(\mathbf r)=\mu_0(4\pi)^{-1}\int\mathbf J(\mathbf r')/s\,dV'$. Differentiation is with respect to $\mathbf r$, so $\mathbf J(\mathbf r')$ is constant under it. Hence

$$
\nabla\times\frac{\mathbf J(\mathbf r')}{s}=\nabla(s^{-1})\times\mathbf J(\mathbf r')=-\frac{\mathbf r-\mathbf r'}{s^3}\times\mathbf J(\mathbf r')=\frac{\mathbf J(\mathbf r')\times(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')}{s^3}.
$$

Taking the curl through the integral reproduces the [Biot-Savart law](../../../../../../biot-savart-law.md), proving $\boxed{\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A}$ and the requested representation.
