A [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) on $X$ is a family $\mathcal T$ of subsets containing $\varnothing$ and $X$, closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections. Its members are the [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md). A [Hausdorff space](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) separates each pair of distinct points by disjoint open neighbourhoods; a [compact space](../../../../../compact-space.md) is one for which every [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) has a finite subcover.
