<h1 id="10g/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For each [involution](../../../../../../involution.md) $t\in G$, its [conjugacy class](../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) has size fifteen, so its [centralizer](../../../../../../centralizer.md) $C_G(t)$ has order four. A group of order four is a [cyclic group](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) or a [Klein four-group](../../../../../../klein-four-group.md); the cyclic possibility would contain an element of order four, excluded by the geometric enumeration. Hence each $C_G(t)$ is a [Klein four-group](../../../../../../klein-four-group.md) containing three [involutions](../../../../../../involution.md).

If $u$ is any of the three [involutions](../../../../../../involution.md) in $C_G(t)$, the latter group is an [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md) and hence is contained in $C_G(u)$. Both [centralizers](../../../../../../centralizer.md) have order four, so they are equal. It follows that the fifteen [involutions](../../../../../../involution.md) partition into five triples, with five distinct [centralizers](../../../../../../centralizer.md). Conjugation by $G$ permutes these five subgroups and gives a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $\theta:G\to S_5$. This action is nontrivial: otherwise each of these order-four subgroups would be a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md), contrary to simplicity. The [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $\theta$ is normal; since $G$ is simple and the action nontrivial, its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is trivial. Thus $\theta$ is injective.

The sign of $\theta(g)$ defines a homomorphism $G\to\{\pm1\}$. If it were nontrivial, simplicity would make it injective, impossible for a group of order sixty mapping into one of order two. Hence every image permutation is even, and $\theta(G)\subseteq A_5$. Both groups have order sixty, so they are equal. This constructs the requested [group isomorphism](../../../../../../group-isomorphism.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Rot}(D)\cong A_5.}
$$
