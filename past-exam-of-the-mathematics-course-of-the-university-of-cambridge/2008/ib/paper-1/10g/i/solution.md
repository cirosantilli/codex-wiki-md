The [alternating group](../../../../../../alternating-group.md) $A_4$ contains

$$
V=\{1,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}.
$$

The product of any two different nonidentity elements is the third, and each squares to the identity, so $V$ is a [Klein four-group](../../../../../../klein-four-group.md). Conjugation by a permutation replaces the labels in each cycle, preserving the property of being a product of two disjoint transpositions. Thus $V$ is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) of $A_4$. Its order is $4$, strictly between $1$ and $|A_4|=12$. **$A_4$ is not simple.**
