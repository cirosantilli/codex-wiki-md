<h1 id="8b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because the velocity is independent of $z$, the vortex-stretching term is $(\boldsymbol\omega\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=\omega_z\partial_z\mathbf u=0$. Substituting $\omega_z=-\nabla^2\psi$, $u=\psi_y$ and $v=-\psi_x$ into the [vorticity equation](../../../../../../vorticity-equation.md) and cancelling a minus sign gives

$$
\boxed{\partial_t(\nabla^2\psi)+\psi_y\partial_x(\nabla^2\psi)
-\psi_x\partial_y(\nabla^2\psi)=0.}
$$

The planar [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) is transported by the flow without vortex stretching under these inviscid assumptions.
