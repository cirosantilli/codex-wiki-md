<h1 id="8b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the stated incompressible planar flow, use the [stream function](../../../../../../stream-function.md) convention $u=\psi_y$, $v=-\psi_x$. It gives $u_x+v_y=0$ and the [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) is

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf u=(0,0,v_x-u_y)
=(0,0,-\psi_{xx}-\psi_{yy}).
$$

Thus $\boxed{\boldsymbol\omega=-\nabla^2\psi\,\hat{\mathbf z}}$, where the Laplacian is the two-dimensional one.
