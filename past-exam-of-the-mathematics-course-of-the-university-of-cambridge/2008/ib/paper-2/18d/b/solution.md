<h1 id="18d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) preserves the Euclidean norm: $\|Qz\|^2=z^TQ^TQz=z^Tz$. For every candidate $x$,

$$
\boxed{\|QAx-Qb\|^2=\|Q(Ax-b)\|^2=\|Ax-b\|^2.}
$$

The two objective functions agree pointwise, so their minimizers coincide, whether or not the minimizer is unique.
