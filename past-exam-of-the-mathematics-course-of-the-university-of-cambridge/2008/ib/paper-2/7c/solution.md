<h1 id="7c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7C](../7c.md)

Choose the incident photon along the $x$ direction. Conservation of [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) preserves the squared total energy invariant $\mathcal S=E_{\rm tot}^2-c^2|\mathbf p_{\rm tot}|^2$. Initially,

$$
\mathcal S=(mc^2+E)^2-E^2=m^2c^4+2mc^2E.
$$

In the centre-of-momentum frame let the outgoing photon energy be $e_*>0$; the larger particle has opposite momentum magnitude $e_*/c$ and energy $\sqrt{M^2c^4+e_*^2}$. Thus

$$
\sqrt{\mathcal S}=e_*+\sqrt{M^2c^4+e_*^2}>Mc^2.
$$

Conversely, whenever $\mathcal S>M^2c^4$, the positive value $e_*=(\mathcal S-M^2c^4)/(2\sqrt{\mathcal S})$ constructs a final state with the required invariant, which can be boosted to the laboratory frame. The limiting [photon excitation threshold with a soft final photon](../../../../../photon-excitation-threshold-with-a-soft-final-photon.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm th}=\frac{M^2-m^2}{2m}c^2.}
$$

Strictly, an outgoing physical photon requires $E>E_{\rm th}$; the printed minimum is the threshold infimum approached as the final photon becomes soft.
