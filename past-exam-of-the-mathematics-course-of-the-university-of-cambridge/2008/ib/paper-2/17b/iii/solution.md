<h1 id="17b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

[Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) with the computed [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) gives $m\dot v=-(B^2\ell^2/R)v$. Separating variables and imposing $v(0)=v_0$ yields

$$
\boxed{v(t)=v_0e^{-t/\tau},\qquad \tau=\frac{mR}{B^2\ell^2}.}
$$

The velocity vector is this scalar times $\hat{\mathbf x}$, and the characteristic decay time is positive for the stated parameters.
