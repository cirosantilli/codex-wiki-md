<h1 id="17b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The current vector along the bar points downward. Integrating the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) along its length gives

$$
\mathbf F=I(-\ell\hat{\mathbf y})\times(B\hat{\mathbf z})
=-IB\ell\hat{\mathbf x}
=\boxed{-\frac{B^2\ell^2v}{R}\hat{\mathbf x}}.
$$

It opposes the motion, as required by [electromagnetic braking of a sliding rod](../../../../../../electromagnetic-braking-of-a-sliding-rod.md).
