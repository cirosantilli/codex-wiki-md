<h1 id="17b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For these [plane waves](../../../../../../plane-wave.md), the vacuum [Faraday law](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) reads

$$
ik\mathbf e_z\times\mathbf E_0=i\omega\mathbf B_0.
$$

Cancel the nonzero factor $i\omega$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B_0=\frac k\omega\mathbf e_z\times\mathbf E_0.}
$$

For positive-frequency propagation in the positive $z$ direction, $k/\omega=1/c$.
