<h1 id="17b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Vacuum [Maxwell's equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations.md) are invariant under spatial rotations. Rotate the preceding coordinate system so that $\mathbf e_z$ becomes the propagation direction $\widehat{\mathbf k}$, and rotate the transverse electric direction to a unit [polarization vector](../../../../../../polarization-vector.md) $\widehat{\mathbf n}$. Dot and cross products transform covariantly under this rotation, so transversality and the field relation are unchanged. With $\omega=c|\mathbf k|$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E(\mathbf r,t)=E_0e^{i(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r-\omega t)}\widehat{\mathbf n},\qquad
\mathbf B(\mathbf r,t)=\frac{E_0}{c}e^{i(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r-\omega t)}(\widehat{\mathbf k}\times\widehat{\mathbf n}),\qquad\widehat{\mathbf k}\cdot\widehat{\mathbf n}=0.}
$$

These complex fields encode real fields through their real parts. The displayed form describes linear [electromagnetic polarization](../../../../../../polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md); a general transverse complex amplitude can describe elliptical or circular [electromagnetic polarization](../../../../../../polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) as well.
