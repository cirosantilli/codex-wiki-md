<h1 id="17b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Substitute the monochromatic [plane waves](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) into the two divergence equations of [Maxwell's equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations.md). They become $ik E_{0z}=0$ and $ik B_{0z}=0$. For nonzero wavenumber,

$$
\boxed{E_{0z}=B_{0z}=0.}
$$

Thus both fields are transverse to the direction of propagation. The wave equation also gives the vacuum [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) $\omega^2=c^2k^2$.
