<h1 id="17b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the curl of the vacuum [Faraday law](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) and use the vacuum [Ampere-Maxwell law](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md):

$$
\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf E)=-\partial_t(\nabla\times\mathbf B)=-\mu_0\varepsilon_0\partial_t^2\mathbf E.
$$

Since the electric divergence vanishes, the double-curl identity makes the left side $-\Delta\mathbf E$. Therefore the [electromagnetic wave equation](../../../../../../electromagnetic-wave-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Delta\mathbf E-\frac1{c^2}\partial_t^2\mathbf E=0,\qquad c=\frac1{\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}}.}
$$

The same calculation interchanging the fields gives the wave equation for $\mathbf B$.
