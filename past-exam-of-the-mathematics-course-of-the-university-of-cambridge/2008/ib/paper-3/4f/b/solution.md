<h1 id="4f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The set is a [rational comb with a connecting spine](../../../../../../rational-comb-with-a-connecting-spine.md): it contains the whole vertical segment $x=0$, and every horizontal segment at rational height. Any point with $x>0$ has rational height and can move horizontally to the vertical segment without leaving the set. It can then move vertically to $(0,0)$. Points already on the vertical segment use only the second step. Concatenating these polygonal paths connects any two points. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\text{the subspace is path-connected, hence connected}.}
$$

No claim about a horizontal segment at irrational height is needed: at such a height, the only point in the set lies on the connecting spine.
