<h1 id="18b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The rigid-rotation [velocity field](../../../../../../velocity-field.md) is $\mathbf v=(-\Omega y,\Omega x,0)$, with spatially uniform $\Omega(t)$. Its divergence is zero, since $\partial_x(-\Omega y)=\partial_y(\Omega x)=0$. It therefore satisfies the incompressible [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md). On the cylindrical wall, $\mathbf v\cdot(x,y,0)=0$, so it has zero normal [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md); on either end cap its vertical component is zero. These are the impermeability [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) for an [inviscid flow](../../../../../../inviscid-flow.md). Tangential slip is permitted; a no-slip condition is not part of this inviscid model.
