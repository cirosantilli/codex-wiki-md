<h1 id="18b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At height $z$, $\mathbf r\times\mathbf v=(-\Omega zx,-\Omega zy,\Omega(x^2+y^2))$. Integrating over a circular cross-section cancels the horizontal components by symmetry. Thus the [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) per unit length is

$$
\mathbf M=\rho\Omega\mathbf e_z\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^ar^2r\,dr\,d\theta
=\boxed{\frac{\pi\rho a^4\Omega}{2}\mathbf e_z.}
$$

The coefficient is the polar moment of inertia per unit length of the liquid column.
