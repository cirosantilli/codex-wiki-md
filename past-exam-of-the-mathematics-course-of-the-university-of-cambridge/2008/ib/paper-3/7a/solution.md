<h1 id="7a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7A](../7a.md)

In the position representation, the [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) operator is

$$
\boxed{\widehat{\mathbf L}=\mathbf r\times\widehat{\mathbf p}=-i\hbar\,\mathbf r\times\nabla,\qquad L_i=\epsilon_{iab}x_ap_b.}
$$

On a common smooth domain use $[x_a,p_b]=i\hbar\delta_{ab}$, $[x_a,x_b]=[p_a,p_b]=0$. Expanding the product commutator gives

$$
[x_ap_b,x_cp_d]=-i\hbar\delta_{bc}x_ap_d+i\hbar\delta_{ad}x_cp_b.
$$

Contracting with the two alternating tensors gives the [orbital angular momentum commutation relations](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum-commutation-relations.md):

$$
\boxed{[L_i,L_j]=i\hbar(x_ip_j-x_jp_i)=i\hbar\epsilon_{ijk}L_k.}
$$

For $L_\pm=L_1\pm iL_2$, these imply $[L_3,L_\pm]=\pm\hbar L_\pm$. Therefore $L_3\psi=0$ yields

$$
L_3(L_\pm\psi)=L_\pm L_3\psi+[L_3,L_\pm]\psi=\pm\hbar L_\pm\psi.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{L_\pm\psi\text{ has eigenvalue }\pm\hbar\text{ whenever }L_\pm\psi\ne0.}
$$

The nonzero qualification is necessary: a rotationally invariant wavefunction is annihilated by all three components, and the zero vector is not an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md).
