<h1 id="2g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2G](../2g.md)

Set $D=1+u^2+v^2$. The two tangent vectors to the [Enneper surface](../../../../../enneper-surface.md) are

$$
\sigma_u=(1-u^2+v^2,2uv,2u),\qquad\sigma_v=(2uv,1-v^2+u^2,-2v).
$$

Their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is $D(-2u,2v,1-u^2-v^2)$. Since $4u^2+4v^2+(1-u^2-v^2)^2=D^2$, its length is $D^2>0$, proving regularity and giving the [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n=\frac1D(-2u,2v,1-u^2-v^2).}
$$

For the [first fundamental form](../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md), direct dot products give $E=G=D^2$ and $F=0$. The second derivatives are $\sigma_{uu}=(-2u,2v,2)$, $\sigma_{uv}=(2v,2u,0)$, and $\sigma_{vv}=(2u,-2v,-2)$. Taking their dot products with $\mathbf n$ gives the [second fundamental form](../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) coefficients $e=2$, $f=0$, $g=-2$. Therefore the [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is

$$
\boxed{K=\frac{eg-f^2}{EG-F^2}=-\frac4{(1+u^2+v^2)^4}.}
$$

Here curvature means [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md); the opposite signs of the second-form diagonal entries also give zero [mean curvature](../../../../../mean-curvature.md), as expected for this [minimal surface](../../../../../minimal-surface.md).
