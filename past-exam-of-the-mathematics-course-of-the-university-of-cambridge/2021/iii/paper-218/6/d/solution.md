<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The sample autocorrelation has one substantial positive spike at lag one and then cuts off, while the partial autocorrelation tails off with alternating signs. This is the characteristic pattern of a [moving-average process of order one](../../../../../../moving-average-process-of-order-one.md).
