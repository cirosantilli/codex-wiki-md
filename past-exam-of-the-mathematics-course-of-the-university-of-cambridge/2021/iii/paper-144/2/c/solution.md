<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose $b\in N^m$ and an $L$-formula $\varphi(\bar x;y)$ such that

$$
X=\{\bar a\in N^n:N\models\varphi(\bar a;b)\}.
$$

Let $p(y)=\operatorname{tp}(b/M)$. Stability and the [Fundamental theorem of stability](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-stability.md) make this a [definable type](../../../../../../definable-type.md). Apply its definition to the formula $\psi(y;\bar x)=\varphi(\bar x;y)$. There is an $M$-formula $d_p\psi(\bar x)$ such that, for every $\bar a\in M^n$,

$$
N\models\varphi(\bar a;b)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\psi(y;\bar a)\in p
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
M\models d_p\psi(\bar a).
$$

**Thus $d_p\psi$ defines $X\cap M^n$ in $M$, which is the [stable trace of a definable set](../../../../../../stable-trace-of-a-definable-set.md) property.**
