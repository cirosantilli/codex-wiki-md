<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Setting $\alpha=0$ gives the [Upper-convected Maxwell model](../../../../../../upper-convected-maxwell-model.md)

$$
\boldsymbol\tau+\lambda
\overset{\triangledown}{\boldsymbol\tau}
=\eta\dot{\boldsymbol\gamma}.
$$

In steady incompressible uniaxial extension of rate $\dot\epsilon$, its tensile and transverse stresses give

$$
\eta_E
=\frac{\tau_{zz}-\tau_{xx}}{\dot\epsilon}
=\frac{2\eta}{1-2\lambda\dot\epsilon}
+\frac{\eta}{1+\lambda\dot\epsilon}.
$$

The [extensional viscosity](../../../../../../extensional-viscosity.md) rises above the [Trouton ratio](../../../../../../trouton-ratio.md) value $3\eta$ and diverges at $\lambda\dot\epsilon=1/2$, the ideal model's extensional catastrophe. An extensional rheometer can locate that rapid growth and estimate $\lambda\simeq1/(2\dot\epsilon_c)$. More robustly, impose a small deformation, stop the flow, and fit the exponential stress decay $\tau\propto e^{-t/\lambda}$.
