↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [generalized Newtonian fluid](../../../../../../generalized-newtonian-fluid.md) responds instantaneously with stress determined only by the current scalar [shear rate](../../../../../../shear-rate.md). It therefore cannot represent [viscoelasticity](../../../../../../viscoelasticity.md), including stress relaxation, elastic recoil, memory, and rate-dependent normal stresses. It also cannot represent [thixotropy](../../../../../../thixotropy.md), in which microstructure and viscosity evolve with the duration and history of shearing. Other omitted effects include hysteresis and genuine finite-time yielding.
