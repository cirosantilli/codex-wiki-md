<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Curves $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n$ in minimal position [fill a surface](../../../../../../filling-set-of-curves.md) $S$ when every essential simple closed curve has positive geometric intersection with at least one $\alpha_i$. On a closed surface this is equivalent to every component of

$$
S\setminus\bigcup_i\alpha_i
$$

being a disc: a non-disc complementary component contains an essential curve, while any curve disjoint from the collection lies in such a component.
