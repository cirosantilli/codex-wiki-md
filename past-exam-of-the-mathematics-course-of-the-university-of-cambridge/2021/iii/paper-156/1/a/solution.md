<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A sufficient condition is that both collections are [Alexander systems](../../../../../../alexander-system.md): within each collection the essential simple closed curves are pairwise nonisotopic, are in pairwise [minimal position of curves or arcs](../../../../../../minimal-position-of-curves-or-arcs.md), have no triple intersection points, and no three curves intersect pairwise. If $\alpha_i$ is isotopic to $\beta_i$ for every $i$, the simultaneous-isotopy lemma for Alexander systems gives an ambient isotopy $H_t$ with $H_1(\alpha_i)=\beta_i$ for all $i$. Successive applications of the [bigon criterion](../../../../../../bigon-criterion.md) prove the lemma while preserving the curves already matched.
