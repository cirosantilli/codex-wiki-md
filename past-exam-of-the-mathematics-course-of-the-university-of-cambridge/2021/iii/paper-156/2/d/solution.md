<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the three-punctured sphere $S_{0,3}$. Its full mapping class group is finite, isomorphic to the permutation group $S_3$, whereas

$$
\pi_1(S_{0,3})\cong F_2
$$

and $\operatorname{Out}(F_2)$ is infinite by part c. Hence the finite subgroup $p(\operatorname{Mod}(S_{0,3}))$ has infinite index in $\operatorname{Out}(\pi_1(S_{0,3}))$.
