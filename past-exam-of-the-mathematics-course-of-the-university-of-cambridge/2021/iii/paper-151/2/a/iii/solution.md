<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An element of the [p-adic integers](../../../../../../../p-adic-integer.md) is a [unit in a ring](../../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) exactly when its reduction modulo $p$ is nonzero. More explicitly, if $\alpha\not\equiv0\pmod p$, its inverses modulo $p^n$ are unique and compatible, so they define $\beta\in\mathbb Z_p$ with $\alpha\beta=1$. The converse follows by reducing $\alpha\beta=1$ modulo $p$. Part ii therefore proves that $\alpha$ topologically generates the additive group if and only if $\alpha$ is a [p-adic unit](../../../../../../../p-adic-unit.md).
