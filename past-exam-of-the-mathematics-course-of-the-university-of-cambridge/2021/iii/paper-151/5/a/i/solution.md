<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a normalized set-theoretic section $s:H\to E$ of $\pi$, so $s(1)=1$. Define the [extension cocycle](../../../../../../../extension-cocycle.md)

$$
\phi(h,k)=s(h)s(k)s(hk)^{-1}\in M.
$$

Associativity of $s(h)s(k)s(l)$ gives, in multiplicative notation for $M$,

$$
\phi(h,k)\phi(hk,l)
=(h\cdot\phi(k,l))\phi(h,kl),
$$

which is exactly the [two-cocycle](../../../../../../../two-cocycle.md) identity. Thus $\phi$ represents the class of the [group extension](../../../../../../../group-extension.md) in $H^2(H,M)$.
