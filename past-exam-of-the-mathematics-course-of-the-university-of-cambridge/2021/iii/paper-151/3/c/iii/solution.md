<h1 id="3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $m$ is coprime to every $|G_j|$, the [power-map criterion for a finite group](../../../../../../../power-map-criterion-for-a-finite-group.md) says that every finite-quotient power map is bijective. Part ii gives surjectivity of $f_m:G\to G$. If $x^m=y^m$, then $p_j(x)=p_j(y)$ for every $j$ by injectivity in $G_j$, and hence $x=y$. Thus the continuous power map is bijective.
