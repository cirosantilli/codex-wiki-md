<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By part ii, $G_n$ is the intersection of finitely many open subgroups, so it is open. Conjugation permutes the subgroups of each index, hence $G_n$ is also a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md). The groups $G_n$ form a descending family. If $U$ is any open normal subgroup and $[G:U]=m$, then $G_m\subseteq U$. Thus $(G_n)$ is cofinal among the open normal neighborhoods of the identity. Part i now gives

$$
\boxed{G\cong\varprojlim_nG/G_n}.
$$
