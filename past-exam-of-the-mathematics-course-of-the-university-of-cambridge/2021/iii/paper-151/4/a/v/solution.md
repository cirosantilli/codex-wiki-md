<h1 id="4/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For the trivial action, crossed homomorphisms $G\to\mathbb Q$ are ordinary [group homomorphisms](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md), while principal crossed homomorphisms vanish. The image of a finite group in the torsion-free additive group $\mathbb Q$ must be trivial, so

$$
\boxed{H^1(G,\mathbb Q)=0}.
$$

Degree-zero cohomology is the [invariant submodule](../../../../../../../invariant-submodule.md); the action is trivial, so

$$
\boxed{H^0(G,\mathbb Q)=\mathbb Q}.
$$
