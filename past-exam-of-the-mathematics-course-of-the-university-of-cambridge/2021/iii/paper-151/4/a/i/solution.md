↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The inhomogeneous [group cochain](../../../../../../../group-cochain.md) group is

$$
C^r(G,M)=\{f:G^r\to M\},
$$

with $C^0(G,M)=M$. Its [group coboundary](../../../../../../../group-coboundary.md) $d:C^r(G,M)\to C^{r+1}(G,M)$ is

$$
(df)(g_1,\ldots,g_{r+1})
=g_1f(g_2,\ldots,g_{r+1})
+\sum_{i=1}^{r}(-1)^if(g_1,\ldots,g_ig_{i+1},\ldots,g_{r+1})
+(-1)^{r+1}f(g_1,\ldots,g_r).
$$

One checks that $d^2=0$, and [group cohomology](../../../../../../../group-cohomology.md) is $H^r(G,M)=\ker d/\operatorname{im}d$.
