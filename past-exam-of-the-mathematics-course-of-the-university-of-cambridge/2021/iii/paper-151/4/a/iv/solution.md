<h1 id="4/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Let $\phi\in Z^2(G,\mathbb Q)$. Part iii gives $d\psi=|G|\phi$. Since division by $|G|$ is possible in the [rational numbers](../../../../../../../rational-number.md),

$$
\phi=d\left(\frac{\psi}{|G|}\right)
$$

is a [two-coboundary](../../../../../../../group-coboundary.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{H^2(G,\mathbb Q)=0}.
$$

This is a degree-two instance of the vanishing of finite-group cohomology when the group order is invertible on the coefficient module.
