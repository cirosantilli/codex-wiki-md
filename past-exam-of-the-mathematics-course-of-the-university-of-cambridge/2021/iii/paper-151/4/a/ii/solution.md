<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [crossed homomorphism](../../../../../../../crossed-homomorphism.md) is a map $\phi:G\to M$ satisfying

$$
\phi(gh)=\phi(g)+g\phi(h).
$$

For a one-cochain, the formula in part i gives

$$
(d\phi)(g,h)=g\phi(h)-\phi(gh)+\phi(g),
$$

so the crossed homomorphisms are exactly the [one-cocycles](../../../../../../../one-cocycle.md). A zero-cochain $m\in M$ has coboundary $g\mapsto gm-m$, the [principal crossed homomorphism](../../../../../../../principal-crossed-homomorphism.md) associated with $m$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{H^1(G,M)=\frac{\{\text{crossed homomorphisms }G\to M\}}
{\{g\mapsto gm-m:m\in M\}}}.
$$
