For an [inverse system](../../../../../../../inverse-system.md) of sets $(X_j,f_{ij})$ indexed by a [directed set](../../../../../../../directed-set.md) $J$, the [inverse limit](../../../../../../../inverse-limit.md) is the set of compatible tuples

$$
\varprojlim_{j\in J}X_j
=\left\{(x_j)\in\prod_{j\in J}X_j:f_{ij}(x_j)=x_i\text{ whenever }i\leq j\right\}.
$$

Its projection to $X_j$ sends $(x_i)_i$ to $x_j$.
