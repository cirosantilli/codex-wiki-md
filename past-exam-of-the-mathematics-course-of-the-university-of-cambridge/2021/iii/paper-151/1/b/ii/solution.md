↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For fixed $h\in G$, the [conjugation](../../../../../../../conjugation.md) map is the composite

$$
G\longrightarrow G\times G\times G,
\qquad g\longmapsto(g,h,g^{-1}),
$$

followed by multiplication. Inversion, constant maps, diagonal maps, and multiplication are continuous in a [topological group](../../../../../../../topological-group-split.md), so $c_h(g)=ghg^{-1}$ is continuous.
