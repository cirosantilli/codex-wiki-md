<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [profinite group](../../../../../../../profinite-group.md) $G$ is [compact](../../../../../../../compact-space.md), and the preceding part shows that

$$
\operatorname{Cl}_G(h)=c_h(G)
$$

is its image. By the [continuous image of a compact space](../../../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md) theorem, the [conjugacy class](../../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) is compact. Since a profinite group is [Hausdorff](../../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md), every compact subset is closed, so $\operatorname{Cl}_G(h)$ is closed.
