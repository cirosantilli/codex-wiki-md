↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The product $\prod_jG_j$ of the [finite groups](../../../../../../../finite-group.md) with their [discrete topology](../../../../../../../discrete-space.md) is a [topological group](../../../../../../../topological-group-split.md) under coordinatewise multiplication and inversion. The compatibility equations defining $G=\varprojlim_jG_j$ are preserved by both operations, so $G$ is a subgroup. Their restrictions to the [subspace topology](../../../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $G$ are continuous. Hence $G$ with its standard [inverse-limit topology](../../../../../../../inverse-limit-topology.md) is a topological group.
