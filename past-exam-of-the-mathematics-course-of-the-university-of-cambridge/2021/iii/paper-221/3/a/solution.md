↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A distribution is [faithful](../../../../../../faithfulness-of-a-directed-acyclic-graph.md) to a [Directed acyclic graph](../../../../../../directed-acyclic-graph.md) $G$ when every [conditional independence](../../../../../../conditional-independence.md) in the distribution is implied by [D-separation](../../../../../../d-separation.md) in $G$. Together with the graphical Markov property, faithfulness makes conditional independence equivalent to D-separation and rules out independences caused only by exact parameter cancellation.
