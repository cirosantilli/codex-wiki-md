↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [backdoor path](../../../../../../backdoor-path.md) from $A$ to $Y$ is a path whose first edge has an arrowhead at $A$. The set $X_I$ satisfies the [backdoor criterion](../../../../../../backdoor-adjustment-set.md) when it contains no descendant of $A$ and blocks every backdoor path from $A$ to $Y$. Under this criterion, consistency, and positivity,

$$
Y(a)\mathrel\perp A\mid X_I,
$$

so adjustment for $X_I$ identifies the intervention mean.
