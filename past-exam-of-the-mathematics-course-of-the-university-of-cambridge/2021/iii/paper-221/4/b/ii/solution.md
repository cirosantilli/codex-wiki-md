<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A sufficient causal condition is [conditional exchangeability](../../../../../../../conditional-exchangeability.md)

$$
\{Y(0),Y(1)\}\mathrel\perp A\mid X,
$$

together with [consistency of potential outcomes](../../../../../../../consistency-in-causal-inference.md) and [positivity in causal inference](../../../../../../../positivity-assumption.md). For the ordinary-least-squares coefficient itself to equal one common causal effect, also require the correctly specified additive conditional-mean model

$$
\mathbb E[Y(a)\mid X]=\theta_0+\theta^TX+\beta a.
$$

Then $\beta$ is the [homogeneous treatment effect](../../../../../../../homogeneous-treatment-effect.md), and the adjusted coefficient consistently estimates both conditional effects and the [average treatment effect](../../../../../../../average-treatment-effect.md) in the analyzed population.
