A compact [real tree](../../../../../../real-tree.md) is a compact [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) $(T,d)$ such that any $x,y\in T$ are joined by a unique arc, and that arc is [isometric](../../../../../../isometry.md) to $[0,d(x,y)]$. The [multiplicity of a point in a real tree](../../../../../../multiplicity-of-a-point-in-a-real-tree.md) $a\in T$ is the number of [connected components](../../../../../../connected-component.md) of $T\setminus\{a\}$.
