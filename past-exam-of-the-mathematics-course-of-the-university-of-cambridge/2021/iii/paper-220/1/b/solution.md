<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $s,t\in[0,1]$, define

$$
m_g(s,t)=\inf_{r\in[s\wedge t,s\vee t]}g(r),
\qquad
d_g(s,t)=g(s)+g(t)-2m_g(s,t).
$$

The function $d_g$ is a [pseudometric](../../../../../../pseudometric.md). Declare $s\sim t$ when $d_g(s,t)=0$, and give the [quotient set](../../../../../../quotient-set.md) $T_g=[0,1]/\!\sim$ the induced metric, again denoted $d_g$. This is the [real tree encoded by an excursion](../../../../../../real-tree-encoded-by-an-excursion.md) $g$.
