<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**False.** Join, at one common endpoint $o$, a [line segment](../../../../../../line-segment.md) of length $1/n$ for every positive [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) $n$, and use the intrinsic [path metric](../../../../../../path-metric.md). This is a [real tree](../../../../../../real-tree.md). It is [totally bounded](../../../../../../totally-bounded-space.md), because outside the first finitely many arms every point lies arbitrarily close to $o$, and it is [complete](../../../../../../completeness.md); hence it is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md). Removing $o$ leaves one [connected component](../../../../../../connected-component.md) for every arm, so $o$ has countably infinite [multiplicity of a point in a real tree](../../../../../../multiplicity-of-a-point-in-a-real-tree.md).
