↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**False.** Excursion coding does not remember the speed of traversal. Let $f$ be any nonzero coding function and let $\phi:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ be a nonidentity increasing [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md). Set $g=f\circ\phi$. Then

$$
m_g(s,t)=m_f(\phi(s),\phi(t)),
\qquad
d_g(s,t)=d_f(\phi(s),\phi(t)).
$$

**Thus $[s]\mapsto[\phi(s)]$ induces an [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md) $T_g\to T_f$, although generally $g\ne f$.**
