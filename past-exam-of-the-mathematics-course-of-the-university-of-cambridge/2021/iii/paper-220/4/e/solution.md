<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Choose a [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) of $\mathbb H$ that fixes $0$ and exchanges $x$ with $\infty$. By [Conformal invariance of SLE](../../../../../../conformal-invariance-of-sle.md), it transforms an $\operatorname{SLE}_6$ from $0$ to $x$ into one from $0$ to $\infty$. Until $x$ is disconnected from infinity, the discrepancy between the two target domains lies beyond the component visible from the growing tip. The [Locality property of SLE](../../../../../../locality-property-of-sle.md) therefore makes the two initial curve laws identical up to that disconnection time. Hence an $\operatorname{SLE}_6$ from $0$ to $x$, stopped at $\tau_x$, has the law of an $\operatorname{SLE}_6$ from $0$ to $\infty$ stopped when it disconnects $x$ from infinity.
