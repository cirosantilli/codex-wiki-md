**False.** Let

$$
A_n=[-n,n]\times(0,n^{-2}].
$$

After filling bounded complementary components if necessary, this is a [compact H-hull](../../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md) with diameter asymptotic to $2n$. The [half-plane capacity of a low rectangle](../../../../../../../half-plane-capacity-of-a-low-rectangle.md) estimate, together with [scaling and translation of half-plane capacity](../../../../../../../scaling-and-translation-of-half-plane-capacity.md), gives

$$
\operatorname{hcap}(A_n)=O(n^{-1})\longrightarrow0.
$$

**Thus unbounded diameter need not force unbounded capacity.**
