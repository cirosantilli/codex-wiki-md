↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** The standard estimate [half-plane capacity is bounded by squared diameter](../../../../../../../half-plane-capacity-is-bounded-by-squared-diameter.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{hcap}(A_n)\leq C\operatorname{diam}(A_n)^2.
$$

**Consequently $\operatorname{hcap}(A_n)\to\infty$ forces $\operatorname{diam}(A_n)\to\infty$.**
