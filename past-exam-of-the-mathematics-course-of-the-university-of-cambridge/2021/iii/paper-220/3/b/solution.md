<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $h_A(z)=\operatorname{Im}(z-g_A(z))$. This is a nonnegative [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) on $\mathbb H\setminus A$, has boundary value $\operatorname{Im}z$ on the hull boundary, and tends to zero on the real boundary. If $\tau$ is the first exit time of planar [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) from $\mathbb H\setminus A$, the [optional stopping theorem](../../../../../../optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) gives

$$
h_A(iy)=\mathbb E_{iy}[\operatorname{Im}B_\tau].
$$

The hydrodynamic expansion yields $h_A(iy)=\operatorname{hcap}(A)/y+O(y^{-2})$, and therefore the [Brownian representation of half-plane capacity](../../../../../../brownian-representation-of-half-plane-capacity.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{hcap}(A)=\lim_{y\to\infty}y\mathbb E_{iy}[\operatorname{Im}B_\tau]}.
$$
