The [Gamma 1 congruence subgroup](../../../../../../gamma-1-congruence-subgroup.md) is

$$
\Gamma_1(N)=\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in SL_2(\mathbb Z):
a\equiv d\equiv1\pmod N, c\equiv0\pmod N
\right\}.
$$

The space $M_k(\Gamma_1(N))$ consists of the [holomorphic functions](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) $f:\mathfrak h\to\mathbb C$ satisfying

$$
f(\gamma\tau)=(c\tau+d)^kf(\tau)
$$

for every $\gamma\in\Gamma_1(N)$ and which are [holomorphic at a cusp](../../../../../../holomorphic-at-a-cusp.md) for every cusp. Its subspace $S_k(\Gamma_1(N))$ consists of the forms that vanish at every cusp.
