<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The space $M_8(SL_2(\mathbb Z))$ is one-dimensional. The two normalized weight-eight forms $E_8$ and $E_4^2$ therefore agree. Their expansions are

$$
E_8=1+480\sum_{n\geq1}\sigma_7(n)q^n
$$

and

$$
E_4^2
=1+480\sum_{n\geq1}\sigma_3(n)q^n
+57600\sum_{n\geq2}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}
\sigma_3(i)\sigma_3(n-i)\right)q^n.
$$

Equating the coefficient of $q^n$ and dividing by $480$ proves the [divisor-sum convolution identity of weights four and eight](../../../../../../divisor-sum-convolution-identity-of-weights-four-and-eight.md)

$$
\boxed{\sigma_7(n)=\sigma_3(n)+120\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}
\sigma_3(i)\sigma_3(n-i).}
$$
