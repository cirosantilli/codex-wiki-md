The [Fourier expansion of a normalized Eisenstein series](../../../../../../fourier-expansion-of-a-normalized-eisenstein-series.md) is

$$
E_k(\tau)=1+\frac{2}{\zeta(1-k)}
\sum_{n\geq1}\sigma_{k-1}(n)q^n
=1-\frac{2k}{B_k}\sum_{n\geq1}\sigma_{k-1}(n)q^n,
\qquad q=e^{2\pi i\tau}.
$$

Thus

$$
a_0(E_k)=1,
\qquad
a_n(E_k)=\frac{2}{\zeta(1-k)}\sigma_{k-1}(n)quad(n\geq1).
$$

The Bernoulli number $B_k$ is rational, so every coefficient is rational. Equivalently, the standard Fourier calculation expresses the coefficient as a rational multiple of $(2\pi i)^k/\zeta(k)$, which is rational by the given fact that $\zeta(k)/\pi^k\in\mathbb Q$.

The supplied values give the familiar expansions

$$
E_4=1+240\sum_{n\geq1}\sigma_3(n)q^n,
\qquad
E_6=1-504\sum_{n\geq1}\sigma_5(n)q^n.
$$

Since every divisor sum is an integer, all their coefficients are integers.
