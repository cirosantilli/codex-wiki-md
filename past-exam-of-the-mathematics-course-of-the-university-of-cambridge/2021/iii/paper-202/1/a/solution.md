<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $V(t)$ be the total variation of $f$ on $[0,t]$. The [Jordan decomposition of a function of bounded variation](../../../../../../jordan-decomposition-of-a-function-of-bounded-variation.md) is

$$
g(t)=\frac{V(t)+f(t)}2,
\qquad
h(t)=\frac{V(t)-f(t)}2.
$$

For $s<t$, the inequality $V(t)-V(s)\geq|f(t)-f(s)|$ shows that both increments are nonnegative, so $g,h$ are nondecreasing and $f=g-h$. Right-continuity of the [finite variation](../../../../../../total-variation-of-a-function.md) function $f$ implies right-continuity of $V$, and hence of $g,h$.
