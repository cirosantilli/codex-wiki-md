<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The rigid lower boundary has $w=0$. For a time-dependent normal mode this gives

$$
P'(-H)=0.
$$

The free-surface condition from part i gives

$$
P'(0)+\frac{N^2}{g}P(0)=0.
$$

Writing $q=N/c$, the lower condition selects

$$
P(z)=A\cos[q(z+H)].
$$

The upper condition therefore requires

$$
q\tan(qH)=\frac{N^2}{g}.
$$

Equivalently, with $\mu=NH/c$ and $\epsilon=N^2H/g$,

$$
\boxed{\mu\tan\mu=\epsilon}.
$$

Each positive root $\mu_n$ determines the [barotropic mode](../../../../../../barotropic-mode.md) or a [baroclinic mode](../../../../../../baroclinic-mode.md) through $c_n=NH/\mu_n$.
