<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Expand both the forcing and response in the orthogonal vertical normal modes. Projection onto $P_n$ gives

$$
\partial_t\left(
\nabla_h^2\phi_n-\frac{f_0^2}{c_n^2}\phi_n
\right)+\beta\partial_x\phi_n
=F_n(x,y)H(t).
$$

If the horizontal forcing scale obeys $L_h\gg c_n/f_0$, the [relative vorticity](../../../../../../relative-vorticity.md) term $\nabla_h^2\phi_n$ is small compared with the stretching term. The resulting long-wave equation is

$$
\boxed{
\partial_t\phi_n-C_n\partial_x\phi_n
=-\frac{c_n^2}{f_0^2}F_n(x,y)H(t)
},
\qquad
C_n=\frac{\beta c_n^2}{f_0^2}.
$$

**Thus each mode communicates the forcing westward at its long [Rossby wave](../../../../../../rossby-wave.md) speed $C_n$, with the barotropic mode fastest because $c_0$ is largest.**
