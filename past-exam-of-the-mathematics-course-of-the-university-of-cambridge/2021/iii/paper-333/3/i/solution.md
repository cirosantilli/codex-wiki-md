<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At the displaced [free surface](../../../../../../free-surface.md), constant atmospheric pressure and the [hydrostatic approximation](../../../../../../hydrostatic-approximation.md) give the dynamic condition

$$
\eta=\frac{f_0}{g}\psi\qquad\text{at }z=0.
$$

The linearized kinematic condition is $w=D_g\eta/Dt$. Combining these relations with

$$
w=-\frac{D_g}{Dt}\left(\frac{f_0\psi_z}{N^2}\right)
$$

gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac{D_g\psi_z}{Dt}+\frac{N^2}{g}\frac{D_g\psi}{Dt}=0
}\qquad\text{at }z=0.
$$
