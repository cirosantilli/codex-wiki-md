<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let every perturbation be proportional to $\exp[i(kx+mz-\omega t)]$ and define the [intrinsic frequency](../../../../../../intrinsic-frequency.md)

$$
\widehat\omega=\omega-kU.
$$

Eliminating pressure with [incompressibility](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md), and then eliminating buoyancy, gives the [internal gravity wave](../../../../../../internal-wave.md) dispersion relation

$$
\boxed{
\widehat\omega^2
=\frac{N^2k^2}{k^2+m^2}
},
\qquad
\omega=kU\mathbin{\pm}\frac{N|k|}{\sqrt{k^2+m^2}}.
$$

The vertical [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) is

$$
c_{gz}=\frac{\partial\omega}{\partial m}
=-\frac{\widehat\omega m}{k^2+m^2}.
$$

**Thus energy propagates upward when $\widehat\omega m<0$ and downward when $\widehat\omega m>0$. Equivalently, on the positive-intrinsic-frequency branch upward propagation requires $m<0$, while on the negative branch it requires $m>0$.**
