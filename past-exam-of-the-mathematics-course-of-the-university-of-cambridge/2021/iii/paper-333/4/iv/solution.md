<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The sloping boundary exerts the [pressure drag](../../../../../../pressure-drag.md)

$$
-\overline{p'h_x}
=-\frac12\rho_0mkU^2h_0^2
$$

on the fluid. At generation this equals $\rho_0\overline{u'w'}$: the mountain supplies a negative vertical flux of horizontal momentum to the wave. Where the wave dissipates, convergence of that flux transfers the same westward momentum to the mean flow. A forcing that maintains the prescribed uniform basic current must supply the compensating eastward momentum.
