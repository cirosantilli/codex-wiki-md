<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The exact no-penetration condition on the stationary boundary is that the velocity be tangent to it. Linearized at $z=0$, it is

$$
w'(x,0)=U\frac{dh}{dx}
=-Uk h_0\sin kx.
$$

The terrain is stationary, so $\omega=0$ and $\widehat\omega=-kU$. The dispersion relation gives

$$
m^2=\frac{N^2}{U^2}-k^2.
$$

A vertically propagating wave therefore exists if and only if

$$
|kU|<N.
$$

The upward [radiation condition](../../../../../../radiation-condition.md) $\widehat\omega m<0$ selects $km>0$ for $U>0$. Taking $k>0$ gives $m>0$. Lines of constant phase satisfy

$$
kx+mz=\text{constant},
$$

so they tilt upstream as height increases. The discarded sign of $m$ gives the mirror-image phase tilt and downward energy propagation.
