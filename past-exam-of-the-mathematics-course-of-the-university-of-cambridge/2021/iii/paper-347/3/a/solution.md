<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Vertically integrating the [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) and imposing a steady [axisymmetric flow](../../../../../../axisymmetric-flow.md) gives

$$
\frac1R\frac d{dR}(R\Sigma u_R)=0.
$$

Thus the inward-positive [mass accretion rate](../../../../../../mass-accretion-rate.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dot m=-2\pi R\Sigma u_R=\text{constant}}.
$$

The zero-torque condition at $R_{\rm ISCO}$ gives the standard [Shakura--Sunyaev thin disk](../../../../../../shakura-sunyaev-thin-disk.md) relation

$$
\nu\Sigma=\frac{\dot m}{3\pi}
\left[1-\left(\frac{R_{\rm ISCO}}R\right)^{1/2}\right].
$$

Combining this relation with mass conservation, or substituting it into the stated drift equation, yields

$$
\boxed{u_R=-\frac{3\nu}{2R}
\left[1-\left(\frac{R_{\rm ISCO}}R\right)^{1/2}\right]^{-1}}.
$$

Far outside the inner edge this reduces to $u_R\simeq-3\nu/(2R)$.
