↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Fluctuation-dissipation theorem](../../../../../../fluctuation-dissipation-theorem.md) for mobility $\widetilde M$ requires

$$
\dot x=-\widetilde M V'(x)
+\sqrt{2\widetilde M k_BT}\,\Lambda_x.
$$

Comparison with the stated noise amplitude gives

$$
\boxed{C^2=2k_BT}.
$$

This choice makes the stationary [Fokker-Planck equation](../../../../../../fokker-planck-equation.md) have the [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) proportional to $e^{-V/(k_BT)}$.
