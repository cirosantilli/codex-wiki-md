<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Impose the fixed [conserved order parameter](../../../../../../conserved-order-parameter.md)

$$
\int\phi\,d\mathbf r=\Phi
$$

with a [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) $\mu_0$. Stationarity of

$$
\mathcal F[\phi]-\mu_0\int\phi\,d\mathbf r
$$

under every local variation gives

$$
\frac{\delta\mathcal F}{\delta\phi(\mathbf r)}
=\mu(\mathbf r)=\mu_0.
$$

**Thus the [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) is spatially constant at constrained equilibrium.**
