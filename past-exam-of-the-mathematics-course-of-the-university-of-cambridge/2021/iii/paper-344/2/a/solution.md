<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a uniform field the gradient term vanishes and minimization over $p=|\mathbf p|$ gives

$$
ap+bp^3=0.
$$

When $a<0$, the nonzero minimum has

$$
\boxed{p_0=\sqrt{-a/b}},
$$

while its direction is arbitrary by [rotational symmetry](../../../../../../rotational-symmetry.md). A sudden [quench](../../../../../../quench-statistical-physics.md) creates many independently oriented domains. Their mismatches contain gradients and [topological defects](../../../../../../topological-defect.md), whose motion and pair annihilation are slow, so the uniform state is not reached promptly.
