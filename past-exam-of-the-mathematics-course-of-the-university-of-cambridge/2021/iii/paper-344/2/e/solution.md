Outside the core, put $p=p_0$. The angular-gradient contribution is

$$
F_{\mathrm{el}}
=\int_{r_0}^L2\pi r\,dr\,
\frac\kappa2\frac{p_0^2}{r^2}
=\boxed{
\pi\kappa p_0^2\log\frac L{r_0}
}.
$$

The omitted core contribution is finite and independent of $L$.
