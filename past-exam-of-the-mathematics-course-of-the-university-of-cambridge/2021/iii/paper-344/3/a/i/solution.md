<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under the [Euclidean isometry](../../../../../../../euclidean-isometry.md) $\widetilde{\mathbf r}=\mathbf a+R\mathbf r$, where $R^TR=I$,

$$
\partial_u\widetilde{\mathbf r}=R\partial_u\mathbf r.
$$

Therefore

$$
\widetilde g
=(R\mathbf r_u)\mathbin{\cdot}(R\mathbf r_u)
=\mathbf r_u\mathbin{\cdot}\mathbf r_u=g.
$$

Since the integration parameter is unchanged,

$$
\widetilde S(u)=\int_0^u\sqrt{\widetilde g(u')}\,du'
=S(u).
$$

Both the [induced metric](../../../../../../../induced-metric.md) and [arc length](../../../../../../../arc-length.md) are invariant.
