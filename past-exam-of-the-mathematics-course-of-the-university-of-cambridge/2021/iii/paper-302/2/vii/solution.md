<h1 id="2/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

Applying $T_a(y)=\mu_a{}^s(y)\partial/\partial y^s$ to a function of $z$ and using part vi,

$$
\mu_a{}^s(y)\frac{\partial z^r}{\partial y^s}
=\mu_a{}^s(y)\lambda_s{}^d(y)\mu_d{}^r(z)
=\mu_a{}^r(z).
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{T_a(y)=T_a(z)}.
$$

These are the same [left-invariant vector fields](../../../../../../left-invariant-vector-field.md) in different coordinates.
