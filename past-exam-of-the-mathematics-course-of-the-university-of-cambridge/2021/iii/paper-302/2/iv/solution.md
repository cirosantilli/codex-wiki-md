<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Insert the expansions from parts ii and iii successively into  
$g(w)=g(x)^{-1}g(y)^{-1}g(x)g(y)$. All constant and linear terms cancel, leaving

$$
\boxed{w^r=(C^r{}_{st}-C^r{}_{ts})x^sy^t+O(3)}.
$$

The antisymmetric part of the local multiplication law is therefore the infinitesimal [group commutator](../../../../../../group-commutator.md).
