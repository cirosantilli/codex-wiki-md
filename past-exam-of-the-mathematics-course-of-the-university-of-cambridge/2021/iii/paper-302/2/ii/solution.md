<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Setting either argument to zero in the expansion and comparing coefficients gives

$$
\boxed{F^r=0,\qquad A=B=1,\qquad
D^r{}_{st}=E^r{}_{st}=0}.
$$

Thus, through quadratic order,

$$
\boxed{\varphi^r(x,y)=x^r+y^r+C^r{}_{st}x^sy^t+O(3).}
$$
