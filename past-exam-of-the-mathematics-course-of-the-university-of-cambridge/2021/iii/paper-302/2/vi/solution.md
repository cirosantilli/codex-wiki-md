<h1 id="2/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The infinitesimal change $dy$ can be written

$$
dy^s=\mu_d{}^s(y)\theta^d,
\qquad
\theta^d=\lambda_s{}^d(y)dy^s.
$$

Right multiplication of $g(y)$ by $g(\theta)$ is also right multiplication of $g(z)=g(x)g(y)$ by the same element. Part v therefore gives

$$
dz^r=\mu_d{}^r(z)\lambda_s{}^d(y)dy^s,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial z^r}{\partial y^s}
=\mu_d{}^r(z)\lambda_s{}^d(y)}.
$$
