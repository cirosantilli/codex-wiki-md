↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Using $\Lambda_a^{-1}=I-\omega_a+\omega_a^2+O(\omega_a^3)$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Lambda_c
&=\Lambda_2^{-1}\Lambda_1^{-1}\Lambda_2\Lambda_1\\
&=I+\omega_2\omega_1-\omega_1\omega_2
+O(\omega_1^2\omega_2,\omega_1\omega_2^2,\omega_1^3,\omega_2^3).
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $(\omega_c)^\mu{}_\nu
=(\omega_2)^\mu{}_\rho(\omega_1)^\rho{}_\nu
-(\omega_1)^\mu{}_\rho(\omega_2)^\rho{}_\nu$.
