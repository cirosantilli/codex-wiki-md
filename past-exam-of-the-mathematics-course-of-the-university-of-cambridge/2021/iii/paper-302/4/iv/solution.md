<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Comparison of $U[\Lambda_c]$ with the operator group commutator gives the [Lorentz algebra](../../../../../../lorentz-algebra.md)

$$
\boxed{
[M^{\mu\nu},M^{\rho\sigma}]
=\eta^{\nu\rho}M^{\mu\sigma}
-\eta^{\mu\rho}M^{\nu\sigma}
-\eta^{\nu\sigma}M^{\mu\rho}
+\eta^{\mu\sigma}M^{\nu\rho}}.
$$

There is no factor of $i$ because the generators are anti-Hermitian.
