<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The squared root lengths and inner product are

$$
|\alpha_1|^2=1,\qquad |\alpha_2|^2=3,\qquad
\alpha_1\mathbin\cdot\alpha_2=-\frac32.
$$

With $A_{ij}=2\alpha_i\mathbin\cdot\alpha_j/|\alpha_j|^2$, the [Cartan matrix](../../../../../../cartan-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-3&2\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

This is the Cartan matrix of the [G2 root system](../../../../../../g2-root-system.md), with $\alpha_1$ short and $\alpha_2$ long.
