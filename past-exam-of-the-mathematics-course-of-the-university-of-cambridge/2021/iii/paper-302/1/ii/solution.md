<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [root string](../../../../../../root-string.md) through $\alpha_1$ in the $\alpha_2$ direction contains $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_1+\alpha_2$, so $\alpha_1$ can be raised once by $\alpha_2$. The string through $\alpha_2$ in the $\alpha_1$ direction contains

$$
\alpha_2,\quad\alpha_2+\alpha_1,\quad
\alpha_2+2\alpha_1,\quad\alpha_2+3\alpha_1,
$$

so $\alpha_2$ can be raised three times by $\alpha_1$.
