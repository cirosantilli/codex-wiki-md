The [Weyl reflection](../../../../../../weyl-reflection.md) in the hyperplane perpendicular to $\alpha$ sends a weight $\mu$ to

$$
\boxed{s_\alpha(\mu)=
\mu-\frac{2\mu\mathbin\cdot\alpha}{\alpha\mathbin\cdot\alpha}\alpha}.
$$
