The matrix is

$$
M=\cos\frac\theta2I-i\sin\frac\theta2
\frac{\sigma_2+\sigma_3}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Its image is the rotation through $\theta$ about

$$
\boxed{n=(0,1,1)/\sqrt2}.
$$
