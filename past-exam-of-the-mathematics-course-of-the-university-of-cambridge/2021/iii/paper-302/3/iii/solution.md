<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since

$$
(x\mathbin\cdot\sigma)^2=|x|^2I,\qquad
\det(x\mathbin\cdot\sigma)=-|x|^2,
$$

conjugation by $A\in SU(2)$ preserves Hermiticity, tracelessness, and the determinant. Hence $A(x\mathbin\cdot\sigma)A^\dagger=x'\mathbin\cdot\sigma$ with $|x'|=|x|$. Thus $\boxed{G=SU(2)}$.
