<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The multivariable [Leibniz rule](../../../../../../leibniz-rule.md) writes each derivative of $a_1a_2$ as a finite sum of products

$$
(D_x^{\alpha_1}D_\theta^{\beta_1}a_1)
(D_x^{\alpha_2}D_\theta^{\beta_2}a_2),
\qquad
\beta_1+\beta_2=\beta.
$$

Each term is bounded by a constant times

$$
(1+|\theta|)^{N_1-|\beta_1|}
(1+|\theta|)^{N_2-|\beta_2|}
=(1+|\theta|)^{N_1+N_2-|\beta|}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{a_1a_2\in\operatorname{Sym}(X,\mathbb R^k;N_1+N_2)}.
$$
