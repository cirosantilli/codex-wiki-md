<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md) expresses conservation of dark-matter mass: $\delta$ is the [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md), $\mathbf v$ is the [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md), and a prime denotes a [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md) derivative. The [cosmological Euler equation](../../../../../../cosmological-euler-equation.md) expresses momentum conservation: $\mathcal H=a'/a$ is the conformal Hubble rate, $\phi$ is the [peculiar gravitational potential](../../../../../../peculiar-gravitational-potential.md), $\rho$ is the density, and $\sigma_{ij}$ is the [velocity-dispersion tensor of collisionless matter](../../../../../../velocity-dispersion-tensor-of-collisionless-matter.md). The terms are respectively Hubble drag, convective acceleration, gravity, and velocity-dispersion stress.

For curl-free flow, introduce the [peculiar-velocity divergence](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity-divergence.md) $\theta=\nabla\cdot\mathbf v$ and set $\sigma_{ij}=0$. A [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) of the nonlinear continuity term gives

$$
\delta'(\mathbf k)+\theta(\mathbf k)
=-\int\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}
\alpha(\mathbf q,\mathbf k-\mathbf q)
\theta(\mathbf q)\delta(\mathbf k-\mathbf q),
$$

with the [alpha mode-coupling kernel](../../../../../../alpha-mode-coupling-kernel.md)

$$
\boxed{\alpha(\mathbf k_1,\mathbf k_2)
=\frac{(\mathbf k_1+\mathbf k_2)\cdot\mathbf k_1}{k_1^2}}.
$$

Taking the divergence of the Euler equation gives the quadratic velocity kernel

$$
\boxed{\beta(\mathbf k_1,\mathbf k_2)
=\frac{|\mathbf k_1+\mathbf k_2|^2
(\mathbf k_1\cdot\mathbf k_2)}{2k_1^2k_2^2}}.
$$

This is the [beta mode-coupling kernel](../../../../../../beta-mode-coupling-kernel.md); its symmetry follows from the two velocity factors.
