<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [effective field theory of large-scale structure](../../../../../../effective-field-theory-of-large-scale-structure.md) supplements the one-loop prediction by the leading deterministic counterterm and a stochastic term,

$$
P_{\rm EFT}(k)=P_{11}+P_{22}+2P_{13}
-2c_{\rm eff}^2k^2P_{11}(k)+P_{\rm stoch}(k)+\cdots,
$$

where the normalization scale may be absorbed into $c_{\rm eff}^2$ and mass and momentum conservation make $P_{\rm stoch}=O(k^4)$ at small $k$. The ultraviolet part of the [P13 contribution to the one-loop matter power spectrum](../../../../../../p13-contribution-to-the-one-loop-matter-power-spectrum.md) has

$$
P_{13}^{\rm UV}(k)\propto
P_L(k)\int^{\Lambda}\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac{k^2}{q^2}P_L(q)
=k^2P_L(k)\frac1{2\pi^2}
\int^{\Lambda}dq\,P_L(q).
$$

Its cutoff dependence has exactly the $k^2P_L(k)$ form of the [effective sound-speed counterterm in large-scale structure](../../../../../../effective-sound-speed-counterterm-in-large-scale-structure.md), so the running of $c_{\rm eff}^2(\Lambda)$ cancels it. The stochastic and higher-derivative counterterms similarly absorb the allowed analytic ultraviolet dependence of $P_{22}$ and higher orders.
