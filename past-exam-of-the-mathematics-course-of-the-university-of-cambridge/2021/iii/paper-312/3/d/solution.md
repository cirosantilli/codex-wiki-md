<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Conservation of tracer number under the map from the [Lagrangian coordinate](../../../../../../lagrangian-coordinate.md) $\mathbf q$ to the Eulerian position $\mathbf x$ gives

$$
[1+\delta_g^{(E)}(\mathbf x)]d^3x
=[1+\delta_g^{(L)}(\mathbf q)]d^3q.
$$

Matter conservation gives $[1+\delta^{(E)}(\mathbf x)]d^3x=d^3q$, and hence

$$
1+\delta_g^{(E)}=(1+\delta^{(E)})(1+\delta_g^{(L)}).
$$

At linear order, $\delta_g^{(L)}=b_1^{(L)}\delta$ and therefore

$$
\delta_g^{(E)}=(1+b_1^{(L)})\delta.
$$

The [Lagrangian-to-Eulerian linear bias relation](../../../../../../lagrangian-to-eulerian-linear-bias-relation.md) is

$$
\boxed{b_1^{(E)}=1+b_1^{(L)}}.
$$
