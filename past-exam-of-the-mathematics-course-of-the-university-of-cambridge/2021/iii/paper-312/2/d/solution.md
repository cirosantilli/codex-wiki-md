The [single-field inflation squeezed-limit consistency relation](../../../../../../single-field-inflation-squeezed-limit-consistency-relation.md) for the [comoving curvature perturbation](../../../../../../comoving-curvature-perturbation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\lim_{q\to0}
B_{\mathcal R}(q,k,|\mathbf k+\mathbf q|)
=-(n_s-1)P_{\mathcal R}(q)P_{\mathcal R}(k)}.
$$

Equivalently, $B_{\mathcal R}/P_{\mathcal R}(q)=-(3+k\partial_k)P_{\mathcal R}(k)$. The scalar shift in parts a--c is an internal symmetry and leaves the hard fields unchanged, so its right-hand side vanishes. A long adiabatic curvature mode instead acts as a spatial dilation of the hard coordinates, and its response is the scale dependence measured by the [scalar spectral index](../../../../../../scalar-spectral-index.md).
