↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The constant [scalar-field shift symmetry](../../../../../../scalar-field-shift-symmetry.md) has momentum-space action

$$
\boxed{\Delta\phi(\mathbf k)=c(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf k)}.
$$

For the [canonical commutation relation](../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) $[\phi(\mathbf x),\Pi(\mathbf y)]=i\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf x-\mathbf y)$, its [Noether charge](../../../../../../noether-charge.md) can be written

$$
\boxed{Q=c\int d^3x\,\Pi(\mathbf x)=c\,\Pi(\mathbf k=0)}.
$$

Indeed, $i[Q,\phi(\mathbf x)]=c$.
