<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The variation of the product is

$$
\Delta[\phi(\mathbf k)\phi(\mathbf k')]
=c(2\pi)^3\left[
\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf k)\phi(\mathbf k')
+\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf k')\phi(\mathbf k)
\right].
$$

Its expectation value vanishes because the background has $\langle\phi\rangle=0$. The [Ward identity](../../../../../../ward-identity.md) and part b therefore give the [shift-symmetry soft theorem for a scalar bispectrum](../../../../../../shift-symmetry-soft-theorem-for-a-scalar-bispectrum.md)

$$
\boxed{\lim_{q\to0}
\frac{B_\phi(q,k,|\mathbf k+\mathbf q|)}{P_\phi(q)}=0}.
$$

**Thus an exact internal shift creates no leading soft response of the two hard modes.**
