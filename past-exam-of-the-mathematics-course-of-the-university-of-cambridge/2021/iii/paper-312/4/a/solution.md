<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the absence of [scalar anisotropic stress](../../../../../../scalar-anisotropic-stress.md), the traceless spatial [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) give

$$
\boxed{\Phi=\Psi}.
$$

The photon [mass-shell condition](../../../../../../string-mass-shell-condition.md) $g_{\mu\nu}P^\mu P^\nu=0$, together with $p^2=g_{ij}P^iP^j$ and $\epsilon=ap$, gives to first order

$$
\boxed{P^\mu=\frac{\epsilon}{a^2}
\left[1-\Psi,(1+\Phi)\widehat p^i\right]}.
$$

Use the time component of the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md), divide it by $d\eta/d\lambda=P^0$, and substitute the stated [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md). Keeping the perturbed ratios $P^i/P^0=(1+\Phi+\Psi)\widehat p^i$ where they multiply the background $\mathcal H$ terms makes those terms cancel. Comparing the result with the total derivative of $P^0=\epsilon a^{-2}(1-\Psi)$ yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\ln\epsilon}{d\eta}
=\Phi'-\widehat p^i\partial_i\Psi}.
$$

The first term is the time-dependent gravitational redshift and the second is the gravitational frequency shift along the photon direction.
