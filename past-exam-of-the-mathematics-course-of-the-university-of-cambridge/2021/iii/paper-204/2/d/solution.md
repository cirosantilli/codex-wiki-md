<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For every $v\in\mathbb Z^3$, sample independent $U_v\sim\operatorname{Unif}[0,1]$ and set $\eta_p(v)=\mathbf1_{\{U_v\leq p\}}$. Then each $\eta_p$ has the required Bernoulli product law and $\eta_p\leq\eta_{p'}$ whenever $p\leq p'$. This is the [monotone coupling of Bernoulli percolation](../../../../../../monotone-coupling-of-bernoulli-percolation.md).
