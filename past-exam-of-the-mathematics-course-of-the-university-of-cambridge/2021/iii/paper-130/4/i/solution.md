<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume condition ii. In a finite coloring

$$
\mathbb N=C_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup C_r,
$$

an [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) contains exactly one color class: at least one must belong to it because their union is $\mathbb N$, and two disjoint classes cannot both belong to a proper filter. The chosen $C_i$ contains a member of $\mathcal S$, which is therefore monochromatic. This proves condition i.
