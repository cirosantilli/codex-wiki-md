**No.** Take

$$
(a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)=(1,1,-1,-1).
$$

The full set of coefficients sums to zero, so the equation is partition regular by the [Rado theorem for one equation](../../../../../../rado-theorem-for-one-equation.md). Every solution satisfies

$$
x_1+x_2-x_3-x_4=0,
$$

which can never be positive.
