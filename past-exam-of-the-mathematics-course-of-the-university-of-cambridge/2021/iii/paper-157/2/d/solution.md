<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the degree-$d$ [Chebyshev polynomial](../../../../../../chebyshev-polynomial.md) $T_d$, characterized by

$$
T_d(\cos\theta)=\cos(d\theta).
$$

Its Julia set is the interval $[-1,1]$. The complement $\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus[-1,1]$ is connected, and it is exactly the basin of infinity. Hence $T_d$ has exactly one Fatou component. This is the [Chebyshev polynomial Julia set](../../../../../../chebyshev-polynomial-julia-set.md) example.
