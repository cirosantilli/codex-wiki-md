<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $\varepsilon=0$, $L^2=-(1-\beta^2)I=-\Gamma^2I$. The [matrix exponential](../../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{
e^{Lt}=I\cos\Gamma t+\frac L\Gamma\sin\Gamma t
=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\Gamma t&-(1+\beta)\sin\Gamma t/\Gamma\\
\Gamma\sin\Gamma t/(1+\beta)&\cos\Gamma t
\end{pmatrix}}.
$$
