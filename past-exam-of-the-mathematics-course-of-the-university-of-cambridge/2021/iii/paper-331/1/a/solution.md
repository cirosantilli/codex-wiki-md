<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $\mathbf u_0=0$ and $\theta_0=-z$, the [incompressibility condition](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) and temperature equation hold because $\nabla^2z=0$. The momentum equation is satisfied by the [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md)

$$
p_0=-\frac12\sigma Ra\,z^2+\text{constant},
$$

because $\nabla p_0=\sigma Ra\,\theta_0\widehat{\mathbf z}$. Thus this is the conductive basic state.
