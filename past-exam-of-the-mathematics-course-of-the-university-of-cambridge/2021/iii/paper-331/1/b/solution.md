<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $\mathbf u=\mathbf u'$ and $\theta=-z+\theta'$. Dropping quadratic perturbation terms gives the [Linearized Boussinesq equations](../../../../../../linearized-boussinesq-equations.md)

$$
\partial_t\mathbf u'+\lambda\widehat{\mathbf z}\times\mathbf u'+\nabla p'
=\sigma Ra\,\theta'\widehat{\mathbf z}+\sigma\nabla^2\mathbf u',
\qquad
\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf u'=0,
$$

and

$$
\partial_t\theta'-W=\nabla^2\theta',
\qquad W=\mathbf u'\mathbin\cdot\widehat{\mathbf z}.
$$

The fixed temperatures give $\theta'=0$ at $z=0,1$. Impermeable [stress-free boundary conditions](../../../../../../stress-free-boundary-condition.md) give

$$
\boxed{W=D^2W=0,
\qquad
D\omega=0,
\qquad
\omega=\widehat{\mathbf z}\mathbin\cdot\nabla\times\mathbf u'.}
$$
