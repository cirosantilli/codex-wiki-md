<h1 id="4/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Varying the gauge-fixed [Polyakov action](../../../../../../polyakov-action.md) gives the bulk wave equation and the boundary term

$$
\delta S_{\partial\Sigma}=-\frac1{2\pi\alpha'}
\int d\tau\,\delta X_\mu\partial_\sigma X^\mu\bigg|_{0}^{\pi}.
$$

It vanishes in each target direction either through the [Neumann boundary condition](../../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md)

$$
\partial_\sigma X^\mu|_{\sigma=0,\pi}=0,
$$

or through the [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md)

$$
\delta X^I|_{\sigma=0,\pi}=0,
$$

which fixes the endpoint positions.
