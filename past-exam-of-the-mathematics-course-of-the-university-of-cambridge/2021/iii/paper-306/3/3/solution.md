<h1 id="3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Fix $z_1=0$, $z_2=1$, and $z_3=\infty$, and write $z_4=z$. For the closed-string tachyon, $k_i^2=4/\alpha'$. Hence

$$
\alpha'k_1\mathbin\cdot k_4=-\frac{\alpha'u}{2}-4,
\qquad
\alpha'k_2\mathbin\cdot k_4=-\frac{\alpha't}{2}-4,
$$

where momentum conservation was used in the second identity. Taking the limit at infinity together with the ghost factor gives the [Virasoro–Shapiro amplitude](../../../../../../virasoro-shapiro-amplitude.md)

$$
\boxed{A_4=\delta^{26}\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^4k_i\right)g_c^2
\int_{\mathbb C}d^2z\,
|z|^{-\alpha'u/2-4}|1-z|^{-\alpha't/2-4}}.
$$

Choosing a different set of three punctures to fix merely applies a Möbius transformation and relabels identical external tachyons. Exchanging the roles of the relevant punctures interchanges $s$ and $t$ without changing the integral, proving crossing symmetry under $s\leftrightarrow t$.
