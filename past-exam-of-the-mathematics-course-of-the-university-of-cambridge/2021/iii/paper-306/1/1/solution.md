↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Define the worldsheet [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md) by

$$
T_{ab}=-\frac{4\pi}{\sqrt{|h|}}\frac{\delta S}{\delta h^{ab}}.
$$

Using $\delta\sqrt{|h|}=-\sqrt{|h|}h_{ab}\delta h^{ab}/2$ in the [Polyakov action](../../../../../../polyakov-action.md) gives

$$
\boxed{T_{ab}=\frac1{\alpha'}\left[
\partial_aX\mathbin\cdot\partial_bX
-\frac12h_{ab}h^{cd}\partial_cX\mathbin\cdot\partial_dX
\right]}.
$$

Because the worldsheet is two-dimensional,

$$
h^{ab}T_{ab}=\frac1{\alpha'}
\left[1-\frac12h^{ab}h_{ab}\right]
h^{cd}\partial_cX\mathbin\cdot\partial_dX=0.
$$

This classical tracelessness is the Noether identity for [Weyl invariance](../../../../../../weyl-transformation.md).
