<h1 id="2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Adding the matter and ghost transformations gives

$$
\boxed{\delta_vT_{\rm tot}=v\partial T_{\rm tot}
+2(\partial v)T_{\rm tot}+\frac{D-26}{12}\partial^3v}.
$$

For the Laurent modes $L_n=\oint dz\,z^{n+1}T_{\rm tot}(z)/(2\pi i)$, the corresponding [Virasoro algebra](../../../../../../virasoro-algebra.md) is

$$
\boxed{[L_m,L_n]=(m-n)L_{m+n}
+\frac{D-26}{12}m(m^2-1)\delta_{m+n,0}}.
$$

At the bosonic-string [critical dimension of string theory](../../../../../../critical-dimension-of-string-theory.md), $D=26$, the total central charge and central term vanish. The total stress tensor then transforms as a genuine conformal tensor of weight two, and the quantum worldsheet gauge anomaly cancels.
