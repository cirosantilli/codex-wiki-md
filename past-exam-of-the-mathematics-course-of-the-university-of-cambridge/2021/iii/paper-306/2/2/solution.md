<h1 id="2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The worldsheet ghosts form an anticommuting [bc system](../../../../../../bc-system.md) of weights $(2,-1)$. Its double contractions give central charge

$$
c_{bc}=-2(6\cdot2^2-6\cdot2+1)=-26.
$$

Therefore

$$
\delta_vT_{\rm gh}=v\partial T_{\rm gh}+2(\partial v)T_{\rm gh}
-\frac{26}{12}\partial^3v,
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{c_{\rm gh}=-\frac{13}{6}}.
$$
