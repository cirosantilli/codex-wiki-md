<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A chain of length $s$ in $R/\mathfrak p$ lifts to a chain

$$
\mathfrak p=\mathfrak p_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq\mathfrak p_s
$$

in $R$. Since $R$ is a domain and $\mathfrak p\ne0$, prepending $(0)\subsetneq\mathfrak p$ gives a chain of length $s+1$. Therefore $s+1\leq r$ and

$$
\boxed{\dim(R/\mathfrak p)\leq r-1<r.}
$$
