<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Going up lifts every prime chain in $A$ to one in $B$, so $\dim B\geq\dim A$. Conversely, contracting a strict chain of primes of $B$ gives a chain in $A$, and the [incomparability theorem for integral extensions](../../../../../../../incomparability-theorem-for-integral-extensions.md) ensures that no strict inclusion contracts to equality. Thus $\dim A\geq\dim B$, and

$$
\boxed{\dim A=\dim B.}
$$
