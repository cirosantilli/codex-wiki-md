The [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md) $M\otimes_RN$ is the free abelian group on symbols $(m,n)$ modulo additivity in each variable and the balancing relation $(rm,n)=(m,rn)$. It has the universal property that balanced bilinear maps $M\times N\to P$ correspond uniquely to homomorphisms $M\otimes_RN\to P$.
