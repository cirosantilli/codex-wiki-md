<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An $R$-module $F$ is [flat](../../../../../../flat-module.md) when $-\otimes_RF$ preserves injections, equivalently all finite exact sequences. Since $-\otimes_RR$ is naturally the identity functor, $R$ is flat. A free module is a direct sum of copies of $R$, and tensor products commute with direct sums, so every free module is flat.

As an $R$-module,

$$
R[X]=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}RX^n
$$

is free. Therefore the polynomial algebra $R[X]$ is a flat $R$-algebra.
