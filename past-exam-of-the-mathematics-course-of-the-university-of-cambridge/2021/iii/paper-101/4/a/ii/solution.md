<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $x\in A$, consider

$$
f_x(T)=\prod_{\sigma\in G}(T-\sigma(x)).
$$

This is monic, and every $\tau\in G$ permutes its factors, so all coefficients lie in $A^G$. Since $f_x(x)=0$, every $x\in A$ is integral over $A^G$. Thus

$$
\boxed{A\text{ is integral over }A^G.}
$$
