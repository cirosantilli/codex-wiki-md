<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow M_1\longrightarrow M_1\oplus M_2
\longrightarrow M_2\longrightarrow0
$$

and part ii show that a direct sum of two modules is Noetherian exactly when both summands are. Induction proves the assertion for every finite direct sum.

Each $R/I_j$ is Noetherian as an $R$-module because its $R$-submodules are precisely its ideals as a Noetherian ring. The diagonal map

$$
R\longrightarrow\bigoplus_{j=1}^rR/I_j
$$

has kernel $\bigcap_jI_j=0$. Hence $R$ is an $R$-submodule of a finite direct sum of Noetherian modules, so $R$ is a Noetherian $R$-module; equivalently, $R$ is a [Noetherian ring](../../../../../../../noetherian-ring.md).
