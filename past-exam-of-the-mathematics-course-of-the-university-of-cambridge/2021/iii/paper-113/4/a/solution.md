<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If the [germ of a sheaf section](../../../../../../germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) $s_p$ is zero, then by the definition of a [stalk of a sheaf](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) there is a neighbourhood $V$ of $p$ on which $s|_V=0$. Every point of $V$ also has zero germ. Thus the complement of $\operatorname{Supp}s$ is open, so the [support of a sheaf section](../../../../../../support-of-a-sheaf-section.md) is closed.
