<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [direct image sheaf](../../../../../../direct-image-sheaf.md) is determined on the basis consisting of [principal open subschemes](../../../../../../principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(b)\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}B$. Their inverse images are

$$
f^{-1}D(b)=D(\varphi(b))\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}A,
$$

and hence

$$
f_*\widetilde M(D(b))
=\widetilde M(D(\varphi(b)))
=M_{\varphi(b)}.
$$

On the other hand, localization of the restricted-scalar $B$-module gives

$$
(M_B)_b\cong M_{\varphi(b)}.
$$

These isomorphisms commute with restriction to smaller principal opens, so they define the equality of [quasi-coherent sheaves](../../../../../../quasi-coherent-sheaf.md)

$$
\boxed{f_*\widetilde M=\widetilde{M_B}.}
$$
