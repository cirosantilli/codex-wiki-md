<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For every homogeneous $s\in S_+$,

$$
D_+(\varphi(s))
=\{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Proj}T:\varphi(s)\notin\mathfrak p\}.
$$

Hence

$$
U=\bigcup_{s\in S_+\text{ homogeneous}}D_+(\varphi(s)),
$$

so $U$ is open in the [Zariski topology](../../../../../../zariski-topology.md).

If $\mathfrak p\in U$, then $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak p)$ is a homogeneous [prime ideal](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md) that does not contain the [irrelevant ideal of a graded ring](../../../../../../irrelevant-ideal-of-a-graded-ring.md) $S_+$. Thus

$$
f(\mathfrak p)=\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak p)
$$

defines a map $U\to\operatorname{Proj}S$. On every [standard affine open of Proj](../../../../../../standard-affine-open-of-proj.md) the graded homomorphism induces

$$
(S_s)_0\longrightarrow (T_{\varphi(s)})_0,
$$

and therefore an [affine scheme](../../../../../../affine-scheme.md) morphism

$$
D_+(\varphi(s))\longrightarrow D_+(s).
$$

These morphisms agree after localization on overlaps, so they glue to the required [morphism of schemes](../../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) $f:U\to\operatorname{Proj}S$.
