↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [mean-field approximation](../../../../../../mean-field-approximation.md) suppresses correlated order-parameter fluctuations. Near a [continuous phase transition](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md), the [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) diverges and long-wavelength fluctuations become increasingly important. The [Ginzburg criterion](../../../../../../ginzburg-criterion.md) therefore fails in sufficiently low dimension, and the interacting [renormalization-group fixed point](../../../../../../renormalization-group-fixed-point.md) changes the mean-field exponents. For the tricritical $m^6$ theory the [upper critical dimension](../../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md) is three: below $d=3$ the exponents are generally non-mean-field, while at $d=3$ one expects logarithmic corrections to mean-field scaling.
