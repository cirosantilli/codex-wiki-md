<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [equilibrium magnetization](../../../../../../equilibrium-magnetization.md) is a [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md) of the [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md). Its candidates are the [stationary points](../../../../../../stationary-point.md)

$$
0=f'(m)=2m\left(a_2+2a_4m^2+3a_6m^4\right).
$$

Thus $m=0$, or $x=m^2\geq0$ is a nonnegative root of

$$
3a_6x^2+2a_4x+a_2=0.
$$

One retains candidates with nonnegative curvature $f''(m)$ and compares their free energies; the candidate with the smallest value is the equilibrium state. Equal global minima describe [phase coexistence](../../../../../../phase-coexistence.md).
