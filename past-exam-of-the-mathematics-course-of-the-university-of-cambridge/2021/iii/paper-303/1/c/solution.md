<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At the [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md), $a_4=0$ and the zero-field [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) is $f=a_2m^2+a_6m^6$. Below $T_c$, minimization gives

$$
m^4=-\frac{a_2}{3a_6}.
$$

Since $a_2\sim T-T_c$, the [order-parameter critical exponent](../../../../../../order-parameter-critical-exponent.md) is

$$
\boxed{\beta=\frac14}.
$$

At this minimum, $f_{\rm sing}=a_2m^2+a_6m^6$ is proportional to $-|T-T_c|^{3/2}$. Comparing this with $f_{\rm sing}\sim|T-T_c|^{2-\alpha}$ gives the [heat-capacity critical exponent](../../../../../../heat-capacity-critical-exponent.md)

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac12}.
$$

After adding the magnetic contribution $-Bm$, the inverse zero-field [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) is the curvature $f''(m)$. Above $T_c$ it is $2a_2$, while below $T_c$ it is $-8a_2$, so $\chi\sim|T-T_c|^{-1}$ and the [magnetic-susceptibility critical exponent](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility-critical-exponent.md) is

$$
\boxed{\gamma=1}.
$$

Finally, exactly at $T_c$ the equation of state is $B=6a_6m^5$. Hence $m\sim B^{1/5}$ and the [critical-isotherm exponent](../../../../../../critical-isotherm-exponent.md) is

$$
\boxed{\delta=5}.
$$
