<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A scalar field of [scaling dimension](../../../../../../scaling-dimension.md) $\Delta_\phi$ has a scale-invariant [two-point correlation function](../../../../../../two-point-correlation-function.md) proportional to $|x|^{-2\Delta_\phi}$. Comparison with the stated form gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta_\phi=\frac{d-2+\eta}{2}}.
$$

The difference $\eta/2$ from the engineering value is the field's [anomalous dimension](../../../../../../anomalous-dimension.md), generated by fluctuations and interactions at a non-Gaussian [renormalization-group fixed point](../../../../../../renormalization-group-fixed-point.md).
