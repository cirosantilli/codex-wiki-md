<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The constraint $\mathbf n\mathbin\cdot\mathbf n=1$ makes $\mathbf n$ dimensionless. Since $d^dx\,(\partial_i n_A)^2$ has engineering dimension $2-d$, dimensional consistency of the [O(N) nonlinear sigma model](../../../../../../o-n-nonlinear-sigma-model.md) gives

$$
\boxed{[g]_{\rm eng}=2-d}.
$$
