<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $E_r(\mathbb Q_2)$ be the $r$th term in the [filtration of elliptic-curve points over a local field](../../../../../../../filtration-of-elliptic-curve-points-over-a-local-field.md). Reduction gives

$$
E(\mathbb Q_2)/E_1(\mathbb Q_2)\simeq\widetilde E(\mathbb F_2),
$$

of order four, while

$$
E_1(\mathbb Q_2)/E_2(\mathbb Q_2)\simeq(\mathbb F_2,+)
$$

has order two. The [formal logarithm](../../../../../../../formal-logarithm.md) is injective on $E_2(\mathbb Q_2)$ and identifies it with an additive subgroup of $\mathbb Q_2$, so $E_2(\mathbb Q_2)$ is torsion-free. A finite subgroup of $E(\mathbb Q_2)$ therefore injects into $E(\mathbb Q_2)/E_2(\mathbb Q_2)$, whose order is $4\cdot2=8$. Thus $|E(\mathbb Q_2)_{\rm tors}|$ divides $8$.
