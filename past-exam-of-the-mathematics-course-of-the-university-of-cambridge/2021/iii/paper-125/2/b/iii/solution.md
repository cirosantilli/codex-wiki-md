<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The reduction of $P=(0,0)$ is a nonidentity point of the group $\widetilde E(\mathbb F_3)$ of prime order seven. Hence $7P$ reduces to the identity and lies in $E_1(\mathbb Q_3)$. Part i shows that $P$ has infinite order, so $7P\ne O$. Every nonidentity point in $E_1(\mathbb Q_3)$ has formal parameter $z=-x/y$ of positive valuation and therefore

$$
x=z^{-2}+O(z^{-1})
$$

has negative valuation. Thus $7P$ does not have integral coordinates.
