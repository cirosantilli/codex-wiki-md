<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The equation can be written

$$
u_t=iu_{xx}-iVu.
$$

For the squared [L2 norm](../../../../../../l2-norm.md), periodic [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) and the reality of $V$ give

$$
\frac d{dt}\int_{-1}^1|u|^2\,dx
=2\operatorname{Re}\int_{-1}^1
\left(iu_{xx}-iVu\right)\overline u\,dx
=2\operatorname{Re}\left[
-i\int_{-1}^1|u_x|^2\,dx
-i\int_{-1}^1V|u|^2\,dx
\right]=0.
$$

**Thus the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) generates a unitary flow and $\|u(\cdot,t)\|_2$ is constant.**
