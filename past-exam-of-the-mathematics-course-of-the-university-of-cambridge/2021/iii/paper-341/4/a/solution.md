<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $L_h=D_{xx}+\alpha D_x$, where the centered second-difference matrix $D_{xx}$ is real symmetric negative definite under homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), and the centered first-difference matrix $D_x$ is real skew-symmetric. Hence

$$
\operatorname{Re}\langle\mathbf u,L_h\mathbf u\rangle
=\langle\mathbf u,D_{xx}\mathbf u\rangle\leq0.
$$

The semidiscrete [energy method](../../../../../../energy-method.md) gives $\|\mathbf u(t)\|_2\leq\|\mathbf u(0)\|_2$ for every real $\alpha$, so the scheme is stable.
