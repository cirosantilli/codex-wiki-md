<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The matter-like scaling is special to a quadratic minimum. More generally, the [effective equation of state of an oscillating scalar field](../../../../../../effective-equation-of-state-of-an-oscillating-scalar-field.md) in $V\propto|\phi|^n$ is

$$
\langle w\rangle=\frac{n-2}{n+2},
\qquad
\rho_\phi\propto a^{-6n/(n+2)}.
$$

For example, a quartic minimum has $\langle w\rangle=1/3$ and redshifts like [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md). Potentials without a stable minimum, oscillations whose period is not short relative to the [Hubble time](../../../../../../hubble-time.md), and significant decay of the inflaton into other particles also invalidate the matter-like argument.
