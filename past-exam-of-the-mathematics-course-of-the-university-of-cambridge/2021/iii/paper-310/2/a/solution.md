<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a spatially homogeneous canonical [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md), spatial derivatives vanish. Reading the time and spatial components of its [energy-momentum tensor](../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md) in the comoving frame gives

$$
\boxed{\rho_\phi=\frac12\dot\phi^2+V(\phi)},
\qquad
\boxed{P_\phi=\frac12\dot\phi^2-V(\phi)}.
$$

**Thus the kinetic term contributes equally to [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) and pressure, whereas the [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) contributes negative pressure.**
