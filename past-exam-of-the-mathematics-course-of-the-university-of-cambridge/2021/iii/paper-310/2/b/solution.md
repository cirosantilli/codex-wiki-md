Substitution of these expressions into the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) gives

$$
\dot\phi\left(\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi+V'(\phi)\right)=0.
$$

Continuity through a turning point therefore yields the homogeneous [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi=-V'(\phi)}.
$$

During [slow-roll inflation](../../../../../../slow-roll-approximation.md), the acceleration and kinetic-energy terms are negligible. In terms of the [reduced Planck mass](../../../../../../reduced-planck-mass.md), the approximate evolution is

$$
\boxed{3H\dot\phi\simeq-V'(\phi)},
\qquad
\boxed{H^2\simeq\frac{V(\phi)}{3M_{\rm Pl}^2}}.
$$
