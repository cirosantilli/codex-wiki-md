<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Near the minimum, the [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) of the potential is

$$
V(\phi)\simeq V_0b^2\phi^2=\frac12m^2\phi^2,
\qquad
m^2=\frac{4V_0}{3M_{\rm Pl}^2}.
$$

Neglecting [Hubble friction](../../../../../../hubble-friction.md) during one short oscillation reduces the [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md) to the [harmonic oscillator equation](../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md)

$$
\boxed{\ddot\phi+m^2\phi\simeq0}.
$$

The [virial theorem](../../../../../../virial-theorem.md) gives $\langle\dot\phi^2/2\rangle=\langle V\rangle$, so $\langle P_\phi\rangle=0$. Restoring the slow cosmological damping in the averaged [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) gives

$$
\dot{\langle\rho_\phi\rangle}+3H\langle\rho_\phi\rangle=0,
\qquad
\boxed{\langle\rho_\phi\rangle\propto a^{-3}}.
$$

The coherently oscillating inflaton therefore behaves as [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md).
