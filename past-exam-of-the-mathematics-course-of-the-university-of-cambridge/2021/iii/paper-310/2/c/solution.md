<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

This is the potential of [Starobinsky inflation](../../../../../../starobinsky-inflation.md). Put $b=\sqrt{2/3}/M_{\rm Pl}$ and $y=e^{-b\phi}$, so $V=V_0(1-y)^2$. Its [potential slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) and [second potential slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../second-potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) are

$$
\epsilon_V=\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2}{2}\left(\frac{V'}V\right)^2
=\frac43\frac{y^2}{(1-y)^2},
$$



$$
\eta_V=M_{\rm Pl}^2\frac{V''}V
=\frac43\frac{-y+2y^2}{(1-y)^2}.
$$

At large positive $\phi$, $y\ll1$, so both $\epsilon_V$ and $|\eta_V|$ are small and [slow-roll inflation](../../../../../../slow-roll-approximation.md) is possible. Near the minimum, a [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) gives $1-y\simeq b\phi$ and hence

$$
\epsilon_V\simeq\eta_V\simeq\frac{2M_{\rm Pl}^2}{\phi^2}.
$$

They are large for $|\phi|\ll M_{\rm Pl}$, so that region cannot sustain slow roll.
