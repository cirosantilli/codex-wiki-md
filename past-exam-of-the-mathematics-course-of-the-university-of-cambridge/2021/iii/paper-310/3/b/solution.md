<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For one species with two internal states, the [relic-neutrino energy density](../../../../../../relic-neutrino-energy-density.md) obtained from the frozen [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) is

$$
\rho_\nu=2\int\frac{d^3p}{(2\pi)^3}
\frac{\sqrt{p^2+m_\nu^2}}{e^{ap/T_\nu}+1}
=\frac1{\pi^2}\int_0^\infty
\frac{p^2\sqrt{p^2+m_\nu^2}}{e^{ap/T_\nu}+1}\,dp.
$$

In the relativistic limit, set $x=ap/T_\nu$ and use the standard [Riemann zeta function](../../../../../../riemann-zeta-function.md) integral

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^3}{e^x+1}\,dx=\frac{7\pi^4}{120}.
$$

This gives

$$
\boxed{\rho_\nu=\frac{7\pi^2}{120}\left(\frac{T_\nu}{a}\right)^4}.
$$
