↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Comparing the correction in part c with the nonrelativistic kinetic energy $m_\nu\langle v^2\rangle/2$ gives the characteristic late-time [speed](../../../../../../speed.md)

$$
\boxed{v_{\rm rms}\simeq
\sqrt{\frac{15\zeta(5)}{\zeta(3)}}\frac{T_\nu}{am_\nu}
\simeq3.60\frac{T_\nu}{am_\nu}}.
$$

**Thus the [late-time relic-neutrino speed](../../../../../../late-time-relic-neutrino-speed.md) redshifts as $a^{-1}$.**
