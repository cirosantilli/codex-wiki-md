<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At [neutrino decoupling](../../../../../../neutrino-decoupling.md), neutrinos, electrons, positrons, and [photons](../../../../../../photon.md) share one temperature. The neutrinos subsequently free stream, so $T_\nu\propto a^{-1}$. In the still-coupled electromagnetic plasma, the effective entropy degrees of freedom change during [electron-positron annihilation in cosmology](../../../../../../electron-positron-annihilation-in-cosmology.md) from

$$
g_{*s,{\rm before}}=2+\frac78(2+2)=\frac{11}{2}
$$

to $g_{*s,{\rm after}}=2$. Separate [cosmological entropy conservation](../../../../../../cosmological-entropy-conservation.md) in that plasma gives $g_{*s}T_\gamma^3a^3={\rm constant}$, while $T_\nu a$ remains constant. Consequently the [Cosmic neutrino background](../../../../../../cosmic-neutrino-background.md) temperature obeys

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_\nu}{T_\gamma}=\left(\frac4{11}\right)^{1/3}}.
$$
