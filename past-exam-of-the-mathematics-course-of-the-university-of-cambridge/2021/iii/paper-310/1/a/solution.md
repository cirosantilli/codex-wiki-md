<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a component with constant [barotropic equation of state](../../../../../../barotropic-equation-of-state.md) $P=w\rho$, the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) gives

$$
\frac{d\rho}{\rho}=-3(1+w)\frac{da}{a}.
$$

Hence $\rho_i=\rho_{i,0}a^{-3(1+w_i)}$. Choose the present [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) to be $a_0=1$, so the [cosmological redshift](../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) obeys $1+z=a^{-1}$, and define the present [cosmological density parameter](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md) by

$$
\Omega_{i,0}=\frac{\rho_{i,0}}{\rho_{\rm crit,0}},
\qquad
\rho_{\rm crit,0}=\frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G}.
$$

Substitution in the spatially flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) then yields

$$
\boxed{H(z)=H_0\left[\sum_i\Omega_{i,0}(1+z)^{3(1+w_i)}\right]^{1/2}}.
$$
