<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) and $H=\dot a/a$ give the [deceleration parameter](../../../../../../deceleration-parameter.md)

$$
q=-\frac{\ddot a}{aH^2}
=\frac{4\pi G}{3H^2}\sum_i\rho_i(1+3w_i)
=\boxed{\frac12\sum_i\Omega_i(z)(1+3w_i)}.
$$

An [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) contains only [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md), so $q_0=1/2$ and cannot explain the observed negative value. For the stated [Lambda-CDM model](../../../../../../lambda-cdm-model.md),

$$
q_0=\frac12\left[0.3+0.7(1-3)\right]=-0.55,
$$

so its [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) produces the required [accelerating universe](../../../../../../accelerating-expansion-of-the-universe.md).
