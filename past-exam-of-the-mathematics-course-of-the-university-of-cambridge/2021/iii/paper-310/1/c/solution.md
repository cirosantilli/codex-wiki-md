<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [age of an FLRW universe](../../../../../../age-of-an-flrw-universe.md) follows from $dt=da/(aH)$. Neglecting radiation and spatial curvature gives

$$
t_0=\frac1{H_0}\int_0^1
\frac{da}{a\sqrt{\Omega_{m,0}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}}
=\frac1{H_0}\int_0^1
\frac{a^{1/2}\,da}{\sqrt{\Omega_{m,0}+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}a^3}}.
$$

For an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), $\Omega_{m,0}=1$ and $\Omega_{\Lambda,0}=0$, so

$$
\boxed{t_0=H_0^{-1}\int_0^1a^{1/2}\,da=\frac{2}{3}H_0^{-1}}.
$$
