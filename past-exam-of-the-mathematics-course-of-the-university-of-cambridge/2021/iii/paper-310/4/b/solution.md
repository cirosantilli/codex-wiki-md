<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The exact mode functions satisfy the Wronskian normalization

$$
f_kf_k^{*\prime}-f_k^*f_k'=i.
$$

Using the [Dirac delta function](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) generated by the ladder-operator commutator therefore gives the exact [canonical commutation relation](../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{[\hat f(\tau,\mathbf x),\hat\pi(\tau,\mathbf y)]
=i\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf x-\mathbf y)}.
$$

This remains true on [superhorizon scales](../../../../../../superhorizon-scale.md), so the exact operators do not literally commute. A claim of classical behavior must instead compare this fixed commutator with the growing statistical fluctuations, or appeal to [quantum decoherence](../../../../../../quantum-decoherence.md).
