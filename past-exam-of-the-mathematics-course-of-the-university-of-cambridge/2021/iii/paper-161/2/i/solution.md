The [chain rule for information entropy](../../../../../../chain-rule-for-information-entropy.md) and [conditioning reduces entropy](../../../../../../conditioning-reduces-entropy.md) give

$$
H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y\mid X)\leq H(X)+H(Y).
$$

Thus [subadditivity of information entropy](../../../../../../subadditivity-of-information-entropy.md) holds for two finitely valued [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md). Applying this inequality to $(X_1,\ldots,X_{n-1})$ and $X_n$ and then inducting gives

$$
\boxed{H(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\leq\sum_{i=1}^nH(X_i)}.
$$
