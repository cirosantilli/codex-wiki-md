<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $\sigma>1$, unique prime factorization and absolute convergence give the [Euler product](../../../../../../euler-product.md)

$$
\zeta(s)=\prod_p(1-p^{-s})^{-1}.
$$

Every factor is nonzero and the product converges to a nonzero limit. Equivalently, the absolutely convergent identity

$$
\frac1{\zeta(s)}=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{\mu(n)}{n^s}
$$

provides a reciprocal. This proves the [Euler-product nonvanishing of the Riemann zeta function](../../../../../../euler-product-nonvanishing-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md).
