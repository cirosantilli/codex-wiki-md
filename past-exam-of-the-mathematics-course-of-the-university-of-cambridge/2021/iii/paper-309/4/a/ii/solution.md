<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Varying the coordinate expression for the curvature and replacing partial derivatives by covariant ones gives

$$
\delta R_{ab}=\nabla_c\delta\Gamma^c{}_{ab}
-\nabla_b\delta\Gamma^c{}_{ac}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\delta R=R_{ab}\delta g^{ab}+g^{ab}\delta R_{ab}
=-R^{ab}h_{ab}+\nabla_aX^a,
$$

where

$$
\boxed{X^a=\nabla_bh^{ab}-\nabla^ah}.
$$

This is the [metric variation of scalar curvature](../../../../../../../metric-variation-of-scalar-curvature.md); its divergence contributes only a boundary term to the variation of the [Einstein-Hilbert action](../../../../../../../einstein-hilbert-action.md).
