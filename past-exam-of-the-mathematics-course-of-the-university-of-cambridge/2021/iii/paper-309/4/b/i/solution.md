<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Varying $\Phi$ and integrating by parts gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla_a\nabla^a\Phi
-\alpha e^{\alpha\Phi}F_{ab}F^{ab}=0}.
$$

Since $\delta F_{ab}=2\nabla_{[a}\delta A_{b]}$, variation of the potential and another integration by parts give

$$
\boxed{\nabla_a(e^{\alpha\Phi}F^{ab})=0}.
$$

These are the scalar and Maxwell equations of [Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton theory](../../../../../../../einstein-maxwell-dilaton-theory.md).
