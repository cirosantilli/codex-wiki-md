<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $k^a$ be null and define $v_c=k^aF_{ac}$. The metric-proportional terms vanish after contraction with $k^ak^b$, leaving

$$
T_{ab}k^ak^b
=\frac12(k^a\nabla_a\Phi)^2
+2e^{\alpha\Phi}v_cv^c.
$$

Antisymmetry of $F$ gives $v_ck^c=0$. A vector orthogonal to a null vector in Lorentzian signature has nonnegative squared norm, so $v_cv^c\geq0$; also $e^{\alpha\Phi}>0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{T_{ab}k^ak^b\geq0},
$$

and the theory satisfies the [null energy condition](../../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md).
