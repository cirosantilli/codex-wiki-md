<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Interchanging the two transverse directions changes the sign of the quadratic profile. Hence

$$
\boxed{Y_q=-yq'(u)\partial_w+q(u)\partial_y,
\qquad q''=-a(u)q}
$$

is another two-parameter family of Killing fields.
