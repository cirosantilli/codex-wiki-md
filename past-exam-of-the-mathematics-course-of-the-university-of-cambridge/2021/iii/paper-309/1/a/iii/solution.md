<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a function, pullback by the flow gives

$$
\mathcal L_Xf=\frac{d}{ds}\bigg|_0 f(\Phi_s(\,\cdot\,))=X(f).
$$

In flow-box coordinates $X=\partial_t$, write $Y=Y^\mu\partial_\mu$. Then

$$
\mathcal L_XY=(\partial_tY^\mu)\partial_\mu
=[\partial_t,Y]=[X,Y].
$$

Both sides are vector fields defined invariantly, so equality in these coordinates proves

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_Xf=X(f),\qquad \mathcal L_XY=[X,Y]}
$$

in every coordinate system.
