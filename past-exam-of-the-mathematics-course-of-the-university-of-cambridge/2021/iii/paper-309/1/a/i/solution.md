<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\Phi_s$ be the local flow generated by the vector field $X$. For a covariant tensor, its [Lie derivative](../../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_XT=\frac{d}{ds}\bigg|_{s=0}\Phi_s^*T}.
$$

For contravariant indices one equivalently uses the differential of the inverse flow, and the construction extends to every tensor product by the Leibniz rule. It measures the infinitesimal change of $T$ under transport by the flow of $X$.
