<h1 id="1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For one-forms, the defining pullback or the identity

$$
(\mathcal L_X\alpha)(Y)=X[\alpha(Y)]-\alpha([X,Y])
$$

gives

$$
(\mathcal L_X\alpha)_\mu=X^\rho\alpha_{\mu,\rho}
+\alpha_\rho X^\rho{}_{,\mu}.
$$

Applying the Leibniz rule to both covariant slots of a type-$(0,2)$ tensor yields

$$
\boxed{(\mathcal L_XT)_{\mu\nu}
=X^\rho T_{\mu\nu,\rho}
+T_{\rho\nu}X^\rho{}_{,\mu}
+T_{\mu\rho}X^\rho{}_{,\nu}}.
$$
