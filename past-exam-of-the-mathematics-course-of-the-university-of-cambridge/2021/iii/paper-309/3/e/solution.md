<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Put

$$
S_1=\sum_{i=1}^3p_i,\qquad S_2=\sum_{i=1}^3p_i^2.
$$

Contracting the curvature components gives

$$
R_{00}=\frac{S_1-S_2}{t^2},
\qquad
R_{ii}=\frac{p_i(S_1-1)}{t^2}
$$

in the orthonormal frame, with no sum on $i$. Vacuum requires $S_1=S_2$ and $p_i(S_1-1)=0$ for every $i$. Since the $p_i$ are not all zero, some $p_i\neq0$, forcing $S_1=1$ and then $S_2=1$. Conversely these conditions make every Ricci component vanish. Thus the [Kasner metric](../../../../../../kasner-metric.md) is vacuum exactly when

$$
\boxed{\sum_i p_i=1,\qquad \sum_i p_i^2=1},
$$

so $A=B=1$.
