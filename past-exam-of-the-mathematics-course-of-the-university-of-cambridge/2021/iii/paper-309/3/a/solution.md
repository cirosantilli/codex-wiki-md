<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The metric directly gives

$$
g(e_0,e_0)=-1,\qquad
g(e_i,e_j)=t^{-p_i}t^{-p_j}t^{2p_i}\delta_{ij}
=\delta_{ij},
$$

with no mixed products. Hence

$$
\boxed{e_0=\partial_t,\qquad e_i=t^{-p_i}\partial_{x^i}}
$$

is an orthonormal frame.
