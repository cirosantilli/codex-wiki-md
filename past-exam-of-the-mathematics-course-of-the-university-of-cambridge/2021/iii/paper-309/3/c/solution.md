<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

One has

$$
de^0=0,\qquad
de^i=\frac{p_i}{t}e^0\wedge e^i.
$$

Cartan's torsion-free first structure equation and metric compatibility give the only nonzero [connection 1-forms](../../../../../../connection-1-form-split.md):

$$
\boxed{\omega^i{}_0=\omega^0{}_i=\frac{p_i}{t}e^i},
\qquad
\omega^i{}_j=0\quad(i\neq j).
$$
