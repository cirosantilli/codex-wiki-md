<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Cartan's second structure equation](../../../../../../cartan-s-second-structure-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Theta^i{}_0=\Theta^0{}_i
=\frac{p_i(p_i-1)}{t^2}e^0\wedge e^i},
$$

and, for $i\neq j$,

$$
\boxed{\Theta^i{}_j=\frac{p_ip_j}{t^2}e^i\wedge e^j}.
$$

With all indices lowered and the curvature convention in the question, the independent nonzero orthonormal [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) components are

$$
\boxed{R_{0i0i}=\frac{p_i(1-p_i)}{t^2},
\qquad
R_{ijij}=\frac{p_ip_j}{t^2}\quad(i\neq j)},
$$

together with those obtained from the Riemann symmetries.
