<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $G\in\mathcal G\cap\mathcal H$, independence makes $G$ independent of itself, so

$$
\mathbb P(G)=\mathbb P(G)^2.
$$

Thus every event in $\mathcal G\cap\mathcal H$ has probability zero or one. The intersection is trivial modulo null sets, and the [independent sigma-algebras have trivial intersection](../../../../../../independent-sigma-algebras-have-trivial-intersection.md) result gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G\cap\mathcal H]=\mathbb E[X]}
$$

almost surely.
