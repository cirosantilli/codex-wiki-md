<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $\xi_k=S_k-S_{k-1}$, then

$$
\mu=\mathbb E\xi_k=p-(1-p)=2p-1.
$$

The centered increments $\xi_k-\mu$ are independent of the past and have mean zero, so

$$
\boxed{M_n=S_n-\mu n}
$$

is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md).
