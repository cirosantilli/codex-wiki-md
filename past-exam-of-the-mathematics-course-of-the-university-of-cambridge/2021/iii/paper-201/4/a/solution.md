A process $(X_t)_{t\geq0}$ is [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) in $\mathbb R^d$ when $X_0=0$, its paths are almost surely continuous, and for $0\leq t_0<\cdots<t_k$ the increments $X_{t_j}-X_{t_{j-1}}$ are independent centered [Gaussian vectors](../../../../../../multivariate-normal-distribution.md) with covariance $(t_j-t_{j-1})I_d$.
