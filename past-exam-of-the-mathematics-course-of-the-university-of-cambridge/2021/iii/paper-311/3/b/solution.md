<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [second law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../second-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md), or [Hawking's area theorem](../../../../../../hawking-s-area-theorem.md), states that the total area of future-event-horizon cross-sections cannot decrease toward the future under the [null energy condition](../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md) and the usual predictability assumptions. If any horizon generator had $\theta<0$, the twist-free [Null Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../null-raychaudhuri-equation.md) and the [null focusing theorem](../../../../../../null-focusing-theorem.md) would force a conjugate point at finite affine parameter. A generator complete to the future cannot pass through such a point while remaining on the achronal boundary of $J^-(\mathcal I^+)$. Hence $\theta\geq0$ everywhere on the regular future horizon, and $dA/d\lambda=\theta A\geq0$ proves the area law.
