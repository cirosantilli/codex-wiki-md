↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [null geodesic congruence](../../../../../../../null-geodesic-congruence.md) is a smooth family of nonintersecting null geodesics filling an open spacetime region. For its affinely parametrized tangent $U^a$,

$$
U^aU_a=0,
\qquad
U^b\nabla_bU^a=0.
$$

Writing $B_{ab}=\nabla_bU_a$, differentiation of the null norm and the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) give respectively

$$
\boxed{U^aB_{ab}=\frac12\nabla_b(U^aU_a)=0,
\qquad
B_{ab}U^b=U^b\nabla_bU_a=0.}
$$
