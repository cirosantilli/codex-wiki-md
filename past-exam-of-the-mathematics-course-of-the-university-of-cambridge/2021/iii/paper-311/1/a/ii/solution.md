<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose at one transverse cross-section a null vector $N^a$ satisfying $N^aN_a=0$ and $U^aN_a=-1$, then [parallel transport](../../../../../../../parallel-transport.md) it along every generator:

$$
U^b\nabla_bN^a=0.
$$

Metric compatibility and the affine [geodesic equation](../../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) imply that both $N^2$ and $U\mathbin\cdot N$ are constant along each generator, so the required normalization persists.
