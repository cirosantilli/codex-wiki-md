↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

On the [null hypersurface](../../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md), the generator covector is proportional to a normal, locally $U_a=f\nabla_a u$ for a level-set function $u$. The [Frobenius theorem](../../../../../../../frobenius-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
U_{[a}\nabla_bU_{c]}=0.
$$

Contracting once with $N^a$ and projecting the remaining indices with $P^a{}_b$ removes every term containing $U$ or $N$ and leaves $P_b{}^dP_c{}^e\nabla_{[d}U_{e]}=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\widehat\omega_{ab}=0}
$$

on the hypersurface.
