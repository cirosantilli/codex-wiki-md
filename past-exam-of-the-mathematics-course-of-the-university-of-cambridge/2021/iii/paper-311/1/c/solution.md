<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The hypersurface generators have zero [null twist](../../../../../../null-twist.md). Contracting the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) twice with the null tangent removes the trace term, and the [null energy condition](../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md) gives $R_{ab}U^aU^b=8\pi T_{ab}U^aU^b\geq0$. Since the squared [null shear](../../../../../../null-shear.md) is nonnegative, the [Null Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../null-raychaudhuri-equation.md) implies

$$
\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda}\leq-\frac12\theta^2.
$$

While $\theta<0$ this is equivalent to

$$
\frac{d}{d\lambda}\left(\frac1\theta\right)\geq\frac12.
$$

Starting from $\theta(0)=\theta_0<0$, the right-hand side reaches zero no later than $\lambda=2/|\theta_0|$. The reciprocal expansion must therefore vanish and

$$
\boxed{\theta\longrightarrow-\infty
\quad\text{within affine distance }2/|\theta_0|}.
$$

This is the [null focusing theorem](../../../../../../null-focusing-theorem.md).
