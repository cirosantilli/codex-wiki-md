<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $a=0$, $T_H=(8\pi M)^{-1}$, so the Schwarzschild black hole has negative [heat capacity](../../../../../../../heat-capacity.md). A small energy gain from a reservoir lowers its temperature below the reservoir temperature and causes further absorption; a small energy loss raises its temperature and causes further emission. The equilibrium is therefore unstable in the [canonical ensemble](../../../../../../../canonical-ensemble.md).
