<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The horizon generator must be null at $r=r_+$. Substitution of $\xi=\partial_v+\Omega_H\partial_\chi$ in the [Kerr black hole](../../../../../../../kerr-black-hole.md) metric gives

$$
\boxed{\Omega_H=\frac{a}{r_+^2+a^2}
=\frac{a}{2Mr_+}}.
$$

The [surface gravity](../../../../../../../surface-gravity.md) and [Hawking temperature](../../../../../../../hawking-temperature.md) are

$$
\kappa=\frac{r_+-r_-}{2(r_+^2+a^2)},
\qquad
\boxed{T_H=\frac{\kappa}{2\pi}
=\frac{r_+-r_-}{4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)}
=\frac{\sqrt{M^2-a^2}}{4\pi Mr_+}}.
$$
