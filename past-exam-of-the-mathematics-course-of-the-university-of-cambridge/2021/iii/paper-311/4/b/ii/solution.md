<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A gravitational-collapse spacetime is stationary and approximately empty in the remote past and settles to a stationary black-hole exterior in the future, with a dynamical region between them. The same comparison of in- and out-positive-frequency modes therefore applies. Tracing an outgoing late-time mode backward through the collapse produces an exponentially blueshifted mixture of early positive and negative frequencies. Its nonzero Bogoliubov beta coefficient yields the thermal occupation numbers of [Hawking radiation](../../../../../../../hawking-radiation.md), while partner modes pass through the [event horizon](../../../../../../../event-horizon.md).
