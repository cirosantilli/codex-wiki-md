<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The early and late stationary regions define distinct positive-frequency mode bases and hence an in-vacuum and out-vacuum. Write their [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md) as

$$
u_i^{\rm out}=\sum_j
\left(\alpha_{ij}u_j^{\rm in}+\beta_{ij}u_j^{{\rm in}*}\right).
$$

The corresponding operators satisfy

$$
\widehat b_i^{\rm out}=\sum_j
\left(\alpha_{ij}^*\widehat a_j^{\rm in}
-\beta_{ij}^*\widehat a_j^{{\rm in}\dagger}\right).
$$

Using $\widehat a_j^{\rm in}|0_{\rm in}\rangle=0$ and the canonical commutator gives the [particle number from Bogoliubov coefficients](../../../../../../../particle-number-from-bogoliubov-coefficients.md):

$$
\boxed{\langle0_{\rm in}|\widehat N_i^{\rm out}|0_{\rm in}\rangle
=\sum_j|\beta_{ij}|^2}.
$$

Time dependence in the sandwich region can make $\beta_{ij}\neq0$, so the in-vacuum contains out-particles.
