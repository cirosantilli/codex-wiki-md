<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $A=p[T]$ for a tree $T\subseteq(\kappa^+\times\omega)^{<\omega}$. For each $\alpha<\kappa^+$, restrict the first-coordinate labels to $\alpha$ and write

$$
T_\alpha=T\cap(\alpha\times\omega)^{<\omega},
\qquad A_\alpha=p[T_\alpha].
$$

Every countable subset of the [successor cardinal](../../../../../../successor-cardinal.md) $\kappa^+$ is bounded in $\kappa^+$, so the first coordinates of any branch through $T$ all lie below some $\alpha<\kappa^+$. Consequently

$$
A=\bigcup_{\alpha<\kappa^+}A_\alpha.
$$

Every $\alpha<\kappa^+$ has cardinality at most $\kappa$, hence injects into $\kappa$. Part i shows that each $A_\alpha$ is $\kappa$-Suslin. This is the [Successor-Suslin decomposition](../../../../../../successor-suslin-decomposition.md).
