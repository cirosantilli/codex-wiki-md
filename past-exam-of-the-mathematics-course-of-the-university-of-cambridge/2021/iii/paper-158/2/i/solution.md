<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $T\subseteq(X\times\omega)^{<\omega}$ be a [Suslin representation](../../../../../../suslin-representation.md) of $A$, and let $f:X\to Y$ be injective. Apply $f$ coordinatewise to the first coordinate of every node and put

$$
T_f=\{(f\circ s,t):(s,t)\in T\}\subseteq(Y\times\omega)^{<\omega}.
$$

The injectivity of $f$ ensures that a sequence is a branch of $T_f$ exactly when its first coordinate decodes to a branch of $T$. Hence $p[T_f]=p[T]=A$, proving that every [X-Suslin set](../../../../../../x-suslin-set.md) is $Y$-Suslin whenever $X$ injects into $Y$.
