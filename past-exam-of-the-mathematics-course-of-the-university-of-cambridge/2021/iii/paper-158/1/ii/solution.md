The [restricted axiom of choice](../../../../../../restricted-axiom-of-choice.md) $\mathsf{AC}_X(Y)$ says that for every $X$-indexed family $(A_x)_{x\in X}$ of nonempty [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) of $Y$, there is a [choice function](../../../../../../choice-function.md) $c:X\to Y$ such that $c(x)\in A_x$ for every $x\in X$.
