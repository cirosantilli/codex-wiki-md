<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a payoff set $A\subseteq M^\omega$, a [quasistrategy](../../../../../../quasistrategy.md) for a player assigns a nonempty subset of $M$ to every finite position at which that player moves. A play is consistent with it when each of that player's moves belongs to the assigned set. The set $A$, or equivalently the [infinite game of perfect information](../../../../../../infinite-game-of-perfect-information.md) $G(A)$, is [quasidetermined](../../../../../../quasidetermined-infinite-game.md) when one player has a quasistrategy under which every consistent play is won by that player.
