<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take

$$
\boxed{X=M^{<\omega},\qquad Y=M.}
$$

Thus $\Phi_M$ is equivalent in ZF to $\mathsf{AC}_{M^{<\omega}}(M)$. Indeed, this choice principle chooses one move from every nonempty value of a winning [quasistrategy](../../../../../../quasistrategy.md), turning it into a [winning strategy in an infinite game](../../../../../../winning-strategy-in-an-infinite-game.md). The converse encodes an arbitrary $M^{<\omega}$-indexed family of nonempty subsets of $M$ into a quasidetermined game. This is the [choice characterization of quasideterminacy](../../../../../../choice-characterization-of-quasideterminacy.md).
