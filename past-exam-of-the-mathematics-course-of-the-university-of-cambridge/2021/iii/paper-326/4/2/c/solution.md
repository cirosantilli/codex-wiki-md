<h1 id="4/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The supports of $\varphi_1$ and $\varphi_2$ are disjoint, so they are [orthogonal vectors](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md). Substitution in the covariance formula gives

$$
C\varphi_1
=\frac12\|\varphi_1\|^2\varphi_1
=\frac{\lambda_1(A)}2\varphi_1,
$$

and

$$
C\varphi_2
=\frac12\|\varphi_2\|^2\varphi_2
=\frac{1-\lambda_1(A)}2\varphi_2.
$$

Therefore the associated [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are

$$
\boxed{\gamma_1=\frac{\lambda_1(A)}2,
\qquad
\gamma_2=\frac{1-\lambda_1(A)}2}.
$$
