↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [indicator functions](../../../../../../../indicator-function.md) satisfy

$$
\|\varphi_1\|_{L^2}^2=\lambda_1(A)<\infty,
\qquad
\|\varphi_2\|_{L^2}^2=1-\lambda_1(A)<\infty.
$$

**Therefore $\varphi_1,\varphi_2\in L^2[0,1]$.**
