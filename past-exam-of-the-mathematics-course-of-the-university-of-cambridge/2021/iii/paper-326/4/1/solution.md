A [Gaussian measure](../../../../../../gaussian-measure.md) $\mu$ on a real separable [Banach space](../../../../../../banach-space-split.md) $X$ is a [Borel probability measure](../../../../../../borel-measure.md) such that $\ell_*\mu$ is a one-dimensional [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) for every [continuous linear functional](../../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) $\ell\in X^*$. Its mean $m\in X$ and [covariance operator of a Gaussian measure](../../../../../../covariance-operator-of-a-gaussian-measure.md) $C:X^*\to X$ are characterized by

$$
\ell(m)=\int_X\ell(u)\,d\mu(u),
$$

and

$$
\ell_2(C\ell_1)
=\int_X\ell_1(u-m)\ell_2(u-m)\,d\mu(u)
$$

for all $\ell_1,\ell_2\in X^*$.
