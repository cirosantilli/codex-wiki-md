<h1 id="3/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For nonnegative $p,q$,

$$
(\sqrt p-\sqrt q)^2
=\frac{(p-q)^2}{(\sqrt p+\sqrt q)^2}
\leq|p-q|.
$$

Integrating and using the density formula for [total variation distance](../../../../../../../total-variation-distance.md) gives

$$
\boxed{
d_{\mathrm{Hel}}(\mu,\mu_0)^2
\leq\int_X|p-q|\,d\rho
=2d_{\mathrm{TV}}(\mu,\mu_0)}.
$$
