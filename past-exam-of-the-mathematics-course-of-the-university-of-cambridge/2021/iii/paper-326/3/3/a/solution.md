<h1 id="3/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take

$$
\boxed{\nu=\mu+\mu_0}.
$$

This is a [finite measure](../../../../../../../finite-measure.md), hence a [sigma-finite measure](../../../../../../../sigma-finite-measure.md). If $\nu(B)=0$, nonnegativity gives $\mu(B)=\mu_0(B)=0$, so both $\mu$ and $\mu_0$ are [absolutely continuous](../../../../../../../absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) with respect to $\nu$.
