<h1 id="3/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $p=d\mu/d\rho$ and $q=d\mu_0/d\rho$, whose existence follows from the [Radon-Nikodym theorem](../../../../../../../radon-nikodym-theorem.md). Since

$$
(\sqrt p-\sqrt q)^2\leq p+q,
$$

we have

$$
\int_X(\sqrt p-\sqrt q)^2\,d\rho
\leq\int_X(p+q)\,d\rho=2.
$$

**Thus the defining integral is finite and the [Hellinger distance](../../../../../../../hellinger-distance.md) is well defined.**
