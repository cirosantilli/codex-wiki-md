<h1 id="3/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The problem is a [well-posed Bayesian inverse problem in total variation](../../../../../../../well-posed-bayesian-inverse-problem-in-total-variation.md) when a unique posterior $\mu^y$ exists for every $y\in Y$ and the posterior map is continuous:

$$
y_n\longrightarrow y
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
d_{\mathrm{TV}}(\mu^{y_n},\mu^y)\longrightarrow0.
$$

**Thus the metric supplies the precise meaning of continuous dependence on the observed data in the Bayesian version of [Hadamard well-posedness](../../../../../../../well-posed-problem.md).**
