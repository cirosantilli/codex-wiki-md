The [total variation distance](../../../../../../../total-variation-distance.md) is

$$
\boxed{d_{\mathrm{TV}}(\mu,\nu)
=\sup_{B\in\mathcal B_X}|\mu(B)-\nu(B)|}.
$$

If both measures have densities $p,q$ with respect to a common [dominating measure](../../../../../../../dominating-measure.md), then $d_{\mathrm{TV}}(\mu,\nu)=\frac12\int|p-q|$.
