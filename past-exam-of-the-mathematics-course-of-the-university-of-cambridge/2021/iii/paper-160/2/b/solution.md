<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Repeated use of the induction branching rule gives

$$
S^{(2)}\!\uparrow_{S_2}^{S_4}
\cong S^{(4)}\oplus2S^{(3,1)}\oplus S^{(2,2)}\oplus S^{(2,1,1)},
$$

while

$$
S^{(1^3)}\!\uparrow_{S_3}^{S_4}
\cong S^{(2,1,1)}\oplus S^{(1^4)}.
$$

On the five conjugacy classes $1,(12),(12)(34),(123),(1234)$, their characters are respectively

$$
(12,2,0,0,0)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(4,-2,0,1,0).
$$

The tensor-product character is their pointwise product $(48,-4,0,0,0)$. Taking [inner products](../../../../../../character-orthogonality.md) with the five irreducible characters of $S_4$ gives multiplicities $1,5,4,7,3$. Therefore the [induced-tensor decomposition for the symmetric group on four points](../../../../../../induced-tensor-decomposition-for-the-symmetric-group-on-four-points.md) is

$$
\boxed{V\cong S^{(4)}\oplus5S^{(3,1)}\oplus4S^{(2,2)}\oplus7S^{(2,1,1)}\oplus3S^{(1^4)}}.
$$
