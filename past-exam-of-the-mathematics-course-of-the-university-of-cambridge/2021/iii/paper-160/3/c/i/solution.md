<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use four beads, for which $(3,1)$ has [beta set](../../../../../../../beta-set-of-a-partition.md)

$$
\{6,3,1,0\}.
$$

On runners of residues $0,1,2,3$, division by four gives respectively the beta sets

$$
\{0\},\quad\{0\},\quad\{1\},\quad\{0\}.
$$

Only $\{1\}$ represents a nonempty partition, namely $(1)$. Therefore the [four-quotient of the partition three-one](../../../../../../../four-quotient-of-the-partition-three-one.md) is

$$
\boxed{Q_4(3,1)=(\varnothing,\varnothing,(1),\varnothing)}.
$$
