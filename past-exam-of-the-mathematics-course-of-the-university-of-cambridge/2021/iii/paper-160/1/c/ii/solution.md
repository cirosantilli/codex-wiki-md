<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because $C(t')=R(t)$,

$$
e(t')=\sum_{r\in R(t)}\operatorname{sgn}(r)\{rt'\}.
$$

Applying $\theta$ and using $r e(u)=\operatorname{sgn}(r)e(u)$ gives

$$
\theta(e(t'))=\left(\sum_{r\in R(t)}r e(t)\right)\otimes e(u).
$$

Thus $m=\sum_{r\in R(t)}r e(t)$. Every $r$ fixes the tabloid $\{t\}$, while $e(t)$ has coefficient one at $\{t\}$. Invariance of the [tabloid bilinear form](../../../../../../../tabloid-bilinear-form.md) now yields

$$
\langle m,\{t\}\rangle
=\sum_{r\in R(t)}\langle r e(t),\{t\}\rangle
=\sum_{r\in R(t)}1
=\boxed{|R(t)|}.
$$
