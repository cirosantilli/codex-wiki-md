Writing $\widehat G$ for the [character group of a finite abelian group](../../../../../../character-group-of-a-finite-abelian-group.md), the [Bohr set](../../../../../../bohr-set.md) with frequency set $\Gamma\subseteq\widehat G$ and width $0\leq\rho\leq2$ is

$$
B(\Gamma,\rho)
=\{x\in G:|\gamma(x)-1|\leq\rho\text{ for every }\gamma\in\Gamma\}.
$$

Its rank is $|\Gamma|$.
