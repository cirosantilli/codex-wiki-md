↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the counting-measure [Lp norm](../../../../../../lp-norm.md), define

$$
\operatorname{AP}_{2m}(f;X)
=\{t\in\mathbb F_p^n:\|\tau_tf-f\|_{2m}\leq X\},
\qquad (\tau_tf)(x)=f(x+t).
$$

This is the set of [Lp almost periods](../../../../../../lp-almost-period.md) of $f$ with exponent $p=2m$ and error $X$.
