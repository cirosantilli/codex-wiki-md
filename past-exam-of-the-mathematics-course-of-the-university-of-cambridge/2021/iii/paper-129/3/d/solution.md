<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The sum over all $x$ of the threefold-convolution representation function is $|A|^3$. Some $x_0$ therefore satisfies

$$
(1_A*1_A*1_A)(x_0)\geq\frac{|A|^3}{p^n}=\alpha|A|^2.
$$

Use part c with $\varepsilon=\alpha/2$. There is a [vector subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) $V$ of codimension $O(\alpha^{-2}\log(1/\alpha))$ such that, for every $t\in V$,

$$
(1_A*1_A*1_A)(x_0+t)
\geq\frac\alpha2|A|^2>0.
$$

Positivity means that $x_0+t$ has a representation as a sum of three elements of $A$. Thus

$$
x_0+V\subseteq A+A+A,
$$

which is the desired translate of a low-codimension subspace.
