The [Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](../../../../../../balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md) states that if a finite set $A$ has at least $\delta|A|^3$ [additive quadruples](../../../../../../additive-quadruple.md), then it has a subset $A'\subseteq A$ such that

$$
|A'|\geq\delta^C|A|,
\qquad
|A'+A'|\leq\delta^{-C}|A'|,
$$

where $C$ is an absolute constant.
