<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Because $A\subseteq\mathbb Z^2$ is finite, choose $R$ with $A\subseteq[-R,R]^2$ and an integer $M>2sR$. Define

$$
\phi(x,y)=x+My.
$$

An equality of $s$-term sums in $\mathbb Z^2$ plainly gives equality after applying this [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md). Conversely, equality of the images gives

$$
\Delta_x+M\Delta_y=0,
$$

where $|\Delta_x|\leq2sR<M$. Therefore $\Delta_x=0$, and then $\Delta_y=0$. The same estimate with one term on each side shows that $\phi$ is injective on $A$. Hence $\phi$ is a [Freiman s-isomorphism](../../../../../../freiman-s-isomorphism.md) from $A$ to the subset $\phi(A)\subseteq\mathbb Z$.
