↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A bijection $\phi:A\to B$ is a [Freiman s-isomorphism](../../../../../../freiman-s-isomorphism.md) when, for every $a_1,\ldots,a_s,a'_1,\ldots,a'_s\in A$,

$$
a_1+\cdots+a_s=a'_1+\cdots+a'_s
$$

holds if and only if

$$
\phi(a_1)+\cdots+\phi(a_s)
=\phi(a'_1)+\cdots+\phi(a'_s).
$$
