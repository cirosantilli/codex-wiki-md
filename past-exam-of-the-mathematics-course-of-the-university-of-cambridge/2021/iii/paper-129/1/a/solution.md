<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a nonempty $X\subseteq A$ for which $|X+A|/|X|$ is minimal, and write this minimum as $K'$. Since $X=A$ is an available choice, $K'\leq K$. The [Petridis minimal-growth lemma](../../../../../../petridis-minimal-growth-lemma.md) gives

$$
|X+A+C|\leq K'|X+C|
$$

for every finite $C$. Taking $C=(t-1)A$ and iterating yields

$$
|X+tA|\leq(K')^t|X|\leq K^t|X|
$$

for every integer $t\geq1$.
