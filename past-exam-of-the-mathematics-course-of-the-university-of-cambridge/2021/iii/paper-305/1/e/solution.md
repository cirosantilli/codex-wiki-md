<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

At energy $E\gg M_X$, the longitudinal polarization behaves as $\epsilon_L^\mu\sim p^\mu/M_X$. A Proca theory with arbitrary interactions can therefore produce longitudinal-vector scattering amplitudes growing like powers of $E/M_X$, eventually violating [partial-wave unitarity](../../../../../../partial-wave-unitarity.md) and invalidating perturbation theory.

In a gauge-theory completion, the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md) identifies the longitudinal mode with a would-be [Goldstone boson](../../../../../../goldstone-boson.md). Gauge relations fix the vector, Goldstone, and Higgs couplings. Higgs-exchange diagrams then cancel the leading energy growth of the gauge diagrams, as in longitudinal $W$-boson scattering, leaving amplitudes compatible with perturbative unitarity up to the scale where the full renormalizable theory itself becomes strongly coupled.
