<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The quadratic [Proca action](../../../../../../proca-action.md) is

$$
\mathcal L=-\frac14X_{\mu\nu}X^{\mu\nu}+\frac12M_X^2X_\mu X^\mu,
\qquad
X_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu X_\nu-\partial_\nu X_\mu.
$$

Its momentum-space kinetic operator is

$$
K_{\mu\nu}=-(p^2-M_X^2)\eta_{\mu\nu}+p_\mu p_\nu.
$$

Inverting it on transverse and longitudinal projectors gives the [Proca propagator](../../../../../../proca-propagator.md)

$$
\boxed{D_{\mu\nu}(p)=\frac{-i}{p^2-M_X^2+i\epsilon}
\left(\eta_{\mu\nu}-\frac{p_\mu p_\nu}{M_X^2}\right)}.
$$
