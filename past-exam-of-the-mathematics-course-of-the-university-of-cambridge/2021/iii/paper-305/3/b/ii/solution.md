<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For one complex scalar in the [fundamental representation](../../../../../../../fundamental-representation.md), the most general power-counting-renormalizable gauge-invariant Lagrangian is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=-\frac14F_{\mu\nu}^aF^{a\mu\nu}
+(D_\mu\Phi)^\dagger D^\mu\Phi-V(\Phi),
\qquad
V(\Phi)=-\mu^2\Phi^\dagger\Phi
+\lambda(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)^2},
$$

apart from a constant and the four-dimensional [Yang-Mills theta term](../../../../../../../yang-mills-theta-term.md). Stability requires $\lambda>0$. A cubic candidate $\epsilon_{ijk}\Phi_i\Phi_j\Phi_k$ vanishes because the scalar components commute.
