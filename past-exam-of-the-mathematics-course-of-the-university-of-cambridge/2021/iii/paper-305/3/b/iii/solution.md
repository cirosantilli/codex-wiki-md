<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $\mu^2>0$, the minima satisfy $\Phi^\dagger\Phi=v^2/2$ with $v^2=\mu^2/\lambda$. A global $SU(3)$ rotation can choose

$$
\Phi_0=\frac1{\sqrt2}(0,0,v)^T.
$$

The transformations acting as $SU(2)$ on the first two entries leave this vector fixed, so the symmetry-breaking pattern is

$$
\boxed{SU(3)\longrightarrow SU(2)}.
$$

There are $8-3=5$ broken generators. If the symmetry were global, the [Goldstone theorem](../../../../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) would therefore give five massless scalar modes, exactly the five tangent directions of the vacuum manifold $SU(3)/SU(2)$.
