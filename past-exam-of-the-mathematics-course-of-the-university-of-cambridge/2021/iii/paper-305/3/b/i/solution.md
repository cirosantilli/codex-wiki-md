<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $U(x)\in SU(3)$ and $A_\mu=A_\mu^aT^a$, choose $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-igA_\mu$. The [Yang-Mills gauge transformation](../../../../../../../yang-mills-gauge-transformation.md) is

$$
A'_\mu=UA_\mu U^{-1}-\frac{i}{g}(\partial_\mu U)U^{-1},
$$

which ensures $D'_\mu(U\Phi)=U D_\mu\Phi$. Since

$$
[D_\mu,D_\nu]=-igF_{\mu\nu},
$$

conjugating this commutator immediately gives

$$
\boxed{F'_{\mu\nu}=UF_{\mu\nu}U^{-1}}.
$$

**Thus the [gauge field strength](../../../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md) is gauge covariant.**
