<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

CP conjugates the charged-current coupling and replaces $V_{\rm CKM}$ by $V_{\rm CKM}^*$. CP is conserved only if quark-field rephasings can make the entire matrix real. For three generations an irreducible phase prevents this. Equivalently, the rephasing-invariant [Jarlskog invariant](../../../../../../jarlskog-invariant.md)

$$
J=\operatorname{Im}(V_{ij}V_{k\ell}V_{i\ell}^*V_{kj}^*)
$$

is nonzero for distinct rows and columns. Since $J$ changes sign under complex conjugation, $J\neq0$ proves [CP violation](../../../../../../cp-violation.md) in electroweak interactions.
