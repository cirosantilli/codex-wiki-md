<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An anomalous chiral redefinition moves phase between the bare QCD angle and the quark mass matrix, so the measurable parameter is

$$
\bar\theta=\theta_{\rm QCD}+\arg\det(M_uM_d).
$$

The neutron [electric dipole moment](../../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md) requires $|\bar\theta|$ to be extremely small, although no generic Standard Model symmetry enforces this. This is the [Strong CP problem](../../../../../../strong-cp-problem.md).

In [Peccei–Quinn theory](../../../../../../peccei-quinn-theory.md), an anomalous spontaneously broken global symmetry replaces $\bar\theta$ by a dynamical [axion](../../../../../../axion.md) field. Nonperturbative QCD generates an axion potential whose minimum lies at the CP-conserving value, dynamically relaxing the effective angle to zero.
