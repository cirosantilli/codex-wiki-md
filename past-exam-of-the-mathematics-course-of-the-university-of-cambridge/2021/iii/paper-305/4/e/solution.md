<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The $SU(2)_L$ [electroweak theta angle](../../../../../../electroweak-theta-angle.md) can be shifted by an anomalous global rephasing generated by [baryon plus lepton number](../../../../../../baryon-plus-lepton-number.md). Because the renormalizable Standard Model has no operator that explicitly breaks this global phase in a way that fixes it, one may choose the rephasing to set $\theta_{\rm EW}=0$ without changing any other physical parameter. The weak theta angle is therefore unobservable in the renormalizable Standard Model; it could enter a physical invariant only after adding suitable explicit violation of [baryon plus lepton number](../../../../../../baryon-plus-lepton-number.md).
