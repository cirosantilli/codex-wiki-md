↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the binary [POVM](../../../../../../../positive-operator-valued-measure.md) $\{E,I-E\}$ and equal priors,

$$
p_{\rm succ}
=\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(E\rho_1)
+\frac12\operatorname{Tr}[(I-E)\rho_2]
=\frac12\left[1+\operatorname{Tr}E(\rho_1-\rho_2)\right].
$$

Maximizing with the [variational characterization of trace distance](../../../../../../../variational-characterization-of-trace-distance.md) gives the equal-prior [Holevo–Helstrom theorem](../../../../../../../holevo-helstrom-theorem.md)

$$
\boxed{p_{\rm succ}^{\max}
=\frac12[1+D(\rho_1,\rho_2)]}.
$$
