<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Distinct [Bell states](../../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) are orthogonal. For orthogonal pure states, the trace distance is one and the fidelity is the absolute overlap, zero. Hence

$$
\boxed{D(|\Phi^+\rangle,|\Psi^-\rangle)=1},
\qquad
\boxed{F(|\Psi^+\rangle,|\Phi^-\rangle)=0}.
$$
