↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Conjugation by $\sigma_z$ maps $(r_x,r_y,r_z)$ to $(-r_x,-r_y,r_z)$. The [phase-flip channel](../../../../../../../../phase-flip-channel.md) therefore acts on the [Bloch vector](../../../../../../../../bloch-vector.md) as

$$
\boxed{(r_x,r_y,r_z)\longmapsto
((1-2p)r_x,(1-2p)r_y,r_z)}.
$$
