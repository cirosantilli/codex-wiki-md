<h1 id="3/a/ii/3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Conjugation by one [Pauli matrix](../../../../../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md) preserves its parallel Bloch component and reverses the other two. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
r_x'&=(p_0+p_x-p_y-p_z)r_x
=[1-2(p_y+p_z)]r_x,\\
r_y'&=(p_0-p_x+p_y-p_z)r_y
=[1-2(p_x+p_z)]r_y,\\
r_z'&=(p_0-p_x-p_y+p_z)r_z
=[1-2(p_x+p_y)]r_z.
\end{aligned}}
$$
