<h1 id="3/a/ii/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

A computational-basis [projective measurement](../../../../../../../../projective-measurement.md) has projections $P_0=|0\rangle\langle0|$ and $P_1=|1\rangle\langle1|$. Discarding its result produces the [nonselective projective measurement](../../../../../../../../nonselective-projective-measurement.md)

$$
\sigma=P_0\rho P_0+P_1\rho P_1
=\begin{pmatrix}|\alpha|^2&0\\0&|\beta|^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\sigma=\Lambda_{1/2}(\rho)}.
$$

Complete phase randomization and unread computational-basis measurement implement the same [dephasing](../../../../../../../../dephasing-channel.md) on this qubit.
