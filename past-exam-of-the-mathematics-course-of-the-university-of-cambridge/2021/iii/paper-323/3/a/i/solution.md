The finite-dimensional [Stinespring dilation](../../../../../../../stinespring-dilation.md) theorem states that every [quantum channel](../../../../../../../quantum-channel.md) $\Lambda:\mathcal B(H_A)\to\mathcal B(H_B)$ has an environment $H_E$ and an [isometry](../../../../../../../isometry.md) $V:H_A\to H_B\otimes H_E$ such that

$$
\boxed{\Lambda(\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}_E(V\rho V^\dagger)}.
$$

Conversely, every map of this form is completely positive and trace preserving. From [Kraus operators](../../../../../../../kraus-operator.md) $A_j$, one may take $V=\sum_jA_j\otimes|j\rangle_E$.
