With $|\Omega\rangle_{RA}=n^{-1/2}\sum_i|i\rangle_R|i\rangle_A$,

$$
|\Psi\rangle_{RA}
=\sqrt n(I_R\otimes\sqrt{\rho_A})|\Omega\rangle
=\sum_{i,j}(\sqrt{\rho_A})_{ji}|i\rangle_R|j\rangle_A.
$$

The [partial trace](../../../../../../../../partial-trace.md) over $R$ is

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_R|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|
=\sqrt{\rho_A}\sqrt{\rho_A}^{\,\dagger}
=\rho_A.
$$

Its norm is $\operatorname{Tr}\rho_A=1$. The [positive square root of an operator](../../../../../../../../positive-square-root-of-an-operator.md) $\sqrt{\rho_A}$ is unique, proving the claim.
