<h1 id="2/a/ii/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For

$$
|\Psi\rangle_{AR}
=\sum_i\sqrt{\lambda_i}|a_i\rangle_A|b_i\rangle_R,
$$

orthonormality of the $|b_i\rangle$ gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_R|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|
=\sum_i\lambda_i|a_i\rangle\langle a_i|
=\rho_A.
$$

Normalization follows from $\sum_i\lambda_i=1$, so this is a [purification of a density operator](../../../../../../../../purification-of-a-density-operator.md).
