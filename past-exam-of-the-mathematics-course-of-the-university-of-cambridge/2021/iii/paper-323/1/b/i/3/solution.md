↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The four [Bell states](../../../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) form an orthonormal basis, so their uniform mixture is

$$
\rho=\frac{I_4}{4}
=\frac{I_2}{2}\otimes\frac{I_2}{2}.
$$

It is a product state and therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{SN}(\rho)=1}.
$$
