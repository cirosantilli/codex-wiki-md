<h1 id="1/b/i/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The Bell state $|\Phi^+\rangle=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$ has two nonzero [Schmidt coefficients](../../../../../../../../schmidt-coefficient.md). Since the density operator is pure, every ensemble decomposition uses vectors in the same one-dimensional support, and hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{SN}(|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|)=2}.
$$
