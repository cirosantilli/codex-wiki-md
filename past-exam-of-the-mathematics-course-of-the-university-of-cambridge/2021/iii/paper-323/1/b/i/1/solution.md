↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The state is a convex combination of product density operators and is therefore a [separable quantum state](../../../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md). Every product density operator has a decomposition into product pure states, so

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{SN}(\rho)=1}.
$$
