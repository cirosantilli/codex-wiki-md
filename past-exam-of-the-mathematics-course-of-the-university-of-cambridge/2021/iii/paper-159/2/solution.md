A [minimal algebraic surface](../../../../../minimal-algebraic-surface.md) is a smooth projective surface containing no [exceptional curve of the first kind](../../../../../exceptional-curve-of-the-first-kind.md), namely no smooth rational curve of self-intersection $-1$. An [abelian surface](../../../../../abelian-surface.md) contains no rational curve because every morphism from $\mathbb P^1$ to an [abelian variety](../../../../../abelian-variety-split.md) is constant. It therefore has no $(-1)$-curve and is minimal.

For every $n\geq2$, the [Minimal Hirzebruch surface](../../../../../minimal-hirzebruch-surface.md) $\mathbb F_n$ is a rational minimal surface. These surfaces are pairwise nonisomorphic: the negative section is the unique irreducible curve of negative self-intersection and has square $-n$, so an isomorphism would recover $n$. Thus there are infinitely many nonisomorphic minimal rational surfaces.

A [K3 surface](../../../../../k3-surface.md) is a smooth projective surface $X$ with $K_X\cong\mathcal O_X$ and $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)=0$. For a smooth curve $C\subset X$ of [geometric genus](../../../../../geometric-genus.md) $g$, the [adjunction formula](../../../../../adjunction-formula.md) gives

$$
2g-2=C\cdot(C+K_X)=C^2,
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{C^2=2g-2}.
$$

An [elliptic surface](../../../../../elliptic-surface.md) is a smooth projective surface with a morphism to a smooth curve whose generic fiber is a smooth genus-one curve. If $E$ is an [elliptic curve](../../../../../elliptic-curve.md), projection

$$
\mathbb P^1\times E\longrightarrow\mathbb P^1
$$

is an elliptic fibration. Its canonical bundle is pulled back from $K_{\mathbb P^1}$, so every positive pluricanonical space vanishes and the [Kodaira dimension](../../../../../kodaira-dimension.md) is $-\infty$. This supplies the requested negative-Kodaira-dimension example.

For an elliptically fibered K3 surface, choose a smooth quartic $X\subseteq\mathbb P^3$ containing a line $L$. The [canonical bundle of a smooth projective hypersurface](../../../../../canonical-bundle-of-a-smooth-projective-hypersurface.md) formula makes $K_X$ trivial, and the standard cohomology sequence gives $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)=0$, so $X$ is K3. The pencil of planes through $L$ cuts $X$ into $L$ plus a residual plane cubic. The residual linear system $|H-L|$ is basepoint-free, has square zero and defines a morphism $X\to\mathbb P^1$ whose generic fiber is a smooth plane cubic. This is the [Elliptic K3 surface from a quartic containing a line](../../../../../elliptic-k3-surface-from-a-quartic-containing-a-line.md).

A [surface of general type](../../../../../surface-of-general-type.md) is a smooth projective surface of [Kodaira dimension](../../../../../kodaira-dimension.md) two. Let $B\subseteq\mathbb P^2$ be a smooth plane curve of degree eight and let

$$
\pi:X\longrightarrow\mathbb P^2
$$

be the degree-two cover branched along $B$. The branch-cover canonical-bundle formula gives

$$
K_X=\pi^*\left(K_{\mathbb P^2}+4H\right)=\pi^*H.
$$

This divisor is ample, so $X$ is a [double plane of general type](../../../../../double-plane-of-general-type.md) and $\pi$ is the required finite morphism of degree two.
