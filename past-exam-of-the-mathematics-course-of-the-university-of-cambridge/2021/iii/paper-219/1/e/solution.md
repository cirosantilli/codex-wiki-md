<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

At distance $r$, inclusion requires $L\geq4\pi r^2f_{\min}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{
\mathbb P(I=1\mid r)
=1-\Phi\left(\frac{4\pi r^2f_{\min}-L_0}{\sigma_L}\right)
=\Phi\left(\frac{L_0-4\pi r^2f_{\min}}{\sigma_L}\right)}.
$$

It equals $1/2$ when

$$
\boxed{r=\sqrt{\frac{L_0}{4\pi f_{\min}}}.}
$$
