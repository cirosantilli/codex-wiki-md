<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The expected number in a spherical shell is $4\pi r^2\rho_0e^{-r/r_0}dr$. Since

$$
\int_0^\infty4\pi r^2\rho_0e^{-r/r_0}dr=8\pi\rho_0r_0^3,
$$

the normalized radial density is the [Gamma distribution](../../../../../../gamma-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{p(r)=\frac{r^2}{2r_0^3}e^{-r/r_0}},\qquad r>0.
$$
