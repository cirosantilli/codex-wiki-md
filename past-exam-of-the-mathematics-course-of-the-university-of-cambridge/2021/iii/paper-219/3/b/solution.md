<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $D_s=M_s+\varepsilon_s$. Then

$$
\widetilde M_s-M_s=b\varepsilon_s+(1-b)(M_0-M_s).
$$

The terms are independent and centered, so

$$
\mathbb E(\widetilde M_s-M_s)^2
=b^2\sigma^2+(1-b)^2\tau^2
=\frac{\tau^2\sigma^2}{\tau^2+\sigma^2}
=b\sigma^2<\sigma^2.
$$

The unpooled estimate $D_s$ has mean squared error $\sigma^2$, so population shrinkage improves it.
