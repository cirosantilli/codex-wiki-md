<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Convolving the population and measurement distributions gives

$$
D_s\mid M_0,\tau^2\sim N(M_0,\tau^2+\sigma^2).
$$

Put $V=\tau^2+\sigma^2$. With a flat prior on $M_0$,

$$
\boxed{M_0\mid\tau^2,D\sim N(\bar D,V/N)}.
$$
