<h1 id="2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [Gravitational Hartree equation](../../../../../../gravitational-hartree-equation.md) can be written

$$
u_t=i\Delta u-i\phi u,
$$

where $\phi$ is real. Consequently

$$
\frac d{dt}\int_{\mathbb R^N}|u|^2\,dx
=2\operatorname{Re}\int_{\mathbb R^N}u_t\overline u\,dx
=2\operatorname{Re}\left(
i\int\Delta u\,\overline u-i\int\phi|u|^2
\right)=0,
$$

because both integrals multiplied by $i$ are purely imaginary after [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md). Hence [Hartree mass conservation](../../../../../../hartree-mass-and-energy-conservation.md) holds.
