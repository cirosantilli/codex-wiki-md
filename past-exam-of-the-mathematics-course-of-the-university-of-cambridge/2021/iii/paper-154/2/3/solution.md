<h1 id="2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

In three dimensions, the [Hardy–Littlewood–Sobolev inequality](../../../../../../hardy-littlewood-sobolev-inequality.md) applied to the Newtonian kernel gives

$$
\int_{\mathbb R^3}|\nabla\phi|^2
=-\int\phi|u|^2
\lesssim\||u|^2\|_{6/5}^2
=\|u\|_{12/5}^4.
$$

The [Gagliardo-Nirenberg interpolation inequality](../../../../../../gagliardo-nirenberg-interpolation-inequality.md) then gives

$$
\|u\|_{12/5}^4
\lesssim\|u\|_2^3\|\nabla u\|_2.
$$

Writing $X=\|\nabla u\|_2$ and using the conserved mass, conservation of energy implies

$$
E(u_0)\geq\frac12X^2-C(u_0)X.
$$

**Thus $X$ remains bounded. Together with the conserved $L^2$ norm this bounds $\|u(t)\|_{H^1}$, and the supplied blowup criterion proves [Global H1 solutions of the three-dimensional gravitational Hartree equation](../../../../../../global-h1-solutions-of-the-three-dimensional-gravitational-hartree-equation.md).**
