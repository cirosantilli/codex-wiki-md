<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a thin atmosphere in [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), take gravity $g$ and mean molecular mass $\mu m_H$ as constant. The [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) equation of state and hydrostatic balance give

$$
\frac{dP}{dz}=-\rho g
=-\frac{\mu m_Hg}{k_BT}P.
$$

For $P>1\ {\rm mbar}$,

$$
\frac{dT}{dP}=\frac{Ax}{P\ln10},
$$

so the [atmospheric lapse rate](../../../../../../lapse-rate-atmosphere.md) follows from the chain rule:

$$
\boxed{\frac{dT}{dz}
=-\frac{A\mu m_Hg}{k_BT\ln10}
\log_{10}\frac{P}{1\ {\rm mbar}}}.
$$

It vanishes in the assumed isothermal region above $1\ {\rm mbar}$. If natural logarithms are used instead, the factor $\ln10$ is absent and the fitted numerical value of $A$ changes accordingly.
