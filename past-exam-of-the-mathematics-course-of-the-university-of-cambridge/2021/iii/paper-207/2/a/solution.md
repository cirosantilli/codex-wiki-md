↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Education is a causal risk factor if an [intervention](../../../../../../treatment.md) that changes educational exposure changes the distribution of the corresponding [potential outcome](../../../../../../potential-outcome.md) for myopia. An observed [statistical association](../../../../../../statistical-association.md) need not have that interpretation. Shared causes such as socioeconomic circumstances, parental behavior, genetic traits, or preference for indoor near work can [confound](../../../../../../confounding.md) education and myopia. [Reverse causality](../../../../../../reverse-causality.md) is also possible: children with impaired distance vision may select more reading and indoor study, which can increase educational attainment without education causing the impairment.
