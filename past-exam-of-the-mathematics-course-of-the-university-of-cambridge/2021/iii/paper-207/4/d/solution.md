<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The quantities $v_i-\widetilde H(x_i)$ act as event-count residuals. Requiring their sum to vanish calibrates the fitted total number of events to the observed total, just as an intercept score equation calibrates fitted means. This is the aggregate zero-residual property of a [martingale residual](../../../../../../martingale-residual.md).
