The [canonical joint distribution for group sequential test statistics](../../../../../../../canonical-joint-distribution-for-group-sequential-test-statistics.md) is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}Z_1\\Z_2\end{pmatrix}
\sim N_2\left[
\begin{pmatrix}\delta\sqrt{I_1}\\ \delta\sqrt{I_2}\end{pmatrix},
\begin{pmatrix}
1&\sqrt{I_1/I_2}\\
\sqrt{I_1/I_2}&1
\end{pmatrix}
\right].
$$
