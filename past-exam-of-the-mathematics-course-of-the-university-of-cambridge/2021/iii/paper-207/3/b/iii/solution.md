↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\varphi_2(z;\mu,\Sigma)$ be the bivariate normal density with the mean and covariance from part ii. Continuing and then not rejecting means $f_1<Z_1<e_1$ and $Z_2<e_2$, so its probability is

$$
\boxed{
\int_{f_1}^{e_1}\int_{-\infty}^{e_2}
\varphi_2(z_1,z_2;\mu,\Sigma)\,dz_2\,dz_1}.
$$
