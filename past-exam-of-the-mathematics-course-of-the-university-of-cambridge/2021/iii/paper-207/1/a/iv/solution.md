After stroke, absence of a dementia effect on the death intensity is the restriction

$$
H_0:q_{35}=q_{45}.
$$

Fit the unrestricted model and the nested model subject to this equality, then use a [likelihood-ratio test](../../../../../../../likelihood-ratio-test.md). Under regularity conditions, twice the log-likelihood difference is asymptotically chi-squared with one degree of freedom.
