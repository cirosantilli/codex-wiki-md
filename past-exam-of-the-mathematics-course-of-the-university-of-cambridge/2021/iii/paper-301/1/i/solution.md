<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Translations produce the conserved symmetric tensor

$$
T^{\mu\nu}
=\partial^\mu\phi\,\partial^\nu\phi-\eta^{\mu\nu}\mathcal L,
\qquad \partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0.
$$

The conserved charges are

$$
\boxed{E=\int_{\mathbb R^3}d^3x\,T^{00}
=\int d^3x\left\{\frac12\dot\phi^2+\frac12|\nabla\phi|^2+V(\phi)\right\}},
$$

and

$$
\boxed{P^i=\int_{\mathbb R^3}d^3x\,T^{0i}}.
$$

Indeed,

$$
\frac d{dt}\int d^3x\,T^{0\nu}
=-\int d^3x\,\partial_iT^{i\nu}
=-\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{S_R}T^{i\nu}n_i\,dS=0
$$

when the field and its derivatives decay sufficiently rapidly.
