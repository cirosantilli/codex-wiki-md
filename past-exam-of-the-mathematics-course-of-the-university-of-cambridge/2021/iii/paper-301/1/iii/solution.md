<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Scale invariance requires the potential to obey

$$
\phi V'(\phi)=4V(\phi).
$$

Thus, apart from the zero choice,

$$
\boxed{V(\phi)=g\phi^4}
$$

for a dimensionless constant $g$; an additive constant is excluded because it introduces a scale in the action. The canonical [dilatation current](../../../../../../dilatation-current.md) is

$$
\boxed{D^\mu=x_\nu T^{\mu\nu}+\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi}.
$$

Using $\Box\phi+V'(\phi)=0$,

$$
\partial_\mu D^\mu
=4V-\phi V'=0.
$$

Equivalently, after improving the stress tensor to make it traceless, the current is $x_\nu T_{\rm imp}^{\mu\nu}$.
