↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Lorentz invariance gives the [Lorentz current](../../../../../../lorentz-current.md)

$$
M^{\mu\rho\sigma}
=x^\rho T^{\mu\sigma}-x^\sigma T^{\mu\rho},
\qquad \partial_\mu M^{\mu\rho\sigma}=0.
$$

An infinitesimal rotation through angle $\theta$ around the $z$-axis has

$$
\delta x^1=-\theta x^2,\qquad
\delta x^2=\theta x^1,\qquad
\delta x^0=\delta x^3=0,
$$

corresponding, up to the stated index convention, to the only nonzero components $\omega^{12}=-\omega^{21}=\theta$. Its conserved angular momentum is

$$
\boxed{J_z=\int d^3x\,M^{012}
=\int d^3x\,(x^1T^{02}-x^2T^{01})}.
$$
