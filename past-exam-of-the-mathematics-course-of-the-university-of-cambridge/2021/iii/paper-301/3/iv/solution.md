<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Under parity, $\overline\psi(t,\mathbf x)\mapsto
\overline\psi(t,-\mathbf x)\gamma^0$. Since  
$\gamma^0\gamma^0\gamma^0=\gamma^0$ and  
$\gamma^0\gamma^i\gamma^0=-\gamma^i$,

$$
\boxed{J_V^0(t,\mathbf x)\mapsto J_V^0(t,-\mathbf x),\qquad
J_V^i(t,\mathbf x)\mapsto-J_V^i(t,-\mathbf x)}.
$$

Moreover $\gamma^0\gamma^5\gamma^0=-\gamma^5$, so the [axial current](../../../../../../axial-current.md) is a pseudovector:

$$
\boxed{J_A^0(t,\mathbf x)\mapsto-J_A^0(t,-\mathbf x),\qquad
J_A^i(t,\mathbf x)\mapsto J_A^i(t,-\mathbf x)}.
$$
