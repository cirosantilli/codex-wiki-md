<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $x'=\Lambda x$, a [Dirac spinor](../../../../../../dirac-spinor.md) transforms as

$$
\psi'(x')=S[\Lambda]\psi(x).
$$

Hence

$$
\partial'_\mu\psi'(x')
=(\Lambda^{-1})^\nu{}_\mu S[\Lambda]\partial_\nu\psi(x).
$$

Using the result of part i, the [Dirac adjoint](../../../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) transforms as

$$
\boxed{\overline\psi'(x')=\overline\psi(x)S[\Lambda]^{-1}}.
$$
