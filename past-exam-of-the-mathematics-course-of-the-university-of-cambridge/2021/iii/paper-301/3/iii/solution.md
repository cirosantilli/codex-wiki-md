↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Dirac action](../../../../../../dirac-action.md) is

$$
S_D=\int d^4x\,\overline\psi(i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu-m)\psi.
$$

To first order,

$$
\Lambda^\mu{}_\nu
=\delta^\mu{}_\nu+\Omega^\mu{}_\nu,\qquad
S[\Lambda]=1+\frac12\Omega_{\rho\sigma}S^{\rho\sigma}.
$$

The commutator found in part i gives

$$
S[\Lambda]^{-1}\gamma^\mu S[\Lambda]
(\Lambda^{-1})^\nu{}_\mu=\gamma^\nu+O(\Omega^2).
$$

Together with $\det\Lambda=1$, the transformations in part ii therefore leave both the kinetic and mass terms invariant through first order. Thus $S_D'=S_D+O(\Omega^2)$.
