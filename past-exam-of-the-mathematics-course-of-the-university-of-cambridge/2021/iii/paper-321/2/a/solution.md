<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The three terms represent competing physical effects:

- $\Omega^2$ is the stabilizing [epicyclic motion](../../../../../../epicyclic-motion.md) of a radially displaced fluid element in a [Keplerian disk](../../../../../../keplerian-disk.md).
- $-2\pi G\Sigma|k|$ is the destabilizing self-gravity of a surface-density perturbation, obtained from the [razor-thin disk Poisson kernel](../../../../../../razor-thin-disk-poisson-kernel.md).
- $c_s^2k^2$ is the stabilizing pressure response, strongest at short wavelength.
