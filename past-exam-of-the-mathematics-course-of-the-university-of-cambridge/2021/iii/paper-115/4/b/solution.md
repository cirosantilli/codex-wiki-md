↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) is bilinear, alternating, satisfies the [Jacobi identity](../../../../../../jacobi-identity.md), and is natural under [diffeomorphisms](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md). Therefore the bracket of two [left-invariant vector fields](../../../../../../left-invariant-vector-field.md) is left-invariant. Define

$$
[\xi,\eta]=[l_\xi,l_\eta]_e.
$$

Then

$$
l_{[\xi,\eta]}=[l_\xi,l_\eta],
$$

and the inherited bilinearity, alternation, and Jacobi identity make $T_eG$ a [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md).
