↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) is a [torsion-free connection](../../../../../../torsion-free-connection.md). Its [torsion form](../../../../../../torsion-form.md) is

$$
T(u,v)=\nabla_uv-\nabla_vu-[u,v].
$$

Since $T=0$,

$$
\nabla_uv-\nabla_vu=[u,v].
$$

In coordinates this is the symmetry $\Gamma^i_{jk}=\Gamma^i_{kj}$ of the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md).
