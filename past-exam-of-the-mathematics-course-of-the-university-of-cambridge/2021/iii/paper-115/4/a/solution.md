<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

As a vector space, the [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) of $G$ is $\mathfrak g=T_eG$. For $\xi\in\mathfrak g$, define

$$
l_\xi(g)=(dL_g)_e\xi,
$$

where $L_g$ is [Left translation on a Lie group](../../../../../../left-and-right-translation-on-a-lie-group.md). This vector field is smooth and left-invariant because $(dL_h)_g l_\xi(g)=l_\xi(hg)$. The assignment $\xi\mapsto l_\xi$ is linear and injective by evaluation at $e$. Conversely, every left-invariant vector field $V$ satisfies $V(g)=(dL_g)_eV(e)$, so it equals $l_{V(e)}$. Hence the map is an isomorphism.
