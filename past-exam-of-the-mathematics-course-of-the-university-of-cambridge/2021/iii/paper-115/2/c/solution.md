<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Each $U_j$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb C$ and is therefore contractible. Every [complex line bundle](../../../../../../complex-line-bundle.md) over a contractible paracompact space is trivial, so $L|_{U_0}$ and $L|_{U_1}$ admit the required trivializations.
