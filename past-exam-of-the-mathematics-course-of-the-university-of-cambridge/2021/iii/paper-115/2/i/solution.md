<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Changing the two local frames by the nowhere-zero functions $\psi_j$ replaces the transition function $f$ by

$$
\frac{\psi_1}{\psi_0}f=z^k.
$$

With the standard convention that the [tautological bundle](../../../../../../tautological-bundle.md) $\mathcal O_{\mathbb{CP}^1}(-1)$ has transition function $z$, its $k$th tensor power $\mathcal O_{\mathbb{CP}^1}(-k)$ has transition function $z^k$. Thus

$$
L\cong\mathcal O_{\mathbb{CP}^1}(-k).
$$

This is the [smooth classification of complex line bundles on the complex projective line](../../../../../../smooth-classification-of-complex-line-bundles-on-the-complex-projective-line.md) by their winding number.
