<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Parametrize the unit circle by a loop $\gamma:[0,2\pi]\to\mathbb C^*$. Part (d) and $f(\gamma(0))=f(\gamma(2\pi))$ imply

$$
\exp\left(-\int_{S^1}\beta\right)=1.
$$

The kernel of the [complex exponential function](../../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) is $2\pi i\mathbb Z$, so

$$
\int_{S^1}\beta=2\pi i k
$$

for some $k\in\mathbb Z$. This integer is the [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md) of $f|_{S^1}$.
