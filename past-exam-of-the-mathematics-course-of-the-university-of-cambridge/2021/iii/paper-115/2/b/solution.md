<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The overlap $U_0\cap U_1$ is $\mathbb C^*$ in the coordinate $z$, and radial projection $z\mapsto z/|z|$ is a [deformation retraction](../../../../../../deformation-retraction.md) onto $S^1$. The [homotopy invariance of de Rham cohomology](../../../../../../homotopy-invariance-of-de-rham-cohomology.md) identifies its first de Rham cohomology with that of $S^1$, and the identification is compatible with integration around the unit circle. Hence the same integral map is an isomorphism.
