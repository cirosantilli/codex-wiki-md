<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For $h=z^{-k}f$,

$$
\alpha=h^{-1}dh=\beta-kz^{-1}dz.
$$

The scalar-valued form $h^{-1}dh$ is closed, and

$$
\int_{S^1}\alpha=2\pi ik-k\int_{S^1}z^{-1}dz=0.
$$

By the period isomorphism from part (b), $[\alpha]=0$ in complexified [de Rham cohomology](../../../../../../de-rham-cohomology.md). Hence there is a smooth complex-valued function $\varphi$ on $U_0\cap U_1$ with $\alpha=d\varphi$.
