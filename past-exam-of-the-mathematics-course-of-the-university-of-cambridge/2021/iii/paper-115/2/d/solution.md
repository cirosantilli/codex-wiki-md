<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Since $\beta=f^{-1}df$,

$$
\frac d{dt}f(\gamma(t))
=f(\gamma(t))\,\beta_{\gamma(t)}(\dot\gamma(t)).
$$

The [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) and the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) then give

$$
F'(t)
=e^{-\int_0^t\gamma^*\beta}
\left(\frac d{dt}f(\gamma(t))-f(\gamma(t))\beta(\dot\gamma(t))\right)=0.
$$

**Thus $F$ is constant.**
