An [orientable smooth manifold](../../../../../../orientable-smooth-manifold.md) is a smooth $n$-manifold that admits a smoothly varying orientation of its tangent spaces. Equivalently, it has an atlas whose coordinate-transition [Jacobian determinants](../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) are positive, or a nowhere-vanishing smooth top-degree [differential form](../../../../../../differential-form-split.md).
