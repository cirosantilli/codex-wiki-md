<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On $S^n\times S^n$, consider $q(x,y)=\langle x,y\rangle$. At a point of $q^{-1}(0)$, its differential is

$$
dq_{(x,y)}(u,v)=\langle u,y\rangle+\langle x,v\rangle.
$$

The tangent vector $(y,x)$ lies in $T_xS^n\oplus T_yS^n$ and has $dq(y,x)=2$, so zero is a [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md). The [regular level set theorem](../../../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md) makes $P=q^{-1}(0)$ a smooth submanifold. Its tangent space is

$$
\boxed{T_{(x,y)}P=\left\{(u,v):
\langle u,x\rangle=0,
\ \langle v,y\rangle=0,
\ \langle u,y\rangle+\langle x,v\rangle=0
\right\}.}
$$
