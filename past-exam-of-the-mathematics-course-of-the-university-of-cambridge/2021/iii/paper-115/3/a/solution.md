A [principal connection](../../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) on $P$ is a $G$-equivariant smooth splitting

$$
T_pP=H_p\oplus V_p,
$$

where $V_p$ is tangent to the $G$-orbit. Equivalently, it is a $\mathfrak g$-valued one-form $\mathcal A$ satisfying $\mathcal A(\xi_P)=\xi$ and $R_g^*\mathcal A=\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\mathcal A$. Its [curvature of a principal connection](../../../../../../curvature-of-a-principal-connection.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal F=d\mathcal A+\frac12[\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal A].}
$$
