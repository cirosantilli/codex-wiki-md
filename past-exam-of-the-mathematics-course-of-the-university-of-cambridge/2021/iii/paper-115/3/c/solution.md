<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The points $(x,y)$ are ordered orthonormal two-frames, so $P$ is the [Stiefel manifold](../../../../../../stiefel-manifold.md) $V_2(\mathbb R^{n+1})$. Changing an orthonormal basis of the same plane gives the displayed free right $O(2)$-action. Since $O(2)$ is compact, the action is proper, and the quotient is the [Grassmannian](../../../../../../grassmannian.md) of unoriented two-planes. The [Free proper Lie-group action theorem](../../../../../../free-proper-lie-group-action-theorem.md) makes

$$
P\longrightarrow P/O(2)
$$

a principal $O(2)$-bundle.
