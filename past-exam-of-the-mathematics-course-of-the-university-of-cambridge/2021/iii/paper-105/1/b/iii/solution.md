<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The initial line $x=0$ has conormal $dx$, and the [principal symbol](../../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) on this conormal is $1$. It is therefore a [non-characteristic hypersurface](../../../../../../../non-characteristic-hypersurface.md) at every $(0,t_0)$. The equation's coefficients and the prescribed [Cauchy data](../../../../../../../cauchy-data.md) $u(0,t)=\cos t$ and $u_x(0,t)=0$ are [real analytic functions](../../../../../../../real-analytic-function.md). The [Cauchy-Kovalevskaya theorem](../../../../../../../cauchy-kovalevskaya-theorem.md) consequently gives a unique analytic solution in a neighbourhood of $(0,t_0)$ for every

$$
\boxed{t_0\in\mathbb R.}
$$
