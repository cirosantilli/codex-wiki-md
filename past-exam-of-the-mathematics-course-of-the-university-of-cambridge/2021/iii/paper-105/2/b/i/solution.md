The [Holder inequality](../../../../../../../holder-inequality.md) interpolates between $L^2(U)$ and $L^6(U)$:

$$
\|v\|_{L^3}^3
=\int_U|v|^{3/2}|v|^{3/2}
\leq\|v\|_{L^2}^{3/2}\|v\|_{L^6}^{3/2}.
$$

Taking cube roots and applying the three-dimensional [Sobolev inequality](../../../../../../../sobolev-inequality.md) $H^1(U)\hookrightarrow L^6(U)$ gives

$$
\|v\|_{L^3(U)}
\leq C\|v\|_{L^2(U)}^{1/2}\|v\|_{H^1(U)}^{1/2}.
$$

This is the [H1 L3 interpolation inequality in three dimensions](../../../../../../../h1-l3-interpolation-inequality-in-three-dimensions.md).
