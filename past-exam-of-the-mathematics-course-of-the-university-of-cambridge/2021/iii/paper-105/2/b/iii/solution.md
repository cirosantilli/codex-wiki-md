<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

After passing to a subsequence, weak compactness and the [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](../../../../../../../rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) give

$$
u_{n_k}\rightharpoonup u\quad\hbox{in }H^1(U),
\qquad
u_{n_k}\longrightarrow u\quad\hbox{in }L^3(U).
$$

For fixed $w\in H^1(U)$, the [Sobolev inequality](../../../../../../../sobolev-inequality.md) gives $w\in L^6(U)$, so

$$
u_{n_k}w\longrightarrow uw\quad\hbox{in }L^2(U).
$$

Meanwhile $\partial_j u_{n_k}\rightharpoonup\partial_j u$ in $L^2(U)$. Pairing this weak convergence with the strong convergence of the products, or equivalently using the [weak-strong product convergence lemma](../../../../../../../weak-strong-product-convergence-lemma.md), yields

$$
\boxed{\int_U(\partial_xu_{n_k}+\partial_yu_{n_k}+\partial_zu_{n_k})u_{n_k}w
\longrightarrow
\int_U(\partial_xu+\partial_yu+\partial_zu)uw.}
$$
