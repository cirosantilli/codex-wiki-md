<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [radiative diffusion in a star](../../../../../../radiative-diffusion-in-a-star.md) equation gives the homology scaling

$$
L\propto\frac{R T_c^4}{\kappa\rho_c}.
$$

Using the central scalings from part (i) and the electron-scattering opacity $\kappa\propto1+X$ yields

$$
\boxed{L\propto\frac{\mu^4}{1+X}M^3}.
$$

The radius cancels because an electron-scattering opacity is independent of density and temperature.
