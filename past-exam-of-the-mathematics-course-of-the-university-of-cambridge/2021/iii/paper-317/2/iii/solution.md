<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A fully convective monatomic [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) is an [adiabatic stellar polytrope](../../../../../../adiabatic-stellar-polytrope.md) with index $n=3/2$. Its [polytropic mass-radius relation](../../../../../../polytropic-mass-radius-relation.md) gives

$$
K=\frac{P}{\rho^{5/3}}\propto GM^{1/3}R.
$$

At the base of a thin [grey atmosphere](../../../../../../grey-atmosphere.md), the [stellar surface boundary condition](../../../../../../stellar-surface-boundary-condition.md) and electron-scattering opacity give

$$
P_{\rm ph}\propto\frac{g}{\kappa}
\propto\frac{M}{R^2(1+X)}.
$$

The ideal-gas equation evaluated on the convective adiabat gives

$$
K\propto\left(\frac{T_{\rm eff}}{\mu}\right)^{5/3}
P_{\rm ph}^{-2/3}.
$$

Combining these relations,

$$
T_{\rm eff}\propto
\frac{\mu M^{3/5}}{(1+X)^{2/5}R^{1/5}}.
$$

The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{L\propto
\frac{\mu^4}{(1+X)^{8/5}}M^{12/5}R^{6/5}}.
$$
