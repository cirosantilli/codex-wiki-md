<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a homologous stellar sequence, [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md), [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), and the [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) equation give

$$
\rho_c\propto\frac M{R^3},
\qquad
P_c\propto\frac{GM^2}{R^4},
\qquad
T_c\propto\frac{\mu M}{R}.
$$

Integrating the given [stellar energy-generation rate](../../../../../../stellar-energy-generation-rate.md) over a fixed dimensionless profile gives

$$
L\propto\epsilon_cM
\propto X\rho_cT_c^{13}M.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{L\propto X\mu^{13}\frac{M^{15}}{R^{16}}}.
$$
