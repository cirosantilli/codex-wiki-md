<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At the encounter radius $r=a_p$, the planet's [circular Kepler orbit](../../../../../../circular-kepler-orbit.md) has speed

$$
v_p^2=\frac{GM_*}{a_p}.
$$

The comet's speed follows from the [vis-viva equation](../../../../../../vis-viva-equation.md):

$$
v_c^2=GM_*\left(\frac2{a_p}-\frac1{a_c}\right)
=v_p^2\left(2-\frac{a_p}{a_c}\right).
$$

Its component parallel to the planet's prograde tangential velocity is fixed by its [specific angular momentum](../../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md),

$$
v_{c,t}=\frac{\sqrt{GM_*a_c(1-e_c^2)}}{a_p}
=v_p\sqrt{\frac{a_c}{a_p}(1-e_c^2)}.
$$

The [relative velocity](../../../../../../relative-velocity.md) therefore satisfies

$$
\Delta v_{pc}^2=v_c^2+v_p^2-2v_pv_{c,t},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{\Delta v_{pc}=v_p\left[3-\frac{a_p}{a_c}
-2\sqrt{\frac{a_c}{a_p}(1-e_c^2)}\right]^{1/2}}.
$$
