<h1 id="1/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The limiting trajectory grazes the planet at [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md) $R_p$. Equating the asymptotic and periapsis values of [specific angular momentum](../../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) and using the [vis-viva equation](../../../../../../vis-viva-equation.md) gives

$$
b_{\min}^2\Delta v_{pc}^2
=R_p^2\left(\Delta v_{pc}^2+\frac{2GM_p}{R_p}\right).
$$

Hence the comet avoids the planet when

$$
\boxed{b>b_{\min}=R_p\sqrt{1+\frac{2GM_p}{R_p\Delta v_{pc}^2}}}.
$$
