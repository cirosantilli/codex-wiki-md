<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the relative position $\mathbf r=\mathbf r_1-\mathbf r_2$, [Newton's law of universal gravitation](../../../../../../newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation.md) gives the [two-body problem](../../../../../../two-body-problem.md) in its reduced one-body form,

$$
\ddot{\mathbf r}=-\frac{\mu}{r^3}\mathbf r,
\qquad
\mu=G(M_1+M_2).
$$

Taking the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with the relative velocity $\mathbf v=\dot{\mathbf r}$ gives

$$
\frac d{dt}\left(\frac12v^2\right)
=-\frac{\mu}{r^3}\mathbf r\mathbin{\cdot}\dot{\mathbf r}
=\frac d{dt}\left(\frac\mu r\right).
$$

Integration therefore yields conservation of [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac12v^2-\frac\mu r=C}.
$$
