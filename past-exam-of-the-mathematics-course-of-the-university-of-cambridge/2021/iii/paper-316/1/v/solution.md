<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For some orbital phase of the moon to permit an impact, the comet's planetocentric [hyperbolic Kepler orbit](../../../../../../hyperbolic-kepler-orbit.md) must at least cross the moon's circular orbit. At the limiting case $a_m$ is the comet's [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md), so the radial velocity in part (iv) vanishes. Consequently

$$
\boxed{b_{\max}=\frac{a_m}{\Delta v_{pc}}
\sqrt{\Delta v_{pc}^2+2v_m^2}
=a_m\sqrt{1+\frac{2v_m^2}{\Delta v_{pc}^2}}}.
$$

This is [gravitational focusing](../../../../../../gravitational-focusing.md) by the planet; an actual collision additionally requires the moon to occupy the crossing point at the right time.
