<h1 id="2/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

The same absorption and re-emission that produce radial [radiation pressure](../../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) also produce [Poynting–Robertson drag](../../../../../../poynting-robertson-drag.md). This velocity-dependent force removes [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) and [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md), destroys the exact [Jacobi constant](../../../../../../jacobi-constant.md), and turns the formal equilibrium points into slowly drifting configurations. [Stellar-wind drag](../../../../../../stellar-wind-drag.md) can provide a similar correction.
