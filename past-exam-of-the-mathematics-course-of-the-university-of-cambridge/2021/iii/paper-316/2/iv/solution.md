<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Define

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{\mu_1(1-\beta_1)}{r_1^3}+\frac{\mu_2}{r_2^3}}.
$$

At an [equilibrium point](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), the velocity and acceleration vanish. Direct differentiation gives

$$
U_z=-Az,
\qquad
U_y=(1-A)y,
$$

and

$$
U_x=(1-A)x-\mu_1\mu_2\left[(1-\beta_1)r_1^{-3}-r_2^{-3}\right].
$$

Thus the equilibrium conditions are

$$
\boxed{Az=0,
\qquad
(1-A)y=0,
\qquad
(1-A)x=\mu_1\mu_2\left[(1-\beta_1)r_1^{-3}-r_2^{-3}\right]}.
$$
