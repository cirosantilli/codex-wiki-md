<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Multiply the three equations by $\dot x,\dot y,\dot z$ and add. The [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md) does no work because its two terms cancel, leaving

$$
\frac d{dt}\left[\frac12(\dot x^2+\dot y^2+\dot z^2)-U\right]=0.
$$

Equivalently, the radiation-modified [Jacobi constant](../../../../../../jacobi-constant.md) is

$$
\boxed{C_J=2U-(\dot x^2+\dot y^2+\dot z^2)=\text{constant}}.
$$
