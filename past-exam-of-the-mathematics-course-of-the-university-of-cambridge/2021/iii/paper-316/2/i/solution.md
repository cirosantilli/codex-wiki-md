<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The unit binary separation and unit [mean motion](../../../../../../mean-motion.md) imply through [Kepler third law](../../../../../../kepler-s-third-law.md) that

$$
\mu_1+\mu_2=1.
$$

The [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) conditions put $M_1$ at $x_1=-\mu_2$ and $M_2$ at $x_2=\mu_1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{r_1=\sqrt{(x+\mu_2)^2+y^2+z^2},
\qquad
r_2=\sqrt{(x-\mu_1)^2+y^2+z^2}}.
$$
