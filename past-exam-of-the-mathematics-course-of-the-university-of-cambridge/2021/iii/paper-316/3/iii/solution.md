<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

First follow the expression printed in the paper. Put $e=1-\delta$ and compare its numerator with $(1-\beta)^2$. To first order in $\delta$,

$$
e_d^2-1
=\frac{\delta}{(1-\beta)^2}
\left[-2(1-\beta)+\frac{4\beta a}{r}\right]+O(\delta^2).
$$

Thus the grain is unbound, $e_d\geq1$, when

$$
r\lesssim\frac{2\beta a}{1-\beta}.
$$

Since the cometary [apoapsis](../../../../../../apoapsis.md) is $Q=a(1+e)=2a+O(\delta a)$,

$$
\boxed{r_{\rm blow}\sim\frac{Q\beta}{1-\beta}}.
$$

For completeness, the energy and angular-momentum-consistent formula derived in part (ii) instead gives $r_{\rm blow}=2\beta a\sim Q\beta$. The distinction is not a matter of approximation: it exposes the typographical inconsistency in the question.
