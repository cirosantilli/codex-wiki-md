<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The expansion separates naturally into three timescales:

- A [short-period orbital perturbation](../../../../../../short-period-orbital-perturbation.md) contains rapidly varying [mean longitudes](../../../../../../mean-longitude.md). It describes individual conjunction kicks and usually averages to zero away from a commensurability.
- A [resonant orbital perturbation](../../../../../../resonant-orbital-perturbation.md) has a nearly stationary integer combination of mean longitudes near a [mean-motion resonance](../../../../../../mean-motion-resonance.md). Successive conjunctions then occur at correlated phases, so their effects accumulate and the [resonant argument](../../../../../../resonant-argument.md) may librate.
- A [secular perturbation](../../../../../../secular-perturbation.md) survives averaging over both mean longitudes. It drives slow [apsidal precession](../../../../../../apsidal-precession.md), [nodal precession](../../../../../../nodal-precession.md), and exchange of [orbital eccentricity](../../../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md) or [orbital inclination](../../../../../../orbital-inclination.md) over many orbital periods.

Short-period terms matter for instantaneous osculating elements and encounters, resonant terms near period commensurabilities, and secular terms for long-term evolution away from strong resonance.
