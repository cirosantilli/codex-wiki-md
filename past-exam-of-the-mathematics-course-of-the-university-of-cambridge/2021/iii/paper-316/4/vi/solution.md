<h1 id="4/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

For $q=2$, at conjunction

$$
\phi=2(\lambda_c-\Omega_2).
$$

The stable geometry puts conjunctions a quarter orbit from either node, where $M_2$ has maximum vertical separation from the inner body's plane and close approaches are avoided. Therefore $\lambda_c-\Omega_2=\pi/2$ or $3\pi/2$, and both possibilities give

$$
\boxed{\phi\ \hbox{librating about}\ \pi\pmod{2\pi}}.
$$

This is [resonance protection](../../../../../../resonance-protection.md): the resonant phase moves conjunctions away from the two node crossings, where the physical separation would be smallest.
