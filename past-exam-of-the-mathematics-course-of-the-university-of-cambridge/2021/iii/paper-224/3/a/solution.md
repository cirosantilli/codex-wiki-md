The [three-point identity for relative entropy](../../../../../../three-point-identity-for-relative-entropy.md) is

$$
\boxed{D(P\Vert Q)=D(P\Vert R)+D(R\Vert Q)
+\sum_a\{P(a)-R(a)\}\log\frac{R(a)}{Q(a)}}.
$$

It follows by expanding and collecting logarithms. When the final sum vanishes, it is the [Pythagorean identity for relative entropy](../../../../../../pythagorean-identity-for-relative-entropy.md).
