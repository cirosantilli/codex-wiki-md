<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $A'=\{a:P'(a)>0\}$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{a\in A'}P'(a)\left(1-\frac{P(a)Q^0(a)}{P'(a)Q^*(a)}\right)
&=1-\sum_{a\in A'}\frac{P(a)Q^0(a)}{Q^*(a)}\\
&\geq1-\sum_{a\in A}\frac{P(a)Q^0(a)}{Q^*(a)}\geq0,
\end{aligned}
$$

using nonnegativity and part i.
