[Stein's lemma](../../../../../../stein-s-lemma-information-theory.md) states that for testing $P^{\otimes n}$ against $Q^{\otimes n}$, the smallest [Type II error](../../../../../../type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) $\beta_n$ among tests with [Type I error](../../../../../../type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) at most any fixed $0<\varepsilon<1$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}-\frac1n\log\beta_n=D(P\Vert Q).}
$$
