<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Repeated formal addition gives

$$
[n]_{\widehat{\mathbb G}_a}(X)=nX,
\qquad
[n]_{\widehat{\mathbb G}_m}(X)=(1+X)^n-1
$$

for positive $n$, and the same formulas extend to all $n\in\mathbb Z$ using the [formal inverse](../../../../../../formal-inverse.md).

Now let $R$ be a field of characteristic $p>0$ and let $h:\widehat{\mathbb G}_a\to\widehat{\mathbb G}_m$ be a homomorphism. Compatibility with multiplication by $p$ gives

$$
h([p]_{\widehat{\mathbb G}_a}(X))
=[p]_{\widehat{\mathbb G}_m}(h(X)).
$$

The left side is $h(0)=0$, while the Frobenius identity gives the right side as

$$
(1+h(X))^p-1=h(X)^p.
$$

The [formal power series ring](../../../../../../formal-power-series.md) over a field is an [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md), so $h(X)^p=0$ implies $h=0$. Hence there are no nonzero homomorphisms from the formal additive group to the formal multiplicative group.
