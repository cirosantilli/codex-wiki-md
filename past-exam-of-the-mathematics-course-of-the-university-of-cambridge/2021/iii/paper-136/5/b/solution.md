The [formal additive group](../../../../../../formal-additive-group.md) and [formal multiplicative group](../../../../../../formal-multiplicative-group.md) have laws

$$
\widehat{\mathbb G}_a(X,Y)=X+Y,
\qquad
\widehat{\mathbb G}_m(X,Y)=X+Y+XY.
$$

If $R$ is an [algebra over a field](../../../../../../algebra-over-a-field.md) whose scalar field is $\mathbb Q$, the series

$$
h(X)=\exp(X)-1
$$

has linear coefficient one and satisfies

$$
h(X+Y)=h(X)+h(Y)+h(X)h(Y).
$$

It is therefore the [exponential isomorphism between the additive and multiplicative formal groups](../../../../../../exponential-isomorphism-between-the-additive-and-multiplicative-formal-groups.md), with inverse $\log(1+X)$.
