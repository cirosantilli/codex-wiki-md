<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ and normalize $v_L$. The lower [ramification groups](../../../../../../../ramification-group.md) are

$$
G_s(L/K)=\{\sigma\in G:v_L(\sigma(x)-x)\geq s+1
\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal O_L\},
\qquad s\geq0,
$$

with $G_{-1}=G$. In particular $G_0$ is the [inertia group](../../../../../../../inertia-group.md), and $G_1$ is the [wild inertia group](../../../../../../../wild-inertia-group.md). When the extension is totally ramified, the [uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups](../../../../../../../uniformizer-criterion-for-lower-ramification-groups.md) permits the equivalent test on one uniformizer.
