**No.** In particular, conjugate subgroups $H'=xHx^{-1}$ produce the same weak irrep-label distribution. Under an irrep, the subgroup average

$$
\sum_{h'\in H'}M_\alpha(h')
=M_\alpha(x)\left(\sum_{h\in H}M_\alpha(h)\right)M_\alpha(x)^{-1}
$$

is related by unitary similarity, so its squared [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](../../../../../../../hilbert-schmidt-norm.md), and hence every $\Pi_H(\alpha)$, is unchanged. Weak Fourier sampling can therefore fail to identify even distinct subgroups of a non-abelian group.
