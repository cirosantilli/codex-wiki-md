↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The process is classically strongly efficiently simulatable if a deterministic classical algorithm can compute the probability of any specified $K$-bit output string to requested additive precision $2^{-m}$ in time polynomial in $n$, the circuit description, and $m$. This is [strong classical simulation of a quantum circuit](../../../../../../strong-classical-simulation-of-a-quantum-circuit.md), and is stronger than merely sampling its output.
