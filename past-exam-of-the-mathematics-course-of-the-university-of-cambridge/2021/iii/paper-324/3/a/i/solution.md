<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $P_G$ project onto the good subspace and write

$$
|\psi\rangle=\sin\theta|\psi_G\rangle
+\cos\theta|\psi_B\rangle.
$$

The [amplitude amplification theorem](../../../../../../../amplitude-amplification.md) says that alternating the reflection $I-2P_G$ with the reflection $2|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|-I$ rotates this two-dimensional plane by $2\theta$. After $j$ amplification iterations the good probability is

$$
\boxed{\sin^2((2j+1)\theta)}.
$$
