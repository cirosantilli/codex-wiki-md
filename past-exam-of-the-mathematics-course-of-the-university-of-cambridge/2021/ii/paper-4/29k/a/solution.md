↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) starts at zero, has continuous paths, and has independent increments with $W_t-W_s\sim N(0,t-s)$. The stated covariance makes every finite collection jointly Gaussian; disjoint increments have zero covariance and hence are independent. Their variances are interval lengths, proving all defining properties.
