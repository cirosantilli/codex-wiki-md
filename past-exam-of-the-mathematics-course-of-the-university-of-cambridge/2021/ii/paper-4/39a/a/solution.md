<h1 id="39a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the shock frame, conservation of mass, normal momentum, and energy gives

$$
ρ_1U=ρ_2(U-u_2),
$$



$$
p_1+ρ_1U^2=p_2+ρ_2(U-u_2)^2,
$$



$$
h_1+U^2/2=h_2+(U-u_2)^2/2.
$$

These are the [Rankine-Hugoniot relations](../../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md).
