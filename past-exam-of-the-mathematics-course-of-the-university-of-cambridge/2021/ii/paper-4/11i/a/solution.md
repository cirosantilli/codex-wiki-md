<h1 id="11i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

$N$ is a [Fermat pseudoprime](../../../../../../fermat-pseudoprime.md) to base $b$ when it is composite, coprime to $b$, and $b^{N-1}\equiv1\pmod N$. Here $N\mid b^k-1$ gives coprimality and $k\mid N-1$, so $b^{N-1}=(b^k)^{(N-1)/k}\equiv1\pmod N$.
