<h1 id="37c/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Contracting the [Ricci tensor](../../../../../../../ricci-tensor.md) gives the [Ricci scalar](../../../../../../../ricci-scalar.md)

$$
R=6\left(\frac{\ddot a}{a}+H^2\right),
\qquad H=\frac{\dot a}{a}.
$$

Hence the time-time component of the [Einstein tensor](../../../../../../../einstein-tensor.md) is $G_{00}=R_{00}-\tfrac12g_{00}R=3H^2$. A comoving [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) has $T_{00}=\rho$, so the time-time [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) give the flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md)

$$
\boxed{H^2=\frac{8\pi}{3}\rho+\frac{\Lambda}{3}}.
$$
