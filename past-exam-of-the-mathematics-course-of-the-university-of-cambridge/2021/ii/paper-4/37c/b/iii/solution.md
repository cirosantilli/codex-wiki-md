<h1 id="37c/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a [null vector](../../../../../../../null-vector.md), the [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) tensor gives

$$
T_{\alpha\beta}V^\alpha V^\beta
=(\rho+P)(u_\alpha V^\alpha)^2,
$$

because $g_{\alpha\beta}V^\alpha V^\beta=0$. For [phantom energy](../../../../../../../phantom-energy.md), $\rho+P=(1+w)\rho<0$. A nonzero $u_\alpha V^\alpha$ therefore makes the contraction negative, so phantom energy violates the [null energy condition](../../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md).
