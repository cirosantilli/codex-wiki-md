<h1 id="37c/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Separate [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) and the constant [equation-of-state parameter](../../../../../../../equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w$ give the [constant-equation-of-state density scaling](../../../../../../../constant-equation-of-state-density-scaling.md)

$$
\rho_{\rm ph}=\rho_{{\rm ph},0}a^{-3(1+w)}.
$$

Because $w<-1$, the [phantom energy](../../../../../../../phantom-energy.md) density grows with the [scale factor](../../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md), whereas the [pressureless matter](../../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) density decays as $a^{-3}$. Phantom energy therefore dominates at late times. With $\Lambda=0$, the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) becomes

$$
\frac{\dot a}{a}=C a^{-3(1+w)/2},
\qquad C=\sqrt{\frac{8\pi\rho_{{\rm ph},0}}3}.
$$

Integrating and absorbing the integration constant into the finite time $t^*$ gives

$$
\boxed{a(t)\sim
\left[-\frac32(1+w)C(t^*-t)\right]^{2/[3(1+w)]}}.
$$

The exponent is negative, so $a(t)\to\infty$ as $t\to t^{*-}$. This is a [Big Rip](../../../../../../../big-rip.md).
