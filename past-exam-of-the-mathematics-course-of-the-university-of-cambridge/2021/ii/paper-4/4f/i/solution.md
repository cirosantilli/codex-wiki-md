The [pumping lemma for regular languages](../../../../../../pumping-lemma-for-regular-languages.md) says that sufficiently long accepted words split as $xyz$ with $|xy|\leq P$, $|y|>0$, and $xy^kz$ accepted for all $k\geq0$. Applying it to $0^P1^P01$ pumps only zeros, so language (i) is not regular.
