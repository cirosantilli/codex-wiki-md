<h1 id="2h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

On the simplex define $f(x)=Ax/(\mathbf1^TAx)$. Strict positivity makes the denominator positive and maps the simplex continuously into its interior. By [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md), $f(x)=x$ for some $x$. Thus $Ax=λx$, where $λ=\mathbf1^TAx>0$, and because $Ax$ is strictly positive, so is $x$.
