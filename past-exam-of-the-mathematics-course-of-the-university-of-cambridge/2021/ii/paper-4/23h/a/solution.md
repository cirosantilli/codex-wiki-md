<h1 id="23h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Strong convergence means $\|f_j-f\|_p\to0$; weak convergence means $\int f_jh\to\int fh$ for every $h\in L^q$. By duality choose $h$ of norm one with $\int fh$ arbitrarily close to $\|f\|_p$. [Holder inequality](../../../../../../holder-inequality.md) and passage to the limit give $\|f\|_p\leq\liminf\|f_j\|_p$.
