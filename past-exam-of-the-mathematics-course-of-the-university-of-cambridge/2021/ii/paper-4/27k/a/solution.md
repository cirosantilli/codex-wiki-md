↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Condition on the first short time interval. A jump contributes the generator term $Qf$, while local time grows at unit speed in the current coordinate and contributes $\partial_{\ell_i}f$. The [Markov property](../../../../../../markov-property.md) and division by the interval length therefore give $\partial_tf_t=Mf_t$.
