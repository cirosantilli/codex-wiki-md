<h1 id="14e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The homogeneous equilibria are $0,r,1$. Since $f\prime(0)=-r<0$, $f\prime(1)=r-1<0$, and $f\prime(r)=r(1-r)>0$, the first and last are linearly stable and the middle one unstable.
