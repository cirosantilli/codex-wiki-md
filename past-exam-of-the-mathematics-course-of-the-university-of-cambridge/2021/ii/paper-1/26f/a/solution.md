<h1 id="26f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For each $p\in S$, the [first fundamental form](../../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) is defined without coordinates by

$$
I_p(v,w)=v\mathbin{\cdot}w,
\qquad v,w\in T_pS,
$$

the restriction of the Euclidean [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) to the [tangent space](../../../../../../tangent-space.md) of $S$.

If $\phi(u,v)$ is a local parametrization and

$$
\xi=\xi^1\partial_u+\xi^2\partial_v,
\qquad
\eta=\eta^1\partial_u+\eta^2\partial_v,
$$

then

$$
d\phi(\xi)=\xi^1\phi_u+\xi^2\phi_v,
\qquad
d\phi(\eta)=\eta^1\phi_u+\eta^2\phi_v.
$$

Taking their dot product gives

$$
I=E\,du^2+2F\,du\,dv+G\,dv^2,
$$

where

$$
\boxed{E=\phi_u\cdot\phi_u,\qquad
F=\phi_u\cdot\phi_v,\qquad
G=\phi_v\cdot\phi_v}.
$$
