<h1 id="26f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a chosen [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md) field $N$, the [second fundamental form](../../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) is

$$
II_p(v,w)=\langle-dN_p(v),w\rangle.
$$

In a local parametrization $\phi$, its coefficients, up to the equivalent simultaneous sign convention, are

$$
e=N\cdot\phi_{uu},
\qquad
f=N\cdot\phi_{uv},
\qquad
g=N\cdot\phi_{vv}.
$$

The [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of the [shape operator](../../../../../../shape-operator.md), hence

$$
K=\det(-dN)=\frac{eg-f^2}{EG-F^2}.
$$

[Theorema Egregium](../../../../../../theorema-egregium.md) states that $K$ is determined entirely by the first fundamental form. In particular, it is intrinsic and is preserved by every [local isometry](../../../../../../local-isometry.md).
