<h1 id="26f/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let

$$
F(x,y,z)=x^2+y^2+z^2-2xy-2yz.
$$

Its [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) is

$$
\nabla F=2(x-y,\ y-x-z,\ z-y).
$$

If this vanishes, the first and third coordinates give $x=y=z$, and the middle coordinate then gives $y=0$. Thus the origin is the only [critical point](../../../../../../../critical-point.md) of $F$. It has been removed from $V$, so $0$ is a [regular value](../../../../../../../regular-value.md) of the restriction under consideration. The [regular level set theorem](../../../../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md) shows that

$$
V=F^{-1}(0)\setminus\{0\}
$$

is a smooth surface.
