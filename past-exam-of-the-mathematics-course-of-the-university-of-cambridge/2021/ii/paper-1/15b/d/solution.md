<h1 id="15b/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the far past, $|ck\tau|\gg1$, the term $2/\tau^2$ is negligible and the rescaled mode is a [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) of angular frequency $ck$. The stated choice of its ground state is the [Bunch-Davies vacuum](../../../../../../bunch-davies-vacuum.md).

At late times, after the mode is far outside the Hubble scale, $|ck\tau|\ll1$. Its [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) becomes

$$
P_k
=\frac{\hbar H_{\rm inf}^2}{2c^3k^3}
\left(1+c^2k^2\tau^2\right)
\longrightarrow
\frac{\hbar H_{\rm inf}^2}{2c^3k^3}.
$$

Since $P_k$ has dimensions of length cubed, the dimensionless power is proportional to $k^3P_k$. More precisely, with the standard isotropic Fourier convention,

$$
\Delta^2(k)=\frac{k^3P_k}{2\pi^2}
\longrightarrow
\frac{\hbar H_{\rm inf}^2}{4\pi^2c^3},
$$

which is independent of the [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) $k$. Thus equal logarithmic intervals in $k$ carry equal late-time power. This is the sense in which inflation naturally generates a [scale-invariant inflationary power spectrum](../../../../../../scale-invariant-inflationary-power-spectrum.md).
