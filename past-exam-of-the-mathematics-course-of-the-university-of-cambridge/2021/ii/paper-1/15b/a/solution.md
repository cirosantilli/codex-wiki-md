<h1 id="15b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the action density as

$$
\mathcal L
=a^3\left[
\frac12\dot\phi^2
-\frac{c^2}{2a^2}\nabla\phi\cdot\nabla\phi
-V(\phi)
\right].
$$

Under a compactly supported [variation](../../../../../../variation.md) $\phi\mapsto\phi+\epsilon\eta$,

$$
\delta S
=\int d^3x\,dt
\left[
a^3\dot\phi\,\dot\eta
-ac^2\nabla\phi\cdot\nabla\eta
-a^3V'(\phi)\eta
\right].
$$

Integrating the first term by parts in time and the second in space, and discarding the [boundary terms](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md), gives

$$
\delta S
=\int d^3x\,dt
\left[
-\frac{\partial}{\partial t}(a^3\dot\phi)
+ac^2\nabla^2\phi
-a^3V'(\phi)
\right]\eta.
$$

The [principle of stationary action](../../../../../../principle-of-stationary-action.md) requires this to vanish for every $\eta$. Since

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}(a^3\dot\phi)
=a^3(\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi),
$$

division by $-a^3$ yields the field [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{
\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi
-\frac{c^2}{a^2}\nabla^2\phi
+\frac{dV}{d\phi}=0
}.
$$

This is the equation generated by the [inflaton action in an expanding universe](../../../../../../inflaton-action-in-an-expanding-universe.md).
