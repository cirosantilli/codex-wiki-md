<h1 id="15b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

When $V$ is approximately constant, $dV/d\phi\simeq0$, and exponential expansion gives the constant [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md) $H=H_{\rm inf}$. Take the spatial [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md)

$$
\phi(\mathbf x,t)
=\int\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}
\widehat\phi_{\mathbf k}(t)e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}.
$$

Since

$$
\nabla^2e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}
=-k^2e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x},
$$

each independent [inflationary scalar Fourier mode](../../../../../../inflationary-scalar-fourier-mode.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{
\ddot{\widehat\phi}_{\mathbf k}
+3H_{\rm inf}\dot{\widehat\phi}_{\mathbf k}
+\frac{c^2k^2}{a^2}\widehat\phi_{\mathbf k}=0
}.
$$
