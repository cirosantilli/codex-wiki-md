<h1 id="9b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the homogeneous background, $\mathbf v=0$ and the physical velocity is the [Hubble flow](../../../../../../hubble-flow.md)

$$
\mathbf u=aH\mathbf x=\dot a\,\mathbf x.
$$

The [comoving coordinate](../../../../../../comoving-coordinate.md) $\mathbf x$ is held fixed by the time derivative in the fluid equation, so

$$
\frac{\partial\mathbf u}{\partial t}=\ddot a\,\mathbf x.
$$

Moreover, spatial homogeneity gives $\nabla\bar P=0$. The [Euler equation](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) therefore reduces to

$$
\bar\rho a\ddot a\,\mathbf x=-\bar\rho\nabla\bar\Phi,
\qquad
\nabla\bar\Phi=-a\ddot a\,\mathbf x.
$$

Taking the [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) in three spatial dimensions gives

$$
\nabla^2\bar\Phi=-3a\ddot a.
$$

Consistency with the [Poisson equation](../../../../../../poisson-equation.md),

$$
\nabla^2\bar\Phi=\frac{4\pi G}{c^2}\bar\rho a^2,
$$

then requires

$$
\boxed{\frac{\ddot a}{a}
=-\frac{4\pi G}{3c^2}\bar\rho}.
$$

This is the pressureless, zero-cosmological-constant [Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md), obtained by the [Newtonian fluid derivation of the Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../newtonian-fluid-derivation-of-the-raychaudhuri-equation.md).
