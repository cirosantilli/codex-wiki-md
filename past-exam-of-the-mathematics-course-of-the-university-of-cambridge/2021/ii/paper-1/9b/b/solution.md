<h1 id="9b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The elementary Newtonian derivation of the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) follows a test particle on the boundary of a finite expanding sphere. It therefore introduces a distinguished centre and can misleadingly suggest that all matter expands away from one origin.

The fluid derivation is local: the [Euler equation](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) and [Poisson equation](../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) must be compatible in every comoving neighbourhood. It consequently describes a genuinely homogeneous infinite universe, in which every comoving observer sees the same [Hubble flow](../../../../../../hubble-flow.md), without selecting a physical centre. This is the improvement captured by the [Newtonian fluid derivation of the Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../newtonian-fluid-derivation-of-the-raychaudhuri-equation.md).
