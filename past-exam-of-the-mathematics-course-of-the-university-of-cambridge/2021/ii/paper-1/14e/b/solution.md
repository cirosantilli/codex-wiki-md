↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An [analytic continuation](../../../../../../analytic-continuation.md) of $f_1$ from $\mathcal D_1$ to $\mathcal D_2$ is a holomorphic function $f_2$ on $\mathcal D_2$ whose restriction to the nonempty overlap $\mathcal D_1\cap\mathcal D_2$ agrees with $f_1$.

Suppose $f_2$ and $\widetilde f_2$ are two such continuations. Both equal $f_1$ on $\mathcal D_1\cap\mathcal D_2$, so

$$
f_2=\widetilde f_2
$$

on that nonempty open set. Since $\mathcal D_2$ is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md), the [identity theorem](../../../../../../identity-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{f_2=\widetilde f_2\text{ throughout }\mathcal D_2}.
$$

**Thus analytic continuation to $\mathcal D_2$ is unique.**
