The [identity theorem](../../../../../../identity-theorem.md) states that if two [holomorphic functions](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) on a connected [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $\mathcal D$ agree on a subset having an accumulation point in $\mathcal D$, then they agree throughout $\mathcal D$. In particular, agreement on any nonempty open subset is sufficient.
