<h1 id="22h/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume (ii). Weak convergence gives

$$
\langle x_n,x_\infty\rangle
\longrightarrow
\langle x_\infty,x_\infty\rangle
=\|x_\infty\|^2.
$$

Using the [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product.md) identity,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|x_n-x_\infty\|^2
&=\|x_n\|^2+\|x_\infty\|^2
-2\operatorname{Re}\langle x_n,x_\infty\rangle\\
&\longrightarrow
\|x_\infty\|^2+\|x_\infty\|^2-2\|x_\infty\|^2=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Hence (ii) implies (i); this is the [Radon-Riesz theorem](../../../../../../../radon-riesz-theorem.md) in a Hilbert space.**
