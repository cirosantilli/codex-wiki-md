<h1 id="22h/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume $x_n\rightharpoonup x_\infty$. For each $n$, define the bounded functional

$$
T_n(h)=\langle h,x_n\rangle.
$$

Weak convergence makes $(T_n(h))_n$ convergent, hence bounded, for every $h\in H$. The [Uniform boundedness principle](../../../../../../../uniform-boundedness-principle.md) gives

$$
\sup_n\|T_n\|<\infty.
$$

By the [Riesz representation theorem](../../../../../../../riesz-representation-theorem.md), $\|T_n\|=\|x_n\|$, so $(x_n)$ is bounded.

Taking $h=e_i$ in the definition of [weak convergence in a Hilbert space](../../../../../../../weak-convergence-in-a-hilbert-space.md) gives

$$
\langle x_n,e_i\rangle
\longrightarrow
\langle x_\infty,e_i\rangle
$$

for every $i$. Thus (i) implies (ii).
