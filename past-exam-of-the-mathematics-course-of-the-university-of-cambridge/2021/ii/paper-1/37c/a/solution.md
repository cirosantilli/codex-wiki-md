<h1 id="37c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With

$$
\partial_\mu=\left(\frac1c\frac{\partial}{\partial t},\nabla\right),
$$

the [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) is the antisymmetric tensor

$$
\boxed{
F^{\mu\nu}
=\partial^\mu A^\nu-\partial^\nu A^\mu}.
$$

Under a Lorentz transformation $x'^\mu=\Lambda^\mu{}_\nu x^\nu$, the four-potential transforms as a four-vector and therefore

$$
\boxed{
F'^{\mu\nu}(x')
=\Lambda^\mu{}_\rho\Lambda^\nu{}_\sigma
F^{\rho\sigma}(x)}.
$$

**Thus $F$ is an antisymmetric rank-two [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md) under [Lorentz transformations](../../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md).**
