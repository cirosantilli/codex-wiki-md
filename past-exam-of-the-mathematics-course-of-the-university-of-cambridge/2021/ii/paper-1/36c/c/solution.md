<h1 id="36c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [adiabatic process](../../../../../../adiabatic-process.md) exchanges no heat with its surroundings, so $\delta Q=0$. For a reversible process it is also [isentropic](../../../../../../entropy.md). The first law then gives

$$
C_V\,dT=dE=-p\,dV.
$$

Using $p=Nk_BT/V$ and the Mayer relation,

$$
\frac{dT}{T}
=-\frac{Nk_B}{C_V}\frac{dV}{V}
=-(\gamma-1)\frac{dV}{V}.
$$

Integration yields

$$
TV^{\gamma-1}=\text{constant}.
$$

Substituting $T=pV/(Nk_B)$ gives the [reversible ideal-gas adiabat](../../../../../../reversible-ideal-gas-adiabat.md)

$$
\boxed{pV^\gamma=\text{constant}}.
$$
