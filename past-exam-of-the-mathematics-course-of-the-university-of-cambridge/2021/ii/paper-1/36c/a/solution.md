↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a fixed number $N$ of particles, the [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) equation of state is

$$
\boxed{pV=Nk_BT}.
$$

Its [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-of-an-ideal-gas.md) depends only on temperature, and

$$
dE=C_V\,dT.
$$

When the [heat capacity at constant volume](../../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) is constant, a choice of the energy zero gives

$$
\boxed{E=C_VT}.
$$
