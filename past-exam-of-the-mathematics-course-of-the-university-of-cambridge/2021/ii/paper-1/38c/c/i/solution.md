<h1 id="38c/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The Schwarzschild exterior is vacuum, so

$$
R_{\mu\nu}=0,\qquad R=0,
$$

and consequently $C_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}=R_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}$. Put

$$
f(r)=1-\frac{2M}{r}.
$$

The relevant [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) are

$$
\Gamma^t{}_{tr}=\frac{f'}{2f},
\qquad
\Gamma^r{}_{rr}=-\frac{f'}{2f},
\qquad
\Gamma^r{}_{tt}=\frac12ff'.
$$

With the convention

$$
R^\alpha{}_{\beta\gamma\delta}
=\partial_\gamma\Gamma^\alpha_{\beta\delta}
-\partial_\delta\Gamma^\alpha_{\beta\gamma}+\cdots,
$$

one finds

$$
R_{trtr}=\frac12f''(r).
$$

Since $f''=-4M/r^3$,

$$
\boxed{
C_{trtr}=-\frac{2M}{r^3}}
\qquad(r\gg2M).
$$

This is the leading [Radial Weyl curvature of Schwarzschild spacetime](../../../../../../../radial-weyl-curvature-of-schwarzschild-spacetime.md). The opposite convention for the Riemann tensor reverses the sign but not the physical tidal magnitude.
