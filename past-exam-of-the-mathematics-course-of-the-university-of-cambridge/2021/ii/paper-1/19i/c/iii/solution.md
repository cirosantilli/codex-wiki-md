**No.** The [center of a group](../../../../../../../center-of-a-group.md) satisfies

$$
Z(C_2\times D_8)
=C_2\times Z(D_8)
\cong C_2\times C_2,
$$

because $Z(D_8)=\{1,r^2\}\cong C_2$. This centre is not [cyclic](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md). The [cyclic-center obstruction to a faithful irreducible representation](../../../../../../../cyclic-center-obstruction-to-a-faithful-irreducible-representation.md) therefore rules out a faithful irreducible complex representation of $C_2\times D_8$.
