<h1 id="20g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) of $\beta$ is

$$
g(T)=T^3-5T^2+11T-12,
$$

and part (b) showed that $\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\beta]$. Modulo $3$,

$$
\overline g(T)
=T(T^2+T-1).
$$

The quadratic has no root in $\mathbb F_3$, so it is irreducible. The [Dedekind factorization theorem](../../../../../../dedekind-factorization-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{
(3)=(3,\beta)(3,\beta^2+\beta-1)
}.
$$

The two factors are distinct [prime ideal](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md), with norms $3$ and $3^2=9$, respectively.

Modulo $2$,

$$
\overline g(T)
=T(T^2+T+1),
$$

and the quadratic is irreducible over $\mathbb F_2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{
(2)=(2,\beta)(2,\beta^2+\beta+1)
}.
$$

This is a product of two distinct proper prime ideals, so $(2)$ is not prime. These decompositions are collected in [prime ideals above two and three in the cubic field of discriminant minus 307](../../../../../../prime-ideals-above-two-and-three-in-the-cubic-field-of-discriminant-minus-307.md).
