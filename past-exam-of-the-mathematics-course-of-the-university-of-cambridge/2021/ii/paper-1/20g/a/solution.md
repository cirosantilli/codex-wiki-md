<h1 id="20g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The element $\alpha$ is a root of

$$
f(T)=T^3-5T+8.
$$

By the [rational root theorem](../../../../../../rational-root-theorem.md), any rational root of this monic polynomial must be one of

$$
\mathord\pm1,\mathord\pm2,\mathord\pm4,\mathord\pm8.
$$

Direct substitution shows that none is a root. A reducible cubic over a field has a linear factor, so $f$ is an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) over $\mathbb Q$. It is therefore the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) of $\alpha$, and

$$
\boxed{[K:\mathbb Q]=\deg f=3}.
$$
