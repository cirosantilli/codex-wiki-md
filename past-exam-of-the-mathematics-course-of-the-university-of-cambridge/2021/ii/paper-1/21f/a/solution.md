Spaces $X$ and $Y$ are [homotopy equivalent](../../../../../../homotopy-equivalence.md) if there are continuous maps

$$
f:X\to Y,
\qquad
g:Y\to X
$$

such that

$$
g\circ f\simeq\operatorname{id}_X,
\qquad
f\circ g\simeq\operatorname{id}_Y.
$$

The maps $f$ and $g$ are called homotopy inverses.
