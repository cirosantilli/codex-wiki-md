<h1 id="21f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A subspace $Y\subseteq X$ is a [retract](../../../../../../retract.md) of $X$ if there is a continuous retraction

$$
r:X\to Y
$$

such that $r(y)=y$ for every $y\in Y$. A space is a [contractible space](../../../../../../contractible-space.md) if its identity map is [homotopic](../../../../../../homotopy.md) to a constant map.

Let $i:Y\hookrightarrow X$ be the inclusion and let

$$
H:X\times I\to X
$$

be a contraction, with $H(x,0)=x$ and $H(x,1)=x_0$. Then

$$
K(y,t)=r(H(i(y),t))
$$

is continuous and satisfies

$$
K(y,0)=r(i(y))=y,
\qquad
K(y,1)=r(x_0).
$$

**Thus $K$ contracts $Y$, proving that every [retract of a contractible space](../../../../../../retract-of-a-contractible-space.md) is contractible.**
