<h1 id="8d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) of the two equal masses is

$$
T=\frac m2(\dot x_1^2+\dot x_2^2).
$$

The extensions of the left, coupling, and right springs are respectively $x_1$, $x_2-x_1$, and $x_2$. Their [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) is therefore

$$
U=\frac12k_1x_1^2+\frac12k_3(x_2-x_1)^2+\frac12k_2x_2^2.
$$

Thus the mechanical [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) $L=T-U$ has the requested quadratic form with the symmetric [small-oscillation mass and stiffness matrices](../../../../../../small-oscillation-mass-and-stiffness-matrices.md)

$$
\boxed{
(T_{ij})=
\begin{pmatrix}m&0\\0&m\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
(V_{ij})=
\begin{pmatrix}
k_1+k_3&-k_3\\
-k_3&k_2+k_3
\end{pmatrix}
}.
$$
