<h1 id="39a/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write

$$
f(r)=A+\frac B{r^3},
\qquad
\mathbf w=\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf x,
\qquad
\mathbf u=f\mathbf w.
$$

The velocity gradient of the solid-rotation part is antisymmetric, so it drops out of the [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](../../../../../../../newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md). Since

$$
\partial_i f=-\frac{3B}{r^5}x_i,
$$

and $p=0$, the stress is

$$
\boxed{
\sigma_{ij}
=-\frac{3\mu B}{r^5}
\left[
x_i(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf x)_j
+x_j(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf x)_i
\right]}.
$$

Equivalently, with $\mathbf n=\mathbf x/r$,

$$
\sigma_{ij}
=-\frac{3\mu B}{r^3}
\left[
n_i(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf n)_j
+n_j(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf n)_i
\right].
$$
