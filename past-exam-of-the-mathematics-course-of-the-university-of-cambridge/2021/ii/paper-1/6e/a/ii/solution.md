<h1 id="6e/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At a positive [equilibrium](../../../../../../../equilibrium-of-an-autonomous-differential-equation.md), division by $N$ gives

$$
be^{-aN}=d.
$$

Since $b>d$, this has the positive solution

$$
\boxed{N^*=\frac1a\log\frac bd}.
$$

Write the vector field as $F(N)=N(be^{-aN}-d)$. Its [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
F'(N)=be^{-aN}-d-abNe^{-aN}.
$$

At $N=N^*$, the first two terms cancel and $be^{-aN^*}=d$, so

$$
F'(N^*)=-adN^*<0.
$$

The one-dimensional [linear stability analysis](../../../../../../../linear-stability.md) therefore shows that $N^*$ is locally [asymptotically stable](../../../../../../../asymptotic-stability.md).
