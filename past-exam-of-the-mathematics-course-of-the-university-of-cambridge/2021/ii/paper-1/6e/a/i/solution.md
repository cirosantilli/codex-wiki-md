<h1 id="6e/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [per-capita](../../../../../../../per-capita.md) net growth rate is the birth rate minus the death rate,

$$
be^{-aN}-d.
$$

Multiplying by the population size gives the [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{dN}{dt}=N\left(be^{-aN}-d\right)}.
$$

This is the [exponential density-dependent birth model](../../../../../../../exponential-density-dependent-birth-model.md).
