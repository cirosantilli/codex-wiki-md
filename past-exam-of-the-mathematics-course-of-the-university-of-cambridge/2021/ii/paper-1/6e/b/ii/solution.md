↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [disease-free equilibrium](../../../../../../../disease-free-equilibrium.md) is $(S,I)=(N^*,0)$. For

$$
F(S,I)=S(be^{-aS}-\beta I-d),
\qquad
G(S,I)=I(\beta S-d-\delta),
$$

the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) there is

$$
J(N^*,0)=
\begin{pmatrix}
-adN^*&-\beta N^*\\
0&\beta N^*-(d+\delta)
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Because this matrix is triangular, its [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are its diagonal entries:

$$
-adN^*<0,
\qquad
\beta N^*-(d+\delta).
$$

The second eigenvalue is positive exactly when

$$
\boxed{N^*>\frac{d+\delta}{\beta}}.
$$

Under this condition the [linear stability analysis](../../../../../../../linear-stability.md) gives an unstable disease-free equilibrium, agreeing with the [epidemic invasion threshold](../../../../../../../epidemic-invasion-threshold.md).
