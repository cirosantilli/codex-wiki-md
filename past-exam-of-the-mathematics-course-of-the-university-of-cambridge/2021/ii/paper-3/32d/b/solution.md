↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The flow equations of the displayed [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) are $\dot ψ_1=-ψ_2$ and $\dot ψ_2=ψ_1$. Hence $dψ/ds=iψ$, whose solution is $ψ(s)=e^{is}ψ(0)$. The vector field therefore generates the stated phase rotation.
