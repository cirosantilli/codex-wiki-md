<h1 id="29k/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $θ_n$ shares are held during period $n$, the remaining wealth $X_{n-1}-θ_nS_{n-1}$ is in the bank account. [Self-financing](../../../../../../self-financing-portfolio.md) therefore gives

$$
X_n=θ_nS_n+(1+r)(X_{n-1}-θ_nS_{n-1}),
$$

which rearranges to the required equation.
