↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The sink speed scales as $U\sim A/R^2$, so the [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md) is $Re\sim UR/ν=A/(νR)$. The approximation applies in the high-Reynolds-number region $R\ll A/ν$. Radial inertia scales as $U\,dU/dR\sim A^2/R^5$, so the radial pressure gradient has the same $R^{-5}$ dependence.
