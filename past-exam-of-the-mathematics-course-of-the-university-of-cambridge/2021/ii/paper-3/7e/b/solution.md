<h1 id="7e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Integration by parts, or the identities obtained by splitting $1=t+(1-t)$, gives

$$
B(p,z+1)=\frac z{p+z}B(p,z).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{(p+z)B(p,z+1)=zB(p,z)}.
$$
