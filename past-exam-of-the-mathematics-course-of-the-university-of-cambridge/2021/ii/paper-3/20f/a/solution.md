<h1 id="20f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Triangulate $X$ and adjoin one new vertex joined to every simplex; the resulting simplicial complex realizes the [cone](../../../../../../simplicial-cone.md) $CX$. The cone contracts to its apex, so

$$
\boxed{H_0(CX)\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad H_i(CX)=0\ (i>0)}.
$$
