<h1 id="20f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $X'=X\cup_A CA$. After replacing the two pieces by small open neighborhoods, their intersection deformation retracts to $A$. The [Mayer-Vietoris sequence](../../../../../../mayer-vietoris-sequence.md), together with $H_i(CA)=0$ for $i>0$ and $H_0(CA)=\mathbb Z$, becomes

$$
\cdots\to H_{i+1}(X')\to H_i(A)\to H_i(X)\to H_i(X')\to H_{i-1}(A)\to\cdots
$$

and at degree zero ends as

$$
\cdots\to H_1(X')\to H_0(A)\to\mathbb Z\oplus H_0(X)\to H_0(X')\to0,
$$

which is the required sequence.
