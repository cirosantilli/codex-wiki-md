↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Bravais lattice](../../../../../../bravais-lattice.md) is the integer span of three linearly independent primitive vectors. Its [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) consists of vectors $G$ satisfying $G\mathbin\cdot r\in2π\mathbb Z$ for every lattice vector $r$.

Translations by lattice vectors commute with the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md). Their simultaneous eigenvalues have the form $e^{ik\cdot r}$, so an energy eigenfunction may be chosen with

$$
ψ_k(x+r)=e^{ik\cdot r}ψ_k(x).
$$

Thus $ψ_k=e^{ik\cdot x}u_k$ with lattice-periodic $u_k$, which is [Bloch theorem](../../../../../../bloch-s-theorem.md). Wavevectors differing by a reciprocal-lattice vector describe the same translation character, and the [Brillouin zone](../../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) is a fundamental Wigner-Seitz cell of the reciprocal lattice.
