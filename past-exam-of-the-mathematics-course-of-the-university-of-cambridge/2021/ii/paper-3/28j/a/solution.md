↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [log-likelihood](../../../../../../log-likelihood.md) is, up to constants, $nα\log β-β\sum_iX_i$, so the [maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) is $\widehat β=α/\overline X$. By the [central limit theorem](../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md),

$$
\sqrt n(\overline X-α/β)\Rightarrow N(0,α/β^2).
$$

Applying the [delta method](../../../../../../delta-method.md) to $g(x)=α/x$ gives

$$
\boxed{\sqrt n(\widehat β-β)\Rightarrow N(0,β^2/α).}
$$
