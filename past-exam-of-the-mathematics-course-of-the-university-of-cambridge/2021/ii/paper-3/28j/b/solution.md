↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $z_{1-γ/2}$ be the corresponding [standard normal distribution](../../../../../../standard-normal-distribution.md) quantile. [Slutsky theorem](../../../../../../slutsky-theorem.md) permits replacing $β$ by the consistent estimator $\widehat β$, giving the confidence interval

$$
\left[\widehat β-z_{1-γ/2}{\widehat β\over\sqrt{nα}},\ 
\widehat β+z_{1-γ/2}{\widehat β\over\sqrt{nα}}\right].
$$

After standardization, the preceding convergence in distribution and continuity of the normal distribution show that its coverage tends to $1-γ$.
