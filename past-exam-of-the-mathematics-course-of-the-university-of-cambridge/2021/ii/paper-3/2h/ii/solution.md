<h1 id="2h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Exhaust the quadrant by compact sets $K_n$ with connected complements and with every compact subset of $\Omega$ eventually contained in the interior of $K_n$. Runge's theorem gives a polynomial $P_n$ satisfying

$$
\sup_{K_n}|P_n-f|<2^{-n}.
$$

Thus $P_n\to f$ locally uniformly. For a fixed $z\in\Omega$, choose a small circle about $z$ eventually contained in every $K_n$. The [Cauchy integral formula for derivatives](../../../../../../cauchy-derivative-formula.md) applied to $P_n-f$ gives

$$
P_n^{(r)}(z)\longrightarrow f^{(r)}(z)
$$

for every $r\geq0$.
