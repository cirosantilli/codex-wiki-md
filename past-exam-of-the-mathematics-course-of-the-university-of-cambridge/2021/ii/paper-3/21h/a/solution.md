A family $F$ of [continuous functions](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on a [compact space](../../../../../../compact-space.md) $K$ is [equicontinuous](../../../../../../equicontinuity.md) if, for every $x$ and every $ε>0$, there is a neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ such that $|f(y)-f(x)|<ε$ for every $y$ in $U$ and every $f$ in $F$. The [Arzelà-Ascoli theorem](../../../../../../arzela-ascoli-theorem.md) says that $F$ has compact closure in $C(K)$ with the [uniform norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md) if and only if it is equicontinuous and pointwise bounded.
