<h1 id="21h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Fix $x$ and $ε>0$. Since $\mathcal K$ is compact in the [uniform norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md), choose a finite $ε/3$-net $f_1,\ldots,f_r$. By [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md), there is a neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ on which $|f_i(y)-f_i(x)|<ε/3$ for every $i$. For $f$ in $\mathcal K$, choose $f_i$ with $\|f-f_i\|_∞<ε/3$. The [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) gives $|f(y)-f(x)|<ε$ on $U$, uniformly in $f$. Thus $\mathcal K$ is [equicontinuous](../../../../../../equicontinuity.md).
