<h1 id="21h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply [Arzelà-Ascoli theorem](../../../../../../arzela-ascoli-theorem.md) on $[-m,m]$. Pointwise boundedness and equicontinuity give a subsequence converging [uniformly](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) there. Successively extract subsequences for $m=1,2,\ldots$, and take the [diagonal subsequence](../../../../../../diagonal-argument.md). It converges uniformly on every closed bounded interval. Its limits on nested intervals agree, so they define $f$ on the [real numbers](../../../../../../real-number.md); the locally uniform limit of [continuous functions](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) is continuous.
