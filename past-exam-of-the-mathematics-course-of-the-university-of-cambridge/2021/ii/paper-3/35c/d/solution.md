<h1 id="35c/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the continuum limit the number outside the ground state is

$$
N_{\rm ex}=BV\int_0^∞{E^{d/α-1}\over e^{β(E-μ)}-1}\,dE.
$$

At the largest allowed chemical potential, $μ=0$, the integrand behaves near zero as $E^{d/α-2}$. The integral is finite exactly when $d/α>1$. Excess particles must then occupy the ground state, so a [Bose-Einstein condensate](../../../../../../bose-einstein-condensation.md) exists precisely for $α<d$.
