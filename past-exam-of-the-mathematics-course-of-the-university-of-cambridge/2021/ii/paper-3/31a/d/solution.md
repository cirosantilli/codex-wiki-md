<h1 id="31a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $ν=μ+1>0$ small, $(1,0)$ has two negative [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and is a [stable node](../../../../../../stable-node.md). The two nearby equilibria satisfy $u=±\sqrt ν+O(ν)$ and are saddles. Their stable and unstable separatrices form the local phase portrait; trajectories in the wedge between the stable manifolds approach $(1,0)$, while trajectories crossing an unstable direction leave the neighbourhood.
