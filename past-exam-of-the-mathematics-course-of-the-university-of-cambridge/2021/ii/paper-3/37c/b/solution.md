<h1 id="37c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $F=1-2M/r$, define the tortoise coordinate by $dr_*/dr=F^{-1}$, and set the advanced time $v=t+r_*$. Since $dt=dv-F^{-1}dr$, substitution gives

$$
ds^2=-Fdv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2dΩ^2.
$$

These are [Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../../../ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md); all coefficients are regular at the [event horizon](../../../../../../schwarzschild-event-horizon.md) $r=2M$.
