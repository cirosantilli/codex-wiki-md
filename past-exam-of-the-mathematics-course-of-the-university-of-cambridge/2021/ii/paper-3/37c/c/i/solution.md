<h1 id="37c/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Outside the ingoing null shell the enclosed mass is $E$, so use the ingoing [Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../../../../ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md)

$$
ds^2=-(1-2E/r)dv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2dΩ^2.
$$

This is the [Schwarzschild metric](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) of mass $E$, regular across $r=2E$ and valid for $r>0$ on the exterior side of the shell.
