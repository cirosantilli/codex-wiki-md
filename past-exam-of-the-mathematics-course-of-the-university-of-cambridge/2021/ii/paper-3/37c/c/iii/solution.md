<h1 id="37c/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Because the shell lies well inside its Schwarzschild radius once it reaches $r=2E$, an [event horizon](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-event-horizon.md) forms and the final exterior is a [black hole](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) of mass $E$. In the ideal instantaneous-shell model the collapse ends at a spacetime singularity.
