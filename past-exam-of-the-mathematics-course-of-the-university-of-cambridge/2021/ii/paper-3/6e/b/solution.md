<h1 id="6e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $n(a,t)=A(a)T(t)$. Separation gives

$$
\frac{T'}T=-\frac{A'}A-d(a)=r
$$

for a constant $r$. Hence

$$
\boxed{T(t)=T_0e^{rt},\qquad
A(a)=A_0\exp\left(-ra-\int_0^ad(s)ds\right)}.
$$
