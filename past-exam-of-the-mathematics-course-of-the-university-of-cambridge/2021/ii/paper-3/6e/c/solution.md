<h1 id="6e/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Substitution into the renewal boundary condition and cancellation of $A_0T(t)$ gives the [Euler-Lotka equation](../../../../../../euler-lotka-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{1=\int_0^\infty b(a)
\exp\left(-ra-\int_0^ad(s)ds\right)da}.
$$

This is the necessary condition selecting the growth exponent $r$.
