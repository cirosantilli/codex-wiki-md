<h1 id="23f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Outside the finite set of [branch points](../../../../../../branch-point.md), the derivative is nonzero. The [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) supplies evenly covered neighbourhoods, and compactness of the [Riemann sphere](../../../../../../riemann-sphere.md) makes every fibre finite, so the restriction is a covering map.

Lift a loop $γ$ from each point of the fibre over $z_0$; its endpoints define a [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) $σ_γ$. The homotopy lifting property shows that this permutation depends only on the based [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) class. The punctured source is connected, so a path between any two points in the fibre projects to a loop whose lift joins them. Hence the monodromy group acts transitively.
