<h1 id="23f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A point is ramified with multiplicity $m_i$ exactly when it is a zero of $f'$ of multiplicity $m_i-1$. Since $f'$ has degree $d-1$, the [fundamental theorem of algebra](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-algebra.md) gives $\sum_i(m_i-1)=d-1$.

Conversely, choose distinct complex numbers $a_i$ and put $g(z)=c\prod_i(z-a_i)^{m_i-1}$ with $c\ne0$. The assumption makes $g$ a polynomial of degree $d-1$. Any [antiderivative](../../../../../../antiderivative.md) $f$ of $g$ has degree $d$ and precisely the prescribed [ramification](../../../../../../ramification-divisor.md) multiplicities.
