<h1 id="33b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Parity conservation and the negative parity of $X$ require odd [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L$. Since the two $Y$ particles are identical bosons, their spin state must then be antisymmetric, so their total spin is $S=1$. Total angular momentum zero forces $L=S=1$.

Conditioning on spin projection $m_S=1$ leaves orbital projection $m_L=-1$. The angular density is therefore proportional to $|Y_{1,-1}(θ,φ)|^2\propto\sin^2θ$. Including the solid-angle measure, the requested probability is

$$
{\int_{π/4}^{3π/4}\sin^3θ\,dθ\over\int_0^π\sin^3θ\,dθ}
= {5\sqrt2\over8}.
$$
