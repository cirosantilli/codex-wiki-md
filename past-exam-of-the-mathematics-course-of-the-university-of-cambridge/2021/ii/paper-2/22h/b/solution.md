<h1 id="22h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The identity map

$$
I:(V,\|\cdot\|_1)\to(V,\|\cdot\|_2)
$$

is a continuous linear bijection. Since both spaces are Banach, the [bounded inverse theorem](../../../../../../bounded-inverse-theorem.md) makes $I^{-1}$ continuous, giving $\|x\|_1\leq C_2\|x\|_2$.

Completeness is essential. On the space $c_{00}$ of finitely supported sequences, $\|x\|_2\leq\|x\|_1$, but for $x=(1,\ldots,1,0,\ldots)$ the ratio $\|x\|_1/\|x\|_2=\sqrt n$ is unbounded. Both normed spaces are incomplete.
