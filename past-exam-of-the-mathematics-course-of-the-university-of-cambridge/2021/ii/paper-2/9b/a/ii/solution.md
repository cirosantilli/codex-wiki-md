↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For fermions the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) permits only $n_{\mathbf p}=0,1$. The two-term partition sum gives the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{\langle N(\mathbf p)\rangle
=\frac1{e^{\beta(E_{\mathbf p}-\mu)}+1}}.
$$
