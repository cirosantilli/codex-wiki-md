↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For bosons the occupation number is $n_{\mathbf p}=0,1,2,\ldots$. Summing the [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md) for the single-mode [grand canonical ensemble](../../../../../../../grand-canonical-ensemble.md) gives the [Bose-Einstein distribution](../../../../../../../bose-einstein-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{\langle N(\mathbf p)\rangle
=\frac1{e^{\beta(E_{\mathbf p}-\mu)}-1}}.
$$
