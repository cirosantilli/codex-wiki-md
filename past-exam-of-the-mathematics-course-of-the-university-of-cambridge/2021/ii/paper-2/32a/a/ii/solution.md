↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The notation

$$
x(t)\sim\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n\phi_n(t)
$$

means that for every $N\geq0$,

$$
x(t)-\sum_{n=0}^Na_n\phi_n(t)=o(\phi_N(t))
\qquad(t\to\infty).
$$

The series need not converge for any fixed $t$.
