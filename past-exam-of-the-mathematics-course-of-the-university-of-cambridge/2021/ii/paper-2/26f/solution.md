<h1 id="26f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [26F](../26f.md)

The map $\phi$ is an [immersion](../../../../../immersion.md) when $d\phi$ has rank two, equivalently $\phi_u$ and $\phi_v$ are linearly independent. It is [isothermal](../../../../../isothermal-coordinates.md) when its first fundamental form has

$$
E=G=\lambda^2>0,
\qquad F=0.
$$

In general the [mean curvature](../../../../../mean-curvature.md) is

$$
H=\frac{eG-2fF+gE}{2(EG-F^2)}.
$$

The surface is [minimal](../../../../../minimal-surface.md) when $H=0$. In isothermal coordinates the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](../../../../../laplace-beltrami-operator.md) identity is

$$
\phi_{uu}+\phi_{vv}=2\lambda^2H N.
$$

Thus $H=0$ exactly when each coordinate function $x,y,z$ is [harmonic](../../../../../harmonic-function.md).

For

$$
\phi(u,v)=(f(u)\cos v,f(u)\sin v,g(u)),
$$

the isothermal conditions are

$$
f'^2+g'^2=f^2,
$$

and harmonicity gives

$$
f''-f=0,
\qquad g''=0.
$$

Consequently

$$
f=Ae^u+Be^{-u},
\qquad g=cu+d,
$$

and the isothermal equation reduces to

$$
c^2=4AB.
$$

These are all the solutions for which $f$ never vanishes. When $AB>0$ they parameterize a [catenoid](../../../../../catenoid.md), up to translations, reflection and scaling. The limiting cases $AB=0$, $c=0$, with exactly one of $A,B$ nonzero, parameterize a punctured plane.
