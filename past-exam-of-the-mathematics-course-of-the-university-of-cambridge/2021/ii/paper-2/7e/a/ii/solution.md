↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The point $z_0$ is a [regular singular point](../../../../../../../regular-singular-point-criterion-for-a-second-order-equation.md) when it is singular but

$$
(z-z_0)p(z),
\qquad
(z-z_0)^2q(z)
$$

extend holomorphically to $z_0$.
