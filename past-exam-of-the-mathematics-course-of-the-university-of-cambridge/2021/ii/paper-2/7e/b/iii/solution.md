<h1 id="7e/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Infinity is irregular singular when at least one of

$$
2-zp(z),
\qquad
z^2q(z)
$$

fails to be holomorphic as a function of $1/z$ at $1/z=0$.
