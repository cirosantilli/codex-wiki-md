<h1 id="38c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The Killing coordinates $t$ and $\theta$ give

$$
E=\frac{\dot t}{r^2},
\qquad
J=r^2\dot\theta.
$$

Together with the norm $\varepsilon=2\mathcal L$, these are the three constants of motion.
