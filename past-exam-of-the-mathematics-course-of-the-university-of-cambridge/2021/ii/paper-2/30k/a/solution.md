<h1 id="30k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A risky-asset portfolio $\varphi$ financed through the risk-free asset has zero-cost discounted gain

$$
G_\varphi=\varphi^T(S_1-(1+r)S_0).
$$

It is an [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md) when $G_\varphi\geq0$ almost surely and $\mathbb P(G_\varphi>0)>0$.
