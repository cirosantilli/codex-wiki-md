<h1 id="30k/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At the optimal terminal wealth, normalized marginal utility defines an [equivalent martingale measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md) $Q$ and hence a marginal utility price

$$
p_Q(Y)=\frac1{1+r}\mathbb E_QY.
$$

This is an arbitrage-free price, so $p_Q(Y)\in I$. Concavity of utility implies that the finite-quantity indifference bid does not exceed its marginal price:

$$
\pi(Y)\leq p_Q(Y).
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\pi(Y)\leq\sup\{p:p\in I\}}.
$$
