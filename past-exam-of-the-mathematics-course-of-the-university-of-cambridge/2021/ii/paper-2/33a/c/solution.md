<h1 id="33a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $\mu\gg1$, trajectories rapidly approach the cubic nullcline

$$
y=\mu(x^3/3-x)
$$

and then move slowly along its attracting outer branches $|x|>1$. For $0<F<1$, the equilibrium lies on the repelling middle branch. Slow motion to a fold, a rapid jump to the opposite outer branch, and repetition produce a [relaxation oscillation](../../../../../../relaxation-oscillation.md). For $F>1$, the equilibrium lies on the attracting right branch; trajectories undergo any necessary fast jump and then drift to that equilibrium, so no relaxation cycle remains.
