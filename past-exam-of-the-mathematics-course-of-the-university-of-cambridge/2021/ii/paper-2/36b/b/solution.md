↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [scattering amplitude](../../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) is defined by

$$
\psi\sim e^{ikz}+f(\theta)\frac{e^{ikr}}r.
$$

For a central potential its [partial-wave expansion](../../../../../../partial-wave-s-matrix.md) is

$$
f(\theta)=\frac1k\sum_{l=0}^\infty(2l+1)e^{i\delta_l}\sin\delta_lP_l(\cos\theta).
$$

Using orthogonality of the [Legendre polynomials](../../../../../../legendre-polynomial.md) in $\sigma_T=\int|f|^2d\Omega$ gives

$$
\boxed{\sigma_T=\frac{4\pi}{k^2}\sum_{l=0}^\infty(2l+1)\sin^2\delta_l}.
$$
