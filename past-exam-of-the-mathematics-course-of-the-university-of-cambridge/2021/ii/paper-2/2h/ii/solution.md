**False.** On the [open unit disk](../../../../../../unit-disk.md), let

$$
\phi(x,y)=-(x^2+y^2).
$$

Then $\Delta\phi=-4<0$, but the unique maximum is $phi(0,0)=0$, whereas $phi=-1$ on the boundary.
