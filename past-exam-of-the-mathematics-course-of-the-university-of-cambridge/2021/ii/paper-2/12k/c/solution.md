<h1 id="12k/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $c\in C$ had odd [Hamming weight](../../../../../../hamming-weight.md), add all $n$ cyclic shifts of $c$. Each coordinate of the sum is the sum of all coordinates of $c$, namely one in $\mathbb F_2$. Linearity and cyclicity would therefore put $(1,\ldots,1)$ in $C$, contrary to the hypothesis. Hence every codeword has even weight.
