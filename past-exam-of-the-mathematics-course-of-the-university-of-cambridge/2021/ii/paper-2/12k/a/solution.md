<h1 id="12k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [binary cyclic code](../../../../../../cyclic-code.md) is a linear subspace $C\leq\mathbb F_2^n$ invariant under cyclic coordinate shift. Identify a word $(c_0,\ldots,c_{n-1})$ with $c_0+c_1X+\cdots+c_{n-1}X^{n-1}$ in

$$
\mathbb F_2[X]/(X^n-1).
$$

Cyclic shift is multiplication by $X$, so cyclic codes are exactly ideals of this quotient. Since $\mathbb F_2[X]$ is a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md), each is generated by a unique monic divisor $g\mid X^n-1$, giving the required bijection.
