<h1 id="14d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) give $\dot q=p$ and $\dot p=-\omega^2q$, hence $\ddot q+\omega^2q=0$. The phase-space energy curve is an ellipse. Its [action variable](../../../../../../action-variable.md) is

$$
I=\frac1{2\pi}\oint p\,dq
=\frac1{2\pi}\times\pi\sqrt{2E}\frac{\sqrt{2E}}\omega
=\frac E\omega.
$$

Thus $\boxed{E=\omega I}$.
