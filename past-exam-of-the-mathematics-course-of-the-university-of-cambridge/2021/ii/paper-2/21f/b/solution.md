<h1 id="21f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [covering map](../../../../../../covering-space.md) induces an injection on fundamental groups. There is no injection $\mathbb Z^2\hookrightarrow\mathbb Z/2$, and there is no injection $\mathbb Z/2\hookrightarrow\mathbb Z^2$ because $\mathbb Z^2$ is torsion-free. Thus neither proposed covering exists.
