<h1 id="11h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the [Gram-Schmidt process](../../../../../../gram-schmidt-process.md) to $1,x,\ldots,x^n$. The last resulting vector $P_n$ is nonzero, has degree exactly $n$, and is orthogonal to every polynomial in $\operatorname{span}(1,x,\ldots,x^{n-1})$.
