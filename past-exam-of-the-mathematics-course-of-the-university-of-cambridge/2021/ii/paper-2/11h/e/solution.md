<h1 id="11h/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Given $\epsilon>0$, the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](../../../../../../weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md) supplies a polynomial $Q$ with $\|f-Q\|_\infty<\epsilon$. For all sufficiently large $n$, the quadrature is exact on $Q$. Positivity of the weights and $\sum_jA_j=\int r$ then give

$$
\left|\int fr-\sum_jA_jf(\omega_j)\right|
\leq2\epsilon\int_{-1}^1r.
$$

Letting $\epsilon\downarrow0$ proves convergence.
