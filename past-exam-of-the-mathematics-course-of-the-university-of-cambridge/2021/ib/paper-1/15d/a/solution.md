<h1 id="15d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) is $B=\nabla\times A$, [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) gives, for any oriented surface $S$ bounded by $C$,

$$
\Phi=\int_SB\mathbin{\cdot}dS
=\int_S(\nabla\times A)\mathbin{\cdot}dS
=\boxed{\oint_CA\mathbin{\cdot}dx}.
$$

Under a [gauge transformation](../../../../../../gauge-transformation.md) $A\mapsto A+\nabla\chi$, the integral changes by

$$
\oint_C\nabla\chi\mathbin{\cdot}dx=0
$$

because $C$ is closed. The flux expression is therefore gauge independent.
