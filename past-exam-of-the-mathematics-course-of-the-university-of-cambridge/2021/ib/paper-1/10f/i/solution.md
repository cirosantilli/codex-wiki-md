<h1 id="10f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume inverse images of [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md) are open. If $x_n\to a$ and $V$ is any open neighbourhood of $f(a)$, then $f^{-1}(V)$ is an open neighbourhood of $a$. By the definition of [convergence in a metric space](../../../../../../convergence-in-a-metric-space.md), $x_n\in f^{-1}(V)$ eventually, and hence $f(x_n)\in V$ eventually. Thus $f(x_n)\to f(a)$.
