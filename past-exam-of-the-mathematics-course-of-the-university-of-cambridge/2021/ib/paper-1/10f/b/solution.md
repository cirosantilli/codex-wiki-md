This may be false because continuity only forces the [continuous image of a compact space](../../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md) $f(X)$ to be compact, not the whole codomain. For example, let $X=\{0\}$, let $Y=\mathbb R$, and set $f(0)=0$. The domain is compact and $f$ is continuous, but $\mathbb R$ is not compact.
