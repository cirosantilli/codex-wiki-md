↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [moment-generating function](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) of $W_i\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1)$ is

$$
M_{W_i}(t)=\frac1{1-t},
\qquad t<1.
$$

Independence makes the moment-generating function of the sum equal the product:

$$
M_{\sum_iW_i}(t)=(1-t)^{-n}.
$$

This is the moment-generating function of the [gamma distribution](../../../../../../gamma-distribution.md) $\Gamma(n,1)$, and moment-generating functions determine distributions in a neighbourhood of zero. Hence

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i=1}^nW_i\sim\Gamma(n,1)}.
$$
