The [Neyman-Pearson lemma](../../../../../../neyman-pearson-lemma.md) states that, for testing one simple hypothesis with density $f_0$ against another with density $f_1$, a size-$\alpha$ test that rejects for the largest values of the [likelihood ratio](../../../../../../likelihood-ratio.md) $f_1/f_0$ is most powerful among all tests of size at most $\alpha$, with boundary randomization if needed.
