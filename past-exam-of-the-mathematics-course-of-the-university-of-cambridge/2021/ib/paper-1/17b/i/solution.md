The [local truncation error](../../../../../../local-truncation-error.md) is the error made by one numerical step started from the exact solution:

$$
\tau_{n+1}
=y(t_{n+1})-y(t_n)
-h\phi(t_n,y(t_n),h).
$$

A one-step method has local order $p+1$ when $\tau_{n+1}=O(h^{p+1})$ uniformly for $t_n$ in each fixed bounded time interval.
