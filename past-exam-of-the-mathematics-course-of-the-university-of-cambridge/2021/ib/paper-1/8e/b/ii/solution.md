↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Jordan normal form](../../../../../../../jordan-normal-form.md) can contain only blocks $J_r(0)$ with eigenvalue zero. Moreover,

$$
J_r(0)^n=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad r\leq n.
$$

**Thus the possible blocks are precisely the nilpotent Jordan blocks of sizes $1\leq r\leq n$.**
