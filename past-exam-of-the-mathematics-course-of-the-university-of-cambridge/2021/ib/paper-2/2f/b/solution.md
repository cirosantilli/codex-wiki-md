**Yes.** [Fubini's theorem](../../../../../../fubini-s-theorem.md) and the triangle inequality give

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_1(Tf,Tg)
&\leq\int_0^1\int_0^1
|K(x,y)|\,|f(y)-g(y)|\,dy\,dx\\
&\leq\|K\|_\infty d_1(f,g).
\end{aligned}
$$

**Thus $T$ is Lipschitz continuous with respect to $d_1$ as well.**
