<h1 id="15c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a one-dimensional [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md),

$$
\boxed{\rho(x,t)=|\psi(x,t)|^2},
\qquad
\boxed{j(x,t)=\frac{\hbar}{2mi}
\left(\psi^*\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x}
-\psi\frac{\partial\psi^*}{\partial x}\right)}
=\frac{\hbar}{m}\operatorname{Im}\left(\psi^*\partial_x\psi\right).
$$

The [probability continuity equation](../../../../../../probability-continuity-equation.md) is

$$
\partial_t\rho+\partial_xj=0.
$$

For a [stationary state](../../../../../../stationary-state.md) $\psi(x,t)=\phi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$, the [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) is independent of time, so $\partial_xj=0$ and the [probability current](../../../../../../probability-current.md) is constant in space.

For the [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md)

$$
\psi=Ae^{ikx-iEt/\hbar},
$$

one obtains

$$
\boxed{\rho=|A|^2},
\qquad
\boxed{j=\frac{\hbar k}{m}|A|^2}.
$$

This is a [momentum eigenstate](../../../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md) with momentum $p=\hbar k$ and, when it satisfies the free [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md), energy $E=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$. Its constant density and current describe a spatially uniform beam carrying probability in the sign of $k$. It is not a [normalizable wavefunction](../../../../../../normalizable-wavefunction.md), so it represents an idealized state rather than a localized particle.
