<h1 id="5a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $z(t)=h_I-\alpha t$, the free-surface area is

$$
\boxed{
A(t)=\frac{\pi r_0^2\sqrt{2g}}{\alpha}
\sqrt{h_I-\alpha t}}
$$

until the emptying time $t_e=h_I/\alpha$. Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\frac{A(t)}{A(0)}
=\sqrt{1-\frac{t}{t_e}}}.
$$
