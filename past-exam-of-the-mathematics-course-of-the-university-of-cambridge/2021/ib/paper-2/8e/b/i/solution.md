<h1 id="8e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose the least $k\geq1$ such that

$$
(f-\alpha I)^kv=0.
$$

Then

$$
w=(f-\alpha I)^{k-1}v
$$

is nonzero by minimality, and

$$
(f-\alpha I)w=0.
$$

**Therefore $fw=\alpha w$, so $w$ is the required nonzero [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md).**
