<h1 id="8e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A direct determinant calculation gives

$$
\chi_A(t)=\det(tI-A)=(t-1)^3.
$$

Moreover,

$$
A-I=
\begin{pmatrix}
-3&-6&-9\\
3&6&9\\
-1&-2&-3
\end{pmatrix}
$$

has rank one, so its kernel has dimension two, while $(A-I)^2=0$ and $A\ne I$. Hence the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is

$$
\boxed{m_A(t)=(t-1)^2}.
$$

There are two [Jordan blocks](../../../../../../jordan-block.md), because the eigenspace has dimension two, and the largest has size two, because the minimal polynomial has exponent two. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{JNF}(A)=J_2(1)\oplus J_1(1)}.
$$
