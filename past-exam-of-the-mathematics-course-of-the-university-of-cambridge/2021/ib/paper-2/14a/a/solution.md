↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Direct evaluation of the [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde f(k)
&=\int_0^1e^{-ikx}\,dx-\int_{-1}^0e^{-ikx}\,dx\\
&=\frac{2-e^{ik}-e^{-ik}}{ik}
=-\frac{2i(1-\cos k)}{k}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The continuous value at $k=0$ is zero, in agreement with the vanishing [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) of the [odd function](../../../../../../odd-function.md) $f$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\widetilde f(k)=-\frac{2i(1-\cos k)}k}.
$$
