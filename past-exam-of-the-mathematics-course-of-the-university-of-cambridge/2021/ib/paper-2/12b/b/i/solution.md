↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Liouville theorem](../../../../../../../liouville-theorem.md) states that every bounded entire function is constant.

Because $\mathbb C$ is simply connected, the harmonic function $u$ has a global [harmonic conjugate](../../../../../../../harmonic-conjugate.md) $v$, so

$$
F=u+iv
$$

is entire. Positivity gives

$$
|e^{-F}|=e^{-u}<1.
$$

Liouville's theorem makes $e^{-F}$ constant. Differentiating this nonzero constant gives $F'=0$, so $F$, and hence $u$, is constant.
