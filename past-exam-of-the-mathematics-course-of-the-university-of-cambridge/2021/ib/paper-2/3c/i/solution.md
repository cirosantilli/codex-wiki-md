<h1 id="3c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiplication by the [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) $e^{2x}$ gives

$$
e^{2x}Ly=(e^{2x}y')'.
$$

Hence the [Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../../sturm-liouville-problem.md) is

$$
\widetilde Ly=-\lambda wy,
\qquad
\boxed{\widetilde Ly=(e^{2x}y')',\quad w(x)=e^{2x}}.
$$
