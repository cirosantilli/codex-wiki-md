<h1 id="18h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A vector $x$ is a [basic feasible solution](../../../../../../basic-feasible-solution.md) when

$$
Ax=b,\qquad x\geq0,
$$

and the columns $A_i$ for which $x_i>0$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md). Equivalently, after extending those columns to a basis of the column space of $A$, one sets all nonbasic variables to zero and solves for the basic variables, obtaining a nonnegative vector.
