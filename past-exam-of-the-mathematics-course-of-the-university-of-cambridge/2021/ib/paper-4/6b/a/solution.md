<h1 id="6b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the nodes in the order $0,1,2,3$. Their first [divided differences](../../../../../../divided-difference.md) are $4,-2,6$, their second divided differences are $-3,4$, and their third divided difference is $7/3$. The [Newton interpolation polynomial](../../../../../../newton-polynomial.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{
p_3(x)=4x-3x(x-1)+\frac73x(x-1)(x-2)
}.
$$

This is the unique [interpolating polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-interpolation.md) of degree at most three through the four data points.
