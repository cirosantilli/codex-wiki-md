Here $X$ has the [binomial distribution](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) $\operatorname{Bin}(n,\theta)$, so, up to a factor independent of $\theta$, the [likelihood function](../../../../../../likelihood-function.md) is

$$
L(\theta;X)=\theta^X(1-\theta)^{n-X}.
$$

For $0<X<n$, differentiating the [log-likelihood](../../../../../../log-likelihood.md) gives

$$
\frac X\theta-\frac{n-X}{1-\theta}=0,
$$

and the boundary cases give the same formula. Thus the [binomial proportion maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../../binomial-proportion-maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) is

$$
\boxed{\widehat\theta=\frac Xn}.
$$
