<h1 id="16a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The radial velocity and [velocity potential](../../../../../../velocity-potential.md) are

$$
u(r,t)=\frac{Q(t)}{4\pi r^2},
\qquad
\phi(r,t)=-\frac{Q(t)}{4\pi r}.
$$

Choose the gauge in the [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md) by evaluating it at infinity, where $u=0$, $\phi=0$, and $p=p_0$:

$$
\phi_t+\frac12u^2+\frac p\rho=\frac{p_0}\rho.
$$

At the cavity surface the vacuum pressure is zero, and the [kinematic boundary condition](../../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition.md) gives $u(a,t)=\dot a$. Since

$$
\phi_t(a,t)=-\frac1{4\pi a}\frac{dQ}{dt},
$$

Bernoulli's equation becomes

$$
-\frac1{4\pi a}\frac{dQ}{dt}
+\frac{\dot a^2}{2}
=\frac{p_0}{\rho}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\frac1{4\pi a}\frac{dQ}{dt}
-\frac{\dot a^2}{2}
=-\frac{p_0}{\rho}
}.
$$
