<h1 id="16a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [spherical symmetry](../../../../../../spherical-symmetry.md), the velocity is radial and constant over each sphere. Hence

$$
Q(R,t)=4\pi R^2u(R,t).
$$

Apply the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) to the fluid shell between radii $R_1$ and $R_2$. Since the flow is [incompressible](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md),

$$
0=\int_{\text{shell}}\nabla\cdot u\,dV
=Q(R_2,t)-Q(R_1,t).
$$

**Thus $Q$ is independent of $R$ and is a function of time alone. This is the flux law for [spherically symmetric incompressible radial flow](../../../../../../spherically-symmetric-incompressible-radial-flow.md).**
