<h1 id="9e/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Row reduction of the matrices whose columns are the displayed spanning vectors gives

$$
\dim U=3,\qquad\dim W=3.
$$

Row-reducing the matrix formed from all six columns gives

$$
\dim(U+W)=4.
$$

The [dimension formula for a sum of subspaces](../../../../../../../dimension-formula-for-a-sum-of-subspaces.md) now yields

$$
\boxed{\dim(U\cap W)
=\dim U+\dim W-\dim(U+W)
=3+3-4=2}.
$$
