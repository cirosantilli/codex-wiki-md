<h1 id="14a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $Q_k=P^{(k)}$. Differentiating [Legendre's differential equation](../../../../../../legendre-differential-equation.md) $k$ times and using the [Leibniz rule](../../../../../../leibniz-rule.md) gives

$$
(1-x^2)Q_k''-2(k+1)xQ_k'
+\bigl[\lambda-k(k+1)\bigr]Q_k=0.
$$

The induction step follows by differentiating this equation once: the derivative of the coefficient $-2(k+1)x$ contributes the additional term $-2(k+1)Q_k'$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\lambda_k=\lambda-k(k+1)}.
$$

For fixed $\lambda$, this is negative for every sufficiently large $k$.
