<h1 id="10g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume that $p$ is a [prime element](../../../../../../prime-element.md). If $p=ab$, then $p$ divides $ab$, so $p$ divides $a$ or $b$. If $p\mid a$, write $a=pc$; cancellation in the [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md) gives $1=bc$, so $b$ is a [unit](../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md). The other case is symmetric. Hence every prime element is [irreducible](../../../../../../irreducible-element.md).
