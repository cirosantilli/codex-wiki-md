<h1 id="10g/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For any commutative ring with identity, the [maximal ideal quotient criterion](../../../../../../maximal-ideal-quotient-criterion.md) states that an ideal $I$ is maximal exactly when $R/I$ is a field. Applying it to $I=(p)$ proves

$$
\boxed{(p)\text{ maximal}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad R/(p)\text{ is a field}.}
$$
