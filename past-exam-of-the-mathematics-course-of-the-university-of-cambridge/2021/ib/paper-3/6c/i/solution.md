<h1 id="6c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The states $\chi_1$ and $\chi_2$ are orthonormal [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) with hydrogenic energies

$$
E_n=-\frac{mK^2}{2\hbar^2n^2}=-\frac{K}{2an^2}.
$$

The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) therefore gives

$$
\langle E\rangle
=\frac14E_1+\frac34E_2
=\frac7{16}E_1
=\boxed{-\frac{7mK^2}{32\hbar^2}}
=\boxed{-\frac{7K}{32a}}.
$$
