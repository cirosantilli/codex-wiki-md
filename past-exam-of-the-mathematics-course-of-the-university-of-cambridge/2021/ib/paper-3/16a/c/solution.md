<h1 id="16a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

When $\Delta\ll1$, the small-argument expansion $\sinh z=z+O(z^3)$ gives

$$
f(y)=\frac{(1+i)\Delta\widehat y+O(\Delta^3)}
{(1+i)\Delta+O(\Delta^3)}
=\widehat y+O(\Delta^2).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
u(y,t)\simeq\frac{U_0y}{L_0}\cos\omega t
}.
$$

The [viscous diffusion time](../../../../../../viscous-diffusion-time.md) $L_0^2/\nu$ is then short compared with the oscillation period. Viscosity communicates the wall motion across the entire layer almost instantaneously, producing the linear profile of quasi-steady [Couette flow](../../../../../../couette-flow.md).
