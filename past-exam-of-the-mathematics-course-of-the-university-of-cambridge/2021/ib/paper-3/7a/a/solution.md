<h1 id="7a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Writing $R=x^2+y^2$, direct differentiation gives

$$
\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}
=\frac{-2x(x^2-3y^2)}{R^3},
\qquad
\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}
=\frac{2x(x^2-3y^2)}{R^3},
$$

so $\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}\mathbf u=0$. Likewise,

$$
\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}
=\frac{-2y(3x^2-y^2)}{R^3}
=\frac{\partial u}{\partial y},
$$

so the two-dimensional [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) $\partial_xv-\partial_yu$ vanishes. The flow is therefore [incompressible](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) and [irrotational](../../../../../../irrotational-flow.md) away from the origin.
