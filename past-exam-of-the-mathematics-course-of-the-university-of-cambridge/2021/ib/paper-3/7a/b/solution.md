<h1 id="7a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With the convention

$$
u=\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial y},
\qquad
v=-\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial x},
$$

a [stream function](../../../../../../stream-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{\psi(x,y)=\frac{y}{x^2+y^2}},
$$

up to an additive constant. Differentiation recovers both velocity components.
