For the [normal linear model](../../../../../../normal-linear-model.md), the [maximum-likelihood estimators](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) are

$$
\boxed{\widehat\beta=(X^TX)^{-1}X^TY}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{
\widehat\sigma^2
=\frac1n\|Y-X\widehat\beta\|^2
}.
$$

The divisor is $n$ for maximum likelihood, rather than the degrees-of-freedom divisor used by the unbiased residual-variance estimator.
