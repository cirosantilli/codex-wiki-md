<h1 id="18h/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $\widehat\beta$ is an affine transformation of $Y$,

$$
\boxed{
\widehat\beta\sim
N_p\!\left(\beta,\sigma^2(X^TX)^{-1}\right)
}.
$$

The residual projection $I-H$ has rank $n-p$. [Cochran's theorem](../../../../../../cochran-s-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac{n\widehat\sigma^2}{\sigma^2}
=\frac{\|(I-H)Y\|^2}{\sigma^2}
\sim\chi^2_{n-p}
}.
$$
