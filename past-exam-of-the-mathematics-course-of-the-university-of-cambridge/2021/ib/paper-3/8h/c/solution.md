<h1 id="8h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $h_i=\mathbb E_iT_6$. Then $h_6=0$, and [first-step analysis](../../../../../../first-step-analysis.md) on the closed class gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
h_1&=1+\frac14h_5,\\
h_2&=1+\frac14h_1+\frac12h_5,\\
h_5&=1+\frac14h_1+\frac12h_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Solving these [linear equations](../../../../../../linear-equation.md) gives

$$
h_5=\frac{20}{7},
\qquad
\boxed{h_1=\frac{12}{7}}.
$$
