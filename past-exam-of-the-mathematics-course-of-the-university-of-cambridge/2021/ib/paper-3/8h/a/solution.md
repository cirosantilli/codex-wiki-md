↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

States $i$ and $j$ [communicate](../../../../../../communicating-states.md) when each is accessible from the other: there exist $m,n\geq0$ with

$$
(P^m)_{ij}>0,\qquad(P^n)_{ji}>0.
$$

This is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md), and its equivalence classes are the [communicating classes](../../../../../../communicating-class.md). A class is [closed](../../../../../../closed-communicating-class.md) when no positive-probability transition leaves it.
