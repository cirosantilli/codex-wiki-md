<h1 id="4f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4F](../4f.md)

Normalization of the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) gives

$$
1=c\int_0^{\sqrt3}\frac{dx}{1+x^2}
=c\arctan(\sqrt3)
=c\frac\pi3,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{c=\frac3\pi}.
$$

The [expected value](../../../../../expected-value.md) is

$$
\mathbb EX
=\frac3\pi\int_0^{\sqrt3}\frac{x}{1+x^2}\,dx
=\boxed{\frac{3\log2}{\pi}}.
$$

The second moment is

$$
\mathbb EX^2
=\frac3\pi\int_0^{\sqrt3}\frac{x^2}{1+x^2}\,dx
=\frac{3\sqrt3}{\pi}-1.
$$

Therefore the [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{var}X
=\frac{3\sqrt3}{\pi}-1
-\frac{9(\log2)^2}{\pi^2}}.
$$

For the [median](../../../../../median.md) $m$,

$$
\frac3\pi\arctan m=\frac12,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{m=\tan\frac\pi6=\frac1{\sqrt3}}.
$$
