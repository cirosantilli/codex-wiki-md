<h1 id="8b/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose the $z$-axis along the line of intersection of the two planes. Their unit normals lie in the $xy$-plane; after choosing the $x$-axis, take

$$
n=(1,0,0),
\qquad
m=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0),
$$

where $\theta$ is the oriented angle between the planes. The two [reflection matrices](../../../../../../../reflection-matrix.md) restrict to

$$
H_n=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
H_m=\begin{pmatrix}
-\cos2\theta&-\sin2\theta\\
-\sin2\theta&\cos2\theta
\end{pmatrix}
$$

on the $xy$-plane and both fix the $z$-axis. Their product is

$$
H_mH_n=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos2\theta&-\sin2\theta\\
\sin2\theta&\cos2\theta
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Thus the [composition of two plane reflections](../../../../../../../composition-of-two-plane-reflections.md) is a rotation about the intersection line through angle $2\theta$.**
