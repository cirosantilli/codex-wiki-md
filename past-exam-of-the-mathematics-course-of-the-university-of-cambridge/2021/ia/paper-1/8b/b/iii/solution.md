<h1 id="8b/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A plane reflection has determinant $-1$, while a [rotation matrix](../../../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) has determinant $+1$. The determinant of their product, in either order, is therefore $-1$. Since every rotation has determinant $+1$, such a product can never be another rotation.
