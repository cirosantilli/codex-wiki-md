↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Writing $n$ as a column vector, the reflection is

$$
\Phi(x)=x-2n(n^Tx),
$$

so its [reflection matrix](../../../../../../../reflection-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{H=I-2nn^T},
\qquad
H_{ij}=\delta_{ij}-2n_in_j.
$$
