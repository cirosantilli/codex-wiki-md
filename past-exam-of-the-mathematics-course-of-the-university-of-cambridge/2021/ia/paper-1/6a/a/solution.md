<h1 id="6a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using the convention of the question, the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) and characteristic equation are

$$
\chi_A(z)=\det(A-zI),
\qquad
\chi_A(z)=0.
$$

For

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},
$$

one has

$$
\chi_A(z)=z^2-(a+d)z+(ad-bc).
$$

Direct multiplication gives

$$
A^2-(a+d)A+(ad-bc)I=0,
$$

entry by entry. Thus $\chi_A(A)=0$, verifying the [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](../../../../../../cayley-hamilton-theorem.md) for $2\times2$ matrices.
