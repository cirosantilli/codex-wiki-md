This can be false because a strictly increasing $g$ need not be surjective. Take

$$
g(x)=e^x,
\qquad
f(y)=
\begin{cases}
0,&y>0,\\
1,&y\leq0.
\end{cases}
$$

Then $g$ is strictly increasing and $f\circ g=0$ is continuous, while $f$ is discontinuous at zero.
