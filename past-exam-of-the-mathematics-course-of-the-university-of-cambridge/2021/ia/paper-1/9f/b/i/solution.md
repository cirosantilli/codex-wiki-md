**True.** If $x_n\to x$, continuity of $g$ gives $g(x_n)\to g(x)$, and continuity of $f$ then gives

$$
f(g(x_n))\to f(g(x)).
$$

Equivalently, this is the [composition of continuous functions](../../../../../../../composition-of-continuous-functions.md) theorem.
