This can fail. Let $X=\{1\}$ and $Y=\{a,b\}$, put $f(1)=a$, and let $g(a)=g(b)=1$. Then the [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) $g\circ f$ is the [identity function](../../../../../../identity-function.md) on $X$, hence is a [surjective function](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), although $f$ is not surjective.
