<h1 id="2e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The stated formula is [Cassini's identity](../../../../../../fibonacci-determinant-identity.md). It holds for $n=1$, and the recurrence gives

$$
F_{n+3}F_{n+1}-F_{n+2}^2
=F_{n+1}^2-F_{n+2}F_n,
$$

which is the negative of the expression at index $n$. This proves the identity by induction.

Consequently

$$
a_{n+1}-a_n
=\frac{F_{n+2}F_n-F_{n+1}^2}{F_{n+1}F_n}
=\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{F_{n+1}F_n}.
$$

The positive [Fibonacci numbers](../../../../../../fibonacci-number.md) tend to [infinity](../../../../../../infinity.md), so this difference tends to zero.
