<h1 id="8e/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each $N$, only finitely many [permutations](../../../../../../../permutation.md) fix every $j>N$: they are precisely the permutations of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$. Thus

$$
C=\bigcup_{N\geq1}S_N
$$

is a countable union of finite sets and is countable.
