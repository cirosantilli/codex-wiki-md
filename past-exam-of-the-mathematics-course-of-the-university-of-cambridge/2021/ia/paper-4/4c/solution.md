<h1 id="4c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4C](../4c.md)

Writing $x_i=(x_i,y_i,z_i)$, rotation about the $z$-axis gives particle $i$ the [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $\omega\sqrt{x_i^2+y_i^2}$. Its [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is therefore $m_i\omega^2(x_i^2+y_i^2)/2$, so

$$
T=\frac12I\omega^2,
\qquad
I=\sum_{i=1}^Nm_i(x_i^2+y_i^2).
$$

This is the defining mass sum for the [moment of inertia](../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) about the $z$-axis.

For the uniform cuboid, the [mass density](../../../../../density.md) is $\rho=M/(8abc)$ and

$$
I=\rho\int_{-a}^a\int_{-b}^b\int_{-c}^c(x^2+y^2)\,dz\,dy\,dx
=\boxed{\frac M3(a^2+b^2)}.
$$
