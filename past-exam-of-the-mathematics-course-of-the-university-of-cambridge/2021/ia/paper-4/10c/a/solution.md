<h1 id="10c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) about the origin is

$$
\tau=r\times F=-\frac{km}{r^3}r\times r=0.
$$

Since $\dot L=\tau$, the [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $L=mr\times\dot r$ is conserved. The force is the negative gradient of the [inverse-square potential](../../../../../../inverse-square-potential.md)

$$
V(r)=-\frac{km}{r},
$$

so the conserved [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) is

$$
E=\frac12m|\dot r|^2-\frac{km}{r}.
$$

Directly, $\dot E=\dot r\mathbin{\cdot}(m\ddot r+km r/r^3)=0$.
