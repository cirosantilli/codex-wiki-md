<h1 id="10c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Motion under a [central force](../../../../../../central-force.md) is planar. In [plane polar coordinates](../../../../../../plane-polar-coordinates.md), the radial component of the [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) is $\ddot r-r\dot\theta^2$, so

$$
\ddot r-r\dot\theta^2=-\frac{k}{r^2}.
$$

The conserved specific [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is $h=r^2\dot\theta$. Substitution of $\dot\theta=h/r^2$ yields

$$
\boxed{\ddot r=-\frac{k}{r^2}+\frac{h^2}{r^3}}.
$$

The last term is the centrifugal part of the radial [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md).
