<h1 id="10c/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At the [periapsis](../../../../../../../periapsis.md) $r_1$ and [apoapsis](../../../../../../../apoapsis.md) $r_2$, the given [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md) satisfies

$$
r_1=\frac{h^2}{k(1+e)},
\qquad
r_2=\frac{h^2}{k(1-e)}.
$$

Hence

$$
e=\frac{r_2-r_1}{r_1+r_2},
\qquad
h^2=\frac{2kr_1r_2}{r_1+r_2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{h=\sqrt{\frac{2kr_1r_2}{r_1+r_2}}},
\qquad \boxed{C=2}.
$$
