<h1 id="10c/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The transfer ellipse has [semi-major axis](../../../../../../../semi-major-axis.md) and [semi-minor axis](../../../../../../../semi-minor-axis.md)

$$
a=\frac{r_1+r_2}{2},
\qquad
b=a\sqrt{1-e^2}=\sqrt{r_1r_2}.
$$

Conservation of angular momentum gives constant [areal velocity](../../../../../../../areal-velocity.md) $h/2$. The satellite sweeps half the ellipse, whose area is $\pi ab/2$, so

$$
t_2-t_1=\frac{\pi ab/2}{h/2}=\frac{\pi ab}{h}.
$$

Using part (i),

$$
\boxed{t_2-t_1
=\frac{\pi(r_1+r_2)^{3/2}}{2\sqrt{2k}}}
=\pi\sqrt{\frac{a^3}{k}}.
$$

This is half the orbital period from [Kepler's third law](../../../../../../../kepler-s-third-law.md).
