<h1 id="3c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3C](../3c.md)

Let $M(t)=m_0-\alpha t$ be the trolley's remaining [mass](../../../../../mass.md). During $dt$, the expelled mass $-dM$ has ground-frame [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $v+u$. Conservation of [momentum](../../../../../momentum.md) gives, to first order,

$$
Mv=(M+dM)(v+dv)+(-dM)(v+u),
$$

and hence $M\,dv=u\,dM$. Integration yields the [rocket equation](../../../../../rocket-equation.md)

$$
v-v_0=u\log\frac{M}{m_0}.
$$

At the stopping time, $M=m_0e^{-v_0/u}$. Therefore the time spent ejecting gas is

$$
\boxed{\frac{m_0}{\alpha}\left(1-e^{-v_0/u}\right)}.
$$
