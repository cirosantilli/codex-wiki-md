<h1 id="7e/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since $p/q$ is not a root, $q^df(p/q)$ is a nonzero [integer](../../../../../../../integer.md), and hence

$$
|f(p/q)|\geq q^{-d}.
$$

Moreover $0<\theta-p/q<1$ implies $|p/q|<|\theta|+1$. Applying part (i) to $s=\theta$, $t=p/q$, and using $f(\theta)=0$ gives

$$
q^{-d}\leq A(|\theta|+1)^{d-1}\left|\theta-\frac pq\right|.
$$

Rearrangement proves

$$
\boxed{\theta-\frac pq\geq
\frac1A\left(\frac1{|\theta|+1}\right)^{d-1}\frac1{q^d}}.
$$

This is the core estimate behind the [Liouville approximation theorem](../../../../../../../liouville-approximation-theorem.md).
