This can be false. For any nonzero [transcendental number](../../../../../../../transcendental-number.md) $\beta$, the number $\gamma=1/\beta$ is also transcendental, since otherwise its reciprocal would be [algebraic](../../../../../../../algebraic-number.md). Yet $\beta\gamma=1$ is algebraic.
