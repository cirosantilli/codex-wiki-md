<h1 id="3b/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The data are rotationally symmetric, so seek a radial solution. The [radial Laplacian](../../../../../../../radial-laplacian.md) in two dimensions gives

$$
\frac1r(ru')'=16r^2.
$$

Twice integrating,

$$
u(r)=r^4+C\log r+D.
$$

Regularity at the center forces $C=0$, and $u(1)=1$ gives $D=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{u(r)=r^4}.
$$
