↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the annulus the logarithmic term is allowed, so

$$
u(r)=r^4+C\log r+D.
$$

The condition $u(1)=1$ gives $D=0$, while $u(2)=1$ gives

$$
16+C\log2=1.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
u(r)=r^4-\frac{15\log r}{\log2}}.
$$
