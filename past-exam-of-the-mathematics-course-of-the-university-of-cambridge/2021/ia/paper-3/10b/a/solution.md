<h1 id="10b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let

$$
I=\int_0^\infty e^{-ax^2}\,dx.
$$

Then

$$
I^2=\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty
e^{-a(x^2+y^2)}\,dx\,dy.
$$

Using [polar coordinates](../../../../../../polar-coordinates.md) on the first quadrant,

$$
I^2
=\int_0^{\pi/2}\int_0^\infty e^{-ar^2}r\,dr\,d\theta
=\frac{\pi}{4a}.
$$

Since $I>0$, the [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{
\int_0^\infty e^{-ax^2}\,dx
=\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4a}}}.
$$
