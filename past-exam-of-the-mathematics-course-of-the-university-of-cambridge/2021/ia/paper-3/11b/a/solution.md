<h1 id="11b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set

$$
x=au,\qquad y=bv,\qquad z=cw.
$$

The ellipsoid becomes the unit ball and the [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is $abc$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Vol}
=abc\operatorname{Vol}(B_1)
=\frac{4\pi abc}{3}},
$$

assuming $a,b,c>0$.
