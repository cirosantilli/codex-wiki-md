<h1 id="5d/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $a$ and $b$ generate $C_m$ and $C_n$. The order of $(a,b)$ in the [direct product of groups](../../../../../../../direct-product-of-groups.md) is

$$
\operatorname{lcm}(m,n).
$$

When $\gcd(m,n)=1$, this is $mn=|C_m\times C_n|$. Hence $(a,b)$ generates the whole product and

$$
\boxed{C_m\times C_n\cong C_{mn}}.
$$
