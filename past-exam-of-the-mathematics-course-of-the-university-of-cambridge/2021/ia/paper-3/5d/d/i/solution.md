<h1 id="5d/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $d=qr+s$ with $0\leq s<r$. Since $x^d=e\in H$ and $x^r\in H$,

$$
x^s=x^d(x^r)^{-q}\in H.
$$

The minimality of the positive integer $r$ forces $s=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{r\mid d}.
$$
