<h1 id="1d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Such a group does exist. Take $H=S_3$. Its center is trivial, so

$$
H/Z(H)\cong S_3
$$

has order six. Therefore

$$
\boxed{H=S_3\text{ is an example}.}
$$
