↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $F=(y,z,x)$,

$$
T_{ij}=\frac{\partial F_i}{\partial x_j}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0\\
0&0&1\\
1&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its antisymmetric part is

$$
\boxed{
\frac12(T-T^T)=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1/2&-1/2\\
-1/2&0&1/2\\
1/2&-1/2&0
\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

Also,

$$
B=\nabla\times F=(-1,-1,-1).
$$

For example, $-(1/2)\epsilon_{12k}B_k=1/2$, and the same componentwise calculation gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac12(T_{ij}-T_{ji})
=-\frac12\epsilon_{ijk}B_k}.
$$
