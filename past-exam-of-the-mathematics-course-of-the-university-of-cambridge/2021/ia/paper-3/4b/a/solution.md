<h1 id="4b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A second-rank tensor is antisymmetric when

$$
T_{ij}=-T_{ji}.
$$

Under an orthogonal Cartesian coordinate change with matrix $R$,

$$
T'_{ij}=R_{ik}R_{j\ell}T_{k\ell}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
T'_{ji}
&=R_{jk}R_{i\ell}T_{k\ell}\\
&=-R_{jk}R_{i\ell}T_{\ell k}\\
&=-T'_{ij},
\end{aligned}
$$

after relabelling the dummy indices. Hence the [antisymmetric second-rank tensor](../../../../../../antisymmetric-second-rank-tensor.md) property is independent of Cartesian coordinates.
