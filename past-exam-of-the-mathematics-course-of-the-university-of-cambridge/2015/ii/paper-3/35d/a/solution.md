<h1 id="35d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The coordinate transformation law for a [affine connection](../../../../../../affine-connection.md) has a homogeneous term with one upper and two lower index factors, and an inhomogeneous term involving second coordinate derivatives. The latter is the same for both [Levi-Civita connections](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md), so it cancels in their difference. Consequently

$$
\boxed{T'{}^a{}_{bc}=\frac{\partial x'^a}{\partial x^d}\frac{\partial x^e}{\partial x'^b}\frac{\partial x^f}{\partial x'^c}T^d{}_{ef},}
$$

which is exactly the [tensor component transformation law](../../../../../../tensor-component-transformation-law.md). Equivalently, $(\widetilde\nabla-\nabla)_XY$ is linear over functions in both $X$ and $Y$.
