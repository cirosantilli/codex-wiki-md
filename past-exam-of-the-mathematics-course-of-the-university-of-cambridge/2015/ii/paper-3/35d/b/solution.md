<h1 id="35d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At any chosen point take [normal coordinates](../../../../../../normal-coordinates.md) for $g$, so $\Gamma=0$ there. Substitute $\widetilde\Gamma=\Gamma+T$ into the stated [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) formula and subtract the original curvature. At that point the mixed products with $\Gamma$ vanish and ordinary derivatives of $T$ equal its covariant derivatives. The result is

$$
\widetilde R^a{}_{bcd}-R^a{}_{bcd}=T^a{}_{bd;c}-T^a{}_{bc;d}-T^a{}_{ed}T^e{}_{cb}+T^a{}_{ec}T^e{}_{db}.
$$

In unit-weight antisymmetrization notation this is **$\boxed{2T^a{}_{b[d;c]}-2T^a{}_{e[d}T^e{}_{c]b}}$**. Every term is a tensor, so the identity established in normal coordinates holds in every coordinate system.
