<h1 id="15f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a finite [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) $G$, subgroups $H,K$, and a complex [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) $V$ of $H$, the [Mackey restriction formula](../../../../../../mackey-restriction-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Res}^{G}_{K}\operatorname{Ind}^{G}_{H}V\cong\bigoplus_{g\in K\backslash G/H}\operatorname{Ind}^{K}_{K\cap gHg^{-1}}\operatorname{Res}^{gHg^{-1}}_{K\cap gHg^{-1}}({}^{g}V).}
$$

Choose one representative $g$ of each [double coset](../../../../../../double-coset.md) $KgH$. The [conjugate subgroup representation](../../../../../../conjugate-subgroup-representation.md) ${}^{g}V$ is a representation of $gHg^{-1}$ on the same vector space, with $(ghg^{-1})v=hv$. [Restriction of a representation](../../../../../../restriction-of-a-representation.md) keeps the vector space and restricts the acting subgroup. [induced representation](../../../../../../induced-representation.md) is $\mathbb CG\otimes_{\mathbb CH}V$, and analogously for each subgroup of $K$. The displayed isomorphism is an isomorphism of $K$-representations. These definitions specify every summand and its subgroup.
