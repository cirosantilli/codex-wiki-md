<h1 id="22j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a Borel set $A\subset[0,1)$, the two inverse branches of the [doubling map](../../../../../../dyadic-transformation.md) give the disjoint sets

$$
T^{-1}A=\frac A2\ \cup\ \frac{A+1}{2}.
$$

Each has half the [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md) of $A$, by translation and scaling invariance. Hence **$\boxed{\mu(T^{-1}A)=\mu(A)}$**. The piecewise affine map is Borel measurable, so it is a measure-preserving transformation. The printed one-dimensional “baker's map” is this doubling map.
