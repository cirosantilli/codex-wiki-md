<h1 id="38e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the Fourier mode $e^{i(i\theta+j\phi)}$, the [Forward Euler method](../../../../../../euler-method.md) has [amplification factor](../../../../../../amplification-factor.md)

$$
H(\theta,\phi)=1-4\mu\left[\sin^2(\theta/2)+c\sin^2(\phi/2)\right].
$$

It is real and ranges from $1-4\mu(1+c)$ to $1$. The two-dimensional [Fourier stability analysis](../../../../../../fourier-stability-analysis.md) therefore requires its minimum to be at least $-1$. Thus **the exact stable range is**

$$
\boxed{0<\mu\leq\frac1{2(1+c)}.}
$$

The endpoint is included; the highest-frequency mode then has amplification $-1$ and bounded norm.
