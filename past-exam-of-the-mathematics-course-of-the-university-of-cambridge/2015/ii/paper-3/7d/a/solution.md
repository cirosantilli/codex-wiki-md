<h1 id="7d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is $\boxed{H(q,p)=p^2/(2m)+V(q)}$. On a regular family of closed [phase-space trajectories](../../../../../../phase-space-trajectory.md), [action-angle variables](../../../../../../action-angle-variables.md) are [canonical coordinates](../../../../../../canonical-variables.md) $(\theta,I)$ in which $\theta$ is periodic modulo $2\pi$ and $H=H(I)$. [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) then give $\dot I=0$ and $\dot\theta=dH/dI$.

The [action variable](../../../../../../action-variable.md) is

$$
\boxed{I=\frac1{2\pi}\oint p\,dq=\frac1\pi\int_{q_-}^{q_+}\sqrt{2m(E-V(q))}\,dq.}
$$

The orientation is that of the motion, so the integral is the positive enclosed [phase-space area](../../../../../../phase-space-area.md) divided by $2\pi$.
