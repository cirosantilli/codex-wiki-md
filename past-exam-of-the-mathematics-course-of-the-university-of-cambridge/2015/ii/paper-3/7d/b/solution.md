<h1 id="7d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A constant-energy [phase-space trajectory](../../../../../../phase-space-trajectory.md) of the [harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) is the ellipse

$$
\frac{q^2}{2E/(m\omega^2)}+\frac{p^2}{2mE}=1.
$$

Its area is $\pi\sqrt{2E/(m\omega^2)}\sqrt{2mE}=2\pi E/\omega$. Thus **the action and slow energy change are**

$$
\boxed{I=E/\omega,\qquad E(t)\simeq I\omega(t).}
$$

The [action variable](../../../../../../action-variable.md) is an [adiabatic invariant](../../../../../../adiabatic-invariant.md) when $|\dot\omega|\ll\omega^2$, with $\omega$ staying positive. Averaging over many short oscillations therefore keeps $I$ approximately fixed; an increase in frequency increases the energy in the same proportion.
