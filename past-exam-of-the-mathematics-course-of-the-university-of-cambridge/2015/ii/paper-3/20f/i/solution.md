<h1 id="20f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $X\subset\mathbb A^n$ with vanishing ideal $I(X)$, the [Zariski tangent space](../../../../../../zariski-tangent-space.md) at $P$ is

$$
\boxed{T_PX=\{v\in k^n:\sum_{j=1}^n\partial_jf(P)v_j=0\ \hbox{for all }f\in I(X)\}.}
$$

It is the kernel of the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) of generators for the ideal, or intrinsically the dual of $\mathfrak m_P/\mathfrak m_P^2$. A point is a [singular point of an algebraic variety](../../../../../../singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) when this tangent dimension exceeds the local dimension of the variety; otherwise it is nonsingular. For an irreducible affine variety the local dimension at a closed point equals its dimension.
