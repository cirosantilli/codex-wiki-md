<h1 id="11e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $N=x+y$. Uninfected births and density limitation give $x(1-N)$, while [horizontal transmission](../../../../../../horizontal-transmission.md) transfers individuals to the infected class at rate $\beta xy$. Infected births are infected by [vertical transmission](../../../../../../vertical-transmission.md), and their net demographic growth is $y(1-N)-dy$. Consequently

$$
\dot x=x(1-N)-\beta xy=x[1-x-(1+\beta)y],\qquad \dot y=y(1-N)-dy+\beta xy.
$$

These give the stated equations. Adding them gives $\dot N=N(1-N)-dy$, displaying the additional mortality due to disease.
