<h1 id="11e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At the disease-free [equilibrium point](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $(1,0)$, the [linearization of a dynamical system](../../../../../../linearization-of-a-dynamical-system.md) of the infected equation is $\dot y=(\beta-d)y$. Thus **exponential invasion occurs exactly when** $\boxed{\beta>d}$. At equality there is no positive linear growth rate; the nonlinear boundary dynamics described below do not give sustained invasion.
