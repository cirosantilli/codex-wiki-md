↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Combining $dH=T\,dS+V\,dp$ with the preceding [Gibbs free-energy Maxwell relation](../../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy-maxwell-relation.md) gives

$$
dH=C_p\,dT+\left[V-T\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_p\right]dp.
$$

Setting $dH=0$ and solving for $dT/dp$ yields **the Joule-Thomson coefficient**

$$
\boxed{\mu=\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_H=\frac{V}{C_p}\left[\frac TV\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_p-1\right].}
$$

When $\mu>0$, a negative pressure change produces cooling.
