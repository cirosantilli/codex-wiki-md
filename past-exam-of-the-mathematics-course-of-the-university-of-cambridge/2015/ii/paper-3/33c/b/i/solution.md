<h1 id="33c/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The pipe is adiabatic, so the gas receives no heat. The work done on it at the inlet is $p_1V_1$, and the work it does at the outlet is $p_2V_2$. Neglecting kinetic and gravitational energy changes, the [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) gives $U_2-U_1=p_1V_1-p_2V_2$. Hence **the throttling process is isenthalpic**:

$$
\boxed{H_2=U_2+p_2V_2=U_1+p_1V_1=H_1.}
$$

The pressure drop is irreversible; constant enthalpy does not imply constant entropy.
