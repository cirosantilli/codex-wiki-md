The [heat capacity at constant pressure](../../../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) is $C_p=(\delta Q/\partial T)_p=T(\partial S/\partial T)_p$ for reversible heat input. The [enthalpy](../../../../../../../enthalpy.md) is $H=U+pV$, so $dH=T\,dS+V\,dp$. Along an isobar this gives **$\boxed{C_p=(\partial H/\partial T)_p}$**.
