<h1 id="33c/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a fixed amount of gas, the [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) gives $dU=T\,dS-p\,dV$. Define the [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) $G=U+pV-TS$, so $dG=-S\,dT+V\,dp$. Equality of its mixed partial derivatives gives **the Maxwell relation**

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial p}\right)_T=-\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_p.}
$$
