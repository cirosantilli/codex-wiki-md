<h1 id="15f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If the two-dimensional [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) were reducible, [Maschke's theorem](../../../../../../maschke-s-theorem.md) would split it into a direct sum of two one-dimensional [group representations](../../../../../../group-representation.md). In a basis respecting that decomposition, every representing [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) would be diagonal. All would therefore commute, contradicting the specified pair. Hence **the [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) is irreducible**. Complete reducibility over $\mathbb C$ is essential to this argument.
