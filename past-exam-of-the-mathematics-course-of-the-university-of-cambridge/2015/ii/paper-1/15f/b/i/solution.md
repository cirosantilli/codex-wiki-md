<h1 id="15f/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Call the two proposed [matrices](../../../../../../../matrix.md) $A$ and $B$. Direct multiplication gives $A^{2n}=I$, $B^2=\epsilon^nI=A^n$, and $B^{-1}AB=A^{-1}$. Both are invertible since $\epsilon\ne0$. They therefore satisfy every defining relation, and the [universal property of a group presentation](../../../../../../../universal-property-of-a-group-presentation.md) gives the desired [group representation](../../../../../../../group-representation.md).
