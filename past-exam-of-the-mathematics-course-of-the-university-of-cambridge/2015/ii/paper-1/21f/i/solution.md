↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [affine varieties](../../../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md) $X\subset\mathbb A^m$ and $Y\subset\mathbb A^n$, a [morphism of affine varieties](../../../../../../morphism-of-affine-varieties.md) is a map whose $n$ coordinate functions are restrictions to $X$ of [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in its $m$ ambient coordinates. Intrinsically, its coordinates belong to the [coordinate ring](../../../../../../coordinate-ring.md) $A(X)$; they must satisfy the defining relations of $Y$.
