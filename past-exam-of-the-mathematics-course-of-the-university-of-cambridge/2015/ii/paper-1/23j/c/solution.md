**No.** On the real line, the disjoint bounded Borel sets $[n,n+1)$, $n\geq0$, each have assigned mass zero, while their union $[0,\infty)$ has assigned mass one. This contradicts [countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md), so the set function is not a [Borel measure](../../../../../../borel-measure.md).
