<h1 id="23j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

On a fixed underlying set, a [pi-system](../../../../../../pi-system.md) is a nonempty family closed under finite intersections. A [Dynkin system](../../../../../../dynkin-system.md), also called a d-system, contains the whole underlying set, is closed under complements relative to it, and under countable disjoint unions. Equivalently it is closed under differences of nested members and increasing countable unions. A [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) contains the whole set, is closed under complements and arbitrary countable unions, hence also countable intersections. A Dynkin system need not be a sigma-algebra unless additional intersection closure is available.
