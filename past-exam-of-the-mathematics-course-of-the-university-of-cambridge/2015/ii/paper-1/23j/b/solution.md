The [dominated convergence theorem](../../../../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md) states that, on a measure space, if measurable $f_n\to f$ almost everywhere and $|f_n|\leq g$ almost everywhere for one integrable $g$, then $f$ is integrable and $\int|f_n-f|\to0$. In particular, **$\int f_n\to\int f$**. The statement applies to real or complex functions.
