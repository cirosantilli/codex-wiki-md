A [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) relative to a [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_n)$ is an adapted sequence of integrable random variables with $E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=X_n$ almost surely for every $n$. Adapted means $X_n$ is $\mathcal F_n$-measurable. Iterating gives $E[X_m\mid\mathcal F_n]=X_n$ for all $m\geq n$.
