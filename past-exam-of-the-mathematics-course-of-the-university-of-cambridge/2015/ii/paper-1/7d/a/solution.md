<h1 id="7d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [principle of stationary action](../../../../../../principle-of-stationary-action.md) says that the physical path makes the action stationary under smooth variations $q+\eta h$ with fixed endpoints $h(t_1)=h(t_2)=0$. It need not minimize the action. Differentiating and integrating by parts gives

$$
0=\delta S=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\left(L_qh+L_{\dot q}\dot h\right)dt
=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\left(L_q-\frac d{dt}L_{\dot q}\right)h\,dt.
$$

The [fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations](../../../../../../fundamental-lemma-of-the-calculus-of-variations.md) then gives the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dt}\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q}-\frac{\partial L}{\partial q}=0}.
$$
