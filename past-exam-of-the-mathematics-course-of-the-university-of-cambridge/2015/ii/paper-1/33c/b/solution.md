<h1 id="33c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For occupations $W_n=Wp(n)$ of $W$ distinguishable copies, the number of arrangements is the [multinomial coefficient](../../../../../../multinomial-coefficient.md) $\mathcal N=W!/\prod_nW_n!$. The ensemble entropy is $S_W=k_B\log\mathcal N$. [Stirling's formula](../../../../../../stirling-formula.md) and $\sum W_n=W$ give

$$
S_W\sim k_B\left[W\log W-\sum_nWp(n)\log(Wp(n))\right]
=-Wk_B\sum_np(n)\log p(n).
$$

Dividing by the number of independent copies and taking the large-ensemble limit recovers **$S=-k_B\sum_np(n)\log p(n)$**. Terms with zero occupation are interpreted by the usual $0\log0=0$ convention.
