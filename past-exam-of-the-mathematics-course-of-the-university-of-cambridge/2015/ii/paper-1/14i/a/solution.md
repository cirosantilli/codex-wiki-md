A [strongly regular graph](../../../../../../strongly-regular-graph.md) with parameters $(k,a,b)$ is a simple graph in which each vertex has degree $k$, adjacent vertices have exactly $a$ common neighbours, and distinct nonadjacent vertices have exactly $b$ common neighbours. Its order $n$ is often included separately as a fourth parameter.
