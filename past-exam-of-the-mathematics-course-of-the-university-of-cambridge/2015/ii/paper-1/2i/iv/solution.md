<h1 id="2i/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Apply the weak [weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](../../../../../../weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) to the [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) $\phi$ and to $-\phi$. Their boundary maxima are both zero, giving $\phi\leq0$ and $\phi\geq0$ on the closure. Consequently **$\phi\equiv0$**.
