<h1 id="2i/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At an interior local maximum, the second [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) in both coordinate directions are nonpositive: restrict the function to each coordinate line and apply the one-variable second-derivative test. Thus $\nabla^2\phi\leq0$ there, contradicting $\nabla^2\phi\geq\epsilon>0$. Since a maximum exists on the closure, **a maximum lies on the boundary**.
