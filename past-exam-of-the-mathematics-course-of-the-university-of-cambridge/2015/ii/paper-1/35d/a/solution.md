<h1 id="35d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The symmetrized covariant [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) equation is $\nabla_a\xi_b+\nabla_b\xi_a=\psi g_{ab}$. Lower $\xi_b=g_{bc}\xi^c$ and expand both [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md). Using the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) of the metric and their symmetry, the coefficient of $\xi^c$ simplifies by

$$
\partial_ag_{bc}+\partial_bg_{ac}-2\Gamma^d_{ab}g_{dc}=\partial_cg_{ab}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\xi^c\partial_cg_{ab}+g_{bc}\partial_a\xi^c+g_{ac}\partial_b\xi^c=\psi g_{ab}}.
$$

This is the coordinate formula for the [Lie derivative](../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) $\mathcal L_\xi g=\psi g$, proving equivalence with the [Conformal Killing vector field](../../../../../../conformal-killing-vector-field.md) condition.
