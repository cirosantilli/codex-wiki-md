<h1 id="35d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the product rule for the [Lie derivative](../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) to $\tilde g=e^{2\phi}g$:

$$
\mathcal L_\xi\tilde g=e^{2\phi}\left[2\xi(\phi)g+\mathcal L_\xi g\right]
=e^{2\phi}(2\xi^c\phi_{,c}+\psi)g.
$$

The prescribed transport equation makes this zero, so **$\xi$ is a [Killing vector field](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) for $\tilde g$**. This is a conformal rescaling that absorbs the original infinitesimal dilation.
