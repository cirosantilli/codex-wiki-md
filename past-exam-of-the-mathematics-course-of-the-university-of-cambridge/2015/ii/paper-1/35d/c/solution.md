<h1 id="35d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In [Minkowski spacetime](../../../../../../minkowski-spacetime.md), the dilation field $\xi=t\partial_t+x\partial_x+y\partial_y+z\partial_z$ has $\partial_a\xi^c=\delta_a^c$, so $\mathcal L_\xi\eta=2\eta$ and $\psi=2$. On $t>0$, take $\phi=-\log t$, for which $\xi(\phi)=-1$. Part (b) gives the explicit [Lorentzian metric](../../../../../../lorentzian-metric.md)

$$
\boxed{ds^2=t^{-2}(-dt^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2)}
$$

with the requested [Killing vector field](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) components. The restriction $t>0$ makes the logarithm smooth and the conformal factor nonzero.
