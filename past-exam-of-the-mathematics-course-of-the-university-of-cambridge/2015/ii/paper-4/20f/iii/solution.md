Here $\deg K_C=2$. For $D$, $D-P$ and $D-P-Q$, including $Q=P$, the degrees are $5,4,3$, all greater than two. Thus the correction term in the [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) vanishes and

$$
\ell(D)=4,\qquad\ell(D-P)=3,\qquad\ell(D-P-Q)=2.
$$

The first drop supplies a section nonzero at every chosen point, proving [base-point-freeness of a linear system](../../../../../../base-point-free-linear-system.md). For distinct $P,Q$, the second drop supplies a section vanishing at one but not the other, proving point separation. For $Q=P$, it supplies a section vanishing to order exactly one, proving tangent separation. Hence the complete [linear system of divisors](../../../../../../linear-system-of-divisors.md) is [very ample](../../../../../../very-ample-line-bundle.md) and $\boxed{C\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^3}$ is the induced [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md).
