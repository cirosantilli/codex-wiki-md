<h1 id="20f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a smooth projective [algebraic curve](../../../../../../algebraic-curve.md), a [linear system of divisors](../../../../../../linear-system-of-divisors.md) comes from a finite-dimensional space $V\subseteq H^0(C,\mathcal O(D))$. A basis of sections $s_0,\ldots,s_n$ defines $f(P)=[s_0(P):\cdots:s_n(P)]$, using any local trivialization; a common scalar change does not alter the projective point. A [base-point-free linear system](../../../../../../base-point-free-linear-system.md) makes this defined everywhere, and $f^*\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^n}(1)\cong\mathcal O(D)$. Pulling back a hyperplane gives the zero divisor of a linear combination of the sections, an element of the original system.

A system with base divisor $B$ can instead define a map after removing $B$, but its hyperplane pullbacks have class $D-B$. Thus base-point-freeness is needed for the stated original-system property. For a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md), the system must also separate distinct points and tangent directions; equivalently it is a [very ample linear system](../../../../../../very-ample-linear-system.md). On a smooth curve the latter tangent condition is separation of the length-two subscheme at each point.
