The [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) states, for a divisor $D$ on a smooth projective curve of genus $g$,

$$
\boxed{\ell(D)-\ell(K_C-D)=\deg D+1-g,}
$$

where $\ell(D)=\dim H^0(C,\mathcal O(D))$ and $K_C$ is a [canonical divisor](../../../../../../canonical-divisor.md), of degree $2g-2$. Negative-degree divisors have no nonzero global sections. The theorem's usual formulation here is over an algebraically closed field, as in the curve course.
