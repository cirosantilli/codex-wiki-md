# 36B

↑ **Parent:** [Paper 4](../paper-4-split.md)

Linearizing the [shallow water equations](../../../../shallow-water-equations.md) about depth $h$ at rest gives wave speed $c=\sqrt{gh}$. Replacing $h$ by $c$ in the nonlinear equations yields $c_t+u c_x+(c/2)u_x=0$ and $u_t+u u_x+2c c_x=0$. Direct addition and subtraction give

$$
[\partial_t+(u\pm c)\partial_x]\,[u\pm2(c-c_0)]=0.
$$

Thus the [Riemann invariants](../../../../riemann-invariant.md) $J_\pm=u\pm2(c-c_0)$ are constant on the [characteristics](../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) $C_\pm$ with speeds $u\pm c$. In this problem $c_0=\sqrt{gh_0}$.
