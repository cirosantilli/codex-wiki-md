<h1 id="34d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $f=1-r^2/a^2$ and $r_*=a\operatorname{artanh}(r/a)$. Since $dr_*/dr=f^{-1}$, $dt=du+dr/f$. Substitution gives the outgoing [Outgoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../../../outgoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md)

$$
\boxed{ds^2=-f\,du^2-2\,du\,dr+r^2d\Omega^2.}
$$

The radial block has determinant $-1$, independent of $f$, and all coefficients are smooth at $r=a$. This is a [coordinate singularity](../../../../../../coordinate-singularity.md) of the static chart, not a spacetime singularity. The transformation was defined inside the horizon, but the new metric itself extends smoothly across it.

At $r=0$ the spherical angular coordinates degenerate as usual. Cartesian spatial coordinates make the original static metric regular there: $f=1+O(r^2)$ and the extra spatial radial correction is $x_ix_j/[a^2(1-r^2/a^2)]$. Thus **the origin is not a spacetime singularity either**.
