<h1 id="37e/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let the distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) be $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{s'}$. Distinct-eigenvalue [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are independent. A relation $\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}a_kA^kv=0$ becomes $\sum_i c_ip(\lambda_i)w_i=0$, with $p(z)=\sum_{k<m}a_kz^k$. Since every $c_i\ne0$, it requires $p(\lambda_i)=0$ for every $i$. No nonzero polynomial of degree below $s'$ has that many distinct roots. Thus the first $s'$ powers are independent, while $\prod_i(A-\lambda_iI)v=0$ gives the next dependence. Hence $\boxed{s=s'}$. Equivalently the coefficients of these powers form a full-rank [Vandermonde matrix](../../../../../../../vandermonde-matrix.md) through this index.
