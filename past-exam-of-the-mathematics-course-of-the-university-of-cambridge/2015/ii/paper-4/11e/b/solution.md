<h1 id="11e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\xi=x-\langle x\rangle$ and $C_{ij}=\langle\xi_i\xi_j\rangle$. In the [linear noise approximation](../../../../../../linear-noise-approximation.md), $A_i=a_{ik}\xi_k$ and $B_{ij}=b_{ij}$. At the stationary mean, the preceding moment identity gives $\dot C_{ij}=a_{ik}C_{kj}+a_{jk}C_{ik}+b_{ij}$. Hence the stationary [covariance matrix](../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) solves the [Continuous Lyapunov equation](../../../../../../continuous-lyapunov-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{aC+Ca^T+b=0,}
$$

which is precisely the requested component formula because $C$ is symmetric.
