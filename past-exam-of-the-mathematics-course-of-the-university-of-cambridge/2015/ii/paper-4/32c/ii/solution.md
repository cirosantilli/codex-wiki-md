<h1 id="32c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $s_i=m+\delta_i$ and neglect the fluctuation product $\delta_i\delta_j$ in the [mean-field approximation](../../../../../../mean-field-approximation.md). Then $s_is_j\simeq m(s_i+s_j)-m^2$. There are $ND$ neighbour pairs, and each spin enters $2D$ pairs. The mean-field energy is

$$
E_{\rm MF}=-(B+2DJm)\sum_i s_i+NDJm^2.
$$

The spins now factorize, giving

$$
\boxed{B_{\rm eff}=B+2DJm,\quad Z_1(x)=2\cosh x,\quad C=e^{-\beta NDJm^2},\quad Z=C[2\cosh(\beta B_{\rm eff})]^N.}
$$

The constant corrects the double counting in the interaction; omitting it would give an incorrect mean-field [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md).
