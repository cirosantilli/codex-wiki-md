<h1 id="32c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [canonical partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) $Z=\sum_{\{s\}}e^{-\beta E}$, differentiation at fixed temperature gives $\partial_B\log Z=\beta\langle\sum_i s_i\rangle$. Thus the average spin per particle, or [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md) in these units, is $\boxed{m=(N\beta)^{-1}\partial_B\log Z}$.
