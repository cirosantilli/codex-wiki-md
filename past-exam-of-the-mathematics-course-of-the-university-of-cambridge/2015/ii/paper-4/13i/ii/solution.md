<h1 id="13i/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the [Kuratowski ordered pair](../../../../../../kuratowski-ordered-pair.md) $(m,n)=\{\{m\},\{m,n\}\}$. Its [rank of a set](../../../../../../rank-of-a-set.md) is $\max(m,n)+2$. Every pair in the [Cartesian product](../../../../../../cartesian-product.md) has finite rank, but those ranks are unbounded in $\omega$. Therefore $\operatorname{rk}(\omega\times\omega)=\sup_{m,n<\omega}(\max(m,n)+3)=\boxed{\omega}$.
