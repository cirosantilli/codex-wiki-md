<h1 id="13i/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Represent a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) by its graph of [Kuratowski ordered pairs](../../../../../../kuratowski-ordered-pair.md). For $f:\omega\to\omega^2$,

$$
\operatorname{rk}(f)=\sup_{n<\omega}\big(\max(n,f(n))+3\big)\leq\omega^2.
$$

The bound is attained by $f(n)=\omega n$, which is a [cofinal function](../../../../../../cofinal-function.md) into $\omega^2$. Taking the rank of the set of all such functions adds one at this attained maximum. Thus $\boxed{\operatorname{rk}((\omega^2)^\omega)=\omega^2+1}$. The extra successor is essential: unlike the previous product, this set actually contains a member of rank $\omega^2$.
