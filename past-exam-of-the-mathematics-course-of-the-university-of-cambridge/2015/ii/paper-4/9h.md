# 9H

↑ **Parent:** [Paper 4](../paper-4-split.md)

The [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md) says that for pairwise coprime positive moduli $n_i$, arbitrary residue classes modulo each $n_i$ specify one residue class modulo $\prod_i n_i$. For an odd positive integer $N=\prod p_i^{e_i}$, the [Jacobi symbol](../../../../jacobi-symbol.md) is $(a/N)=\prod_i(a/p_i)^{e_i}$, where each factor is a [Legendre symbol](../../../../legendre-symbol.md); for $N=1$ the empty product is one. The [Jacobi symbol](../../../../jacobi-symbol.md) is multiplicative in $a$, and is zero exactly when $a$ and $N$ have a common prime factor.
