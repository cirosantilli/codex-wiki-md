For nonnegative measurable functions $f_n$ on a [measure space](../../../../../../measure-space.md), the [Fatou lemma](../../../../../../fatou-s-lemma.md) states

$$
\boxed{\int\liminf_n f_n\,d\mu\leq\liminf_n\int f_n\,d\mu.}
$$

In particular $\mathbb E[\liminf X_n]\leq\liminf\mathbb E X_n$ for nonnegative [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md); infinite values are allowed. Nonnegativity, or a common integrable lower bound after subtraction, is essential.
