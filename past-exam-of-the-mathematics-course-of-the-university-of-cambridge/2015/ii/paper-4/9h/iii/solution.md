↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**False.** The [Carmichael number](../../../../../../carmichael-number.md) $561=3\cdot11\cdot17$ supplies a counterexample. For every $a$ coprime to $561$, the [Fermat little theorem](../../../../../../fermat-little-theorem.md) gives $a^{560}\equiv1$ modulo each of these primes, since $2,10,16$ all divide $560$. The [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md) then gives $a^{560}\equiv1\pmod{561}$. Thus the proposed [Fermat primality test](../../../../../../fermat-primality-test.md) can fail to detect a composite for every coprime base.
