**False.** The [Jacobi symbol](../../../../../../jacobi-symbol.md) $(2/15)=(2/3)(2/5)=(-1)(-1)=1$, yet $2$ is not a [quadratic residue](../../../../../../quadratic-residue.md) modulo three. A solution modulo fifteen would give one modulo three, which is impossible. A positive [Jacobi symbol](../../../../../../jacobi-symbol.md) need not indicate a square modulo a composite modulus.
