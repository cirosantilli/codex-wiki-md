↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Before a signal reaches the wall, the portion to the right of the leading characteristic $x=c_0t$ is unaffected. Its initial depth and velocity persist, giving $\boxed{u=0,\qquad c=c_0\quad(c_0t\leq x\leq a)}$ for $0\leq t\leq a/c_0$.
