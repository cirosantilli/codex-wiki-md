<h1 id="12c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a frame centered at the fixed point or centre of mass, a rotating material point has velocity $\dot r=\omega\times r$. The identity $|\omega\times r|^2=|\omega|^2|r|^2-(\omega\cdot r)^2$ converts the [rotational kinetic energy](../../../../../../rotational-kinetic-energy.md) into $T=\tfrac12\omega_iI_{ij}\omega_j$, where the [inertia tensor](../../../../../../inertia-tensor.md) is

$$
\boxed{I_{ij}=\int\rho(r)(|r|^2\delta_{ij}-r_ir_j)\,d^3r.}
$$

Likewise integrating $r\times(\omega\times r)$ gives the [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $\boxed{L=I\omega}$. These expressions refer to rotational motion about the chosen origin.
