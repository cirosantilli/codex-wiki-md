<h1 id="12c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [principal axes](../../../../../../principal-axis.md), $2E=\sum I_i\omega_i^2$ and $L^2=\sum I_i^2\omega_i^2$. Substitute the [Euler equations for a torque-free rigid body](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-a-torque-free-rigid-body.md):

$$
\dot E=\omega_1\omega_2\omega_3[(I_2-I_3)+(I_3-I_1)+(I_1-I_2)]=0.
$$

For the squared [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md),

$$
\tfrac12\frac d{dt}L^2=\omega_1\omega_2\omega_3[I_1(I_2-I_3)+I_2(I_3-I_1)+I_3(I_1-I_2)]=0.
$$

Thus **both $E$ and $L^2$ are conserved**, even though the body-frame components of $L$ generally change.
