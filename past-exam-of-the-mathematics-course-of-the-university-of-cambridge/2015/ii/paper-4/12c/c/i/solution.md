<h1 id="12c/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Place the origin at the centre and the axes along the edges. Symmetry makes the off-diagonal entries of the [inertia tensor](../../../../../../../inertia-tensor.md) zero. Uniform density gives $\langle x^2\rangle=a^2/12$, $\langle y^2\rangle=b^2/12$, $\langle z^2\rangle=c^2/12$. Consequently the [principal moments of inertia](../../../../../../../principal-moment-of-inertia.md) are

$$
\boxed{I_1=\frac M{12}(b^2+c^2),\quad I_2=\frac M{12}(c^2+a^2),\quad I_3=\frac M{12}(a^2+b^2).}
$$

For example $I_1=\rho\int(y^2+z^2)\,dx\,dy\,dz$ gives the first expression directly.
