<h1 id="15f/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The matrix $w=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\in\mathrm{SU}(2)$ conjugates $\operatorname{diag}(z,z^{-1})$ to $\operatorname{diag}(z^{-1},z)$. A [character of a representation](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is invariant under [conjugation](../../../../../../../conjugation.md), since the trace is invariant under similarity. Therefore $\boxed{\chi_V(z)=\chi_V(z^{-1})}$, and opposite torus weights have equal multiplicities.
