<h1 id="15f/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [character of a representation](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is $\chi_V(g)=\operatorname{tr}\rho_V(g)$. Average any positive inner product over the compact group using [Haar measure](../../../../../../../haar-measure.md) to obtain a [unitary representation](../../../../../../../unitary-representation.md). On the [maximal torus](../../../../../../../maximal-torus.md) $\operatorname{diag}(z,z^{-1})$, the commuting unitary operators admit a simultaneous eigenbasis. Each eigenline carries a circle [character of a representation](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $z^n$ by part (a). Hence

$$
\boxed{\chi_V(z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}m_nz^n,\qquad m_n\in\mathbb N_0,}
$$

with finitely many nonzero coefficients. Every element of [special unitary group](../../../../../../../special-unitary-group.md) is conjugate to an element of this torus, so this describes the full [character of a representation](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) via its torus restriction. Here the coefficient notation includes zero multiplicities.
