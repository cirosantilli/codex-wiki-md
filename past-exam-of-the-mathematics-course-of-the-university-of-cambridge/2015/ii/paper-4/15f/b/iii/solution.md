<h1 id="15f/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $V_r=\operatorname{Sym}^r\mathbb C^2$ for the irreducible [SU(2) representation](../../../../../../../representation-theory-of-su-2.md) of dimension $r+1$, with [character of a representation](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $z^r+z^{r-2}+\cdots+z^{-r}$. Here $V=V_3$, with weights $3,1,-1,-3$. Squaring its [character of a representation](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) and successively removing the highest-weight irreducible characters gives

$$
\boxed{V\otimes V\cong V_6\oplus V_4\oplus V_2\oplus V_0.}
$$

The dimensions $7+5+3+1=16$ check the decomposition; complete reducibility follows from the invariant inner product.

For the [exterior square](../../../../../../../exterior-square.md), add distinct pairs of weights. Its [character of a representation](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is $z^4+z^2+2+z^{-2}+z^{-4}=\chi_{V_4}+\chi_{V_0}$. Equivalently it is $(\chi_V(z)^2-\chi_V(z^2))/2$. Hence $\boxed{\Lambda^2V\cong V_4\oplus V_0}$, of dimensions five and one. The complementary [symmetric square](../../../../../../../symmetric-square.md) is $V_6\oplus V_2$.
