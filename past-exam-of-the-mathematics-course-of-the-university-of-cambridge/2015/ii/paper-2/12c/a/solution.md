<h1 id="12c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [canonical momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is $p=L_{\dot q}$, and a regular [Legendre transform in mechanics](../../../../../../legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) defines $H(q,p,t)=p\dot q-L(q,\dot q,t)$ after solving for $\dot q$. Its differential is

$$
dH=\dot q\,dp-L_q\,dq-L_t\,dt,
$$

because the $d\dot q$ terms cancel. With the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) $\dot p=L_q$, this gives [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot q=H_p,\qquad\dot p=-H_q.}
$$

In particular $H_q=-L_q$ at corresponding velocities, so a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md) for the [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) is also cyclic for the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md).
