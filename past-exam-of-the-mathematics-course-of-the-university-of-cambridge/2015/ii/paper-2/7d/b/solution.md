<h1 id="7d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Eliminate the cyclic velocities using $\dot\phi=(L_z-L_3\cos\theta)/(I_1\sin^2\theta)$. The conserved [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) becomes

$$
E-\frac{L_3^2}{2I_3}=\frac{I_1}2\dot\theta^2+V_{\rm eff}(\theta),\qquad\boxed{V_{\rm eff}=\frac{(L_z-L_3\cos\theta)^2}{2I_1\sin^2\theta}+Mgl\cos\theta.}
$$

Differentiating the [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md) gives

$$
-\frac{dV_{\rm eff}}{d\theta}=I_1\dot\phi^2\sin\theta\cos\theta-L_3\dot\phi\sin\theta+Mgl\sin\theta,
$$

which is also the $\theta$ [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md). Hence **$I_1\ddot\theta=-V_{\rm eff}'(\theta)$**. Deriving it directly from the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) ensures it also holds at turning points, where division by $\dot\theta$ in an [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) argument would be invalid.
