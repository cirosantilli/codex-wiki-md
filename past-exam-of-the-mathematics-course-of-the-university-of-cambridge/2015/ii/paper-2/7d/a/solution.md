<h1 id="7d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The two cyclic [Euler angles](../../../../../../euler-angles.md) give conserved [canonical momenta](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md)

$$
L_3=p_\psi=I_3(\dot\psi+\dot\phi\cos\theta),\qquad L_z=p_\phi=I_1\dot\phi\sin^2\theta+L_3\cos\theta.
$$

Here $L_3$ is the body-axis [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and $L_z$ the vertical [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md). Thus **$\omega_3=L_3/I_3$ is constant**. Since the [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) has no explicit time dependence, the [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) is also conserved:

$$
E=\frac{I_1}2\dot\theta^2+\frac{I_1}2\dot\phi^2\sin^2\theta+\frac{L_3^2}{2I_3}+Mgl\cos\theta.
$$

These are the three independent conserved quantities furnished by the continuous symmetries of the [heavy symmetric top](../../../../../../heavy-symmetric-top.md).
