<h1 id="17f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [cyclotomic polynomial](../../../../../../cyclotomic-polynomial.md) is $\Phi_8(t)=t^4+1$. Over $\mathbb F_7$,

$$
\boxed{\Phi_8(t)=(t^2+3t+1)(t^2-3t+1),}
$$

since the middle coefficient in the product is $2-9=-7=0$. Both factors have positive degree, proving reducibility. In fact their discriminant $5$ is not a square modulo $7$, so these are the two irreducible quadratic factors.
