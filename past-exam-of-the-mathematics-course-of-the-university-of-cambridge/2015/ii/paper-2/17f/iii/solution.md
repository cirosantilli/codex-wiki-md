<h1 id="17f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $K=\mathbb Q(\mu_m)$, $L=\mathbb Q(\mu_n)$. For coprime $m,n$, [Bézout's identity](../../../../../../bezout-identity.md) and powers of a primitive $mn$-th root give $KL=\mathbb Q(\mu_{mn})$. The degrees of these [cyclotomic fields](../../../../../../cyclotomic-field.md) are $\varphi(m)$, $\varphi(n)$ and $\varphi(mn)=\varphi(m)\varphi(n)$. The natural restriction map

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(KL/\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)\times\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q)
$$

is injective and, by these orders, bijective. Under this product description the [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) fixing $K$ and $L$ are the two complementary factors. Together they generate the whole group, so their common fixed field is $\mathbb Q$. By the [Fundamental theorem of Galois theory](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-galois-theory.md), **$K\cap L=\mathbb Q$**. This also includes $m=1$ or $n=1$.
