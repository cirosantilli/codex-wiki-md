<h1 id="8c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8C](../8c.md)

The flat matter-dominated [Friedmann equation](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $\rho=1/(6\pi Gt^2)$ when $a=t^{2/3}$. With $P=C\rho^{4/3}$,

$$
c_s^2=\frac{dP}{d\rho}=\frac{4C}3\rho^{1/3}=\bar c_s^2t^{-2/3},\qquad\bar c_s^2=\frac{4C}3(6\pi G)^{-1/3}.
$$

For [linear density modes with polytropic pressure](../../../../../linear-density-modes-with-polytropic-pressure.md), substitution gives the [Euler-Cauchy equation](../../../../../euler-cauchy-equation.md)

$$
\ddot\delta+\frac4{3t}\dot\delta+\frac{\bar c_s^2k^2-2/3}{t^2}\delta=0.
$$

A [power law](../../../../../power-law.md) has exponent satisfying $n(n-1)+4n/3+\bar c_s^2k^2-2/3=0$. Thus, for distinct roots,

$$
\boxed{\delta=A_kt^{n_+}+B_kt^{n_-},\qquad n_\pm=-\frac16\pm\sqrt{\frac{25}{36}-\bar c_s^2k^2}.}
$$

For $k\ll k_J$, the two modes approach $t^{2/3}$ and $t^{-1}$: gravity produces a growing [linear cosmological density perturbation](../../../../../linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) and a decaying mode. For $k\gg k_J$, put $\nu=\sqrt{\bar c_s^2k^2-25/36}$; the real solution is

$$
\delta=t^{-1/6}\{C_k\cos(\nu\log t)+D_k\sin(\nu\log t)\}.
$$

These are pressure-supported oscillations with a decaying envelope. For the [repeated-root cosmological density mode](../../../../../repeated-root-cosmological-density-mode.md) $k=k_J$, the complete solution is **$t^{-1/6}(A_k+B_k\log t)$**. The specified $k_J=5/(6\bar c_s)$ separates real exponents from logarithmic oscillations; the growing exponent actually crosses zero at $k=\sqrt{2/3}/\bar c_s$, a slightly smaller value.
