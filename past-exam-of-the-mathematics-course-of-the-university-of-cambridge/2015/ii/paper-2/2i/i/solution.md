<h1 id="2i/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A degree-$n$ [Legendre polynomial](../../../../../../legendre-polynomial.md) $P_n$ is orthogonal, for integration on $[-1,1]$, to every [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $n-1$, and has $n$ distinct roots in $(-1,1)$. Divide $p$ by $P_n$:

$$
p=P_nq+r,\qquad \deg q\leq n-1,\quad\deg r\leq n-1.
$$

The [Orthogonality of Legendre polynomials](../../../../../../orthogonality-of-legendre-polynomials.md) gives $\int p=\int r$. At every quadrature node $x_j$, $p(x_j)=r(x_j)$. The assumed exactness for $r$ therefore proves **exactness up to degree $2n-1$**, the characteristic property of [Gaussian quadrature](../../../../../../gaussian-quadrature.md).
