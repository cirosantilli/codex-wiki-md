A standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) has $B_0=0$, continuous paths almost surely, and independent increments with $B_t-B_s\sim N(0,t-s)$ for $0\leq s<t$. With a specified filtration, the increment is also independent of the information available at time $s$.
