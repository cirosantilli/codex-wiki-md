↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The fluid's tangential traction on the lower stationary surface is $\tau=\mu u_y|_{y=0}$ in the positive $x$ direction. Using the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) gradient found above gives

$$
\tau=\frac{\mu U}{h}-\frac h2p_X=4\mu U\left(\frac1h-\frac{h_0}{h^2}\right).
$$

The [drag on a plane beneath a translating cylinder](../../../../../../drag-on-a-plane-beneath-a-translating-cylinder.md) follows by integrating along the wall:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf e_x\cdot\mathbf F=4\mu U\frac\ell{h_0}\left(\pi-\frac\pi2\right)=2\pi\mu U\sqrt{\frac{2a}{h_0}}}
$$

per unit cylinder-axis length. This is the force on the lower surface, so its direction follows the cylinder's translation.
