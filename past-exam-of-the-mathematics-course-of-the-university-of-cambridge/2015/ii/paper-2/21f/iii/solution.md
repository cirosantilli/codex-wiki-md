<h1 id="21f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If a power of $a$ lies in $I\cap J$, then $a\in\sqrt I\cap\sqrt J$. Conversely, if $a^m\in I$ and $a^n\in J$, the power $a^{\max(m,n)}$ lies in both ideals. Therefore **$\sqrt{I\cap J}=\sqrt I\cap\sqrt J$** for any commutative ring.
