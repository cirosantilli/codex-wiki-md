↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [uniform prior](../../../../../../uniform-prior.md) has $s=1$, so the posterior is $\operatorname{Beta}(X+1,n-X+1)$. Its mean and mode are

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[\theta\mid X]=\frac{X+1}{n+2},\qquad m_{\theta\mid X}=\frac Xn.}
$$

Thus **the uniform-prior [posterior mode](../../../../../../maximum-a-posteriori-estimate.md) is the [maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md)**, including $X=0,n$. The mean shrinks toward $1/2$ and equals the [maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) only when $X=n/2$.
