<h1 id="29d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The flow of the [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is obtained by solving

$$
\frac{d\widetilde x}{d\varepsilon}=\xi(\widetilde x,\widetilde u),\qquad\frac{d\widetilde u}{d\varepsilon}=\eta(\widetilde x,\widetilde u),\qquad(\widetilde x,\widetilde u)|_{\varepsilon=0}=(x,u).
$$

Define $g^\varepsilon(x,u)$ to be this solution at parameter $\varepsilon$. Uniqueness implies $g^0=\operatorname{id}$ and $g^{\varepsilon+\delta}=g^\varepsilon\circ g^\delta$ wherever both sides are defined: shifting the solution parameter gives the same initial-value problem. In particular $g^{-\varepsilon}$ is the local inverse. This constructs the [one-parameter group](../../../../../../one-parameter-subgroup.md) generated by the [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md), generally as a local group if the flow is not complete.
