A [Lie point symmetry](../../../../../../lie-point-symmetry.md) is a one-parameter transformation of $(x,u)$ which maps the [graph of a function](../../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) solving the [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) to another such [graph of a function](../../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md), wherever the transformed independent variable is invertible along the [graph of a function](../../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md). Its induced transformation on derivatives is the prolongation. Infinitesimally the criterion is $\operatorname{pr}^{(n)}V(\Delta)=0$ on $\Delta=0$. The derivative coefficients can be computed recursively from $\eta^{(0)}=\eta$ by $\eta^{(j)}=D_x\eta^{(j-1)}-u^{(j)}D_x\xi$. This expresses the requirement that the transformed derivatives still satisfy the equation.
