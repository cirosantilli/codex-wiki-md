↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**Ordinary equilibrium blackbody [photons](../../../../../../photon.md) do not undergo this fixed-number condensation.** Their number adjusts through absorption and emission, while their [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) stays zero; cooling reduces the equilibrium [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) number rather than forcing an excess into the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md). This answer concerns the thermal blackbody model, not specially constrained systems where an effective [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) number is maintained.
