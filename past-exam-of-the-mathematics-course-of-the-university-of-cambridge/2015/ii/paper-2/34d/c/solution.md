<h1 id="34d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For Alice's circular geodesic, the conserved quantities or radial geodesic equation give $\Omega=d\phi/dt=\sqrt{r_s/(2r^3)}$. Consequently

$$
\left(\frac{d\tau_A}{dt}\right)^2=f-r^2\Omega^2=1-\frac{3r_s}{2r}.
$$

Bob at rest far away has $d\tau_B/dt\simeq1$. Thus the ratio of ageing rates is

$$
\boxed{k^2=\frac1{1-3r_s/(2r)}=\frac{2r}{2r-3r_s}.}
$$

For Alice's stable orbit $r>3r_s$, the denominator factor exceeds $1/2$, proving **$k<\sqrt2$**. Both gravitational time dilation and Alice's orbital motion enter this proper-time ratio.
