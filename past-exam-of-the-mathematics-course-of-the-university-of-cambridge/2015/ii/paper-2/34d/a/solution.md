<h1 id="34d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $f(r)=1-r_s/r$ and parametrize the [timelike geodesic](../../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) by [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) $\tau$. Time-translation and rotational symmetry conserve $E=f\dot t$ and $h=r^2\dot\phi$ in the equatorial plane. The normalized [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md) obeys

$$
-1=-f\dot t^2+f^{-1}\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2.
$$

Substitution and multiplication by $f$ give $\dot r^2+f(1+h^2/r^2)=E^2$, or

$$
\boxed{\tfrac12\dot r^2+V(r)=\tfrac12E^2,\qquad2V=(1-r_s/r)(1+h^2/r^2).}
$$

The dot denotes a proper-time derivative, and $E,h$ are [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) and [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) per unit rest mass.
