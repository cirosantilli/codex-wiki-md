<h1 id="34d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At a circular orbit, $V'=0$. Differentiating the Schwarzschild [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md) at fixed $h$ gives

$$
V'=\frac{r_s}{2r^2}-\frac{h^2}{r^3}+\frac{3r_sh^2}{2r^4},\qquad\boxed{h^2=\frac{r^2r_s}{2r-3r_s}.}
$$

A timelike circular orbit requires $r>3r_s/2$. Evaluating the second derivative after this substitution gives

$$
V''=\frac{r_s(r-3r_s)}{r^3(2r-3r_s)}.
$$

A stable nondegenerate minimum therefore requires **$r>3r_s$**. The boundary $r=3r_s$ is the marginal [innermost stable circular orbit](../../../../../../innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md), not a strict stable minimum.
