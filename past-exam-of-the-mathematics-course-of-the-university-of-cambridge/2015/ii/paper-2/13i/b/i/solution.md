↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first assertion is **false**: $1<2$, but $1+\omega=2+\omega=\omega$. The second is **true**. In the concatenation definition of [ordinal addition](../../../../../../../ordinal-addition.md), the copy of $\alpha$ is a proper initial segment of the copy of $\beta$ when $\alpha<\beta$, so $\gamma+\alpha$ is a proper initial segment of $\gamma+\beta$.
