<h1 id="20f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [surjective inverse-polynomial germ projection without even covering](../../../../../../surjective-inverse-polynomial-germ-projection-without-even-covering.md) comes from the inverse of $P(w)=w^3-3w$ over $G=\mathbb C$. Consider $R=\mathbb C\setminus\{-1,1\}$, with $\pi(w)=P(w)$. Since $P'(w)=3(w^2-1)\ne0$ on $R$, each $w$ defines a holomorphic inverse germ of $P$ at $P(w)$. This identifies $R$ with a component of the [space of germs of holomorphic functions](../../../../../../space-of-germs-of-holomorphic-functions.md): inverse germs continue along paths in $R$, and the holomorphic identity $P(f(z))=z$ persists under continuation. A germ with value $\pm1$ cannot exist, because differentiating that identity would give $P'(\pm1)f'(z)=1$. Since $R$ is connected, all its inverse germs belong to one [complete analytic function](../../../../../../complete-analytic-function.md).

The projection is surjective. A generic value has three distinct regular preimages; the critical values $2,-2$ each retain a simple regular preimage, respectively $2,-2$, even after their double critical preimages $-1,1$ are removed. Thus fibre size drops from $3$ to $1$ at these values, making an evenly covered neighbourhood impossible. This is **surjective but not a regular topological covering**.

If “regular” is specifically used for deck transitivity among genuine coverings, there is also a genuine nonnormal example. Remove the critical values from the base and all their preimages from the source:

$$
G=\mathbb C\setminus\{-2,2\},\qquad R=\mathbb C\setminus\{-2,-1,1,2\},\qquad\pi(w)=w^3-3w.
$$

This is a connected three-sheeted analytic covering. A small loop about each critical value swaps the two roots meeting there. The two swaps are distinct and generate $S_3$ (connectedness also forces transitive monodromy). The stabilizer of one sheet is an order-$2$ [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md), which is not normal in $S_3$. The [classification of connected covering spaces](../../../../../../classification-of-connected-covering-spaces.md) therefore shows **this covering is not regular in the deck-transitive sense**. Either convention has a fully explicit example.
