↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the inclusion, $\|Tf\|_\infty\leq\|f\|_\infty+\|f'\|_\infty$, with equality for a nonzero constant. Thus **$\|T\|=1$**. The domain unit ball is uniformly bounded and has $\|f'\|_\infty\leq1$, so it is [equicontinuous](../../../../../../../equicontinuity.md). The [Arzelà-Ascoli theorem](../../../../../../../arzela-ascoli-theorem.md) makes its image relatively compact in $C[0,1]$. Therefore **$T$ is compact**.
