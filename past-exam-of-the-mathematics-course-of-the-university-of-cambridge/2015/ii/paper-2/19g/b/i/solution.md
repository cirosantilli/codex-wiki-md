Take $f_n(t)=\sin(2\pi nt)$. Its domain [norm](../../../../../../../norm.md) is $1$, whereas its image [norm](../../../../../../../norm.md) is $1+2\pi n$. Hence **$T$ is unbounded**, so it has no finite [operator norm](../../../../../../../operator-norm.md) and is not a [compact operator](../../../../../../../compact-operator-split.md).
