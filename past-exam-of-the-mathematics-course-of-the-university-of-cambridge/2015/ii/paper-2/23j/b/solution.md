[Hölder's inequality](../../../../../../holder-s-inequality.md) states that for conjugate exponents $1/p+1/q=1$, $f\in L^p$ and $g\in L^q$ imply $fg\in L^1$ and

$$
\boxed{\int|fg|\,d\mu\leq\|f\|_p\|g\|_q.}
$$

It includes $p=1,q=\infty$ (and the reversed case), with the essential-supremum [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) for $L^\infty$.
