<h1 id="23j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $1\leq p<\infty$, $L^p(E,\mathcal E,\mu)$ consists of [measurable functions](../../../../../../measurable-function.md) with $\int_E|f|^p\,d\mu<\infty$, identifying functions equal [almost everywhere](../../../../../../almost-everywhere.md). Its [Lp norm](../../../../../../lp-norm.md) is $\|f\|_p=(\int|f|^p)^{1/p}$. Thus membership is a condition on an almost-everywhere [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md), not on individual point values.
