↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) says that, for a finite [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) $G$ and [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$,

$$
\boxed{|G|=[G:H]|H|,\qquad |H|\mid|G|.}
$$

Here $[G:H]$ is the number of left [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md). Every $g\in G$ lies in $gH$. If $gH$ and $g'H$ meet, write $gh=g'h'$; then $g'=gh(h')^{-1}$, so $g'H=gH$. Hence the left [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md) partition $G$. The bijection $h\mapsto gh$ makes each [coset](../../../../../../coset.md) have $|H|$ elements. Adding their cardinalities proves [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md).
