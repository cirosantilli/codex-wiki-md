**False.** Let $G=S_3$ and $H=K=\langle(12)\rangle$. For $g=(13)$, direct multiplication gives the [double coset](../../../../../../../double-coset.md)

$$
HgH=\{(13),(132),(123),(23)\}.
$$

It has four elements, and $4\nmid6=|S_3|$. This is a concrete example of [double-coset sizes not dividing group order](../../../../../../../double-coset-sizes-not-dividing-group-order.md); [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md) applies to [subgroups](../../../../../../../subgroup.md), not to arbitrary [double cosets](../../../../../../../double-coset.md).
