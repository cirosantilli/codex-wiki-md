<h1 id="12a/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**True.** Since $A$ and $A'$ are antisymmetric, the matrix $D=R^TA'R-A$ is antisymmetric. Invariance of contractions with every [antisymmetric second-rank tensor](../../../../../../../antisymmetric-second-rank-tensor.md) gives $D:B=0$ for every antisymmetric test matrix $B$. Such a matrix can be freely prescribed in one basis and then transformed to define a valid test [tensor](../../../../../../../tensor.md). Choose $B=D$ in that basis. Then

$$
D:D=\sum_{ij}D_{ij}^2=0,
$$

so $D=0$ and $A'=RAR^T$. Together with the given antisymmetry, this proves that $A$ is an [antisymmetric second-rank tensor](../../../../../../../antisymmetric-second-rank-tensor.md). The [antisymmetric contraction test for an antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../../../antisymmetric-contraction-test-for-an-antisymmetric-tensor.md) works because the [Frobenius inner product](../../../../../../../frobenius-inner-product.md) is nondegenerate on the antisymmetric subspace.
