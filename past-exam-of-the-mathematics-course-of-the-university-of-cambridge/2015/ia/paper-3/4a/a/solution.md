<h1 id="4a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [regular curve](../../../../../../regular-curve.md) parametrized by $u$, put $v(u)=|\mathbf x'(u)|>0$ and orient [arc length](../../../../../../arc-length.md) in the direction of increasing $u$. Since $ds=|d\mathbf x|=v\,du$, the [unit tangent vector](../../../../../../unit-tangent-vector.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{du}{ds}=\frac1v,\qquad \mathbf t=\frac{d\mathbf x}{ds}=\frac{\mathbf x'}v.}
$$

Regularity is the condition that permits division by $v$.
