<h1 id="4a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

This [plane curve](../../../../../../plane-curve.md) is an [Archimedean spiral](../../../../../../archimedean-spiral.md). Put $\mathbf e_r=(\cos u,\sin u,0)$ and $\mathbf e_\theta=(-\sin u,\cos u,0)$, so $\mathbf e_r'=\mathbf e_\theta$ and $\mathbf e_\theta'=-\mathbf e_r$. Then

$$
\mathbf x'=\mathbf e_r+u\mathbf e_\theta,\qquad
\mathbf x''=2\mathbf e_\theta-u\mathbf e_r,\qquad
|\mathbf x'|^2=1+u^2,\qquad
\mathbf x'\times\mathbf x''=(u^2+2)\mathbf e_z.
$$

Therefore the [curvature of a plane curve](../../../../../../curvature-of-a-plane-curve.md) is

$$
\boxed{\kappa(u)=\frac{u^2+2}{(1+u^2)^{3/2}},\qquad u\ge0.}
$$

The parametrization is regular even at $u=0$, where the [curvature of a plane curve](../../../../../../curvature-of-a-plane-curve.md) is $2$.
