<h1 id="6d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**True.** The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) gives a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md)

$$
\det:O(3)\longrightarrow\{1,-1\},\qquad \det(AB)=\det(A)\det(B).
$$

For an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), $A^TA=I$ implies $(\det A)^2=1$, and a reflection has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $-1$, so the target has order two and the map is onto. Its [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is exactly

$$
\boxed{\ker(\det)=SO(3).}
$$

This is the defining [special orthogonal group](../../../../../../special-orthogonal-group.md) inside the [orthogonal group](../../../../../../orthogonal-group.md).
