**True.** The [sign of a permutation](../../../../../../sign-of-a-permutation.md) is a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $\operatorname{sgn}:S_3\to\{1,-1\}$, as follows, for example, from the multiplicative [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of [permutation matrices](../../../../../../permutation-matrix.md). Its [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is the [alternating group](../../../../../../alternating-group.md)

$$
\boxed{A_3=\{e,(123),(132)\},\qquad |A_3|=3.}
$$

A transposition maps to $-1$, so this homomorphism is onto.
