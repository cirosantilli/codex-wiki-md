<h1 id="1d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $a,b$ be the generating [involutions](../../../../../../involution.md). If $a\ne b$, set $x=ab$ and $y=b$. Then $x\ne e$, so its [order of a group element](../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) is at least two or infinite, and

$$
yxy^{-1}=b(ab)b=ba=(ab)^{-1}=x^{-1},\qquad a=xy.
$$

Therefore $x,y$ generate the [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) and satisfy the required dihedral inversion relation. If $a=b$, the [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is $C_2$ and $x=y=a$ gives its permitted degenerate description. This proves the [two-involution characterization of a dihedral group](../../../../../../two-involution-characterization-of-a-dihedral-group.md) without excluding either $C_2$ or the [infinite dihedral group](../../../../../../infinite-dihedral-group.md).
