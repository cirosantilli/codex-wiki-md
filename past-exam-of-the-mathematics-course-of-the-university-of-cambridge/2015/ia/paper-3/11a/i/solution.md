<h1 id="11a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) to $\mathbf F=\psi\nabla\phi$, for functions smooth enough on the closure of the bounded piecewise smooth volume. The [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
\nabla\cdot(\psi\nabla\phi)=\nabla\psi\cdot\nabla\phi+\psi\nabla^2\phi,
\qquad (\psi\nabla\phi)\cdot\mathbf n=\psi\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial n}.
$$

Thus [Green's first theorem](../../../../../../green-s-first-identity.md), also called [Green's first identity](../../../../../../green-s-first-identity.md), is

$$
\boxed{\int_V(\psi\nabla^2\phi+\nabla\psi\cdot\nabla\phi)\,dV
=\int_{\partial V}\psi\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial n}\,dS.}
$$

The normal is outward. This identifies the boundary term and its sign directly from the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md).
