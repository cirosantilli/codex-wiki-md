<h1 id="4c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4C](../4c.md)

Use the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) convention $+---$. A particle's [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) is $P=(E/c,\mathbf p)$, with $P\cdot P=m^2c^2$ for [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) $m$. For a [photon](../../../../../photon.md), its energy and [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) obey $E=hc/\lambda$, and its [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) is null. Here $h$ is the [Planck constant](../../../../../planck-constant.md).

The initially stationary electron has $P=(mc,0,0,0)$. Choose the scattering plane as the $xy$ plane. The incoming and outgoing [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md) are

$$
K_1=\frac h{\lambda_1}(1,1,0,0),\qquad K_2=\frac h{\lambda_2}(1,\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0).
$$

By [four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md), the final electron has $P'=P+K_1-K_2$. Its [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) is unchanged, so the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) gives $P'^2=P^2=m^2c^2$. Since $K_1^2=K_2^2=0$, expansion yields

$$
P\cdot(K_1-K_2)=K_1\cdot K_2,
$$

or

$$
mc h\left(\frac1{\lambda_1}-\frac1{\lambda_2}\right)=\frac{h^2}{\lambda_1\lambda_2}(1-\cos\theta).
$$

Multiplying through gives the [Compton scattering](../../../../../compton-scattering.md) shift:

$$
\boxed{\lambda_2-\lambda_1=\frac h{mc}(1-\cos\theta)=\frac{2h}{mc}\sin^2(\theta/2).}
$$

It is nonnegative and vanishes for forward scattering, consistent with the recoil energy being supplied by the [photon](../../../../../photon.md).
