# 8E

↑ **Parent:** [Paper 4](../paper-4-split.md)

A [countable set](../../../../countable-set.md) means here a finite set or a countably infinite set, equivalently a set admitting an [injection](../../../../injective-function.md) into $\mathbb N$. This convention includes the empty set. An infinite set is countably infinite precisely when it can be put in [bijection](../../../../bijection.md) with $\mathbb N$.
