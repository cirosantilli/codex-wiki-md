<h1 id="8e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $j:B\to\mathbb N$ be an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) witnessing that $B$ is a [countable set](../../../../../../countable-set.md). The composition $j\circ f:A\to\mathbb N$ is an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md), so $A$ is a [countable set](../../../../../../countable-set.md). This proof includes finite and empty sets.
