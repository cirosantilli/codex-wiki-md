<h1 id="8e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose [injections](../../../../../../../injective-function.md) $a:X\to\mathbb N$ and $b:Y\to\mathbb N$. The map $(x,y)\mapsto(a(x),b(y))$ is an [injection](../../../../../../../injective-function.md) into the [countable set](../../../../../../../countable-set.md) $\mathbb N\times\mathbb N$. Composing with the [Cantor pairing function](../../../../../../../cantor-pairing-function.md) gives an [injection](../../../../../../../injective-function.md) into $\mathbb N$. Thus **the Cartesian product of two countable sets is countable**, including the case of an empty factor.
