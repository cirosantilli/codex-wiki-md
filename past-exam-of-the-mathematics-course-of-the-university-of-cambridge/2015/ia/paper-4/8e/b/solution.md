<h1 id="8e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) $j:A\to\mathbb N$. For each $b\in B$, its fiber under the [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) $f$ is nonempty, so define

$$
h(b)=\min\{j(a):f(a)=b\}.
$$

Two distinct fibers cannot contain the same label $j(a)$, because $j$ is an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) and $f(a)$ has a single value. Thus $h:B\to\mathbb N$ is an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md), proving that $B$ is a [countable set](../../../../../../countable-set.md). Taking the least label avoids any need for a separate choice of one element from each fiber. If $A$ is empty, surjectivity makes $B$ empty as well.

For the unheaded enumeration request, list pairs of positive [natural numbers](../../../../../../natural-number.md) by their sum. An explicit [Cantor pairing function](../../../../../../cantor-pairing-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{\pi(m,n)=\frac{(m+n-2)(m+n-1)}2+n.}
$$

For $d=m+n-2\ge0$, the positive [integers](../../../../../../integer.md) $n=1,\ldots,d+1$ give the consecutive labels $d(d+1)/2+1,\ldots,(d+1)(d+2)/2$. These blocks are disjoint, consecutive and cover $\mathbb N$. Consequently $\pi$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) from $\mathbb N\times\mathbb N$ to $\mathbb N$, so **$\mathbb N\times\mathbb N$ is countable**.
