<h1 id="5b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) to the path from $x_1$ through $y_2$ to $y_1$:

$$
\|x_1-y_1\|-\|x_1-y_2\|\leq\|y_1-y_2\|.
$$

This is one direction of the [reverse triangle inequality](../../../../../../reverse-triangle-inequality.md). A second application of the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md), this time through $x_2$, gives

$$
\boxed{\|x_1-y_1\|-\|x_1-y_2\|\leq\|y_1-y_2\|\leq\|x_2-y_1\|+\|x_2-y_2\|.}
$$

The resulting [four-point triangle inequality](../../../../../../four-point-triangle-inequality.md) follows without any assumptions on coincidences among the four points.
