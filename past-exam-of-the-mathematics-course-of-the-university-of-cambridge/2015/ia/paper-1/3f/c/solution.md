<h1 id="3f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $x>0$, the exponent $x/(1+x)$ lies in $(0,1)$, and $0\leq\cos^4x\leq1$. Consequently

$$
0\leq\frac{(1+x)^{x/(1+x)}\cos^4x}{e^x}\leq\frac{1+x}{e^x}\leq\frac{2(1+x)}{x^2}.
$$

The last inequality uses the positive-term [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) $e^x=\sum_{k\geq0}x^k/k!\geq x^2/2$ for $x>0$. The final bound tends to zero, so the [squeeze theorem](../../../../../../squeeze-theorem.md) gives **limit $0$**.
