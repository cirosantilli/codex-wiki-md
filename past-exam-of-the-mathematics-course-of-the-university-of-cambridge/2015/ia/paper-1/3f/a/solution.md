<h1 id="3f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $0<x<\pi/2$, the usual unit-circle area comparison, with angles measured in radians, gives $\sin x\leq x\leq\tan x$. Hence

$$
\cos x\leq\frac{\sin x}{x}\leq1.
$$

The [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) is a [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at zero, so the [squeeze theorem](../../../../../../squeeze-theorem.md) yields the right-hand [limit of a function](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) $1$. The quotient $\sin x/x$ is even, so the left-hand [limit of a function](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) is the same. Thus **the limit is $1$**.
