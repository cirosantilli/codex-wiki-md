<h1 id="7a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $C=S\Lambda_CS^{-1}$ and $D=S\Lambda_DS^{-1}$ for an invertible $S$ and diagonal matrices $\Lambda_C,\Lambda_D$. Since diagonal matrices commute,

$$
CD=S\Lambda_C\Lambda_DS^{-1}=S\Lambda_D\Lambda_CS^{-1}=DC.
$$

Therefore **simultaneous diagonalization implies commutation**. This argument works over either $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ and explains why [simultaneous diagonalization](../../../../../../simultaneous-diagonalization.md) produces [commuting operators](../../../../../../commuting-operators.md).
