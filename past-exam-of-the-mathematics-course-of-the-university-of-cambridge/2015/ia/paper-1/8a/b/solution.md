<h1 id="8a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), $A^\dagger=A$, so $A^\dagger A=A^2=AA^\dagger$ and $A$ is a [normal matrix](../../../../../../normal-matrix.md). If $Ax=\lambda x$ with $x\ne0$, part (a)(iii) gives $A^\dagger x=\overline\lambda x$. Since also $A^\dagger x=Ax=\lambda x$, we obtain $\lambda=\overline\lambda$. Thus **every eigenvalue is real**.
