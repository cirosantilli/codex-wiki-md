<h1 id="8a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md), $A^\dagger=A^{-1}$, so both defining products for a [normal matrix](../../../../../../normal-matrix.md) are $I$. The matrix is invertible, hence no [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is zero. If $Ax=\lambda x$, then $A^{-1}x=\lambda^{-1}x$, while part (a)(iii) gives $A^\dagger x=\overline\lambda x$. Therefore $\overline\lambda=\lambda^{-1}$, and

$$
\boxed{|\lambda|=1.}
$$

Thus **all eigenvalues have unit modulus**.
