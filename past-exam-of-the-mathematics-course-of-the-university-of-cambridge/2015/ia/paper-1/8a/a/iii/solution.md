<h1 id="8a/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $Ax=\lambda x$, then $M=A-\lambda I$ is a [normal matrix](../../../../../../../normal-matrix.md) by part (ii), and $Mx=0$. Part (i), applied to $M$, yields $\|M^\dagger x\|=\|Mx\|=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{A^\dagger x=\overline\lambda x.}
$$

The nonzero vector $x$ is consequently an [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of the [conjugate transpose](../../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\overline\lambda$. This [adjoint eigenvector identity for a normal matrix](../../../../../../../adjoint-eigenvector-identity-for-a-normal-matrix.md) will also determine the special spectral restrictions below.
