<h1 id="8a/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the standard complex [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product.md), $\|y\|^2=y^\dagger y$. Using the [conjugate transpose](../../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) and the defining equality for a [normal matrix](../../../../../../../normal-matrix.md),

$$
\|Ax\|^2=x^\dagger A^\dagger Ax=x^\dagger AA^\dagger x=\|A^\dagger x\|^2.
$$

Both [norms](../../../../../../../norm.md) are nonnegative, so **$\|Ax\|=\|A^\dagger x\|$** for every $x$.
