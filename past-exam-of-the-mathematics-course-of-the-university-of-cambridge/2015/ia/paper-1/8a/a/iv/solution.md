The [adjoint eigenvector identity for a normal matrix](../../../../../../../adjoint-eigenvector-identity-for-a-normal-matrix.md) gives $A^\dagger x_\lambda=\overline\lambda x_\lambda$. Hence

$$
\mu x_\lambda^\dagger x_\mu=x_\lambda^\dagger Ax_\mu=(A^\dagger x_\lambda)^\dagger x_\mu=\lambda x_\lambda^\dagger x_\mu.
$$

As $\mu\ne\lambda$, **$x_\lambda^\dagger x_\mu=0$**. Thus [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) in distinct [eigenspaces](../../../../../../../eigenspace.md) of a [normal matrix](../../../../../../../normal-matrix.md) are orthogonal in the complex [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product.md).
