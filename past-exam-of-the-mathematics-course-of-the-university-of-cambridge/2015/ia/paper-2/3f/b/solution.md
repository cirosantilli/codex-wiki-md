<h1 id="3f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Condition on the fair coin, taken independent of $U$. For the [squared uniform distribution](../../../../../../squared-uniform-distribution.md), the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) of $U^2$ is $\sqrt{x}$ on $0<x<1$, giving [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $1/(2\sqrt{x})$. Reflection about $1/2$ gives the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) of $1-U^2$ as $1/(2\sqrt{1-x})$. Averaging the two conditional [probability density functions](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) produces this [mixture model](../../../../../../mixture-model.md):

$$
\boxed{f_X(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac1{4\sqrt{x}}+\dfrac1{4\sqrt{1-x}}&0<x<1,\\0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}}
$$

There are no atoms at the endpoints; each integrable singularity contributes half the total mass. Equivalently the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is $[\sqrt{x}+1-\sqrt{1-x}]/2$ on the interval.
