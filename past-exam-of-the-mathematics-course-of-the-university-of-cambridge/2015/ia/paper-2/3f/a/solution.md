<h1 id="3f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $x\geq0$, the monotonicity of the logarithm and the [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) give

$$
P\left(-\frac{\log U}{\lambda}\leq x\right)=P(U\geq e^{-\lambda x})=1-e^{-\lambda x}.
$$

For $x<0$ the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is zero. Differentiation gives the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $f(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$ for $x>0$, and zero elsewhere. **The distribution is $\boxed{\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda)}$**, with rate $\lambda$. This is [inverse transform sampling](../../../../../../inverse-transform-sampling.md) of an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md).
