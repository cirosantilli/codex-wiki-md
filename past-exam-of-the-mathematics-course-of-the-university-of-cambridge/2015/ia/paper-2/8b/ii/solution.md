<h1 id="8b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The three [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) still form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md), so the same coefficient calculation applies to this [diagonalizable matrix](../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) matrix. **The general solution is**

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x(t)=A_1e^{\lambda_1t}\mathbf e_1+e^{\lambda_2t}(A_2\mathbf e_2+A_3\mathbf e_3).}
$$

A repeated [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) alone does not create a polynomial factor in time; that factor is needed only when a nontrivial [Jordan block](../../../../../../jordan-block.md) prevents a full eigenvector [basis](../../../../../../basis.md).
