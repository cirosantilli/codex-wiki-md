<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For two [density operators](../../../../../../density-matrix.md), the [trace distance](../../../../../../trace-distance.md) and the unsquared [quantum fidelity](../../../../../../fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) are

$$
\boxed{D(\rho,\sigma)=\frac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1,\qquad F(\rho,\sigma)=\operatorname{Tr}\sqrt{\sqrt\rho\,\sigma\sqrt\rho}=\|\sqrt\rho\sqrt\sigma\|_1.}
$$

The unsquared convention matters: if $P=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ is a [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md), then $\sqrt\rho P\sqrt\rho$ is a rank-one [positive operator](../../../../../../positive-operator.md) with its sole nonzero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) equal to $\langle\psi|\rho|\psi\rangle$. Hence

$$
\boxed{F(\rho,P)^2=\langle\psi|\rho|\psi\rangle.}
$$
