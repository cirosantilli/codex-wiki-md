<h1 id="4/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every word in the [typical set](../../../../../../../typical-set.md) has probability at least $2^{-n(H(Z)+\delta)}$. Consequently

$$
1\geq\sum_{z^n\in T_\delta^n}P_Z^n(z^n)\geq|T_\delta^n|\,2^{-n(H(Z)+\delta)}.
$$

Rearranging proves the upper estimate in the [typical-set cardinality bounds](../../../../../../../typical-set-cardinality-bounds.md):

$$
\boxed{|T_\delta^n(P_Z)|\leq2^{n(H(Z)+\delta)}.}
$$
