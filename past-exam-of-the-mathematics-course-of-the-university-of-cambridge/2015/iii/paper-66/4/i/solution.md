The [Shannon entropy](../../../../../../information-entropy.md) of a finite-valued [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{H(X)=-\sum_{x\in\mathcal A_X}P_X(x)\log_2P_X(x),\qquad0\log_2 0=0.}
$$

An [epsilon-sufficient set](../../../../../../epsilon-sufficient-set.md) is a subset $S\subseteq\mathcal A_X$ retaining all but at most $\epsilon$ of the probability:

$$
\boxed{\Pr(X\in S)=\sum_{x\in S}P_X(x)\geq1-\epsilon.}
$$

Usually $0\leq\epsilon\leq1$. Sufficiency here concerns retained probability mass, rather than a [sufficient statistic](../../../../../../sufficient-statistic.md).
