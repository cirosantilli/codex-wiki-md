<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a bipartite [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) $\rho_{AB}$, let $S(A)_\rho=S(\rho_A)$, with $\rho_A=\operatorname{Tr}_B\rho_{AB}$, and similarly for other systems. The [quantum conditional entropy](../../../../../../quantum-conditional-entropy.md) and [coherent information](../../../../../../coherent-information.md) are

$$
\boxed{H(A|B)_\rho=S(AB)_\rho-S(B)_\rho,\qquad I(A\rangle B)_\rho=S(B)_\rho-S(AB)_\rho=-H(A|B)_\rho.}
$$

Unlike classical [conditional entropy](../../../../../../conditional-entropy.md), [quantum conditional entropy](../../../../../../quantum-conditional-entropy.md) can be negative. For a [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md), $S(AB)=0$ and $S(B)=1$, giving $H(A|B)=-1$ and [coherent information](../../../../../../coherent-information.md) equal to one bit.
