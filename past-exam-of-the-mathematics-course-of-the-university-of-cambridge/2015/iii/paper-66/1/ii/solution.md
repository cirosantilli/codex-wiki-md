↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By [spectral decomposition](../../../../../../spectral-decomposition.md), the [Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md) is the [Shannon entropy](../../../../../../information-entropy.md) of the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md):

$$
\boxed{S(\rho_Q)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_Q\log_2\rho_Q)=-\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}\lambda_i\log_2\lambda_i.}
$$

Throughout, logarithms have base two, so the [Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md) and [Shannon entropy](../../../../../../information-entropy.md) are measured in bits. The continuous convention $0\log_2 0=0$ includes zero [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
