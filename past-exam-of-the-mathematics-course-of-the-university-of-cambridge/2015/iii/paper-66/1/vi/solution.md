<h1 id="1/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Take a [purification of a density operator](../../../../../../purification-of-a-density-operator.md) $|\Psi\rangle_{ABCD}$ of $\rho_{ABC}$. Apply [Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md) to the reduced state on $ACD$, with $A$ the conditioning system:

$$
S(AC)+S(AD)\geq S(A)+S(ACD).
$$

Complementary subsystems of a [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md) have the same [Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md), by the [Schmidt decomposition](../../../../../../schmidt-decomposition.md). Therefore $S(AD)=S(BC)$ and $S(ACD)=S(B)$. Substitution gives

$$
\boxed{S(A)+S(B)\leq S(AC)+S(BC).}
$$

This is [weak monotonicity of quantum entropy](../../../../../../weak-monotonicity-of-quantum-entropy.md). The [purification of a density operator](../../../../../../purification-of-a-density-operator.md) is only a proof device; no purity assumption is imposed on $\rho_{ABC}$.
