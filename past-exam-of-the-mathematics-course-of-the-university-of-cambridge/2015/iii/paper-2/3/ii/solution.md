<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $N=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and $\mathfrak g=\mathbb C(I+N)\subseteq\mathfrak{gl}_2(\mathbb C)$. This one-dimensional [abelian Lie algebra](../../../../../../abelian-lie-algebra.md) is not a [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md). For $X=I+N$, its [Additive Jordan decomposition](../../../../../../jordan-chevalley-decomposition.md) is $X_s=I$ and $X_n=N$. Neither belongs to $\mathfrak g$: comparing diagonal entries and then the upper-right entry rules out both scalar multiples. Therefore **nonsemisimple matrix [Lie subalgebras](../../../../../../lie-subalgebra.md) need not be closed under [Additive Jordan decomposition](../../../../../../jordan-chevalley-decomposition.md)**.
