<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the [Lie subalgebra](../../../../../../lie-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak b\subseteq\mathfrak{gl}_2(\mathbb C)$ of all upper-triangular matrices. It is a [solvable Lie algebra](../../../../../../solvable-lie-algebra.md) with a nonzero abelian [ideal of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md), hence is not a [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md). For every $X\in\mathfrak b$, the polynomial description $X_s=p(X)$ in the [Additive Jordan decomposition](../../../../../../jordan-chevalley-decomposition.md) keeps $X_s$ upper triangular; then $X_n=X-X_s$ is upper triangular too. Thus **the upper-triangular matrix algebra is closed under [Additive Jordan decomposition](../../../../../../jordan-chevalley-decomposition.md)** despite being nonsemisimple. A diagonal matrix algebra gives an even simpler [abelian Lie algebra](../../../../../../abelian-lie-algebra.md) example.
