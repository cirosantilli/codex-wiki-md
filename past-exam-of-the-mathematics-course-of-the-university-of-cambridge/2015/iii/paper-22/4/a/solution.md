<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [general adjoint functor theorem](../../../../../../freyd-general-adjoint-functor-theorem.md) has the following limit form. Let $U:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ be a [functor](../../../../../../functor.md), where $\mathcal C$ is a [complete category](../../../../../../complete-category.md) and both [categories](../../../../../../category-split.md) are [locally small](../../../../../../locally-small-category.md). Then **$U$ has a left adjoint exactly when it preserves small limits and satisfies the solution-set condition**.

The [solution-set condition](../../../../../../solution-set-condition.md) requires that, for each $D\in\mathcal D$, there be a [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) of pairs $(C_i,d_i:D\to UC_i)$ such that every $d:D\to UC$ factors as

$$
\boxed{d=U(h)d_i\quad\text{for some }i\text{ and }h:C_i\to C.}
$$

Equivalently, each [comma category](../../../../../../comma-category.md) $(D\downarrow U)$ has a [weakly initial set](../../../../../../weakly-initial-set.md). All completeness and preservation requirements here concern small [categorical limits](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md). Dually, a small-colimit-preserving [functor](../../../../../../functor.md) from a [cocomplete category](../../../../../../cocomplete-category.md) has a [right adjoint](../../../../../../adjoint-functors.md) precisely when each $(U\downarrow D)$ has a [weakly terminal set](../../../../../../weakly-terminal-set.md).
