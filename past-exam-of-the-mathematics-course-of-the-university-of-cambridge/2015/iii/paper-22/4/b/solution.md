<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Special adjoint functor theorem](../../../../../../special-adjoint-functor-theorem.md) says: if $\mathcal C$ is a [locally small category](../../../../../../locally-small-category.md), a [complete category](../../../../../../complete-category.md), a [well-powered category](../../../../../../well-powered-category.md), and has a [small cogenerating family](../../../../../../cogenerating-set.md), then a [functor](../../../../../../functor.md) $U:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ into any [locally small category](../../../../../../locally-small-category.md) has a [left adjoint](../../../../../../adjoint-functors.md) if and only if it preserves small [categorical limits](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md).

A [small cogenerating family](../../../../../../cogenerating-set.md) $(Q_i)_{i\in I}$, with $I$ a [set](../../../../../../set-split.md), distinguishes unequal parallel [morphisms](../../../../../../morphism.md) by postcomposition: for $f\ne g:A\to B$, some $q:B\to Q_i$ has $qf\ne qg$. Being [well-powered](../../../../../../well-powered-category.md) means that the [subobjects](../../../../../../subobject.md) of each object form a [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) up to the usual equivalence of [monomorphisms](../../../../../../monomorphism.md). **These hypotheses make the solution-set condition automatic.**

The dual formulation uses a [cocomplete category](../../../../../../cocomplete-category.md), being a [well-copowered category](../../../../../../well-copowered-category.md) and having a [small generating family](../../../../../../generating-set-in-a-category.md), and concludes that every small-colimit-preserving [functor](../../../../../../functor.md) has a [right adjoint](../../../../../../adjoint-functors.md).
