<h1 id="6/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If $G^{\mathbb T}$ is [fully faithful](../../../../../../../full-and-faithful-functor.md), the criterion proved for a [right adjoint](../../../../../../../adjoint-functors.md) says that the [adjunction counit](../../../../../../../counit-of-an-adjunction.md) of $F^{\mathbb T}\dashv G^{\mathbb T}$ is a [natural isomorphism](../../../../../../../natural-isomorphism.md). Its component at $(A,a)$ is precisely the algebra action $a$. In particular, its component at the free [algebra for a monad](../../../../../../../algebra-for-a-monad.md) $(TX,\mu_X)$ has underlying [morphism](../../../../../../../morphism.md) $\mu_X:T^2X\to TX$. Thus every $\mu_X$ is an [isomorphism](../../../../../../../isomorphism.md), and the [natural transformation](../../../../../../../natural-transformation.md) $\mu$ is invertible. **This closes the cycle and proves all four conditions equivalent**:

$$
\boxed{\mu\text{ invertible}\iff T\eta=\eta T
\iff\text{every algebra action is invertible}
\iff G^{\mathbb T}\text{ is fully faithful}.}
$$
