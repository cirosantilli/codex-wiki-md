<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume $T\eta=\eta T$, and let $(A,a)$ be an [algebra for a monad](../../../../../../../algebra-for-a-monad.md). Its unit identity gives $a\eta_A=1_A$. [Naturality](../../../../../../../naturality.md) of $\eta$ at $a:TA\to A$, followed by the assumed equality, gives

$$
\eta_Aa=Ta\,\eta_{TA}=Ta\,T\eta_A=T(a\eta_A)=1_{TA}.
$$

Thus **every algebra action is invertible**, with its inverse prescribed by the unit:

$$
\boxed{a^{-1}=\eta_A.}
$$
