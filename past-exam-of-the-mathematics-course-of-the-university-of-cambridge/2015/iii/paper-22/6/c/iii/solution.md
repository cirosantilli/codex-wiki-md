<h1 id="6/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose every algebra action is an [isomorphism](../../../../../../../isomorphism.md). Its inverse is $\eta_A$, by the [unit law for a monad algebra](../../../../../../../unit-law-for-a-monad-algebra.md). For any underlying [morphism](../../../../../../../morphism.md) $f:A\to B$ between two [algebras for a monad](../../../../../../../algebra-for-a-monad.md), [naturality](../../../../../../../naturality.md) of $\eta$ gives

$$
\eta_B f=Tf\,\eta_A\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
fa=b\,Tf.
$$

Therefore every underlying [morphism](../../../../../../../morphism.md) is automatically a [monad algebra morphism](../../../../../../../morphism-of-algebras-for-a-monad.md). The [forgetful functor](../../../../../../../forgetful-functor.md) is always faithful, because a [monad algebra morphism](../../../../../../../morphism-of-algebras-for-a-monad.md) has no data beyond its underlying [morphism](../../../../../../../morphism.md), and it is now full as well. Hence **invertible algebra actions imply**

$$
\boxed{G^{\mathbb T}:\mathcal X^{\mathbb T}\to\mathcal X\text{ is fully faithful}.}
$$
