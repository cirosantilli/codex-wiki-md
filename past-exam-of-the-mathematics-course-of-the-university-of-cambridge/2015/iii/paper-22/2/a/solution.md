<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fix the chosen [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) object $LA$ and its [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) $p_{A,j}:LA\to(DA)_j$. For $a:A\to A'$, the family

$$
LA\xrightarrow{p_{A,j}}(DA)_j\xrightarrow{(Da)_j}(DA')_j
$$

is a [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md), by [naturality](../../../../../../naturality.md) of $Da$. The [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md) of $LA'$ supplies a unique [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md)

$$
\boxed{La:LA\to LA',\qquad p_{A',j}La=(Da)_jp_{A,j}\text{ for every }j.}
$$

The [identity morphism](../../../../../../identity-morphism.md) of $LA$ satisfies the equations for $a=1_A$, so $L1_A=1_{LA}$. For composable $a,a'$, the equations for $La'\,La$ agree with those for $L(a'a)$; uniqueness gives $L(a'a)=La'\,La$. Hence **the chosen limits define a functor** $L:\mathcal A\to\mathcal C$. This argument also handles an empty indexing [category](../../../../../../category-split.md), when each chosen [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) is a [terminal object](../../../../../../terminal-object.md).

Equivalently, $LA$ represents the [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) [functor](../../../../../../functor.md) $C\mapsto\operatorname{Nat}(\Delta_JC,DA)$, and [functoriality of chosen representations](../../../../../../functoriality-of-chosen-representations.md) supplies the same $L$.
