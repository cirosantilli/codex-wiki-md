<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The representing object in the [functor category](../../../../../../functor-category.md) $[J,\mathcal C]$ is the constant [diagram in a category](../../../../../../diagram-category-theory.md) $\Delta_JC$. If $p_j:\lim_jE_j\to E_j$ is the chosen [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) cone, the [natural bijection](../../../../../../natural-bijection.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal C(C,\lim_jE_j)\cong[J,\mathcal C](\Delta_JC,E),
\qquad f\longmapsto(p_jf)_j.}
$$

A [natural transformation](../../../../../../natural-transformation.md) from the constant [diagram in a category](../../../../../../diagram-category-theory.md) is precisely a [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) with vertex $C$, and its inverse is the unique mediating [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md) from the [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md) of the [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md). For $\alpha:E\Rightarrow E'$, the defining equations for $\lim\alpha$ show that postcomposition by $\lim\alpha$ corresponds to postcomposition by $\alpha$ on the right. This establishes [naturality](../../../../../../naturality.md) in $E$ and proves **representability by the constant diagram**.
