↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For every $C$, the preceding part identifies the composite $\mathcal C(C,\lim_J-)$ with the covariant [representable functor](../../../../../../representable-functor.md) $[J,\mathcal C](\Delta_JC,-)$. A covariant [representable functor](../../../../../../representable-functor.md) preserves every existing [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md), since a [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md) into a [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) is the same as a compatible family of [morphisms](../../../../../../morphism.md) into the diagram objects. **The limit functor preserves limits.** The [hom-set detection of categorical limits](../../../../../../hom-set-detection-of-categorical-limits.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_J:[J,\mathcal C]\to\mathcal C\text{ preserves categorical limits}.}
$$

In particular, it preserves all small [categorical limits](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md). They exist in $[J,\mathcal C]$: compute each one at every $j$ using completeness of $\mathcal C$, and use the uniqueness of the pointwise mediating [morphisms](../../../../../../morphism.md) to obtain its [functor](../../../../../../functor.md) structure and universal [natural transformations](../../../../../../natural-transformation.md). Another expression of the same result is the [adjunction](../../../../../../adjoint-functors.md) $\Delta_J\dashv\lim_J$.
