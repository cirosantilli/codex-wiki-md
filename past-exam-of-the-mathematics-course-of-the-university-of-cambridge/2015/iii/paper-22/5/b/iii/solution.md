<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $\rho_B:B\to KB$ is an [isomorphism](../../../../../../../isomorphism.md), then $B$ is isomorphic in $\mathcal B$ to an object of $\mathcal C$. Since $\mathcal C$ is a [replete subcategory](../../../../../../../replete-subcategory.md), it contains $B$. Thus **invertibility of the unit implies membership**, completing the cycle of implications:

$$
\boxed{B\in\mathcal C\iff
\bigl[h\mapsto h\rho_{B'}\text{ is bijective for every }B'\bigr]
\iff\rho_B\text{ is invertible}.}
$$
