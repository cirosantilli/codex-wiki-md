<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $B$ belongs to the [full subcategory](../../../../../../../full-subcategory.md) $\mathcal C$, the [adjunction](../../../../../../../adjoint-functors.md) and fullness of its inclusion give, for every $B'\in\mathcal B$,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal B(KB',B)=\mathcal C(KB',B)\cong\mathcal B(B',B),
\qquad h\longmapsto h\rho_{B'}.}
$$

Therefore **membership implies the hom-set condition**. In addition, $J$ is [fully faithful](../../../../../../../full-and-faithful-functor.md), so the [adjunction counit](../../../../../../../counit-of-an-adjunction.md) is invertible by the preceding criterion. The triangular equation $J\varepsilon_B\,\rho_B=1_B$ then shows that $\rho_B$ is an [isomorphism](../../../../../../../isomorphism.md). As in the printed inclusion notation, $J$ is suppressed when writing $KB$ as an object of $\mathcal B$.
