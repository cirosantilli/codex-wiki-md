<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In SI units, [magnetostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../magnetostatic-equilibrium.md) without gravity is $\nabla p=\mu_0^{-1}(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\times\mathbf B$. For a purely axial [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md), the [magnetic tension](../../../../../../magnetic-tension.md) term $(\mathbf B\cdot\nabla)\mathbf B$ vanishes. Thus the radial balance is

$$
\frac{d}{ds}\left(p+\frac{B_z^2}{2\mu_0}\right)=0.
$$

Matching the [magnetohydrodynamic total pressure](../../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-total-pressure.md) to the field-free exterior gives **the gas pressure and axial field bound**:

$$
\boxed{p(s)=p_e-\frac{B_z(s)^2}{2\mu_0},\qquad |B_z(s)|\leq\sqrt{2\mu_0p_e}=B_p.}
$$

The inequality follows directly from nonnegative gas [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md). It is the [magnetic pressure](../../../../../../magnetic-pressure.md) limit for an untwisted [flux tube](../../../../../../flux-tube.md), without inward hoop tension.
