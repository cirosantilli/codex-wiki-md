<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the central-field identity and substitute the prescribed relation between the [toroidal magnetic field](../../../../../../toroidal-magnetic-field.md) and axial [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md):

$$
B_z(0)^2=B_p^2-2\mu_0p(0)+\frac{2\gamma^2}{s_0^2}\int_0^{s_0}sB_z(s)^2\,ds.
$$

The [flux-tube axial field virial identity](../../../../../../flux-tube-axial-field-virial-identity.md) bounds the last average by $B_p^2$, while $p(0)\geq0$. Therefore **the [uniform-twist central axial field bound](../../../../../../uniform-twist-central-axial-field-bound.md) is**

$$
\boxed{B_z(0)^2\leq(1+\gamma^2)B_p^2.}
$$

The stronger exact expression is $B_z(0)^2=(1+\gamma^2)B_p^2-2\mu_0p(0)-(4\mu_0\gamma^2/s_0^2)\int_0^{s_0}sp(s)\,ds$.
