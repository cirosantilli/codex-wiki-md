<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the radial velocity as $u(r)$, allowing $u<0$ for [accretion flow](../../../../../../accretion-flow.md), and let $c^2=\gamma p/\rho$ be the squared [adiabatic sound speed](../../../../../../adiabatic-sound-speed.md). The steady [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md), radial [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) and [entropy advection equation](../../../../../../entropy-advection-equation.md) give

$$
\frac{d}{dr}(r^2\rho u)=0,\qquad
u\frac{du}{dr}=-\frac1\rho\frac{dp}{dr}-\frac{d\Phi}{dr},\qquad
u\frac{d}{dr}\left(\frac{p}{\rho^\gamma}\right)=0.
$$

On a nonzero smooth flow branch, the [polytropic equation of state](../../../../../../polytropic-equation-of-state.md) is $p=K\rho^\gamma$ with constant $K$. Thus the two useful first integrals for [spherically symmetric adiabatic flow](../../../../../../spherically-symmetric-adiabatic-flow.md) are

$$
\boxed{4\pi r^2\rho u=\mathcal J,\qquad
\frac{u^2}{2}+\frac{c^2}{\gamma-1}+\Phi=\mathcal B.}
$$

Here $\mathcal J$ is the signed mass flux and $\mathcal B$ is the [Bernoulli function](../../../../../../bernoulli-function.md). The [specific enthalpy](../../../../../../specific-enthalpy.md) is $c^2/(\gamma-1)$; the prescribed [Newtonian gravitational potential](../../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) has no contribution from the gas's own gravity. Eliminating the density derivative using [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives the differential form

$$
\boxed{\left(u-\frac{c^2}{u}\right)u'
=\frac{2c^2}{r}-\Phi'.}
$$

This [sonic point](../../../../../../sonic-point.md) equation displays both the singular coefficient and the numerator that must vanish for a smooth [transonic branch](../../../../../../transonic-branch.md).
