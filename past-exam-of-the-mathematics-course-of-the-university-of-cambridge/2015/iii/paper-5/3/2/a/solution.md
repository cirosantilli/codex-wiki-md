<h1 id="3/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $R=\Lambda/\lambda$. The coefficient is measurable, so interpret the [divergence-form elliptic operator](../../../../../../../divergence-form-elliptic-operator.md) weakly: $\int A u'\phi'=0$ for compactly supported $H^1$ tests. Testing with $\phi=\zeta^2u$ gives

$$
\lambda\int\zeta^2|u'|^2\le2\Lambda\int\zeta|u'||u||\zeta'|\le2\Lambda\left(\int\zeta^2|u'|^2\right)^{1/2}\left(\int u^2|\zeta'|^2\right)^{1/2}.
$$

Divide if the first integral is positive; the zero case is immediate. Since $\zeta=1$ on the inner interval,

$$
\boxed{\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}|u'|^2\le4R^2\int_{-1}^1u^2|\zeta'|^2.}
$$

This [Caccioppoli inequality](../../../../../../../caccioppoli-inequality.md) applies in particular to the smooth solutions in the question. No derivative of $A$ is taken.
