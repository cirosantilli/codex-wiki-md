<h1 id="1/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [characteristic curves](../../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) solve $\dot X=X$, hence $X(t,\xi)=e^t\xi$. By the [chain rule](../../../../../../../chain-rule.md), $d[u(t,X(t,\xi))]/dt=0$. Pulling back along the [characteristic flow map](../../../../../../../characteristic-flow-map.md) gives

$$
\boxed{u(t,x)=u_0(e^{-t}x).}
$$

For $u_0\in C^1$, this is a [classical solution](../../../../../../../classical-solution.md): $u_t=-e^{-t}x u_0'(e^{-t}x)$ and $u_x=e^{-t}u_0'(e^{-t}x)$ cancel in the [transport equation](../../../../../../../transport-equation.md), and at $t=0$ the [initial condition](../../../../../../../initial-condition.md) holds. Conversely, constancy along every [characteristic curve](../../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) forces this formula.
