<h1 id="1/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [weak solution](../../../../../../../weak-solution.md) is an [equivalence class](../../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) $u\in L^\infty((0,\infty)\times\mathbb R)$ satisfying, for every [test function](../../../../../../../test-function.md) $\varphi\in C_c^\infty([0,\infty)\times\mathbb R)$,

$$
\boxed{\int_0^\infty\!\int_{\mathbb R}u\bigl(\varphi_t+\partial_x(c\varphi)\bigr)\,dx\,dt+\int_{\mathbb R}u_0(x)\varphi(0,x)\,dx=0.}
$$

This is [integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) for the nonconservative [transport equation](../../../../../../../transport-equation.md). In particular, the [weak formulation](../../../../../../../weak-formulation.md) contains $c_x\varphi$ as well as $c\varphi_x$. The last integral encodes the [initial condition](../../../../../../../initial-condition.md); a pointwise boundary value of an arbitrary [Lebesgue space](../../../../../../../lp-space.md) representative is not the definition. Compact support of the [test function](../../../../../../../test-function.md) makes the identity meaningful even if $c$ is unbounded.
