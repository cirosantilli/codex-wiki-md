<h1 id="1/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The compact support just established and $f(0)=0$ permit differentiation of the [antiderivative](../../../../../../../antiderivative.md) and integration of the [scalar conservation law](../../../../../../../scalar-conservation-law.md) from $-\infty$:

$$
U_x=u,\qquad U_t=-f(u),\qquad \boxed{U_t+f(U_x)=0,\quad U(0,x)=\int_{-\infty}^x u_0(y)\,dy.}
$$

This is a [Hamilton-Jacobi equation](../../../../../../../hamilton-jacobi-equation.md). There is no arbitrary function of time: the normalization at $-\infty$ and the zero exterior flux determine it.
