<h1 id="1/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) applied to $X_t$ makes $u_0\circ X_t^{-1}$ smooth. Since $u_0(\pm A)=0$, $X_t(\pm A)=\pm A+ct$. Monotonicity of the [characteristic flow map](../../../../../../../characteristic-flow-map.md) and the vanishing of $u_0$ outside $[-A,A]$ imply

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{supp}u(t,\cdot)\subset[-A+ct,A+ct]\quad(0<t<t^*).}
$$

Thus the solution is a [smooth function](../../../../../../../smooth-function.md) with compact support at each such time. The fact that some interior [characteristic speeds](../../../../../../../characteristic-speed.md) differ from $c$ causes no difficulty: they cannot cross the two exterior [characteristic curves](../../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) before $t^*$.
