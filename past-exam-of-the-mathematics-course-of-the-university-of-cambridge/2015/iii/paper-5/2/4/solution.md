**No.** On the complex [l2 sequence space](../../../../../../l2-sequence-space.md), let $L(x_n)=(x_n/n)$. This is a bounded [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md). At $\lambda=0$, $\ker L=\{0\}$, so zero is not an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) (an eigenvector must be nonzero). But $y=(1/n)\in\ell^2$ has no preimage in $\ell^2$: its only formal preimage is the constant-one sequence. Thus $L$ is not surjective and

$$
\boxed{0\in\Sigma(L)\quad\hbox{but}\quad0\notin\sigma_{\mathrm p}(L).}
$$

Alternatively $\|Le_n\|=1/n\to0$ excludes a bounded inverse. This example separates the [spectrum of a bounded operator](../../../../../../spectrum-of-a-bounded-operator.md) from its [point spectrum](../../../../../../point-spectrum.md).
