<h1 id="2/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

For a bounded [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md), $g$ is orthogonal to its range exactly when $\langle Lf,g\rangle=\langle f,Lg\rangle=0$ for every $f$, or equivalently $Lg=0$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\overline{\operatorname{ran}L}=(\ker L)^{\perp}=H\quad\hbox{if }\ker L=\{0\}.}
$$

This is [image-kernel orthogonality for an adjoint](../../../../../../image-kernel-orthogonality-for-an-adjoint.md). Taking the double [orthogonal complement](../../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) gives the closure; it does not assert that the range itself is closed.
