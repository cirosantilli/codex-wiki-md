<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With only $u\in L^2(\Omega)$ available, use a [distributional weak solution](../../../../../../weak-solution.md): for every [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) $\eta\in C_c^\infty(\Omega)$ require

$$
\boxed{\int_\Omega u\,\Delta\eta=-\sum_{i=1}^n\int_\Omega b^iu\,D_i\eta+\int_\Omega(cu+f)\eta.}
$$

All terms are meaningful because the smooth coefficients are bounded on the compact support of the [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) and $u$ is locally integrable. This is exactly $\Delta u=\sum_iD_i(b^iu)+cu+f$ as an identity of [distributions](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). No first [weak derivative](../../../../../../weak-derivative.md) of $u$ is assumed in this definition, and no boundary condition is being imposed.
