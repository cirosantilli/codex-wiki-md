↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Two useful features of [autoregressive conditional heteroscedasticity](../../../../../../autoregressive-conditional-heteroscedasticity.md) are **persistent changes in conditional scale** and **excess unconditional [kurtosis](../../../../../../kurtosis.md)**. Its time-varying [conditional variance](../../../../../../conditional-variance.md) can explain [volatility clustering](../../../../../../volatility-clustering.md), where large absolute returns occur in groups even when signed returns have little [autocorrelation](../../../../../../autocorrelation.md). A homoscedastic [autoregressive moving-average model](../../../../../../autoregressive-moving-average-model.md) has a fixed innovation [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md).

Also, a conditional [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) with a random scale is a [Gaussian scale mixture](../../../../../../gaussian-scale-mixture.md). Its unconditional [kurtosis](../../../../../../kurtosis.md) can exceed $3$, or its [fourth moment](../../../../../../fourth-moment.md) can be infinite. A Gaussian [autoregressive moving-average model](../../../../../../autoregressive-moving-average-model.md) remains jointly Gaussian and cannot reproduce this effect. In the particular [lag-two ARCH process](../../../../../../lag-two-arch-process.md), persistence of the squared scale occurs within each parity subsequence; the two parity subsequences are independent.
