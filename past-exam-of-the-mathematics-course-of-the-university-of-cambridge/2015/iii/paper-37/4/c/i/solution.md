<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Integrating the [Weibull distribution](../../../../../../../weibull-distribution.md) density gives $F(x)=1-\exp(-x^\alpha/\beta)$ for $x>0$. [Inverse transform sampling](../../../../../../../inverse-transform-sampling.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{X=[-\beta\log(1-U_1)]^{1/\alpha}.}
$$

Replacing $1-U_1$ by $U_1$ produces the same [probability distribution](../../../../../../../probability-distribution.md). Both uniforms belong to $(0,1)$ with probability one. Here the conventional Weibull scale is $\beta^{1/\alpha}$, rather than $\beta$.
