↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The algorithm is a [Random-scan Gibbs sampler](../../../../../../../random-scan-gibbs-sampler.md). Start at a point where the target is positive, with a proper normalizing integral and well-defined [full conditional distributions](../../../../../../../full-conditional-distribution.md). Its state is the entire coordinate vector. Choosing the initial point fixes the initial law; it need not already be the invariant law. The transition rule below preserves the target irrespective of this choice.
