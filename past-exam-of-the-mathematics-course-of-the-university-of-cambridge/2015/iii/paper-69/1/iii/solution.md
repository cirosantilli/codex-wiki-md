<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply the periodic [Korovkin theorem](../../../../../../korovkin-theorem.md) to the constant sequence $U_n=U$. Since it fixes the three test functions, the theorem gives $Uf=f$ for every $f$.

One can also see the rigidity directly. For fixed $x$, the [positive linear functional](../../../../../../positive-linear-functional.md) $L_x(f)=(Uf)(x)$ has $L_x(1)=1$ and is represented by a [probability measure](../../../../../../probability-measure.md). The fixed sine and cosine imply $L_x(1-\cos(t-x))=0$. The integrand is nonnegative with unique zero $x$ on the circle, so the measure is $\delta_x$. Thus $(Uf)(x)=f(x)$ at every point. **The operator is uniquely the identity**: $\boxed{U=I}$.
