<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [adiabatic mode](../../../../../../adiabatic-mode.md) corresponds locally to a common displacement along the background history. Its density changes obey $\delta\rho_i/\dot{\bar\rho}_i=\delta t$ for every component. The [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) then implies that $\Delta_i/(1+w_i)$ is common to all fluids. Thus, in the common [comoving gauge in cosmology](../../../../../../comoving-gauge-in-cosmology.md) relevant to the growing superhorizon mode,

$$
\boxed{\Delta_r=\frac43\Delta_c.}
$$

Equivalently, the relative entropy perturbation $\Delta_c-(3/4)\Delta_r$ vanishes. A common time-slicing shift preserves this relation because it changes both normalized density perturbations by the same amount.

The flat background [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $4\pi Ga^2\bar\rho=(3/2)\mathcal H^2$. During [radiation domination](../../../../../../radiation-domination.md), $\mathcal H=1/\tau$ and the matter background is negligible. Substituting the adiabatic relation into the density equation therefore gives

$$
\Delta_c''+\frac1\tau\Delta_c'=\frac4{\tau^2}\Delta_c.
$$

With $\Delta_c\propto\tau^p$, the indicial equation is $p^2=4$, so the modes are $\tau^2$ and $\tau^{-2}$.

During [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md), $\mathcal H=2/\tau$ and the radiation background is negligible. The same calculation gives

$$
\Delta_c''+\frac2\tau\Delta_c'=\frac6{\tau^2}\Delta_c,
$$

whose indicial equation is $p^2+p-6=(p-2)(p+3)=0$. Its modes are $\tau^2$ and $\tau^{-3}$. Thus the [superhorizon growing density mode of adiabatic matter and radiation](../../../../../../superhorizon-growing-density-mode-of-adiabatic-matter-and-radiation.md) satisfies **$\Delta_c\propto\tau^2$ in both eras**. The common conformal-time exponent does not imply the same scale-factor dependence: it is $\Delta_c\propto a^2$ in the radiation era and $\Delta_c\propto a$ in the matter era.
