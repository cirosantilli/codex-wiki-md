<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md), the radiation source is negligible and cold matter has negligible pressure. The comoving density equation consequently contains no Jeans-pressure term proportional to $k^2\Delta_c$. On every linear scale, including superhorizon and subhorizon modes, it reduces to

$$
\Delta_c''+\frac2\tau\Delta_c'-\frac6{\tau^2}\Delta_c=0.
$$

The [matter-era growing and decaying density modes](../../../../../../matter-era-growing-and-decaying-density-modes.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta_c=C_g\tau^2+C_d\tau^{-3},\qquad\Delta_{c,\rm grow}\propto\tau^2\propto a.}
$$

The absence of a $k$-dependent pressure term makes the time dependence scale-independent; the coefficients still depend on wavenumber and on the earlier radiation-era evolution. This is linear growth in the stated cold-matter model, before nonlinear collapse or any additional late-time component changes the evolution.
