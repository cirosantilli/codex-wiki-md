<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $\Omega=\Omega_{m,0}$ and $b=1-\Omega$. Integrating the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) for the [separately conserved cosmological fluids](../../../../../../separately-conserved-cosmological-fluids.md) gives

$$
\rho_m=\rho_{m,0}a^{-3},\qquad \rho_X=\rho_{X,0}a^{-2}.
$$

In particular, **$\rho_Xa^2$ is constant**. At the present epoch the flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) implies $\Omega_{X,0}=b$. Nonnegative fluid densities therefore require $0\leq\Omega\leq1$.

The [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md) relation $dt=a\,d\tau$ gives the [conformal Hubble parameter](../../../../../../conformal-hubble-parameter.md) $\mathcal H=aH$. Consequently

$$
\mathcal H^2=H_0^2\left(\frac{\Omega}{a}+b\right),\qquad 2\mathcal H\mathcal H'=-H_0^2\frac{\Omega a'}{a^2}.
$$

On the expanding branch, divide by $\mathcal H$ and use $a'=a\mathcal H$:

$$
2\mathcal H'=-\frac{H_0^2\Omega}{a}.
$$

Eliminating the matter term yields the [conformal Riccati equation for matter and a coasting fluid](../../../../../../conformal-riccati-equation-for-matter-and-a-coasting-fluid.md),

$$
\boxed{2\mathcal H'+\mathcal H^2-\alpha^2=0,\qquad \alpha=H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega_{m,0}}.}
$$

The nonnegative square root fixes the convenient parameter convention; only $\alpha^2$ enters the differential equation.
