<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A light [inflaton](../../../../../../inflaton.md) behaves approximately as a scalar field in de Sitter space while a wavelength is well inside the [Hubble radius](../../../../../../hubble-radius.md). Its [Bunch-Davies vacuum](../../../../../../bunch-davies-vacuum.md) has [quantum fluctuations](../../../../../../quantum-fluctuation.md). [Cosmic inflation](../../../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) stretches each [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) until $k=aH$, after which its physical wavelength exceeds the [Hubble radius](../../../../../../hubble-radius.md). The nearly constant growing field mode has a typical fluctuation per logarithmic wavenumber interval $\delta\phi\sim H/(2\pi)$.

A field fluctuation changes the local position on the rolling background trajectory. Neighboring regions therefore reach the same field value, and the end of inflation, at slightly different times. A clock displacement of magnitude $|\delta\phi/\dot\phi|$ becomes a difference in local expansion of order $H|\delta\phi/\dot\phi|$. Equivalently, in a conventional sign choice the [comoving curvature perturbation](../../../../../../comoving-curvature-perturbation.md) is related to the field fluctuation on spatially flat slices by

$$
\boxed{\mathcal R=\frac{H}{\dot\phi}\,\delta\phi_{\rm flat},\qquad |\mathcal R|\sim\frac{H}{|\dot\phi|}\frac{H}{2\pi}.}
$$

Changing the sign convention for spatial curvature changes the sign of $\mathcal R$, but not its spectrum. This is the [inflaton clock-shift origin of curvature perturbations](../../../../../../inflaton-clock-shift-origin-of-curvature-perturbations.md). For a single-field slow-roll attractor there is no independent entropy mode. On a [super-Hubble scale](../../../../../../superhorizon-scale.md), gradient terms are negligible and the [superhorizon conservation of single-field comoving curvature](../../../../../../superhorizon-conservation-of-single-field-comoving-curvature.md) preserves the growing [adiabatic mode](../../../../../../adiabatic-mode.md), so fluctuations generated near exit persist as primordial curvature perturbations. This conservation requires the attractor and adiabatic assumptions; a freely chosen non-attractor background would not have the same conclusion.
