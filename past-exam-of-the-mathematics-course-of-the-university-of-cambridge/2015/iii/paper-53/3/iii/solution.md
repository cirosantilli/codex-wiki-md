<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the [quadratic-potential slow-roll solution](../../../../../../quadratic-potential-slow-roll-solution.md), $V'/V=2/\phi$, so $\epsilon=\eta=2M_{\rm pl}^2/\phi^2$. Inflation ends when $\epsilon\simeq1$, giving $\phi_{\rm end}^2=2M_{\rm pl}^2$. The [slow-roll e-fold count](../../../../../../slow-roll-e-fold-count.md) remaining before that endpoint is

$$
N_*\simeq\frac1{M_{\rm pl}^2}\int_{\phi_{\rm end}}^{\phi_*}\frac{V}{V'}\,d\phi=\frac{\phi_*^2-\phi_{\rm end}^2}{4M_{\rm pl}^2}.
$$

With $N_*=50$, this gives $\phi_*^2=202M_{\rm pl}^2$ and $\epsilon_*=\eta_*=1/101$. The [quadratic-inflation amplitude calibration](../../../../../../quadratic-inflation-amplitude-calibration.md) is

$$
\Delta_{\mathcal R}^2(k_*)=\frac{m^2\phi_*^4}{96\pi^2M_{\rm pl}^6}=\frac{m^2}{M_{\rm pl}^2}\frac{(2N_*+1)^2}{24\pi^2}.
$$

Matching the supplied amplitude therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{m}{M_{\rm pl}}=\frac{\sqrt{24\pi^2(2.2\times10^{-9})}}{101}\simeq7.15\times10^{-6}.}
$$

Taking $M_{\rm pl}\simeq2.4\times10^{18}\,\mathrm{GeV}$, this is **$m\simeq1.7\times10^{13}\,\mathrm{GeV}$**. Retaining $\phi_{\rm end}$ produces the small $+1$ correction; dropping it gives almost the same leading slow-roll estimate.

The [scalar spectral index](../../../../../../scalar-spectral-index.md) is

$$
\boxed{n_s(k_*)\simeq1-\frac4{101}\simeq0.9604.}
$$

It agrees with the supplied central value and lies well inside the stated uncertainty interval. This conclusion concerns the scalar tilt; the tensor prediction is a separate test of the same model.
