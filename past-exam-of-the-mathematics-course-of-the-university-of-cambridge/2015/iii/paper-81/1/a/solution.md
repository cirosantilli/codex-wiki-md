<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Each bond of the classical [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](../../../../../../heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md) is minimized when its two [spins](../../../../../../spin.md) are antiparallel. Since the periodic chain has an [even](../../../../../../even-function.md) number of sites, all bonds can be minimized simultaneously:

$$
\mathbf S_n=(-1)^nS\widehat{\mathbf n},\qquad |\widehat{\mathbf n}|=1,\qquad \boxed{E_{\mathrm{cl}}=-NJS^2}.
$$

The [ground-state degeneracy](../../../../../../ground-state-degeneracy.md) is a continuous sphere of orientations $\widehat{\mathbf n}\in S^2$. Choose the [Néel state](../../../../../../neel-state.md) with even sites in $|S,S\rangle$ and odd sites in $|S,-S\rangle$.

This product state is not an [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md). The [spin ladder operators](../../../../../../spin-ladder-operator.md) in

$$
\mathbf S_n\cdot\mathbf S_{n+1}=S_n^zS_{n+1}^z+\frac12(S_n^+S_{n+1}^-+S_n^-S_{n+1}^+)
$$

produce states with both neighboring magnetic quantum numbers changed. On an up-down bond, $S_n^-S_{n+1}^+$ has a nonzero matrix element $2S$, hence coefficient $JS$ in the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md). These orthogonal spin configurations show explicitly why the classical minimum is not an exact quantum eigenstate for $S>0$.
