<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Under the [bipartite spin rotation](../../../../../../bipartite-spin-rotation.md), the [staggered magnetization](../../../../../../staggered-magnetization.md) becomes the uniform transformed $S^z$. In the [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md) vacuum $\alpha_k|0_\alpha\rangle=0$,

$$
\langle a_k^\dagger a_k\rangle=\sinh^2\theta_k=\frac12\left(\frac A{\omega_k}-1\right)=\frac12\left(\frac1{|\sin k|}-1\right).
$$

The [quantum depletion of Néel order](../../../../../../quantum-depletion-of-neel-order.md) therefore gives, with the zero modes regulated,

$$
\boxed{M_s=S-\frac1N\sum_k\sinh^2\theta_k\ \longrightarrow\ S-\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\frac{dk}{2\pi}\frac12\left(\frac1{|\sin k|}-1\right)}.
$$

Near each zero of $\sin k$, the integrand behaves as $1/(2|k-k_0|)$, producing a [logarithmic divergence](../../../../../../logarithmic-divergence.md). With a finite-size cutoff of order $N^{-1}$, the depletion grows as $\pi^{-1}\log N+O(1)$. Thus the large-$S$ expansion about a state with finite [Néel order](../../../../../../neel-state.md) is not self-consistent in the infinite one-dimensional chain. The divergent expression is not a negative physical magnetization; it signals breakdown of that ordered approximation. It does not determine whether the exact excitation spectrum is gapped.
