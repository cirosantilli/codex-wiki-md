<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) are preserved precisely when the real matrix acting on $(\gamma_{k\uparrow},\gamma_{-k\downarrow}^\dagger)$ is orthogonal. Directly,

$$
\{c_{k\uparrow},c_{k\uparrow}^\dagger\}=u_k^2+v_k^2,\qquad \{c_{k\uparrow},c_{-k\downarrow}\}=-u_kv_k+v_ku_k=0.
$$

The other relations are identical or vanish between disjoint pairs. Hence **$u_k^2+v_k^2=1$**, and one may write $u_k=\cos\theta_k$, $v_k=\sin\theta_k$. This fermionic [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md) uses an ordinary rotation; bosonic mixing instead preserves a difference of squared coefficients.
