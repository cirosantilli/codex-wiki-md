<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The bosonic [coherent-state resolution of identity](../../../../../../coherent-state-resolution-of-identity.md) gives the ordinary trace by insertion into a number-state basis. For fermions, the [fermionic coherent-state trace](../../../../../../fermionic-coherent-state-trace.md) requires a sign in the bra. In one mode, for an even operator $A$,

$$
\langle-\psi|A|\psi\rangle=A_{00}-\bar\psi\psi A_{11},\qquad \int d\bar\psi\,d\psi\,e^{-\bar\psi\psi}\langle-\psi|A|\psi\rangle=A_{00}+A_{11}.
$$

Without that sign the result would be the [supertrace](../../../../../../supertrace.md) $A_{00}-A_{11}$. Applying this identity mode by mode to the even operator $A=e^{-\beta(H-\mu N)}$ gives the [grand canonical partition function](../../../../../../grand-canonical-partition-function.md)

$$
\boxed{Z=\int d[\bar\psi,\psi]e^{-\sum_n\bar\psi_n\psi_n}\langle\zeta\psi|e^{-\beta(H-\mu N)}|\psi\rangle}.
$$
