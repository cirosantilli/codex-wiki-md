<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The exponential acts on the [Fock vacuum](../../../../../../fock-vacuum.md): $|\psi\rangle=e^{\zeta\sum_n\psi_na_n^\dagger}|0\rangle$. For bosons, $\psi_n$ are commuting [complex numbers](../../../../../../complex-number.md). Since $[a_n,\sum_m\psi_ma_m^\dagger]=\psi_n$, commuting $a_n$ through the exponential gives **$a_n|\psi\rangle=\psi_n|\psi\rangle$**. These are [unnormalized bosonic coherent states](../../../../../../unnormalized-bosonic-coherent-state.md).

For fermions, the labels are independent odd [Grassmann variables](../../../../../../grassmann-variable.md), which anticommute with one another and with the fermionic operators. For one mode, the [fermionic coherent state](../../../../../../fermionic-coherent-state.md) is $|\psi\rangle=(1-\psi a^\dagger)|0\rangle$. The [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) give $a(-\psi a^\dagger)|0\rangle=\psi|0\rangle=\psi|\psi\rangle$, using $\psi^2=0$. The even factors $1-\psi_na_n^\dagger$ commute between modes, so the same argument applies to every $n$. A Grassmann eigenvalue is a formal extension of the state space, not an ordinary complex eigenvalue of the nilpotent annihilation operator.

With dual state $\langle\psi|=\langle0|e^{\zeta\sum_na_n\bar\psi_n}$, both cases obey $\langle\psi|\psi'\rangle=e^{\sum_n\bar\psi_n\psi_n'}$.
