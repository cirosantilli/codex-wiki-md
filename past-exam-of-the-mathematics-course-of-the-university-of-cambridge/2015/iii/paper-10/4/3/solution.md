<h1 id="4/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**[Harmonic maps](../../../../../../harmonic-map.md) give nonconstant [stationary wave maps](../../../../../../stationary-wave-map.md).** For $n=2$, inverse [stereographic projection](../../../../../../stereographic-projection.md) gives

$$
Q(x)=\frac{(2x_1,2x_2,|x|^2-1)}{1+|x|^2}.
$$

It satisfies $|Q|=1$ and $\Delta Q=-|\nabla Q|^2Q$, so $\phi(t,x)=Q(x)$ is a smooth global [wave map](../../../../../../wave-map.md). Its [wave map energy](../../../../../../wave-map-energy.md) is $4\pi$, since $|\nabla Q|^2=8/(1+|x|^2)^2$. Translations and rescalings give further examples; these [harmonic maps](../../../../../../harmonic-map.md) approach a constant at infinity.
