**[Small data global regularity for wave maps](../../../../../../small-data-global-regularity-for-wave-maps.md) also holds in three spatial dimensions.** For localized compatible data small in high weighted [Sobolev norms](../../../../../../sobolev-norm.md), the key is the [classical null condition for wave equations](../../../../../../classical-null-condition-for-wave-equations.md). Each derivative contraction is a [null form for wave equations](../../../../../../null-form-for-wave-equations.md), vanishing for parallel null derivatives. The [vector field method for wave equations](../../../../../../vector-field-method-for-wave-equations.md) exploits derivatives tangent to the [light cone](../../../../../../light-cone.md) and weighted [energy estimates](../../../../../../energy-estimate.md) to close the [bootstrap argument](../../../../../../bootstrap-argument.md); ordinary $(1+t)^{-1}$ decay alone is insufficient.
