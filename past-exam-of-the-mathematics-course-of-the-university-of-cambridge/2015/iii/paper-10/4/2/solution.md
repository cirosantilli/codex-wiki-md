<h1 id="4/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**Smooth compatible [wave map Cauchy data](../../../../../../wave-map-cauchy-data.md) give a unique local smooth [wave map](../../../../../../wave-map.md).** Relative to a constant map, [Sobolev spaces](../../../../../../sobolev-space-split.md) $H^s\times H^{s-1}$ with $s>n/2+1$ provide a classical local theory. Iteration for the [semilinear wave equation](../../../../../../semilinear-wave-equation.md), [Sobolev algebra](../../../../../../sobolev-algebra.md) and [energy estimates](../../../../../../energy-estimate.md) give existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence. The [smooth continuation criterion for semilinear wave equations](../../../../../../smooth-continuation-criterion-for-semilinear-wave-equations.md) extends the solution while these norms stay bounded. The [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) constraint and tangency constraint remain satisfied.
