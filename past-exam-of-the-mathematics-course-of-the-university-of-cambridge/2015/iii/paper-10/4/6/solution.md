<h1 id="4/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**[Small data global regularity for wave maps](../../../../../../small-data-global-regularity-for-wave-maps.md) holds in four spatial dimensions.** Smallness is measured in sufficiently high weighted [Sobolev norms](../../../../../../sobolev-norm.md) relative to a constant map, with localized compatible [Cauchy data](../../../../../../cauchy-data.md). The [vector field method for wave equations](../../../../../../vector-field-method-for-wave-equations.md) gives derivative decay $(1+t)^{-3/2}$. This is time-integrable, so [commuted wave energy](../../../../../../commuted-wave-energy.md) estimates close a small-data [bootstrap argument](../../../../../../bootstrap-argument.md) for the derivative-quadratic [semilinear wave equation](../../../../../../semilinear-wave-equation.md). Higher regularity persists. No smallness of energy alone is asserted.
