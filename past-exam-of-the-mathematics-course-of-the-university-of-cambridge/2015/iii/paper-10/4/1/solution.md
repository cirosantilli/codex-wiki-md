↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**Compatible [Cauchy data](../../../../../../cauchy-data.md)** for a [wave map](../../../../../../wave-map.md) are $\phi_0:\mathbb R^n\to S^2$ and $\phi_1(x)\in T_{\phi_0(x)}S^2$: thus $|\phi_0|=1$ and $\phi_0\cdot\phi_1=0$. Smooth localized [wave map Cauchy data](../../../../../../wave-map-cauchy-data.md) equal a constant $p\in S^2$ with zero velocity outside a compact set. It is $\phi_0-p$, rather than the [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md)-valued map itself, that has [compact support](../../../../../../compact-support.md). The geometric constraints propagate under the [wave map](../../../../../../wave-map.md) equation.
