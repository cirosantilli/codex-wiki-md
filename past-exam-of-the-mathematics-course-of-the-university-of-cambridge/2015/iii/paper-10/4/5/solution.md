<h1 id="4/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**[Global regularity for one-dimensional wave maps](../../../../../../global-regularity-for-one-dimensional-wave-maps.md) holds for arbitrary smooth localized compatible data.** In [retarded and advanced null coordinates](../../../../../../retarded-and-advanced-null-coordinates.md) $u=t-x$, $v=t+x$, the [wave map](../../../../../../wave-map.md) equation says $D_v\phi_u=D_u\phi_v=0$, with $D$ the target [covariant derivative](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md). Hence $|\phi_u|^2$ depends only on $u$, and $|\phi_v|^2$ only on $v$. Initial derivative bounds persist. Compactness of the [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) and differentiated [energy estimates](../../../../../../energy-estimate.md) then prevent finite-time loss of smoothness.
