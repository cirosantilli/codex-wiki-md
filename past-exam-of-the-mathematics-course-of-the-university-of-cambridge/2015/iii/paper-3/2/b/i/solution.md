<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There is one constant path and $r$ loop generators; every other path is a word in those loops. Distinct words are distinct basis paths, so there are no relations among the generators. Therefore

$$
\boxed{kL_r\cong k\langle x_1,\ldots,x_r\rangle,}
$$

the [free associative algebra](../../../../../../../free-associative-algebra.md) on $r$ generators, with each loop sent to its corresponding generator. For $r=1$ this is $k[x_1]$; for $r\geq2$ it is noncommutative. The [quiver with one loop](../../../../../../../quiver-with-one-loop.md) is the $r=1$ case of the [quiver with r loops](../../../../../../../quiver-with-r-loops.md).
