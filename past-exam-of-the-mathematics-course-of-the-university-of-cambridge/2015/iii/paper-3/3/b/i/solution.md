<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If an arrow map failed to have maximal [matrix rank](../../../../../../../matrix-rank.md), it would have both a nonzero [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) and a nonzero [cokernel](../../../../../../../cokernel.md). Apply the preceding obstruction with $Y=X$. It would give $\operatorname{Ext}^1_Q(X,X)\ne0$, contrary to the assumption. Therefore **every arrow map is injective or surjective**, equivalently its rank is the minimum of the two vertex dimensions. This is a necessary property of a [rigid quiver representation](../../../../../../../rigid-quiver-representation.md).
