<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An acyclic finite [quiver](../../../../../../quiver.md) has integer vertex weights $w_i$ such that $w_j>w_i$ for every arrow $i\to j$; for example, let $w_i$ be the maximum length of a path ending at $i$. For $t\ne0$, act by $g_i(t)=t^{w_i}I_{n_i}$. Then

$$
(g(t)\cdot f)_\rho=t^{w_{t(\rho)}-w_{s(\rho)}}f_\rho.
$$

Every exponent is positive. The arrow matrices therefore extend polynomially to $t=0$ with all arrows zero, which is the origin of the [quiver representation space](../../../../../../quiver-representation-space.md). The values for $t\ne0$ lie in $\mathcal O_X$, so

$$
\boxed{0\in\overline{\mathcal O_X}\quad\text{for every }X.}
$$

This proves that [acyclic quiver representations degenerate to zero](../../../../../../acyclic-quiver-representations-degenerate-to-zero.md), also when some vertex spaces are zero.
