<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $k=0$ and $l\ne0$, divide the previous determinant equation by $l^2(l^2+2F)$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\sigma^2-lV\sigma+\frac{F l^2V^2}{l^2+2F}=0.}
$$

Thus

$$
\sigma=\frac{lV}{2}\pm\frac{lV}{2}\sqrt{\frac{l^2-2F}{l^2+2F}}.
$$

With the chosen $e^{-i\sigma t}$ convention, a positive imaginary part of $\sigma$ is growth. For a nonzero basic shear $V$, the necessary condition for [meridional-wave instability of a two-layer Sverdrup flow](../../../../../../meridional-wave-instability-of-a-two-layer-sverdrup-flow.md) is

$$
\boxed{0<l^2<2k_I^2,\qquad V\ne0.}
$$

For these nondegenerate modes it is also sufficient. The growth rate is

$$
\boxed{\gamma=\frac{|lV|}{2}\sqrt{\frac{2k_I^2-l^2}{l^2+2k_I^2}}.}
$$

Modes with $l^2>2k_I^2$ have real frequencies. Equality is the zero-growth coalescence of the two roots, while $l=0=k$ is a spatially uniform degeneracy and is not a growing finite-wavelength disturbance.

For $k=0$, the perturbation has $v'=\psi'_x=0$, so it does not directly advect the northward [planetary vorticity](../../../../../../planetary-vorticity.md) gradient. Consequently $\beta$ has **no explicit restoring contribution to the fixed-$V$ instability criterion**. It still sets the [Sverdrup balance](../../../../../../sverdrup-balance.md) velocity: at fixed wind forcing, $V=W/\beta$, and

$$
\gamma=\frac{|lW|}{2|\beta|}\sqrt{\frac{2k_I^2-l^2}{l^2+2k_I^2}}.
$$

Increasing $|\beta|$ at fixed $W$ reduces the shear and the growth rate, without creating a finite-$\beta$ wavelength cutoff for these purely meridional modes. The limit $\beta\to0$ at fixed $W$ is not a valid finite [Sverdrup balance](../../../../../../sverdrup-balance.md); its divergent velocity violates the weak-flow assumptions. Growth draws on the interfacial displacement and vertical shear, rather than on a time-dependent wind forcing, whose perturbation was set to zero.
