<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A four-dimensional $\mathcal N=1$ [chiral superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) is a complex scalar [superfield](../../../../../../superfield.md) satisfying

$$
\boxed{\bar D_{\dot\alpha}\Phi=0\quad(\dot\alpha=1,2).}
$$

The [supersymmetric covariant derivatives](../../../../../../supersymmetric-covariant-derivative.md) anticommute with the [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md), so the constraint is preserved by [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md). Use [left Grassmann derivatives](../../../../../../left-grassmann-derivative.md) and the printed convention $\bar D_{\dot\alpha}=-\bar\partial_{\dot\alpha}-i\theta^\beta\sigma^\mu_{\beta\dot\alpha}\partial_\mu$. The conjugate [antichiral superfield](../../../../../../antichiral-superfield.md) satisfies $D_\alpha\Phi^\dagger=0$.
