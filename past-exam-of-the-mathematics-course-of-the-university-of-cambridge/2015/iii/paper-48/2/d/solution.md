<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For ungauged [chiral superfields](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md), the two-derivative [supersymmetric action](../../../../../../supersymmetric-action.md) is a real full-[superspace](../../../../../../superspace.md) integral plus a holomorphic half-[superspace](../../../../../../superspace.md) integral:

$$
\boxed{S=\int d^4x\left[\int d^2\theta\,d^2\bar\theta\,K(\Phi,\Phi^\dagger)+\left(\int d^2\theta\,W(\Phi)+\mathrm{h.c.}\right)\right].}
$$

The real [Kähler potential](../../../../../../kahler-potential.md) $K$ determines the [Kähler metric](../../../../../../kahler-metric.md) and kinetic terms; the [superpotential](../../../../../../superpotential.md) $W$ determines [Yukawa interactions](../../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) and the [F-term scalar potential](../../../../../../f-term-scalar-potential.md). For canonical normalization, $K=\sum_i\Phi_i^\dagger\Phi_i$. [Berezin integration](../../../../../../berezin-integral.md) is normalized to extract the highest component, $\int d^2\theta\,\theta^2=1$, and the conjugate measure is chosen so the canonical term is $F_i^*F_i$. The [D-term](../../../../../../d-term.md) and [F-term](../../../../../../f-term.md) highest components vary by spacetime [total derivatives](../../../../../../total-derivative.md), making the action invariant under [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md). Gauging would replace the canonical bilinear by its gauge-covariant version and add a gauge kinetic [F-term](../../../../../../f-term.md); no gauge multiplet is needed for the chiral theory here.
