<h1 id="1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Extend $J$ complex-linearly to the [complexification of a real vector bundle](../../../../../../complexification-of-a-real-vector-bundle.md) $T_{\mathbb C}X=TX\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C$. Since $J^2=-\operatorname{id}$, its $+i$ and $-i$ [eigenbundles](../../../../../../eigenbundle.md) have smooth projections

$$
P^{1,0}=\tfrac12(\operatorname{id}-iJ),\qquad
P^{0,1}=\tfrac12(\operatorname{id}+iJ),\qquad
T_{\mathbb C}X=T^{1,0}X\oplus T^{0,1}X.
$$

Each has complex rank $n$: [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) interchanges them, and together they have rank $2n$. These are the [type decomposition of the complexified tangent bundle](../../../../../../type-decomposition-of-the-complexified-tangent-bundle.md). For an arbitrary [almost complex structure](../../../../../../almost-complex-manifold.md), they are smooth complex [vector bundles](../../../../../../vector-bundle.md); a [holomorphic vector bundle](../../../../../../holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) structure requires integrability.

When $J$ comes from a [holomorphic atlas](../../../../../../holomorphic-atlas.md), write a [holomorphic coordinate](../../../../../../holomorphic-coordinate.md) as $z_j=x_j+iy_j$. Its [Wirtinger derivatives](../../../../../../wirtinger-derivatives.md) are

$$
\frac\partial{\partial z_j}=\frac12\left(\frac\partial{\partial x_j}-i\frac\partial{\partial y_j}\right),\qquad
\frac\partial{\partial\bar z_j}=\frac12\left(\frac\partial{\partial x_j}+i\frac\partial{\partial y_j}\right).
$$

The induced [almost complex structure](../../../../../../almost-complex-manifold.md) has $J\partial_{x_j}=\partial_{y_j}$ and $J\partial_{y_j}=-\partial_{x_j}$. Hence the displayed [vector fields](../../../../../../vector-field.md) are respectively $+i$ and $-i$ [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md). They are linearly independent, and each collection has $n$ elements. Thus **they give local frames for $T^{1,0}X$ and $T^{0,1}X$ respectively**, with $T^{1,0}X$ the [holomorphic tangent bundle](../../../../../../holomorphic-tangent-bundle.md). The repeated $T^{0,1}X$ in the first sentence of the printed item must be read as the two complementary eigenbundles.
