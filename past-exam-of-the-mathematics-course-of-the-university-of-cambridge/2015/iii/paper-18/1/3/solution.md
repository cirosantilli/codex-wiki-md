<h1 id="1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

With the normalization used here, $N=2N_J$, where $N_J$ is the standard [Nijenhuis tensor](../../../../../../nijenhuis-tensor.md). First check that it is a [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md): the [Lie bracket](../../../../../../lie-bracket.md) identity $[fU,V]=f[U,V]-V(f)U$ gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
N_J(fU,V)-fN_J(U,V)
&=-(JV)(f)JU+V(f)U+(JV)(f)JU+V(f)J^2U\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Skew symmetry gives the same $C^\infty$-linearity in the second input. In coordinates from a [holomorphic atlas](../../../../../../holomorphic-atlas.md), the real coordinate [vector fields](../../../../../../vector-field.md) $\partial_{x_j},\partial_{y_j}$ commute, and $J$ sends each to another such field with a constant sign. Every [Lie bracket](../../../../../../lie-bracket.md) in $N_J$ therefore vanishes on these coordinate fields. Tensoriality then gives **$\boxed{N=0}$** on all smooth [vector fields](../../../../../../vector-field.md). The factor two in this paper does not change this vanishing conclusion.
