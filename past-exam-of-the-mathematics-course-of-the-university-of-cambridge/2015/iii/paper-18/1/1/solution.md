A [holomorphic atlas](../../../../../../holomorphic-atlas.md) on a real [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) of dimension $2n$ consists of compatible smooth charts $\varphi_a:U_a\to V_a\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ whose transition maps $\varphi_b\varphi_a^{-1}$ are [biholomorphic](../../../../../../biholomorphism.md) on their domains. An [almost complex structure](../../../../../../almost-complex-manifold.md) is a smooth real [vector bundle](../../../../../../vector-bundle.md) endomorphism $J:TX\to TX$ with $J^2=-\operatorname{id}$. In each chart define

$$
J_x=(d\varphi_a)_x^{-1}\,i\,(d\varphi_a)_x.
$$

The [differential](../../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) of a [holomorphic map](../../../../../../holomorphic-map.md) is a [complex-linear map](../../../../../../complex-linear-map.md), so the transition [differentials](../../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) commute with multiplication by $i$. Consequently the local definitions agree on overlaps. They are smooth and square to $-\operatorname{id}$, yielding **the natural [almost complex structure induced by a complex atlas](../../../../../../almost-complex-structure-induced-by-a-complex-atlas.md)**. This construction uses the atlas, rather than an arbitrary choice of coordinates on the underlying real [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md).
