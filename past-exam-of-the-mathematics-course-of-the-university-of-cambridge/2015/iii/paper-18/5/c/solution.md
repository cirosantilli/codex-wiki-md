<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take the threshold $k_0\geq1$ in the stated [spectral gap for powers of a positive line bundle](../../../../../../spectral-gap-for-powers-of-a-positive-line-bundle.md). For $q\geq1$ and $k\geq k_0$, it gives

$$
\langle\Delta''_{L^k}\alpha,\alpha\rangle\geq\varepsilon k\|\alpha\|^2
\quad\text{on }\mathcal A^{0,q}(X,L^k).
$$

A harmonic form must therefore be zero. The [Dolbeault Hodge decomposition](../../../../../../dolbeault-hodge-decomposition-on-a-compact-hermitian-manifold.md) and the [Dolbeault theorem](../../../../../../dolbeault-theorem.md) identify its zero harmonic space with $H^q(X,\mathcal O(L^k))$. Hence **$\boxed{H^q(X,L^k)=0\quad(q\geq1,\ k\geq k_0)}$**. For $q>\dim_{\mathbb C}X$ the form spaces already vanish. The gap controls the lowest eigenvalue including zero, so it rules out harmonic forms rather than just controlling the positive spectrum.
