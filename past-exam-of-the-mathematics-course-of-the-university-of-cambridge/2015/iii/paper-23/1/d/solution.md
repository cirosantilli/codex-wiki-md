↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) $U$ on $X$ is [kappa-complete](../../../../../../kappa-complete-filter.md) if, for every index set $J$ with $|J|<\kappa$ and every family $(A_j)_{j\in J}$ of members of $U$,

$$
\boxed{\bigcap_{j\in J}A_j\in U.}
$$

The bound is strictly fewer than $\kappa$ sets. In particular every [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) is $\aleph_0$-complete, since this requires only finite intersections; [countably complete ultrafilters](../../../../../../countably-complete-ultrafilter.md) require intersections of countably many members, equivalently $\aleph_1$-completeness.
