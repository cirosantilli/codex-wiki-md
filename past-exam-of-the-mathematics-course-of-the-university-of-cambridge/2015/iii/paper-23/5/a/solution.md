<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expand the language by constants for $A$ and write $T_A=\operatorname{Th}(\mathcal M,a)_{a\in A}$. An [A-type over a structure](../../../../../../a-type-over-a-structure.md) is a set of formulas in a fixed finite tuple $\bar x$, with parameters from $A$, consistent with this [complete theory](../../../../../../complete-theory.md).

Let $Q=\{\varphi_1(\bar x),\ldots,\varphi_m(\bar x)\}\subseteq P$. If no tuple of $M$ realized $Q$, then

$$
\mathcal M\models\neg\exists\bar x\,\bigwedge_{j=1}^m\varphi_j(\bar x).
$$

That sentence belongs to $T_A$ and contradicts the consistency of $T_A\cup P$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\text{every finite subset of an A-type is realized in }\mathcal M.}
$$

For $Q=\varnothing$, any tuple works because domains are nonempty. This proves finite satisfiability; realization of the entire [partial type](../../../../../../partial-type.md) may require an [elementary extension](../../../../../../elementary-extension.md).
