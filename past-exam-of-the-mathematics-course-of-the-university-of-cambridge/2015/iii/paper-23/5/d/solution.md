<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $p(\bar x)$ be an [isolated type](../../../../../../isolated-type.md) over $A$, with isolating formula $\theta(\bar x)$ over $A$. Its consistency with $T_A$ and the [complete theory](../../../../../../complete-theory.md) property imply

$$
T_A\models\exists\bar x\,\theta(\bar x):
$$

otherwise $\neg\exists\bar x\,\theta$ would belong to $T_A$, contradicting consistency. Choose a tuple $\bar b\in M$ satisfying $\theta$. By isolation,

$$
T_A\models\forall\bar x\,(\theta\to\psi)\qquad(\psi\in p),
$$

so $\bar b$ satisfies every member of $p$. Therefore **every [isolated A-type](../../../../../../isolated-type.md) is realized in the original structure**. The same reasoning works for a [principal partial type](../../../../../../principal-partial-type.md) whose isolating formula need not itself be a member of the type.
