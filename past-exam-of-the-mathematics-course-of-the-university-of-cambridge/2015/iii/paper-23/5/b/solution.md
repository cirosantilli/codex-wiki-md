↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Stone topology on a type space](../../../../../../stone-topology-on-a-type-space.md) has basic open sets

$$
\boxed{[\varphi]=\{p\in S_n^{\mathcal M}(A):\varphi(\bar x)\in p\},}
$$

where $\varphi$ ranges over formulas with parameters in $A$ and $n$ free variables. Here points are [complete types](../../../../../../complete-type.md) consistent with $T_A=\operatorname{Th}(\mathcal M,a)_{a\in A}$. Arbitrary open sets are unions of these basic sets.

The identities

$$
[\varphi]\cap[\psi]=[\varphi\wedge\psi],\qquad
S_n^{\mathcal M}(A)\setminus[\varphi]=[\neg\varphi]
$$

show that the basic sets are [clopen sets](../../../../../../clopen-set.md) and form a basis. Distinct [complete types](../../../../../../complete-type.md) contain opposite choices for some formula, so this topology is Hausdorff. The [compactness theorem](../../../../../../compactness-theorem.md) gives compactness: a family of basic closed sets with the [finite intersection property](../../../../../../finite-intersection-property.md) determines a consistent set of formulas, which extends to a [complete type](../../../../../../complete-type.md). Thus logical formulas provide a basis of clopen tests for distinguishing points of the [type space](../../../../../../type-space.md).
