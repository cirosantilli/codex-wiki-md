<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume $T$ has [quantifier elimination](../../../../../../quantifier-elimination.md), and let $j:\mathcal M\to\mathcal N$ be a [structure embedding](../../../../../../structure-embedding.md) between its models. For any [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\varphi(\bar x)$, choose a [quantifier-free formula](../../../../../../quantifier-free-formula.md) $\psi$ equivalent to it modulo $T$. A [structure embedding](../../../../../../structure-embedding.md) preserves and reflects [atomic formulas](../../../../../../atomic-formula.md); induction through the Boolean connectives therefore preserves every [quantifier-free formula](../../../../../../quantifier-free-formula.md). Thus

$$
\mathcal M\models\varphi(\bar a)
\iff\mathcal M\models\psi(\bar a)
\iff\mathcal N\models\psi(j\bar a)
\iff\mathcal N\models\varphi(j\bar a).
$$

The embedding is elementary. **Every theory with [quantifier elimination](../../../../../../quantifier-elimination.md) is [model-complete](../../../../../../model-complete-theory.md).**
