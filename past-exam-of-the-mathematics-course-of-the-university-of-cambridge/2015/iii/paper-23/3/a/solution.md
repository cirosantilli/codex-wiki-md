<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [first-order theory](../../../../../../first-order-theory.md) $T$ is [model-complete](../../../../../../model-complete-theory.md) if every [structure embedding](../../../../../../structure-embedding.md) between models of $T$ is an [elementary embedding](../../../../../../elementary-embedding.md). Equivalently, whenever $\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal N$ and both satisfy $T$, one has $\mathcal M\preccurlyeq\mathcal N$:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M\models\varphi(\bar a)\iff\mathcal N\models\varphi(\bar a)}
$$

for every [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\varphi$ and every finite tuple $\bar a\in M$. This is preservation of all formulas with parameters, rather than merely all [first-order sentences](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md).
