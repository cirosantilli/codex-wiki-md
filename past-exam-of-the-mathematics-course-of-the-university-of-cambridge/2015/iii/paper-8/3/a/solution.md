<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the normalized standard [Gaussian density](../../../../../../multivariate-normal-density.md),

$$
\nabla\gamma=-v\gamma,\qquad
\Delta\gamma=(|v|^2-d)\gamma,\qquad
\nabla\cdot(v\gamma)=(d-|v|^2)\gamma.
$$

The two terms cancel, so **the [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Fokker-Planck equation](../../../../../../ornstein-uhlenbeck-fokker-planck-equation.md) has [stationary density for a Fokker-Planck equation](../../../../../../stationary-density-for-a-fokker-planck-equation.md)**

$$
\boxed{\partial_t\gamma=0,\qquad
\Delta\gamma+\nabla\cdot(v\gamma)=0.}
$$

Equivalently its [Fokker-Planck probability current](../../../../../../fokker-planck-probability-current.md) $-\nabla\gamma-v\gamma$ vanishes identically. The normalization follows from the [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) in each coordinate.
