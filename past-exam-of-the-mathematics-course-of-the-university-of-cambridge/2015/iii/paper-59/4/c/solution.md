<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

- **Muted near-infrared molecular bands:** [exoplanet transmission spectra](../../../../../../exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md) across approximately $1.1$–$1.7\,\mu\mathrm m$, or a broader $1$–$5\,\mu\mathrm m$ interval, can show weak [water](../../../../../../water.md) features because an opaque [exoplanet cloud deck](../../../../../../exoplanet-cloud-deck.md) truncates the atmospheric annulus before the gas becomes transparent in the continuum.
- **A rising transit radius towards blue wavelengths:** optical [exoplanet transmission spectroscopy](../../../../../../transit-spectroscopy.md) over roughly $0.3$–$1\,\mu\mathrm m$ can show a [Rayleigh scattering](../../../../../../rayleigh-scattering.md) or aerosol slope from small haze particles. Larger particles can instead give a nearly wavelength-independent, grey continuum.
- **Enhanced reflected light:** an optical [exoplanet secondary eclipse](../../../../../../exoplanet-secondary-eclipse.md) or [reflected-light planetary phase curve](../../../../../../reflected-light-planetary-phase-curve.md) observation around $0.4$–$0.9\,\mu\mathrm m$ can indicate a large [geometric albedo](../../../../../../geometric-albedo.md) and wavelength-dependent reflection from bright condensate [exoplanet clouds](../../../../../../exoplanet-cloud.md).

Cloud particles can scatter or absorb, so not every [atmospheric haze](../../../../../../haze.md) makes a high-albedo planet. Weak molecular bands alone also admit low abundance or high [mean molecular weight](../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight.md); a consistent spectral combination is stronger evidence than a single signature.
