<h1 id="4/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

For typical well-mixed structures, the dominant processes are:

- **[Gas giant](../../../../../../gas-giant.md) interiors:** efficient [convection](../../../../../../convection.md) through most of the deep fluid envelope; [radiative transfer](../../../../../../radiative-transfer.md) releases the heat near the [photosphere](../../../../../../photosphere.md).
- **Rocky interiors:** slow solid-state [mantle convection](../../../../../../mantle-convection.md) over geological time, with [heat conduction](../../../../../../thermal-conduction.md) dominant across the rigid [lithosphere](../../../../../../lithosphere.md). A liquid core can also convect; being solid does not prevent creep-driven heat transport in the mantle.
- **Weakly irradiated giant atmospheres at $0.1$–$10\,\mathrm{bar}$:** usually [convection](../../../../../../convection.md) in the [planetary troposphere](../../../../../../planetary-troposphere.md), becoming radiative near and above the [tropopause](../../../../../../tropopause.md). The transition pressure and cloud or compositional effects vary between planets.
- **Strongly irradiated [hot Jupiter](../../../../../../hot-jupiter.md) atmospheres at $0.1$–$10\,\mathrm{bar}$:** usually a stable radiative region; the deep [radiative-convective boundary](../../../../../../radiative-convective-boundary.md) can lie at substantially larger pressure. Atmospheric winds also redistribute energy horizontally.

Representative temperatures must specify the level: **[Earth](../../../../../../earth.md) has about $288\,\mathrm K$ at the surface** (about $255\,\mathrm K$ effective emission temperature); **[Jupiter](../../../../../../jupiter.md) has about $165\,\mathrm K$ near one bar** (about $125\,\mathrm K$ effective temperature); **[hot Jupiters](../../../../../../hot-jupiter.md) commonly have photospheric temperatures of order $1000$–$2500\,\mathrm K$**; and **the [Sun](../../../../../../sun.md)'s [photosphere](../../../../../../photosphere.md) is about $5800\,\mathrm K$**. Upper layers, nightsides, deep interiors and the solar corona have different temperatures. These are characteristic values, not constant temperatures throughout each atmosphere.
