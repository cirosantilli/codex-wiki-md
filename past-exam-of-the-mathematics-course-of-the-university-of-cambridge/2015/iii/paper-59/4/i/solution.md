<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Three detection methods are **[exoplanet transit photometry](../../../../../../exoplanet-transit-photometry.md), the [radial-velocity method](../../../../../../doppler-spectroscopy.md), and [exoplanet direct imaging](../../../../../../exoplanet-direct-imaging.md)**. Transits detect obscuration of the star; radial velocities detect stellar reflex motion; imaging separates the planet's own reflected or thermal light from the star.

[Exoplanet transit photometry](../../../../../../exoplanet-transit-photometry.md) supports wavelength-dependent [exoplanet transmission spectra](../../../../../../exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md), while the same orbital geometry supports [exoplanet secondary eclipses](../../../../../../exoplanet-secondary-eclipse.md) and phase-resolved planetary spectra. [Exoplanet direct imaging](../../../../../../exoplanet-direct-imaging.md) provides resolved light for atmospheric spectroscopy. Stellar radial-velocity discovery alone does not measure an atmosphere, although high-resolution follow-up can separate a moving planetary molecular spectrum through its changing [Doppler effect](../../../../../../doppler-effect.md).

Two favorable conditions for detecting wide-orbit planets by [exoplanet direct imaging](../../../../../../exoplanet-direct-imaging.md) are:

- **Resolvable projected separation:** the angular separation is $\theta\simeq a_\perp/d$, so a wide orbit around a nearby star places the planet beyond the instrument's inner working angle, related to $\lambda/D$ and the [coronagraph](../../../../../../coronagraph.md) design.
- **Sufficient planet-star contrast:** young, massive planets remain intrinsically hot and bright in the [infrared](../../../../../../infrared.md), improving contrast against the star; starlight suppression and favorable observing wavelengths further help.

A wide orbit improves separation but weakens reflected-light illumination and lengthens the orbital period. These conditions therefore describe imaging sensitivity, not an assertion that every detection method becomes easier at large separation.
