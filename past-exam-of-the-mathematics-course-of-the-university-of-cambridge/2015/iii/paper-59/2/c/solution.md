<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In [local thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md), thermal intensity samples the [Planck function](../../../../../../planck-function.md) near an optical depth of order unity. The [Eddington-Barbier relation](../../../../../../eddington-barbier-relation.md) makes this explicit: $I_\lambda(0,\mu)\simeq B_\lambda[T(\tau_\lambda=\mu)]$. A molecular band has greater [opacity](../../../../../../opacity.md) than its adjacent continuum and therefore samples a higher layer. A band in emission relative to the continuum implies that this higher layer is hotter: **the line-forming region has an [atmospheric thermal inversion](../../../../../../inversion-meteorology.md)** under the assumed LTE, thermal interpretation.

The continuum is thermal radiation from an optically thick, deeper [photosphere](../../../../../../photosphere.md), with comparatively smooth [opacity](../../../../../../opacity.md). In an H/He [hot Jupiter](../../../../../../hot-jupiter.md), [collision-induced absorption](../../../../../../collision-induced-absorption-and-emission.md) by H2-H2 and H2-He collisions supplies an important continuum; weak overlapping molecular lines and opaque [exoplanet clouds](../../../../../../exoplanet-cloud.md) can contribute too. It is not a separate [blackbody](../../../../../../blackbody.md) emitter floating above the gas. A strongly isothermal layer would erase LTE molecular contrast rather than generate emission peaks.

In the emitting inversion, $dT/dz>0$, whereas the [dry adiabatic lapse rate](../../../../../../dry-adiabatic-lapse-rate.md) has $dT/dz=-g/C_p<0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{dT}{dz}>0>-\frac g{C_p},}
$$

which lies on the stable side of the [Schwarzschild criterion](../../../../../../schwarzschild-criterion.md). An upward-displaced parcel cools and becomes denser than the ambient hot upper gas. The region can thus carry and redistribute thermal energy by [radiative transfer](../../../../../../radiative-transfer.md), **not by unstable thermal [convection](../../../../../../convection.md)**. Winds may transport energy horizontally; stability rules out the specified buoyant vertical [convection](../../../../../../convection.md), not every possible motion.
