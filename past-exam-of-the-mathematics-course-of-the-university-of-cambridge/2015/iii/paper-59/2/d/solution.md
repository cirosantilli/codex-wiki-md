An [atmospheric thermal inversion](../../../../../../inversion-meteorology.md) is an altitude interval with $dT/dz>0$. Absorption of incoming stellar radiation above the usual thermal-emitting layers can heat the upper gas faster than it cools, producing an inversion. In a [semi-grey irradiated atmosphere](../../../../../../semi-grey-irradiated-atmosphere.md), a large shortwave-to-infrared [opacity](../../../../../../opacity.md) ratio favors such high-altitude energy deposition; local infrared emitters and the intrinsic flux also matter.

In the [Solar system](../../../../../../solar-system.md), **[Earth](../../../../../../earth.md) and all four giant planets have well-known stratospheric inversions**. The [ozone layer](../../../../../../ozone-layer.md) absorbs ultraviolet sunlight on [Earth](../../../../../../earth.md); [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) and photochemical hydrocarbons absorb solar radiation in the giant planets, with aerosols contributing. The giant planets are [Jupiter](../../../../../../jupiter.md), [Saturn](../../../../../../saturn.md), [Uranus](../../../../../../uranus.md) and [Neptune](../../../../../../neptune.md). This refers to their stratospheric temperature rise, not to the gradient at every atmospheric level.

For [hot Jupiters](../../../../../../hot-jupiter.md), influential factors include the stellar flux and spectrum; the abundances of high-altitude absorbers such as [titanium monoxide](../../../../../../titanium-monoxide.md) and [vanadium monoxide](../../../../../../vanadium-ii-oxide.md); [atmospheric metallicity of a giant planet](../../../../../../atmospheric-metallicity-of-a-giant-planet.md) and [atmospheric carbon-to-oxygen ratio](../../../../../../atmospheric-carbon-to-oxygen-ratio.md); [thermal dissociation](../../../../../../thermal-dissociation.md), [atmospheric photochemistry](../../../../../../atmospheric-photochemistry.md) and condensation; a [atmospheric cold trap](../../../../../../atmospheric-cold-trap.md) or [atmospheric condensate rainout](../../../../../../atmospheric-condensate-rainout.md) that removes absorbers; replenishment by vertical mixing; [exoplanet clouds](../../../../../../exoplanet-cloud.md) and [atmospheric hazes](../../../../../../haze.md); and heat redistribution by circulation. The ratio of visible heating to infrared cooling, rather than a single chemical species in isolation, determines whether an inversion persists.
