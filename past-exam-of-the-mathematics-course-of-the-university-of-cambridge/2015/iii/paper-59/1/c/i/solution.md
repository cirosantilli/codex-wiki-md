↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The continuum [exoplanet transit photometry](../../../../../../../exoplanet-transit-photometry.md) depth is approximately the opaque projected area ratio, assuming a corrected or negligible [limb darkening](../../../../../../../limb-darkening.md) contribution:

$$
D_0=\left(\frac{R_p}{R_s}\right)^2=0.01,\qquad R_p=0.1R_s.
$$

For a Sun-sized host, **$R_p\simeq6.96\times10^7\,\mathrm m\simeq R_J\simeq11R_\oplus$**. The natural size analogue is [Jupiter](../../../../../../../jupiter.md), not [Earth](../../../../../../../earth.md).

The expected bulk constituent is **[molecular hydrogen](../../../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md)**, with [helium](../../../../../../../helium.md) next most abundant for a retained [primary planetary atmosphere](../../../../../../../primary-planetary-atmosphere.md). The [water](../../../../../../../water.md) band detects a strong trace absorber; it does not imply that [water](../../../../../../../water.md) is the dominant gas. This interpretation assumes a conventional H/He [gas giant](../../../../../../../gas-giant.md); the measured radius by itself is not a measurement of mass or composition.
