↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../../random-variable-split.md), $T=\int_0^\infty\mathbf1\{T>t\}\,dt$. [Tonelli theorem](../../../../../../../tonelli-theorem.md) permits interchanging expectation and this nonnegative integral, giving the [tail integral formula for moments](../../../../../../../tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb ET=\int_0^\infty\mathbb P(T>t)\,dt=\int_0^\infty S(t)\,dt.}
$$

The equality also holds when the mean is infinite. Thus mean completion time is the total area under the [survival function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md), while a [restricted mean survival time](../../../../../../../restricted-mean-survival-time.md) integrates only to a specified follow-up horizon.
