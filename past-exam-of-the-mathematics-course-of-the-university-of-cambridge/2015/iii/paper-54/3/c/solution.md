<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Since $v=t+r_\star$, $dt=dv-f^{-1}dr$. Substitution into the radial metric cancels the $dr^2/f$ terms. The [Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../../../ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md) give

$$
\boxed{ds^2=-f(r)dv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2d\Omega_3^2.}
$$

The radial block has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $-1$, even when $f=0$. Its coefficients are analytic for every $r>0$, so this form extends through $r=r_+$ and also through $r=r_-$ to $0<r<r_+$. Ordinary angular coordinate singularities are removed by angular charts on the three-sphere. The genuine endpoint $r=0$ is not included in this extension.

The field $n=-\partial_r$ is an ingoing null tangent. It is future-directed in the original exterior, since its inner product with the future stationary [Killing vector field](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) is $-1$, and it remains future-directed by continuation. This fixes the time orientation used in the next part.
