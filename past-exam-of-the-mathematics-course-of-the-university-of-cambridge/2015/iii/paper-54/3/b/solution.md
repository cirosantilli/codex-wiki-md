<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For radial [null geodesics](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md), the metric gives $dt/dr=\pm f^{-1}$ in the exterior. Define the [tortoise coordinate](../../../../../../tortoise-coordinate.md) by

$$
\boxed{r_\star(r)=\int^r\frac{dx}{f(x)}.}
$$

An outgoing future radial ray has $dt/dr=f^{-1}$, so $u=t-r_\star$ is constant; an ingoing ray has $dt/dr=-f^{-1}$, so $v=t+r_\star$ is constant. These describe the null trajectories independently of the choice of [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md).

For the [tortoise coordinate of a five-dimensional charged black hole](../../../../../../tortoise-coordinate-of-a-five-dimensional-charged-black-hole.md), with $a=r_+$, $b=r_-$ and $\Delta=a^2-b^2$, partial fractions give

$$
\frac1{f(r)}=1+\frac{a^4}{\Delta(r^2-a^2)}-\frac{b^4}{\Delta(r^2-b^2)}.
$$

Thus a useful explicit [tortoise coordinate](../../../../../../tortoise-coordinate.md) is

$$
\boxed{r_\star=r+\frac{a^3}{2\Delta}\log\left|\frac{r-a}{r+a}\right|-\frac{b^3}{2\Delta}\log\left|\frac{r-b}{r+b}\right|+C.}
$$

The logarithms signal failure of the static coordinates at the horizons, rather than a curvature singularity there.
