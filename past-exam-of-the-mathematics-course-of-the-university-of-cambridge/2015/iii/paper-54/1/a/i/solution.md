<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the vacuum exterior, the [Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equation](../../../../../../../tolman-oppenheimer-volkoff-equation.md) gives $m'=0$, so $m(r)=M$. The remaining metric function obeys

$$
\Phi'(r)=\frac{M}{r(r-2M)}=\frac12\frac{d}{dr}\log\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right).
$$

Thus $e^{2\Phi}=C(1-2M/r)$. A constant rescaling of the time coordinate removes $C$; equivalently, [asymptotic flatness](../../../../../../../asymptotically-flat-spacetime.md) fixes $C=1$ when time is normalized at infinity. Consequently **the exterior is the [Schwarzschild metric](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md)**:

$$
\boxed{ds^2=-\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right)dt^2+\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right)^{-1}dr^2+r^2d\Omega^2,\qquad r>R.}
$$

The [mass](../../../../../../../mass.md) $M=m(R)$ includes the full [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) interior. No equation of state beyond the surface enters this vacuum result.
