<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) with nonnegative [energy density](../../../../../../../energy-density.md) and [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md), the [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md) is $p\leq\rho$. [Pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) decreases outward, so it is enough to test the central [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md). Put $s=\sqrt{1-2M/R}$; the regular [Interior Schwarzschild metric](../../../../../../../interior-schwarzschild-metric.md) requires $3s-1>0$. The [central pressure of a constant-density relativistic star](../../../../../../../central-pressure-of-a-constant-density-relativistic-star.md) gives

$$
\frac{p_c}{\rho}=\frac{1-s}{3s-1}\leq1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad1-s\leq3s-1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad s\geq\frac12.
$$

Therefore the [dominant-energy compactness bound for an incompressible star](../../../../../../../dominant-energy-compactness-bound-for-an-incompressible-star.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac MR\leq\frac38.}
$$

Equality has $p_c=\rho$ and a finite central [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md); it is allowed by the non-strict [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md). This is stronger than the regular-star [Buchdahl's theorem](../../../../../../../buchdahl-s-theorem.md) limit $M/R<4/9$.
