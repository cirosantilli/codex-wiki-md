<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [spherical null expansions in double-null coordinates](../../../../../../../spherical-null-expansions-in-double-null-coordinates.md), the cross-sectional area of a symmetry sphere is $A=4\pi r^2$. For the two affine [null geodesic congruences](../../../../../../../null-geodesic-congruence.md), the definition of [null expansion](../../../../../../../null-expansion.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\theta_k=\frac{2r_{,V}}{fr},\qquad\theta_\ell=\frac{2r_{,U}}{fr}.}
$$

There is no extra derivative of $f$ in these formulas: the [null expansion](../../../../../../../null-expansion.md) differentiates the transverse area, not the normalization of the tangent. Equivalently, the projected metric is $q_{AB}=r^2\gamma_{AB}$ and its [optical tensor](../../../../../../../optical-tensor.md) is $(k(r)/r)q_{AB}$, or $(\ell(r)/r)q_{AB}$ for the other congruence. It is purely trace, so both [null shears](../../../../../../../null-shear.md) vanish.

The [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md) vanishes because the covector tangents are gradients of $-U$ and $-V$, and hence their [exterior derivatives](../../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md) vanish. Each congruence is orthogonal to a family of null hypersurfaces. This also identifies the [Frobenius theorem](../../../../../../../frobenius-theorem.md) reason for zero [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md).
