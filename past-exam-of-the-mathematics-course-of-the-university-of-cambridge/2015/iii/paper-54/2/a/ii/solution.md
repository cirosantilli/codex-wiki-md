<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an affinely parametrized [null geodesic congruence](../../../../../../../null-geodesic-congruence.md), put $B^a{}_b=\nabla_bU^a$. Differentiating along a ray and commuting [covariant derivatives](../../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) gives

$$
U^c\nabla_c B^a{}_b=-B^a{}_cB^c{}_b+R^a{}_{dcb}U^dU^c,
$$

using $[\nabla_c,\nabla_b]V^a=R^a{}_{dcb}V^d$. The geodesic acceleration term vanishes. Since $U$ is null and affine, $B^a{}_bU^b=0$ and $U_aB^a{}_b=0$. Its trace is therefore the screen trace $\theta$, and the trace of its square equals that of the [optical tensor](../../../../../../../optical-tensor.md) squared. Taking the trace, with $R_{db}=R^a{}_{dab}$, yields

$$
\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda}=-\widehat B_{ab}\widehat B^{ba}-R_{ab}U^aU^b.
$$

Now decompose the [optical tensor](../../../../../../../optical-tensor.md) into [null expansion](../../../../../../../null-expansion.md), [null shear](../../../../../../../null-shear.md) and [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md). The symmetric trace-free part is orthogonal to the trace and to the antisymmetric part, while $\widehat\omega_{ab}\widehat\omega^{ba}=-\widehat\omega_{ab}\widehat\omega^{ab}$. Hence

$$
\widehat B_{ab}\widehat B^{ba}=\frac12\theta^2+\widehat\sigma_{ab}\widehat\sigma^{ab}-\widehat\omega_{ab}\widehat\omega^{ab}.
$$

Substitution proves the [Null Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../../null-raychaudhuri-equation.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda}=-\frac12\theta^2-\widehat\sigma_{ab}\widehat\sigma^{ab}+\widehat\omega_{ab}\widehat\omega^{ab}-R_{ab}U^aU^b.}
$$

The squares use the positive-definite screen metric. Thus [null shear](../../../../../../../null-shear.md) contributes to focusing, whereas nonzero [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md) has the opposite sign.
