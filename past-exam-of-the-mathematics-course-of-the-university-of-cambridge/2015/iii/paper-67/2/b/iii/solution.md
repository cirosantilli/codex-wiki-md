<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Because each term in the [history-subspace propagation Hamiltonian](../../../../../../../history-subspace-propagation-hamiltonian.md) is [positive semidefinite](../../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md), the uniform history vector has zero energy and is a [ground state](../../../../../../../ground-state.md):

$$
\boxed{|\varphi\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt{T+1}}\sum_{t=0}^T
|\psi_t\rangle\otimes|1\rangle^{\otimes t}|0\rangle^{\otimes(T-t)}.}
$$

Indeed every edge term annihilates the uniform coefficients. Conversely, a zero-energy vector must have equal coefficients across every edge of the connected path, so this [computational history state](../../../../../../../computational-history-state.md) is the unique [ground state](../../../../../../../ground-state.md) in $\mathcal L$. It coherently records every intermediate state of the [quantum circuit](../../../../../../../quantum-circuit-split.md).
