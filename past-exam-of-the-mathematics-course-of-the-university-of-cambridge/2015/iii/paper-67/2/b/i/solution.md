The matrix $D=I-|e_0\rangle\langle e_0|$ is an [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md). Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are

$$
\boxed{0\ \text{with multiplicity }1,\qquad
1\ \text{with multiplicity }T.}
$$

Its unique [ground state](../../../../../../../ground-state.md) in $\mathcal L$ is $|e_0\rangle=|\psi_0\rangle|0\rangle^{\otimes T}$, and its [spectral gap](../../../../../../../spectral-gap.md) is one.
