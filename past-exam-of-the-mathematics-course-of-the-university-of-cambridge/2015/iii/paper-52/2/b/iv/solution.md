<h1 id="2/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The metric dependence of the [minimally coupled scalar field](../../../../../../../minimally-coupled-scalar-field.md) [Lagrangian](../../../../../../../lagrangian.md) is $\partial L/\partial g^{\alpha\beta}=-\tfrac12\nabla_\alpha\phi\nabla_\beta\phi$. Part (ii) therefore gives **the stress-energy tensor**

$$
\boxed{T_{\alpha\beta}=\nabla_\alpha\phi\nabla_\beta\phi-g_{\alpha\beta}\left[\frac12\nabla_\rho\phi\nabla^\rho\phi+V(\phi)\right].}
$$

Use [metric compatibility](../../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) and the [symmetric Hessian of a scalar field](../../../../../../../symmetric-hessian-of-a-scalar-field.md) to compute

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_\mu T^\mu{}_{\alpha}
&=(\Box\phi)\nabla_\alpha\phi+\nabla^\mu\phi\,\nabla_\mu\nabla_\alpha\phi
-\nabla^\mu\phi\,\nabla_\alpha\nabla_\mu\phi-V'(\phi)\nabla_\alpha\phi\\
&=(\Box\phi-V'(\phi))\nabla_\alpha\phi.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is the [scalar stress-energy divergence identity](../../../../../../../scalar-stress-energy-divergence-identity.md), an instance of the [diffeomorphism Noether identity for a scalar field](../../../../../../../diffeomorphism-noether-identity-for-a-scalar-field.md). On the scalar equation of motion it gives **stress-energy conservation**, $\boxed{\nabla_\mu T^\mu{}_{\alpha}=0}$.
