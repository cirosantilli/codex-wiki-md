<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Vary the inverse [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) while holding the matter fields fixed. Differentiating $g_{a\mu}g^{\mu b}=\delta_a{}^b$ gives $\delta g_{ab}=-g_{a\mu}g_{b\nu}\delta g^{\mu\nu}$. The [Jacobi determinant derivative formula](../../../../../../../jacobi-determinant-derivative-formula.md) consequently implies the [inverse metric volume variation](../../../../../../../inverse-metric-volume-variation.md)

$$
\delta\sqrt{-g}=-\frac12\sqrt{-g}\,g_{\alpha\beta}\delta g^{\alpha\beta}.
$$

Since the matter [Lagrangian](../../../../../../../lagrangian.md) in this question contains no derivatives of the [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md), its metric variation is algebraic. The product rule gives

$$
\frac{\partial(\sqrt{-g}L)}{\partial g^{\alpha\beta}}
=\sqrt{-g}\left(\frac{\partial L}{\partial g^{\alpha\beta}}-\frac12g_{\alpha\beta}L\right).
$$

Multiplying by $-2/\sqrt{-g}$ proves **the equivalence of the stress-energy definitions**:

$$
\boxed{T_{\alpha\beta}=-2\frac{\partial L}{\partial g^{\alpha\beta}}+Lg_{\alpha\beta}.}
$$

The variations of the symmetric [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) are understood symmetrically. If the matter [action](../../../../../../../action.md) contained metric derivatives, the definition would instead require a functional derivative and the corresponding integration by parts.
