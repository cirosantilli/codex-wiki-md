↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Contract the [Christoffel symbol](../../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) formula for the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md). The first and third derivative terms cancel after relabelling their summed indices:

$$
\Gamma^\rho{}_{\rho\mu}=\frac12g^{\rho\sigma}\partial_\mu g_{\rho\sigma}.
$$

The [Jacobi determinant derivative formula](../../../../../../../jacobi-determinant-derivative-formula.md) gives $\partial_\mu g=g\,g^{\rho\sigma}\partial_\mu g_{\rho\sigma}$. In a Lorentzian chart $g<0$, and hence $\partial_\mu\sqrt{-g}=\tfrac12\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\rho\sigma}\partial_\mu g_{\rho\sigma}$. Thus **the trace is the logarithmic derivative of the volume density**:

$$
\boxed{\Gamma^\rho{}_{\rho\mu}=\frac1{\sqrt{-g}}\partial_\mu\sqrt{-g}.}
$$

In particular, the [covariant divergence formula](../../../../../../../covariant-divergence-formula.md) for a [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) is $\nabla_\mu A^\mu=(-g)^{-1/2}\partial_\mu(\sqrt{-g}A^\mu)$.
