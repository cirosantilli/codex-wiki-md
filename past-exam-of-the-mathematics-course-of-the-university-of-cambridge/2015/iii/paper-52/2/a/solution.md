<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [contracted Bianchi identity](../../../../../../contracted-bianchi-identity.md) gives $\nabla^aR_{ab}=\tfrac12\nabla_bR$. Combining the proposed equation with [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) therefore imposes $\nabla_bR=0$. Taking its trace gives $R=\kappa T$, with $T=g^{ab}T_{ab}$, so for nonzero $\kappa$ the matter trace must also be constant. Ordinary matter need not satisfy this extra condition. For example, if asymptotic flatness forces the constant [scalar curvature](../../../../../../scalar-curvature.md) to vanish, the proposal would require $T=0$ everywhere.

**The problem is an unjustified restriction on the matter trace**, rather than the absence of every possible solution. The divergence-free [Einstein tensor](../../../../../../einstein-tensor.md) avoids it:

$$
\boxed{G_{ab}=R_{ab}-\frac12Rg_{ab},\qquad\nabla^aG_{ab}=0.}
$$

The [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) $G_{ab}+\Lambda g_{ab}=\kappa T_{ab}$, with constant [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) $\Lambda$, are consequently compatible with [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md).
