<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Treat each $x^\alpha$ as a [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md). Its second [covariant derivative](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) is $\nabla_\beta\nabla_\gamma x^\alpha=-\Gamma^\alpha{}_{\gamma\beta}$, so $\Box x^\alpha=-H^\alpha$, where $H^\alpha=g^{\beta\gamma}\Gamma^\alpha{}_{\beta\gamma}$.

On the other hand, [metric compatibility](../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) gives

$$
0=\nabla_\nu g^{\mu\nu}=\partial_\nu g^{\mu\nu}+\Gamma^\mu{}_{\lambda\nu}g^{\lambda\nu}+\Gamma^\nu{}_{\lambda\nu}g^{\mu\lambda}.
$$

Using the [Christoffel trace identity](../../../../../../christoffel-trace-identity.md) in the last term yields

$$
\partial_\nu(\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\mu\nu})=-\sqrt{-g}\,H^\mu.
$$

Since the volume density never vanishes in a valid chart, **the harmonic coordinate conditions are equivalent**:

$$
\boxed{\Box x^\alpha=0\iff g^{\beta\gamma}\Gamma^\alpha{}_{\beta\gamma}=0
\iff\partial_\nu(\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\mu\nu})=0.}
$$
