<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Use the equivalent density form of [harmonic slicing](../../../../../../harmonic-slicing.md) from part (ii). With $\sqrt{-g}=\alpha\sqrt\gamma$, positive [lapse function](../../../../../../lapse-function.md) $\alpha$, and the corrected inverse [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md), its time component becomes

$$
0=-\partial_t\left(\frac{\sqrt\gamma}{\alpha}\right)
+\partial_i\left(\frac{\sqrt\gamma}{\alpha}\beta^i\right).
$$

Thus $D_t(\sqrt\gamma/\alpha)=(\sqrt\gamma/\alpha)\partial_i\beta^i$, or

$$
D_t\log\sqrt\gamma-D_t\log\alpha=\partial_i\beta^i.
$$

The [spatial volume evolution identity](../../../../../../spatial-volume-evolution-identity.md) gives $D_t\log\sqrt\gamma=\partial_i\beta^i-\alpha K$. Subtraction yields $D_t\log\alpha=-\alpha K$, hence **the [harmonic lapse evolution](../../../../../../harmonic-lapse-evolution.md) is**

$$
\boxed{\partial_t\alpha-\beta^i\partial_i\alpha=-\alpha^2K,\qquad f(\alpha)=-\alpha^2.}
$$

The [shift vector](../../../../../../shift-vector.md) need not vanish; its transport term is essential in a general [3+1 decomposition of spacetime](../../../../../../3-plus-1-decomposition-of-spacetime.md).
