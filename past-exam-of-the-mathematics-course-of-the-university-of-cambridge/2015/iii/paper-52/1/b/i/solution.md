<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In a [stationary spacetime](../../../../../../../stationary-spacetime.md) chart, take $V=\partial_0$. Its squared length is $g_{00}<0$, so $V$ is a [timelike vector](../../../../../../../timelike-vector.md). Since its components are constant, the [Lie derivative of a tensor field](../../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md) obeys $(\mathcal L_Vg)_{\mu\nu}=\partial_0g_{\mu\nu}=0$. For the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md),

$$
(\mathcal L_Vg)_{\mu\nu}=\nabla_\mu V_\nu+\nabla_\nu V_\mu,
$$

so $V$ satisfies the [Killing equation](../../../../../../../killing-equation.md).

Conversely, a timelike [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) is nowhere zero. Apply the [flow-box theorem](../../../../../../../straightening-theorem.md) to choose coordinates with $V=\partial_0$. The [Killing equation](../../../../../../../killing-equation.md) then gives $\partial_0g_{\mu\nu}=0$, and $g_{00}=g(V,V)<0$. Thus **local stationarity is equivalent to the existence of a timelike Killing vector field**.
