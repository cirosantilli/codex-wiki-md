<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the standard meaning of [static spacetime](../../../../../../../static-spacetime.md): a [stationary spacetime](../../../../../../../stationary-spacetime.md) chart whose time-space components also vanish. The printed static definition needs to inherit the timelike requirement $g_{00}<0$; without it, the requested timelike conclusion does not follow.

The preceding argument shows that $V=\partial_0$ is a timelike [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md). Its metric-dual [one-form](../../../../../../../one-form.md) is $v=V_\mu dx^\mu=g_{00}dx^0$. Its [exterior derivative](../../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md) satisfies

$$
v\wedge dv=g_{00}dx^0\wedge (dg_{00}\wedge dx^0)=0.
$$

For a [torsion-free connection](../../../../../../../torsion-free-connection.md), antisymmetrizing the [covariant derivative](../../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) of a [one-form](../../../../../../../one-form.md) gives its [exterior derivative](../../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md). Consequently **the Killing vector has hypersurface orthogonality**:

$$
\boxed{V_{[\alpha}\nabla_\mu V_{\nu]}=0.}
$$

Geometrically, the hypersurfaces $x^0=\mathrm{constant}$ are orthogonal to $V$.
