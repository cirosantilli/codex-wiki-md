<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose local coordinates $y^1,\ldots,y^{D-1}$ on the [hypersurface](../../../../../../hypersurface.md) $\Sigma$, and let $\Phi_s$ be the [local flow](../../../../../../local-flow.md) of the [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) $X$. The map $F(s,y)=\Phi_s(p(y))$ follows the [integral curves of a vector field](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) starting at $p(y)\in\Sigma$. At $s=0$, its differential maps the $s$ direction to $X$ and the remaining directions to a basis of $T_p\Sigma$. Transversality makes these vectors linearly independent, so the [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) makes $F$ a local [manifold chart](../../../../../../manifold-chart.md).

Set $x^0=s$ and transport the $y^i$ unchanged along each [integral curve of a vector field](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md). Then **the adapted coordinates satisfy**

$$
\boxed{X=\frac{\partial}{\partial x^0},\qquad X(x^i)=0.}
$$

The [flow-box theorem](../../../../../../straightening-theorem.md) gives this construction locally. The parameter is the flow parameter, and need not be [arc length](../../../../../../arc-length.md); a global [manifold chart](../../../../../../manifold-chart.md) would require additional hypotheses.
