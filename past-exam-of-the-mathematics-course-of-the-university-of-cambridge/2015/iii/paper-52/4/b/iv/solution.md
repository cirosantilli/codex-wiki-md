<h1 id="4/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Choose a basis of $T_pM$ and use the [affine exponential map](../../../../../../../affine-exponential-map.md) to define [affine normal coordinates](../../../../../../../affine-normal-coordinates.md). Its differential at zero is the identity, so the [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) gives a chart near $p$. By part (iii), radial [geodesics](../../../../../../../geodesic.md) have coordinates $x^\mu(s)=sX^\mu$. Their geodesic equations at $p$ imply $\Gamma^\lambda{}_{\mu\nu}(p)X^\mu X^\nu=0$ for every $X$. The [polarization identity](../../../../../../../polarization-identity.md) therefore gives $\Gamma^\lambda{}_{(\mu\nu)}(p)=0$.

The [disformation tensor](../../../../../../../disformation-tensor.md) $W_{\mu\nu}{}^\lambda$ is symmetric in $\mu,\nu$, whereas $T_{\mu\nu}{}^\lambda$ is antisymmetric. Also $T_\nu{}^\lambda{}_\mu=-T^\lambda{}_{\nu\mu}$. Taking the symmetric part of the corrected [affine connection decomposition](../../../../../../../affine-connection-decomposition.md) consequently gives **the normal-coordinate identity**

$$
\boxed{S^\lambda{}_{\mu\nu}+W_{\mu\nu}{}^\lambda-\frac12T^\lambda{}_{\mu\nu}-\frac12T^\lambda{}_{\nu\mu}=0\quad\text{at }p.}
$$

Thus the final identity in the PDF is correct after the half-factor repair in part (i). In general $\Gamma^\lambda{}_{\mu\nu}(p)=\tfrac12T_{\mu\nu}{}^\lambda(p)$ in the paper's convention, rather than zero. Neither $S$ nor the first derivatives of the [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) need vanish in these [affine normal coordinates](../../../../../../../affine-normal-coordinates.md). With the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md), both torsion and nonmetricity vanish and the usual vanishing of the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) is recovered.
