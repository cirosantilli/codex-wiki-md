The [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) is the unique [affine connection](../../../../../../../affine-connection.md) that has both vanishing [torsion tensor](../../../../../../../torsion-tensor.md) and [metric compatibility](../../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md). The [affine connection decomposition](../../../../../../../affine-connection-decomposition.md) shows exactly how an arbitrary [affine connection](../../../../../../../affine-connection.md) departs from it: the [contorsion tensor](../../../../../../../contorsion-tensor.md) contributes the metric-compatible torsion correction, and the [disformation tensor](../../../../../../../disformation-tensor.md) contributes the nonmetricity correction. The torsion and [nonmetricity tensor](../../../../../../../nonmetricity-tensor.md), together with the [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md), determine the difference uniquely.

In the paper's convention the actual contorsion correction is $-K$, with the corrected half factor from part (i), and the disformation correction is $W$. Both corrections are [tensors](../../../../../../../tensor.md), although the separate connection coefficients $\Gamma$ and $S$ are not [tensors](../../../../../../../tensor.md). **If both torsion and nonmetricity vanish, then** $\boxed{\Gamma=S}$. Vanishing nonmetricity alone permits torsion, and vanishing torsion alone permits nonmetricity.
