A [spacetime](../../../../../../spacetime.md) is a smooth four-dimensional [manifold](../../../../../../topological-manifold.md) equipped with a smooth [Lorentzian metric](../../../../../../lorentzian-metric.md), conventionally of [metric signature](../../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, and a choice of [time orientation](../../../../../../time-orientation.md). The usual manifold assumptions include the [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) and [second-countable space](../../../../../../second-countable-space.md) conditions. A physical model also specifies matter fields and requires the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) and matter equations.

[Diffeomorphism invariance of general relativity](../../../../../../diffeomorphism-invariance-of-general-relativity.md) means that relabelling events by a smooth invertible map, while transforming the [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) and every matter field together, preserves the form of the equations. Passively, a coordinate change gives new components for the same geometric fields. Actively, pulling all fields back by a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) gives another representative of the same physical geometry, subject to any prescribed boundary conditions or boundary symmetries.

**The geometry and matter together carry the physical content; coordinate labels do not.** A [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) need not be an [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md) of a fixed metric, and unrelated metrics are not automatically physically equivalent.
