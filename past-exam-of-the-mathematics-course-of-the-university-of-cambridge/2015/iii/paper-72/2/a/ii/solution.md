<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the same [method of multiple scales](../../../../../../../method-of-multiple-scales.md) and its [solvability condition in the method of multiple scales](../../../../../../../solvability-condition-in-the-method-of-multiple-scales.md). The [position-dependent damping](../../../../../../../position-dependent-damping.md) function is odd, so the lemma that [odd position-dependent damping has zero first-order amplitude drift](../../../../../../../odd-position-dependent-damping-has-zero-first-order-amplitude-drift.md) applies. In its [wave amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) average, $\psi\mapsto\psi+\pi$ changes $\sin(A\cos\psi)$ to its negative while preserving $\sin^2\psi$, so the average is zero. Hence $A'=0$; the [wave phase](../../../../../../../phase-waves.md) average again gives $\theta'=0$.

Equivalently, expand the [position-dependent damping](../../../../../../../position-dependent-damping.md) in its uniformly convergent [power series](../../../../../../../power-series.md) for bounded $A$. Each term $\cos^{2m+1}\psi\sin\psi$ has only even sine [Fourier harmonics](../../../../../../../fourier-harmonic.md), so none resonates with the unit-frequency oscillator. With the initial [wave amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) and [wave phase](../../../../../../../phase-waves.md) unchanged, **the leading uniform approximation is**

$$
\boxed{x(t;\epsilon)=\sqrt2\cos(t-\pi/4)+O(\epsilon)
=\cos t+\sin t+O(\epsilon),\qquad0\leq t\leq T_*/\epsilon.}
$$

There is no first-order slow drift. Effects at the next order accumulate only an $O(\epsilon)$ correction on this time scale.
