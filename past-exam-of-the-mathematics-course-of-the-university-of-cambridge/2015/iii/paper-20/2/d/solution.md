<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $f:X\to Y$ be a surjective [morphism of schemes](../../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) and let $Y'\to Y$ be any [morphism of schemes](../../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md). Take any point $y'\in Y'$ and let $y$ be its image in $Y$. Surjectivity supplies $x\in X$ with $f(x)=y$. By the [point-lifting property of a scheme fibre product](../../../../../../point-lifting-property-of-a-scheme-fibre-product.md) proved in part (c), there is a point of $X\times_YY'$ projecting to both $x$ and $y'$. Thus every point of $Y'$ is hit by the projection.

**This proves that [surjectivity is preserved by base change](../../../../../../surjectivity-is-preserved-by-base-change.md).** No restriction is imposed on the [base change of a morphism of schemes](../../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md). No flatness or finite-type assumption is required.
