↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [morphism of schemes](../../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) is a [morphism of locally ringed spaces](../../../../../../morphism-of-locally-ringed-spaces.md). Thus it consists of a continuous map $f:|X|\to|Y|$ and a homomorphism of [sheaves of rings](../../../../../../sheaf-of-rings.md)

$$
f^\#: \mathcal O_Y\longrightarrow f_*\mathcal O_X
$$

such that, for every $x\in X$, the induced map on [stalks](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md)

$$
f_x^\#: \mathcal O_{Y,f(x)}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{X,x}
$$

is a [local homomorphism](../../../../../../local-homomorphism-of-local-rings.md): it carries the maximal ideal of the source into the maximal ideal of the target. Equivalently, the inverse image of the target maximal ideal is the source maximal ideal. **The locality condition on every [stalk](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) distinguishes a [morphism of schemes](../../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) from a general morphism of ringed spaces.**
