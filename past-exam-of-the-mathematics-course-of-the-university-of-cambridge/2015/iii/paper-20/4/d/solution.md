A [morphism of schemes](../../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) $f:X\to Y$ is a [separated morphism](../../../../../../separated-morphism.md) if its [diagonal morphism](../../../../../../diagonal-morphism.md)

$$
\boxed{\Delta_f:X\longrightarrow X\times_YX}
$$

is a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md). The [diagonal morphism](../../../../../../diagonal-morphism.md) is induced by the pair of identity maps on $X$. Thus separatedness is a condition relative to the given target $Y$, not a requirement that $X$ be absolutely separated.
