↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md) $i:X\to Y$ is a [morphism of schemes](../../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) whose underlying map is a homeomorphism onto a closed subset and whose map of [structure sheaves](../../../../../../structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md)

$$
\mathcal O_Y\longrightarrow i_*\mathcal O_X
$$

is surjective. Equivalently, $X$ is the [closed subscheme](../../../../../../closed-subscheme.md) defined by a quasi-coherent [ideal sheaf of a closed subscheme](../../../../../../ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) on $Y$. The affine-local characterization is particularly useful: for every [affine open subscheme](../../../../../../affine-open-subscheme.md) $V=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq Y$,

$$
\boxed{i^{-1}(V)\simeq\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A}
$$

for an ideal $I\subseteq A$, with the map induced by the quotient homomorphism.
