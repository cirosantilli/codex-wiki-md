<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Over an [affine open subscheme](../../../../../../affine-open-subscheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}A$ of the target, a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md) has the form $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\to\operatorname{Spec}A$. Pulling it back along an [affine scheme](../../../../../../affine-scheme.md) map $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ gives

$$
\operatorname{Spec}((A/I)\otimes_AB)\simeq\operatorname{Spec}(B/IB).
$$

The identification follows directly by tensoring the quotient presentation; it does not require $B$ to be flat over $A$. Since $B\to B/IB$ is surjective, this is again a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md). These affine calculations cover every [base change of a morphism of schemes](../../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md).

**Being a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md) is stable under arbitrary [base change of a morphism of schemes](../../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md).**
