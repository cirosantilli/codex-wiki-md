<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Work over an [affine open subscheme](../../../../../../affine-open-subscheme.md) $V=\operatorname{Spec}A$ of $Z$. Since $g$ is a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md), its inverse image is $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$ for some ideal $I$. Since $f$ is a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md), the inverse image of this [affine scheme](../../../../../../affine-scheme.md) in $X$ is $\operatorname{Spec}((A/I)/J)$ for an ideal $J\subseteq A/I$. Let $\widetilde J$ be the inverse image of $J$ in $A$. The composite on this open is induced by the surjection

$$
A\longrightarrow(A/I)/J\simeq A/\widetilde J.
$$

The affine-local characterization from part (a) therefore shows that **$g\circ f$ is a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md)**. In particular, closed-subscheme ideals compose by taking the inverse image of the second quotient ideal.
