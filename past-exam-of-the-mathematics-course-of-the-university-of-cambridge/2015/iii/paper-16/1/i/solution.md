<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Construct the [tensor product of sheaves](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-sheaves.md) by first forming the [presheaf](../../../../../../presheaf-of-sets-on-a-topological-space.md) $U\mapsto\mathcal F(U)\otimes_{\mathcal O_X(U)}\mathcal G(U)$, with restriction maps induced by those of the two [sheaves of modules](../../../../../../sheaf-of-modules.md), and then applying [sheafification](../../../../../../sheafification.md). The local [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) actions are compatible with restrictions and therefore give the sheaf an $\mathcal O_X$-module structure. Its [stalks](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) are

$$
(\mathcal F\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal G)_x\cong\mathcal F_x\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,x}}\mathcal G_x.
$$

Indeed, a finite collection of [germs of sheaf sections](../../../../../../germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) can be represented on a common neighbourhood, and every finite tensor relation holds on a sufficiently small neighbourhood. Equivalently, this construction represents [bilinear maps](../../../../../../bilinear-map.md) of [sheaves of modules](../../../../../../sheaf-of-modules.md) that are balanced over the [structure sheaf](../../../../../../structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md). Sections of the resulting sheaf need not themselves be tensors of [global sections](../../../../../../global-section.md): that is why the [sheafification](../../../../../../sheafification.md) step matters.
