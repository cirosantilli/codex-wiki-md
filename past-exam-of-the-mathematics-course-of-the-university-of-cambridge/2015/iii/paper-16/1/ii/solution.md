<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [direct image sheaf](../../../../../../direct-image-sheaf.md) is defined on an [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $V\subseteq Y$ by

$$
(\phi_*\mathcal F)(V)=\mathcal F(\phi^{-1}V).
$$

Restrictions are those of $\mathcal F$, and the [sheaf gluing axiom](../../../../../../sheaf-gluing-axiom.md) follows by taking inverse images of an [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md). The [morphism of ringed spaces](../../../../../../morphism-of-ringed-spaces.md) supplies $\phi^\#: \mathcal O_Y\to\phi_*\mathcal O_X$. Thus $a\in\mathcal O_Y(V)$ acts on $s\in\mathcal F(\phi^{-1}V)$ by $\phi^\#(a)s$, making $\phi_*\mathcal F$ a [sheaf of modules](../../../../../../sheaf-of-modules.md) over $\mathcal O_Y$. This definition uses no [quasi-coherence](../../../../../../quasi-coherent-sheaf.md).
