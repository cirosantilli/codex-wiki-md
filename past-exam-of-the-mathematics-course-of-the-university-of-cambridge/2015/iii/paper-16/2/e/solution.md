<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $Y$ be the [affine variety](../../../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md) with [coordinate ring](../../../../../../coordinate-ring.md) $A$. Its principal opens $D(f_i)$ cover $Y$ because $\sum_i g_if_i=1$. On $X_{f_i}$, the [localization of global sections on a principal open](../../../../../../localization-of-global-sections-on-a-principal-open.md) identifies its [coordinate ring](../../../../../../coordinate-ring.md) with $A_{f_i}$, giving an [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md)

$$
\phi_i:X_{f_i}\xrightarrow{\sim}D(f_i)\subseteq Y.
$$

On an overlap the two maps are induced by the same elements of $A$, or equivalently by the same identification with $A_{f_if_j}$, so they agree. Glue them to $\phi:X\to Y$. The inverses agree on the overlaps as well and glue to its inverse. At a [closed point](../../../../../../closed-point.md) $P$, this is the map corresponding to the evaluation [ring homomorphism](../../../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) $A\to k$, $a\mapsto a(P)$. This proves the [cohomological criterion for affineness](../../../../../../cohomological-criterion-for-affineness.md) by an explicit global [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md).
