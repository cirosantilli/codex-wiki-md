<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [quasi-compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) of $X$ gives a finite cover by the affine principal neighbourhoods constructed above. Because at every point some $f_i$ is a unit in the [local ring](../../../../../../local-ring.md), the map of [coherent sheaves](../../../../../../coherent-sheaf.md)

$$
\mathcal O_X^r\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X,\qquad(h_i)\longmapsto\sum_i f_ih_i
$$

is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md). Its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) $\mathcal F$ is a coherent submodule of the trivial bundle, so $H^1(X,\mathcal F)=0$ by part (a). The [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) makes the map on [global sections](../../../../../../global-section.md) surjective. Lifting $1$ supplies

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i=1}^r g_if_i=1,\qquad g_i\in A.}
$$

This is the [unit-ideal certificate from a principal affine cover](../../../../../../unit-ideal-certificate-from-a-principal-affine-cover.md).
