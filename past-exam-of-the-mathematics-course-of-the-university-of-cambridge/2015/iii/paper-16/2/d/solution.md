<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [localization of global sections on a principal open](../../../../../../localization-of-global-sections-on-a-principal-open.md) gives $A_{f_i}=\Gamma(X_{f_i},\mathcal O_X)$. Each of these rings is a [finitely generated algebra](../../../../../../finitely-generated-algebra.md) because $X_{f_i}$ is an [affine variety](../../../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md). Choose finite generators of every $A_{f_i}$ and write them as $a_{ij}/f_i^{e_{ij}}$ with $a_{ij}\in A$. Let $B$ be the $k$-subalgebra of $A$ generated by all $f_i,g_i,a_{ij}$. It is a [finitely generated algebra](../../../../../../finitely-generated-algebra.md), and $B_{f_i}=A_{f_i}$ since these localizations contain the chosen generators and $f_i^{-1}$.

For any integer $N\geq1$, raise $\sum_i g_if_i=1$ to the power $r(N-1)+1$. Every resulting monomial contains some $f_i^N$, so it gives a [unit-ideal identity for powers](../../../../../../unit-ideal-identity-for-powers.md)

$$
1=\sum_i c_if_i^N,\qquad c_i\in B.
$$

For an arbitrary $a\in A$, equality $A_{f_i}=B_{f_i}$ implies $f_i^{N_i}a\in B$ for some $N_i$: multiply by an additional power if equality of localized fractions requires it. Choose a common $N$ and the identity above. Then $a=\sum_i c_i(f_i^Na)\in B$. Consequently $A=B$, so $A$ is a [finitely generated algebra](../../../../../../finitely-generated-algebra.md). It is a [reduced ring](../../../../../../reduced-ring.md), since a [nilpotent element](../../../../../../nilpotent.md) global [regular function](../../../../../../regular-function.md) has zero germ everywhere on the reduced [variety](../../../../../../algebraic-variety.md) $X$. This is [finite generation from finitely many principal localizations](../../../../../../finite-generation-from-finitely-many-principal-localizations.md).
