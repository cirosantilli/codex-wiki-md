<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\mathcal I_Y$ be the [coherent ideal sheaf](../../../../../../coherent-ideal-sheaf.md) of $Y$, and let $\mathfrak m_P$ be the [ideal sheaf of a closed point](../../../../../../ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-point.md). Because $P\notin Y$, the [stalk](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) $(\mathcal I_Y)_P$ is $\mathcal O_{X,P}$. Evaluation at $P$ therefore gives a [surjective morphism of sheaves](../../../../../../surjective-morphism-of-sheaves.md) $\mathcal I_Y\to k_P$ to the [skyscraper sheaf](../../../../../../skyscraper-sheaf.md) at $P$. Its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) $\mathcal J=\mathcal I_Y\cap\mathfrak m_P$ is again a [coherent ideal sheaf](../../../../../../coherent-ideal-sheaf.md). From

$$
0\to\mathcal J\to\mathcal I_Y\to k_P\to0
$$

and $H^1(X,\mathcal J)=0$, the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) shows that $\Gamma(X,\mathcal I_Y)\to k$ is onto. Choose $f$ mapping to $1$. It vanishes on $Y$ and satisfies $f(P)=1$. Hence $X_f\subseteq U$, and inside the [affine variety](../../../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md) $U$ it is the [principal open subset](../../../../../../principal-open-subscheme.md) defined by $f|_U$. A principal open of an [affine variety](../../../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md) is affine. This gives [affine principal neighbourhoods from ideal-sheaf vanishing](../../../../../../affine-principal-neighbourhoods-from-ideal-sheaf-vanishing.md).
