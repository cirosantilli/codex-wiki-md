<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

On an [affine chart](../../../../../affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq V$, the [module of Kähler differentials](../../../../../kahler-differential.md) $\Omega_{A/k}$ is generated by symbols $da$ subject to $k$-linearity and $d(ab)=a\,db+b\,da$. It represents $k$-[derivations](../../../../../derivation-of-an-algebra.md). These [modules](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) commute with [localization](../../../../../localization-of-a-ring.md), so their associated [quasi-coherent sheaves](../../../../../quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) glue to the [Kähler differential sheaf](../../../../../sheaf-of-kahler-differentials-over-a-field.md) $\Omega^1_{V/k}$. It is coherent: if $A=k[x_1,\dots,x_N]/I$ and $I=(f_1,\dots,f_s)$, then it has the [finite presentation of a module](../../../../../finite-presentation-of-a-module.md)

$$
A^s\xrightarrow{(\partial f_j/\partial x_i)}A^N\longrightarrow\Omega_{A/k}\longrightarrow0.
$$

For a [closed point](../../../../../closed-point.md) $P$, put $R=\mathcal O_{V,P}$ with [maximal ideal](../../../../../maximal-ideal.md) $\mathfrak m$ and residue field $k$. The [Zariski tangent space](../../../../../zariski-tangent-space.md) is $\operatorname{Hom}_k(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2,k)$, equivalently $\operatorname{Der}_k(R,k)$. A derivation kills constants and $\mathfrak m^2$, so it factors through $a\mapsto a-a(P)$; conversely every [linear functional](../../../../../linear-functional.md) on $\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$ defines such a derivation by the product rule. The [universal property of Kähler differentials](../../../../../universal-property-of-kahler-differentials.md) therefore identifies this space with

$$
\boxed{T_{V,P}=\operatorname{Hom}_k(\Omega^1_{V,P}/\mathfrak m_P\Omega^1_{V,P},k).}
$$

In particular, $\Omega^1_{V,P}/\mathfrak m_P\Omega^1_{V,P}\cong\mathfrak m_P/\mathfrak m_P^2$, the [algebraic cotangent space](../../../../../algebraic-cotangent-space.md).

A point is a [smooth point of a variety](../../../../../smooth-point-of-a-variety.md) when its [local ring](../../../../../local-ring.md) is a [regular local ring](../../../../../regular-local-ring.md); over this algebraically closed field this says $\dim_kT_{V,P}=n=\dim V$. Tensor the [finite presentation of a module](../../../../../finite-presentation-of-a-module.md) above with $k(P)$. The tangent space is the [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) $J(P)$, hence has dimension $N-\operatorname{rank}J(P)$. This proves the [Jacobian criterion](../../../../../jacobian-criterion.md)

$$
\boxed{P\text{ smooth}\iff\operatorname{rank}J(P)=N-n.}
$$

For a smooth [irreducible variety](../../../../../irreducible-variety.md) $V$, choose at each point an invertible $(N-n)$-minor and shrink the [affine chart](../../../../../affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) so that it remains invertible. Its relations eliminate $N-n$ of the generators of $\Omega_{A/k}$, yielding a surjection $A^n\to\Omega_{A/k}$. At the [generic point](../../../../../generic-point.md), its target has dimension $n$: over a perfect ground field, a separating transcendence basis of the [function field](../../../../../function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) has differentials forming a basis. Equivalently this follows from the assumed [density of the smooth locus](../../../../../density-of-the-smooth-locus.md). The [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) therefore becomes zero over the [fraction field](../../../../../field-of-fractions.md) of the [integral domain](../../../../../integral-domain.md) $A$. As a submodule of $A^n$, it is a [torsion-free module](../../../../../torsion-free-module.md), so it is already zero. Thus these maps give local [isomorphisms](../../../../../isomorphism.md) with $\mathcal O_V^n$, proving [local freeness of differentials on a smooth variety](../../../../../local-freeness-of-differentials-on-a-smooth-variety.md) with rank $n$.

For an [affine chart](../../../../../affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq V$, write $W\cap\operatorname{Spec}A=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$ and $\mathcal M=\widetilde M$. The [restriction of a module sheaf to a closed subvariety](../../../../../restriction-of-a-module-sheaf-to-a-closed-subvariety.md) is $\widetilde{M\otimes_AA/I}=\widetilde{M/IM}$. The [Conormal exact sequence for Kähler differentials](../../../../../conormal-exact-sequence-for-kahler-differentials.md) is

$$
I/I^2\xrightarrow{\bar f\mapsto df\otimes1}\Omega_{A/k}\otimes_AA/I\longrightarrow\Omega_{(A/I)/k}\longrightarrow0.
$$

It follows from the generators and relations: passing to $A/I$ imposes precisely the additional relations $df=0$ for $f\in I$. The first map is well defined because $d(I^2)$ lies in $I\Omega_{A/k}$. Glue these exact [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) sequences, using [exactness of localization](../../../../../exactness-of-localization.md), to obtain

$$
\mathcal I_W/\mathcal I_W^2\longrightarrow\Omega_V^1|_W\longrightarrow\Omega_W^1\longrightarrow0.
$$

In the final assertion, interpret [locally principal subvariety](../../../../../locally-principal-subvariety.md) as a proper local hypersurface. Its ideal on each chart is $(f)$ with $f\ne0$. Since $V$ is an [irreducible variety](../../../../../irreducible-variety.md) and reduced, $f$ is a [non-zero-divisor](../../../../../non-zero-divisor.md), and $A/(f)\to(f)/(f^2)$, $\bar a\mapsto af$, is an [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md). These local rank-one descriptions make the [conormal sheaf](../../../../../conormal-sheaf.md) $\mathcal I_W/\mathcal I_W^2$ invertible.

Because $W$ is not contained in the singular locus of $V$, there is a dense open subset of $W$ where both $V$ and $W$ are [smooth varieties](../../../../../smooth-algebraic-variety.md). At a [closed point](../../../../../closed-point.md) there, $\dim W=\dim V-1$ by the [Krull principal ideal theorem](../../../../../krull-principal-ideal-theorem.md). The tangent description $T_{W,P}=\ker(df:T_{V,P}\to k)$ then forces $df\ne0$. Hence the conormal map is injective at the [generic point](../../../../../generic-point.md) of $W$. Its [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is a subsheaf of a [line bundle](../../../../../line-bundle.md) on the integral [variety](../../../../../algebraic-variety.md) $W$, so it is a [torsion-free sheaf](../../../../../torsion-free-sheaf.md); a [torsion-free sheaf](../../../../../torsion-free-sheaf.md) with zero generic fibre is zero. This proves [conormal injectivity for a generically smooth Cartier divisor](../../../../../conormal-injectivity-for-a-generically-smooth-cartier-divisor.md). If zero equations were allowed in the phrase locally principal, $W=V$ would be a counterexample to invertibility; the proper-hypersurface convention is essential.
