<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**The filtration and its meaning.** Let $L/K$ be a finite [Galois extension](../../../../../finite-galois-extension.md) of non-Archimedean [local fields](../../../../../local-field.md), with group $G$, normalized [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) $v_L$, and [residue fields](../../../../../residue-field.md) $k_L/k_K$ of characteristic $\ell$. The [lower ramification numbering](../../../../../lower-ramification-numbering.md) is

$$
G_{-1}=G,\qquad
G_j=\{\sigma\in G:v_L(\sigma a-a)\geq j+1
\text{ for every }a\in\mathcal O_L\}\quad(j\geq0).
$$

The [higher ramification groups](../../../../../ramification-group.md) are normal, decrease with $j$, and eventually become trivial: for each nonidentity automorphism some integral element is moved by a nonzero amount. The first group $G_0$ is the [inertia group](../../../../../inertia-group.md), with

$$
G/G_0\cong\operatorname{Gal}(k_L/k_K).
$$

Its fixed field is the maximal [unramified extension](../../../../../unramified-extension.md) inside $L$. The [wild inertia group](../../../../../wild-inertia-group.md) is $G_1$. For a [uniformizer](../../../../../uniformizer.md) $\pi_L$, there are injective homomorphisms

$$
G_0/G_1\hookrightarrow k_L^\times,\quad
\sigma\longmapsto\overline{\sigma(\pi_L)/\pi_L},
\qquad
G_j/G_{j+1}\hookrightarrow(k_L,+),\quad
\sigma\longmapsto\overline{\frac{\sigma(\pi_L)-\pi_L}{\pi_L^{j+1}}}
\quad(j\geq1).
$$

The kernels are the indicated next groups; the additive homomorphism assertion follows by expanding a product of automorphisms modulo $\pi_L^{j+2}$. These maps show that $G_0/G_1$ is cyclic of order prime to $\ell$, while $G_1$ is an $\ell$-group. Thus [tame ramification](../../../../../tamely-ramified-extension.md) is exactly the case $G_1=1$.

To calculate the groups, the [uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups](../../../../../uniformizer-criterion-for-lower-ramification-groups.md) says

$$
\boxed{G_j=\{\sigma\in G_0:v_L(\sigma\pi_L-\pi_L)\geq j+1\}.}
$$

Indeed, $\mathcal O_L$ is generated by $\pi_L$ over the integers of the maximal unramified subextension, which $G_0$ fixes. Differences of powers of $\pi_L$ are divisible by $\sigma\pi_L-\pi_L$, so the one test implies all the defining inequalities.

**Eisenstein facts used in the calculations.** For a complete [discrete valuation ring](../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md) $A$ with [uniformizer](../../../../../uniformizer.md) $\varpi$, a monic polynomial

$$
f(T)=T^d+a_{d-1}T^{d-1}+\cdots+a_0
$$

is an [Eisenstein polynomial](../../../../../eisenstein-polynomial.md) if $\varpi\mid a_j$ for every $j<d$ and $\varpi^2\nmid a_0$. The [Eisenstein criterion](../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) makes it irreducible. If $\theta$ is a root, its [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) relative to the base normalization is $1/d$, the extension has degree and [ramification index](../../../../../ramification-index.md) $d$, its [residue field](../../../../../residue-field.md) is unchanged, and

$$
\boxed{\mathcal O_{K(\theta)}=A[\theta],\qquad
\theta\text{ is a uniformizer}.}
$$

The root [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) follows by comparing the terms of its equation. The [ramification index](../../../../../ramification-index.md) must then be at least $d$, hence exactly $d$. In the basis $1,\theta,\ldots,\theta^{d-1}$, the [valuations](../../../../../valuation.md) of $b_j\theta^j$ are distinct modulo $d$, so an integral linear combination has every $b_j\in A$. This proves the integer-ring assertion. Conversely, a [uniformizer](../../../../../uniformizer.md) in a [totally ramified extension](../../../../../totally-ramified-extension.md) generates the field, and its minimal polynomial is [Eisenstein](../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md); this follows from its value $1/d$, equal [valuations](../../../../../valuation.md) of its conjugates, and the [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) one of its norm.

For a monogenic separable integer ring $A[\theta]$, the [different ideal](../../../../../different-ideal.md) is generated by $f'(\theta)$. For a [Galois extension](../../../../../finite-galois-extension.md), the [different exponent from ramification groups](../../../../../different-exponent-from-ramification-groups.md) is

$$
\boxed{v_L(\mathfrak D_{L/K})=\sum_{j\geq0}(|G_j|-1).}
$$

In the totally ramified case these agree directly: $f'(\pi_L)=\prod_{\sigma\ne1}(\pi_L-\sigma\pi_L)$, and an automorphism with displacement [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) $h$ contributes once to each of $G_0,\ldots,G_{h-1}$.

**Upper numbering.** Extend the lower indexing to real $s\geq0$ by $G_s=G_{\lceil s\rceil}$, and set

$$
\varphi_{L/K}(s)=\int_0^s\frac{dt}{[G_0:G_t]},\qquad
\psi_{L/K}=\varphi_{L/K}^{-1},\qquad
G^u=G_{\psi_{L/K}(u)}.
$$

On $[-1,0]$ take $\varphi(s)=s$. This [Herbrand function](../../../../../herbrand-function.md) slows the indexing as the groups shrink. Lower numbering is compatible with subgroups, while [upper ramification numbering](../../../../../upper-ramification-numbering.md) is compatible with quotients: for normal $H$, $(G/H)^u=G^uH/H$. This is the fact that [upper ramification groups commute with quotients](../../../../../upper-ramification-groups-commute-with-quotients.md). Thus upper breaks are particularly useful when comparing intermediate [Galois extensions](../../../../../finite-galois-extension.md).

**The eighth-root cyclotomic extension.** Put $\zeta=\zeta_8$ and $\pi=\zeta-1$. The shifted cyclotomic polynomial

$$
(1+T)^4+1=T^4+4T^3+6T^2+4T+2
$$

is [Eisenstein](../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at two. Hence $L=\mathbb Q_2(\zeta)$ is totally ramified of degree four, $v_L(2)=4$, and $\pi$ is a [uniformizer](../../../../../uniformizer.md). Its [Galois group](../../../../../galois-group.md) consists of $\sigma_a(\zeta)=\zeta^a$ for $a=1,3,5,7$, and is $C_2\times C_2$.

Since $\zeta$ is a unit,

$$
v_L(\sigma_a\pi-\pi)=v_L(\zeta^{a-1}-1).
$$

For $a=3,7$, this is the [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) of $i-1$ or $-i-1$, namely two, because their squares are units times two. For $a=5$, it is $v_L(-2)=4$. The [ramification groups of the eighth-root cyclotomic extension of the 2-adic field](../../../../../ramification-groups-of-the-eighth-root-cyclotomic-extension-of-the-2-adic-field.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{
G_{-1}=G_0=G_1=C_2\times C_2,\qquad
G_2=G_3=\{1,\sigma_5\}\cong C_2,\qquad
G_j=1\ (j\geq4).}
$$

The lower breaks are one and three, and

$$
\varphi(s)=
\begin{cases}
s,&0\leq s\leq1,\\
1+(s-1)/2,&1\leq s\leq3,\\
2+(s-3)/4,&s\geq3.
\end{cases}
$$

Thus the upper breaks are one and two:

$$
\boxed{
G^u=
\begin{cases}
C_2\times C_2,&-1\leq u\leq1,\\
\{1,\sigma_5\},&1<u\leq2,\\
1,&u>2.
\end{cases}}
$$

The [different exponent from ramification groups](../../../../../different-exponent-from-ramification-groups.md) is $3+3+1+1=8$, agreeing with $v_L(4\zeta^3)=8$ from the derivative of $T^4+1$.

**The cubic splitting field at three.** Let $\alpha^3=2$, $\zeta=\zeta_3$, and put

$$
\beta=\alpha+1,\qquad\lambda=\zeta-1.
$$

The polynomials

$$
\beta^3-3\beta^2+3\beta-3=0,\qquad
\lambda^2+3\lambda+3=0
$$

are [Eisenstein](../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) at three. They give totally ramified subextensions of degrees three and two. Their intersection is the base field, so $L=\mathbb Q_3(\alpha,\zeta)$ has degree six. Its [ramification index](../../../../../ramification-index.md) is divisible by both three and two, hence equals six; therefore $L/\mathbb Q_3$ is totally ramified. It is the splitting field of $T^3-2$, with [Galois group](../../../../../galois-group.md) $S_3$.

Normalize $v_L$ so that $v_L(3)=6$. Then $v_L(\beta)=2$ and $v_L(\lambda)=3$, making

$$
\boxed{\pi=\frac{\lambda}{\beta}}
$$

a [uniformizer](../../../../../uniformizer.md). Let $\sigma(\alpha)=\zeta\alpha,\ \sigma(\zeta)=\zeta$ and $\tau(\alpha)=\alpha,\ \tau(\zeta)=\zeta^2$. They generate $S_3$. Since $\tau(\lambda)=(\zeta+1)\lambda$,

$$
\tau(\pi)-\pi=\zeta\pi,\qquad v_L(\tau(\pi)-\pi)=1.
$$

For the order-three automorphism,

$$
\sigma(\pi)-\pi
=-\frac{\alpha\lambda^2}{(\alpha+1)(\zeta\alpha+1)},
\qquad v_L(\sigma(\pi)-\pi)=6-2-2=2.
$$

Here $\alpha$ is a unit and $v_L(\zeta\alpha+1)=v_L(\sigma\beta)=2$. The same calculation gives [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) two for $\sigma^2$. The three transpositions are conjugate and the defining filtration is normal, so they all have displacement [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) one. The [ramification groups of the splitting field of T3 minus 2 over Q3](../../../../../ramification-groups-of-the-splitting-field-of-t3-minus-2-over-q3.md) are

$$
\boxed{G_{-1}=G_0=S_3,\qquad
G_1=\langle\sigma\rangle\cong C_3,\qquad
G_j=1\ (j\geq2).}
$$

The lower breaks are zero and one. Since $[G_0:G_s]=2$ for $0<s\leq1$,

$$
\varphi(s)=
\begin{cases}s/2,&0\leq s\leq1,\\
1/2+(s-1)/6,&s\geq1,
\end{cases}
$$

and the upper groups are

$$
\boxed{
G^u=
\begin{cases}
S_3,&-1\leq u\leq0,\\
C_3,&0<u\leq1/2,\\
1,&u>1/2.
\end{cases}}
$$

The [different exponent from ramification groups](../../../../../different-exponent-from-ramification-groups.md) is $5+2=7$. As an independent check, the cubic subfield has different exponent $v_{\mathbb Q_3(\alpha)}(3\alpha^2)=3$, and the [quadratic extension](../../../../../quadratic-extension.md) above it is tame with different exponent one. Transitivity of the [different ideal](../../../../../different-ideal.md) gives $1+2\cdot3=7$, as required.
