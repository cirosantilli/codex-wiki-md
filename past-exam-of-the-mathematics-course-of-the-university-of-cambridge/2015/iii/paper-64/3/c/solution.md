<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

When the imposed [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) vanishes, the dynamical [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) becomes

$$
\sigma^2\left[\sigma^2+(N^2+\Omega^2)\right]=0.
$$

The nonstationary hydrodynamic branch therefore has $\sigma^2=-(N^2+\Omega^2)$. **The [radial Solberg–Høiland instability criterion](../../../../../../radial-solberg-hoiland-instability-criterion.md)** in this local model is

$$
\boxed{N^2+\Omega^2<0.}
$$

The [radial buoyancy frequency](../../../../../../radial-buoyancy-frequency.md) supplies either restoration or a driving force, while the [radial epicyclic frequency](../../../../../../radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) $\kappa=\Omega$ supplies rotational restoration. Zero sum is marginal, and positive sum gives stable oscillations. The neutral roots do not themselves signify exponential growth.

Because both equilibrium [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) and dimensionless [specific entropy](../../../../../../specific-entropy.md) decrease outward, their radial gradients have the same sign. The leading minus sign in the expression for $N^2$ therefore makes $N^2<0$: the radial stratification is adverse. For smooth profiles varying over [radius](../../../../../../radius.md) $r$, $|\partial_rP|\sim P/r$ and $|\partial_rS|\sim1/r$, so

$$
N^2\sim-\frac{P}{\rho r^2}\sim-\frac{c_s^2}{r^2}\sim-\left(\frac Hr\right)^2\Omega^2.
$$

For a [thin disk](../../../../../../thin-disk.md), the magnitude of this negative [radial buoyancy frequency](../../../../../../radial-buoyancy-frequency.md) squared is much less than $\Omega^2$. **Rotation stabilizes the hydrodynamic mode despite the adverse entropy gradient.** This estimate assumes gradients on the global radial scale; a sharp thermal feature may be different. It addresses the ideal nondiffusive equations here. Processes such as [convective overstability](../../../../../../convective-overstability.md) require additional thermal relaxation and are not ruled out by this particular criterion.
