<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the [accretion rate](../../../../../../accretion-rate.md) $\dot M>0$ to mean inward flow. In a [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state.md), [conservation of mass](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) makes the inward [mass flux](../../../../../../mass-flux.md) independent of [radius](../../../../../../radius.md), so $\mathcal F=\dot M$. Integrating its radial expression gives

$$
r^{1/2}\bar\nu\Sigma=\frac{\dot M}{3\pi}r^{1/2}+C.
$$

For [Keplerian rotation](../../../../../../keplerian-disk.md), $\Omega=(GM_*/r^3)^{1/2}$ and the [viscous torque in an accretion disk](../../../../../../viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) is $\mathcal G=3\pi\bar\nu\Sigma\sqrt{GM_*r}$. The [zero-torque inner boundary condition](../../../../../../zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) therefore sets $\bar\nu\Sigma=0$ at $r_*$. It fixes $C=-\dot M\sqrt{r_*}/(3\pi)$, giving **the steady density profile**

$$
\boxed{\Sigma(r)=\frac{\dot M}{3\pi\bar\nu(r)}\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{r_*}{r}}\right).}
$$

This is the [Keplerian accretion disk](../../../../../../keplerian-accretion-disk.md) solution on $r_*<r<r_{\rm out}$. It determines the [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md)–[surface density](../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) product; a separate viscosity closure is needed to turn it into an explicit power law. If the [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) is finite and nonzero at the inner edge, the [surface density](../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) tends to zero there.
