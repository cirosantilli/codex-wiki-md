<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [standard thin-disk dissipation flux](../../../../../../standard-thin-disk-dissipation-flux.md), summed over both faces, follows by substituting the [Keplerian accretion disk](../../../../../../keplerian-accretion-disk.md) profile into the viscous heating rate:

$$
\mathcal H=\frac94\bar\nu\Sigma\Omega^2=\frac{3GM_*\dot M}{4\pi r^3}\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{r_*}{r}}\right).
$$

The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives $\mathcal C=2\sigma T^4$ because there are two emitting faces. Local [radiative equilibrium](../../../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md) consequently gives **the [effective-temperature profile of a zero-torque disk](../../../../../../effective-temperature-profile-of-a-zero-torque-disk.md)**

$$
\boxed{T(r)=T_{\rm in}\left(\frac{r_*}{r}\right)^{3/4}\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{r_*}{r}}\right)^{1/4},\qquad T_{\rm in}^4=\frac{3GM_*\dot M}{8\pi\sigma r_*^3}.}
$$

Here $\sigma$ is the [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-constant.md). The scale $T_{\rm in}$ is not the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) exactly at the inner boundary: the [zero-torque inner boundary condition](../../../../../../zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) makes that formal [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) zero. Maximizing $T^4$ shows that the [maximum effective temperature of a zero-torque disk](../../../../../../maximum-effective-temperature-of-a-zero-torque-disk.md) occurs at $r=49r_*/36$, with $T_{\max}\simeq0.488T_{\rm in}$.

At equal central mass and [accretion rate](../../../../../../accretion-rate.md), characteristic [effective temperatures](../../../../../../effective-temperature.md) scale as $r_*^{-3/4}$. Thus, comparing corresponding values of $r/r_*$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_{\rm NS}}{T_{\rm WD}}\simeq\left(\frac{10^4\,\mathrm{km}}{10\,\mathrm{km}}\right)^{3/4}=10^{9/4}\simeq178.}
$$

**The neutron-star disk is about 180 times hotter.** The [Planck law](../../../../../../planck-s-law.md) and [Wien displacement law](../../../../../../wien-s-displacement-law.md) move its characteristic emission to about 180 times higher [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md), or 180 times shorter [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md). A [white dwarf](../../../../../../white-dwarf.md) disk commonly emits in optical and ultraviolet bands, while the hotter [neutron star](../../../../../../neutron-star.md) disk can emit in X-rays. Absolute bands require an actual [accretion rate](../../../../../../accretion-rate.md); the relative shift follows directly from the stated scaling.
