**Allowed via a charged-current weak decay.** The quark emits a virtual $W^-$ in $b\to uW^{-*}$, followed by $W^{-*}\to e^-\bar\nu_e$. The relevant terms are the quark and lepton [weak charged currents](../../../../../../../charged-current.md), with amplitude proportional to the [CKM matrix](../../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) element $V_{ub}$. At low energy their product gives a [four-fermion interaction](../../../../../../../four-fermion-interaction.md) of the form $(\bar u_L\gamma^\mu b_L)(\bar e_L\gamma_\mu\nu_{eL})$ plus its conjugate.
