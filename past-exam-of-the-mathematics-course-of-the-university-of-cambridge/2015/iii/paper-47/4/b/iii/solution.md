<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**The coupling is allowed, with an on-shell threshold condition.** The [Higgs field](../../../../../../../higgs-field.md) kinetic term produces

$$
\mathcal L\supset\frac{2m_W^2}{v}hW_\mu^+W^{-\mu},
$$

and hence a tree-level [Feynman vertex](../../../../../../../interaction-vertex.md). A decay into two real [W bosons](../../../../../../../w-boson.md) requires $m_h>2m_W$ for nonzero phase space. Below that threshold the vertex still mediates $h\to WW^*$ or decays through two virtual bosons into fermions. Thus an allowed Lagrangian coupling does not by itself guarantee an on-shell two-body decay.
