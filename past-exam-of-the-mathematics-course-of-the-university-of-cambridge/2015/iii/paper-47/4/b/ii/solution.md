**Allowed through second-order weak interactions, not a tree-level neutral-current vertex.** This is the flavour-changing transition responsible for [neutral kaon mixing](../../../../../../../neutral-kaon-mixing.md), with change of strangeness by two. Box [Feynman diagrams](../../../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) contain two [W bosons](../../../../../../../w-boson.md) and internal up-type quarks. Their vertices come from the [weak charged current](../../../../../../../charged-current.md) and contain [CKM matrix](../../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) factors. At low energy they generate an operator $(\bar s_L\gamma_\mu d_L)(\bar s_L\gamma^\mu d_L)$ plus its conjugate. The [GIM mechanism](../../../../../../../gim-mechanism.md) cancels the flavour-independent part of the internal-quark sum; unequal quark masses leave a nonzero loop amplitude.
