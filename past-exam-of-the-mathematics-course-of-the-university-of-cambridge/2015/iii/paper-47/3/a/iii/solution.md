↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

There are **two diagrams**: $t$-channel [Z boson](../../../../../../../z-boson.md) exchange and $s$-channel [W boson](../../../../../../../w-boson.md) exchange. The incoming [Electron](../../../../../../../electron.md) and [Electron](../../../../../../../electron.md)-antineutrino can annihilate into $W^-$, which then produces the outgoing pair. The charged-current propagator carries momentum $k+p$, hence invariant $s$. The solid-line arrows in the diagram indicate fermion-number flow; those on antineutrino legs run opposite their physical motion.
