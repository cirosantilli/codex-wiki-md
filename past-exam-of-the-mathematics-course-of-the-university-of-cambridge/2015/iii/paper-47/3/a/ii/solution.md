↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

There are **two diagrams**: the $t$-channel [Z boson](../../../../../../../z-boson.md) exchange from the [weak neutral current](../../../../../../../neutral-current.md), and a crossed $u$-channel [W boson](../../../../../../../w-boson.md) exchange from the [weak charged current](../../../../../../../charged-current.md). The charged-current vertices pair the incoming [neutrino](../../../../../../../neutrino.md) $k$ with the outgoing [Electron](../../../../../../../electron.md) $p'$ and the incoming [Electron](../../../../../../../electron.md) $p$ with the outgoing [neutrino](../../../../../../../neutrino.md) $k'$. The exchanged momentum has square $u=(k-p')^2$. With charge flow from the first vertex to the second, the exchanged boson is $W^+$. The corresponding panels show both topologies; no photon or Higgs diagram survives the stated [neutrino](../../../../../../../neutrino.md) assumptions.
