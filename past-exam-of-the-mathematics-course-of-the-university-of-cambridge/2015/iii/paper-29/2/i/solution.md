↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [càdlàg martingale](../../../../../../cadlag-martingale.md) is an [adapted process](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) $X$ with [càdlàg](../../../../../../cadlag.md) paths, integrable $X_t$ at each finite time, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=X_s\quad(0\leq s\leq t).}
$$

The [càdlàg](../../../../../../cadlag.md) requirement means that the chosen path version is right-continuous and has a finite left limit at every positive time. With a complete [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md), it suffices that this holds on one common event of probability one, since the process can be redefined on the exceptional [null set](../../../../../../null-set.md).

The [usual hypotheses for a filtration](../../../../../../usual-conditions-for-a-filtration.md) are **completeness and right continuity**. Completeness means that $\mathcal F_0$ contains every subset of every null event of the ambient [probability space](../../../../../../probability-space.md); right continuity means

$$
\boxed{\mathcal F_t=\bigcap_{u>t}\mathcal F_u.}
$$
