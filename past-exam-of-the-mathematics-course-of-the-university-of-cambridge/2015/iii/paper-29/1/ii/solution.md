<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) has [uniform integrability](../../../../../../uniform-integrability.md) when its entire family of time values has uniformly small integrable tails:

$$
\boxed{\lim_{K\to\infty}\sup_{n\geq0}\mathbb E\bigl[|X_n|\mathbf1_{\{|X_n|>K\}}\bigr]=0.}
$$

Here every $X_n$ is an [integrable random variable](../../../../../../integrable-random-variable.md). This implies $\sup_n\mathbb E|X_n|<\infty$, because the contribution from $|X_n|\leq K$ is at most $K$. Mere boundedness of these [expectations](../../../../../../expected-value.md) does not imply [uniform integrability](../../../../../../uniform-integrability.md).
