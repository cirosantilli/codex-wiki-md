<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For every [bounded sequence](../../../../../../bounded-sequence.md) $x$, $x-Sx\in Y$ and $L|_Y=0$. Linearity gives **$\boxed{L(x)=L(Sx)}$**, as required. Thus the same $L$ satisfies all three conditions and is a [Banach limit](../../../../../../banach-limit.md).

The usual positivity condition for a [Banach limit](../../../../../../banach-limit.md) follows automatically here. If $x_n\geq0$ and $a=\|x\|_\infty>0$, then $\|\mathbf1-x/a\|_\infty\leq1$. Since $\|L\|=L(\mathbf1)=1$, we have $|1-L(x)/a|\leq1$, whence $L(x)\geq0$. The case $x=0$ is immediate. This explains why the constructed [bounded linear functional](../../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) is also a [positive linear functional](../../../../../../positive-linear-functional.md) extending the ordinary limit while remaining invariant under the [left shift on bounded sequences](../../../../../../left-shift-on-bounded-sequences.md).
