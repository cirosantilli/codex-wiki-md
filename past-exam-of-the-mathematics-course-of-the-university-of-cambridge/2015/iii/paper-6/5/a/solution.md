<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $y=x-Sx\in Y$, the telescoping arithmetic means satisfy

$$
\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^Ny_n=\frac{x_1-x_{N+1}}N\longrightarrow0,
$$

because $x$ is a [bounded sequence](../../../../../../bounded-sequence.md). Hence $\|\mathbf1-y\|_\infty\geq|1-N^{-1}\sum_{n=1}^Ny_n|$ for every $N$, and passage to the limit gives $\|\mathbf1-y\|_\infty\geq1$. Taking $y=0$ shows $d(\mathbf1,Y)=1$. The proved [Hahn-Banach distance formula](../../../../../../hahn-banach-distance-formula.md) therefore yields a [bounded linear functional](../../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) $L$ on the real [l-infinity sequence space](../../../../../../l-infinity-sequence-space.md) with

$$
\boxed{\|L\|=1,\qquad L(\mathbf1)=1,\qquad L|_Y=0.}
$$

We verify that this single [linear functional](../../../../../../linear-functional.md) also has the two remaining properties.
