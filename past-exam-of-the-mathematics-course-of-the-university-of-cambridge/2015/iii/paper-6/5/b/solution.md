<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every [finitely supported sequence](../../../../../../finitely-supported-sequence.md) $v$ lies in $Y$: if $v_n=0$ for $n>N$, set $x_n=\sum_{k=n}^Nv_k$ for $n\leq N$ and $x_n=0$ afterwards. Then $x$ is a [bounded sequence](../../../../../../bounded-sequence.md) and $x-Sx=v$. Consequently $L(v)=0$. The [finitely supported sequences](../../../../../../finitely-supported-sequence.md) are dense in the [space of sequences converging to zero](../../../../../../space-of-sequences-converging-to-zero.md) $c_0$ for the [supremum norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md), by truncation, so continuity of the [bounded linear functional](../../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) $L$ gives $L|_{c_0}=0$. For $x$ in the real [convergent sequence space](../../../../../../convergent-sequence-space.md), with ordinary limit $\ell$, we have $x-\ell\mathbf1\in c_0$. Therefore **$\boxed{L(x)=\ell L(\mathbf1)=\lim_{n\to\infty}x_n}$**.
