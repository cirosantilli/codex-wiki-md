<h1 id="7/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Integrate the type-A first-event density from zero to $t$. **The type-A cumulative incidence is**

$$
\boxed{F_A(t)=\Pr(T\leq t,J=A)
=\int_0^t\alpha e^{-(\alpha+\beta)u}du
=\frac\alpha{\alpha+\beta}(1-e^{-(\alpha+\beta)t}).}
$$

This is the [cumulative incidence function](../../../../../../cumulative-incidence-function.md) in the [competing risks model](../../../../../../competing-risks-model.md), distinct from the hypothetical net risk $1-e^{-\alpha t}$ obtained by removing B.
