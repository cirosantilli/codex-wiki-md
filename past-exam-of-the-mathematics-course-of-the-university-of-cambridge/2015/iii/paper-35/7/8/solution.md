↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

**The observed death fraction is**

$$
\boxed{\widehat\pi=\frac{100}{150}=\frac23.}
$$

In the [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) two-exponential [competing risks model](../../../../../../competing-risks-model.md), $\pi=\lambda/(\lambda+\gamma)$. Thus the observed fraction estimates the share of the combined rate attributable to death. Combining it with an estimate of $s=\lambda+\gamma$ yields $\widehat\lambda=\widehat\pi\widehat s$ and $\widehat\gamma=(1-\widehat\pi)\widehat s$. This uses the event-type information that the death-only time analysis ignored.
