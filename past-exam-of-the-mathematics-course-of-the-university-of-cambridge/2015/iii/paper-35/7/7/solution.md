<h1 id="7/7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Let $\gamma$ be the exponential censoring rate and assume its time is [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the exponential death time. Regard censoring as the competing event B. The preceding conditional-density calculation gives

$$
X\mid\{\text{death observed}\}\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda+\gamma).
$$

Hence **the reported death-only rate estimates the combined rate**:

$$
\boxed{0.3\ \text{year}^{-1}\ \text{estimates}\ \lambda+\gamma.}
$$

It is not, in general, an estimate of the death [cause-specific hazard](../../../../../../cause-specific-hazard.md) alone. This interpretation assumes the stated two-exponential competition without an additional administrative end of observation that truncates the conditional time distribution.
