<h1 id="7/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Letting $t\to\infty$ in the [cumulative incidence function](../../../../../../cumulative-incidence-function.md) gives **the probability that the observed first event is of type A**:

$$
\boxed{\Pr(J=A)=\Pr(T_A<T_B)=\frac\alpha{\alpha+\beta}.}
$$

This is the sense of occurrence in the [competing risks model](../../../../../../competing-risks-model.md). If one instead followed the latent exponential $T_A$ even after B, its eventual-occurrence probability would be one; that is a different question from observing A before the competing event.
