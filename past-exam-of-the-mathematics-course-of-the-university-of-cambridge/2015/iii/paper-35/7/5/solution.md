<h1 id="7/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Divide the joint first-event/type-A density by the type probability. For $\alpha>0$,

$$
f_{T\mid J=A}(t)=\frac{\alpha e^{-(\alpha+\beta)t}}{\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)}
=\boxed{(\alpha+\beta)e^{-(\alpha+\beta)t},\qquad t\geq0.}
$$

It integrates to one, so **the conditional time is exponential with rate** $\alpha+\beta$. Type-A events that win the competition tend to occur sooner than an unrestricted latent A time. In fact the same conditional density holds for B, so the observed first-event time and its type are [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) in this constant-rate model.
