<h1 id="7/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Multiply survival to $t$ by the conditional type-A event probability in the next infinitesimal interval. **The unconditional type-A increment probability is**

$$
\boxed{\Pr(t\leq T<t+dt,J=A)=e^{-(\alpha+\beta)t}\alpha\,dt+o(dt).}
$$

Equivalently, its exact value over a positive interval of length $u$ is $\int_t^{t+u}\alpha e^{-(\alpha+\beta)v}dv$. The factor $e^{-(\alpha+\beta)t}$ is essential because a prior B event removes an individual from the relevant [risk set](../../../../../../risk-set.md).
