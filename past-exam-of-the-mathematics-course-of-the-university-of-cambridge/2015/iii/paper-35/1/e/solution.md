<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Ordinary [multiple imputation by chained equations](../../../../../../multiple-imputation-by-chained-equations.md) relies on [missing at random](../../../../../../missing-at-random.md), conditional on the variables included in its models, together with adequate model specification and suitable [conditional distributions](../../../../../../conditional-distribution.md). For [Bayesian inference](../../../../../../bayesian-statistics.md) that ignores the response mechanism, the usual sufficient condition also includes [distinct parameters](../../../../../../distinct-parameters.md) for data and missingness models, with an appropriately separate prior.

**The suspected dependence on withheld responses makes the usual missing-at-random assumption doubtful.** An accurately fitted observed-data model cannot, on its own, establish what the nonresponders would have reported. Use substantive judgement and [sensitivity analysis](../../../../../../sensitivity-analysis.md) under plausible [missing not at random](../../../../../../missing-not-at-random.md) departures, for example a [pattern-mixture sensitivity analysis](../../../../../../pattern-mixture-sensitivity-analysis.md) shifting the imputed condom [log odds](../../../../../../log-odds.md) or partner-count distribution relative to the observed-data prediction. Report how the final association changes. Additional informative measurements may make [missing at random](../../../../../../missing-at-random.md) more credible, but cannot verify it.
