↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Condition on the bulb being alive at age $\tau$. The next hour's survival [likelihood contribution](../../../../../../../likelihood-contribution.md) is

$$
\frac{F(\tau+1)}{F(\tau)}
=\frac{e^{-\lambda(\tau+1)}}{e^{-\lambda\tau}}
=\boxed{e^{-\lambda}}.
$$

**It does not depend on the unknown age** $\tau$. This is [memorylessness of the exponential distribution](../../../../../../../memorylessness-of-the-exponential-distribution.md): the residual lifetime given survival has the same [exponential distribution](../../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) as a new lifetime.
