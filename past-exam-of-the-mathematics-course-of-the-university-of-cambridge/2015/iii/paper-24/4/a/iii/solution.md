<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An [absolute formula](../../../../../../../absolute-formula.md) has the same truth value in the structures under comparison for parameters common to them. For transitive models $M\subseteq N$ and $a_1,\ldots,a_n\in M$, the requirement is

$$
\boxed{M\models\varphi(a_1,\ldots,a_n)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
N\models\varphi(a_1,\ldots,a_n).}
$$

One specifies the relevant class of models, for example transitive models of [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md). A formula is upward absolute when the forward implication holds and downward absolute when the reverse implication holds. [Bounded formulas in set theory](../../../../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md) are absolute between transitive membership structures: all their quantifiers range over the same elements of their parameter sets.
