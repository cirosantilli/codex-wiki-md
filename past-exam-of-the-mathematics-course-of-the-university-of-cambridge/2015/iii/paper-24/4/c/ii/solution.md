<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

First express inaccessibility by universally excluding witnesses to its failure. Besides $\kappa$ being an ordinal greater than $\omega$, require that for every $\alpha<\kappa$ there is no function from $\alpha$ cofinal in $\kappa$, and no injection from $\kappa$ into $\mathcal P(\alpha)$. The first condition gives regular cardinalhood; the second says that $\kappa$ is a [strong limit cardinal](../../../../../../../strong-limit-cardinal.md). Under the [axiom of choice](../../../../../../../axiom-of-choice.md) these are exactly the conditions for a [strongly inaccessible cardinal](../../../../../../../strongly-inaccessible-cardinal.md).

These are [Pi-one formulas modulo ZF](../../../../../../../pi-one-formula-modulo-zf.md): the only unbounded quantifiers universally range over possible functions. Being a function of the specified domain, having bounded or cofinal range in $\kappa$, and being an injection whose values are subsets of $\alpha$ can all be written with [bounded formulas in set theory](../../../../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md), without using a power-set object as an unbounded existential witness. Consequently inaccessibility is downward absolute to a transitive [inner model](../../../../../../../inner-model.md) of [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md).

Now suppose $\kappa$ is Mahlo in $V$. It is inaccessible in the [constructible universe](../../../../../../../constructible-universe.md) $L$, and every $V$-inaccessible ordinal below it is also inaccessible in $L$. Let $C\in L$ be any set that $L$ regards as a [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md) in $\kappa$. Closure and unboundedness are absolute properties of this fixed set of ordinals, so $C$ is also a [club set](../../../../../../../club-set.md) in $V$. By Mahloness in $V$, it contains an ordinal that is inaccessible in $V$, hence in $L$. Every $L$-club therefore meets the $L$-inaccessible ordinals below $\kappa$. Thus **[Mahloness is downward absolute to the constructible universe](../../../../../../../mahloness-is-downward-absolute-to-the-constructible-universe.md)**:

$$
\boxed{L\models\text{“}\kappa\text{ is Mahlo”}.}
$$
