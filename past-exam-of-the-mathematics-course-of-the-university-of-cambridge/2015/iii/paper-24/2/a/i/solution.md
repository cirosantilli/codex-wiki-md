A [Suslin line](../../../../../../../suslin-line.md) is a [dense linear order without endpoints](../../../../../../../dense-linear-order-without-endpoints.md) that is [order-complete](../../../../../../../order-completeness.md), has the [countable chain condition for a linear order](../../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-a-linear-order.md), and is not separable in its [order topology](../../../../../../../order-topology.md). Thus every disjoint family of nonempty open intervals is countable, but there is no countable [order-dense subset](../../../../../../../order-dense-subset.md). The completeness condition says that every nonempty bounded-above subset has a [supremum](../../../../../../../supremum.md). These requirements distinguish a [Suslin line](../../../../../../../suslin-line.md) from the real line, which has the countable [order-dense subset](../../../../../../../order-dense-subset.md) $\mathbb Q$.
