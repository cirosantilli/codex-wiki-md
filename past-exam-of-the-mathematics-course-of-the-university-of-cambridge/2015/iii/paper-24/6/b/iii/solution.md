<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the [generic extension](../../../../../../../generic-extension.md) from the preceding construction, a surjection from $\omega_1$ onto all reals gives $2^{\aleph_0}\leq\aleph_1$. [Cantor theorem](../../../../../../../cantor-s-theorem.md) gives the reverse inequality, so the [Continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../../continuum-hypothesis.md) holds.

For the exact consistency-strength statement, existence of a [countable transitive model](../../../../../../../countable-transitive-model.md) is stronger than bare consistency of [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md). One can formalize the forcing proof syntactically, or use the [constructible universe theorem](../../../../../../../constructible-universe-theorem.md): [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) proves that its [constructible universe](../../../../../../../constructible-universe.md) satisfies [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) and the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md). If there is any model of [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md), its internal constructible universe is therefore a model of [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) with CH. By the [Godel completeness theorem](../../../../../../../godel-s-completeness-theorem.md),

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{CH}).}
$$

This deduction does not silently replace consistency by existence of a transitive model.
