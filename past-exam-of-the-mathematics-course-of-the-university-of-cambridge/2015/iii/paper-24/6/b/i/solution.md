<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

This is the [Rasiowa–Sikorski lemma](../../../../../../../rasiowa-sikorski-lemma.md). Put $p_0=p$ and recursively choose

$$
p_{n+1}\leq p_n,\qquad p_{n+1}\in D_n,
$$

using density. Set

$$
\boxed{G=\{q\in P:\exists n\ p_n\leq q\}.}
$$

It contains $p$, is upward closed toward weaker conditions, and is directed: if $p_i\leq q$ and $p_j\leq r$, then $p_{\max(i,j)}$ is a common stronger condition in $G$. It meets each $D_n$ through $p_{n+1}$. Thus $G$ is the required [generic filter](../../../../../../../generic-filter.md) for the prescribed countable family of dense sets. For a [countable transitive model](../../../../../../../countable-transitive-model.md), externally enumerate its dense sets to obtain a generic filter over the model by this same argument.
