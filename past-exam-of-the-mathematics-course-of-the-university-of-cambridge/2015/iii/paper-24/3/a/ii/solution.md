[Fodor lemma](../../../../../../../fodor-lemma.md) says that if $\lambda$ is a regular uncountable [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md), $S\subseteq\lambda$ is stationary, and $f:S\to\lambda$ is regressive, then

$$
\boxed{\exists\xi<\lambda\quad\{\alpha\in S:f(\alpha)=\xi\}\text{ is stationary}.}
$$

Here a [regressive function](../../../../../../../regressive-function.md) satisfies $f(\alpha)<\alpha$ at every nonzero $\alpha\in S$; removing $0$ has no effect on stationarity. Both regularity and stationarity are part of the hypotheses.
