The [club principle](../../../../../../../club-principle.md) $\clubsuit$ asserts that there is a sequence $\langle A_\delta:\delta\in\operatorname{Lim}(\omega_1)\rangle$ such that $A_\delta\subseteq\delta$ has [order type](../../../../../../../order-type.md) $\omega$, is cofinal in $\delta$, and

$$
\boxed{\forall X\in[\omega_1]^{\omega_1}\ \exists\delta\in\operatorname{Lim}(\omega_1)\quad A_\delta\subseteq X.}
$$

It guesses a cofinal countable subset of an uncountable set, with inclusion as the guessing requirement.
