<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If two [order automorphisms](../../../../../../../order-automorphism.md) agree on $\mathbb Q$, their values at any real $x$ are the same [supremum](../../../../../../../supremum.md) of the common values at rational points below $x$. This is the uniqueness argument for [extension of an order automorphism from a dense subset](../../../../../../../extension-of-an-order-automorphism-from-a-dense-subset.md); it does not require either automorphism to carry $\mathbb Q$ onto itself.

Their restrictions belong to $\mathbb R^{\mathbb Q}$, whose [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) is

$$
(2^{\aleph_0})^{\aleph_0}=2^{\aleph_0}.
$$

This gives the upper bound. The translations $x\mapsto x+r$, one for each real $r$, are distinct [order automorphisms](../../../../../../../order-automorphism.md), giving the lower bound. Consequently

$$
\boxed{|\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb R,<)|=2^{\aleph_0}.}
$$
