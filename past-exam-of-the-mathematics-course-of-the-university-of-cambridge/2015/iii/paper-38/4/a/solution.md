[P](../../../../../../p-complexity.md) is the class of [decision problems](../../../../../../decision-problem.md) decided by a deterministic [algorithm](../../../../../../algorithm.md) in time polynomial in the encoded input length. [NP](../../../../../../np-complexity.md) is the class whose yes-instances have [complexity certificates](../../../../../../certificate-complexity.md) of polynomial length that a deterministic [algorithm](../../../../../../algorithm.md) verifies in [polynomial time](../../../../../../polynomial-time.md).

A [decision problem](../../../../../../decision-problem.md) is [NP-complete](../../../../../../np-completeness.md) if it belongs to [NP](../../../../../../np-complexity.md) and is [NP-hard](../../../../../../np-hardness.md): every problem in [NP](../../../../../../np-complexity.md) admits a [polynomial-time many-one reduction](../../../../../../polynomial-time-many-one-reduction.md) to it. Such a reduction maps an instance $I$ to an instance $R(I)$ in [polynomial time](../../../../../../polynomial-time.md) and satisfies $I$ is a yes-instance if and only if $R(I)$ is a yes-instance. **Membership in NP and NP-hardness are both required.**
