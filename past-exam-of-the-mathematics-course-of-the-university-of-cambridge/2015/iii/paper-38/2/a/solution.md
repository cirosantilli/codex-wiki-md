<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Setting the third coordinate to zero and the first to four leaves $6x_2\geq4$ and $4x_2\leq3$. Thus $x_2=2/3$ works. Direct substitution gives

$$
\boxed{x=(4,2/3,0)^T},\qquad Ax=(20/3,-8,0)^T\leq b,\qquad Cx=(-2/3,-4,-4)^T\leq d.
$$

Hence this point lies in both [linear polyhedra](../../../../../../linear-polyhedron.md).
