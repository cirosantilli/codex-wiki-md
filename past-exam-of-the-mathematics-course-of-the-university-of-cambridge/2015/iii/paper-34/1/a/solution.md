↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value.md) $\mu$,

$$
\mathbb EX_1=\mu,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X_1)=\mu^2.
$$

The [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) has both [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value.md) and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) equal to $\lambda$. Substitution into the [random sum of independent claims](../../../../../../random-sum-of-independent-claims.md) formulas gives **the portfolio A moments**

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E S_A=\lambda\mu,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(S_A)=2\lambda\mu^2.}
$$
