<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Boussinesq approximation](../../../../../../boussinesq-approximation.md) requires $\delta=(\rho-\rho_0)/\rho_0=\phi(\rho_p/\rho_0-1)\ll1$. The reference [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) $\rho_0$ can then be used in inertia, while the small density excess is retained in [buoyancy](../../../../../../buoyancy.md). Dilute [particle volume fraction](../../../../../../particle-volume-fraction.md) $\phi\ll1$ alone does not suffice if $\rho_p/\rho_0$ is exceptionally large. Define $G=g(\rho_p-\rho_0)/\rho_0$, so the [reduced gravity](../../../../../../reduced-gravity-split.md) is $g'=G\phi$.

The [shallow water equations](../../../../../../shallow-water-equations.md) require depth small compared with the horizontal evolution scale, weak vertical acceleration and approximately [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md). Here the ambient is deep and quiescent, so its leading contribution is a hydrostatic reference pressure; the excess pressure in the layer is $\rho_0G\phi(h-z)$. The model also needs nearly uniform streamwise [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) across the section and the stated absence of secondary circulation. The advancing [gravity current](../../../../../../gravity-current.md) nose is a separate region where these assumptions can fail.

A nearly uniform [particle volume fraction](../../../../../../particle-volume-fraction.md) can be maintained by turbulent mixing with [eddy diffusivity](../../../../../../eddy-diffusivity.md) $K_z$, provided $h^2/K_z\ll h/w$ and the mixing time is short compared with horizontal advection and bulk evolution. Equivalently, $wh/K_z\ll1$ keeps the bulk settling-induced gradient small. The particles should respond rapidly enough to follow the mixing motions. A thin boundary layer adjacent to the absorbing bed or walls need not be well mixed.

**Uniform bulk [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) does not imply zero sedimentation.** The relative downward particle [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) still supplies a [particle deposition flux](../../../../../../particle-deposition-flux.md) of approximately $w\phi$ to an absorbing boundary. Mixing redistributes the remaining particles and maintains the bulk [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) profile while its overall level decreases; it need not suspend every particle indefinitely. Deposition requires negligible resuspension in the model.
