The real [Stone-Weierstrass theorem](../../../../../../stone-weierstrass-theorem.md) states that if $X$ is a compact Hausdorff space and $A\subseteq C(X,\mathbb R)$ is a subalgebra containing the constants and separating points of $X$, then $A$ is dense in $C(X,\mathbb R)$ in the [uniform norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md).

The [Tietze extension theorem](../../../../../../tietze-extension-theorem.md) states that if $X$ is a normal topological space, $F\subseteq X$ is closed, and $f:F\to\mathbb R$ is continuous, then $f$ extends to a continuous function on $X$. In its bounded form, an $f$ taking values in $[-M,M]$ has an extension with the same range bound. The underlying separation step is the [Urysohn lemma](../../../../../../urysohn-s-lemma.md).
