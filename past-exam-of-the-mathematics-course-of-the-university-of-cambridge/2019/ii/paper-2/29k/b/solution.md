The [fundamental theorem of asset pricing](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-asset-pricing.md) for a finite discrete-time market states that the market is free of [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md) if and only if it admits an [equivalent martingale measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md). Subject to the usual finite-market hypotheses, the arbitrage-free market is complete if and only if that measure is unique.
