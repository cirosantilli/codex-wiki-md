An [equivalent martingale measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md) is a probability measure $\mathbb Q$ on the terminal sigma-algebra such that $\mathbb Q$ and the physical measure $\mathbb P$ are mutually absolutely continuous and every asset price discounted by the chosen [numéraire](../../../../../../numeraire.md) is a $\mathbb Q$-martingale with respect to the market filtration.
