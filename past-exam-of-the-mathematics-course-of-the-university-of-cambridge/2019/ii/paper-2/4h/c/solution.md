<h1 id="4h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The function is the [predecessor function](../../../../../../predecessor-function.md). It has the [primitive recursion](../../../../../../primitive-recursion.md) definition

$$
f(0)=0,
\qquad
f(n+1)=n.
$$

The initial function is the zero function and the recursion step is a projection function, both of which are [primitive recursive functions](../../../../../../primitive-recursive-function.md). Closure under primitive recursion therefore proves that $f$ is primitive recursive.
