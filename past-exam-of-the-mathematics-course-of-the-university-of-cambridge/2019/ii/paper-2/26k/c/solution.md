<h1 id="26k/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $U$ be uniformly distributed on $X$ and let $\eta$ be the law of $(U,U)$. Both marginals are $\mu$, because

$$
\eta(X\times A)=\mathbb P(U\in A)=\eta(A\times X).
$$

However, $\eta$ is concentrated on the diagonal $\{(x,x):x\in X\}$, which has two-dimensional Lebesgue measure zero. Thus $\eta$ is not Lebesgue measure on $X^2$.
