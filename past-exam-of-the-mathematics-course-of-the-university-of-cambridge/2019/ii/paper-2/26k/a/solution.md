The [product sigma-algebra](../../../../../../product-sigma-algebra.md) is

$$
\mathcal A_1\otimes\mathcal A_2
=\sigma\{A_1\times A_2:A_i\in\mathcal A_i\}.
$$

The [product measure](../../../../../../product-measure.md) $\mu_1\otimes\mu_2$ is the probability measure on this sigma-algebra satisfying

$$
(\mu_1\otimes\mu_2)(A_1\times A_2)=\mu_1(A_1)\mu_2(A_2).
$$

The measurable rectangles form a [pi-system](../../../../../../pi-system.md) generating $\mathcal A_1\otimes\mathcal A_2$. Any two probability measures agreeing on them therefore agree on the generated sigma-algebra by the [pi-lambda theorem](../../../../../../pi-lambda-theorem.md), proving uniqueness.
