<h1 id="32c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Suppose a normalizable solution satisfies $L\psi=-\kappa^2\psi$. Multiplication by $\overline\psi$, integration over $\mathbb R$, and [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) give

$$
-\kappa^2\int_{\mathbb R}|\psi|^2\,dx
=\int_{\mathbb R}\overline\psi L\psi\,dx
=\int_{\mathbb R}p^2|\psi'|^2\,dx\geq0.
$$

The boundary term vanishes for a normalizable eigenfunction. The left side is negative unless $\psi=0$, so no nontrivial normalizable solution exists.
