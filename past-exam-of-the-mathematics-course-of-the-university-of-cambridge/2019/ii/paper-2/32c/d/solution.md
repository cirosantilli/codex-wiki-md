<h1 id="32c/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

As $|x|\to\infty$, the operator $A$ tends to $-4\partial_x^3$. Evolve the normalized scattering eigenfunction by

$$
\partial_t\phi_k=A\phi_k+4ik^3\phi_k.
$$

The added scalar term keeps the coefficient of $e^{-ikx}$ at $-\infty$ equal to one. At $+\infty$,

$$
(-4\partial_x^3+4ik^3)e^{-ikx}=0,
\qquad
(-4\partial_x^3+4ik^3)e^{ikx}=8ik^3e^{ikx}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\dot a(k,t)=0,
\qquad
\dot b(k,t)=8ik^3b(k,t).
$$

It follows that

$$
\boxed{T(k,t)=T(k,0),
\qquad
R(k,t)=R(k,0)e^{8ik^3t}.}
$$
