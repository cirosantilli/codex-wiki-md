<h1 id="32c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Differentiating

$$
L=-\frac d{dx}\,p^2\frac d{dx}
$$

with respect to time gives

$$
L_t=-\frac d{dx}\left(2pp_t\frac d{dx}\right).
$$

If $p_t=p^3p_{xxx}$, then

$$
L_t=-\frac d{dx}\left(2p^4p_{xxx}\frac d{dx}\right)=LA-AL.
$$

**Thus $L_t=[L,A]$ is an [Isospectral Lax equation](../../../../../../isospectral-lax-equation.md), and $(L,A)$ is a [Lax pair](../../../../../../lax-pair.md) for the [Harry Dym equation](../../../../../../harry-dym-equation.md).**
