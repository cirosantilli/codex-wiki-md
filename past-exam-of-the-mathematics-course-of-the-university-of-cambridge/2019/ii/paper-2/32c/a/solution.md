<h1 id="32c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Normalize the solution by its incoming amplitude at $+\infty$. The [transmission coefficient](../../../../../../transmission-coefficient.md) and [reflection coefficient](../../../../../../reflection-coefficient.md) are

$$
\boxed{T(k)=\frac1{a(k)},
\qquad
R(k)=\frac{b(k)}{a(k)}.}
$$

For two solutions of the same equation in [Sturm-Liouville form](../../../../../../sturm-liouville-form.md), the modified [Wronskian](../../../../../../wronskian.md)

$$
W=p^2(\psi_1\psi_2'-\psi_2\psi_1')
$$

is independent of $x$. Take $\psi_1=\phi_k$ and $\psi_2=\overline{\phi_k}$. At $-\infty$ this gives $W=-2ik$, while at $+\infty$ it gives

$$
W=-2ik(|a(k)|^2-|b(k)|^2).
$$

Therefore $|a|^2-|b|^2=1$, and division by $|a|^2$ yields conservation of scattering flux:

$$
\boxed{|R(k)|^2+|T(k)|^2=1.}
$$
