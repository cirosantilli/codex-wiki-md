<h1 id="37a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $d_+=\sigma+h$ and $d_-=h-\sigma$ for the lower and upper layer thicknesses. A steady unidirectional velocity $\mathbf u=u(y)\mathbf e_x$ with no pressure gradient satisfies the [Navier-Stokes equations](../../../../../../navier-stokes-equation.md) through $u''=0$ in each layer. Continuity of velocity and tangential stress at the interface gives one constant [shear stress](../../../../../../shear-stress.md)

$$
\tau=\mu_+u_+'=\mu_-u_-'
=\frac{U_--U_+}{d_+/\mu_++d_-/\mu_-}.
$$

The [two-layer Couette flow](../../../../../../two-layer-couette-flow.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{
u_+(y)=U_++\frac\tau{\mu_+}(y+h),
\quad -h\leq y\leq\sigma,}
$$



$$
\boxed{
u_-(y)=U_--\frac\tau{\mu_-}(h-y),
\quad \sigma\leq y\leq h.}
$$
