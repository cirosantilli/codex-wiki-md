<h1 id="37a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppose $U_+=U$ and $U_-=-U$. Integrating the two velocity profiles gives the [volume fluxes](../../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit span

$$
Q_+=d_+U+\frac{\tau d_+^2}{2\mu_+},
\qquad
Q_-=-d_-U-\frac{\tau d_-^2}{2\mu_-}.
$$

Here

$$
\tau=\frac{-2U}{d_+/\mu_++d_-/\mu_-}.
$$

Consequently

$$
Q_+=\frac{Ud_+d_-/\mu_-}{d_+/\mu_++d_-/\mu_-},
\qquad
Q_-=-\frac{Ud_+d_-/\mu_+}{d_+/\mu_++d_-/\mu_-},
$$

so all dependence on the interface position cancels from the ratio:

$$
\boxed{\frac{Q_-}{Q_+}=-\frac{\mu_-}{\mu_+}.}
$$
