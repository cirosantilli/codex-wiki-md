The fluid exerts the tangential traction $-\tau\mathbf e_x$ on the upper boundary and $+\tau\mathbf e_x$ on the lower boundary. Equivalently, the boundaries exert $+\tau\mathbf e_x$ and $-\tau\mathbf e_x$ on the fluids, respectively.
