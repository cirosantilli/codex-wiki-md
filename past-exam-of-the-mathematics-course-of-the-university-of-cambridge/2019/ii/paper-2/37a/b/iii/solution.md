<h1 id="37a/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The rate at which the upper and lower boundaries do work on the fluid, per unit area, is

$$
\mathcal W=\tau U_- -\tau U_+
=\tau(U_--U_+).
$$

The definition of $\tau$ gives

$$
\mathcal W=\tau^2\left(\frac{d_+}{\mu_+}+\frac{d_-}{\mu_-}\right)=\mathcal D,
$$

which is the required mechanical-energy balance.
