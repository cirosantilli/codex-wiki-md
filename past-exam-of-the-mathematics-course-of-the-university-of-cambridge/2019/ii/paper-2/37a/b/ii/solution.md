The [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) per unit boundary area is

$$
\mathcal D
=\int_{-h}^{\sigma}\mu_+(u_+')^2,dy
+\int_{\sigma}^{h}\mu_-(u_-')^2,dy
=\boxed{\tau^2\left(\frac{d_+}{\mu_+}+\frac{d_-}{\mu_-}\right)}.
$$
