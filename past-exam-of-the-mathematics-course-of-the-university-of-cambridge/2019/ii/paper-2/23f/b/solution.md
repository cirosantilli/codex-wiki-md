<h1 id="23f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $|z|<1$, sum the geometric series and its derivative:

$$
\sum_{n\geq0}(-1)^nz^n=\frac1{1+z},
\qquad
\sum_{n\geq0}n(-1)^nz^n=-\frac z{(1+z)^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
h(z)=\frac1{1+z}-\frac{2z}{(1+z)^2}
=\frac{1-z}{(1+z)^2}.
$$

This rational continuation is holomorphic across every point of the unit circle except $z=-1$, where it has a double pole. The set of singular points is consequently

$$
\boxed{\{-1\}}.
$$
