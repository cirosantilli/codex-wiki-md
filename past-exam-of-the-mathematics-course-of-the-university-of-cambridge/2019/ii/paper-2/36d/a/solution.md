<h1 id="36d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

This is the [Schwarzschild-de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../schwarzschild-de-sitter-spacetime.md). Its [geodesic Lagrangian](../../../../../../geodesic-lagrangian.md) may be taken as

$$
\mathcal L=\frac12\left[-f\dot t^2+f^{-1}\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2+r^2\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi^2\right].
$$

Time-translation and rotational symmetry give three independent [integrals of motion](../../../../../../integral-of-motion.md), which may be chosen as

$$
E=f\dot t,
\qquad
L_z=r^2\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi,
\qquad
L^2=r^4\left(\dot\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi^2\right).
$$

The constant norm of the tangent is $g_{ab}\dot x^a\dot x^b=-\kappa$, where $\kappa=1$ for a timelike geodesic parametrized by proper time and $\kappa=0$ for a [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md). Substitution gives

$$
-\kappa=-\frac{E^2}{f}+\frac{\dot r^2}{f}+\frac{L^2}{r^2},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{\dot r^2+V(r)=E^2,
\qquad
V(r)=f(r)\left(\kappa+\frac{L^2}{r^2}\right).}
$$
