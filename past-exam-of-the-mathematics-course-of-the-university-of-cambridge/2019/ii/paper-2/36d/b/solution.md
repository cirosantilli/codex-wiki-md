<h1 id="36d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a nonradial [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md),

$$
V(r)=L^2\left(\frac1{r^2}-\frac{2m}{r^3}-H^2\right).
$$

It tends to $-\infty$ as $r\to0^+$, rises to one maximum, and tends to $-L^2H^2$ as $r\to\infty$. A [circular null geodesic](../../../../../../circular-null-geodesic.md) requires $V'(r)=0$, so

$$
-\frac2{r^3}+\frac{6m}{r^4}=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{r=3m}.
$$

Since $V''(3m)<0$, this [photon sphere](../../../../../../photon-sphere.md) is unstable. Its energy satisfies $E^2=L^2(1/(27m^2)-H^2)$.
