<h1 id="19i/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The subgroups $\langle A,B\rangle$ and $\langle C,D\rangle$ each have order eight, their elements commute across the two subgroups, and their intersection is exactly $\langle Z\rangle$. Therefore

$$
|G|=\frac{8\cdot8}{2}=32.
$$

Since $G'=\langle Z\rangle$, the [abelianization](../../../../../../../abelianization.md) $G/G'$ has order $16$ and is generated by the four commuting involutions represented by $A,B,C,D$. It is isomorphic to $(C_2)^4$, so it has sixteen one-dimensional complex representations, obtained by independently sending $A,B,C,D$ to $\pm1$ and sending $Z$ to $1$.

These characters contribute $16$ to the [sum of squares of irreducible degrees](../../../../../../../sum-of-squares-of-irreducible-degrees.md). The given irreducible representation has degree four and contributes another $16$. Part (a) and $|G|=32$ show that no other irreducible representations exist. Thus the complete list is the sixteen linear characters and the given four-dimensional representation.
