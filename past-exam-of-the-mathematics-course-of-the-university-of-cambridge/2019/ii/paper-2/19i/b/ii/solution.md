<h1 id="19i/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using the commutation relations, every element has a normal form

$$
g=Z^eA^aB^bC^cD^d,
\qquad e,a,b,c,d\in\{0,1\}.
$$

On squaring, equal generators cancel and every interchange contributes only the central element $Z$, so $g^2\in\langle Z\rangle$. There are at most $2^5=32$ normal forms, and every element has order dividing four. Hence $G$ is a [finite p-group](../../../../../../../finite-p-group.md) with $p=2$, of order at most $32$.
