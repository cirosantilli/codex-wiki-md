<h1 id="19i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\chi_{\rm reg}$ be the character of the [regular representation](../../../../../../regular-representation.md). It has $\chi_{\rm reg}(1)=|G|$ and vanishes away from the identity. The multiplicity of an irreducible character $\chi_j$ in it is

$$
\langle\chi_{\rm reg},\chi_j\rangle
=\frac1{|G|}|G|\chi_j(1)=n_j.
$$

Thus the regular representation decomposes as $\bigoplus_j n_j\rho_j$. Comparing dimensions gives

$$
\boxed{|G|=\sum_{j=1}^k n_j^2.}
$$
