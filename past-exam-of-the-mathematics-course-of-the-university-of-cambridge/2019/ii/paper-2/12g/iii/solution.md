**True.** The Huffman tree is a full binary tree, so its leaves form a complete prefix code. The [Kraft inequality](../../../../../../kraft-mcmillan-inequality.md) is therefore an equality:

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i=1}^m2^{-s_i}=1.}
$$
