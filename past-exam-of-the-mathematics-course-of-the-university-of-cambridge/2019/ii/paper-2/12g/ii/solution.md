**False.** For probabilities $(0.30,0.25,0.25,0.20)$, every length-two code is optimal. A code in which the $0.30$ and $0.20$ symbols are sibling leaves is optimal, but no Huffman construction produces that labelled tree, because its first merge must pair $0.20$ with a $0.25$ symbol.
