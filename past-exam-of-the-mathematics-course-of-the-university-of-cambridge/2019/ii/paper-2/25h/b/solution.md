<h1 id="25h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $\tau=0$ everywhere, the third of the [Frenet-Serret formulas](../../../../../../frenet-serret-formulas.md) gives $B'=0$, so $B$ is a constant vector. Moreover

$$
\frac d{ds}(\alpha\cdot B)=T\cdot B=0.
$$

**Thus $\alpha(s)\cdot B$ is constant, and the image of $\alpha$ lies in the plane perpendicular to $B$.**
