<h1 id="8e/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $f$ and $g$ be [conserved quantities](../../../../../../conserved-quantity.md), allowing explicit time dependence. Then

$$
\partial_t f=-\{f,H\},
\qquad
\partial_t g=-\{g,H\}.
$$

Using bilinearity of the [Poisson bracket](../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md), commutation of partial differentiation with it, and the [Jacobi identity for the Poisson bracket](../../../../../../jacobi-identity-for-the-poisson-bracket.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac d{dt}\{f,g\}
&=\{\{f,g\},H\}+\{\partial_t f,g\}+\{f,\partial_t g\}\\
&=\{\{f,g\},H\}-\{\{f,H\},g\}-\{f,\{g,H\}\}=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Thus $\{f,g\}$ is also conserved.**
