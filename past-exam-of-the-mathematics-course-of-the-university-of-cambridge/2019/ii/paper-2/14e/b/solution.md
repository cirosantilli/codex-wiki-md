<h1 id="14e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [canonical momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is $\mathbf p=m\dot{\mathbf r}+q\mathbf A$, and the [Legendre transform](../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) gives

$$
H=\mathbf p\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}-L
=\frac{|\mathbf p-q\mathbf A|^2}{2m}+q\phi.
$$

Its [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) are

$$
\boxed{\dot{\mathbf r}=\frac{\partial H}{\partial\mathbf p}
=\frac{\mathbf p-q\mathbf A}{m}}
$$

and, componentwise,

$$
\boxed{\dot p_i=-\frac{\partial H}{\partial r_i}
=q\dot r_j\partial_iA_j-q\partial_i\phi.}
$$

Combining the second equation with $\mathbf p=m\dot{\mathbf r}+q\mathbf A$ recovers the [Lorentz force law](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md).
