<h1 id="9b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At equality, let $\rho_{\rm cc}=\rho_{\rm dust}=\rho_*$. The [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) has $P_{\rm cc}=-\rho_*$, while nonrelativistic matter has $P_{\rm dust}=0$. Therefore

$$
\rho+3P=2\rho_*-3\rho_*=-\rho_*<0.
$$

The [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) consequently has $\ddot a>0$: the expansion is accelerated at $a=a_{\rm eq}$.
