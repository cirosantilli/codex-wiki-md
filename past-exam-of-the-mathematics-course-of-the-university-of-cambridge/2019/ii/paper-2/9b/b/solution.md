↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) $P=w\rho$ with positive [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md),

$$
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}\rho(1+3w).
$$

The expansion is accelerated for $w<-1/3$, coasting for $w=-1/3$, and decelerated for $w>-1/3$.
