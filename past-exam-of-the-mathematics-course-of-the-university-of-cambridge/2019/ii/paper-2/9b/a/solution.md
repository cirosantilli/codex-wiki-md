<h1 id="9b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiate the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) $H^2=8\pi G\rho/3$ and use the [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) $\dot\rho=-3H(\rho+P)$:

$$
2H\dot H=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\dot\rho
=-8\pi GH(\rho+P),
$$

so $\dot H=-4\pi G(\rho+P)$. Since $H=\dot a/a$,

$$
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=\dot H+H^2
=-4\pi G(\rho+P)+\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho.
$$

Hence the [Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}(\rho+3P).}
$$
