<h1 id="9b/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [cosmological constant energy](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) is constant, whereas the [matter density](../../../../../../matter-density.md) scales as $a^{-3}$. Hence

$$
\rho_{\rm dust}(a)=\rho_{\rm cc}
\left(\frac{a_{\rm eq}}a\right)^3.
$$

The transition occurs when $\rho+3P=\rho_{\rm dust}-2\rho_{\rm cc}=0$, so

$$
\left(\frac{a_{\rm eq}}a\right)^3=2,
\qquad
\boxed{\frac a{a_{\rm eq}}=2^{-1/3}.}
$$
