**False.** Take $\alpha=\omega$ and $\beta=\omega\cdot2$. Then

$$
\alpha+\beta=\omega\cdot3=\beta+\alpha,
$$

but associativity and the limit clause for ordinal multiplication give

$$
\boxed{\alpha\beta=\omega^2\cdot2,
\qquad
\beta\alpha=(\omega\cdot2)\omega=\omega^2.}
$$
