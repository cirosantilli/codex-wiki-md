<h1 id="27k/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [reversible Markov chain](../../../../../../reversible-markov-chain.md) must satisfy [detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md). For every $i\geq1$, the rate from $i$ to $i+1$ is positive, while the reverse rate from $i+1$ to $i$ is zero. No positive measure can satisfy $\pi_i g_{i,i+1}=\pi_{i+1}g_{i+1,i}$, so the irreducible chain is not reversible.
