<h1 id="27k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The total jump rate is $\lambda_0$ at zero and $\lambda_i(1+\rho_i)$ at $i>0$. Hence the [jump chain](../../../../../../jump-chain.md) has transition probabilities

$$
P_{0,1}=1,
$$

and, for $i>0$,

$$
\boxed{P_{i,i+1}=\frac1{1+\rho_i},
\qquad
P_{i,0}=\frac{\rho_i}{1+\rho_i}.}
$$

All other entries vanish.
