<h1 id="20g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The element $\omega$ satisfies

$$
\omega^2-\omega+12=0.
$$

Modulo both $2$ and $3$, this polynomial factors as $X(X-1)$. Hence

$$
(2)=\mathfrak p_2\mathfrak q_2,
\quad
\mathfrak p_2=(2,\omega),
\quad
\mathfrak q_2=(2,\omega-1),
$$

and

$$
(3)=\mathfrak p_3\mathfrak q_3,
\quad
\mathfrak p_3=(3,\omega),
\quad
\mathfrak q_3=(3,\omega-1),
$$

where the respective [ideal norms](../../../../../../ideal-norm.md) are $2,2,3,3$.

Now $N(\omega)=12$, and $\omega$ lies in $\mathfrak p_2$ and $\mathfrak p_3$ but not in their conjugate primes. Similarly $N(2+\omega)=18$, while $2+\omega$ lies in $\mathfrak p_2$ and $\mathfrak q_3$. Comparing norms gives

$$
\boxed{(\omega)=\mathfrak p_2^2\mathfrak p_3,
\qquad
(2+\omega)=\mathfrak p_2\mathfrak q_3^2.}
$$
