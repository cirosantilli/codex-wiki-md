<h1 id="33b/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

During the field region the exact [time-evolution operator](../../../../../../../time-evolution-operator.md) is

$$
U(T)=\exp\!\left[
\frac{i\mu T}{\hbar}(B\sigma_z+b\sigma_x)
\right].
$$

Using the stated [Pauli matrix](../../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md) exponential with $\Omega=\mu\sqrt{B^2+b^2}/\hbar$,

$$
U(T)=\cos(\Omega T)I
+i\sin(\Omega T)\frac{B\sigma_z+b\sigma_x}{\sqrt{B^2+b^2}}.
$$

Only the $\sigma_x$ term connects the two $\sigma_z$ eigenstates. Thus

$$
\boxed{P_{\uparrow\to\downarrow}
=\frac{b^2}{B^2+b^2}
\sin^2\!\left(
\frac{\mu L}{\hbar v}\sqrt{B^2+b^2}
\right).}
$$

Expanding this expression for $b/B\to0$ recovers part (i).
