↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [interaction picture](../../../../../../interaction-picture.md), first-order [time-dependent perturbation theory](../../../../../../time-dependent-perturbation-theory.md) gives the transition amplitude

$$
c_j^{(1)}(T)
=-\frac{i}{\hbar}\langle j|V|i\rangle
\int_0^T e^{i\omega_{ji}t}\,dt
=-\frac{\langle j|V|i\rangle}{\hbar\omega_{ji}}
\left(e^{i\omega_{ji}T}-1\right).
$$

Taking its squared modulus and using $|e^{i\theta}-1|^2=4\sin^2(\theta/2)$ gives

$$
\boxed{P_{ij}\simeq
4|\langle i|V|j\rangle|^2
\frac{\sin^2(\omega_{ij}T/2)}{(\hbar\omega_{ij})^2}.}
$$
