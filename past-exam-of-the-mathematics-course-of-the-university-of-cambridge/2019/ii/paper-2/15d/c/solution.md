<h1 id="15d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

By the [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](../../../../../../rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md), $\operatorname{tr}(\Pi_0D)$ is maximized by taking $\Pi_0$ to project onto the positive-eigenvalue eigenspace of $D$. Put $|\langle\alpha_0|\alpha_1\rangle|=\cos\theta$. On $S$, the characteristic polynomial of $D$ is

$$
\lambda^2-(p_0-p_1)\lambda-p_0p_1\sin^2\theta,
$$

so its positive eigenvalue is

$$
\lambda_+=\frac{p_0-p_1+\sqrt{1-4p_0p_1\cos^2\theta}}2.
$$

Part (b) then gives the [Helstrom-Holevo bound](../../../../../../helstrom-holevo-bound.md)

$$
\boxed{P_S^{\rm opt}=p_1+\lambda_+
=\frac{1+\sqrt{1-4p_0p_1\cos^2\theta}}2.}
$$
