The conclusions of (b) and (c) remain true. Indeed, the [unit circle](../../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) is a [path-connected space](../../../../../../path-connected-space.md), and the upper semicircle supplies a path from $-1$ to $1$; restricting the proposed closed cover or two-valued continuous function to that path reduces either claim to the corresponding result on $I$.

The fixed-point conclusion (d) fails. For example, any nontrivial [spatial rotation](../../../../../../spatial-rotation.md) $f(z)=e^{i\theta}z$ of the unit circle has no fixed point when $e^{i\theta}\ne1$; the antipodal map $f(z)=-z$ is the simplest example.
