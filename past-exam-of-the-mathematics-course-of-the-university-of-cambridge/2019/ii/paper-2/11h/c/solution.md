<h1 id="11h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If such a [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) existed, the inverse images

$$
A=f^{-1}(\{-1\}),
\qquad
B=f^{-1}(\{1\})
$$

would be closed, would cover $I$, and would contain $-1$ and $1$ respectively. Part (b) gives a point in $A\cap B$, which is impossible.
