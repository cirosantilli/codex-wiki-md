<h1 id="11h/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Suppose $I=A\cup B$, where $A$ and $B$ are disjoint closed sets with $-1\in A$ and $1\in B$. Since each is the complement of the other, both are also open in $I$. Define $f=-1$ on $A$ and $f=1$ on $B$. This [locally constant function](../../../../../../locally-constant-function.md) is continuous and contradicts part (c). Therefore $A\cap B\ne\varnothing$.
