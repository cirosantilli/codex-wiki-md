<h1 id="11h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Read the $2n+1$ colours from left to right. If every neighbouring pair had the same colour, [mathematical induction](../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) would make every point the same colour as $-1$, contradicting the different colour of $1$. Therefore at least one adjacent pair has different colours.
