**No.** Before any operation, Bob's [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) is $I/2$, independent of $|\alpha\rangle$. Any trace-preserving operation performed only by Alice leaves Bob's reduced state unchanged when her uncommunicated outcomes are averaged. This is the [no-communication theorem](../../../../../../no-communication-theorem.md), or [quantum no-signalling](../../../../../../quantum-no-signalling.md).

If Bob received the unknown state, his reduced state would instead be the pure state $|\alpha\rangle\langle\alpha|$, which depends on Alice's input. That would let Alice signal by choosing different inputs, contradicting no-signalling. The two classical outcome bits are therefore essential.
