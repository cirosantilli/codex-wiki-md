<h1 id="10d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Alice performs a projective [Bell-basis measurement](../../../../../../bell-basis-measurement.md) on her qubits $C$ and $A$. Outcome $(x,z)$ occurs with probability $1/4$ and leaves Bob's qubit in

$$
X^xZ^z|\alpha\rangle.
$$

Alice sends the two classical bits $(x,z)$ to Bob. He applies the inverse Pauli operation

$$
(X^xZ^z)^{-1}=Z^zX^x,
$$

recovering $|\alpha\rangle$ exactly, up to an irrelevant [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md). This is [quantum teleportation](../../../../../../quantum-teleportation.md): only [local operations and classical communication](../../../../../../local-operations-and-classical-communication.md) are used, while the shared Bell pair supplies the entanglement resource.
