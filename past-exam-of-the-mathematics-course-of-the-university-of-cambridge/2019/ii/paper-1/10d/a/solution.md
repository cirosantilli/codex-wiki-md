<h1 id="10d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $|\alpha\rangle=a|0\rangle+b|1\rangle$. Expanding in the orthonormal [Bell basis](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) of systems $CA$ gives the [Bell-basis teleportation identity](../../../../../../bell-basis-teleportation-identity.md)

$$
|\alpha\rangle_C|\beta_{00}\rangle_{AB}
=\frac12\bigl(
|\beta_{00}\rangle_{CA}|\alpha\rangle_B
+|\beta_{01}\rangle_{CA}Z|\alpha\rangle_B
+|\beta_{10}\rangle_{CA}X|\alpha\rangle_B
+|\beta_{11}\rangle_{CA}XZ|\alpha\rangle_B
\bigr).
$$

Thus, uniformly for $x,z\in\{0,1\}$,

$$
\boxed{|\mu_{xz}\rangle=X^xZ^z|\alpha\rangle}.
$$

The states are unique because the four Bell states are an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of the two-qubit space. In particular,

$$
\boxed{|\mu_{10}\rangle=X|\alpha\rangle}.
$$
