<h1 id="14a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\omega$ solve the equation represented by the original symbol and define a [dependent-variable rescaling of a Papperitz symbol](../../../../../../dependent-variable-rescaling-of-a-papperitz-symbol.md)

$$
\omega_1(z)=\left(\frac{z-a}{z-b}\right)^\delta\omega(z),
$$

with a consistent local choice of branch. Near $a$, the multiplier is a nonzero analytic factor times $(z-a)^\delta$, so both exponents at $a$ increase by $\delta$. Near $b$, it is a nonzero analytic factor times $(z-b)^{-\delta}$, so both exponents at $b$ decrease by $\delta$. It is analytic and nonzero near $c$, so the exponents there are unchanged. Therefore the transformed equation has symbol

$$
\boxed{
P\left\{
\begin{matrix}
a&b&c\\
\alpha+\delta&\beta-\delta&\gamma\\
\alpha'+\delta&\beta'-\delta&\gamma'
\end{matrix};z
\right\}}.
$$

The opposite shifts also preserve the [Fuchs relation](../../../../../../fuchs-relation.md).
