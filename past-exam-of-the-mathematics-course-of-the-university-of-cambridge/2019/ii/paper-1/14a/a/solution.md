<h1 id="14a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Papperitz symbol](../../../../../../papperitz-symbol.md)

$$
P\left\{
\begin{matrix}
a&b&c\\
\alpha&\beta&\gamma\\
\alpha'&\beta'&\gamma'
\end{matrix};z
\right\}
$$

represents the two-dimensional [solution space of a homogeneous linear differential equation](../../../../../../solution-space-of-a-homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md) for a second-order [Fuchsian differential equation](../../../../../../fuchsian-differential-equation.md) on the [Riemann sphere](../../../../../../riemann-sphere.md). Its only singularities are the three [regular singular points](../../../../../../regular-singular-point.md) $a,b,c$. The two entries beneath each point are its [characteristic exponents at a regular singular point](../../../../../../characteristic-exponent-at-a-regular-singular-point.md): for example, in the nonresonant case a local basis near $a$ has leading behaviours $(z-a)^\alpha$ and $(z-a)^{\alpha'}$. If one of the points is infinity, exponent $\rho$ conventionally means behaviour $z^{-\rho}$ there.

The six exponents obey the [Fuchs relation](../../../../../../fuchs-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{\alpha+\alpha'+\beta+\beta'+\gamma+\gamma'=1}.
$$

Subject to this relation, the symbol determines the corresponding Riemann differential equation; resonant differences between exponents may produce [Logarithmic Frobenius solutions](../../../../../../logarithmic-solution-from-a-repeated-frobenius-exponent.md) without changing the exponent data recorded by the symbol.
