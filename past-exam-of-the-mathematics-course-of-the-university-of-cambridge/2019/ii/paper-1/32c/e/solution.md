**No.** Consider two uncoupled oscillators with nonzero amplitudes:

$$
q_i(t)=A_i\cos(\omega_it+\phi_i),
\qquad
p_i(t)=-A_i\omega_i\sin(\omega_it+\phi_i),
\qquad i=1,2.
$$

The orbit is bounded. If it had a [period](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) $T>0$, then $\omega_1T$ and $\omega_2T$ would both be integer multiples of $2\pi$, forcing $\omega_1/\omega_2\in\mathbb Q$. Choosing an irrational frequency ratio gives an [incommensurate harmonic-oscillator orbit](../../../../../../incommensurate-harmonic-oscillator-orbit.md), which is quasiperiodic on an [invariant torus](../../../../../../invariant-torus.md) and not periodic.
