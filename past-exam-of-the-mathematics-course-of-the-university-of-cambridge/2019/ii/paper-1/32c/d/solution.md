<h1 id="32c/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) as the sum of separated one-dimensional energies

$$
H=\sum_{i=1}^n H_i,
\qquad
H_i=\frac12\left(p_i^2+\omega_i^2q_i^2\right).
$$

Each $(q_i,p_i)$ system is a [simple harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) and is integrable away from $(q_i,p_i)=(0,0)$ by part (b). The separated energies satisfy

$$
\{H_i,H_j\}=0,
$$

and their differentials are independent whenever every oscillator pair is nonzero. The [Poisson-commuting separated energies](../../../../../../poisson-commuting-separated-energies.md) therefore make the $n$-dimensional oscillator integrable. The exceptional coordinate strata have measure zero, so this proves [Liouville integrability](../../../../../../integrable-hamiltonian-system.md) in its usual almost-everywhere sense; the origin is the only point where $dH$ itself vanishes.
