<h1 id="32c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For one degree of freedom, take $F_1=H$. Antisymmetry of the [Poisson bracket](../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) gives $\{H,H\}=0$, so [energy is conserved](../../../../../../hamiltonian-conservation-law.md). The stated condition is exactly $dH\ne0$, which supplies the required functional independence. Hence the system is integrable at every such regular point.
