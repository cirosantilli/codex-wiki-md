↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**No.** The [monodromy action of a covering space](../../../../../../monodromy-action-of-a-covering-space.md) for a two-sheeted covering takes values in $S_2$. Its nonidentity transposition commutes with every subgroup of $S_2$, so the [deck transformation group as a monodromy centralizer](../../../../../../deck-transformation-group-as-a-monodromy-centralizer.md) always contains that transposition. Equivalently, every double cover has a [deck involution of a double covering](../../../../../../deck-involution-of-a-double-covering.md) that exchanges the two points in each fibre. Thus a two-sheeted cover cannot have trivial deck group.
