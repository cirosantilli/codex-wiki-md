<h1 id="34b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose the [Landau gauge](../../../../../../landau-gauge-for-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md)

$$
\mathbf A=(0,Bx,0),\qquad \phi=0.
$$

It preserves [translation invariance](../../../../../../translation-invariance.md) in the $y$-direction, so $p_y=\hbar k$ is conserved. With $p_z=0$, [magnetic minimal coupling](../../../../../../magnetic-minimal-coupling.md) gives

$$
H=\frac1{2m}\left[p_x^2+(p_y-qBx)^2\right]
=\frac{p_x^2}{2m}+\frac{m\omega_c^2}{2}(x-x_k)^2,
$$

where

$$
\omega_c=\frac{|qB|}{m},
\qquad
x_k=\frac{\hbar k}{qB}.
$$

This is a [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md), so the [Landau levels](../../../../../../landau-level.md) are

$$
\boxed{E_n=\hbar\omega_c\left(n+\frac12\right),\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots,}
$$

independent of $k$.

The periodic boundary condition in $y$ quantizes $k=2\pi r/L_y$. Requiring the guiding centre to lie in a strip of width $L_x$ gives a $k$-interval of width $|qB|L_x/\hbar$. The number of allowed integers is therefore approximately

$$
\boxed{D=\frac{L_y}{2\pi}\frac{|qB|L_x}{\hbar}
=\frac{|qB|A}{2\pi\hbar},}
$$

the [degeneracy of a Landau level](../../../../../../degeneracy-of-a-landau-level.md), up to boundary corrections.
