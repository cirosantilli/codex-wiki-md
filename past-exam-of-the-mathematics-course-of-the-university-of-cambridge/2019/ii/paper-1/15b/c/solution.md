<h1 id="15b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [number of e-folds](../../../../../../number-of-e-folds.md) $N=\log a$ satisfies

$$
dN=d\log a=H\,dt.
$$

When $\phi$ is monotone, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $dt=d\phi/\dot\phi$, and hence

$$
dN=\frac{H}{\dot\phi}\,d\phi.
$$

Using the two [slow-roll equations](../../../../../../slow-roll-approximation.md), $3H\dot\phi\simeq-V'(\phi)$ and $H^2\simeq8\pi GV/3$, eliminates both $H$ and time:

$$
\boxed{dN\simeq-8\pi G\frac{V(\phi)}{V'(\phi)}\,d\phi}.
$$

This is the differential form of the [slow-roll e-fold count](../../../../../../slow-roll-e-fold-count.md).
