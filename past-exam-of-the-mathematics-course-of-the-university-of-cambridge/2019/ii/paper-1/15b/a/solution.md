<h1 id="15b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $H=\dot a/a$, differentiation gives the [Hubble parameter identity](../../../../../../hubble-parameter-identity.md)

$$
\dot H=\frac{\ddot a}{a}-H^2.
$$

Subtracting the given [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) from the [Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) therefore yields

$$
\dot H
=-\frac{8\pi G}{3}(\dot\phi^2-V)
-\frac{8\pi G}{3}\left(\frac12\dot\phi^2+V\right)
=-4\pi G\dot\phi^2.
$$

On the other hand, differentiating the Friedmann equation gives

$$
2H\dot H
=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\dot\phi(\ddot\phi+V'(\phi)).
$$

Substitute $\dot H=-4\pi G\dot\phi^2$ and divide by $(8\pi G/3)\dot\phi$, which is permitted by the assumption $\dot\phi\ne0$. The result is the homogeneous [inflaton equation of motion](../../../../../../inflaton-equation-of-motion.md)

$$
\boxed{\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi+V'(\phi)=0}.
$$

The middle term is [Hubble friction](../../../../../../hubble-friction.md).
