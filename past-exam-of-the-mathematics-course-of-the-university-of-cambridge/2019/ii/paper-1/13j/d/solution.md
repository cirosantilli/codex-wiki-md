<h1 id="13j/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Both variables are [ordinal categorical variables](../../../../../../ordinal-categorical-variable.md), and the proposed alternative is directional: higher scores should tend to occur at higher prices. Assign increasing numerical scores to low, medium, high price and to poor, good, excellent quality, and use a one-sided [linear-by-linear association test](../../../../../../linear-by-linear-association-test.md). Its statistic is based on the sample covariance, or equivalently the correlation, of those scores under the fixed margins.

This test uses one degree of freedom and directs its rejection region toward positive association. The four-degree-of-freedom [likelihood-ratio test of independence in a contingency table](../../../../../../likelihood-ratio-test-of-independence-in-a-contingency-table.md) spends power on every possible departure from independence, including unordered and negative patterns, so the ordered one-sided test is more powerful when the stated alternative is credible.
