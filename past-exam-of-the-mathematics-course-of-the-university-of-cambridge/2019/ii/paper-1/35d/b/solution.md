<h1 id="35d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At fixed volume, [entropy from heat capacity](../../../../../../entropy-from-heat-capacity.md) obeys $dS=C(T)dT/T$. The [Third law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../third-law-of-thermodynamics.md) fixes $S(0)=0$ when the ground state is nondegenerate, so below the transition

$$
S(T_c^-)=\int_0^{T_c}\frac{\alpha T}{T}\,dT=\alpha T_c.
$$

Above the transition,

$$
S(T)=S(T_c^+)+\int_{T_c}^{T}\frac\beta{T'}\,dT'
=S(T_c^+)+\beta\log\frac{T}{T_c}.
$$

For a transition with no [latent heat](../../../../../../latent-heat.md), entropy is continuous, and comparison with $S=\beta\log T+\gamma$ gives the directly testable relation

$$
\boxed{\gamma=\alpha T_c-\beta\log T_c.}
$$

Thus calorimetric measurements of $C(T)$ and $T_c$ verify $\gamma$. More generally, if a measurable latent heat $L$ is present, $S(T_c^+)-S(T_c^-)=L/T_c$, and the formula becomes

$$
\boxed{\gamma=\alpha T_c+\frac{L}{T_c}-\beta\log T_c.}
$$
