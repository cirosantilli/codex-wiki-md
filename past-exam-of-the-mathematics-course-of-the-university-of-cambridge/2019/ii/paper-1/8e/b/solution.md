<h1 id="8e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md),

$$
L=\frac m2(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2+\dot z^2)-f(r,\phi-kz).
$$

The screw transformation

$$
z\mapsto z+s,
\qquad
\phi\mapsto\phi+ks
$$

leaves $r$ and $\phi-kz$ fixed, so it is the [helical symmetry of a potential](../../../../../../helical-symmetry-of-a-potential.md). Its infinitesimal generator has $\xi_z=1$ and $\xi_\phi=k$. Noether's theorem therefore gives the conserved quantity

$$
\boxed{p_z+kp_\phi
=m\dot z+kmr^2\dot\phi}.
$$

This is a linear combination of axial [linear momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) and axial [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md).
