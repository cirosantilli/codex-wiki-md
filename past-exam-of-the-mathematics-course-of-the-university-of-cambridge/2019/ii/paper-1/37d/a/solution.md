<h1 id="37d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Because the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) is torsion-free, its [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) satisfy $\Gamma^\mu_{\rho\alpha}=\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\rho}$. Substitute the stated coordinate expression for each term in the cyclic sum

$$
R^\mu{}_{\rho\alpha\beta}
+R^\mu{}_{\beta\rho\alpha}
+R^\mu{}_{\alpha\beta\rho}.
$$

The partial-derivative terms cancel pairwise by the lower-index symmetry of $\Gamma$, and the quadratic terms cancel after relabelling their dummy indices. Therefore

$$
\boxed{R^\mu{}_{\rho\alpha\beta}
+R^\mu{}_{\beta\rho\alpha}
+R^\mu{}_{\alpha\beta\rho}=0,}
$$

which is the [first Bianchi identity](../../../../../../first-bianchi-identity.md).
