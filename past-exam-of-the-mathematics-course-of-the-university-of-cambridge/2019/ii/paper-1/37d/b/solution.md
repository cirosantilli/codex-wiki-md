<h1 id="37d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expanding the two [covariant derivatives](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) of a vector, the second partial derivatives cancel in the commutator. The remaining derivatives and products of Christoffel symbols combine into the coordinate definition of the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md), giving the [Ricci identity](../../../../../../curvature-commutator-on-a-covariant-tensor.md)

$$
\boxed{2\nabla_{[\alpha}\nabla_{\beta]}V^\gamma
=[\nabla_\alpha,\nabla_\beta]V^\gamma
=R^\gamma{}_{\rho\alpha\beta}V^\rho.}
$$

For a type-$(2,0)$ tensor, the connection acts on both upper indices, so the [curvature commutator on a contravariant tensor](../../../../../../curvature-commutator-on-a-contravariant-tensor.md) is

$$
\boxed{2\nabla_{[\alpha}\nabla_{\beta]}T^{\mu\nu}
=R^\mu{}_{\rho\alpha\beta}T^{\rho\nu}
+R^\nu{}_{\rho\alpha\beta}T^{\mu\rho}.}
$$
