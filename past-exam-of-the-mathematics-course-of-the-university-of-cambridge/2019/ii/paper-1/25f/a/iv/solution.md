↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The irreducible [affine hypersurface](../../../../../../../affine-hypersurface.md) $y^2-z^3=0$ in the $(y,z)$-plane has dimension one, while a closed point has dimension zero. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\dim C=1,
\qquad
\dim\{P\}=\dim\{Q\}=0}.
$$
