↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The curve $C$ has dimension one. Its tangent has dimension one at every point except the origin and dimension two at the origin. By the [smooth locus of a variety](../../../../../../../smooth-locus-of-a-variety.md),

$$
\boxed{C_{\mathrm{sm}}=C\setminus\{(0,0,0)\}},
$$

and the origin is its unique [singular point of an algebraic variety](../../../../../../../singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md). Each reduced point component has dimension zero and zero-dimensional tangent space, so both $P$ and $Q$ are smooth on their respective components.
