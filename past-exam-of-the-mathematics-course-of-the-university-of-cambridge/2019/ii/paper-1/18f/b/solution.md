<h1 id="18f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

View every $k$-automorphism $\sigma_i:K\to K$ as an element of the $K$-vector space

$$
\operatorname{Hom}_k(K,K),
$$

where $K$ acts by multiplying output values. If $n=[K:k]$ and $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ is a $k$-basis of $K$, a $k$-linear map is determined freely by its $n$ values on that basis. Thus

$$
\dim_K\operatorname{Hom}_k(K,K)=n.
$$

Part (a) says that the $m$ distinct automorphisms are [linearly independent vectors](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) in this space, so

$$
\boxed{m\leq[K:k]}.
$$

Now let $k=K^G$, the [fixed field](../../../../../../fixed-field.md) of the finite automorphism group $G$. Its elements are fixed by every member of $G$, so the members of $G$ are distinct $K^G$-automorphisms of $K$. Applying the inequality gives the [automorphism-count bound for a finite field extension](../../../../../../automorphism-count-bound-for-a-finite-field-extension.md)

$$
\boxed{|G|\leq[K:K^G]}.
$$
