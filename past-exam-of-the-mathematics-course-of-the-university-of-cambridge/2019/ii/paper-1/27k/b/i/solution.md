<h1 id="27k/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) $A$, invariance in distribution gives

$$
\varphi_X(A^Tu)
=\varphi_{AX}(u)
=\varphi_X(u).
$$

The orthogonal group is transitive on each Euclidean sphere, so $\varphi_X(u)$ depends only on $\lVert u\rVert^2$. Define

$$
f(r)=\varphi_X(\sqrt r,e_1),
\qquad r\geq0.
$$

The [continuity of a characteristic function](../../../../../../../continuity-of-a-characteristic-function.md) makes $f$ continuous. Taking $A=-I$ also gives $X\stackrel d=-X$, and hence

$$
\varphi_X(u)=\varphi_X(-u)=\overline{\varphi_X(u)},
$$

so $f$ is real-valued. Thus

$$
\boxed{\varphi_X(u)=f(u_1^2+\cdots+u_d^2)},
$$

as for every [orthogonally invariant random vector](../../../../../../../orthogonally-invariant-random-vector.md).
