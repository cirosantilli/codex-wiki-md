↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Combining the preceding parts,

$$
\varphi_X(u)=e^{-\alpha\lVert u\rVert^2}.
$$

This is the characteristic function of $N(0,2\alpha I_d)$. The [uniqueness theorem for characteristic functions](../../../../../../../uniqueness-theorem-for-characteristic-functions.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{X\sim N(0,2\alpha I_d)}.
$$

When $\alpha=0$, this means the degenerate distribution concentrated at the origin. This proves the [Maxwell characterization of the normal distribution](../../../../../../../maxwell-characterization-of-the-normal-distribution.md).
