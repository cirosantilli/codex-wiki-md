Assume (ii). A [martingale](../../../../../../../martingale-split.md) has constant [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) by the [tower property of conditional expectation](../../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md), so for every [stopping time](../../../../../../../stopping-time.md) $\tau$ and every $n$,

$$
\mathbb E[M_{n\wedge\tau}]=\mathbb E[M_0].
$$

This is (iii).
