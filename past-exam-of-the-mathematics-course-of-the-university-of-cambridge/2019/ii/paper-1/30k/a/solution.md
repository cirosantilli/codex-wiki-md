↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The pair $(M_n,\mathcal F_n)_{n\geq0}$ is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) if $(\mathcal F_n)$ is a [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md), $(M_n)$ is an [adapted process](../../../../../../adapted-process.md), every $M_n$ is [integrable](../../../../../../integrable-random-variable.md), and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[M_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=M_{n-1}\quad\text{almost surely for every }n\geq1.}
$$
