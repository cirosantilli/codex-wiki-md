↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

This language is context-free. It is the language of [even-length palindromes](../../../../../../../context-free-grammar-for-even-palindromes.md) over $\{a,b\}$, generated by the [context-free grammar](../../../../../../../context-free-grammar.md)

$$
S\to aSa\mid bSb\mid\epsilon.
$$

Every derivation adds matching outer symbols, and removing the matching first and last symbols gives the converse induction.
