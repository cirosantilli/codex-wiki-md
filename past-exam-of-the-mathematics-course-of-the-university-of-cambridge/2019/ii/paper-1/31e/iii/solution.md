<h1 id="31e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Away from $x=\pm1$, the sign of $\dot x=x^2-1$ is fixed within each of the three invariant strips, so $x(t)$ is strictly monotone. On $x=\pm1$, the equation is $\dot y=\pm ky$, whose nonconstant solutions are exponential and hence not periodic. Therefore the [monotone coordinate excludes periodic orbits](../../../../../../monotone-coordinate-excludes-periodic-orbits.md), and there are

$$
\boxed{\text{no nonconstant periodic orbits for any }k.}
$$
