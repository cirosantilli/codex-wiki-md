<h1 id="19i/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $G=C_3=\langle g\rangle$ and let $e_0,e_1,e_2$ be the standard basis of its real [regular representation](../../../../../../../regular-representation.md), with $ge_j=e_{j+1}$ modulo three. Set

$$
U=\operatorname{span}\{e_0+e_1+e_2\},
\qquad
W=\{x_0e_0+x_1e_1+x_2e_2:x_0+x_1+x_2=0\}.
$$

Both are invariant, they are orthogonal, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb RG=U\oplus W}.
$$

The line $U$ is the [trivial representation](../../../../../../../trivial-representation.md). On the plane $W$, the generator acts as a rotation through $2\pi/3$. Such a rotation has no real eigenline, so $W$ has no nonzero proper invariant real subspace and is irreducible.
