↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

When $n$ is odd there is no alternating sign mode. The same cosine-sine construction gives invariant irreducible planes $W_k$ for

$$
1\leq k\leq\frac{n-1}{2},
$$

again pairwise nonisomorphic because their generator traces are distinct. The real Fourier decomposition is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb RC_n
=\langle c_0\rangle
\oplus\bigoplus_{k=1}^{(n-1)/2}W_k}.
$$

Its dimensions sum to $1+2(n-1)/2=n$. Thus the [real regular representation of a finite cyclic group](../../../../../../../real-regular-representation-of-a-finite-cyclic-group.md) has one one-dimensional irreducible summand and $(n-1)/2$ nonisomorphic two-dimensional irreducible summands.
