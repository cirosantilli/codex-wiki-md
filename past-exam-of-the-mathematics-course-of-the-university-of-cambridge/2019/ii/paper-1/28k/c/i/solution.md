<h1 id="28k/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [jump chain](../../../../../../../jump-chain.md) of $(X_t)$ is exactly $(Y_m)$: from $i$ it visits $j$ next with probability $p_{ij}$. Consequently the two chains have exactly the same directed paths of positive-probability transitions. Thus

$$
\boxed{X\text{ is irreducible}\iff Y\text{ is irreducible}.}
$$
