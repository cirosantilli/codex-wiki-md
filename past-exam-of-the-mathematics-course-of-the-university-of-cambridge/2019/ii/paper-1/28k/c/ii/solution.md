<h1 id="28k/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because $J_m\to\infty$ almost surely, no infinite portion of the [jump chain](../../../../../../../jump-chain.md) is hidden inside a finite time interval. After either chain leaves a state $i$, the event that it ever returns is therefore the same event for both chains. Hence [irreducibility and recurrence pass between a CTMC and its jump chain](../../../../../../../irreducibility-and-recurrence-pass-between-a-ctmc-and-its-jump-chain.md) gives

$$
\boxed{X\text{ is recurrent}\iff Y\text{ is recurrent}.}
$$
