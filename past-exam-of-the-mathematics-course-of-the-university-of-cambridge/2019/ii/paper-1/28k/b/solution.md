A [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is [irreducible](../../../../../../irreducible-markov-chain.md) if every state can reach every other state with positive probability. A state $i$ is [recurrent](../../../../../../recurrent-state.md) if, after leaving $i$, the chain returns to $i$ [with probability one](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md); an irreducible chain is recurrent if one, and hence every, state is recurrent. A recurrent state is [positive recurrent](../../../../../../positive-recurrent-state.md) if its expected continuous [first return time](../../../../../../first-return-time.md) is finite, and an irreducible chain is positive recurrent when all its states are.
