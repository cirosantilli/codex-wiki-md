<h1 id="39a/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At $t=t_c$, the piston velocity reaches

$$
u_v=-\frac{2c_0}{\gamma-1}.
$$

The simple-wave relation then gives $c=0$ and $\rho=0$. For later times the formal continuation would require negative sound speed, so the gas can no longer follow the piston. By [vacuum formation behind a receding piston](../../../../../../../vacuum-formation-behind-a-receding-piston.md), a vacuum region opens between $X_p(t)$ and the gas edge

$$
\boxed{x_v(t)=X_p(t_c)-\frac{2c_0}{\gamma-1}(t-t_c).}
$$

The earlier rarefaction solution remains to the right of $x_v(t)$, while $X_p(t)<x<x_v(t)$ is vacuum.
