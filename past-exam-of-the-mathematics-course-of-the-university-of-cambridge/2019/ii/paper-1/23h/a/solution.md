<h1 id="23h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For each $n\in\mathbb N$, the open interval

$$
\left(n-\frac12,n+\frac12\right)
$$

meets $\mathbb N$ only in $\{n\}$. Thus every singleton is open in the [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $\mathbb N$. Every subset $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ is the union $A=\bigcup_{n\in A}\{n\}$ and is therefore open. Hence the induced topology is the [discrete topology](../../../../../../discrete-space.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T=\mathcal P(\mathbb N)}.
$$
