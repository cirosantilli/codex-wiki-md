<h1 id="23h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Because every subset of $\mathbb N$ is open, the [Borel sigma-algebra of a discrete space](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra-of-a-discrete-space.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal B(\mathbb N)=\mathcal P(\mathbb N)}.
$$

If $f:\mathbb N\to\mathbb R$ or $f:\mathbb N\to[0,\infty]$ and $B$ is a Borel subset of the codomain, then $f^{-1}(B)$ is some subset of $\mathbb N$, hence is Borel. Thus every such function is a [Borel measurable function](../../../../../../borel-measurable-function.md).
