<h1 id="16i/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** For each $y\in x$, the [singleton set](../../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md) $\{y\}$ is a finite subset of $x$, so the hypothesis gives

$$
\operatorname{rank}(y)+1
=\operatorname{rank}(\{y\})\leq c.
$$

Taking the [supremum](../../../../../../supremum.md) over all $y\in x$ yields

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}(x)
=\sup_{y\in x}\bigl(\operatorname{rank}(y)+1\bigr)
\leq c.}
$$
