<h1 id="16i/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**True.** Regard each [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\alpha$ as its von Neumann representative, the set of all smaller ordinals. [Transfinite induction](../../../../../../transfinite-induction.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{rank}(\alpha)
=\sup_{\beta<\alpha}\bigl(\operatorname{rank}(\beta)+1\bigr)
=\sup_{\beta<\alpha}(\beta+1)
=\alpha.
$$

**Thus the set $\alpha$ itself has rank $\alpha$.**
