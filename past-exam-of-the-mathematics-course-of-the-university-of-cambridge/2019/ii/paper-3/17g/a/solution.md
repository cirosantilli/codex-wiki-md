↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [graph](../../../../../../graph-split.md) $G=(V,E)$ is [bipartite](../../../../../../bipartite-graph.md) when its set of [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) can be partitioned as

$$
V=A\sqcup B
$$

so that every [edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) has one endpoint in $A$ and the other in $B$. Equivalently, $G$ has a proper [graph colouring](../../../../../../graph-coloring.md) with at most two colours.
