<h1 id="13c/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $\epsilon(n)=an+b$, [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) gives $\langle\epsilon(n)\rangle=am+b$. The mean therefore satisfies

$$
m'=\lambda+kb-(\beta-ak)m.
$$

If $\beta>ak$, the coefficient of $m$ is negative and the [stable equilibrium](../../../../../../../stable-equilibrium.md) is

$$
\boxed{m(t)\longrightarrow m_*=\frac{\lambda+kb}{\beta-ak}.}
$$

If $\beta<ak$, the positive feedback from batch addition dominates deaths and $m(t)$ grows exponentially at rate $ak-\beta$. At the threshold $\beta=ak$, it grows linearly as $m(t)=m(0)+(\lambda+kb)t$.
