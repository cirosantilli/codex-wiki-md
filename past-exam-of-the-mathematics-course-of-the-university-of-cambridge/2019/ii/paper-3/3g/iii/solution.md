<h1 id="3g/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $p>1/2$, complementing every received bit converts the channel into a binary symmetric channel with crossover probability $1-p<1/2$. This deterministic and invertible output relabelling does not change the [channel capacity](../../../../../../channel-capacity.md). Since $h_2(p)=h_2(1-p)$,

$$
\boxed{C(p)=1-h_2(p)=C(1-p).}
$$

**Thus the capacity rises again from zero as $p$ increases beyond $1/2$, reaching one at $p=1$, where every bit is flipped deterministically and can be recovered by complementation.**
