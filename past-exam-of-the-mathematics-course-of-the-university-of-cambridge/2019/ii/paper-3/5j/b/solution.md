<h1 id="5j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For independent observations from an [exponential dispersion family](../../../../../../exponential-dispersion-model.md) with common known [dispersion parameter](../../../../../../dispersion-parameter.md) $\sigma^2$, the [generalized linear model](../../../../../../generalized-linear-model.md) log-likelihood is

$$
\boxed{
\ell(\beta)
=\sum_{i=1}^n
\left[
\frac{y_i\theta_i-b(\theta_i)}{\sigma^2}
+c(y_i,\sigma^2)
\right],
}
$$

where $\mu_i=b'(\theta_i)$ and the [link function](../../../../../../link-function.md) satisfies $g(\mu_i)=\eta_i=x_i^T\beta$.
