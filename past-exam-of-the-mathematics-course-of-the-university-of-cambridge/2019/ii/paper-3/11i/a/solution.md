↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [primitive root](../../../../../../primitive-root-modulo-n.md) modulo $p$ is an integer $g$ whose residue class generates the [multiplicative group of a finite field](../../../../../../multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_p^\times$. Equivalently, its [multiplicative order](../../../../../../multiplicative-order.md) modulo $p$ is $p-1$, so

$$
\boxed{\{1,g,g^2,\ldots,g^{p-2}\}=\mathbb F_p^\times.}
$$
