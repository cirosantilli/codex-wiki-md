<h1 id="19i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On the orbit $G/H$, an element $g\in G$ fixes the coset $xH$ exactly when

$$
gxH=xH
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x^{-1}gx\in H.
$$

The [character of a representation](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) of the corresponding [permutation representation](../../../../../../permutation-representation.md) therefore is

$$
\boxed{
\chi_{\mathbb C(G/H)}(g)
=|(G/H)^g|
=\frac1{|H|}
|\{x\in G:x^{-1}gx\in H\}|
=(1_H\mathbin{\uparrow}^G)(g).}
$$

This is the [permutation character of a coset action](../../../../../../permutation-character-of-a-coset-action.md).
