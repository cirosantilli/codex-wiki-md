↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The vector

$$
\mathbf1_X=\sum_{x\in X}x
$$

is fixed by every element of $G$, so $\mathbb C\mathbf1_X\cong\mathbb C_G$ is a [trivial representation](../../../../../../../trivial-representation.md). The coefficient-sum subspace

$$
V=\left\{\sum_{x\in X}a_xx:\sum_{x\in X}a_x=0\right\}
$$

is also $G$-invariant, and every vector has a unique decomposition into a constant vector and an element of $V$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb C X=\mathbb C_G\oplus V.}
$$

Here $V$ is the [augmentation subrepresentation of a permutation representation](../../../../../../../augmentation-subrepresentation-of-a-permutation-representation.md).
