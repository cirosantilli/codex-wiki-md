<h1 id="19i/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $D$ represent $H\backslash G/H$. Combining [Frobenius reciprocity](../../../../../../../frobenius-reciprocity.md) with the [Mackey's theorem](../../../../../../../mackey-restriction-formula.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left\langle1_H\mathbin{\uparrow}^G,
1_H\mathbin{\uparrow}^G\right\rangle_G
&=\left\langle1_H,
\operatorname{Res}_H^G(1_H\mathbin{\uparrow}^G)\right\rangle_H\\
&=\sum_{x\in D}
\left\langle1_H,
1_{H\cap xHx^{-1}}\mathbin{\uparrow}^H\right\rangle_H\\
&=\sum_{x\in D}
\left\langle1_{H\cap xHx^{-1}},
1_{H\cap xHx^{-1}}\right\rangle_{H\cap xHx^{-1}}\\
&=|D|.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{
\left\langle1_H\mathbin{\uparrow}^G,
1_H\mathbin{\uparrow}^G\right\rangle_G
=|H\backslash G/H|.}
$$
