<h1 id="25h/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The coordinate tangent vectors are

$$
\phi_u=(-f\sin u,f\cos u,0),
\qquad
\phi_v=(f'\cos u,f'\sin u,g'),
$$

where $f,f',g'$ are evaluated at $v$. Their [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md) is

$$
\phi_u\times\phi_v
=\bigl(fg'\cos u,fg'\sin u,-ff'\bigr),
$$

and therefore

$$
\lVert\phi_u\times\phi_v\rVert
=f(v)\sqrt{f'(v)^2+g'(v)^2}>0.
$$

The positivity follows because $f(v)>0$ and the profile is a [regular curve](../../../../../../../regular-curve.md). Hence $d\phi$ has rank two everywhere, so $\phi$ is an [immersion](../../../../../../../immersion.md). The angle interval $(0,2\pi)$ and the absence of profile self-intersections make $\phi$ injective with a continuous local inverse. It is therefore an [embedded surface parametrization](../../../../../../../embedded-surface-parametrization.md), and its image is a [regular surface](../../../../../../../smooth-surface.md).
