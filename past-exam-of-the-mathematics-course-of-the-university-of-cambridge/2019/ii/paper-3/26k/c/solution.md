<h1 id="26k/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose $M<\infty$ such that $|X|,|Y|\leq M$ almost surely. Equality of the positive moments, together with equality of the zeroth moments $\mathbb E[1]=1$, gives

$$
\mathbb E[p(X)]=\mathbb E[p(Y)]
$$

for every [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $p$. By the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](../../../../../../weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md), every continuous function on $[-M,M]$ is a uniform limit of polynomials. Expectations preserve this limit, so $X$ and $Y$ have equal expectations for every continuous function on their common compact support. The [moment determinacy on a compact interval](../../../../../../moment-determinacy-on-a-compact-interval.md) therefore gives equality of their laws.
