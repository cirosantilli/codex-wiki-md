<h1 id="32c/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $f=1$, the translation generator satisfies

$$
\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}V_1(S-1)=0.
$$

For $V_2$ and $V_3$, restriction to $S=1$ gives respectively $-2$ and $-4x$, which do not vanish identically. **Thus $S[u]=1$ has the symmetry $V_1$ among the three listed generators.**
