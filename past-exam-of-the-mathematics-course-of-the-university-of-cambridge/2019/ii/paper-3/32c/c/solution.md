<h1 id="32c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the [Schwarzian derivative](../../../../../../schwarzian-derivative.md) as

$$
S=\frac{u_3}{u_1}-\frac32\frac{u_2^2}{u_1^2}.
$$

The first prolonged generator is $\partial_x$, and $S$ has no explicit dependence on $x$, so $\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}V_1(S)=0$. Under $\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}V_2$, each $u_j$ has weight $-j$. Both terms in $S$ therefore have weight $-2$, giving $\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}V_2(S)=-2S$. Substitution of

$$
\eta_1=-2xu_1,\qquad
\eta_2=-2u_1-4xu_2,\qquad
\eta_3=-6u_2-6xu_3
$$

for $V_3$ gives $\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}V_3(S)=-4xS$. Therefore the [projective transformation weights of the Schwarzian derivative](../../../../../../projective-transformation-weights-of-the-schwarzian-derivative.md) are

$$
\boxed{c_1=0,\qquad c_2=-2,\qquad c_3=-4x.}
$$

For an equation $\Delta=S-f(x)=0$, the [Lie point symmetry of an ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../lie-point-symmetry-of-an-ordinary-differential-equation.md) criterion is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}V_i(\Delta)=0
\qquad\text{whenever }\Delta=0.}
$$
