<h1 id="39a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [linear homentropic acoustic equations](../../../../../../linear-homentropic-acoustic-equations.md) are

$$
\rho'_t+\rho_0\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf u=0,
\qquad
\rho_0\mathbf u_t=-\nabla p',
\qquad
p'=c_0^2\rho'.
$$

Differentiate the first equation in time, take the divergence of the second, and use the third. This eliminates $\rho'$ and $\mathbf u$ and gives the [acoustic pressure wave equation](../../../../../../acoustic-pressure-wave-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{p'_{tt}=c_0^2\nabla^2p'.}
$$
