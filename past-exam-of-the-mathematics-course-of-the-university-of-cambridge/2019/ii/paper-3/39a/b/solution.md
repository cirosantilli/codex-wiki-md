<h1 id="39a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a progressive [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md), write

$$
p'=P e^{i(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x-\omega t)},
\qquad
\mathbf u=U\widehat{\mathbf k}e^{i(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x-\omega t)}.
$$

The momentum equation gives $\rho_0\omega U=kP$, while the [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) is $\omega=c_0k$. Therefore the pressure and longitudinal velocity amplitudes obey

$$
\boxed{\frac{P}{U}=\rho_0c_0=:Z.}
$$

The constant $Z$ is the [acoustic impedance](../../../../../../acoustic-impedance.md).
