<h1 id="21h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For each $n\geq0$, define

$$
E_n=\{z\in\mathbb C:f^{(n)}(z)=0\}.
$$

Every $f^{(n)}$ is an [entire function](../../../../../../entire-function.md), hence continuous, so $E_n$ is closed. The hypothesis says

$$
\mathbb C=\bigcup_{n=0}^{\infty}E_n.
$$

The [complex plane](../../../../../../complex-plane.md) is a [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md). The [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md) therefore implies that some $E_N$ has nonempty interior. Thus $f^{(N)}$ vanishes on an open set, and the [identity theorem](../../../../../../identity-theorem.md) gives

$$
f^{(N)}\equiv0
$$

on all of $\mathbb C$. If $N=0$, then $f=0$; otherwise the [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) of $f$ has no term of degree $N$ or higher. Hence $f$ is a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $N-1$. This is the [pointwise vanishing derivative criterion for a polynomial](../../../../../../pointwise-vanishing-derivative-criterion-for-a-polynomial.md).
