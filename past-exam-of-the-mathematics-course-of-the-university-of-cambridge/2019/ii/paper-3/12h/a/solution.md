<h1 id="12h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fix an acceptable effective enumeration $f_{e,n}$ of the $n$-ary [partial computable functions](../../../../../../computable-function.md). The [S-m-n theorem](../../../../../../smn-theorem.md) states that for every $m,n\geq1$ there is a [total computable function](../../../../../../total-computable-function.md) $s_n^m$ such that

$$
f_{s_n^m(e,x_1,\ldots,x_m),n}(y_1,\ldots,y_n)
\simeq f_{e,m+n}(x_1,\ldots,x_m,y_1,\ldots,y_n),
$$

where $\simeq$ means that both sides have the same value when defined and are otherwise both undefined.

The [recursion theorem](../../../../../../kleene-s-recursion-theorem.md) states that every total computable transformation $h$ of program indices has a semantic fixed point: there is an $e$ such that

$$
\boxed{f_{e,1}\simeq f_{h(e),1}.}
$$
