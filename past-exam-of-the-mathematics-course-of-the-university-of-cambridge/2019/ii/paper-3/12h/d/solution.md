<h1 id="12h/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For each $x$, let $h(x)$ be an effectively obtained index of the partial function

$$
y\longmapsto
\begin{cases}
0,&y<x^2,\\
\text{undefined},&y\geq x^2.
\end{cases}
$$

The existence of the total computable index transformation $h$ follows from the [S-m-n theorem](../../../../../../smn-theorem.md). By the [recursion theorem](../../../../../../kleene-s-recursion-theorem.md), some $m$ satisfies $f_{m,1}\simeq f_{h(m),1}$. Therefore its domain is

$$
W_m=\{0,1,\ldots,m^2-1\},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{|W_m|=m^2.}
$$
