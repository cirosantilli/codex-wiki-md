<h1 id="12h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply the [S-m-n theorem](../../../../../../smn-theorem.md) to $g$. It gives a total computable function $h$ such that

$$
f_{h(x),1}(y)\simeq g(x,y).
$$

The [recursion theorem](../../../../../../kleene-s-recursion-theorem.md) supplies an index $e$ satisfying $f_{e,1}\simeq f_{h(e),1}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{f_{e,1}(y)\simeq g(e,y)\quad\text{for every }y\in\mathbb N_0.}
$$
