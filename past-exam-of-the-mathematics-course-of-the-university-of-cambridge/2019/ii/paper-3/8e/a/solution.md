<h1 id="8e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let

$$
\omega=\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}},
\qquad
H(x,p)=\frac{p^2}{2m}+\frac12m\omega^2x^2.
$$

An orbit of energy $E>0$ is an ellipse in [phase space](../../../../../../phase-space.md), with intercepts $x=\pm\sqrt{2E/(m\omega^2)}$ and $p=\pm\sqrt{2mE}$. Its enclosed area is $2\pi E/\omega$, so the [action variable](../../../../../../action-variable.md) is

$$
I=\frac1{2\pi}\oint p\,dx=\frac E\omega.
$$

The transformation

$$
\boxed{x=\sqrt{\frac{2I}{m\omega}}\sin\theta,
\qquad
p=\sqrt{2m\omega I}\cos\theta}
$$

is canonical and gives the [action-angle variables](../../../../../../action-angle-variables.md). Substitution yields

$$
\boxed{H(I)=\omega I,\qquad \dot I=0,\qquad\dot\theta=\omega.}
$$
