<h1 id="15d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\omega_d=e^{2\pi i/d}$. The [quantum Fourier transform](../../../../../../quantum-fourier-transform.md) modulo $d$ is the [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{QFT}_d|x\rangle
=\frac1{\sqrt d}\sum_{y=0}^{d-1}\omega_d^{xy}|y\rangle.}
$$

Its images $|\xi_x\rangle=\operatorname{QFT}_d|x\rangle$ form the Fourier basis.
