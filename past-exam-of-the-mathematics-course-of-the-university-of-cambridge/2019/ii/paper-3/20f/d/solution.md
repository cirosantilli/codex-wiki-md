<h1 id="20f/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the pair from part (c), every simplex of dimension below $n$ lies in $L_{n-1}$. Consequently the [relative simplicial chain complex](../../../../../../relative-simplicial-chain-complex.md) has

$$
C_k(K_n,L_{n-1})
\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&k=n,\\
0,&k\ne n,
\end{cases}
$$

where the degree-$n$ generator is the class of $[v_0\cdots v_n]$. Its relative boundary is zero because every codimension-one face lies in $L_{n-1}$. All differentials therefore vanish, and

$$
\boxed{
H_k(K_n,L_{n-1})
\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&k=n,\\
0,&k\ne n.
\end{cases}}
$$

This is the [relative homology of a simplex and its boundary](../../../../../../relative-homology-of-a-simplex-and-its-boundary.md). Equivalently, the [long exact sequence in relative homology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-relative-homology.md), together with the fact that $|K_n|$ is a [contractible space](../../../../../../contractible-space.md) and the [homology of a sphere](../../../../../../homology-of-a-sphere.md) $|L_{n-1}|\cong S^{n-1}$, gives the [reduced homology](../../../../../../reduced-homology.md) identification $H_k(K_n,L_{n-1})\cong\widetilde H_{k-1}(S^{n-1})$.
