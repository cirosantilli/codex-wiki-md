<h1 id="20f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $K_n$ be the abstract [simplex](../../../../../../simplex.md) on the vertex set

$$
\{v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_n\};
$$

thus every subset of these vertices is a simplex. Let $L_{n-1}$ be its [boundary subcomplex of a simplex](../../../../../../boundary-subcomplex-of-a-simplex.md), consisting of all proper faces, so the only simplex of $K_n$ absent from $L_{n-1}$ is the full $n$-simplex $[v_0\cdots v_n]$.

The [geometric realization of a simplicial complex](../../../../../../geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) $|K_n|$ is the usual solid geometric $n$-simplex, which is homeomorphic to the [closed disk](../../../../../../closed-disc.md) $D^n$. Under such a homeomorphism its topological boundary is $|L_{n-1}|$, and

$$
\boxed{|L_{n-1}|\cong S^{n-1}.}
$$
