<h1 id="33b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The states $|2\rangle$ and $|3\rangle$ are degenerate and couple to $|1\rangle$ with amplitudes $\varepsilon V_0$ and $\varepsilon^2V_0$. The [bright and dark state reduction of a star-coupled Hamiltonian](../../../../../../bright-and-dark-state-reduction-of-a-star-coupled-hamiltonian.md) gives one exact dark eigenstate proportional to $-\varepsilon|2\rangle+|3\rangle$, with energy $E$. The orthogonal bright state couples to $|1\rangle$ with magnitude

$$
q=V_0\sqrt{\varepsilon^2+\varepsilon^4}
=\varepsilon V_0\sqrt{1+\varepsilon^2}.
$$

Diagonalizing the remaining two-dimensional Hermitian matrix gives the exact spectrum

$$
\boxed{
E,\qquad
E_\pm=\frac{E_1+E}{2}
\pm\sqrt{\frac{(E_1-E)^2}{4}
+\varepsilon^2V_0^2(1+\varepsilon^2)}.}
$$

Writing $\Delta=E_1-E\ne0$ and expanding the branches according to the eigenvalue to which they converge,

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\text{near }E_1}
&=E_1+\frac{\varepsilon^2V_0^2}{E_1-E}
+O(\varepsilon^4),\\
E_{\text{near }E}
&=E+\frac{\varepsilon^2V_0^2}{E-E_1}
+O(\varepsilon^4),\\
E_{\rm dark}&=E.
\end{aligned}
$$

These are exactly the three perturbative energies from part b through order $\varepsilon^2$.
