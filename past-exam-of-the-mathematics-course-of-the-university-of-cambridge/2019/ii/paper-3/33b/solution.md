<h1 id="33b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [33B](../33b.md)

For a normalized eigenstate $|n\rangle$ with a [nondegenerate energy eigenvalue](../../../../../nondegenerate-energy-eigenvalue.md), [first-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](../../../../../first-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) and [second-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](../../../../../second-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) give

$$
\boxed{
E_n(H)=E_n+\langle n|V|n\rangle
+\sum_{m\ne n}
\frac{|\langle m|V|n\rangle|^2}{E_n-E_m}
+O(V^3).}
$$
