<h1 id="33b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $E_1=E_2=E$. Within the degenerate eigenspace spanned by $|1\rangle,|2\rangle$, the perturbation is

$$
V_{\rm deg}
=\varepsilon V_0
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

[Degenerate perturbation theory](../../../../../../degenerate-perturbation-theory.md) diagonalizes this matrix. Its normalized eigenstates $(|1\rangle\pm|2\rangle)/\sqrt2$ have first-order shifts $\pm\varepsilon V_0$. The coupling to $|3\rangle$ has order $\varepsilon^2$, so it cannot affect an energy through order $\varepsilon$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{
E_+=E+\varepsilon V_0,\qquad
E_-=E-\varepsilon V_0,\qquad
E_3'=E_3
\quad\text{through order }\varepsilon.}
$$
