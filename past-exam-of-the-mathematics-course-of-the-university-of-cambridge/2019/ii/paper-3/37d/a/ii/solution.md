<h1 id="37d/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For every smooth function $f$, equality of mixed partial derivatives gives

$$
[\partial_\alpha,\partial_\beta]f=\partial_\alpha\partial_\beta f-\partial_\beta\partial_\alpha f=0.
$$

Consequently the [commuting coordinate basis](../../../../../../../commuting-coordinate-basis.md) property is

$$
\boxed{[\partial_\alpha,\partial_\beta]=0.}
$$
