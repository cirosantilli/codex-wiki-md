↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [plane polar coordinates](../../../../../../../plane-polar-coordinates.md), $x=r\cos\theta$ and $y=r\sin\theta$. The [chain rule](../../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\partial_r=\cos\theta\,\partial_x+\sin\theta\,\partial_y,\qquad\partial_\theta=-r\sin\theta\,\partial_x+r\cos\theta\,\partial_y.}
$$

Their Euclidean norms are $\|\partial_r\|=1$ and $\|\partial_\theta\|=r$ for $r>0$.
