<h1 id="37d/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The unit fields are $\widehat r=\partial_r$ and $\widehat\theta=r^{-1}\partial_\theta$. Relation (3) and $[\partial_r,\partial_\theta]=0$ give

$$
[\widehat r,\widehat\theta]=\left[\partial_r,\frac1r\partial_\theta\right]=-\frac1{r^2}\partial_\theta=-\frac1r\widehat\theta\ne0.
$$

A [coordinate basis](../../../../../../../coordinate-basis.md) must commute, so

$$
\boxed{(\widehat r,\widehat\theta)\text{ is not a coordinate basis}.}
$$

This is the [noncommuting orthonormal polar frame](../../../../../../../noncommuting-orthonormal-polar-frame.md).
