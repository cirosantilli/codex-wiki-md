<h1 id="14c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $N=S+I$. In

$$
\dot S=N-NS-\beta IS,
$$

the term $N$ represents production of healthy new plants by both healthy and diseased plants at unit per-capita rate. The term $-NS$ is density-dependent loss of healthy plants through competition, and $-\beta IS$ is loss through [mass-action infection](../../../../../../mass-action-infection.md). In

$$
\dot I=-NI+\beta IS,
$$

the term $-NI$ is the same density-dependent loss for diseased plants, while $+\beta IS$ is the matching gain when healthy plants become diseased. Thus $\beta$ is the transmission-rate coefficient per healthy-diseased encounter.

Adding the equations cancels the infection terms and gives the [logistic differential equation](../../../../../../logistic-differential-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot N=N(1-N).}
$$

The disease therefore changes how the population is divided between healthy and diseased plants but has **no effect on the total-population dynamics**: infected plants reproduce at the same rate, all offspring enter the healthy class, and infection causes no additional death in this model.
