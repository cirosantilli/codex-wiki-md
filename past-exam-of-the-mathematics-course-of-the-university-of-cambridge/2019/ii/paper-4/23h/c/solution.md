↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [zero-boundary Sobolev space](../../../../../../zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md) is

$$
\boxed{H_0^1(\Omega)=\overline{C_c^\infty(\Omega)}^{\,H^1(\Omega)}}.
$$

Here $H^1(\Omega)$ consists of the $L^2$ functions whose first [weak derivatives](../../../../../../weak-derivative.md) also belong to $L^2$, with norm

$$
\lVert u\rVert_{H^1}^2=\int_\Omega\bigl(|u|^2+|\nabla u|_2^2\bigr).
$$

On a sufficiently regular domain, the closure condition is equivalent to having zero [boundary trace](../../../../../../sobolev-trace-theorem.md).
