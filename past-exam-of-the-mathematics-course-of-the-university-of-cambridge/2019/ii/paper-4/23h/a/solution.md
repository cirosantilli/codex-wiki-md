<h1 id="23h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

This is the [boundedness criterion for a bilinear form](../../../../../../boundedness-criterion-for-a-bilinear-form.md). Since $B$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $(0,0)$, there are $r,s>0$ such that $|B(x,y)|\leq1$ whenever $\lVert x\rVert<r$ and $\lVert y\rVert<s$. For nonzero $u,v$, apply this to

$$
x=\frac{r}{2\lVert u\rVert}u,
\qquad
y=\frac{s}{2\lVert v\rVert}v.
$$

[Linearity](../../../../../../linearity.md) in each argument then gives

$$
|B(u,v)|\leq\frac4{rs}\lVert u\rVert\lVert v\rVert.
$$

The same inequality is immediate if either vector is zero, so **one may take $M=4/(rs)$**.
