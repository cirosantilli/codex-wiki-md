<h1 id="23h/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Use the [Poisson integral](../../../../../../poisson-integral.md) to choose a smooth [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) $G$ on the [unit disk](../../../../../../unit-disk.md) whose boundary value is $g$. Seek $v$ in the form $v=w+G$, which is the standard [homogenization of Dirichlet boundary data](../../../../../../homogenization-of-dirichlet-boundary-data.md). Then $w\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ and, since $\Delta G=0$,

$$
-\Delta w+w+A\mathbin\cdot\nabla w=-G-A\mathbin\cdot\nabla G\in L^2(\Omega).
$$

Part (d) gives a unique such $w$, and hence

$$
\boxed{v=w+G}
$$

is the unique [weak solution](../../../../../../weak-solution.md) with boundary value $g$.
