↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [compact topological group](../../../../../../compact-group.md) is a group $G$ equipped with a compact Hausdorff topology for which multiplication $G\times G\to G$ and inversion $G\to G$ are continuous.

The [special unitary group](../../../../../../special-unitary-group.md) is

$$
SU(n)=\{U\in M_n(\mathbb C):U^*U=I,\ \det U=1\}.
$$

It is closed in the finite-dimensional real vector space $M_n(\mathbb C)$ because the equations $U^*U=I$ and $\det U=1$ are closed conditions. It is bounded because every column of a unitary matrix has norm one. The [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md) therefore makes it compact. Matrix multiplication is continuous, and inversion is continuous because $U^{-1}=U^*$. Hence **$SU(n)$ is a compact topological group**.
