<h1 id="19i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let

$$
V=\{A\in M_2(\mathbb C):\operatorname{tr}A=0,\ A+A^*=0\}.
$$

For $U\in SU(2)$ define

$$
\rho(U)A=UAU^{-1}.
$$

Conjugation preserves trace, and unitarity gives

$$
(UAU^{-1})^*=UA^*U^{-1}=-UAU^{-1},
$$

so $V$ is invariant. This gives a continuous real-linear representation $\rho:SU(2)\to GL(V)$.
