<h1 id="19i/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Define

$$
B(A,C)=-\operatorname{tr}(AC).
$$

It is symmetric because $\operatorname{tr}(AC)=\operatorname{tr}(CA)$. Writing $A=iH$ and $C=iK$ with $H,K$ Hermitian shows that $B(A,C)=\operatorname{tr}(HK)$ is real and bilinear over $\mathbb R$. Moreover,

$$
B(A,A)=-\operatorname{tr}(A^2)
=\operatorname{tr}(A^*A)>0
$$

for $A\ne0$, so the form is positive definite. Finally, cyclicity of the trace gives

$$
B(UAU^{-1},UCU^{-1})
=-\operatorname{tr}(UACU^{-1})
=B(A,C).
$$

Hence $B$ is an invariant positive-definite symmetric bilinear form, and after identifying $V$ with Euclidean $\mathbb R^3$ we have $\rho(SU(2))\subseteq O(3)$.
