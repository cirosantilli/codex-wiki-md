<h1 id="26k/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume $X_n\to X$ in $L^2$. The reverse [triangle inequality](../../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) for the $L^2$ norm gives

$$
\left|\lVert X_n\rVert_2-\lVert X\rVert_2\right|
\leq\lVert X_n-X\rVert_2\longrightarrow0.
$$

Squaring the norms yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X_n^2]\longrightarrow\mathbb E[X^2].}
$$
