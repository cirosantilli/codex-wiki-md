<h1 id="8e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiply the three [Euler equations for a torque-free rigid body](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-a-torque-free-rigid-body.md) by $\omega_1,\omega_2,\omega_3$ and add. The cubic terms cancel, giving conservation of the [rotational kinetic energy](../../../../../../rotational-kinetic-energy.md)

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12\left(I_1\omega_1^2+I_2\omega_2^2+I_3\omega_3^2\right).}
$$

Instead multiply the equations by $I_1\omega_1,I_2\omega_2,I_3\omega_3$ and add. The cubic terms again cancel, giving

$$
\boxed{L^2=I_1^2\omega_1^2+I_2^2\omega_2^2+I_3^2\omega_3^2,}
$$

the squared magnitude of the conserved [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md). For an asymmetric body these two quadratic forms are not proportional, so the [torque-free rigid-body invariants](../../../../../../torque-free-rigid-body-invariants.md) are independent.
