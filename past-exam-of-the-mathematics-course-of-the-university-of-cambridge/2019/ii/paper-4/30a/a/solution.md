<h1 id="30a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Between the [classical turning points](../../../../../../classical-turning-point.md), $q<0$, while boundedness selects exponentially decaying [WKB approximation](../../../../../../wkb-approximation.md) solutions in the forbidden regions $x<a$ and $x>b$. The [Airy turning-point connection formula](../../../../../../airy-turning-point-connection-formula.md) at the left turning point matches the left decaying solution to

$$
\psi_L(x)\sim C_L|q(x)|^{-1/4}
\sin\left(\frac1\epsilon\int_a^x|q(\xi)|^{1/2}\,d\xi+\frac\pi4\right)
$$

in the allowed region. Matching the right decaying solution across $b$ gives

$$
\psi_R(x)\sim C_R|q(x)|^{-1/4}
\sin\left(\frac1\epsilon\int_x^b|q(\xi)|^{1/2}\,d\xi+\frac\pi4\right).
$$

For these expressions to be proportional throughout $(a,b)$, their phases must be compatible. Each simple turning point contributes a phase shift $\pi/4$, giving the [two-turning-point WKB quantization condition](../../../../../../two-turning-point-wkb-quantization-condition.md)

$$
\boxed{
\frac1\epsilon\int_a^b|q(\xi)|^{1/2}\,d\xi
=\left(n+\frac12\right)\pi,
\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$
