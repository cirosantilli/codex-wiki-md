↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

This language is recognized by the [parity-counting deterministic finite automaton](../../../../../../../parity-counting-deterministic-finite-automaton.md) with four states

$$
Q=\{(p_b,p_c):p_b,p_c\in\mathbb Z_2\}.
$$

The initial state is $(0,0)$ and the only accepting state is $(1,0)$. Reading $a$ leaves the state unchanged, reading $b$ toggles $p_b$, and reading $c$ toggles $p_c$. The state therefore records exactly the parities of the numbers of $b$'s and $c$'s. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{the language is regular}.}
$$
