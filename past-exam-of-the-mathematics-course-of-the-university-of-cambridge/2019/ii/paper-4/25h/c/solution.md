↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**No.** Fix $0<A<1$ and, near $s=0$, let

$$
f(s)=A\cos s,
\qquad
g(s)=\int_0^s\sqrt{1-A^2\sin^2t}\,dt.
$$

Then $f'^2+g'^2=1$, so this is a regular unit-speed profile with $f>0$ on a sufficiently small interval. Rotate it around the $z$-axis. For an arc-length [surface of revolution](../../../../../../surface-of-revolution.md), $K=-f''/f$, and therefore

$$
K=-\frac{-A\cos s}{A\cos s}=1.
$$

At $s=0$, the formulas from part (a) give the [principal curvatures](../../../../../../principal-curvature.md)

$$
k_1=\frac1A,
\qquad k_2=A.
$$

They are unequal because $A\ne1$, so the point is not an [umbilical point](../../../../../../umbilical-point.md). Every point of a sphere is umbilical. Consequently no neighbourhood of this point can lie on a sphere, giving a [non-spherical surface of constant Gaussian curvature one](../../../../../../non-spherical-surface-of-constant-gaussian-curvature-one.md).
