<h1 id="6c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Again put $x=1+u$. Then

$$
u'(t)=\alpha\bigl(1+u(t-T)\bigr)(-u(t))
=-\alpha u(t)-\alpha u(t-T)u(t).
$$

The linearized equation is therefore

$$
u'=-\alpha u,
$$

whose sole characteristic exponent is $-\alpha<0$ and is independent of $T$. By [delay-independent stability from an undelayed linearization](../../../../../../delay-independent-stability-from-an-undelayed-linearization.md),

$$
\boxed{x(t)=1\text{ is locally asymptotically stable for every }T\geq0.}
$$
