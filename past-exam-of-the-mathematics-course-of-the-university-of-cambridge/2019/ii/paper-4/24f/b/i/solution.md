For a [divisor on an algebraic curve](../../../../../../../divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) $D$, define its [Riemann-Roch space](../../../../../../../riemann-roch-space.md) by

$$
L(D)=\{0\}\cup\{f\in\mathbb C(X)^\times:(f)+D\geq0\}.
$$

Then

$$
\boxed{\ell(D)=\dim_{\mathbb C}L(D).}
$$
