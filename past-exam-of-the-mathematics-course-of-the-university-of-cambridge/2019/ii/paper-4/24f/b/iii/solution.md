<h1 id="24f/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $N>2g-2$, the divisor $K_X-Np$ has negative degree, so $\ell(K_X-Np)=0$. The [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives

$$
\ell(Np)=N+1-g.
$$

Starting from $\ell(0)=1$ and using part (ii), each integer $1\leq n\leq N$ contributes one to $\ell(Np)$ exactly when it is not a gap. Therefore

$$
\ell(Np)=1+N-|G(p)\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|.
$$

Comparison with the preceding formula gives $|G(p)\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|=g$. Moreover every $n>N$ is a nongap because the Riemann--Roch formula makes every subsequent dimension jump equal to one. Thus the [Weierstrass gap theorem](../../../../../../../weierstrass-gap-theorem.md) says

$$
\boxed{|G(p)|=g.}
$$
