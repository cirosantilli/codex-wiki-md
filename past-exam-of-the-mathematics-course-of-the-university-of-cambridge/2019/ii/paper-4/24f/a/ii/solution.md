↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [adjunction formula](../../../../../../../adjunction-formula.md) gives $K_X\cong\mathcal O_X(d-3)$. Since a [hyperplane section](../../../../../../../hyperplane-section.md) has degree $d$, the [canonical degree of a smooth plane curve](../../../../../../../canonical-degree-of-a-smooth-plane-curve.md) is

$$
\boxed{\deg\mathcal K_X=d(d-3).}
$$
