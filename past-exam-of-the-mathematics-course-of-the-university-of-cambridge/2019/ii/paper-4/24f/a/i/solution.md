<h1 id="24f/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $H=V(L)$ for a nonzero homogeneous linear polynomial $L$. Because $H$ does not contain $X$, the restriction $L|_X$ is a nonzero section of $\mathcal O_X(1)$. Its zero divisor is the [hyperplane-section divisor of a projective plane curve](../../../../../../../hyperplane-section-divisor-of-a-projective-plane-curve.md)

$$
[X\cap H]=\sum_{p\in X\cap H}I_p(X,H)p,
$$

where $I_p(X,H)$ is the local [intersection multiplicity](../../../../../../../intersection-multiplicity.md); equivalently, it is the order of vanishing of $L|_X$ at $p$.

The curves $X$ and $H$ have no common component and have degrees $d$ and $1$. The [Bézout theorem](../../../../../../../bezout-s-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\deg[X\cap H]=\sum_{p\in X\cap H}I_p(X,H)=d.}
$$
