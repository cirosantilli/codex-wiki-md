↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The componentwise [product rule for the Laplacian](../../../../../../../product-rule-for-the-laplacian.md) and the harmonicity of $\boldsymbol\Phi$ give

$$
\nabla^2(\mathbf x\mathbin{\cdot}\boldsymbol\Phi)
=2\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}\boldsymbol\Phi
+\mathbf x\mathbin{\cdot}\nabla^2\boldsymbol\Phi
=2\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}\boldsymbol\Phi.
$$

Hence

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}\mathbf u
&=\nabla^2(\mathbf x\mathbin{\cdot}\boldsymbol\Phi+\chi)
-2\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}\boldsymbol\Phi\\
&=2\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}\boldsymbol\Phi+0
-2\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}\boldsymbol\Phi=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $\boxed{\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}\mathbf u=0}$.
