<h1 id="21f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [21F](../21f.md)

For a continuous self-map $f$ of a finite CW complex, its [Lefschetz number](../../../../../lefschetz-number.md) over $\mathbb Q$ is

$$
L(f)=\sum_{q\geq0}(-1)^q
\operatorname{tr}\!\left(f_*:H_q(X;\mathbb Q)\to H_q(X;\mathbb Q)\right).
$$

The [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](../../../../../lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md) states that $L(f)\ne0$ implies that $f$ has a fixed point. Consequently every fixed-point-free self-map has Lefschetz number zero.

The space in the question is a [cyclic point-gluing of two-spheres](../../../../../cyclic-point-gluing-of-two-spheres.md). Before gluing, the $n$ spheres have total Euler characteristic $2n$. Each of the $n$ point identifications lowers the Euler characteristic by one, so

$$
\boxed{\chi(X)=n.}
$$

Equivalently, $X\simeq S^1\vee\bigvee_{j=1}^nS^2$, with rational homology dimensions $1,1,n$ in degrees zero, one, and two.
