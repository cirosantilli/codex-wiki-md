↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Now suppose $\phi^2=\operatorname{Id}_X$. The map is fixed-point-free, so $L(\phi)=0$. On each rational homology group,

$$
P_q=\frac12(I+\phi_*)
$$

projects onto the invariant subspace and has integral trace. Hence

$$
\sum_q(-1)^q\operatorname{tr}P_q
=\frac12\{L(\operatorname{Id}_X)+L(\phi)\}
=\frac n2
$$

is an integer. Therefore

$$
\boxed{2\mid n.}
$$
