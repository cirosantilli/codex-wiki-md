<h1 id="12e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For any $\varepsilon>0$, the balls $\{B_\varepsilon(x):x\in X\}$ form an [open cover](../../../../../../../open-cover.md) of the compact metric space $X$. Compactness supplies a finite subcover

$$
X=B_\varepsilon(x_1)\cup\cdots\cup B_\varepsilon(x_N).
$$

This is exactly [total boundedness](../../../../../../../totally-bounded-space.md).
