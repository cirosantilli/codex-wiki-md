**No.** Give an infinite set $X$ the [discrete metric](../../../../../../../discrete-metric.md)

$$
d(x,y)=\begin{cases}0,&x=y,\\1,&x\ne y.\end{cases}
$$

This space is bounded. Every Cauchy sequence is eventually constant, so it is complete. However, the open cover by the singleton balls $B_{1/2}(x)=\{x\}$ has no finite subcover. Hence $X$ is not [compact](../../../../../../../compact-space.md).
