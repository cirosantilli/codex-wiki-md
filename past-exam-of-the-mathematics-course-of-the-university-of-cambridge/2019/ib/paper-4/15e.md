# 15E

↑ **Parent:** [Paper 4](../paper-4-split.md)

The group

$$
PSL_2(\mathbb R)=SL_2(\mathbb R)/\{\pm I\}
$$

acts on the [Poincaré half-plane model](../../../../poincare-half-plane-model.md) by

$$
g(z)=\frac{az+b}{cz+d},
\qquad ad-bc=1.
$$

A direct calculation gives

$$
\operatorname{Im}g(z)=\frac{\operatorname{Im}z}{|cz+d|^2},
\qquad
dg=\frac{dz}{(cz+d)^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\frac{|dg|^2}{(\operatorname{Im}g(z))^2}
=\frac{|dz|^2}{(\operatorname{Im}z)^2},
$$

so every element of [PSL2(R)](../../../../psl2-r.md) is an isometry of the hyperbolic metric.
