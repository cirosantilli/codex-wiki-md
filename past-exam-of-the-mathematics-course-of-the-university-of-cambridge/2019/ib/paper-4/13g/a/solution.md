↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $A\subseteq X$, the [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $A$ is

$$
\{A\cap U:U\text{ is open in }X\}.
$$

If $\sim$ is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md) on $X$ and $q:X\to X/{\sim}$ is the natural projection, the [quotient topology](../../../../../../quotient-topology.md) declares $V\subseteq X/{\sim}$ open exactly when $q^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$. For topological spaces $X$ and $Y$, the [product topology](../../../../../../product-topology.md) on $X\times Y$ has as a basis all sets $U\times V$ with $U$ open in $X$ and $V$ open in $Y$.
