<h1 id="16a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the admissible [variation](../../../../../../variation.md) as $y_\varepsilon=y+\varepsilon\eta$, where $\eta(x)\to0$ at both ends. Differentiating the [functional](../../../../../../functional.md) under the integral sign gives the [first variation](../../../../../../first-variation.md)

$$
\delta I[y;\eta]
=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\left(y'\eta'+U(y)U'(y)\eta\right)dx.
$$

After [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), with the boundary term vanishing,

$$
\boxed{\delta I[y;\eta]
=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\left[-y''+U(y)U'(y)\right]\eta\,dx}.
$$

Thus the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is

$$
y''=U(y)U'(y).
$$

A second differentiation gives the [second variation](../../../../../../second-variation.md)

$$
\boxed{\delta^2I[y;\eta]
=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\left\{(\eta')^2+\left[U'(y)^2+U(y)U''(y)\right]\eta^2\right\}dx}.
$$
