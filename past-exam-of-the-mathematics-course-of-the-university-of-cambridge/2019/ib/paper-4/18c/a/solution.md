↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The constant $f=2\Omega\sin\varphi$ is the positive [Coriolis parameter](../../../../../../coriolis-parameter.md) at northern latitude $\varphi$, $g$ is the [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md), and $h$ is the undisturbed mean depth of the layer.

The [linearized shallow water equations](../../../../../../linearized-shallow-water-equations.md) assume a homogeneous, incompressible, inviscid layer over a flat bed; a horizontal scale much larger than the depth, so the [shallow-water approximation](../../../../../../shallow-water-approximation.md) and [hydrostatic approximation](../../../../../../hydrostatic-approximation.md) apply; depth-independent horizontal velocity; a free-surface displacement $|\eta|\ll h$ and sufficiently small velocity to neglect nonlinear products; and an [f-plane](../../../../../../f-plane.md) on which $f$ is constant. The equations also omit viscosity, forcing, and bottom topography.
