**No.** Define

$$
f_n(x)=\sqrt{x^2+\frac1n}.
$$

Each $f_n$ is a [differentiable function](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) on $[-1,1]$, while

$$
0\leq f_n(x)-|x|
=\frac{1/n}{\sqrt{x^2+1/n}+|x|}
\leq\frac1{\sqrt n}.
$$

**Thus $f_n$ converges uniformly to the [absolute value function](../../../../../../absolute-value.md) $|x|$, which is not differentiable at $0$. Uniform convergence alone therefore does not preserve differentiability.**
