<h1 id="7a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7A](../7a.md)

In vacuum the [Maxwell equations](../../../../../maxwell-equations.md) are

$$
\nabla\mathbin{\cdot}E=0,
\qquad \nabla\mathbin{\cdot}B=0,
\qquad \nabla\times E=-\frac{\partial B}{\partial t},
\qquad \nabla\times B=\frac1{c^2}\frac{\partial E}{\partial t}.
$$

Substitution of the complex [plane electromagnetic wave](../../../../../plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) amplitudes into these equations gives

$$
k\mathbin{\cdot}E_0=0,
\qquad
B_0=\frac{k\times E_0}{\omega},
\qquad
\omega^2=c^2|k|^2.
$$

Thus the electric field must be [transverse](../../../../../transverse-polarization.md) to $k$; taking the positive-frequency branch gives $\omega=c|k|$.

For $E_0=E(1,i,0)$, $k=k\widehat z$, and $\varphi=kz-\omega t$, the real fields are

$$
E(x,t)=E(\cos\varphi,-\sin\varphi,0),
\qquad
B(x,t)=\frac Ec(\sin\varphi,\cos\varphi,0).
$$

The electric vector rotates with constant magnitude, so this is [circular polarization](../../../../../circular-polarization.md). Its [Poynting vector](../../../../../poynting-vector.md) is

$$
\boxed{S=\frac1{\mu_0}E\times B
=\frac{E^2}{\mu_0c}\widehat z}.
$$

The Poynting vector is the electromagnetic [energy flux](../../../../../energy-flux.md): its direction is the direction of propagation and its magnitude is the power crossing unit area.
