<h1 id="11g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

There are

$$
\boxed{0}
$$

such conjugacy classes. Every matrix in $GL_{10}(\mathbb Q)$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), so by the [minimal polynomial of an invertible matrix](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial-of-an-invertible-matrix.md) its minimal polynomial must have nonzero constant term. But

$$
X^7-4X^3=X^3(X^4-4)
$$

has constant term zero and cannot be the minimal polynomial of an invertible matrix.
