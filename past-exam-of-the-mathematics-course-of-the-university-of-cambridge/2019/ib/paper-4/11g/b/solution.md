<h1 id="11g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Present a [finitely generated abelian group](../../../../../../finitely-generated-abelian-group.md) $G$ as the [cokernel](../../../../../../cokernel.md) of an integer matrix $A:\mathbb Z^m\to\mathbb Z^n$. Changing $A$ by the invertible row and column operations used in its [Smith normal form](../../../../../../smith-normal-form.md) does not change the isomorphism type of the cokernel. If

$$
UAV=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_s,0,\ldots,0),
$$

then

$$
\boxed{G\cong\mathbb Z^r\oplus\mathbb Z/d_1\mathbb Z\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathbb Z/d_s\mathbb Z},
\qquad d_1\mid\cdots\mid d_s,
$$

where factors with $d_i=1$ may be omitted. This is the [Fundamental theorem of finitely generated abelian groups](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-finitely-generated-abelian-groups.md); the [rank of an abelian group](../../../../../../rank-of-an-abelian-group.md) $r$ and invariant factors $d_i>1$ are unique.
