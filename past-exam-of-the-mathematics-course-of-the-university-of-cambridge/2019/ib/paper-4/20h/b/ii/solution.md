After these augmentations, the vertices reachable from $S$ in the [residual network](../../../../../../../residual-network.md) are

$$
A=\{S,a,c\}.
$$

The edges leaving this set are $S\to b$, $a\to d$, and $c\to e$, with capacities $3$, $1$, and $4$. Hence

$$
c(A,V\setminus A)=3+1+4=8.
$$

The flow in part (i) has the same value, so the [max-flow min-cut theorem](../../../../../../../max-flow-min-cut-theorem.md) proves that it is maximum and that this cut is minimum.
