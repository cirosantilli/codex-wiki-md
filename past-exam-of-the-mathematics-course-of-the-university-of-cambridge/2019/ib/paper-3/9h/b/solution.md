The [period of a state in a Markov chain](../../../../../../period-of-a-state-in-a-markov-chain.md) $a$ is

$$
\boxed{d(a)=\gcd\{n\geq1:P^n(a,a)>0\}}.
$$

A state is aperiodic when this greatest common divisor is one.
