↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

States $i,j\in S$ [communicate](../../../../../../communicating-states.md) when each is accessible from the other: there exist nonnegative integers $m,n$ such that $P^m(i,j)>0$ and $P^n(j,i)>0$. A [communicating class](../../../../../../communicating-class.md) is an equivalence class for this relation.
