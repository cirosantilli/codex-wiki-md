<h1 id="12e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $1\leq t\leq b$ and $c\geq0$,

$$
b^{-c}\leq t^{-c}\leq1.
$$

Taking suprema gives

$$
\boxed{b^{-c}\|f\|_\infty\leq\|f\|_c\leq\|f\|_\infty}.
$$

**Thus the weighted norm and the usual [uniform norm](../../../../../../../supremum-norm.md) are [equivalent norms](../../../../../../../equivalent-norms.md).**
