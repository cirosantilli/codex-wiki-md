↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [sphere](../../../../../../../sphere.md), $\chi=2$, and the same incidence identity gives

$$
2=V-\frac E3,
\qquad E=3(V-2).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\overline d=\frac{2E}{V}=6-\frac{12}{V}<6}.
$$
