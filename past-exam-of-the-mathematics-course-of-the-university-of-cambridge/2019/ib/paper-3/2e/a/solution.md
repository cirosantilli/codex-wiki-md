↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A function $f:A\to\mathbb R$ is [uniformly continuous](../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md) if, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a $\delta>0$ such that

$$
x,y\in A,\quad |x-y|<\delta
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
|f(x)-f(y)|<\varepsilon.
$$

The same $\delta$ must work at every pair of points in the domain.
