<h1 id="2e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The function $x\mapsto x^2$ is not uniformly continuous on $\mathbb R$. Take $x_n=n$ and $y_n=n+1/n$. Then $|x_n-y_n|\to0$, whereas

$$
|y_n^2-x_n^2|=2+\frac1{n^2}\longrightarrow2.
$$

This contradicts the [sequential criterion for uniform continuity](../../../../../../../sequential-criterion-for-uniform-continuity.md).
