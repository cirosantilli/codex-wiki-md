<h1 id="2e/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The function $x\mapsto\cos(1/x)$ is uniformly continuous on $[1,\infty)$. Its [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) satisfies

$$
\left|\frac{\sin(1/x)}{x^2}\right|\leq1,
$$

so the [mean value theorem](../../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) gives

$$
|\cos(1/x)-\cos(1/y)|\leq|x-y|.
$$

**Thus the function is [Lipschitz](../../../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md), which implies uniform continuity.**
