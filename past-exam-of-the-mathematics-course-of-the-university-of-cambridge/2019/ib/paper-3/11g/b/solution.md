<h1 id="11g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every [Euclidean domain](../../../../../../euclidean-domain.md) is a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md): in a nonzero ideal choose an element of least positive Euclidean value and divide every other element by it; minimality forces every remainder to vanish. Every principal ideal domain is a [unique factorization domain](../../../../../../unique-factorization-domain.md): the ascending-chain condition supplies factorizations into irreducibles, and [Euclid lemma](../../../../../../euclid-lemma.md) follows because every irreducible generates a prime ideal. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb Z[\omega]\text{ is a unique factorization domain}}.
$$
