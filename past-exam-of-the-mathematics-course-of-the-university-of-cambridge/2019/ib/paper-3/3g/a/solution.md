A [compact space](../../../../../../compact-space.md) is one for which every [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) has a finite subcover. A metric space is [sequentially compact](../../../../../../sequentially-compact-space.md) when every [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) in it has a [convergent subsequence](../../../../../../convergent-subsequence.md) whose limit belongs to the space.
