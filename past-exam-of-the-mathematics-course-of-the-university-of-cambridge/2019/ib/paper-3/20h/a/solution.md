↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [sample mean](../../../../../../sample-mean.md) is

$$
\boxed{\overline X\sim N\left(\mu,\frac{\sigma^2}{n}\right)}.
$$

Write the Gaussian sample vector as its orthogonal projection onto the span of $(1,\ldots,1)$ plus its projection onto the orthogonal complement. These two Gaussian projections are independent. Their squared standardized lengths give [Cochran's theorem](../../../../../../cochran-s-theorem.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{S_{XX}}{\sigma^2}\sim\chi^2_{n-1},
\qquad \overline X\ \text{is independent of }S_{XX}}.
$$
