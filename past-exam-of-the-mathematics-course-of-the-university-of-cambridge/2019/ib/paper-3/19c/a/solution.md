<h1 id="19c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Bilinearity and symmetry follow from the corresponding properties of the [integral](../../../../../../integral.md). Since $w(x)>0$,

$$
\langle f,f\rangle=\int_a^b f(x)^2w(x)\,dx\geq0.
$$

If this integral is zero, the nonnegative continuous integrand must vanish everywhere, so $f=0$. Therefore the displayed formula is a real [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) on $C[a,b]$.
