<h1 id="10f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Then $AB=B\ne0$ and $(AB)^2=0$, whereas $BA=0$. Hence their [minimal polynomials](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) are

$$
\boxed{m_{AB}(t)=t^2,\qquad m_{BA}(t)=t}.
$$
