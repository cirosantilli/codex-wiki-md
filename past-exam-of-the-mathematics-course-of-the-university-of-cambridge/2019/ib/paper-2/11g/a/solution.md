<h1 id="11g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Because $f$ is [irreducible](../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md), the ideal $(f)$ is maximal in the [polynomial ring](../../../../../../polynomial-ring.md) $k[X]$. Hence

$$
F=k[X]/(f)
$$

is a [field extension](../../../../../../field-extension.md) of $k$. Let $\alpha=X+(f)\in F$. Evaluation in the quotient gives $f(\alpha)=0$, and the [factor theorem](../../../../../../factor-theorem.md) in $F[X]$ gives

$$
\boxed{f(X)=(X-\alpha)g(X)}
$$

for some $g\in F[X]$. This is the standard [adjoining a root of an irreducible polynomial](../../../../../../adjoining-a-root-of-an-irreducible-polynomial.md) construction.
