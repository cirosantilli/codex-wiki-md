<h1 id="12e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each positive integer $N$, let $x^{(N)}$ have its first $N$ coordinates equal to one and all later coordinates zero. This is an element of the [l-p sequence space](../../../../../../../l-p-sequence-space.md) for every finite $p$, and

$$
\|x^{(N)}\|_\infty=1,
\qquad
\|x^{(N)}\|_p=N^{1/p}.
$$

**No constant can bound $\|x\|_p$ by a fixed multiple of $\|x\|_\infty$ for all $N$.** Thus $\|\cdot\|_p$ and $\|\cdot\|_\infty$ are not Lipschitz equivalent.
