The [power function](../../../../../../power-function-of-a-statistical-test.md) of a test is the probability of rejection as a function of the true parameter. Since $S=n\overline X\sim\Gamma(n,\lambda)$,

$$
\boxed{\beta(\lambda)=\mathbb P_\lambda(\overline X>c_\alpha)
=1-G_{n,\lambda}(nc_\alpha)}.
$$

At the null value, $\beta(\lambda_0)=\alpha$.
