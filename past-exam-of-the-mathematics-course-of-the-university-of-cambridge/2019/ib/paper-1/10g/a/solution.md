<h1 id="10g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The group $G$ is a nontrivial [finite p-group](../../../../../../finite-p-group.md), so the [nontrivial center of a finite p-group](../../../../../../nontrivial-center-of-a-finite-p-group.md) gives $Z(G)\ne\{e\}$. If $Z(G)\ne G$, then $Z(G)$ is already a nontrivial proper [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md), so $G$ is not a [simple group](../../../../../../simple-group.md). If $Z(G)=G$, then $G$ is [abelian](../../../../../../abelian-group.md). By [Cauchy's theorem for finite groups](../../../../../../cauchy-theorem-for-groups.md), it has a subgroup of order $p$; every subgroup of an abelian group is normal, and this subgroup is nontrivial and proper because $|G|=p^4$. Thus in every case

$$
\boxed{G\text{ is not simple}}.
$$
