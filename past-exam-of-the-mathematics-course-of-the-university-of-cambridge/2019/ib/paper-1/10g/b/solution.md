The [Sylow theorems](../../../../../../sylow-theorems.md) state that if $|G|=p^am$ with $p\nmid m$, then:

- $G$ has a [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) of order $p^a$;
- every $p$-subgroup is contained in a Sylow $p$-subgroup;
- all Sylow $p$-subgroups are conjugate;
- their number $n_p$ satisfies $n_p\mid m$ and $n_p\equiv1\pmod p$.
