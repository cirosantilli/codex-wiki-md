↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

By the definition of conditional probability and [stationarity](../../../../../../stationary-distribution.md),

$$
P^*(x,y)
=\frac{\mathbb P(X_0=y,X_1=x)}{\mathbb P(X_1=x)}
=\boxed{\frac{\pi(y)P(y,x)}{\pi(x)}}.
$$

This is the transition matrix of the [time-reversed Markov chain](../../../../../../time-reversed-markov-chain.md).
