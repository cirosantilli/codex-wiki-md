<h1 id="20h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Conditional on $X_0=x$, the random variable $T$ is the [first return time](../../../../../../first-return-time.md) $T_x^+$. The [Kac's lemma](../../../../../../kac-s-lemma.md) mean-recurrence formula for a finite [irreducible Markov chain](../../../../../../irreducible-markov-chain.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E_xT_x^+=\frac1{\pi(x)}.
$$

Averaging over $X_0\sim\pi$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[T]=\sum_x\pi(x)\frac1{\pi(x)}=N}.
$$
