<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The player random intercept models stable player-to-player scoring ability and accounts for the six repeated observations on each player. The manager included it because treating those observations as conditionally independent with one common baseline would ignore both heterogeneity and within-player dependence.

The coefficient $0.4385$ says that, holding competition and player effect fixed, the expected scoring rate against S is multiplied by

$$
\boxed{e^{0.4385}\approx1.55}
$$

relative to R. More goals are scored against S, so **R appears to be the stronger rival**.
