<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $r_S=2R_g$. On the midplane, [circular orbit](../../../../../../circular-orbit.md) balance gives $r\Omega^2=\partial_r\Phi=GM/(r-r_S)^2$. The [specific angular momentum](../../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md), [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) and [orbital shear parameter](../../../../../../orbital-shear-parameter.md) of a [Paczyński-Wiita circular orbit](../../../../../../paczynski-wiita-circular-orbit.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\Omega^2=\frac{GM}{r(r-2R_g)^2}},\qquad
\boxed{h=r^2\Omega=\frac{\sqrt{GM}\,r^{3/2}}{r-2R_g}},
$$



$$
\boxed{\varepsilon=\frac12r^2\Omega^2+\Phi(r,0)
=\frac{GM(4R_g-r)}{2(r-2R_g)^2}},\qquad
\boxed{q=-\frac{d\log\Omega}{d\log r}
=\frac12+\frac r{r-2R_g}}.
$$

For a small radial displacement at fixed [specific angular momentum](../../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md), the [effective potential stability criterion](../../../../../../effective-potential-stability-criterion.md) gives the [radial epicyclic frequency](../../../../../../radial-epicyclic-frequency.md)

$$
\boxed{\kappa_r^2=\frac1{r^3}\frac{d(h^2)}{dr}
=\frac{GM(r-6R_g)}{r(r-2R_g)^3}
=2(2-q)\Omega^2}.
$$

Here $\kappa_r$ is the question's epicyclic $\kappa$. Its square is negative for $2R_g<r<6R_g$, so a radial displacement grows instead of undergoing [epicyclic motion](../../../../../../epicyclic-motion.md). The orbit at $r_{\rm in}=6R_g$ is marginal, and the outer orbits are stable to small radial displacements. At this inner edge,

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon(6R_g)=-\frac{GM}{16R_g}=-\frac{c^2}{16}},\qquad
\boxed{\eta=\frac1{16}}.
$$

This efficiency belongs to the [Paczyński-Wiita potential](../../../../../../paczynski-wiita-potential.md); it is an approximation to the relativistic black-hole result.
