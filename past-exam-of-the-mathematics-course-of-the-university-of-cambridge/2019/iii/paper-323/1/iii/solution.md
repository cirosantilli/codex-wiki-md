<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [partial transpose](../../../../../../partial-transpose.md) of the [spin singlet state](../../../../../../spin-singlet-state.md) projector is the [Hermitian operator](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md)

$$
W=\frac12\left(|01\rangle\langle01|+|10\rangle\langle10|
-|00\rangle\langle11|-|11\rangle\langle00|\right)
=\frac I2-|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|.
$$

For every [separable quantum state](../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md) $\tau$, the [positive partial transpose criterion](../../../../../../positive-partial-transpose-criterion.md) and the [trace-adjoint identity for partial transpose](../../../../../../trace-adjoint-identity-for-partial-transpose.md) give

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(W\tau)
=\operatorname{Tr}(\Psi_-\tau^{T_B})
=\langle\Psi^-|\tau^{T_B}|\Psi^-\rangle\geq0.
$$

For the specified [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md), however,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Tr}\left(W|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|\right)=-\frac12}.
$$

Thus $W$ is an [entanglement witness](../../../../../../entanglement-witness.md) detecting $|\Phi^+\rangle$. In fact, it detects any state whose overlap with that [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) exceeds $1/2$.
