<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use two precise finite-dimensional results. The [positive-map separability criterion](../../../../../../positive-map-separability-criterion.md) says that a state on $\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^3$ is separable exactly when $(\operatorname{id}_2\otimes\Phi)(\sigma)\geq0$ for every [positive linear map](../../../../../../positive-linear-map.md) $\Phi:M_3(\mathbb C)\to M_2(\mathbb C)$. The [Størmer-Woronowicz decomposability theorem](../../../../../../stormer-woronowicz-decomposability-theorem.md) says that every such map is a [decomposable positive map](../../../../../../decomposable-positive-map.md), so

$$
\Phi=\Phi_1+\Phi_2\circ T,
$$

with $\Phi_1,\Phi_2$ [completely positive maps](../../../../../../completely-positive-map.md) and $T$ the [matrix transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) on $M_3$.

If $\sigma$ has [positive partial transpose](../../../../../../positive-partial-transpose.md), both $\sigma$ and $\sigma^{T_B}$ are positive. Hence

$$
(\operatorname{id}_2\otimes\Phi)(\sigma)
=(\operatorname{id}_2\otimes\Phi_1)(\sigma)
+(\operatorname{id}_2\otimes\Phi_2)(\sigma^{T_B})\geq0.
$$

The [positive-map separability criterion](../../../../../../positive-map-separability-criterion.md) now proves that $\sigma$ is a [separable quantum state](../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md). The dimension-specific decomposability theorem is essential: the conclusion does not extend to arbitrary bipartite dimensions.
