<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A bipartite [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) $\rho_{AB}$ has [positive partial transpose](../../../../../../positive-partial-transpose.md) when $\rho_{AB}^{T_B}\geq0$ in a product basis. Positivity is independent of the choice of local bases, although the matrix representing the [partial transpose](../../../../../../partial-transpose.md) changes.

If the state is a [separable quantum state](../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md), write $\rho_{AB}=\sum_jp_j\rho_j^A\otimes\rho_j^B$. Taking its [partial transpose](../../../../../../partial-transpose.md) gives

$$
\rho_{AB}^{T_B}=\sum_jp_j\rho_j^A\otimes(\rho_j^B)^T\geq0.
$$

Indeed, the [matrix transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) preserves the nonnegative [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of each local [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md); a [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md) of [positive semidefinite operators](../../../../../../positive-operator.md) is positive, and so is their nonnegative sum. This proves the necessary direction of the [positive partial transpose criterion](../../../../../../positive-partial-transpose-criterion.md).
