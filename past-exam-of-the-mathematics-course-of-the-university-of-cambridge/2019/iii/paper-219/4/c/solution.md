<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For each $i$, place $\xi_i$ and $\eta_i$ inside a plate replicated $N$ times. The directed edges are represented by

$$
(\mu,\tau^2)\longrightarrow\xi_i\longrightarrow x_i,
\qquad
(\alpha,\beta,\sigma^2,\xi_i)\longrightarrow\eta_i\longrightarrow y_i.
$$

The shaded observed nodes are $x_i,y_i$; the unshaded nodes $\xi_i,\eta_i$ are latent; and $\alpha,\beta,\sigma^2,\mu,\tau^2$ lie outside the plate. This is the [probabilistic graphical model](../../../../../../probabilistic-graphical-model.md) encoded by the joint factorization.
