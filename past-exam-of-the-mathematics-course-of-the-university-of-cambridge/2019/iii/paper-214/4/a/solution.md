A [spanning tree](../../../../../../spanning-tree.md) of a finite connected graph $G$ is a connected acyclic subgraph containing every vertex. A [uniform spanning tree](../../../../../../uniform-spanning-tree.md) is a random spanning tree chosen uniformly from the finite set of all spanning trees of $G$.
