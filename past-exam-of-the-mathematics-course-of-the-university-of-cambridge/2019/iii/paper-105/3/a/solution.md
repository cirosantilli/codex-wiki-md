<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The function $f$ is [real analytic](../../../../../../real-analytic-function.md) at $y$ if there is a [neighborhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $y$ on which its multivariable [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md)

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\sum_{\alpha\in\mathbb N^n}
\frac{D^\alpha f(y)}{\alpha!}(x-y)^\alpha}
$$

converges to $f(x)$. Here $\alpha$ is a [multi-index](../../../../../../multi-index-notation.md); equivalently, $f$ agrees locally with a convergent real [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) centred at $y$.
