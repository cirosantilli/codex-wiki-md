<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

One prescribes analytic [Cauchy data](../../../../../../../cauchy-data.md): the value of $u$ and one first derivative transverse to $\Sigma$, for example

$$
u|_\Sigma=\phi,
\qquad
\partial_\nu u|_\Sigma=\psi,
$$

where $\phi$ and $\psi$ are real analytic on $\Sigma$. Being a [non-characteristic hypersurface](../../../../../../../non-characteristic-hypersurface.md) allows the equation to solve for the second derivative in the transverse direction. The [Cauchy-Kovalevskaya theorem](../../../../../../../cauchy-kovalevskaya-theorem.md) then gives **one and only one local real analytic solution near $p$**.
