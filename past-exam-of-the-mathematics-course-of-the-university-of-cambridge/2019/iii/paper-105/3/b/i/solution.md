<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Locally write the real analytic [hypersurface](../../../../../../../hypersurface.md) as $\Sigma=\{F=0\}$ with $dF\ne0$. The [conormal bundle](../../../../../../../conormal-bundle.md) is spanned by $dF$. The surface is a [characteristic hypersurface](../../../../../../../characteristic-hypersurface.md) at $p$ precisely when the [principal symbol](../../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) vanishes on that conormal:

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i,j=1}^n a_{ij}(p)F_{x_i}(p)F_{x_j}(p)=0.}
$$

Only the matrix $(a_{ij}+a_{ji})/2$, which is a [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md), contributes to this expression.
