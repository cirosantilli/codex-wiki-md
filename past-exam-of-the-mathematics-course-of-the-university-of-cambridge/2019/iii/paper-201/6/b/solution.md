<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $g\geq0$, the [Tonelli theorem](../../../../../../tonelli-theorem.md) and conditioning on $N_t$ give

$$
\mathbb E[X_t\mid N_t]
=N_t\mathbb E[g(Y_1)]
=N_t\int_0^1g(y)\,dy.
$$

Since $\mathbb E[N_t]=\lambda t$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X_t]
=\lambda t\int_0^1g(y)\,dy,}
$$

with both sides allowed to be $+\infty$.
