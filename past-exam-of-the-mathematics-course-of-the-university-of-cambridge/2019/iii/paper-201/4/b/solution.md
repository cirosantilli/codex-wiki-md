<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The paths of $UX_t$ are continuous, and linear transformation preserves independence of increments. Moreover,

$$
U(X_t-X_s)\sim N\!\left(0,(t-s)UI_dU^T\right)
=N(0,(t-s)I_d)
$$

because $U$ is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md). Thus all defining properties are preserved, proving the [orthogonal invariance of Brownian motion](../../../../../../orthogonal-invariance-of-brownian-motion.md):

$$
\boxed{(UX_t)_{t\geq0}\text{ is Brownian motion in }\mathbb R^d.}
$$
