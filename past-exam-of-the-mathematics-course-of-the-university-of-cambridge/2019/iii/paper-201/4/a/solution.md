↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An $\mathbb R^d$-valued process $(X_t)_{t\geq0}$ is a [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) started at $x$ when:

- $X_0=x$ almost surely;
- its sample paths are almost surely continuous;
- increments over disjoint time intervals are independent; and
- for $0\leq s<t$, $X_t-X_s$ has the centered [multivariate normal distribution](../../../../../../multivariate-normal-distribution.md) with covariance matrix $(t-s)I_d$.
