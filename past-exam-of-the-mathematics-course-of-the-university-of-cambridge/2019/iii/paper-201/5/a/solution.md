<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Planar Brownian motion is recurrent. More explicitly, the [planar Brownian annulus hitting probability](../../../../../../planar-brownian-annulus-hitting-probability.md) gives

$$
\mathbb P_x(T_1<T_R)
=\frac{\log R-\log|x|}{\log R}
\longrightarrow1
\qquad(R\to\infty)
$$

when $|x|>1$. Thus the unit disc is hit almost surely from every starting point. Applying the [Strong Markov property](../../../../../../strong-markov-property.md) after each departure and return shows that such returns occur after arbitrarily large times. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\{t\geq0:|X_t|\leq1\}\text{ is almost surely unbounded}.}
$$
