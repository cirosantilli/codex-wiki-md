<h1 id="3/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Let $Y_{it}$ be patient $i$'s QoL at visit $t$ and let $R_i$ be the last visit at which QoL is observed. The data are [monotone missing-data pattern](../../../../../../monotone-missing-data-pattern.md) when

$$
Y_{it}\text{ missing}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Y_{iu}\text{ missing for every }u>t.
$$

Thus each patient contributes an observed prefix $(Y_{i0},\ldots,Y_{iR_i})$ followed by a missing suffix.
