↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The mean condition gives $a/(a+b)=0.1$, hence $b=9a$. The [Beta distribution](../../../../../../beta-distribution.md) variance is then

$$
\frac{ab}{(a+b)^2(a+b+1)}
=\frac{0.09}{10a+1}.
$$

Since $0.02985^2=9/10100$, equating variances gives $10a+1=101$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{a=10,\qquad b=90.}
$$
