<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

By [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md), with respect to Lebesgue measure on $(0,1)^2$,

$$
\boxed{
\pi(\lambda,\rho_G\mid y_G)
\propto
(\lambda\rho_G)^{y_G}(1-\lambda\rho_G)^{N-y_G}
\rho_G^{a-1}(1-\rho_G)^{b-1}.}
$$

The omitted constant includes the [binomial coefficient](../../../../../../binomial-coefficient.md) and the normalizing constant of the [Beta distribution](../../../../../../beta-distribution.md); the uniform prior contributes only the support indicator.
