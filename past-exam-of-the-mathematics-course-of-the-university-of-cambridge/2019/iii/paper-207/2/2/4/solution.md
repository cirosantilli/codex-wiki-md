The mediation estimate relies on several strong assumptions. Possible failures include [mediator-outcome confounding](../../../../../../../mediator-outcome-confounding.md), residual exposure-outcome or exposure-mediator confounding, and an exposure-induced mediator-outcome confounder. Smoking duration may itself be part of the causal pathway, making adjustment inappropriate. Self-reported cigarettes per day has [measurement error](../../../../../../../measurement-error.md) and does not fully measure tobacco exposure. The case-control sampling can create [selection bias](../../../../../../../selection-bias.md); population stratification can confound the genotype relations; cancer may alter reported smoking; and the product-of-coefficients calculation can be inappropriate for a binary outcome because [odds ratio](../../../../../../../odds-ratio.md) effects are nonlinear and noncollapsible. Any of these can attenuate or distort the indirect effect.
