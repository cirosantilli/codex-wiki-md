↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The evidence would be stronger with prospectively measured smoking before diagnosis, repeated measures of intensity and duration, objective biomarkers such as cotinine, and explicit modeling of cumulative exposure. The authors could use modern counterfactual mediation estimators suited to case-control data and binary outcomes, allow exposure-mediator interaction, report effects on interpretable risk scales, and perform sensitivity analyses for unmeasured mediator-outcome confounding and measurement error. Replication, ancestry adjustment, negative controls, and a [Mendelian randomization](../../../../../../../../mendelian-randomization.md) analysis using additional smoking instruments with credible exclusion restrictions would help distinguish smoking-mediated effects from direct pleiotropic effects of this locus.
