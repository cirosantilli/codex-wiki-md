↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Taken at face value, the mediation analysis says that the rs8034191 allele raises lung-cancer risk mainly through a pathway not captured by reported cigarettes per day: the direct-effect odds ratio is $1.35$, whereas the estimated indirect-effect odds ratio through smoking intensity is $1.01$ and is compatible with no mediation. It does **not** show that smoking intensity has no causal effect on lung cancer. It concerns mediation of this genetic variant's effect through this measured mediator, and a weak variant-to-intensity association, [measurement error](../../../../../../measurement-error.md), or mediation through smoking duration, inhalation, or nicotine exposure could make that indirect pathway appear small.
