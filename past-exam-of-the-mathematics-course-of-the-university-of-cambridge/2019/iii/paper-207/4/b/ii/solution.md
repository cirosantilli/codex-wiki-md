<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $T=$ age at death minus $60$, the observations are

$$
2,\ 7,\ 14^+,\ 15,\ 19^+,\ 21^+.
$$

There are three distinct event times and hence three independent discrete hazards $q_2,q_7,q_{15}$. Their risk-set sizes are $6,5,3$, so

$$
\boxed{L(q_2,q_7,q_{15})
=q_2(1-q_2)^5q_7(1-q_7)^4q_{15}(1-q_{15})^2.}
$$

The [maximum-likelihood estimates](../../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) are $\widehat q_2=1/6$, $\widehat q_7=1/5$, and $\widehat q_{15}=1/3$.
