<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The estimated survivor function is

$$
\widehat S(t)=
\begin{cases}
1,&t<2,\\
5/6,&2\leq t<7,\\
(5/6)(4/5)=2/3,&7\leq t<15,\\
(2/3)(2/3)=4/9,&t\geq15
\end{cases}
$$

until the last observed time. It first falls to at most one half at $t=15$, so

$$
\boxed{\widehat{\operatorname{median}}(T)=15\text{ years}.}
$$
