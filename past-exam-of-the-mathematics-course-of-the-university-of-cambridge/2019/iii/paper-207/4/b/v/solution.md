The corrected [Kaplan–Meier estimator](../../../../../../../kaplan-meier-estimator.md) is $2/3$ immediately after $t=2$ and

$$
(2/3)^2=4/9
$$

immediately after $t=7$. It first crosses one half there, so

$$
\boxed{\widehat{\operatorname{median}}(T)=7\text{ years}.}
$$
