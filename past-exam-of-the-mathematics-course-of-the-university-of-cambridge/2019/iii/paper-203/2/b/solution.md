<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $D$ be [simply connected domain](../../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md) with distinct marked boundary points $a,b$, and choose a [conformal map](../../../../../../conformal-map.md) $f:D\to\mathbb H$ with $f(a)=0$ and $f(b)=\infty$. Chordal $\operatorname{SLE}_\kappa$ from $a$ to $b$ is the unparameterized curve $f^{-1}(\gamma)$, where $\gamma$ is chordal SLE in $(\mathbb H,0,\infty)$.

Any other such map is $\widetilde f=rf$ for some $r>0$. The [Scaling invariance of SLE](../../../../../../scaling-invariance-of-sle.md) says that $r^{-1}\gamma$ has the same unparameterized law as $\gamma$; only its capacity clock changes. Hence the pullback law is independent of $f$. This proves the [Conformal invariance of SLE](../../../../../../conformal-invariance-of-sle.md) definition is well-defined.
