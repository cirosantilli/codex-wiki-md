<h1 id="2/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For compact $K\subset\mathbb H$, let

$$
E_\epsilon=\{z\in K:\tau_\epsilon(z)<\infty\}.
$$

By the [Tonelli theorem](../../../../../../../tonelli-theorem.md) and part (ii),

$$
\mathbb E[\operatorname{Leb}(E_\epsilon)]
=\int_K\mathbb P_z(\tau_\epsilon<\infty)\,dz
\leq C_K\operatorname{Leb}(K)\epsilon^{(8-\kappa)/8}
\longrightarrow0.
$$

Every point in the SLE range has conformal radius tending to zero and therefore belongs to every $E_\epsilon$. Hence the range inside $K$ has zero expected [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md), and so has zero measure almost surely. Exhausting $\mathbb H$ by countably many compact sets proves

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Leb}(\gamma[0,\infty))=0\quad\text{almost surely}.}
$$
