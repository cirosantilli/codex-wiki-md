<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

It has the [Loewner local growth property](../../../../../../../loewner-local-growth-property.md) when, after mapping out the old hull, each short new increment is small: for every $T,\epsilon>0$ there is $\delta>0$ such that

$$
0\leq s<t\leq T,\quad t-s<\delta
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{diam}\bigl(g_s(A_t\setminus A_s)\bigr)<\epsilon.
$$

Equivalent formulations use a crosscut of diameter below $\epsilon$ separating the new increment from infinity.
