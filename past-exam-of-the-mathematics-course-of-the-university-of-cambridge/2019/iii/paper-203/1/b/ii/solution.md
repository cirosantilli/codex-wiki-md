↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Map out $A$ first. The image

$$
D=g_A(C\setminus A)
$$

with its bounded filling is a [compact H-hull](../../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md), and uniqueness of hydrodynamic normalization gives

$$
g_C=g_D\circ g_A.
$$

Comparing the coefficients of $1/z$ at infinity yields the [half-plane-capacity composition rule](../../../../../../../half-plane-capacity-composition-rule.md)

$$
\operatorname{hcap}(C)
=\operatorname{hcap}(A)+\operatorname{hcap}(D)
\geq\operatorname{hcap}(A).
$$

Thus **half-plane capacity is monotone under inclusion**.
