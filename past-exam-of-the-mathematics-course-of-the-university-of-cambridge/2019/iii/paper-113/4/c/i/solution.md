<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [closed immersion](../../../../../../../closed-immersion.md) $i:Y\hookrightarrow X$, the [ideal sheaf of a closed subscheme](../../../../../../../ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) gives

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal I_{Y/X}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X
\longrightarrow i_*\mathcal O_Y\longrightarrow0.
$$

Because the [projective hypersurface](../../../../../../../projective-hypersurface.md) is cut out by one [homogeneous polynomial](../../../../../../../homogeneous-polynomial.md) $f$ of degree $d$, multiplication by $f$ identifies

$$
\boxed{\mathcal I_{Y/X}\cong\mathcal O_X(-d)=\mathcal O_X(1)^{\otimes(-d)}.}
$$

This is the [ideal-sheaf sequence of a projective hypersurface](../../../../../../../ideal-sheaf-sequence-of-a-projective-hypersurface.md).
