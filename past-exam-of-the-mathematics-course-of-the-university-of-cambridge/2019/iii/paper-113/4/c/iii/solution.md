↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Set $n=0$ in part (ii). The constants give an injection $k\hookrightarrow H^0(Y,\mathcal O_Y)$ because the [projective hypersurface](../../../../../../../projective-hypersurface.md) $Y$ is nonempty, while part (ii) gives surjectivity. Thus

$$
H^0(Y,\mathcal O_Y)=k.
$$

A disconnected scheme has a nontrivial [idempotent](../../../../../../../idempotent.md) global regular function, equal to zero and one on its two clopen pieces. A [field](../../../../../../../field.md) has no such idempotent, so the [connectedness from global regular functions](../../../../../../../connectedness-from-global-regular-functions.md) criterion gives

$$
\boxed{Y\text{ is connected}.}
$$
