<h1 id="4/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use again the twisted [ideal-sheaf sequence of a projective hypersurface](../../../../../../../ideal-sheaf-sequence-of-a-projective-hypersurface.md). For $0<i<\dim Y=r-1$, the relevant part of its [long exact sequence in cohomology](../../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) is

$$
H^i(X,\mathcal O_X(n))\longrightarrow H^i(Y,\mathcal O_Y(n))
\longrightarrow H^{i+1}(X,\mathcal O_X(n-d)).
$$

Both outer terms are intermediate cohomology groups on $\mathbb P_k^r$, because $1\leq i<i+1\leq r-1$. They vanish by the [cohomology of twisting sheaves on projective space](../../../../../../../cohomology-of-twisting-sheaves-on-projective-space.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{H^i(Y,\mathcal O_Y(n))=0\qquad(0<i<\dim Y,\ n\in\mathbb Z).}
$$
