<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Tensor the sequence from part (i) with the [twisting sheaf on projective space](../../../../../../../twisting-sheaf-on-projective-space.md) $\mathcal O_X(n)$:

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X(n-d)\xrightarrow{\cdot f}\mathcal O_X(n)
\longrightarrow i_*\mathcal O_Y(n)\longrightarrow0.
$$

Its [long exact sequence in cohomology](../../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) contains

$$
H^0(X,\mathcal O_X(n))\longrightarrow H^0(Y,\mathcal O_Y(n))
\longrightarrow H^1(X,\mathcal O_X(n-d)).
$$

Since $\dim Y\geq1$, we have $r\geq2$, and the final group is intermediate cohomology of projective space. It vanishes by the [cohomology of twisting sheaves on projective space](../../../../../../../cohomology-of-twisting-sheaves-on-projective-space.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{H^0(X,\mathcal O_X(n))\twoheadrightarrow H^0(Y,\mathcal O_Y(n))\quad\text{for every }n\in\mathbb Z.}
$$
