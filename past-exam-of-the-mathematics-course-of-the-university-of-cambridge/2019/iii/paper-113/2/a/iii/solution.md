<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The unique [structure morphism](../../../../../../../structure-morphism.md) $X\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$ factors through the [closed subscheme](../../../../../../../closed-subscheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)$ exactly when the ideal sheaf $p\mathcal O_X$ is zero. By (i), this is equivalent to all rings of local sections having [characteristic of a ring](../../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-ring.md) $p$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{(i)},\ \text{(ii)},\ \text{and (iii) are equivalent}.}
$$
