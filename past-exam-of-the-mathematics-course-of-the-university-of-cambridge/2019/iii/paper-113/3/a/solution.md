<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A standard sufficient hypothesis is that $X$ is both a [Noetherian scheme](../../../../../../noetherian-scheme.md) and an [integral scheme](../../../../../../integral-scheme.md), and is [regular in codimension one](../../../../../../regular-in-codimension-one.md); in particular, a Noetherian [normal scheme](../../../../../../normal-scheme.md) qualifies. A [Weil divisor](../../../../../../weil-divisor.md) is then a finite sum

$$
D=\sum_Zn_Z[Z],\qquad n_Z\in\mathbb Z,
$$

over integral codimension-one closed subschemes $Z$. The local ring at the generic point of each $Z$ is a [discrete valuation ring](../../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md), so every nonzero [rational function](../../../../../../rational-function.md) $g\in k(X)^\times$ has a [principal divisor](../../../../../../principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md)

$$
\operatorname{div}(g)=\sum_Zv_Z(g)[Z].
$$

The [divisor class group](../../../../../../divisor-class-group.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cl}(X)=\operatorname{Div}(X)/\operatorname{Prin}(X).}
$$
