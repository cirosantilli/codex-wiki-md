<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $X=\bigcup_{i=1}^rU_i$ be the stated finite affine cover. By the description of sections on a [principal open subscheme](../../../../../../principal-open-subscheme.md), after increasing denominators separately we may write

$$
b|_{(U_i)_f}=a_i/f^{n_i}
$$

for some $a_i\in\Gamma(U_i,\mathcal O_X)$. Multiplying the numerators by powers of $f$ lets us use one exponent $n$ for every $i$.

On $W_{ij}=U_i\cap U_j$, the section $a_i-a_j$ vanishes after restriction to $(W_{ij})_f$. Each $W_{ij}$ is [quasi-compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md), so part (b) supplies $m_{ij}$ with $f^{m_{ij}}(a_i-a_j)=0$ on $W_{ij}$. Choose one $m$ valid for all the finitely many pairs. The sections $f^ma_i$ now agree on every overlap, and the gluing axiom of a [sheaf of rings](../../../../../../sheaf-of-rings.md) produces $a\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$ with $a|_{U_i}=f^ma_i$. On $X_f$,

$$
a=f^{m+n}b.
$$

Thus **some power of $f$ times $b$ extends to a global [regular function](../../../../../../regular-function.md) on $X$**.
