<h1 id="1/1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The standard [cellular chain complex](../../../../../../../cellular-chain-complex.md) of the [Klein bottle](../../../../../../../klein-bottle.md) gives

$$
H_0(K;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z,
\qquad H_1(K;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2,
\qquad H_2(K;\mathbb Z)=0.
$$

The [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](../../../../../../../universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) therefore yields $H^0=\mathbb Z$, $H^1=\mathbb Z$, and $H^2=\mathbb Z/2$. The degree-one generator is pulled back from the base circle in the circle-bundle description of $K$, so its square is zero. Thus every positive-degree product vanishes. The same calculation for $\mathbb{RP}^2\vee S^1$ gives the [integral cohomology ring of the wedge of the real projective plane and a circle](../../../../../../../integral-cohomology-ring-of-the-wedge-of-the-real-projective-plane-and-a-circle.md), and hence

$$
\boxed{H^*(K;\mathbb Z)\cong H^*(\mathbb{RP}^2\vee S^1;\mathbb Z).}
$$

There is no map $Y\to K$ inducing this isomorphism. The Klein bottle is an [aspherical space](../../../../../../../aspherical-space.md), and its [fundamental group](../../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) is torsion-free. Any map $\mathbb{RP}^2\to K$ therefore induces the trivial homomorphism $\mathbb Z/2\to\pi_1(K)$ and is [null-homotopic](../../../../../../../null-homotopic-map.md). Its pullback on $H^2$ is zero, while the circle summand has no degree-two cohomology. Consequently every map $Y\to K$ is zero on $H^2(K;\mathbb Z)$ and cannot be a cohomology isomorphism.
