<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For unit [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) incidence $\psi_i=e^{ik_0\widehat{\mathbf r}_0\cdot\mathbf r}$, define the scattering [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) difference $\mathbf q=k_0(\widehat{\mathbf r}_0-\widehat{\mathbf r})$ and

$$
A(\widehat{\mathbf r},\widehat{\mathbf r}_0)=\frac1{4\pi}\int_D V(\mathbf r')e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r'}\,d\mathbf r'.
$$

The [outgoing Green function for the three-dimensional Helmholtz equation](../../../../../../outgoing-green-function-for-the-three-dimensional-helmholtz-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\psi_B(\mathbf r)\simeq e^{ik_0\widehat{\mathbf r}_0\cdot\mathbf r}+\frac{e^{ik_0r}}r A,\qquad \psi_R(\mathbf r)\simeq e^{ik_0\widehat{\mathbf r}_0\cdot\mathbf r}\exp\left[\frac{e^{ik_0(r-\widehat{\mathbf r}_0\cdot\mathbf r)}}r A\right].}
$$

The Born [far-field pattern](../../../../../../far-field-pattern.md) is $A$, a [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) sample of the potential. Expanding the far-field Rytov exponential in $1/r$ gives the same leading outgoing term, while the logarithmic representation preserves its accumulated phase and amplitude correction.
