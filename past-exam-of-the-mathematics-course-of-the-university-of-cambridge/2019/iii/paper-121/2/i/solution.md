The [definable power set](../../../../../../definable-power-set-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal D(A)=\operatorname{Def}(A,1)
=\left\{\{x\in A:(A,\in)\models\varphi(x,a_1,\ldots,a_n)\}:\varphi\text{ is a first-order formula and }a_1,\ldots,a_n\in A\right\}.}
$$

Thus it contains exactly the [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) of $A$ definable over the structure $(A,\in)$ using finitely many parameters from $A$.
