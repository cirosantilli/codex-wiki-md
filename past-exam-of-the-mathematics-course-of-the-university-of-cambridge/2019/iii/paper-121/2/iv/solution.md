<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Take a [transitive model](../../../../../../transitive-model.md) $M\models\mathsf{ZFC}+2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_2$. In $M$, choose a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) $e:\omega_2\to\mathcal P(\omega)$ and encode its graph by a set $A\subseteq\omega_2$, using a fixed bijection between $\omega_2\times\omega$ and $\omega_2$. The [relative constructible universe](../../../../../../relative-constructible-universe.md) $L(A)$ can decode $e$, and therefore contains every [real number](../../../../../../real-number.md) of $M$; being an inner model of $M$, it has no additional reals.

Models with the same reals have the same [first uncountable ordinal](../../../../../../first-uncountable-ordinal.md), because their reals code exactly the same countable well-orders. If $L(A)$ satisfied the [Continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../continuum-hypothesis.md), its bijection between $\omega_1$ and the reals would also belong to $M$, contradicting $M\models2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_2$. This is the construction in [relative constructible universe can violate the continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../relative-constructible-universe-can-violate-the-continuum-hypothesis.md), and it gives

$$
\boxed{A\subseteq\omega_2\quad\text{and}\quad L(A)\models\neg\mathsf{CH}.}
$$
