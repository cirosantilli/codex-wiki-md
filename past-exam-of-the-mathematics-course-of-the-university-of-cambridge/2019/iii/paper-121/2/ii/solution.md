<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Fix $a\in L$. Inside the ambient [transitive model](../../../../../../transitive-model.md) $M$, the [Axiom of power set](../../../../../../axiom-of-power-set.md) makes the collection of constructible subsets of $a$ a set. For each such subset $x$, choose the least stage of the [constructible hierarchy](../../../../../../constructible-hierarchy.md) at which $x$ appears. The [Axiom schema of replacement](../../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) and the [supremum](../../../../../../supremum.md) of a set of [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal.md) give an ordinal $\beta$ bounding all these stages; enlarge $\beta$ so that $a\in L_\beta$.

Now

$$
\mathcal P^L(a)=\{x\in L_\beta:x\subseteq a\}
$$

is definable over $L_\beta$ with parameter $a$. It therefore belongs to the [definable power set](../../../../../../definable-power-set-split.md) $\mathcal D(L_\beta)=L_{\beta+1}\subseteq L$. This set contains exactly the subsets of $a$ that belong to the [constructible universe](../../../../../../constructible-universe.md), so it witnesses the [Axiom of power set](../../../../../../axiom-of-power-set.md) in $L$. **Therefore $L\models$ Power Set.**
