↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a condition $p\in\mathbb P$, a set $D\subseteq\mathbb P$ is [dense below a forcing condition](../../../../../../../dense-below-a-forcing-condition.md) $p$ when

$$
\boxed{\forall q\leq p\ \exists r\leq q\ (r\in D).}
$$

Thus every extension of $p$ can be strengthened into $D$.
