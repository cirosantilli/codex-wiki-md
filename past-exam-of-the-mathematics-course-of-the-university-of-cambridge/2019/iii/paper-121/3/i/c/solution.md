↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The forcing order $\mathbb P$ has the [chain condition for forcing](../../../../../../../chain-condition-for-forcing.md) at $\kappa$ when every [antichain in a forcing order](../../../../../../../antichain-in-a-forcing-order.md) has cardinality below $\kappa$:

$$
\boxed{\forall A\subseteq\mathbb P\ \bigl(A\text{ an antichain}\Longrightarrow |A|<\kappa\bigr).}
$$

When this assertion is evaluated inside $M$, both the quantified subsets and their [cardinalities](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) are those of $M$.
