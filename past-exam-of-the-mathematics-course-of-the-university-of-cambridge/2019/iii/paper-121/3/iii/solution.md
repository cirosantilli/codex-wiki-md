<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose first that $p\Vdash^*\varphi$. If $G$ is any [generic filter](../../../../../../generic-filter.md) over $M$ containing $p$, the [forcing theorem](../../../../../../forcing-theorem.md) gives $M[G]\models\varphi$. Hence $p\Vdash\varphi$ in the [semantic forcing relation](../../../../../../semantic-forcing-relation.md).

Conversely, suppose $p\not\Vdash^*\varphi$. By the stated density equivalence for the [syntactic forcing relation](../../../../../../syntactic-forcing-relation.md), some $r\leq p$ satisfies $r\Vdash^*\neg\varphi$. The [Rasiowa–Sikorski lemma](../../../../../../rasiowa-sikorski-lemma.md) supplies a generic filter $G$ over the countable model $M$ containing $r$, and upward closure then gives $p\in G$. The [forcing theorem](../../../../../../forcing-theorem.md) yields $M[G]\models\neg\varphi$, so $p\not\Vdash\varphi$ semantically. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p\Vdash\varphi\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad p\Vdash^*\varphi.}
$$
