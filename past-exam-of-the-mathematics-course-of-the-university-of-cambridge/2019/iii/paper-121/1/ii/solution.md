<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose that the [countable ordinal](../../../../../../countable-ordinal.md) $\alpha$ satisfied $V_\alpha\models\mathsf{ZFC}$. The axioms force $\alpha$ to be a [limit ordinal](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md) above $\omega$, so choose an externally countable [cofinal function](../../../../../../cofinal-function.md) $n\mapsto\alpha_n$ into $\alpha$, with $\alpha_n+1<\alpha$. The internal [Axiom of choice](../../../../../../axiom-of-choice.md) gives a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) in $V_\alpha$ between each $V_{\alpha_n}$ and some ordinal below $\alpha$. Every such ordinal is externally a [countable set](../../../../../../countable-set.md), hence every $V_{\alpha_n}$ is externally countable. The [countable union of countable sets](../../../../../../countable-union-of-countable-sets.md) is countable, so

$$
V_\alpha=\bigcup_{n<\omega}V_{\alpha_n}
$$

would be countable. But $V_\alpha$ contains the full [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) $\mathcal P(\omega)$, which is uncountable by [Cantor theorem](../../../../../../cantor-s-theorem.md). This contradiction is the result [Countable rank-initial segment cannot model ZFC](../../../../../../countable-rank-initial-segment-cannot-model-zfc.md), and therefore

$$
\boxed{V_\alpha\not\models\mathsf{ZFC}.}
$$
