<h1 id="1/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By part (a), the [Paris model](../../../../../../../paris-model.md) $M$ is well-founded. Let $\pi$ be an [automorphism of a first-order structure](../../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md) of $(M,\in^M)$. External [epsilon induction](../../../../../../../epsilon-induction.md) shows that every element is fixed: if $\pi(y)=y$ for every $y\in^M x$, then preservation of membership and extensionality give

$$
\pi(x)=\{\pi(y):y\in^M x\}=x.
$$

Hence $\pi$ is the [identity function](../../../../../../../identity-function.md). This is [Paris models are rigid when their complete theory has a well-founded model](../../../../../../../paris-models-are-rigid-when-their-complete-theory-has-a-well-founded-model.md), so **every Paris model of $T$ is a rigid first-order structure.**
