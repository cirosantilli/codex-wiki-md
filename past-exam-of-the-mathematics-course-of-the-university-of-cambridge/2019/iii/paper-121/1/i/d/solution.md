**U+D.** The [natural numbers](../../../../../../../natural-number.md) are absolute between [transitive models](../../../../../../../transitive-model.md) of [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md), and the statement $x\subseteq\mathbb N$ is the bounded assertion $\forall y\in x\,(y\in\mathbb N)$. Hence the property of being a [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) of $\mathbb N$ is absolute.
