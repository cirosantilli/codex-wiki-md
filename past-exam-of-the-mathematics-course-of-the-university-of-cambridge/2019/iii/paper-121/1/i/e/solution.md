**U.** A witness in the smaller model that $x$ is a [countable set](../../../../../../../countable-set.md) remains a function in the larger model, so countability is described by an [upward absolute formula](../../../../../../../upward-absolute-formula.md). It is not downward absolute: the larger model may have a new enumeration of $x$ that is absent from the smaller model, as explained by [upward absoluteness of countability](../../../../../../../upward-absoluteness-of-countability.md).
