**U+D.** The assertion that a given relation is a [partial order](../../../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) quantifies only over its underlying set and checks that it is a [reflexive relation](../../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md), an [antisymmetric relation](../../../../../../../antisymmetric-relation.md) and a [transitive relation](../../../../../../../transitive-relation.md). It is therefore a [bounded formula in set theory](../../../../../../../bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md) and is absolute between [transitive models](../../../../../../../transitive-model.md).
