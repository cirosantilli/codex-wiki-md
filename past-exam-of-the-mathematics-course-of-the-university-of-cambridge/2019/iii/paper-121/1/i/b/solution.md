**D.** [Downward absoluteness of cardinalhood](../../../../../../../downward-absoluteness-of-cardinalhood.md) holds because, if the larger [transitive model](../../../../../../../transitive-model.md) sees no [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) with a smaller [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md), neither can the smaller model, whose functions form only a subset of those in the larger model. Cardinalhood is not described by an [upward absolute formula](../../../../../../../upward-absolute-formula.md), because the larger model can contain a new bijection collapsing an ordinal that the smaller model regards as a [cardinal number](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md).
