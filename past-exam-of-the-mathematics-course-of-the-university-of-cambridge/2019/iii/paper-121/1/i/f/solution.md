↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

**N.** A larger model may collapse a singular [cardinal number](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md), destroying cardinalhood, so the assertion is not described by an [upward absolute formula](../../../../../../../upward-absolute-formula.md). It may instead add a short [cofinal function](../../../../../../../cofinal-function.md) to a [regular cardinal](../../../../../../../regular-cardinal.md) in the smaller model, so it is not described by a [downward absolute formula](../../../../../../../downward-absolute-formula.md). This is the [nonabsoluteness of singular cardinalhood](../../../../../../../nonabsoluteness-of-singular-cardinalhood.md).
