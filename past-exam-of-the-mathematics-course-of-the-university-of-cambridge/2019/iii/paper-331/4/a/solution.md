<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The zero [streamfunction](../../../../../../stream-function.md) gives zero [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md). The temperature $\theta_0=-z$ obeys both prescribed temperature values, has no horizontal [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md), and has zero [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md). Thus it satisfies both equations with $\psi_0=0$:

$$
\boxed{\psi_0=0,\qquad\theta_0=-z.}
$$

This is the conductive state of [Darcy-Bénard convection](../../../../../../darcy-benard-convection.md).
