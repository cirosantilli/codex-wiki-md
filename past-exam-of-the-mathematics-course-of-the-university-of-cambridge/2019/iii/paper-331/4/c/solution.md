<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The full perturbation equations are

$$
\nabla^2\psi'+R\theta'_x=0,\qquad
\nabla^2\theta'-\psi'_x=\theta'_t+J(\psi',\theta'),
$$

where the [streamfunction advection bracket](../../../../../../streamfunction-advection-bracket.md) is $J(f,g)=f_zg_x-f_xg_z$. Put $R=R_c+\epsilon^2$ and introduce the [slow time](../../../../../../slow-time.md) $T=\epsilon^2t$, so $\partial_t=\epsilon^2\partial_T$ on the amplitude-dependent fields. Then

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\psi'+R_c\theta'_x=-\epsilon^2\theta'_x,\qquad
\nabla^2\theta'-\psi'_x=\epsilon^2\theta'_T+J(\psi',\theta').}
$$

The [weakly nonlinear expansion](../../../../../../weakly-nonlinear-expansion.md) balances the small linear growth against the cubic saturation on this slow time scale.
