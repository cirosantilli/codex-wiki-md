<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose $a=1/2$ in the [power-transformed Taylor–Goldstein energy identity](../../../../../../../power-transformed-taylor-goldstein-energy-identity.md) and let $Q=N^2-(U')^2/4$. The result is

$$
\int V(|q'|^2+k^2|q|^2)dz=\int\left(\frac QV-\frac{U''}2\right)|q|^2dz.
$$

Because $\operatorname{Im}V=-c_i$ and $\operatorname{Im}(1/V)=c_i/|V|^2$, its [imaginary part](../../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) gives

$$
c_i\int_{-L}^L\left[|q'|^2+k^2|q|^2+\frac{Q}{|U-c|^2}|q|^2\right]dz=0.
$$

If $4N^2>(U')^2$ everywhere, the integral is strictly positive for every nonzero [eigenfunction](../../../../../../../eigenfunction.md), so a mode with $c_i\ne0$ is impossible. Thus the flow is neutrally stable to these inviscid [normal modes](../../../../../../../normal-mode.md). This is the [Miles–Howard theorem](../../../../../../../miles-howard-theorem.md), expressed as a lower bound $\mathrm{Ri}>1/4$ on the [gradient Richardson number](../../../../../../../gradient-richardson-number.md). It excludes exponential modal growth; it does not by itself exclude [transient growth](../../../../../../../transient-growth.md).
