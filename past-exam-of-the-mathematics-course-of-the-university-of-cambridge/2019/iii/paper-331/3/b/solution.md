<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Termwise [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) of the [matrix exponential](../../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) gives $A'=LA$ and $A(0)=I$. Thus $x(t)=A(t)x(0)$ satisfies $x'=Lx$ and the prescribed [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md). Uniqueness for a [linear differential equation](../../../../../../linear-differential-equation.md) makes it the solution.
