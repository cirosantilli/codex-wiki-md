<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [perfect gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) with constant [specific heat capacity](../../../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md) $c_v$, the [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) gives

$$
ds=c_v\,d\log\left(\frac p{\rho^\gamma}\right).
$$

Using the [pressure-density Hugoniot relation for a perfect gas](../../../../../../pressure-density-hugoniot-relation-for-a-perfect-gas.md), the [entropy production in a perfect-gas shock](../../../../../../entropy-production-in-a-perfect-gas-shock.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{[s]}{c_v}=\log P-\gamma\log\left[\frac{(\gamma+1)P+\gamma-1}{(\gamma-1)P+\gamma+1}\right].}
$$

This is positive for $P>1$, as required by the [Second law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../second-law-of-thermodynamics.md) for the physical compressive [normal shock wave](../../../../../../normal-shock-wave.md).
