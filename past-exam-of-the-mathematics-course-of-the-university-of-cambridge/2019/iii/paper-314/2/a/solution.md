<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [shock frame](../../../../../../shock-frame.md), integrate [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md), momentum conservation, and total-energy conservation across a thin interval containing the stationary [normal shock wave](../../../../../../normal-shock-wave.md). No mass, momentum, or energy is stored in the vanishingly thin interval, so each flux has the same value on both sides. For a [perfect gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md), the [specific enthalpy](../../../../../../specific-enthalpy.md) is $h=\gamma p/[(\gamma-1)\rho]$. The resulting [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions for a perfect gas](../../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\rho_1u_1&=\rho_2u_2,\\
p_1+\rho_1u_1^2&=p_2+\rho_2u_2^2,\\
u_1\left(\frac{\rho_1u_1^2}{2}+\frac{\gamma p_1}{\gamma-1}\right)
&=u_2\left(\frac{\rho_2u_2^2}{2}+\frac{\gamma p_2}{\gamma-1}\right).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The momentum flux includes both transported momentum and [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) force. The energy flux includes kinetic energy and [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy.md), the latter accounting for internal energy and the work needed to push gas through the interval. [Entropy production](../../../../../../entropy-production.md) can occur inside the [normal shock wave](../../../../../../normal-shock-wave.md) even though total energy is conserved.
