<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The calculation in part (a) leaves the [cross-helicity conservation law](../../../../../../cross-helicity-conservation-law.md) source

$$
\boxed{Q_h=T\,\mathbf B\cdot\nabla s.}
$$

At positive [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md), this vanishes exactly when the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) is tangent to constant-[specific entropy](../../../../../../specific-entropy.md) surfaces: $\mathbf B\cdot\nabla s=0$. A [homentropic flow](../../../../../../homentropic-flow.md) is a sufficient special case; uniform [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) throughout space is not necessary. Mere advection of [specific entropy](../../../../../../specific-entropy.md), $Ds/Dt=0$, constrains its variation along the velocity rather than along the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md), so it does not by itself eliminate this source. To conserve total [cross-helicity](../../../../../../cross-helicity.md) in a volume, the net boundary flux must also vanish.
