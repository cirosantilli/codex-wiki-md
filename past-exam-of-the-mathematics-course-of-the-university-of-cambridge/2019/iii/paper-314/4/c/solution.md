<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A regular [sonic point](../../../../../../sonic-point.md) must make both sides of the flow equation vanish:

$$
v_s=c_s,\qquad r_s=\frac{GM_\star}{3c_s^2}.
$$

Substituting into the [Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) yields

$$
c_s^2\left(\frac1{\gamma-1}-\frac52\right)=\frac{c_0^2}{\gamma-1},
\qquad
c_s^2=\frac{2c_0^2}{7-5\gamma}.
$$

For $\gamma>1$, positive finite [sound speed](../../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) therefore requires the [critical adiabatic index for dipolar accretion](../../../../../../critical-adiabatic-index-for-dipolar-accretion.md)

$$
\boxed{1<\gamma<\gamma_{\rm crit}=\frac75.}
$$

To check that this gives real regular crossings, differentiate the flow equation at the [sonic point](../../../../../../sonic-point.md) and put $x=r_sv'_s/c_s$. The [transonic accretion in a power-law tube](../../../../../../transonic-accretion-in-a-power-law-tube.md) calculation gives

$$
(\gamma+1)x^2+6(\gamma-1)x+9\gamma-12=0,
\qquad
x=\frac{-3(\gamma-1)\pm\sqrt{3(7-5\gamma)}}{\gamma+1}.
$$

The minus sign gives the [transonic branch](../../../../../../transonic-branch.md) whose [Mach number](../../../../../../mach-number.md) increases inward. At $\gamma=7/5$ the positive-energy reservoir cannot match a finite [sonic point](../../../../../../sonic-point.md); for larger $\gamma$ the required $c_s^2$ is negative. A physical surface-crossing solution also requires $r_s>R_\star$ and validity of the narrow [flux tube](../../../../../../flux-tube.md) approximation up to the sonic region.
