<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the narrow polar [flux tube](../../../../../../flux-tube.md), the [magnetic field lines](../../../../../../magnetic-field-line.md) are almost radial and the transverse width is much smaller than the radial scale. The [Lorentz force density](../../../../../../lorentz-force-density.md) has no component along the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md), so the longitudinal [magnetically channelled accretion](../../../../../../magnetically-channelled-accretion.md) is hydrodynamic. Let $v(r)>0$ denote inward speed. [Mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md), the [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md), and the [polytropic equation of state](../../../../../../polytropic-equation-of-state.md) give

$$
\rho vA=\dot M_{\rm tube},\qquad
v\frac{dv}{dr}=-\frac1\rho\frac{dp}{dr}-\frac{GM_\star}{r^2},\qquad
p=K\rho^\gamma.
$$

Using the [adiabatic sound speed](../../../../../../adiabatic-sound-speed.md) $c^2=\gamma K\rho^{\gamma-1}$ and $A\propto r^3$,

$$
\frac{\rho'}\rho=-\frac{v'}v-\frac3r,
\qquad
\boxed{\left(v-\frac{c^2}{v}\right)v'=\frac{3c^2}{r}-\frac{GM_\star}{r^2}.}
$$

The [Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{v^2}{2}+\frac{c^2}{\gamma-1}-\frac{GM_\star}{r}=\frac{c_0^2}{\gamma-1},}
$$

where the right-hand side comes from matching to a nearly stationary reservoir with negligible [gravitational potential](../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md). The fixed [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) provides transverse confinement; neglecting magnetic forces in this one-dimensional equation concerns their longitudinal component.
