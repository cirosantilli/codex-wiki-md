The [spectral norm](../../../../../../../matrix-2-norm.md) is the [operator norm](../../../../../../../operator-norm.md) induced by the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md):

$$
\|A\|=\sup_{v\ne0}\frac{\|Av\|_2}{\|v\|_2}
=\sqrt{\lambda_{\max}(A^\dagger A)}.
$$

The [Pauli operators](../../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) $X_1$ and $Z_2$ act on different [qubits](../../../../../../../qubit.md), and each is a [unitary operator](../../../../../../../unitary-operator.md). Their product is therefore unitary and preserves the norm of every vector. Hence

$$
\boxed{\|X_1Z_2\|=1.}
$$
