<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For finite-dimensional [matrices](../../../../../../../matrix.md) $A_1,\ldots,A_m$, the [Lie-Trotter product formula](../../../../../../../lie-product-formula.md) states

$$
\boxed{e^{t\sum_{j=1}^m A_j}
=\lim_{k\to\infty}\left(e^{tA_1/k}\cdots e^{tA_m/k}\right)^k,}
$$

where convergence is in [operator norm](../../../../../../../operator-norm.md). In particular, for [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) $H_j$, set $A_j=-iH_j$ to obtain a product of [unitary operators](../../../../../../../unitary-operator.md) approximating $e^{-it\sum_jH_j}$.
