<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The map $x\mapsto-x\pmod K$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) and an [involution](../../../../../../../involution.md), so its [permutation matrix](../../../../../../../permutation-matrix.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{S^\dagger=S,\qquad S^2=I.}
$$

In particular $S^\dagger S=I$, which proves that $S$ is a [unitary operator](../../../../../../../unitary-operator.md). Equivalently, it permutes the [computational basis](../../../../../../../computational-basis.md) and therefore preserves every [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product.md).
