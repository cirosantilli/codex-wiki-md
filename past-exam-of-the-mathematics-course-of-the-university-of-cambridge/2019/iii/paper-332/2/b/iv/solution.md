<h1 id="2/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Impose the [eutectic temperature](../../../../../../../eutectic-temperature.md) at $\zeta=0$ in the [large-concentration thermal profile of a pulled mush](../../../../../../../large-concentration-thermal-profile-of-a-pulled-mush.md):

$$
-1=-\frac{\theta_\infty}{\Omega}(e^{r\Omega H}-1).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{Vh}{\kappa}=H=\frac1{r\Omega}\log\left(1+\frac\Omega{\theta_\infty}\right),\qquad \Omega=1+\frac S{r\mathcal C}.}
$$

The thickness is proportional to [thermal diffusivity](../../../../../../../thermal-diffusivity.md) divided by pulling speed. Latent-heat release modifies the exponential decay rate through $\Omega$, while the far-liquid temperature fixes how far the profile can remain below the [liquidus](../../../../../../../liquidus.md).
