<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let a representative fixed volume have mixture [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) $\bar\rho=\rho_s\varphi+\rho_l(1-\varphi)$. Since the crystal framework is rigid and stationary, the liquid mass flux is $\rho_l\mathbf u$, where $\mathbf u$ is [Darcy velocity](../../../../../../darcy-velocity.md). [Mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives

$$
\partial_t\bar\rho+\nabla\cdot(\rho_l\mathbf u)=0.
$$

The constant phase densities therefore imply the [phase-change volume source in a rigid mush](../../../../../../phase-change-volume-source-in-a-rigid-mush.md)

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=(1-r)\varphi_t,\qquad r=\rho_s/\rho_l.}
$$

For ice less dense than brine, increasing [solid fraction](../../../../../../solid-fraction.md) creates an expansion flow; when the phase densities agree, this source vanishes.
