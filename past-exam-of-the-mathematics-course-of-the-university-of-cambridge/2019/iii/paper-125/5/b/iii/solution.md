<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $T$ have order $m>0$. Property (ii) gives $\widehat h(T)=0$, since

$$
m^2\widehat h(T)=\widehat h(mT)=\widehat h(O)=0.
$$

It also gives

$$
m^2\widehat h(P+T)=\widehat h(mP+mT)=\widehat h(mP)=m^2\widehat h(P).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\widehat h(P+T)=\widehat h(P)}
$$

for every rational [torsion point of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../../torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). The construction in part (i), quadraticity in part (ii), and this identity establish existence; the bounded-difference and doubling argument in part (i) establishes uniqueness.
