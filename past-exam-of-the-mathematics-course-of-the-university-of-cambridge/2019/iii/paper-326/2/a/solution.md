<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [Banach space](../../../../../../banach-space-split.md) $X$, [weak convergence](../../../../../../weak-convergence.md) $u_k\rightharpoonup u$ means

$$
\ell(u_k)\to\ell(u)\quad\text{for every }\ell\in X^*.
$$

For its [continuous dual space](../../../../../../continuous-dual-space-split.md) $X^*$, convergence in the [weak-star topology](../../../../../../weak-star-topology.md) means

$$
\ell_k(x)\to\ell(x)\quad\text{for every }x\in X.
$$

Weak convergence on $X^*$ tests against all of $X^{**}$, whereas weak-star convergence tests only against the canonical image of $X$. Thus weak convergence always implies weak-star convergence. If $X$ is a [reflexive Banach space](../../../../../../reflexive-banach-space.md), that image is all of $X^{**}$, so **the weak and weak-star topologies on $X^*$ coincide**. Weak-star topology is defined relative to a chosen predual; it is not an extra intrinsic topology on an arbitrary Banach space without such an identification.
