<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Radon-Riesz property](../../../../../../radon-riesz-property.md) of a norm is the implication

$$
u_k\rightharpoonup u,\quad\|u_k\|\to\|u\|
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\|u_k-u\|\to0.
$$

In a [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), expand the square using its [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md):

$$
\|u_k-u\|^2=\|u_k\|^2+\|u\|^2-2\operatorname{Re}\langle u_k,u\rangle.
$$

[Weak convergence](../../../../../../weak-convergence.md) gives $\langle u_k,u\rangle\to\|u\|^2$, and convergence of norms gives $\|u_k\|^2\to\|u\|^2$. Hence the right side tends to zero. This proves the Hilbert-space [Radon-Riesz theorem](../../../../../../radon-riesz-theorem.md).
