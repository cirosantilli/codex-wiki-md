<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $V_i=\partial V/\partial\phi_i$ and $M^2_{ij}=\partial_i\partial_jV|_{\phi_0}$ be the [scalar mass matrix](../../../../../../scalar-mass-matrix.md), which is the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) of the [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) at the vacuum. Invariance under the infinitesimal [Lie group action](../../../../../../lie-group-action.md) gives

$$
V_i(\phi)(it^a\phi)_i=0.
$$

Differentiate with respect to $\phi_j$ and evaluate at the [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) $\phi_0$. Since $V_i(\phi_0)=0$ at a minimum,

$$
M^2_{ji}(it^a\phi_0)_i=0.
$$

Thus every tangent vector $it^a\phi_0$ generated by a broken [Lie algebra generator](../../../../../../lie-algebra-generator.md) is a [zero eigenvalue](../../../../../../zero-eigenvalue.md) eigenvector of the [scalar mass matrix](../../../../../../scalar-mass-matrix.md). The unbroken generators are precisely those in the stabilizer Lie algebra $\mathfrak h$ and give the zero tangent vector; independent broken directions span $\mathfrak g/\mathfrak h$. Hence [Goldstone theorem](../../../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\dim G-\dim H}
$$

massless scalar modes at the classical level.
