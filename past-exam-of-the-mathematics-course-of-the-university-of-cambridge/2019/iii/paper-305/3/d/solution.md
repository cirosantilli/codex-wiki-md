<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The result grows linearly with the squared [centre-of-momentum energy](../../../../../../centre-of-momentum-energy.md), $\sigma\propto G_F^2s$. This growth eventually violates [partial-wave unitarity](../../../../../../partial-wave-unitarity.md) and signals the breakdown of the pointlike [Fermi interaction](../../../../../../fermi-interaction.md). At energies comparable to $M_W$ or $M_Z$, the full gauge-boson propagators must replace the contact interaction; the renormalizable electroweak theory then softens the high-energy behavior.
