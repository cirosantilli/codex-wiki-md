**Allowed.** A single $t$-channel [W boson](../../../../../../../w-boson.md) mediates the [weak charged current](../../../../../../../charged-current.md) transitions $u\to d$ and $\bar c\to\bar s$. The amplitude is proportional to the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) product $V_{ud}V_{cs}^*$.
