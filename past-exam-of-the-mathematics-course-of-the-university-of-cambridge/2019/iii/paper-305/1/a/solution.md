↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [mode expansion of a Dirac field](../../../../../../mode-expansion-of-a-dirac-field.md), $b^s(p)$ is the [fermionic annihilation operator](../../../../../../fermionic-annihilation-operator.md) for a particle of [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) $p$ and [spin angular momentum](../../../../../../spin.md) label $s$, while $d^{s\dagger}(p)$ is the [fermionic creation operator](../../../../../../fermionic-creation-operator.md) for the corresponding [antiparticle](../../../../../../antiparticle.md). The [Dirac spinor](../../../../../../dirac-spinor.md) $u^s(p)$ is the positive-frequency particle wavefunction and $v^s(p)$ is the negative-frequency antiparticle wavefunction; they solve $(\not p-m)u^s=0$ and $(\not p+m)v^s=0$, respectively.
