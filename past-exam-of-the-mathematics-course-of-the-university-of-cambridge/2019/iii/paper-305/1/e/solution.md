↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The operator $ia\bar\psi\sigma^{\mu\nu}\gamma^5\psi F_{\mu\nu}$ is the relativistic [fermion electric dipole moment operator](../../../../../../fermion-electric-dipole-moment-operator.md). Its nonrelativistic limit contains $\mathbf S\cdot\mathbf E$: [spin angular momentum](../../../../../../spin.md) is an [axial vector](../../../../../../pseudovector.md), whereas the [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md) is a polar vector. It is consequently odd under [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md). Both the pseudotensor fermion bilinear and the [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) are odd under [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md), so their product is even. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P:-1,\qquad C:+1,\qquad CP:-1.}
$$

The [CPT theorem](../../../../../../cpt-theorem.md) then makes it odd under [time-reversal symmetry](../../../../../../t-symmetry.md). Such an interaction can arise only from [CP violation](../../../../../../cp-violation.md). The [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) therefore induces a nonzero Standard Model electric dipole moment at sufficiently high loop order, but its flavor structure and loop suppressions make the result extraordinarily small.
