<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $\alpha_i=g_i^2/(4\pi)$, the stated [renormalization-group beta function](../../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) implies

$$
\frac{d\alpha_i}{d\log\mu}=-\frac{\beta_i}{2\pi}\alpha_i^2,
\qquad
\frac{d\alpha_i^{-1}}{d\log\mu}=\frac{\beta_i}{2\pi}.
$$

For $\beta_i>0$, the [running coupling](../../../../../../running-coupling.md) decreases at high energy: the theory is [asymptotically free](../../../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md) and becomes strong in the infrared, as in [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md). For $\beta_i<0$, it is infrared free but grows toward an ultraviolet [Landau pole](../../../../../../landau-pole.md).
