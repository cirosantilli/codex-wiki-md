<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Integrating the [renormalization-group beta function](../../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) and defining $\Lambda$ by $\alpha_3^{-1}(\Lambda)=0$ gives

$$
\alpha_3^{-1}(\mu)=\frac{\beta_3}{2\pi}\log\frac\mu\Lambda,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{\alpha_3(\mu)=\frac{2\pi}{\beta_3\log(\mu/\Lambda)}}.
$$

The scale $\Lambda$ is the [QCD scale](../../../../../../qcd-scale.md) in this one-loop approximation.
