↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For one generation, the [Standard Model representation](../../../../../../standard-model-representation.md) content under $SU(2)_L\times SU(3)_C$ is

$$
Q_L=(u_L,d_L):(\mathbf2,\mathbf3),\quad
u_R:(\mathbf1,\mathbf3),\quad d_R:(\mathbf1,\mathbf3),
$$



$$
L_L=(\nu_{eL},e_L):(\mathbf2,\mathbf1),\quad
e_R:(\mathbf1,\mathbf1),\quad
H:(\mathbf2,\mathbf1),
$$

with no [right-handed neutrino](../../../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md). For $SU(3)_C$, three generations give $n_L=6$ from the two left-handed quark flavors and $n_R=6$ from the two right-handed quark flavors, while $n_s=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\beta_3=\frac{11}{3}(3)-\frac13(6+6)=7.}
$$

For $SU(2)_L$, each generation supplies three colored quark doublets and one lepton doublet, so $n_L=12$, $n_R=0$, and the single complex [Higgs doublet](../../../../../../higgs-field.md) gives $n_s=1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\beta_2=\frac{11}{3}(2)-\frac13(12)-\frac16=\frac{19}{6}.}
$$
