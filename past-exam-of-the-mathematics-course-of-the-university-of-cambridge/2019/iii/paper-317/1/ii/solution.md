<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using $R\propto M^{1/13}$ in the [radiative diffusion in a star](../../../../../../radiative-diffusion-in-a-star.md) scaling from part (i),

$$
L\propto M^{11/2}R^{-1/2}\propto M^{11/2-1/26}.
$$

Therefore the [mass-luminosity relation](../../../../../../mass-luminosity-relation.md) is

$$
\boxed{L\propto M^{71/13}.}
$$

The nuclear scaling independently gives $L\propto M^6R^{-7}\propto M^{6-7/13}=M^{71/13}$, consistent with [radiative homology with proton-proton burning and Kramers opacity](../../../../../../radiative-homology-with-proton-proton-burning-and-kramers-opacity.md).
