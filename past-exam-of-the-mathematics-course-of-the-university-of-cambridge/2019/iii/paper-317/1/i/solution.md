<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume [stellar homology](../../../../../../stellar-homology.md): fixed dimensionless profiles, uniform fixed composition, negligible [radiation pressure](../../../../../../radiation-pressure.md), [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) support, and radiative transport throughout the model. [Mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives $\rho_c\propto M/R^3$. The [hydrostatic pressure support equation](../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure-support-equation.md) gives $P_c\propto GM^2/R^4$; combining with $P\propto\rho T$ gives $T_c\propto M/R$.

Integrating specific [proton–proton chain](../../../../../../proton-proton-chain.md) energy generation over mass gives

$$
L_{\rm nuc}\propto M\rho_cT_c^4\propto M^6R^{-7}.
$$

For [radiative diffusion in a star](../../../../../../radiative-diffusion-in-a-star.md), $T_c/R\propto\kappa_c\rho_cL/(R^2T_c^3)$, hence $L_{\rm rad}\propto RT_c^4/(\kappa_c\rho_c)$. With the [Kramers' opacity law](../../../../../../kramers-opacity-law.md) $\kappa_c\propto\rho_cT_c^{-7/2}$,

$$
L_{\rm rad}\propto\frac{RT_c^{15/2}}{\rho_c^2}\propto M^{11/2}R^{-1/2}.
$$

Equating generated and transported luminosity gives $M^{1/2}\propto R^{13/2}$, so the [radiative homology with proton-proton burning and Kramers opacity](../../../../../../radiative-homology-with-proton-proton-burning-and-kramers-opacity.md) relation is

$$
\boxed{M\propto R^{13}.}
$$

The proportionality constants are fixed only within the adopted homologous family, not for arbitrary evolving stars.
