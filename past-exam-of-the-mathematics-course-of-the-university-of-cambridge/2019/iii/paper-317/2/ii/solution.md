<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Eddington closure approximation](../../../../../../eddington-closure-approximation.md) with the standard surface boundary condition gives the [grey atmosphere](../../../../../../grey-atmosphere.md) profile

$$
T^4=\frac34T_{\rm eff}^4\left(\tau+\frac23\right).
$$

Together with the specified pressure law, logarithmic differentiation gives

$$
\frac{d\log T}{d\tau}=\frac1{4(\tau+2/3)},\qquad
\frac{d\log P}{d\tau}=\frac{3}{4(1+3\tau/2)\log(1+3\tau/2)}.
$$

Consequently the [radiative temperature gradient](../../../../../../radiative-temperature-gradient.md) is

$$
\nabla_{\rm rad}=\frac{d\log T}{d\log P}=\frac12\log(1+3\tau/2).
$$

For a monatomic [perfect gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md), $\gamma=5/3$ and the [adiabatic temperature gradient](../../../../../../adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md) is $(\gamma-1)/\gamma=2/5$. Uniform composition makes the [Schwarzschild criterion](../../../../../../schwarzschild-criterion.md) applicable. The first marginal layer therefore satisfies $\log(1+3\tau_c/2)=4/5$, giving the [convection onset in a logarithmic-pressure grey atmosphere](../../../../../../convection-onset-in-a-logarithmic-pressure-grey-atmosphere.md)

$$
\boxed{\tau_c=\frac23(e^{4/5}-1)\simeq0.817.}
$$

The assumed profile becomes convectively unstable for $\tau>\tau_c$; further inward, a self-consistent model must account for convective transport.
