<h1 id="3/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\omega=-\xi_1^2\theta'(\xi_1)$ be the [Lane-Emden surface mass constant](../../../../../../../lane-emden-surface-mass-constant.md). The [Lane-Emden mass formula](../../../../../../../lane-emden-mass-formula.md) gives

$$
R=a\xi_1,\qquad M=4\pi a^3\rho_c\omega,
\qquad a^2=\frac{5K_{\rm NR}}{8\pi G}\rho_c^{-1/3}.
$$

Thus $R\propto\rho_c^{-1/6}$ while $M\propto\rho_c^{1/2}$ at fixed composition. Eliminating central [mass density](../../../../../../../density.md) gives the [polytropic mass-radius relation](../../../../../../../polytropic-mass-radius-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{M\propto R^{-3},\qquad s=-3.}
$$

More explicitly,

$$
R=\xi_1(4\pi\omega)^{1/3}\frac{5K_{\rm NR}}{8\pi G}M^{-1/3}.
$$

Increasing the mass of a [nonrelativistic white dwarf](../../../../../../../nonrelativistic-white-dwarf.md) decreases its radius. This scaling ceases to apply when relativistic [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) becomes important.
