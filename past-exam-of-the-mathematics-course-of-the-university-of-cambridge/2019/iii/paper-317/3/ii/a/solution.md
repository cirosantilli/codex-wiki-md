<h1 id="3/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [nonrelativistic white dwarf](../../../../../../../nonrelativistic-white-dwarf.md), combine the [hydrostatic pressure support equation](../../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure-support-equation.md) and [enclosed mass](../../../../../../../enclosed-mass.md) equation:

$$
\frac{dP}{dr}=-\frac{Gm\rho}{r^2},\qquad
\frac{dm}{dr}=4\pi r^2\rho.
$$

Eliminating $m$ yields

$$
\boxed{\frac1{r^2}\frac d{dr}\left(\frac{r^2}{\rho}\frac{dP}{dr}\right)=-4\pi G\rho,\qquad P=K_{\rm NR}\rho^{5/3}.}
$$

Introduce the [Lane-Emden variables for a stellar polytrope](../../../../../../../lane-emden-variables-for-a-stellar-polytrope.md),

$$
\rho=\rho_c\theta^{3/2},\qquad r=a\xi,\qquad
 a^2=\frac{5K_{\rm NR}}{8\pi G}\rho_c^{-1/3}.
$$

The resulting [Lane-Emden equation](../../../../../../../lane-emden-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac1{\xi^2}\frac d{d\xi}(\xi^2\theta')=-\theta^{3/2},\qquad
\theta(0)=1,\quad\theta'(0)=0.}
$$

The [white dwarf](../../../../../../../white-dwarf.md) surface is at the first zero $\xi_1$ of $\theta$, with $R=a\xi_1$.
