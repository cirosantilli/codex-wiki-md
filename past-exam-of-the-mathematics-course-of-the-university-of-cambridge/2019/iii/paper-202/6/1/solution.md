<h1 id="6/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A continuous finite-variation path has zero [quadratic variation](../../../../../../quadratic-variation.md). Hence a continuous finite-variation martingale $M$ satisfies $\langle M\rangle=0$. After localizing to make it square-integrable,

$$
\mathbb E[(M_t-M_0)^2]=\mathbb E[\langle M\rangle_t]=0.
$$

Letting the localization level tend to infinity shows that **$M_t=M_0$ for every $t$ almost surely**; continuity makes the equality simultaneous in $t$.
