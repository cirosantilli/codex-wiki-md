<h1 id="4/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For every real $\lambda$, the [quadratic variation](../../../../../../quadratic-variation.md) of $\lambda(M-M_s)+(N-N_s)$ on $[s,t]$ is nonnegative:

$$
\lambda^2\Delta\langle M\rangle
+2\lambda\Delta\langle M,N\rangle
+\Delta\langle N\rangle\geq0.
$$

Its discriminant is therefore nonpositive. This gives the pathwise [Kunita-Watanabe inequality](../../../../../../kunita-watanabe-inequality.md)

$$
\boxed{|\langle M,N\rangle_t-\langle M,N\rangle_s|
\leq\sqrt{\langle M\rangle_t-\langle M\rangle_s}
\sqrt{\langle N\rangle_t-\langle N\rangle_s}.}
$$
