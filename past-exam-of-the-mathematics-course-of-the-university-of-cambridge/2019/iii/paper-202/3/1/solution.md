<h1 id="3/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For an SDE driven by Brownian motion, a [strong solution of a stochastic differential equation](../../../../../../strong-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md) is adapted to the completed filtration generated by a prescribed Brownian motion and satisfies the equation on that space. A [weak solution of a stochastic differential equation](../../../../../../weak-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md) consists of a probability space, filtration, Brownian motion, and adapted solution satisfying the equation. [Uniqueness in law](../../../../../../uniqueness-in-law.md) means that any two weak solutions with the same initial law have the same law as processes. [Pathwise uniqueness](../../../../../../pathwise-uniqueness.md) means that two solutions on the same filtered space, driven by the same Brownian motion and having the same initial value, are indistinguishable.
