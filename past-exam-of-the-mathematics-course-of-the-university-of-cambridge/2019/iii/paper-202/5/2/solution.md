<h1 id="5/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Since $B_t=\int_0^t1\,dB_s$, the [Itô isometry](../../../../../../ito-isometry.md) and polarization give

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E\left[B_t\int_0^tf(s)dB_s\right]
=\int_0^t1\cdot f(s)ds
=\int_0^tf(s)ds.}
$$
