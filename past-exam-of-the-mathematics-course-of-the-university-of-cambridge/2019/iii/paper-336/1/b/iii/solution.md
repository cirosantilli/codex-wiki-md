<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At $a=1$, the endpoint is degenerate: $S(t)=\sinh t-t=t^3/6+O(t^5)$. The contributing width is therefore $t=O(\nu^{-1/3})$, rather than the width in part (i). With $v=\nu t^3/6$, the formula for a [degenerate endpoint in Laplace's method](../../../../../../../degenerate-endpoint-in-laplace-s-method.md) gives

$$
\boxed{A_\nu(\nu)\sim\int_0^\infty e^{-\nu t^3/6}\,dt=\frac{6^{1/3}}{3\nu^{1/3}}\Gamma(1/3).}
$$

The coefficient is a [Gamma function](../../../../../../../gamma-function.md) value obtained by the [Gamma integral](../../../../../../../gamma-integral.md).
