<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The hypothesis says exactly that $\mathcal A$ is a [k-Sperner family](../../../../../../k-sperner-family.md) with $k=2$: it contains no three members forming a strict inclusion chain. Every [maximal chain in a Boolean lattice](../../../../../../maximal-chain-in-a-boolean-lattice.md) consequently contains at most two members of $\mathcal A$, so the same random-chain argument gives $\lambda(\mathcal A)\leq2$. For a fixed amount of [Lubell mass](../../../../../../lubell-mass.md), cardinality is maximized by using the levels with the two largest [binomial coefficients](../../../../../../binomial-coefficient.md). The [Erdős theorem on k-Sperner families](../../../../../../erdos-theorem-on-k-sperner-families.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{
|\mathcal A|\leq
\begin{cases}
\binom{2m}{m}+\binom{2m}{m-1},&n=2m,\\[2mm]
2\binom{2m+1}{m},&n=2m+1.
\end{cases}}
$$

The bound is attained by taking the union of two levels having those sizes.
