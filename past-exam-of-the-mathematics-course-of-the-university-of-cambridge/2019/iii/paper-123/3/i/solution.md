A finite extension $L/K$ of non-Archimedean [local fields](../../../../../../local-field.md) is an [unramified extension](../../../../../../unramified-extension.md) when its [ramification index](../../../../../../ramification-index.md) is one and its [residue-field degree](../../../../../../residue-field-degree.md) equals $[L:K]$. Equivalently, its maximal ideal is generated by a [uniformizer](../../../../../../uniformizer.md) of $K$ and its residue-field extension is separable.

A finite extension of [number fields](../../../../../../number-field.md) is an [everywhere unramified extension of number fields](../../../../../../everywhere-unramified-extension-of-number-fields.md) when every nonzero prime ideal of $K$ is unramified in $L$. Under the convention that includes infinite places, one also requires every real embedding of $K$ to remain real.
