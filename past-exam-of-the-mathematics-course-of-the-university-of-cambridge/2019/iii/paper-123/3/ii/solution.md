↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Hilbert class field](../../../../../../hilbert-class-field.md) $\widetilde K$ is the maximal everywhere unramified [abelian extension](../../../../../../abelian-extension.md) of the [number field](../../../../../../number-field.md) $K$, with complete splitting at real places if infinite places are included. [Global class field theory](../../../../../../class-field-theory.md) gives the canonical [Artin reciprocity map](../../../../../../artin-reciprocity-law.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cl}(K)\cong\operatorname{Gal}(\widetilde K/K),}
$$

so $[\widetilde K:K]$ is the [class number](../../../../../../class-number.md) of $K$.
