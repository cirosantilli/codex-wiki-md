<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Krasner's lemma](../../../../../../krasner-s-lemma.md) states that if $K$ is complete, $\alpha$ is separable over $K$, and an algebraic element $\beta$ satisfies

$$
|\beta-\alpha|<|\alpha'-\alpha|
$$

for every other $K$-conjugate $\alpha'$ of $\alpha$, then $K(\alpha)\subseteq K(\beta)$.

Let $f\in\mathbb C_p[X]$ be nonconstant and let $\alpha$ be a root in an algebraic closure of $\mathbb C_p$. Because the characteristic is zero, replace $f$ by the separable minimal polynomial of $\alpha$. Approximate its coefficients arbitrarily closely by elements of $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$. By [continuity of roots over a non-Archimedean field](../../../../../../continuity-of-roots-over-a-non-archimedean-field.md), the approximating polynomial has a root $\beta$ arbitrarily close to $\alpha$. Since $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$ is algebraically closed, $\beta\in\overline{\mathbb Q}_p\subset\mathbb C_p$.

Choose the approximation so that $\beta$ is closer to $\alpha$ than every other $\mathbb C_p$-conjugate of $\alpha$. Krasner's lemma gives

$$
\mathbb C_p(\alpha)\subseteq\mathbb C_p(\beta)=\mathbb C_p,
$$

so $\alpha\in\mathbb C_p$. Hence [completion of an algebraic closure of a p-adic field is algebraically closed](../../../../../../completion-of-an-algebraic-closure-of-a-p-adic-field-is-algebraically-closed.md) proves that **$\mathbb C_p$ is algebraically closed.**
