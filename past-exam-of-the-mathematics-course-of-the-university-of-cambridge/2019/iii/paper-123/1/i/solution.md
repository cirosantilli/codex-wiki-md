<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [prime ideal factorization](../../../../../../prime-ideal-factorization.md) of $pB$ is

$$
pB=\prod_{q\mid p}q^{e_{q/p}},
$$

which defines the [ramification index of a prime ideal](../../../../../../ramification-index-of-a-prime-ideal.md) $e_{q/p}=v_q(pB)$. The [residue-field degree](../../../../../../residue-field-degree.md) is

$$
f_{q/p}=[B/q:R/p].
$$

Localizing at $p$ makes $B_p$ a free $R_p$-module of rank $[L:K]$, so $B/pB$ has dimension $[L:K]$ over the [residue field](../../../../../../residue-field.md) $R/p$. The [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md) and the filtration by powers of each $q$ decompose this vector space into $e_{q/p}$ successive quotients isomorphic to $B/q$, each of dimension $f_{q/p}$. Therefore the [fundamental identity for prime decomposition](../../../../../../fundamental-identity-for-prime-decomposition.md) gives

$$
\boxed{[L:K]=\sum_{q\mid p}e_{q/p}f_{q/p}.}
$$
