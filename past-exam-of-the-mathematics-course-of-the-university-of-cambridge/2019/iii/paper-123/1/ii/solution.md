<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [completion of a number field at a prime ideal](../../../../../../completion-of-a-number-field-at-a-prime-ideal.md) does not change either invariant. More precisely, $\widehat B_q$ is the [valuation ring](../../../../../../valuation-ring.md) of $L_q$, its maximal ideal is $q\widehat B_q$, and

$$
\boxed{e(L_q/K_p)=e_{q/p},\qquad
\widehat B_q/q\widehat B_q\cong B/q.}
$$

Thus the completed extension has the same [ramification index](../../../../../../ramification-index.md) and the same [residue-field extension](../../../../../../residue-field-extension.md), hence the same [residue-field degree](../../../../../../residue-field-degree.md) $f_{q/p}$. This is [completion preserves ramification and residue data](../../../../../../completion-preserves-ramification-and-residue-data.md).
