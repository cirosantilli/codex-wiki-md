<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [discrete valuation](../../../../../../discrete-valuation.md) gives a split exact sequence

$$
1\longrightarrow\mathcal O^\times\longrightarrow K^\times
\xrightarrow{v_K}\mathbb Z\longrightarrow0,
$$

split by $n\mapsto\pi^n$. Reduction gives a second exact sequence

$$
1\longrightarrow1+\mathfrak m\longrightarrow\mathcal O^\times
\longrightarrow\mathbb F_q^\times\longrightarrow1.
$$

The [Teichmuller lift](../../../../../../teichmuller-representative.md) identifies the cyclic group $\mathbb F_q^\times$ with the group $\mu_{q-1}$ of prime-to-$p$ [roots of unity](../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) in $K$, where $p$ is the [residue characteristic](../../../../../../residue-characteristic.md). Consequently the [multiplicative group of a non-Archimedean local field](../../../../../../multiplicative-group-of-a-non-archimedean-local-field.md) decomposes as

$$
\boxed{K^\times\cong\mathbb Z\times\mu_{q-1}\times(1+\mathfrak m).}
$$

If $d=[K:\mathbb Q_p]$, the [p-adic logarithm](../../../../../../p-adic-logarithm.md) identifies a sufficiently deep [higher principal-unit group](../../../../../../higher-principal-unit-group.md) with the additive group of a rank-$d$ $\mathbb Z_p$-lattice. The remaining kernel is precisely the finite group $\mu_{p^a}(K)$ of $p$-power roots of unity in $K$. Thus the [principal-unit group of a mixed-characteristic local field](../../../../../../principal-unit-group-of-a-mixed-characteristic-local-field.md) has the topological group structure

$$
\boxed{1+\mathfrak m\cong\mu_{p^a}(K)\times\mathbb Z_p^d.}
$$
