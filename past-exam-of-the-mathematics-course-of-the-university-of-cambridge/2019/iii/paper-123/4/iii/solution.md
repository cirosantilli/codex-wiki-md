<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $L_n=\mathbb Q_2(\xi_{2^n})$. The extension is a [cyclotomic extension of a p-adic field](../../../../../../cyclotomic-extension-of-a-p-adic-field.md) and is totally ramified. Under [Local Artin reciprocity](../../../../../../local-artin-reciprocity.md), the action on $\xi_{2^n}$ is the reduction of a [2-adic unit](../../../../../../2-adic-unit.md) modulo $2^n$, up to the harmless inverse determined by the Frobenius convention. A [uniformizer](../../../../../../uniformizer.md) acts trivially because $L_n/\mathbb Q_2$ has no unramified part. The kernel is therefore

$$
2^{\mathbb Z}\ker\left(\mathbb Z_2^\times\longrightarrow(\mathbb Z/2^n\mathbb Z)^\times\right)
=2^{\mathbb Z}(1+2^n\mathbb Z_2).
$$

By the [norm subgroup of a local field extension](../../../../../../norm-subgroup-of-a-local-field-extension.md), this kernel is exactly the required norm group. Hence, for every $n>0$,

$$
\boxed{N_{\mathbb Q_2(\xi_{2^n})/\mathbb Q_2}
\bigl(\mathbb Q_2(\xi_{2^n})^\times\bigr)
=2^{\mathbb Z}(1+2^n\mathbb Z_2).}
$$

For $n=1$, the factor $1+2\mathbb Z_2$ is all of $\mathbb Z_2^\times$, consistently with $\mathbb Q_2(\xi_2)=\mathbb Q_2$.
