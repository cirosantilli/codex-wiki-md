<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In [resonant trapping of dust](../../../../../../resonant-trapping-of-dust.md), the resonant torque must compensate the inward [Poynting–Robertson drag](../../../../../../poynting-robertson-drag.md). At the nearly fixed resonant [semi-major axis](../../../../../../semi-major-axis.md), $\dot a_{\rm res}+\dot a_{\rm pr}=0$ requires

$$
C e^q\sin\phi=-\frac{A}{a^2(p+q)}(1+3e^2).
$$

Since $|\sin\phi|\le1$, a necessary torque-balance condition is $|C|e^q\ge A(1+3e^2)/[a^2(p+q)]$. When the limiting [orbital eccentricity](../../../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md) is small, this gives

$$
\boxed{e_{\min}\simeq\left(\frac{A}{|C|a^2(p+q)}\right)^{1/q}.}
$$

Equality allows only the extreme phase $|\sin\phi|=1$, with no margin for restoring torque; a robust trapped state needs $e>e_{\min}$. This is a necessary condition for [resonance capture](../../../../../../resonance-capture.md), not a sufficient probability-of-capture criterion for a migrating particle.
