<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expand the secular [Poynting–Robertson drag](../../../../../../poynting-robertson-drag.md) rates using $(1-e^2)^{-3/2}=1+\tfrac32e^2+O(e^4)$ and $(1-e^2)^{-1/2}=1+O(e^2)$. Through second order in [orbital eccentricity](../../../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md),

$$
\boxed{\dot a_{\rm pr}=-\frac{2A}{a}(1+3e^2)+O(e^4),\qquad
\frac{d(e^2)}{dt}\bigg|_{\rm pr}=-\frac{5A}{a^2}e^2+O(e^4).}
$$

The second formula follows from $d(e^2)/dt=2e\dot e$. The cubic correction in $\dot e$ contributes only at fourth order in $d(e^2)/dt$.
