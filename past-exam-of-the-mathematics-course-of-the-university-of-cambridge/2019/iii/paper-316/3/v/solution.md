<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Let $\mathbf V$ be the [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) velocity and $\mathbf v$ the binary relative velocity. Summing the two linear [gas drag](../../../../../../gas-drag.md) forces gives

$$
\dot{\mathbf V}\big|_{\rm drag}
=-\kappa_b(\mathbf V-\mathbf v_g)
-\frac{m_1m_2}{(m_1+m_2)^2}\Delta\mathbf v,
\qquad
\kappa_b=\frac{m_1/t_{s1}+m_2/t_{s2}}{m_1+m_2}.
$$

The last term averages to zero over the internal circular orbit. On the nearly circular stellar orbit, $\mathbf V\simeq v_K\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}$ and $\mathbf v_g=(1-\eta)v_K\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}$, so the averaged tangential acceleration is $-\eta\kappa_bv_K$.

With [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) $-GM_\star/(2a_b)$, the [orbit-averaged drag work](../../../../../../orbit-averaged-drag-work.md) is $-\eta\kappa_bv_K^2$, where $v_K^2=GM_\star/a_b$. Thus [gas-driven migration of a binary centre of mass](../../../../../../gas-driven-migration-of-a-binary-centre-of-mass.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\dot a_b=-2\eta\kappa_ba_b=-\frac{a_b}{\tau_b},\qquad
\tau_b=\frac{m_1+m_2}{2\eta(m_1/t_{s1}+m_2/t_{s2})}.}
$$

This uses the weak-drag, nearly Keplerian approximation implicit in applying the circular-orbit work relation. Strongly gas-coupled orbits require a coupled radial-azimuthal drift solution.
