<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\mathbf r=\mathbf r_2-\mathbf r_1$ and $r=|\mathbf r|$. [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) for the two [planetesimals](../../../../../../planetesimal.md) gives

$$
\ddot{\mathbf r}_1=Gm_2\frac{\mathbf r}{r^3}+\frac{\mathbf F_1}{m_1},\qquad
\ddot{\mathbf r}_2=-Gm_1\frac{\mathbf r}{r^3}+\frac{\mathbf F_2}{m_2}.
$$

Subtracting yields the forced relative [two-body problem](../../../../../../two-body-problem.md)

$$
\boxed{\ddot{\mathbf r}+G(m_1+m_2)\frac{\mathbf r}{r^3}
=\frac{\mathbf F_2}{m_2}-\frac{\mathbf F_1}{m_1}.}
$$

The internal gravitational forces cancel from the [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) equation but add in the relative equation; external forces affect the internal orbit through their difference in acceleration.
