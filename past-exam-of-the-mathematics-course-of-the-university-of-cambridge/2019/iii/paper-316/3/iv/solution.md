<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For weak [gas drag](../../../../../../gas-drag.md), evaluate the work on the unperturbed circular orbit and average over one [orbital period](../../../../../../orbital-period.md). The gas velocity changes negligibly during that period and $\langle\cos\theta\rangle=0$. Hence [gas-driven contraction of a planetesimal binary](../../../../../../gas-driven-contraction-of-a-planetesimal-binary.md) obeys

$$
\langle\dot a\rangle=-2\kappa a=-\frac a\tau,
\qquad
\boxed{\tau=\frac{m_1+m_2}{2(m_1/t_{s2}+m_2/t_{s1})}.}
$$

With constant [aerodynamic stopping times](../../../../../../aerodynamic-stopping-time.md),

$$
\boxed{a(t)=a_0e^{-t/\tau}.}
$$

This is the secular, orbit-averaged separation. It assumes the perturbations are small enough for the orbit to remain nearly circular; a large differential headwind can excite eccentricity or disrupt a weakly bound pair instead.
