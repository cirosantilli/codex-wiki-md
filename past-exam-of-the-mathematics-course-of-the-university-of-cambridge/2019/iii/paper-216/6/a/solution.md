<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let

$$
H(x,p)=U(x)+\frac12p^Tp
$$

be the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md). The [Hamiltonian Monte Carlo](../../../../../../hamiltonian-monte-carlo.md) proposal is accepted with the [Metropolis–Hastings acceptance probability](../../../../../../metropolis-hastings-acceptance-probability.md)

$$
\boxed{
\alpha=1\wedge
\exp\{H(X_t,P_t)-H(x(L\varepsilon),p(L\varepsilon))\}.}
$$

The leapfrog map is volume preserving and reversible after momentum reversal, so no proposal-density Jacobian appears.
