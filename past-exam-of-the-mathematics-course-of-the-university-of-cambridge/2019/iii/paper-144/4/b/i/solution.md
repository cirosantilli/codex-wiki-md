<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\kappa=|N|$. If the [saturated model](../../../../../../../saturated-model.md) $N$ had a basis $B$ of cardinality below $\kappa$, then $N=\operatorname{acl}(B)$ would omit the consistent [generic type in a strongly minimal theory](../../../../../../../generic-type-in-a-strongly-minimal-theory.md) over $B$. This contradicts $\kappa$-saturation. A basis is a subset of $N$, so its cardinality is at most $\kappa$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{N\text{ saturated}\Longrightarrow\dim(N)=|N|.}
$$
