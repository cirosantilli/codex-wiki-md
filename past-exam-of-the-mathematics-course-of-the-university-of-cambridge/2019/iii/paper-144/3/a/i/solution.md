<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Suppose $a\in\operatorname{acl}(A)$ in the sense of [model-theoretic algebraic closure](../../../../../../../model-theoretic-algebraic-closure.md). Some formula $\varphi(x,\bar c)$ over $A$ has exactly $n<\infty$ realizations in the [monster model](../../../../../../../monster-model.md) and includes $a$. Every model $M$ containing $A$ is an [elementary substructure](../../../../../../../elementary-substructure.md) of the monster and therefore contains $n$ distinct realizations. Since there are only $n$ in the monster, it contains all of them, including $a$. Hence

$$
\boxed{a\in\operatorname{acl}(A)\Longrightarrow a\in M\text{ for every model }M\supseteq A.}
$$
