<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At [cosmological horizon exit](../../../../../../cosmological-horizon-exit.md), $d\log k/dN=1-\epsilon\simeq1$. Differentiating the [slow-roll curvature power spectrum](../../../../../../slow-roll-curvature-power-spectrum.md) and using the [Hubble slow-roll parameters](../../../../../../hubble-slow-roll-parameter.md) gives

$$
n_s-1=\frac{d\log\Delta_{\mathcal R}^2}{d\log k}
=-2\epsilon-\eta.
$$

The supplied relations imply

$$
\epsilon=\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2}{2}\left(\frac{V'}V\right)^2,
\qquad
\eta=4\epsilon-2M_{\rm Pl}^2\frac{V''}V.
$$

Consequently the [scalar spectral index](../../../../../../scalar-spectral-index.md) is

$$
\boxed{n_s-1
=-3M_{\rm Pl}^2\left(\frac{V'}V\right)^2
+2M_{\rm Pl}^2\frac{V''}V}.
$$
