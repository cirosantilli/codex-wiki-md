A [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) has [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md)

$$
\boxed{T_{\mu\nu}=(\rho+P)u_\mu u_\nu+Pg_{\mu\nu}},
$$

where $u^\mu u_\mu=-1$. Besides the [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md), one needs the [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) $\rho$, the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) $P$, and three independent components of the normalized [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md) $u^\mu$: **five scalar functions of spacetime in total**.

The four equations of [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md), $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0$, split parallel and perpendicular to $u^\mu$ into

$$
\boxed{u^\mu\nabla_\mu\rho+(\rho+P)\nabla_\mu u^\mu=0},
$$



$$
\boxed{(\rho+P)u^\mu\nabla_\mu u^\alpha
+(g^{\alpha\mu}+u^\alpha u^\mu)\nabla_\mu P=0}.
$$

An [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md), such as a [barotropic equation of state](../../../../../../barotropic-equation-of-state.md) $P=P(\rho)$, supplies the fifth relation needed to close the evolution.
