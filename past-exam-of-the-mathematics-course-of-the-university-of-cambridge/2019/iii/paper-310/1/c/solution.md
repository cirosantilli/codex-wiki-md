<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Normalize the present [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) to $a(t_0)=1$. In a flat universe containing [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md), [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md), and a [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md), the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) is

$$
H(a)=H_0\sqrt{\Omega_{r,0}a^{-4}+\Omega_{m,0}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}},
$$

where $\Omega_{r,0}+\Omega_{m,0}+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}=1$. Since $dt=da/(aH)$, the [age of an FLRW universe](../../../../../../age-of-an-flrw-universe.md) is

$$
\boxed{t_0=\frac1{H_0}\int_0^1
\frac{da}{a\sqrt{\Omega_{r,0}a^{-4}+\Omega_{m,0}a^{-3}+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}}}.
$$

For a matter-only flat [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md),

$$
\boxed{t_0=\frac{2}{3H_0}}.
$$

Taking the present value $H_0\simeq70\ {\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$, for which the [Hubble time](../../../../../../hubble-time.md) is $H_0^{-1}\simeq14.0\ {\rm Gyr}$, gives

$$
\boxed{t_0\simeq9.3\ {\rm Gyr}}.
$$
