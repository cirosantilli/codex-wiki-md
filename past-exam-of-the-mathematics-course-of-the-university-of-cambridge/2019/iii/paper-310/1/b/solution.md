<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [cosmic time](../../../../../../cosmic-time.md), all three [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metrics](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) can be written

$$
ds^2=-dt^2+a^2(t)\left[d\chi^2+S_K^2(\chi)d\Omega_2^2\right],
$$

where

$$
S_K(\chi)=
\begin{cases}
\sin\chi,&K=+1\quad\hbox{closed},\\
\chi,&K=0\quad\hbox{flat},\\
\sinh\chi,&K=-1\quad\hbox{open}.
\end{cases}
$$

The closed spatial slice is a [three-sphere](../../../../../../three-sphere.md) of radius $a(t)$, so

$$
\boxed{V_{K=+1}=2\pi^2a^3}.
$$

The complete flat and open spatial slices are noncompact and have

$$
\boxed{V_{K=0}=V_{K=-1}=\infty}.
$$

For a homogeneous fluid in the [Spatially flat FLRW metric](../../../../../../spatially-flat-flrw-metric.md), the equations in part (a) reduce to the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot\rho+3H(\rho+P)=0},
$$

while homogeneity makes the spatial [relativistic Euler equation](../../../../../../relativistic-euler-equation.md) automatic. The [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) give the flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) and [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md),

$$
\boxed{H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho,
\qquad
\dot H=-4\pi G(\rho+P)},
$$

equivalently $\ddot a/a=-4\pi G(\rho+3P)/3$.
