↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A larger [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) changes both the expansion history and the growth history: it alters cosmological distances, the [baryon acoustic oscillation](../../../../../../baryon-acoustic-oscillation.md) scale as observed in angle and redshift, and the time at which growth begins to freeze. The new interaction in part (d) leaves the background [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) unchanged but changes clustering abruptly from $t_f$ onward.

Redshift-resolved measurements of the [linear growth factor](../../../../../../linear-growth-factor.md), weak [gravitational lensing](../../../../../../gravitational-lensing.md), galaxy clustering, and [cosmological distance measures](../../../../../../cosmological-distance-measure.md) can therefore compare geometry with growth. Equal present amplitudes do not imply equal $D(z)$, and only the higher-dark-energy model changes the distance-redshift relation. This breaks the degeneracy.
