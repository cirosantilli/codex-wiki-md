<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The background remains matter dominated, so $H=2/(3t)$ and $4\pi G\bar\rho_m=2/(3t^2)$. For the [reduced gravitational clustering strength](../../../../../../reduced-gravitational-clustering-strength.md), substitute $\Delta_m\propto t^s$ into the modified equation:

$$
s(s-1)+\frac43s-\frac23(1-f)=0.
$$

The growing root is the [matter-era growth with reduced gravitational clustering strength](../../../../../../matter-era-growth-with-reduced-gravitational-clustering-strength.md)

$$
\boxed{s(f)=\frac{-1+\sqrt{25-24f}}6}.
$$

It satisfies $0<s(f)<2/3$ for $0<f<1$. Between $t_f$ and the onset $t_\Lambda$ of dark-energy domination, standard growth supplies $(t_\Lambda/t_f)^{2/3}$ whereas the modified model supplies $(t_\Lambda/t_f)^{s(f)}$. Subsequent growth freezes in both, so

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\Delta_{m,{\rm new}}(t_0)}
{\Delta_{m,{\rm standard}}(t_0)}
\simeq
\left(\frac{t_\Lambda}{t_f}\right)^{s(f)-2/3}}.
$$
