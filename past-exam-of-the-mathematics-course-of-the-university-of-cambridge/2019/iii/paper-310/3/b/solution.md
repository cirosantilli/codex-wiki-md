<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

During [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md), $a\propto t^{2/3}$, $H=2/(3t)$, and $4\pi G\bar\rho_m=2/(3t^2)$. Substituting $\Delta_m\propto t^s$ into the [linear growth equation](../../../../../../linear-growth-equation.md) gives

$$
s(s-1)+\frac43s-\frac23=0,
$$

whose roots are $s=2/3$ and $s=-1$. Therefore

$$
\Delta_m=C_+t^{2/3}+C_-t^{-1}
=C_+a+C_-a^{-3/2}.
$$

The growing one of the [matter-dominated density-perturbation modes](../../../../../../matter-dominated-density-perturbation-modes.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Delta_m\propto a}.
$$
