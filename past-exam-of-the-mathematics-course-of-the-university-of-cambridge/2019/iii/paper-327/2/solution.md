The [space of test functions](../../../../../space-of-test-functions.md) is $\mathcal D(\mathbb R)=C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$. A sequence $\varphi_j$ converges to $\varphi$ when its supports eventually lie in a single compact set and every derivative converges uniformly. The [distribution](../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) space $\mathcal D'(\mathbb R)$ is its continuous linear dual; weak convergence means $\langle u_j,\varphi\rangle\to\langle u,\varphi\rangle$ for every test function. We use $\langle u',\varphi\rangle=-\langle u,\varphi'\rangle$ and $\langle au,\varphi\rangle=\langle u,a\varphi\rangle$ for [multiplication of a distribution by a smooth function](../../../../../multiplication-of-a-distribution-by-a-smooth-function.md).
