<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [distributional derivative of the Heaviside step function](../../../../../../distributional-derivative-of-the-heaviside-step-function.md) gives $H'=\delta_0$, while $((x-1)H(x-1))''=\delta_1$. A particular solution is therefore $H(x)-2(x-1)H(x-1)$.

The difference from any other solution has second derivative zero. Applying [a distribution with zero derivative is constant](../../../../../../a-distribution-with-zero-derivative-is-constant.md) twice shows that difference is affine. Thus

$$
\boxed{u=H(x)-2(x-1)H(x-1)+ax+b,\qquad a,b\in\mathbb C.}
$$

The ramp function is continuous at $x=1$, and its first derivative jumps there, producing the indicated delta source in its second derivative.
