<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose a covering set $X$ of size at most $K$ such that $A^2\subseteq XA$, as allowed by the definition of an [approximate group](../../../../../../approximate-group.md). Discard every $x\in X$ for which $xA$ does not meet $A^2$. Each remaining $x$ belongs to $A^2A^{-1}=A^3$, so $X\subseteq\langle A\rangle$. Induction gives

$$
A^m\subseteq X^{m-1}A.
$$

Because $A$ is symmetric and contains the identity, $\langle A\rangle=\bigcup_{m\geq1}A^m$. Hence

$$
\langle A\rangle=\langle X\rangle A.
$$

We use the [bounded-exponent finitely generated nilpotent group order bound](../../../../../../bounded-exponent-finitely-generated-nilpotent-group-order-bound.md). In an $s$-step [nilpotent group](../../../../../../nilpotent-group.md), a subgroup generated by $k$ elements is generated in collected form by the simple [group commutators](../../../../../../group-commutator.md) in those generators of weights at most $s$. There are at most

$$
k+k^2+\cdots+k^s\leq sk^s
$$

such commutators. Every one has order at most $r$, so

$$
|\langle X\rangle|\leq r^{s|X|^s}\leq r^{sK^s}.
$$

Taking $H=\langle A\rangle$ now gives

$$
\boxed{A\subseteq H,\qquad |H|\leq|\langle X\rangle|\,|A|
\leq r^{sK^s}|A|.}
$$
