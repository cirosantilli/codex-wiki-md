The [Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality](../../../../../../plunnecke-ruzsa-inequality.md) states that, for all [nonnegative integers](../../../../../../natural-number.md) $m,n$,

$$
\boxed{|mA-nA|\leq K^{m+n}|A|.}
$$

Here $mA$ is the [iterated sumset](../../../../../../iterated-sumset.md) of $m$ copies of $A$, with $0A=\{0\}$. In particular, $|mA|\leq K^m|A|$.
