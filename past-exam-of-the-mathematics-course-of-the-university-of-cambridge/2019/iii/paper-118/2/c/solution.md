<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\Omega^p$ be the sheaf of holomorphic $p$-forms and $\mathcal A^{p,q}$ the sheaf of smooth $(p,q)$-forms. The [Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma](../../../../../../dolbeault-poincare-lemma.md) makes

$$
0\longrightarrow\Omega^p\longrightarrow\mathcal A^{p,0}\xrightarrow{\bar\partial}\mathcal A^{p,1}\xrightarrow{\bar\partial}\cdots
$$

an exact resolution by [fine sheaves](../../../../../../fine-sheaf.md), which are acyclic for [global sections](../../../../../../global-section.md). Its global cochain complex computes [Dolbeault cohomology](../../../../../../dolbeault-cohomology.md), proving the [Dolbeault theorem](../../../../../../dolbeault-theorem.md)

$$
\boxed{H^q(X,\Omega^p)\cong H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(X).}
$$
