<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $\check H^1(S,\mathcal O)=0$, that cocycle is a [Čech coboundary](../../../../../../../cech-coboundary.md): there are $f_i\in\mathcal O(U_i)$ with

$$
P_m=f_m-f_0\quad\text{on }U_0\cap U_m.
$$

Set $F=-f_0$ on $U_0$ and $F=P_m-f_m$ on $U_m$. These formulas agree on overlaps, so the [sheaf gluing axiom](../../../../../../../sheaf-gluing-axiom.md) gives a global [meromorphic function](../../../../../../../meromorphic-function.md). Moreover $F-P_m=-f_m$ is holomorphic near $x_m$. Thus $F$ solves the [Mittag-Leffler problem on a Riemann surface](../../../../../../../mittag-leffler-problem-on-a-riemann-surface.md) with precisely the prescribed principal parts.
