↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The tangent-normal [short exact sequence](../../../../../../short-exact-sequence.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow TY\longrightarrow TX|_Y\longrightarrow N_{Y/X}\longrightarrow0
$$

gives $\det(TX|_Y)\cong\det(TY)\otimes N_{Y/X}$. Dualizing and using the [canonical bundle](../../../../../../canonical-bundle.md),

$$
K_X|_Y\cong K_Y\otimes N_{Y/X}^*.
$$

Since $N_{Y/X}\cong\mathcal O(Y)|_Y$, rearrangement proves the [adjunction formula](../../../../../../adjunction-formula.md)

$$
\boxed{K_Y\cong(K_X\otimes\mathcal O(Y))|_Y.}
$$
