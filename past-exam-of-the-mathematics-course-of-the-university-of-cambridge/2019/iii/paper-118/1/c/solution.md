<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

On each member of a cover, choose a reduced [local defining function of a complex analytic hypersurface](../../../../../../local-defining-function-of-a-complex-analytic-hypersurface.md) $f_i$ for $Y$. On overlaps $f_i=g_{ij}f_j$ for $g_{ij}\in\mathcal O_X^*$. Since $f_j|_Y=0$,

$$
df_i|_Y=g_{ij}df_j|_Y.
$$

Each $df_i$ annihilates $TY$ and is nonzero in the normal direction because $Y$ is smooth. If $e_i=g_{ij}^{-1}e_j$ are the frames of the [holomorphic line bundle associated to a divisor](../../../../../../holomorphic-line-bundle-associated-to-a-divisor.md) $\mathcal O(Y)$, then

$$
[v]\longmapsto df_i(v)e_i
$$

is a well-defined nowhere-zero holomorphic map of line bundles. Thus the [normal bundle of a smooth analytic hypersurface](../../../../../../normal-bundle-of-a-smooth-analytic-hypersurface.md) is

$$
\boxed{N_{Y/X}\cong\mathcal O(Y)|_Y.}
$$
