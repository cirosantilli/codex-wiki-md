<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a [holomorphic coordinate](../../../../../../holomorphic-coordinate.md) chart, $T^{1,0}X$ has frame $\partial/\partial z^1,\ldots,\partial/\partial z^n$. Under a holomorphic coordinate change $w=w(z)$, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial z^j}=\sum_k\frac{\partial w^k}{\partial z^j}\frac{\partial}{\partial w^k}.
$$

The Jacobian is an invertible matrix of [holomorphic functions](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md). These frames therefore make $T^{1,0}X$ the [holomorphic tangent bundle](../../../../../../holomorphic-tangent-bundle.md).
