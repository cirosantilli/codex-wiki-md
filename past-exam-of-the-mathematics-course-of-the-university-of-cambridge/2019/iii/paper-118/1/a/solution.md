A [complex manifold](../../../../../../complex-manifold.md) of complex dimension $n$ is a Hausdorff, second-countable [topological manifold](../../../../../../topological-manifold.md) with charts to open subsets of $\mathbb C^n$ whose transition maps are [biholomorphic](../../../../../../biholomorphism.md). A rank-$r$ [holomorphic vector bundle](../../../../../../holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) is a complex [vector bundle](../../../../../../vector-bundle.md) with local trivializations $E|_U\cong U\times\mathbb C^r$ whose transition matrices are holomorphic maps to $\operatorname{GL}_r(\mathbb C)$.
