↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [connection on a vector bundle](../../../../../../connection-vector-bundle.md) is a complex-linear map $D:\Omega^0(E)\to\Omega^1(E)$ satisfying the [Leibniz rule](../../../../../../leibniz-rule.md) $D(fs)=df\otimes s+fDs$. It is compatible with the [Hermitian metric on a holomorphic vector bundle](../../../../../../hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) $h$ when

$$
d\,h(s,t)=h(Ds,t)+h(s,Dt),
$$

and compatible with the holomorphic structure when its type-$(0,1)$ part is the [Dolbeault partial connection](../../../../../../dolbeault-partial-connection.md):

$$
\boxed{D^{0,1}=\bar\partial_E.}
$$
