<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a unitary frame the metric matrix is $I$. If $A$ is the [connection one-form](../../../../../../connection-one-form.md), [metric compatibility](../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) gives $0=dI=A^*+A$, so

$$
\boxed{A^*=-A;}
$$

the matrix is [Skew-Hermitian](../../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md). In a [holomorphic local frame](../../../../../../holomorphic-local-trivialization.md), every frame vector is annihilated by $\bar\partial_E$, and compatibility with the holomorphic structure gives

$$
\boxed{A^{0,1}=0.}
$$
