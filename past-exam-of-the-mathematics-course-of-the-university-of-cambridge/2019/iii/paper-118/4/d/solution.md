<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $D_1$ and $D_2$ each be a [connection on a vector bundle](../../../../../../connection-vector-bundle.md). Their difference is tensorial, so $D_1=D_2+a$ with $a\in\Omega^1(\operatorname{End}E)$. Extend $D_2$ to the [endomorphism bundle connection](../../../../../../endomorphism-bundle-connection.md). Expanding $(D_2+a)^2$ with the supplied graded [Leibniz rule](../../../../../../leibniz-rule.md) gives the [curvature difference formula](../../../../../../curvature-difference-formula.md)

$$
\boxed{F_{D_1}=F_{D_2}+D_2(a)+a\wedge a.}
$$
