<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose a [very ample line bundle](../../../../../../very-ample-line-bundle.md) $A$ on $X$. By the theorem that a [high ample twist is very ample](../../../../../../high-ample-twist-is-very-ample.md), for sufficiently large $m$ both

$$
H_1=A^{\otimes m}\otimes L,\qquad H_2=A^{\otimes m}
$$

are very ample. The [dual bundle](../../../../../../dual-bundle.md) cancels the power of $A$, giving

$$
\boxed{L\cong H_1\otimes H_2^*.}
$$
