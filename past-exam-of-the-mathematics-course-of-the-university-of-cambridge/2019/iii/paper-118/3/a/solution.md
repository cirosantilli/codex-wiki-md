<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\mathcal K_X^*$ be the sheaf of nonzero meromorphic functions. A local equation for a [divisor on a complex manifold](../../../../../../divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md) is determined modulo a nowhere-zero holomorphic factor and hence defines a section of the [divisor sheaf on a complex manifold](../../../../../../divisor-sheaf-on-a-complex-manifold.md) $\mathcal K_X^*/\mathcal O_X^*$. Conversely, local representatives of such a section have quotients in $\mathcal O_X^*$, so their zero and pole orders agree on overlaps and define a divisor. The constructions are inverse:

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Div}(X)\cong H^0(X,\mathcal K_X^*/\mathcal O_X^*).}
$$
