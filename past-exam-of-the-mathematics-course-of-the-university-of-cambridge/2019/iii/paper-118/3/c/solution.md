<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [holomorphic line bundle](../../../../../../holomorphic-line-bundle.md) is [ample](../../../../../../ample-line-bundle.md) when some positive [tensor power](../../../../../../tensor-power.md) is [very ample](../../../../../../very-ample-line-bundle.md), so its sections define an embedding into [Complex projective space](../../../../../../complex-projective-space.md). It is a [positive holomorphic line bundle](../../../../../../positive-holomorphic-line-bundle.md) when it has a [Hermitian metric](../../../../../../hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) with positive Chern curvature $iF_\nabla$. The [Kodaira embedding theorem](../../../../../../kodaira-embedding-theorem.md) says that a compact [complex manifold](../../../../../../complex-manifold.md) carrying a positive holomorphic line bundle is projective; sufficiently high tensor powers give a [holomorphic embedding](../../../../../../holomorphic-embedding.md) into projective space.
