<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Write $L\cong H_1\otimes H_2^*$ as in part (d). Each [very ample line bundle](../../../../../../very-ample-line-bundle.md) is the pullback of $\mathcal O(1)$ under a projective embedding. A [hyperplane section](../../../../../../hyperplane-section.md) therefore supplies an effective [divisor on a complex manifold](../../../../../../divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md) $D_i$ with $H_i\cong\mathcal O(D_i)$. Hence

$$
L\cong\mathcal O(D_1-D_2).
$$

Every [Picard group](../../../../../../picard-group.md) class is therefore in the image of the [divisor-to-Picard map](../../../../../../divisor-to-picard-map.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Div}(X)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}(X)\text{ is surjective}.}
$$
