<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X^*\longrightarrow\mathcal K_X^*
\longrightarrow\mathcal K_X^*/\mathcal O_X^*\longrightarrow0
$$

induces a [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md). Under $H^0(\mathcal K_X^*/\mathcal O_X^*)=\operatorname{Div}(X)$ and $H^1(\mathcal O_X^*)=\operatorname{Pic}(X)$, its [connecting homomorphism](../../../../../../connecting-homomorphism.md) is the [divisor-to-Picard map](../../../../../../divisor-to-picard-map.md) $D\mapsto\mathcal O(D)$. Exactness identifies its kernel with divisors of global nonzero [meromorphic functions](../../../../../../meromorphic-function.md), namely [principal divisors](../../../../../../principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md):

$$
\boxed{\ker(\operatorname{Div}(X)\to\operatorname{Pic}(X))=\operatorname{Prin}(X).}
$$
