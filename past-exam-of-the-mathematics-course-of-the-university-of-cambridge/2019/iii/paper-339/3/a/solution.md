<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $z\in\mathbb R^n$, define $x_i=z_i^2$. Then $x$ lies in the [nonnegative orthant](../../../../../../nonnegative-orthant.md) and

$$
p(z)=\sum_{i,j}A_{ij}z_i^2z_j^2=x^TAx.
$$

If $A$ is a [copositive matrix](../../../../../../copositive-matrix.md), this is nonnegative for every $z$, so $p$ is a globally [nonnegative polynomial](../../../../../../nonnegative-polynomial.md). Conversely, every $x\geq0$ has the form $x_i=z_i^2$ by taking $z_i=\sqrt{x_i}$. If $p$ is globally nonnegative, then $x^TAx=p(z)\geq0$ for every such $x$, proving that $A$ is a [copositive matrix](../../../../../../copositive-matrix.md). The map $z\mapsto(z_i^2)_i$ covers the entire [nonnegative orthant](../../../../../../nonnegative-orthant.md), which is the key to both directions.
