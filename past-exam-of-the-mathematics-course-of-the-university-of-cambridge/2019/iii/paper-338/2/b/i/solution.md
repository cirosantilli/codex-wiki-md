<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The projected actuator pitch is $p=D/N$ in the question's sampling convention. A sinusoidal [wavefront error](../../../../../../../wavefront-error.md) needs at least two samples per spatial period, by the [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](../../../../../../../nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md). Thus the shortest limiting correctable scale at the primary is

$$
\boxed{\Lambda_{\min}=2p=\frac{2D}N.}
$$

The [Nyquist spatial frequency](../../../../../../../nyquist-spatial-frequency.md) is $N/(2D)$ cycles per unit length. Exactly at that limiting frequency some phases are poorly sampled; actual [deformable mirror](../../../../../../../deformable-mirror.md) performance also depends on actuator influence functions, so this is an ideal bandwidth limit.
