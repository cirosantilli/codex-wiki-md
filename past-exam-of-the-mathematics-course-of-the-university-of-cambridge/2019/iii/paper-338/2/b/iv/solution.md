<h1 id="2/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A [wavefront error](../../../../../../../wavefront-error.md) ripple of period $\Lambda$ generates a pair of speckles at angular displacement $\lambda/\Lambda$. Combining this with $\Lambda_{\min}=2D/N$ gives the [deformable-mirror control radius](../../../../../../../deformable-mirror-control-radius.md) along an actuator row or column:

$$
\boxed{\theta_{\max}=\frac{N\lambda}{2D}.}
$$

A square actuator lattice has a square ideal frequency region: $|\theta_x|,|\theta_y|\leq N\lambda/(2D)$. Its full width is $N\lambda/D$, approximately $N$ [diffraction-limited resolution](../../../../../../../diffraction-limited-resolution.md) elements per side, or $N^2$ in area. The circular subset within the row-direction radius contains approximately $\pi N^2/4$ such elements. The primary aperture shapes each speckle's [point spread function](../../../../../../../point-spread-function.md); it does not turn the square sampling limit into a circular one.

This full square describes phase-error control. Simultaneous amplitude and phase correction with a single pupil-plane [deformable mirror](../../../../../../../deformable-mirror.md) generally requires restricting the dark region to a half-plane; it is a different constraint from the sampling bandwidth.
