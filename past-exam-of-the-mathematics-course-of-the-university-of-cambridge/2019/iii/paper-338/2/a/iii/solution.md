↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Strehl ratio](../../../../../../../strehl-ratio.md) is

$$
\boxed{S=\frac{\text{observed point-spread-function peak}}{\text{ideal diffraction-limited peak}},}
$$

using the same total flux, pupil, and [wavelength](../../../../../../../wavelength.md) for both images. A value near one means that little light has been redistributed from the ideal central peak by [wavefront errors](../../../../../../../wavefront-error.md). For small residual phase variance $\sigma_\phi^2$, the [Maréchal approximation](../../../../../../../marechal-approximation.md) is $S\simeq e^{-\sigma_\phi^2}$, with $\sigma_\phi=2\pi h_{\rm rms}/\lambda$ for the residual optical-path [root mean square](../../../../../../../root-mean-square.md) error.
