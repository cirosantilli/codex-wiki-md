**[Astronomical seeing](../../../../../../../astronomical-seeing.md) is the atmospheric angular blurring of a point-source image, usually reported in [arcseconds](../../../../../../../arcsecond.md).** Operationally it is commonly characterized by the [full width at half maximum](../../../../../../../full-width-at-half-maximum.md) of a long-exposure stellar [point spread function](../../../../../../../point-spread-function.md). The full observed width can also contain telescope and instrumental contributions; the atmospheric seeing is the turbulence contribution.
