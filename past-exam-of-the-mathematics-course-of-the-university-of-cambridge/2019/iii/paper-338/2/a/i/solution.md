**[Adaptive optics](../../../../../../../adaptive-optics.md) corrects rapidly changing atmospheric [wavefront errors](../../../../../../../wavefront-error.md) to improve angular resolution and image concentration.** A [wavefront sensor](../../../../../../../wavefront-sensor.md) estimates those errors and a feedback controller commands a [deformable mirror](../../../../../../../deformable-mirror.md) to oppose them, aiming toward the [diffraction limit of a telescope](../../../../../../../diffraction-limit-of-a-telescope.md) rather than the uncorrected [astronomical seeing](../../../../../../../astronomical-seeing.md) limit.
