<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $\Phi_i=1/f_{id}$ and $\Phi=1/f_d$. The [achromatic doublet power balance](../../../../../../../achromatic-doublet-power-balance.md) is

$$
\Phi_1+\Phi_2=\Phi,\qquad \frac{\Phi_1}{V_{1d}}+\frac{\Phi_2}{V_{2d}}=0.
$$

Solving these two linear equations gives $\Phi_1=\Phi V_{1d}/(V_{1d}-V_{2d})$ and $\Phi_2=-\Phi V_{2d}/(V_{1d}-V_{2d})$. Taking reciprocals gives the required [focal lengths](../../../../../../../focal-length.md):

$$
\boxed{f_{1d}=\frac{f_d(V_{1d}-V_{2d})}{V_{1d}},\qquad f_{2d}=\frac{f_d(V_{2d}-V_{1d})}{V_{2d}}.}
$$

The design requires distinct [Abbe numbers](../../../../../../../abbe-number.md); identical relative dispersion cannot yield a nonzero total achromatic power from this two-element thin model.
