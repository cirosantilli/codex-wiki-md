<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For thin lenses in contact, [optical powers](../../../../../../../optical-power.md) add. At the two reference wavelengths, the [lensmaker's equation](../../../../../../../lensmaker-s-equation.md) gives total power

$$
\Phi_F=\rho_1(n_{1F}-1)+\rho_2(n_{2F}-1),\qquad\Phi_C=\rho_1(n_{1C}-1)+\rho_2(n_{2C}-1).
$$

An [achromatic lens](../../../../../../../achromatic-lens.md) has $\Phi_F=\Phi_C$. Subtracting and solving for the curvature-factor ratio yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{\rho_1}{\rho_2}=-\frac{n_{2F}-n_{2C}}{n_{1F}-n_{1C}}.}
$$

Opposite curvature-weighted powers compensate the two materials' [optical dispersion](../../../../../../../dispersion-optics.md).
