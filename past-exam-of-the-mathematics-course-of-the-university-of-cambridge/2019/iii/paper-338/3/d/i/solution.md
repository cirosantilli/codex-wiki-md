The two standard designs are **[multi-slit spectrographs](../../../../../../../multi-slit-spectrograph.md)** and **[fiber-fed spectrographs](../../../../../../../fiber-fed-spectrograph.md)**. Both select many astronomical targets simultaneously and arrange their light so the resulting [optical spectra](../../../../../../../optical-spectrum.md) remain distinguishable on the detector.
