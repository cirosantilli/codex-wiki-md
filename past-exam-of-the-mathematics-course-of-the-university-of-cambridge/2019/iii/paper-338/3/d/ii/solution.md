A [multi-slit spectrograph](../../../../../../../multi-slit-spectrograph.md) uses a focal-plane mask containing slitlets at the target positions, or movable slitlets placed there. Each transmits its target and nearby sky into the [collimator](../../../../../../../collimator.md); a [diffraction grating](../../../../../../../diffraction-grating.md) or other disperser produces a separate [optical spectrum](../../../../../../../optical-spectrum.md) on the detector. The layout must avoid overlap between spectra, and the length of a slit allows local sampling of [sky brightness](../../../../../../../sky-brightness.md) and sometimes spatial information within the target.

A [fiber-fed spectrograph](../../../../../../../fiber-fed-spectrograph.md) places [optical fibers](../../../../../../../optical-fiber.md) at target positions in the telescope focal plane. The fibers carry the selected light to a spectrograph and their outputs are lined up as a pseudo-slit. The spectrograph can be mechanically stable and separate from the telescope's focal plane. Additional fibers aimed at blank sky provide a simultaneous estimate of [sky brightness](../../../../../../../sky-brightness.md); fiber positioning, coupling losses, transmission, and [focal-ratio degradation](../../../../../../../focal-ratio-degradation.md) must be accounted for.
