<h1 id="3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At [minimum deviation](../../../../../../../minimum-deviation.md), $\theta_2=\theta_3=\phi/2$ and $\theta_1=\theta_4=(\gamma_{\min}+\phi)/2$. Substituting in [Snell's law](../../../../../../../snell-s-law.md) gives

$$
\boxed{n=\frac{\sin[(\gamma_{\min}+\phi)/2]}{\sin(\phi/2)}.}
$$

For a known prism apex angle, measuring the minimum deviation with monochromatic light determines its [refractive index](../../../../../../../refractive-index.md). Repeating the measurement at several [wavelengths](../../../../../../../wavelength.md) measures [optical dispersion](../../../../../../../dispersion-optics.md). If the surrounding medium is not air, the measured ratio is relative to that medium's refractive index.
