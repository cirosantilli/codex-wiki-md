<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [magnitude zero point](../../../../../../../magnitude-zero-point.md) is defined by the reference response $I_0$. With zero magnitude assigned to that reference, the [apparent magnitude](../../../../../../../apparent-magnitude.md) is

$$
\boxed{m=-2.5\log_{10}\left(\frac I{I_0}\right).}
$$

The two responses must use the same [photometric passband](../../../../../../../photometric-passband.md) and instrumental weighting. If the reference has assigned magnitude $m_0$ instead, add $m_0$ to the right-hand side.
