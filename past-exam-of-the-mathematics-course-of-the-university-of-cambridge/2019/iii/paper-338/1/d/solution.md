[Atmospheric extinction](../../../../../../atmospheric-extinction.md) changes the amplitude of the incoming light through absorption and scattering. Its [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) dependence alters the measured [optical spectrum](../../../../../../optical-spectrum.md) and colors; clouds and aerosols introduce additional time dependence. Scattered moonlight and atmospheric emission increase [sky brightness](../../../../../../sky-brightness.md).

[Atmospheric refraction](../../../../../../atmospheric-refraction.md) changes the apparent position of the source. Its wavelength dependence, [atmospheric dispersion](../../../../../../atmospheric-dispersion.md), spreads a broadband image toward the zenith. The mean [refractive index](../../../../../../refractive-index.md) gradient therefore affects direction even in the absence of small-scale turbulence.

[Atmospheric turbulence](../../../../../../atmospheric-turbulence.md) produces rapidly varying [optical path lengths](../../../../../../optical-path-length.md), distorting the phase and curvature of a nominally plane [wavefront](../../../../../../wavefront.md). Different pupil regions acquire different phase delays, producing [astronomical seeing](../../../../../../astronomical-seeing.md), image motion, and short-exposure [speckle patterns](../../../../../../speckle-pattern.md). Different lines of sight sample different fluctuations, giving [anisoplanatism](../../../../../../anisoplanatism.md).

Propagation through the fluctuating medium also changes the intensity through [atmospheric scintillation](../../../../../../scintillation-astronomy.md), the familiar twinkling of stars. A phase-only [adaptive optics](../../../../../../adaptive-optics.md) correction can reduce atmospheric phase errors but does not remove absorption, all scintillation, or the atmospheric emission background.
