<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

With $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+ieA_\mu$ and $F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu$, the [quantum electrodynamics](../../../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md) Lagrangian is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L
=-\frac14F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}
+\bar\psi(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu-m)\psi
=-\frac14F^2+\bar\psi(i\not\partial-m)\psi
-e\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi A_\mu.}
$$
