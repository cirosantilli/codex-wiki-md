For metric $\eta^{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$, the defining [Clifford algebra](../../../../../../clifford-algebra.md) relation is

$$
\boxed{\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}
=\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu+\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu
=2\eta^{\mu\nu}\mathbf1_4.}
$$
