<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For bosonic fields, [time ordering](../../../../../../time-ordering.md) places operators with later time arguments to the left:

$$
T\{\phi_1\cdots\phi_n\}
=\phi_{\pi(1)}\cdots\phi_{\pi(n)},
\qquad t_{\pi(1)}\geq\cdots\geq t_{\pi(n)}.
$$

[Normal ordering](../../../../../../normal-ordering.md) places all creation operators to the left of all annihilation operators and is denoted by colons. A [Wick contraction](../../../../../../wick-contraction.md) is

$$
\operatorname{contr}(\phi_i,\phi_j)
=\langle0|T\{\phi_i\phi_j\}|0\rangle
=D_F(x_i-x_j).
$$

[Wick theorem](../../../../../../wick-s-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{T\{\phi_1\cdots\phi_n\}
=:\!\phi_1\cdots\phi_n\!:
+\sum_{\text{single contractions}}:\!\cdots\!:
+\sum_{\text{double contractions}}:\!\cdots\!:+\cdots,}
$$

where each sum runs over inequivalent disjoint pairings and contracted fields are replaced by their [Feynman propagator](../../../../../../feynman-propagator.md).
