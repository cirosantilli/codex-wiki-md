<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A spacetime translation $x^\mu\mapsto x^\mu+\epsilon^\mu$ gives, by [Noether theorem](../../../../../../noether-theorem.md), the [canonical stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md)

$$
\boxed{T^{\mu\nu}=\partial^\mu\phi\,\partial^\nu\phi
-\eta^{\mu\nu}\mathcal L.}
$$

The [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md) implies $\partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0$. With canonical momentum $\pi=\dot\phi$, the conserved physical three-momentum is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf P=\int d^3x\,T^{0i}\mathbf e_i
=-\int d^3x\,\pi(\mathbf x)\boldsymbol\nabla\phi(\mathbf x).}
$$

The minus sign follows from $\partial^i=-\partial_i$ for metric signature $(+---)$.
