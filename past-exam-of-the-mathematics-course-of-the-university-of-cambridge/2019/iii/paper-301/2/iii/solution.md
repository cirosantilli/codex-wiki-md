<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The tree amplitude is, up to an overall convention-dependent sign,

$$
\mathcal M=-\lambda\bar u(q_2)\not p\,v(q_1),
\qquad p=q_1+q_2.
$$

The [Clifford algebra](../../../../../../clifford-algebra.md) gives the momentum-space [Dirac equation](../../../../../../dirac-equation.md) and its adjoint:

$$
\not q_1v(q_1)=-m v(q_1),
\qquad \bar u(q_2)\not q_2=m\bar u(q_2).
$$

Therefore

$$
\mathcal M=-\lambda\bar u(q_2)(\not q_1+\not q_2)v(q_1)=0.
$$

Although $\mu>2m$ permits the [relativistic two-body decay](../../../../../../relativistic-two-body-decay.md) kinematically, the matrix element vanishes. Thus

$$
\boxed{\sum_{\rm spins}|\mathcal M|^2=0,
\qquad \Gamma(\phi\to\bar\psi\psi)=0.}
$$
