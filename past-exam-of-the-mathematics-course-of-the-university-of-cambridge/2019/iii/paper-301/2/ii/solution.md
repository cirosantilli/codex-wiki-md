<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In four spacetime dimensions $[\psi]=3/2$, $[\phi]=1$, and $[\partial_\mu]=1$. Hence

$$
[\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi\,\partial_\mu\phi]=5,
\qquad\boxed{[\lambda]=-1.}
$$

The coupling is an [irrelevant coupling](../../../../../../irrelevant-coupling.md) by [power counting in quantum field theory](../../../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md), so it is a [nonrenormalizable interaction](../../../../../../nonrenormalizable-interaction.md) that would ordinarily define only an effective theory with a cutoff. Here it is also a [redundant operator](../../../../../../redundant-operator.md): integration by parts gives $\lambda\phi\,\partial_\mu j^\mu$ up to a boundary term, and the [Dirac current](../../../../../../dirac-current.md) $j^\mu=\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi$ is conserved. This [derivative coupling to a conserved current](../../../../../../derivative-coupling-to-a-conserved-current.md) is removed exactly by the local phase redefinition $\psi=e^{-i\lambda\phi}\chi$.
