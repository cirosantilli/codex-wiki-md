<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The difference between the two squared actions is $|\dot p+\Gamma\mu|^2-|-\dot p+\Gamma\mu|^2=4\Gamma\dot p\cdot\mu$. The common factor cancels, giving

$$
\boxed{\frac{P_F}{P_B}=\exp\left[-\frac{2\Gamma}{\sigma^2}\int\dot p\cdot\frac{\delta F}{\delta p}\,d\mathbf r\,dt\right]
=\exp\left[-\frac{2\Gamma}{\sigma^2}(F_2-F_1)\right].}
$$

The last equality uses the [functional chain rule](../../../../../../functional-chain-rule.md) with the same midpoint convention as the [Onsager–Machlup functional](../../../../../../onsager-machlup-functional.md).

At equilibrium, [microscopic reversibility](../../../../../../microscopic-reversibility.md) requires $e^{-F_1/(k_BT)}P_F=e^{-F_2/(k_BT)}P_B$, equivalently $P_F/P_B=e^{-(F_2-F_1)/(k_BT)}$. Comparing the coefficients of an arbitrary free-energy change gives the [model A fluctuation-dissipation relation](../../../../../../model-a-fluctuation-dissipation-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{\sigma^2=2\Gamma k_BT.}
$$
