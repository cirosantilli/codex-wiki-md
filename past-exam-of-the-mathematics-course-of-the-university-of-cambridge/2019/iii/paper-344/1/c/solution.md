<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply the [Jensen inequality](../../../../../../jensen-s-inequality.md) to the convex [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) under the trial equilibrium measure:

$$
\left\langle e^{-(H-H_0)}\right\rangle_0\geq e^{-\langle H-H_0\rangle_0}.
$$

The given identity therefore implies $e^{-F}\geq e^{-F_0-\langle H-H_0\rangle_0}$. Taking minus the [logarithm](../../../../../../logarithm.md) reverses the inequality and proves the [Feynman-Bogoliubov inequality](../../../../../../gibbs-bogoliubov-feynman-inequality.md)

$$
\boxed{F\leq F_0+\langle H-H_0\rangle_0=F_0-\langle H_0\rangle_0+\langle H\rangle_0.}
$$

The units $\beta=1$ make $H$ and $F$ dimensionless here. Equality holds when $H-H_0$ is constant on the support of the trial measure. Optimizing the trial parameters tightens an upper bound, which is the basis of the [Gaussian variational approximation](../../../../../../gaussian-variational-approximation.md).
