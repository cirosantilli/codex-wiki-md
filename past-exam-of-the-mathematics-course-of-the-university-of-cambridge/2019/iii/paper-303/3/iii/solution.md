<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Gaussian fixed point](../../../../../../gaussian-fixed-point.md) is

$$
\boxed{(\mu_*^2,g_*)=(0,0).}
$$

Linearizing there gives $d\mu^2/ds=2\mu^2+\cdots$, so the [mass term](../../../../../../mass-term.md) is relevant with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $y_{\mu^2}=2$. The coupling $g$ has no linear term and is classically marginal, $y_g=0$; for positive $g$ the term $bg^2$ makes it marginally relevant in the infrared convention of the question. Since the inverse thermal eigenvalue is the [correlation-length critical exponent](../../../../../../correlation-length-critical-exponent.md),

$$
\boxed{\nu_{\rm G}=\frac1{y_{\mu^2}}=\frac12.}
$$
