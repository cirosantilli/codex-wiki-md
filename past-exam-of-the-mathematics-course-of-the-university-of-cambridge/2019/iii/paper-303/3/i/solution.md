<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At a critical point, a [local field operator](../../../../../../local-operator-physics.md) $\mathcal O$ has [scaling dimension](../../../../../../scaling-dimension.md) $\Delta_{\mathcal O}$ if $\mathcal O(x)\mapsto b^{-\Delta_{\mathcal O}}\mathcal O(x/b)$ under coarse-graining by $b$. The coupling $u$ in $\int d^dx\,u\mathcal O$ has renormalization-group [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
y_u=d-\Delta_{\mathcal O}.
$$

A perturbation is a [relevant operator](../../../../../../relevant-operator.md) when $y_u>0$, an [irrelevant operator](../../../../../../irrelevant-operator.md) when $y_u<0$, and a [marginal operator](../../../../../../marginal-operator.md) when $y_u=0$; nonlinear terms in its [renormalization-group beta function](../../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) decide the fate of a marginal coupling. Irrelevant microscopic interactions decay under coarse-graining, so many distinct systems approach the same fixed point and share one [universality class](../../../../../../universality-class.md).

Near criticality the singular [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) density is of order one per correlation volume:

$$
f_{\rm s}\sim\xi^{-d}\sim t^{\nu d}.
$$

The [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) contains two derivatives with respect to [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md), so

$$
c_{\rm s}\sim\frac{d^2f_{\rm s}}{dt^2}
\sim t^{\nu d-2}=t^{-\alpha}.
$$

Consequently the [hyperscaling relation](../../../../../../hyperscaling-relation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\alpha=2-d\nu.}
$$
