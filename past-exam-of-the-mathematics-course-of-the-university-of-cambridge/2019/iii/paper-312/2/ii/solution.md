<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The linear [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) is Gaussian, so its cubic [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) vanishes. The leading contribution is

$$
\langle\delta^3\rangle=3\langle(\delta^{(1)})^2\delta^{(2)}\rangle+\cdots.
$$

Two connected [Wick contractions](../../../../../../wick-contraction.md) give

$$
\langle\delta^3\rangle
=6D^4\int\frac{d^3q_1d^3q_2}{(2\pi)^6}
P_{\rm lin}(q_1)P_{\rm lin}(q_2)F_{2,s}(\mathbf q_1,\mathbf q_2).
$$

The third pairing vanishes because $F_{2,s}(\mathbf q,-\mathbf q)=0$. Isotropy makes $\langle\mu\rangle=0$ and $\langle\mu^2\rangle=1/3$, so the angular average of the kernel is

$$
\frac57+\frac27\frac13=\frac{17}{21}.
$$

Since $\sigma^2=D^2\int d^3q\,P_{\rm lin}(q)/(2\pi)^3$ to leading order, the [unsmoothed matter-density reduced skewness](../../../../../../unsmoothed-matter-density-reduced-skewness.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle\delta^3\rangle=\frac{34}{7}\sigma^4+O(\sigma^6),
\qquad S_3=\frac{34}{7}+O(\sigma^2)}.
$$

This assumes finite unsmoothed [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md); inserting a smoothing window generally changes $S_3$.
