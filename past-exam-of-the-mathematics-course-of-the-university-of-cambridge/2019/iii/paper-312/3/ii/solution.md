<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Dropping gravity and expansion from the [photon Boltzmann hierarchy](../../../../../../photon-boltzmann-hierarchy.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\dot\Theta_0=-\frac{k}{3}\Theta_1},
\qquad
\boxed{\dot\Theta_1=k\Theta_0-\frac{2k}{5}\Theta_2+
\dot\tau(\Theta_1+v_b)}.
$$

The first equation is photon energy continuity: the [photon dipole](../../../../../../photon-dipole.md) transports the [photon monopole](../../../../../../photon-monopole.md). The second is the [photon Euler equation](../../../../../../photon-euler-equation.md): a pressure gradient accelerates the radiation, its quadrupole contributes shear stress, and [Thomson scattering](../../../../../../thomson-scattering.md) drags the velocity toward the baryon velocity.

With $v_\gamma=-\Theta_1$, the scattering contribution to $\dot v_\gamma$ is $\dot\tau(v_\gamma-v_b)$. Radiation momentum density is $(4\bar\rho_\gamma/3)\mathbf v_\gamma$, whereas baryon momentum density is $\bar\rho_b\mathbf v_b$. Conservation of total momentum therefore requires

$$
\frac43\bar\rho_\gamma\,\dot v_\gamma\big|_{\rm sc}
+\bar\rho_b\,\dot v_b\big|_{\rm sc}=0,
$$

or

$$
\boxed{\dot v_b=-\frac{\dot\tau}{R}(v_\gamma-v_b),
\qquad R=\frac{3\bar\rho_b}{4\bar\rho_\gamma}}.
$$

Since the [cosmological optical depth](../../../../../../cosmological-optical-depth.md) is measured to the observation time, $\dot\tau<0$; the two drag terms reduce their relative velocity.
