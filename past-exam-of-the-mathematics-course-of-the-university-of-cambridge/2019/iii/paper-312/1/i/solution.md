<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The early-time state is the adiabatic [Bunch-Davies vacuum](../../../../../../bunch-davies-vacuum.md): modes well inside the [sound horizon](../../../../../../sound-horizon.md) approach positive-frequency Minkowski modes, and the tilted early-time contour selects the interacting vacuum by damping unwanted excitations. The [in-in formalism](../../../../../../keldysh-formalism.md) evolves this one initial state forward to the observation time and backward, computing $\langle\Omega|Q(\tau)|\Omega\rangle$. An in-out calculation instead computes a transition amplitude between prescribed initial and final states.

In the [interaction picture](../../../../../../interaction-picture.md), the free quadratic Hamiltonian evolves both $Q^I$ and the free fields inside $H_{\rm int}^I$. The background Hamiltonian determines the homogeneous solution; the cubic Hamiltonian supplies the perturbative interaction insertions. In particular, **the interaction-picture fields evolve under the quadratic Hamiltonian**.
