<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For each $0<r<1$, the spherical [mean value property for harmonic functions](../../../../../../mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md) gives

$$
\int_{S^{d-1}}u(x+\varepsilon r\omega)\,d\omega=d\omega_d u(x).
$$

Insert this into part 1(iii). The normalization of the radial [mollifier](../../../../../../mollifier.md) is $d\omega_d\int_0^1\phi(r)r^{d-1}dr=1$, hence

$$
\boxed{(u*\phi_\varepsilon)(x)=u(x).}
$$

A convolution with a [smooth function](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) of compact support is smooth wherever it is defined. Every point admits such an $\varepsilon$, so $u\in C^\infty(\Omega)$.
