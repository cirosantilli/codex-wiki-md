<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $w\in X_\delta$, hypothesis (2) gives $F(w)\in C^{0,\alpha}(\overline\Omega)$. Thus $f-F(w)$ is Hölder continuous and the classical [Dirichlet problem](../../../../../../dirichlet-problem.md) for the [Poisson equation](../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) has a unique solution $u=T(w)\in C^{2,\alpha}(\overline\Omega)$. The [global Schauder estimate](../../../../../../global-schauder-estimate.md) gives

$$
\|T(w)\|_{C^{2,\alpha}}
\leq C\left(\|F(w)\|_{C^{0,\alpha}}+
\|f\|_{C^{0,\alpha}}+
\|T(w)\|_{C^0}+\|\phi\|_{C^{2,\alpha}}\right).
$$

The maximum estimate for the Poisson problem also gives

$$
\|T(w)\|_{C^0}\leq C\left(\|f\|_{C^0}+\|F(w)\|_{C^0}+\|\phi\|_{C^0}\right).
$$

These estimates prove that $T$ is well defined and give the requested norm control.
