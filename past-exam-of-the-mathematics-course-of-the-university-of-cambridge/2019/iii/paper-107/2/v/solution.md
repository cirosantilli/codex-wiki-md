<h1 id="2/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The stated assumptions alone do not imply regularity up to the boundary: continuous boundary data need not have two Hölder derivatives. A standard sufficient set of hypotheses for the [global Schauder estimate](../../../../../../global-schauder-estimate.md) is

$$
\partial\Omega\in C^{2,\alpha},\qquad
\phi\in C^{2,\alpha}(\overline\Omega),\qquad
f\in C^{0,\alpha}(\overline\Omega),
$$

with $a^{ij},b^i,c\in C^{0,\alpha}(\overline\Omega)$ and uniform ellipticity. Then the [global Schauder estimate](../../../../../../global-schauder-estimate.md) gives

$$
\boxed{u\in C^{2,\alpha}(\overline\Omega)}
$$

and bounds its norm by the forcing, boundary data, and $C^0$ norm.
