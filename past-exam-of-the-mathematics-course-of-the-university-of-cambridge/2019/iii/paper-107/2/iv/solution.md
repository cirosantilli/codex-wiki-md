<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If $u_1,u_2$ solve the same [Dirichlet problem](../../../../../../dirichlet-problem.md), their difference $v=u_1-u_2$ satisfies

$$
Lv=0\text{ in }\Omega,
\qquad
v=0\text{ on }\partial\Omega.
$$

Part 2(ii) applied to $v$ gives $v\leq0$, and applied to $-v$ gives $v\geq0$. Hence $v=0$ and **the solution is unique**.
