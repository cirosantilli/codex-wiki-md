<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Yes, but there is only one nontrivial isomorphism type that works. If $G=C_2$, then

$$
G*G=C_2*C_2\cong D_\infty.
$$

The [infinite dihedral group](../../../../../../infinite-dihedral-group.md) contains its infinite cyclic rotation subgroup with index two, so it is amenable by [virtually abelian groups are amenable](../../../../../../virtually-abelian-groups-are-amenable.md).

Suppose now that $G\not\cong C_2$. The action of $G*G$ on its [Bass-Serre tree](../../../../../../bass-serre-tree.md) is non-elementary: one vertex degree is greater than two, so there are hyperbolic elements with disjoint pairs of endpoints. Suitable powers satisfy the [ping-pong lemma](../../../../../../ping-pong-lemma.md) on the boundary and generate a copy of $F_2$. Since [a subgroup of an amenable group is amenable](../../../../../../a-subgroup-of-an-amenable-group-is-amenable.md), an amenable group cannot contain this nonamenable subgroup. The [amenability of a free product](../../../../../../amenability-of-a-free-product.md) therefore yields

$$
\boxed{G*G\text{ is amenable}\iff G\cong C_2}
$$

for nontrivial finitely generated $G$.
