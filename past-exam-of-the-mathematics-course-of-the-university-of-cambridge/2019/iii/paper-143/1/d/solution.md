<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $G$ have a [generating set of a group](../../../../../../generating-set-of-a-group.md) with $r$ elements. Every subgroup $H$ of index $n$ gives a [transitive group action](../../../../../../transitive-group-action.md) of $G$ on the $n$ left cosets of $H$, hence a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $\rho_H:G\to S_n$ after the cosets are labelled. Conversely, $H$ is the [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) of one point in this action.

A homomorphism $G\to S_n$ is determined by the images of the $r$ generators, so there are at most $(n!)^r$ such homomorphisms and at most $n(n!)^r$ point stabilizers. Therefore the [finiteness of subgroups of a fixed finite index](../../../../../../finiteness-of-subgroups-of-a-fixed-finite-index.md) gives **only finitely many subgroups of index $n$**.
