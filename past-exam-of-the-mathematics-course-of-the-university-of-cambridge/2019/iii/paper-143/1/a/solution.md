<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Nielsen–Schreier theorem](../../../../../../nielsen-schreier-theorem.md) says that every [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of a [free group](../../../../../../free-group.md) is free. Realize $F_n$ as the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) of the rose $R_n$, the graph with one vertex and $n$ oriented loops. A subgroup $H\leq F_n$ of finite [index of a subgroup](../../../../../../index-of-a-subgroup.md) $d$ corresponds to a connected $d$-sheeted [covering graph](../../../../../../covering-graph.md) $X\to R_n$. The graph $X$ has $d$ vertices and $dn$ unoriented edges. Choosing a [spanning tree](../../../../../../spanning-tree.md) leaves

$$
dn-(d-1)=1+d(n-1)
$$

edges outside the tree, and these freely generate $\pi_1(X)\cong H$. Thus the [Nielsen–Schreier formula](../../../../../../nielsen-schreier-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}(H)=1+d(n-1).}
$$
