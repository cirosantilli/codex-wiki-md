<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take the [free abelian group](../../../../../../free-abelian-group.md) $G=\mathbb Z^2$, whose [rank of a group](../../../../../../rank-of-a-group.md) is two, and its [finite-index subgroup](../../../../../../finite-index-subgroup.md) $H=2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z$. The subgroup has index two and is again isomorphic to $\mathbb Z^2$, so its rank is two. The [Nielsen–Schreier formula](../../../../../../nielsen-schreier-formula.md) would instead give $1+2(2-1)=3$. Hence

$$
\boxed{G=\mathbb Z^2,\qquad H=2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z}
$$

is a counterexample.
