↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Yes. Take $G=F_2$. The [free product](../../../../../../free-product.md) satisfies

$$
G*G\cong F_2*F_2\cong F_4.
$$

A connected three-sheeted cover of the two-petal rose has [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) of rank $1+3(2-1)=4$ by the [Nielsen–Schreier formula](../../../../../../nielsen-schreier-formula.md). Thus $F_4$ is isomorphic to an index-three subgroup of $F_2$. A [finite-index subgroup quasi-isometry](../../../../../../finite-index-subgroup-quasi-isometry.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{F_2\simeq_{\mathrm{qi}}F_4\cong F_2*F_2.}
$$
