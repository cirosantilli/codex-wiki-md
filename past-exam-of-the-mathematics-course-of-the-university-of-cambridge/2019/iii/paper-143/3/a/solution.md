<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a locally finite [discrete metric](../../../../../../discrete-metric.md) space $(X,d)$ with basepoint $x_0$, the [growth function of a discrete metric space](../../../../../../growth-function-of-a-discrete-metric-space.md) is

$$
\beta_{X,x_0}(r)=|B(x_0,r)|
=\#\{x\in X:d(x,x_0)\leq r\}.
$$

In a [Cayley graph](../../../../../../cayley-graph.md), left multiplication by $gx_0^{-1}$ is a graph isometry carrying $x_0$ to any other vertex $g$. It therefore gives a bijection $B(x_0,r)\to B(g,r)$ for every $r$. Thus **the growth function of a fixed Cayley graph is exactly independent of the basepoint**.
