<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose any finite generating set $T$ of the [finitely generated group](../../../../../../finitely-generated-group.md) $H$. Starting from a finite generating set $S_0$ of $G$, set

$$
S=S_0\cup T.
$$

Every $T$-word in $H$ of length at most $r$ is also an $S$-word in $G$ of length at most $r$. The inclusion of the corresponding word-metric balls is injective, so the [growth function of a finitely generated group](../../../../../../growth-function-of-a-finitely-generated-group.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{\beta_{H,T}(r)\leq\beta_{G,S}(r)\qquad(r\geq0).}
$$
