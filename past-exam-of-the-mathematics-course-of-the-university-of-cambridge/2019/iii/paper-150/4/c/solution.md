<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

There is an absolute $c>0$ such that the product of the [Dirichlet L-functions](../../../../../../dirichlet-l-function.md) modulo $q$ has no zero in

$$
\boxed{\sigma\geq1-\frac{c}{\log(q(|t|+2))}}
$$

except possibly one zero. If it exists, this [exceptional zero](../../../../../../siegel-zero.md) $\beta$ is real and simple, belongs to a real nonprincipal character $\chi_1$, and lies very close to one. Among the primitive characters whose conductors divide $q$, at most one can have such a zero. This is the [classical zero-free region for Dirichlet L-functions](../../../../../../classical-zero-free-region-for-dirichlet-l-functions.md).
