<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $\chi_1$ be the real character associated with the exceptional zero $\beta$. The [prime number theorem in an arithmetic progression with an exceptional zero](../../../../../../prime-number-theorem-in-an-arithmetic-progression-with-an-exceptional-zero.md) gives, uniformly for $x\geq2$ and $(a,q)=1$,

$$
\boxed{
\psi(x;q,a)
=\frac{x}{\varphi(q)}
-\frac{\chi_1(a)x^\beta}{\beta\varphi(q)}
+O\left(
x(\log q)^2
\exp\left[-\frac{c\log x}{\log q+\sqrt{\log x}}\right]
\right).
}
$$

If no exceptional zero exists, the middle term is omitted. The constant $c>0$ is absolute.
