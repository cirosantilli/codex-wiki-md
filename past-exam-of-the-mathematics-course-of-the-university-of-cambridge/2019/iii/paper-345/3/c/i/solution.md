<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $f_0=F_0/(\pi\rho_0)$ as above. A [self-similar starting plume](../../../../../../../self-similar-starting-plume.md) has no source length or time scale; [dimensional analysis](../../../../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{Z=c_Zf_0^{1/4}t^{3/4},\qquad
R=c_Rf_0^{1/4}t^{3/4},\qquad
U=\dot Z=\frac34c_Zf_0^{1/4}t^{-1/4},\qquad
 g_t=c_gf_0^{1/4}t^{-5/4}.}
$$

The dimensionless constants depend on the [entrainment coefficient](../../../../../../../entrainment-coefficient.md) and thermal closure. Thus $R/B$ is constant because the underlying [Boussinesq point-source plume](../../../../../../../boussinesq-point-source-plume.md) has $B=b(Z)=(6\alpha/5)Z$. The steadily increasing thermal volume scales as $t^{9/4}$, even though its [reduced gravity](../../../../../../../reduced-gravity-split.md) decreases.
