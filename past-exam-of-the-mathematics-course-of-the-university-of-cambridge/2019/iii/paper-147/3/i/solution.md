↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For nonempty $A\subseteq X$ and $B\subseteq Y$, their [edge density of a bipartite graph](../../../../../../edge-density-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) is

$$
d(A,B)=\frac{e(A,B)}{|A||B|}.
$$

The pair $(A,B)$ is $\varepsilon$-regular when every $A'\subseteq A$ and $B'\subseteq B$ with $|A'|\geq\varepsilon|A|$ and $|B'|\geq\varepsilon|B|$ satisfy

$$
|d(A',B')-d(A,B)|\leq\varepsilon.
$$

For [set partitions](../../../../../../set-partition.md) $X=X_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup X_r$ and $Y=Y_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup Y_s$, the [regular pair of bipartite partitions](../../../../../../regular-pair-of-bipartite-partitions.md) condition is

$$
\boxed{\sum_{(i,j):\,(X_i,Y_j)\text{ is not }\varepsilon\text{-regular}}|X_i||Y_j|
\leq\varepsilon|X||Y|.}
$$
