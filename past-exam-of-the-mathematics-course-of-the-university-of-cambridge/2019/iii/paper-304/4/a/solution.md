<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a short [Wilson line](../../../../../../wilson-line.md), a [gauge transformation](../../../../../../gauge-transformation.md) gives

$$
U'(x+an,x)=V(x+an)U(x+an,x)V^\dagger(x).
$$

Use $V(x+an)=1+i\alpha(x)+ia n^\mu\partial_\mu\alpha(x)+O(a^2,\alpha^2)$ and $U=1+iga n^\mu A_\mu+O(a^2)$. Keeping terms of order $a$, including $a\alpha$, gives

$$
U'=1+iga n^\mu
\left(A_\mu+\frac1g\partial_\mu\alpha+i[\alpha,A_\mu]\right)+O(a^2,\alpha^2).
$$

Since the [Lie bracket](../../../../../../lie-bracket.md) is encoded by the [Lie algebra structure constants](../../../../../../structure-constant-of-a-lie-algebra.md), $i[\alpha,A_\mu]^a=f^{abc}A_\mu^b\alpha^c$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{(A^\alpha)_\mu^a=A_\mu^a
+\frac1g\partial_\mu\alpha^a
+f^{abc}A_\mu^b\alpha^c.}
$$

This is the infinitesimal form of the [Wilson-line gauge transformation](../../../../../../wilson-line-gauge-transformation.md).
