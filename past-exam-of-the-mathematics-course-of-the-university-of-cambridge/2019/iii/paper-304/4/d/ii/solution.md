The anticommuting fields $c$ and $\bar c$ represent the field-dependent [Faddeev-Popov determinant](../../../../../../../faddeev-popov-determinant.md). They are Lorentz-scalar [Grassmann fields](../../../../../../../grassmann-field.md), appear only on internal lines, and contribute a minus sign for each closed [ghost loop](../../../../../../../ghost-loop.md). Their diagrams cancel unphysical gauge-polarization contributions and are required for gauge-independent, unitary amplitudes in a non-Abelian covariant gauge.
