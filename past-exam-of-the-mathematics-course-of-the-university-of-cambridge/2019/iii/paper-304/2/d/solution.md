<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [continuum limit of a quantum field theory](../../../../../../continuum-limit-of-a-quantum-field-theory.md) sends the [ultraviolet cutoff](../../../../../../ultraviolet-cutoff.md) $\Lambda_0$ to infinity while holding chosen physical masses and amplitudes fixed. In cutoff units this requires a diverging [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md), so the bare couplings must be tuned onto the [critical surface](../../../../../../critical-surface.md) of an ultraviolet [renormalization-group fixed point](../../../../../../renormalization-group-fixed-point.md). Each [relevant direction of a fixed point](../../../../../../relevant-direction-of-a-fixed-point.md) requires one coordinate fixed by measurement; predictivity requires only finitely many such directions, and irrelevant microscopic details disappear.

Several behaviors are possible. In an [asymptotically free](../../../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md) theory the trajectory approaches a [Gaussian fixed point](../../../../../../gaussian-fixed-point.md) in the ultraviolet and interactions vanish logarithmically. In an [asymptotically safe quantum field theory](../../../../../../asymptotic-safety.md) it approaches a non-Gaussian fixed point with finitely many relevant directions. A theory with a [Landau pole](../../../../../../landau-pole.md) may possess only a [trivial quantum field theory](../../../../../../quantum-triviality.md) as its continuum limit; retaining a nonzero interaction then requires a finite cutoff. Finally, if no ultraviolet-complete trajectory exists, the model remains an [effective field theory](../../../../../../effective-field-theory.md) valid only below a physical cutoff.
