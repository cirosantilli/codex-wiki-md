<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Introduce the [renormalization-group beta functions](../../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) and field anomalous dimension

$$
\beta_i=\Lambda\frac{dg_i}{d\Lambda},
\qquad
\beta_{m^2}=\Lambda\frac{dm^2}{d\Lambda},
\qquad
\gamma_\phi=\frac12\Lambda\frac{d\log Z_\Lambda}{d\Lambda}.
$$

Independence of physics from the arbitrary sliding scale gives the functional [Callan-Symanzik equation](../../../../../../callan-symanzik-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\left[
\Lambda\partial_\Lambda+\beta_{m^2}\partial_{m^2}
+\sum_i\beta_i\partial_{g_i}
-\gamma_\phi\int d^4x\,\phi(x)\frac{\delta}{\delta\phi(x)}
\right]S_\Lambda^{\rm eff}=0,}
$$

up to the equivalent sign convention obtained by defining $\gamma_\phi$ with a minus sign. For an operator $O_i$ of dimension $d_i$ containing $n_i$ fields, differentiating its coefficient shows the canonical and wave-function pieces

$$
\beta_i=(d_i-4+n_i\gamma_\phi)g_i+\beta_i^{\rm vertex},
$$

where $\beta_i^{\rm vertex}$ contains mixing among [local field operators](../../../../../../local-operator-physics.md) and genuine corrections at higher [loop order](../../../../../../loop-order.md). Thus $d_i<4$, $d_i=4$, and $d_i>4$ canonically produce [relevant operators](../../../../../../relevant-operator.md), [marginal operators](../../../../../../marginal-operator.md), and [irrelevant operators](../../../../../../irrelevant-operator.md), respectively, before anomalous corrections.
