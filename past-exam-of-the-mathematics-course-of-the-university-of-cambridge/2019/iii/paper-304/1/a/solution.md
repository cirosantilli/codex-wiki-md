<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) with classical [action](../../../../../../action.md) $S[\phi]$, the Euclidean source convention used throughout this question gives

$$
Z[J]=\int\mathcal D\phi\,
\exp\left[-\frac1\hbar\left(S[\phi]+\int d^dx\,J(x)\phi(x)\right)\right].
$$

The [generating functional](../../../../../../generating-functional.md) produces [correlation functions](../../../../../../correlation-function.md) through [functional derivatives](../../../../../../functional-derivative.md). For example,

$$
\boxed{\langle\phi(x)\phi(y)\rangle
=\left.\frac{\hbar^2}{Z[0]}
\frac{\delta^2Z[J]}{\delta J(x)\delta J(y)}\right|_{J=0}.}
$$

More generally, each derivative brings down $-\phi/\hbar$, so an $n$-point function carries $(-\hbar)^n/Z[0]$.
