<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [kinetic terms](../../../../../../kinetic-term.md) determine the [engineering dimensions](../../../../../../engineering-dimension.md) in $d$ dimensions:

$$
[A_\mu]=\frac{d-2}{2},
\qquad [\psi]=\frac{d-1}{2}.
$$

Requiring $e\bar\psi\gamma^\mu A_\mu\psi$ to have dimension $d$ gives

$$
\boxed{[e]=\frac{4-d}{2}=\frac\epsilon2.}
$$

Thus the electric charge is dimensionless only in four dimensions. In [dimensional regularization](../../../../../../dimensional-regularization.md) one writes the bare interaction with $e_0=\mu^{\epsilon/2}Z_e e$, where $e$ is dimensionless and the [renormalization scale](../../../../../../renormalization-scale.md) $\mu$ supplies the missing dimension.
