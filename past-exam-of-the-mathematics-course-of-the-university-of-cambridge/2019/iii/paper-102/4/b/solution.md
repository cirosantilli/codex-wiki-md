↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [B2 root system](../../../../../../b2-root-system.md) with $\alpha_1$ short, the positive roots are

$$
\alpha_1,\quad\alpha_2,\quad\alpha_1+\alpha_2,\quad2\alpha_1+\alpha_2.
$$

Substituting $\lambda=a\omega_1+b\omega_2$ into the [Weyl dimension formula for B2](../../../../../../weyl-dimension-formula-for-b2.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\dim V(a\omega_1+b\omega_2)
=\frac{(a+1)(b+1)(a+b+2)(a+2b+3)}6.}
$$
