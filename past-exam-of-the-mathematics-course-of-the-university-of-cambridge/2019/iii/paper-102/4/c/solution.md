<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $V=V(\omega_2)$ be the five-dimensional defining representation of the [so5 Lie algebra](../../../../../../so5-lie-algebra.md). The [tensor square](../../../../../../tensor-square.md) splits into its [symmetric square](../../../../../../symmetric-square.md) and [exterior square](../../../../../../exterior-square.md):

$$
V\otimes V=S^2V\oplus\Lambda^2V.
$$

The invariant symmetric form spans a trivial subrepresentation of $S^2V$, while its traceless complement is the irreducible $V(2\omega_2)$ of dimension $14$. The identification $\Lambda^2V\cong\mathfrak{so}_5$ makes the exterior square the ten-dimensional [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), whose highest weight is the highest root $2\omega_1$. Therefore the [Tensor-square decomposition of the defining so5 representation](../../../../../../tensor-square-decomposition-of-the-defining-so5-representation.md) is

$$
\boxed{V\otimes V\cong V(0)\oplus V(2\omega_1)\oplus V(2\omega_2).}
$$
