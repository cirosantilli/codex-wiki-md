↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a dominant integral [highest weight](../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\lambda$, the [Weyl dimension formula](../../../../../../weyl-dimension-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dim V(\lambda)=\prod_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}
\frac{\langle\lambda+\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}
{\langle\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle},}
$$

where $\Phi^+$ is the chosen [positive system of a root system](../../../../../../positive-system-of-a-root-system.md), $\alpha^\vee$ is a [coroot](../../../../../../coroot.md), and $\rho$ is the [Weyl vector](../../../../../../half-sum-of-positive-roots.md).
