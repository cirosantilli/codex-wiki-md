<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $t\in\mathfrak t$, the [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md) gives its [centralizer](../../../../../../centralizer.md)

$$
\mathfrak z_{\mathfrak g}(t)
=\mathfrak t\oplus\bigoplus_{\alpha(t)=0}\mathfrak g_\alpha.
$$

Every root space is one-dimensional, so this centralizer has the minimum possible dimension $\ell=\dim\mathfrak t$ exactly when no summand on the right occurs. By the [Regular element criterion in a Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../regular-element-criterion-in-a-cartan-subalgebra.md),

$$
\boxed{t\text{ is regular}\iff \alpha(t)\ne0\text{ for every root }\alpha.}
$$
