<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose a [positive system of a root system](../../../../../../positive-system-of-a-root-system.md) in which $\alpha$ is a [simple root](../../../../../../simple-root.md); this is possible after applying an element of the [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md). Let $\theta$ be the [highest root](../../../../../../highest-root.md). Since the rank is greater than one, $\theta\ne\alpha$, and the maximality of $\theta$ implies that $\theta+\alpha$ is not a root.

For $0\ne x\in\mathfrak g_\alpha$, the $(\ell-1)$-dimensional space $\ker\alpha\subset\mathfrak t$ centralizes $x$. The line $\mathbb Cx$ also centralizes $x$, and $[x,\mathfrak g_\theta]\subseteq\mathfrak g_{\alpha+\theta}=0$. These independent spaces give

$$
\dim\mathfrak z_{\mathfrak g}(x)\geq(\ell-1)+1+1=\ell+1.
$$

Hence **a nonzero simple-root vector is not regular when $\ell>1$**.
