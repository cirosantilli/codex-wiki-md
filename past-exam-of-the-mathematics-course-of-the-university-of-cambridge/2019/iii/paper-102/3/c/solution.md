<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $h_0=\psi(h)$ and $e_0=\psi(e)=\sum_{i=1}^{\ell}e_i$, where $0\ne e_i\in\mathfrak g_{\alpha_i}$. Since $[h_0,e_0]=2e_0$ and the [root spaces](../../../../../../root-space.md) are a [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md), $\alpha_i(h_0)=2$ for every simple root. Every root has simple-root coefficients of one sign, so no root vanishes on $h_0$. The [Regular element criterion in a Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../regular-element-criterion-in-a-cartan-subalgebra.md) therefore shows that **$h_0$ is regular**.

Restrict the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of $\mathfrak g$ along $\psi$. By [complete reducibility of semisimple Lie algebra representations](../../../../../../weyl-s-theorem-on-complete-reducibility.md), it is a direct sum of finite-dimensional [sl2 Lie algebra](../../../../../../sl2-lie-algebra.md) modules. The $h_0$-eigenvalues on a root space are twice the heights of the roots, so they are all even. Each irreducible summand consequently has even highest weight, contains exactly one zero-weight vector, and has a one-dimensional kernel for the raising operator $e_0$ by the [classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](../../../../../../classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md).

Because $h_0$ is regular, its zero-weight space in $\mathfrak g$ is precisely $\mathfrak t$ and has dimension $\ell$. There are therefore exactly $\ell$ irreducible summands, whence

$$
\dim\ker(\operatorname{ad}e_0)=\ell.
$$

Thus **$e_0$ is regular**; equivalently it is a [principal nilpotent element](../../../../../../principal-nilpotent-element.md) in the given [Principal sl2 subalgebra](../../../../../../principal-sl2-subalgebra.md).
