<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write the simple roots of the [G2 root system](../../../../../../g2-root-system.md) as $\alpha$ short and $\beta$ long. The [Long-root A2 subsystem of G2](../../../../../../long-root-a2-subsystem-of-g2.md) has simple roots

$$
\delta_1=\beta,\qquad\delta_2=3\alpha+\beta.
$$

If $\omega_1,\omega_2$ are its [fundamental weights](../../../../../../fundamental-weight.md), then the $A_2$ root-weight relations give

$$
\alpha=\omega_2-\omega_1,\qquad
\alpha^\vee=\delta_2^\vee-\delta_1^\vee,\qquad
\beta^\vee=\delta_1^\vee.
$$

For the $A_2$-dominant weight $\lambda=a\omega_1+b\omega_2$, therefore,

$$
\langle\lambda,\beta^\vee\rangle=a,
\qquad
\langle\lambda,\alpha^\vee\rangle=b-a.
$$

The [dominant weight](../../../../../../dominant-weight.md) inequalities for $G_2$ are thus equivalent to

$$
\boxed{b\geq a}
$$

because $A_2$-dominance already gives $a,b\geq0$.
