<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The weights of the seven-dimensional irreducible $G_2$ representation are zero and the six short roots. In the $A_2$ [weight lattice](../../../../../../weight-lattice.md), the three positive short roots are

$$
\alpha=\omega_2-\omega_1,\qquad
\alpha+\beta=\omega_1,\qquad
2\alpha+\beta=\omega_2.
$$

Together with their negatives, they split into the weight sets of the two dual three-dimensional [Fundamental representations of sl3](../../../../../../fundamental-representations-of-sl3.md); the zero weight supplies a trivial representation. By the [Restriction of the seven-dimensional G2 representation to long-root A2](../../../../../../restriction-of-the-seven-dimensional-g2-representation-to-long-root-a2.md),

$$
\boxed{V_7\!\downarrow_{\mathfrak{sl}_3}
\cong V(\omega_1)\oplus V(\omega_2)\oplus V(0).}
$$
