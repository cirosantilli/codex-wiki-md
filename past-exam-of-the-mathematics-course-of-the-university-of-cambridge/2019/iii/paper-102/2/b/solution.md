↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A convenient [root basis](../../../../../../fundamental-system-of-a-root-system.md) is

$$
\boxed{\alpha_1=\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2,\qquad
\alpha_2=\varepsilon_2-\varepsilon_3,\qquad
\alpha_3=2\varepsilon_3.}
$$

The corresponding [Cartan matrix](../../../../../../cartan-matrix.md) is

$$
\left(\langle\alpha_i,\alpha_j^\vee\rangle\right)_{ij}
=\begin{pmatrix}
2&-1&0\\
-1&2&-1\\
0&-2&2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus the [Dynkin diagram](../../../../../../dynkin-diagram.md) is the three-node $C_3$ chain, with a double edge between $\alpha_2$ and $\alpha_3$ and its arrow pointing toward the shorter root $\alpha_2$.
