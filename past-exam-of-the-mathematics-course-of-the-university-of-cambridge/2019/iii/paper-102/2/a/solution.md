<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write an element of the diagonal [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) as

$$
t=\operatorname{diag}(t_1,t_2,t_3,-t_1,-t_2,-t_3)
$$

and define the [linear functionals](../../../../../../linear-functional.md) $\varepsilon_i(t)=t_i$. The [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md) of the [symplectic Lie algebra](../../../../../../symplectic-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sp}_6(\mathbb C)$ then has the [C3 root system](../../../../../../c3-root-system.md)

$$
\boxed{\Phi=\{\pm\varepsilon_i\pm\varepsilon_j:1\leq i<j\leq3\}\cup\{\pm2\varepsilon_i:1\leq i\leq3\}.}
$$
