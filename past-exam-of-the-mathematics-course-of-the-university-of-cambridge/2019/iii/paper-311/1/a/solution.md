<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An isolated collapse emits transient matter and [gravitational waves](../../../../../../gravitational-wave.md). The exterior is expected to settle to an asymptotically flat [stationary spacetime](../../../../../../stationary-spacetime.md). Because the star is uncharged, the [black-hole uniqueness theorem](../../../../../../black-hole-uniqueness-theorem.md) identifies the regular final state with a [Kerr black hole](../../../../../../kerr-black-hole.md). Its higher [multipole moments](../../../../../../multipole-moment.md) are fixed by its conserved charges: the [black-hole no-hair theorem](../../../../../../black-hole-no-hair-theorem.md) leaves only the total [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) $M$ and [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $J$. Thus **the late-time spacetime is characterized by $M$ and $J$**.
