<h1 id="1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For this [charged scalar field](../../../../../../../charged-scalar-field.md), $\sqrt{-g}=r^3\sin\theta\cos\theta$. On $e^{-i\omega t}$, the [gauge-covariant derivative of a charged scalar field](../../../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative-of-a-charged-scalar-field.md) supplies the shifted frequency

$$
\omega+qA_t=\omega-\frac{qQ}{r^2}.
$$

The wave equation becomes

$$
\frac1{r^3}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^3f\frac{dR}{dr}\right)+\frac{(\omega-qQ/r^2)^2}{f}R
+\frac{R}{r^2\Theta\sin\theta\cos\theta}\frac{d}{d\theta}\left(\sin\theta\cos\theta\frac{d\Theta}{d\theta}\right)=0.
$$

[Separation of variables](../../../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) with constant $\lambda$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac1{\sin\theta\cos\theta}\frac{d}{d\theta}\left(\sin\theta\cos\theta\frac{d\Theta}{d\theta}\right)+\lambda\Theta=0}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\frac1{r^3}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^3f\frac{dR}{dr}\right)+\left[\frac{(\omega-qQ/r^2)^2}{f}-\frac{\lambda}{r^2}\right]R=0}.
$$

The angular equation is the axisymmetric [spherical harmonic](../../../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md) equation on the [three-sphere](../../../../../../../three-sphere.md); regular solutions have $\lambda=\ell(\ell+2)$ with $\ell=0,2,4,\ldots$, the even values selected by the absence of $\phi$ and $\psi$ dependence.
