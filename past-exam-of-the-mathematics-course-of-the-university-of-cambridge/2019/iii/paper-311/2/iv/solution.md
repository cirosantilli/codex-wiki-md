<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Contract the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) $G_{ab}=8\pi T_{ab}$ twice with $n^a$. Since $n^an_a=-1$ and $\rho=T_{ab}n^an^b$,

$$
R+2R_{ab}n^an^b=16\pi\rho.
$$

If $K_{ab}$ is proportional to $h_{ab}$ in three dimensions, tracing gives $K_{ab}=(K/3)h_{ab}$ and $K_{ab}K^{ab}=K^2/3$. The [Scalar Gauss equation](../../../../../../scalar-gauss-equation.md) gives the [Hamiltonian constraint](../../../../../../hamiltonian-constraint.md)

$$
{}^{(3)}R=16\pi\rho-\frac23K^2=\frac23(24\pi\rho-K^2).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{24\pi\rho-K^2\geq0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad{}^{(3)}R\geq0}.
$$
