<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $D$ be the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) of $h$. Apply $(D_cD_d-D_dD_c)$ to a tangent vector, replace each $D$ by the tangential projection of $\nabla$, and decompose $\nabla n$ using $K_{ab}$. Comparison with the intrinsic [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) gives the [Gauss equation](../../../../../../gauss-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{{}^{(3)}R_{abcd}=h_a{}^eh_b{}^fh_c{}^gh_d{}^h\,{}^{(4)}R_{efgh}-K_{ac}K_{bd}+K_{ad}K_{bc}}.
$$

Changing the sign convention for $K_{ab}$ does not alter these quadratic terms.
