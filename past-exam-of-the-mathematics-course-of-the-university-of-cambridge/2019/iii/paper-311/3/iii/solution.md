<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The relevant implications in the [causal hierarchy](../../../../../../causal-hierarchy.md) are

$$
\text{stable causality}\Longrightarrow\text{strong causality}\Longrightarrow\text{future-distinguishing spacetime}.
$$

The first follows from strict cone widening: failure of [strong causality](../../../../../../strong-causality.md) would give causal curves leaving and re-entering arbitrarily small neighborhoods, and the wider metric turns the limiting recurrence into a [closed timelike curve](../../../../../../closed-timelike-curve.md). For the second, if distinct $p,q$ had $I^+(p)=I^+(q)$, arbitrarily short timelike segments near either point and this equality would produce a causal curve leaving and re-entering a sufficiently small causally convex neighborhood. Therefore

$$
\boxed{I^+(p)=I^+(q)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p=q}.
$$

The stated [null geodesic completeness](../../../../../../null-geodesic-completeness.md) is stronger than needed: stable causality already gives the conclusion.
