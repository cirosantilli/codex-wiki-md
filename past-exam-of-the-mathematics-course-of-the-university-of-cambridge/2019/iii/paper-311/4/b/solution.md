<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Classically, the [laws of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../laws-of-black-hole-mechanics.md) have the same form as the laws of [thermodynamics](../../../../../../thermodynamics-split.md), but this could have been only a formal analogy. [Hawking radiation](../../../../../../hawking-radiation.md) makes it physical: a stationary black hole emits approximately [thermal radiation](../../../../../../thermal-radiation.md) with

$$
\boxed{T_H=\frac{\hbar\kappa}{2\pi k_Bc}},
$$

so [surface gravity](../../../../../../surface-gravity.md) is proportional to genuine [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md).

The correspondence is:


- constancy of $\kappa$ on a stationary horizon becomes constancy of equilibrium temperature;
- the [First law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../first-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md)


$$
dM=\frac{\kappa}{8\pi G}\,dA+\Omega_H\,dJ+\Phi_H\,dQ
$$

matches the [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) and fixes the [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](../../../../../../bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md)

$$
\boxed{S_{BH}=\frac{k_Bc^3A}{4G\hbar}};
$$


- [Hawking's area theorem](../../../../../../hawking-s-area-theorem.md) becomes a second law, replaced in the quantum theory by the [generalized second law](../../../../../../generalized-second-law.md) for $S_{BH}+S_{\rm outside}$;
- the impossibility of reaching $\kappa=0$ by a finite process parallels the unattainability of absolute zero.

A real Hawking temperature therefore identifies horizon area as thermodynamic entropy and turns the geometric laws into thermodynamic laws.
