<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $D$ act on the [group algebra](../../../../../../../group-algebra.md) $kG$ by conjugation. Its fixed-point algebra is

$$
(kG)^D=\{a\in kG:dad^{-1}=a\text{ for every }d\in D\},
$$

and the [centralizer](../../../../../../../centralizer.md) $C_G(D)$ consists of the elements of $G$ commuting with every element of $D$. The [Brauer morphism](../../../../../../../brauer-morphism.md) is

$$
\beta=\operatorname{Br}_D:(kG)^D\longrightarrow kC_G(D),
\qquad
\sum_{g\in G}a_gg\longmapsto\sum_{g\in C_G(D)}a_gg.
$$

Thus $\beta$ deletes the coefficients of basis elements outside $C_G(D)$. The nonfixed $D$-orbits have cardinality divisible by $p$, so the usual orbit argument shows that this projection is a unital [ring homomorphism](../../../../../../../ring-homomorphism.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{\beta=\operatorname{Br}_D:(kG)^D\to kC_G(D).}
$$
