↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The divisibility in (i) is the standard dimension test for a projective modular representation. If $P$ is a projective $kG$-module and $S$ is a Sylow p-subgroup of order $p^d$, then $\operatorname{Res}_S^GP$ is projective over $kS$. The [group algebra of a p-group in characteristic p is local](../../../../../../../group-algebra-of-a-p-group-in-characteristic-p-is-local.md), so every finitely generated projective $kS$-module is free. Consequently

$$
\boxed{p^d\mid\dim_kP.}
$$

This will apply to $P=\overline W$ in the implication (v)$\Rightarrow$(i).
