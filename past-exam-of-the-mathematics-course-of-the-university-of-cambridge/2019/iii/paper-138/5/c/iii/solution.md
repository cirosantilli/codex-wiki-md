↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The rows of the [Cartan matrix of a group algebra](../../../../../../../cartan-matrix-of-a-group-algebra.md) express the [projective characters](../../../../../../../projective-character.md) in the simple Brauer-character basis. Thus

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_1&=2\phi_1+\phi_3+\phi_{\overline3},\\
\Phi_3&=\phi_1+3\phi_3+2\phi_{\overline3},\\
\Phi_{\overline3}&=\phi_1+2\phi_3+3\phi_{\overline3},\\
\Phi_8&=\phi_8.
\end{aligned}
$$

Evaluating gives

$$
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|rrrr}
&1&3&7A&7B\\\hline
\Phi_1&8&2&1&1\\
\Phi_3&16&1&\alpha-1&\overline\alpha-1\\
\Phi_{\overline3}&16&1&\overline\alpha-1&\alpha-1\\
\Phi_8&8&-1&1&1
\end{array}.}
$$
