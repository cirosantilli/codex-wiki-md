<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply part (b) with $P=P_i$ and $U=S_j$. A homomorphism $P_i\to S_j$ kills $J(P_i)$ and therefore factors through the head $P_i/J(P_i)\cong S_i$. By [Schur lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) over the splitting field,

$$
\dim\operatorname{Hom}_{kG}(P_i,S_j)=\delta_{ij}.
$$

Consequently the two stated bases satisfy the [Duality of simple and projective Brauer characters](../../../../../../duality-of-simple-and-projective-brauer-characters.md):

$$
\boxed{\langle\chi_{P_i},\chi_{S_j}\rangle=\delta_{ij}.}
$$

The form is nondegenerate directly: if $\phi\ne0$, then

$$
\langle\phi,\phi\rangle
=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\ p\text{-regular}}|\phi(g)|^2>0.
$$

Equivalently, in coordinates indexed by the p-regular conjugacy classes it is a positive diagonal [Hermitian form](../../../../../../hermitian-form.md) with weights $1/|C_G(x)|$.
