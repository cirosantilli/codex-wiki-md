<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $1\leq r\leq n$, let $S_r=k$ with a lower triangular matrix $a=(a_{ij})$ acting by the scalar $a_{rr}$. These are pairwise nonisomorphic [simple modules](../../../../../../../irreducible-module.md), since the diagonal [matrix units](../../../../../../../matrix-unit.md) $e_{ii}$ distinguish them.

Let $N$ be the ideal of strictly lower triangular matrices. It is nilpotent, and

$$
A/N\cong k^n
$$

is a [semisimple algebra](../../../../../../../semisimple-algebra.md). Hence $N$ is the [Jacobson radical](../../../../../../../jacobson-radical.md); explicitly,

$$
\boxed{J(A)=N=\operatorname{span}_k\{e_{rs}:r>s\}.}
$$
