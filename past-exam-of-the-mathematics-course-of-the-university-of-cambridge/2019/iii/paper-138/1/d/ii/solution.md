↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the left regular module, the [radical series of a module](../../../../../../../radical-series-of-a-module.md) is obtained by multiplying by powers of the [Jacobson radical](../../../../../../../jacobson-radical.md):

$$
J^i({}_AA)=J(A)^i=N^i.
$$

Products of [matrix units](../../../../../../../matrix-unit.md) show that

$$
\boxed{N^i=\operatorname{span}_k\{e_{rs}:r-s\geq i\},
\qquad0\leq i\leq n,}
$$

with $N^0=A$ and $N^n=0$. Thus each step removes one subdiagonal, and the [Loewy length](../../../../../../../loewy-length.md) of ${}_AA$ is $n$.
