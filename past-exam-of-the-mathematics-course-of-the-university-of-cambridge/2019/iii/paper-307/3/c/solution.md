<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The quantized fermions obey a [Clifford algebra](../../../../../../clifford-algebra.md), $\{\widehat\psi^a,\widehat\psi^b\}=g^{ab}$. When $M$ is a [spin manifold](../../../../../../spin-manifold.md), they act on the spinor bundle and

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H=L^2(M,S)},
$$

the Hilbert space of square-integrable [spinor fields](../../../../../../spinor-field.md). The supercharge becomes the [Dirac operator](../../../../../../dirac-operator.md), the Hamiltonian is one half of its square, and because $M$ is even dimensional, $(-1)^F$ is the spinor chirality operator.
