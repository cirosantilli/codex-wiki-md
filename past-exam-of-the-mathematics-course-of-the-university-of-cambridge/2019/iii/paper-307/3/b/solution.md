<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substitution of $\delta x^a=\epsilon\psi^a$ and $\delta\psi^a=-i\epsilon\dot x^a$ into the covariant component action leaves a total time derivative; the connection-dependent terms cancel by metric compatibility and the symmetry of the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md). The boundary term vanishes for the stated decay. The [Noether charge](../../../../../../noether-charge.md) is

$$
\boxed{Q=g_{ab}(x)\psi^a\dot x^b},
$$

up to the overall convention inherited from the supersymmetry parameter. Its graded square gives the Hamiltonian, $\{Q,Q\}=2H$ after canonical quantization.
