<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first-order kinetic term makes $\eta^a$ and $\bar\eta_b$ canonically conjugate [Grassmann variables](../../../../../../grassmann-variable.md). Canonical quantization gives the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md)

$$
\boxed{\{\widehat\eta^a,\widehat{\bar\eta}_b\}=\delta^a{}_b,
\qquad\{\widehat\eta^a,\widehat\eta^b\}=0,
\qquad\{\widehat{\bar\eta}_a,\widehat{\bar\eta}_b\}=0.}
$$
