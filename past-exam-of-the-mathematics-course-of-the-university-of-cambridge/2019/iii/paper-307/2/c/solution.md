<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The coherent-state representation of an ordinary fermionic trace identifies the endpoints with a minus sign, producing antiperiodic fields. Inserting [fermion parity](../../../../../../fermion-parity.md) $(-1)^N$ supplies a second minus sign. The resulting [fermionic path integral](../../../../../../fermionic-path-integral.md) therefore has

$$
\boxed{\eta(1)=\eta(0),\qquad\bar\eta(1)=\bar\eta(0)},
$$

and its time-sliced coherent-state action is precisely $S[\eta,\bar\eta]$. This proves the stated supertrace representation.
