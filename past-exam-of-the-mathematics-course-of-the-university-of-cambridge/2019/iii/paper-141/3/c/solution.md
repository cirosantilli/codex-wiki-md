<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose [Seifert longitudes](../../../../../../seifert-longitude.md) $\lambda_i$ disjoint from the small arcs removed in forming the [connected sum of knots](../../../../../../connected-sum-of-knots.md). Join the two resulting longitude arcs through the same connecting tube used for the knots. Their oriented band sum is a zero-linking parallel of $K_1\mathbin\#K_2$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{\lambda_{K_1\#K_2}=\lambda_1\mathbin\#\lambda_2.}
$$
