<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For

$$
r_1=ad^{-1}b,
\qquad r_2=bd^{-1}c,
\qquad r_3=cd^{-1}a,
$$

the [Fox calculus](../../../../../../fox-calculus.md) product rule and $\partial d^{-1}/\partial d=-d^{-1}$ give, in generator order $(a,b,c,d)$,

$$
\left(\phi\frac{\partial r_i}{\partial x_j}\right)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&t^{-1}&0&-t^{-1}\\
0&1&t^{-1}&-t^{-1}\\
t^{-1}&0&1&-t^{-1}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Multiplication of every row by the unit $t$ gives the equivalent [Alexander matrix](../../../../../../alexander-matrix.md)

$$
\boxed{A=
\begin{pmatrix}
t&1&0&-1\\
0&t&1&-1\\
1&0&t&-1
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

For example, deleting the $a$-column gives determinant $-(t^2-t+1)$, the [Alexander polynomial of a knot](../../../../../../alexander-polynomial.md) of the trefoil up to a unit.
