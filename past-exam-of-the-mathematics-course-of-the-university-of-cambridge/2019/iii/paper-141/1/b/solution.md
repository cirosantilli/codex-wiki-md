<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The generators are the alpha-compressing disks, equivalently the four bounded regions. Each beta curve supplies the relator read from its signed intersections with the alpha curves. Since the basepoint region has generator $r_0=1$, the presentation is

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(X_T,x_0)
\cong\langle a,b,c,d\mid ad^{-1}b,\ bd^{-1}c,\ cd^{-1}a\rangle.}
$$

The relations say $d=ba=cb=ac$. Eliminating $c,d$ leaves $bab=aba$, the standard two-generator presentation of the [trefoil knot](../../../../../../trefoil-knot.md) group, which checks the region conventions.
