<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The manifold $X_P$ is obtained from $X_{U,P}$ by filling the $U$-boundary along $\mu_U$. The filling core represents $\iota_*^{\partial X_U}[\lambda_U]$ in $H_1(X_P)$. The [Turaev-torsion Dehn-filling formula](../../../../../../turaev-torsion-dehn-filling-formula.md) therefore gives

$$
\iota_*^{X_{U,P}}\tau(X_{U,P})
=\bigl(1-\iota_*^{\partial X_U}[\lambda_U]\bigr)\tau(X_P).
$$

Since a knot exterior has first Betti number one, its normalized [Turaev torsion](../../../../../../turaev-torsion.md) is

$$
\tau(X_P)=\frac{\Delta(X_P)}{1-\iota_*^{\partial X_P}[\mu_P]}.
$$

Substitution proves

$$
\boxed{
\iota_*^{X_{U,P}}\tau(X_{U,P})
=\frac{\Delta(X_P)\bigl(1-\iota_*^{\partial X_U}[\lambda_U]\bigr)}
{1-\iota_*^{\partial X_P}[\mu_P]}.}
$$
