<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The slope-$4/3$ pattern has winding number $4$, so the [Satellite formula for the Alexander polynomial](../../../../../../satellite-formula-for-the-alexander-polynomial.md) gives

$$
\Delta_{T_{3,2}^{(4,3)}}(t)
\doteq\Delta_{T_{3,2}}(t^4)\Delta_{T_{4,3}}(t).
$$

Using the stated [torus knot](../../../../../../torus-knot.md) formula in each factor,

$$
\boxed{
\Delta_{T_{3,2}^{(4,3)}}(t)
=
\frac{(1-t^4)(1-t^{24})}{(1-t^{12})(1-t^8)}
\frac{(1-t)(1-t^{12})}{(1-t^4)(1-t^3)}.}
$$

Up to a Laurent unit this may be simplified to $(1-t)(1-t^{24})/((1-t^8)(1-t^3))$.
