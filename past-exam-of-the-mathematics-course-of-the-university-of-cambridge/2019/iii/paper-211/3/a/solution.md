<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [local martingale deflator](../../../../../../local-martingale-deflator.md) makes both $YB$ and $YS$ local martingales. Since the filtration is generated by $W$, the martingale representation theorem and the finite-variation drift forced by $YB$ give

$$
dY_t=-r_tY_tdt+\eta_t dW_t
=-Y_t(r_tdt+\lambda_t dW_t)
$$

for a continuous adapted $\lambda$, where $\eta=-Y\lambda$. Applying the [Itô product rule](../../../../../../ito-product-rule.md) to $YS$ gives drift

$$
Y_tS_t(\mu_t-r_t-\lambda_t\sigma_t)dt.
$$

It vanishes exactly when

$$
\boxed{\lambda_t=\frac{\mu_t-r_t}{\sigma_t}.}
$$
