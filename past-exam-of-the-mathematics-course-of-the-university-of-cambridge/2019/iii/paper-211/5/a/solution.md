<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At maturity, $P_T^T=1$. If $P_t^T\leq0$ on an event $A\in\mathcal F_t$ of positive probability, buying one bond on $A$ has nonpositive cost and certain payoff $1$ on $A$ at $T$; any negative purchase cost can also be consumed or retained. This is an [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md). Therefore absence of arbitrage implies

$$
\boxed{P_t^T>0\quad\text{almost surely}.}
$$
