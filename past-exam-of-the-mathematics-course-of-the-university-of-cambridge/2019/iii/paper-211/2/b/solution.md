Here

$$
S^\theta=(1+t)^\theta e^{-t\theta X}.
$$

The [Laplace transform of an exponential distribution](../../../../../../laplace-transform-of-an-exponential-distribution.md) is $\mathbb E[e^{-sX}]=(1+s)^{-1}$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>-1$. Since $\operatorname{Re}(t\theta)\geq0$,

$$
\boxed{M(\theta)=\frac{(1+t)^\theta}{1+t\theta}.}
$$
