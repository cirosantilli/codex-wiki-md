<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The terms independent of $v$ in the substituted PDE satisfy

$$
B'(t)+\frac r2+aA(t)=r,
$$

so

$$
B'(t)=\frac r2-aA(t),
\qquad B(T)=0.
$$

Integrating backward from $T$ yields

$$
\boxed{
B(t)=-\frac12(T-t)r
+a\int_t^TA(s)ds.}
$$

Thus the requested constant is

$$
\boxed{k=a.}
$$
