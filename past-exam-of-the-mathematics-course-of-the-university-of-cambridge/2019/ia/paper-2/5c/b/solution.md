↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [characteristic equation of a linear recurrence](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-linear-recurrence.md) is

$$
r^2-2r+1=(r-1)^2=0.
$$

Its repeated root gives

$$
\boxed{y_n=A+Bn}.
$$

Since $t_n=nh$, this can be written $y_n=A+(B/h)t_n$, exactly the discrete form of the continuous solution $y(t)=C+Dt$ of $y''=0$.
