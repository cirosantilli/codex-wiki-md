<h1 id="9f/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The prior probabilities of choosing the two urns are equal. Their white-ball probabilities are

$$
\mathbb P(W\mid I)=\frac{45}{75}=\frac35,
\qquad
\mathbb P(W\mid II)=\frac{12}{48}=\frac14.
$$

By [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(I\mid W)=\frac{3/5}{3/5+1/4}=\frac{12}{17}}.
$$
