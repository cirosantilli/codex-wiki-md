<h1 id="9f/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The two possible ways to observe one ball of each colour have probabilities

$$
\mathbb P(W_I,B_{II})=\frac35\frac34=\frac9{20},
\qquad
\mathbb P(B_I,W_{II})=\frac25\frac14=\frac2{20}.
$$

Conditioning on their union therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(W_I,B_{II}\mid\text{one white and one black})=\frac9{11}}.
$$
