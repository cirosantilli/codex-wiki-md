<h1 id="8c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Along a trajectory, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{dH}{dt}=2x\dot x+(2y-4y^3)\dot y
=2x(y-2y^3)-(2y-4y^3)x=0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{H=x^2+y^2-y^4}$ is a [first integral](../../../../../../first-integral.md), and every trajectory lies on one of its [level sets](../../../../../../level-set.md).
