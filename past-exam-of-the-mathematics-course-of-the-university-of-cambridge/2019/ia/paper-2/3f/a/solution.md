<h1 id="3f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since the [natural logarithm](../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) is increasing, comparison with an [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) gives

$$
\int_1^n\log x\,dx\leq\sum_{k=1}^n\log k\leq n\log n.
$$

The lower bound is $n\log n-n+1$. Dividing all three expressions by $n\log n$ and applying the [squeeze theorem](../../../../../../squeeze-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\log(n!)\sim n\log n}.
$$
