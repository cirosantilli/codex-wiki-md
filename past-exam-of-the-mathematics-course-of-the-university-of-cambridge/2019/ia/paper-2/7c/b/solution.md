↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Integrating the equation through $t=1$ gives the [jump condition for an impulse](../../../../../../jump-condition-for-an-impulse.md)

$$
T_1(1^+)-T_1(1^-)=-1.
$$

For $1<t<2$ the equation is again homogeneous, so propagation of the post-jump temperature gives

$$
T_1(t)-T_\infty=(\Delta e^{-a}-1)e^{-a(t-1)}
=(\Delta-e^a)e^{-at}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{T_1(t)=T_\infty+(T_0-T_\infty-e^a)e^{-at}}.
$$
