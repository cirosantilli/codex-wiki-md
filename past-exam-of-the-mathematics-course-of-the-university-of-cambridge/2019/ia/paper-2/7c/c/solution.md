<h1 id="7c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $t>2$, the [Heaviside step function](../../../../../../heaviside-step-function.md) contributes a constant unit forcing. Solving the resulting [first-order linear differential equation](../../../../../../first-order-linear-differential-equation.md) from the value at $t=2$ gives

$$
\boxed{T_1(t)=T_\infty+(\Delta-e^a)e^{-at}
+\frac1a\left(1-e^{-a(t-2)}\right)}.
$$
