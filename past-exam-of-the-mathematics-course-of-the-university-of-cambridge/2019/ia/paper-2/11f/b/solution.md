↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $D=R-L$ be right-arm length minus left-arm length. A [linear combination of jointly normal random variables](../../../../../../linear-combination-of-jointly-normal-random-variables.md) is normal, and

$$
\mathbb E D=102-100=2,
$$

while

$$
\operatorname{Var}(D)=2^2+1^2-2\left(\frac12\right)(2)(1)=3.
$$

Thus $D\sim N(2,3)$, and [standardization of a normal random variable](../../../../../../standardization-of-a-normal-random-variable.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(R>L)=\mathbb P(D>0)=\Phi\left(\frac2{\sqrt3}\right)}.
$$
