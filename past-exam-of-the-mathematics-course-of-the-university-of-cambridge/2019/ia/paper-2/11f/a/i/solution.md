<h1 id="11f/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For every $u\in\mathbb R^2$,

$$
u^T(AX)=(A^Tu)^TX.
$$

The right-hand side is a [normal random variable](../../../../../../../gaussian-random-variable.md) by the definition of a [bivariate normal distribution](../../../../../../../bivariate-normal-distribution.md). Therefore every linear combination of the coordinates of $AX$ is normal, so $\boxed{AX\text{ is bivariate normal}}$.
