<h1 id="10a/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Taking the [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) of the quadratic [potential energy](../../../../../../../potential-energy.md) found in part (ii) resolves the linearized force into identical $x$ and $y$ components:

$$
F_x=-\frac{3q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0\rho^3}x,
\qquad
F_y=-\frac{3q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0\rho^3}y.
$$

Each component therefore performs [simple harmonic motion](../../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) with

$$
\omega^2=\frac{3q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0m\rho^3}
=\frac{9\sqrt3}{8\pi}\frac{q^2}{m\epsilon_0a^3}.
$$

Thus the [angular frequency](../../../../../../../angular-frequency.md) is

$$
\boxed{\omega=\frac{3^{5/4}}{\sqrt{8\pi}}\frac{|q|}{\sqrt{m\epsilon_0a^3}},
\qquad A=\frac{3^{5/4}}{\sqrt{8\pi}}}.
$$
