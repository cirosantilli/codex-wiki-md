<h1 id="10a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The three fixed charges are related by rotations through $2\pi/3$ about the origin. Their three [Coulomb's law](../../../../../../../coulomb-s-law.md) forces on a charge placed at the origin have equal magnitudes and directions separated by $2\pi/3$, so their vector sum vanishes. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r=\mathbf0}
$$

is an equilibrium point.
