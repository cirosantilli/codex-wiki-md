<h1 id="9a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The rod imposes a [mechanical constraint](../../../../../../constraint-mechanics.md), and its force is perpendicular to the wrecking ball's instantaneous [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md), so it does no [work](../../../../../../work.md). In the constant parallel [Newtonian gravitational field](../../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-field.md), the ball falls through the vertical distance $l-l\cos\theta_0$. [Conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) between $B$ and $W$ therefore gives

$$
\frac12mv_1^2=mgl(1-\cos\theta_0),
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{v_1=\sqrt{2gl(1-\cos\theta_0)}}.
$$
