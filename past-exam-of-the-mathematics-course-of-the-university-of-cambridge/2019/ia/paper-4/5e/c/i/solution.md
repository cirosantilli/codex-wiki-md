↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** Write the odd integer as $m=2n+1$. The identity from part (b) and $p\mid F_m$ give

$$
F_n^2+F_{n+1}^2\equiv0\pmod p.
$$

Consecutive [Fibonacci numbers](../../../../../../../fibonacci-number.md) are coprime, so $F_{n+1}$ is invertible modulo $p$. Hence

$$
(F_nF_{n+1}^{-1})^2\equiv-1\pmod p.
$$

Thus $-1$ is a [quadratic residue](../../../../../../../quadratic-residue.md) modulo the odd prime $p$, which occurs exactly when $\boxed{p\equiv1\pmod4}$.
