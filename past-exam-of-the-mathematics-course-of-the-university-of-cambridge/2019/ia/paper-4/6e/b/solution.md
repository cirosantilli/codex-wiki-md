<h1 id="6e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The condition $\gcd(a,b,n)=1$ says that the ideal of $\mathbb Z$ generated by $a,b,n$ is all of $\mathbb Z$. The multi-integer [Bezout identity](../../../../../../bezout-identity.md) therefore supplies integers $r,s,t$ such that

$$
\boxed{ra+sb+tn=1}.
$$

Equivalently, first apply Bézout's identity to $a,b$ and then to $\gcd(a,b),n$.
