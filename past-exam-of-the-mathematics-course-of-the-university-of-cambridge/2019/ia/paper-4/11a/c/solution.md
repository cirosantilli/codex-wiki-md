<h1 id="11a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At time $t_0$, the accelerated [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) has [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) $p=qEt_0$ and, by the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md), energy

$$
\mathcal E_p=\sqrt{m^2c^4+q^2E^2t_0^2c^2}.
$$

The incident [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background.md) [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) adds energy $E_\gamma$. Since the final state contains only the [Delta-plus baryon](../../../../../../delta-plus-baryon.md), [conservation of relativistic energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-relativistic-energy.md) gives its laboratory-frame [Lorentz factor](../../../../../../lorentz-factor.md)

$$
\gamma_\Delta=\frac{\mathcal E_p+E_\gamma}{m_\Delta c^2}.
$$

Its rest-frame decay time $t_\Delta$ is a [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md), so [time dilation](../../../../../../time-dilation.md) gives the laboratory decay time

$$
\boxed{t_{\rm lab}=\frac{t_\Delta}{m_\Delta c^2}\left(\sqrt{m^2c^4+q^2E^2t_0^2c^2}+E_\gamma\right)}.
$$
