<h1 id="12a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\{\mathbf e_i(t)\}$ be an orthonormal [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) fixed in $S'$ and write $\mathbf a=a_i\mathbf e_i$. A basis vector rotating with [angular velocity](../../../../../../angular-velocity.md) $\boldsymbol\omega$ satisfies

$$
\left(\dot{\mathbf e}_i\right)_S=\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf e_i.
$$

Differentiating the components and the basis vectors in $S$ therefore gives the [rotating-frame derivative formula](../../../../../../rotating-frame-derivative-formula.md)

$$
\boxed{(\dot{\mathbf a})_S
=\dot a_i\mathbf e_i+a_i\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf e_i
=(\dot{\mathbf a})_{S'}+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf a.}
$$
