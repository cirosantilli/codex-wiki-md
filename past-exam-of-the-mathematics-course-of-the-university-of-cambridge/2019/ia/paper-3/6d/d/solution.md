A three-cycle is an [even permutation](../../../../../../even-permutation.md), as is a product of two disjoint three-cycles. Thus every element counted in part (c) lies in $A_7$, and every order-three element of $A_7$ was already counted there. The answer remains $\boxed{350}$.
