<h1 id="8a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Direct multiplication and the identities $\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta=1$ and $\cosh^2\theta-\sinh^2\theta=1$ give

$$
\boxed{RR^T=I},
\qquad
\boxed{SJS=J},
\qquad
J=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Thus $R$ is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), while $S$ preserves the indefinite [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form.md) $x_1^2-x_2^2$.**
