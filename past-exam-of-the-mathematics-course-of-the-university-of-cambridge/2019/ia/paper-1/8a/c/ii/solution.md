<h1 id="8a/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The matrix $B$ fixes the second coordinate and satisfies the hyperbolic-generator relation on the first and third coordinates. Therefore

$$
\boxed{e^{xB}=\begin{pmatrix}
\cosh x&0&\sinh x\\
0&1&0\\
\sinh x&0&\cosh x
\end{pmatrix}}.
$$
