<h1 id="4f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The inequality $(1+1/n)^n\leq e$ gives, for $|x|<1$,

$$
\sum_{n=1}^\infty |x|^n\left(1+\frac1n\right)^n
\leq e\sum_{n=1}^\infty|x|^n<\infty.
$$

For $|x|>1$, the terms have magnitude at least $|x|^n$ and therefore do not tend to zero, so the series diverges by the [term test for divergence](../../../../../../term-test-for-divergence.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{R=1}.
$$
