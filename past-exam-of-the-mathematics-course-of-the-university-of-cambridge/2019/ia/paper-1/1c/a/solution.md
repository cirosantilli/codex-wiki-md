<h1 id="1c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The powers of the [imaginary unit](../../../../../../imaginary-unit.md) have period four. Hence

$$
\sum_{a=0}^{200}i^a
=50(1+i-1-i)+i^{200}=1,
$$

while

$$
\prod_{b=1}^{50}i^b=i^{1+2+\cdots+50}=i^{1275}=i^3=-i.
$$

Thus $x+iy=1-i$, so

$$
\boxed{xy=-1}.
$$
