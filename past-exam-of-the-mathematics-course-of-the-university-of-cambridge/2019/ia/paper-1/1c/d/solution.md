<h1 id="1c/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write $z=re^{i\theta}$ with $r>0$. On a fixed [branch of the complex logarithm](../../../../../../branch-of-the-complex-logarithm.md) on which $\log z=\log r+i\theta$ and $\log\bar z=\log r-i\theta$, the equation becomes

$$
\log r+i\theta=i(\log r-i\theta)=\theta+i\log r.
$$

Its real and imaginary parts give the same condition $\log r=\theta$, or

$$
\boxed{r=e^\theta}.
$$

This is a [logarithmic spiral](../../../../../../logarithmic-spiral.md). For the [principal complex logarithm](../../../../../../principal-complex-logarithm.md), it is the portion parametrized by $-\pi<\theta<\pi$ that avoids the branch cut.
