<h1 id="1c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the [principal complex logarithm](../../../../../../principal-complex-logarithm.md), for which $\operatorname{Log}i=i\pi/2$, and set

$$
a=\frac{2\log2}{\pi},
\qquad
\boxed{z=-1-\frac{2i}{\pi}\log a}.
$$

Then the definition of [complex exponentiation](../../../../../../complex-exponentiation.md) gives

$$
i^z=e^{z\operatorname{Log}i}
=e^{\log a-i\pi/2}=-ia,
$$

and therefore

$$
i^{,i^z}=e^{(-ia)i\pi/2}=e^{a\pi/2}=e^{\log2}=2.
$$

Other choices of [branch of the complex logarithm](../../../../../../branch-of-the-complex-logarithm.md) produce further valid answers.
