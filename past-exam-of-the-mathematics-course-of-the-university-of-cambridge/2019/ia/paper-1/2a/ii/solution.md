<h1 id="2a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The scalar multiplication $\lambda A$ multiplies every one of the $n$ rows by $\lambda$. Applying [multilinearity of the determinant](../../../../../../multilinearity-of-the-determinant.md) once to each row gives

$$
\boxed{\det(\lambda A)=\lambda^n\det A}.
$$
