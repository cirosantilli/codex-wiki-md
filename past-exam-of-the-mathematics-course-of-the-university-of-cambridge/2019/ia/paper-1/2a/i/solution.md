The [Leibniz formula for determinants](../../../../../../leibniz-formula-for-determinants.md) is

$$
\boxed{\det A=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)
\prod_{r=1}^n A_{r,\sigma(r)}}.
$$

Equivalently, the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is the unique alternating function of the rows that is [multilinear](../../../../../../multilinearity-of-the-determinant.md) and takes value one on the [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md). Multiplying one row by $\lambda$ therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\det B=\lambda\det A}.
$$
