The [Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem](../../../../../../bolzano-weierstrass-theorem.md) states that every [bounded sequence](../../../../../../bounded-sequence.md) of real numbers has a [convergent subsequence](../../../../../../convergent-subsequence.md).

Condition (i) is insufficient because the allowed limit may be zero. For example, $a_n=1/n$ is nonzero and converges to zero, whereas $1/a_n=n$ does not converge in $\mathbb R$.
