<h1 id="10e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$, [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) on $(a,b)$, and $f(a)=f(b)$, then some $c\in(a,b)$ has $f\prime(c)=0$. A maximum or minimum is attained; unless $f$ is constant, one extremum lies inside, where the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) vanishes.
