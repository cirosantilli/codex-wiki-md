<h1 id="7b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

From $Hv=\mu v$, $v^\dagger Hv=\mu\|v\|^2$ is real, so $\mu\in\mathbb R$. For distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), $\mu_i v_i^\dagger v_j=v_i^\dagger Hv_j=\mu_jv_i^\dagger v_j$, hence orthogonality. Normalising the [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) and taking them as the rows of $U$ gives $H=U^\dagger\Lambda U$.
