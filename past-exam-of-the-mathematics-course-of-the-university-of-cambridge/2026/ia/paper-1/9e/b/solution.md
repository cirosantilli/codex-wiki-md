<h1 id="9e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $f$ has $n+1$ [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) near $x_0$, then $f(x)=\sum_{k=0}^nf^{(k)}(x_0)(x-x_0)^k/k!+f^{(n+1)}(\xi)(x-x_0)^{n+1}/(n+1)!$ for some intermediate $\xi$.
