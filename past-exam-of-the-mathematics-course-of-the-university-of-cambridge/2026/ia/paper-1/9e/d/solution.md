↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

$e^x$ is real analytic because $e^{x_0+h}=e^{x_0}\sum_{n\ge0}h^n/n!$; it is not a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) since all its [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) are nonzero.
