<h1 id="5a/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The centre-to-plane distance is $|g-2|/\sqrt2$. Intersection occurs iff this is at most $2\sqrt2$, i.e. $-2\le g\le6$. Equality gives one tangent point; strict inequality gives a circle of radius $\sqrt{8-(g-2)^2/2}$.
