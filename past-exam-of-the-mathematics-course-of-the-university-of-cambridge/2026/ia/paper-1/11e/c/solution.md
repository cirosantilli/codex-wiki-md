<h1 id="11e/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $h=f_1-f_2$, $h\prime=\sqrt{1+x}(f_1+f_2)h$ and $h(0)=0$. On every compact subinterval the coefficient is continuous, so [Gronwall inequality](../../../../../../gronwall-inequality.md) gives $h=0$; hence equality holds throughout $[0,a)$.
