Let $P$ have the old domain [basis](../../../../../basis.md) as columns, $C$ the old codomain [basis](../../../../../basis.md), and $D$ the new codomain [basis](../../../../../basis.md). Change of coordinates gives $A\prime=D^{-1}CAP^{-1}$, hence

$$
A\prime=\begin{pmatrix}1&4&3\\0&-3&-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$
