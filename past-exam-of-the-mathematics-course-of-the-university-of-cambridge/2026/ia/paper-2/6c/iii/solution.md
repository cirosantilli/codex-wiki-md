Writing $\xi=x-1$, $\eta=y$, the [linearization of a dynamical system](../../../../../../linearization-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is $\dot\xi=-6\xi$, $\dot\eta=-4\eta$. Eliminating $t$ gives $\eta=C\xi^{2/3}$.
