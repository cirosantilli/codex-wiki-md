$dU/dt=\nabla U\cdot(-\nabla U)=-|\nabla U|^2\le0$. The descending [gradient flow](../../../../../../gradient-flow.md) trajectory from $(2,1)$ stays in its bounded contour basin and tends to the only [critical point](../../../../../../critical-point.md) there, $(1,0)$.
