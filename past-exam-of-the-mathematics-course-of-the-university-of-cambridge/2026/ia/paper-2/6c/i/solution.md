<h1 id="6c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

$\nabla U=(3x^2-3+y^2,2y(x+1))$, so the [critical points](../../../../../../critical-point.md) are $(\pm1,0)$. The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) at $(1,0)$ is $\operatorname{diag}(6,4)$, making it the [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md); $(-1,0)$ is degenerate and not a minimum.
