[Differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) under the [integral](../../../../../integral.md) gives $I\prime\prime=\int_0^1y^4e^{xy}dy$. Integrating the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of $e^{xy}(xy^3-3y^2)$ and then once more yields $x^2\int_0^1y^4e^{xy}dy-12\int_0^1y^2e^{xy}dy=e^x(x-4)$.
