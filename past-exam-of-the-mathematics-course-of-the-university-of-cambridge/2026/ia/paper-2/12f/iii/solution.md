↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The extinction probability is the smallest fixed point of the convex generating [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $F_1$ in $[0,1]$. Since $F_1\prime(1)=\mu\le1$ and $p_0,p_2\gt 0$ make strict convexity relevant, the only fixed point is $1$.
