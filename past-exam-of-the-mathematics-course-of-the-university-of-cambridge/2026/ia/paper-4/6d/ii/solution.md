<h1 id="6d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $x^2\equiv a$ and $y^2\equiv a\pmod p$, then $(x-y)(x+y)\equiv0$, hence $y\equiv\pm x$; these differ for odd $p$. It fails for composite [moduli](../../../../../../modulus.md): $1$ has four square roots modulo $8$.
