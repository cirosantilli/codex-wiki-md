<h1 id="6d/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

By Fermat, $a^{p-1}=1$. Thus $a^{(p-1)/2}=\pm1$. The squaring map on $\mathbb F_p^*$ has kernel $\{\pm1\}$, so exactly $(p-1)/2$ inputs are squares; the [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $x^{(p-1)/2}-1$ has at most that many roots and already has all squares, proving the two cases.
