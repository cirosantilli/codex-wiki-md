↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

$L^2=J^2-(q\beta)^2=J^2\sin^2\alpha$. Splitting [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) into radial and angular parts therefore gives $E=\tfrac12m\dot r^2+J^2\sin^2\alpha/(2mr^2)+V(r)$, proving the stated [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md).
