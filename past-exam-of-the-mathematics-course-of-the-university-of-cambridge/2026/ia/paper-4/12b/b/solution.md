<h1 id="12b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let the incident positron total energy be $E$. For perpendicular photon [momenta](../../../../../../momentum.md), real positive photon energies require  
$(E+m_ec^2)^2-4m_ec^2(E+m_ec^2)\ge0$.  
**Thus the threshold is $E=3m_ec^2$.**
