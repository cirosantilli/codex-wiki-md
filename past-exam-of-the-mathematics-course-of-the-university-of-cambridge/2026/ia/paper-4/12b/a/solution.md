<h1 id="12b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Two-body energy-momentum conservation in the pion rest frame gives $E_e=(m_\pi^2+m_e^2)c^2/(2m_\pi)$ and $p_e=(m_\pi^2-m_e^2)c/(2m_\pi)$. Thus $v_e=c(m_\pi^2-m_e^2)/(m_\pi^2+m_e^2)$.
