↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

$X_1$ is the set of real algebraic numbers and is countable because each nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has finitely many roots. For $n\ge2$, $X_n$ is uncountable: the [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $x_1$ vanishes on $\{0\}\times\mathbb R^{n-1}$.
