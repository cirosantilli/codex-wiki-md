↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On a simply connected region, $\nabla\times E=0$ gives $E=-\nabla\phi$. Then [Gauss's law](../../../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives $\nabla^2\phi=-\rho/\varepsilon_0$.
