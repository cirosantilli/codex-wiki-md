↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The Möbius [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) consists of maps $z\mapsto(az+b)/(cz+d)$ with $ad-bc\ne0$, including the usual action on $\infty$. [Scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md) [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) act trivially and are the whole kernel, so $M\cong GL_2(\mathbb C)/(\mathbb C^*I)$.
