↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On $C$, $dx=a e_\phi d\phi$, so $I=\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi=2\pi$. Locally $f=\phi$, but no single-valued global potential exists on the punctured domain; this is why irrotationality does not force this closed [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md) to vanish.
