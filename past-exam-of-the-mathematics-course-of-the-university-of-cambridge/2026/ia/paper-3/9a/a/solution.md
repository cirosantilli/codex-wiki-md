Using $\varepsilon_{ijk}\varepsilon_{klm}=\delta_{il}\delta_{jm}-\delta_{im}\delta_{jl}$,  
$[\nabla\times(F\times G)]_i=\partial_j(F_iG_j)-\partial_j(F_jG_i)$; expanding the [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) gives the stated four terms.
