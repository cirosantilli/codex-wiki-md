<h1 id="4a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4A](../4a.md)

[Divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) says $\int_V\nabla\cdot F\,dV=\int_{\partial V}F\cdot n\,dS$ for the outward normal. Here $\nabla\cdot(xe^{-r^2})=(3-2r^2)e^{-r^2}$, so radial integration gives $4\pi a^3e^{-a^2}$. On the sphere $F\cdot n=ae^{-a^2}$, whose [integral](../../../../../integral.md) is the same $4\pi a^3e^{-a^2}$.
