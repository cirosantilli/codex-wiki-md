<h1 id="3g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3G](../3g.md)

The argument principle says $(2\pi i)^{-1}\int_\gamma f\prime/f$ equals zeros minus poles. Applying it to the homotopy $f+tg$ proves Rouché when $|g|\lt |f|$ on the boundary. On $|z|=1$, $15z$ dominates $z^6+1$, so there is one zero inside; on $|z|=2$, $z^6$ dominates $15z+1$, so there are six. Hence the annulus contains five. For the [limit](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) theorem, if nonconstant $f$ took the same value at two points, use disjoint small circles and Rouché on $f_n-f_n(a)$ to force a second preimage, contradicting injectivity.
