The transition [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is symmetric with stationary projector $J/m$ and one other [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda=1-\alpha m/(m-1)$. Therefore, starting at BBC1, the probability of BBC2 after $60$ steps is $[1-\lambda^{60}]/m$.
