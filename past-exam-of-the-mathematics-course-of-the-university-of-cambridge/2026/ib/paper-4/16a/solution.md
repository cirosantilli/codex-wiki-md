<h1 id="16a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [16A](../16a.md)

Vertical hydrostatic balance gives $p=\rho g(h-z)$; depth-independent horizontal [momentum](../../../../../momentum.md) and depth-integrated incompressibility yield the two shallow-water equations. Curling [momentum](../../../../../momentum.md) and combining with mass conservation gives $D[(\zeta+f)/h]/Dt=0$. Dot [momentum](../../../../../momentum.md) with $\rho h u$ and use continuity to obtain $E_t+\nabla\cdot J=0$ with $E=\rho h|u|^2/2+\rho gh^2/2$ and $J=u(E+\rho gh^2/2)$. It is kinetic plus gravitational [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md). A no-normal-flow boundary kills the flux and conserves its [integral](../../../../../integral.md); a localized nonuniform height has excess positive energy relative to the uniform rest state with the same mass, so it cannot decay to that state without dissipation.
