<h1 id="5d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5D](../5d.md)

The dispersion relation is $\omega=ck$. At a perfect conductor the tangential [electric field](../../../../../electric-field.md) and normal [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field.md) vanish. Thus $E_{ref}=-E_0\hat x e^{i(-kz+\omega t)}$ and $B_{ref}=-E_0\hat y e^{i(-kz+\omega t)}/c$. At the surface the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field.md) is $-2E_0\hat y/c$, so with outward normal $\hat z$ the surface current is $K=2E_0\hat x/(\mu_0c)$ (real parts understood).
