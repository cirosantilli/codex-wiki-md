<h1 id="10f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10F](../10f.md)

Bounded means finite diameter; sequential compactness means every [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) has a convergent subsequence. A failure of uniform continuity supplies two close [sequences](../../../../../sequence.md) whose image distances stay apart; a convergent subsequence contradicts continuity. Into a discrete metric, continuity is local constancy, and compactness upgrades the radii uniformly. The Cantor set is closed in compact $[0,1]$, hence sequentially compact. Uniform local constancy gives a positive separation scale, so finitely many initial Cantor-code (binary) digits determine $f$. Removing zero destroys compactness: the hinted [function](../../../../../function-split.md) reading the digit after the first $1$ is continuous at every remaining [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) but depends on arbitrarily late digits.
