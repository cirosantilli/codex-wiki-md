<h1 id="6c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6C](../6c.md)

A [Givens rotation](../../../../../givens-rotation.md) is identity except for a $2\times2$ block $\begin{pmatrix}c&s\\-s&c\end{pmatrix}$ in rows $p,q$. Choose $p=j,q=i$ and $c=a_{jj}/r$, $s=a_{ij}/r$, $r=(a_{jj}^2+a_{ij}^2)^{1/2}$, with sign adjusted to the block convention; this mixes only those rows and zeros $a_{ij}$. For $3\times3$, successively apply rotations $(1,2)$, $(1,3)$, $(2,3)$, producing patterns $***;0**;***$, then $***;0**;0**$, then an upper triangular $R$. The product of transposed rotations is $Q$.
