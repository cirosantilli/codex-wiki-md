<h1 id="15d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [15D](../15d.md)

With $P^\mu=mU^\mu$, $U^\mu=dx^\mu/d\tau$, and $F^{\mu\nu}$ the field tensor, separating temporal and spatial components yields the [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) and power equations. The invariants are $E\cdot B$ and $E^2-c^2B^2$. For perpendicular fields with $E\lt cB$, boost with $v=E\times B/B^2$ (here $-E\hat y/B$); then $E\prime=0$ and $B\prime=\sqrt{B^2-E^2/c^2}\,\hat z$. Perpendicular motion is circular with $\omega=|q|B\prime/(\gamma m)$ and radius $p/(|q|B\prime)$. Transforming back adds the frame [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), giving the nonrelativistic $E\times B/B^2$ drift, independent of $m,q$; a parallel [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) would additionally produce a helix.
