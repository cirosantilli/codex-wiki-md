<h1 id="3c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3C](../3c.md)

Integrate $(1+z^p)^{-1}$ around the wedge $0\le\arg z\le2\pi/p$. The radial [integrals](../../../../../integral.md) differ by the factor $e^{2\pi i/p}$, the arc vanishes, and the wedge contains the simple pole $e^{i\pi/p}$ with residue $-[p e^{-i\pi/p}]^{-1}$. Solving the resulting identity gives $I=(\pi/p)\csc(\pi/p)$.
