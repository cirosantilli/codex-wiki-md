<h1 id="7a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7A](../7a.md)

Unsteady Bernoulli gives the surface [pressure](../../../../../pressure.md) from $p+\rho\partial_t\phi+\rho|\nabla\phi|^2/2=C(t)$. The velocity-squared contribution integrates to zero net force, while the unsteady term gives $F=-m_a\dot V$ with [added mass](../../../../../added-mass.md) $m_a=\tfrac12(4\pi\rho a^3/3)=2\pi\rho a^3/3$. Hence $(m+m_a)a=G$, so $m^*=m+2\pi\rho a^3/3$: accelerating the bubble also accelerates surrounding fluid.
