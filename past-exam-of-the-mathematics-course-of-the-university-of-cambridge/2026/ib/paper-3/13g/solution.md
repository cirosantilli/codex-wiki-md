<h1 id="13g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [13G](../13g.md)

Expanding the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) [limit](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) along real and imaginary increments gives $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$. Since $\operatorname{Re}(ze^z)=e^x(x\cos y-y\sin y)$, the required [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is $f(z)=ze^z$, and $f(0)=0$. Two such [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) differ by a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) with zero real part; the open mapping theorem makes it constant, and the value at zero makes that constant zero.
