<h1 id="12f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [12F](../12f.md)

A base covers the space and refines intersections. On $X^k$, products of opens form a base. The weighted metric satisfies the metric axioms and has exactly this topology; convergence is coordinatewise. For $X^{\mathbb N}$ the metric $d(f,g)=\sum_{n\ge1}2^{-n}\min\{1,\rho(f(n),g(n))\}$ induces the stated [product topology](../../../../../product-topology.md): finitely many coordinates control a basic neighbourhood and the tail is uniformly small. Thus [sequences](../../../../../sequence.md) converge exactly coordinatewise.
