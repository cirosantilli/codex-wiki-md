<h1 id="1e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1E](../1e.md)

$A=\mathbb Z^2/\langle(6,9)\rangle$. The class of $(2,3)$ has order $3$, so $A$ has torsion and is not free. A unimodular change of [basis](../../../../../basis.md) ([Smith normal form](../../../../../smith-normal-form.md), using $\gcd(6,9)=3$) gives $A\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z$.
