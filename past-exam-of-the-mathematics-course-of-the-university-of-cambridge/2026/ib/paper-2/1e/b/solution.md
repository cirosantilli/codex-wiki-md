<h1 id="1e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At the maximal [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) $\mathfrak m=(2,X)$, the images of $4,2X,X^2$ form a three-dimensional [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of $I/\mathfrak mI$ over $\mathbb F_2$, so two generators are impossible. More generally $(2^{n-1},2^{n-2}X,\ldots,2X^{n-2},X^{n-1})$ needs exactly $n$ generators by the same argument.
