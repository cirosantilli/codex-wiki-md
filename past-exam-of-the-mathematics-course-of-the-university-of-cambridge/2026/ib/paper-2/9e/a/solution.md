<h1 id="9e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Sylow I gives a [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of order equal to the full $p$-part of $|G|$. Sylow II says every $p$-subgroup lies in a conjugate of any Sylow $p$-subgroup; act by the [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) on the Sylow cosets and use a fixed orbit because the number of cosets is prime to $p$.
