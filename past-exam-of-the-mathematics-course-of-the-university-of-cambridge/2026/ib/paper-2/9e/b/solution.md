<h1 id="9e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let an order-$p^{d-1}$ [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ act on $G/H$. Since the number of cosets is divisible by $p$, orbit counting and Cauchy produce a fixed coset with stabiliser extending $H$ by index $p$. Induction yields [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of every $p$-power order. Applying the same orbit count to the action of $H$ on $G/H$ leaves fixed cosets indexed by $N(H)/H$, giving $|G:H|\equiv|N(H):H|\pmod p$.
