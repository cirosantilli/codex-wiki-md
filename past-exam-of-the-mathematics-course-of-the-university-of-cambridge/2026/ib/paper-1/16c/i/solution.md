<h1 id="16c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For fixed $x$ add $K\Pi(t)$ to $p(t)-f(t)$ so the resulting [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) also vanishes at $t=x$. Repeated Rolle gives a point $\xi$ where its $(n+1)$st [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) vanishes, yielding the formula.
