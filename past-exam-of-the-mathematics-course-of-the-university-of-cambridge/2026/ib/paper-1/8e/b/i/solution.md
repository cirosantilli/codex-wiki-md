↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

$D$ lowers total degree, so it is nilpotent and has only [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) zero. Solving $(\partial_z+\partial_w)f=0$ gives $f=h(z-w)$; the degree restrictions give the eigenspace $\operatorname{span}\{1,z-w,(z-w)^2\}$.
