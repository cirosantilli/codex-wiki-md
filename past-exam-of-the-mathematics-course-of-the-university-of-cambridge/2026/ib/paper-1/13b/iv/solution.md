<h1 id="13b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Only odd [stationary states](../../../../../../stationary-state.md) contribute, and for $j=2n+1$ their squared coefficients are $128/[\pi^2(j^2-16)^2]$. Since $E_{av}/E_1=16$, substitution gives

$$
\pi^2=8\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{(2n+1)^2}{[(2n+1)^2-16]^2},
$$

so $B=8$.
