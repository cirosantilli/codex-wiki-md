<h1 id="11/11-1g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Apply the maximum [modulus](../../../../../../../modulus.md) principle to $f(z)/z$, with its removable value $f\prime(0)$, to obtain $|f(z)|\le|z|$. Equality at a nonzero interior point makes $f(z)/z$ constant, so $f(z)=az$ with $|a|=1$.
