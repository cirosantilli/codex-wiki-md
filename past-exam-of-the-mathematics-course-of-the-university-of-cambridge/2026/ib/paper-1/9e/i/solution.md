<h1 id="9e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $a^m=b^n=0$, then every term in $(a+b)^{m+n-1}$ vanishes; also $(ra)^m=0$. Thus the nilpotents form an [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) $N$. The quotient $R/N$ has no nonzero nilpotents.
