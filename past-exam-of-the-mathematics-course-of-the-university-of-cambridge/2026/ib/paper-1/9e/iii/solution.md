<h1 id="9e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $b^k=0$, then $(u+b)^{-1}=u^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}(-bu^{-1})^j$. For the [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md), its positive-degree part is nilpotent by (ii), so adding it to the unit $a_0$ gives a unit.
