<h1 id="10g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For each $a\gt 1$, $n^{-x}\le n^{-a}$ on $[a,\infty)$ and the p-series converges. The M-test gives locally [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) there, so the sum $\zeta$ is continuous on $(1,\infty)$.
