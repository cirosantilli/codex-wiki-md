↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) for $S$ converges uniformly by the M-test. Therefore [termwise integration](../../../../../../../termwise-integration.md) is valid and every term integrates to $\sin(n^3\pi)/n^7=0$, so the [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md) is zero.
