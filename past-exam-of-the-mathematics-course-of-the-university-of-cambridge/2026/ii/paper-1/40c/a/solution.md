<h1 id="40c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a [velocity potential](../../../../../../velocity-potential.md) proportional to $e^{ik(x-Vt)+ik\beta_1y}$, where  
$\beta_1=\sqrt{(V/c_1)^2-1}$. The wall condition gives amplitude $-V\epsilon/\beta_1$. With $p'=-\rho_1\partial_t\phi$, [acoustic energy flux](../../../../../../acoustic-energy-flux.md) gives

$$
\langle I_y\rangle=\frac{\rho_1k^2V^3\epsilon^2}{2\beta_1}.
$$

For $V<c_1$ the vertical wave number is imaginary: the disturbance is evanescent and carries no mean vertical energy flux.
