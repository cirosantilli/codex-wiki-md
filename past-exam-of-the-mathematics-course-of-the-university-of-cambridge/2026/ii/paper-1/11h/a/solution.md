<h1 id="11h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The parity-check [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) has rank $3$, so its kernel has dimension $7-3=4$ and size $16$; see [linear code](../../../../../../linear-code.md). Its columns are precisely the seven distinct nonzero [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) of $\mathbb F_2^3$. Thus no weight-one or weight-two word is in the kernel, while three dependent columns give a weight-three word. Hence $d=3$.
