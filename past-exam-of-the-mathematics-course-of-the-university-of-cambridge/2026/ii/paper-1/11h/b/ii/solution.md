↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write the three blocks as $u=a+x$, $v=b+x$, $w=a+b+x$. Over $\mathbb F_2$,

$$
x=u+v+w,\qquad a=u+x,\qquad b=v+x.
$$

**Thus the parameters are unique and $|G|=|H'|^2|K'|=16^3=2^{12}$.**
