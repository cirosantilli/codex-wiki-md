↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By [parity extension](../../../../../../../parity-extension.md), each code keeps size $16$, gains length $8$, and has distance $4$. Extension is injective. A common extended word punctures to a word in $H\cap K$, so only the zero and all-one words occur; both extend to $00000000$ and $11111111$.
