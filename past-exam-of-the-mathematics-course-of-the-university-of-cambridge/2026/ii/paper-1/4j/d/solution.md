<h1 id="4j/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Assume a complete list $f$ were primitive recursive. Closure under diagonal composition and successor makes

$$
d(k)=f(k,k)+1
$$

primitive recursive. Completeness gives a row $n$ with $f(n,k)=d(k)$ for every $k$, but at $k=n$ this says $f(n,n)=f(n,n)+1$. This is the [diagonal argument](../../../../../../diagonal-argument.md).
