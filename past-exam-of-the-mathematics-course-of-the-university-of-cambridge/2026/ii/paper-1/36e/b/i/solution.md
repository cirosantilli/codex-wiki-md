↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [ideal gas free energy](../../../../../../../ideal-gas-free-energy.md) contributes $-Nk_BT\log V$. With $V=4\pi r^3/3$ and surface energy $4\pi tr^2$, the radius-dependent free energy is therefore

$$
F(r)=-Nk_BT\log V+4\pi tr^2+\text{constant}.
$$

For equilibrium against external [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) $P_0$, minimize $F+P_0V$.
