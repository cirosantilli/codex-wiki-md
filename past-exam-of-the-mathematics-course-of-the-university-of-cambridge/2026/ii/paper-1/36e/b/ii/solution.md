[Differentiation](../../../../../../../differentiation.md) gives

$$
-\frac{3Nk_BT}{r}+8\pi tr+4\pi P_0r^2=0,
$$

equivalently the Laplace-pressure condition

$$
P_{\rm in}=\frac{Nk_BT}{V}=P_0+\frac{2t}{r}.
$$
