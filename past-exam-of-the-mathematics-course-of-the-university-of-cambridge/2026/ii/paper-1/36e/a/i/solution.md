<h1 id="36e/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At equal [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) and [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md), [ideal](../../../../../../../ideal.md) gases have equal molecular number density. The mean air molecular mass is $.8(28)+.2(32)=28.8$, while helium has mass $4$, so

$$
\boxed{\rho_{\rm He}/\rho_{\rm air}=4/28.8=5/36.}
$$
