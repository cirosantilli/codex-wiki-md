↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [power method](../../../../../../power-method.md) repeatedly normalizes $Aq^{(k)}$ and estimates the [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) with the Rayleigh quotient $(q^{(k)})^TAq^{(k)}$. It converges when the [dominant eigenvalue](../../../../../../dominant-eigenvalue.md) is unique in [modulus](../../../../../../modulus.md) and the initial [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) has a nonzero component in its eigendirection.
