<h1 id="41b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [real spectral theorem](../../../../../../real-spectral-theorem.md) gives real [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and orthogonal distinct eigenspaces. Constructively, a [Householder reflection](../../../../../../householder-transformation.md) sends an [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) to $e_1$; symmetry makes the remaining block symmetric. Induction diagonalizes that block, producing orthogonal $Q$ with $Q^TAQ=\Lambda$. Multiplying by $Q$ gives $AQe_k=\lambda_kQe_k$.
