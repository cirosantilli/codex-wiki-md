↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $g\in L^2$ is orthogonal to every translate of $f$, then the correlation $g*\widetilde f$ vanishes. By [convolution theorem](../../../../../../../convolution-theorem.md),  
$\widehat g\,\overline{\widehat f}=0$ almost everywhere. Since $\widehat f$ vanishes only at one point, a null set, $\widehat g=0$ almost everywhere. By [Plancherel theorem](../../../../../../../plancherel-theorem.md), $g=0$. Orthogonal-complement characterization proves density in $L^2$.
