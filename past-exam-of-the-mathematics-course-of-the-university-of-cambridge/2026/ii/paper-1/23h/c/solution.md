<h1 id="23h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Approximate $g\in L^2$ by Schwartz $g_n$. Young's inequality gives  
$\|f*(g_n-g)\|_2\le\|f\|_1\|g_n-g\|_2$, while multiplication by bounded $\widehat f$ is continuous in $L^2$. Pass to the [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) in  
$\widehat{f*g_n}=\widehat f\,\widehat g_n$.
