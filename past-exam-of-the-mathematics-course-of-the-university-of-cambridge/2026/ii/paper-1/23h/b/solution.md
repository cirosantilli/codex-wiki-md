<h1 id="23h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For Schwartz $f,g$, Fourier inversion and Fubini give

$$
\int\widehat f(\xi)\overline{\widehat g(\xi)}\,d\xi
=\int f(x)\overline{g(x)}\,dx.
$$

Taking $g=f$ proves [Plancherel theorem](../../../../../../plancherel-theorem.md); polarization gives the full identity.
