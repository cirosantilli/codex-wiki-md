A type-2 rule is $A\to\alpha$ with one nonterminal $A$ on the left; a type-2 grammar and its language are [context-free language](../../../../../../context-free-language.md)s. The required repetition criterion is the [pumping lemma for context-free languages](../../../../../../pumping-lemma-for-context-free-languages.md).
