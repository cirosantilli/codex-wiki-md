↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

It is not context-free. Intersecting it with the regular language $a^*b^*c^*$ would give $\{a^nb^nc^n:n\ge0\}$, which violates the [pumping lemma for context-free languages](../../../../../../../pumping-lemma-for-context-free-languages.md). Context-free languages are closed under intersection with regular languages.
