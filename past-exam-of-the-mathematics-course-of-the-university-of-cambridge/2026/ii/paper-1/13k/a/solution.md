↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For one observation,

$$
P_\lambda(Y=y)=\frac1{y!}\exp\{y\log\lambda-\lambda\}.
$$

**Thus this is an [exponential family](../../../../../../exponential-family-split.md) with natural parameter $\theta=\log\lambda$, statistic $T(y)=y$, base measure $1/y!$, and cumulant $A(\theta)=e^\theta$.**
