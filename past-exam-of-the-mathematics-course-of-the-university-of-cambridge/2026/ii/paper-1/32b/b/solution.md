<h1 id="32b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The fixed points are $(0,0)$ and $(\pm|\mu|,\mu^2)$. At the origin the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $\mu^2$ and $-1$, so it is a saddle. At either nonzero point the characteristic [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is

$$
\lambda^2+\lambda+2\mu^2.
$$

They are stable nodes for $0<|\mu|<1/(2\sqrt2)$, degenerate nodes at equality, and stable spirals above it.

The stable manifold of the origin is exactly $x=0$. Write the [stable manifold](../../../../../../stable-manifold.md) in the unstable direction as $y=ax^2+bx^4+O(x^6)$. Graph invariance gives

$$
a=\frac1{1+2\mu^2},\qquad
b=\frac{2a^2}{1+4\mu^2}.
$$

Thus $W^u$ has

$$
\boxed{y=\frac{x^2}{1+2\mu^2}
+\frac{2x^4}{(1+2\mu^2)^2(1+4\mu^2)}+O(x^6).}
$$
