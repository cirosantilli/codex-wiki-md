<h1 id="32b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The required properties are those of a [strict Lyapunov function](../../../../../../strict-lyapunov-function.md); the domain of stability is the set of initial states whose forward trajectories converge to the fixed point.

For $V=(x^2+y^2)/2$,

$$
\dot V=(x^2+y^2)(-2+x^2+4y^2).
$$

Thus $\dot V<0$ when $V<1/4$, proving that disc lies in the basin. On $V=1$, $\dot V=6y^2\ge0$ (with the tangency points checked from the [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) field), so trajectories outside cannot enter the basin through that boundary. Hence the basin is contained in $V\le1$.
