<h1 id="31k/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

On an $n$-sample, each coordinate partition has at most $(n+1)^{m-1}$ placements, hence all $p$ partitions have at most $(n+1)^{p(m-1)}$ configurations. For each fixed configuration, the sign class from a [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of dimension $1+p(m-1)$ has at most $(n+1)^{1+p(m-1)}$ patterns. Multiplication gives

$$
\boxed{s(H_{F_3},n)\le(n+1)^{1+2p(m-1)}.}
$$
