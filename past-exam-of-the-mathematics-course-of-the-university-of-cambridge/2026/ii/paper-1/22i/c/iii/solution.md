<h1 id="22i/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For an isomorphism $T$, if $\|T^{-1}(S-T)\|<1$, then

$$
S=T[I+T^{-1}(S-T)]
$$

is invertible by [neumann-series perturbation](../../../../../../../neumann-series-perturbation.md).
