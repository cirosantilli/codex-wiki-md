↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using $X=(A+A^\dagger)/\sqrt{2\omega}$ and $H=\omega(A^\dagger A+1/2)$,

$$
\langle X\rangle=\sqrt{\frac2\omega}\Re\alpha,\qquad
\Delta X=\frac1{\sqrt{2\omega}},
$$



$$
\langle H\rangle=\omega(|\alpha|^2+\tfrac12),\qquad
\Delta H=\omega|\alpha|.
$$
