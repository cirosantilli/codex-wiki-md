The displacement operator is unitary, so $\||\alpha\rangle\|=1$. By [coherent state](../../../../../../coherent-state.md), $A|\alpha\rangle=\alpha|\alpha\rangle$, hence $\|A|\alpha\rangle\|=|\alpha|$.
