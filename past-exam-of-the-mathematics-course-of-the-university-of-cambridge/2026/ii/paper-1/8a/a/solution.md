<h1 id="8a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Stationarity of the action under variations with fixed endpoints gives the Euler-Lagrange equation. The [generalized momentum](../../../../../../generalized-momentum.md) is $p=\partial L/\partial\dot q$. The [Legendre transform in mechanics](../../../../../../legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) is

$$
H(q,p,t)=p\dot q-L(q,\dot q,t),
$$

after solving $p(q,\dot q,t)$ for $\dot q$. This requires at least local invertibility, $\partial^2L/\partial\dot q^2\ne0$; global single-valuedness requires global invertibility.
