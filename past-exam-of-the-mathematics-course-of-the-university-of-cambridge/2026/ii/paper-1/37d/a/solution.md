<h1 id="37d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $u^\mu=\gamma(c,\mathbf v)$ and $d/d\tau=\gamma d/dt$, the spatial components of the [relativistic Lorentz force](../../../../../../relativistic-lorentz-force.md) give

$$
\frac{d(\gamma m\mathbf v)}{dt}=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B).
$$

The time component gives $mc^2\,d\gamma/dt=q\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v$. It says [magnetic fields](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) do no work and electric power changes relativistic energy.
