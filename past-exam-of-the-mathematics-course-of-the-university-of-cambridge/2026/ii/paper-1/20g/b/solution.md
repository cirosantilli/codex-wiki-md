<h1 id="20g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The trace/norm integrality conditions give the stated congruences for  
$\theta=(a+bi+c\sqrt m+di\sqrt m)/2$. Solving them yields these [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) [bases](../../../../../../basis.md):

$$
m\equiv1\pmod4:\quad
1,\ i,\ \frac{1+\sqrt m}{2},\ \frac{i+i\sqrt m}{2},
$$



$$
m\equiv3\pmod4:\quad
1,\ i,\ \frac{i+\sqrt m}{2},\ \frac{1+i\sqrt m}{2}.
$$

The three quadratic subfields have fundamental discriminants $-4,D_m,D_{-m}$. By [discriminant of a biquadratic field](../../../../../../discriminant-of-a-biquadratic-field.md),

$$
D_L=(-4)D_mD_{-m}=16m^2
$$

in both residue classes.
