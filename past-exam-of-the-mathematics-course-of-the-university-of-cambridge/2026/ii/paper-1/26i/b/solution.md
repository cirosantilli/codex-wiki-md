<h1 id="26i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For unit speed on an oriented surface,

$$
T'=\kappa_n n+\kappa_g(n\times T).
$$

These coefficients are normal and [geodesic curvature](../../../../../../geodesic-curvature.md); the curve is geodesic iff $\kappa_g=0$.
