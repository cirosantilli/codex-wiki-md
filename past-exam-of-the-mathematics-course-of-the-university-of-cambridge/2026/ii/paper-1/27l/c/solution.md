<h1 id="27l/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $S_n/n$ converged almost surely to a finite [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md), then

$$
\frac{X_n}{n}=\frac{S_n}{n}-\frac{n-1}{n}\frac{S_{n-1}}{n-1}\longrightarrow0.
$$

But $\mathbb P(X_n/n>1)=\mathbb P(X_1>n)\sim1/(\pi n)$. These independent-event probabilities have divergent sum, so the second [Borel-Cantelli lemmas](../../../../../../borel-cantelli-lemmas.md) says $X_n/n>1$ infinitely often almost surely, a contradiction.
