↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The characteristic [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) of $S_n/n$ is

$$
\Phi(\xi/n)^n=e^{-n|\xi|/n}=e^{-|\xi|}.
$$

**Thus every $S_n/n$ already has the standard Cauchy law, and convergence in law follows from [characteristic-function convergence theorem](../../../../../../levy-s-continuity-theorem.md).**
