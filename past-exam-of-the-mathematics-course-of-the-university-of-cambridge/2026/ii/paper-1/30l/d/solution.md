<h1 id="30l/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The marginal utility price is

$$
\pi_0(Y)=\frac{E[U'(X_0)Y]}{(1+r)E[U'(X_0)]}.
$$

The value [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is concave; its tangent at zero lies above its graph. Evaluating that supporting inequality at the utility-indifference displacement $Y-(1+r)\pi(Y)$ gives  
$E[U'(X_0)(Y-(1+r)\pi(Y))]\ge0$, hence $\pi_0(Y)\ge\pi(Y)$.
