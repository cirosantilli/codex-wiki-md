<h1 id="30l/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $X_i$ optimizes $Z_i$, then $pX_1+(1-p)X_0$ is attainable because the wealth set is affine. Concavity gives

$$
\boxed{u(pZ_1+(1-p)Z_0)\ge E\,U(p(X_1+Z_1)+(1-p)(X_0+Z_0))
\ge pu(Z_1)+(1-p)u(Z_0).}
$$
