<h1 id="24g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A covering evenly covers every base point. It is [regular covering map](../../../../../../regular-covering-map.md) when deck transformations act transitively on fibres. For a concrete nonregular example, restrict $z\mapsto z^3-3z$ to

$$
\mathbb C\setminus p^{-1}\{-2,2\}\longrightarrow\mathbb C\setminus\{-2,2\}.
$$

It is a surjective three-sheeted unbranched cover with monodromy $S_3$, so its deck [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is not transitive.
