<h1 id="24g/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiate a germ of a continuation of $F$ to obtain a germ of the continuation of $f$. This defines a surjective cover $R_{\widetilde F}\to R_F$. Two primitive germs above the same [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) germ differ by an additive period. Translation by periods gives all deck transformations and acts transitively on each fibre, so the cover is regular.
