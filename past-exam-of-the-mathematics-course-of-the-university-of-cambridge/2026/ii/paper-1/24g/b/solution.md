↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

$(g,V)$ continues $(f,U)$ when they agree on a nonempty connected component reached by an overlapping chain of [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) elements. A [complete analytic function](../../../../../../complete-analytic-function.md) contains every continuation. Its Riemann surface is the set of its germs; germ neighbourhoods are charts, projection records the base point, and germ evaluation gives a holomorphic map to $\mathbb C$.
