<h1 id="18f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For odd $n>1$, the primitive $2n$th roots are exactly the negatives of primitive $n$th roots. Since $\varphi(2n)=\varphi(n)$, their monic root [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) satisfy $\Phi_{2n}(X)=\Phi_n(-X)$.
