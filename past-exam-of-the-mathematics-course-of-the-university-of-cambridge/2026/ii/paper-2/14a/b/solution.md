<h1 id="14a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Time and azimuthal invariance give energy

$$
E=\frac{7m}{10}(\dot r^2\csc^2\alpha+r^2\dot\phi^2)+mgr\cot\alpha
$$

and [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $J=(7m/5)r^2\dot\phi$. They represent mechanical energy and vertical [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md).
