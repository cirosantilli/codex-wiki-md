<h1 id="32d/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Repeated integration by parts gives

$$
E(x)\sim e^{-x}x^{-1}\sum_{n\ge0}(-1)^n n!x^{-n},
$$

so $a_n=(-1)^nn!$.
