<h1 id="15e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

After transmission Alice and Bob announce [bases](../../../../../../basis.md), retain matching-basis bits, reveal a random sample, and abort if its error rate is too large; privacy amplification and error correction produce the key. A Breidbart intercept-resend causes error $1/4$ conditional on matching Alice and Bob [bases](../../../../../../basis.md). Since matching occurs with probability $1/2$, one transmitted qubit detects Eve with probability $1/8$.
