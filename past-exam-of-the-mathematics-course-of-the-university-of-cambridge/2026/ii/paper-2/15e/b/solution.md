<h1 id="15e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Conditional on Alice and Bob using the same [basis](../../../../../../basis.md), Eve chooses the wrong [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) with probability $2/3$, then Bob disagrees with probability $1/2$, so the conditional error is $1/3$. Alice and Bob match with probability $1/3$, hence the per-transmission detection probability is $1/9$.
