<h1 id="24g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a nonconstant holomorphic map $f:X\to Y$ of degree $d$ between compact [Riemann surfaces](../../../../../../riemann-surfaces.md), [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](../../../../../../riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) is $2g_X-2=d(2g_Y-2)+\sum_p(e_p-1)$. Triangulate $Y$ with branch values as vertices and lift the triangulation: faces and edges lift $d$ times, while the vertex deficit is exactly the ramification sum. Comparing Euler characteristics proves the formula.
