For $k_BT\ll\hbar\omega_D$, extend the [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md) to infinity:

$$
\log Z=-\int_0^\infty g(\omega)\log(1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega})d\omega
=\frac{A k_B^2T^2}{\pi c_s^2\hbar^2}\sum_{n\ge1}\frac1{n^3}.
$$

**Thus $X=\zeta(3)/\pi$.**
