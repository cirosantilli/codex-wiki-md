**No.** Point evaluation is continuous on $H^1(\mathbb R)$, and every [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) of [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) supported away from zero has trace $u(0)=0$. A smooth [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) with nonzero value at zero belongs to $H^1$ but not to this $H_0^1$.
