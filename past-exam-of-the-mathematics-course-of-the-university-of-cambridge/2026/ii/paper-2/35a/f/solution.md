<h1 id="35a/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Applying $I\otimes I,X,Z,$ or $XZ$ to one qubit permutes the Bell [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) up to phases. Composing two such Pauli operations maps any chosen Bell state to any other, proving local equivalence.
