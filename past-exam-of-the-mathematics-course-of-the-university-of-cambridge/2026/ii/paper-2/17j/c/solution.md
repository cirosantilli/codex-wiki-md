<h1 id="17j/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Alternating suitable half-open vertical strips of width $\sqrt3/2$ gives a two-colouring in which the horizontal projections forced by a unit equilateral triangle cannot all occupy strips of one parity; boundary strips are assigned consistently. For three colours, embed the [Moser spindle](../../../../../../moser-spindle.md), a finite unit-distance graph of chromatic number four, in the plane. Any three-colouring therefore has a monochromatic unit edge.
