[Homotopy invariance of winding number](../../../../../../homotopy-invariance-of-winding-number.md) states that a homotopy $H(s,t)$ through closed paths in $\mathbb C^*$ preserves [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md). Uniform continuity divides the $s$ interval so successive paths differ pointwise by less than the minimum [modulus](../../../../../../modulus.md) of the earlier path; the supplied perturbation lemma makes their [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md)s equal. Chaining the subdivisions proves the result.
