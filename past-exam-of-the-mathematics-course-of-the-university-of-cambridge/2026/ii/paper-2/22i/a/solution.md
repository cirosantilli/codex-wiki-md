↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Riesz representation theorem](../../../../../../riesz-representation-theorem.md) says every bounded linear functional $f$ on a Hilbert space has a unique $z$ with $f(x)=\langle x,z\rangle$ and $\|f\|=\|z\|$. For $f\ne0$, decompose relative to $\ker f$; its one-dimensional orthogonal complement supplies $z$, and Cauchy-Schwarz proves the norm statement and uniqueness.
