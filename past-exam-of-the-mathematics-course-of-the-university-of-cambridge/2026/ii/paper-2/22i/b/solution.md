<h1 id="22i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For fixed $y$, Riesz gives a unique $Sy$ with $\theta(x,y)=\langle x,Sy\rangle$. Conjugate [linearity](../../../../../../linearity.md) in $y$ and the inner-product convention make $S$ linear. Moreover $\|Sy\|=\sup_{\|x\|\le1}|\theta(x,y)|\le\|\theta\|\|y\|$, and taking both suprema proves $\|S\|=\|\theta\|$.
