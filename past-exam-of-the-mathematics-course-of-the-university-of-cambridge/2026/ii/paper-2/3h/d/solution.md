<h1 id="3h/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [asymptotic equipartition property](../../../../../../asymptotic-equipartition-property.md) is $-n^{-1}\log_2p(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\to H$ in probability. For an i.i.d. source this is the weak law applied to $-\log_2p(X_i)$, and independence gives $H(X_1,\ldots,X_n)=nH(X)$. Thus its [information rate](../../../../../../information-rate.md) and AEP constant are both $H(X)$.
