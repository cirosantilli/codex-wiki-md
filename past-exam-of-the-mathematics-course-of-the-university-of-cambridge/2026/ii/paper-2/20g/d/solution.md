<h1 id="20g/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Since $\beta$ is [integral](../../../../../../integral.md), $R[\beta]$ is generated over $R$ by finitely many powers, hence is fractional. It is invertible by (c). Because it is a [ring](../../../../../../ring.md), $I^2=I$; multiplying by $I^{-1}$ gives $I=R$. Thus every $\beta\in\mathcal O_K$ lies in $R$, so $R=\mathcal O_K$.
