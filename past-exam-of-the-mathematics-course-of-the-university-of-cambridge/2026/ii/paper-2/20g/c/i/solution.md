↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Given a nonzero fractional $I$, scale it to an [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md) [ideal](../../../../../../../ideal.md) $aI$. By (i), $(aI)J=(r)$ for some nonzero [ideal](../../../../../../../ideal.md) $J$. Then $I^{-1}=ar^{-1}J$ is fractional and $II^{-1}=R$.
