<h1 id="26i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $v\in T_p\Sigma$, let $\gamma_v$ be the maximal geodesic with $\gamma_v(0)=p$, $\dot\gamma_v(0)=v$. Then $\exp_p(v)=\gamma_v(1)$ on the open star-shaped set of $v$ for which time $1$ exists.
