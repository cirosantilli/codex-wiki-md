The substitution $x=\cos\theta$ identifies the weighted norm with the $L^2(0,\pi)$ norm of $f(\cos\theta)$. Completeness of cosine Fourier [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) therefore makes the Chebyshev partial sums converge in $\|\cdot\|_w$.
