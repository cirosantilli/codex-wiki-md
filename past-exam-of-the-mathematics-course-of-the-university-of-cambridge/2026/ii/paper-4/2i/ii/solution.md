<h1 id="2i/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\deg p\le2n-1$, divide $p=q p_n+r$ with $\deg q\le n-1$, $\deg r\le n-1$. Orthogonality makes $\int qp_n=0$, and $p(t_j)=r(t_j)$. Apply part (i) to $r$. This is [Gaussian quadrature](../../../../../../gaussian-quadrature.md).
