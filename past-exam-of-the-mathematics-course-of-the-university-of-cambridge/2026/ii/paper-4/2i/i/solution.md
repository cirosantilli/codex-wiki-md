↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\ell_j$ be the Lagrange [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) for the nodes. Necessity and existence give uniquely $A_j=\int_{-1}^1\ell_j(t)dt$, because every [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $n-1$ is $\sum_jp(t_j)\ell_j(t)$.
