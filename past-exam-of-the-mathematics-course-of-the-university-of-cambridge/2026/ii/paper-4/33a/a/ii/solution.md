↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Spherical symmetry makes $H_0$ commute with every $L_i$ and it acts trivially on spin, so energy is independent of $m,\sigma$. For fixed $\ell$ and spin $1/2$, degeneracy is $2(2\ell+1)=4\ell+2$.
