<h1 id="33a/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $j=\ell+1/2$, $L\cdot S=\hbar^2\ell/2$, giving $E_n+\lambda\hbar^2\ell/2$ with degeneracy $2\ell+2$. For $j=\ell-1/2$, it is $-\hbar^2(\ell+1)/2$, giving $E_n-\lambda\hbar^2(\ell+1)/2$ with degeneracy $2\ell$. Thus the original multiplet splits into two.
