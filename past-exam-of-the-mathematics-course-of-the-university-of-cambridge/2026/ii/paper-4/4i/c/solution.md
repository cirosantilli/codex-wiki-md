**No.** Context-free languages are not closed under symmetric difference (a standard counterexample is obtained from the two CFLs enforcing respectively equality of the first two and last two blocks in $a^*b^*c^*$). Transport such a pair through the bijection $\beta$; pointwise addition corresponds exactly to symmetric difference.
