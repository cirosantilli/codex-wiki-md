<h1 id="10e/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The amplitude at $y$ is proportional to $e^{2\pi ix_0y/8}\sum_{j=0}^{A-1}e^{2\pi ijry/8}$. The geometric sum vanishes unless $ry\equiv0\pmod8$, i.e. unless $y$ is a multiple of $A=8/r$. At those $r$ outcomes its [modulus](../../../../../../modulus.md) is $1/\sqrt r$, so each probability is $1/r$. The phase contains $x_0$, but measurement probabilities do not.
