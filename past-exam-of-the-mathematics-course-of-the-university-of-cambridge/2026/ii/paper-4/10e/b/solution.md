<h1 id="10e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The controlled phases and final bit reversal implement QFT. Thus $|000\rangle\mapsto2^{-3/2}\sum_{j=0}^7|j\rangle$, while

$$
|011\rangle\mapsto2^{-3/2}\sum_{j=0}^7e^{2\pi i(3j)/8}|j\rangle,
$$

which agrees with $\operatorname{QFT}_8|0\rangle$ and $\operatorname{QFT}_8|3\rangle$.
