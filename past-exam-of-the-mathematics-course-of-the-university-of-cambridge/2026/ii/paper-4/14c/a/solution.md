↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The left side is the flux of newborn trees through size zero. Every tree of size $s$ produces seedlings at rate $b(s)$, so total births are $\int_0^\infty b(s)n(s,t)ds$, giving the [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md).
