↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Substitution gives $(g\tilde n)'=-(\mu+r)\tilde n$, hence

$$
\tilde n(s)=\frac{C}{g(s)}\exp\left[-\int_0^s\frac{\mu(u)+r}{g(u)}du\right].
$$

The [boundary condition](../../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) yields the Euler-Lotka equation

$$
\boxed{1=\int_0^\infty\frac{b(s)}{g(s)}\exp\left[-\int_0^s\frac{\mu(u)+r}{g(u)}du\right]ds.}
$$
