<h1 id="31d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $y=q^{-1/4}e^{\lambda S}$, leading order gives $S'=\pm i\sqrt q$ and the transport equation gives the $q^{-1/4}$ amplitude. Superposition yields the stated [WKB approximation](../../../../../../wkb-approximation.md) form.
