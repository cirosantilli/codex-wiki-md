Multiplying two asymptotic eigenfunctions by $q$ reduces the [integral](../../../../../../../integral.md), after the phase coordinate $s=\int_a^x\sqrt q$, to a constant multiple of $\int_0^L\sin(n\pi s/L)\sin(m\pi s/L)ds=0$.
