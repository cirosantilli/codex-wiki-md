<h1 id="35e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

From $dF=-S,dT-p,dV$, Maxwell gives $(\partial S/\partial V)_T=(\partial p/\partial T)_V$. With $dE=T,dS-p,dV$, $(\partial E/\partial V)_T=T(\partial p/\partial T)_V-p=T^2\partial_T(p/T)|_V$.
