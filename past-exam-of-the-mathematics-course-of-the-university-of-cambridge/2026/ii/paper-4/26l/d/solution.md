Again the [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) converges absolutely. $T=2\sum_jB_j/3^j$ has the Cantor distribution. Every particular digit [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) has probability zero, so it has no atoms; its support is the Cantor set, which has Lebesgue measure zero, so it cannot have a Lebesgue density.
