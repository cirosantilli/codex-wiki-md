↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Dedekind's criterion factors $(p)$ according to the factorization of $X^3-7$ modulo $p$ (the monogenic assumption removes the index obstruction). With $\alpha=\sqrt[3]7$,

$$
(2)=(2,\alpha-1)(2,\alpha^2+\alpha+1),\quad
(3)=(3,\alpha-1)^3,
$$



$$
(5)=(5,\alpha-3)(5,\alpha^2+3\alpha+4),\quad
(7)=(\alpha)^3.
$$

Minkowski's bound is below 11, so classes are generated by prime [ideals](../../../../../../ideal.md) over $2,3,5,7$. Relations from the displayed factorizations and principal [ideals](../../../../../../ideal.md) reduce them to $\mathfrak p=(3,\alpha-1)$ with $\mathfrak p^3$ principal. Part (a) shows $\mathfrak p$ is not principal, so $\operatorname{Cl}(K)\simeq\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z$.
