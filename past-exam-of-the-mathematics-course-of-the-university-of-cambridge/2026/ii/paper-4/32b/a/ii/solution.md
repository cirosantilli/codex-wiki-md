↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Glendinning chaos means there is an invariant set on which an iterate is semiconjugate to the full shift, giving sensitive dependence and periodic points of every period. For a 3-cycle ordered $x_1<x_2<x_3$, the intermediate-value theorem applied to the images of the intervening intervals constructs a horseshoe for an iterate; hence $F$ is chaotic.
