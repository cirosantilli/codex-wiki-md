<h1 id="32b/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With $q=0$, expand $F(F(x))$ for $x=O(\sqrt\mu)$ through $O(\mu^{3/2})$. Besides zero, $F^2(x)=x$ gives $x^2=\mu/p+O(\mu^2)$, and the two points are exchanged by $F$. The [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) product is $1-4\mu+O(\mu^2)$, so the period-two orbit $|x|=\sqrt{\mu/p}+O(\mu^{3/2})$ is stable.
