[Artin fixed-field theorem](../../../../../../artin-fixed-field-theorem.md) says that for a finite [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) $G\le\operatorname{Aut}L$, $L/L^G$ is Galois with [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) $G$ and degree $|G|$. Let any finite $G$ act faithfully by left translation on variables $X_g$ in $L=\mathbb C(X_g:g\in G)$. Artin's theorem then realizes $G$ as $\operatorname{Gal}(L/L^G)$.
