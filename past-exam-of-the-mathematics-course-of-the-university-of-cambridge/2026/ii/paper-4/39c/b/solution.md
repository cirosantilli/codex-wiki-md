<h1 id="39c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Mass, [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md), energy and $c^2=\gamma p/\rho$ eliminate the density and [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) ratios. The result is

$$
M_2^2=\frac{2+(\gamma-1)M_1^2}{2\gamma M_1^2-(\gamma-1)},
$$

so a supersonic upstream flow becomes subsonic.
