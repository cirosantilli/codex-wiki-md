↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiplying Euler's equations by $\omega_i$ and by $I_i\omega_i$ respectively shows $T=\tfrac12\sum I_i\omega_i^2$ and $L^2=\sum I_i^2\omega_i^2$ are constant. Their ellipsoids are tangent when their normals are parallel, which occurs on the principal axes.
