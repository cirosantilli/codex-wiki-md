<h1 id="15a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Linearization about $(0,\Omega,0)$ gives $\dot u=-Av$, $\dot v=-Bu$, where $A=(I_3-I_2)\Omega/I_1$, $B=(I_2-I_1)\Omega/I_3$. With $\kappa=\sqrt{AB}$,

$$
u=u_0\cosh\kappa t-(A/\kappa)v_0\sinh\kappa t,quad v=v_0\cosh\kappa t-(B/\kappa)u_0\sinh\kappa t.
$$

The exponential growth confirms intermediate-axis instability.
