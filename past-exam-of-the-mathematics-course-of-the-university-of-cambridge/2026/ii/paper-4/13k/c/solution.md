<h1 id="13k/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

First regress $X$ on $Z$, then regress $Y$ on the fitted values. With one centred instrument this equals $S_{ZY}/S_{ZX}$. Its [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) is $\operatorname{Cov}(Z,Y)/\operatorname{Cov}(Z,X)=1/1=1$, the causal coefficient of $X$ in $Y=X+U+\epsilon_Y$.
