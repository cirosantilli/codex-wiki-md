<h1 id="19f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Mackey and [induced representation](../../../../../../induced-representation.md) sum over $H\backslash G/K$. Coprimality makes every $H\cap gKg^{-1}$ trivial, each double coset has size $|H||K|$, and each local [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) is $(\dim V)(\dim W)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\langle\operatorname{Ind}_H^GV,\operatorname{Ind}_K^GW\rangle_G=\frac{|G|}{|H||K|}\dim V\dim W.}
$$
