↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The same Jordan calculation gives convergence of $\sum A^k$ exactly for $\rho(A)<1$. Finite sums satisfy $(I-A)\sum_{k=0}^mA^k=I-A^{m+1}$; taking [limits](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) gives $(I-A)^{-1}$. Conversely convergence forces $A^k\to0$, so part (a) applies.
