<h1 id="36d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With no free surface charge, tangential $E$ and normal $D$ are continuous. The image ansatz gives

$$
\frac{q'}q=\frac{1-\epsilon_r}{1+\epsilon_r},\qquad \frac{q''}q=\frac{2\epsilon_r}{1+\epsilon_r}.
$$

At $z=0^-$, with $R=(r^2+d^2)^{1/2}$,

$$
E_r=\frac{2q}{1+\epsilon_r}\frac{r}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^3},\qquad E_z=\frac{2\epsilon_rq}{1+\epsilon_r}\frac{d}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^3}.
$$
