<h1 id="36d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Insert $\rho=\epsilon_0\nabla\cdot E$ and $J=\mu_0^{-1}\nabla\times B-\epsilon_0E_t$ into the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) and use [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) identities plus [Faraday's law](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md). Rearrangement gives $\partial_tg_i+\partial_j\sigma_{ij}=-f_i$. Here $g=\epsilon_0E\times B$ is field [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) density and $\sigma$ is the [Maxwell stress tensor](../../../../../../maxwell-stress-tensor.md), whose surface traction transports [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md).
