<h1 id="25i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Define $F:TX\to\mathbb R$ by $F(p,v)=I_p(v,v)$. On $F^{-1}(1)$, the vertical [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) in direction $v$ is $2I_p(v,v)=2$, so 1 is regular. Since $\dim TX=4$, the [preimage theorem](../../../../../../preimage-theorem.md) makes the unit tangent bundle $SX$ a 3-manifold.
