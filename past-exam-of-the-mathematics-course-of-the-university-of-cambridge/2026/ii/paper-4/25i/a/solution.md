↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a local parametrization $\phi:U\subset\mathbb R^k\to X$, the map $(q,w)\mapsto(\phi(q),d\phi_qw)$ parametrizes $TX$ locally. Its inverse uses the base chart and [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md), and its rank is $2k$. These charts make $TX$ a $2k$-manifold.
