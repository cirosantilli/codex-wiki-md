<h1 id="23h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For separable Banach $X$, the closed unit ball of $X^*$ is weak-star compact. Embed it in the product of compact discs by evaluations on a countable [dense subset](../../../../../../dense-set.md); diagonal subsequences converge on that subset, boundedness extends the [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) to all $X$, and the [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) functional remains in the ball. Metrizability of the bounded weak-star topology turns sequential compactness into compactness. Scaling gives the general [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](../../../../../../banach-alaoglu-theorem.md).
