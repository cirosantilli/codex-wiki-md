<h1 id="23h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

$H$ is a closed linear subspace of reflexive $L^p$. A minimizing [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) is bounded by the triangle inequality. Reflexivity supplies a weakly convergent subsequence with [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) $f_0\in H$; each norm $\|f-g_i\|_p$ is weakly lower semicontinuous, so their sum attains the infimum at $f_0$.
