↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The allowed [Landau level](../../../../../../landau-level.md) are $E_n=\hbar\omega(n+1/2)$. The commuting $b^\dagger$ creates distinct guiding-centre states without changing energy, so every level is infinitely degenerate on the plane; in finite area $A$ the degeneracy is approximately $A/(2\pi r_B^2)$.
