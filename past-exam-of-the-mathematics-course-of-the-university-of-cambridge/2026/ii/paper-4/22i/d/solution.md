<h1 id="22i/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) defining $\phi_y$ converges and $\|\phi_y\|\le\|y\|_1$. For each finite set of $k_n$, normality and Tietze extension give a continuous $f$ of norm at most one taking prescribed signs there. Letting the finite set grow proves $\|\phi_y\|=\|y\|_1$. Thus $y\mapsto\phi_y$ is an isometric embedding of $\ell_1$ into $C(K)^*$.
