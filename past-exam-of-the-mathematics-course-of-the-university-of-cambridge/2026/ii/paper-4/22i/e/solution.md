**No.** The point evaluations $\delta_x$ satisfy $\|\delta_x-\delta_y\|=2$ for $x\ne y$ (choose a [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) taking values $1,-1$ at the two points). Thus $C[0,1]^*$ is nonseparable, whereas $\ell_1$ is separable.
