<h1 id="8a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The energy sublevel $H\le1/2$ is compact and has finite phase volume because both $p$ and $q$ are bounded. If all $\Phi_{nT}(U)$ were disjoint, volume preservation would put infinitely many equal positive volumes inside it, impossible. Thus two iterates intersect. Pulling the intersection back shows a point of $U$ returns to $U$; applying the argument to arbitrarily small neighbourhoods gives [Poincaré recurrence theorem](../../../../../../poincare-recurrence-theorem.md).
