<h1 id="28l/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Replace the two $G$ quantiles by conditional [bootstrap](../../../../../../bootstrapping-statistics.md) quantiles $\hat q_u$ in the interval from (a). [Uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) implies $\hat q_u\to q_u$ in probability at continuity points, so Slutsky and continuity of $G$ give coverage tending to $1-\alpha$.
