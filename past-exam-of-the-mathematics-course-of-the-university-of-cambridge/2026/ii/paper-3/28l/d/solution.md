<h1 id="28l/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Generate $B$ resamples, compute $\sqrt n(\hat\theta^{*j}-\hat\theta)$, and use their empirical $\alpha/2$ and $1-\alpha/2$ quantiles. Large $B$ approximates the otherwise unavailable conditional [bootstrap](../../../../../../bootstrapping-statistics.md) distribution.
