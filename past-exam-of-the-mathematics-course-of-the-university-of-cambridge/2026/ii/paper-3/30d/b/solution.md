<h1 id="30d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The saddle is $z_s=1+i$. Completing the square gives $\phi(z)=i-(z-1-i)^2/2$, so its steepest-descent line is the horizontal line $z=1+i+s$, $s\in\mathbb R$.
