Atomic one-variable formulas reduce to [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) equalities and inequalities after clearing terms. Their solution sets are basic by the supplied fact. Basic sets are closed under finite union, intersection, and complement; structural induction through the Boolean connectives therefore proves the claim.
