<h1 id="35e/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $B=J=0$, $m=0$ remains stationary. At large $\beta K$, evaluating the free energy near a positive $m$ makes the negative cubic energy dominate the bounded [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) cost, so some $m>0$ has lower free energy than zero. At high [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) uniquely favors zero. Continuity in [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) therefore forces a transition at an intermediate $\beta_*$.
