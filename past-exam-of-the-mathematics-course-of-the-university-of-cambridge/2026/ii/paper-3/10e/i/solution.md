<h1 id="10e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The all-zero amplitude is $2^{-n}\sum_x(-1)^{f(x)}=1-2k/2^n$, so its probability is $(1-2k/2^n)^2$. For a general nontrivial $k$ it lies strictly between zero and one.
