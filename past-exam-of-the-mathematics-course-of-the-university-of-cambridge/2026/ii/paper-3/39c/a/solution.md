↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Stationary points](../../../../../../stationary-point.md) satisfy $x/t=\omega'(k_j)$. For [nondegenerate points](../../../../../../nondegenerate-stationary-point-of-a-phase.md),

$$
\boxed{\eta(x,t)\sim\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi t}}\sum_j\frac{F(k_j)}{\sqrt{|\omega''(k_j)|}}\exp\{i[k_jx-\omega(k_j)t-i\tfrac\pi4\operatorname{sgn}\omega''(k_j)]\}.}
$$
