<h1 id="22h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Approximate $f$ in $L^1$ by continuous $g$. The limsup local oscillation of $f$ is bounded by the maximal [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) of $f-g$ plus the vanishing oscillation of $g$. The weak $(1,1)$ [Hardy-Littlewood maximal inequality](../../../../../../hardy-littlewood-maximal-inequality.md) makes the exceptional set have measure at most $C\|f-g\|_1/\epsilon$; let the approximation error tend to zero.
