<h1 id="26l/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

On disjoint events $A_n$ with $P(A_n)=c/n^2$, set $X_n=n1_{A_n}$. Then $X_n\to0$ almost surely and $E X_n^2=c$, but any common dominator has [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) at least $\sum_n nP(A_n)=c\sum1/n=\infty$. Thus no integrable dominating variable exists.
