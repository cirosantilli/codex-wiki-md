<h1 id="26l/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fatou says $\int\liminf f_n\le\liminf\int f_n$ for nonnegative [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md). Apply it to $g+f_n$ and $g-f_n$ when $|f_n|\le g\in L^1$ to obtain both inequalities and hence dominated convergence.
