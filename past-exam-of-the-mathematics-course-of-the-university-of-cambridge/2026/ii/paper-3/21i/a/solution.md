<h1 id="21i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

$\sigma(T)=\{\lambda:T-\lambda I\text{ is not invertible}\}$ and $r(T)=\sup_{\lambda\in\sigma(T)}|\lambda|$, well-defined because the [spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) is nonempty compact. If $|\lambda|>\|T^n\|^{1/n}$, factor $T^n-\lambda^nI$ and use a Neumann [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md); spectral mapping then excludes $\lambda$. Thus $r(T)\le\inf_n\|T^n\|^{1/n}$.
