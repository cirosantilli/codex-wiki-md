The third [Kramers-Moyal expansion](../../../../../../../kramers-moyal-expansion.md) coefficient is $F=\tfrac16\sum_rr^3W=(a-b)/6$. At a first minimum the third [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) has no fixed sign, so the maximum-principle argument fails; finite truncations beyond second order need not preserve positivity.
