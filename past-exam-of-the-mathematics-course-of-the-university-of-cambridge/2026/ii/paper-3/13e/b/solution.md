<h1 id="13e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

When jumps are small relative to a large population and moments exist, the [Kramers-Moyal expansion](../../../../../../kramers-moyal-expansion.md) through second order gives

$$
P_t=-\partial_x(uP)+\partial_x^2(DP),
\qquad
u=\sum_r rW(x,r),
\qquad
D=\frac12\sum_r r^2W(x,r).
$$

This [Fokker-Planck equation](../../../../../../fokker-planck-equation.md) is a conservation law with vanishing boundary flux; $u$ is the drift and $D$ the diffusivity. Equal constant rates for jumps $r=\mathord\pm1$ give pure diffusion.
