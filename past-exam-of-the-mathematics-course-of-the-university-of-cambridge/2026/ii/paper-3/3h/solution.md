<h1 id="3h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3H](../3h.md)

A cyclic code is a linear subspace invariant under cyclic coordinate shift. Identifying words with $\mathbb F_2[X]/(X^n-1)$ makes such codes exactly [ideals](../../../../../ideal.md). Since this quotient is a principal [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) [ring](../../../../../ring.md), every code is generated uniquely by a monic divisor of $X^n-1$. For $n=7$,

$$
X^7-1=(X+1)(X^3+X+1)(X^3+X^2+1),
$$

so its three distinct irreducible factors have $2^3=8$ monic divisors and there are eight cyclic codes.
