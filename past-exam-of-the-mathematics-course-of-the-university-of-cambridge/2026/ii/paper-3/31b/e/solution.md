<h1 id="31b/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For $a=-1,b=1$, $\dot y=-1+x-y-x^2-y^2\le-1/2<0$ after maximizing the two quadratic expressions. Thus no orbit is periodic. A proper forward bounding region or Lyapunov trapping [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) would, in a planar flow with no equilibria, force a periodic [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) set by Poincare-Bendixson; therefore no such global bounding [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) exists.
