<h1 id="38c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [38C](../38c.md)

The axial equation is $\mu r^{-1}(ru')'=-G$. Regularity at zero, no slip $u_a(a)=0$, continuity of [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) and shear at $c$ give

$$
u_a(r)=\frac{G(a^2-r^2)}{4\mu_a},\qquad u_c(r)=\frac{G(c^2-r^2)}{4\mu_c}+\frac{G(a^2-c^2)}{4\mu_a}.
$$

The annulus is controlled only by its own [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity.md) and the outer no-slip condition, explaining the absence of $\mu_c$. For the annular control volume, kinetic-energy rates vanish; [pressure](../../../../../pressure.md) work plus interfacial traction work equals $\int_V2\mu_ae_{ij}e_{ij}dV$. Substituting $u_a'=-Gr/(2\mu_a)$ evaluates both sides to the same boundary expression, verifying the mechanical-energy equation.
