<h1 id="15e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Across the cut $A|BC$, the two conditional $BC$ [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) $\alpha|00\rangle$ and $\gamma|00\rangle+\beta|11\rangle$ are linearly independent when $\alpha\beta\ne0$, so the Schmidt rank exceeds one. Hence the state cannot be a three-qubit product for any $\gamma$.
