<h1 id="15e/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Charlie measures in the $X$ [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) and sends one bit to Alice or Bob; the remaining pair becomes $|\Phi^+\rangle$ or $|\Phi^-\rangle$, corrected by a local $Z$. Alice then performs standard teleportation: CNOT from $|\xi\rangle$ to her GHZ qubit, Hadamard, two computational measurements, and sends the two outcomes to Bob, who applies the corresponding Pauli correction.
