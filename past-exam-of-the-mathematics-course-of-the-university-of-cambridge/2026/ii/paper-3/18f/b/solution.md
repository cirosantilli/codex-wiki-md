<h1 id="18f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An algebraic $x$ is separable when its minimal [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has distinct roots. In characteristic zero its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) cannot vanish. In characteristic $p$, repeatedly write an inseparable minimal [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) as a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $X^p$; after finitely many steps the minimal [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of $x^{p^n}$ has nonzero [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) and is separable.
