<h1 id="18f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Finite means $[L:K]<\infty$; algebraic means every element satisfies a nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) over $K$. Linear dependence among $1,x,\ldots,x^n$ proves finite implies algebraic. The algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q$ is algebraic but infinite.
