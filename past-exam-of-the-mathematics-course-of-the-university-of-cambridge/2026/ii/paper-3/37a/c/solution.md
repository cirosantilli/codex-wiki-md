<h1 id="37a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Under a coordinate change, the inhomogeneous second-derivative terms arising from each [partial derivative](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) cancel between $V^\alpha\partial_\alpha W^\mu$ and $W^\alpha\partial_\alpha V^\mu$. The remainder transforms with one contravariant Jacobian, so the commutator is a [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) field.
