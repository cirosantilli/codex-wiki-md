<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose all four axes in one plane, at angles

$$
a=0,
\qquad b=0,
\qquad a'=-\frac\pi6,
\qquad b'=\frac\pi6.
$$

Their relevant unsigned separations are

$$
\theta(a,b)=0,
\quad \theta(a,b')=\frac\pi6,
\quad \theta(a',b)=\frac\pi6,
\quad \theta(a',b')=\frac\pi3.
$$

The specified correlation function therefore gives $E(a,b)=E(a,b')=E(a',b)=1$ and $E(a',b')=-1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{E(a,b)+E(a,b')+E(a',b)-E(a',b')=4},
$$

the algebraic maximum and thus a maximal violation of the [CHSH inequality](../../../../../../chsh-inequality.md).
