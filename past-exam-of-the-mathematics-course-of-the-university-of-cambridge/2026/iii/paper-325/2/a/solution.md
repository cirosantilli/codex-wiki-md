↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen criterion of reality](../../../../../../einstein-podolsky-rosen-criterion-of-reality.md) says that if, without disturbing a system, one can predict a physical quantity with certainty, then an element of physical reality corresponds to that quantity.

For two settings on each wing and outcomes in $\{-1,1\}$, the [CHSH inequality](../../../../../../chsh-inequality.md) for every [local hidden-variable theory](../../../../../../local-hidden-variable-theory.md) is

$$
\boxed{|E(A_0B_0)+E(A_0B_1)+E(A_1B_0)-E(A_1B_1)|\leq2}.
$$
