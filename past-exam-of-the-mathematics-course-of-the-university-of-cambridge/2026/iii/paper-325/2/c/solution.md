<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppose local elements of reality predetermined outputs $x(a,\lambda),x(a',\lambda),y(b,\lambda),y(b',\lambda)\in\{-1,1\}$. For every hidden state $\lambda$,

$$
x(a)[y(b)+y(b')]+x(a')[y(b)-y(b')]
$$

equals $2$ or $-2$, because exactly one bracket vanishes and the other equals $\pm2$. Averaging over $\lambda$ proves the [CHSH inequality](../../../../../../chsh-inequality.md). Part (b) instead gives $4$, so no such locally predetermined response functions can reproduce the device. This is the standard [Bell theorem](../../../../../../bell-theorem.md) obstruction.
