<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a subsystem $A$ of a multipartite system with [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) $\rho_{A E_1\cdots E_n}$, its [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) is the unique operator

$$
\boxed{\rho_A=\operatorname{Tr}_{E_1\cdots E_n}\rho_{A E_1\cdots E_n}}
$$

such that $\operatorname{Tr}(M_A\rho_A)=\operatorname{Tr}[(M_A\otimes I_E)\rho_{AE}]$ for every observable $M_A$ on $A$. In an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $\{|e_j\rangle\}$ of the other subsystems, the [partial trace](../../../../../../partial-trace.md) is

$$
\rho_A=\sum_j(I_A\otimes\langle e_j|)\rho_{AE}(I_A\otimes|e_j\rangle),
$$

which is independent of the chosen basis.
