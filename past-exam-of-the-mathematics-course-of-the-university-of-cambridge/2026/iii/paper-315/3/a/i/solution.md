In the [exoplanet transmission spectrum](../../../../../../../exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md), the constant $a$ is the wavelength-independent reference transit depth, set mainly by the opaque planetary radius and any grey cloud deck. The Gaussian term can represent a resolved atomic or molecular absorption band centered at $\lambda_0$, with amplitude $b$ and width controlled by $\alpha$. The power-law term represents a continuum such as Rayleigh scattering or haze extinction; for Rayleigh scattering the opacity index is approximately $\gamma=-4$.
