<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $R$ be a [valuation ring](../../../../../../valuation-ring.md) with [fraction field](../../../../../../field-of-fractions.md) $K$. For every commutative square

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
\operatorname{Spec}K&\longrightarrow&X\\
\downarrow&&\downarrow f\\
\operatorname{Spec}R&\longrightarrow&Y,
\end{array}
$$

the [valuative criterion for separatedness](../../../../../../valuative-criterion-for-separatedness.md) says that a [finite type morphism](../../../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md) $f$ between [Noetherian schemes](../../../../../../noetherian-scheme.md) is [separated](../../../../../../separated-morphism.md) exactly when there is at most one dotted lift $\operatorname{Spec}R\to X$ completing the diagram.

Under the same finiteness hypotheses, the [valuative criterion for properness](../../../../../../valuative-criterion-for-properness.md) says that $f$ is [proper](../../../../../../proper-morphism.md) exactly when every such square has a unique lift. Thus separatedness supplies uniqueness, while properness supplies existence as well.
