↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The $\mathcal O_Y$-module $\mathcal E$ is a [locally free sheaf](../../../../../../locally-free-sheaf.md) of finite rank if every $y\in Y$ has an open neighborhood $V$ and a finite integer $r$ for which

$$
\mathcal E|_V\cong\mathcal O_V^{\oplus r}.
$$

The rank is locally constant and is therefore constant on each connected component.
