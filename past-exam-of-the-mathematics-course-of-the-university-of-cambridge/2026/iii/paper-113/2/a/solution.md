The [tensor product of sheaves](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-sheaves.md) $\mathcal F\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal G$ is the sheafification of the presheaf

$$
U\longmapsto\mathcal F(U)\otimes_{\mathcal O_X(U)}\mathcal G(U).
$$

Equivalently, its stalk at $x$ is $\mathcal F_x\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,x}}\mathcal G_x$.

The [direct image sheaf](../../../../../../direct-image-sheaf.md) is defined on each open set $V\subseteq Y$ by

$$
(f_*\mathcal F)(V)=\mathcal F(f^{-1}V).
$$

The [pullback of a sheaf of modules](../../../../../../pullback-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md) is

$$
f^*\mathcal E=\mathcal O_X\otimes_{f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y}f^{-1}\mathcal E,
$$

where $f^{-1}\mathcal E$ is the [inverse image sheaf](../../../../../../inverse-image-sheaf.md) and the tensor product uses the structural morphism $f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y\to\mathcal O_X$ of the given [ringed-space morphism](../../../../../../morphism-of-ringed-spaces.md).
