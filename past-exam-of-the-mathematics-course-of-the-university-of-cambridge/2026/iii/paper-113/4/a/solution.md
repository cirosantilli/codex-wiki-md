↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [morphism of schemes](../../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) $i:Z\to X$ is a [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md) if it identifies $Z$ homeomorphically with a closed subset of $X$ and the morphism of sheaves

$$
\mathcal O_X\longrightarrow i_*\mathcal O_Z
$$

is surjective. Equivalently, every point of $X$ has an affine neighborhood $\operatorname{Spec}A$ on which $i$ is isomorphic to $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ for some [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) $I\subseteq A$.

A [closed subscheme](../../../../../../closed-subscheme.md) of $X$ is a scheme $Z$ supplied with a closed immersion $Z\hookrightarrow X$, considered up to isomorphism over $X$.
