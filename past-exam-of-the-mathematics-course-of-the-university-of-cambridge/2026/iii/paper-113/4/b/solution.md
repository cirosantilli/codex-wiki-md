<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On an affine open $W=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$, write $Z\cap W=V(I)$. Give $Z\cap W$ the [reduced induced subscheme](../../../../../../reduced-induced-subscheme.md) structure $\operatorname{Spec}(A/\sqrt I)$. These constructions agree under localization and therefore glue to a reduced closed subscheme $Z\hookrightarrow X$.

Let $Z'\hookrightarrow X$ be another [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md) with the same underlying closed set. Affine-locally write $Z'=\operatorname{Spec}(A/J)$. Since $V(J)=Z\cap W$, its vanishing ideal is $\sqrt J=\sqrt I$, and the inclusion $J\subseteq\sqrt J$ induces a quotient homomorphism

$$
A/J\longrightarrow A/\sqrt J.
$$

Contravariance of the [spectrum of a commutative ring](../../../../../../spectrum-of-a-commutative-ring.md) gives a factorization

$$
Z\cap W\longrightarrow Z'\cap W\longrightarrow W.
$$

The quotient maps force these local factorizations to agree on overlaps, so they glue. They are also the only possible maps over $X$, which proves uniqueness.
