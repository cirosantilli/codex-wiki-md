The [Dirichlet form of a Markov chain](../../../../../../../dirichlet-form-of-a-markov-chain.md) is

$$
\mathcal E(f,g)=\frac12\sum_{x,y\in S}
\pi(x)P(x,y)(f(x)-f(y))(g(x)-g(y)).
$$

When $P$ is [reversible](../../../../../../../reversible-markov-chain.md), this equals $\langle f,(I-P)g\rangle_\pi$.
