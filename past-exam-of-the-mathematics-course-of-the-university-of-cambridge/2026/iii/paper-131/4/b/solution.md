The [Cheeger-Gromoll splitting theorem](../../../../../../cheeger-gromoll-splitting-theorem.md) states that a complete connected [Riemannian manifold](../../../../../../riemannian-manifold.md) with nonnegative [Ricci curvature](../../../../../../ricci-curvature.md) that contains a [line in a Riemannian manifold](../../../../../../line-in-a-riemannian-manifold.md) is isometric to a [Riemannian product](../../../../../../riemannian-product.md)

$$
N\times\mathbb R.
$$

The [Hadamard-Cartan theorem](../../../../../../cartan-hadamard-theorem.md) states that if a complete simply connected Riemannian manifold has nonpositive [sectional curvature](../../../../../../sectional-curvature.md), then for every point $p$ its [exponential map](../../../../../../exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md)

$$
\exp_p:T_pM\longrightarrow M
$$

is a diffeomorphism. In particular, the manifold is diffeomorphic to Euclidean space and is [contractible](../../../../../../contractible-space.md).
