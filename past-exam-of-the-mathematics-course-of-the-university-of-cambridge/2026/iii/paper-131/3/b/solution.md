<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Yes to both questions.** Since $\omega_g$ has top degree, $d\omega_g=0$. Moreover $*\omega_g=1$, so the formula for the [codifferential](../../../../../../codifferential.md) gives

$$
\delta\omega_g=\pm *d*\omega_g=\pm *d1=0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\Delta\omega_g=(d\delta+\delta d)\omega_g=0,
$$

and the [Riemannian volume form](../../../../../../riemannian-volume-form.md) is a [harmonic differential form](../../../../../../harmonic-differential-form.md).

The [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) preserves both the [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md) and its chosen [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md). At any point, extend a positively oriented orthonormal basis to a local frame whose covariant derivatives vanish at that point. Differentiating $\omega_g(e_1,\ldots,e_n)=1$ there gives $\nabla\omega_g=0$. Hence $\omega_g$ is a [parallel differential form](../../../../../../parallel-differential-form.md).
