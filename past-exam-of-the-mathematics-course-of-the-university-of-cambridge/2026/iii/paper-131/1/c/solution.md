<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The metric $g$ is [geodesically complete](../../../../../../geodesic-completeness.md) when every maximal affinely parametrized [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) is defined on all of $\mathbb R$; equivalently, $\exp_p$ is defined on every $T_pM$ for every $p\in M$.

The [Hopf-Rinow theorem](../../../../../../hopf-rinow-theorem.md) says that for a connected [Riemannian manifold](../../../../../../riemannian-manifold.md), the following are equivalent: geodesic completeness; completeness of the [Riemannian distance](../../../../../../riemannian-distance.md) $d_g$; compactness of every closed bounded subset; and the existence, between every two points, of a length-minimizing geodesic. It is enough in the exponential-map formulation that $\exp_p$ be defined on all of $T_pM$ for one point $p$.
