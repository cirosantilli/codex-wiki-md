Near-infrared light is dominated over much of the [Milky Way](../../../../../../milky-way.md) by old, cool, low-mass stars, with extra emission from young stars and hot dust in active regions. Because [interstellar dust](../../../../../../interstellar-dust.md) attenuates it much less strongly than optical light, near-infrared surface brightness and star counts are useful tracers of stellar mass and the obscured Galactic bulge and bar.
