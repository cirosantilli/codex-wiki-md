↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Optical continuum light maps photospheric emission from stars and therefore depends on stellar density, luminosity, temperature, age, and composition. Recombination and forbidden lines additionally trace warm ionized gas. Strong wavelength-dependent absorption and scattering by [interstellar dust](../../../../../../interstellar-dust.md) obscure the inner disk, so an optical map is also a map of the foreground extinction.
