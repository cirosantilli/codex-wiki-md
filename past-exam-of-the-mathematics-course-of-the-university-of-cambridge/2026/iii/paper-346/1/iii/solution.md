↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Low rotational transitions of carbon monoxide are excited by collisions in cold [molecular clouds](../../../../../../molecular-cloud.md). CO survives in shielded gas and is readily observable, so the [carbon-monoxide tracer of molecular hydrogen](../../../../../../carbon-monoxide-tracer-of-molecular-hydrogen.md) uses its integrated intensity with $X_{\rm CO}$ to infer the otherwise difficult-to-observe $\mathrm H_2$ column. Line ratios also constrain excitation temperature and density, but the dominant large-scale use is to map molecular-gas mass and velocity.
