<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Translation and rotation invariance make the two-point function depend only on $|x_{12}|$. Scale covariance then fixes its power, and inversion or special-conformal invariance requires equal dimensions for a nonzero scalar two-point function. For identical [scalar primary operators](../../../../../../../primary-field.md),

$$
\boxed{\langle O(x_1)O(x_2)\rangle
=\frac{C_2}{|x_{12}|^{2\Delta}}}.
$$

Only the normalization $C_2$ depends on the operator convention.
