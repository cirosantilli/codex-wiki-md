Under the [AdS/CFT correspondence](../../../../../../../ads-cft-correspondence.md), each [single-trace operator](../../../../../../../single-trace-operator.md) is represented by one bulk field. The leading two-point [Witten diagram](../../../../../../../witten-diagram.md) is a bulk propagator joining the two boundary insertions, equivalently two bulk-to-boundary legs contracted through the quadratic bulk action. The leading connected three-point diagram has one bulk cubic vertex integrated over AdS and three bulk-to-boundary propagators ending at $x_1,x_2,x_3$. The bulk kinetic normalization fixes $C_2$, while the cubic coupling fixes $C_{123}$.
