<h1 id="2/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose an [algebra norm](../../../../../../../algebra-norm.md) made $C(\mathbb R)$ a [Banach algebra](../../../../../../../banach-algebra-split.md). Its unital [character space of an algebra](../../../../../../../character-space-of-an-algebra.md) would be compact in the [Gelfand topology](../../../../../../../gelfand-topology.md). By part i it consists of the evaluations $\delta_x$. The map

$$
x\longmapsto\delta_x
$$

is continuous from the usual topology because every $h\in C(\mathbb R)$ is continuous, and its inverse is the continuous map $\varphi\mapsto\varphi(t)$ defined by the coordinate function $t$. Thus the character space is homeomorphic to the noncompact space $\mathbb R$, a contradiction. No complete algebra norm exists.
