A [character of an algebra](../../../../../../character-of-an-algebra.md) is a nonzero multiplicative complex-linear functional $\varphi:A\to\mathbb C$. The [character space of an algebra](../../../../../../character-space-of-an-algebra.md) is

$$
\Phi_A=\{\varphi:A\to\mathbb C:\varphi\text{ is a character}\}.
$$

If $A$ is unital, multiplicativity and nonzeroness force $\varphi(1)=1$.
