<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a compact Hausdorff space $K$, every character of the [Banach algebra](../../../../../../banach-algebra-split.md) $C(K)$ is an [evaluation character](../../../../../../evaluation-character.md)

$$
\delta_x(f)=f(x)
$$

at a unique $x\in K$. The map $x\mapsto\delta_x$ is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md) from $K$ onto $\Phi_{C(K)}$ with its [Gelfand topology](../../../../../../gelfand-topology.md).
