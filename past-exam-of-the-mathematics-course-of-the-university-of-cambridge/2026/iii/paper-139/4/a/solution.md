<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A multiplicatively closed set $S$ is a [left Ore set](../../../../../../left-ore-set.md) if for every $s\in S$ and $r\in R$ there are $s'\in S,r'\in R$ with $s'r=r's$. This condition gives common left annihilators, so $t_S(M)$ is closed under addition and scalar multiplication in every module.

Conversely apply the assumed submodule property to $M=R/Rs$. The element $1+Rs$ is $S$-torsion, hence so is $r+Rs$. Thus some $s'\in S$ satisfies $s'r\in Rs$, say $s'r=r's$, which is precisely the Ore condition.
