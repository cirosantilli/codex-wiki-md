<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $K_M=-\partial^2+M^2$ and let $\Delta_M=K_M^{-1}$ be its Euclidean [quantum field theory propagator](../../../../../../../propagator.md). With the source-sign convention suited to the expression in the question, define

$$
Z_0[J]=N_0\int\mathcal D\chi\,
\exp\left[-S_2[\chi]-\int d^dx\,J(x)\chi(x)\right]
=\exp\left[\frac12\int d^dx\,d^dy\,
J(x)\Delta_M(x-y)J(y)\right],
$$

where $N_0$ makes $Z_0[0]=1$. Since inserting $\chi(x)$ is equivalent to acting with $-\delta/\delta J(x)$, the [integral over $\chi$](../../../../../../../integrating-out-a-field.md) gives

$$
\boxed{W[\phi]=S_1[\phi]-\log\left.
\left\{\exp\left[-S_3\left(\phi,-\frac{\delta}{\delta J}\right)\right]Z_0[J]\right\}\right|_{J=0}.}
$$
