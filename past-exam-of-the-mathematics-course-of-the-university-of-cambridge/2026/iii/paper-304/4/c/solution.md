<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the gauge-fixing and ghost action as the [BRST transformation](../../../../../../brst-symmetry.md) of the [gauge-fixing fermion](../../../../../../gauge-fixing-fermion.md):

$$
S_{\rm gf}+S_{\rm gh}=s\int d^4x\,
\bar c^a\left(n^\mu A_\mu^a-\frac\xi2B^a\right),
$$

with harmless rescalings of $B$ accommodating the convention $s\bar c^a=\xi B^a$ used in the question. The gauge-invariant action obeys $sS[A]=0$, while nilpotence gives

$$
s(S_{\rm gf}+S_{\rm gh})=s^2\Psi_n=0.
$$

**Thus the entire gauge-fixed action is [BRST invariant](../../../../../../brst-symmetry.md). Eliminating the auxiliary field $B^a$ by its algebraic field equation reproduces the axial gauge-fixing term and the ghost action found in part (b).**
