↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first two terms are the [kinetic term](../../../../../../kinetic-term.md) and [mass term](../../../../../../mass-term.md) of a [real scalar field](../../../../../../real-scalar-field.md); together they determine the free [quantum field theory propagator](../../../../../../propagator.md). The term $-\lambda\phi^4/4!$ is its quartic [field interaction term](../../../../../../field-interaction-term.md). The remaining three terms are [counterterms](../../../../../../counterterm.md): $\delta_Z$ renormalizes the field normalization, $\delta_m$ renormalizes the mass, and $\delta_\lambda$ renormalizes the quartic coupling. Their regulator dependence cancels the ultraviolet divergences of loop diagrams, while their finite parts implement the chosen [renormalization conditions](../../../../../../renormalization-condition.md).
