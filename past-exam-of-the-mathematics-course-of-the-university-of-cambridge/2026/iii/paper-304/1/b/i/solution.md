↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Through order $\lambda^2$, the quartic [one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagrams](../../../../../../../one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md) are the tree-level four-point vertex, the local $\delta_\lambda$ counterterm vertex, and three one-loop bubble diagrams. The bubbles have two quartic vertices and differ by whether the momentum through the loop is the $s$, $t$, or $u$ [Mandelstam variables](../../../../../../../mandelstam-variables.md); each has symmetry factor $1/2$. External-leg self-energies are not part of the 1PI four-point vertex, and $\delta_Z$ begins beyond the required order in this theory.
