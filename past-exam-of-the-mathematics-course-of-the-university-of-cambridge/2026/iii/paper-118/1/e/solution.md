↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The form $iF_\nabla$ is the [Ricci form of a Kähler manifold](../../../../../../ricci-form-of-a-kahler-manifold.md). In complex dimension one, $iF_\nabla=K_g\omega_X=\frac12\operatorname{Scal}_g\omega_X$. Thus

$$
\lambda=K_g=\frac12\operatorname{Scal}_g,
$$

where $K_g$ is the Gaussian curvature.
