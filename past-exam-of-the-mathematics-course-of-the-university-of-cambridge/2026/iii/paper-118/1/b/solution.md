↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [adjunction formula](../../../../../../adjunction-formula.md), $K_X\cong(K_{\mathbb{CP}^2}\otimes\mathcal O(d))|_X$, and $K_{\mathbb{CP}^2}\cong\mathcal O(-3)$ give

$$
K_X\cong\mathcal O_X(d-3).
$$

If $h=c_1(\mathcal O(1))$, then

$$
c_1(TX)=-c_1(K_X)=(3-d)h|_X\in H^2(X;\mathbb Z).
$$

With the standard integral normalization of the [Fubini-Study form](../../../../../../fubini-study-form.md), this becomes $c_1(TX)=(3-d)[\omega_{FS}|_X/(2\pi)]$ in real cohomology.
