<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [Kähler manifold](../../../../../../kahler-manifold.md) has a real closed $(1,1)$-form $\omega$ for which $g(u,v)=\omega(u,Jv)$ is positive definite. The [Fubini-Study form](../../../../../../fubini-study-form.md) $\omega_{FS}$ has these properties on $\mathbb{CP}^2$. Pullback by the holomorphic inclusion $i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb{CP}^2$ preserves reality, type, and closedness, while positivity restricts to every nonzero vector in $TX$. Hence $\omega_X=i^*\omega_{FS}$ is a Kähler form on $X$.
