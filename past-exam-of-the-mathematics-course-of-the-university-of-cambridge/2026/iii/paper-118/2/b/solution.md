<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The forms $dz_I\wedge d\overline z_J$ are parallel for the flat metric. Consequently the Hodge Laplacian acts coefficientwise:

$$
\Delta\alpha=\sum_{I,J}(\Delta\alpha_{I,J}),dz_I\wedge d\overline z_J.
$$

If every coefficient is constant, this vanishes. Conversely, if $\Delta\alpha=0$, orthogonality of the constant frame gives $\Delta\alpha_{I,J}=0$ for every $I,J$. Each coefficient is a harmonic function on a compact connected manifold and hence is constant by the [strong maximum principle for harmonic functions](../../../../../../strong-maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md).
