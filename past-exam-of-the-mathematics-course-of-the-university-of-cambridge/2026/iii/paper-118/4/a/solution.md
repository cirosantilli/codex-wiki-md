↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Dolbeault cohomology](../../../../../../dolbeault-cohomology.md) group is

$$
H^{p,q}_{\bar\partial}(X)=\ker(\bar\partial:\Omega^{p,q}\to\Omega^{p,q+1})/\operatorname{im}(\bar\partial:\Omega^{p,q-1}\to\Omega^{p,q}).
$$

The complex orientation of the codimension-$r$ submanifold $Z$ gives an integral Poincaré-dual class $[Z]\in H^{2r}(X;\mathbb Z)$. Integration over $Z$ defines a closed current of type $(r,r)$, so under the Dolbeault identification its complexification belongs to $H^{r,r}_{\bar\partial}(X)$.
