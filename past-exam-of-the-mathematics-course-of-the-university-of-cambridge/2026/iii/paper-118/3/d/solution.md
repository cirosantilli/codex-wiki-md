<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose a local holomorphic frame $e$ of the [holomorphic line bundle](../../../../../../holomorphic-line-bundle.md) $E$ and define

$$
\bar\partial_E(fe)=\bar\partial f\otimes e.
$$

If $e'=ge$ is another holomorphic frame, then $g$ is nowhere-zero and holomorphic, so $\bar\partial g=0$ and the two definitions agree. They therefore glue to a well-defined partial connection. In each holomorphic frame its square is ordinary $\bar\partial^2$, hence $\bar\partial_E^2=0$.
