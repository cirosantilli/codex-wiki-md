↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Complexifying the cotangent bundle of an [almost complex manifold](../../../../../../almost-complex-manifold.md) gives the $\pm i$ eigenspace decomposition $T^*X\otimes\mathbb C=T^{1,0*}X\oplus T^{0,1*}X$. Define

$$
\bigwedge^{p,q}T^*X=\bigwedge^pT^{1,0*}X\otimes\bigwedge^qT^{0,1*}X.
$$

For a form $\alpha$ of type $(p,q)$, the [Dolbeault operator](../../../../../../dolbeault-operator.md) is the type-$(p,q+1)$ projection of $d\alpha$:

$$
\boxed{\bar\partial\alpha=\operatorname{pr}_{p,q+1}(d\alpha).}
$$
