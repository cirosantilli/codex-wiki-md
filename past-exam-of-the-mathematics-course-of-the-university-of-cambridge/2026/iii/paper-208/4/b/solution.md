↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The same edge exposure shows that $f$ has bounded differences constants $c_e=1$ for its $m=\binom n2$ independent inputs. [McDiarmid inequality](../../../../../../mcdiarmid-s-inequality.md) therefore gives both one-sided bounds

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(f(G)-\mathbb Ef(G)\geq t),
\quad
\mathbb P(f(G)-\mathbb Ef(G)\leq-t)
\leq\exp\!\left(-\frac{2t^2}{\binom n2}\right).}
$$
