<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For

$$
\psi'=A(x,y,t)\cos\frac{n\pi z}{H},
$$

the surface value is simply $\psi'(0)=A$. The dynamic free-surface condition from part i gives

$$
\boxed{
\eta(x,y,t)=\frac{f_0}{g}A(x,y,t)}.
$$

The cosine is the leading small-surface-displacement approximation to the exact [quasi-geostrophic vertical mode](../../../../../../../quasi-geostrophic-vertical-mode.md); its derivative vanishes at both rigid-boundary locations.
