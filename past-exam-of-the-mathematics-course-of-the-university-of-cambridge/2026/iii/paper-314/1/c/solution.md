<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $V=abc$ and write $T=K V^{-(\gamma-1)}$ for a constant $K$. The axis equations are Newton equations in the effective potential

$$
U(a,b,c)=\frac12\Omega^2(a^2+b^2+c^2)
+\frac{2T}{\gamma-1},
$$

because

$$
-\frac{\partial}{\partial a}
\left(\frac{2T}{\gamma-1}\right)=\frac{2T}{a},
$$

and similarly for $b,c$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E=\frac12(\dot a^2+\dot b^2+\dot c^2)
+\frac12\Omega^2(a^2+b^2+c^2)
+\frac{2T}{\gamma-1}}
$$

is conserved. Multiplying by the fixed profile-dependent mass moment converts $\mathcal E$ into the physical kinetic, trapping-potential, and internal energy of the star, so it is proportional to total energy.
