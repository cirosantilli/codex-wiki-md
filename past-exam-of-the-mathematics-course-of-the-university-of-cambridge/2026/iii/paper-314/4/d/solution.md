<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Define the poloidal [Alfvén number](../../../../../../alfven-number.md) by

$$
\boxed{A^2=\frac{\mu_0\rho u_p^2}{B_p^2}
=\frac{\mu_0k^2}{\rho}},
$$

where $u_p=kB_p/\rho$. Thus, along a given field line,

$$
\boxed{\rho=\frac{\mu_0k^2}{A^2}};
$$

density decreases as the Alfvén number grows.

The azimuthal velocity representation gives

$$
u_\phi=r\omega+\frac{kB_\phi}{\rho}.
$$

Substitution into $\ell=ru_\phi-rB_\phi/(\mu_0k)$ yields

$$
\ell=r^2\omega+\frac{rB_\phi}{\mu_0k}(A^2-1).
$$

At the [Alfvén surface](../../../../../../alfven-surface.md), $A^2=1$. Smoothness requires the numerator of the solution for $B_\phi$ to vanish there, so

$$
\ell=\omega r_a^2.
$$

Solving away from the surface then gives

$$
\boxed{B_\phi
=\frac{\mu_0k\omega}{r}
\frac{r^2-r_a^2}{1-A^2}}.
$$
