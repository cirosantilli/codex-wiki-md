<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Although the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) can exchange energy between poloidal and toroidal motion, it does no net work in the frame rotating with a magnetic surface. Dotting steady momentum with the poloidal field and using the mass-loading, isorotation, and angular-momentum invariants combines the magnetic work with the toroidal kinetic term to give

$$
\mathbf B_p\mathbin\cdot\nabla\widetilde\epsilon=0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\epsilon(\psi)
=\frac12u_p^2+\frac12(u_\phi-r\omega)^2
+\Phi_{cg}+h}
$$

is constant on each magnetic surface. Here

$$
\boxed{\Phi_{cg}=\Phi-\frac12\omega^2r^2}
$$

is the gravitational potential plus the centrifugal potential in the frame corotating at the field-line angular velocity. This is the modified [Bernoulli function](../../../../../../bernoulli-function.md) of the wind.
