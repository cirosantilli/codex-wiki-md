<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The representation

$$
\mathbf B_p=\nabla\psi\times\nabla\phi
$$

gives

$$
B_r=-\frac1r\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial z},
\qquad
B_z=\frac1r\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial r}.
$$

Hence $\mathbf B\mathbin\cdot\nabla\psi=0$: the level sets of the [poloidal magnetic flux function](../../../../../../poloidal-magnetic-flux-function.md) are magnetic surfaces. Moreover, the magnetic flux through a circular disc is

$$
\int_0^r2\pi r'B_z(r',z)\,dr'
=2\pi[\psi(r,z)-\psi(0,z)].
$$

**Thus $2\pi\psi$ is the enclosed poloidal magnetic flux up to an additive axial reference.**
