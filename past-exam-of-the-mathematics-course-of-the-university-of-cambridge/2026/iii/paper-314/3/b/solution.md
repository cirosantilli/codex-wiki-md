<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The quantities $B_x$ and $u_xB_y-u_yB_x=-E_z$ are each constant across the shock. A single tangential Galilean boost therefore changes the common $E_z$ to zero on both sides. This is the [de Hoffmann–Teller frame](../../../../../../de-hoffmann-teller-frame.md). Ideal MHD then gives

$$
\mathbf u_i\times\mathbf B_i=0,
\qquad
\boxed{\mathbf u_i\parallel\mathbf B_i}.
$$

Let $j=\rho u_x$ and retain the common $B_x$. The independent jump conditions simplify to

$$
[j]=0,
\qquad [B_x]=0,
$$



$$
\left[\rho u_x^2+p+\frac{B_y^2}{2\mu_0}\right]=0,
$$



$$
\left[\rho u_xu_y-\frac{B_xB_y}{\mu_0}\right]=0,
$$



$$
\left[\frac12u^2+h\right]=0,
\qquad
\frac{u_y}{u_x}=\frac{B_y}{B_x}.
$$

Terms involving the common $B_x^2$ cancel from normal momentum, and the electromagnetic term vanishes from energy because $E_z=0$.
