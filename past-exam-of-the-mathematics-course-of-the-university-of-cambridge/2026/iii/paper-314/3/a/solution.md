<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Integrate each conservative [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../../../ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md) law through a thin pillbox around the stationary shock. Mass conservation gives

$$
[\rho u_x]=0.
$$

The MHD momentum-flux tensor is

$$
\rho\mathbf u\mathbf u
+\left(p+\frac{B^2}{2\mu_0}\right)I
-\frac{\mathbf B\mathbf B}{\mu_0}.
$$

Its normal and tangential components give

$$
\left[\rho u_x^2+\Pi-\frac{B_x^2}{\mu_0}\right]=0,
\qquad
\left[\rho u_xu_y-\frac{B_xB_y}{\mu_0}\right]=0,
$$

where $\Pi=p+B^2/(2\mu_0)$ is total pressure. The solenoidal condition gives $[B_x]=0$, while steady [Faraday's law](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives continuity of tangential electric field,

$$
[-E_z]=[u_xB_y-u_yB_x]=0.
$$

Finally, material energy flux plus the normal [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) component gives

$$
\left[\rho u_x\left(\frac12u^2+h\right)
-\frac{E_zB_y}{\mu_0}\right]=0,
$$

where $h$ is [specific enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy.md) per unit mass.

Gravity is a bounded volume force, so its integral across a shock whose thickness tends to zero vanishes. Viscosity and resistivity inside the layer may produce entropy; consequently entropy flux need not be equal on both sides, although the second law requires nonnegative net entropy production.
