<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the aligned frame, define the [Alfvén number](../../../../../../alfven-number.md)

$$
A_i^2=\frac{u_i^2}{v_{Ai}^2}
=\frac{\mu_0\rho_i u_{xi}^2}{B_x^2},
$$

where the second equality uses $\mathbf u_i\parallel\mathbf B_i$. Since $j=\rho_i u_{xi}$ and $B_x$ are common,

$$
A_i^2=\frac{\mu_0j u_{xi}}{B_x^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}
=\frac{u_{x1}}{u_{x2}}
=\frac{A_1^2}{A_2^2}}.
$$

Using alignment in the tangential momentum flux gives

$$
\rho u_xu_y-\frac{B_xB_y}{\mu_0}
=\frac{B_xB_y}{\mu_0}(A^2-1).
$$

Its continuity then yields

$$
B_{y2}(A_2^2-1)=B_{y1}(A_1^2-1),
$$

or

$$
\boxed{\frac{B_{y2}}{B_{y1}}
=\frac{A_1^2-1}{A_2^2-1}}
$$

whenever the ratios are determinate.
