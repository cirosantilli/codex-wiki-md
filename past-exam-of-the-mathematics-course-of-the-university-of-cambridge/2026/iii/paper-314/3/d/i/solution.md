<h1 id="3/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $B_{y1}=B_{y2}=0$, alignment also gives $u_{y1}=u_{y2}=0$. The common normal magnetic field contributes the same constant magnetic stress on both sides and carries no Poynting flux in the aligned frame. The remaining mass, normal-momentum, and energy conditions are therefore exactly

$$
[\rho u_x]=0,
\qquad
[\rho u_x^2+p]=0,
\qquad
\left[\frac12u_x^2+h\right]=0,
$$

which are the [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions for a perfect gas](../../../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md).
