<h1 id="3/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $B_{y2}=0$ but $B_{y1}\ne0$, tangential momentum continuity in the form

$$
B_{y2}(A_2^2-1)=B_{y1}(A_1^2-1)
$$

forces $A_1^2=1$. Since the normal components are positive,

$$
\boxed{A_1=1},
$$

so the upstream velocity equals the upstream [Alfvén speed](../../../../../../../alfven-speed.md). This is the limiting switch-off shock condition.
