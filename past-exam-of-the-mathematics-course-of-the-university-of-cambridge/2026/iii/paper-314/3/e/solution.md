<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Take $B_{y2}=-B_{y1}\ne0$ and choose

$$
A_1=A_2=1.
$$

Then both tangential momentum fluxes vanish, so that jump condition is satisfied. The density relation from part (c) gives $\rho_2=\rho_1$, and mass conservation gives $u_{x2}=u_{x1}$. Alignment makes $u_{y2}=-u_{y1}$, so $u_2^2=u_1^2$.

Because $B_{y2}^2=B_{y1}^2$, normal momentum now gives

$$
\boxed{p_2=p_1}.
$$

The energy condition is then automatic. Thus density and pressure are continuous while the tangential magnetic field and velocity reverse direction. This is a $180^\circ$ [rotational discontinuity in magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../../../rotational-discontinuity-in-magnetohydrodynamics.md), carrying no compression or entropy jump.
