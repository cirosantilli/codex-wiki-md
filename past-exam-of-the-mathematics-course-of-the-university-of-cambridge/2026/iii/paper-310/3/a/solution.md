Local energy-momentum conservation is the vanishing [covariant divergence](../../../../../../covariant-divergence.md) of the [energy-momentum tensor](../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla_\mu T^\mu{}_{\nu}=0}.
$$
