<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Every term in part (d) is a spatial [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md). Extracting the longitudinal scalar coefficient, up to a spatially homogeneous function that can be absorbed into the zero mode, yields the [Cosmological Euler equation in synchronous gauge](../../../../../../cosmological-euler-equation-in-synchronous-gauge.md):

$$
\boxed{
\delta P+\nabla^2\pi^S
+\partial_0[(\bar\rho+\bar P)\delta u]
+3H(\bar\rho+\bar P)\delta u=0}.
$$
