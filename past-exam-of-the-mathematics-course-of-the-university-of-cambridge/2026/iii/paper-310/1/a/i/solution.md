In natural units, the equilibrium [phase-space distribution function](../../../../../../../phase-space-distribution-function.md) is

$$
f(\mathbf p)=\frac{1}{\exp[(E(\mathbf p)-\mu)/T]\mp1},
\qquad E(\mathbf p)=\sqrt{\mathbf p^2+m^2},
$$

where the minus sign gives the [Bose-Einstein distribution](../../../../../../../bose-einstein-distribution.md) for [bosons](../../../../../../../boson.md) and the plus sign gives the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) for [fermions](../../../../../../../fermion.md).
