↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [entropy density](../../../../../../../entropy-density.md) is

$$
s=\frac{\rho+P-\mu n}{T}.
$$

For the homogeneous cosmological plasma, adiabaticity gives the [cosmological entropy conservation](../../../../../../../cosmological-entropy-conservation.md) equation

$$
\nabla_\mu(su^\mu)=0,
\qquad
\dot s+3Hs=0,
$$

or equivalently $sa^3=\text{constant}$ in a comoving volume.
