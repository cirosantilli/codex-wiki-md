↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [constant-equation-of-state density scaling](../../../../../../../constant-equation-of-state-density-scaling.md) gives

$$
\bar\rho_D\propto a^{-3(1+w)}=a^{-1}.
$$

For a spatially flat universe dominated by this component, the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $H^2\propto a^{-1}$. Integrating $\dot a/a\propto a^{-1/2}$ yields

$$
\boxed{a(t)\propto(t-t_*)^2,
\qquad H(t)=\frac{2}{t-t_*}}.
$$

This is accelerated expansion because $w=-2/3<-1/3$.
