↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Comparing the stated curvature [power spectrum](../../../../../../power-spectrum.md) with $P_{\mathcal R}=2\pi^2A_s/k^3$ at the pivot scale gives the [primordial scalar amplitude](../../../../../../primordial-scalar-amplitude.md)

$$
A_s=\frac{H^2}{8\pi^2\epsilon_VM_{\rm Pl}^2}
\simeq
\boxed{\frac{\Lambda^{12}}
{12\pi^2M_{\rm Pl}^6m^4\phi^2}}.
$$

The [scalar spectral index](../../../../../../scalar-spectral-index.md) is

$$
n_s-1\simeq-6\epsilon_V+2\eta_V
\simeq
\boxed{-\frac{3M_{\rm Pl}^2m^4\phi^2}{\Lambda^8}
-\frac{2M_{\rm Pl}^2m^2}{\Lambda^4}}.
$$

Sufficiently close to the hilltop, $\epsilon_V$ is suppressed by $\phi^2$ and the $\eta_V$ term controls the [spectral tilt](../../../../../../scalar-spectral-index.md).
