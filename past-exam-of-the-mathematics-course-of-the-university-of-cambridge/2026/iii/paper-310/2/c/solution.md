<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The homogeneous [inflaton](../../../../../../inflaton.md) obeys

$$
\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi+V'(\phi)=0.
$$

Under the [slow-roll approximation](../../../../../../slow-roll-approximation.md), the acceleration and kinetic-energy corrections may be neglected, so the field equation and [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) become

$$
3H\dot\phi\simeq-V'=m^2\phi,
\qquad
H^2\simeq\frac{V}{3M_{\rm Pl}^2}
\simeq\frac{\Lambda^4}{3M_{\rm Pl}^2}.
$$

**Thus a positive field rolls away from the hilltop while $H$ is approximately constant.**
