<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [hilltop inflation](../../../../../../hilltop-inflation.md) potential,

$$
V'=-m^2\phi,
\qquad
V''=-m^2.
$$

The two [potential slow-roll parameters](../../../../../../potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) are consequently

$$
\epsilon_V
=\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2m^4\phi^2}
{2(\Lambda^4-\tfrac12m^2\phi^2)^2}
\simeq\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2m^4\phi^2}{2\Lambda^8}
$$

and

$$
\eta_V
=-\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2m^2}{\Lambda^4-\tfrac12m^2\phi^2}
\simeq-\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2m^2}{\Lambda^4}.
$$
