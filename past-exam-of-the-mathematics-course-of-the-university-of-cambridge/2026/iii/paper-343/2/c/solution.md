<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In a local gapped ground state, correlations and the entanglement needed to lower a local interaction energy are concentrated over a finite distance. Degrees of freedom deep inside a region use most of their entanglement with nearby degrees of freedom that are also inside; [entanglement monogamy](../../../../../../entanglement-monogamy.md) limits their simultaneous entanglement with the exterior. Only degrees of freedom within a correlation length of the boundary can contribute extensively across the cut, giving an [entanglement area law](../../../../../../entanglement-area-law.md)

$$
S(A)=O(|\partial A|).
$$

Monogamy supplies the physical mechanism, while locality and suitable ground-state assumptions are essential; monogamy alone is not a general mathematical proof of an area law.
