A [quantum channel](../../../../../../quantum-channel.md) is a linear completely positive trace-preserving map. Its [Kraus representation](../../../../../../kraus-representation.md) is

$$
\mathcal E(\rho)=\sum_\alpha K_\alpha\rho K_\alpha^\dagger,
\qquad
\boxed{\sum_\alpha K_\alpha^\dagger K_\alpha=I}.
$$

Kraus operators are not unique: two representations of the same channel are related by an isometry on the Kraus index, and by a unitary when both lists are minimal and equally long. A differentiable Markovian family of infinitesimal channels gives the [Lindblad equation](../../../../../../lindblad-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot\rho=-i[H,\rho]
+\sum_\alpha\left(
L_\alpha\rho L_\alpha^\dagger
-\frac12\{L_\alpha^\dagger L_\alpha,\rho\}
\right).}
$$
