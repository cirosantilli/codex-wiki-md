<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Although the physical symmetry generators commute,

$$
V_aV_b=\sigma_x\sigma_z=-\sigma_z\sigma_x=-V_bV_a.
$$

Their virtual commutator is

$$
\boxed{
V_aV_bV_a^{-1}V_b^{-1}=-I}.
$$

This phase cannot be removed by rephasing $V_a$ and $V_b$. The virtual matrices therefore form a nontrivial projective representation, which is the nontrivial [projective virtual symmetry of a matrix product state](../../../../../../../projective-virtual-symmetry-of-a-matrix-product-state.md) and proves that the [cluster state](../../../../../../../cluster-state.md) lies in a nontrivial SPT phase.
