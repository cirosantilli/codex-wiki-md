<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [fundamental weights](../../../../../../fundamental-weight.md) satisfy $2(\omega_i,\alpha_j)/(\alpha_j,\alpha_j)=\delta_{ij}$. Since $|\alpha_1|^2=1$ and $|\alpha_2|^2=2$, solving gives

$$
\omega_1=\left(\frac12,\frac12\right),
\qquad \omega_2=(0,1).
$$

The full [B2 root system](../../../../../../b2-root-system.md) is

$$
\{(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1),(\pm1,\pm1)\}.
$$

The [weight lattice](../../../../../../weight-lattice.md) is generated by $\omega_1,omega_2$; geometrically it consists of the integer lattice together with the translate in which both coordinates are half-integers.
