↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [Fundamental representations of B2](../../../../../../fundamental-representations-of-b2.md) have weight sets

$$
d_{[1,0]}:\quad
\left\{\left(\frac12,\frac12\right),
\left(\frac12,-\frac12\right),
\left(-\frac12,\frac12\right),
\left(-\frac12,-\frac12\right)\right\},
$$

so $\dim d_{[1,0]}=4$, and

$$
d_{[0,1]}:\quad
\{(1,0),(-1,0),(0,1),(0,-1),(0,0)\},
$$

so $\dim d_{[0,1]}=5$. The [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) has all eight roots as nonzero weights and zero with multiplicity two. Its [highest root](../../../../../../highest-root.md) is

$$
2\alpha_1+\alpha_2=(1,1)=2\omega_1,
$$

so its highest-weight label is $[2,0]$ and its dimension is $8+2=10$.
