<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Parity preserves the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) but reverses orientation. Hence ordinary tensor contraction makes

$$
C_1=M_{\mu\nu}M^{\mu\nu}
$$

parity even, while the Levi-Civita pseudotensor changes sign and makes

$$
C_2=M_{\mu\nu}\widetilde M^{\mu\nu}
$$

parity odd. Equivalently, $C_1$ is proportional to $\mathbf J^2-\mathbf K^2$ and $C_2$ to $\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf K$. The combinations $C_1\pm iC_2$ are proportional to the two quadratic [Lorentz Casimir invariants](../../../../../../lorentz-casimir-invariants.md), and parity exchanges them exactly as it exchanges the two factors in the complexified algebra.
