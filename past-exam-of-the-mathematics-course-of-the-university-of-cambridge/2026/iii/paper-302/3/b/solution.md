<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Killing form](../../../../../../killing-form.md) is $\kappa(X,Y)=\operatorname{Tr}(\operatorname{ad}_X\operatorname{ad}_Y)$. From part (a),

$$
\begin{aligned}
\kappa([X,Y],Z)
&=\operatorname{Tr}([\operatorname{ad}_X,\operatorname{ad}_Y]\operatorname{ad}_Z)\\
&=\operatorname{Tr}(\operatorname{ad}_X[\operatorname{ad}_Y,\operatorname{ad}_Z])\\
&=\kappa(X,[Y,Z]),
\end{aligned}
$$

where the middle equality uses cyclicity of the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md).
