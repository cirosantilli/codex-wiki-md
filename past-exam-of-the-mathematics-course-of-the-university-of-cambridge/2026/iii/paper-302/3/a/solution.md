↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is

$$
\operatorname{ad}:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak{gl}(\mathfrak g),
\qquad \operatorname{ad}_X(Y)=[X,Y].
$$

It is linear. The [Jacobi identity](../../../../../../jacobi-identity.md) gives

$$
[\operatorname{ad}_X,\operatorname{ad}_Y]Z
=[X,[Y,Z]]-[Y,[X,Z]]=[[X,Y],Z]
=\operatorname{ad}_{[X,Y]}Z,
$$

so it is a [Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md).
