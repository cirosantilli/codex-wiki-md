<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $W\subseteq\mathfrak g$ be invariant under the [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Then $[\mathfrak g,W]\subseteq W$, so $W$ is an ideal. If $\mathfrak g$ is a [simple Lie algebra](../../../../../../simple-lie-algebra.md), its only ideals are $0$ and $\mathfrak g$. Hence the adjoint representation is irreducible. Compact type is compatible with the anti-Hermitian realization used below, although simplicity alone proves this [adjoint irreducibility of a simple Lie algebra](../../../../../../adjoint-irreducibility-of-a-simple-lie-algebra.md).
