<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because $v$ is a [divergence-free vector field](../../../../../../../divergence-free-vector-field.md), the [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
\nabla\mathbin\cdot(v\eta\chi)
=(v\mathbin\cdot\nabla\eta)\chi
+(v\mathbin\cdot\nabla\chi)\eta.
$$

The integral of a divergence over the [periodic domain](../../../../../../../periodic-domain.md) vanishes. Hence [integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\int_\Omega((v\mathbin\cdot\nabla)\eta)\chi\,dx
=-\int_\Omega((v\mathbin\cdot\nabla)\chi)\eta\,dx}.
$$

This is the [skew-symmetry of incompressible transport](../../../../../../../skew-symmetry-of-incompressible-transport.md).
