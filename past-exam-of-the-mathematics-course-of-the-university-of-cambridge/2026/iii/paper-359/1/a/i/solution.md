<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [sobolev embedding theorem](../../../../../../../sobolev-embedding-theorem.md) in three dimensions and the [periodic elliptic estimate](../../../../../../../periodic-elliptic-estimate.md) for the [Stokes operator](../../../../../../../stokes-operator.md) give

$$
\|(v\mathbin\cdot\nabla)\eta\|_2
\leq \|v\|_\infty\|\nabla\eta\|_2
\leq C\|v\|_{H^2}\|\eta\|_{H^1}
\leq C|Av|\|\eta\|_{H^1}.
$$

The last step uses the absence of the zero Fourier mode: on mean-zero periodic fields, the [Poincaré inequality](../../../../../../../poincare-inequality.md) makes the homogeneous $H^2$ norm controlled by $\|\Delta v\|_2=|Av|$.
