A [minimal primary decomposition](../../../../../../minimal-primary-decomposition.md) is an expression

$$
I=Q_1\cap\cdots\cap Q_s
$$

in which every $Q_i$ is a [primary ideal](../../../../../../primary-ideal.md), the prime ideals $\sqrt{Q_i}$ are pairwise distinct, and the decomposition is irredundant: deleting any $Q_i$ changes the intersection.
