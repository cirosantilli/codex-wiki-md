↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**No.** Take the [Noetherian ring](../../../../../../noetherian-ring.md) $R=k[x]$, the finitely generated $R$-module

$$
M=R\oplus R/(x),
$$

and $N=0$. Its [annihilator of a module](../../../../../../annihilator-of-a-module.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Ann}_R(M)=0,
$$

which is a [prime ideal](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md) and hence a [primary ideal](../../../../../../primary-ideal.md). But with $r=x$ and $m=(0,\overline1)$ we have $m\ne0$ and $rm=0$, while $x^kM\ne0$ for every $k$ because the free summand survives. Thus $N$ is not a [primary submodule](../../../../../../primary-submodule.md).
