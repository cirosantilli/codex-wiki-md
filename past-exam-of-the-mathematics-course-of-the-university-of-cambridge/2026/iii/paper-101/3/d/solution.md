<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

If the coefficients of $p$ are integral over $R$, they generate a finite $R$-algebra $B$. Then $B[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$ is a finite $R[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$-module, so every one of its elements, including $p$, is integral.

Conversely, use the fact that the [integral closure of a graded ring is graded](../../../../../../integral-closure-of-a-graded-ring-is-graded.md). Give $A[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$ its $\mathbb N^n$-grading and regard $R[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$ as a graded subring. If $p$ is integral, each homogeneous component $a_\alpha t^\alpha$ is integral. Applying the evaluation homomorphism $t_1=\cdots=t_n=1$ shows that every coefficient $a_\alpha$ is integral over $R$.
