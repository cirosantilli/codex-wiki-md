↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Going-down theorem](../../../../../../going-down-theorem.md) states: let $R\subseteq A$ be an [integral extension](../../../../../../integral-extension.md) of [integral domains](../../../../../../integral-domain.md), with $R$ integrally closed in its [fraction field](../../../../../../field-of-fractions.md). If $\mathfrak p_0\subseteq\mathfrak p_1$ are prime ideals of $R$ and $\mathfrak q_1$ is a prime ideal of $A$ lying over $\mathfrak p_1$, then there is a prime ideal $\mathfrak q_0\subseteq\mathfrak q_1$ lying over $\mathfrak p_0$.
