<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Two decreasing [filtrations of a module](../../../../../../filtration-of-a-module.md) $(M_n)$ and $(M'_n)$ are equivalent when each contains a fixed shift of the other: there are $a,b\ge0$ such that

$$
M_{n+a}\subseteq M'_n,
\qquad
M'_{n+b}\subseteq M_n
$$

for every $n\ge0$.

For an $I$-filtration, $IM_n\subseteq M_{n+1}$, so $I^nM_0\subseteq M_n$. If it is stable from $s$ onward, then

$$
M_{s+n}=I^nM_s\subseteq I^nM_0.
$$

It is therefore equivalent to the [I-adic filtration](../../../../../../i-adic-filtration.md). Any two stable $I$-filtrations are consequently equivalent to each other.
