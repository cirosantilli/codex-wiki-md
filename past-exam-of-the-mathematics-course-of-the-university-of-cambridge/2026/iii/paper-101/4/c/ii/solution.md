**Yes.** Scalar multiplication in the [quotient module](../../../../../../../quotient-module.md) gives directly

$$
R_n(M/N)=\{r(m+N):r\in R_n,m\in M\}
=(R_nM+N)/N=(M_n+N)/N.
$$

**Hence the displayed filtration is precisely the induced filtration.**
