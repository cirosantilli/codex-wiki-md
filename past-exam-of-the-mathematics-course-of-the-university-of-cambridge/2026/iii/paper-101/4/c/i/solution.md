↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**No.** Give $R=k[x]$ the [x-adic filtration](../../../../../../../x-adic-filtration.md) $R_n=(x^n)$, take $M=R$, and let $N=(x)$. At $n=1$ the intersection filtration gives

$$
M_1\cap N=(x),
$$

whereas the induced filtration of $N$ gives

$$
R_1N=(x^2).
$$

**Thus intersection with a [submodule](../../../../../../../submodule.md) need not equal the induced filtration.**
