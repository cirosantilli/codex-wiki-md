**No.** Let $Q$ be the [quiver](../../../../../../../quiver.md) $1\to2$ over a field $k$, and take the [representation of a quiver](../../../../../../../representation-of-a-quiver.md)

$$
k\xrightarrow{1_k}k.
$$

An [endomorphism](../../../../../../../endomorphism.md) is a pair of scalar maps $(a,b)$ satisfying $b=a$, so its [endomorphism ring](../../../../../../../endomorphism-ring.md) is $k$. Every nonzero endomorphism is therefore an [isomorphism](../../../../../../../isomorphism.md), making this representation a [brick module](../../../../../../../brick-module.md). It nevertheless has the proper nonzero subrepresentation $0\to k$, so it is not an [irreducible module](../../../../../../../irreducible-module.md).

Equivalently, this is a nonsimple module over the [path algebra](../../../../../../../path-algebra.md) $kQ$ whose endomorphism ring is a division ring.
