<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $u:M'\to M$ be an [R-module homomorphism](../../../../../../module-homomorphism.md). Naturality in the left argument means that precomposition by $u\otimes1_N$ on the left corresponds under the [Tensor-hom adjunction](../../../../../../tensor-hom-adjunction.md) to precomposition by $u$ on the right. For $f:M\otimes_RN\to P$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_{M',N,P}\bigl(f\circ(u\otimes1_N)\bigr)(m')(n)
&=f\bigl(u(m')\otimes n\bigr)\\
&=\Phi_{M,N,P}(f)\bigl(u(m')\bigr)(n).
\end{aligned}
$$

**Thus the naturality square commutes pointwise on every $m'\in M'$ and $n\in N$.**
