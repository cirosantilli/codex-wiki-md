<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an $\mathfrak m$-primary ideal $I$ in a [Noetherian local ring](../../../../../../../noetherian-local-ring.md), the function

$$
n\longmapsto\operatorname{length}_R(R/I^n)
$$

agrees for all sufficiently large $n$ with a polynomial in $n$. This is the [Hilbert-Samuel polynomial](../../../../../../../hilbert-samuel-polynomial.md), also called here the characteristic polynomial of $I$. Using $R/I^{n+1}$ instead merely shifts its variable.
