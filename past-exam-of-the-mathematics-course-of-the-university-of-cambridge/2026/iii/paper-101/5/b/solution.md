<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The weighted [Hilbert series](../../../../../../hilbert-series.md) of $K[x,y,t_1,\ldots,t_n]$ is

$$
\frac1{(1-z)^2\prod_{i=1}^n(1-z^i)}.
$$

The homogeneous polynomial $x^k-y^k$ has degree $k$ and is a [non-zero-divisor](../../../../../../non-zero-divisor.md), so quotienting by it multiplies the series by $1-z^k$. Therefore the requested [Poincare series of a graded module](../../../../../../poincare-series-of-a-graded-module.md) is

$$
\boxed{P_R(z)=\frac{1-z^k}{(1-z)^2\prod_{i=1}^n(1-z^i)}.}
$$
