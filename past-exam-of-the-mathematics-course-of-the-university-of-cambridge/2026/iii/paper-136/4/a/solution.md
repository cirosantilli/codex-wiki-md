<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The residue field $k_K=\mathbb F_q$ has a unique degree-$n$ extension $\mathbb F_{q^n}$. Choose a monic irreducible polynomial $\bar f$ defining it and lift $\bar f$ to a monic $f\in\mathcal O_K[X]$. Hensel's lemma shows that a root generates an unramified extension $L/K$ of degree $n$ with that residue field. Any two such extensions embed into a common algebraic closure and have the same Teichmuller lifts of $\mathbb F_{q^n}$, which generate them; hence they coincide. This proves existence and uniqueness of the [unramified extension](../../../../../../unramified-extension.md).
