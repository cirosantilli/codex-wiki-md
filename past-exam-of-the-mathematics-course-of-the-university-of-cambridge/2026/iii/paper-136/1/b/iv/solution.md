This is always true. Every open ball in an [ultrametric space](../../../../../../../ultrametric-space.md) is also closed: a point outside a ball has a disjoint ball of the same radius around it. Distinct points can therefore be separated by clopen sets, so every connected subset is a singleton and $K$ is [totally disconnected](../../../../../../../totally-disconnected-space.md).
