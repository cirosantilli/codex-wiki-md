This can be false. In a valued field with value group $\mathbb Q$, such as the [Hahn series field](../../../../../../../hahn-series-field.md) $k((t^{\mathbb Q}))$, the maximal ideal

$$
\mathfrak m=\{x:v(x)>0\}
$$

of the [valuation ring](../../../../../../../valuation-ring.md) is not principal: if $v(a)=q>0$, an element of valuation $q/2$ belongs to $\mathfrak m$ but not to $(a)$. Thus the valuation ring need not be a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md).
