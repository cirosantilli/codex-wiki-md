<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Restrict the given finite edge-colouring to the positive integers inside $\mathbb Q$. The infinite [Ramsey theorem](../../../../../../ramsey-theorem.md) gives an infinite monochromatic subset; listing it in increasing order produces

$$
x_1<x_2<\cdots.
$$

Apply the same theorem separately to the negative integers. If the selected indices satisfy $n_1<n_2<\cdots$, then

$$
y_i=-n_i
$$

forms a monochromatic set with $y_1>y_2>\cdots$. The two monochromatic colours need not agree.
