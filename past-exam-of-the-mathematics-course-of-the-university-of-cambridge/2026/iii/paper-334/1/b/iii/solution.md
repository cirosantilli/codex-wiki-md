<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $\delta=0$, then $\varepsilon=0$, the cell-rich material fills the gap, and

$$
\boxed{\mu_{\rm eff}=\mu}.
$$

If $\delta=h$, then the core disappears and

$$
\boxed{\mu_{\rm eff}=\mu_w}.
$$

For the physical ordering $\mu_w<\mu$, increasing the [cell-free layer](../../../../../../../cell-free-layer.md) thickness monotonically lowers $\mu_{\rm eff}$ between these limits, exactly as intuition and the [Fahraeus--Lindqvist effect](../../../../../../../fahraeus-lindqvist-effect.md) suggest.
