<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Murray's law](../../../../../../../murray-s-law.md) minimizes the sum of the power needed to pump a [Newtonian fluid](../../../../../../../newtonian-fluid.md) and the metabolic power needed to maintain blood volume. For a cylindrical vessel of radius $R$, length $\ell$, and prescribed volume flux $Q$, [Hagen-Poiseuille flow](../../../../../../../hagen-poiseuille-equation.md) gives

$$
\mathcal P_{\rm pump}
=\Delta p\,Q
=\frac{8\mu\ell Q^2}{\pi R^4}.
$$

If maintenance costs $\alpha$ per unit volume,

$$
\mathcal P_{\rm met}=\alpha\pi R^2\ell.
$$

Setting the derivative of $\mathcal P_{\rm pump}+\mathcal P_{\rm met}$ with respect to $R$ to zero gives

$$
Q=\frac{\pi}{4}\sqrt{\frac{\alpha}{\mu}}\,R^3.
$$

Thus $Q\propto R^3$. Conservation of volume flux at a bifurcation gives

$$
\boxed{R_0^3=R_1^3+R_2^3}.
$$
