<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $c'=\chi(y)\bar c_x$. The balance and reflecting boundary conditions give

$$
D\chi''=\frac Uh y,
\qquad
\chi'(\pm h)=0,
\qquad
\bar\chi=0.
$$

Integration yields

$$
\boxed{
\chi(y)=\frac UD
\left(\frac{y^3}{6h}-\frac{hy}{2}\right)}.
$$

Averaging the full transport equation gives

$$
\bar c_t+\overline{u\chi}\,\bar c_{xx}
=D\bar c_{xx}.
$$

Now

$$
\overline{u\chi}
=\frac{U^2}{D}
\left(\frac{\overline{y^4}}{6h^2}
-\frac{\overline{y^2}}2\right)
=-\frac{2U^2h^2}{15D},
$$

because $\overline{y^2}=h^2/3$ and $\overline{y^4}=h^4/5$. Thus the flow-induced [Taylor dispersion](../../../../../../../taylor-dispersion.md) coefficient and total effective diffusivity are

$$
\boxed{
D_T=\frac{2U^2h^2}{15D}},
\qquad
\boxed{
D_{\rm eff}=D+\frac{2U^2h^2}{15D}}.
$$
