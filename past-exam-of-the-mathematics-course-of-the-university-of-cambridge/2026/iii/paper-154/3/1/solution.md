<h1 id="3/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [NLS ground state](../../../../../../nls-ground-state.md) is the positive radial solution of

$$
-\Delta Q+Q-Q^3=0.
$$

It is, up to phase, translation, and scaling, the optimizer of the [Sharp Gagliardo-Nirenberg inequality](../../../../../../sharp-gagliardo-nirenberg-inequality.md)

$$
\|w\|_4^4
\leq\frac2{\|Q\|_2^2}\|w\|_2^2\|\nabla w\|_2^2.
$$

Consequently

$$
E(w)
\geq\frac12\left(1-\frac{\|w\|_2^2}{\|Q\|_2^2}\right)
\|\nabla w\|_2^2.
$$

Equality in the sharp inequality occurs precisely for the symmetry orbit of $Q$.
