<h1 id="3/3/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

By the [Blowup alternative for the nonlinear Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../../blowup-alternative-for-the-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md), choose $t_n\uparrow T$ with $\|\nabla u(t_n)\|_2\to\infty$, and set

$$
\lambda_n=\frac{\|\nabla Q\|_2}{\|\nabla u(t_n)\|_2}\longrightarrow0,
\qquad
v_n(x)=\lambda_nu(t_n,\lambda_nx).
$$

The mass-critical scaling gives

$$
\|v_n\|_2=\|Q\|_2,
\qquad
\|\nabla v_n\|_2=\|\nabla Q\|_2,
\qquad
E(v_n)=\lambda_n^2E(u_0)\to0.
$$

The sequence is bounded and radial in $H^1$, so part 4 yields, after extraction, weak $H^1$ and strong $L^4$ convergence to some $v$. The energy identity gives $\|v_n\|_4^4\to2\|\nabla Q\|_2^2$. The [Sharp Gagliardo-Nirenberg inequality](../../../../../../../sharp-gagliardo-nirenberg-inequality.md) and weak lower semicontinuity force equality in every limiting norm inequality. Hence $v_n\to v$ strongly in $H^1$, and the variational characterization identifies

$$
v=Qe^{i\gamma}
$$

for some $\gamma\in\mathbb R$; radiality removes translations and the gradient normalization fixes the scale.
