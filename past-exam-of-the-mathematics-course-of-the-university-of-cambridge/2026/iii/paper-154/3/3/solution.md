<h1 id="3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

If $\|u_0\|_2<\|Q\|_2$, the sharp variational estimate and the conservation laws give

$$
E(u_0)=E(u(t))
\geq\frac12\left(1-\frac{\|u_0\|_2^2}{\|Q\|_2^2}\right)
\|\nabla u(t)\|_2^2.
$$

**Thus the $H^1$ norm remains bounded on every finite time interval. The [Blowup alternative for the nonlinear Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../blowup-alternative-for-the-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md) then prevents a finite endpoint of the maximal lifespan, so $T=\infty$.**
