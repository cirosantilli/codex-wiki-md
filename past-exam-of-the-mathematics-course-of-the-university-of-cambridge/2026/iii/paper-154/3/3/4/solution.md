<h1 id="3/3/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Let $(u_n)$ be bounded in $H^1_r(\mathbb R^2)$. After passing to a subsequence it converges weakly in $H^1$, while the [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](../../../../../../../rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) gives strong $L^4$ convergence on every bounded ball. The [Radial Sobolev inequality](../../../../../../../radial-sobolev-inequality.md) gives uniformly

$$
\int_{|x|\geq R}|u_n|^4
\leq\|u_n\|_{L^\infty(|x|\geq R)}^2\|u_n\|_2^2
\leq\frac C R.
$$

The same estimate applies to the weak limit. Choosing $R$ large and then using local compactness proves strong convergence in $L^4(\mathbb R^2)$. Hence

$$
H^1_r(\mathbb R^2)\hookrightarrow L^4(\mathbb R^2)
$$

is compact.
