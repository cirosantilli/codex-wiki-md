<h1 id="3/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For every real $a$, the [gauge transform](../../../../../../gauge-transform.md) $w_a=e^{ia\psi}w$ has the same $L^2$ norm as $w$. Since $\|w\|_2\leq\|Q\|_2$, the variational bound from part 1 gives $E(w_a)\geq0$. Expanding its gradient gives

$$
E(w_a)=E(w)+aI+\frac{a^2}{2}A,
$$

where

$$
I=\operatorname{Im}\int\overline w\,\nabla w\mathbin{\cdot}\nabla\psi,
\qquad
A=\int|\nabla\psi|^2|w|^2.
$$

This quadratic polynomial is nonnegative for every $a\in\mathbb R$, so its discriminant is nonpositive. Therefore

$$
I^2\leq2E(w)A,
$$

which is the desired estimate.
