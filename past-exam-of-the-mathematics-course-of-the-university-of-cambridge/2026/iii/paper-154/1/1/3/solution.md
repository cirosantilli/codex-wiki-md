<h1 id="1/1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Seek a homogeneous solution $u(r)=cr^{-\beta}$. Matching the powers in $\Delta u+u^p=0$ gives $\beta p=\beta+2$, hence

$$
\beta=\frac2{p-1}=a.
$$

Since

$$
\Delta r^{-\beta}=-\beta(d-2-\beta)r^{-\beta-2},
$$

matching coefficients gives

$$
c^{p-1}=\beta(d-2-\beta).
$$

Thus the positive [Singular homogeneous solution of the Lane-Emden equation](../../../../../../../singular-homogeneous-solution-of-the-lane-emden-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{u_\infty(r)=c_\infty r^{-2/(p-1)}.}
$$
