↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

When $s_c=1$, one has $p=(d+2)/(d-2)$. The normalized [Aubin-Talenti bubble](../../../../../../../aubin-talenti-bubble.md)

$$
u(r)=\left(1+\frac{r^2}{d(d-2)}\right)^{-(d-2)/2}
$$

solves the radial equation, has $u(0)=1$, and tends to zero. It also follows from the stated one-dimensional soliton after the [Emden-Fowler transformation](../../../../../../../emden-fowler-transformation.md), because the damping term vanishes.

As $r\to\infty$,

$$
r^{d-2}u(r)\longrightarrow[d(d-2)]^{(d-2)/2}>0,
$$

so $\alpha=d-2$. Moreover $u'(r)=O(r^{-(d-1)})$ at infinity and $u'(r)=O(r)$ near zero. Therefore

$$
\int_{\mathbb R^d}|\nabla u|^2,dx
\asymp\int_0^\infty|u'(r)|^2r^{d-1},dr<\infty,
$$

and $\nabla u\in L^2(\mathbb R^d)$.
