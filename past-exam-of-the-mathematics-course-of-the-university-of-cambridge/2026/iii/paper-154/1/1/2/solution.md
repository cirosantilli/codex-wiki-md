<h1 id="1/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Put

$$
a=\frac2{p-1},\qquad s=\log r,
\qquad v(s)=r^au(r).
$$

Then $u=r^{-a}v$ and direct differentiation gives

$$
\Delta u=r^{-a-2}
\left(\ddot v+(d-2-2a)\dot v-a(d-2-a)v\right).
$$

Since $ap=a+2$ and $d-2-2a=2(s_c-1)$, the [Emden-Fowler transformation](../../../../../../../emden-fowler-transformation.md) turns the equation into

$$
\ddot v+2(s_c-1)\dot v-c_\infty^{p-1}v+v^p=0,
$$

where

$$
c_\infty^{p-1}=a(d-2-a)
=\frac2{p-1}\left(d-2-\frac2{p-1}\right)>0.
$$
