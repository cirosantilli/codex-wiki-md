<h1 id="1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a radial function $u=u(r)$ on $\mathbb R^d$, the [radial Laplacian](../../../../../../radial-laplacian.md) is

$$
\Delta u=u''+\frac{d-1}{r}u'.
$$

The problem therefore becomes

$$
u''(r)+\frac{d-1}{r}u'(r)+u(r)^p=0,
\qquad u(0)>0,\quad u'(0)=0,
\qquad u(r)\to0.
$$

The equation has the [scaling symmetry](../../../../../../scaling-symmetry-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md)

$$
u(r)\longmapsto u_\lambda(r)=\lambda^{2/(p-1)}u(\lambda r).
$$

Choosing $\lambda=u(0)^{-(p-1)/2}$ normalizes any positive solution to $u_\lambda(0)=1$.
