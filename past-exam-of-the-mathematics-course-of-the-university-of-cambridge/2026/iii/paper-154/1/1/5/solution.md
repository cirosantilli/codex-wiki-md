<h1 id="1/1/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Let $A=c_\infty^{p-1}$ and

$$
J=\dot v^2+f(v),
\qquad
f(v)=-Av^2+\frac2{p+1}v^{p+1}.
$$

Using the transformed equation,

$$
\dot J
=2\dot v(\ddot v-Av+v^p)
=-4(s_c-1)\dot v^2\leq0.
$$

Thus $J$ is a strict [Lyapunov function](../../../../../../../lyapunov-function.md) away from equilibria.

On $\mathbb R_+$, $f(0)=0$, it decreases to its unique minimum at $v=c_\infty$, where

$$
f(c_\infty)=-\frac{p-1}{p+1}c_\infty^{p+1}<0,
$$

then increases, crosses zero at $v=((p+1)/2)^{1/(p-1)}c_\infty$, and tends to $+\infty$.
