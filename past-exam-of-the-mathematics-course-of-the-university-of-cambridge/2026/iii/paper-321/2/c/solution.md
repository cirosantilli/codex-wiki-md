<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\beta=P_{\rm gas}/P_{\rm rad}$. Constancy of $\beta$ gives

$$
\rho=\frac{4\mu_m m_p\sigma}{3ck}\,\beta T^3.
$$

The total pressure is

$$
P=(1+\beta)\frac{4\sigma T^4}{3c}.
$$

Consequently, if $T=T_0f$, then $\rho=\rho_0f^3$ and $P=P_0f^4$, where

$$
\boxed{\rho_0=\frac{4\mu_m m_p\sigma}{3ck}\,\beta T_0^3},
\qquad
\boxed{P_0=\frac{4\sigma}{3c}(1+\beta)T_0^4}.
$$

Substitution in hydrostatic balance shows that $f'= -2z/H^2$ and

$$
\boxed{f(z)=1-\frac{z^2}{H^2}},
\qquad
\boxed{H^2=\frac{8kT_0}{\mu_m m_p\Omega^2}
\frac{1+\beta}{\beta}}.
$$

Finally,

$$
\Sigma=\rho_0H\int_{-1}^{1}(1-x^2)^3dx.
$$

Absorbing the numerical factor $4/3$ and this dimensionless integral into $A$ gives the requested form

$$
\boxed{H=\frac1A\left(\frac{ck}{\mu_m m_p\sigma}\right)
\frac{\Sigma}{\beta T_0^3}}.
$$
