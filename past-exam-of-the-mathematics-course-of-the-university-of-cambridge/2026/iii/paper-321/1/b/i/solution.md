<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In steady state, mass conservation makes the outward flux constant, $\mathcal F=\dot M$. Angular-momentum conservation then gives

$$
\dot Mh+\mathcal G=\text{constant}.
$$

The zero-torque condition at $r_{\rm out}$ fixes the constant to $\dot Mh_{\rm out}$, so

$$
\mathcal G=\dot M(h_{\rm out}-h).
$$

For a [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md), $h=\sqrt{GMr}$ and $d\Omega/dr=-3\Omega/(2r)$. The viscous torque is consequently $\mathcal G=3\pi\bar\nu\Sigma h$. Equating the two expressions yields the steady [decretion disk](../../../../../../../decretion-disk.md) structure

$$
\boxed{\bar\nu\Sigma=\frac{\dot M}{3\pi}
\left(\sqrt{\frac{r_{\rm out}}r}-1\right)}.
$$
