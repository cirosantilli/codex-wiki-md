<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At marginal stability write $s=i\omega$ with real $\omega$. The imaginary part of the pressure factor is

$$
\operatorname{Im}\frac{1+i\gamma\tau\omega}
{1+i\tau\omega}
=\frac{(\gamma-1)\tau\omega}{1+\tau^2\omega^2}.
$$

For $\gamma>1$, $\tau>0$, and nonzero $k$, the dispersion relation can have zero imaginary part only when $\omega=0$. Thus every marginal mode has

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}s=0,\qquad s=0}.
$$

The marginal equation is consequently

$$
c_0^2k^2-2\pi G\Sigma_0|k|+\Omega^2=0.
$$

Defining the isothermal Toomre parameter $Q_0=c_0\Omega/(\pi G\Sigma_0)$, its two roots are

$$
\boxed{|k|_\pm=\frac{\pi G\Sigma_0}{c_0^2}
\left(1\pm\sqrt{1-Q_0^2}\right)}.
$$

They exist when $Q_0\leq1$, and the interval between them is unstable when $Q_0<1$. Finite thermal relaxation therefore leaves the onset criterion

$$
\boxed{Q_0<1}
$$

equal to the isothermal criterion, regardless of $\gamma$ and the nonzero value of $\tau$.
