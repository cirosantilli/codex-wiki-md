<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For [quantum electrodynamics](../../../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md), $j^\mu=\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi$ is an operator built from a dynamical [Dirac field](../../../../../../dirac-field.md). Integrating over $\psi,\bar\psi$ generates arbitrarily high powers of $e$, so the Gaussian-source truncation fails.

For the connected photon two-point function, the required diagrams through $e^4$ are: the bare photon line at $e^0$; one fermion-loop [photon vacuum polarization](../../../../../../photon-vacuum-polarization.md) insertion at $e^2$; and at $e^4$, a photon line with two successive one-loop polarization insertions together with the two-loop one-particle-irreducible fermion loop whose loop contains one internal photon chord. The latter includes the cyclic placements conventionally interpreted as self-energy and vertex corrections. Counterterm insertions must be added in a renormalized calculation. Disconnected vacuum bubbles cancel against the vacuum normalization.
