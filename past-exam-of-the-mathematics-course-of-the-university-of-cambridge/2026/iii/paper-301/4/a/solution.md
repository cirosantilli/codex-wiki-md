<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expanding the covariant derivatives of [scalar quantum electrodynamics](../../../../../../scalar-electrodynamics.md) gives

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{free}}
=-\frac14F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}
+\partial_\mu\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-m^2\phi^*\phi
$$

and

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{int}}
=ieA_\mu(\phi\partial^\mu\phi^*-\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi)
+e^2A_\mu A^\mu\phi^*\phi.
$$

In four dimensions, $[A_\mu]=[\phi]=1$ and $[\partial_\mu]=1$, so $[e]=0$. Both interactions have dimension four and $e$ is a [marginal coupling](../../../../../../marginal-coupling.md) by [power counting in quantum field theory](../../../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md).
