<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Three connected [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) contribute at order $e^2$: a scalar-exchange diagram in which photon $k_1$ is emitted before $k_2$ along the charged scalar line; the crossed scalar-exchange diagram with $k_1$ and $k_2$ interchanged; and the local [seagull vertex](../../../../../../seagull-vertex.md) joining both incoming scalars and both outgoing photons. The exchange denominators are $(p_1-k_1)^2-m^2$ and $(p_1-k_2)^2-m^2$.
