<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Insert the [mode expansion of a free field](../../../../../../mode-expansion-of-a-free-field.md) into the parity law and change integration variable $\mathbf k\mapsto-\mathbf k$. Independence of the plane waves gives

$$
\mathcal P a_{\mathbf k}\mathcal P^{-1}=\gamma_p a_{-\mathbf k},
\qquad
\mathcal P a_{\mathbf k}^\dagger\mathcal P^{-1}=\gamma_p a_{-\mathbf k}^\dagger.
$$

Since $\mathcal P|0\rangle=|0\rangle$, an $n$-particle [Fock state](../../../../../../fock-state.md) therefore transforms as

$$
\boxed{\mathcal P|\mathbf k_1,\ldots,\mathbf k_n\rangle
=(\gamma_p)^n|-\mathbf k_1,\ldots,-\mathbf k_n\rangle.}
$$
