<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The translation changes the [Lagrangian density](../../../../../../lagrangian-density.md) by $\delta\mathcal L_0=\epsilon^\nu\partial_\nu\mathcal L_0=\partial_\mu(\epsilon^\mu\mathcal L_0)$. The [Noether current](../../../../../../noether-current.md) is therefore

$$
j^\mu=\frac{\partial\mathcal L_0}{\partial(\partial_\mu\psi)}\delta\psi-\epsilon^\mu\mathcal L_0
=\epsilon_\nu T^{\mu\nu},
\qquad
T^{\mu\nu}=i\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\partial^\nu\psi-\eta^{\mu\nu}\mathcal L_0.
$$

This is the [canonical stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md). Directly,

$$
\partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu}
=\left(\frac{\partial\mathcal L_0}{\partial\psi}
-\partial_\mu\frac{\partial\mathcal L_0}{\partial(\partial_\mu\psi)}\right)\partial^\nu\psi
$$

up to the analogous adjoint-field term, so the [Euler-Lagrange field equations](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) imply $\partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0$ [on shell](../../../../../../on-shell.md). The [Dirac equation](../../../../../../dirac-equation.md) also gives $\mathcal L_0=0$ on shell.
