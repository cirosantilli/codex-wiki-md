<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\overline\nabla$ be the Euclidean connection and split the ambient tangent bundle along the [embedded submanifold](../../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md) as $T\mathbb R^{n+m}|_M=TM\oplus NM$. The [second fundamental form](../../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) is the normal-bundle-valued bilinear form

$$
A(X,Y)=(\overline\nabla_XY)^\perp.
$$

It is symmetric because $\overline\nabla$ is torsion-free and the Lie bracket of tangent vector fields remains tangent:

$$
A(X,Y)-A(Y,X)
=\bigl(\overline\nabla_XY-\overline\nabla_YX\bigr)^\perp
=[X,Y]^\perp=0.
$$

This is the [symmetry of the second fundamental form](../../../../../../symmetry-of-the-second-fundamental-form.md).
