↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With the curvature convention $R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z$, the [Gauss equation](../../../../../../gauss-equation.md) for a Euclidean embedded submanifold is

$$
\langle R(X,Y)Z,W\rangle
=\langle A(X,W),A(Y,Z)\rangle
-\langle A(X,Z),A(Y,W)\rangle.
$$

The [Codazzi equation](../../../../../../codazzi-equation.md) is

$$
(\nabla_XA)(Y,Z)=(\nabla_YA)(X,Z),
$$

where the derivative uses the Levi-Civita connection on tangent arguments and the normal connection on the value of $A$.
