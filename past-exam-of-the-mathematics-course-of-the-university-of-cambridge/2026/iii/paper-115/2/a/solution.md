<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The metric and orientation determine the [Riemannian volume form](../../../../../../riemannian-volume-form.md): in a positively oriented coordinate chart $(x^1,\ldots,x^n)$,

$$
d\operatorname{vol}_g=\sqrt{\det(g_{ij})}\,dx^1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx^n.
$$

Writing $|g|=\det(g_{ij})$ and $(g^{ij})=(g_{ij})^{-1}$, the [Dirichlet energy on a Riemannian manifold](../../../../../../dirichlet-energy-on-a-riemannian-manifold.md) with source $f$ is

$$
\boxed{E(u)=\int_M\left(\frac12g^{ij}(x)\,\partial_i u\,\partial_j u-fu\right)\sqrt{|g|}\,dx^1\cdots dx^n.}
$$
