<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Ambient matrix multiplication

$$
M_{2n}(\mathbb R)\times M_{2n}(\mathbb R)\longrightarrow M_{2n}(\mathbb R),
\qquad(A,B)\longmapsto AB,
$$

is bilinear and therefore smooth. Its restriction to the embedded submanifold $G\times G$ is smooth, and part a shows that its image lies in $G$. By the defining smooth structure on an [embedded submanifold](../../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md), this restriction is a smooth map $G\times G\to G$.
