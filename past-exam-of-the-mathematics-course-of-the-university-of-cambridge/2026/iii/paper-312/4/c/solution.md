<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $R=0$, $c_s=1/\sqrt3$. If the [Newtonian potentials](../../../../../../newtonian-gauge.md) are constant and equal, the equation in part (b) becomes

$$
\Theta_0''+c_s^2k^2\Theta_0=-c_s^2k^2\Psi.
$$

The [Sachs-Wolfe combination](../../../../../../sachs-wolfe-combination.md) $D=\Theta_0+\Psi$ therefore obeys

$$
D''+c_s^2k^2D=0.
$$

Adiabatic initial conditions have vanishing initial fluid velocity, hence $D'(0)=0$. With the [sound horizon](../../../../../../sound-horizon.md) $r_s(\eta)=\int_0^\eta c_s,d\eta'$, the solution is

$$
\boxed{\Theta_0+\Psi=(\Theta_0+\Psi)_{\rm ini}\cos(kr_s)}.
$$
