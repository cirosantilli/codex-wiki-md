<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For distinct halos, insert their linearly biased correlation

$$
\xi_{hh}(r|M_1,M_2)=b(M_1)b(M_2)\xi_{\rm lin}(r)
$$

into the double sum. Fourier transformation converts $\xi_{\rm lin}$ into $P_{\rm lin}$, while the two independent mass integrals factorize. The result is the [halo model](../../../../../../halo-model.md)

$$
P(k)=P_{1h}(k)+P_{2h}(k),
$$

where

$$
\boxed{P_{2h}(k)=
\left[\int_0^\infty dM\,\frac{dn}{dM}
\frac{M}{\bar\rho}b(M)\widetilde u(k|M)\right]^2
P_{\rm lin}(k)}.
$$

Thus the requested function is

$$
\boxed{f(M,\bar\rho)=\frac{M}{\bar\rho}}.
$$

At small $k$, profile normalization gives $\widetilde u\to1$ and the bias consistency relation makes the square bracket tend to one, so the [two-halo term](../../../../../../two-halo-term.md) approaches the linear matter spectrum.
