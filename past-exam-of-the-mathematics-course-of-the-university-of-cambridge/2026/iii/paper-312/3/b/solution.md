<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the [peak-background split](../../../../../../peak-background-split.md), a long-wavelength overdensity changes the local threshold from $\delta_c$ to $\delta_c-\delta_l$. At fixed mass, the [Press-Schechter halo mass function](../../../../../../press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md) depends on the threshold through $\nu e^{-\nu^2/2}$. Therefore

$$
b_L
=\left.\frac1n\frac{\partial n}{\partial\delta_l}\right|_{\delta_l=0}
=-\frac{\partial\log n}{\partial\delta_c}
=-\frac1{\delta_c}\frac{\partial}{\partial\log\nu}
\log(\nu e^{-\nu^2/2}).
$$

It follows that the [linear Lagrangian halo bias](../../../../../../linear-lagrangian-halo-bias.md) is

$$
\boxed{b_L(M)=\frac{\nu^2-1}{\delta_c}}.
$$
