↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If

$$
P(X)=a_d\prod_{i=1}^d(X-\alpha_i),
$$

its [Mahler measure](../../../../../../mahler-measure.md) is

$$
M(P)=|a_d|\prod_{i=1}^d\max(1,|\alpha_i|).
$$

If $f_\alpha\in\mathbb Z[X]$ is the primitive minimal polynomial of an algebraic number $\alpha$ and $d=\deg f_\alpha$, the [height-Mahler measure formula](../../../../../../height-mahler-measure-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{H(\alpha)^d=M(f_\alpha).}
$$
