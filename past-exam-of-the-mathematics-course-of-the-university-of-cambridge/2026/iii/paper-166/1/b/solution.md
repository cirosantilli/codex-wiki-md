↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

One Archimedean form of the [Schmidt subspace theorem](../../../../../../schmidt-subspace-theorem.md) is as follows. Let $L_1,\ldots,L_n$ be linearly independent [linear forms](../../../../../../linear-functional.md) in $n$ variables with algebraic coefficients. For every $\varepsilon>0$, all nonzero $\mathbf x\in\mathbb Z^n$ satisfying

$$
\prod_{i=1}^n|L_i(\mathbf x)|
<\|\mathbf x\|^{-\varepsilon}
$$

belong to a finite union of proper rational [linear subspaces](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $\mathbb Q^n$.

We will also use its finite-place form: if $S$ is a finite set of places containing the Archimedean ones and, for each $v\in S$, the forms $L_{1,v},\ldots,L_{n,v}$ are independent, then the integer solutions of

$$
\prod_{v\in S}\prod_{i=1}^n|L_{i,v}(\mathbf x)|_v
<H(\mathbf x)^{-\varepsilon}
$$

lie in finitely many proper rational subspaces. The absolute values are normalized so that the [product formula](../../../../../../product-formula.md) holds.
