↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) $\rho$ on a finite-dimensional [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), the [Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{S(\rho)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\log\rho)},
$$

with $0\log0=0$. Its [concavity](../../../../../../concavity-of-von-neumann-entropy.md) says that, for $0\leq p\leq1$,

$$
\boxed{S(p\rho_1+(1-p)\rho_2)
\geq pS(\rho_1)+(1-p)S(\rho_2)}.
$$
