The other convex decomposition is $\omega=q\sigma+(1-q)\Delta$. Applying the [concavity of quantum conditional entropy](../../../../../../../concavity-of-quantum-conditional-entropy.md), which follows from the [Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../../strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md), yields

$$
\boxed{H(A|B)_\omega\geq
\frac{H(A|B)_\sigma+\varepsilon H(A|B)_\Delta}{1+\varepsilon}}.
$$
