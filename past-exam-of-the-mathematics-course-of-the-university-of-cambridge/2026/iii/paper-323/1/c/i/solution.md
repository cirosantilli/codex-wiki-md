↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $T(\rho)=\sum_kp_kU_k\rho U_k^\dagger$,

$$
C_T=\sum_kp_k
(U_k\otimes I)|\phi\rangle\langle\phi|
(U_k^\dagger\otimes I).
$$

Each $(U_k\otimes I)|\phi\rangle$ is a [maximally entangled state](../../../../../../../maximally-entangled-state.md), so the [Choi matrix](../../../../../../../choi-matrix.md) is a convex combination of maximally entangled pure states.
