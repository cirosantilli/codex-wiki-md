↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [positive partial transpose criterion](../../../../../../../positive-partial-transpose-criterion.md) says separability implies $\rho_{AB}^{T_B}\geq0$. A nonpositive partial transpose therefore proves entanglement. Positive partial transpose is also sufficient for separability in dimensions $2\otimes2$ and $2\otimes3$, but not in general higher dimensions.
