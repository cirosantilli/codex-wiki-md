<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a bipartite input,

$$
(T\otimes\operatorname{id})(\rho_{AB})
=\sum_k\sigma_k^A\otimes
\operatorname{Tr}_A[(M_k\otimes I)\rho_{AB}].
$$

Each second factor is positive and has trace equal to the probability of measurement outcome $k$. Dividing nonzero factors by their traces therefore writes the output as a convex combination of product states. Hence every measure-and-prepare channel is an [entanglement-breaking channel](../../../../../../../entanglement-breaking-channel.md).
