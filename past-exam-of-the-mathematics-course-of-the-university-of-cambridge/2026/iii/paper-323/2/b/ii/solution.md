<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Partial transposition interchanges the positions occupied by $x$ and $y$. Positivity of $\rho^{T_B}$ therefore requires

$$
|y|^2\leq ad,
\qquad
|x|^2\leq bc.
$$

Because the system is $2\otimes2$, the [positive partial transpose criterion](../../../../../../../positive-partial-transpose-criterion.md) is necessary and sufficient. Combining these inequalities with validity of the original state gives

$$
\boxed{|x|^2\leq\min(ad,bc),\qquad
|y|^2\leq\min(ad,bc)}.
$$
