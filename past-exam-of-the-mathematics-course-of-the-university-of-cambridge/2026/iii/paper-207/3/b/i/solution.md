<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Minimizing the expected failures $n_H(1-p_H)+n_L(1-p_L)$ at a fixed variance gives the ethically optimal ratio

$$
\frac{n_H}{n_L}=\sqrt{\frac{p_H}{p_L}}.
$$

It allocates more patients to the dose with the larger response probability.
