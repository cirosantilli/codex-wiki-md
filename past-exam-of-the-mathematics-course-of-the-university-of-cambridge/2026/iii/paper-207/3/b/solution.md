<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Writing $v_H=p_H(1-p_H)$ and $v_L=p_L(1-p_L)$, [Neyman allocation](../../../../../../neyman-allocation.md) minimizes the variance $v_H/n_H+v_L/n_L$ at fixed total sample size and gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{n_H}{n_L}=\sqrt{\frac{v_H}{v_L}}
=\sqrt{\frac{p_H(1-p_H)}{p_L(1-p_L)}}.}
$$
