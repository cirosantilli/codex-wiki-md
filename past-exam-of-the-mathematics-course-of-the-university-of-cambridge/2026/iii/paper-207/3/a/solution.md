<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under the boundary null $p=\pi_0=0.20$, rejection requires both $X_1>r_1$ and $X_1+X_2\geq r$. Independence of the two [binomial distributions](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) gives

$$
\alpha=\sum_{x=r_1+1}^{n_1}b(x;n_1,\pi_0)
\left[1-B(r-x-1;n_2,\pi_0)\right].
$$

The rejection event is increasing in $p$, so this boundary value is the supremum over the composite null $p\leq\pi_0$.
