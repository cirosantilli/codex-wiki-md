<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

If sex predicts apprenticeship uptake, extreme UTC sex ratios create both [confounding](../../../../../../confounding.md) and weak overlap. Matching only the aggregate percentage of boys may leave no genuinely comparable control, and an additive distance can hide a large imbalance in this influential variable. Sex may also be an [effect modifier](../../../../../../effect-modifier.md), so the UTC effect can differ across these unusual compositions. One should enforce close sex-ratio balance and estimate sex-specific effects before standardizing them to the intended policy population.
