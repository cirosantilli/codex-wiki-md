<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $Y_s(1)$ and $Y_s(0)$ be the [potential outcomes](../../../../../../potential-outcome.md) for the apprenticeship proportion at school $s$ if it is or is not converted to a UTC. The policy-relevant estimand is the average causal effect

$$
\mathbb E\{Y_s(1)-Y_s(0)\}
$$

over the population of schools eligible for conversion. Ideally this would be identified by a [randomized controlled trial](../../../../../../randomized-controlled-trial.md) of conversion, or by an observational design that reproduces its exchangeability.
