<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The raw difference is generally not a [causal effect](../../../../../../causal-effect.md). Existing UTCs may differ systematically from non-UTCs in location, admissions, pupil composition, prior attainment, resources, and the local apprenticeship market. These are possible [confounders](../../../../../../confounder.md) of school type and outcome. The comparison also weights the 43 UTCs and roughly 3500 controls very differently from a clearly defined target population. Without [conditional exchangeability](../../../../../../conditional-exchangeability.md), [positivity](../../../../../../positivity-assumption.md), and [consistency](../../../../../../consistency-in-causal-inference.md), the difference mixes the effect of UTC status with selection bias.
