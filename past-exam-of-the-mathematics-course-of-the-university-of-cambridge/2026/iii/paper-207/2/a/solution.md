↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The crude incidence-rate estimator is

$$
\widehat q_{SE}=\frac{10}{1000\times3}=\frac1{300}
$$

per person-month. This treats all 3000 person-months as susceptible time and assumes infection times are observed exactly, follow-up is complete, and each first infection is detected. In practice tests occur intermittently, so infection is [interval-censored](../../../../../../interval-censoring.md); latent, brief, or asymptomatic infections may also be missed.
