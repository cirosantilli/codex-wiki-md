↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The $S\to E$ transition must be redefined. A constant $q_{SE}$ gives each susceptible the same external infection pressure even when no infectious person remains. For influenza, the [force of infection](../../../../../../../force-of-infection.md) should depend on the current infectious prevalence. The progression $E\to I$ and recovery $I\to R$ can retain rates $q_{EI}$ and $q_{IR}$.
