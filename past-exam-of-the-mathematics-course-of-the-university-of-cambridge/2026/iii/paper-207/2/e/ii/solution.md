↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With frequency-dependent transmission parameter $\beta$, the nonlinear SEIR equations are

$$
\boxed{\dot S=-\beta\frac{SI}{N},
\qquad
\dot E=\beta\frac{SI}{N}-q_{EI}E,
\qquad
\dot I=q_{EI}E-q_{IR}I,
\qquad
\dot R=q_{IR}I.}
$$
