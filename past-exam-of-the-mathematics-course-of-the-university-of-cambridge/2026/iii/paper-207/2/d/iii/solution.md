↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Substitute part (ii) into $\dot E+q_{EI}E=q_{SE}S_0e^{-q_{SE}t}$. Multiplication by the [integrating factor](../../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) $e^{q_{EI}t}$ gives

$$
\frac d{dt}\bigl(e^{q_{EI}t}E(t)\bigr)
=q_{SE}S_0e^{(q_{EI}-q_{SE})t}.
$$

Since $E(0)=0$, integration and rearrangement yield

$$
\boxed{E(t)=\frac{S_0q_{SE}}{q_{EI}-q_{SE}}
\left(e^{-q_{SE}t}-e^{-q_{EI}t}\right).}
$$
