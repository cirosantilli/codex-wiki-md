<h1 id="2/d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

At an interior maximum, $\dot E(t)=0$, equivalently

$$
q_{EI}e^{-q_{EI}t}=q_{SE}e^{-q_{SE}t}.
$$

Thus

$$
t_{\max}=\frac{\log(q_{EI}/q_{SE})}{q_{EI}-q_{SE}}.
$$

This is positive for distinct positive rates. Its continuous limit when $q_{EI}=q_{SE}=q$ is $1/q$.
