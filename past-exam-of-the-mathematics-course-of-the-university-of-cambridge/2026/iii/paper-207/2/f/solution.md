<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

While $I>0$, divide the susceptible equation by the recovered equation:

$$
\frac{dS}{dR}=-\frac{\beta}{Nq_{IR}}S.
$$

Taking $R(0)=0$ and integrating gives

$$
S(t)=S_0\exp\!\left[-\frac{\beta}{Nq_{IR}}R(t)\right].
$$

At the end of the epidemic $E_\infty=I_\infty=0$, so conservation gives $R_\infty=N-S_\infty$. Defining

$$
\mathcal R=\frac{\beta}{Nq_{IR}},
$$

we obtain the [final size relation for an epidemic](../../../../../../final-size-relation-for-an-epidemic.md)

$$
S_\infty=S_0\exp[-\mathcal R(N-S_\infty)].
$$
