<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Conditioning on survival to $L_i$ changes the constant-hazard likelihood contribution to

$$
\theta^{v_i}e^{-\theta(x_i-L_i)}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\widehat\theta=\frac{\sum_i v_i}{\sum_i(x_i-L_i)},
$$

using only person-time accumulated after entry.
