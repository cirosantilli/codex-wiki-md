In the [Kaplan–Meier estimator](../../../../../../../kaplan-meier-estimator.md), replace the ordinary risk set at event time $t$ by

$$
R(t)=\{i:L_i<t\leq X_i\}.
$$

The product-limit factor remains $1-d_j/|R(t_j)|$, but only individuals who have entered and not yet exited contribute to its denominator.
