<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With constant hazard $\theta$, observation $i$ contributes

$$
\theta^{v_i}e^{-\theta x_i}.
$$

Writing $D=\sum_i v_i$ and $T=\sum_i x_i$, the [log-likelihood](../../../../../../log-likelihood.md) is $D\log\theta-\theta T$, whose derivative is $D/\theta-T$. Hence

$$
\widehat\theta=\frac{\sum_i v_i}{\sum_i x_i},
$$

the number of events divided by total person-time.
