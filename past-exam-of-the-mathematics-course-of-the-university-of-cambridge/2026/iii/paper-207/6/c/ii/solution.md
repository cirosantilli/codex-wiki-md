↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Under the two-rate alternative, maximize the [piecewise-exponential survival model](../../../../../../../piecewise-exponential-survival-model.md) likelihood separately to obtain the estimates in part (i). Under $H_0:\theta_1=\theta_2=\theta$, there are 15 deaths and 2200 days of exposure, so $\widehat\theta=15/2200$. Twice the maximized log-likelihood difference is

$$
2\left[
7\log\frac7{400}+8\log\frac8{1800}
-15\log\frac{15}{2200}
\right].
$$

Under the null this [likelihood-ratio test](../../../../../../../likelihood-ratio-test.md) statistic is asymptotically $\chi_1^2$.
