<h1 id="4/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Independent relabelling gives $\pi=q$, $\mathbb E v=1-q$, and leaves $X=T$ with mean $1/\lambda$. Its ratio is therefore $\lambda(1-q)$ rather than $\lambda$. To retain event hazard $\lambda$, simulate the common occurrence time with rate

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{all}}=\frac\lambda{1-q}
$$

and label it as the target event with probability $1-q$. This is equivalent to independent competing clocks with target-event rate $\lambda$ and censoring-cause rate $q\lambda/(1-q)$; their minimum has rate $\lambda/(1-q)$.
