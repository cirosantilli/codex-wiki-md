<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an independent $C\sim\operatorname{Exponential}(\mu)$, censoring is noninformative and

$$
q=\mathbb P(C<T)=\frac\mu{\lambda+\mu},
\qquad
\mu=\frac{q\lambda}{1-q}.
$$

The observed time is exponential with rate $\lambda+\mu$, while $\mathbb E v=\lambda/(\lambda+\mu)=1-q$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\frac{\mathbb E v}{\mathbb E X}
=\frac{\lambda/(\lambda+\mu)}{1/(\lambda+\mu)}=\lambda.}
$$
