↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $U\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$, the [inverse transform sampling](../../../../../../inverse-transform-sampling.md) formula

$$
T=-\frac1\lambda\log U
$$

has survival function $\mathbb P(T>t)=e^{-\lambda t}$ and hence the required [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md).
