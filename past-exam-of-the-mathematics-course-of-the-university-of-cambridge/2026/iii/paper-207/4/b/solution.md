<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Without censoring, every $v_i=1$ and $\mathbb E T_i=1/\lambda$. Therefore

$$
\frac{\mathbb E\sum_i v_i}{\mathbb E\sum_i x_i}
=\frac n{n/\lambda}=\lambda.
$$

This is events divided by total [person-time at risk](../../../../../../person-time-at-risk.md).
