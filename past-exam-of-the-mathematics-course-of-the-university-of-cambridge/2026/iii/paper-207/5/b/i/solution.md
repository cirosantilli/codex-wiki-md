<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use each matched pair as a separate [stratum](../../../../../../../stratum.md) with its own baseline hazard and fit one common treatment coefficient $\beta$. The [Stratified Cox model](../../../../../../../stratified-cox-model.md) partial likelihood multiplies the within-pair risk-set contributions, eliminating all pair-specific baselines. Test $H_0:\beta=0$ by the corresponding conditional score, likelihood-ratio, or Wald test.
