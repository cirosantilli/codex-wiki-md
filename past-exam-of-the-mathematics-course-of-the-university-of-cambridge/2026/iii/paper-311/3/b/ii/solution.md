<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In two dimensions every future causal vector is a nonnegative linear combination of $k_+$ and $k_-$. The [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md) is therefore equivalent to nonnegativity of $T(k_+,k_+)$, $T(k_-,k_-)$, and the mixed pairing

$$
T(k_+,k_-)=a-c.
$$

Thus the necessary and sufficient conditions are

$$
\boxed{a+c\geq2|b|,
\qquad a\geq c}.
$$
