↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The two future null directions are proportional to $k_+=(1,1)$ and $k_-=(1,-1)$. The [null energy condition](../../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md) requires

$$
T(k_+,k_+)=a+2b+c\geq0,
\qquad
T(k_-,k_-)=a-2b+c\geq0.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{a+c\geq2|b|}.
$$
