↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A black-hole metric is [stationary](../../../../../../../stationary-spacetime.md) when it admits a Killing field $K$ generating time translations and timelike near spatial infinity. It is [static](../../../../../../../static-spacetime.md) when $K$ is also hypersurface orthogonal,

$$
K_{[a}\nabla_bK_{c]}=0.
$$

In coordinates adapted to a static field, the metric coefficients are time independent and the time-space cross terms vanish.
