<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [stationary axisymmetric spacetime](../../../../../../../stationary-axisymmetric-spacetime.md) additionally admits an axial Killing field $m$ with closed orbits. The two symmetry generators commute,

$$
[K,m]=0,
$$

and preserve the black-hole exterior and horizon. Coordinates adapted to them make the metric independent of both $t$ and $\phi$.
