<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $k^a=(d/d\lambda)^a$ be an affine horizon generator, normalized so that the horizon Killing field is $\xi^a=\kappa\lambda k^a$. To first order in the small total injected energy, expansion and shear-squared terms are second order, and the [Null Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../null-raychaudhuri-equation.md) on the horizon becomes

$$
\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda}=-8\pi T_{ab}k^ak^b.
$$

The flux of Killing energy through all the shells is

$$
\Delta M
=\int_{\mathcal H^+}T_{ab}\xi^ak^b\,d\lambda\,dA
=\kappa\int\lambda T_{ab}k^ak^b\,d\lambda\,dA.
$$

Substitute the linearized focusing equation and integrate by parts. Stationarity before and after the process makes the endpoint term $\lambda\theta$ vanish, while $dA/d\lambda=\theta A$ to first order. Therefore

$$
\Delta M
=\frac{\kappa}{8\pi}\int\theta\,d\lambda\,dA
=\frac{\kappa}{8\pi}\Delta A.
$$

Using the [Hawking temperature](../../../../../../hawking-temperature.md) $T_H=\kappa/(2\pi)$ and [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](../../../../../../bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md) $S_{BH}=A/4$ gives the [Physical-process first law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../physical-process-first-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md)

$$
\boxed{\Delta M=\frac{\kappa}{8\pi}\Delta A
=T_H\Delta S_{BH}}.
$$

The derivation is linear in the stress tensor, so separated shells simply contribute additively.
