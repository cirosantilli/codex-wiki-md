<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Because $U$ is a [null](../../../../../../../null-vector.md) tangent, metric compatibility gives

$$
U^aB_{ab}=U^a\nabla_bU_a
=\frac12\nabla_b(U^aU_a)=0.
$$

Because $U$ is tangent to an affinely parametrized [null geodesic congruence](../../../../../../../null-geodesic-congruence.md),

$$
B_{ab}U^b=U^b\nabla_bU_a=0
$$

by the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md). Thus $B$ has no component along $U$ in either index.
