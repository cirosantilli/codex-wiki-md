<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Decompose the [optical tensor](../../../../../../../optical-tensor.md) on the two-dimensional screen space as

$$
\widehat B_{ab}
=\frac12\theta P_{ab}
+\widehat\sigma_{ab}
+\widehat\omega_{ab}.
$$

The [null expansion](../../../../../../../null-expansion.md), [null shear](../../../../../../../null-shear.md), and [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md) are respectively

$$
\boxed{\theta=P^{ab}\widehat B_{ab}},
\qquad
\boxed{\widehat\sigma_{ab}
=\widehat B_{(ab)}-\frac12\theta P_{ab}},
\qquad
\boxed{\widehat\omega_{ab}=\widehat B_{[ab]}}.
$$

The shear is symmetric and trace-free, while the twist is antisymmetric.
