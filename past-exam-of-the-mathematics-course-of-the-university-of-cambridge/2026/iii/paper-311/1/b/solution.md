<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate $B_{ab}=\nabla_bU_a$ along an affinely parametrized generator. Commuting [covariant derivatives](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) and using $\nabla_UU=0$ gives the optical evolution equation

$$
U^c\nabla_cB_{ab}
=-B_{ac}B^c{}_b-R_{acbd}U^cU^d.
$$

Taking its screen trace and using

$$
\widehat B_{ab}\widehat B^{ba}
=\frac12\theta^2
+\widehat\sigma_{ab}\widehat\sigma^{ab}
-\widehat\omega_{ab}\widehat\omega^{ab}
$$

produces the [Null Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../null-raychaudhuri-equation.md)

$$
\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda}
=-\frac12\theta^2
-\widehat\sigma_{ab}\widehat\sigma^{ab}
+\widehat\omega_{ab}\widehat\omega^{ab}
-R_{ab}U^aU^b.
$$

The generators lie in a [null hypersurface](../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md), so they are hypersurface orthogonal. The [Frobenius theorem](../../../../../../frobenius-theorem.md) therefore gives $\widehat\omega_{ab}=0$, leaving

$$
\boxed{
\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda}
=-\frac12\theta^2
-\widehat\sigma_{ab}\widehat\sigma^{ab}
-R_{ab}U^aU^b}.
$$
