<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $K=|\phi|^2$,

$$
D_\phi W=m^2[1+\phi^*(\phi+\beta)],
$$

and the [supergravity F-term potential](../../../../../../supergravity-f-term-potential.md) is

$$
V(\phi)=m^4e^{|\phi|^2}
\left(
\left|1+\phi^*(\phi+\beta)\right|^2
-3|\phi+\beta|^2
\right).
$$

The potential is invariant under complex conjugation. Writing $\phi=x+iy$, its derivative with respect to $y$ vanishes at $y=0$, so the extremisation equations consistently admit a real solution. On that slice,

$$
\boxed{V(x)=m^4e^{x^2}
\left[(1+x^2+\beta x)^2-3(x+\beta)^2\right].}
$$
