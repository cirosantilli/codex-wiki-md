With canonical [Kähler potential](../../../../../kahler-potential.md), the [Wess–Zumino model](../../../../../wess-zumino-model.md) action is

$$
S=\int d^4x\left[
\int d^4\theta\,\Phi^\dagger\Phi
+\left\{\int d^2\theta
\left(\frac12m\Phi^2+\frac13g\Phi^3\right)
+\mathrm{h.c.}\right\}\right].
$$

A [spurion](../../../../../spurion.md) is a nondynamical background superfield assigned charges so that a coupling transforms covariantly and the action can be treated as formally symmetric.
