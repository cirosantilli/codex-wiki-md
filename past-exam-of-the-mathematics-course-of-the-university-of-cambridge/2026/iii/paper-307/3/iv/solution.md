<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Eliminating the [auxiliary field](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) gives

$$
V(\phi)=|W'(\phi)|^2
=|m\phi+g\phi^2|^2.
$$

Explicitly,

$$
V=m^2|\phi|^2
+m(g\phi+g^*\phi^*)|\phi|^2
+|g|^2|\phi|^4.
$$

The two supersymmetric vacua for $g\ne0$ are $\phi=0$ and $\phi=-m/g$.
