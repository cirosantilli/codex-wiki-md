In the chiral coordinate $y^\mu=x^\mu+i\bar\theta\bar\sigma^\mu\theta$, a [chiral superfield](../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) is

$$
\Phi(y,\theta)=\phi(y)+\sqrt2\,\theta^\alpha\psi_\alpha(y)+\theta^\alpha\theta_\alpha F(y).
$$

Here $\phi$ is a complex scalar, $\psi_\alpha$ is a two-component [Weyl spinor](../../../../../weyl-spinor.md), and $F$ is a complex [auxiliary field](../../../../../auxiliary-field.md). Off shell, $\phi$ and $F$ contribute two real bosonic components each, while $\psi$ has two complex Grassmann components. Thus there are four real bosonic and four real fermionic degrees of freedom.
