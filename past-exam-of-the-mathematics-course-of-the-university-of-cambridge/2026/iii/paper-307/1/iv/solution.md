[Soft supersymmetry breaking](../../../../../../soft-supersymmetry-breaking.md) means adding supersymmetry-breaking operators with positive-mass-dimension coefficients that do not create new ultraviolet quadratic divergences. Standard examples are scalar masses, holomorphic bilinear and trilinear scalar couplings, gaugino masses, and singlet tadpoles when the field content and symmetries allow them.
