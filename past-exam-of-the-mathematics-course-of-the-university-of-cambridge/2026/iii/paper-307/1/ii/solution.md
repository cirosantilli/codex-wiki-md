↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The canonical [Kähler potential](../../../../../../kahler-potential.md) is

$$
K=\sum_{i=1}^3\Phi_i^\dagger\Phi_i.
$$

Under $\Phi\mapsto U\Phi$ with $U\in SU(3)$, it becomes $\Phi^\dagger U^\dagger U\Phi=K$, so it respects $G$.
