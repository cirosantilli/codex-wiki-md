<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) $\phi:M\to M$ is a smooth bijection with smooth inverse. It acts covariantly on a differential form by the [pullback of a differential form](../../../../../../pullback-of-a-differential-form.md):

$$
\omega\longmapsto\phi^*\omega.
$$

For vector fields $X_0,\ldots,X_p$,

$$
(\phi^*\omega)_x(X_1,\ldots,X_p)
=\omega_{\phi(x)}(d\phi_xX_1,\ldots,d\phi_xX_p).
$$

The coordinate definition of the [exterior derivative](../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md), or its characterization as the unique natural graded derivation extending the differential of functions, gives

$$
\boxed{d(\phi^*\omega)=\phi^*(d\omega).}
$$
