↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Hodge star operator](../../../../../../hodge-star-operator.md) is defined by

$$
\alpha\wedge *_g\beta
=\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle_g\,\operatorname{vol}_g
$$

for forms of the same degree. In four dimensions, under $\widehat g=e^{2\psi}g$, the inner product on $p$-forms scales by $e^{-2p\psi}$ and the volume form scales by $e^{4\psi}$. Hence

$$
*_{\widehat g}\big|_{\Omega^p}
=e^{(4-2p)\psi}*_g.
$$

For $p=2$ the exponent vanishes, so the Hodge star on two-forms is conformally invariant.
