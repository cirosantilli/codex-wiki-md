<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The flow of a [Killing vector field](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) consists of local isometries. Isometries preserve both the metric and its unique torsion-free metric-compatible [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md), hence $\mathcal L_K\nabla=0$. Set $X=K$ and $Y=Z=U$ in part (b). Since $\nabla_UU=0$,

$$
0=R(K,U)U+\nabla_U\nabla_UK,
$$

and antisymmetry of the first two curvature arguments gives

$$
\nabla_U\nabla_UK=R(U,K)U.
$$

This is the [geodesic deviation](../../../../../../geodesic-deviation.md) equation with connecting field $K$: applying the isometry to the original geodesic produces a neighboring geodesic, and curvature determines their relative acceleration.
