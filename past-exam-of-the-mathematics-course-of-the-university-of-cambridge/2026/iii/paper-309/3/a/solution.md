An [orthonormal frame in spacetime](../../../../../../orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) is a local basis $\{e_a\}$ satisfying $g(e_a,e_b)=\eta_{ab}$. The metric determines it only up to a point-dependent Lorentz transformation, with orientation and time orientation optionally restricting the component of the Lorentz group. The [orthonormal coframe in spacetime](../../../../../../orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) is defined by

$$
\theta^a(e_b)=\delta^a_b,
$$

and the metric is

$$
\boxed{g=\eta_{ab}\theta^a\otimes\theta^b.}
$$
