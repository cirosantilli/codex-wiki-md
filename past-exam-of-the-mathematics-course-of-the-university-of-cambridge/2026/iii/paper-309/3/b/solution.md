↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [connection 1-forms](../../../../../../connection-1-form-split.md) are defined by $\nabla e_b=\omega^a{}_b\otimes e_a$. Metric compatibility gives $\omega_{ab}=-\omega_{ba}$, while vanishing torsion gives Cartan's first structure equation

$$
d\theta^a+\omega^a{}_b\wedge\theta^b=0.
$$

The [curvature 2-forms](../../../../../../curvature-2-form.md) are

$$
\mathcal R^a{}_b=d\omega^a{}_b+\omega^a{}_c\wedge\omega^c{}_b
=\frac12R^a{}_{bcd}\theta^c\wedge\theta^d,
$$

which displays their relation to the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md).
