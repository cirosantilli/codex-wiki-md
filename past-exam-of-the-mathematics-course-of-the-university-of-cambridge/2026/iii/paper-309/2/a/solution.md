<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An infinitesimal coordinate change generated by $\xi_a$ acts on the [trace-reversed metric perturbation](../../../../../../trace-reversed-metric-perturbation.md) as

$$
\bar h_{ab}\longmapsto\bar h_{ab}
-\partial_a\xi_b-\partial_b\xi_a
+\eta_{ab}\partial_c\xi^c.
$$

Its divergence changes by

$$
\partial^a\bar h_{ab}\longmapsto
\partial^a\bar h_{ab}-\Box\xi_b.
$$

Solving $\Box\xi_b=\partial^a\bar h_{ab}$ therefore imposes [Lorenz gauge in linearized gravity](../../../../../../lorenz-gauge-in-linearized-gravity.md), $\partial^a\bar h_{ab}=0$. Transformations satisfying $\Box\xi_b=0$ remain and form the [residual gauge symmetry of linearized gravity](../../../../../../residual-gauge-symmetry-of-linearized-gravity.md).
