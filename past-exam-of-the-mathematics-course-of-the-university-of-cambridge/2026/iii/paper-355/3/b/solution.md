<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\theta=a_1\cos(\pi s/L)$, expansion through fourth order gives

$$
\widetilde E:=E-FL
=\boxed{\frac L4(F_c-F)a_1^2
+\frac{FL}{64}a_1^4},
$$

where

$$
\boxed{F_c=\frac{A\pi^2}{L^2}}.
$$

Stationarity gives

$$
a_1=0
\quad\text{or}\quad
\boxed{a_1^2=\frac{8(F-F_c)}F
=\frac{8\epsilon}{1+\epsilon}},
\qquad
\epsilon=\frac{F-F_c}{F_c}.
$$

The two signs of $a_1$ are the symmetry-related buckled states of a supercritical [pitchfork bifurcation](../../../../../../pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md).

The projected length is $L_\parallel=\int_0^L\cos\theta\,ds$. Hence

$$
C=1-\frac{L_\parallel}{L}
=\frac{a_1^2}{4}-\frac{a_1^4}{64}+O(a_1^6).
$$

On the buckled branch,

$$
\boxed{C(F)=\frac{2\epsilon+\epsilon^2}{(1+\epsilon)^2}
=2\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2)}.
$$
