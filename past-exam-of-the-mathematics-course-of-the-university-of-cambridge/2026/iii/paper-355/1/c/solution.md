<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Far from the midpoint at $\operatorname{Sp}\gg1$, the filament aligns with the flow: $\theta=\pi/2$, $K=\mathcal N=0$. The tangential equation and free-end condition give the outer tension

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T_{\rm out}(\xi)
=\frac{\operatorname{Sp}}2(1-\xi)}.
$$

Near $\xi=0$, the outer tension is $\operatorname{Sp}/2$ to leading order. The normal equation is dominated by bending and tension,

$$
K''-\frac{\operatorname{Sp}}2K=0,
$$

because the direct normal drag is smaller by $\operatorname{Sp}^{-1/2}$ in the turning layer. Hence the decaying inner curvature is

$$
\boxed{K(\xi)=C
\exp\!\left[-\sqrt{\frac{\operatorname{Sp}}2}\,\xi\right]},
$$

where matching and the midpoint angle determine $C$. The [elastohydrodynamic boundary layer of a filament](../../../../../../elastohydrodynamic-boundary-layer-of-a-filament.md) therefore has scaled arclength

$$
\boxed{\delta\xi=O(\operatorname{Sp}^{-1/2})}.
$$
