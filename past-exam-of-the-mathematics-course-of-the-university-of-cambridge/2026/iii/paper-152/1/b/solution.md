<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Insert the primitive ray

$$
v_3=(0,-1)=\frac12(v_0+v_1)
$$

inside the singular cone. The corresponding [star subdivision](../../../../../../star-subdivision.md) replaces $\langle v_0,v_1\rangle$ by $\langle v_0,v_3\rangle$ and $\langle v_3,v_1\rangle$. Both new determinants have absolute value one, as do the two unchanged cones, so the subdivided fan is smooth. The induced proper birational [toric morphism](../../../../../../toric-morphism.md) $\widetilde X\to X$ is therefore a [toric resolution of singularities](../../../../../../toric-resolution-of-singularities.md). Its exceptional invariant curve has self-intersection $-2$, and $\widetilde X$ is the [Hirzebruch surface](../../../../../../hirzebruch-surface.md) $\mathbb F_2$.
