<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Cox construction](../../../../../../cox-construction.md) uses coordinates $x_1,\ldots,x_4$ of degrees

$$
\deg x_1=\deg x_3=F,
\qquad
\deg x_2=S,
\qquad
\deg x_4=S+kF.
$$

Its irrelevant locus is

$$
V(x_1,x_3)\cup V(x_2,x_4),
$$

so put $U=\mathbb A^4\setminus\bigl(V(x_1,x_3)\cup V(x_2,x_4)\bigr)$. The [algebraic torus](../../../../../../algebraic-torus.md) $G=(\mathbb G_m)^2$ acts by

$$
(\lambda,\mu)\mathbin{\cdot}(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
=(\mu x_1,\lambda x_2,\mu x_3,\lambda\mu^k x_4).
$$

Every point of $U$ has a nonzero coordinate from each opposing pair, so the action has the expected closed orbits and trivial stabilizers. The [geometric quotient](../../../../../../geometric-quotient.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb F_k=U/G.}
$$
