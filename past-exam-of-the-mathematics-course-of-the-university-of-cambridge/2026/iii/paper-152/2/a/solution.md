<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the invariant prime divisors in the order of the rays

$$
v_1=(1,0),\quad v_2=(0,1),\quad v_3=(-1,k),\quad v_4=(0,-1)
$$

as $D_1,\ldots,D_4$. The [toric divisor class sequence](../../../../../../toric-divisor-class-sequence.md) is

$$
0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^4
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Cl}(\mathbb F_k)\longrightarrow0,
$$

and the two characters in the standard basis of $M$ give

$$
D_1-D_3=0,
\qquad
D_2+kD_3-D_4=0.
$$

Thus

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(\mathbb F_k)
\cong\mathbb Z[S]\oplus\mathbb Z[F],
$$

where $S=D_2$, $F=D_1=D_3$, and $D_4=S+kF$. Since the fan is smooth, this is also the [Picard group](../../../../../../picard-group.md).

The [orbit-cone correspondence](../../../../../../orbit-cone-correspondence.md) decomposes $\mathbb F_k$ into one two-dimensional torus, four one-dimensional torus orbits, and four fixed points. Only the zero-dimensional orbits contribute to the compactly supported [Euler characteristic](../../../../../../euler-characteristic.md), so

$$
\boxed{\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(\mathbb F_k)=4.}
$$
