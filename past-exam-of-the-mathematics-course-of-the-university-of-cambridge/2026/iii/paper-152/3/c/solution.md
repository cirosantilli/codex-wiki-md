<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two standard [affine](../../../../../../affine-scheme.md) charts of the blowup restrict to an affine open cover of $X_0$. On the $x$-chart write $y=xu$. Since $xy=x^2u$, the fiber is

$$
U_x\cap X_0=\operatorname{Spec}k[x,u]/(x^2u).
$$

On the $y$-chart write $x=yv$, giving

$$
U_y\cap X_0=\operatorname{Spec}k[y,v]/(y^2v).
$$

On their overlap, $u$ and $v$ are invertible with $v=u^{-1}$ and $y=xu$. The squared factors $x^2$ and $y^2$ display the doubled exceptional component explicitly.
