<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a torus character $\chi^m$, its [principal divisor on a toric variety](../../../../../../principal-divisor-on-a-toric-variety.md) is

$$
\operatorname{div}(\chi^m)=\sum_\rho\langle m,v_\rho\rangle D_\rho.
$$

Here $t=xy=\chi^{(1,1)}$, so on the blown-up fan

$$
\operatorname{div}(f^*t)=D_{e_1}+2D_{e_1+e_2}+D_{e_2}.
$$

The [scheme-theoretic fiber](../../../../../../scheme-theoretic-fiber.md) $X_0=V(f^*t)$ therefore contains the exceptional divisor $D_{e_1+e_2}$ with multiplicity two. Its defining ideal is not radical along that divisor, so $X_0$ is a [nonreduced scheme](../../../../../../nonreduced-scheme.md).
