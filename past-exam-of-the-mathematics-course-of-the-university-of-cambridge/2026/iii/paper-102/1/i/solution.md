<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For this [Heisenberg Lie algebra](../../../../../../heisenberg-lie-algebra.md),

$$
[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]=\mathbb Cc,
\qquad
[\mathfrak g,\mathbb Cc]=0,
$$

because $c$ belongs to the [center](../../../../../../center-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Thus its [lower central series of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../lower-central-series-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is $\mathfrak g,\mathbb Cc,0$, so $\mathfrak g$ is a two-step [nilpotent Lie algebra](../../../../../../nilpotent-lie-algebra.md).
