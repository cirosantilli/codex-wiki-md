<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $C$ be the [fundamental chamber of a root system](../../../../../../fundamental-chamber-of-a-root-system.md). The chambers $wC$ and $ws_\alpha C$ are adjacent across the reflecting hyperplane orthogonal to $w(\alpha)$. The chamber $wC$ lies on the side on which $w(\alpha)$ is positive. If $w(\alpha)\in\Pi$, then $C$ lies on that same side, so crossing this wall moves one step farther from $C$; if $w(\alpha)\in-\Pi$, it moves one step nearer. The gallery distance from $C$ to $wC$ is the [Coxeter length](../../../../../../coxeter-length.md) $\ell(w)$, and adjacent chamber distances differ by one. Consequently

$$
\boxed{w(\alpha)\in\Pi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\ell(ws_\alpha)=\ell(w)+1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\ell(ws_\alpha)>\ell(w).}
$$
