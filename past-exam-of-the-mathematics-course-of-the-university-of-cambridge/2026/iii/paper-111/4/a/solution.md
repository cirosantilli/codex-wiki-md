The [Coxeter number](../../../../../../coxeter-number.md) is the order of a [Coxeter element](../../../../../../coxeter-element.md). In the stated families the values are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
W&\text{Coxeter type}&h\\ \hline
D_{2n}&I_2(n)&n\\
S_n&A_{n-1}&n\\
SS_n&B_n&2n\\
ESS_n&D_n&2n-2.
\end{array}
$$

Here $D_{2n}$ is the [dihedral group](../../../../../../dihedral-group.md) of order $2n$, $S_n$ is the [symmetric group](../../../../../../symmetric-group.md), $SS_n$ is the [signed symmetric group](../../../../../../hyperoctahedral-group.md), and $ESS_n$ is the [even signed symmetric group](../../../../../../even-signed-symmetric-group.md).
