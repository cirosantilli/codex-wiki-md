The [Coxeter graph](../../../../../../coxeter-graph.md) has vertex set $I$. Distinct vertices $i,j$ are joined precisely when $m_{ij}\geq3$, and the edge is labelled $m_{ij}$ when $m_{ij}>3$; the customary unlabelled edge therefore means $m_{ij}=3$. The [Coxeter system](../../../../../../coxeter-system.md) is an [Irreducible Coxeter system](../../../../../../irreducible-coxeter-system.md) precisely when this graph is [connected](../../../../../../connected-graph.md).
