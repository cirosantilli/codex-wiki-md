<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $V$ be the real [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) with basis $(e_i)_{i\in I}$. The [Coxeter Gram matrix](../../../../../../coxeter-gram-matrix.md) defines the symmetric [bilinear form](../../../../../../bilinear-form.md)

$$
\langle e_i,e_j\rangle=G(W)_{ij}=-2\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{m_{ij}}\right).
$$

Its diagonal entries are $2$. The [Geometric representation of a Coxeter group](../../../../../../geometric-representation-of-a-coxeter-group.md) is generated by the reflections

$$
\sigma(x_i)(v)=v-\langle v,e_i\rangle e_i.
$$

Each has square one, and on $\operatorname{span}\{e_i,e_j\}$ the product $\sigma(x_i)\sigma(x_j)$ has order $m_{ij}$. The reflections therefore satisfy the Coxeter relations and define a [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) $\sigma:W\to GL(V)$.
