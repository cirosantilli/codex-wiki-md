<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Every [Hecke parameter of a BN-pair](../../../../../../hecke-parameter-of-a-bn-pair.md) $q_i$ divides $|B|$ and, because $B$ is a $q$-group, is a power of $q$. If $q\equiv1\pmod p$, then $q_i=1$ in the field $k$ of [characteristic](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$. The specialized quadratic relation becomes

$$
T_i^2=1,
$$

while the braid relations are unchanged. These are the defining relations of the [Coxeter group](../../../../../../coxeter-group.md) $W$, so $x_i\mapsto T_i$ induces a surjective homomorphism

$$
k[W]\longrightarrow H_k(G,B).
$$

Both algebras have bases indexed by $W$, hence the homomorphism is an isomorphism of [group algebras](../../../../../../group-algebra.md).
