<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [BN-pair](../../../../../../bn-pair.md) consists of subgroups $B,N\leq G$ for which $G=\langle B,N\rangle$, the subgroup $H=B\cap N$ is normal in $N$, the quotient $W=N/H$ is generated by a distinguished set $S$ of involutions, and the Bruhat multiplication and nondegeneracy axioms hold. The quotient is the associated [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md), and the axioms give the [Bruhat decomposition of a BN-pair](../../../../../../bruhat-decomposition-of-a-bn-pair.md)

$$
G=\bigsqcup_{w\in W}B\dot wB.
$$

The [Iwahori-Hecke algebra of a BN-pair](../../../../../../iwahori-hecke-algebra-of-a-bn-pair.md) may be defined, up to the usual opposite-algebra convention, by

$$
H_k(G,B)=\operatorname{End}_{kG}(k[G/B]).
$$

Its standard basis $(T_w)_{w\in W}$ is indexed by the Bruhat double cosets. For a simple generator $x_i$ represented by $\dot x_i\in N$, set

$$
q_i=[B:B\cap\dot x_iB\dot x_i^{-1}].
$$

The double-coset multiplication rule is the generic rule from part a with $a_i$ specialized to $q_i\cdot1_k$. Thus $H_k(G,B)$ is a [specialization](../../../../../../specialization-of-an-algebra.md) of the generic algebra.
