<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $\mathbb E X_1=\mu$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X_1)=\sigma^2<\infty$. For rational $t=m/n$, stationarity and independence of the $n$ increments over intervals of length $1/n$ give

$$
\mathbb E X_t=t\mu,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(X_t)=t\sigma^2,
$$

using [variance additivity for independent random variables](../../../../../../variance-additivity-for-independent-random-variables.md). [Stochastic continuity](../../../../../../stochastic-continuity.md) extends both identities from rational to real $t$. In the centered case $\mu=0$, this becomes $\mathbb E X_t=0$ and $\mathbb E X_t^2=t\sigma^2$.
