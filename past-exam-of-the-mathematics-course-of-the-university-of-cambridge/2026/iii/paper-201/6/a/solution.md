↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Lévy process](../../../../../../levy-process.md) $(X_t)_{t\geq0}$ starts at zero, has [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) and stationary increments, is [stochastically continuous](../../../../../../stochastic-continuity.md), and is taken with [càdlàg](../../../../../../cadlag.md) sample paths. Thus for $0\leq t_0<\cdots<t_n$, the increments $X_{t_j}-X_{t_{j-1}}$ are independent, and the law of $X_{s+t}-X_s$ depends only on $t$.
