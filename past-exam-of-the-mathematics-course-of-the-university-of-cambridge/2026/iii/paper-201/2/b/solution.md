<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ be [independent and identically distributed random variables](../../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md), let $S_n=\sum_{j=1}^nX_j$, and write $m=\mathbb E X_1$. [Cramér theorem](../../../../../../cramer-s-theorem.md) states that the empirical means $S_n/n$ obey a [large deviation principle](../../../../../../large-deviation-principle.md) with good [rate function](../../../../../../rate-function.md) $\psi^*$. In particular, for $x\geq m$,

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log\mathbb P(S_n/n\geq x)=-\psi^*(x),
$$

with the usual extended-real interpretation; the analogous lower-tail formula holds for $x\leq m$.
