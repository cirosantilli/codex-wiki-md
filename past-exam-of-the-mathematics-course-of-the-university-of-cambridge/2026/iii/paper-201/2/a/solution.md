<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an integrable real [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $X$, its [cumulant-generating function](../../../../../../cumulant-generating-function.md) is the extended-real [convex function](../../../../../../convex-function.md)

$$
\psi(\theta)=\log\mathbb E[e^{\theta X}],
\qquad \theta\in\mathbb R,
$$

where $\psi(\theta)=+\infty$ if the [exponential moment](../../../../../../exponential-moment.md) diverges. Its [Legendre transform of a cumulant-generating function](../../../../../../legendre-transform-of-a-cumulant-generating-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{\psi^*(x)=\sup_{\theta\in\mathbb R}\{\theta x-\psi(\theta)\}.}
$$
