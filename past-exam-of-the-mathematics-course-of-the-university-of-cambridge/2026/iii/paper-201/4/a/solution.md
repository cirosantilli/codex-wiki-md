<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) $(B_t)_{t\geq0}$ satisfies $B_0=0$ almost surely, has almost surely [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) sample paths, and has independent stationary increments such that

$$
\boxed{B_t-B_s\sim N(0,t-s)
\qquad(0\leq s<t).}
$$
