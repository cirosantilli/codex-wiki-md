<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The recursive theory $T=PA^-+\varphi$ is consistent because $\mathbb N\models T$. By the Gödel-Rosser theorem it has an undecidable sentence $\rho$, so both $T+\rho$ and $T+\neg\rho$ are consistent. Exactly one of $\rho,\neg\rho$ is false in $\mathbb N$; add that one to $T$. The first-order completeness theorem gives a model, and the [Downward Lowenheim-Skolem theorem](../../../../../../downward-lowenheim-skolem-theorem.md) gives a countable model $M$. Then $M\models PA^-+\varphi$, but $M$ disagrees with $\mathbb N$ on the chosen sentence and is therefore not elementarily equivalent to it.
