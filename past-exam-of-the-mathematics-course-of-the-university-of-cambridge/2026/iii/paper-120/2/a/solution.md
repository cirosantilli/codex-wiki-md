A [Sigma-1 formula](../../../../../../sigma-1-formula.md) is a formula equivalent in first-order arithmetic to

$$
\exists y_1\cdots\exists y_k\,\delta,
$$

where $\delta$ is bounded. A [Pi-1 formula](../../../../../../pi-1-formula.md) is similarly equivalent to $\forall y_1\cdots\forall y_k\,\delta$ with bounded $\delta$.
