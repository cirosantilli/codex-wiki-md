<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A partial function $f:\mathbb N^k\rightharpoonup\mathbb N$ is [lambda-definable](../../../../../../lambda-definable-partial-function.md) if there is a lambda term $F$ such that

$$
F c_{n_1}\cdots c_{n_k}\equiv_\beta c_{f(n_1,\ldots,n_k)}
$$

whenever the value is defined, while outside the domain the application reduces to no [Church numeral](../../../../../../church-numeral.md).
