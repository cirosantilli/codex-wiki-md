The [Strong normalization theorem for simply typed lambda calculus](../../../../../../strong-normalization-theorem-for-simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md) says that every well-typed term has no infinite beta-reduction sequence. In the untyped calculus,

$$
\Omega=(\lambda x.xx)(\lambda x.xx)
$$

reduces to itself and is therefore not strongly normalizing.
