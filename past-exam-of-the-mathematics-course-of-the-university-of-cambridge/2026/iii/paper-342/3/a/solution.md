<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The inverse definitions are

$$
a_j=\frac12(c_{2j-1}+ic_{2j}),
\qquad
a_j^\dagger=\frac12(c_{2j-1}-ic_{2j}).
$$

Substitution into the hopping and pairing terms expresses the Hamiltonian as a bilinear in the [Majorana fermion operators](../../../../../../majorana-fermion-operator.md). Hermiticity makes its off-diagonal coefficients purely imaginary in the Majorana bilinear, while

$$
c_jc_k=-c_kc_j\qquad(j\ne k)
$$

removes the symmetric part; diagonal terms are constants because $c_j^2=1$. Therefore, up to that additive constant,

$$
\boxed{
H=\frac i4\sum_{j,k=1}^{2M}A_{jk}c_jc_k},
$$

where

$$
\boxed{A_{jk}\in\mathbb R,\qquad A^T=-A}.
$$

Conversely every real antisymmetric $A$ makes this expression Hermitian, so this is the general Majorana form of a [quadratic fermion Hamiltonian](../../../../../../quadratic-fermion-hamiltonian.md).
