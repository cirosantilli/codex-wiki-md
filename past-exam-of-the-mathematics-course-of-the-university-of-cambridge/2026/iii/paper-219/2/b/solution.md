↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The simulation catalog is a sample from the joint prior. [Self-normalized importance sampling](../../../../../../self-normalized-importance-sampling.md) therefore estimates the posterior mean by

$$
\overline m_w=\sum_{i=1}^Km_iw_i,
\qquad
w_i=\frac{p(d\mid x_i)}{\sum_{j=1}^Kp(d\mid x_j)}.
$$

The likelihood weights update each simulated system according to how well its satellite resembles the measured one.
