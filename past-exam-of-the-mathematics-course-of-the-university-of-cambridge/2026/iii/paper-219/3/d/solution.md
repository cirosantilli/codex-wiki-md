<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Discard burn-in from the MCMC output and retain the sampled $\Delta t$ coordinate. A normalized histogram or [kernel density estimation](../../../../../../kernel-density-estimation.md) of these draws approximates $p(\Delta t\mid D)$. Autocorrelation changes the Monte Carlo uncertainty, so uncertainty bands should use the chain's effective sample size rather than its raw length.
