↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A real [positive-semidefinite kernel](../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-kernel.md) is a symmetric function $k:X\times X\to\mathbb R$ such that every finite Gram matrix $(k(x_i,x_j))$ is positive semidefinite. The [Moore-Aronszajn theorem](../../../../../../moore-aronszajn-theorem.md) says that there are a Hilbert space $H$ and a feature map $\phi:X\to H$ such that

$$
k(x,y)=\langle\phi(x),\phi(y)\rangle_H.
$$

Equivalently, $H$ can be chosen as the unique [Reproducing kernel Hilbert space](../../../../../../reproducing-kernel-hilbert-space.md) with reproducing kernel $k$.
