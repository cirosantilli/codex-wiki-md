<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The $\xi_i$ are [slack variables of a support vector machine](../../../../../../slack-variables-of-a-support-vector-machine.md). The solid line is the [support-vector-machine decision boundary](../../../../../../support-vector-machine-decision-boundary.md)

$$
\widehat\alpha+x^\top\widehat\beta=0.
$$

The dashed lines are the two [support-vector-machine margin boundaries](../../../../../../support-vector-machine-margin-boundaries.md)

$$
\widehat\alpha+x^\top\widehat\beta=1,
\qquad
\widehat\alpha+x^\top\widehat\beta=-1.
$$

Each is at perpendicular distance $1/\lVert\widehat\beta\rVert_2$ from the decision boundary, so the full margin width is $2/\lVert\widehat\beta\rVert_2$.
