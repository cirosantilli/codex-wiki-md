<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Increasing $C$ raises the cost of [slack variables of a support vector machine](../../../../../../slack-variables-of-a-support-vector-machine.md). The fit therefore generally accepts fewer margin violations and misclassifications, at the price of a larger $\lVert\widehat\beta\rVert_2$ and hence a narrower [support-vector-machine margin](../../../../../../support-vector-machine-margin.md). Fewer observations will generally lie on or inside the narrower margin, so the number of [support vectors](../../../../../../support-vector.md) tends to decrease. These are qualitative tendencies; individual counts need not vary monotonically for every data set.
