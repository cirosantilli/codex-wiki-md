<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The neural-network likelihood is nonconvex, so optimization can stop at a local optimum or saddle rather than a global maximum. One hundred epochs may be insufficient for convergence. Mini-batch gradient noise together with a fixed positive learning rate can keep the iterates fluctuating around a stationary point rather than reaching it exactly. Any of these prevents the final parameters from being exact [maximum-likelihood estimators](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md).
