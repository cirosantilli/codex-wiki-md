<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

By the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), part (b), and part (e),

$$
\mathbb E|\widehat p_1(x)-p_1(x)|
\leq\frac1{\sqrt L}
+C\left\{
\left(\frac{2L}{nM}\right)^{1/d}+\frac2L
\right\}.
$$

This bound tends uniformly to zero if

$$
L\longrightarrow\infty,
\qquad \frac Ln\longrightarrow0.
$$

The [plug-in classifier excess-risk bound](../../../../../../plug-in-classifier-excess-risk-bound.md) then shows that the misclassification risk of the nearest-neighbour classifier converges to the [Bayes risk](../../../../../../bayes-risk.md).
