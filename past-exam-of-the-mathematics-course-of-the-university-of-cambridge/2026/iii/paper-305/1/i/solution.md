<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The continuous symmetry is

$$
G=U(N)_L\times U(N)_R,
\qquad
M\longmapsto LMR^\dagger.
$$

Strictly, the diagonal central $U(1)$ acts trivially, so the faithful group is the quotient by that common center. Since $L$ and $R$ are [unitary matrices](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md),

$$
\partial_\mu M\longmapsto L(\partial_\mu M)R^\dagger,
\qquad
M^\dagger M\longmapsto R(M^\dagger M)R^\dagger.
$$

Every term is therefore unchanged by cyclicity of the [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md):

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(\partial^\mu M^\dagger\partial_\mu M)
\longmapsto
\operatorname{Tr}[R(\partial^\mu M^\dagger\partial_\mu M)R^\dagger],
$$

and the same conjugation argument applies to $\operatorname{Tr}(M^\dagger M)$ and $\operatorname{Tr}[(M^\dagger M)^2]$.
