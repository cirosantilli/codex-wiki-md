↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A global symmetry is [spontaneously broken](../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) when it preserves the action but does not preserve a chosen [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md). If $G$ is broken to the stabilizer $H$, the degenerate vacua form a [vacuum manifold](../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md) $G/H$. The [Goldstone theorem](../../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) states that a relativistic theory has one massless scalar mode for each broken continuous internal generator, so the standard counting gives $\dim G-\dim H$ Goldstone bosons.
