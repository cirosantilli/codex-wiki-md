<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Every vacuum can be written as $M=vU$ with $U(x)\in U(N)$. Freezing the massive radial modes and substituting this parameterization into the action gives, up to a constant vacuum energy,

$$
S_{\rm eff}=v^2\int d^4x\,
\operatorname{Tr}(\partial^\mu U^\dagger\partial_\mu U)+\cdots.
$$

Writing $U=\exp(i\pi^aT^a/v)$ displays the $N^2$ Goldstone fields $\pi^a$. The omitted terms contain more derivatives or arise from integrating out radial excitations, so this is the leading [nonlinear sigma model](../../../../../../nonlinear-sigma-model.md) on the [vacuum manifold](../../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md) $U(N)$.
