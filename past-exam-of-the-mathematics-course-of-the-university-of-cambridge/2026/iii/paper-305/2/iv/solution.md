<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Expanding $F_{\mu\nu}$, using antisymmetry of $\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}$, and cyclically rearranging the [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(F_{\mu\nu}{}^\star F^{\mu\nu})
=\partial_\mu K^\mu,
$$

where the [Chern-Simons current](../../../../../../chern-simons-current.md) is

$$
K^\mu=2\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}
\operatorname{Tr}\left(
A_\nu\partial_\rho A_\sigma-\frac{2ig}{3}A_\nu A_\rho A_\sigma
\right).
$$

The theta contribution to the action is therefore a boundary term. For variations that vanish at the spacetime boundary, its variation vanishes, so it contributes nothing to the classical [Euler-Lagrange field equations](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md). Its integral can still distinguish topologically nontrivial gauge configurations in the quantum theory.
