<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $\delta F_{\mu\nu}=-ig[F_{\mu\nu},\alpha]$, both $F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}$ and $F_{\mu\nu}{}^\star F^{\mu\nu}$ vary by a commutator. Cyclicity of the [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) therefore makes both terms gauge invariant. In electric and magnetic variables,

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})
\ \text{is proportional to}\ 
\operatorname{Tr}(\mathbf B^2-\mathbf E^2),
\qquad
\operatorname{Tr}(F_{\mu\nu}{}^\star F^{\mu\nu})
\ \text{is proportional to}\ 
\operatorname{Tr}(\mathbf E\mathbin\cdot\mathbf B).
$$

The Yang-Mills kinetic term is even under $C$, $P$, and $T$. The [Yang-Mills theta term](../../../../../../yang-mills-theta-term.md) is even under $C$ because both fields change sign and transpose, but odd under $P$ and under $T$ because exactly one of $\mathbf E,\mathbf B$ changes sign.
