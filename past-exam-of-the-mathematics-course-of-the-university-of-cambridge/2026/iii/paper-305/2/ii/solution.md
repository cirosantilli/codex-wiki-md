<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With matrix-valued fields, [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) acts as

$$
C:\quad A_\mu(t,\mathbf x)\mapsto-A_\mu^T(t,\mathbf x),
\qquad
F_{\mu\nu}\mapsto-F_{\mu\nu}^T.
$$

Under [parity](../../../../../../parity.md), $A_0$ is even and $A_i$ is odd:

$$
P:\quad A_0(t,\mathbf x)\mapsto A_0(t,-\mathbf x),
\qquad
A_i(t,\mathbf x)\mapsto-A_i(t,-\mathbf x).
$$

Consequently $F_{0i}$ is odd and $F_{ij}$ is even. Under [time-reversal symmetry](../../../../../../t-symmetry.md), which is antiunitary, $A_0$ is even and $A_i$ is odd after $t\mapsto-t$; hence $F_{0i}$ is even and $F_{ij}$ is odd. Equivalently, the non-Abelian electric and magnetic fields transform as

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
&C&P&T\\ \hline
\mathbf E&-\mathbf E^T&-\mathbf E&+\mathbf E\\
\mathbf B&-\mathbf B^T&+\mathbf B&-\mathbf B
\end{array}.
$$
