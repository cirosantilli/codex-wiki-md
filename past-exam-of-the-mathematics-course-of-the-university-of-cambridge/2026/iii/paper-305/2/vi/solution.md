<h1 id="2/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Since $\partial_\mu K^\mu=\operatorname{Tr}(F_{\mu\nu}{}^\star F^{\mu\nu})$, define

$$
\widetilde J_A^\mu=J_A^\mu-\frac{N_fg^2}{8\pi^2}K^\mu.
$$

The anomaly equation gives $\partial_\mu\widetilde J_A^\mu=0$. The price is that the [Chern-Simons current](../../../../../../chern-simons-current.md) $K^\mu$ is not gauge invariant, so neither is $\widetilde J_A^\mu$.
