↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Varying the [Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../../yang-mills-theory.md) field strength and using $\delta A_\mu=D_\mu\alpha$ gives

$$
\delta F_{\mu\nu}
=D_\mu D_\nu\alpha-D_\nu D_\mu\alpha
=-ig[F_{\mu\nu},\alpha]
=ig[\alpha,F_{\mu\nu}].
$$

**Thus $F_{\mu\nu}$ transforms covariantly in the adjoint representation.**
