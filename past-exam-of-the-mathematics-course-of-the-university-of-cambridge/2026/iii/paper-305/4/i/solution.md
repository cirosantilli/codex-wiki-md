<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [unitary gauge](../../../../../../unitary-gauge.md) to set

$$
H=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0\\v+h\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Then

$$
(D_\mu H)^\dagger D^\mu H
=\frac12(\partial_\mu h)^2
+\frac{(v+h)^2}{8}
\left[g^2\{(W_\mu^1)^2+(W_\mu^2)^2\}
+(gW_\mu^3-g'B_\mu)^2\right].
$$

Define

$$
W_\mu^\pm=\frac{W_\mu^1\mp iW_\mu^2}{\sqrt2},
\quad
Z_\mu=\frac{gW_\mu^3-g'B_\mu}{\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}},
\quad
A_\mu=\frac{g'W_\mu^3+gB_\mu}{\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}}.
$$

The propagating fields are $W^\pm_\mu,Z_\mu,A_\mu$, and the [Higgs boson](../../../../../../higgs-boson.md) $h$. Expanding

$$
V(H)=\lambda\left(H^\dagger H-\frac{v^2}{2}\right)^2
=\lambda v^2h^2+\lambda vh^3+\frac\lambda4h^4
$$

and reading the quadratic terms gives

$$
m_W=\frac{gv}{2},
\qquad
m_Z=\frac{v}{2}\sqrt{g^2+g'^2},
\qquad
m_A=0,
\qquad
m_h=\sqrt{2\lambda}\,v.
$$

The factors $(v+h)^2$ in the kinetic term also give the corresponding Higgs-vector interactions.
