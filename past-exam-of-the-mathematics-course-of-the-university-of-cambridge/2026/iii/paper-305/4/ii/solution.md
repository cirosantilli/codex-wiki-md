<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The lower Higgs component has $T^3=-1/2$ and [hypercharge](../../../../../../hypercharge.md) $Y=1/2$, so the generator annihilating the vacuum is

$$
Q=T^3+Y.
$$

This is the unbroken electromagnetic $U(1)_Q$. Since $[Q,T^\pm]=\pm T^\pm$, the two [W boson](../../../../../../w-boson.md) fields have charges $Q(W^\pm)=\pm1$. The [Z boson](../../../../../../z-boson.md), photon, and physical Higgs excitation are neutral.
