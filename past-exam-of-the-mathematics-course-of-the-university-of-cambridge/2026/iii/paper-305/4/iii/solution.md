Under $SU(3)_c\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$, one [Standard Model fermion](../../../../../../standard-model-fermion.md) generation including a [right-handed neutrino](../../../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md) is

$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{field}&\text{representation}\\ \hline
Q_L=(u_L,d_L)&(\mathbf3,\mathbf2)_{1/6}\\
u_R&(\mathbf3,\mathbf1)_{2/3}\\
d_R&(\mathbf3,\mathbf1)_{-1/3}\\
L_L=(\nu_L,e_L)&(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{-1/2}\\
e_R&(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_{-1}\\
\nu_R&(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0
\end{array}.
$$
