<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the normalization supplied, a left-handed Weyl fermion in the two-index antisymmetric representation has cubic $SU(N)$ gauge-anomaly coefficient $N-4$, while an antifundamental has coefficient $-1$. Cancellation of the [gauge anomaly](../../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) requires

$$
(N-4)-p=0,
$$

so

$$
p=N-4.
$$
