A [gauge anomaly](../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) makes the gauge symmetry inconsistent and must cancel. An [Adler-Bell-Jackiw anomaly](../../../../../chiral-anomaly.md) breaks a classical global chiral symmetry quantum mechanically but does not by itself invalidate the gauge theory. A ['t Hooft anomaly](../../../../../t-hooft-anomaly.md) is an obstruction to gauging a global symmetry; it remains invariant along renormalization-group flow and constrains the infrared spectrum or symmetry-breaking pattern.
