<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the charges $q_\lambda=N-4$ and $q_\psi=-(N-2)$ and the index convention $I(\mathbf p)=1$. The $N$ gauge-colour copies of the flavour fundamental $\psi$ give

$$
\mathcal A_{SU(p)^3}=N,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{SU(p)^2U(1)}=Nq_\psi=-N(N-2).
$$

Summing the charge cubed and the charge over all Weyl components gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A_{U(1)^3}
&=\frac{N(N-1)}2(N-4)^3
+N(N-4)[-(N-2)]^3\\
&=-\frac{N^3(N-4)(N-3)}2,\\
\mathcal A_{U(1)\text{-grav}^2}
&=\frac{N(N-1)}2(N-4)
+N(N-4)[-(N-2)]\\
&=-\frac{N(N-4)(N-3)}2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Each of these four coefficients is an ultraviolet ['t Hooft anomaly](../../../../../../t-hooft-anomaly.md).
