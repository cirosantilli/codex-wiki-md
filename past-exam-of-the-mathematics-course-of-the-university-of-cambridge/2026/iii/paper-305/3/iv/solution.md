<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The composite $\chi^{ij}=\psi^{(i}(\lambda\psi^{j)})$ is a flavour-symmetric left-handed Weyl fermion. Its $U(1)$ charge is

$$
q_\chi=q_\lambda+2q_\psi=(N-4)-2(N-2)=-N.
$$

Because $p=N-4$, the supplied symmetric-representation data give

$$
A(\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathbf p)=p+4=N,
\qquad
I(\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathbf p)=p+2=N-2.
$$

Thus

$$
\mathcal A_{SU(p)^3}=N,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{SU(p)^2U(1)}=(N-2)(-N)=-N(N-2).
$$

There are $p(p+1)/2=(N-4)(N-3)/2$ components, so

$$
\mathcal A_{U(1)^3}=\frac{p(p+1)}2(-N)^3,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{U(1)\text{-grav}^2}=\frac{p(p+1)}2(-N),
$$

which exactly equal the ultraviolet values. The proposed confined spectrum therefore satisfies ['t Hooft anomaly matching](../../../../../../t-hooft-anomaly-matching.md).
