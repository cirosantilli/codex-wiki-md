↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Iterate the correction: after constructing $b_{j-1}\in S^{-(j-1)}$, set

$$
a_j=a\,b_{j-1}\in S^{-M-(j-1)}.
$$

The same calculation improves the remainder by one order:

$$
Q(x,\lambda+D_x)\sum_{j=1}^Na_j=1-b_N,\qquad b_N\in S^{-N}.
$$

Let $A_j$ and $B_N$ be the corresponding inverse [oscillatory integrals](../../../../../../../oscillatory-integral.md). Then

$$
\boxed{Q(x,D)(A_1+\cdots+A_N)=\delta_0-B_N}.
$$

When $N>n$, the frequency integral defining $B_N$ converges absolutely and depends continuously on $x$, so $B_N\in C(X)$. This completes the finite-order [parametrix](../../../../../../../parametrix.md) construction.
