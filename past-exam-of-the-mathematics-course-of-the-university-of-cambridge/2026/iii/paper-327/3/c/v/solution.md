<h1 id="3/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Take

$$
\boxed{a_2=a\,b_1}.
$$

By [symbol calculus](../../../../../../../symbol-calculus.md), $a_2\in S^{-M-1}=S^{N_Q-1}$. Its zeroth-order contribution is $Qa_2=b_1$ outside the compact transition region and therefore cancels the order $-1$ remainder. Every term in

$$
Q(x,\lambda+D_x)a_2-Qa_2
$$

contains at least one $\lambda$ derivative of $Q$ and has order at most $(M-1)+(-M-1)=-2$. Absorbing these terms and the smoothing cutoff contribution into $b_2$ gives

$$
\boxed{Q(x,\lambda+D_x)(a_1+a_2)=1-b_2},\qquad b_2\in S^{-2}.
$$
