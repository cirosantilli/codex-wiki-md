↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [path integral](../../../../../../path-integral.md) is obtained by slicing an evolution operator into short time intervals and inserting complete sets of field eigenstates between successive factors. An operator inserted at a later slice therefore appears to the left of one inserted at an earlier slice. Summing over the intermediate fields preserves this ordering, so real-time path integrals generate time-ordered correlators and Euclidean thermal path integrals generate [imaginary-time-ordered correlation functions](../../../../../../imaginary-time-ordered-correlation-function.md).
