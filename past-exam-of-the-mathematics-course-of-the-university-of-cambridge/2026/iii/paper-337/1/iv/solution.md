<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Let $\beta=1/T$ and $A(\tau)=e^{H\tau}Ae^{-H\tau}$. For $0<\tau<\beta$, cyclicity of the trace gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(e^{-\beta H}A(\tau)A(0)\right)
=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(e^{-\beta H}A(0)A(\tau-\beta)\right).
$$

This is the bosonic [Kubo--Martin--Schwinger condition](../../../../../../kubo-martin-schwinger-condition.md). It identifies the two time orderings across the end of the thermal interval, so

$$
\boxed{C(\tau+\beta)=C(\tau)}.
$$
