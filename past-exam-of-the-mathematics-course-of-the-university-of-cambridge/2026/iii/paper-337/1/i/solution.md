<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The defining integral for the [retarded Green function](../../../../../../retarded-green-function.md) has support only at $t\geq0$. If $\omega=\omega_R+i\omega_I$ with $\omega_I>0$, then $e^{i\omega t}=e^{i\omega_Rt}e^{-\omega_It}$ supplies exponential damping. Under the usual tempered-growth condition on the thermal commutator, the integral and all its $\omega$ derivatives converge locally uniformly. Therefore

$$
\boxed{G_R(\omega)\ \text{is analytic for }\operatorname{Im}\omega>0}.
$$

This is the frequency-space expression of causality.
