<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The increment law immediately gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(W_{t+h}-W_t)^2=h}.
$$

The difference quotient has second moment

$$
\mathbb E\left(\frac{W_{t+h}-W_t}{h}\right)^2=\frac1h\to\infty.
$$

**Thus increments scale as $\sqrt h$, rather than $h$, and no finite derivative is suggested. In fact this heuristic is strengthened by the theorem on [nowhere differentiability of Brownian motion](../../../../../../../nowhere-differentiability-of-brownian-motion.md).**
