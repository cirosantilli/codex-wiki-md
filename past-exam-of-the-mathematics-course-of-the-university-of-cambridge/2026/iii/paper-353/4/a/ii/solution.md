<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The scaled cumulant-generating function is

$$
\frac1t\log G(\lambda)=\mu(e^\lambda-1).
$$

By [Cramér theorem](../../../../../../../cramer-s-theorem.md), its Legendre transform is stationary at $e^{\lambda_*}=x/\mu$ for $x\geq0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{I(x)=x\log\frac x\mu-x+\mu},
\qquad x\geq0,
$$

with $I(x)=+\infty$ for $x<0$ and the continuous convention $0\log0=0$.
