<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The dimensionless equation is $H^3H'''=1-H^3$. At a crest, $H\gg1$, so

$$
H'''\sim-1.
$$

The imposed far-field flux is negligible there; the dominant physical balance is between local gravity-driven drainage and the capillary-pressure gradient.

At a trough, $H\ll1$, so

$$
H^3H'''\sim1.
$$

Local gravity-driven flux is negligible; the capillary-pressure gradient drives the fixed flux through the trough against viscous resistance. These alternating balances generate the strongly nonlinear [capillary waves](../../../../../../capillary-wave.md).
