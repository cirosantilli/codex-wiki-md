<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The depth-integrated ice flux is

$$
q=\int_0^h u\,dz
=u_bh-\frac{\rho g}{3\mu}h^3h_x.
$$

Local [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) with accumulation rate $a$ says

$$
h_t+q_x=a.
$$

Substituting the flux gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\partial h}{\partial t}
+\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(u_bh)
=\frac{\rho g}{3\mu}
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(h^3h_x\right)+a},
$$

as required. Equivalently, after using $u_b=-\beta h_x$,

$$
h_t=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}
\left[
\left(\beta h+\frac{\rho g}{3\mu}h^3\right)h_x
\right]+a.
$$
