<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The ratio of sliding flux to deformation flux at thickness $h$ is

$$
\frac{\beta h}{(\rho g/(3\mu))h^3}
=\frac{3\mu\beta}{\rho gh^2}.
$$

Thus basal sliding dominates throughout the dynamically important thick part of the cap when

$$
\boxed{\frac{3\mu\beta}{\rho g(h^*)^2}\gg1},
$$

with the divide thickness giving a more conservative choice if it is known. In this limit $s\gg1$, and the divide-to-nose extent from part c becomes

$$
x_n=\ell X_n
\sim2\ell\sqrt{s}
=\boxed{2h^*\sqrt{\frac{\beta}{\alpha}}}.
$$

The full width of the symmetric cap is $2x_n$.
