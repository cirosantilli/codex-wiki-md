<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the surface $F(x,z,t)=z-\zeta(x,t)=0$, an outward [normal vector](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) and positively oriented [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) are

$$
\boxed{
\hat{\mathbf n}
=\frac{(-\zeta_x,1)}{\sqrt{1+\zeta_x^2}}},
\qquad
\boxed{
\hat{\mathbf t}
=\frac{(1,\zeta_x)}{\sqrt{1+\zeta_x^2}}}.
$$

With $\zeta=\epsilon e^{ikx}$ and $|k\epsilon|\ll1$, [linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md) gives

$$
\boxed{
\hat{\mathbf n}=(-ik\epsilon e^{ikx},1)+O(\epsilon^2k^2)},
\qquad
\boxed{
\hat{\mathbf t}=(1,ik\epsilon e^{ikx})+O(\epsilon^2k^2)}.
$$
