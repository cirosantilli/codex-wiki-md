<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $z$ vertically downward from the cold surface. The ice occupies $0<z<h(t)$ and the water occupies $z>h(t)$. The temperature satisfies the [heat equation](../../../../../../heat-equation.md)

$$
T_t=\kappa T_{zz}
$$

in both phases, with

$$
T_i(0,t)=T_b,\qquad
T_i(h,t)=T_w(h,t)=T_m,\qquad
T_w(z,t)\longrightarrow T_\infty
\quad(z\longrightarrow\infty).
$$

The remaining interfacial condition is the [Stefan condition](../../../../../../stefan-condition.md)

$$
\rho L\dot h
=kT_{i,z}(h^-,t)-kT_{w,z}(h^+,t).
$$

A schematic should show the cold surface, the descending phase boundary, the ice temperature rising from $T_b$ to $T_m$, and the water temperature tending from $T_m$ to $T_\infty$.
