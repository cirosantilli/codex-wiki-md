<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the [Szemerédi theorem](../../../../../../szemeredi-s-theorem.md) with density $\varepsilon$ and progression length five. For all sufficiently large $n$, the set $A$ contains a nonconstant five-term [arithmetic progression](../../../../../../arithmetic-progression.md)

$$
e-2r,\quad e-r,\quad e,\quad e+r,\quad e+2r,
$$

where $r\ne0$. Set

$$
a=e-2r,
\quad b=e-r,
\quad c=e+r,
\quad d=e+2r.
$$

These four elements and $e$ are distinct, and direct addition gives

$$
\boxed{a+b+c+d
=(e-2r)+(e-r)+(e+r)+(e+2r)
=4e.}
$$
