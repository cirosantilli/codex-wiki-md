<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a matrix [Lie group](../../../../../../lie-group.md), the left-invariant [Maurer-Cartan form](../../../../../../maurer-cartan-form.md) is

$$
\boxed{\rho=g^{-1}dg}.
$$

Differentiating $g^{-1}g=I$ gives $d(g^{-1})=-g^{-1}(dg)g^{-1}$. Hence

$$
d\rho=d(g^{-1})\wedge dg
=-g^{-1}dg\wedge g^{-1}dg
=-\rho\wedge\rho,
$$

and therefore

$$
\boxed{d\rho+\rho\wedge\rho=0}.
$$
