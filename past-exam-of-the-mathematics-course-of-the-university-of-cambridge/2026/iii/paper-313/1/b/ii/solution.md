<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a zero of order $N$ at the origin and no other zeros,

$$
u(r)=2N\log r+O(1)
\quad(r\to0),
\qquad
u(r)\to0
\quad(r\to\infty).
$$

Away from the zero, the flat [Taubes equation](../../../../../../../taubes-equation.md) is

$$
\Delta u=e^u-1.
$$

If $u$ had a positive interior maximum, then the [second-derivative test](../../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) would give $\Delta u\leq0$ there, while $e^u-1>0$, a contradiction. The boundary values are $-\infty$ at the zero and $0$ at infinity, so the [maximum principle](../../../../../../../maximum-principle-for-subharmonic-functions.md) gives $u\leq0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{|\phi|=e^{u/2}\leq1}.
$$
