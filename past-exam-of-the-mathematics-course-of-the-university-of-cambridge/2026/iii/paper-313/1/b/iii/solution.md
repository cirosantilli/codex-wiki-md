<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The surface area is

$$
\operatorname{Area}(\Sigma)
=\int_{\mathbb R^2}\frac{a^2}{(1+|z|^2)^2}\,dx\,dy
=2\pi a^2\int_0^\infty\frac{r\,dr}{(1+r^2)^2}
=\pi a^2.
$$

Integrating the [Taubes equation](../../../../../../../taubes-equation.md) over the compact surface eliminates the Laplacian and gives

$$
4\pi N=\int_\Sigma\Omega(1-e^u)d^2x
<\operatorname{Area}(\Sigma)
$$

for a nontrivial solution. For $N=2$, the [Bradlow bound](../../../../../../../bradlow-bound.md) therefore requires

$$
\boxed{\pi a^2>8\pi,
\qquad a^2>8}.
$$

Equality is the dissolved-vortex limit with identically vanishing Higgs field and does not give the stipulated ordinary two-vortex solution.
