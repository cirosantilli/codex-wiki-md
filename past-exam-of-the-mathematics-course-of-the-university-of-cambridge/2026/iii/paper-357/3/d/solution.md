<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

By definition,

$$
Q^\alpha=(\delta^\alpha{}_\mu+n^\alpha n_\mu)J^\mu
=J^\alpha+n^\alpha(n_\mu J^\mu)
=J^\alpha-Qn^\alpha.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{J^\alpha=Qn^\alpha+Q^\alpha},
$$

with $n_\alpha Q^\alpha=0$.
