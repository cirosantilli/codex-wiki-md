↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $d<4$, $y_g=4-d>0$, so the quartic coupling is a [relevant direction of a fixed point](../../../../../../../relevant-direction-of-a-fixed-point.md). Generic Ising-like systems therefore flow away from the Gaussian point toward the interacting [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](../../../../../../../wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md), which controls the Ising [universality class](../../../../../../../universality-class.md) below four dimensions.
