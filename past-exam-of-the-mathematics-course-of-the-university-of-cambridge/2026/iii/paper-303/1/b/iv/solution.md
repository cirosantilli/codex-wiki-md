The quadratic kernel still has $K\sim at+\gamma k^2$, so $\alpha_{\mathrm{fl}}=2-d/2$. The tricritical mean-field exponent is $\alpha=1/2$. Equating them gives $2-d/2=1/2$, hence the tricritical [upper critical dimension](../../../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md) is $d_c=3$.
