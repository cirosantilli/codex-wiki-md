↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fourier expansion in volume $V$ diagonalizes the quadratic [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md):

$$
F=\frac12\sum_{|k|<\Lambda}K(T,k^2)|\phi_k|^2,
$$

where

$$
K=\mu^2+\gamma k^2+\sum_{n\geq2}\delta_n(k^2)^n
=\mu^2+\gamma k^2+(k^2)^2g(T,k^2),
\qquad
g=\sum_{n\geq2}\delta_n(k^2)^{n-2}.
$$

Each independent real mode contributes a [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) proportional to $(T/K)^{1/2}$. Taking $-T\log Z$, dividing by $V$, and replacing the sum by an integral gives, up to field-independent conventions,

$$
\boxed{-\frac T2\int_{|k|<\Lambda}\frac{d^dk}{(2\pi)^d}
\log\frac{\pi VT}{K(T,k^2)}.}
$$
