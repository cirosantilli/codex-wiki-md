<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $t'=\zeta^{\Delta_t}t$, lengths shrink by $\zeta$, so $\xi(t')=\zeta^{-1}\xi(t)$. Substituting $\xi(t)\sim t^{-\nu}$ gives $\zeta^{-\nu\Delta_t}=\zeta^{-1}$ and hence

$$
\boxed{\Delta_t=\frac1\nu.}
$$
