<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Choose $\zeta=t^{-\nu}$. The singular free-energy density scales as $t^{d\nu}$, giving the [hyperscaling relation](../../../../../../../hyperscaling-relation.md) $\alpha=2-d\nu$. The order parameter gives

$$
\beta=\nu\Delta_\phi=\frac12(d-2+\eta)\nu.
$$

The susceptibility has exponent

$$
\gamma=\nu(d-2\Delta_\phi)=\nu(2-\eta).
$$

At $t=0$, choosing $\zeta=B^{-1/\Delta_B}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\delta=\frac{\Delta_B}{\Delta_\phi}
=\frac{d+2-\eta}{d-2+\eta}.}
$$
