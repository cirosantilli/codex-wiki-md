<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The source term transforms as

$$
\int d^dx\,B\phi
=\int d^dx'\,\zeta^{d-\Delta_\phi}B\phi'.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta_B=d-\Delta_\phi=\frac{d+2-\eta}{2}.}
$$
