<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the normalized [Green function of a transient weighted graph](../../../../../../green-function-of-a-transient-weighted-graph.md), the Green identity is

$$
\mathcal E(g(\mathord\cdot,0),f)=f(0),
\qquad f\in H_0.
$$

Taking $f=g(\mathord\cdot,0)$ and using $h=g(\mathord\cdot,0)/g(0,0)$ therefore gives

$$
\mathcal E(h,h)
=\frac{\mathcal E(g(\mathord\cdot,0),g(\mathord\cdot,0))}{g(0,0)^2}
=\frac1{g(0,0)}.
$$

This is also the [capacity of a finite set for a transient random walk](../../../../../../capacity-of-a-finite-set-for-a-transient-random-walk.md) $\operatorname{cap}(\{0\})$.
