The [Dirichlet energy space with zero boundary at infinity](../../../../../../dirichlet-energy-space-with-zero-boundary-at-infinity.md) is

$$
H_0=\overline{C_c(G)}^{\,\lVert\cdot\rVert_{H_0}},
\qquad
\lVert f\rVert_{H_0}=\mathcal E(f,f)^{1/2},
$$

where $C_c(G)$ is the vector space of finitely supported real functions on the vertices. Transience makes the [Dirichlet form of a Markov chain](../../../../../../dirichlet-form-of-a-markov-chain.md) positive definite on this completion.
