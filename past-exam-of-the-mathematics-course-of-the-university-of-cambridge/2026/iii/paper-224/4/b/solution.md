<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Order the symbols so that $p_1\geq p_2\geq\cdots$. For each $l\geq0$, there are exactly $2^l$ binary strings of length $l$, while precisely the indices

$$
2^l\leq i\leq2^{l+1}-1
$$

satisfy $\lfloor\log i\rfloor=l$. Assign those $2^l$ symbols bijectively to the strings of length $l$. The resulting map is an [injective function](../../../../../../injective-function.md) and hence a [one-to-one source code](../../../../../../one-to-one-source-code.md), with $L^*(x_i)=\lfloor\log i\rfloor$. Assigning shorter available words to more probable symbols also shows that this is an [optimal one-to-one binary code](../../../../../../optimal-one-to-one-binary-code.md).
