<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $Z=\mathbb E_Qe^g$ and define the exponentially tilted [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md)

$$
P^*(x)=\frac{Q(x)e^{g(x)}}Z.
$$

For this choice every ratio $e^{g(x)}Q(x)/P^*(x)$ equals $Z$, so equality holds in the [Jensen inequality](../../../../../../jensen-s-inequality.md) used in part (d). Hence

$$
\log_e\mathbb E_Qe^g
=\sup_P\{\mathbb E_Pg-D_e(P\Vert Q)\},
$$

and $P^*$ is the maximizer. This is the finite-alphabet [Gibbs variational principle for relative entropy](../../../../../../gibbs-variational-principle-for-relative-entropy.md).
