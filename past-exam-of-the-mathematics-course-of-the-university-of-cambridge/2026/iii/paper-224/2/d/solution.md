Here $e_2^{(n)}=P^{\otimes n}(B_n^c)$. The [method of types](../../../../../../method-of-types.md) gives at most $(n+1)^{|A|}$ possible values of [type](../../../../../../type-information-theory.md), and a type class $T_R$ has

$$
P^{\otimes n}(T_R)\leq2^{-nD(R\Vert P)}.
$$

Every [type](../../../../../../type-information-theory.md) in $B_n^c$ has $D(R\Vert P)>\delta$, so

$$
e_2^{(n)}
\leq(n+1)^{|A|}2^{-n\delta}.
$$

The polynomial prefactor has zero exponential rate. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log e_2^{(n)}\leq-\delta.}
$$
