<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Neyman-Pearson decision region](../../../../../../neyman-pearson-decision-region.md) accepting $P^{\otimes n}$ is

$$
B_n(t)=\left\{x_1^n:
\frac{P^{\otimes n}(x_1^n)}{Q^{\otimes n}(x_1^n)}\geq t
\right\},
$$

with randomization on the boundary when needed. If $R=\widehat P_{x_1^n}$ is the [type](../../../../../../type-information-theory.md) of the observed string, then

$$
\frac1n\log\frac{P^{\otimes n}(x_1^n)}{Q^{\otimes n}(x_1^n)}
=D(R\Vert Q)-D(R\Vert P).
$$

Thus the equivalent [relative entropy](../../../../../../kullback-leibler-divergence.md) form is

$$
\boxed{B_n(t)=\{x_1^n:D(\widehat P_{x_1^n}\Vert Q)
-D(\widehat P_{x_1^n}\Vert P)\geq n^{-1}\log t\}.}
$$
