↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [Satellite formula for the Levine-Tristram signature](../../../../../../satellite-formula-for-the-levine-tristram-signature.md) applied to the $(2,5)$ [cable knot](../../../../../../cable-knot.md) gives

$$
\sigma_\omega(K_{2,5})
=\sigma_\omega(T_{2,5})+\sigma_{\omega^2}(K).
$$

At $\omega=-1$, the second term is $\sigma_1(K)=0$, whereas part b gives

$$
|\sigma_{-1}(T_{2,5})|=4.
$$

The [Levine-Tristram signature bound on the slice genus](../../../../../../levine-tristram-signature-bound-on-the-slice-genus.md) now yields

$$
2g_4(K_{2,5})\geq|\sigma_{-1}(K_{2,5})|=4.
$$

**Hence $g_4(K_{2,5})\geq2$, so the cable cannot bound a punctured torus in $B^4$.**
