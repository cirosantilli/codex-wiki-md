<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $V=S^1\times D^2$ contain the [pattern of a satellite knot](../../../../../../pattern-of-a-satellite-knot.md) $P$. Its class in $H_1(V;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$ is the [winding number of a satellite pattern](../../../../../../winding-number-of-a-satellite-pattern.md). Winding number zero therefore makes $P$ null-homologous in $V$, so $P$ bounds an oriented embedded surface $F_P\subset V$. Let

$$
g_P=g(F_P).
$$

For any companion [knot](../../../../../../knot.md) $K$, an embedding $V\hookrightarrow S^3$ as a tubular neighborhood of $K$ carries $F_P$ to a [Seifert surface](../../../../../../seifert-surface.md) for the [satellite knot](../../../../../../satellite-knot.md) $P(K)$. Hence

$$
g_s(P(K))\leq g(F_P)=g_P,
$$

and the bound depends only on the pattern.
