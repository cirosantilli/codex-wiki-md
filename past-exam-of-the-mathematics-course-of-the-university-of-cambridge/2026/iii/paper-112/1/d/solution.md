<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Induct on the [Seifert genus](../../../../../../seifert-genus.md) $g_s(K)$. A genus-zero knot is the [unknot](../../../../../../unknot.md). If $K$ is prime there is nothing to prove. Otherwise write $K=K_1\mathbin{\#}K_2$ with both summands nontrivial. Part c gives

$$
g_s(K)=g_s(K_1)+g_s(K_2),
$$

so each summand has strictly smaller genus than $K$. Apply the induction hypothesis to both. Since the genus drops at every nontrivial split, the process terminates after finitely many steps and expresses $K$ as a finite [connected sum](../../../../../../connected-sum-of-knots.md) of [prime knots](../../../../../../prime-knot.md).
