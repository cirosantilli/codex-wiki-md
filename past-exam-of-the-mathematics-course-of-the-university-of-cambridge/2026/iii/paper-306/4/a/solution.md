<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a reference metric in each conformal class. An infinitesimal [worldsheet diffeomorphism](../../../../../../worldsheet-diffeomorphism.md) generated by $c^\alpha$ and a [Weyl transformation](../../../../../../weyl-transformation.md) decompose the metric variation into its trace and the traceless operator

$$
(P_1c)_{\alpha\beta}
=\nabla_\alpha c_\beta+\nabla_\beta c_\alpha
-g_{\alpha\beta}\nabla_\gamma c^\gamma.
$$

Inserting the gauge condition and its [Faddeev-Popov determinant](../../../../../../faddeev-popov-determinant.md) cancels the formal gauge-orbit volume. Representing $\det P_1$ by anticommuting [worldsheet ghost fields](../../../../../../worldsheet-ghost-field.md) gives

$$
S_{\rm Gh}[b,c,g]
=\frac1{2\pi}\int_\Sigma d^2\sigma\,\sqrt g\,
b^{\alpha\beta}(P_1c)_{\alpha\beta},
$$

where $b_{\alpha\beta}$ is symmetric and traceless and $c^\alpha$ is a vector. The gauge-fixed integral is consequently

$$
Z=\int\mathcal DX\,\mathcal Db\,\mathcal Dc\,
\exp(-S_{\rm P}-S_{\rm Gh}).
$$

On higher-genus worldsheets one must additionally integrate over moduli and treat conformal-Killing and ghost zero modes separately; these finite-dimensional factors are suppressed in the displayed formal expression.
