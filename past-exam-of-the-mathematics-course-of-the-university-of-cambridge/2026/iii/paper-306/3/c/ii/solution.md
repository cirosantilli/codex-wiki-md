<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because [normal ordering](../../../../../../../normal-ordering.md) removes contractions within either exponential, [Wick theorem](../../../../../../../wick-s-theorem.md) sums all cross-contractions into

$$
\begin{aligned}
G_{k,-k}
&=\exp\left[(ik)(-ik)
\langle X(z,\bar z)X(w,\bar w)\rangle\right]\\
&=\exp\left[-\frac{k^2}{2}\log|z-w|^2\right]
=|z-w|^{-k^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Under $z\mapsto\lambda z$, this scales as $\lambda^{-k^2}$. Each operator has scaling dimension $\Delta=h+\widetilde h=k^2/2$, so a two-point function of two such primaries must scale as $\lambda^{-2\Delta}=\lambda^{-k^2}$. The two calculations agree.
