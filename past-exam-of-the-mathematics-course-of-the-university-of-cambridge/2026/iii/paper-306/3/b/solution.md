<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

After integration by parts, the quadratic operator in the action is $K=-\pi^{-1}\partial\bar\partial$. Its [Green function](../../../../../../green-s-function.md) $G(z,w)=\langle X(z,\bar z)X(w,\bar w)\rangle$ therefore satisfies

$$
-\frac1\pi\partial\bar\partial G(z,w)
=\delta^{(2)}(z-w).
$$

Using the given distributional identity gives

$$
\boxed{\displaystyle
\langle X(z,\bar z)X(w,\bar w)\rangle
=-\frac12\log|z-w|^2+C},
$$

where the additive constant depends on the infrared convention and cancels from neutral correlators.
