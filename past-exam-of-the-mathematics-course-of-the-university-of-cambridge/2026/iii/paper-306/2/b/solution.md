<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Virasoro constraints](../../../../../../virasoro-constraint.md) determine $X^-$ in terms of the $d=D-2$ transverse coordinates $X^i$. Their closed-string expansion introduces independent left- and right-moving [string oscillators](../../../../../../string-oscillator.md) satisfying

$$
[\alpha_m^i,\alpha_n^j]
=m\delta^{ij}\delta_{m+n,0},
\qquad
[\widetilde\alpha_m^i,\widetilde\alpha_n^j]
=m\delta^{ij}\delta_{m+n,0}.
$$

For $n>0$, $\alpha_n^i$ and $\widetilde\alpha_n^i$ annihilate the momentum vacuum, while $\alpha_{-n}^i$ and $\widetilde\alpha_{-n}^i$ create excitations. A general state is a product of these creation operators acting on $|p\rangle$, subject to [closed-string level matching](../../../../../../closed-string-level-matching.md) $N=\widetilde N$.

Normal ordering the quantum constraints introduces the [normal-ordering constant of a string](../../../../../../normal-ordering-constant-of-a-string.md) $a$. Requiring the Lorentz generators $J^{-i}$ to obey the Lorentz algebra without an anomalous term fixes

$$
a=1,\qquad D=26.
$$

**Thus Lorentz invariance resolves both the ordering ambiguity and the [critical dimension of string theory](../../../../../../critical-dimension-of-string-theory.md).**
