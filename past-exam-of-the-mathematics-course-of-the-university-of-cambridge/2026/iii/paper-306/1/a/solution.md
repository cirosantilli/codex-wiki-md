<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [induced worldsheet metric](../../../../../../induced-worldsheet-metric.md) is

$$
\gamma_{\alpha\beta}
=\partial_\alpha X^\mu\partial_\beta X^\nu\eta_{\mu\nu}
=\partial_\alpha X\mathbin\cdot\partial_\beta X.
$$

The [Nambu–Goto action](../../../../../../nambu-goto-action.md) is

$$
S_{\rm NG}=-T\int_\Sigma d^2\sigma\,\sqrt{-\det\gamma},
$$

which is minus the [string tension](../../../../../../string-tension.md) times the invariant area of the [string worldsheet](../../../../../../worldsheet.md). Varying the independent metric in the [Polyakov action](../../../../../../polyakov-action.md) gives

$$
0=T_{\alpha\beta}
=\gamma_{\alpha\beta}
-\frac12g_{\alpha\beta}g^{\rho\sigma}\gamma_{\rho\sigma}.
$$

In two dimensions this says $g_{\alpha\beta}=e^{2\omega}\gamma_{\alpha\beta}$ for an undetermined [Weyl transformation](../../../../../../weyl-transformation.md) factor. Substitution gives $\sqrt{-g}\,g^{\alpha\beta}\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=2\sqrt{-\det\gamma}$, and hence $S_{\rm P}=S_{\rm NG}$.
