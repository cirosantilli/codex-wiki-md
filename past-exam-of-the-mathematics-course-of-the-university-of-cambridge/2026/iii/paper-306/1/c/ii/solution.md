↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the circular embedding,

$$
\gamma_{\tau\tau}=-1+\dot R^2,
\qquad
\gamma_{\tau\sigma}=0,
\qquad
\gamma_{\sigma\sigma}=R^2.
$$

On $R=R_0\cos(\tau/R_0)$ this becomes

$$
(\gamma_{\alpha\beta})
=\cos^2\left(\frac{\tau}{R_0}\right)
\begin{pmatrix}
-1&0\\
0&R_0^2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Thus the worldsheet metric is conformally flat between collapse events and becomes degenerate at the instant when the loop shrinks to zero size.**
