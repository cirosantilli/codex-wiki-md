<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In [static gauge](../../../../../../../static-gauge.md), take $\sigma\in[0,2\pi)$ and use the circular ansatz

$$
X^\mu(\tau,\sigma)
=\bigl(\tau,R(\tau)\cos\sigma,R(\tau)\sin\sigma\bigr).
$$

Its Nambu–Goto Lagrangian is

$$
L=-2\pi T R\sqrt{1-\dot R^2}.
$$

The conserved energy gives

$$
\frac{R}{\sqrt{1-\dot R^2}}=R_0
$$

for $R(0)=R_0$ and $\dot R(0)=0$. Equivalently,

$$
R\ddot R+1-\dot R^2=0,
$$

whose solution is

$$
R(\tau)=R_0\cos\left(\frac{\tau}{R_0}\right).
$$

The signed continuation is periodic; the geometric radius is $|R(\tau)|$, and the loop collapses whenever the cosine vanishes.
