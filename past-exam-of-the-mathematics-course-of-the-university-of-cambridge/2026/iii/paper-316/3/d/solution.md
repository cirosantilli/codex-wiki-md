<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

If $Q_D^*\propto D^b$, part (b) gives

$$
R_{\rm cc}(D)\propto
D^{3-\alpha+b(1-\alpha)/3}.
$$

The mass in one logarithmic size interval scales as $D^3[D n(D)]\propto D^{4-\alpha}$. A steady [collisional cascade](../../../../../../collisional-cascade.md) requires the mass destroyed per unit time, and hence the mass flux through every logarithmic interval, to be independent of $D$. Its exponent is therefore

$$
4-\alpha+3-\alpha+\frac{b(1-\alpha)}3=0.
$$

Solving gives

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{21+b}{6+b}}.
$$
