<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $e=0$, regard

$$
T_p(x)=\frac1x+2\cos I\sqrt x.
$$

Its minimum occurs at $x=(\cos I)^{-2/3}$ and equals $3(\cos I)^{2/3}$. A circular orbit is therefore possible for $T_p=5/2$ only if

$$
3(\cos I)^{2/3}\leq\frac52.
$$

The smallest required [orbital inclination](../../../../../../orbital-inclination.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{I_{\min}=\arccos\left(\frac56\right)^{3/2}\simeq40.5^\circ}.
$$
