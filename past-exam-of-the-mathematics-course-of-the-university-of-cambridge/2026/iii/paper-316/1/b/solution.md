<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $u=1/r$. Since $h=r^2\dot\theta$, the radial equation becomes the [Binet equation](../../../../../../binet-equation.md)

$$
u''+u=\frac\mu{h^2},
$$

where primes denote derivatives with respect to the polar angle. Choosing the angular origin at [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md) gives

$$
u=\frac\mu{h^2}(1+e\cos f).
$$

Writing the semi-latus rectum as $h^2/\mu=a(1-e^2)$ yields the [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md)

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{a(1-e^2)}{1+e\cos f}}.
$$

Hence the [specific angular momentum](../../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) and [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) are

$$
\boxed{h=\sqrt{\mu a(1-e^2)}},
\qquad
\boxed{C=-\frac\mu{2a}}.
$$
