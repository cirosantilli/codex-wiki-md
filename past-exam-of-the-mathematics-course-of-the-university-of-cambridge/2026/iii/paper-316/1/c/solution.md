<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In axes directed toward [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md) and along the motion there, the position is $\mathbf r=r(\cos f,\sin f)$. Differentiating it and using $\dot f=h/r^2$ gives

$$
v_x=\dot r\cos f-r\dot f\sin f,
\qquad
v_y=\dot r\sin f+r\dot f\cos f.
$$

Differentiating the orbit equation gives $\dot r=(\mu e/h)\sin f$, and substitution simplifies the components to

$$
\boxed{v_x=-\frac\mu h\sin f},
\qquad
\boxed{v_y=\frac\mu h(e+\cos f)}.
$$
