<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $v_K=\sqrt{\mu/a}$ be the speed of the [circular Kepler orbit](../../../../../../circular-kepler-orbit.md) at radius $a$. The release velocity relative to the [planetesimal](../../../../../../planetesimal.md) is

$$
\Delta\mathbf v=\gamma v_K(-\sin\phi,\cos\phi).
$$

The particle starts at the same position as its parent, so the change in [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) is

$$
C'-C=\mathbf v\mathbin\cdot\Delta\mathbf v+\frac12|\Delta\mathbf v|^2.
$$

Using the velocity components from part (c),

$$
\mathbf v\mathbin\cdot\Delta\mathbf v
=\frac{\gamma\mu}{a\sqrt{1-e^2}}
\bigl[\cos(\phi-f)+e\cos\phi\bigr].
$$

Since $C=-\mu/(2a)$ and $C'=-\mu/(2a')$, rearrangement gives

$$
\boxed{\frac a{a'}=1-\gamma^2-
\frac{2\gamma}{\sqrt{1-e^2}}
\bigl[\cos(\phi-f)+e\cos\phi\bigr]}.
$$
