<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

An orbit is unbound precisely when its [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) is nonnegative, equivalently $a/a'\leq0$. At [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md), $f=0$, and for $e=1/2$ the condition from part (d) is

$$
1-\gamma^2-2\sqrt3\,\gamma\cos\phi\leq0,
\qquad
\cos\phi\geq\frac{1-\gamma^2}{2\sqrt3\,\gamma}.
$$

The directions are sampled from the [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on a circle. The fraction satisfying $\cos\phi\geq x$ is $\arccos x/\pi$; setting it equal to $1/6$ gives $x=\sqrt3/2$. Therefore

$$
\frac{1-\gamma^2}{2\sqrt3\,\gamma}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2},
\qquad
\gamma^2+3\gamma-1=0.
$$

The positive root is

$$
\boxed{\gamma=\frac{\sqrt{13}-3}{2}}.
$$
