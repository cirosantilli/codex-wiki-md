<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Poynting–Robertson drag](../../../../../../poynting-robertson-drag.md) removes [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md), so a trapped grain requires positive resonant [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) from the planet. Part (c) shows that conjunction must occur after the dust has passed apoapsis. In the planet's rotating frame, the four-lobed pattern is therefore rotated so that the relevant outer lobe lags the fixed planet in the direction of orbital motion. Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\sin\phi<0},
$$

with the stable phase displaced beyond $\phi=\pi$ rather than sitting at the torque-free value $\phi=\pi$.
