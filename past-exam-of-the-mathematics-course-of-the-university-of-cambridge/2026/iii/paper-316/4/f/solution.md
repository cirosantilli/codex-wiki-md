<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

While resonance fixes $a_d$, substitute the phase relation from part (e) into the supplied eccentricity equation:

$$
\dot e_d
=\frac{\beta GM_*}{4ca_d^2e_d}
-\frac52\frac{\beta GM_*}{ca_d^2}e_d
=\frac{\beta GM_*}{4ca_d^2e_d}(1-10e_d^2).
$$

To the requested first order in eccentricity, discard the $e_d^2$ correction. It follows that

$$
\frac d{dt}(e_d^2)=\frac{\beta GM_*}{2ca_d^2}.
$$

Using $\beta_{\max}=4ce_{d0}a_d^2D/(GM_*)$ and integrating from $e_d(0)=e_{d0}$ gives

$$
\boxed{e_d(t)=\sqrt{e_{d0}^2+2De_{d0}
\left(\frac\beta{\beta_{\max}}\right)t}}.
$$

The neglected term eventually matters and prevents indefinite validity of this small-eccentricity growth law.
