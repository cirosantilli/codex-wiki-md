<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At exact conjunction the planet's force is radial and has no instantaneous [torque](../../../../../../torque.md), but the approach and departure do not cancel when conjunction is displaced from [apoapsis](../../../../../../apoapsis.md). Write the conjunction longitude as $\lambda_c=\varpi_d+\pi+\delta$. At conjunction,

$$
\phi=\lambda_c-\varpi_d=\pi+\delta,
\qquad
\sin\phi=-\sin\delta.
$$

The resonant part of the given evolution law has $\dot a_d=-8e_da_dD\sin\phi$. Thus a conjunction after apoapsis, $\delta>0$, gives $\sin\phi<0$ and transfers [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) to the dust, increasing $a_d$. A conjunction before apoapsis removes angular momentum, while one exactly at apoapsis has zero secular transfer by symmetry.
