<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\psi=\lambda_d-\lambda_p$ be azimuth in the frame rotating with the planet, with the planet fixed at $\psi=0$. Since $n_d/n_p=4/5$, one synodic circuit contains four radial oscillations. To first order in $e_d$, choosing conjunction at [apoapsis](../../../../../../apoapsis.md) gives

$$
M_d=\pi-4\psi,
\qquad
\boxed{r(\psi)\simeq a_d[1+e_d\cos(4\psi)]}.
$$

The requested sketch is therefore a four-lobed closed curve centred on the star. For $e_d=0.2$, its radii range from $0.8a_d$ to $1.2a_d$; an outer lobe points along the star-planet line, and the planet lies at $(a_p,0)$. At that radial alignment $\phi=\pi$, because $\lambda_d=\lambda_p$ and $\lambda_d=\varpi_d+\pi$ at apoapsis.
