<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

During [resonant trapping of dust](../../../../../../resonant-trapping-of-dust.md), the mean semi-major axis is stationary. Setting the supplied $\dot a_d$ to zero gives

$$
\sin\phi=-\frac{\beta GM_*}{4ce_da_d^2D}.
$$

A real resonant phase exists only when $|\sin\phi|\leq1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\beta\leq\beta_{\max}},
\qquad
\boxed{\beta_{\max}=\frac{4ce_{d0}a_d^2D}{GM_*}},
$$

where $e_{d0}$ is the grain's [orbital eccentricity](../../../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md) when trapping begins. The inequality also has the expected sign $\sin\phi<0$, allowing the planetary torque to oppose [Poynting–Robertson drag](../../../../../../poynting-robertson-drag.md).
