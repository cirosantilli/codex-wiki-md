↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $M$ regards $\mathbb P$ as satisfying the [$\kappa$-chain condition](../../../../../../../chain-condition-for-forcing.md), then forcing with $\mathbb P$ preserves every cardinal and cofinality at least $\kappa$. If $M$ regards $|\mathbb P|$ as $\mu$, then every antichain has size at most $\mu$, so $\mathbb P$ has the $\mu^+$-chain condition. It follows that every cardinal and cofinality at least $\mu^+$ is preserved in a $\mathbb P$-generic extension.
