The [constructible hierarchy](../../../../../../../constructible-hierarchy.md) is defined by transfinite recursion:

$$
L_0=\varnothing,
\qquad
L_{\beta+1}=\mathcal D(L_\beta),
\qquad
L_\lambda=\bigcup_{\beta<\lambda}L_\beta
$$

when $\lambda$ is a [limit ordinal](../../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md). Its union over all ordinals is the constructible universe $L$.
