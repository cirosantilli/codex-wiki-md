<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The language of set theory has only countably many [sentences](../../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md), so there are at most $2^{\aleph_0}$ possible complete sets of sentences $T_\xi$. Choose $(2^{\aleph_0})^++1$ ordinals above $\alpha$. By cardinal pigeonhole, two of their theories agree. Hence some $\gamma>\beta>\alpha$ satisfy $T_\beta=T_\gamma$.
