<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With the convention that $p\leq q$ means that $p$ is stronger, a set $G\subseteq\mathbb P$ is a [generic filter](../../../../../../generic-filter.md) over $M$ when it is a filter and meets every [dense set](../../../../../../dense-subset-of-a-forcing-order.md) $D\subseteq\mathbb P$ with $D\in M$. Explicitly, $G$ is upward closed toward weaker conditions, every two members have a common stronger member in $G$, and $G\cap D\ne\varnothing$ for every such $D$.
