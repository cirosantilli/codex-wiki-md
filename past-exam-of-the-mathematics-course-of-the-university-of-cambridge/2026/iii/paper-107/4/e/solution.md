<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Part c supplies a [bounded slope condition](../../../../../../bounded-slope-condition.md) constant $K$. Choose $k>K$. Parts a and d give a constrained minimizer with $\operatorname{Lip}(u)\leq K<k$, and part b makes it a minimizer over all of $\operatorname{Lip}_g(B_1)$.

For $F(\xi)=\sqrt{1+|\xi|^2}$, the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is the [minimal surface equation for a graph](../../../../../../minimal-surface-equation-for-a-graph.md)

$$
\operatorname{div}\left(\frac{Du}{\sqrt{1+|Du|^2}}\right)=0.
$$

The Lipschitz bound confines $Du$ to a compact set on which $D^2F$ is uniformly positive definite, so the equation is uniformly elliptic. Interior regularity gives $C^{1,\alpha}$ first, and repeated [Schauder estimates](../../../../../../schauder-estimates.md) then give smoothness in the interior.
