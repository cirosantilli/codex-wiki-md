<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $y\in\partial\Omega$, let $b_y^-\leq g\leq b_y^+$ be the affine barriers from the [bounded slope condition](../../../../../../bounded-slope-condition.md). Their constant gradients satisfy the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md), so they minimize the autonomous convex functional for their own boundary values. The [comparison principle](../../../../../../comparison-principle-for-convex-variational-integrals.md) gives

$$
b_y^-(x)\leq u(x)\leq b_y^+(x).
$$

Since both barriers equal $u(y)$ at $y$ and are $K$-Lipschitz,

$$
|u(x)-u(y)|\leq K|x-y|
$$

for $x\in\Omega$ and $y\in\partial\Omega$. The supplied boundary-to-interior criterion now gives $\operatorname{Lip}(u)\leq K$.
