<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A minimizing sequence in $\operatorname{Lip}_g(\Omega,k)$ is uniformly bounded and equi-Lipschitz. The [Arzelà-Ascoli theorem](../../../../../../arzela-ascoli-theorem.md) gives a uniformly convergent subsequence with limit $u$ having the same boundary data and Lipschitz constant at most $k$. Its gradients have a weak-star convergent subsequence in $L^\infty$, with limit $Du$. Convexity of $F$ makes the integral functional weak-star lower semicontinuous, so

$$
\mathcal F[u]\leq\liminf_j\mathcal F[u_j].
$$

**Thus $u$ attains the infimum by the [direct method in the calculus of variations](../../../../../../direct-method-in-the-calculus-of-variations.md).**
