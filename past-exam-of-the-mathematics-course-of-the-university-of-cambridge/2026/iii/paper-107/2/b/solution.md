<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The strict maximum principle applied to $a_{ij}\partial_{ij}u=f>0$ with zero boundary data gives $u<0$ in $\Omega$. On $\Omega'$ set $w=v-u$. Then $a_{ij}\partial_{ij}w=0$, while $w=-u\geq0$ on $\partial\Omega'$ and this boundary value is positive somewhere because $\Omega'$ is proper. Hence $w>0$ inside and $w(y_0)=0$. The [Hopf boundary point lemma](../../../../../../hopf-lemma.md) at this boundary minimum gives

$$
D_\nu w(y_0)<0.
$$

**Therefore $D_\nu v(y_0)-D_\nu u(y_0)<0$, proving the normal derivatives are unequal.**
