<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The restricted [trace map](../../../../../../sobolev-trace-theorem.md) is continuous, and $V$ is its kernel, so $V$ is a [closed vector subspace](../../../../../../closed-vector-subspace.md) of the [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) $H^1(U)$. It is therefore complete in the $H^1$ norm. Part a shows that the gradient norm

$$
\lVert v\rVert_V=\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^2(U)}
$$

is equivalent to that norm, so it is complete as well. It comes from the [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md)

$$
(u,v)_V=\int_U\nabla u\mathbin\cdot\nabla v\,dx.
$$

**Consequently $(V,\lVert\cdot\rVert_V)$ is a [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md).**
