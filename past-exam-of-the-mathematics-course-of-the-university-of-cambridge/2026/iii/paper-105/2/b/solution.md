<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiply the [Poisson equation](../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) by $v\in V$ and apply [Green's first identity](../../../../../../green-s-first-identity.md). The [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) makes the trace of $v$ vanish on $\Gamma_1$, while the [Neumann boundary condition](../../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md) makes the boundary flux vanish on $\Gamma_2$. Thus the [weak formulation](../../../../../../weak-formulation.md) is: find $u\in V$ such that

$$
B(u,v)=\ell(v)
\qquad\text{for every }v\in V,
$$

where

$$
B(u,v)=\int_U\nabla u\mathbin\cdot\nabla v\,dx,
\qquad
\ell(v)=\int_Ufv\,dx.
$$
