<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At $x\in\Sigma$, keep the [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md) $N(x)$ fixed and define the $j$th [normal derivative](../../../../../../normal-derivative.md) by

$$
\partial_N^ju(x)
=\left.\frac{d^j}{ds^j}u\bigl(x+sN(x)\bigr)\right|_{s=0}.
$$

For $j=1$, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) and $N=\nabla\phi/|\nabla\phi|$ give

$$
\boxed{\partial_Nu
=N\mathbin\cdot\nabla u
=\frac{\phi_tu_t+\phi_ru_r+\phi_zu_z}
{\sqrt{\phi_t^2+\phi_r^2+\phi_z^2}}
\qquad\text{on }\Sigma.}
$$
