<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Only the second-order terms contribute to the [principal symbol](../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md). For a [covector](../../../../../../covector.md) $\xi=(\xi_t,\xi_r,\xi_z)$ it is

$$
p(\xi)=\xi_t^2-(1+u_z)^2(\xi_r^2+\xi_z^2).
$$

The conormal to the [hypersurface](../../../../../../hypersurface.md) $\Sigma=\{\phi=0\}$ is $d\phi=(\phi_t,\phi_r,\phi_z)$. After evaluating the coefficient at the prescribed boundary value of $u_z$, the [non-characteristic condition](../../../../../../non-characteristic-hypersurface.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\phi_t^2-(1+u_z|_\Sigma)^2(\phi_r^2+\phi_z^2)\ne0.}
$$
