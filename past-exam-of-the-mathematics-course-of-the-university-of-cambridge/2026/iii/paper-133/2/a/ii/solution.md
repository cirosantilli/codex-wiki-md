The [HNN extension](../../../../../../../hnn-extension.md) is the [Baumslag-Solitar group](../../../../../../../baumslag-solitar-group.md)

$$
\operatorname{BS}(1,2)
=\langle u,t\mid tut^{-1}=u^2\rangle.
$$

Its [Bass-Serre tree](../../../../../../../bass-serre-tree.md) has vertices $G/\langle u\rangle$ and oriented edges $G/\langle u\rangle$, with the two endpoint maps induced by the identity embedding and the index-two embedding $n\mapsto2n$. At each vertex there is one incident edge on the identity side and two on the index-two side. The underlying unoriented tree is therefore infinite and $3$-regular, with an orientation in which every vertex has one incoming and two outgoing edges, up to reversing the convention.
