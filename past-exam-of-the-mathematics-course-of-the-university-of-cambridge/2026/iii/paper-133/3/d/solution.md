<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A hyperbolic [isometry of a tree](../../../../../../isometry-of-a-tree.md) has a unique invariant [axis of a tree isometry](../../../../../../axis-of-a-tree-isometry.md), on which it acts by a nonzero translation. Let $L$ be the axis of $x$. By part (c), $x$ is central, so for every $g\in G$,

$$
x(gL)=g(xL)=gL.
$$

**Thus $gL$ is another axis of $x$. Uniqueness gives $gL=L$, and hence the whole group $G$ preserves the line $L$.**
