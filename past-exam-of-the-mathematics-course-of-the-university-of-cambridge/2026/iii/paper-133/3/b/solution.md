<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let

$$
\Delta(2,3,7)
=\langle A,B,C\mid A^2=B^3=C^7=ABC=1\rangle
$$

be the orientation-preserving $(2,3,7)$ [hyperbolic triangle group](../../../../../../hyperbolic-triangle-group.md). Define

$$
a\mapsto A,\qquad b\mapsto B,\qquad c\mapsto C,
\qquad x\mapsto1,\qquad y\mapsto1.
$$

Every defining relator of $G$ maps to the identity, and $A,B,C$ lie in the image, so this is a surjective [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md). Since

$$
\frac12+\frac13+\frac17<1,
$$

$\Delta(2,3,7)$ is a non-elementary [Fuchsian group](../../../../../../fuchsian-group.md).
