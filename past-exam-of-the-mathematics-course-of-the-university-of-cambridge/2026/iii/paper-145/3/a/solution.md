<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A nonempty [Dyck path](../../../../../../dyck-path.md) decomposes uniquely as an up-step, a Dyck path, a down-step, and another Dyck path. Marking each matched outer pair by $x$ gives

$$
C(x)=1+xC(x)^2.
$$

The solution with constant term one is

$$
\boxed{C(x)=\frac{1-\sqrt{1-4x}}{2x}.}
$$
