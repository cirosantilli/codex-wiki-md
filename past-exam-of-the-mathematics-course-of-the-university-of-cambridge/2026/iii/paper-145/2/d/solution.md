↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The sum is the chromatic polynomial of the path, so

$$
\sum_{k=1}^n
\left\{\begin{matrix}n\\k\end{matrix}\right\}_{P_n}
x^{\underline k}
=x(x-1)^{n-1}.
$$
