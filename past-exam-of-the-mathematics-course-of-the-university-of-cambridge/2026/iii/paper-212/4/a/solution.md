<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\tau_p(x)=\mathbb P_p(0\longleftrightarrow x)$ and $r=1-\chi(p)^{-1}$. The [percolation susceptibility](../../../../../../percolation-susceptibility.md) decomposes over the $\ell^1$ spheres as

$$
\chi(p)=\mathbb E_p|C|
=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\mathbb E_pM_n,
\qquad \mathbb E_pM_0=1.
$$

If $\mathbb E_pM_n>r^n$ for every $n\geq1$, then

$$
\chi(p)>1+\sum_{n=1}^\infty r^n
=1+\frac r{1-r}=\chi(p),
$$

a contradiction. Hence some $m\geq1$ satisfies $\mathbb E_pM_m\leq r^m$.
