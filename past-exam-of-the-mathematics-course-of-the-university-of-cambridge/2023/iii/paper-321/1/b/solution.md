<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [Keplerian shearing sheet](../../../../../../keplerian-shearing-sheet.md), the shear rate is $S=3\Omega/2$ and the [shearing-sheet tidal potential](../../../../../../shearing-sheet-tidal-potential.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Phi_{t,m}=-\Omega Sx^2=-\frac32\Omega^2x^2}.
$$

A uniform steady solution is

$$
\Sigma=\Sigma_0,
\qquad P=P_0,
\qquad \mathbf u_0=-Sx\,\mathbf e_y.
$$

The [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md) of this [linear shear flow](../../../../../../linear-shear-flow.md) balances the radial tidal acceleration. The only nonzero background component of the [viscous stress tensor](../../../../../../viscous-stress-tensor.md) that matters is $T_{xy}=-\nu(\Sigma_0)\Sigma_0S$, which is spatially constant. Hence $\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf T=0$: a local uniform patch has no stress gradient or torque divergence to drive an [accretion flow](../../../../../../accretion-flow.md).
