<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Setting $\nu=\nu_b=0$, the [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) reduces to

$$
\lambda\left(\lambda^2+\Omega^2-2\pi G\Sigma_0k+v_s^2k^2\right)=0.
$$

An axisymmetric density mode grows when

$$
2\pi G\Sigma_0k-v_s^2k^2>\Omega^2.
$$

The left-hand side is a concave [quadratic function](../../../../../../quadratic-function.md) of $k$, with maximum $(\pi G\Sigma_0)^2/v_s^2$ at $k=\pi G\Sigma_0/v_s^2$. Growth is therefore possible precisely when the [Toomre stability criterion](../../../../../../toomre-s-stability-criterion.md) is violated:

$$
\boxed{Q=\frac{v_s\Omega}{\pi G\Sigma_0}<1}.
$$
