<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Take the [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) in $x$, with convention $\widetilde v(k_x)=\int v(x)e^{-ik_xx}\,dx$. The differentiation rules $\widehat{v''}=-k_x^2\widetilde v$ and $\widehat{x^2v}=-\widetilde v''$ turn the forced equation into

$$
S^2k_y^2\frac{d^2\widetilde v}{dk_x^2}
+[\kappa_r^2+v_s^2(k_x^2+k_y^2)]\widetilde v
=i\left[(2\Omega-S)k_x\widetilde\psi
-Sk_y^2\frac{d\widetilde\psi}{dk_x}\right].
$$

For the unforced equation, $dk_x/dt=Sk_y$ implies

$$
\frac{d^2}{dt^2}=S^2k_y^2\frac{d^2}{dk_x^2}.
$$

It is therefore exactly the [shearing-wave oscillator](../../../../../../shearing-wave-oscillator.md) found in part c, now parametrized by radial [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) rather than time.
