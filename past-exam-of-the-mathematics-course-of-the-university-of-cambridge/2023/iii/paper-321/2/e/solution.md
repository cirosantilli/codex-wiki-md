<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The homogeneous equation can be written

$$
v''+\left[
\frac{S^2k_y^2x^2}{v_s^2}-k_y^2-
\frac{\kappa_r^2}{v_s^2}
\right]v=0.
$$

Its solutions are locally oscillatory where

$$
S^2k_y^2x^2>\kappa_r^2+v_s^2k_y^2,
$$

equivalently

$$
\boxed{|Sx|>\sqrt{v_s^2+\frac{\kappa_r^2}{k_y^2}}>v_s}.
$$

The relative background orbital speed is $|\mathbf u_0|=|Sx|$, so propagating wave zones occur only where the relative motion of the satellite and disk material is [supersonic](../../../../../../supersonic-flow.md).
