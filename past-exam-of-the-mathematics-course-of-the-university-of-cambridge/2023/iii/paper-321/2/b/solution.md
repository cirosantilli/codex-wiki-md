<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first-order perturbation of the [vortensity](../../../../../../vortensity.md) is

$$
\boxed{f'=
\frac1\Sigma(\partial_xv_y-\partial_yv_x)
-\frac{2\Omega-S}{\Sigma^2}\Sigma'}.
$$

Taking the curl of the linearized momentum equation and using the linearized continuity equation yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{Df'}{Dt}=0}.
$$

This is [linearized vortensity conservation](../../../../../../linearized-vortensity-conservation.md). It is the perturbative form of material conservation of [potential vorticity](../../../../../../potential-vorticity.md) in an inviscid [barotropic fluid](../../../../../../barotropic-fluid.md); the forcing $-\nabla\Psi$ is a gradient and therefore creates no [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md).
