<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A satellite on a circular orbit is stationary in the corotating sheet. Write its tidal forcing and the response as

$$
\Psi=\psi(x)e^{ik_yy},
\qquad
v_y=v(x)e^{ik_yy}.
$$

Since $D=-iSk_yx$, the forced wave equation becomes

$$
\boxed{
-v_s^2v''+
[\kappa_r^2+v_s^2k_y^2-S^2k_y^2x^2]v
=(2\Omega-S)\psi'-Sxk_y^2\psi}.
$$

This ordinary differential equation describes a [satellite-forced density wave in an astrophysical disk](../../../../../../satellite-forced-density-wave-in-an-astrophysical-disk.md).
