<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives

$$
\frac{dp}{dz}=-\rho\Omega_z^2z.
$$

Define vertically integrated pressure and the density-weighted [disk scale height](../../../../../../disk-scale-height.md) by

$$
P=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}p\,dz,
\qquad
H^2=\frac1\Sigma\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\rho z^2\,dz.
$$

Multiply the hydrostatic equation by $z$ and integrate. Since $zp\to0$ at both boundaries, [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
-P=-\Omega_z^2\Sigma H^2.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{P=\Sigma H^2\Omega_z^2}.
$$
