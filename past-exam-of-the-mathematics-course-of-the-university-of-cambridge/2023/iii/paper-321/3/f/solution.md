<h1 id="3/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

At the equilibrium thickness $H_0$,

$$
\Omega_z^2H_0=CH_0^{-\gamma}.
$$

Set $H=H_0+h$ and retain terms linear in $h$. The [linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md) is

$$
\ddot h+(\gamma+1)\Omega_z^2h=0.
$$

Thus the [vertical breathing mode of an astrophysical disk](../../../../../../vertical-breathing-mode-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md) has angular frequency

$$
\boxed{\omega_H=\sqrt{\gamma+1}\,\Omega_z}.
$$
