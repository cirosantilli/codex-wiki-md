<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $B\Subset\Omega$ and let $h$ be the harmonic function whose boundary values are $\max(u_1,u_2)$. The harmonic extensions $(u_i)_B$ satisfy $(u_i)_B\leq h$ by the [maximum principle for harmonic functions](../../../../../../../maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md), while subharmonicity gives $u_i\leq(u_i)_B$ in $B$. Hence $\max(u_1,u_2)\leq h$, proving that the maximum of two [subharmonic functions](../../../../../../../subharmonic-function.md) is subharmonic.
