<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take a nonnegative radial [mollifier](../../../../../../../mollifier.md) $\eta_\varepsilon$ supported in $B_\varepsilon(0)$, with $\varepsilon<\operatorname{dist}(x,\partial\Omega)$. Writing the convolution in polar coordinates and applying the spherical [mean value property for harmonic functions](../../../../../../../mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md) at every radius shows directly that

$$
(u*\eta_\varepsilon)(x)=u(x).
$$

The convolution is [smooth](../../../../../../../smooth-function.md), so $u$ agrees locally with a smooth function. Since $x$ was arbitrary, $u\in C^\infty(\Omega)$.
