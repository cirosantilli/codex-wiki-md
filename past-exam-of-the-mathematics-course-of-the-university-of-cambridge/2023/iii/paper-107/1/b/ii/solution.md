<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On a relatively compact ball, mollification commutes with the [Laplacian](../../../../../../../laplacian.md), so $u_\varepsilon=u*\eta_\varepsilon$ is a smooth harmonic function. Repeated [interior derivative estimates](../../../../../../../interior-derivative-estimate-for-a-harmonic-function.md), together with the [Caccioppoli inequality](../../../../../../../caccioppoli-inequality.md), bound every derivative of $u_\varepsilon$ on a smaller ball by the local $L^2$ norm of $u$, uniformly as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. The [Arzelà-Ascoli theorem](../../../../../../../arzela-ascoli-theorem.md) and a diagonal argument give a smooth local limit, while mollification gives $u_\varepsilon\to u$ in $L^2_{\rm loc}$. Thus the limit equals $u$ almost everywhere. After choosing this smooth representative, $u$ is harmonic pointwise. This is the [Weyl lemma](../../../../../../../weyl-lemma.md) for an $H^1$ weak solution.
