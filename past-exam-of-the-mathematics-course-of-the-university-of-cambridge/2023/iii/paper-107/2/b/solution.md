↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](../../../../../../weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) fails without a condition at infinity. The function

$$
u(x)=x_n
$$

is harmonic on the upper half-space, continuous on its closure, and vanishes on the boundary, but it is positive and unbounded in the interior.
