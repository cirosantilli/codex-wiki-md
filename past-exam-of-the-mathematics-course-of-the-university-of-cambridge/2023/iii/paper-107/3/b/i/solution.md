<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a compactly supported variation $v+t\phi$, differentiation of the area functional at $t=0$ gives the [first variation](../../../../../../../first-variation.md)

$$
0=\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_{t=0}\int\sqrt{1+|D(v+t\phi)|^2}
=\int\frac{Dv\mathbin\cdot D\phi}{\sqrt{1+|Dv|^2}}.
$$

After [integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), this is the [minimal surface equation for a graph](../../../../../../../minimal-surface-equation-for-a-graph.md)

$$
D_i\left(\frac{D_iv}{\sqrt{1+|Dv|^2}}\right)=0.
$$

For $v_R(x)=R^{-1}v(Rx)$ one has $Dv_R(x)=Dv(Rx)$. The equation is invariant under this scaling, so $v_R$ solves it on $B_1$ for every $R>0$.
