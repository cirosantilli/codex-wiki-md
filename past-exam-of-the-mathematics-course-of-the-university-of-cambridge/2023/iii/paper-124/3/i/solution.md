<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the three [rank-one quadratic forms](../../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md)

$$
P_1=ac,\qquad
P_2=bd,\qquad
P_3=(a+b)(c+d).
$$

The first two already give $ac$ and $bd$, while

$$
ad+bc=P_3-P_1-P_2.
$$

**Hence all three required polynomials lie in their linear span.**
