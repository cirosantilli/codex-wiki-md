<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An infinitesimal [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) is $\delta X^\mu=\omega^\mu{}_{\nu}X^\nu$ with $\omega_{\mu\nu}=-\omega_{\nu\mu}$. Applying [Noether theorem](../../../../../../noether-theorem.md) to this continuous symmetry gives the conserved [Lorentz current](../../../../../../lorentz-current.md)

$$
J_\alpha^{\mu\nu}=\frac1{2\pi\alpha'}
\left(X^\mu\partial_\alpha X^\nu-X^\nu\partial_\alpha X^\mu\right),
\qquad \partial^\alpha J_\alpha^{\mu\nu}=0.
$$

Its [Noether charge](../../../../../../noether-charge.md) is $M^{\mu\nu}=\int_0^\pi d\sigma\,J_\tau^{\mu\nu}$. Substituting the [open-string mode expansion](../../../../../../open-string-mode-expansion.md) and using [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of the cosine modes yields

$$
M^{\mu\nu}=x^\mu p^\nu-x^\nu p^\mu
-i\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1n
\left(\alpha_{-n}^\mu\alpha_n^\nu-
\alpha_{-n}^\nu\alpha_n^\mu\right).
$$

The first term is orbital angular momentum; the sum is the contribution of the [string oscillators](../../../../../../string-oscillator.md).
