<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A local operator $\mathcal O(w,\bar w)$ is a [primary operator](../../../../../../primary-field.md) of [conformal weights](../../../../../../conformal-weight.md) $(h,\widetilde h)$ when its [operator product expansions](../../../../../../operator-product-expansion.md) with the holomorphic and antiholomorphic stress tensors are

$$
T(z)\mathcal O(w,\bar w)\sim
\frac{h\mathcal O(w,\bar w)}{(z-w)^2}
+\frac{\partial\mathcal O(w,\bar w)}{z-w},
$$



$$
\bar T(\bar z)\mathcal O(w,\bar w)\sim
\frac{\widetilde h\mathcal O(w,\bar w)}{(\bar z-\bar w)^2}
+\frac{\bar\partial\mathcal O(w,\bar w)}{\bar z-\bar w},
$$

with no more singular poles. Equivalently, under a local [conformal transformation](../../../../../../conformal-map.md) it transforms covariantly with holomorphic exponent $h$ and antiholomorphic exponent $\widetilde h$.
