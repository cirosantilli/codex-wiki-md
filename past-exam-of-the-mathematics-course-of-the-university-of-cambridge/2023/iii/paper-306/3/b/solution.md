<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let

$$
V=\epsilon_{\mu\nu}:\!\partial X^\mu\bar\partial X^\nu e^{ik\cdot X}\!:.
$$

The exponential is a [primary operator](../../../../../../primary-field.md) with [conformal weights](../../../../../../conformal-weight.md) $(\alpha'k^2/4,\alpha'k^2/4)$. In the $T(z)V(w,\bar w)$ OPE, contracting one derivative in $T$ with $\partial X^\mu$ and the other with the exponential produces a third-order pole proportional to $k^\mu\epsilon_{\mu\nu}$. Its antiholomorphic counterpart is proportional to $\epsilon_{\mu\nu}k^\nu$. Therefore $V$ is primary exactly when its [polarization tensor](../../../../../../polarization-tensor.md) is transverse in both indices,

$$
\boxed{k^\mu\epsilon_{\mu\nu}=0,
\qquad \epsilon_{\mu\nu}k^\nu=0}.
$$

The remaining second-order poles give

$$
\boxed{(h,\widetilde h)=
\left(1+\frac{\alpha'k^2}{4},
1+\frac{\alpha'k^2}{4}\right)}.
$$
