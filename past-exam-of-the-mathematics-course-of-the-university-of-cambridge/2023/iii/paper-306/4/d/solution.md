<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The classical elimination in parts b and c discarded the field-dependent [Gaussian functional integral](../../../../../../gaussian-functional-integral.md) determinant. At one loop that determinant is a local curvature coupling and supplies the [Buscher rules](../../../../../../buscher-rules.md) dilaton shift

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\Phi=\Phi-\frac12\log V}.
$$

The metric alone consequently need not obey $\widetilde R_{\mu\nu}=0$. The relevant leading [sigma-model beta function](../../../../../../sigma-model-beta-function.md) in the dual background instead contains

$$
\beta^{\widetilde G}_{\mu\nu}
=\alpha'\left(\widetilde R_{\mu\nu}
+2\widetilde\nabla_\mu\widetilde\nabla_\nu\widetilde\Phi\right)+\cdots.
$$

The new dilaton term cancels the failure of the dual metric to be Ricci-flat, so the complete metric-dilaton background remains conformal and physically [T-dual](../../../../../../t-duality.md) to the original one.
