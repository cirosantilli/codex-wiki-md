<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The target metric is a coupling of the [string nonlinear sigma model](../../../../../../string-nonlinear-sigma-model.md). Quantum consistency requires the gauge-fixed worldsheet theory to preserve [Weyl invariance](../../../../../../weyl-transformation.md), so its [sigma-model beta functions](../../../../../../sigma-model-beta-function.md) must vanish. With no B-field or varying dilaton, the metric beta function begins as

$$
\beta^G_{\mu\nu}=\alpha'R_{\mu\nu}+O(\alpha'^2).
$$

Therefore vanishing of the [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](../../../../../../worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) requires

$$
\boxed{R_{\mu\nu}=0}
$$

to leading order in $\alpha'$: the target-space metric must be Ricci-flat at this order.
