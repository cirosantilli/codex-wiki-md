<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Integrating by parts makes the $Z$ dependence $-i\int Z\,\partial_\alpha p^\alpha$. Its [functional integral](../../../../../../functional-measure.md) imposes the constraint $\partial_\alpha p^\alpha=0$. On the simply connected plane, the [Poincaré lemma](../../../../../../poincare-lemma.md) therefore permits the local and global parametrization

$$
p^\alpha=\frac1{2\pi\alpha'}\epsilon^{\alpha\beta}\partial_\beta\widetilde Z.
$$

Substitution into $S_2$ gives

$$
S_{\rm dual}[Y,\widetilde Z]
=\frac1{4\pi\alpha'}\int d^2\sigma
\left(G_{ij}(Y)\partial^\alpha Y^i\partial_\alpha Y^j
+V(Y)^{-1}\partial^\alpha\widetilde Z\partial_\alpha\widetilde Z\right).
$$

This is the [Buscher procedure](../../../../../../buscher-procedure.md) for the translation isometry in $z$, and its [T-duality](../../../../../../t-duality.md) replaces $G_{zz}=V$ by $\widetilde G_{\widetilde z\widetilde z}=V^{-1}$.
