<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The first term of the action is the [free boson conformal field theory](../../../../../../free-boson-conformal-field-theory.md). For the curvature coupling, insert the supplied [first variation](../../../../../../first-variation.md) of $\sqrt gR^{(2)}$, integrate by parts twice and discard the boundary term. Its metric variation is the stress-tensor improvement

$$
-q\left(\nabla_\alpha\nabla_\beta X-g_{\alpha\beta}\nabla^2X\right)
$$

in the conventions of the question. In a locally flat complex coordinate, the holomorphic component of the complete [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md) is therefore

$$
T(z)=-\frac1{\alpha'}:\!\partial X\partial X\!:(z)-q:\!\partial^2X\!:(z),
$$

as required. Thus the coupling $q\int\sqrt g\,XR^{(2)}$ makes $X$ a [background-charge scalar field](../../../../../../background-charge-scalar-field.md), equivalently a worldsheet coordinate in a [linear dilaton conformal field theory](../../../../../../linear-dilaton-conformal-field-theory.md).
