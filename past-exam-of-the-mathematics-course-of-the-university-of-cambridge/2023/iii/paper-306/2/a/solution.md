The universal [stress-tensor operator-product expansion](../../../../../../stress-tensor-operator-product-expansion.md) in a [two-dimensional conformal field theory](../../../../../../two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md) is

$$
T(z)T(w)\sim
\frac{c/2}{(z-w)^4}
+\frac{2T(w)}{(z-w)^2}
+\frac{\partial T(w)}{z-w}.
$$

Its fourth-order coefficient defines the [central charge](../../../../../../central-charge.md) $c$.
