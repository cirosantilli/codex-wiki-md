<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take $D$ embedding coordinates and give one coordinate the background charge $q$. The remaining $D-1$ free bosons contribute $D-1$, while the distinguished coordinate contributes $1+6\alpha'q^2$, so the matter [central charge](../../../../../../central-charge.md) is

$$
c_{\rm matter}=D+6\alpha'q^2.
$$

The [worldsheet ghosts](../../../../../../worldsheet-ghost-field.md) contributes $-26$. Cancellation of the [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](../../../../../../worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) therefore requires

$$
\boxed{q^2=\frac{26-D}{6\alpha'}},
$$

which is real for $D<26$ and produces a noncritical bosonic string.

In target-space language the [dilaton](../../../../../../dilaton.md) is linear in this coordinate, so the local [string coupling](../../../../../../string-coupling.md) $g_s=e^\Phi$ changes exponentially. One end of the target direction is weakly coupled and the other is strongly coupled. Consequently [string perturbation theory](../../../../../../string-perturbation-theory.md) is trustworthy only in the weak-coupling region, rather than throughout the full background.
