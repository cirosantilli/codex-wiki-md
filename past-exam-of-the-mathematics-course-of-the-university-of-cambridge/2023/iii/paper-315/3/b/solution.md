<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume a thin [isothermal atmosphere](../../../../../../isothermal-atmosphere.md), so $g\simeq GM_p/R_p^2$ and the surface area are constant through it. Integrating [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) from base pressure $P_0$ to negligible top pressure gives atmospheric column mass $P_0/g$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{
M_{\rm atm}\simeq4\pi R_p^2\frac{P_0}{g}
=\frac{4\pi P_0R_p^4}{GM_p}}.
$$

Equivalently, $M_{\rm atm}\sim4\pi R_p^2\rho_0H$, with $\rho_0=P_0\mu m_H/(k_BT_0)$ and $H=k_BT_0/(\mu m_Hg)$; the explicit temperature dependence cancels.
