<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiate the result of part i and use hydrostatic equilibrium:

$$
\frac{dT}{dz}
=aT\frac{d\log P}{dz}
=-\frac{a\mu m_Hg}{k_B}.
$$

Thus the constant [atmospheric lapse rate](../../../../../../../lapse-rate-atmosphere.md) is

$$
\boxed{
\Gamma=-\frac{dT}{dz}
=\frac{\mu m_Hg}{k_B}
\frac{\log(T_b/T_t)}{\log(P_b/P_t)}}.
$$
