<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply the [formal solution of the radiative transfer equation](../../../../../../../formal-solution-of-the-radiative-transfer-equation.md) successively to the two isothermal layers at normal incidence. In [local thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../../local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md),

$$
\boxed{
I_{\nu,2}=I_{\nu,0}e^{-(\tau_1+\tau_2)}
+B_\nu(T_1)(1-e^{-\tau_1})e^{-\tau_2}
+B_\nu(T_2)(1-e^{-\tau_2})}.
$$

For [optically thin](../../../../../../../optically-thin-medium.md) layers this becomes

$$
\boxed{
I_{\nu,2}=I_{\nu,0}
+\tau_1[B_\nu(T_1)-I_{\nu,0}]
+\tau_2[B_\nu(T_2)-I_{\nu,0}]
+O(\tau^2)}.
$$

This assumes no scattering and negligible reflection between layers.
