<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a real boundary point $x\ne0$, the [Boundary-point Bessel flow for SLE](../../../../../../boundary-point-bessel-flow-for-sle.md) says that $V_t^x/\sqrt\kappa$ is a Bessel process of dimension

$$
d=1+\frac4\kappa.
$$

When $\kappa\leq4$, one has $d\geq2$, so part 2(b), including its logarithmic $d=2$ case, shows that no fixed boundary point is swallowed.

If the trace touched the real line away from its starting point, or if a later segment crossed an earlier segment, the resulting hull would disconnect from infinity a nonempty real interval, which contains a [rational number](../../../../../../rational-number.md). Applying the preceding argument after every rational time and using the [Conformal Markov property of SLE](../../../../../../conformal-markov-property-of-sle.md) rules this out on a countable probability-one event. Continuity of the trace then shows that no two distinct times have the same image. Thus $\operatorname{SLE}_\kappa$ is simple for $0<\kappa\leq4$.
