<h1 id="3/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let

$$
R=\sup\{r>0:\{z\in\mathbb H:|z|<r\}\subseteq K_1\}.
$$

By the stated fact, $R>0$ almost surely. Given $\epsilon\in(0,1)$, choose a deterministic $r_0>0$ with $\mathbb P(R\geq r_0)\geq1-\epsilon$. The [Scaling invariance of SLE](../../../../../../../scaling-invariance-of-sle.md) gives

$$
K_t\overset d=\sqrt t\,K_1,
$$

and therefore, for every $t\geq0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P\!\left(
\{z\in\mathbb H:|z|<r_0\sqrt t\}\subseteq K_t
\right)\geq1-\epsilon.}
$$
