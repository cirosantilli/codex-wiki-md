<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\widehat B_t=rB_{t/r^2}$. By [Brownian scaling](../../../../../../brownian-scaling.md), $\widehat B$ is standard Brownian motion. Substituting $Y_t=rX_{t/r^2}$ into the Bessel equation gives

$$
dY_t
=d\widehat B_t+\frac{d-1}{2Y_t}dt,
\qquad
Y_0=rx.
$$

Weak uniqueness for the Bessel equation shows that $Y$ is a Bessel process of dimension $d$ started at $rx$. This is the [Scaling invariance of a Bessel process](../../../../../../scaling-invariance-of-a-bessel-process.md).
