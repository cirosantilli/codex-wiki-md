↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Equip $C_0^\infty(D)$ with the [Dirichlet inner product](../../../../../../../dirichlet-inner-product.md)

$$
(f,g)_\nabla=\frac1{2\pi}\int_D\nabla f(x)\mathbin\cdot\nabla g(x)\,dx.
$$

The [zero-boundary Sobolev space](../../../../../../../zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md) $H_0^1(D)$ is its [Hilbert space completion](../../../../../../../hilbert-space-completion.md) in the norm $\|f\|_\nabla=(f,f)_\nabla^{1/2}$.
