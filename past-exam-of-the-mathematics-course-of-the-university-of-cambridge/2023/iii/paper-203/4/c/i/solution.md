↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The zero-Dirichlet [Green function of the Laplacian](../../../../../../../green-function-of-the-laplacian.md) $G_D(x,y)$ is symmetric, vanishes at the boundary in the appropriate sense, is harmonic in $x$ away from $y$, and satisfies

$$
-\Delta_xG_D(x,y)=2\pi\delta_y
$$

as a [distribution](../../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Equivalently, for suitable $\phi$,

$$
\boxed{(-2\pi\Delta^{-1}\phi)(x)
=\int_DG_D(x,y)\phi(y)\,dy.}
$$
