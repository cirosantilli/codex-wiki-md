<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Conformal Markov property of SLE](../../../../../../conformal-markov-property-of-sle.md) says that, conditionally on the curve through time $t$, mapping out the initial hull by $g_t-U_t$ turns the future into an independent [Schramm–Loewner evolution](../../../../../../schramm-loewner-evolution.md) in $(\mathbb H,0,\infty)$. More precisely,

$$
\widetilde K_s
=\bigl(g_t(K_{t+s}\setminus K_t)-U_t\bigr)^{\mathrm{fill}}
$$

has driving function $\widetilde U_s=U_{t+s}-U_t$. Since $U_t=\sqrt\kappa B_t$, the [stationary increments](../../../../../../stationary-increments.md) and [independent increments](../../../../../../independent-increments.md) of Brownian motion give the asserted independence and equality in law.
