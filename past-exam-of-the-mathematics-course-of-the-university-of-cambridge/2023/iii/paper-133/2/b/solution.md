<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $N=\mathbb Z^2$ and let $t$ generate the other factor of $\Gamma_B=N\rtimes_B\mathbb Z$. For $n\in N$,

$$
[t,n]=tnt^{-1}n^{-1}=(B-I)n.
$$

After [abelianization](../../../../../../abelianization.md), the image of $N$ is therefore

$$
N/(B-I)N.
$$

If $1$ is not an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $B$, then $\det(B-I)\ne0$. Hence $(B-I)N$ is a full-rank sublattice of $N$ with finite [index of a subgroup](../../../../../../index-of-a-subgroup.md) $|\det(B-I)|$. The displayed quotient, and therefore the image of the $\mathbb Z^2$ factor in $\Gamma_B^{\mathrm{ab}}$, is finite. In fact the [abelianization of a semidirect product by the integers](../../../../../../abelianization-of-a-semidirect-product-by-the-integers.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_B^{\mathrm{ab}}
\cong\mathbb Z\oplus N/(B-I)N.}
$$
