<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

This is the [Baumslag-Solitar group](../../../../../../baumslag-solitar-group.md) $G=\operatorname{BS}(1,2)$. Its defining relation gives, by [mathematical induction](../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md),

$$
b^nab^{-n}=a^{2^n}.
$$

In the cyclic subgroup $H=\langle a\rangle$ with generator $a$,

$$
d_H(1,a^{2^n})=2^n,
$$

whereas in $G$, with generators $a,b$,

$$
d_G(1,a^{2^n})\leq2n+1.
$$

If inclusion were a $(\lambda,\varepsilon)$-[quasi-isometric embedding](../../../../../../quasi-isometric-embedding.md), its lower bound would imply

$$
\lambda^{-1}2^n-\varepsilon\leq2n+1
$$

for every $n$, which is impossible because an [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) eventually dominates every [linear function](../../../../../../linear-function.md). Hence $H$ is not quasi-isometrically embedded; it is an [exponentially distorted subgroup](../../../../../../exponentially-distorted-subgroup.md).
