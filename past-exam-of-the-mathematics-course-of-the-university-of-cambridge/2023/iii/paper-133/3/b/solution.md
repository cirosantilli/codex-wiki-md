<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $r:G\to H$ be a retraction. Part (a) supplies a constant $C$ such that

$$
d_S(h_1,h_2)\leq C\,d_T(h_1,h_2).
$$

The finite set $r(S)$ generates $H$. Put

$$
D=\max_{s\in S}|r(s)|_T.
$$

If a shortest $S$-word represents $h_1^{-1}h_2\in H$, applying the [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $r$ gives a $T$-word for the same element of length at most $D\,d_S(h_1,h_2)$. Hence

$$
d_T(h_1,h_2)\leq D\,d_S(h_1,h_2).
$$

The inclusion is therefore bilipschitz and in particular a [quasi-isometric embedding](../../../../../../quasi-isometric-embedding.md). Thus every finitely generated [retract subgroup](../../../../../../retract-subgroup.md) is quasi-isometrically embedded.
