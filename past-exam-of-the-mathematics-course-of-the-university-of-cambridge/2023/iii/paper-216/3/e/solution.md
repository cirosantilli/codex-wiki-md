<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Part c gives the integral representation

$$
\mathcal E_K(f)
=\frac12\int\pi(dx)K(x,dy)(f(y)-f(x))^2.
$$

The integrand vanishes on the diagonal $y=x$. Thus the assumed off-diagonal [Peskun ordering](../../../../../../peskun-ordering.md) implies

$$
\mathcal E_K(f)\geq\mathcal E_Q(f)
$$

for every $f\in L^2(\pi)$.

On the mean-zero subspace, the variational characterization of the [spectral gap](../../../../../../spectral-gap.md) of a positive reversible kernel is

$$
\operatorname{gap}(K)
=\inf_{\pi(f)=0,\ f\ne0}
\frac{\mathcal E_K(f)}{\operatorname{Var}_\pi(f)}.
$$

It follows immediately that

$$
\operatorname{gap}(K)\geq\operatorname{gap}(Q).
$$

Equivalently, the energy inequality says $K\preceq Q$ in the [Löwner order](../../../../../../lowner-order.md) on $L^2_0(\pi)$. Positivity permits the [operator monotonicity of the square root](../../../../../../operator-monotonicity-of-the-square-root.md) and hence $K^{1/2}\preceq Q^{1/2}$; the [spectral representations](../../../../../../projection-valued-measure.md) in the question identify the top spectral values and give the same gap inequality.
