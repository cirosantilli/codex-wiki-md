<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $f,g\in L^2(\pi)$, [detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md) says that the measure $\pi(dx)K(x,dy)$ is invariant under exchanging $x$ and $y$. Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle f,Kg\rangle_\pi
&=\int f(x)g(y)\,\pi(dx)K(x,dy)\\
&=\int f(y)g(x)\,\pi(dx)K(x,dy)
=\langle Kf,g\rangle_\pi.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is precisely the defining identity for a [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md).
