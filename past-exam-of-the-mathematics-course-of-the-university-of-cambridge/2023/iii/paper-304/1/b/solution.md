<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Replace each occurrence of $x(t)$ in the interaction by the [functional derivative](../../../../../../functional-derivative.md) that inserts it. In standard Minkowski source conventions,

$$
Z[J]
=\exp\left[
-\frac{i\lambda}{3!}\int dt
\left(\frac1i\frac{\delta}{\delta J(t)}\right)^3
\right]Z_0[J]
$$

and hence

$$
Z[J]
=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac1{n!}
\left[-\frac{i\lambda}{3!}\int dt
\left(\frac1i\frac{\delta}{\delta J(t)}\right)^3\right]^n
Z_0[J].
$$

The factors of $i$ are adjusted together if one uses the source convention of part a directly.

This is a [perturbation series](../../../../../../perturbation-series.md), generally an asymptotic rather than convergent series because the number of [Wick contractions](../../../../../../wick-s-theorem.md) grows factorially. For real cubic coupling the potential is also unbounded on one side, so the real-axis theory does not possess a stable nonperturbative ground state without a contour prescription or further stabilizing interactions.
