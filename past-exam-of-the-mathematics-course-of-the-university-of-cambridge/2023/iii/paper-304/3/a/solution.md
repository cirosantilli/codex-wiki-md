<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the convention in the question, a finite [Yang-Mills gauge transformation](../../../../../../yang-mills-gauge-transformation.md) acts covariantly on the field strength:

$$
F_{\mu\nu}\mapsto F'_{\mu\nu}=UF_{\mu\nu}U^\dagger
$$

or with $U$ and $U^\dagger$ exchanged if the opposite convention is used for $D_\mu$. Cyclicity of the [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(F'_{\mu\nu}F'^{\mu\nu})
=\operatorname{Tr}(UF_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}U^\dagger)
=\operatorname{Tr}(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}),
$$

so the [Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../../yang-mills-theory.md) Lagrangian is gauge invariant.

The quadratic gauge-field operator has zero directions $A_\mu\sim A_\mu+D_\mu\alpha$ along each [gauge orbit](../../../../../../gauge-orbit.md). It therefore has no inverse on the full field space. [Gauge fixing](../../../../../../gauge-fixing.md) removes this degeneracy and produces a propagator, while the [Faddeev-Popov determinant](../../../../../../faddeev-popov-determinant.md) accounts for the corresponding Jacobian.
