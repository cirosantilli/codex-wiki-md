↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For massless [phi-fourth theory](../../../../../../quartic-interaction.md) in four dimensions, the momentum-space rules are

$$
\text{propagator: }\frac{i}{p^2+i\epsilon},
\qquad
\text{quartic vertex: }-i\lambda.
$$

Writing

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{ct}}
=\frac12\delta_Z(\partial\phi)^2
-\frac12\delta m^2\phi^2
-\frac{\delta\lambda}{4!}\phi^4,
$$

the two-point counterterm insertion is $i(\delta_Zp^2-\delta m^2)$ and the four-point counterterm is $-i\delta\lambda$.

The renormalized [one-particle-irreducible correlation function](../../../../../../one-particle-irreducible-correlation-function.md) $\Gamma_4$ through order $\lambda^2$ contains the tree quartic vertex, the quartic counterterm, and three one-loop bubble diagrams. The bubbles are the $s$, $t$, and $u$ channels and each has symmetry factor $1/2$.
