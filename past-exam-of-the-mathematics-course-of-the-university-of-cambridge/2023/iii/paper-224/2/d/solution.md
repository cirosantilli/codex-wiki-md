<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $P_i$ be the marginal [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md) of $X_i$. [Subadditivity of information entropy](../../../../../../subadditivity-of-information-entropy.md) and the [concavity of information entropy](../../../../../../concavity-of-information-entropy.md) give

$$
H(X_1^n)\leq\sum_{i=1}^nH(P_i)
\leq nH\!\left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nP_i\right).
$$

For each $a\in A$,

$$
\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nP_i(a)
=\mathbb P(X_J=a)=\overline P(a),
$$

so $H(X_1^n)\leq nH(\overline P)$.
