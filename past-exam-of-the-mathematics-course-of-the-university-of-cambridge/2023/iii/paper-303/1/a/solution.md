↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Landau theory](../../../../../../landau-theory.md) treats the [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) as spatially uniform and expands the free-energy density in powers allowed by its symmetries. The [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md) promotes it to a field $\phi(\mathbf x)$ and adds gradient terms such as $|\nabla\phi|^2$. It therefore describes spatial fluctuations, interfaces, defects, and [correlation lengths](../../../../../../correlation-length.md), while reducing to Landau theory for uniform fields.
