<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\Delta$ be the torus graph Laplacian and let $v:\Lambda_L\to\mathbb R^n$ have zero spatial mean. [Gaussian domination](../../../../../../gaussian-domination.md) states, in a standard normalization, that

$$
\frac{Z(v)}{Z(0)}
\leq
\exp\left\{\frac1{2\beta}(v,(-\Delta)^{-1}v)\right\}.
$$

Differentiating twice at zero gives the [infrared bound](../../../../../../infrared-bound.md): for every nonzero torus momentum $k$,

$$
\widehat G_L(k)
\leq\frac{n}{2\beta\,\varepsilon(k)},
\qquad
\varepsilon(k)=\sum_{j=1}^d(1-\cos k_j),
$$

up to the harmless normalization convention used for the Fourier transform and Hamiltonian.
