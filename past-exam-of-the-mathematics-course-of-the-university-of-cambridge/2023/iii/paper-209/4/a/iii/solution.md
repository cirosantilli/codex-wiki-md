<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The three-dimensional [Peierls argument](../../../../../../../peierls-argument.md) replaces planar contours by closed dual plaquette surfaces surrounding the negative cluster of the origin. A surface of area $m$ costs $e^{-2\beta m}$, and the number of connected surfaces of area $m$ containing a fixed nearby plaquette is at most $C^m$. Hence

$$
\mathbb P_{\beta}^{\mathbb Z^3,+}(\sigma_0=-1)
\leq\sum_{m\geq m_0}C^me^{-2\beta m}.
$$

This is below $1/2$ for sufficiently large $\beta$, so $\langle\sigma_0\rangle_{\beta,0}^{\mathbb Z^3,+}>0$.
