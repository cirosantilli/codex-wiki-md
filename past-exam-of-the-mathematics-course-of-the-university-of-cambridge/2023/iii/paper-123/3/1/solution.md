<h1 id="3/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a set $S$ of nonzero prime ideals of $K$, its [Dirichlet density](../../../../../../dirichlet-density.md) is

$$
\delta(S)=\lim_{s\to1^+}
\frac{\sum_{\mathfrak p\in S}(N\mathfrak p)^{-s}}
{\log(1/(s-1))},
$$

provided the limit exists.
