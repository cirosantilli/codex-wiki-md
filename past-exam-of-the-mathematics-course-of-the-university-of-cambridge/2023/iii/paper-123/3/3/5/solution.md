<h1 id="3/3/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

By Question 1, a prime ideal of $K$ is principal exactly when it splits completely in the [Hilbert class field](../../../../../../../hilbert-class-field.md) $H_K$. Question 3(4) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\delta\{\mathfrak p:\mathfrak p\text{ is principal}\}
=\frac1{[H_K:K]}
=\frac1{h_K}.}
$$
