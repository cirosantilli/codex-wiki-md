<h1 id="3/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Let $\mathfrak f_{L/K}$ be the [conductor of an abelian extension](../../../../../../conductor-of-an-abelian-extension.md) and let $\mathfrak m$ be divisible by it. For any $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, the [Chebotarev density theorem](../../../../../../chebotarev-density-theorem.md) supplies infinitely many unramified prime ideals $\mathfrak p$ with

$$
\left(\frac{L/K}{\mathfrak p}\right)=\sigma.
$$

Only finitely many primes divide $\mathfrak m_0$, so $\mathfrak p$ may be chosen prime to $\mathfrak m_0$. It then belongs to $I_K(\mathfrak m)$ and its image under the [Artin reciprocity map](../../../../../../artin-reciprocity-law.md) is $\sigma$. Hence the Artin map is surjective.
