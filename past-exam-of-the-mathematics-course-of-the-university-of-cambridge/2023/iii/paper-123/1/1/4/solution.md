↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

For an unramified prime $\mathfrak p$, the [ideal-theoretic decomposition law](../../../../../../../ideal-theoretic-decomposition-law.md) gives

$$
\mathfrak p\text{ splits completely in }H_K
\iff
\left(\frac{H_K/K}{\mathfrak p}\right)=1.
$$

Under the [Hilbert class field](../../../../../../../hilbert-class-field.md) isomorphism, this Artin symbol is the image of $[\mathfrak p]\in\operatorname{Cl}(K)$. It is trivial exactly when $[\mathfrak p]=1$, which means exactly that $\mathfrak p$ is a [principal ideal](../../../../../../../principal-ideal.md).
