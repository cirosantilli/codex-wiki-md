The [Hilbert class field](../../../../../../../hilbert-class-field.md) $H_K$ is the maximal unramified abelian extension of $K$, including the condition that real places do not become complex when infinite ramification is included. The [Artin reciprocity law](../../../../../../../artin-reciprocity-law.md) induces a canonical isomorphism

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(K)\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Gal}(H_K/K),
\qquad
[\mathfrak a]\longmapsto\left(\frac{H_K/K}{\mathfrak a}\right).
$$

**Consequently $[H_K:K]=h_K$.**
