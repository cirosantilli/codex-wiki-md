↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Suppose $d_K<-4$. Then $K$ has signature $(0,1)$, regulator $1$, and exactly $w_K=2$ roots of unity. The [analytic class number formula](../../../../../../../analytic-class-number-formula.md) becomes

$$
\operatorname*{res}_{s=1}\zeta_K(s)
=\frac{\pi h_K}{\sqrt{|d_K|}}.
$$

Because $\zeta_K(s)=\zeta(s)L(s,\chi_{d_K})$ and $\operatorname*{res}_{s=1}\zeta(s)=1$,

$$
h_K=\frac{\sqrt{|d_K|}}{\pi}|L(1,\chi_{d_K})|.
$$

Putting $m=|d_K|$ into the supplied odd-character identity yields the [quadratic class number formula](../../../../../../../quadratic-class-number-formula.md)

$$
h_K
=\frac1{|2-\chi_{d_K}(2)|}
\left|
\sum_{\substack{a\in(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times\\a<m/2}}
\chi_{d_K}(a)
\right|.
$$
