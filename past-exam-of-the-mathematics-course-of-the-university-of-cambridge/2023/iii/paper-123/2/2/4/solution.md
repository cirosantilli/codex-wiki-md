<h1 id="2/2/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Let $K/\mathbb Q$ be quadratic with discriminant $d_K$, and let

$$
\chi_{d_K}(n)=\left(\frac{d_K}{n}\right)
$$

be its primitive quadratic [Dirichlet character](../../../../../../../kronecker-symbol.md). For an unramified prime $p$, the Euler factor of $\zeta_K$ is

$$
\begin{cases}
(1-p^{-s})^{-2},&p\text{ splits},\\
(1-p^{-2s})^{-1},&p\text{ is inert}.
\end{cases}
$$

Since $\chi_{d_K}(p)$ is respectively $1$ or $-1$, this equals the product of the Euler factors of $\zeta(s)$ and $L(s,\chi_{d_K})$. At a ramified prime $\chi_{d_K}(p)=0$, and the same equality holds. Thus

$$
\boxed{\zeta_K(s)=\zeta(s)L(s,\chi_{d_K}).}
$$
