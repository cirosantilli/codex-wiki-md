<h1 id="4/2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ and let

$$
M=L^{[G,G]}
$$

be the fixed field of the commutator subgroup. Then $M/K$ is the maximal abelian subextension of $L/K$, with

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(M/K)\cong G^{\mathrm{ab}}.
$$

The [nonabelian norm-residue kernel theorem](../../../../../../../nonabelian-norm-residue-kernel-theorem.md) identifies the kernel of the global reciprocity map

$$
C_K\longrightarrow G^{\mathrm{ab}}
$$

with $N_{L/K}C_L$. Applied to the abelian extension $M/K$, the [Artin reciprocity law](../../../../../../../artin-reciprocity-law.md) identifies the kernel of the same map with $N_{M/K}C_M$. Therefore

$$
N_{L/K}C_L=N_{M/K}C_M,
$$

so the norm group of the Galois extension $L/K$ is the norm group of the abelian extension $M/K$.
