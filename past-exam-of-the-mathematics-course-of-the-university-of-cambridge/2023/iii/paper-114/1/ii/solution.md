<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The involution $(z_1,z_2)\mapsto(-z_1,\overline z_2)$ is free. Taking the first circle modulo the half-turn exhibits the quotient as the [mapping torus](../../../../../../mapping-torus.md) of a reflection of $S^1$, hence as the [Klein bottle](../../../../../../klein-bottle.md). It has a finite CW structure with one zero-cell, two one-cells, and one two-cell. With suitable generators its integral cellular differential is

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{(0,2)}\mathbb Z^2\xrightarrow0\mathbb Z\longrightarrow0.
$$

Therefore

$$
H_q(T^2/{\sim};\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&q=0,\\
\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2,&q=1,\\
0,&q\geq2,
\end{cases}
$$

whereas reduction modulo two kills the only nonzero boundary and gives

$$
H_q(T^2/{\sim};\mathbb F_2)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb F_2,&q=0,2,\\
\mathbb F_2^2,&q=1,\\
0,&q>2.
\end{cases}
$$
