<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The quotient is the [mapping torus](../../../../../../mapping-torus.md) of

$$
\tau:T^2\to T^2,\qquad(z_2,z_3)\mapsto(\overline z_2,\overline z_3).
$$

A mapping torus of a cellular map has a finite CW structure. On integral homology, $\tau_*$ is $1$ on $H_0(T^2)$ and $H_2(T^2)$ and is $-I$ on $H_1(T^2)\cong\mathbb Z^2$. The [Wang sequence](../../../../../../wang-sequence.md) therefore gives

$$
H_q(T^3/{\sim};\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&q=0,2,3,\\
\mathbb Z\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^2,&q=1,\\
0,&q>3.
\end{cases}
$$

Over $\mathbb F_2$, the map $\tau_*$ is the identity. The Wang sequence splits as vector spaces into one copy of $H_q(T^2;\mathbb F_2)$ and one copy of $H_{q-1}(T^2;\mathbb F_2)$, yielding

$$
H_q(T^3/{\sim};\mathbb F_2)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb F_2,&q=0,3,\\
\mathbb F_2^3,&q=1,2,\\
0,&q>3.
\end{cases}
$$
