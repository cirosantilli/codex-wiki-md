↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [almost sure supermartingale convergence theorem](../../../../../almost-sure-supermartingale-convergence-theorem.md) says that a supermartingale whose negative parts have uniformly bounded expectations converges almost surely to a finite integrable limit. In particular, every nonnegative supermartingale converges almost surely.

Here the process is uniformly bounded, say $0\leq X_n\leq C$. Let $X_\infty=\lim_nX_n$, whose existence follows from the theorem. The [dominated convergence theorem](../../../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md) then gives

$$
\mathbb E[X_\infty]=\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb E[X_n].
$$

The limit on the right also exists directly because the expectations of a [supermartingale](../../../../../supermartingale.md) form a decreasing sequence.
