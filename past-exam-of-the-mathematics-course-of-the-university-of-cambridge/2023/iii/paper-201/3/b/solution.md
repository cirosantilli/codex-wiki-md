<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Poisson central limit theorem](../../../../../../poisson-central-limit-theorem.md), or the ordinary [central limit theorem](../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) applied to the $X_i$, gives $Y_n\xrightarrow dY$ for a standard normal random variable $Y$. The negative-part map $x\mapsto x^-:=\max(-x,0)$ is continuous, so the [continuous mapping theorem](../../../../../../continuous-mapping-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{Y_n^-\xrightarrow dY^-.}
$$
