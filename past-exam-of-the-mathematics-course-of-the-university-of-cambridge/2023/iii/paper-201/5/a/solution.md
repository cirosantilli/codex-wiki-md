<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Brownian reflection principle](../../../../../../reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) reflects a path after its first hit of $x>0$ and gives

$$
\mathbb P(M_t\geq x)=2\mathbb P(B_t\geq x).
$$

Symmetry of the centered [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) gives $2\mathbb P(B_t\geq x)=\mathbb P(|B_t|\geq x)$. Both random variables are nonnegative, so their tail distributions agree for every $x$, proving the [Brownian running maximum](../../../../../../brownian-running-maximum.md) identity $M_t\stackrel d=|B_t|$.
