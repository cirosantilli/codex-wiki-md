<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first-order [subgradient inequality](../../../../../../subgradient-inequality.md) for a differentiable [convex function](../../../../../../convex-function.md) gives

$$
f(v)\geq f(w)+\langle\nabla f(w),v-w\rangle
$$

and, after interchanging $v$ and $w$,

$$
f(w)\geq f(v)+\langle\nabla f(v),w-v\rangle.
$$

Adding and rearranging yields

$$
\boxed{\langle\nabla f(v)-\nabla f(w),v-w\rangle\geq0.}
$$

**Thus the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) $F=\nabla f$ is a [monotone operator](../../../../../../monotone-operator.md).**
