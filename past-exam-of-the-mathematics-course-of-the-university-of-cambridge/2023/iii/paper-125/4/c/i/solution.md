<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An admissible change of [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../../weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) replaces the $x$-coordinate by a degree-one rational function, so the two logarithmic heights differ by $O(1)$. Dividing this bounded difference by $4^r$ in the defining limit shows that the canonical height is unchanged. It is intrinsic to the elliptic curve and the chosen point.
