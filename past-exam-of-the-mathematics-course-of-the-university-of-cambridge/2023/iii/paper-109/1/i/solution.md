<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Associate to each set $A_i\subseteq[n]$ its [characteristic vector of a set](../../../../../../characteristic-vector-of-a-set.md) $v_i\in\mathbb F_2^n$. The hypotheses say

$$
v_i\mathbin\cdot v_i=1,
\qquad
v_i\mathbin\cdot v_j=0\quad(i\ne j).
$$

If $\sum_i c_iv_i=0$, taking the [dot product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) with $v_j$ gives $c_j=0$. Thus the vectors are [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md), so $m\leq\dim\mathbb F_2^n=n$. Equality is attained by the $n$ singleton sets.
