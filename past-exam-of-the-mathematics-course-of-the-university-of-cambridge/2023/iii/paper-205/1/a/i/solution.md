The [Gaussian kernel](../../../../../../../gaussian-kernel.md) with bandwidth $\sigma^2>0$ is

$$
\boxed{k_\sigma(x,y)=\exp\!\left(-\frac{\|x-y\|_2^2}{2\sigma^2}\right).}
$$
