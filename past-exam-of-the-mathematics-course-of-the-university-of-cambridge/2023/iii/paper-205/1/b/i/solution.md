<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For points $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ and coefficients $c_1,\ldots,c_n$,

$$
\sum_{r,s=1}^n c_rc_s k(x_r,x_s)
=\mathbb E\!\left[\left(\sum_{r=1}^n c_r\widehat\phi(x_r)\right)^2\right]\geq0.
$$

The bound $|\widehat\phi|\leq M$ ensures that this [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) is finite. Symmetry is immediate, so an expected outer product of a [random feature map](../../../../../../../random-feature-map.md) always defines a [positive-semidefinite kernel](../../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-kernel.md).
