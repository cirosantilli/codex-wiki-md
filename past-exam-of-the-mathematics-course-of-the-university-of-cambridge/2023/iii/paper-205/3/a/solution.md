<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under the null, $\varepsilon_1$ and $\xi_1$ have [conditional independence](../../../../../../conditional-independence.md) given $z_1$. The [law of total expectation](../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(\varepsilon_1^2\xi_1^2)
=\mathbb E\!\left[
\mathbb E(\varepsilon_1^2\mid z_1)
\mathbb E(\xi_1^2\mid z_1)
\right]
\leq C^2.}
$$
