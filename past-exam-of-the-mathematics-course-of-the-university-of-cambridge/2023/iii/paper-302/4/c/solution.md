<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Direct expansion gives

$$
[D_\mu,D_\nu]\phi
=\{\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu+[A_\mu,A_\nu]\}\phi
=F_{\mu\nu}\phi.
$$

Since $D'_\mu(g\phi)=gD_\mu\phi$,

$$
F'_{\mu\nu}(g\phi)
=[D'_\mu,D'_\nu](g\phi)
=g[D_\mu,D_\nu]\phi
=gF_{\mu\nu}\phi.
$$

Therefore the non-Abelian [gauge field strength](../../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md) transforms covariantly:

$$
\boxed{F'_{\mu\nu}=gF_{\mu\nu}g^{-1}.}
$$
