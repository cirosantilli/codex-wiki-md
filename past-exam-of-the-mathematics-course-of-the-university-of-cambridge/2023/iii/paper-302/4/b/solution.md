<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first term $gA_\mu g^{-1}$ lies in $\mathfrak g$ because the [adjoint action](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md) preserves the Lie algebra. For the second, fix $x$ and consider the group curve $s\mapsto g(x+s e_\mu)g(x)^{-1}$ through the identity. Its tangent at zero is $(\partial_\mu g)g^{-1}$, so this is also in $\mathfrak g$. Since a [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) is a vector space, $A'_\mu\in\mathfrak g$.
