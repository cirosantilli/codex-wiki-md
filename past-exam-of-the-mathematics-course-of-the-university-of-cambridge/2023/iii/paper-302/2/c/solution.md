<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The transformed generators are

$$
J'_1=-J_1,
\qquad J'_2=J_2,
\qquad J'_3=-J_3.
$$

For example,

$$
[J'_1,J'_2]=[-J_1,J_2]=-iJ_3=iJ'_3.
$$

The other two cyclic commutators work identically, so

$$
[J'_i,J'_j]=i\epsilon_{ijk}J'_k.
$$

Conjugation by $C$ is therefore an [automorphism of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
