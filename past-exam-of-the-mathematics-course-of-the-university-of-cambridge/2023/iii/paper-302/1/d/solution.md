<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a smooth [Lie-group representation](../../../../../../lie-group-representation.md) $D:G\to GL(V)$, define its [derived representation](../../../../../../derived-representation.md)

$$
dD(X)=\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_{t=0}D(e^{tX}).
$$

Differentiation makes $dD$ linear. Applying $D$ to the group commutator curve

$$
e^{tX}e^{sY}e^{-tX}e^{-sY}
$$

and taking the mixed derivative at $(0,0)$ gives

$$
dD([X,Y])=[dD(X),dD(Y)].
$$

**Thus $dD:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak{gl}(V)$ is a [Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md).**
