<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $X$ belongs to the [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](../../../../../../special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md), which is also the Lie algebra of $O(3)$, then $e^{tX}\in O(3)$ near $t=0$. Differentiating

$$
e^{tX^{\mathsf T}}e^{tX}=I
$$

at zero gives $X^{\mathsf T}+X=0$. Conversely, the [matrix exponential](../../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) of every real [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) is orthogonal. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak o(3)=\{X\in M_3(\mathbb R):X^{\mathsf T}=-X\}.}
$$
