<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Differentiate $\operatorname{Ad}_{e^{tX}}Y=e^{tX}Ye^{-tX}$ at zero. This gives the [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

$$
\operatorname{ad}_X(Y)=[X,Y]=XY-YX.
$$

The [Jacobi identity](../../../../../../jacobi-identity.md) is exactly

$$
[\operatorname{ad}_X,\operatorname{ad}_Y]
=\operatorname{ad}_{[X,Y]},
$$

so this is a Lie-algebra representation on $\mathfrak o(3)$.
