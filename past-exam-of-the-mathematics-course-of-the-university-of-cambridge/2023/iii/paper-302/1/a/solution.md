<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The matrices are rotations about the third coordinate axis. Direct multiplication gives

$$
g(\theta)g(\varphi)=g(\theta+\varphi\bmod 2\pi),
\qquad g(0)=I,
\qquad g(\theta)^{-1}=g(-\theta),
$$

and $g(\theta)^{\mathsf T}g(\theta)=I$. Thus they form a [one-parameter subgroup](../../../../../../one-parameter-subgroup.md) of the [orthogonal group](../../../../../../orthogonal-group.md) $O(3)$, isomorphic to the [circle group](../../../../../../circle-group.md).
