<h1 id="3/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The highest weight $(0,1,0)$ gives the six-dimensional second [exterior power](../../../../../../../exterior-power.md) of the defining representation. Adding each unordered pair of the four defining weights from part i gives

$$
\begin{gathered}
(0,1,0),\qquad
(1,-1,1),\qquad
(1,0,-1),\\
(-1,0,1),\qquad
(-1,1,-1),\qquad
(0,-1,0).
\end{gathered}
$$

There are no multiplicities, in agreement with the assumption in the question.
