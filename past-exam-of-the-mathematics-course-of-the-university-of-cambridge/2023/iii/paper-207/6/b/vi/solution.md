↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The second derivative is

$$
\ell''(\theta)=-\frac{v_+}{\theta^2}.
$$

It is negative when $v_+>0$, verifying a strict maximum. The [Observed Fisher information](../../../../../../../observed-fisher-information.md) is $v_+/\theta^2$: more observed side-effects sharpen the likelihood, while the curvature vanishes when no side-effect is observed and the maximum then lies at the boundary $\widehat\theta=0$.
