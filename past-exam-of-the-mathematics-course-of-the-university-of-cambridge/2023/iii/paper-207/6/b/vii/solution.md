<h1 id="6/b/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

At $\theta_0$, the expected Fisher information is

$$
\mathcal I(\theta_0)=\frac{\mathbb E_{\theta_0,\phi_0}[v_+]}{\theta_0^2}.
$$

Requiring it to exceed $I_0$ directly controls the large-sample variance of the [maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md), approximately $\mathcal I(\theta_0)^{-1}$. Choose $I_0$ from the desired standard error, confidence-interval width, or power for clinically relevant alternatives, allowing for any planned significance level.
