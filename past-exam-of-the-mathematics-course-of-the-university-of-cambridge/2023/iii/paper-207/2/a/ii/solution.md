<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Writing $R=n_0/n_1$ gives $n_0=nR/(1+R)$ and $n_1=n/(1+R)$, so apart from the positive factor $1/n$ the variance is

$$
f(R)=(1+R)\left(\frac aR+b\right).
$$

Hence

$$
f'(R)=-\frac a{R^2}+b,
\qquad
f''(R)=\frac{2a}{R^3}>0.
$$

The unique minimum is

$$
R^*=\sqrt{\frac ab}
=\sqrt{\frac{p_0(1-p_1)}{p_1(1-p_0)}}.
$$

This is the [Neyman allocation](../../../../../../../neyman-allocation.md) for the log relative risk of failure.
