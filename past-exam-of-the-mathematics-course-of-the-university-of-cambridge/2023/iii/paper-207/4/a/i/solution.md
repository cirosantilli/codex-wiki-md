<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [risk set](../../../../../../../risk-set.md) at $x_i$ contains every individual still under observation and event-free immediately before $x_i$. Since the observed times are strictly ordered increasingly, these are individuals $i,i+1,\ldots,n$, so its size is

$$
\boxed{Y(x_i)=n-i+1.}
$$
