With a common hazard and the [Nelson–Aalen estimator](../../../../../../../nelson-aalen-estimator.md) from part a,

$$
\widehat M_i
=v_i-\widehat H(x_i)
=v_i-\sum_{j=1}^i\frac{v_j}{n-j+1}.
$$
