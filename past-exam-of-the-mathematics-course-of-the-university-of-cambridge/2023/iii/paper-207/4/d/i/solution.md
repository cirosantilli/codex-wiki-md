For the fitted [Cox proportional-hazards model](../../../../../../../cox-proportional-hazards-model.md),

$$
\widehat M_i
=v_i-e^{\widehat\beta z_i}\widehat H_0(x_i),
$$

where $\widehat H_0$ is the fitted baseline cumulative hazard, usually obtained with the [Breslow estimator](../../../../../../../breslow-estimator.md).
