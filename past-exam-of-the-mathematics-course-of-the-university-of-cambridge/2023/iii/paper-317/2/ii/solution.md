<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $P=K\rho^{1+1/n}$, introduce the [Lane-Emden equation](../../../../../../lane-emden-equation.md) variables

$$
\rho=\lambda\theta^n,
\qquad
r=\alpha\xi,
\qquad
\lambda=\rho_c,
$$



$$
\boxed{
\alpha^2=\frac{(n+1)K}{4\pi G}
\lambda^{1/n-1}}.
$$

Combining mass conservation with hydrostatic equilibrium gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac1{\xi^2}\frac d{d\xi}
\left(\xi^2\frac{d\theta}{d\xi}\right)
=-\theta^n}.
$$

Regularity and normalization impose

$$
\theta(0)=1,
\qquad
\theta'(0)=0.
$$

The stellar surface is the first positive zero $\xi_1$ of $\theta$. Integrating the equation once gives

$$
\boxed{
m(\xi)=-4\pi\alpha^3\lambda\xi^2\theta'(\xi)},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{
R=\alpha\xi_1,
\qquad
M=-4\pi\alpha^3\lambda\xi_1^2\theta'(\xi_1)}.
$$
