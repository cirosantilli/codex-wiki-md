<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

When $s=0$, part i gives the [polytropic index](../../../../../../polytropic-index.md) $n=3$. [Stellar homology](../../../../../../stellar-homology.md) gives

$$
\rho\sim\frac{M}{R^3},
\qquad
T\sim\beta\frac{\mu m_uGM}{k_BR},
$$

because gas pressure is the fraction $\beta$ of the pressure required by hydrostatic equilibrium. The [Kramers' opacity law](../../../../../../kramers-opacity-law.md) therefore scales as

$$
\kappa\propto\rho T^{-7/2}
\propto\beta^{-7/2}M^{-5/2}R^{1/2}.
$$

With constant $\eta$, the opacity relation from part i gives

$$
L\propto(1-\beta)\frac{M}{\kappa}
\propto(1-\beta)\beta^{7/2}M^{7/2}R^{-1/2}.
$$

For an $n=3$ polytrope, $M\propto(K/G)^{3/2}$. The explicit $K(\beta)$ of part i obeys

$$
K^3\propto\frac{1-\beta}{\beta^4},
$$

so the [Eddington quartic relation](../../../../../../eddington-quartic-relation.md) is

$$
\frac{1-\beta}{\beta^4}\propto M^2
$$

at fixed composition. Substitution gives

$$
\boxed{L\propto\beta^{15/2}M^{11/2}R^{-1/2}}.
$$
