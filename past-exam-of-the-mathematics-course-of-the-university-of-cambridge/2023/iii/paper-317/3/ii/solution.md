<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Equate the [accretion luminosity](../../../../../../accretion-luminosity.md) to the surface luminosity:

$$
\frac{GM\dot M}{R}\propto R^2T_{\rm eff}^4.
$$

Using $R\propto M^3T_{\rm eff}^{-24}$ from part i gives

$$
M^8\propto\dot M T_{\rm eff}^{68}.
$$

Substitution into $L\propto M^6T_{\rm eff}^{-44}$ yields the accreting [Hayashi track](../../../../../../hayashi-track.md)

$$
\boxed{L\propto\dot M^{3/4}T_{\rm eff}^{7}}.
$$
