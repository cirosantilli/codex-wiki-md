<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A fully convective monatomic perfect-gas star follows an $n=3/2$ [adiabatic stellar polytrope](../../../../../../adiabatic-stellar-polytrope.md). At the photosphere, $\tau=2/3$ and [hydrostatic equilibrium in optical depth](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium-in-optical-depth.md) gives

$$
P_{\rm ph}\sim\frac{g}{\kappa_{\rm ph}}
\propto\frac{M}{R^2}T_{\rm eff}^{-19/2}.
$$

For an ideal gas on an adiabat,

$$
K=\frac{P}{\rho^{5/3}}
\propto T^{5/3}P^{-2/3}.
$$

Evaluating this at the photosphere gives

$$
K\propto M^{-2/3}R^{4/3}T_{\rm eff}^{8}.
$$

The $n=3/2$ [polytropic mass-radius relation](../../../../../../polytropic-mass-radius-relation.md) is $R\propto KM^{-1/3}$, so

$$
R\propto M^3T_{\rm eff}^{-24}.
$$

Finally the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives

$$
\boxed{
L=4\pi R^2\sigma T_{\rm eff}^4
\propto M^6T_{\rm eff}^{-44}}.
$$

This is the specified [Hayashi track](../../../../../../hayashi-track.md).
