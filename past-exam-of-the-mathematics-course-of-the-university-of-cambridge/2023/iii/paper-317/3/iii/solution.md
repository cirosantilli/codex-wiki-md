<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The specific [deuterium-burning energy-generation rate](../../../../../../deuterium-burning-energy-generation-rate.md) is $\epsilon=\epsilon_0\rho T^{15}$. By [stellar homology](../../../../../../stellar-homology.md),

$$
\rho_c\sim\frac{M}{R^3},
\qquad
T_c\sim\frac{M}{R},
$$

so

$$
L_{\rm nuc}\sim\int\epsilon\,dm
\propto\rho_c^2T_c^{15}R^3
\propto\frac{M^{17}}{R^{18}}.
$$

Since $L_{\rm acc}\propto M\dot M/R$,

$$
\frac{L_{\rm nuc}}{L_{\rm acc}}
\propto\frac{M^{16}}{\dot M R^{17}}.
$$

Use $R\propto M^3T_{\rm eff}^{-24}$ and $T_{\rm eff}^{68}\propto M^8/\dot M$ from part ii. Then

$$
\boxed{
\frac{L_{\rm nuc}}{L_{\rm acc}}
\propto M^{13}\dot M^{-7}}.
$$

The two track relations also give

$$
M\propto L^{1/6}T_{\rm eff}^{22/3},
\qquad
\dot M\propto L^{4/3}T_{\rm eff}^{-28/3}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\frac{L_{\rm nuc}}{L_{\rm acc}}
\propto L^{-43/6}T_{\rm eff}^{964/6}.
$$

Equality occurs at

$$
\boxed{L_0\propto T_{\rm eff}^{964/43}}.
$$

The preceding formulae imply $L_{\rm nuc}>L_{\rm acc}$ for $L<L_0$, not $L>L_0$. Thus the inequality printed in the question is reversed relative to its requested intermediate scaling; the exponent $964/43$ is consistent.
