<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Define the [wave-action density](../../../../../../../wave-action-density.md)

$$
W=\frac{\partial\overline L^{(0)}}{\partial\omega}
=\frac12A^2\omega.
$$

The local dispersion relation implies

$$
c_g=\frac{\partial\omega}{\partial k}
=\frac{c^2k}{\omega},
$$

which is the [group velocity](../../../../../../../group-velocity.md). Moreover,

$$
\frac{\partial\overline L^{(0)}}{\partial k}
=-\frac12A^2c^2k=-c_gW.
$$

Substitution into the phase-variation equation gives the [wave-action conservation law](../../../../../../../wave-action-conservation-law.md)

$$
\boxed{
\partial_TW+\partial_X(c_gW)=0.}
$$

On the two nondispersive branches $\omega=\pm ck$, the group velocities are $c_g=\pm c$.
