<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $M_s$ is constant, the frictional acceleration instead scales as $r_o^{-2}$. The angular-momentum equation gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac{dr_o}{dt}=-K_c r_o^{-3/2},
\qquad
K_c=\frac{4GM_s\mathcal L}{3\sqrt C}}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
r_o(t)=\left[
r_{o0}^{5/2}-\frac52K_ct
\right]^{2/5}}.
$$

The orbit again reaches the centre in finite time, but its inward speed accelerates without the strong tidal-mass suppression present in part b.
