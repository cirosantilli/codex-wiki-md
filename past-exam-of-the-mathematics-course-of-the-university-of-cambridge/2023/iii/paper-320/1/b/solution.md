<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At fixed energy, the circular orbit has $L_c^2=GMa$, while

$$
L=L_c\sqrt{1-e^2}.
$$

An isotropic [galactic distribution function](../../../../../../galactic-distribution-function.md) has conditional angular-momentum density $n(L)\,dL\propto L\,dL$. Therefore

$$
n(e)\,de\propto L\left|\frac{dL}{de}\right|de
=L_c^2e\,de.
$$

Normalization on $0\leq e<1$ gives the [thermal eccentricity distribution](../../../../../../thermal-eccentricity-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{n(e)=2e}.
$$
