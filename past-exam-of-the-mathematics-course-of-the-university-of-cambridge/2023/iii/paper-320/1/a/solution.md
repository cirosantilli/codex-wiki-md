<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a bound [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md) about mass $M$, the specific orbital energy and specific angular momentum are

$$
\boxed{E=-\frac{GM}{2a}},
\qquad
\boxed{L^2=GMa(1-e^2)}.
$$

The apsidal radii are

$$
\boxed{r_-=a(1-e),
\qquad r_+=a(1+e)},
$$

equivalently $a=(r_-+r_+)/2$ and $e=(r_+-r_-)/(r_++r_-)$. Combining the formulas also gives

$$
\boxed{r_-r_+=a^2(1-e^2)=\frac{L^2a}{GM}
=-\frac{L^2}{2E}.}
$$
