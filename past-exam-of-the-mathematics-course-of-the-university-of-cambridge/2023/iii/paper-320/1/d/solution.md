<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Average the phase probability over the [thermal eccentricity distribution](../../../../../../thermal-eccentricity-distribution.md):

$$
f_{\rm unbound}
=\int_0^1 2e\left(\frac12-\frac e\pi\right)de.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
f_{\rm unbound}=\frac12-\frac{2}{3\pi}
\simeq0.288}.
$$
