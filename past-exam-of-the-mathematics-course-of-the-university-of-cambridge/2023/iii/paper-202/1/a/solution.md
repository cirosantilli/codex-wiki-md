A [semimartingale](../../../../../../semimartingale.md) is an [adapted process](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) of the form $X=X_0+M+A$, where $M$ is a [local martingale](../../../../../../local-martingale.md) and $A$ is an adapted [finite-variation process](../../../../../../finite-variation-process.md). A sequence of processes $X^n$ converges to $X$ in [uniform convergence on compacts in probability](../../../../../../uniform-convergence-on-compacts-in-probability.md), abbreviated ucp, when for every $T<\infty$ and $\delta>0$,

$$
\mathbb P\!\left(\sup_{0\leq t\leq T}|X_t^n-X_t|>\delta\right)\longrightarrow0.
$$
