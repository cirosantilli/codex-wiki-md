<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Each $A^\epsilon$ is continuous, adapted, and increasing. From ucp convergence one can choose a [subsequence](../../../../../../convergent-subsequence.md) that converges uniformly almost surely on every compact interval. Its limit $A$ is therefore also continuous and increasing, hence a [finite-variation process](../../../../../../finite-variation-process.md). The [stochastic integral](../../../../../../stochastic-integral.md) $M_t=\int_0^t\operatorname{sgn}(B_s)dB_s$ is a [continuous local martingale](../../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md), and part e gives the [semimartingale decomposition](../../../../../../semimartingale-decomposition.md)

$$
|B_t|=M_t+A_t.
$$

**Consequently $|B|$ is a semimartingale. In fact, comparison with the [Tanaka formula](../../../../../../tanaka-s-formula.md) identifies $A_t$ as the [local time of a semimartingale](../../../../../../local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md) $L_t^0(B)$.**
