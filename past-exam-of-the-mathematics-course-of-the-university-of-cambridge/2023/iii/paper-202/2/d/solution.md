<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

If $X:(\Omega\times[0,\infty),\mathcal P)\to(\mathbb R,\mathcal B(\mathbb R))$ is measurable and $f$ is [Borel measurable](../../../../../../borel-measurable-function.md), then $f\circ X$ is measurable by [composition of measurable functions](../../../../../../composition-of-measurable-functions.md). Hence applying a Borel function pointwise to a predictable process again gives a predictable process.
