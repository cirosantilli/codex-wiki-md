The [predictable sigma-algebra](../../../../../../predictable-sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal P$ on $\Omega\times[0,\infty)$ is generated by the sets $A\times\{0\}$ with $A\in\mathcal F_0$ and $A\times(s,t]$ with $0\leq s<t$ and $A\in\mathcal F_s$. A process is [previsible](../../../../../../predictable-process.md) precisely when it is $\mathcal P$-measurable.
