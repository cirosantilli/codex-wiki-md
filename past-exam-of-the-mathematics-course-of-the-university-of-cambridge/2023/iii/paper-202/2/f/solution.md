<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Let $Y$ be a nonconstant [Bernoulli random variable](../../../../../../bernoulli-distribution.md), let $\mathcal F_t$ be trivial for $t<1$, and let $\mathcal F_t=\sigma(Y)$ for $t\geq1$. This is a right-continuous filtration after completion. The process

$$
X_t=Y\mathbf1_{[1,\infty)}(t)
$$

is adapted and càdlàg. If it were predictable, its value $X_1=Y$ at the deterministic time $1$ would be measurable with respect to the [left-limit sigma-algebra](../../../../../../left-limit-sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal F_{1-}=\sigma(\bigcup_{t<1}\mathcal F_t)$, which is trivial. That contradicts the choice of $Y$, so $X$ is not predictable.
