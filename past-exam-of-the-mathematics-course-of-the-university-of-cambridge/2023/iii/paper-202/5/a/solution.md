<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The drift $v/2$ is continuously differentiable and hence [locally Lipschitz](../../../../../../locally-lipschitz-function.md) on the open interval $(0,1)$, while the diffusion coefficient is the constant one. The local [existence and pathwise uniqueness theorem for a stochastic differential equation](../../../../../../existence-and-pathwise-uniqueness-theorem-for-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md) therefore gives a unique strong solution up to its first exit from every compact subinterval. These solutions agree by [pathwise uniqueness](../../../../../../pathwise-uniqueness.md), producing a unique [maximal local solution of a stochastic differential equation](../../../../../../maximal-local-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md) whose lifetime is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T=\inf\{t\geq0:X_t\in\{0,1\}\}.}
$$
