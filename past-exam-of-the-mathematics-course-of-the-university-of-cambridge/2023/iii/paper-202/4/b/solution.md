<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $Z_t=e^{B_t+at}$, the [Itô formula](../../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md) gives

$$
dZ_t=Z_t\,dB_t+\left(a+\frac12\right)Z_t\,dt.
$$

Thus its local-martingale part is $M_t=\int_0^tZ_s,dB_s$, its finite-variation part is $(a+1/2)\int_0^tZ_sds$, and its [quadratic variation](../../../../../../quadratic-variation.md) clock is

$$
\boxed{[M]_t=\int_0^tZ_s^2ds.}
$$
