<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a local frame of $E$, a [connection on a vector bundle](../../../../../../connection-vector-bundle.md) has the form

$$
d^{\mathcal A}=d+A\wedge,
$$

where $A$ is a matrix of one-forms. Under a frame change $g$, its matrix transforms as

$$
A'=g^{-1}Ag+g^{-1}dg.
$$

The [covariant exterior derivative](../../../../../../exterior-covariant-derivative.md) on an $E$-valued $r$-form $\sigma$ is

$$
d^{\mathcal A}\sigma=d\sigma+A\wedge\sigma.
$$

The [curvature form of a connection](../../../../../../curvature-form.md) is

$$
F=dA+A\wedge A.
$$

Using the graded Leibniz rule,

$$
(d^{\mathcal A})^2\sigma
=d(A\wedge\sigma)+A\wedge d\sigma+A\wedge A\wedge\sigma
=(dA+A\wedge A)\wedge\sigma
=F\wedge\sigma.
$$
