<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose a coordinate ball $W$ around $q_1$ and a smaller convex coordinate ball $U$ whose closure lies in $W$. For $q_2\in U$, let $a$ be the constant coordinate vector from $q_1$ to $q_2$. Choose a [smooth bump function](../../../../../../smooth-bump-function.md) $\rho$ supported in $W$ and equal to one on $U$, and define in the chart

$$
w=\rho a,
$$

extending it by zero outside $W$. This is a compactly supported smooth vector field. The segment $q_1+ta$ stays in $U$, where $w=a$, so uniqueness for ordinary differential equations gives

$$
\Psi^t(q_1)=q_1+ta,\qquad 0\leq t\leq1.
$$

In particular, $\Psi^1(q_1)=q_2$.
