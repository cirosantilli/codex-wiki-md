<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the stated flat metric and orientation,

$$
*dx^i=(-1)^{i-1}
dx^1\wedge\cdots\wedge\widehat{dx^i}\wedge\cdots\wedge dx^n.
$$

Because the metric coefficients and the coordinate one-forms are constant, the [Hodge Laplacian](../../../../../../hodge-laplacian.md) acts coefficientwise:

$$
\Delta(\alpha_i\,dx^i)=(\Delta\alpha_i)\,dx^i.
$$

Thus a harmonic one-form $\alpha=\sum_i\alpha_i dx^i$ has harmonic coefficient functions. Every harmonic function on the compact connected torus is constant by the [maximum principle for harmonic functions](../../../../../../maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md). Hence

$$
\mathcal H^1(T^n)
=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{dx^1,\ldots,dx^n\}
\cong\mathbb R^n.
$$

The [Hodge decomposition theorem](../../../../../../hodge-decomposition-theorem.md) now gives

$$
\boxed{H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(T^n)\cong\mathbb R^n.}
$$
