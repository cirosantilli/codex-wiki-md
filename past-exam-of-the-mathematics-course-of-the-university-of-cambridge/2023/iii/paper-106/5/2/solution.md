<h1 id="5/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For hermitian $x$ with $\|x\|\leq1$, its spectrum lies in $[-1,1]$. Continuous functional calculus therefore shows that $1-x^2$ is positive. Let

$$
v=(1-x^2)^{1/2}.
$$

It commutes with $x$, and

$$
u=x+iv
$$

satisfies $u^*u=uu^*=x^2+v^2=1$. Thus $u$ is [unitary](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md) and

$$
\boxed{x=\frac12(u+u^*).}
$$
