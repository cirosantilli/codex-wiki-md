<h1 id="5/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Positivity and $\|x\|\leq t$ give $\sigma(x)\subset[0,t]$. Hence

$$
\sigma(t1-x)\subset[0,t].
$$

The element $t1-x$ is hermitian, so its norm equals its spectral radius and is at most $t$.
