↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Put $s=\|x\|+\|y\|$. Part 4 gives

$$
\|\|x\|1-x\|\leq\|x\|,\qquad
\|\|y\|1-y\|\leq\|y\|.
$$

Therefore

$$
\|s1-(x+y)\|
\leq\|\|x\|1-x\|+\|\|y\|1-y\|
\leq s.
$$

Part 3 now proves that $x+y$ is positive.
