<h1 id="5/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The hypothesis is possible only for $t\geq0$. For every $\lambda\in\sigma(x)\subset\mathbb R$,

$$
|t-\lambda|\leq\|t1-x\|\leq t,
$$

so $0\leq\lambda\leq2t$. The [spectral characterization of a positive element in a C\*-algebra](../../../../../../spectral-characterization-of-a-positive-element-in-a-c-star-algebra.md) gives $x\geq0$.
