↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [modular function](../../../../../../modular-function-split.md) of weight $k$ and level $\Gamma(1)$ is a [meromorphic function](../../../../../../meromorphic-function.md) $f:\mathfrak h\to\mathbb C$ satisfying

$$
f(\gamma\tau)=(c\tau+d)^k f(\tau)
$$

for every $\gamma=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\Gamma(1)$, and having a meromorphic Fourier expansion at the cusp infinity. Equivalently, $f|_k\gamma=f$ for the [slash operator for modular forms](../../../../../../slash-operator-for-modular-forms.md).

A [modular form](../../../../../../modular-form.md) is a modular function that is [holomorphic](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) on $\mathfrak h$ and [holomorphic at infinity](../../../../../../holomorphic-at-a-cusp.md). Its Fourier expansion therefore has the form

$$
f(\tau)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nq^n,
\qquad q=e^{2\pi i\tau}.
$$

Since $-I\in\Gamma(1)$, a nonzero level-one form must have even weight.
