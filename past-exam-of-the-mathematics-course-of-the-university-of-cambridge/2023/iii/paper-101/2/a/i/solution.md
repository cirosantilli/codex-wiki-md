A [ring extension](../../../../../../../ring-extension.md) $A\subseteq B$ is an [integral extension](../../../../../../../integral-extension.md) when every $b\in B$ is an [integral element](../../../../../../../integral-element.md) over $A$: there is a [monic polynomial](../../../../../../../monic-polynomial.md)

$$
b^d+a_{d-1}b^{d-1}+\cdots+a_0=0
$$

with all $a_i\in A$.
