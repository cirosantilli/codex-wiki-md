A [ring extension](../../../../../../../ring-extension.md) $A\subseteq B$ is [finite](../../../../../../../module-finite-ring-extension.md) when $B$ is a [finitely generated module](../../../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) over $A$. Every finite extension is integral by the [determinant trick](../../../../../../../determinant-trick.md).
