↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [prime ideal correspondence for localization](../../../../../../prime-ideal-correspondence-for-localization.md) identifies primes of $B_{\mathfrak p}$ with primes $\mathfrak q$ of $B$ disjoint from $A\setminus\mathfrak p$. Passing to the quotient by $\mathfrak pB_{\mathfrak p}$ retains exactly those containing $\mathfrak pB_{\mathfrak p}$. Thus the image is the [fiber of the map on spectra](../../../../../../fiber-of-the-map-on-spectra.md):

$$
\boxed{\left\{\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Spec}B:\mathfrak q\cap A=\mathfrak p\right\}.}
$$
