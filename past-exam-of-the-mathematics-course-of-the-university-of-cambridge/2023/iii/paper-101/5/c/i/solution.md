The [Poincare series of a graded module](../../../../../../../poincare-series-of-a-graded-module.md) is

$$
P_A(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}\dim_k(A_n)t^n.
$$

It is the [generating function](../../../../../../../poincare-series-of-a-graded-module.md) of the [Hilbert function](../../../../../../../hilbert-function.md) $n\mapsto\dim_kA_n$.
