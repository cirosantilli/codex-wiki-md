<h1 id="5/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The degree-$n$ component is

$$
(A\otimes_kB)_n=\bigoplus_{i+j=n}A_i\otimes_kB_j.
$$

Dimensions therefore satisfy the [Cauchy product](../../../../../../../cauchy-product.md) rule, giving

$$
\boxed{P_{A\otimes_kB}(t)=P_A(t)P_B(t).}
$$
