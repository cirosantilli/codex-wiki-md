<h1 id="5/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take $A=k[x]$ with $\deg x=2$. This is a Noetherian [graded algebra](../../../../../../../graded-algebra.md) with $A_0=k$, but

$$
\dim_kA_n=
\begin{cases}
1,&n\ \text{even},\\
0,&n\ \text{odd}.
\end{cases}
$$

**No polynomial can agree eventually with these alternating values, so $A$ has no Hilbert polynomial.**
