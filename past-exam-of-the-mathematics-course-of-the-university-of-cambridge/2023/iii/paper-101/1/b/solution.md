<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\mathfrak m$ be the [maximal ideal](../../../../../../maximal-ideal.md) and $k=A/\mathfrak m$ the finite [residue field](../../../../../../residue-field.md). The maximal ideal of an [Artinian local ring](../../../../../../artinian-local-ring.md) is [nilpotent](../../../../../../nilpotent-ideal.md), so $\mathfrak m^r=0$ for some $r$. Every quotient

$$
\mathfrak m^j/\mathfrak m^{j+1}
$$

is both an Artinian $A$-module and a [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over $k$. An Artinian vector space is finite-dimensional, hence each quotient is a [finite set](../../../../../../finite-set.md). The finite filtration

$$
A\supset\mathfrak m\supset\cdots\supset\mathfrak m^r=0
$$

therefore proves that the underlying set of $A$ is finite. This is the [Finiteness criterion for an Artinian local ring](../../../../../../finiteness-criterion-for-an-artinian-local-ring.md).
