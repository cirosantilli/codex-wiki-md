<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** The [integers](../../../../../../../integer.md) $\mathbb Z$ form a [Noetherian ring](../../../../../../../noetherian-ring.md), since every [ideal](../../../../../../../ideal.md) is [principal](../../../../../../../principal-ideal.md), but this ring is not an [Artinian ring](../../../../../../../artinian-ring.md): the strictly descending chain

$$
(2)\supsetneq(2^2)\supsetneq(2^3)\supsetneq\cdots
$$

never stabilizes.
