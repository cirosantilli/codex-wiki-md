↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** This is the [Artinian commutative ring is Noetherian theorem](../../../../../../../artinian-commutative-ring-is-noetherian-theorem.md). One proof uses the nilpotent [nilradical](../../../../../../../nilradical.md) $N$ of an [Artinian ring](../../../../../../../artinian-ring.md) $A$. The quotient $A/N$ is a finite product of [fields](../../../../../../../field.md). Each quotient $N^j/N^{j+1}$ is an Artinian module over the [semisimple ring](../../../../../../../semisimple-ring.md) $A/N$, hence has [finite length](../../../../../../../length-of-a-module.md) and is [Noetherian](../../../../../../../noetherian-module.md). The finite filtration

$$
A\supseteq N\supseteq\cdots\supseteq N^r=0
$$

then makes $A$ a Noetherian module over itself, which is exactly the [ascending chain condition](../../../../../../../ascending-chain-condition.md) on its ideals.
