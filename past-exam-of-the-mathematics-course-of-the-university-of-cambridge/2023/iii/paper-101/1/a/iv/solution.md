↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**False.** For a [prime number](../../../../../../../prime-number.md) $p$, the [Prüfer p-group](../../../../../../../prufer-group.md) $C_{p^\infty}$ is an Artinian $\mathbb Z$-module: every proper subgroup is a finite [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md), so no infinite strictly descending chain of subgroups exists. It is not Noetherian because its cyclic subgroups form the strict ascending chain

$$
\boxed{C_p\subsetneq C_{p^2}\subsetneq C_{p^3}\subsetneq\cdots.}
$$
