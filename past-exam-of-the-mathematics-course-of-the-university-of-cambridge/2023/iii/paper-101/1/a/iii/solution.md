<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**False.** The [module over a ring](../../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) $\mathbb Z$ over itself is [Noetherian](../../../../../../../noetherian-module.md), because its submodules are the [principal ideals](../../../../../../../principal-ideal.md) $n\mathbb Z$, but the descending chain

$$
2\mathbb Z\supsetneq2^2\mathbb Z\supsetneq2^3\mathbb Z\supsetneq\cdots
$$

shows that it is not an [Artinian module](../../../../../../../artinian-module.md).
