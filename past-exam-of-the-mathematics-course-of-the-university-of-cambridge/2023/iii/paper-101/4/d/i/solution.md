<h1 id="4/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $A$ be a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) that is not a field. A PID is [Noetherian](../../../../../../../noetherian-ring.md) and is a [unique factorization domain](../../../../../../../unique-factorization-domain.md). Every nonzero prime ideal is generated by a prime element. If

$$
(p)\subseteq(a)\subsetneq A,
$$

then $a\mid p$, so primality of $p$ makes $a$ a unit or an associate of $p$. The proper alternative is $(a)=(p)$, and every nonzero prime is therefore maximal. Since $A$ has a nonzero prime ideal, its [Krull dimension](../../../../../../../krull-dimension.md) is exactly one.
