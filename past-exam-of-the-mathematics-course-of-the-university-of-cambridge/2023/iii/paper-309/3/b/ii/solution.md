↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Stationarity changes $\Box$ to the spatial [Laplace operator](../../../../../../../laplace-operator.md). The Green function of the [Poisson equation](../../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\bar h_{00}(\mathbf r)=4\int_{\mathbb R^3}
\frac{\rho(\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d^3r'},
$$



$$
\boxed{\bar h_{0i}(\mathbf r)=4\Omega\int_{\mathbb R^3}
\frac{\rho(\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}
(y',-x',0)_i\,d^3r'},
\qquad
\boxed{\bar h_{ij}=0}.
$$

Here the bar denotes the [trace-reversed metric perturbation](../../../../../../../trace-reversed-metric-perturbation.md); this is the variable denoted by $h$ in the displayed field equation of the question.
