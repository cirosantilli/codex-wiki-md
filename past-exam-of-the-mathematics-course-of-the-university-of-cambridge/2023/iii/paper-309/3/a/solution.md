<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}+\epsilon h_{\mu\nu}$. The [linearized inverse metric](../../../../../../linearized-inverse-metric.md) is $g^{\mu\nu}=\eta^{\mu\nu}-\epsilon h^{\mu\nu}$, and all quadratic Christoffel products are $O(\epsilon^2)$. The wave-coordinate condition linearizes to

$$
\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0,
\qquad
\bar h_{\mu\nu}=h_{\mu\nu}-\frac12\eta_{\mu\nu}h.
$$

Using the supplied Ricci formula then gives $G^{(1)}_{\mu\nu}=-\tfrac12\Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}$. Consequently the [Linearized Einstein equations](../../../../../../linearized-einstein-equations.md) in [Lorenz gauge in linearized gravity](../../../../../../lorenz-gauge-in-linearized-gravity.md) are

$$
\boxed{\Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}=-16\pi T_{\mu\nu}},
\qquad
\boxed{\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0}.
$$

This Lorenz condition is the first-order form of the [harmonic coordinate](../../../../../../harmonic-coordinate.md) equations.
