<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The vector $T^a$ is the tangent to a reference member of a one-parameter family of affinely parametrized [geodesics](../../../../../../geodesic.md). The [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) $Y^a$ is the infinitesimal connecting vector from that geodesic to a neighboring one at equal parameter. The [geodesic deviation](../../../../../../geodesic-deviation.md) equation states that curvature determines their relative acceleration.
