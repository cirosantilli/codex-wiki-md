<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Contract the isotropic-curvature formula on $a,c$. In dimension $n$ it gives

$$
R_{bd}=(n-1)K g_{bd},
\qquad
R=n(n-1)K.
$$

Substitution into the [contracted Bianchi identity](../../../../../../contracted-bianchi-identity.md) gives

$$
(n-1)\nabla_bK=\frac12n(n-1)\nabla_bK.
$$

Since $n>2$, $\nabla_bK=0$, so $K$ is constant on each connected component. Thus this is [constant sectional curvature](../../../../../../constant-sectional-curvature.md), with

$$
\boxed{K=\frac{R}{n(n-1)}}.
$$

This argument is [Schur theorem in pseudo-Riemannian geometry](../../../../../../schur-theorem-in-pseudo-riemannian-geometry.md).
