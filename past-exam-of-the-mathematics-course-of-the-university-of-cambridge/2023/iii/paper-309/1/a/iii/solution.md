<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At an arbitrary point choose [normal coordinates](../../../../../../../normal-coordinates.md), so the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) vanish there. Torsion freedom and commutation of partial derivatives then give the algebraic [first Bianchi identity](../../../../../../../first-bianchi-identity.md)

$$
\boxed{R^a{}_{[bcd]}=0}.
$$

Differentiate the coordinate curvature expression and cyclically antisymmetrize. Third derivatives cancel, giving the [second Bianchi identity](../../../../../../../second-bianchi-identity.md)

$$
\boxed{\nabla_{[e}R^a{}_{|b|cd]}=0}.
$$

Both statements are tensorial and hence hold in every coordinate system. Contracting the differential identity, using the curvature symmetries and [metric compatibility](../../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md), yields

$$
\boxed{\nabla_aR^a{}_b-\frac12\nabla_bR=0},
$$

the [contracted Bianchi identity](../../../../../../../contracted-bianchi-identity.md).
