<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fix the convention

$$
(\nabla_c\nabla_d-\nabla_d\nabla_c)X^a=R^a{}_{bcd}X^b.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
R(U,V)X=\nabla_U\nabla_VX-\nabla_V\nabla_UX-\nabla_{[U,V]}X.
$$

This defines the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) because it is $C^\infty(M)$-linear in $U,V,X$: derivatives of a multiplying function cancel between the three terms. Thus its value at a point depends only on the three tangent vectors there, rather than their extensions.
