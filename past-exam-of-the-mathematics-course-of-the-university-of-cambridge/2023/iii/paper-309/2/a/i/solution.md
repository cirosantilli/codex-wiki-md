<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Vary $g^{ac}g_{cb}=\delta^a_b$. The [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
(\delta g^{ac})g_{cb}+g^{ac}\delta g_{cb}=0,
$$

and multiplication by $g^{bd}$ yields the [metric variation](../../../../../../../metric-variation.md)

$$
\boxed{\delta g^{ab}=-g^{ac}g^{bd}\delta g_{cd}}.
$$
