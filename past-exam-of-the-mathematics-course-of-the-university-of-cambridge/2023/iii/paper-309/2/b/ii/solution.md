<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using both metric-variation formulas from part a gives the [Klein-Gordon scalar stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../../klein-gordon-scalar-stress-energy-tensor.md)

$$
\boxed{T^{ab}=\nabla^a\psi\nabla^b\psi
-\frac12g^{ab}\left(\nabla_c\psi\nabla^c\psi+\mu^2\psi^2\right)}.
$$

Its divergence is

$$
\nabla_aT^{ab}
=(\nabla_a\nabla^a\psi-\mu^2\psi)\nabla^b\psi,
$$

because the two Hessian terms cancel by symmetry. It vanishes on every solution of the [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md).
