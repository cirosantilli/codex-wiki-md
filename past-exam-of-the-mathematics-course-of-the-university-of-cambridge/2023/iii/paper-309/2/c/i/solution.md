The coordinate formula for the [Lie derivative](../../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) is

$$
(\mathcal L_Xg)_{ab}=X^c\partial_cg_{ab}
+g_{cb}\partial_aX^c+g_{ac}\partial_bX^c.
$$

For $X=\partial_t$, its components are constant and every metric coefficient is independent of $t$, so $\mathcal L_{\partial_t}g=0$. Hence $\partial_t$ is a timelike [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) and the metric is a [static spacetime](../../../../../../../static-spacetime.md).
