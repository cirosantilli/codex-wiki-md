<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the orthonormal frame, the comoving [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) has $T_{00}=\rho$ and $T_{ij}=p\delta_{ij}$. The [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) with [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) therefore give

$$
\frac34-\Lambda=8\pi\rho,
\qquad
-\frac14+\Lambda=8\pi p.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\rho=\frac{3/4-\Lambda}{8\pi},
\qquad p=\frac{\Lambda-1/4}{8\pi}}.
$$

Choosing

$$
\boxed{\Lambda=\frac14}
$$

gives $p=0$ and $\rho=1/(16\pi)>0$. The metric is then the [Einstein static universe](../../../../../../einstein-static-universe.md) supported by pressureless matter and positive vacuum energy.
