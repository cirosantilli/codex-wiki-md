<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Contract $\nabla_aT^{ab}=0$ for the [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) with $u_b$. Using $u_bu^b=-1$ and $u_b\nabla_au^b=0$ gives the [relativistic perfect-fluid energy equation](../../../../../../relativistic-perfect-fluid-energy-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{u^a\nabla_a\rho+(\rho+p)\nabla_au^a=0},
$$

the local [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md). Project instead with $g^a{}_b+u^au_b$ to obtain the [relativistic Euler equation](../../../../../../relativistic-euler-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{(\rho+p)u^b\nabla_bu^a
=-(g^{ab}+u^au^b)\nabla_bp}.
$$
