<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [monotone graph property](../../../../../../monotone-graph-property.md) $P=(P_n)$, a function $p^*(n)$ is a [threshold function for a monotone graph property](../../../../../../threshold-function-for-a-monotone-graph-property.md) when

$$
p/p^*\longrightarrow0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathbb P(G(n,p)\in P_n)\longrightarrow0,
$$

and

$$
p/p^*\longrightarrow\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathbb P(G(n,p)\in P_n)\longrightarrow1.
$$
