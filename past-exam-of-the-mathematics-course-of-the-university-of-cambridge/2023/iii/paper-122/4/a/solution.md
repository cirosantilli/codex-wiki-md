<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For disjoint nonempty vertex sets $A,B$, write

$$
d(A,B)=\frac{e(A,B)}{|A||B|}.
$$

The pair $(A,B)$ is [$\varepsilon$-uniform](../../../../../../regular-pair-of-vertex-sets.md) if

$$
|d(X,Y)-d(A,B)|\leq\varepsilon
$$

whenever $X\subseteq A$, $Y\subseteq B$, $|X|\geq\varepsilon|A|$, and $|Y|\geq\varepsilon|B|$.

The [Szemerédi regularity lemma](../../../../../../szemeredi-regularity-lemma.md) states that for every $\varepsilon>0$ and integer $m_0$ there are $M,n_0$ such that every graph on $n\geq n_0$ vertices has a partition

$$
V=V_0\sqcup V_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup V_m
$$

with $m_0\leq m\leq M$, $|V_0|\leq\varepsilon n$, equal sizes $|V_1|=\cdots=|V_m|$, and at most $\varepsilon m^2$ pairs $(V_i,V_j)$ that are not $\varepsilon$-uniform.
