<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every term in $\widetilde S$ is proportional to $N$. The [Large-N expansion](../../../../../../large-n-expansion.md) therefore suppresses fluctuations of the auxiliary field by powers of $1/N$, and the leading partition function comes from a [saddle-point approximation](../../../../../../saddle-point-approximation.md).

Vary with respect to $i\lambda$ and use $\delta\operatorname{Tr}\log K=\operatorname{Tr}(K^{-1}\delta K)$. At a translation-invariant saddle $i\lambda=m^2$, the [gap equation](../../../../../../gap-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{
\frac1{vg}
=T\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}
\int\frac{d^2k}{(2\pi)^2}
\frac1{\omega_n^2+v^2k^2+m^2},
\qquad \omega_n=2\pi nT.}
$$

The frequencies are the bosonic [Matsubara frequencies](../../../../../../matsubara-frequency.md) imposed by periodicity around the imaginary-time thermal circle.
