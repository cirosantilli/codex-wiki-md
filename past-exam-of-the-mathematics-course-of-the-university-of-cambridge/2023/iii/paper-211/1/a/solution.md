A portfolio $H\in\mathbb R^n$ is an [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md) when $H\cdot P_0\leq0$, $H\cdot P_1\geq0$ almost surely, and at least one inequality supplies a strict gain: either $H\cdot P_0<0$ or $\mathbb P(H\cdot P_1>0)>0$. It is a [terminal-consumption arbitrage](../../../../../../terminal-consumption-arbitrage.md) when $H\cdot P_0=0$, $H\cdot P_1\geq0$ almost surely, and the terminal inequality is strict with positive probability.
