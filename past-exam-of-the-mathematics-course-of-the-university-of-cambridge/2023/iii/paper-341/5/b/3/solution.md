The [Butcher tableau](../../../../../../../butcher-tableau.md) has one stage with $a_{11}=1/2$ and $b_1=1$, so it is the [implicit midpoint rule](../../../../../../../implicit-midpoint-rule.md)

$$
y_{n+1}=y_n+h f\left(\frac{y_n+y_{n+1}}2\right).
$$

Again, interchanging the endpoints and changing $h$ to $-h$ leaves the equation invariant. Hence $\boxed{\text{method 3 is time symmetric}}$.
