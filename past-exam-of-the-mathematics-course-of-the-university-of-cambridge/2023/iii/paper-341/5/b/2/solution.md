The [trapezoidal rule](../../../../../../../trapezoidal-rule.md) is

$$
y_{n+1}-y_n=\frac h2(f(y_n)+f(y_{n+1})).
$$

Interchanging $y_n$ and $y_{n+1}$ while replacing $h$ by $-h$ leaves the equation unchanged. Its backward step is therefore exactly its inverse, and $\boxed{\text{method 2 is time symmetric}}$.
