A process $(X_t)_{t\in\mathbb Z}$ is [weakly stationary](../../../../../../weakly-stationary-process.md) when it has finite second moments, a time-independent mean $\mathbb E X_t=\mu$, and an [autocovariance function](../../../../../../autocovariance.md)

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X_t,X_s)=\gamma(t-s)
$$

that depends only on the lag.
