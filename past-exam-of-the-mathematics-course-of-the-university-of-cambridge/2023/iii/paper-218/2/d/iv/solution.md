↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Part iii and the stated characterization imply that $U$ has a [moving-average process of order one](../../../../../../../moving-average-process-of-order-one.md) representation $U_t=\eta_t+\theta\eta_{t-1}$. Since

$$
(1-\phi B)Y_t=U_t,
$$

where $B$ is the [backshift operator](../../../../../../../backshift-operator.md),

$$
(1-\phi B)Y_t=(1+\theta B)\eta_t.
$$

**Thus $Y$ is a causal [autoregressive moving-average process](../../../../../../../autoregressive-moving-average-model.md) of order $(1,1)$.**
