↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $\phi(x)=x$ and centered data, the objective is the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md)

$$
u^TSu,
\qquad
S=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nX_iX_i^T,
\qquad \lVert u\rVert_2=1.
$$

This is the first-direction optimization in [principal component analysis](../../../../../../../principal-component-analysis.md). Therefore $\widehat u$ is any unit [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of the sample [covariance matrix](../../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) $S$ corresponding to its largest [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
