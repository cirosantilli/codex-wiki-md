<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Because models 1 and 3 are full likelihood models for the same response, one can compare their [Akaike information criterion](../../../../../../akaike-information-criterion.md) values; that favors model 1. One can also compare held-out count prediction by [K-fold cross-validation](../../../../../../k-fold-cross-validation.md), using a common loss such as Poisson deviance or negative log predictive density.

The AIC comparison cannot include model 2 because a Quasi-Poisson fit specifies only mean and variance and has no full likelihood. Cross-validation can compare model 2 with model 3 if all predictions are scored by the same proper out-of-sample loss.
