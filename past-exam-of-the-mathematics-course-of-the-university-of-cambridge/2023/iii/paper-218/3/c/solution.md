<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a likelihood $L(\theta)$ with $k$ estimated parameters, the [Akaike information criterion](../../../../../../akaike-information-criterion.md) is

$$
\operatorname{AIC}=-2\log L(\widehat\theta)+2k,
$$

where $\widehat\theta$ is the [maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md). Among likelihoods for the same observed response and reference measure, smaller AIC estimates smaller expected out-of-sample [Kullback-Leibler divergence](../../../../../../kullback-leibler-divergence.md) up to a model-independent constant.
