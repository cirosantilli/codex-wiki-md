<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The functional

$$
\ell(\varphi)=-\int_\Omega f\varphi
$$

is bounded on $H_0^1(\Omega)$ by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and the Poincare inequality:

$$
|\ell(\varphi)|\leq C\|f\|_{L^2}\|\nabla\varphi\|_{L^2}.
$$

The [Riesz representation theorem](../../../../../../riesz-representation-theorem.md) therefore supplies a unique $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ satisfying

$$
(u,\varphi)_{H_0^1}=\ell(\varphi)
$$

for every test function. This is exactly the weak identity from part (a). Uniqueness also follows by testing the homogeneous difference with itself.
