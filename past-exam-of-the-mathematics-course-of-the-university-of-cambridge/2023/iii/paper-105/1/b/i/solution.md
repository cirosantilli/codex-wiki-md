↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [wave operator](../../../../../../../d-alembert-operator.md) has principal symbol

$$
p(\tau,\xi)=-\tau^2+|\xi|^2.
$$

The conormal to the initial plane is $dt$, and $p(dt)=-1\ne0$. The plane is therefore non-characteristic.
