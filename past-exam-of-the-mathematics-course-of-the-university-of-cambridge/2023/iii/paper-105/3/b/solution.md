<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Strong Huygens principle](../../../../../../strong-huygens-principle.md) in three spatial dimensions says that the solution at $(t,x)$ depends only on the initial data on the sphere

$$
\{y:|y-x|=|t|\},
$$

rather than on the full ball bounded by that sphere. This follows immediately from the Kirchhoff formula and its time derivative. Consequently a disturbance has no tail inside the light cone: if the initial data are supported in a compact set $K$, then $u(t,x)=0$ whenever that sphere misses $K$. This is sharper than [finite propagation speed](../../../../../../finite-propagation-speed.md), which only excludes influence from outside the ball.
