<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a [strongly minimal theory](../../../../../../strongly-minimal-theory.md), [model-theoretic algebraic closure](../../../../../../model-theoretic-algebraic-closure.md) is a [pregeometry](../../../../../../pregeometry.md). If $C$ is algebraically closed, every element outside $C$ realizes the [generic type in a strongly minimal theory](../../../../../../generic-type-in-a-strongly-minimal-theory.md): every one-variable definable set is finite or cofinite, and a point outside $C$ belongs to none of the finite ones.

Enumerate a finite tuple $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ of distinct elements of the independent set $A$. Independence says

$$
a_i\notin\operatorname{acl}(a_1,\ldots,a_{i-1}).
$$

Its image tuple under the bijection has the same property. Inductively, $a_i$ and $f(a_i)$ realize the same generic type over the algebraic closures of the preceding tuples, so every finite restriction of $f$ is elementary. First-order formulas involve only finitely many parameters, hence the entire bijection is an elementary map. This is the [independent set in a strongly minimal theory](../../../../../../independent-set-in-a-strongly-minimal-theory.md) principle.
