<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $g:A\to B$ be a finite partial elementary map in $M$. In a countable language, the algebraic closures of finite sets are countable. An alternating back-and-forth construction extends $g$ to an isomorphism

$$
\operatorname{acl}(A)\cap M
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{acl}(B)\cap M.
$$

At each step, an element algebraic over the current domain has a finite algebraic type, and elementarity provides a matching realization on the other side.

Choose a [basis of a pregeometry](../../../../../../basis-of-a-pregeometry.md) $C$ over the first closed set and a [basis of a pregeometry](../../../../../../basis-of-a-pregeometry.md) $D$ over the second. The [dimension of a pregeometry](../../../../../../dimension-of-a-pregeometry.md) is the same for the two extensions: the isomorphism preserves the finite ranks already contributed by $A$ and $B$, while both sides have the same ambient dimension. Choose a bijection $C\to D$. Part (a) and uniqueness of the [generic type in a strongly minimal theory](../../../../../../generic-type-in-a-strongly-minimal-theory.md) make the enlarged map elementary. Since an elementary submodel is algebraically closed in the monster,

$$
M=\operatorname{acl}(A\cup C)=\operatorname{acl}(B\cup D),
$$

and the map extends over algebraic closure to an automorphism of $M$. Thus every finite partial elementary map extends to an automorphism: every model is homogeneous, as stated in [homogeneity of models of a countable strongly minimal theory](../../../../../../homogeneity-of-models-of-a-countable-strongly-minimal-theory.md).
