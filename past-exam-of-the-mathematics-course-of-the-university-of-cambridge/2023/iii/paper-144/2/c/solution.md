↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [model-theoretic algebraic closure](../../../../../../model-theoretic-algebraic-closure.md) is

$$
\operatorname{acl}(A)=
\{c:\text{$c$ belongs to a finite set definable over $A$}\}.
$$

Equivalently, it consists of elements with finite orbit under $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal U/A)$. The [model-theoretic definable closure](../../../../../../model-theoretic-definable-closure.md) is

$$
\operatorname{dcl}(A)=
\{c:\text{$c$ is uniquely definable over $A$}\},
$$

equivalently the set fixed pointwise by every automorphism fixing $A$. Consequently $\operatorname{dcl}(A)\subseteq\operatorname{acl}(A)$.
