<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $p:A\to\mathcal U$ be a small [partial embedding](../../../../../../partial-embedding.md). To add a vertex $c$, prescribe for its image adjacency to $p(a)$ exactly when $c$ is adjacent to $a$, together with inequalities excluding the existing image. Every finite part of this prescription is realized by the extension axioms of the [theory of the random graph](../../../../../../theory-of-the-random-graph.md), and saturation realizes the whole type. The same argument applies in the reverse direction. A transfinite [back-and-forth method](../../../../../../back-and-forth-method.md) therefore extends $p$ to an automorphism of $\mathcal U$. Automorphisms preserve every first-order formula, so $p$ is elementary.
