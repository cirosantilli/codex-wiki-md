↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $\kappa=|N|$, [saturation](../../../../../../homogeneity-plus-realization-of-empty-set-types-implies-saturation.md) means that every complete type over a subset of $N$ of cardinality less than $\kappa$ is realized in $N$. The model $N$ is a [universal model](../../../../../../universal-model.md) when every model of $T$ of cardinality less than $\kappa$ elementarily embeds into $N$, and it is a [homogeneous model](../../../../../../homogeneous-model.md) when every partial elementary map between subsets of $N$ of cardinality less than $\kappa$ extends to an automorphism of $N$.
