↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The corresponding rotation representations are

$$
\boxed{
E_0:j=0,
\qquad
E_1:j=2,
\qquad
E_2:j=2\ \text{and}\ 3.}
$$

The would-be $j=1$ representation at $E_2$ is absent because it is the null descendant expressing conservation of the stress tensor.
