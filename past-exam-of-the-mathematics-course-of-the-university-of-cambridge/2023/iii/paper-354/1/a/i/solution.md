<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By the [state–operator correspondence](../../../../../../../state-operator-correspondence.md), a CFT operator of [scaling dimension](../../../../../../../scaling-dimension.md) $\Delta$ creates a cylinder state of energy $E=\Delta/R$. The identity gives the vacuum; the first nontrivial low-energy single-trace operator is the conserved [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md), with $\Delta=3$ in three dimensions; and one translation raises the dimension by one. Taking the scheme-dependent vacuum energy to vanish,

$$
\boxed{E_0=0,
\qquad E_1=\frac3R,
\qquad E_2=\frac4R.}
$$

All other single-trace primaries are heavy by assumption, while the first two-graviton state begins at $6/R$.
