↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [AdS/CFT correspondence](../../../../../../../ads-cft-correspondence.md) maps the vacuum to empty anti-de Sitter spacetime and the stress-tensor conformal family to one-graviton normal modes. Therefore

$$
\boxed{n_g(E_0)=0,
\qquad n_g(E_1)=1,
\qquad n_g(E_2)=1.}
$$

The distinction between $E_1$ and $E_2$ is a different excitation within the same one-particle conformal family, not an additional graviton.
