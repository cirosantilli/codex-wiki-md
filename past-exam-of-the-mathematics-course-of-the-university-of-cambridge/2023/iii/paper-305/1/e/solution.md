<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For emission of a soft photon of momentum $q$ from external charged particles, the [Soft photon theorem](../../../../../../soft-photon-theorem.md) gives

$$
\mathcal M^\mu(q)
=\left(\sum_i\eta_i Q_i\frac{p_i^\mu}{p_i\mathbin\cdot q}\right)\mathcal M_0+O(q^0),
$$

where $\eta_i=+1$ for outgoing and $-1$ for incoming particles. Contracting with $q_\mu$ and applying the Ward identity gives

$$
\left(\sum_i\eta_iQ_i\right)\mathcal M_0=0.
$$

For a nonzero underlying amplitude, total outgoing charge equals total incoming charge. Thus photon gauge redundancy enforces [conservation of electric charge](../../../../../../charge-conservation.md).

The analogous [Soft graviton theorem](../../../../../../soft-graviton-theorem.md) for helicity two requires universal coupling to energy-momentum and yields conservation of total four-momentum, the seed of the [Equivalence principle](../../../../../../equivalence-principle.md). For massless helicity greater than two, the corresponding soft consistency conditions demand conserved higher-rank momentum charges that generic interacting scattering cannot satisfy. Under the assumptions of a Lorentz-invariant local S-matrix with long-range interactions, such particles therefore have no nontrivial coupling; this is the soft-theorem obstruction to interacting massless higher-spin particles.
