<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With the convention that the fields have charges $q_\Phi=2$ and $q_\Psi=1$, the [Noether current](../../../../../../noether-current.md) may be written

$$
J^\mu
=2i\left(\Phi^*\partial^\mu\Phi-\Phi\partial^\mu\Phi^*\right)
+\overline\Psi\gamma^\mu\Psi,
$$

up to an overall sign convention for the generator. The field equations, including the invariant Yukawa interaction, give $\partial_\mu J^\mu=0$. Subject to vanishing flux at spatial infinity, the [Noether charge](../../../../../../noether-charge.md)

$$
Q=\int d^3x\,J^0
$$

is conserved and generates the global $U(1)$ transformations.
