<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At energies well below $\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}$, collect the pion fields into

$$
U(x)=\exp\left(\frac{i\pi^a(x)\tau^a}{f_\pi}\right),
\qquad
U\mapsto LUR^\dagger.
$$

The leading [chiral perturbation theory](../../../../../../../chiral-perturbation-theory.md) Lagrangian is

$$
\mathcal L_\chi
=\frac{f_\pi^2}{4}\operatorname{Tr}
(\partial_\mu U^\dagger\partial^\mu U)
+\frac{f_\pi^2B_0}{2}
\operatorname{Tr}(MU^\dagger+UM^\dagger)
+\cdots,
$$

where $M=\operatorname{diag}(m_u,m_d)$ is treated as a symmetry-breaking spurion. The derivative and quark-mass expansion organizes the omitted terms.
