<h1 id="4/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Exact isospin symmetry would make the proton and neutron degenerate. Their observed mass difference is generated by explicit isospin breaking from $m_d-m_u$ together with electromagnetic effects. The approximate symmetry explains why the splitting is small compared with the nucleon mass, while [chiral perturbation theory](../../../../../../../chiral-perturbation-theory.md) parametrizes it through symmetry-breaking operators and low-energy constants; symmetry alone does not predict its numerical value.
