<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let

$$
L=\log\frac{M_{\mathrm{GUT}}}{M_Z}.
$$

Running each coupling down from the common value $\alpha_G$ gives

$$
\frac1{\alpha_i(M_Z)}
=\frac1{\alpha_G}-\frac{b_i}{2\pi}L.
$$

Subtracting the $i=1,2$ equations determines $L$, and eliminating it from the $i=3,2$ difference gives

$$
\frac1{\alpha_3(M_Z)}
=\frac1{\alpha_2(M_Z)}
+\frac{b_3-b_2}{b_1-b_2}
\left[
\frac1{\alpha_1(M_Z)}-\frac1{\alpha_2(M_Z)}
\right],
$$

which is the claimed [gauge coupling unification](../../../../../../../gauge-coupling-unification.md) relation.
