<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $b>0$, the theory is [asymptotically free](../../../../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md): it is weakly coupled at high energies and strongly coupled in the infrared. For $b<0$, the coupling decreases toward the infrared but grows toward a finite ultraviolet [Landau pole](../../../../../../../landau-pole.md); such a theory is infrared free and requires an ultraviolet completion before that pole.
