<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Use [singular value decomposition](../../../../../../singular-value-decomposition.md) to choose unitary matrices satisfying

$$
U_{uL}^\dagger M_uU_{uR}=\operatorname{diag}(m_u,m_c,m_t),
\qquad
U_{dL}^\dagger M_dU_{dR}=\operatorname{diag}(m_d,m_s,m_b).
$$

The neutral currents remain flavour diagonal because the same unitary matrix occurs on both sides of each bilinear. The charged current contains the mismatch

$$
V_{\mathrm{CKM}}=U_{uL}^\dagger U_{dL},
$$

and becomes

$$
\mathcal L_W=\frac g{\sqrt2}\left[
W_\mu^+\overline u_{iL}\gamma^\mu(V_{\mathrm{CKM}})_{ij}d_{jL}
+W_\mu^-\overline d_{iL}\gamma^\mu(V_{\mathrm{CKM}}^\dagger)_{ij}u_{jL}
\right].
$$

A general unitary $3$ by $3$ matrix has nine real parameters. Independent rephasings of the six quark fields remove five phases because one common baryon-number phase changes nothing. The [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) therefore has four physical parameters: three mixing angles and one [CP-violating phase](../../../../../../cp-violating-phase.md).
