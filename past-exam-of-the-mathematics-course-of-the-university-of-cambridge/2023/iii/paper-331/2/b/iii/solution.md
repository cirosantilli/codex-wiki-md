↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The linear normal-mode problem is

$$
\lambda\mathbf u=-\nabla p+sigma\operatorname{Ra}\theta\widehat{\mathbf z}
+\sigma\nabla^2\mathbf u,
\qquad
\lambda\theta=w+\nabla^2\theta,
\qquad
\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf u=0.
$$

At $\lambda=0$ it is exactly the variational Euler--Lagrange system from part ii. Therefore the [energy-stability threshold](../../../../../../../energy-stability-threshold.md) coincides with the stated linear neutral threshold $\operatorname{Ra}_{\rm crit}$. Below it the quadratic production functional is negative for every nonzero perturbation, so there is no transient energy growth; at the threshold a neutral perturbation exists, and above it the leading real eigenvalue produces exponential growth.
