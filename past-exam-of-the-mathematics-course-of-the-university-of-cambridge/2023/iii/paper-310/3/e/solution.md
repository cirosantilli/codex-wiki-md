**Yes.** The baryons carry the oscillatory [baryon transfer function](../../../../../../baryon-transfer-function.md) inherited from the pre-recombination [photon-baryon fluid](../../../../../../photon-baryon-fluid.md). Their later infall toward cold-dark-matter overdensities suppresses the relative mode and drives $\delta_b/\delta_c\to1$, but gravitational evolution does not remove every scale-dependent acoustic phase. The total [cosmological density power spectrum](../../../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md) therefore contains the small wiggles known as a [baryon acoustic oscillation in the matter power spectrum](../../../../../../baryon-acoustic-oscillation-in-the-matter-power-spectrum.md).
