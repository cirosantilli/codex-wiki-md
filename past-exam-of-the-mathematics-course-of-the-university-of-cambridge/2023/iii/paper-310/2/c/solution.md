<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

When $\mu_\nu/T_\nu\gg1$, the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) approaches the [step function](../../../../../../step-function.md) $f(p)=1$ for $p<\mu_\nu$ and zero for $p>\mu_\nu$. For one internal state and negligible mass, the [degenerate relic neutrino](../../../../../../degenerate-relic-neutrino.md) energy density is therefore

$$
\rho_\nu\simeq\frac1{2\pi^2}\int_0^{\mu_\nu}p^3\,dp
=\boxed{\frac{\mu_\nu^4}{8\pi^2}},
$$

so $\boxed{A=1/(8\pi^2)}$.

At [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md), the [antineutrino](../../../../../../antineutrino.md) chemical potential is $-\mu_\nu$. Its occupation is approximately $e^{-(p+\mu_\nu)/T_\nu}$, giving

$$
\rho_{\bar\nu}\simeq
\frac{3T_\nu^4}{\pi^2}e^{-\mu_\nu/T_\nu}.
$$

It is exponentially smaller than the neutrino density; this particle-antiparticle imbalance is a [cosmological lepton asymmetry](../../../../../../cosmological-lepton-asymmetry.md).
