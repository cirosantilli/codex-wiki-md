↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A large [neutrino degeneracy parameter](../../../../../../neutrino-degeneracy-parameter.md) raises the relativistic energy density and therefore the expansion rate. [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](../../../../../../big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) tests this through primordial light-element abundances; an electron-neutrino chemical potential also shifts neutron--proton chemical equilibrium directly. The [Cosmic microwave background power spectrum](../../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-power-spectrum.md) tests the changed expansion rate, matter-radiation equality, sound horizon, damping scale, and neutrino anisotropic stress. Finally, [large-scale structure of the universe](../../../../../../large-scale-structure-of-the-universe-split.md) and the matter power spectrum test the altered equality scale and [neutrino free streaming](../../../../../../neutrino-free-streaming.md). These observables distinguish a degenerate-neutrino cosmology from the standard thermal relic scenario in complementary epochs.
