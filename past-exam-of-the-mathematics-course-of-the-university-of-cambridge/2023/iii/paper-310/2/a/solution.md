<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Before [electron-positron annihilation in cosmology](../../../../../../electron-positron-annihilation-in-cosmology.md), photons and electron-positron pairs share a temperature. Their effective entropy degrees of freedom are

$$
g_{*s}^{\rm before}=2+\frac78(2+2)=\frac{11}{2}.
$$

After annihilation the electromagnetic plasma contains only the two photon polarizations, so $g_{*s}^{\rm after}=2$. The already decoupled neutrinos receive none of this entropy and simply cool as $T_\nu\propto a^{-1}$. Applying [cosmological entropy conservation](../../../../../../cosmological-entropy-conservation.md) separately to the coupled electromagnetic plasma gives

$$
g_{*s}^{\rm before}T_{\rm before}^3a_{
m before}^3
=g_{*s}^{\rm after}T^3a^3,
$$

whereas $T_\nu^3a^3=T_{\rm before}^3a_{
m before}^3$. Division yields the [Cosmic neutrino background](../../../../../../cosmic-neutrino-background.md) temperature

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_\nu}{T}=\left(\frac4{11}\right)^{1/3}}.
$$
