↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**Yes.** A [radial baryon acoustic oscillation measurement](../../../../../../radial-baryon-acoustic-oscillation-measurement.md) and a [transverse baryon acoustic oscillation measurement](../../../../../../transverse-baryon-acoustic-oscillation-measurement.md) of the same ruler at one redshift obey

$$
\Delta z=H(z)\chi_d,
\qquad
\Delta\theta=\frac{\chi_d}{\chi(z)}.
$$

Their ratio cancels both the unknown ruler length and the overall Hubble scale:

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Delta z}{\Delta\theta}
=H(z)\chi(z)=y(z,\Omega_{m,0})}.
$$

This is the [Alcock-Paczyński parameter](../../../../../../alcock-paczynski-parameter.md). At a specified redshift its dependence on $\Omega_{m,0}$ permits a one-redshift determination within the assumed flat [Lambda-CDM model](../../../../../../lambda-cdm-model.md).
