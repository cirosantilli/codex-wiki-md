<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The right-hand side of the [slow-roll curvature power spectrum](../../../../../../slow-roll-curvature-power-spectrum.md) is evaluated at [cosmological horizon exit](../../../../../../cosmological-horizon-exit.md), $k=aH$. Using

$$
\dot\phi^2=2\epsilon M_{\rm Pl}^2H^2
$$

rewrites it as

$$
\Delta_{\mathcal R}^2
=\frac{H^2}{8\pi^2M_{\rm Pl}^2\epsilon}.
$$

At horizon exit, $d\log k=d\log(aH)=(1-\epsilon)dN$, so $d/d\log k=d/dN$ to first slow-roll order. With the [first Hubble slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../first-hubble-slow-roll-parameter.md) and [second Hubble slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../second-hubble-slow-roll-parameter.md),

$$
n_s-1=\frac{d\log\Delta_{\mathcal R}^2}{d\log k}
=-2\epsilon-\eta.
$$

The supplied relations $\epsilon=\epsilon_V$ and $\eta_V=2\epsilon-\eta/2$ imply $\eta=4\epsilon_V-2\eta_V$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{n_s-1=-6\epsilon_V+2\eta_V}.
$$
