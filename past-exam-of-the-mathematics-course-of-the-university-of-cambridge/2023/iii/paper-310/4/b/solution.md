<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a monotonic inflaton trajectory, the [potential slow-roll parameter](../../../../../../potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) satisfies

$$
\left|\frac{d\phi}{dN}\right|
=M_{\rm Pl}\sqrt{2\epsilon_V(\phi)}.
$$

The remaining [slow-roll e-fold count](../../../../../../slow-roll-e-fold-count.md) is the integral from the present field value to its end value along the direction of motion:

$$
\boxed{\widetilde N(\phi)
=\int_\phi^{\phi_e}
\frac{d\widetilde\phi}
{M_{\rm Pl}\sqrt{2\epsilon_V(\widetilde\phi)}}}.
$$
