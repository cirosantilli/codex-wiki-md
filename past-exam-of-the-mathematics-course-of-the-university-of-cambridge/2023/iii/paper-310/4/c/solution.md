<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Taking the ratio of the supplied [primordial tensor power spectrum](../../../../../../primordial-tensor-power-spectrum.md) and scalar spectrum cancels the common factor $(H/2\pi)^2$:

$$
r=\frac{8}{M_{\rm Pl}^2}
\frac{\dot\phi^2}{H^2}
=16\epsilon.
$$

To first slow-roll order $\epsilon=\epsilon_V$, so the [tensor-to-scalar ratio](../../../../../../tensor-to-scalar-ratio.md) is

$$
\boxed{r=16\epsilon_V},
\qquad
\boxed{C=16}.
$$
