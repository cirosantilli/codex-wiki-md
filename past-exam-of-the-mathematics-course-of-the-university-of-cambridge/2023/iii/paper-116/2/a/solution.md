<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [first-order theory](../../../../../../first-order-theory.md) $R$ extending ZFC, let $C_R$ be its set of formal consequences and let $\mathrm{Cons}$ denote the class of [formal consistency statements](../../../../../../formal-consistency-statement.md) $\operatorname{Con}(Q)$ for recursively axiomatized extensions $Q$ of ZFC. Using [Gödel numbering](../../../../../../godel-numbering.md) to code proofs and theories, these objects and the following comparison are definable in the base theory ZFC.

The [consistency-strength preorder](../../../../../../consistency-strength-preorder.md) is

$$
T\leq_{\mathrm{Cons}}S
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathrm{Cons}\cap C_T\subseteq\mathrm{Cons}\cap C_S.
$$

Thus every consistency assertion provable in $T$ is also provable in $S$. Its strict part is

$$
\boxed{T<_{\mathrm{Cons}}S
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
T\leq_{\mathrm{Cons}}S\ \land\ \neg(S\leq_{\mathrm{Cons}}T).}
$$
