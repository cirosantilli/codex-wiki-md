**No.** Let

$$
\alpha=(\kappa^+)^M,
$$

the [successor cardinal](../../../../../../successor-cardinal.md) of $\kappa$ computed by $M$. Then $\alpha\in M$, and $M$ regards $\alpha$ as a [cardinal number](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md). Because $M$ is transitive, $\alpha\subseteq M$, so externally

$$
|\alpha|\leq|M|=\kappa.
$$

On the other hand $\kappa<\alpha$, hence $|\alpha|=\kappa$ in the ambient universe. A corresponding [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) belongs to $V_\lambda$ because its [rank](../../../../../../rank-of-a-set.md) is below the inaccessible limit $\lambda$. Thus $V_\lambda$ regards $\alpha$ as equinumerous with $\kappa$ and therefore not as a cardinal. This is an instance of [cardinal nonabsoluteness in a small transitive model](../../../../../../cardinal-nonabsoluteness-in-a-small-transitive-model.md).
