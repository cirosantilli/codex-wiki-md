<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $u=-v_r>0$ denote the inward speed. Steady spherical [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) and the radial [momentum equation](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) for an [isothermal gas](../../../../../../isothermal-bondi-accretion.md) give

$$
\dot M=4\pi r^2\rho u,
\qquad
u\frac{du}{dr}=-c_s^2\frac{d\log\rho}{dr}-\frac{GM}{r^2}.
$$

Eliminating $d\log\rho/dr$ produces the [Isothermal Bondi equation](../../../../../../isothermal-bondi-equation.md)

$$
\left(u-\frac{c_s^2}{u}\right)\frac{du}{dr}
=\frac{2c_s^2}{r}-\frac{GM}{r^2}.
$$

A smooth flow can cross [Mach number](../../../../../../mach-number.md) one only where both sides vanish. Its [Bondi sonic point](../../../../../../bondi-sonic-point.md) is therefore

$$
u_s=c_s,
\qquad
r_s=\frac{GM}{2c_s^2}.
$$

The integrated [Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) which approaches rest and density $\rho_\infty$ at infinity is

$$
\frac{u^2}{2}+c_s^2\log\frac{\rho}{\rho_\infty}-\frac{GM}{r}=0.
$$

At the [sonic point](../../../../../../sonic-point.md) this gives $\rho_s=e^{3/2}\rho_\infty$, and hence the unique regular [transonic branch](../../../../../../transonic-branch.md) has

$$
\boxed{\dot M
=4\pi r_s^2\rho_s c_s
=\pi e^{3/2}\frac{G^2M^2\rho_\infty}{c_{s,\infty}^3}.}
$$
