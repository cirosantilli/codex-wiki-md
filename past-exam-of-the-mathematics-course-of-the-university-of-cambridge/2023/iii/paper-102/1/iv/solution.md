<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A bilinear form $B$ on a [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) is [invariant](../../../../../../invariant-bilinear-form-on-a-lie-algebra.md) when

$$
B([x,y],z)=B(x,[y,z]),
$$

equivalently $B([x,y],z)+B(y,[x,z])=0$.

Choose a basis $(x_i)$ of $\mathfrak g$ and its $B$-dual basis $(x^i)$. The [Casimir element](../../../../../../casimir-element.md) is

$$
\Omega=\sum_i x_ix^i\in U(\mathfrak g),
$$

where $U(\mathfrak g)$ is the [universal enveloping algebra](../../../../../../universal-enveloping-algebra.md). The tensor $\sum_i x_i\otimes x^i$ corresponds under $B:\mathfrak g\cong\mathfrak g^*$ to the identity endomorphism, so invariance of $B$ makes it fixed by the diagonal adjoint action. Applying multiplication $U(\mathfrak g)\otimes U(\mathfrak g)\to U(\mathfrak g)$ gives

$$
[x,\Omega]=0
$$

for every $x\in\mathfrak g$. Thus $\Omega$ lies in the [center of an associative algebra](../../../../../../center-of-an-associative-algebra.md) of $U(\mathfrak g)$.
