↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The distinct affine algebraic groups

$$
SL_2(\mathbb C)
\quad\text{and}\quad
PGL_2(\mathbb C)=SL_2(\mathbb C)/\{\pm I\}
$$

both have the [special linear Lie algebra](../../../../../../special-linear-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)$. Quotienting a [Lie group](../../../../../../lie-group.md) by a discrete central subgroup does not change its tangent Lie algebra.
