↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The result is weaker than the pointwise [null energy condition](../../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md) used in the elementary proof of the [second law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../../second-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md): $T_{ab}U^aU^b$ may be negative over a finite interval, so the expansion and area can have local behavior that the pointwise argument does not control. Nevertheless, the [averaged null energy condition for a nonminimally coupled scalar](../../../../../../../averaged-null-energy-condition-for-a-nonminimally-coupled-scalar.md) supplies the integrated positivity required by strengthened global focusing and area theorems when their completeness, endpoint, and genericity hypotheses hold. Thus the calculation supports a suitable global second law for $\xi<0$, but the displayed averaged inequality alone does not prove pointwise area monotonicity without those additional assumptions.
