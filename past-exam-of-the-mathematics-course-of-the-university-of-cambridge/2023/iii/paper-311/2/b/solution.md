<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $U^a$ be the affine null tangent and choose another null vector $N^a$ with $U\mathbin\cdot N=-1$. The [screen-space projector](../../../../../../screen-space-projector.md)

$$
P^a{}_b=\delta^a_b+U^aN_b+N^aU_b
$$

projects onto the $(D-2)$-dimensional transverse space. The [optical tensor](../../../../../../optical-tensor.md) is

$$
\widehat B_{ab}=P_a{}^cP_b{}^d\nabla_dU_c.
$$

Its irreducible decomposition defines

$$
\boxed{\theta=P^{ab}\widehat B_{ab}},
$$

the [null expansion](../../../../../../null-expansion.md),

$$
\boxed{\widehat\sigma_{ab}
=\widehat B_{(ab)}-\frac{\theta}{D-2}P_{ab}},
$$

the [null shear](../../../../../../null-shear.md), and

$$
\boxed{\widehat\omega_{ab}=\widehat B_{[ab]}},
$$

the [null twist](../../../../../../null-twist.md), also called rotation.
