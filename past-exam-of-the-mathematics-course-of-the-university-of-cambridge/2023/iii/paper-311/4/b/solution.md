<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The in-vacuum satisfies $a_j^{\rm in}|0_{\rm in}\rangle=0$. For the out-mode number operator $N_i^{\rm out}=a_i^{{\rm out}\dagger}a_i^{\rm out}$, substitute the operator transformation and use the [canonical commutation relations](../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md). Only the $a_j^{\rm in}a_k^{{\rm in}\dagger}$ contraction survives, giving the [particle number from Bogoliubov coefficients](../../../../../../particle-number-from-bogoliubov-coefficients.md)

$$
\boxed{
\langle0_{\rm in}|N_i^{\rm out}|0_{\rm in}\rangle
=\sum_j|\beta_{ij}|^2}.
$$

For a continuous mode label, the sum becomes the corresponding integral with Dirac-delta normalization.
