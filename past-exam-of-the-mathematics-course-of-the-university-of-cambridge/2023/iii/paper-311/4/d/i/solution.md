<h1 id="4/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a metric $ds^2=-f(r)dv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2d\Omega_2^2$ with a simple Killing horizon, the [surface gravity](../../../../../../../surface-gravity.md) of $k=\partial_v$ is $\kappa=f'(r_+)/2$. Here

$$
f'(r)=\frac{2r}{L^2}
+\frac{r_+}{r^2}\left(1+\frac{r_+^2}{L^2}\right),
$$

so

$$
\kappa=\frac1{2r_+}+\frac{3r_+}{2L^2}.
$$

The [Hawking temperature](../../../../../../../hawking-temperature.md) of the [Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter black hole](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-anti-de-sitter-black-hole.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{T_H=\frac\kappa{2\pi}
=\frac{1+3r_+^2/L^2}{4\pi r_+}}.
$$
