<h1 id="4/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The horizon area is $A=4\pi r_+^2$, while

$$
\frac{dM}{dr_+}=\frac12\left(1+\frac{3r_+^2}{L^2}\right),
\qquad
\frac\kappa{8\pi}\frac{dA}{dr_+}
=\kappa r_+
=\frac12\left(1+\frac{3r_+^2}{L^2}\right).
$$

Thus the [First law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../../first-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md)

$$
\boxed{dM=\frac\kappa{8\pi}dA=T_H\,dS_{BH}}
$$

holds with the [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](../../../../../../../bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md) $S_{BH}=A/4$.

For equilibrium with an infinite fixed-temperature reservoir, local stability requires positive [heat capacity](../../../../../../../heat-capacity.md). Since $dM/dr_+>0$, its sign is the sign of

$$
\frac{dT_H}{dr_+}
=\frac1{4\pi}\left(-\frac1{r_+^2}+\frac3{L^2}\right).
$$

Therefore the [Canonical stability of a Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter black hole](../../../../../../../canonical-stability-of-a-schwarzschild-anti-de-sitter-black-hole.md) occurs precisely for

$$
\boxed{r_+>\frac L{\sqrt3}}.
$$

The equality is the minimum-temperature, marginal case; smaller black holes have negative heat capacity and unstable thermal equilibrium.
