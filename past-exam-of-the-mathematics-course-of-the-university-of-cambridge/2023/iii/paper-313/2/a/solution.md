<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Away from a zero of the Higgs field, write

$$
\phi=e^{u/2+i\chi},
\qquad u=\log|\phi|^2.
$$

Separating real and imaginary parts of $(D_x+iD_y)\phi=0$ gives

$$
A_x=\partial_x\chi+\frac12\partial_yu,
\qquad
A_y=\partial_y\chi-\frac12\partial_xu.
$$

Thus $B=-\Delta u/2$ away from zeros. Combining this with $B=(1-e^u)/2$ gives

$$
\Delta u+1-e^u=0.
$$

If $\phi$ has a zero of multiplicity $N$ at the origin, its [vortex number](../../../../../../vortex-number.md) is the [winding number](../../../../../../winding-number.md) $N$ and $u=2N\log r+O(1)$. Since $\Delta\log r=2\pi\delta^{(2)}(\mathbf x)$ as a [distributional identity](../../../../../../distributional-identity.md), the complete [Taubes equation](../../../../../../taubes-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{
\Delta u+1-e^u=4\pi N\delta^{(2)}(\mathbf x)}.
$$

The boundary conditions for a single $N$-vortex are

$$
\boxed{
u(r)=2N\log r+O(1)\quad(r\to0),
\qquad
u(r)\to0\quad(r\to\infty)}.
$$

The latter is the finite-energy condition $|\phi|\to1$.
