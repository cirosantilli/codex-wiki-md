<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The even-degree subring is the second [Veronese subring](../../../../../../veronese-subring.md)

$$
R^{(2)}=k[x^2,xy,y^2]\cong k[u,v,w]/(uw-v^2),
$$

where $u,v,w$ have degree one in the regraded ring. The canonical invariance of the [Proj construction](../../../../../../proj-construction.md) under passage to a Veronese subring gives

$$
\operatorname{Proj}R\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Proj}R^{(2)}.
$$

On homogeneous points this is the degree-two [Veronese embedding](../../../../../../veronese-embedding.md)

$$
[x:y]\longmapsto[x^2:xy:y^2].
$$

It is an isomorphism onto the closed subscheme

$$
V_+(uw-v^2)\subseteq\mathbb P_k^2.
$$

**Thus the quotient homomorphism $k[u,v,w]\twoheadrightarrow R^{(2)}$ supplies the requested [closed immersion](../../../../../../closed-immersion.md) of $\operatorname{Proj}R^{(2)}$ into the [projective plane](../../../../../../projective-plane.md).**
