<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [open immersion](../../../../../../open-immersion.md) $j:U\to X$ is a [morphism of schemes](../../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) that identifies $U$, including its [structure sheaf](../../../../../../structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md), with an open subscheme of $X$.

Let $X=\mathbb A_k^2$ and $U=X\setminus\{(0,0)\}$. The inclusion is an open immersion and $X$ is an [affine scheme](../../../../../../affine-scheme.md), but $U$ is not affine. Indeed, regular functions extend across the missing codimension-two point, so

$$
\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)=k[x,y]=\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X).
$$

Were $U$ affine, the inclusion would therefore correspond to an isomorphism of coordinate rings and would be an isomorphism $U\cong X$, a contradiction.

For an example with both schemes affine, the [principal open subscheme](../../../../../../principal-open-subscheme.md)

$$
D(t)=\operatorname{Spec}k[t,t^{-1}]\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}k[t]=\mathbb A_k^1
$$

is a nontrivial open immersion. The affine line is connected because $k[t]$ has no nontrivial [idempotent elements](../../../../../../idempotent.md).
