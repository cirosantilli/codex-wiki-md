<h1 id="4/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Only the $p$ Spin(8) vectors $q$ and the one spinor $t$ contribute to the magnetic gauge beta function. The supplied indices give

$$
b_0^{\rm magnetic}
=\frac32(6)-\frac12(p+1)
=\frac{17-p}{2}=\frac{13-N}{2}.
$$

The magnetic theory is infrared free when $b_0^{\rm magnetic}<0$, namely

$$
\boxed{N>13}.
$$

At $N=13$ the one-loop coefficient vanishes and higher-order dynamics decides the flow. For $N>13$, [Seiberg duality](../../../../../../seiberg-duality.md) says that the strongly coupled low-energy limit of the asymptotically free electric $SU(N)$ theory is described by the weakly coupled Spin(8) fields and the superpotential from part v.
