<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Two theories are [Seiberg duals](../../../../../seiberg-duality.md) when they are distinct ultraviolet gauge theories that flow to the same infrared quantum field theory. Their gauge-invariant operator spectra, global symmetries, ['t Hooft anomalies](../../../../../t-hooft-anomaly.md) and responses to deformations agree, even though one description may be strongly coupled where the other is weakly coupled.
