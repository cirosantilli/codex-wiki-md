↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Quantum consistency requires cancellation of the $SU(N)^3$ [gauge anomaly](../../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md). The two-index [symmetric square](../../../../../../symmetric-square.md) has [cubic anomaly coefficient](../../../../../../cubic-anomaly-coefficient.md) $A(S)=N+4$, whereas each antifundamental has coefficient $-1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p=N+4}.
$$
