<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Using the supplied [Dynkin indices](../../../../../../dynkin-index.md) and $p=N+4$, the [one-loop beta function of a supersymmetric gauge theory](../../../../../../one-loop-beta-function-of-a-supersymmetric-gauge-theory.md) is

$$
b_0=\frac32(2N)-\frac12\left[(N+2)+p\right]
=3N-\frac12(2N+6)=2N-3.
$$

**Thus $b_0>0$ for every nontrivial $SU(N)$ in this family, and the electric theory is [asymptotically free](../../../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md).**
