<h1 id="2/n-1-quotient-metric/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [N = 1 quotient metric](../n-1-quotient-metric.md)

There is a conflict in the printed question: with dynamical $X$ and the displayed [superpotential](../../../../../../superpotential.md), $F_X=\widetilde\phi\phi=M$ forces $M=0$, so the $N=1$ theory has no vacuum branch parametrized by nonzero $M$. The natural intended calculation is the [Kähler quotient](../../../../../../kahler-quotient.md) of the $N=1$ D-flat SQED matter fields before imposing that extra F-term.

On this quotient, $|\phi|=|\widetilde\phi|$ and the gauge-invariant coordinate is $M=\widetilde\phi\phi$. Hence $|\phi|^2=|\widetilde\phi|^2=|M|$, and the canonical [Kähler potential](../../../../../../kahler-potential.md) descends to

$$
\boxed{K(M,\overline M)=2\sqrt{M\overline M}=2|M|}.
$$

The associated [Kähler metric](../../../../../../kahler-metric.md) is

$$
\boxed{g_{M\overline M}=\partial_M\partial_{\overline M}K
=\frac1{2|M|}}.
$$

It is smooth for $M\ne0$ and has a conical singularity at $M=0$, locally $\mathbb C/\mathbb Z_2$. Physically, the charged fields become massless and the U(1) gauge symmetry is restored there, so integrating out the vector multiplet to obtain a sigma-model metric ceases to be valid.
