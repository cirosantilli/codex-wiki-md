<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $m_i=0$ and first take $\zeta>0$. The mass terms and [D-flatness](../../../../../../d-flatness.md) require $x=0$ and $\sum_i|\phi_i|^2-\sum_i|\widetilde\phi_i|^2=\zeta$, while [F-flatness](../../../../../../f-flatness.md) adds $\sum_i\widetilde\phi_i\phi_i=0$. Dividing by the U(1) gauge action gives the [Higgs branch](../../../../../../higgs-branch.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M_H=T^*\mathbb {CP}^{N-1}},
$$

with complex dimension $2N-2$. For $\zeta<0$, the same result follows after exchanging $\phi$ and $\widetilde\phi$.
