<h1 id="2/ii-2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii-2.md)

For $\zeta=0$ and distinct masses, any nonzero charged field can occur only at one $x=m_j$. At such a point, [D-flatness](../../../../../../d-flatness.md) gives $|\phi_j|=|\widetilde\phi_j|$, whereas [F-flatness](../../../../../../f-flatness.md) gives $\widetilde\phi_j\phi_j=0$; together they force both fields to vanish. Thus only the [Coulomb branch](../../../../../../coulomb-branch.md) remains,

$$
\boxed{\phi_i=\widetilde\phi_i=0,\qquad x\in\mathbb C},
$$

of complex dimension one. The points $x=m_i$ are special because the corresponding charged multiplets become massless there.
