<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $W=XYZ$,

$$
V=|YZ|^2+|XZ|^2+|XY|^2.
$$

The [F-flatness](../../../../../../f-flatness.md) equations require at least two of $X,Y,Z$ to vanish. The [vacuum space](../../../../../../vacuum-moduli-space-of-a-supersymmetric-gauge-theory.md) is therefore the union of the three coordinate axes in $\mathbb C^3$,

$$
\boxed{\{Y=Z=0\}\cup\{X=Z=0\}\cup\{X=Y=0\}},
$$

and every point on it has zero vacuum energy.
