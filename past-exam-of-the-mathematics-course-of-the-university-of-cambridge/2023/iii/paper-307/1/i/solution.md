<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $W=X^2Z$, the [F-term scalar potential](../../../../../../f-term-scalar-potential.md) is

$$
V=|W_X|^2+|W_Z|^2
=4|X|^2|Z|^2+|X|^4.
$$

Both terms vanish exactly when $X=0$, while $Z$ is arbitrary. Thus the [supersymmetric vacua](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vacuum.md) form one complex line,

$$
\boxed{X=0,\qquad Z\in\mathbb C,\qquad V_{\rm vac}=0}.
$$
