<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For

$$
W=\alpha Y+\beta YX^2+\gamma XZ,
$$

the three F-terms are

$$
W_X=2\beta XY+\gamma Z,
\qquad W_Y=\alpha+\beta X^2,
\qquad W_Z=\gamma X.
$$

The equation $W_Z=0$ forces $X=0$, after which $W_Y=\alpha\ne0$; hence no [supersymmetric vacuum](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vacuum.md) exists. Minimizing over $Z$ sets $Z=-2\beta XY/\gamma$, leaving

$$
V(X)=|\alpha+\beta X^2|^2+|\gamma X|^2.
$$

The two real quadratic eigenvalues about $X=0$ are $|\gamma|^2\pm2|\alpha\beta|$, so the stated hierarchy makes $X=0$ stable. The vacua are

$$
\boxed{X=Z=0,\qquad Y\text{ arbitrary},\qquad V_{\rm vac}=|\alpha|^2}.
$$

This is an [O'Raifeartaigh model](../../../../../../o-raifeartaigh-model.md): supersymmetry is spontaneously broken and $Y$ is a classical [pseudomodulus](../../../../../../pseudomodulus.md).
