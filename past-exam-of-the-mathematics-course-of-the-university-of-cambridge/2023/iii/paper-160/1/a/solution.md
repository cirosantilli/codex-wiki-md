<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [symmetric group](../../../../../../symmetric-group.md) acts transitively on the [Young tableaux](../../../../../../young-tableau.md) of a fixed shape. Moreover, $g e(t)=e(gt)$ for every permutation $g$, so every [polytabloid](../../../../../../polytabloid.md) is a translate of any fixed one. Hence the [Specht module](../../../../../../specht-module.md) is a [cyclic module](../../../../../../cyclic-module.md) generated by $e(t)$.

It remains to see that $e(t)\ne0$. In its expansion, the coefficient of the [tabloid](../../../../../../tabloid.md) $\{t\}$ is one: if $\pi\in C(t)$ and $\{\pi t\}=\{t\}$, then $\pi\in R(t)\cap C(t)=\{1\}$. Thus the generator, and therefore the module, is nonzero.
