<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Conjugation by a permutation merely permutes the transpositions. Their sum $C$ is therefore a [conjugacy class](../../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) sum and belongs to the [center of an associative algebra](../../../../../../../center-of-an-associative-algebra.md) $\mathbb C S_n$. Since the complex [Specht module](../../../../../../../specht-module.md) is an [irreducible representation](../../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md), [Schur lemma](../../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) says that $C$ acts on it as a [scalar](../../../../../../../scalar.md), say $c(\lambda)$.
