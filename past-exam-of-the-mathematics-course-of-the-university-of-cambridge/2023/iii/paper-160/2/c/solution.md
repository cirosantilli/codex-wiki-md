<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose $g$ whose [disjoint permutation cycles](../../../../../../disjoint-permutation-cycles.md) have lengths equal to the [principal hook lengths](../../../../../../principal-hook-lengths-of-a-partition.md) of $\lambda$. Those lengths are distinct and sum to $n$, so this is a permutation in $S_n$. In the iterated [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](../../../../../../murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md), there is a unique complete sequence that removes the corresponding principal rim hooks. Its contribution is one sign, and hence the [Principal-hook character value of a symmetric group](../../../../../../principal-hook-character-value-of-a-symmetric-group.md) gives $\chi^\lambda(g)\in\{1,-1\}$.
