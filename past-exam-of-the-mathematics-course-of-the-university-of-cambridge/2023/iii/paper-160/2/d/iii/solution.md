↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a nonzero example, take $n=3$ and let $g$ be a transposition. The two terms are the trivial and standard characters of $S_3$, whose values at a transposition are respectively $1$ and $0$. Thus $F(g)=1$ although $g$ contains a cycle of even length.

For a zero example, take $n=5$ and again let $g$ be a transposition, of cycle type $(2,1^3)$. The values of $\chi^{(5)},\chi^{(4,1)},\chi^{(3,2)}$ there are $1,2,1$, respectively. Consequently

$$
\boxed{F(g)=1-2+1=0.}
$$
