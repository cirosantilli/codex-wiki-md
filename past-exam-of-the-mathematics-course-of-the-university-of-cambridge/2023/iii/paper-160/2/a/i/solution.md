The [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](../../../../../../../murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) states that if a permutation has a $k$-cycle and remaining cycle type $\rho$, then

$$
\chi^\lambda(k,\rho)
=\sum_H(-1)^{\operatorname{leg}(H)}\chi^{\lambda\setminus H}(\rho),
$$

where $H$ runs over the removable [rim hooks](../../../../../../../rim-hook.md) of length $k$. Iterating removes rim hooks whose lengths are the cycle lengths, and the character value is the signed sum over all complete removal sequences.
