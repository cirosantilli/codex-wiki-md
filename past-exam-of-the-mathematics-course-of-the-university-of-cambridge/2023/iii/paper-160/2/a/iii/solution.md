<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For each $m$, the shifted sequence $\beta_{i,m+1}-\mathrm{id}$ is obtained from $\beta_{i,m}-\mathrm{id}$ by swapping the adjacent entries in positions $i+m$ and $i+m+1$. Part a(ii) therefore gives

$$
\psi^{\beta_{i,m+1}}=-\psi^{\beta_{i,m}}.
$$

This is the usual [character straightening](../../../../../../../straightening-of-a-symmetric-group-character-indexed-by-a-composition.md) procedure: the distinguished entry $\alpha_i-i-k$ is moved successively to the right. Either it reaches the unique place that makes some $\beta_{i,m}$ a [partition of an integer](../../../../../../../partition-of-an-integer.md), or it meets an equal shifted entry. Under the hypothesis the first alternative never occurs, so two entries of some $\beta_{i,m}-\mathrm{id}$ coincide. The corresponding alternating sum is fixed by swapping those entries but changes sign by part a(ii), and is therefore zero. Repeatedly applying the displayed relation gives $\psi^{\beta_{i,0}}=0$.
