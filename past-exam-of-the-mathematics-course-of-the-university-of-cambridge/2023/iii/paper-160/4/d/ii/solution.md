↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The nonzero homomorphism $\theta$ cannot kill $e(t)$, because the [Specht module](../../../../../../../specht-module.md) is generated by the translates of this [polytabloid](../../../../../../../polytabloid.md). Equivariance and part d(i) therefore give

$$
b_t\theta(e(u))=\theta(b_t e(u))=\alpha\theta(e(t))\ne0.
$$

**Thus $b_t$ acts nontrivially on $M^\mu/V$, and hence on $M^\mu$. Some $\mu$-tabloid $\{v\}$ must satisfy $b_t\{v\}\ne0$. Fact 2 now says that $\lambda$ dominates $\mu$ in the [dominance order on partitions](../../../../../../../dominance-order-on-partitions.md).**
