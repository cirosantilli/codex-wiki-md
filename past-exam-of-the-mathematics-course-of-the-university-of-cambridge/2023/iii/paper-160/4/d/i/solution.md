↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Because $\lambda$ is $p$-regular, Fact 1 gives a tableau $u$ for which

$$
\alpha:=\langle e(t),e(u)\rangle
=\prod_{j=1}^n(a_j!)^j\ne0.
$$

Part a, applied linearly to the tabloid expansion of $e(u)$, gives

$$
\boxed{b_t e(u)=\langle e(t),e(u)\rangle e(t)=\alpha e(t).}
$$
