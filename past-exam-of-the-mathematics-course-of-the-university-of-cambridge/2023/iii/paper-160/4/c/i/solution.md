<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The partition is $p$-regular precisely when

$$
a_j<p\qquad\text{for every }j,
$$

that is, when no part occurs $p$ or more times. This is the definition of a [regular partition](../../../../../../../regular-partition.md) in [characteristic of a field](../../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$.
