<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use a [beta set](../../../../../../../beta-set-of-a-partition.md) $X$ on an $e$-runner [partition abacus](../../../../../../../abacus-of-a-partition.md). A hook of length divisible by $e$ corresponds to a bead $x$ and a gap $x-eh$ on the same runner. Divide both runner positions by $e$. They become a bead and a gap at distance $h$ in the runner partition $\lambda^{(s)}$, and hence determine a hook there. This gives the required [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md), with $|H|=e|f(H)|$.

Removing $H$ replaces the bead $x$ by the gap $x-eh$. On its runner this is exactly the bead move that removes $f(H)$, while every other runner is unchanged. Thus hook removal commutes with the construction and

$$
Q_e(\lambda\setminus H)=Q_e(\lambda)\setminus f(H).
$$

This is the [abacus divisible-hook correspondence](../../../../../../../hooks-divisible-by-the-abacus-modulus.md).
