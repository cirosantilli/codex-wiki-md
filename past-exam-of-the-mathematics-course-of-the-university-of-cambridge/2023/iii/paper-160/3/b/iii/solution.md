<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** On an $f$-runner [partition abacus](../../../../../../../abacus-of-a-partition.md), taking the $e$-quotient of each runner refines its positions by their residue modulo $e$. The resulting $ef$ runners are indexed by pairs of residues. Ordering those pairs may differ from the conventional residue order modulo $ef$, but only by a permutation. Hence $Q_e(Q_f(\lambda))$ is a permutation of $Q_{ef}(\lambda)$.
