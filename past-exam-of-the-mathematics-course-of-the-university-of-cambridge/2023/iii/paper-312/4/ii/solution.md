<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The linearized [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md), the divergence of the [cosmological Euler equation](../../../../../../cosmological-euler-equation.md), and the [cosmological Poisson equation](../../../../../../cosmological-poisson-equation.md) give

$$
\boxed{\delta'+\theta=0},
\qquad
\boxed{\theta'+\mathcal H\theta
+\frac32\mathcal H^2\Omega_m\delta=0}.
$$

Eliminating $\theta=-\delta'$ yields the equation for a [linear cosmological density perturbation](../../../../../../linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md):

$$
\boxed{\delta''+\mathcal H\delta'
-\frac32\mathcal H^2\Omega_m\delta=0}.
$$

In an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), $\Omega_m=1$ and $\mathcal H=2/\tau$, so

$$
\delta''+\frac2\tau\delta'-\frac6{\tau^2}\delta=0.
$$

Substitution of the [power-law ansatz](../../../../../../power-law-ansatz.md) $\delta\propto\tau^p$ gives $(p-2)(p+3)=0$. Hence

$$
\delta=C_+\tau^2+C_-\tau^{-3}
=D_+a+D_-a^{-3/2}.
$$

The leading one of the [matter-era growing and decaying density modes](../../../../../../matter-era-growing-and-decaying-density-modes.md) is $\delta\propto a\propto\tau^2$, as direct substitution confirms, and its velocity divergence is

$$
\boxed{\theta=-\delta'=-\mathcal H\delta\propto-\tau}.
$$
