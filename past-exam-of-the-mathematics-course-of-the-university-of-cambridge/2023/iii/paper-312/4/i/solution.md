<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Linearizing the [cosmological Euler equation](../../../../../../cosmological-euler-equation.md) gives

$$
\mathbf v'+\mathcal H\mathbf v=-\nabla\phi.
$$

Taking the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) removes the gradient force, so the [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) $\mathbf w=\nabla\mathbin\times\mathbf v$ obeys

$$
\boxed{\mathbf w'+\mathcal H\mathbf w=0}.
$$

Since $\mathcal H=a'/a$, its solution is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf w\propto a^{-1}}.
$$

**Thus expansion dilutes linear vorticity; in an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), $a\propto\tau^2$ and $\mathbf w\propto\tau^{-2}$.**
