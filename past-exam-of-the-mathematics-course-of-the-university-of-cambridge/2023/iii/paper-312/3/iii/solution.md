<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [line-of-sight solution for free-streaming photons](../../../../../../line-of-sight-solution-for-free-streaming-photons.md) follows the unperturbed ray $\mathbf x(\eta)=\mathbf x_0-\widehat{\mathbf p}(\eta_f-\eta)$. If $\Phi=\Psi$ is spatially homogeneous, its gradient vanishes and $d\log\epsilon/d\eta=\Phi'$. Integration along the ray gives

$$
\boxed{
\Theta(\eta_f,\mathbf x_0,\widehat{\mathbf p})
=\Theta(\eta_i,
\mathbf x_0-\widehat{\mathbf p}(\eta_f-\eta_i),
\widehat{\mathbf p})
+\Phi(\eta_f)-\Phi(\eta_i)}.
$$

The needed initial condition is the temperature pattern on the initial hypersurface; for a superhorizon adiabatic mode during matter domination one may use $\Theta_0(\eta_i)=-2\Psi(\eta_i)/3$.

This calculation contains free streaming of the initial pattern and the gravitational temperature shift caused by the evolving potential, the homogeneous limit of the [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../../../integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md). A perfectly homogeneous potential changes only the unobservable sky monopole and therefore contributes no [CMB angular power spectrum](../../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-power-spectrum.md) for $\ell\geq1$. Direction-dependent [Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../../../sachs-wolfe-effect.md), Doppler, acoustic, polarization, lensing, and rescattering contributions require spatially varying perturbations or collision terms and are outside this special calculation.
