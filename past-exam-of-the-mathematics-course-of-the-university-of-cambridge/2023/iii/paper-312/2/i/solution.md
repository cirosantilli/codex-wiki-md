<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The background spatial metric is $g_{ij}=a^2\delta_{ij}$. For the [large spatial diffeomorphism](../../../../../../large-spatial-diffeomorphism.md)

$$
\epsilon^0=0,
\qquad \epsilon^i=\omega^i{}_jx^j,
\qquad \omega_{ij}=\omega_{ji},
\qquad \omega_i{}^i=0,
$$

homogeneity and isotropy imply that the purely spatial background [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) vanish. Hence

$$
\nabla_i\epsilon_j=a^2\omega_{ji},
\qquad
\boxed{\Delta\gamma_{ij}=-2\omega_{ij}}.
$$

The shift is constant, transverse and traceless, and is therefore the zero-momentum [adiabatic tensor mode](../../../../../../adiabatic-tensor-mode.md).

A scalar transforms by its [Lie derivative](../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md):

$$
\Delta\varphi(\mathbf x)
=-\omega_{ij}x^j\partial_i\varphi(\mathbf x).
$$

Fourier transformation and [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) in momentum space give

$$
\boxed{\Delta\varphi(\mathbf k)
=\omega_{ij}k_i\frac{\partial}{\partial k_j}\varphi(\mathbf k)}.
$$

The term proportional to $\omega_i{}^i$ vanishes because the deformation is traceless.
