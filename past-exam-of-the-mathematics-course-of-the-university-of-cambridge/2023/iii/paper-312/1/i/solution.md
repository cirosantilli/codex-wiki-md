<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under [spatial reflection](../../../../../../spatial-reflection.md), a [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) obeys $P\phi(\mathbf x)P=\phi(-\mathbf x)$, and hence each [spatial derivative](../../../../../../spatial-derivative.md) changes sign. The interaction contains three such derivatives, so changing the integration variable from $\mathbf x$ to $-\mathbf x$ gives

$$
PH_{\rm int}P=-H_{\rm int}.
$$

Thus this is a [parity-odd scalar interaction](../../../../../../parity-odd-scalar-interaction.md).

Put $O(\mathbf k_a)=\prod_{a=1}^n\phi_a(\mathbf k_a)$ and $A(\mathbf k_a)=\langle H_{\rm int}O(\mathbf k_a)\rangle$. [Hermitian conjugation](../../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md) and commutativity of equal-time scalar fields give

$$
\langle O(\mathbf k_a)H_{\rm int}\rangle
=A(-\mathbf k_a)^*.
$$

The [parity-invariant vacuum](../../../../../../parity-invariant-vacuum.md) and the odd parity of $H_{\rm int}$ imply $A(-\mathbf k_a)=-A(\mathbf k_a)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\langle[H_{\rm int},O]\rangle
=A-A(-\mathbf k_a)^*=A+A^*=2\operatorname{Re}A}.
$$

There is no conflict with the usual $2i\operatorname{Im}A$ rule for two [Hermitian operators](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md): a momentum-space product at fixed $\mathbf k_a$ is generally not itself Hermitian, since its adjoint carries momenta $-\mathbf k_a$.
