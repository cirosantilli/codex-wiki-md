The [Stone prime filter theorem](../../../../../../stone-prime-filter-theorem.md) says that if a [lattice filter](../../../../../../lattice-filter.md) $F$ and a [lattice ideal](../../../../../../lattice-ideal.md) $I$ of a [distributive lattice](../../../../../../distributive-lattice.md) are disjoint, then there is a [prime filter of a distributive lattice](../../../../../../prime-filter-of-a-distributive-lattice.md) $P$ such that

$$
F\subseteq P
\quad\text{and}\quad
P\cap I=\varnothing.
$$

Equivalently, whenever $a\nleq b$, there is a prime filter containing $a$ and omitting $b$.
