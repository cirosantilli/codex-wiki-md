A [prime filter of a distributive lattice](../../../../../../prime-filter-of-a-distributive-lattice.md) $P$ is a proper [lattice filter](../../../../../../lattice-filter.md): it contains the top element, is upward closed, and is closed under finite meets. Primality means

$$
\boxed{a\vee b\in P
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
a\in P\ \text{or}\ b\in P.}
$$
