<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Priestley dual space](../../../../../../priestley-dual-space.md) $\widehat L$ of a [distributive lattice](../../../../../../distributive-lattice.md) $L$ has all [prime filters](../../../../../../prime-filter-of-a-distributive-lattice.md) of $L$ as its points. Its order is inclusion. For each $a\in L$, put

$$
a^*=\{P\in\widehat L:a\in P\}.
$$

The topology is generated by the sets $a^*$ and their complements. Each $a^*$ is therefore a [clopen up-set](../../../../../../clopen-up-set.md), and these sets separate points and order. With this topology and order, $\widehat L$ is a compact totally order-disconnected ordered space.
