**No.** The [Omega combinator](../../../../../../omega-combinator.md) is

$$
\Omega=(\lambda x.xx)(\lambda x.xx).
$$

Its only [beta-redex](../../../../../../beta-redex.md) contracts back to $\Omega$ itself. Every reduction sequence therefore repeats the same term, which is not in [beta-normal form](../../../../../../beta-normal-form.md). Thus $\Omega$ has no beta-normal form.
