↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A term is in [beta-normal form](../../../../../../beta-normal-form.md) when it contains no [beta-redex](../../../../../../beta-redex.md), meaning no subterm of the form

$$
(\lambda x.M)N.
$$

Equivalently, no [beta reduction](../../../../../../beta-reduction.md) can be performed anywhere in the term.
