The [Kripke completeness theorem for intuitionistic propositional logic](../../../../../../kripke-completeness-theorem-for-intuitionistic-propositional-logic.md) states that, for every set of formulae $\Gamma$ and formula $A$,

$$
\Gamma\vdash_{\mathrm{IPC}}A
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{every world of every intuitionistic Kripke model that forces $\Gamma$ also forces $A$}.
$$

The forward implication is [soundness](../../../../../../soundness-theorem-for-propositional-logic.md), and the reverse implication is completeness.
