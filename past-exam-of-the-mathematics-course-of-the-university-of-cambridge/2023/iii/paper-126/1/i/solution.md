↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $X=V/\Gamma$ be a [complex torus](../../../../../../complex-torus.md). A [Riemann form on a complex torus](../../../../../../riemann-form-on-a-complex-torus.md) is a [Hermitian form](../../../../../../hermitian-form.md) $H$ on $V$ whose imaginary part

$$
E(v,w)=\operatorname{Im}H(v,w)
$$

takes integer values on $\Gamma\times\Gamma$. Equivalently, $E$ is an integral alternating form satisfying

$$
E(iv,iw)=E(v,w),
\qquad E(iv,v)>0
$$

for every nonzero $v$. A [polarisation of a complex torus](../../../../../../polarization-of-a-complex-torus.md) is such a positive Riemann form, or equivalently its integral cohomology class. By the [Appell–Humbert theorem](../../../../../../appell-humbert-theorem.md), it is the first Chern class of an ample holomorphic line bundle. A polarisation is principal when the homomorphism $\Gamma\to\Gamma^*$ induced by $E$ is an isomorphism.
