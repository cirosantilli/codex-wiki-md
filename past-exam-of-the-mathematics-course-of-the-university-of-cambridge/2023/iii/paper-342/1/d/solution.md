<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $x$ and $y$ denote the two oriented noncontractible cycles of the [torus](../../../../../../torus.md). Their transport operators intersect once, so the mutual braiding phase gives the [Wilson-loop algebra of an Abelian Chern--Simons theory](../../../../../../wilson-loop-algebra-of-an-abelian-chern-simons-theory.md)

$$
\boxed{T_x(q')T_y(q)
=e^{2\pi i q'^TK^{-1}q}T_y(q)T_x(q').}
$$

The integer charge vectors are identified modulo local electrons: $q$ and $q+K\ell$ have identical braiding with every quasiparticle. The distinct topological charges therefore form the finite [quotient group](../../../../../../quotient-group.md)

$$
\mathcal A=\mathbb Z^n/K\mathbb Z^n,
\qquad |\mathcal A|=|\det K|.
$$

Choose a ground state that diagonalizes the mutually commuting $x$-cycle operators. Acting with $T_y(q)$ multiplies their eigenvalues by the character $q'\mapsto e^{2\pi i q'^TK^{-1}q}$. This pairing is nondegenerate on $\mathcal A$: if the character is trivial for every $q'$, then $K^{-1}q$ is integral and $q$ is the trivial class. Thus the $|\mathcal A|$ states obtained from inequivalent $q$ have distinct loop eigenvalues and are orthogonal. The [torus ground-state degeneracy of an Abelian Chern--Simons theory](../../../../../../torus-ground-state-degeneracy-of-an-abelian-chern-simons-theory.md) is consequently at least $|\det K|$.

The all-ones matrix has eigenvalue $n$ along $t$ and eigenvalue zero on the $(n-1)$-dimensional perpendicular subspace. Hence $K=I_n+pM$ has eigenvalues $1+np,1,\ldots,1$, and

$$
\boxed{|\det K|=|1+np|.}
$$
