<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $M^2=nM$, direct multiplication gives the [matrix inverse](../../../../../../matrix-inverse.md)

$$
\boxed{(I_n+pM)^{-1}=I_n-\frac p{1+np}M,}
$$

provided $1+np\ne0$, as required by invertibility of the [K-matrix](../../../../../../k-matrix.md). For $q=e_J$,

$$
K^{-1}e_J=e_J-\frac p{1+np}\mathbf1.
$$

The attached gauge-flux vector is therefore

$$
\Phi=2\pi\hbar e_J
-\frac{2\pi\hbar p}{1+np}\mathbf1.
$$

Relative to the $K=I_n$ electron in part a, every one of the $n$ gauge fields consequently carries the additional flux

$$
\boxed{\Delta\Phi_I=-\frac{2\pi\hbar p}{1+np}.}
$$
