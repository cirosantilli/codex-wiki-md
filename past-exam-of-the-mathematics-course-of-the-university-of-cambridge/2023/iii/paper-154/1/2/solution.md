<h1 id="1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Split the integral at radius $R$. The [Holder inequality](../../../../../../holder-inequality.md) on the ball and the spatial moment outside it give

$$
\|v\|_2^2
\leq |B_R|^{1/2}\|v\|_4^2
+R^{-2}\||x|v\|_2^2
\leq\sqrt\pi R\|v\|_4^2+R^{-2}\|v\|_\Sigma^2.
$$

Taking $R=\varepsilon^{-1/2}$ proves the stated estimate with $C_\varepsilon=\sqrt\pi\varepsilon^{-1/2}$. Choose $\varepsilon<1/\omega$. If $X=\|v\|_4^2$, then

$$
J(v)\geq\frac{1-\omega\varepsilon}{2}\|v\|_\Sigma^2
+\frac14X^2-\frac{\omega C_\varepsilon}{2}X.
$$

The final two terms form a quadratic polynomial bounded below, so $\inf_\Sigma J> -\infty$.
