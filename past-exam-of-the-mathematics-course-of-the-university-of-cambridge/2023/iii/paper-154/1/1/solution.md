<h1 id="1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For $\psi(x)=e^{-|x|^2/2}$ in two dimensions,

$$
\partial_j\psi=-x_j\psi,
\qquad
\partial_j^2\psi=(x_j^2-1)\psi.
$$

Thus the [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) satisfies $\Delta\psi=(|x|^2-2)\psi$, and the isotropic harmonic-oscillator operator gives

$$
\boxed{(-\Delta+|x|^2)\psi=2\psi.}
$$
