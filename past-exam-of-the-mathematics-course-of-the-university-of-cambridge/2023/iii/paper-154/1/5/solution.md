<h1 id="1/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Put $y=x/\lambda(t)$ and

$$
u(t,x)=\lambda^{-1}v(y)e^{-ib|y|^2/4+i\gamma}.
$$

Differentiate the scaling and quadratic phase. After multiplying the nonlinear Schrödinger equation by $\lambda^3e^{ib|y|^2/4-i\gamma}$, the terms proportional to $y\cdot\nabla v+v$, $|y|^2v$, and $v$ vanish respectively when

$$
\frac{ds}{dt}=\lambda^{-2},
\qquad
b=-\lambda^{-1}\frac{d\lambda}{ds},
\qquad
\frac{db}{ds}+b^2=-4,
\qquad
\frac{d\gamma}{ds}=-\omega.
$$

The remaining expression is

$$
-\Delta v+|y|^2v-\omega v+v^3,
$$

which vanishes by part 4. Hence the [Schrödinger expanding lens ansatz for the mass-critical equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-expanding-lens-ansatz-for-the-mass-critical-equation.md) solves the defocusing cubic equation.
