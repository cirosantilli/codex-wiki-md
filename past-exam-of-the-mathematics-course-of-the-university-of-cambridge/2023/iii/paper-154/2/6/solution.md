<h1 id="2/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Write $q=w_t+w_r$. The equation in part 5 says

$$
q_t-q_r=-\frac{|w|^{p-1}w}{r^{p-1}}.
$$

Differentiate $J_\psi$, replace $q_t$ by $q_r-|w|^{p-1}w/r^{p-1}$, and integrate the $qq_r$ and $|w|^{p-1}ww_r$ terms by parts. This gives the [outgoing-energy identity for a radial defocusing wave](../../../../../../outgoing-energy-identity-for-a-radial-defocusing-wave.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{dJ_\psi}{dt}
=-\frac12\int_0^\infty\psi' q^2dr
+\frac1{p+1}\int_0^\infty
\frac{|w|^{p+1}}{r^{p-1}}
\left(\psi'-(p-1)\frac\psi r\right)dr.}
$$
