<h1 id="2/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

For a radial function in three dimensions,

$$
\Delta u=u_{rr}+\frac2r u_r
=\frac1r\partial_{rr}(ru).
$$

Set $w=ru$. Multiplying the wave equation by $r$ gives the [radial reduction of the three-dimensional wave equation](../../../../../../radial-reduction-of-the-three-dimensional-wave-equation.md)

$$
w_{tt}-w_{rr}+\frac{|w|^{p-1}w}{r^{p-1}}=0,
\qquad r>0,
$$

with the regularity boundary condition $w(t,0)=0$.
