<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Steady spherical [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives $4\pi r^2\rho u=\dot M$. For the [polytropic equation of state](../../../../../../polytropic-equation-of-state.md), $c_s^2=dp/d\rho$, so

$$
\frac{\rho'}\rho=-\frac{u'}u-\frac2r,
\qquad
\frac{p'}\rho=c_s^2\frac{\rho'}\rho.
$$

Substitution into the radial [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) gives the [Parker wind equation](../../../../../../parker-wind-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{
\left(u-\frac{c_s^2}{u}\right)\frac{du}{dr}
=\frac{2c_s^2}{r}-\frac{GM_*}{r^2}}.
$$

At a [sonic point](../../../../../../sonic-point.md), the coefficient of $du/dr$ vanishes. A smooth [transonic branch](../../../../../../transonic-branch.md) can pass it only if the right-hand side vanishes simultaneously. Hence

$$
\boxed{u_s=c_{s,s},
\qquad
r_s=\frac{GM_*}{2c_{s,s}^2}}.
$$
