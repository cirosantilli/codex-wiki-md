<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the midplane, the vacuum [magnetic dipole field](../../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-field.md) has scale $B_0\sim\mu/r^3$, while the result of part i gives $j\sim\Omega^2\mu/(cr^2)$. [Ampère's law](../../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) then gives the induced-field estimate

$$
\frac{B_i}{r}\sim\frac{j}{c},
\qquad
B_i\sim\frac{\Omega^2\mu}{c^2r},
$$

where numerical factors are immaterial in this [dimensional analysis](../../../../../../../dimensional-analysis.md). Thus $B_i\sim B_0$ at

$$
\boxed{R_e\sim\frac c\Omega}.
$$

This is the [light cylinder radius](../../../../../../../light-cylinder-radius.md): rigid corotation would have $u=\Omega R_e\sim c$. The nonrelativistic approximation and the assumed unmodified vacuum field therefore fail at the same scale, and a self-consistent [pulsar magnetosphere](../../../../../../../pulsar-magnetosphere.md) must replace them.
