<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [magnetic-field-line equation](../../../../../../../magnetic-field-line-equation.md) for the dipole is

$$
\frac{dr}{r\,d\theta}=\frac{B_r}{B_\theta}=2\cot\theta.
$$

Integration gives the [dipole magnetic-field line](../../../../../../../dipole-magnetic-field-line.md)

$$
r=C\sin^2\theta.
$$

The line crosses the midplane at $r=R_e$, so $C=R_e$. At the stellar surface,

$$
R_*=R_e\sin^2\theta_*.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\theta_*=\arcsin\sqrt{\frac{R_*}{R_e}}}
\simeq\sqrt{\frac{\Omega R_*}{c}}
$$

for a small [polar-cap opening angle](../../../../../../../polar-cap-opening-angle.md).
