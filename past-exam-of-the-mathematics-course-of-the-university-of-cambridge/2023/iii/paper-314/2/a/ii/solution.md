<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The physical filament ends when the [mass density](../../../../../../../density.md) first reaches zero. If $x_1\simeq2.4$ is the first positive zero of the [Bessel function](../../../../../../../bessel-function.md) $J_0$, then

$$
\boxed{
R_f=x_1a
=x_1\sqrt{\frac{K+B_0^2/(8\pi\rho_0^2)}{2\pi G}}}.
$$

The first zero must be used because continuing to the next lobe would make the density negative.
