<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The shock speed is

$$
V_s=\dot R=\frac{C}{3}t^{-2/3}=\frac{R}{3t}.
$$

Apply the [Strong-shock Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](../../../../../../strong-shock-rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) in the shock frame and transform the downstream velocity back to the ambient-medium frame. With the [shock compression ratio](../../../../../../shock-compression-ratio.md)

$$
\chi=\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1},
$$

the post-shock values are

$$
\boxed{
\rho_{\rm ps}=\chi\rho_0,
\qquad
u_{\rm ps}=\frac{2}{\gamma+1}V_s,
\qquad
p_{\rm ps}=\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\rho_0V_s^2}.
$$
