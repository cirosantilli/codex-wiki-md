<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Resolve the uniform upstream field into components normal and tangential to the spherical shock:

$$
B_{0r}=B_0\cos\theta,
\qquad
B_{0\theta}=-B_0\sin\theta,
\qquad
B_{0\phi}=0.
$$

The [ideal magnetohydrodynamic shock conditions](../../../../../../ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-shock-conditions.md) leave the normal field continuous and multiply the tangential field by the gas compression ratio $\chi$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf B_{\rm ps}
=B_0\cos\theta\,\mathbf e_r
-\chi B_0\sin\theta\,\mathbf e_\theta,
\qquad
\chi=\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}.
$$
