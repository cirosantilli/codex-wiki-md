<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Label positron [Compton scattering](../../../../../../compton-scattering.md) as

$$
\gamma(k,\lambda)+e^+(p,s)
\longrightarrow\gamma(k',\lambda')+e^+(p',s').
$$

There are two positron-exchange diagrams: the $s$-channel ordering with internal momentum $-p-k$, and the $u$-channel ordering with internal momentum $-p+k'$. Reading opposite to the fermion arrow gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
i\mathcal M=(-ie)^2\overline v(p)\bigg[&
\gamma^\mu\frac{i(-\not p-\not k+m)}{(p+k)^2-m^2+i\epsilon}\gamma^\nu\\
&+\gamma^\nu\frac{i(-\not p+\not k'+m)}{(p-k')^2-m^2+i\epsilon}\gamma^\mu
\bigg]v(p')
\epsilon_\mu(k,\lambda)\epsilon_\nu^*(k',\lambda').
\end{aligned}
$$

It is the crossed form of electron Compton scattering and again satisfies the [Ward identity](../../../../../../ward-identity.md) only after both orderings are included.
