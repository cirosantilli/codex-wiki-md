<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) sends each spatial derivative to $iq_i$, so at Gaussian level

$$
\boxed{G(q)=a+\kappa q^2+\gamma q^4.}
$$

For $\kappa<0<\gamma$, minimizing over $s=q^2\geq0$ gives

$$
s_0=-\frac\kappa{2\gamma},
\qquad
\boxed{q_0=\sqrt{-\frac\kappa{2\gamma}}.}
$$

The minimum kernel is $G(q_0)=a-\kappa^2/(4\gamma)$. Gaussian fluctuations first diverge when this vanishes, so the [nonzero-wavevector soft-mode sphere](../../../../../../nonzero-wavevector-soft-mode-sphere.md) becomes unstable at

$$
\boxed{a_c=\frac{\kappa^2}{4\gamma}.}
$$
