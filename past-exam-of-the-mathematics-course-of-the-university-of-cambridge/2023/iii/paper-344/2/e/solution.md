<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Every term in the free energy contracts the vector components with the [Euclidean inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md): it depends only on $|\mathbf p|^2$, $|\mathbf p|^4$, $(\nabla_i p_j)(\nabla_i p_j)$, and $|\nabla^2\mathbf p|^2$. A constant $\mathcal R\in SO(3)$ preserves all these contractions and commutes with spatial differentiation. Therefore

$$
F[\mathcal R\mathbf p]=F[\mathbf p],
$$

so every constant rotation of candidate (ii) has the same free energy. The orientation of the rotation plane of the [polar helical smectic](../../../../../../polar-helical-smectic.md) is thus continuously degenerate.
