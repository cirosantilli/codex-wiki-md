<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Thermal molecular motion causes the coarse droplet radius to fluctuate as well as drift down $F(R)$, so its effective equation must be an [overdamped Langevin dynamics](../../../../../../overdamped-langevin-dynamics.md). With $\langle\Lambda(t)\Lambda(t')\rangle=\delta(t-t')$, detailed balance with equilibrium density proportional to $e^{-F/(k_BT)}$ imposes the [Fluctuation-dissipation theorem](../../../../../../fluctuation-dissipation-theorem.md). The [Model A fluctuation-dissipation relation](../../../../../../model-a-fluctuation-dissipation-relation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{A=\sqrt{2k_BT\,\overline{\mathcal M}}.}
$$

The factor of mobility ensures that the diffusion in $R$ and the dissipative drift have the same equilibrium Gibbs distribution.
