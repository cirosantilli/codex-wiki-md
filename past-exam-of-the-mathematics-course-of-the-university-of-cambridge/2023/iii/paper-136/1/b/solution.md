<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The main theorem of [local class field theory](../../../../../../local-class-field-theory.md) gives a continuous [Local Artin map](../../../../../../local-artin-map.md)

$$
\operatorname{Art}_K:K^\times\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Gal}(K^{\mathrm{ab}}/K)
$$

with dense image, normalized by sending a [uniformizer](../../../../../../uniformizer.md) to a chosen [Frobenius](../../../../../../frobenius-automorphism.md). For every finite abelian extension $L/K$, it induces the [Local Artin reciprocity](../../../../../../local-artin-reciprocity.md) isomorphism

$$
K^\times/N_{L/K}(L^\times)\cong\operatorname{Gal}(L/K).
$$

The [existence theorem of local class field theory](../../../../../../existence-theorem-of-local-class-field-theory.md) says that the finite-index open subgroups of $K^\times$ are exactly the [norm subgroups](../../../../../../norm-subgroup-of-a-local-field-extension.md) $N_{L/K}(L^\times)$ for finite abelian extensions $L/K$, and that the extension is uniquely determined inside $K^{\mathrm{ab}}$.

Write $e=e(L/K)$ and $f=f(L/K)$. The valuation of a [field norm](../../../../../../field-norm.md) satisfies

$$
v_K(N_{L/K}x)=f\,v_L(x),
$$

so the valuation image of the norm subgroup is $f\mathbb Z$. The exact sequence obtained from $v_K:K^\times\to\mathbb Z$ therefore gives

$$
[K^\times:N_{L/K}(L^\times)]
=f\,[\mathcal O_K^\times:N_{L/K}(\mathcal O_L^\times)].
$$

The left side is $[L:K]=ef$ by [Local Artin reciprocity](../../../../../../local-artin-reciprocity.md). Cancelling $f$ proves

$$
\boxed{[\mathcal O_K^\times:N_{L/K}(\mathcal O_L^\times)]=e.}
$$
