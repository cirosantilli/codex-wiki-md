<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $I(L/K)$ be the [inertia group](../../../../../../inertia-group.md) and let $\varphi$ denote [Frobenius](../../../../../../frobenius-automorphism.md) on the residue-field extension. The [Relative Weil group](../../../../../../relative-weil-group.md) is

$$
W(L/K)=\{\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(L/K):\bar\sigma=\varphi^n\text{ for some }n\in\mathbb Z\}.
$$

Its [Weil-group topology](../../../../../../weil-group-topology.md) makes $I(L/K)$ an open [profinite group](../../../../../../profinite-group.md) with its usual topology and gives $W(L/K)/I(L/K)$ the discrete topology. Thus every inertia coset is an open copy of $I(L/K)$.

Take $L=K^{\mathrm{nr}}$, the [maximal unramified extension](../../../../../../maximal-unramified-extension.md) of $K$. Then

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\cong\widehat{\mathbb Z},
\qquad
W(L/K)\cong\mathbb Z.
$$

The subgroup $\{0\}\subset\mathbb Z$ is open in the discrete Weil-group topology, but it is not open in the [profinite topology](../../../../../../profinite-topology.md) inherited from $\widehat{\mathbb Z}$.
