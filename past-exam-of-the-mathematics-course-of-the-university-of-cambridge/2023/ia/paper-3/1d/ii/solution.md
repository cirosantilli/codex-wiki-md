↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** The [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) has order six. A Sylow $3$-subgroup $P$ has order three, while the given normal [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $N$ has order two. Their intersection is trivial and $NP=G$. Conjugation by $P$ on $N$ is trivial because $\operatorname{Aut}(C_2)$ is trivial, so $G=N\times P\cong C_2\times C_3\cong C_6$.
