**False.** Let $G=C_4$ and $H=C_2\times C_2$, and in each choose a [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of order two. Then the chosen normal [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) are isomorphic and both quotients are $C_2$, while $G$ and $H$ are not isomorphic.
