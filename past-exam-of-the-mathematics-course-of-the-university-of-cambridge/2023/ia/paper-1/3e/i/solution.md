**No.** Take $f(x)=x^2$, $a=0$, and $g(y)=|y|$. The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $g$ is not [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) at $f(0)=0$, but $g(f(x))=x^2$ is [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md). Neither [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is constant on an interval.
