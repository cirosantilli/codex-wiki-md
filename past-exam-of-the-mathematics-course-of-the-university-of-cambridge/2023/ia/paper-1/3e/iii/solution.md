**No.** Take

$$
f(x)=\begin{cases}x,&x\geq0,\\2x,&x<0,\end{cases}
\qquad
g(y)=\begin{cases}2y,&y\geq0,\\y,&y<0.
\end{cases}
$$

Both [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) are nonconstant on every interval and nondifferentiable at zero, but $g(f(x))=2x$ is [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md).
