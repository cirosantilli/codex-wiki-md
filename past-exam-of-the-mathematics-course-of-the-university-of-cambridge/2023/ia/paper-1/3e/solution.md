<h1 id="3e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3E](../3e.md)

If $f$ is [differentiable](../../../../../differentiable-function.md) at $a$ and $g$ at $f(a)$, then $g\circ f$ is [differentiable](../../../../../differentiable-function.md) at $a$ and

$$
(g\circ f)'(a)=g'(f(a))f'(a).
$$

Write $g(f(a)+u)-g(f(a))=u\{g'(f(a))+\varepsilon(u)\}$, where $\varepsilon(u)\to0$, and put $u=f(a+h)-f(a)$. Division by $h$ and passage to the [limit](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) proves the formula.
