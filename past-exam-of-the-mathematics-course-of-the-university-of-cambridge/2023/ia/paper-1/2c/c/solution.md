<h1 id="2c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply part (b) to the normal [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $C=D-\lambda I$. Since $(D-\lambda I)e=0$, one has

$$
(D^\dagger-\overline\lambda I)e=C^\dagger e=0.
$$

**Thus $e$ is an [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $D^\dagger$ with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\overline\lambda$.**
