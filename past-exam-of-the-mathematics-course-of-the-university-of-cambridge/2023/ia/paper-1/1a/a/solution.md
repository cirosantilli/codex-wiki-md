↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the stated angular-frequency [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) convention, the [convolution theorem](../../../../../../convolution-theorem.md) gives

$$
\widetilde{f*g}(k)=\widetilde f(k)\widetilde g(k),
\qquad
(f*g)(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(\xi)g(x-\xi)\,d\xi.
$$

The [Fourier inversion theorem](../../../../../../fourier-inversion-theorem.md) and the assumed absolute integrability therefore imply

$$
\boxed{h(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(\xi)g(x-\xi)\,d\xi.}
$$

Since $|-i|=1$ and the principal argument is $-\pi/2$,

$$
\operatorname{Log}(-i)=-\frac{\pi i}{2}.
$$

All logarithms are $-\pi i/2+2\pi i n$, $n\in\mathbb Z$.
