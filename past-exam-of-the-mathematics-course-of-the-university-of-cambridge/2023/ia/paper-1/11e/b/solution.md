<h1 id="11e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The ordinary mean value theorem gives $\Delta_hf(a)=hf'(b_1)$ for some $b_1\in(a,a+h)$. More generally, applying it to the difference of two translated [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) shows inductively that

$$
\Delta_h^kf(a)=h^kf^{(k)}(b_k)
$$

for some $b_k\in(a,a+kh)$. Taking $k=n$ proves the result.
