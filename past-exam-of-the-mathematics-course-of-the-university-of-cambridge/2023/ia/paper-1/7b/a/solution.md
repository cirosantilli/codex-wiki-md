<h1 id="7b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $x\ne0$,

$$
x^\dagger Gx=|Ax|^2>0,
$$

so the Hermitian [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $G$ has positive real [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md). If $Ge_i=\lambda_i e_i$, then

$$
H(Ae_i)=AA^\dagger Ae_i=A(Ge_i)=\lambda_iAe_i.
$$

Moreover $|Ae_i|^2=\lambda_i|e_i|^2$, so $|f_i|/|e_i|=\sqrt{\lambda_i}$.
