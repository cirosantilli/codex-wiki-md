<h1 id="6c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $B=S^{-1}AS$, then

$$
\det(tI-B)=\det(S^{-1}(tI-A)S)=\det(tI-A),
$$

so the characteristic [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md), [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), and algebraic multiplicities agree. The converse is false: $I_2$ and $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ have the same characteristic [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md), but the first is diagonalisable and the second is not, so they cannot be similar.
