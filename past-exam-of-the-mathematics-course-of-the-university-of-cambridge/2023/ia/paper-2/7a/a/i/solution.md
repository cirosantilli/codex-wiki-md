<h1 id="7a/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For every test [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $\varphi$ and nonzero $p$, substitution $u=pt$ with the orientation handled by $|p|$ gives

$$
\int\varphi(t)\delta(pt)dt=\frac1{|p|}\varphi(0).
$$

**Hence the [Dirac delta scaling](../../../../../../../dirac-delta-scaling.md) identity is $\delta(pt)=\delta(t)/|p|$.**
