↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) of a distribution is defined by integration by parts. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(t)\delta'(t)dt
=-\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f'(t)\delta(t)dt
=-f'(0).}
$$
