<h1 id="7a/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a compactly supported test [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $f$,

$$
\langle H',f\rangle=-\langle H,f'\rangle
=-\int_0^\infty f'(t)dt=f(0)
=\langle\delta,f\rangle.
$$

**Thus this is the [distributional derivative of the Heaviside step function](../../../../../../../distributional-derivative-of-the-heaviside-step-function.md): $H'=\delta$.**
