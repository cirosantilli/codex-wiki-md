↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

This is the [Erlang distribution](../../../../../../erlang-distribution.md) with shape $n$ and rate one. Direct integration gives, for $t<1$,

$$
\boxed{M_X(t)=\frac1{(n-1)!}\int_0^\infty x^{n-1}e^{-(1-t)x}dx
=(1-t)^{-n}.}
$$
