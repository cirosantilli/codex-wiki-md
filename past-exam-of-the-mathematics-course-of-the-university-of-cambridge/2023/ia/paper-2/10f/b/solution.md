Differentiating the moment-generating [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) at zero gives

$$
\mathbb EX=M_X'(0)=n,
$$



$$
\operatorname{var}X=M_X''(0)-M_X'(0)^2
=n(n+1)-n^2=n.
$$
