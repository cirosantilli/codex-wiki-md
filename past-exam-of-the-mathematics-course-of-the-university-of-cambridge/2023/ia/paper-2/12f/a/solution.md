↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The probability generating [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is

$$
G_X(t)=\mathbb E[t^X]=\sum_{k=0}^\infty p_kt^k,
\qquad 0\leq t\leq1.
$$

Termwise [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) gives

$$
G_X'(t)=\sum_{k\geq1}kp_kt^{k-1},
\qquad
G_X''(t)=\sum_{k\geq2}k(k-1)p_kt^{k-2}.
$$

The coefficients are nonnegative, and $\mathbb P(X\geq2)>0$, so both [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) are positive and nondecreasing on $(0,1]$.
