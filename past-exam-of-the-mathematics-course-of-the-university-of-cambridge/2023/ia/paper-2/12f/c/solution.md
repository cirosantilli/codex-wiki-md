<h1 id="12f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Since $G_X(1)=1$ and $G_X'(1)=\mu>1$, for $t<1$ sufficiently close to one,

$$
G_X(t)-t<0.
$$

At zero, $G_X(0)-0\geq0$. Continuity therefore gives a fixed point in $[0,1)$, and part (b) implies $d<1$.
