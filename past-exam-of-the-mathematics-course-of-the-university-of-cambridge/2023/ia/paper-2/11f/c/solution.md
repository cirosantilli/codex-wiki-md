<h1 id="11f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

After the first of two equal-rate clocks [rings](../../../../../../ring.md), the residual lifetime of the other clock is, by memorylessness, a fresh exponential variable of rate $\lambda$, independent of the first ringing time and of which clock rang. Hence

$$
V\sim\operatorname{Exp}(2\lambda),
\qquad W-V\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda),
$$

and these variables are independent.
