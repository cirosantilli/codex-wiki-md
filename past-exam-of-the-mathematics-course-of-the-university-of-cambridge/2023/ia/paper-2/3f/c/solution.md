↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For any $t>0$, the [exponential Markov bound](../../../../../../exponential-markov-bound.md) and part (b) give

$$
\mathbb P(X\geq v)
\leq e^{-tv}M_X(t)=e^{-tv+t^2/2}.
$$

The minimum occurs at $t=v$, so the [Gaussian tail bound](../../../../../../gaussian-tail-bound.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_v^\infty\frac{e^{-x^2/2}}{\sqrt{2\pi}}dx
\leq e^{-v^2/2}.}
$$
