<h1 id="3f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $Y$ and $a>0$, the [Markov inequality](../../../../../../markov-inequality.md) is

$$
\mathbb P(Y\geq a)\leq\frac{\mathbb EY}{a}.
$$

Indeed, $Y\geq a\mathbf1_{\{Y\geq a\}}$ pointwise. Taking expectations and dividing by $a$ proves the claim.
