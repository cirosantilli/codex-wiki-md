<h1 id="8a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With $y=\dot x$, the system is

$$
\dot x=y,
\qquad
\dot y=-3y+4\log\frac{x^2+1}{2}.
$$

At equilibrium $y=0$ and $x^2=1$, so the equilibrium points are $(1,0)$ and $(-1,0)$.
