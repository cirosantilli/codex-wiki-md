<h1 id="8a/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The Jacobian is

$$
J(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\8x/(x^2+1)&-3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

At $(1,0)$ its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1,-4$, so this point is a saddle. At $(-1,0)$ they are $(-3\pm i\sqrt7)/2$, so this point is a stable spiral.
