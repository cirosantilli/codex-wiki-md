<h1 id="5f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The recurrence shows that $T_n$ is a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) with integer coefficients. Part (a) gives

$$
T_n(\cos(\pi/n))+1=\cos\pi+1=0.
$$

**Thus $\cos(\pi/n)$ is a root of a nonzero integer [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) and is algebraic.**
