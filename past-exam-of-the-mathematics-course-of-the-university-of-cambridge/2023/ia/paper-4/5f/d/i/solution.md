<h1 id="5f/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every term is nonnegative. Since $1-\cos x\leq x^2/2$,

$$
0\leq1-\cos(\pi/k)\leq\frac{\pi^2}{2k^2}.
$$

Comparison with $\sum k^{-2}$ shows that the partial sums are bounded. Being increasing, they converge to the finite [limit](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md)

$$
\boxed{\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(1-\cos(\pi/k)).}
$$
