<h1 id="8d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Sequences](../../../../../../sequence.md) with finitely many ones correspond to finite subsets of $\mathbb N$, a countable union of the countable sets of $k$-element subsets. Complementation gives the same result for finitely many zeros. Their union is therefore countable.
