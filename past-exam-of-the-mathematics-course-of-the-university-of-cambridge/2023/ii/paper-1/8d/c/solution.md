<h1 id="8d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\widehat b=B/|B|$. For a constant uniform [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md), choose the symmetric gauge

$$
A=\frac12B\times r,
$$

which has $\nabla\times A=B$ and $A\cdot\widehat b=0$. With $E=0$, one may take $\phi=0$. The coordinate $r_\parallel=r\cdot\widehat b$ is then absent from the Lagrangian, so it is an [ignorable coordinate](../../../../../../ignorable-coordinate.md). Its [generalized momentum](../../../../../../generalized-momentum.md)

$$
p_\parallel
=\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot r_\parallel}
=(m\dot r+qA)\cdot\widehat b
=m\dot r\cdot\widehat b
$$

is conserved. Hence

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dt}(p\cdot\widehat b)=0},
$$

which is the [canonical momentum parallel to a uniform magnetic field](../../../../../../canonical-momentum-parallel-to-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md) in this gauge.
