<h1 id="8d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under the stated [gauge transformation](../../../../../../gauge-transformation.md), the [charged-particle electromagnetic Lagrangian](../../../../../../charged-particle-electromagnetic-lagrangian.md) becomes

$$
\begin{aligned}
L'&=\frac12m\dot r^2
-q\left(\phi-\partial_tf
 -\dot r\cdot(A+\nabla f)\right)\\
&=L+q\left(\partial_tf+\dot r\cdot\nabla f\right)
 =L+q\frac{df(r(t),t)}{dt}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{L'-L=q\,df/dt}.
$$

The action changes only by $q[f(t_2)-f(t_1)]$, whose variation vanishes for fixed endpoints. By [total-time-derivative invariance of a Lagrangian](../../../../../../total-time-derivative-invariance-of-a-lagrangian.md), the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is unchanged.
