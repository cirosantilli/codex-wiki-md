<h1 id="12i/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For an effective enumeration $(W_w)_{w\in\mathbb W}$ of the computably enumerable languages, the diagonal [halting problem](../../../../../../halting-problem.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf K=\{w\in\mathbb W:w\in W_w\}}.
$$

Equivalently, if $f_{w,1}$ is the unary partial function coded by $w$, then

$$
w\in\mathbf K
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
f_{w,1}(w)\mathbin\downarrow.
$$

**Thus $\mathbf K$ is the [diagonal halting set](../../../../../../diagonal-halting-set.md).**
