↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A set $A$ is $\mathcal C$-complete under many-one reductions when

$$
\boxed{A\in\mathcal C
\quad\text{and}\quad
(\forall B\in\mathcal C)\ B\leq_mA}.
$$

The first condition is membership in the class; the second is $\mathcal C$-hardness. This is a [many-one complete set](../../../../../../many-one-complete-set.md).
