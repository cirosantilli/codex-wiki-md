<h1 id="12i/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A set $A\subseteq\mathbb W$ is a [Sigma-1 set](../../../../../../sigma-1-set.md) when it is the domain of a [partial computable function](../../../../../../computable-function.md): there is a partial computable $f$ such that

$$
\boxed{w\in A\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(w)\mathbin\downarrow}.
$$

Equivalently, $w\in A$ iff $\exists t\,R(w,t)$ for some computable predicate $R$; one may take $R(w,t)$ to say that the [truncated computation function](../../../../../../truncated-computation-function.md) observes a halt within $t$ steps.
