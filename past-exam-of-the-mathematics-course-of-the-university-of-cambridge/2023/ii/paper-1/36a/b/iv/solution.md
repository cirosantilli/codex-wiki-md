<h1 id="36a/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $N$ identical, noninteracting classical particles, the one-particle phase-space integrals factorize and indistinguishability supplies the Gibbs factor. Thus the [classical ideal-gas partition function](../../../../../../../classical-ideal-gas-partition-function.md) rule gives

$$
\boxed{
Z_N=\frac{Z_1^N}{N!}
=\frac1{N!}
\left[
\frac{4\pi^2m}{h^2\beta^2\kappa}
\left(1-e^{-\beta\kappa R^2/2}\right)
\right]^N
}.
$$
