<h1 id="1g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [linear Diophantine equation solvability criterion](../../../../../../linear-diophantine-equation-solvability-criterion.md) says that $ax+by=c$ has an integer solution exactly when $\gcd(a,b)$ divides $c$. The [Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
2314&=2(1007)+300,\\
1007&=3(300)+107,\\
300&=2(107)+86,\\
107&=86+21,\\
86&=4(21)+2,\\
21&=10(2)+1.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $\gcd(1007,2314)=1$, which divides $37$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{1007x+2314y=37\text{ has integer solutions}.}
$$
