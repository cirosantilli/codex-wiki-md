↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Applying the [Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
3211&=2508+703,\\
2508&=3(703)+399,\\
703&=399+304,\\
399&=304+95,\\
304&=3(95)+19,\\
95&=5(19).
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence $\gcd(2508,3211)=19$. Since $19$ does not divide $55$, the [linear Diophantine equation solvability criterion](../../../../../../linear-diophantine-equation-solvability-criterion.md) gives

$$
\boxed{2508x+3211y=55\text{ has no integer solution}.}
$$
