<h1 id="39c/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the exterior domain $r>a$, the streamfunction obeys

$$
\boxed{\nabla^4\psi=0}.
$$

Using the [Streamfunction in polar coordinates](../../../../../../../streamfunction-in-polar-coordinates.md) convention

$$
u_r=\frac1r\psi_\theta,
\qquad
u_\theta=-\psi_r,
$$

the no-slip condition on the stationary disk is

$$
u_r(a,\theta)=u_\theta(a,\theta)=0.
$$

Thus $\psi$ is constant on the disk and $\psi_r=0$ there. Choose the arbitrary constant to be zero:

$$
\boxed{\psi(a,\theta)=0,
\qquad
\psi_r(a,\theta)=0}.
$$

The imposed shear $\mathbf u_\infty=\gamma y\,\mathbf e_x$ has streamfunction

$$
\psi_\infty=\frac\gamma2y^2
=\frac{\gamma r^2}{4}(1-\cos2\theta).
$$

The far-field condition is therefore

$$
\boxed{\nabla\psi-\nabla\psi_\infty\longrightarrow0
\quad(r\to\infty)}.
$$
