<h1 id="4i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The length is

$$
\boxed{2n-1}.
$$

Indeed, the [parse tree](../../../../../../parse-tree.md) has $n$ terminal-production nodes and, as a full binary tree with $n$ leaves, $n-1$ binary-production nodes. This is the [Chomsky-normal-form derivation length](../../../../../../chomsky-normal-form-derivation-length.md).
