<h1 id="28j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Superpose the two independent [Poisson processes](../../../../../../poisson-process.md). The combined process has rate $9$, and each arrival is independently a Number 1 bus with probability $1/9$ and a Number 8 bus with probability $8/9$. Therefore the number of Number 8 buses before the first Number 1 has the [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) on $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, and

$$
\mathbb P(\text{exactly three Number 8 buses})
=\left(\frac89\right)^3\frac19
=\boxed{\frac{512}{6561}}.
$$

This is [number of one Poisson type before another](../../../../../../number-of-one-poisson-type-before-another.md) with rates $1$ and $8$.
