<h1 id="31j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A function $f:C\to\mathbb R$ is [strictly convex](../../../../../../strictly-convex-function.md) when, for distinct $x,y\in C$ and $0<t<1$,

$$
\boxed{f(tx+(1-t)y)<tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)}.
$$

Suppose distinct points $x$ and $y$ were both minimizers, with minimum value $m$. Since $C$ is a [convex set](../../../../../../convex-set.md), their midpoint belongs to $C$, while strict convexity gives

$$
f\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)
<\frac{f(x)+f(y)}2=m,
$$

a contradiction. Thus any minimizer is unique, which is the [uniqueness of a minimizer of a strictly convex function](../../../../../../uniqueness-of-a-minimizer-of-a-strictly-convex-function.md).
