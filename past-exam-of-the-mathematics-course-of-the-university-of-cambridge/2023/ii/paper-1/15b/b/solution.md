<h1 id="15b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Separate continuity gives

$$
\rho_r=\rho_{r0}a^{-4}
=\beta\frac{3c^2H_0^2}{8\pi G}a^{-4},
\qquad
\rho_\Lambda=\rho_{\Lambda0}
=\frac{3c^2H_0^2}{8\pi G},
$$

using the scalings for [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md) and [cosmological constant energy](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md). At $t=t_0$, where $a=1$, the Friedmann equation becomes

$$
H_0^2+Kc^2=(\beta+1)H_0^2,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{Kc^2=\beta H_0^2}.
$$

At general $a$,

$$
H^2+\frac{\beta H_0^2}{a^2}
=H_0^2\left(\frac\beta{a^4}+1\right),
$$

which rearranges to

$$
\boxed{
H^2=\frac{H_0^2}{a^4}(\beta-\beta a^2+a^4)
}.
$$

Part (a), applied to $\rho=\rho_r+\rho_\Lambda$ and $P=\rho_r/3-\rho_\Lambda$, gives

$$
\dot H+H^2
=-H_0^2\left(\frac\beta{a^4}-1\right).
$$

Subtracting the displayed formula for $H^2$ yields

$$
\boxed{
\dot H=-\beta\frac{H_0^2}{a^4}(2-a^2)
}.
$$

These relations define the [closed radiation--cosmological-constant turning polynomial](../../../../../../closed-radiation-cosmological-constant-turning-polynomial.md).
