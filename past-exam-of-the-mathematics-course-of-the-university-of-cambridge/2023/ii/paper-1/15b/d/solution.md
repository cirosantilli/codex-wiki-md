<h1 id="15b/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $\beta=4$,

$$
H^2=\frac{H_0^2}{a^4}(a^2-2)^2.
$$

The branch expanding from $a=0$ has

$$
H=\frac{H_0(2-a^2)}{a^2}
$$

until it approaches $a^2=2$. With $x=a^2$,

$$
\dot x=2a\dot a=2a^2H=2H_0(2-x),
\qquad x(0)=0.
$$

Solving this linear equation gives the [critical closed radiation--cosmological-constant solution](../../../../../../critical-closed-radiation-cosmological-constant-solution.md)

$$
\boxed{a(t)=\sqrt{2(1-e^{-2H_0t})}}.
$$

For $H_0t\ll1$,

$$
1-e^{-2H_0t}\sim2H_0t,
\qquad
\boxed{a(t)\sim2\sqrt{H_0t}}.
$$

For $H_0t\gg1$,

$$
a(t)=\sqrt2\left(1-\frac12e^{-2H_0t}
+O(e^{-4H_0t})\right),
$$

so

$$
\boxed{a(t)\longrightarrow\sqrt2}
$$

exponentially from below.
