<h1 id="37a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md) and particle energy are

$$
u^\mu=\gamma(c,\mathbf v),
\qquad
\mathcal E=\gamma mc^2,
\qquad
p^0=mu^0=\frac{\mathcal E}{c}.
$$

With the conventions for the given metric, $F^{0i}=E_i/c$. The temporal component of the covariant [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) is therefore

$$
\frac{d(\mathcal E/c)}{d\tau}
=qF^{0i}u_i
=\frac q c\gamma\mathbf E\mathbin{\cdot}\mathbf v.
$$

Since $dt/d\tau=\gamma$, cancellation of $\gamma/c$ gives the [relativistic electromagnetic work-energy theorem](../../../../../../relativistic-electromagnetic-work-energy-theorem.md)

$$
\boxed{
\frac{d\mathcal E}{dt}=q\mathbf E\mathbin{\cdot}\mathbf v
}.
$$

The absence of $\mathbf B$ reflects that magnetic force is perpendicular to the velocity and does no work.
