<h1 id="9b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $K=8\pi G/(3c^2)$. Differentiating $Q=K\rho a^2-\dot a^2$ gives

$$
\dot Q
=K(a^2\dot\rho+2a\dot a\rho)-2\dot a\ddot a.
$$

The continuity equation and the [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) imply

$$
a^2\dot\rho+2a\dot a\rho
=-a\dot a(\rho+3P)
$$

and

$$
-2\dot a\ddot a
=K a\dot a(\rho+3P).
$$

These terms cancel, so

$$
\boxed{\dot Q=0}.
$$

This is the [First integral of the Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../first-integral-of-the-friedmann-acceleration-equation.md).
