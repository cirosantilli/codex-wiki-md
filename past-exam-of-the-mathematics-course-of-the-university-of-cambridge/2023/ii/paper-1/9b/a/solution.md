<h1 id="9b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a comoving region, $E=\rho V$ and $V=a^3V_0$. The work law gives

$$
d(\rho V)=-P\,dV,
$$

so differentiation with respect to time yields

$$
V\dot\rho+(\rho+P)\dot V=0.
$$

Since $\dot V/V=3\dot a/a$, division by $V$ gives the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot\rho+3\frac{\dot a}{a}(\rho+P)=0}.
$$
