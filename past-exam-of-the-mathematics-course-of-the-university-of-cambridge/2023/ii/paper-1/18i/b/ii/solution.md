↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** Let

$$
K=\mathbb Q,qquad L=\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]2),qquad
M=\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]2,\omega),
$$

where $\omega$ is a primitive cube root of unity. Then $M$ is the [splitting field](../../../../../../../splitting-field.md) of $T^3-2$ over $\mathbb Q$, so $M/K$ is normal. The [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) $T^3-2$ has a root in $L$ but does not split there, so $L/K$ is not normal.
