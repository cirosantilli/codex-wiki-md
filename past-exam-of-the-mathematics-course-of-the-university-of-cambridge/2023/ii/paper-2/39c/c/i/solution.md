<h1 id="39c/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Work in the frame translating with the bubble. The bubble is the fixed circle $r=a$, while the velocity tends to $-U\mathbf e_x$ at infinity. The [potential flow around a circular cylinder](../../../../../../../potential-flow-around-a-circular-cylinder.md) has [velocity potential](../../../../../../../velocity-potential.md)

$$
\boxed{
\phi=-U\left(r+\frac{a^2}{r}\right)\cos\theta
}.
$$

It is [harmonic](../../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) for $r>a$ and gives

$$
\boxed{
u_r=-U\left(1-\frac{a^2}{r^2}\right)\cos\theta,
\qquad
u_\theta=U\left(1+\frac{a^2}{r^2}\right)\sin\theta.}
$$

**Thus $u_r=0$ on $r=a$, satisfying the [kinematic boundary condition](../../../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition.md), and $\mathbf u\to-U\mathbf e_x$ as $r\to\infty$. The laboratory-frame flow is obtained by adding $U\mathbf e_x$.**
