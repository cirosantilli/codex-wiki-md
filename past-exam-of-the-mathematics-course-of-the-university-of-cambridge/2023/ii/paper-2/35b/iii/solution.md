<h1 id="35b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The exact energies and mixing coefficients contain

$$
\sqrt{\frac{\Delta^2}{4}+\lambda^2}.
$$

As a function of complex $\lambda$, its nearest [branch points](../../../../../../branch-point.md) to the origin occur when its argument vanishes:

$$
\lambda=\pm\frac{i\Delta}{2}.
$$

Their distance from the expansion point $\lambda=0$ determines the [radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{R=\frac{\Delta}{2}=\frac{E_+-E_-}{2}.}
$$

The real-axis perturbation series converges for $|\lambda|<(E_+-E_-)/2$.
