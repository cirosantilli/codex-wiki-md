<h1 id="13e/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put

$$
D=\mathbb C\setminus\bigl((-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty)\bigr).
$$

This slit [complex plane](../../../../../../../complex-plane.md) is a [simply connected domain](../../../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md). On $D$ there is one branch of $\sqrt{1-z^2}$ that equals $1$ at zero, and its reciprocal is [holomorphic](../../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md). The [primitive of a holomorphic function on a simply connected domain](../../../../../../../primitive-of-a-holomorphic-function-on-a-simply-connected-domain.md) therefore makes the integral from zero path-independent, so $G$ is single-valued.

For unrestricted paths, going once around either [branch point](../../../../../../../branch-point.md) changes the sign of the square root. The resulting [analytic continuation](../../../../../../../analytic-continuation.md) therefore need not return to the original germ, and the value of $g(z)$ can depend on the path.
