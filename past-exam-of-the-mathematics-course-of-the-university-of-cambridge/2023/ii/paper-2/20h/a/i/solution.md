↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [trace form of a number field](../../../../../../../trace-form-of-a-number-field.md) is

$$
B(x,y)=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(xy).
$$

It is bilinear because the [field trace](../../../../../../../field-trace.md) is linear. If $x\ne0$, choose $y=x^{-1}$. Then

$$
B(x,y)=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(1)
=[K:\mathbb Q]\ne0.
$$

**Thus no nonzero $x$ lies in the radical, and $B$ is a [nondegenerate bilinear form](../../../../../../../nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md). This is the [nondegeneracy of the trace form of a number field](../../../../../../../nondegeneracy-of-the-trace-form-of-a-number-field.md).**
