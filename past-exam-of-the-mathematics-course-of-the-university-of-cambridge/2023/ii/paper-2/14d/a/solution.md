<h1 id="14d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Because the three positive gaps make one circuit,

$$
\boxed{\alpha+\beta+\gamma=2\pi}.
$$

They describe only relative positions. The missing [degree of freedom](../../../../../../degree-of-freedom.md) is the collective rigid rotation of all three particles around the circle, represented by the mean angular coordinate $\Phi$ in [collective rotation and gap coordinates on a circle](../../../../../../collective-rotation-and-gap-coordinates-on-a-circle.md).
