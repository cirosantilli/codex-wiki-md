<h1 id="14d/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Permuting the gaps, or equivalently relabelling the particles, leaves both quadratic forms invariant. The three-dimensional [permutation representation](../../../../../../permutation-representation.md) splits as the rigid-rotation line spanned by $(1,1,1)$ and the two-dimensional sum-zero [standard representation of the symmetric group](../../../../../../standard-representation-of-the-symmetric-group.md).

The two displayed vibrational vectors merely choose a basis of that sum-zero plane. A permutation generally mixes them, but their common frequency leaves the whole plane invariant. This is precisely the [degenerate normal modes from permutation symmetry](../../../../../../degenerate-normal-modes-from-permutation-symmetry.md) required by the physical symmetry.
