Writing $\omega_N=e^{2\pi i/N}$, the [quantum Fourier transform](../../../../../../quantum-fourier-transform.md) acts on a computational-basis state as

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{QFT}_N|x\rangle
=\frac1{\sqrt N}\sum_{y=0}^{N-1}\omega_N^{xy}|y\rangle.
}
$$
