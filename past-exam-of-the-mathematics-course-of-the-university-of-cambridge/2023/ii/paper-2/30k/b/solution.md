<h1 id="30k/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The increments of $Y_n=M_{n\wedge T}$ satisfy

$$
Y_n-Y_{n-1}
=\mathbf1_{\{T\geq n\}}(M_n-M_{n-1}).
$$

Since $\{T\geq n\}=\{T>n-1\}\in\mathcal F_{n-1}$, the indicator is predictable. Part (a), applied to this bounded integrand, shows that $Y$ is a martingale. Equivalently, this is the [stopped martingale](../../../../../../stopped-martingale.md) theorem.
