<h1 id="30k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The process is adapted because $X_0$ is $\mathcal F_0$-measurable, $A_k$ is $\mathcal F_{k-1}$-measurable, and $M_k-M_{k-1}$ is $\mathcal F_k$-measurable. Using the [predictability](../../../../../../predictable-process.md) of $A_n$ and the martingale increment property,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E[X_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]
&=X_{n-1}+A_n\mathbb E[M_n-M_{n-1}\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]\\
&=X_{n-1}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The assumed integrability therefore proves that $X$ is the [martingale transform](../../../../../../martingale-transform.md) of $M$ by $A$ and is a martingale.
