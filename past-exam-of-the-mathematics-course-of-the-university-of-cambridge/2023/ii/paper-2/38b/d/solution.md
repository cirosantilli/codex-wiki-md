<h1 id="38b/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Contracting the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) gives the nonzero components of the [Ricci tensor](../../../../../../ricci-tensor.md):

$$
R_{rr}=R^\phi{}_{r\phi r}=-1,
\qquad
R_{\phi\phi}=R^r{}_{\phi r\phi}=-\sinh^2r.
$$

The [Ricci scalar](../../../../../../ricci-scalar.md) is the contraction with the [inverse metric](../../../../../../inverse-metric.md). Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
R
&=g^{rr}R_{rr}+g^{\phi\phi}R_{\phi\phi}\\
&=\frac1{a^2}(-1)
+\frac1{a^2\sinh^2r}(-\sinh^2r)\\
&=\boxed{-\frac2{a^2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

It is independent of both coordinates, so the [hyperbolic plane](../../../../../../hyperbolic-plane.md) has constant negative scalar curvature.
