<h1 id="38b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For coordinates $x^\alpha$, the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) of the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) are

$$
\Gamma^\alpha_{\beta\gamma}
=\frac12g^{\alpha\delta}
\left(
\partial_\beta g_{\delta\gamma}
+\partial_\gamma g_{\delta\beta}
-\partial_\delta g_{\beta\gamma}
\right),
$$

where $g^{\alpha\delta}$ is the [inverse metric](../../../../../../inverse-metric.md).

For the [two-dimensional hyperbolic metric in polar coordinates](../../../../../../two-dimensional-hyperbolic-metric-in-polar-coordinates.md),

$$
g_{rr}=a^2,
\qquad
g_{\phi\phi}=a^2\sinh^2r,
\qquad
g^{rr}=a^{-2},
\qquad
g^{\phi\phi}=\frac1{a^2\sinh^2r}.
$$

Only $g_{\phi\phi}$ has a nonzero derivative, namely

$$
\partial_r g_{\phi\phi}=2a^2\sinh r\cosh r.
$$

It follows that the nonzero connection coefficients are

$$
\boxed{\Gamma^r_{\phi\phi}=-\sinh r\cosh r},
\qquad
\boxed{\Gamma^\phi_{r\phi}=\Gamma^\phi_{\phi r}=\coth r}.
$$
