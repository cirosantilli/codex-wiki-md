<h1 id="38b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using the stated convention for the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md), the first independent component is

$$
\begin{aligned}
R^r{}_{\phi r\phi}
&=\partial_r\Gamma^r_{\phi\phi}
-\Gamma^\phi_{\phi r}\Gamma^r_{\phi\phi}\\
&=-(\cosh^2r+\sinh^2r)+\cosh^2r\\
&=\boxed{-\sinh^2r}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The other is

$$
\begin{aligned}
R^\phi{}_{r\phi r}
&=-\partial_r\Gamma^\phi_{r\phi}
-\Gamma^\phi_{r\phi}\Gamma^\phi_{\phi r}\\
&=\operatorname{csch}^2r-\coth^2r\\
&=\boxed{-1},
\end{aligned}
$$

using the [hyperbolic Pythagorean identity](../../../../../../hyperbolic-pythagorean-identity.md) $\coth^2r-\operatorname{csch}^2r=1$. Antisymmetry in the final two indices supplies the components with those indices reversed. These are the [curvature of the two-dimensional hyperbolic polar metric](../../../../../../curvature-of-the-two-dimensional-hyperbolic-polar-metric.md) components.
