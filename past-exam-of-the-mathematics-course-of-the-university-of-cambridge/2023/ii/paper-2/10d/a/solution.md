<h1 id="10d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expanding either [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) and using $\langle0|1\rangle_B=0$ removes the cross terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\Phi^\pm|Q\otimes I|\Phi^\pm\rangle
&=\frac12(\langle00|\pm\langle11|)(Q\otimes I)(|00\rangle\pm|11\rangle)\\
&=\frac12\bigl(\langle0|Q|0\rangle+\langle1|Q|1\rangle\bigr)\\
&=\frac12\operatorname{tr}Q.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Thus the two expectation values agree. Equivalently, both states have the same [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) $I/2$ on qubit $A$; the positivity of $Q$ makes it a possible measurement effect but is not needed for the algebraic equality.**
