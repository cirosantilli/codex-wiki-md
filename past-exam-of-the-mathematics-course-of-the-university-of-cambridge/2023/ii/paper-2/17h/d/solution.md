<h1 id="17h/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the infinite family

$$
G_n=K_n,\qquad n\geq2.
$$

Each [complete graph](../../../../../../complete-graph.md) is connected and has [graph diameter](../../../../../../graph-diameter.md) one. If $A_n$ is its [adjacency matrix](../../../../../../adjacency-matrix.md), then

$$
A_n=J-I
$$

and direct multiplication gives the [adjacency-matrix quadratic relation for a complete graph](../../../../../../adjacency-matrix-quadratic-relation-for-a-complete-graph.md)

$$
A_n^2=(n-2)A_n+(n-1)I.
$$

Consequently

$$
A_n^2-(n-2)A_n-(n-1)I=0,
$$

so $I,A_n,A_n^2$, which are precisely the powers through $d(G_n)+1=2$, are linearly dependent for every $n\geq2$.
