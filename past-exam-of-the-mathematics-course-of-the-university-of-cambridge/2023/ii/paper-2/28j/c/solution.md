**False.** Take the chain from part (b) with $\lambda=1$. It is irreducible and nonexplosive, and it has the invariant distribution

$$
\pi_i=2^{-i-1}.
$$

Hence every state is a [positive recurrent state](../../../../../../positive-recurrent-state.md) for the continuous-time chain.

Its jump chain is the reflected simple symmetric random walk on $\mathbb N$. This chain is recurrent but has only the infinite invariant measure

$$
\mu_0=1,
\qquad
\mu_i=2\quad(i\geq1),
$$

so every state is a [null recurrent state](../../../../../../null-recurrent-state.md). Equivalently, the [invariant-measure transfer between a jump chain and a CTMC](../../../../../../invariant-measure-transfer-between-a-jump-chain-and-a-ctmc.md) divides this infinite jump-chain measure by the rapidly growing holding rates and produces the summable continuous-time invariant measure. Thus [fast holding rates create continuous-time positive recurrence](../../../../../../fast-holding-rates-create-continuous-time-positive-recurrence.md), and positive recurrence need not pass from a continuous-time chain to its jump chain.
