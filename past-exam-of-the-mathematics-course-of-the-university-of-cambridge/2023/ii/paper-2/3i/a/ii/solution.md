<h1 id="3i/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [Bernoulli source](../../../../../../../bernoulli-source.md), independence makes entropy additive, so

$$
H(X_1,\ldots,X_n)=\sum_{j=1}^nH(X_j)=nH(X_1).
$$

Its [information rate](../../../../../../../information-rate.md) is therefore $H(X_1)$. If $c$ is an [optimal prefix code](../../../../../../../optimal-prefix-code.md) and $L(c)=\mathbb E|c(X_1)|$ is its [expected codeword length](../../../../../../../expected-codeword-length.md), the [Shannon noiseless coding theorem](../../../../../../../shannon-s-source-coding-theorem.md) gives

$$
H(X_1)\leq L(c)<H(X_1)+1.
$$

**Consequently the information rate is at most the expected word length of the optimal code.**
