<h1 id="37a/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The condition $L\ll na$ implies $|\tau a|\ll1$. The [small-argument expansion of the hyperbolic tangent](../../../../../../small-argument-expansion-of-the-hyperbolic-tangent.md) gives $\tanh x=x+O(x^3)$, so

$$
\frac{L}{na}\simeq\tau a.
$$

Using $f=k_BT\tau$ yields the [entropic Hooke law for a one-dimensional chain](../../../../../../entropic-hooke-law-for-a-one-dimensional-chain.md)

$$
\boxed{f\simeq\frac{k_BT}{na^2}L}.
$$

Thus the effective spring constant is $k_BT/(na^2)$.

At fixed tension, the exact extension is

$$
L=na\tanh\!\left(\frac{fa}{k_BT}\right).
$$

Increasing the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) decreases the positive argument of the [hyperbolic tangent](../../../../../../hyperbolic-tangent.md), so the chain contracts. In the small-extension regime this becomes

$$
\boxed{L\simeq\frac{na^2f}{k_BT}},
$$

which is inversely proportional to $T$.
