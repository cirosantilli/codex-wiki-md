<h1 id="37a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The extension of the [one-dimensional freely jointed chain](../../../../../../one-dimensional-freely-jointed-chain.md) is

$$
l=(n_+-n_-)a=(2n_+-n)a.
$$

As the [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) $n_+$ runs from $0$ to $n$, the possible extensions are therefore

$$
\boxed{l=-na,-(n-2)a,\ldots,(n-2)a,na.}
$$

For fixed $n_+$ and $n_-=n-n_+$, choosing which $n_+$ of the $n$ links point in the positive direction determines the [microstate](../../../../../../microstate.md). The [state degeneracy](../../../../../../state-degeneracy.md) is consequently the [binomial coefficient](../../../../../../binomial-coefficient.md)

$$
\boxed{g(l)=\binom n{n_+}=\frac{n!}{n_+!n_-!}.}
$$
