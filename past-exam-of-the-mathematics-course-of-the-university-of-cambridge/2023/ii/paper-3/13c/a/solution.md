<h1 id="13c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Setting $\epsilon=0$ makes the inhibitor equation quasistatic:

$$
0=v_{xx}+u-v.
$$

Taking the spatial [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) and using the [Fourier transform of a derivative](../../../../../../fourier-transform-of-a-derivative.md) gives

$$
0=-k^2\widehat v+\widehat u-\widehat v.
$$

Therefore the [fast-inhibitor elimination in a reaction-diffusion system](../../../../../../fast-inhibitor-elimination-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md) is

$$
\boxed{\widehat v(k,t)=\frac{\widehat u(k,t)}{1+k^2}.}
$$
