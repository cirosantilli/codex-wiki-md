<h1 id="13c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $y=k^2\geq0$. At the threshold of a [Turing instability](../../../../../../turing-instability.md), the maximum of

$$
\sigma(y)=-Dy-r+\rho\frac{y}{1+y}
$$

touches zero. Hence

$$
\sigma(y_c)=0,
\qquad
\frac{d\sigma}{dy}(y_c)
=-D+\frac{\rho}{(1+y_c)^2}=0.
$$

The second equation gives $\rho=D(1+y_c)^2$. Substitution in the first gives

$$
0=-Dy_c-r+Dy_c(1+y_c)=Dy_c^2-r.
$$

Therefore

$$
y_c=\sqrt{\frac rD},
\qquad
\boxed{k_c=\left(\frac rD\right)^{1/4}},
$$

and

$$
\boxed{
\rho_c(r)=D\left(1+\sqrt{\frac rD}\right)^2
=(\sqrt r+\sqrt D)^2.}
$$
