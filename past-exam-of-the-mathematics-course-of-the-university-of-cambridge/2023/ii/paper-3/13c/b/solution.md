<h1 id="13c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $f(0)=0$ and $f'(0)=-r$, the [linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md) is

$$
f(u)=-ru+O(u^2).
$$

For a spatial [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) of the perturbation, part a gives

$$
\frac{\partial\widehat u}{\partial t}
=\left[
-Dk^2-r+\rho\left(1-\frac1{1+k^2}\right)
\right]\widehat u.
$$

Thus $\widehat u(k,t)\propto e^{\sigma(k)t}$ with the [dispersion relation after fast-inhibitor elimination](../../../../../../dispersion-relation-after-fast-inhibitor-elimination.md)

$$
\boxed{\sigma(k)=-Dk^2-r+\rho\frac{k^2}{1+k^2}.}
$$
