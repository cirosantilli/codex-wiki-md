<h1 id="30e/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For fixed $n$, the right-hand side of the quantization equation tends to zero with $\varepsilon$. If

$$
\pi\varepsilon\left(n+\frac12\right)<f(1)=\frac{16}{3},
$$

the unique energy satisfies $E_n<1$, so the first branch of the [action integral for a linear-to-square-root potential](../../../../../../action-integral-for-a-linear-to-square-root-potential.md) applies exactly. Solving

$$
\frac{16}{3}E_n^{3/2}
=\pi\varepsilon\left(n+\frac12\right)
$$

gives

$$
\boxed{
E_n=\left[
\frac{3\pi\varepsilon}{16}
\left(n+\frac12\right)
\right]^{2/3}.}
$$

Thus this formula is valid whenever

$$
\boxed{
\varepsilon<\frac{16}{3\pi(n+1/2)}.}
$$
