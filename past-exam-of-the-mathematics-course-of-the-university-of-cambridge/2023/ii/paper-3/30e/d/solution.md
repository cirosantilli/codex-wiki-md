<h1 id="30e/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $E\to\infty$, the outer part of the [action integral for a linear-to-square-root potential](../../../../../../action-integral-for-a-linear-to-square-root-potential.md) dominates:

$$
f(E)
=\frac{16}{15}(E-1)^{5/2}
+O(E^{3/2})
\sim\frac{16}{15}E^{5/2}.
$$

The [WKB quantization condition](../../../../../../wkb-quantization-condition.md) therefore gives

$$
\frac{16}{15}E_n^{5/2}
\sim\pi\varepsilon n.
$$

Taking the power $2/5$ yields the [asymptotic equivalence](../../../../../../asymptotic-equivalence.md)

$$
\boxed{
E_n\sim
\left(\frac{15\pi\varepsilon}{16}\right)^{2/5}n^{2/5}.}
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{
c=\left(\frac{15\pi\varepsilon}{16}\right)^{2/5},
\qquad \alpha=\frac25.}
$$
