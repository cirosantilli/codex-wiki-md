<h1 id="30e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The endpoints are the two [classical turning points](../../../../../../classical-turning-point.md), determined by $V(x)=E$. Since the potential is even, they are symmetric. For $0\leq E\leq1$, they lie in the linear part of the potential and satisfy $|x|/4=E$; for $E\geq1$, they lie in the outer part and satisfy $\sqrt{|x|}-1=E$. Hence

$$
\boxed{
(a,b)=
\begin{cases}
(-4E,4E),&0\leq E\leq1,\\
(-(E+1)^2,(E+1)^2),&E\geq1.
\end{cases}}
$$

The [two-turning-point WKB quantization condition](../../../../../../two-turning-point-wkb-quantization-condition.md) uses

$$
\boxed{n=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$
