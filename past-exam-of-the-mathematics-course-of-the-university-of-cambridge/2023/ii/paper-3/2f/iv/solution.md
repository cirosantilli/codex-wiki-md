↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Statement (iv) is true. Apply the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](../../../../../../weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md) separately to the real and imaginary parts of the [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $f$ on the [compact interval](../../../../../../compact-space.md) $[0,1]$. Combining the two real polynomial sequences gives complex polynomials converging [uniformly](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) to $f$ on $I$.
