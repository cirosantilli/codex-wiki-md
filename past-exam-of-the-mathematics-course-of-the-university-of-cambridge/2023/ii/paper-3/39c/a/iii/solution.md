<h1 id="39c/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [wave crest](../../../../../../../wave-crest.md) moves at the phase velocity, while the envelope of a narrow [wave packet](../../../../../../../wave-packet.md) moves at the group velocity. Since $c_g>c_p$ for every nonzero wavenumber, the crests move more slowly than the packet.
