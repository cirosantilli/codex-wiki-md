<h1 id="35a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an ideal gas of identical [bosons](../../../../../../boson.md), the [Bose-Einstein distribution](../../../../../../bose-einstein-distribution.md) gives the mean occupation of a discrete one-particle state $r$ as

$$
\boxed{n_r=\frac1{e^{\beta(E_r-\mu)}-1}},
\qquad \beta=\frac1{k_BT},
$$

where $\mu$ is the [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md). If the spectrum is sufficiently dense to use a [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md) $g(E)$, the discrete [sum](../../../../../../sum.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{
N=\int_0^\infty
\frac{g(E)}{e^{\beta(E-\mu)}-1}\,dE,
\qquad
E_{\rm tot}=\int_0^\infty
\frac{E g(E)}{e^{\beta(E-\mu)}-1}\,dE.}
$$
