<h1 id="35a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use periodic boundary conditions on the square crystal. Each allowed [wavevector](../../../../../../../wavevector.md) occupies area $(2\pi)^2/A$ in two-dimensional $k$-space. Including the two [phonon](../../../../../../../phonon.md) polarizations, the number of modes in the disk $|k|\leq K$ is

$$
\mathcal N(K)
=2\frac{A}{(2\pi)^2}\pi K^2
=\frac{AK^2}{2\pi}.
$$

The [phonon dispersion relation](../../../../../../../phonon-dispersion-relation.md) $\omega=C|k|^\alpha$ gives $K=(\omega/C)^{1/\alpha}$, so

$$
\mathcal N(\omega)=\frac{A}{2\pi}
\left(\frac\omega C\right)^{2/\alpha}.
$$

Differentiating the cumulative mode count produces the [density of states for a two-dimensional power-law phonon dispersion](../../../../../../../density-of-states-for-a-two-dimensional-power-law-phonon-dispersion.md):

$$
\boxed{
g(\omega)=B\omega^b,
\qquad
B=\frac{A}{\pi\alpha C^{2/\alpha}},
\qquad
b=\frac2\alpha-1.}
$$
