<h1 id="35a/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $\alpha=1/2$, the preceding [density of states](../../../../../../../density-of-states.md) has

$$
b=3,
\qquad
B=\frac{2A}{\pi C^4}.
$$

When $T\gg T_D$, the [Bose-Einstein distribution](../../../../../../../bose-einstein-distribution.md) has the classical approximation

$$
\frac{\hbar\omega}{e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1}
\sim k_BT.
$$

Each of the $2n$ modes therefore contributes $k_BT$ to the energy, so the [Dulong-Petit law](../../../../../../../dulong-petit-law.md) gives

$$
E_{\rm tot}\sim2nk_BT,
\qquad
\boxed{C_V\sim2nk_B}.
$$

When $T\ll T_D$, the upper integration limit tends to infinity. The [Bose integral](../../../../../../../bose-integral.md)

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^4}{e^x-1}\,dx
=\Gamma(5)\zeta(5)=24\zeta(5)
$$

then yields

$$
E_{\rm tot}
\sim\frac{24B\zeta(5)k_B^5}{\hbar^4}T^5.
$$

Hence the [heat capacity at constant volume](../../../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) is

$$
\boxed{
C_V\sim
\frac{120B\zeta(5)k_B^5}{\hbar^4}T^4
=\frac{240A\zeta(5)k_B^5}{\pi C^4\hbar^4}T^4,}
$$

so $d=4$. The high-temperature constant is the classical [equipartition theorem](../../../../../../../equipartition-theorem.md) result, while the low-temperature power law makes $C_V\to0$ as required by the [Third law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../../third-law-of-thermodynamics.md).
