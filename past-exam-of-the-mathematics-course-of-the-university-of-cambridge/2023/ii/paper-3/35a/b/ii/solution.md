The [Debye model](../../../../../../../debye-model.md) fixes its cutoff by requiring the continuum to contain the $2n$ vibrational degrees of freedom:

$$
2n=\int_0^{\omega_D}g(\omega)\,d\omega
=\frac{A}{2\pi}
\left(\frac{\omega_D}{C}\right)^{2/\alpha}.
$$

Therefore the [Debye frequency](../../../../../../../debye-frequency.md) and [Debye temperature](../../../../../../../debye-temperature.md) are

$$
\boxed{
\omega_D=C\left(\frac{4\pi n}{A}\right)^{\alpha/2},
\qquad
T_D=\frac{\hbar\omega_D}{k_B}.}
$$
