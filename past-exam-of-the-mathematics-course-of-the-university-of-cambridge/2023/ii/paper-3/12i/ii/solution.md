<h1 id="12i/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $N=|Q|$. The [pumping lemma for regular languages](../../../../../../pumping-lemma-for-regular-languages.md) states that every $w\in L(D)$ with $|w|\geq N$ can be written

$$
w=xyz
$$

with

$$
|xy|\leq N,
\qquad
|y|\geq1,
\qquad
xy^iz\in L(D)\quad\text{for every }i\geq0.
$$

**Thus one may take the pumping number to be exactly the number $N$ of states of the given automaton, whether or not all of them are accessible.**
