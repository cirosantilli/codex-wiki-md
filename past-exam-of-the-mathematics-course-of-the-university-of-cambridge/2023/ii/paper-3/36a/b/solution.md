<h1 id="36a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put

$$
F(s)=s+\frac{R(s)}c,
\qquad
R(s)=|x-y(s)|,
\qquad
n(s)=\frac{x-y(s)}{R(s)}.
$$

Because $dR/ds=-n\cdot v$, differentiation gives

$$
F'(s)=1-\frac{n\cdot v}{c}.
$$

A massive charge has the [subluminal speed](../../../../../../subluminal-speed.md) $|v|<c$, so $F'(s)>0$. Hence $F$ is [strictly increasing](../../../../../../strictly-increasing-function.md), and $F(s)=t$ has at most one root. This is the [uniqueness of retarded time for a subluminal source](../../../../../../uniqueness-of-retarded-time-for-a-subluminal-source.md).
