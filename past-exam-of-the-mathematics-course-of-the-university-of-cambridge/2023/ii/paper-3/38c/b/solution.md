<h1 id="38c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The leading streamwise momentum equation in [lubrication theory](../../../../../../lubrication-theory.md) is

$$
0=-p_x+\mu u_{yy},
$$

with $p=p(x)$. The [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) is

$$
u(x,0)=U,\qquad u(x,h(x))=0.
$$

Twice integrating in $y$ gives the local [Couette-Poiseuille flow in a thin gap](../../../../../../couette-poiseuille-flow-in-a-thin-gap.md)

$$
\boxed{
u(x,y)=U\left(1-\frac yh\right)
+\frac{p_x}{2\mu}y(y-h).}
$$

Its volume flux per unit span is

$$
\boxed{
Q(x)=\int_0^hu\,dy
=\frac{Uh}{2}-\frac{h^3p_x}{12\mu}.}
$$
