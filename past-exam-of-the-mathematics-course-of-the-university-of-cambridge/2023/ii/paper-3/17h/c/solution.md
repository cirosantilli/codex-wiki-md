<h1 id="17h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Colour the vertices of $K_{(s-1)t}$ in $s-1$ groups of size $t$. Colour edges within groups blue and edges between groups red. The red graph is complete $(s-1)$-partite, so it has no red $K_s$. Every blue component has only $t$ vertices, so it has no [path](../../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md) of length $t$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{r(K_s,P_t)\geq(s-1)t+1.}
$$
