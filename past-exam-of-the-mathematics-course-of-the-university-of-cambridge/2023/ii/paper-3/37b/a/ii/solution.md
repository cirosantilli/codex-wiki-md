<h1 id="37b/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the flat-spacetime limit $M=0$, the orbit equation is the [harmonic oscillator equation](../../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md)

$$
y''+y=0.
$$

Its general solution is $y=A\cos\phi+B\sin\phi$. The conditions $r\to\infty$, hence $y\to0$, at $\phi=0$ and $\phi=\pi$ set $A=0$. Choosing $r=b$ at closest approach $\phi=\pi/2$ fixes $B=1/b$, so

$$
\boxed{y_0(\phi)=\frac{\sin\phi}{b},\qquad
r(\phi)=\frac{b}{\sin\phi}.}
$$

This is a straight line at perpendicular distance $b$ from the origin in [polar coordinates](../../../../../../../polar-coordinates.md).
