<h1 id="37b/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The radial first integral for an equatorial [null geodesic](../../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) in [Schwarzschild spacetime](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) is

$$
\dot r^2+\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right)\frac{L^2}{r^2}=E^2.
$$

Since $L=r^2\dot\phi$, regarding $r$ as a function of $\phi$ gives

$$
\dot r=\frac{dr}{d\phi}\dot\phi
=\frac{L}{r^2}\frac{dr}{d\phi}.
$$

Set $y=1/r$. Then $dr/d\phi=-y'/y^2$ and therefore $\dot r=-Ly'$. Division by $L^2$ gives the [equatorial null orbit in Schwarzschild spacetime](../../../../../../../equatorial-null-orbit-in-schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

$$
\boxed{(y')^2+y^2-2My^3=\frac{E^2}{L^2}=\frac1{b^2},}
$$

where $b=L/E$ is the [impact parameter](../../../../../../../impact-parameter.md). Differentiating with respect to $\phi$ gives

$$
2y'(y''+y-3My^2)=0.
$$

By continuity this yields

$$
\boxed{y''+y=3My^2.}
$$
