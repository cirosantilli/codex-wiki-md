<h1 id="37b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An affinely parameterized [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) $x^\mu(\lambda)$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{
\frac{d^2x^\mu}{d\lambda^2}
+\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}
\frac{dx^\alpha}{d\lambda}
\frac{dx^\beta}{d\lambda}=0.}
$$

For the [conformally flat metric](../../../../../../conformally-flat-metric.md) $g_{\mu\nu}=\varphi^2\eta_{\mu\nu}$, its [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) are

$$
\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}
=\delta^\mu_\alpha\partial_\beta\log\varphi
+\delta^\mu_\beta\partial_\alpha\log\varphi
-\eta_{\alpha\beta}\partial^\mu\log\varphi.
$$

Let $u^\mu=dx^\mu/d\lambda$. Along a [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md), $\eta_{\alpha\beta}u^\alpha u^\beta=0$, so the equation reduces to

$$
\frac{du^\mu}{d\lambda}
+2\frac{d\log\varphi}{d\lambda}u^\mu=0.
$$

The acceleration is parallel to the tangent. A change of parameter removes it, leaving a straight null line of the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md). Thus conformal rescaling changes the affine parameter but not the unparametrized light ray.

Consequently [Nordstrom theory of gravitation](../../../../../../nordstrom-s-theory-of-gravitation.md) predicts

$$
\boxed{\delta\phi=0}
$$

for light passing a star. This contradicts the observed nonzero deflection and the successful general-relativistic value $4GM/(bc^2)$.
