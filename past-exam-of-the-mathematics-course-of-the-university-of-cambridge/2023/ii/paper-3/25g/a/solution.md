<h1 id="25g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose the [orientation of a surface](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-surface.md) represented by a smooth [Gauss map](../../../../../../gauss-map.md) $N:S\to\mathbb S^2$. The [shape operator](../../../../../../shape-operator.md) at $p$ is

$$
S_p=-dN_p:T_pS\longrightarrow T_pS.
$$

If its [principal curvatures](../../../../../../principal-curvature.md) are $k_1,k_2$, then by definition

$$
\boxed{K(p)=\det S_p=k_1k_2,
\qquad
H(p)=\frac12\operatorname{tr}S_p=\frac{k_1+k_2}{2}.}
$$

Let $E(x)=Ax+b$ be a [proper Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space](../../../../../../proper-euclidean-motion-of-euclidean-three-space.md), so $A\in SO(3)$. Orient $\widetilde S=E(S)$ by transporting the normal:

$$
\widetilde N(E(p))=AN(p).
$$

The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives the orthogonal conjugacy

$$
\widetilde S_{E(p)}
=A S_pA^{-1}.
$$

The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) and [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) are invariant under [similarity](../../../../../../matrix-similarity.md), so the [Euclidean invariance of the shape operator](../../../../../../euclidean-invariance-of-the-shape-operator.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\widetilde K(E(p))=K(p),
\qquad
\widetilde H(E(p))=H(p).}
$$

If the surface orientation is reversed, then $N$ and $S_p$ are multiplied by $-1$. Therefore the [orientation reversal of surface curvature](../../../../../../orientation-reversal-of-surface-curvature.md) says

$$
\boxed{K\text{ is independent of orientation},
\qquad H\text{ changes sign}.}
$$
