↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In [characteristic](../../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) two, the [formal derivative in positive characteristic](../../../../../../../formal-derivative-in-positive-characteristic.md) of

$$
f(T)=T^3+aT+b
$$

is

$$
f'(T)=T^2+a.
$$

A [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is separable exactly when it and its derivative have no common root. If $r$ is a root of $f'$, then

$$
f(r)=r^3+ar+b=r(r^2+a)+b=b.
$$

**Thus $f$ and $f'$ have a common root exactly when $b=0$. Therefore $f$ is a [separable polynomial](../../../../../../../separable-polynomial.md) if and only if $b\ne0$.**
