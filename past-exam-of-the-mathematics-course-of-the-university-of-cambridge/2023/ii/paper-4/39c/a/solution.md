<h1 id="39c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The one-dimensional [mass flux](../../../../../../mass-flux.md) and [momentum flux](../../../../../../momentum-flux.md) are respectively

$$
J_m=\rho u,\qquad J_p=p+\rho u^2.
$$

In a steady flow, the [conservation laws](../../../../../../conservation-law.md) make both the mass flux and the given [energy flux](../../../../../../energy-flux.md)

$$
J_E=u\left(p+W+\frac12\rho u^2\right)
$$

constant. Wherever $J_m\ne0$, their ratio is therefore constant:

$$
\frac{J_E}{J_m}
=\frac{p+W}{\rho}+\frac12u^2
=\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}\frac p\rho+\frac12u^2
=C,
$$

where $W=p/(\gamma-1)$ is the [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy.md) per unit volume of the [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md). This is the required steady-flow energy integral.
