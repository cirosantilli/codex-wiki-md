<h1 id="20h/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$, put

$$
\omega=\frac{-1+\sqrt{-3}}2.
$$

Then the [ring of integers of a quadratic field](../../../../../../../ring-of-integers-of-a-quadratic-field.md) is $\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\omega]$, and

$$
N(a+b\omega)=a^2-ab+b^2.
$$

A unit must have norm $1$. Multiplying by four gives

$$
(2a-b)^2+3b^2=4,
$$

so $b\in\{0,\pm1\}$, and checking these cases gives exactly

$$
\mathcal O_K^\times
=\{\mathord\pm1,\mathord\pm\omega,\mathord\pm\omega^2\}
\cong C_6.
$$

These are precisely the sixth [roots of unity](../../../../../../../root-of-unity.md), proving the description of the [units of Q of square root minus three](../../../../../../../units-of-q-of-square-root-minus-three.md).
