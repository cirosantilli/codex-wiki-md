<h1 id="36a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Substitution of the sinusoidal [plane waves](../../../../../../plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) into [Faraday's law](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives

$$
\mathbf k\times\mathbf e=\omega\mathbf b.
$$

The source-free [Ampère-Maxwell equation](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md), with $\mathbf H=\mathbf B/\mu$ and $D_i=\varepsilon_{ij}E_j$, gives

$$
\mathbf k\times\frac{\mathbf b}{\mu}
=-\omega\boldsymbol\varepsilon\mathbf e.
$$

Eliminating $\mathbf b=(\mathbf k\times\mathbf e)/\omega$ yields the wave equation for a [plane wave in an anisotropic dielectric](../../../../../../plane-wave-in-an-anisotropic-dielectric.md):

$$
\boxed{
[\mathbf k\times(\mathbf k\times\mathbf e)]_i
+\omega^2\mu\varepsilon_{ij}e_j=0}.
$$
