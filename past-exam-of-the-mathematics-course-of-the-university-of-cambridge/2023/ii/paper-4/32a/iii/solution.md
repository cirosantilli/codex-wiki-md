<h1 id="32a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $\lambda>1$, the new fixed point in $[0,1]$ is

$$
x^*=\sqrt{1-\frac1\lambda}.
$$

The origin has multiplier $F_x(0,\lambda)=\lambda>1$ and is therefore unstable. At the new fixed point,

$$
F_x(x^*,\lambda)
=\lambda\left(1-3\left(1-\frac1\lambda\right)\right)
=3-2\lambda.
$$

Hence

$$
|F_x(x^*,\lambda)|<1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
1<\lambda<2.
$$

The point $x^*$ is stable in that interval and unstable for $\lambda>2$. At $\lambda=2$ its multiplier is $-1$, the threshold for a [period-doubling bifurcation](../../../../../../period-doubling-bifurcation.md).
