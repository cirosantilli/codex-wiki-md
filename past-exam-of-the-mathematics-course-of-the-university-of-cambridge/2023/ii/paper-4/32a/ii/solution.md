<h1 id="32a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point-stability-for-an-iteration.md) satisfies

$$
x=\lambda x(1-x^2),
$$

so either $x=0$ or $x^2=1-1/\lambda$. When $0<\lambda<1$, the latter equation has no real solution, and the origin is the only fixed point. Since

$$
F_x(x,\lambda)=\lambda(1-3x^2),
\qquad
F_x(0,\lambda)=\lambda,
$$

the criterion for [fixed point stability for an iteration](../../../../../../fixed-point-stability-for-an-iteration.md) shows that the origin is stable for $\lambda<1$.
