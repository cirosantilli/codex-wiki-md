↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the tensor-product Hilbert space, [addition of angular momentum](../../../../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md) gives

$$
\mathbf J=\mathbf J_1\otimes I_2+I_1\otimes\mathbf J_2.
$$

Suppressing the identity operators, its components and [ladder operators](../../../../../../../spin-ladder-operator.md) are

$$
J_x=J_{1x}+J_{2x},
\quad
J_y=J_{1y}+J_{2y},
\quad
J_z=J_{1z}+J_{2z},
$$



$$
J_\pm=J_x\pm iJ_y=J_{1\pm}+J_{2\pm}.
$$
