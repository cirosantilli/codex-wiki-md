<h1 id="38c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The unperturbed [fluid wake](../../../../../../wake-physics.md) is the horizontal strip $-h<y<h$: the fluid is stationary there and moves to the right with [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $U\mathbf e_x$ above and below it. Its two [shear layers](../../../../../../shear-layer.md) are the straight lines $y=\pm h$. After the symmetric disturbance, draw the upper layer at $y=h+\eta(x,t)$ and the lower one as its reflection at $y=-h-\eta(x,t)$, so the local wake width is $2(h+\eta)$.
