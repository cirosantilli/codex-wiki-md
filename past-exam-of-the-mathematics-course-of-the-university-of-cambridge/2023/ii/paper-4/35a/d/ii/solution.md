<h1 id="35a/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Heat enters only during the constant-pressure step, so the [heat capacity at constant pressure](../../../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) gives

$$
Q_1=C_p(T_C-T_B).
$$

The positive magnitude of the heat rejected during constant-volume cooling is

$$
Q_2=C_V(T_D-T_A).
$$

Because the working gas returns to its initial state, the [thermodynamic cycle](../../../../../../../thermodynamic-cycle.md) has zero net change in internal energy and $W=Q_1-Q_2$. Its [thermal efficiency](../../../../../../../thermal-efficiency.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{
\eta=\frac{W}{Q_1}
=1-\frac{C_V(T_D-T_A)}{C_p(T_C-T_B)}
=1-\frac1\gamma\frac{T_D-T_A}{T_C-T_B}
}.
$$
