<h1 id="35a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [adiabatic process](../../../../../../adiabatic-process.md) has no heat transfer, $\delta Q=0$. With work done by the gas taken as positive, the [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) gives

$$
dE=\delta Q-p\,dV=-p\,dV.
$$

Using $dE=C_VdT$ and $p=Nk_BT/V$,

$$
C_V\frac{dT}{T}=-Nk_B\frac{dV}{V}.
$$

Integration, followed by the [Mayer relation](../../../../../../mayer-s-relation.md) $Nk_B/C_V=\gamma-1$, yields

$$
TV^{\gamma-1}=\text{constant}.
$$

Eliminating $T$ with the equation of state gives the [reversible ideal-gas adiabat](../../../../../../reversible-ideal-gas-adiabat.md)

$$
\boxed{pV^\gamma=\text{constant}},
\qquad \gamma=\frac{C_p}{C_V}.
$$
