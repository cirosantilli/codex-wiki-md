For $N$ particles, the [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) equation of state is

$$
\boxed{pV=Nk_BT}.
$$

The [internal energy of an ideal gas](../../../../../../internal-energy-of-an-ideal-gas.md) depends only on temperature and obeys

$$
dE=C_V\,dT.
$$

For constant heat capacity and a convenient choice of energy zero,

$$
\boxed{E=C_VT}.
$$
