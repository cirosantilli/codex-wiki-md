<h1 id="37b/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A suitable [Spatially flat FLRW metric](../../../../../../../spatially-flat-flrw-metric.md) is

$$
\boxed{ds^2=-c^2dt^2+a^2(t)(dx^2+dy^2+dz^2)}.
$$

Both source and detector are comoving, so [cosmological redshift](../../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) stretches the wave period in proportion to the [scale factor](../../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md). If $f_e$ is the local source frequency at emission and $f_o$ is the frequency measured in Earth's local frame, then

$$
\boxed{
f_o=f_e\frac{a(t_e)}{a(t_o)}
=\frac{f_e}{1+z}},
\qquad
1+z=\frac{a(t_o)}{a(t_e)}.
$$

The same relation applies to gravitational waves propagating on the FLRW background in the geometric-optics regime.
