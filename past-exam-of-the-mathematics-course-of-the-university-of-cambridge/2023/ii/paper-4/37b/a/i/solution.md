↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Lorenz gauge in linearized gravity](../../../../../../../lorenz-gauge-in-linearized-gravity.md) gives

$$
0=\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}
=ik^\mu H_{\mu\nu}e^{ik\cdot x},
$$

so the symmetric amplitude is transverse:

$$
\boxed{k^\mu H_{\mu\nu}=0}.
$$

Under this condition the supplied linearized Einstein tensor reduces to

$$
G_{\mu\nu}=-\frac12\Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}.
$$

The vacuum [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) and the plane-wave ansatz then imply

$$
\boxed{k^\rho k_\rho=0}.
$$

**Thus the wavevector is null and the perturbation is a [plane gravitational wave in linearized gravity](../../../../../../../plane-gravitational-wave-in-linearized-gravity.md). Lorenz gauge alone does not require the trace of $H_{\mu\nu}$ to vanish; that is a further gauge choice.**
