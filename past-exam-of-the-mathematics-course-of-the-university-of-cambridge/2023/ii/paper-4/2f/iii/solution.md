The [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) for the closed unit disc $\overline D\subset\mathbb R^2$ states that every continuous map

$$
f:\overline D\to\overline D
$$

has a fixed point.
