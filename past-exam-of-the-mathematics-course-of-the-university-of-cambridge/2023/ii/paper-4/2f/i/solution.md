<h1 id="2f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let

$$
h(x)=f(x)-x.
$$

The function $h$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md), with

$$
h(0)=f(0)\geq0,
\qquad
h(1)=f(1)-1\leq0.
$$

The [intermediate value theorem](../../../../../../intermediate-value-theorem.md) gives $c\in[0,1]$ such that $h(c)=0$, and hence $f(c)=c$. This proves the [fixed-point property of a closed interval](../../../../../../fixed-point-property-of-a-closed-interval.md).

Now suppose $0,1\in f([0,1])$. For any $y\in[0,1]$, choose $a,b\in[0,1]$ with $f(a)=0$ and $f(b)=1$. Applying the intermediate value theorem to $f$ on the interval with endpoints $a,b$ gives a point $x$ with $f(x)=y$. Thus $f$ is a [surjective function](../../../../../../surjective-function.md).
