<h1 id="2f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $h:A\to B$ be a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md), and let $f:B\to B$ be continuous. The conjugate map

$$
g=h^{-1}\circ f\circ h:A\to A
$$

is continuous. By hypothesis it has a fixed point $a\in A$. Therefore

$$
h^{-1}(f(h(a)))=a,
$$

and applying $h$ gives $f(h(a))=h(a)$. Hence every continuous self-map of $B$ has a fixed point. This is the [homeomorphism invariance of the fixed-point property](../../../../../../homeomorphism-invariance-of-the-fixed-point-property.md).
