A [Non-homogeneous Poisson point process](../../../../../../non-homogeneous-poisson-point-process.md) with [intensity function](../../../../../../intensity-function-of-a-point-process.md) $\lambda$ is a random counting measure $N$ such that, for every finite family of pairwise disjoint bounded Borel sets $A_1,\ldots,A_k$, the counts $N(A_1),\ldots,N(A_k)$ are [independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) and

$$
N(A_i)\sim\operatorname{Poisson}\left(\int_{A_i}\lambda(x)\,dx\right).
$$

The assumed local integrability makes each [Poisson](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) parameter finite. This count law and independence characterize the process.
