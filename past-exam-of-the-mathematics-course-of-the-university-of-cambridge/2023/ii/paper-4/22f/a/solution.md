<h1 id="22f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For each $n$, define the bounded linear functional

$$
T_n:H\longrightarrow\mathbb C,
\qquad T_n(y)=\langle x_n,y\rangle.
$$

The hypothesis is precisely that $x_n$ converges [weakly](../../../../../../weak-convergence-in-a-hilbert-space.md) to $x$. In particular, for each fixed $y\in H$, the scalar sequence $T_n(y)$ converges and is therefore bounded. Thus the family $\{T_n\}$ is pointwise bounded.

The [Uniform boundedness principle](../../../../../../uniform-boundedness-principle.md) gives

$$
\sup_n\lVert T_n\rVert<\infty.
$$

By the [Riesz representation theorem](../../../../../../riesz-representation-theorem.md), $\lVert T_n\rVert=\lVert x_n\rVert_H$. Hence

$$
\sup_n\lVert x_n\rVert_H<\infty,
$$

so the sequence is bounded.
