**No.** Take the [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) $H=\ell^2$ and its standard [orthonormal sequence](../../../../../../orthonormal-sequence.md) $(e_n)$. Let

$$
C=\{u\in\ell^2:\lVert u\rVert_2=1\},
$$

the unit sphere. This set is norm closed but is not convex. By the [Bessel inequality](../../../../../../bessel-s-inequality.md), for every $y=(y_j)\in\ell^2$,

$$
\langle e_n,y\rangle=\overline{y_n}\longrightarrow0,
$$

so $e_n\rightharpoonup0$; equivalently, this is a [weakly null orthonormal sequence](../../../../../../weakly-null-orthonormal-sequence.md). Every $e_n$ lies in $C$, but the weak limit $0$ does not. Hence convexity is essential.
