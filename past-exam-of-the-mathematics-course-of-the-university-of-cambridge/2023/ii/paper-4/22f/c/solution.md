↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply part (b). Since every $x_n$ belongs to the [convex set](../../../../../../convex-set.md) $C$, every convex combination $\widetilde x_k$ also belongs to $C$. Since $C$ is norm closed and $\widetilde x_k\to x$ in norm, it follows that $x\in C$. Thus a [norm-closed convex set is weakly closed](../../../../../../norm-closed-convex-set-is-weakly-closed.md).
