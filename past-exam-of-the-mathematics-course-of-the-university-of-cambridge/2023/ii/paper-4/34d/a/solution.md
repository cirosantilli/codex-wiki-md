<h1 id="34d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $B>0$ and use the [Landau gauge](../../../../../../landau-gauge-for-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md) $\mathbf A=(0,Bx,0)$. With zero $z$-momentum, the Hamiltonian becomes

$$
H=\frac1{2m}\left[p_x^2+(p_y+eBx)^2\right].
$$

For a state $e^{ik_y y}\chi(x)$, this is a [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) in $x$, centred at

$$
x_0=-\frac{\hbar k_y}{eB},
$$

with [cyclotron frequency](../../../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md)

$$
\omega_c=\frac{|eB|}{m}.
$$

The [Landau levels](../../../../../../landau-level.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{E_n=\hbar\omega_c\left(n+\frac12\right)},
\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots.
$$

Periodicity in $y$ quantizes $k_y=2\pi\ell/R_y$. Requiring the corresponding oscillator centre to lie in an $x$ interval of length $R_x$ leaves

$$
\boxed{D=\frac{|eB|R_xR_y}{2\pi\hbar}}
$$

allowed centres for each $n$. This is the [degeneracy of a Landau level](../../../../../../degeneracy-of-a-landau-level.md), equal to the magnetic flux through the rectangle divided by the [magnetic flux quantum](../../../../../../magnetic-flux-quantum.md) $h/|e|$. Consistent magnetic periodic boundary conditions make $D$ an integer.
