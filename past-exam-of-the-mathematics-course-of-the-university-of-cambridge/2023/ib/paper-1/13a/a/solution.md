↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The Lagrange identity for this [Sturm-Liouville theory](../../../../../../sturm-liouville-theory.md) operator is

$$
y_0L(y;\lambda_0)-yL(y_0;\lambda_0)
=\frac d{dx}\left[p(y_0y'-yy_0')\right].
$$

Integrating from zero to one, the boundary term vanishes because both $y$ and $y_0$ vanish at both endpoints. Since $L(y_0;\lambda_0)=0$ and $L(y;\lambda_0)=f$, it follows that

$$
\int_0^1y_0f\,dx=0.
$$

This is the [solvability condition at a Sturm-Liouville eigenvalue](../../../../../../solvability-condition-at-a-sturm-liouville-eigenvalue.md).
