<h1 id="9e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Assume (iii). Because $R$ is a [Noetherian ring](../../../../../../noetherian-ring.md), every [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) $I\subseteq R$ is finitely generated. Regard $I$ as an $R$-submodule of $F$. By (iii), $I=Rx$ for some $x\in F$; since $x\in I\subseteq R$ unless $I=0$, this is a principal [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) of $R$. Thus every [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) is principal and (i) holds.

Together with the previous two implications, this proves (i), (ii), and (iii) equivalent.
