<h1 id="9e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9E](../9e.md)

Suppose the additive [group](../../../../../group-split.md) of the [integral](../../../../../integral.md) domain $R$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb Z^n$. Every [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) $I$ is then an additive [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) of a finitely generated free abelian [group](../../../../../group-split.md), so it has finitely many additive generators $x_1,\ldots,x_m$. These also generate $I$ as an $R$-ideal: integer coefficients are coefficients from the canonical copy of $\mathbb Z$ in $R$. Thus every [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md) is finitely generated, proving that $R$ is Noetherian by [noetherianity from finite additive rank](../../../../../noetherianity-from-finite-additive-rank.md).

For an example that fails (i)--(iv), take

$$
R=\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-5}].
$$

It is an [integral](../../../../../integral.md) domain and has additive [group](../../../../../group-split.md) $\mathbb Z^2$. The [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md)

$$
I=(2,1+\sqrt{-5})
$$

has index two: modulo $I$, one has $2=0$ and $\sqrt{-5}=-1=1$, and the resulting quotient is $\mathbb F_2$. If $I=(a+b\sqrt{-5})$, multiplication by its generator would have [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) and absolute index

$$
a^2+5b^2=2,
$$

which has no integer solution. Hence $I$ is not principal, as detailed in [nonprincipal ideal in the integers adjoined a square root of minus five](../../../../../nonprincipal-ideal-in-the-integers-adjoined-a-square-root-of-minus-five.md). Condition (i), and therefore all four equivalent conditions, fails.
