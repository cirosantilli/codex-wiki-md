<h1 id="9e/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If (i) holds, the [submodule theorem for free modules over a principal ideal domain](../../../../../../submodule-theorem-for-free-modules-over-a-principal-ideal-domain.md) says that any submodule $N\subseteq R^n$ is free. Its rank is at most $n$, since tensoring the inclusion with $F$ embeds $N\otimes_R F$ into $F^n$. Hence $N$ has at most $n$ generators, proving (iv).

Conversely, apply (iv) with $n=1$. Every [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) is an $R$-submodule of $R$ and therefore has one generator, which is (i). Thus all four conditions are equivalent.
