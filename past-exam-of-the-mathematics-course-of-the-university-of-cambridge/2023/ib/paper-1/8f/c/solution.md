<h1 id="8f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**True.** Since $\alpha$ is invertible, every [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$ is nonzero. If $\alpha$ had a nontrivial Jordan block $\lambda I+N$, then for the stated positive integer $N_0$,

$$
(\lambda I+N)^{N_0}
=\lambda^{N_0}I+N_0\lambda^{N_0-1}N+\cdots
$$

would still have a nonzero nilpotent part and could not be diagonalizable. Therefore all Jordan blocks of $\alpha$ have size one. This also proves [diagonalizability inherited from an invertible power](../../../../../../diagonalizability-inherited-from-an-invertible-power.md).
