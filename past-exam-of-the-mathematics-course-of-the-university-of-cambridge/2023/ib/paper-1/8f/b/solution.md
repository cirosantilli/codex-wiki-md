**False.** At [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $2$, algebraic multiplicity two and minimal-polynomial exponent two force one block $J_2(2)$. At [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1$, the blocks have total size five and largest size two, so there are only two possible partitions,

$$
2+2+1\qquad\hbox{or}\qquad2+1+1+1.
$$

Hence there are exactly two conjugacy classes satisfying the given [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) data, represented by

$$
J_2(2)\oplus J_2(1)\oplus J_2(1)\oplus J_1(1)
$$

and

$$
J_2(2)\oplus J_2(1)\oplus J_1(1)\oplus J_1(1)\oplus J_1(1).
$$

There cannot be three mutually non-conjugate examples.
