**False.** The real [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
$$

has the two distinct complex [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $i$ and $-i$, so it is diagonalizable over $\mathbb C$. It has no real [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and hence no real eigenbasis, so it is not diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$.
