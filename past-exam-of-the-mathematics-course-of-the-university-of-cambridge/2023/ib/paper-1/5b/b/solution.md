<h1 id="5b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $A$ has full column rank, then for every nonzero $h$,

$$
h^TA^TAh=\|Ah\|^2>0.
$$

Thus $A^TA$ is positive definite and invertible, and the normal equation has the unique solution

$$
\boxed{c^*=(A^TA)^{-1}A^Ty.}
$$
