<h1 id="5b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For any $h$,

$$
\|A(c^*+h)-y\|_2^2
=\|Ac^*-y\|_2^2+2h^TA^T(Ac^*-y)+\|Ah\|_2^2.
$$

If the [normal equation](../../../../../../normal-equation.md) $A^TAc^*=A^Ty$ holds, the last two terms reduce to $\|Ah\|^2\geq0$, so $c^*$ minimizes. Conversely, at a minimizer the directional [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) in every $h$ vanishes, forcing $A^T(Ac^*-y)=0$.
