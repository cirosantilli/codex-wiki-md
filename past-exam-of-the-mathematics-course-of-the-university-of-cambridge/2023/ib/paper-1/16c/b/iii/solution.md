<h1 id="16c/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At $G=G_0$, the linear terms cancel and

$$
u(y)=U\frac{y^2}{h^2}.
$$

The volume flux per unit width is

$$
Q=\int_0^h u(y)\,dy
=\boxed{\frac{Uh}{3}}.
$$
