<h1 id="16c/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For steady flow the [momentum](../../../../../../../momentum.md) equation is

$$
\mu u''=G.
$$

Applying no slip, $u(0)=0$ and $u(h)=U$, gives the [Couette-Poiseuille flow in a thin gap](../../../../../../../couette-poiseuille-flow-in-a-thin-gap.md)

$$
\boxed{u(y)=\frac{Uy}{h}+\frac{G}{2\mu}y(y-h)}.
$$
