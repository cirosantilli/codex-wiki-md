<h1 id="16c/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) force in the $x$-direction on a rectangle of dimensions $\delta x\times\delta y$ is

$$
-\frac{\partial p}{\partial x}\,\delta x\delta y+o(\delta x\delta y).
$$

The shear stress is $\tau_{xy}=\mu u_y$, so the net viscous force is

$$
\mu u_{yy}\,\delta x\delta y+o(\delta x\delta y).
$$

There is no convective [acceleration](../../../../../../../acceleration.md). Dividing the [momentum](../../../../../../../momentum.md) balance by the area and taking the [limit](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\rho u_t=-\frac{dp}{dx}+\mu u_{yy}}.
$$

The $y$-momentum balance gives $p_y=0$, so the [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) depends only on $x$.
