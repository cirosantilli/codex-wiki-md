<h1 id="12b/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $g(\mathbb C)$ is not dense. Then some [open disc](../../../../../../../open-disc.md) $D(w,r)$ is disjoint from the image, so

$$
F(z)=\frac1{g(z)-w}
$$

is entire and satisfies $|F(z)|\leq1/r$. Liouville's theorem makes $F$, and hence $g$, constant, a contradiction. Therefore every nonconstant entire $g$ has dense image, as asserted by [dense image of a nonconstant entire function](../../../../../../../dense-image-of-a-nonconstant-entire-function.md).
