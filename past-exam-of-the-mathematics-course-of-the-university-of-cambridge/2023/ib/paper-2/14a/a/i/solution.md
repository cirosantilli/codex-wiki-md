<h1 id="14a/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Regularity at the origin excludes the [natural logarithm](../../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) and every negative radial power. Thus

$$
\boxed{c_0=0,\qquad c_n=d_n=0\quad(n\geq1)}.
$$

The coefficients $a_0,a_n,b_n$ remain arbitrary.
