<h1 id="14a/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Regularity at infinity excludes the [natural logarithm](../../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) and every positive radial power. Thus

$$
\boxed{c_0=0,\qquad a_n=b_n=0\quad(n\geq1)}.
$$

The constant $a_0$ and the decaying coefficients $c_n,d_n$ remain arbitrary.
