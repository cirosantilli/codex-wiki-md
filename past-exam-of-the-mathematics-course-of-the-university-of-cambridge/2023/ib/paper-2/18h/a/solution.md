↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**True.** If the [Markov chain](../../../../../../markov-chain.md) with [transition matrix](../../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) $P$ is irreducible, then for every pair of states $i,j$ there is a path

$$
i=i_0,i_1,\ldots,i_m=j
$$

with $P_{i_{r-1}i_r}>0$ at every step. The support assumption gives $Q_{i_{r-1}i_r}>0$ for every edge of the same path. Thus every state can reach every other state under $Q$, so the second chain is also an [irreducible Markov chain](../../../../../../irreducible-markov-chain.md).
