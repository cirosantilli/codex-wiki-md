**False.** Again use two states, but now take

$$
P=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Q=
\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac12&\tfrac12\\
\tfrac12&\tfrac12
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The support condition holds. Under $P$, the [first return time](../../../../../../first-return-time.md) to state one is identically one, so $\mu_1=1$. Under $Q$, the chain is irreducible with [stationary distribution](../../../../../../stationary-distribution.md) $(1/2,1/2)$. The [mean recurrence time](../../../../../../mean-recurrence-time.md) formula gives

$$
\eta_1=\frac1{\pi_1}=2>1=\mu_1.
$$

**Thus enlarging transition support does not imply a smaller mean return time.**
