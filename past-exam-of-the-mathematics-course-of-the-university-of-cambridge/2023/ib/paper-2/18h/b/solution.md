<h1 id="18h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**True.** For a state $i$, let

$$
\mathcal R_P(i)=\{n\geq1:(P^n)_{ii}>0\}
$$

be its possible return times under $P$, and define $\mathcal R_Q(i)$ similarly. Every positive $P$-path is a positive $Q$-path, so

$$
\mathcal R_P(i)\subseteq\mathcal R_Q(i).
$$

The greatest common divisor of the larger set divides that of the smaller set. Since the latter is one by the assumed [aperiodic Markov chain](../../../../../../aperiodic-markov-chain.md) property, the former is also one. Hence every state is aperiodic under $Q$.
