<h1 id="16d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $x^\mu=(ct,\mathbf x)$, let $\tau$ be [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md), and define the [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md) and [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) by

$$
u^\mu=\frac{dx^\mu}{d\tau}=\gamma(c,\mathbf v),
\qquad
p^\mu=mu^\mu=(\mathcal E/c,\mathbf p).
$$

If $F^{\mu\nu}$ is the [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md), the covariant [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) law is

$$
\boxed{\frac{dp^\mu}{d\tau}=qF^{\mu\nu}u_\nu}.
$$

The temporal component and three spatial components respectively give

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\mathcal E}{dt}=q\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v},
\qquad
\boxed{\frac{d\mathbf p}{dt}
=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)}.
$$

Here $\mathcal E=\gamma mc^2$ is the relativistic [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) and $\mathbf p=\gamma m\mathbf v$ is the relativistic [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md). In the nonrelativistic [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md), $\gamma\to1$, so the spatial equation becomes

$$
m\frac{d\mathbf v}{dt}
=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B),
$$

the usual Lorentz-force law.
