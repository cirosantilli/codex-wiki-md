<h1 id="16d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The temporal component found in part (a) gives directly

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E(t)-\mathcal E(0)
=q\int_0^t\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v(s)\,ds
}.
$$

For a constant [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md), this is the work done by the field along the particle trajectory:

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E(t)=\mathcal E(0)
+q\mathbf E\cdot[\mathbf x(t)-\mathbf x(0)]
}.
$$
