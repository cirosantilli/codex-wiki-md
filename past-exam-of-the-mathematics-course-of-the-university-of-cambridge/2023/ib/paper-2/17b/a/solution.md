<h1 id="17b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Apply a one-step numerical method to the [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md) test [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md)

$$
y'=\lambda y.
$$

If one step has the form

$$
y_{n+1}=R(z)y_n,
\qquad z=h\lambda,
$$

then $R$ is the [stability function](../../../../../../stability-function.md) and the [linear stability domain](../../../../../../linear-stability-domain.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal S=\{z\in\mathbb C:|R(z)|\leq1\}}.
$$

The method is [A-stable](../../../../../../a-stability.md) when

$$
\boxed{\{z:\operatorname{Re}z\leq0\}\subseteq\mathcal S},
$$

so it does not amplify any mode that the exact [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) does not amplify.
