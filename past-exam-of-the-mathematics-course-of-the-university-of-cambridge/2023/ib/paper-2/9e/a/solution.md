<h1 id="9e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $Q$ act by left multiplication on the set $G/P$ of left cosets. Every orbit has size a power of $p$, while

$$
|G/P|
$$

is not divisible by $p$. Therefore at least one orbit has size one. If $gP$ is fixed, then $qgP=gP$ for every $q\in Q$, so $g^{-1}Qg\leq P$, equivalently

$$
Q\leq gPg^{-1}.
$$

This is [Sylow containment from a coset fixed point](../../../../../../sylow-containment-from-a-coset-fixed-point.md).

The remaining [Sylow theorems](../../../../../../sylow-theorems.md) state that Sylow $p$-subgroups exist, that any two are conjugate, and that their number $n_p$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{n_p\equiv1\pmod p,
\qquad
n_p\mid |G|/|P|.}
$$
