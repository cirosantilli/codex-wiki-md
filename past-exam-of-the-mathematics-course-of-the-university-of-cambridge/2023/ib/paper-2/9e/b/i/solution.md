↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By transitivity and the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md),

$$
|G|=7|G_x|=7\cdot24=168=2^3\cdot3\cdot7.
$$

Inside $G_x\cong S_4$, there are three Sylow $2$-subgroups, each of order eight, and four Sylow $3$-subgroups, each of order three. These are also Sylow [subgroups](../../../../../../../subgroup.md) of $G$.
