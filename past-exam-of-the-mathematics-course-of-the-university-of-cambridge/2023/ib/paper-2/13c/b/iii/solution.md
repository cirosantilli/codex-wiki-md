↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Evaluating the [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) at the endpoint gives the [displacement](../../../../../../../displacement.md)

$$
\Delta=y(L)
=-\frac{\rho gL^4}{8A}+\frac{FL^3}{3A}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{h_0=-\frac{\rho gL^4}{8A}},
\qquad
\boxed{h=\frac{FL^3}{3A}},
\qquad
\boxed{\Delta=h_0+h}.
$$

The force-induced displacement $h$ is a [linear map](../../../../../../../linear-map.md) of $F$.
