↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Taking the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) of the force-dependent energy gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac{d\mathcal E}{dF}
=\frac{FL^3}{3A}
=h
}.
$$

**Thus the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) of the additional minimized internal energy with respect to the applied [force](../../../../../../../force.md) equals the resulting endpoint [displacement](../../../../../../../displacement.md). This is the [endpoint force derivative of clamped-free beam energy](../../../../../../../endpoint-force-derivative-of-clamped-free-beam-energy.md).**
