<h1 id="13c/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Substitute $y=y_0+y_F$ into the energy [functional](../../../../../../../functional.md) and integrate the resulting [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
E
&=\int_0^L
\left[\frac A2(y'')^2+\rho gy\right]\,dx\\
&=-\frac{\rho^2g^2L^5}{40A}
+\frac{F^2L^3}{6A}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The term linear in $F$ cancels. Hence

$$
\boxed{E_0=-\frac{\rho^2g^2L^5}{40A}},
\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal E=\frac{F^2L^3}{6A}},
\qquad
\boxed{E=E_0+\mathcal E}.
$$

As required, $E_0$ is independent of the [force](../../../../../../../force.md) and $\mathcal E$ is a [quadratic function](../../../../../../../quadratic-function.md) of it.
