↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Part (a) shows that $f-p_n^*$ is orthogonal to $p_n^*\in\mathcal P_n$. Since

$$
f=(f-p_n^*)+p_n^*,
$$

the [Pythagorean theorem in an inner-product space](../../../../../../pythagorean-theorem-in-an-inner-product-space.md) immediately gives

$$
\boxed{\lVert f\rVert^2
=\lVert f-p_n^*\rVert^2+\lVert p_n^*\rVert^2}.
$$

This is the norm decomposition associated with [orthogonal projection onto a finite-dimensional subspace](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection-onto-a-finite-dimensional-subspace.md).
