<h1 id="3g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The domains are not [conformally equivalent](../../../../../../conformal-equivalence.md). Suppose that $F:\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}\to\{w:0<|w|<1\}$ were a conformal equivalence. Since $F$ is bounded near zero, the [Riemann removable singularity theorem](../../../../../../riemann-removable-singularity-theorem.md) extends it holomorphically across zero. The extension is a bounded [entire function](../../../../../../entire-function.md), so the [Liouville theorem](../../../../../../liouville-theorem.md) makes it constant, contradicting bijectivity. This is the [punctured plane is not conformally equivalent to the punctured unit disc](../../../../../../punctured-plane-is-not-conformally-equivalent-to-the-punctured-unit-disc.md) obstruction.
