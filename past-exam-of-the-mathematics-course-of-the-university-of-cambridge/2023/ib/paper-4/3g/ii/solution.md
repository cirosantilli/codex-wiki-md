<h1 id="3g/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The domains are not [conformally equivalent](../../../../../../conformal-equivalence.md). If $F:\mathbb C\to\mathbb H$ were a conformal equivalence, composing it with a [Cayley transform](../../../../../../cayley-transform-hyperbolic-geometry.md) from the upper half-plane $\mathbb H$ to the unit disc would produce a bounded nonconstant [entire function](../../../../../../entire-function.md). This contradicts the [Liouville theorem](../../../../../../liouville-theorem.md), proving that the [complex plane is not conformally equivalent to the upper half-plane](../../../../../../complex-plane-is-not-conformally-equivalent-to-the-upper-half-plane.md).
