<h1 id="16c/b/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The denominators in part (v) vanish when the forcing frequency equals the natural [surface gravity wave](../../../../../../../surface-gravity-wave.md) frequency

$$
\boxed{\omega=\Omega
=\sqrt{gk}
=\left(\frac{\pi g\sqrt5}{L}\right)^{1/2}}.
$$

At this value, the initially quiescent resonant solution is

$$
H(t)=-\frac{kp_0}{2\rho\Omega},t\sin(\Omega t),
$$



$$
F(t)=-\frac{p_0}{2\rho\Omega}
\bigl(\sin(\Omega t)+\Omega t\cos(\Omega t)\bigr).
$$

Both contain a term growing linearly in time. This is [resonance](../../../../../../../resonance.md): the pressure forcing repeatedly supplies energy in phase with the box's $(1,2)$ standing-wave mode. In the ideal inviscid linear model there is no damping or nonlinear saturation, so its amplitude is unbounded. This is the [resonance of a pressure-forced rectangular surface-gravity mode](../../../../../../../resonance-of-a-pressure-forced-rectangular-surface-gravity-mode.md).
