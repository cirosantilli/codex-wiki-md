<h1 id="6d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

As $|x|\to\infty$, the equation approaches

$$
\chi''+\frac{2m}{\hbar^2}(E-U_0)\chi=0.
$$

If $E>U_0$, the asymptotic solutions are oscillatory and do not decay, so there are no [bound states](../../../../../../bound-state.md). If $0<E<U_0$, the asymptotic solutions are exponential; one decaying branch can be selected at each end. [Bound states](../../../../../../bound-state.md) are therefore possible, although only energies satisfying the global matching conditions actually occur.
