↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The spatial [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md) $\chi(x)$ is the position amplitude of a stationary [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md), so $|\chi(x)|^2$ is its [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) after normalization. The real [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $U(x)$ is the [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md), $E$ is the energy [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), $m$ is the particle mass, and $\hbar$ is the reduced [Planck constant](../../../../../../planck-constant.md).
