<h1 id="15d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Parametrize the loop by $r'=R\hat e_r(\phi)$ and $d\ell'=R\hat e_\phi(\phi)d\phi$. At the observation point $r=z\hat e_z$, the [Biot-Savart law](../../../../../../biot-savart-law.md) for a [line current](../../../../../../line-current.md) gives

$$
dB=\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi}
\frac{d\ell'\times(r-r')}{(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}.
$$

The components perpendicular to the axis cancel by [rotational symmetry](../../../../../../rotational-symmetry.md), while the axial component of the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) is $R^2d\phi$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{B(z)=\frac{\mu_0IR^2}{2(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\,\hat e_z}.
$$

The direction is fixed by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md) and reverses when the [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) reverses. At the centre,

$$
\boxed{B(0)=\frac{\mu_0I}{2R}\,\hat e_z}.
$$

This is the [on-axis magnetic field of a circular current loop](../../../../../../on-axis-magnetic-field-of-a-circular-current-loop.md).
