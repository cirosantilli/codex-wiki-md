<h1 id="16c/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../../unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md) at $r=a(t)$ and $r=b(t)$ and subtract, thereby eliminating its time-dependent constant. For $A=a\dot a$ and $\phi=A\log r+C(t)$,

$$
\partial_t\phi(a,t)-\partial_t\phi(b,t)=\dot A\log\frac ab,
$$

where each partial derivative is taken at fixed $r$. Since $u_r=A/r$ and $p(b,t)=p_\infty$,

$$
\boxed{p(a,t)-p_\infty
=\rho\left[\dot A\log\frac ba
-\frac{A^2}{2}\left(\frac1{a^2}-\frac1{b^2}\right)\right]},
\qquad A=a\dot a.
$$

Equivalently, $\dot A=\dot a^2+a\ddot a$. This is the [pressure in radially symmetric annular potential flow](../../../../../../../pressure-in-radially-symmetric-annular-potential-flow.md).
