<h1 id="16c/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A radially symmetric [velocity potential](../../../../../../../velocity-potential.md) has $u_r=\partial_r\phi$. The [incompressible flow](../../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) condition in two-dimensional polar coordinates is

$$
\frac1r\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(ru_r)=0,
$$

so $ru_r=A(t)$. The [kinematic boundary condition](../../../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition.md) at the bubble surface gives $u_r(a,t)=\dot a$, hence

$$
\boxed{u_r(r,t)=\frac{a\dot a}{r}},
\qquad
\boxed{\phi(r,t)=a\dot a\log r+C(t)}.
$$

At the outer interface, $\dot b=u_r(b,t)=a\dot a/b$. Therefore

$$
\frac d{dt}(b^2-a^2)=2b\dot b-2a\dot a=0,
$$

and

$$
\boxed{b(t)^2-a(t)^2=b_0^2-a_0^2}.
$$

The area $\pi(b^2-a^2)$ of the incompressible liquid is conserved. These formulas are the [radially symmetric incompressible flow in a planar annulus](../../../../../../../radially-symmetric-incompressible-flow-in-a-planar-annulus.md).
