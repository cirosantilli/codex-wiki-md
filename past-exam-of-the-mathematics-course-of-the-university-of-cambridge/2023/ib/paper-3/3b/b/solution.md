<h1 id="3b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the affine relation with respect to $x$ and $y$:

$$
au_x+bv_x=0,
\qquad
au_y+bv_y=0.
$$

Using the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md), the second equation becomes

$$
bu_x-av_x=0.
$$

Thus

$$
\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&-a\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}u_x\\v_x\end{pmatrix}=0.
$$

The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is $-(a^2+b^2)\ne0$, so $u_x=v_x=0$ and therefore $f'=0$. A [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) with zero [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) on a connected [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) is constant. Hence $f$ is constant, as in [analytic function with image in an affine real line](../../../../../../analytic-function-with-image-in-an-affine-real-line.md).
