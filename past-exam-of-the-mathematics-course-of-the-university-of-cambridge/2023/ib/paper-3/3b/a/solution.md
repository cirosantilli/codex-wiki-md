<h1 id="3b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Taking the complex [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) along the real direction gives

$$
\boxed{f'(z)=u_x+iv_x}.
$$

Taking it along the imaginary direction and equating the two expressions gives the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md)

$$
\boxed{u_x=v_y,\qquad u_y=-v_x}.
$$
