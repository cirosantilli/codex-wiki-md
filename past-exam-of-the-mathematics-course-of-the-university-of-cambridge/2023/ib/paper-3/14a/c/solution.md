<h1 id="14a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using [Euler's formula](../../../../../../euler-s-formula.md) and the [Inverse Fourier transforms of phase factors](../../../../../../inverse-fourier-transforms-of-phase-factors.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathcal F^{-1}[\cos(ka)](x)
=\frac12\{\delta(x+a)+\delta(x-a)\}},
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\mathcal F^{-1}[\sin(ka)](x)
=\frac1{2i}\{\delta(x+a)-\delta(x-a)\}}.
$$

These are [distributional identities](../../../../../../distributional-identity.md), with $\delta$ the [Dirac delta function](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md).
