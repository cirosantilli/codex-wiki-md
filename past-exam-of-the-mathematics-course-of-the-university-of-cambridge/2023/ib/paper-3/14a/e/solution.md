<h1 id="14a/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Here the [D'Alembert formula with initial velocity](../../../../../../d-alembert-formula-with-initial-velocity.md) becomes

$$
u(x,t)=\frac12\int_{x-t}^{x+t}g(\xi)\,d\xi.
$$

When $t>1$ and

$$
1-t\leq x\leq t-1,
$$

the integration interval contains the whole support $[-1,1]$. Since $g$ is [odd](../../../../../../odd-function.md),

$$
u(x,t)=\frac12\int_{-1}^{1}\xi\,d\xi=0.
$$

Thus the requested region containing the line $x=0$ is

$$
\boxed{t>1,\qquad |x|\leq t-1.}
$$

This is the [central zero region for odd compactly supported initial velocity](../../../../../../central-zero-region-for-odd-compactly-supported-initial-velocity.md). The initial disturbance splits into left-moving and right-moving waves; after they pass, their opposite signed impulses cancel and leave an expanding undisturbed region around the origin.
