<h1 id="19h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The origin belongs to $S$, so $S$ is nonempty. Each displayed inequality defines a [closed half-space](../../../../../../closed-half-space.md), and every half-space is a [convex set](../../../../../../convex-set.md). An intersection of convex sets is convex: if $x,y\in S$ and $0\leq t\leq1$, each linear inequality remains valid for $(1-t)x+ty$. Thus $S$ is a nonempty [convex polytope](../../../../../../convex-polytope.md).
