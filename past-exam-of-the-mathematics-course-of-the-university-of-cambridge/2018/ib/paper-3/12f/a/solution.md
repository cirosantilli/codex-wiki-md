↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The sequence $(f_n)$ [converges uniformly](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) to $f$ on $A$ when, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there is $N$ such that

$$
n\geq N\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |f_n(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon
$$

for **every** $x\in A$.

The function $f$ is [uniformly continuous](../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md) when, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there is $\delta>0$ such that $x,y\in A$ and $\|x-y\|<\delta$ imply $|f(x)-f(y)|<\varepsilon$. The same $\delta$ works throughout the domain.
