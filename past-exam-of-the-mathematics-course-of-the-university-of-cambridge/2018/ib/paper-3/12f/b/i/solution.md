↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** Given $\varepsilon>0$, [uniform continuity](../../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md) supplies $\delta>0$ such that $|x-y|<\delta$ implies $|f(x)-f(y)|<\varepsilon$. For all sufficiently large $n$, $1/n<\delta$, and then

$$
\sup_{x\in\mathbb R}|f(x+1/n)-f(x)|<\varepsilon.
$$

This is precisely [uniform convergence](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) of $f_n$ to $f$.
