<h1 id="4a/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $1<x<2$ the specified value is $g(x)=i\sqrt{(2-x)(x^2-1)}$. Continue towards $0$ around the [branch point](../../../../../../../branch-point.md) $1$. Along the upper bank, the argument of $(z-2)(z^2-1)$ increases from $\pi$ to $2\pi$, so its square root reaches the negative real value; along the lower bank it decreases from $\pi$ to $0$, giving the positive value. Since the radicand at $0$ is $2$,

$$
\boxed{g(0^+)=-\sqrt2,\qquad g(0^-)=\sqrt2.}
$$
