<h1 id="4a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Near infinity, $g(z)^2=(z-2)(z^2-1)\sim z^3$, so $g(z)\sim z^{3/2}$. One positive circuit around infinity changes $z^{3/2}$ by the [monodromy](../../../../../../../monodromy.md) factor $e^{3\pi i}=-1$. Thus analytic continuation does not return to the original value, and **$\infty$ is a branch point**.
