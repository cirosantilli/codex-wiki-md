<h1 id="9h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The three year states are [transient states](../../../../../../transient-state.md): from each there is positive probability of entering an absorbing state, and repeated trials make eventual departure certain because $q<1$. The singleton classes $\{L\}$ and $\{G\}$ are closed [absorbing states](../../../../../../absorbing-state.md), hence recurrent. Thus the chain has two closed recurrent classes and three transient singleton classes.
