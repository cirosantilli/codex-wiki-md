<h1 id="1g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [algebraic norm](../../../../../../algebraic-norm.md) on $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-5}]$ is $N(a+b\sqrt{-5})=a^2+5b^2$ and satisfies [multiplicativity of the algebraic norm](../../../../../../multiplicativity-of-the-algebraic-norm.md). The calculation in part (a) therefore gives all elements of norm $9$:

$$
\pm3,\qquad \pm2\pm\sqrt{-5}.
$$

If one of these factored into two [nonunits](../../../../../../nonunit.md), multiplicativity would force both factors to have norm $3$. But the [Diophantine equation](../../../../../../diophantine-equation.md) $a^2+5b^2=3$ has no solution. Hence **all six displayed elements are irreducible**. This is a standard example in which an [irreducible element](../../../../../../irreducible-element.md) need not have [prime](../../../../../../prime-number.md) norm.
