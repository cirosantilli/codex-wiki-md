<h1 id="1g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $5y^2\leq9$, we have $y\in\{-1,0,1\}$. For $y=0$, $x^2=9$; for $y=\pm1$, $x^2=4$. Thus the solutions of the [Diophantine equation](../../../../../../diophantine-equation.md) are

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(\pm3,0),(\pm2,\pm1),}
$$

where the two signs in the second family are independent.
