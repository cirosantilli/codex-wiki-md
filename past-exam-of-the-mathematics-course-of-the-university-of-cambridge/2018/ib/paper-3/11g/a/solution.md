[Gauss lemma for polynomials](../../../../../../gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md) says that the product of two [primitive polynomials](../../../../../../primitive-polynomial.md) in $\mathbb Z[X]$ is primitive. Equivalently, a primitive polynomial in $\mathbb Z[X]$ is reducible over $\mathbb Q$ exactly when it is reducible over $\mathbb Z$.
