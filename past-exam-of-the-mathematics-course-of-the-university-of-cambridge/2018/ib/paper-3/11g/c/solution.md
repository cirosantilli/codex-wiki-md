<h1 id="11g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Translate the variable by one:

$$
f(X+1)=2X^3+25X^2-10X-30.
$$

This satisfies [Eisenstein criterion](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) with $p=5$: every nonleading coefficient is divisible by $5$, while $25\nmid30$. Translation $X\mapsto X+1$ is an [automorphism of a polynomial ring](../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-polynomial-ring.md), so it preserves irreducibility. Hence **$f$ is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$**.
