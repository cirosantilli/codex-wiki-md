<h1 id="15a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For every [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) $f$, the [change of variables](../../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $u=\alpha x$ gives

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\delta(\alpha x)\,dx=\frac1{|\alpha|}f(0).
$$

Equality of [distributions](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\delta(\alpha x)=|\alpha|^{-1}\delta(x).}
$$
