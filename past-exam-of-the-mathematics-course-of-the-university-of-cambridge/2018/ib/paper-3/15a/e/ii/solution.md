<h1 id="15a/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Direct integration gives

$$
\frac1\pi\int_0^n\cos(kx)\,dk=\frac{\sin(nx)}{\pi x}.
$$

The [sinc approximate identity](../../../../../../../sinc-approximate-identity.md) converges distributionally to the [Dirac delta function](../../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md), using the supplied integral of $\sin y/y$. Thus the limit is **$\delta(x)$**.
