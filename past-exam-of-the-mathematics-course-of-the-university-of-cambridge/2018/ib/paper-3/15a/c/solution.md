↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Repeated [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), or the definition of the $m$th [distributional derivative](../../../../../../distributional-derivative.md), gives

$$
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\delta^{(m)}(x)\,dx=(-1)^m f^{(m)}(0).}
$$
