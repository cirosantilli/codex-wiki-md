<h1 id="15a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using the definition of a [distributional derivative](../../../../../../distributional-derivative.md),

$$
\langle x\delta',f\rangle=\langle\delta',xf\rangle=-(xf)'\big|_{x=0}=-f(0)=\langle-\delta,f\rangle.
$$

Hence **$x\delta'(x)=-\delta(x)$** as distributions.
