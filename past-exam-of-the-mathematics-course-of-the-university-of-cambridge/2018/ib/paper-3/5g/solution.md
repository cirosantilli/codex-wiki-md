<h1 id="5g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5G](../5g.md)

The [hyperbolic lines](../../../../../hyperbolic-line.md) $AB$ and $CD$ are both perpendicular to $BC$. They are therefore [ultraparallel hyperbolic lines](../../../../../ultraparallel-hyperbolic-lines.md), and $BC$ is their unique [common perpendicular of ultraparallel hyperbolic lines](../../../../../common-perpendicular-of-ultraparallel-hyperbolic-lines.md).

The common perpendicular realizes the minimum [hyperbolic distance](../../../../../hyperbolic-distance.md) between two ultraparallel lines. This follows directly in the [Poincaré half-plane model](../../../../../poincare-half-plane-model.md) after a [Möbius transformation](../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) sends the two lines to concentric semicircles: the supplied distance formula is minimized along their common radial geodesic. The segment $AD$ also joins the two lines, but it is different from $BC$ in a nondegenerate quadrilateral. Hence

$$
\boxed{\rho(A,D)>\rho(B,C).}
$$
