<h1 id="13f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

This is [Schwarz lemma](../../../../../../schwarz-lemma.md). Define $g(z)=f(z)/z$ for $z\ne0$ and $g(0)=f'(0)$. The [removable singularity](../../../../../../removable-singularity.md) makes $g$ holomorphic. Applying the [maximum modulus principle](../../../../../../maximum-modulus-principle.md) to $g$ on disks of radius $r<1$, then letting $r\uparrow1$, gives $|g(z)|\leq1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{|f(z)|\leq|z|.}
$$
