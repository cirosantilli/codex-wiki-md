<h1 id="6b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6B](../6b.md)

The [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) and [probability current](../../../../../probability-current.md) in one dimension give

$$
\rho=|\Psi|^2,
\qquad
j=\frac{\hbar}{2mi}\left(\Psi^*\Psi_x-\Psi\Psi_x^*\right).
$$

Multiply the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md) by $\Psi^*$, subtract its complex conjugate multiplied by $\Psi$, and rearrange to obtain the [continuity equation](../../../../../continuity-equation.md) $\rho_t+j_x=0$.

For the stated superposition, the interference terms in $j$ are real and cancel from its imaginary part. Hence

$$
\boxed{j=\frac{\hbar k}{m}\left(|A|^2-|B|^2\right),}
$$

independent of $x$ and $t$.
