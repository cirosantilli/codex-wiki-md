<h1 id="2g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the presentation $D_6=\langle r,s:r^3=s^2=1, srs=r^{-1}\rangle$. Its subgroup $\langle r\rangle$ is the unique subgroup of order $3$, hence is a [characteristic subgroup](../../../../../../characteristic-subgroup.md). An [automorphism](../../../../../../automorphism.md) must send $r$ to $r$ or $r^{-1}$ and $s$ to one of the three reflections $r^js$. Thus there are at most six automorphisms.

The [center of a group](../../../../../../center-of-a-group.md) of $D_6$ is trivial, so conjugation embeds $D_6$ as a group of six [inner automorphisms](../../../../../../inner-automorphism.md). These exhaust the possibilities. Hence **every automorphism of $D_6$ is inner**.
