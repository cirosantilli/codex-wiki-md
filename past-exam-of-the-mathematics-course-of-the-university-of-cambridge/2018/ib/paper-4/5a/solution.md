<h1 id="5a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5A](../5a.md)

[Separation of variables](../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) with $u=X(x)Y(y)$ and the three homogeneous boundary conditions gives $X_n=\sin(n\pi x)$ and $Y_n=\sinh(n\pi y)$. The boundary value at $y=1$ contains only the third [Fourier sine series](../../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) mode. Therefore

$$
\boxed{u(x,y)=2\frac{\sinh(3\pi y)}{\sinh(3\pi)}\sin(3\pi x).}
$$

This [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md) has all four prescribed boundary values.
