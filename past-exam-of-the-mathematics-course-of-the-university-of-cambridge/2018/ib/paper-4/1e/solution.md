A [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form.md) on a finite-dimensional real [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $V$ is a function $q(v)=B(v,v)$ for some symmetric [bilinear form](../../../../../bilinear-form.md) $B$. A [positive-definite quadratic form](../../../../../positive-definite-quadratic-form.md) satisfies $q(v)>0$ for every $v\ne0$.

Completing squares gives

$$
x^2+2xy+2y^2+2yz+3z^2=(x+y)^2+(y+z)^2+2z^2.
$$

Set $u=x+y$, $v=y+z$, $w=z$. The corresponding ordered basis in the original coordinates is

$$
\boxed{(1,0,0),\ (-1,1,0),\ (1,-1,1),}
$$

and the diagonal form is $u^2+v^2+2w^2$. All diagonal coefficients are positive, so **the form is positive definite**.
