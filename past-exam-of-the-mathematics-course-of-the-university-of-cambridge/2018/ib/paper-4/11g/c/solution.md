<h1 id="11g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $(t-\tfrac12)^3$. For $N=A-\tfrac12I$, one finds $N\ne0$ but $N^2=0$, so the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) is $(t-\tfrac12)^2$. Hence the invariant factors are $t-\tfrac12$ and $(t-\tfrac12)^2$, and the rational canonical form is

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}
\tfrac12&0&0\\
0&0&-\tfrac14\\
0&1&1
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
