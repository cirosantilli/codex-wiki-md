The [structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain](../../../../../../structure-theorem-for-finitely-generated-modules-over-a-principal-ideal-domain.md) says that a finitely generated module over a Euclidean domain $R$ is

$$
R^r\oplus R/(d_1)\oplus\cdots\oplus R/(d_k),
\qquad d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_k,
$$

with the rank and nonunit [invariant factors of a linear operator](../../../../../../invariant-factors-of-a-linear-operator.md) unique up to associates.
