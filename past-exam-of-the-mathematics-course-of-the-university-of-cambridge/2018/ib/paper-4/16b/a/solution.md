<h1 id="16b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) are $\ddot x+\omega^2x=0$ and $\ddot y+\omega^2y=0$. They imply

$$
\dot J=\ddot xy-\ddot yx=0.
$$

Writing

$$
x=A\cos\omega t+B\sin\omega t,
\qquad y=C\cos\omega t+D\sin\omega t,
$$

gives the conserved value

$$
\boxed{J=\omega(BC-AD).}
$$
