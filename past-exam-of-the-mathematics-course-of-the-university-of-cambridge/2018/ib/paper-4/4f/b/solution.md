↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The inequality implies $u(u-1)\geq v^2\geq0$, so $u\leq0$ or $u\geq1$. Since $u$ is continuous and $\mathbb C$ is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md), its image lies wholly in one of these intervals. If $u\geq1$, then $g$ has no zero and $|1/g|\leq1$; [Liouville theorem](../../../../../../liouville-theorem.md) makes $1/g$, hence $g$, constant. If $u\leq0$, then $|g-1|\geq1$, so the same argument applied to $1/(g-1)$ proves that **$g$ is constant**.
