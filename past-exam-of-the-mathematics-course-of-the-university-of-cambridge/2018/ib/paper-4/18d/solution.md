<h1 id="18d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [18D](../18d.md)

Let $\phi$ be the [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md). The linearized deep-water equations are $\nabla^2\phi=0$ for $z<0$, $\eta_t=\phi_z$ and $\phi_t+g\eta=0$ at $z=0$, with zero normal velocity at the four side walls. The [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md) are

$$
\phi=C e^{kz}\cos\frac{m\pi x}{a}\cos\frac{n\pi y}{a}e^{-i\omega t},
\quad
k=\frac\pi a\sqrt{m^2+n^2},
$$

where $m,n\geq0$ are not both zero. The free-surface conditions give the dispersion relation for a [deep-water gravity wave](../../../../../deep-water-gravity-wave.md):

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=gk.}
$$

The initial displacement is the $(m,n)=(3,4)$ mode, so $k=5\pi/a$. Release from rest selects a cosine in time:

$$
\boxed{\eta=\varepsilon\cos\frac{3\pi x}{a}\cos\frac{4\pi y}{a}
\cos\left(\sqrt{\frac{5\pi g}{a}}\,t\right).}
$$

Velocity decays as $e^{kz}$, so it falls to $1/e$ of its surface value at depth **$a/(5\pi)$**.
