<h1 id="2a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}z>0$,

$$
|z+1|^2-|z-1|^2=4\operatorname{Re}z>0,
$$

so $w=(z-1)/(z+1)$ lies in the unit disc. Its inverse is $z=(1+w)/(1-w)$, whose real part is $(1-|w|^2)/|1-w|^2>0$. Both derivatives are nonzero, proving this [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) is conformal.
