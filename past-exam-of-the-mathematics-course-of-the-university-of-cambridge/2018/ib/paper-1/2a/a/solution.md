<h1 id="2a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose

$$
\operatorname{Log}z=\log|z|+i\arg z,\qquad -\frac\pi2<\arg z<\frac\pi2.
$$

It is holomorphic on the right half-plane with derivative $1/z\ne0$, and its inverse $z=e^w$ has positive real part precisely for $|\operatorname{Im}w|<\pi/2$. Thus this branch of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) is a conformal bijection onto $S$.
