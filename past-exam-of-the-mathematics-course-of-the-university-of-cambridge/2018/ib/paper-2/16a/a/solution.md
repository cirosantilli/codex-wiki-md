<h1 id="16a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The function is an [even function](../../../../../../even-function.md), so every sine [Fourier coefficient](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) vanishes. Direct integration gives

$$
a_0=\frac2\pi\int_0^\pi x\,dx=\pi,
\qquad
a_n=\frac2\pi\int_0^\pi x\cos(nx)\,dx
=\frac{2}{\pi n^2}((-1)^n-1).
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{|x|=\frac\pi2-\frac4\pi
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
\frac{\cos((2k+1)x)}{(2k+1)^2}}
$$

for the $2\pi$-periodic extension.
