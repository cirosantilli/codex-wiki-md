<h1 id="11g/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Any [unit in a ring](../../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) of $R$ must also be a unit of $\mathbb Q[X]$, hence a nonzero constant $q\in\mathbb Q$. Membership of both $q$ and $q^{-1}$ in $R$ requires $q,q^{-1}\in\mathbb Z$, so

$$
\boxed{R^\times=\{1,-1\}.}
$$
