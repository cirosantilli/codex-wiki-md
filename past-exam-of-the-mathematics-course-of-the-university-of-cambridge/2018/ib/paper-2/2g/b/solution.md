<h1 id="2g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $J$ be an [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) of $R'=S^{-1}R$ and contract it to

$$
I=\{r\in R:r/1\in J\}.
$$

This is an ideal of $R$, so $I=(d)$ because $R$ is a [principal ideal domain](../../../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md). Every $x^n/1$ is a [unit in a ring](../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) $R'$. Consequently, if $r/x^n\in J$, then $r/1=(x^n/1)(r/x^n)\in J$, so $r\in(d)$ and $r/x^n\in(d/1)$. Conversely $d/1\in J$, hence every multiple of it belongs to $J$. Thus

$$
\boxed{J=(d/1),}
$$

so every ideal of $R'$ is principal. The localization remains an [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md); hence **$R'$ is a principal ideal domain.**
