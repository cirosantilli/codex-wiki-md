<h1 id="14g/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the [parabolic element of PSL2(R)](../../../../../../parabolic-element-of-psl2-r.md)

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad z\longmapsto z+1.
$$

It fixes no point in $\mathbb H$. A [Geodesic in the Poincare half-plane model](../../../../../../geodesic-in-the-poincare-half-plane-model.md) is determined by its unordered pair of ideal endpoints: either two real numbers or one real number together with $\infty$. Translation adds one to every finite endpoint, so no such pair is invariant. Therefore **this $A$ preserves neither a point nor a hyperbolic line in $\mathbb H$.**
