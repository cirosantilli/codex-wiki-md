<h1 id="14g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A fixed point of $A$ satisfies

$$
cz^2+(d-a)z-b=0.
$$

Its discriminant is

$$
(d-a)^2+4bc=(a+d)^2-4=(\operatorname{tr}A)^2-4<0,
$$

where $ad-bc=1$ was used. Thus the two roots are nonreal complex conjugates, and exactly one lies in the upper half-plane. Consequently **every [elliptic element of PSL2(R)](../../../../../../elliptic-element-of-psl2-r.md) fixes a point of $\mathbb H$.**
