<h1 id="14g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $B=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda,\lambda^{-1})$ and $q=\lambda^2>0$, so its [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) is $z\mapsto qz$, with $q\ne1$. For $z=u+iv$, the [Hyperbolic distance in the Poincare half-plane](../../../../../../hyperbolic-distance-in-the-poincare-half-plane.md) satisfies

$$
\cosh\rho(z,qz)
=1+\frac{|z-qz|^2}{2\operatorname{Im}z\operatorname{Im}(qz)}
=1+\frac{(q-1)^2}{2q}
\left(1+\frac{u^2}{v^2}\right).
$$

At $z=i$, the final parenthesis is $1$. Since $\cosh$ is strictly increasing on nonnegative arguments, every $z$ off the imaginary axis has

$$
\boxed{\rho(z,Bz)>\rho(i,Bi).}
$$
