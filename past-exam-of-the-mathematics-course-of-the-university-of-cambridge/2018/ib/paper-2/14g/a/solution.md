<h1 id="14g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The characteristic polynomial of $A$ is

$$
t^2-(\operatorname{tr}A)t+1.
$$

When $|\operatorname{tr}A|>2$, its discriminant is positive, so $A$ has two distinct real [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda,\lambda^{-1}$ and two independent real [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md). Let $P$ have those eigenvectors as columns. Rescaling one column makes $\det P=1$ without changing the diagonalization. Hence $P\in SL_2(\mathbb R)$ and

$$
\boxed{P^{-1}AP=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda&0\\0&\lambda^{-1}\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

This is the normal form of a [hyperbolic element of PSL2(R)](../../../../../../hyperbolic-element-of-psl2-r.md).
