<h1 id="13a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using the Taylor series of the exponential and series division,

$$
\frac1{e^{2z}-1}
=\frac1{2z}-\frac12+\frac z6-\frac{z^3}{90}+O(z^5).
$$

Thus the first three nonzero Laurent terms are

$$
\boxed{\frac1{2z}-\frac12+\frac z6.}
$$

The singularities occur at $z=k\pi i$. The nearest nonzero ones are $\pm\pi i$, so this [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) is valid on **$0<|z|<\pi$**.
