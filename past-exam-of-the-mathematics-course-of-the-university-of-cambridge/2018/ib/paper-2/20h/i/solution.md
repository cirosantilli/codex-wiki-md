<h1 id="20h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a finite irreducible [Markov chain](../../../../../../markov-chain.md) with [invariant distribution](../../../../../../stationary-distribution.md) $\pi$, the [mean recurrence time](../../../../../../mean-recurrence-time.md) of state $i$ is

$$
\mathbb E_iT_i^+=\frac1{\pi_i}.
$$

The [simple random walk on the hypercube](../../../../../../simple-random-walk-on-the-hypercube.md) has the uniform invariant distribution because the [hypercube graph](../../../../../../hypercube-graph.md) is regular, so every vertex has mass $2^{-n}$. Therefore the expected first positive return time to the initial vertex is

$$
\boxed{2^n.}
$$
