<h1 id="13c/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With $A_i=a_{ij}x_j$ and constant $B_{ij}=b_{ij}$, the [Fokker-Planck probability current](../../../../../../fokker-planck-probability-current.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{J_i=a_{ij}x_jP-\frac12b_{ij}\frac{\partial P}{\partial x_j}.}
$$

In the stationary state we impose the stronger zero-current condition $J_i=0$, corresponding to [detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md).
