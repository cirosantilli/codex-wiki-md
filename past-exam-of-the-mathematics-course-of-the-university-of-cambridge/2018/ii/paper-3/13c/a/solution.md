<h1 id="13c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Define the [Multivariate Fokker-Planck probability current](../../../../../../multivariate-fokker-planck-probability-current.md) by

$$
\boxed{J_i=A_iP-\frac12\frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}(B_{ij}P).}
$$

Then

$$
-\frac{\partial J_i}{\partial x_i}
=-\frac{\partial}{\partial x_i}(A_iP)
+\frac12\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_i\partial x_j}(B_{ij}P),
$$

so the [Fokker-Planck equation](../../../../../../fokker-planck-equation.md) is exactly the [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) $\partial_tP+\nabla\cdot\mathbf J=0$.
