↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The global [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](../../../../../../gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) for a compact oriented surface without boundary is

$$
\boxed{\int_SK\,dA=2\pi\chi(S).}
$$

For a connected closed orientable surface of [genus](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$, this is

$$
\boxed{\int_SK\,dA=4\pi(1-g).}
$$
