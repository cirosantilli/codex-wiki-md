<h1 id="24i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [divisor on an algebraic curve](../../../../../../divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) $D$ on a smooth projective curve $C$ of [geometric genus](../../../../../../geometric-genus.md) $g$, and a [canonical divisor](../../../../../../canonical-divisor.md) $K_C$, the [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\ell(D)-\ell(K_C-D)=\deg D+1-g.}
$$
