<h1 id="20h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The wedge intersection is simply connected after taking suitable neighbourhoods, so repeated application of the [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](../../../../../../seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) gives the free product of $n$ copies of $\mathbb Z$ with

$$
\pi_1(\mathbb{RP}^2)\cong C_2.
$$

Thus, for any basepoint,

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(X_n,x_0)
\cong
\langle a_1,\ldots,a_n,b\mid b^2=1\rangle
\cong F_n*C_2.}
$$

This is the [fundamental group of a wedge of circles and a real projective plane](../../../../../../fundamental-group-of-a-wedge-of-circles-and-a-real-projective-plane.md).
