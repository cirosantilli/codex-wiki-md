<h1 id="20h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $X=Y\cup Z$, where $Y$, $Z$, and $Y\cap Z$ are path-connected subcomplexes containing the basepoint; small open neighbourhoods may be used to reduce to the usual open-cover statement. The [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](../../../../../../seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) says that

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(X)\cong
\pi_1(Y)*_{\pi_1(Y\cap Z)}\pi_1(Z).}
$$

More explicitly, this is the quotient of the [free product](../../../../../../free-product.md) $\pi_1(Y)*\pi_1(Z)$ by the normal closure of

$$
i_{Y*}(\gamma)i_{Z*}(\gamma)^{-1},
\qquad \gamma\in\pi_1(Y\cap Z).
$$

Equivalently, $\pi_1(X)$ is the pushout of the two inclusion-induced homomorphisms.
