<h1 id="38e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put

$$
B^2=\frac{(1-A)^2}{(1+A)^2},
\qquad C=(1+A)^4.
$$

The rest-frame metric can be written as $C\,d\bar s_{\rm Minkowski}^2+(C-B^2)d\bar t^2$. The Minkowski term is invariant under the [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md), while $d\bar t=\gamma(dt-vdx)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(A)=(1+A)^4,
\qquad
g(A)=(1+A)^4-\frac{(1-A)^2}{(1+A)^2},}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{m}{2\sqrt{\gamma^2(x-vt)^2+y^2+z^2}}.}
$$

These substitutions give precisely

$$
ds^2=f(A)(-dt^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2)+\gamma^2g(A)(dt-vdx)^2.
$$
