**Yes.** In fact, measurability of $f$ is unnecessary. Define the oscillation

$$
\omega_f(x)=\inf_{r>0}\sup\{|f(y)-f(z)|:y,z\in(x-r,x+r)\}.
$$

For every $\varepsilon>0$, the set $\{x:\omega_f(x)<\varepsilon\}$ is open, and $f$ is continuous at $x$ exactly when $\omega_f(x)=0$. Hence the [continuity set of a function](../../../../../../continuity-set-of-a-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}\{x:\omega_f(x)<1/n\},}
$$

a $G_\delta$ set and therefore a [Borel set](../../../../../../borel-set.md).
