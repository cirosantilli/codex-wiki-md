A function $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is [Borel measurable](../../../../../../borel-measurable-function.md) when

$$
f^{-1}(B)
$$

is a [Borel set](../../../../../../borel-set.md) for every Borel set $B\subseteq\mathbb R$. It is enough to require this for every open set, or for every interval of the form $(-\infty,a)$.
