A set $E\subseteq\mathbb R$ is [Lebesgue measurable](../../../../../../lebesgue-measurable-set.md) if it satisfies the [Caratheodory criterion](../../../../../../caratheodory-s-criterion.md)

$$
m^*(A)=m^*(A\cap E)+m^*(A\setminus E)
$$

for every $A\subseteq\mathbb R$, where $m^*$ is [Lebesgue outer measure](../../../../../../lebesgue-outer-measure.md). Equivalently, $E$ belongs to the completion of the Borel sigma-algebra with respect to [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md).
