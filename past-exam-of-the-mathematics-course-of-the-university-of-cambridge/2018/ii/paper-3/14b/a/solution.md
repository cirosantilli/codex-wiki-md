<h1 id="14b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At the centre, spherical symmetry and regularity require a finite central density $\rho(0)=\rho_c$ and

$$
\left.\frac{dP}{dr}\right|_{r=0}=0.
$$

At the stellar surface $r=R$, the pressure vanishes:

$$
\boxed{P(R)=0.}
$$

For a [stellar polytrope](../../../../../../stellar-polytrope.md) these become the dimensionless conditions $\theta(0)=1$, $\theta'(0)=0$, and the surface is the first zero $\theta(\xi_1)=0$; specifying $\rho_c$ fixes the overall scale.
