<h1 id="14b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using the [enclosed mass](../../../../../../enclosed-mass.md) integral, $R=a\xi_1$, and the Lane-Emden equation,

$$
\begin{aligned}
M
&=4\pi\int_0^R\rho(r)r^2\,dr\\
&=4\pi a^3\rho_c\int_0^{\xi_1}\theta^n\xi^2\,d\xi\\
&=4\pi a^3\rho_c
\left[-\xi^2\theta'(\xi)\right]_{\xi=\xi_1}
=4\pi a^3\rho_cY_n.
\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting the value of $a$ from part (b) gives the [Lane-Emden mass formula](../../../../../../lane-emden-mass-formula.md)

$$
\boxed{
M=\frac1{2\sqrt\pi}
\left(\frac{(n+1)K}{G}\right)^{3/2}
\rho_c^{(3-n)/(2n)}Y_n,
\qquad
Y_n=-\xi_1^2\theta'(\xi_1).}
$$
