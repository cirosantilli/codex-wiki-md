<h1 id="18i/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every $h\in G$ permutes the factors:

$$
h\bigl(f(t,\alpha)\bigr)
=\prod_{g\in G}(t-hg(\alpha))
=f(t,\alpha).
$$

Thus all coefficients lie in the [fixed field](../../../../../../../fixed-field.md) $L^G$. Let $E$ be the field they generate over $K$. Then

$$
K\subseteq E\subseteq L^G.
$$

Because $L=K(\alpha)=E(\alpha)$ and $f(t,\alpha)\in E[t]$ has degree $|G|$ with root $\alpha$,

$$
[L:E]\leq|G|.
$$

Part (a) and the [tower law for field extensions](../../../../../../../tower-law-for-field-extensions.md) give

$$
[L:E]=[L:L^G][L^G:E]=|G|[L^G:E].
$$

Consequently $[L^G:E]=1$, so

$$
\boxed{E=L^G.}
$$

This is the [coefficients of an orbit polynomial for a primitive generator](../../../../../../../coefficients-of-an-orbit-polynomial-for-a-primitive-generator.md) result.
