<h1 id="36a/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Above $T_B$, essentially all $N$ particles occupy the excited levels, so

$$
\boxed{\langle E\rangle\simeq N\epsilon,\qquad C\simeq0
\quad(T>T_B).}
$$

Below $T_B$, the ground-state condensate carries no energy and $\mu\simeq0$, hence

$$
\boxed{\langle E\rangle\simeq\frac{M\epsilon}{e^{\beta\epsilon}-1}
\quad(T<T_B).}
$$

Differentiating gives the [heat capacity](../../../../../../../heat-capacity.md)

$$
\boxed{C=\frac{\partial\langle E\rangle}{\partial T}
\simeq\frac{M\epsilon^2e^{\beta\epsilon}}
{kT^2(e^{\beta\epsilon}-1)^2}
\quad(T<T_B).}
$$

The displayed formula in the examination paper omits the factor $e^{\beta\epsilon}$. Its stated expression follows after the additional high-temperature approximation $\beta\epsilon\ll1$, for which $e^{\beta\epsilon}\simeq1$.
