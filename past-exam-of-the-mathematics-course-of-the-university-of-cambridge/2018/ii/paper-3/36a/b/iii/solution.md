↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At the [critical temperature](../../../../../../../critical-temperature.md) $T_B$, the excited states are just saturated and the condensate is about to form, so

$$
N=\frac{M}{e^{\epsilon/(kT_B)}-1}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{T_B=\frac{\epsilon}{k\log(1+M/N)}.}
$$
