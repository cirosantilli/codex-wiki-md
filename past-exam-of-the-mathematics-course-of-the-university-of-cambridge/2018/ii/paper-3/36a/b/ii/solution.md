↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At fixed [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md), the excited population is largest as $\mu\uparrow0$, hence

$$
\boxed{N_\epsilon^{\max}(T)=\frac{M}{e^{\epsilon/(kT)}-1}.}
$$

If $N>N_\epsilon^{\max}$, the excited levels cannot accommodate all particles. The excess $N-N_\epsilon^{\max}$ occupies the zero-energy level macroscopically: this is [Bose-Einstein condensation](../../../../../../../bose-einstein-condensation.md).
