<h1 id="36a/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $T>T_B$, the ground-state occupation is negligible to leading order, so $N\simeq N_\epsilon$. Comparing the number equation with its value at $T_B$ gives

$$
\frac{\epsilon-\mu}{T}=\frac\epsilon{T_B},
\qquad
\boxed{\mu=\epsilon\frac{T_B-T}{T_B}.}
$$

For $T<T_B$, $\mu$ is close to zero and

$$
N_0\simeq-\frac{kT}{\mu},
\qquad
N_\epsilon\simeq\frac{M}{e^{\epsilon/(kT)}-1}.
$$

Using $M=N(e^{\epsilon/(kT_B)}-1)$ in $N_0=N-N_\epsilon$ and solving for $\mu$ gives

$$
\boxed{\mu\simeq-\frac{kT}{N}
\frac{e^{\epsilon/(kT)}-1}
{e^{\epsilon/(kT)}-e^{\epsilon/(kT_B)}}.}
$$
