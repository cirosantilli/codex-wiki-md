<h1 id="36a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For each one-particle state $i$, a [boson](../../../../../../boson.md) occupation number can be any $n_i\geq0$. Its contribution to the [grand canonical partition function](../../../../../../grand-canonical-partition-function.md) is the [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md)

$$
\sum_{n_i=0}^{\infty}e^{-\beta n_i(\epsilon_i-\mu)}
=\frac1{1-e^{-\beta(\epsilon_i-\mu)}}.
$$

The noninteracting modes factorize, so

$$
\mathcal Z=\prod_i\frac1{1-e^{-\beta(\epsilon_i-\mu)}},
\qquad
\boxed{\log\mathcal Z=-\sum_i\log\left(1-e^{-\beta(\epsilon_i-\mu)}\right).}
$$

Convergence requires $\mu<\min_i\epsilon_i$. Since the lowest energy is zero, the limiting maximum [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mu_{\max}=0.}
$$
