<h1 id="16g/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The class of well-orderings is **not first-order axiomatizable**. If $S$ axiomatized it, add constants $c_0,c_1,\ldots$ and sentences

$$
c_{n+1}<c_n\qquad(n\geq0).
$$

Every finite subset has a finite well-order containing the required finite descending chain. By the [compactness theorem](../../../../../../compactness-theorem.md), the whole set has a model; that model satisfies $S$ but contains an infinite descending chain, so it is not a [well-order](../../../../../../well-order.md). This contradiction proves the claim, as summarized by [well-orders are not first-order axiomatizable](../../../../../../well-orders-are-not-first-order-axiomatizable.md).
