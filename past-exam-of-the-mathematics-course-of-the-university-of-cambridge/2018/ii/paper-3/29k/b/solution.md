The finite-horizon discrete-time [fundamental theorem of asset pricing](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-asset-pricing.md) states that the market has no [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md) if and only if there exists a probability measure $\mathbb Q$ equivalent to $\mathbb P$ under which every integrable discounted asset price $X^i$ is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md). Such a $\mathbb Q$ is an [equivalent martingale measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md).
