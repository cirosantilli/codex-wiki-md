<h1 id="29k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $V_t$ denote wealth measured in units of the strictly positive [numéraire](../../../../../../numeraire.md). A predictable [self-financing portfolio](../../../../../../self-financing-portfolio.md) is an [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md) if

$$
\boxed{V_0=0,\qquad V_T\geq0\ \mathbb P\text{-almost surely},
\qquad\mathbb P(V_T>0)>0.}
$$

Equivalently, one may permit $V_0\leq0$ provided one of the two gains is strict.
