<h1 id="29k/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Suppose an [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md) existed. Its discounted terminal wealth would satisfy $V_T\geq0$ $\mathbb P$-almost surely and $\mathbb P(V_T>0)>0$. Equivalence of $\mathbb Q$ and $\mathbb P$ gives the same two statements under $\mathbb Q$, so

$$
\mathbb E_{\mathbb Q}V_T>0.
$$

But part (c), after the usual admissibility or localization needed for integrability, gives

$$
\mathbb E_{\mathbb Q}V_T=V_0=0,
$$

a contradiction. Hence **the existence of the stated martingale measure rules out arbitrage.**
