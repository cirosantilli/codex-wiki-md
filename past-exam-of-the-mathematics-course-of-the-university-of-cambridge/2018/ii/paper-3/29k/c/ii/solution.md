↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $V$ is a $\mathbb Q$-martingale, then

$$
\mathbb E_{\mathbb Q}[V_T]
=\mathbb E_{\mathbb Q}[V_0]
=V_0,
$$

because $\mathcal F_0$ is $\mathbb P$-trivial and equivalence makes it $\mathbb Q$-trivial as well. Thus **(ii) $\Rightarrow$ (iii)**.
