↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The powers of $3$ modulo $10$ are

$$
3^0,3^1,3^2,3^3,3^4\equiv1,3,9,7,1\pmod{10}.
$$

They then repeat, and none of the first three positive exponents returns $1$. Hence **the least period is $\boxed{r=4}$.**
