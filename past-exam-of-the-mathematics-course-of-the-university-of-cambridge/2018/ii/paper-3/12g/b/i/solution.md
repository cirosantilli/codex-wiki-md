<h1 id="12g/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Because $D$ is minimal, every state is [accessible](../../../../../../../accessible-state-of-a-deterministic-finite-automaton.md). Choose a word $u$ taking the initial state to $p$, and let the nonempty word $v$ label the closed path $\gamma$. If a word $w$ took $p$ to an accept state, then

$$
uv^rw\in\mathcal L(D)\qquad(r=0,1,2,\ldots).
$$

These words have strictly increasing lengths because $v\ne\epsilon$, contradicting the assumption that $\mathcal L(D)$ is finite. Hence **no accept state is reachable from $p$.**
