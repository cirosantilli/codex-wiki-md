<h1 id="33a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Writing $\psi^s(x,u)=(\widetilde x(s;x,u),\widetilde u(s;x,u))$, the [infinitesimal generator of a Lie point symmetry](../../../../../../infinitesimal-generator-of-a-lie-point-symmetry.md) is

$$
V=\xi(x,u)\partial_x+\eta(x,u)\partial_u,
\qquad
\boxed{\xi=\left.\frac{\partial\widetilde x}{\partial s}\right|_{s=0},
\quad
\eta=\left.\frac{\partial\widetilde u}{\partial s}\right|_{s=0}.}
$$
