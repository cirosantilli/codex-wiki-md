<h1 id="33a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On the [jet space of a scalar ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../jet-space-of-a-scalar-ordinary-differential-equation.md), the $n$th [prolongation of a vector field](../../../../../../prolongation-of-a-vector-field.md) is

$$
\operatorname{pr}^{(n)}V
=V+\sum_{j=1}^n\eta^{(j)}\partial_{u^{(j)}},
\qquad
\eta^{(j)}=D_x\eta^{(j-1)}-u^{(j)}D_x\xi,
\qquad \eta^{(0)}=\eta,
$$

where $D_x$ is the [total derivative operator](../../../../../../total-derivative-operator.md). In particular,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{pr}^{(1)}V
=\xi\partial_x+\eta\partial_u+
\left[\eta_x+(\eta_u-\xi_x)u'-\xi_u(u')^2\right]\partial_{u'}.}
$$
