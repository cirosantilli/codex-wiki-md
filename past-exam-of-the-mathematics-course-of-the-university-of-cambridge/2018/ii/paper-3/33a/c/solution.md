<h1 id="33a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\Delta(x,u,u')=u'-F(x,u)$. Applying the [first prolongation](../../../../../../first-prolongation.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{pr}^{(1)}V(\Delta)
=\eta_x+(\eta_u-\xi_x)u'-\xi_u(u')^2-\xi F_x-\eta F_u.
$$

On the [solution manifold](../../../../../../solution-manifold.md) $\Delta=0$, one has $u'=F$. The infinitesimal symmetry criterion is therefore exactly

$$
\boxed{0=\eta_x+(\eta_u-\xi_x-F\xi_u)F-\xi F_x-\eta F_u.}
$$
