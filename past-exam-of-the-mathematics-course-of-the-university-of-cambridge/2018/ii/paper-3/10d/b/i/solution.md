↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $f$ is constant, then

$$
|f\rangle=\pm\frac12\sum_{b_1,b_2}|b_1b_2\rangle
=\pm H^{\otimes2}|00\rangle.
$$

The sign is a physically irrelevant [global phase](../../../../../../../global-phase.md).
