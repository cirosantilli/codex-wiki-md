↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $p_{-1}=0$, the [birth-death master equation](../../../../../../birth-death-master-equation.md) for birth rate $b_n=Bn$ and death rate $d_n=Dn$ is

$$
\dot p_n=B(n-1)p_{n-1}+D(n+1)p_{n+1}-(B+D)np_n,
\qquad n\geq0.
$$
