<h1 id="6c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The deterministic equation has solution $n(t)=n(0)e^{(B-D)t}$ and is identical in form to the equation for the stochastic [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value.md). It therefore identifies only the net rate

$$
r=B-D.
$$

The [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) equation also contains the total event rate $s=B+D$. Once $r$ and $s$ are inferred,

$$
\boxed{\ B=\frac{s+r}{2},\qquad D=\frac{s-r}{2}\ }.
$$

The deterministic trajectory cannot distinguish frequent births and deaths that nearly cancel from infrequent events with the same net growth, whereas fluctuations can.
