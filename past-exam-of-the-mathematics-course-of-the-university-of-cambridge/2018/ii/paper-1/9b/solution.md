<h1 id="9b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9B](../9b.md)

Let $\mathcal H=a'/a=\dot a$, where a prime denotes differentiation with respect to [conformal time](../../../../../conformal-time.md) $d\tau=dt/a$. Multiplying the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) by $a^2$ gives

$$
\mathcal H^2+kc^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho a^2.
$$

The [critical density](../../../../../critical-density.md) is the value obtained when $k=0$, so

$$
\Omega=\frac{\rho}{\rho_{\rm crit}}
=\frac{8\pi G\rho a^2}{3\mathcal H^2},
\qquad
\boxed{\ \frac{kc^2}{\mathcal H^2}=\Omega-1\ }.
$$

Since $\mathcal H'=a\ddot a$, the pressure-free [Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives

$$
2\mathcal H'=-\frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho a^2
=-(\mathcal H^2+kc^2),
$$

or

$$
2\mathcal H'+\mathcal H^2+kc^2=0.
$$

Differentiating $\Omega=1+kc^2/\mathcal H^2$ and substituting this equation yields

$$
\boxed{\ \Omega'=\mathcal H\Omega(\Omega-1)\ }.
$$

For an expanding universe $\mathcal H>0$. The [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) $\Omega=1$ is an unstable constant solution: curves starting with $0<\Omega<1$ decrease towards $0$, while curves starting with $\Omega>1$ rise away from $1$ and diverge as the closed model reaches maximum expansion. This is the [flatness problem](../../../../../flatness-problem.md).
