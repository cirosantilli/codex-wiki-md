<h1 id="21h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $v(x,y,z)$ for the displayed upper-unitriangular matrix. Matrix multiplication gives

$$
v(x,y,z)v(x',y',z')=v(x+x',y+y'+xz',z+z').
$$

Thus $V$ is the [real Heisenberg group](../../../../../../heisenberg-group.md) and $\Gamma$ is its [Integer Heisenberg group](../../../../../../integer-heisenberg-group.md). The subgroup $\Gamma$ is discrete. Choose an identity neighbourhood $U\subset V$ so small that

$$
UU^{-1}\cap\Gamma=\{1\}.
$$

Then the translates $\gamma U$ are pairwise disjoint, and the quotient map restricts on each one to a homeomorphism onto the same open subset of $N$. Hence every point has an evenly covered neighbourhood and

$$
\boxed{\ V\longrightarrow\Gamma\backslash V\text{ is a covering map}.\ }
$$

This is the general [quotient of a topological group by a discrete subgroup](../../../../../../quotient-of-a-topological-group-by-a-discrete-subgroup.md) construction.
