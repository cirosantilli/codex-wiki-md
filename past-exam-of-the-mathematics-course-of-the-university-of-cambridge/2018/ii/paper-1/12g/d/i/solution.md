<h1 id="12g/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The total computable maps

$$
n\longmapsto2n,\qquad n\longmapsto2n+1
$$

satisfy

$$
n\in X\iff2n\in X\oplus Y,
\qquad
n\in Y\iff2n+1\in X\oplus Y.
$$

Hence **$X\leq_mX\oplus Y$ and $Y\leq_mX\oplus Y$**.
