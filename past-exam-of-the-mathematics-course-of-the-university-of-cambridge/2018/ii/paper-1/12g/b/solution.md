<h1 id="12g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [many-one reduction](../../../../../../many-one-reduction.md) $A\leq_mB$ is a [total computable function](../../../../../../total-computable-function.md) $f$ such that

$$
n\in A\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(n)\in B.
$$

If $B$ is a [computably enumerable set](../../../../../../recursively-enumerable-set.md), compute $f(n)$ and run a semidecision procedure for $B$ on that value. It halts exactly when $f(n)\in B$, equivalently exactly when $n\in A$. **Therefore $A$ is computably enumerable.**
