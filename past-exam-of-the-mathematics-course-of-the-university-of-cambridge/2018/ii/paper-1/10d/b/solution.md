<h1 id="10d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

This is [superdense coding](../../../../../../superdense-coding.md). To encode $00,01,10,11$, Alice applies respectively $I,X,Z,XZ$ to her half of $|\Phi^+\rangle$. These local [Pauli operators](../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) transform the shared state into the four mutually orthogonal [Bell states](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) $|\Phi^+\rangle,|\Psi^+\rangle,|\Phi^-\rangle,|\Psi^-\rangle$, up to irrelevant global phases. Alice sends her one qubit to Bob. Bob now holds both qubits and performs a [Bell-basis measurement](../../../../../../bell-basis-measurement.md), whose outcome identifies Alice's two-bit message with certainty. **One transmitted qubit communicates two classical bits because one shared Bell pair was available beforehand.**
