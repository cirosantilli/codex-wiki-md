The [pumping lemma for context-free languages](../../../../../../pumping-lemma-for-context-free-languages.md) states that every [context-free language](../../../../../../context-free-language.md) $L$ has a [pumping length](../../../../../../pumping-length.md) $p$ such that every $w\in L$ with $|w|\geq p$ can be written

$$
w=uvxyz
$$

with $|vxy|\leq p$, $|vy|>0$, and $uv^ixy^iz\in L$ for every integer $i\geq0$.
