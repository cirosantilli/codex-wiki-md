<h1 id="27j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using the convention

$$
\widehat f(u)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e^{-iux}\,dx,
$$

the [Fourier transform of an interval indicator](../../../../../../fourier-transform-of-an-interval-indicator.md) is

$$
\widehat f(u)=\int_{-1}^{1}e^{-iux}\,dx
=\frac{e^{iu}-e^{-iu}}{iu}
=\frac{2\sin u}{u}.
$$

The removable value at the origin is obtained by taking the limit. Thus

$$
\boxed{\ \widehat f(u)=2\frac{\sin u}{u},
\qquad \widehat f(0)=2.\ }
$$
