<h1 id="30k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The pair $(M_n,\mathcal F_n)_{n\geq0}$ is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) when $(\mathcal F_n)$ is a [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md), each $M_n$ is $\mathcal F_n$-measurable and integrable, and

$$
\boxed{\ \mathbb E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=M_n
\quad\text{almost surely for every }n.\ }
$$
