<h1 id="30k/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every martingale is a [supermartingale](../../../../../../../supermartingale.md), with equality in its defining conditional expectation. Taking expectations and using the [tower property of conditional expectation](../../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E[X_{n+1}]=\mathbb E[X_n],
$$

so induction yields

$$
\boxed{\ \mathbb E[X_n]=\mathbb E[X_0]\quad\text{for every }n.\ }
$$
