↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Arnold-Liouville theorem](../../../../../../liouville-arnold-theorem.md) states that if $F_1,\ldots,F_n$ are independent commuting first integrals and a connected component $L$ of a regular common level

$$
F_1=c_1,\ldots,F_n=c_n
$$

is compact, then $L$ is diffeomorphic to the $n$-torus. A neighbourhood of $L$ admits [action-angle variables](../../../../../../action-angle-variables.md) $(I_1,\ldots,I_n,\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n)$ in which

$$
\omega=\sum_jdI_j\wedge d\theta_j,
\qquad H=H(I),
$$

and the motion is linear on each invariant torus:

$$
\dot I_j=0,
\qquad
\dot\theta_j=\frac{\partial H}{\partial I_j}.
$$
