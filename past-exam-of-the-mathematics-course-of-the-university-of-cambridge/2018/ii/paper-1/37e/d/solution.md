This is the [Past incompleteness of the expanding flat slicing of de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../past-incompleteness-of-the-expanding-flat-slicing-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md). The coordinate patch covers only half of global [de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../de-sitter-spacetime.md), and the surface $t=-\infty$ is its past boundary. The finite proper-time endpoint is **a coordinate-patch boundary, not a curvature singularity**: the timelike geodesic continues smoothly after passing to global de Sitter coordinates.
