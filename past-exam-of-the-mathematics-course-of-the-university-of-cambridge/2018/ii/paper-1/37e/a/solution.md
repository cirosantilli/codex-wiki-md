↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [Expanding flat slicing of de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../expanding-flat-slicing-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md), take the [geodesic Lagrangian](../../../../../../geodesic-lagrangian.md)

$$
L=\frac12g_{\mu\nu}\dot x^\mu\dot x^\nu
=\frac12\left[-\dot t^2+e^{2Ht}(\dot x^2+\dot y^2+\dot z^2)\right].
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) for $t$ is

$$
\ddot t+He^{2Ht}(\dot x^2+\dot y^2+\dot z^2)=0,
$$

while each spatial coordinate $x^i$ obeys

$$
\ddot x^i+2H\dot t\dot x^i=0.
$$

Comparing these equations with the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) gives the complete list of nonzero [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md):

$$
\boxed{\Gamma^t{}_{ij}=He^{2Ht}\delta_{ij},
\qquad
\Gamma^i{}_{tj}=\Gamma^i{}_{jt}=H\delta^i{}_j,
\qquad i,j\in\{x,y,z\}.}
$$
