The [Erdős-Stone theorem](../../../../../../erdos-stone-theorem.md) states that if the [chromatic number](../../../../../../chromatic-number.md) of $H$ is $r+1\geq2$, then

$$
\operatorname{ex}(n,H)
=\left(1-\frac1r+o(1)\right)\binom n2.
$$

If $H$ is a [bipartite graph](../../../../../../bipartite-graph.md) with at least one edge, its chromatic number is two, so $r=1$ and therefore

$$
\boxed{\ \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\operatorname{ex}(n,H)}{\binom n2}=0.\ }
$$
