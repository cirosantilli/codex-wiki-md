<h1 id="15b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiate the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md):

$$
2H\dot H
=\frac{1}{3M_{\rm Pl}^2}
\left(\dot\phi\,\ddot\phi+c^2V'(\phi)\dot\phi\right).
$$

The scalar-field equation gives $\ddot\phi+c^2V'=-3H\dot\phi$, hence

$$
2H\dot H=-\frac{H\dot\phi^2}{M_{\rm Pl}^2}.
$$

By continuity this also holds through isolated zeros of $H$, so the [Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\ \dot H=-\frac{\dot\phi^2}{2M_{\rm Pl}^2}\ }.
$$
