<h1 id="13j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

No intercept should be included unless the ordering of the two teams has an external meaning such as home advantage. If

$$
\eta_{ij,k\ell}
=\beta_i+\beta_j+\beta_{\{i,j\}}
-\beta_k-\beta_\ell-\beta_{\{k,\ell\}},
$$

then reversing the teams gives $\eta_{k\ell,ij}=-\eta_{ij,k\ell}$, so the [logistic model](../../../../../../logistic-model.md) automatically gives

$$
\mathbb P(\{k,\ell\}\text{ wins against }\{i,j\})
=1-\mathbb P(\{i,j\}\text{ wins against }\{k,\ell\}).
$$

An intercept would not change sign when the teams are swapped and would violate this required symmetry. Thus **an intercept does not make sense in the stated model**.
