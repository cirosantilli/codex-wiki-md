<h1 id="25i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A twice differentiable curve $\gamma:I\to S$ is a [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) when its velocity is parallel along the curve:

$$
\frac{D\dot\gamma}{dt}=0.
$$

For a surface embedded in $\mathbb R^3$, the [ambient acceleration criterion for a surface geodesic](../../../../../../ambient-acceleration-criterion-for-a-surface-geodesic.md) gives the equivalent condition

$$
\boxed{\ddot\gamma(t)\perp T_{\gamma(t)}S\quad(t\in I)}.
$$

The parameter $t$ is then an [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md), and $|\dot\gamma|$ is constant.
