<h1 id="25i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take the [circular cylinder](../../../../../../circular-cylinder.md)

$$
S=\{(R\cos\theta,R\sin\theta,z):\theta\in\mathbb R, z\in\mathbb R\},
\qquad R>0.
$$

Its [first fundamental form](../../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) is $R^2d\theta^2+dz^2$. The universal covering parametrization $\mathbb R^2\to S$ is therefore a [local isometry](../../../../../../local-isometry.md) from the [Euclidean plane](../../../../../../euclidean-plane.md), after scaling the first coordinate. By [geodesic preservation by a local isometry](../../../../../../geodesic-preservation-by-a-local-isometry.md), every geodesic on the cylinder lifts to a straight line defined for all $t\in\mathbb R$. Thus **the cylinder is geodesically complete**. It is noncompact because $z$ is unbounded, and it is not a plane because one of its [principal curvatures](../../../../../../principal-curvature.md) is $1/R$.
