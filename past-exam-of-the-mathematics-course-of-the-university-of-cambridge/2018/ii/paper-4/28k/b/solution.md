The [Taylor theorem with Lagrange remainder](../../../../../../taylor-theorem-with-lagrange-remainder.md) supplies a point $\xi_i$ between $x_0$ and $X_i=x_0+hZ_i$ such that

$$
g(X_i)=g(x_0)+hZ_i g'(x_0)+\frac{h^2Z_i^2}{2}g''(\xi_i).
$$

Since $Z_i^2=1$ and $Y_i=g(X_i)+\varepsilon_i$,

$$
\boxed{Y_i-g(x_0)=hZ_i g'(x_0)+\frac{h^2}{2}g''(\xi_i)+\varepsilon_i}.
$$
