<h1 id="28k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Define

$$
Z_i=\begin{cases}1,&U_i^*\geq1/2,\\-1,&U_i^*<1/2.\end{cases}
$$

The $Z_i$ are independent [Rademacher random variables](../../../../../../rademacher-distribution.md). Sending

$$
\boxed{X_i=x_0+hZ_i}
$$

to the machine therefore produces the required independent design points.
