<h1 id="26j/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For every $x\in X$, invariance gives

$$
\mu(T^{-1}\{x\})=\mu(\{x\})>0.
$$

Thus $T^{-1}\{x\}$ is nonempty, so $T$ is a [surjective function](../../../../../../surjective-function.md). A surjection from a [finite set](../../../../../../finite-set.md) to itself is a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{T\text{ is bijective}}.
$$

This is the [finite full-support measure-preserving map is bijective](../../../../../../finite-full-support-measure-preserving-map-is-bijective.md) principle.
