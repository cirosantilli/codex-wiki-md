The [Birkhoff ergodic theorem](../../../../../../birkhoff-ergodic-theorem.md) states that if $T$ is a [measure-preserving transformation](../../../../../../measure-preserving-transformation.md) and $f\in L^1(\mu)$, then

$$
\boxed{
\frac1n\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}f(T^jx)
\longrightarrow
\mathbb E[f\mid\mathcal I](x)
\quad\text{for almost every }x
},
$$

where $\mathcal I$ is the [invariant sigma-algebra](../../../../../../invariant-sigma-algebra.md). The limit has the same integral as $f$. If $T$ is an [ergodic transformation](../../../../../../ergodicity.md), the limit is the constant $\int_Xf\,d\mu$ almost everywhere.
