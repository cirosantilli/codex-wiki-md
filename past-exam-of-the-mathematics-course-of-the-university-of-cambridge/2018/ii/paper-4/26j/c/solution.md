<h1 id="26j/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take the unit circle $X=\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ with its [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md) and normalized [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md), and let

$$
T(x)=2x\pmod1.
$$

This is the [doubling map](../../../../../../dyadic-transformation.md). Its two inverse branches halve length, so [Lebesgue invariance of the doubling map](../../../../../../lebesgue-invariance-of-the-doubling-map.md) gives $\mu(T^{-1}A)=\mu(A)$. Moreover

$$
\boxed{T^{-1}\{x\}=\left\{\frac x2,\frac{x+1}2\right\}\quad\text{and hence}\quad|T^{-1}\{x\}|=2}
$$

for every point of the circle.
