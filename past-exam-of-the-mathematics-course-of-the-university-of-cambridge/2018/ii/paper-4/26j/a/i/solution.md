A [probability measure](../../../../../../../probability-measure.md) $\mu$ is $T$-invariant when

$$
\boxed{\mu(T^{-1}A)=\mu(A)\quad\text{for every }A\in\mathcal A}.
$$

Equivalently, the [pushforward measure](../../../../../../../pushforward-measure.md) satisfies $T_*\mu=\mu$, so $T$ is a [measure-preserving transformation](../../../../../../../measure-preserving-transformation.md).
