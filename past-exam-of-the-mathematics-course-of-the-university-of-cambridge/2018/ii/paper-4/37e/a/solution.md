<h1 id="37e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a slowly moving particle in the [weak-field approximation](../../../../../../weak-field-approximation.md), $dt/d\tau=1+O(\Phi,v^2)$ and terms containing two spatial velocities may be neglected. The spatial [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) reduces to

$$
\frac{d^2x^i}{dt^2}=-\Gamma^i{}_{00}.
$$

For $g_{00}=-(1+2\Phi)$, the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) is

$$
\Gamma^i{}_{00}
=\frac12g^{ij}(-\partial_jg_{00})
=\partial_i\Phi+O(\Phi^2).
$$

Hence the [Newtonian limit of general relativity](../../../../../../newtonian-limit.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{d^2\mathbf x}{dt^2}=-\nabla\Phi}.
$$
