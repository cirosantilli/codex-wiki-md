No. The property

$$
\mathcal P(f)\iff|\operatorname{dom}f|=9
$$

is [extensional](../../../../../../extensional-property-of-programs.md): it depends only on the partial function computed, not on its program index. It is nontrivial, since some partial recursive functions have a nine-element domain and others do not. The [Rice theorem](../../../../../../rice-s-theorem.md) therefore implies that its [index set](../../../../../../index-set.md)

$$
\{n:|W_n|=9\}
$$

is undecidable. Hence

$$
\boxed{\text{there is no algorithm that decides whether }|W_n|=9.}
$$
