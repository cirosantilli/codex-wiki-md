<h1 id="4g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply part (b) to

$$
g(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
0,&y<x,\\
\uparrow,&y\geq x.
\end{cases}
$$

There is an index $m$ such that $f_{m,1}(y)\simeq g(m,y)$. Its [domain of a partial function](../../../../../../domain-of-a-partial-function.md) is exactly

$$
W_m=\operatorname{dom}f_{m,1}=\{0,1,\ldots,m-1\}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{|W_m|=m.}
$$
