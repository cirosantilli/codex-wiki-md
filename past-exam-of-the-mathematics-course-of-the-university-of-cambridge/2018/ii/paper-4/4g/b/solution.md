<h1 id="4g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By the [S-m-n theorem](../../../../../../smn-theorem.md), there is a total recursive function $h$ such that

$$
f_{h(x),1}(y)\simeq g(x,y)
$$

for all $x,y$. Apply the [recursion theorem](../../../../../../kleene-s-recursion-theorem.md) to $h$. It supplies an index $e$ for which

$$
f_{e,1}\simeq f_{h(e),1}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{f_{e,1}(y)\simeq g(e,y)\quad\text{for every }y\in\mathbb N.}
$$
