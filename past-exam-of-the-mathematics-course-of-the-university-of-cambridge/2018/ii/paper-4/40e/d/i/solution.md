Here $\omega_4=i$. Splitting into even and odd pairs gives

$$
y^{(E)}=(1,1),\qquad y^{(O)}=(-1,-1),
$$

whose two-point [inverse discrete Fourier transforms](../../../../../../../inverse-discrete-fourier-transform.md) are $(2,0)$ and $(-2,0)$. The butterfly therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\mathcal F_4^{-1}(1,-1,1,-1)=(0,0,4,0)}.
$$
