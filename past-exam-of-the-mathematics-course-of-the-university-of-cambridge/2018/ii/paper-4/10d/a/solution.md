<h1 id="10d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $P=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$. Since $|\psi\rangle$ is normalized, $P^2=P=P^\dagger$. Hence the [Householder reflection](../../../../../../householder-transformation.md)

$$
A=I-2P
$$

is Hermitian and

$$
A^\dagger A=A^2=I-4P+4P^2=I.
$$

Therefore **$A$ is unitary**.
