<h1 id="21h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The given homeomorphisms and [homotopy invariance of homology](../../../../../../homotopy-invariance-of-homology.md) give

$$
H_1(K)=H_1(S^n)=0
$$

because $n\geq2$, while $M\simeq S^{n-1}$ is path connected and $L$ is the [disjoint union](../../../../../../disjoint-union.md) of two copies of $S^{n-1}$. Hence

$$
H_0(M)\cong\mathbb Z,
\qquad
H_0(L)\cong\mathbb Z^2,
\qquad
H_0(K)\cong\mathbb Z.
$$

The degree-one and degree-zero part of the [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](../../../../../../mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) is therefore

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^2
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\oplus H_0(N)
\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\longrightarrow0.
$$

Taking the [rank of an abelian group](../../../../../../rank-of-an-abelian-group.md) throughout this short exact sequence gives

$$
2=1+\operatorname{rank}H_0(N)-1,
$$

so $\operatorname{rank}H_0(N)=2$. Since the [zeroth homology group](../../../../../../zeroth-homology-group.md) is freely generated by the [path components](../../../../../../path-component.md),

$$
\boxed{|N|\text{ has exactly two path components}.}
$$
