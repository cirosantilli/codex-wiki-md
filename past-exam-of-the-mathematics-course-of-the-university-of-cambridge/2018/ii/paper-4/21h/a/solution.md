<h1 id="21h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $i:L\hookrightarrow M$, $j:L\hookrightarrow N$, $r:M\hookrightarrow K$, and $s:N\hookrightarrow K$ be the inclusions. The [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](../../../../../../mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) is the [long exact sequence in homology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-homology.md)

$$
\cdots\longrightarrow H_k(L)
\xrightarrow{(i_*,-j_*)}
H_k(M)\oplus H_k(N)
\xrightarrow{r_*+s_*}
H_k(K)
\xrightarrow{\partial_*}
H_{k-1}(L)
\longrightarrow\cdots.
$$

The same sequence continues through degree zero, or uniformly in all degrees when written with [reduced homology](../../../../../../reduced-homology.md).
