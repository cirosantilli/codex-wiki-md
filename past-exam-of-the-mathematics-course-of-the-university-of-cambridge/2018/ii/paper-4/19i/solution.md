<h1 id="19i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [19I](../19i.md)

The [special unitary group](../../../../../special-unitary-group.md) in two dimensions is

$$
G=SU(2)=\{A\in M_2(\mathbb C):A^*A=I,\ \det A=1\}.
$$

Let $V_1=\mathbb C^2$ be its defining [group representation](../../../../../group-representation.md), and for $n\geq0$ let

$$
V_n=S^nV_1,
$$

the [homogeneous polynomial representation of SU2](../../../../../homogeneous-polynomial-representation-of-su2.md) on homogeneous polynomials of degree $n$ in two variables. It has [degree of a representation](../../../../../degree-of-a-representation.md) $n+1$. The representations $V_0,V_1,V_2,\ldots$ are pairwise nonisomorphic, continuous, finite-dimensional [irreducible representations](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md), and the [classification of finite-dimensional representations of SU2](../../../../../classification-of-finite-dimensional-representations-of-su2.md) says that this is the complete list.

Every $A\in SU(2)$ is conjugate to $\operatorname{diag}(z,z^{-1})$ with $|z|=1$. The monomial basis of $V_n$ gives the [character of the homogeneous polynomial representation of SU2](../../../../../character-of-the-homogeneous-polynomial-representation-of-su2.md)

$$
\boxed{\chi_{V_n}(A)=z^n+z^{n-2}+\cdots+z^{-n}}.
$$

Writing $z=e^{i\theta}$, this is $\sin((n+1)\theta)/\sin\theta$, interpreted by continuity when $\sin\theta=0$. The [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition for SU2](../../../../../clebsch-gordan-decomposition-for-su2.md) is

$$
\boxed{V_m\otimes V_n\cong\bigoplus_{j=0}^{\min(m,n)}V_{m+n-2j}}.
$$
