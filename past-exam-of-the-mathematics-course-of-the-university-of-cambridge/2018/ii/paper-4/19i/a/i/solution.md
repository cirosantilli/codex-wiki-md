↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Applying the [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition for SU2](../../../../../../../clebsch-gordan-decomposition-for-su2.md) gives

$$
\boxed{V_4\otimes V_3\cong V_7\oplus V_5\oplus V_3\oplus V_1},
$$

and

$$
V_3\otimes V_3\cong V_6\oplus V_4\oplus V_2\oplus V_0.
$$

On the summand $V_{6-2j}$, interchange of the two tensor factors acts by $(-1)^j$; this is the [flip parity in the SU2 tensor square](../../../../../../../flip-parity-in-the-su2-tensor-square.md). The even-$j$ summands make the [symmetric square](../../../../../../../symmetric-square.md), so

$$
\boxed{S^2V_3\cong V_6\oplus V_2}.
$$
