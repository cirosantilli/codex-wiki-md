<h1 id="19i/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Repeatedly using $V_r\otimes V_1\cong V_{r+1}\oplus V_{r-1}$ shows that the multiplicity of $V_{n-2j}$ in the nth [tensor power](../../../../../../../tensor-power.md) satisfies Pascal's recursion. Consequently

$$
\boxed{V_1^{\otimes n}\cong
\bigoplus_{j=0}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}
\left(\binom nj-\binom n{j-1}\right)V_{n-2j}},
$$

where $\binom n{-1}=0$. The multiplicity is the difference between the numbers of weight vectors of weights $n-2j$ and $n-2j+2$.
