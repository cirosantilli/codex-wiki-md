↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Writing $\rho(A)X=A_1XA_1^{-1}$, we have

$$
\rho(A)\rho(B)X=A_1B_1X B_1^{-1}A_1^{-1}=(AB)_1X(AB)_1^{-1}=\rho(AB)X,
$$

and $\rho(I)=I$, so this [conjugation representation](../../../../../../conjugation-representation.md) is indeed a [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md).

The underlying three-dimensional module is

$$
W=\mathbb C^3\cong V_1\oplus V_0.
$$

Using $M_3(\mathbb C)=\operatorname{End}(W)\cong W\otimes W^*$, the [self-duality of finite-dimensional SU2 representations](../../../../../../self-duality-of-finite-dimensional-su2-representations.md), and $V_1\otimes V_1\cong V_2\oplus V_0$, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_3(\mathbb C)
&\cong(V_1\oplus V_0)\otimes(V_1\oplus V_0)\\
&\cong(V_2\oplus V_0)\oplus V_1\oplus V_1\oplus V_0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore the irreducible decomposition is

$$
\boxed{M_3(\mathbb C)\cong V_2\oplus2V_1\oplus2V_0}.
$$
