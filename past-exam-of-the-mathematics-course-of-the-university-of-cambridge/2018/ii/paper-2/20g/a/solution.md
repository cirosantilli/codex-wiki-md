<h1 id="20g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [cyclotomic polynomial](../../../../../../cyclotomic-polynomial.md) of the primitive $p$th root $\omega$ is

$$
\Phi_p(X)=1+X+\cdots+X^{p-1}.
$$

By the [irreducibility of cyclotomic polynomials](../../../../../../irreducibility-of-cyclotomic-polynomials.md), this is the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) of $\omega$ over $\mathbb Q$. The [prime cyclotomic field degree](../../../../../../prime-cyclotomic-field-degree.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{[L:\mathbb Q]=\deg\Phi_p=p-1.}
$$
