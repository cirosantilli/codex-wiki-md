<h1 id="20g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The conjugates of $\omega$ are $\omega,ldots,\omega^{p-1}$. From

$$
X^p-1=(X-1)\Phi_p(X)
$$

we obtain, at every nontrivial $p$th root $\alpha$,

$$
\Phi_p'(\alpha)=\frac{p\alpha^{-1}}{\alpha-1}.
$$

The product of the roots is one and $\prod_{a=1}^{p-1}(1-\omega^a)=\Phi_p(1)=p$. The derivative formula for a [polynomial discriminant](../../../../../../polynomial-discriminant.md) therefore yields the [discriminant of a prime cyclotomic power basis](../../../../../../discriminant-of-a-prime-cyclotomic-power-basis.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{disc}(1,\omega,\ldots,\omega^{p-2})
=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}p^{p-2}.}
$$

Since $p\equiv1\pmod4$, the sign is positive. The [Vandermonde determinant](../../../../../../vandermonde-determinant.md)

$$
\Delta=\prod_{1\leq a<b\leq p-1}(\omega^a-\omega^b)
$$

belongs to $L$ and satisfies $\Delta^2=p^{p-2}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\sqrt p=\mathord\pm\frac{\Delta}{p^{(p-3)/2}}\in L.}
$$
