A [branch point](../../../../../../branch-point.md) of a multivalued analytic function is a point around which analytic continuation along a closed loop can return a different value. A [branch cut](../../../../../../branch-cut.md) is a curve or union of curves removed from the domain so that loops causing this monodromy are excluded and one single-valued branch can be chosen.
