<h1 id="36a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [canonical ensemble](../../../../../../canonical-ensemble.md), a one-particle velocity state has kinetic energy $E(\mathbf v)=m|\mathbf v|^2/2$ and hence probability density proportional to $e^{-\beta E}$, where $\beta=(k_BT)^{-1}$. The three Cartesian [Gaussian integrals](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) normalize the density to the [Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution](../../../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-velocity-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{
f(\mathbf v)=
\left(\frac{m}{2\pi k_BT}\right)^{3/2}
\exp\left(-\frac{m|\mathbf v|^2}{2k_BT}\right).}
$$

Integrating over a spherical shell of radius $v$ and area $4\pi v^2$ gives the [Maxwell speed distribution](../../../../../../maxwell-speed-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{
g(v)=4\pi v^2
\left(\frac{m}{2\pi k_BT}\right)^{3/2}
e^{-mv^2/(2k_BT)},\qquad v\geq0.}
$$

Since

$$
\frac d{dv}\log g(v)=\frac2v-\frac{mv}{k_BT},
$$

its unique positive maximum is the [Most probable speed in the Maxwell distribution](../../../../../../most-probable-speed-in-the-maxwell-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{v_{\rm mp}=\sqrt{\frac{2k_BT}{m}}.}
$$
