↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The $x$ component of the [Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution](../../../../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-velocity-distribution.md) is a centered [normal random variable](../../../../../../../gaussian-random-variable.md), so $\langle v_x\rangle=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\langle\omega\rangle
=\omega_0\left(1+\frac{\langle v_x\rangle}{c}\right)
=\omega_0.}
$$
