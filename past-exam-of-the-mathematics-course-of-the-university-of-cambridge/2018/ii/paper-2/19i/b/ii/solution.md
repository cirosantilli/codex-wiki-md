<h1 id="19i/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [permutation character of a coset action](../../../../../../../permutation-character-of-a-coset-action.md) on $G/H$ is $1_G+\lambda$. The [tensor identity for an induced character](../../../../../../../tensor-identity-for-an-induced-character.md) therefore gives

$$
\operatorname{Ind}_H^G(\chi\mathbin{\downarrow}_H)
=\chi(1_G+\lambda)=\chi+\chi\lambda.
$$

If $\psi\mathbin{\downarrow}_H=\chi\mathbin{\downarrow}_H$ is irreducible, then [Frobenius reciprocity](../../../../../../../frobenius-reciprocity.md) gives

$$
1=\langle\chi\mathbin{\downarrow}_H,
\psi\mathbin{\downarrow}_H\rangle_H
=\langle\chi+\chi\lambda,\psi\rangle_G.
$$

Both $\chi$ and $\chi\lambda$ are irreducible, so

$$
\boxed{\psi=\chi\quad\text{or}\quad\psi=\chi\lambda.}
$$
