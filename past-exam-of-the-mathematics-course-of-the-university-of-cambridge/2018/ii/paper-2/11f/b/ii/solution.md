**False.** Take the bounded nonconstant continuous function $f(x)=\sin x$. If polynomials $P_n$ converged uniformly to $f$ on $\mathbb R$, then each sufficiently late $P_n$ would be bounded on $\mathbb R$ and hence constant. A uniform limit of constants is constant, a contradiction. More generally, the [globally uniform limit of real polynomials](../../../../../../../globally-uniform-limit-of-real-polynomials.md) is itself a polynomial.
