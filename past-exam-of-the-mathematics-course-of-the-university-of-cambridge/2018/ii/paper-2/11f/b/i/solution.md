↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** For each $n$, the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](../../../../../../../weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md) gives a polynomial $P_n$ such that

$$
\sup_{|x|\leq n}|P_n(x)-f(x)|<\frac1n.
$$

For each fixed $x\in\mathbb R$, this estimate applies once $n\geq|x|$, so $P_n(x)\to f(x)$ pointwise.
