The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E|ZX_n|
\leq(\mathbb E|Z|^2)^{1/2}
(\mathbb E|X_n|^2)^{1/2}
\leq\lVert Z\rVert_2<\infty.
$$

Thus **$ZX_n$ is integrable** for every $n$.
