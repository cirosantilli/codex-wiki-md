<h1 id="37e/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $\Lambda=-|\Lambda|$. For $k=0$,

$$
V(a)=-\frac{\mu}{a}+\frac{|\Lambda|a^2}{3},
\qquad C=0.
$$

The potential is strictly increasing from $-\infty$ to $+\infty$ and crosses the energy line at

$$
\boxed{a_{\max}=\left(\frac{3\mu}{|\Lambda|}\right)^{1/3}.}
$$

The expanding universe reaches this [turning point](../../../../../../../turning-point.md), reverses, and ends in a [Big Crunch](../../../../../../../big-crunch.md). Thus **a negative cosmological constant forces recollapse in this spatially flat pressureless model**.
