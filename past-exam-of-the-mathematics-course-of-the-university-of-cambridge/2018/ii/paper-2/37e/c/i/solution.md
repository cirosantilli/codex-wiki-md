<h1 id="37e/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With $\Lambda>0$ and $k=-1$, the [Friedmann effective potential for pressureless matter](../../../../../../../friedmann-effective-potential-for-pressureless-matter.md) is

$$
V(a)=-\frac{\mu}{a}-\frac{\Lambda a^2}{3},
\qquad C=1.
$$

Although $V$ has one maximum, it is negative, so $C=1$ lies above it and $\dot a^2=C-V(a)$ never vanishes. An initially small expanding universe therefore expands forever. At large $a$,

$$
\frac{\dot a}{a}\longrightarrow\sqrt{\frac{\Lambda}{3}},
$$

so the expansion approaches the exponential [de Sitter scale factor in flat slicing](../../../../../../../de-sitter-scale-factor-in-flat-slicing.md). **There is no turning point.**
