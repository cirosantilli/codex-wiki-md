<h1 id="21h/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Triangulate $S^n$ and $S^m$. By the [Simplicial approximation theorem](../../../../../../simplicial-approximation-theorem.md), after subdividing the domain, a continuous map $f:S^n\to S^m$ is homotopic to the realization of a simplicial map $g$. Since $n<m$, the image of $|g|$ lies in the $n$-skeleton of the target triangulation and therefore misses an interior point of every $m$-simplex. A sphere with one point removed is homeomorphic to the [Euclidean space](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $\mathbb R^m$, which is [contractible](../../../../../../contractible-space.md). Hence $|g|$, and therefore $f$, is homotopic to a constant map. Thus

$$
\boxed{n<m\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{every }f:S^n\to S^m\text{ is null-homotopic}.}
$$
