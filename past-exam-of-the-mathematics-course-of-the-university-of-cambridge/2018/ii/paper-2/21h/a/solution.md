<h1 id="21h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For every nonempty simplex $\sigma$ of $K$, let $b_\sigma$ be its barycentre. The first [barycentric subdivision](../../../../../../barycentric-subdivision.md) $K'$ has the $b_\sigma$ as vertices, and

$$
[b_{\sigma_0},\ldots,b_{\sigma_q}]
$$

is a simplex exactly when, after reordering, $\sigma_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq\sigma_q$. Its realization is the same [polyhedron](../../../../../../polyhedron.md) as that of $K$. The [iterated barycentric subdivision](../../../../../../iterated-barycentric-subdivision.md) is defined by

$$
\boxed{K^{(0)}=K,
\qquad K^{(r+1)}=(K^{(r)})'.}
$$
