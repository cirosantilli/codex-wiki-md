<h1 id="25i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose unequal positive numbers $a,b$. The planar [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md)

$$
\gamma(t)=(a\cos t,b\sin t,0),
\qquad 0\leq t\leq2\pi,
$$

is a simple closed curve and is not a circle. The nonidentity proper [rigid motion](../../../../../../rigid-transformation.md)

$$
R=\operatorname{diag}(-1,-1,1),
\qquad T=0,
$$

is rotation through $\pi$ about the $z$-axis, and $R\gamma(t)=\gamma(t+\pi)$. It therefore preserves the curve as a set.
