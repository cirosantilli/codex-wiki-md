<h1 id="1c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $z\ne0$, the principal value of [complex exponentiation](../../../../../../complex-exponentiation.md) is $z^w=\exp(w\operatorname{Log}z)$, where the [principal complex logarithm](../../../../../../principal-complex-logarithm.md) is $\operatorname{Log}z=\log|z|+i\operatorname{Arg}z$ with $-\pi<\operatorname{Arg}z\leq\pi$. [De Moivre's theorem](../../../../../../de-moivre-s-theorem.md) states that $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n=\cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta)$ for every [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) $n$.

Since $\sqrt3+i=2e^{i\pi/6}$, the six roots are

$$
\boxed{z=2^{1/6}\exp\left(i\left(\frac{\pi}{36}+\frac{k\pi}{3}\right)\right),\qquad k=0,1,\ldots,5.}
$$
