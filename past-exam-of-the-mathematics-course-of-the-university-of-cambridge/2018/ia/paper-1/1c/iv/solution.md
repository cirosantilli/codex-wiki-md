↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The base $e^{5i\pi/2}$ is the [complex number](../../../../../../complex-number.md) $i$. Principal exponentiation uses the principal logarithm of that base, so this is the same equation as in part iii:

$$
\boxed{z=\frac13+4k-\frac{2i\log2}{\pi},\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$
