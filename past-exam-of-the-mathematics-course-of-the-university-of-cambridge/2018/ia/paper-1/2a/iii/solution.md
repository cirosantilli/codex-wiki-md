↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In [Einstein notation](../../../../../../einstein-notation.md), $(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf x)n_i=n_in_jx_j$, so the matrix and, for $\alpha\ne0$, its [matrix inverse](../../../../../../matrix-inverse.md) are

$$
\boxed{A_{ij}=\delta_{ij}+(\alpha-1)n_in_j,\qquad B_{ij}=\delta_{ij}+(\alpha^{-1}-1)n_in_j.}
$$
