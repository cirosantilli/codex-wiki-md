<h1 id="2a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The only noninvertible case is $\alpha=0$. Then $\Phi$ is the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto $\mathbf n^\perp$, so

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{im}\Phi=\mathbf n^\perp,\quad \ker\Phi=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf n\},\quad \operatorname{rank}\Phi=2,\quad \operatorname{nullity}\Phi=1.}
$$

These dimensions agree with the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md).
