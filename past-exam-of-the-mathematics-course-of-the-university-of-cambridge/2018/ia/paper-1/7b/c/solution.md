↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [generalized characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../generalized-characteristic-polynomial.md) is

$$
\det(A-\lambda B)=-(\lambda-2)(\lambda-4)(3\lambda-10).
$$

The [generalized Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../generalized-rayleigh-quotient.md) is maximized by the largest generalized eigenvalue, $4$. Solving $(A-4B)\mathbf v=0$ gives

$$
\boxed{\max_{\mathbf v\ne0}F(\mathbf v)=4,\qquad \mathbf v\propto(-1,2,0)^T.}
$$
