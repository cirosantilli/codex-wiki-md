↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Eigenvectors belonging to distinct eigenvalues are [linearly independent](../../../../../../../linear-independence.md). With $n$ distinct eigenvalues there are $n$ eigenvectors forming a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md), and the matrix is [diagonalizable](../../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md). In this eigenbasis, the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) is a weighted average of the eigenvalues, so it is maximized by choosing $\mathbf v$ in the eigenspace of the largest eigenvalue.
