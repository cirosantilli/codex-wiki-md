<h1 id="7b/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Multiplication by $\mathbf v^*$ gives

$$
\lambda=\frac{\mathbf v^*A\mathbf v}{\mathbf v^*B\mathbf v}.
$$

The numerator is real because $A$ is symmetric, while the denominator is strictly positive because $B$ is [positive definite](../../../../../../../positive-definite-matrix.md). Hence $\lambda$ is real. As $A-\lambda B$ is real and singular, its kernel contains a nonzero real generalized eigenvector.
