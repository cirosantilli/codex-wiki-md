<h1 id="7b/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $A\mathbf u=\lambda B\mathbf u$ and $A\mathbf v=\mu B\mathbf v$, symmetry gives

$$
\lambda\mathbf u^TB\mathbf v=(A\mathbf u)^T\mathbf v=\mathbf u^TA\mathbf v=\mu\mathbf u^TB\mathbf v.
$$

If $\lambda\ne\mu$, **$\mathbf u^TB\mathbf v=0$**: the eigenvectors are orthogonal in the [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product.md) induced by $B$.
