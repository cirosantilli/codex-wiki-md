<h1 id="7b/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

From $A\mathbf v=\lambda B\mathbf v$ we have $(A-\lambda B)\mathbf v=0$ with $\mathbf v\ne0$. Thus $A-\lambda B$ is singular and **$\det(A-\lambda B)=0$**.
