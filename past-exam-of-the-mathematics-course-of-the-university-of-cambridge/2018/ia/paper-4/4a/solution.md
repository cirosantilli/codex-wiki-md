<h1 id="4a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4A](../4a.md)

A [central force](../../../../../central-force.md) has the form $\mathbf F=f(r)\widehat{\mathbf r}$, so it always points along the line from a fixed centre to the particle. Its torque about the centre vanishes:

$$
\frac{d\mathbf L}{dt}=\mathbf r\times\mathbf F=0,
\qquad
\mathbf L=m\mathbf r\times\dot{\mathbf r}.
$$

Thus [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is conserved. Since $\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf L=0$, the trajectory lies in the fixed plane through the origin perpendicular to $\mathbf L$.

The area swept in time $dt$ is $dA=\frac12|\mathbf r\times d\mathbf r|$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{dA}{dt}=\frac12|\mathbf r\times\dot{\mathbf r}|=\frac{|\mathbf L|}{2m},}
$$

which is constant. This proves [Kepler's second law](../../../../../kepler-s-second-law.md).
