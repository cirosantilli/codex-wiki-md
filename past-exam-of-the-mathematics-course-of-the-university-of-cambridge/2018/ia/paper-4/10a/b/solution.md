<h1 id="10a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Four-momentum conservation](../../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives $P=\sum_iQ_i$, while the [mass-shell condition](../../../../../../string-mass-shell-condition.md) gives $P\cdot P=M^2c^2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{M=\frac1c\sqrt{\left(\sum_iQ_i\right)\cdot\left(\sum_jQ_j\right)}.}
$$

In the parent rest frame, $Mc^2=\sum_iE_i$, and each relativistic energy obeys $E_i\geq m_ic^2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{M\geq\sum_{i=1}^Nm_i.}
$$
