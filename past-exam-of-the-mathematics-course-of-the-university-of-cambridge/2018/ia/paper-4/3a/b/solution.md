The basic [Galilean transformations](../../../../../../galilean-transformation.md) include spacetime translations $\mathbf x'=\mathbf x-\mathbf a$, $t'=t-b$; spatial rotations $\mathbf x'=R\mathbf x$, $t'=t$; and boosts $\mathbf x'=\mathbf x-\mathbf vt$, $t'=t$, where $R$ is a [rotation matrix](../../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) and $\mathbf v$ is constant.
