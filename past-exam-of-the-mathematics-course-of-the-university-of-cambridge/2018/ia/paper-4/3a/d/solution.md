<h1 id="3a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The equation of motion is

$$
m\ddot x=-V'(x).
$$

Multiplication by $\dot x$ gives

$$
\frac d{dt}\left(\frac12m\dot x^2+V(x)\right)=\dot x(m\ddot x+V'(x))=0,
$$

so the [mechanical energy](../../../../../../mechanical-energy.md) $E=\frac12m\dot x^2+V(x)$ is conserved. On a segment where the direction of motion is fixed,

$$
\boxed{t-t_0=\pm\sqrt{\frac m2}\int_{x_0}^{x}\frac{du}{\sqrt{E-V(u)}}.}
$$
