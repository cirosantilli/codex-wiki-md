<h1 id="12c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Taking the symmetric part gives

$$
\boxed{S_{ij}=\frac12(T_{ij}+T_{ji}).}
$$

Contracting the antisymmetric part with the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) gives

$$
\boxed{V_k=\frac12\varepsilon_{kij}T_{ij}.}
$$

Indeed, $\frac12\varepsilon_{ijk}\varepsilon_{k\ell m}T_{\ell m}=\frac12(T_{ij}-T_{ji})$, so $S_{ij}+\varepsilon_{ijk}V_k=T_{ij}$.
