↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In three dimensions, the most general [isotropic tensors](../../../../../../isotropic-tensor.md) of ranks zero through three are

$$
\boxed{T=\alpha,\qquad T_i=0,\qquad T_{ij}=\alpha\delta_{ij},\qquad T_{ijk}=\alpha\varepsilon_{ijk}.}
$$

The scalar and zero vector are unchanged by rotations. For every $R\in SO(3)$, $R_{ia}R_{jb}\delta_{ab}=\delta_{ij}$ and $R_{ia}R_{jb}R_{kc}\varepsilon_{abc}=(\det R)\varepsilon_{ijk}=\varepsilon_{ijk}$, proving isotropy.
