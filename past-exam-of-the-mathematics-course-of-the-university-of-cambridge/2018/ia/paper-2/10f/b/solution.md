<h1 id="10f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using $\max\{X^2,Y^2\}=(X^2+Y^2+|X^2-Y^2|)/2$ and [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md),

$$
\mathbb E\max\{X^2,Y^2\}
\leq1+\frac12\sqrt{\mathbb E(X-Y)^2\,\mathbb E(X+Y)^2}
=\boxed{1+\sqrt{1-\rho^2}}.
$$
