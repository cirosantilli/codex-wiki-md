<h1 id="2b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2B](../2b.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $dF(x,y(x))/dx=F_x+F_yy'$. Equality with $P+Qy'$ for every $y(x)$ therefore requires $F_x=P$ and $F_y=Q$, which implies $P_y=Q_x$. Conversely, on the plane this compatibility condition makes $P\,dx+Q\,dy$ an [exact differential](../../../../../exact-differential.md), so a potential $F$ exists.

Here $P=4x^3+3y$, $Q=2y+3x$, and $P_y=Q_x=3$. Integrating gives $F=x^4+3xy+y^2$, hence

$$
\boxed{x^4+3xy+y^2=C.}
$$
