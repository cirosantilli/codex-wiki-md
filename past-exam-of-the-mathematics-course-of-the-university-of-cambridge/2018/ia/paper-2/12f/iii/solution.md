<h1 id="12f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Using part ii and symmetry of the [simple random walk](../../../../../../simple-random-walk.md),

$$
\mathbb EX_n^2-\mathbb EM_n^2
=\sum_{k\geq1}(2k-1)\mathbb P(X_n=k)>0
$$

for $n\geq1$. Thus **$\mathbb EM_n^2<\mathbb EX_n^2=n$**.
