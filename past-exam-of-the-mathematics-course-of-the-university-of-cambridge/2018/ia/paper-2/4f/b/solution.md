<h1 id="4f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With $\mu=\mathbb EX$,

$$
\operatorname{Var}X=\sum_x\mathbb P(X=x)(x-\mu)^2.
$$

Every summand is nonnegative. If the sum is zero, every value of positive probability equals $\mu$, so **$\mathbb P(X=\mu)=1$**.
