<h1 id="9f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For one Bernoulli variable, direct expansion gives $\operatorname{Var}F(X_1)=p(1-p)(F(1)-F(0))^2$. Applying the law of total variance successively to the coordinates gives the [variance tensorization](../../../../../../variance-tensorization.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}F(X)\leq p(1-p)\sum_{i=1}^n
\mathbb E\bigl(F(X)-F(X^i)\bigr)^2.}
$$
