<h1 id="9f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Independence gives $\mathbb EE(Z)=\sum_{z,y}F(y,z)\mathbb P(Y=y)\mathbb P(Z=z)=\mathbb EF(Y,Z)$. Moreover, $\mathbb EV(Z)=\mathbb E(F(Y,Z)^2)-\mathbb E(E(Z)^2)$, while $\operatorname{Var}E(Z)=\mathbb E(E(Z)^2)-(\mathbb EF(Y,Z))^2$. Adding proves the [law of total variance](../../../../../../law-of-total-variance.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}F(Y,Z)=\mathbb EV(Z)+\operatorname{Var}E(Z).}
$$
