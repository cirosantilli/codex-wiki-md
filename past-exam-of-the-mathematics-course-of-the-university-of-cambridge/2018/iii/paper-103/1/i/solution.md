<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fix a [Young tableau](../../../../../../young-tableau.md) $t$ of shape $\lambda$, let $C(t)$ be its column stabilizer, and put

$$
b_t=\sum_{g\in C(t)}\operatorname{sgn}(g)g,\qquad e_t=b_t\{t\}.
$$

These are the [Column antisymmetrizer of a Young tableau](../../../../../../column-antisymmetrizer-of-a-young-tableau.md) and its [polytabloid](../../../../../../polytabloid.md). The [Specht module](../../../../../../specht-module.md) is generated by $e_t$ as a [cyclic module](../../../../../../cyclic-module.md), so the nonzero [R-module homomorphism](../../../../../../module-homomorphism.md) $\phi$ has $\phi(e_t)\ne0$. Because its extension $\Phi$ commutes with the [group algebra](../../../../../../group-algebra.md) action,

$$
0\ne\phi(e_t)=\Phi(b_t\{t\})=b_t\Phi(\{t\}).
$$

Expand $\Phi(\{t\})$ in the [tabloid](../../../../../../tabloid.md) basis of the [Young permutation module](../../../../../../young-permutation-module.md) $M^\mu$. At least one [tabloid](../../../../../../tabloid.md) $\{u\}$ therefore satisfies $b_t\{u\}\ne0$.

If a row of $u$ contains two entries from the same column of $t$, their transposition $\tau\in C(t)$ fixes $\{u\}$. Pairing the terms indexed by $g$ and $g\tau$ in $b_t\{u\}$ makes them cancel. This remains valid in [characteristic](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) two, since the paired coefficients sum to zero there as well. Thus each row of $u$ meets each column of $t$ in at most one entry.

The first $r$ rows of $u$ consequently contain at most $\min(r,\lambda'_j)$ entries from column $j$ of $t$, where $\lambda'$ is the [conjugate partition](../../../../../../conjugate-partition.md). Hence

$$
\sum_{i=1}^r\mu_i\leq\sum_j\min(r,\lambda'_j)=\sum_{i=1}^r\lambda_i
\qquad(r\geq1).
$$

By the [dominance order on partitions](../../../../../../dominance-order-on-partitions.md), this proves

$$
\boxed{\lambda\unrhd\mu.}
$$

This is [dominance from a nonzero column antisymmetrizer](../../../../../../dominance-from-a-nonzero-column-antisymmetrizer.md). The extension is what allows us to apply $b_t$ to an element of $M^\mu$ without dividing by the column stabilizer's order, which might vanish in the given [field](../../../../../../field.md).
