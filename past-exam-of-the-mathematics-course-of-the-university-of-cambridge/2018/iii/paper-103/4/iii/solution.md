<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For every $\chi^\mu\in C$, level zero of the [core tower of a partition](../../../../../../core-tower-of-a-partition.md) is its [core of a partition](../../../../../../core-of-a-partition.md) $\gamma$, of size $m$. The [character degree coprime to p from the core tower](../../../../../../character-degree-coprime-to-p-from-the-core-tower.md) criterion says that if $p\nmid\chi^\mu(1)$, this level must have size equal to the units digit $\alpha_0$ of $n$ in base $p$.

But $0\leq\alpha_0<p$, whereas $m\geq p$. Hence every character in $C$ has degree divisible by $p$, and

$$
\boxed{|C\cap\operatorname{Irr}_{p'}(S_n)|=0.}
$$

Equivalently, the core-tower size coefficient $m$ at position zero needs at least one carry in normalization to the base-$p$ digits. Each carry contributes one to the [P-adic valuation of a symmetric-group character degree from the core tower](../../../../../../p-adic-valuation-of-a-symmetric-group-character-degree-from-the-core-tower.md).
