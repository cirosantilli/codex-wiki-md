<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The implication is **false**. Choose

$$
\boxed{p=3,\quad k=1,\quad a=2,\quad m=0,\quad n=6,\quad\lambda=(3,3).}
$$

The [hook lengths](../../../../../../hook-length.md) are

$$
\begin{matrix}
4&3&2\\
3&2&1
\end{matrix},
\qquad
\chi^{(3,3)}(1)=\frac{6!}{4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1}=5.
$$

Thus $3$ does not divide this [irreducible character degree](../../../../../../irreducible-character-degree.md), but no hook has length $ap^k=6$.

There is no contradiction with part (i): removing two horizontal [rim hooks](../../../../../../rim-hook.md) of length three gives the empty [core of a partition](../../../../../../core-of-a-partition.md), so $w_3(\lambda)=2=a$ and the core degree is one. Having total [weight of a partition](../../../../../../weight-of-a-partition.md) two does not force a single hook of length twice the modulus.
