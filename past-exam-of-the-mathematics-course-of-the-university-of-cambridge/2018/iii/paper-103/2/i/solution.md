<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [hook criterion in a beta set](../../../../../../hook-criterion-in-a-beta-set.md): for a finite [beta set of a partition](../../../../../../beta-set-of-a-partition.md) $X$, each [hook length](../../../../../../hook-length.md) is a difference $b-c$ with $b\in X$, $0\leq c<b$ and $c\notin X$, and every such difference is a hook length.

A hook length $pr$ therefore gives a bead $b$ and a gap $c=b-pr$. Along the progression

$$
b,\ b-p,\ b-2p,\ldots,\ b-rp=c,
$$

the first position is a bead and the last is a gap. Some adjacent positions are consequently a bead followed by a gap. Their difference is $p$, so $p$ is a [hook length](../../../../../../hook-length.md). Similarly the progression from $b$ to $c$ in steps of $r$ gives a hook length $r$. Every position used is nonnegative because it lies between $c$ and $b$.

Thus the [divisor closure of hook lengths](../../../../../../divisor-closure-of-hook-lengths.md) yields

$$
\boxed{pr\in\mathcal H(\lambda)\ \Longrightarrow\ p,r\in\mathcal H(\lambda).}
$$

Neither $p$ nor $r$ needs to be a [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number.md).
