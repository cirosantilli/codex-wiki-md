↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The converse is **false**. Take $p=2$, $r=3$, $n=6$ and the [partition of an integer](../../../../../../partition-of-an-integer.md) $\lambda=(3,3)$. Its [hook lengths](../../../../../../hook-length.md), arranged in its [Young diagram](../../../../../../young-diagram.md), are

$$
\begin{matrix}
4&3&2\\
3&2&1
\end{matrix}.
$$

Both $2$ and $3$ occur, but $6$ does not, even though $pr=6=n$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\lambda=(3,3),\quad p=2,\quad r=3}
$$

is a counterexample with distinct $p,r$.
