<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

We prove $(ii)\Rightarrow(i)$. The central block [idempotents](../../../../../../../idempotent.md) split every finite-dimensional [module](../../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) into its block components. A nonzero [indecomposable representation](../../../../../../../indecomposable-representation.md), in particular an indecomposable [projective module](../../../../../../../projective-module.md), has only one such component; otherwise these give a nontrivial direct-sum decomposition.

Every [Jordan–Hölder factor](../../../../../../../jordan-holder-factor.md) of a module in a block belongs to that block, since its central idempotent acts as identity on [submodules](../../../../../../../submodule.md) and quotients. Thus any two simple factors of the same projective indecomposable are in the same block. Applying this along the proposed chain yields $\boxed{S,T\text{ belong to the same block}}$.
