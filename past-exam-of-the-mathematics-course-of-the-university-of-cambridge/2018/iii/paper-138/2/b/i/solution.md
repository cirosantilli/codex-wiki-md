<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $g$ be a [p-regular element](../../../../../../../p-regular-element.md), with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_i$ on $V$. On both $g^{-1}$ acting on $V$ and $g$ acting on the [dual representation](../../../../../../../dual-representation.md) $V^*$, the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $\lambda_i^{-1}$. Multiplicative lifts of [roots of unity](../../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) satisfy $\widehat{\lambda_i^{-1}}=\widehat\lambda_i^{-1}=\overline{\widehat\lambda_i}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\chi_V(g^{-1})=\overline{\chi_V(g)}=\chi_{V^*}(g)}.
$$

All these identities concern the domain of a [Brauer character](../../../../../../../brauer-character.md), namely the [p-regular elements](../../../../../../../p-regular-element.md). If necessary, extend the [p-modular system](../../../../../../../p-modular-system.md) to contain the relevant [roots of unity](../../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) and use one consistent choice of lifts.
