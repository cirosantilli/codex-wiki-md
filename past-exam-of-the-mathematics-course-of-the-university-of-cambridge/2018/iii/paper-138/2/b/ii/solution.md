<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [p-regular element](../../../../../../../p-regular-element.md) $g$, diagonalize its actions on $V$ and $V'$. If their [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $\lambda_i$ and $\nu_j$, the [tensor product of group representations](../../../../../../../tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) has [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_i\nu_j$. The lift of [roots of unity](../../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) is multiplicative, so

$$
\chi_{V\otimes V'}(g)=\sum_{i,j}\widehat\lambda_i\widehat\nu_j=\chi_V(g)\chi_{V'}(g).
$$

Thus $\boxed{\chi_{V\otimes V'}=\chi_V\chi_{V'}}$ as [Brauer characters](../../../../../../../brauer-character.md).
