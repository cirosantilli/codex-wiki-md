<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [integral form of a group representation](../../../../../../integral-form-of-a-group-representation.md) $V$ is a $G$-stable finite free $\mathcal O$-submodule $W\subset V$ with $K\otimes_{\mathcal O}W\cong V$. Such a form exists: take a basis lattice $L$ and replace it by $\sum_{g\in G}gL$, which is finite and torsion-free and therefore free over the [discrete valuation ring](../../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md) $\mathcal O$.

Choose ordinary [simple modules](../../../../../../irreducible-module.md) $V_i$ with forms $W_i$, and modular [simple modules](../../../../../../irreducible-module.md) $S_j$ with [projective covers](../../../../../../projective-cover.md) $P_j$. With $\pi$ a [uniformizer](../../../../../../uniformizer.md), put $\overline W_i=W_i/\pi W_i$. The [decomposition matrix](../../../../../../decomposition-matrix-modular-representation-theory.md) and [Cartan matrix of a group algebra](../../../../../../cartan-matrix-of-a-group-algebra.md) have entries

$$
d_{ij}=[\overline W_i:S_j],\qquad c_{\ell j}=[P_j:S_\ell].
$$

The first numbers do not depend on the integral form: the [Brauer character](../../../../../../brauer-character.md) of its reduction is the ordinary character restricted to [p-regular elements](../../../../../../p-regular-element.md), and part 2(a) determines all composition multiplicities from this restriction.

For the assertion in (i), set $I=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal OG}(W,W')$. It is a [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md) of the [finite free module](../../../../../../finite-free-module.md) $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O}(W,W')$, hence is finite free over $\mathcal O$. There is a natural injective map

$$
K\otimes_{\mathcal O}I\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_{KG}(M,M').
$$

For any homomorphism $f$ in the target, clearing the finitely many denominators of its values on an $\mathcal O$-basis of $W$ gives $\pi^Nf(W)\subset W'$. Thus $\pi^Nf\in I$, and the map is surjective. Therefore $\boxed{I\text{ is an }\mathcal O\text{-form of }\operatorname{Hom}_{KG}(M,M')}$, regarding this Hom space as a vector space. The original PDF supplies part (i), which is missing from the supplied TeX.
