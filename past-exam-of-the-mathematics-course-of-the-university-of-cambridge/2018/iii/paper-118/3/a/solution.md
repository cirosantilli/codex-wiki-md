<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the integral normalization of the [Chern connection](../../../../../../chern-connection.md) curvature. For a [holomorphic local frame](../../../../../../holomorphic-local-trivialization.md) $e$, put $h_e=h(e,e)>0$. The [fundamental form of a Hermitian holomorphic line bundle](../../../../../../normalized-curvature-form-of-a-hermitian-holomorphic-line-bundle.md) is

$$
\boxed{\omega_{(\mathcal L,h)}=\frac{i}{2\pi}F_h
=-\frac{i}{2\pi}\partial\bar\partial\log h_e.}
$$

This follows from the [local formula for the Chern connection on a line bundle](../../../../../../local-formula-for-the-chern-connection-on-a-line-bundle.md), $F_h=\bar\partial\partial\log h_e$. It is a closed [real (1, 1)-form](../../../../../../real-1-1-form.md) representing the [First Chern class](../../../../../../first-chern-class.md). If $h_e=e^{-\varphi}$, the formula becomes $(i/(2\pi))\partial\bar\partial\varphi$.

The [Hermitian metric](../../../../../../hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) is positive when this is a [positive real (1, 1)-form](../../../../../../positive-real-1-1-form.md): for every nonzero $v\in T^{1,0}M$,

$$
\boxed{-i\omega_{(\mathcal L,h)}(v,\bar v)>0.}
$$

Equivalently, the matrix $(\partial^2\varphi/\partial z_j\partial\bar z_k)$ is a [positive-definite matrix](../../../../../../positive-definite-matrix.md) in every frame. Such a metric makes $\mathcal L$ a [positive holomorphic line bundle](../../../../../../positive-holomorphic-line-bundle.md). Omitting the normalization factor $2\pi$ changes none of these positivity conditions.
