<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply [Serre duality for compact complex manifolds](../../../../../../serre-duality-for-compact-complex-manifolds.md) to $\mathcal L\otimes K_M$, where $K_M$ is the [canonical bundle](../../../../../../canonical-bundle.md):

$$
H^n(M,\mathcal L\otimes K_M)^*\cong H^0(M,\mathcal L^{-1}).
$$

We can show the group on the right vanishes directly from the embedding. Since $\mathcal L^{-1}=i^*\mathcal O(-1)$, the [tautological bundle](../../../../../../tautological-bundle.md) inclusion gives an inclusion of [holomorphic vector bundles](../../../../../../holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

$$
\mathcal L^{-1}\hookrightarrow M\times\mathbb C^{r+1}.
$$

A global [holomorphic section](../../../../../../holomorphic-section.md) of $\mathcal L^{-1}$ consequently determines $r+1$ global [holomorphic functions](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) on $M$. [Compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) and the [maximum modulus principle](../../../../../../maximum-modulus-principle.md) make these functions constant on every [connected component](../../../../../../connected-component.md). On such a component the section is therefore a constant vector $v$ lying in the tautological line $i(x)$ for every $x$.

If $v\ne0$, all the points $i(x)$ equal $[v]$. This contradicts the fact that $i$ is an embedding of a component of positive dimension $n\geq1$. Hence $v=0$ on every component, and $H^0(M,\mathcal L^{-1})=0$. Duality proves

$$
\boxed{H^n(M,\mathcal L\otimes K_M)=0.}
$$
