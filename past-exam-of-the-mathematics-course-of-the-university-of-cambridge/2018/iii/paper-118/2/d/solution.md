<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Evaluation is surjective as a sheaf morphism: near $p$, any prescribed value is supplied by a constant [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md). We therefore have a [short exact sequence of sheaves](../../../../../../short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal I_p\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X\xrightarrow{\operatorname{ev}_p}\mathbb C_p\longrightarrow0,
$$

where $\mathcal I_p$ is the [point ideal sheaf](../../../../../../ideal-sheaf-of-a-point-on-a-complex-manifold.md) and $\mathcal O_X$ the [sheaf of holomorphic functions](../../../../../../structure-sheaf-of-a-complex-manifold.md). By part (c), the [skyscraper sheaf](../../../../../../skyscraper-sheaf.md) is flasque, so $H^0(X,\mathbb C_p)=\mathbb C$ and $H^i(X,\mathbb C_p)=0$ for $i>0$.

On the connected compact [elliptic curve](../../../../../../elliptic-curve.md), the [maximum modulus principle](../../../../../../maximum-modulus-principle.md) makes every global [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) constant. Consequently $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=\mathbb C$, and evaluation on [global sections](../../../../../../global-section.md) is an isomorphism. Also $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)\cong\mathbb C$: by [Serre duality for compact complex manifolds](../../../../../../serre-duality-for-compact-complex-manifolds.md), its dual is $H^0(X,K_X)$, generated by the nowhere-zero form $dz$ descended from $\mathbb C$. Higher cohomology of $\mathcal O_X$ vanishes by the [Dolbeault theorem](../../../../../../dolbeault-theorem.md) in complex dimension one.

The [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) thus begins

$$
0\to H^0(X,\mathcal I_p)\to\mathbb C\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathbb C
\to H^1(X,\mathcal I_p)\to\mathbb C\to0.
$$

It follows that

$$
\boxed{H^i(X,\mathcal I_p)\cong
\begin{cases}0,&i=0,\\\mathbb C,&i=1,\\0,&i\geq2.\end{cases}}
$$
