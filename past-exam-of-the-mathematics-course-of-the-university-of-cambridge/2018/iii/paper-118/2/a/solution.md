<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a [flasque resolution](../../../../../../flasque-resolution.md) of the [sheaf of abelian groups](../../../../../../sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md) $\mathcal F$,

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal F\longrightarrow\mathcal I^0\xrightarrow{d^0}\mathcal I^1\xrightarrow{d^1}\cdots.
$$

A [flasque sheaf](../../../../../../flasque-sheaf.md) has surjective restriction maps; such resolutions exist for every [sheaf of abelian groups](../../../../../../sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md). Apply the [global section functor](../../../../../../global-section-functor.md) to obtain a [cochain complex](../../../../../../cochain-complex.md). The [sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../sheaf-cohomology.md) groups are

$$
\boxed{H^i(X,\mathcal F)=
\frac{\ker\bigl(\Gamma(X,\mathcal I^i)\to\Gamma(X,\mathcal I^{i+1})\bigr)}
{\operatorname{im}\bigl(\Gamma(X,\mathcal I^{i-1})\to\Gamma(X,\mathcal I^i)\bigr)}.}
$$

For $i=0$, the denominator is zero and $H^0(X,\mathcal F)=\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)$. Different resolutions give canonically isomorphic groups; the [resolution principle for sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../resolution-principle-for-sheaf-cohomology.md) is what permits other acyclic resolutions to compute these same groups.
