<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $M$ has complex dimension $n$, its complexified [cotangent bundle](../../../../../../cotangent-bundle.md) splits into the $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ [cotangent bundles](../../../../../../cotangent-bundle.md). The space of global smooth [differential forms of type (p, q)](../../../../../../differential-form-of-type-p-q.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A^{p,q}(M)=\Gamma_{C^\infty}\!\left(M,\Lambda^p(T^{1,0}M)^*\otimes\Lambda^q(T^{0,1}M)^*\right).}
$$

In [holomorphic coordinates](../../../../../../holomorphic-coordinate.md), its elements have the form $\alpha=\sum_{I,J}a_{I,J}\,dz^I\wedge d\bar z^J$, where the coefficients are complex-valued [smooth functions](../../../../../../smooth-function.md), $|I|=p$, and $|J|=q$.

The [exterior derivative](../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md) decomposes as $d=\partial+\bar\partial$. The [conjugate Dolbeault operator](../../../../../../conjugate-dolbeault-operator.md) $\partial$ and [Dolbeault operator](../../../../../../dolbeault-operator.md) $\bar\partial$ are its type components:

$$
\boxed{\partial\alpha=\sum_{I,J,k}\frac{\partial a_{I,J}}{\partial z_k}\,dz_k\wedge dz^I\wedge d\bar z^J,\qquad
\bar\partial\alpha=\sum_{I,J,k}\frac{\partial a_{I,J}}{\partial\bar z_k}\,d\bar z_k\wedge dz^I\wedge d\bar z^J.}
$$

They have bidegrees $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ respectively. Keeping the displayed wedge order avoids hiding a sign; moving $d\bar z_k$ past $dz^I$ introduces $(-1)^p$. Decomposing $d^2=0$ by type gives $\partial^2=\bar\partial^2=0$ and $\partial\bar\partial+\bar\partial\partial=0$.
