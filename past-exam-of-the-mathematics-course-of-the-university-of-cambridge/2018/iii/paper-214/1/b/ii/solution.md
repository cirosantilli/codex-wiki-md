<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each of the $2d$ coordinate faces $F$ of $B(N)$, define the [increasing event](../../../../../../../increasing-event.md) $A_F(m,N)$ that an open [path in a graph](../../../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md) lying in $B(N)$ joins $B(m)$ to $F$. Their union is exactly the event of connection from $B(m)$ to $\partial B(N)$ within $B(N)$. The [bond percolation](../../../../../../../bond-percolation-split.md) law and the centered boxes are invariant under coordinate permutations and sign changes, so all $2d$ events have the same probability.

Apply the [square-root trick for positively associated events](../../../../../../../square-root-trick-for-positively-associated-events.md) from part (a), with $2d$ in place of the number of events. If $\delta_m=1-\mathbb P_p(D_m)$, part (b.i) gives, for every $N=m+n$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P_p(A_F(m,N))\geq1-\delta_m^{1/(2d)}\longrightarrow1,\qquad\text{uniformly in }n\geq1.}
$$

In particular this holds for the left and right faces, and it proves the stronger statement in which each connecting [path in a graph](../../../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md) stays inside the outer box.
