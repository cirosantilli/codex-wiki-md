<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For [negative hydrogen ion opacity](../../../../../../negative-hydrogen-ion-opacity.md), $n=1/2$ and $s=-9$, so the exponents of the [power-law opacity radiative envelope](../../../../../../power-law-opacity-radiative-envelope.md) are $a=3/2$ and $b=-9/2$. Dividing the integrated pressure relation by $CT^b$ gives

$$
\nabla=\frac ab+\left(\nabla_{\rm phot}-\frac ab\right)\left(\frac{T_{\rm eff}}T\right)^b.
$$

Since $a/b=-1/3$ and $\nabla_{\rm phot}=1/8$,

$$
\boxed{\nabla(r)=-\frac13+\frac{11}{24}\left(\frac{T_{\rm eff}}{T(r)}\right)^{-9/2}.}
$$

It starts below the monatomic [adiabatic temperature gradient](../../../../../../adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md) and rises as the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) increases inward.
