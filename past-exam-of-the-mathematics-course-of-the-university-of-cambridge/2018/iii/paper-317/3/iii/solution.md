<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Taking the [logarithmic derivative](../../../../../../logarithmic-derivative.md) of the [grey atmosphere](../../../../../../grey-atmosphere.md) temperature law, $4\log T=\log(\tau+2/3)+\text{constant}$, gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\log T}{d\tau}=\frac{1}{4(\tau+2/3)}=\frac{3}{8+12\tau}.}
$$
