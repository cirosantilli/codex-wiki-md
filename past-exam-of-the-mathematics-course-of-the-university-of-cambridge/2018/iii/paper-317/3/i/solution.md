<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The outward [radiative flux](../../../../../../radiative-flux.md) is $F=L/(4\pi r^2)$. A [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) of energy $E$ carries [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) $E/c$, so the momentum transferred per unit volume and time to matter is $\kappa\rho F/c$, when $\kappa$ is the appropriate transport opacity. Momentum conservation makes the radiation stress decrease outward by this amount. In the thin atmosphere, where the radiation stress is treated by its scalar [radiation pressure](../../../../../../radiation-pressure.md),

$$
\boxed{\frac{dP_{\rm rad}}{dr}=-\frac{\kappa\rho F}{c}=-\frac{\kappa\rho L}{4\pi r^2c}.}
$$

This is the first moment of the [radiative transfer equation](../../../../../../radiative-transfer-equation.md). The [grey atmosphere](../../../../../../grey-atmosphere.md) approximation uses a frequency-independent transport opacity; in an optically thick diffusion regime it becomes the [Rosseland mean opacity](../../../../../../rosseland-mean-opacity.md). Near the surface the [plane-parallel atmosphere](../../../../../../plane-parallel-atmosphere.md) approximation removes geometric stress-divergence corrections.
