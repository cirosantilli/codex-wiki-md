<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Assume an isolated star of initial mass five [solar masses](../../../../../solar-mass.md) of approximately solar [stellar metallicity](../../../../../stellar-metallicity.md). Rotation, [convective overshooting](../../../../../convective-overshoot.md) and mass loss change numerical ages and the extent of a [blue loop](../../../../../blue-loop.md), so the ages below are estimates. Take zero age at the [zero-age main sequence](../../../../../zero-age-main-sequence.md); the [pre-main-sequence star](../../../../../pre-main-sequence-star.md) phase adds a much shorter contraction time.

The original schematic [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](../../../../../hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) marks the stages discussed below. The strip denotes the approximate [Cepheid instability strip](../../../../../cepheid-instability-strip.md); the track is illustrative, rather than a computed stellar model.

![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-mathematics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2018/iii/paper-317-evolution.png)

**[Figure 1](#4/image-schematic-stellar-evolution-of-an-initially-five-solar-mass-star). Schematic stellar evolution of an initially five-solar-mass star**.

The [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md) lasts roughly $8\times10^7$–$10^8$ years. [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md) [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) is concentrated in a mixed [convective core](../../../../../convective-core.md). Its declining [hydrogen mass fraction](../../../../../hydrogen-mass-fraction.md) and rising [helium mass fraction](../../../../../helium-mass-fraction.md) are nearly uniform there, while the retreating core leaves a composition gradient. The unprocessed envelope remains [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) rich. At the [terminal-age main sequence](../../../../../terminal-age-main-sequence.md), core [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) is exhausted and a [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) takes over.

The inert helium core grows through shell burning. The [Schönberg-Chandrasekhar limit](../../../../../schonberg-chandrasekhar-limit.md) is approximately

$$
q_{\rm SC}\simeq0.37\left(\frac{\mu_{\rm env}}{\mu_{\rm core}}\right)^2\sim0.08\text{--}0.1.
$$

Beyond this limit an isothermal nondegenerate core cannot remain in thermal equilibrium with its envelope. Core contraction and envelope expansion carry the star across the [Hertzsprung gap](../../../../../hertzsprung-gap.md). An initial slower shell-burning interval can precede the rapid crossing; the crossing itself is governed by [Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction](../../../../../kelvin-helmholtz-mechanism.md), with a representative $GM^2/(RL)\sim10^5$–$10^6$ years. [First dredge-up](../../../../../first-dredge-up.md) then mixes hydrogen-processed material into the [convective envelope](../../../../../convective-envelope.md), lowering its [hydrogen mass fraction](../../../../../hydrogen-mass-fraction.md) and increasing [helium](../../../../../helium.md) and [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), while leaving a composition discontinuity where the envelope later retreats.

At an age still of order $10^8$ years, [core helium burning](../../../../../core-helium-burning.md) begins quietly: the core is nondegenerate, so there is no [helium flash](../../../../../helium-flash.md). The [Triple-alpha process](../../../../../triple-alpha-process.md) and subsequent alpha capture build a [carbon-oxygen core](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-core.md). Core helium burning lasts roughly $1.6\times10^7$–$2.2\times10^7$ years in representative solar-composition models. [Pols's stellar-evolution notes](https://www.astro.ru.nl/~onnop/education/stev_utrecht_notes/chapter9-11.pdf) illustrate the substantial dependence of these lifetimes on [convective overshooting](../../../../../convective-overshoot.md).

During a [blue loop](../../../../../blue-loop.md) the envelope contracts and [effective temperature](../../../../../effective-temperature.md) increases, before the star returns redward. The loop depends on core and envelope structure and the hydrogen discontinuity left by [first dredge-up](../../../../../first-dredge-up.md). A loop reaching the [Cepheid instability strip](../../../../../cepheid-instability-strip.md) produces two further crossings, blueward and redward, in addition to the earlier rapid crossing of the [Hertzsprung gap](../../../../../hertzsprung-gap.md). [Cepheid variables](../../../../../cepheid-variable.md) pulsate through the [opacity mechanism](../../../../../kappa-mechanism.md), involving [helium](../../../../../helium.md) [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md). Neither loop extent nor all three strip crossings are guaranteed for every composition or mixing prescription. The [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) continues moving outward in enclosed mass while central [helium](../../../../../helium.md) is depleted. [Lattanzio's five-solar-mass tutorial](https://users.monash.edu.au/~johnl/StellarEvolnV1/m5z02evoln.html) shows these composition changes.

After helium exhaustion, at an age roughly $1.0\times10^8$–$1.2\times10^8$ years, the star ascends the [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md). An inert [carbon-oxygen core](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-core.md) is surrounded by [helium](../../../../../helium.md)-rich material and a [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md)-rich envelope. [Second dredge-up](../../../../../second-dredge-up.md) mixes [helium](../../../../../helium.md) and [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) products into the envelope and reduces the hydrogen-exhausted core mass. Subsequently a [helium-burning shell](../../../../../helium-burning-shell.md) and [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) alternate in importance. A [thermal pulse of an asymptotic-giant-branch star](../../../../../thermal-pulse-of-an-asymptotic-giant-branch-star.md) causes intershell convection, expansion and temporary quenching of [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md); [third dredge-up](../../../../../third-dredge-up.md) can carry [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and [slow neutron-capture process](../../../../../s-process.md) products outward. Between pulses [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) rebuilds the [helium](../../../../../helium.md) layer. These mechanisms are illustrated in [Lattanzio's AGB tutorial](https://users.monash.edu.au/~johnl/StellarEvolnV1/AGBevoln.html).

The following original [stellar composition profile](../../../../../stellar-composition-profile.md) sketches distinguish hydrogen exhaustion from helium exhaustion; their mass boundaries are illustrative.

The final giant phases add at most a few million years at this level of accuracy. Strong [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) remove the envelope; the hot remnant can illuminate a [planetary nebula](../../../../../planetary-nebula.md) and then cool as a [carbon-oxygen white dwarf](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-white-dwarf.md). A typical remnant is of order one [solar mass](../../../../../solar-mass.md). The ordinary isolated $5M_\odot$ case does not reach sustained [carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md) and core collapse. Thus **the usual endpoint is a carbon-oxygen white dwarf**, at a total age of order $10^8$ years, with its subsequent cooling age added separately.
