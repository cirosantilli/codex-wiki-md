<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a deterministic [partition of an interval](../../../../../../partition-of-an-interval.md) $0=t_0<\cdots<t_m=t$, put $Q_\pi=\sum_j\Delta_jB\,\Delta_j\widetilde B$. [Independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the two [Brownian motions](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) and their [independent increments](../../../../../../independent-increments.md) gives

$$
\mathbb EQ_\pi=0,\qquad\mathbb EQ_\pi^2=\sum_j(t_j-t_{j-1})^2\leq t|\pi|\longrightarrow0.
$$

The defining sums for [quadratic covariation](../../../../../../quadratic-covariation.md) therefore converge to zero in the [L2 space](../../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) and in [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). Hence, with the continuous versions of the [quadratic covariation](../../../../../../quadratic-covariation.md),

$$
\boxed{\langle B,\widetilde B\rangle_t=0\quad(t\geq0).}
$$

Equality holds simultaneously for all $t$ by checking rational times and using continuity. For [continuous local martingales](../../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md), the angle-bracket notation here agrees with [quadratic covariation](../../../../../../quadratic-covariation.md).
