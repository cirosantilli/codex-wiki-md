<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\mathcal P$ be the [predictable sigma-algebra](../../../../../../predictable-sigma-algebra.md) on $\Omega\times[0,\infty)$, generated by $A\times\{0\}$ with $A\in\mathcal F_0$ and $A\times(s,u]$ with $A\in\mathcal F_s$. A [stochastic process](../../../../../../stochastic-process-split.md) $H$ is [previsible](../../../../../../predictable-process.md) precisely when $(\omega,t)\mapsto H_t(\omega)$ is $\mathcal P$-measurable.

**Every left-continuous [adapted process](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) is [previsible](../../../../../../predictable-process.md).** Indeed, on intervals $(k2^{-n},(k+1)2^{-n}]$, put $H^{(n)}_t=H_{k2^{-n}}$, and put $H^{(n)}_0=H_0$. Each approximation is a [predictable process](../../../../../../predictable-process.md), since its coefficient is $\mathcal F_{k2^{-n}}$-measurable; left continuity gives $H^{(n)}_t\to H_t$ for every $t$, so measurability passes to the limit.

**An [adapted](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) [càdlàg process](../../../../../../cadlag-process.md) need not be [previsible](../../../../../../predictable-process.md).** For a concrete example, let $\xi$ take values $0,1$ with equal probabilities, let $\mathcal F_t$ be trivial for $t<1$ and $\mathcal F_t=\sigma(\xi)$ for $t\geq1$, and set $H_t=\xi\mathbf1_{[1,\infty)}(t)$. This is an [adapted](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) [càdlàg process](../../../../../../cadlag-process.md), but $H_1=\xi$ is not $\mathcal F_{1-}$-measurable, where $\mathcal F_{1-}=\sigma(\bigcup_{s<1}\mathcal F_s)$. Sections at time $1$ of the generators of $\mathcal P$ all belong to $\mathcal F_{1-}$, so every [predictable process](../../../../../../predictable-process.md) has an $\mathcal F_{1-}$-measurable value there; this proves the counterexample directly.
