<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $H_+$ be the positively oriented [Hopf link](../../../../../../hopf-link.md) obtained by closing a two-strand braid with two positive crossings. Switching one crossing gives the two-component [unlink](../../../../../../unlink.md); the [oriented smoothing](../../../../../../oriented-smoothing.md) gives the [unknot](../../../../../../unknot.md). The printed [skein relation](../../../../../../skein-relation.md) gives

$$
qV(U_2)-q^{-1}V(H_+)=q^{1/2}-q^{-1/2},
\qquad V(H_+)=q^{5/2}+q^{1/2}.
$$

Represent the right-handed [trefoil knot](../../../../../../trefoil-knot.md) by the closure of the two-strand braid with three positive crossings. Switching one crossing gives the [unknot](../../../../../../unknot.md), while its [oriented smoothing](../../../../../../oriented-smoothing.md) gives $H_+$. Consequently

$$
q-q^{-1}V(T_+)=(q^{1/2}-q^{-1/2})(q^{5/2}+q^{1/2}),
$$

so the two [Jones polynomials](../../../../../../jones-polynomial.md) are

$$
\boxed{V(T_+)=q+q^3-q^4,\qquad V(T_-)=q^{-1}+q^{-3}-q^{-4}.}
$$

The second expression follows from the [mirror of a link](../../../../../../mirror-of-a-link.md) identity. They are unequal [Laurent polynomials](../../../../../../laurent-polynomial.md), so invariance under [link isotopy](../../../../../../link-isotopy.md) proves that **the right-handed and left-handed trefoils are not isotopic**.
