<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Reflection through the projection plane reverses the orientation of $S^3$ and exchanges the overpassing and underpassing strands at every crossing. Thus switching all crossings gives the [mirror of a link](../../../../../../mirror-of-a-link.md), with the component orientations transported by the reflection.

Define $W(L)(q)=V(\overline L)(q^{-1})$. Reflection exchanges $D_-$ with $D_+$ and preserves the [oriented smoothing](../../../../../../oriented-smoothing.md). Apply the printed [skein relation](../../../../../../skein-relation.md) to the reflected triple at parameter $q^{-1}$:

$$
q^{-1}W(D_+)-qW(D_-)=(q^{-1/2}-q^{1/2})W(D_0).
$$

Multiplication by $-1$ gives the original [skein relation](../../../../../../skein-relation.md) for $W$. Also $W(U_1)=1$. The stated uniqueness of the [Jones polynomial](../../../../../../jones-polynomial.md) now gives $W=V$, hence

$$
\boxed{V(\overline L)(q)=V(L)(q^{-1}).}
$$
