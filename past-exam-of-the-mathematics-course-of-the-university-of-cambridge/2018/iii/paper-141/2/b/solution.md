<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Insert a kink on any component of an oriented [link diagram](../../../../../../link-diagram.md) for $L$. Switching the kink's crossing gives another [link diagram](../../../../../../link-diagram.md) of $L$; its [oriented smoothing](../../../../../../oriented-smoothing.md) separates off a small [unknot](../../../../../../unknot.md) in a ball disjoint from the rest of $L$. The [skein relation](../../../../../../skein-relation.md) therefore gives

$$
(q-q^{-1})V(L)=(q^{1/2}-q^{-1/2})V(L\amalg U_1).
$$

Cancel the nonzero factor $q^{1/2}-q^{-1/2}$ in the [Laurent polynomial](../../../../../../laurent-polynomial.md) ring to obtain

$$
\boxed{V(L\amalg U_1)=(q^{1/2}+q^{-1/2})V(L)=V(U_2)V(L).}
$$
