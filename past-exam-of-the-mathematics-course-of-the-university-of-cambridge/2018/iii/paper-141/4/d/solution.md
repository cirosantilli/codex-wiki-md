<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose a [fiber monodromy](../../../../../../fiber-monodromy.md) representative fixing the boundary of the fiber [Seifert surface](../../../../../../seifert-surface.md) pointwise. The boundary of each fiber is the [Seifert longitude](../../../../../../seifert-longitude.md), precisely the slope used by zero [Dehn filling](../../../../../../dehn-filling.md). Attach the filling [solid torus](../../../../../../solid-torus.md) as $D^2\times S^1$, matching $\partial D^2\times\{\theta\}$ with the boundary of the fiber at $\theta$. The fibers cap off to closed [topological surfaces](../../../../../../topological-surface.md) $\widehat\Sigma$ of the same [genus](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$; extend the [fiber monodromy](../../../../../../fiber-monodromy.md) across the disk by the identity. Thus

$$
\boxed{M=X_{(p,q)}(0)\cong M_{\widehat\phi},\qquad
\mathcal F=\{\widehat\Sigma\times\{\theta\}\}_{\theta\in S^1}.}
$$

This is a [foliation](../../../../../../foliation.md) by fibers of a bundle over $S^1$, and every leaf is compact with [genus](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $(p-1)(q-1)/2$.

Choose a point in the interior of the capping disk, which $\widehat\phi$ fixes. Its suspension is an embedded closed curve $\{x\}\times S^1$ in the [mapping torus](../../../../../../mapping-torus.md). It is transverse to the [foliation](../../../../../../foliation.md) and meets every fiber once. Hence every leaf meets a [closed transversal to a foliation](../../../../../../closed-transversal-to-a-foliation.md), which is the defining criterion for a [taut foliation](../../../../../../taut-foliation.md). Therefore **the fiber foliation is taut**.
