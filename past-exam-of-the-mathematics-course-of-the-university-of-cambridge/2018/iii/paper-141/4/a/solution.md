<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $V=M_{S^2}(p^*/q,*)$. Filling one end of $S^1$ times an [annulus](../../../../../../annulus-mathematics.md) gives a [solid torus](../../../../../../solid-torus.md), so $V$ is a [solid torus](../../../../../../solid-torus.md). The prescribed [algebraic intersection number of curves on an oriented surface](../../../../../../algebraic-intersection-number-of-curves-on-an-oriented-surface.md) convention gives $\tilde f_2\cdot\tilde h_2=1$. Hence

$$
\mu_2\cdot\lambda_2=(-q^*)q-(-p)p^*=pp^*-qq^*=1.
$$

In $H_1(V;\mathbb Z)$, the first [Dehn filling](../../../../../../dehn-filling.md) imposes $p^*f-qh_1=0$, while $h_1+h_2=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\iota_{2*}(\lambda_2)=p^*f+qh_2=0.}
$$

The class $\lambda_2$ is primitive because $\gcd(p^*,q)=1$, a consequence of $pp^*-qq^*=1$. It is therefore the disk-bounding [meridian of a solid torus](../../../../../../meridian-of-a-solid-torus.md) of $V$. Since $\mu_2$ meets it once, [Dehn filling](../../../../../../dehn-filling.md) $V$ along $\mu_2$ glues two [solid torus](../../../../../../solid-torus.md) pieces with disk boundaries meeting once. This is the standard genus-one [Heegaard splitting](../../../../../../heegaard-splitting.md) of $S^3$, so

$$
\boxed{M_{S^2}(p^*/q,-q^*/p)\cong S^3.}
$$

To identify the regular fiber geometrically, put it on their common boundary [torus](../../../../../../torus.md). Invert the displayed basis change:

$$
\boxed{\tilde f_2=q\mu_2+p\lambda_2.}
$$

Here $\mu_2$ bounds a disk in the newly attached [solid torus](../../../../../../solid-torus.md) and $\lambda_2$ bounds a disk in $V$. Thus the curve has winding numbers $p$ and $q$ in the two complementary [solid torus](../../../../../../solid-torus.md) pieces. Both coefficients are positive and $\gcd(p,q)=1$, so it is **the positive $(p,q)$ torus knot**. This identifies the [torus knot](../../../../../../torus-knot.md) directly from the genus-one splitting, without invoking a theorem specifically about [torus knots](../../../../../../torus-knot.md).
