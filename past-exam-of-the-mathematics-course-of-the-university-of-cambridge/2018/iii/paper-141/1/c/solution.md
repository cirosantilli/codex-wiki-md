<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [elliptization theorem](../../../../../../thurston-elliptization-conjecture.md) says that a closed oriented [three-manifold](../../../../../../3-manifold.md) with finite [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) has spherical geometry. When the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) is a [cyclic group](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md), it is a [lens space](../../../../../../lens-space.md). Consequently the candidates are $L(5,q)$ with $q\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, using the same surgery orientation convention throughout.

The [oriented classification of lens spaces](../../../../../../oriented-classification-of-lens-spaces.md) says that $L(p,q)$ and $L(p,q')$ are related by an orientation-preserving [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md) exactly when $q'\equiv q^{\pm1}\pmod p$. Since $1^{-1}=1$, $4^{-1}=4$, and $2^{-1}=3$ modulo five, the complete list is

$$
\boxed{L(5,1),\qquad L(5,2)\cong L(5,3),\qquad L(5,4).}
$$

These are three distinct oriented [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md) classes. Reversing orientation interchanges $L(5,1)$ and $L(5,4)$; the class containing $L(5,2)$ and $L(5,3)$ admits an orientation-reversing [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md). The [elliptization theorem](../../../../../../thurston-elliptization-conjecture.md) and the stated [oriented classification of lens spaces](../../../../../../oriented-classification-of-lens-spaces.md) are the general classification results used here.
