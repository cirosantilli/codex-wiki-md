↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [solid torus](../../../../../../solid-torus.md) supplied by the $\alpha$ side has [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) generated by the image $x$ of $\mu$, while $\lambda$ is trivial. The $\beta$ [two-handle](../../../../../../two-handle.md) imposes $x^5=1$. The [three-handles](../../../../../../three-handle.md) do not change the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md), so

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(L(5,3))=\langle x\mid x^5=1\rangle\cong\mathbb Z/5.}
$$
