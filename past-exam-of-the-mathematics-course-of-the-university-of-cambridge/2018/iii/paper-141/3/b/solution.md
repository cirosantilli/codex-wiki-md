<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the region labels and curve orientations from part (a). A [Dehn presentation of a knot group](../../../../../../dehn-presentation-of-a-knot-group.md) generator goes from the basepoint above the projection plane through its region and returns through the unbounded region. Equivalently it is dual to the correspondingly labeled $\alpha$ disk of the [Heegaard diagram](../../../../../../heegaard-diagram.md). A crossing [relator](../../../../../../relator.md) records the signed intersections of its $\beta$ curve with these disks. Setting $o=1$ gives

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(X_8)=\langle d,e,a,b,c\mid ae^{-1}d^{-1},\ ad^{-1}cb^{-1},\ ba^{-1}ec^{-1},\ cd^{-1}e^{-1}\rangle.}
$$

For example, the overpassing strand at $A$ equates the [meridian of a knot](../../../../../../meridian-of-a-knot.md) words $d$ and $ae^{-1}$; at $B$ it equates $ad^{-1}$ and $bc^{-1}$. The other two crossings equate $ba^{-1}$ with $ce^{-1}$ and $cd^{-1}$ with $e$.

Choose the region-generator direction so that $e$ is the shown [meridian of a knot](../../../../../../meridian-of-a-knot.md) $\mu$. The [Alexander numbering](../../../../../../alexander-numbering.md) of the regions, starting from zero outside and increasing by one on crossing an oriented strand from right to left, is

$$
\boxed{\varphi(d)=\varphi(e)=\varphi(\mu),\qquad \varphi(a)=\varphi(c)=2\varphi(\mu),\qquad \varphi(b)=3\varphi(\mu).}
$$

One can also obtain this directly by [abelianization](../../../../../../abelianization.md) of the [relators](../../../../../../relator.md): $a=d+e$, $c=d+e$, $b=a-d+c$, and $b-a+e-c=0$. The last two imply $d=e$, and the resulting [first homology group](../../../../../../first-homology.md) is freely generated by $e$. Thus $H_1(X_8;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$, with no extra torsion.
