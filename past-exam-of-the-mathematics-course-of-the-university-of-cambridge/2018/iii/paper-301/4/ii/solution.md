<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) preserves the quadratic form of the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) for every four-vector. Substituting $x'^\sigma=\Lambda^\sigma{}_{\mu}x^\mu$ gives

$$
\eta_{\sigma\tau}\Lambda^\sigma{}_{\mu}\Lambda^\tau{}_{\nu}x^\mu x^\nu
=\eta_{\mu\nu}x^\mu x^\nu.
$$

The coefficient difference is a symmetric matrix, and a symmetric quadratic form that vanishes on every vector is zero: evaluating it on basis vectors and their sums recovers each coefficient. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\Lambda^T\eta\Lambda=\eta,\qquad
\eta_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\sigma\tau}\Lambda^\sigma{}_{\mu}\Lambda^\tau{}_{\nu}.}
$$

This is the defining metric-preservation relation of the [Lorentz group](../../../../../../lorentz-group.md).
