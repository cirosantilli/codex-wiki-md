↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under a [gauge transformation](../../../../../../gauge-transformation.md), the [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) changes by

$$
\delta F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\xi-\partial_\nu\partial_\mu\xi=0.
$$

The partial derivatives commute for a smooth gauge parameter, or in the distributional formulation. Thus the [Maxwell Lagrangian](../../../../../../maxwell-lagrangian.md) depends only on a gauge-invariant tensor:

$$
\boxed{F'_{\mu\nu}=F_{\mu\nu},\qquad\mathcal L'=-\frac14F'_{\mu\nu}F'^{\mu\nu}=\mathcal L.}
$$

Strictly, the pointwise calculation requires the second derivatives to exist; smooth gauge parameters are the usual convention. Mere first differentiability in the wording can instead be understood through commuting distributional derivatives.
