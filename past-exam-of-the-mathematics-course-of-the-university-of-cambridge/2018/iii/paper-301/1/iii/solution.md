<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At this order there is one annihilation [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md). The [squared-amplitude diagram](../../../../../../squared-amplitude-diagram.md) is its product with its complex conjugate, with the same external momenta and spin labels in both factors:

```
 e-(p,s) --->\          /---> mu-(p',s')
             o ~~~~~~ o
 e+(q,r) <---/          \<--- mu+(q',r')

                     times

 e-(p,s) --->\          /---> mu-(p',s')
             o ~~~~~~ o                       *
 e+(q,r) <---/          \<--- mu+(q',r')
```

A wavy line is the virtual [photon](../../../../../../photon.md); the arrows show fermion-number flow. The star means complex conjugation of the entire second graph, including its external spinors. This represents $|\mathcal M|^2=\mathcal M\mathcal M^*$ for fixed spins, without adding a new physical loop to the amplitude.

For the [spin sum](../../../../../../spin-sum.md) in part (iv), identify each external spin label between the two copies and sum it. The [fermion spin sum](../../../../../../fermion-spin-sum.md) sews each spinor chain into a [gamma-matrix trace](../../../../../../gamma-matrix-trace.md). The two incoming unpolarized spin states are then handled by a [spin average](../../../../../../spin-average.md), giving the factor $1/4$. In particular, the diagrammatic contraction becomes

$$
\boxed{\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=\frac{e^4}{4s^2}
\operatorname{tr}(\not q\gamma^\mu\not p\gamma^\nu)
\operatorname{tr}(\not p'\gamma_\mu\not q'\gamma_\nu).}
$$
