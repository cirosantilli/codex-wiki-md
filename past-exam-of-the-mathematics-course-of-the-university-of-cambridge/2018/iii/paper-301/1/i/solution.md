<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use $\hbar=c=1$ and the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$ throughout. In the four-component [Dirac spinor](../../../../../../dirac-spinor.md) representation, the [gamma matrices](../../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) generate the [Clifford algebra](../../../../../../clifford-algebra.md)

$$
\boxed{\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu+\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu=2\eta^{\mu\nu}I_4.}
$$

Thus $(\gamma^0)^2=I_4$, $(\gamma^i)^2=-I_4$, and matrices with distinct indices anticommute. Indices are lowered with $\eta$, so $\gamma_\mu=\eta_{\mu\nu}\gamma^\nu$ and $\not p=p_\mu\gamma^\mu$.
