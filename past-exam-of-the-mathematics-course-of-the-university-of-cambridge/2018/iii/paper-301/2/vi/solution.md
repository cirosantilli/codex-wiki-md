<h1 id="2/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

For the two free [real scalar fields](../../../../../../real-scalar-field.md), let $\mathcal L=\mathcal L_1+\mathcal L_2$. The infinitesimal [internal rotation symmetry of two real scalar fields](../../../../../../internal-rotation-symmetry-of-two-real-scalar-fields.md) has $\delta\phi_1=\theta\phi_2$ and $\delta\phi_2=-\theta\phi_1$, with constant $\theta$. Its kinetic terms are invariant because this is an orthogonal rotation of the two-component field.

Under the assumed invariance of the full [Lagrangian density](../../../../../../lagrangian-density.md), [Noether's theorem](../../../../../../noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) gives the current per unit transformation parameter:

$$
j^\mu=\sum_{a=1}^2\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu\phi_a)}\frac{\delta\phi_a}{\theta}
=\phi_2\partial^\mu\phi_1-\phi_1\partial^\mu\phi_2.
$$

The [Noether current](../../../../../../noether-current.md) and [Noether charge](../../../../../../noether-charge.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{j^\mu=\phi_2\partial^\mu\phi_1-\phi_1\partial^\mu\phi_2,\qquad
Q=\int d^3x\,(\phi_2\pi_1-\phi_1\pi_2).}
$$

Independent species commute at equal time, so these products require no extra ordering sign. With vanishing boundary flux, $Q$ is conserved. Part (vii) checks precisely what the symmetry assumption requires of the masses.
