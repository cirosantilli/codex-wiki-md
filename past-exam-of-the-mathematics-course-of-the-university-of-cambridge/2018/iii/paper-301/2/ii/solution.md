<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a free [real scalar field](../../../../../../real-scalar-field.md), the [Lagrangian density](../../../../../../lagrangian-density.md) contains its quadratic [kinetic term](../../../../../../kinetic-term.md) and [mass term](../../../../../../mass-term.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_1=\frac12\partial_\mu\phi_1\partial^\mu\phi_1-\frac12m_1^2\phi_1^2
=\frac12\dot\phi_1^2-\frac12|\nabla\phi_1|^2-\frac12m_1^2\phi_1^2.}
$$

The field's conjugate momentum is $\pi_1=\partial\mathcal L_1/\partial\dot\phi_1=\dot\phi_1$. The positive sign of the time-derivative term uses the metric convention fixed in Question 1.
