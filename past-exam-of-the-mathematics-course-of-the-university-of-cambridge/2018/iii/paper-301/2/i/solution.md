<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a time-independent [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) and a [Schrödinger picture](../../../../../../schrodinger-picture.md) observable with no explicit time dependence, the [Heisenberg picture](../../../../../../heisenberg-picture.md) operator is

$$
\boxed{O_H(t)=e^{iHt}O_Se^{-iHt}.}
$$

Differentiation gives $\dot O_H=iHO_H-iO_HH=i[H,O_H]$, so

$$
\boxed{[H,O_H]=-i\frac{dO_H}{dt}.}
$$

If $O_S$ itself depends explicitly on time, one must add $e^{iHt}(\partial_tO_S)e^{-iHt}$ to $\dot O_H$; the displayed commutator relation uses the usual convention of no explicit time dependence for the observable.
