<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply the [Legendre transform in mechanics](../../../../../../legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) pointwise to the field [Lagrangian density](../../../../../../lagrangian-density.md). Using $\pi_1=\dot\phi_1$, the Hamiltonian density is

$$
\mathcal H_1=\pi_1\dot\phi_1-\mathcal L_1
=\frac12\pi_1^2+\frac12|\nabla\phi_1|^2+\frac12m_1^2\phi_1^2.
$$

The free [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is its spatial integral:

$$
\boxed{H_1=\frac12\int_{\mathbb R^3}d^3x\,[\pi_1^2+(\nabla\phi_1)^2+m_1^2\phi_1^2].}
$$

A constant vacuum-energy subtraction, if made, does not affect the commutators or the ensuing field equation.
