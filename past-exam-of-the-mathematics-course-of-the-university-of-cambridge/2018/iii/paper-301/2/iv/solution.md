The [canonical quantization of a real scalar field](../../../../../../canonical-quantization-of-a-real-scalar-field.md) promotes the classical equal-time brackets to the [canonical commutation relations](../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md), in units $\hbar=1$:

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
[\phi_1(t,\mathbf x),\phi_1(t,\mathbf y)]&=0,\\
[\pi_1(t,\mathbf x),\pi_1(t,\mathbf y)]&=0,\\
[\phi_1(t,\mathbf x),\pi_1(t,\mathbf y)]&=i\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf x-\mathbf y).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The [Dirac delta distribution](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) expresses locality of the conjugate variables. These are identities of operator-valued distributions, interpreted after spatial smearing where necessary.
