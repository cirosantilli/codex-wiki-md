<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In four spacetime dimensions with $\hbar=c=1$, a [relativistic scattering cross-section](../../../../../../relativistic-scattering-cross-section.md) has [mass dimension](../../../../../../mass-dimension.md) $-2$, since it is an area. The action is dimensionless, so the [Lagrangian density](../../../../../../lagrangian-density.md) has dimension four. The scalar [kinetic term](../../../../../../kinetic-term.md) then gives $[\phi]=1$, and the cubic term gives $[\lambda]=1$.

Each term of the [four-point tree amplitude in phi cubed theory](../../../../../../four-point-tree-amplitude-in-phi-cubed-theory.md) has dimension $[\lambda^2]-[s-m^2]=2-2=0$. Hence $[|\mathcal M|^2]=0$. In the final integral,

$$
[dt]=2,\qquad [s]=[s-4m^2]=2,
$$

while the numerical factors $X,Y$ are dimensionless. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\left[\int dt\,\frac{|\mathcal M|^2}{s(s-4m^2)}\right]=2+0-4=-2=[\sigma].}
$$

Including the identical-particle factor changes only a dimensionless constant and preserves this check.
