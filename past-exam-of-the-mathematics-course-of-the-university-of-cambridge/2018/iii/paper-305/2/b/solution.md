<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the matrix expression, $t^a=\lambda^a/2$ denotes a fundamental generator, whereas the component [adjoint covariant derivative](../../../../../../adjoint-covariant-derivative.md) uses adjoint generators. With $\operatorname{Tr}(t^dt^h)=\delta^{dh}/2$,

$$
[t^a,\Phi]=if^{acd}\phi_ct^d,\qquad
-g^2\operatorname{Tr}([t^a,\Phi][t^b,\Phi])B_\mu^aB^{b\mu}
=\frac{g^2}{2}f^{acd}f^{bed}\phi_c\phi_eB_\mu^aB^{b\mu}.
$$

Using antisymmetry to write $f^{acd}=f^{dac}$ and $f^{bed}=f^{dbe}$ gives exactly the component [mass term](../../../../../../mass-term.md). This is the [gauge-boson mass matrix from an adjoint scalar](../../../../../../gauge-boson-mass-matrix-from-an-adjoint-scalar.md).

For $\Phi_0=v\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2)$, the [Gell-Mann matrices](../../../../../../gell-mann-matrices.md) $t^1,t^2,t^3,t^8$ commute with $\Phi_0$. Their [gauge bosons](../../../../../../gauge-boson.md) are therefore massless. The generators $t^4,t^5,t^6,t^7$ connect the first two [eigenspaces](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) to the third; the [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) difference is $3v$. Directly taking the [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}([t^a,\Phi_0][t^b,\Phi_0])=-\frac{9v^2}{2}\delta^{ab},\qquad a,b\in\{4,5,6,7\},
$$

with all remaining entries zero. Thus

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm mass}=\frac{9g^2v^2}{2}\sum_{a=4}^7 B_\mu^aB^{a\mu},\qquad
\boxed{m_{1,2,3,8}=0,\qquad m_{4,5,6,7}=3g|v|.}
$$

The factor $1/2$ is the canonical real-vector [mass term](../../../../../../mass-term.md) normalization. The unbroken [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) is $\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)$, giving the usual description **SU(2) × U(1)**. More precisely, the [centralizer](../../../../../../centralizer.md) of the [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) inside $SU(3)$ is

$$
\boxed{S(U(2)\times U(1))\simeq (SU(2)\times U(1))/\mathbb Z_2.}
$$

This global quotient refines the [adjoint-Higgs breaking of SU(3) to SU(2) and U(1)](../../../../../../adjoint-higgs-breaking-of-su-3-to-su-2-and-u-1.md) without changing the local [gauge boson](../../../../../../gauge-boson.md) spectrum.
