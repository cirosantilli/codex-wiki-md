<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $|1\rangle=|D^0\rangle$, $|2\rangle=|\bar D^0\rangle$. In the rest frame, the [CPT theorem](../../../../../../cpt-theorem.md) interchanges these states up to irrelevant phases. For the weak [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H'$, with $H'=H'^{\dagger}$, and its antiunitary [CPT symmetry](../../../../../../cpt-symmetry.md) operator $\Theta$,

$$
\langle\Theta1|H'|\Theta1\rangle=\langle1|H'|1\rangle^*.
$$

Its diagonal [matrix elements](../../../../../../matrix-element.md) are real by [Hermitian conjugation](../../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md), so

$$
\boxed{R_{11}=R_{22}.}
$$

In the specified phase convention, the [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md) operator on this two-state subspace is $-\sigma_x$. If the [weak interaction](../../../../../../weak-interaction.md) preserves [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md), $\sigma_xR\sigma_x=R$, giving

$$
\boxed{R_{12}=R_{21}.}
$$

Independently, [Hermitian conjugation](../../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md) gives $R_{21}=R_{12}^*$ for this $H'$. Hence [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md) makes the off-diagonal element real in this convention; that conjugation relation alone does not imply [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md).

For [neutral meson mixing](../../../../../../neutral-meson-mixing.md) described by the decay-effective [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $R=M-i\Gamma/2$, the effective matrix is generally not a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). The [CPT constraints on neutral-meson mixing](../../../../../../cpt-constraints-on-neutral-meson-mixing.md) still give equality of the complex diagonal entries, expressing equal masses and total [decay widths](../../../../../../decay-width.md), and [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md) gives equality of the off-diagonal entries in the specified convention. One must not additionally impose $R_{21}=R_{12}^*$ on this decay-effective matrix.
