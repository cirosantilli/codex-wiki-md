<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Both the [D meson](../../../../../../d-meson.md) and [kaon](../../../../../../kaon.md) are spin-zero [pseudoscalars](../../../../../../pseudoscalar.md). The [strong interaction](../../../../../../strong-interaction.md) states obey [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md). Consequently an [axial current](../../../../../../axial-current.md) [matrix element](../../../../../../matrix-element.md) between them would have to be a [pseudovector](../../../../../../pseudovector.md) formed from only $p$ and $k$, which is impossible: an [totally antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../../totally-antisymmetric-tensor.md) would require more [linearly independent vectors](../../../../../../linear-independence.md). Thus

$$
\langle K^+(k)|\bar c\gamma^\mu\gamma^5s|\bar D^0(p)\rangle=0.
$$

[Lorentz covariance](../../../../../../lorentz-covariance.md) then leaves two [linearly independent vectors](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) for the [vector current](../../../../../../vector-current.md) [matrix element](../../../../../../matrix-element.md), giving the [pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar form factors](../../../../../../pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar-form-factor.md)

$$
\langle K^+(k)|\bar c\gamma^\mu s|\bar D^0(p)\rangle
=(p+k)^\mu f_+(q^2)+(p-k)^\mu f_-(q^2),\qquad q=p-k.
$$

The vanishing [axial current](../../../../../../axial-current.md) here follows from [strong interaction](../../../../../../strong-interaction.md) [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md), not [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) of the [weak interaction](../../../../../../weak-interaction.md).

To obtain the requested decay formula, use [naive factorization of a nonleptonic meson decay](../../../../../../naive-factorization-of-a-nonleptonic-meson-decay.md): approximate the four-quark [matrix element](../../../../../../matrix-element.md) by the product of the $\bar D^0\to K^+$ current [matrix element](../../../../../../matrix-element.md) and the vacuum-to-[pion](../../../../../../pion.md) [matrix element](../../../../../../matrix-element.md). This is an additional hadronic approximation; tree-level weak vertices alone do not establish it, and nonfactorizable [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) effects can change the result. The vacuum-to-[pion](../../../../../../pion.md) [vector current](../../../../../../vector-current.md) [matrix element](../../../../../../matrix-element.md) vanishes by [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md), while the specified [pion decay constant](../../../../../../pion-decay-constant.md) normalization gives

$$
\langle\pi^-(q)|\bar d\gamma_\mu(1-\gamma^5)u|0\rangle=+i\sqrt2 F_\pi q_\mu.
$$

The $\sqrt2$ cancels the $1/\sqrt2$ in the [four-fermion interaction](../../../../../../four-fermion-interaction.md). Up to an irrelevant overall phase, the [scattering amplitude](../../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) is therefore

$$
\mathcal M=G_F V_{cs}V_{ud}^*F_\pi\left[(m_D^2-m_K^2)f_+(q^2)+q^2f_-(q^2)\right].
$$

For $m_\pi=0$, $q^2=0$, so only $f_+(0)$ survives. Integrating the [two-body decay phase space](../../../../../../two-body-decay-phase-space.md) in the [D meson](../../../../../../d-meson.md) rest frame gives

$$
|\boldsymbol k|=\frac{m_D^2-m_K^2}{2m_D},\qquad
\Gamma=\frac{|\boldsymbol k|}{8\pi m_D^2}|\mathcal M|^2.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{(m_D^2-m_K^2)^3}{16\pi m_D^3},\qquad
\Gamma=\frac{G_F^2(m_D^2-m_K^2)^3}{16\pi m_D^3}\left|V_{ud}^*V_{cs}F_\pi f_+(0)\right|^2.}
$$

This coefficient uses exactly the stated [pion decay constant](../../../../../../pion-decay-constant.md) convention and the stated massless-[pion](../../../../../../pion.md) approximation.
