<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the physical positive-energy branch, put $\omega=q^0+\xi E_P$. The massless outgoing [parton](../../../../../../../parton.md) obeys

$$
E_{k'}^2-\omega^2=-(\xi P+q)^2=-q^2-2\xi\nu=2\nu(x-\xi).
$$

Since $E_{k'}=\omega>0$ on the support and $\nu>0$, the [positive-energy on-shell delta function identity](../../../../../../../positive-energy-on-shell-delta-function-identity.md) gives

$$
\frac{\delta(\omega-E_{k'})}{E_{k'}}=2\delta(E_{k'}^2-\omega^2)=\frac{\delta(x-\xi)}{\nu}.
$$

Insert this into the [parton model](../../../../../../../parton-model.md) sum, and write

$$
S(x)=\sum_f Q_f^2\left[q_f(x)+\bar q_f(x)\right].
$$

The [parton distribution functions](../../../../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) $q_f,\bar q_f$ are number densities in momentum fraction. The contributing tensor becomes

$$
W_H^{\mu\nu}\doteq S(x)\left[\frac{x}{\nu}P^\mu P^\nu-\frac12g^{\mu\nu}\right].
$$

Comparing with part (b), under the same [leptonic tensor](../../../../../../../leptonic-tensor.md) contraction, gives

$$
F_1(x)=\frac12 S(x),\qquad F_2(x)=xS(x),\qquad
\boxed{F_2(x,q^2)=2xF_1(x,q^2).}
$$

This is the [Callan-Gross relation](../../../../../../../callan-gross-relation.md) for massless [partons](../../../../../../../parton.md) of [spin angular momentum](../../../../../../../spin.md) $1/2$ at leading order. The [longitudinal deep-inelastic structure function](../../../../../../../longitudinal-deep-inelastic-structure-function.md) is $F_L=F_2-2xF_1=0$ in this approximation. Target-mass effects and radiative [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections can change the relation. The leading [parton model](../../../../../../../parton-model.md) also gives [Bjorken scaling](../../../../../../../bjorken-scaling.md): at this level the [deep-inelastic structure functions](../../../../../../../deep-inelastic-structure-function.md) depend on $x$ alone.
