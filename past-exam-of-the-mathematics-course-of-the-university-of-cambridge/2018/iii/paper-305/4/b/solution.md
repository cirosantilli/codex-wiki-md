<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an unpolarized electromagnetic target, [Lorentz covariance](../../../../../../lorentz-covariance.md) and [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) permit a symmetric [hadronic tensor](../../../../../../hadronic-tensor.md) built from $g^{\mu\nu}$, $P^\mu$ and $q^\mu$, where $P=P_H$. Its surviving general form is

$$
W^{\mu\nu}=a g^{\mu\nu}+bP^\mu P^\nu+cq^\mu q^\nu+d(P^\mu q^\nu+q^\mu P^\nu).
$$

The [totally antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../../totally-antisymmetric-tensor.md) structure $\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}P_\rho q_\sigma$ is excluded by electromagnetic [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) for this unpolarized target. The other possible antisymmetric structure, $P^\mu q^\nu-q^\mu P^\nu$, cannot be transverse for generic scattering kinematics and hence is excluded by [current conservation](../../../../../../conserved-current.md). [Current conservation](../../../../../../conserved-current.md) gives the [Ward identities](../../../../../../ward-identity.md) $q_\mu W^{\mu\nu}=q_\nu W^{\mu\nu}=0$, imposing

$$
b\nu+dq^2=0,\qquad a+cq^2+d\nu=0,\qquad \nu=P\cdot q.
$$

Two scalar functions remain. Taking $\widetilde P^\mu=P^\mu-\nu q^\mu/q^2$ gives the transverse basis

$$
\boxed{W_H^{\mu\nu}=\left(-g^{\mu\nu}+\frac{q^\mu q^\nu}{q^2}\right)F_1(x,q^2)+\widetilde P^\mu\widetilde P^\nu\frac{F_2(x,q^2)}{\nu},\qquad x=-\frac{q^2}{2\nu}.}
$$

These are the [deep-inelastic structure functions](../../../../../../deep-inelastic-structure-function.md); $x$ is [Bjorken x](../../../../../../bjorken-x.md). At fixed target mass, the two independent [Lorentz scalar](../../../../../../lorentz-scalar.md) invariants can equivalently be chosen as $x,q^2$. In this paper $\nu=P\cdot q$ has dimensions of mass squared, rather than the alternative convention $\nu=P\cdot q/M$ used for energy transfer.
