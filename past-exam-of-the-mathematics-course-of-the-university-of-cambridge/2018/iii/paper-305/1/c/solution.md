<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [normal ordering](../../../../../../normal-ordering.md) of a [fermion bilinear](../../../../../../fermion-bilinear.md), the result of part (a) and the [fermionic sign](../../../../../../fermionic-sign.md) from interchanging the fields give

$$
\hat C(\bar\psi\Gamma\psi)\hat C^{-1}
=-\psi^T C^{-1}\Gamma C\bar\psi^T
=\bar\psi(C^{-1}\Gamma C)^T\psi.
$$

For the [Dirac current](../../../../../../dirac-current.md), $(C^{-1}\gamma^\mu C)^T=-\gamma^\mu$. For the [axial current](../../../../../../axial-current.md), the definition of $\gamma^5$ and the [gamma matrix](../../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) identities imply

$$
C^{-1}\gamma^5C=\gamma^{5T},\qquad
(C^{-1}\gamma^\mu\gamma^5C)^T
=-\gamma^5\gamma^\mu=\gamma^\mu\gamma^5.
$$

Thus the [charge conjugation of fermion bilinears](../../../../../../charge-conjugation-of-fermion-bilinears.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\hat C(\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi)\hat C^{-1}=-\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi,\qquad
\hat C(\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi)\hat C^{-1}=+\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi.}
$$

The [Dirac current](../../../../../../dirac-current.md) is **odd under [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md)**, and the [axial current](../../../../../../axial-current.md) is **even under [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md)**. The [normal ordering](../../../../../../normal-ordering.md) specifies the usual treatment of coincident-field contact terms.
