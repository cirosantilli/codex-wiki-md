<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The flavour-diagonal [weak neutral current](../../../../../../neutral-current.md) coupled to the real [Z boson](../../../../../../z-boson.md) has real vector and [axial current](../../../../../../axial-current.md) couplings. With $\eta_C(Z)=-1$, part (e) shows that this tree-level interaction is **invariant under [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md)**, although the simultaneous presence of the [Dirac current](../../../../../../dirac-current.md) and [axial current](../../../../../../axial-current.md) generally violates [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) and [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) separately. This conclusion concerns the tree-level [weak neutral current](../../../../../../neutral-current.md), rather than every interaction or radiative effect in the [Standard Model](../../../../../../standard-model-split.md).

The [weak charged current](../../../../../../charged-current.md) couples distinct [quark](../../../../../../quark.md) flavours and involves the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md):

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm cc}=-\frac{g}{\sqrt2}\bar u_{Li}\gamma^\mu V_{ij}d_{Lj}W_\mu^++\text{h.c.}
$$

Here $u_{Li}$ and $d_{Lj}$ are [left-handed](../../../../../../chirality-physics.md) [quark](../../../../../../quark.md) fields. A [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md) transformation exchanges the two conjugate-field operators; because it acts through a [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md), it does not complex-conjugate their numerical coefficients. Equality with the original interaction therefore requires a [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) that can be made real by [quantum field rephasings](../../../../../../quantum-field-rephasing.md). With three generations, a physical [CP-violating phase](../../../../../../cp-violating-phase.md) remains in general, measured by the [Jarlskog invariant](../../../../../../jarlskog-invariant.md). Therefore the [W boson](../../../../../../w-boson.md) interactions **can violate [CP symmetry](../../../../../../cp-symmetry.md)**. Merely seeing a complex coefficient is insufficient: the phase must survive [quantum field rephasing](../../../../../../quantum-field-rephasing.md), unlike the real two-generation [Cabibbo angle](../../../../../../cabibbo-angle.md) rotation.
