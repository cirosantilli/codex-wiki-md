<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [bosonic annihilation operator](../../../../../../bosonic-annihilation-operator.md) $a^\lambda(p)$ removes a vector particle of [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) $p$ and [polarization vector](../../../../../../polarization-vector.md) $\varepsilon^{\mu,\lambda}(p)$. The [bosonic creation operator](../../../../../../bosonic-creation-operator.md) $c^{\lambda\dagger}(p)$ creates its [antiparticle](../../../../../../antiparticle.md) with the same [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) and polarization label. The [polarization vector](../../../../../../polarization-vector.md) describes the corresponding spin-one [mode expansion of a free field](../../../../../../mode-expansion-of-a-free-field.md).

Because [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) acts through a [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md), it leaves the numerical [polarization vector](../../../../../../polarization-vector.md) coefficients unchanged. Applying the specified [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) to the operators gives

$$
\hat C V^\mu(x)\hat C^{-1}
=\eta_C\sum_{p,\lambda}\left[\varepsilon^{\mu,\lambda}(p)c^\lambda(p)e^{-ip\cdot x}+\varepsilon^{\mu,\lambda *}(p)a^{\lambda\dagger}(p)e^{ip\cdot x}\right].
$$

Comparison with the [Hermitian conjugation](../../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md) of the original expansion therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\hat C V^\mu(x)\hat C^{-1}=\eta_C V^{\mu\dagger}(x).}
$$

The factor $\eta_C$ is the [intrinsic charge-conjugation phase](../../../../../../intrinsic-charge-conjugation-phase.md).
