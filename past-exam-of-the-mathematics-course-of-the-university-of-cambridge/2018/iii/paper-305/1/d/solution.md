<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a real vector [relativistic quantum field](../../../../../../relativistic-quantum-field-split.md), $V^\mu=V^{\mu\dagger}$. Its transformed field must also be a [Hermitian operator](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), so the [intrinsic charge-conjugation phase](../../../../../../intrinsic-charge-conjugation-phase.md) satisfies $\eta_C=\eta_C^*$; combined with $|\eta_C|=1$, this gives

$$
\boxed{\eta_C=\pm1.}
$$

The [quantum electrodynamics](../../../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md) interaction is $-e\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi A_\mu$. Since the [Dirac electromagnetic current](../../../../../../dirac-electromagnetic-current.md) is odd under [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md), [charge conjugation](../../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) invariance of this interaction requires the [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) field to be odd too:

$$
\boxed{\eta_C(A)=-1,\qquad \hat C A^\mu\hat C^{-1}=-A^\mu.}
$$
