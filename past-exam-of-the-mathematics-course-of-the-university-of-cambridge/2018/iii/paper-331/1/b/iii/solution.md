↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The stationary-point condition for the squared [growth rate](../../../../../../../growth-rate.md) is $A-3\gamma k_m^2=0$. Its second derivative is $-6\gamma k_m/(\rho_1+\rho_2)<0$, so

$$
\boxed{k_m=\frac{k_c}{\sqrt3}=\sqrt{\frac{g(\rho_1-\rho_2)}{3\gamma}}.}
$$
