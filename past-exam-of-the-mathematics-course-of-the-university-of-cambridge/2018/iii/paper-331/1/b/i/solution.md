<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $A=g(\rho_1-\rho_2)>0$. In the deep-layer limit, the [finite-depth Rayleigh-Taylor dispersion relation](../../../../../../../finite-depth-rayleigh-taylor-dispersion-relation.md) gives $\omega^2=(\gamma k^3-Ak)/(\rho_1+\rho_2)$. Its sign changes at

$$
\boxed{k_c=\sqrt{\frac{g(\rho_1-\rho_2)}{\gamma}}.}
$$

The [Rayleigh-Taylor instability](../../../../../../../rayleigh-taylor-instability.md) band is $0<k<k_c$; $k=k_c$ is neutral and $k>k_c$ is oscillatory.
