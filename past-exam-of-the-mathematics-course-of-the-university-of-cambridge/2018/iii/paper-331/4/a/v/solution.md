<h1 id="4/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For $q(t)=Bq_0$, the energy ratio is the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) of the [Hermitian positive-definite matrix](../../../../../../../hermitian-positive-definite-matrix.md) $B^\dagger B$:

$$
\frac{\|Bq_0\|_2^2}{\|q_0\|_2^2}=\frac{q_0^\dagger B^\dagger Bq_0}{q_0^\dagger q_0}.
$$

Expanding $q_0$ in the orthonormal [right singular vectors](../../../../../../../right-singular-vector.md) makes this a weighted average of $\sigma_j^2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{G(t)=\|e^{Lt}\|_2^2=\sigma_1^2(t),\qquad q_0\propto v_1(t).}
$$

Any nonzero vector in the largest right-singular subspace is optimal if $\sigma_1$ is repeated. Its amplified state is proportional to the paired [left singular vector](../../../../../../../left-singular-vector.md). For a [non-normal matrix](../../../../../../../non-normal-matrix.md) $L$, this optimal initial direction need not be an eigenvector of $L$, explaining [transient growth from non-normal modes](../../../../../../../transient-growth-from-non-normal-modes.md).
