<h1 id="3/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $W=(D^2-1)\widehat v$ and $\delta^3=Re^{-1}$. The constant-shear [Orr-Sommerfeld equation](../../../../../../../orr-sommerfeld-equation.md) gives

$$
(y-c)W=-i\delta^3(W''-W),\qquad
W''=\left[1+\frac{i(y-c)}{\delta^3}\right]W
=\frac{i(y-c-i\delta^3)}{\delta^3}W.
$$

Let $a=e^{i\pi/6}$ and $Z=a(y-c-i\delta^3)/\delta$. Since $d/dy=(a/\delta)d/dZ$ and $a^3=i$, the equation becomes

$$
\frac{a^2}{\delta^2}W_{ZZ}=\frac{i}{a\delta^2}ZW,\qquad
\boxed{W_{ZZ}-ZW=0.}
$$

This is the [Airy ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../../airy-ordinary-differential-equation.md), yielding the [Airy reduction of the Orr-Sommerfeld equation in constant shear](../../../../../../../airy-reduction-of-the-orr-sommerfeld-equation-in-constant-shear.md). It is distinct from the evolution PDE represented by the existing [Airy equation](../../../../../../../airy-equation.md) article.
