<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $F(x,t)=t-\tau(x)$, the conormal to its zero set is $dF=(-D\tau,1)$. The [principal symbol](../../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) of $\partial_t^2+L$ is

$$
p(x,t;\xi,\sigma)=\sigma^2-\sum_{i,j}a^{ij}(x,t)\xi_i\xi_j.
$$

A [characteristic hypersurface](../../../../../../../characteristic-hypersurface.md) requires $p(x,t;dF)=0$ on the surface. Thus $\tau$ satisfies the anisotropic [eikonal equation](../../../../../../../eikonal-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i,j}a^{ij}(x,\tau(x))\tau_{x_i}(x)\tau_{x_j}(x)=1.}
$$

The first-order and zeroth-order terms do not affect this condition.
