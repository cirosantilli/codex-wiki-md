<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expanding the equation gives

$$
u_{tt}+2\rho u_{\rho t}+(\rho^2-1)u_{\rho\rho}+u_t+2\rho u_\rho=0.
$$

For the [hyperbolic partial differential equation](../../../../../../../hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) discriminant, $A=1$, $B=\rho$, $C=\rho^2-1$, so

$$
\boxed{B^2-AC=1>0\quad\text{everywhere}.}
$$

For a curve $\rho=\rho(t)$, the [characteristic hypersurface](../../../../../../../characteristic-hypersurface.md) equation is $(\rho')^2-2\rho\rho'+\rho^2-1=0$, or $\rho'=\rho\pm1$. Therefore the two families of [characteristic curves](../../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) are

$$
\boxed{\rho=-1+Ce^t,\qquad\rho=1+Ce^t,\qquad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The values $C=0$ include the characteristic lines $\rho=-1$ and $\rho=1$. Every curve in the first family approaches $\rho=-1$ as $t\to-\infty$; every curve in the second approaches $\rho=1$. The two curves through $(\rho,t)=(0,0)$ are $\rho=e^t-1$ and $\rho=1-e^t$, shown as the thick curves below. Equivalently, the [characteristic coordinates](../../../../../../../characteristic-coordinate.md) are $e^{-t}(\rho+1)$ and $e^{-t}(\rho-1)$.
