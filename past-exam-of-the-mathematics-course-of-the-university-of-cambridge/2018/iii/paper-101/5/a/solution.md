<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**A [Dedekind domain](../../../../../../dedekind-domain.md) is a [Noetherian ring](../../../../../../noetherian-ring.md) $D$ which is an [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md), is an [integrally closed domain](../../../../../../integrally-closed-domain.md), and has [Krull dimension](../../../../../../krull-dimension.md) one**. Explicitly, every [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) is finitely generated; every element of the [fraction field](../../../../../../field-of-fractions.md) that is an [integral element](../../../../../../integral-element.md) over $D$ already belongs to $D$; and every nonzero [prime ideal](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md) is a [maximal ideal](../../../../../../maximal-ideal.md), with $D$ itself not a [field](../../../../../../field.md).

With this convention, an equivalent local characterization is that $D$ is a [Noetherian](../../../../../../noetherian-ring.md) [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md) that is not a [field](../../../../../../field.md) and whose [localization at a prime ideal](../../../../../../localization-at-a-prime-ideal.md) $D_P$ is a [discrete valuation ring](../../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md) for every nonzero [prime ideal](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md) $P$. The [integrally closed domain](../../../../../../integrally-closed-domain.md) condition rules out singularities such as those represented by the subring $k[t^2,t^3]\subset k[t]$, where $t$ is integral but missing.

Some conventions also include [fields](../../../../../../field.md) by allowing [Krull dimension](../../../../../../krull-dimension.md) at most one; the conclusions about [ideals](../../../../../../ideal.md) below remain valid under that convention.
