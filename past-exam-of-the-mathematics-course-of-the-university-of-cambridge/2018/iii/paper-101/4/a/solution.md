**A [ring](../../../../../../ring.md) is a [Noetherian ring](../../../../../../noetherian-ring.md) if it satisfies the [ascending chain condition](../../../../../../ascending-chain-condition.md) on [ideals](../../../../../../ideal.md)**: every chain

$$
I_1\subseteq I_2\subseteq I_3\subseteq\cdots
$$

eventually becomes constant. Equivalently, every [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) has a finite [generating set of an ideal](../../../../../../generating-set-of-an-ideal.md). To see this equivalence, if an [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) were not finitely generated, one could repeatedly choose an element outside the [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) generated by the preceding choices, producing a strictly ascending chain. Conversely, the union of an ascending chain is an [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md); if it is generated by finitely many elements, all of them lie in one term of the chain, and every later term equals that term.

**A [ring](../../../../../../ring.md) is an [Artinian ring](../../../../../../artinian-ring.md) if it satisfies the [descending chain condition](../../../../../../descending-chain-condition.md) on [ideals](../../../../../../ideal.md)**: every chain

$$
I_1\supseteq I_2\supseteq I_3\supseteq\cdots
$$

eventually becomes constant. These are stabilization conditions on all [ideals](../../../../../../ideal.md), not merely on [principal ideals](../../../../../../principal-ideal.md).
