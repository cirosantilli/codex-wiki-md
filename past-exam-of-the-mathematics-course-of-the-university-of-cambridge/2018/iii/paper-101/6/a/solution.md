The [Krull dimension](../../../../../../krull-dimension.md) of a [commutative ring](../../../../../../commutative-ring.md) $R$ is

$$
\boxed{\dim R=\sup\{n:\mathfrak p_0\subsetneq\mathfrak p_1\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq\mathfrak p_n
\text{ is a chain of prime ideals of }R\}.}
$$

The length counts strict inclusions, so a chain with $n+1$ [prime ideals](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md) has length $n$. The supremum may be infinite. A [field](../../../../../../field.md), for example, has just the [prime ideal](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md) $(0)$ and therefore has [Krull dimension](../../../../../../krull-dimension.md) zero. For the zero [ring](../../../../../../ring.md), the [spectrum of a commutative ring](../../../../../../spectrum-of-a-commutative-ring.md) is empty; under the convention $\sup\varnothing=-\infty$, its dimension is $-\infty$.
