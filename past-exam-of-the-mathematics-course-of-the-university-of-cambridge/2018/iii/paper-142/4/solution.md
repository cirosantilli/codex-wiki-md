<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Use the standard complex [K-theory Thom class](../../../../../k-theory-thom-class.md) convention. For a rank-$r$ complex [vector bundle](../../../../../vector-bundle.md), its [K-theory Euler class](../../../../../k-theory-euler-class.md) is the pullback of the [K-theory Thom class](../../../../../k-theory-thom-class.md) along the zero section, namely

$$
\boxed{e_K(E)=\lambda_{-1}(E^*)=\sum_{j=0}^r(-1)^j[\Lambda^jE^*]\in K^0(X).}
$$

Here $\Lambda^j$ denotes an [exterior power](../../../../../exterior-power.md), and a [Hermitian metric](../../../../../hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) identifies the [dual bundle](../../../../../dual-bundle.md) with the conjugate [vector bundle](../../../../../vector-bundle.md). Choosing $\lambda_{-1}(E)$ instead changes the [K-theory Euler class](../../../../../k-theory-euler-class.md) by the unit $(-1)^r[\det E]$, so its kernel and cokernel computations are unchanged.

For the [disk bundle](../../../../../disk-bundle.md) $D(E)$ and [sphere bundle](../../../../../sphere-bundle.md) $S(E)$, the [K-theory Thom class](../../../../../k-theory-thom-class.md) identifies $K^i(D(E),S(E))$ with $K^i(X)$. The [exact sequence](../../../../../exact-sequence.md) of this pair becomes the [K-theory Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](../../../../../k-theory-gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md):

$$
\boxed{\cdots\longrightarrow K^i(X)\xrightarrow{\ e_K(E)\cdot\ }K^i(X)
\xrightarrow{\pi^*}K^i(S(E))\xrightarrow{\partial}K^{i+1}(X)
\xrightarrow{\ e_K(E)\cdot\ }K^{i+1}(X)\longrightarrow\cdots.}
$$

The groups are two-periodic by [Bott periodicity](../../../../../bott-isomorphism.md).

View [Real projective space](../../../../../real-projective-space.md) as the [circle bundle](../../../../../circle-bundle.md) of $\gamma^{\otimes2}$ over [Complex projective space](../../../../../complex-projective-space.md), where $\gamma\to\mathbb{CP}^n$ is the [tautological bundle](../../../../../tautological-bundle.md). Indeed, $S^{2n+1}/\{\pm1\}=\mathbb{RP}^{2n+1}$ maps to $S(\gamma^{\otimes2})$ by $[v]\mapsto v\otimes v$; in each fiber squaring identifies exactly $v$ and $-v$.

Use [Complex K-theory of complex projective space](../../../../../complex-k-theory-of-complex-projective-space.md) in the form

$$
R=K^0(\mathbb{CP}^n)=\mathbb Z[t]/(t^{n+1}),\qquad
 t=1-[\gamma],\qquad K^{-1}(\mathbb{CP}^n)=0.
$$

The [K-theory Euler class](../../../../../k-theory-euler-class.md) of $\gamma^{\otimes2}$ is $1-[\gamma]^{-2}$. Multiplying it by the unit $-[\gamma]^2$ gives $1-[\gamma]^2=2t-t^2$. The [K-theory Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](../../../../../k-theory-gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) therefore gives

$$
K^0(\mathbb{RP}^{2n+1})\cong R/(2t-t^2),\qquad
K^{-1}(\mathbb{RP}^{2n+1})\cong\ker((2t-t^2)\cdot:R\to R).
$$

Under the projection $q:\mathbb{RP}^{2n+1}\to\mathbb{CP}^n$, the [pullback vector bundle](../../../../../pullback-vector-bundle.md) $q^*\gamma$ is the [complexification of a real vector bundle](../../../../../complexification-of-a-real-vector-bundle.md) $L$ of the [tautological bundle](../../../../../tautological-bundle.md) of [Real projective space](../../../../../real-projective-space.md): the fiber map sends $[v,z]$ to $zv$. Thus $t$ pulls back to $1-[L]$. Put $\mu=[L]-1$. The relation $L^{\otimes2}\cong1$ gives $\mu^2=-2\mu$, and $t^{n+1}=0$ gives $2^n\mu=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{K^0(\mathbb{RP}^{2n+1})\cong
\mathbb Z[\mu]/(\mu^2+2\mu,\,2^n\mu),\qquad \mu=[L]-1.}
$$

For completeness, these relations give precisely the [abelian group](../../../../../abelian-group.md) $\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2^n$. In the basis $t,\ldots,t^n$, the image of multiplication by $2t-t^2$ is generated by $2t-t^2,2t^2-t^3,\ldots,2t^n$; its relation matrix is triangular with determinant $2^n$, and the relations express every $t^j$ as $2^{j-1}t$. Thus there is exactly one cyclic [torsion subgroup](../../../../../torsion-subgroup.md) of order $2^n$.

If $n\geq1$ and $a=a_0+a_1t+\cdots+a_nt^n$ is in the kernel, successive coefficients of $(2t-t^2)a$ give $2a_0=0$, $2a_1-a_0=0$, and so on, up to $2a_{n-1}-a_{n-2}=0$. Hence $a_0=\cdots=a_{n-1}=0$, with $a_n$ arbitrary. For $n=0$ the multiplier is zero on $R=\mathbb Z$. In all cases,

$$
\boxed{K^{-1}(\mathbb{RP}^{2n+1})\cong\mathbb Z\qquad(n\geq0).}
$$

Finally let $E=L^{\oplus k}$ with $k\geq1$, over $B=\mathbb{RP}^{2n+1}$. Since $L^*\cong L$, multiplicativity of the [K-theory Euler class](../../../../../k-theory-euler-class.md) gives

$$
e_K(E)=(1-[L])^k=(-\mu)^k=-2^{k-1}\mu.
$$

On $K^0(B)=\mathbb Z\oplus(\mathbb Z/2^n)\mu$, the image of multiplication by this [K-theory Euler class](../../../../../k-theory-euler-class.md) is the subgroup generated by $2^{k-1}\mu$: multiplication by an integer produces every such multiple, while multiplication by $\mu$ only adds multiples of $2^k\mu$. Therefore

$$
\operatorname{coker}(e_K(E):K^0(B)\to K^0(B))
\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2^{\min(n,k-1)}.
$$

Multiplication by $\mu$ is zero on $K^{-1}(B)\cong\mathbb Z$, since $2^n\mu=0$ and this [abelian group](../../../../../abelian-group.md) has no nonzero [torsion elements](../../../../../torsion-element.md). Thus the [K-theory Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](../../../../../k-theory-gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) gives a [short exact sequence](../../../../../short-exact-sequence.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2^{\min(n,k-1)}
\longrightarrow K^0(S(E))\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\longrightarrow0.
$$

It splits as an [exact sequence](../../../../../exact-sequence.md) of [abelian groups](../../../../../abelian-group.md) because its quotient is a [free abelian group](../../../../../free-abelian-group.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{K^0(S(L^{\oplus k}))\cong\mathbb Z^2\oplus\mathbb Z/2^{\min(n,k-1)}\qquad(n\geq0,\ k\geq1).}
$$

The convention $\mathbb Z/1=0$ covers $n=0$ and $k=1$. For example, when $k=1$ the [sphere bundle](../../../../../sphere-bundle.md) is $(S^{2n+1}\times S^1)/\{(v,z)\sim(-v,-z)\}$, a [mapping torus](../../../../../mapping-torus.md) of the antipodal map; its [Topological K-theory](../../../../../topological-k-theory.md) has no nonzero [torsion elements](../../../../../torsion-element.md), agreeing with the formula. If $k=0$ is allowed, the rank-zero [vector bundle](../../../../../vector-bundle.md) has an empty unit [sphere bundle](../../../../../sphere-bundle.md), so $K^0(S(E))=0$ instead.
