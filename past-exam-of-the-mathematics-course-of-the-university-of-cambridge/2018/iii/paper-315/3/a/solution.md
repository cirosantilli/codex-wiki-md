<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Four mechanisms that can produce [disequilibrium chemistry in an exoplanet atmosphere](../../../../../../disequilibrium-chemistry-in-an-exoplanet-atmosphere.md) are:

- **[Atmospheric photochemistry](../../../../../../atmospheric-photochemistry.md).** Stellar ultraviolet photons initiate reactions whose products need not follow local [thermochemical equilibrium](../../../../../../thermochemical-equilibrium.md). The [ozone layer](../../../../../../ozone-layer.md) on [Earth](../../../../../../earth.md) is a solar-system example. Calculations for [HD 189733 b](../../../../../../hd-189733-b.md) predict enhanced [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) and [acetylene](../../../../../../acetylene.md) from the processing of [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) and [ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md); these are model examples rather than assertions of an unambiguous detection.
- **Vertical transport and chemical quenching.** When the [eddy mixing time](../../../../../../eddy-mixing-time.md) is shorter than the [chemical relaxation time](../../../../../../chemical-relaxation-time.md), gas retains a deeper abundance above its [chemical quench level](../../../../../../chemical-quench-level.md). The excess [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) in [Jupiter](../../../../../../jupiter.md)'s cool atmosphere exemplifies [carbon monoxide–methane quenching](../../../../../../carbon-monoxide-methane-quenching.md). Models of [HD 189733 b](../../../../../../hd-189733-b.md) predict quenched [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) and [ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) abundances differing from their local [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) values. The enhancement or depletion depends on the underlying [atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](../../../../../../atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md).
- **[Horizontal chemical quenching](../../../../../../horizontal-chemical-quenching.md).** If the [atmospheric advection time](../../../../../../atmospheric-advection-time.md) is short, winds move chemically processed gas into regions with different irradiation or [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) faster than it can re-equilibrate. Transport of gas within [Earth](../../../../../../earth.md)'s [ozone layer](../../../../../../ozone-layer.md) moves material away from its local photochemical production regions. Models of [HD 209458 b](../../../../../../hd-209458-b.md) show that dayside [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md)-rich composition can persist into the cooler nightside instead of forming the local [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) amount of [methane](../../../../../../methane.md).
- **Condensation with sedimentation or rainout.** Finite-rate cloud formation can depart from phase equilibrium, while [atmospheric condensate rainout](../../../../../../atmospheric-condensate-rainout.md) removes elements from a layer and changes its gas composition. [Earth](../../../../../../earth.md)'s [water](../../../../../../water.md) [atmospheric cold trap](../../../../../../atmospheric-cold-trap.md) limits the supply of [water](../../../../../../water.md) to the stratosphere. In [HD 209458 b](../../../../../../hd-209458-b.md) models, titanium-bearing condensates can settle and suppress upper-atmospheric [titanium monoxide](../../../../../../titanium-monoxide.md). The remaining gas can still be in local [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) with its depleted inventory: rainout is an open-column effect, not necessarily a failure of equilibrium among all gas reactions.

The exoplanet transport and photochemical examples follow [kinetic atmosphere calculations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0063) and [models including horizontal transport](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201322895); the condensate example is examined in [cold-trap calculations](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.3995).
