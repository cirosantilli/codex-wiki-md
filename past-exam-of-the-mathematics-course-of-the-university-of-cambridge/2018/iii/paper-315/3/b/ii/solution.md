<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For solar elemental abundances, take a [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md)- and [helium](../../../../../../../helium.md)-dominated atmosphere with [atmospheric carbon-to-oxygen ratio](../../../../../../../atmospheric-carbon-to-oxygen-ratio.md) near $0.55$. The principal oxygen-bearing molecule is generally [water](../../../../../../../water.md); the carbon and nitrogen carriers depend on both [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md) and [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md).

At the cool $500\,\mathrm{K}$ observable upper layers, [methane](../../../../../../../methane.md) is the expected main carbon reservoir under [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md), with abundant [water](../../../../../../../water.md) and much less [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md). At the hotter layers approaching $1400\,\mathrm{K}$, [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) becomes the main carbon carrier and [water](../../../../../../../water.md) contains much of the [oxygen](../../../../../../../oxygen.md) not bound in it. The relevant [law of mass action](../../../../../../../law-of-mass-action.md) follows from

$$
\mathrm{CO}+3\mathrm{H}_2\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{CH}_4+\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O},\qquad
K_p(T)=\frac{(p_{\rm CH_4}/p^\circ)(p_{\rm H_2O}/p^\circ)}{(p_{\rm CO}/p^\circ)(p_{\rm H_2}/p^\circ)^3}.
$$

Lower [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md) favors the exothermic methane-forming direction, while increasing [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) favors the side with fewer molecules. This explains why the change of dominant carrier cannot be specified by [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md) alone.

Nitrogen is distributed between [molecular nitrogen](../../../../../../../molecular-nitrogen.md) and [ammonia](../../../../../../../ammonia.md). Cooling favors [ammonia](../../../../../../../ammonia.md), but low [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) favors [molecular nitrogen](../../../../../../../molecular-nitrogen.md); it is therefore unsafe to call [ammonia](../../../../../../../ammonia.md) dominant throughout the $500\,\mathrm{K}$ low-pressure region. The exact partition requires the [equilibrium constant](../../../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) for $\mathrm{N}_2+3\mathrm{H}_2\rightleftharpoons2\mathrm{NH}_3$.

[Carbon dioxide](../../../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) is usually a minor constituent at solar composition; [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) and [acetylene](../../../../../../../acetylene.md) are much less abundant than the principal carbon carriers in this oxygen-rich equilibrium case. Condensation can remove refractory species where a condensation curve is crossed. **The robust cool-atmosphere expectation is a [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md)–[helium](../../../../../../../helium.md) background with [methane](../../../../../../../methane.md) and [water](../../../../../../../water.md), changing toward [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) in hotter layers.** Precise mixing ratios require thermodynamic data and an explicitly specified elemental inventory after rainout.
