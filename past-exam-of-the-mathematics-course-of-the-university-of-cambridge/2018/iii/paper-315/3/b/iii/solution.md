<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Four possible molecular signatures of [disequilibrium chemistry in an exoplanet atmosphere](../../../../../../../disequilibrium-chemistry-in-an-exoplanet-atmosphere.md), measured through an [exoplanet transmission spectrum](../../../../../../../exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md) or [exoplanet emission spectrum](../../../../../../../exoplanet-emission-spectrum.md), are:

- **Excess [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) in the cool upper layers**, where local [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) would place most carbon in [methane](../../../../../../../methane.md). This can indicate [carbon monoxide–methane quenching](../../../../../../../carbon-monoxide-methane-quenching.md) from deeper hot gas.
- **Suppressed [methane](../../../../../../../methane.md) bands relative to the same cool equilibrium model**, consistent with transport preventing complete [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) conversion or with photochemical loss.
- **An [ammonia](../../../../../../../ammonia.md) abundance inconsistent with the local equilibrium nitrogen partition**, potentially recording [nitrogen–ammonia quenching](../../../../../../../nitrogen-ammonia-quenching.md). Its direction must be evaluated for the actual [atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](../../../../../../../atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md); a depleted value is possible when hotter, [molecular nitrogen](../../../../../../../molecular-nitrogen.md)-rich gas is transported upward.
- **Enhanced [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) or [acetylene](../../../../../../../acetylene.md) bands**, consistent with [atmospheric photochemistry](../../../../../../../atmospheric-photochemistry.md) acting on the transported carbon and nitrogen reservoirs.

The first two signatures can be consequences of the same process and are not independent evidence for two mechanisms. Changes in [atmospheric carbon-to-oxygen ratio](../../../../../../../atmospheric-carbon-to-oxygen-ratio.md), [atmospheric metallicity of a giant planet](../../../../../../../atmospheric-metallicity-of-a-giant-planet.md), [exoplanet cloud](../../../../../../../exoplanet-cloud.md) coverage, and the [atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](../../../../../../../atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md) can mimic abundance changes. A convincing inference compares several molecular bands with a chemically consistent equilibrium model rather than identifying one unusual band alone.
