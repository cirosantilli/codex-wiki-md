<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [geometric albedo](../../../../../../geometric-albedo.md) $A_g$ compares the planet's reflected brightness at full phase with that of a perfectly reflecting [Lambertian surface](../../../../../../lambertian-surface.md) in the form of a flat disc of the same projected area, illuminated by the same incident flux. The [Bond albedo](../../../../../../bond-albedo.md) $A_B$ is the fraction of total incident stellar power reflected in all directions and over all wavelengths. They need not be equal: the [planetary phase integral](../../../../../../planetary-phase-integral.md) and the incident spectral weighting connect them.

For a [blackbody](../../../../../../blackbody.md) star, the incident flux at orbital distance $a$ is

$$
S=\frac{L_\star}{4\pi a^2}=\sigma_{\rm SB}T_\star^4\left(\frac{R_\star}{a}\right)^2.
$$

Assume negligible intrinsic luminosity, thermal emissivity one, and efficient [day-night heat redistribution](../../../../../../day-night-heat-redistribution.md) giving uniform emission over the whole planet. The absorbed and emitted powers are

$$
\pi R_p^2S(1-A_B)=4\pi R_p^2\sigma_{\rm SB}T_e^4.
$$

Using the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives the [planetary equilibrium temperature](../../../../../../planetary-equilibrium-temperature.md)

$$
\boxed{T_e=T_\star\sqrt{\frac{R_\star}{2a}}(1-A_B)^{1/4}}.
$$

For a representative emitting area encoded by a [stellar flux redistribution factor](../../../../../../stellar-flux-redistribution-factor.md) $f$, the equivalent expression is

$$
T_e=T_\star\sqrt{R_\star/a}\,[f(1-A_B)]^{1/4},
$$

with $f=1/4$ for global uniform emission and $1/2$ for uniform dayside-only emission. The [Bond albedo](../../../../../../bond-albedo.md), rather than the [geometric albedo](../../../../../../geometric-albedo.md), belongs in the energy budget. Internal heat, wavelength-dependent [emissivity](../../../../../../emissivity.md) and imperfect redistribution require corresponding modifications.
