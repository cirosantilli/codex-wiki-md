<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For uniform composition, the [Schwarzschild criterion](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-criterion.md) gives stability against [convection](../../../../../../../convection.md) when

$$
\boxed{\nabla_{\rm rad}\equiv\frac{d\ln T}{d\ln P}<\nabla_{\rm ad}\equiv\left(\frac{\partial\ln T}{\partial\ln P}\right)_s}.
$$

A parcel displaced upward expands approximately adiabatically. If the ambient logarithmic [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md) gradient is shallower than the [adiabatic temperature gradient](../../../../../../../adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md), the parcel cools more than its surroundings, becomes denser, and sinks back. Equality is neutral stability; a steeper gradient drives buoyant growth.

For a diatomic [ideal gas](../../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) with active translational and rotational degrees of freedom, $c_p/c_v=7/5$ and $\nabla_{\rm ad}=2/7$. If the infrared [opacity](../../../../../../../opacity.md) is constant, $P\propto\tau$, so the profile's [radiative temperature gradient](../../../../../../../radiative-temperature-gradient.md) is

$$
\nabla_{\rm rad}=\frac{\tau}{4T^4}\frac{dT^4}{d\tau}.
$$

An [atmospheric thermal inversion](../../../../../../../inversion-meteorology.md) has negative $\nabla_{\rm rad}$ and is stable by this criterion. Composition gradients require the [Ledoux criterion](../../../../../../../ledoux-criterion.md); molecular dissociation and changing heat capacities can also change the numerical [adiabatic temperature gradient](../../../../../../../adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md).
