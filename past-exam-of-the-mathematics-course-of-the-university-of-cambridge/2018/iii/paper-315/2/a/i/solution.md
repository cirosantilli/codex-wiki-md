<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

This is a [semi-grey irradiated atmosphere](../../../../../../../semi-grey-irradiated-atmosphere.md) in [radiative equilibrium](../../../../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md). The quantities have these meanings:

- $T$ is the local atmospheric [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature.md).
- $T_{\rm int}$ is the [internal effective temperature of a planet](../../../../../../../internal-effective-temperature-of-a-planet.md), defined by the upward intrinsic flux $F_{\rm int}=\sigma_{\rm SB}T_{\rm int}^4$.
- $T_{\rm irr}$ is the [irradiation temperature](../../../../../../../irradiation-temperature.md), conventionally $T_\star\sqrt{R_\star/a}$, so $\sigma_{\rm SB}T_{\rm irr}^4$ is the incident substellar bolometric stellar flux.
- $f_r$ is the [stellar flux redistribution factor](../../../../../../../stellar-flux-redistribution-factor.md). With albedo treated separately, uniform global averaging gives $f_r=1/4$ and dayside averaging gives $1/2$. If reflected power is included, the absorbed factor is reduced accordingly; these choices must not be double-counted in $T_{\rm irr}$.
- $\tau$ is the downward thermal [optical depth](../../../../../../../optical-depth.md), measured from the top with mean infrared [opacity](../../../../../../../opacity.md) $\kappa_{\rm IR}$.
- $\gamma=\kappa_{\rm vis}/\kappa_{\rm IR}$ is the ratio of the mean stellar-band absorption [opacity](../../../../../../../opacity.md) to the mean thermal [opacity](../../../../../../../opacity.md), not the gas's adiabatic index.

The model assumes a static [plane-parallel atmosphere](../../../../../../../plane-parallel-atmosphere.md), separate grey stellar and thermal bands with constant [opacities](../../../../../../../opacity.md), [local thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../../local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) for thermal emission, negligible scattering, and transport by [radiative transfer](../../../../../../../radiative-transfer.md) rather than [convection](../../../../../../../convection.md). The [Eddington closure approximation](../../../../../../../eddington-closure-approximation.md) sets the thermal angular-moment ratio to $1/3$, and the upper boundary supplies the usual $2/3$ term. Treating the incoming radiation with representative direction cosine $1/\sqrt3$ gives its attenuation $e^{-\sqrt3\gamma\tau}$. Intrinsic flux enters from below, while the imposed external stellar flux is absorbed from above.

With constant gravity and [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), $\tau\simeq\kappa_{\rm IR}P/g$, allowing conversion to an [atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](../../../../../../../atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md). This last relation additionally assumes constant thermal [opacity](../../../../../../../opacity.md). The grey treatment describes the energy balance approximately; it does not resolve individual molecular absorption lines.
