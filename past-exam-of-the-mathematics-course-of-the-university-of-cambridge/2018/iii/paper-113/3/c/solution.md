<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [sheaf of nonzero rational functions on an irreducible variety](../../../../../../sheaf-of-nonzero-rational-functions-on-an-irreducible-variety.md) $\mathcal K_X^*$ is a [flasque sheaf](../../../../../../flasque-sheaf.md). Indeed, its sections on any nonempty open subset are $K^*$: irreducibility makes nonempty open subsets connected and makes any two of them meet, so locally constant rational-function values must agree. Restrictions between such opens are identities; restriction to the empty set is also surjective. The preliminary argument therefore gives

$$
H^i(X,\mathcal K_X^*)=0\qquad(i>0).
$$

Apply the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) to the [short exact sequence of sheaves](../../../../../../short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md) of multiplicative [abelian groups](../../../../../../abelian-group.md)

$$
1\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X^*\longrightarrow\mathcal K_X^*\longrightarrow\mathcal Q\longrightarrow1.
$$

Its relevant part is

$$
K^*\longrightarrow\Gamma(X,\mathcal Q)\longrightarrow H^1(X,\mathcal O_X^*)\longrightarrow H^1(X,\mathcal K_X^*)=0.
$$

Part (b) identifies the first map with the [principal divisor](../../../../../../principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) map $K^*\to\operatorname{Div}(X)$. Exactness therefore identifies its cokernel with $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X^*)$. Combining this with part (a) gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cl}(X)\cong\operatorname{Pic}(X)\cong H^1(X,\mathcal O_X^*).}
$$

The connecting map sends a divisor to the class of the unit ratios of its local rational representatives; these are precisely the transition data of the associated [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md), up to the usual choice of inverse transition convention.
