↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

If $K=\{\lambda_0\}$, the [projection-valued measure](../../../../../../../projection-valued-measure.md) satisfies $P(\{\lambda_0\})=P(K)=I$. Its integral representation therefore reduces to

$$
\boxed{T=\int_Kz\,dP(z)=\lambda_0P(K)=\lambda_0I.}
$$

Thus a [normal operator](../../../../../../../normal-operator.md) with a singleton spectrum is a scalar multiple of the [identity operator](../../../../../../../identity-operator.md). Normality is essential: a nonzero nilpotent matrix has spectrum $\{0\}$ but need not be zero.
