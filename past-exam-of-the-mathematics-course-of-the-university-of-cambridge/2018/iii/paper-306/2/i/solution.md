<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The real [canonical variables](../../../../../../canonical-variables.md) $x^m,p_m$ describe the center-of-mass position and total [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) of the [open string](../../../../../../open-string.md). The nonzero [string oscillators](../../../../../../string-oscillator.md) are complex [Fourier modes](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) with $\alpha_{-k}^m=(\alpha_k^m)^*$; the [Lagrange multipliers](../../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) obey $\lambda_{-j}=\lambda_j^*$. The [Virasoro constraints](../../../../../../virasoro-constraint.md) satisfy $L_{-j}=L_j^*$, so the multiplier term is real.

For each $k>0$, the oscillator kinetic term differs from the manifestly real expression

$$
\frac{i}{2k}\bigl(\dot\alpha_k\cdot\alpha_{-k}
-\alpha_k\cdot\dot\alpha_{-k}\bigr)
$$

by $\frac{i}{2k}\frac{d}{dt}(\alpha_k\cdot\alpha_{-k})$. Thus the written [action](../../../../../../action.md) is real up to a boundary term, which does not change its [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) or bulk dynamics. Adding the corresponding endpoint term makes reality exact.

The independent nonzero [Poisson brackets](../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) are

$$
\boxed{\{x^m,p_n\}=\delta^m_n,\qquad
\{\alpha_j^m,\alpha_k^n\}=-ij\,\eta^{mn}\delta_{j+k,0},\quad j,k\neq0.}
$$

All brackets between these independent center-of-mass and oscillator variables vanish. The dependent zero mode is $\alpha_0^m=p^m/\sqrt{\pi T}$, so, if it is used, $\{x^m,\alpha_0^n\}=\eta^{mn}/\sqrt{\pi T}$.

The [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) of the [Virasoro constraints](../../../../../../virasoro-constraint.md) are

$$
L_j=\frac12\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}\alpha_{j-k}\cdot\alpha_k,\qquad
L_0=\frac{p^2}{2\pi T}+\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\alpha_{-k}\cdot\alpha_k.
$$

Their [Poisson brackets](../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) are

$$
\boxed{\{L_j,L_k\}=-i(j-k)L_{j+k}.}
$$

They form the classical [Witt algebra](../../../../../../witt-algebra.md), with no [Virasoro central extension](../../../../../../virasoro-central-extension.md). In particular they are [first-class constraints](../../../../../../first-class-constraint.md), closing on the constraint surface, and generate the remaining [worldsheet diffeomorphisms](../../../../../../worldsheet-diffeomorphism.md) rather than independent physical excitations.
