<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the classically equivalent [Polyakov action](../../../../../../polyakov-action.md), fix [worldsheet diffeomorphisms](../../../../../../worldsheet-diffeomorphism.md) and [Weyl invariance](../../../../../../weyl-transformation.md), and perform a [Wick rotation](../../../../../../wick-rotation.md) of the [string worldsheet](../../../../../../worldsheet.md). In complex coordinates, the [worldsheet ghost action](../../../../../../worldsheet-ghost-action.md) for the [closed string](../../../../../../closed-string.md) is

$$
\boxed{S_{bc}=\frac1{2\pi}\int d^2z\,
\bigl(b_{zz}\bar\partial c^z+\bar b_{\bar z\bar z}\partial\bar c^{\bar z}\bigr).}
$$

The [worldsheet ghost fields](../../../../../../worldsheet-ghost-field.md) are anticommuting. Their [conformal weights](../../../../../../conformal-weight.md) are $b:(2,0)$, $c:(-1,0)$ and $\bar b:(0,2)$, $\bar c:(0,-1)$. The two chiral sectors are counted separately.

In either chiral sector, each embedding coordinate is a [free boson conformal field theory](../../../../../../free-boson-conformal-field-theory.md) with [central charge](../../../../../../central-charge.md) one. For the [bc system](../../../../../../bc-system.md), putting $J=2$ in $c_{bc}=-2(6J^2-6J+1)$ gives $c_{bc}=-26$. Cancellation of the [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](../../../../../../worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) requires

$$
\boxed{c_{\mathrm{tot}}=D-26=0,\qquad D=26.}
$$

The antiholomorphic sector gives the same equation, rather than doubling the required [critical dimension of string theory](../../../../../../critical-dimension-of-string-theory.md). This dimension count assumes flat target spacetime with just the embedding fields; more general internal [conformal field theories](../../../../../../conformal-field-theory.md) change the matter accounting.

For the [spinning string](../../../../../../spinning-string.md), the [worldsheet Majorana fermions](../../../../../../worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md) have [conformal weight](../../../../../../conformal-weight.md) $1/2$. At $J=1/2$, the complex anticommuting [bc system](../../../../../../bc-system.md) has $c=1$; a real [Majorana spinor](../../../../../../majorana-spinor.md) contributes half of this. Hence $D$ real [worldsheet Majorana fermions](../../../../../../worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md) contribute $D/2$ per chiral sector. [Ramond sector](../../../../../../ramond-sector.md) versus [Neveu–Schwarz sector](../../../../../../neveu-schwarz-sector.md) changes their mode numbers and vacuum energies, not this local [central charge](../../../../../../central-charge.md).

The additional local [worldsheet supersymmetry](../../../../../../worldsheet-supersymmetry.md) has a fermionic gauge parameter, so its [superconformal ghosts](../../../../../../superconformal-ghost.md) are commuting fields forming a [beta-gamma system](../../../../../../beta-gamma-system.md), with [conformal weights](../../../../../../conformal-weight.md) $\beta:3/2$ and $\gamma:-1/2$ in the holomorphic sector. Commuting statistics reverse the sign of the [bc system](../../../../../../bc-system.md) formula, giving

$$
c_{\beta\gamma}=2\left(6\left(\frac32\right)^2-6\left(\frac32\right)+1\right)=11.
$$

Together with the reparameterization [worldsheet ghost fields](../../../../../../worldsheet-ghost-field.md), their total [central charge](../../../../../../central-charge.md) is $-26+11=-15$. Thus

$$
\boxed{c_{\mathrm{tot}}=D+\frac D2-26+11=0,\qquad D=10.}
$$

These cancellations make the [string worldsheet](../../../../../../worldsheet.md) gauge symmetries compatible with quantization; in the [BRST symmetry](../../../../../../brst-symmetry.md) formulation they enter the nilpotence condition for the [BRST operator](../../../../../../brst-operator.md).
