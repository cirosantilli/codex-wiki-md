<h1 id="1/b/iv/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The distinction between the evolution equation and the constraint is essential. For strings alone, the exact equations reduce to

$$
\ddot a=0,\qquad\dot a^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}C_s-K.
$$

Linearizing the acceleration equation gives $\delta a=A+Bt$. At the exactly tuned static $C_s$, however, the full [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) requires $\dot a^2=0$, so $B=0$ for an admissible exact perturbation.

A small nonzero velocity $v$ can be admissible if its initial density also changes:

$$
C_s=C_E+\frac{3v^2}{8\pi G},\qquad
\delta\rho(t_E)=\frac{3v^2}{8\pi Ga_E^2},\qquad
\boxed{a(t)=a_E+v(t-t_E).}
$$

Arbitrarily small $v$ and the corresponding second-order density change therefore produce a secular departure. There is no [Lyapunov stability](../../../../../../../../lyapunov-stability.md) against all nearby constrained initial data for the [static closed cosmic-string universe](../../../../../../../../static-closed-cosmic-string-universe.md). Neutral radius shifts at fixed normalization and drift when the normalization changes are different perturbation classes.
