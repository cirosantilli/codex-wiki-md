<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a constant [equation-of-state parameter](../../../../../../../equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w=P/\rho$, the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) integrates to $\rho\propto a^{-3(1+w)}$. Comparing the given powers yields

$$
\boxed{w_s=-\frac13,\qquad w_w=-\frac23,\qquad P_s=-\frac{\rho_s}{3},\quad P_w=-\frac{2\rho_w}{3}.}
$$

This [constant-equation-of-state density scaling](../../../../../../../constant-equation-of-state-density-scaling.md) describes a [cosmic string network](../../../../../../../cosmic-string-network.md) and a [cosmological domain wall network](../../../../../../../cosmological-domain-wall-network.md). An intuitive check is that the energy in a fixed comoving cell grows in proportion to the total string length, $a$, or total wall area, $a^2$, while the physical cell volume grows as $a^3$. Thus the densities scale as $a^{-2}$ and $a^{-1}$ respectively. These effective equations of state use the noninteracting, statistically isotropic network assumptions of the problem.
