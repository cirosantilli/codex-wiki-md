<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md), $\sqrt{-g}=a^4$ and $g^{\mu\nu}=a^{-2}\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$. The [inflaton action in an expanding universe](../../../../../../inflaton-action-in-an-expanding-universe.md) becomes

$$
S=\int d\tau\,d^3x\left\{\frac{a^2}{2}(\phi')^2-\frac{a^2}{2}(\nabla\phi)^2-a^4V(\phi)\right\}.
$$

Vary $\phi$ with compact support, integrate the derivative terms by parts, and discard the boundary term. The [Euler-Lagrange field equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is

$$
(a^2\phi')'-a^2\nabla^2\phi+a^4V_{,\phi}=0.
$$

Dividing by $a^2$ proves

$$
\boxed{\phi''+2\frac{a'}a\phi'-\nabla^2\phi+a^2V_{,\phi}=0.}
$$

The factor two is the conformal-time form of [Hubble friction](../../../../../../hubble-friction.md). Using $d\tau=dt/a$ converts it to the usual [inflaton equation of motion](../../../../../../inflaton-equation-of-motion.md) $\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi-a^{-2}\nabla^2\phi+V_{,\phi}=0$, providing a sign and normalization check.
