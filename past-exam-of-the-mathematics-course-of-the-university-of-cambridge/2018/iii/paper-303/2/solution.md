<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Absorb $k_BT$ into the [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md), so the statistical weight is $e^{-F}$. Under the [Gaussian fixed point](../../../../../gaussian-fixed-point.md) rescaling, invariance of the [gradient energy](../../../../../gradient-energy.md) gives the [engineering dimension](../../../../../engineering-dimension.md)

$$
\boxed{[\phi]=\frac{d-2}{2}=\frac12\quad(d=3)}.
$$

The [engineering dimensions](../../../../../engineering-dimension.md) of $\phi^2,\phi^4,\phi^6$ are respectively $1,2,3$. Their coupling [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $d-[\phi^{2m}]$, hence $2,1,0$. **The quadratic and quartic interactions are [relevant operators](../../../../../relevant-operator.md); the sextic interaction is a [marginal operator](../../../../../marginal-operator.md) at this order.**

The three steps of the [momentum-shell renormalization group](../../../../../momentum-shell-renormalization-group.md) are:

- Split $\phi=\varphi+\eta$ into slow modes $|k|<\Lambda/b$ and fast modes $\Lambda/b<|k|<\Lambda$, and integrate over $\eta$.
- Rescale $x'=x/b$ to restore the [ultraviolet cutoff](../../../../../ultraviolet-cutoff.md) to $\Lambda$.
- Rescale the field as $\phi'(x')=b^{(d-2)/2}\varphi(bx')$ to normalize the [gradient energy](../../../../../gradient-energy.md).

Here $b=e^s>1$, so increasing $s$ means flowing toward longer distances. At leading order, the fast field has a [Gaussian distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) with covariance

$$
C_b(x-y)=\int_{\Lambda/b<|k|<\Lambda}\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{e^{ik\cdot(x-y)}}{k^2+\mu_0^2},\qquad I_b=C_b(0).
$$

This perturbative integration requires $k^2+\mu_0^2>0$ on the shell. By the [Wick theorem](../../../../../wick-s-theorem.md), odd fast-field moments vanish and $\langle\eta^4\rangle=3I_b^2$, $\langle\eta^6\rangle=15I_b^3$. Therefore the first term of the [cumulant expansion of a coarse-grained free energy](../../../../../cumulant-expansion-of-a-coarse-grained-free-energy.md) is

$$
\lambda_0\langle(\varphi+\eta)^6\rangle=\lambda_0\left(\varphi^6+15I_b\varphi^4+45I_b^2\varphi^2+15I_b^3\right).
$$

This is the [quartic interaction generated by a sextic interaction](../../../../../quartic-interaction-generated-by-a-sextic-interaction.md). Including the rescaling, the requested flow is

$$
\boxed{g(b)=15b\lambda_0\!\int_{\Lambda/b<|k|<\Lambda}\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{1}{k^2+\mu_0^2}+O(\lambda_0^2)}.
$$

In spherical coordinates, $I_b=(2\pi^2)^{-1}\int_{\Lambda/b}^{\Lambda}k^2/(k^2+\mu_0^2)\,dk$. In particular, for $\mu_0^2=0$,

$$
g(b)=\frac{15\lambda_0\Lambda}{2\pi^2}(b-1)+O(\lambda_0^2),\qquad \frac{dg}{ds}=g+\frac{15\Lambda}{2\pi^2}\lambda+\cdots.
$$

The same integration shifts the quadratic coefficient by $90\lambda_0 I_b^2$ before rescaling and adds an irrelevant constant to the [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). Thus setting the bare quartic coupling to zero does not place the theory on the tricritical [critical surface](../../../../../critical-surface.md).

To calculate the sextic [renormalization-group beta function](../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) to second order, let $V=\lambda_0\int d^3x\,(\varphi+\eta)^6$. The required [cumulant expansion](../../../../../cumulant-expansion.md) is

$$
F_{\mathrm{eff}}=F_0+\langle V\rangle-\frac12\left(\langle V^2\rangle-\langle V\rangle^2\right)+O(\lambda_0^3).
$$

The subtraction removes disconnected contributions. The term $20\lambda_0\varphi^3\eta^3$ at each vertex supplies the primitive sextic correction. The [Wick contractions](../../../../../wick-contraction.md) give

$$
\langle\eta(x)^3\eta(y)^3\rangle=6C_b(x-y)^3+9I_b^2C_b(x-y).
$$

The first term contains three propagators connecting the two vertices; its factor $6$ is the number of bijections between the three fields at each vertex. The second contains a local [tadpole diagram](../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md) at each vertex and one connecting propagator. The primitive contribution to the [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is therefore

$$
\Delta F_6=-1200\lambda_0^2\int d^3x\,d^3y\,\varphi(x)^3\varphi(y)^3C_b(x-y)^3.
$$

Expanding the slow fields about a common point gives a negative local sextic correction proportional to

$$
J_b=\int d^3z\,C_b(z)^3=\int\frac{d^3p\,d^3q}{(2\pi)^6}\frac{\chi_b(p)\chi_b(q)\chi_b(p+q)}{(p^2+\mu_0^2)(q^2+\mu_0^2)((p+q)^2+\mu_0^2)}\geq0,
$$

where $\chi_b$ restricts a momentum to the shell. This [connected Feynman diagram](../../../../../connected-feynman-diagram.md) has two independent loop momenta. Other [Wick contractions](../../../../../wick-contraction.md) also generate lower interactions and [tadpole diagrams](../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md); they must be included or subtracted consistently when fixing the tricritical [critical surface](../../../../../critical-surface.md).

To extract the logarithmic [tricritical sextic beta function](../../../../../tricritical-sextic-beta-function.md), use [normal ordering](../../../../../normal-ordering.md) to remove local tadpoles and tune the quadratic and quartic [relevant operators](../../../../../relevant-operator.md). The massless three-dimensional propagator is $C(z)=1/(4\pi|z|)$. The same three-line contraction over short separations $a<|z|<ba$ gives

$$
\int_{a<|z|<ba}d^3z\,C(z)^3=\frac{1}{16\pi^2}\log b.
$$

Its positive coefficient and the negative sign of the second [cumulant expansion](../../../../../cumulant-expansion.md) term establish the sign of the flow. Matching this logarithm to a renormalized coupling, or performing consistent iterated shell integration, gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\lambda}{ds}=-C\lambda^2+O(\lambda^3),\qquad C>0},\qquad \lambda(s)=\frac{\lambda(0)}{1+C\lambda(0)s}+\cdots.
$$

The precise normalization of $C$ is not needed. In a sharp momentum cutoff scheme, simply forcing all three internal momenta into one infinitesimal shell does not extract this two-loop logarithm: the shell restrictions remove its linear-in-$ds$ phase space. The finite-shell cumulant and the logarithmic renormalized calculation above are distinct stages of the computation. Field normalization corrections at order $\lambda^2$ enter this [renormalization-group beta function](../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) only at order $\lambda^3$.

**A positive, stabilizing sextic coupling is [marginally irrelevant](../../../../../marginally-irrelevant-operator.md) in three dimensions on the tricritical [critical surface](../../../../../critical-surface.md).** Reversing the convention for $s$ reverses the sign of the [renormalization-group beta function](../../../../../beta-function-physics.md). A negative sextic coupling without higher stabilizing powers makes the potential unbounded; moreover, the untuned bare choice $g=0$ generally flows away through its generated [relevant operator](../../../../../relevant-operator.md).
