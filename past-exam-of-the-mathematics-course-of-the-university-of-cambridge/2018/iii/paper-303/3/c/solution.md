<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Assume $g>0$ for stability. With equal quadratic coefficients and $\lambda=2g$, the internal [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) is the full [orthogonal group](../../../../../../orthogonal-group.md) $O(2)$. In two dimensions at positive [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md), with short-range interactions, the [Mermin-Wagner theorem](../../../../../../mermin-wagner-theorem.md) forbids [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) of this [continuous symmetry](../../../../../../continuous-symmetry.md). Thus $\langle\phi_1\rangle=\langle\phi_2\rangle=0$ in both phases in the infinite system; a nonzero average [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) does not distinguish them.

Instead measure the [correlation function](../../../../../../correlation-function.md)

$$
G(r)=\langle\boldsymbol\phi(x)\cdot\boldsymbol\phi(0)\rangle,\qquad r=|x|.
$$

**The low-temperature phase has [quasi-long-range order](../../../../../../quasi-long-range-order.md) and algebraic correlations; the high-temperature phase has a finite [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) and exponential correlations.** More precisely,

$$
\boxed{G(r)\sim r^{-\eta(T)}\ \text{below }T_{\mathrm{BKT}},\qquad G(r)\ \text{decays exponentially for }T>T_{\mathrm{BKT}}}.
$$

A finite [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) amplitude allows $\boldsymbol\phi\simeq v(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$. Its long-distance [Goldstone-mode effective free energy](../../../../../../goldstone-mode-effective-free-energy.md) is $F_\theta=(\rho_s/2)\int d^2x\,(\nabla\theta)^2$. Restoring the [Boltzmann constant](../../../../../../boltzmann-constant.md) and using the renormalized [phase stiffness](../../../../../../phase-stiffness.md), the [thermal phase fluctuations](../../../../../../thermal-phase-fluctuation.md) give

$$
\langle[\theta(x)-\theta(0)]^2\rangle\simeq\frac{k_BT}{\pi\rho_s}\log(r/a),\qquad \eta(T)=\frac{k_BT}{2\pi\rho_s}.
$$

The [Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition](../../../../../../berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-transition.md) separates [vortex-antivortex binding](../../../../../../vortex-antivortex-binding.md) from unbound [vortex](../../../../../../phase-vortex.md) defects. At the boundary, the [universal stiffness jump](../../../../../../universal-stiffness-jump.md) gives $\rho_s(T_{\mathrm{BKT}}^-)=2k_BT_{\mathrm{BKT}}/\pi$ and $\eta(T_{\mathrm{BKT}})=1/4$. The low-temperature [phase stiffness](../../../../../../phase-stiffness.md) is nonzero; it vanishes at long distances in the disordered phase. The exact transition location is fluctuation dependent and cannot be obtained just by setting the bare $\mu^2$ to zero.
