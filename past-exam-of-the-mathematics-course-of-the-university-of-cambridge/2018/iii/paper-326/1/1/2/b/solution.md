↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On $\mathcal U=\mathbb R$ with its usual [topology](../../../../../../../../topology-split.md), the following examples isolate the three failures.

A nonproper [functional](../../../../../../../../functional.md) is $E(x)=+\infty$ for every $x$. Its [effective domain](../../../../../../../../effective-domain.md) is empty, so **it has no finite-valued [global minimizer](../../../../../../../../global-minimizer.md)**. It nevertheless has [coercivity](../../../../../../../../coercive-function.md) and is [sequentially lower semicontinuous](../../../../../../../../sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md). If a minimizer is instead defined only by $E(x)=\inf E$ without requiring finiteness, every point formally minimizes this function; under that convention the requested nonproper counterexample is impossible with the given codomain.

A noncoercive [functional](../../../../../../../../functional.md) is $E(x)=e^x$. It is a [proper extended-real function](../../../../../../../../proper-extended-real-function.md) and is [continuous](../../../../../../../../continuous-function.md), but its [infimum](../../../../../../../../infimum.md) zero is approached as $x\to-\infty$ and is never attained. Hence **it has no [global minimizer](../../../../../../../../global-minimizer.md)**.

A failure of [sequential lower semicontinuity](../../../../../../../../sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md) is

$$
E(x)=\begin{cases}x^2,&x\neq0,\\1,&x=0.\end{cases}
$$

It is a [proper extended-real function](../../../../../../../../proper-extended-real-function.md) with [coercivity](../../../../../../../../coercive-function.md), but $E(1/n)\to0<E(0)$. Its [infimum](../../../../../../../../infimum.md) is zero, while every function value is positive. Thus **it has no [global minimizer](../../../../../../../../global-minimizer.md)**.
