<h1 id="3/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [functional](../../../../../../../functional.md) $E$ on a real [Banach space](../../../../../../../banach-space-split.md), its [convex conjugate](../../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) is defined on the [dual space](../../../../../../../dual-space.md) by

$$
\boxed{E^*(p)=\sup_{u\in\mathcal U}\{\langle p,u\rangle-E(u)\},\qquad p\in\mathcal U^*.}
$$

Here $\langle p,u\rangle$ is the [dual pairing](../../../../../../../dual-pairing.md). For complex spaces, use its real part, equivalently regard the space as real for [convex analysis](../../../../../../../convex-analysis.md). The [convex conjugate](../../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) is a [convex function](../../../../../../../convex-function.md), being the supremum of [affine functions](../../../../../../../affine-function.md) of $p$, even when $E$ itself is not [convex](../../../../../../../convex-function.md).
