↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [orthonormal wavelet](../../../../../../orthonormal-wavelet.md) is a function whose integer translates and dyadic dilates

$$
\psi_{j,k}(x)=2^{j/2}\psi(2^jx-k),\qquad j,k\in\mathbb Z,
$$

form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of the [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) $L^2(\mathbb R)$. Thus their pairwise [inner products](../../../../../../inner-product.md) are $\delta_{j,j'}\delta_{k,k'}$, and their closed linear span is the whole space. Equivalently, every $f\in L^2(\mathbb R)$ has the [norm](../../../../../../norm.md)-convergent expansion

$$
\boxed{f=\sum_{j,k}\langle f,\psi_{j,k}\rangle\psi_{j,k},\qquad \|f\|_2^2=\sum_{j,k}|\langle f,\psi_{j,k}\rangle|^2.}
$$

The factor $2^{j/2}$ preserves the $L^2$ [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) under dilation.
