↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [total variation seminorm on a domain](../../../../../../total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md) is

$$
\boxed{|Df|(\Omega)=\sup\left\{\int_\Omega f\,\operatorname{div}\zeta\,dx:\zeta\in C_c^1(\Omega;\mathbb R^2),\ \sup_x|\zeta(x)|_2\le1\right\}.}
$$

The class is symmetric under negation, so no additional absolute value is needed around the integral. The [bounded-variation space](../../../../../../function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) is

$$
BV(\Omega)=\{f\in L^1(\Omega):|Df|(\Omega)<\infty\},\qquad
\|f\|_{BV}=\|f\|_1+|Df|(\Omega).
$$

Equivalently, the [distributional derivative](../../../../../../distributional-derivative.md) is a finite vector-valued [Radon measure](../../../../../../radon-measure.md), and the displayed quantity is its total mass in variation. For $f\in W^{1,1}(\Omega)$, it equals $\int_\Omega|\nabla f|_2\,dx$. Thus jumps are allowed, with their interface contributions measured by variation.
