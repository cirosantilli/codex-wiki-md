<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [sigmoid function](../../../../../../sigmoid-function.md) is strictly increasing. Therefore the risky region is $\widehat\eta(x)\geq\log\{p/(1-p)\}$. Its [decision boundary](../../../../../../decision-boundary.md) is the [hyperplane](../../../../../../hyperplane.md)

$$
\boxed{-1.322701-0.005916\,\mathrm{income}-0.008580\,\mathrm{age}+0.012981\,\mathrm{amount}=\log\frac{p}{1-p}.}
$$

Points on or above this score threshold are assigned to the risky class. For $p=1/2$, the right side is zero.
