<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take $A=\{2,4,6,\ldots\}$ and

$$
O=[\varnothing,A]=[A]^\omega.
$$

This is a nonempty basic [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) in the [Ellentuck topology](../../../../../../ellentuck-topology.md). It is a [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md) in the [ordinary topology on infinite subsets](../../../../../../ordinary-topology-on-infinite-subsets.md): an infinite set outside $O$ contains an odd integer, and its finite [initial segment](../../../../../../initial-segment.md) through that integer gives an ordinary cylinder disjoint from $O$.

Its ordinary [interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md) is empty. In any nonempty ordinary cylinder $[s]$, choose an odd integer $b>\max s$ and continue $s\cup\{b\}$ by any infinite increasing tail. The resulting point belongs to $[s]$ and lies outside $O$. Indeed the smaller cylinder $[s\cup\{b\}]$ is disjoint from $O$. Since $O$ is ordinary-closed with empty ordinary [interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md), it is a [nowhere dense set](../../../../../../nowhere-dense-set.md) for $\tau$. Hence

$$
\boxed{[2\mathbb N]^\omega\text{ is nonempty and star-open, but }\tau\text{-nowhere-dense}.}
$$
