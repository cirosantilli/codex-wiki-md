<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $E=\bigcup_{j\geq1}B_{2j}$. For every even index $2j$, $B_{2j}\subseteq E$ and $B_{2j}\in U_{2j}$ imply $E\in U_{2j}$. For every odd index $2j-1$, disjointness gives $B_{2j-1}\subseteq E^c$, so $E^c\in U_{2j-1}$ and $E\notin U_{2j-1}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{E\in U_n\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad n\text{ is even}.}
$$

The limit [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) $U$ contains either $E$ or $E^c$. Whichever it contains, the corresponding basic [clopen set](../../../../../../clopen-set.md) $\widehat E$ or $\widehat{E^c}$ is a [neighbourhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $U$, but infinitely many sequence terms lie outside it. This contradicts convergence. Therefore pairwise distinct [ultrafilters](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) cannot form a [convergent sequence](../../../../../../convergent-sequence.md) in $\beta\mathbb N$, the key step in [no nontrivial convergent sequences in the Stone-Čech compactification](../../../../../../no-nontrivial-convergent-sequences-in-the-stone-cech-compactification.md).
