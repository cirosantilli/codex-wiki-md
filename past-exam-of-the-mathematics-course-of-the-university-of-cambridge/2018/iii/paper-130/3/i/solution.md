↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The ultrafilter space.** An [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) $U$ on $\mathbb N$ is a proper [filter on a set](../../../../../../filter-set-theory.md): it is upward closed, closed under finite intersections, excludes $\varnothing$, and contains exactly one of $A,A^c$ for each $A\subseteq\mathbb N$. The [Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers](../../../../../../stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers.md) is the set of these [ultrafilters](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md), with basic sets

$$
\widehat A=\{U:A\in U\}.
$$

They form a basis since $\widehat A\cap\widehat B=\widehat{A\cap B}$ and $\widehat{\mathbb N}=\beta\mathbb N$. Moreover $(\widehat A)^c=\widehat{A^c}$, so each is a [clopen set](../../../../../../clopen-set.md). The [principal ultrafilter](../../../../../../principal-ultrafilter.md) at $n$ embeds $\mathbb N$ in this space; every nonempty $\widehat A$ contains such a point, so the embedded copy is [dense](../../../../../../dense-set.md).

Distinct [ultrafilters](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) $U,V$ have some $A\in U$ with $A\notin V$. Then $A^c\in V$, and $\widehat A,\widehat{A^c}$ are disjoint [open sets](../../../../../../open-set.md) separating them. Thus the space is [Hausdorff](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md).

For [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md), any [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) cover can be refined to a cover by basic sets. If $\{\widehat{A_i}\}$ has no finite subcover, the sets $A_i^c$ have the [finite intersection property](../../../../../../finite-intersection-property.md): otherwise finitely many $A_i$ cover $\mathbb N$, and their basic sets cover every [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md). Extend the proper [filter on a set](../../../../../../filter-set-theory.md) generated by these complements to an [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) $U$. This extension follows from [Zorn lemma](../../../../../../zorn-s-lemma.md): unions of chains of proper filters remain proper, and a maximal proper filter decides every $A$ versus $A^c$. Indeed, if adding $A$ made the filter improper, some existing filter member would be disjoint from $A$, forcing $A^c$ into the filter. This $U$ contains every $A_i^c$ and misses every $\widehat{A_i}$, a contradiction. Hence

$$
\boxed{\beta\mathbb N\text{ is compact and Hausdorff}.}
$$

This proves the [compact Hausdorff topology on ultrafilters](../../../../../../compact-hausdorff-topology-on-ultrafilters.md) directly.

**An idempotent.** The [addition on the Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers](../../../../../../addition-on-the-stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers.md) can be defined by

$$
A\in U+V\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\{n:A-n\in V\}\in U,\qquad A-n=\{k:n+k\in A\}.
$$

Associativity is supplied. For fixed $V$, the inverse image of $\widehat A$ under $U\mapsto U+V$ is $\widehat{\{n:A-n\in V\}}$, so this translation is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) directly from the definition. This makes a [left-topological semigroup](../../../../../../left-topological-semigroup.md) in the convention used here.

We prove the [Ellis–Numakura lemma](../../../../../../ellis-numakura-lemma.md) in this case. Among nonempty compact [subsemigroups](../../../../../../subsemigroup.md) choose one, $K$, minimal by inclusion. It exists by [Zorn lemma](../../../../../../zorn-s-lemma.md): a descending chain has nonempty intersection by [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md), and that intersection is again a compact [subsemigroup](../../../../../../subsemigroup.md). For $a\in K$, the set $K+a$ is a nonempty compact [subsemigroup](../../../../../../subsemigroup.md) contained in $K$. [Compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) follows from [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md); closure under addition follows from

$$
(x+a)+(y+a)=(x+a+y)+a\in K+a.
$$

Minimality gives $K+a=K$. Hence there is $b\in K$ with $b+a=a$. The set

$$
L=\{x\in K:x+a=a\}
$$

is nonempty, compact, and a [subsemigroup](../../../../../../subsemigroup.md): if $x,y\in L$, then $(x+y)+a=x+(y+a)=x+a=a$. Thus minimality gives $L=K$, and in particular $a+a=a$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{there is an idempotent ultrafilter in }\beta\mathbb N.}
$$

This is an [idempotent ultrafilter on the natural numbers](../../../../../../idempotent-ultrafilter-on-the-natural-numbers.md).

**Isolating the sequence terms.** Now use the hypothetical [convergent sequence](../../../../../../convergent-sequence.md) of distinct [ultrafilters](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md). For each $n$, choose $C_n\in U_n$ with $C_n^c\in U$, possible since $U_n\ne U$. Convergence implies $C_n^c\in U_m$ for all sufficiently large $m$. Thus only finitely many indices $m\ne n$ have $C_n\in U_m$. Call this finite set $E_n$.

For each $m\in E_n$, choose $D_{n,m}\in U_n$ with $D_{n,m}\notin U_m$, using distinctness. Set

$$
A_n=C_n\cap\bigcap_{m\in E_n}D_{n,m}.
$$

Finite-intersection closure puts $A_n$ in $U_n$. For $m\ne n$, either $C_n\notin U_m$ or one of the chosen $D_{n,m}$ is absent from $U_m$; upward closure then prevents $A_n$ from belonging to $U_m$. Hence

$$
\boxed{A_n\in U_m\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad m=n.}
$$
