↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Linearity of expectation gives $\mathbb E_\mu[\widehat\mu]=(\alpha_1+\alpha_2)\mu$. Thus a [linear unbiased estimator](../../../../../../linear-unbiased-estimator.md) for every value of the [distance modulus](../../../../../../distance-modulus.md) satisfies $\boxed{\alpha_1+\alpha_2=1}$. Requiring unbiasedness at the single value $\mu=0$ would not impose this constraint; an unbiased estimator must work throughout the parameter space.
