<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Equal [variances](../../../../../../variance-split.md) make every weight $1/N$, so the [inverse-variance weighted mean](../../../../../../inverse-variance-weighted-mean.md) reduces to the [arithmetic mean](../../../../../../arithmetic-mean.md). Independence gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\mu)=\frac{\sigma^2}{N},\qquad\operatorname{SD}(\widehat\mu)=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt N}}.
$$

Thus the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) scales as $N^{-1}$, whereas the uncertainty expressed as a [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) scales as $N^{-1/2}$.
