↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\mathcal D=(\{\widehat m_s\},\{z_s\})$. [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) and the stated prior factorization give the [posterior density](../../../../../../posterior-density.md)

$$
\boxed{p(M_0,v,H_0,w,\Omega_M\mid\mathcal D)\propto L(M_0,v,H_0,w,\Omega_M)\,p(M_0)p(v)p(H_0)p(w,\Omega_M)}.
$$

The normalizing constant, when finite and positive, is the integral of this product over its admissible parameter domain. An [improper prior](../../../../../../improper-prior.md) requires checking [posterior propriety](../../../../../../posterior-propriety.md) before interpreting this expression as a probability density.
