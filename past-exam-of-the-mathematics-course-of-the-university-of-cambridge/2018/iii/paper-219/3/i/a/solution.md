$\boxed{(a)\longleftrightarrow P2}$. The [exponential covariance function](../../../../../../../exponential-covariance-function.md) gives a stationary [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](../../../../../../../ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md). At a small lag $h$,

$$
\mathbb E[(f(t+h)-f(t))^2]=2A^2(1-e^{-|h|/\tau})\sim\frac{2A^2}{\tau}|h|.
$$

The [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) of a difference quotient therefore diverges as $h\to0$, so the process has no mean-square derivative. Its continuous version has rough, nondifferentiable paths, matching the jagged P2 trace in the original PDF.
