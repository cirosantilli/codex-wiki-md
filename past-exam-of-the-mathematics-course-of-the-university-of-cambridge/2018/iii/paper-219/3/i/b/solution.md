$\boxed{(b)\longleftrightarrow P3}$. This is a [Gaussian kernel](../../../../../../../gaussian-kernel.md) [covariance](../../../../../../../covariance.md). Near zero, $2A^2(1-e^{-h^2/\tau^2})\sim2A^2h^2/\tau^2$, consistent with a finite mean-square derivative [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md). The kernel has derivatives of every order, and the resulting [Gaussian process](../../../../../../../gaussian-process.md) admits a smooth version. P3 is smooth but does not repeat, unlike P1.
