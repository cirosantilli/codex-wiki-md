<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a neutrally buoyant horizontal [turbulent round jet](../../../../../../turbulent-round-jet.md), a segment's horizontal [momentum flux](../../../../../../momentum-flux.md) changes only if an external horizontal [force](../../../../../../force.md) or ambient [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) input exists. Neither is present, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{dM_x}{ds}=0,\qquad M_x=M_0\quad(F_0=0,\ \theta_0=0)}.
$$

Positive [buoyancy](../../../../../../buoyancy.md) adds a vertical [force](../../../../../../force.md), not a horizontal one. Therefore $dM_x/ds=0$ remains true for an [inclined forced plume](../../../../../../inclined-forced-plume.md) even when $F_0\ne0$; in general $M_x=M_0\cos\theta_0$.

The net upward [force](../../../../../../force.md) on a segment is $g(\rho_0-\rho)\pi b^2ds$. Since $F=g(\rho_0-\rho)\pi b^2V$ and $V=|\mathbf M|/Q$, vertical [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) balance gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{dM_z}{ds}=\frac FV=\frac{FQ}{|\mathbf M|}}.
$$

In [kinematic plume fluxes](../../../../../../kinematic-plume-fluxes.md), the equations are $m_x'=0$, $m_z'=fq/m$, where $m=(m_x^2+m_z^2)^{1/2}$. The centreline geometry satisfies

$$
\frac{dx}{ds}=\cos\theta=\frac{m_x}{m},\qquad
\frac{dz}{ds}=\sin\theta=\frac{m_z}{m}.
$$

For $F_0>0$, $Q>0$ beyond the source, and an initially nonvertical [turbulent round jet](../../../../../../turbulent-round-jet.md) pointing right, $M_z$ increases and the centreline turns toward the vertical. The magnitude of [momentum flux](../../../../../../momentum-flux.md) is not conserved once the tangent has a vertical component.
