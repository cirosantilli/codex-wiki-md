The [Lévy continuity theorem](../../../../../../levy-continuity-theorem.md) states that if [characteristic functions](../../../../../../characteristic-function.md) $\varphi_n$ of [probability distributions](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md) $\mu_n$ converge pointwise on $\mathbb R$ to $\varphi$, and $\varphi$ is continuous at zero, then $\varphi$ is the [characteristic function](../../../../../../characteristic-function.md) of a unique [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md) $\mu$ and

$$
\boxed{\mu_n\Rightarrow\mu.}
$$

Here the arrow denotes [weak convergence of probability measures](../../../../../../weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md), equivalently [convergence in distribution](../../../../../../convergence-in-distribution.md) of associated [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md). Conversely, [weak convergence of probability measures](../../../../../../weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) implies pointwise convergence of their [characteristic functions](../../../../../../characteristic-function.md) to the [characteristic function](../../../../../../characteristic-function.md) of the limit. The continuity-at-zero condition is essential to ensure that the pointwise limit is a [characteristic function](../../../../../../characteristic-function.md).
