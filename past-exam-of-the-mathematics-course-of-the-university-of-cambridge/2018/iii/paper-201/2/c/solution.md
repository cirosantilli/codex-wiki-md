<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Pointwise, an integer-valued nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) has the representation

$$
T=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathbf1_{\{T>n\}}:
$$

when $T=k$, exactly the terms $n=0,\ldots,k-1$ equal one. Applying the [Tonelli theorem](../../../../../../tonelli-theorem.md), or the [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) to the finite sums of [indicator random variables](../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md), gives the [tail-sum formula for expectation](../../../../../../tail-sum-formula-for-expectation.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbb ET=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathbb P(T>n).}
$$

Both sides may be infinite; no [integrability](../../../../../../integrable-random-variable.md) assumption is necessary.
