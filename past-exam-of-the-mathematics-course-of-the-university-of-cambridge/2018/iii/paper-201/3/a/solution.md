The [tail sigma-algebra](../../../../../../tail-sigma-algebra.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T=\bigcap_{N\geq1}\sigma(X_N,X_{N+1},\ldots).}
$$

Its [events](../../../../../../event.md) depend only on arbitrarily late coordinates. The [Kolmogorov zero-one law](../../../../../../kolmogorov-s-zero-one-law.md) states that for a sequence of [independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), every [tail event](../../../../../../tail-event.md) has probability zero or one:

$$
\boxed{A\in\mathcal T\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathbb P(A)\in\{0,1\}.}
$$

Identical [probability distributions](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md) and [integrability](../../../../../../integrable-random-variable.md) are not required for the [Kolmogorov zero-one law](../../../../../../kolmogorov-s-zero-one-law.md).
