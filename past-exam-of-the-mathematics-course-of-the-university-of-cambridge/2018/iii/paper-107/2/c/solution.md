<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The cone condition implies $u(\lambda x)=\lambda u(x)$, so $Du(\lambda x)=Du(x)$. The [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is homogeneous of degree zero, and $v=|Du|^2$ attains its global maximum on the compact unit sphere. For $n\geq2$ the punctured space is connected. Part (b) gives $\mathcal Lv\geq0$ for a locally [uniformly elliptic operator](../../../../../../uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) with no zeroth-order term. The [strong maximum principle for elliptic operators](../../../../../../strong-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) makes $v$ constant. Hence

$$
0=\mathcal Lv=2\sum_k a^{ij}u_{ki}u_{kj}.
$$

Positive definiteness gives $D^2u=0$. Thus $u=a\cdot x+b$, and degree-one homogeneity forces $b=0$: $\boxed{u(x)=a\cdot x}$. This proves that a [minimal graphical cone is a plane](../../../../../../minimal-graphical-cone-is-a-plane.md). Strictly, the graph restricted to $x\ne0$ is the plane with its origin omitted; its closure is the full plane through the origin.
