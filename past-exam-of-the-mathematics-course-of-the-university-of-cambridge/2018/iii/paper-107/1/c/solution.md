<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Shift $u$ by a constant and, if necessary, change sign to obtain an entire nonnegative [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) $v$. The [Harnack inequality for harmonic functions](../../../../../../harnack-inequality-for-harmonic-functions.md) on arbitrarily large balls centred at $x_0$ gives $v(x)\leq3^nv(x_0)$ for every $x$. Thus $v$ is globally bounded. The [interior derivative estimate for a harmonic function](../../../../../../interior-derivative-estimate-for-a-harmonic-function.md) now gives $|Dv(x)|\leq C\|v\|_\infty/R$ on balls of arbitrarily large radius $R$. Letting $R\to\infty$ proves $Dv=0$, hence $u$ is constant. This proves the one-sided [harmonic Liouville theorem](../../../../../../harmonic-liouville-theorem.md) in every dimension.
