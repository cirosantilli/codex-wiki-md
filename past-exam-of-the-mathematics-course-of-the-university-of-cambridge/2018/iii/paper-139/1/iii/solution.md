<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose [very ample divisors](../../../../../../very-ample-divisor.md) $H_2$ and $H_1\sim D+H_2$, with defining sections avoiding the [associated points](../../../../../../associated-point-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md) of $X$. Such a choice is possible after taking a sufficiently high ample twist. Put $R_m=mD-H_1=(m-1)D-H_2$. The two indicated [short exact sequences of sheaves](../../../../../../short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md) and their [long exact sequences in sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) imply

$$
h^j(X,mD)\leq h^j(X,(m-1)D)+h^j(H_1,mD)+h^{j-1}(H_2,(m-1)D).
$$

We abbreviate $h^i(Z,\mathcal O_Z(L))$ by $h^i(Z,L)$. Each $H_a$ has dimension $n-1$ and the restricted divisor is [nef](../../../../../../nef-line-bundle.md). For positive degrees below $n-1$, the assumed induction estimate bounds the error terms by $O(m^{n-2})$. The displayed hypothesis omits top-degree cohomology; use [top cohomology boundedness for nef twists](../../../../../../top-cohomology-boundedness-for-nef-twists.md) for that degree, and [Grothendieck vanishing](../../../../../../grothendieck-vanishing.md) above it. These give the same error bound. The auxiliary top-degree result follows from [Fujita vanishing](../../../../../../fujita-vanishing.md) by cutting a [coherent sheaf](../../../../../../coherent-sheaf.md) with an ample divisor, as proved in [cohomology growth for nef twists](../../../../../../cohomology-growth-for-nef-twists.md).

For $n\geq2$, summing the inequality over successive $m$ gives $h^j(X,mD)=O(m^{n-1})$ for every $j>1$. For $n\leq1$, all such groups vanish by [Grothendieck vanishing](../../../../../../grothendieck-vanishing.md).
