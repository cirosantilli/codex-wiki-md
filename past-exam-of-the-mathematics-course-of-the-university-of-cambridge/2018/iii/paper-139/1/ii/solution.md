<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Nakai–Moishezon criterion](../../../../../../nakai-moishezon-criterion.md) says that a [Cartier divisor](../../../../../../cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$ on a [projective scheme](../../../../../../projective-scheme.md) is [ample](../../../../../../ample-line-bundle.md) exactly when

$$
D^{\dim Y}\cdot Y>0
$$

for every positive-dimensional integral closed [subvariety](../../../../../../closed-subvariety.md) $Y$. [Kleiman's criterion](../../../../../../kleiman-s-criterion.md) says that the [ample cone](../../../../../../ample-cone.md) is the interior of the [nef cone](../../../../../../nef-cone.md); equivalently, the [numerical class](../../../../../../real-numerical-divisor-classes.md) of $D$ is ample exactly when it is strictly positive on every nonzero element of the [closed cone of curves](../../../../../../closed-cone-of-curves.md) $\overline{\operatorname{NE}}(X)$. Positivity merely on individual curves is insufficient: the closure of the cone is essential. A [nef divisor](../../../../../../nef-line-bundle.md) has nonnegative [intersection number](../../../../../../intersection-number-of-a-cartier-divisor-with-a-curve.md) with every integral curve, and its restriction to every closed subscheme is nef.
