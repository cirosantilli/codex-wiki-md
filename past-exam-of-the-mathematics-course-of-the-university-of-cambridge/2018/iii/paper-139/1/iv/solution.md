<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

By [asymptotic Riemann–Roch](../../../../../../asymptotic-riemann-roch.md) and the preceding bounds for higher [sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../sheaf-cohomology.md),

$$
\boxed{h^0(X,mD)-h^1(X,mD)=\frac{D^n}{n!}m^n+O(m^{n-1}).}
$$

A [nef divisor](../../../../../../nef-line-bundle.md) satisfies $D^n\geq0$: for a fixed [ample divisor](../../../../../../ample-cartier-divisor.md) $A$, the divisors $D+\varepsilon A$ are ample for positive rational $\varepsilon$, and continuity of the [intersection product](../../../../../../intersection-product-of-cartier-divisors.md) gives the inequality as $\varepsilon\to0$. If $h^0(X,mD)=0$ eventually, the left side is nonpositive. Dividing by $m^n$ forces $D^n=0$, since $h^1\geq0$ and $D^n\geq0$. The same identity then gives $h^1(X,mD)=O(m^{n-1})$.
