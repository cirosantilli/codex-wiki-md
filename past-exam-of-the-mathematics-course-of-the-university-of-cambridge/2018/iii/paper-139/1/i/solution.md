<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [projective scheme](../../../../../../projective-scheme.md) $X$ of [dimension of a scheme](../../../../../../dimension-of-a-scheme.md) $n$ and a [Cartier divisor](../../../../../../cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$, [asymptotic Riemann–Roch](../../../../../../asymptotic-riemann-roch.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\chi(X,\mathcal O_X(mD))=\frac{D^n}{n!}m^n+O(m^{n-1}).}
$$

Here [Euler characteristic of a coherent sheaf](../../../../../../euler-characteristic-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md) means $\chi=\sum_{i=0}^n(-1)^ih^i$, and $D^n$ is the degree of the top [intersection product](../../../../../../intersection-product-of-cartier-divisors.md) with the fundamental cycle of $X$, including its component multiplicities. No [ampleness](../../../../../../ample-cartier-divisor.md) assumption on $D$ is needed.
