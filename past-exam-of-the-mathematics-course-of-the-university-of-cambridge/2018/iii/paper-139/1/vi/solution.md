<h1 id="1/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The divisor is understood to be on $X$; the printed “on $D$” is a typo. Combining [asymptotic Riemann–Roch](../../../../../../asymptotic-riemann-roch.md) with [cohomology growth for nef twists](../../../../../../cohomology-growth-for-nef-twists.md) gives

$$
h^0(X,\mathcal O_X(mD))=\frac{D^n}{n!}m^n+O(m^{n-1}),\qquad
\boxed{\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{h^0(X,\mathcal O_X(mD))}{m^n}=\frac{D^n}{n!}.}
$$

Since a [nef divisor](../../../../../../nef-line-bundle.md) has nonnegative top [self-intersection number](../../../../../../self-intersection-number.md), this [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) is positive exactly when $D^n>0$. This is the [volume of a nef divisor](../../../../../../volume-of-a-nef-divisor.md) criterion.
