<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A connected integral [projective variety](../../../../../../projective-variety.md) has only constant regular functions, so $h^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=1$. By [Serre duality](../../../../../../serre-duality.md) and $K_X\sim0$, $h^2(X,\mathcal O_X)=h^0(X,\mathcal O_X(K_X))=1$. The assumed first [sheaf cohomology](../../../../../../sheaf-cohomology.md) vanishes. Therefore the [Euler characteristic of a coherent sheaf](../../../../../../euler-characteristic-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md) is

$$
\boxed{\chi(X,\mathcal O_X)=1-0+1=2.}
$$

These hypotheses characterize the [K3 surface](../../../../../../k3-surface.md) setting used below.
