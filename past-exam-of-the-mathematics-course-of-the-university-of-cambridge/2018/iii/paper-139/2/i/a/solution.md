<h1 id="2/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $F=\sum_C\min_{G\in|H|}\operatorname{mult}_C(G)\,C$. This is the [fixed part of a linear system](../../../../../../../fixed-part-of-a-linear-system.md). The [movable part of a linear system](../../../../../../../movable-part-of-a-linear-system.md) is $|M|$, with $M=H-F$; multiplication by the defining section of $F$ identifies its sections with those of $H$. In particular $|H|=F+|M|$, and no curve occurs in every member of $|M|$. The occurrence of $|F|$ in the definition request is understood as $|H|$.

For any integral curve $C$, choose a member $G\in|M|$ not containing $C$. Their [intersection number](../../../../../../../intersection-number-of-a-cartier-divisor-with-a-curve.md) is a sum of nonnegative local [intersection multiplicities](../../../../../../../intersection-multiplicity.md), so $M\cdot C=G\cdot C\geq0$. Therefore **the movable divisor $M$ is nef**. If $M=0$, the same assertion is immediate.
