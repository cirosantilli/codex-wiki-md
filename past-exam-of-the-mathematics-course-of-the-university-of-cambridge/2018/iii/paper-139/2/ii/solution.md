<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because $|D|$ has no [fixed components](../../../../../../fixed-component.md), choose two members $G_1,G_2$ with no common component. This uses the infinitude of the algebraically closed field and avoidance of finitely many proper linear subspaces of the section space. On a [smooth algebraic surface](../../../../../../smooth-algebraic-surface.md), their [intersection number](../../../../../../intersection-number-of-a-cartier-divisor-with-a-curve.md) is the sum of local [intersection multiplicities](../../../../../../intersection-multiplicity.md). Since $G_1\cdot G_2=D^2=0$, they are disjoint. A basepoint of $|D|$ would belong to both, so **$|D|$ is basepoint-free**.

The resulting [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md) has nonconstant image since $\dim|D|\geq1$. Its image cannot be a surface: a generically finite morphism defined by $D$ would make $D^2$ the positive product of its degree and the degree of its image. Thus the image is a curve. Apply [Stein factorization](../../../../../../stein-factorization.md) to obtain $g:X\to C$ with connected fibers and $g_*\mathcal O_X=\mathcal O_C$, where $C$ is a smooth projective curve. There is a positive-degree [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) $L$ on $C$ with $\mathcal O_X(D)=g^*L$. The [projection formula for sheaves](../../../../../../projection-formula.md) and [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) give $h^0(X,mD)=h^0(C,L^m)=m\deg L+O(1)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\kappa(D)=1.}
$$
