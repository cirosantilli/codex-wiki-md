Condition (b) immediately implies (c): a positive real multiple $tA$ of an [ample divisor](../../../../../../../ample-cartier-divisor.md) is an [ample real divisor](../../../../../../../ample-real-divisor.md), and [real linear equivalence of divisors](../../../../../../../real-linear-equivalence-of-divisors.md) implies [numerical equivalence of divisors](../../../../../../../numerical-equivalence-of-divisors.md).

More explicitly, the real numerical class of $D$ is the sum of a class in the [ample cone](../../../../../../../ample-cone.md) and an effective real divisor class. This separates strict positivity from the possibly degenerate effective part; it does not claim that the effective part itself is ample.
