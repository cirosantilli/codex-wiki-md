<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Choose a [very ample divisor](../../../../../../very-ample-divisor.md) $A$. By the stronger form of [Kodaira's lemma](../../../../../../kodaira-s-lemma.md), $jD\sim A+E$ for some effective divisor $E$. The subsystem $s_EH^0(X,A)\subseteq H^0(X,jD)$ defines the embedding given by $A$ on $X\setminus\operatorname{Supp}E$. Ratios of its sections generate the [function field](../../../../../../function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) $k(X)$. The map from the complete [complete linear system of a divisor](../../../../../../complete-linear-system-of-a-divisor.md) contains these ratios, so it induces the same function field and is **birational onto its image**.

**The converse is true; smoothness is unnecessary.** If $|jD|$ gives a birational map, choose $n$ algebraically independent ratios $s_1/s_0,\ldots,s_n/s_0$ among a generating set of its section ratios. For every $q$, the sections

$$
s_0^{q-|\alpha|}\prod_{i=1}^ns_i^{\alpha_i},\qquad \alpha\in\mathbb N^n,\quad |\alpha|\leq q,
$$

are linearly independent by [algebraic independence](../../../../../../algebraic-independence.md). Therefore $h^0(X,qjD)\geq\binom{q+n}{n}$, giving condition (1) of part (iii). This is the [birational linear system criterion for bigness](../../../../../../birational-linear-system-criterion-for-bigness.md).
