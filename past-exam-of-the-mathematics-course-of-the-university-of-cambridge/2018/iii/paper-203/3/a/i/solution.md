<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For real [test functions](../../../../../../../test-function.md) $f,g\in C_c^\infty(D)$, the [Dirichlet inner product](../../../../../../../dirichlet-inner-product.md) in the normalization compatible with the logarithmic Green kernel is

$$
\boxed{(f,g)_\nabla=\frac1{2\pi}\int_D\nabla f(x)\cdot\nabla g(x)\,dx.}
$$

Its norm measures gradient energy rather than the values of the functions. A [change of variables](../../../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) shows that this energy is invariant under planar [conformal maps](../../../../../../../conformal-map.md): the squared derivative in the transformation of the [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) cancels the area [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md).
