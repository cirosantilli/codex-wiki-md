The [Phase classification of the SLE trace](../../../../../../../phase-classification-of-the-sle-trace.md) gives a [space-filling curve](../../../../../../../space-filling-curve.md) precisely for

$$
\boxed{\kappa\geq8.}
$$

In particular, the critical value $\kappa=8$ is included.
