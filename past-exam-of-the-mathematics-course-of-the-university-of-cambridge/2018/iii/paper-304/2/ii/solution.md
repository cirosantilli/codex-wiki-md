<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [kinetic term](../../../../../../kinetic-term.md) fixes the [mass dimension](../../../../../../mass-dimension.md) of the [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md), and the interaction then fixes that of its coupling:

$$
[\phi]=\frac{d-2}{2},\qquad [\lambda]=d-\frac{n(d-2)}2.
$$

A [relevant coupling](../../../../../../relevant-coupling.md) has positive [mass dimension](../../../../../../mass-dimension.md); a [marginal coupling](../../../../../../marginal-coupling.md) has zero [mass dimension](../../../../../../mass-dimension.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{d\leq2:\ n\geq3\text{ arbitrary};\qquad d>2:\ 3\leq n\leq\frac{2d}{d-2}.}
$$

For integer dimensions the complete interacting list is

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
d&\text{relevant }n&\text{marginal }n\\\hline
1,2&\text{every }n\geq3&\text{none}\\
3&3,4,5&6\\
4&3&4\\
5&3&\text{none}\\
6&\text{none}&3\\
d\geq7&\text{none}&\text{none}
\end{array}.
$$

If the noninteracting $n=1,2$ terms are included, their couplings have [mass dimensions](../../../../../../mass-dimension.md) $(d+2)/2$ and $2$, respectively, and are relevant for every positive $d$. This is a classification by [power counting in quantum field theory](../../../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md); it does not assert the stability of odd monomial potentials.
