<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In Cartesian coordinates on [Euclidean space](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md), the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) and the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) vanish. The [second covariant derivative of a Killing vector](../../../../../../second-covariant-derivative-of-a-killing-vector.md) therefore gives

$$
\partial_\mu\partial_\rho K^\nu=0.
$$

Every first partial derivative is constant on the connected space $\mathbb R^n$. Integrating once more gives $K^\nu=A^\nu+M_\mu{}^\nu x^\mu$, with constant coefficients. The [Killing equation](../../../../../../killing-equation.md) now becomes

$$
0=\partial_\mu K_\nu+\partial_\nu K_\mu=M_{\mu\nu}+M_{\nu\mu},\qquad
M_{\mu\nu}=M_\mu{}^\kappa\delta_{\kappa\nu}.
$$

Thus the coefficient is an [antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md). Conversely, any such constant coefficients satisfy the [Killing equation](../../../../../../killing-equation.md). The complete [Euclidean Killing vector field](../../../../../../euclidean-killing-vector-field.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{K=A^\nu\frac{\partial}{\partial x^\nu}
+M_\mu{}^\nu x^\mu\frac{\partial}{\partial x^\nu},\qquad M^T=-M.}
$$

The $n$ translation parameters and $n(n-1)/2$ rotation parameters give $n(n+1)/2$ independent [Killing vector fields](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md).
