<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A radial [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) has $L=0$ and $\epsilon=0$, so $\dot r=\pm E$ and $\dot t=E/V$. In the exterior, $E>0$ for a future-directed ray. Define the analogue of the [Schwarzschild tortoise coordinate](../../../../../../schwarzschild-tortoise-coordinate.md) by

$$
\boxed{\frac{dr_*}{dr}=\frac1V,\qquad r_*=r+r_+\log\left|\frac r{r_+}-1\right|+C.}
$$

Then an outgoing ray satisfies $\dot u=\dot t-\dot r/V=0$, whereas an ingoing ray satisfies $\dot v=\dot t+\dot r/V=0$. Thus $u=t-r_*$ is constant on outgoing rays and $v=t+r_*$ on ingoing rays. The radial two-dimensional metric becomes $-Vdu\,dv$.

The ingoing coordinate is regular at the future horizon: substituting $t=v-r_*$ gives $ds^2=-Vdv^2+2dv\,dr+R^2d\Omega_2^2$. Inside the horizon, the words outgoing and ingoing refer to the continued null families; both future-directed families in the future interior decrease $r$.
