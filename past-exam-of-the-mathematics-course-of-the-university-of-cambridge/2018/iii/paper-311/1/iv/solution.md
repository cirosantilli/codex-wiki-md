<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A closed spatial [Cauchy hypersurface](../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md) is compact and has no boundary. Thus there is no asymptotic surface charge, and the complete gravitational [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is the integral of the [Hamiltonian constraint](../../../../../../hamiltonian-constraint.md) and [momentum constraint](../../../../../../momentum-constraint.md) derived above. Both constraints vanish on solutions, giving

$$
\boxed{H_{\rm total}=0.}
$$

This is the [vanishing canonical Hamiltonian of a closed universe](../../../../../../vanishing-canonical-hamiltonian-of-a-closed-universe.md): canonical evolution is generated by constraints and there is no asymptotic time translation whose boundary charge could define an [Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy](../../../../../../arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md). It does not require local matter energy densities or local curvature to vanish. With matter included, the corresponding total constraints still vanish.
