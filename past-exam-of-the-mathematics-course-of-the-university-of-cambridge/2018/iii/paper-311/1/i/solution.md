<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The action contains neither $\dot N$ nor $\dot N^i$, so their [conjugate momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) vanishes. They impose constraints rather than independent evolution equations: after the [Legendre transform in mechanics](../../../../../../legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md), the [lapse function](../../../../../../lapse-function.md) and [shift vector](../../../../../../shift-vector.md) are [Lagrange multipliers](../../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) in the [Hamiltonian formulation of general relativity](../../../../../../hamiltonian-formulation-of-general-relativity.md).

Use precisely the paper's sign convention for [extrinsic curvature](../../../../../../extrinsic-curvature.md), $K_{ij}=(\dot h_{ij}-D_iN_j-D_jN_i)/(2N)$. At fixed $h_{ij},N,N^i$,

$$
\delta K_{ij}=\frac{\delta\dot h_{ij}}{2N},\qquad
\delta\bigl[\sqrt hN(K_{ab}K^{ab}-K^2)\bigr]
=\sqrt h(K^{ij}-Kh^{ij})\delta\dot h_{ij}.
$$

Consequently the [canonical momentum of the spatial metric](../../../../../../canonical-momentum-of-the-spatial-metric.md) is

$$
\boxed{\pi^{ij}=\sqrt h(K^{ij}-Kh^{ij}).}
$$

This is a weight-one [tensor density](../../../../../../tensor-density.md), with indices raised and lowered using $h_{ij}$. Its trace and inverse relation are

$$
\pi=h_{ij}\pi^{ij}=-2\sqrt hK,\qquad
K^{ij}=\frac1{\sqrt h}\left(\pi^{ij}-\frac12\pi h^{ij}\right).
$$

The sign of $K_{ij}$ used here is opposite to the convention in [extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface](../../../../../../extrinsic-curvature-of-a-spatial-hypersurface.md); mixing the two conventions would reverse the momentum formula.
