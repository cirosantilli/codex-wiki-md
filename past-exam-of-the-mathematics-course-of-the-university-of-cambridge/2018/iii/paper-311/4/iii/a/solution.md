<h1 id="4/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Klein-Gordon inner product](../../../../../../../klein-gordon-inner-product.md) satisfies $(\overline\alpha,\overline\beta)=-(\beta,\alpha)$, so $(\overline\psi_i,\overline\psi_j)=-\delta_{ij}$. Orthogonality of positive and negative modes in the field expansion therefore gives $(\psi_i,\Phi)=a_i$ and $(\overline\psi_i,\Phi)=-a_i^\dagger$.

Extracting the primed coefficient in the same way, and using antilinearity in the first entry,

$$
a'_i=(\psi'_i,\Phi)=\sum_j\left[\overline A_{ij}(\psi_j,\Phi)+\overline B_{ij}(\overline\psi_j,\Phi)\right].
$$

Consequently the [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md) of the [creation and annihilation operators](../../../../../../../creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) is

$$
\boxed{a'_i=\sum_j(\overline A_{ij}a_j-\overline B_{ij}a_j^\dagger).}
$$

The minus sign comes from the negative norm of the conjugate modes, and the complex conjugates come from the first argument of the Hermitian pairing.
