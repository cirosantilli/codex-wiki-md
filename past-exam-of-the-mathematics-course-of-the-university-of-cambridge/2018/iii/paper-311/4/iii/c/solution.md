<h1 id="4/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose the unprimed modes as the inertial positive-frequency modes in the far past, and the primed modes as those in the far future. In the [in-vacuum and out-vacuum](../../../../../../../in-vacuum-and-out-vacuum.md) construction, $|0_{\rm in}\rangle$ is annihilated by every $a_j$. The number of late-time quanta in mode $i$ is measured by $N'_i=a_i'{}^\dagger a'_i$. Expanding using the [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md), the only surviving vacuum contraction is $\langle0_{\rm in}|a_ja_k^\dagger|0_{\rm in}\rangle=\delta_{jk}$. Thus the [particle number from Bogoliubov coefficients](../../../../../../../particle-number-from-bogoliubov-coefficients.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle0_{\rm in}|N'_i|0_{\rm in}\rangle=\sum_j|B_{ij}|^2.}
$$

If $B\ne0$, at least one row has positive norm and late-time observers detect particles. If $B=0$, each $a'_i$ is a linear combination of past annihilation operators and annihilates the same state, so all these occupation numbers vanish. The intervening nonstationary geometry can mix positive and negative frequencies, realizing [vacuum ambiguity in a nonstationary spacetime](../../../../../../../vacuum-ambiguity-in-a-nonstationary-spacetime.md) even though the asymptotic inertial choices are unambiguous.

For infinitely many modes, the expressions are understood with suitable convergence or wave-packet regularization. The total expected number is $\sum_{ij}|B_{ij}|^2$, finite precisely when $B$ is a [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](../../../../../../../hilbert-schmidt-operator.md) in the specified orthonormal mode spaces. A divergent sum is not a finite occupation number in a common vacuum representation; it signals the extra infinite-dimensional issue rather than changing the finite-mode calculation.
