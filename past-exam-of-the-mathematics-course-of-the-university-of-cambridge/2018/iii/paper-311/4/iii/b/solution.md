<h1 id="4/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [canonical commutation relation](../../../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) for the [creation and annihilation operators](../../../../../../../creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) and the transformation derived above. Directly,

$$
[a'_i,a_k'{}^\dagger]=\sum_j(\overline A_{ij}A_{kj}-\overline B_{ij}B_{kj}),\qquad
[a'_i,a'_k]=\sum_j(-\overline A_{ij}\overline B_{kj}+\overline B_{ij}\overline A_{kj}).
$$

Requiring these to equal $\delta_{ik}$ and zero, respectively, and conjugating the equations yields the [canonical identities for a bosonic Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../../../../canonical-identities-for-a-bosonic-bogoliubov-transformation.md):

$$
\boxed{AA^\dagger-BB^\dagger=I,\qquad AB^T=BA^T.}
$$

The commutator of the primed creation operators then vanishes by taking adjoints. These are exactly the corresponding positive-mode norm and positive-negative orthogonality conditions for the [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md). They are sufficient for these algebraic commutators whenever the sums are well defined. Infinitely many modes introduce further questions of convergence and whether the two choices admit a common [bosonic Fock space](../../../../../../../bosonic-fock-space.md) representation; the matrix identities alone do not settle unitary implementability.
