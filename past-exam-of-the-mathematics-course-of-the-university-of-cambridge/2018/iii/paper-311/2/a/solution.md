<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $k$ generate the [Killing horizon](../../../../../../killing-horizon.md) and let $\ell=fk$ be an affinely parametrized generator wherever $k\ne0$. Choose an auxiliary null vector $n$ with $n\cdot\ell=-1$ and the [screen-space projector](../../../../../../screen-space-projector.md) $q_{ab}=g_{ab}+\ell_an_b+n_a\ell_b$. The [optical tensor](../../../../../../optical-tensor.md) is the screen projection $B_{ab}=q_a{}^cq_b{}^d\nabla_d\ell_c$.

Since the projector annihilates $k$, the symmetric part is

$$
B_{(ab)}=f q_a{}^cq_b{}^d\nabla_{(d}k_{c)}=0
$$

by the [Killing equation](../../../../../../killing-equation.md). Its trace is the [null expansion](../../../../../../null-expansion.md) and its trace-free symmetric part is the [null shear](../../../../../../null-shear.md), so both vanish. Also $\ell$ is normal to the null hypersurface. [Frobenius theorem](../../../../../../frobenius-theorem.md) gives $\ell_{[a}\nabla_b\ell_{c]}=0$, which implies $q_a{}^cq_b{}^d\nabla_{[d}\ell_{c]}=0$: the [null twist](../../../../../../null-twist.md) vanishes. These arguments use derivatives tangent to the horizon and hence apply to any smooth [null geodesic congruence](../../../../../../null-geodesic-congruence.md) containing its generators, independently of its extension off the horizon. At a regular [bifurcation surface](../../../../../../bifurcation-surface.md), use the smooth affine generator and continuity. Thus

$$
\boxed{\theta=0,\qquad\hat\sigma_{ab}=0,\qquad\hat\omega_{ab}=0\quad\text{on the horizon}.}
$$

This is the vanishing of the [optical scalars on a Killing horizon](../../../../../../optical-scalars-on-a-killing-horizon.md).
