<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [geometric drift condition](../../../../../../geometric-drift-condition.md) requires a measurable $V:\mathcal X\to[1,\infty)$, a [small set](../../../../../../small-set.md) $C$, constants $\lambda\in(0,1)$ and $b<\infty$, and

$$
\boxed{PV(x)\leq\lambda V(x)+b\mathbf1_C(x)\qquad(x\in\mathcal X).}
$$

Smallness means that for some $m\geq1$, $\varepsilon>0$, and [probability measure](../../../../../../probability-measure.md) $\nu$, $P^m(x,\cdot)\geq\varepsilon\nu(\cdot)$ for every $x\in C$. With irreducibility and aperiodicity, this [drift-minorisation condition](../../../../../../drift-minorisation-condition.md) implies [geometric ergodicity](../../../../../../geometric-ergodicity.md). The function $V$ controls the chain away from the small set; inside that set the additive constant allows unrestricted bounded drift.
