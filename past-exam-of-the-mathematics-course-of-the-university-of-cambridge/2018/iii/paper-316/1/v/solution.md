<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

A coplanar target sweeps a strip of width $2R_p$ through the [comet](../../../../../../comet.md)'s local position probability. Hence the relevant [geometric collision cross-section](../../../../../../geometric-collision-cross-section.md) is a length, and the rare collision rate per [comet](../../../../../../comet.md) is $\Gamma_{\rm col}=2R_p\Sigma_c(a_p)v_{\rm rel}$.

Using the corrected [phase-mixed comet surface density](../../../../../../phase-mixed-comet-surface-density.md) and the notation $s=q/a_p$, $\gamma=\sqrt{3-2\sqrt{2s}}$, the [coplanar comet collision time](../../../../../../coplanar-comet-collision-time.md) is

$$
\boxed{t_{\rm col}\simeq\frac{\pi^2Q^{3/2}a_p\sqrt{1-s}}{2R_p\gamma\sqrt{GM_\star}}.}
$$

For $q\ll a_p$, this becomes $\pi^2Q^{3/2}a_p/(2\sqrt3R_p\sqrt{GM_\star})$, so $t_{\rm col}\propto Q^{3/2}M_p^{-1/3}a_p$ at fixed stellar mass and planetary [mass density](../../../../../../density.md). This is the mean waiting time for one orbiting [comet](../../../../../../comet.md); the mean interval between impacts from $N_c$ such independent [comets](../../../../../../comet.md) is $t_{\rm col}/N_c$. It assumes rare encounters and orbital [phase mixing](../../../../../../phase-mixing.md), not a [Poisson process](../../../../../../poisson-process.md) derived from a single deterministic trajectory.
