<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

With negligible [comet](../../../../../../comet.md) radius and mass, the [planet](../../../../../../planet.md)'s radius and [escape velocity](../../../../../../escape-velocity.md) are

$$
R_p=\left(\frac{3M_p}{4\pi\rho_p}\right)^{1/3},\qquad
v_{\rm esc,p}^2=\frac{2GM_p}{R_p}.
$$

[Gravitational focusing](../../../../../../gravitational-focusing.md) changes the limiting collision [impact parameter](../../../../../../impact-parameter.md) from $R_p$ to $R_p\sqrt{1+v_{\rm esc,p}^2/v_{\rm rel}^2}$. Thus it is negligible when $v_{\rm esc,p}^2\ll v_{\rm rel}^2$.

Put $s=q/a_p$ and $\gamma=\sqrt{3-2\sqrt{2s}}$ in the $Q\gg a_p$ prograde approximation. Substituting $v_{\rm rel}=\gamma\sqrt{GM_\star/a_p}$ gives

$$
\boxed{M_p\ll\left(\frac{\gamma^2M_\star}{2a_p}\right)^{3/2}\left(\frac{3}{4\pi\rho_p}\right)^{1/2}.}
$$

Equivalently $2(M_p/M_\star)(a_p/R_p)\ll\gamma^2$. For $q\ll a_p$, use $\gamma^2\simeq3$. If negligible [gravitational focusing](../../../../../../gravitational-focusing.md) is required for every coplanar near-parabolic orbit, including nearly tangential prograde encounters, use the smaller value $\gamma^2=(\sqrt2-1)^2$.
