<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an unperturbed [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md), successive revolutions advance the planet-relative orientation by $-n_pT_c$ modulo $2\pi$, where $T_c$ is the [comet](../../../../../../comet.md)'s [orbital period](../../../../../../orbital-period.md). An irrational period ratio makes these orientations dense; sufficiently distant [mean-motion resonance](../../../../../../mean-motion-resonance.md) and adequate observation time justify the corresponding [phase mixing](../../../../../../phase-mixing.md) approximation.

At each radius between its [pericentre distance](../../../../../../pericentre-distance.md) and [apocentre distance](../../../../../../apocentre-distance.md), the [comet](../../../../../../comet.md) eventually visits every azimuth in the [rotating reference frame](../../../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md). The spatial projection is therefore

$$
\boxed{q\leq r\leq Q,\quad 0\leq\theta<2\pi.}
$$

It is an [annulus](../../../../../../annulus-mathematics.md), with a nonuniform radial residence probability, rather than a uniformly filled area. Strictly, the full position-velocity [phase space](../../../../../../phase-space.md) is not this [annulus](../../../../../../annulus-mathematics.md): at each radius the energy and angular momentum constrain the velocity, with inward and outward branches. The [annulus](../../../../../../annulus-mathematics.md) is its position-space projection. This description assumes the [orbital elements](../../../../../../orbital-element.md) have not yet been substantially changed by [planetary scattering](../../../../../../planetary-scattering.md).
