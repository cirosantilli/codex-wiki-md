<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

To first order in [orbital eccentricity](../../../../../../orbital-eccentricity.md), the [semi-minor axis](../../../../../../semi-minor-axis.md) is $a+O(e^2)$, while the [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md)'s center is displaced from the [star](../../../../../../star.md) by $-a\mathbf e$. Thus each orbit is a circle of radius $a$ about $-az$, in complex spatial coordinates.

Write $z=z_f+e_pe^{i\varpi_p}$ at one secular epoch. The centers of the individual circles lie on a circle of radius $ae_p$ about $C_f=-az_f$. Their union is the [proper-eccentricity annulus](../../../../../../proper-eccentricity-annulus.md)

$$
\boxed{a(1-e_p)\leq|\mathbf r-C_f|\leq a(1+e_p),\qquad C_f=-a\mathbf e_f.}
$$

Its full radial width is $2ae_p$ and its center is displaced by $ae_f$ toward the forced [apoapsis](../../../../../../apoapsis.md) direction. Relative to the [star](../../../../../../star.md), if the forced [longitude of periapsis](../../../../../../longitude-of-periapsis.md) is zero,

$$
r_{\rm inner,outer}(\theta)\simeq a[1-e_f\cos\theta\mp e_p].
$$

The middle panel of the preceding figure shows this construction. For fully sampled [orbital phases](../../../../../../orbital-phase.md) and proper phases, the [annulus](../../../../../../annulus-mathematics.md) is occupied throughout, but its density need not be uniform; turning points in the proper radial excursion give integrable edge enhancements. The construction requires $e_f,e_p\ll1$ and is an instantaneous secular geometry.
