<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For one isolated perturbing [planet](../../../../../../planet.md), its [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md) has fixed [longitude of periapsis](../../../../../../longitude-of-periapsis.md), so $z_1$ is constant and its secular forcing frequency is zero. A constant [forced eccentricity](../../../../../../forced-eccentricity.md) satisfies $0=iAz_f+iA_1z_1$. Since $A=-A_1f(\alpha_1)$,

$$
\boxed{z_f=-\frac{A_1}{A}z_1=\frac{z_1}{f(\alpha_1)}.}
$$

This assumes $A\ne0$. If an external process made the [planet](../../../../../../planet.md) precess at frequency $g$, the particular solution would instead be $z_f=A_1z_1/(g-A)$; the stated formula relies on the isolated single-planet case.
