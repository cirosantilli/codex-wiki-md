<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For a circular [Kepler orbit](../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md), $e=0$ remains an exact solution of the averaged [Poynting–Robertson drag](../../../../../../poynting-robertson-drag.md) equations, and $\dot a=-2A/a$. Integrating gives $a^2(t)=a_0^2-4At$, so the point-star [inspiral time under Poynting–Robertson drag](../../../../../../inspiral-time-under-poynting-robertson-drag.md) is

$$
\boxed{t_{\rm circ}=\frac{a_0^2}{4A}=\frac{ca_0^2}{4\beta GM_\star}.}
$$

For a [star](../../../../../../star.md) of radius $R_\star$, contact occurs instead at $(a_0^2-R_\star^2)/(4A)$ within this model. Sublimation or other forces can remove a real grain earlier.
