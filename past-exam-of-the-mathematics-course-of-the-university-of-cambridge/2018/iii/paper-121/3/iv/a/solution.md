<h1 id="3/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [cardinal-preserving forcing](../../../../../../../cardinal-preserving-forcing.md) over $M$ leaves every ground-model [cardinal number](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) a cardinal in every [generic extension](../../../../../../../generic-extension.md). In the present notation, for every $M$-generic filter $G$ and every ordinal $\kappa\in M$,

$$
\boxed{M\models\text{“}\kappa\text{ is a cardinal”}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad M[G]\models\text{“}\kappa\text{ is a cardinal”}.}
$$

Set [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) preserves ordinals, and an old bijection witnessing that an ordinal is not a cardinal remains available. Thus this condition also means that the two models have exactly the same cardinals. Cardinality here is computed inside the respective models, not from their external sizes.
