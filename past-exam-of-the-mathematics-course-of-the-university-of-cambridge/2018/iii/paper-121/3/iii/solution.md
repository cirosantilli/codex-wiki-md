A [Delta-system](../../../../../../delta-system.md) is a family $\mathcal A$ of sets with a fixed root $r$ such that

$$
\boxed{A\cap B=r\qquad\text{whenever }A,B\in\mathcal A\text{ are distinct}.}
$$

The [Delta-system lemma](../../../../../../delta-system-lemma.md) says that every uncountable family of finite sets has an uncountable subfamily forming a [Delta-system](../../../../../../delta-system.md). The root may be empty. Finiteness is a hypothesis on the family to which this lemma is applied, rather than a requirement in the general definition of a [Delta-system](../../../../../../delta-system.md).
