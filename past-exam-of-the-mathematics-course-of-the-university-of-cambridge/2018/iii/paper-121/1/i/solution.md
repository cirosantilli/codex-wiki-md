↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Tarski-Vaught test](../../../../../../tarski-vaught-test.md) says that $(M,\in)$ is an [elementary substructure](../../../../../../elementary-substructure.md) of $(N,\in)$ if and only if every existential statement true in $N$ with parameters from $M$ has a witness in $M$ that satisfies its matrix in $N$. Explicitly, for every [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\psi(x,\bar y)$ and every parameter tuple $\bar a\in M^{|\bar y|}$, require

$$
\boxed{N\models\exists x\,\psi(x,\bar a)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\exists b\in M\;N\models\psi(b,\bar a).}
$$

The truth of the matrix in this test is evaluated in the larger structure $N$. Under this criterion, $(M,\in)\prec(N,\in)$ means that all [first-order formulas](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) with parameters from $M$ have identical truth values in the two structures.
