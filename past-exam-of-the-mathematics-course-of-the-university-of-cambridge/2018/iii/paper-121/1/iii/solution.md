A [cardinal number](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) is identified with an [initial ordinal](../../../../../../initial-ordinal.md), namely an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) not in [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) with any smaller ordinal. An [inaccessible cardinal](../../../../../../strongly-inaccessible-cardinal.md), in the strong sense used here, is an uncountable [regular cardinal](../../../../../../regular-cardinal.md) that is also a [strong limit cardinal](../../../../../../strong-limit-cardinal.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\kappa>\aleph_0,\qquad\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)=\kappa,\qquad 2^\lambda<\kappa\text{ for every cardinal }\lambda<\kappa.}
$$

A [worldly cardinal](../../../../../../worldly-cardinal.md) is a [cardinal number](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) whose level of the [Von Neumann hierarchy](../../../../../../von-neumann-hierarchy.md) satisfies all of [ZFC](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md):

$$
\boxed{(V_\kappa,\in)\models\mathsf{ZFC}.}
$$

Worldliness is this model-theoretic condition; regularity is not part of its definition. The original PDF asks for these two notions; the extra comma in the local TeX makes “cardinal” look like a separate requested item.
