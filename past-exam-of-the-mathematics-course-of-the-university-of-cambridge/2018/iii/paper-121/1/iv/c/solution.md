↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A formula has [set-theoretic absoluteness](../../../../../../../set-theoretic-absoluteness.md) between these structures when both directions hold for every $\bar a\in M^n$:

$$
\boxed{(M,\in)\models\varphi(\bar a)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(N,\in)\models\varphi(\bar a).}
$$

Equivalently, it is both a [downward absolute formula](../../../../../../../downward-absolute-formula.md) and an [upward absolute formula](../../../../../../../upward-absolute-formula.md) between them.
