<h1 id="1/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For every tuple of parameters $\bar a\in M^n$, a [downward absolute formula](../../../../../../../downward-absolute-formula.md) transfers truth from the larger structure to the smaller one:

$$
\boxed{(N,\in)\models\varphi(\bar a)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(M,\in)\models\varphi(\bar a).}
$$

The condition concerns every parameter tuple from $M$, not merely parameter-free sentences.
