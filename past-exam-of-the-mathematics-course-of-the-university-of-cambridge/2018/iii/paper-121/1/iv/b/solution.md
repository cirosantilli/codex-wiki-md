<h1 id="1/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every tuple of parameters $\bar a\in M^n$, an [upward absolute formula](../../../../../../../upward-absolute-formula.md) transfers truth from the smaller structure to the larger one:

$$
\boxed{(M,\in)\models\varphi(\bar a)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(N,\in)\models\varphi(\bar a).}
$$

These definitions of directional [set-theoretic absoluteness](../../../../../../../set-theoretic-absoluteness.md) do not themselves require the structures to be transitive.
