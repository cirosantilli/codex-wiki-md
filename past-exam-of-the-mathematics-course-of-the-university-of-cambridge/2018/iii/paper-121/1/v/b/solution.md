↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the model $M$ constructed in part (a), the collapsing map $X\to M$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md). The [Downward Lowenheim-Skolem theorem](../../../../../../../downward-lowenheim-skolem-theorem.md) supplied countable $X$, so $M$ is externally countable. It is infinite because it satisfies the [axiom of infinity](../../../../../../../axiom-of-infinity.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{|M|=\aleph_0.}
$$

This is external [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md). A [countable transitive model](../../../../../../../countable-transitive-model.md) still regards many of its own sets and ordinals as uncountable, because external enumerations need not belong to it.
