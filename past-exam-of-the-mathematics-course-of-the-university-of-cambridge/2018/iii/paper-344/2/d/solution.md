<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At $\xi=0$, the [polar order parameter](../../../../../../polar-order-parameter.md) obeys

$$
\dot\theta=-\frac g2,\qquad \dot p=-\Gamma(a+bp^2)p.
$$

For $a<0$, $b>0$, the magnitude relaxes to $\sqrt{-a/b}$ while the direction rotates continuously. Thus the likely ordered behaviour is **tumbling rather than a fixed alignment**.

More generally, for $|\xi|<1$ and $g\ne0$, $\xi\cos2\theta-1$ never vanishes, so the [tumbling of a polar order parameter](../../../../../../tumbling-of-a-polar-order-parameter.md) persists. The [polar order parameter](../../../../../../polar-order-parameter.md) completes a full $2\pi$ rotation in

$$
\boxed{T_{\rm polar}=\frac{4\pi}{|g|\sqrt{1-\xi^2}}},
$$

obtained by integrating $dt=2\,|d\theta|/(|g|(1-\xi\cos2\theta))$ over a full rotation. An unoriented [nematic director](../../../../../../nematic-director.md) repeats after half this period, whereas a [polar order parameter](../../../../../../polar-order-parameter.md) distinguishes opposite directions. For $\xi\ne0$ its magnitude can also oscillate. If the [polar order parameter](../../../../../../polar-order-parameter.md) decays to zero, its orientation is no longer a physical observable; the tumbling conclusion concerns a persistent ordered state.
