<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $b>0$ for a stable quartic [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md). The [cubic-term removal in a conserved quartic Landau free energy](../../../../../../cubic-term-removal-in-a-conserved-quartic-landau-free-energy.md) uses $\phi=\psi-c/(3b)$ and gives, apart from a constant,

$$
f=\frac{a'}2\psi^2+\frac b4\psi^4+d'\psi,
\qquad a'=a-\frac{c^2}{3b},
\qquad d'=d-\frac{ac}{3b}+\frac{2c^3}{27b^2}.
$$

For a closed [binary fluid mixture](../../../../../../binary-fluid-mixture.md), the [compositional order parameter](../../../../../../compositional-order-parameter.md) has fixed integral. Adding $d\int\phi$ therefore adds the same constant to every allowed state. Equivalently, in the [common-tangent construction for phase coexistence](../../../../../../common-tangent-construction-for-phase-coexistence.md), a linear term changes the tangent slope, hence the [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md), by $d$, but leaves the coexistence compositions unchanged. This is why [linear composition bias leaves coexistence compositions unchanged](../../../../../../linear-composition-bias-leaves-coexistence-compositions-unchanged.md); it would not mean an unchanged composition at a fixed externally imposed [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md).

In the shifted variable, the [binodal](../../../../../../binodal.md) compositions are $\psi_\pm=\pm\sqrt{-a'/b}$ for $a'<0$. They merge at

$$
\boxed{a_c=\frac{c^2}{3b},\qquad \phi_c=-\frac{c}{3b}}.
$$

The [continuous phase transition](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md) therefore has the same scalar quartic [Landau theory](../../../../../../landau-theory.md) as the symmetric [binary fluid mixture](../../../../../../binary-fluid-mixture.md), with a shifted critical composition and control parameter. In the [mean-field approximation](../../../../../../mean-field-approximation.md), the composition difference vanishes with [order-parameter critical exponent](../../../../../../order-parameter-critical-exponent.md) $1/2$. This argument presumes the critical composition lies in the physically accessible composition range; conservation affects the dynamics, not this static [phase coexistence](../../../../../../phase-coexistence.md) construction.
