<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A scalar coupling linear in the [nematic order parameter](../../../../../../nematic-order-parameter.md) requires a second-rank anisotropy. A single [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md) supplies no such tensor, while $E_iE_j$ does. Its isotropic part gives $E^2\operatorname{Tr}Q=0$. Hence the lowest joint order in $E$ and $Q$ of the [electric-field coupling to nematic order](../../../../../../electric-field-coupling-to-nematic-order.md) is

$$
\boxed{f_E=-\chi E_iQ_{ij}E_j}.
$$

Terms depending only on $E$ do not affect minimization over $Q$. With $\vartheta$ the angle between the [nematic director](../../../../../../nematic-director.md) and the [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md),

$$
f_E=-\frac32\chi\lambda E^2\left(\cos^2\vartheta-\frac13\right).
$$

For $\chi\lambda>0$, the lowest [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) occurs at $\cos^2\vartheta=1$, so the [nematic director](../../../../../../nematic-director.md) aligns along either $+\mathbf E$ or $-\mathbf E$. Then $E_iQ_{ij}E_j=\lambda E^2$, giving

$$
\boxed{f_E=\bar d\lambda,\qquad \bar d=-\chi E^2<0\quad(E\ne0,\ \chi>0)}.
$$

Unlike the linear composition term in a conserved [binary fluid mixture](../../../../../../binary-fluid-mixture.md), this term changes the equilibrium [nematic order parameter](../../../../../../nematic-order-parameter.md): its spatial integral is not fixed.
