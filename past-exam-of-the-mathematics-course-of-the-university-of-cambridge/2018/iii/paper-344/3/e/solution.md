<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Within this analytic quartic [Landau theory](../../../../../../landau-theory.md), equilibrium of a nonconserved scalar [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) requires $f'=0$. At a [continuous phase transition](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md), its restoring curvature vanishes, so $f''=0$. A nonzero $f'''$ would leave a cubic leading term in the [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) about that state and prevent it from being a local minimum. Thus a stable [critical endpoint of a quartic Landau free energy](../../../../../../critical-endpoint-of-a-quartic-landau-free-energy.md) requires all three derivatives to vanish, with $f''''>0$. The condition $f''=0$ alone can instead identify a [spinodal point](../../../../../../spinodal-point.md).

For $f=\bar b\lambda^4+\bar c\lambda^3+\bar a\lambda^2+\bar d\lambda$, $\bar b>0$, the successive equations are

$$
f'''=24\bar b\lambda+6\bar c=0,
\quad f''=12\bar b\lambda^2+6\bar c\lambda+2\bar a=0,
\quad f'=4\bar b\lambda^3+3\bar c\lambda^2+2\bar a\lambda+\bar d=0.
$$

They give

$$
\boxed{\lambda_c=-\frac{\bar c}{4\bar b},
\qquad \bar a_c=\frac{3\bar c^2}{8\bar b},
\qquad \bar d_c=\frac{\bar c^3}{16\bar b^2}}.
$$

At these values, the [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is exactly $f(\lambda_c)+\bar b(\lambda-\lambda_c)^4$, proving stability. With no [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md), $\bar d=0$, so necessarily $\bar c=0$, followed by $\lambda_c=\bar a_c=0$.

For $\bar c<0$ and $\chi>0$, the [electric-field coupling to nematic order](../../../../../../electric-field-coupling-to-nematic-order.md) supplies the required negative $\bar d_c$ at

$$
\boxed{E_c^2=-\frac{\bar c^3}{16\chi\bar b^2},
\qquad E_c=\frac{(-\bar c)^{3/2}}{4\sqrt\chi\,\bar b}}>0.
$$

Tuning the quadratic coefficient to $\bar a_c$ simultaneously reaches the [field-induced critical endpoint of the isotropic-nematic transition](../../../../../../field-induced-critical-endpoint-of-the-isotropic-nematic-transition.md). The PDF's $\tilde c$ in the last clause is undefined; it is interpreted here as the previously defined $\bar c$. The endpoint is a prediction of the stable quartic, small-field [mean-field approximation](../../../../../../mean-field-approximation.md); higher-order terms or a field beyond the expansion's validity can shift it.
