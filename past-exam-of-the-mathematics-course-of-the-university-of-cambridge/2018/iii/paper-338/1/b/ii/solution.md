<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In a [classical Cassegrain reflector](../../../../../../../classical-cassegrain-reflector.md), a concave parabolic primary sends light toward an intermediate focus, but a convex hyperbolic secondary intercepts it before it reaches that focus. The secondary returns the beam through a central hole in the primary to a [focal plane](../../../../../../../focal-plane.md) behind the primary. The two relevant foci of the secondary’s [hyperbola](../../../../../../../hyperbola.md) are the primary’s would-be focus and the final focus.

<a id="1/b/ii/image-light-paths-in-a-classical-cassegrain-reflector"></a>
![](../../../../../../../past-exam-of-the-mathematics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2018/iii/paper-338-cassegrain.png)

**[Figure 2](#1/b/ii/image-light-paths-in-a-classical-cassegrain-reflector). Light paths in a classical Cassegrain reflector**.

The secondary magnifies the effective [focal length](../../../../../../../focal-length.md), giving a compact tube and convenient rear-mounted instruments. The classical conics correct on-axis [spherical aberration](../../../../../../../spherical-aberration.md), but not the off-axis [coma](../../../../../../../coma-optics.md), [astigmatism](../../../../../../../astigmatism-optical-systems.md) or [field curvature](../../../../../../../petzval-field-curvature.md). There is secondary obscuration, [diffraction](../../../../../../../diffraction.md) from its supports, and sensitivity to mirror alignment. A long effective [focal length](../../../../../../../focal-length.md) is useful for a small angular image scale per detector pixel, but yields a small field for a fixed detector size.

**Parabolic primary → convex hyperbolic secondary before prime focus → rear focus.**
