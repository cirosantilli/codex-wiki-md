<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Three complementary approaches replace the simple thick solid disk:

- A thin meniscus on an [active optics](../../../../../../../active-optics.md) support distributes and adjusts forces to preserve the figure despite changing gravity and temperature. The [Very Large Telescope](../../../../../../../very-large-telescope.md) has four 8.2-metre unit telescopes of this type; the [ESO active-optics account](https://elt.eso.org/public/teles-instr/technology/active_optics/) describes their controlled mirror supports.
- A [lightweight telescope mirror](../../../../../../../lightweight-telescope-mirror.md) uses a honeycomb or ribbed backing: a deep structure retains stiffness without a massive solid interior and cools more readily. The [Large Binocular Telescope](../../../../../../../large-binocular-telescope.md) uses two 8.4-metre primaries; its [official history](https://www.lbto.org/our-history/) describes their honeycomb construction.
- A [segmented mirror](../../../../../../../segmented-mirror.md) constructs the aperture from manageable pieces. Support actuators and [mirror segment phasing](../../../../../../../mirror-segment-phasing.md) control figure, piston and tilt. Each ten-metre [Keck telescope](../../../../../../../w-m-keck-observatory.md) uses 36 hexagonal segments, as recorded in the [observatory’s telescope description](https://keckobservatory.org/our-story/telescopes/).

“Operational” and “planned” here refer to the 2018 examination date. The next generation included the 39-metre [Extremely Large Telescope](../../../../../../../extremely-large-telescope.md) with 798 segments, the thirty-metre [Thirty Meter Telescope](../../../../../../../thirty-meter-telescope.md) with 492 segments, and the [Giant Magellan Telescope](../../../../../../../giant-magellan-telescope.md) using seven 8.4-metre primary mirrors. These designs extend segmentation or lightweight casting with active control, rather than simply making a thicker slab. The specifications are documented by the [2017 ELT announcement](https://www.eso.org/public/announcements/ann17085/), [TMT optics design](https://www.tmt.org/page/optics/) and [GMT primary-mirror description](https://giantmagellan.org/gallery/primary-mirrors/). [Adaptive optics](../../../../../../../adaptive-optics.md) is also needed to exploit the large apertures against [atmospheric turbulence](../../../../../../../atmospheric-turbulence.md); it corrects faster disturbances than [active optics](../../../../../../../active-optics.md).

**Thin actively supported menisci, lightweight honeycomb mirrors, and phased segmented primaries enable larger apertures.**
