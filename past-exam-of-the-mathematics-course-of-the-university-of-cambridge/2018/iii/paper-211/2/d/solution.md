<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For fixed integers $s\leq t$, $A_s=\{\tau=s\}$ belongs to $\mathcal F_s$ and $X_s=0$ there. The [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md) property and nonnegativity imply

$$
0\leq\mathbb E[\mathbf1_{A_s}X_t]\leq\mathbb E[\mathbf1_{A_s}X_s]=0.
$$

Therefore $X_t=0$ [almost surely](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md) on $A_s$. Taking the finite union over $s\leq t$ gives $X_t=0$ on $\{\tau\leq t\}$ [almost surely](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md); taking the countable intersection over all integer $t$ makes the assertion simultaneous at every time. Thus **zero is absorbing for a nonnegative [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md) in discrete time.**
