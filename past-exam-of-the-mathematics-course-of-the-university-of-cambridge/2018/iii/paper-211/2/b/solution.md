<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Under the standard [local martingale](../../../../../../local-martingale.md) definition, $X_0$ is integrable. For a nonnegative $X$, the [Fatou lemma](../../../../../../fatou-s-lemma.md) and a [localizing sequence](../../../../../../localizing-sequence.md) give

$$
\mathbb EX_t\leq\liminf_n\mathbb EX_{t\wedge\tau_n}=\mathbb EX_0<\infty.
$$

Hence every $X_t$ is integrable, and part (a) applies. Therefore **a nonnegative discrete-time [local martingale](../../../../../../local-martingale.md) is a true [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md).** This is stronger than the continuous-time [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md) conclusion.
