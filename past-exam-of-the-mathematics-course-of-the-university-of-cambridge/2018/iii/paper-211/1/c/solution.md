<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For any holdings $h$, the [pricing kernel](../../../../../../state-price-density.md) identity implies $V_0=\mathbb E[RV_1]$. If $V_1\geq0$ [almost surely](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md), then $V_0\geq0$, so an [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md) cannot have $V_0<0$. If also $V_0=0$, then the nonnegative integrable [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $RV_1$ has zero [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value.md) and hence is zero [almost surely](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md). Since $R>0$ [almost surely](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md), $V_1=0$ [almost surely](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md), ruling out a positive terminal gain. Therefore **a [pricing kernel](../../../../../../state-price-density.md) rules out [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md).**
