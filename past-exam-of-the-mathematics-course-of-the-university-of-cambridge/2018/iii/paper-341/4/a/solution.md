<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a sufficiently regular solution with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), differentiate its squared [L2 norm](../../../../../../l2-norm.md). Since $u_t=-i(\Delta u-Vu)$,

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\|u(t)\|_{L^2}^2
=2\operatorname{Re}\int_\Omega\overline u\,u_t
=2\operatorname{Re}\left[-i\left(\int_\Omega\overline u\,\Delta u
-\int_\Omega V|u|^2\right)\right].
$$

[Integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) and the zero boundary trace give $\int\overline u\Delta u=-\int|\nabla u|^2$. The potential contribution is real because $V$ is real. The quantity in parentheses is consequently real, and its product with $-i$ has zero real part. Hence

$$
\boxed{\|u(t)\|_{L^2(\Omega)}=\|u(0)\|_{L^2(\Omega)}.}
$$

This is the [energy method](../../../../../../energy-method.md) for a [skew-adjoint](../../../../../../skew-adjoint-generator.md) evolution. For general [L2 space](../../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) data, the same result follows from the [strongly continuous unitary group](../../../../../../strongly-continuous-unitary-group.md) generated by a self-adjoint realization of $\Delta-V$. A sufficient assumption is a bounded real potential with the Dirichlet Laplacian; arbitrary real potentials require appropriate domain and self-adjointness assumptions. The differentiated proof presumes the regularity needed for the displayed integrals, rather than asserting it for every pointwise real function $V$.
