<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [magnetic torque](../../../../../../surface-magnetic-torque-on-an-accretion-disk.md) transfers [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) from the faster rotator to the slower one. When $\Omega_m>\Omega_K(r_m)$, the [neutron star](../../../../../../neutron-star.md) supplies [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) to the inner [accretion disk](../../../../../../accretion-disk.md) and possible [propeller flow](../../../../../../propeller-effect.md), so it tends to **spin down**. When the inner [accretion disk](../../../../../../accretion-disk.md) rotates faster than the star, the magnetic connection and accreted [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) tend to **spin it up**.

For slowly varying [mass accretion rate](../../../../../../mass-accretion-rate.md) and [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md), these tendencies favour a [magnetic spin equilibrium](../../../../../../magnetic-spin-equilibrium.md) with the [corotation radius](../../../../../../corotation-radius.md) near the [magnetospheric truncation radius](../../../../../../magnetospheric-truncation-radius.md):

$$
\boxed{\Omega_{\rm eq}\sim\left(\frac{GM}{r_m^3}\right)^{1/2},\qquad
P_{\rm eq}\sim\frac{2\pi\epsilon^{3/7}\mu^{6/7}}{(GM)^{5/7}\dot M^{3/7}}.}
$$

The precise balance depends on accreted and ejected [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and on the extent of magnetic coupling across the [accretion disk](../../../../../../accretion-disk.md); corotation is a scaling estimate, not an exact zero-torque theorem.
