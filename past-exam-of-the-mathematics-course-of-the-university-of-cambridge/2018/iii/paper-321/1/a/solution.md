<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) released in a [black-hole accretion](../../../../../../black-hole-accretion.md) flow reaching an inner radius $r_{\rm in}$ is of order $GM/r_{\rm in}$. For a circular [Keplerian orbit](../../../../../../kepler-orbit.md), the binding energy is more precisely $GM/(2r_{\rm in})$, with the other half of the potential-energy decrease retained as orbital [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). Multiplying by the [mass accretion rate](../../../../../../mass-accretion-rate.md) gives the [accretion luminosity](../../../../../../accretion-luminosity.md) scale

$$
L_{\rm acc}\sim\frac{GM\dot M}{r_{\rm in}}.
$$

For an inner radius of order the [Schwarzschild radius](../../../../../../schwarzschild-radius.md), $r_{\rm in}\sim2GM/c^2$, this becomes

$$
\boxed{L_{\rm acc}\sim\dot M c^2.}
$$

This is an energy-release scaling, not unit [radiative efficiency of black-hole accretion](../../../../../../radiative-efficiency-of-black-hole-accretion.md). The actual inner orbit, stresses and advected energy determine the coefficient and the emitted luminosity; the [innermost stable circular orbit](../../../../../../innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md) of a [Schwarzschild black hole](../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) lies outside its horizon.
