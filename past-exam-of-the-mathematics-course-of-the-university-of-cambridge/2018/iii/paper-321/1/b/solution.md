<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take $v>0$ to mean the magnitude of the inward radial velocity. In the [Gaussian units](../../../../../../gaussian-units.md) used for the [magnetic pressure](../../../../../../magnetic-pressure.md), approximate the [surface density of a disk](../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) by $\Sigma\simeq2H\rho$. The [mass accretion rate](../../../../../../mass-accretion-rate.md) and [free-fall speed](../../../../../../free-fall-speed.md) then give

$$
\rho\simeq\frac{\dot M}{4\pi rHv}=\frac{\dot M}{4\pi\epsilon r^2v},\qquad
v=\left(\frac{2GM}{r}\right)^{1/2}.
$$

At the [magnetospheric truncation radius](../../../../../../magnetospheric-truncation-radius.md), balance the [magnetic pressure](../../../../../../magnetic-pressure.md) of the [magnetic dipole field](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-field.md) with the specified [ram pressure](../../../../../../ram-pressure.md):

$$
\frac{\mu^2}{8\pi r_m^6}\simeq\frac12\rho v^2\simeq\frac{\dot M v}{8\pi\epsilon r_m^2}.
$$

Thus

$$
r_m^{7/2}\simeq\frac{\epsilon\mu^2}{\dot M\sqrt{2GM}},\qquad
r_m\simeq\left(\frac{\epsilon^2\mu^4}{2GM\dot M^2}\right)^{1/7}.
$$

Discarding the convention-dependent numerical factor gives

$$
\boxed{r_m\sim\left(\frac{\epsilon^2\mu^4}{GM\dot M^2}\right)^{1/7}.}
$$

The [magnetic pressure](../../../../../../magnetic-pressure.md) grows inward as $r^{-6}$, faster than the [ram pressure](../../../../../../ram-pressure.md) $\propto r^{-5/2}$, so the field disrupts the flow inside this radius. The numerical coefficient depends on the vertical averaging and on treating the inflow as radial [free fall](../../../../../../free-fall.md), as stipulated here; a detailed [thin disk](../../../../../../thin-disk.md) boundary model need not have the same coefficient.
