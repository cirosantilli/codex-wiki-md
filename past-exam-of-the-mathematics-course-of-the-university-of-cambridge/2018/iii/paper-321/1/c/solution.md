<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [corotation radius](../../../../../../corotation-radius.md) is where the stellar spin matches [Keplerian rotation](../../../../../../keplerian-disk.md):

$$
r_{\rm co}=\left(\frac{GM}{\Omega_m^2}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

A parcel forced into corotation at $r_m$ has outward [centrifugal acceleration](../../../../../../centrifugal-acceleration.md) $\Omega_m^2r_m$. This exceeds the inward [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md) when

$$
\boxed{r_m>r_{\rm co}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\Omega_m>\Omega_K(r_m).}
$$

This is the onset condition for a centrifugal [propeller effect](../../../../../../propeller-effect.md). It does not by itself guarantee escape to infinity. For example, a parcel released with only azimuthal speed $\Omega_m r_m$ has nonnegative [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) only if

$$
\frac12\Omega_m^2r_m^2-\frac{GM}{r_m}\geq0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad r_m\geq2^{1/3}r_{\rm co}.
$$

Additional magnetic work, radial motion or [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) can alter that escape condition.
