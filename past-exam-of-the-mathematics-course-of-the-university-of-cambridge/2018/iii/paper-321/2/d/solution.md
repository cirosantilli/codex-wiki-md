<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For [slow cooling of an astrophysical disk](../../../../../../slow-cooling-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md), let $t_{\rm cool}\gg\Omega^{-1}$ and assume a vertically symmetric laminar column with no significant radial mass exchange. Its vertical velocity is of order $H/t_{\rm cool}$, so the vertical [material derivative](../../../../../../material-derivative.md) of that velocity is of order $H/t_{\rm cool}^2$. Relative to gravity or [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) acceleration $\sim\Omega^2H$, the inertial correction is $O[(\Omega t_{\rm cool})^{-2}]$.

The [hydrostatic approximation](../../../../../../hydrostatic-approximation.md) therefore holds to leading order while the column slowly cools and contracts. Non-turbulent evolution removes the prescribed turbulent heating, but not the compressional work in the [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) energy equation:

$$
\boxed{\partial_zP=-\Omega^2z\rho,\qquad
\frac1{\gamma-1}\left(D_tP-\frac{\gamma P}{\rho}D_t\rho\right)=-AT^{1+\beta},\qquad
P=\mathcal R\rho T.}
$$

The [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) must still determine the slow vertical flow; $D_t$ is not generally $\partial_t$ at fixed height. This is a quasi-static approximation for a well-prepared, mechanically stable column, with fast free oscillations or growing [convection](../../../../../../convection.md) excluded from the assumed slow solution.
