<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take $\gamma>1$ for the [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md). At fixed [cylindrical radius](../../../../../../cylindrical-radius.md), put $\mathcal R=k/(\mu m_p)$, where $k$ is the [Boltzmann constant](../../../../../../boltzmann-constant.md), $\mu$ the [mean molecular weight](../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight.md) and $m_p$ the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) mass. Also write $a=9\alpha\Omega/4$. Vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md) and the [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) law require

$$
\frac{dP}{dz}=-\Omega^2z\rho,\qquad aP=AT^{1+\beta},\qquad P=\mathcal R\rho T.
$$

Consequently, with $K=A/a$,

$$
P=KT^{1+\beta},\qquad\rho=\frac K{\mathcal R}T^\beta,\qquad
(1+\beta)\mathcal R\frac{dT}{dz}=-\Omega^2z.
$$

Integrating from the midplane yields

$$
T=T_0-\frac{\Omega^2z^2}{2(1+\beta)\mathcal R},\qquad
H^2=\frac{2(1+\beta)\mathcal R T_0}{\Omega^2}.
$$

The [polytropic vertical structure in stellar gravity](../../../../../../polytropic-vertical-structure-in-stellar-gravity.md) therefore has, for $|z|\leq H$,

$$
\boxed{T=T_0\left(1-\frac{z^2}{H^2}\right),\quad
\rho=\rho_0\left(1-\frac{z^2}{H^2}\right)^\beta,\quad
P=P_0\left(1-\frac{z^2}{H^2}\right)^{1+\beta},}
$$

where

$$
P_0=\frac{4A}{9\alpha\Omega}T_0^{1+\beta},\qquad
\rho_0=\frac{P_0}{\mathcal R T_0}.
$$

This is a [polytropic equation of state](../../../../../../polytropic-equation-of-state.md) $P\propto\rho^{1+1/\beta}$ with [polytropic index](../../../../../../polytropic-index.md) $\beta$. For $|z|>H$, set $\rho=P=0$; [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) has no physical gas value in that vacuum. Here $H$ is the finite semi-thickness, rather than an exponential [disk scale height](../../../../../../disk-scale-height.md).
