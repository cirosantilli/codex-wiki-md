<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [Keplerian shearing sheet](../../../../../../keplerian-shearing-sheet.md) background $\boldsymbol U=-3\Omega x\boldsymbol e_y/2$, the only spatial derivative is $\partial_xU_y=-3\Omega/2$. Since $U_x=0$ and all fields are independent of $y$, $\boldsymbol U\cdot\nabla\boldsymbol U=0$ and $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol U=0$.

The [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md) exactly balances the tidal term:

$$
-2\Omega\boldsymbol e_z\times\boldsymbol U=-3\Omega^2x\boldsymbol e_x.
$$

With constant [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) and $\theta=0$, the [buoyancy displacement variable](../../../../../../buoyancy-displacement-variable.md) equation also has zero advection, zero source $U_x$ and zero diffusion. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol u=-\frac32\Omega x\boldsymbol e_y,\qquad\theta=0,\qquad P=\text{constant}}
$$

is a steady solution. Its [absolute vorticity](../../../../../../absolute-vorticity.md) is $\boldsymbol\omega_0=(\Omega/2)\boldsymbol e_z$, since the background [relative vorticity](../../../../../../relative-vorticity.md) is $-3\Omega\boldsymbol e_z/2$.
