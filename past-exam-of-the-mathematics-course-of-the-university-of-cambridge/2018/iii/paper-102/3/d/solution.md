<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An [automorphism of a root system](../../../../../../automorphism-of-a-root-system.md) outside the [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) is the sign change

$$
\boxed{\tau(\varepsilon_1)=\varepsilon_1,\qquad
\tau(\varepsilon_2)=\varepsilon_2,\qquad
\tau(\varepsilon_3)=-\varepsilon_3.}
$$

It preserves the set $\{\pm\varepsilon_i\pm\varepsilon_j\}$, fixes $\alpha_1$, and interchanges $\alpha_2$ and $\alpha_3$. It therefore realizes the nontrivial symmetry of the [Dynkin diagram](../../../../../../dynkin-diagram.md) while preserving the chosen [root basis](../../../../../../fundamental-system-of-a-root-system.md).

To verify that $\tau\notin W$, note that $s_1$ swaps $\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2$, $s_2$ swaps $\varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_3$, and $s_3$ swaps $\varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_3$ while changing both their signs. Every product of these generators is a signed permutation with an even number of sign changes, whereas $\tau$ has one sign change. Thus it cannot be a [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) element. The parity here concerns sign changes, rather than the determinant of the permutation.
