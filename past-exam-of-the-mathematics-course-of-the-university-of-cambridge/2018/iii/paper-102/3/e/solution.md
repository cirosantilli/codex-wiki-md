<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [Dynkin diagram](../../../../../../dynkin-diagram.md) in part (b) is a three-node chain, hence $D_3=A_3$. By the classification of complex [semisimple Lie algebras](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) by their [root systems](../../../../../../root-system.md), this gives the [Isomorphism between so6 and sl4](../../../../../../isomorphism-between-so6-and-sl4.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak{so}_6\cong\mathfrak{sl}_4.}
$$

There is also a concrete realization. On $U=\mathbb C^4$, choose a nonzero alternating four-form $\mathrm{vol}$. The six-dimensional [exterior square](../../../../../../exterior-square.md) $\Lambda^2U$ carries the [nondegenerate](../../../../../../nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) [symmetric bilinear form](../../../../../../symmetric-bilinear-form.md) $B$ specified by

$$
B(u\wedge v,w\wedge z)=\mathrm{vol}(u,v,w,z).
$$

It is symmetric because exchanging two pairs entails four transpositions, and it is nondegenerate because each basis bivector pairs nontrivially with its complementary pair. The [special linear Lie algebra](../../../../../../special-linear-lie-algebra.md) acts by $A(u\wedge v)=Au\wedge v+u\wedge Av$. Its action on $\Lambda^4U$ is multiplication by $\operatorname{tr}A=0$, so it preserves $B$. We obtain a nonzero [Lie algebra homomorphism](../../../../../../lie-algebra-homomorphism.md) $\mathfrak{sl}_4\to\mathfrak{so}(\Lambda^2U,B)$. Its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is an [ideal of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md), hence zero because $\mathfrak{sl}_4$ is a [simple Lie algebra](../../../../../../simple-lie-algebra.md). Both sides have dimension $15$, so this homomorphism is a [Lie algebra isomorphism](../../../../../../lie-algebra-isomorphism.md).
