<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [dominant integral weight](../../../../../../dominant-integral-weight.md) $\lambda$, let $V(\lambda)$ be the finite-dimensional irreducible [highest-weight representation](../../../../../../highest-weight-representation.md) with [highest weight](../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\lambda$. Set $\rho=\frac12\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}\alpha$, the [half-sum of positive roots](../../../../../../half-sum-of-positive-roots.md). With $\alpha^\vee$ the [coroot](../../../../../../coroot.md) of $\alpha$, the [Weyl dimension formula](../../../../../../weyl-dimension-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dim V(\lambda)=\prod_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}
\frac{\langle\lambda+\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}
{\langle\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}.}
$$

Here $\Phi^+$ is the [positive system of a root system](../../../../../../positive-system-of-a-root-system.md) specified by the chosen [root basis](../../../../../../fundamental-system-of-a-root-system.md). The [fundamental weights](../../../../../../fundamental-weight.md) are the basis dual to the simple [coroots](../../../../../../coroot.md): $\langle\omega_i,\alpha_j^\vee\rangle=\delta_{ij}$. Thus $\lambda=\sum_i a_i\omega_i$ with nonnegative integers $a_i$. The denominators are positive; equivalently one can use $(\lambda+\rho,\alpha)/(\rho,\alpha)$ in each factor, since the coroot normalization cancels.
