<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a finite-dimensional complex [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$, the following three conditions are equivalent descriptions of a [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md).

First, its [solvable radical](../../../../../../radical-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is zero. An [ideal of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is a linear subspace $I$ satisfying $[\mathfrak g,I]\subseteq I$. A [solvable Lie algebra](../../../../../../solvable-lie-algebra.md) is one whose [derived series](../../../../../../derived-series.md), defined by $I^{(0)}=I$ and $I^{(r+1)}=[I^{(r)},I^{(r)}]$, eventually becomes zero. The [solvable radical](../../../../../../radical-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is the largest solvable [ideal of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md); its vanishing is equivalent to the absence of nonzero solvable ideals.

Second, the [Killing form](../../../../../../killing-form.md)

$$
\kappa(u,v)=\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}u\operatorname{ad}v)
$$

is a [nondegenerate bilinear form](../../../../../../nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md): if $\kappa(u,v)=0$ for every $v$, then $u=0$. Here $\operatorname{ad}u(v)=[u,v]$ is the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), and $\operatorname{tr}$ is the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md). This equivalence is the [Cartan criterion for semisimplicity](../../../../../../cartan-criterion-for-semisimplicity.md).

Third, $\mathfrak g$ is a [direct sum of Lie algebras](../../../../../../direct-sum-of-lie-algebras.md) that are nonabelian [simple Lie algebras](../../../../../../simple-lie-algebra.md). A [simple Lie algebra](../../../../../../simple-lie-algebra.md) is nonabelian and has no [ideals of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md) other than zero and itself. Thus the three equivalent criteria are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rad}\mathfrak g=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\kappa\text{ nondegenerate}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\mathfrak g=\bigoplus_j\mathfrak g_j\text{ with each }\mathfrak g_j\text{ simple}.}
$$
