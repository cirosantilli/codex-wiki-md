<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose the given [short root](../../../../../../short-root.md) $\alpha$ as a [simple root](../../../../../../simple-root.md), and let $\beta$ be the long [simple root](../../../../../../simple-root.md). This entails no loss of generality: the [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) is transitive on the [short roots](../../../../../../short-root.md) of the [G2 root system](../../../../../../g2-root-system.md). The relevant [Cartan integers](../../../../../../cartan-integer.md) are $\langle\beta,\alpha^\vee\rangle=-3$ and $\langle\alpha,\beta^\vee\rangle=-1$. The [positive roots](../../../../../../positive-root.md) are

$$
\alpha,\ \beta,\ \alpha+\beta,\ 2\alpha+\beta,\ 3\alpha+\beta,\ 3\alpha+2\beta.
$$

Under $\mathfrak m_\alpha\cong\mathfrak{sl}_2$, the [root space](../../../../../../root-space.md) for $m\alpha+n\beta$ has $h_\alpha$ eigenvalue $2m-3n$. The subalgebra $\mathfrak m_\alpha$ itself is the three-dimensional irreducible $V(2)$ in the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md).

The four [root spaces](../../../../../../root-space.md) along the [root string](../../../../../../root-string.md) $\beta,\alpha+\beta,2\alpha+\beta,3\alpha+\beta$ have eigenvalues $-3,-1,1,3$. Consecutive spaces are connected by nonzero [raising operators](../../../../../../raising-operator.md) and [lowering operators](../../../../../../lowering-operator.md), so their sum is $V(3)$. The negative [root string](../../../../../../root-string.md) supplies another $V(3)$. The [root spaces](../../../../../../root-space.md) for $\pm(3\alpha+2\beta)$ have eigenvalue zero, and neither adding nor subtracting $\alpha$ gives a root; they are two copies of $V(0)$. Finally, the one-dimensional space $\ker\alpha\subset\mathfrak t$ commutes with $\mathfrak m_\alpha$, giving one more $V(0)$. We have accounted for all $14$ dimensions, and therefore the [G2 adjoint branching to a short-root sl2 subalgebra](../../../../../../g2-adjoint-branching-to-a-short-root-sl2-subalgebra.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak g\cong V(3)^{\oplus2}\oplus V(2)\oplus V(0)^{\oplus3}.}
$$

For a nonzero [root vector](../../../../../../root-vector.md) $x\in\mathfrak g_\alpha$, its [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) action is a nonzero scalar multiple of the [raising operator](../../../../../../raising-operator.md) on each summand. On each irreducible $V(n)$ this operator has a one-dimensional [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md), including $V(0)$. Since the [Lie algebra centralizer](../../../../../../centralizer-of-an-element-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is that [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md),

$$
\boxed{\dim Z_{\mathfrak g}(x)=2+1+3=6.}
$$
