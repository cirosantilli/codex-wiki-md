<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The only possible rational roots of the monic cubic are $1,-1$, and neither is a root. Thus $f$ is irreducible. The [discriminant of a depressed cubic](../../../../../../discriminant-of-a-depressed-cubic.md) gives

$$
\Delta=-4\cdot2^3-27\cdot1^2=-59.
$$

It is not a rational square. By the permitted [cubic discriminant criterion](../../../../../../cubic-discriminant-criterion.md), the splitting field has [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) $S_3$, degree six, and contains its quadratic subfield generated by $\sqrt\Delta$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-59})\subseteq L,\qquad
[L:K]=3,\qquad\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)=A_3\cong C_3.}
$$

For a rational prime $r\ne59$, the reduction of $f$ is separable because its discriminant is a unit at $r$. Choose a finite extension of $\mathbb F_r$ in which that reduction splits. Part 1(a) lifts it to an [unramified extension](../../../../../../unramified-extension.md) of $\mathbb Q_r$, and the [Hensel lemma](../../../../../../hensel-s-lemma.md) lifts all the simple roots into that extension. The local splitting field is therefore unramified over $\mathbb Q_r$. In particular all primes of $L/K$ above such $r$ are unramified.

At $59$, the supplied simple residue root $-28$ lifts to a root in $\mathbb Q_{59}$. The remaining factor is quadratic, so the local splitting field has degree at most two over $\mathbb Q_{59}$. It contains $\sqrt{-59}$, which is not a square in $\mathbb Q_{59}$ because its valuation is odd. It is therefore exactly $\mathbb Q_{59}(\sqrt{-59})$. This is also the completion of $K$ at its unique prime above $59$: the polynomial $T^2+59$ is [Eisenstein](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md) there. Thus

$$
L_{\mathfrak P}=K_{\mathfrak p}=\mathbb Q_{59}(\sqrt{-59})
$$

for a prime $\mathfrak P$ above $\mathfrak p$. All the other primes above it give the same local conclusion by Galois conjugacy. The relative extension is therefore unramified at $59$ as well; indeed that prime splits completely in $L/K$.

The field $K$ has only complex places, so there is no infinite-place ramification. Hence $\boxed{L/K\text{ is unramified everywhere}}$.

We use the [Hilbert class field](../../../../../../hilbert-class-field.md) theorem: for a number field $E$, its maximal everywhere unramified abelian extension $H_E$ is finite, contains every such extension, and satisfies $\operatorname{Gal}(H_E/E)\cong\operatorname{Cl}_E$, so $[H_E:E]=h_E$. The cyclic degree-three extension just constructed is contained in $H_K$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{3\mid h_K,\qquad h_K>1.}
$$

This is the [unramified cubic extension of Q of square root minus fifty-nine](../../../../../../unramified-cubic-extension-of-q-of-square-root-minus-fifty-nine.md); only divisibility of the class number, rather than its exact value, is needed here.
