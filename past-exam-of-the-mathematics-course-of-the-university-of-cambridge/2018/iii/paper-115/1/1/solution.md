↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [tangent bundle](../../../../../../tangent-bundle.md) is $TM=\bigsqcup_{p\in M}T_pM$, with its smooth vector-bundle structure from coordinate charts. A [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is a smooth section of this bundle. The [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) is their commutator as derivations:

$$
[X,Y](h)=X(Yh)-Y(Xh).
$$

In coordinates, $[X,Y]^k=X^j\partial_jY^k-Y^j\partial_jX^k$.

The four numbered conditions here are hypotheses of one argument, so the following sections assemble its proof. Let $j:\partial M\hookrightarrow M$. Because all $X_i$ are tangent to the boundary, evaluating $\eta=\iota_{X_1}\cdots\iota_{X_n}\omega$ on a tangent vector $Y$ there evaluates the [volume form](../../../../../../volume-form.md) on $n+1$ vectors in the $n$-dimensional boundary tangent space. Alternation gives $\boxed{j^*\eta=0}$. This uses the [interior product of a differential form](../../../../../../interior-product.md) and will make the boundary restriction of the [de Rham cohomology](../../../../../../de-rham-cohomology.md) class vanish.
