<h1 id="1/1/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

At any point, the linearly independent vectors $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ can be completed by $Y$ to a basis of $T_pM$. A nowhere-zero [volume form](../../../../../../../volume-form.md) is nonzero on every basis, so

$$
\eta_p(Y)=\omega_p(X_n,\ldots,X_1,Y)\ne0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{\eta\text{ is nowhere zero}}$. In particular, if it is an [exact differential form](../../../../../../../exact-differential-form.md) $df$, its potential has no [critical points](../../../../../../../critical-point.md). This is the role of pointwise independence in the [boundary obstruction for commuting volume-preserving vector fields](../../../../../../../boundary-obstruction-for-commuting-volume-preserving-vector-fields.md).
