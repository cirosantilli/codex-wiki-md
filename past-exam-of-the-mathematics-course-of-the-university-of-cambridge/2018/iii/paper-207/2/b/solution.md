↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The difference of the [Bernoulli distribution](../../../../../../bernoulli-distribution.md) variances factors as

$$
v_0-v_1=p_0(1-p_0)-p_1(1-p_1)
=(p_0-p_1)(1-p_0-p_1).
$$

The first factor is positive, whereas $p_0>1-p_1$ makes the second negative. Thus $v_0<v_1$ and $R^*=\sqrt{v_0/v_1}<1$. Therefore **[Neyman allocation](../../../../../../neyman-allocation.md) assigns more patients to treatment 1, the inferior treatment**. Minimizing the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) of a treatment comparison and maximizing the number of desirable responses are different objectives.
