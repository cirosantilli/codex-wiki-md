<h1 id="4/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

At an added [potential event time](../../../../../../../potential-event-time.md) with no event and a nonempty [risk set](../../../../../../../risk-set.md), $d_j=0$, so [maximum likelihood estimation](../../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimation.md) gives $\widehat q_j=0$. Its factor in the [Kaplan–Meier estimator](../../../../../../../kaplan-meier-estimator.md) is therefore $1-d_j/r_j=1$.

Adding or deleting such times also leaves the [risk sets](../../../../../../../risk-set.md) at actual event times unchanged: those depend on the observed event and censoring histories. Consequently **the fitted [survivor function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md) depends only on observed event times and their [risk sets](../../../../../../../risk-set.md)**. Beyond the last observed follow-up, the tail is not identified; the standard flat continuation is a convention, rather than an additional consequence of likelihood maximization.
