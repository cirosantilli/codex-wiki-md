<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With no [censoring](../../../../../../../censoring-statistics.md) or new entry within the interval, all $r$ individuals can be classified at its end: $d$ have experienced the event and $r-d$ remain event-free. Thus the empirical conditional [survival probability](../../../../../../../survival-probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{\widehat{\mathbb P}(T>t_R\mid T>t_L)=\frac{r-d}{r}=1-\frac dr.}
$$

This assumes $r>0$ and that the observed individuals represent the group whose conditional [survival probability](../../../../../../../survival-probability.md) is being estimated.
