<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An individual is [right-censored](../../../../../../../right-censoring.md) at $t$ when follow-up establishes that the event has not occurred through $t$, but does not reveal the subsequent event time. The observed information is $T>t$ for the continuous-time convention.

Knowing only the total number censored inside an interval does not specify the [risk sets](../../../../../../../risk-set.md) at the event times. For example, start with two individuals, one event and one censoring. If censoring precedes the event, the [Kaplan–Meier estimator](../../../../../../../kaplan-meier-estimator.md) contributes $1-1/1=0$; if the event precedes censoring, it contributes $1-1/2=1/2$. The totals $r=2,d=1,c=1$ are identical.

Therefore **the timing of [right censoring](../../../../../../../right-censoring.md) relative to the events is needed**. [Independent censoring](../../../../../../../independent-censoring.md) is also required for these changing [risk sets](../../../../../../../risk-set.md) to yield a valid estimate of the underlying [survivor function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md).
