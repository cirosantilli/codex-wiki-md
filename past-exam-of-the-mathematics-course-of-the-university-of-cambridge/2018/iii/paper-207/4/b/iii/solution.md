<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**The estimate remains $(r-d)/r$.** Individuals [right-censored](../../../../../../../right-censoring.md) at $t_R$ are observed throughout the preceding interval, so they contribute to each [risk set](../../../../../../../risk-set.md) until the end. They must not be removed from the denominator at $t_L$.

If events and [right censoring](../../../../../../../right-censoring.md) share the recorded time $t_R$, use the usual [Kaplan–Meier estimator](../../../../../../../kaplan-meier-estimator.md) convention: count events first, using a [risk set](../../../../../../../risk-set.md) that still includes those censored at that time, and then remove the censored observations. With no earlier [censoring](../../../../../../../censoring-statistics.md), the product of event factors over the interval telescopes to $(r-d)/r$.
