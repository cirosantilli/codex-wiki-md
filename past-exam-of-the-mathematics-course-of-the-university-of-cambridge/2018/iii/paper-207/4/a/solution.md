↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [survivor function](../../../../../../survival-function.md) of a nonnegative event time is

$$
\boxed{F(t)=\mathbb P(T>t)=\int_t^\infty f_T(u)\,du.}
$$

Here the paper uses $F$ for the [survival function](../../../../../../survival-function.md), rather than for the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md); the latter is $1-F(t)$ for a continuous distribution. The [survivor function](../../../../../../survival-function.md) is nonincreasing, starts at 1 when there is no atom at zero, and tends to zero for an almost surely finite event time.
