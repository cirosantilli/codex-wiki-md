<h1 id="6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Condition on the event time $T_A=u$. [Independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) makes the chance that $T_B$ is still later equal to its [survivor function](../../../../../../survival-function.md) $F_B(u)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\pi_A=\mathbb P(T_A<T_B)=\int_0^\infty f_A(u)F_B(u)\,du.}
$$

The continuous distributions give zero probability of a tie. The complementary probability is $\pi_B=\int_0^\infty F_A(u)f_B(u)\,du=1-\pi_A$.
