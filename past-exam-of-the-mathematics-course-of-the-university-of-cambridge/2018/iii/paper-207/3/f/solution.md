<h1 id="3/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Two useful ways to strengthen the [causal inference](../../../../../../causal-inference-split.md) are:

- **Run a [randomized controlled trial](../../../../../../randomized-controlled-trial.md) of the proposed dietary intervention.** Assign a well-defined change in [calcium intake](../../../../../../calcium-intake.md), measure its achieved effect on [serum calcium](../../../../../../serum-calcium.md), and prospectively ascertain [migraine](../../../../../../migraine.md) outcomes. [Randomization](../../../../../../randomization.md) addresses baseline [confounding](../../../../../../confounding.md), and measuring the intermediate [exposure](../../../../../../exposure.md) tests whether the intervention actually changes the quantity implicated by the genetic analysis. This directly evaluates the proposed dietary intervention rather than assuming equivalence with a lifelong genetic shift.
- **Replicate and challenge the genetic result in independent data.** Use a prespecified [two-sample Mendelian randomization](../../../../../../two-sample-mendelian-randomization.md) analysis with additional well-supported [genetic variants](../../../../../../genetic-variant.md), examine associations with possible [confounders](../../../../../../confounder.md), and perform leave-one-variant-out and [horizontal pleiotropy](../../../../../../horizontal-pleiotropy.md) [sensitivity analyses](../../../../../../sensitivity-analysis.md). In particular, removing the strongest [serum calcium](../../../../../../serum-calcium.md) variant tests whether the result depends on one possibly invalid instrument. Replication checks reproducibility, while [within-family Mendelian randomization](../../../../../../within-family-mendelian-randomization.md) can reduce [population stratification](../../../../../../population-stratification.md) and family-level genetic/environmental biases.

These are complementary: the first changes the proposed [exposure](../../../../../../exposure.md) experimentally, and the second probes the validity and reproducibility of its genetic evidence.
