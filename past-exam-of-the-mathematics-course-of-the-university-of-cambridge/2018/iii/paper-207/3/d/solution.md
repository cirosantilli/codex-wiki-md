The supplementary excerpt uses **an adjusted [observational study](../../../../../../observational-study.md) and a two-sample [Mendelian randomization](../../../../../../mendelian-randomization.md) analysis**.

The [observational study](../../../../../../observational-study.md) examines co-occurrence of [hypercalcaemia](../../../../../../hypercalcaemia.md) and [migraine](../../../../../../migraine.md) diagnoses in electronic health records, adjusting for age, sex, and ancestry. Its adjusted [odds ratio](../../../../../../odds-ratio.md) is 1.58, with $P=4.75\times10^{-13}$.

The [two-sample Mendelian randomization](../../../../../../two-sample-mendelian-randomization.md) analysis combines the associations of eight [genetic variants](../../../../../../genetic-variant.md) with [serum calcium](../../../../../../serum-calcium.md) and with [migraine](../../../../../../migraine.md), using a weighted [genetic risk score](../../../../../../genetic-risk-score.md). It estimates an [odds ratio](../../../../../../odds-ratio.md) of 1.80 per genetically predicted 1 mg/dL higher [serum calcium](../../../../../../serum-calcium.md), with 95% [confidence interval](../../../../../../confidence-interval.md) $(1.31,2.46)$. The [weighted-median Mendelian randomization estimator](../../../../../../weighted-median-mendelian-randomization-estimator.md) and [MR-Egger regression](../../../../../../mr-egger-regression.md) provide [sensitivity analyses](../../../../../../sensitivity-analysis.md).

The measured and instrumented [exposure](../../../../../../exposure.md) is **[serum calcium](../../../../../../serum-calcium.md), rather than dietary [calcium intake](../../../../../../calcium-intake.md) itself**. [Calcium intake](../../../../../../calcium-intake.md) and [serum calcium](../../../../../../serum-calcium.md) are not interchangeable interventions, and an [odds ratio](../../../../../../odds-ratio.md) of 1.80 is an odds contrast rather than necessarily an 80% increase in probability. The shortened excerpt supplies two approaches; a further analysis in the full research paper is outside this excerpt.
