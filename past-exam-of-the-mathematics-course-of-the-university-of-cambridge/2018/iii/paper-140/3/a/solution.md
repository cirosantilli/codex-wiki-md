<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the usual convention that the [smooth isotopy](../../../../../../smooth-isotopy.md) starts at $\rho_0=\mathrm{id}$. The [exterior derivative](../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md) commutes with a [pullback of a differential form](../../../../../../pullback-of-a-differential-form.md), so

$$
d(\rho_t^*\lambda-\lambda)
=\rho_t^*d\lambda-d\lambda
=\omega-\rho_t^*\omega.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\rho_t^*\lambda-\lambda\text{ is closed for every }t
\iff \rho_t^*\omega=\omega\text{ for every }t.}
$$

The right-hand condition says precisely that $\rho_t$ is a [symplectic isotopy](../../../../../../symplectic-isotopy.md).

Here an [exact symplectic manifold](../../../../../../exact-symplectic-manifold.md) is necessarily noncompact in positive dimension: if $\dim M=2n$ and $\omega=-d\lambda$, then $\omega^n=-d(\lambda\wedge\omega^{n-1})$, contradicting the positive [symplectic volume](../../../../../../symplectic-volume.md) and the [Generalized Stokes theorem](../../../../../../generalized-stokes-theorem.md) on a [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) without boundary. Thus the paper's introductory word “closed” must here be read as “without boundary”, as its parenthesis suggests, rather than as including [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md).
