<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The level equation is $|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=|z_0|^2$. In particular $z_0\ne0$, so the global affine coordinates on this level are $w_j=z_j/z_0$. They identify it with

$$
N=\{(w_1,w_2)\in\mathbb C^2:|w_1|^2+|w_2|^2=1\}=S^3.
$$

The diagonal [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) action becomes $(w_1,w_2)\mapsto(tw_1,tw_2)$ and is free: at least one coordinate is nonzero, so fixing a point forces $t=1$. Since the [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md), the [Free proper Lie-group action theorem](../../../../../../free-proper-lie-group-action-theorem.md) makes the quotient a smooth two-dimensional [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md).

The map $(w_1,w_2)\mapsto[w_1,w_2]$ is the [Hopf fibration](../../../../../../hopf-fibration.md). It is onto the [complex projective line](../../../../../../complex-projective-line.md), and two unit representatives determine the same complex line precisely when they differ by a scalar of modulus one, hence by a [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) orbit. Local sections, for example $[1,u]\mapsto(1,u)/\sqrt{1+|u|^2}$, show that the induced identification is a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{M_{\mathrm{red}}=\mu^{-1}(-1/4)/S^1\cong\mathbb{CP}^1\cong S^2.}
$$

This identifies the [symplectic reduction](../../../../../../symplectic-reduction.md) as a [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md); no computation of its reduced [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) is needed.
