<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

On $N=\mu^{-1}(-1/4)$, the diagonal [fundamental vector field](../../../../../../fundamental-vector-field.md) $X$ is nonzero, since the [fixed-point set](../../../../../../fixed-point-set.md) lies at [moment map](../../../../../../moment-map.md) values $0$ and $-1/2$. The [nondegenerate bilinear form](../../../../../../nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) $\omega_p$ and $d\mu=\iota_X\omega$ imply $d\mu\ne0$, so $-1/4$ is a [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md). By the [regular level set theorem](../../../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md), $N$ is a three-dimensional [embedded submanifold](../../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md) of the four-dimensional [Complex projective plane](../../../../../../complex-projective-plane.md).

At $p\in N$, set $W=T_pN=\ker d\mu_p$. Then $\omega(X,v)=0$ for every $v\in W$, so $X\in W^\omega$, the [symplectic orthogonal complement](../../../../../../symplectic-orthogonal-complement.md). This complement is one-dimensional and $d\mu(X)=\omega(X,X)=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{(T_pN)^\omega=\mathbb RX_p\subset T_pN.}
$$

Thus **the level set is a [coisotropic submanifold](../../../../../../coisotropic-submanifold.md), neither an [isotropic submanifold](../../../../../../isotropic-submanifold.md) nor a [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md)**. Indeed an [isotropic subspace of a symplectic vector space](../../../../../../isotropic-subspace-of-a-symplectic-vector-space.md) in dimension four has dimension at most two, whereas $\dim N=3$. Its [characteristic line field](../../../../../../characteristic-line-field.md) is generated by the diagonal [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) action.
