<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $X\in\mathfrak h$, the [fundamental vector field](../../../../../../fundamental-vector-field.md) of the restricted [Lie group action](../../../../../../lie-group-action.md) is $(iX)_M$. If $\phi$ is the original [moment map](../../../../../../moment-map.md), then

$$
d\langle\pi\circ\phi,X\rangle_H
=d\langle\phi,iX\rangle_G
=\iota_{(iX)_M}\omega
=\iota_{X_M}\omega.
$$

This is the defining [Hamiltonian action](../../../../../../hamiltonian-group-action.md) identity, in the convention $\iota_{X_H}\omega=dH$.

If equivariance is included in the definition of a [moment map](../../../../../../moment-map.md), it is also preserved: the [dual map](../../../../../../transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) $\pi=i^*$ intertwines the [coadjoint actions](../../../../../../coadjoint-representation.md) of $H$, since $i(\operatorname{Ad}_hX)=\operatorname{Ad}_h(iX)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\phi_H=i^*\phi=\pi\circ\phi}
$$

is a [moment map](../../../../../../moment-map.md) for the restricted [Hamiltonian action](../../../../../../hamiltonian-group-action.md) of the [Lie subgroup](../../../../../../lie-subgroup.md) $H$.
