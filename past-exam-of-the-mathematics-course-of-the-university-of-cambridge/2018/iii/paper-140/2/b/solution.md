<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the decomposition into [Lagrangian subspaces](../../../../../../lagrangian-subspace.md) $V=V_+\oplus V_-$ from part (a). The [bilinear form](../../../../../../bilinear-form.md) $\Omega$ pairs $V_+$ and $V_-$ nondegenerately: a [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) in either summand annihilating the other already annihilates its own summand and hence all of $V$, so is zero.

Choose a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ of $V_+$. The [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md)

$$
V_-\longrightarrow V_+^*,\qquad f\longmapsto\bigl(e\longmapsto\Omega(e,f)\bigr)
$$

is an [linear isomorphism](../../../../../../linear-isomorphism.md). Let $f_j$ correspond to the [dual basis](../../../../../../dual-basis.md) of the $e_i$. Then $\Omega(e_i,f_j)=\delta_{ij}$, and the pairings within each summand vanish. Thus

$$
\boxed{(e_1,\ldots,e_n,f_1,\ldots,f_n)\text{ is a symplectic basis},\quad Se_i=e_i,\quad Sf_i=-f_i.}
$$
