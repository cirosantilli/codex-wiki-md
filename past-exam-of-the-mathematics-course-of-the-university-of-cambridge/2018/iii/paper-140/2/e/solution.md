<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

As usual for [Complex projective space](../../../../../../complex-projective-space.md) and [Real projective space](../../../../../../real-projective-space.md), [homogeneous coordinates](../../../../../../homogeneous-coordinate.md) are represented by nonzero vectors. Componentwise [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) descends to the [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md)

$$
\tau:\mathbb{CP}^n\longrightarrow\mathbb{CP}^n,\qquad [z]\longmapsto[\overline z].
$$

It is well defined because $\overline{\lambda z}=\overline\lambda\,\overline z$, and $\tau^2=I$. Every standard [manifold chart](../../../../../../manifold-chart.md) is invariant under $\tau$, with $\varphi_i\circ\tau=\sigma\circ\varphi_i$. Part (d) and the gluing of the [Fubini-Study form](../../../../../../fubini-study-form.md) therefore imply $\tau^*\omega_{FS}=-\omega_{FS}$.

A point $[z]$ in the [fixed-point set](../../../../../../fixed-point-set.md) satisfies $\overline z=\lambda z$. Applying [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) again gives $|\lambda|=1$. Choose $a\ne0$ with $a/\overline a=\lambda$; then $\overline{az}=az$, so $[z]$ has a real representative. Conversely every real representative determines a point in the [fixed-point set](../../../../../../fixed-point-set.md). Hence $\operatorname{Fix}(\tau)=\mathbb{RP}^n$, and part (c) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb{RP}^n\subset(\mathbb{CP}^n,\omega_{FS})\text{ is a Lagrangian submanifold}.}
$$
