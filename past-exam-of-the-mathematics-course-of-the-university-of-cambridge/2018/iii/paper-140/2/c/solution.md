<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $p\in\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma)$, differentiation gives $(d\sigma_p)^2=I$ and $(d\sigma_p)^*\omega_p=-\omega_p$. Thus $d\sigma_p$ is an [anti-symplectic involution](../../../../../../anti-symplectic-involution.md) of the [symplectic vector space](../../../../../../symplectic-vector-space.md) $T_pM$. By part (a), its $+1$ [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) is a [Lagrangian subspace](../../../../../../lagrangian-subspace.md).

In the local [manifold chart](../../../../../../manifold-chart.md) supplied in the question, $\sigma$ is linear, so its [fixed-point set](../../../../../../fixed-point-set.md) is the intersection of the chart with this $+1$ [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md). Therefore the [fixed-point set](../../../../../../fixed-point-set.md) is an [embedded submanifold](../../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md) and

$$
T_p\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma)=\ker(d\sigma_p-I).
$$

Every such [tangent space](../../../../../../tangent-space.md) has half the ambient dimension and the [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) vanishes on it. Hence **every nonempty [fixed-point set](../../../../../../fixed-point-set.md) is a [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md)**. The [fixed-point set](../../../../../../fixed-point-set.md) itself need not be [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md).
