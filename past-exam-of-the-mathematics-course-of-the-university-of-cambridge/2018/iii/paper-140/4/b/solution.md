<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [cotangent fiber translation](../../../../../../cotangent-fiber-translation.md) $f$ is a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md), with inverse $(x,\xi)\mapsto(x,\xi-\beta_x)$. Because $\pi\circ f=\pi$, the definition of the [canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle](../../../../../../canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) gives

$$
f^*\lambda=\lambda+\pi^*\beta.
$$

Taking the [exterior derivative](../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md) yields

$$
\boxed{f^*\omega=\omega-\pi^*d\beta.}
$$

If $\beta$ is a [closed differential form](../../../../../../closed-differential-form.md), this is $f^*\omega=\omega$, proving that **the [cotangent fiber translation](../../../../../../cotangent-fiber-translation.md) is a [symplectomorphism](../../../../../../symplectomorphism.md)**.
