<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\pi:T^*X\to X$ be the [cotangent bundle](../../../../../../cotangent-bundle.md) projection and $\lambda$ its [canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle](../../../../../../canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md), defined by $\lambda_{(x,\xi)}(v)=\xi(d\pi(v))$. Use the [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) $\omega=-d\lambda$, so in local coordinates $\omega=\sum_jdx_j\wedge d\xi_j$.

The graph map $s_\beta(x)=(x,\beta_x)$ is a [smooth embedding](../../../../../../smooth-embedding.md), with $s_\beta^*\lambda=\beta$, and hence

$$
s_\beta^*\omega=-d\beta.
$$

Its image has dimension $\dim X$, half the dimension of $T^*X$. By the definition of a [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md),

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{graph}(\beta)\text{ is Lagrangian}\iff d\beta=0.}
$$

This is the [graph of a closed one-form is Lagrangian](../../../../../../graph-of-a-closed-one-form-is-lagrangian.md) criterion. The opposite conventional sign for the [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) gives the same criterion.
