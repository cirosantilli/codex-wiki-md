<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the [exact differential form](../../../../../../exact-differential-form.md) as $\beta=dh$ and take the [cotangent fiber translation](../../../../../../cotangent-fiber-translation.md) family $\rho_t(x,\xi)=(x,\xi+t\,dh_x)$. Its generating [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is vertical and in local coordinates is

$$
X=\sum_j\frac{\partial h}{\partial x_j}\frac{\partial}{\partial\xi_j}.
$$

With $\omega=\sum_jdx_j\wedge d\xi_j$ and the [Hamiltonian vector field](../../../../../../hamiltonian-vector-field.md) convention from Question 3,

$$
\boxed{\iota_X\omega=-\pi^*dh=dH,\qquad H=-h\circ\pi.}
$$

Thus $\rho_t$ is a [Hamiltonian isotopy](../../../../../../hamiltonian-isotopy.md) and $f=\rho_1$ is a [Hamiltonian diffeomorphism](../../../../../../hamiltonian-isotopy.md). The [local flow](../../../../../../local-flow.md) is explicitly defined for every real $t$, regardless of [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md). With the alternative convention $\iota_{X_H}\omega=-dH$, the same family is generated by $H=h\circ\pi$.

The definition in the question does not require compact support for the [Hamiltonian function](../../../../../../hamiltonian-function.md); the displayed [Hamiltonian diffeomorphism](../../../../../../hamiltonian-isotopy.md) uses that definition.
