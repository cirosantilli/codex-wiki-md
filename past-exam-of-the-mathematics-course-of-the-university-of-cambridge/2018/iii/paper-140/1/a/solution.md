<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Moser theorem](../../../../../../moser-s-trick.md) says that if $M$ is a [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) without boundary and $\omega_t$, $0\leq t\leq1$, is a smooth family of [symplectic forms](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) with constant [de Rham cohomology](../../../../../../de-rham-cohomology.md) class, then there is a [smooth isotopy](../../../../../../smooth-isotopy.md) $\psi_t$, with $\psi_0=\mathrm{id}$, such that

$$
\boxed{\psi_t^*\omega_t=\omega_0.}
$$

Thus the endpoints are [symplectomorphic](../../../../../../symplectomorphism.md). The hypothesis is a whole path of [symplectic forms](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md), not merely equality of the endpoint [de Rham cohomology](../../../../../../de-rham-cohomology.md) classes.
