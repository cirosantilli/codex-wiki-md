<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [symplectomorphism](../../../../../../symplectomorphism.md) is a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md), so it preserves the [genus of a surface](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md). It also preserves the [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) determined by the [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) and, by integration of a [pullback of a differential form](../../../../../../pullback-of-a-differential-form.md), preserves the [symplectic area](../../../../../../symplectic-area.md).

Conversely, equal [genus of a surface](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) gives a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) $f:\Sigma_0\to\Sigma_1$. We can choose $f$ to preserve the [orientations](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) determined by $\omega_0,\omega_1$: if necessary, compose with an [orientation-reversing diffeomorphism](../../../../../../orientation-reversing-diffeomorphism.md) of the standard [closed orientable surface](../../../../../../closed-orientable-surface.md) of that [genus of a surface](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md), obtained by reflection of a symmetric handle model. On $\Sigma_0$ put $\eta=f^*\omega_1$. Both $\eta$ and $\omega_0$ are positive [volume forms](../../../../../../volume-form.md) for the same [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md), and their integrals agree. By [top-degree de Rham cohomology](../../../../../../top-degree-de-rham-cohomology.md), $[\eta]=[\omega_0]$.

The family $\omega_t=(1-t)\omega_0+t\eta$ consists of positive [volume forms](../../../../../../volume-form.md), hence [symplectic forms](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) in dimension two, with constant [de Rham cohomology](../../../../../../de-rham-cohomology.md) class. [Moser theorem](../../../../../../moser-s-trick.md) supplies $\psi$ with $\psi^*\eta=\omega_0$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{(f\circ\psi)^*\omega_1=\omega_0.}
$$

Therefore **the [genus of a surface](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) and [symplectic area](../../../../../../symplectic-area.md) classify [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) [symplectic surfaces](../../../../../../symplectic-surface.md)**.
