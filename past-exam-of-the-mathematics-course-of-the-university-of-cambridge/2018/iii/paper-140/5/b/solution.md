<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At a point $p$, write $W=T_pL$ and take the [orthogonal complement](../../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) with respect to the [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md) $g$. The [compatible triple](../../../../../../compatible-triple.md) identity gives

$$
g(Ju,v)=\omega(u,v).
$$

If $L$ is a [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md), then $\omega(u,v)=0$ for $u,v\in W$, so $JW\subset W^\perp$. Both have dimension $\frac12\dim M$, hence equality.

Conversely, if $JW=W^\perp$, equality of dimensions gives $\dim W=\frac12\dim M$, and $g(Ju,v)=0$ for $u,v\in W$ gives $\omega|_W=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{L\text{ is Lagrangian}\iff J(T_pL)=(T_pL)^\perp\text{ for every }p.}
$$

A positive-definite [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) has $W\cap W^\perp=\{0\}$. Hence $W\cap JW=\{0\}$, proving that **every [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md) is a [totally real submanifold](../../../../../../totally-real-submanifold.md) for a [compatible almost complex structure](../../../../../../compatible-almost-complex-structure.md)**.
