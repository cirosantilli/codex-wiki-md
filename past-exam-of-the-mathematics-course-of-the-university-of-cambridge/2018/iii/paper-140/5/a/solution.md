<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [compatible almost complex structure](../../../../../../compatible-almost-complex-structure.md) is a smooth bundle map $J:TM\to TM$ such that, for all [tangent vectors](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) $u,v$,

$$
\boxed{J^2=-I,\qquad\omega(Ju,Jv)=\omega(u,v),\qquad\omega(u,Ju)>0\quad(u\ne0).}
$$

It determines the [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md)

$$
\boxed{g(u,v)=\omega(u,Jv).}
$$

Indeed, the invariance of the [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) under $J$ and its antisymmetry imply $\omega(v,Ju)=\omega(u,Jv)$, so $g$ is symmetric; the positivity condition makes it positive definite. A [compatible triple](../../../../../../compatible-triple.md) consists of this [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md), this [compatible almost complex structure](../../../../../../compatible-almost-complex-structure.md), and this [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md). Useful equivalent identities are $\omega(u,v)=g(Ju,v)$ and $g(Ju,Jv)=g(u,v)$.
