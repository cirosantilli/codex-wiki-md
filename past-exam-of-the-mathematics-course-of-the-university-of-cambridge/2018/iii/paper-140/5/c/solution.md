<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use $\mathbb C^2$ with the [standard symplectic form](../../../../../../standard-symplectic-form.md) $\omega_0=dx_1\wedge dy_1+dx_2\wedge dy_2$ and the [compatible almost complex structure](../../../../../../compatible-almost-complex-structure.md) given by multiplication by $i$. Consider the [embedded submanifold](../../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md)

$$
\boxed{L=\{(s+it,t):s,t\in\mathbb R\}.}
$$

In coordinates $(x_1,y_1,x_2,y_2)$, its [tangent space](../../../../../../tangent-space.md) is $W=\{(a,b,b,0):a,b\in\mathbb R\}$. Applying $J$ gives $J(a,b,b,0)=(-b,a,0,b)$. If this is again in $W$, its fourth coordinate forces $b=0$, and its second and third coordinates then force $a=0$. Thus $W\cap JW=0$ and $\dim_\mathbb R L=2$: $L$ is a [totally real submanifold](../../../../../../totally-real-submanifold.md).

However, the [pullback of a differential form](../../../../../../pullback-of-a-differential-form.md) under the parametrization is

$$
\boxed{\omega_0|_L=ds\wedge dt\ne0.}
$$

Thus **this [totally real submanifold](../../../../../../totally-real-submanifold.md) is not a [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md)**.
