<h1 id="14b/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

At each fixed time, $|\psi(x,t)|^2$ is a bell-shaped [Gaussian](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) centred at $x=h(t)$, with width determined by $\operatorname{Re}F(t)>0$. Completing the square as in part (d) makes the density symmetric about $h(t)$. Therefore its position [expectation value](../../../../../../expectation-value.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle\widehat x\rangle_\psi=h(t)}.
$$

The sketch is a Gaussian peak whose centre oscillates between the two turning positions found in part (e), while its width varies periodically through $\operatorname{Re}F(t)$.
