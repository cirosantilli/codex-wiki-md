<h1 id="14b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For

$$
\psi=N(t)e^{-F(t)x^2+G(t)x},
$$

direct differentiation gives

$$
\psi_x=(-2Fx+G)\psi,
\qquad
\psi_{xx}=(4F^2x^2-4FGx+G^2-2F)\psi
$$

and

$$
\psi_t=\left(\frac{\dot N}{N}-\dot F x^2+\dot Gx\right)\psi.
$$

Substitution into the Schrodinger equation and equality of the coefficients of $x^2,x,1$ give the necessary and sufficient [Riccati equation](../../../../../../riccati-equation.md) system

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
i\hbar\dot F&=\frac{2\hbar^2}{m}F^2-\frac12m\omega^2,\\
i\hbar\dot G&=\frac{2\hbar^2}{m}FG,\\
i\hbar\frac{\dot N}{N}
&=\frac{\hbar^2}{m}F-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}G^2.
\end{aligned}}
$$
