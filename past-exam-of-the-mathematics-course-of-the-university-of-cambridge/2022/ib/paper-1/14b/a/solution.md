↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the one-dimensional [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md),

$$
\boxed{
i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial t}
=\left(
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}
+\frac12m\omega^2x^2
\right)\psi}.
$$

This is the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md) with harmonic potential.
