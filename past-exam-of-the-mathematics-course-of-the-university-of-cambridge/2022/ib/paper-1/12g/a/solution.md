<h1 id="12g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The hypothesis makes $\Omega$ [star-shaped](../../../../../../star-shaped-set.md) with centre $z_0$. For small $h$ with the triangle having vertices $z_0,w,w+h$ contained in $\Omega$, the vanishing triangular integral gives

$$
g(w+h)-g(w)=\int_{[w,w+h]}f(z)\,dz.
$$

Parametrizing the final segment,

$$
\frac{g(w+h)-g(w)}h
=\int_0^1f(w+th)\,dt
\longrightarrow f(w)
$$

by continuity. Thus $g$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) and

$$
\boxed{g'(w)=f(w)}.
$$

This is the triangle-integral construction behind [Morera's theorem](../../../../../../morera-s-theorem.md).
