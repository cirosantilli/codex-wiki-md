<h1 id="8f/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [adjoint operator](../../../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) $\alpha^*$ is defined by

$$
\langle\alpha v,w\rangle=\langle v,\alpha^*w\rangle
\qquad(v,w\in V).
$$

Since $V$ is a nonzero finite-dimensional complex vector space, $\alpha$ has an [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $v$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$. Normality gives

$$
\|(\alpha-\lambda I)x\|^2
=\|(\alpha^*-\overline\lambda I)x\|^2
$$

for every $x$, by expanding both sides and using $\alpha\alpha^*=\alpha^*\alpha$. Taking $x=v$ yields

$$
\boxed{\alpha v=\lambda v,\qquad
\alpha^*v=\overline\lambda v}.
$$
