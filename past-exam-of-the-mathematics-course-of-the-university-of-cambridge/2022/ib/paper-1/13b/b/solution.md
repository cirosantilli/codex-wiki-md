<h1 id="13b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With gravity omitted, the initial data contain only the first fixed-end [normal mode](../../../../../../normal-mode.md), so write

$$
y(x,t)=q(t)\sin\frac{\pi x}{l}.
$$

Then

$$
q''+2kq'+\left(\frac{\pi c}{l}\right)^2q=0,
\qquad q(0)=0,\quad q'(0)=A.
$$

Because $k=\pi c/l$, this oscillator is [critically damped](../../../../../../critical-damping.md), and

$$
q(t)=Ate^{-kt}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x,t)=Ate^{-kt}\sin\frac{\pi x}{l}}.
$$
