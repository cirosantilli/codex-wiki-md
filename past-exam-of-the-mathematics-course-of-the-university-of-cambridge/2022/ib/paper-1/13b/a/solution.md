<h1 id="13b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take positive $y$ vertically downward. For a short string element, the transverse tension resultant is $T y_{xx}\,dx$, while gravity and linear drag contribute $\mu g\,dx$ and $-2k\mu y_t\,dx$. [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) gives

$$
\mu y_{tt}\,dx
=Ty_{xx}\,dx-2k\mu y_t\,dx+\mu g\,dx.
$$

Since $T=\mu c^2$, the [damped wave equation](../../../../../../linearly-damped-string.md) is

$$
\boxed{y_{tt}+2ky_t-c^2y_{xx}=g},
\qquad
y(0,t)=y(l,t)=0.
$$

Choosing upward displacement reverses the sign of the gravity term.
