↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

This is always true. Given $\varepsilon>0$, choose $N_A,N_B$ such that

$$
|f_n(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon
$$

on $A$ for $n\geq N_A$ and on $B$ for $n\geq N_B$. For $n\geq\max(N_A,N_B)$ the same inequality holds at every point of $X=A\cup B$. Hence $f_n\to f$ [uniformly](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) on $X$.
