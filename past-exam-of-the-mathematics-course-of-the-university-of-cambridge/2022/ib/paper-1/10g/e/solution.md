This is always true. Since $f$ is bounded and $f_n\to f$ uniformly, there are $M$ and $N$ such that

$$
|f(x)|\leq M,\qquad |f_n(x)|\leq M+1
$$

for every $x\in X$ and $n\geq N$. The continuous function $g$ is [uniformly continuous](../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md) on the compact interval $[-M-1,M+1]$. Therefore, given $\varepsilon>0$, a sufficiently small uniform bound on $|f_n-f|$ implies

$$
\sup_{x\in X}|g(f_n(x))-g(f(x))|<\varepsilon.
$$

**Hence $g\circ f_n\to g\circ f$ uniformly.**
