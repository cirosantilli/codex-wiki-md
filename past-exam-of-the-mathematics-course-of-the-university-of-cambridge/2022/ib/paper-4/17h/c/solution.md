<h1 id="17h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The null value $\pi=1/2$ is interior and the model is regular. [Wilks theorem](../../../../../../wilks-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{W\xrightarrow{d}\chi_1^2}
$$

under the null, because the alternative adds one free parameter. An asymptotic size-$\alpha$ test rejects when

$$
\boxed{W>\chi^2_{1,1-\alpha}}.
$$
