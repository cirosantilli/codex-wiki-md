<h1 id="5d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The divergence equation requires $B_3=0$, and the wave equation requires

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=c^2k^2}.
$$

For propagation in the positive $z$ direction, Faraday's law gives

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf E=\frac\omega k(B_2,-B_1,0)\cos(kz-\omega t)}.
$$

**Thus $\mathbf E$, $\mathbf B$, and the propagation direction are mutually perpendicular.**
