<h1 id="12a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Repeated integration by parts gives

$$
\mathcal L\{t^n\}(s)=\frac n s\mathcal L\{t^{n-1}\}(s),
\qquad \mathcal L\{1\}=\frac1s,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L\{t^n\}=\frac{n!}{s^{n+1}}}.
$$

Multiplication by $e^{at}$ shifts the transform:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L\{e^{at}t^n\}(s)=\frac{n!}{(s-a)^{n+1}}}.
$$
