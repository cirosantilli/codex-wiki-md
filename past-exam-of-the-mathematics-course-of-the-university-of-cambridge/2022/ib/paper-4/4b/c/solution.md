<h1 id="4b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The higher state has principal quantum number $l+2$ and the lower one $l+1$. Energy conservation for the emitted [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) gives

$$
\boxed{
\nu=\frac{me^4}{2h(4\pi\varepsilon_0)^2\hbar^2}
\left(\frac1{(l+1)^2}-\frac1{(l+2)^2}\right)}.
$$
