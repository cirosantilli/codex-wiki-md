<h1 id="4b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substitute $g=r^ae^{-br}$ into the radial [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) and equate the coefficients of $r^{-2}$, $r^{-1}$, and the constant term. Regularity selects

$$
a=l,
\qquad
b=\frac{me^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\hbar^2(l+1)}.
$$

The corresponding bound-state energy is

$$
\boxed{
E_l=-\frac{me^4}{2(4\pi\varepsilon_0)^2\hbar^2(l+1)^2}}.
$$
