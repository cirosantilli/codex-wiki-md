<h1 id="15b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md) is $i\hbar\partial_t\psi=\widehat H\psi$. Differentiate $\langle\widehat A\rangle=\langle\psi|\widehat A|\psi\rangle$, use the equation and its adjoint, and include explicit time dependence of the operator. This gives [Ehrenfest theorem](../../../../../../ehrenfest-theorem.md)

$$
\boxed{i\hbar\frac d{dt}\langle\widehat A\rangle
=\langle[\widehat A,\widehat H]\rangle
+i\hbar\left\langle\frac{\partial\widehat A}{\partial t}\right\rangle}.
$$
