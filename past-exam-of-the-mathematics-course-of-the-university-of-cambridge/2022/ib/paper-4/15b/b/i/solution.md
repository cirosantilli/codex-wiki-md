<h1 id="15b/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Substitution of the stated phase into

$$
i\hbar\psi_t=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\psi_{xx}+mgx\psi
$$

cancels the potential and scalar phase terms, leaving

$$
\boxed{i\hbar\Phi_t
=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\Phi_{xx}+i\hbar gt\Phi_x}.
$$
