<h1 id="15b/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The phase has unit modulus and $X=x+gt^2/2$, hence

$$
\langle X\rangle_\Theta=\langle x\rangle_\psi+\frac12gt^2.
$$

The free-particle equations imply $\langle X\rangle=a+vt$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\langle x\rangle_\psi=a+vt-\frac12gt^2}.
$$

The wavepacket centre obeys the classical constant-acceleration trajectory, an instance of the [correspondence principle](../../../../../../../correspondence-principle.md).
