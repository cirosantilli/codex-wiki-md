<h1 id="14b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The standard contour transform

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x}{x^2+\alpha^2}e^{-ikx}dx
=-i\pi\operatorname{sgn}(k)e^{-\alpha|k|}
$$

holds for $\operatorname{Re}\alpha>0$, with the appropriate upper or lower pole chosen according to the sign of $k$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{m(x)=\frac{x}{x^2+\alpha^2}}.
$$
