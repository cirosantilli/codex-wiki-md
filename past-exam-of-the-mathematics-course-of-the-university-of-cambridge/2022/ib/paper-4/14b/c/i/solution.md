<h1 id="14b/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fourier transformation in $x$ changes Laplace's equation into

$$
\widetilde u_{yy}-k^2\widetilde u=0.
$$

Imposing the two boundary values gives

$$
\boxed{
\widetilde u(k,y)=
\frac{\widetilde f(k)\sinh(|k|(a-y))
+\widetilde g(k)\sinh(|k|y)}{\sinh(|k|a)}}.
$$

The value at $k=0$ is understood by continuity.
