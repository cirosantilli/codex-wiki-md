The [Poincare disc model](../../../../../../poincare-disk-model.md) has metric

$$
ds^2=\frac{4(dx^2+dy^2)}{(1-x^2-y^2)^2}.
$$

Rotations about the origin are hyperbolic isometries, so a Euclidean circle centred there is a hyperbolic circle. Its radius is the radial length

$$
\boxed{R=\int_0^r\frac{2,dt}{1-t^2}
=\log\frac{1+r}{1-r}=2\operatorname{artanh}r}.
$$
