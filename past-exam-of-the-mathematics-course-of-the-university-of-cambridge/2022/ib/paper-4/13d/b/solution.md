<h1 id="13d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate

$$
Q=f-\left(f_{y'}-\frac d{dx}f_{y''}\right)y'-f_{y''}y''.
$$

The product rule cancels all terms except

$$
Q'=y'\left(f_y-\frac d{dx}f_{y'}+\frac{d^2}{dx^2}f_{y''}\right)+f_x.
$$

On an extremal with no explicit $x$ dependence both terms vanish, proving the stated conserved quantity.
