<h1 id="6c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Symmetry gives nodes $\pm c$ with equal weights $b$. Exactness for $1$ and $x^2$ requires

$$
2b=\int_{-1}^1x^2dx=\frac23,
\qquad
2bc^2=\int_{-1}^1x^4dx=\frac25.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{b_1=b_2=\frac13,
\qquad c_1=-\sqrt{\frac35},
\quad c_2=\sqrt{\frac35}}.
$$

Odd moments vanish automatically, so the rule is exact through degree three.
