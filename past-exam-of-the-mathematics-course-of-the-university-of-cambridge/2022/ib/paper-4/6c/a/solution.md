<h1 id="6c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\ell_i$ be the degree-$n-1$ cardinal polynomial with $\ell_i(c_j)=\delta_{ij}$. Exactness applied to the nonnegative polynomial $\ell_i^2$ gives

$$
b_i=\sum_jb_j\ell_i(c_j)^2
=\int_a^bw(x)\ell_i(x)^2\,dx>0,
$$

because $w>0$ and $\ell_i$ is nonzero. Thus every [Gaussian quadrature](../../../../../../gaussian-quadrature.md) weight is positive.
