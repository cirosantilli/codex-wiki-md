<h1 id="7h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a transient irreducible chain, every state $j$ is transient, so the expected number of visits satisfies

$$
\sum_{n=0}^\infty P^n(i,j)=\mathbb E_iV_j<\infty.
$$

The terms are nonnegative, and every convergent series has terms tending to zero. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P^n(i,j)\to0}.
$$
