<h1 id="7h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $q=\mathbb P_i(T_i<\infty)$. By the [Strong Markov property](../../../../../../strong-markov-property.md), after every return to $i$ the chance of another return is again $q$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P_i(V_i=k)=(1-q)q^{k-1},
\qquad k=1,2,\ldots}.
$$

**Thus $V_i$ is geometric with success parameter $1-q$; when $q=1$, it is infinite almost surely.**
