<h1 id="2f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let

$$
F(x,y,z)=x^2+y^2+z^3+az+b.
$$

By the [regular level set theorem](../../../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md), $F^{-1}(0)$ is a smooth surface wherever $\nabla F\ne0$. A critical point on the level set would satisfy

$$
x=y=0,
\qquad a=-3z^2,
\qquad b=2z^3.
$$

These equations imply $4a^3+27b^2=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{4a^3+27b^2\ne0\Longrightarrow S_{a,b}\text{ is a smooth surface}.}
$$
