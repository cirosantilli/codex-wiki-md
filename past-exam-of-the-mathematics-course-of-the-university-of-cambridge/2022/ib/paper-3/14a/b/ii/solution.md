<h1 id="14a/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Reflect the source point $\mathbf r_0=(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ across the plane to $\mathbf r_0^*=(x_0,y_0,-z_0)$. Equal-sign source and image make the normal derivatives cancel on the plane. Thus the [method of images](../../../../../../../method-of-images.md) gives the Neumann Green function

$$
\boxed{
G(\mathbf r;\mathbf r_0)
=-\frac1{4\pi}
\left(\frac1{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0|}
+\frac1{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0^*|}\right)},
\qquad
\left.\frac{\partial G}{\partial z}\right|_{z=0}=0.
$$
