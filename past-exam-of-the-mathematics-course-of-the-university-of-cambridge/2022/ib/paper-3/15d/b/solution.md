↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Transforming the [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) by $F'=\Lambda F\Lambda^T$ and reading off its components gives

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&E_1'=E_1,
&&E_2'=\gamma(E_2-vB_3),
&&E_3'=\gamma(E_3+vB_2),\\
&B_1'=B_1,
&&B_2'=\gamma\left(B_2+\frac{vE_3}{c^2}\right),
&&B_3'=\gamma\left(B_3-\frac{vE_2}{c^2}\right).
\end{aligned}}
$$

These are the parallel and transverse [field transformation laws](../../../../../../lorentz-transformation-of-electromagnetic-fields.md) for a boost in the $x$ direction.
