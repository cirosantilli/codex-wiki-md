<h1 id="6b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a free particle, the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is

$$
H=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2}{dx^2}.
$$

Since $\chi''=-k^2\chi$,

$$
H\chi=\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m}\chi.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{E=\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m}\geq0,
\qquad p=\hbar k},
$$

where the momentum follows because $-i\hbar\,d\chi/dx=\hbar k\chi$.
