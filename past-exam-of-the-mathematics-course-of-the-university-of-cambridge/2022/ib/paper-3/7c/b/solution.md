↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a two-dimensional incompressible flow, the [stream function](../../../../../../stream-function.md) convention

$$
u_r=\frac1r\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial\theta},
\qquad
u_\theta=-\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial r}
$$

is satisfied by

$$
\boxed{\psi(r,\theta)=\frac{d\sin\theta}{r}+C}.
$$
