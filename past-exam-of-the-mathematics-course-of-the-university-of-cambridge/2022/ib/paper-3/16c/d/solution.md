<h1 id="16c/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The vorticity is normal to the plane, so the stretching term vanishes. Axisymmetry also makes angular advection vanish, and part (a) gives no radial advection. Hence the scalar [vorticity equation](../../../../../../vorticity-equation.md) is the radial [diffusion equation](../../../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md)

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\partial\omega}{\partial t}
=\nu\left(\frac{\partial^2\omega}{\partial r^2}
 +\frac1r\frac{\partial\omega}{\partial r}\right)}.
$$
