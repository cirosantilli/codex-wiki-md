<h1 id="16c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Axisymmetry and [incompressibility](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) give

$$
0=\nabla\cdot\mathbf u
=\frac1r\frac{d}{dr}(ru_r).
$$

Thus $ru_r=C$. Finiteness at the origin forces $C=0$, so

$$
\boxed{u_r=0}.
$$
