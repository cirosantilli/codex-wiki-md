<h1 id="9f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $\alpha v=\lambda v$ for nonzero $v$, then

$$
\alpha^3v=\lambda^3v,
$$

so $\lambda^3$ is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md). Let $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$. Since $\lambda\ne0$, the three factors of

$$
x^3-\lambda^3=(x-\lambda)(x-\omega\lambda)(x-\omega^2\lambda)
$$

are pairwise coprime. The [kernel decomposition for coprime polynomials](../../../../../../kernel-decomposition-for-coprime-polynomials.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{
E_{\lambda^3}(\alpha^3)
=E_\lambda(\alpha)\oplus E_{\omega\lambda}(\alpha)
\oplus E_{\omega^2\lambda}(\alpha)},
$$

where an absent eigenspace is interpreted as zero.
