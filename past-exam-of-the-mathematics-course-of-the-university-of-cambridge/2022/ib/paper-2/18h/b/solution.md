<h1 id="18h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The self-loop at zero makes the irreducible chain [aperiodic](../../../../../../aperiodic-markov-chain.md). The convergence theorem for irreducible, aperiodic, positive recurrent countable-state chains gives

$$
\mathbb P(X_n=i)\longrightarrow\pi_i.
$$

Because $X$ and $Y$ are [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md),

$$
\mathbb P(X_n=0,Y_n=1)
=\mathbb P(X_n=0)\mathbb P(Y_n=1)
\longrightarrow\pi_0\pi_1.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb P(X_n=0,Y_n=1)
=\frac12\cdot\frac14=\frac18}.
$$
