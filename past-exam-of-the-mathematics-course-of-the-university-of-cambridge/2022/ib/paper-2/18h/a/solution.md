<h1 id="18h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The chain is an irreducible [birth-death process](../../../../../../birth-death-process.md). The [detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md) equations are

$$
\pi_i\frac13=\pi_{i+1}\frac23,
$$

so $\pi_{i+1}=\pi_i/2$. Normalization gives the [stationary distribution](../../../../../../stationary-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{\pi_i=2^{-(i+1)},\qquad i\geq0}.
$$

An irreducible countable-state Markov chain that possesses a stationary probability distribution is [positive recurrent](../../../../../../positive-recurrent-state.md). Hence $X$ is positive recurrent.
