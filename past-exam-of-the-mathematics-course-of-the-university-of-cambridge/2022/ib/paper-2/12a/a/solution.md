<h1 id="12a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Close the contour in the upper half-plane. The degree assumption and [Jordan lemma](../../../../../../jordan-s-lemma.md) make the integral over the large semicircle tend to zero, while the absence of real zeros avoids indentations of the contour. The [residue theorem](../../../../../../residue-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}R(x)e^{ix}\,dx
=2\pi i\sum_{\substack{Q(z_k)=0\\ \operatorname{Im}z_k>0}}
\operatorname{Res}_{z=z_k}\bigl(R(z)e^{iz}\bigr)}.
$$

Repeated roots are handled by the usual higher-order [residue](../../../../../../residue.md) formula.
