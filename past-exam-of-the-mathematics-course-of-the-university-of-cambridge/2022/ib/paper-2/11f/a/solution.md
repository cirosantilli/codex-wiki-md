<h1 id="11f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With

$$
X(u,v)=(\sinh u\cos v,\sinh u\sin v,v),
$$

the coordinate tangent vectors satisfy

$$
X_u\cdot X_u=\cosh^2u,
\qquad X_u\cdot X_v=0,
\qquad X_v\cdot X_v=\cosh^2u.
$$

Thus the [first fundamental form](../../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) is

$$
\boxed{ds^2=\cosh^2u\,(du^2+dv^2)}.
$$

Geometrically, $S$ is the half of a [helicoid](../../../../../../helicoid.md) with positive radius, winding upwards indefinitely.

Consider the upper half of the [catenoid](../../../../../../catenoid.md), parametrized as the [surface of revolution](../../../../../../surface-of-revolution.md)

$$
Y(u,v)=(\cosh u\cos v,\cosh u\sin v,u),
\qquad u,v>0.
$$

It has the same first fundamental form, so $(u,v)\mapsto Y(u,v)$ defines a [local isometry](../../../../../../local-isometry.md). By the invariance of [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) under local isometry, the curvatures agree; directly, the conformal metric above gives

$$
\boxed{K=-\operatorname{sech}^4u}.
$$
