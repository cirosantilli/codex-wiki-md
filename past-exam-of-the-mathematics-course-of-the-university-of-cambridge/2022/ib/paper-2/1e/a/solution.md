<h1 id="1e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The zero element is torsion. If $m,n\in T$, choose nonzero $r,s\in R$ with $rm=0$ and $sn=0$. Since $R$ is an [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md), $rs\ne0$, and

$$
rs(m+n)=s(rm)+r(sn)=0.
$$

**Thus $m+n\in T$. If $a\in R$, then $r(am)=a(rm)=0$, so $am\in T$. Hence $T$ is closed under addition and scalar multiplication and is an [submodule](../../../../../../primary-submodule.md) of $M$.**
