<h1 id="16d/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By cylindrical symmetry the field is azimuthal and constant on a circle of radius $r$. The [Ampère's law](../../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) for a steady current gives

$$
2\pi r B=\mu_0I,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}\,\mathbf e_\phi}.
$$

In the plane $x=0$ with $y>0$, it points in the $-x$ direction.
