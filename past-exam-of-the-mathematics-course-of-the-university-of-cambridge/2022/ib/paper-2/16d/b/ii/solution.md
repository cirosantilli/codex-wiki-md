<h1 id="16d/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At time $t$, let the centre of the loop have coordinate $Y=d+vt$. With normal $+\mathbf e_x$, the [magnetic flux](../../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) is

$$
\Phi_B
=-\int_{Y-a}^{Y+a}\int_{-a}^{a}
\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi y},dz,dy
=-\frac{\mu_0Ia}{\pi}\log\frac{Y+a}{Y-a}.
$$

[Faraday's law](../../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) and [Ohm's law](../../../../../../../ohm-s-law.md) therefore give a current of magnitude

$$
\boxed{
|I_{\rm loop}(t)|
=\frac{2\mu_0Ia^2v}
{\pi R\bigl((d+vt)^2-a^2\bigr)}}.
$$

The original flux points into the plane and decreases in magnitude as the loop recedes. By [Lenz's law](../../../../../../../lenz-s-law.md), the induced current reinforces the into-plane field: it is clockwise when viewed from the $+x$ side.
