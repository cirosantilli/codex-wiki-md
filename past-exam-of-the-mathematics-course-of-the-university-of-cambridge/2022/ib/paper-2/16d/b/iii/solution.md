<h1 id="16d/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Translation parallel to the wire leaves every point of the loop at the same distance from the wire, so its [magnetic flux](../../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) is constant. Equivalently, the line integrals of the motional field $\mathbf u\times\mathbf B$ cancel on opposite sides. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E=0,
\qquad I_{\rm loop}=0}.
$$
