<h1 id="14a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substitution of $y=e^{-x}$ gives

$$
(x+\lambda+1)e^{-x}-(x+\lambda)e^{-x}-e^{-x}=0.
$$

For a second solution $y=ax+b$, the [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) reduces to $a\lambda-b=0$. Taking $a=1$ gives the linearly independent solution

$$
\boxed{y_1=x+\lambda}.
$$
