↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Inside the [infinite square well](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md), the time-independent [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) has [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) at $0$ and $a$. Its normalized [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) and [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../../energy-eigenvalue.md) are

$$
\boxed{
\chi_n(x)=\sqrt{\frac2a}\sin\frac{n\pi x}{a},
\qquad
E_n=\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2ma^2},
\qquad n=1,2,\ldots .}
$$
