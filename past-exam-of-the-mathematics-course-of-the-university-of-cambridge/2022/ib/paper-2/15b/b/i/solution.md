<h1 id="15b/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expand the normalized initial [wavefunction](../../../../../../../wave-function.md) in the orthonormal energy basis:

$$
c_n=\int_0^a\chi_n(x)^*f(x),dx.
$$

The [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md) then gives the [unitary time evolution](../../../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md)

$$
\boxed{
\psi(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty
c_n\chi_n(x)e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}}.
$$
