<h1 id="15b/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The specified initial state is normalized. The requested energy is $E_2$, and its [probability amplitude](../../../../../../../probability-amplitude.md) is

$$
c_2=\int_0^{a/2}
\sqrt{\frac2a}\sin\frac{2\pi x}{a}
\frac2{\sqrt a}\sin\frac{2\pi x}{a},dx
=\frac1{\sqrt2}.
$$

Thus the [Born rule](../../../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(E=E_2)=|c_2|^2=\frac12}.
$$

Unitary evolution changes each energy coefficient only by a phase, so this probability remains $1/2$ at both $T$ and $2T$. If an [energy measurement](../../../../../../../energy-measurement.md) returns $E_2$, the state collapses to the nondegenerate eigenstate $\chi_2$; a subsequent energy measurement therefore returns $E_2$ with probability

$$
\boxed{1}.
$$
