<h1 id="13d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For fixed endpoint values, the first variation of the [functional](../../../../../../functional.md)

$$
I[z]=\int_a^b f(z,z';x)\,dx
$$

vanishes exactly when the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dx}\frac{\partial f}{\partial z'}
-\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}=0}
$$

holds. If $f$ has no explicit $x$ dependence, differentiation along an extremal gives the [Beltrami identity](../../../../../../beltrami-identity.md)

$$
\boxed{f-z'\frac{\partial f}{\partial z'}=\text{constant}}.
$$

For a further constraint $J[z]=J_0$, introduce a constant [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) $\lambda$ and apply the Euler-Lagrange equation to $I+\lambda J$; $\lambda$ is then chosen so that the constraint holds.
