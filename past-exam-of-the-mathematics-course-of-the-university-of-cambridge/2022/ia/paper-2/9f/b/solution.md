↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Normalization of the [Gaussian distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) gives

$$
A=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}.
$$

Completing the square,

$$
tx-\frac{x^2}{2}
=-\frac{(x-t)^2}{2}+\frac{t^2}{2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
M_X(t)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{tx-x^2/2}\,dx
=e^{t^2/2},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{M_X(t)=e^{t^2/2}}.
$$
