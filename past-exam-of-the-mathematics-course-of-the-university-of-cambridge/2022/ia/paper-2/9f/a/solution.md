<h1 id="9f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Because $U$ and $V$ are bounded, their exponential series may be integrated term by term for every real $t$. Thus their [moment-generating functions](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) satisfy

$$
M_U(t)=\mathbb E[e^{tU}]
=\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\frac{t^j}{j!}\mathbb E[U^j],
$$

and similarly for $V$. Equality of every moment gives equality term by term, so

$$
\boxed{M_U(t)=M_V(t)\quad\text{for every }t\in\mathbb R}.
$$
