↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) on $\{1,2,\ldots\}$,

$$
\mathbb P(X=m)=p(1-p)^{m-1}.
$$

Summing the [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md),

$$
G_X(s)
=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}p(1-p)^{m-1}s^m
=\boxed{\frac{ps}{1-(1-p)s}}.
$$
