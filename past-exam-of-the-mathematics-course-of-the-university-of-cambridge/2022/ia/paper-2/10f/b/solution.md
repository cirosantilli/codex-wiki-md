<h1 id="10f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $0\leq s\leq1$, independence gives

$$
G_{X+Y}(s)
=\mathbb E[s^{X+Y}]
=\mathbb E[s^Xs^Y]
=\mathbb E[s^X]\,\mathbb E[s^Y].
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{G_{X+Y}(s)=G_X(s)G_Y(s)}.
$$
