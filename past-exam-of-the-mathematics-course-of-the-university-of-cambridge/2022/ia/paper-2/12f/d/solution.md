<h1 id="12f/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Divide the keystrokes into disjoint blocks of five, and let $A_n$ be the event that block $n$ is exactly HELLO. The events are [independent events](../../../../../../independent-events.md), and

$$
\mathbb P(A_n)=26^{-5}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\mathbb P(A_n)=\infty.
$$

Part (c) shows that infinitely many of these block events occur almost surely. Each occurrence is an occurrence of HELLO in the full typed sequence, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(\text{HELLO appears infinitely often})=1}.
$$
