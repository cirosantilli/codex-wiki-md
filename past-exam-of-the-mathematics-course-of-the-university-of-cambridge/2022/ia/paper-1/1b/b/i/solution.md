↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The required [root of unity](../../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) is

$$
\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}=-\frac12+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}i,
$$

so

$$
1+\omega=\frac12+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}i=e^{i\pi/3}.
$$

Therefore

$$
(1+\omega)^{10}=e^{10\pi i/3}=e^{4\pi i/3}
=\boxed{-\frac12-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}i}.
$$
