↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [triangle inequality](../../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) gives

$$
|a-b|=|(a-z)+(z-b)|\leq |z-a|+|z-b|.
$$

**Hence the left-hand side of the proposed equation is always at least $|a-b|$. There are no solutions when $c<|a-b|$.**
