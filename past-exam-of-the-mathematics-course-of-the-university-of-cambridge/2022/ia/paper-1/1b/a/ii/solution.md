<h1 id="1b/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Equality in the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) holds exactly when the two displacement vectors $a-z$ and $z-b$ point in the same direction. Thus $z$ lies on the closed line segment joining $a$ and $b$. Conversely, every point of that segment satisfies

$$
|z-a|+|z-b|=|a-b|.
$$

The solution set is therefore precisely that segment in the [complex plane](../../../../../../../complex-plane.md).
