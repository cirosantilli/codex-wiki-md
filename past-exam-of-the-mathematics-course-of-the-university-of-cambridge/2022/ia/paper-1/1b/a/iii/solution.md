↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By the focal definition of an [ellipse](../../../../../../../ellipse.md), the solution set is the ellipse with foci $a$ and $b$. Its centre is $(a+b)/2$, its major axis lies along the line through the foci, and its semiaxes are

$$
\frac c2,\qquad \frac12\sqrt{c^2-|a-b|^2}.
$$

The sketch is consequently a [nondegenerate ellipse](../../../../../../../nondegenerate-ellipse.md) symmetric about both the [focal line](../../../../../../../focal-line.md) and its [perpendicular bisector](../../../../../../../perpendicular-bisector.md).
