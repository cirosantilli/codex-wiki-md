<h1 id="2b/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $M_1^TJM_1=J$ and $M_2^TJM_2=J$, then [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) and the transpose rule give

$$
(M_1M_2)^TJ(M_1M_2)
=M_2^T(M_1^TJM_1)M_2
=M_2^TJM_2=J.
$$

Thus the product also satisfies the equation.

The equation and part (i) imply that $M_1$ is invertible. Multiplying  
$M_1^TJM_1=J$ on the left by $(M_1^{-1})^T$ and on the right by $M_1^{-1}$ gives

$$
(M_1^{-1})^TJ M_1^{-1}=J.
$$

**Hence the set of solutions is closed under products and [matrix inverses](../../../../../../../matrix-inverse.md).**
