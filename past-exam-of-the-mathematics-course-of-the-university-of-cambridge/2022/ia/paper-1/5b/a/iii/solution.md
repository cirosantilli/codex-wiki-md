<h1 id="5b/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let

$$
\Delta=A\cdot(B\times C).
$$

Linear independence implies that the [scalar triple product](../../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) $Delta$ is nonzero. Applying the vector triple-product formulas to $(A\times B)\times(C\times D)$ recovers the coefficients of $D$ in the basis $A,B,C$. Equivalently, the [reciprocal basis](../../../../../../../reciprocal-basis.md) to $A,B,C$ is

$$
\frac{B\times C}{\Delta},\qquad
\frac{C\times A}{\Delta},\qquad
\frac{A\times B}{\Delta}.
$$

Taking scalar products with $D$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{
D=
\frac{D\cdot(B\times C)}{\Delta}A
+\frac{D\cdot(C\times A)}{\Delta}B
+\frac{D\cdot(A\times B)}{\Delta}C}.
$$
