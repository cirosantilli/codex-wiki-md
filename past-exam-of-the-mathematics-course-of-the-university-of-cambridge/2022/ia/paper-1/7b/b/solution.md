↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an [orthogonal projection matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) $P=P^\dagger=P^2$,

$$
(x,Px)
=x^\dagger P^\dagger Px
=(Px)^\dagger(Px)
=\lVert Px\rVert^2\geq0.
$$

**Thus $P$ is a [positive semidefinite matrix](../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md).**
