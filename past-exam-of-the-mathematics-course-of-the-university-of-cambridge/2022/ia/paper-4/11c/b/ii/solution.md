<h1 id="11c/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Without friction, [rotational kinetic energy](../../../../../../../rotational-kinetic-energy.md) is conserved:

$$
K_0=\frac12I\omega^2.
$$

The constant angular speed is $\omega=\sqrt{2K_0/I}$, so one full rotation takes

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{2\pi}{\omega}
=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{I}{2K_0}}}.
$$
