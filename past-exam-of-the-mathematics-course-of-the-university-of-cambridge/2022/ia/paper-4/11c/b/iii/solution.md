<h1 id="11c/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The work law means that friction exerts a constant opposing [torque](../../../../../../../torque.md) of magnitude $W$. Until the cone stops,

$$
I\dot\omega=-W.
$$

Its initial angular speed is $\omega_0=\sqrt{2K_0/I}$, and uniform angular deceleration gives

$$
0=\omega_0-\frac WI t.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{t=\frac{I\omega_0}{W}
=\sqrt{\frac{2K_0I}{W^2}}}.
$$
