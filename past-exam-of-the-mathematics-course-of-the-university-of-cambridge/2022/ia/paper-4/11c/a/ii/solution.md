↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The axis $y=0,z=h$ is parallel to the $x$-axis and lies a distance $|h|$ from it. The [parallel axis theorem](../../../../../../../parallel-axis-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{I=\frac14Mr^2+Mh^2
=M\left(\frac{r^2}{4}+h^2\right)}
$$

to leading order in the thickness.
