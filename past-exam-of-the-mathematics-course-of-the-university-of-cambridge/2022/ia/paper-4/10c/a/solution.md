<h1 id="10c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The term $-2\boldsymbol\Omega\times\dot{\mathbf x}$ is the [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md), and $-\boldsymbol\Omega\times(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf x)$ is the [centrifugal acceleration](../../../../../../centrifugal-acceleration.md). With $\boldsymbol\Omega=-\Omega\widehat{\mathbf z}$, the Cartesian components are

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\ddot x&=-2\Omega\dot y+\Omega^2x+\frac{N_x}{m},\\
\ddot y&= 2\Omega\dot x+\Omega^2y+\frac{N_y}{m},\\
\ddot z&=g+\frac{N_z}{m}.
\end{aligned}}
$$
