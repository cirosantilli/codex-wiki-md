<h1 id="7f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Because $P(\mathbf x)\subseteq P(\mathbf x)$, the relation is [reflexive](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md). If $\mathbf x\preceq\mathbf y$ and $\mathbf y\preceq\mathbf z$, then

$$
P(\mathbf x)\subseteq P(\mathbf y)\subseteq P(\mathbf z),
$$

so it is [transitive](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md). It is not [symmetric](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md): for

$$
\mathbf x=(1,0,\ldots,0),
\qquad
\mathbf y=(1,1,0,\ldots,0),
$$

one has $\mathbf x\preceq\mathbf y$ but not $\mathbf y\preceq\mathbf x$.
