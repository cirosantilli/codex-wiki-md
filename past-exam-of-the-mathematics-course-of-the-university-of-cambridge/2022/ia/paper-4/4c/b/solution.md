<h1 id="4c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the special-relativistic matrix,

$$
A\binom11=\gamma(1-\beta)\binom11,
\qquad
A\binom1{-1}=\gamma(1+\beta)\binom1{-1}.
$$

Thus the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and corresponding [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_+=\sqrt{\frac{1-\beta}{1+\beta}},
\quad v_+=\binom11},
\qquad
\boxed{
\lambda_-=\sqrt{\frac{1+\beta}{1-\beta}},
\quad v_-=\binom1{-1}}.
$$

The eigenvector equations are $x=ct$ and $x=-ct$. They are the two [null directions](../../../../../../null-directions.md) of the [light cone](../../../../../../light-cone.md), representing right-moving and left-moving light rays. A Lorentz boost preserves each light-ray direction while rescaling its null coordinate.
