<h1 id="6d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $0\leq k\leq n$, the [binomial coefficient](../../../../../../binomial-coefficient.md) is

$$
\binom nk=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}.
$$

When $1\leq k\leq n-1$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\binom{n-1}k+\binom{n-1}{k-1}
&=\frac{(n-1)!}{k!(n-1-k)!}
+\frac{(n-1)!}{(k-1)!(n-k)!}\\
&=\frac{(n-1)![(n-k)+k]}{k!(n-k)!}\\
&=\boxed{\binom nk}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is [Pascal's identity](../../../../../../pascal-s-rule.md).
