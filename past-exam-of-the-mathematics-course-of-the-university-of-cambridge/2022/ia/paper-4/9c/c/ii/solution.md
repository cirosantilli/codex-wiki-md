<h1 id="9c/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The equation has the one-dimensional [effective potential](../../../../../../../effective-potential.md) per unit mass

$$
\boxed{\Phi(z)=-\frac{2mk}{\sqrt{z^2+a^2}}},
\qquad
\ddot z=-\Phi'(z).
$$

Thus the conserved specific energy is

$$
\mathcal E=\frac12\dot z^2+\Phi(z).
$$

The potential has its minimum $-2mk/a$ at $z=0$ and tends to $0$ from below as $|z|\to\infty$. Therefore $-2mk/a<\mathcal E<0$ gives bounded oscillation between two [turning points](../../../../../../../classical-turning-point.md); $\mathcal E=0$ gives marginal escape with asymptotic speed zero; and $\mathcal E>0$ gives escape with nonzero asymptotic speed. The minimum itself is the equilibrium $z=0$.
