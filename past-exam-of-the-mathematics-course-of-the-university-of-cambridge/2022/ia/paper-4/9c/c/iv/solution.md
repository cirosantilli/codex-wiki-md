<h1 id="9c/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The initial specific energy is

$$
\mathcal E=\frac12u^2-\frac{2mk}{a}.
$$

Escape to infinity is possible exactly when $\mathcal E\geq0$. Thus the [escape velocity](../../../../../../../escape-velocity.md) criterion is

$$
\boxed{|u|\geq2\sqrt{\frac{mk}{a}}}.
$$

Equality corresponds to marginal escape.
