<h1 id="9c/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $|z|\ll a$, [linearization](../../../../../../../linearization.md) gives

$$
\ddot z=-\frac{2mk}{a^3}z+O(z^3).
$$

The leading motion is [simple harmonic motion](../../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) with angular frequency

$$
\omega_z=\sqrt{\frac{2mk}{a^3}}.
$$

With $z(0)=z_0$ and $\dot z(0)=0$,

$$
z(t)\simeq z_0\cos(\omega_zt),
$$

and its period is

$$
\boxed{T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{a^3}{2mk}}}.
$$
