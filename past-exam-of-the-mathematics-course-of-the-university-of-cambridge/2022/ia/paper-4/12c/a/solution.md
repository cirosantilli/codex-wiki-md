<h1 id="12c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [four-vector](../../../../../../four-vector.md) is an object whose components transform between inertial frames by a [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md),

$$
U'^\mu=\Lambda^\mu{}_\nu U^\nu.
$$

Lorentz transformations are defined by

$$
\Lambda^T\eta\Lambda=\eta,
$$

where $\eta$ is the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md). Consequently

$$
U'\cdot U'
=(\Lambda U)^T\eta(\Lambda U)
=U^T\Lambda^T\eta\Lambda U
=U^T\eta U
=U\cdot U.
$$

**Thus the [Minkowski norm](../../../../../../minkowski-norm.md) of a four-vector is the same in every inertial frame.**
