<h1 id="12c/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Along the particle's worldline,

$$
x=u_xt,\qquad y=u_yt.
$$

The boost from $S$ to $S'$ gives

$$
x'=\gamma(x-Vt),\qquad
y'=y,\qquad
t'=\gamma\left(t-\frac{Vx}{c^2}\right).
$$

Therefore

$$
\frac{dt'}{dt}
=\gamma\left(1-\frac{Vu_x}{c^2}\right),
$$

and division of the transformed coordinate velocities by this factor gives the [relativistic velocity-addition formula](../../../../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md)

$$
\boxed{
u'_x=\frac{u_x-V}{1-Vu_x/c^2},
\qquad
u'_y=\frac{u_y}{\gamma(1-Vu_x/c^2)},
\qquad
u'_z=0}.
$$
