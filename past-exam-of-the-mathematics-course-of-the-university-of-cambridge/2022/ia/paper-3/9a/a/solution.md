<h1 id="9a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since $r=(x_jx_j)^{1/2}$, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial r}{\partial x_i}=\frac{x_i}{r}}.
$$

For the radial [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) $g(r)\mathbf x$, the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) and [Einstein summation convention](../../../../../../einstein-notation.md) give

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla\cdot\bigl(g(r)\mathbf x\bigr)
&=\frac{\partial}{\partial x_i}\bigl(g(r)x_i\bigr)\\
&=g'(r)\frac{x_i}{r}x_i+3g(r)\\
&=\boxed{rg'(r)+3g(r)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
