<h1 id="36b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The outgoing Green function of the [electromagnetic wave equation](../../../../../../electromagnetic-wave-equation.md) has support on the past light cone. Convolution with that [retarded Green function](../../../../../../causal-green-function.md) yields the [retarded electromagnetic potential](../../../../../../retarded-potential.md)

$$
\boxed{
A^\mu(\mathbf x,t)
=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}
\int_{\mathbb R^3}
\frac{J^\mu(\mathbf x',t')}{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x'|}
\,d^3x'
},
$$

where the source is evaluated at the [retarded time](../../../../../../retarded-time.md)

$$
\boxed{
t'=t-\frac{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x'|}{c}
}.
$$

This is the causal solution with no incoming radiation.
