<h1 id="36b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Lorenz gauge](../../../../../../lorenz-gauge-condition.md) is $\partial_\mu A^\mu=0$. Substituting the [electromagnetic four-potential](../../../../../../electromagnetic-four-potential.md) into the covariant [Maxwell equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations.md) then removes the mixed derivative and gives

$$
\boxed{\Box A^\mu=\mu_0J^\mu},
\qquad
\Box=\frac1{c^2}\partial_t^2-\nabla^2.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\left(\nabla^2-\frac1{c^2}\partial_t^2\right)A^\mu=-\mu_0J^\mu.
$$
