The [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) $K$ is the determinant of the shape operator, equivalently the product of the two principal curvatures. Yes,

$$
\boxed{K_R\circ\phi=K_S}.
$$

This is the [Theorema Egregium](../../../../../../theorema-egregium.md): Gaussian curvature depends only on the first fundamental form, which a local isometry preserves.
