↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For binary length-$n$ [linear codes](../../../../../../linear-code.md) $C_2\subseteq C_1$, their [bar product of binary linear codes](../../../../../../bar-product-of-binary-linear-codes.md) is

$$
\boxed{
C_1|C_2
=\{(u,u+v):u\in C_1,\ v\in C_2\}
}.
$$

It is a binary linear code of length $2n$ and dimension $\dim C_1+\dim C_2$.
