<h1 id="16f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This class is axiomatisable in the original language. For every positive integer $n$, let $\sigma_n$ say

$$
\text{``there are at least $n$ maximal elements''}
\quad\lor\quad
\text{``there are at least $n$ minimal elements''.}
$$

Both clauses are [first-order](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md). If one of the two sets is infinite, every $\sigma_n$ holds. Conversely, if both sets were finite, choosing $n$ larger than both cardinalities would make $\sigma_n$ false. Hence the [first-order theory](../../../../../../first-order-theory.md) consisting of the poset axioms and all the $\sigma_n$ has exactly the required models.
