↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $0<\mu=\mathbb E\xi_1<\infty$, the [size-biased distribution](../../../../../../size-biased-distribution.md) of $\xi_1$ is the law of $\widehat\xi$ defined by

$$
\mathbb E h(\widehat\xi)
=\frac{\mathbb E[\xi_1h(\xi_1)]}{\mu}
$$

for bounded measurable $h$. Equivalently,

$$
\mathbb P(\widehat\xi\in dx)=\frac{x}{\mu}\,\mathbb P(\xi_1\in dx).
$$
