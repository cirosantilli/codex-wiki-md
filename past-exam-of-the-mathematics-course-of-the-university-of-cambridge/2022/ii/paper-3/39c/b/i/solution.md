<h1 id="39c/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the given [internal gravity wave](../../../../../../../internal-wave.md),

$$
\Omega(k,m;x,z)
=\frac{Nk}{\sqrt{k^2+m^2}}
+\gamma(kx-mz).
$$

The wavevector part of the [Hamiltonian ray-tracing equations](../../../../../../../hamiltonian-ray-tracing-equations.md) is

$$
\dot k=-\frac{\partial\Omega}{\partial x}=-\gamma k,
\qquad
\dot m=-\frac{\partial\Omega}{\partial z}=\gamma m.
$$

Applying the initial data gives

$$
\boxed{
k(t)=k_0e^{-\gamma t},
\qquad
m(t)=m_0e^{\gamma t}
}.
$$

In particular, $k(t)m(t)=k_0m_0$ is conserved.
