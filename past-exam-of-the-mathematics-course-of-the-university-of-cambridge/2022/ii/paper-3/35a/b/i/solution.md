<h1 id="35a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [ultrarelativistic particle](../../../../../../../ultrarelativistic-particle.md), $\varepsilon=pc$. Including the two [spin](../../../../../../../spin.md) states of an electron, counting wavevectors in a sphere gives the [density of states](../../../../../../../density-of-states.md)

$$
g(\varepsilon)
=\frac{V\varepsilon^2}{\pi^2\hbar^3c^3}.
$$

The [grand canonical partition function](../../../../../../../grand-canonical-partition-function.md) of the ideal [Fermi gas](../../../../../../../fermi-gas.md) is therefore

$$
\log\mathcal Z
=\int_0^\infty g(\varepsilon)
\log\!\left(1+e^{-\beta(\varepsilon-\mu)}\right)d\varepsilon.
$$

Since the [grand potential](../../../../../../../grand-potential.md) satisfies $pV=k_BT\log\mathcal Z$, [integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
pV
=\frac13\int_0^\infty
\varepsilon g(\varepsilon)
\frac{d\varepsilon}{e^{\beta(\varepsilon-\mu)}+1}
=\frac E3.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{pV=AE,\qquad A=\frac13}.
$$

This is the [equation of state of an ideal ultrarelativistic gas](../../../../../../../equation-of-state-of-an-ideal-ultrarelativistic-gas.md).
