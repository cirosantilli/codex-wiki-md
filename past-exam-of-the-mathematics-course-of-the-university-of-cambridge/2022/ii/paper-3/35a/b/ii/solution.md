↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At zero temperature the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) is the indicator of $0\leq\varepsilon<E_F$. Hence

$$
N=\int_0^{E_F}g(\varepsilon)\,d\varepsilon
=\frac{VE_F^3}{3\pi^2\hbar^3c^3}.
$$

Solving for the [Fermi energy](../../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) gives

$$
\boxed{
E_F=D\left(\frac NV\right)^{1/3},
\qquad
D=\hbar c(3\pi^2)^{1/3}
}.
$$
