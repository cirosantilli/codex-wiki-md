↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Bosons](../../../../../../boson.md) have symmetric many-particle states, so any number may occupy the same one-particle state. At low [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md), an ideal Bose gas therefore places a macroscopic population in its lowest one-particle state; below the critical temperature this is [Bose-Einstein condensation](../../../../../../bose-einstein-condensation.md).

[Fermions](../../../../../../fermion.md) have antisymmetric many-particle states and obey the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md), so each one-particle state can hold at most one fermion of each available internal label. At zero temperature, a noninteracting Fermi gas fills distinct states in increasing order of [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) up to the [Fermi energy](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md). Its many-particle ground state is thus the filled [Fermi sea](../../../../../../fermi-sea.md).
