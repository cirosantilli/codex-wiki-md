<h1 id="11i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An integer $b$ is a [primitive root](../../../../../../primitive-root-modulo-n.md) modulo $n$ when it is coprime to $n$ and its residue class generates the [multiplicative group of integers modulo n](../../../../../../multiplicative-group-of-integers-modulo-n.md). Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}_n(b)=\varphi(n)}.
$$
