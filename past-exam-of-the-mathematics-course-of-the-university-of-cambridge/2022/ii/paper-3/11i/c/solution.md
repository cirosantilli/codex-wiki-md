<h1 id="11i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

By [lifting a primitive root to odd prime powers](../../../../../../lifting-a-primitive-root-to-odd-prime-powers.md), it is enough to test primitivity modulo $25$. The positive integers below eight either fail to be units or have orders

$$
\operatorname{ord}_{25}(1)=1,\quad
\operatorname{ord}_{25}(4)=10,\quad
\operatorname{ord}_{25}(6)=5,\quad
\operatorname{ord}_{25}(7)=4,
$$

whereas

$$
\operatorname{ord}_{25}(2)
=\operatorname{ord}_{25}(3)
=\operatorname{ord}_{25}(8)=20.
$$

Therefore the three smallest required integers are

$$
\boxed{2,\ 3,\ 8}.
$$
