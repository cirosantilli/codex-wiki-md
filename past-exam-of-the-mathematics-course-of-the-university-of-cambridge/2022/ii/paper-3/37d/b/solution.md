<h1 id="37d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A static worldline has [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md)

$$
u^\mu=(f^{-1/2},0,0,0).
$$

Its [four-acceleration](../../../../../../four-acceleration.md) is $a^\mu=u^\nu\nabla_\nu u^\mu$. The only nonzero component is

$$
a^r=\Gamma^r_{tt}(u^t)^2=\frac{M}{r^2}.
$$

Taking its norm with $g_{rr}=f^{-1}$ gives the [proper acceleration of a static Schwarzschild observer](../../../../../../proper-acceleration-of-a-static-schwarzschild-observer.md)

$$
\boxed{
|a|=\sqrt{a_\mu a^\mu}
=\frac{M}{r_{\rm st}^2\sqrt{1-2M/r_{\rm st}}}
}.
$$

It points radially outward, because that is the acceleration needed to prevent the station from falling. As $r_{\rm st}\to\infty$ the square-root factor tends to one, and $|a|\sim M/r_{\rm st}^2$, the [Newtonian gravitational field](../../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-field.md) in units $G=1$.
