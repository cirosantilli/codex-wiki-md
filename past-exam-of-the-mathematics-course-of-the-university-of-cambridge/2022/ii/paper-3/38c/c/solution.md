<h1 id="38c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At negligible [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md), the [Stokes equations](../../../../../../stokes-flow-split.md) and their no-slip boundary conditions are linear in velocity. Scaling or superposing a prescribed rigid translation therefore scales or superposes the resulting stress and total hydrodynamic force. Hence, for a fixed rod orientation,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=-\mathbf R\cdot\mathbf U}.
$$

The minus sign records that viscous drag opposes motion, and $\mathbf R$ is the translational [hydrodynamic resistance matrix](../../../../../../hydrodynamic-resistance-matrix.md).
