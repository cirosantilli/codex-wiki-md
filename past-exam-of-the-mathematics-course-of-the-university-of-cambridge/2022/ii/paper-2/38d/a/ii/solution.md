<h1 id="38d/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Along the constant latitude, use $\phi$ as parameter. The [parallel transport](../../../../../../../parallel-transport.md) equations are

$$
\frac{dV^\theta}{d\phi}
-\sin\theta\cos\theta\,V^\phi=0,
\qquad
\frac{dV^\phi}{d\phi}
+\cot\theta\,V^\theta=0.
$$

With $W=\sin\theta\,V^\phi$, these become

$$
\frac{dV^\theta}{d\phi}=\cos\theta\,W,
\qquad
\frac{dW}{d\phi}=-\cos\theta\,V^\theta.
$$

The initial components $(1,0)$ therefore evolve as

$$
V^\theta(\phi)=\cos(\phi\cos\theta),
\qquad
V^\phi(\phi)=-\frac{\sin(\phi\cos\theta)}{\sin\theta}.
$$

After one circuit, the [parallel transport around a latitude of the unit sphere](../../../../../../../parallel-transport-around-a-latitude-of-the-unit-sphere.md) gives

$$
\boxed{
(V^\theta,V^\phi)
=\left(
\cos(2\pi\cos\theta),
-\frac{\sin(2\pi\cos\theta)}{\sin\theta}
\right)}.
$$
