<h1 id="38d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At any point choose orthonormal coordinates. The antisymmetries and pair symmetry of the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) leave only $R_{1212}$ independent. In these coordinates,

$$
R_{11}=R_{1212},\qquad
R_{22}=R_{1212},\qquad
R_{12}=0,
$$

while $R=2R_{1212}$. Since this is a tensor identity, it follows in every coordinate system that the [Ricci tensor in two dimensions](../../../../../../ricci-tensor-in-two-dimensions.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{R_{\alpha\beta}=\frac12g_{\alpha\beta}R}.
$$

The [Einstein tensor](../../../../../../einstein-tensor.md) consequently vanishes identically. Hence the two-dimensional vacuum field equation contains no local gravitational dynamics, while coupling the unmodified equation to matter would require $T_{\alpha\beta}=0$. This is the basic degeneracy of [two-dimensional general relativity](../../../../../../two-dimensional-general-relativity.md).
