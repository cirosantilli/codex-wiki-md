<h1 id="38d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Contracting the stated [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) with $g^{\alpha\beta}$ in $n$ dimensions gives

$$
T=R-\frac n2R=-\frac{n-2}{2}R.
$$

Thus $R=-2T/(n-2)$, and substitution back yields the [Trace-reversed Einstein field equations in D dimensions](../../../../../../trace-reversed-einstein-field-equations-in-d-dimensions.md)

$$
\boxed{
R_{\alpha\beta}
=T_{\alpha\beta}-\frac1{n-2}g_{\alpha\beta}T
},
\qquad n>2.
$$
