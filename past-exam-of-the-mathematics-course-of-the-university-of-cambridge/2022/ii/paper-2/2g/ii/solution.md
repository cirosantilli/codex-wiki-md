**False.** Fix $y_0\in\mathbb R$ and take

$$
A=\mathbb R\times\{y_0\}.
$$

This closed set has empty interior in $\mathbb R^2$, so it is nowhere dense and hence meagre. Its section at $y_0$ is $C_{y_0}=\mathbb R$, which is not meagre in itself by the [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md).
