<h1 id="37a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Substitution of the canonical probabilities into the [Gibbs entropy](../../../../../../gibbs-entropy.md) gives

$$
S=-k_B\sum_np_n(-\beta E_n-\log Z)
=k_B\log Z+k_B\beta E.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{S=k_B\log Z+\frac ET}.
$$
