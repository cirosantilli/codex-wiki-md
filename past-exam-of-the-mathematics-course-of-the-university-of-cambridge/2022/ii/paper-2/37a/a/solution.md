The [microcanonical ensemble](../../../../../../microcanonical-ensemble.md) describes an isolated system with fixed energy, volume, and particle number. It assigns equal probabilities to the accessible microstates in the chosen narrow energy shell. The [canonical ensemble](../../../../../../canonical-ensemble.md) describes a system in thermal contact with a large heat reservoir: its temperature, volume, and particle number are fixed, while its energy can fluctuate.
