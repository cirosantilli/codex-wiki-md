<h1 id="37a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Put $x=\beta\epsilon$. The one-particle [canonical partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is

$$
z=e^x+1+e^{-x}=1+2\cosh x,
$$

and independence gives $Z=z^N$. Therefore the [independent symmetric three-level system](../../../../../../independent-symmetric-three-level-system.md) has

$$
\boxed{E=-\frac{\partial\log Z}{\partial\beta}
=-\frac{2N\epsilon\sinh x}{1+2\cosh x}}
$$

and, using part (c),

$$
\boxed{
S=Nk_B\left[
\log(1+2\cosh x)
-\frac{2x\sinh x}{1+2\cosh x}
\right]}.
$$

As $T\to\infty$, $x\to0$, all three levels become equally likely and

$$
E\to0,\qquad S\to Nk_B\log3.
$$

As $T\to0^+$, every particle occupies the nondegenerate ground level $-\epsilon$, and

$$
E\to-N\epsilon,\qquad S\to0.
$$
