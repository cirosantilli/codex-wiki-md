↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**True.** The hypothesis says that $\alpha+\alpha$ and $\beta$ commute under [ordinal addition](../../../../../../../ordinal-addition.md). By the [commuting ordinal addition](../../../../../../../commuting-ordinal-addition.md) classification, comparison of their [Cantor normal forms](../../../../../../../cantor-normal-form.md) implies that $\alpha$ and $\beta$ themselves commute. Therefore

$$
\alpha+\alpha+\beta
=\alpha+\beta+\alpha
=\beta+\alpha+\alpha,
$$

so both outer expressions equal the middle one.
