<h1 id="16f/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**True.** Every $\beta<\omega_1$ is a [countable ordinal](../../../../../../../countable-ordinal.md), and

$$
\beta+\omega_1=\sup_{\xi<\omega_1}(\beta+\xi)=\omega_1.
$$

Writing any $\alpha\geq\omega_1$ as $\omega_1+\delta$ and using [associativity](../../../../../../../associativity-of-ordinal-addition.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\beta+\alpha=(\beta+\omega_1)+\delta
=\omega_1+\delta=\alpha.}
$$
