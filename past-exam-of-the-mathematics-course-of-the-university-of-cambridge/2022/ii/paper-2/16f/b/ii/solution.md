↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Left distributivity over a sum in the right argument,

$$
\boxed{\alpha(\beta+\gamma)=\alpha\beta+\alpha\gamma},
$$

follows by transfinite induction on $\gamma$ from the recursive definition of [ordinal multiplication](../../../../../../../ordinal-multiplication.md); the successor and limit steps are exactly its defining clauses.
