↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**False.** The [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\alpha=\omega\cdot2$ is a [limit ordinal](../../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md), but

$$
\boxed{\alpha\omega=(\omega\cdot2)\omega=\omega^2,
\qquad
\omega\alpha=\omega(\omega\cdot2)=\omega^2\cdot2.}
$$
