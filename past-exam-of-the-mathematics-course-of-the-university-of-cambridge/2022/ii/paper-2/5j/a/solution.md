A one-parameter [exponential family](../../../../../../exponential-family-split.md) has densities

$$
f(y;\theta)=h(y)\exp\{\theta T(y)-A(\theta)\},
$$

with support independent of the [natural parameter](../../../../../../natural-parameter-of-an-exponential-family.md) $\theta$. In the present canonical setting the natural statistic is $T(y)=y$.
