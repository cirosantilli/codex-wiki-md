↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Independence gives the joint density

$$
\prod_{i=1}^nh(y_i)
\exp\left\{\theta\sum_{i=1}^ny_i-nA(\theta)\right\}.
$$

By the factorization criterion, its dependence on the sample relevant to $\theta$ is only through $\sum_iY_i$, equivalently through $\overline Y$. Thus $\overline Y$ is a [sufficient statistic](../../../../../../sufficient-statistic.md) for $\theta$.
