<h1 id="40c/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Eliminating $T$ from the two boundary conditions gives

$$
\frac{1+R}{1-R}
=\frac{\lambda'k'\cos\theta}
{\lambda k\cos\theta'}.
$$

The [Snell law for elastic and acoustic waves](../../../../../../../snell-law-for-elastic-and-acoustic-waves.md) implies

$$
\frac{k'}k=\frac{\sin\theta}{\sin\theta'}.
$$

Therefore the [displacement reflection from an interface between two inviscid elastic liquids](../../../../../../../displacement-reflection-from-an-interface-between-two-inviscid-elastic-liquids.md) is

$$
\boxed{
R=\frac{\lambda'\sin2\theta-\lambda\sin2\theta'}
{\lambda'\sin2\theta+\lambda\sin2\theta'}}.
$$
