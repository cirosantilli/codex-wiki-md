<h1 id="24f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The sum of two [meromorphic functions](../../../../../../meromorphic-function.md) has no poles away from the union of their pole sets, and at each $p_i$ its pole order is at most the larger of the two orders, hence at most $m_i$. Scalar multiplication cannot increase a pole order. The zero function belongs to the set, so $V$ is a complex vector space.
