<h1 id="35a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $c=\cos(\theta/2)$ and $s=\sin(\theta/2)$. Then

$$
|\uparrow_\theta\downarrow_\theta\rangle
-|\downarrow_\theta\uparrow_\theta\rangle
=(c^2+s^2)
\bigl(|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle-|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle\bigr).
$$

Thus $|\psi\rangle$ is unchanged. It is the [spin-one-half singlet state](../../../../../../spin-one-half-singlet-state.md), so joint rotations leave it invariant. Equivalently, direct use of the [Pauli matrices](../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md) shows

$$
(S_i^{(1)}+S_i^{(2)})|\psi\rangle=0
\qquad(i=x,y,z).
$$

It is therefore an [eigenstate](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) of every Cartesian component of the combined spin, with eigenvalue zero.
