<h1 id="35a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using the states from part (a),

$$
|\chi\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2}
\left[
-s|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle
+c|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle
-c|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle
-s|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle
\right].
$$

The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) therefore gives

$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{outcome}&\text{probability}\\ \hline
(+\hbar/2,+\hbar/2)&\frac12\sin^2(\theta/2)\\
(+\hbar/2,-\hbar/2)&\frac12\cos^2(\theta/2)\\
(-\hbar/2,+\hbar/2)&\frac12\cos^2(\theta/2)\\
(-\hbar/2,-\hbar/2)&\frac12\sin^2(\theta/2).
\end{array}
$$

After the equal-sign outcomes the state is respectively $|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle$ or $|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle$. These are [triplet states](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md), hence [eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) of $\mathbf S^2$ with eigenvalue

$$
\boxed{2\hbar^2}.
$$

The opposite-sign product states are each a nontrivial [linear combination](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of the singlet and the $m=0$ triplet, so they are not eigenstates of $\mathbf S^2$.
