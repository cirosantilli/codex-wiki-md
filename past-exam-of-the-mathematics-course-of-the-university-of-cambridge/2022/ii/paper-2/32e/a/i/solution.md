↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

This is an [asymptotic sequence](../../../../../../../asymptotic-sequence.md). Since

$$
\log(\cos u)\sim-\frac{u^2}{2}\qquad(u\to0),
$$

one has

$$
\frac{\phi_{n+1}(x)}{\phi_n(x)}
\sim x^2\longrightarrow0.
$$
