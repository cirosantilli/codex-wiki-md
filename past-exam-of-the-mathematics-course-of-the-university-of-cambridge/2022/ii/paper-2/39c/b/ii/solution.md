↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With zero imposed pressure gradient, the [two-dimensional boundary-layer equations](../../../../../../../two-dimensional-boundary-layer-equations.md) are

$$
u_x+v_y=0,
\qquad
uu_x+vu_y=\nu u_{yy}.
$$

Introduce the [stream function](../../../../../../../stream-function.md) by

$$
u=\psi_y,\qquad v=-\psi_x.
$$

The boundary conditions are

$$
\psi_y(x,0)=\alpha x,\qquad
\psi_x(x,0)=0,\qquad
\psi_y(x,y)\to0\quad(y\to\infty).
$$

The additive constant in $\psi$ may be chosen so that $\psi(x,0)=0$.
