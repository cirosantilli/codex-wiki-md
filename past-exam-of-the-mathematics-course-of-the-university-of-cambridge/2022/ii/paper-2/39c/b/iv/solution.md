<h1 id="39c/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Set $f=A+Be^{-C\eta}$ with $C>0$. The differential equation reduces to

$$
BC^2(A-C)e^{-C\eta}=0,
$$

so $A=C$. The two wall conditions give

$$
A+B=0,\qquad -BC=1.
$$

Hence $C^2=1$, and positivity of $C$ yields

$$
\boxed{A=1,\qquad B=-1,\qquad C=1},
$$

or

$$
\boxed{f(\eta)=1-e^{-\eta}}.
$$
