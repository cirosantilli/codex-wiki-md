<h1 id="39c/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put

$$
\delta=\sqrt{\frac{\nu}{\alpha}},
\qquad
\eta=\frac y\delta,
\qquad
\psi=\alpha x\delta f(\eta).
$$

Then

$$
u=\alpha x f'(\eta),
\qquad
v=-\alpha\delta f(\eta).
$$

Substitution in the boundary-layer momentum equation, using $\nu/\delta^2=\alpha$, gives

$$
\boxed{f'''+ff''-(f')^2=0}.
$$

The wall and far-field conditions become

$$
\boxed{f(0)=0,\qquad f'(0)=1,\qquad f'(\infty)=0}.
$$

These are the similarity equations for the [boundary layer over a linearly stretching sheet](../../../../../../../boundary-layer-over-a-linearly-stretching-sheet.md).
