<h1 id="15d/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

There are [uncountably](../../../../../../../uncountable-set.md) many physically distinct one-qubit [unitaries](../../../../../../../unitary-operator.md). Part (ii) would require an [orthogonal](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) program state for every one, whereas a $K$-qubit register has [dimension](../../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) only $2^K$. This contradiction is the [no-programming theorem](../../../../../../../no-programming-theorem.md), so no deterministic gate satisfying (PROG) exists.
