<h1 id="3k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a $q$-ary code with codeword lengths $l_1,\ldots,l_m$, [Kraft inequality](../../../../../../kraft-mcmillan-inequality.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i=1}^m q^{-l_i}\leq1}.
$$

Choose an infinite random $q$-ary string with independent uniform symbols. The event that it begins with codeword $i$ has probability $q^{-l_i}$. For a [prefix-free code](../../../../../../prefix-code.md), these events are disjoint, so their probabilities sum to at most one. This proves necessity.
