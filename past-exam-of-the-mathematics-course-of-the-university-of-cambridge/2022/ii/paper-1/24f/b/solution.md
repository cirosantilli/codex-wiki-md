<h1 id="24f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the principal branch $\operatorname{Log}z=\log|z|+i\arg z$, with $-\pi<\arg z<\pi$. Then

$$
\boxed{f(z)=i\operatorname{Log}z=-\arg z+i\log|z|}
$$

is a [conformal equivalence](../../../../../../conformal-equivalence.md) from the slit plane to the vertical strip. Its imaginary part tends to $-\infty$ as $z\to0$ and to $+\infty$ as $z\to\infty$.
