<h1 id="24f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [uniformization theorem](../../../../../../uniformization-theorem.md) says that every simply connected [Riemann surface](../../../../../../riemann-surfaces.md) is conformally equivalent to exactly one of the [Riemann sphere](../../../../../../riemann-sphere.md), the [complex plane](../../../../../../complex-plane.md), and the [unit disc](../../../../../../unit-disc.md). A proper simply connected plane domain is noncompact, so it is not the sphere. It cannot be uniformized by the plane: after choosing a point outside the domain, a branch of a square root and then a [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) would produce a nonconstant bounded entire function, contradicting the [Liouville theorem](../../../../../../liouville-theorem.md). It is therefore conformally equivalent to the disc, which is the [Riemann mapping theorem](../../../../../../riemann-mapping-theorem.md).
