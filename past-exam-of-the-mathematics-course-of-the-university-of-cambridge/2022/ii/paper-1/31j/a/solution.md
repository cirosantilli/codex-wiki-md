For a binary function class $\mathcal F$, the [shattering coefficient](../../../../../../shattering-coefficient.md) is

$$
s(\mathcal F,n)=\max_{x_1,\ldots,x_n}
\left|\{(f(x_1),\ldots,f(x_n)):f\in\mathcal F\}\right|.
$$

Its [VC dimension](../../../../../../vc-dimension.md) is the largest $n$ for which $s(\mathcal F,n)=2^n$, with value infinity if there is no largest such $n$. The [Sauer-Shelah lemma](../../../../../../sauer-shelah-lemma.md) states that, when $\operatorname{VC}(\mathcal F)=d<\infty$,

$$
\boxed{s(\mathcal F,n)\leq\sum_{j=0}^{d}\binom nj.}
$$
