<h1 id="10d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A unitary cloner with blank state $|0\rangle$ would satisfy

$$
U(|\psi\rangle|0\rangle)=|\psi\rangle|\psi\rangle
$$

for every $|\psi\rangle\in S$. For $|\psi\rangle,|\phi\rangle\in S$, preservation of inner products gives

$$
\langle\psi|\phi\rangle
=\langle\psi|\phi\rangle^2.
$$

Hence every pair is either orthogonal or represents the same state. Conversely, an orthonormal set can be cloned by defining the map on its basis states and extending it to a unitary. Therefore the [no-cloning theorem](../../../../../../quantum-cloning.md) says that a set of pure states is unitarily clonable exactly when its distinct members are mutually orthogonal.
