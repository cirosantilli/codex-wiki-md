<h1 id="15a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The homogeneous part of the [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) gives $\dot{\bar n}+3H\bar n=0$. At first order,

$$
\dot{\delta n}+3H\delta n+\frac{\bar n}{a}\nabla\cdot\delta\mathbf v=0.
$$

For the [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) $\delta=\delta n/\bar n$, the background equation cancels the expansion terms, leaving

$$
\boxed{\dot\delta+\frac1a\nabla\cdot\delta\mathbf v=0}.
$$
