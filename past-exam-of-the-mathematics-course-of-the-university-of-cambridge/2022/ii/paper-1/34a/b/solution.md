<h1 id="34a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Orthogonality of the energy eigenstates gives

$$
\langle z|z\rangle=e^{-|z|^2}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{|z|^{2n}}{n!}=1.
$$

Using the [lowering-operator](../../../../../../creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) action from part (a) and shifting the summation index,

$$
A|z\rangle=e^{-|z|^2/2}\sum_{n\geq1}
\frac{z^n\sqrt n}{\sqrt{n!}}|n-1\rangle
=z|z\rangle.
$$

**Thus this [coherent state](../../../../../../coherent-state.md) is a normalized eigenstate of $A$ with eigenvalue $z$.**
