<h1 id="11k/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For received polynomial $r$, define the syndromes $S_j=r(\alpha^j)$. If the error positions are $i_1,\ldots,i_\nu$, the [error locator polynomial](../../../../../../../error-locator-polynomial.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sigma(Z)=\prod_{h=1}^{\nu}(1-\alpha^{i_h}Z)}.
$$

Its roots are $\alpha^{-i_h}$, and its coefficients satisfy the Newton syndrome recurrences. For this design-distance-five code, bounded-distance decoding seeks degree at most two.
