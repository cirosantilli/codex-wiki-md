<h1 id="36a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Extensivity makes $\Phi(T,V,\mu)$ proportional to $V$. Comparing its differential  
$d\Phi=-S\,dT-p\,dV-\langle N\rangle d\mu$  
with Euler scaling gives

$$
\boxed{\Phi=-pV}.
$$
