The [Markov property](../../../../../../../markov-property.md) and conditioning on the intermediate state give the bridge probability

$$
\mathbb P(X_t=2\mid X_0=1,X_{3t}=1)
=\frac{p_{12}(t)p_{21}(2t)}{p_{11}(3t)}.
$$

Substitution from part (i) yields

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\lambda\mu(1-e^{-st})(1-e^{-2st})}
{s(\lambda+\mu e^{-3st})}}
\qquad(s=\lambda+\mu).
$$
