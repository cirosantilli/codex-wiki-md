<h1 id="28j/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Writing $\pi=(\pi_1,\pi_2)$, the equations $\pi Q=0$ and $\pi_1+\pi_2=1$ give

$$
\boxed{\pi=\left(\frac\lambda{s},\frac\mu{s}\right)}.
$$

This is the [invariant distribution](../../../../../../../invariant-distribution-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md). Since $e^{-st}\to0$, both rows of $P(t)$ converge to $\pi$, directly verifying convergence to equilibrium.
