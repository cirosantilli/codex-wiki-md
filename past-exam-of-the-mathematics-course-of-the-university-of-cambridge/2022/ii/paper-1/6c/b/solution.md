<h1 id="6c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Jury stability criterion](../../../../../../jury-stability-criterion.md) for $\lambda^2-\lambda+r$ gives both roots inside the unit disc exactly when

$$
\boxed{0<r<1}.
$$

The decay is monotone when both roots are real and positive. Since the discriminant is $1-4r$, this occurs for

$$
\boxed{0<r\leq\frac14}.
$$
