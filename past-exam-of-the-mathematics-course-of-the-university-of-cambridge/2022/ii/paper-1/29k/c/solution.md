<h1 id="29k/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The one-observation [score function](../../../../../../informant-function.md) is

$$
S_\sigma(X)=-\frac1\sigma+\frac{X^2}{\sigma^3}.
$$

Its variance, or equivalently minus the expected second derivative of the log-likelihood, is the [Fisher information](../../../../../../fisher-information-matrix.md)

$$
\boxed{I(\sigma)=\frac2{\sigma^2}}.
$$
