↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $k=-1$, the [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) is

$$
ds^2=-dt^2+a^2(t)\left[d\chi^2+\sinh^2\chi
\,(d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2)\right].
$$

The empty first [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md), with $\Lambda=0$, gives

$$
\frac{\dot a^2-1}{a^2}=0.
$$

Choosing the expanding branch and shifting the origin of proper time yields

$$
\boxed{a(t)=t,\qquad t>0}.
$$
