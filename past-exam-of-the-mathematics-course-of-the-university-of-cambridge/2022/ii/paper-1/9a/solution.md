<h1 id="9a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9A](../9a.md)

Chemical equilibrium for $p+e\leftrightarrow H$ gives

$$
\mu_H=\mu_p+\mu_e,
$$

and [charge neutrality](../../../../../charge-neutrality.md) gives $n_p=n_e$. Insert the nonrelativistic Maxwell-Boltzmann number densities, use $m_Hc^2=m_pc^2+m_ec^2-E_{\rm bind}$, and neglect the proton correction in the reduced mass. The spin degeneracies cancel in the ground-state approximation. One obtains [Saha equation](../../../../../saha-ionization-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{
\frac{n_H}{n_e^2}
=\left(\frac{2\pi\hbar^2}{m_ek_BT}\right)^{3/2}
\exp\!\left(\frac{E_{\rm bind}}{k_BT}\right)}.
$$

Equivalently, its reciprocal is the ionization ratio. The exponential favors neutral hydrogen as the temperature falls, while the translational phase-space factor favors ionization at high temperature.
