<h1 id="22g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take $x_n=(-1)^n$. Its shift is $-x$, so linearity and shift invariance give

$$
\phi(x)=\phi(-x)=-\phi(x).
$$

Thus every such functional assigns the common value

$$
\boxed{\phi(x)=0},
$$

although the sequence does not converge.
