↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) of a [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md) is its [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) under [spatial reflection](../../../../../../spatial-reflection.md): $\psi(-x)=+\psi(x)$ for [even](../../../../../../even-function.md) parity and $\psi(-x)=-\psi(x)$ for [odd](../../../../../../odd-function.md) parity. If $V(-x)=V(x)$, the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) commutes with reflection, so its [energy eigenspaces](../../../../../../energy-eigenspace.md) admit a parity [eigenbasis](../../../../../../eigenbasis.md). In particular, every [nondegenerate](../../../../../../nondegenerate-energy-eigenvalue.md) bound-state [energy eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) has definite parity; within a [degenerate](../../../../../../state-degeneracy.md) scattering eigenspace one must choose the even and odd [linear combinations](../../../../../../linear-combination.md).
