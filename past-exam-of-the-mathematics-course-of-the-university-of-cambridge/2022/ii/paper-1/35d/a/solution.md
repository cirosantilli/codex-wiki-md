<h1 id="35d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For incidence from the left, normalize the asymptotic wavefunction by

$$
\psi(x)\sim e^{ikx}+r e^{-ikx}\quad(x\to-\infty),
\qquad
\psi(x)\sim t e^{ikx}\quad(x\to+\infty).
$$

For incidence from the right, define $r',t'$ analogously with the directions reversed. Conservation of the [probability current](../../../../../../probability-current.md) gives

$$
|r|^2+|t|^2=1,\qquad |r'|^2+|t'|^2=1.
$$

Applying the same conserved sesquilinear current to the two scattering solutions gives

$$
t'^*r+r'^*t=0.
$$

These are exactly the column orthonormality relations $S^\dagger S=I$ for the stated [One-dimensional S-matrix](../../../../../../one-dimensional-s-matrix.md), so $S$ is unitary.
