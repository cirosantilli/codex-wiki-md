↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Statement (ii) is always true even when $y$ is not transitive. Given a nonempty subset $z\subseteq y$, the [Axiom of foundation](../../../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) supplies $a\in z$ with $a\cap z=\varnothing$. Thus no member of $z$ is a member of $a$, so $a$ is minimal for membership restricted to $y$. An isomorphic relation $r$ is therefore well-founded.
