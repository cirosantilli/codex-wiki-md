<h1 id="6c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Adding the equations for the free enzyme and its two complexes gives

$$
\frac d{dt}(e+c_1+c_2)=0.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{e(t)+c_1(t)+c_2(t)=e_0}.
$$

This [conservation law](../../../../../../conservation-law.md) says that the enzyme is neither created nor destroyed: each enzyme molecule is either free, bound in $C_1$, or bound in $C_2$.
