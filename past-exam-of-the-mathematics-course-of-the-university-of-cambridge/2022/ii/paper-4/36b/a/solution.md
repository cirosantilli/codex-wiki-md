A dielectric is an electrically insulating material whose bound positive and negative charges can be displaced slightly by an [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md), producing [electric polarization](../../../../../../polarization-density.md) without sustained conduction current. Its macroscopic electric response is described by its permittivity.
