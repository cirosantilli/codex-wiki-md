<h1 id="38c/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The volume flux per unit transverse width is

$$
\begin{aligned}
q(x,t)
&=\int_0^h u\,dy\\
&=\frac{\rho gh^3}{3\mu}
\left(\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha\,h_x\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

Integrating incompressibility across the layer and using the wall and free-surface kinematic conditions gives local mass conservation $h_t+q_x=0$. Hence the [gravity-driven thin film on an incline](../../../../../../gravity-driven-thin-film-on-an-incline.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{
h_t+\frac{\rho g}{3\mu}
\partial_x\left[
h^3(\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha\,h_x)
\right]=0}.
$$
