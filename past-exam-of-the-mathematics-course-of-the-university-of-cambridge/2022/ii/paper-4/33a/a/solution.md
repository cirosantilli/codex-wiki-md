<h1 id="33a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Insert the stated expansion into the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md)

$$
i\hbar\frac d{dt}|\psi(t)\rangle
=(H_0+v(t))|\psi(t)\rangle.
$$

When the derivative acts on each free phase, its energy factor is cancelled by the action of $H_0$ on the corresponding [eigenstate](../../../../../../eigenstate.md). The remaining terms obey

$$
i\hbar\left[
\dot a(t)e^{-iE_0t/\hbar}|0\rangle
+\int_0^\infty e^{-iE_kt/\hbar}
\bigl(\dot b_k(t)|k,+\rangle+\dot c_k(t)|k,-\rangle\bigr)\,dk
\right]
=-F e^{-i\omega t}x|\psi(t)\rangle.
$$

Division by $i\hbar$ gives

$$
\boxed{
\dot a(t)e^{-iE_0t/\hbar}|0\rangle
+\int_0^\infty e^{-iE_kt/\hbar}
\bigl(\dot b_k|k,+\rangle+\dot c_k|k,-\rangle\bigr)\,dk
=\frac{iF}{\hbar}e^{-i\omega t}x|\psi(t)\rangle}.
$$
