<h1 id="31e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

After division by $x^2$, the equation becomes

$$
y''+\frac1xy'-\frac1{x^4}y=0.
$$

The [regular singular point criterion for a second-order equation](../../../../../../regular-singular-point-criterion-for-a-second-order-equation.md) would require $x(1/x)$ and $x^2(-1/x^4)$ to be analytic at zero. The second expression is $-x^{-2}$, so the criterion fails:

$$
\boxed{x=0\text{ is an irregular singular point}.}
$$
