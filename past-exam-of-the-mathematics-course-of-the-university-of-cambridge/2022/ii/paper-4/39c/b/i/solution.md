↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Before shock formation, the piston launches a right-moving [simple wave](../../../../../../../pressure-in-a-perfect-gas-simple-wave.md) into the undisturbed gas. The opposite invariant is fixed at its ambient value:

$$
R_-=u-\frac{2c}{\gamma-1}
=-\frac{2c_0}{\gamma-1}.
$$

Therefore the [simple-wave relation](../../../../../../../pressure-in-a-perfect-gas-simple-wave.md) is

$$
\boxed{c=c_0+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}u}.
$$
