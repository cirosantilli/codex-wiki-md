<h1 id="39c/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The motion is isentropic before a shock forms, and the perfect-gas relations give

$$
\frac p{p_0}
=\left(\frac c{c_0}\right)^{2\gamma/(\gamma-1)}.
$$

Writing $\delta=\delta c/c_0$ and applying the [generalized binomial expansion](../../../../../../../binomial-theorem.md),

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\delta p}{p_0}
=\frac{2\gamma}{\gamma-1}\delta
+\frac{\gamma(\gamma+1)}{(\gamma-1)^2}\delta^2
+O(\delta^3)}.
$$
