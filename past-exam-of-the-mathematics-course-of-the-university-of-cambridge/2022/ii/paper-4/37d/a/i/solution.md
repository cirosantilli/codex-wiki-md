<h1 id="37d/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The antipodal action fixes the origin. Away from the origin it acts freely, so the quotient is locally Euclidean there. A small punctured neighborhood of the image of the origin, however, has link

$$
S^2/(x\sim-x)=\mathbb{RP}^2,
$$

the [real projective plane](../../../../../../../real-projective-plane.md), whereas the link of a point in a three-dimensional [smooth manifold](../../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) is $S^2$. Equivalently, the neighborhood is a cone on $\mathbb{RP}^2$ and cannot be a three-ball. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbb R^3/(x\sim-x)\text{ is not a manifold}.}
$$
