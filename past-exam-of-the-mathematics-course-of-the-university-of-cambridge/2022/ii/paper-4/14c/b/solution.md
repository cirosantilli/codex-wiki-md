<h1 id="14c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $d=1$, the condition would require

$$
f_u+g_v>2\sqrt J>0.
$$

But homogeneous stability requires $f_u+g_v<0$. These inequalities are incompatible, so equal diffusivities cannot produce a [Turing instability](../../../../../../turing-instability.md).
