↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The sequence $(f_n)$ is [equicontinuous](../../../../../../equicontinuity.md) on $[0,1]$ when, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a $\delta>0$ such that

$$
|s-t|<\delta
\Longrightarrow
|f_n(s)-f_n(t)|<\varepsilon
$$

for every $n$. The [Arzelà-Ascoli theorem](../../../../../../arzela-ascoli-theorem.md) says that a uniformly bounded equicontinuous sequence of continuous real-valued functions on a compact interval has a uniformly convergent subsequence.
