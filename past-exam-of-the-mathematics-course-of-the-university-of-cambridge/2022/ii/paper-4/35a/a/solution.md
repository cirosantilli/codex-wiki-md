<h1 id="35a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Carnot theorem](../../../../../../carnot-s-theorem.md) states that no engine between two fixed heat reservoirs can be more efficient than a reversible engine, and that all reversible engines between those reservoirs have the same efficiency. Thus the heat ratio of a reversible engine depends only on the two reservoirs. Its multiplicative consistency for three reservoirs permits a state variable $T$, unique up to an overall scale, such that

$$
\frac{Q_c}{Q_h}=\frac{T_c}{T_h}.
$$

Fixing one reference value defines the [thermodynamic temperature](../../../../../../thermodynamic-temperature.md) scale, and the reversible efficiency is

$$
\boxed{\eta_{\rm rev}=1-\frac{T_c}{T_h}}.
$$
