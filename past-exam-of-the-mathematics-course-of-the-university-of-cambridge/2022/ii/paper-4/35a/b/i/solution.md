↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At fixed volume, a reversible infinitesimal heating supplies $\delta Q_{\rm rev}=C_V\,dT$. Since [entropy](../../../../../../../entropy.md) satisfies $dS=\delta Q_{\rm rev}/T$,

$$
\Delta S=C_V\int_{T_i}^{T_f}\frac{dT}{T}
=\boxed{C_V\log\frac{T_f}{T_i}}.
$$
