<h1 id="35a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The fundamental thermodynamic relation is

$$
dE=T\,dS-p\,dV+\mu\,dN.
$$

Because $E(S,V,N)$ is extensive, the [Euler theorem for homogeneous functions](../../../../../../euler-theorem-for-homogeneous-functions.md) gives

$$
E=TS-pV+\mu N.
$$

Substitution into the [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) $G=E+pV-TS$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{G=\mu(T,p)N}.
$$

For a one-component equilibrium phase, the intensive [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) depends on the intensive state variables $T$ and $p$.
