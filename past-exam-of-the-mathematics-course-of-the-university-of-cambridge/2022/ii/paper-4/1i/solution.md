↑ **Parent:** [1I](../1i.md)

The [simple continued fraction](../../../../../continued-fraction.md) algorithm for $\sqrt{29}$ begins with $a_0=5$. Its complete quotients repeat after

$$
\sqrt{29}
=5+\cfrac1{
2+\cfrac1{
1+\cfrac1{
1+\cfrac1{
2+\cfrac1{
10+\cdots}}}}}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{29}=[5;\overline{2,1,1,2,10}]}.
$$

The convergents through one period are

$$
5,\quad \frac{11}{2},\quad\frac{16}{3},
\quad\frac{27}{5},\quad\frac{70}{13}.
$$

The last one gives the [negative Pell equation](../../../../../pell-type-norm-from-a-periodic-continued-fraction.md) solution

$$
70^2-29\cdot13^2=4900-4901=-1.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{x=70,\qquad y=13}.
$$
