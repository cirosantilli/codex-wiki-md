<h1 id="12g/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The open interval does not have the fixed-point property. The continuous self-map

$$
(x,0)\longmapsto(x/2,0)
$$

has its only possible fixed point at the excluded endpoint $(0,0)$.
