The [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](../../../../../../../brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) says that every continuous self-map of the closed unit disc has a fixed point.

A union of two disjoint closed discs does not have the [fixed-point property](../../../../../../../fixed-point-property.md). Define a map that is constant from the first component to a point in the second and constant from the second component to a point in the first. It is continuous because the components are disjoint and has no fixed point.
