<h1 id="18h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The products $xy$ with $x\in L_1$ and $y\in L_2$ span the [field compositum](../../../../../../field-compositum.md) $L_1L_2$ over $K$, so

$$
[L_1L_2:K]\leq d_1d_2.
$$

The [tower law](../../../../../../tower-law.md) gives

$$
[L_1L_2:K]
=d_1[L_1L_2:L_1]
=d_2[L_1L_2:L_2].
$$

Thus the degree is divisible by both $d_1$ and $d_2$. Since they are coprime, it is divisible by $d_1d_2$. Combining divisibility with the upper bound gives

$$
\boxed{[L_1L_2:K]=d_1d_2}.
$$

Equivalently, finite extensions of coprime degrees are [linearly disjoint field extensions](../../../../../../linear-disjointness.md).
