<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

During [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md), $4\pi G\bar\rho_m=3H^2/2$ and $a\propto t^{2/3}$. Substitution of $\delta_m\propto t^p$ gives $p=2/3,-1$, hence

$$
\boxed{\delta_m=C_1a+C_2a^{-3/2}}.
$$

During [radiation domination](../../../../../../radiation-domination.md), neglecting the rapidly oscillating radiation perturbation and the subdominant matter source leaves

$$
\ddot\delta_m+2H\dot\delta_m=0,
\qquad a\propto t^{1/2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\delta_m=C_3+C_4\log a}.
$$

This logarithmic behavior is the [Mészáros effect](../../../../../../meszaros-effect.md) for subhorizon cold dark matter.
