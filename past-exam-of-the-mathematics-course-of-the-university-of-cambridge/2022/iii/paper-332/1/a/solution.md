<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $n=1-\phi$ be the pore fraction and put $K=k\rho g/\mu$. In the [long-wave approximation](../../../../../../long-wave-approximation.md), the pore pressure is hydrostatic, $p=\rho g(h-z)$, so [Darcy's law](../../../../../../darcy-law.md) gives the horizontal [volume flux](../../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit width

$$
q=\int_0^h-\frac{k}{\mu}p_x\,dz
=-Khh_x.
$$

Local [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) $nh_t+q_x=R$ therefore gives the [Boussinesq equation for an unconfined aquifer](../../../../../../boussinesq-equation-for-an-unconfined-aquifer.md)

$$
\boxed{(1-\phi)h_t=\frac{k\rho g}{\mu}(hh_x)_x+R.}
$$

The river and drainage-divide conditions are

$$
\boxed{h(0,t)=0,
\qquad
q(L,t)=0.}
$$
