<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

After rainfall stops, the late finite-domain solution is the [separable aquifer drawdown](../../../../../../../separable-aquifer-drawdown.md)

$$
h(x,t)\sim\frac{nL^2}{Kt}F(X),
\qquad X=\frac xL.
$$

The dimensionless profile is determined by

$$
\boxed{(FF')'+F=0,
\qquad F(0)=0,
\qquad F'(1)=0.}
$$

If $C_d=\lim_{X\downarrow0}FF'>0$, then

$$
\boxed{q(0,t)\sim-\frac{n^2L^3}{Kt^2}C_d,}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{F(X)\sim(2C_dX)^{1/2},
\qquad
h(x,t)\sim\frac{nL^2}{Kt}\left(\frac{2C_dx}{L}\right)^{1/2}.}
$$

**Thus aquifer thickness decays as $t^{-1}$ and river discharge as $t^{-2}$.**
