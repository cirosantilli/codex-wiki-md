<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a continuous real driving function $U$, solve the [Chordal Loewner equation](../../../../../../chordal-loewner-equation.md)

$$
\partial_tg_t(z)=\frac2{g_t(z)-U_t},
\qquad g_0(z)=z,
$$

up to the first time $T_z$ at which $g_t(z)-U_t$ tends to zero. The generated hulls are

$$
K_t=\{z\in\mathbb H:T_z\leq t\}.
$$

They form an increasing [Loewner chain](../../../../../../loewner-chain.md) of compact H-hulls, satisfy $\operatorname{hcap}(K_t)=2t$, and $g_t$ is the mapping-out function of $K_t$.
