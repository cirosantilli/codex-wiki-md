<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Uniqueness under [hydrodynamic normalization at infinity](../../../../../../hydrodynamic-normalization-at-infinity.md) gives

$$
g_{rA}(z)=r\,g_A(z/r)
$$

and

$$
g_{A+x}(z)=x+g_A(z-x).
$$

Indeed, each right-hand side maps the required complement conformally onto $\mathbb H$ and has expansion $z+O(1/z)$. This is the [scaling and translation of half-plane capacity](../../../../../../scaling-and-translation-of-half-plane-capacity.md).
