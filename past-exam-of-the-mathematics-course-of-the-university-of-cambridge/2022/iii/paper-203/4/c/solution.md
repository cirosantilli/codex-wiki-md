<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Stop after a positive initial segment and map it out. By the [Conformal Markov property of SLE](../../../../../../conformal-markov-property-of-sle.md), the future is a fresh SLE in the remaining domain. Part b says that it hits that domain's boundary away from its current and terminal points. Choosing a bounded crosscut neighborhood whose real-boundary part is shielded from the future forces such a hit to occur on the image of the earlier trace with positive probability. Scale invariance and repeated conditional trials upgrade this to probability one. Thus the trace has self-intersections for $\kappa>4$, in agreement with the [Phase classification of the SLE trace](../../../../../../phase-classification-of-the-sle-trace.md).
