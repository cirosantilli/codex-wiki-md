<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write an amplitude with an external massless vector of momentum $k$ as

$$
\mathcal M=\epsilon_\mu(k)\mathcal M^\mu(k,\ldots).
$$

Changing the polarization representative by $\epsilon_\mu\mapsto\epsilon_\mu+c,k_\mu$ is a [gauge transformation](../../../../../../gauge-transformation.md). The physical amplitude must be unchanged, so the [Ward identity](../../../../../../ward-identity.md) is

$$
\boxed{k_\mu\mathcal M^\mu=0}.
$$

**Thus a longitudinal pure-gauge polarization decouples from the S-matrix. This on-shell statement is the amplitude counterpart of the off-shell [Ward-Takahashi identity](../../../../../../ward-identity.md).**
