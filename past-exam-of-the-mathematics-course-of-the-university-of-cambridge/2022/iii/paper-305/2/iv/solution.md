<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [longitudinal polarization](../../../../../../longitudinal-polarization.md) of a massive vector behaves as $\epsilon_L^\mu(p)\sim p^\mu/m$ at high energy. Generic self-interactions therefore make amplitudes grow as powers of $E/m$, eventually violating [partial-wave unitarity](../../../../../../partial-wave-unitarity.md) and destroying perturbative predictivity. Simply adding a Proca mass also spoils the gauge cancellations that control ultraviolet behavior.

The Standard Model obtains the $W$ and $Z$ masses through the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md) inside a renormalizable gauge theory. [Goldstone bosons](../../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) supply the longitudinal polarizations, and relations among gauge and Higgs couplings enforce cancellations of the growing terms. In particular, Higgs exchange cancels the remaining $E^2$ growth in longitudinal vector-boson scattering.
