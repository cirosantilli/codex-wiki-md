<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Biunitary transformations diagonalize the up- and down-type [Yukawa matrices](../../../../../../yukawa-matrix.md):

$$
U_{uL}^\dagger Y_uU_{uR}=Y_u^{\rm diag},
\qquad
U_{dL}^\dagger Y_dU_{dR}=Y_d^{\rm diag}.
$$

The neutral Higgs couplings are then diagonal, but the charged weak current becomes

$$
\mathcal L_W=\frac g{\sqrt2}\bar u_L\gamma^\mu
V_{\rm CKM}d_LW_\mu^++\mathrm{h.c.},
\qquad
\boxed{V_{\rm CKM}=U_{uL}^\dagger U_{dL}}.
$$

A $3\times3$ [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md) has nine real parameters. Rephasing the six quark mass eigenfields removes five phases because their common phase is baryon number. The [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) therefore has four physical parameters: three mixing angles and one [CP-violating phase](../../../../../../cp-violating-phase.md).
