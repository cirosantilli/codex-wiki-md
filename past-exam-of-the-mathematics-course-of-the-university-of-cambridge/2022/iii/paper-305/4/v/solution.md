<h1 id="4/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The Standard Model has two independent sources of [CP violation](../../../../../../cp-violation.md). Weak CP violation comes from the irreducible phase of the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md); its basis-independent measure is the [Jarlskog invariant](../../../../../../jarlskog-invariant.md). Strong CP violation is governed by

$$
\bar\theta=\theta_{\rm QCD}+\arg\det(Y_uY_d),
$$

which multiplies the [Yang-Mills theta term](../../../../../../yang-mills-theta-term.md) $G_{\mu\nu}^a\widetilde G^{a\mu\nu}$. Quark chiral rephasings shift $\theta_{\rm QCD}$ and the mass-matrix phase oppositely, leaving $\bar\theta$ invariant. The CKM phase and $\bar\theta$ are otherwise independent parameters: observed weak CP violation does not explain why neutron-electric-dipole bounds require $|\bar\theta|\ll1$. This unexplained smallness is the [Strong CP problem](../../../../../../strong-cp-problem.md).
