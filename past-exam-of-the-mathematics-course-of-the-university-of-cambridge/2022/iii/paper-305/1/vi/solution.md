The unique dimension-five operator built solely from Standard Model fields is the [Weinberg operator](../../../../../../weinberg-operator.md)

$$
\frac{c_5}{\Lambda}(L^iH^j)\epsilon_{ij}(L^kH^\ell)\epsilon_{k\ell}+\mathrm{h.c.},
$$

which gives a [Majorana mass term](../../../../../../majorana-mass-term.md) $m_\nu\sim c_5v^2/\Lambda$ after the Higgs condenses. A dimension-six example that mediates [proton decay](../../../../../../proton-decay.md) is

$$
\frac{c_6}{\Lambda^2}\epsilon_{abc}(Q^aQ^b)(Q^cL),
$$

with Lorentz and weak indices contracted appropriately; $u^cu^cd^ce^c/\Lambda^2$ is another. Their dimensions explain why neutrino masses are tiny and proton decay is rare: both probe a high scale, while the stronger proton-lifetime bound places especially severe constraints on baryon-number violation.
