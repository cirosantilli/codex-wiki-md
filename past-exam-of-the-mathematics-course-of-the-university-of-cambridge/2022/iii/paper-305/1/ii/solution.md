↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Asymptotic freedom](../../../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md) means that the QCD [running coupling](../../../../../../running-coupling.md) decreases at high momentum because its [beta function](../../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) is negative near zero. Quarks and gluons therefore interact weakly in sufficiently hard processes, justifying perturbation theory and explaining approximate parton behavior. [Dimensional transmutation](../../../../../../dimensional-transmutation.md) replaces the dimensionless ultraviolet coupling by the [QCD scale](../../../../../../qcd-scale.md) $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}$, defined schematically by

$$
\alpha_s(\mu)\simeq\frac1{b_0\log(\mu^2/\Lambda_{\rm QCD}^2)}.
$$

At energies of order $\Lambda_{\rm QCD}$ the coupling becomes strong, [confinement](../../../../../../confinement.md) and [chiral symmetry breaking](../../../../../../chiral-symmetry-breaking.md) occur, and most visible hadron mass is generated dynamically.
