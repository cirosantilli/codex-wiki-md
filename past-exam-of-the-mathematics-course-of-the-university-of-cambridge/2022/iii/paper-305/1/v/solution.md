<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

After [electroweak symmetry breaking](../../../../../../electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md), the [Higgs field](../../../../../../higgs-field.md) has vacuum expectation value $\langle H\rangle=(0,v/\sqrt2)^T$. A [Yukawa interaction](../../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) $-y_f\bar f_LHf_R+\mathrm{h.c.}$ then becomes a fermion mass with $m_f=y_fv/\sqrt2$. The Higgs [gauge-covariant kinetic term](../../../../../../gauge-covariant-kinetic-term.md) $|D_\mu H|^2$ gives

$$
m_W=\frac{gv}{2},
\qquad
m_Z=\frac{v}{2}\sqrt{g^2+g'^2},
$$

while the radial fluctuation is the massive [Higgs boson](../../../../../../higgs-boson.md). The [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) and all eight [gluons](../../../../../../gluon.md) correspond to unbroken gauge generators and remain massless. In the minimal renormalizable Standard Model, which has no right-handed neutrinos, the neutrinos also remain massless; adding right-handed neutrinos permits Higgs-generated Dirac masses.
