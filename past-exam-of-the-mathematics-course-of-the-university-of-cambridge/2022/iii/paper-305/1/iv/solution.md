<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

With the Standard Model field content, every gauge-invariant, Lorentz-invariant operator of dimension at most four automatically preserves [baryon number](../../../../../../baryon-number.md) and total [lepton number](../../../../../../lepton-number.md). They are therefore [accidental symmetries](../../../../../../accidental-symmetry.md), rather than symmetries imposed in constructing the renormalizable Lagrangian. This explains perturbative conservation in ordinary reactions. They are not exact principles: the electroweak [chiral anomaly](../../../../../../chiral-anomaly.md) violates $B+L$, the dimension-five [Weinberg operator](../../../../../../weinberg-operator.md) violates lepton number, and dimension-six [proton-decay operators](../../../../../../proton-decay-operator.md) can violate baryon number. The combination $B-L$ is anomaly free when a [right-handed neutrino](../../../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md) is included in each generation.
