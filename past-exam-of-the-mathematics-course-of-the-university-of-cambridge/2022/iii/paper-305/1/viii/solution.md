<h1 id="1/viii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Viii](../viii.md)

First, [electroweak gauge invariance](../../../../../../electroweak-gauge-invariance.md) places left-handed quarks in $SU(2)_L$ doublets, pairing one charge-$2/3$ flavor with one charge-$-1/3$ flavor in every generation. Second, cancellation of Standard Model [gauge anomalies](../../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) ties each colored quark doublet and its singlets to a lepton doublet and its singlet; removing either member destroys the hypercharge-anomaly cancellations. Equivalently, the weak charged current and anomaly-free chiral spectrum organize quarks into complete up-down generations, so the number of quark flavors is even.
