<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Constant $c$ and $\lambda$ preserve the six spatial Euclidean isometries, namely three [spatial translations](../../../../../../spatial-translation.md) and three [spatial rotations](../../../../../../spatial-rotation.md). They also preserve the [de Sitter dilation](../../../../../../de-sitter-dilation.md) $(\tau,\mathbf x)\mapsto(s\tau,s\mathbf x)$, under which an equal-time correlator transforms covariantly together with its observation time. For generic $c\ne1$, the preferred propagation speed breaks the three [special conformal transformations](../../../../../../special-conformal-transformation.md). The correlators therefore obey seven of the ten [de Sitter isometries](../../../../../../de-sitter-isometry.md). When $c=1$, the action is fully de Sitter invariant and all ten are restored.
