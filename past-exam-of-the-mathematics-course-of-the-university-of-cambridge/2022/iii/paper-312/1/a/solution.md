<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is

$$
\ddot\varphi+3H\dot\varphi-\frac{c^2}{a^2}\nabla^2\varphi+2H^2\varphi=0.
$$

After a spatial [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) and the change to [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md), $\partial_t=a^{-1}\partial_\tau$, this becomes

$$
\varphi_{\mathbf k}''+2\mathcal H\varphi_{\mathbf k}'
+(c^2k^2+2a^2H^2)\varphi_{\mathbf k}=0.
$$

For the exact [de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../de-sitter-spacetime.md) scale factor $a=-1/(H\tau)$, one has $a''/a=2a^2H^2$. Setting $u_{\mathbf k}=a\varphi_{\mathbf k}$ therefore cancels both the friction term and the effective mass term, leaving

$$
\boxed{(\partial_\tau^2+c^2k^2)(a\varphi_{\mathbf k})=0}.
$$

This is the special cancellation for a [conformally coupled scalar field](../../../../../../conformally-coupled-scalar-field.md).
