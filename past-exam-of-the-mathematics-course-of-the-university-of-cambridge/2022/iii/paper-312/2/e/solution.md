<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Hermitian conjugation of part d gives the corresponding charge insertion on the bra. The [Ward identity](../../../../../../ward-identity.md) $i\langle[Q,\mathcal O]\rangle=\langle\delta_b\mathcal O\rangle$ therefore becomes

$$
\boxed{
\lim_{\mathbf q\to0}a^3b^i\left[
\frac{\dot f_q}{f_q}\langle A_i^V(-\mathbf q)\mathcal O\rangle
-\frac{\dot f_q^*}{f_q^*}\langle\mathcal O A_i^V(\mathbf q)\rangle
\right]
=-i\langle\delta_b\mathcal O\rangle}.
$$

For a neutral operator, $\delta_b\mathcal O=0$, so the two soft limits, multiplied by their respective wavefunctional coefficients, are equal. If $\mathcal O$ is charged, the right side is nonzero. For a product of fields of charges $e_a$ at positions $\mathbf x_a$, it is proportional to $\sum_ae_a b_i x_a^i\langle\mathcal O\rangle$; in momentum space this becomes the corresponding momentum derivative. This is a soft-vector [Ward-Takahashi identity](../../../../../../ward-identity.md).
