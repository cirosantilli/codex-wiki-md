<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the spatial [transverse-longitudinal decomposition](../../../../../../transverse-longitudinal-decomposition.md)

$$
A_0=\phi,
\qquad
A_i=A_i^V+\partial_i\chi,
\qquad
\partial_iA_i^V=0.
$$

Under the [U(1) gauge symmetry](../../../../../../u-1-gauge-symmetry.md) $A_\mu\mapsto A_\mu+\partial_\mu\epsilon$,

$$
\phi\mapsto\phi+\dot\epsilon,
\qquad
\chi\mapsto\chi+\epsilon,
\qquad
A_i^V\mapsto A_i^V.
$$

The assumption $\nabla^2\epsilon\ne0$ makes the longitudinal split unambiguous. The scalar

$$
\boxed{\Phi_A=\phi-\dot\chi}
$$

is therefore [gauge-invariant](../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md).
