For symmetrized [standard perturbation theory density kernels](../../../../../../standard-perturbation-theory-density-kernel.md), the two one-loop contractions are

$$
\boxed{P_{22}(k)=2\int\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}
\left[F_2(\mathbf q,\mathbf k-\mathbf q)\right]^2
P(q)P(|\mathbf k-\mathbf q|)},
$$

and

$$
\boxed{P_{13}(k)=3P(k)\int\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}
F_3(\mathbf k,\mathbf q,-\mathbf q)P(q)}.
$$

The factors $2$ and $3$ count the relevant [Wick contractions](../../../../../../wick-contraction.md). Since the cross-correlation occurs in both orders, the [one-loop matter power spectrum](../../../../../../one-loop-matter-power-spectrum.md) is

$$
\boxed{P_{\rm 1-loop}(k)=a^2P(k)+a^4[P_{22}(k)+2P_{13}(k)].}
$$
