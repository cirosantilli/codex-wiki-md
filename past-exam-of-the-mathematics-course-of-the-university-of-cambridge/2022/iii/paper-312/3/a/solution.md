<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For nonzero momentum, the Fourier transform of the local [bias expansion](../../../../../../bias-expansion.md) is

$$
\delta_g(\mathbf k)=b_1\delta(\mathbf k)
+\frac{b_2}{2}\int\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}
\delta(\mathbf q)\delta(\mathbf k-\mathbf q).
$$

The subtracted variance contributes only at $\mathbf k=0$. Because $\delta$ is a [Gaussian random field](../../../../../../gaussian-random-field.md), its three-point function vanishes and its four-point function factorizes by [Wick theorem](../../../../../../wick-s-theorem.md). At leading order one of the three galaxy fields supplies the quadratic term and the other two supply linear terms. The two cross-contractions cancel the factor $1/2$, giving

$$
\boxed{
\langle\delta_g(\mathbf k_1)\delta_g(\mathbf k_2)
\delta_g(\mathbf k_3)\rangle
=b_1^2b_2(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}\!\left(\sum_a\mathbf k_a\right)
\left[P(k_1)P(k_2)+P(k_2)P(k_3)+P(k_3)P(k_1)\right]}.
$$

**Thus even Gaussian matter fluctuations acquire a nonzero [galaxy bispectrum](../../../../../../galaxy-bispectrum.md) through [local quadratic galaxy bias](../../../../../../local-quadratic-galaxy-bias.md).**
