<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Insert the [photon temperature multipole](../../../../../../../photon-temperature-multipole.md) expansion into the Fourier-space transport equation. The [Legendre polynomial recurrence relation](../../../../../../../legendre-polynomial-recurrence-relation.md) turns multiplication by $\mu$ into nearest-neighbour multipole couplings, while [Orthogonality of Legendre polynomials](../../../../../../../orthogonality-of-legendre-polynomials.md) projects onto a fixed $\ell$. The monopole projection has no collision term because Thomson scattering conserves photon number:

$$
\boxed{\Theta_0'+k\Theta_1=\Phi'}.
$$

The dipole projection receives the electron-velocity source,

$$
\boxed{3\Theta_1'+k(\Theta_2-\Theta_0)
=k\Psi-\Gamma(3\Theta_1+v_e)}.
$$

For every $\ell\geq2$, the collision term damps the anisotropic multipole and the [photon Boltzmann hierarchy](../../../../../../../photon-boltzmann-hierarchy.md) is

$$
\boxed{
\Theta_\ell'+\frac{k}{2\ell+1}
\left[(\ell+1)\Theta_{\ell+1}-\ell\Theta_{\ell-1}\right]
=-\Gamma\Theta_\ell}.
$$

**Thus the [Free-streaming photon Boltzmann equation](../../../../../../../free-streaming-photon-boltzmann-equation.md) moves angular structure between neighbouring multipoles, whereas [Thomson scattering](../../../../../../../thomson-scattering.md) suppresses all multipoles above the dipole in the [tight-coupling approximation](../../../../../../../tight-coupling-approximation.md).**
