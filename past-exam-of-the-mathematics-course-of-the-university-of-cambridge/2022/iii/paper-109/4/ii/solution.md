<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The answer is $\Theta(n)$.** The family

$$
\{\{1,2,3,i\}:4\leq i\leq n\}
$$

has size $n-3$ and pairwise intersection three. For the upper bound, work modulo two. Every member has size $0$ modulo two, while every allowed intersection has residue $1$. The [Frankl-Wilson theorem](../../../../../../frankl-wilson-theorem.md) with $L=\{1\}$ gives $|\mathcal F|\leq\binom n1=n$.
