<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The answer is $\Theta(n^2)$.** All four-sets containing one fixed pair form a family of size

$$
\binom{n-2}{2}=\Theta(n^2)
$$

whose distinct intersections have size two or three. The [Ray-Chaudhuri–Wilson theorem](../../../../../../ray-chaudhuri-wilson-theorem.md) for the two allowed intersection sizes gives the matching $O(n^2)$ upper bound.
