<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**The answer is $\Theta(n^2)$.** Take a [Steiner triple system](../../../../../../steiner-triple-system.md), or a partial one of quadratic size, on $\{2,\ldots,n\}$ and adjoin the fixed point $1$ to every triple. Distinct triples meet in zero or one point, so the resulting four-sets meet in one or two points. This gives $\Omega(n^2)$ members. The [Ray-Chaudhuri–Wilson theorem](../../../../../../ray-chaudhuri-wilson-theorem.md) with the two allowed intersection sizes gives $O(n^2)$.
