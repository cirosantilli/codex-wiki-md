<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a constant-time boundary interval whose endpoints differ by $\pi$ in $\phi$. Its [Ryu–Takayanagi formula](../../../../../../ryu-takayanagi-formula.md) surface is the diameter through $r=0$. Cutting it off at $r_c=\pi/2-\epsilon$, its length is

$$
\ell=2R_{\rm AdS}\int_0^{r_c}\frac{dr}{\cos r}
=2R_{\rm AdS}\log(\sec r_c+\tan r_c)
=2R_{\rm AdS}\log\frac2\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2).
$$

The [holographic entanglement entropy](../../../../../../holographic-entanglement-entropy.md) is therefore

$$
S=\frac\ell{4G}
=\frac{R_{\rm AdS}}{2G}\log\epsilon^{-1}+\text{finite}
=\boxed{\frac c3\log\epsilon^{-1}+\text{finite},}
$$

where the [Brown--Henneaux central charge](../../../../../../brown-henneaux-central-charge.md) $c=3R_{\rm AdS}/(2G)$ was used.
