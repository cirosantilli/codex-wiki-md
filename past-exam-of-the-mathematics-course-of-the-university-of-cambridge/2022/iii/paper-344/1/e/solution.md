<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Use $\partial_x\mapsto iq$ under the [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) and the reality conditions $\phi(-q)=\phi(q)^*$ and $p(-q)=p(q)^*$. With $\Psi=(\phi,p)$, the quadratic free energy is

$$
F=\frac12\sum_q\Psi_i(q)G_{ij}(q)\Psi_j(-q),
$$

where

$$
\boxed{
G(q)=
\begin{pmatrix}
a+\kappa q^2+\gamma q^4&i\lambda q\\
-i\lambda q&\nu
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The opposite imaginary off-diagonal entries make $G(q)$ a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). Reversing the Fourier-sign convention reverses both of those signs without changing any correlator.
