↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Surfactants accumulate at an interface and orient their two chemically distinct ends toward their preferred fluid components. The relaxation found in part c lowers the coefficient of the [gradient energy](../../../../../../gradient-energy.md), and hence generally lowers the [surface tension](../../../../../../surface-tension.md). If the reduction makes $\widetilde\kappa<0$, the positive fourth-gradient term proportional to $\gamma$ can stabilize structure at a nonzero [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md), producing the modulated correlations characteristic of a [microemulsion](../../../../../../microemulsion.md).
