↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The covariance of a centered [multivariate Gaussian distribution](../../../../../../multivariate-gaussian-distribution.md) is the inverse of its quadratic kernel. Writing $A(q)=a+\kappa q^2+\gamma q^4$, one finds

$$
G(q)^{-1}
=\frac1{\nu A(q)-\lambda^2q^2}
\begin{pmatrix}
\nu&-i\lambda q\\
i\lambda q&A(q)
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Consequently the composition [static structure factor](../../../../../../static-structure-factor.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle\phi(q)\phi(-q)\rangle
=\frac1{a+\widetilde\kappa q^2+\gamma q^4}.}
$$
