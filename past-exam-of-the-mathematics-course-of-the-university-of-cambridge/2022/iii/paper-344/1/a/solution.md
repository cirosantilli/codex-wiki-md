<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [binary fluid mixture](../../../../../../binary-fluid-mixture.md), the scalar [compositional order parameter](../../../../../../compositional-order-parameter.md) $\phi(\mathbf r)$ may be taken as the local concentration difference between its two molecular species. Its spatial integral is fixed by their separately fixed total amounts:

$$
\int_V\phi(\mathbf r)\,d\mathbf r=V\bar\phi.
$$

Locally, $\phi$ can change only through transport and therefore obeys a [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md). By contrast, $\mathbf p$ is the local [polar order parameter](../../../../../../polar-order-parameter.md) measuring the mean tail-to-head orientation of the [surfactant](../../../../../../surfactant.md) molecules. Individual molecules can rotate in place, so the integral of $\mathbf p$ need not be conserved.
