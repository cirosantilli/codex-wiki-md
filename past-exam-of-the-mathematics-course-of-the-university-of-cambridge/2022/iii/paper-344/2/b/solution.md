<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The bulk potential gives fluctuations in $\lambda$ a nonzero restoring force, whereas slowly rotating the [nematic director](../../../../../../nematic-director.md) costs only gradients. At lengths much larger than the amplitude correlation length, it is therefore the leading [gradient expansion](../../../../../../gradient-expansion.md) approximation to set $\lambda=\lambda_0$ while retaining $\mathbf n(\mathbf r)$.

Differentiating

$$
Q_{ij}=2\lambda_0\left(n_in_j-\frac12\delta_{ij}\right)
$$

gives

$$
\nabla_iQ_{ij}
=2\lambda_0\left[(\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf n)n_j
+(\mathbf n\mathbin\cdot\nabla)n_j\right].
$$

Substitution into the elastic term yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbb F_{\rm elastic}
=2K\lambda_0^2
\left|(\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf n)\mathbf n
+(\mathbf n\mathbin\cdot\nabla)\mathbf n\right|^2.}
$$
