↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics.md), with $\Delta Q$ defined as heat lost by the particle to the bath, is

$$
\Delta H=W-\Delta Q.
$$

Thus $W-\Delta H=\Delta Q$, and part d becomes the [local detailed balance](../../../../../../local-detailed-balance.md) identity

$$
\boxed{\frac{\mathbb P_F}{\mathbb P_B}=e^{\beta\Delta Q}.}
$$

The logarithmic path-probability ratio is the bath's [entropy production](../../../../../../entropy-production.md) $\Delta S_{\rm bath}=\Delta Q/T$ in units of $k_B$.
