<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md) says that each equilibrium transition is balanced by its time reverse:

$$
P_{\rm eq}(1)\mathbb P_F(1\to2)
=P_{\rm eq}(2)\mathbb P_B(2\to1).
$$

With the [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) $P_{\rm eq}\propto e^{-\beta H}$ and $\Delta H=H_2-H_1$, this requires

$$
\frac{\mathbb P_F}{\mathbb P_B}=e^{-\beta\Delta H}.
$$

Parts a and b instead give $\exp[-(2\zeta/\sigma^2)\Delta H]$. Equality for every pair of endpoints yields the [fluctuation-dissipation relation for a Langevin particle](../../../../../../fluctuation-dissipation-relation-for-a-langevin-particle.md)

$$
\boxed{\sigma^2=\frac{2\zeta}{\beta}=2\zeta k_BT.}
$$
