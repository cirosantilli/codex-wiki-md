<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under the global [U(1) gauge symmetry](../../../../../../u-1-gauge-symmetry.md), the phases in $\bar\psi\psi$ cancel and $\alpha$ is constant, so

$$
D_\mu(e^{i\alpha}\psi)=e^{i\alpha}D_\mu\psi.
$$

Both the fermion term and $F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}$ are therefore invariant. To extract the [Noether current](../../../../../../noether-current.md), temporarily promote $\alpha$ to a function. The variation of the action is

$$
\delta S=i\int d^4x\,(\partial_\mu\alpha)\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi
=-i\int d^4x\,\alpha\,\partial_\mu j^\mu,
\qquad j^\mu=\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi.
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) then imply the [current conservation](../../../../../../conserved-current.md) law $\partial_\mu j^\mu=0$.
