<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In components the [BRST transformation](../../../../../../brst-symmetry.md) is

$$
sA_\mu^a=\partial_\mu c^a+gf^{abc}A_\mu^bc^c,
\qquad
sc^a=-\frac g2f^{abc}c^bc^c,
\qquad
s\bar c^a=B^a,
\qquad
sB^a=0.
$$

The last two equations immediately give $s^2\bar c=s^2B=0$. Applying the graded Leibniz rule to $s^2c$ produces a sum of three ghost monomials whose coefficient is the [Jacobi identity](../../../../../../jacobi-identity.md) $f^{e[ab}f^{c]de}$, so $s^2c=0$. Finally,

$$
s^2A_\mu=D_\mu(sc)-ig[D_\mu c,c]=0;
$$

the derivative terms cancel by anticommutation of the [Faddeev-Popov ghost fields](../../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md), and the remaining terms again cancel by the Jacobi identity. Hence $s^2=0$ on every field: $s$ is a nilpotent Grassmann-odd differential.
