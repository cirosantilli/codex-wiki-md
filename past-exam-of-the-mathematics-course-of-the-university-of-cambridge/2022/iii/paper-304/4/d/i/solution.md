<h1 id="4/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $L[A^a]=\partial^\mu A_\mu^a$, eliminating the [Nakanishi-Lautrup field](../../../../../../../nakanishi-lautrup-field.md) gives the [covariant gauge](../../../../../../../covariant-gauge.md) Lagrangian

$$
\mathcal L=\frac14F_{\mu\nu}^aF^{\mu\nu,a}
+\frac1{2\xi}(\partial^\mu A_\mu^a)^2
-\bar c^a\partial^\mu(D_\mu c)^a.
$$

The first term supplies the gluon kinetic term, three-gluon vertex, and four-gluon vertex. The second makes the quadratic gauge-field operator invertible. The last supplies the ghost propagator and ghost-antighost-gluon vertex.

The nonzero one-loop contributions to the [gluon propagator](../../../../../../../gluon-propagator.md) are a gluon bubble with two three-gluon vertices and a closed ghost bubble with two ghost-antighost-gluon vertices. A four-gluon tadpole is also present with a cutoff regulator; for massless fields it is a scaleless integral and vanishes in [dimensional regularization](../../../../../../../dimensional-regularization.md).
