<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substituting $\delta A_\mu=g^{-1}\partial_\mu\alpha-i[A_\mu,\alpha]$ into the curvature and collecting terms gives the covariant transformation

$$
\boxed{\delta F_{\mu\nu}=-i[F_{\mu\nu},\alpha]}.
$$

Equivalently, $\delta F_{\mu\nu}^a=f^{abc}F_{\mu\nu}^b\alpha^c$ in the stated conventions. Since $F_{\mu\nu}^aF^{\mu\nu,a}$ is proportional to $\operatorname{tr}(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})$, cyclicity of the [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) gives

$$
\delta\operatorname{tr}(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})
=-i\operatorname{tr}([F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu},\alpha])=0.
$$

**Thus the [Yang-Mills action](../../../../../../yang-mills-action.md) is [gauge-invariant](../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md).**
