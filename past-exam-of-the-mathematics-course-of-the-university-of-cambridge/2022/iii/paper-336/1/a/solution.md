<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $L=\log(1/\epsilon)$. Taking logarithms gives $x^2+\log x=L$. Equivalently, $2x^2=W(2/\epsilon^2)$ in terms of the [Lambert W function](../../../../../../lambert-w-function.md). Iteration for large $L$ gives $x^2=L-\tfrac12\log L+O((\log L)/L)$ and hence

$$
\boxed{x=\sqrt L-\frac{\log L}{4\sqrt L}
+O\left(\frac{(\log L)^2}{L^{3/2}}\right).}
$$
