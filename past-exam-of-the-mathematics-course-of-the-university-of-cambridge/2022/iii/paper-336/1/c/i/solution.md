↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $F(t)=\operatorname{sech}\alpha\sinh t-t$, the relevant [saddle point](../../../../../../../saddle-point.md) is $t=\alpha$, where $F(\alpha)=\tanh\alpha-\alpha$ and $F''(\alpha)=\tanh\alpha$. The [method of steepest descent](../../../../../../../method-of-steepest-descent.md) gives

$$
\boxed{J_\nu(\nu\operatorname{sech}\alpha)
\sim\frac{\exp[\nu(\tanh\alpha-\alpha)]}
{\sqrt{2\pi\nu\tanh\alpha}}}
\qquad(\alpha>0).
$$
