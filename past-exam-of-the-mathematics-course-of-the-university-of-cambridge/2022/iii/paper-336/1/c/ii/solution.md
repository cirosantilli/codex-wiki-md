<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At $\alpha=0$, the ordinary saddles coalesce at zero and $\sinh t-t=t^3/6+O(t^5)$. The contributing scale is $t=O(\nu^{-1/3})$. The standard [cubic saddle-point approximation](../../../../../../../cubic-saddle-point-approximation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{J_\nu(\nu)
\sim\frac{2^{1/3}}{3^{2/3}\Gamma(2/3)}\nu^{-1/3}.}
$$

Equivalently, the coefficient is $2^{1/3}\operatorname{Ai}(0)$.
