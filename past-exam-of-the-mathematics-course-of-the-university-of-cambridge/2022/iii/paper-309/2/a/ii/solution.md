<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Under an infinitesimal [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) generated by $\xi^a$,

$$
\delta g_{ab}=2\nabla_{(a}\xi_{b)},
\qquad
\delta\omega_a=\mathcal L_\xi\omega_a
=\xi^c\nabla_c\omega_a+\omega_c\nabla_a\xi^c.
$$

Insert these variations into

$$
\delta S_{\rm matter}=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}
\left(-\frac12T^{ab}\delta g_{ab}+E^a\delta\omega_a\right)
$$

and integrate derivatives of $\xi^a$ by parts. Diffeomorphism invariance and arbitrariness of $\xi^b$ give the [Noether identity](../../../../../../../noether-identity.md)

$$
\boxed{\nabla_aT^a{}_b=E^a\nabla_b\omega_a-\nabla_a(E^a\omega_b)}.
$$

On the covector equation of motion $E^a=0$, this reduces to [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md), $\nabla_aT^a{}_b=0$.
