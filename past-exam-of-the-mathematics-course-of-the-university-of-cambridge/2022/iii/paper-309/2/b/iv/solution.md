<h1 id="2/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Choose any tangent vector $V^a\in T_p\Sigma$ and let the affinely parametrized geodesic with initial tangent $V^a$ start at $p$. If $\Sigma$ is [totally geodesic](../../../../../../../totally-geodesic-hypersurface.md), then $U^an_a$ remains zero. Its initial derivative is therefore zero. Part iii, with $U^an_a=0$ at $p$, gives

$$
K_{ab}V^aV^b=0.
$$

This holds for every tangent $V$. Since $K$ is symmetric, the [polarization identity](../../../../../../../polarization-identity.md) implies

$$
\boxed{K_{ab}=0}.
$$
