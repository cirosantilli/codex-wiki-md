<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using [Cartan's second structure equation](../../../../../../cartan-s-second-structure-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Theta_{01}=\frac12e^0\wedge e^1,
\qquad \Theta_{02}=\frac12e^0\wedge e^2,
\qquad \Theta_{12}=\frac12e^1\wedge e^2}.
$$

Since $\Theta_{\mu\nu}=R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}e^\rho\wedge e^\sigma/2$, the independent nonzero lowered [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) components are

$$
\boxed{R_{0101}=R_{0202}=R_{1212}=\frac12},
$$

with all others determined by the symmetries of the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md).
