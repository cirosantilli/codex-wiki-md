<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under [parallel transport](../../../../../../../parallel-transport.md), the gyroscope spin obeys $u^b\nabla_b s^a=0$, and its freely falling circular orbit is a [geodesic](../../../../../../../geodesic.md), $u^b\nabla_bu^a=0$. Metric compatibility therefore gives

$$
u^c\nabla_c(s^au_a)
=(u^c\nabla_cs^a)u_a+s^au^c\nabla_cu_a=0.
$$

**Thus $s^au_a$ is constant along the orbit.**
