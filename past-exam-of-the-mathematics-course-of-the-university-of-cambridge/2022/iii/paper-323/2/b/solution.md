<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every ancillary system,

$$
(\Lambda\otimes\operatorname{id})(X)
=\sum_k(A_k\otimes I)X(A_k^\dagger\otimes I)\geq0
$$

when $X\geq0$, proving complete positivity. Cyclicity of trace gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}\Lambda(\rho)
=\operatorname{Tr}\left(\rho\sum_kA_k^\dagger A_k\right).
$$

This equals $\operatorname{Tr}\rho$ for every $\rho$ exactly when

$$
\boxed{\sum_kA_k^\dagger A_k=I},
$$

the trace-preserving condition in the [Kraus representation](../../../../../../kraus-representation.md).
