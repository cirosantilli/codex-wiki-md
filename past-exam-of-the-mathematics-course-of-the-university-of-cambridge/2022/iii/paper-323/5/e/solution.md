<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For an [anti-degradable quantum channel](../../../../../../anti-degradable-quantum-channel.md), a channel from $E$ simulates $Q$. The [data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy](../../../../../../data-processing-inequality-for-quantum-relative-entropy.md) applied to [quantum mutual information](../../../../../../quantum-mutual-information.md) gives $I(R:Q)\leq I(R:E)$. Part d then implies $I_c(\Lambda,\rho)\leq0$ for every input. Part c supplies a pure input attaining zero, so

$$
\boxed{Q^{(1)}(\Lambda)=\max_\rho I_c(\Lambda,\rho)=0}.
$$
