<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Because $\sigma_{RQE}$ is pure, $S(RQ)=S(E)$ and $S(RE)=S(Q)$. Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
I(R:Q)-I(R:E)
&=[S(R)+S(Q)-S(RQ)]\\
&\quad-[S(R)+S(E)-S(RE)]\\
&=2[S(Q)-S(E)].
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{I_c(\Lambda,\rho)=\frac12[I(R:Q)-I(R:E)]}.
$$
