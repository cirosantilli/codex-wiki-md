↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Schmidt decomposition](../../../../../../schmidt-decomposition.md) is $|\psi\rangle=\sum_js_j|a_j\rangle|b_j\rangle$. Its reduced states are $\rho_A=\sum_js_j^2|a_j\rangle\langle a_j|$ and $\rho_B=\sum_js_j^2|b_j\rangle\langle b_j|$. They have the same nonzero eigenvalues, so

$$
\boxed{S(\rho_A)=S(\rho_B)}.
$$
