<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose a basis $|a\rangle$ of $H$ and define Kraus operators from $H$ to $\mathbb C^m$ by

$$
K_{ka}=|k\rangle\langle a|\sqrt{\Lambda_k}.
$$

Then

$$
\sum_{k,a}K_{ka}\rho K_{ka}^\dagger
=\sum_k|k\rangle\langle k|\operatorname{Tr}(\Lambda_k\rho),
$$

and

$$
\sum_{k,a}K_{ka}^\dagger K_{ka}
=\sum_k\Lambda_k=I.
$$

The displayed map is therefore a completely positive trace-preserving [measure-and-prepare channel](../../../../../../measure-and-prepare-channel.md).
