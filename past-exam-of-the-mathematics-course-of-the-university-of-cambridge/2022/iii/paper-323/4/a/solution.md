<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For Hermitian $X$, $\|X\|_1=\max_{-I\leq H\leq I}\operatorname{Tr}(HX)$. The adjoint of a trace-preserving completely positive map is unital and positive, so $-I\leq H\leq I$ implies $-I\leq\Lambda^*(H)\leq I$. Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|\Lambda(\rho)-\Lambda(\sigma)\|_1
&=\max_{-I\leq H\leq I}\operatorname{Tr}[\Lambda^*(H)(\rho-\sigma)]\\
&\leq\|\rho-\sigma\|_1.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Hence [trace distance](../../../../../../trace-distance.md) is contractive under quantum channels.**
