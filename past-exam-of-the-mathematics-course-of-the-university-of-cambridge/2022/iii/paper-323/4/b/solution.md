<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $X=\rho-\sigma=X_+-X_-$ be its positive-negative spectral decomposition and let $P$ project onto the support of $X_+$. Since $\operatorname{Tr}X=0$, $\operatorname{Tr}X_+=\operatorname{Tr}X_-=\|X\|_1/2$. Thus

$$
|\operatorname{Tr}(PX)|+|\operatorname{Tr}((I-P)X)|
=\operatorname{Tr}X_++\operatorname{Tr}X_-
=\boxed{\|\rho-\sigma\|_1}.
$$
