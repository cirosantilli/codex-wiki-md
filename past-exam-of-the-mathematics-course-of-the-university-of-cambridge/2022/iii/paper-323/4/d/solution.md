<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For any POVM, part c followed by trace-distance contraction gives

$$
\sum_k|\operatorname{Tr}(\Lambda_k\rho)-\operatorname{Tr}(\Lambda_k\sigma)|
\leq\|\rho-\sigma\|_1.
$$

For the binary POVM $\{P,I-P\}$ from part b, equality holds. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\|\rho-\sigma\|_1
=\max_{\{\Lambda_k\}}\sum_k
|\operatorname{Tr}(\Lambda_k\rho)-\operatorname{Tr}(\Lambda_k\sigma)|}.
$$
