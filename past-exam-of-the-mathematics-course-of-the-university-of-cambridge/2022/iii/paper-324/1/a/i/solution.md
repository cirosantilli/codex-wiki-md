↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A $Z$ measurement is a [quantum measurement in the computational basis](../../../../../../../quantum-measurement-in-the-computational-basis.md). To measure $X$, apply a [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md), measure $Z$, and apply another [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) to the measured qubit. Since $HXH=Z$, this gives the $X$ outcome and leaves the qubit in the corresponding $X$ [eigenstate](../../../../../../../eigenstate.md).
