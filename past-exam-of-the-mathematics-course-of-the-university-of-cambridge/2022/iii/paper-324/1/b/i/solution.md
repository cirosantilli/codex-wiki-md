<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $P|\psi\rangle=\lambda_P|\psi\rangle$, $P^2=I$, and $P$ [anticommutes](../../../../../../../anticommutator.md) with $Q$,

$$
\langle\psi|Q|\psi\rangle
=\langle\psi|PQP|\psi\rangle
=-\langle\psi|Q|\psi\rangle,
$$

so the [expectation value](../../../../../../../expectation-value.md) of $Q$ is zero. The two [spectral projectors](../../../../../../../spectral-projector.md) of the Hermitian [Pauli operator](../../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) $Q$ are $(I\mathbin\pm Q)/2$, and hence the [Born rule](../../../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Pr(\lambda_Q=\pm1)
=\left\langle\psi\middle|\frac{I\pm Q}{2}\middle|\psi\right\rangle
=\frac12.}
$$
