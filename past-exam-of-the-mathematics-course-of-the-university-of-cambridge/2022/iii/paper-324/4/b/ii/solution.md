<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Regard bit strings as the [elementary abelian group](../../../../../../../elementary-abelian-group.md) $G=(\mathbb Z_2)^n$ under [bitwise exclusive or](../../../../../../../exclusive-or.md). The promise says

$$
f(x)=f(y)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x\mathbin\oplus y\in H,
\qquad
H=\{0^n,p\}.
$$

**Thus $f$ is constant exactly on the cosets of $H$ and distinct between them. Determining the hidden subgroup determines its nonzero element $p$, so this is [Simon's problem](../../../../../../../simon-s-problem.md) as an instance of the [hidden subgroup problem](../../../../../../../hidden-subgroup-problem.md).**
