<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The relation $z^{2^n}=1$ makes $z$ a [root of unity](../../../../../../../root-of-unity.md), so $|z|=1$ and normalization gives $T=2^{n/2}$. Write the [binary expansion](../../../../../../../binary-expansion.md) $y=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}2^jy_j$. Then $z^y=\prod_j(z^{2^j})^{y_j}$, and hence

$$
|\phi\rangle
=\bigotimes_{j=0}^{n-1}
\frac{|0\rangle+z^{2^j}|1\rangle}{\sqrt2},
$$

up to the convention for ordering the binary digits. This explicit [tensor product](../../../../../../../tensor-product.md) of one-qubit states proves that $|\phi\rangle$ is a [product state](../../../../../../../product-state.md).
