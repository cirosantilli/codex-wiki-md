↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The vertices $v_2,v_3,v_5$ form a [triangle in a graph](../../../../../../../triangle-in-a-graph.md), so every [cut of a graph](../../../../../../../cut-graph-theory.md) leaves at least one of their three edges uncut. Hence the cut size is at most four. Take

$$
S_1=\{v_2,v_5\},
\qquad
S_2=\{v_1,v_3,v_4\}.
$$

The crossing edges are $(1,5),(2,3),(3,5),(4,5)$, so $C=4$. The upper bound is attained and this is a [maximum cut](../../../../../../../maximum-cut.md).
