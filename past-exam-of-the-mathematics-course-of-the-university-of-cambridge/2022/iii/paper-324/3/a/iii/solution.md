<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $A=X\otimes X\otimes I$ and $B=Z\otimes I\otimes Z$. The two [Pauli operators](../../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) anticommute because their local factors anticommute at exactly one qubit. Since $A^2=B^2=I$, the mixed terms cancel and

$$
H^2=(5A-4B)^2=25I+16I=41I,
\qquad
H^4=1681I.
$$

This is a scalar, or $0$-local, Hamiltonian, so the smallest value is $\boxed{k=0}$.
