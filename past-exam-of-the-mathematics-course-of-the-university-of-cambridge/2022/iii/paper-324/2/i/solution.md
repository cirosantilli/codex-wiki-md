An $n$-qubit [stabilizer state](../../../../../../stabilizer-state.md) is the unique simultaneous $+1$ [eigenstate](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) of an [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md) $G$ of [Pauli operators](../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) having $2^n$ elements and not containing $-I$. The group $G$ is the state's [stabilizer group](../../../../../../stabilizer-group.md); equivalently, it is generated by $n$ independent commuting Hermitian Pauli operators.
