<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The administrative data include only encounters with $M=1$. Conditioning on the stop indicator selects on the [collider](../../../../../../collider.md)

$$
D\to M\leftarrow U,
$$

which opens the noncausal path $D\leftrightarrow U\to Y$ and creates [collider bias](../../../../../../collider-bias.md). The equal entries estimate only the selected risks $\mathbb P(Y=1\mid D=d,M=1)$. They ignore racial differences in the probability of being stopped and therefore do not identify the total [causal effect](../../../../../../causal-effect.md) of race on violence. This is also [selection bias](../../../../../../selection-bias.md), because encounters with $M=0$ never enter the dataset.
