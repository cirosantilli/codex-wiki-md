<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $Y(d,m)$ be the potential violence outcome if race were set to $d$ and stop status to $m$. The substantive assumption is the [structural zero](../../../../../../structural-zero.md)

$$
Y(d,0)=0
\qquad\text{for every unit and }d\in\{0,1\}.
$$

Equivalently, with the natural stop status $M(d)$, $M(d)=0$ implies $Y(d)=Y(d,M(d))=0$.
