<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

At stationarity,

$$
\alpha P_1^{\rm ss}=\frac{\alpha\beta}{\alpha+\beta}
=\beta P_2^{\rm ss}.
$$

**Thus every edge current vanishes and the two-state system satisfies [detailed balance](../../../../../../detailed-balance.md). A network containing only one undirected edge cannot sustain a stationary cycle current.**
