<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply axial momentum balance to the fluid between the remote sections $x_1$ and $x_2$. The pressure forces on the end discs, the integrated tube-wall shear, and the force exerted by the sphere are the only resultant axial forces. The sphere is [force-free](../../../../../../force-free.md), so its contribution vanishes and

$$
\boxed{
a\int_{x_1}^{x_2}\int_0^{2\pi}
\sigma_{rx}(a,\theta,x)\,d\theta\,dx
=\pi a^2[p(x_2)-p(x_1)].}
$$

This is the [wall-shear and pressure-drop balance in a tube](../../../../../../wall-shear-and-pressure-drop-balance-in-a-tube.md).
