<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [Papkovich–Neuber representation](../../../../../../papkovich-neuber-representation.md) in the form

$$
\mu\mathbf u=\boldsymbol\Phi-\frac12\nabla(\mathbf x\mathbin\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi)+\nabla\chi,
\qquad
p=-\nabla\mathbin\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi,
$$

where $\boldsymbol\Phi$ and $\chi$ are [harmonic functions](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md). A torque is a [axial vector](../../../../../../pseudovector.md), so rotational covariance and decay select the harmonic vector field

$$
\boldsymbol\Phi=\frac{\mathbf G\times\mathbf x}{8\pi r^3},
\qquad \chi=0.
$$

Here $\mathbf x\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi=0$ and $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\Phi=0$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf u(\mathbf x)=\frac{\mathbf G\times\mathbf x}{8\pi\mu r^3},\qquad p=0.}
$$

This [rotlet](../../../../../../rotlet.md) equals the [rotating sphere in Stokes flow](../../../../../../rotating-sphere-in-stokes-flow.md)

$$
\mathbf u=\frac{a^3}{r^3}\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf x
$$

when $\boldsymbol\Omega=\mathbf G/(8\pi\mu a^3)$. It satisfies the [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) on $r=a$, decays at infinity, and its [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](../../../../../../newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md) transmits the applied couple $\mathbf G$.
