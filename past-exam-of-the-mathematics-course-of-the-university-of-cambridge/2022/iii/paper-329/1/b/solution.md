<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $S(\mathbf x)=\mathbf x\cdot\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf x$. Since $\mathbf u=-Sr^{-5}\mathbf x$ and $\nabla\times\mathbf x=0$, the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf u
=\nabla(-Sr^{-5})\times\mathbf x.
$$

For the symmetric [stresslet](../../../../../../force-dipole-flow.md) tensor $\mathbf A$,

$$
\nabla(Sr^{-5})=2r^{-5}\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf x-5Sr^{-7}\mathbf x.
$$

The final term is parallel to $\mathbf x$ and drops out of the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md), leaving

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol\omega(\mathbf x)=\frac{2\mathbf x\times(\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf x)}{r^5}.}
$$
