<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a positive integer $d$, define

$$
|\mathcal A_d|=\sum_{\substack{n\geq1\\d\mid n}}a_n.
$$

The sequence $\mathcal A$ has [sieve distribution](../../../../../../sieve-distribution.md) $(X,P_z,g,(r_d))$ when $g$ is a [multiplicative arithmetic function](../../../../../../multiplicative-function.md) on the squarefree divisors of $P_z$, with $0\leq g(p)<1$, and

$$
|\mathcal A_d|=Xg(d)+r_d
$$

for every $d\mid P_z$. The function $g(d)$ models the local density of divisibility by $d$, while $r_d$ is the remainder.
