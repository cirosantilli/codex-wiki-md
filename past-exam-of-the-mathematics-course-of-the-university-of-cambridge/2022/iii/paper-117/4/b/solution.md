<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the normalized [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md)

$$
\langle g,h\rangle=\mathbb E_{n\leq N}g(n)h(n)=\frac1N\sum_{n\leq N}g(n)h(n).
$$

The [test-function seminorm](../../../../../../test-function-seminorm.md) generated by $\mathcal F$ and its [dual test-function norm](../../../../../../dual-test-function-norm.md) are

$$
\|g\|_{\mathcal F}=\sup_{f\in\mathcal F}|\langle g,f\rangle|,
\qquad
\|g\|_{\mathcal F}^{*}=\sup_{\|h\|_{\mathcal F}\leq1}|\langle g,h\rangle|.
$$

The first quantity may only be a [seminorm](../../../../../../seminorm.md) if $\mathcal F$ does not separate all functions in $\mathcal H_N$; correspondingly, the second may be infinite outside the linear span detected by $\mathcal F$.
