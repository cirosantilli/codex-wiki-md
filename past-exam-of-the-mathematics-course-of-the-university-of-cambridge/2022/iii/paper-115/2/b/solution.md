<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $(\theta^1,\ldots,\theta^n)$ be the [dual basis](../../../../../../dual-basis.md) of the positively oriented [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$. By the definition of the [Riemannian volume form](../../../../../../riemannian-volume-form.md),

$$
\omega_X=\theta^1\wedge\cdots\wedge\theta^n.
$$

The [interior product of a differential form](../../../../../../interior-product.md) with the outward unit normal is

$$
\iota_{v_1}\omega_X=\theta^2\wedge\cdots\wedge\theta^n.
$$

The vectors $(v_2,\ldots,v_n)$ form a positive orthonormal frame of $T\partial X$ by the [outward-normal-first boundary orientation](../../../../../../outward-normal-first-boundary-orientation.md). Consequently the pullback of the last display is the positive unit boundary volume form:

$$
\boxed{\omega_{\partial X}=F^*(\iota_{v_1}\omega_X).}
$$
