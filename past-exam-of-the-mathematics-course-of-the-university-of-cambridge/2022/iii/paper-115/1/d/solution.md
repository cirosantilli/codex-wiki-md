<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $x\in S^2\subset\mathbb R^3$, let $F(x)=x^T$, viewed as a nonzero $1$ by $3$ matrix. This defines a [smooth map](../../../../../../smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) $F:S^2\to X_{1,3}$. Its pulled-back fiber is

$$
(F^*E_{1,3})_x=\ker x^T=x^\perp=T_xS^2,
$$

so the evident fiberwise identity gives an isomorphism of [vector bundles](../../../../../../vector-bundle.md) $F^*E_{1,3}\cong TS^2$.

If $E_{1,3}$ were a [trivial vector bundle](../../../../../../trivial-vector-bundle.md), its [pullback](../../../../../../pullback-vector-bundle.md) would be trivial. A trivial rank-two bundle has a [nowhere-zero section](../../../../../../nowhere-zero-section.md), whereas the assumed form of the [Hairy ball theorem](../../../../../../hairy-ball-theorem.md) says that the [tangent bundle](../../../../../../tangent-bundle.md) $TS^2$ does not. Hence $E_{1,3}$ is nontrivial.
