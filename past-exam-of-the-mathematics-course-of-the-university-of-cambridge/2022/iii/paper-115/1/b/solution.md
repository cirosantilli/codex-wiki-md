<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Define the [smooth map](../../../../../../smooth-map-between-manifolds.md)

$$
\Phi:X_{m,n}\times\mathbb R^n\longrightarrow\mathbb R^m,
\qquad \Phi(M,v)=Mv.
$$

At $(M,v)$ its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) in the direction $(A,w)$ is

$$
D\Phi_{(M,v)}(A,w)=Av+Mw.
$$

The restriction to variations $(0,w)$ is the [surjective linear map](../../../../../../surjective-linear-map.md) $M:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$, since $M$ has full row rank. Thus $0$ is a [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md) of $\Phi$. The [regular level set theorem](../../../../../../regular-level-set-theorem.md) now shows that

$$
E_{m,n}=\Phi^{-1}(0)
$$

is an [embedded submanifold](../../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md) of $X_{m,n}\times\mathbb R^n$, of dimension $mn+n-m$.
