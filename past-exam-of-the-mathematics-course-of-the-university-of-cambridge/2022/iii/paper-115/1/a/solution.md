<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An $m$ by $n$ matrix has [matrix rank](../../../../../../matrix-rank.md) $m$ exactly when at least one of its $m$ by $m$ minors has nonzero [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md). For each multi-index $I$, the set

$$
U_I=\{M:\det M_I\ne0\}
$$

is [open](../../../../../../open-set.md) in the [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of all real $m$ by $n$ matrices, because the determinant is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). Their union is $X_{m,n}$, so $X_{m,n}$ is itself open in $\mathbb R^{mn}$. It therefore inherits the standard [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) structure and has [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $mn$. This is the [full-row-rank matrix manifold](../../../../../../full-row-rank-matrix-manifold.md).
