<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [principal connection](../../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) with connection form $\mathcal A$, the [horizontal distribution of a principal connection](../../../../../../horizontal-distribution-of-a-principal-connection.md) is $H=\ker\mathcal A$, the complement of the tangent spaces to the $G$-orbits. Its [curvature of a principal connection](../../../../../../curvature-of-a-principal-connection.md) is

$$
\mathcal F=d\mathcal A+\frac12[\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal A].
$$

If $X,Y$ are horizontal vector fields, then $\mathcal A(X)=\mathcal A(Y)=0$, and hence

$$
\mathcal F(X,Y)=d\mathcal A(X,Y)=-\mathcal A([X,Y]).
$$

The [Frobenius theorem](../../../../../../frobenius-theorem.md) says that $H$ is integrable exactly when $[X,Y]$ is horizontal for all horizontal $X,Y$. The displayed identity makes this equivalent to the vanishing of the horizontal two-form $\mathcal F$, hence to $\mathcal F=0$. Thus the horizontal distribution is integrable exactly for a [flat principal connection](../../../../../../flat-principal-connection.md).
