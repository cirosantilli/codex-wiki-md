<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\xi$ in the [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$, set $\Phi^t=R_{\exp(t\xi)}$. The [one-parameter subgroup](../../../../../../one-parameter-subgroup.md) law gives the flow law, and

$$
\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_{0}g\exp(t\xi)=(dL_g)_e\xi=l_\xi(g),
$$

so this is the global flow of the [left-invariant vector field](../../../../../../left-invariant-vector-field.md) $l_\xi$.

For $f(g)=R_g^*\alpha$, every tangent vector at $g$ is the initial velocity of $g\exp(t\xi)$ for some $\xi$. Since $R_{g\exp(t\xi)}=R_{\exp(t\xi)}\circ R_g$,

$$
\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_0f(g\exp(t\xi))
=R_g^*(\mathcal L_{l_\xi}\alpha).
$$

**Thus all $\mathcal L_{l_\xi}\alpha$ vanish exactly when every derivative of $f$ vanishes. This is equivalent to $f$ being a [locally constant function](../../../../../../locally-constant-function.md).**
