↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A real [linear operator](../../../../../../../linear-operator.md) $\mathcal L$ on an [inner product space](../../../../../../../inner-product-space.md) is a [positive-definite operator](../../../../../../../positive-definite-operator.md) when it is self-adjoint and

$$
\langle\mathcal Lu,u\rangle>0
$$

for every nonzero $u$ in its domain. In the variational setting one normally requires the stronger uniform estimate $\langle\mathcal Lu,u\rangle\geq c\|u\|_V^2$ for some $c>0$, which is [coercivity](../../../../../../../coercive-function.md).
