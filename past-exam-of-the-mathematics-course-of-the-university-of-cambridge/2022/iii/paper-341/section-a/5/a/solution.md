<h1 id="section-a/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Near $t=0$, take $\Omega(t)=\log F(t)$ to be the continuous local [matrix logarithm](../../../../../../../matrix-logarithm.md) with $\Omega(0)=0$. The symmetry identity gives $F(-t)=F(t)^{-1}$, and uniqueness of this logarithm yields

$$
\Omega(-t)=\log(F(t)^{-1})=-\log F(t)=-\Omega(t).
$$

**Thus $\Omega$ is an [odd function](../../../../../../../odd-function.md). The local-logarithm qualification is necessary because the [matrix exponential](../../../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) is not globally injective; the statement is naturally understood either near $t=0$ or as an identity of formal power series.**
