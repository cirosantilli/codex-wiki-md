<h1 id="section-a/5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The three substeps have total length $2\alpha+(1-2\alpha)=1$. Their leading cubic defects add, so cancellation requires

$$
2\alpha^3+(1-2\alpha)^3=0.
$$

Taking the real cube root and solving gives

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac1{2-2^{1/3}}.}
$$

This is the coefficient in the [higher-order composition of a symmetric splitting](../../../../../../../higher-order-composition-of-a-symmetric-splitting.md); the middle substep $1-2\alpha$ is negative.
