<h1 id="section-a/5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The composition is palindromic, and hence

$$
G_\alpha(-t)=F(-\alpha t)F(-(1-2\alpha)t)F(-\alpha t)
=G_\alpha(t)^{-1}.
$$

Thus $G_\alpha$ is symmetric. Part c cancels the cubic term in its odd [formal logarithm](../../../../../../../formal-logarithm.md); symmetry forbids a fourth-degree term, so the next possible defect has degree five. Therefore

$$
\boxed{G_\alpha(t)=e^{t(A+B)}+O(t^5),}
$$

which makes the composition a fourth-order method.
