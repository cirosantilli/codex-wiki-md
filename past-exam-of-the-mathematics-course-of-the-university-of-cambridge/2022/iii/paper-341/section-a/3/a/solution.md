<h1 id="section-a/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $H=\partial_x^2-V(x)$. The real potential and [periodic boundary condition](../../../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) make $H$ a [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) on the periodic domain. Since $u_t=-iHu$,

$$
\frac d{dt}\|u(t)\|_{L^2}^2
=2\operatorname{Re}\langle u,u_t\rangle
=2\operatorname{Re}\bigl(-i\langle u,Hu\rangle\bigr)=0,
$$

because the [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product.md) $\langle u,Hu\rangle$ is real. Thus the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) preserves the $L^2$ norm.
