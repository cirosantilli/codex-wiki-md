<h1 id="section-a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A highest-order method in this family has order $s+1$. The [Second Dahlquist barrier](../../../../../../../second-dahlquist-barrier.md) says that an irreducible [A-stable](../../../../../../../a-stability.md) multistep method has order at most two, so $s+1\leq2$ and hence $s=1$. Part b then leaves only the [trapezoidal rule](../../../../../../../trapezoidal-rule.md). Its amplification factor is

$$
R(z)=\frac{1+z/2}{1-z/2},
$$

and $|R(z)|\leq1$ whenever $\operatorname{Re}z\leq0$. Thus the trapezoidal rule is the unique highest-order A-stable method of the stated form, apart from representations containing removable common factors.
