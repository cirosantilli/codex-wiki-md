<h1 id="section-a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substitute the exact solution and expand every value about $t_n$. The coefficient of $h^q y^{(q)}(t_n)$ in the defect is zero for $q=0,\ldots,p$ exactly when

$$
\boxed{\sum_{k=0}^s\rho_k=0,\qquad
\sum_{k=0}^sk^q\rho_k
=q\left[s^{q-1}\sigma_s+(s-1)^{q-1}\sigma_{s-1}\right]
\quad(1\leq q\leq p).}
$$

These are the [order conditions for a linear multistep method](../../../../../../../order-conditions-for-a-linear-multistep-method.md), specialized to the two nonzero derivative coefficients. The first failed identity determines the leading [local truncation error](../../../../../../../local-truncation-error.md).
