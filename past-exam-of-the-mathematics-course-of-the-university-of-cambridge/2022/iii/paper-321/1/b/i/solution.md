<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [perfect gas](../../../../../../../ideal-gas.md), $c_s^2\sim kT/(\mu_m m_p)$. At the margin of the [Toomre stability criterion](../../../../../../../toomre-s-stability-criterion.md),

$$
c_s\sim\frac{G\Sigma}{\Omega},
\qquad
T\sim\frac{\mu_m m_p}{k}\frac{G^2\Sigma^2}{\Omega^2}.
$$

Integrating viscous heating through the disk and applying [radiative diffusion](../../../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md) gives

$$
F\sim\alpha P\Omega H\sim\alpha\Sigma c_s^2\Omega,
\qquad
F\sim\frac{\sigma T^4}{\kappa\rho H}
\sim\frac{\sigma T^4}{\kappa\Sigma}.
$$

Equating these fluxes yields

$$
\boxed{
\alpha\sim\frac\sigma\kappa
\left(\frac{\mu_m m_p\Sigma}{k}\right)^4
G^6\Omega^{-7}}.
$$

The [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) is $\bar\nu\sim\alpha c_s^2/\Omega$, so

$$
\bar\nu\propto\Sigma^4\Omega^{-7},Sigma^2\Omega^{-3}
=\Sigma^6\Omega^{-10}.
$$

Because a [Keplerian accretion disk](../../../../../../../keplerian-accretion-disk.md) has $\Omega\propto r^{-3/2}$,

$$
\boxed{\bar\nu\propto r^{15}\Sigma^6}.
$$
