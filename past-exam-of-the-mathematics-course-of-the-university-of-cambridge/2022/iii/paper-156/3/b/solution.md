<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose a generic isotopy from $\alpha_0$ to $\alpha_1$ while keeping $\beta_0$ fixed. Except at finitely many times, all intersections are transverse. At each exceptional time a tangency creates or removes a pair of crossings. In oriented local coordinates the two crossings have opposite signs, so their contributions cancel. The signed sum is constant throughout the isotopy, and therefore

$$
\langle\alpha_0,\beta_0\rangle
=\langle\alpha_1,\beta_0\rangle.
$$

This is the [isotopy invariance of algebraic intersection number](../../../../../../isotopy-invariance-of-algebraic-intersection-number.md).
