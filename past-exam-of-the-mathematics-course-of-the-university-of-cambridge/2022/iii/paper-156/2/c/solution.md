<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If two arcs bound a bigon, pushing one side across its disc is an [isotopy](../../../../../../isotopy.md) relative to the ends that removes the two corners. Such representatives cannot be in minimal position.

Conversely, compare $\alpha$ with an isotopic representative having the fewest intersections with $\beta$, and lift the isotopy to $\overline{\mathbb H^2}$. At the first stage where the original excess intersections disappear, two lifted arcs enclose an innermost disc. Part b rules out escape through a puncture or repeated intersections at the ideal boundary, so this disc projects injectively to a bigon on $S$. Thus absence of a bigon implies minimal position. This proves the [bigon criterion](../../../../../../bigon-criterion.md) for essential simple proper arcs.
