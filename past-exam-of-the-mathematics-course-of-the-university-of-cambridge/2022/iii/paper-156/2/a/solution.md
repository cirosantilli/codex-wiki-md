↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The arcs $\alpha$ and $\beta$ bound a [bigon](../../../../../../bigon-formed-by-two-arcs.md) if there are subarcs with common endpoints whose union is the boundary of an embedded disc and whose interiors are disjoint. They are in [minimal position](../../../../../../minimal-position-of-curves-or-arcs.md) if $|\alpha\cap\beta|$ is the least possible intersection count among proper arcs isotopic to $\alpha$ and $\beta$ relative to their ends.
