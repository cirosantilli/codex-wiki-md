<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $G$ be generated by $r$ elements and choose $h\geq\max\{2,r\}$. The [fundamental group of a closed orientable surface](../../../../../../fundamental-group-of-a-closed-orientable-surface.md) has the presentation

$$
\pi_1(S_h)=
\langle a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_h,b_h\mid
\textstyle\prod_{i=1}^h[a_i,b_i]=1\rangle.
$$

Mapping $a_1,\ldots,a_r$ to generators of $G$ and every $b_i$ and remaining $a_i$ to the identity defines a [surjective group homomorphism](../../../../../../surjective-group-homomorphism.md) $\pi_1(S_h)\twoheadrightarrow G$.

Its kernel determines a connected [regular covering](../../../../../../regular-covering.md) $S\to S_h$ with [deck transformation group](../../../../../../deck-transformation-group.md) $G$. The action is free and orientation preserving. The covering surface has genus at least two, and the lifted hyperbolic metric makes every deck transformation an isometry. Part a now gives $G\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Mod}(S)$. This is the [realization of a finite group as a surface deck group](../../../../../../realization-of-a-finite-group-as-a-surface-deck-group.md).
