<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Because the action is free, the quotient $S_g/G$ is a closed orientable surface $S_h$, and $S_g\to S_h$ is a covering of degree $|G|$. The [Euler characteristic under a finite covering](../../../../../../euler-characteristic-under-a-finite-covering.md) gives

$$
2-2g=|G|(2-2h),
$$

or equivalently

$$
g-1=|G|(h-1).
$$

The quotient inherits a [hyperbolic metric](../../../../../../hyperbolic-metric.md), so $h\geq2$. Hence $h-1\geq1$ and $|G|\leq g-1$.
