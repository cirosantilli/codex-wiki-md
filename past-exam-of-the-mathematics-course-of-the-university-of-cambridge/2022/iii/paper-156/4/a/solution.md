↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Define the [arc complex](../../../../../../arc-complex.md) $\mathcal A(S)$ as follows. Its vertices are isotopy classes, relative to the punctures, of unoriented [essential simple proper arcs](../../../../../../essential-proper-arc-on-a-punctured-surface.md). A set of $k+1$ vertices spans a $k$-dimensional [simplex](../../../../../../simplex.md) when the classes have representatives with pairwise disjoint interiors. Every orientation-preserving homeomorphism sends such representatives to such representatives, and isotopic homeomorphisms induce the same permutation of classes. Hence

$$
\operatorname{Mod}(S)\curvearrowright\mathcal A(S)
$$

by simplicial automorphisms.
