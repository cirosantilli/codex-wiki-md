↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Part 1 gives $\mathbb EM_t^2=\mathbb E[M]_t$. Hence

$$
\sup_{t\geq0}\mathbb EM_t^2
\leq\mathbb E[M]_\infty<\infty,
$$

so $M$ is $L^2$-bounded.
