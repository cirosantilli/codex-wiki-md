<h1 id="5/a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For a partition $\pi$ of $[0,t]$, apply the first inequality to each increment of the covariation and then the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md):

$$
\sum_{\pi}|\Delta[M,N]|
\leq\sum_\pi\sqrt{\Delta[M]\,\Delta[N]}
\leq\sqrt{[M]_t[N]_t}.
$$

Taking the supremum over partitions proves the [Kunita-Watanabe inequality](../../../../../../../kunita-watanabe-inequality.md)

$$
\boxed{V_t([M,N])\leq\sqrt{[M]_t[N]_t}.}
$$
