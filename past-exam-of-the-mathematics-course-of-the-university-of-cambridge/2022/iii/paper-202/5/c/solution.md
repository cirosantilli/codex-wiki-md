<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [strong solution of a stochastic differential equation](../../../../../../strong-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md) is adapted to the completed filtration of a prescribed Brownian motion $B$ on a prescribed probability space and satisfies

$$
X_t=X_0+\int_0^tb(X_s)\,ds+\int_0^t\sigma(X_s)\,dB_s
$$

almost surely. A [weak solution of a stochastic differential equation](../../../../../../weak-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md) may choose the filtered probability space, Brownian motion, and adapted process as part of the solution; only the displayed integral equation and the prescribed initial law are required.
