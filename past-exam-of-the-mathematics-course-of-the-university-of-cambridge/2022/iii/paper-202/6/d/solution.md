<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Every linear combination of $X_{t_1},\ldots,X_{t_n}$ is a deterministic constant plus one stochastic integral of a deterministic $L^2$ function against $B$. Part a makes every such combination Gaussian. By the linear-combination characterization of a [multivariate normal distribution](../../../../../../multivariate-normal-distribution.md), $(X_{t_1},\ldots,X_{t_n})$ is jointly Gaussian, so $X$ is a [Gaussian process](../../../../../../gaussian-process.md).
