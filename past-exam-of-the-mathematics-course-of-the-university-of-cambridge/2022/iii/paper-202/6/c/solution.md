Part a applied to the deterministic kernel gives

$$
\boxed{X_t\sim N\left(
xe^{-\lambda t},
\int_0^te^{-2\lambda(t-s)}ds\right)
=N\left(xe^{-\lambda t},
\frac{1-e^{-2\lambda t}}{2\lambda}\right).}
$$
