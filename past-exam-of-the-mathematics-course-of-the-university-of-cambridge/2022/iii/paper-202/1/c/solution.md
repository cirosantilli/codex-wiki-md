↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Indicators of the rectangles

$$
A\times(s,t],\qquad A\in\mathcal F_s,
$$

together with $A\times\{0\}$ generate the [predictable sigma-algebra](../../../../../../predictable-sigma-algebra.md). Their finite linear span is precisely the set of simple processes. The monotone-class theorem therefore makes this span dense among bounded predictable functions in measure. Since $\nu$ is finite, truncation followed by bounded approximation proves density in $L^2(\mathcal P,\nu)$.
