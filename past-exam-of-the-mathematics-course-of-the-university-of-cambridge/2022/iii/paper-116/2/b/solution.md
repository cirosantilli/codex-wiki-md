<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Suppose that a [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\varphi$ described the inaccessible cardinal $\kappa$, so that $\kappa$ were the least ordinal with $V_\kappa\models\varphi$. Since $\kappa$ is inaccessible, $V_\kappa$ is a [model](../../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of ZFC. Apply the [Lévy reflection theorem](../../../../../../levy-reflection-theorem.md) inside this model to the single formula $\varphi$. There is some $\alpha<\kappa$ for which

$$
V_\alpha\models\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
V_\kappa\models\varphi.
$$

The right side holds, so the left side contradicts the asserted minimality of $\kappa$. Hence no first-order formula describes an inaccessible cardinal, as recorded by [ordinal described by a first-order formula](../../../../../../ordinal-described-by-a-first-order-formula.md).
