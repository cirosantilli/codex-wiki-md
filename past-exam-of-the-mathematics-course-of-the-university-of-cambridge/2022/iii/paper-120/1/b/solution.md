↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [Heyting algebra](../../../../../../heyting-algebra.md) is a bounded [lattice](../../../../../../lattice.md) $H$ equipped with an operation $a\Rightarrow b$ satisfying

$$
x\leq(a\Rightarrow b)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x\wedge a\leq b.
$$

Thus, for fixed $a$, the map $x\mapsto x\wedge a$ is left adjoint to $y\mapsto a\Rightarrow y$. A left adjoint preserves joins, so

$$
a\wedge(b\vee c)=(a\wedge b)\vee(a\wedge c).
$$

This is one [distributive law for lattices](../../../../../../distributive-law-for-lattices.md); the other follows from it and the absorption laws. Hence every Heyting algebra is a [distributive lattice](../../../../../../distributive-lattice.md). This argument is the [distributivity of a Heyting algebra](../../../../../../distributivity-of-a-heyting-algebra.md).
