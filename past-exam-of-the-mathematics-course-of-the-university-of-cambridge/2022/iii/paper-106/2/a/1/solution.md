**True.** If $(x_n)$ is dense in $B_X$, then on $B_{X^*}$ the weak-star topology is induced by a metric such as

$$
d(f,g)=\sum_{n\geq1}2^{-n}
\frac{|(f-g)(x_n)|}{1+|(f-g)(x_n)|}.
$$

The [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](../../../../../../../banach-alaoglu-theorem.md) makes this metric space compact, and every compact metric space is sequentially compact.
