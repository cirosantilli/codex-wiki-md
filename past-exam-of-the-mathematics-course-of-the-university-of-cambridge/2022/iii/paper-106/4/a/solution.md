<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

By the [spectral radius formula](../../../../../../spectral-radius-formula.md), $r(T)<1$. Choose

$$
r(T)<r<s<1.
$$

The formula gives a constant $C$ such that $\lVert T^n\rVert\leq Cr^n$ for every $n$. Since $f$ is holomorphic on the unit disc, the [Cauchy estimate](../../../../../../cauchy-estimate.md) on the circle of radius $s$ gives $|a_n|\leq M_s s^{-n}$. Therefore

$$
\sum_{n\geq0}|a_n|\lVert T^n\rVert
\leq CM_s\sum_{n\geq0}(r/s)^n<\infty.
$$

**Thus $\sum_{n\geq0}a_nT^n$ converges absolutely in operator norm.**
