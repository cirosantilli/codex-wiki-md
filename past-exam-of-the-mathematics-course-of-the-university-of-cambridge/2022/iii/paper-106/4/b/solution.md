<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $T$ is normal, then $S=T-I$ is normal and $\sigma(S)=\{0\}$. The [C-star identity](../../../../../../c-star-identity.md) and normality imply $\lVert S^n\rVert=\lVert S\rVert^n$. The [spectral radius formula](../../../../../../spectral-radius-formula.md) consequently gives

$$
\lVert S\rVert=r(S)=0,
$$

so $T=I$.

Normality is essential. On $\ell^2$, let $N$ send $e_2$ to $e_1$ and every other basis vector to zero. Then $N\ne0$ but $N^2=0$, so

$$
\sigma(I+N)=1+\sigma(N)=\{1\},
$$

while $I+N\ne I$.
