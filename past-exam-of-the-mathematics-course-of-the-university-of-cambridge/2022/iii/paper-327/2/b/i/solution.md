↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By [multiplication of a distribution by a smooth function](../../../../../../../multiplication-of-a-distribution-by-a-smooth-function.md), for every [test function](../../../../../../../test-function.md) $\psi$,

$$
\langle\rho\delta_0,\psi\rangle
=\langle\delta_0,\rho\psi\rangle
=\rho(0)\psi(0)=\psi(0)
=\langle\delta_0,\psi\rangle.
$$

**Hence $\rho\delta_0=\delta_0$.**
