<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [distributional derivative of the Heaviside step function](../../../../../../../distributional-derivative-of-the-heaviside-step-function.md) is $H'=\delta_0$. The [Leibniz rule](../../../../../../../leibniz-rule.md) and $x\delta_0=0$ therefore give

$$
\boxed{(xH)'=H+x\delta_0=H,
\qquad
(xH)''=H'=\delta_0.}
$$
