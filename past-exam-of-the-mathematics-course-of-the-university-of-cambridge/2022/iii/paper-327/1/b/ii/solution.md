The [scaling property of the Fourier transform](../../../../../../../scaling-property-of-the-fourier-transform.md) gives, first for [Schwartz functions](../../../../../../../schwartz-function.md) and then by duality,

$$
\widehat{\delta_tu}=t^{-n}\delta_{1/t}\widehat u.
$$

If the [homogeneous distribution](../../../../../../../homogeneous-distribution.md) $u$ has degree $\sigma$, then

$$
t^\sigma\widehat u=t^{-n}\delta_{1/t}\widehat u.
$$

Putting $s=1/t$ yields $\delta_s\widehat u=s^{-n-\sigma}\widehat u$. Thus the [Fourier transform of a homogeneous distribution](../../../../../../../fourier-transform-of-a-homogeneous-distribution.md) has degree $\boxed{-n-\sigma}$.
