<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [wave operator](../../../../../../wave-equation-split.md) $\partial_x^2-\partial_y^2$, use the symbol

$$
P(\xi,\eta)=\eta^2-\xi^2
$$

and complexify $\eta$. Its roots $\eta=\pm\xi$ are real for every real $\xi$, so the single horizontal step

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}\eta=1}
$$

never meets a root and is an explicit [Hörmander staircase](../../../../../../hormander-staircase.md).
