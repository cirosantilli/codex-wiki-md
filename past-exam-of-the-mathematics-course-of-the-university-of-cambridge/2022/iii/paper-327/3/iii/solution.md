<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the [heat operator](../../../../../../heat-equation.md) $\partial_x-\partial_y^2$, complexify the first frequency. Its symbol is

$$
P(\xi,\eta)=i\xi+\eta^2,
$$

whose root in $\xi$ is $\xi=i\eta^2$ and therefore lies in the closed upper half-plane for real $\eta$. The single step

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}\xi=-1}
$$

lies strictly below every root and is an explicit [Hörmander staircase](../../../../../../hormander-staircase.md).
