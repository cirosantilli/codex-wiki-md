<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The free massless [Dirac field](../../../../../../dirac-field.md) has action

$$
S_{\rm free}=\int d^4x\,i\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\psi.
$$

Under a finite dilation,

$$
\psi'(x)=\lambda^{-\Delta}\psi(\lambda^{-1}x),
\qquad
\bar\psi'(x)=\lambda^{-\Delta}\bar\psi(\lambda^{-1}x).
$$

The measure, derivative, and fields contribute $\lambda^4$, $\lambda^{-1}$, and $\lambda^{-2\Delta}$, so invariance requires

$$
4-1-2\Delta=0,
\qquad \Delta=\frac32.
$$

For $\lambda=e^\alpha$, the infinitesimal active variations are

$$
\delta\psi=-\alpha\left(x^\nu\partial_\nu+\frac32\right)\psi,
\qquad
\delta\bar\psi=-\alpha\left(x^\nu\partial_\nu+\frac32\right)\bar\psi.
$$

The associated [dilatation current](../../../../../../dilatation-current.md) is

$$
D^\mu=x_\nu T^{\mu\nu},
\qquad
T^{\mu\nu}=i\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\partial^\nu\psi,
$$

up to an improvement term. Its divergence is the on-shell trace $T^\mu{}_{\mu}=0$.
