↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Subtracting $T_\alpha u_n=z_n$ and $T_\alpha u_m=z_m$ and applying the coercive estimate from part i yields

$$
\|u_n-u_m\|_X
\leq\frac1\alpha\|z_n-z_m\|_X.
$$

**Thus $(z_n)$ Cauchy implies $(u_n)$ Cauchy. Completeness of the [Hilbert space](../../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) $X$ gives $u_n\to u\in X$, and boundedness of $T_\alpha$ gives $z_n=T_\alpha u_n\to T_\alpha u$.**
