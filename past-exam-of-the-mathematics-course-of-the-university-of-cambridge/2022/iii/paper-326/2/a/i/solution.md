The extended-real functional $f:X\to\overline{\mathbb R}$ is [sequentially lower semicontinuous](../../../../../../../sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md) in the topology $\tau_X$ when every sequence $x_n\to x$ in $\tau_X$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{f(x)\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}f(x_n).}
$$
