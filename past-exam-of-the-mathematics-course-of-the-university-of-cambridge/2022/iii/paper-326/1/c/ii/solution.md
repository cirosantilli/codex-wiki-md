↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Part i gives $\|D\|\leq B$. Conversely, choose indices $j_k$ with $|d_{j_k}|\to B$. Since $\|e_{j_k}\|_2=1$,

$$
\|D\|\geq\|De_{j_k}\|_2=|d_{j_k}|.
$$

Taking the [limit](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) proves $\boxed{\|D\|=B}$, whether or not the [supremum](../../../../../../../supremum.md) is attained.
