<h1 id="1/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The proposed stability property is false. Set $u^*=0$, $f^*=0$, and take $u_n=e_n$. Then

$$
f_n=Du_n=\frac1n e_n,
\qquad
\|f_n-f^*\|_2=\frac1n\longrightarrow0,
$$

while $\|u_n-u^*\|_2=1$ for every $n$. Hence the inverse is not continuous on its range and the recovery problem is not [stable with respect to perturbations](../../../../../../../well-posed-problem.md). This is the standard [unbounded inverse on a nonclosed operator range](../../../../../../../unbounded-inverse-on-a-nonclosed-operator-range.md).
