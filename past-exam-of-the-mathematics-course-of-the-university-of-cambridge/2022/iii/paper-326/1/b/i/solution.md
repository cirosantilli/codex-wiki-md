**The statement is true without an additional [rank](../../../../../../../matrix-rank.md) assumption.** If $A=U\Sigma V^H$ is a [singular value decomposition](../../../../../../../singular-value-decomposition.md), then

$$
\boxed{(A^\dagger)^H=(V\Sigma^\dagger U^H)^H
=U\Sigma^\dagger V^H=(A^H)^\dagger.}
$$
