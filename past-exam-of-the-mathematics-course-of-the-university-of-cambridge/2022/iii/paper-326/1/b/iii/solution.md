↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** The matrix $A^\dagger A$ is the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto the row space $\mathcal R(A^H)$, so its eigenvalues are zero and one. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}(A^\dagger A)
=\operatorname{rank}(A)
=\operatorname{rank}(A^\dagger)
=\operatorname{tr}(A^\dagger A).}
$$
