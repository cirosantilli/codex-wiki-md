The countable direct sum

$$
\bigoplus_{n\geq0}\mathbb Z
$$

is abelian and therefore nilpotent of class one. It is not finitely generated, whereas every [polycyclic group](../../../../../../../polycyclic-group.md) is finitely generated. Hence it is nilpotent but not polycyclic.
