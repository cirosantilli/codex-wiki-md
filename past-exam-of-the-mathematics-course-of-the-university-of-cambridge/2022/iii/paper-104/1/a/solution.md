<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Because the generating sets are disjoint, the [free product](../../../../../../free-product.md) has the presentation

$$
\boxed{G*H=\langle X\sqcup Y\mid R\sqcup S\rangle.}
$$

This is immediate from the [universal property of a group presentation](../../../../../../universal-property-of-a-group-presentation.md): a map out of this presented group is exactly a pair of homomorphisms from $G$ and $H$ to the target group.
