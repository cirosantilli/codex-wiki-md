<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose distinct nonidentity elements $g_1,g_2\in G$ and $h_1,h_2\in H$, and put

$$
u=g_1h_1,
\qquad
v=g_2h_2.
$$

Expand a freely reduced word in $u^{\pm1},v^{\pm1}$. At a boundary where two syllables from one factor meet, their product is one of $g_1^{-1}g_2$, $g_2^{-1}g_1$, $h_1h_2^{-1}$, or $h_2h_1^{-1}$, all nonidentity by the choices above. Every other boundary already alternates between the factors. Thus the expansion reduces to a nonempty reduced word in $G*H$ and cannot represent the identity. The homomorphism from the rank-two [free group](../../../../../../free-group.md) sending its free generators to $u,v$ is injective, so

$$
\boxed{\langle u,v\rangle\cong F_2.}
$$
