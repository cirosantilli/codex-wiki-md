↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A group $G$ is [residually finite](../../../../../../residually-finite-group.md) when, for every $1\ne g\in G$, there are a [finite group](../../../../../../finite-group.md) $Q$ and a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $q:G\to Q$ such that $q(g)\ne1$. Equivalently, the intersection of all finite-index normal subgroups of $G$ is trivial.
