<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For the free basis $X=\{x_0,x_1,x_2,\ldots\}$, define

$$
\alpha(x_0)=1,
\qquad
\alpha(x_{n+1})=x_n.
$$

The [universal property of a free group](../../../../../../universal-property-of-a-free-group.md) extends this assignment to an endomorphism of $F(X)$. It is surjective because every $x_n$ is the image of $x_{n+1}$, but it is not injective because $x_0\ne1$ lies in its kernel. Thus $F(\mathbb N)$ is not Hopfian.
