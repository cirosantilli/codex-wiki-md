<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A monomial of weighted degree $n$ is $x^{n-2j}y^j$ with $0\leq j\leq\lfloor n/2\rfloor$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{F_S(n)=\dim_kS_n=\left\lfloor\frac n2\right\rfloor+1.}
$$

This is a [quasipolynomial](../../../../../../quasipolynomial.md) of period two, not eventually one polynomial: it equals $n/2+1$ for even $n$ and $(n+1)/2$ for odd $n$. The usual eventual-polynomial theorem for a graded algebra assumes a [standard graded algebra](../../../../../../standard-graded-algebra.md), generated in degree one. Here the generator $y$ has degree two, so there is no contradiction.
