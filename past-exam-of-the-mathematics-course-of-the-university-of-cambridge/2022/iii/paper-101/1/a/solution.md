↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [ideal of definition](../../../../../../ideal-of-definition.md) of a [Noetherian local ring](../../../../../../noetherian-local-ring.md) $(A,\mathfrak m)$ is an $\mathfrak m$-primary ideal $I$; equivalently, $\sqrt I=\mathfrak m$, or some power $\mathfrak m^r$ is contained in $I$.

If $M$ is a finitely generated module of dimension $d$, the [Hilbert–Samuel function](../../../../../../hilbert-samuel-function.md)

$$
\chi(M,I;n)=\ell(M/I^nM)
$$

agrees for all sufficiently large $n$ with a polynomial of degree $d$. Its leading term is

$$
\frac{e_I(M)}{d!}n^d,
$$

where $e_I(M)$ is the [Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity](../../../../../../hilbert-samuel-multiplicity.md).
