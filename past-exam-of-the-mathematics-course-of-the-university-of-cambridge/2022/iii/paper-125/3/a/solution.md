↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a short Weierstrass equation $E:y^2=x^3+ax+b$ in characteristic different from two, the [invariant differential on an elliptic curve](../../../../../../invariant-differential-on-an-elliptic-curve.md)

$$
\omega=\frac{dx}{2y}
$$

is nonzero and regular, including at $O$.
