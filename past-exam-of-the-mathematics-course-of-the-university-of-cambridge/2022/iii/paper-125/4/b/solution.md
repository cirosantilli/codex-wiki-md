<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume $\mu_n\subset K$ and put $\alpha=\sqrt[n]{a}$. All roots of $X^n-a$ are $\zeta\alpha$ with $\zeta\in\mu_n\subset K$, so $K(\alpha)$ is its splitting field and is a [Finite Galois extension](../../../../../../finite-galois-extension.md). The map

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(K(\alpha)/K)\longrightarrow\mu_n,
\qquad
\sigma\longmapsto\frac{\sigma(\alpha)}{\alpha}
$$

is an injective group homomorphism.
