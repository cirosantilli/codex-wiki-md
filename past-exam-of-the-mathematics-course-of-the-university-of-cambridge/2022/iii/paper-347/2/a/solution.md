<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The standard [Shakura--Sunyaev thin disk](../../../../../../shakura-sunyaev-thin-disk.md) assumes: a steady state; axial symmetry; a geometrically thin disk $H/R\ll1$; Newtonian, nearly Keplerian circular motion outside the inner edge; subsonic radial drift $|u_R|\ll u_\phi$; vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md); negligible disk self-gravity; an optically thick, locally thermal spectrum; local radiative balance between viscous heating and cooling; and a local alpha-viscosity stress with a zero-torque inner boundary near the [innermost stable circular orbit](../../../../../../innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md). These assumptions also exclude dynamically dominant winds and large-scale external torques from the standard solution.

Start from the [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md)

$$
\partial_t\rho+\nabla\mathbin\cdot(\rho\mathbf u)=0.
$$

Integrate vertically, use axial symmetry and steadiness, and define the [surface density of a disk](../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $\Sigma=\int\rho\,dz$. Then

$$
\frac1R\frac d{dR}(R\Sigma u_R)=0,
$$

so the inward-positive [accretion rate](../../../../../../accretion-rate.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dot m=-2\pi R\Sigma u_R=\text{constant}.}
$$
