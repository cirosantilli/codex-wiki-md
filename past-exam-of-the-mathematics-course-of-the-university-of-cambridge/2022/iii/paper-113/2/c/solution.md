**False.** For $n\geq2$, take the affine hypersurface

$$
X_n=\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y,z]/(xy-z^n)\subset\mathbb A_k^3.
$$

It is an [integral scheme](../../../../../../integral-scheme.md), and its only possible singular point is the origin, which has codimension two. Hence it is [regular in codimension one](../../../../../../regular-in-codimension-one.md); as a hypersurface it satisfies Serre's condition $(S_2)$, so the [Serre criterion for normality](../../../../../../serre-s-criterion-for-normality.md) also makes it a [normal scheme](../../../../../../normal-scheme.md). The [Divisor class group of an A-type surface singularity](../../../../../../divisor-class-group-of-an-a-type-surface-singularity.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(X_n)\cong\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z,
$$

generated by $V(x,z)$. Thus a closed affine subscheme satisfying $(\star)$ can have nonzero torsion in its class group.
