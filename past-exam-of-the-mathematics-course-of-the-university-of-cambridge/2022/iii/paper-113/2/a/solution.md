<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Weil divisor](../../../../../../weil-divisor.md) on $X$ is a finite formal sum

$$
D=\sum_Zn_Z[Z],\qquad n_Z\in\mathbb Z,
$$

over integral codimension-one closed subschemes $Z$. Since $X$ is [regular in codimension one](../../../../../../regular-in-codimension-one.md), the local ring at the generic point of each $Z$ is a [discrete valuation ring](../../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md). A nonzero [rational function](../../../../../../rational-function.md) $f\in k(X)^\times$ therefore defines the principal divisor $\operatorname{div}(f)=\sum_Zv_Z(f)[Z]$. The [divisor class group](../../../../../../divisor-class-group.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cl}(X)=\operatorname{Div}(X)/\{\operatorname{div}(f):f\in k(X)^\times\}.}
$$
