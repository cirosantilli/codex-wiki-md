<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take the [projective line with a doubled point](../../../../../../projective-line-with-a-doubled-point.md) over $\mathbb C$, obtained by gluing two copies of $\mathbb P_{\mathbb C}^1$ along the complement of one point. After every [base change of a morphism of schemes](../../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md), a closed subset has closed image from each of the two projective-line charts, so its total image, the union of those two images, is closed. The structure morphism is therefore [universally closed](../../../../../../universally-closed-morphism.md). The two doubled points have no disjoint neighborhoods, so the scheme is not [separated](../../../../../../separated-scheme.md) and hence is not [proper](../../../../../../proper-morphism.md).
