<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Multiplication by the homogeneous equation $f_d$ and restriction to its zero scheme give the [structure-sheaf sequence of a hypersurface](../../../../../../structure-sheaf-sequence-of-a-hypersurface.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^3}(-d)\xrightarrow{\cdot f_d}\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^3}\longrightarrow i_*\mathcal O_X\longrightarrow0.
$$

By [cohomology under a closed immersion](../../../../../../cohomology-under-a-closed-immersion.md), $H^q(\mathbb P^3,i_*\mathcal O_X)\cong H^q(X,\mathcal O_X)$. The associated [long exact sequence in cohomology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) contains

$$
H^1(\mathbb P^3,\mathcal O)\longrightarrow H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)\longrightarrow H^2(\mathbb P^3,\mathcal O(-d)).
$$

Both outer groups vanish by the [cohomology of twisting sheaves on projective space](../../../../../../cohomology-of-twisting-sheaves-on-projective-space.md), because they are intermediate cohomology groups on $\mathbb P^3$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)=0.}
$$
