<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $A=k[x,y]$. If $\dim Z=1$, its prime ideal has height one and is generated by an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) $f$, because $A$ is a [unique factorization domain](../../../../../../unique-factorization-domain.md). Then $U=D(f)$ is a [principal open subscheme](../../../../../../principal-open-subscheme.md) and

$$
H^0(U,\mathcal O_U)=A_f=k[x,y,f^{-1}].
$$

If $\dim Z=0$, its complement has codimension two. Since $\mathbb A_k^2$ is [normal](../../../../../../normal-scheme.md), regular functions extend across that codimension-two subset, giving

$$
H^0(U,\mathcal O_U)=k[x,y].
$$

This includes the calculation for the [punctured affine plane](../../../../../../punctured-affine-plane.md). If $Z=\mathbb A_k^2$ has dimension two, then $U$ is empty and its ring of sections is the zero ring.
