<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an open cover $\mathcal U=(U_i)$ and a sheaf $\mathcal F$, the [Čech cochain complex](../../../../../../cech-cochain-complex.md) is

$$
\check C^p(\mathcal U,\mathcal F)=\prod_{i_0<\cdots<i_p}\mathcal F(U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_p}),
$$

with the alternating sum of restriction maps as differential. Its cohomology is the [Čech cohomology](../../../../../../cech-cohomology.md) $\check H^p(\mathcal U,\mathcal F)$.

Cover $\mathbb P_k^1$ by $U_0=D_+(x_0)$ and $U_1=D_+(x_1)$, and write $t=x_1/x_0$. This is an [acyclic affine cover](../../../../../../leray-s-theorem.md) for the [twisting sheaf on projective space](../../../../../../twisting-sheaf-on-projective-space.md) $\mathcal O(-1)$. In compatible trivializations, its degree-one Čech quotient is

$$
H^1(\mathbb P_k^1,\mathcal O(-1))
\cong
\frac{k[t,t^{-1}]}{k[t]+t^{-1}k[t^{-1}]}.
$$

The first summand contains every nonnegative power of $t$ and the second every negative power, so

$$
\boxed{H^1(\mathbb P_k^1,\mathcal O(-1))=0.}
$$
