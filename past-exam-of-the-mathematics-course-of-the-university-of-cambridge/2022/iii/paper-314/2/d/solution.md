<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Across a stationary [normal shock wave](../../../../../../normal-shock-wave.md), the [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions for a perfect gas](../../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md) conserve

$$
j=\rho_1u_1=\rho_2u_2,\qquad
p_1+\rho_1u_1^2=p_2+\rho_2u_2^2,
\qquad
C_B=\frac{u^2}{2}+\frac{c_s^2}{\gamma-1}.
$$

Momentum conservation and $1/\rho=u/j$ give

$$
p_2-p_1=j(u_1-u_2).
$$

Direct elimination of the two pressures and densities from these three jump conditions gives

$$
u_1u_2=\frac{2(\gamma-1)}{\gamma+1}C_B.
$$

Part c identifies the right side with the square of the critical speed, so the [Prandtl shock relation](../../../../../../prandtl-shock-relation.md) is

$$
\boxed{u_1u_2=c_{\rm cr}^2}.
$$

It maps the unique upstream supersonic state on a given Bernoulli streamline to its downstream subsonic state.
