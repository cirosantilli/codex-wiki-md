<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Bernoulli function](../../../../../../bernoulli-function.md) for steady unmagnetized flow without gravity is

$$
C_B=\frac{u^2}{2}+h.
$$

For a [polytropic equation of state](../../../../../../polytropic-equation-of-state.md), $h=c_s^2/(\gamma-1)$. Evaluation at the critical point found in part b gives

$$
\boxed{
C_B=\frac{c_{\rm cr}^2}{2}
+\frac{c_{\rm cr}^2}{\gamma-1}
=\frac{\gamma+1}{2(\gamma-1)}c_{\rm cr}^2}.
$$

Consequently

$$
c_{\rm cr}^2=\frac{2(\gamma-1)}{\gamma+1}C_B.
$$

Since $C_B$ is conserved along a streamline, that streamline has a unique critical speed.
