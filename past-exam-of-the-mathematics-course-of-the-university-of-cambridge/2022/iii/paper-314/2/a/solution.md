<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Project the steady [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) along a streamline. With no gravity,

$$
u\,du+\frac{dp}{\rho}=0.
$$

For an isentropic fluid, $dp=c_s^2d\rho$, so

$$
\frac{d\rho}{du}=-\frac{\rho u}{c_s^2}.
$$

The scalar mass flux is $j=\rho u$, and hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{dj}{du}=\rho\left(1-\frac{u^2}{c_s^2}\right)}.
$$

It increases with speed in [subsonic flow](../../../../../../subsonic-flow.md) and decreases with speed in [supersonic flow](../../../../../../supersonic-flow.md).
