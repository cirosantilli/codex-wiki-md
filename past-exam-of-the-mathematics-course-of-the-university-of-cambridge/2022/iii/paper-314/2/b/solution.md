<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The continuous [transonic branch](../../../../../../transonic-branch.md) passes from $dj/du>0$ to $dj/du<0$, so $j(u)$ has a maximum where its derivative vanishes. At this [critical speed of a polytropic flow](../../../../../../critical-speed-of-a-polytropic-flow.md),

$$
\boxed{u=c_s=c_{\rm cr}}.
$$

The maximum mass flux is therefore attained at the [sonic point](../../../../../../sonic-point.md).
