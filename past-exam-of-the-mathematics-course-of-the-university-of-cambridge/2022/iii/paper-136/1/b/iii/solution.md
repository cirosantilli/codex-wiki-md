The ring $\mathbb Z[\zeta_3]$ is the full ring of integers of the [quadratic number field](../../../../../../../quadratic-field.md) $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$. Every ring of integers of a number field is a Dedekind domain, so this ring is Dedekind.
