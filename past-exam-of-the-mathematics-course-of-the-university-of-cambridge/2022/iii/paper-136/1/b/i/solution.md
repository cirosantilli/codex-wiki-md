The ring $\mathbb Z[T]$ is Noetherian and integrally closed, but it is not a [Dedekind domain](../../../../../../../dedekind-domain.md) because it has Krull dimension two. Concretely, the nonzero prime ideal $(2)$ is properly contained in the prime ideal $(2,T)$ and is therefore not maximal.
