<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\mathfrak m_K$ be the maximal ideal. For all sufficiently large $r$, the convergent [p-adic logarithm](../../../../../../p-adic-logarithm.md) and [p-adic exponential](../../../../../../p-adic-exponential-function.md) give the [principal-unit logarithm](../../../../../../principal-unit-logarithm.md) isomorphism

$$
1+\mathfrak m_K^r\xrightarrow{\ \log\ }(\mathfrak m_K^r,+).
$$

Multiplication by a power of a uniformizer identifies the additive group $\mathfrak m_K^r$ with $(\mathcal O_K,+)$. Since $1+\mathfrak m_K^r$ has finite index in $\mathcal O_K^\times$, it is the required subgroup.
