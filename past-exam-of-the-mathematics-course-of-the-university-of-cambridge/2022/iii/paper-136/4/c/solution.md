<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $Y=X^2$. Modulo two, $Y^2+9Y-2$ has the two simple roots zero and one, so [Hensel lemma](../../../../../../hensel-s-lemma.md) lifts them to roots $y_0,y_1\in\mathbb Z_2$, with $v_2(y_0)=1$ and $y_1\equiv1\pmod8$. The [2-adic unit](../../../../../../2-adic-unit.md) criterion makes $y_1$ a square, so $X^2-y_1$ splits into two linear factors. The polynomial $X^2-y_0$ is Eisenstein and remains irreducible. Thus $X^4+9X^2-2$ has three irreducible factors, of degrees $1,1,2$, as in [factorization of X4 plus 9X2 minus 2 over the 2-adic numbers](../../../../../../factorization-of-x4-plus-9x2-minus-2-over-the-2-adic-numbers.md).
