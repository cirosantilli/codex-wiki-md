↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The leading quartic correction is the one-loop [bubble diagram](../../../../../../../bubble-diagram.md) with two quartic vertices joined by two fast propagators. The four external legs can be distributed in the three exchange channels. One family of index contractions contains a freely summed closed component loop and contributes $N$; the other contractions contribute eight, giving the factor $N+8$.
