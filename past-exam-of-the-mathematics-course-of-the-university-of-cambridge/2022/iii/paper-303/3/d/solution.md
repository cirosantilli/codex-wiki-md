<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $d=4-\epsilon$, define dimensionless variables

$$
r=\frac{\mu^2}{\Lambda^2},
\qquad
u=\widetilde g=\Lambda^{-\epsilon}g.
$$

To leading order, with $K_4=1/(8\pi^2)$,

$$
\frac{dr}{ds}=2r+4(N+2)K_4\frac{u}{1+r},
$$



$$
\frac{du}{ds}=\epsilon u-4(N+8)K_4\frac{u^2}{(1+r)^2}.
$$

The [Gaussian fixed point](../../../../../../gaussian-fixed-point.md) is $(r_*,u_*)=(0,0)$. Its thermal eigenvalue is $y_t=2$, so

$$
\boxed{\nu_{\rm G}=\frac12}.
$$

The interacting [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](../../../../../../wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md) is

$$
\boxed{
u_*=\frac{2\pi^2}{N+8}\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2),
\qquad
r_*=-\frac{N+2}{2(N+8)}\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2).}
$$

Linearizing the flow gives

$$
y_t=2-\frac{N+2}{N+8}\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2).
$$

Since the [correlation-length critical exponent](../../../../../../correlation-length-critical-exponent.md) is $\nu=1/y_t$,

$$
\boxed{
\nu_{\rm WF}=\frac12+\frac{N+2}{4(N+8)}\epsilon+O(\epsilon^2).}
$$
