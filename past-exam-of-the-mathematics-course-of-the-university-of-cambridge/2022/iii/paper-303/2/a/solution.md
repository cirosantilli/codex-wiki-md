<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A momentum-shell [renormalization group](../../../../../../renormalization-group.md) step has three parts:

1. Split $\phi=\phi_<+\phi_>$ into slow modes $|k|<\Lambda/\zeta$ and fast modes $\Lambda/\zeta<|k|<\Lambda$, then integrate out $\phi_>$ using a [cumulant expansion](../../../../../../cumulant-expansion.md) to obtain a [Wilsonian effective action](../../../../../../wilsonian-effective-action.md) for $\phi_<$.  
2. Rescale momenta by $k'=\zeta k$, equivalently coordinates by $x'=x/\zeta$, restoring the cutoff from $\Lambda/\zeta$ to $\Lambda$.  
3. Rescale the field by $\phi'(x')=\zeta^{(d-2)/2}\phi_<(x)$ at the [Gaussian fixed point](../../../../../../gaussian-fixed-point.md), restoring the normalized kinetic term.

The resulting functional has the original form and cutoff but new masses and couplings. Iteration produces a [renormalization-group flow](../../../../../../renormalization-group-flow.md).
