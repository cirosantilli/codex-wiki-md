↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Requiring $\int d^dx\,(\nabla\phi)^2$ to be dimensionless gives the [engineering dimensions](../../../../../../engineering-dimension.md)

$$
[\phi]=\frac{d-2}{2},
\qquad [\mu_0]=1,
$$

and

$$
[\lambda_0]=d-3[\phi]=\frac{6-d}{2},
$$



$$
[g_0]=d-4[\phi]=4-d,
$$



$$
[\gamma_0]=d-6[\phi]=6-2d.
$$

**Thus $\mu_0$ is relevant. The cubic coupling is relevant, marginal, or irrelevant for $d<6$, $d=6$, or $d>6$; the quartic coupling has the same classification around $d=4$; and the sextic coupling has it around $d=3$.**
