<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $h=g=0$, the expansion $I(m)=-\log2+m^2/2+O(m^4)$ gives

$$
f={\rm constant}+Jd\,m_em_o
+\frac1{4\beta}(m_e^2+m_o^2)+O(m^4).
$$

With $m_\pm=(m_e\pm m_o)/2$, one has $m_em_o=m_+^2-m_-^2$ and $m_e^2+m_o^2=2(m_+^2+m_-^2)$. Hence

$$
f={\rm constant}
+\left(Jd+\frac1{2\beta}\right)m_+^2
+\left(\frac1{2\beta}-Jd\right)m_-^2+O(m^4).
$$

The [staggered magnetization](../../../../../../../staggered-magnetization.md) $m_-$ becomes unstable at

$$
\boxed{T_c=2Jd},
$$

producing the antiferromagnetic transition. The coefficient of the uniform magnetization $m_+$ remains positive for every $T>0$, so there is no $m_+$ transition.
