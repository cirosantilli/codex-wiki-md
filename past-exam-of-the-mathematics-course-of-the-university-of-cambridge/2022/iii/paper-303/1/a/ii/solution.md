<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The ordered solution gives

$$
|m_{\rm eq}|\sim(T_c-T)^{1/(2n-2)},
$$

so the [order-parameter critical exponent](../../../../../../../order-parameter-critical-exponent.md) is

$$
\boxed{\beta=\frac1{2(n-1)}}.
$$

At $T=T_c$, adding a magnetic term $-hm$ gives $h=2nu m^{2n-1}$, hence

$$
\boxed{\delta=2n-1}.
$$

Above $T_c$, the zero-field susceptibility is $\chi=(2a)^{-1}$. Below $T_c$, the curvature at a minimum is $-4(n-1)a$, so both sides give

$$
\boxed{\gamma=1}.
$$

At the ordered minimum, $a=-nu m^{2n-2}$ and

$$
f_{\rm sing}=(1-n)u m^{2n}\sim-|T-T_c|^{n/(n-1)}.
$$

Since $f_{\rm sing}\sim|T-T_c|^{2-\alpha}$, the [heat-capacity critical exponent](../../../../../../../heat-capacity-critical-exponent.md) is

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{n-2}{n-1}}.
$$

These values obey the [Widom scaling relation](../../../../../../../widom-scaling-relation.md) and [Rushbrooke scaling relation](../../../../../../../rushbrooke-scaling-relation.md) at mean-field level.
