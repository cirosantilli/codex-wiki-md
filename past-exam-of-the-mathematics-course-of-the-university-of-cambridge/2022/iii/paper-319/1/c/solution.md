<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $(u_n)$ be a [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) in the [graph norm](../../../../../../graph-norm.md)

$$
\|u\|_Y=\|u\|+\|Au\|.
$$

Then $(u_n)$ and $(Au_n)$ are [Cauchy sequences](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) in the [Banach space](../../../../../../banach-space-split.md) $H$, so for some $u,v\in H$,

$$
u_n\to u,
\qquad
Au_n\to v.
$$

Because $A$ is a [closed linear operator](../../../../../../closed-linear-operator.md), $u\in D(A)$ and $Au=v$. It follows that

$$
\|u_n-u\|_Y=\|u_n-u\|+\|Au_n-Au\|\longrightarrow0.
$$

Thus every [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) converges in $Y=(D(A),\|\cdot\|_Y)$, and

$$
\boxed{Y\text{ is a Banach space}}.
$$
