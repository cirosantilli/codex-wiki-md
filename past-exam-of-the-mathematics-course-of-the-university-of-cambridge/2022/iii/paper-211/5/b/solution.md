<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The product rule and self-financing identity show that $XY$ is the stochastic integral of $H$ against the vector of deflated prices $YP$, hence is a [local martingale](../../../../../../local-martingale.md). It is nonnegative by admissibility and positivity of $Y$. Every nonnegative local martingale is a [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md), so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(X_TY_T)\leq X_0Y_0.}
$$
