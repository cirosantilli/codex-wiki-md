<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the bond maturing one period later,

$$
\mathbb E(Y_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})
=Y_{t-1}P_{t-1}^t
=\frac{Y_{t-1}}{1+r_t}.
$$

If $r_t\geq0$, this is at most $Y_{t-1}$, which is exactly the [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md) property.
