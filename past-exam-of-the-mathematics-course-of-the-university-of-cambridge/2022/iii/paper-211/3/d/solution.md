<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The proposed variable satisfies

$$
\mathbb E(ZP_1)
=\mathbb E(P_1P_1^T)Q^{-1}P_0=P_0.
$$

No arbitrage and the [fundamental theorem of asset pricing](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-asset-pricing.md) provide a strictly positive one-period deflator $Y$ with $\mathbb E(YP_1)=P_0$. Part c makes such a deflator unique in a complete market, so $Z=Y>0$ almost surely.
