<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The two-dimensional [Itô formula](../../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md), using $d[W^X,W^Z]_t=\rho\,dt$, gives the drift of $U(t,Z_t,X_t)$ as

$$
U_t+BU_z+\frac12C^2U_{zz}
+zC\rho U_{zx}
+\frac12z^2(U_{xx}-U_x).
$$

The PDE makes this zero, leaving only stochastic-integral terms. Thus $M_t=U(t,Z_t,X_t)$ is a [local martingale](../../../../../../local-martingale.md).
