<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substitute $U=e^{\theta x}V$. After dividing by $e^{\theta x}$, the PDE becomes

$$
V_t+(B+\theta\rho zC)V_z
+\frac12C^2V_{zz}
+\frac12\theta(\theta-1)z^2V=0,
$$

with terminal condition $V(T,z)=1$.
