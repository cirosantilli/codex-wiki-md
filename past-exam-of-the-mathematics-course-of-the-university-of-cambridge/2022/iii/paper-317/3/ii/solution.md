The three [stellar adiabatic exponents](../../../../../../stellar-adiabatic-exponent.md) are defined by

$$
\Gamma_1=\left(\frac{\partial\log P}{\partial\log\rho}\right)_s,
\qquad
\Gamma_3-1=\left(\frac{\partial\log T}{\partial\log\rho}\right)_s,
$$



$$
\frac{\Gamma_2}{\Gamma_2-1}
=\left(\frac{\partial\log P}{\partial\log T}\right)_s.
$$

For the gas-radiation mixture,

$$
\chi_\rho=\left(\frac{\partial\log P}{\partial\log\rho}\right)_T=\beta,
\qquad
\chi_T=\left(\frac{\partial\log P}{\partial\log T}\right)_\rho=4-3\beta,
$$

and

$$
c_V=\frac{\mathcal R}{2\beta}(24-21\beta).
$$

It follows that

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_3-1=\frac{8-6\beta}{24-21\beta}},
$$



$$
\boxed{\Gamma_1=\frac{32-24\beta-3\beta^2}{24-21\beta}},
\qquad
\boxed{\Gamma_2=\frac{32-24\beta-3\beta^2}
{24-18\beta-3\beta^2}}.
$$

Their defining derivatives imply the identity

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Gamma_1}{\Gamma_3-1}
=\frac{\Gamma_2}{\Gamma_2-1}}.
$$
