<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In the gas-pressure limit $\beta\to1$, the mixture becomes a monatomic [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md), so

$$
\boxed{\gamma=\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2=\Gamma_3=\frac53}.
$$

In the radiation-pressure limit $\beta\to0$,

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2=\Gamma_3=\frac43},
$$

whereas $\chi_\rho=\beta\to0$ and therefore

$$
\boxed{\gamma=\frac{c_P}{c_V}\to\infty}.
$$

The divergence reflects the singular constant-pressure heat capacity of pure radiation: its pressure fixes its temperature independently of density.
