<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $q=32/31$. The supplied approximation $x=qy(qy+1)$ and $x\propto T^{1/2}$ imply

$$
\rho\propto yx^6,\qquad
P\propto(1+y)x^8.
$$

Therefore

$$
\frac{d\log x}{d\log y}
=\frac{1+2qy}{1+qy},
$$

and direct logarithmic differentiation gives

$$
1+\frac1n=\frac{d\log P}{d\log\rho}
=\frac{544y^2+791y+248}{(1+y)(416y+217)}.
$$

Thus the model is locally a [stellar polytrope](../../../../../../stellar-polytrope.md) with

$$
\boxed{
n(y)=\frac{(1+y)(416y+217)}
{128y^2+158y+31}}.
$$

Since $y=\beta/(1-\beta)$,

$$
\boxed{n(\beta)=\frac{199\beta+217}
{\beta^2+96\beta+31}}.
$$

The radiation-pressure and gas-pressure limits are

$$
\boxed{n(0)=7,\qquad n(1)=\frac{13}{4}}.
$$
