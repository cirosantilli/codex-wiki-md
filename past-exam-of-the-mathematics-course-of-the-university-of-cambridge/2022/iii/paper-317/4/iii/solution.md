<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At any radius,

$$
\frac{dP_{\rm rad}}{dP}
=\frac{\kappa L_r}{4\pi cGM_r}
=\frac{L_r}{L_{\rm crit}},
\qquad
L_{\rm crit}=\frac{4\pi cGM_r}{\kappa}.
$$

Both gas and radiation pressures decrease outwards, so  
$0<dP_{\rm rad}/dP<1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{L_r<L_{\rm crit}=\frac{4\pi cGM_r}{\kappa}}.
$$

Equality is the [Eddington luminosity](../../../../../../eddington-luminosity.md), where radiation supplies the entire outward force and hydrostatic gas support is lost.
