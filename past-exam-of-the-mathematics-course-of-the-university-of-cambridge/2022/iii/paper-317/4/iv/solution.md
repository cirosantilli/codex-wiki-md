<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

[Stellar homology](../../../../../../stellar-homology.md) gives

$$
P_c\sim\frac{GM^2}{R^4},\qquad
\rho_c\sim\frac{M}{R^3}.
$$

For an $n=3$ polytrope, $P=K\rho^{4/3}$, so these scalings imply

$$
K\sim GM^{2/3},\qquad M\propto(K/G)^{3/2}.
$$

The equation of state in part i gives

$$
K^3\propto\frac{1-\beta}{\beta^4}
$$

at fixed chemical composition. Substitution into the homologous mass scale proves the [Eddington quartic relation](../../../../../../eddington-quartic-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{M\propto\frac{(1-\beta)^{1/2}}{\beta^2}}.
$$
