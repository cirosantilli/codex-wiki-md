<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The gas-to-radiation pressure ratio gives

$$
\frac{\beta}{1-\beta}
=\frac{P_g}{P_{\rm rad}}
=\frac{3\rho k_B}{a_{\rm rad}\mu H\,T^3}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{
T(\beta,\rho)=
\left[\frac{3k_B}{a_{\rm rad}\mu H}
\frac{1-\beta}{\beta}\rho\right]^{1/3}},
$$

and either pressure component gives

$$
\boxed{
P(\beta,\rho)=\frac{k_B}{\mu H\beta}
\left[\frac{3k_B}{a_{\rm rad}\mu H}
\frac{1-\beta}{\beta}\right]^{1/3}
\rho^{4/3}}.
$$
