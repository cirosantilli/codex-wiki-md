The reported 95% [credible interval](../../../../../../credible-interval.md) for $\beta_1$ is

$$
[-0.33,\,2.37].
$$

Conditional on the model, prior, and observed data, its posterior probability is 0.95. A frequentist 95% [confidence interval](../../../../../../confidence-interval.md) instead has 95% coverage under repeated sampling before the data are observed; it does not assign a sampling probability to the fixed parameter after observing this dataset.
