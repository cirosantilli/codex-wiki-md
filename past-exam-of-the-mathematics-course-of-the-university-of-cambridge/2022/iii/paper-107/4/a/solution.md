The [Weak Harnack inequality](../../../../../../weak-harnack-inequality.md) states that there are $p>0$ and $C=C(n,\lambda,\Lambda)$ such that every nonnegative weak supersolution satisfies

$$
\boxed{
\left(\frac1{|B_{3/4}|}\int_{B_{3/4}}u^p\right)^{1/p}
\leq C\inf_{B_{1/2}}u.}
$$

The radii may be replaced by any fixed nested pair of balls, with the constant adjusted accordingly.
