↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**No.** Let $\lambda_1>0$ be the [First Dirichlet eigenvalue](../../../../../../first-dirichlet-eigenvalue.md) of $-\Delta$ on $B$, choose a corresponding nonzero eigenfunction $w$, and take

$$
L=\Delta+\lambda_1,
\qquad
u_k=kw.
$$

Then $Lu_k=0$ and every $u_k$ vanishes on $\partial B$, so the boundary values converge uniformly to those of $v=0$. But $|u_k|_{0;B}\to\infty$, and hence no subsequence converges uniformly on $B$.
