↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Alexandrov–Bakelman–Pucci maximum principle](../../../../../../alexandrov-bakelman-pucci-estimate.md) and the absence of a zeroth-order term give

$$
\boxed{|u|_{0;B}\leq C(n,\lambda,\beta)|f|_{0;B}.}
$$

The global [Schauder estimate](../../../../../../schauder-estimates.md) is

$$
\boxed{|u|_{2,\alpha;B}
\leq C(n,\alpha,\beta,\lambda)
\big(|f|_{0,\alpha;B}+|u|_{0;B}\big).}
$$

Combining the two bounds gives $|u|_{2,\alpha;B}\leq C|f|_{0,\alpha;B}$.
