↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](../../../../../../weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) says that if $c\leq0$ and

$$
\Delta u+cu\geq0,
$$

then

$$
\boxed{\sup_\Omega u\leq\max\{0,\sup_{\partial\Omega}u\}.}
$$

Indeed, a positive interior maximum has $Du=0$ and $\Delta u\leq0$, so the differential inequality is incompatible with a strict positive maximum. Applying this argument to a standard strictly perturbed function and then letting the perturbation tend to zero handles equality and proves the weak statement.
