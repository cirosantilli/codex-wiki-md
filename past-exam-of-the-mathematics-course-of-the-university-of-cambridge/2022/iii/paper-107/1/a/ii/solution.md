<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $u_1,u_2$ are two solutions with the same boundary values, put $w=u_1-u_2$. The [mean value theorem](../../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) gives

$$
\Delta w=q(x)w,
\qquad
q(x)=\int_0^1\partial_tg(x,u_2+s(u_1-u_2))\,ds\geq0.
$$

**Therefore $\Delta w-qw=0$ with zero boundary data. Apply the [weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](../../../../../../../weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) to $w$ and $-w$ with zeroth-order coefficient $-q\leq0$. It follows that $w=0$, proving uniqueness.**
