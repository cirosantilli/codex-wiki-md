<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an arbitrary finite-dimensional complex [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) $L$, a [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) is a nilpotent [Lie subalgebra](../../../../../../lie-subalgebra.md) $H$ equal to its own [normalizer](../../../../../../normalizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md). When $L$ is [semisimple](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), this is equivalently a maximal abelian subalgebra consisting of elements that act semisimply in the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md).

Choose such an $H$. Simultaneous diagonalization gives the [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md)

$$
L=H\oplus\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Phi}L_\alpha,
\qquad
L_\alpha=\{x\in L:[h,x]=\alpha(h)x\text{ for every }h\in H\}.
$$

The nonzero [weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) $\alpha\in H^*$ are the roots. The restriction of the [Killing form](../../../../../../killing-form.md) $B$ to $H$ is a [nondegenerate bilinear form](../../../../../../nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md), so each $\alpha$ corresponds to a unique $t_\alpha\in H$ with $\alpha(h)=B(t_\alpha,h)$. On the real span of these $t_\alpha$, the restriction of $B$ supplies a positive-definite inner product after choosing the standard real form. The [sl2 subalgebra associated with a root](../../../../../../sl2-subalgebra-associated-with-a-root.md) gives

$$
s_\alpha(\beta)=\beta-\langle\beta,\alpha^\vee\rangle\alpha,
\qquad
\langle\beta,\alpha^\vee\rangle\in\mathbb Z,
$$

and shows that these reflections preserve the finite set $\Phi$. Thus the roots form a finite reduced crystallographic [root system](../../../../../../root-system.md), whose [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) is generated by these reflections.
