<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For every $x\in BC^{-1}$, choose one representation $x=b_xc_x^{-1}$. Define

$$
F:A\times BC^{-1}\longrightarrow AB^{-1}\times AC^{-1},
\qquad
F(a,x)=(ab_x^{-1},ac_x^{-1}).
$$

The image determines

$$
(ab_x^{-1})^{-1}(ac_x^{-1})=b_xc_x^{-1}=x,
$$

and the chosen representative then determines $a=(ab_x^{-1})b_x$. Thus $F$ is injective, and counting its domain and codomain proves the [Noncommutative Ruzsa triangle inequality](../../../../../../noncommutative-ruzsa-triangle-inequality.md)

$$
\boxed{|A|\,|BC^{-1}|\leq|AB^{-1}|\,|AC^{-1}|.}
$$
