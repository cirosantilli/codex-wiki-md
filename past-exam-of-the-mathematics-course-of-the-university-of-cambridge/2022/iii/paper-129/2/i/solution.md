<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4\in\phi^{-1}(W)$ with $x_1+x_2=x_3+x_4$. Write $x_3=x_1+a$ and $x_4=x_1+b$; then $x_2=x_1+a+b$. Hence

$$
D=\phi(x_1)-\phi(x_3)-\phi(x_4)+\phi(x_2)
$$

belongs to $X$. All four values of $\phi$ lie in the same affine subspace $W=V+w$, and their coefficients in $D$ sum to zero, so $D\in V$. The hypothesis $V\cap X=\{0\}$ gives $D=0$, precisely the additive-quadruple identity required of a [Freiman homomorphism](../../../../../../freiman-homomorphism.md). This is the [second-difference obstruction to a Freiman homomorphism](../../../../../../second-difference-obstruction-to-a-freiman-homomorphism.md).
