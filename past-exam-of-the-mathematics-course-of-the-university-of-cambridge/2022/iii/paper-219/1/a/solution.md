<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For distinct [sibling supernovae](../../../../../../type-ia-supernova.md) $s$ and $s'$ in the same galaxy, the shared fluctuation is the only common random term. Independence and [linearity of covariance](../../../../../../linearity-of-covariance.md) therefore give

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(M_s,M_{s'})=\sigma_G^2,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(M_s)=\sigma_G^2+\sigma_I^2
\equiv\sigma_{\rm tot}^2.
$$

Their [correlation coefficient](../../../../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Corr}(M_s,M_{s'})
=\frac{\sigma_G^2}{\sigma_G^2+\sigma_I^2}.
$$

This is the [intraclass correlation coefficient](../../../../../../intraclass-correlation-coefficient.md) of the galaxy-level [random intercept](../../../../../../random-intercept.md) model.
