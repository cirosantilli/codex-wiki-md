<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In a real closed field, the formula $\exists z\,(z^2=x)$ defines the nonnegative elements. A quantifier-free formula in one variable over the prime field is a Boolean combination of polynomial equations, so in an infinite field it defines a finite or cofinite set. The nonnegative cone is neither finite nor cofinite. Hence the [Theory of real closed fields](../../../../../../theory-of-real-closed-fields.md) does not eliminate quantifiers in the pure ring language.
