<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expand RCF by a constant $c$ and the sentences $c>n$ for all natural numbers $n$. Every finite subset is realized in $\mathbb R$. The [compactness theorem](../../../../../../compactness-theorem.md) gives a model realizing all of them, hence a [Non-Archimedean real closed field](../../../../../../non-archimedean-real-closed-field.md).
