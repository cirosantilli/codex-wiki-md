The [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](../../../../../../../murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) states that if a permutation has a $k$-cycle and remaining cycle type $\rho$, then

$$
\chi^\lambda(k,\rho)
=\sum_H(-1)^{\operatorname{leg}(H)}
\chi^{\lambda\setminus H}(\rho),
$$

where $H$ ranges over removable [rim hooks](../../../../../../../rim-hook.md) of length $k$ and $\operatorname{leg}(H)$ is one less than the number of rows occupied by $H$.
