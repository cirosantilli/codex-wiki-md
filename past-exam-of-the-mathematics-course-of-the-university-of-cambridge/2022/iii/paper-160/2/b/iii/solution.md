<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Conjugating a tableau exchanges row symmetrization with column antisymmetrization. The resulting module is the original Specht module twisted by the [sign representation](../../../../../../../sign-representation.md), giving the [conjugate Specht character](../../../../../../../conjugate-specht-character.md)

$$
S^{\lambda'}\cong S^\lambda\otimes\operatorname{sgn}_{S_n}.
$$

Taking traces yields

$$
\boxed{\chi^{\lambda'}=\chi^\lambda\operatorname{sgn}_{S_n}.}
$$
