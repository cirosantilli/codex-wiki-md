↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the staircase $\delta=(m,m-1,\ldots,1)$,

$$
h_{i,j}(\delta)=2(m+1-i-j)+1
$$

whenever $(i,j)$ is a cell, so every hook length is odd. A removable rim hook of length $k$ corresponds to a hook of length $k$ in the original diagram. If a cycle type $\mu$ contains an even part $k$, apply the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](../../../../../../../murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) to that part first. There are no terms in the sum, and therefore $\chi^\delta(\mu)=0$.
