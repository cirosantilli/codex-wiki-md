<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using the [abacus of a partition](../../../../../../abacus-of-a-partition.md) in James's convention, read runner $r$ as a one-runner abacus: replace each occupied position $r+eq$ by $q$. After the usual harmless shift of its beta set, the resulting partition is $\lambda^{(r)}$. Doing this for $r=0,\ldots,e-1$ produces

$$
Q_e(\lambda)=(\lambda^{(0)},\ldots,\lambda^{(e-1)}).
$$

Conjugating a partition complements beads and gaps and reflects the abacus. Reflection sends runner $r$ to runner $e-1-r$, reverses bead-gap order, and conjugates the runner partition. Hence

$$
\boxed{Q_e(\lambda')
=\bigl((\lambda^{(e-1)})',\ldots,
(\lambda^{(1)})',(\lambda^{(0)})'\bigr).}
$$
