<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Removing an $e$-hook removes one cell of every residue modulo $e$. If $\lambda$ and $\mu$ have the same $e$-core and the same size, part a(i) shows that they have the same $e$-weight. Their residue multisets are therefore obtained from the common core by adjoining the same number of complete residue sets $\{0,1,\ldots,e-1\}$. Thus their [$e$-contents](../../../../../../../residue-content-of-a-partition.md) are equal.
