<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The beta number in row $i$ is

$$
h_i=\lambda_i+m-i.
$$

Since $e\mid m$,

$$
r_e(i,\lambda_i)
\equiv\lambda_i-i
\equiv h_i-m
\equiv h_i\pmod e.
$$
