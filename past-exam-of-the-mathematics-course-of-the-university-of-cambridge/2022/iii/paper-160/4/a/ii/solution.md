<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Subtract one from each of the $e$ positive parts. This bijects partitions into exactly $e$ parts with partitions into at most $e$ parts and decreases the size by $e$. The required generating function is therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{x^e}{(1-x)(1-x^2)\cdots(1-x^e)}.}
$$
