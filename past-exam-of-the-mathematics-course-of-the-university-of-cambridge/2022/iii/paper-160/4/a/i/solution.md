<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each permitted part $i\leq e$, its multiplicity contributes the [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md) $1+x^i+x^{2i}+\cdots$. Thus the generating function is

$$
\prod_{i=1}^e\frac1{1-x^i}.
$$

The [conjugate partition](../../../../../../../conjugate-partition.md) bijects diagrams with largest part at most $e$ and diagrams with at most $e$ rows, so the same series counts partitions into at most $e$ parts.
