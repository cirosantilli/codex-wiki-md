<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a copy of $S^\alpha$ inside $M^\lambda$ and a nonzero polytabloid in it. Its image under a suitable [Column antisymmetrizer of a Young tableau](../../../../../../../column-antisymmetrizer-of-a-young-tableau.md) is nonzero. By the fact allowed in the question, this can happen only if $\alpha$ dominates $\lambda$. Hence $m_\alpha>0$ implies $\alpha\mathrel{\unrhd}\lambda$.
