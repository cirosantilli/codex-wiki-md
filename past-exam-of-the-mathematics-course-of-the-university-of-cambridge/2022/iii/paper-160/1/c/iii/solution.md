<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By part ii, the common tabloids in the supports of $e(t)$ and $e(t^*)$ are precisely $k\{t\}$ with $k\in C(t)\cap C(t^*)$, and each contributes $\operatorname{sgn}(k)^2=1$. Consequently

$$
\langle e(t),e(t^*)\rangle=|C(t)\cap C(t^*)|.
$$

For the $a_j$ rows of length $j$, an element of the intersection applies the same arbitrary permutation of those rows independently in each of their $j$ columns. Hence that row length contributes $(a_j!)^j$, and

$$
\boxed{\langle e(t),e(t^*)\rangle
=\prod_{j=1}^n(a_j!)^j.}
$$
