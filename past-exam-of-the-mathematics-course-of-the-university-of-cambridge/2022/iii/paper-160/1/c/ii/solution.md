<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The nonzero coefficients and part a(ii) give unique elements $h\in C(t)$ and $h^*\in C(t^*)$ such that

$$
\{u\}=h\{t\}=h^*\{t^*\}.
$$

Part i gives $h=h^*=:k$, so $k\in C(t)\cap C(t^*)$. Both coefficients are then $\operatorname{sgn}(k)$ and hence are equal.
