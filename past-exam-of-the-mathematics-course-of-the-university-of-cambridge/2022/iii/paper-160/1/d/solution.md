<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $\theta\in\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb F S_n}(S^\lambda)$ and put

$$
q=\prod_{j=1}^n(a_j!)^j.
$$

Since $\lambda$ is [$p$-regular](../../../../../../regular-partition.md), every $a_j<p$, so $q\ne0$ in $\mathbb F$. Parts b and c give $b_t e(t^*)=q e(t)$. Since $\theta$ commutes with the group-algebra action,

$$
b_t\theta(e(t^*))
=\theta(b_t e(t^*))
=q\,\theta(e(t)).
$$

The left side belongs to $b_tM^\lambda=\mathbb F e(t)$. Division by $q$ shows that $\theta(e(t))$ is a scalar multiple of $e(t)$. Part a(i) says that $e(t)$ generates $S^\lambda$, so $\theta$ is that scalar multiple of the identity. This proves the [endomorphism theorem for a regular Specht module](../../../../../../endomorphism-theorem-for-a-regular-specht-module.md).
