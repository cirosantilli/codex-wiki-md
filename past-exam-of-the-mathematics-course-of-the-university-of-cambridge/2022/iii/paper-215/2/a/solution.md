<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

One step of the [Glauber dynamics](../../../../../../glauber-dynamics.md) chooses a vertex $v$ uniformly and resamples its spin from its conditional [Ising model](../../../../../../ising-model.md) distribution given all other spins. If the proposed new spin is $x$ and $S_v(\sigma)=\sum_{w\sim v}\sigma(w)$, its update probability is

$$
\boxed{\frac1n
\frac{e^{\beta xS_v(\sigma)}}
{e^{\beta S_v(\sigma)}+e^{-\beta S_v(\sigma)}}
=\frac{1+\tanh\{\beta xS_v(\sigma)\}}{2n}.}
$$
