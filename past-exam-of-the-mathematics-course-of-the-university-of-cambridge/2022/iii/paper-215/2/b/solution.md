↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The worst-case distance to stationarity is

$$
d(t)=\max_\sigma\|P^t(\sigma,\cdot)-\pi_\beta\|_{\rm TV}.
$$

The [Path coupling theorem](../../../../../../path-coupling-theorem.md) says that if a coupling contracts an integer-valued path metric by a factor $\alpha<1$ for every adjacent pair, then

$$
d(t)\leq\operatorname{diam}(\Omega)\alpha^t.
$$
