<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Observe the lazy walk on $\mathbb Z_{2^k}$ only after every second genuine jump. Two independent nearest-neighbor signs have sum $-2,0,2$ with probabilities $1/4,1/2,1/4$. Division by two therefore produces one step of the lazy simple random walk on $\mathbb Z_{2^{k-1}}$. The [Strong Markov property](../../../../../../strong-markov-property.md) at successive jump times proves

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{X_{T_j^{(k)}}^{(k)}}2\right)_{j\geq0}
\overset d=
\left(X_j^{(k-1)}\right)_{j\geq0}.}
$$
