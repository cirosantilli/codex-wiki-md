<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [strong energy condition](../../../../../../strong-energy-condition.md) and Einstein's equation imply $R_{ab}t^at^b\geq0$. Since the congruence is hypersurface orthogonal, its vorticity vanishes. The tensor in the hint is the trace-free spatial shear, so

$$
S_{ab}S^{ab}
=(\nabla_at_b)(\nabla^at^b)-\frac13(\nabla_at^a)^2\geq0.
$$

Because $\nabla_at^a=-c$, part b gives

$$
\boxed{t^a\nabla_ac\geq\frac13c^2}.
$$
