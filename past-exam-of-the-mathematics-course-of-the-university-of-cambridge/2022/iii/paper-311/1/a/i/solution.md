<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Outside the star, $p=\rho=0$. The first [Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equation](../../../../../../../tolman-oppenheimer-volkoff-equation.md) gives $m(r)=M$, and the second gives

$$
\Phi'(r)=\frac{M}{r(r-2M)}
=\frac12\frac d{dr}\log\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right).
$$

Asymptotic flatness fixes the integration constant, so $e^{2\Phi}=1-2M/r$. The exterior line element is therefore the [Schwarzschild metric](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) with mass $M=m(R)$.
