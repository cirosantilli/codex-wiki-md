<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Every metric coefficient and the gauge potential are independent of $t$. Therefore

$$
\mathcal L_{\partial_t}g=0,
$$

so $K=\partial_t$ is a [Killing vector field](../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md).
