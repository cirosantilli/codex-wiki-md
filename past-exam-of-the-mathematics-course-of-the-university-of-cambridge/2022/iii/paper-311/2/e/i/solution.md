<h1 id="2/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate $E=-mK_aU^a+q\Phi$ along the worldline. The symmetric contraction $U^aU^b\nabla_aK_b$ vanishes by the [Killing equation](../../../../../../../killing-equation.md). Using the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) and $K^aF_{ab}=\nabla_b\Phi$, the remaining force term cancels $qU^a\nabla_a\Phi$. Hence

$$
\boxed{U^a\nabla_aE=0}.
$$
