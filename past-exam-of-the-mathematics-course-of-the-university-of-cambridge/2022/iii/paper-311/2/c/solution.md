<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The normal to $r=r_+$ is $dr$, whose squared norm is $g^{rr}=f(r_+)=0$, so the surface is a [null hypersurface](../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md). In ingoing coordinates $K=\partial_v$, and on the horizon

$$
K_a=g_{av}=(0,1,0,\ldots)=\nabla_ar.
$$

Thus $K$ is both tangent and normal there, making the surface a [Killing horizon](../../../../../../killing-horizon.md). For a static metric of this form, the [surface gravity](../../../../../../surface-gravity.md) is $\kappa=f'(r_+)/2$. With $n=d-3$,

$$
\boxed{\kappa=\frac{d-3}{2r_+}
\left[1-\left(\frac{r_-}{r_+}\right)^{d-3}\right]}.
$$
