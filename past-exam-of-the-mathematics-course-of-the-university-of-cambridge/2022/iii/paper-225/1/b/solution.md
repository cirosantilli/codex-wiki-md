<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every orthonormal basis $(e_j)$ of the infinite-dimensional Hilbert space $L^2[0,1]$, the [identity operator](../../../../../../identity-operator.md) satisfies

$$
\operatorname{tr}I
=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\langle Ie_j,e_j\rangle
=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}1=\infty.
$$

Part a shows that every covariance operator of a square-integrable Hilbert-space random element is trace-class. Therefore the identity cannot be a covariance operator.
