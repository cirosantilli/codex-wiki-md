<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose a unit vector $e$ and define the rank-one operator

$$
Th=-\langle h,e\rangle e.
$$

It is bounded, self-adjoint, and [Hilbert-Schmidt](../../../../../../hilbert-schmidt-operator.md), with $\lVert T\rVert_{\rm HS}=1$. But

$$
\langle Te,e\rangle=-1<0,
$$

so it is not positive. Since every covariance operator is positive, $T$ is the required counterexample.
