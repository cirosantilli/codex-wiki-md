<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Uniform orientation on the [unit sphere](../../../../../../unit-sphere.md) means that probability is proportional to the solid-angle element

$$
d\Omega=\sin i\,di\,d\varphi.
$$

Because the minor axis is unoriented, it is enough to take $0\leq i\leq\pi/2$. After integration over the azimuth and normalization,

$$
\boxed{p_i(i)=\sin i,
\qquad 0\leq i\leq\frac\pi2}.
$$

Equivalently, the [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $\mu=\cos i$ has the [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $[0,1]$.
