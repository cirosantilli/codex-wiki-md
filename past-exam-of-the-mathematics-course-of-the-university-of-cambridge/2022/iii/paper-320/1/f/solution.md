<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

With

$$
\mathcal P(q)=\frac4\pi\sqrt{1-q^2},
$$

the factor $\sqrt{1-q^2}$ cancels from the [Abel transform](../../../../../../abel-transform.md):

$$
\mathcal P(Q)
=\frac{4Q}{\pi}\int_0^Q\frac{dq}{\sqrt{Q^2-q^2}}
=\frac{4Q}{\pi}\cdot\frac\pi2.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathcal P(Q)=2Q,
\qquad 0\leq Q\leq1}.
$$

This [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is normalized and again favors rounder projections.
