<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For compactly supported smooth vector fields, two integrations by parts show that $-\Delta$ is formally self-adjoint. Pointwise,

$$
(A\phi)\cdot v=\phi\cdot(A^Tv).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{L^\dagger=-\Delta+A^T.}
$$

It follows that $L=L^\dagger$ exactly when $A=A^T$, that is, when $A$ is a [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) at every point.
