<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $\phi\in C^2(\overline U)^2$, take $v\in C_c^\infty(U)^2$ and integrate the gradient term by parts. The weak identity becomes

$$
\int_U(-\Delta\phi+A\phi-F)\cdot v=0.
$$

The [fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations](../../../../../../../fundamental-lemma-of-the-calculus-of-variations.md) gives $-\Delta\phi+A\phi=F$ pointwise. Membership in $H_0^1(U)^2$, together with continuity up to the boundary, says that the boundary trace is zero, so the Dirichlet condition also holds classically.
