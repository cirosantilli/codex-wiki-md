<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The assumed linear estimate and the first bound give

$$
\lVert A(w)\rVert_{L_t^\infty H_x^1}
\leq\alpha\big(\lVert\psi\rVert_{H^1}
+\beta\tau^{1/2}b^3\big).
$$

Choose $b>2\alpha\lVert\psi\rVert_{H^1}$ and then choose $\tau>0$ so small that $\alpha\beta\tau^{1/2}b^3\leq b/2$. This proves that $A$ maps $X_{b,\tau}$ into itself.

For $w,\widetilde w\in X_{b,\tau}$, linearity of the heat equation and the second cubic estimate give

$$
\lVert A(w)-A(\widetilde w)\rVert_{L_t^\infty H_x^1}
\leq2\alpha\gamma\tau^{1/2}b^2
\lVert w-\widetilde w\rVert_{L_t^\infty H_x^1}.
$$

Shrinking $\tau$ again makes $2\alpha\gamma\tau^{1/2}b^2<1$. Thus $A$ is a contraction of the closed ball $X_{b,\tau}$ in the Banach space $L^\infty((0,\tau);H_0^1(U))$.
