<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The functional

$$
I:H^1(U)\to\mathbb R,
\qquad I(u)=\int_Uu
$$

is bounded by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Hence $H=\ker I$ is a closed vector subspace of the Hilbert space $H^1(U)$. Every closed subspace of a Hilbert space is complete in the inherited norm, so $H$ is a Hilbert space with the standard $H^1$ inner product.
