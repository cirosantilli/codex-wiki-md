↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

For fixed $f\in L^2$, the functional

$$
\ell_f(v)=\langle v,f\rangle_{L^2}
$$

is bounded on $\Sigma$ by the continuous embedding from part 2:

$$
|\ell_f(v)|\leq\|v\|_2\|f\|_2\leq C\|v\|_\Sigma\|f\|_2.
$$

The [Riesz representation theorem](../../../../../../../riesz-representation-theorem.md) gives a unique $T(f)\in\Sigma$ such that $\langle v,T(f)\rangle_\Sigma=\ell_f(v)$ for every $v\in\Sigma$. It also gives

$$
\|T(f)\|_\Sigma=\|\ell_f\|_{\Sigma^*}\leq C\|f\|_2,
$$

so the [linear operator](../../../../../../../linear-operator.md) $T:L^2\to\Sigma$ is bounded.
