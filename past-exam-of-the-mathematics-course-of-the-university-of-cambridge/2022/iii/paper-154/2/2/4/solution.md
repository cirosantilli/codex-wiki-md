The [Pohozaev identity for the mass-critical NLS ground state](../../../../../../../pohozaev-identity-for-the-mass-critical-nls-ground-state.md) gives

$$
E(Q)=0,\qquad
\|Q\|_p^p=\frac p2\|\nabla Q\|_2^2.
$$

Since $Q$ is a minimizer,

$$
\boxed{I=J(Q)
=\frac{\|\nabla Q\|_2^2\|Q\|_2^{4/d}}{\|Q\|_p^p}
=\frac2p\|Q\|_2^{4/d}
=\frac{2}{2+4/d}\|Q\|_2^{4/d}.}
$$
