<h1 id="2/2/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The definition of the [infimum](../../../../../../../infimum.md) $I$ gives

$$
\|u\|_p^p
\leq I^{-1}\|\nabla u\|_2^2\|u\|_2^{4/d}.
$$

Substituting the value of $I$ from part 4 into the energy yields

$$
\begin{aligned}
E(u)
&=\frac12\|\nabla u\|_2^2-\frac1p\|u\|_p^p\\
&\geq\frac12\|\nabla u\|_2^2
\left[1-\left(\frac{\|u\|_2}{\|Q\|_2}\right)^{4/d}\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

This is [coercivity below the NLS ground-state mass](../../../../../../../coercivity-below-the-nls-ground-state-mass.md).
