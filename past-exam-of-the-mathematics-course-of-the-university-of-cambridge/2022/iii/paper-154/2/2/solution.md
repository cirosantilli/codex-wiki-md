<h1 id="2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

By the two invariances from part 1, normalize a minimizing sequence $(u_n)$ so that

$$
\|u_n\|_2=\|\nabla u_n\|_2=1.
$$

Replacing $u_n$ by $|u_n|$ and then by its [symmetric decreasing rearrangement](../../../../../../symmetric-decreasing-rearrangement.md) preserves its $L^2$ and $L^p$ norms and does not increase the gradient norm. A harmless dilation restores the normalization, so we may take the sequence nonnegative, radial, and radially decreasing.

The sequence has a weakly convergent subsequence in $H^1$. Radial compactness and the fixed scale give strong convergence in $L^p$; in particular the limit is nonzero because $\|u_n\|_p^p\to I^{-1}$. [Weak lower semicontinuity](../../../../../../weak-lower-semicontinuity.md) of the $L^2$ and gradient norms then shows that the limit attains the infimum. This is the [existence of a Weinstein-functional minimizer](../../../../../../existence-of-a-weinstein-functional-minimizer.md).
