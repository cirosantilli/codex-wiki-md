For the equality constraint, the [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) and dual function are

$$
L(x,z)=f(x)+z^T(Ax-b),
\qquad q(z)=\inf_xL(x,z),
$$

and the [Lagrangian dual problem](../../../../../../lagrangian-dual-problem.md) is $\sup_{z\in\mathbb R^m}q(z)$. [Weak duality](../../../../../../weak-duality.md) says $q(z)\leq p^*$ for every $z$. [Strong duality](../../../../../../strong-duality.md) means the dual supremum equals the primal infimum, usually with a dual maximizer. A sufficient convex constraint qualification is that $f$ be proper, closed and convex and that some $x\in\operatorname{ri}(\operatorname{dom}f)$ satisfy $Ax=b$.
