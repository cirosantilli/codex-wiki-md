<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $g(u)=tf(u)+\|u\|^2/2$. Expanding the square gives

$$
\boxed{M_tf(x)=\frac1{2t}\|x\|^2-\frac1t g^*(x).}
$$

The function $g$ is one-[strongly convex](../../../../../../../strongly-convex-function.md), so its [Fenchel conjugate](../../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) is differentiable with one-Lipschitz gradient. The displayed identity therefore proves that the [Moreau envelope](../../../../../../../moreau-envelope.md) $M_tf$ is differentiable even when $f$ is nonsmooth.
