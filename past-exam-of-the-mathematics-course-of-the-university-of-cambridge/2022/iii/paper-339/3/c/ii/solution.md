<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The function

$$
d_D(x)=\min_{y\in D}\frac12\|y-x\|^2
$$

is the [Moreau envelope](../../../../../../../moreau-envelope.md) of the convex indicator $I_D$, and is therefore convex. It is nonnegative and vanishes exactly on $D$. Since $C\cap D\ne\varnothing$, the minimum of $d_D$ over $C$ is zero, and every minimizer belongs to both $C$ and $D$.
