<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A map $T$ is a [firmly nonexpansive mapping](../../../../../../firmly-nonexpansive-mapping.md) when

$$
\|Tx-Ty\|^2\leq(Tx-Ty)^T(x-y)
$$

for all $x,y$. Put $p=\operatorname{prox}_f(x)$ and $q=\operatorname{prox}_f(y)$. Then $x-p\in\partial f(p)$ and $y-q\in\partial f(q)$. Monotonicity of the [subdifferential](../../../../../../subdifferential.md) gives

$$
[(x-p)-(y-q)]^T(p-q)\geq0,
$$

which rearranges to

$$
\boxed{\|p-q\|^2\leq(p-q)^T(x-y).}
$$
