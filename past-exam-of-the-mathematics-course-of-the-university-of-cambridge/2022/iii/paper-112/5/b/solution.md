<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Distinct components of the positively oriented [torus link](../../../../../../torus-link.md) $T_{n,n}$ have [linking number](../../../../../../linking-number.md) one. Since every component also has framing $+1$ relative to the [Seifert framing](../../../../../../seifert-framing.md), the [surgery linking matrix](../../../../../../surgery-linking-matrix.md) and hence the [intersection form](../../../../../../intersection-form.md) of the [surgery trace](../../../../../../surgery-trace.md) $W(\widehat L_1)$ are

$$
Q_1=J_n=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&\cdots&1\\
\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
1&\cdots&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [Smith normal form](../../../../../../smith-normal-form.md) is $\operatorname{diag}(1,0,\ldots,0)$. The surgery exact sequence, equivalently the kernel and cokernel of $Q_1$, gives

$$
H_i(S^3_{\widehat L_1};\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&i=0,3,\\
\mathbb Z^{n-1},&i=1,2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

In [Kirby calculus](../../../../../../kirby-calculus.md), sliding the components over one chosen component diagonalizes the framed link to a split $+1$-framed unknot and $n-1$ zero-framed unknots. Hence

$$
W(\widehat L_1)\cong(\mathbb{CP}^2\setminus\operatorname{int}B^4)\,\natural\,\mathop{\natural}_{n-1}(S^2\times D^2),
\qquad
\boxed{S^3_{\widehat L_1}\cong\mathop{\#}_{n-1}(S^1\times S^2),}
$$

where $\natural$ denotes [boundary connected sum](../../../../../../boundary-connected-sum.md).
