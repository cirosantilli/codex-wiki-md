<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [framing of an embedded sphere](../../../../../../framing-of-an-embedded-sphere.md) $C:S^{k-1}\hookrightarrow N^{n-1}$ is a trivialization of its rank-$(n-k)$ [normal bundle](../../../../../../normal-bundle.md). The standard complex line $\mathbb{CP}^1\subset\mathbb{CP}^2$ has normal bundle of [Euler number](../../../../../../euler-number-of-a-vector-bundle.md) $+1$, so this embedded $S^2$ has no framing.

If one framing $f_0$ exists, every other orientation-compatible framing is obtained from it by a map $S^{k-1}\to SO(n-k)$. Consequently the set of homotopy classes is a torsor for

$$
[S^{k-1},SO(n-k)]=\pi_{k-1}(SO(n-k)).
$$

For $k=2$ and $n=4$, this is $\pi_1(SO(2))\cong\mathbb Z$; the integer is the winding number of one framing relative to $f_0$.
