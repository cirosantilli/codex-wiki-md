<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Consider the symmetric [Laurent polynomial](../../../../../../laurent-polynomial.md)

$$
f(t)=t^2-1+t^{-2}.
$$

It satisfies $f(1)=1$, so the [Alexander polynomial realization theorem](../../../../../../alexander-polynomial-realization-theorem.md) gives a [knot](../../../../../../knot.md) $K_3$ with $\Delta_{K_3}(t)\doteq f(t)$. The polynomial is not a [unit](../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) of $\mathbb Z[t^{\pm1}]$, whereas

$$
\boxed{\det K_3=|\Delta_{K_3}(-1)|=|1-1+1|=1.}
$$
