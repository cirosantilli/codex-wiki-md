<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the [twist knot](../../../../../../twist-knot.md) whose standard diagram has $2020$ half-twists in its twist region and two crossings in its clasp. Every nontrivial twist knot has [Seifert genus](../../../../../../seifert-genus.md) one. This diagram is a [reduced](../../../../../../reduced-knot-diagram.md) [alternating knot diagram](../../../../../../alternating-knot-diagram.md), so the [Tait crossing-number theorem](../../../../../../tait-crossing-number-theorem.md) says that its $2020+2=2022$ crossings realize the [crossing number of a knot](../../../../../../crossing-number-of-a-knot.md). Thus this knot has $g_s(K_2)=1$ and $c(K_2)=2022$.
