Let $K_1$ be the [connected sum](../../../../../../connected-sum-of-knots.md) of $2022$ [trefoils](../../../../../../trefoil-knot.md). The trefoil has [Seifert genus](../../../../../../seifert-genus.md) one, and [additivity of Seifert genus](../../../../../../additivity-of-seifert-genus.md) gives

$$
\boxed{g_s(K_1)=2022.}
$$
