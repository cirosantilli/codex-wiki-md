<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Take the untwisted [Whitehead double](../../../../../../whitehead-double.md) of a [trefoil knot](../../../../../../trefoil-knot.md). The Whitehead pattern has [winding number of a satellite pattern](../../../../../../winding-number-of-a-satellite-pattern.md) zero and becomes the [unknot](../../../../../../unknot.md) when its companion is the unknot. The [Satellite formula for the Alexander polynomial](../../../../../../satellite-formula-for-the-alexander-polynomial.md) therefore gives

$$
\Delta_{K_4}(t)\doteq\Delta_{P(U)}(t)\Delta_{T_{2,3}}(1)=1.
$$

The Whitehead pattern is geometrically essential in its solid torus, so the [satellite knot](../../../../../../satellite-knot.md) with nontrivial companion $T_{2,3}$ is nontrivial. Hence $K_4$ is not isotopic to the unknot despite having trivial [Alexander polynomial of a knot](../../../../../../alexander-polynomial.md).
