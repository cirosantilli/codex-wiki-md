<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

As $b\downarrow0$, the Huber estimating equation approaches the sign equation

$$
\sum_i\operatorname{sgn}(x_i-\theta)=0,
$$

whose solution is the [sample median](../../../../../../sample-median.md). Hence the most B-robust location M-estimator is the median, with minimum [gross-error sensitivity](../../../../../../gross-error-sensitivity.md)

$$
\boxed{\gamma^*=\frac1{2\phi(0)}=\sqrt{\frac\pi2}.}
$$
