<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\Delta=\theta_1-\theta_0>0$. For one observation from the unit-variance normal location family,

$$
\log\frac{f_{\theta_1}(x)}{f_{\theta_0}(x)}
=\Delta\left(x-\frac{\theta_0+\theta_1}{2}\right).
$$

The sample log-[likelihood ratio](../../../../../../likelihood-ratio.md) is therefore $nT_n$. By the [Neyman-Pearson lemma](../../../../../../neyman-pearson-lemma.md), the level-$\alpha$ most powerful test rejects for a sufficiently large likelihood ratio, equivalently when $T_n>C_\alpha$, with $C_\alpha$ chosen to give null rejection probability $\alpha$.
