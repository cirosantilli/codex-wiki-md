<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $m=F^{-1}(1/2)$ be the unique [median](../../../../../../median.md). The [influence function of the sample median](../../../../../../influence-function-of-the-sample-median.md) is

$$
\operatorname{IF}(x;T,F)
=\frac{\mathbf1_{\{x>m\}}-\mathbf1_{\{x<m\}}}{2f(m)}
$$

away from $m$. Its second moment, equivalently the [asymptotic distribution of a sample median](../../../../../../asymptotic-distribution-of-a-sample-median.md), gives

$$
A(T,F)=\mathbb E_F[\operatorname{IF}(X;T,F)^2]
=\frac1{4f(F^{-1}(1/2))^2}.
$$

**No symmetry assumption is used:** the density is evaluated at the actual median of $F$.
