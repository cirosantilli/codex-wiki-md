<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $g(u)=e^{-u}$,

$$
\overline S(t)=\int_0^\infty e^{-u\{1+H_0(t)\}}\,du
=\frac1{1+H_0(t)}.
$$

Therefore the population [hazard function](../../../../../../hazard-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{\overline h(t)
=-\frac d{dt}\log\overline S(t)
=\frac{h_0(t)}{1+H_0(t)}.}
$$
