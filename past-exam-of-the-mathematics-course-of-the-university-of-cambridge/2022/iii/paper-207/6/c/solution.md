<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The conditional mean frailty among survivors is

$$
\mathbb E(U\mid T>t)=\frac1{1+H_0(t)}.
$$

Because the [cumulative hazard function](../../../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md) is nondecreasing, this mean is nonincreasing: high-frailty individuals tend to experience the event earlier, leaving a progressively more robust surviving population.
