By [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md), the [frailty distribution among survivors](../../../../../../frailty-distribution-among-survivors.md) has density

$$
g(u,t)
=\frac{\mathbb P(T>t\mid U=u)g(u)}{\overline S(t)}
=\{1+H_0(t)\}e^{-u\{1+H_0(t)\}},
\qquad u\geq0.
$$

It is an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with rate $1+H_0(t)$.
