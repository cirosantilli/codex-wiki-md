↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Conditionally on survival, average the individual hazard $Uh_0(t)$:

$$
\overline h(t)
=h_0(t)\mathbb E(U\mid T>t)
=\frac{h_0(t)}{1+H_0(t)},
$$

which agrees with part a.
