<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a [constant hazard survival model](../../../../../../constant-hazard-survival-model.md), the [maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) is the number of observed events divided by total [person-time at risk](../../../../../../person-time-at-risk.md). Treatment A contributes $3$ deaths in $1+2+3+3+5=14$ months, while Treatment B contributes $2$ deaths in $1+5+5+7+10=28$ months. Hence

$$
\boxed{\widehat h_A=\frac3{14},
\qquad
\widehat h_B=\frac2{28}=\frac1{14},
\qquad
\widehat{\operatorname{HR}}=3.}
$$
