<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The expected Treatment A deaths are $1/2$ at month 1, $1/2$ at month 2, and $3(2/6)=1$ at the three tied deaths at month 5. Thus

$$
E_A=2,
\qquad O_A=3.
$$

This is not half of the five deaths because [right censoring](../../../../../../right-censoring.md) changes the treatment proportions in successive [risk sets](../../../../../../risk-set.md). For Treatment B, $O_B=2$ and $E_B=3$. One observed-to-expected relative-risk estimate is therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{O_A/E_A}{O_B/E_B}
=\frac{3/2}{2/3}=\frac94.}
$$
