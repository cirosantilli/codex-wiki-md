Vitamin D is a causal determinant of mortality risk if an [intervention](../../../../../../treatment.md) that changes vitamin-D status changes the distribution of the corresponding [potential outcome](../../../../../../potential-outcome.md) for mortality. This is a claim about a [causal effect](../../../../../../causal-effect.md), rather than merely an observed [association](../../../../../../statistical-association.md).
