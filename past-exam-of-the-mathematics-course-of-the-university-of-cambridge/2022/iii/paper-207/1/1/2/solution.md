First, an [observational study](../../../../../../../observational-study.md) can suffer from [confounding](../../../../../../../confounding.md) or [reverse causality](../../../../../../../reverse-causality.md). Ill health may both lower circulating vitamin D and raise mortality, while lifestyle, socioeconomic status, and comorbidity may affect both variables. [Randomization](../../../../../../../randomization.md) breaks these baseline associations in expectation.

Second, the interventions answer different questions. Supplementation may be too small, too late, too short, or poorly adhered to, and an average effect can be nearly zero when benefit is confined to people with severe deficiency. Such [heterogeneous treatment effects](../../../../../../../heterogeneous-treatment-effect.md) can coexist with a strong observational gradient.
