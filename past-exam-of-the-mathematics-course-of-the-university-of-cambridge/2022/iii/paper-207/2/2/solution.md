<h1 id="2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [maximum-likelihood estimators](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) are the success proportions

$$
\widehat\phi_0=\frac{120}{200}=\frac35,
\qquad
\widehat\phi_1=\frac{110}{220}=\frac12.
$$

The [invariance property of maximum likelihood estimation](../../../../../../invariance-property-of-maximum-likelihood-estimation.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\widehat\alpha=\widehat\phi_1-\widehat\phi_0
=\frac12-\frac35=-\frac1{10}.}
$$
