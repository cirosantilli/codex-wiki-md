Under [missing at random](../../../../../../../missing-at-random.md), dropout before the one-month visit may depend on observed treatment $Z$ but, conditional on $Z$, not on the unseen $Y$ or $W$. Dropout between the one- and six-month visits may depend on the observed history $(Z,Y)$ but, conditional on that history, not on the unseen $W$.
