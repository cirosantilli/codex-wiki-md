↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Ignoring factors that do not depend on the parameters, the product of the two [binomial likelihoods](../../../../../../binomial-likelihood.md) is

$$
L(\phi_0,\phi_1)
=\phi_0^{120}(1-\phi_0)^{80}
 \phi_1^{110}(1-\phi_1)^{110}.
$$

Including the sampling constants multiplies this by ${200\choose120}{220\choose110}$.
