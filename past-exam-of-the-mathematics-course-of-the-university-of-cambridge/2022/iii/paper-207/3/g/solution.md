<h1 id="3/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Dividing the $I$ equation by the $S$ equation gives

$$
\frac{dI}{dS}=-1+\frac{\gamma}{\beta S}.
$$

Therefore

$$
I+S-\frac\gamma\beta\log S
=I(0)+S(0)-\frac\gamma\beta\log S(0)
$$

is a [first integral](../../../../../../first-integral.md). Define the [basic reproduction number](../../../../../../basic-reproduction-number.md) by $R_0=\beta S(0)/\gamma$. At the removal peak, $S=S(0)/R_0$, and hence

$$
R_{\rm peak}=\frac{S(0)}{R_0}\log R_0,
$$

and, using $S+I+R=N$,

$$
I_{\rm peak}=I(0)+S(0)
-\frac{S(0)}{R_0}{1+\log R_0}.
$$
