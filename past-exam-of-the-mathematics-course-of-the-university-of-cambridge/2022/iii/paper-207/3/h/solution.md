<h1 id="3/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

The same first integral, evaluated initially and after the epidemic when $I(\infty)=0$, gives

$$
\log\frac{S(0)}{S(\infty)}
=\frac\beta\gamma R(\infty).
$$

When $I(0)$ is negligible and $R(0)=0$, [conservation of population](../../../../../../conservation-of-population.md) gives $S(\infty)\simeq S(0)-R(\infty)$. Since $\beta/\gamma=R_0/S(0)$, this is equivalent to the [final size relation for an epidemic](../../../../../../final-size-relation-for-an-epidemic.md)

$$
\boxed{R(\infty)\simeq
\frac{S(0)}{R_0}
\log\frac{S(0)}{S(0)-R(\infty)}.}
$$
