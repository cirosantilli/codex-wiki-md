<h1 id="3/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The removal rate is $\dot R=\gamma I$. Because $\gamma$ is constant, its interior peak occurs when $I$ is maximal. At such a point,

$$
0=\dot I=I(\beta S-\gamma),
$$

and $I>0$, so

$$
S=\frac\gamma\beta.
$$
