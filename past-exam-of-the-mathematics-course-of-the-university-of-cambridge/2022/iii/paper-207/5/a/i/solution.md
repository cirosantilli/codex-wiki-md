↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Conditioning on $T_1=t$ and using [independent random variables](../../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) gives

$$
\mathbb P(T_1<T_2)
=\int_0^\infty
\mathbb P(T_2>t\mid T_1=t)f_1(t)\,dt
=\int_0^\infty f_1(t)F_2(t)\,dt,
$$

where the notation in the paper uses $F_2$ for the [survival function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md).
