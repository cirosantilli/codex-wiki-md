↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $T_i$ has [cumulative hazard function](../../../../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md) $\phi_iH_0(t)$,

$$
\mathbb P\{H_0(T_i)>u\}
=\mathbb P\{T_i>H_0^{-1}(u)\}
=e^{-\phi_i u}.
$$

**Thus the cumulative-hazard time change $U_i=H_0(T_i)$ has an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) of rate $\phi_i$.**
