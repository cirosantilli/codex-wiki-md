<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The area form $\sigma$ on $S^2$ is closed. For any closed two-form $B$ on a base, the [twisted cotangent symplectic form](../../../../../../twisted-cotangent-symplectic-form.md) $\omega+\pi^*B$ is closed and nondegenerate: pairing a putative kernel vector first with vertical vectors kills its horizontal component, and then pairing with horizontal vectors kills its vertical component. Thus both $\omega_+$ and $\omega_-$ are symplectic.

The cotangent bundle deformation-retracts onto its zero section. Their cohomology classes satisfy

$$
[\omega_+]-[\omega_-]=2\pi^*[\sigma]\ne0\in H^2(T^*S^2;\mathbb R).
$$

They are therefore not [strongly isotopic](../../../../../../strong-isotopy-of-symplectic-forms.md).

Let $f:S^2\to S^2$ be an orientation-reversing isometry. Then $f^*\sigma=-\sigma$, while its cotangent lift preserves the canonical form and satisfies $\pi\circ f_\#=f\circ\pi$. Consequently

$$
f_\#^*\omega_+=\omega+\pi^*f^*\sigma=\omega_-,
$$

so the two forms are [symplectomorphic](../../../../../../symplectomorphism.md).
