The [toric divisor class sequence](../../../../../../../toric-divisor-class-sequence.md) is

$$
M\longrightarrow\mathbb ZD_1\oplus\mathbb ZD_2\oplus\mathbb ZD_3
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Cl}(X)\longrightarrow0.
$$

The two characters give

$$
D_1-aD_3=0,
\qquad
D_2-bD_3=0.
$$

Thus

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(\mathbb P(1,a,b))\cong\mathbb Z,
$$

generated by $[D_3]$.
