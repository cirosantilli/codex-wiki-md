<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the rays $v_1=(1,0)$, $v_2=(0,1)$, and $v_3=(-a,-b)$, the determinants of consecutive pairs have absolute values

$$
1,\qquad a,\qquad b.
$$

By the [smoothness criterion for a toric variety](../../../../../../../smoothness-criterion-for-a-toric-variety.md), $\mathbb P(1,a,b)$ is smooth exactly when $a=b=1$.
