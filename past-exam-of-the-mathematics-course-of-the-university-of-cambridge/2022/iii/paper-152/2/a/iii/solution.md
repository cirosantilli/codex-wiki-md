<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An invariant Weil divisor is Cartier precisely when its local linear support functions are integral on every maximal cone. Applying this to the three cones shows that a class $n[D_3]$ is Cartier exactly when both $a$ and $b$ divide $n$. Since $\gcd(a,b)=1$, this means $ab\mid n$. Therefore

$$
\operatorname{Pic}(\mathbb P(1,a,b))\cong\mathbb Z,
$$

and its map to the class group identifies it with $ab\mathbb Z\subseteq\mathbb Z$. This is the [divisor class and Picard groups of a weighted projective plane](../../../../../../../divisor-class-and-picard-groups-of-a-weighted-projective-plane.md).
