<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every maximal cone, one endpoint of the interval of lattice points from part a realizes the corresponding Cartier datum. The toric basepoint-free criterion therefore makes $\mathcal O(kD_1)$ basepoint-free for $k\geq0$. Equivalently, part d identifies it with the pullback of the globally generated bundle $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(k)$.
