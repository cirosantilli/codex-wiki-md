<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $D=kD_1$, the [lattice polytope of a toric divisor](../../../../../../lattice-polytope-of-a-toric-divisor.md) consists of $(m_1,m_2)$ satisfying

$$
m_1\geq-k,\qquad m_2\geq0,\qquad-m_1+m_2\geq0,\qquad-m_2\geq0.
$$

Thus $m_2=0$ and $-k\leq m_1\leq0$. Its lattice points index a basis of global sections, so

$$
\dim\Gamma(\mathbb F_1,\mathcal O(kD_1))=
\begin{cases}
k+1,&k\geq0,\\
0,&k<0.
\end{cases}
$$
