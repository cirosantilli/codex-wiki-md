<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The divisor $D_1$ is a fiber of the ruling and has $D_1^2=0$. Hence $(kD_1)^2=0$ for every $k$. An ample divisor on a complete surface has positive self-intersection, so no $\mathcal O(kD_1)$ with $k\geq0$ is ample. These results are summarized by [multiples of a fiber on the first Hirzebruch surface](../../../../../../multiples-of-a-fiber-on-the-first-hirzebruch-surface.md).
