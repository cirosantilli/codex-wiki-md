<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Insert the ray generated by $w=(0,1)$ between $v_1=(1,0)$ and $v_2=(-1,3)$. Since

$$
\det(v_1,w)=1,
\qquad
\det(w,v_2)=1,
$$

the subdivided fan is smooth and gives a [toric resolution of singularities](../../../../../../../toric-resolution-of-singularities.md). The unique exceptional divisor is $D_w$. As

$$
v_1+v_2=(0,3)=3w,
$$

the [self-intersection formula for a toric surface divisor](../../../../../../../self-intersection-formula-for-a-toric-surface-divisor.md) gives $D_w^2=-3$.
