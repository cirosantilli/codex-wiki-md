↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The diagonal action of $\mu_3$ on $\mathbb A^2_{x,y}$ fixes precisely the monomials whose total degree is divisible by three. Its invariant ring is generated by

$$
x^3,\quad x^2y,\quad xy^2,\quad y^3.
$$

The assignment

$$
x^{3-j}y^j\longmapsto t_1^jt_2,
\qquad0\leq j\leq3,
$$

identifies this invariant ring with the ring in part i. Taking spectra gives the [diagonal cyclic quotient singularity of order three](../../../../../../../diagonal-cyclic-quotient-singularity-of-order-three.md)

$$
\boxed{X_\sigma\cong\mathbb A^2/\mu_3.}
$$
