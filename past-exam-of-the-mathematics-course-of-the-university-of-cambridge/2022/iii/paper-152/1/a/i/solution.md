↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $m=(u,v)\in M\cong\mathbb Z^2$, membership in the [dual cone](../../../../../../../dual-cone.md) is equivalent to

$$
u\geq0,
\qquad -u+3v\geq0.
$$

The [Hilbert basis of a rational cone](../../../../../../../hilbert-basis-of-a-rational-cone.md) is $(0,1),(1,1),(2,1),(3,1)$. Therefore the [coordinate ring of an affine toric variety](../../../../../../../coordinate-ring-of-an-affine-toric-variety.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb C[S_\sigma]
=\mathbb C[t_2,t_1t_2,t_1^2t_2,t_1^3t_2]
\subseteq\mathbb C[t_1^{\pm1},t_2^{\pm1}].}
$$
