<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The transverse force density is $f=-Ah_{xxxx}=\zeta h_t$. Its resultant is

$$
\int_0^Lf\,dx=-A[h_{xxx}]_0^L=0
$$

by the free-end shear conditions. To linear order, its torque about $x=0$ is

$$
\int_0^Lxf\,dx=-A[xh_{xxx}-h_{xx}]_0^L
=A[h_{xx}]_0^L=0,
$$

because both end curvatures equal the same imposed $\kappa_0$. Thus every decaying mode is force-free and torque-free, consistently leaving the translational and rotational zero modes unchanged.
