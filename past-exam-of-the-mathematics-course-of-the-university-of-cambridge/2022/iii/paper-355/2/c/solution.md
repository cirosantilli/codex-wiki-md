<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Force and torque freedom of the cell--helix pair give

$$
(A+A_0)U+B(\Omega+\omega)=0,
$$



$$
BU+(D+D_0)\Omega+D\omega=0.
$$

Writing

$$
\Delta=(A+A_0)(D+D_0)-B^2,
$$

solution of this linear system yields

$$
\boxed{U=-\frac{BD_0}{\Delta}\omega,
\qquad
\Omega=\frac{B^2-(A+A_0)D}{\Delta}\omega.}
$$

The body counter-rotates relative to the motor, and the swimming direction reverses with the helix handedness through $B$.
