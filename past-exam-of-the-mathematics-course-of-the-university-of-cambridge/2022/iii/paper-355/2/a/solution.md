<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [resistive-force theory](../../../../../../resistive-force-theory.md), the force per unit length is local and linear in the filament velocity relative to the fluid:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf f=-c_\parallel(\mathbf u\mathbin\cdot\mathbf t)\mathbf t
-c_\perp[\mathbf u-(\mathbf u\mathbin\cdot\mathbf t)\mathbf t].}
$$

The distinct parallel and perpendicular drag coefficients encode the anisotropic resistance of a slender filament. Neglecting nonlocal hydrodynamic interactions makes this a leading logarithmic approximation to [slender-body theory](../../../../../../slender-body-theory.md).
