<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The helix exerts on the fluid the torque

$$
N=BU+D(\Omega+\omega).
$$

Overall torque balance also gives $N=-D_0\Omega$. Hence

$$
\boxed{N=\frac{D_0[(A+A_0)D-B^2]}{(A+A_0)(D+D_0)-B^2}\omega.}
$$

For the usual choice $\omega>0$, this is positive when the resistance matrix is positive definite.
