<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an integer $k$ and a [congruence subgroup](../../../../../../congruence-subgroup.md) $\Gamma\leq SL_2(\mathbb Z)$, the space $M_k(\Gamma)$ consists of [holomorphic functions](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) $f:\mathfrak h\to\mathbb C$ satisfying

$$
(f|_k\gamma)(\tau)=(c\tau+d)^{-k}f(\gamma\tau)=f(\tau)
$$

for every $\gamma=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\Gamma$, and which are [holomorphic at every cusp](../../../../../../holomorphic-at-a-cusp.md). This is the space of [modular forms](../../../../../../modular-form.md) of weight $k$ and level $\Gamma$.
