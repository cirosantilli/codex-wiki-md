<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Set

$$
r=\tan\theta=\frac{\omega}{\sqrt{N^2-\omega^2}},
\qquad
s=\frac{H_0}{L},
$$

where $r$ is the magnitude of the [internal-wave ray](../../../../../../internal-wave-ray-tracing.md) slope and $s$ is the bottom slope. A rightward ray remains in the triangular basin precisely when its bottom reflection is [subcritical internal-wave reflection](../../../../../../subcritical-internal-wave-reflection.md), namely $r>s$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{NH_0}{\sqrt{L^2+H_0^2}}<\omega<N.}
$$

Conservation of frequency and of the component of the [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) tangent to the slope gives the [focusing power of internal-wave reflection](../../../../../../focusing-power-of-internal-wave-reflection.md)

$$
\boxed{\gamma=\frac{k_{\rm r}}{k_{\rm i}}
=\frac{\sin(\theta+\alpha)}{\sin(\theta-\alpha)}
=\frac{r+s}{r-s}
=\frac{L\omega+H_0\sqrt{N^2-\omega^2}}
{L\omega-H_0\sqrt{N^2-\omega^2}},}
$$

where $\tan\alpha=s$. In this range $\gamma>1$, so the reflected [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) is shorter by the factor $\gamma$. The reflected normal [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) is smaller by $\gamma^2$; conservation of normal [energy flux](../../../../../../energy-flux.md) therefore increases the wavelength-averaged [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) by

$$
\boxed{\overline E_{\rm r}=\gamma^2\overline E_{\rm i}.}
$$
