<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let the [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) make an angle $\theta$ above the horizontal. A convenient right-handed choice of rotated unit vectors is

$$
\widehat{\boldsymbol\xi}=(\sin\theta,-\cos\theta),
\qquad
\widehat{\boldsymbol\zeta}=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta).
$$

The first vector is parallel to the [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md), the second is parallel to the [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md), and their perpendicularity is the defining geometry of an [internal-wave phase and group velocity](../../../../../../internal-wave-phase-and-group-velocity.md). Thus

$$
\partial_x=\sin\theta\,\partial_\xi+\cos\theta\,\partial_\zeta,
\qquad
\nabla^2=\partial_\xi^2+\partial_\zeta^2.
$$

Writing $\Delta_{\xi\zeta}=\partial_\xi^2+\partial_\zeta^2$, the equation becomes

$$
\boxed{\left[(\partial_t-\nu\Delta_{\xi\zeta})(\partial_t-\kappa\Delta_{\xi\zeta})\Delta_{\xi\zeta}
+N^2(\sin\theta\,\partial_\xi+\cos\theta\,\partial_\zeta)^2\right]\psi=0.}
$$

For an inviscid [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) proportional to $e^{i(k\xi-\omega t)}$, its [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) is $\omega=N\sin\theta$.
