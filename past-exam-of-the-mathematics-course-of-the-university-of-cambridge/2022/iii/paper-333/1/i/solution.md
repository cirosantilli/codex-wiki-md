<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first two equations are horizontal momentum balances: local acceleration plus the [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md) equals the pressure-gradient force. The third is linearized [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md): convergence raises the free surface, represented by $\phi$, while divergence lowers it. The constant $c$ is the long-wave gravity-wave speed.
