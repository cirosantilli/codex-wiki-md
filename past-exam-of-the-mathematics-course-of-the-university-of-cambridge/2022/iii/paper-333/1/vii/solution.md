<h1 id="1/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

Form the $x$-momentum equation plus $1/c$ times continuity, multiply by $e^{\mp fy/c}$, and integrate across the channel. Integration by parts makes the $v_y$ term cancel the Coriolis term, and $v=0$ removes the endpoint contribution. The result is the pair of [Kelvin-wave channel invariants](../../../../../../kelvin-wave-channel-invariant.md)

$$
\boxed{
\left(\frac\partial{\partial t}\pm\frac1c\frac\partial{\partial x}\right)
\int_0^Le^{\mp fy/c}\left(u+\frac\phi c\right)dy=0.}
$$
