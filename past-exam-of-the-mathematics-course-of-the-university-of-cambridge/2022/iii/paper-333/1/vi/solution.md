<h1 id="1/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Set $v=0$. For $F_+(x+ct)$ the equations require $\widetilde\phi_+=-c\widetilde u_+$ and $\widetilde u_+'=(f/c)\widetilde u_+$; for $F_-(x-ct)$ they require $\widetilde\phi_-=c\widetilde u_-$ and $\widetilde u_-'=-(f/c)\widetilde u_-$. Hence the channel supports the [Kelvin waves](../../../../../../kelvin-wave.md)

$$
\boxed{
\widetilde u_\pm=C_\pm e^{\pm fy/c},
\qquad
\widetilde\phi_\pm=\mp c\widetilde u_\pm.}
$$

Each wave propagates along one wall and is exponentially trapped toward that wall on the deformation scale $c/|f|$.
