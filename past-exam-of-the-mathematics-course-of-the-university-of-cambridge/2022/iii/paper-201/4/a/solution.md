<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $t\leq s$, then $\mathbb E(X_t\mid\mathcal F_s)=X_t$. If $s<t\leq1$, [Gaussian conditional expectation](../../../../../../gaussian-conditional-expectation.md) for the [Brownian bridge](../../../../../../brownian-bridge.md) between $(s,X_s)$ and $(1,X_1)$ gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(X_t\mid\mathcal F_s)
=X_s+\frac{t-s}{1-s}(X_1-X_s).}
$$
