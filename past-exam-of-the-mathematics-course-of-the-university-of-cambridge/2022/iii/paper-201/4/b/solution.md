<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $X_1-X_s$ is centered Gaussian with variance $1-s$,

$$
\mathbb E\int_0^1\frac{|X_1-X_s|}{1-s}\,ds
=C\int_0^1(1-s)^{-1/2}\,ds<\infty.
$$

[Fubini's theorem](../../../../../../fubini-s-theorem.md) therefore shows that the defining integral for $A_1$ is absolutely finite almost surely.
