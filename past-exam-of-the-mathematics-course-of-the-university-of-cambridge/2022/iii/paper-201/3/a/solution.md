[Prokhorov's theorem](../../../../../../prokhorov-s-theorem.md) says that a sequence of Borel probability measures on $\mathbb R$ is relatively compact for [weak convergence of probability measures](../../../../../../weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) exactly when it is tight: for every $\epsilon>0$, some compact $K$ satisfies $\sup_n\mu_n(\mathbb R\setminus K)<\epsilon$.
