↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Yes.** Quotient only the first coordinate modulo $\mathbb Z$. The resulting process lives on $\mathbb T\times\mathbb R^2$ and the image of $S_1$ is the radius-$\epsilon$ ball about $(0,0,0)$. The two noncompact coordinates form [planar Brownian motion](../../../../../../planar-brownian-motion.md), which is recurrent; during its infinitely many returns to a smaller disc, the independent circle coordinate has a fixed positive chance of lying in the required interval. The [Strong Markov property](../../../../../../strong-markov-property.md) then shows that the target ball is visited infinitely often. Lifting back proves that $S_1$ is hit at unbounded times.
