<h1 id="4/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

For the magnetic theory,

$$
b_0^{\rm mag}=3(\widetilde N_c+1)-N_f
=2N_f-3(N_c+1).
$$

It ceases to be asymptotically free when

$$
\boxed{N_f\le\frac32(N_c+1)}.
$$

This is precisely the lower edge of the electric conformal window. Inside the window both descriptions flow to the same interacting [infrared fixed point](../../../../../../infrared-fixed-point.md); below it the magnetic variables provide a weakly coupled infrared description.
