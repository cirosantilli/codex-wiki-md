<h1 id="4/viii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Viii](../viii.md)

The electric quarks give $2N_c$ copies of the $SU(2N_f)$ fundamental, so

$$
\mathcal A_{\rm el}[SU(2N_f)^3]=2N_c.
$$

In the magnetic theory the $2\widetilde N_c$ color components of $q$ transform as flavor antifundamentals and contribute $-2\widetilde N_c$. The singlet $M^{ij}$ is the [two-index antisymmetric representation](../../../../../../two-index-antisymmetric-representation.md) of $SU(2N_f)$, whose [cubic anomaly coefficient](../../../../../../cubic-anomaly-coefficient.md) is $2N_f-4$. Hence

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A_{\rm mag}[SU(2N_f)^3]
&=-2(N_f-N_c-2)+(2N_f-4)\\
&=\boxed{2N_c}
=\mathcal A_{\rm el}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is a direct anomaly check of [Seiberg duality](../../../../../../seiberg-duality.md).
