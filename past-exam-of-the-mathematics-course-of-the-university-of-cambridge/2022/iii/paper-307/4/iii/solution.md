<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In a four-dimensional supersymmetric conformal field theory, a [chiral primary operator in four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry](../../../../../../chiral-primary-operator-in-four-dimensional-n-1-supersymmetry.md) obeys $\Delta=3R/2$. The meson has [R-charge](../../../../../../r-charge.md) $R[M]=2R[\Phi]$, hence

$$
\Delta(M)=3R[\Phi]=3\left(1-\frac{N_c+1}{N_f}\right).
$$

At $N_f=3(N_c+1)$ this gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta(M)=2}.
$$
