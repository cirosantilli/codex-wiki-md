<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The fermion in a chiral multiplet has [R-charge](../../../../../../r-charge.md) $R[\Phi]-1$, while the gluino has charge one. Cancellation of the $Sp(N_c)^2U(1)_R$ anomaly gives

$$
I(\mathrm{adj})+2N_fI(\square)(R[\Phi]-1)=0.
$$

Using $I(\mathrm{adj})=2(N_c+1)$ and $I(\square)=1$ yields

$$
\boxed{R[\Phi]=1-\frac{N_c+1}{N_f}
=\frac{N_f-N_c-1}{N_f}}.
$$
