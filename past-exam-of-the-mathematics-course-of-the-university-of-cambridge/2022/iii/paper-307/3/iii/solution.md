<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Only the $\psi$ fields transform under flavor $SU(p)$; their gauge-color index gives $N$ copies of its fundamental. With $p=N-4$, the ultraviolet ['t Hooft anomalies](../../../../../../t-hooft-anomaly.md) are

$$
\begin{aligned}
SU(p)^3 &: &&N,\\
SU(p)^2U(1)&:&&Nq_\psi=-N(N-2),\\
U(1)^3&:&&\frac{N(N-1)}2p^3+pN[-(N-2)]^3,\\
U(1)\text{-gravity}^2&:&&\frac{N(N-1)}2p+pN[-(N-2)].
\end{aligned}
$$

The last two expressions simplify to

$$
-\frac12p(p+1)N^3,
\qquad
-\frac12p(p+1)N,
$$

respectively.
