<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalize the [cubic anomaly coefficient](../../../../../../cubic-anomaly-coefficient.md) of the $SU(N)$ fundamental to $+1$. The [two-index antisymmetric representation](../../../../../../two-index-antisymmetric-representation.md) has anomaly coefficient $N-4$, while an antifundamental contributes $-1$. Cancellation of the $SU(N)^3$ [gauge anomaly](../../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) requires

$$
(N-4)-p=0,
\qquad
\boxed{p=N-4}.
$$
