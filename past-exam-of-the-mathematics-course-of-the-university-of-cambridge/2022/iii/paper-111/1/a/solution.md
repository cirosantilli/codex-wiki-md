The [deletion condition for involutory generators](../../../../../../deletion-condition-for-involutory-generators.md) says that whenever a word

$$
s_1s_2\cdots s_m,qquad s_i\in S,
$$

is not reduced, there are indices $i<j$ such that deleting $s_i$ and $s_j$ leaves a word representing the same group element.

The [exchange condition for a Coxeter group](../../../../../../exchange-condition-for-a-coxeter-group.md) says that if $w=s_1\cdots s_m$ is reduced and $s\in S$ satisfies $\ell_S(sw)<\ell_S(w)$, then

$$
sw=s_1\cdots\widehat{s_i}\cdots s_m
$$

for some $i$. There is an equivalent right-handed form for $\ell_S(ws)<\ell_S(w)$.
