<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write the basic construction as

$$
U(W,K)=(W\times K)/\sim,
$$

where $(w,x)\sim(w',x)$ exactly when $w^{-1}w'$ belongs to the subgroup generated by the mirrors containing $x$. Let $v_T$ be the vertex of $K$ corresponding to a [spherical subset of a Coxeter system](../../../../../../spherical-subset-of-a-coxeter-system.md) $T$. Its stabilizer is $W_T$. Therefore

$$
\operatorname{Stab}_W([w,v_T])
=wW_Tw^{-1}.
$$

Every conjugate of every spherical standard parabolic subgroup consequently occurs as a vertex stabilizer.
