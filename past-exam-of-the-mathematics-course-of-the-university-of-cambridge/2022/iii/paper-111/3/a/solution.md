<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Restrict the Coxeter matrix of $(W,S)$ to $T\times T$ and let $W'$ be the Coxeter group defined by that matrix. Sending its generators to the corresponding elements of $T$ gives a surjection $W'\to W_T$. The [Geometric representation of a Coxeter group](../../../../../../geometric-representation-of-a-coxeter-group.md) for $W'$ is the restriction of the geometric representation for $W$ to the span of the simple roots indexed by $T$. Faithfulness of the geometric representation makes the map injective. Hence

$$
\boxed{(W_T,T)\text{ is a Coxeter system}.}
$$
