<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Part (a) gives a reduced $T$-word for $w$. It is also reduced as an $S$-word, since a shorter $S$-word would contradict the length function obtained from the restricted geometric representation. By [Matsumoto theorem](../../../../../../matsumoto-theorem.md), this expression and the given reduced expression $s_1\cdots s_k$ differ by braid moves. Every braid move starting with letters in $T$ replaces them by the same two letters in the opposite alternating order, so it never introduces a generator outside $T$. Therefore every $s_i$ lies in $T$.
