<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In old covariant quantization, an open-string [physical string state](../../../../../../physical-string-state.md) obeys

$$
(L_0-1)|\psi\rangle=0,
\qquad
L_n|\psi\rangle=0\quad(n>0),
$$

with analogous left- and right-moving conditions for a closed string. A [spurious string state](../../../../../../spurious-string-state.md) is a [Virasoro descendant](../../../../../../virasoro-descendant.md) orthogonal to every physical state. A [null string state](../../../../../../null-string-state.md) is both physical and spurious; it has zero norm and zero inner product with every physical state, so quotienting by null states removes gauge redundancy without removing observable states.
