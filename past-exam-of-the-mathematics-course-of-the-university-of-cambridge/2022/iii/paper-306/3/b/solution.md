<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At closed-string level $N=\widetilde N$, the [bosonic string mass spectrum](../../../../../../bosonic-string-mass-spectrum.md) is

$$
M^2=\frac4{\alpha'}(N-1).
$$

The state on the leading [Regge trajectory](../../../../../../regge-trajectory.md) uses only level-one oscillators and has maximal spin $J=N+\widetilde N=2N$. Eliminating $N$ gives

$$
\boxed{J=2+\frac{\alpha'M^2}{2}}.
$$

Because $N$ is unbounded, the spectrum contains infinitely many particles of increasing mass and spin. The intercept $J=2$ at $M^2=0$ includes the graviton.
