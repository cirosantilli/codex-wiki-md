<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) of the gauge-fixed [Polyakov action](../../../../../../polyakov-action.md) is the two-dimensional wave equation

$$
(\partial_\tau^2-\partial_\sigma^2)X^\mu=0.
$$

Its general solution is a sum of left- and right-moving functions. Periodicity in $\sigma$ and a Fourier expansion give the [closed-string mode expansion](../../../../../../closed-string-mode-expansion.md)

$$
X^\mu=x^\mu+\alpha'p^\mu\tau
+i\sqrt{\frac{\alpha'}2}\sum_{r\ne0}\frac1r
\left(\alpha_r^\mu e^{-ir(\tau-\sigma)}
+\widetilde\alpha_r^\mu e^{-ir(\tau+\sigma)}\right).
$$

The zero modes satisfy $\alpha_0^\mu=\widetilde\alpha_0^\mu=\sqrt{\alpha'/2},p^\mu$. Here $x^\mu$ and $p^\mu$ are the center-of-mass position and momentum, while the nonzero [string oscillators](../../../../../../string-oscillator.md) describe shape excitations.
