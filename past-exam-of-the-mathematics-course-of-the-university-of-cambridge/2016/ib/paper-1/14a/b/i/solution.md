<h1 id="14a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply the equation by $x^2$ and combine the derivative terms:

$$
(x^3y')'+(1+\lambda)xy=0.
$$

Thus **the self-adjoint form** is

$$
\boxed{-(x^3y')'-xy=\lambda xy,\qquad p=x^3,\quad q=-x,\quad w=x.}
$$

The [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) remain at $1$ and $e$, and the weight $w=x$ is positive on this interval, as required for the [Sturm-Liouville problem](../../../../../../../sturm-liouville-problem.md).
