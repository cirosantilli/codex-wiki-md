↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A sequence of real [orthogonal polynomials](../../../../../../orthogonal-polynomial.md) consists of real polynomials $p_n$ with [degree of a polynomial](../../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $n$ and

$$
\langle p_m,p_n\rangle=0\quad(m\ne n),\qquad \langle p_n,p_n\rangle>0.
$$

Orthogonality is relative to the specified [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md), rather than to the ordinary coefficients. A [monic polynomial](../../../../../../monic-polynomial.md) has leading coefficient one, so a [monic orthogonal polynomial](../../../../../../monic-orthogonal-polynomial.md) has the form $p_n(x)=x^n+\text{terms of lower degree}$. In particular, the monic degree-zero polynomial is $p_0=1$.
