↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**A suitable [Poincare disc automorphism](../../../../../../poincare-disc-automorphism.md)** is the [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md)

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\frac{z-a}{1-\overline a z}.}
$$

It sends $a$ to zero and has inverse $(w+a)/(1+\overline a w)$. To verify the [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md) directly, note that

$$
1-|f(z)|^2=\frac{(1-|a|^2)(1-|z|^2)}{|1-\overline a z|^2},\qquad |f'(z)|=\frac{1-|a|^2}{|1-\overline a z|^2}.
$$

These identities show both that $f$ maps the [unit disc](../../../../../../unit-disc.md) onto itself and that $2|df|/(1-|f|^2)=2|dz|/(1-|z|^2)$, preserving the [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md).
