<h1 id="3f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The radial segment is a [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) of the [Poincare disc model](../../../../../../poincare-disk-model.md). Its [hyperbolic length](../../../../../../hyperbolic-length-in-the-poincare-disc.md) is

$$
\boxed{d_D(0,re^{i\theta})=\int_0^r\frac{2\,dt}{1-t^2}=\log\frac{1+r}{1-r}=2\operatorname{artanh}r.}
$$

One can also verify that it minimizes [length of a curve](../../../../../../length-of-a-curve.md): along any piecewise smooth curve from zero to radius $r$, the Euclidean speed is at least the absolute radial speed. Integrating $2|dr|/(1-r^2)$ is at least the absolute change in $\log((1+r)/(1-r))$, with equality on the radial segment. Thus the answer is independent of $\theta$.
