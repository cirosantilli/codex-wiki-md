The [Poincare disc model](../../../../../../poincare-disk-model.md) is the open [unit disc](../../../../../../unit-disc.md) $D=\{z=x+iy:|z|<1\}$ equipped with the [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md)

$$
\boxed{ds^2=\frac{4(dx^2+dy^2)}{(1-x^2-y^2)^2}=\frac{4|dz|^2}{(1-|z|^2)^2}.}
$$

This normalization gives [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) $-1$. In particular, a Euclidean curve has [hyperbolic length](../../../../../../hyperbolic-length-in-the-poincare-disc.md) $\int 2|dz|/(1-|z|^2)$; the boundary circle is infinitely far away in [hyperbolic distance](../../../../../../hyperbolic-distance.md).
