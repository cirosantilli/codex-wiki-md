↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) $A,B$ and scalars $r,s\in\mathbb F$, the definition of the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(rA+sB)=\sum_i(ra_{ii}+sb_{ii})=r\operatorname{Tr}(A)+s\operatorname{Tr}(B).
$$

Hence **the trace is a linear functional** on the [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of $n\times n$ [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md).
