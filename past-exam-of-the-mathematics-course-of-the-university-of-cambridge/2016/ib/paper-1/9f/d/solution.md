<h1 id="9f/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Every [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) $[A,B]=AB-BA$ has zero [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md), by the cyclic identity. Hence its linear span $W$ is contained in $\ker\operatorname{Tr}$. For the reverse inclusion, the [matrix units](../../../../../../matrix-unit.md) satisfy

$$
E_{ij}=[E_{ii},E_{ij}]\quad(i\ne j),\qquad E_{ii}-E_{jj}=[E_{ij},E_{ji}].
$$

The off-diagonal [matrix units](../../../../../../matrix-unit.md) and the differences $E_{ii}-E_{nn}$ for $i<n$ span all [traceless matrices](../../../../../../traceless-matrix.md): a traceless diagonal part is $\sum_{i<n}a_{ii}(E_{ii}-E_{nn})$. Thus every element of the [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is a linear combination of [commutators](../../../../../../commutator.md), proving

$$
\boxed{W=\ker\operatorname{Tr}.}
$$

This establishes that [traceless matrices are spanned by commutators](../../../../../../traceless-matrices-are-spanned-by-commutators.md). For $n=1$, both [vector subspaces](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) are zero. Notice that the argument needs a span of [commutators](../../../../../../commutator.md); it does not assume that every such sum has already been written as a single commutator.
