↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The definition of [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(AB)=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}b_{ji}=\sum_{j,i}b_{ji}a_{ij}=\operatorname{Tr}(BA).
$$

The middle equality uses commutativity of scalar multiplication in $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, together with interchange of finite sums. Thus **the trace is unchanged by cyclically moving the two factors**.
