<h1 id="4c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a twice differentiable function, [convexity](../../../../../../convex-function.md) on $\mathbb R^2$ is equivalent to its [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) being [positive semidefinite](../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) everywhere. Here the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is constant:

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}4&\alpha\\\alpha&2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its diagonal entries are positive, so [positive semidefiniteness](../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) is equivalent to $\det H=8-\alpha^2\geq0$. **The required parameter range is**

$$
\boxed{-2\sqrt2\leq\alpha\leq2\sqrt2.}
$$

The endpoints give [convex functions](../../../../../../convex-function.md) with one flat direction; strict inequalities give a [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) defining a [positive-definite quadratic form](../../../../../../positive-definite-quadratic-form.md) and a [strictly convex function](../../../../../../strictly-convex-function.md).
