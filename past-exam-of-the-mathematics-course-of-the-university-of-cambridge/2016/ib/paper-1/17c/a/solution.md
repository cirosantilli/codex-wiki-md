<h1 id="17c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf u$ be the [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md). In component notation, contracting two [Levi-Civita symbols](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) gives

$$
(\mathbf u\times\boldsymbol\omega)_i=\varepsilon_{ijk}u_j\varepsilon_{klm}\partial_lu_m=u_j\partial_i u_j-u_j\partial_j u_i.
$$

The first term is $\partial_i(|\mathbf u|^2/2)$ and the second is the $i$th component of the [convective acceleration](../../../../../../convective-acceleration.md). Rearranging proves **the velocity identity**

$$
\boxed{(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=\nabla\left(\tfrac12|\mathbf u|^2\right)-\mathbf u\times\boldsymbol\omega.}
$$
