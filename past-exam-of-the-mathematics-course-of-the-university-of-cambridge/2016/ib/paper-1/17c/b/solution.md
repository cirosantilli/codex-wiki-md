<h1 id="17c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Parametrize a [streamline](../../../../../../streamline.md) by $\mathbf r(s)$ with $d\mathbf r/ds=\mathbf u(\mathbf r(s))$. The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac d{ds}H(\mathbf r(s))=\nabla H\cdot\frac{d\mathbf r}{ds}=\mathbf u\cdot\nabla H=0.
$$

Therefore **$H$ is constant along every streamline**. A different regular parametrization merely multiplies this derivative by a scalar and gives the same conclusion. For time-dependent flow, the claim concerns streamlines at a fixed time, rather than [Lagrangian trajectories](../../../../../../lagrangian-trajectory.md).
