<h1 id="10e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The evaluation map $\varphi:\mathbb R[x]\to\mathbb C$, $p\mapsto p(i)$, is a [ring homomorphism](../../../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) and is onto because $a+bx$ maps to any $a+bi$. By [polynomial division](../../../../../../polynomial-division.md), write

$$
p(x)=(x^2+1)q(x)+a+bx.
$$

Then $p(i)=0$ precisely when $a=b=0$. Thus $\ker\varphi=(x^2+1)$, and the [first isomorphism theorem](../../../../../../first-isomorphism-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb R[x]/(x^2+1)\cong\mathbb C.}
$$

Since the [quotient ring](../../../../../../quotient-ring.md) is a [field](../../../../../../field.md), **$(x^2+1)$ is a maximal ideal**.
