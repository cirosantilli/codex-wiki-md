<h1 id="15b/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) acquires the phase $e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}$ under [unitary time evolution](../../../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md). Consequently **at the requested time $t=1$**,

$$
\boxed{\psi(x,1)=\frac1{\sqrt{10}}\left(3e^{-iE_1/\hbar}\phi_1(x)-e^{-i9E_1/\hbar}\phi_3(x)\right),\qquad 0<x<a,}
$$

and the [wavefunction](../../../../../../../wave-function.md) is zero outside the well. Equivalently, in elementary functions,

$$
\psi(x,1)=\frac1{\sqrt{5a}}\left(3e^{-i\pi^2\hbar/(2ma^2)}\sin\frac{\pi x}{a}-e^{-i9\pi^2\hbar/(2ma^2)}\sin\frac{3\pi x}{a}\right).
$$

The distinct phases retain their relative sign from the initial expansion; an overall [global phase](../../../../../../../global-phase.md) would not change measurement probabilities.
