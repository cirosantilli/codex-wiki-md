<h1 id="11g/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Given $\varepsilon>0$, the [Cauchy sequence](../../../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md) property gives $N$ with $d(x_m,x_n)<\varepsilon/2$ for all $m,n\geq N$. Fixing $m\geq N$ and letting $n\to\infty$ yields $0\leq d_m\leq\varepsilon/2<\varepsilon$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{d_m\longrightarrow0.}
$$
