↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Apply the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) with a third point $x_k$:

$$
d(x_m,x_k)-d(x_n,x_k)\leq d(x_m,x_n)\leq d(x_m,x_k)+d(x_n,x_k).
$$

Letting $k\to\infty$ gives the desired bounds. Interchanging $m,n$ also gives the sharper symmetric lower bound:

$$
\boxed{|d_m-d_n|\leq d(x_m,x_n)\leq d_m+d_n.}
$$
