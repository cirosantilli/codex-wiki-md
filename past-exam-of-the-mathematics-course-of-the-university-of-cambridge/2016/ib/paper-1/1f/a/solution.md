<h1 id="1f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of the given [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is $(t-1)(t-2)^2$. Solving the two [eigenvalue equations](../../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) gives

$$
E_1=\operatorname{span}\{(3,-1,3)^T\},\qquad E_2=\operatorname{span}\{(2,1,0)^T,(2,0,1)^T\}.
$$

For example, multiplying the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) by these three [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) gives respectively $1$, $2$ and $2$ times the original [vector](../../../../../../vector.md). The [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md): the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) with these columns has nonzero [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $-1$. Thus **an eigenbasis and its diagonal matrix** are

$$
\boxed{\mathcal B=((3,-1,3)^T,(2,1,0)^T,(2,0,1)^T),\qquad [\alpha]_{\mathcal B}=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,2).}
$$
