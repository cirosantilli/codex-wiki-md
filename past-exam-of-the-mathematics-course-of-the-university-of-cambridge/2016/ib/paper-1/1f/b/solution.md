<h1 id="1f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $J_r(\lambda)$ for a size-$r$ [Jordan block](../../../../../../jordan-block.md). The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) fixes the total dimensions at $2$ and $-7$, while the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) fixes the largest [Jordan block](../../../../../../jordan-block.md) at each [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md). Consequently, the blocks for $2$ have total size four, largest size two, and at least one size-two block. The blocks for $-7$ both have size one. **The complete list, up to similarity, is**

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{diag}(J_2(2),J_2(2),-7,-7),\qquad \operatorname{diag}(J_2(2),2,2,-7,-7),\quad J_2(2)=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

These [Jordan normal forms](../../../../../../jordan-normal-form.md) are inequivalent because their $2$-[eigenspaces](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) have different dimensions, two and three.
