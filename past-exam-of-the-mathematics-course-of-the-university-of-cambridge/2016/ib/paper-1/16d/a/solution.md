<h1 id="16d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [electrostatics](../../../../../../electrostatics.md), the relevant [Maxwell equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations.md) reduce to $\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\rho/\varepsilon_0$ and $\nabla\times\mathbf E=0$. For a volume $V$ with outward unit normal $\mathbf n$, integrate the first equation and apply the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md):

$$
\boxed{\oint_{\partial V}\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=\int_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf E\,dV=\frac1{\varepsilon_0}\int_V\rho\,dV=\frac{Q_V}{\varepsilon_0}.}
$$

This is **the integral form of Gauss's law**, with $Q_V$ the enclosed [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge.md). The zero [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) is the accompanying condition allowing an [electrostatic potential](../../../../../../electric-potential.md) locally, and globally when the domain permits one.
