<h1 id="16d/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Differentiating the [image charge](../../../../../../image-charge.md) potential gives

$$
E_x=\frac{\mu}{2\pi\varepsilon_0}\left(\frac{x}{x^2+y^2}-\frac{x-2d}{(x-2d)^2+y^2}\right).
$$

At the conducting plane, $E_x(d^-)=\mu d/[\pi\varepsilon_0(d^2+y^2)]$. With outward conductor normal $-\mathbf e_x$, the [surface charge density](../../../../../../surface-charge-density.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\sigma(y,z)=-\varepsilon_0 E_x(d^-,y,z)=-\frac{\mu d}{\pi(d^2+y^2)}.}
$$

It is independent of $z$ by translational symmetry. As a check, integrating across the plane gives $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\sigma(y,z)\,dy=-\mu$, the total induced charge per unit length, opposite to the real [line charge](../../../../../../line-charge.md).
