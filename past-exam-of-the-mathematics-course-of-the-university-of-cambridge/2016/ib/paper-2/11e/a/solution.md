<h1 id="11e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $|G|=p^r m$ with $p$ prime and $p\nmid m$. The [Sylow theorems](../../../../../../sylow-theorems.md) assert that a subgroup of order $p^r$ exists; every [p-group](../../../../../../p-group.md) subgroup is contained in a conjugate of a [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md); all [Sylow subgroups](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) are conjugate; and their number $n_p$ satisfies $n_p\equiv1\pmod p$ and $n_p\mid m$.

For the containment and conjugacy assertions, fix a [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) $P$ and let any [p-group](../../../../../../p-group.md) subgroup $Q$ act by left multiplication on the $m$ left [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md) $G/P$. By the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md), every orbit has size a power of $p$. Since $p\nmid m$, at least one orbit has size one. If $gP$ is such a fixed point, then $qgP=gP$ for all $q\in Q$, equivalently $g^{-1}Qg\subseteq P$. Thus $Q\subseteq gPg^{-1}$. If $Q$ too has order $p^r$, this inclusion is equality, proving conjugacy. This is [Sylow containment from a coset fixed point](../../../../../../sylow-containment-from-a-coset-fixed-point.md).
