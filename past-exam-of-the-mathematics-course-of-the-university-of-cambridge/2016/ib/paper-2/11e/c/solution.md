<h1 id="11e/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [general linear group](../../../../../../general-linear-group.md) over $\mathbb F_{31}$, choose a nonzero first column in $31^2-1$ ways and a second column outside its span in $31^2-31$ ways. Thus

$$
|\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_{31})|=(31^2-1)(31^2-31)=2^7\,3^2\,5^2\,31.
$$

Since $5^3=125\equiv1\pmod{31}$ and $5\ne1$, the residue $5$ has order three. The nine [diagonal matrices](../../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md) $\operatorname{diag}(5^i,5^j)$, with $0\le i,j<3$, form a subgroup isomorphic to $C_3\times C_3$. It is an [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md) and a [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) for $p=3$. Every other [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) for $p=3$ is conjugate to it by the [Sylow theorems](../../../../../../sylow-theorems.md), hence is also [Abelian](../../../../../../abelian-group.md).
