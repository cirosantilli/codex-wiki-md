<h1 id="3g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Differentiability](../../../../../../differentiability.md) here is understood in the sense of the [Fréchet derivative](../../../../../../frechet-derivative.md): there is a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) $L:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ such that

$$
\boxed{f(x+h)=f(x)+Lh+r(h),\qquad \frac{\|r(h)\|}{\|h\|}\longrightarrow0\quad(h\to0).}
$$

The map $L=Df(x)$ is the [Fréchet derivative](../../../../../../frechet-derivative.md). In finite dimensions a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) is bounded, so $\|Lh\|\le C\|h\|$ for some $C$. The defining estimate therefore implies

$$
\|f(x+h)-f(x)\|\le C\|h\|+o(\|h\|)\longrightarrow0.
$$

This is precisely [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $x$: **differentiability implies continuity.**
