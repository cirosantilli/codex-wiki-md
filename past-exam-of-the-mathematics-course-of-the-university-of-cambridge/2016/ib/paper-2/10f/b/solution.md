<h1 id="10f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Right multiplication by $B^*$ acts separately on the rows. After transposing each row its matrix is $(B^*)^T=\overline B$, so its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is $(\overline{\det B})^n$. Composing this [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) with left multiplication and using part (a) gives

$$
\boxed{\det M_B=(\det B)^n(\overline{\det B})^n=|\det B|^{2n}.}
$$

Here the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is taken on the $n^2$-dimensional complex [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of matrices.
