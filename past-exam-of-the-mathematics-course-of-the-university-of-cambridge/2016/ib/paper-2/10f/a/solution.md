<h1 id="10f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**Group the matrix entries by columns.** Each column of $BA$ is $B$ times the corresponding column of $A$. In the column-ordered [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of [matrix units](../../../../../../matrix-unit.md), the [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) $L_B$ consequently has a [block diagonal matrix](../../../../../../block-diagonal-matrix.md) with $n$ copies of $B$. The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of a [block diagonal matrix](../../../../../../block-diagonal-matrix.md) is the product of its block [determinants](../../../../../../determinant.md), giving

$$
\boxed{\det L_B=(\det B)^n.}
$$

This argument does not require $B$ to be an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md).
