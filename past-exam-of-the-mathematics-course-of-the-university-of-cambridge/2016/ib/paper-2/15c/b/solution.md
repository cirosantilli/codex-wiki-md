<h1 id="15c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Vary $y$ by $y+\varepsilon\eta$, where the clamped endpoint conditions require $\eta=\eta'=0$ at both endpoints. The [first variation](../../../../../../first-variation.md) of the quadratic [elastic energy](../../../../../../elastic-energy.md) is

$$
\delta E_y=\int_0^L\bigl(Ay''\eta''-Fy'\eta'\bigr)\,dx
=\int_0^L\bigl((Ay'')''+Fy''\bigr)\eta\,dx.
$$

Both integrations by parts have zero boundary terms. Since the variation is arbitrary in the interior, the [fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations](../../../../../../fundamental-lemma-of-the-calculus-of-variations.md) gives the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md). Varying $z$ independently gives the second equation:

$$
\boxed{(Ay'')''+Fy''=0,\qquad(Bz'')''+Fz''=0.}
$$

The clamped endpoint values accompany these equations. **If $A$ or $B$ depends on $x$, it must remain inside the derivatives.**
