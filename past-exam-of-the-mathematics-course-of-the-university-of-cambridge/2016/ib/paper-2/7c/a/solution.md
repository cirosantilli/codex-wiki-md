<h1 id="7c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For this steady [unidirectional flow](../../../../../../unidirectional-flow.md), the convective acceleration $(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u$ vanishes because $u$ depends only on $y$. The axial [Navier-Stokes equation](../../../../../../navier-stokes-equation.md) reduces to $0=-p_x+\mu u''$, with $p_x=-G$. The [no-slip boundary conditions](../../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) therefore give

$$
\boxed{\mu u''(y)=-G,\qquad u(0)=0,\quad u(h)=U.}
$$

This is a superposition of [Couette flow](../../../../../../couette-flow.md) and pressure-driven [Poiseuille flow](../../../../../../hagen-poiseuille-equation.md).
