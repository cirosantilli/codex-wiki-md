<h1 id="7c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Integrate the axial [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) twice: $u=-Gy^2/(2\mu)+C_1y+C_2$. The [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) at zero gives $C_2=0$, and the one at $h$ gives $C_1=U/h+Gh/(2\mu)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{u(y)=\frac{U}{h}y+\frac{G}{2\mu}y(h-y).}
$$

The first term is [Couette flow](../../../../../../couette-flow.md); the second is [Poiseuille flow](../../../../../../hagen-poiseuille-equation.md) driven in positive $x$ when $G>0$. Their addition is valid because the reduced [Stokes equation](../../../../../../stokes-equation.md) is linear.
