<h1 id="7c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The bottom-wall [shear stress](../../../../../../shear-stress.md) vanishes exactly when $u'(0)=0$. Differentiating the [velocity profile](../../../../../../velocity-profile.md) gives

$$
u'(0)=\frac Uh+\frac{Gh}{2\mu},\qquad \boxed{U=-\frac{Gh^2}{2\mu}.}
$$

With this value the [velocity profile](../../../../../../velocity-profile.md) becomes $u(y)=-Gy^2/(2\mu)$, so the [volume flux](../../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit transverse width is

$$
\boxed{q=\int_0^h u(y)\,dy=-\frac{Gh^3}{6\mu}.}
$$

For $G>0$, the plate must move against the pressure-driven direction, and the resulting signed [volume flux](../../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) is negative. **Zero bottom-wall shear does not mean zero flow.**
