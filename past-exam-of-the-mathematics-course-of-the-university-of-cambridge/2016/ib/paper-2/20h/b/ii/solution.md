<h1 id="20h/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $h$ be the probability of ever hitting $c$ starting at $a$. A [first-step analysis](../../../../../../../first-step-analysis.md) gives a direct success with probability $1/3$, a return to the same problem with probability $1/3$, and entry into the closed class $\{b,d\}$ with probability $1/3$, after which $c$ cannot be reached. Therefore

$$
h=\frac13+\frac13h,
\qquad\boxed{h=\frac12.}
$$

The possibility of infinitely many self-loops has probability zero and introduces no extra solution of this equation.
