<h1 id="17b/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) of the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) is the sum of two positive quadratic forms. For a nonzero [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) in its domain,

$$
E\|\phi\|^2=\langle\phi,\hat H\phi\rangle
=\frac{\|\hat p\phi\|^2}{2m}+\frac{m\omega^2}{2}\|\hat x\phi\|^2\ge0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{E\ge0}$. Part (d) supplies the stronger bound $E\ge\hbar\omega/2$.
