<h1 id="17b/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Apply the [adjoint operator](../../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) identity of part (c) with $\chi=\hat A\phi$ and use part (a):

$$
\|\hat A\phi\|^2=\langle\phi,\hat B\hat A\phi\rangle
=2m(E-\tfrac12\hbar\omega)\|\phi\|^2.
$$

For a nonzero [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md), positivity of the [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) in particular forces $E\ge\hbar\omega/2$. Hence **the requested norm is**

$$
\boxed{\|\hat A\phi\|=\sqrt{2m(E-\tfrac12\hbar\omega)}\,\|\phi\|.}
$$

It vanishes exactly at the ground-state energy.
