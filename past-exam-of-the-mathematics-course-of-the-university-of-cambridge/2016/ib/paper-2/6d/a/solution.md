<h1 id="6d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In SI units the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) is $\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J+\mu_0\varepsilon_0\partial_t\mathbf E$. Since $\mathbf E=0$, the displacement-current term vanishes and $\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J$. Apply [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) to an oriented surface $S$ with positively oriented boundary $C$:

$$
\oint_C\mathbf B\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=\int_S(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=\mu_0\int_S\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf n\,dS.
$$

Hence the integral form of [Ampère's law](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) is

$$
\boxed{\oint_C\mathbf B\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=\mu_0 I_{\rm enclosed}.}
$$

Here $\mu_0$ is vacuum [magnetic permeability](../../../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md), $\mathbf J$ is [current density](../../../../../../current-density.md), and positive enclosed [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) is defined by the orientation of $S$.
