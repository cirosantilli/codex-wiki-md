<h1 id="6d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Only the signed enclosed [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) changes. Between the shells it remains $I$, but outside both it is $I-I=0$. The same circular-loop application of [Ampère's law](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B(r)=\begin{cases}0,&r<a,\\\dfrac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}\mathbf e_\phi,&a<r<b,\\0,&r>b.\end{cases}}
$$

**Opposite currents confine the generated magnetic field to the annulus**, as in an ideal infinitely long coaxial cable. The two shell [current sheets](../../../../../../current-sheet.md) account for the field jumps.
