<h1 id="14f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [hyperbolic law of cosines](../../../../../../hyperbolic-law-of-cosines.md), the angle opposite $a$ being at least $\pi/2$ makes its cosine nonpositive. Thus

$$
\cosh a=\cosh b\cosh c-\sinh b\sinh c\cos A
\ge\cosh b\cosh c\ge\cosh b,\cosh c.
$$

Since $\cosh$ is increasing on $[0,\infty)$, **the opposite side is longest:** $\boxed{a\ge b,\ a\ge c}$. For a nondegenerate [hyperbolic triangle](../../../../../../hyperbolic-triangle.md) both comparisons are strict.
