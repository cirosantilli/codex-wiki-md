A [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) on a real or complex [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $V$ is a function $\|\cdot\|:V\to[0,\infty)$ with $\|v\|=0$ exactly when $v=0$, $\|\lambda v\|=|\lambda|\|v\|$, and the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) $\|v+w\|\le\|v\|+\|w\|$. These are positivity, absolute homogeneity and the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md).
