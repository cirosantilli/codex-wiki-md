<h1 id="12g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The function $X\mapsto\det(I-X)$ is a polynomial in the matrix entries, hence [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). Consequently $U$ is the inverse image of the open set $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$ and is an [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md).

If $\|X\|<1$ and $(I-X)v=0$, then $v=Xv$ implies $\|v\|\le\|X\|\|v\|$. This forces $v=0$. The square matrix $I-X$ is therefore [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md), hence an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), so

$$
\boxed{V\subseteq U.}
$$

The argument works with any of the induced [operator norms](../../../../../../operator-norm.md) in part (b).
