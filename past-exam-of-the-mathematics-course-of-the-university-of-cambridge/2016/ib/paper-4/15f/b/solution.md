<h1 id="15f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The generating circle has $f'^2+g'^2=1$, and its revolution is a [torus](../../../../../../torus.md). From part (a), $K=\cos u/(2+\cos u)$ and the [area element of a surface](../../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md) is $dA=f\,du\,dv=(2+\cos u)\,du\,dv$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_S K\,dA=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\cos u\,dv\,du=0.}
$$

The positive [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) on the outer side exactly cancels the negative curvature on the inner side. Equivalently, the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](../../../../../../gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) gives $\int_S K\,dA=2\pi\chi(T^2)=0$ because the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) of the torus is zero.
