↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) [linear operator](../../../../../../linear-operator.md) is the cross product of the [position operator](../../../../../../position-operator.md) and the [momentum operator](../../../../../../momentum-operator.md):

$$
\boxed{\widehat{\mathbf L}=\widehat{\mathbf r}\times\widehat{\mathbf p},\qquad \widehat L_i=\sum_{j,k}\varepsilon_{ijk}\widehat x_j\widehat p_k.}
$$

In position space, $\widehat{\mathbf p}=-i\hbar\nabla$, so $\widehat{\mathbf L}=-i\hbar\,\mathbf r\times\nabla$. The [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) gives $\widehat L_1=\widehat x_2\widehat p_3-\widehat x_3\widehat p_2$ and its cyclic analogues.
