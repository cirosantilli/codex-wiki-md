<h1 id="7d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a charge-free and current-free vacuum, [Maxwell equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations.md) give

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=0,\quad \nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0,\quad \nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t\mathbf B,\quad \nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t\mathbf E.
$$

Take the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) of [Faraday's law](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) and use the [curl of the curl identity](../../../../../../curl-of-the-curl-identity.md) $\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf E)=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf E)-\nabla^2\mathbf E$. The [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) term vanishes by [Gauss's law](../../../../../../gauss-s-law.md), while the time derivative of the magnetic [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) supplies $-\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t^2\mathbf E$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\frac1{c^2}\partial_t^2\mathbf E-\nabla^2\mathbf E=\mathbf0,\quad \nabla\cdot\mathbf E=0,\quad c=(\epsilon_0\mu_0)^{-1/2}.}
$$

This is the transverse [electromagnetic wave equation](../../../../../../electromagnetic-wave-equation.md).
