<h1 id="18c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

By definition the [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) is $\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf u$. The [curl of the curl identity](../../../../../../curl-of-the-curl-identity.md) yields

$$
\nabla\times\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf u)-\nabla^2\mathbf u.
$$

For [incompressible flow](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md), $\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0$, so

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\boldsymbol\omega=-\nabla^2\mathbf u.}
$$
