<h1 id="14a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $a>0$, the [Heaviside step function](../../../../../../heaviside-step-function.md) vanishes before $a$ and is one afterwards. For $\operatorname{Re}p>0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L[H(t-a)](p)=\int_a^\infty e^{-pt}\,dt=\frac{e^{-ap}}p.}
$$

The value assigned at the single jump point does not affect this [Laplace transform](../../../../../../laplace-transform.md). The positive real part ensures convergence at infinity.
