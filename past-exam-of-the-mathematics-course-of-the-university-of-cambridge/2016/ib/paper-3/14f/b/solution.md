<h1 id="14f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With $T$ from part (a), the first, second and fourth points map to $1,0,\infty$. The inverse image of the extended real line under a [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) is a [generalized circle](../../../../../../generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md), meaning a circle or a line together with infinity. Thus, if $r=T(z_3)$ is real, all four original points lie on that generalized circle.

Conversely, if the four points lie on one [generalized circle](../../../../../../generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md), its image under $T$ is another generalized circle containing $0,1,\infty$. A generalized circle containing infinity is a line, and the line through zero and one is the real line. Therefore $T(z_3)=r$ is real. Hence **the cross-ratio is real exactly when the four points are concyclic or collinear**.
