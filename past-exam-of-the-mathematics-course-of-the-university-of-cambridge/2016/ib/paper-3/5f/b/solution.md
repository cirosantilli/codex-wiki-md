<h1 id="5f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $p$ and $h$ count the pentagonal and hexagonal faces. Counting incidences of edges with vertices and faces gives

$$
3V=2E,\qquad 5p+6h=2E,\qquad F=p+h.
$$

The [Euler formula for a sphere](../../../../../../euler-formula-for-a-sphere.md) also applies to this polygonal decomposition: triangulating a polygon by adding one diagonal increases both $E$ and $F$ by one, preserving $V-E+F$. Substitution gives

$$
2=\frac{2E}{3}-E+p+h=p+h-\frac{5p+6h}{6}=\frac p6.
$$

Thus **there are exactly 12 pentagons**, independently of the number of hexagons.
