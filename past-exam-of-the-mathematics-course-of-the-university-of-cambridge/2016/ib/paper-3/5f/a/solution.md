↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a finite [triangulation of a surface](../../../../../../triangulation-of-a-surface.md) of the [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md), [Euler formula for a sphere](../../../../../../euler-formula-for-a-sphere.md) is

$$
\boxed{V-E+F=2,}
$$

where $V$, $E$ and $F$ count vertices, edges and triangular faces. Since each face has three edges and each edge borders two faces, also $3F=2E$.
