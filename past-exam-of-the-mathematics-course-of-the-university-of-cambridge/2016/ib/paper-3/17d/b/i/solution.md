<h1 id="17d/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take positive current around $D\to E\to F\to G\to D$, so it runs upward through the bar. With $\mathbf B=B_0\mathbf e_z$, the oriented [magnetic flux](../../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) is $B_0Ls(t)$ and the [motional electromotive force](../../../../../../../motional-electromotive-force.md) is $-B_0L\dot s$. [Ohm's law](../../../../../../../ohm-s-law.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{I(t)=\frac{\mathcal E_0-B_0L\dot s(t)}R.}
$$

**If $I>0$, the bar current is $E\to F$; if $I<0$, it is $F\to E$.** The induced part opposes the battery for a bar moving to the right when $B_0>0$.
