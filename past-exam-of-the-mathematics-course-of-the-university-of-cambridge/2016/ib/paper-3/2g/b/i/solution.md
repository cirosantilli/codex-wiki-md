<h1 id="2g/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each fixed $0<x<1$, $x^n\to0$, so the [pointwise limit](../../../../../../../pointwise-limit.md) is $f(x)=1/(1-x)$. However

$$
|f_n(x)-f(x)|=\frac{x^n}{1-x}\longrightarrow\infty\quad\text{as }x\uparrow1
$$

for every fixed $n$. Hence the error has infinite [supremum](../../../../../../../supremum.md) on $(0,1)$ and **the convergence is not uniform**. This does not contradict [uniform convergence](../../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) on any smaller closed interval $[0,r]$, $r<1$, where the error is at most $r^n/(1-r)$.
