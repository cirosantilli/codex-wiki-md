<h1 id="2g/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For each fixed real $x$, $x/n\to0$, so the [pointwise limit](../../../../../../../pointwise-limit.md) is zero. But $\sup_{x\in\mathbb R}|x/n|=\infty$ for every $n$. Alternatively, $x=n$ gives error one whatever $n$ is. Thus **the convergence is not uniform on $\mathbb R$**. It is uniform on each bounded interval, where $|x/n|\leq M/n$.
