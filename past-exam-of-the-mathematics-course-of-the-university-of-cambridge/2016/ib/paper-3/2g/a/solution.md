↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The sequence has [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) on $X$ if there is a function $f:X\to\mathbb R$ such that

$$
\boxed{\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\ \forall n\geq N\ \forall x\in X:\quad |f_n(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon.}
$$

The same $N$ must work for every $x$. Equivalently, the [supremum](../../../../../../supremum.md) of the error over $X$ tends to zero. In [pointwise convergence](../../../../../../pointwise-convergence.md), $N$ is allowed to depend on $x$.
