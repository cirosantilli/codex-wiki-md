<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Define $K=N_s^2z_s^{-\beta}>0$. The [buoyancy frequency](../../../../../../buoyancy-frequency.md) definition gives $F_z=-KQz^\beta$. The powers in a [plume similarity in power-law stratification](../../../../../../plume-similarity-in-power-law-stratification.md) satisfy

$$
q-1=\frac m2,\qquad m-1=f+q-m,\qquad f-1=q+\beta.
$$

Solving gives

$$
\boxed{q=3+\frac\beta2,\qquad m=4+\beta,\qquad f=4+\frac{3\beta}{2}.}
$$

A rising positive-buoyancy [turbulent plume](../../../../../../turbulent-plume-split.md) entrains, so $Q_z>0$; its [momentum flux](../../../../../../momentum-flux.md) increases, so $M_z>0$; its [buoyancy flux](../../../../../../buoyancy-flux.md) decreases in the stable ambient, so $F_z<0$. With positive coefficients, this requires $q>0$, $m>0$ and $f<0$. The strongest restrictions are

$$
\boxed{-4<\beta<-\frac83.}
$$

In this interval $1<q<5/3$, $0<m<4/3$ and $-2<f<0$.

Coefficient matching makes the positive [similarity solution](../../../../../../similarity-solution.md) explicit:

$$
qQ_o=2\alpha\sqrt{M_o},\qquad mM_o^2=F_oQ_o,\qquad fF_o=-KQ_o,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{M_o=-\frac{4\alpha^2K}{fmq^2},\qquad Q_o=\frac{2\alpha\sqrt{M_o}}q,\qquad F_o=-\frac{KQ_o}f.}
$$

It is an ideal scaling for $z>0$, often a far-field description. As $z\to0$, the prescribed ambient stratification and this model's [buoyancy flux](../../../../../../buoyancy-flux.md) are singular; a finite physical source would require a regularized near-source region.
