<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take logarithmic height $\xi=\ln(z/z_*)$ with an arbitrary fixed length $z_*$, so $d/d\xi=z\,d/dz$. This is dimensionally meaningful and has the same derivatives as the abbreviated $\ln z$. For example,

$$
\frac{d\widehat Q}{d\xi}=\frac{zQ_z}{Q_s}-q\widehat Q=\frac{2\alpha\sqrt{M_o}}{Q_o}z^{1+m/2-q}\widehat M^{1/2}-q\widehat Q.
$$

The exponent vanishes and the coefficient is $q$, by the [plume similarity in power-law stratification](../../../../../../plume-similarity-in-power-law-stratification.md). Likewise the momentum coefficient is $m$ and the buoyancy coefficient is $f$. The [logarithmic-height plume equations](../../../../../../logarithmic-height-plume-equations.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\widehat Q_\xi&=-q(\widehat Q-\widehat M^{1/2}),\\
\widehat M_\xi&=-m(\widehat M-\widehat F\widehat Q/\widehat M),\\
\widehat F_\xi&=-f(\widehat F-\widehat Q).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The reference [similarity solution](../../../../../../similarity-solution.md) becomes the equilibrium $(1,1,1)$ of an [autonomous differential equation](../../../../../../autonomous-system-mathematics.md). Normalizing each flux by its own height-dependent similarity value is essential; simply differentiating the unnormalized fluxes would miss the three subtraction terms.
