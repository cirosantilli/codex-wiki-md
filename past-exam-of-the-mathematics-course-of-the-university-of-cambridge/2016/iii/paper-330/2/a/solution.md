<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the steady [Boussinesq top-hat plume in a stratified ambient](../../../../../../boussinesq-top-hat-plume-in-a-stratified-ambient.md), with negligible ambient vertical momentum and no externally added heat or particles. The [Batchelor entrainment hypothesis](../../../../../../batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md) supplies inward [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $\alpha w$ across circumference $2\pi b$. [Volume conservation](../../../../../../volume-conservation.md) gives $\pi Q_z=2\pi b\alpha w$, and $bw=\sqrt M$, hence

$$
\boxed{Q_z=2\alpha\sqrt M.}
$$

The [momentum flux](../../../../../../momentum-flux.md) increases by the integrated [buoyancy](../../../../../../buoyancy.md) force. Writing $g'=g\Delta\rho/\rho_o$, the force per reference density and per $\pi$ is $g'b^2$. Since $F=g'Q$ and $b^2=Q^2/M$, this gives

$$
\boxed{M_z=g'b^2=\frac{FQ}{M}.}
$$

For the [buoyancy flux](../../../../../../buoyancy-flux.md), entrained material has the local environmental [mass density](../../../../../../density.md). Let $\rho_p$ be the plume's averaged density. Its scalar [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) is $(\rho_pQ)_z=\rho_a Q_z$. Since $F=(g/\rho_o)(\rho_a-\rho_p)Q$, differentiating cancels both [entrainment](../../../../../../fluid-entrainment.md) terms and gives

$$
\boxed{F_z=\frac g{\rho_o}\left[(\rho_aQ)_z-(\rho_pQ)_z\right]=Q\frac g{\rho_o}\rho_a'.}
$$

In [stable density stratification](../../../../../../stable-density-stratification.md), $\rho_a'<0$ and a rising plume loses [buoyancy flux](../../../../../../buoyancy-flux.md). These are kinematic fluxes with the common factor $\pi\rho_o$ removed, so no additional factor of $\pi$ belongs in the three differential equations.
