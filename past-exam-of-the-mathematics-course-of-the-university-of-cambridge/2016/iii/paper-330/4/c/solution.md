<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Retain $a=\phi_1$, $\phi_2=2a$ and the three [settling shock speeds](../../../../../../settling-shock-speed.md). A [triple-shock sedimentation point](../../../../../../triple-shock-sedimentation-point.md) requires the meeting of $V_2$ with $V_3$ to coincide with the meeting of $V_1$ with $V_2$. Hence

$$
\frac{z_m}{u_s(1-a)}=\frac{H-z_m}{2u_sa}.
$$

Solving, and using the deposit-front position $2u_sa t$, gives

$$
\boxed{z_m=\frac{1-a}{1+a}H,\qquad t_c=\frac{H}{u_s(1+a)},\qquad h_c=\frac{2aH}{1+a}.}
$$

The common height also equals $a(H+z_m)$, independently checking [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md). The second panel of the compression-[shock](../../../../../../shock-wave.md) diagram shows $V_1,V_2,V_3$ meeting at this point. For $t>t_c$ the suspension is exhausted: deposit below $h_c$ and clear fluid above it are separated by a stationary [shock](../../../../../../shock-wave.md), with zero flux on both sides.
