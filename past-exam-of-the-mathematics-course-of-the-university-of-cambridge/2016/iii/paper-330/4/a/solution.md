<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The conserved particle volume per unit horizontal area is

$$
\mathcal P=\phi_1(H-z_m)+\phi_2z_m.
$$

The [sediment mass determines final deposit thickness](../../../../../../sediment-mass-determines-final-deposit-thickness.md): with the stipulated deposit [particle volume fraction](../../../../../../particle-volume-fraction.md) $\phi_{\max}=1$, all particle volume becomes bed thickness, so $h_c=\mathcal P$. For $z_m=H/2$,

$$
\boxed{\bar h=h_c=\frac H2(\phi_1+\phi_2),\qquad\bar\phi=\frac1H\int_0^H\phi\,dz=\frac{\phi_1+\phi_2}{2}=\frac{h_c}{H}.}
$$

The final [particle volume fraction](../../../../../../particle-volume-fraction.md) averaged over the entire container remains the initial average: clear fluid occupies $h_c<z<H$ and the deposit occupies $0<z<h_c$. Averaging over the deposit alone would instead give one, which is a different average.
