An experimentally observed example is **$K_L\to\pi^+\pi^-$**, a decay of the long-lived neutral [kaon](../../../../../../kaon.md) into a CP-even two-pion state. Without [CP violation](../../../../../../cp-violation.md), the long-lived state would be CP-odd and this decay would be forbidden.

For the [CKM matrix](../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) mechanism of [CP violation](../../../../../../cp-violation.md), **three [quark generations](../../../../../../quark-generation.md) are the minimum**. Rephasings remove every complex phase for one or two generations, while three generations leave one physical [CP-violating phase](../../../../../../cp-violating-phase.md). This count concerns weak quark mixing. The [Yang-Mills theta term](../../../../../../yang-mills-theta-term.md) in part (c) is a separate possible source of [CP violation](../../../../../../cp-violation.md) and does not require three generations.
