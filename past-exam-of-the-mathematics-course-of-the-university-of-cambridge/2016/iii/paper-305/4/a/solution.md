<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Both the left-handed [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) and the electron [neutrino](../../../../../../neutrino.md) participate in [weak charged currents](../../../../../../charged-current.md) through $W^\pm$, and both have [weak neutral currents](../../../../../../neutral-current.md) mediated by the [Z boson](../../../../../../z-boson.md). Their [electromagnetic interactions](../../../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) differ: the [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) has electric charge $-e$ and couples to the [photon](../../../../../../photon.md), while the [neutrino](../../../../../../neutrino.md) is electrically neutral and has no tree-level electromagnetic coupling.

In the minimal massless-neutrino [Standard Model](../../../../../../standard-model-split.md), the active neutrino has only a left-handed particle state and a right-handed antiparticle state. The [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) has both chiralities: its right-handed component has [electromagnetic interactions](../../../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) and a [weak neutral current](../../../../../../neutral-current.md), but no [weak charged current](../../../../../../charged-current.md). Its nonzero mass comes from a [Yukawa coupling](../../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) to the [Higgs field](../../../../../../higgs-field.md); the minimal massless-neutrino model contains no right-handed neutrino for an analogous Dirac mass term.

**Observed [neutrino oscillations](../../../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) establish nonzero neutrino mass-squared differences.** Atmospheric muon-neutrino disappearance depends on travel distance and energy, and solar electron neutrinos convert into other active flavors; reactor and accelerator oscillations provide complementary evidence. In propagation, the relative phase of two mass eigenstates is $\Delta m_{ij}^2L/(2E)$. If all masses were zero there would be no such relative phase. Thus at least two neutrino mass eigenstates are nonzero in the three-neutrino description with two independent measured splittings. A flavor state is a superposition of mass eigenstates, so the evidence does not assign a single definite mass to a flavor label.
