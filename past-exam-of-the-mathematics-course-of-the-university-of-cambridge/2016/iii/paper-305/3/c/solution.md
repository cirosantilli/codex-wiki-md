<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [weak charged current](../../../../../../charged-current.md) creates a left-handed [neutrino](../../../../../../neutrino.md) and a right-handed outgoing positron in the massless limit. In the parent's rest frame their momenta are opposite, so these two fixed [helicities](../../../../../../helicity.md) give total angular-momentum projection of magnitude one along their flight axis. A spin-one [W boson](../../../../../../w-boson.md) can supply that projection, and its leptonic width remains nonzero:

$$
\boxed{\Gamma(W^+\to e^+\nu_e)\big|_{m_e=0}=\frac{G_FM_W^3}{6\pi\sqrt2}.}
$$

A spin-zero [pion](../../../../../../pion.md) cannot produce the same helicity configuration. The forbidden component is rescued only by a charged-lepton mass insertion that reverses its [helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md), giving [helicity suppression](../../../../../../helicity-suppression.md).

This can also be seen directly from the [pion decay constant](../../../../../../pion-decay-constant.md). Lorentz symmetry gives a vacuum-to-pion [axial current](../../../../../../axial-current.md) matrix element proportional to $f_\pi p_\pi^\mu$. Contracting $p_\pi=k_e+k_\nu$ with the leptonic current gives

$$
\mathcal M_\pi\ \propto\ f_\pi\bar u_\nu(\not k_\nu+\not k_e)(1-\gamma^5)v_e
=-m_e f_\pi\bar u_\nu(1+\gamma^5)v_e,
$$

using the massless neutrino equation and $(\not k_e+m_e)v_e=0$. Hence **$\mathcal M_\pi\propto m_e$ and $\Gamma_\pi\propto m_e^2\to0$**. The [W boson](../../../../../../w-boson.md) polarization vector is not constrained to be proportional to the total momentum, so its amplitude has no analogous compulsory mass factor.
