<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

On the same [measurable space](../../../../../../measurable-space.md), **[absolute continuity of measures](../../../../../../absolute-continuity-of-measures.md)** means

$$
\boxed{\widetilde{\mathbb P}\ll\mathbb P\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\mathbb P(A)=0\implies\widetilde{\mathbb P}(A)=0\text{ for every }A\in\mathcal F.}
$$

The [Radon-Nikodym theorem](../../../../../../radon-nikodym-theorem.md) then supplies a nonnegative [measurable](../../../../../../measurability.md) density $Z$ with $\mathbb E_{\mathbb P}Z=1$ and $\widetilde{\mathbb P}(A)=\mathbb E_{\mathbb P}[Z\mathbf1_A]$. The converse follows directly from this formula. Strict positivity of the [Radon-Nikodym derivative](../../../../../../radon-nikodym-derivative.md) gives equivalence of the two [probability measures](../../../../../../probability-measure.md); [absolute continuity of measures](../../../../../../absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) alone need not give equivalence.
