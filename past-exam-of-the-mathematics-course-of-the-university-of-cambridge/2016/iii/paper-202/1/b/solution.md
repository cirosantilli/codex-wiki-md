<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For fixed $u>0$, define the [left-limit sigma-algebra](../../../../../../left-limit-sigma-algebra.md) by $\mathcal F_{u-}=\sigma(\bigcup_{s<u}\mathcal F_s)$. The section at $u$ of a generating rectangle $A\times(s,t]$ of the [predictable sigma-algebra](../../../../../../predictable-sigma-algebra.md) is $A$ when $s<u\leq t$, and empty otherwise. In the first case $A\in\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_{u-}$. Sections of the time-zero generators are empty for $u>0$.

The collection of subsets of sample-time space whose section at $u$ belongs to $\mathcal F_{u-}$ is a [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md): sections commute with complements and countable unions. It therefore contains all of $\mathcal P$. Apply this to $\{(\omega,t):H_t(\omega)\in E\}$ for every [Borel set](../../../../../../borel-set.md) $E$. Thus **[predictable sections at deterministic times](../../../../../../predictable-sections-at-deterministic-times.md) satisfy**

$$
\boxed{H_u\text{ is }\mathcal F_{u-}\text{-measurable}.}
$$

At $u=0$ the same argument gives $\mathcal F_0$-[measurability](../../../../../../measurability.md), using the convention $\mathcal F_{0-}=\mathcal F_0$. The PDF asks for information strictly before $u$; the TeX aid drops the minus sign.
