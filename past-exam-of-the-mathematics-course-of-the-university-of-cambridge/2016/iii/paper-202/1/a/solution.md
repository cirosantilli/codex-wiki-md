<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a [filtered probability space](../../../../../../filtered-probability-space.md) $(\Omega,\mathcal F,(\mathcal F_t),\mathbb P)$, and put $\mathcal F_{0-}=\mathcal F_0$. The **[predictable sigma-algebra](../../../../../../predictable-sigma-algebra.md)** on $\Omega\times[0,\infty)$ is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal P=\sigma\{A\times\{0\},\ A\in\mathcal F_0;\ A\times(s,t],\ A\in\mathcal F_s,\ 0\leq s<t<\infty\}.}
$$

Equivalently it is generated by left-continuous [adapted processes](../../../../../../adapted-process.md). A real-valued **[previsible process](../../../../../../predictable-process.md)** is a function $(\omega,t)\mapsto H_t(\omega)$ that is [measurable](../../../../../../measurability.md) for $\mathcal P$ and the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md) of $\mathbb R$.

A **[simple predictable process](../../../../../../simple-predictable-process.md)** has the form

$$
H=h_{-1}\mathbf1_{\{0\}}+\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i\mathbf1_{(t_i,t_{i+1}]},\qquad 0=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n<\infty,
$$

where $h_{-1}$ is bounded and $\mathcal F_0$-[measurable](../../../../../../measurability.md), and each $h_i$ is bounded and $\mathcal F_{t_i}$-[measurable](../../../../../../measurability.md). Set $H=0$ after $t_n$; allowing a final infinite interval gives an equivalent dense class. The values may be restricted to finitely many values if desired, by approximating bounded [measurable functions](../../../../../../measurable-function.md) by simple functions. The initial-time term matters for arbitrary [finite measures](../../../../../../finite-measure.md) on $\mathcal P$: a measure can charge time zero, even though the [stochastic integral](../../../../../../stochastic-integral.md) against a continuous [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) does not.
