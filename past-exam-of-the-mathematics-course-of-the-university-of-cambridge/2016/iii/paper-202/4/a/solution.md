A **[continuous martingale](../../../../../../continuous-martingale.md)** is an [adapted process](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) with almost surely continuous paths, $\mathbb E|M_t|<\infty$ for every $t$, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[M_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=M_s\qquad(s\leq t).}
$$

A **[continuous local martingale](../../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md)** is continuous and [adapted](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) and has a [localizing sequence](../../../../../../localizing-sequence.md) of [stopping times](../../../../../../stopping-time.md) $T_n\uparrow\infty$ almost surely such that every $M^{T_n}_t=M_{t\wedge T_n}$ is a true [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md). The initial value is normally integrable; the frequently used class $\mathcal M_{c,\mathrm{loc}}$ is normalized to start at zero.

The **[usual conditions for a filtration](../../../../../../usual-conditions-for-a-filtration.md)** are completeness and right continuity. Completeness means that $\mathcal F_0$ contains every subset of each $\mathbb P$-null set in the ambient $\mathcal F$; right continuity means $\mathcal F_t=\bigcap_{u>t}\mathcal F_u$. These conditions concern the [filtered probability space](../../../../../../filtered-probability-space.md), whereas continuity of a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) concerns its sample paths.
