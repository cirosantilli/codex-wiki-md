<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose a constant positive [Hermitian form](../../../../../../hermitian-form.md) on $V$. Its underlying flat [Kähler metric](../../../../../../kahler-metric.md) is translation-invariant and descends through the [Euclidean lattice](../../../../../../euclidean-lattice.md) to the compact [complex torus](../../../../../../complex-torus.md) $X$. The [Dolbeault Hodge decomposition](../../../../../../dolbeault-hodge-decomposition-on-a-compact-hermitian-manifold.md) gives a unique [Dolbeault Laplacian](../../../../../../dolbeault-laplacian.md)-harmonic representative of every [Dolbeault cohomology](../../../../../../dolbeault-cohomology.md) class. By the [Kähler Laplacian identity](../../../../../../kahler-laplacian-identity.md), these are also [Hodge Laplacian](../../../../../../hodge-laplacian.md)-harmonic.

Choose a constant coframe on $V$. A lifted harmonic form is a sum of constant wedge basis forms with smooth periodic coefficients. For the flat metric, the [Hodge Laplacian](../../../../../../hodge-laplacian.md) acts coefficientwise by the scalar constant-coefficient Laplacian. Each coefficient is a [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) on a compact flat real torus, hence is constant: [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives $\int|Da|^2=0$. Conversely every constant-coefficient form is harmonic. Thus the harmonic representatives are exactly the translation-invariant [differential forms of type (p, q)](../../../../../../differential-form-of-type-p-q.md).

The constant $(1,0)$ covectors form $V^*=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb C}(V,\mathbb C)$; the constant $(0,1)$ covectors form the space of [antilinear maps](../../../../../../antilinear-map.md) $\overline{V^*}=\operatorname{Hom}_{\overline{\mathbb C}}(V,\mathbb C)$. Sending a constant wedge form to its cohomology class gives

$$
\boxed{H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(X)\cong\bigwedge^pV^*\otimes\bigwedge^q\overline{V^*}.}
$$

The map is intrinsic and does not depend on the auxiliary flat metric: that metric proves every class has exactly one constant representative, while the inclusion of constant forms is defined directly by the quotient. **The Dolbeault cohomology of a complex torus consists of constant forms**, and in particular

$$
h^{p,q}(X)=\binom gp\binom gq,\qquad g=\dim_{\mathbb C}V.
$$

For indices outside $0,\ldots,g$, both the exterior-power expression and the cohomology group vanish.
