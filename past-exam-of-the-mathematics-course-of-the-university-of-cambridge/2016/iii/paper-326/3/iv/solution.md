↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the [convex conjugate](../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md), use a dual scalar $q$ to avoid confusing it with the fixed centre $z$. Differentiating $qx-\lambda(x-z)^2/2$ gives $q-\lambda(x-z)=0$, so the unique maximizing point is $x=z+q/\lambda$. Substitution yields

$$
\boxed{E^*(q)=qz+\frac{q^2}{2\lambda}.}
$$

The positivity of $\lambda$ makes the maximized quadratic strictly concave and the [convex conjugate](../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) finite for every real $q$.
