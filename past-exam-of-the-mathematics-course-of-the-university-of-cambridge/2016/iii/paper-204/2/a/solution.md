An $n$-step [self-avoiding walk](../../../../../../self-avoiding-walk.md) on a [graph](../../../../../../graph-split.md) $G=(V,E)$ is a sequence $(v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ of $n+1$ distinct [graph vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) such that $\{v_{i-1},v_i\}\in E$ for $1\leq i\leq n$. **It has $n$ edges and no repeated vertex**, including its starting [graph vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md). The $0$-step [self-avoiding walk](../../../../../../self-avoiding-walk.md) consists of its starting [graph vertex](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) alone.
