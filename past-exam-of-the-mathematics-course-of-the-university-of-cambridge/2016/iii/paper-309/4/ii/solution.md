<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\delta h_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu V_\nu+\partial_\nu V_\mu$. Substitution into the [linearized Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../linearized-levi-civita-connection.md) cancels four terms and leaves

$$
\delta\Gamma^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}=\partial_\nu\partial_\rho V^\mu.
$$

To first order in the [metric perturbation](../../../../../../linearized-gravity.md), the products of [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) are quadratic and may be discarded. Therefore the change in the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) is

$$
\delta R^\mu{}_{\nu\rho\sigma}
=\partial_\rho\delta\Gamma^\mu{}_{\sigma\nu}-\partial_\sigma\delta\Gamma^\mu{}_{\rho\nu}
=\partial_\rho\partial_\sigma\partial_\nu V^\mu-\partial_\sigma\partial_\rho\partial_\nu V^\mu=0.
$$

This is the [gauge invariance of the linearized Riemann tensor](../../../../../../gauge-invariance-of-the-linearized-riemann-tensor.md). Lowering the first index with the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) does not change the conclusion; the change from using the full [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) instead is of second order because the background curvature is zero. Hence

$$
\boxed{\delta R^{(1)}_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=0.}
$$

The result is **invariance at linear order about flat spacetime**, not componentwise invariance of arbitrary nonlinear curvature under finite coordinate changes. Directly in the four-term formula, the eight third derivatives of $V$ cancel in pairs by commuting partial derivatives.
