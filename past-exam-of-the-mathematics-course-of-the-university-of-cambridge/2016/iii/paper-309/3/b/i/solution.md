<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Lie derivative of a function](../../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-function.md) is differentiation along the flow, while the [Lie derivative of a vector field](../../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-vector-field.md) compares a vector with its pullback by that flow. Their index-free expressions are

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_X f=X(f)=df(X),\qquad \mathcal L_XY=[X,Y].}
$$

Here $X(f)$ is the [directional derivative](../../../../../../../directional-derivative.md) of the [smooth function](../../../../../../../smooth-function.md), and $[X,Y]$ is the [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md), characterized by $[X,Y](f)=X(Y(f))-Y(X(f))$. To see the second expression directly, if $\phi_t$ is the [local flow](../../../../../../../local-flow.md) of $X$, differentiating $(d\phi_t)^{-1}Y\circ\phi_t$ at $t=0$ gives $dY(X)-dX(Y)=[X,Y]$. This uses the [flow definition of the Lie derivative of a tensor field](../../../../../../../flow-definition-of-the-lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md).
