<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\lambda$ be an [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) and $X=dx^\mu/d\lambda\,\partial_\mu$ the tangent to the [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md). Since $\nabla_{\partial_\mu}\partial_\nu=\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\nu}\partial_\rho$, the product rule for the [covariant derivative](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) gives

$$
\nabla_XX=\left(\frac{d^2x^\rho}{d\lambda^2}
+\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\nu}\frac{dx^\mu}{d\lambda}\frac{dx^\nu}{d\lambda}\right)\partial_\rho.
$$

Independence of the coordinate basis vectors yields the **coordinate geodesic equation**

$$
\boxed{\frac{d^2x^\rho}{d\lambda^2}+\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\nu}\frac{dx^\mu}{d\lambda}\frac{dx^\nu}{d\lambda}=0.}
$$

The [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) condition matters: an arbitrary reparametrization instead allows a multiple of the tangent on the right, as in the [unparametrized geodesic equation](../../../../../../unparametrized-geodesic-equation.md).
