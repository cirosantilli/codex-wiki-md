<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Rearranging the outgoing [radial null geodesics of the Vaidya metric](../../../../../../radial-null-geodesics-of-the-vaidya-metric.md) equation along $r=r_0(v)$ gives

$$
\boxed{M(v)=\frac{r_0(v)}2\left(1-2\frac{dr_0}{dv}\right).}
$$

For the prescribed parametrization by $w$, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) yields

$$
\frac{dv}{dw}=1+2\beta n w^{n-1}>0,
\qquad
\frac{dr_0}{dv}=\frac{\beta n w^{n-1}}{1+2\beta n w^{n-1}},
\qquad
M(v(w))=\frac{\beta w^n}{2(1+2\beta n w^{n-1})}.
$$

Since $n>1$, $v/w=1+2\beta w^{n-1}\to1$ as $w\to0^+$. The [asymptotic equivalence](../../../../../../asymptotic-equivalence.md) $w\sim v$ therefore implies

$$
\boxed{M(v)\sim\frac\beta2 v^n,\qquad c_1=\frac\beta2.}
$$

More precisely $w=v+O(v^n)$ and $M(v)=(\beta/2)v^n[1+O(v^{n-1})]$, using [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md). The positivity of $dv/dw$ justifies treating $w$ as a single-valued function of $v$ locally.
