<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use signature $(-,+,+,+)$, so the future-directed [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md) obeys $n^\mu n_\mu=-1$. The [spatial projection tensor](../../../../../../spatial-projection-tensor.md) and the [induced metric](../../../../../../induced-metric.md) are

$$
P^\mu{}_\alpha=\delta^\mu{}_\alpha+n^\mu n_\alpha,
\qquad \gamma_{\alpha\beta}=g_{\alpha\beta}+n_\alpha n_\beta.
$$

Indeed $P^\mu{}_\alpha n^\alpha=0$ and $P^\mu{}_\rho P^\rho{}_\alpha=P^\mu{}_\alpha$: this is an [idempotent linear map](../../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) onto the [tangent space](../../../../../../tangent-space.md) of the spatial [hypersurface](../../../../../../hypersurface.md). For a covariant [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md) both slots must be projected:

$$
\boxed{(\perp T)_{\alpha\beta}=P^\mu{}_\alpha P^\nu{}_\beta T_{\mu\nu}.}
$$

Contracting either slot with $n$ supplies the vanishing factor $P^\mu{}_\alpha n^\alpha$. Thus **both normal contractions vanish**, without requiring symmetry of $T$.
