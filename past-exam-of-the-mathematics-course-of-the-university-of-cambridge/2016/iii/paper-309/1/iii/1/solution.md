<h1 id="1/iii/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For the [Vacuum Einstein equations](../../../../../../../vacuum-einstein-equations.md) with zero [cosmological constant](../../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md), $R_{\alpha\beta}=0$, and its [tensor contraction](../../../../../../../tensor-contraction.md) gives $R=0$. Every [Ricci tensor](../../../../../../../ricci-tensor.md) and [scalar curvature](../../../../../../../scalar-curvature.md) term in the [Weyl tensor](../../../../../../../weyl-tensor.md) consequently disappears:

$$
\boxed{C_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}=R_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}.}
$$

Thus **the [Weyl tensor](../../../../../../../weyl-tensor.md) contains all vacuum curvature**. Here vacuum means Ricci-flat; a nonzero [cosmological constant](../../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) would retain a constant-curvature contribution to the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md).
