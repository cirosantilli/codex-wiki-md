↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Weak-star-to-weak continuity of the adjoint implies its weak compactness.** By the [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](../../../../../../banach-alaoglu-theorem.md), $B_{Y^*}$ is compact for the [weak-star topology](../../../../../../weak-star-topology.md). Under the assumed continuous map $T^*:(Y^*,w^*)\to(X^*,w)$, its image $T^*(B_{Y^*})$ is weakly compact in $X^*$.

This image is closed for the [weak topology](../../../../../../weak-topology-split.md), and hence for the [norm topology](../../../../../../norm-topology.md), because the [weak topology](../../../../../../weak-topology-split.md) is Hausdorff and weaker than the norm topology. Thus its norm closure is the same weakly compact set. By the definition of a [weakly compact operator](../../../../../../weakly-compact-operator.md), $T^*$ is weakly compact, proving $(iii)\Longrightarrow(iv)$.
