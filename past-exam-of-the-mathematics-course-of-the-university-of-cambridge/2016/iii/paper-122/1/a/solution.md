<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $x$ denote the generating object, the image of the unique object of the [terminal category](../../../../../../terminal-category.md). The [free braided monoidal category on one object](../../../../../../free-braided-monoidal-category-on-one-object.md) can be described syntactically. Its objects are all fully parenthesized expressions built from $x$, the [monoidal unit object](../../../../../../monoidal-unit-object.md) $I$, and a binary [monoidal tensor product](../../../../../../monoidal-tensor-product.md). Thus $x\otimes(I\otimes x)$ and $(x\otimes I)\otimes x$ are distinct objects, though canonically isomorphic. Its [morphisms](../../../../../../morphism.md) are generated by the [associators](../../../../../../associator.md), [unitors](../../../../../../unitor.md), [braidings](../../../../../../braiding.md) and their inverses, closed under composition and tensor product, subject to the pentagon, triangle, [naturality](../../../../../../naturality.md) and hexagon axioms. No symmetry relation is imposed on the [braiding](../../../../../../braiding.md). The embedding of the [terminal category](../../../../../../terminal-category.md) selects $x$.

The [braid category](../../../../../../braid-category.md) $\mathbf B$ has objects the nonnegative integers, with

$$
\mathbf B(m,n)=\begin{cases}B_n,&m=n,\\\varnothing,&m\ne n,\end{cases}
$$

where $B_n$ is the [braid group](../../../../../../braid-group.md) on $n$ strands and $B_0,B_1$ are trivial. Composition is stacking braids, with the first morphism followed by the second. The [monoidal tensor product](../../../../../../monoidal-tensor-product.md) is addition on objects and side-by-side juxtaposition of braids; its [monoidal unit object](../../../../../../monoidal-unit-object.md) is $0$. The [associators](../../../../../../associator.md) and [unitors](../../../../../../unitor.md) are identities, so it is a [strict monoidal category](../../../../../../strict-monoidal-category.md).

Choose the positive crossing convention once and for all. Its [braiding](../../../../../../braiding.md) $c_{m,n}:m+n\to n+m$ is the block braid moving the first $m$ strands over the next $n$ strands while retaining the order within each block. In particular $c_{1,1}=\sigma_1$. The usual [braid group](../../../../../../braid-group.md) relations express the [naturality](../../../../../../naturality.md) and hexagon laws for these block braids. The generating [functor](../../../../../../functor.md) $\mathbf1\to\mathbf B$ selects $1$.

**The distinction is parenthesized tensor expressions versus strand counts:** the first construction keeps the structural isomorphisms visible, while $\mathbf B$ has strict tensor arithmetic. These descriptions give the two requested free constructions without needing to establish their [universal properties](../../../../../../universal-property.md).
