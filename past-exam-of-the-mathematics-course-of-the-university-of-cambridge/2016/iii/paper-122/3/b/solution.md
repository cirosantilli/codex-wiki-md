<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the specified self-dual-pairing meaning of “coseparable”; categorically this is the [Frobenius monoid](../../../../../../frobenius-monoid.md) structure relevant here. The induced [monoid object](../../../../../../monoid-object.md) on $FA$ has maps

$$
\boxed{J=F(j)\varphi_0,\qquad M=F(m)\varphi_{A,A},\qquad E_A=\psi_0F(\varepsilon).}
$$

Its associativity follows from associativity of $m$ and the [lax monoidal functor](../../../../../../monoidal-functor.md) axiom for $\varphi$: both iterated products are $F$ of the corresponding triple product, preceded by the same coherent iterated tensor comparison. The unit laws follow from those of $j,m$ and the two monoidal unit axioms. Thus $(FA,J,M)$ is a [monoid object](../../../../../../monoid-object.md).

Its pairing is exactly the evaluation transported in the preceding part:

$$
E_AM=\psi_0F(\varepsilon)F(m)\varphi_{A,A}=\psi_0F(\varepsilon m)\varphi_{A,A}.
$$

If $n:I\to A\otimes A$ is the original [coevaluation morphism](../../../../../../coevaluation-morphism.md), the corresponding new one is $\psi_{A,A}F(n)\varphi_0$. The preceding [Frobenius monoidal functor](../../../../../../frobenius-monoidal-functor.md) calculation proves both [snake identities](../../../../../../snake-identity.md). Hence **the pairing of the induced monoid remains self-dual**, giving the claimed canonical structure on $FA$. The terminology in the paper imposes this duality condition; it does not add a separability or semisimplicity condition.
