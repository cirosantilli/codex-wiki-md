<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**False.** [Adiabatic invariance of an orbital action](../../../../../../adiabatic-invariance-of-an-orbital-action.md) preserves actions under sufficiently slow regular evolution, not the ratios of their frequencies. With [action-angle variables](../../../../../../action-angle-variables.md), $\Omega_i=\partial H/\partial J_i$ can change even when $J_i$ does not. A closed bound orbit requires commensurate radial and angular frequencies; rational commensurability is not an adiabatic invariant.

For an explicit counterexample, slowly change the physical potential from Kepler form to

$$
\Phi(r)=-\frac{GM}{r}+\frac12\alpha(t)\omega^2r^2,
$$

with a small, slowly varying positive $\alpha$. The [radial epicyclic frequency](../../../../../../radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) and circular [angular frequency](../../../../../../angular-frequency.md) at a nearly [circular orbit](../../../../../../circular-orbit.md) are

$$
\Omega^2=\frac{GM}{r^3}+\alpha\omega^2,\qquad\kappa^2=\Phi''+\frac{3\Phi'}r=\frac{GM}{r^3}+4\alpha\omega^2.
$$

Initially their ratio is one. For generic nonzero $\alpha$, $\Omega/\kappa$ is irrational, producing the [Kepler-harmonic epicyclic precession](../../../../../../kepler-harmonic-epicyclic-precession.md). An initially slightly eccentric ellipse with nonzero radial action therefore becomes a precessing nonclosed orbit, while its radial action remains approximately fixed and its [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) remains exactly fixed by [spherical symmetry](../../../../../../spherical-symmetry.md).

**Adiabatic actions do not preserve orbital closure.** Exceptional circular or resonantly commensurate final orbits do not rescue a claim about every initially closed orbit.
