<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a null [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) with $E>0$ and orient the third axis so that $2\sigma^\mu p_\mu=\operatorname{diag}(4E,0)$. The [Super-Poincaré algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md) gives

$$
\{Q_1^A,(Q_1^B)^\dagger\}=4E\delta^{AB},\qquad
\{Q_2^A,(Q_2^B)^\dagger\}=0.
$$

For every state $|v\rangle$ in a [unitary representation](../../../../../../unitary-representation.md), the second [anticommutator](../../../../../../anticommutator.md) is the sum $\|Q_2^A v\|^2+\|(Q_2^A)^\dagger v\|^2$. Thus both operators act trivially. A [central charge in supersymmetry](../../../../../../central-charge-in-supersymmetry.md) also vanishes in this [massless supermultiplet](../../../../../../massless-supermultiplet.md): its mixed-spinor [anticommutator](../../../../../../anticommutator.md) contains the now-trivial $Q_2$. The remaining $\mathcal N$ pairs, normalized by $2\sqrt E$, satisfy the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md)

$$
\{a_A,a_B^\dagger\}=\delta_{AB},\qquad
\{a_A,a_B\}=\{a_A^\dagger,a_B^\dagger\}=0.
$$

Label a [Clifford vacuum](../../../../../../clifford-vacuum.md) by its highest [helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md) $\lambda$ and choose the lowering convention $[J_3,a_A^\dagger]=-a_A^\dagger/2$. Acting with distinct [fermionic creation operators](../../../../../../fermionic-creation-operator.md) produces the [fermionic Fock space](../../../../../../fermionic-fock-space.md)

$$
a_{A_1}^\dagger\cdots a_{A_k}^\dagger|p,\lambda\rangle,
\qquad
\boxed{h_k=\lambda-\frac{k}{2},\quad n_k=\binom{\mathcal N}{k},\quad k=0,\ldots,\mathcal N}.
$$

This proves the [helicity spectrum of a massless supermultiplet](../../../../../../helicity-spectrum-of-a-massless-supermultiplet.md) and the $2^{\mathcal N}$ state count. If the internal charges or [helicities](../../../../../../helicity.md) do not already make the spectrum invariant under the [CPT theorem](../../../../../../cpt-theorem.md), add its conjugate using the [CPT completion of a supermultiplet](../../../../../../cpt-completion-of-a-supermultiplet.md).

The [spin bound for massless supermultiplets](../../../../../../spin-bound-for-massless-supermultiplets.md) follows directly from the width $\mathcal N/2$ of this [helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md) interval. Requiring $|h|\le1$ gives **$\mathcal N\le4$**, achieved by the [four-dimensional N=4 super Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../../four-dimensional-n-4-super-yang-mills-theory.md) [vector multiplet](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) with multiplicities $1,4,6,4,1$ at [helicities](../../../../../../helicity.md) $1,1/2,0,-1/2,-1$. These [helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md) bounds are necessary spin restrictions for the usual interacting [renormalizable quantum field theory](../../../../../../renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md); they do not by themselves establish renormalizability for an arbitrary action. Requiring $|h|\le2$ gives **$\mathcal N\le8$**. The [four-dimensional N=8 supergravity](../../../../../../four-dimensional-n-8-supergravity.md) [supergravity multiplet](../../../../../../supergravity-multiplet.md) attains this bound with highest [helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md) $2$ and multiplicities $1,8,28,56,70,56,28,8,1$. There is one $+2$ state and one $-2$ state, the two polarizations of a single [graviton](../../../../../../graviton.md).

The stronger restriction relevant to matter is the [chirality constraint on extended supersymmetry](../../../../../../chirality-constraint-on-extended-supersymmetry.md). An $\mathcal N=1$ [chiral superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) can contain a left-handed [Weyl spinor](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) in a complex [gauge group representation](../../../../../../gauge-group-representation.md) $R$. By contrast, a full $\mathcal N=2$ [hypermultiplet](../../../../../../hypermultiplet.md) consists of $\mathcal N=1$ matter in $R\oplus\overline R$, and the [gauginos](../../../../../../gaugino.md) of a [vector multiplet](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) are in the real adjoint [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md). Higher unbroken [extended supersymmetry](../../../../../../extended-supersymmetry.md) retains this pairing. A [pseudoreal representation](../../../../../../pseudoreal-representation.md) may allow a half-[hypermultiplet](../../../../../../hypermultiplet.md), but it does not yield net chirality in a genuinely complex [gauge group representation](../../../../../../gauge-group-representation.md). Consequently the [chiral gauge spectrum](../../../../../../chiral-gauge-spectrum.md) of the [Standard Model](../../../../../../standard-model-split.md) permits **at most unbroken $\mathcal N=1$ supersymmetry** in its four-dimensional low-energy matter sector.
