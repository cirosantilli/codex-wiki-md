<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take a canonical [Kähler potential](../../../../../../kahler-potential.md) $K=\Phi^\dagger\Phi$ and the [Wess–Zumino model](../../../../../../wess-zumino-model.md) [superpotential](../../../../../../superpotential.md)

$$
W(\Phi)=\frac m2\Phi^2+\frac g3\Phi^3.
$$

The [F-term](../../../../../../f-term.md) component gives

$$
\mathcal L_F=F^*F+F W'(\phi)+F^*\overline{W'(\phi)}
-\frac12 W''(\phi)\psi\psi-\frac12\overline{W''(\phi)}\bar\psi\bar\psi.
$$

Eliminating the [auxiliary field](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) by $F=-\overline{W'(\phi)}$ gives the [F-term scalar potential](../../../../../../f-term-scalar-potential.md) $V=|W'(\phi)|^2$. Consequently

$$
V=|m\phi+g\phi^2|^2
=|m|^2|\phi|^2+m\bar g\phi\bar\phi^2+\bar m g\bar\phi\phi^2
+\boxed{|g|^2|\phi|^4},
\qquad
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{Yukawa}}=-\frac12m\psi\psi-\boxed{g\phi\psi\psi}+\mathrm{h.c.}
$$

This is the [supersymmetric relation between quartic and Yukawa couplings](../../../../../../supersymmetric-relation-between-quartic-and-yukawa-couplings.md): there is one holomorphic parameter $g$, with the quartic coefficient $|g|^2$ and the [Yukawa coupling](../../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) $g$ in the displayed normalization. For real $g$ the quartic coefficient is the $g^2$ of the question. They cannot be varied independently while retaining this canonical [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md).

The relation produces the [supersymmetric cancellation of quadratic divergences](../../../../../../supersymmetric-cancellation-of-quadratic-divergences.md). Bosonic and fermionic [Feynman diagrams](../../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) correcting a [scalar mass](../../../../../../scalar-mass.md) have equal ultraviolet quadratic coefficients and opposite signs, so the mass is not driven directly to the ultraviolet cutoff scale. In a theory with [soft supersymmetry breaking](../../../../../../soft-supersymmetry-breaking.md), residual corrections instead have the schematic size

$$
\delta m_H^2\sim\frac{|g|^2}{16\pi^2}m_{\mathrm{soft}}^2
\log\frac{\Lambda}{m_{\mathrm{soft}}},
$$

with coefficients and logarithms depending on the spectrum. Thus superpartner masses near the electroweak scale can stabilize the [Higgs boson](../../../../../../higgs-boson.md) mass against a much larger cutoff, addressing the radiative part of the [hierarchy problem](../../../../../../hierarchy-problem.md). Heavy [sparticles](../../../../../../sparticle.md) leave a corresponding residual sensitivity; the cancellation does not by itself explain every mass scale.
