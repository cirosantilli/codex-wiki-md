<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In the conventions $\{Q_\alpha,Q_\beta^\dagger\}=2\sigma^\mu_{\alpha\dot\beta}P_\mu$, the trace over the two spinor components of the [Super-Poincaré algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md) gives

$$
\boxed{H=\frac14\sum_{\alpha=1}^2\{Q_\alpha,Q_\alpha^\dagger\}}.
$$

Hence, on a normalized state in the domain of the [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md),

$$
\langle v|H|v\rangle
=\frac14\sum_{\alpha=1}^2\left(\|Q_\alpha v\|^2+\|Q_\alpha^\dagger v\|^2\right)\ge0.
$$

This proves [energy positivity in global supersymmetry](../../../../../../energy-positivity-in-global-supersymmetry.md). The zero of energy is fixed by the algebra; adding an arbitrary constant to $H$ while keeping that algebra unchanged is not allowed.

For a finite-dimensional [supermultiplet](../../../../../../supermultiplet.md) at fixed positive energy and [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md), choose a nonzero component $q$ with $\{q,q^\dagger\}=cI$, $c>0$, and $q^2=0$. The Hermitian odd operator

$$
A=\frac{q+q^\dagger}{\sqrt c}
\quad\text{satisfies}\quad A^2=I,
\qquad A(-1)^F=-(-1)^F A.
$$

It is therefore an invertible map from the even [fermion parity](../../../../../../fermion-parity.md) subspace to the odd subspace and conversely. This proves [boson-fermion degeneracy in a supermultiplet](../../../../../../boson-fermion-degeneracy-in-a-supermultiplet.md), **$n_B=n_F$**, for every positive-energy physical particle [supermultiplet](../../../../../../supermultiplet.md). Equivalently, the [supertrace](../../../../../../supertrace.md) of the supercharge [anticommutator](../../../../../../anticommutator.md) vanishes because odd operators interchange these two subspaces. A zero-energy vacuum is an exception to the literal claim about every possible representation: it can be a one-dimensional bosonic singlet with all [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) acting trivially.

For a vacuum $|\Omega\rangle$, the sum-of-squares formula gives $E_0=0$ exactly when all [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) and their adjoints annihilate it. If global [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) is spontaneously broken and a vacuum exists, at least one such norm is nonzero, so **the vacuum energy is positive**. In infinite volume this is the statement about the [vacuum energy](../../../../../../vacuum-energy.md) density, understood with a finite-volume regulator. Explicit [supersymmetry breaking](../../../../../../supersymmetry-breaking.md) need not preserve the algebra used in the proof. The conclusion also does not extend to the [supergravity F-term potential](../../../../../../supergravity-f-term-potential.md), whose negative $-3e^K|W|^2$ term permits, for example, the zero-energy broken vacua of [no-scale supergravity](../../../../../../no-scale-supergravity.md).
