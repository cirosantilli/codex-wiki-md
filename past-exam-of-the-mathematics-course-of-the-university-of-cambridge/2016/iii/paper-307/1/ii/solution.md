<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On shell, a massless $\mathcal N=1$ [vector multiplet](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) contains a [gauge boson](../../../../../../gauge-boson.md) with two transverse polarizations and a [gaugino](../../../../../../gaugino.md) with two fermionic states. A [chiral superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) contains a [complex scalar field](../../../../../../complex-scalar-field.md), hence two real bosonic states, and a [Weyl spinor](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) with two fermionic states. Their total is $4$ bosonic and $4$ fermionic states.

Suppose charged [scalar fields](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) acquire [vacuum expectation values](../../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) at a [supersymmetric vacuum](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vacuum.md), where [F-flatness](../../../../../../f-flatness.md) and [D-flatness](../../../../../../d-flatness.md) hold. For each broken generator of the [gauge symmetry](../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md), the [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md) combines one massless [vector multiplet](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) and one [chiral superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) into a [massive N=1 vector multiplet](../../../../../../massive-n-1-vector-multiplet.md). The [gauge boson](../../../../../../gauge-boson.md) absorbs one real [Goldstone boson](../../../../../../goldstone-boson.md), acquiring its third polarization. The other real [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) remains physical. The [gaugino](../../../../../../gaugino.md) and the relevant [higgsino](../../../../../../higgsino.md) mix into the four fermionic states. Thus the spin content is

$$
\boxed{(1,\tfrac12,\tfrac12,0),\qquad n_B=3+1=4,quad n_F=2+2=4}.
$$

Unbroken [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) gives all these states the same mass through [supersymmetric mass degeneracy](../../../../../../supersymmetric-mass-degeneracy.md). Remaining massive [chiral superfields](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) instead have spin content $(1/2,0,0)$, with two fermionic and two bosonic states of a common mass. The [auxiliary fields](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) ensure matching off shell but are not additional propagating particles in these counts.

The [super-Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../super-higgs-mechanism.md) occurs when local [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) is spontaneously broken in [supergravity](../../../../../../supergravity.md). Its spin-$3/2$ gauge field, the [gravitino](../../../../../../gravitino.md), absorbs the spin-$1/2$ [goldstino](../../../../../../goldstino.md):

$$
\boxed{2\text{ massless gravitino states}+2\text{ goldstino states}
\longrightarrow4\text{ massive gravitino states}}.
$$

The extra states have [helicities](../../../../../../helicity.md) $\pm1/2$, supplementing $\pm3/2$. The ordinary [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md) can preserve [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) while breaking the internal [gauge symmetry](../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md); the [super-Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../super-higgs-mechanism.md) breaks the local [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) itself. Spontaneously broken global [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) leaves a physical massless [goldstino](../../../../../../goldstino.md), because there is no dynamical [gravitino](../../../../../../gravitino.md) to absorb it.
