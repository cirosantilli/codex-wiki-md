<h1 id="4/j/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [J](../j.md)

Three solar-system effects illustrate [chemical disequilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-disequilibrium.md). First, vertical mixing carries [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) from hot deep regions into cooler observable layers of giant planets, where its local-equilibrium abundance would be small. Second, [phosphine](../../../../../../phosphine.md) survives in the observable atmospheres of Jupiter and Saturn because deep supply competes with its destruction, making it a tracer of transport. Third, [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) photolysis creates hydrocarbons such as ethane and [acetylene](../../../../../../acetylene.md), and can help form upper-atmospheric [atmospheric hazes](../../../../../../haze.md) in giant planets and Titan.

Three exoplanet effects are complementary. First, [carbon monoxide–methane quenching](../../../../../../carbon-monoxide-methane-quenching.md) can keep [carbon monoxide](../../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) abundant and [methane](../../../../../../methane.md) depleted in cool observable gas, including hot-Neptune/giant atmospheres that would locally favor [methane](../../../../../../methane.md). Second, [atmospheric photochemistry](../../../../../../atmospheric-photochemistry.md) dissociates [methane](../../../../../../methane.md)/[ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) and produces radicals, [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md), hydrocarbons and possibly [atmospheric hazes](../../../../../../haze.md), changing spectral bands from their equilibrium strengths. Third, [horizontal chemical quenching](../../../../../../horizontal-chemical-quenching.md) can preserve hot-dayside carbon chemistry on the cooler nightside/terminator and reduce the abundance contrast expected from separate local-equilibrium columns.

**Transport, photon-driven reactions and finite chemical adjustment times change the observable composition**. The effects are conditional on the species' chemical timescale relative to mixing, advection and irradiation; they do not imply that every molecule is out of equilibrium everywhere.
