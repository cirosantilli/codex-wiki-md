<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Expand the [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md):

$$
c=at a t^{-1}a^{-1}t a^{-1}t^{-1}.
$$

Its possible pinches have intervening coefficients $a$, $a^{-1}$, and $a^{-1}$. For a $t(\cdot)t^{-1}$ pinch the coefficient must lie in $\langle a^2\rangle$; neither $a$ nor $a^{-1}$ does. For the $t^{-1}(\cdot)t$ pinch it must lie in $\langle a^3\rangle$, and again $a^{-1}$ does not. Here $\langle a\rangle\cong\mathbb Z$ embeds by the [normal form theorem for an HNN extension](../../../../../../normal-form-theorem-for-an-hnn-extension.md), so these divisibility tests are valid inside $S$.

The word is a [reduced sequence in an HNN extension](../../../../../../reduced-sequence-in-an-hnn-extension.md) with four stable letters. [Britton's lemma](../../../../../../britton-s-lemma.md) gives

$$
\boxed{c\ne1,\qquad c\in\ker\theta.}
$$

Thus $S$ is a finitely presented [non-Hopfian group](../../../../../../non-hopfian-group.md): it has a surjective [endomorphism](../../../../../../endomorphism.md) which is not injective. This group is the [Baumslag-Solitar group](../../../../../../baumslag-solitar-group.md) $BS(2,3)$.
