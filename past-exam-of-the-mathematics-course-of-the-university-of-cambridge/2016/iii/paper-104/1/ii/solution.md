<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

$\boxed{\mathbb Z^4\text{ is not a nontrivial free product}.}$ In a [nontrivial free product](../../../../../../nontrivial-free-product.md), nonidentity elements $g,h$ from different factors satisfy $gh\ne hg$, since these are distinct alternating normal forms. Such a [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is not an [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md), whereas the [free abelian group](../../../../../../free-abelian-group.md) $\mathbb Z^4$ is abelian.
