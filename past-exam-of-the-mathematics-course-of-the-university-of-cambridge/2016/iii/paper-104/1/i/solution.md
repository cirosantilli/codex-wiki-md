<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

$\boxed{A_5\text{ is not a nontrivial free product}.}$ If $g\ne1$ and $h\ne1$ belong to different factors, the powers $(gh)^r$ have distinct normal forms of length $2r$. Every [nontrivial free product](../../../../../../nontrivial-free-product.md) is therefore an [infinite group](../../../../../../infinite-group.md), whereas the [alternating group](../../../../../../alternating-group.md) $A_5$ is a [finite group](../../../../../../finite-group.md) of order $60$.
