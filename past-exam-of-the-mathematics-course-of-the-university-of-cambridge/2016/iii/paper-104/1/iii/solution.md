<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Eliminate $t$ using $t=xsx^{-1}$. The remaining relation $xtx^{-1}=s$ becomes $x^2sx^{-2}=s$, which follows from $x^2=1$. These [Tietze transformations](../../../../../../tietze-transformations.md) give

$$
\boxed{G\cong\langle x,s\mid x^2=1\rangle\cong C_2*\mathbb Z.}
$$

Both factors are nontrivial, so this is a [nontrivial free product](../../../../../../nontrivial-free-product.md). Conversely, in $C_2*\langle s\rangle$ the definition $t=xsx^{-1}$ satisfies both conjugation relations, confirming that eliminating $t$ loses no relation.
