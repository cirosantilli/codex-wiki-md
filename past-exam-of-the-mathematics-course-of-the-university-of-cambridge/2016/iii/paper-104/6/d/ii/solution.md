↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Part (c) shows that every finitely presented [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) embeds in a finitely presented [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) with no nontrivial [finite quotients of a group](../../../../../../../finite-quotient-of-a-group.md). Consequently this property also has no possible forbidden witness $A_-$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{Having no nontrivial finite quotients is not a Markov property}.}
$$

Both properties have positive witnesses, for example $C_2$ and the [trivial group](../../../../../../../trivial-group.md) respectively. It is the required embedding obstruction that fails in each case.
