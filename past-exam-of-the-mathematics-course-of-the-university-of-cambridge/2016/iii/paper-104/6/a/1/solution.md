<h1 id="6/a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

If $w=1$ in $P$, its image is the identity in the output group, so $c=1$. The equations $u_j^{-1}cu_j=g_j$ then give $z=d=1$ and $x_i z=1$ for every $i$, hence $x_i=1$. Therefore $C=1$, and the relation $C=a_1$ gives $a_1=1$. Since the [normal closure](../../../../../../../normal-closure.md) of $a_1$ is $Q$, every $a_i$ is $1$. The remaining identifications make every $u_j$ equal to $1$ as well. All output generators vanish, proving

$$
\boxed{w=1\text{ in }P\quad\Longrightarrow\quad P(w)=\{1\}.}
$$

This proves collapse even when the original [group](../../../../../../../group-split.md) $P$ itself is nontrivial.
