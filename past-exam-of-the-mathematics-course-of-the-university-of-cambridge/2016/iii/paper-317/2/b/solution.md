<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For [PP chain](../../../../../../proton-proton-chain.md) burning and the [Kramers' opacity law](../../../../../../kramers-opacity-law.md), insert $\eta=4$, $\lambda=1$, and $\nu=-7/2$ into the [homology relations for radiative stars](../../../../../../homology-relations-for-radiative-stars.md):

$$
X=\frac{1/2}{13/2}=\frac1{13},\qquad
Y=6-7X=\frac{71}{13}.
$$

Thus the [radiative homology with proton-proton burning and Kramers opacity](../../../../../../radiative-homology-with-proton-proton-burning-and-kramers-opacity.md) is

$$
\boxed{R\propto M^{1/13},\qquad L\propto M^{71/13}.}
$$

To obtain the [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](../../../../../../hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) slope, apply the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) $L=4\pi R^2\sigma T_{\rm eff}^4$:

$$
T_{\rm eff}\propto M^{(Y-2X)/4}=M^{69/52},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{d\log L}{d\log T_{\rm eff}}=\frac{4Y}{Y-2X}
=\frac{284}{69}\simeq4.116.}
$$

Equivalently $T_{\rm eff}\propto L^{69/284}$. The [Hertzsprung-Russell slope of a stellar homology sequence](../../../../../../hertzsprung-russell-slope-of-a-stellar-homology-sequence.md) refers here to logarithmic [luminosity](../../../../../../luminosity.md) versus logarithmic effective [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md); a conventional diagram reverses its horizontal axis, so the line descends toward the right.
