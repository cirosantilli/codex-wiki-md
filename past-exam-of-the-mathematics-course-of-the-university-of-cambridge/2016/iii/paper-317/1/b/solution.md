<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the [radiative-zero white-dwarf envelope](../../../../../../radiative-zero-white-dwarf-envelope.md), $dP/dT=(17/4)P/T=(17/4)\mathcal R\rho$. Substitute this derivative into the [stellar hydrostatic equation](../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure-support-equation.md):

$$
\frac{17}{4}\mathcal R\rho\frac{dT}{dr}=-\frac{GM\rho}{r^2},\qquad
\frac{dT}{dr}=-\frac4{17}\frac{GM}{\mathcal Rr^2}.
$$

Integrating from the idealized zero-temperature surface at $R$ gives

$$
\boxed{T(r)=\frac4{17}\frac{GM}{\mathcal R}\left(\frac1r-\frac1R\right)
=\frac4{17}\frac{\mu m_H}{k}GM\left(\frac1r-\frac1R\right).}
$$

This also gives $d\log T/d\log P=4/17$. A finite surface [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) adds $T_s$ to the integrated profile if the pure power-law [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) relation is retained locally. Near the actual [photosphere](../../../../../../photosphere.md), the integration constant in the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) law also matters; the displayed expression is the radiative-zero limit implicit in the printed result and accurate in the deep envelope when $T_c\gg T_{\rm eff}$.
