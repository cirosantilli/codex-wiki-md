↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A standard real [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) relative to a [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_t)$ is an [adapted](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) [stochastic process](../../../../../../stochastic-process-split.md) with $B_0=0$ almost surely, continuous paths almost surely, and

$$
\boxed{B_t-B_s\sim\mathcal N(0,t-s),\qquad B_t-B_s\text{ independent of }\mathcal F_s\quad(0\leq s<t).}
$$

Equivalently, it has [independent increments](../../../../../../independent-increments.md) and [stationary increments](../../../../../../stationary-increments.md), each with a [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) of mean zero and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) equal to the elapsed time, together with continuous paths and the stated initial value. The filtration may be its [natural filtration](../../../../../../natural-filtration.md). A [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) started at $z$ is $z+W_t$ for a standard process $W$; multidimensional standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) has independent standard real coordinate processes. **The normalization is [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) $t$ at time $t$.**
