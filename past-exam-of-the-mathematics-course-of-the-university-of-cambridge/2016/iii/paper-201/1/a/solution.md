<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Work on a [filtered probability space](../../../../../../filtered-probability-space.md) $(\Omega,\mathcal F,(\mathcal F_n)_{n\geq0},\mathbb P)$. A [submartingale](../../../../../../submartingale.md) is a real-valued [stochastic process](../../../../../../stochastic-process-split.md) satisfying the following three conditions: $Y_n$ is $\mathcal F_n$-measurable, $\mathbb E|Y_n|<\infty$, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[Y_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\geq Y_n\quad\text{almost surely}.}
$$

Thus it is [adapted](../../../../../../adapted-process.md), has [integrable random variables](../../../../../../integrable-random-variable.md) at every time, and its conditional expected next value is at least its current value. Repeated conditioning gives $\mathbb E[Y_m\mid\mathcal F_n]\geq Y_n$ for every $m\geq n$. **The inequality concerns [conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md); individual [sample paths](../../../../../../sample-path.md) need not increase.** If no [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) is specified, the definition can be applied to the [natural filtration](../../../../../../natural-filtration.md) generated by the process.
