<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Throughout this question dots denote [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md) derivatives, and $\mathcal H=\dot a/a$ is the [conformal Hubble parameter](../../../../../../conformal-hubble-parameter.md). The monopole of the [photon Boltzmann hierarchy](../../../../../../photon-boltzmann-hierarchy.md) gives $\dot\Theta_0+k\Theta_1/3=\dot\phi$. With the specified velocity convention, the [photon continuity equation](../../../../../../photon-continuity-equation.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\dot\delta_\gamma-\frac43kv_\gamma=4\dot\phi.}
$$

The dipole equation is $\dot\Theta_1+k(2\Theta_2/5-\Theta_0)=\dot\tau(\Theta_1+v_b)+k\psi$. Thus the [photon Euler equation](../../../../../../photon-euler-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dot v_\gamma+\frac{k}{4}\delta_\gamma-\frac{2k}{5}\Theta_2+k\psi=\dot\tau(v_\gamma-v_b).}
$$

In particular, $\dot\tau<0$ makes the scattering term damp the [photon-baryon velocity slip](../../../../../../photon-baryon-velocity-slip.md); it does not amplify it.

The linear momentum density of the [photon-baryon fluid](../../../../../../photon-baryon-fluid.md) is weighted by enthalpy, not merely energy density:

$$
q_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac43\bar\rho_\gamma v_\gamma+\bar\rho_bv_b.
$$

Elastic [Thomson scattering](../../../../../../thomson-scattering.md) exchanges momentum internally. Its [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) force density is $(4\bar\rho_\gamma/3)\dot\tau(v_\gamma-v_b)$, so the [baryon](../../../../../../baryon.md) force density is its negative. Dividing by $\bar\rho_b$ gives the [baryon Euler equation with Thomson drag](../../../../../../baryon-euler-equation-with-thomson-drag.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot v_b+\mathcal H v_b+k\psi=\frac{\dot\tau}{R}(v_b-v_\gamma),\qquad R=\frac{3\bar\rho_b}{4\bar\rho_\gamma}.}
$$

This $R$ is the [baryon loading parameter](../../../../../../baryon-loading-parameter.md), the [baryon](../../../../../../baryon.md)-to-[photon](../../../../../../photon.md) inertia ratio. The two collision terms cancel exactly when the equations are weighted by their enthalpies. Background conservation gives $\bar\rho_b\propto a^{-3}$ and $\bar\rho_\gamma\propto a^{-4}$, so $\dot R=\mathcal H R$.
