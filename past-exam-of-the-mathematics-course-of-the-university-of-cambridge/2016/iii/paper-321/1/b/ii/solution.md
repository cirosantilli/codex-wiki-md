<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use characteristic, vertically averaged quantities at fixed radius. Let $H$ be a disk thickness, $\rho\sim\Sigma/H$, and let the [alpha disk](../../../../../../../alpha-disk.md) prescription give $\mu\sim\alpha p/\Omega$, up to a convention-dependent constant. From the preceding [radiation pressure](../../../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) and constant-[opacity](../../../../../../../opacity.md) balance, $\mu\sim c/\kappa$ is independent of $\Sigma$; thus

$$
p\sim\frac{c\Omega}{\alpha\kappa}.
$$

Vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $p\sim\rho\Omega^2H^2\sim\Sigma\Omega^2H$, hence

$$
H\sim\frac{c}{\alpha\kappa\Omega\Sigma}\propto\Sigma^{-1}.
$$

The vertically averaged [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) satisfies $\bar\nu\Sigma=\int\mu\,dz\sim\mu H$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\bar\nu\Sigma\sim\frac{c^2}{\alpha\kappa^2\Omega}\,\Sigma^{-1}.}
$$

At fixed radius the omitted factors are independent of $\Sigma$, so this is the requested inverse-density scaling of the [viscous torque in an accretion disk](../../../../../../../viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md).

This is an order-of-magnitude vertical model, as requested. Imposing both exact pointwise $\mu=\text{constant}$ and exact pointwise $\mu\propto p(z)$ would force $p$ to be constant, contrary to nontrivial vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md). The characteristic-pressure prescription supplies a scaling, not an exact simultaneous vertical solution to all those idealized equations.
