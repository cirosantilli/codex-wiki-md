<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $W(\Sigma)=\bar\nu\Sigma$. In a small radial patch $r=r_c+\chi$ with disturbance scale much smaller than $r_c$, the leading disk [diffusion equation](../../../../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md) is $\partial_t\Sigma\simeq3\partial_\chi^2W$. Taking the [linearization](../../../../../../../linearization.md) around a uniform [surface density](../../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $\Sigma_c$ gives

$$
\partial_t\delta\Sigma=3W'(\Sigma_c)\partial_\chi^2\delta\Sigma.
$$

For a local [Fourier mode](../../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) $\delta\Sigma\propto e^{st+ik\chi}$,

$$
\boxed{s=-3k^2W'(\Sigma_c)>0,}
$$

because $W\propto\Sigma^{-1}$ has $W'<0$. Thus the effective density [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) is negative and the disk has a [viscous instability of an accretion disk](../../../../../../../viscous-instability-of-an-accretion-disk.md).

A localized overdensity is a maximum of $\Sigma$ but a minimum of $W$. At its center $\partial_\chi^2W>0$, so $\partial_t\Sigma>0$: it grows. The local radial mass flux is proportional to $-\partial_\chi W$, directing matter toward that minimum from both sides. This is the physical feedback: an overdense patch has a smaller [viscous torque in an accretion disk](../../../../../../../viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) and attracts further accumulation. The arbitrarily rapid $k\to\infty$ growth in this ideal negative-diffusion model is not a resolved prediction below the validity scale of the thin-disk and local viscous closure.
