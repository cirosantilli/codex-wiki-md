<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The four equations describe, respectively, vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) in the central object's gravity, the vertical divergence of [radiative flux](../../../../../../../radiative-flux.md) balancing [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md), optically thick [radiative diffusion](../../../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md), and the [equation of state](../../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) combining [ideal gas](../../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) [gas pressure](../../../../../../../gas-pressure.md) with [radiation pressure](../../../../../../../radiation-pressure.md). Here $\mu$ is [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity.md), not the mean molecular weight $\mu_m$; $\kappa$ is the [opacity](../../../../../../../opacity.md), $\sigma$ the [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](../../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-constant.md), and $\Omega$ the local orbital frequency.

In the radiation-pressure limit, $p=4\sigma T^4/(3c)$, so [radiative diffusion](../../../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md) gives

$$
\frac{dp}{dz}=\frac{16\sigma T^3}{3c}\frac{dT}{dz}=-\frac{\kappa\rho F}{c}.
$$

Compare this with vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md). Wherever $\rho\ne0$,

$$
F(z)=\frac{c\Omega^2}{\kappa}z.
$$

At fixed radius, constant [opacity](../../../../../../../opacity.md) makes $dF/dz=c\Omega^2/\kappa$. The [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) equation therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\mu=\frac{4c}{9\kappa},}
$$

independent of height within the optically thick radiation-supported layer. The result assumes the supplied gravity, heating and diffusion equations; optically thin surface corrections are outside that approximation.
