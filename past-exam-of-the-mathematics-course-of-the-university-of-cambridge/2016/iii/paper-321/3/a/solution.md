<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At $x_n=0$ and $y_n=hn$, every radial pair displacement vanishes, so $f_x^n=0$. The particles with indices $n+l$ and $n-l$ give opposite azimuthal accelerations,

$$
\frac{Gm}{h^2}\frac{l}{|l|^3}\mathbf e_y,\qquad -\frac{Gm}{h^2}\frac{l}{|l|^3}\mathbf e_y,
$$

which cancel pairwise. The acceleration sum has [absolute convergence](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) because $\sum_{l\ge1}l^{-2}<\infty$. With zero velocities and $x_n=0$, both the [Coriolis force](../../../../../../coriolis-force.md) and radial [tidal force](../../../../../../tidal-force.md) vanish. **The equally spaced row is therefore an equilibrium of the [infinite gravitating particle lattice](../../../../../../infinite-gravitating-particle-lattice.md).** Its absolute gravitational potential would require an additional reference or regularization; the acceleration used here is well defined.
