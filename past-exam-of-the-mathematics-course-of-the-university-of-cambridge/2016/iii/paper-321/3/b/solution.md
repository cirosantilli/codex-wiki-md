<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\mathbf d_{jn}=h(j-n)\mathbf e_y$ and $\Delta\boldsymbol\xi=\boldsymbol\xi_j-\boldsymbol\xi_n$, with $\boldsymbol\xi_n=(x'_n,y'_n)$. Linearizing the [Newton's law of universal gravitation](../../../../../../newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation.md) uses

$$
\delta\left(\frac{\mathbf d}{|\mathbf d|^3}\right)=\frac1{|\mathbf d|^3}\left(I-3\frac{\mathbf d\mathbf d^T}{|\mathbf d|^2}\right)\Delta\boldsymbol\xi.
$$

For an azimuthal separation, the matrix is $\operatorname{diag}(1,-2)$. After canceling the equilibrium forces, the linearized [shearing sheet](../../../../../../shearing-sheet.md) equations are

$$
\boxed{\ddot x'_n-2\Omega\dot y'_n=3\Omega^2x'_n+\frac{Gm}{h^3}\sum_{j\ne n}\frac{x'_j-x'_n}{|j-n|^3},}
$$



$$
\boxed{\ddot y'_n+2\Omega\dot x'_n=-\frac{2Gm}{h^3}\sum_{j\ne n}\frac{y'_j-y'_n}{|j-n|^3}.}
$$

The transverse gravitational response tends to restore a radial displacement, while the longitudinal response reinforces an azimuthal density concentration. Both sums converge for the bounded [Fourier modes](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) considered next.
