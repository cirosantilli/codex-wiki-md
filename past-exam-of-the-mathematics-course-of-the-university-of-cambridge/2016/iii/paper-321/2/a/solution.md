<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\mathbf u=-(3/2)\Omega x\mathbf e_y+\mathbf v$ and $\rho=\rho_0(1+\delta)$, with $\delta$ and $\mathbf v$ small. Define the [sound speed](../../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) by $c^2=(dp/d\rho)_0$, assumed positive. The background [Keplerian shear](../../../../../../keplerian-shear.md) balances the radial [Coriolis force](../../../../../../coriolis-force.md) and the [shearing-sheet tidal potential](../../../../../../shearing-sheet-tidal-potential.md). For disturbances preserving [axisymmetry](../../../../../../axisymmetric-vector-field.md) $\partial_y=0$, its advective derivative $\mathbf u_0\cdot\nabla$ vanishes, but $\mathbf v\cdot\nabla\mathbf u_0=-(3/2)\Omega v_x\mathbf e_y$ remains. The linearized [Euler equations](../../../../../../euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) and [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\partial_t v_x-2\Omega v_y&=-c^2\partial_x\delta,\\
\partial_t v_y+\frac\Omega2v_x&=0,\\
\partial_t v_z&=-c^2\partial_z\delta,\\
\partial_t\delta+\partial_xv_x+\partial_zv_z&=0.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The azimuthal coefficient is $(2-3/2)\Omega=\Omega/2$, not $2\Omega$: it combines the background velocity gradient with the [Coriolis force](../../../../../../coriolis-force.md). The uniform background has no buoyancy or vertical stratification in this stated model.
