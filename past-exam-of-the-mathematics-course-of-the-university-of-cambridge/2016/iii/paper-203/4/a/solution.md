<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [conformal automorphism of the upper half-plane](../../../../../../conformal-automorphism-of-the-upper-half-plane.md) is a real [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md)

$$
\phi(z)=\frac{az+b}{cz+d},\qquad a,b,c,d\in\mathbb R,
\quad ad-bc>0,
$$

with coefficients determined up to a common nonzero factor. The boundary interval $(\infty,1)$ is the oriented real boundary arc from infinity to $1$, namely the finite real interval $(-\infty,1)$. The map must take the left endpoint $-1$ to infinity and the right endpoint infinity to $1$, while fixing zero. These three boundary values determine the [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) uniquely:

$$
\boxed{\phi(z)=\frac{z}{1+z}.}
$$

Its determinant is positive, $\operatorname{Im}\phi(z)=\operatorname{Im}z/|1+z|^2>0$, and on $(-1,\infty)$ its derivative is $(1+x)^{-2}>0$. Its endpoint limits are $-\infty$ at $-1+$ and $1$ at $+\infty$, so it has exactly the required boundary extension.
