<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [half-plane-capacity parameterization](../../../../../../half-plane-capacity-parameterization.md), let $W$ be standard real [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) and put $\xi_t=\sqrt\kappa\,W_t$. Solve the [Chordal Loewner equation](../../../../../../chordal-loewner-equation.md)

$$
\partial_tg_t(z)=\frac2{g_t(z)-\xi_t},\qquad g_0(z)=z.
$$

The points for which this flow ceases to exist form the growing [compact H-hulls](../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md) $K_t$, with [half-plane capacity](../../../../../../half-plane-capacity.md) $2t$. The process is an [SLE](../../../../../../schramm-loewner-evolution.md) trace when its initial segments generate these hulls and

$$
\boxed{\gamma_t=\lim_{y\downarrow0}g_t^{-1}(\xi_t+iy),
\qquad \xi_t=\sqrt\kappa\,W_t.}
$$

The limit is the continuous [trace of a Loewner chain](../../../../../../trace-of-a-loewner-chain.md) started at zero. If the curve disconnects a region from infinity, that region is included in $K_t$; the hull is not generally just the visited curve. For $\kappa=0$ the driver is identically zero and the trace is the deterministic vertical slit $\gamma_t=2i\sqrt t$.
