<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [mapping-out function of a compact H-hull](../../../../../../mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md) is the unique [conformal bijection](../../../../../../biholomorphism.md) from $H=\mathbb H\setminus K$ onto the [complex upper half-plane](../../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) with [hydrodynamic normalization at infinity](../../../../../../hydrodynamic-normalization-at-infinity.md):

$$
\boxed{g_K:H\to\mathbb H,\qquad
g_K(z)=z+\frac{a_K}{z}+O(|z|^{-2})\quad(z\to\infty).}
$$

The nonnegative coefficient $a_K$ is the [half-plane capacity](../../../../../../half-plane-capacity.md) of $K$. The leading coefficient one and constant term zero remove the affine ambiguity of a half-plane map. In the [compact H-hull](../../../../../../compact-h-hull.md) convention, $K$ is bounded and relatively closed in $\mathbb H$, and its complement is a [simply connected domain](../../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md); boundary attachment points can be included in its closure.

We will use these standard properties explicitly: $g_K$ and its inverse extend holomorphically by [Schwarz reflection principle](../../../../../../schwarz-reflection-principle.md) across real intervals outside the hull's real attachment region; their real boundary restrictions are strictly increasing; and their normalized [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) hold in a full reflected neighbourhood of infinity. In particular, when $K$ lies in the unit disc, these extensions exist across $(-\infty,-1)$ and $(1,\infty)$.
