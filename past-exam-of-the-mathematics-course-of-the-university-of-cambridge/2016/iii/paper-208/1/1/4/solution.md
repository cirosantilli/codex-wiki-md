The root-reflected, invertible [autoregressive moving-average model](../../../../../../../autoregressive-moving-average-model.md) is

$$
\boxed{X_t-\tfrac14X_{t-1}=\varepsilon_t-\tfrac13\varepsilon_{t-1},\qquad\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_t)=9.}
$$

This follows by multiplying the filter identity for $\varepsilon$ by $1-B/3$. Its moving-average root $3$ and autoregressive root $4$ are both outside the unit disk. Thus it is the representation in terms of the [innovation process](../../../../../../../innovation-process.md), rather than merely another noise representation with the same spectrum.
