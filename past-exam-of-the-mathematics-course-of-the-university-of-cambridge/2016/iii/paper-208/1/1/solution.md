A [weak white noise](../../../../../../weak-white-noise.md) is a sequence with mean zero, a common finite [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, and $\operatorname{Cov}(\eta_s,\eta_t)=0$ for $s\ne t$. A [strong white noise](../../../../../../strong-white-noise.md) is a sequence of [independent and identically distributed random variables](../../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with mean zero and finite [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md). [Strong white noise](../../../../../../strong-white-noise.md) is therefore [weak white noise](../../../../../../weak-white-noise.md), but the converse need not hold. Neither definition, by itself, requires a [Gaussian distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md).
