<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An [isomorphism of schemes](../../../../../../isomorphism-of-schemes.md) over $\mathbb R$ induces a bijection on [rational points](../../../../../../rational-point.md). A real point of this [projective plane curve](../../../../../../projective-plane-curve.md) would be represented by a nonzero real triple with $t_0^2+t_1^2+t_2^2=0$. Each summand is nonnegative, so every coordinate would vanish. Thus $X(\mathbb R)=\varnothing$, whereas $[1:0]\in\mathbb P^1(\mathbb R)$. **The two real schemes are not isomorphic.** The obstruction is the [real conic without real rational points](../../../../../../real-conic-without-real-rational-points.md); nonsingularity alone does not make a real conic a projective line over its ground field.
