<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**The first implication is false.** On the [Noetherian scheme](../../../../../../noetherian-scheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}k[t]$, the [short exact sequence of sheaves](../../../../../../short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X\xrightarrow{\,t\,}\mathcal O_X\longrightarrow\widetilde{k[t]/(t)}\longrightarrow0
$$

has locally free first and middle terms. The quotient is not [locally free](../../../../../../locally-free-sheaf.md): at $(t)$ it is a nonzero module annihilated by $t$, whereas a free module over the [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md) $k[t]_{(t)}$ has no such element.

**The second implication is true.** Near any point trivialize $\mathcal F$ and $\mathcal H$. Choose a finite local basis of $\mathcal H$. Surjectivity as a sheaf gives local lifts of its basis sections; shrink to the intersection of their finitely many neighbourhoods. The lifts define a splitting $\mathcal H\to\mathcal G$ on that neighbourhood. Thus $\mathcal G\cong\mathcal F\oplus\mathcal H$ there and is [locally free](../../../../../../locally-free-sheaf.md). This is [local splitting when a quotient sheaf is locally free](../../../../../../local-splitting-when-a-quotient-sheaf-is-locally-free.md); no global splitting is asserted.
