↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [empirical characteristic function](../../../../../../empirical-characteristic-function.md) replaces the [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value.md) in a [characteristic function](../../../../../../characteristic-function.md) by the sample average:

$$
\boxed{\varphi_n(u)=\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^n e^{iuX_j},\qquad u\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Its centered fluctuation at the [central limit theorem](../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) scale is the [empirical characteristic process](../../../../../../empirical-characteristic-process.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathcal C_n(u)=\sqrt n\bigl(\varphi_n(u)-\varphi(u)\bigr),\qquad
\varphi(u)=\mathbb E e^{iuX_1}.}
$$

In particular, $\varphi_n(0)=1$ and $\mathcal C_n(0)=0$.
