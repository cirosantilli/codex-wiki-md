<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $T=1/(2\pi\alpha')$ and take the closed-string spatial coordinate periodic with period $2\pi$. One convenient Lorentzian convention for the [conformal gauge](../../../../../../conformal-gauge.md) action is

$$
\boxed{I=\frac1{4\pi\alpha'}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\left(\dot X^m\dot X_m-X'^mX'_m\right)
+\frac{i}{2\pi}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\left[b(\partial_\tau-\partial_\sigma)c+\widetilde b(\partial_\tau+\partial_\sigma)\widetilde c\right].}
$$

The two anticommuting [bc ghost systems](../../../../../../bc-system.md) are the [Faddeev-Popov ghosts](../../../../../../faddeev-popov-ghost.md) of the [worldsheet](../../../../../../worldsheet.md) metric [gauge fixing](../../../../../../gauge-fixing.md). Their fields have conformal weights $2$ and $-1$. Ghost normalizations can be rescaled without changing the algebra. After a [Wick rotation](../../../../../../wick-rotation.md) the two terms become the familiar holomorphic and antiholomorphic $b\bar\partial c$ and $\widetilde b\partial\widetilde c$ actions.

Let $\mathcal L_n$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal L}_n$ include both matter and ghost contributions. With the conventional plane normalization of the zero mode, their [Virasoro algebra](../../../../../../virasoro-algebra.md) is

$$
\boxed{[\mathcal L_m,\mathcal L_n]=(m-n)\mathcal L_{m+n}+\frac{D-26}{12}(m^3-m)\delta_{m+n,0},}
$$



$$
\boxed{[\widetilde{\mathcal L}_m,\widetilde{\mathcal L}_n]=(m-n)\widetilde{\mathcal L}_{m+n}+\frac{D-26}{12}(m^3-m)\delta_{m+n,0},\qquad[\mathcal L_m,\widetilde{\mathcal L}_n]=0.}
$$

Each free coordinate boson contributes [central charge](../../../../../../central-charge.md) $1$, including the timelike coordinate after the usual analytic continuation. The weight-$(2,-1)$ [bc ghost system](../../../../../../bc-system.md) contributes $-26$ in each chiral sector. Thus the total [central charge](../../../../../../central-charge.md) is $D-26$.

The [Virasoro constraints](../../../../../../virasoro-constraint.md) originate in a gauge symmetry. A nonzero central term is a quantum obstruction to realizing that gauge symmetry without an anomaly; equivalently, the [BRST charge](../../../../../../brst-charge.md) fails to be nilpotent. Cancellation of the [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](../../../../../../worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) therefore requires

$$
\boxed{D=26.}
$$

This is the [critical dimension of the bosonic string](../../../../../../critical-dimension-of-string-theory.md). The accompanying physical-state normal-ordering condition gives the usual matter intercept $1$ in each sector. A shift of the zero-mode convention changes the linear term in the central polynomial, but cannot change the required cancellation of its cubic coefficient.
