# 3

↑ **Parent:** [Paper 304](../paper-304-split.md)

Set $P(z)=W'(z)$ and $\overline P(\bar z)=\overline{W'(z)}$, treating $z,\bar z$ as independent coordinates for [Wirtinger derivatives](../../../../wirtinger-derivatives.md). Use [left Grassmann derivatives](../../../../left-grassmann-derivative.md), so $\partial_{\theta_2}(\theta_1\theta_2)=-\theta_1$. The given odd vector field acts by $V_1z=\theta_1$, $V_1\theta_2=-\overline P$, and zero on $\theta_1,\bar z,\overline\theta_i$. Consequently its square vanishes on every coordinate. The square of an odd [graded derivation](../../../../graded-derivation.md) is an even [graded derivation](../../../../graded-derivation.md), so **the [nilpotent operator](../../../../nilpotent-linear-map.md) property holds on every function**:

$$
\boxed{\{V_1,V_1\}=2V_1^2=0.}
$$

For the action in this [zero-dimensional supersymmetric field theory](../../../../zero-dimensional-supersymmetric-field-theory.md), $\partial_zS=P'\overline P-P''\theta_1\theta_2$ and $\partial_{\theta_2}S=P'\theta_1$. The cubic term vanishes after multiplication by $\theta_1$, and the other two terms cancel:

$$
\boxed{V_1S=\theta_1P'\overline P-\overline P P'\theta_1=0.}
$$

**Three further independent odd symmetries** are

$$
\boxed{V_2=\theta_2\partial_z+\overline P\,\partial_{\theta_1},\qquad\overline V_1=\overline\theta_1\partial_{\bar z}-P\,\partial_{\overline\theta_2},\qquad\overline V_2=\overline\theta_2\partial_{\bar z}+P\,\partial_{\overline\theta_1}.}
$$

For $V_2$, use $\partial_{\theta_1}S=-P'\theta_2$; the same cancellation proves invariance. The barred calculations exchange barred and unbarred variables and coefficients. Each [graded derivation](../../../../graded-derivation.md) is also a [nilpotent operator](../../../../nilpotent-linear-map.md), by its coordinate action. Their different odd coefficients and coordinate derivatives make them linearly independent. These are [odd symmetries of a zero-dimensional polynomial model](../../../../odd-symmetries-of-a-zero-dimensional-polynomial-model.md).
