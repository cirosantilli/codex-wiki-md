<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [odd symmetries of a zero-dimensional polynomial model](../../../../../../odd-symmetries-of-a-zero-dimensional-polynomial-model.md) preserve both the action and the flat integration measure: their coefficients have zero superdivergence. The confining polynomial weight removes boundary terms at infinity. Thus the integral of an odd-symmetry derivative is zero, a [supersymmetric Ward identity](../../../../../../supersymmetric-ward-identity.md) for this finite-dimensional integral in this [zero-dimensional supersymmetric field theory](../../../../../../zero-dimensional-supersymmetric-field-theory.md).

Since $g(z)$ is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md), the barred symmetry obeys $\overline V_1(\overline\theta_2g(z))=-P(z)g(z)$. Applying the [supersymmetric Ward identity](../../../../../../supersymmetric-ward-identity.md) gives **the requested unnormalized insertion**:

$$
\boxed{\langle W'(z)g(z)\rangle=\langle P(z)g(z)\rangle=0.}
$$

One can also verify the result after [Berezin integration](../../../../../../berezin-integral.md), without invoking the symmetry terminology:

$$
\int_{\mathbb C}d^2z\,|P'|^2P g\,e^{-|P|^2}=-\int_{\mathbb C}d^2z\,g P'\,\partial_{\bar z}e^{-|P|^2}=0.
$$

The final [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) uses $\partial_{\bar z}(gP')=0$ and rapid decay. The derivative on $W'$ in the insertion is essential.
