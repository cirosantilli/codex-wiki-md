<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Every generalized frequency in the [inviscid Couette continuous spectrum](../../../../../../../inviscid-couette-continuous-spectrum.md) is real: $\omega_\xi=\alpha\xi$ for real $\alpha,\xi$. Thus every time factor has unit modulus and **there is no exponentially growing normal mode**:

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}\omega_\xi=0.}
$$

The vorticity equation also gives $q(z,t)=q_0(z)e^{-i\alpha zt}$, preserving its $L^2$ norm. For fixed nonzero $\alpha$, the bounded [Dirichlet Green function](../../../../../../../dirichlet-green-function.md) inversion therefore gives a uniform bound on the reconstructed velocity in terms of the initial vorticity norm. Superpositions can still rearrange their velocity energy and display [transient growth from non-normal modes](../../../../../../../transient-growth-from-non-normal-modes.md); neutral [hydrodynamic stability](../../../../../../../hydrodynamic-stability-split.md) does not require every velocity component to decrease monotonically. Treating only smooth discrete modes would miss this continuous neutral family.
