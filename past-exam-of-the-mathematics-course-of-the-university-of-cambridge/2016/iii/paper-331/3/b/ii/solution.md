<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [normal mode](../../../../../../../normal-mode.md), $\partial_t\mapsto-i\omega$, $\partial_x\mapsto ik$, and $\partial_x^2\mapsto-k^2$. Substitution into the [linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation](../../../../../../../linear-complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) gives

$$
-i\omega+ikU-\mu+(1+ic_d)k^2=0.
$$

Therefore **the dispersion relation is**

$$
\boxed{D(k,\omega)=-i\omega+ikU-\mu+(1+ic_d)k^2=0,\qquad
\omega=Uk+(c_d-i)k^2+i\mu.}
$$

For real $k$, its [temporal growth rate](../../../../../../../growth-rate.md) is $\omega_i=\mu-k^2$, with unique maximum $\mu$ at $k=0$. Its real [angular frequency](../../../../../../../angular-frequency.md) is $Uk+c_dk^2$, displaying the separate [advection](../../../../../../../advection.md) and [wave dispersion](../../../../../../../wave-dispersion.md) terms.
