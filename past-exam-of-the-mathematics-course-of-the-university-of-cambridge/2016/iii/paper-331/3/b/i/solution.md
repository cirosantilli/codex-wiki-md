↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the [linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation](../../../../../../../linear-complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md), take $U,c_d,\mu$ real. **$U$ gives [advection](../../../../../../../advection.md)**, carrying the envelope downstream at speed $U$. **$c_d$ gives [wave dispersion](../../../../../../../wave-dispersion.md)**: the imaginary part of the second-derivative coefficient changes wave phase without directly dissipating a real-[wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) [Fourier mode](../../../../../../../fourier-mode.md). **$\mu$ gives local linear production or damping**: a spatially uniform perturbation grows for $\mu>0$ and decays for $\mu<0$.

The real part of the diffusion coefficient is the scaled value one. It smooths the envelope and damps a real-[wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) [Fourier mode](../../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) at rate $k^2$. Thus the [linear complex Ginzburg-Landau equation](../../../../../../../linear-complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md) models competing [advection](../../../../../../../advection.md), smoothing through a [diffusion equation](../../../../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md), [wave dispersion](../../../../../../../wave-dispersion.md) and local growth, rather than nonlinear saturation.
