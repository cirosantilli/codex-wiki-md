↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under the convention $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$, a mode's amplitude grows as $e^{\omega_i t}$. The [linear stability analysis](../../../../../../../linear-stability.md) of real-[wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) disturbances is therefore summarized by

$$
\boxed{\omega_{i,\max}<0:\ \text{strictly stable},\qquad
\omega_{i,\max}>0:\ \text{linearly unstable}.}
$$

At $\omega_{i,\max}=0$ there is marginal or neutral [hydrodynamic stability](../../../../../../../hydrodynamic-stability-split.md): no [normal mode](../../../../../../../normal-mode.md) grows exponentially, but some may fail to decay. If “linearly stable” includes neutral modes, its condition is $\omega_{i,\max}\leq0$. These modal statements do not rule out [transient growth from non-normal modes](../../../../../../../transient-growth-from-non-normal-modes.md) or algebraic growth.
