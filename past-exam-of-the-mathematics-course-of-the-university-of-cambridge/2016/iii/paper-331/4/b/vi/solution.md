<h1 id="4/b/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Both [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the linear generator, $-1/R$ and $-1/(4R)$, are negative. Nevertheless the [non-normal matrix](../../../../../../../non-normal-matrix.md) transfers the slowly decaying second component into the first before viscous decay wins. **Modal decay coexists with energy amplification of order $R^2$ over a time of order $R$.**

This illustrates [transient growth from non-normal modes](../../../../../../../transient-growth-from-non-normal-modes.md): large [Reynolds number](../../../../../../../reynolds-number.md) permits much greater disturbance amplification even though the [linear stability analysis](../../../../../../../linear-stability.md) finds no unstable [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md). The [energy-neutral rotational nonlinearity](../../../../../../../energy-neutral-rotational-nonlinearity.md) can redirect the amplified state and affect its subsequent evolution. The linear gain alone establishes neither sustained nonlinear instability nor a nonlinear transition threshold.
