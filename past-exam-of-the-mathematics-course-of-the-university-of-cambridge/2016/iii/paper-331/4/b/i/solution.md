<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) $N$ is a [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md), so $x^TNx=0$ for real $x$. Therefore the [energy-neutral rotational nonlinearity](../../../../../../../energy-neutral-rotational-nonlinearity.md) contributes nothing directly to the energy balance:

$$
\boxed{\frac{dE}{dt}=x^T\dot x=|x|x^TNx+x^TLx=x^TLx
=-\frac{x_1^2}{R}+x_1x_2-\frac{x_2^2}{4R},\qquad R=\mathrm{Re}.}
$$

The nonlinear term rotates the state without changing its [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md). It can still change the orientation, and thereby affect later energy growth through the linear term. Thus energy cancellation does not mean the nonlinear and linear trajectories have identical energy histories.
