<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By the [canonical colimit presentation of a covariant set-valued functor](../../../../../../canonical-colimit-presentation-of-a-covariant-set-valued-functor.md) established in the first part,

$$
F\cong\operatorname*{colim}_{(A,x)\in(1\downarrow F)^{\mathrm{op}}}\mathcal C(A,-).
$$

Each vertex is a [representable functor](../../../../../../representable-functor.md). Under condition $(iii)$ the indexing [category of elements](../../../../../../category-of-elements.md), with its arrows reversed, is a [filtered category](../../../../../../filtered-category.md), so this is a [filtered colimit in a category](../../../../../../filtered-colimit-in-a-category.md). Therefore **$(iii)\Rightarrow(iv)$**, with the requested diagram itself providing the filtered presentation.
