<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A future [trapped surface](../../../../../../trapped-surface.md) is a smooth compact spacelike two-surface without boundary whose two future-directed orthogonal [null expansions](../../../../../../null-expansion.md) are strictly negative. In four-dimensional [general relativity](../../../../../../general-relativity-split.md), the [Penrose singularity theorem](../../../../../../penrose-singularity-theorem.md) states: a time-oriented [globally hyperbolic spacetime](../../../../../../globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md) with a noncompact [Cauchy hypersurface](../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md), a [trapped surface](../../../../../../trapped-surface.md), and the [null convergence condition](../../../../../../null-convergence-condition.md) $R_{ab}k^ak^b\ge0$ for every [null vector](../../../../../../null-vector.md) is future [null-geodesically incomplete](../../../../../../null-geodesic-incompleteness.md). With the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md), the [null energy condition](../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md) implies this [null convergence condition](../../../../../../null-convergence-condition.md); a [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) drops out of the null contraction.

The [Kruskal spacetime](../../../../../../kruskal-spacetime.md) is an example. In its [black hole](../../../../../../black-hole.md) interior, use future null normals in [Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../../../ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md):

$$
l=-\partial_r,\qquad k=\partial_v+\frac f2\partial_r,\qquad g(l,k)=-1.
$$

For a round sphere of [areal radius](../../../../../../areal-radius.md) $r$, its area is $4\pi r^2$ and its [null expansions](../../../../../../null-expansion.md) are

$$
\theta_l=\frac{2l(r)}r=-\frac2r,\qquad \theta_k=\frac{2k(r)}r=\frac fr.
$$

Both are negative for $0<r<2M$. The vacuum [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) give $R_{ab}=0$, and a two-ended Kruskal [Cauchy hypersurface](../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md) is noncompact. The future [radial null geodesics](../../../../../../radial-null-geodesic.md) reaching $r=0$ in finite [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) provide precisely the incompleteness predicted by the [Penrose singularity theorem](../../../../../../penrose-singularity-theorem.md). A [trapped surface](../../../../../../trapped-surface.md) at $r<2M$ is strictly trapped; the horizon sphere has one zero [null expansion](../../../../../../null-expansion.md) instead.
