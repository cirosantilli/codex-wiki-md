<h1 id="2/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md) says that, for every future [timelike vector](../../../../../../../timelike-vector.md) $u^a$, the energy current $J^a=-T^a{}_b u^b$ is future causal or zero. Choose [normal coordinates](../../../../../../../normal-coordinates.md) whose future unit time vector at $p$ is $u^a=(1,0,0,0)$. With [metric signature](../../../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$ and symmetric [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md),

$$
J^0=T_{00},\qquad J^i=-T_{i0}.
$$

A vector with these components is future causal or zero exactly when

$$
\boxed{T_{00}\ge\sqrt{\sum_iT_{0i}^2}.}
$$

This proves necessity. Conversely, every future unit [timelike vector](../../../../../../../timelike-vector.md) can be made the time axis of an orthonormal frame by a proper orthochronous [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md), and that frame can be extended to [normal coordinates](../../../../../../../normal-coordinates.md) at $p$. Requiring the displayed inequality in every such chart therefore gives the [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md) for every future [timelike vector](../../../../../../../timelike-vector.md); positive rescaling handles nonunit vectors. Future [null vectors](../../../../../../../null-vector.md) follow by continuity if they are included in the definition. **The condition must hold in every local Lorentz frame; one chart alone is insufficient.**
