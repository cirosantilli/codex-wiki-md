<h1 id="2/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the [P(X, phi) scalar field theory](../../../../../../../p-x-phi-scalar-field-theory.md) specialized to $F(X)$, vary the inverse [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) while holding $\phi$ fixed. With $q_a=\partial_a\phi$,

$$
\delta X=-\frac12q_aq_b\delta g^{ab},\qquad \delta\sqrt{-g}=-\frac12\sqrt{-g}\,g_{ab}\delta g^{ab}.
$$

Using $T_{ab}=-2(-g)^{-1/2}\delta S/\delta g^{ab}$ yields

$$
\boxed{T_{ab}=F'(X)q_aq_b+F(X)g_{ab}.}
$$

In any local [Lorentz frame](../../../../../../../lorentz-frame.md), set $A=F'(X)\ge0$, $Y=XF'(X)-F(X)\ge0$, $s=\sum_iq_i^2$, and $X=(q_0^2-s)/2$. Then

$$
\rho=T_{00}=Aq_0^2-F=\frac A2(q_0^2+s)+Y\ge0,\qquad T_{0i}=Aq_0q_i.
$$

A direct calculation gives

$$
\boxed{\rho^2-\sum_iT_{0i}^2=A^2X^2+A(q_0^2+s)Y+Y^2\ge0.}
$$

Hence $\rho\ge\sqrt{T_{0i}T_{0i}}$ in every local [Lorentz frame](../../../../../../../lorentz-frame.md), proving the [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md). This proof covers timelike, null, spacelike, and zero $\nabla\phi$ alike; it does not assume $X>0$ or use a [perfect fluid in general relativity](../../../../../../../perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) rest frame. The hypotheses need only hold at every attained value of $X$; the stated all-$X$ assumptions are sufficient. For example, $F=X-V_0$, $V_0\ge0$, satisfies them.
