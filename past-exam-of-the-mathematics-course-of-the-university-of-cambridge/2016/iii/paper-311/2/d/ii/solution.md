<h1 id="2/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In an orthonormal frame, $F_{0i}=-E_i$ and $F_{ij}=\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$. The [electromagnetic stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../../electromagnetic-stress-energy-tensor.md) gives

$$
T_{00}=\frac{E^2+B^2}{8\pi},\qquad T_{0i}=-\frac{(\mathbf E\times\mathbf B)_i}{4\pi}.
$$

The sign of $T_{0i}$ follows directly from $F_{0j}F_i{}^j=-E_j\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$. Thus

$$
T_{00}^2-\sum_iT_{0i}^2
=\frac{(E^2-B^2)^2+4(\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf B)^2}{64\pi^2}\ge0,
$$

and $T_{00}\ge0$. These formulas hold in every local [Lorentz frame](../../../../../../../lorentz-frame.md), so the criterion just proved establishes the [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md). Equality occurs for a nonzero null [Maxwell field](../../../../../../../electromagnetic-field.md) with $E^2=B^2$ and $\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf B=0$, whose energy current is [null vector](../../../../../../../null-vector.md).
