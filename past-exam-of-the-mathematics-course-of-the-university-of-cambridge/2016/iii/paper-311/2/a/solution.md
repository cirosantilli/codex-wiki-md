<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Locally write a smooth [hypersurface](../../../../../../hypersurface.md) as $\Phi=0$ with $n_a=\nabla_a\Phi\ne0$. It is a [null hypersurface](../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md) when $n_an^a=0$ on it. Then $n^a$ is both normal and tangent, and the [induced metric](../../../../../../induced-metric.md) is degenerate along $n^a$.

Because [covariant derivatives](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) commute on a scalar,

$$
n^b\nabla_bn_a=n^b\nabla_an_b=\frac12\nabla_a(n_bn^b).
$$

The scalar $n^2$ vanishes on the [hypersurface](../../../../../../hypersurface.md), so its derivative annihilates every tangent direction there. Its derivative is consequently proportional to $n_a$: locally $n^2=\Phi q$ for a smooth function $q$, giving

$$
\boxed{n^b\nabla_bn^a=\kappa n^a,\qquad \kappa=q/2\text{ on }\Phi=0.}
$$

Thus the normal generates [null pregeodesics](../../../../../../null-pregeodesic.md). Rescale $k^a=\alpha n^a$, choosing $n^b\nabla_b\log\alpha=-\kappa$, to get $k^b\nabla_bk^a=0$. These are the affinely parametrized generators of the [null hypersurface](../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md). The proportionality need not vanish: setting $n^2=0$ only on the [hypersurface](../../../../../../hypersurface.md) does not set its full transverse derivative to zero.
