<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Work in the [Boyer-Lindquist coordinates](../../../../../../../boyer-lindquist-coordinates.md) exterior $r>r_+=M+\sqrt{M^2-a^2}$, away from the axes. There $\Delta>0$, $\Sigma>0$, and

$$
g_{\phi\phi}=\frac{\sin^2\theta}{\Sigma}\left[(r^2+a^2)\Sigma+2Mra^2\sin^2\theta\right]>0.
$$

Inverting the $t,\phi$ block of the [Kerr metric](../../../../../../../kerr-metric.md) gives

$$
g^{tt}=-\frac{g_{\phi\phi}}D<0,\qquad D=g_{t\phi}^2-g_{tt}g_{\phi\phi}.
$$

Thus $W^a=-g^{ab}(dt)_b=-\nabla^at$ has $g(W,W)=g^{tt}<0$, and the smooth nonvanishing [timelike vector field](../../../../../../../timelike-vector-field.md) $W$ defines a [time orientation](../../../../../../../time-orientation.md). Choose it future-directed, matching increasing $t$ near infinity. For every nonzero future [causal vector](../../../../../../../causal-vector.md) $Z$, the [Lorentzian inner product](../../../../../../../lorentzian-inner-product.md) satisfies $g(W,Z)<0$, hence

$$
\boxed{dt(Z)=-g(W,Z)>0.}
$$

So $t$ increases strictly along every regular future [causal curve](../../../../../../../causal-curve.md) in this exterior. Notice that $\partial_t$ itself can be spacelike in the [Kerr ergoregion](../../../../../../../kerr-ergoregion.md); the timelike object used here is $-\nabla t$. Direct substitution also gives $D=\Delta\sin^2\theta$.
