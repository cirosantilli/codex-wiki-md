<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a closed universe, the spatial [Cauchy hypersurface](../../../../../../../cauchy-surface.md) is compact without boundary. There is consequently no asymptotic surface term in the gravitational [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../../hamiltonian.md). On solutions, the [Hamiltonian constraint](../../../../../../../hamiltonian-constraint.md) and [momentum constraint](../../../../../../../momentum-constraint.md) give

$$
\boxed{H=0.}
$$

This is the [vanishing canonical Hamiltonian of a closed universe](../../../../../../../vanishing-canonical-hamiltonian-of-a-closed-universe.md): evolution generated by lapse and shift is a [gauge transformation](../../../../../../../gauge-transformation.md), and there is no asymptotic time-translation charge. It does not mean the local [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md) or [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) is zero. In an asymptotically flat universe the same bulk [constraint equations in field theory](../../../../../../../constraint-equation-in-field-theory.md) hold, but a boundary [Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy](../../../../../../../arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md) can be nonzero.
