<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Define [Outgoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../../../../outgoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md) by $u=t-r_*$. The radial [Schwarzschild metric](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) becomes

$$
ds^2=-f\,du^2-2\,du\,dr.
$$

Outgoing radio rays have constant $u$. Two nearby wave crests emitted at Alice's [proper times](../../../../../../../proper-time.md) $\tau$ and $\tau+d\tau$ have separation $du=\dot u_A\,d\tau$. At Bob's fixed radius $r_B$, $dt_B=du$ and $d\tau_B=\sqrt{f_B}\,du$, where $f_B=1-2M/r_B$. Thus the exact local [redshift](../../../../../../../redshift.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\lambda_B}{\lambda_A}=\frac{d\tau_B}{d\tau_A}=\sqrt{f_B}\,\frac{du_A}{d\tau_A}.}
$$

Here the [wavelength](../../../../../../../wavelength.md) ratio equals the [proper time](../../../../../../../proper-time.md) period ratio in [geometric optics](../../../../../../../geometrical-optics.md). Equivalently, the phase gradient $k_a=-\omega_\infty(du)_a$ gives Alice's measured [frequency](../../../../../../../frequency.md) $\omega_A=-k_aU_A^a=\omega_\infty\dot u_A$ and Bob's $\omega_B=\omega_\infty/\sqrt{f_B}$.

**In the asymptotically distant-observer limit $f_B\to1$, this becomes $\lambda_B/\lambda_A=du_A/d\tau_A$.** “Far away” is the approximation behind the printed formula; at finite $r_B$ the factor $\sqrt{f_B}$ remains.
