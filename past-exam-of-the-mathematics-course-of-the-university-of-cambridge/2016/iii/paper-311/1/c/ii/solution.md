<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For radial free fall, let $E=f\dot t_A>0$ be Alice's conserved [Killing energy](../../../../../../../killing-energy.md). The inward branch of the [timelike geodesic](../../../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) has $\dot r=-\sqrt{E^2-f}$, hence

$$
\dot u_A=\dot t_A-\frac{\dot r}{f}=\frac{E+\sqrt{E^2-f}}{f}\sim\frac{2E}{f}.
$$

With $\delta=r-2M$, the near-horizon [geodesic equations](../../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) give

$$
f\sim\frac{\delta}{2M},\quad \delta\sim E(\tau_H-\tau),\quad t_A=-2M\log(\tau_H-\tau)+O(1),\quad r_* =2M\log(\tau_H-\tau)+O(1).
$$

The constants in the logarithms are understood to make their arguments dimensionless. Therefore $u_A=t_A-r_*=2t_A+O(1)$. For an outgoing signal reaching the fixed-radius Bob, $t_B=u_A+r_*(r_B)$, so $t_B=2t_A+O(1)$. The [redshift](../../../../../../../redshift.md) consequently behaves as

$$
\frac{\lambda_B}{\lambda_A}\propto e^{t_B/(4M)},\qquad \boxed{M=\frac1{4a}}.
$$

This is the answer when $t$ is Bob's [Schwarzschild time](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-time.md), as appropriate to the stated observation. It is the [Schwarzschild surface gravity](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-surface-gravity.md) $a=\kappa=1/(4M)$. If $t$ instead means the emission [Schwarzschild time](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-time.md) $t_A$, the same [redshift](../../../../../../../redshift.md) is proportional to $e^{t_A/(2M)}$ and gives $M=1/(2a)$. The two answers use different clocks, not different dynamics. If the measured exponential uses Bob's [proper time](../../../../../../../proper-time.md) $\tau_B$, its rate is $1/(4M\sqrt{f_B})$. In SI units the reception-time result is $M=c^3/(4Ga)$.

The reception-time exponent also holds for smooth radial infall crossing the future [Schwarzschild event horizon](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-event-horizon.md) with finite nonzero $dr/d\tau$: regular [Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../../../../ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md) give finite $v$ there, $u=v-2r_*\sim-4M\log(\tau_H-\tau)$. Thus no special value of $E$ is needed.
