<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The radial [null geodesics](../../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) satisfy

$$
0=dv(-f\,dv+2\,dr).
$$

The ingoing family has $v=\text{constant}$, with $r$ decreasing toward the future; its tangent $-\partial_r$ is affinely parametrized because $\Gamma^a{}_{rr}=0$. The outgoing family has

$$
\boxed{\frac{dr}{dv}=\frac f2.}
$$

Away from the [Schwarzschild event horizon](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-event-horizon.md), integration gives $v=2r_*+\text{constant}$. These are [null geodesics](../../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) up to reparametrization: a null direction in the two-dimensional radial geometry is automatically [pregeodesic](../../../../../../../pregeodesic.md), and the angular [geodesic equations](../../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) are satisfied by constant angles. On the [Schwarzschild event horizon](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-event-horizon.md) the outgoing family instead has $r=2M$, with tangent proportional to $\partial_v$.

In a [Finkelstein diagram](../../../../../../../finkelstein-diagram.md), plot $T=v-r$ vertically and $r$ horizontally. Ingoing rays obey $T+r=\text{constant}$; outgoing rays obey

$$
T=r+4M\log\left|\frac r{2M}-1\right|+\text{constant}.
$$

Outside the [Schwarzschild event horizon](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-event-horizon.md), outgoing rays increase $r$; on it they remain at $r=2M$; inside it they decrease $r$ even though $v$ increases. Thus both radial future [null directions](../../../../../../../null-directions.md) point toward smaller $r$ inside the [black hole](../../../../../../../black-hole.md). Every future [timelike direction](../../../../../../../timelike-vector.md) lies between these [null directions](../../../../../../../null-directions.md), so it also moves toward smaller $r$ there.
