<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [geometrized units](../../../../../../../geometrized-units.md) $G=c=1$ and [metric signature](../../../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$. Put $f=1-2M/r$ in the [Schwarzschild metric](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md). The [Schwarzschild tortoise coordinate](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-tortoise-coordinate.md) satisfies $dr_*/dr=f^{-1}$, so

$$
r_*=r+2M\log\left|\frac r{2M}-1\right|,\qquad v=t+r_*.
$$

Substituting $dt=dv-f^{-1}dr$ gives the [Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](../../../../../../../ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md):

$$
\boxed{ds^2=-f\,dv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2(d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2).}
$$

The radial [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) has determinant $-1$ and inverse components $g^{vv}=0$, $g^{vr}=1$, $g^{rr}=f$. Thus it is nondegenerate and analytic at $r=2M$. The same expression defines a [Lorentzian metric](../../../../../../../lorentzian-metric.md) for every $r>0$, extending the exterior across the future [Schwarzschild event horizon](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-event-horizon.md) into the [black hole](../../../../../../../black-hole.md). It does not include the other exterior or the [white hole](../../../../../../../white-hole.md) of the full [Kruskal spacetime](../../../../../../../kruskal-spacetime.md). At $r=0$, the [Kretschmann scalar](../../../../../../../kretschmann-scalar.md) $R_{abcd}R^{abcd}=48M^2/r^6$ diverges, so this is a [curvature singularity](../../../../../../../curvature-singularity.md), not a removable [coordinate singularity](../../../../../../../coordinate-singularity.md).
