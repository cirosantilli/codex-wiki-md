<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [spacetime](../../../../../../spacetime.md) is [geodesically complete](../../../../../../geodesic-completeness.md) if every [maximal geodesic](../../../../../../maximal-geodesic.md) has an [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) ranging over all of $\mathbb R$. For [timelike geodesics](../../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) this is equivalent to unbounded [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) in both directions. An [extendible geodesic](../../../../../../extendible-geodesic.md) segment can be prolonged in the same [spacetime](../../../../../../spacetime.md); an [inextendible geodesic](../../../../../../maximal-geodesic.md) cannot. A finite coordinate endpoint need not imply finite [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md).

For the [Kruskal spacetime](../../../../../../kruskal-spacetime.md), use $U,V$ with

$$
UV=\left(1-\frac r{2M}\right)e^{r/(2M)},\qquad ds^2=-\frac{32M^3}{r}e^{-r/(2M)}dU\,dV+r^2d\Omega^2.
$$

In the right exterior $U<0<V$. A truncated ray $U=U_0<0$, $V_1<V<V_2$ is an [extendible geodesic](../../../../../../extendible-geodesic.md) of radial null type: neither artificial endpoint is a [spacetime](../../../../../../spacetime.md) boundary. A future ray $U=U_0>0$ in the [black hole](../../../../../../black-hole.md) reaches $UV=1$, hence $r=0$, and is [inextendible geodesic](../../../../../../maximal-geodesic.md) and future [null-geodesically incomplete](../../../../../../null-geodesic-incompleteness.md). Its [Killing energy](../../../../../../killing-energy.md) $E\ne0$ gives $dr/d\lambda=-|E|$, so the [Schwarzschild singularity](../../../../../../schwarzschild-singularity.md) occurs at finite [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md). The maximal continuation toward the past supplies the other half of this same [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md).

**There is no inextendible, complete radial timelike geodesic in positive-mass Kruskal spacetime.** This requested example is impossible as printed. For a radial [timelike geodesic](../../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md), the conserved [Killing energy](../../../../../../killing-energy.md) and normalization give

$$
\dot r^2=E^2-1+\frac{2M}{r},\qquad \ddot r=-\frac M{r^2}.
$$

If $E^2<1$, $r$ has at most one turning point, a maximum $r_{\max}=2M/(1-E^2)$; a maximal trajectory runs from the [white hole](../../../../../../white-hole.md) [Schwarzschild singularity](../../../../../../schwarzschild-singularity.md) to the [Schwarzschild singularity](../../../../../../schwarzschild-singularity.md). If $E^2\ge1$, there is no finite turning point; one end can lie at infinity but the other reaches $r=0$. The exceptional $E=0$ trajectory through the [bifurcation surface](../../../../../../bifurcation-surface.md) also reaches $r=0$ in both time directions. Near $r=0$,

$$
d\tau\sim\sqrt{\frac r{2M}}\,dr,
$$

whose integral is finite. Constant-$r$ radial [timelike curves](../../../../../../timelike-curve.md) are accelerated, not [geodesics](../../../../../../geodesic.md).

Two plausible repairs have different meanings. Removing “radial” permits a complete circular [timelike geodesic](../../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) at $r=R>6M$, with nonzero [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) ranging over $\mathbb R$. Replacing “timelike” by “null” permits a complete horizon [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md): $U=0$, $V\in\mathbb R$, with $V$ an [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md). The [Penrose diagram](../../../../../../penrose-diagram.md) shows both repairs explicitly, together with the two valid requested examples; the circular trajectory is only a radial projection and is labelled as nonradial.
