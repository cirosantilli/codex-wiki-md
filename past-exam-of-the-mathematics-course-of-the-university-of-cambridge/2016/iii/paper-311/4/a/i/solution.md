<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use an [affine parameter](../../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) $\lambda$ along a generator of a [null hypersurface](../../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md). Its [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md) vanishes by [hypersurface orthogonality](../../../../../../../hypersurface-orthogonality.md). The screen metric is positive definite, so $\widehat\sigma^{ab}\widehat\sigma_{ab}\ge0$. The [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) and [null energy condition](../../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md) imply $R_{ab}U^aU^b\ge0$, giving from the [Null Raychaudhuri equation](../../../../../../../null-raychaudhuri-equation.md)

$$
\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda}\le-\frac12\theta^2.
$$

Starting with $\theta_0<0$, the [null expansion](../../../../../../../null-expansion.md) stays negative as long as the congruence remains regular. Set $z=-1/\theta>0$. Then $z'=\theta'/\theta^2\le-1/2$, so

$$
0<z(\lambda)\le\frac1{|\theta_0|}-\frac{\lambda-\lambda_0}{2}.
$$

A finite negative [null expansion](../../../../../../../null-expansion.md) cannot persist to $\lambda-\lambda_0=2/|\theta_0|$. Thus the [null focusing theorem](../../../../../../../null-focusing-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\theta\longrightarrow-\infty\quad\text{within affine distance at most }\frac2{|\theta_0|}.}
$$

This is a focal breakdown of the transverse congruence, provided the generator and regular geometry extend that far; it is not by itself a [curvature singularity](../../../../../../../curvature-singularity.md) or a statement that the individual [null geodesic](../../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) terminates there.
