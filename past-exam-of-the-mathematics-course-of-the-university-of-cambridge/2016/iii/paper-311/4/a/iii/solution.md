<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For each initial [Kerr black hole](../../../../../../../kerr-black-hole.md), $r_+=M+\sqrt{M^2-a^2}$ and the horizon area follows by integrating its [induced metric](../../../../../../../induced-metric.md):

$$
A_K=\int_0^{2\pi}\!d\phi\int_0^\pi\!d\theta\,(r_+^2+a^2)\sin\theta=4\pi(r_+^2+a^2)=8\pi M\left(M+\sqrt{M^2-a^2}\right).
$$

The final [Schwarzschild black hole](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) has area $16\pi M'^2$. Applying the [second law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../../second-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md) to the sum of the two initial areas gives

$$
16\pi M'^2\ge2A_K,\qquad \frac{M'}{2M}\ge\frac12\sqrt{1+\sqrt{1-(a/M)^2}}.
$$

Consequently the radiated [energy](../../../../../../../energy.md) fraction satisfies

$$
\boxed{\eta\le1-\frac12\sqrt{1+\sqrt{1-(a/M)^2}}.}
$$

The bound is $1-1/\sqrt2\approx0.293$ for nonrotating initial holes and tends to $1/2$ as $|a|/M\to1$. It is an upper bound from area alone, not a predicted efficiency or an assurance that equality is realizable. A final [Schwarzschild black hole](../../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) has zero [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md); any nonzero total initial [angular momentum](../../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) must also be removed or compensated, which can further restrict a physical merger.
