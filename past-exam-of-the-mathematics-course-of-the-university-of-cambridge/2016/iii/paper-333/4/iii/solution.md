<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For uniform depth on a [beta plane](../../../../../../beta-plane.md), $\widetilde{\mathbf u}=(-\beta/H,0)$. With weak bottom drag the equation reduces to [Sverdrup balance](../../../../../../sverdrup-balance.md),

$$
\beta\psi_x=HF,\qquad \beta v=F.
$$

Restore a dimensional forcing amplitude $F_0$; the printed unit-amplitude forcing means $F_0=1$ in the chosen units. The general interior [ocean transport streamfunction](../../../../../../ocean-transport-streamfunction.md) is

$$
\boxed{\psi=\frac{HF_0}{\beta}(x-x_e)\sin\left(\frac{2\pi y}{L_y}\right)+C(y).}
$$

Taking $\psi=0$ on a straight eastern boundary $x=x_e$ sets $C=0$; without a zonal boundary condition the function $C(y)$ remains arbitrary. Then

$$
v=\frac{F_0}{\beta}\sin(2\pi y/L_y),\qquad u=-\frac{2\pi F_0}{\beta L_y}(x-x_e)\cos(2\pi y/L_y).
$$

The [Sverdrup balance](../../../../../../sverdrup-balance.md) interior cannot satisfy both zonal-wall conditions; a [western boundary current](../../../../../../western-boundary-current.md) supplies the return circulation.

For basin scales $L_x,L_y$, use $V_S=F_0/\beta$, $U_S=V_SL_x/L_y$, and $\Psi_S=HV_SL_x$. The ratio of drag to [Sverdrup balance](../../../../../../sverdrup-balance.md) terms is of order

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_r=\frac r\beta\left(\frac1{L_x}+\frac{L_x}{L_y^2}\right)\ll1.}
$$

This criterion applies away from boundary layers and zeros of the leading forcing, with positive $H$, $\beta\ne0$, and the [beta plane](../../../../../../beta-plane.md) and small-[Rossby number](../../../../../../rossby-number.md) assumptions. For an approximately square basin it is $r/(\beta L)\ll1$. Neglect of nonlinear relative-vorticity advection further requires $U_S/(\beta L^2)\ll1$ for comparable velocity and length scales. Thus weak drag relative to the balance, rather than just a small absolute $r$, is the appropriate condition.
