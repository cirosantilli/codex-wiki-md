<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Each row of the [oriented incidence matrix](../../../../../../oriented-incidence-matrix.md) records one signed endpoint difference, hence

$$
\boxed{s(\theta)=\|D\theta\|_1.}
$$

Reversing an [edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) changes the sign of its row, and permuting the [edges](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) permutes rows; neither operation changes the sum of absolute differences. Thus the [graph total variation](../../../../../../graph-total-variation.md) is independent of the chosen orientation and ordering. If $D\theta=0$, values agree at the endpoints of every [edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md). Any two [graph vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) of a [connected graph](../../../../../../connected-graph.md) are joined by a [graph path](../../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md), so their values agree. Therefore **$\ker D=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1\}$**.
