<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $h=\widehat\theta-\theta^*$. The defining minimum for [graph total variation denoising](../../../../../../graph-total-variation-denoising.md), compared with the feasible value $\theta^*$, gives $n^{-1}\|z-h\|_2^2+\lambda\|D\widehat\theta\|_1\leq n^{-1}\|z\|_2^2+\lambda\|D\theta^*\|_1$. Expand the squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) and cancel $\|z\|_2^2/n$ to obtain the [basic inequality for a penalized least-squares estimator](../../../../../../basic-inequality-for-a-penalized-least-squares-estimator.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{\|h\|_2^2}{n}\leq\frac2n\langle z,h\rangle+\lambda\|D\theta^*\|_1-\lambda\|D\widehat\theta\|_1.}
$$
