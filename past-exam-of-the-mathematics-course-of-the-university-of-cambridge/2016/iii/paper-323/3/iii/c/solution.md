<h1 id="3/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Every [separable quantum state](../../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md) has [positive partial transpose](../../../../../../../positive-partial-transpose.md). Here the [partial transpose](../../../../../../../partial-transpose.md) is

$$
\rho_{AB}^{T_B}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0&0\\0&0&\alpha&0\\0&\alpha&0&0\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix},
$$

with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1/2,1/2,\alpha/2,-\alpha/2$. Any nonzero $\alpha$ therefore gives a negative [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), excluding a [separable quantum state](../../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md). At $\alpha=0$, the explicit decomposition

$$
\rho_{AB}=\frac12|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes|0\rangle\langle0|+
\frac12|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes|1\rangle\langle1|
$$

is a mixture of [product states](../../../../../../../product-state.md). **Consequently**

$$
\boxed{\rho_{AB}\text{ is separable}\iff\alpha=0.}
$$

Only necessity of the [positive partial transpose criterion](../../../../../../../positive-partial-transpose-criterion.md) is needed here; sufficiency at zero follows directly from the decomposition.
