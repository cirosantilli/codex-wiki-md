<h1 id="3/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [density operator](../../../../../../../density-matrix.md) is a [pure state](../../../../../../../pure-state.md) exactly when its [matrix rank](../../../../../../../matrix-rank.md) is one. Within the admissible interval from part (a), one of $(1+\alpha)/2$ and $(1-\alpha)/2$ must vanish. Therefore **the pure-state parameters and vectors are**

$$
\boxed{\alpha=1:\quad\rho_{AB}=|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|,\qquad
\alpha=-1:\quad\rho_{AB}=|\Phi^-\rangle\langle\Phi^-|,}
$$

where the [Bell states](../../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) are $|\Phi^\pm\rangle=(|00\rangle\pm|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$. Equivalently, the [purity of a density operator](../../../../../../../purity-of-a-density-operator.md) is $\operatorname{Tr}\rho_{AB}^2=(1+\alpha^2)/2$, reaching one exactly at these two endpoints.
