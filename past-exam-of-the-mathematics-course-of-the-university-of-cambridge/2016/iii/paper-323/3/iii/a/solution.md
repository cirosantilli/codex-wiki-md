<h1 id="3/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [computational basis](../../../../../../../computational-basis.md) ordered as $00,01,10,11$, the [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) is

$$
\rho_{AB}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0&\alpha\\0&0&0&0\\0&0&0&0\\\alpha&0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [trace](../../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is one, and its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $(1+\alpha)/2$, $(1-\alpha)/2$, $0$, and $0$. Thus the remaining [density operator](../../../../../../../density-matrix.md) condition, positivity, holds exactly when **both nonzero candidates are nonnegative**:

$$
\boxed{-1\leq\alpha\leq1.}
$$

This is the admissible parameter interval for the [dephased Bell-state mixture](../../../../../../../dephased-bell-state-mixture.md).
