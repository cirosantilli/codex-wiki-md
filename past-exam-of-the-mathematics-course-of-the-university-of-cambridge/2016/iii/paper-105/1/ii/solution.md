↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Characteristic.** The second-order [principal symbol](../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) of $i\partial_t+\partial_x^2$ is $\nu_x^2$, which vanishes on the normal $(0,1)$. This uses total differential order, rather than an anisotropic convention that assigns different weights to time and space derivatives.
