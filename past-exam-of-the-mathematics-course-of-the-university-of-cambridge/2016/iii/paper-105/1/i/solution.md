**Characteristic.** The third-order [principal symbol](../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) of $\partial_t-\partial_x^3$ is $-\nu_x^3$. The initial line has normal $(\nu_x,\nu_t)=(0,1)$, on which this symbol vanishes. Its evolution form does not make it non-characteristic in the ordinary total-order definition.
