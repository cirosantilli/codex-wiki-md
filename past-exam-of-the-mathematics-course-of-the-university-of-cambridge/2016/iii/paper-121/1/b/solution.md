<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $\mathfrak c=2^{\aleph_0}$. Its [cofinality](../../../../../../cofinality.md) is $\aleph_1$, so choose a strictly increasing sequence of infinite [cardinal numbers](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $(\kappa_\xi)_{\xi<\omega_1}$ below $\mathfrak c$ with supremum $\mathfrak c$. Such a sequence is obtained by refining a cofinal sequence and choosing larger cardinals recursively; $\mathfrak c$ is a [singular cardinal](../../../../../../singular-cardinal.md), hence a [limit cardinal](../../../../../../limit-cardinal.md).

By the [axiom of choice](../../../../../../axiom-of-choice.md), fix a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) $b:\mathfrak c\to\mathbb R$. Set

$$
A_\xi=b[\kappa_\xi],\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal A=\{A_\xi:\xi<\omega_1\}}.
$$

Every $A_\xi$ is infinite, $|A_\xi|=\kappa_\xi<\mathfrak c$, and distinct indices give distinct [cardinalities](../../../../../../cardinality.md). The [cofinality](../../../../../../cofinality.md) of the sequence ensures

$$
\bigcup_{\xi<\omega_1}A_\xi=b\left[\bigcup_{\xi<\omega_1}\kappa_\xi\right]=b[\mathfrak c]=\mathbb R.
$$

Thus $|\mathcal A|=\aleph_1$. Pairwise different sizes means different sizes for distinct members; without that qualification the parenthetical condition would contradict the case $A=B$.
