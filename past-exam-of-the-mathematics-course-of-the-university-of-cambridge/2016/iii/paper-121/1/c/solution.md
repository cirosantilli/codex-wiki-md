<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The least index is $\boxed{\alpha=\omega_1}$, with

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\omega_1}=\aleph_{\omega_1},\qquad
\operatorname{cf}(\sigma_{\omega_1})=\aleph_1}.
$$

Indeed, for every nonzero [limit ordinal](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md) $\beta$, continuity of the [aleph number](../../../../../../aleph-number.md) enumeration and the [cofinality of an increasing ordinal supremum](../../../../../../cofinality-of-an-increasing-ordinal-supremum.md) give

$$
\operatorname{cf}(\aleph_\beta)=\operatorname{cf}(\beta).
$$

If $\beta<\omega_1$ is a nonzero [limit ordinal](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md), this [cofinality](../../../../../../cofinality.md) is $\omega$, so $\aleph_\beta$ is a [singular cardinal](../../../../../../singular-cardinal.md). The other infinite [cardinal numbers](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) below $\aleph_{\omega_1}$ are successor-indexed or $\aleph_0$, and are [regular cardinals](../../../../../../regular-cardinal.md). For a [successor cardinal](../../../../../../successor-cardinal.md) $\mu^+$, a cofinal sequence of length at most $\mu$ would express $\mu^+$ as a union of at most $\mu$ sets of size at most $\mu$, contradicting [infinite cardinal arithmetic](../../../../../../infinite-cardinal-arithmetic.md).

There are $\aleph_1$ nonzero countable [limit ordinals](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md), and each countable initial segment contains only countably many. In increasing order they therefore have [order type](../../../../../../order-type.md) $\omega_1$. These are precisely the indices of the uncountable [singular cardinals](../../../../../../singular-cardinal.md) below $\aleph_{\omega_1}$. Hence every $\sigma_\alpha$ with $\alpha<\omega_1$ has countable [cofinality](../../../../../../cofinality.md), and the next one is $\aleph_{\omega_1}$, whose [cofinality](../../../../../../cofinality.md) is $\omega_1<\aleph_{\omega_1}$.
