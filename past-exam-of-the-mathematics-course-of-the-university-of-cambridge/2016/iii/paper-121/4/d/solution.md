<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A witness to failure of the [Axiom of power set](../../../../../../axiom-of-power-set.md) is $\boxed{\omega_1\in H_{\omega_2}}$. Every subset $A\subseteq\omega_1$ belongs to $H_{\omega_2}$, since its [transitive closure](../../../../../../transitive-closure.md) has [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) at most $\aleph_1$. If some $p\in H_{\omega_2}$ were the internal [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) of $\omega_1$, transitivity and bounded subsethood would force $p$ to contain every ambient subset of $\omega_1$, and only those subsets. Thus it would be the full $\mathcal P(\omega_1)$.

By the [Cantor theorem](../../../../../../cantor-s-theorem.md),

$$
|p|=2^{\aleph_1}\geq\aleph_2.
$$

Its [transitive closure](../../../../../../transitive-closure.md) contains all its members, so $|\operatorname{tc}(\{p\})|\geq|p|\geq\aleph_2$. This contradicts $p\in H_{\omega_2}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{H_{\omega_2}\not\models\mathrm{PowerSet}}.
$$

The [power-set failure in hereditarily small sets](../../../../../../power-set-failure-in-hereditarily-small-sets.md) needs no assumption about the [Continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../continuum-hypothesis.md).
