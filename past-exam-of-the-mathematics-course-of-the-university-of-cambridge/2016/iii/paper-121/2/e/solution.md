<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**The continuum hypothesis holds.** Let $A$ be the assumed [Lusin set](../../../../../../lusin-set.md) of [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) $\mathfrak c=2^{\aleph_0}$. If $\mathfrak c>\aleph_1$, choose $B\subseteq A$ with $|B|=\aleph_1$. The size assumption makes $B$ a [meagre set](../../../../../../meagre-set.md), but then $A\cap B=B$ is uncountable, contradicting the [Lusin set](../../../../../../lusin-set.md) property. Thus

$$
\boxed{2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_1}.
$$

One can also use (d) directly: [well-order](../../../../../../well-order.md) $A$ in type $\mathfrak c$. Each proper initial segment has size below $\mathfrak c$, so is meagre and consequently countable, since it is a subset of the [Lusin set](../../../../../../lusin-set.md). These initial segments form a [linear increasing union of countable sets](../../../../../../increasing-chain-of-countable-sets.md) equal to $A$. The bound in (d) gives $\mathfrak c\leq\aleph_1$.
