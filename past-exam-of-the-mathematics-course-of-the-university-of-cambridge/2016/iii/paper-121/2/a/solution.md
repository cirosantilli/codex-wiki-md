The [Freiling axiom of symmetry](../../../../../../freiling-axiom-of-symmetry.md) is

$$
\boxed{\forall f:\mathbb R\to[\mathbb R]^{<\omega_1}\quad
\exists x,y\in\mathbb R\quad
x\notin f(y)\ \land\ y\notin f(x).}
$$

The values of $f$ are countable [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) of the [real numbers](../../../../../../real-number.md), including finite ones. One may require $x\ne y$ without changing the axiom: apply the displayed version to $x\mapsto f(x)\cup\{x\}$.

The [Generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) states

$$
\boxed{\forall\text{ infinite cardinals }\kappa,\quad2^\kappa=\kappa^+.}
$$

Here $\kappa^+$ is the [successor cardinal](../../../../../../successor-cardinal.md); equivalently $2^{\aleph_\alpha}=\aleph_{\alpha+1}$ for every [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\alpha$.
