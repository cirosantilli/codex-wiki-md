<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fix a regular uncountable [cardinal number](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$. A [club set](../../../../../../club-set.md) $C\subseteq\kappa$ is unbounded in $\kappa$ and closed under suprema below $\kappa$ of nonempty increasing sequences with no last term. Equivalently, every [limit ordinal](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md) $\delta<\kappa$ with $\sup(C\cap\delta)=\delta$ belongs to $C$. The [club filter](../../../../../../club-filter.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal D_\kappa=\{A\subseteq\kappa:\exists C\subseteq A\text{ with }C\text{ club in }\kappa\}}.
$$

It consists of all sets containing a [club set](../../../../../../club-set.md), not only the club sets themselves.

An [omega-measurable cardinal](../../../../../../omega-measurable-cardinal.md) is a [cardinal number](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) carrying a [nonprincipal ultrafilter](../../../../../../nonprincipal-ultrafilter.md) closed under countable intersections, that is, a [countably complete ultrafilter](../../../../../../countably-complete-ultrafilter.md). Such a cardinal must be uncountable: on a countable set the intersection of the complements of all singletons would be empty.

A [measurable cardinal](../../../../../../measurable-cardinal.md) is an uncountable [cardinal number](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$ carrying a nonprincipal $\kappa$-complete [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md). A [kappa-complete filter](../../../../../../kappa-complete-filter.md) is closed under intersections of every family of size strictly below $\kappa$. Thus for an [omega-measurable cardinal](../../../../../../omega-measurable-cardinal.md) the completeness required is countable completeness, not merely closure under finitely many intersections.
