<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**Weakly inaccessible cardinals are unbounded below the given cardinal.** In fact, they form a [stationary set](../../../../../../stationary-set.md) there. Let $\kappa$ be the [weakly Mahlo cardinal](../../../../../../weakly-mahlo-cardinal.md), so it is a regular uncountable [limit cardinal](../../../../../../limit-cardinal.md) and the set $R=\{\alpha<\kappa:\alpha=\operatorname{cf}(\alpha)\}$ is stationary.

The set $C$ of uncountable [limit cardinals](../../../../../../limit-cardinal.md) below $\kappa$ is a [club set](../../../../../../club-set.md). For unboundedness, above any starting point choose a strictly increasing countable sequence of [cardinal numbers](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) below $\kappa$; its supremum remains below $\kappa$ by regularity and is an uncountable [limit cardinal](../../../../../../limit-cardinal.md). For closure, a limit of such limit cardinals is again a [limit cardinal](../../../../../../limit-cardinal.md).

Every $\alpha\in R\cap C$ is an uncountable [regular cardinal](../../../../../../regular-cardinal.md) and a [limit cardinal](../../../../../../limit-cardinal.md), hence a [weakly inaccessible cardinal](../../../../../../weakly-inaccessible-cardinal.md). The intersection of a [stationary set](../../../../../../stationary-set.md) with a [club set](../../../../../../club-set.md) is stationary, because its intersection with any further club is nonempty. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\{\lambda<\kappa:\lambda\text{ is weakly inaccessible}\}\text{ is stationary, hence unbounded}.}
$$

This proves the stronger form of the requested conclusion.
