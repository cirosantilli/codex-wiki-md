<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since $X_1=0$, every joint weight has **$a_1=0$**. In $S_2$, $X_2=(1\ 2)$ acts by $1$ on the [trivial representation](../../../../../../trivial-representation.md) and by $-1$ on the [sign representation](../../../../../../sign-representation.md), which exhaust the [irreducible representations](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Spec}(2)=\{(0,1),(0,-1)\}}.
$$

The [content vector of a standard Young tableau](../../../../../../content-vector-of-a-standard-young-tableau.md) conditions similarly require the second coordinate to be a neighbour of $0$, so

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cont}(2)=\{(0,1),(0,-1)\}}.
$$

These vectors correspond respectively to the single-row tableau of shape $(2)$ and the single-column tableau of shape $(1,1)$.
