↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $n=4$, take the three vectors from part (iii):

$$
v_1=(1,-1,0,0),\qquad
v_2=(1,1,-2,0),\qquad
v_3=(1,1,1,-3).
$$

Their joint [Young–Jucys–Murphy element](../../../../../../../jucys-murphy-element.md) weights, in the order $(X_1,X_2,X_3,X_4)$, are

$$
(0,-1,1,2),\qquad(0,1,-1,2),\qquad(0,1,2,-1).
$$

They are distinct, and part (ii) says that these operators generate $GZ_4$. Therefore **a [Gelfand–Tsetlin basis](../../../../../../../gelfand-tsetlin-basis.md) is**

$$
\boxed{\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}}.
$$

If an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) is desired, use $v_1/\sqrt2$, $v_2/\sqrt6$, and $v_3/\sqrt{12}$. The corresponding [standard Young tableaux](../../../../../../../standard-young-tableau.md) have shape $(3,1)$, with the second-row entry respectively $2,3,4$. Their contents agree with the three displayed weights.
