<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an increasing chain of finite groups $G_1\subseteq\cdots\subseteq G_n$, regard every $\mathbb C G_r$ as a subalgebra of $\mathbb C G_n$. The [Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra](../../../../../../gelfand-tsetlin-algebra.md) is the unital algebra generated by their centres:

$$
\boxed{GZ_n=\operatorname{alg}\bigl(Z(\mathbb C G_1),\ldots,Z(\mathbb C G_n)\bigr)}.
$$

For this paper the chain is $S_1\subseteq\cdots\subseteq S_n$, so these centres are $Z_1,\ldots,Z_n$. The generators commute: if $r\leq s$, an element of $Z_s$ commutes with all of $\mathbb C S_s$, including $Z_r$. Thus the [Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra](../../../../../../gelfand-tsetlin-algebra.md) is commutative. Its joint eigenspaces record successive choices in the restriction of an [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) down the chain.
