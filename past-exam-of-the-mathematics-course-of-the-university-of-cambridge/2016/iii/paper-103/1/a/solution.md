<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Work over $\mathbb C$, with the standard inclusions $S_r\subseteq S_n$ fixing the letters $r+1,\ldots,n$. In the [group algebra](../../../../../../group-algebra.md), the [Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](../../../../../../jucys-murphy-element.md) are

$$
\boxed{X_1=0,\qquad X_i=\sum_{a=1}^{i-1}(a\ i)\quad(2\leq i\leq n)}.
$$

Thus $X_i$ adds all [transpositions](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) involving the newest letter. Conjugating by $S_{i-1}$ merely permutes the summands, so $X_i$ commutes with $\mathbb C S_{i-1}$. In particular, if $j<i$, then $X_j\in\mathbb C S_{i-1}$ and $X_iX_j=X_jX_i$. Hence the whole family is commuting.

Put $\Omega_r=\sum_{1\leq a<b\leq r}(a\ b)$, the [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) [conjugacy-class sum](../../../../../../conjugacy-class-sum.md) in $S_r$, with $\Omega_1=0$. Then

$$
X_i=\Omega_i-\Omega_{i-1}.
$$

This identity will connect the [Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](../../../../../../jucys-murphy-element.md) with the centres of the successive [group algebras](../../../../../../group-algebra.md).
