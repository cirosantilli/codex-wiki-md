<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\lambda=(1^n)$, there is exactly one [standard Young tableau](../../../../../../standard-young-tableau.md), the column with entries $1,2,\ldots,n$ from top to bottom. Its [content vector of a standard Young tableau](../../../../../../content-vector-of-a-standard-young-tableau.md) is

$$
\boxed{C(T)=(0,-1,\ldots,-(n-1))}.
$$

Every adjacent distance is $-1$, so [Young orthogonal form](../../../../../../young-orthogonal-form.md) gives **$s_rw_T=-w_T$ for every $r$**. Therefore

$$
\boxed{S^{(1^n)}\cong\mathbb C_{\rm sgn}},
$$

the [sign representation](../../../../../../sign-representation.md).
