<h1 id="3/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

The first event causes extinction precisely when it is recovery of the sole initially infectious individual. An infection would instead give two infectious individuals. The [competing exponential clocks](../../../../../../competing-exponential-clocks.md) calculation therefore gives **the first-event extinction probability**

$$
\boxed{\frac{\gamma}{\beta N+\gamma}.}
$$
