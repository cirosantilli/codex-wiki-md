↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Initially, the infection and recovery [transition intensities](../../../../../../transition-intensity.md) are $\beta N$ and $\gamma$. By [competing exponential clocks](../../../../../../competing-exponential-clocks.md), **the first-event waiting time** has distribution

$$
\boxed{\tau_1\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\beta N+\gamma),\qquad\mathbb P(\tau_1>u)=e^{-(\beta N+\gamma)u}\quad(u\ge0).}
$$
