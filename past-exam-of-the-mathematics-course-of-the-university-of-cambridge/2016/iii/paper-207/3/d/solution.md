<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The infectious count in the [SIR model](../../../../../../sir-model.md) obeys $\dot I=I(\beta S-\gamma)$. At the initial state, $\dot I(0)=\beta N-\gamma$, so initial epidemic growth requires **the [epidemic threshold for the closed SIR model](../../../../../../epidemic-threshold-for-the-closed-sir-model.md)**

$$
\boxed{N>\frac\gamma\beta,\qquad\mathcal R_0=\frac{\beta N}{\gamma}>1.}
$$

Here $\mathcal R_0$ is the [basic reproduction number](../../../../../../basic-reproduction-number.md) in this [mass-action infection](../../../../../../mass-action-infection.md) convention. Necessity also follows globally: $S(t)$ is nonincreasing, so if $\beta N\le\gamma$, then $\beta S(t)-\gamma\le0$ for all time and $I(t)$ never increases. When $\beta=0$, growth is impossible.
