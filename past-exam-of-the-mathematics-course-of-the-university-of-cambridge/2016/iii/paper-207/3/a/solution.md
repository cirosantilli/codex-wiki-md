<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [SIR model](../../../../../../sir-model.md), $S,I,R$ count susceptible, infectious, and recovered immune individuals. The closed population has $S+I+R=N+1$. Under homogeneous mixing, use the paper's [mass-action infection](../../../../../../mass-action-infection.md) convention: each susceptible has infection [hazard function](../../../../../../hazard-function.md) $\beta I$, so the population infection rate is $\beta SI$. Each infectious individual recovers with [hazard function](../../../../../../hazard-function.md) $\gamma$, giving population recovery rate $\gamma I$ and mean infectious duration $1/\gamma$.

The arrows are one-way: recovered individuals are immune, and there are no demographic entries or exits. The [transition intensities](../../../../../../transition-intensity.md) attached to the arrows are population flow rates, rather than individual [hazard functions](../../../../../../hazard-function.md).
