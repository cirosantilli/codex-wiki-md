For a continuous proper event time with [cumulative hazard function](../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md) $H$ and [survivor function](../../../../survival-function.md) $F(t)=e^{-H(t)}$, use the assumed inverse of $H$. For $u\ge0$,

$$
\mathbb P(H(T)>u)=\mathbb P(T>H^{-1}(u))=F(H^{-1}(u))=e^{-u}.
$$

Therefore **the integrated-hazard transformation is unit exponential**:

$$
\boxed{H(T)\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1).}
$$

This [cumulative hazard probability transformation](../../../../cumulative-hazard-probability-transformation.md) is the basis for the following survival-model residual diagnostics.
