# 4

↑ **Parent:** [Paper 207](../paper-207-split.md)

Throughout the survival questions, use the paper's notation $F(t)=\mathbb P(T>t)$ for the [survivor function](../../../../survival-function.md), not for a cumulative distribution function. To construct the [Kaplan–Meier estimator](../../../../kaplan-meier-estimator.md), sort the distinct observed event times $a_j$. Let $r_j$ be the size of the [risk set](../../../../risk-set.md) immediately before $a_j$, and $d_j$ the number of events at that time. The estimated conditional chance of surviving that event time is $1-d_j/r_j$. Multiplying these conditional probabilities gives

$$
\boxed{\widehat F(t)=\prod_{a_j\le t}\left(1-\frac{d_j}{r_j}\right).}
$$

A [right censoring](../../../../right-censoring.md) time reduces later [risk sets](../../../../risk-set.md) but causes no jump itself. The usual interpretation as a population [survivor function](../../../../survival-function.md) uses [independent censoring](../../../../independent-censoring.md).
