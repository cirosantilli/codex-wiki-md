↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Without censoring, $Y_i(t)=\mathbf1_{T_i\ge t}$, so **its unconditional expectation is survival**:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E Y_i(t)=F_i(t).}
$$

The distinction between $>$ and $\ge$ is immaterial for a continuous event time.
