<h1 id="7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

With [independent censoring](../../../../../../../independent-censoring.md), the [at-risk process](../../../../../../../at-risk-process.md) is $Y_i(t)=\mathbf1_{T_i\ge t,C_i\ge t}$. Independence and the common censoring [survivor function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md) $G$ give

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E Y_i(t)=F_i(t)G(t).}
$$
