This is [administrative censoring](../../../../../administrative-censoring.md). Under the usual assumption that entry calendar time is independent of the subsequent event time, it is an example of [independent censoring](../../../../../independent-censoring.md), not [informative censoring](../../../../../informative-censoring.md): the planned study closure, rather than a patient's prognosis, determines the available follow-up. Uniform entry by itself does not prove that assumption; if entry date is associated with prognosis, independence must instead be justified conditionally on the relevant [covariates](../../../../../covariate.md). The calculations below use independence.
