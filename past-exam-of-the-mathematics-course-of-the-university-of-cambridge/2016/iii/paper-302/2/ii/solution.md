<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [Euclidean motion Lie algebra in two dimensions](../../../../../../euclidean-motion-lie-algebra-in-two-dimensions.md), use the ordered basis $(J,E_1,E_2)$ and keep the printed rotation signs. The [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) matrices are

$$
\operatorname{ad}_J=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\0&0&1\\0&-1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\operatorname{ad}_{E_1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\0&0&0\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\operatorname{ad}_{E_2}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\-1&0&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Only the square of $\operatorname{ad}_J$ has a nonzero trace. Hence the [Killing form of the planar Euclidean motion Lie algebra](../../../../../../killing-form-of-the-planar-euclidean-motion-lie-algebra.md) has matrix

$$
\boxed{[\kappa]_{(J,E_1,E_2)}=\operatorname{diag}(-2,0,0),\qquad\kappa(aJ+u_1E_1+u_2E_2,bJ+v_1E_1+v_2E_2)=-2ab.}
$$

Its [radical of the Killing form](../../../../../../radical-of-the-killing-form.md) is $\operatorname{span}\{E_1,E_2\}$, precisely the translation subspace. This is a nonzero abelian [ideal of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md), so the [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) is neither simple nor semisimple. In fact its first [derived series of a Lie algebra](../../../../../../derived-series-of-a-lie-algebra.md) term is this translation ideal and its next is zero. Thus the degeneracy and the failure of semisimplicity agree with the structural proofs above; the adjoint action nevertheless remains nontrivial.
