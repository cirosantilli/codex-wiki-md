<h1 id="5d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Take two [disjoint permutation cycles](../../../../../../disjoint-permutation-cycles.md) of lengths five and four:

$$
\boxed{\sigma=(1\,2\,3\,4\,5)(6\,7\,8\,9).}
$$

Disjoint [permutation cycles](../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) commute, and their powers act independently on their supports. Thus $\sigma^k=1$ exactly when both $5$ and $4$ divide $k$, so its [order of a group element](../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) is $\operatorname{lcm}(5,4)=20$.
