<h1 id="5d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [order of a group element](../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) represented by a [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) is the [least common multiple](../../../../../../least-common-multiple.md) of its disjoint cycle lengths. An order divisible by nine therefore requires a [permutation cycle](../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) whose length is divisible by nine. In $S_9$ that must be a nine-cycle, which uses every letter and has order nine, not eighteen. **There is no element of $S_9$ of order eighteen.**
