<h1 id="12c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Away from the origin, the given [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) of the globally single-valued radius:

$$
\mathbf F=\nabla r,\qquad r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}.
$$

The sphere has centre $(5,3,2)$, at distance $\sqrt{38}$ from the origin, and radius one. Its intersection circle is therefore wholly away from the origin, since every point on the sphere has distance at least $\sqrt{38}-1>0$. Along the closed curve the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf x=dr$. **Consequently, independently of orientation,**

$$
\boxed{\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf x=0.}
$$

The intersection is nondegenerate: the centre's distance from the plane is $4/\sqrt{35}<1$. No parametrization of the resulting circle is needed because $r$ is a [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) throughout the punctured space.
