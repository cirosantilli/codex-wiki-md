↑ **Parent:** [3F](../3f.md)

The [independent and identically distributed random variables](../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) have a [continuous probability distribution](../../../../../continuous-probability-distribution-split.md), so any tie has probability zero. Their [joint probability density](../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) is unchanged by a [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md) of their indices: this is [order symmetry of independent random variables](../../../../../order-symmetry-of-independent-random-variables.md). The $n!$ strict orderings are disjoint and exhaust the sample space apart from the zero-probability ties. Each ordering therefore has the same probability, and their probabilities sum to one.

**The probability is**

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X_1>X_2>\cdots>X_n)=\frac1{n!}.}
$$
