<h1 id="1a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of this [second-order linear differential equation](../../../../../../second-order-linear-differential-equation.md) factors as $r^2-r-6=(r-3)(r+2)$. Its two distinct roots give the [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) $y=Ae^{3x}+Be^{-2x}$. Boundedness as $x\to\infty$ forces $A=0$, and the [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $B=1$.

**The required solution is**

$$
\boxed{y(x)=e^{-2x}.}
$$
