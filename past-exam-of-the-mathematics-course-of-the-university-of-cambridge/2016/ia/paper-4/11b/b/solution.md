<h1 id="11b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The meetings occur where Bob's constant position $x=\alpha ct_0$ intersects Alice's positive-$x$ branch of the [hyperbolic motion](../../../../../../hyperbolic-motion.md):

$$
t_\pm=\pm t_0\sqrt{\alpha^2-1}.
$$

Bob is at rest in this [inertial frame](../../../../../../inertial-frame.md), so **his elapsed [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md)** is

$$
\boxed{\Delta\tau_B=2t_0\sqrt{\alpha^2-1}}.
$$

Parametrize Alice's [world line](../../../../../../world-line.md) by

$$
x=ct_0\cosh\eta,\qquad t=t_0\sinh\eta.
$$

Since $t$ increases with $\eta$, the [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) element is

$$
d\tau=\sqrt{dt^2-\frac{dx^2}{c^2}}
=t_0\sqrt{\cosh^2\eta-\sinh^2\eta}\,d\eta=t_0\,d\eta.
$$

At the meetings, $\cosh\eta=\alpha$, giving endpoints $\eta=\pm\operatorname{arcosh}\alpha$. Thus **Alice's elapsed proper time** is

$$
\boxed{\Delta\tau_A=2t_0\operatorname{arcosh}\alpha}.
$$

Here $\operatorname{arcosh}$ is the nonnegative real [inverse hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../../inverse-hyperbolic-cosine.md). As a check, if $\alpha=\cosh\eta_*$ with $\eta_*>0$, then Bob ages by $2t_0\sinh\eta_*$ and Alice by $2t_0\eta_*$; $\sinh\eta_*>\eta_*$ confirms that Bob again ages more.
