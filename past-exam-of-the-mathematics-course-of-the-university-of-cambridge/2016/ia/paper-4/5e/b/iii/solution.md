<h1 id="5e/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Partition the enumeration of $B$ into the pairs $\{b_{2j-1},b_{2j}\}$, for $j\geq1$. An infinite [binary sequence](../../../../../../../bitstream.md) $x$ determines a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) $h_x:B\to B$: leave the $j$th pair fixed when $x_j=0$, and interchange its two elements when $x_j=1$. Each element stays in its own pair, so this function is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md), indeed its own inverse.

Different [binary sequences](../../../../../../../bitstream.md) give different [bijections](../../../../../../../bijection.md), since a changed bit changes the action on the corresponding pair. We have therefore embedded an [uncountable set](../../../../../../../uncountable-set.md) into $S_3$. Hence **$S_3$ is uncountable**, proving [uncountability of permutations of a countably infinite set](../../../../../../../uncountability-of-permutations-of-a-countably-infinite-set.md).
