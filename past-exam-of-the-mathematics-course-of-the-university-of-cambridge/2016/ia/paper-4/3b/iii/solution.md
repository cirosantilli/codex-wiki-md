<h1 id="3b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [work](../../../../../../work.md) done by a [force](../../../../../../force.md) is $W=\int\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r$. By the [work-energy theorem](../../../../../../work-energy-theorem.md), this has the same [physical dimension](../../../../../../physical-dimension.md) as [energy](../../../../../../energy.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{[E]=[F]L=ML^2T^{-2}}.
$$

Although [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) and [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) have the same [physical dimension](../../../../../../physical-dimension.md), they represent different physical quantities.
