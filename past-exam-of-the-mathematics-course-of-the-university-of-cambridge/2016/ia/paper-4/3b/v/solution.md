↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

An [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md) is defined through the electric [force](../../../../../../force.md) on a stationary [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge.md): $\mathbf F=q\mathbf E$. Therefore **the electric-field dimension** is

$$
\boxed{[\mathbf E]=[F]Q^{-1}=MLT^{-2}Q^{-1}}.
$$
