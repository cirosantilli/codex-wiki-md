↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

For the magnetic part of the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md), $\mathbf F=q\,\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$. Since [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) has [physical dimension](../../../../../../physical-dimension.md) $LT^{-1}$, the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) has dimension

$$
\boxed{[\mathbf B]=\frac{[F]}{Q\,LT^{-1}}=MT^{-1}Q^{-1}}.
$$
