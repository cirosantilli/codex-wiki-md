↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) about an origin is $\boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf r\times\mathbf F$. A [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) multiplies the dimensions of its factors, so [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) has the [physical dimension](../../../../../../physical-dimension.md) of length times [force](../../../../../../force.md):

$$
\boxed{[\tau]=L[F]=ML^2T^{-2}}.
$$
