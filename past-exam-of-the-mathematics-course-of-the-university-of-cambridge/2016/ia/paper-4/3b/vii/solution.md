<h1 id="3b/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

Use [Coulomb's law](../../../../../../coulomb-s-law.md) in vacuum, $F=q_1q_2/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^2)$. The constant $4\pi$ is dimensionless, so **the vacuum-permittivity dimension** is

$$
\boxed{[\epsilon_0]=\frac{Q^2}{[F]L^2}=M^{-1}L^{-3}T^2Q^2}.
$$

Thus the [physical dimension](../../../../../../physical-dimension.md) of [vacuum permittivity](../../../../../../vacuum-permittivity.md) follows directly from the dimensions of [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge.md), [force](../../../../../../force.md), and separation.
