<h1 id="3b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md), $F=ma$. [Acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) has [physical dimension](../../../../../../physical-dimension.md) $LT^{-2}$, so multiplying by [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) gives **the dimension of force**

$$
\boxed{[F]=MLT^{-2}}.
$$

The bracket notation records a [physical dimension](../../../../../../physical-dimension.md), rather than the numerical value or the choice of units.
