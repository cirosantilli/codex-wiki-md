↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

From [Newton's law of universal gravitation](../../../../../../newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation.md), $F=Gm_1m_2/r^2$, the [gravitational constant](../../../../../../gravitational-constant.md) has [physical dimension](../../../../../../physical-dimension.md)

$$
\boxed{[G]=\frac{[F]L^2}{M^2}=M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}}.
$$

Here the two factors of [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) and the inverse square of the separation determine the powers of $M$ and $L$.
