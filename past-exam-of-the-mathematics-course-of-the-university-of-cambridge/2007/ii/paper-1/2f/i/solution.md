↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** Define the [Chebyshev polynomials](../../../../../../chebyshev-polynomial.md) by $T_0(x)=1$, $T_1(x)=x$, and $T_{n+1}(x)=2xT_n(x)-T_{n-1}(x)$. The [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) addition identities give the same recurrence for $\cos(nt)$, so [mathematical induction](../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) proves $T_n(\cos t)=\cos(nt)$. For $n\ge1$, the leading coefficient is $2^{n-1}$, proving that its [polynomial degree](../../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) is exactly $n$.
