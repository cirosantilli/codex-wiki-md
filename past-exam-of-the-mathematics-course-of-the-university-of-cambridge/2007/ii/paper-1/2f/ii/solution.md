↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True, with $R_n=T_n$.** The [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md) identity $\cosh((n+1)t)=2\cosh t\cosh(nt)-\cosh((n-1)t)$ and the initial values $1,\cosh t$ give $T_n(\cosh t)=\cosh(nt)$ by [mathematical induction](../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md), using the same [Chebyshev polynomial](../../../../../../chebyshev-polynomial.md) recurrence.
