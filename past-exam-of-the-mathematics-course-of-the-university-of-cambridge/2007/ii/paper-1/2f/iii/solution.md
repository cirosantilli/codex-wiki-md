↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**False.** A function of $\cos t$ is [even](../../../../../../even-function.md) in $t$, whereas $\sin(nt)$ is [odd](../../../../../../odd-function.md). If the identity held, replacing $t$ by $-t$ would imply $\sin(nt)=0$ for every $t$, contradicted by $t=\pi/(2n)$. Thus no [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of any degree has the required property.
