<h1 id="29a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Along the [characteristics](../../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md), $x_1+x_2$ is constant. Since $dx_2/dt=-1$, the amplitude equation gives $du/dx_2=-x_2u$. Matching the initial value at $x_2=0$ yields

$$
\boxed{u(x_1,x_2)=f(x_1+x_2)e^{-x_2^2/2}.}
$$

Differentiation directly verifies both the [partial differential equation](../../../../../../partial-differential-equation-split.md) and the initial data.
