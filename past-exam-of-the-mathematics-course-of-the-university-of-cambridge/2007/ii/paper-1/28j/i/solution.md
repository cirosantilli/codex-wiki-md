Relative to a [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_t)$, a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) is an adapted integrable process satisfying $\mathbb E[M_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=M_s$ for $s\le t$. The [martingale convergence theorem](../../../../../../martingale-convergence-theorem.md) gives an almost-sure finite limit for a nonnegative martingale, with $\mathbb E[M_\infty]\le\mathbb E[M_0]$ by [Fatou's lemma](../../../../../../fatou-s-lemma.md). In continuous time take the usual right-continuous version and filtration hypotheses. Convergence in $L^1$, and preservation of expectation at infinity, additionally require [uniform integrability](../../../../../../uniform-integrability.md).
