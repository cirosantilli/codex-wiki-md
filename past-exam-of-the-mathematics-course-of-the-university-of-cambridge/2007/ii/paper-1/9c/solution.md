<h1 id="9c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9C](../9c.md)

For variations $q_i+\varepsilon\eta_i$ with $\eta_i(t_1)=\eta_i(t_2)=0$, differentiation of the [action](../../../../../action.md) and [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) give

$$
\delta S=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\left(L_{q_i}-\frac d{dt}L_{\dot q_i}\right)\eta_i\,dt.
$$

The [fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations](../../../../../fundamental-lemma-of-the-calculus-of-variations.md) therefore yields the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) $dL_{\dot q_i}/dt=L_{q_i}$. An [ignorable coordinate](../../../../../ignorable-coordinate.md) has $L_{q_j}=0$, so its [conjugate momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) $p_j=L_{\dot q_j}$ is constant.

For the sphere, $\varphi$ is [ignorable](../../../../../ignorable-missingness-mechanism.md) and

$$
\boxed{p_\varphi=ma^2\sin^2\theta\,\dot\varphi,\qquad
H=\frac{ma^2}{2}(\dot\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,\dot\varphi^2)+V(\theta).}
$$

The [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is the [mechanical energy](../../../../../mechanical-energy.md). Along solutions, $\dot H=-\partial L/\partial t=0$, because the [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) has no explicit time dependence.
