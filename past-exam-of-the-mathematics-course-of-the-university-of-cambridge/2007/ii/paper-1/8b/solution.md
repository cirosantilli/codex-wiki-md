<h1 id="8b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8B](../8b.md)

Continuation around one positively oriented loop induces an invertible linear map $M$ on the two-dimensional solution space. Its inverse is continuation around the reversed loop. Over $\mathbb C$, choose an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda\ne0$, and let $w$ be the corresponding nonzero solution. After continuation it becomes $\lambda w$.

Choose $\sigma$ with $e^{2\pi i\sigma}=\lambda$. The product $h(z)=z^{-\sigma}w(z)$ has trivial [monodromy](../../../../../monodromy.md), so it is single-valued and [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) on the punctured disc. Its [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md), convergent on compact subannuli, gives the [Monodromy eigenfunction Laurent representation](../../../../../monodromy-eigenfunction-laurent-representation.md)

$$
\boxed{w(z)=z^\sigma\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}c_nz^n.}
$$

No assumption of a [regular singular point](../../../../../regular-singular-point.md) has been made, so the negative part need not terminate.
