<h1 id="38c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Apply the numerical method to the test equation $y'=\lambda y$ and put $z=h\lambda$. Its [linear stability domain](../../../../../../linear-stability-domain.md) is the set of $z$ for which the computed amplification is stable, for a one-step method $|R(z)|\le1$ with all stage solves well-defined. The method is [A-stable](../../../../../../a-stability.md) if this includes the whole closed left half-plane (or its interior with boundary limits).
