<h1 id="24h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Coordinate projections are [open maps](../../../../../../open-map.md), so the local form of the [submersion](../../../../../../submersion.md) proves that $f$ is open. If $X$ is compact and nonempty, its continuous image is compact and hence closed in the Hausdorff manifold $Y$. Therefore $f(X)$ is a nonempty set both open and closed in connected $Y$, giving **$f(X)=Y$**.
