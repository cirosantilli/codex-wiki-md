<h1 id="24h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At a point where $df$ is surjective, choose charts and reorder the domain coordinates so that an $\ell\times\ell$ minor of the derivative is invertible. The map $F=(f_1,\ldots,f_\ell,x_{\ell+1},\ldots,x_k)$ has invertible derivative. By the [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) it gives local coordinates, in which $f$ is the projection $(u_1,\ldots,u_k)\mapsto(u_1,\ldots,u_\ell)$. This is the [submersion theorem](../../../../../../submersion-theorem.md).
