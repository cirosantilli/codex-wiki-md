<h1 id="21h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The genus-two surface has [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) $G=\langle a_1,b_1,a_2,b_2\mid[a_1,b_1][a_2,b_2]=1\rangle$. Connected double [covering spaces](../../../../../../covering-space.md) correspond to index-two subgroups. Each is the [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of a nonzero homomorphism $G\to C_2$; conjugacy creates no identifications because these kernels are normal. The commutator relation is automatic in $C_2$, so there are $2^4-1$ choices:

$$
\boxed{15\text{ connected double coverings}.}
$$

To obtain a surjection onto the [free group](../../../../../../free-group.md) $F_2=\langle x,y\rangle$, send $a_1\mapsto x$, $a_2\mapsto y$ and $b_1,b_2\mapsto1$. The defining relation again vanishes, and the images generate $F_2$.
