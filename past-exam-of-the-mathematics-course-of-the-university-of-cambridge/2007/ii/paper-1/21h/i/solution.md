<h1 id="21h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The polygon presentation of the [Klein bottle](../../../../../../klein-bottle.md), or [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](../../../../../../seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md) applied to its cell structure, gives

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(K)=\langle a,b\mid aba^{-1}=b^{-1}\rangle.}
$$

Send $a$ to $(12)$ and $b$ to $(123)$ in $S_3$. Conjugation by $(12)$ inverts $(123)$, so this defines a surjective [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md). Since $S_3$ is nonabelian, the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) is nonabelian.
