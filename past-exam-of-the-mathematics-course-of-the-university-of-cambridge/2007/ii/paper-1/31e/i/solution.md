<h1 id="31e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $u=-2\nu\partial_x\log\phi$. Direct differentiation gives

$$
u_t+uu_x-\nu u_{xx}=-2\nu\partial_x\left(\frac{\phi_t-\nu\phi_{xx}}{\phi}\right).
$$

Thus the [Cole-Hopf transformation](../../../../../../cole-hopf-transformation.md) maps every nonvanishing solution of the [heat equation](../../../../../../heat-equation.md) to a solution of the [viscous Burgers equation](../../../../../../viscous-burgers-equation.md). Conversely the Burgers equation makes the ratio inside the derivative a function of time alone; a time-dependent rescaling of $\phi$, which leaves $u$ unchanged, removes that function and gives the heat equation exactly.
