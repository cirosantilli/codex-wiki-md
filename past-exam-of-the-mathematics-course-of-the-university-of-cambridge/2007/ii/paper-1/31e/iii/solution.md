<h1 id="31e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Balancing the time derivative, cubic nonlinearity and third spatial derivative gives $x\sim t^{1/3}$ and $u\sim t^{-1/3}$. Choose $\zeta=x/(3t)^{1/3}$ and $u=(3t)^{-1/3}w(\zeta)$. Then

$$
u_t=(3t)^{-4/3}(-w-\zeta w'),\quad
6u^2u_x=(3t)^{-4/3}6w^2w',\quad
u_{xxx}=(3t)^{-4/3}w''',\quad
u/t=(3t)^{-4/3}3w.
$$

Thus the [similarity solution](../../../../../../similarity-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{w'''+6w^2w'-\zeta w'+2w=0.}
$$

The extra spherical term changes the coefficient of $w$. This third-order equation cannot be integrated directly into the standard second-order [Painlevé II equation](../../../../../../painleve-ii-equation.md) of the ordinary modified KdV reduction.
