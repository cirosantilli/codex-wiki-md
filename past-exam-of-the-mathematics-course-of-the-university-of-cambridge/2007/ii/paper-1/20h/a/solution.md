<h1 id="20h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\alpha=\sqrt{-26}$. Since the squarefree radicand is not congruent to one modulo four, the [ring of integers of a quadratic field](../../../../../../ring-of-integers-of-a-quadratic-field.md) is $\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\alpha]$. The [discriminant](../../../../../../discriminant.md) of the basis $(1,\alpha)$ is $4(-26)$, giving

$$
\boxed{\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-26}],\qquad d_K=-104.}
$$
