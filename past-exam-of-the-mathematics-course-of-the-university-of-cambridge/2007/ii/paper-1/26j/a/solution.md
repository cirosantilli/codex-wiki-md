↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the successive gaps of the rate-$\lambda$ [Poisson process](../../../../../../poisson-process.md) as independent [exponential random variables](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) $E_j$. The first arrivals in the three streams occur at $E_1$, $E_1+E_2$, and $E_1+E_2+E_3$. They have [Erlang distributions](../../../../../../erlang-distribution.md) of shapes one, two and three:

$$
\boxed{f_i(t)=\frac{\lambda^i t^{i-1}e^{-\lambda t}}{(i-1)!},\quad t\ge0,\quad i=1,2,3.}
$$
