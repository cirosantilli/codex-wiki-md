<h1 id="25j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) is both a [pi-system](../../../../../../pi-system.md) and a [Dynkin system](../../../../../../dynkin-system.md). Conversely, in a family having both properties, intersections and complements give finite unions and differences. For any sequence $(A_n)$ in it, replace $A_n$ by the disjoint sets $B_n=A_n\setminus\bigcup_{j<n}A_j$, which still belong to the family. Their disjoint union equals $\bigcup_nA_n$, proving closure under arbitrary countable unions. Thus the family is a [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md).
