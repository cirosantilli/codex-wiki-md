<h1 id="25j/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Under [complete convergence of random variables](../../../../../../complete-convergence-of-random-variables.md), for each integer $k\ge1$ the first [Borel-Cantelli lemma](../../../../../../borel-cantelli-lemmas.md) gives $\Pr(|X_n-X|>1/k\text{ infinitely often})=0$. Intersecting these [probability](../../../../../../probability.md)-one conclusions over the countably many $k$ shows that every tolerance is eventually satisfied. Therefore **[complete convergence](../../../../../../complete-convergence-of-random-variables.md) implies [almost sure convergence](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md)**, without [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md).
