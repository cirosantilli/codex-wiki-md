<h1 id="25j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first [Borel-Cantelli lemma](../../../../../../borel-cantelli-lemmas.md) says that $\sum_n\Pr(A_n)<\infty$ implies $\Pr(A_n\text{ infinitely often})=0$, without an [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) assumption. Indeed, for every $N$,

$$
\Pr\left(\bigcup_{n\ge N}A_n\right)\le\sum_{n\ge N}\Pr(A_n)\longrightarrow0.
$$

The decreasing intersection of these tail unions is the infinitely-often event; continuity of [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) from above proves the assertion. The second lemma says that for independent $A_n$, divergence of the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) sum implies $\Pr(A_n\text{ infinitely often})=1$.
