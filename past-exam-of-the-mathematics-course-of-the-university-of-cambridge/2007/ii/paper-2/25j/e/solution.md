<h1 id="25j/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $U$ be one uniform [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) on $(0,1)$, and define

$$
\boxed{X_n=\mathbf1_{\{U\le1/n\}}.}
$$

For every $U>0$ the sequence is eventually zero, so $X_n\to0$ [almost surely](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md). But $\Pr(|X_n|>1/2)=1/n$, whose sum diverges. This sequence is not completely convergent; the exceedance events are nested and dependent.
