<h1 id="36b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The outward flux through a spherical cap of radius $R$ inside the cone is

$$
\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\alpha\left(-\frac A{R^2}\right)R^2\sin\theta\,d\theta,d\phi
=-2\pi A(1-\cos\alpha)=-Q.
$$

Thus **the inward extraction flux is $Q$** at leading order. The thin no-slip wall layer is omitted from this approximation; its smaller displacement-flux effect supplies the later correction.
