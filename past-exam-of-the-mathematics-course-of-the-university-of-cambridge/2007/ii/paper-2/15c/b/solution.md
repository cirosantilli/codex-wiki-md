<h1 id="15c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a time-independent function $F$, [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) give

$$
\frac{dF}{dt}=\sum_k(F_{q_k}H_{p_k}-F_{p_k}H_{q_k})=[F,H].
$$

Thus a constant of motion has **$[F,H]=0$**. For $F=\sum_kp_k$ and the given [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md), $[F,H]=-\sum_kV_{q_k}$. Simultaneous translation $q_k\mapsto q_k+s$ leaves every difference $q_i-q_j$, and hence $V$, unchanged. Differentiation with respect to $s$ gives $\sum_kV_{q_k}=0$, so **$[F,H]=0$ also in this example**. This is conservation of total momentum from translational symmetry.
