<h1 id="15c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) generate the phase-space vector field $(H_{p_1},\ldots,H_{p_n},-H_{q_1},\ldots,-H_{q_n})$. Its divergence vanishes:

$$
\sum_k\left(\partial_{q_k}H_{p_k}-\partial_{p_k}H_{q_k}\right)=0,
$$

by equality of mixed derivatives. The Jacobian $J(t)$ of the flow obeys $\dot J=(\operatorname{div}V)J=0$ with $J(0)=1$. Consequently **$d\tau=dq_1\cdots dq_n\,dp_1\cdots dp_n$ is preserved** for every time on which the smooth flow exists. This proves [Liouville theorem in Hamiltonian mechanics](../../../../../../liouville-s-theorem-hamiltonian.md) directly; explicit time dependence of the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) would not change the divergence argument.
