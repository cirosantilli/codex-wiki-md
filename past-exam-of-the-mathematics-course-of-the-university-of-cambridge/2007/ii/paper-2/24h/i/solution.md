<h1 id="24h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [minimal surface](../../../../../../minimal-surface.md) has zero [mean curvature](../../../../../../mean-curvature.md) $H=(k_1+k_2)/2$; equivalently its area is stationary under compactly supported normal variations. Its [principal curvatures](../../../../../../principal-curvature.md) satisfy $k_2=-k_1$, so its [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is

$$
\boxed{K=k_1k_2=-k_1^2\le0.}
$$

Equality occurs precisely where its [second fundamental form](../../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md) vanishes.
