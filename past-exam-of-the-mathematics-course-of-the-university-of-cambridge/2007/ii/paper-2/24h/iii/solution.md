<h1 id="24h/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At a non-planar point of a [minimal surface](../../../../../../minimal-surface.md), the [shape operator](../../../../../../shape-operator.md) is not zero. Minimality gives nonzero opposite [principal curvatures](../../../../../../principal-curvature.md), so $K<0$ nearby and $dN$ is invertible. The [inverse function theorem](../../../../../../inverse-function-theorem.md) makes the [Gauss map](../../../../../../gauss-map.md) a local diffeomorphism onto a patch of the sphere; part (ii) makes it conformal.

Choose stereographic coordinates $(u,v)$ on that sphere patch. Their metric is $4(du^2+dv^2)/(1+u^2+v^2)^2$. Pulling them back by $N$, the conformal identity $N^*g_{S^2}=\lambda g_S$ gives

$$
g_S=\frac4{\lambda(1+u^2+v^2)^2}(du^2+dv^2).
$$

The positive coefficient shows that **these are local [isothermal coordinates](../../../../../../isothermal-coordinates.md)**. No general isothermal-coordinate existence theorem is needed: the nondegenerate conformal [Gauss map](../../../../../../gauss-map.md) supplies them explicitly.
