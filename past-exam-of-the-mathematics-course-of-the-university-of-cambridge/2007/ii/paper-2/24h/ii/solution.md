<h1 id="24h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $A=-dN$ be the self-adjoint [shape operator](../../../../../../shape-operator.md). In an orthonormal principal basis, $\langle dN(v),dN(w)\rangle=\langle Av,Aw\rangle$, with diagonal coefficients $k_1^2,k_2^2$. The [Gauss map](../../../../../../gauss-map.md) is conformal with nonzero factor exactly when $k_1^2=k_2^2=\lambda>0$.

If the surface is minimal and has no umbilics, $k_2=-k_1$ and $k_1\ne0$, so this equality holds. Conversely equality of the squares gives either $k_1=k_2$ or $k_1=-k_2$. The first possibility is excluded by the no-umbilic hypothesis. Hence $H=0$, proving **minimality is equivalent to conformality of the [Gauss map](../../../../../../gauss-map.md) under the stated hypothesis**. The factor is automatically positive because it equates positive definite inner products. The hypothesis is essential: a sphere has conformal [Gauss map](../../../../../../gauss-map.md) and nonzero [mean curvature](../../../../../../mean-curvature.md).
