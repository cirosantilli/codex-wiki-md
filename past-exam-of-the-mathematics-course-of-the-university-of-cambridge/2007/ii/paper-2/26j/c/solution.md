<h1 id="26j/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $R>0$, **the chain has one [communicating class](../../../../../../communicating-class.md) exactly when every $r_j>0$**. Sufficiency follows from the positive direct transition rate between each distinct pair of states. For necessity, if $r_j=0$ and there is another state, no other state can jump to $j$, either directly or as the final step of a path, so $j$ cannot communicate with them. The one-state case also satisfies the condition because $R=r_1>0$. A single class containing the entire state space is automatically closed.
