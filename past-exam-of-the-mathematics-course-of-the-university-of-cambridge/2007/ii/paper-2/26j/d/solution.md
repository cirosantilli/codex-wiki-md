<h1 id="26j/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $S_+=\{j:r_j>0\}$, nonempty since $R>0$. Every pair of distinct states in $S_+$ communicates directly, and no transition leaves $S_+$ for a zero-rate state. Hence **$S_+$ is the unique [closed communicating class](../../../../../../closed-communicating-class.md)**. Each zero-rate state forms an open singleton: it has total departure rate $R>0$, goes into $S_+$, and can never be revisited. This includes the case that $S_+$ itself is a single absorbing state.
