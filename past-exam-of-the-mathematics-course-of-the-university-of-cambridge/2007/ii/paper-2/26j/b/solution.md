<h1 id="26j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

When $R=0$ all rates vanish. Every state is absorbing and **the communicating classes are the $s$ singletons**, all closed. There are no open communicating classes. Every [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) distribution is stationary in this degenerate case.
