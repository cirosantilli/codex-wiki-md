↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The stationary equation $\pi Q=0$ reads $r^T-R\pi=0$, because $\pi\mathbf1=1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\pi_j=\frac{r_j}{R}.}
$$

It is the unique stationary law and vanishes on the open classes. For distinct $i,j$, $\pi_iq_{ij}=r_ir_j/R=\pi_jq_{ji}$. Thus **the chain is reversible in its equilibrium law**, including the reducible cases; on the closed class this is the usual reversible irreducible chain. This structure is the [destination-dependent reset chain](../../../../../../destination-dependent-reset-chain.md).
