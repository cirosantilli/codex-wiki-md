<h1 id="27i/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The likelihood-ratio statistic for nested parameter spaces is

$$
\Lambda_n=\frac{\sup_{\theta\in\Theta_0}L_n(\theta)}{\sup_{\theta\in\Theta_1}L_n(\theta)},\qquad W_n=-2\log\Lambda_n.
$$

Under the null, [Wilks theorem](../../../../../../wilks-theorem.md) gives $W_n\Rightarrow\chi^2_{d_1-d_0}$ when the full model and null submodel are smooth and identifiable, the true parameter is an interior regular point, [Fisher information](../../../../../../fisher-information-matrix.md) is nonsingular in the identifiable directions, and the usual differentiability and likelihood-asymptotic conditions hold. A level-$\alpha$ test rejects for $W_n$ exceeding the upper $\alpha$ quantile of that chi-square law. The approximation is for large samples; boundary parameters, singularities or nonidentifiability can give a different limiting distribution.
