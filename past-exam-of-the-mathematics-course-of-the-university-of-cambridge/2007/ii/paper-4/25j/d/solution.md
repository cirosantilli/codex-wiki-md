<h1 id="25j/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Since the measure is finite and $f$ is bounded, $f\in L^1$. By [Birkhoff ergodic theorem](../../../../../../birkhoff-ergodic-theorem.md), $A_nf\to f^*$ almost everywhere. If $|f|\leq M$, then $|A_nf|\leq M$ and therefore $|f^*|\leq M$ almost everywhere. For any finite $p\geq1$,

$$
|A_nf-f^*|^p\leq(2M)^p,
$$

and this constant is integrable because $\mu(E)<\infty$. The [dominated convergence theorem](../../../../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md) gives $\int|A_nf-f^*|^p\,d\mu\to0$. Thus **the ergodic averages converge in every $L^p$, $1\leq p<\infty$**, without assuming ergodicity.
