<h1 id="7e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Given a binary cylinder with finite prefix $b_1\ldots b_N$, repeat a finite block beginning with that prefix indefinitely. The resulting binary sequence is periodic under the shift and represents a [periodic point of an interval map](../../../../../../periodic-point-of-an-interval-map.md) of $F$ in the cylinder. If the given prefix consists entirely of ones, append a zero to the block so that its value is less than one; this still preserves the prefix. Since cylinders can be taken inside any nonempty open interval, **periodic points are dense**.
