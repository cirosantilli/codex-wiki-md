<h1 id="24h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) with affine parameter is a curve whose velocity is parallel along itself, $\nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma=0$. Its energy on a fixed parameter interval is $E(\gamma)=\frac12\int\langle\dot\gamma,\dot\gamma\rangle\,dt$. For a smooth fixed-endpoint variation with variation field $V$, metric compatibility and the torsion-free connection give

$$
\delta E=\int\langle\nabla_tV,\dot\gamma\rangle\,dt
=[\langle V,\dot\gamma\rangle]_{t_0}^{t_1}
-\int\langle V,\nabla_t\dot\gamma\rangle\,dt.
$$

The endpoint term vanishes. Thus a [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) is a critical point; conversely, vanishing for every interior variation field implies $\nabla_t\dot\gamma=0$. **Affinely parametrized [geodesics](../../../../../../geodesic.md) are precisely the fixed-endpoint energy critical points.**
