<h1 id="8b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the Euler integral to converge directly, assume $\operatorname{Re}b>0$ and $\operatorname{Re}(c-b)>0$. At zero it is the [beta function](../../../../../../beta-function.md) $B(b,c-b)=\Gamma(b)\Gamma(c-b)/\Gamma(c)$. The normalization is therefore

$$
\boxed{K=\frac{\Gamma(c)}{\Gamma(b)\Gamma(c-b)}.}
$$

Outside these initial parameter conditions the [hypergeometric function](../../../../../../hypergeometric-function.md) is defined by analytic continuation wherever the parameters permit it.
