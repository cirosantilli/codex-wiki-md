<h1 id="25j/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $x^T\mathbf1=0$, then $x^TQ=-Rx^T$. Induction gives $x^TQ^n=(-R)^nx^T$. Summing the matrix-exponential [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) yields

$$
\boxed{x^TP(t)=e^{-Rt}x^T.}
$$

Thus deviations of total mass zero decay with the single rate $R$.
