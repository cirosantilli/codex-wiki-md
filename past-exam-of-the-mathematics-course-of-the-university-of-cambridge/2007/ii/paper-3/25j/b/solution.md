<h1 id="25j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $Q=\mathbf1r^T-RI$, with $r=R\pi$. Then $\pi^T\mathbf1=1$ and

$$
\pi^TQ=r^T-R\pi^T=0.
$$

Thus $\pi$ is a stationary probability [vector](../../../../../../vector.md), a left [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) for [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) zero. If any probability [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $\nu$ is stationary, the same calculation gives $\nu^TQ=r^T-R\nu^T=0$, forcing $\nu=\pi$. Hence **the [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is unique**, even if some $r_j$ vanish and the chain is not irreducible; those zero-rate states are transient or inaccessible rather than extra stationary classes.
