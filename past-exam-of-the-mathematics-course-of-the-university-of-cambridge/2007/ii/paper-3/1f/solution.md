<h1 id="1f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1F](../1f.md)

Apply the reciprocal-and-integer-part algorithm for the [continued fraction](../../../../../continued-fraction.md). The successive complete quotients after the integer part two are $(\sqrt7+2)/3$, $(\sqrt7+1)/2$, $(\sqrt7+1)/3$, and $\sqrt7+2$. Their integer parts are $1,1,1,4$, and taking the next reciprocal repeats the first complete quotient. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\sqrt7=[2;\overline{1,1,1,4}].}
$$

The first four [continued fraction convergents](../../../../../continued-fraction-convergent.md) are $2,3,5/2,8/3$. The last gives $8^2-7\cdot3^2=1$. Squaring its unit $8+3\sqrt7$ gives $127+48\sqrt7$, whose [norm](../../../../../norm.md) is again one. Thus two positive solutions of the [Pell equation](../../../../../pell-equation.md) are

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(8,3),\quad(127,48).}
$$

Direct verification gives $127^2-7\cdot48^2=16129-16128=1$.
