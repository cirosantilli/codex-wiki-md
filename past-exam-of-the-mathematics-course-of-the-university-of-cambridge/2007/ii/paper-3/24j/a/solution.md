↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $1\leq p<\infty$, the [Lp norm](../../../../../../lp-norm.md) is $\|f\|_p=(\int_E|f|^p\,d\mu)^{1/p}$; for $p=\infty$ it is the [essential supremum](../../../../../../essential-supremum.md) of $|f|$. The [Lp space](../../../../../../lp-space.md) consists of measurable functions with finite [norm](../../../../../../norm.md), identifying functions equal almost everywhere. This identification makes the quantity a [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) rather than only a seminorm. **Convergence in $L^p$ means $\|f_n-f\|_p\to0$**, including the essential-supremum [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) when $p=\infty$.
