<h1 id="24j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Minkowski inequality](../../../../../../minkowski-inequality.md) states the triangle inequality for every $L^p$ [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) with $1\leq p\leq\infty$. Apply it to $f_n=(f_n-f)+f$ and then to $f=(f-f_n)+f_n$. The two bounds combine to

$$
\boxed{\bigl|\|f_n\|_p-\|f\|_p\bigr|\leq\|f_n-f\|_p\longrightarrow0.}
$$

Thus [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) convergence implies convergence of the [norms](../../../../../../norm.md) themselves.
