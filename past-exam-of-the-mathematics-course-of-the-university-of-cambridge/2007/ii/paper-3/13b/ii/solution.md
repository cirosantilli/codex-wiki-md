<h1 id="13b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The positive homogeneous [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is **$n=n_0$, $C=\alpha n_0/\beta$**. For spatially constant perturbations $u,v$, linearization gives $\dot u=-\gamma n_0u$ and $\dot v=\alpha u-\beta v$. The triangular [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) has [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $-\gamma n_0$ and $-\beta$, both negative, proving asymptotic stability to homogeneous perturbations. The other homogeneous [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), zero population and zero chemical, has positive population [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\gamma n_0$ and is not the desired stable state.
