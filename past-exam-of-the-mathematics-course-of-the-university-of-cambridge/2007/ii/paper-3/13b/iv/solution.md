<h1 id="13b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

At the threshold, the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) quadratic has a double zero. Its vertex lies at $s=(\chi\alpha n_0-\beta D_n-\gamma n_0D_c)/(2D_nD_c)$. Substituting the equality case of part (iii) gives

$$
\boxed{k_c^2=\sqrt{\frac{\beta\gamma n_0}{D_nD_c}},\qquad
k_c=\left(\frac{\beta\gamma n_0}{D_nD_c}\right)^{1/4}.}
$$

The mode is neutral exactly at onset and grows when the chemotactic coefficient increases beyond that threshold.
