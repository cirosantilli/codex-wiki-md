↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The term $\gamma n(n_0-n)$ is logistic population growth, with carrying density $n_0$ and low-density growth rate $\gamma n_0$. The cell flux is $-D_n\nabla n+\chi n\nabla C$: its first part is [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) down the density gradient, and its second is [chemotaxis](../../../../../../chemotaxis.md) toward higher chemical concentration. Taking minus its divergence gives the two transport terms. Chemical production is proportional to cell density at rate $\alpha n$, degradation is first order at rate $\beta C$, and the chemical diffuses with coefficient $D_c$. All the given positive signs correspond to growth, attraction, production, degradation and ordinary [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) respectively.
