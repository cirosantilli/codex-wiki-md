↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The European put has terminal values $0,0,0,5$. Discounted backward expectation gives value zero at stock 30 and $(4/5)(5/6\cdot0+1/6\cdot5)=2/3$ at stock 12. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P_E(0)=\tfrac45\bigl(\tfrac38\cdot0+\tfrac58\cdot\tfrac23\bigr)=\tfrac13.}
$$

For replication, denote by $B$ the money currently in the bank, which grows to $RB$ next period. At time zero hold **$\Delta=-1/27$ shares and $B=8/9$**, costing $15\Delta+B=1/3$. At stock 30 rebalance to zero shares and zero bank balance. At stock 12 rebalance to **$\Delta=-5/6$, $B=32/3$**. This costs $12(-5/6)+32/3=2/3$ and gives terminal wealth zero at 16 and five at 10. The initial portfolio produces exactly the two node values, so these rebalancings are self-financing.
