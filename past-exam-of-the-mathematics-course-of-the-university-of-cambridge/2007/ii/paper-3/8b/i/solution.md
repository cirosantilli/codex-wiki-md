<h1 id="8b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $u_1,u_2$ be a local fundamental pair at zero, including a logarithmic Frobenius solution if the exponents are resonant. Any other independent pair on a common branch domain has $w_1=au_1+bu_2$ and $w_2=cu_1+du_2$ with constant coefficients and $ad-bc\ne0$. Bilinearity and antisymmetry of the [Wronskian](../../../../../../wronskian.md) give

$$
\boxed{W(w_1,w_2)=(ad-bc)W(u_1,u_2).}
$$

Thus changing the independent branches multiplies the principal-pair [Wronskian](../../../../../../wronskian.md) by a nonzero constant, once the branches are continued onto the same domain.
