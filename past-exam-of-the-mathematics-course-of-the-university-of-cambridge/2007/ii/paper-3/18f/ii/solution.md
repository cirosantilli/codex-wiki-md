<h1 id="18f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $a=i\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}$ and $b=i\sqrt{2-\sqrt2}$. The roots are $\pm a,\pm b$. Since $\sqrt2=-a^2-2$ and $ab=-\sqrt2$, we have $b=-\sqrt2/a\in\mathbb Q(a)$. Hence **the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is $K=\mathbb Q(a)$**. The quartic is Eisenstein at two, so its degree is four.

The automorphism $r$ sending $a$ to $b$ sends $\sqrt2$ to $-\sqrt2$, and therefore sends $b=-\sqrt2/a$ to $\sqrt2/b=-a$. Its successive images of $a$ are $a,b,-a,-b,a$. Thus $r$ has order four and supplies all automorphisms:

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)\cong C_4.}
$$

Complex conjugation is $r^2$. The images of $a$ specify the full action because $K$ is generated by that one root.
