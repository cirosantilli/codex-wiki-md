<h1 id="23h/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Parametrize the [torus](../../../../../../torus.md) by $F(u,v)=((2+\cos u)\cos v,(2+\cos u)\sin v,\sin u)$. Its outward [Gauss map](../../../../../../gauss-map.md) is

$$
N(u,v)=(\cos u\cos v,\cos u\sin v,\sin u).
$$

The derivative $N_u$ has unit length, while $N_v$ has length $|\cos u|$ and is perpendicular to $N_u$. Thus rank drops precisely at $u=\pi/2$ or $3\pi/2$. These are the two circles of height $z=1$ and $z=-1$ and radius two, and their images are the north and south poles. Therefore **the critical values are $(0,0,\pm1)$**.

For any nonpolar target point, its third coordinate determines two values of $u$, one with positive and one with negative $\cos u$. For each, the longitude fixes a unique $v$ modulo $2\pi$, with a shift of $\pi$ when $\cos u$ changes sign. Thus every [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md) has exactly two preimages, one on the outer and one on the inner side of the [torus](../../../../../../torus.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{\deg_2N=0.}
$$

This explicit two-sheet description justifies the count without relying on a sketch.
