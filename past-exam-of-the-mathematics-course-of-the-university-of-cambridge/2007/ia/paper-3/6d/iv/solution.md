↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**Can be false.** Use $G=S_3$ and the [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) $H=A_3$ from the root solution. The [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ is cyclic of order three, hence an [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md), and the [quotient group](../../../../../../quotient-group.md) has the two [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md) $A_3$ and $(12)A_3$, so is cyclic of order two and abelian. Yet $(12)(23)=(123)$ whereas $(23)(12)=(132)$, proving that $S_3$ is not abelian. An abelian kernel and an abelian quotient do not force all elements of the original group to commute.
