<h1 id="6d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Can be false.** Take the [Klein four-group](../../../../../../klein-four-group.md) $G=C_2\times C_2$ and $H=C_2\times\{e\}$. It is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) because $G$ is an [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md). Both $H$ and $G/H$ have order two and are [cyclic groups](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md). But every nonidentity element of $G$ has order two, so none generates its four elements. Thus $G$ is not cyclic.
