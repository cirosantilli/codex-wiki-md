<h1 id="11a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The radial [Laplace equation](../../../../../../laplace-equation.md) has solution $a+b/r$. Boundedness at the origin requires $b=0$, and the value at $r=1$ fixes $a=1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\psi(r)=1,\qquad0\le r\le1.}
$$
