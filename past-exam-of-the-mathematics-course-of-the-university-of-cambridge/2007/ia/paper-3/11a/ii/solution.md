<h1 id="11a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The radial [Poisson equation](../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) with constant source one has $\psi=r^2/6+a+b/r$. The boundary conditions give

$$
\frac16+a+b=1,\qquad\frac46+a+\frac b2=1.
$$

Subtracting gives $b=1$, then $a=-1/6$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\psi(r)=\frac{r^2-1}{6}+\frac1r,\qquad1\le r\le2.}
$$

Its [radial Laplacian](../../../../../../radial-laplacian.md) is one and both endpoint values are one. The prescribed values ensure continuity with the adjacent region solutions, but do not impose derivative continuity.
