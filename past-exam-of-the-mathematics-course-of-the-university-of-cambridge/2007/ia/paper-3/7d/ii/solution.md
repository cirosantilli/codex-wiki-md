<h1 id="7d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the [Jordan block](../../../../../../jordan-block.md)

$$
\boxed{C=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its only [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is one. An [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) satisfies $(C-I)v=0$, so its second coordinate vanishes; the [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) is one-dimensional. A [diagonalizable matrix](../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) of size two requires a basis of two independent [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md), which this matrix lacks even over $\mathbb C$.
