<h1 id="7d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Take

$$
\boxed{E=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The real [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are one and two, with eigenlines spanned by $(1,0)^T$ and $(1,1)^T$ respectively. These independent vectors form a real [eigenbasis](../../../../../../eigenbasis.md), so $E$ is a [diagonalizable matrix](../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) over $\mathbb R$. Their [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) is one, and any two nonzero vectors on those eigenlines have nonzero [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md). Rescaling can normalize their lengths but cannot make them orthogonal. Hence no orthonormal [eigenbasis](../../../../../../eigenbasis.md) exists.
