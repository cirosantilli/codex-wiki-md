<h1 id="6c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $\mathbf x=s\mathbf n+\mathbf v$, with $\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf n=0$. The [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) sends $s\mathbf n$ to $as\mathbf n$ and sends $\mathbf v$ to $a\mathbf v+b(\mathbf n\times\mathbf v)$. On the perpendicular [plane](../../../../../../plane.md), $\mathbf v$ and $\mathbf n\times\mathbf v$ are perpendicular vectors of equal length. Thus

$$
a\mathbf v+b(\mathbf n\times\mathbf v)
=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\bigl(\cos\vartheta\,\mathbf v+\sin\vartheta\,\mathbf n\times\mathbf v\bigr),
\qquad\vartheta=\arctan(b/a).
$$

The expression in parentheses is a [rotation in three dimensions](../../../../../../rotation-in-three-dimensions.md) by $\vartheta$ about the oriented $\mathbf n$ axis, with the positive direction fixed by the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md). Therefore **the map scales the axial component by $a$, scales the perpendicular component by $\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$, and rotates the perpendicular [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) by $\arctan(b/a)$**. Since $a,b>0$, the angle lies between zero and $\pi/2$. This is an [axis-angle decomposition](../../../../../../axis-angle-decomposition.md), with different axial and transverse scale factors.
