<h1 id="6c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In [suffix notation](../../../../../../einstein-notation.md), $x'_i=ax_i+b\epsilon_{ikj}n_kx_j$, so the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) entries are

$$
\boxed{M_{ij}=a\delta_{ij}+b\epsilon_{ikj}n_k
=a\delta_{ij}-b\epsilon_{ijk}n_k.}
$$

Here $\delta$ is the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../../kronecker-delta.md) and $\epsilon$ the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md). The diagonal determines $a=2$. Comparing the off-diagonal entries with

$$
b[\mathbf n\times]=\begin{pmatrix}
0&-bn_3&bn_2\\bn_3&0&-bn_1\\-bn_2&bn_1&0
\end{pmatrix}
$$

gives $b\mathbf n=(1,2,2)$. As $\mathbf n$ is a [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) and $b>0$, **$b=3$ and $\mathbf n=(1,2,2)/3$**. Thus

$$
\boxed{a=2,\qquad b=3,\qquad\mathbf n=\tfrac13(1,2,2).}
$$

In this example the transverse scaling is $\sqrt{13}$ and the axial scaling is two; the [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) angle is $\arctan(3/2)$.
