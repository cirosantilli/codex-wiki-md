↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $x>0$, the function is a composition and product of [continuous functions](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). At zero,

$$
|x\sin(1/x)-0|\leq x\longrightarrow0.
$$

Thus it is continuous on the whole closed interval, including its assigned value at zero. The continuous-function proof in the introductory solution applies, so **this function is Riemann integrable**. The vanishing factor $x$ controls the infinitely rapid oscillation at the endpoint.
