<h1 id="2b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

When $p=1$ but $t^2\ne3t-2$, the dependent coefficient rows would require an impossible equality between their right-hand sides. Thus the [augmented matrix](../../../../../../augmented-matrix.md) has larger [matrix rank](../../../../../../matrix-rank.md) than the coefficient [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md), and the [linear system](../../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) is inconsistent. For $p\ne1$ part (i) already supplies a unique solution. Consequently **there is no solution exactly when $p=1$ and $t\notin\{1,2\}$**.
