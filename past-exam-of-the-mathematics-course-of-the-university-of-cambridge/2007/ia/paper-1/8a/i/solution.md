<h1 id="8a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write a [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) in Cartesian form $Ax+By+C=0$, where $A,B,C$ are real and $(A,B)\ne(0,0)$. With $z=x+iy$, set $c=(A+iB)/2$ and $r=C$. Using the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md),

$$
\overline c z+c\overline z=2\operatorname{Re}(\overline c z)=Ax+By.
$$

Thus the [real linear equation of a line in the complex plane](../../../../../../real-linear-equation-of-a-line-in-the-complex-plane.md) is

$$
\boxed{\overline c z+c\overline z+r=0,\qquad c\ne0,\quad r\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Conversely, writing $c=u+iv$ gives $2ux+2vy+r=0$, an ordinary [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) with normal [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $(u,v)$. The nonzero condition on $c$ matters: $c=0$ would give either the whole [complex plane](../../../../../../complex-plane.md) or the empty set.
