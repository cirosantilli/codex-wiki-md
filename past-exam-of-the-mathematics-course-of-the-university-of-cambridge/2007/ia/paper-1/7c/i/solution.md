<h1 id="7c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) to $(x,y,z)$ and $(1,1,1)$ in the Euclidean [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) on $\mathbb R^3$. It gives

$$
(x+y+z)^2\leq3(x^2+y^2+z^2).
$$

After expansion and division by two,

$$
\boxed{x^2+y^2+z^2\geq xy+yz+zx.}
$$

The equality criterion from the introductory proof says $x=y=z$. One can also see both the inequality and equality case from $x^2+y^2+z^2-xy-yz-zx=\tfrac12[(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2]$.
