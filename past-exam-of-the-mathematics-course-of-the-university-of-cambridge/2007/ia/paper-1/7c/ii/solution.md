<h1 id="7c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $s=x+y+z$ and $Q=x^2+y^2+z^2$. The expression in the equation is $4(Q+4)-(s+2)^2$. Apply the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) in $\mathbb R^4$ to

$$
\mathbf v=(x,y,z,2),\qquad\mathbf e=(1,1,1,1).
$$

Their [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) is $s+2$, and $|\mathbf e|^2=4$. Thus the expression is nonnegative, and it is zero precisely when equality holds in [Cauchy-Schwarz](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). That requires $\mathbf v=\lambda\mathbf e$. The fourth coordinate forces $\lambda=2$, and the other three coordinates then give

$$
\boxed{x=y=z=2.}
$$

Substitution gives $3(4+4+4+4)-2(4+4+4)-4(2+2+2)=48-24-24=0$, so this unique possible choice really is a solution.
