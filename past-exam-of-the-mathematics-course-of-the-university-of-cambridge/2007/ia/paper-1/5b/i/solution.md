<h1 id="5b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Decompose the position [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) into its components parallel and perpendicular to the [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) $\mathbf u$:

$$
\mathbf r=s\mathbf u+\mathbf v,\qquad s=\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf u,\qquad\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf u=0.
$$

This is an [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md), and the [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) gives $|\mathbf r|^2=s^2+|\mathbf v|^2$. The two surfaces are distinguished by fixing an angle in part (a), and fixing distance from an axis in part (b).
