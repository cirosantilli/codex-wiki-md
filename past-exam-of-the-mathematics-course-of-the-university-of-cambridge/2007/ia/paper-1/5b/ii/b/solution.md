↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substitute the signed displacement $\lambda=p-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf c$ into the [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) relation from part (a):

$$
\boxed{\rho=\sqrt{a^2-(p-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf c)^2}.}
$$

A genuine [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) requires $|p-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf c|<a$. Equality gives a tangent [plane](../../../../../../../plane.md) and a single point, interpreted as radius zero; a larger distance gives no intersection. Under the stipulated [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) intersection, the radicand is positive. Its centre is $\mathbf c+(p-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf c)\mathbf n$.
