The [sphere-plane intersection](../../../../../../sphere-plane-intersection.md) is governed by the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) of the [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md)'s centre onto the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md). Since $\mathbf n$ has unit length, let $h=p-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf c$, the signed distance from $\mathbf c$ to the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) along $\mathbf n$. The following parts determine the centre and radius and retain this sign; the radii alone cannot distinguish the two possible sides of the oriented [plane](../../../../../../plane.md).
