<h1 id="9c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The relevant parameters are $a,h,g$ and the ring mass $m$, with dimensions length, length, length/time$^2$ and mass. The wire is fixed and smooth, so there is no additional friction parameter. Because $m$ is the only parameter carrying a mass dimension, it cannot enter a dimensionless group governing the period. Using $a$ and $g$ as the length and time scales, the independent dimensionless variables are $T\sqrt{g/a}$ and $h/a$. [Dimensional analysis](../../../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{T=\sqrt{\frac ag}\,G(h/a).}
$$

The physical mass cancellation also follows from the energy equation in part (ii), since kinetic and gravitational [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) are both proportional to $m$.
