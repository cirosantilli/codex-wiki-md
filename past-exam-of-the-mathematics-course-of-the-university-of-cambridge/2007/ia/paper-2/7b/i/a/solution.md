↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Inserting the [Dirac delta](../../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) forcing into the integral gives the [impulse response](../../../../../../../impulse-response.md)

$$
\boxed{y(t)=\frac1\alpha e^{-t/\alpha}H(t).}
$$

It is zero before the impulse and obeys the homogeneous equation afterward. Integrating the equation across zero gives $\alpha[y(0^+)-y(0^-)]=1$, exactly the jump $1/\alpha$ in this formula. Thus the formula also verifies the [differential equation](../../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) as a [distribution](../../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md); assigning a particular value at the jump does not affect that distribution.
