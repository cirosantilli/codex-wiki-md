↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [Heaviside step function](../../../../../../../heaviside-step-function.md) forcing, the integral begins at zero when $t>0$:

$$
y(t)=\frac1\alpha\int_0^t e^{-(t-s)/\alpha}\,ds=1-e^{-t/\alpha}.
$$

Before zero it vanishes. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(t)=(1-e^{-t/\alpha})H(t).}
$$

This [causal response of a first-order relaxation equation](../../../../../../../causal-response-of-a-first-order-relaxation-equation.md) is continuous at zero and approaches the new constant equilibrium exponentially.
