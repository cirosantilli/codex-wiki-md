↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The statement is true.** Use the [union bound](../../../../../../boole-s-inequality.md) on the complementary events:

$$
P\left(\bigcap_{k=1}^n A_k\right)
=1-P\left(\bigcup_{k=1}^n A_k^c\right)
\geq1-\sum_{k=1}^nP(A_k^c)
=\sum_{k=1}^nP(A_k)-(n-1)>0.
$$

This proves the claim for arbitrary events, with no [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) requirement.
