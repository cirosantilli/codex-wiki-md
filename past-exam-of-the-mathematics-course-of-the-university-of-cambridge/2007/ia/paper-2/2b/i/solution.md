<h1 id="2b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $F(y)=(y^2-4)\arctan y$. Its zeros, and hence the [equilibrium points](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), are $-2,0,2$. For a perturbation $y=y_*+\eta$, [linear stability analysis](../../../../../../linear-stability.md) gives $\dot\eta=F'(y_*)\eta+O(\eta^2)$, with

$$
F'(y)=2y\arctan y+\frac{y^2-4}{1+y^2}.
$$

At zero, $F'(0)=-4$, so sufficiently small perturbations decay and **$y=0$ is asymptotically stable**. At either outer [equilibrium point](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), $F'(\pm2)=4\arctan2>0$, so **$y=-2$ and $y=2$ are unstable**. The [phase line](../../../../../../phase-line.md) confirms the result: $F$ has signs $-,+,-,+$ on the intervals cut out by $-2,0,2$, respectively. Flow points toward zero on both sides and away from both outer equilibria.
