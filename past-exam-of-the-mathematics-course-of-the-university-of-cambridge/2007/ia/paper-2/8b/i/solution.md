<h1 id="8b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the homogeneous [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md), a trial $u_k=r^k$ gives the [characteristic equation of a linear recurrence](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-linear-recurrence.md) $r^2+5r+6=(r+2)(r+3)=0$. Thus the two homogeneous sequences are $(-2)^k$ and $(-3)^k$. Seek a particular solution $u_k=ak+b$; substitution gives $12ak+12b-5a=12k+1$, so $a=1$ and $b=1/2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{u_k=A(-2)^k+B(-3)^k+k+\frac12.}
$$

The two independent constants supply arbitrary initial values for this second-order [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md).
