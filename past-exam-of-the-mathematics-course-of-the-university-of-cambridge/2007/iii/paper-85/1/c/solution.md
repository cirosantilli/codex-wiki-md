<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Local [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) and [Hopf bifurcations](../../../../../../hopf-bifurcation.md) do not locate the larger cycles' endpoints. In the large-$s$ case, the stable origin cycle is confined between two symmetry-related saddles. A natural global completion is a [heteroclinic cycle](../../../../../../heteroclinic-cycle.md) formed by the two separatrix connections between those saddles. The stable [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) expands into that connecting cycle and disappears; the two connections occur together because $(q,p)\mapsto(-q,-p)$ exchanges them. This is a global transition in the positive-$\kappa$, positive-$\lambda$ region where the [Bendixson-Dulac criterion](../../../../../../bendixson-dulac-theorem.md) permits cycles. Beyond it, the cubic inverted potential allows trajectories to leave the local region; the local reduction does not specify their ultimate fate in the original four-dimensional system.

In the small-$s$ case, the two stable cycles born around the nonzero well [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) can encounter the central saddle simultaneously. Their limiting orbits form a symmetric figure-eight pair of [homoclinic orbits](../../../../../../homoclinic-orbit.md). A [symmetric homoclinic gluing bifurcation](../../../../../../symmetric-homoclinic-gluing-bifurcation.md) can then replace the two one-well cycles by one larger cycle visiting both wells. There is also an outer unstable cycle associated with the subcritical origin Hopf branch, continued into the double-well region. A [saddle-node bifurcation of periodic orbits](../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation-of-periodic-orbits.md) between the large stable and unstable cycles provides a possible later termination. Thus the global curves needed for the usual completion are **a symmetric heteroclinic connection in the large-$s$ case, and paired homoclinic gluing together with a fold of cycles in the small-$s$ case**.

These connections, and any resulting basin changes, are not detected by the [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) Jacobians. Their precise parameter curves require a global return-map or separatrix calculation, which is not part of this requested qualitative description. Symmetry forces paired connections where indicated, but the local [centre manifold](../../../../../../center-manifold.md) and Hopf calculations alone should not be presented as numerical locations or a proof of the full large-amplitude diagram.
