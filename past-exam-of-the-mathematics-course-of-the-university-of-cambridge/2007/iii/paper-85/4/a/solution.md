<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a Hopf critical eigenspace, equip the real space $V\oplus V$ with the complex structure defined by the temporal phase action. A subgroup $\Sigma\subseteq G\times S^1$ is a [complex axial isotropy subgroup](../../../../../../complex-axial-isotropy-subgroup.md) when it is the full stabilizer of a nonzero vector and

$$
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb C}\operatorname{Fix}(\Sigma)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\dim_{\mathbb R}\operatorname{Fix}(\Sigma)=2.}
$$

The isotropy is for the combined spatial and temporal action, so its elements encode [spatiotemporal symmetry of a periodic orbit](../../../../../../spatiotemporal-symmetry-of-a-periodic-orbit.md), not necessarily pointwise spatial symmetry. “Complex dimension” here refers to the Hopf complex structure; it need not agree with independently chosen spatial complex coordinates. Any kernel of the combined action belongs to every actual isotropy subgroup.
