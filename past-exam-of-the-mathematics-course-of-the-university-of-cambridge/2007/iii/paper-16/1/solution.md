↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [cellular homology of complex projective space](../../../../../cellular-homology-of-complex-projective-space.md) gives one integral generator $[\mathbb{CP}^j]$ in each degree $2j$, for $0\le j\le2$, and zero in odd degrees. Since these groups are free, the [Künneth theorem](../../../../../kunneth-theorem.md) gives the [homology cross product](../../../../../homology-cross-product.md) basis

$$
\boxed{[\mathbb{CP}^i]\times[\mathbb{CP}^j],\qquad0\le i,j\le2,\qquad\text{degree }2(i+j).}
$$

Thus the ranks in degrees $0,2,4,6,8$ are $1,2,3,2,1$, respectively, and every other [integral homology](../../../../../integral-homology.md) group vanishes.

Let $h$ be the normalized [hyperplane class](../../../../../hyperplane-class.md), with $\langle h^2,[\mathbb{CP}^2]\rangle=1$, and write $a=p_1^*h$, $b=p_2^*h$. The [cohomology ring of complex projective space](../../../../../cohomology-ring-of-complex-projective-space.md) and the [Künneth theorem](../../../../../kunneth-theorem.md) give $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^2\times\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z[a,b]/(a^3,b^3)$. If $d$ is the [diagonal embedding](../../../../../diagonal-map.md), then $d^*a=d^*b=h$. Hence each of $a^2,ab,b^2$ evaluates as one on the diagonal's [fundamental class](../../../../../fundamental-class.md). These three classes are dual to the three degree-four product generators, so the [diagonal class of complex projective space](../../../../../diagonal-class-of-complex-projective-space.md) gives

$$
\boxed{[\Delta]=[\mathbb{CP}^2]\times[\mathrm{pt}]+[\mathbb{CP}^1]\times[\mathbb{CP}^1]+[\mathrm{pt}]\times[\mathbb{CP}^2].}
$$

Equivalently its [Poincare dual](../../../../../poincare-dual.md) is $a^2+ab+b^2$. The signs are positive with the complex and product [orientations](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
