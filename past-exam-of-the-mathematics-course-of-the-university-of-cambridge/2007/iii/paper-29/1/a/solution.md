<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\Lambda=\mathbb Z\omega_1+\mathbb Z\omega_2$ be a [complex lattice](../../../../../../complex-lattice.md). Orient its basis so that $\operatorname{Im}(\omega_1/\omega_2)>0$. Multiplication by $\omega_2^{-1}$ gives the normalized [complex lattice](../../../../../../complex-lattice.md) $\mathbb Z\tau+\mathbb Z$ with $\tau=\omega_1/\omega_2\in\mathbb H$. Thus every [homothety of complex lattices](../../../../../../homothety-of-complex-lattices.md) class has a normalized representative.

If $\gamma=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in SL_2(\mathbb Z)$, then

$$
\mathbb Z(\gamma\tau)+\mathbb Z
=(c\tau+d)^{-1}\bigl(\mathbb Z(a\tau+b)+\mathbb Z(c\tau+d)\bigr)
=(c\tau+d)^{-1}(\mathbb Z\tau+\mathbb Z).
$$

The last equality follows because the two integer [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) are an integral basis: their [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is one. Hence points in the same modular [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) give [homothetic complex lattices](../../../../../../homothety-of-complex-lattices.md).

Conversely, suppose $\Lambda_{\tau'}=\alpha\Lambda_\tau$ for $\alpha\ne0$. There is an integer basis-change [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) of [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $\pm1$ such that

$$
\tau'=\alpha(a\tau+b),\qquad 1=\alpha(c\tau+d).
$$

Both normalized bases have positive [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md), and multiplication by $\alpha$ preserves real [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) because its real [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is $|\alpha|^2>0$. The basis-change [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is therefore $+1$. Dividing the two equalities gives $\tau'=(a\tau+b)/(c\tau+d)$. Thus **the modular orbits and [homothety of complex lattices](../../../../../../homothety-of-complex-lattices.md) classes correspond bijectively**.
