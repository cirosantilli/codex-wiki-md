<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [complex lattice](../../../../../../complex-lattice.md), put $g_2=60G_4$ and $g_3=140G_6$. The [complex lattice](../../../../../../complex-lattice.md) form of the [Klein j-invariant](../../../../../../klein-j-invariant.md) is

$$
j(\Lambda)=1728\frac{g_2^3}{g_2^3-27g_3^2}.
$$

The denominator is nonzero for a [complex lattice](../../../../../../complex-lattice.md): after normalization it is a nonzero scalar multiple of $E_4^3-E_6^2$, whose absence of zeros was proved in part (b). The equal [lattice Eisenstein sums](../../../../../../lattice-eisenstein-sum.md) therefore give equal $j$ values, so parts (a) and (b) give $\Lambda'=\alpha\Lambda$ for some $\alpha\ne0$. Scaling the absolutely convergent [lattice Eisenstein sums](../../../../../../lattice-eisenstein-sum.md) gives

$$
G_4(\alpha\Lambda)=\alpha^{-4}G_4(\Lambda),\qquad
G_6(\alpha\Lambda)=\alpha^{-6}G_6(\Lambda).
$$

If both sums are nonzero, $\alpha^4=\alpha^6=1$, so $\alpha^2=1$ and $\alpha\Lambda=\Lambda$.

If $G_6=0$, then $G_4\ne0$ and $j=1728$. The [complex lattice](../../../../../../complex-lattice.md) is homothetic to $\mathbb Z i+\mathbb Z$, which is preserved by multiplication by $i$. The constraint $\alpha^4=1$ allows only powers of $i$, each already preserving the [complex lattice](../../../../../../complex-lattice.md). If $G_4=0$, then $G_6\ne0$ and $j=0$. The [complex lattice](../../../../../../complex-lattice.md) is homothetic to $\mathbb Z\rho+\mathbb Z$. Multiplication by $\rho$ preserves this [complex lattice](../../../../../../complex-lattice.md) because $\rho^2=-1-\rho$, and multiplication by $-1$ always preserves it. The constraint $\alpha^6=1$ allows exactly the sixth roots generated by these two multiplications. Again $\alpha\Lambda=\Lambda$. Both sums cannot be zero simultaneously because the discriminant is nonzero. Hence in every case

$$
\boxed{\Lambda'=\Lambda.}
$$

The exceptional cases are essential in [a complex lattice is determined by its fourth and sixth Eisenstein sums](../../../../../../a-complex-lattice-is-determined-by-its-fourth-and-sixth-eisenstein-sums.md); merely deducing [homothety of complex lattices](../../../../../../homothety-of-complex-lattices.md) would not prove equality.
