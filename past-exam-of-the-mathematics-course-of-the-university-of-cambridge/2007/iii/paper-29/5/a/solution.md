<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Pass to the effective subgroup $\overline\Gamma\subset PSL_2(\mathbb Z)$. Its index is $\mu$: adjoining $-I$ halves the original index precisely when $-I\notin\Gamma$. The natural map of compactified modular curves

$$
\pi:X(\Gamma)\longrightarrow X(1)\cong\mathbb P^1
$$

has degree $\mu$, since a generic [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) has that many lifts. It branches only over the order-two elliptic [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), the order-three elliptic [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), and the [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md). Away from these, the modular quotient charts are genuine unramified covering charts.

Over $i$, a lift with its own effective [stabilizer](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) of order two is unramified; there are $\nu_2$ such lifts. Every other lift has [ramification index](../../../../../../ramification-index.md) two. As the sum of all ramification indices in that fibre is $\mu$, their number is $(\mu-\nu_2)/2$ and their contribution to the ramification [divisor](../../../../../../divisor.md) is $(\mu-\nu_2)/2$. Similarly, over $\rho$ there are $\nu_3$ unramified elliptic lifts and $(\mu-\nu_3)/3$ lifts of [ramification index](../../../../../../ramification-index.md) three, contributing $2(\mu-\nu_3)/3$.

At a [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) of width $h$, the local parameters are $q_h=e^{2\pi i\tau/h}$ upstairs and $q=e^{2\pi i\tau}$ downstairs. Thus $q=q_h^h$ and the [ramification index](../../../../../../ramification-index.md) is $h$. Summing the widths gives $\mu$, so all $\nu_\infty$ [modular cusps](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) contribute $\mu-\nu_\infty$ to ramification. The [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](../../../../../../riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) now gives

$$
2g-2=-2\mu+\frac{\mu-\nu_2}{2}
+\frac{2(\mu-\nu_3)}{3}+\mu-\nu_\infty
=\frac{\mu}{6}-\frac{\nu_2}{2}-\frac{2\nu_3}{3}-\nu_\infty.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{g=1+\frac{\mu}{12}-\frac{\nu_2}{4}-\frac{\nu_3}{3}-\frac{\nu_\infty}{2}.}
$$

The effective index and the projective [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) widths ensure that the formula also applies when the original subgroup does not contain $-I$; no spurious factor of two is introduced.
