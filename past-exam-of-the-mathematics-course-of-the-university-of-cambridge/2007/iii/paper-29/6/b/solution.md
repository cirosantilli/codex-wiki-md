<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $\tau=x+iy$ and $\sigma=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$. Since $ad-bc=1$,

$$
\operatorname{Im}(\sigma\tau)=\frac{y}{|c\tau+d|^2}.
$$

Consequently, for the [invariant norm of a modular form](../../../../../../invariant-norm-of-a-modular-form.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
\phi(\sigma\tau)
&=|f(\sigma\tau)|\left(\frac{y}{|c\tau+d|^2}\right)^{k/2}\\
&=|(c\tau+d)^{-k}f(\sigma\tau)|y^{k/2}
=|f|_k\sigma(\tau)|y^{k/2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This computation applies to an arbitrary function; modularity is needed only to deduce $\Gamma$-invariance of $\phi$. The exponent is **$k/2$**, as printed in the PDF, rather than the converted TeX's $1/2$.
