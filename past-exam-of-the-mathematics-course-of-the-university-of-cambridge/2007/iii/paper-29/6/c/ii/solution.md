<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The transformation condition is necessary by the definition of a [cusp form](../../../../../../../cusp-form.md). For sufficiency, fix $\sigma\in SL_2(\mathbb Z)$ representing any [modular cusp](../../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md), and set $g=f|_k\sigma$. By item (i), $g$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) in the upper half-plane. Choose an actual period $h$ with $\sigma T^h\sigma^{-1}=\gamma\in\Gamma$. Associativity of slash operations and the given transformation law give

$$
g|_kT^h=f|_k\sigma T^h=f|_k\gamma\sigma=(f|_k\gamma)|_k\sigma=g.
$$

Thus $g(\tau+h)=g(\tau)$ and $g$ is a single-valued [holomorphic function](../../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) on a punctured disk with coordinate $q_h=e^{2\pi i\tau/h}$. The calculation in part (b) transports the original uniform bound to this translate:

$$
|g(\tau)|y^{k/2}=\phi(\sigma\tau)\leq B.
$$

This puts every [modular cusp](../../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) in exactly the situation needed in item (iii). No assumption that a single [modular cusp](../../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) represents all of $\Gamma$'s [modular cusps](../../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) is made.
