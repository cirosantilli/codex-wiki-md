<h1 id="6/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

First suppose $f$ is a [cusp form](../../../../../../../cusp-form.md). Choose finitely many representatives $\sigma_j$ for $\Gamma\backslash SL_2(\mathbb Z)$; their translates of the standard modular region cover a fundamental region for $\Gamma$. On the compact truncated part of each translate, the [continuous function](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $|f(\tau)|y^{k/2}$ is bounded. On its tail, the corresponding [modular cusp](../../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) expansion of $g_j=f|_k\sigma_j$ has zero constant term, so $g_j=O(e^{-2\pi y/h_j})$ uniformly in the real part. Therefore $y^{k/2}|g_j|$ is bounded there and tends to zero. Part (b) identifies this expression with $\phi(\sigma_j\tau)$. There are only finitely many representatives; taking the maximum of these bounds and using $\Gamma$-invariance proves that **$\phi$ is bounded on all of $\mathbb H$**.

Conversely assume all three conditions and $k>0$. Items (i) and (ii) show that at each [modular cusp](../../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) a [holomorphic](../../../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) periodic translate $g$ satisfies $|g(\tau)|\leq B y^{-k/2}$. In its punctured-disk coordinate,

$$
y=\frac{h}{2\pi}\log\frac1{|q_h|},\qquad
|g(q_h)|\leq B\left(\frac{h}{2\pi}\log\frac1{|q_h|}\right)^{-k/2}\longrightarrow0.
$$

The [Riemann removable singularity theorem](../../../../../../../riemann-removable-singularity-theorem.md) extends $g$ holomorphically to zero with $g(0)=0$. Equivalently its Laurent expansion has no negative powers and no constant term. Since this holds at every [modular cusp](../../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md), $f$ is a [cusp form](../../../../../../../cusp-form.md). Hence the three conditions are sufficient as well as necessary in positive weight, with the weight-zero exception explained in the root solution.
