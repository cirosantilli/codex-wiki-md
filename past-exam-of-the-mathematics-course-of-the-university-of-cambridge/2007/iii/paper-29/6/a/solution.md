<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an integer weight $k$, a [cusp form](../../../../../../cusp-form.md) for a [congruence subgroup](../../../../../../congruence-subgroup.md) $\Gamma$ is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) on $\mathbb H$ with $f|_k\gamma=f$ for every $\gamma\in\Gamma$, whose [Fourier expansion of a modular form](../../../../../../fourier-expansion-of-a-modular-form.md) vanishes at every [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md). More explicitly, choose $\sigma\in SL_2(\mathbb Z)$ representing a [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) $\sigma\infty$, and a genuine translation period $h>0$ with $\sigma T^h\sigma^{-1}\in\Gamma$. Then the requirement is

$$
(f|_k\sigma)(\tau)=\sum_{n\geq1}a_{\sigma,n}e^{2\pi in\tau/h},
$$

convergent for sufficiently large imaginary part. Thus the slash translate extends holomorphically at $q_h=0$ and has value zero. The [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) space of these functions is $S_k(\Gamma)$.

A genuine period is convenient when $-I$ is absent and the weight is odd: the smaller effective [cusp width](../../../../../../width-of-a-cusp.md) may supply a negative rather than positive translation in $\Gamma$, but an actual period always exists, possibly twice that width. Using an actual period avoids imposing an incorrect periodicity. The zero-value condition is independent of the chosen representative or period. If $-I\in\Gamma$ and $k$ is odd, its slash relation forces $f=(-1)^kf=-f$, so the space is zero.
