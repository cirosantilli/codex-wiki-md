↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Kleinian group](../../../../../../kleinian-group.md) is a discrete subgroup of the [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md)-preserving isometry group of $\mathbb H^3$, identified with $\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb C)$. For an abstract finitely generated group this means a faithful representation into $\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb C)$ whose image is discrete; the chosen image is the geometric realization. Its boundary action is by [Möbius transformations](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) on $S^2_\infty\cong\mathbb C\cup\{\infty\}$.

Discreteness means the identity is isolated in the group topology, and for a subgroup it also excludes an infinite sequence of distinct elements converging anywhere in the isometry group. Torsion-freeness is not part of this definition: [elliptic Möbius transformations](../../../../../../elliptic-element-of-psl2-r.md) of finite order are allowed. Therefore a Kleinian quotient need not be a smooth hyperbolic manifold.
