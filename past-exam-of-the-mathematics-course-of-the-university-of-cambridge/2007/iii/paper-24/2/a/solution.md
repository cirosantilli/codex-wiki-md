<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Work in the smooth or piecewise-linear category; embedded spheres and disks are locally flat. A closed connected orientable [three-manifold](../../../../../../3-manifold.md) is an [irreducible three-manifold](../../../../../../irreducible-three-manifold.md) when every embedded $S^2$ bounds an embedded three-ball. It is a [prime three-manifold](../../../../../../prime-three-manifold.md) when $M\cong P\mathbin\#Q$ forces $P\cong S^3$ or $Q\cong S^3$. A separating sphere gives such a [connected sum](../../../../../../connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) after capping its two sides with balls. Irreducibility therefore implies primeness, but a prime manifold can still have a nonseparating sphere.

A [geometrizable three-manifold](../../../../../../geometrizable-three-manifold.md) is one whose prime summands, after sphere splitting and capping, can be cut along disjoint essential [tori](../../../../../../torus.md) into pieces whose interiors admit complete geometric structures modeled on the eight [Thurston geometries](../../../../../../thurston-geometry.md):

$$
S^3,\quad\mathbb R^3,\quad\mathbb H^3,\quad S^2\times\mathbb R,\quad
\mathbb H^2\times\mathbb R,\quad\widetilde{SL}_2(\mathbb R),\quad\mathrm{Nil},\quad\mathrm{Sol}.
$$

Here a geometric structure is a locally homogeneous quotient structure modeled on the stated simply connected space. The original manifold need not itself carry one geometry before decomposition.

A [Heegaard splitting](../../../../../../heegaard-splitting.md) is $M=H_1\cup_\phi H_2$, where $H_1,H_2$ are [handlebodies](../../../../../../handlebody.md) with their boundaries identified. Its genus is the common boundary genus. The [Heegaard genus](../../../../../../heegaard-genus.md) $g_H(M)$ is the least possible genus of such a splitting. Thus **“genus $g$” as a minimum requires both a genus-$g$ splitting and the nonexistence of one of smaller genus**; an arbitrary genus-$g$ splitting only proves $g_H(M)\le g$.
