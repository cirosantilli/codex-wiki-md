<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $|\phi_A\rangle=\sum_jc_j|j\rangle_A$ and choose the [conjugate index vector](../../../../../../conjugate-index-vector.md)

$$
|\phi_B^*\rangle=\sum_jc_j^*|j\rangle_B,\qquad\langle\phi_B^*|=\sum_jc_j\langle j|_B.
$$

With $|\widetilde\Psi_{AB}\rangle=\sqrt d|\Psi_{AB}\rangle=\sum_j|j\rangle_A|j\rangle_B$, the partial inner product gives

$$
\boxed{(I_A\otimes\langle\phi_B^*|)|\widetilde\Psi_{AB}\rangle=\sum_jc_j|j\rangle_A=|\phi_A\rangle.}
$$

This is the [relative state](../../../../../../relative-state-of-a-bipartite-vector.md) construction. The coefficient conjugation is defined relative to the fixed paired bases; it is essential, because the bra conjugates those coefficients again. Using the normalized entangled vector instead would give $|\phi_A\rangle/\sqrt d$.
