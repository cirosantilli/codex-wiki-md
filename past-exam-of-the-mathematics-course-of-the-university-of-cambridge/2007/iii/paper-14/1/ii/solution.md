↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [finite colouring](../../../../../../finite-coloring.md) $c$, colour each unordered pair $\{a,b\}$, written with $a<b$, by $c(a+2b)$. The infinite [Ramsey theorem for r-sets](../../../../../../ramsey-s-theorem.md) again gives an infinite [homogeneous set for a colouring](../../../../../../homogeneous-set-for-a-colouring.md). If its increasing enumeration is $x_1<x_2<\cdots$, then

$$
\boxed{\{x_i+2x_j:i<j\}\text{ is monochromatic}.}
$$

The ordering in this pair colouring is essential: it assigns the coefficient $2$ to the larger member.
