<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Every [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) contains exactly one cell of any finite [set partition](../../../../../../set-partition.md). Indeed, two disjoint cells cannot both belong to a proper [filter](../../../../../../filter-set-theory.md), while if none belonged to the [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md), all their complements would belong to it and their finite [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) would be empty.

Apply this property to the two [colour classes](../../../../../../colour-class.md) in (iii). One belongs to any proposed [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md), but neither contains a symmetric weighted-pair configuration from an increasing infinite [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md). This contradicts the proposed property of every member. Therefore **no such [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) exists**.
