<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The same [cone on an infinite coinfinite ground set](../../../../../../cone-on-an-infinite-coinfinite-ground-set.md) works:

$$
\boxed{E=[2\mathbb N]^\omega.}
$$

It is $\tau$-closed: a [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) outside $E$ has an odd point, and the ordinary [neighbourhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) given by its initial segment through that point remains outside $E$. Its $\tau$-[interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md) is empty, since every ordinary basic [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) admits an extension having an odd point. Hence its ordinary [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md) equals itself and has empty [interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md), proving that it is a $\tau$-[nowhere dense set](../../../../../../nowhere-dense-set.md).

In the [Ellentuck topology](../../../../../../ellentuck-topology.md), however, $E$ is itself a nonempty [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md). Its $*$-[closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md) therefore has nonempty $*$-[interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md), so $E$ is **not $*$-nowhere dense**.
