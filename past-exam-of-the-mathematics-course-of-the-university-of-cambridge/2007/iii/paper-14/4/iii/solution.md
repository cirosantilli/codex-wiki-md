<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let

$$
\boxed{D=\{X\subseteq\mathbb N:X\text{ is cofinite}\}.}
$$

This is the [dense countable family of cofinite infinite subsets](../../../../../../dense-countable-family-of-cofinite-infinite-subsets.md). Every ordinary basic [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $[s]$ contains the [cofinite set](../../../../../../cofinite-set.md) $s\cup\{n:n>\max s\}$. Thus $D$ is $\tau$-dense: its ordinary [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md) is the whole space, so it is **not $\tau$-nowhere dense**.

It is $*$-closed. Indeed, for an infinite noncofinite $X$, the [Ellentuck topology](../../../../../../ellentuck-topology.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $[\varnothing,X]$ contains only noncofinite [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md), because every [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of $X$ misses the infinite [complement](../../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) of $X$. This [neighbourhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) is disjoint from $D$.

Finally every nonempty basic $*$-[open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $[s,A]$ contains a smaller basic $*$-[open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) disjoint from $D$: enumerate $A$ and let $B$ consist of every second point. Then $A\setminus B$ is infinite, and $[s,B]\subseteq[s,A]$ contains no [cofinite set](../../../../../../cofinite-set.md). In particular $D$ has empty $*$-[interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md). As it is already $*$-closed, its $*$-[closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md) has empty $*$-[interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md), proving that it is **$*$-nowhere dense**.
