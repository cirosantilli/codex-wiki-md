<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the [cone on an infinite coinfinite ground set](../../../../../../cone-on-an-infinite-coinfinite-ground-set.md)

$$
\boxed{E=[2\mathbb N]^\omega.}
$$

It is $*$-open because $E=[\varnothing,2\mathbb N]$ is a basic [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) of the [Ellentuck topology](../../../../../../ellentuck-topology.md). It is not $\tau$-open: if $X\in E$, every ordinary basic [neighbourhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $[s]$ of $X$ contains an infinite extension of $s$ having an odd point larger than $\max s$. That extension is outside $E$. Therefore no point of $E$ has an ordinary open [neighbourhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) contained in $E$.
