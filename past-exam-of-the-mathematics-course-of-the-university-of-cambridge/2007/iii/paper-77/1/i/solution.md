<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $0<\omega<N$, put $\alpha=\omega/\sqrt{N^2-\omega^2}$. The [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) permits $k=\pm\alpha m$. On the positive-frequency branch, for $k>0$,

$$
\omega=\frac{Nk}{\sqrt{k^2+m^2}},\qquad c_{gx}=\frac{Nm^2}{(k^2+m^2)^{3/2}}>0,\qquad c_{gz}=-\frac{Nkm}{(k^2+m^2)^{3/2}}.
$$

The [radiation condition](../../../../../../radiation-condition.md) therefore selects $k=\alpha m$, since [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) must travel into the fluid. Substitution into the [internal-wave polarization](../../../../../../internal-wave-polarization.md) relations gives the complex fields

$$
\boxed{u=\epsilon e^{i(\alpha mx+mz-\omega t)},\qquad w=-\alpha\epsilon e^{i(\alpha mx+mz-\omega t)},\qquad \sigma=\frac{iN^2\alpha\epsilon}{\omega}e^{i(\alpha mx+mz-\omega t)}.}
$$

The physical fields are their real parts; $v=0$. The vertical component of [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) is negative, so this positive-$m$ [plane internal gravity wave](../../../../../../plane-internal-gravity-wave.md) carries [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) down and right, although its phase propagates in a different direction.
