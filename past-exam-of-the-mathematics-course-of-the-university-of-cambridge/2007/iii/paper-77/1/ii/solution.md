<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\omega>N$, the [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) gives $k^2=-m^2\omega^2/(\omega^2-N^2)$. Put $\kappa=m\omega/\sqrt{\omega^2-N^2}>0$. Boundedness as $x\to+\infty$ selects $k=i\kappa$, rather than the exponentially growing root. The [internal-wave polarization](../../../../../../internal-wave-polarization.md) relations now yield

$$
\boxed{u=\epsilon e^{-\kappa x+i(mz-\omega t)},\qquad w=-\frac{i\kappa}{m}\epsilon e^{-\kappa x+i(mz-\omega t)},\qquad \sigma=-\frac{N^2\kappa}{m\omega}\epsilon e^{-\kappa x+i(mz-\omega t)}.}
$$

Again take real parts and set $v=0$. This is an [evanescent wave](../../../../../../evanescent-wave.md), decaying over distance $\kappa^{-1}$; there is no propagating far-field [internal gravity wave](../../../../../../internal-wave.md) at this [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md). The [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) amplitude $P=-i\omega u/\kappa$ is in quadrature with the horizontal [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md), so the time-averaged horizontal pressure-work flux vanishes. The equality $\omega=N$ is a singular limiting case and is outside the two [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) ranges considered here.
