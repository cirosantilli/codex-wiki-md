<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), choose a [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) and a [positive root](../../../../../../positive-root.md) system $R^+$, and let $\lambda$ be a dominant integral [highest weight](../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md). Write $L(\lambda)$ for the corresponding finite-dimensional irreducible representation, $\alpha^\vee=2\alpha/(\alpha,\alpha)$ for a [coroot](../../../../../../coroot.md), and $\rho=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha\in R^+}\alpha$ for the [Weyl vector](../../../../../../half-sum-of-positive-roots.md). The [Weyl dimension formula](../../../../../../weyl-dimension-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dim L(\lambda)=\prod_{\alpha\in R^+}
\frac{\langle\lambda+\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}{\langle\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}.}
$$

The bracket denotes the natural pairing of [weights](../../../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) with [coroots](../../../../../../coroot.md). The normalization of an invariant inner product cancels from each ratio, so the formula can equivalently use roots instead of [coroots](../../../../../../coroot.md) in both numerator and denominator.
