<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

On $\mathbb C^3$ take the [matrix units](../../../../../../matrix-unit.md)

$$
\boxed{\rho(p)=E_{12},\qquad\rho(q)=E_{23},\qquad\rho(c)=E_{13}.}
$$

Their products give $[E_{12},E_{23}]=E_{13}$, while $E_{13}$ commutes with each of the other two. Thus this is a [Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md). The three images are [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md), so its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is zero: it is **faithful and finite-dimensional**. It is reducible, as expected from the classification in part ii; for example, the first coordinate line is invariant.
