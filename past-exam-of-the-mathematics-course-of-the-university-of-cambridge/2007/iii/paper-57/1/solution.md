<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [Noether gauging procedure](../../../../../noether-gauging-procedure.md) begins with a rigid symmetry parameter $\epsilon$. Replacing it by $\epsilon(x)$ leaves in the action variation a term proportional to $j^\mu\partial_\mu\epsilon$. Introduce a [gauge field](../../../../../gauge-field.md) whose variation contains $\partial_\mu\epsilon$ and couple it to the [Noether current](../../../../../noether-current.md) $j^\mu$ to cancel that term. The new coupling produces further variations, so both the action and transformation laws are corrected successively in the coupling constant until local invariance is obtained. [Covariant derivatives](../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) organize this iterative completion.

For the [Wess–Zumino model](../../../../../wess-zumino-model.md), the parameter is a spinor and the current is the [supercurrent](../../../../../supercurrent.md). Its derivative part has the structure $\gamma^\nu\partial_\nu(A-i\gamma_5B)\gamma^\mu\psi$, up to the fixed [Majorana spinor](../../../../../majorana-spinor.md) normalization and improvements. The [gauge field](../../../../../gauge-field.md) is the [spin](../../../../../spin.md)-$3/2$ [gravitino](../../../../../gravitino.md) $\Psi_\mu$, with leading transformation $\delta\Psi_\mu=\kappa^{-1}D_\mu\epsilon+\cdots$. A coupling of $\bar\Psi_\mu$ to that current cancels the derivative of the local parameter. Since the commutator of two local [supersymmetries](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) includes a spacetime transformation, the [vierbein](../../../../../orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) and gravity must participate as well. This gives the [Wess-Zumino chiral multiplet coupled to supergravity](../../../../../wess-zumino-chiral-multiplet-coupled-to-supergravity.md).

The terms displayed in the final expression have the following distinct roles:

- The [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) [kinetic term](../../../../../kinetic-term.md) acquires the invariant volume density $e$ and the inverse [metric tensor](../../../../../metric-tensor.md). For a [Lorentz scalar](../../../../../lorentz-scalar.md) the spacetime [covariant derivative](../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) is just its ordinary derivative; the metric contraction is what changes from the rigid theory.
- The [Majorana spinor](../../../../../majorana-spinor.md) [kinetic term](../../../../../kinetic-term.md) uses curved [gamma matrices](../../../../../gamma-matrices.md) $\gamma^\mu=e_a{}^\mu\gamma^a$ and the [spinor covariant derivative](../../../../../spinor-covariant-derivative.md). The factor one half avoids double counting the real Majorana degrees of freedom. The [spin connection](../../../../../spin-connection.md) makes the term locally [Lorentz covariant](../../../../../lorentz-covariance.md).
- The curvature term is the [Einstein-Hilbert action](../../../../../einstein-hilbert-action.md), with gravitational coupling $\kappa$. Its variation cancels the curvature terms generated when [covariant derivatives](../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) are commuted in the [gravitino](../../../../../gravitino.md) variation. Its sign follows the source's curvature and metric conventions.
- The [gravitino](../../../../../gravitino.md) [kinetic term](../../../../../kinetic-term.md) is the curved-space [Rarita-Schwinger field](../../../../../rarita-schwinger-field.md) action. It supplies the dynamical [gauge field](../../../../../gauge-field.md) needed to localize [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md), just as a gauge-field kinetic term accompanies an ordinary gauging.
- The term of order $\kappa$ is the [gravitino](../../../../../gravitino.md)–[supercurrent](../../../../../supercurrent.md) coupling. Its scalar factor must be differentiated: in standard notation it contains $\not\!D(A-i\gamma_5B)$. The glyph rendered as $\phi$ before the parentheses in the source formula must be read as a slashed derivative, or corrected to one; the undifferentiated scalar product would not be the free [Wess–Zumino model](../../../../../wess-zumino-model.md) [supercurrent](../../../../../supercurrent.md).
- The order-$\kappa^2$ term bilinear in [gravitini](../../../../../gravitino.md) couples their chiral current to the [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) phase [Noether current](../../../../../noether-current.md) $J_\mu=A D_\mu B-(D_\mu A)B$.
- The remaining order-$\kappa^2$ term couples the matter [axial current](../../../../../axial-current.md) $\bar\psi\gamma_5\gamma^\mu\psi$ to the same $J_\mu$.

The last two interactions are part of the higher-order local [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) completion. They can be organized through the [composite Kähler connection](../../../../../composite-kahler-connection.md) in the fermion derivatives, or derived by eliminating the corresponding supergravity auxiliary connection. Matter fermions and [gravitini](../../../../../gravitino.md) carry the appropriate chiral Kähler weights, fixing the relative current couplings. They are not new adjustable [Yukawa interactions](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md). In reading this organization, one must not include the composite connection in $D_\mu$ and then count its separately displayed current terms a second time.

Finally the [spin connection](../../../../../spin-connection.md) itself acquires [gravitino-induced torsion](../../../../../gravitino-induced-torsion.md) and matter spin-current contributions. Eliminating this algebraic connection, or solving for [auxiliary fields](../../../../../auxiliary-field.md), can make further fermion contact terms implicit in the chosen [covariant derivatives](../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) and component convention. The key mechanism is **current coupling at first order, followed by the covariant and higher-order terms required by local [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md)**, rather than merely changing ordinary derivatives in the original matter Lagrangian.
