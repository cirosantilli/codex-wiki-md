Put $V=\mathbb R^d$ and write $a^{(k)}$ for the degree-$k$ component of a tensor. We use the [truncated tensor algebra](../../../../../truncated-tensor-algebra.md)

$$
T^{(N)}(V)=\bigoplus_{k=0}^N V^{\otimes k},\qquad
(a\otimes b)^{(k)}=\sum_{j=0}^k a^{(j)}\otimes b^{(k-j)}.
$$

Here $V^{\otimes0}=\mathbb R$, and products of degree above $N$ are discarded. The answers to the three printed parts follow with these conventions; the middle part is missing from the converted TeX.
