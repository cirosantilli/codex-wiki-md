↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The process $W_t=a^{-1}B_{a^2t}$ is standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md), by [Brownian scaling](../../../../../../brownian-scaling.md). Its first exit from $(-1,1)$ occurs at $\sigma_a/a^2$, so

$$
\sigma_a\ \stackrel{d}=\ a^2\sigma_1.
$$

The first part ensures integrability. Set $C=\mathbb E\sigma_1$; it is finite and strictly positive because path continuity at zero gives $\sigma_1>0$ almost surely. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E\sigma_a=Ca^2\quad(a>0).}
$$

We keep $C$ unspecified until the comparison of limits requested below.
