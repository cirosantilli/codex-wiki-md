<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In units $\hbar=1$, a [bilinear Hamiltonian control system](../../../../../../bilinear-hamiltonian-control-system.md) has

$$
\dot U(t)=-i\left(H_0+\sum_{k=1}^m u_k(t)H_k\right)U(t),\qquad U(0)=I,
$$

where the [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) $H_0,H_k$ are fixed, and the real functions $u_k$ are admissible [control inputs](../../../../../../control-input.md). The corresponding vector or [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) dynamics are $\dot\psi=-iH(u)\psi$ or $\dot\rho=-i[H(u),\rho]$. The dynamics are linear in the state at fixed input and affine in the inputs at fixed state; this is the bilinear terminology.

The [reachable set](../../../../../../reachable-set.md) from an initial state $x_0$ at time $T$ is $\mathcal R_T(x_0)=\{x(T;x_0,u):u\text{ admissible}\}$. With freely chosen duration, take $\mathcal R(x_0)=\bigcup_{T\geq0}\mathcal R_T(x_0)$. **Controllability means every allowed target is reachable from every allowed initial state**. The allowed space and phase convention must be specified. For closed [quantum control](../../../../../../quantum-control.md), [density operators](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) remain on their initial [unitary orbit of a density operator](../../../../../../unitary-orbit-of-a-density-operator.md), rather than becoming arbitrary density operators with different [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
