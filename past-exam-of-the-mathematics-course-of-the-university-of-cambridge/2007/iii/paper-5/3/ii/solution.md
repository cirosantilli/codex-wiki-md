Conversely, if all $d(P_i)$ equal $d(G)$, the same Frattini-quotient identity gives $[P_i:P_{i+1}]=p^{d(G)}$ for every $i$. This is exactly the index definition of a [uniform pro-p group](../../../../../../uniform-pro-p-group.md). Thus **(ii)$\Rightarrow$(i)**, completing the equivalence of the first two conditions.
