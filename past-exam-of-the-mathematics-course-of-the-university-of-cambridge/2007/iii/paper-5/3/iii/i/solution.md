<h1 id="3/iii/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The map $\sigma_n:G\to U_n$, $x\mapsto x^{p^n}$, is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md), and the operation $+_n$ is exactly multiplication transported through it. Explicitly,

$$
\begin{aligned}
((x+_ny)+_nz)^{p^n}&=x^{p^n}y^{p^n}z^{p^n},\\
(x+_n(y+_nz))^{p^n}&=x^{p^n}y^{p^n}z^{p^n}.
\end{aligned}
$$

[Injectivity](../../../../../../../injective-function.md) of $\sigma_n$ proves **$(x+_ny)+_nz=x+_n(y+_nz)$**. The approximate operation need not be [commutative](../../../../../../../commutativity.md); its [associativity](../../../../../../../associative-property.md) is exact.
