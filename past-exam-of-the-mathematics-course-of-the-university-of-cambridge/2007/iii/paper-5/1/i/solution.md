<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $H$ is an [open subgroup](../../../../../../open-subgroup.md), each left [coset](../../../../../../coset.md) $gH$ is open because left translation is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md) in a [topological group](../../../../../../topological-group-split.md). Its complement is the union of all the other [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md),

$$
G\setminus H=\bigcup_{g\notin H}gH.
$$

This is open, so **$H$ is closed**. No [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) or separation assumption is required.
