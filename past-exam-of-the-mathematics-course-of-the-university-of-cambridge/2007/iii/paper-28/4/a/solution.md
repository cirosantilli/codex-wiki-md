<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expand the translated [cyclotomic polynomial](../../../../../../cyclotomic-polynomial.md):

$$
\frac{(1+T)^p-1}{T}=p+\binom p2T+\cdots+\binom p{p-1}T^{p-2}+T^{p-1}.
$$

Every nonleading coefficient is divisible by $p$, and the constant term is not divisible by $p^2$. The polynomial is [Eisenstein](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md), hence irreducible. Its root $\lambda=\zeta_p-1$ is a uniformizer of $E=\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_p)$, and the [Eisenstein polynomial](../../../../../../eisenstein-polynomial.md) valuation criterion gives

$$
\boxed{[E:\mathbb Q_p]=e(E/\mathbb Q_p)=p-1.}
$$

Thus this local cyclotomic extension is totally ramified.
