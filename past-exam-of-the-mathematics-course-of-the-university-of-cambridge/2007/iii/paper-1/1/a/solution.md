<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Interpret the map as the action on $\mathbb C^n$. A [smooth representation](../../../../../../smooth-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md) requires every vector to have an open stabilizer in the [locally profinite group](../../../../../../locally-profinite-group.md) $\operatorname{GL}_n(F)$. Take the first standard basis vector $e_1$. Every identity neighbourhood contains matrices $d_a=\operatorname{diag}(1+a,1,\ldots,1)$ with $a\ne0$ sufficiently small in the [local field](../../../../../../local-field.md). We may require $|a|<1$, so $1+a\ne0$. Injectivity of the [field embedding](../../../../../../field-embedding.md) gives

$$
\pi(d_a)e_1=(1+\iota(a))e_1\ne e_1.
$$

Thus no identity neighbourhood fixes $e_1$, and its stabilizer is not open. Consequently **the representation is not smooth for any $n\ge1$**. This includes $n=1$, when a nonzero vector's stabilizer is just the identity. The field embedding need not be continuous, but no continuity assumption would repair this stabilizer obstruction. The existence of the stated embedding itself forces characteristic zero; in positive characteristic no field embedding into $\mathbb C$ exists.
