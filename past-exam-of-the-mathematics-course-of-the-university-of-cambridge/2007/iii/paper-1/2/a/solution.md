<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $U_m=1+\mathfrak p^m$, $m\ge1$, for the [principal-unit filtration of a local field](../../../../../../principal-unit-filtration-of-a-local-field.md). Each [linear character](../../../../../../linear-character.md) $\chi_i$ is a [smooth character](../../../../../../smooth-character.md), since its kernel contains $U_{i+1}$. A vector in the algebraic [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md) has only finitely many nonzero coordinates. Taking $m$ larger than all their indices gives a common subgroup $U_m$ fixing the vector, so the [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md) is smooth.

If $i<m$, then $U_m\subseteq U_{i+1}$, so $V_i$ is fixed by $U_m$. If $i\ge m$, the subgroup $U_i$ lies inside $U_m$, and $\chi_i$ is nontrivial on $U_i$; hence $V_i^{U_m}=0$. It follows that

$$
\boxed{V^{U_m}=\bigoplus_{1\le i<m}V_i,\qquad\dim V^{U_m}=m-1.}
$$

Every [compact-open subgroup](../../../../../../compact-open-subgroup.md) $K$ of $F^\times$ contains some $U_m$, because it is an identity neighbourhood. Therefore $V^K\subseteq V^{U_m}$ is finite-dimensional. **The [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md) is an admissible representation**, despite its infinite total dimension.
