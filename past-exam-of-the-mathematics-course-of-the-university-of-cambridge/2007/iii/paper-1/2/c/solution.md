<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\ell=(\ell_i)_i$ be a [smooth vector](../../../../../../smooth-vector.md) in the product description of the [algebraic contragredient representation](../../../../../../algebraic-contragredient-representation.md). Its open stabilizer contains $U_m$ for some $m$. For every $i\ge m$, choose $u_i\in U_i\subseteq U_m$ with $\chi_i(u_i)\ne1$. Invariance of $\ell$ at its $i$th coordinate gives

$$
\chi_i(u_i)^{-1}\ell_i=\ell_i,
$$

forcing $\ell_i=0$. Hence a smooth functional has finite support, contained among $i<m$.

Conversely a finitely supported family has indices bounded by some $N$. Each inverse [linear character](../../../../../../linear-character.md) has the same kernel as its original [linear character](../../../../../../linear-character.md), and all those kernels contain $U_{N+1}$. Thus this subgroup fixes the family. The [smooth dual](../../../../../../smooth-dual.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{V^\vee=\bigoplus_{i\ge1}V_i^*.}
$$

This is the principle that [conductors force finite support in a smooth dual](../../../../../../conductors-force-finite-support-in-a-smooth-dual.md). For instance a product vector with every coordinate nonzero is a perfectly valid algebraic functional on the original [direct sum](../../../../../../direct-sum.md), but is not smooth.
