<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply part (a) with $\chi=\xi=1$. It gives $\dim\operatorname{End}_G(V(1))=1$, so every endomorphism is a scalar multiple of the identity. The [local Steinberg representation of GL2](../../../../../../local-steinberg-representation-of-gl2.md) in the supplied sequence is nonzero: $V(1)$ is the space of locally constant functions on $B\backslash G\cong\mathbb P^1(F)$, and the characteristic function of the compact-open affine set $\mathfrak o$ is not constant. Its class in the quotient by constants is therefore nonzero.

If the sequence split, $V(1)$ would be $1\oplus\operatorname{St}$ with both summands nonzero. Projection onto either summand would be a $G$-endomorphism. These two projections are linearly independent: restrict a proposed linear dependence first to one summand and then to the other. This contradicts the one-dimensional endomorphism space. Equivalently the projection onto the trivial summand would be a nonzero idempotent different from the identity, whereas a scalar idempotent can only be zero or the identity. The principle that [scalar endomorphisms obstruct a split extension](../../../../../../scalar-endomorphisms-obstruct-a-split-extension.md) gives

$$
\boxed{0\longrightarrow1\longrightarrow V(1)\longrightarrow\operatorname{St}\longrightarrow0\text{ does not split}.}
$$

No assertion of irreducibility of $V(1)$ is needed; this representation has a proper invariant line of constants.
