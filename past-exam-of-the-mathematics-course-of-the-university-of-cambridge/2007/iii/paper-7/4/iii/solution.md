<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Relabel each [duad](../../../../../../duad.md) of each [synthematic total](../../../../../../total-of-synthemes.md) by the chosen [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) and compare the resulting [synthematic total](../../../../../../total-of-synthemes.md) with the six labelled [synthematic totals](../../../../../../total-of-synthemes.md). For example, $(1\ 2)$ interchanges $T_1,T_2$, interchanges $T_3,T_4$, and interchanges $T_5,T_6$. Applying the same calculation to the other adjacent [transpositions](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
(1\ 2)^\alpha&=(1\ 2)(3\ 4)(5\ 6),\\
(2\ 3)^\alpha&=(1\ 6)(2\ 4)(3\ 5),\\
(3\ 4)^\alpha&=(1\ 2)(3\ 6)(4\ 5),\\
(4\ 5)^\alpha&=(1\ 6)(2\ 5)(3\ 4),\\
(5\ 6)^\alpha&=(1\ 2)(3\ 5)(4\ 6).
\end{aligned}}
$$

These formulae use the PDF's numbering of the [synthematic totals](../../../../../../total-of-synthemes.md), which is essential for the particular fixed elements below. Compose permutations from left to right, consistently with the right action on [synthematic totals](../../../../../../total-of-synthemes.md). Multiplying the second and fourth images gives $(2\ 3)(4\ 5)$, while multiplying the third and fifth gives $(3\ 4)(5\ 6)$. Hence the two [involutions](../../../../../../involution.md)

$$
\boxed{u=(2\ 3)(4\ 5),\qquad v=(3\ 4)(5\ 6)}
$$

are fixed by the [outer automorphism](../../../../../../outer-automorphism-of-a-group.md) $\alpha$. Since $\alpha$ is a [group automorphism](../../../../../../group-automorphism.md), their product is fixed as well. Applying $u$ and then $v$ yields

$$
\boxed{uv=(2\ 4\ 6\ 5\ 3),\qquad (uv)^\alpha=uv.}
$$

Under right-to-left composition the product is the inverse cycle, which is also fixed; the displayed convention removes that ambiguity.
