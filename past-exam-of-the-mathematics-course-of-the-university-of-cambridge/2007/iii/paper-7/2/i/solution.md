<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Identify $G$ with a subgroup of $\operatorname{Sym}(\Omega)$ and adjoin one new point $\infty$, fixed by every element of $G$. A [one-point extension of a permutation group](../../../../../../one-point-extension-of-a-permutation-group.md) is a transitive subgroup $G^+\le\operatorname{Sym}(\Omega\cup\{\infty\})$ whose [point stabilizer](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) at $\infty$ is exactly this embedded copy of $G$. Equivalently, one may use an isomorphic permutation representation with a distinguished point whose stabilizer acts on the remaining points as the original group.

Since $G$ is transitive on $\Omega$, the extension is [doubly transitive group action](../../../../../../two-transitive-group-action.md). The [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives the useful necessary order condition

$$
\boxed{|G^+|=(|\Omega|+1)|G|.}
$$
