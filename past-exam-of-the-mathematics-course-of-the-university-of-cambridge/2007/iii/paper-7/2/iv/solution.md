<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Adjoin $\infty$ to $\Omega=\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and take $G=A_n$ fixing it. Choose three distinct points $a,b,c\in\Omega$ and put $t=(\infty\ a)(b\ c)$. This is an even [involution](../../../../../../involution.md) interchanging $\infty$ and $a$. Conjugation by it preserves $H=G_a$, because it permutes the remaining $n-1$ points and preserves parity. Thus $tHt=H$.

If $g\in G\setminus H$, the even permutation $k=tgt$ moves $\infty$ into $\Omega$. The [alternating group](../../../../../../alternating-group.md) $A_n$ is transitive for $n\ge3$: a suitable 3-cycle carries any prescribed point to another. Choose $u\in G$ with $u(a)=k(\infty)$. Then $v=tu^{-1}k$ is even and fixes $\infty$, so $v\in G$ and $k=utv\in GtG$. All conditions of the [double-coset criterion for a one-point extension](../../../../../../double-coset-criterion-for-a-one-point-extension.md) hold, including when $n=3$ and $H$ is trivial.

The constructed extension lies in $A_{n+1}$ and has order $(n+1)|A_n|=(n+1)!/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{G^+=A_{n+1}\text{ in its natural action is a one-point extension of }A_n.}
$$
