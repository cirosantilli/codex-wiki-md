<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $x_{ij}(a)=I+aE_{ij}$ for $i\ne j$. For $a\ne0$ these are [transvections](../../../../../../transvection.md), and for $a=0$ they are the identity. Left multiplication by $x_{ij}(a)$ adds $a$ times row $j$ to row $i$. We show that these [elementary transvection matrices](../../../../../../elementary-transvection-matrix.md) generate the [special linear group](../../../../../../special-linear-group.md).

Apply [Gaussian elimination](../../../../../../gaussian-elimination.md) using only row additions. If a pivot is zero, invertibility ensures some lower entry in its column is nonzero; adding that row produces a nonzero pivot without interchanging or scaling rows. Clear entries below each pivot, and then above pivots in reverse order. This reduces any invertible matrix to a diagonal matrix $D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_n)$, with all $d_i\ne0$, preserving [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) throughout. For a determinant-one matrix, $\prod_i d_i=1$.

A two-coordinate diagonal block is itself a product of [elementary transvection matrices](../../../../../../elementary-transvection-matrix.md). On those two coordinates put

$$
w(a)=x_{12}(a)x_{21}(-a^{-1})x_{12}(a)=\begin{pmatrix}0&a\\-a^{-1}&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad w(a)w(-1)=\begin{pmatrix}a&0\\0&a^{-1}\end{pmatrix}.
$$

This formula is valid in characteristic two as well. For $n\ge2$, multiply the diagonal blocks with entries $d_i,d_i^{-1}$ on coordinates $(i,n)$ for $1\le i<n$. Their product is $D$, because its last entry is $\prod_{i<n}d_i^{-1}=d_n$. Thus $D$ and the original matrix lie in the subgroup generated by [transvections](../../../../../../transvection.md). Conversely every [transvection](../../../../../../transvection.md) has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) one by part ii. Hence

$$
\boxed{\langle T\rangle=\operatorname{SL}(V).}
$$

For $n=1$, both the [special linear group](../../../../../../special-linear-group.md) and the subgroup generated by the degenerate identity [transvection](../../../../../../transvection.md) are trivial. The argument also establishes [transvections generate the special linear group](../../../../../../transvections-generate-the-special-linear-group.md) over any field.
