<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Pressureless matter obeys the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md), $\dot\rho_m+3H\rho_m=0$. Integrating gives $\rho_m=\rho_{m,0}(a_0/a)^3$. Define the present [critical density](../../../../../../critical-density.md) and [cosmological density parameters](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md) by

$$
\rho_{\mathrm{crit},0}=\frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G},\qquad
\Omega_{m,0}=\frac{\rho_{m,0}}{\rho_{\mathrm{crit},0}},\qquad
\Omega_{\Lambda,0}=\frac{\Lambda}{3H_0^2},\qquad
\Omega_{k,0}=-\frac{k}{H_0^2a_0^2}.
$$

Here $c=1$ and the curvature contribution is an effective density parameter, not an additional matter fluid. Substitution into the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives

$$
H^2=H_0^2\left[\Omega_{m,0}\left(\frac{a_0}{a}\right)^3+\Omega_{k,0}\left(\frac{a_0}{a}\right)^2+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}\right].
$$

Using the [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md) $H=\dot a/a$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\dot a^2=H_0^2\left(\frac{\Omega_{m,0}a_0^3}{a}+\Omega_{k,0}a_0^2+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}a^2\right).}
$$

At $a=a_0$ the same equation implies $\Omega_{m,0}+\Omega_{k,0}+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}=1$. The power in the matter term is $a_0^3$; the converted TeX's $a_0^2$ is an error not present in the original PDF.
