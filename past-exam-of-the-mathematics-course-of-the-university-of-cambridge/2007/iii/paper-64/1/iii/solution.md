<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the spatial-potential convention of this paper and assume $\bar\rho+\bar P\ne0$. A passive time displacement changes the spatial scalar by $\widetilde\Psi-\Psi=\bar NH\xi^0$ and changes the [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) by

$$
\delta\widetilde\rho-\delta\rho=-\dot{\bar\rho}\xi^0
=3\bar NH(\bar\rho+\bar P)\xi^0.
$$

The two contributions to the [uniform-density curvature perturbation](../../../../../../uniform-density-curvature-perturbation.md) therefore cancel:

$$
\widetilde\zeta-\zeta=-\bar NH\xi^0+
\frac{3\bar NH(\bar\rho+\bar P)\xi^0}{3(\bar\rho+\bar P)}=0.
$$

A spatial scalar displacement changes $E$ but not this combination. Hence **$\zeta$ is gauge invariant at linear order**. Pure cosmological-constant matter, for which $\bar\rho+\bar P=0$, does not supply a [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) clock and this formula is not defined for that component alone.

Now take the definite constant-$w$ [barotropic equation of state](../../../../../../barotropic-equation-of-state.md) and use physical-time differentiation $\mathcal D=\bar N^{-1}\partial_t$. Put $A=\bar\rho+\bar P$. Background [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) gives $\mathcal DA=-3H(1+w)A$. Differentiating $\zeta=-\Psi+\delta\rho/(3A)$, then substituting the two supplied perturbation equations, gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal D\zeta
&=-\mathcal D\Psi+\frac{\mathcal D\delta\rho}{3A}
+H(1+w)\frac{\delta\rho}{A}\\
&=H\Phi-\frac\kappa3-\frac{\Delta\chi}3
-H\frac{\delta\rho+\delta P}{A}+\frac\kappa3-H\Phi
-\frac{\Delta u}{3A}+H(1+w)\frac{\delta\rho}{A}\\
&=-\frac H A(\delta P-w\delta\rho)
-\frac13\Delta\left(\chi+\frac uA\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the lapse and expansion perturbations cancel without imposing a gauge. In terms of the [non-adiabatic pressure perturbation](../../../../../../non-adiabatic-pressure-perturbation.md) $\delta P_{\rm nad}=\delta P-w\delta\rho$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{\dot\zeta}{\bar N}=-\frac{H\delta P_{\rm nad}}{\bar\rho+\bar P}
-\frac13\Delta\left(\chi+\frac{u}{\bar\rho+\bar P}\right).}
$$

For the stipulated [barotropic equation of state](../../../../../../barotropic-equation-of-state.md), $\delta P=w\delta\rho$, so the first term vanishes. For a [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md), $\Delta=-k^2/a^2$. For regular long-wavelength solutions the remaining gradients are suppressed when $k\ll aH$. Consequently **$\dot\zeta\simeq0$ on [superhorizon scales](../../../../../../superhorizon-scale.md)**, with exact conservation in the leading zero-gradient limit. It is not an exact finite-$k$ identity. Entropy perturbations or a singular [gradient expansion](../../../../../../gradient-expansion.md) can invalidate that conclusion; the supplied adiabatic assumption is what removes the unsuppressed [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) source.

This [superhorizon conservation of uniform-density curvature](../../../../../../superhorizon-conservation-of-uniform-density-curvature.md) is useful because inflationary fluctuations leave the horizon during [cosmic inflation](../../../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) and may re-enter only much later. In an adiabatic single-clock evolution, the conserved [uniform-density curvature perturbation](../../../../../../uniform-density-curvature-perturbation.md) transports their initial amplitude through reheating and the later [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md) and matter eras without solving every short-distance process. It then determines the initial conditions for large-scale structure and [Cosmic microwave background anisotropy](../../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-anisotropy-split.md). Extra fluctuating fields or fluids can introduce [isocurvature perturbations](../../../../../../cosmological-entropy-perturbation.md) and convert them into curvature, so conservation must be checked rather than assumed during such transitions.
