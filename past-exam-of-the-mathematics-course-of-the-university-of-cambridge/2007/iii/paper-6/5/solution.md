<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

An [almost commutative algebra](../../../../../almost-commutative-algebra.md) over $\mathbb C$ has an exhaustive nonnegative increasing multiplicative [ascending filtration](../../../../../ascending-filtration.md)

$$
0=F_{-1}R\subseteq F_0R\subseteq F_1R\subseteq\cdots,\qquad 1\in F_0R,\qquad F_iR\,F_jR\subseteq F_{i+j}R,
$$

for which the [associated graded ring](../../../../../associated-graded-ring.md)

$$
\operatorname{gr}R=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}F_nR/F_{n-1}R
$$

is a commutative finitely generated complex [algebra](../../../../../algebra-split.md). This includes the customary degree filtration with $F_0R=\mathbb C$, finitely many degree-one generators, and commutators lowering degree. The finite-generation clause is part of the convention used here and is necessary for the conclusion.

A finitely generated commutative complex [algebra](../../../../../algebra-split.md) is a quotient of a [polynomial ring](../../../../../polynomial-ring.md) $\mathbb C[X_1,\ldots,X_d]$. Repeated application of the central-variable case of the [Hilbert basis theorem](../../../../../hilbert-basis-theorem.md) makes that polynomial [ring](../../../../../ring.md) [Noetherian](../../../../../noetherian-ring.md); quotients preserve the [ascending chain condition](../../../../../ascending-chain-condition.md) by lifting ideal chains. Thus $\operatorname{gr}R$ is [Noetherian](../../../../../noetherian-ring.md).

We prove [ascending filtered-graded transfer of Noetherianity](../../../../../ascending-filtered-graded-transfer-of-noetherianity.md) directly. Give a [left ideal](../../../../../left-ideal.md) $I\subseteq R$ the induced [ascending filtration](../../../../../ascending-filtration.md) $I\cap F_nR$. Its [associated graded module](../../../../../associated-graded-module.md) embeds into $\operatorname{gr}R$ as a homogeneous [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md), denoted $\operatorname{gr}I$. Choose finitely many homogeneous generators $\overline u_1,\ldots,\overline u_m$ of this ideal, of degrees $d_1,\ldots,d_m$, and lifts $u_j\in I\cap F_{d_j}R$. Homogeneous generators may be chosen by taking homogeneous components of any finite generating set.

For $u\in I\cap F_nR$, its degree-$n$ symbol can be expressed as

$$
\overline u=\sum_j\overline a_j\overline u_j,\qquad\deg\overline a_j=n-d_j,
$$

with terms of negative coefficient degree omitted. Lift each coefficient to $a_j\in F_{n-d_j}R$. Then

$$
u-\sum_ja_ju_j\in I\cap F_{n-1}R.
$$

Induction on $n$, ending at $F_{-1}R=0$, expresses $u$ in the [left ideal](../../../../../left-ideal.md) generated by the $u_j$. Hence every [left ideal](../../../../../left-ideal.md) is finitely generated. Applying the same argument to [right ideals](../../../../../right-ideal.md), or to the [opposite ring](../../../../../opposite-ring.md), proves

$$
\boxed{R\text{ is left and right Noetherian}.}
$$

This degree-lowering proof terminates in finitely many steps and requires no completeness assumption on the [filtered ring](../../../../../filtered-ring.md). Without finite generation of the graded algebra, a counterexample would be the commutative [polynomial ring](../../../../../polynomial-ring.md) in infinitely many variables, whose ideals $(X_1)\subsetneq(X_1,X_2)\subsetneq\cdots$ do not stabilize.

For the [universal enveloping algebra](../../../../../universal-enveloping-algebra.md) $U(\mathfrak g)$, use the degree [ascending filtration](../../../../../ascending-filtration.md) inherited from the [tensor algebra](../../../../../tensor-algebra.md) of the finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$. For $x,y\in\mathfrak g$, the defining relation is $xy-yx=[x,y]\in F_1U(\mathfrak g)$, whereas the product $xy$ has filtration degree at most $2$. Thus the degree-one symbols commute in $\operatorname{gr}U(\mathfrak g)$. Those symbols generate the graded algebra, so it is a quotient of

$$
\operatorname{Sym}(\mathfrak g)\cong\mathbb C[X_1,\ldots,X_d],\qquad d=\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathfrak g.
$$

This already proves the required almost-commutative property. The [Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem](../../../../../poincare-birkhoff-witt-theorem.md) gives the stronger identification $\operatorname{gr}U(\mathfrak g)\cong\operatorname{Sym}(\mathfrak g)$, but injectivity of this map is not needed for the chain-condition argument. Therefore

$$
\boxed{U(\mathfrak g)\text{ is left and right Noetherian}.}
$$
