<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Let $R=MU^*(\mathrm{pt})$ be the [coefficient ring of complex cobordism](../../../../../coefficient-ring-of-complex-cobordism.md). We use its standard coefficient theorem: $R\cong\mathbb Z[b_1,b_2,\ldots]$ with $|b_j|=-2j$. In particular $R$ is concentrated in nonpositive even degrees. Let $H$ be the [quaternionic tautological line bundle](../../../../../quaternionic-tautological-line-bundle.md) on $\mathbb{HP}^2$, regarded as a complex rank-two bundle by right multiplication by a fixed imaginary quaternion. Set

$$
q=c_2^{MU}(H)\in MU^4(\mathbb{HP}^2).
$$

We will prove

$$
\boxed{MU^*(\mathbb{HP}^2)\cong R[q]/(q^3),\qquad |q|=4.}
$$

The argument must establish the multiplication as well as the additive groups, since collapse of a [spectral sequence](../../../../../spectral-sequence.md) alone does not settle ring extensions.

The [quaternionic projective space](../../../../../quaternionic-projective-space.md) has one cell in each dimension $0,4,8$, giving integral [cohomology](../../../../../cohomology-split.md) $\mathbb Z$ in exactly these three degrees. The [sphere bundle](../../../../../sphere-bundle.md) of $H$ is the quaternionic Hopf fibration

$$
S^3\longrightarrow S^{11}\longrightarrow\mathbb{HP}^2.
$$

Write $u=e(H_{\mathbb R})=c_2(H)$ for its ordinary degree-four class, fixing its sign by this equality. The [Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](../../../../../gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) gives surjections, in fact isomorphisms,

$$
H^0(\mathbb{HP}^2;\mathbb Z)\xrightarrow{\ \smile u\ }H^4(\mathbb{HP}^2;\mathbb Z),
\qquad
H^4(\mathbb{HP}^2;\mathbb Z)\xrightarrow{\ \smile u\ }H^8(\mathbb{HP}^2;\mathbb Z).
$$

Indeed the corresponding neighboring [cohomology](../../../../../cohomology-split.md) groups of $S^{11}$ vanish. Both source and target groups are $\mathbb Z$, so these maps make $u$ and $u^2$ generators. There are no higher [cohomology](../../../../../cohomology-split.md) groups. This proves the [cohomology ring of quaternionic projective space](../../../../../cohomology-ring-of-quaternionic-projective-space.md) in the form $H^*(\mathbb{HP}^2;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z[u]/(u^3)$.

Apply the multiplicative [Atiyah-Hirzebruch spectral sequence](../../../../../atiyah-hirzebruch-spectral-sequence.md) for [complex cobordism](../../../../../complex-cobordism.md):

$$
E_2^{s,t}=H^s(\mathbb{HP}^2;R^t)\Longrightarrow MU^{s+t}(\mathbb{HP}^2),
\qquad d_r:E_r^{s,t}\to E_r^{s+r,t-r+1}.
$$

The skeletal construction obtains this sequence from the [long exact sequences](../../../../../long-exact-sequence.md) of successive skeleta; the quotients are wedges of spheres, and the first [differential](../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) is the ordinary cellular [differential](../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) with coefficient groups $R^t$. Its multiplication on the associated graded group is the ordinary [cup product](../../../../../cup-product.md) with these coefficients. The finite eight-dimensional skeleton makes the filtration finite, so there is no convergence ambiguity.

Only columns $s=0,4,8$ and even rows $t$ are nonzero. If $r$ is odd, the target column $s+r$ is odd and zero. If $r$ is even, the target coefficient degree $t-r+1$ is odd and zero. The same observations exclude incoming [differentials](../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md). Consequently $E_\infty=E_2$, with associated graded $R$-module freely generated by $1,u,u^2$ in filtrations $0,4,8$.

The canonical map from [complex cobordism](../../../../../complex-cobordism.md) to integral [cohomology](../../../../../cohomology-split.md) preserves the chosen complex orientation and hence sends $q$ to $u$. The class $q$ vanishes on the zero-dimensional skeleton since $R^4=0$, and its leading skeletal term is $u$ in filtration four. Multiplicativity makes the leading term of $q^2$ the generator $u^2$ in filtration eight. Thus $1,q,q^2$ lift the associated graded basis.

This resolves all module extensions explicitly. For a class of total degree $d$, subtract its coefficient $a_0\in R^d$ in filtration zero. From the remainder subtract $a_1q$, where $a_1\in R^{d-4}$ is its leading coefficient in filtration four, and then subtract $a_2q^2$ with $a_2\in R^{d-8}$. The remaining filtration is beyond dimension eight and is zero. Conversely a relation $a_0+a_1q+a_2q^2=0$ has zero leading coefficient in each of the three successive filtrations, forcing all three $a_j$ to vanish. Therefore

$$
MU^*(\mathbb{HP}^2)=R\,1\oplus R\,q\oplus R\,q^2.
$$

Finally $q^3$ has degree twelve. Every associated graded contribution in that total degree would have coefficient degree $12-s>0$ for $s=0,4,8$, and all such coefficients vanish. Thus $MU^{12}(\mathbb{HP}^2)=0$ and $q^3=0$. The polynomial map $R[t]/(t^3)\to MU^*(\mathbb{HP}^2)$ taking $t$ to $q$ is surjective by the basis argument and injective by its uniqueness. This proves the [complex cobordism ring of the quaternionic projective plane](../../../../../complex-cobordism-ring-of-the-quaternionic-projective-plane.md) as a graded ring over the stated coefficient ring, including the absence of any additional coefficient-dependent relation.
