<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $\epsilon=v_a^2/c^2\ll1$. Expanding the [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) in part (a) gives

$$
\sqrt{(1+\epsilon)^2-4\epsilon\cos^2\theta}
=1+\epsilon(1-2\cos^2\theta)+O(\epsilon^2).
$$

Hence, to first absolute order in $\epsilon$,

$$
v_f^2=c^2+v_a^2\sin^2\theta+O(v_a^4/c^2),\qquad
v_{\rm slow}^2=v_a^2\cos^2\theta+O(v_a^4/c^2).
$$

The [high-pressure limit of magnetosonic waves](../../../../../../high-pressure-limit-of-magnetosonic-waves.md) can also be expressed as nonnegative speeds. Retaining the next squared-speed term gives the first relative correction for the slow mode:

$$
\boxed{v_f=c\left[1+\frac\epsilon2\sin^2\theta+O(\epsilon^2)\right],\qquad
v_{\rm slow}=v_a|\cos\theta|\left[1-\frac\epsilon2\sin^2\theta+O(\epsilon^2)\right]}.
$$

The exact [Alfvén speed](../../../../../../alfven-speed.md) along the propagation direction remains $v_a|\cos\theta|$.

The [fast magnetosonic wave](../../../../../../fast-magnetosonic-wave.md) is predominantly an [acoustic wave](../../../../../../acoustic-wave.md), with [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) almost parallel to $\mathbf k$ and appreciable [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) and gas [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) variations. Its [magnetic pressure](../../../../../../magnetic-pressure.md) correction is weak. The [Alfvén wave](../../../../../../alfven-wave.md) is exactly incompressible, polarized perpendicular to the $\mathbf k$-$\mathbf B_0$ plane and restored by [magnetic tension](../../../../../../magnetic-tension.md).

The [slow magnetosonic wave](../../../../../../slow-magnetosonic-wave.md) is polarized inside that plane and almost perpendicular to $\mathbf k$. Indeed, the leading [sound speed](../../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) part of the matrix in (a) annihilates a vector perpendicular to $\mathbf k$, so only the smaller magnetic restoring force acts on that [MHD wave polarization](../../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-wave-polarization.md). Its compression is smaller by order $\epsilon$, with gas [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) changes almost cancelling [magnetic pressure](../../../../../../magnetic-pressure.md) changes. It is often called the [pseudo-Alfvén wave](../../../../../../pseudo-alfven-wave.md) [MHD wave polarization](../../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-wave-polarization.md) in this high [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) limit. Thus two distinct almost-incompressible magnetic [MHD wave polarizations](../../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-wave-polarization.md) have the same leading speed, while the third is predominantly sound. For exactly perpendicular propagation the [Alfvén wave](../../../../../../alfven-wave.md) and [slow magnetosonic wave](../../../../../../slow-magnetosonic-wave.md) frequencies vanish; for parallel propagation the two transverse magnetic [MHD wave polarizations](../../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-wave-polarization.md) are degenerate.
