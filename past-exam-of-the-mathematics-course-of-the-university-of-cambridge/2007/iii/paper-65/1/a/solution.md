<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [energy](../../../../../../energy.md)-conserving blast regime, neglect ambient [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md), radiative losses, ejecta mass and gravity compared with the explosion. The cold ambient [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) has no [sound speed](../../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) scale, and its power-law [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) has no preferred radius. The point explosion introduces only its [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) $E$ and elapsed time $t$. This scale-free setting supports a [self-similar blast wave](../../../../../../self-similar-blast-wave.md); extra physics would limit the time interval over which that description applies.

The mass swept up to radius $R$ is

$$
M(R)=4\pi\int_0^R Cr^{2-\beta}\,dr=\frac{4\pi C}{3-\beta}R^{3-\beta}.
$$

It is finite because $\beta<3$. With no [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) loss, the [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy.md) both scale as $CR^{3-\beta}\dot R^2$. Therefore $R^{3-\beta}\dot R^2$ is constant and integration gives

$$
\boxed{R(t)=A\left(\frac EC\right)^{1/(5-\beta)}t^{2/(5-\beta)}}.
$$

The dimensionless coefficient $A$ depends on the [adiabatic exponent](../../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) and ambient exponent through the [similarity profiles](../../../../../../similarity-profile.md). Indeed, $[E/C]=L^{5-\beta}T^{-2}$, which independently verifies the scaling. The [shock wave](../../../../../../shock-wave.md) decelerates for $0<\beta<3$, since its time exponent lies between $2/5$ and $1$.
