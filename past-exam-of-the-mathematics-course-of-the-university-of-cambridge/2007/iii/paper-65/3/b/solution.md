<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On one [poloidal flux surface](../../../../../../axisymmetric-magnetic-flux-surface.md), the [field-line angular velocity](../../../../../../field-line-angular-velocity.md) $\omega(\psi)$ is constant. In a frame rotating at that rate the relative [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is $\mathbf v=\mathbf u-R\omega\mathbf e_\phi=k\mathbf B/\rho$, parallel to the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md). The [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) is perpendicular to this motion, and the [Coriolis force](../../../../../../coriolis-force.md) also does no work. Centrifugal acceleration comes from the effective potential $-R^2\omega^2/2$. Since [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) is constant along the surface, [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) work integrates to [specific enthalpy](../../../../../../specific-enthalpy.md) $w$, with $dw=dp/\rho$ along the flow.

Therefore [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) relative to the rotating [poloidal flux surface](../../../../../../axisymmetric-magnetic-flux-surface.md), [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy.md) and effective gravitational-plus-centrifugal potential have a constant sum:

$$
\boxed{\frac12|\mathbf u-R\omega\mathbf e_\phi|^2+w+\Phi-\frac12R^2\omega^2=\varepsilon(\psi)}.
$$

This is a corotating [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) integral, not the laboratory [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) alone: [Maxwell stress tensor](../../../../../../maxwell-stress-tensor.md) stresses can transfer [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) and [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) in the laboratory frame. In fact the expression equals $|\mathbf u|^2/2+w+\Phi-\omega Ru_\phi$, namely the total [magnetohydrodynamic Bernoulli invariant](../../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-bernoulli-invariant.md) minus $\omega\ell$. Differential rotation between surfaces does not invalidate the argument, because the chosen [angular velocity](../../../../../../angular-velocity.md) is fixed along the particular surface being followed.
