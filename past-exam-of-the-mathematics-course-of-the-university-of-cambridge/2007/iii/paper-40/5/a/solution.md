<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A buyer accepts a [posted price](../../../../../../posted-price.md) precisely when their valuation exceeds it; equality has probability zero. The independent uniform valuations therefore give probability $p^2$ that neither accepts, for $0\leq p\leq1$. The seller's [expected revenue](../../../../../../expected-seller-revenue.md) is

$$
R_a(p)=p(1-p^2),\qquad R_a'(p)=1-3p^2.
$$

The interior critical point is $p=1/\sqrt3$, with negative second derivative; the endpoint revenues are zero. Prices above one make no sale and nonpositive prices cannot improve on this positive optimum. Hence

$$
\boxed{p_*=\frac1{\sqrt3},\qquad R_a^*=\frac{2}{3\sqrt3}.}
$$

Which willing buyer is served if both accept does not affect this revenue.
