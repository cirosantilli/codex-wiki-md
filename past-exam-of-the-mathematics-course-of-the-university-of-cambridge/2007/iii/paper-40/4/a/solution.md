<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A payoff allocation is [Pareto efficient](../../../../../../pareto-efficiency.md) if no feasible allocation weakly increases every player's payoff and strictly increases at least one. Here feasibility requires total payment at most the available estate. The proposed payments have

$$
\frac83+\frac{14}3+\frac{23}3=15.
$$

Any such improvement would have total payment greater than $15$, which is impossible. Each payment is also between zero and its claim, and exceeds its singleton [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) value. Hence the allocation is an [imputation](../../../../../../imputation-in-a-coalitional-game.md) and

$$
\boxed{\text{The allocation is Pareto optimal: (a) is true.}}
$$
