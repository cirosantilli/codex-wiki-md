<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [core of a cooperative game](../../../../../../core-game-theory.md) consists of efficient allocations satisfying $\sum_{i\in S}x_i\geq v(S)$ for every [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md), so no [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) can obtain a greater total payoff by leaving. Efficiency has already been checked. For singletons,

$$
\frac83\geq0,\qquad\frac{14}3\geq2,\qquad\frac{23}3\geq5.
$$

For pairs,

$$
\frac{22}3\geq6,\qquad\frac{31}3\geq9,\qquad\frac{37}3\geq11.
$$

The empty constraint is automatic and the grand constraint is equality. Thus every core inequality holds and

$$
\boxed{x\in\operatorname{core}(v):\quad\text{(d) is true.}}
$$

All four assertions about this allocation are true, although their definitions impose different requirements.
