↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Use the same [Minkowski metric](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$ as in Question 1. For the conjugate charged field, the kinetic term means $(D_\mu\varphi)^*D^\mu\varphi$, with $(D_\mu\varphi)^*=(\partial_\mu-ieA_\mu)\varphi^*$. Thus the conjugate field has the opposite charge in the [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md).
