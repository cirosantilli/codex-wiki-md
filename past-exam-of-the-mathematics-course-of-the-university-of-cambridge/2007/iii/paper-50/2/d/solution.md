<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the [Feynman vertex](../../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) $-i\lambda$ for the scalar [Yukawa coupling](../../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md), the internal [scalar propagator](../../../../../../scalar-propagator.md) $i/(k^2-\mu^2+i0)$, incoming factors $u_r(p)$ and outgoing [Dirac adjoints](../../../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) $\bar u_{r'}(p')$. Define $i\mathcal M$ as the coefficient in the connected [S-matrix](../../../../../../s-matrix.md) element after removing its overall momentum-conserving [Dirac delta function](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md). A direct pairing contributes

$$
i\mathcal M_t=(-i\lambda)^2\frac{i}{(p-p')^2-\mu^2+i0}\,[\bar u_{r'}(p')u_r(p)]\,[\bar u_{s'}(q')u_s(q)].
$$

The crossed pairing interchanges the identical outgoing [fermions](../../../../../../fermion.md) and therefore has a relative [fermionic sign](../../../../../../fermionic-sign.md) of minus. With $t=(p-p')^2$ and $u=(p-q')^2$, the result is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M=-\lambda^2\left[\frac{[\bar u_{r'}(p')u_r(p)][\bar u_{s'}(q')u_s(q)]}{t-\mu^2+i0}
-\frac{[\bar u_{s'}(q')u_r(p)][\bar u_{r'}(p')u_s(q)]}{u-\mu^2+i0}\right].}
$$

It changes sign under exchange of the two outgoing momenta and spin labels, as required for identical [fermions](../../../../../../fermion.md). No [fermion spin sum](../../../../../../fermion-spin-sum.md) or identical-particle phase-space factor belongs in this amplitude; those enter a [relativistic scattering cross-section](../../../../../../relativistic-scattering-cross-section.md) calculation.
