<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At fixed particle number $N_p=\int|\psi|^2d\mathbf x$, an equilibrium [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md) is a constrained critical point of the [energy functional](../../../../../../energy-functional.md). Introduce the [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) $\mu$ as the [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) for $N_p$, and impose $\delta(E-\mu N_p)=0$. The resulting stationary [cubic-quintic Gross–Pitaevskii equation](../../../../../../cubic-quintic-gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\psi_0
+\left(V_0|\psi_0|^2+W_0|\psi_0|^4\right)\psi_0=\mu\psi_0}.
$$

The time-dependent equilibrium has $\psi(\mathbf x,t)=e^{-i\mu t/\hbar}\psi_0(\mathbf x)$, so its [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) is stationary even though its [quantum phase](../../../../../../quantum-phase.md) changes uniformly. The specified particle number fixes $\mu$ together with the boundary conditions. In an infinite uniform bulk, particle number is understood through a finite volume or a prescribed bulk density.
