<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Treat $\psi$ and its [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) as independent variables in the [functional derivative](../../../../../../functional-derivative.md). Integrating the gradient variation by parts, with vanishing boundary terms, gives

$$
\delta E=\int\left[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\psi
+V_0|\psi|^2\psi+W_0|\psi|^4\psi\right]\delta\psi^*\,d\mathbf x
+\text{complex conjugate}.
$$

The factors follow from $\partial_{\psi^*}|\psi|^4=2|\psi|^2\psi$ and $\partial_{\psi^*}|\psi|^6=3|\psi|^4\psi$. Thus the [cubic-quintic Gross–Pitaevskii equation](../../../../../../cubic-quintic-gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{i\hbar\partial_t\psi=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\psi
+V_0|\psi|^2\psi+W_0|\psi|^4\psi}.
$$
