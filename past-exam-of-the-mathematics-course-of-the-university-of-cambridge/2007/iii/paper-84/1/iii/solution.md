<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a nonzero uniform [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md), the [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) vanishes. Dividing the stationary [cubic-quintic Gross–Pitaevskii equation](../../../../../../cubic-quintic-gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) by $\psi_0$ yields

$$
\boxed{\mu=V_0n_0+W_0n_0^2}.
$$

Equivalently, the interaction energy density is $e(n)=V_0n^2/2+W_0n^3/3$ and its derivative $e'(n_0)$ is the [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md). Positive local [compressibility](../../../../../../compressibility.md) requires $\mu'(n_0)=V_0+2W_0n_0>0$. This stability condition is distinct from the sign of $\mu$ itself.
