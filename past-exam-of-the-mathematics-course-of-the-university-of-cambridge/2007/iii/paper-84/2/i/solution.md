<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

To first order, $|1+u+iv|^2=1+2u$ and $(1-|\psi|^2)\psi=-2u$. Equating real and imaginary parts of the [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](../../../../../../gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{2v_t=\nabla^2u-2u,\qquad -2u_t=\nabla^2v}.
$$

Differentiate the second equation in time and use the first to eliminate $v$:

$$
\boxed{u_{tt}=\frac12\nabla^2u-\frac14\nabla^4u}.
$$

For a [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) with $k=|\mathbf k|$, the [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) is

$$
-\omega^2=-\frac{k^2}{2}-\frac{k^4}{4},\qquad
\boxed{\omega_\pm(k)=\pm\frac{k}{2}\sqrt{k^2+2}}.
$$

This is the normalized [Bogoliubov spectrum](../../../../../../bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md). At small [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) the positive branch has sound speed $\lim_{k\to0}\omega_+(k)/k=1/\sqrt2$; at large [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) it approaches the free-particle quadratic dispersion. The linear frequencies are real, confirming stability of the repulsive uniform bulk.
