Here equilibrium means a time-independent [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) and current, with a common temporal [quantum phase](../../../../../../quantum-phase.md). Write $\psi(\mathbf x,t)=e^{-i\mu t/\hbar}\Psi(\mathbf x)$ with real $\mu$, since an imaginary temporal frequency would change the density. The stationary pumped [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](../../../../../../gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mu\Psi=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\Psi
+V_0|\Psi|^2\Psi+i(\gamma-\Gamma|\Psi|^2)\Psi}.
$$

The [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) is the common phase-rotation frequency times $\hbar$; this open system has no conserved particle-number constraint fixing it as in the conservative problem. Local gain and loss need not cancel separately in an inhomogeneous equilibrium, because a stationary [condensate number current](../../../../../../condensate-number-current.md) can transport particles between gain and loss regions.
