<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

During [radiation domination](../../../../../../radiation-domination.md), the [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md) gives $\rho_R\propto a^{-4}$. The flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) in [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md) is $(a'/a)^2=8\pi Ga^2\rho_R/3$. Hence $a'^2$ is constant, and choosing the [Big Bang](../../../../../../big-bang.md) at $\tau=0$ yields

$$
\boxed{a\propto\tau,\qquad8\pi G\rho_Ra^2=\frac3{\tau^2}.}
$$

For the [CDM density equation in a matter-radiation universe](../../../../../../cdm-density-equation-in-a-matter-radiation-universe.md), neglect $\rho_C\delta_C$ while retaining $\delta_C$ itself. The [adiabatic initial conditions](../../../../../../adiabatic-initial-conditions.md) give $\delta_R=4\delta_C/3$, so

$$
\delta_C''+\frac1\tau\delta_C'=\frac4{\tau^2}\delta_C.
$$

This [Euler-Cauchy equation](../../../../../../euler-cauchy-equation.md) has powers $\tau^p$ with $p(p-1)+p-4=0$, namely $p=2,-2$. Selecting the growing [superhorizon adiabatic CDM mode in radiation domination](../../../../../../superhorizon-adiabatic-cdm-mode-in-radiation-domination.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\delta_C(\tau,k)=A_R(k)\tau^2.}
$$
