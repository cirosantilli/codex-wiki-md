<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

During [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md), $\rho_C\propto a^{-3}$. The flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) in [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md) implies $a'^2\propto a$, and hence

$$
\boxed{a\propto\tau^2,\qquad8\pi G\rho_Ca^2=\frac{12}{\tau^2}.}
$$

With radiation neglected, the [CDM density equation in a matter-radiation universe](../../../../../../cdm-density-equation-in-a-matter-radiation-universe.md) reduces to

$$
\delta_C''+\frac2\tau\delta_C'-\frac6{\tau^2}\delta_C=0.
$$

For this [Euler-Cauchy equation](../../../../../../euler-cauchy-equation.md), $\delta_C\propto\tau^p$ requires $p(p-1)+2p-6=(p-2)(p+3)=0$. Thus the [matter-era growing and decaying density modes](../../../../../../matter-era-growing-and-decaying-density-modes.md) are $\tau^2$ and $\tau^{-3}$, respectively, and

$$
\boxed{\delta_C(\tau,k)\propto\tau^2\propto a.}
$$

There is no explicit $k$ in this pressureless equation, so the result holds on all scales within the stated CDM-comoving [synchronous gauge](../../../../../../synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md) description. The amplitude can still depend on $k$ through its earlier evolution; the result does not equate [density contrasts](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) on different time slicings.
