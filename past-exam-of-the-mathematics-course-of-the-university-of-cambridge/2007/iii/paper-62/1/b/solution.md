<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $0<\Omega_M<1$, set $x=a^{3/2}$ and choose the expanding branch of the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md). Then

$$
\dot x=\frac32H_0\sqrt{\Omega_M+(1-\Omega_M)x^2}.
$$

Separating variables and choosing [cosmic time](../../../../../../cosmic-time.md) $t=0$ at the [Big Bang](../../../../../../big-bang.md) gives

$$
\frac{\operatorname{arsinh}\!\left(x\sqrt{(1-\Omega_M)/\Omega_M}\right)}{\sqrt{1-\Omega_M}}=\frac32H_0t.
$$

Thus the [flat dust-and-vacuum expansion](../../../../../../flat-dust-and-vacuum-expansion.md) is

$$
\boxed{a(t)=\left(\frac{\Omega_M}{1-\Omega_M}\right)^{1/3}\sinh^{2/3}\!\left(\frac32H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega_M}\,t\right).}
$$

Setting $a(t_0)=1$ gives the [age of a flat matter-Lambda universe](../../../../../../age-of-a-flat-matter-lambda-universe.md),

$$
t_0=\frac{2}{3H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega_M}}\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{\frac{1-\Omega_M}{\Omega_M}}.
$$

The two [matter-vacuum age endpoint limits](../../../../../../matter-vacuum-age-endpoint-limits.md) follow below.
