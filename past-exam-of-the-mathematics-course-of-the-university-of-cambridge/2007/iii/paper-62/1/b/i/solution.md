As $\Omega_M\downarrow0$, use $\operatorname{arsinh}z=\log(z+\sqrt{1+z^2})\sim\log(2z)$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{H_0t_0\sim\frac13\log\frac4{\Omega_M}\ \longrightarrow\ \infty.}
$$

At fixed [Hubble constant](../../../../../../../hubble-constant.md), the [age of a flat matter-Lambda universe](../../../../../../../age-of-a-flat-matter-lambda-universe.md) diverges logarithmically. The limiting purely vacuum-dominated [de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../../de-sitter-spacetime.md) has an exponential [scale factor](../../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) and no [Big Bang](../../../../../../../big-bang.md) at a finite value of [cosmic time](../../../../../../../cosmic-time.md) in this flat slicing.
