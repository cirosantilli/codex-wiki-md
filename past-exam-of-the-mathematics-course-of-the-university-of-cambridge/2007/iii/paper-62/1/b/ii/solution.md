As $\Omega_M\uparrow1$, use $\operatorname{arsinh}z\sim z$ near zero. Consequently

$$
\boxed{t_0\longrightarrow\frac2{3H_0}.}
$$

The [flat dust-and-vacuum expansion](../../../../../../../flat-dust-and-vacuum-expansion.md) tends to $a(t)=(3H_0t/2)^{2/3}$, the [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md).
