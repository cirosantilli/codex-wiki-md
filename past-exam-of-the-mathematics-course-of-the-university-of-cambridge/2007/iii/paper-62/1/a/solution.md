<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $H=\dot a/a$ be the [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md), and normalize the [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) by $a(t_0)=1$. The [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md) gives $\rho_M=\rho_{M0}a^{-3}$ for [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md), whereas the [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) has constant energy density $\rho_\Lambda$. The spatially flat [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) therefore becomes

$$
H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}(\rho_{M0}a^{-3}+\rho_\Lambda).
$$

The present [Hubble constant](../../../../../../hubble-constant.md) is $H_0=H(t_0)$. In terms of the present [critical density](../../../../../../critical-density.md) $\rho_{\mathrm{crit},0}=3H_0^2/(8\pi G)$, the matter [cosmological density parameter](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md) is $\Omega_M=\rho_{M0}/\rho_{\mathrm{crit},0}$. Spatial flatness gives $\rho_\Lambda/\rho_{\mathrm{crit},0}=1-\Omega_M$, so

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\dot a}{a}\right)^2=H_0^2\left[\frac{\Omega_M}{a^3}+1-\Omega_M\right].}
$$
