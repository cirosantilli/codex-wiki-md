<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $p+e\rightleftharpoons H+\gamma$, [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) and the vanishing [photon chemical potential](../../../../../../photon-chemical-potential.md) imply $\mu_H=\mu_p+\mu_e$. Taking the ratio of the three [nonrelativistic Maxwell--Boltzmann number densities](../../../../../../nonrelativistic-maxwell-boltzmann-number-density.md) cancels these [chemical potentials](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md):

$$
\frac{n_H}{n_pn_e}=\frac{g_H}{g_pg_e}\left(\frac{2\pi M_H}{M_pM_eT}\right)^{3/2}\exp\!\left(\frac{M_p+M_e-M_H}{T}\right).
$$

The ground-state [hydrogen atom](../../../../../../hydrogen-atom.md) has four combined [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) and [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) states if the small hyperfine splitting is ignored, whereas $g_p=g_e=2$. Hence $g_H/(g_pg_e)=1$. With [Hydrogen binding energy](../../../../../../hydrogen-binding-energy.md) $B=M_p+M_e-M_H$ and $M_H\simeq M_p$, the [Saha ionization equation](../../../../../../saha-ionization-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{n_H}{n_pn_e}\simeq\left(\frac{2\pi}{M_eT}\right)^{3/2}e^{B/T}.}
$$

The approximation in the prefactor neglects relative corrections of order $(M_e+B)/M_p$; the [Hydrogen binding energy](../../../../../../hydrogen-binding-energy.md) must be retained in the exponential.
