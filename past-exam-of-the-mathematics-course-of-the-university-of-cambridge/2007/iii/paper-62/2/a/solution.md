<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [degeneracy factor](../../../../../../degeneracy-factor.md) $g_A$ counts internal states with the same energy, including the allowed [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) states. The [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) $\mu_A$ measures the free-energy cost of changing the number of particles of species $A$; in this convention the rest energy remains in the exponential of the [nonrelativistic Maxwell--Boltzmann number density](../../../../../../nonrelativistic-maxwell-boltzmann-number-density.md).

The [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) is fixed by the densities of [conserved charges](../../../../../../conserved-charge.md) and by [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) between reactions. For a reaction $\sum_A\nu_A A=0$, [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) requires $\sum_A\nu_A\mu_A=0$. Equivalently, when these are the only constraints, $\mu_A$ is a linear combination of the [chemical potentials](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) of the [conserved charges](../../../../../../conserved-charge.md) carried by $A$. An unconstrained species has zero [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md). In particular, the [photon chemical potential](../../../../../../photon-chemical-potential.md) vanishes in thermal equilibrium when photon-number-changing reactions are effective.
