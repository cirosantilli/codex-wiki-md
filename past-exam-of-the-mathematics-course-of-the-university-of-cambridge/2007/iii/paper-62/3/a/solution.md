<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With an approximately constant [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md) $H$, the [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) is $a(t)=e^{Ht}$ after a choice of normalization. Integrate $d\tau=dt/a(t)$ and choose the integration constant so that the future endpoint is $\tau=0$:

$$
\tau=-\frac{e^{-Ht}}H,\qquad\boxed{a(\tau)=-\frac1{H\tau},\quad-\infty<\tau<0.}
$$

Substituting $dt=a\,d\tau$ in the [FLRW metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) gives $ds^2=a^2(\tau)(-d\tau^2+d\mathbf x^2)$. This is [Conformal time during de Sitter expansion](../../../../../../conformal-time-during-de-sitter-expansion.md).
