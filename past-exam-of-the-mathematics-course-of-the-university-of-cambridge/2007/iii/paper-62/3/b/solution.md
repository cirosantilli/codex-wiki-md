<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [FLRW metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) in [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md), $\sqrt{-g}=a^4$, $g^{00}=-a^{-2}$ and $g^{ij}=a^{-2}\delta^{ij}$. The equation for a [minimally coupled massless scalar field](../../../../../../minimally-coupled-massless-scalar-field.md) is consequently

$$
-(a^2\delta\phi')'+a^2\nabla^2\delta\phi=0.
$$

For a [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) of comoving wavevector $\mathbf k$, the [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) contributes $-k^2$, where $k=|\mathbf k|$. Thus

$$
\delta\phi_k''+2\frac{a'}a\delta\phi_k'+k^2\delta\phi_k=0.
$$

Since $a'/a=-1/\tau$, this yields

$$
\boxed{\delta\phi_k''-\frac2\tau\delta\phi_k'=-k^2\delta\phi_k.}
$$

The absence of a curvature-coupling term is material: a [conformally coupled scalar field](../../../../../../conformally-coupled-scalar-field.md) obeys a different mode equation.
