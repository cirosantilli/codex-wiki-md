<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since the [Riemannian orthonormal frame](../../../../../../riemannian-orthonormal-frame.md) is positively oriented, its dual [coframe](../../../../../../coframe.md) satisfies $\omega_g=\omega_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\omega_n$. The form $\nu$ is the [interior product](../../../../../../interior-product.md) $\iota_X\omega_g$. The contraction rule for an [exterior product](../../../../../../exterior-product.md) gives

$$
\iota_{E_i}(\omega_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\omega_n)
=(-1)^{i-1}\omega_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\widehat{\omega_i}\wedge\cdots\wedge\omega_n
=(-1)^{i-1}\theta_i.
$$

One can also obtain the sign by moving $E_i$ from the first argument into slot $i$ of the alternating form, using $i-1$ transpositions. Linearity in $X=\sum_i f_iE_i$ proves the [interior contraction of the Riemannian volume form](../../../../../../interior-contraction-of-the-riemannian-volume-form.md) formula:

$$
\boxed{\nu=\sum_{i=1}^n(-1)^{i+1}f_i\theta_i.}
$$

The exponents $i-1$ and $i+1$ have the same parity.
