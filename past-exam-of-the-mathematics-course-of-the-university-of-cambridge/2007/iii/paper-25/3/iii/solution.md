<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose $F=pJ=p'J'$ are two such factorizations, with $J,J'$ final and $p,p'$ [discrete fibrations](../../../../../../discrete-fibration.md). Apply the unique diagonal construction to the square with left side $J$, top $J'$, bottom $p$ and right side $p'$. It gives a unique [functor](../../../../../../functor.md) $L:\mathcal E\to\mathcal E'$ such that $LJ=J'$ and $p'L=p$. Reversing the two factorizations gives $M:\mathcal E'\to\mathcal E$ with $MJ'=J$ and $pM=p'$.

Both $ML$ and $1_{\mathcal E}$ fill the square with left side $J$, top $J$, bottom $p$ and right side $p$. Uniqueness from part (i) gives $ML=1_{\mathcal E}$. Similarly $LM=1_{\mathcal E'}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{L:\mathcal E\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal E'\text{ is the unique compatible isomorphism}.}
$$

In particular the conclusion is a canonical isomorphism of intermediate [categories](../../../../../../category-split.md), not just an unspecified equivalence.
