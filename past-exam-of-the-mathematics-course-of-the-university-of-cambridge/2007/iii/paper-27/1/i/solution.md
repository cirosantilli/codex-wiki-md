<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

We take $d\in\mathbb Q^\times$, as required for this rational [elliptic curve](../../../../../../elliptic-curve.md). The tangent at $O=(1:-1:0)$ is $X+Y=0$. Its intersection with the curve has $dZ^3=0$, so it meets the curve three times at $O$: the identity is an [inflection point of a plane cubic](../../../../../../inflection-point-of-a-plane-cubic.md).

For $P,Q$, let their chord meet the [diagonal cubic elliptic curve](../../../../../../diagonal-cubic-elliptic-curve.md) at the third point $R$, using the tangent if $P=Q$ and counting [intersection multiplicities](../../../../../../intersection-multiplicity.md). Then join $R$ to $O$ and take the third intersection $S$. Define $P+Q=S$. Equivalently, three collinear intersection points satisfy $P+Q+R=O$.

Here inversion has the particularly simple form

$$
\boxed{-(X:Y:Z)=(Y:X:Z).}
$$

Indeed $P$, its displayed inverse, and $O$ lie on the same line, since swapping $X,Y$ preserves both the curve and $X+Y$. Thus **addition is the chord or tangent operation followed by swapping the first two coordinates**. The rule includes limiting cases involving $O$ and repeated intersections. Its [associativity](../../../../../../associative-property.md) follows from the [elliptic curve group law from Riemann-Roch](../../../../../../elliptic-curve-group-law-from-riemann-roch.md): the identification $P\mapsto[P-O]$ with the degree-zero [Picard group](../../../../../../picard-group.md) turns the line-intersection [Weil divisor](../../../../../../weil-divisor.md) relation into addition of [divisor classes](../../../../../../divisor-class.md). Since a line cuts a [Weil divisor](../../../../../../weil-divisor.md) linearly equivalent to $3O$, that relation is exactly the chord rule just described.
