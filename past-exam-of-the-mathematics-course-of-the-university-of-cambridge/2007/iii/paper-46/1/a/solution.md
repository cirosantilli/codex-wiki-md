<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Treating the two-year observation period as approximately complete, divide total [person-time at risk](../../../../../../person-time-at-risk.md) by two to estimate the average number of children being observed. Dividing again by the number of families gives

$$
\frac{3595+3888}{2(1700+1700)}\simeq1.10.
$$

Thus the estimate is **about 1.1 eligible children per family**. This is a person-time-based approximation for children contributing to the study, not a count of older children or an exact reconstruction of family composition if follow-up or eligibility changed.
