↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

One possibility is **increased recognition and care seeking**: consultation may make caregivers more alert to minor injuries or more inclined to seek professional care, increasing recorded attendance without increasing injury frequency. This is the distinction in [care-seeking effects on a recorded injury outcome](../../../../../../care-seeking-effects-on-a-recorded-injury-outcome.md).

A second possibility is **reduced vigilance or greater exposure after feeling protected**: reliance on supplied equipment may lead caregivers to relax other precautions. This is [risk compensation in a prevention trial](../../../../../../risk-compensation-in-a-prevention-trial.md). Both are hypotheses to investigate; an elevated attendance [incidence rate ratio](../../../../../../rate-ratio.md) does not establish which mechanism occurred.
