<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A centre $c$ turns position into a displacement $x-c$. If $v_1,\ldots,v_N$ are a basis of the $N$-dimensional [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md), every position has unique coordinates

$$
\boxed{x=c+\sum_{j=1}^N x_jv_j.}
$$

Thus **$N$ linearly independent vectors are necessary and sufficient**. Fewer vectors span a proper subspace and cannot represent arbitrary displacements. The coordinates use the field defining the space. For qubit tomography, the relevant parameter space is the real three-dimensional space of [Bloch vectors](../../../../../../bloch-vector.md), rather than the complex two-dimensional space of state amplitudes.
