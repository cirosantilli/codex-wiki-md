<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\{|u_j\rangle\}_{j=1}^N$ be another complete [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), and write $|u_j\rangle=\sum_n U_{nj}|n\rangle$. Orthonormality makes $U$ a [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md), so $UU^\dagger=I$. Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_j|u_j\rangle\langle u_j|
&=\sum_{n,k}\left(\sum_jU_{nj}U_{kj}^*\right)|n\rangle\langle k|\\
&=\sum_n|n\rangle\langle n|=I_N.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus **the resolution of the identity is independent of the orthonormal basis**. Equivalently, expanding any vector in the new complete basis shows that the sum of its [orthogonal projections](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) returns that vector.
