<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) annihilates every vector orthogonal to both of its defining states:

$$
H_G|\phi\rangle=\Delta\bigl[|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|\phi\rangle+|w\rangle\langle w|\phi\rangle\bigr]=0.
$$

Every positive power also annihilates that vector. The exponential series therefore gives

$$
\boxed{e^{-iH_Gt}|\phi\rangle=|\phi\rangle.}
$$

It is a stationary zero-energy state under this [unitary time evolution](../../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md). The displayed evolution uses $\hbar=1$, as in the question. For $N=1$ the two independent defining vectors already span the whole qubit space, so no nonzero vector satisfies both orthogonality conditions; for larger $N$ their orthogonal complement has dimension $2^N-2$.
