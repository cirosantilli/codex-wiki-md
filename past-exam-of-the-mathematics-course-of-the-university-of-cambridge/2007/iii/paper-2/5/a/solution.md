<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For unital [algebras over a field](../../../../../../algebra-over-a-field.md) $A,B$, a [measuring coalgebra](../../../../../../measuring-coalgebra.md) is a [coalgebra](../../../../../../coalgebra.md) $C$ together with a linear map $p:C\to\operatorname{Hom}_k(A,B)$ such that, in [Sweedler notation](../../../../../../sweedler-notation.md),

$$
\boxed{p(c)(ab)=\sum p(c_{(1)})(a)p(c_{(2)})(b),\qquad
p(c)(1_A)=\varepsilon(c)1_B.}
$$

Thus a [group-like element](../../../../../../group-like-element.md) acts by a unital [algebra homomorphism over a field](../../../../../../algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md), while skew-primitive elements give twisted product rules.

The [universal measuring coalgebra](../../../../../../universal-measuring-coalgebra.md) $P(A,B)$ has a universal measuring map $\pi:P(A,B)\to\operatorname{Hom}_k(A,B)$ with this property: for every measuring map $p$ from a [coalgebra](../../../../../../coalgebra.md) $C$, there is a unique [coalgebra](../../../../../../coalgebra.md) homomorphism $f:C\to P(A,B)$ satisfying $\pi f=p$. The uniqueness is among [coalgebra](../../../../../../coalgebra.md) homomorphisms; $\pi$ itself need not be an injective map of [vector spaces](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md).
