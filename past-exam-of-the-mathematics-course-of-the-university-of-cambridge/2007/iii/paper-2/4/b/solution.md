<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every ordered [monomial](../../../../../../monomial.md) in the degree-$n$ homogeneous component has the form $x^{n-j}y^j$, $0\le j\le n$. The formulas in part (a) show

$$
\begin{aligned}
K(x^{n-j}y^j)&=q^{n-2j}x^{n-j}y^j,\\
E(x^{n-j}y^j)&=[j]x^{n-j+1}y^{j-1},\\
F(x^{n-j}y^j)&=[n-j]x^{n-j-1}y^{j+1}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Each nonzero image still has total degree $n$; $K^{-1}$ does too. Since $E,F,K,K^{-1}$ generate the [quantum enveloping algebra of sl2](../../../../../../quantum-enveloping-algebra-of-sl2.md), **the degree-$n$ component is a [submodule](../../../../../../submodule.md)**, with [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $x^n,x^{n-1}y,\ldots,y^n$ and dimension $n+1$.
