<h1 id="10g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The integral is linear in its first argument and conjugate-linear in its second, and [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) of the integrand shows $\langle g,f\rangle=\overline{\langle f,g\rangle}$. Thus it is a [Hermitian form](../../../../../../hermitian-form.md).

If $f(z)=\sum_{j=0}^N a_jz^j$, [Fourier orthogonality](../../../../../../fourier-orthogonality.md) gives

$$
\langle f,f\rangle
=\sum_{j,k}a_j\overline{a_k}\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}e^{i(j-k)\theta}\,d\theta
=\sum_{j=0}^N|a_j|^2.
$$

This is nonnegative and vanishes only when every coefficient vanishes, that is, only for the zero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md). Hence **the [Hermitian form](../../../../../../hermitian-form.md) is positive definite**.
