↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** Give $X=\mathbb R$ its usual [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md), let $Y$ be a one-point space, and take the constant [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) $f:X\to Y$. The set $K=Y$ is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md), but $f^{-1}(K)=\mathbb R$ is not [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md). For example, the [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) $\{(-n,n):n\ge1\}$ has no finite subcover. Thus continuity alone does not preserve [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) under inverse images.
