↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** Let $\{V_\alpha\}$ be an [open cover](../../../../../../open-cover.md) of $f(S)$ in its [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md). Write $V_\alpha=f(S)\cap W_\alpha$ with $W_\alpha$ open in $Y$. Since $f$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md), the sets $S\cap f^{-1}(W_\alpha)$ are open in $S$ and cover $S$. By [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md), finitely many cover $S$. Their corresponding $V_\alpha$ then cover $f(S)$, proving that the [continuous image of a compact space](../../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md) is compact.
