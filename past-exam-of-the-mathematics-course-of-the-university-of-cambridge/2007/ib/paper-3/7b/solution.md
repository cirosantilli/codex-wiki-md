<h1 id="7b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7B](../7b.md)

For the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md), split the two [expectation values](../../../../../expectation-value.md) into their [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md) and squared means:

$$
E=\frac{(\Delta p)^2}{2m}+\frac{m\omega^2(\Delta x)^2}{2}+\frac{\langle p\rangle^2}{2m}+\frac{m\omega^2\langle x\rangle^2}{2}.
$$

The last two terms are nonnegative, so

$$
\boxed{E\geq\frac{(\Delta p)^2}{2m}+\frac{m\omega^2(\Delta x)^2}{2}.}
$$

Apply the [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](../../../../../arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) to the two remaining nonnegative terms, then the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md):

$$
E\geq2\sqrt{\frac{(\Delta p)^2}{2m}\frac{m\omega^2(\Delta x)^2}{2}}=\omega\Delta p\Delta x\geq\boxed{\frac{\hbar\omega}{2}}.
$$

The argument applies to any normalized state with finite oscillator energy; the stated [stationary state](../../../../../stationary-state.md) is a particular case. Equality requires zero position and momentum means, minimum [quantum uncertainty](../../../../../quantum-uncertainty.md), and balanced kinetic and potential contributions, as in the oscillator [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md).
