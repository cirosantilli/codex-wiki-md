<h1 id="11g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) of the finite [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) as $|G|=p^am$, where $p$ is [prime](../../../../../../prime-number.md) and $p\nmid m$. The [Sylow theorems](../../../../../../sylow-theorems.md) say that a [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) of order $p^a$ exists; every [p-group](../../../../../../p-group.md) is contained in a [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md); and all [Sylow subgroups](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) are [conjugate subgroups](../../../../../../conjugate-subgroup.md). If $n_p$ is their number, then

$$
\boxed{n_p\equiv1\pmod p,\qquad n_p\mid m.}
$$

Equivalently, for a [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md) $P$, conjugation and the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) give $n_p=[G:N_G(P)]$, where $N_G(P)$ is the [normalizer](../../../../../../normalizer.md). The containment statement is often included in the conjugacy form: every [p-group](../../../../../../p-group.md) lies in a conjugate of any fixed [Sylow subgroup](../../../../../../sylow-subgroup.md).
