<h1 id="4a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Every point has arbitrarily small [path-connected](../../../../../../path-connected-space.md) open neighbourhoods. Indeed, in a [coordinate chart](../../../../../../manifold-chart.md) homeomorphic to $\mathbb R^n$, take the inverse image of a sufficiently small open [Euclidean ball](../../../../../../euclidean-ball.md). The ball is [convex](../../../../../../convex-function.md), hence [path-connected](../../../../../../path-connected-space.md), and its inverse image is [path-connected](../../../../../../path-connected-space.md) by the [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md). If the supplied neighbourhood is not itself open, first take an open neighbourhood within it and a ball contained in its chart image. This proves that $X$ is a [locally path-connected space](../../../../../../locally-path-connected-space.md).

Fix $x_0\in X$ and let $A$ consist of the points joined to $x_0$ by a [path](../../../../../../continuous-path.md). For $x\in A$, a [path-connected](../../../../../../path-connected-space.md) open neighbourhood $U_x$ is contained in $A$: concatenate a [path](../../../../../../continuous-path.md) from $x_0$ to $x$ with one from $x$ to any point of $U_x$. Hence $A$ is [open](../../../../../../open-set.md). For $x\notin A$, the same kind of neighbourhood cannot meet $A$, since a meeting point would provide a [path](../../../../../../continuous-path.md) to $x$. Hence $X\setminus A$ is also [open](../../../../../../open-set.md). Since $X$ is a [connected space](../../../../../../connected-space.md) and $A$ is nonempty, $A=X$. This proves **$X$ is path-connected**, including the general principle that [connected locally path-connected spaces are path-connected](../../../../../../connected-locally-path-connected-spaces-are-path-connected.md). The empty-space case, if allowed, is vacuous.
