The [row rank](../../../../../../row-rank.md) of a [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is the [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the [linear span](../../../../../../linear-span.md) of its row vectors. Its [column rank](../../../../../../column-rank.md) is the [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the [linear span](../../../../../../linear-span.md) of its column vectors, equivalently the [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of the corresponding [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md). The [equality of row rank and column rank](../../../../../../equality-of-row-rank-and-column-rank.md) states that **these two numbers are equal for every matrix**. Their common value is the [rank of a matrix](../../../../../../matrix-rank.md).
