<h1 id="14a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For each integer $n\ge1$, the [open balls](../../../../../../open-ball.md) $\{B(x,1/n):x\in M\}$ cover the [compact metric space](../../../../../../compact-metric-space.md) $M$. [Compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) supplies a finite subcover with centres forming a [finite set](../../../../../../finite-set.md) $S_n$. Define $S=\bigcup_{n\ge1}S_n$, a [countable union](../../../../../../countable-union.md) of [finite sets](../../../../../../finite-set.md) and therefore countable.

For any $x\in M$ and $\varepsilon>0$, choose $n$ with $1/n<\varepsilon$. One of the covering balls contains $x$, so there is $s\in S_n\subseteq S$ with $d(x,s)<1/n<\varepsilon$. Every open [neighbourhood](../../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $x$ therefore meets $S$, giving $x\in\operatorname{cl}(S)$. Thus **$\operatorname{cl}(S)=M$, and $S$ is a countable [dense subset](../../../../../../dense-set.md)**. If $M$ is empty, simply take $S=\varnothing$.
