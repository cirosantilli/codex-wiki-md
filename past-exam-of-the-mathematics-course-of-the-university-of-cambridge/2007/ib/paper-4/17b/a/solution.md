<h1 id="17b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let the [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) momenta be $\mathbf p_1,\mathbf p_2$, with $|\mathbf p_i|=E_i/c$. [Four-momentum conservation](../../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives total energy $E_1+E_2$ and total momentum $\mathbf p_1+\mathbf p_2$ for the [pion](../../../../../../pion.md). Its [mass shell](../../../../../../mass-shell.md) relation therefore gives

$$
m_\pi^2c^4=(E_1+E_2)^2-c^2|\mathbf p_1+\mathbf p_2|^2=2E_1E_2(1-\cos\theta)=4E_1E_2\sin^2(\theta/2).
$$

Since $0\le\theta\le\pi$, the [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) is nonnegative, and taking the positive [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\sin\frac\theta2=\frac{m_\pi c^2}{2\sqrt{E_1E_2}}.}
$$
