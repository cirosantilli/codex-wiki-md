<h1 id="17b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the [pion](../../../../../../pion.md) [rest frame](../../../../../../rest-frame.md), conservation of three-momentum makes the [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) and massless [neutrino](../../../../../../neutrino.md) momenta equal and opposite, of common magnitude $p$. Their energies obey

$$
E_e+pc=m_\pi c^2,\qquad E_e^2=p^2c^2+m_e^2c^4.
$$

Squaring $E_e=m_\pi c^2-pc$ and cancelling $p^2c^2$ gives

$$
pc=\frac{m_\pi^2-m_e^2}{2m_\pi}c^2,\qquad E_e=\frac{m_\pi^2+m_e^2}{2m_\pi}c^2.
$$

The [Electron](../../../../../../electron.md) [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) satisfies $p=E_ev/c^2$. Thus, for the physically allowed decay $m_\pi>m_e$,

$$
\boxed{v=c\frac{m_\pi^2-m_e^2}{m_\pi^2+m_e^2}=c\frac{1-(m_e/m_\pi)^2}{1+(m_e/m_\pi)^2}.}
$$
