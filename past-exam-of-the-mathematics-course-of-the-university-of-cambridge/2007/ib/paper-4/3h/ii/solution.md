<h1 id="3h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each fixed $x$, $x/n\to0$, so the [pointwise limit](../../../../../../pointwise-limit.md) is $1$. Moreover $1-\cos u=2\sin^2(u/2)\le u^2/2$ gives

$$
\sup_{0<x<1}|\cos(x/n)-1|\le\frac1{2n^2}\longrightarrow0.
$$

Thus **the limit is $1$, with [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) on $(0,1)$**.
