↑ **Parent:** [2G](../2g.md)

The [Fundamental theorem of finitely generated abelian groups](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-finitely-generated-abelian-groups.md) expresses every [finite abelian group](../../../../../finite-abelian-group.md) of this order as a product of [cyclic groups](../../../../../cyclic-group.md) of prime-power order. Their exponents correspond to the [partitions of an integer](../../../../../partition-of-an-integer.md) $4$:

$$
\boxed{C_{p^4},\quad C_{p^3}\times C_p,\quad C_{p^2}\times C_{p^2},\quad C_{p^2}\times C_p\times C_p,\quad C_p^4.}
$$

These are pairwise nonisomorphic: the largest [order of a group element](../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) distinguishes all except the two groups with largest element order $p^2$, and those have respectively $p^2$ and $p^3$ elements annihilated by multiplication by $p$. The classification exhausts the possibilities, so **there are five [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md) classes**.
