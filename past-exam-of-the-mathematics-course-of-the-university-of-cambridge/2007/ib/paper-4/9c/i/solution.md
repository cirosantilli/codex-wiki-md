<h1 id="9c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a time-homogeneous [Markov chain](../../../../../../markov-chain.md) with [transition matrix](../../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) $P$, two states are [communicating states](../../../../../../communicating-states.md) when each can be reached from the other with positive probability in finitely many steps:

$$
\boxed{i\leftrightarrow j\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \exists m,n\ge0:\ (P^m)_{ij}>0,\ (P^n)_{ji}>0.}
$$

The convention $P^0=I$ includes communication of a state with itself.
