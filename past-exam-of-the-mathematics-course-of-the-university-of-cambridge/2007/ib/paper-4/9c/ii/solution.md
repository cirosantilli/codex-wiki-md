<h1 id="9c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $T_i^+=\inf\{n\ge1:X_n=i\}$ be the first positive return time. A [recurrent state](../../../../../../recurrent-state.md) is one for which

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P_i(T_i^+<\infty)=1.}
$$

The subscript means that the [Markov chain](../../../../../../markov-chain.md) starts at $i$. The positive-time condition distinguishes a genuine return from the automatic visit at time zero.
