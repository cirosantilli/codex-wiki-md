<h1 id="12a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume $X$ is [Hausdorff](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md). For any $(x_1,x_2)$ outside the diagonal, the points differ, so choose disjoint open neighborhoods $U_1,U_2$. Their rectangle contains $(x_1,x_2)$ and misses the diagonal. Thus the complement of the diagonal is open in the [product topology](../../../../../../product-topology.md), proving **(i) implies (ii)**. This is one direction of the [closed diagonal theorem](../../../../../../closed-diagonal-theorem.md).
