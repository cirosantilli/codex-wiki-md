<h1 id="5d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The velocity divergence is $\partial_xu_x+\partial_yu_y=\alpha-\alpha=0$, satisfying the constant-density [incompressible flow](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) condition. Choose the [stream function](../../../../../../stream-function.md) convention $(u_x,u_y)=(\psi_y,-\psi_x)$. Integration then gives

$$
\boxed{\psi(x,y)=\alpha xy-\frac\beta2(x^2+y^2)+C.}
$$

Indeed, $\psi_y=\alpha x-\beta y$ and $-\psi_x=\beta x-\alpha y$. [Streamlines](../../../../../../streamline.md) are contours of this function. The [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is also compatible with steady inviscid momentum balance without body forces: $(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=(\alpha^2-\beta^2)(x,y)$ is balanced by [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) $p=p_0-\rho(\alpha^2-\beta^2)(x^2+y^2)/2$.
