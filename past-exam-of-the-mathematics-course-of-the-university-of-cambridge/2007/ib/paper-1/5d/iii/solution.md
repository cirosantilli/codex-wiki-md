<h1 id="5d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Rotate coordinates to $s=(x+y)/\sqrt2$ and $t=(x-y)/\sqrt2$. The [stream function](../../../../../../stream-function.md) becomes

$$
\psi=\frac12\left((\alpha-\beta)s^2-(\alpha+\beta)t^2\right).
$$

For $\alpha<\beta$, both coefficients are negative, so every nonzero contour is a bounded ellipse surrounding the stationary origin. For $\alpha>\beta$, the form is indefinite: nonzero contours are unbounded hyperbolas, and the zero contour consists of straight separatrices. Consequently **all nonstationary [streamlines](../../../../../../streamline.md) are bounded exactly when $\alpha<\beta$**. The origin is itself a stationary bounded trajectory in every case; the assertion about boundedness concerns the nonstationary [streamlines](../../../../../../streamline.md). The equality case is handled separately below. These are the [linear planar strain and rotation streamlines](../../../../../../linear-planar-strain-and-rotation-streamlines.md).
