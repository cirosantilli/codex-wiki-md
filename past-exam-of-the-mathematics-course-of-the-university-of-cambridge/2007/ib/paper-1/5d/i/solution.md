↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) is the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) of the velocity, perpendicular to the flow plane:

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol\omega=(0,0,\partial_xu_y-\partial_yu_x)=(0,0,2\beta).}
$$

The planar rotation rate is half this scalar [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md), not the [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) itself.
