The [Fundamental theorem of finitely generated abelian groups](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-finitely-generated-abelian-groups.md) says that every finitely generated [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md) is isomorphic to

$$
\boxed{\mathbb Z^r\oplus\mathbb Z/d_1\mathbb Z\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathbb Z/d_s\mathbb Z,\qquad
1<d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_s.}
$$

The [rank of an abelian group](../../../../../../rank-of-an-abelian-group.md) $r$ and the invariant factors $d_j$ are uniquely determined, with the finite summand allowed to be absent. Equivalently, one can decompose the torsion subgroup into cyclic groups of prime-power orders. The infinite cyclic part and torsion part must both be included in the statement.
