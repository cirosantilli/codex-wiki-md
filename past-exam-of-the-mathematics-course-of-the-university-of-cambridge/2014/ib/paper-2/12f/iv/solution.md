**True**, and the closedness assumption on $Y$ is unnecessary. The [Minkowski sum](../../../../../../minkowski-addition.md) has the representation

$$
X+Y=\bigcup_{y\in Y}(X+y).
$$

Each translate of the open set $X$ is open, and an arbitrary union of open sets is open. If $Y$ is empty the union is empty, which is also open.
