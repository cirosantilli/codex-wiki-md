↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** Let $g=f^{-1}$. For every open $U\subseteq X$,

$$
g^{-1}(U)=f(U),
$$

which is open in $Y$ because $f$ is an [open map](../../../../../../open-map.md). Thus $g$ is continuous. Together with the given continuity and bijectivity of $f$, this establishes a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md). This is [inverse continuity from an open bijection](../../../../../../inverse-continuity-from-an-open-bijection.md).
