For [topological spaces](../../../../topological-space.md), [continuity](../../../../continuous-function.md) means that $f^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$ for every open set $V$ in $Y$. Equivalently, inverse images of closed sets are closed. A [homeomorphism](../../../../homeomorphism.md) is a bijection for which both the map and its inverse are continuous.
