<h1 id="5d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Every sine term vanishes at zero, so **$G(0)=0$**. The one-sided values of the periodic extension of $f'$ are $f'_{\rm per}(0-)= -1$ and $f'_{\rm per}(0+)=1$. The [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) converges at this jump to their midpoint,

$$
\boxed{G(0)=\frac{f'_{\rm per}(0-)+f'_{\rm per}(0+)}2=0.}
$$

This midpoint need not equal the arbitrarily assigned value of the periodic derivative at the endpoint.
