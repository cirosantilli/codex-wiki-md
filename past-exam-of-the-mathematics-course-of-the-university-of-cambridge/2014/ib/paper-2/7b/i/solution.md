Here $u=x$, $v=-y$. The [streamlines](../../../../../../streamline.md) are **$xy=\text{constant}$**, rectangular hyperbolas, together with the coordinate axes as separatrices. Particles satisfy $x(t)=x(0)e^t$, $y(t)=y(0)e^{-t}$: the flow stretches along the $x$ axis and compresses along the $y$ axis, with a saddle [stagnation point](../../../../../../stagnation-point.md) at the origin. The left panel shows the flow directions.
