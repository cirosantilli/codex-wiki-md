<h1 id="17a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [near-origin probability in a maximal-angular-momentum hydrogen state](../../../../../../near-origin-probability-in-a-maximal-angular-momentum-hydrogen-state.md), integrate the normalized radial probability density:

$$
P(r<r_p)=|A|^2\int_0^{r_p}r^{2l+2}e^{-2r/[a(l+1)]}\,dr.
$$

Throughout this small interval the exponential is $1+O(r_p/a)$ for fixed $l$. Hence

$$
P(r<r_p)=\frac{|A|^2r_p^{2l+3}}{2l+3}
\left[1+O(r_p/a)\right].
$$

Using the normalization above,

$$
\boxed{P(r<r_p)=
\frac{2^{2l+3}}{(2l+3)!(l+1)^{2l+3}}
\left(\frac{r_p}{a}\right)^{2l+3}
\left[1+O(r_p/a)\right],\qquad
C=(2l+3)!(l+1)^{2l+3}.}
$$

The high power of the small radius reflects the near-origin behavior $R\propto r^l$ of the [hydrogen atom](../../../../../../hydrogen-atom.md) state.
