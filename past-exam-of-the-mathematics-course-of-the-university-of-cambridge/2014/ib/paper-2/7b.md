# 7B

↑ **Parent:** [Paper 2](../paper-2-split.md)

For the [linear planar flow with strain and rotation](../../../../linear-planar-flow-with-strain-and-rotation.md), $\partial_xu+\partial_yv=\epsilon-\epsilon=0$, proving [incompressibility](../../../../incompressible-flow.md). The [Cartesian streamfunction](../../../../cartesian-streamfunction.md) convention gives $u=\psi_y$, $v=-\psi_x$, and direct integration gives

$$
\boxed{\psi=\epsilon xy-\frac\gamma2(x^2+y^2)}
$$

up to an irrelevant additive constant. Along a [streamline](../../../../streamline.md), $\mathbf u\cdot\nabla\psi=\psi_y\psi_x-\psi_x\psi_y=0$. Since

$$
4\psi=(\epsilon-\gamma)(x+y)^2-(\epsilon+\gamma)(x-y)^2,
$$

the stated quadratic expression is constant along each streamline. The three cases below distinguish strain, rotation and their degenerate shear limit.
