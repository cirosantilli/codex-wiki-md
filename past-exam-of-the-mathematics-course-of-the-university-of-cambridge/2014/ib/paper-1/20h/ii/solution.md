<h1 id="20h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The convergence theorem for an irreducible aperiodic positive recurrent [Markov chain](../../../../../../markov-chain.md) says $\mathbb P_i(X_n=j)\to\pi_j$ for every pair of states. Apply it to $i=j=0$ and take the complement:

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb P_0(X_n\ne0)=1-\pi_0=\frac1{1+p}.}
$$

Although $p_{00}=0$, the chain is aperiodic because its possible return lengths have greatest common divisor one; a self-loop is sufficient but not necessary for [aperiodicity](../../../../../../aperiodic-markov-chain.md).
