<h1 id="15a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [Parseval identity](../../../../../../parseval-identity.md) for the orthonormal stationary-state expansion is $\sum_{n\geq1}|c_n|^2=\|\psi\|^2=1$. Splitting the amplitudes into even and odd indices gives

$$
1=\frac3{\pi^2}\sum_{p=1}^\infty\frac1{p^2}+\frac{12}{\pi^2}\sum_{p=1}^\infty\frac1{(2p-1)^2}\left[1+\frac{2(-1)^p}{(2p-1)\pi}\right]^2.
$$

Multiply by $\pi^2/3$ to obtain the requested identity. Its integer constants are

$$
\boxed{A=1,\qquad B=4,\qquad C=2.}
$$

The series converges absolutely, since both parity contributions are $O(p^{-2})$. This derivation uses the normalization of the prepared state and completeness of the well [stationary states](../../../../../../stationary-state.md), rather than a separate summation trick.
