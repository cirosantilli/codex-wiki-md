<h1 id="15a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The squared sine has integral $a$ over the interval of length $2a$, since it contains an integer number of half-wavelengths. A real positive phase convention therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\alpha_n=\frac1{\sqrt a}.}
$$

Inside the [infinite square well](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md), the [Time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) is $-\hbar^2\psi_n''/(2m)=E_n\psi_n$. The wavenumber is $\pi n/(2a)$, so

$$
\boxed{E_n=\frac{\hbar^2\pi^2n^2}{8ma^2},\qquad n\geq1.}
$$

The [stationary states](../../../../../../stationary-state.md) vanish at both walls and form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of the square-integrable state space. A common arbitrary phase on each basis function does not alter any energy or measurement probability.
