<h1 id="15a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The original PDF threshold contains $\hbar$, not $h$. It is $8E_1$, so the energies strictly above it are exactly those with $n\geq3$. By the [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md), the required probability is $1-|c_1|^2-|c_2|^2$. The two excluded amplitudes are

$$
c_1=\frac{2\sqrt3}{\pi}\left(1-\frac2\pi\right),\qquad c_2=-\frac{\sqrt3}{\pi}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(E>8E_1)=1-\frac{15}{\pi^2}+\frac{48}{\pi^3}-\frac{48}{\pi^4}.}
$$

Thus, in increasing powers of $1/\pi$,

$$
\boxed{(b_0,b_1,b_2,b_3,b_4)=(1,0,-15,48,-48).}
$$

The threshold lies strictly between $E_2$ and $E_3$, so no equality-at-threshold convention affects the answer.
