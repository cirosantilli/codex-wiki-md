<h1 id="9g/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since the first $k$ basis vectors span the [invariant subspace](../../../../../../invariant-subspace.md) $W$, their images have no components in the last $n-k$ coordinates. Thus

$$
\boxed{[T]=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\0&D\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Here $A$ is the matrix of $T|_W$, $D$ is the matrix of the induced map on the [quotient vector space](../../../../../../quotient-vector-space.md) $V/W$, and $B$ is unrestricted. The chosen complementary span need not itself be invariant, so $D$ should not in general be called a restriction to that complement. The block-triangular [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\chi_T(t)=\chi_A(t)\chi_D(t).}
$$
