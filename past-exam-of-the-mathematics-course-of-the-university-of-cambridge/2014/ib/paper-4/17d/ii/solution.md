The [Parseval identity](../../../../../../parseval-identity.md) for a [complex Fourier series](../../../../../../complex-fourier-series.md) is

$$
 \boxed{\frac1{2L}\int_{-L}^L|f(x)|^2dx=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}|c_n|^2.}
$$

It holds for every [square-integrable function](../../../../../../square-integrable-function.md), with complex conjugation expressed by the absolute squares. It is the [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) extended to the complete orthonormal system of complex exponentials.
