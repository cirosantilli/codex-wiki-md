↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [complex Fourier series](../../../../../../complex-fourier-series.md) of a $2L$-periodic function is

$$
 \boxed{f(x)\sim\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}c_ne^{in\pi x/L},\qquad
 c_n=\frac1{2L}\int_{-L}^L f(x)e^{-in\pi x/L}\,dx.}
$$

The coefficient formula follows from [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of the exponential functions. For an arbitrary [square-integrable function](../../../../../../square-integrable-function.md), this representation means convergence in the $L^2$ [norm](../../../../../../norm.md). With appropriate additional regularity it also gives pointwise convergence, to the mean of the one-sided limits at a jump. No pointwise representation is asserted for an unrestricted function merely from periodicity.
