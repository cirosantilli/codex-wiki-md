<h1 id="17d/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For real nonintegral $\alpha$, the [Parseval identity](../../../../../../parseval-identity.md) and the coefficient formula give

$$
 \frac{\alpha^2\sin^2(\pi\alpha)}{\pi^2}
 \sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\frac1{(n^2-\alpha^2)^2}
 =\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^\pi\cos^2(\alpha x)dx
 =\frac12+\frac{\sin(2\pi\alpha)}{4\pi\alpha}.
$$

Solving for the sum yields

$$
 \boxed{\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\frac1{(n^2-\alpha^2)^2}
 =\frac{\pi^2}{2\alpha^2\sin^2(\pi\alpha)}
 +\frac{\pi\cot(\pi\alpha)}{2\alpha^3}.}
$$

At $\alpha=5/2$, the sine square is one and the cotangent is zero, giving **$2\pi^2/25$**.
