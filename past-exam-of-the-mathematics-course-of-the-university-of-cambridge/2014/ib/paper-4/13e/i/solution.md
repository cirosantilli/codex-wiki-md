<h1 id="13e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the incomplete [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) $X=(0,1)$ with its usual distance, and

$$
 \boxed{A_n=(0,1/(n+1)]\subset X.}
$$

Each is nonempty and closed relative to $X$: it is $X\cap(-\infty,1/(n+1)]$. The sequence is strictly descending and its [diameters](../../../../../../diameter.md) are $1/(n+1)\to0$. Its intersection is empty, since every positive point exceeds $1/(n+1)$ for sufficiently large $n$. The excluded limiting point is zero.
