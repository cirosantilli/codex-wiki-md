<h1 id="13e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In $\mathbb R$, take

$$
 \boxed{A_n=[n,\infty).}
$$

The sets are nonempty, closed and strictly descending, but their intersection is empty. Their [diameters](../../../../../../diameter.md) are infinite, so the shrinking-diameter assumption is essential.
