<h1 id="13e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In $\mathbb R$, take

$$
 \boxed{A_n=(0,1/n).}
$$

These are nonempty descending sets of [diameter](../../../../../../diameter.md) $1/n$, with empty intersection. They are not [closed sets](../../../../../../closed-set.md), so there is no contradiction with the [Cantor intersection theorem](../../../../../../cantor-s-intersection-theorem.md) for the complete [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md) $\mathbb R$.
