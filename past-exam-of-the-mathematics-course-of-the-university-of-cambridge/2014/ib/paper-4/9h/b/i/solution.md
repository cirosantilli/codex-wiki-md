↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The authoritative definition here is $Y_n=|X_n|$. On the conditioning event, $X_{n-1}=0$. Since $p_{0,1}=0$, the additional condition $Y_n=1$ then forces $X_n=-1$. The [Markov property](../../../../../../../markov-property.md) therefore gives

$$
 \boxed{\mathbb P(Y_{n+1}=0\mid Y_n=1,Y_{n-1}=0)=p_{-1,0}.}
$$

As usual, this [conditional probability](../../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is asserted when the conditioning event has positive probability.
