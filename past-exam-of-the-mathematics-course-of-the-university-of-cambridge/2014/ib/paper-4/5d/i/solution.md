<h1 id="5d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Away from zero the [Dirac delta distribution](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) is absent. On either half-line, use $u=1+k|z|$ and the [power-law ansatz](../../../../../../power-law-ansatz.md) $\psi=u^\alpha$. The [Euler-Cauchy equation](../../../../../../euler-cauchy-equation.md) becomes

$$
 \alpha(\alpha-1)-\frac{15}{4}=0,
 \qquad \alpha=\frac52\ \text{or}\ -\frac32.
$$

The growing term is excluded by boundedness at either end. Thus the independently bounded half-line solutions are

$$
 \boxed{\psi(z)=C_+(1+kz)^{-3/2}\quad(z>0),\qquad
 \psi(z)=C_-(1-kz)^{-3/2}\quad(z<0).}
$$
