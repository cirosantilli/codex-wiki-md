<h1 id="7e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use [uncountability by interleaving prescribed binary coordinates](../../../../../../uncountability-by-interleaving-prescribed-binary-coordinates.md). For each arbitrary [binary sequence](../../../../../../bitstream.md) $b=(b_k)_{k\geq1}$, define another [binary sequence](../../../../../../bitstream.md) by

$$
x_{3k-2}=0,\qquad x_{3k-1}=a_{3k-1},\qquad x_{3k}=b_k\quad(k\geq1).
$$

There are infinitely many zero coordinates, namely $3k-2$, so $x\in X$. There are also infinitely many coordinates agreeing with the prescribed [binary expansion](../../../../../../binary-expansion.md), namely $3k-1$, so $x\in Y$. The coordinates $3k$ recover $b$, making this map an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) of the [uncountable set](../../../../../../uncountable-set.md) of all [binary sequences](../../../../../../bitstream.md) into $Y$. If $Y$ were a [countable set](../../../../../../countable-set.md), composing with an [injection](../../../../../../injective-function.md) into $\mathbb N$ would make all [binary sequences](../../../../../../bitstream.md) countable, contrary to part (i). **Therefore $\boxed{Y\text{ is uncountable}.}$** The construction works for every prescribed [binary sequence](../../../../../../bitstream.md) $(a_n)$; no special distribution property of the digits of $\sqrt2$ is needed.
