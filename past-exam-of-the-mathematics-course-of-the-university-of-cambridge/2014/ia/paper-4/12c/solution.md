<h1 id="12c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [12C](../12c.md)

For a massive particle of [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) $m$, its [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) is $P^\mu=mU^\mu=(E/c,\mathbf p)$, where the [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) is $U^\mu=\gamma(c,\mathbf v)$, $E=\gamma mc^2$ and $\mathbf p=\gamma m\mathbf v$. With [metric signature](../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, $P\cdot P=-m^2c^2$. A [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) $(E/c,(E/c)\hat{\mathbf n})$, a future-pointing [null vector](../../../../../null-vector.md). [Four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) states that the total incoming and outgoing [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md) agree for an isolated interaction; this includes both [energy](../../../../../energy.md) and [momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) in every inertial frame.

Take the incoming [photon](../../../../../photon.md) direction as $x$. Its [energy](../../../../../energy.md) is $\hbar\omega=\mu mc^2$, so the total initial [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) is

$$
P=mc(1+\mu,\mu,0,0),\qquad -P\cdot P=m^2c^2(1+2\mu).
$$

The [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) available after [photon absorption](../../../../../photon-absorption.md) is therefore $M=m\sqrt{1+2\mu}$. In the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), the two equal daughters have opposite [momenta](../../../../../momentum.md). Their total [energy](../../../../../energy.md) is at least their combined [rest energy](../../../../../rest-energy.md) $2\alpha mc^2$, with equality precisely when both daughters have zero [momentum](../../../../../momentum.md) in that frame. Thus the [mass threshold after photon absorption](../../../../../mass-threshold-after-photon-absorption.md) is

$$
\boxed{\alpha_{\max}=\frac12\sqrt{1+2\mu}.}
$$

The bound is attainable kinematically by taking each daughter [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) equal to $P/2$; each then has the required [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md). It immediately gives **$\alpha_{\max}\to1/2$ as $\mu\to0$**.

At this threshold both daughters move with the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). The common laboratory [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) follows from $\mathbf v=c\mathbf P/P^0$:

$$
\boxed{v=\frac{c\mu}{1+\mu}=\frac{c}{1+\mu^{-1}}.}
$$

For $\mu=0$, its continuous limiting value is zero. At smaller allowed daughter [rest masses](../../../../../invariant-mass.md), some of the [centre-of-momentum energy](../../../../../centre-of-momentum-energy.md) appears as their relative [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), so their [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md) need not be $P/2$.
