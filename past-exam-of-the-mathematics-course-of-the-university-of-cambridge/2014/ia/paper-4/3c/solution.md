<h1 id="3c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3C](../3c.md)

In classical [charged particle in a uniform magnetic field](../../../../../charged-particle-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md) motion, the [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) and [Newton's second law](../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) give

$$
m\dot{\mathbf v}=q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B.
$$

Assume $\mathbf B\ne0$ and choose an orthonormal coordinate system with $\mathbf B=B\mathbf e_z$, $B=|\mathbf B|$. Write $\Omega=qB/m$, retaining the sign of the [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge.md). The [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) equations are

$$
\dot v_x=\Omega v_y,\qquad\dot v_y=-\Omega v_x,\qquad\dot v_z=0.
$$

Thus the parallel [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is constant, and the perpendicular [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) rotates at constant [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md). Choosing the time origin and perpendicular axes conveniently gives

$$
v_x=v_\perp\cos\Omega t,\qquad v_y=-v_\perp\sin\Omega t,\qquad v_z=v_\parallel.
$$

Integrating the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), one obtains

$$
x=x_c+\frac{v_\perp}{\Omega}\sin\Omega t,\qquad y=y_c+\frac{v_\perp}{\Omega}\cos\Omega t,\qquad z=z_0+v_\parallel t.
$$

The first two coordinates describe a [circle](../../../../../circle.md) of radius $mv_\perp/(|q|B)$, while the third advances uniformly. This is [helical motion in a uniform magnetic field](../../../../../helical-motion-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md): the [helix](../../../../../helix.md) has **axis parallel to $\mathbf B$** through the [guiding centre](../../../../../guiding-center.md). Its [cyclotron frequency](../../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md) is

$$
\boxed{\omega_c=\frac{|q|B}{m}},
$$

and its signed [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) vector about that axis is $-q\mathbf B/m$. The minus sign follows from the ordering $\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$ in the [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md). The [helix](../../../../../helix.md) degenerates to a [circle](../../../../../circle.md) when $v_\parallel=0$ and to a straight line when $v_\perp=0$. If $q=0$ or $B=0$, there is only straight-line motion. In a relativistic extension with fixed [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma$, the frequency becomes $|q|B/(\gamma m)$; the classical result above uses $m\mathbf v$ as [momentum](../../../../../momentum.md).
