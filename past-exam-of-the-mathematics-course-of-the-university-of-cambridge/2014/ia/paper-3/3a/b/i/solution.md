<h1 id="3a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply the [product rule](../../../../../../../product-rule.md) and the preceding [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) formula to the [vector field](../../../../../../../vector-field.md) $F_i=f(r)x_i$. Summing over the repeated index gives

$$
\partial_iF_i=f'(r)\frac{x_i x_i}{r}+f(r)\delta_{ii}
=rf'(r)+nf(r).
$$

Here $\delta_{ii}=n$ for the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../../../kronecker-delta.md). Thus the [divergence and curl of a radial vector field](../../../../../../../divergence-and-curl-of-a-radial-vector-field.md) give, in any dimension,

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot[f(r)\mathbf x]=rf'(r)+nf(r).}
$$
