In three dimensions the [curl](../../../../../../../curl.md) can be written with the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md):

$$
(\nabla\times\mathbf F)_i
=\epsilon_{ijk}\partial_j(f(r)x_k)
=\epsilon_{ijk}\left(\frac{f'(r)}r x_jx_k+f(r)\delta_{jk}\right)=0.
$$

Both terms contract an antisymmetric array with a symmetric one, and therefore vanish. **Every differentiable radial field of this form has zero [curl](../../../../../../../curl.md) away from the origin.** This is the three-dimensional part of the [divergence and curl of a radial vector field](../../../../../../../divergence-and-curl-of-a-radial-vector-field.md).
