<h1 id="3a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Away from the origin, differentiate $r^2=x_jx_j$ using [Einstein notation](../../../../../../einstein-notation.md):

$$
2r\,\partial_i r=2x_i.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\partial_i r=x_i/r,\qquad \nabla r=\mathbf x/r.}
$$

This [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is the radial unit [vector](../../../../../../vector.md). Division by $r$ is legitimate on the punctured domain; the distance [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is not differentiable at the origin.
