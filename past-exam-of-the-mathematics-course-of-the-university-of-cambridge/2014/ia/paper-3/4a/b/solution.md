<h1 id="4a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A sufficient condition is that $G$ be an open [simply connected](../../../../../../simply-connected-space.md) domain, with $\mathbf F$ continuously differentiable. Under this condition a [curl-free vector field](../../../../../../irrotational-vector-field.md) has a global [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) and hence [path independence](../../../../../../path-independence.md) by the [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md). One way to see the global step is to deform the closed loop formed by two paths into a point inside $G$: the integral is unchanged during the deformation because the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) is zero, as expressed by [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md). Therefore **simple connectivity is a sufficient domain hypothesis**. It is not a claim that every particular [curl](../../../../../../curl.md)-free field needs such a domain; a given field can have a global potential on a domain with holes.
