<h1 id="10a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an oriented piecewise smooth surface $S$ and a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) on a neighborhood of it, [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\int_S(\nabla\times\mathbf F)\cdot d\mathbf S
=\oint_{\partial S}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf x.}
$$

The [oriented surface element](../../../../../../oriented-surface-element.md) and boundary direction must agree by the right-hand rule. Every boundary component is included; for an upward-oriented graph over an [annulus](../../../../../../annulus-mathematics.md) the outer boundary is anticlockwise and the inner boundary clockwise when viewed from above.
