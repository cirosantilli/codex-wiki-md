<h1 id="8d/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use additive notation and take $G=\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$, $N=\{0,2\}$. Its [quotient group](../../../../../../../quotient-group.md) is $C_2$. A [group homomorphism](../../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) from $C_2$ sends its generator to an element annihilated by $2$, so the image must be either $0$ or $2$. Both project to the identity coset $N$, and neither can project to the nonidentity generator of $G/N$. Therefore **this quotient projection has no homomorphic section**. It is a nonsplit [group extension](../../../../../../../group-extension.md), despite both $G$ and $N$ being nontrivial finite [groups](../../../../../../../group-split.md).
