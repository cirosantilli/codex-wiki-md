<h1 id="8d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $N$ is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) of $G$ if $gNg^{-1}=N$ for every $g\in G$. Define multiplication on its left [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md) by

$$
(gN)(hN)=ghN.
$$

To check that this is well-defined, replace $g,h$ by $gn_1,hn_2$ with $n_1,n_2\in N$. Then

$$
(gn_1)(hn_2)=gh(h^{-1}n_1h)n_2\in ghN,
$$

because normality gives $h^{-1}n_1h\in N$. Thus the product depends only on the two [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md). Associativity follows from that of $G$, the identity is $N$, and the inverse of $gN$ is $g^{-1}N$. These are all the [group axioms](../../../../../../group-axioms.md), so **$G/N$ is a [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) under coset multiplication**. The natural projection $g\mapsto gN$ is a surjective [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) with kernel $N$.
