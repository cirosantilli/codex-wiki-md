<h1 id="4f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $a_n=n!/n^n$. Its consecutive coefficient ratio is

$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}=\left(\frac n{n+1}\right)^n\longrightarrow e^{-1}.
$$

The [ratio test](../../../../../../ratio-test.md) for the full terms therefore has limit $|z|/e$: the series has [absolute convergence](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) when $|z|<e$ and its terms eventually grow in magnitude when $|z|>e$. Thus **the [radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md) is** $\boxed{R=e}$. No boundary decision is needed to determine the [radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md).
