An [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of a [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $A$ is a nonzero vector $v$ for which $Av=\lambda v$. The scalar $\lambda$ is its [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md). For a fixed [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), the zero vector together with all corresponding [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) forms the [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) $\ker(A-\lambda I)$. The requirement $v\ne0$ prevents every scalar from being an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) merely because $A0=\lambda0$.
