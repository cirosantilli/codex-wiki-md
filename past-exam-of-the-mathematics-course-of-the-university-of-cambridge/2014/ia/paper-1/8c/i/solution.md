<h1 id="8c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expanding the [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) immediately gives $[A,A]=0$, $[A,B]=-[B,A]$, and $[A,\lambda B]=\lambda[A,B]$. For its [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md), write

$$
\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\sum_{i,j}A_{ij}B_{ji}
=\sum_{j,i}B_{ji}A_{ij}=\operatorname{tr}(BA).
$$

Consequently **every [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) is traceless**: $\boxed{\operatorname{tr}[A,B]=0}$. This is the [trace of a matrix commutator](../../../../../../trace-of-a-matrix-commutator.md) identity, valid for arbitrary square [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) over the [complex numbers](../../../../../../complex-number.md).
