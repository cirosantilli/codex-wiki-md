<h1 id="8c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Every traceless [skew-Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md) of size two has the form

$$
C=\begin{pmatrix}ip&w\\-\overline w&-ip\end{pmatrix},\qquad p\in\mathbb R,
$$

so it equals $\mathcal M(c)$ for $c=(2p,2\operatorname{Re}w,2\operatorname{Im}w)$. If $c=0$, take $A=B=0$. Otherwise choose a [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) $u$ perpendicular to $c$ and set $a=u$, $b=c\times u$. The [vector triple product](../../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) gives $a\times b=c$.

By part (iii), choosing $A=\mathcal M(a)$ and $B=\mathcal M(b)$ gives **the required representation**

$$
\boxed{C=[A,B].}
$$

In fact both factors can themselves be chosen as traceless [skew-Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md). The [cross-product model of su(2)](../../../../../../cross-product-model-of-su-2.md) explains this geometric [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) construction.
