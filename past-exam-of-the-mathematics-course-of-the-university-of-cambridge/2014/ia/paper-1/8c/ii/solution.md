<h1 id="8c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [conjugate transpose](../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) reverses multiplication. If $A^\dagger=-A$ and $B^\dagger=-B$, then

$$
[A,B]^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger-A^\dagger B^\dagger
=BA-AB=-[A,B].
$$

Thus **the [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) is again a [skew-Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md)**. The order reversal is what produces the required minus sign.
