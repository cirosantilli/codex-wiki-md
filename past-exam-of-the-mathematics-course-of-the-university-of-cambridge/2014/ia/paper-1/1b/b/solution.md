<h1 id="1b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The exponent in the PDF is $2z^2$. A [complex exponential](../../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) equals one precisely when its exponent is an integer multiple of $2\pi i$: its real part must be zero, and its imaginary part must be a full rotation. Therefore $z^2=\pi i k$ for $k\in\mathbb Z$. Taking both square roots yields **all solutions**

$$
\boxed{\{0\}\ \cup\ \left\{\pm\sqrt{\frac{\pi k}2}(1+i):k=1,2,\ldots\right\}
\ \cup\ \left\{\pm\sqrt{\frac{\pi k}2}(1-i):k=1,2,\ldots\right\}.}
$$

The first nonzero family corresponds to positive exponent index, and the second to negative index. Both signs are required.
