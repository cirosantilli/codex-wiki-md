<h1 id="1b/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write a nonzero [complex number](../../../../../../../complex-number.md) as $w=re^{i\theta}$. Matching [modulus](../../../../../../../modulus.md) and [complex argument](../../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) gives $r^4=2\sqrt2$ and $4\theta=\pi/4+2\pi k$. Thus **the four distinct fourth roots are**

$$
\boxed{w_k=2^{3/8}\exp\!\left(i\left(\frac\pi{16}+\frac{k\pi}2\right)\right),\qquad k=0,1,2,3.}
$$

These angles differ by $\pi/2$, so the values are distinct. Every further integer $k$ repeats one of them; this is multiplication by the fourth [roots of unity](../../../../../../../root-of-unity.md).
