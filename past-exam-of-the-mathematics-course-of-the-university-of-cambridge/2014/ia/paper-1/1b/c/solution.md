<h1 id="1b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a real line $ux+vy+w=0$, with $(u,v)\ne(0,0)$, choose $B=(u-iv)/2$ and $C=w$. Direct multiplication gives $Bz+\overline B\,\overline z=ux+vy$, so **every line has the required form** with $B\ne0$.

For a [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) of centre $c$ and radius $\rho>0$, expand its [modulus](../../../../../../modulus.md) equation:

$$
|z-c|^2=\rho^2
\iff z\overline z-\overline c z-c\overline z+|c|^2-\rho^2=0.
$$

Thus **the required [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) parameters are** $\boxed{B=-c,\ C=|c|^2-\rho^2}$. Conversely, completing the square in the [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) form gives $|z+B|^2=|B|^2-C$, which describes a genuine [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) when $|B|^2-C>0$. The [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) terms make both equations real-valued.
