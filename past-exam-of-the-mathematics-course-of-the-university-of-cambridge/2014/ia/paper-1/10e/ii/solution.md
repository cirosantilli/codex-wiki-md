<h1 id="10e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at any $x$, choose $\delta=\eta^{1/\alpha}$ for a desired error $\eta>0$. If $|y-x|<\delta$, the stated bound gives $|f(y)-f(x)|<\eta$. Thus **$f$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md)**, in fact [uniformly continuous](../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md).

When $\alpha>1$, the difference quotient satisfies

$$
\left|\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}h\right|\leq|h|^{\alpha-1}\longrightarrow0.
$$

Therefore the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) exists at every point and is zero. Part (i) proves **$f$ is constant**. This is the lemma that [Hölder exponent greater than one forces constancy](../../../../../../holder-exponent-greater-than-one-forces-constancy.md); no differentiability was assumed in advance.
