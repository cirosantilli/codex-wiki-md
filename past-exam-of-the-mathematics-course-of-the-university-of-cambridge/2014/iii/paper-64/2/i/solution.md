<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a scalar $u\in BV(\Omega)$, put $E_t=\{x\in\Omega:u(x)>t\}$. The [coarea formula for BV functions](../../../../../../coarea-formula-for-bv-functions.md) is the [measure](../../../../../../measure.md) identity

$$
\boxed{|Du|(A)=\int_{\mathbb R}\operatorname{Per}(E_t;A)\,dt}
$$

for every Borel $A\subseteq\Omega$. For almost every real $t$, $E_t$ is a [finite-perimeter set](../../../../../../set-of-finite-perimeter.md), with $\operatorname{Per}(E_t;A)=|D\chi_{E_t}|(A)$. In particular $\operatorname{TV}(u)=\int_{\mathbb R}\operatorname{Per}(E_t;\Omega)dt$. This is [relative perimeter](../../../../../../relative-perimeter.md): it counts interfaces inside $\Omega$, and does not add the boundary jump of a zero extension.
