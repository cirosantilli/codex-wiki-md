<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a defining function $\phi$ with $d\phi\ne0$, the [characteristic hypersurface](../../../../../../characteristic-hypersurface.md) test is that the [principal symbol](../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) vanish at $d\phi$.

For the [wave equation](../../../../../../wave-equation-split.md) with speed $c>0$,

$$
u_{tt}-c^2\Delta_xu=0,\qquad p(\tau,\xi)=\tau^2-c^2|\xi|^2.
$$

Thus its [characteristic hypersurfaces](../../../../../../characteristic-hypersurface.md) satisfy $\phi_t^2=c^2|\nabla_x\phi|^2$. In one space dimension the two families are $x\pm ct=\text{constant}$; cones are characteristic away from their vertices.

For the [free Schrodinger equation](../../../../../../free-schrodinger-equation.md), in normalized units,

$$
i u_t+\Delta_xu=0,\qquad p(\tau,\xi)=|\xi|^2.
$$

Its total-order [characteristic hypersurfaces](../../../../../../characteristic-hypersurface.md) satisfy $\nabla_x\phi=0$. Their normal is purely temporal, so locally they are constant-time hypersurfaces. Multiplying the equation by a nonzero constant or choosing the opposite sign convention does not change this test.

For the [Laplace equation](../../../../../../laplace-equation.md),

$$
\Delta_xu=0,\qquad p(\xi)=|\xi|^2.
$$

**There are no real [characteristic hypersurfaces](../../../../../../characteristic-hypersurface.md) for the [Laplace equation](../../../../../../laplace-equation.md)**, since their normal cannot be zero. These statements concern the ordinary total-order [principal symbol](../../../../../../principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md), not a weighted space-time grading.
