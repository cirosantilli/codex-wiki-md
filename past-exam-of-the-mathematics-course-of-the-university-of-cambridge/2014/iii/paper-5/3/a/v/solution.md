↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The constant function $v=1$ is an admissible $H^1(U)$ test for the [Neumann Poisson problem](../../../../../../../neumann-poisson-problem.md). Its [weak gradient](../../../../../../../weak-gradient.md) is zero, so the weak identity immediately gives

$$
\boxed{\int_Uf\,dx=0.}
$$

This is necessary, and the preceding Hilbert-space construction proves sufficiency for $f\in L^2(U)$. It is the balance condition corresponding to zero total boundary flux.
