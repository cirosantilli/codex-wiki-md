<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The difference $w$ of two [weak solutions](../../../../../../../weak-solution.md) lies in the [clamped second-order Sobolev space](../../../../../../../clamped-second-order-sobolev-space.md). Testing with $w$ yields $\int_U(\Delta w)^2=0$, so $\Delta w=0$. Since $w\in H_0^1(U)$, [integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\|\nabla w\|_2^2=-\int_Uw\Delta w=0.
$$

The [Poincaré inequality](../../../../../../../poincare-inequality.md) for zero boundary values now implies $\|w\|_2=0$. **The [clamped biharmonic problem](../../../../../../../clamped-biharmonic-problem.md) has at most one [weak solution](../../../../../../../weak-solution.md).** Unlike the [Neumann Poisson problem](../../../../../../../neumann-poisson-problem.md), no additive constant is allowed by these boundary traces.
