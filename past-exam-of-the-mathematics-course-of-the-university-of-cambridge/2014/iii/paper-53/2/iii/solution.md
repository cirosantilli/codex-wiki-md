<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

All dots in this solution denote differentiation with respect to [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md) $\eta$. This is required by comoving $k$, the streaming term $k\Theta_\ell$, and $\dot\tau=-a\bar n_e\sigma_T$. The PDF's reference to proper-time derivatives is inconsistent with these coefficients. Proper-time equations follow by dividing the conformal-time equations by $a$, replacing streaming $k$ by $k/a$ and the collision rate by $-\bar n_e\sigma_T$.

The monopole equation of the [photon Boltzmann hierarchy](../../../../../../photon-boltzmann-hierarchy.md) is $\dot\Theta_0+k\Theta_1/3=\dot\phi$; its collision term vanishes. Consequently the [photon continuity equation](../../../../../../photon-continuity-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dot\delta_\gamma-\frac43kv_\gamma-4\dot\phi=0.}
$$

For the dipole the hierarchy gives

$$
\dot\Theta_1+k\left(\frac25\Theta_2-\Theta_0\right)
=\dot\tau(\Theta_1+v_b)+k\psi.
$$

Using the relations from the preceding part, the [photon Euler equation](../../../../../../photon-euler-equation.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{\dot v_\gamma+\frac k4\delta_\gamma+k\psi+\frac23k\Pi_\gamma
=\dot\tau(v_\gamma-v_b).}
$$

Because $\dot\tau<0$, the collision term drives the photon velocity towards the baryon velocity, as [Thomson scattering](../../../../../../thomson-scattering.md) should.

For pressureless baryons, [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) without energy exchange gives $\boxed{\dot\delta_b-kv_b-3\dot\phi=0}$. At this order [Thomson scattering](../../../../../../thomson-scattering.md) changes momentum but not the local photon energy: energy transfer by the relative bulk motion is second order, and recoil/thermal energy exchange is neglected. Total [momentum conservation](../../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) fixes the baryon force to be the negative of the photon force. The momentum densities are proportional to their enthalpy densities, $4\bar\rho_\gamma/3$ and $\bar\rho_b$. Thus

$$
\frac43\bar\rho_\gamma\,\dot\tau(v_\gamma-v_b)+\bar\rho_b\,\mathcal F_b=0,
\qquad \mathcal F_b=-\frac{\dot\tau}{R}(v_\gamma-v_b),\qquad
\boxed{R=\frac{3\bar\rho_b}{4\bar\rho_\gamma}.}
$$

Including the pressureless expansion and gravitational terms yields the [baryon Euler equation with Thomson drag](../../../../../../baryon-euler-equation-with-thomson-drag.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot v_b+\frac{\dot a}{a}v_b+k\psi=-\frac{\dot\tau}{R}(v_\gamma-v_b).}
$$

The expansion coefficient for photons cancels against the changing background pressure, while pressureless baryons retain the Hubble drag term. The opposite drag signs and the enthalpy ratio ensure that the combined [photon-baryon fluid](../../../../../../photon-baryon-fluid.md) conserves momentum.
