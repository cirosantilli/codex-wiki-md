<h1 id="3/3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $Y_t=B_{\tau+t}-B_\tau$. Because $B_\tau$ is the maximum on $[0,1]$,

$$
Y_t\leq0\qquad(0\leq t\leq1-\tau).
$$

The preceding part makes this a strictly positive random interval with probability one. A standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) cannot have this path property. For each deterministic $h>0$, the [Brownian reflection principle](../../../../../../../reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) gives $\mathbb P(\sup_{0\leq t\leq h}B_t\leq0)=0$. Taking the countable union over $h=1/k$ shows that the probability of staying nonpositive on any initial interval of positive length is zero.

**Therefore $Y$ is not a [Brownian motion](../../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md).** This is the obstruction for [Brownian motion shifted at its finite-horizon maximum](../../../../../../../brownian-motion-shifted-at-its-finite-horizon-maximum.md). It concerns its path law, irrespective of any proposed filtration for the shifted process.
