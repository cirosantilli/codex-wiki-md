<h1 id="2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) $\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ is a $\mathcal G$-measurable integrable [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $Z$ such that

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[Z\mathbf1_A]=\mathbb E[X\mathbf1_A]\quad\text{for every }A\in\mathcal G.}
$$

It is defined up to [almost sure equality](../../../../../../almost-sure-equality.md). Measurability with respect to the sub-[sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) and equality of these integrals are both essential: the first expresses that only the information in $\mathcal G$ is retained, and the second preserves all averages visible through that information. Existence follows from the [Radon-Nikodym theorem](../../../../../../radon-nikodym-theorem.md); uniqueness is up to sets of probability zero.
