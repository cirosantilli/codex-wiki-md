<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

There are exactly two tree [Feynman diagrams](../../../../../../feynman-diagram.md): the incident scalar can be absorbed before the final scalar is emitted, or after it. The internal fermion [four-momenta](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) are respectively $r_s=p+k=q+l$ and $r_u=p-l=q-k$. A scalar exchange diagram would require an absent scalar self-interaction, so there is no additional tree channel.

Define the [S-matrix](../../../../../../s-matrix.md) convention $S_{fi}=\delta_{fi}+i(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(p+k-q-l)T_{s's}$. The two tree [scattering amplitudes](../../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) are

$$
iT^{(s)}_{s's}=\bar u(q,s')(ig\gamma^5)S_F(p+k)(ig\gamma^5)u(p,s),
\qquad
iT^{(u)}_{s's}=\bar u(q,s')(ig\gamma^5)S_F(p-l)(ig\gamma^5)u(p,s).
$$

Using the [Dirac propagator](../../../../../../dirac-propagator.md) from the preceding [Feynman rules](../../../../../../feynman-rule.md),

$$
\boxed{T_{s's}=-g^2\bar u(q,s')\gamma^5\left[
\frac{M-i(\not p+\not k)}{(p+k)^2+M^2-i0}
+\frac{M-i(\not p-\not l)}{(p-l)^2+M^2-i0}
\right]\gamma^5u(p,s)}.
$$

The two terms add with the same relative sign: neither diagram exchanges identical external [fermions](../../../../../../fermion.md). Their interference must be retained when squaring the complete [scattering amplitude](../../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md). In the phase convention above, $\gamma^5(M-i\not r)\gamma^5=-(M+i\not r)$, which gives an equivalent simplified numerator. An overall phase depends on the [S-matrix](../../../../../../s-matrix.md) convention and does not change a [relativistic scattering cross-section](../../../../../../relativistic-scattering-cross-section.md).
