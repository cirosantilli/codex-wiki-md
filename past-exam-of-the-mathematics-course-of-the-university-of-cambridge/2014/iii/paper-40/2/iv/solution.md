<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [Dirac field](../../../../../../dirac-field.md) has the global [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) $\psi\mapsto e^{i\alpha}\psi$, $\bar\psi\mapsto e^{-i\alpha}\bar\psi$, while the real scalar is unchanged. Both the free [Dirac action](../../../../../../dirac-action.md) and the [pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction](../../../../../../pseudoscalar-yukawa-interaction.md) preserve this [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md). Therefore [Dirac fermion number conservation](../../../../../../dirac-fermion-number-conservation.md) holds: its charge counts particles minus [antiparticles](../../../../../../antiparticle.md).

The initial state has charge $+1$, whereas a final [antiparticle](../../../../../../antiparticle.md) and a neutral scalar have charge $-1$. Since the [S-matrix](../../../../../../s-matrix.md) commutes with that charge,

$$
(Q_f-Q_i)\langle f|S|i\rangle=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{T(f\phi\to\bar f\phi)=0,\qquad\sigma=0}.
$$

This holds at every order, not just tree level. In the [Feynman rules](../../../../../../feynman-rule.md), a continuous fermion arrow cannot connect these specified external states. Replacing only the outgoing $\bar u$ by a $v$ in the previous expression would not give a physical amplitude. Changing both external [fermions](../../../../../../fermion.md) to [antiparticles](../../../../../../antiparticle.md) would instead produce an allowed process with reversed fermion flow and the appropriate $v$ spinors; moving a leg between initial and final states is a different operation governed by [crossing symmetry](../../../../../../crossing-symmetry.md).
