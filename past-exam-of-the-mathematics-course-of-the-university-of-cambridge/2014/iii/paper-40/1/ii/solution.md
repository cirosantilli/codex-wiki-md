<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the [four-divergence](../../../../../../four-divergence.md) of the [Proca equation](../../../../../../proca-equation.md). Commuting partial derivatives and using that $F^{ab}$ is an [antisymmetric second-rank tensor](../../../../../../antisymmetric-second-rank-tensor.md) give

$$
\partial_b\partial_aF^{ab}
=\tfrac12\partial_a\partial_b(F^{ab}+F^{ba})=0,
\qquad m^2\partial_bA^b=0.
$$

Consequently, **for $m\ne0$, $\partial_aA^a=0$**. This is the [Lorenz constraint in Proca theory](../../../../../../lorenz-constraint-in-proca-theory.md): an [equation of motion](../../../../../../equation-of-motion.md) enforces it, rather than a choice of [gauge fixing](../../../../../../gauge-fixing.md). It leaves $(\Box-m^2)A^a=0$. At $m=0$ the divergence argument supplies no such constraint; the massless [gauge symmetry](../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md) requires a separate treatment.
