<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose the [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) convention

$$
A^a(x)=\int\frac{d^4q}{(2\pi)^4}\,e^{iq\cdot x}\widehat A^a(q),
\qquad q\cdot x=-q^0t+\boldsymbol q\cdot\boldsymbol x.
$$

Then $\partial_a$ becomes $iq_a$. Applying this to the [Lorenz constraint in Proca theory](../../../../../../lorenz-constraint-in-proca-theory.md) yields

$$
\boxed{q_a\widehat A^a(q)=0}.
$$

Thus every physical [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) mode is orthogonal to its [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) in the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md).
