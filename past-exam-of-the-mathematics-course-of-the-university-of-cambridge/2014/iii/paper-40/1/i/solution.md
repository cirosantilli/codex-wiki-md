<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$, $\hbar=c=1$, and $\Box=\partial_a\partial^a=-\partial_t^2+\nabla^2$. The [Proca field](../../../../../../proca-field.md) has nonzero mass $m$. Vary its [action](../../../../../../action.md), using the fact that the [gauge field strength](../../../../../../gauge-field-strength.md) is an [antisymmetric second-rank tensor](../../../../../../antisymmetric-second-rank-tensor.md):

$$
\delta S=\int d^4x\left[-F^{ab}\partial_a\delta A_b-m^2A^b\delta A_b\right]
=\int d^4x\left(\partial_aF^{ab}-m^2A^b\right)\delta A_b.
$$

The [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) discards a boundary term, with the variation fixed at the boundary. Thus the [Euler-Lagrange field equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is the [Proca equation](../../../../../../proca-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\partial_aF^{ab}-m^2A^b=0},\qquad
(\Box-m^2)A^b-\partial^b(\partial_aA^a)=0.
$$

The minus sign on $m^2$ is required by the chosen [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md); after imposing the constraint below, the [plane waves](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) have positive [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) $E^2=|\boldsymbol q|^2+m^2$.
