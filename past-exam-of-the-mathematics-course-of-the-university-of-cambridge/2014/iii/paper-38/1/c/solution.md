<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Predictability means $K_s$ is $\mathcal F_{s-1}$-measurable. If $|K_s|\leq L$, each product $K_s(M_s-M_{s-1})$ is [integrable](../../../../../../integrability.md), and the finite sum defining $Y_t$ is [integrable](../../../../../../integrability.md). Pulling the bounded [predictable](../../../../../../predictable-process.md) factor out of the [conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E[Y_t-Y_{t-1}\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]
=K_t\mathbb E[M_t-M_{t-1}\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]=0.
$$

Therefore **the bounded [predictable](../../../../../../predictable-process.md) [martingale transform](../../../../../../martingale-transform.md) $Y$ is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) starting at zero**.
