<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The stopped processes are nonnegative [martingales](../../../../../../martingale-split.md). Their [expectations](../../../../../../expected-value.md) equal the finite deterministic value $X_0$. The [Fatou lemma](../../../../../../fatou-s-lemma.md) gives, for each fixed integer $t$,

$$
\mathbb E X_t\leq\liminf_j\mathbb E X_{t\wedge\tau_j}=X_0<\infty.
$$

Thus every $X_t$ is [integrable](../../../../../../integrability.md). Apply the preceding discrete-time criterion to conclude **$X$ is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md)**, not merely a [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md). This is the [nonnegative discrete-time local martingale is a martingale](../../../../../../nonnegative-discrete-time-local-martingale-is-a-martingale.md) result; its conclusion does not extend to arbitrary continuous-time nonnegative [local martingales](../../../../../../local-martingale.md).
