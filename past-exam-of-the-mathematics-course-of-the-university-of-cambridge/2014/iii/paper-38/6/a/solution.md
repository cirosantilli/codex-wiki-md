<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The mark-to-market value of holdings $H_t$ in the asset-price vector is the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) $X_t=H_t\cdot P_t$. A [self-financing strategy](../../../../../../self-financing-portfolio.md) pays for every rebalancing from within the [portfolio](../../../../../../investment-portfolio.md). Over a short interval, the gains on the currently held assets are $H_t\cdot dP_t$, while [consumption](../../../../../../consumption.md) removes $c_tdt$ units of wealth. Therefore

$$
\boxed{X_t=H_t\cdot P_t,\qquad dX_t=H_t\cdot dP_t-c_tdt.}
$$

The holdings are [predictable](../../../../../../predictable-process.md) and [integrable](../../../../../../integrability.md) against the price [semimartingale](../../../../../../semimartingale.md); the [consumption](../../../../../../consumption.md) rate is nonnegative and suitably measurable. This is the continuous-time self-financing-with-consumption convention.
