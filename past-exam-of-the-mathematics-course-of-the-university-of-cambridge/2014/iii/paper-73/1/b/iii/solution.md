<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\zeta=6\pi\mu a$, $\epsilon=a/R$ and define the dimensionless leading cross-mobility tensor

$$
\mathsf M=\frac{3a}{4R}(I+\mathbf n\mathbf n).
$$

For a given applied [force](../../../../../../../force.md), the outgoing [Stokeslet](../../../../../../../stokeslet.md) is fixed by that [force](../../../../../../../force.md), regardless of the sphere's entrainment [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md). At the other center the [Faxén translation law](../../../../../../../faxen-s-first-law.md) gives its force-only [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md) plus the incident [Stokeslet](../../../../../../../stokeslet.md) [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md). The finite-size dipole and the Laplacian Faxén correction first contribute $O(\epsilon^3|\mathbf V|)$; an emitted [stresslet](../../../../../../../force-dipole-flow.md) reflection is still smaller at this order. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf U_1=\mathbf U_2=(I+\mathsf M)\mathbf V+O(\epsilon^3|\mathbf V|).}
$$

There is no second-order translational term for prescribed equal forces.

The [vorticity of translating-sphere Stokes flow](../../../../../../../vorticity-of-translating-sphere-stokes-flow.md) is $\boldsymbol\omega=3a\mathbf V\times\mathbf r/(2r^3)$; its potential dipole has zero curl. Apply the torque-free [Faxén rotation law](../../../../../../../faxen-s-rotational-law.md) to the other sphere's field, using $\mathbf r=-\mathbf R$ at sphere 1 and $\mathbf r=\mathbf R$ at sphere 2. This yields

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol\Omega_1=\frac{3a}{4R^2}\mathbf n\times\mathbf V,
\qquad\boldsymbol\Omega_2=-\frac{3a}{4R^2}\mathbf n\times\mathbf V,}
$$

through second order in the dimensionless spin $a\boldsymbol\Omega/|\mathbf V|$. Neglected spin terms are higher order. This is the [method of reflections for Stokes flow](../../../../../../../method-of-reflections-for-stokes-flow.md), with the ambient field at each application excluding that sphere's own disturbance.
