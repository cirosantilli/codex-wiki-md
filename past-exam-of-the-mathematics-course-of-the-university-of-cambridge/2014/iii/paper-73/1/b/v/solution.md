<h1 id="1/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Sphere 2 is now [force-free](../../../../../../../force-free.md) and [torque-free](../../../../../../../torque-free.md). Its translation and spin follow the incident rigid-motion components, so it emits neither a [Stokeslet](../../../../../../../stokeslet.md) nor a [rotlet](../../../../../../../rotlet.md). Its first disturbance is a [stresslet](../../../../../../../force-dipole-flow.md) from the incident rate of strain, of magnitude $\mu a^3(a|\mathbf V|/R^2)$. Its return [velocity](../../../../../../../velocity.md) at sphere 1 is consequently $O(a^4|\mathbf V|/R^4)$:

$$
\boxed{|\mathbf U_1-\mathbf V|=O(|\mathbf V|\epsilon^4).}
$$

More precisely, that leading correction is $-(15/4)\epsilon^4(\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf n)\mathbf n$. It vanishes for purely transverse forcing, for which the first translational correction is of order $\epsilon^6$.

The first-return spin requires an additional cancellation. The leading incident strain at sphere 2 is

$$
\mathbf E=\frac{3a}{4R^2}(\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf n)(I-3\mathbf n\mathbf n).
$$

It is axisymmetric about the line of centers, so the reflected [stresslet](../../../../../../../force-dipole-flow.md) has zero [vorticity](../../../../../../../vorticity.md) at sphere 1 on that axis. A naive [vorticity](../../../../../../../vorticity.md) estimate of order $(|\mathbf V|/a)\epsilon^5$ therefore has zero coefficient. The finite-size correction to the incident strain and the next force-free scattering multipoles are two powers of $\epsilon$ smaller; these generically give

$$
\boxed{|\boldsymbol\Omega_1|=O\left(\frac{|\mathbf V|}{a}\epsilon^7\right).}
$$

This [suppressed spin from a force-free distant sphere](../../../../../../../suppressed-spin-from-a-force-free-distant-sphere.md) is also consistent with the reciprocal torque-to-self-translation coupling. Purely longitudinal forcing gives zero spin exactly by axial symmetry. Distinguishing the [stresslet](../../../../../../../force-dipole-flow.md)'s on-axis curl from its generic off-axis scale is essential here.
