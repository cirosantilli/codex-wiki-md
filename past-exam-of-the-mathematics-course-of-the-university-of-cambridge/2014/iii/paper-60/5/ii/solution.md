<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../subadditivity-of-von-neumann-entropy.md) applied to $RE$ gives $S(RE)\leq S(R)+S(E)$. Together with the environment expression for [coherent information](../../../../../../coherent-information.md),

$$
I_c(\Lambda_1,\rho)=S(RE)-S(E)\leq S(R).
$$

The channel does not act on $R$. Its reduced state has the same nonzero eigenvalues as the original input $\rho$, because $RA$ initially purifies that input. Hence

$$
\boxed{I_c(\Lambda_1,\rho)\leq S(\rho).}
$$

More precisely, $I_c=S(\rho)-I(R:E)$: the [coherent information upper bound by input entropy](../../../../../../coherent-information-upper-bound-by-input-entropy.md) is a consequence of nonnegative [quantum mutual information](../../../../../../quantum-mutual-information.md) with the environment. Equality holds when $R$ and $E$ are uncorrelated.
