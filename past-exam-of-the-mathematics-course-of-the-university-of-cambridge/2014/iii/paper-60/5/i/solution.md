<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a [Stinespring dilation](../../../../../../stinespring-dilation.md) $V:A\longrightarrow B_1E$ of the [quantum channel](../../../../../../quantum-channel.md). Applying it to the input purification gives the pure vector

$$
|\Omega\rangle_{RB_1E}=(I_R\otimes V)|\psi_{RA}^\rho\rangle.
$$

Its $RB_1$ marginal is the output state in the definition of [coherent information](../../../../../../coherent-information.md). Complementary subsystems of a pure state have the same nonzero eigenvalues, by the [Schmidt decomposition](../../../../../../schmidt-decomposition.md). Thus $S(B_1)=S(RE)$ and $S(RB_1)=S(E)$. It follows that

$$
\boxed{I_c(\Lambda_1,\rho)=S(RE)-S(E)=S(R\mid E).}
$$

Here $S(R\mid E)$ denotes [quantum conditional entropy](../../../../../../quantum-conditional-entropy.md), evaluated on the complementary output $RE$. This is [coherent information as an environment conditional entropy](../../../../../../coherent-information-as-an-environment-conditional-entropy.md). Equivalently $I_c=-S(R\mid B_1)$; the environment expression has the positive sign, while the receiving-system expression has the negative sign.
