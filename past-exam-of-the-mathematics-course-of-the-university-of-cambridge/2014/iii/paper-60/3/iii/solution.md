<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $q_y=\langle y|\rho|y\rangle$. Positivity and normalization of the [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) imply $q_y\geq0$ and $\sum_yq_y=1$. The [completely dephasing channel](../../../../../../rank-one-dephasing.md) produces

$$
\sigma=\sum_yq_y|y\rangle\langle y|,
$$

which is already a [spectral decomposition](../../../../../../spectral-decomposition.md). Its [Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{S(\sigma)=-\sum_yq_y\log_2q_y=H(q).}
$$

The quantum output entropy is exactly the [Shannon entropy](../../../../../../information-entropy.md) of the basis-measurement probabilities, including possible zero eigenvalues via $0\log_2 0=0$.
