<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Sum the [spectral filtering of Hamiltonian terms](../../../../../../spectral-filtering-of-hamiltonian-terms.md) over the original finite decomposition. The total [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) commutes with its own evolution, so

$$
\sum_ZA^{(Z)}=\int w(t)e^{itH}\left(\sum_Zh_Z\right)e^{-itH}dt=H\int w(t)dt=H.
$$

The absence of all ground-to-excited [matrix elements](../../../../../../matrix-element.md), proved in part (a), makes each filtered term block diagonal relative to $P_0=|\phi_0\rangle\langle\phi_0|$ and its orthogonal complement. Therefore

$$
\boxed{H=\sum_ZA^{(Z)},\qquad[A^{(Z)},P_0]=0.}
$$

A filtered term may act on the whole system, since [unitary time evolution](../../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md) spreads its original support. Neither commutation with $P_0$ nor preservation of its ground-state expectation asserts that the global [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) minimizes each individual term. In particular, this construction does not generally turn a frustrated decomposition into a [frustration-free Hamiltonian](../../../../../../frustration-free-quantum-hamiltonian.md).
