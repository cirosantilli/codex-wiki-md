<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $U(t)=e^{-itH}$. Define the second initial state by $|\widetilde\psi_0\rangle=U(t)^\dagger|\psi_2\rangle$, while $|\psi_0\rangle=U(t)^\dagger|\psi_1\rangle$. The [unitary time evolution](../../../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md) preserves their [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product.md):

$$
\boxed{\langle\widetilde\psi_0|\psi_0\rangle=\langle\psi_2|UU^\dagger|\psi_1\rangle=0.}
$$

Both initial states are normalized and evolve to the chosen final pair. This supplies the orthogonal partner needed for [topological quantum order](../../../../../../../topological-quantum-order.md). The remaining requirement is [local indistinguishability](../../../../../../../local-indistinguishability.md), which is proved in the next condition's solution.
