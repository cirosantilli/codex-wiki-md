<h1 id="1/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume the [Continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../../continuum-hypothesis.md) and enumerate $\mathbb R=\{r_\alpha:\alpha<\omega_1\}$. Define

$$
f(r_\alpha)=\{r_\beta:\beta\le\alpha\}.
$$

Every value is countable. For any two indices, say $\alpha\le\beta$, one has $r_\alpha\in f(r_\beta)$, so the two required nonmembership conditions cannot both hold. Including the endpoint in each initial segment also rules out taking the two points equal. Therefore the free-pair assertion implies the negation of [Continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../../continuum-hypothesis.md). [Cantor theorem](../../../../../../../cantor-s-theorem.md) and choice already give $2^{\aleph_0}\ge\aleph_1$, so

$$
\boxed{A_{<\aleph_1}(\mathbb R)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ 2^{\aleph_0}>\aleph_1\quad\text{in ZFC}.}
$$
