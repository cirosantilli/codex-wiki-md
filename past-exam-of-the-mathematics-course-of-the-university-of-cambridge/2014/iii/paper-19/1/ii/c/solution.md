<h1 id="1/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Gödel constructible universe theorem](../../../../../../../constructible-universe-theorem.md) gives $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})\Rightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{CH})$. The [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) independence theorem for the [Continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../../continuum-hypothesis.md) gives $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})\Rightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}+\neg\mathrm{CH})$. For example, the latter can be obtained by first passing to the [constructible universe](../../../../../../../constructible-universe.md) and then adding sufficiently many Cohen reals. By the equivalence in part (b), these are respectively models of the negation and affirmation of the free-pair assertion. Thus **if [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) is consistent, the assertion is independent of [ZFC](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md)**. The consistency qualification is essential: an inconsistent theory proves every sentence. These are syntactic relative-consistency implications, not a claim that bare consistency supplies a [countable transitive model](../../../../../../../countable-transitive-model.md).
