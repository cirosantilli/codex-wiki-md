<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [axiom of infinity](../../../../../../axiom-of-infinity.md) excludes $V_\omega$ and all smaller stages, so the [cardinal](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\lambda$ is uncountable. [Full semantics for second-order logic](../../../../../../full-semantics-for-second-order-logic.md) is decisive: every externally specified functional relation whose [ordered pairs](../../../../../../ordered-pair.md) lie in $V_\lambda$ is an allowed class parameter, even if the relation itself is not an element of $V_\lambda$.

If $\operatorname{cf}(\lambda)=\mu<\lambda$, choose an external cofinal [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $h:\mu\to\lambda$. Its graph is an allowed class parameter: each input, output and [ordered pair](../../../../../../ordered-pair.md) has [rank of a set](../../../../../../rank-of-a-set.md) below the infinite [cardinal](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\lambda$, which is a [limit ordinal](../../../../../../limit-ordinal.md). The domain $\mu$ belongs to $V_\lambda$. The second-order form of [Axiom schema of replacement](../../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) would give its range as an element of $V_\lambda$. But a cofinal [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of $\lambda$ has [rank of a set](../../../../../../rank-of-a-set.md) $\lambda$ and cannot belong to $V_\lambda$. Therefore $\operatorname{cf}(\lambda)=\lambda$.

If $2^\theta\ge\lambda$ for a [cardinal](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\theta<\lambda$, choice supplies an external [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) $q:\mathcal P(\theta)\to\lambda$. The domain belongs to $V_\lambda$ and the [ordered pairs](../../../../../../ordered-pair.md) of its graph have ranks below $\lambda$. Applying [Axiom schema of replacement](../../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) to this external functional class would put its range, the [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\lambda$, in $V_\lambda$, again impossible. Hence $2^\theta<\lambda$ for every $\theta<\lambda$.

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\lambda>\aleph_0,\qquad \operatorname{cf}(\lambda)=\lambda,\qquad
\forall\theta<\lambda\ (2^\theta<\lambda).}
$$

These are exactly the defining conditions for a [strongly inaccessible cardinal](../../../../../../strongly-inaccessible-cardinal.md). This [full second-order replacement rank obstruction](../../../../../../full-second-order-replacement-rank-obstruction.md) would not follow from [Henkin semantics](../../../../../../henkin-semantics.md), where the available class relations may exclude the external [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) just used.
