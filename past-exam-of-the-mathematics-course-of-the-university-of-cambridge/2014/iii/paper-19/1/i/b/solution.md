↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [Sierpiński set](../../../../../../../sierpinski-set.md) is an uncountable [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) $S$ of $\mathbb R$ such that $S\cap N$ is countable for every [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) $N$ of [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md) zero. Equivalently it is enough to test [Borel null sets](../../../../../../../borel-null-set.md), because every [null set](../../../../../../../null-set.md) is contained in a Borel [null set](../../../../../../../null-set.md). **Uncountability and countable intersection with every [null set](../../../../../../../null-set.md) are both required.** This is the measure analogue of the category-based [Luzin set](../../../../../../../luzin-set.md) condition; it does not say that $S$ itself is measurable.
