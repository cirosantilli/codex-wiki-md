**A [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) $G\subseteq\mathbb P$ is a [generic filter](../../../../../../../generic-filter.md) over $M$ if it is nonempty, is closed towards weaker conditions, is directed towards stronger common extensions, and meets every dense [subset](../../../../../../../subset.md) $D\subseteq\mathbb P$ belonging to $M$.** Density means that every condition has a stronger extension in $D$. Genericity is relative to $M$, not to every [dense subset of a forcing order](../../../../../../../dense-subset-of-a-forcing-order.md) in the ambient universe. Under the printed weaker-first convention in Question 5, closure is towards smaller conditions and directedness is towards larger ones; in the standard stronger-first notation these inequalities reverse.
