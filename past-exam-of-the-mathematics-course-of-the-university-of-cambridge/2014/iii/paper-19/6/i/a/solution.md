<h1 id="6/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) [collapses a cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-collapse.md) $\kappa$ over $M$ if it forces that the ground [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\kappa$ is no longer a [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md): in the extension there is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) from some smaller [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) onto $\kappa$. Equivalently there is a [surjection](../../../../../../../surjective-function.md) onto $\kappa$ from an [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) whose ground [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) is smaller. [Forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) preserves the [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) itself; **collapse changes [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md), not the identity of the [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md)**. If the assertion is about one particular [generic extension](../../../../../../../generic-extension.md), the witnessing collapse is interpreted there; a forcing-wide assertion means it is forced by the weakest condition.
