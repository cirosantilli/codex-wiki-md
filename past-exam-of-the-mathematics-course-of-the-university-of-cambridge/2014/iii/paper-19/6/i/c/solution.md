The [Delta-system lemma](../../../../../../../delta-system-lemma.md) states that every uncountable family of finite [sets](../../../../../../../set-split.md) contains an uncountable subfamily $\mathcal D$ and a fixed finite root $r$ such that

$$
\boxed{A\cap B=r\quad\text{for all distinct }A,B\in\mathcal D.}
$$

At a regular uncountable [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$, a family of $\kappa$ finite [sets](../../../../../../../set-split.md) has a [Delta-system](../../../../../../../delta-system.md) subfamily of size $\kappa$. The root may be empty. Neither version asserts that all [sets](../../../../../../../set-split.md) in the original family have the same intersection.
