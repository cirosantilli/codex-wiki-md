<h1 id="6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Start with a model of [ZFC](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md)+$V=L$, which also satisfies [Generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md), and add one Cohen real by the countable [forcing](../../../../../../forcing-split.md) of finite binary sequences. Its [countable chain condition for forcing](../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) preserves [cardinals](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md). For every infinite ground [cardinal](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) $\lambda$, a nice [forcing name](../../../../../../forcing-name.md) for a [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of $\lambda$ is specified by $\lambda$ countable [forcing antichains](../../../../../../forcing-antichain.md) of a countable [forcing](../../../../../../forcing-split.md). The number of such [forcing names](../../../../../../forcing-name.md) is at most

$$
(2^{\aleph_0})^\lambda\le(2^\lambda)^\lambda=2^\lambda=\lambda^+
$$

in the ground model. The ground [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) already supply the preserved lower bound $\lambda^+$. Thus the extension still satisfies $2^\lambda=\lambda^+$ for every infinite [cardinal](../../../../../../cardinal-number.md), namely [Generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md).

The Cohen real is not in the ground model: for each ground real it is dense to disagree at a new coordinate. [Forcing](../../../../../../forcing-split.md) leaves the [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal.md) unchanged, and constructible levels are absolute, so the extension has the same [constructible universe](../../../../../../constructible-universe.md) as the ground model. The new real is therefore not constructible. We obtain

$$
\boxed{M[G]\models\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{GCH}+V\ne L.}
$$

The [forcing theorem](../../../../../../forcing-theorem.md) makes this a relative-consistency construction. Thus, if [ZFC](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) is consistent, [ZFC](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md)+[Generalized continuum hypothesis](../../../../../../generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) does not prove $V=L$. This is the [one-Cohen-real preservation of GCH](../../../../../../one-cohen-real-preservation-of-gch.md) argument, not a claim that every arbitrary GCH-preserving [forcing](../../../../../../forcing-split.md) leaves $V=L$ unchanged.
