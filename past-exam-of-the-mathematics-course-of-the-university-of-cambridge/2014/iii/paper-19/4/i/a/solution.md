The [partition relation](../../../../../../../partition-relation.md) $\kappa\to(\lambda)^\alpha_\beta$ means that for every coloring $c:[\kappa]^\alpha\to\beta$, there is $H\subseteq\kappa$ with $|H|=\lambda$ such that $c$ is constant on $[H]^\alpha$. Here $[X]^\alpha$ denotes the [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md) of $X$ of [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) $\alpha$, and the subscript is the number of colors. **The domain consists of [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md) of size $\alpha$, not ordered tuples**, and the definition also makes sense for infinite arity.
