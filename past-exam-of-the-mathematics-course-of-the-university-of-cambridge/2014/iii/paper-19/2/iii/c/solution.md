<h1 id="2/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $N$ be any transitive class model of [ZF](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) containing every [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md). By part (b), all its internally computed constructible levels are the actual levels, and each belongs to, and is contained in, $N$. Their union therefore gives $L\subseteq N$. Using the permitted fact that $L\models\mathrm{ZF}$, and that $L$ itself contains every [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md), we conclude

$$
\boxed{L\text{ is the smallest transitive class model of ZF containing all ordinals}.}
$$

The word class matters: a [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) cannot contain every [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md). Set-sized standard models instead contain their own truncated [constructible universe](../../../../../../../constructible-universe.md) as described in part (b).
