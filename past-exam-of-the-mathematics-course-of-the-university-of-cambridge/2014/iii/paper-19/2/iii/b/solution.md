<h1 id="2/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the transitive-model convention for “standard” in this assertion: the domain is a [transitive set](../../../../../../../transitive-set.md) and membership is actual membership. A [standard membership model of set theory](../../../../../../../standard-membership-model-of-set-theory.md) without transitivity is a weaker notion and would not justify the asserted absoluteness. Ordinalhood is bounded-formula absolute, so the internal [ordinals](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) are precisely $A\cap\operatorname{Ord}$. [Satisfaction for a set structure](../../../../../../../satisfaction-for-a-set-structure.md) is absolute between transitive [ZF](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) models containing that structure: the domain, finite parameter tuples, formula codes and the recursive truth clauses are the same. Equivalently one may use the $\Delta_1$ absoluteness of the predicate defining $L_\alpha$.

Transfinite induction now gives $L_\alpha^{\mathbb A}=L_\alpha$ for each [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\alpha\in A$. At successors both computations use the same definable [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md) of the same [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) structure; at limits they take the same union over all smaller [ordinals](../../../../../../../ordinal.md), which belong to $A$ by transitivity. Thus

$$
\boxed{L^{\mathbb A}=\bigcup_{\alpha\in A\cap\operatorname{Ord}}L_\alpha.}
$$

For a set-sized model of [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) height $\theta$, this is $L_\theta$. The restriction to actual [ordinals](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) makes the printed union precise. Mere well-foundedness of a nontransitive membership substructure would not justify these absoluteness steps; transitivity is the intended standard-model convention.
