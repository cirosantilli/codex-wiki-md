<h1 id="2/i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The predicate is defined by the [constructible hierarchy](../../../../../../../constructible-hierarchy.md):

$$
\boxed{L_0=\varnothing,\qquad L_{\alpha+1}=\operatorname{Def}(L_\alpha),\qquad
L_\delta=\bigcup_{\alpha<\delta}L_\alpha\quad(\delta\text{ limit}).}
$$

Here $\operatorname{Def}(A)$ consists of [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md) of $A$ definable in the [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) structure $(A,\in)$ by a [first-order formula](../../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) with finitely many parameters from $A$. To express $x\in L_\alpha$ in the language of [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) theory, assert that $\alpha$ is an [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) and there is a hierarchy history of length $\alpha+1$ satisfying this recursion whose last stage contains $x$. Formulas are coded by [natural numbers](../../../../../../../natural-number.md) and truth is the definable [satisfaction for a set structure](../../../../../../../satisfaction-for-a-set-structure.md). [Transfinite recursion](../../../../../../../transfinite-recursion.md) gives a unique history. The standard coding makes this predicate $\Delta_1$ over [ZF](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md); thus the [constructible-level absoluteness over ZF](../../../../../../../constructible-level-absoluteness-over-zf.md) applies to [transitive models](../../../../../../../transitive-model.md) of [ZF](../../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md).
