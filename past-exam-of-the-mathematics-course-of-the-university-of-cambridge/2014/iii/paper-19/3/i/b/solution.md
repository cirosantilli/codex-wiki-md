↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Martin's axiom](../../../../../../../martin-s-axiom.md) at $\kappa$, denoted $\mathrm{MA}_\kappa$, says that whenever a [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) order satisfies the [countable chain condition for forcing](../../../../../../../countable-chain-condition-for-forcing.md) and $\{D_\xi:\xi<\kappa\}$ is a family of dense [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md), there is a [filter in an ordered set](../../../../../../../filter-mathematics.md) meeting every $D_\xi$. Equivalently, allow any family of at most $\kappa$ dense [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md). The [filter in an ordered set](../../../../../../../filter-mathematics.md) is directed towards stronger common extensions and closed towards weaker conditions. **Full [Martin's axiom](../../../../../../../martin-s-axiom.md) requires $\mathrm{MA}_\kappa$ for every $\kappa<2^{\aleph_0}$.** The bound below the continuum is part of the usual full axiom, explaining its role in part (iv)(b).
