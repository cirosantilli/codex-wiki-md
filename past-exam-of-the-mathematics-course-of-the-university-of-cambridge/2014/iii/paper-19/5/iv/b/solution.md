<h1 id="5/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every infinite $\alpha<\kappa$, the generic union at coordinate $\alpha$ gives a [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $g_\alpha:\omega\to\alpha$. The requirement to assign $(\alpha,n)$ is dense for each $n$, and the requirement to use any specified value $\beta<\alpha$ is dense by assigning it at a fresh natural-number position. Therefore $g_\alpha$ is a [surjection](../../../../../../../surjective-function.md), and every [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) below $\kappa$ becomes countable.

The $\kappa$-chain condition preserves the regularity of $\kappa$. For a direct verification, a [forcing name](../../../../../../../forcing-name.md) for a [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) from some $\mu<\kappa$ into $\kappa$ has fewer than $\kappa$ possible values at each coordinate, using a maximal deciding [forcing antichain](../../../../../../../forcing-antichain.md). The union of these possible-value [sets](../../../../../../../set-split.md) has size less than $\kappa$ by regularity and is bounded in $\kappa$. No such [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) can be cofinal. In particular $\kappa$ remains uncountable, while every smaller [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) is countable. Hence

$$
\boxed{\kappa=\aleph_1^{M[H]}.}
$$

This is the [finite Lévy collapse to omega-one](../../../../../../../finite-levy-collapse-to-omega-one.md).
