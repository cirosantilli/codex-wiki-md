<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $x=\tau_G$ be any [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) in the extension. The [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $D=\{\sigma:\exists p\ ((\sigma,p)\in\tau)\}$ belongs to $M$. Ground-model choice [well-orders](../../../../../../well-order.md) it, so fix an enumeration $\langle\sigma_\xi:\xi<\theta\rangle$ by an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) in $M$. In $M[G]$, take

$$
I=\{\xi<\theta:\exists p\in G\ ((\sigma_\xi,p)\in\tau)\}.
$$

Evaluation gives a [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) $\xi\mapsto(\sigma_\xi)_G$ from $I$ onto $x$. This is a [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) by the [ZF](../../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) part of the [forcing theorem](../../../../../../forcing-theorem.md); no extension choice is needed. For each $y\in x$, take its least preimage [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md). Distinct $y$ have distinct least preimages, which [well-order](../../../../../../well-order.md) $x$ by their inherited [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) order. Since every extension [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) has a [forcing name](../../../../../../forcing-name.md), every [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) in $M[G]$ is well-orderable. Therefore

$$
\boxed{M[G]\models\mathrm{AC}.}
$$

This [choice preservation by well-ordered names](../../../../../../choice-preservation-by-well-ordered-names.md) tolerates repeated evaluations, because the least-index step removes them canonically.
