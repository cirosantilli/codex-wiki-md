<h1 id="5/i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) [preserves cofinalities](../../../../../../../cofinality-preserving-forcing.md) over $M$ if every [generic extension](../../../../../../../generic-extension.md) has

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{cf}^{M[G]}(\alpha)=\operatorname{cf}^{M}(\alpha)
\quad\text{for every ordinal }\alpha\in M.}
$$

The two values are compared as [ordinals](../../../../../../../ordinal.md), using preservation of [ordinals](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) by [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md). Equivalently the [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) asserts that no ground [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) acquires a smaller [cofinality](../../../../../../../cofinality.md). The assertion concerns all [ordinal](../../../../../../../ordinal.md) [cofinalities](../../../../../../../cofinality.md), not just that one specified [cardinal](../../../../../../../cardinal-number.md) remains uncountable.
