<h1 id="5/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [forcing name](../../../../../../../forcing-name.md) is built by well-founded recursion: every member of a $\mathbb P$-name $\tau$ is a pair $(\sigma,p)$ with $p\in\mathbb P$ and $\sigma$ itself a $\mathbb P$-name of lower [forcing name rank](../../../../../../../forcing-name-rank.md). In the ground model all such [forcing names](../../../../../../../forcing-name.md) form the recursively defined class $V^{\mathbb P}$. Its evaluation by a [generic filter](../../../../../../../generic-filter.md) is

$$
\boxed{\tau_G=\{\sigma_G:\exists p\in G\ ((\sigma,p)\in\tau)\}.}
$$

The recursion is on [forcing name rank](../../../../../../../forcing-name-rank.md), not on the [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) order. [Forcing names](../../../../../../../forcing-name.md) need not have a unique evaluation across different generics, and many different [forcing names](../../../../../../../forcing-name.md) may have the same evaluation.
