The [forcing truth lemma](../../../../../../../forcing-truth-lemma.md) says that for a formula $\varphi$ and ground-model [forcing names](../../../../../../../forcing-name.md) $\tau_1,\ldots,\tau_n$,

$$
\boxed{M[G]\models\varphi((\tau_1)_G,\ldots,(\tau_n)_G)
\ \Longleftrightarrow\
\exists p\in G\quad M\models p\Vdash\varphi(\tau_1,\ldots,\tau_n).}
$$

The [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) relation is the ground-model relation furnished by the [forcing theorem](../../../../../../../forcing-theorem.md). The witnessing condition must belong to the particular [generic filter](../../../../../../../generic-filter.md), not merely to the [forcing](../../../../../../../forcing-split.md) order.
