<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A type $(0,s)$ [covariant tensor](../../../../../../../covariant-tensor.md) is a multilinear form on $s$ vectors. For arbitrary $V_1,\ldots,V_s\in T_p\mathcal M$, the [pullback of a covariant tensor](../../../../../../../pullback-of-a-covariant-tensor.md) is

$$
(\phi^*\omega)(V_1,\ldots,V_s)
=\omega(d\phi V_1,\ldots,d\phi V_s).
$$

Projection of all covariant slots means $(P\omega)(X_1,\ldots,X_s)=\omega(PX_1,\ldots,PX_s)$. Since every pushed-forward vector is already tangential,

$$
\begin{aligned}
[\phi^*(P\omega)](V_1,\ldots,V_s)
&=\omega(Pd\phi V_1,\ldots,Pd\phi V_s)\\
&=\omega(d\phi V_1,\ldots,d\phi V_s).
\end{aligned}
$$

Equality on all arguments proves **$\phi^*\omega=\phi^*(P\omega)$**. No antisymmetry is needed: this holds for every [covariant tensor](../../../../../../../covariant-tensor.md), not only for [differential forms](../../../../../../../differential-form-split.md).
