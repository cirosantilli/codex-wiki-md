<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [pushforward of a contravariant tensor](../../../../../../../pushforward-of-a-contravariant-tensor.md) applies $d\phi_p$ to each of its $r$ vector slots. Choose a basis $e_i$ of $T_p\mathcal M$ and expand

$$
T=T^{i_1\cdots i_r}e_{i_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes e_{i_r}.
$$

Its pushforward is $T^{i_1\cdots i_r}(d\phi e_{i_1})\otimes\cdots\otimes(d\phi e_{i_r})$. Each factor is tangent to the [hypersurface](../../../../../../../hypersurface.md) and fixed by $P$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{P^{\otimes r}(\phi_*T)=\phi_*T.}
$$

The basis expansion proves the result for arbitrary tensors, rather than only a single decomposable [tensor product](../../../../../../../tensor-product.md).
