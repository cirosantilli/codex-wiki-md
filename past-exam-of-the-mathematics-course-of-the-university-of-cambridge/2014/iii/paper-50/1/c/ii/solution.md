<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose the outward [normal vector](../../../../../../../normal-vector.md) $n=(\cos\varphi,\sin\varphi,0)$ and the positive convention

$$
K_{ij}=e_i^ae_j^b\nabla_a n_b=e_j\cdot\partial_i n.
$$

The ambient connection vanishes in Cartesian coordinates. Since $\partial_\varphi n=e_\varphi/\rho$ and $\partial_{\widehat z}n=0$, the [extrinsic curvature](../../../../../../../extrinsic-curvature.md) components are $K_{\varphi\varphi}=\rho$, $K_{\varphi\widehat z}=K_{\widehat z\widehat z}=0$. Taking the trace using the [induced metric](../../../../../../../induced-metric.md),

$$
\boxed{K=\gamma^{ij}K_{ij}=\frac1\rho.}
$$

**Reversing the normal or using the negative extrinsic-curvature convention gives $-1/\rho$**. The two [principal curvatures](../../../../../../../principal-curvature.md) are $1/\rho$ and zero in the chosen convention; the averaged [mean curvature](../../../../../../../mean-curvature.md) would be $K/2$, so it must not be confused with the requested trace.
