<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The two coordinate tangent vectors of the embedding are

$$
e_\varphi=(-\rho\sin\varphi,\rho\cos\varphi,0),\qquad e_{\widehat z}=(0,0,1).
$$

Their Euclidean inner products give the [pullback of a Riemannian metric](../../../../../../../pullback-of-a-riemannian-metric.md), equivalently the [induced metric](../../../../../../../induced-metric.md):

$$
\boxed{\phi^*g=\rho^2d\varphi^2+d\widehat z^2,\qquad
\gamma_{ij}=\begin{pmatrix}\rho^2&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Locally set $u=\rho\varphi$. The line element becomes $du^2+d\widehat z^2$, so **the induced [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) vanishes identically**. This is the [intrinsic flatness of a circular cylinder](../../../../../../../intrinsic-flatness-of-a-circular-cylinder.md). Its bending in the ambient space is [extrinsic curvature](../../../../../../../extrinsic-curvature.md), not intrinsic curvature; periodicity of the angular coordinate does not change the local flatness.
