<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [smooth map between manifolds](../../../../../../smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) $\phi:\mathcal M\to\mathcal N$, the four constructions go in the directions dictated by composition and the [differential of a smooth map](../../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md).

The [pullback of a smooth function](../../../../../../pullback-of-a-smooth-function.md) $f\in C^\infty(\mathcal N)$ is **$\phi^*f=f\circ\phi$**, a function on $\mathcal M$. The [pushforward of a curve](../../../../../../pushforward-of-a-curve.md) $\lambda:I\to\mathcal M$ is **$\phi_*\lambda=\phi\circ\lambda$**, a curve in $\mathcal N$ with the same parameter interval.

For a [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) $V\in T_p\mathcal M$, its pushforward is **$\phi_*V=d\phi_p(V)\in T_{\phi(p)}\mathcal N$**. Intrinsically, treating a [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) as a derivation on functions,

$$
(\phi_*V)[f]=V[f\circ\phi].
$$

For a [covector](../../../../../../covector.md) $\eta\in T_{\phi(p)}^*\mathcal N$, the [pullback of a covector](../../../../../../pullback-of-a-covector.md) is the dual linear map:

$$
\boxed{(\phi^*\eta)_p(V)=\eta_{\phi(p)}(d\phi_pV).}
$$

No inverse map is required. A pushed-forward field for a general map is a field along that map; it need not assign a unique vector to an image point with several preimages.
