<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [inverse different](../../../../../inverse-different.md) is the [trace-dual lattice](../../../../../trace-dual-lattice.md)

$$
\mathfrak D_{L/K}^{-1}=\{a\in L:\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(a\mathcal O_L)\subseteq\mathcal O_K\}.
$$

The integral closure $\mathcal O_L$ is finite free over the complete [discrete valuation ring](../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md) $\mathcal O_K$. Choose an integral basis and use the nondegenerate [trace pairing](../../../../../trace-pairing.md) to form its dual basis over $K$. This exhibits $\mathfrak D^{-1}$ as a full, finite $\mathcal O_K$-lattice. It is stable under multiplication by $\mathcal O_L$, because $b\mathcal O_L\subseteq\mathcal O_L$ for $b\in\mathcal O_L$, and bounded denominators make it a [fractional ideal](../../../../../fractional-ideal.md) of $\mathcal O_L$. Integral elements have integral [field traces](../../../../../field-trace.md), so $\mathcal O_L\subseteq\mathfrak D^{-1}$. A nonzero [fractional ideal](../../../../../fractional-ideal.md) of a [discrete valuation ring](../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md) is invertible; its inverse is consequently an integral [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md), the [different ideal](../../../../../different-ideal.md) $\mathfrak D_{L/K}$.

Assume now $A=\mathcal O_K[x]=\mathcal O_L$, with monic separable [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) $g$ of degree $n$. [Lagrange interpolation](../../../../../lagrange-polynomial.md) at its distinct roots gives

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}\!\left(\frac{x^j}{g'(x)}\right)=\begin{cases}0&0\leq j<n-1,\\1&j=n-1.\end{cases}
$$

Indeed, these are the leading coefficients in the interpolation formula for $X^j$. For any element of $A$, this trace is the coefficient of $X^{n-1}$ in its degree-less-than-$n$ representative modulo $g$. The resulting pairing on the basis $1,x,\ldots,x^{n-1}$ is integral and unimodular: reversing the order of one basis makes its matrix triangular with diagonal ones, since entries vanish when the exponent sum is less than $n-1$. Thus it identifies $A$ with its full $\mathcal O_K$-dual. Translating back to the [trace pairing](../../../../../trace-pairing.md) proves

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak D_{L/K}^{-1}=g'(x)^{-1}\mathcal O_L,\qquad\mathfrak D_{L/K}=g'(x)\mathcal O_L.}
$$

For a [totally ramified extension](../../../../../totally-ramified-extension.md) of degree $n$, any [uniformizer](../../../../../uniformizer.md) $\pi_L$ is an [Eisenstein generator of a totally ramified extension](../../../../../eisenstein-generator-of-a-totally-ramified-extension.md), and $\mathcal O_L=\mathcal O_K[\pi_L]$. To see the latter equality, use the common [residue field](../../../../../residue-field.md) to expand an integral element in powers of $\pi_L$ with digits from $\mathcal O_K$; reduce powers using its [Eisenstein polynomial](../../../../../eisenstein-polynomial.md) and take limits in the finite complete $\mathcal O_K$-module generated by $1,\pi_L,\ldots,\pi_L^{n-1}$. Write that polynomial as $X^n+\sum_{j<n}a_jX^j$, with $v_L(a_j)\geq n$ for $j<n$. When $p\nmid n$, the derivative's leading term has [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) $n-1$, while every other nonzero derivative term has [valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) at least $n$. There can be no cancellation of the unique smallest term. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak D_{L/K}=\pi_L^{n-1}\mathcal O_L.}
$$

For the prime-power [p-adic cyclotomic extension](../../../../../cyclotomic-extension-of-a-p-adic-field.md), put $\zeta=\zeta_{p^r}$ and $e=p^{r-1}(p-1)$. Modulo $p$, the shifted [cyclotomic polynomial](../../../../../cyclotomic-polynomial.md) $\Phi_{p^r}(1+T)$ is $T^e$, while its constant term is $p$, not a multiple of $p^2$. It is therefore [Eisenstein](../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md), so $\pi=\zeta-1$ is a [uniformizer](../../../../../uniformizer.md) and $\mathcal O_{K_r}=\mathbb Z_p[\zeta]$. From

$$
\Phi_{p^r}(X)=\frac{X^{p^r}-1}{X^{p^{r-1}}-1}
$$

we obtain, by differentiating the numerator at $\zeta$,

$$
\Phi'_{p^r}(\zeta)=\frac{p^r\zeta^{-1}}{\zeta^{p^{r-1}}-1}.
$$

The denominator is a primitive-$p$ [root of unity](../../../../../root-of-unity.md) minus one and has $K_r$-[valuation](../../../../../valuation.md) $p^{r-1}$. The [different exponent](../../../../../different-exponent.md) is consequently $r e-p^{r-1}$, giving

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak D_{K_r/\mathbb Q_p}=(\zeta-1)^{p^{r-1}(r(p-1)-1)}\mathcal O_{K_r}.}
$$

This includes $p=2,r=1$: the extension is trivial and its [different exponent](../../../../../different-exponent.md) is zero.
