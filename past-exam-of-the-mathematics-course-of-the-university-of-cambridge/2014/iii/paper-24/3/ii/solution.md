<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [principal unit](../../../../../../principal-unit.md) $u$, apply the [Hensel lemma](../../../../../../hensel-s-lemma.md) to $F(T)=T^{p-1}-u$. The residue class one is a root, and $F'(1)=p-1$ is a [unit](../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md). Thus there is a unique $v\in1+\pi_K\mathcal O_K$ with

$$
\boxed{v^{p-1}=u.}
$$

In particular it is a [unit](../../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) of $\mathcal O_K$. Choose this $v$ for the $u$ just obtained and put $\alpha=\pi_K/v$. Then $\alpha^{p-1}=-p$, so $\mathbb Q_p(\alpha)\subseteq K$. The polynomial $T^{p-1}+p$ is [Eisenstein](../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md), giving degree $p-1$ for the left-hand field. The [p-adic cyclotomic extension](../../../../../../cyclotomic-extension-of-a-p-adic-field.md) $K$ also has degree $p-1$, so

$$
\boxed{K=\mathbb Q_p\!\left(\sqrt[p-1]{-p}\right).}
$$
