<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $E=L^1(\mathbb R_x^d\times\mathbb R_v^d)$, and denote the [free-transport semigroup](../../../../../../free-transport-semigroup.md) by $(U_tg)(x,v)=g(x-tv,v)$. For fixed $v$, the spatial translation has unit [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md), so the [Tonelli theorem](../../../../../../tonelli-theorem.md) gives

$$
 \boxed{\|U_tf_0\|_1=\int\int|f_0(x-tv,v)|\,dx\,dv
 =\int\int|f_0(y,v)|\,dy\,dv=\|f_0\|_1.}
$$

Thus the given free term is an [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md) on $E$. The operators form a [strongly continuous semigroup](../../../../../../c0-semigroup.md): continuity first holds for smooth compactly supported functions by dominated convergence, and density plus the isometry extends it to every $L^1$ function.
