<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Fourier coefficient](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) of the velocity-integrated density is its mixed transform at zero velocity frequency. Thus

$$
 \widehat\rho_t(k)=\widehat f(t,k,0)=\widehat f_0(k,kt).
$$

The constant $\rho_\infty$ contributes only to the zero [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md), where it equals $\widehat f_0(0,0)$. Therefore

$$
 \boxed{\widehat r_t(k)=\widehat f_0(k,kt)-\mathbf1_{\{k=0\}}\widehat f_0(0,0)
 =\begin{cases}\widehat f_0(k,kt),&k\ne0,\\0,&k=0.\end{cases}}
$$

It is important to remove the zero mode, which is conserved rather than mixed away.
