<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $g:C\to D$ have the [left lifting property against monomorphisms](../../../../../../left-lifting-property-against-monomorphisms.md), and suppose $\alpha,\beta:D\to B$ satisfy $\alpha g=\beta g$. Use the [categorical diagonal](../../../../../../categorical-diagonal.md) $\Delta_B:B\to B\times B$, which is a [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md) by part (a). The square with top arrow $\alpha g$, bottom arrow $\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle$, left arrow $g$ and right arrow $\Delta_B$ commutes, since both product components are $\alpha g$.

Its lift $t:D\to B$ satisfies $\Delta_Bt=\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle$. Applying the two [product in a category](../../../../../../product-category-theory.md) projections gives $t=\alpha$ and $t=\beta$. Thus **$g$ is an epimorphism**. This [binary-product criterion for lifting-only strong epimorphisms](../../../../../../binary-product-criterion-for-lifting-only-strong-epimorphisms.md) requires binary products, rather than any assumption about [equalizers](../../../../../../equaliser.md).
