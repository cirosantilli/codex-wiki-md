A [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md) is a morphism $m:X\to Y$ such that $mu=mv$ implies $u=v$ for every pair $u,v:Z\to X$. Dually, an [epimorphism](../../../../../../epimorphism.md) $e:X\to Y$ satisfies $ue=ve\Rightarrow u=v$. A [regular epimorphism](../../../../../../regular-epimorphism.md) is a [coequalizer](../../../../../../coequalizer.md) of some parallel pair. An [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md) has a two-sided inverse.

For a [product in a category](../../../../../../product-category-theory.md), write $\Delta_A:A\to A\times A$ for its [categorical diagonal](../../../../../../categorical-diagonal.md). Since $\pi_1\Delta_A=1_A$, equality $\Delta_Au=\Delta_Av$ gives $u=\pi_1\Delta_Au=\pi_1\Delta_Av=v$. Thus **the diagonal is a monomorphism**, indeed a [split monomorphism](../../../../../../split-monomorphism.md).
