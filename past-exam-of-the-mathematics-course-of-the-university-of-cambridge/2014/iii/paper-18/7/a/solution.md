↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md) $f:A\to B$ is a morphism with an inverse $f^{-1}:B\to A$ satisfying $f^{-1}f=1_A$ and $ff^{-1}=1_B$. A [groupoid](../../../../../../groupoid.md) is a [category](../../../../../../category-split.md) in which every morphism is an [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md).

With the given one-sided inverses,

$$
g=g1_B=g(fh)=(gf)h=1_Ah=h.
$$

Consequently **$f$ is an isomorphism with $f^{-1}=g=h$**. This argument uses only the category axioms.
