<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $U\cong\mathcal C(R,-)$ be a [representable functor](../../../../../../representable-functor.md), and let $(L,p_j)$ be the [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) of a small diagram $D:J\to\mathcal C$. A morphism $R\to L$ is uniquely equivalent to a family $f_j:R\to D(j)$ satisfying $D(u)f_j=f_k$ for every $u:j\to k$.

Such compatible families are exactly the elements of the [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) of the set-valued diagram $\mathcal C(R,D(-))$. Consequently the canonical comparison

$$
\mathcal C(R,L)\longrightarrow\lim_{j\in J}\mathcal C(R,D(j)),\qquad f\longmapsto(p_jf)_j,
$$

is a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md). Transporting it through the representing [natural isomorphism](../../../../../../natural-isomorphism.md) proves that **$U$ preserves every small limit that exists in $\mathcal C$**. This proves that [covariant representables preserve limits](../../../../../../covariant-representables-preserve-limits.md). For an empty diagram this says that maps into a terminal object form a singleton.
