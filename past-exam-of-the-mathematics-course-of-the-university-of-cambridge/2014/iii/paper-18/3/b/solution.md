<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a set $S$, let $\iota_s:1\to S$ select $s$. Given any family $f_s:1\to X$, define $f:S\to X$ by $f(s)=f_s(*)$. Then $f\iota_s=f_s$, and these equations determine every value of $f$, so it is unique.

This is precisely the universal property of a [coproduct in a category](../../../../../../coproduct.md), giving

$$
\boxed{S\cong\coprod_{s\in S}1.}
$$

The empty set gives the empty [coproduct in a category](../../../../../../coproduct.md), namely the [initial object](../../../../../../initial-object.md) of the [Category of sets](../../../../../../category-of-sets.md).
