<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use net monetary gain as payoff and order each player's [pure strategies](../../../../../../pure-strategy.md) as $1,2,4$. The [payoff matrix](../../../../../../payoff-matrix.md) for the first player is

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}0&2&-1\\1&0&-2\\-4&-4&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The second player's matrix is $-A$. Equal choices return both stakes, so the diagonal is zero; when the first player wins, their net gain is the other's stake, and when they lose it is minus their own stake. This is a [matrix game](../../../../../../matrix-game.md), hence a [zero-sum game](../../../../../../zero-sum-game.md) and a [normal-form game](../../../../../../normal-form-game.md).
