↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**False.** Take three players of weight one and strict threshold one, so a [coalition](../../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) wins exactly when it contains at least two players. Set $S=\{1,2\}$ and $T=\{2,3\}$. Then

$$
v(S)=v(T)=v(S\cup T)=1,\qquad v(S\cap T)=v(\{2\})=0.
$$

The [convex cooperative game](../../../../../../convex-cooperative-game.md) inequality would require $1\ge1+1-0=2$. Thus even this elementary majority [weighted voting game](../../../../../../weighted-voting-game.md) is not convex.
