<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Single-valued rotor wavefunctions are periodic in $\phi$. The normalized eigenfunctions are $\langle\phi|n\rangle=e^{in\phi}/\sqrt{2\pi}$ with integer $n$, angular momentum $\hbar n$ and energy $E_n=\hbar^2n^2/(2I)$. The [quantum rotor](../../../../../../quantum-rotor.md) consequently has

$$
\boxed{\mathcal Z=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}e^{-\beta\hbar^2n^2/(2I)}.}
$$

The nonzero levels have the $n\leftrightarrow-n$ degeneracy; the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) is $n=0$. No twisted boundary condition or applied flux has been introduced.
