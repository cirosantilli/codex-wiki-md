<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For smooth phase differences, $-J\cos(\phi_{n+1}-\phi_n)=-J+J(\phi_{n+1}-\phi_n)^2/2+\cdots$. After integrating the angular momenta in the same inverse-energy time coordinate $\tau$, the [coupled quantum rotor chain](../../../../../../coupled-quantum-rotor-chain.md) has the fluctuation action

$$
\boxed{\mathcal S=\int_0^\beta d\tau\int dx\left[\frac{I}{2\hbar^2}(\partial_\tau\phi)^2+\frac J2(\partial_x\phi)^2\right].}
$$

Unit spacing replaces the lattice sum and difference by their continuum limits. The constant energy $-J$ per bond contributes an overall factor $e^{\beta JN}$, suppressed when describing fluctuations. The phase is compact; the Gaussian action describes its smooth sector.

There is an [imaginary-time normalization for a coupled rotor chain](../../../../../../imaginary-time-normalization-for-a-coupled-rotor-chain.md) issue in the printed formula. Factoring out $1/\hbar^2$ from the action above places $\hbar^2J$, not $J$, in its spatial bracket. The printed bracket is therefore correct in $\hbar=1$ units, or with its spatial coefficient redefined as $J_{\rm bracket}=\hbar^2J_{\rm Hamiltonian}$. With the same dimensional $J$ as in the Hamiltonian, it lacks that factor.

In physical real time the wave equation is $I\partial_t^2\phi=J\partial_x^2\phi$. The [harmonic phase mode of a quantum rotor chain](../../../../../../harmonic-phase-mode-of-a-quantum-rotor-chain.md) consequently has

$$
\boxed{\omega(q)=\sqrt{J/I}\,|q|,\qquad E(q)=\hbar\sqrt{J/I}\,|q|.}
$$

The lattice expression is $\omega(q)=2\sqrt{J/I}|\sin(q/2)|$. The mode is a gapless phase wave analogous to sound in a [superfluid](../../../../../../superfluid.md), with rotor angular momentum conjugate to its phase. In one dimension this harmonic regime has strong long-distance phase fluctuations rather than true continuous-symmetry long-range order; compact [phase slips](../../../../../../phase-slip.md) can change the infrared phase outside the smooth-phase approximation.
