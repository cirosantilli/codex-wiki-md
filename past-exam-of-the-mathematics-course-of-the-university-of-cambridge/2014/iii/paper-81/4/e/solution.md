<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Differentiate the [homogeneous spin-field effective action](../../../../../../homogeneous-spin-field-effective-action.md):

$$
\frac{d\mathcal S_{\rm eff}}{dM}=\beta\left[\frac{NM}{U}-\sum_{\mathbf k,\sigma}\sigma n_F(\epsilon_{\mathbf k}-\sigma M)\right].
$$

At a stationary field, $M=(U/N)\sum_{\mathbf k,\sigma}\sigma n_F(\epsilon_{\mathbf k}-\sigma M)$. Define the [spin-summed density of states](../../../../../../spin-summed-density-of-states.md) per lattice site by

$$
\nu(\epsilon)=\frac2N\sum_{\mathbf k}\delta(\epsilon-\epsilon_{\mathbf k}).
$$

Then the requested form is

$$
\boxed{M=\frac U2\sum_{\sigma=\pm1}\sigma\int d\epsilon\,\nu(\epsilon)n_F(\epsilon-\sigma M).}
$$

The factor one half fixes the spin convention. With a [single-spin density of states](../../../../../../single-spin-density-of-states.md) $\nu_s=\nu/2$, the prefactor would instead be $U$. In terms of site occupations, the same equation is $M=U(n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow)=2U\langle S^z\rangle$. The [Fermi distribution](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) includes the [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) in its argument definition; it must not be subtracted a second time.
