<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) of the diagonal Hamiltonian, $\alpha_{\mathbf k}|0_B\rangle=0$. The [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md) therefore gives $\langle b_{\mathbf k}^\dagger b_{\mathbf k}\rangle=v_k^2$. Summing all nonzero momenta proves the [quantum depletion of a Bose condensate](../../../../../../quantum-depletion-of-a-bose-condensate.md):

$$
\boxed{n-n_0=\frac1{2L^3}\sum_{\mathbf k\ne0}\left(\frac{\epsilon_{\mathbf k}+nV_{\mathbf k}}{E_{\mathbf k}}-1\right).}
$$

The nonzero depletion arises from correlated pairs in the interacting [quasiparticle](../../../../../../quasiparticle.md) vacuum, not thermally populated [quasiparticles](../../../../../../quasiparticle.md).

The printed expression is the zero-temperature result. At a nonzero temperature the complete leading-order expression instead has

$$
\langle b_{\mathbf k}^\dagger b_{\mathbf k}\rangle=v_k^2+(u_k^2+v_k^2)n_B(E_k),\qquad
n_B(E)=\frac1{e^{\beta E}-1}.
$$

The additional term is thermal depletion and cannot be discarded merely because the gas is condensed.
