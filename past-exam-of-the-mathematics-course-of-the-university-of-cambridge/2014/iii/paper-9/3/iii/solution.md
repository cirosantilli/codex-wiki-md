<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If the complement of a truth set belongs to a proper [filter on a set](../../../../../../filter-set-theory.md), the truth set itself cannot belong: their intersection is empty. Hence the right side always implies the left side.

The reverse implication can fail. In the [cofinite filter](../../../../../../cofinite-filter.md), the set of even integers is absent, but its odd complement is absent as well. The filter-quantified evenness assertion is false, while the filter-quantified assertion of oddness is also false. Therefore **(iii) can be false**.

For an [ultrafilter](../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) the equivalence is true, precisely because it contains exactly one of any set and its complement. The [filter quantifier](../../../../../../filter-quantifier.md) preserves conjunction for arbitrary proper filters, but its full classical Boolean behaviour requires the ultrafilter property.
