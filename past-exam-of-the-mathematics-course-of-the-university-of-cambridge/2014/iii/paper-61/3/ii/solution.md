<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since $U$ is a [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md), its [eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md) form an [orthonormal](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) basis. Expand $|\xi\rangle=\sum_j c_j|v_j\rangle$, with eigenphases $\phi_j=y_j/2^n$. By linearity, the unmeasured [quantum phase estimation](../../../../../../quantum-phase-estimation.md) output is

$$
\boxed{V\bigl(|0^n\rangle|\xi\rangle\bigr)
=\sum_j c_j|y_j\rangle|v_j\rangle}.
$$

This is generally an [entangled](../../../../../../entangled-state.md) state, not a phase label attached to an unchanged pure system state. The distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) give distinct phase labels, so a [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) measurement yields $y_j$ with [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) probability $|c_j|^2$ and leaves $|v_j\rangle$ in the system register. Before measurement, all relative phases remain coherent; that is essential for the following spectral transformation. If phases were degenerate, a measured label would instead select the corresponding eigenspace component.
