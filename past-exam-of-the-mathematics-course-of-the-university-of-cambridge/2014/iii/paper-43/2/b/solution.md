<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The defining restriction is the reality condition

$$
\boxed{V^\dagger=V.}
$$

For a non-Abelian [gauge group](../../../../../../gauge-group.md), write the [vector superfield](../../../../../../vector-superfield.md) in a Hermitian generator basis with real [superfield](../../../../../../superfield.md) coefficients. This is a condition on the full [superfield](../../../../../../superfield.md), not a chirality constraint. It relates conjugate component coefficients and makes the vector and $D$ [auxiliary field](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) real. [Wess-Zumino gauge](../../../../../../wess-zumino-gauge.md) is a further [supergauge transformation](../../../../../../supergauge-transformation.md) choice removing redundant components; it is not the defining condition for a [vector superfield](../../../../../../vector-superfield.md).
