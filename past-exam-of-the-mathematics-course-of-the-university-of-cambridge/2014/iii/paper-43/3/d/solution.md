<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the stable branch found above, $t=x+\beta=1$ and $s=1$. Hence the [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) is

$$
\boxed{\langle z\rangle=\sqrt3-1,\qquad C=3,\quad D=-1,}
$$

in the stated Planck units. To verify that it is a vacuum rather than merely a zero-energy [stationary point](../../../../../../stationary-point.md), evaluate the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md). At either zero-energy stationary branch,

$$
H_{xx}=4s\sqrt3,\qquad H_{yy}=8-4s\sqrt3,\qquad H_{xy}=0.
$$

Because $H$ and its first derivatives vanish there, the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) of $V$ is just $|m|^4e^{x^2}$ times this [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md). For $s=1$, both eigenvalues are positive. This proves a strict [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md) in both real scalar directions. For $s=-1$, $H_{xx}<0$, so the alternative $\beta=2+\sqrt3$, $\langle z\rangle=-\sqrt3-1$ is a [saddle point](../../../../../../saddle-point.md) and is excluded from the [stable zero-energy Polonyi vacuum](../../../../../../stable-zero-energy-polonyi-vacuum.md).

There is also a useful global check. Set $u=x-(\sqrt3-1)$ on the stable branch. Directly completing squares gives

$$
H=\left(u^2+y^2+\sqrt3\,u\right)^2+(2\sqrt3-3)u^2+(4-2\sqrt3)y^2.
$$

Both remaining coefficients are positive. Thus $H\geq0$ everywhere, with equality only at $u=y=0$. The positive exponential prefactor proves that this is the unique [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md), not just a metastable vacuum.

Finally, at the stable vacuum $z+\beta=1$ and $D_zW=\sqrt3\,m^2$. Its [supergravity auxiliary field](../../../../../../supergravity-auxiliary-field.md) has

$$
\boxed{|F^z|=\sqrt3\,|m|^2e^{(\sqrt3-1)^2/2}>0\qquad(m\ne0).}
$$

Thus **the Minkowski vacuum breaks supersymmetry**, even though its [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) vanishes. If $m=0$, the potential is flat and the displayed tuned parameter and scalar value are not selected.
