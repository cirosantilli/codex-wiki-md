<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [Polonyi model](../../../../../../polonyi-model.md), the [Kähler metric](../../../../../../kahler-metric.md) is $K_{z\bar z}=1$. The relevant [Kähler covariant derivative of a superpotential](../../../../../../kahler-covariant-derivative-of-a-superpotential.md) is

$$
D_zW=m^2\{1+\bar z(z+\beta)\}.
$$

The [supergravity auxiliary field](../../../../../../supergravity-auxiliary-field.md) is $F^z=-e^{|z|^2/2}\overline{D_zW}$, up to an irrelevant common phase convention. Thus a constant vacuum preserves [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) exactly when this [auxiliary field](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) vanishes. For **$m=0$**, the [superpotential](../../../../../../superpotential.md) and [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) vanish identically, and every constant scalar value is a [supersymmetric vacuum](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vacuum.md).

For $m\ne0$, put $\langle z\rangle=x+iy$. Its [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) condition becomes

$$
1+x^2+y^2+\beta x-i\beta y=0.
$$

Since $\beta>0$, it requires $y=0$ and $x^2+\beta x+1=0$. Hence the [Polonyi supersymmetry branches](../../../../../../polonyi-supersymmetry-branches.md) are

$$
\boxed{\text{unbroken supersymmetry: }\beta\geq2,\quad
\langle z\rangle=\frac{-\beta\pm\sqrt{\beta^2-4}}2,\quad m\ne0.}
$$

At these points $W\ne0$, so the [supergravity F-term potential](../../../../../../supergravity-f-term-potential.md) is negative, $V=-3e^K|W|^2$: these are supersymmetric [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) vacua, not zero-energy ones. The condition $D_zW=0$ also makes them stationary, as follows by differentiating the [supergravity F-term potential](../../../../../../supergravity-f-term-potential.md).

For $0<\beta<2$ and $m\ne0$, the [auxiliary field](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) cannot vanish anywhere, so any vacuum has [supersymmetry breaking](../../../../../../supersymmetry-breaking.md). For $\beta\geq2$, a stationary vacuum at any other scalar value still breaks [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md); the parameter condition alone does not determine which vacuum is selected. In particular, a nontrivial zero-energy vacuum cannot preserve [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md): $D_zW=0$ and $V=0$ would also imply $W=0$, whereas $W=0$ gives $z=-\beta$ and $D_zW=m^2\ne0$.
