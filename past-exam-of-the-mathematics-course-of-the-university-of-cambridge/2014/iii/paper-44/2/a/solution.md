<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [Wilsonian effective action](../../../../../../wilsonian-effective-action.md) picture, integrate out field modes in a high-[momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) shell and encode their effects in the action for the retained modes. Rescale lengths and fields to compare the resulting theory at the original cutoff. The couplings then follow a [renormalization-group flow](../../../../../../renormalization-group-flow.md), while low-energy predictions are preserved. In general all local interactions allowed by the symmetries are generated, even if only a few appear in the initial action.

Near a [renormalization-group fixed point](../../../../../../renormalization-group-fixed-point.md), a perturbation $u\int d^dx\,\mathcal O(x)$ with scaling dimension $\Delta$ has linearized eigenvalue $y=d-\Delta$. Under coarse graining by $b>1$, its dimensionless coupling scales as $u'\simeq b^yu$. A [relevant operator](../../../../../../relevant-operator.md) has $y>0$, so its perturbation grows toward long distances; an [irrelevant operator](../../../../../../irrelevant-operator.md) has $y<0$ and decreases; a [marginal operator](../../../../../../marginal-operator.md) has $y=0$ and needs nonlinear flow to determine its behavior. **Marginality at linear order need not mean exact scale independence**. Interactions can be marginally relevant or [marginally irrelevant operators](../../../../../../marginally-irrelevant-operator.md).
