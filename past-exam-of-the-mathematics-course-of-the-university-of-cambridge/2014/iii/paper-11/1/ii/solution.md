<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [down-set](../../../../../../down-set.md) in the [Boolean lattice](../../../../../../boolean-lattice.md) is a family closed under taking subsets. Restricting the minimization to down-sets cannot decrease the minimum from the [edge-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube](../../../../../../edge-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md).

The coordinate subcube consisting of all subsets of a fixed $k$-element set is itself a down-set and attains that minimum. Thus

$$
\boxed{g(2^k)=2^k(n-k)}.
$$
