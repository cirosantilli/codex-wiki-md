<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the [four functions theorem](../../../../../../ahlswede-daykin-inequality.md), take $\alpha=\mathbf1_{\mathcal A}$, $\beta=\mathbf1_{\mathcal B}$, $\gamma=\mathbf1_{\mathcal A\vee\mathcal B}$ and $\delta=\mathbf1_{\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal B}$, where the [union and intersection of set families](../../../../../../union-and-intersection-of-set-families.md) are the collections of all pairwise unions and intersections.

If $\alpha(A)\beta(B)=1$, then $A\cup B\in\mathcal A\vee\mathcal B$ and $A\cap B\in\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal B$, so the pointwise hypothesis holds. Otherwise its left side is zero. Summing these [indicator functions](../../../../../../indicator-function.md) gives

$$
\boxed{|\mathcal A\vee\mathcal B|\,|\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal B|\ge|\mathcal A|\,|\mathcal B|}.
$$
