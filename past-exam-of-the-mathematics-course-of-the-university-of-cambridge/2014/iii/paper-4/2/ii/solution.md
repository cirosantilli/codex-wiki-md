<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply part (i) with $S=L$ and use multiplicativity of the [index of a subgroup](../../../../../../index-of-a-subgroup.md) along a subgroup chain:

$$
\boxed{[G:H\cap L]=[G:L]\,[L:H\cap L]\leq[G:L]\,[G:H]<\infty.}
$$

Thus the intersection of two [finite-index subgroups](../../../../../../finite-index-subgroup.md) again has finite index.
