<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The map of coset [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md)

$$
G/H\longrightarrow R/\theta(H),\qquad gH\longmapsto\theta(g)\theta(H)
$$

is well defined and [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), because the [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $\theta$ is surjective. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[R:\theta(H)]\leq[G:H]<\infty.}
$$

No normality assumption on $H$ is required.
