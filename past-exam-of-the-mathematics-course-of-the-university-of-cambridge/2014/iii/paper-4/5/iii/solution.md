<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**No prime and no presentation of $G_3$ have [p-deficiency](../../../../../../p-deficiency.md) at least one.** Set $r=xy$. The relations give

$$
r^{1024}=1,\qquad x^2=1,\qquad xrx^{-1}=r^{-1},\qquad y=xr.
$$

Every element has form $r^j$ or $r^jx$ with $0\leq j<1024$. Conversely, the usual rotations and reflections of a regular $1024$-gon satisfy the presentation and give $2048$ distinct elements. Hence $G_3$ is the finite [dihedral group](../../../../../../dihedral-group.md) of order $2048$. The criterion [p-deficiency at least one implies infinitude](../../../../../../p-deficiency-at-least-one-implies-infinitude.md) excludes every alternative presentation and every prime. As a check, the given presentation has

$$
\operatorname{def}_2=2-\frac12-\frac12-\frac1{1024}=1-\frac1{1024}<1.
$$
