<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**No prime and no presentation of $G_1$ have [p-deficiency](../../../../../../p-deficiency.md) at least one.** Abelianizing the cyclic squaring relations makes each generator zero: for example $vwv^{-1}=w^2$ becomes $w=2w$, so $w=0$, and the other four relations kill $x,y,z,v$. Thus the [abelianization](../../../../../../abelianization.md) of $G_1$ is trivial and $d_p(G_1)=0$ for every [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number.md) $p$. The presentation-independent [p-rank of a group](../../../../../../p-rank-of-a-group.md) bound from the general solution gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{def}_p(\mathcal P)\leq d_p(G_1)=0}
$$

for every [group presentation](../../../../../../group-presentation.md) $\mathcal P$ of $G_1$. This rules out alternative presentations, not just the one displayed.
