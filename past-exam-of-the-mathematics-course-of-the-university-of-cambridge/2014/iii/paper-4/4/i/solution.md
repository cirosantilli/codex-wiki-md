<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first [Baumslag-Solitar group](../../../../../../baumslag-solitar-group.md) is

$$
\langle a,b\mid bab^{-1}=a^{-1}\rangle\cong\mathbb Z\rtimes\mathbb Z,
$$

where the second [infinite cyclic group](../../../../../../infinite-cyclic-group.md) acts on the first by inversion. The [presentation of a semidirect product](../../../../../../presentation-of-a-semidirect-product.md) proves this identification; both copies of $\mathbb Z$ are [residually finite groups](../../../../../../residually-finite-group.md), since reduction modulo a suitable positive integer separates any nonzero integer. The normal factor is finitely generated, so the [residual finiteness of semidirect products](../../../../../../residual-finiteness-of-semidirect-products.md) theorem applies. **$\boxed{BS(1,-1)\text{ is residually finite}.}$**
