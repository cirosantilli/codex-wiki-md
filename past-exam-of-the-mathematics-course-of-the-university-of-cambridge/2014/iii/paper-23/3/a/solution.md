<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The map $X(\Gamma)\to X(\Gamma(1))\cong\mathbb P^1$ has degree $d$, the effective projective index. Ramification occurs only over the two elliptic points and the [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md). Over the order-two elliptic point, $r_2$ points have index one and the other $(d-r_2)/2$ points have index two, contributing $(d-r_2)/2$ to the [ramification divisor](../../../../../../ramification-divisor.md). The analogous order-three contribution is $2(d-r_3)/3$.

The local degree at a [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) is its effective [cusp width](../../../../../../width-of-a-cusp.md). The [cusp widths](../../../../../../width-of-a-cusp.md) sum to $d$, so the [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) contribution is $d-r_\infty$. The [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](../../../../../../riemann-hurwitz-formula.md), $2g(X)-2=d(2g(Y)-2)+\sum_P(e_P-1)$, now yields

$$
2g-2=-2d+\frac{d-r_2}{2}+\frac{2(d-r_3)}3+d-r_\infty.
$$

Therefore the [genus formula for a modular curve](../../../../../../genus-formula-for-a-modular-curve.md) is

$$
\boxed{g=1+\frac d{12}-\frac{r_2}4-\frac{r_3}3-\frac{r_\infty}2.}
$$

Using the index in $\mathrm{SL}_2$ without accounting for its center would give the wrong degree when $-I\notin\Gamma$.
