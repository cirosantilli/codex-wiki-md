<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the three left-coset representatives $I,S,ST$ for $\Gamma_0(2)\backslash\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)$. If a nonzero weight-four [cusp form](../../../../../../cusp-form.md) $h$ existed, its [coset norm of a modular form](../../../../../../coset-norm-of-a-modular-form.md)

$$
H=h\,(h|_4S)\,(h|_4ST)
$$

would be a nonzero weight-twelve [modular form](../../../../../../modular-form.md) for the full group: right multiplication permutes its factors. At infinity $h$ has order at least one in $q$. The other two factors correspond to the zero [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) of [cusp width](../../../../../../width-of-a-cusp.md) two and each have order at least $1/2$ in $q$. Thus $H$ has order at least two there.

The ratio $H/\Delta$ is weight zero, holomorphic on the half-plane because $\Delta$ has no zeros there, and holomorphic at the [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) with value zero. It descends to a [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) on the compact full modular curve. Such a function is constant, hence zero, contradicting $H\ne0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{S_4(\Gamma_0(2))=0.}
$$

Constant terms at the two [modular cusps](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) define a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) $M_4(\Gamma_0(2))\to\mathbb C^2$ whose kernel is this [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) space. It is injective, so the dimension is at most two. The forms $E_4(\tau)$ and $E_4(2\tau)$ are holomorphic weight-four forms for this group. For the second, the same conjugation used in 1(c) proves transformation, and $E_4(2S\tau)|$ after the weight factor is $2^{-4}E_4(\tau/2)$, proving holomorphy at zero. Their constant terms are both one but their $q$ coefficients are respectively $240$ and zero, so they are independent. We obtain

$$
\boxed{M_4(\Gamma_0(2))=\mathbb CE_4(\tau)\oplus\mathbb CE_4(2\tau),\qquad\dim M_4(\Gamma_0(2))=2.}
$$

This is the [weight-four Eisenstein basis at level two](../../../../../../weight-four-eisenstein-basis-at-level-two.md).
