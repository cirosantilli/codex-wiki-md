<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take regular [adiabatic initial conditions](../../../../../../adiabatic-initial-conditions.md) and a common scale-invariant primordial amplitude, $k^{3/2}\Phi_{\rm prim}(k)=\text{constant}$. Let $k_{\rm eq}=\mathcal H_{\rm eq}$. The three histories are distinguished by their time of [cosmological horizon crossing](../../../../../../cosmological-horizon-crossing.md):

- For $k\ll k_{\rm eq}$, the mode remains outside the [Hubble radius](../../../../../../hubble-radius.md) throughout [radiation domination](../../../../../../radiation-domination.md). Its potential remains nearly constant through equality, changing to $9/10$ of the primordial radiation value for a purely adiabatic transition. It stays constant after later horizon entry in [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md).
- For $k\gg k_{\rm eq}$, horizon entry occurs during [radiation domination](../../../../../../radiation-domination.md). Radiation pressure produces oscillations and a rapidly decreasing potential. The small [cold dark matter](../../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md) component eventually supports a much smaller constant matter-era potential; the pure-radiation oscillations are not continued forever after equality.
- For $k\sim k_{\rm eq}$, the transition and horizon entry overlap, giving an intermediate suppression before the potential approaches a constant.

The $9/10$ factor follows from conserving the large-scale adiabatic curvature: the constant potential is proportional to $3(1+w)/(5+3w)$, whose matter-to-radiation ratio is $(3/5)/(2/3)=9/10$. Define the [cold-dark-matter transfer function](../../../../../../cold-dark-matter-transfer-function.md) by $\Phi_{\rm late}=(9/10)T(k)\Phi_{\rm prim}$. Then $T\to1$ on large scales, while $T\sim(k_{\rm eq}/k)^2\ln(k/k_{\rm eq})$ far below the equality length, as explained by the density growth below.

**Late amplitudes are $\Phi_{\rm late}=(9/10)T(k)\Phi_{\rm prim}$, with $T\simeq1$ for $k\ll k_{\rm eq}$ and $T\ll1$ for $k\gg k_{\rm eq}$.**

The upper panel sketches $k^{3/2}\Phi$ for the three modes. The lower panel provides the corresponding [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) histories used in the next part. A negative common primordial potential was chosen so the growing density is positive; this arbitrary phase has no effect on a [cosmological density power spectrum](../../../../../../matter-power-spectrum.md).
