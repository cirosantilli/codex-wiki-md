<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The fields constitute an [RNS string](../../../../../spinning-string.md) coupled to two-dimensional [worldsheet supersymmetry](../../../../../worldsheet-supersymmetry.md). The [zweibein](../../../../../zweibein.md) and [worldsheet gravitino](../../../../../worldsheet-gravitino.md) impose constraints; they do not supply extra propagating string polarizations. Worldsheet diffeomorphisms, local frame rotations and Weyl transformations put the metric locally in [conformal gauge](../../../../../conformal-gauge.md), $h_{\mu\nu}=e^{2\omega}\eta_{\mu\nu}$. Local [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) together with [super-Weyl symmetry](../../../../../super-weyl-symmetry.md) removes the [worldsheet gravitino](../../../../../worldsheet-gravitino.md), giving [superconformal gauge](../../../../../superconformal-gauge.md) $\chi_\mu=0$. On a general closed worldsheet, moduli and spin structures remain and must still be summed or integrated over; gauge fixing is not a declaration that every surface is globally a flat cylinder.

Variation with respect to the metric and [worldsheet gravitino](../../../../../worldsheet-gravitino.md) before gauge fixing gives the stress-tensor and supercurrent constraints $T_{++}=T_{--}=0$ and $G_+=G_-=0$. The remaining matter consists of free $X^a$ and their worldsheet fermions. In covariant quantization, these become the [N=1 super-Virasoro algebra](../../../../../n-1-super-virasoro-algebra.md) physical-state conditions: positive Virasoro and supercurrent modes annihilate a physical state, $(L_0-a)|\mathrm{phys}\rangle=0$, and the [R sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md) also has the zero-mode condition $F_0|\mathrm{phys}\rangle=0$. Null gauge states are quotiented out. Equivalently, physical states are [BRST cohomology](../../../../../brst-cohomology.md) classes, so [BRST-exact operators](../../../../../brst-exact-operator.md) do not represent additional physical states.

The diffeomorphism $bc$ ghosts have [central charge](../../../../../central-charge.md) $-26$, while the bosonic [superconformal ghosts](../../../../../superconformal-ghost.md) $(\beta,\gamma)$ have charge $11$. Matter contributes $D+D/2$. Therefore quantum gauge consistency requires

$$
\boxed{\frac{3D}{2}-26+11=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad D=10.}
$$

This is the [critical dimension of the RNS superstring](../../../../../critical-dimension-of-the-rns-superstring.md). In [light-cone gauge in string theory](../../../../../light-cone-gauge-in-string-theory.md), choose nonzero $p^+$, make $X^+$ linear in worldsheet time and set $\psi^+=0$. The stress-tensor and supercurrent constraints solve for the longitudinal $X^-$ and $\psi^-$ oscillators. Only eight transverse bosons and eight transverse fermions remain, with positive norm. This explicitly eliminates the time-like and longitudinal unphysical states.

In a chiral sector the fermions have half-integral modes in the [NS sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-sector.md) and integral modes in the [R sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md). The [normal-ordering constant of a string](../../../../../normal-ordering-constant-of-a-string.md) is $a_{\mathrm{NS}}=1/2$ and $a_R=0$. The [GSO projection](../../../../../gso-projection.md) retains odd fermion-excitation parity in the [NS sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-sector.md), removing its tachyonic vacuum. In the [R sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md) it keeps one chirality of the zero-mode spinor, with oscillator parity included in the projection. The [Ramond zero-mode Clifford algebra](../../../../../ramond-zero-mode-clifford-algebra.md) then leaves eight ground-state polarizations in [light-cone gauge in string theory](../../../../../light-cone-gauge-in-string-theory.md).

[Worldsheet supersymmetry](../../../../../worldsheet-supersymmetry.md) alone does not imply spacetime [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md). The [GSO projection](../../../../../gso-projection.md) makes the spinorial worldsheet currents mutually local with the retained vertex operators and pairs their NS and R states. More explicitly, let $S_A$ be an [RNS spin field](../../../../../rns-spin-field.md) and $\phi_{\rm gh}$ the bosonized superghost scalar. The spacetime supercharges are generated by

$$
Q_A=\oint dz\,e^{-\phi_{\rm gh}/2}S_A(z),
$$

and similarly in the other chiral sector. The spin field has conformal weight $10/16=5/8$, and its superghost factor has weight $3/8$, so the current has weight one. Its operator products generate the spacetime translation operator; schematically $\{Q_A,Q_B\}\propto(C\Gamma^a)_{AB}P_a$. This is the [spacetime supercharge from an RNS spin field](../../../../../spacetime-supercharge-from-an-rns-spin-field.md). For [type IIA superstring theory](../../../../../type-iia-superstring-theory.md), the left and right Ramond projections select opposite ten-dimensional Majorana-Weyl chiralities, giving 32 real supercharges and a nonchiral spacetime theory.

The massless physical states are best counted with the transverse [little group](../../../../../little-group.md) $\operatorname{Spin}(8)$. Choose the left Ramond ground representation $8_s$ and the right one $8_c$. Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{NS\! -\! NS}:&\quad 8_v\otimes8_v=35_v\oplus28\oplus1,\\
\mathrm{R\! -\! R}:&\quad 8_s\otimes8_c=8_v\oplus56_v,\\
\mathrm{NS\! -\! R}:&\quad 8_v\otimes8_c=8_s\oplus56_s,\\
\mathrm{R\! -\! NS}:&\quad 8_s\otimes8_v=8_c\oplus56_c.
\end{aligned}
$$

The [NS-NS sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-neveu-schwarz-sector.md) supplies the [graviton](../../../../../graviton.md), [Kalb–Ramond field](../../../../../kalb-ramond-field.md) and [dilaton](../../../../../dilaton.md), with $35$, $28$ and $1$ polarizations. The [RR sector](../../../../../ramond-ramond-sector.md) supplies a one-form potential and a three-form potential, with $\binom81=8$ and $\binom83=56$ polarizations. The mixed sectors supply two [gravitinos](../../../../../gravitino.md) of opposite chirality, each with $56$ polarizations, and two [dilatinos](../../../../../dilatino.md), each with $8$. Covariantly the dilatino has chirality opposite to its corresponding [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) parameter; the two chirality sets are both present. Thus the [massless type IIA spectrum](../../../../../massless-type-iia-spectrum.md) has

$$
\boxed{n_B=35+28+1+8+56=128,\qquad n_F=2(56+8)=128.}
$$

These fields form the massless [type IIA supergravity](../../../../../type-iia-supergravity.md) multiplet.

For the [first massive level of a chiral RNS sector](../../../../../first-massive-level-of-a-chiral-rns-sector.md), restore $\alpha'$ through

$$
\frac{\alpha'M^2}{4}=N_{\mathrm{NS}}-\frac12=N_R.
$$

The first positive value is one. The NS states therefore have level $N_{\mathrm{NS}}=3/2$. A complete transverse basis after [GSO projection](../../../../../gso-projection.md) is

$$
b_{-3/2}^i|0\rangle,\qquad
\alpha_{-1}^i b_{-1/2}^j|0\rangle,\qquad
b_{-1/2}^i b_{-1/2}^j b_{-1/2}^k|0\rangle\quad(i<j<k).
$$

All contain odd fermion-excitation parity. Their respective dimensions are $8$, $8^2=64$ and $\binom83=56$, so **the NS count is $128$**. These are the [first massive GSO-projected Neveu–Schwarz states](../../../../../first-massive-gso-projected-neveu-schwarz-states.md). The middle family decomposes into $35_v+28+1$. Together the families assemble into the massive $\operatorname{Spin}(9)$ representations $44+84$: a symmetric traceless rank-two tensor and a three-form, since $44\downarrow\operatorname{Spin}(8)=35_v+8_v+1$ and $84\downarrow\operatorname{Spin}(8)=56_v+28$.

In the [R sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md), the first massive level is $N_R=1$. If the retained right-moving ground spinor is $8_c$, its two families are

$$
\alpha_{-1}^i|s_c\rangle,\qquad d_{-1}^i|s_s\rangle.
$$

The Ramond spinors have opposite zero-mode chiralities in these two families: inserting one fermionic oscillator reverses the oscillator contribution to the GSO condition, so the second family must use the opposite zero-mode chirality. Each family has $8\times8=64$ states. Hence

$$
\boxed{n_R=64+64=128=n_{\mathrm{NS}},\qquad M^2=4/\alpha'.}
$$

These [first massive GSO-projected Ramond states](../../../../../first-massive-gso-projected-ramond-states.md) form the gamma-traceless vector-spinor representation of $\operatorname{Spin}(9)$, of dimension $9\cdot16-16=128$. Its transverse branching is $8_s+56_s+8_c+56_c$. The right-moving sector therefore contains $128$ bosons and $128$ fermions, a [massive chiral superstring supersymmetry multiplet](../../../../../massive-chiral-superstring-supersymmetry-multiplet.md).

For the closed string, [closed-string level matching](../../../../../closed-string-level-matching.md) requires the left and right shifted levels to agree. Both chiral sectors at this first massive mass have $128$ NS states and $128$ R states. The bosons lie in the [NS-NS sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-neveu-schwarz-sector.md) and [RR sector](../../../../../ramond-ramond-sector.md), while the mixed sectors are fermionic. Consequently

$$
\boxed{n_B=2(128)^2=32768,\qquad n_F=2(128)^2=32768.}
$$

Their total is $65536=2^{16}$. A massive ten-dimensional state with 32 real supercharges and no central charges has sixteen fermionic creation operators in its rest-frame [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) algebra, giving a long multiplet of this size. Thus the chiral equality, the four closed sectors and level matching are consistent with the [first massive type IIA long supermultiplet](../../../../../first-massive-type-iia-long-supermultiplet.md), rather than merely matching an isolated number of right-moving states.
