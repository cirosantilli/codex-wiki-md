<h1 id="5/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Interpret the paired $A,B,C$ disks as the three [one-handles](../../../../../../one-handle.md) of a genus-three [handlebody](../../../../../../handlebody.md). Attach [two-handles](../../../../../../two-handle.md) on the two displayed $\beta$ curves. Orient the three disk-crossing generators as $a,b,c$. Reading the signed crossings, starting at the upper-left portion of each attaching curve and changing the start point when needed, gives

$$
r_1=a b c a^{-1}c^{-1}b^{-1},\qquad r_2=a b c b^{-1}a^{-1}c^{-1}.
$$

The first says $abc=bca$ and the second $abc=cab$. Thus the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) presentation is

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(Y)=\langle a,b,c\mid [a,bc]=1,\ [ab,c]=1\rangle.}
$$

Here $[u,v]=uvu^{-1}v^{-1}$. Cyclically changing the starting point, reversing an attaching curve, or changing generator orientations gives equivalent presentations. With $h=abc$, these relations make $h$ central; eliminating $c=b^{-1}a^{-1}h$ gives

$$
\pi_1(Y)\cong\langle a,b,h\mid[a,h]=[b,h]=1\rangle\cong F_2\times\mathbb Z.
$$

For the topological identification, thicken the diagram's two nested bands and identify the three paired disk mouths. The complement of those bands is the product region of a [pair of pants](../../../../../../pair-of-pants-mathematics.md) with a circle; its two compressing curves are exactly $[a,bc]$ and $[ab,c]$. Equivalently, the standard cell decomposition of this product has three [one-handles](../../../../../../one-handle.md) and the two commuting [two-handle](../../../../../../two-handle.md) attachments shown. Thus this is a [generalized Heegaard diagram](../../../../../../generalized-heegaard-diagram.md) of $P\times S^1$.

The [Hopf fibration](../../../../../../hopf-fibration.md) of $S^3$ has three disjoint regular fibers whose removal leaves $P\times S^1$. Its three fibers are the components of the [torus link](../../../../../../torus-link.md) $T_{3,3}$, as is also apparent from the full three-strand twist in the later [link diagram](../../../../../../link-diagram.md). This identifies $Y$ with that [link exterior](../../../../../../link-exterior.md), using the diagram and product structure rather than just its [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md). In particular **it is a link exterior in the three-sphere**.
