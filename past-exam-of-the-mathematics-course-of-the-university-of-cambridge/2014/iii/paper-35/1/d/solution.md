<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Independent increments of the [Poisson process](../../../../../../poisson-process.md) give $M\mid\lambda\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda)$ for a one-hour interval. The [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(M=0\mid\lambda)=e^{-\lambda}.}
$$

This conditional probability still depends on the unknown rate.
