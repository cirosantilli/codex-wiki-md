A [conjugate prior](../../../../../../conjugate-prior.md) is a family of [prior distributions](../../../../../../prior-probability.md) whose members remain in that family after updating by the [likelihood function](../../../../../../likelihood-function.md). Here multiplying a shape-rate [gamma distribution](../../../../../../gamma-distribution.md) density by the [Poisson process](../../../../../../poisson-process.md) likelihood changes its power of $\lambda$ and its exponential rate, leaving a [gamma distribution](../../../../../../gamma-distribution.md). The parameters change with the observations; [conjugate prior](../../../../../../conjugate-prior.md) does not mean that the [Bayesian posterior](../../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) equals the [prior distribution](../../../../../../prior-probability.md).
