<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [log odds ratio](../../../../../../log-odds-ratio.md) for treatment relative to control is the difference of the two [log odds](../../../../../../log-odds.md):

$$
\log\frac{\theta_T/(1-\theta_T)}{\theta_C/(1-\theta_C)}
=(\alpha+\beta/2)-(\alpha-\beta/2)=\boxed{\beta}.
$$

Thus the treatment-to-control [odds ratio](../../../../../../odds-ratio.md) is **$e^\beta$**. Its direction refers to whatever event the binomial count records.
