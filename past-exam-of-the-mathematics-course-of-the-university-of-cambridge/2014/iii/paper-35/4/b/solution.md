<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The intercept is the midpoint of the two [log odds](../../../../../../log-odds.md):

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\tfrac12[\operatorname{logit}\theta_C+\operatorname{logit}\theta_T].}
$$

Equivalently $e^\alpha$ is the geometric mean of the two odds. The [logistic function](../../../../../../logistic-function.md) evaluated at $\alpha$ represents a central event probability, and approximates the average of the two group probabilities when $\beta$ is small. **It is not generally their arithmetic average**, nor is $\alpha$ specifically the control-group [log odds](../../../../../../log-odds.md).
