↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Conditional on $p_M$, the auxiliary [binomial distribution](../../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) gives $\mathbb E[X/n\mid p_M]=p_M$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X/n\mid p_M)=p_M(1-p_M)/n$. Thus **large $n$ makes $X/n$ close to $p_M$**. The [law of total variance](../../../../../../../law-of-total-variance.md) also gives

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=n m(1-m)+n(n-1)v
=\frac{n\alpha\beta(s+n)}{s^2(s+1)}.
$$

The [latent variable](../../../../../../../latent-variable.md) encodes the first draw increasingly accurately.
