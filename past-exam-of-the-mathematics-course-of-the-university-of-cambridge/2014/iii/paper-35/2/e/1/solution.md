Start with the [Beta distribution](../../../../../../../beta-distribution.md) draw in the [Beta-binomial exchangeable coupling](../../../../../../../beta-binomial-exchangeable-coupling.md). Write $s=\alpha+\beta$ and

$$
m=\frac\alpha s,\qquad v=\frac{\alpha\beta}{s^2(s+1)}.
$$

These are the [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) and [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) of $p_M$. The fixed $n$ in this coupling is a dependence parameter chosen by the investigator, not necessarily the observed number of cups. The four numbered stages together address the single printed correlation request.
