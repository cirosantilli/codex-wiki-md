The next [Beta distribution](../../../../../../../beta-distribution.md) has conditional [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) and [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md)

$$
\mathbb E[p_T\mid X]=\frac{\alpha+X}{s+n},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(p_T\mid X)=\frac{(\alpha+X)(\beta+n-X)}{(s+n)^2(s+n+1)}.
$$

For large $n$, its [expected value](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) approaches $X/n$, while its [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) is at most $1/[4(s+n+1)]$. The final draw therefore stays close to the first. These are [prior distributions](../../../../../../../prior-probability.md) for the cup probabilities: the auxiliary count is a device for constructing dependence, rather than additional observed tea data.
