↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [law of iterated expectation](../../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) yields

$$
\mathbb E[p_T\mid p_M]=\frac{\alpha+n p_M}{s+n},\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(p_M,p_T)=\frac n{s+n}v.
$$

One may obtain $\operatorname{Var}(p_T)=v$ either from its unchanged [marginal distribution](../../../../../../../marginal-distribution.md) proved below or directly from the [law of total variance](../../../../../../../law-of-total-variance.md): the variance of its conditional mean is $nv/(s+n)$ and its expected conditional variance is $sv/(s+n)$. Hence the [correlation coefficient](../../../../../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) is exactly

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Corr}(p_M,p_T)=\frac n{n+\alpha+\beta}\longrightarrow1.}
$$

Also $\mathbb E[(p_T-p_M)^2]=2v s/(s+n)\to0$. **The two probabilities become close while keeping their original beta marginals.**
