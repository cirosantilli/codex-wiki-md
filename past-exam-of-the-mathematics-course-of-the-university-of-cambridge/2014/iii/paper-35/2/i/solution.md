<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Two [exchangeable random variables](../../../../../../exchangeable-random-variables.md) satisfy $(X,Y)\overset d=(Y,X)$: their [joint probability distribution](../../../../../../joint-probability-distribution.md) is unchanged by swapping the coordinates. Thus for every measurable set $A$,

$$
\mathbb P(X\in A)=\mathbb P((X,Y)\in A\times\mathcal Y)
=\mathbb P((Y,X)\in A\times\mathcal Y)=\mathbb P(Y\in A).
$$

**[Exchangeability](../../../../../../exchangeable-random-variables.md) implies identical [marginal distributions](../../../../../../marginal-distribution.md)**, but does not imply [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md).
