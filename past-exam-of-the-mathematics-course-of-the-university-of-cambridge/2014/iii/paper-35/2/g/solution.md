↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Using the conditional [Beta distribution](../../../../../../beta-distribution.md) [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value.md) and the [law of iterated expectation](../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md),

$$
\mathbb E[p_T]=\mathbb E\left[\frac{\alpha+X}{s+n}\right]
=\frac{\alpha+n\alpha/s}{s+n}
=\boxed{\frac\alpha{\alpha+\beta}}.
$$

Equality of the [expected values](../../../../../../expected-value.md) alone does not prove equality of the [marginal distributions](../../../../../../marginal-distribution.md); the later [exchangeability](../../../../../../exchangeable-random-variables.md) argument does.
