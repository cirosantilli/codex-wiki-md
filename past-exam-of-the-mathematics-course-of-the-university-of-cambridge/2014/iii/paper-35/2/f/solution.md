<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Apply the [law of iterated expectation](../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) to the auxiliary [binomial distribution](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=\mathbb E[\mathbb E[X\mid p_M]]=n\mathbb E[p_M]=\frac{n\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}.}
$$

This expectation is over the full [prior distribution](../../../../../../prior-probability.md) construction, not over a particular observed auxiliary count.
