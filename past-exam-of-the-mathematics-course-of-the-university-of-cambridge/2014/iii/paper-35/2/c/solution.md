<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Independent [Jeffreys priors](../../../../../../jeffreys-prior.md) and the two independent [binomial distribution](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) likelihood factors give, by [Beta-binomial conjugacy](../../../../../../beta-binomial-conjugacy.md),

$$
\boxed{p_M\mid\mathcal D\sim\operatorname{Beta}(7/2,3/2),\qquad p_T\mid\mathcal D\sim\operatorname{Beta}(3/2,7/2).}
$$

The [Bayesian posteriors](../../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) remain independent because each observation factor involves only its own probability. Their [posterior means](../../../../../../posterior-mean.md) are respectively **$0.7$ and $0.3$**. Notice that $p_T$ is the probability of saying milk first when tea was first, rather than the probability of a correct tea identification.
