For each auxiliary count $x$, the correctly normalized joint expression above is symmetric in $p_M,p_T$. Summing it over $x=0,\ldots,n$ preserves that symmetry, so these are [exchangeable random variables](../../../../../../exchangeable-random-variables.md). Their [marginal distributions](../../../../../../marginal-distribution.md) are therefore identical. Since $p_M$ was generated from a [Beta distribution](../../../../../../beta-distribution.md),

$$
\boxed{p_T\sim\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\beta).}
$$

The [Beta-binomial exchangeable coupling](../../../../../../beta-binomial-exchangeable-coupling.md) changes the dependence, while leaving both [prior distributions](../../../../../../prior-probability.md) unchanged.
