<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $q_0=n_0$, $q_1=n_0/c^2$ and $y=\overline y$. These are the two prior [precision parameters](../../../../../../precision-parameter.md). Completing the square in $n(\beta-y)^2+q_i\beta^2$ gives the [normal-normal conjugacy](../../../../../../normal-normal-conjugacy-with-known-observation-variance.md) update

$$
\boxed{\beta\mid y,H_i\sim N\left(\frac{n}{n+q_i}y,\frac1{n+q_i}\right),\qquad i=0,1.}
$$

Thus the [posterior mean](../../../../../../posterior-mean.md) is a precision-weighted average of the observed mean and the prior center zero. The [posterior variance](../../../../../../posterior-variance.md) is the reciprocal of the total precision.
