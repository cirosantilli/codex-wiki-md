<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At zero a centered [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) with [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) $\sigma$ has density $1/(\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma)$. The two prior standard deviations are $1/\sqrt{n_0}$ and $c/\sqrt{n_0}$, giving

$$
\boxed{p_0(0)/p_1(0)=c.}
$$

The wider [prior distribution](../../../../../../prior-probability.md) has a lower density at its center.
