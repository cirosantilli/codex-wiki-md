<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Divide the two [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) predictive densities to obtain the [Bayes factor](../../../../../../bayes-factor.md)

$$
\boxed{B_{01}(y)=\sqrt{\frac{V_1}{V_0}}\exp\left[-\frac{y^2}{2}\left(\frac1{V_0}-\frac1{V_1}\right)\right].}
$$

At $y=0$ this becomes

$$
\boxed{B_{01}(0)=\sqrt{\frac{n_0/n+c^2}{n_0/n+1}}.}
$$

The wider [prior distribution](../../../../../../prior-probability.md) spreads its predictive mass over more possible means, giving the narrower model more [Bayesian model evidence](../../../../../../bayesian-model-evidence.md) for observations very near zero. Away from zero, the exponential term opposes that factor.
