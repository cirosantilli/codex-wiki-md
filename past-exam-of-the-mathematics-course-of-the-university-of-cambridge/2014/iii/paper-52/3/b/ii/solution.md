<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The change to [ingoing Kerr coordinates](../../../../../../../ingoing-kerr-coordinates.md) adds functions of $r$ to $t$ and $\phi$, and leaves $r,\theta$ unchanged. Differentiating at fixed $r,\theta,\phi$ therefore gives $\partial_t v=1$ and $\partial_t\chi=0$. Differentiating at fixed $r,\theta,t$ gives $\partial_\phi\chi=1$ and $\partial_\phi v=0$. Hence the two [Killing vector fields](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) are

$$
\boxed{k=\partial_v,\qquad m=\partial_\chi.}
$$

They remain the stationary and axial [Killing vector fields](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md); the coordinate change does not mix their generators with $\partial_r$.
