<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An isolated uncharged collapsing star initially has higher [multipole moments](../../../../../../multipole-moment.md) and possibly time-dependent motion. The changing exterior emits [gravitational waves](../../../../../../gravitational-wave.md), carrying away energy and nonspherical structure. Perturbations of the final [black hole](../../../../../../black-hole.md) decay, so the late exterior is expected to approach a [stationary spacetime](../../../../../../stationary-spacetime.md).

Under the regularity, asymptotic flatness, and connected-horizon hypotheses of the [black-hole uniqueness theorem](../../../../../../black-hole-uniqueness-theorem.md), a stationary four-dimensional vacuum [black hole](../../../../../../black-hole.md) is a [Kerr black hole](../../../../../../kerr-black-hole.md). Since the electric charge is zero, its intrinsic parameters are

$$
\boxed{M\quad\text{and}\quad J,\qquad a=J/M.}
$$

The [black-hole no-hair theorem](../../../../../../black-hole-no-hair-theorem.md) expresses the loss of independently specifiable higher multipoles: those of the final [Kerr black hole](../../../../../../kerr-black-hole.md) are determined by $M,J$. The direction of the rotation axis can be chosen by orienting the coordinates and is not an additional intrinsic parameter of the geometry.

This is a statement about the settled, isolated exterior in classical [general relativity](../../../../../../general-relativity-split.md), conditional on settling and the hypotheses of the [black-hole uniqueness theorem](../../../../../../black-hole-uniqueness-theorem.md). It does not describe the entire radiating collapse spacetime with only two numbers, nor does it extend unchanged to additional long-range matter fields.
