<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [laws of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../laws-of-black-hole-mechanics.md) initially relate geometric quantities in a way resembling [thermodynamics](../../../../../../thermodynamics-split.md). [Hawking radiation](../../../../../../hawking-radiation.md) supplies a physical temperature: in units $c=1$, while displaying $G,\hbar,k_B$,

$$
\boxed{T_H=\frac{\hbar\kappa}{2\pi k_B},\qquad S_{\rm BH}=\frac{k_B A}{4G\hbar}.}
$$

The [Hawking temperature](../../../../../../hawking-temperature.md) $T_H$ is measured with the stationary time normalized at infinity. The radiation's thermal occupation factor is physically observable; propagation to infinity also introduces [greybody factors](../../../../../../greybody-factor.md), so the distant spectrum need not be a perfect blackbody spectrum at every frequency.

The [Zeroth law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../zeroth-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md) says that [surface gravity](../../../../../../surface-gravity.md) $\kappa$ is constant on a stationary [Killing horizon](../../../../../../killing-horizon.md) under its standard hypotheses. Through the [Hawking temperature](../../../../../../hawking-temperature.md), this becomes uniform equilibrium temperature. The [First law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../first-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md) is

$$
\delta M=\frac\kappa{8\pi G}\delta A+\Omega_H\delta J+\Phi_H\delta Q.
$$

Using $T_H$ identifies its area term as $T_H\delta S_{\rm BH}$. Integrating $\delta S_{\rm BH}=k_B\delta A/(4G\hbar)$ gives the [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](../../../../../../bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md), up to an additive constant. Thus $M$ is the energy, the angular and charge terms are work terms, and the geometrical law is the ordinary thermodynamic first law with a fixed entropy normalization.

The [second law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../second-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md), or [Hawking's area theorem](../../../../../../hawking-s-area-theorem.md), gives nondecreasing area in the classical setting with the requisite energy and predictability assumptions. It corresponds to increasing [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](../../../../../../bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md). Semiclassical [black-hole evaporation](../../../../../../black-hole-evaporation.md) can decrease the area: the classical [null energy condition](../../../../../../null-energy-condition.md) need not hold for the quantum expectation of the [stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../stress-energy-tensor.md). The appropriate extension is the [generalized second law](../../../../../../generalized-second-law.md), that $S_{\rm BH}+S_{\rm outside}$ does not decrease, with the exterior entropy and its renormalization treated consistently. Hawking's temperature identification motivates this law; thermality alone does not prove every form of it.

The [third law of black-hole mechanics](../../../../../../third-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md) is the unattainability, by an admissible finite physical process, of zero [surface gravity](../../../../../../surface-gravity.md). With [Hawking temperature](../../../../../../hawking-temperature.md) it becomes unattainability of absolute zero. It is not the assertion that an [extremal black hole](../../../../../../extremal-black-hole.md) has vanishing entropy: its area can remain nonzero when $\kappa=0$.

For a [Schwarzschild black hole](../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md), $\kappa=1/(4GM)$ and $A=16\pi G^2M^2$, giving $T_H=\hbar/(8\pi k_BGM)$ and $S_{\rm BH}=4\pi k_BGM^2/\hbar$. Their product satisfies $T_H\,dS_{\rm BH}=dM$, explicitly checking the first law. **Quantum radiation turns the temperature and entropy in the mechanical analogy into physical thermodynamic quantities.**
