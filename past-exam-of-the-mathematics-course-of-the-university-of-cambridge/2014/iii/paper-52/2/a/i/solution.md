<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [null geodesic congruence](../../../../../../../null-geodesic-congruence.md) is a smooth local family of [null geodesics](../../../../../../../null-geodesic.md), with one generator through each point of the region being described. Choose an [affine parameter](../../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) $\lambda$ on each generator and write $U^a=dx^a/d\lambda$. Before a caustic, $U$ is a smooth, nonzero [null vector](../../../../../../../null-vector.md) field satisfying $U^aU_a=0$ and $U^b\nabla_bU^a=0$.

With $B^a{}_b=\nabla_bU^a$, compatibility of the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) with the [metric](../../../../../../../metric.md) gives

$$
U_aB^a{}_b=U_a\nabla_bU^a=\frac12\nabla_b(U^aU_a)=0.
$$

The [geodesic equation](../../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) with [affine parameter](../../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) gives the other contraction:

$$
B^a{}_bU^b=U^b\nabla_bU^a=0.
$$

Thus **both contractions vanish**. Nullness supplies the first identity, and affine parametrization supplies the second; a general nonaffine tangent would instead have $B^a{}_bU^b=\kappa U^a$.
