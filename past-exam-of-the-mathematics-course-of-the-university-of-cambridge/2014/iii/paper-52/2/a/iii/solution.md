<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [screen-space projector](../../../../../../../screen-space-projector.md) annihilates both [null vectors](../../../../../../../null-vector.md): $P^a{}_bU^b=P^a{}_bN^b=0$, and satisfies $P^2=P$. Its image is the two-dimensional spacelike screen orthogonal to $U,N$. The screen metric is

$$
q_{ab}=g_{ab}+U_aN_b+N_aU_b.
$$

It is positive definite on that screen. Projecting both indices of $B_{ab}=\nabla_bU_a$ gives the [optical tensor](../../../../../../../optical-tensor.md) $\widehat B_{ab}=P_a{}^cP_b{}^dB_{cd}$.

Define its three parts by

$$
\boxed{\theta=q^{ab}\widehat B_{ab},\qquad \omega_{ab}=\widehat B_{[ab]},\qquad \sigma_{ab}=\widehat B_{(ab)}-\frac12\theta q_{ab}.}
$$

Thus $\widehat B_{ab}=\tfrac12\theta q_{ab}+\sigma_{ab}+\omega_{ab}$. The [null expansion](../../../../../../../null-expansion.md) $\theta$ is its trace and equals $d\log\mathcal A/d\lambda$ for the infinitesimal beam area $\mathcal A$. The [null shear](../../../../../../../null-shear.md) $\sigma$ is symmetric and trace-free, measuring shape change at fixed first-order area. The [null twist](../../../../../../../null-twist.md) $\omega$, also called rotation, is antisymmetric and measures failure of screen directions to remain hypersurface-orthogonal. In $D$ dimensions replace $1/2$ by $1/(D-2)$. Here and below expansion means the trace, rather than its average over the screen dimensions.
