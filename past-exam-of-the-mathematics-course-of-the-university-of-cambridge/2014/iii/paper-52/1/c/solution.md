<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The physical argument for the [Penrose inequality](../../../../../../penrose-inequality.md) combines [weak cosmic censorship conjecture](../../../../../../weak-cosmic-censorship-conjecture.md), the [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md), and relaxation to a stationary [black hole](../../../../../../black-hole.md). Work in [geometrized units](../../../../../../geometrized-units.md). Let $M_f$ and $A_f$ be the final [Kerr black hole](../../../../../../kerr-black-hole.md) mass and horizon area. Positive energy radiated to infinity gives $E_{\rm ADM}\geq M_f$, where $E_{\rm ADM}$ is the initial [ADM energy](../../../../../../arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md). For a [Kerr black hole](../../../../../../kerr-black-hole.md) with $a_f=J_f/M_f$,

$$
A_f=8\pi M_f\left(M_f+\sqrt{M_f^2-a_f^2}\right)\leq16\pi M_f^2.
$$

If the initial [apparent horizon](../../../../../../apparent-horizon.md) obeys the necessary [apparent-horizon area comparison](../../../../../../apparent-horizon-area-comparison.md) with the enclosing [event horizon](../../../../../../event-horizon.md), and [Hawking's area theorem](../../../../../../hawking-s-area-theorem.md) applies during the evolution, then

$$
A_{\rm app}\leq A_{\rm EH,initial}\leq A_f\leq16\pi M_f^2\leq16\pi E_{\rm ADM}^2.
$$

Consequently the anticipated answer, under those additional hypotheses, is

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm ADM}\geq\sqrt{\frac{A_{\rm app}}{16\pi}}.}
$$

The bound is saturated by a nonrotating [Schwarzschild black hole](../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) with no energy loss. Rotation or outgoing radiation makes the argument's inequalities stricter.

There is an essential qualification: inclusion inside an [event horizon](../../../../../../event-horizon.md) does not by itself compare areas. An arbitrary [apparent horizon](../../../../../../apparent-horizon.md) on general, non-time-symmetric initial data need not satisfy the displayed [apparent-horizon area comparison](../../../../../../apparent-horizon-area-comparison.md); the unqualified version with its area is not universally true, even with the [dominant energy condition](../../../../../../dominant-energy-condition.md). On time-symmetric data the relevant outermost [minimal surface](../../../../../../minimal-surface.md) is an [outer area-minimizing surface](../../../../../../outer-area-minimizing-surface.md), as used in the [Riemannian Penrose inequality](../../../../../../riemannian-penrose-inequality.md), with nonnegative [scalar curvature](../../../../../../scalar-curvature.md). In more general formulations an appropriate enclosing-area quantity is needed. **The physical expectation is conditional on this area comparison**, as well as on censorship, predictability, settling, and the energy assumptions; the mere presence of a [trapped surface](../../../../../../trapped-surface.md) does not supply every step.
