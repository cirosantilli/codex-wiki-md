<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [conservative forcing in averaged oscillator equations](../../../../../../../conservative-forcing-in-averaged-oscillator-equations.md), $f=f(y)$, the average determining $R_T$ vanishes. If $F'=f$, then

$$
f(R\cos\phi)\sin\phi=-\frac1R\frac{d}{d\phi}F(R\cos\phi),
$$

whose [period average](../../../../../../../period-average.md) is zero. Thus $R=R_0$ to this order, while a position-dependent force generally shifts the [oscillation phase](../../../../../../../phase-waves.md) and [frequency](../../../../../../../frequency.md). The exact equation also conserves the [energy](../../../../../../../energy.md) $\tfrac12\dot y^2+\tfrac12y^2-\varepsilon F(y)$, consistent with the absence of [amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) drift for bounded orbits.

For the cubic [position](../../../../../../../position.md) force, $\langle f\cos\phi\rangle=R^3\langle\cos^4\phi\rangle=3R^3/8$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{R(T)=R_0,\qquad \theta(T)=\theta_0-\frac38R_0^2T}.
$$

The leading oscillation is $R_0\cos[(1-3\varepsilon R_0^2/8)t+\theta_0]$. For positive $\varepsilon$ it is a softening cubic oscillator, obtained by weakly perturbing a [harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md). The weak-force condition is $\varepsilon R_0^2\ll1$; the constant $R$ is the leading harmonic [amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md), not a claim that the exact waveform remains sinusoidal.
