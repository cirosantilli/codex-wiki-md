<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

[Critical exponents](../../../../../../critical-exponent.md) describe leading power-law singularities as the [reduced temperature](../../../../../../reduced-temperature.md) $t=(T-T_c)/T_c$ and the [conjugate field](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md) tend to zero. Their amplitudes depend on microscopic details, while the exponents are characteristic of a [universality class](../../../../../../universality-class.md). Define $m_0\sim(-t)^\beta$ on a selected ordered branch, $\chi\sim|t|^{-\gamma}$ at zero field, $C_{V,s}\sim|t|^{-\alpha}$ for the singular heat capacity, and $m(t=0,h)\sim\operatorname{sgn}(h)|h|^{1/\delta}$. Also define $\xi\sim|t|^{-\nu}$ and $G(r,t=0)\sim r^{-(D-2+\eta)}$. In these statements $\beta$ is the [order-parameter critical exponent](../../../../../../order-parameter-critical-exponent.md), not inverse temperature.

Assume $r=r_t t$ with $r_t>0$ and a nonzero positive quartic coefficient $u$. Minimizing the quartic potential gives $m_0=\sqrt{-r/u}$, hence $\beta=1/2$. Differentiating its [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) gives

$$
\chi=(r+3um^2)^{-1}=\begin{cases}r^{-1},&t>0,\\(-2r)^{-1},&t<0\text{ on a selected phase},\end{cases}
$$

so $\gamma=1$ with different amplitudes. At $r=0$, $h=um^3$, giving $\delta=3$. The minimized potential is zero above the transition and $-r^2/(4u)$ below, so its second temperature derivative has a finite jump: $\alpha=0$.

The quadratic fluctuation kernel around the stable uniform minimum is $\kappa p^2+V''(m_0)$, so $\xi^2=\kappa/V''(m_0)$. Since $V''$ is $r$ above and $-2r$ below, $\nu=1/2$. Its critical momentum dependence is $p^{-2}$, giving $\eta=0$. Thus the ordinary [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md) are

$$
\boxed{\alpha=0,\quad\beta=\tfrac12,\quad\gamma=1,\quad\delta=3,\quad\nu=\tfrac12,\quad\eta=0.}
$$

These are predictions of the [Landau approximation](../../../../../../landau-approximation.md), whose validity is constrained by the [Ginzburg criterion](../../../../../../ginzburg-criterion.md); they need not be the exponents of the fluctuating theory below its [upper critical dimension](../../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md).
