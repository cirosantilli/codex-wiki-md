<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Along an isolated two-phase coexistence line, two distinct stable minima have equal [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). If this line ends at an ordinary interior endpoint without encountering another phase, the distinction between the two phases must disappear: their minima and the intervening barrier merge. For separated nondegenerate minima, their stationary values are smooth functions of the controls. The difference $\Delta V=V(m_1)-V(m_2)$ has $\partial_h\Delta V=-m_1+m_2\ne0$. The [implicit function theorem](../../../../../../implicit-function-theorem.md) therefore continues the equation $\Delta V=0$ as a local coexistence curve. It cannot end while those distinct stable minima persist.

At the merger the potential satisfies

$$
\boxed{V'(m_c)=V''(m_c)=V^{(3)}(m_c)=0,\qquad V^{(4)}(m_c)>0.}
$$

The third-derivative condition is essential: a stationary zero-curvature point with nonzero third derivative is an inflection, not a stable equilibrium. Shifting to $\psi=m-m_c$ gives an ordinary quartic [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) with two independent control directions, temperature-like and field-like. The curvature and the order-parameter discontinuity vanish, the [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) diverges, and the [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) diverges. **The endpoint is therefore associated with a continuous transition.** This is the [critical endpoint of a quartic Landau free energy](../../../../../../critical-endpoint-of-a-quartic-landau-free-energy.md) mechanism.

In the symmetric quartic example, the coexistence line is $h=0,r<0$. Its jump $2\sqrt{-r/u}$ vanishes at $(r,h)=(0,0)$, where $V=um^4/4$. This supplies a concrete realization. The word “must” requires the stated ordinary-endpoint assumptions: a first-order line can instead meet a third phase, a multicritical point, a boundary of parameter space, or continue without a finite endpoint. A [spinodal point](../../../../../../spinodal-point.md) alone is not an equilibrium critical endpoint.
