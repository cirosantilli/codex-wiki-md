↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Substitute $R=r^{\pi/\alpha}$ from part (a) into part (c). The [Brownian wedge-exit probability](../../../../../../brownian-wedge-exit-probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P_1\{B[0,T(r)]\subset W_\alpha\}
=\frac4\pi\arctan\!\left(r^{-\pi/\alpha}\right)
=\frac2\pi\arctan\!\left(\frac{2r^{\pi/\alpha}}{r^{2\pi/\alpha}-1}\right).}
$$

It tends to $1$ as $r\downarrow1$, and is asymptotic to $(4/\pi)r^{-\pi/\alpha}$ as $r\to\infty$. The exponent reflects how the [conformal map](../../../../../../conformal-map.md) stretches wedge angles; wider wedges give slower decay.
