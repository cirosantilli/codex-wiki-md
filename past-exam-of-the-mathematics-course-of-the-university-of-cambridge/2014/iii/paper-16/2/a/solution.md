<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [symplectic manifold](../../../../../../symplectic-manifold.md) is a [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) $M$ equipped with a [differential two-form](../../../../../../2-form.md) $\omega$ such that

$$
\boxed{d\omega=0,\qquad\omega_p(v,w)=0\text{ for all }w\in T_pM\Longrightarrow v=0.}
$$

The first condition says it is a [closed differential form](../../../../../../closed-differential-form.md); the second says it is pointwise [nondegenerate](../../../../../../nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md). A nondegenerate skew-symmetric matrix has even size, so the dimension is $2n$. Equivalently, $\omega^n$ is a nowhere-zero top-degree [differential form](../../../../../../differential-form-split.md). It supplies an [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) and the [volume form](../../../../../../volume-form.md) $\omega^n/n!$. These are the defining conditions on the [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md).
