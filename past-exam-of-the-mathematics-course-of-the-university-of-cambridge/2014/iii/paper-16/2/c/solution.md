<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**There are no symplectic structures on the [Möbius strip](../../../../../../mobius-band.md).** A [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md) on a surface would be a nowhere-vanishing two-form, hence would provide an [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md). The [Möbius strip](../../../../../../mobius-band.md) is nonorientable: transport around its core reverses a transverse direction and therefore reverses any local [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md). This is incompatible with such a two-form. The argument applies whether the boundary is included or only the interior is considered.
