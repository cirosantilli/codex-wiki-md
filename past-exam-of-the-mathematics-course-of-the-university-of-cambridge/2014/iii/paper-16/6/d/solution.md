<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**No nonconstant [J-holomorphic curve](../../../../../../pseudoholomorphic-curve.md) with this closed connected source exists.** The [standard symplectic form](../../../../../../standard-symplectic-form.md) on $\mathbb R^{2n}$ is exact; for instance

$$
\omega_0=d\lambda_0,\qquad\lambda_0=\frac12\sum_j(x_jdy_j-y_jdx_j).
$$

The [Energy identity for a J-holomorphic curve](../../../../../../energy-identity-for-a-j-holomorphic-curve.md) and the [Generalized Stokes theorem](../../../../../../generalized-stokes-theorem.md) give

$$
E(u)=\int_\Sigma u^*\omega_0
=\int_\Sigma d(u^*\lambda_0)
=\int_{\partial\Sigma}u^*\lambda_0=0.
$$

The target metric from the [compatible almost complex structure](../../../../../../compatible-almost-complex-structure.md) is positive definite, so the nonnegative continuous energy density must vanish everywhere. Thus $du=0$. Connectedness of $\Sigma$ makes $u$ constant. This argument uses exactness and compatibility, and works even when $J$ is nonintegrable; it does not require the ordinary holomorphic maximum principle on the target.
