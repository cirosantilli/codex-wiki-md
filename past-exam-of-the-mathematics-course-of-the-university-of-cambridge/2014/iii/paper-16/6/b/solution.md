<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [Riemann surface](../../../../../../riemann-surfaces.md) $(\Sigma,j)$ and an [almost complex manifold](../../../../../../almost-complex-manifold.md) $(M,J)$, a [J-holomorphic curve](../../../../../../pseudoholomorphic-curve.md) is a [smooth](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) map $u:\Sigma\to M$ satisfying

$$
\boxed{du\circ j=J\circ du,\qquad\bar\partial_Ju:=\frac12(du+J\circ du\circ j)=0.}
$$

Thus its differential is complex-linear at every point. In oriented local coordinates $s,t$ with $j\partial_s=\partial_t$, this is $u_t=Ju_s$, equivalently $u_s+Ju_t=0$. No integrability of the target [almost complex structure](../../../../../../almost-complex-manifold.md) is required. Constant maps satisfy the definition; some usages reserve the word curve for nonconstant maps, so that restriction should be stated separately when intended.
