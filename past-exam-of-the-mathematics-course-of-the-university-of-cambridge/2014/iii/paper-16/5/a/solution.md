<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Hamiltonian group action](../../../../../../hamiltonian-group-action.md) is a [smooth](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) [Lie group action](../../../../../../lie-group-action.md) of $G$ on $(M,\omega)$ by [symplectomorphisms](../../../../../../symplectomorphism.md), together with an equivariant [moment map](../../../../../../moment-map.md) $\mu:M\to\mathfrak g^*$. For $\xi\in\mathfrak g$, let $\xi_M(p)=\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_0\exp(t\xi)\cdot p$ be its [fundamental vector field](../../../../../../fundamental-vector-field.md). In our sign convention the defining identities are

$$
\boxed{d\langle\mu,\xi\rangle=\iota_{\xi_M}\omega,\qquad
\mu(g\cdot p)=\operatorname{Ad}_g^*\mu(p).}
$$

Here the left [coadjoint action](../../../../../../coadjoint-representation.md) means $(\operatorname{Ad}_g^*\nu)(\xi)=\nu(\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\xi)$. Each component $\mu^\xi$ is therefore a [Hamiltonian function](../../../../../../hamiltonian-function.md) for the corresponding infinitesimal action. Equivariance is part of the definition; merely requiring each infinitesimal generator to be a [Hamiltonian vector field](../../../../../../hamiltonian-vector-field.md) is the weaker condition of a [weakly Hamiltonian action](../../../../../../weakly-hamiltonian-action.md). Reversing the defining sign of [Hamiltonian vector fields](../../../../../../hamiltonian-vector-field.md) reverses the [moment map](../../../../../../moment-map.md) sign as well.
