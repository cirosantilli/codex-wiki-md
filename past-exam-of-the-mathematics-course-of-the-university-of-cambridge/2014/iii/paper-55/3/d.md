The chosen [opacity](../../../../../opacity.md) law makes $\kappa L_r/m_r=\alpha L/M$ spatially constant. This closes the [Eddington standard model](../../../../../eddington-standard-model.md) relation between its radiation-pressure and total-pressure gradients. The four roman-numbered proofs use the whole model, not only this [opacity](../../../../../opacity.md) assumption.
