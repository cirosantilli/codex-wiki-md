<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Divide the radiation-pressure gradient by the total-pressure gradient. [Radiative diffusion in a star](../../../../../../radiative-diffusion-in-a-star.md) and [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) give

$$
\frac{dP_r}{dr}=-\frac{\kappa\rho L_r}{4\pi c r^2},\qquad
\frac{dP}{dr}=-\frac{Gm_r\rho}{r^2},\qquad
\frac{dP_r}{dP}=\frac{\kappa L_r}{4\pi cGm_r}
=\frac{\alpha L}{4\pi cGM}=:f.
$$

This last quantity is independent of radius under the specified [opacity](../../../../../../opacity.md) assumption. Integration gives $P_r=fP+C$. With the standard idealized zero-pressure outer boundary, where both components vanish, $C=0$. The [stellar gas-pressure fraction](../../../../../../stellar-gas-pressure-fraction.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\beta=\frac{P_g}{P}=1-f
=1-\frac{\alpha L}{4\pi cGM},}
$$

**constant throughout the model**. This is the [Eddington standard model](../../../../../../eddington-standard-model.md) idealization. A finite photospheric [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) offset would need its boundary treatment; the differential relation alone does not set the integration constant to zero.
