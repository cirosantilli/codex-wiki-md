<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Expand $\psi=\epsilon\psi_1+\epsilon^2\psi_2+\cdots$ and $\mathcal U=\epsilon^2\mathcal U_2+\cdots$. The [screened first-order transverse sheet flow](../../../../../../screened-first-order-transverse-sheet-flow.md) has $\psi_1=f(y)\sin\xi$. Set $s=\sqrt{1+A^2}$. The two decaying roots of the [Brinkman swimming sheet](../../../../../../brinkman-swimming-sheet.md) operator are $1$ and $s$, and $f(0)=1$, $f'(0)=0$ select

$$
\boxed{f(y)=\frac{s e^{-y}-e^{-sy}}{s-1},\qquad
\mathbf u_1=(f'(y)\sin\xi,-f(y)\cos\xi).}
$$

The physical first-order flow is $\epsilon(\omega/k)\mathbf u_1$ in scaled coordinates. The dimensionless first-order [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md), determined from momentum balance, is

$$
\boxed{p_1=-s(s+1)e^{-y}\cos\xi.}
$$

Although the expression for $f$ appears singular at $A=0$, its continuous limit is $f=(1+y)e^{-y}$, the ordinary [transverse mode of a Taylor swimming sheet](../../../../../../transverse-mode-of-a-taylor-swimming-sheet.md).
