<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On the axis of an identical [stresslet](../../../../../../force-dipole-flow.md), $\mathbf r=\ell\mathbf e$ gives [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $S\mathbf e/(4\pi\mu\ell^2)$, while the point on the opposite axis has its negative. Their identical intrinsic swimming velocities cancel from the relative motion. The [axial repulsion of pusher stresslets](../../../../../../axial-repulsion-of-pusher-stresslets.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{\dot\ell=\frac S{2\pi\mu\ell^2}>0,\qquad
\ell(t)=\left(\ell_0^3+\frac{3S}{2\pi\mu}t\right)^{1/3}.}
$$

This point-singularity approximation assumes the bodies are separated by distances large compared with their own sizes.
