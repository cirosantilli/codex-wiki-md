↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The same affine transformation of the [normal distribution](../../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{W_n\mid\delta=\delta^*\sim N\left(\frac{\sqrt n\delta^*}{\sigma},1\right).}
$$

Its [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) stays one while its mean moves positively. This is the exact alternative distribution used to calculate [statistical power](../../../../../../../statistical-power.md). The square, if used instead, has a [noncentral chi-squared distribution](../../../../../../../noncentral-chi-squared-distribution.md) with one degree of freedom and noncentrality $n(\delta^*)^2/\sigma^2$.
