A rank-$r$ [holomorphic vector bundle](../../../../../../holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) is a [complex manifold](../../../../../../complex-manifold.md) $E$ with a holomorphic projection to $X$ and local holomorphic trivializations $E|_{U_i}\cong U_i\times\mathbb C^r$ that are complex-linear on each fibre. Its transition maps have the form $(x,v)\mapsto(x,g_{ij}(x)v)$ with holomorphic $g_{ij}:U_i\cap U_j\to GL_r(\mathbb C)$ satisfying the cocycle identities.

The [holomorphic Picard group](../../../../../../holomorphic-picard-group.md) consists of isomorphism classes of [holomorphic line bundles](../../../../../../holomorphic-line-bundle.md), with [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md) as multiplication, the trivial [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) as identity and the [dual bundle](../../../../../../dual-bundle.md) as inverse. The classification on the [complex projective line](../../../../../../complex-projective-line.md) gives

$$
 \boxed{\operatorname{Pic}(\mathbb P^1)\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad
 n\longmapsto[\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(n)].}
$$

The inverse is degree; $\mathcal O(1)$ is the positive generator.
