<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A large spherical region contains $N\sim V_R/\ell^3$ energy-containing eddies. If their [hydrodynamic impulses](../../../../../../hydrodynamic-impulse.md) have zero mean, finite [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) and sufficiently short-range correlations, the mean-square sum grows as $N$, rather than $N^2$. In the ideal independent case,

$$
\left\langle\left|\sum_{a=1}^N\mathbf I_a\right|^2\right\rangle
=N\langle|\mathbf I_a|^2\rangle.
$$

The spherical [impulse](../../../../../../impulse.md)-[momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) relation makes the integrated velocity proportional to this sum for isolated enclosed structures. In a full field, external dipolar velocities and boundary effects can change the coefficient; they do not alter the volume scaling under these random-[impulse](../../../../../../impulse.md) statistics. Thus

$$
\boxed{\langle|\mathbf P_R|^2\rangle\propto V_R},\qquad
|\mathbf P_R|_{\rm rms}\propto V_R^{1/2}.
$$

It is a [random walk](../../../../../../random-walk.md) law for [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) fluctuations, not an extensive mean flow. Although the mean [impulse](../../../../../../impulse.md) is zero by isotropy, its [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) need not vanish. The [Saffman integral](../../../../../../saffman-integral.md) measures this large-volume [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md)-fluctuation density. If every eddy had exactly zero [impulse](../../../../../../impulse.md) or correlations enforced systematic cancellation, this leading density could vanish and a different small-wavenumber spectrum could result.
