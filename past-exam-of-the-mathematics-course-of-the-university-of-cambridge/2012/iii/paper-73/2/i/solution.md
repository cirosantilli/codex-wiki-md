[Internal intermittency](../../../../../../internal-intermittency.md) is the uneven distribution of activity within an already turbulent fluid: intense gradients and [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) occupy relatively small, fluctuating regions separated by much quieter regions. It concerns the internal field, rather than a turbulent/non-turbulent interface intermittently passing a probe.

[Integral-scale intermittency](../../../../../../integral-scale-intermittency.md) is the variability of the energy-containing eddies and their local or time-dependent energy supply. Its modulation occurs on lengths and times of order $\ell$ and $\ell/u$, can depend strongly on forcing or initial conditions, and changes the background intensity experienced by smaller eddies.

Equilibrium-range intermittency concerns the increasing fluctuations of local transfer and [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) as the averaging scale is reduced below the [integral scale of turbulence](../../../../../../integral-scale-of-turbulence.md). It appears as non-Gaussian velocity-gradient statistics, fluctuating coarse-grained [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) and anomalous [longitudinal structure function](../../../../../../longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md) exponents. It includes the [inertial range](../../../../../../inertial-range.md) and its continuation into the [dissipation range](../../../../../../dissipation-range.md), where [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) ultimately limits gradients. **Large-scale modulation varies the local energy supply; small-scale intermittency concentrates and redistributes that supply.** These effects can coexist, so they should not be identified simply because both broaden a distribution.
