<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose $R_0$ with $K\subset\{(y,w):|y|\leq R_0,\ |w|\leq R_0\}$ and let $A_0=\max_{|w|\leq R_0}|a(w)|$, which is finite by [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). Every point transported from $K$ satisfies

$$
|X(t)|\leq R_0+TA_0,\qquad |V(t)|\leq R_0
\quad(0\leq t\leq T).
$$

Hence any

$$
\boxed{R_T>\sqrt{(R_0+TA_0)^2+R_0^2}}
$$

contains the [support of a function](../../../../../../support.md) in the required [phase space](../../../../../../phase-space.md) ball throughout $[0,T]$. Equivalently, the image of the compact set $[0,T]\times K$ under the [characteristic flow map](../../../../../../characteristic-flow-map.md) is compact. This uniform support control puts the solution in $\mathcal C_T$ and justifies the following differentiations under the [integral](../../../../../../integral.md).
