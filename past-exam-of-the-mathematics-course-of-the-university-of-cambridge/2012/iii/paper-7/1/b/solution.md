<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $d[f(t,X(t),V(t))]/dt=0$ along each [characteristic curve](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md). Pulling the initial datum back by the inverse [characteristic flow map](../../../../../../characteristic-flow-map.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{f(t,x,v)=f_{\mathrm{in}}(x-ta(v),v).}
$$

Direct differentiation also gives $f_t=-a(v)\cdot\nabla_xf$, so this is a classical solution of the [transport equation](../../../../../../transport-equation.md). Composition with a [smooth function](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) makes $f$ smooth jointly in $(t,x,v)$; nonnegative initial data remain nonnegative.

If $K=\operatorname{supp}f_{\mathrm{in}}$, then $\operatorname{supp}f(t)=S_{0,t}(K)$. A continuous image of a [compact set](../../../../../../compact-space.md) is compact. Thus [compact support](../../../../../../compact-support.md) is preserved at every finite time; there is no claim of one fixed compact support for all $t\geq0$.
