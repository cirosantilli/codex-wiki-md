↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Yes.** Choose a global [differential one-form](../../../../../../one-form.md) $\beta$ with $d\beta=\sigma$, and use the [cotangent fiber translation](../../../../../../cotangent-fiber-translation.md)

$$
T_\beta(q,p)=(q,p+\beta_q).
$$

It is a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) with inverse $T_{-\beta}$, and $\pi\circ T_\beta=\pi$. Directly from the [Liouville one-form](../../../../../../canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md),

$$
T_\beta^*\alpha=\alpha+\pi^*\beta.
$$

The sign convention $\omega=-d\alpha$ therefore gives

$$
\boxed{T_\beta^*\omega_\sigma
=\omega-\pi^*(d\beta)+\pi^*\sigma=\omega.}
$$

Thus $T_\beta:(T^*X,\omega)\to(T^*X,\omega_\sigma)$ is the required [symplectomorphism](../../../../../../symplectomorphism.md). Equivalently, $T_{-\beta}^*\omega=\omega_\sigma$ gives the inverse direction. This [translation equivalence of exact twisted cotangent bundles](../../../../../../translation-equivalence-of-exact-twisted-cotangent-bundles.md) is global even when $X$ is not compact; no completeness argument for a [Moser theorem](../../../../../../moser-s-trick.md) flow is needed.
