<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [differential one-form](../../../../../../one-form.md) $\theta$ defines a smooth [section of a vector bundle](../../../../../../section-of-a-vector-bundle.md), and its [one-form graph](../../../../../../graph-of-a-differential-one-form.md) is an embedded copy of $X$ because $\pi\circ\theta=\operatorname{id}_X$. The defining property of the [Liouville one-form](../../../../../../canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) gives $\theta^*\alpha=\theta$. Consequently

$$
\theta^*\omega_\sigma=-d\theta+(\pi\circ\theta)^*\sigma=\sigma-d\theta.
$$

The [one-form graph](../../../../../../graph-of-a-differential-one-form.md) has dimension $n$, half the dimension of the [cotangent bundle](../../../../../../cotangent-bundle.md). It is therefore a [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md) precisely when this [pullback of a differential form](../../../../../../pullback-of-a-differential-form.md) vanishes:

$$
\boxed{\theta(X)\text{ is Lagrangian in }(T^*X,\omega_\sigma)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad d\theta=\sigma.}
$$

This is the [twisted Lagrangian graph criterion](../../../../../../twisted-lagrangian-graph-criterion.md).

Now suppose the projection restricts to a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) on a [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md) $L$. Its inverse followed by the inclusion defines a smooth section $s:X\to T^*X$, hence a global [differential one-form](../../../../../../one-form.md) $\theta$ with image $L$. The criterion forces $\sigma=d\theta$, so its class in [de Rham cohomology](../../../../../../de-rham-cohomology.md) is zero. Thus **$[\sigma]\ne0$ rules out every such Lagrangian section**. This [cohomological obstruction to a Lagrangian section](../../../../../../cohomological-obstruction-to-a-lagrangian-section.md) concerns graphs over the entire base; it does not claim that all [Lagrangian submanifolds](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md) are absent.
