<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

As $v\to\infty$, $[v(v-1)]^{-4/\kappa}\sim v^{-8/\kappa}$. Therefore

$$
s_\kappa(z)\sim
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{z^{1-8/\kappa}}{1-8/\kappa},&\kappa>8,\\
\log z,&\kappa=8.
\end{cases}
$$

Thus **every positive solution is unbounded when κ ≥ 8**, including the logarithmically divergent critical case. For $4<\kappa<8$, by contrast,

$$
S_\kappa:=s_\kappa(\infty)<\infty.
$$

For example the substitution $r=1-1/v$ identifies this normalizing constant as $B(1-4/\kappa,8/\kappa-1)$, with both [beta function](../../../../../../../beta-function.md) parameters positive.
