<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under [quota share reinsurance](../../../../../../quota-share-reinsurance.md), $S_I=\alpha S$ and $S_R=(1-\alpha)S$. Write $v=\operatorname{Var}(S)=\lambda\mathbb E[X^2]$. Then

$$
\operatorname{Var}(S_I)+\operatorname{Var}(S_R)
=v\bigl(\alpha^2+(1-\alpha)^2\bigr)
=\frac v2+2v\left(\alpha-\frac12\right)^2.
$$

The [variance-minimizing quota share split](../../../../../../variance-minimizing-quota-share-split.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\alpha^*=\frac12,\qquad \min\{\operatorname{Var}(S_I)+\operatorname{Var}(S_R)\}=\frac{\lambda\mathbb E[X^2]}2.}
$$

For positive claims and $\lambda>0$ this minimizer is unique. If the aggregate has zero [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md), every retained fraction gives the same zero objective.
