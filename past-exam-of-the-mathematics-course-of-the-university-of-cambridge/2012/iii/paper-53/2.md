↑ **Parent:** [Paper 53](../paper-53-split.md)

For a quantitative thermal-relic prediction, assume one ordinary weakly interacting [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) flavor: one populated [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) helicity and one [antineutrino](../../../../antineutrino.md) helicity, negligible chemical potential, early thermal equilibrium, and relativistic decoupling around the MeV epoch. Equal [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) and [antineutrino](../../../../antineutrino.md) populations imply no new lepton asymmetry. The [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) is maintained while the [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md) rate exceeds the expansion rate. After [neutrino decoupling](../../../../neutrino-decoupling.md), the distribution free streams, its momentum scale falls as $a^{-1}$, and its comoving number is conserved. A mass of $1\,\mathrm{keV}$ is negligible at [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](../../../../big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) temperatures, but the relic later becomes nonrelativistic. Its energy density then changes from radiation-like $a^{-4}$ scaling to matter-like $a^{-3}$ scaling. Stability prevents its removal by decay; decoupling while relativistic prevents a subsequent equilibrium Boltzmann suppression of its number.
