<h1 id="4/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Take the usual smooth late [dark energy](../../../../../../dark-energy.md), in particular a [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md), and hold the primordial perturbations fixed. Increasing its physical density while holding the matter and radiation densities fixed raises $H_0$ in a flat universe. It lowers $\Omega_{m0}$, but leaves the physical product $\Omega_{m0}H_0^2=\kappa\rho_{m0}/3$ fixed. It also leaves the early equality ratio $a_{\rm eq}=\rho_{r0}/\rho_{m0}$ and the equality wavenumber in $\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ essentially unchanged, provided [dark energy](../../../../../../dark-energy.md) remains negligible at equality. The early [cosmological transfer function](../../../../../../cosmological-transfer-function.md), including the acoustic scale, is then unchanged.

More [dark energy](../../../../../../dark-energy.md) causes accelerated expansion to start earlier, reducing the time for matter growth. The [suppression of matter growth by smooth accelerated expansion](../../../../../../suppression-of-matter-growth-by-smooth-accelerated-expansion.md) lowers $D(1)$ and hence the present spectrum. For the subhorizon scales shown,

$$
\boxed{\frac{P_m^{\rm new}(k,0)}{P_m^{\rm old}(k,0)}
\simeq\left[\frac{D_{\rm new}(1)}{D_{\rm old}(1)}\right]^2<1.}
$$

This is approximately a downward amplitude change with the same turnover and wiggle locations in physical inverse-megaparsec units. For matter plus a cosmological constant, an explicit early-normalized growth solution is

$$
D(a)=\frac{5\Omega_{m0}H_0^2}{2}H(a)
\int_0^a\frac{da'}{a'^3H(a')^3},
$$

with radiation neglected in this late-time growth equation. A numerical factor needs the old and new dark-energy densities. A change to axes expressed using $h$ would additionally relabel wavenumbers and volumes; the printed axes use $\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ and $\mathrm{Mpc}^3$.
