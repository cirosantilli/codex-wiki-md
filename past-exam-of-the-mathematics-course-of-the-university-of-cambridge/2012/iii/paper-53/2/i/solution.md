<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Before [electron-positron annihilation in cosmology](../../../../../../electron-positron-annihilation-in-cosmology.md), the pair has two spin states, just as [photons](../../../../../../photon.md) do, and the fermionic number integral gives $n_{\nu+\bar\nu}/n_\gamma=3/4$ at equal temperature. The factor follows by expanding $(e^x+1)^{-1}$ into an alternating exponential series: its $x^2$ integral is $3/4$ of the bosonic one.

After [neutrino decoupling](../../../../../../neutrino-decoupling.md), [cosmological entropy conservation](../../../../../../cosmological-entropy-conservation.md) in the photon-electron plasma changes its relativistic entropy count from $2+(7/8)4=11/2$ to $2$. The decoupled [neutrinos](../../../../../../neutrino.md) do not share this heating. Hence

$$
\left(\frac{T_\nu}{T_\gamma}\right)^3=\frac4{11},\qquad
\boxed{n_{\nu+\bar\nu,0}=\frac34\frac4{11}n_{\gamma0}
=\frac3{11}(410)\simeq111.8\,\mathrm{cm}^{-3}.}
$$

These counts include both the new [neutrino](../../../../../../neutrino.md) and its [antineutrino](../../../../../../antineutrino.md); another factor of two would double count them. This is the [number density of a relativistic neutrino relative to photons](../../../../../../number-density-of-a-relativistic-neutrino-relative-to-photons.md), evaluated after photon heating.

They are nonrelativistic today, so the [relic-neutrino energy density](../../../../../../relic-neutrino-energy-density.md) is dominated by rest mass:

$$
\rho_{\nu0}\simeq (10^3\,\mathrm{eV})(111.8\,\mathrm{cm}^{-3})
=1.118\times10^5\,\mathrm{eV}\,\mathrm{cm}^{-3},\qquad
\boxed{\Omega_{\nu0}\simeq22.4.}
$$

The number follows from the supplied [critical density](../../../../../../critical-density.md), without a Hubble-unit convention. It is conditional on the thermal history stated above; a sterile, colder or nonthermally produced state need not have this abundance.
