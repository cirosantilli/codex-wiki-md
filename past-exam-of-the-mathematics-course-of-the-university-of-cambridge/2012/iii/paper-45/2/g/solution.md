<h1 id="2/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

The remaining non-derivative component term in the Lagrangian is the [Yukawa interaction](../../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md)

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm fermion,pot}
=-\frac12W''(v+\widetilde\varphi)\psi\psi+\mathrm{h.c.}
=-\frac12(M_v+g\widetilde\varphi)\psi\psi+\mathrm{h.c.}
$$

Equivalently the interaction Hamiltonian has the opposite displayed sign. The first term is the two-component [fermion](../../../../../../fermion.md) mass, and the second its scalar-fermion interaction. A phase redefinition of $\psi$ makes the mass coefficient nonnegative, so the physical [Weyl spinor](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) mass is

$$
\boxed{m_{\rm fermion}=|M_v|=\sqrt{|m^2-2g\kappa|}.}
$$

It is again independent of $\alpha$, the choice of root and any sign of a real $M_v$. This distinguishes the complex bilinear coefficient from the nonnegative physical mass. In the pure-linear supersymmetry-breaking branch $W''=0$, the [fermion](../../../../../../fermion.md) is the massless [Goldstino](../../../../../../goldstino.md).
