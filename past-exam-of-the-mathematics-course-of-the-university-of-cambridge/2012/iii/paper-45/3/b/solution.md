<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the same four Grassmann generators and a fixed order $a,b,c,d$. Their squares vanish, so

$$
(bc-ad)^2=-bcad-adbc=-2abcd.
$$

Meanwhile the two self-contractions are

$$
(\psi\psi)=dc-cd=-2cd,\qquad
(\chi\chi)=ba-ab=-2ab,
$$

and moving the pair $cd$ past $ab$ gives no sign, yielding $(\psi\psi)(\chi\chi)=4abcd$. Thus the [square of a Weyl spinor bilinear](../../../../../../square-of-a-weyl-spinor-bilinear.md) is

$$
\boxed{(\chi\psi)^2=-\frac12(\psi\psi)(\chi\chi),\qquad D=-\frac12.}
$$

The order of individual odd components, rather than an informal commuting-spinor substitution, fixes this sign. For commuting spinors both self-contractions vanish, so no corresponding universal factorization of the generally nonzero mixed square is possible.
