↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Measure [baseline covariates](../../../../../../../baseline-covariate.md) before the intervention can change them. These measurements describe the recruited population, permit assessment of [covariate balance](../../../../../../../covariate-balance.md), and provide prognostic predictors that can improve [statistical efficiency](../../../../../../../efficiency-statistics.md). In particular, including prognostic [baseline covariates](../../../../../../../baseline-covariate.md) in a [linear regression](../../../../../../../linear-regression-split.md) reduces unexplained outcome variation without conditioning on a treatment consequence.
