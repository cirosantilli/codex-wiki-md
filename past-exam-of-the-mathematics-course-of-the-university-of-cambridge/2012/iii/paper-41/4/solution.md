<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

At each event time within a stratum, let $r_A,r_B$ be the group [risk sets](../../../../../risk-set.md) and let $d$ be the total number of observed failures. Under the null of equal [hazard functions](../../../../../hazard-function.md), the expected A count is $d r_A/(r_A+r_B)$. The [log-rank statistic](../../../../../log-rank-statistic.md) adds observed minus expected A counts over event times; the [stratified log-rank statistic](../../../../../stratified-log-rank-statistic.md) first makes these comparisons within each patient stratum, then adds across patients. Let $V$ be the sum of the corresponding null conditional variances. The standardized statistic $U/\sqrt V$ has an approximate standard [normal distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) under the null, and its square has an approximate one-degree-of-freedom [chi-squared distribution](../../../../../chi-squared-distribution.md). Compare it with the appropriate quantile for a two-sided test, under the usual [independent censoring](../../../../../independent-censoring.md) and exchangeability assumptions.
