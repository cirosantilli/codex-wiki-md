<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A nonzero contribution occurs when a failure, rather than a censoring, is the earlier observation while both members remain at risk. By the [paired log-rank reduction to a sign test](../../../../../../paired-log-rank-reduction-to-a-sign-test.md), the two possible contributions are $+1/2$ for an earlier A failure and $-1/2$ for an earlier B failure. Under the exchangeable null and the usual [independent censoring](../../../../../../independent-censoring.md) assumptions they are equally likely, conditional on being informative. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[U_i\mid U_i\ne0]=0,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(U_i\mid U_i\ne0)=\tfrac14.}
$$

Once the first member leaves the [risk set](../../../../../../risk-set.md), the second member's later observation adds no group-comparison information within this stratum.
