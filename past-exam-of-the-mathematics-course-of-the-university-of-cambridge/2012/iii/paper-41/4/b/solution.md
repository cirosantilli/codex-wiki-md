<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the A-group observed-minus-expected convention for the [stratified log-rank statistic](../../../../../../stratified-log-rank-statistic.md). At the first failure both teeth are at risk, so A has one observed event and null expectation $1/2$, giving $+1/2$. At the later B failure, A is no longer at risk; the A contribution is zero. **This patient's contribution is $+1/2$**, favoring B. The opposite group convention reverses the sign but gives the same test.
