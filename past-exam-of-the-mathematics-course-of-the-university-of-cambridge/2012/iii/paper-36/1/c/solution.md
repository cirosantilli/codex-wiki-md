<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Fix $P=P_{\theta,\eta}$. Let $\mathcal N$ be the [nuisance tangent space](../../../../../../nuisance-tangent-space.md): the closed linear span in $L^2(P)$ of [score functions](../../../../../../informant-function.md) of [statistical paths](../../../../../../statistical-path.md) that vary only the [nuisance parameter](../../../../../../nuisance-parameter.md) $\eta$. By the preceding argument, $\mathcal N$ is contained in the [mean-zero L2 space](../../../../../../mean-zero-l2-space.md) $L^2_0(P)$.

Let $\Pi_{\mathcal N}$ denote [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto this [closed subspace of a Hilbert space](../../../../../../closed-subspace-of-a-hilbert-space.md). The [efficient score](../../../../../../efficient-score.md) and scalar [efficient information](../../../../../../efficient-information.md) are

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\ell_{\theta,\eta}=\dot\ell_{\theta,\eta}-\Pi_{\mathcal N}\dot\ell_{\theta,\eta},
\qquad
\widetilde I_{\theta,\eta}=P_{\theta,\eta}\widetilde\ell_{\theta,\eta}^{\,2}.}
$$

The [efficient score](../../../../../../efficient-score.md) is the component of the parametric [score function](../../../../../../informant-function.md) that cannot be reproduced by changing the [nuisance parameter](../../../../../../nuisance-parameter.md). The [efficient information](../../../../../../efficient-information.md) is its squared [L2 norm](../../../../../../l2-norm.md); it can be zero, so positivity must not be assumed in the definition.
