<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The map $h\mapsto Ph$ is a [bounded linear functional](../../../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) on [L2 space](../../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md), since $|Ph|\leq\|h\|_2$ by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Its kernel, the [mean-zero L2 space](../../../../../../mean-zero-l2-space.md), is therefore closed. Every nuisance [score function](../../../../../../informant-function.md) and the parametric [score function](../../../../../../informant-function.md) $\dot\ell$ are centered, so both $\Pi_{\mathcal N}\dot\ell$ and $\widetilde\ell$ are centered. Hence

$$
\boxed{P\widetilde\ell=0.}
$$

Moreover, the [efficient score](../../../../../../efficient-score.md) belongs to the [orthogonal complement](../../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) of the [nuisance tangent space](../../../../../../nuisance-tangent-space.md). Writing $\dot\ell=\widetilde\ell+\Pi_{\mathcal N}\dot\ell$ gives

$$
P(\dot\ell\widetilde\ell)
=P\widetilde\ell^2+P\bigl((\Pi_{\mathcal N}\dot\ell)\widetilde\ell\bigr)
=\boxed{\widetilde I}.
$$

This is the [efficient-score projection identity](../../../../../../efficient-score-projection-identity.md); it remains valid when the [efficient information](../../../../../../efficient-information.md) is zero.
