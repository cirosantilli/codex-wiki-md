<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [statistical path](../../../../../../statistical-path.md) through $P$ is a family $t\mapsto P_t\in\mathcal P$, defined on an open interval containing zero, with $P_0=P$. Write $p_t=dP_t/d\mu$ and $p=p_0$. Its [differentiability in quadratic mean](../../../../../../differentiability-in-quadratic-mean.md) at zero means that there is a [square-integrable function](../../../../../../square-integrable-function.md) $g\in L^2(P)$ such that

$$
\boxed{\int\left(\sqrt{p_t}-\sqrt p-\frac t2g\sqrt p\right)^2\,d\mu=o(t^2).}
$$

The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $g$ is the [score function](../../../../../../informant-function.md) of the [statistical path](../../../../../../statistical-path.md). Thus it is the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of the square-root [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md), multiplied by two and divided by $\sqrt p$ where $p>0$. The [score function](../../../../../../informant-function.md) is determined only $P$-almost everywhere. The displayed definition also controls any probability mass entering a region where $p=0$; a pointwise [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of the log [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) alone would not do that. **The required [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is in the square-root-density $L^2$ [norm](../../../../../../norm.md).**
