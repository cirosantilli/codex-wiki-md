<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a collection of [statistical paths](../../../../../../statistical-path.md) through $P_f$ that are [differentiable in quadratic mean](../../../../../../differentiability-in-quadratic-mean.md). A [statistical tangent set](../../../../../../statistical-tangent-set.md) at $P_f$ is the set of their [score functions](../../../../../../informant-function.md). In particular each member belongs to $L^2_0(P_f)$, by the [mean-zero score identity under quadratic-mean differentiability](../../../../../../mean-zero-score-identity-under-quadratic-mean-differentiability.md).

A [statistical tangent set](../../../../../../statistical-tangent-set.md) records which first-order directions the chosen [statistical paths](../../../../../../statistical-path.md) can realize. Its closed linear span in [L2 space](../../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) is the [statistical tangent space](../../../../../../statistical-tangent-space.md). **The tangent set consists of attainable scores; the tangent space also includes their [linear combinations](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) and $L^2(P_f)$ limits.**
