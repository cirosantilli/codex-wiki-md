<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The allowed operators are exactly the [positive contractions](../../../../../../positive-contraction.md) $0\leq T\leq I$, or binary measurement effects. As in (ii),

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(TX)=\operatorname{Tr}(TQ)-\operatorname{Tr}(TR)
\leq\operatorname{Tr}Q,
$$

because $\operatorname{Tr}(TR)\geq0$ and $\operatorname{Tr}[(I-T)Q]\geq0$. The positive-spectral [orthogonal projector](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) $P_+$ is among these effects and attains the bound. Thus

$$
\boxed{D(\rho,\sigma)=\max_{0\leq T\leq I}\operatorname{Tr}[T(\rho-\sigma)].}
$$

Allowing general binary effects instead of projectors does not improve the optimal [trace distance](../../../../../../trace-distance.md) test.
