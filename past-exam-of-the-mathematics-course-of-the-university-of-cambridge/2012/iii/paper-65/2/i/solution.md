<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The $+1$ measurement effect of the [Pauli operator](../../../../../../pauli-operator.md) $\sigma_x$ is $P_+=(I+\sigma_x)/2$. The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) and the [Bloch vector](../../../../../../bloch-vector.md) representation give

$$
\Pr(+1)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho P_+)=\frac{1+s_x}{2}
=\boxed{\frac34}.
$$

The other two [Bloch vector](../../../../../../bloch-vector.md) components do not enter this [projective measurement](../../../../../../projective-measurement.md) probability.
