<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Indifference means equality of the representing utility values. Since $U_0(\lambda)>U_0(\mu)>U_0(\nu)$, set

$$
\boxed{p=\frac{U_0(\mu)-U_0(\nu)}{U_0(\lambda)-U_0(\nu)}\in(0,1).}
$$

Applying the [affine function](../../../../../../affine-function.md) identity gives $U_0(p\lambda+(1-p)\nu)=U_0(\mu)$, so $\mu\sim p\lambda+(1-p)\nu$. This proves [mixture solvability of affine preferences](../../../../../../mixture-solvability-of-affine-preferences.md). The displayed weight is unique because the two endpoint utilities differ.
