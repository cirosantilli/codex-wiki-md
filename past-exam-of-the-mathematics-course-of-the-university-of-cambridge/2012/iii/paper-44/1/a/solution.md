<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [affine function](../../../../../../affine-function.md) preserves mixtures, so

$$
U_0(p\lambda+(1-p)\nu)-U_0(p\mu+(1-p)\nu)=p[U_0(\lambda)-U_0(\mu)]>0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{p\lambda+(1-p)\nu\succ p\mu+(1-p)\nu.}
$$

The positive mixture weight preserves the strict sign; the common component cancels. This is the [independence axiom for lottery preferences](../../../../../../independence-axiom-for-lottery-preferences.md) for the [affine utility on probability measures](../../../../../../affine-utility-on-probability-measures.md).
