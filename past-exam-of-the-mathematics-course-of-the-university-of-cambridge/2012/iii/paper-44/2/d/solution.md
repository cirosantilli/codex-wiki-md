<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The stated [portfolio](../../../../../../investment-portfolio.md) has initial wealth $5$ and terminal payoffs $W_u=4\cdot6-3\cdot7=3$ and $W_d=4\cdot6-3\cdot4=12$. For [CRRA utility](../../../../../../constant-relative-risk-aversion-utility.md), $U'(w)=w^{-R}$. The market is complete, and an interior optimum in its positive payoff domain has marginal utility proportional to the unique [state-price density](../../../../../../state-price-density.md). Therefore

$$
\frac{3^{-R}}{12^{-R}}=\frac{10/9}{5/9}=2,\qquad4^R=2,\qquad\boxed{R=\frac12.}
$$

The [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) is $y^*=9/(10\sqrt3)$, and both state equations $U'(W)=y^*Z$ are satisfied. Part (b) verifies sufficiency and [strict concavity](../../../../../../strict-concavity.md) gives uniqueness of the optimal payoff. This illustrates [risk aversion recovered from an optimal two-state payoff](../../../../../../risk-aversion-recovered-from-an-optimal-two-state-payoff.md).
