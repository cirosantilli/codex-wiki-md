<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [supermartingale](../../../../../../supermartingale.md) conclusion gives

$$
\mathbb E\left[Z_tX_t+\int_0^tZ_sc_s\,ds\right]\leq x.
$$

Discard the nonnegative terminal-wealth term and let $t\to\infty$. The cumulative deflated [consumption](../../../../../../consumption.md) increases, so [monotone convergence theorem](../../../../../../monotone-convergence-theorem.md) yields the [state-price budget constraint](../../../../../../state-price-budget-constraint.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E\int_0^\infty Z_tc_t\,dt\leq X_0.}
$$

The normalization is relevant: with $Z_0\ne1$ the right side is $Z_0X_0$; with random integrable initial deflated wealth its [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value.md) is the unconditional budget bound.
