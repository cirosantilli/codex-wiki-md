<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The marked-to-market value of the current holdings is $X_t=H_t\cdot P_t$. In a [self-financing portfolio](../../../../../../self-financing-portfolio.md), purchases are paid for by sales within the [portfolio](../../../../../../investment-portfolio.md), so rebalancing itself changes no net wealth. Asset price changes contribute $H_t\cdot dP_t$, and nonnegative [consumption](../../../../../../consumption.md) removes $c_t\,dt$ from wealth. Hence

$$
\boxed{X_t=H_t\cdot P_t,\qquad dX_t=H_t\cdot dP_t-c_t\,dt.}
$$

The holdings must be [predictable](../../../../../../predictable-process.md) and stochastically integrable. The second identity is the self-financing definition with withdrawals; it is not obtained by incorrectly setting the changes in the holdings themselves to zero.
