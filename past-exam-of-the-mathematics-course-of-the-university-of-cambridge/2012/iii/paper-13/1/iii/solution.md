<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take the [three-dimensional affine quadric cone](../../../../../../three-dimensional-affine-quadric-cone.md)

$$
X=V(xy-zw)\subset\mathbb A^4,\qquad
Y=V_X(x,z),\qquad Z=V_X(y,w).
$$

The [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $xy-zw$ is an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md): as a polynomial in $x$ over $k[y,z,w]$, it is primitive because $y$ and $zw$ are [coprime elements of a unique factorization domain](../../../../../../coprime-elements-of-a-unique-factorization-domain.md), and it is a linear [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) over $k(y,z,w)$. [Gauss lemma for polynomials](../../../../../../gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md) then applies. Thus $X$ is an [irreducible variety](../../../../../../irreducible-variety.md) that is an [affine variety](../../../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md), of [algebraic dimension](../../../../../../dimension-of-an-algebraic-set.md) $3$ by the [principal hypersurface dimension lemma](../../../../../../principal-hypersurface-dimension-lemma.md) in [affine space](../../../../../../affine-space.md) $\mathbb A^4$.

The [coordinate rings](../../../../../../coordinate-ring.md) of $Y$ and $Z$ are respectively $k[y,w]$ and $k[x,z]$. Both are [affine planes](../../../../../../affine-plane.md), hence [irreducible closed subsets](../../../../../../irreducible-closed-subset.md) of [algebraic dimension](../../../../../../dimension-of-an-algebraic-set.md) $2$. Their intersection is precisely the origin. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\dim X=3,\quad\dim Y=\dim Z=2,\quad\dim(Y\cap Z)=0.}
$$

The origin is the [singular point of an algebraic variety](../../../../../../singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) of this [three-dimensional affine quadric cone](../../../../../../three-dimensional-affine-quadric-cone.md). The example shows why a [smoothness of an algebraic variety](../../../../../../smoothness-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) hypothesis matters in [intersection dimension estimates](../../../../../../intersection-dimension-bound-on-a-smooth-variety.md).
