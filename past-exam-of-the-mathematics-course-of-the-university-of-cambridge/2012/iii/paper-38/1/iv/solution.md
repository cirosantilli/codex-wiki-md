↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $|\phi|<1$, the causal representation is $X_t=\sum_{j\ge0}\phi^j\varepsilon_{t-j}$. Thus

$$
\gamma_k=\frac{\sigma^2}{1-\phi^2}\phi^{|k|}.
$$

Alternatively the rational spectrum from (iii) is $\sigma^2/\{\pi(1-\phi z)(1-\phi z^{-1})\}$ on $|z|=1$. The absolutely convergent geometric sums give

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}\phi^{|k|}z^k
&=1+\frac{\phi z}{1-\phi z}+\frac{\phi z^{-1}}{1-\phi z^{-1}}\\
&=\frac{1-\phi^2}{(1-\phi z)(1-\phi z^{-1})}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{f_X(\omega)=\frac{\sigma^2}{\pi(1-\phi^2)}
\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}\phi^{|k|}e^{ik\omega}.}
$$

This is the [Poisson-kernel expansion of an AR(1) spectrum](../../../../../../poisson-kernel-expansion-of-an-ar-1-spectrum.md), with the same one-sided normalization as (i).
