↑ **Parent:** [Paper 38](../paper-38-split.md)

To generate a chi-squared variable with one degree of freedom by the [Marsaglia polar method](../../../../marsaglia-polar-method.md), repeatedly draw independent $V,W$ uniformly on $(-1,1)$ until $0<S=V^2+W^2<1$. Return

$$
Z=V\sqrt{\frac{-2\log S}{S}},\qquad Y=Z^2.
$$

The accepted point is uniform on the unit disk; its squared radius is uniform on $(0,1)$ and independent of angle. The radial rescaling gives a standard normal coordinate $Z$, hence **$Y\sim\chi_1^2$**. The same accepted pair also supplies another independent standard normal coordinate, if needed. The proof stages below follow the four printed sampler labels.
