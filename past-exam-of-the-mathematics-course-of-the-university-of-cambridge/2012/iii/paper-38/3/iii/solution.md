<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $0<x<\mu$, only the smaller-root branch can produce a value near $x$. Conditional on $Y=h(x)$, its selection probability is $\mu/(\mu+x)$. The output density on that interval is therefore

$$
p_-(x)=\frac{\mu}{\mu+x}\,g(h(x))\,|h'(x)|.
$$

Use

$$
\sqrt{h(x)}=\frac{\sqrt\lambda\,|x-\mu|}{\mu\sqrt x},
\qquad
|h'(x)|=\frac{\lambda|x-\mu|(x+\mu)}{\mu^2x^2}.
$$

The factors cancel to give

$$
\boxed{p_-(x)=\sqrt{\frac{\lambda}{2\pi x^3}}
\exp\!\left(-\frac{\lambda(x-\mu)^2}{2\mu^2x}\right),
\qquad 0<x<\mu.}
$$

Thus the selected lower branch has exactly the lower part of the desired inverse Gaussian density, rather than the unweighted density of the smaller root alone.
