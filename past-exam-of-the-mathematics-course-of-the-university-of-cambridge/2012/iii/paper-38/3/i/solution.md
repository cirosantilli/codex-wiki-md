<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Squaring a variable with the [standard normal distribution](../../../../../../standard-normal-distribution.md) gives the required [chi-squared distribution](../../../../../../chi-squared-distribution.md). Its two inverse images at $y>0$ are $\pm\sqrt y$, so the change-of-variable formula gives

$$
g(y)=\frac{\exp(-y/2)}{\sqrt{2\pi y}}.
$$

Draw the branch-selection uniform variable independently of the randomness used for the normal draw. Then the pair $(Y,U)$ has the product law: conditioning on $Y$ leaves $U$ uniform. This independence is exactly what justifies interpreting the subsequent threshold as the [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) of selecting the smaller root.
