<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each finite place $v\in S$, choose $d_v\in K_v^\times$ such that $K_v(\sqrt{d_v})/K_v$ is the unramified [quadratic extension](../../../../../../../quadratic-extension.md). Such a choice exists even at residue characteristic two. At odd residue characteristic use a unit with nonsquare residue. At residue characteristic two, choose $\overline c$ for which $X^2-X-\overline c$ is irreducible over the finite [residue field](../../../../../../../residue-field.md), lift $c$ to $\mathcal O_v$, and set $d_v=1+4c$. The lifted polynomial has unit derivative at every residue point and gives the unramified degree-two extension; its root is $(1+\sqrt{d_v})/2$.

The [square classes](../../../../../../../square-class.md) are open in $K_v^\times$. Indeed the [strong form of Hensel lemma](../../../../../../../strong-form-of-hensel-lemma.md) applied to $X^2-u$ at $1$ shows that $u$ is a square whenever $v(u-1)>2v(2)$. Thus any sufficiently close approximation $a$ to $d_v$ has $a/d_v$ square. By [weak approximation for number fields](../../../../../../../weak-approximation-for-number-fields.md), choose $a\in K$ satisfying this at every finite $v\in S$. Then the local extensions $K_v(\sqrt a)$ are all unramified of degree two. At least one such condition ensures $a$ is not a square in $K$. If $S$ contains no finite place, impose the same condition at one auxiliary finite place.

Set $L=K(\sqrt a)$. At every specified finite prime there is one prime of $L$ above it, with [ramification index](../../../../../../../ramification-index.md) one and [residue degree](../../../../../../../residue-degree.md) two. **Hence $\boxed{\mathfrak p\mathcal O_L\text{ is prime for every finite }\mathfrak p\in S}$.** The dyadic case is covered by the unramified construction, rather than by a nonexistent nonsquare residue unit in characteristic two.
