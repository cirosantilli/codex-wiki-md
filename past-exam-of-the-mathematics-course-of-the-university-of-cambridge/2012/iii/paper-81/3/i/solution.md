<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [number field](../../../../../../number-field.md) $K$ and a [modulus of a number field](../../../../../../modulus-of-a-number-field.md) $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak m_0\mathfrak m_\infty$, let $I_K(\mathfrak m)$ be the group of [fractional ideals](../../../../../../fractional-ideal.md) prime to $\mathfrak m_0$, and let $P_{K,1}(\mathfrak m)$ consist of [principal fractional ideals](../../../../../../principal-fractional-ideal.md) $(a)$ with $a\equiv1$ at the finite modulus and $a>0$ at the real places in $\mathfrak m_\infty$. The [ray class group](../../../../../../ray-class-group.md) is $\operatorname{Cl}_{\mathfrak m}(K)=I_K(\mathfrak m)/P_{K,1}(\mathfrak m)$.

The [Main theorem of class field theory](../../../../../../main-theorem-of-class-field-theory.md) states that there is a unique finite abelian [ray class field](../../../../../../ray-class-field.md) $K_{\mathfrak m}/K$ for which the [Artin reciprocity map](../../../../../../artin-reciprocity-law.md) induces

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cl}_{\mathfrak m}(K)\simeq
\operatorname{Gal}(K_{\mathfrak m}/K).}
$$

It is [unramified](../../../../../../unramified-extension.md) outside the finite modulus and has no ramification at real places omitted from $\mathfrak m_\infty$. More generally, subgroups $H$ with $P_{K,1}(\mathfrak m)\subseteq H\subseteq I_K(\mathfrak m)$ correspond, with inclusions reversed, to intermediate abelian fields, with [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) $I_K(\mathfrak m)/H$. Every finite abelian extension occurs for some modulus. Equivalently, $K_{\mathfrak m}$ is the maximal abelian extension with [conductor of an abelian extension](../../../../../../conductor-of-an-abelian-extension.md) dividing $\mathfrak m$. This states both reciprocity and the [existence theorem of global class field theory](../../../../../../existence-theorem-of-global-class-field-theory.md).
