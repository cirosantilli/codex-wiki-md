<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a direct check of [unramified](../../../../../../unramified-extension.md) behavior, the [cyclotomic polynomial](../../../../../../cyclotomic-polynomial.md) $\Phi_{25}$ divides $X^{25}-1$. Modulo $7$, the latter has derivative $25X^{24}=4X^{24}$ and has no common root with that derivative. Thus $\overline\Phi_{25}$ is separable, so $7$ does not divide its [polynomial discriminant](../../../../../../polynomial-discriminant.md). The [field discriminant](../../../../../../field-discriminant.md) divides this polynomial discriminant, and therefore $7$ is [unramified](../../../../../../unramified-extension.md) in $\mathbb Q(\zeta_{25})$. The [Frobenius automorphism](../../../../../../frobenius-automorphism.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Frob}_7(\zeta_{25})=\zeta_{25}^{\,7}.}
$$

Indeed its residue action is seventh powering. The reduced $25$th roots of unity are distinct because $7\nmid25$, so the residue congruence identifies the exponent uniquely modulo $25$.

The [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) is $(\mathbb Z/25\mathbb Z)^\times$, of order $\varphi(25)=20$. Since $7^2=49\equiv-1\pmod{25}$, $7^4\equiv1$ but $7^2\not\equiv1$. Consequently the [Frobenius automorphism](../../../../../../frobenius-automorphism.md) has order four. An [unramified](../../../../../../unramified-extension.md) [decomposition group](../../../../../../decomposition-group.md) is generated by that element, and the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{g=20/4=5,\qquad f=4,\qquad e=1.}
$$

There are therefore **five primes above $7$**, each with [residue field](../../../../../../residue-field.md) $\mathbb F_{7^4}$.
