<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $m:B\times B\to B$ be addition, and let $p_1,p_2$ be the projections. Consider the [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md)

$$
M=m^*L\otimes p_1^*L^{-1}\otimes p_2^*L^{-1}.
$$

Its restriction to $B\times\{y\}$ has [Picard group](../../../../../../picard-group.md) class $[T_y^*L\otimes L^{-1}]=\phi_L(y)$; the factor $L_y^{-1}$ is a constant line and has trivial class. Its restriction to $\{0\}\times B$ is also trivial. Choose a trivialization of the fiber $L_0$ and use it to normalize along the zero sections. The defining universal property of the normalized [Poincaré line bundle](../../../../../../poincare-line-bundle.md) then identifies

$$
M\cong(\operatorname{id}_B,\phi_L)^*P_B.
$$

More explicitly, both sides have the same restrictions to all fibers of the second projection, and both are normalized on $\{0\}\times B$; the [Seesaw theorem](../../../../../../seesaw-theorem.md) makes their quotient trivial. Here $\phi_L:B\to\widehat B$ is the [homomorphism associated to a line bundle on an abelian variety](../../../../../../homomorphism-associated-to-a-line-bundle-on-an-abelian-variety.md), and $\widehat B$ is the [dual abelian variety](../../../../../../dual-abelian-variety.md). Pull back by $(f,g)$ to obtain **the required identity**

$$
\boxed{D_L(f,g)\cong(f,\phi_L\circ g)^*P_B.}
$$

The choice of fiber trivialization is immaterial to this identity in the [Picard group](../../../../../../picard-group.md).
