<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $M\in\operatorname{Pic}^0(B)$, translations preserve its [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) class, so $\phi_M=0$. Equivalently, the kernel of $L\mapsto\phi_L$ is the [Identity component of the Picard group](../../../../../../identity-component-of-the-picard-group.md). Applying the preceding [Poincaré line bundle](../../../../../../poincare-line-bundle.md) formula to $M$ yields

$$
D_M(f,g)\cong(f,0)^*P_B\cong\mathcal O_A,
$$

since the normalized [Poincaré line bundle](../../../../../../poincare-line-bundle.md) is trivial on $B\times\{0\}$. Pullback and [tensor products of sheaves](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-sheaves.md) also give $D_{L\otimes M}=D_L\otimes D_M$. **Thus $D_L$ depends only on $[L]\in\boxed{\operatorname{NS}(B)}$, the [Néron-Severi group](../../../../../../neron-severi-group.md).**
