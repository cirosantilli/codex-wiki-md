<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The bundle is on $A$, so the printed, undefined target $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$ must be read as the [Picard group](../../../../../../picard-group.md) $\operatorname{Pic}(A)$. Write this [Picard group](../../../../../../picard-group.md) additively and set $q(h)=[h^*L]$. The zero morphism pulls back $L$ to a constant one-dimensional vector space tensored with $\mathcal O_A$, so $q(0)=0$. The [Theorem of the Cube](../../../../../../theorem-of-the-cube.md), pulled back along $(f,g,h):A\to B^3$, gives

$$
q(f+g+h)-q(f+g)-q(f+h)-q(g+h)+q(f)+q(g)+q(h)=0.
$$

This is the [bilinear cross-effect of a line bundle on an abelian variety](../../../../../../bilinear-cross-effect-of-a-line-bundle-on-an-abelian-variety.md): set $d(f,g)=q(f+g)-q(f)-q(g)$. Substitution into this identity gives

$$
d(f+g,h)=d(f,h)+d(g,h).
$$

The definition gives $d(f,g)=d(g,f)$, so additivity holds in both arguments. Additivity includes negative multiples and the zero morphism. **Hence $D_L$ is a symmetric [bilinear map](../../../../../../bilinear-map.md) with values in $\operatorname{Pic}(A)$.** The equalities refer to isomorphism classes of [line bundles](../../../../../../line-bundle.md); without chosen rigidifications they are not asserted to be specified canonical isomorphisms of bundles.
