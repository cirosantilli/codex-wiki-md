<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Pull $L$ back by the [morphism of varieties](../../../../../../morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md)

$$
a:C\times X\longrightarrow X,\qquad (c,x)\longmapsto\varphi(c)+x.
$$

The fiber of this [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) over $x$ is $\varphi^*T_x^*L$. An [abelian variety](../../../../../../abelian-variety-split.md) is connected, so the preceding [constancy of line bundle degree in a family](../../../../../../constancy-of-line-bundle-degree-in-a-family.md) applies. **Consequently $\boxed{\deg(\varphi^*T_x^*L)=\deg(\varphi^*L)}$ for every $x\in X$.**
