<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The defining section of $\mathcal O_X(D)$ is nowhere zero on $C$, so its restriction is trivial and has degree zero. Every translated [line bundle](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) also has degree zero on $C$, by the preceding part. For any $t\in X$, either $C\subseteq D-t$ or the defining section of $T_t^*\mathcal O_X(D)$ restricts to a nonzero section on $C$. In the second case its zero [effective divisor](../../../../../../effective-cartier-divisor.md) has degree zero, so it has no zeros. Thus **a translate of $D$ either contains all of $C$ or misses all of $C$.**

Take $d\in D$ and $x_1,x_2\in C$. The translate $D-(d-x_1)$ contains $x_1$, hence all of $C$. In particular $d+(x_2-x_1)\in D$. This proves $D+(x_2-x_1)\subseteq D$; exchanging $x_1,x_2$ proves the reverse inclusion. Since the prime [Weil divisor](../../../../../../weil-divisor.md) $D$ is reduced and translation is an automorphism, equality of these sets gives equality of [Weil divisors](../../../../../../weil-divisor.md). Remembering that pullback by $T_t$ translates the divisor by $-t$, **the answer is**

$$
\boxed{T_{x_1-x_2}^*D=D.}
$$
