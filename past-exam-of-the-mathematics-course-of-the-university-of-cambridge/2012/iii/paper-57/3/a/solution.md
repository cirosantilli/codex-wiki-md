<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $X=\partial^{-2}\zeta$, with an inverse spatial [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) defined by a boundary or zero-mode prescription. It depends on $\zeta$, not on its time derivative. Differentiation gives the [canonical momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md)

$$
\boxed{\pi=\dot\zeta+3\alpha\dot\zeta^2+2\gamma X\dot\zeta.}
$$

Count $\zeta,\pi,X$ as first order. Inverting perturbatively gives

$$
\dot\zeta=\pi-3\alpha\pi^2-2\gamma X\pi+O(\zeta^3).
$$

For the [Legendre transform in mechanics](../../../../../../legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md), write $v=\dot\zeta=\pi+\Delta$ with $\Delta$ second order. The quadratic kinetic contribution is $\pi v-v^2/2=\pi^2/2-\Delta^2/2$, whose correction is fourth order; in the cubic terms one may consequently replace $v$ by $\pi$. The [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) density is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H=\tfrac12\pi^2+\tfrac12(\partial\zeta)^2-\alpha\pi^3-\beta\zeta(\partial\zeta)^2-\gamma(\partial^{-2}\zeta)\pi^2+O(\zeta^4).}
$$

The cubic [interaction Hamiltonian](../../../../../../interaction-hamiltonian.md) density is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H_{\rm int}=-\alpha\pi^3-\beta\zeta(\partial\zeta)^2-\gamma(\partial^{-2}\zeta)\pi^2.}
$$

In the free [interaction picture](../../../../../../interaction-picture.md), $\pi_I=\dot\zeta_I$. Thus here $\mathcal H_{\rm int}=-\mathcal L_3$ with the time derivatives interpreted as free fields. This equality follows from the [cubic Legendre transform for scalar derivative interactions](../../../../../../cubic-legendre-transform-for-scalar-derivative-interactions.md); at higher orders additional terms can appear.
