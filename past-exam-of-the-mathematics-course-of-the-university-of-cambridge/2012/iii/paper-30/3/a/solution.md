<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Order configurations coordinatewise: $\omega\le\eta$ means $\omega(e)\le\eta(e)$ for every [edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md). Then [stochastic domination](../../../../../../stochastic-domination-of-probability-measures.md) is the condition

$$
\boxed{\mu_1(A)\ge\mu_2(A)\quad\text{for every increasing event }A.}
$$

Equivalently, $\mathbb E_{\mu_1}f\ge\mathbb E_{\mu_2}f$ for every real-valued [order-preserving function](../../../../../../order-preserving-function.md) $f$. The equivalence follows on this finite space by writing $f$, up to an additive constant, as a nonnegative linear combination of indicators of its increasing upper level sets. A [coupling of probability distributions](../../../../../../coupling.md) supported on pairs $(X_1,X_2)$ with $X_1\ge X_2$ proves this domination; conversely such an ordered [coupling of probability distributions](../../../../../../coupling.md) exists on a finite partially ordered space.
