<h1 id="1/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply preservation of [Poisson brackets](../../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) to the coordinate functions. It gives

$$
\{Q_i,Q_j\}=0,\qquad\{P_i,P_j\}=0,\qquad\{Q_i,P_j\}=\delta_{ij}.
$$

Conversely, the entries of $AJA^T$ are exactly the [Poisson brackets](../../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) between all transformed coordinate functions. The displayed relations therefore say $AJA^T=J$, which implies the [canonical transformation](../../../../../../../canonical-transformation.md) condition and, by the [chain rule](../../../../../../../chain-rule.md), preservation of every [Poisson bracket](../../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md). Thus **all three conditions are equivalent**:

$$
\boxed{f^*\omega_0=\omega_0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{all brackets are preserved}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\{Q_i,Q_j\}=\{P_i,P_j\}=0,\ \{Q_i,P_j\}=\delta_{ij}.}
$$

In particular the original coordinate bracket $\{q_i,p_j\}$ is $\delta_{ij}$ with the chosen [Poisson bracket](../../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) convention.
