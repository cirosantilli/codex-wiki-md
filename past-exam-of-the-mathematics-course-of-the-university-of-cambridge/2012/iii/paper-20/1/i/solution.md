<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [standard symplectic form](../../../../../../standard-symplectic-form.md) $\omega_0=\sum_{j=1}^n dq_j\wedge dp_j$. A [canonical transformation](../../../../../../canonical-transformation.md) is a [diffeomorphism](../../../../../../diffeomorphism.md) $f$ preserving this [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md):

$$
\boxed{f^*\omega_0=\omega_0.}
$$

Thus, writing $f(q,p)=(Q,P)$, the defining identity is $\sum_jdQ_j\wedge dP_j=\sum_jdq_j\wedge dp_j$. For a local [canonical transformation](../../../../../../canonical-transformation.md), require this on its open domain. In particular preservation of the [symplectic form](../../../../../../symplectic-form.md), rather than just of [phase-space volume](../../../../../../symplectic-volume-form.md), is the condition in dimensions greater than two.

Throughout these solutions choose the [Hamiltonian vector field](../../../../../../hamiltonian-vector-field.md) convention $\iota_{X_F}\omega=dF$. It gives $\dot q=F_p$, $\dot p=-F_q$ and agrees with the positive coordinate [Poisson bracket](../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) used below. The opposite convention for [Hamiltonian vector fields](../../../../../../hamiltonian-vector-field.md) changes signs in the [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) identity but none of the commuting or integrability conclusions.
