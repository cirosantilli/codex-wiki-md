<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [first-order phase transition](../../../../../../first-order-phase-transition.md) exchanges two distinct [global minima](../../../../../../global-minimum.md) of the [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md). The equilibrium [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) remains continuous, but a first derivative such as the [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md) or [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) jumps. A temperature-driven transition generically has latent heat; crossing $h=0$ between symmetry-related ordered phases instead has a [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md) jump without requiring latent heat. A [continuous phase transition](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md) has no such order-parameter jump and usually has a divergent [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) and nonanalytic higher responses.

For $u>0$, $h=0$ and $r$ crossing zero, minimizing $V=rM^2/2+uM^4/4$ gives $M=0$ for $r>0$ and $M=\pm\sqrt{-r/u}$ for $r<0$. The minima merge continuously at zero. The curvature vanishes there, so the long-distance response becomes singular.

For $u<0<v$, put $z=M^2$. A nonzero stationary minimum satisfies $r+uz+vz^2=0$. Equality with the $M=0$ [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) gives

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{3u^2}{16v},\qquad M=0,\quad M=\pm\sqrt{-\frac{3u}{4v}}.}
$$

The finite separation demonstrates the [first-order phase transition](../../../../../../first-order-phase-transition.md). [Metastability](../../../../../../metastability.md) occurs where a nonglobal [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md) persists; its disappearance at a [spinodal point](../../../../../../spinodal-point.md) is not the equilibrium coexistence criterion.
