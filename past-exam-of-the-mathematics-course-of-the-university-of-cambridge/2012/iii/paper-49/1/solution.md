<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

At the [tricritical point](../../../../../tricritical-point.md), tune $u=0$, hold $v>0$, and let $r=a t$ cross zero. The equation of state becomes $h=rM+vM^5$. At $h=0,r<0$, $M^4=-r/v$, while the inverse susceptibility on the ordered branch is $r+5vM^4=4|r|$. On the disordered side it is $r$. At $r=0$, $M=\operatorname{sgn}(h)(|h|/v)^{1/5}$. Therefore $\beta=1/4$, $\gamma=1$ and $\delta=5$.

The minimized singular [free-energy density](../../../../../free-energy-density.md) is

$$
f_s(r,0)=-\frac{|r|^{3/2}}{3\sqrt v}\quad(r<0),\qquad f_s(r,0)=0\quad(r>0).
$$

Two temperature derivatives give a singular [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) proportional to $|t|^{-1/2}$ on the ordered side. Consequently the [mean-field tricritical exponent calculation](../../../../../mean-field-tricritical-exponent-calculation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)=(\tfrac12,\tfrac14,1,5).}
$$

The displayed [scaling relations for critical exponents](../../../../../scaling-relation-for-critical-exponents.md) hold numerically:

$$
\frac12+2\left(\frac14\right)+1=2,\qquad\frac14\cdot5=\frac14+1.
$$

These are [Landau theory](../../../../../landau-theory.md) values with both relevant coefficients tuned: along a generic path with fixed $u>0$, the asymptotic transition is ordinary rather than tricritical. The sextic [upper critical dimension](../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md) is three, so fluctuations can change the powers below three dimensions, and logarithmic corrections are possible at three. The vanishing disordered mean-field singular amplitude does not invalidate the ordered-side exponent.
