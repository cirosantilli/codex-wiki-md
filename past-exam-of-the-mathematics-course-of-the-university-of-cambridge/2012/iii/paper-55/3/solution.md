A [principal bundle](../../../../../principal-bundle.md) consists of smooth manifolds $P,B$, a [Lie group](../../../../../lie-group.md) $G$, a smooth surjection $\pi:P\to B$, and a smooth free right action of $G$ on $P$, whose orbits are the fibers. Every $b\in B$ has an open neighborhood $U$ with a $G$-equivariant [local trivialization](../../../../../local-trivialization.md) $\pi^{-1}(U)\cong U\times G$, under which $\pi$ is projection and the action is $(x,\gamma)h=(x,\gamma h)$. Equivalently a local section $s:U\to P$ writes every fiber point uniquely as $p=s(x)\gamma$. On overlaps, sections differ by smooth transition functions $s_\beta=s_\alpha u_{\alpha\beta}$ satisfying the cocycle identity.

A [principal connection](../../../../../connection-principal-bundle.md) is a Lie-algebra-valued one-form $\omega$ on $P$ satisfying $R_h^*\omega=\operatorname{Ad}_{h^{-1}}\omega$ and $\omega(\xi^\#)=\xi$ for the [fundamental vector field](../../../../../fundamental-vector-field.md) $\xi^\#(p)=\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_0p\exp(t\xi)$. Its [horizontal distribution of a principal connection](../../../../../horizontal-distribution-of-a-principal-connection.md) is the complement $\ker\omega$ to the vertical tangent spaces. **The local [gauge potential of a principal connection](../../../../../local-principal-connection-form.md) is Lie-algebra-valued**, specifically $A=s^*\omega$; matrix notation identifies the adjoint action with conjugation. The displayed connection expression is understood on each coordinate trivialization, not as a choice of a global section.
