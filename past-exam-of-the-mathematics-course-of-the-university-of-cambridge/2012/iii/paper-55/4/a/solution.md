<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiate the [circle group](../../../../../../circle-group.md) action at angle zero. The resulting real [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is

$$
\boxed{K=iz\partial_z-i\bar z\partial_{\bar z}.}
$$

In real coordinates $z=x+iy$, this is $K=-y\partial_x+x\partial_y$; the complex coefficients are conjugates, so it is indeed real. Its integral curves satisfy $\dot z=iz$ and hence $z(t)=e^{it}z(0)$, with period $2\pi$ away from the fixed points.

The affine coordinate does not cover the whole [complex projective line](../../../../../../complex-projective-line.md), so check the other chart. With $w=1/z$, the same [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is $K=-iw\partial_w+i\bar w\partial_{\bar w}$, smooth at $w=0$. It vanishes at $z=0$ and $w=0$, the two fixed poles. **It therefore generates the stated global rotation of $S^2$, not merely a flow in one chart.**
