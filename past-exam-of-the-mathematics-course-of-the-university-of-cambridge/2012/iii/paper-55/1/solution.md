<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Use the [Lie bracket](../../../../../lie-bracket.md) convention $[X,Y]=XY-YX$. If $E_{ij}$ denotes a [matrix unit](../../../../../matrix-unit.md), then $E_{ij}E_{kl}=\delta_{jk}E_{il}$. Here this gives

$$
[X_0,X_1]=X_1,\qquad [X_0,X_2]=X_2,\qquad [X_1,X_2]=0.
$$

Thus, writing $[X_j,X_k]=f^i{}_{jk}X_i$, **the nonzero [structure constants of a Lie algebra](../../../../../structure-constant-of-a-lie-algebra.md) are**

$$
\boxed{f^1{}_{01}=f^2{}_{02}=1,\qquad f^1{}_{10}=f^2{}_{20}=-1.}
$$

All other entries vanish. This is the [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) of the [translation-dilation group of the plane](../../../../../translation-dilation-group-of-the-plane.md): its two-dimensional translation ideal is abelian, and $X_0$ acts on that ideal as the identity. In group coordinates the multiplication and inverse are

$$
(\rho,x)(\rho',x')=(\rho\rho',x+\rho x'),\qquad (\rho,x)^{-1}=(\rho^{-1},-\rho^{-1}x).
$$

These formulas also make the [semidirect product](../../../../../semidirect-product.md) structure explicit.
