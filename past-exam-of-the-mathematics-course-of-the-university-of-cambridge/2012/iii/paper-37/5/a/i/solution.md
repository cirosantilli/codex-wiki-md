[Right censoring](../../../../../../../right-censoring.md) occurs when a subject's exact failure time $T$ is unobserved but it is known that $T>C$, where $C$ is the censoring time. The observed pair is $U=\min(T,C)$ and $\Delta=\mathbf1_{\{T\le C\}}$. For example, a living participant at study closure contributes a survival lower bound rather than a recorded death at closure. [Right censoring](../../../../../../../right-censoring.md) differs from simply omitting the subject: time spent in the [risk set](../../../../../../../risk-set.md) remains informative.
