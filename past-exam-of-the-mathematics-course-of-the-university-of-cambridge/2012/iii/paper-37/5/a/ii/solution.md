A standard sufficient non-[informative censoring](../../../../../../../informative-censoring.md) assumption is [independent censoring](../../../../../../../independent-censoring.md): $T$ and $C$ are independent, or [conditionally independent](../../../../../../../conditional-independence.md) given the covariates used in the analysis. Their distributions need not be equal, have the same shape, or have proportional hazards. In a likelihood analysis the censoring distribution should also carry no information about the survival parameters being estimated, allowing its factor to be omitted. In the more general risk-set formulation, the censoring history must not alter the subsequent failure hazard conditional on the observed history used by the model. [Informative censoring](../../../../../../../informative-censoring.md) can arise if worsening unrecorded health causes withdrawal.
