<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Among subjects alive at any given time and with the same relevant covariates or observed history, those censored at that time have the same subsequent failure distribution as those who remain under observation. Equivalently, the observed [risk set](../../../../../../../risk-set.md) represents the failure hazard of comparable subjects still alive, rather than selectively retaining lower- or higher-risk subjects. This is a statement about their future conditional risk, not that the observed failure-time distribution among censored people equals the distribution among people who eventually fail: their different observation patterns make that comparison inappropriate.
