<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

There are 23 independent vial responses. The intercept-only fit has 22 residual [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md) and deviance 33.631. Adding two day indicators removes two [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md) and reduces the [residual deviance](../../../../../../residual-deviance.md) by $33.631-33.358=0.273$. Adding the two species indicators then reduces it by $33.358-14.646=18.712$. Thus the six replacements, in their printed order, are **$22,\ 33.631,\ 2,\ 0.273,\ 18.712,\ 14.646$**.

The [residual deviance](../../../../../../residual-deviance.md) [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md) are $23-1=22$, $23-3=20$, and $23-5=18$. These sequential analysis-of-deviance rows compare nested [generalized linear models](../../../../../../generalized-linear-model.md) in the displayed order. In an unbalanced design, the first day row does not test day after controlling for species.
