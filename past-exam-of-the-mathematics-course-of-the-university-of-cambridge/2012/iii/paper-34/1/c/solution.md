↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The stopping-interval indicator is an [elementary predictable process with stopping-time intervals](../../../../../../elementary-predictable-process-with-stopping-time-intervals.md). Its [stochastic integral](../../../../../../stochastic-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{(\mathbf1_{(S,T]}\cdot M)_t=M_{t\wedge T}-M_{t\wedge S}.}
$$

It is zero before $S$, equals $M_t-M_S$ between $S$ and $T$, and equals $M_T-M_S$ after $T$. For a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md) with jumps, the jump at $S$ is excluded and the jump at $T$ is included, exactly as required by the half-open interval.
