<h1 id="5/with-transaction-costs/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under a [proportional transaction cost](../../../../../../../proportional-transaction-cost.md), the sample gain $h_x(\theta)=\theta x-\varepsilon|\theta|$ is concave when $\varepsilon\geq0$. The [utility function](../../../../../../../utility-function-split.md) is increasing and concave, so

$$
U(h_x(s\theta+(1-s)\eta))\geq U(sh_x(\theta)+(1-s)h_x(\eta))\geq sU(h_x(\theta))+(1-s)U(h_x(\eta)).
$$

Take finite [expectations](../../../../../../../expected-value.md). **Thus $\boxed{G\text{ is concave}}$.** The monotonicity of $U$ is essential to composing it with the concave transaction-cost payoff.
