<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $0\leq s\leq1$, pointwise concavity of the [utility function](../../../../../../utility-function-split.md) gives

$$
U((s\theta+(1-s)\eta)X)\geq sU(\theta X)+(1-s)U(\eta X).
$$

Taking [expectations](../../../../../../expected-value.md) is legitimate because all three values are finite. **Therefore $\boxed{F(s\theta+(1-s)\eta)\geq sF(\theta)+(1-s)F(\eta)}$: $F$ is concave.** Strict concavity is not required and is not asserted.
