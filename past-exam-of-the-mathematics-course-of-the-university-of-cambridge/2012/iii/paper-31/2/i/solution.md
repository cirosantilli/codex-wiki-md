A [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfies [Lipschitz continuity](../../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) with bound $L\geq0$ if

$$
\boxed{|f(x)-f(y)|\leq L|x-y|\quad\text{for every }x,y\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Here $L$ is an admissible [Lipschitz bound](../../../../../../lipschitz-bound.md); the least admissible value is the [Lipschitz constant](../../../../../../lipschitz-constant.md). The later parts use the admissible bound $1$.
