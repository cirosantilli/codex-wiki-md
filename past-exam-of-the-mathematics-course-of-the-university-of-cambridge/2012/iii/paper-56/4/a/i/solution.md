<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) $\xi$ generates a local one-parameter family of [isometries](../../../../../../../isometry.md) of the [metric tensor](../../../../../../../metric-tensor.md). Equivalently, its [tensor Lie derivative](../../../../../../../lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md) of the metric vanishes:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_\xi g_{ab}=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\nabla_a\xi_b+\nabla_b\xi_a=0.}
$$

The latter is the [Killing equation](../../../../../../../killing-equation.md); the equivalence uses [metric compatibility](../../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) and the [torsion-free connection](../../../../../../../torsion-free-connection.md).
