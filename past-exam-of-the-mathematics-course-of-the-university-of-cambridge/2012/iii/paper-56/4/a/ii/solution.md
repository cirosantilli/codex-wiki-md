<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $u$ be the [tangent vector](../../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) to a [geodesic](../../../../../../../geodesic.md) with [affine parameter](../../../../../../../affine-parameter.md) $\lambda$. Its [geodesic equation](../../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) is $\nabla_uu=0$. For a [Killing vector field](../../../../../../../killing-vector-field.md) $\xi$, [metric compatibility](../../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) gives

$$
\frac{d}{d\lambda}(\xi_au^a)=u^au^b\nabla_b\xi_a+\xi_a u^b\nabla_bu^a=u^au^b\nabla_{(a}\xi_{b)}=0.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\xi_au^a\text{ is constant along every affinely parametrized geodesic}.}
$$

This [geodesic conserved quantity from a Killing vector](../../../../../../../geodesic-conserved-quantity-from-a-killing-vector.md) is independent of whether the [geodesic](../../../../../../../geodesic.md) is timelike, spacelike or null. For a [timelike geodesic](../../../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) one can use [proper time](../../../../../../../proper-time.md); for a [null geodesic](../../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) choose an [affine parameter](../../../../../../../affine-parameter.md). An arbitrary nonaffine tangent rescales the displayed quantity and need not make it constant.
