<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For every member of the [geodesic variation](../../../../../../geodesic-variation.md), [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) normalization gives $g(T,T)=-1$. By [metric compatibility](../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md), the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md), and $\nabla_TS=\nabla_ST$,

$$
\frac{d}{d\tau}g(S,T)=g(\nabla_TS,T)=g(\nabla_ST,T)=\frac12S\bigl(g(T,T)\bigr)=0.
$$

The last equality uses normalization across the whole variation, not merely constancy along one curve. Hence $g(S,T)$ is constant along each [timelike geodesic](../../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md), proving

$$
\boxed{g(S,T)|_{\tau_0}=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g(S,T)=0\text{ for all }\tau.}
$$

This is [orthogonality of proper-time deviation vectors](../../../../../../orthogonality-of-proper-time-deviation-vectors.md).
