<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Contracting the assumed [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) gives the [Ricci tensor](../../../../../../ricci-tensor.md)

$$
R_{bd}=g^{ac}R_{abcd}=\frac{R}{12}(4g_{bd}-g_{bd})=\frac R4g_{bd}.
$$

The [contracted Bianchi identity](../../../../../../contracted-bianchi-identity.md) and [metric compatibility](../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) now imply

$$
\frac14\nabla_bR=\nabla^aR_{ab}=\frac12\nabla_bR.
$$

Thus **$\nabla_bR=0$, so the scalar curvature is constant on each connected component of spacetime.** The sectional curvature is consequently the constant $\kappa=R/12$. This is the four-dimensional case of the [Schur theorem in pseudo-Riemannian geometry](../../../../../../schur-theorem-in-pseudo-riemannian-geometry.md).
