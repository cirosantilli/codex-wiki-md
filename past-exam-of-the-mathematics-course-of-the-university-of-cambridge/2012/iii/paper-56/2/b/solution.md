<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use precisely the stated convention for the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md), and let $D=\nabla_T$. The [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) says $\nabla_TT=0$ because [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) is an [affine parameter](../../../../../../affine-parameter.md). The commuting variation and the [torsion-free connection](../../../../../../torsion-free-connection.md) give

$$
D^2S=\nabla_T\nabla_ST=\nabla_S\nabla_TT+R(T,S)T+\nabla_{[T,S]}T=R(T,S)T.
$$

Thus the [geodesic deviation](../../../../../../geodesic-deviation.md) equation is

$$
\boxed{\nabla_T^2S=R(T,S)T,\qquad (D^2S)^a=R^a{}_{bcd}T^bT^cS^d.}
$$

Here $(R(X,Y)Z)^a=R^a{}_{bcd}Z^bX^cY^d$. Declaring this index convention fixes the sign: writing $R(S,T)T$ instead would require a minus sign. A [deviation vector](../../../../../../deviation-vector.md) satisfying this equation is a [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md).
