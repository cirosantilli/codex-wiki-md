<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Describe the [geodesic variation](../../../../../../geodesic-variation.md) by a smooth map $F(s,\tau)$, with each curve $\tau\mapsto F(s,\tau)$ a [timelike geodesic](../../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) parametrized by [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md). Its [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) is $T=F_*\partial_\tau$ and its [deviation vector](../../../../../../deviation-vector.md) is $S=F_*\partial_s$. Thus $S$ measures the first-order separation of neighboring members at the same value of [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md).

Mixed coordinate derivatives on the parameter surface commute. Consequently, along the variation,

$$
\boxed{[S,T]=0.}
$$

For an embedded two-dimensional variation this is the usual [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) on its image; more generally the same identity is read along the map $F$ using its commuting parameter derivatives. Since the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) is [torsion-free](../../../../../../torsion-free-connection.md), it follows that $\nabla_TS=\nabla_ST$.
