<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If every regular circle has trivial [holonomy](../../../../../../../holonomy.md), the preceding calculation says that the continuous function $f'$ takes values in $\mathbb Z$. On a connected radial interval it must therefore be constant. Integrating gives the complete family

$$
\boxed{f(r)=nr+b,\qquad n\in\mathbb Z,\quad b\in\mathbb R,\quad nr+b>0\text{ on the chosen interval}.}
$$

Conversely, every such function has identity [holonomy](../../../../../../../holonomy.md) around every circle. With the opposite sign convention for $f$, replace the positivity condition by nonvanishing; the [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md) is unchanged by changing its sign.

**The slope parameter is discrete.** Thus the printed parameter count can mean one integer and one real parameter, but it cannot mean two freely varying real parameters. Allowing $f=ar+b$ with arbitrary real $a$ gives a locally flat family, not trivial circular [holonomy](../../../../../../../holonomy.md): for example $a=1/2$ gives a rotation by $\pi$. This distinction is captured by [flat circular metrics with trivial holonomy](../../../../../../../flat-circular-metrics-with-trivial-holonomy.md).
