<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Work on a connected regular radial interval, where $f$ is smooth and nonzero; replacing $f$ by $|f|$ allows us to take $f>0$. The only nonzero [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) are

$$
\Gamma^r{}_{\phi\phi}=-ff',\qquad \Gamma^\phi{}_{r\phi}=\Gamma^\phi{}_{\phi r}=f'/f.
$$

Along the circle, write a [tangent vector](../../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) in the single-valued [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $E_r=\partial_r$, $E_\phi=f^{-1}\partial_\phi$ as $V=uE_r+vE_\phi$. The equations for [parallel transport](../../../../../../../parallel-transport.md) reduce to

$$
\frac{du}{d\phi}=f'(r_0)v,\qquad \frac{dv}{d\phi}=-f'(r_0)u.
$$

Consequently $u+iv$ acquires the factor $e^{-2\pi i f'(r_0)}$ after one circuit. The basis itself agrees at $\phi=0$ and $2\pi$, so no extra basis change is missing. The [holonomy](../../../../../../../holonomy.md) is the identity on every [tangent vector](../../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) precisely when

$$
\boxed{f(r_0)\ne0,\qquad f'(r_0)\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The first condition makes this a regular circle of the [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md); the second is the actual transport condition. In particular, zero slope is sufficient but not necessary. This is [holonomy around circular fibres](../../../../../../../holonomy-around-circular-fibres.md).
