<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [covariant derivative](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) on the [tangent bundle](../../../../../../tangent-bundle.md) is an [affine connection](../../../../../../affine-connection.md): it assigns a [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) $\nabla_XY$ to [vector fields](../../../../../../vector-field.md) $X,Y$, is linear over [smooth functions](../../../../../../smooth-function.md) in $X$, is real-linear in $Y$, and obeys the [Leibniz rule](../../../../../../leibniz-rule.md) $\nabla_X(aY)=X(a)Y+a\nabla_XY$. Thus it differentiates a [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) while taking account of how its local [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) changes. It extends to [tensor fields](../../../../../../tensor-field.md) by the [Leibniz rule](../../../../../../leibniz-rule.md), compatibility with [tensor contraction](../../../../../../tensor-contraction.md), and $\nabla_Xa=X(a)$ for a [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md).

In a local [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $e_\mu$, the [connection components](../../../../../../connection-components.md) are defined by $\nabla_{e_\rho}e_\nu=\Gamma^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}e_\mu$. In a [coordinate basis](../../../../../../coordinate-basis.md), $e_\rho=\partial_\rho$, so

$$
(\nabla_\rho V)^\mu=\partial_\rho V^\mu+\Gamma^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}V^\nu,\qquad (\nabla_\rho\alpha)_\mu=\partial_\rho\alpha_\mu-\Gamma^\nu{}_{\mu\rho}\alpha_\nu.
$$

A [covariant derivative](../../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) of a [tensor field](../../../../../../tensor-field.md) has tensorial transformation properties, but the [connection components](../../../../../../connection-components.md) themselves do not: changing the [coordinate basis](../../../../../../coordinate-basis.md) introduces second derivatives of the coordinate change. **They describe the connection in a chosen basis, rather than components of a tensor.**
