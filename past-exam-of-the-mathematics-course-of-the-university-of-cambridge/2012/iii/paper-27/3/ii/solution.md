<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an integral [Minimal Weierstrass equation](../../../../../../minimal-weierstrass-equation.md), let $E_0(\mathbb Q_p)$ be the subgroup of points whose reduction is a smooth point of the reduced cubic. Its reduction is a homomorphism to the [smooth-locus group of a singular Weierstrass cubic](../../../../../../smooth-locus-group-of-a-singular-weierstrass-cubic.md), or to the whole reduced [elliptic curve](../../../../../../elliptic-curve.md) in the good-reduction case. Define the [formal kernel of a minimal Weierstrass equation](../../../../../../formal-kernel-of-a-minimal-weierstrass-equation.md) by

$$
\boxed{E_1(\mathbb Q_p)=\ker\bigl(E_0(\mathbb Q_p)\longrightarrow\widetilde E_{\mathrm{ns}}(\mathbb F_p)\bigr).}
$$

It consists of the identity and the points reducing to the identity at infinity. For a nonidentity affine point this is equivalent to $v_p(x)=-2r$ and $v_p(y)=-3r$ for some positive integer $r$.

The [local parameter](../../../../../../local-parameter-on-a-smooth-algebraic-curve.md) $t=-x/y$, with $t(O)=0$, gives the group isomorphism

$$
\boxed{E_1(\mathbb Q_p)\cong\widehat E(p\mathbb Z_p),}
$$

where $\widehat E$ is the [formal group of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../formal-group-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). To see the local coordinates, put $w=-1/y$ in the [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). The resulting relation is $w=t^3+a_1tw+a_2t^2w+a_3w^2+a_4tw^2+a_6w^3$, and its unit linear coefficient gives a unique integral series $w(t)=t^3+O(t^4)$. Thus $x=t/w$ and $y=-1/w$ have valuations $-2v_p(t)$ and $-3v_p(t)$. The [chord-and-tangent group law](../../../../../../chord-and-tangent-group-law.md) expressed in this parameter is precisely the integral [formal group law](../../../../../../formal-group-law.md) $\widehat E$. This description includes bad reduction; only the smooth identity neighborhood is involved.
