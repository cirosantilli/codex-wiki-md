<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $B=2\Omega-S$, $A(t)=c_s^2-2\pi G\Sigma_0/k(t)$, and $w=i k_x\widetilde v_y-i k_y\widetilde v_x$. Differentiation, including $\dot k_x=Sk_y$, gives

$$
\dot w=iSk_y\widetilde v_y+i k_x(-B\widetilde v_x-ik_yA\widetilde\Sigma'/\Sigma_0)-ik_y(2\Omega\widetilde v_y-ik_xA\widetilde\Sigma'/\Sigma_0)=-iB(k_x\widetilde v_x+k_y\widetilde v_y).
$$

The two [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md)/self-gravity cross terms cancel. Continuity gives $\dot{\widetilde\Sigma}'=-i\Sigma_0(k_x\widetilde v_x+k_y\widetilde v_y)$, so $\dot w=B\dot{\widetilde\Sigma}'/\Sigma_0$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dt}\left[\frac{ik_x\widetilde v_y-ik_y\widetilde v_x}{\Sigma_0}-\frac{(2\Omega-S)\widetilde\Sigma'}{\Sigma_0^2}\right]=0.}
$$

The conserved amplitude is [linearized vortensity](../../../../../../linearized-vortensity.md): the perturbation of the absolute vertical [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) divided by [surface density](../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md),

$$
q=\frac{2\Omega+\partial_xu_y-\partial_yu_x}{\Sigma},\qquad q_0=\frac{2\Omega-S}{\Sigma_0}.
$$

A two-dimensional inviscid [barotropic fluid](../../../../../../barotropic-fluid.md) materially conserves this [potential vorticity](../../../../../../potential-vorticity.md). Both [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) enthalpy and gravitational forces are gradients, so they create no baroclinic [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) source. Since $q_0$ is uniform, its linear perturbation obeys $D_0q'=0$, explaining why a [shearing wave](../../../../../../shearing-wave.md) has constant [vortensity](../../../../../../vortensity.md) amplitude.
