<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the positive constant ratio $\lambda=p_r/p_g$. The [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) implies

$$
\rho=\frac{4\sigma}{3c\lambda\mathcal R}T^3,\qquad p=(1+\lambda)\mathcal R\rho T\propto T^4.
$$

This is a [polytropic equation of state](../../../../../../polytropic-equation-of-state.md) of index three. Differentiating the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) and using vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $4(1+\lambda)\mathcal R\rho\,T_z=-\rho\Omega^2z$. Hence

$$
T=T_0-\frac{\Omega^2z^2}{8(1+\lambda)\mathcal R}.
$$

Define the semi-thickness $H$ by $T(H)=0$ and put $s=1-z^2/H^2$. Then the [constant gas-radiation ratio vertical disc structure](../../../../../../constant-gas-radiation-ratio-vertical-disc-structure.md) is

$$
\boxed{T=T_0s,\qquad\rho=\rho_0s^3,\qquad p=p_0s^4,\qquad |z|<H,}
$$

with

$$
\boxed{T_0=\frac{\Omega^2H^2}{8(1+\lambda)\mathcal R},\quad\rho_0=\frac{\sigma\Omega^6H^6}{384c\lambda(1+\lambda)^3\mathcal R^4},\quad p_0=\frac{\rho_0\Omega^2H^2}{8}=\frac{\sigma\Omega^8H^8}{3072c\lambda(1+\lambda)^3\mathcal R^4}.}
$$

The [surface density](../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) is the integral across both faces. The supplied polynomial integral gives $\int_0^1(1-x^2)^3dx=16/35$, so $\Sigma=(32/35)\rho_0H$. Substitution yields

$$
\boxed{420\lambda(1+\lambda)^3\Sigma=\left(\frac{\mu_m m_p}{k}\right)^4\frac\sigma cH^7\Omega^6.}
$$

All coefficients here are local in radial position; the derivation fixes the vertical profile rather than a global accretion rate. The zero-temperature surface is a formal boundary of this bulk model; optically thick diffusion ultimately fails in a thin photospheric layer.
