<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first relation is vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md). In a [thin disc](../../../../../../thin-disk.md), the central object's gravity near the midplane is $g_z=-\Omega^2z$; the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) balances it. The second is an [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) combining [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) with isotropic [radiation pressure](../../../../../../radiation-pressure.md). Write $\mathcal R=k/(\mu_m m_p)$, so $p_g=\mathcal R\rho T$ and $p_r=4\sigma T^4/(3c)$; $\sigma$ is the [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-constant.md) and $\mu_m$ the [mean molecular weight](../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight.md) in proton-mass units.

The third is local energy balance: the increase of upward [radiative flux](../../../../../../radiative-flux.md) with height equals viscous dissipation. Here $\mu$ is [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity.md), not mean molecular weight. A [Keplerian disc](../../../../../../keplerian-disk.md) has shear $r\,d\Omega/dr=-3\Omega/2$, so its heating rate is $\mu(r\Omega')^2=9\mu\Omega^2/4$. The fourth is optically thick [radiative diffusion](../../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md), with [opacity](../../../../../../opacity.md) per unit mass $\kappa$ and diffusion directed down the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md). Midplane symmetry gives $F(0)=0$ and oppositely directed fluxes from the two faces. **The four relations express vertical force balance, gas–radiation thermodynamics, local heating, and diffusive cooling.**
