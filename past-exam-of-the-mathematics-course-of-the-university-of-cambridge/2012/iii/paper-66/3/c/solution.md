<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The equal mixture of $|\Phi^+\rangle$ and $|\Phi^-\rangle$ cancels their off-diagonal coherence, leaving the [separable quantum state](../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md)

$$
\rho(0)=\frac12|00\rangle\langle00|+\frac12|11\rangle\langle11|.
$$

A trivial [LHV model](../../../../../../local-hidden-variable-theory.md) chooses a shared random bit $j$, uniformly zero or one, and prepares both local [qubits](../../../../../../qubit.md) in $|j\rangle$. Each party uses the local [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) for its own chosen spin direction, with independent local random seeds if a deterministic description is desired. This reproduces the entire joint measurement distribution, not merely the $z$-axis correlation.

Since the state has an explicit [local hidden-variable theory](../../../../../../local-hidden-variable-theory.md) for these local measurements, **it cannot violate the CHSH inequality**, by the derivation in part (b). Classical correlation of the shared preparation bit is sufficient; the cancellation of coherence removes the [entanglement](../../../../../../entangled-state.md).
