<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

[Stellar nucleosynthesis](../../../../../stellar-nucleosynthesis.md) and the subsequent return of matter explain most elements heavier than the primordial [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) and [helium](../../../../../helium.md). [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](../../../../../big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) supplied predominantly those two elements, with small light-isotope abundances; stars do not make every element from an initially metal-free mixture. Different stellar masses reach different central temperatures and leave different remnants. The mass comparisons below describe representative isolated, near-solar-composition stars; mass loss and binary interaction can alter the final core. A nucleus contributes to later generations only if it is eventually expelled into the [interstellar medium](../../../../../interstellar-medium.md), rather than remaining locked in a remnant.

**A $1M_\odot$ star** spends its [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md) life converting [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) to [helium](../../../../../helium.md), predominantly through the [proton–proton chain](../../../../../proton-proton-chain.md). After core [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) ends, a [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) supplies an expanding [red giant](../../../../../red-giant.md) envelope and builds an electron-degenerate helium core. [First dredge-up](../../../../../first-dredge-up.md) exposes material whose [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md) has redistributed the original carbon and nitrogen abundances. The [helium flash](../../../../../helium-flash.md) starts [core helium burning](../../../../../core-helium-burning.md): the [Triple-alpha process](../../../../../triple-alpha-process.md) makes [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), and [carbon-12 alpha capture](../../../../../carbon-12-alpha-capture.md) makes [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). Once core helium is exhausted, the [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md) has a [carbon-oxygen core](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-core.md) with [helium-burning shell](../../../../../helium-burning-shell.md) and [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md). Shell instability causes [AGB thermal pulses](../../../../../thermal-pulse-of-an-asymptotic-giant-branch-star.md); subsequent [third dredge-up](../../../../../third-dredge-up.md), where efficient enough, can bring carbon and neutron-capture products into the envelope. Its efficiency at such low mass depends on composition and mixing. The star does not ordinarily ignite core [carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md). [Stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) remove the envelope, a hot remnant may illuminate a [planetary nebula](../../../../../planetary-nebula.md), and a [carbon-oxygen white dwarf](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-white-dwarf.md) retains much of the core's synthesized matter.

**A $5M_\odot$ star** generally uses the more temperature-sensitive [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md) for main-sequence [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md), with a substantial convective core. Its hotter helium core ignites [core helium burning](../../../../../core-helium-burning.md) without the low-mass degeneracy-driven [helium flash](../../../../../helium-flash.md), producing [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). After core helium exhaustion it develops double-shell [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md) burning and a degenerate [carbon-oxygen core](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-core.md). [Second dredge-up](../../../../../second-dredge-up.md) and repeated [third dredge-up](../../../../../third-dredge-up.md) alter the envelope composition. In sufficiently hot models, [hot-bottom burning](../../../../../hot-bottom-burning.md) at the base of the convective envelope converts dredged-up [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) into [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) through the [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md); consequently a carbon-producing shell need not produce a carbon-rich final envelope. Hot helium-shell pulses can activate the $^{22}\mathrm{Ne}(\alpha,n){}^{25}\mathrm{Mg}$ neutron source. Strong [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) expel these processed layers. A conventional isolated five-solar-mass model ends as a [carbon-oxygen white dwarf](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-white-dwarf.md), below the mass range that normally develops sustained core [carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md); mass loss and mixing govern the exact boundaries between outcomes.

**A $32M_\odot$ star** has core [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md) [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md), followed by [core helium burning](../../../../../core-helium-burning.md). The core then contracts through [carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md), [neon burning](../../../../../neon-burning.md), [oxygen burning](../../../../../oxygen-burning.md) and [silicon burning](../../../../../silicon-burning.md), with active shells outside the successive exhausted cores. [Carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md) produces chiefly nuclei such as [neon](../../../../../neon.md) and [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md). [Neon burning](../../../../../neon-burning.md) combines [photodisintegration](../../../../../nuclear-photodisintegration.md) and alpha capture; [oxygen burning](../../../../../oxygen-burning.md) makes silicon-group nuclei. [Silicon burning](../../../../../silicon-burning.md) is a network of captures and [photodisintegration](../../../../../nuclear-photodisintegration.md) approaching [nuclear statistical equilibrium](../../../../../nuclear-statistical-equilibrium.md), rather than simply fusion of pairs of silicon nuclei. It produces an iron-group mixture governed by temperature, density and weak reactions. The maximum in [nuclear binding energy](../../../../../nuclear-binding-energy.md) per nucleon near the iron group prevents continued exothermic fusion from supplying ordinary hydrostatic support. [Stellar neutrino energy loss](../../../../../stellar-neutrino-energy-loss.md) accelerates the advanced stages; [electron capture](../../../../../electron-capture.md) and [photodisintegration](../../../../../nuclear-photodisintegration.md) help remove support during iron-core collapse.

The massive star can return products both before and during collapse. Radiation-driven [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) may expose and expel layers enriched in [helium](../../../../../helium.md), [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). A successful [core-collapse supernova](../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) ejects additional hydrostatic products and creates further nuclei by shock-driven explosive burning, including alpha-rich and iron-group products. The [nickel-56 decay chain](../../../../../nickel-56-decay-chain.md) converts radioactive [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) into [iron](../../../../../iron.md) through [cobalt](../../../../../cobalt.md). Initial mass alone does not fix the explosion or remnant: substantial fallback or a [failed supernova](../../../../../failed-supernova.md) can leave a [black hole](../../../../../black-hole.md) and retain much of the core material. Thus the possible [core-collapse supernova](../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) contribution is not a guarantee that a thirty-two-solar-mass star ejects every element it made.

For nuclei well beyond the iron group, [neutron capture](../../../../../neutron-capture.md) is a principal assembly mechanism, avoiding the large [Coulomb barrier](../../../../../coulomb-barrier.md) for charged-particle fusion. [Beta-minus decay](../../../../../beta-minus-decay.md) converts captured neutrons into protons and raises the atomic number. In the [s-process](../../../../../s-process.md), captures are slower than relevant beta decays, so the sequence usually follows nuclei near stability. The main site is the [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md): $^{13}\mathrm C(\alpha,n){}^{16}\mathrm O$ is an important neutron source in low-mass intershell material, while $^{22}\mathrm{Ne}(\alpha,n){}^{25}\mathrm{Mg}$ becomes important in hotter pulses and in massive-star helium/carbon burning. [Third dredge-up](../../../../../third-dredge-up.md) and [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) make these products available to the [interstellar medium](../../../../../interstellar-medium.md). [Barium](../../../../../barium.md) and [lead](../../../../../lead.md) illustrate heavy products; the detailed yield depends on seed abundance, neutron exposure and branching at unstable nuclei.

In the [r-process](../../../../../r-process.md), neutron captures outrun beta decays, building very neutron-rich nuclei before they decay toward stable heavy isotopes. This needs much larger neutron densities than the [s-process](../../../../../s-process.md) and can produce very heavy nuclei, including [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md). Neutron-rich ejecta from [neutron star mergers](../../../../../neutron-star-merger.md) provide an established site: observed [kilonova](../../../../../kilonova.md) emission and spectroscopy support heavy-element synthesis in such ejecta. Rare magnetically driven stellar-collapse outflows are additional candidate sites. An ordinary [core-collapse supernova](../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) cannot automatically be assumed to supply a complete heavy r-process pattern.

Not every heavy isotope belongs to the two capture sequences. [Gamma-process nucleosynthesis](../../../../../gamma-process-nucleosynthesis.md) in hot supernova material uses [photodisintegration](../../../../../nuclear-photodisintegration.md) on pre-existing heavy seed nuclei through neutron, proton and alpha-emission channels, helping produce proton-rich isotopes bypassed by the main capture paths. This is distinct from simply using charged-particle fusion to build every element beyond the iron group.

Finally, [binary stars](../../../../../binary-star.md) change both production and delivery. [Roche-lobe overflow](../../../../../roche-lobe-overflow.md) and [wind mass transfer in a binary star](../../../../../wind-mass-transfer-in-a-binary-star.md) strip envelopes, change subsequent core growth, and expose processed layers. Mass transfer from an [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md) donor can enrich a surviving companion in carbon and [s-process](../../../../../s-process.md) products, as in a [barium star](../../../../../barium-star.md); this redistributes existing yields rather than creating them in the recipient. Accretion onto a [white dwarf](../../../../../white-dwarf.md) can trigger a [classical nova](../../../../../classical-nova.md) with explosive hydrogen-burning products. Thermonuclear disruption of a [carbon-oxygen white dwarf](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-white-dwarf.md) in a [Type Ia supernova](../../../../../type-ia-supernova.md), through suitable accretion or the [Double-degenerate Type Ia supernova scenario](../../../../../double-degenerate-type-ia-supernova-scenario.md), supplies a major delayed iron-group contribution, including [iron](../../../../../iron.md); it is not the same event as collapse of a massive iron core. Compact binaries containing two [neutron stars](../../../../../neutron-star.md) can later merge and eject [r-process](../../../../../r-process.md) material. Thus binaries affect stellar mass histories, enrich companions, open thermonuclear explosion channels, and create heavy-element-producing compact-remnant mergers.

**The key distinction is between making nuclei and ejecting them:** ordinary low/intermediate-mass stars mainly return processed envelopes, massive stars add advanced and explosive burning products when those layers escape, and neutron-capture environments supply much of the material beyond [iron](../../../../../iron.md).
