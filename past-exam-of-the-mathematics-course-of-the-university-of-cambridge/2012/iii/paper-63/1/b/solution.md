<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [helium mass fraction](../../../../../../helium-mass-fraction.md) is $Y=1-X-Z$. For the two compositions, $Y_1=0.28$ and $Y_2=0.18$. The [mean molecular weight](../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight.md) is therefore

$$
\mu_1^{-1}=1.62,\quad \mu_1=\frac{50}{81}\simeq0.6173,\qquad
\mu_2^{-1}=1.745,\quad \mu_2=\frac{200}{349}\simeq0.5731.
$$

At fixed mass and with the same prescribed opacity coefficient, part (a) gives $T_e\propto\mu^{1/2}R^{1/8}$ and $L\propto\mu^2R^{5/2}$. Eliminating radius gives

$$
\boxed{L\propto\mu^{-8}T_e^{20},\qquad
T_e\big|_L\propto\mu^{2/5}.}
$$

This is the [composition shift of a convective pre-main-sequence track](../../../../../../composition-shift-of-a-convective-pre-main-sequence-track.md). The higher-[helium mass fraction](../../../../../../helium-mass-fraction.md) case $X_1=0.7$ is hotter at a given [luminosity](../../../../../../luminosity.md), lying to the left of the $X_2=0.8$ case in a [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](../../../../../../hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md). Its fixed-luminosity temperature ratio is $(349/324)^{2/5}\simeq1.030$.

Both stars contract, so both $L$ and $T_e$ decrease in this particular opacity model. Their nearly vertical [Hayashi tracks](../../../../../../hayashi-track.md) therefore run **downward and slightly to the right**, with arrows in that direction:<a id="1/b/image-composition-dependent-pre-main-sequence-tracks-with-contraction-arrows"></a>


![](../../../../../../past-exam-of-the-mathematics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2012/iii/paper-63-premain-sequence.png)

**[Figure 1](#1/b/image-composition-dependent-pre-main-sequence-tracks-with-contraction-arrows). Composition-dependent pre-main-sequence tracks with contraction arrows**.

The plotted [luminosity](../../../../../../luminosity.md) and [effective temperature](../../../../../../effective-temperature.md) are normalized to arbitrary common references; no opacity normalization or absolute age was specified. The comparison holds the given opacity coefficient fixed.

These paths cease to apply when their assumptions fail. [Deuterium-burning energy-generation rate](../../../../../../deuterium-burning-energy-generation-rate.md) can temporarily slow contraction; sustained [hydrogen burning](../../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md) ends the purely gravitational phase. Formation of a [radiative core](../../../../../../radiative-core.md) breaks the fully convective adiabat and can lead to a [Henyey track](../../../../../../henyey-track.md) toward hotter [effective temperature](../../../../../../effective-temperature.md). Partial [ionization](../../../../../../ionization.md), a changing atmospheric [opacity](../../../../../../opacity.md), and [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) alter the [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) or boundary relation. [Accretion luminosity](../../../../../../accretion-luminosity.md), changing mass, and magnetic inhibition of [convection](../../../../../../convection.md) can also modify a young star's path.
