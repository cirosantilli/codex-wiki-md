<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Keep the fixed [stellar homology](../../../../../../stellar-homology.md) profile, so that $\rho_c=C_\rho M/R^3$ and $P_c=C_PGM^2/R^4$. Solving the partially degenerate [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) for the central [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) gives

$$
T_c=\frac{\mu_i}{\mathcal R}
\left(\frac{P_c}{\rho_c}-K_{\rm nr}\rho_c^{2/3}\right)
=\frac{\mu_i}{\mathcal R}
\left(A\frac{GM}{R}-B K_{\rm nr}\frac{M^{2/3}}{R^2}\right),
$$

where $A=C_P/C_\rho$ and $B=C_\rho^{2/3}$ are positive constants. The [ion mean molecular weight](../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight-per-ion.md) is distinct from the total-particle [mean molecular weight](../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight.md) used in part (a).

Set $y=1/R$. The expression in parentheses is a concave quadratic in $y$. Its maximum occurs at

$$
y_{\max}=\frac{AG}{2BK_{\rm nr}}M^{1/3},\qquad
R_{\max}=\frac{2BK_{\rm nr}}{AG}M^{-1/3}.
$$

Substitution gives the **contraction-limited central temperature**

$$
\boxed{T_{\max}=\frac{A^2G^2}{4BK_{\rm nr}\mathcal R}\,\mu_iM^{4/3}
\ \propto\ \mu_iM^{4/3}.}
$$

At this point [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) supplies half the required central pressure. Further contraction increasingly goes into the density-dependent degeneracy term rather than heating the ions. The formula applies along the positive-temperature part of the idealized homologous sequence; a negative value beyond it is not a physical temperature.

For sufficiently small mass, this maximum never reaches the temperature needed for sustained [hydrogen burning](../../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md). Such an object stays below the [hydrogen-burning minimum mass](../../../../../../hydrogen-burning-minimum-mass.md) and becomes a cooling [brown dwarf](../../../../../../brown-dwarf.md), rather than an ordinary [main sequence](../../../../../../main-sequence.md) star. Some such objects burn their initial [deuterium](../../../../../../deuterium.md), but that finite reservoir does not provide lasting [hydrogen burning](../../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md).
