A [connection on a vector bundle](../../../../connection-vector-bundle.md) is a $\mathbb C$-linear map $D:A^0(E)\to A^1(E)$ satisfying $D(fs)=df\otimes s+fDs$ for smooth functions. Its extension is the [covariant exterior derivative](../../../../exterior-covariant-derivative.md)

$$
D(\eta\otimes s)=d\eta\otimes s+(-1)^{\deg\eta}\eta\wedge Ds.
$$

Squaring gives a $C^\infty$-linear operator $D^2s=\Theta s$, defining the [vector-bundle curvature](../../../../curvature-form.md) $\Theta\in A^2(\operatorname{End}E)$.

For a [line bundle](../../../../line-bundle.md), $\operatorname{End}E$ is canonically the trivial [line bundle](../../../../line-bundle.md), so this [vector-bundle curvature](../../../../curvature-form.md) is an ordinary global complex two-form. In a local frame with $D=d+A$, the scalar identity $A\wedge A=0$ gives $\Theta=dA$. Therefore **$d\Theta=0$**. The calculations in the two subparts also explain the gluing directly.
