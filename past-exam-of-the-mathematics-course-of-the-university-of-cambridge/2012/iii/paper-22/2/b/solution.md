<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a section represented by a column $u$, expand the [covariant exterior derivative](../../../../../../exterior-covariant-derivative.md) using the graded [Leibniz rule](../../../../../../leibniz-rule.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
D^2u
&=d(du+Au)+A\wedge(du+Au)\\
&=(dA)u-A\wedge du+A\wedge du+(A\wedge A)u.
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore the [Cartan curvature matrix equation](../../../../../../cartan-curvature-matrix-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Theta=dA+A\wedge A,\qquad
(A\wedge A)_{ij}=\sum_k A_{ik}\wedge A_{kj}.}
$$

The product includes [endomorphism](../../../../../../endomorphism.md) composition; it need not vanish for a matrix-valued one-form. For a scalar frame change $g$, $A'=A+g^{-1}dg$ and $d(g^{-1}dg)=0$, confirming that the line-bundle two-forms $dA$ patch globally.
