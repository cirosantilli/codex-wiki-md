<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a row of local frame sections $e=(e_1,\ldots,e_m)$ and columns of coefficients: $D(eu)=e(du+Au)$. If $e'=eg$ for a smooth invertible matrix-valued function $g$, then

$$
D(e')=D(eg)=e(Ag+dg)=e'\bigl(g^{-1}Ag+g^{-1}dg\bigr).
$$

Hence the [change of frame of a vector-bundle connection](../../../../../../change-of-frame-of-a-vector-bundle-connection.md) is

$$
\boxed{A'=g^{-1}Ag+g^{-1}dg.}
$$

This convention specifies both the placement of the frame matrix and the sign of the inhomogeneous term. The [vector-bundle curvature](../../../../../../curvature-form.md) transforms by $\Theta'=g^{-1}\Theta g$, either by applying $D^2$ to the new frame or by substituting in the formula of the next subpart.
