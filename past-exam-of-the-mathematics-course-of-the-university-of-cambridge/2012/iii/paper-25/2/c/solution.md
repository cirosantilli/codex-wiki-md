<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An object of the [slice category](../../../../../../slice-category.md) $\mathcal C/A$ is a [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md) $x:X\to A$; a map from $x$ to $y:Y\to A$ is $u:X\to Y$ with $yu=x$. The [identity morphism](../../../../../../identity-morphism.md) $1_A:A\to A$ is a [terminal object](../../../../../../terminal-object.md) of this [slice category](../../../../../../slice-category.md), even when $\mathcal C$ itself has no [terminal object](../../../../../../terminal-object.md).

For a cospan $X\xrightarrow{u}Z\xleftarrow{v}Y$ in the [slice category](../../../../../../slice-category.md), form its underlying [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) $P$ in $\mathcal C$, with maps $p:P\to X$ and $q:P\to Y$. If $x,y,z$ are the structure maps to $A$, then $xp=zup=zvq=yq$. This common map equips $P$ with its slice structure. An underlying mediating map into $P$ is automatically over $A$, because its composite with $xp$ is the prescribed structure map. Hence the same square is a [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) in $\mathcal C/A$.

Part (b) applies to the slice's [terminal object](../../../../../../terminal-object.md) and [pullbacks in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md). **Every [slice category](../../../../../../slice-category.md) has all [finite limits](../../../../../../finite-limit.md) when the ambient [category](../../../../../../category-split.md) has [pullbacks in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md).** The [finite completeness of slice categories](../../../../../../finite-completeness-of-slice-categories.md) result needs no ambient terminal-object hypothesis.
