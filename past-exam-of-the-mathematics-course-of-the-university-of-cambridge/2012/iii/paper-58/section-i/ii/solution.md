<h1 id="section-i/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Locally write the [null hypersurface](../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md) as $u=0$, $du\ne0$, with normal $\ell_\mu=f\partial_\mu u$, $f\ne0$. A vector $V$ is tangent exactly when $V(u)=0$. Nullness gives $\ell(u)=\ell^2/f=0$ on the hypersurface. **Hence $\boxed{\ell\in T\mathcal N}$**: its normal direction lies inside its tangent space.

Put $n=du$ and $q=n_\mu n^\mu$. Torsion freedom gives $n^\mu\nabla_\mu n_\nu=\tfrac12\partial_\nu q$. Since $q$ vanishes on $\mathcal N$, all tangential derivatives vanish there and $dq=C\,du$ there for a scalar $C$. Consequently

$$
\ell^\mu\nabla_\mu\ell_\nu=\left(\ell(\log|f|)+\tfrac12fC\right)\ell_\nu.
$$

This is the unparametrized [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) equation. The integral curves are therefore generators of $\mathcal N$. An [affine rescaling of a null normal](../../../../../../affine-rescaling-of-a-null-normal.md) removes the proportionality coefficient locally: if $\nabla_\ell\ell=\kappa\ell$, set $K=h\ell$ with $\ell(\log h)=-\kappa$.
