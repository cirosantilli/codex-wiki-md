<h1 id="section-i/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [metric signature](../../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$ and a future-oriented worldline. Variation of the [worldline einbein](../../../../../../worldline-einbein.md) gives the [mass-shell condition](../../../../../../string-mass-shell-condition.md); variation of momentum gives

$$
p^2+m^2=0,\qquad \dot x^\mu=e\,g^{\mu\nu}p_\nu.
$$

Eliminating $p$ first leaves $L=\dot x^2/(2e)-em^2/2$. Its $e$ equation is $\dot x^2=-e^2m^2$. Choose the positive lapse branch $e=\sqrt{-\dot x^2}/m$; substituting gives $L=-m\sqrt{-\dot x^2}$. **Thus $\boxed{S=-m\int\sqrt{-g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu}=-m\int d\tau}$**, the [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) action. The lapse branch fixes the sign convention. Eliminating auxiliary variables on their algebraic equations preserves the worldline equations, which are timelike [geodesics](../../../../../../geodesic.md) up to parametrization.
