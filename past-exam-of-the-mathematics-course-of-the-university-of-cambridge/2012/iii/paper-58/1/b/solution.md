<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A static observer has [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md) $u=F^{-1/2}\partial_t$. Its only nonzero acceleration component is $a^r=\Gamma^r{}_{tt}/F=F'/2=-r/R^2$. Using $g_{rr}=F^{-1}$, **the magnitude is**

$$
\boxed{a=\frac{|F'|}{2\sqrt F}=\frac{r}{R^2\sqrt{1-r^2/R^2}}.}
$$

It vanishes only at $r=0$, where $F=1$ and $d\tau=\sqrt F\,dt=dt$. Thus **$t$ is [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) for the central inertial static observer**. Other static observers need inward acceleration to resist de Sitter separation and have a redshifted [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md).
