<h1 id="1c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $z=1+iy$ with $y\in\mathbb R$. In the [complex plane](../../../../../../complex-plane.md), this is the vertical line through $1$. The [complex exponential](../../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) gives $e^z=e(\cos y+i\sin y)$, so its [complex modulus](../../../../../../complex-modulus.md) is always $e$. Conversely, every point of that modulus has an [complex argument](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) $y$ and is obtained in this way. **The image is the entire circle centered at zero with radius $e$**, traversed repeatedly as $y$ varies; it is not a disk.
