<h1 id="1c/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $w$ denote the value of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) used in the equation. Factoring $w^2+\pi^2/4$ gives $w=i\pi/2$ or $w=-i\pi/2$. Since $z=e^w$, **the complete answer is**

$$
\boxed{z=i\quad\text{or}\quad z=-i.}
$$

The [principal complex logarithm](../../../../../../../principal-complex-logarithm.md) takes exactly these values at $i$ and $-i$, so both are solutions with that convention. If the [complex logarithm](../../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) is interpreted as multivalued, the same answer holds in the sense that a logarithm value satisfies the equation. The equation is not an assertion that every logarithm value of these numbers has the stated square.
