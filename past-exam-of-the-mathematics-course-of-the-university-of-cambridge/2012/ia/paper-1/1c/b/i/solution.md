<h1 id="1c/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $z=x+iy$, the [complex exponential](../../../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) has [complex modulus](../../../../../../../complex-modulus.md) $e^x$. Equality to the positive real number $e$ therefore forces $x=1$ and $\cos y+i\sin y=1$. The latter holds precisely when $y$ is an integer multiple of $2\pi$. Hence **all solutions are**

$$
\boxed{z=1+2\pi i k,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Substitution verifies every listed value; the modulus and angle arguments exclude any others.
