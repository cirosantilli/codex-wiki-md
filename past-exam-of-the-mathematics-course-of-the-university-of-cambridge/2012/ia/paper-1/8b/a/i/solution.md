<h1 id="8b/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The symmetry between the last two coordinates gives the [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $(0,1,-1)^T$ with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $2$. On vectors $(s,t,t)^T$, the remaining [eigenvalue equation](../../../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}3&2\\1&2\end{pmatrix}\binom st=\lambda\binom st,
$$

whose [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\lambda^2-5\lambda+4=(\lambda-1)(\lambda-4)$. Corresponding [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are $(1,-1,-1)^T$ and $(2,1,1)^T$. Thus **all [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and [eigenspaces](../../../../../../../eigenspace.md) are**

$$
\boxed{\lambda=1:\ \operatorname{span}\{(1,-1,-1)^T\};\quad
\lambda=2:\ \operatorname{span}\{(0,1,-1)^T\};\quad
\lambda=4:\ \operatorname{span}\{(2,1,1)^T\}.}
$$

Nonzero scalar multiples give all [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) in each [eigenspace](../../../../../../../eigenspace.md). The three displayed vectors are mutually [orthogonal](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) and have squared norms $3,2,6$, respectively.
