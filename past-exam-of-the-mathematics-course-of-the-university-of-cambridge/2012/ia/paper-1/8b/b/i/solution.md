↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a real [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), $P^TP=I$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{(P\mathbf u)\cdot(P\mathbf v)=\mathbf u^TP^TP\mathbf v=\mathbf u^T\mathbf v=\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v.}
$$

Thus it preserves every real [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product.md), and in particular lengths and angles.
