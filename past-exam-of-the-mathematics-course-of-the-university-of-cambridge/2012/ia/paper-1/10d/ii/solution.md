<h1 id="10d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take **$\boxed{a_n=(-1)^n}$**. Then $|a_n/n|=1/n\to0$, but $a_{n+1}-a_n=-2(-1)^n$ alternates between $2$ and $-2$. Thus convergence of the scaled [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) does not imply convergence of its successive differences.
