↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**No.** Take **$\boxed{a_n=\log n}$**. For each fixed positive integer $k$,

$$
a_{n+k}-a_n=\log(1+k/n)\longrightarrow0
$$

by continuity of the logarithm at $1$. Yet $a_n\to+\infty$, so $(a_n)$ is not a [convergent sequence](../../../../../../convergent-sequence.md) of real numbers. Vanishing differences at every fixed lag do not control the accumulation of many small increments.
