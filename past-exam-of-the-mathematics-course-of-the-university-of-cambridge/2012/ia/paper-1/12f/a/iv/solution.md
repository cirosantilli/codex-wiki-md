The [termwise differentiation of a power series](../../../../../../../termwise-differentiation-of-a-power-series.md) theorem states that inside its [radius of convergence](../../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md), the sum is differentiable and its derivative is the sum of the differentiated series. Accordingly **$\boxed{f'(x)=g(x)\text{ for }|x|<R}$**. There is no blanket differentiability assertion at the convergence endpoints.
