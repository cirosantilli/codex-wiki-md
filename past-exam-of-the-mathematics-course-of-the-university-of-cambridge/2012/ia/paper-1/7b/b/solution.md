<h1 id="7b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $B=S^{-1}AS$ with $S$ invertible, then $tI-B=S^{-1}(tI-A)S$. Multiplicativity of the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) gives

$$
\chi_B(t)=\det(S^{-1})\det(tI-A)\det(S)=\chi_A(t).
$$

The roots of the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md), counted with their multiplicities, are the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) with their [algebraic multiplicities](../../../../../../algebraic-multiplicity.md). Thus **[similar matrices](../../../../../../matrix-similarity.md) have the same [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) with the same [algebraic multiplicities](../../../../../../algebraic-multiplicity.md)**.

**The converse is false.** The matrices

$$
I_2\quad\text{and}\quad J=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
$$

both have [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) $(t-1)^2$, but every matrix similar to $I_2$ is $S^{-1}I_2S=I_2$, whereas $J\ne I_2$. Alternatively, their [geometric multiplicities](../../../../../../geometric-multiplicity.md) for $1$ differ.
