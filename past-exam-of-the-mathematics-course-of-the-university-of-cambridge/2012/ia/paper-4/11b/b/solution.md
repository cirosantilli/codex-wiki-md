<h1 id="11b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) of the uniform disc about its central perpendicular axis is

$$
I_O=\int_0^a r^2\frac{m}{\pi a^2}2\pi r\,dr=\frac12ma^2.
$$

The [parallel axis theorem](../../../../../../parallel-axis-theorem.md) gives $I_A=I_O+ma^2=3ma^2/2$. For angle $\beta$ from the downward resting position, the gravitational [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) is $-mga\sin\beta$, so $I_A\ddot\beta=-mga\sin\beta$. Linearizing at $\beta=0$ gives $\ddot\beta+(2g/(3a))\beta=0$. Thus the [uniform disc pivoted at its rim](../../../../../../uniform-disc-pivoted-at-its-rim.md) is a [physical pendulum](../../../../../../physical-pendulum.md) with period

$$
\boxed{T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{3a}{2g}}.}
$$
