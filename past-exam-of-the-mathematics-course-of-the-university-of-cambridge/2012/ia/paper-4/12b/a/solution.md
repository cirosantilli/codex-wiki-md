<h1 id="12b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [metric signature](../../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(+---)$, as required by the sign of the scalar product in this question. For a massive particle, the [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) is

$$
\boxed{P=(E/c,p)=m\gamma(c,v),\qquad\gamma=(1-|v|^2/c^2)^{-1/2}.}
$$

For a [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) of positive frequency $\nu$ and direction $e$, the [Planck constant](../../../../../../planck-constant.md) gives $E=h\nu$ and

$$
\boxed{K=\left(\frac{h\nu}{c},\frac{h\nu}{c}e\right),\qquad K^2=0.}
$$

A [future-pointing timelike four-vector](../../../../../../future-pointing-timelike-four-vector.md) $P=(P^0,p)$ has $P^0>|p|$ and $P^0>0$ in this signature. The ordinary Euclidean [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) yields

$$
P_1\cdot P_2=P_1^0P_2^0-p_1\cdot p_2\geq P_1^0P_2^0-|p_1||p_2|>0.
$$

This is stronger than the requested nonnegative inequality. For an electron and a positron of rest [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) $m>0$, their future-pointing timelike [four-momenta](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) have $P_1^2=P_2^2=m^2c^2$. Thus

$$
(P_1+P_2)^2=2m^2c^2+2P_1\cdot P_2>0,
$$

which cannot equal the null norm of a single [photon](../../../../../../photon.md). **[Four-momentum conservation](../../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) forbids spontaneous [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) decay into an electron-positron pair in vacuum.** An external body or field able to exchange momentum would be a different system.
