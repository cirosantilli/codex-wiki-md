<h1 id="2d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2D](../2d.md)

An [equivalence relation](../../../../../equivalence-relation.md) $R$ on $X$ is a [reflexive relation](../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) ($xRx$), a [symmetric relation](../../../../../symmetric-relation.md) ($xRy\Rightarrow yRx$), and a [transitive relation](../../../../../transitive-relation.md) ($xRy$, $yRz\Rightarrow xRz$). Its [equivalence class](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) at $x$ is $[x]_R=\{y\in X:xRy\}$. Because $R$ is a [reflexive relation](../../../../../reflexive-relation.md), every class is nonempty and every $x$ belongs to its own class, so the classes cover $X$. If $[x]_R$ and $[z]_R$ share an element $y$, symmetry and [transitivity](../../../../../transitive-relation.md) give $xRz$. For every $w\in[x]_R$, [transitivity](../../../../../transitive-relation.md) then gives $zRw$, so $[x]_R\subseteq[z]_R$; exchanging $x,z$ gives equality. Thus distinct classes are disjoint. **The equivalence classes form a [set partition](../../../../../set-partition.md) of $X$.** On the [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md), the empty family is the corresponding partition.
