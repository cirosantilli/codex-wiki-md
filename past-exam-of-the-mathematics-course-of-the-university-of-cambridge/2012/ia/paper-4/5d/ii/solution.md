<h1 id="5d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**It can be false even on a two-element [finite set](../../../../../../finite-set.md).** On $X=\{0,1\}$, let $f$ and $g$ both be the constant [map](../../../../../../function-class.md) with value $0$. Then $fg=g$, but $f(1)=0\ne1$. In general the equation says exactly that $f$ fixes the image $g(X)$; it says nothing about other points. If $g$ happens to be a [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), it does [force](../../../../../../force.md) $f$ to be the [identity map](../../../../../../identity-function.md), but finiteness alone does not ensure this. The empty [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) and a singleton have only one self-map, so the assertion is trivially true on those [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md).
