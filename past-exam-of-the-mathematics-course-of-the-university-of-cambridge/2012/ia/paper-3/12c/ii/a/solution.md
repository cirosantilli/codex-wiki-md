<h1 id="12c/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) gives $B=-a^2/6$, so the unique radial solution is

$$
\boxed{u(r)=\frac{r^2-a^2}{6}.}
$$

Its [radial Laplacian](../../../../../../../radial-laplacian.md) is one, it is smooth at the origin, and its boundary value is zero. The uniqueness result in part (i) shows that this is also the only solution among all classical [functions](../../../../../../../function-split.md) on the ball, not merely among spherically symmetric ones.
