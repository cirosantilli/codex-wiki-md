<h1 id="12c/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every regular radial solution has outward normal derivative $u'(a)=a/3$, which is nonzero since $a>0$. Therefore **there is no solution** with the stated homogeneous [Neumann boundary condition](../../../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md). This also follows without imposing spherical symmetry: the compatibility condition from part (i) would require

$$
\frac{4\pi a^3}{3}=\int_V1\,dV=\int_S0\,dS=0,
$$

which is impossible. Thus the full-ball [Poisson equation](../../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) has no classical solution for these boundary data, radial or otherwise. A singular $1/r$ term could formally cancel the derivative at $r=a$, but would introduce a source at the origin and violate the problem's equation there.
