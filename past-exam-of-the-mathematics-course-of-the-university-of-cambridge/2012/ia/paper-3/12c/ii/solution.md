<h1 id="12c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a spherically symmetric [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) in three dimensions, the [radial Laplacian](../../../../../../radial-laplacian.md) is $\nabla^2u=r^{-2}(r^2u')'$. On $0<r\leq a$, integrate $(r^2u')'=r^2$ twice to obtain

$$
u'=\frac r3+\frac{A}{r^2},\qquad u(r)=\frac{r^2}{6}-\frac Ar+B.
$$

The region includes the origin. A classical solution must be regular there, so $A=0$: the $1/r$ term is singular and is not a solution of the stated full-ball problem. Hence the complete family on the ball is

$$
\boxed{u(r)=\frac{r^2}{6}+B.}
$$

The boundary cases below use this regular family. Allowing punctured-ball solutions would change the problem and the compatibility calculation.
