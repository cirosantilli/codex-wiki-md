<h1 id="5e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose $x_0\in X$ with [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) $H$. Transitivity means that every point is $gx_0$ for some $g$. Its [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is

$$
G_{gx_0}=gHg^{-1},
$$

since $a(gx_0)=gx_0$ exactly when $g^{-1}ag\in H$. The [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) arising from the [group action](../../../../../../group-action.md) is the set of elements fixing every point, hence

$$
\boxed{\ker(G\to\operatorname{Sym}(X))=\bigcap_{g\in G}gHg^{-1}=\operatorname{Core}_G(H).}
$$

This is the [subgroup core](../../../../../../core-group-theory.md). A [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) is injective precisely when its kernel is trivial. The desired equivalence is therefore exactly the equivalence between a trivial [subgroup core](../../../../../../core-group-theory.md) and a [faithful group action](../../../../../../faithful-group-action.md). No finiteness assumption is required in this part.
