<h1 id="10f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is $F(x)=P(X\leq x)$. For the differentiable distribution in the question, its [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is $f(x)=F'(x)$, so [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) over intervals are obtained by integrating $f$. The survival [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is $P(X>x)=1-F(x)$. Differentiating immediately gives

$$
\boxed{f(x)=-\frac d{dx}P(X>x)}.
$$

The strict inequality is consistent with the definition using $X\leq x$. This relation connects the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) with the decline of the survival [probability](../../../../../../probability.md).
