<h1 id="13b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Removing the death term with $u(x,t)=e^{\mu t}n(x,t)$ gives the [heat equation](../../../../../../heat-equation.md) $u_t=Du_{xx}$ with initial data $N\delta(x)$. The [heat kernel](../../../../../../heat-kernel.md) therefore yields

$$
n(x,t)=\frac{N}{\sqrt{4\pi Dt}}
\exp\left(-\frac{x^2}{4Dt}-\mu t\right).
$$

For each fixed $x$, the exponential mortality factor and diffusive prefactor show that $n(x,t)\to0$ as $t\to\infty$. Its spatial integral is $Ne^{-\mu t}$, the expected number of surviving larvae.
