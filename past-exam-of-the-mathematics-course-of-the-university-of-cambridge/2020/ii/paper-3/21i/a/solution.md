For a bounded [linear operator](../../../../../../linear-operator.md) $T:H\to H$, the [spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) is

$$
\sigma(T)=\{\lambda\in\mathbb C:T-\lambda I\text{ has no bounded inverse}\},
$$

while the [point spectrum](../../../../../../point-spectrum.md) is the set of [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), equivalently the $\lambda$ for which $T-\lambda I$ is not injective.

A [compact operator](../../../../../../compact-operator-split.md) maps the unit ball to a relatively compact set. A [self-adjoint operator](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) satisfies $T=T^*$, or $\langle Tx,y\rangle=\langle x,Ty\rangle$ for all $x,y\in H$. A [finite-rank operator](../../../../../../finite-rank-operator.md) has finite-dimensional image.
