<h1 id="15c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The promise is [Simon's problem](../../../../../../simon-s-problem.md). If $z=0^n$, then $f(x)=f(y)$ implies $x=y$, so $f$ is an [injective function](../../../../../../injective-function.md). If $z\ne0^n$, then each value has exactly the two preimages $x$ and $x\mathbin\oplus z$; thus $f$ is [two-to-one](../../../../../../two-to-one-function.md) and is constant on the two-element cosets of $\{0^n,z\}\leq(\mathbb Z_2)^n$.
