↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

When $z=0^n$, the function $f$ is injective, so the states $|f(x)\rangle$ are distinct [computational basis](../../../../../../../computational-basis.md) states and hence form an [orthonormal set](../../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md). Therefore, for arbitrary signs $\varphi(x,y)$,

$$
\boxed{\left\|\sum_x\varphi(x,y)|f(x)\rangle\right\|^2
=\sum_x|\varphi(x,y)|^2
=2^n
=\left\|\sum_x\varphi(x,y)|x\rangle\right\|^2.}
$$
