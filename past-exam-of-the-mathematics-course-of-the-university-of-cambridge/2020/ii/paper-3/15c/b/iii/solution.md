<h1 id="15c/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the final state, the second-register amplitude vector associated with outcome $y$ is

$$
2^{-n}\sum_x(-1)^{x\cdot y}|f(x)\rangle.
$$

For injective $f$, part (ii) says that its squared norm is $2^{-2n}2^n=2^{-n}$. By the [Born rule for a product-basis measurement](../../../../../../../born-rule-for-a-product-basis-measurement.md), every $y\in B_n$ is therefore observed with probability $2^{-n}$: the input register is uniformly distributed.
