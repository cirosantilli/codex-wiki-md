<h1 id="11h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an odd [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number.md) $p$, the [Legendre symbol](../../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) is

$$
\left(\frac xp\right)=
\begin{cases}
0,&p\mid x,\\
1,&x\not\equiv0\pmod p\text{ is a <quadratic residue>},\\
-1,&x\text{ is a <quadratic nonresidue>}.
\end{cases}
$$

If $p\nmid x$, [multiplicativity of the Legendre symbol](../../../../../../multiplicativity-of-the-legendre-symbol.md) gives

$$
\left(\frac xp\right)\left(\frac{x^{-1}}p\right)
=\left(\frac1p\right)=1.
$$

Both factors belong to $\{1,-1\}$, so they are equal. Thus $(x^{-1}/p)=(x/p)$.
