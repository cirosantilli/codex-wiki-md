↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [eleventh cyclotomic field](../../../../../../eleventh-cyclotomic-field.md) $L=\mathbb Q(\xi_{11})$ has

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q)
\cong(\mathbb Z/11\mathbb Z)^\times\cong C_{10}.
$$

The cyclic group $C_{10}$ has exactly one subgroup of each order $1,2,5,10$. The [Galois correspondence](../../../../../../galois-correspondence.md) therefore gives exactly four subfields:

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
M&[M:\mathbb Q]&\text{an explicit generator}\\ \hline
\mathbb Q&1&-\\
\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-11})&2&
\eta=\xi_{11}+\xi_{11}^3+\xi_{11}^4+\xi_{11}^5+\xi_{11}^9\\
\mathbb Q(\xi_{11}+\xi_{11}^{-1})&5&
\theta=\xi_{11}+\xi_{11}^{-1}\\
L&10&\xi_{11}.
\end{array}
$$

The [Quadratic Gaussian period in the eleventh cyclotomic field](../../../../../../quadratic-gaussian-period-in-the-eleventh-cyclotomic-field.md) satisfies $\eta=(-1+\sqrt{-11})/2$, while $\theta$ generates the [maximal real subfield of the eleventh cyclotomic field](../../../../../../maximal-real-subfield-of-the-eleventh-cyclotomic-field.md). Every subgroup of the abelian Galois group is normal, so all four subfields are Galois over $\mathbb Q$.

Each nontrivial proper subfield has prime degree, two or five. If $a\in M\setminus\mathbb Q$, then $\mathbb Q\subsetneq\mathbb Q(a)\subseteq M$, and the tower law forces $\mathbb Q(a)=M$. Thus every such element is a [primitive element](../../../../../../primitive-element-of-a-field-extension.md). The displayed $\eta$ and $\theta$ are the requested examples. Finally, $\xi_{11}$ itself is a primitive element for $L/\mathbb Q$.
