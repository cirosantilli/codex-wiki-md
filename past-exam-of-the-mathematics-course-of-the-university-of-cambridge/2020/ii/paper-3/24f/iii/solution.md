<h1 id="24f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The intersection with a line $\ell$ is a [hyperplane section](../../../../../../hyperplane-section.md):

$$
D=\ell\cap C,\qquad \mathcal O_C(D)\cong\mathcal O_C(1).
$$

The [canonical bundle of a smooth projective hypersurface](../../../../../../canonical-bundle-of-a-smooth-projective-hypersurface.md) formula, obtained from the [adjunction formula](../../../../../../adjunction-formula.md), gives for a smooth degree-four curve in $\mathbb P^2$

$$
K_C\cong
\left(K_{\mathbb P^2}\otimes\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(4)\right)|_C
\cong\mathcal O_C(-3+4)
\cong\mathcal O_C(1).
$$

**Thus $\mathcal O_C(D)\cong K_C$, so the [divisor class](../../../../../../divisor-class.md) of $D$ is the [canonical divisor](../../../../../../canonical-divisor.md) class. Therefore $D$ is a canonical divisor. Its degree is also $4=2g-2$, consistently with $g=3$.**
