<h1 id="24f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let the [smooth projective curve](../../../../../../smooth-projective-curve.md) be the [projective completion](../../../../../../projective-completion.md) of $f(x,y)=0$, of [degree](../../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $d$, and suppose after interchanging $x$ and $y$ if necessary that $f_y=\partial f/\partial y$ is nonzero. A basis of its [holomorphic differential forms](../../../../../../holomorphic-differential-form.md) is

$$
\left\{
x^iy^j\frac{dx}{f_y}:i,j\geq0,\ i+j\leq d-3
\right\}.
$$

Equivalently one may use $-x^iy^jdy/f_x$. These expressions agree on their common domain because differentiating $f(x,y)=0$ gives $f_xdx+f_ydy=0$. The allowed [monomials](../../../../../../monomial.md) are precisely the homogeneous polynomials of degree at most $d-3$, in accordance with the [adjunction formula](../../../../../../adjunction-formula.md) $K_C\cong\mathcal O_C(d-3)$.
