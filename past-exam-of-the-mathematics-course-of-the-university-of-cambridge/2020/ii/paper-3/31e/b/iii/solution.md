<h1 id="31e/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The factor $x^2(2-x^2)$ is positive for $0<|x|<\sqrt2$. The product $y^3\sin2y$ is positive for $0<|y|<\pi/2$. Therefore $\dot V<0$ away from the origin throughout

$$
\boxed{|x|<x_0=\sqrt2,
\qquad |y|<y_0=\frac\pi2}.
$$

Neither side length can be enlarged while retaining a strict Lyapunov function on the whole rectangle: at $(x,0)$ with $|x|>\sqrt2$, the first term makes $\dot V>0$, while at $(0,y)$ immediately beyond $|y|=\pi/2$, the second term makes $\dot V>0$.

Strict decrease on this rectangle does not by itself put the whole rectangle in the [domain of stability](../../../../../../../basin-of-attraction.md), because the rectangle has not been shown to be [positively invariant](../../../../../../../positively-invariant-set.md); a trajectory may leave it before convergence.
