<h1 id="7e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each nonzero [period lattice](../../../../../../period-lattice.md) point $\omega$, the [binomial series](../../../../../../binomial-series.md) gives

$$
\frac1{(z-\omega)^2}-\frac1{\omega^2}
=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(k+1)\frac{z^k}{\omega^{k+2}}.
$$

Pairing $\omega$ with $-\omega$ cancels every odd power. Hence the [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) of the [Weierstrass elliptic function](../../../../../../weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) is

$$
\wp(z)=\frac1{z^2}
+3z^2\sum_{\omega\ne0}\omega^{-4}
+5z^4\sum_{\omega\ne0}\omega^{-6}+O(z^6)
=\frac1{z^2}+\frac{g_2}{20}z^2+\frac{g_3}{28}z^4+O(z^6),
$$

which is the stated [coefficient formula](../../../../../../laurent-coefficients-of-the-weierstrass-elliptic-function.md).
