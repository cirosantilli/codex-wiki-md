↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Yes.** Let $A$ be $T$-invariant. Since the itinerary variables generate $\mathcal A$ modulo null sets, $A$ can be regarded as an event in the canonical sequence model. For every $N$, invariance and $X_n\circ T=X_{n+1}$ imply

$$
A=T^{-N}A\in\sigma(X_N,X_{N+1},\ldots).
$$

**Thus $A$ belongs to the [tail sigma-algebra](../../../../../../../tail-sigma-algebra.md) of the i.i.d. sequence $(X_n)$. By the [Kolmogorov zero-one law](../../../../../../../kolmogorov-s-zero-one-law.md), $m(A)$ is zero or one. Hence the [tent map](../../../../../../../tent-map.md) is an [ergodic measure-preserving transformation](../../../../../../../ergodicity.md).**
