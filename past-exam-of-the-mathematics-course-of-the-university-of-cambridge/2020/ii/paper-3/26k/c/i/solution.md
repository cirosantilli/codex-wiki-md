↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [Borel set](../../../../../../../borel-set.md) $A\subseteq[0,1]$, the two inverse branches of the [tent map](../../../../../../../tent-map.md) are $y\mapsto y/2$ and $y\mapsto1-y/2$. Apart from their immaterial common endpoint,

$$
T^{-1}A
=\frac A2\ \cup\ \left(1-\frac A2\right).
$$

Each branch scales [Lebesgue measure](../../../../../../../lebesgue-measure.md) by $1/2$, and their images lie in opposite half-intervals. Hence

$$
m(T^{-1}A)=\frac12m(A)+\frac12m(A)=m(A).
$$

Also $m([0,1])=1$, so $m$ is an invariant probability measure.
