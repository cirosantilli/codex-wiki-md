↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [measure-preserving transformation](../../../../../../measure-preserving-transformation.md) $T$ on a [probability space](../../../../../../probability-space.md) $(X,\mathcal A,m)$ is [ergodic](../../../../../../ergodicity.md) if every [invariant event](../../../../../../invariant-sigma-algebra.md) $A\in\mathcal A$, meaning

$$
T^{-1}A=A
$$

up to an $m$-null set, satisfies $m(A)\in\{0,1\}$. Thus the [invariant sigma-algebra](../../../../../../invariant-sigma-algebra.md) is trivial modulo null sets.
