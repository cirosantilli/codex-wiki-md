<h1 id="14d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On long wavelengths the gradient term is negligible. The [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) implies

$$
c_s^2k_J^2=\frac{4\pi G\bar\rho}{c^2}=\frac32H^2.
$$

During [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md), $a\propto t^{2/3}$ and $H=2/(3t)$, so the [linear growth equation](../../../../../../linear-growth-equation.md) becomes

$$
\ddot\delta+\frac4{3t}\dot\delta-\frac2{3t^2}\delta=0.
$$

The [power-law ansatz](../../../../../../power-law-ansatz.md) $\delta\propto t^s$ gives $s=2/3$ or $s=-1$. Since $a\propto t^{2/3}$, the growing and decaying modes are

$$
\boxed{\delta_+\propto a,\qquad \delta_-\propto a^{-3/2}}.
$$
