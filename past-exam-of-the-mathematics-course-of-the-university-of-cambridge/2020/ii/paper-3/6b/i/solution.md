Adding the three equations of the [SIR model with waning immunity](../../../../../../sir-model-with-waning-immunity.md) gives

$$
\frac{d}{dt}(S+I+R)=0.
$$

**Hence [conservation of population](../../../../../../conservation-of-population.md) holds: $N=S+I+R$ is an [integral of motion](../../../../../../integral-of-motion.md).**
