<h1 id="6b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At an [endemic equilibrium](../../../../../../endemic-equilibrium.md), $I^*>0$, so $0=\dot I=I^*(\beta S^*-\gamma)$ forces

$$
S^*=\frac\gamma\beta.
$$

The equation $\dot R=0$ gives $R^*=\gamma I^*/\alpha$. The [conservation law](../../../../../../conservation-law.md) $S^*+I^*+R^*=N$ therefore yields

$$
I^*=\frac{\alpha(\beta N-\gamma)}{\beta(\alpha+\gamma)},
\qquad
R^*=\frac{\gamma(\beta N-\gamma)}{\beta(\alpha+\gamma)}.
$$

A positive endemic state exists exactly when $\beta N>\gamma$, equivalently when the [basic reproduction number](../../../../../../basic-reproduction-number.md) $\mathcal R_0=\beta N/\gamma$ exceeds one.
