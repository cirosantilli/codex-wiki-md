<h1 id="35a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expanding $(e^x-1)^{-1}=\sum_{n\geq1}e^{-nx}$ and using the [Gamma integral](../../../../../../gamma-integral.md) gives

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^3}{e^x-1}\,dx
=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\int_0^\infty x^3e^{-nx}\,dx
=6\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac1{n^4}
=\frac{\pi^4}{15}.
$$

Thus the energy of [blackbody radiation](../../../../../../black-body-radiation.md) is

$$
\boxed{E(T,V)=\frac{\pi^2}{15\hbar^3c^3}VT^4}.
$$
