↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Conjugating the [reflection operator](../../../../../../reflection-operator.md) by a [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md) reflects about the conjugated state:

$$
UR_{00}^\pi U^\dagger
=I-2U|00\rangle\langle00|U^\dagger
=I-2|\psi_{\rm in}\rangle\langle\psi_{\rm in}|
=R_{\psi_{\rm in}}^\pi.
$$

**Thus one may take $|\psi\rangle=|\psi_{\rm in}\rangle=U|00\rangle$.**
