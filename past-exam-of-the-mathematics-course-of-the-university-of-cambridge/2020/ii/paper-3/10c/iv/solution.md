<h1 id="10c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Choose $U$ to prepare the uniform superposition, so the marked-state probability is $p=1/4$. Apply the [marked-state phase oracle](../../../../../../marked-state-phase-oracle.md) $R_g^\pi$, followed by the reflection $UR_{00}^\pi U^\dagger$ about the prepared state. Part (iii) shows that this single [Grover iteration](../../../../../../grover-s-algorithm.md) produces $-|g\rangle$. A [projective measurement](../../../../../../projective-measurement.md) in the four-item basis therefore returns the marked element with probability one. This is [exact Grover search on four entries](../../../../../../exact-grover-search-on-four-entries.md).
