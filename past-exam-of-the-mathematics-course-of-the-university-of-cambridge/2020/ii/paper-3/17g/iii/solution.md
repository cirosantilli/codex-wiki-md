<h1 id="17g/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $t(G)$ be the number of [triangles in a graph](../../../../../../triangle-in-a-graph.md). Count incidences $(e,\Delta)$ in which the edge $e$ lies in the triangle $\Delta$. Every edge contributes at least one incidence by hypothesis, while every triangle contributes exactly three. Therefore

$$
3t(G)\geq e(G)=n^2+1,
\qquad
 t(G)\geq\frac{n^2+1}{3}.
$$

This is the [edge-triangle incidence bound](../../../../../../edge-triangle-incidence-bound.md).
