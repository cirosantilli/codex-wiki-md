<h1 id="37d/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $u=t-r$ and $R=r$, so

$$
dt=du+dR,
\qquad dr=dR.
$$

Writing now $\Sigma=R^2+a^2\cos^2\theta$ and $\Delta=R^2+a^2$, the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) becomes

$$
ds^2=-du^2-2\,du\,dR
-\frac{a^2\sin^2\theta}{\Delta}dR^2
+\Sigma d\theta^2
+\Delta\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2.
$$

The squared norm of the coordinate vector is its $RR$ metric component:

$$
g(\partial_R,\partial_R)=g_{RR}
=-\frac{a^2\sin^2\theta}{R^2+a^2}.
$$

For $a>0$ this is nonzero away from the symmetry axis, so $\partial_R$ is not a [null vector](../../../../../../../null-vector.md).
