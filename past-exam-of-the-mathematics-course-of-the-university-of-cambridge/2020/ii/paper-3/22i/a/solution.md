↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [continuous dual space](../../../../../../continuous-dual-space-split.md) $X'$ consists of all bounded linear maps $\Lambda:X\to\mathbb C$, with

$$
\|\Lambda\|_{X'}
=\sup_{\|x\|_X\leq1}|\Lambda x|.
$$

If $Y=L^p(\mathbb R^n)$ with $1\leq p<\infty$ and $q$ is the [conjugate exponent](../../../../../../holder-inequality.md), then [duality of Lp spaces](../../../../../../duality-of-lp-spaces.md) identifies $Y'$ isometrically with $L^q(\mathbb R^n)$, including $q=\infty$ when $p=1$. Every functional has the form

$$
\Lambda_h(f)=\int_{\mathbb R^n}f(x)\overline{h(x)}\,dx,
\qquad h\in L^q,
$$

and $\|\Lambda_h\|=\|h\|_q$.
