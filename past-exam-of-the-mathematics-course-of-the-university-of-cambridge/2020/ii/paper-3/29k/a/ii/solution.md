<h1 id="29k/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $M_t=\sup_{0\leq s\leq t}B_s$ and let $a>0$. The [Brownian reflection principle](../../../../../../../reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) says that reflecting a Brownian path about the level $a$ after its first hitting time of $a$ preserves its law. In particular, for $b\leq a$,

$$
\mathbb P(M_t\geq a,\ B_t\leq b)
=\mathbb P(B_t\geq2a-b).
$$

Taking $b=a$ gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(M_t\geq a)=2\mathbb P(B_t\geq a).}
$$
