A real-valued process $(B_t)_{t\geq0}$ is a standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) if:

- $B_0=0$ almost surely;
- it has [independent increments](../../../../../../../independent-increments.md);
- $B_t-B_s$ has the [normal distribution](../../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) $N(0,t-s)$ whenever $0\leq s<t$; and
- its sample paths are almost surely [continuous](../../../../../../../continuous-function.md).
