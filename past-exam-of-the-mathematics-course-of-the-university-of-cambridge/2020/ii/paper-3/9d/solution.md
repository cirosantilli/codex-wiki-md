<h1 id="9d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9D](../9d.md)

For equal internal degeneracy, the [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) of an isotropic ultrarelativistic species is proportional to

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{p^2\,dp}{e^{\beta pc}\mp1}.
$$

With $x=\beta pc$, compare the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) and [Bose-Einstein distribution](../../../../../bose-einstein-distribution.md) using

$$
\frac1{e^x+1}=\frac1{e^x-1}-\frac2{e^{2x}-1}.
$$

For $s>0$, a [change of variables](../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) in the second term gives the [fermion-to-boson thermal integral ratio](../../../../../fermion-to-boson-thermal-integral-ratio.md)

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^s\,dx}{e^x+1}
=\left(1-2^{-s}\right)
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^s\,dx}{e^x-1}.
$$

Particle number has $s=2$, so $n_f=(1-2^{-2})n_b=3n_b/4$. The [energy density](../../../../../energy-density.md) contains one further factor $E=pc$, so it has $s=3$ and

$$
\rho_f=(1-2^{-3})\rho_b=\frac78\rho_b.
$$
