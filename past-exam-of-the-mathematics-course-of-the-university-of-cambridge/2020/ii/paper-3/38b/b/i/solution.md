<h1 id="38b/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Conservation of volume flux through a circular arc of radius $r$ gives

$$
\alpha r(-u_r)=\alpha Q,
$$

so the outer flow is the [sink flow in a sector](../../../../../../../sink-flow-in-a-sector.md)

$$
\boxed{u_r=-\frac Qr,
\qquad u_\theta=0,
\qquad \psi=-Q\theta}.
$$

This is a [potential flow](../../../../../../../potential-flow.md) and already satisfies the [no-penetration boundary condition](../../../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md) on the radial walls. It has nonzero tangential speed $Q/r$ there, however, and therefore violates the [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md). Thin vorticity-bearing [boundary layers](../../../../../../../boundary-layer.md) are needed to reduce that speed to zero at each wall.
