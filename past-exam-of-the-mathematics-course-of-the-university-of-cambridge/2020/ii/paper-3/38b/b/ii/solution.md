<h1 id="38b/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Along $y=0$, put $x=r$ and $U(x)=-Q/x$. If $\delta(x)$ is the layer thickness, then

$$
u u_x=O\!\left(\frac{Q^2}{x^3}\right),
\qquad
\nu u_{yy}=O\!\left(\frac{\nu Q}{x\delta^2}\right).
$$

The [boundary-layer thickness from an advection-diffusion balance](../../../../../../../boundary-layer-thickness-from-an-advection-diffusion-balance.md) therefore gives

$$
\frac{Q^2}{x^3}\sim\frac{\nu Q}{x\delta^2},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{\delta=x\sqrt{\frac\nu Q}}
$$

up to a constant factor absorbed into the similarity coordinate.
