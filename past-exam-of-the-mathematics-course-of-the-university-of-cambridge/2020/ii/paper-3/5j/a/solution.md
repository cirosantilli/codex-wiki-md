<h1 id="5j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Conditional independence makes the [log-likelihood](../../../../../../log-likelihood.md)

$$
\ell(\theta)=\sum_{i=1}^n\log f_{x_i}(Y_i;\theta).
$$

A [maximum-likelihood estimator](../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) is any $\widehat\theta\in\operatorname*{argmax}_{\theta\in\Theta}\ell(\theta)$. If the model has $q$ fitted real parameters, its [Akaike information criterion](../../../../../../akaike-information-criterion.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{AIC}=-2\ell(\widehat\theta)+2q.}
$$
