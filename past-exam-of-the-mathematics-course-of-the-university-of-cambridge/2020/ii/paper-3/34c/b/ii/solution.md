<h1 id="34c/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let

$$
\kappa_0=\lim_{k\to0}\kappa
=\frac{\sqrt{2mV_0}}\hbar.
$$

Using $\sin(2ka)=2ka+O(k^3)$ and $\cos(2ka)=1+O(k^2)$ in the phase-shift formula gives

$$
\frac{\tan\delta_0}{k}
\longrightarrow
\frac1{\kappa_0\cot(\kappa_0a)}-2a
=\frac{\tan(\kappa_0a)}{\kappa_0}-2a.
$$

By the definition of the [scattering length from a partial-wave S-matrix](../../../../../../../scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md),

$$
\boxed{
a_s=-\lim_{k\to0}\frac{\tan\delta_0}{k}
=2a-\frac{\tan(\kappa_0a)}{\kappa_0}}.
$$

Thus $a_s$ diverges exactly when

$$
\boxed{\kappa_0a=\left(n+\frac12\right)\pi,
\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots}.
$$

At each such value, an S-wave [bound state](../../../../../../../bound-state.md) lies at the continuum threshold: the exterior zero-energy radial solution becomes constant, its logarithmic derivative vanishes, and the system has a [zero-energy scattering resonance](../../../../../../../zero-energy-scattering-resonance.md). As the well deepens through the critical value, a new bound state appears and the scattering length passes through infinity and changes sign.
