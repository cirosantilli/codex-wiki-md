<h1 id="21f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $A\in GL_2(\mathbb Z)$. If $r-r'\in\mathbb Z^2$, then $A(r-r')\in\mathbb Z^2$, so

$$
f_A(p(r))=p(Ar)
$$

defines a continuous map on $X=\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2$. Since $\det A=\pm1$, the [integral general linear group](../../../../../../integral-general-linear-group.md) contains $A^{-1}$ as another integer matrix, and $f_{A^{-1}}$ is the inverse of $f_A$. Hence $f_A$ is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md), and $f_A(x_0)=x_0$.

The map $A:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ is the lift of $f_A$ fixing the origin. A loop whose lift ends at $m\in\mathbb Z^2$ is sent to a loop whose lift ends at $Am$. Under the isomorphism in part (a), the induced map is therefore

$$
\boxed{f_{A*}(m)=Am}.
$$

This is precisely the [integral linear automorphism of the torus](../../../../../../integral-linear-automorphism-of-the-torus.md) associated with $A$.
