<h1 id="7e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}z>0$, integration by parts gives

$$
I(z+1)
=\int_0^\infty t^ze^{-t}\,dt
=\left[-t^ze^{-t}\right]_0^\infty
+z\int_0^\infty t^{z-1}e^{-t}\,dt.
$$

The boundary term vanishes, so the [Gamma function recurrence](../../../../../../gamma-function-recurrence.md) is

$$
\boxed{I(z+1)=zI(z)}.
$$
