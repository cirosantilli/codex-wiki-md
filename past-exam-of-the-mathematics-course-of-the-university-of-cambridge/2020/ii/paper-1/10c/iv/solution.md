<h1 id="10c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [spectral projector](../../../../../../spectral-projector.md) for outcome $+1$ is

$$
\Pi_+=\frac{I+A}{2}.
$$

For $|1\rangle=(0,1)^T$, the [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\mathbb P(+1)
=\langle1|\Pi_+|1\rangle
=\frac{1-n_z}{2}
=\boxed{\sin^2\frac\theta2}.
$$
