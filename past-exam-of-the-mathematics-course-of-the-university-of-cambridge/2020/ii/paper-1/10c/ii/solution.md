↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In a [spherical coordinate system](../../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md),

$$
\widehat n=(\sin\theta\cos\phi,\sin\theta\sin\phi,\cos\theta).
$$

Since $n_x\mp in_y=\sin\theta\,e^{\mp i\phi}$,

$$
\boxed{
A=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\theta&e^{-i\phi}\sin\theta\\
e^{i\phi}\sin\theta&-\cos\theta
\end{pmatrix}}.
$$
