↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) says that the probability of a projective-measurement outcome is the squared norm of the state projected onto the corresponding eigenspace. The basis vectors in the state whose second qubit is one are

$$
|010\rangle,\qquad |110\rangle,\qquad |111\rangle.
$$

They are mutually orthogonal, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(\text{second qubit}=1)
=|b|^2+|c|^2+|d|^2}.
$$
