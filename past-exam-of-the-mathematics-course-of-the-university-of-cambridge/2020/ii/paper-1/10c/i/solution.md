Using the [Pauli matrices](../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md),

$$
\boxed{
A=n_x\sigma_x+n_y\sigma_y+n_z\sigma_z
=\begin{pmatrix}
n_z&n_x-in_y\\
n_x+in_y&-n_z
\end{pmatrix}}.
$$
