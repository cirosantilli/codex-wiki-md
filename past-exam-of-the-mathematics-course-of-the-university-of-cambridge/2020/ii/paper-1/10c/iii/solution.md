<h1 id="10c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The Pauli multiplication relations and $|\widehat n|=1$ give

$$
A^2=(\widehat n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)^2=I.
$$

Therefore the possible measurement outcomes, the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $A$, are

$$
\boxed{+1\quad\text{and}\quad-1}.
$$
