<h1 id="16h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assertion (ii) is always true. Since $\kappa$ is infinite and $\kappa\le\lambda$,

$$
2^\lambda\le\kappa^\lambda\le\lambda^\lambda
\le(2^\lambda)^\lambda
=2^{\lambda^2}=2^\lambda,
$$

where the last equality uses $\lambda^2=\lambda$. Thus the [cardinal exponentiation between two and the exponent](../../../../../../cardinal-exponentiation-between-two-and-the-exponent.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\kappa\le\lambda\implies\kappa^\lambda=2^\lambda}.
$$
