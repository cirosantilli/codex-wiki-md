<h1 id="18g/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because $\operatorname{char}K=0$, the [formal derivative](../../../../../../../formal-derivative-in-positive-characteristic.md) $f'$ is nonzero and has smaller degree than $f$. Since $f$ is [irreducible](../../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md), it cannot divide $f'$, so

$$
\gcd(f,f')=1.
$$

By the [Bezout identity](../../../../../../../bezout-identity.md) for polynomials, there are $a,b\in K[X]$ with $af+bf'=1$. A repeated root of $f$ in $M$ would also be a root of $f'$, contradicting this identity. Thus the result that an [irreducible polynomial in characteristic zero is separable](../../../../../../../irreducible-polynomial-in-characteristic-zero-is-separable.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\text{all roots of }f\text{ in }M\text{ are distinct}}.
$$
