<h1 id="18g/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $q=p^r$, where $p=\operatorname{char}K$. For

$$
h(X)=X^{q^n}-X,
$$

the derivative is

$$
h'(X)=q^nX^{q^n-1}-1=-1,
$$

so $h$ is a [separable polynomial](../../../../../../../separable-polynomial.md) and all its roots in its [splitting field](../../../../../../../splitting-field.md) $L$ are distinct.

Let $R$ be the set of these roots. The [Frobenius endomorphism](../../../../../../../frobenius-endomorphism.md) and the identities

$$
(x+y)^{q^n}=x^{q^n}+y^{q^n},
\qquad
(xy)^{q^n}=x^{q^n}y^{q^n}
$$

show that $R$ is closed under addition and multiplication. It is also closed under additive inverses and, for $x\ne0$, inversion. Thus $R$ is a [finite field](../../../../../../../finite-field.md). Since $h$ splits into $q^n$ distinct linear factors, $|R|=q^n$. The field generated by all roots is therefore $R$ itself, so $L=R$ and

$$
q^{[L:K]}=|L|=q^n.
$$

Hence the [polynomial characterization of a finite field](../../../../../../../polynomial-characterization-of-a-finite-field.md) gives

$$
\boxed{[L:K]=n}.
$$

Now let $f\in K[X]$ be irreducible of degree $n$, and let $\alpha$ be a root in an [algebraic closure](../../../../../../../algebraic-closure.md). The [minimal polynomial of an algebraic element](../../../../../../../minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) gives

$$
[K(\alpha):K]=n,
$$

so $K(\alpha)$ has $q^n$ elements. Its nonzero elements form a [finite group](../../../../../../../finite-group.md) under multiplication, of order $q^n-1$; hence $\alpha^{q^n}=\alpha$. Therefore $\alpha$ is a root of $X^{q^n}-X$, and its minimal polynomial $f$ divides every polynomial over $K$ that vanishes at $\alpha$. Thus the [irreducible factors of a finite-field Frobenius polynomial](../../../../../../../irreducible-factors-of-a-finite-field-frobenius-polynomial.md) satisfy

$$
\boxed{f(X)\mid X^{q^n}-X}.
$$
