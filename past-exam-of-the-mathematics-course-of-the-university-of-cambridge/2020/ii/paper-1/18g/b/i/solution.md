A [splitting field](../../../../../../../splitting-field.md) of $f\in K[X]$ is an extension $L/K$ in which

$$
f(X)=c\prod_{r=1}^d(X-\alpha_r)
$$

for roots $\alpha_r\in L$, and which is generated by those roots:

$$
\boxed{L=K(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_d)}.
$$

The generation condition is the required minimality: no proper intermediate field containing $K$ can still contain every root.
