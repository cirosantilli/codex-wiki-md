<h1 id="18g/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For any prime $p$, take

$$
K=\mathbb F_p(t),
\qquad
f(X)=X^p-t.
$$

The [Eisenstein criterion](../../../../../../../eisenstein-criterion.md), applied at the prime element $t$ of $\mathbb F_p[t]$, shows that $f$ is irreducible over $K$ and therefore has degree $p$. If $\alpha$ is a root in any extension field, the [Frobenius endomorphism](../../../../../../../frobenius-endomorphism.md) gives

$$
f(X)=X^p-\alpha^p=(X-\alpha)^p.
$$

It follows that this [purely inseparable polynomial over a rational function field](../../../../../../../purely-inseparable-polynomial-over-a-rational-function-field.md) has at most one distinct root in every extension, and that root has multiplicity $p$, as required.
