<h1 id="11i/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At coordinate $j$, addition in $\mathbb F_2$ gives $x_j+y_j=1$ exactly when $x_j\ne y_j$. Therefore the number of ones in $x+y$ is exactly the number of coordinates in which the two words differ:

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{wt}(x+y)=d_H(x,y)}.
$$
