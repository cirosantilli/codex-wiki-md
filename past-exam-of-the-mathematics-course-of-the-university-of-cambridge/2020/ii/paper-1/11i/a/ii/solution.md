<h1 id="11i/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Form the [punctured code](../../../../../../../punctured-code.md) by deleting one fixed coordinate from every codeword. Two distinct original words differ in at least $d>1$ positions, so after one deletion they still differ and cannot coalesce. The new code therefore retains size $M$, while every pairwise [Hamming distance](../../../../../../../hamming-distance.md) falls by at most one. It is an $[n-1,M,d']$ code with $d'\ge d-1$, and hence also an $[n-1,M,d-1]$ code in the minimum-distance-at-least convention. Therefore

$$
\boxed{A(n,d)\le A(n-1,d-1)}.
$$
