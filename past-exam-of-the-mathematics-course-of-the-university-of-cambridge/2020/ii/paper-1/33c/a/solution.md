↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using the rules for the [matrix transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) together with $L^T=L$ and $B^T=-B$,

$$
[B,L]^T=(BL-LB)^T=L^TB^T-B^TL^T=-LB+BL=[B,L].
$$

**Thus the [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) $[B,L]$ is a [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md).**
