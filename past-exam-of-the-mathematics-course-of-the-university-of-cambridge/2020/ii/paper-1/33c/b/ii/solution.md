↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The $(j,j+1)$ entry of $dot L$ is $dot a_j$. Comparing it with the result of part (i) gives

$$
\boxed{
\dot a_j=f(a_{j-1},a_j,a_{j+1})
=a_j(a_{j+1}^2-a_{j-1}^2),
\qquad 1\leq j\leq n-1.
}
$$

For $q_j=a_j^2$, this becomes $dot q_j=2q_j(q_{j+1}-q_{j-1})$, the [Finite Volterra lattice](../../../../../../../finite-volterra-lattice.md).
