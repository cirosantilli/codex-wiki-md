<h1 id="13j/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

`Kilometres` is an [ordinal categorical variable](../../../../../../ordinal-categorical-variable.md): its five levels have a meaningful order. Treating it as an ordinary factor would use four [indicator variables](../../../../../../indicator-variable.md) and hence four unrelated [regression coefficients](../../../../../../regression-coefficient.md). Converting the levels to numerical scores instead imposes a single linear trend across successive mileage bands. This is more parsimonious and gives greater precision when that trend is a reasonable approximation, although it also assumes that adjacent levels are equally spaced in their effect.
