The graph is a [profile likelihood](../../../../../../profile-likelihood.md) for the parameter $\lambda$ of a [Box–Cox transformation](../../../../../../box-cox-transformation.md). Its maximum is close to $\lambda=0$, and the displayed likelihood interval contains zero but excludes $\lambda=1$. Since $\lambda=0$ gives a [logarithmic transformation](../../../../../../logarithmic-transformation.md) whereas $\lambda=1$ leaves the response unchanged up to an affine transformation, the plot supports fitting

$$
\log(\text{Paymentperpolicyyr})
$$

as the response in `model2`.
