<h1 id="26i/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The identity matrix belongs to $O(1,3)$. If $A,B\in O(1,3)$, then

$$
(AB)^TM(AB)=B^T(A^TMA)B=B^TMB=M,
$$

so the set is closed under multiplication. Taking determinants in $A^TMA=M$ gives $(\det A)^2=1$, so $A$ is invertible. Rearranging the defining identity gives

$$
A^{-1}=M^{-1}A^TM=MA^TM.
$$

Its inverse also preserves $M$, either directly or because

$$
(A^{-1})^TM A^{-1}=M.
$$

**Thus the [Lorentz group](../../../../../../../lorentz-group.md) $O(1,3)$ is a group under matrix multiplication.**
