The [Huffman coding](../../../../../../../huffman-coding.md) merges are

$$
\frac1{10}+\frac2{10}=\frac3{10},
\qquad
\frac3{10}+\frac3{10}=\frac6{10},
\qquad
\frac4{10}+\frac6{10}=1.
$$

For example, the resulting code may be

$$
\boxed{1\mapsto110,\quad
2\mapsto111,\quad
3\mapsto10,\quad
4\mapsto0}.
$$

Its expected length is

$$
\boxed{\overline\ell_{\rm H}
=\frac1{10}3+\frac2{10}3+\frac3{10}2+\frac4{10}1
=1.9}.
$$
