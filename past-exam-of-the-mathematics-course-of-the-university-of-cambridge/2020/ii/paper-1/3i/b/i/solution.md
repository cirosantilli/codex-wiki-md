The Shannon-Fano lengths are

$$
(\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3,\ell_4)=(4,3,2,2).
$$

One corresponding prefix-free assignment is

$$
\boxed{1\mapsto1010,\quad
2\mapsto100,\quad
3\mapsto00,\quad
4\mapsto01}.
$$

Its expected word length is

$$
\boxed{\overline\ell_{\rm SF}
=\frac1{10}4+\frac2{10}3+\frac3{10}2+\frac4{10}2
=2.4}.
$$
