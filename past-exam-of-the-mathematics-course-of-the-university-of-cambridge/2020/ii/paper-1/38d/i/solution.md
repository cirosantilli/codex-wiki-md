↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The coordinate expression for the [Lie bracket of vector fields](../../../../../../lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) is

$$
[V,W]^\alpha
=V^\mu\partial_\mu W^\alpha-W^\mu\partial_\mu V^\alpha.
$$

For the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md),

$$
V^\mu\nabla_\mu W^\alpha-W^\mu\nabla_\mu V^\alpha
=V^\mu\partial_\mu W^\alpha-W^\mu\partial_\mu V^\alpha
+\Gamma^\alpha_{\mu\nu}(V^\mu W^\nu-W^\mu V^\nu).
$$

The final term vanishes because the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) are symmetric in $\mu,\nu$ while the vector product in parentheses is antisymmetric. Hence

$$
\boxed{[V,W]^\alpha
=V^\mu\nabla_\mu W^\alpha-W^\mu\nabla_\mu V^\alpha.}
$$
