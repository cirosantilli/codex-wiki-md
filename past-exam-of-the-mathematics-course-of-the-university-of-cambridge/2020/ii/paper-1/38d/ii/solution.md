<h1 id="38d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The coordinate vector fields on the parameter surface commute, so their pushforwards satisfy

$$
[T,S]=0.
$$

Because the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) is torsion free, part (i) then gives

$$
\nabla_TS=\nabla_ST.
$$

Every $t$-curve is an affinely parametrized [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md), hence $\nabla_TT=0$. Applying the definition of the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
R(T,S)T
&=\nabla_T\nabla_ST-\nabla_S\nabla_TT-\nabla_{[T,S]}T\\
&=\nabla_T\nabla_ST\\
&=\nabla_T\nabla_TS.
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore the connecting vector is a [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) and obeys the [geodesic deviation](../../../../../../geodesic-deviation.md) equation

$$
\boxed{\nabla_T\nabla_TS=R(T,S)T.}
$$
