<h1 id="38d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

First differentiate the unit normalization $X^\alpha X_\alpha=-1$. [Metric compatibility](../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) gives

$$
B_{\alpha\beta}X^\alpha
=X^\alpha\nabla_\beta X_\alpha
=\frac12\nabla_\beta(X^\alpha X_\alpha)=0.
$$

The [timelike geodesic vector field](../../../../../../timelike-geodesic-vector-field.md) equation gives the other contraction,

$$
B_{\alpha\beta}X^\beta
=X^\beta\nabla_\beta X_\alpha=0.
$$

For the [spatial projector of a timelike congruence](../../../../../../spatial-projector-of-a-timelike-congruence.md),

$$
h_{\alpha\beta}X^\alpha
=X_\beta+(X_\alpha X^\alpha)X_\beta=0,
$$

and symmetry also gives $h_{\alpha\beta}X^\beta=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{B_{\alpha\beta}X^\alpha=B_{\alpha\beta}X^\beta
=h_{\alpha\beta}X^\alpha=h_{\alpha\beta}X^\beta=0.}
$$

Splitting the [deformation tensor of a timelike geodesic congruence](../../../../../../deformation-tensor-of-a-timelike-geodesic-congruence.md) into symmetric and antisymmetric parts and substituting the definitions gives

$$
B_{\alpha\beta}
=B_{(\alpha\beta)}+B_{[\alpha\beta]}
=\frac13\Theta h_{\alpha\beta}+\sigma_{\alpha\beta}+\omega_{\alpha\beta}.
$$

Moreover, the orthogonality of $B_{\alpha\beta}$ implies

$$
\Theta=B^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}
=B^\alpha{}_\alpha,
$$

while in four dimensions

$$
g^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}
=4+X^\alpha X_\alpha=3.
$$

It follows that

$$
g^{\alpha\beta}\sigma_{\alpha\beta}
=B^\alpha{}_\alpha-\frac13\Theta\,g^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}=0.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{B_{\alpha\beta}=\frac13\Theta h_{\alpha\beta}
+\sigma_{\alpha\beta}+\omega_{\alpha\beta},
\qquad g^{\alpha\beta}\sigma_{\alpha\beta}=0,}
$$

which is the [kinematic decomposition of a timelike geodesic congruence](../../../../../../kinematic-decomposition-of-a-timelike-geodesic-congruence.md).
