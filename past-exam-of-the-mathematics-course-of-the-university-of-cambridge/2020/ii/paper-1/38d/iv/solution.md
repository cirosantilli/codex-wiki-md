<h1 id="38d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The fields $X$ and $S$ arise by pushing forward the two coordinate vectors on the two-parameter family of geodesics, so $[X,S]=0$. Part (i), or equivalently the torsion-free property, gives

$$
\nabla_XS=\nabla_SX.
$$

In components,

$$
X^\mu\nabla_\mu S^\alpha
=S^\mu\nabla_\mu X^\alpha
=S^\mu B^\alpha{}_\mu.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{X^\mu\nabla_\mu S^\alpha=B^\alpha{}_\mu S^\mu.}
$$

This says that the [deformation tensor of a timelike geodesic congruence](../../../../../../deformation-tensor-of-a-timelike-geodesic-congruence.md) gives the relative velocity of neighboring geodesics represented by the [geodesic deviation](../../../../../../geodesic-deviation.md) vector.
