<h1 id="2h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Define

$$
H(s,t)=\gamma(t)+s\sigma(t),
\qquad 0\le s,t\le1.
$$

The strict inequality $|s\sigma(t)|<|\gamma(t)|$ ensures that $H(s,t)\ne0$ for every $(s,t)$, while $H(s,0)=H(s,1)$. Thus $H$ is a homotopy through closed loops in $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$ from $\gamma$ to $\gamma+\sigma$. By [homotopy invariance of winding number](../../../../../../homotopy-invariance-of-winding-number.md),

$$
\boxed{w(\gamma+\sigma;0)=w(\gamma;0)}.
$$
