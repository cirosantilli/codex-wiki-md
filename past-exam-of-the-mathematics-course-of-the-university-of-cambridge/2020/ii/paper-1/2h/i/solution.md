<h1 id="2h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalize the loop by

$$
u(t)=\frac{\gamma(t)}{|\gamma(t)|}\in S^1.
$$

Choose a continuous lift $\vartheta:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ satisfying

$$
u(t)=e^{2\pi i\vartheta(t)}.
$$

Because $\gamma(0)=\gamma(1)$, the difference $\vartheta(1)-\vartheta(0)$ is an integer independent of the chosen lift. The [winding number of a continuous closed path](../../../../../../winding-number-of-a-continuous-closed-path.md) is

$$
\boxed{w(\gamma;0)=\vartheta(1)-\vartheta(0)}.
$$

If $\eta$ is a lift of $\delta/|\delta|$, then $\vartheta+\eta$ is a lift of

$$
\frac{\gamma\delta}{|\gamma\delta|}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{w(\gamma\delta;0)
=w(\gamma;0)+w(\delta;0)}.
$$
