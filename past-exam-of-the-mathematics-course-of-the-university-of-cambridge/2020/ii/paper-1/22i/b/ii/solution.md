<h1 id="22i/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $x^{(n)}=e_n$, the $n$th standard unit vector, and let $x=0$. Under the identification $(\ell^p)^*=\ell^q$, a functional represented by $y=(y_j)\in\ell^q$ satisfies

$$
\phi_y(e_n)=y_n\longrightarrow0.
$$

Thus the [standard unit vectors are weakly null in lp](../../../../../../../standard-unit-vectors-are-weakly-null-in-lp.md), but

$$
\|e_n-0\|_p=1
$$

for every $n$. Hence weak convergence does not imply norm convergence.
