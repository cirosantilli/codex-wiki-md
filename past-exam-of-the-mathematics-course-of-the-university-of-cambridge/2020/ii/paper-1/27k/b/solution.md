<h1 id="27k/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $U$ have the [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $[0,1]$, and set

$$
Z_1=U,\qquad Z_2=U.
$$

The variables have the same law. They are not [independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), since

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(Z_1\le\tfrac12,\ Z_2\le\tfrac12)=\frac12
\ne\frac14
=\mathbb P(Z_1\le\tfrac12)\mathbb P(Z_2\le\tfrac12).}
$$
