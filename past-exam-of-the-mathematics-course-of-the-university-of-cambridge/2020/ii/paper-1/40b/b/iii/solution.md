<h1 id="40b/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The normal [acoustic energy flux](../../../../../../../acoustic-energy-flux.md) is

$$
I_y=p'u_y=p'\phi_y.
$$

In either half-space, the pressure fields above are proportional to $\cos(kx-\omega t)$, whereas the normal velocities are proportional to $\sin(kx-\omega t)$. They are therefore in temporal quadrature. Averaging over one period gives

$$
\boxed{
\langle I_y\rangle
\mathrel\propto\langle\cos(kx-\omega t)\sin(kx-\omega t)\rangle=0.
}
$$

**Thus the field is evanescent and stores [reactive acoustic energy flux](../../../../../../../reactive-acoustic-energy-flux.md) near the membrane without transporting net acoustic energy away from it in the normal direction; the wave's mean energy transport is along the membrane.**
