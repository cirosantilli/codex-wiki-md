<h1 id="39b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The outer circle obeys $x^2+(y-2a)^2=(2a)^2$, and hence

$$
\boxed{r=4a\sin\theta.}
$$

Its counterclockwise tangent is again $(\cos2\theta,\sin2\theta)$. Its angular velocity is $-\Omega/4$ and radius is $2a$, so its signed tangential speed is $-a\Omega/2$. The boundary conditions are

$$
\boxed{\psi=0,
\qquad u_r\cos\theta+u_\theta\sin\theta=-\frac{a\Omega}{2}
\quad\text{at }r=4a\sin\theta.}
$$

The first follows both from impermeability and from the common streamfunction value forced by the contact point.
