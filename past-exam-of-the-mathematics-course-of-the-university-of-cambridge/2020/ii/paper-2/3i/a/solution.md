↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [discrete memoryless channel](../../../../../../discrete-memoryless-channel.md) with input $X$ and output $Y$, its [information capacity](../../../../../../channel-capacity.md) is

$$
C=\max_{p_X}I(X;Y),
$$

where $I(X;Y)$ is the [mutual information](../../../../../../mutual-information.md) and the maximum is over all input [distributions](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md). Capacity is measured in bits when logarithms have base two.
