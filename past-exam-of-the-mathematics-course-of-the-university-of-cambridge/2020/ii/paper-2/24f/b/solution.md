<h1 id="24f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from two, a projective change of coordinates puts every [smooth quadric surface](../../../../../../smooth-quadric-surface.md) in the form

$$
V(x_0x_3-x_1x_2).
$$

The [Segre embedding](../../../../../../segre-embedding.md) identifies this surface with $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$ by

$$
([s:t],[u:v])\longmapsto[su:sv:tu:tv].
$$

Two distinct fibres of either coordinate projection are disjoint lines in one of the [rulings of a smooth quadric surface](../../../../../../rulings-of-a-smooth-quadric-surface.md). Explicitly,

$$
L_1=\{x_2=x_3=0\},\qquad
L_2=\{x_0=x_1=0\}
$$

both lie on $V$ and have no common projective point. Thus $V$ contains a pair of disjoint lines.
