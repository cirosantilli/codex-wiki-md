<h1 id="10c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md) sends $|0x\rangle$ to $|0x\rangle$ and $|1\bar x\rangle$ to $|1x\rangle$. Hence

$$
\operatorname{CX}|\beta_{zx}\rangle
=\frac{|0\rangle+(-1)^z|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}\otimes|x\rangle
=H|z\rangle\otimes|x\rangle.
$$

Since the [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) satisfies $H^2=I$,

$$
\boxed{(H\otimes I)\operatorname{CX}|\beta_{zx}\rangle=|zx\rangle}.
$$
