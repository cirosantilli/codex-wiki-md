↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This is [superdense coding](../../../../../../superdense-coding.md). To send the two classical bits $(z,x)$, Alice applies $Z^zX^x$ to her half of the shared [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) $|\beta_{00}\rangle$, producing $|\beta_{zx}\rangle$, and sends that one qubit to Bob. Bob applies $\operatorname{CX}$ followed by $H$ on the first qubit, as in part (i), and measures both qubits in the [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md). The outcome is $zx$ with certainty, so one transmitted qubit communicates two classical bits using the previously shared [quantum entanglement](../../../../../../entangled-state.md).
