<h1 id="15c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Starting from the $n$-qubit [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) state $|0\rangle^{\otimes n}$, apply one [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) to every qubit. Since $H|0\rangle=(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$,

$$
H^{\otimes n}|0\rangle^{\otimes n}
=\frac1{\sqrt{2^n}}(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)^{\otimes n}
=\frac1{\sqrt{2^n}}\sum_{x\in B_n}|x\rangle
=\boxed{|\psi_n\rangle}.
$$

**Thus parallel Hadamard gates prepare the [uniform quantum superposition](../../../../../../uniform-quantum-superposition.md).**
