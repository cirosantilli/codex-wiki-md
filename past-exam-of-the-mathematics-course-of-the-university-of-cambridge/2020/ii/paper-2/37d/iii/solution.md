<h1 id="37d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $r>3M$,

$$
\boxed{\Delta\tau_1<\Delta\tau_2<\Delta\tau_3}.
$$

Relative to the distant observer, the static clock $O_2$ suffers gravitational [time dilation](../../../../../../time-dilation.md). The orbiting clock $O_1$ suffers the same gravitational effect together with the kinematic time dilation caused by its orbital motion, so it accumulates the least proper time.
