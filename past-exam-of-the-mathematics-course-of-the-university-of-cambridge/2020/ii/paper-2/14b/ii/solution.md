<h1 id="14b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The coordinates $\phi$ and $\psi$ are [cyclic](../../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md), so their [canonical momenta](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) are conserved:

$$
\boxed{L_3=p_\psi
=I_3(\dot\psi+\dot\phi\cos\theta)},
$$



$$
\boxed{L_z=p_\phi
=I_1\dot\phi\sin^2\theta+L_3\cos\theta}.
$$

Because the [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) has no explicit time dependence, the conserved [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) is

$$
\boxed{E=
\frac{I_1}{2}\left(\dot\theta^2+\dot\phi^2\sin^2\theta\right)
+\frac{I_3}{2}(\dot\psi+\dot\phi\cos\theta)^2
+Mgl\cos\theta}.
$$

These are the three [integrals of motion](../../../../../../integral-of-motion.md) requested.
