<h1 id="14b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Solving the two momentum equations gives

$$
\dot\phi=\frac{L_z-L_3\cos\theta}{I_1\sin^2\theta},
\qquad
\dot\psi+\dot\phi\cos\theta=\frac{L_3}{I_3}.
$$

Substitution into the conserved energy reduces the [heavy symmetric top](../../../../../../heavy-symmetric-top.md) to one-dimensional [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md) motion:

$$
\boxed{\frac12I_1\dot\theta^2+V_{\mathrm{eff}}(\theta)=E'},
$$

where

$$
\boxed{V_{\mathrm{eff}}(\theta)
=\frac{(L_z-L_3\cos\theta)^2}{2I_1\sin^2\theta}
+Mgl\cos\theta},
\qquad
\boxed{E'=E-\frac{L_3^2}{2I_3}}.
$$

This is the [heavy symmetric top reduction](../../../../../../heavy-symmetric-top-reduction.md).
