↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The operator $|e\rangle\langle g|\otimes A$ annihilates one field quantum and excites the atom. Its adjoint $|g\rangle\langle e|\otimes A^\dagger$ de-excites the atom and creates one field quantum. These are the two exchange processes in the [Jaynes-Cummings model](../../../../../../../jaynes-cummings-model.md).

[Unitary time evolution](../../../../../../../unitary-time-evolution.md) requires the Hamiltonian to be Hermitian. Since the common coefficient $\tfrac12\hbar(\Omega-\omega)$ is real, the two terms must be adjoints with equal coefficients. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\beta=1}.
$$
