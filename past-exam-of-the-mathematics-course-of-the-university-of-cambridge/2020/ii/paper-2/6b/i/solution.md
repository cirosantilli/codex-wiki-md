<h1 id="6b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At a nonzero [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md), both bracketed per-capita growth rates vanish:

$$
-\epsilon_1+\alpha N_2^*=0,
\qquad
\epsilon_2-\alpha N_1^*=0.
$$

Hence the coexistence equilibrium of this [Lotka-Volterra predator-prey model](../../../../../../lotka-volterra-predator-prey-model.md) is

$$
\boxed{(N_1^*,N_2^*)=\left(\frac{\epsilon_2}{\alpha},\frac{\epsilon_1}{\alpha}\right)}.
$$
