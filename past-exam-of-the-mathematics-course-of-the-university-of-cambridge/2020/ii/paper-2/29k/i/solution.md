<h1 id="29k/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\phi=(\phi_n^0,\phi_n^1,\ldots,\phi_n^d)$ be a predictable [self-financing portfolio](../../../../../../self-financing-portfolio.md), and let $V_n^\phi=\phi_n\mathbin{\cdot}S_n$ be its value. An [arbitrage](../../../../../../arbitrage.md) is such a strategy with

$$
\boxed{V_0^\phi=0,\qquad
V_T^\phi\geq0\ \hbox{almost surely},\qquad
\mathbb P(V_T^\phi>0)>0.}
$$
