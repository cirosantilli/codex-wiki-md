<h1 id="18g/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A group is [solvable](../../../../../../../solvable-group.md) when its [derived series](../../../../../../../derived-series.md)

$$
G^{(0)}=G,\qquad G^{(r+1)}=[G^{(r)},G^{(r)}]
$$

eventually reaches the trivial group; equivalently, it has a subnormal series with [abelian](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md) quotients.

The [symmetric group](../../../../../../../symmetric-group.md) $S_4$ is solvable because

$$
\{1\}\triangleleft V_4\triangleleft A_4\triangleleft S_4
$$

has abelian quotients $V_4$, $C_3$, and $C_2$, where $V_4$ is the [Klein four-group](../../../../../../../klein-four-group.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{S_4\text{ is solvable}}.
$$
