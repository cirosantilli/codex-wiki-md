<h1 id="18g/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $x_1,\ldots,x_5$ be algebraically independent indeterminates and set $a_i=e_i(x_1,\ldots,x_5)$, the elementary symmetric polynomials. Then

$$
\prod_{i=1}^5(T-x_i)
=T^5-a_1T^4+a_2T^3-a_3T^2+a_4T-a_5.
$$

The [Fundamental theorem of symmetric polynomials](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-symmetric-polynomials.md) identifies the fixed field of the natural $S_5$ action on $\mathbb Q(x_1,\ldots,x_5)$ with $\mathbb Q(a_1,\ldots,a_5)=K$. Every permutation of the roots therefore gives a $K$-automorphism, and the [Galois group of a polynomial](../../../../../../galois-group-of-a-polynomial.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(f/K)\cong S_5.
$$

A characteristic-zero polynomial is [solvable by radicals](../../../../../../solvability-by-radicals.md) exactly when its Galois group is a [solvable group](../../../../../../solvable-group.md). Part (c) shows that $S_5$ is not solvable, so

$$
\boxed{f\text{ is not solvable by radicals over }K}.
$$
