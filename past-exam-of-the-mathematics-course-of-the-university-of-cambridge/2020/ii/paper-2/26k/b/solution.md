The [Caratheodory extension theorem](../../../../../../caratheodory-s-extension-theorem.md) states that a [premeasure](../../../../../../premeasure.md) $\mu_0$ on an algebra of subsets of $X$ extends to a measure on the generated sigma-algebra. If $\mu_0$ is sigma-finite, this extension is unique. In particular, a finite premeasure has a unique extension.
