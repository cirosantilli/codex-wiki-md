A [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal F$ on $X$ contains $\varnothing$, is closed under complements in $X$, and is closed under countable unions. A [measure](../../../../../../measure.md) on the measurable space $(X,\mathcal F)$ is a function

$$
\mu:\mathcal F\to[0,\infty]
$$

such that $\mu(\varnothing)=0$ and, for every pairwise disjoint sequence $(A_n)$,

$$
\mu\left(\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n\right)
=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\mu(A_n).
$$

A [probability measure](../../../../../../probability-measure.md) is a measure normalized by

$$
\boxed{\mu(X)=1.}
$$
