The [Sauer-Shelah lemma](../../../../../../sauer-shelah-lemma.md) states that if $\operatorname{VC}(\mathcal F)=v<\infty$, then

$$
\boxed{
s(\mathcal F,n)\leq
\sum_{j=0}^{\min(v,n)}\binom nj
}.
$$

In particular, the [Sauer-Shelah growth bound](../../../../../../sauer-shelah-growth-bound.md) gives $s(\mathcal F,n)\leq(n+1)^v$.
