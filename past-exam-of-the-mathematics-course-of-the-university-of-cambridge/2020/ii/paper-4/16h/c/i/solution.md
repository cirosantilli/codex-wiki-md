<h1 id="16h/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The family $\mathcal U_n=\{A\subseteq\mathbb N:n\in A\}$ is nonempty, excludes the empty set, is closed under finite intersections, and is upward closed. It is therefore a [filter](../../../../../../../filter-set-theory.md). For every $A\subseteq\mathbb N$, either $n\in A$ or $n\in A^c$, so either $A\in\mathcal U_n$ or $A^c\in\mathcal U_n$. Hence $\mathcal U_n$ is the [principal ultrafilter](../../../../../../../principal-ultrafilter.md) at $n$.
