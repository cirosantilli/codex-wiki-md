[Zorn lemma](../../../../../../zorn-s-lemma.md) states that if every [chain](../../../../../../chain-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) in a nonempty [partially ordered set](../../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) has an [upper bound](../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) in that set, then the set has a [maximal element](../../../../../../maximal-element-of-a-partially-ordered-set.md). It is equivalent, over the usual axioms of set theory, to the [axiom of choice](../../../../../../axiom-of-choice.md).
