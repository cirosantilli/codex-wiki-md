<h1 id="35a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Clausius gas](../../../../../../clausius-gas.md) equation can be written

$$
P=\frac{NT}{V-Nb}=\frac{nT}{1-nb}=nT\left(1+nb+O(n^2)\right).
$$

Comparison with the [virial expansion](../../../../../../virial-expansion.md) gives

$$
\boxed{B(T)=b}.
$$

For a [hard-sphere gas](../../../../../../hard-sphere-gas.md) of atomic radius $r_0$, the [pair potential](../../../../../../pair-potential.md) is infinite when the center separation is below $2r_0$ and zero otherwise. Thus

$$
B=\frac12\int_{r<2r_0}d^3r
=\frac12\frac{4\pi}{3}(2r_0)^3
=\boxed{\frac{16\pi r_0^3}{3}}.
$$

This is four times the volume of one atom and is the per-particle [excluded volume](../../../../../../excluded-volume.md) appearing in the dilute [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md).
