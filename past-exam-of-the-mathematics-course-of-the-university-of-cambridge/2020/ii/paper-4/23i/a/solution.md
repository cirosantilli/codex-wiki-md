<h1 id="23i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\mathcal S'(\mathbb R^n)$ be the space of [tempered distributions](../../../../../../tempered-distribution.md). For $s\in\mathbb R$, the [Sobolev space](../../../../../../sobolev-space-split.md) $H^s(\mathbb R^n)$ is

$$
H^s(\mathbb R^n)=\left\{u\in\mathcal S'(\mathbb R^n):(1+|\xi|^2)^{s/2}\widehat u(\xi)\in L^2(\mathbb R^n)\right\},
$$

with norm

$$
\lVert u\rVert_{H^s}^2=\int_{\mathbb R^n}(1+|\xi|^2)^s|\widehat u(\xi)|^2\,d\xi.
$$

Changing the normalization of the [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) changes this norm only by a fixed constant.
