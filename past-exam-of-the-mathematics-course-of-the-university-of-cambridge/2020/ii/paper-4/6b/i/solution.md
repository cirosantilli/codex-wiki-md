<h1 id="6b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

This is a [Yule process](../../../../../../yule-process.md), a [pure birth process](../../../../../../birth-process.md) with state-dependent birth rate $\lambda_n=\lambda n$. Probability flows into state $n$ from $n-1$ and leaves it for $n+1$, so the [master equation](../../../../../../master-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{dP_1}{dt}=-\lambda P_1,
\qquad
\frac{dP_n}{dt}=\lambda(n-1)P_{n-1}-\lambda nP_n\quad(n\geq2).}
$$
