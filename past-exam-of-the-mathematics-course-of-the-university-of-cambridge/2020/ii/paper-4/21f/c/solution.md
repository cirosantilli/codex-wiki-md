↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**No.** Here $T$ is the [pair of pants](../../../../../../pair-of-pants-mathematics.md), and part (b) gives $H_2(T)=0$, whereas

$$
H_2(S^1\times S^1)\cong H_2(T^2)\cong\mathbb Z.
$$

If $g:T^2\to T$ made $f\circ g$ [homotopic](../../../../../../homotopy.md) to the [identity map](../../../../../../identity-function.md) of the [torus](../../../../../../torus.md), [functoriality of homology](../../../../../../functoriality-of-homology.md) and [homotopy invariance of homology](../../../../../../homotopy-invariance-of-homology.md) would give

$$
(f\circ g)_*=f_*\circ g_*=(\operatorname{id}_{T^2})_*
$$

on $H_2(T^2)$. But $g_*$ factors through the zero group $H_2(T)$, so the left side is zero while the right side is the identity on $\mathbb Z$, a contradiction.
