<h1 id="21f/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Suppose $X=A\cup B$ and that the [interiors](../../../../../../../interior-topology.md) of $A$ and $B$ cover $X$. The [Mayer–Vietoris theorem](../../../../../../../mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) gives the [long exact sequence in homology](../../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-homology.md)

$$
\cdots\longrightarrow H_n(A\cap B)\xrightarrow{(i_*,-j_*)}H_n(A)\oplus H_n(B)\xrightarrow{k_*+l_*}H_n(X)\xrightarrow{\partial}H_{n-1}(A\cap B)\longrightarrow\cdots,
$$

where all four displayed maps except the [connecting homomorphism](../../../../../../../connecting-homomorphism.md) $\partial$ are induced by the relevant [inclusion maps](../../../../../../../inclusion-map.md).

To prove it, let $C_*^{A,B}(X)$ be the [chain complex](../../../../../../../chain-complex.md) generated by those [singular simplices](../../../../../../../singular-simplex.md) whose images lie wholly in $A$ or wholly in $B$. The sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow C_*(A\cap B)\xrightarrow{c\mapsto(c,-c)}C_*(A)\oplus C_*(B)\xrightarrow{(a,b)\mapsto a+b}C_*^{A,B}(X)\longrightarrow0
$$

is a [short exact sequence](../../../../../../../short-exact-sequence.md) of [chain complexes](../../../../../../../chain-complex.md). Repeated [barycentric subdivision](../../../../../../../barycentric-subdivision.md) makes every singular simplex small enough to lie in $A$ or $B$, and the subdivision operator is [chain homotopic](../../../../../../../chain-homotopy.md) to the identity. The inclusion $C_*^{A,B}(X)\hookrightarrow C_*(X)$ therefore induces an [isomorphism](../../../../../../../isomorphism.md) on every [homology group](../../../../../../../homology-group.md). The [long exact homology sequence of a short exact sequence of chain complexes](../../../../../../../short-exact-sequence-of-chain-complexes.md) now gives the displayed sequence. Explicitly, if an $n$-cycle $z=a+b$ with $a\in C_n(A)$ and $b\in C_n(B)$, then $\partial a=-\partial b$ lies in $C_{n-1}(A\cap B)$ and represents the connecting class $\partial[z]$.
