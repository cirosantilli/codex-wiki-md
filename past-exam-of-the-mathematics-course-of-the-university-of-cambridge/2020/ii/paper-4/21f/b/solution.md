<h1 id="21f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $T$ be the [surface with boundary](../../../../../../surface-with-boundary.md) obtained by deleting the interiors of the $n$ disks. By [excision](../../../../../../excision-theorem.md),

$$
H_i(S,T)\cong\bigoplus_{j=1}^nH_i(D^2,S^1),
$$

so $H_2(S,T)\cong\mathbb Z^n$ and the other relative groups vanish. In the [long exact sequence in relative homology](../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-in-relative-homology.md), the map

$$
H_2(S)\cong\mathbb Z\longrightarrow H_2(S,T)\cong\mathbb Z^n
$$

sends an [orientation class](../../../../../../fundamental-class.md) to $(1,\ldots,1)$, up to an overall choice of sign. It is therefore injective and has [cokernel](../../../../../../cokernel.md) $\mathbb Z^{n-1}$. Exactness gives $H_2(T)=0$ and a [short exact sequence](../../../../../../short-exact-sequence.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^{n-1}\longrightarrow H_1(T)\longrightarrow H_1(S)\cong\mathbb Z^{2g}\longrightarrow0.
$$

This sequence splits because its right-hand group is a [free abelian group](../../../../../../free-abelian-group.md). Since $T$ is connected, we obtain

$$
\boxed{H_i(T;\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&i=0,\\
\mathbb Z^{2g+n-1},&i=1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}}
$$
