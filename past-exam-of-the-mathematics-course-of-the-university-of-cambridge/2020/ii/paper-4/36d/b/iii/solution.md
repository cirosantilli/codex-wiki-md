<h1 id="36d/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The condition $\mathbf E_I\cdot\widehat{\mathbf x}=0$, together with [transversality](../../../../../../../transversality-of-an-electromagnetic-plane-wave.md) $\mathbf k_I\cdot\mathbf E_I=0$, selects polarization along $\widehat{\mathbf y}$ away from the normal-incidence degeneracy. [Phase matching at a planar wave interface](../../../../../../../phase-matching-at-a-planar-wave-interface.md) gives

$$
\omega_T=\omega_I,
\qquad k_{Ty}=0,
\qquad k_{Tz}=k_{Iz}=k_I\sin\theta_I.
$$

The two [dispersion relations](../../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) give $|\mathbf k_T|^2=(\varepsilon_+/\varepsilon_-)k_I^2$. Choosing propagation into $x>0$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf k_T=k_I\left[
\sqrt{\frac{\varepsilon_+}{\varepsilon_-}-\sin^2\theta_I}\,\widehat{\mathbf x}
+\sin\theta_I\,\widehat{\mathbf z}
\right]}.
$$

Equivalently, this is [Snell law for electromagnetic waves](../../../../../../../snell-law-for-electromagnetic-waves.md) with [refractive index](../../../../../../../refractive-index.md) proportional to $\sqrt\varepsilon$ because the two media have equal [magnetic permeability](../../../../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md).
