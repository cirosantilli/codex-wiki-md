<h1 id="36d/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a time-independent field, [Faraday's law](../../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives $\nabla\times\mathbf E=0$. Integrating around a thin rectangular loop crossing $x=0$ and shrinking its height gives continuity of the [tangential component](../../../../../../../tangential-component.md):

$$
\boxed{E_y^-=E_y^+,qquad E_z^-=E_z^+}.
$$

A thin [Gaussian pillbox](../../../../../../../gaussian-pillbox.md) and $\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=0$ give continuity of the normal [electric displacement field](../../../../../../../electric-displacement-field.md):

$$
\boxed{D_x^-=D_x^+,qquad \varepsilon_-E_x^-=\varepsilon_+E_x^+}.
$$

These are the electrostatic cases of the [dielectric-interface boundary conditions](../../../../../../../dielectric-interface-boundary-conditions.md).
