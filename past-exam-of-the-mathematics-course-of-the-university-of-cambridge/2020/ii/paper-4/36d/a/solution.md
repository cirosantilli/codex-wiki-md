<h1 id="36d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the absence of free [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge.md) and [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md), the [source-free Maxwell equations in a linear medium](../../../../../../source-free-maxwell-equations-in-a-linear-medium.md) are

$$
\boxed{
\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf D=0,
\qquad
\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf B=0,
\qquad
\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t\mathbf B,
\qquad
\nabla\times\mathbf H=\partial_t\mathbf D},
$$

together with the [constitutive relations](../../../../../../constitutive-relations.md) $\mathbf D=\varepsilon\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf B=\mu\mathbf H$.
