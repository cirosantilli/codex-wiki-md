<h1 id="34c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a particle of mass $m$ and charge $q$, the [Hamiltonian of a charged particle in an electromagnetic field](../../../../../../hamiltonian-of-a-charged-particle-in-an-electromagnetic-field.md) is

$$
H(\mathbf x,\mathbf p,t)=\frac1{2m}[\mathbf p-q\mathbf A(\mathbf x,t)]^2+q\phi(\mathbf x,t).
$$

The [canonical momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is $\mathbf p=m\dot{\mathbf x}+q\mathbf A$. Hamilton's equations are equivalent to the [Lorentz force law](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md)

$$
\boxed{m\ddot{\mathbf x}=q(\mathbf E+\dot{\mathbf x}\times\mathbf B),
\qquad
\mathbf E=-\nabla\phi-\partial_t\mathbf A,
\qquad
\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A.}
$$
