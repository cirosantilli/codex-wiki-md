<h1 id="34c/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $f(w)=w^M=r^Me^{iM\varphi}$, the [angular momentum operator](../../../../../../../angular-momentum-operator.md) $L_z=-i\hbar\partial_\varphi$ gives

$$
\boxed{L_z\psi=M\hbar\psi}.
$$

The probability density is proportional to

$$
r^{2M}e^{-qBr^2/(2\hbar)},
$$

whose maximum occurs at

$$
\boxed{r_M=\sqrt{\frac{2M\hbar}{qB}}}.
$$

In a large disk of area $A=\pi R^2$, the states that fit inside satisfy $M\lesssim qBR^2/(2\hbar)$. Thus the degeneracy of each [Landau level](../../../../../../../landau-level.md) is asymptotically

$$
\boxed{N\simeq\frac{qBA}{2\pi\hbar}=\frac{\Phi_B}{h/q}},
$$

where $\Phi_B=BA$ is the [magnetic flux](../../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md). There is one state per [magnetic flux quantum](../../../../../../../magnetic-flux-quantum.md) $h/q$, up to boundary corrections.
