<h1 id="34c/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

These potentials give the uniform [magnetic field](../../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) $\mathbf B=B\widehat{\mathbf z}$ and zero [electric field](../../../../../../../electric-field.md). With the signed frequency $\omega_c=qB/m$, the [Lorentz force law](../../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) becomes

$$
\ddot x=\omega_c\dot y,
\qquad
\ddot y=-\omega_c\dot x,
\qquad
\ddot z=0.
$$

Thus

$$
\dot x+i\dot y=(\dot x_0+i\dot y_0)e^{-i\omega_ct},
$$

and explicitly

$$
\begin{aligned}
x(t)&=x_0+\frac{\dot x_0\sin(\omega_ct)+\dot y_0[1-\cos(\omega_ct)]}{\omega_c},\\
y(t)&=y_0+\frac{\dot y_0\sin(\omega_ct)-\dot x_0[1-\cos(\omega_ct)]}{\omega_c},\\
z(t)&=z_0+\dot z_0t.
\end{aligned}
$$

The transverse trajectory is a circle about the fixed [guiding centre](../../../../../../../guiding-center.md) $(x+\dot y/\omega_c,y-\dot x/\omega_c)$. The positive [cyclotron frequency](../../../../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md) is

$$
\boxed{\omega_B=\frac{|qB|}{m}}.
$$
