↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The model has a unique [equivalent martingale measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md) $\mathbb Q$ under which

$$
dS_t=rS_t\,dt+\sigma S_t\,dW_t^{\mathbb Q}
$$

and the discounted stock is a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md). The time-zero value of an integrable time-$T$ [contingent claim](../../../../../../contingent-claim.md) $C$ is therefore

$$
\boxed{V_0=e^{-rT}\mathbb E_{\mathbb Q}[C]}.
$$
