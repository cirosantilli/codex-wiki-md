<h1 id="29k/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In a [Black-Scholes model](../../../../../../black-scholes-model.md), the risk-free asset and risky asset satisfy

$$
dS_t^0=rS_t^0\,dt,
\qquad
dS_t=\mu S_t\,dt+\sigma S_t\,dB_t,
$$

where $B$ is a [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md), $\sigma>0$, and the coefficients are constant. With $S_0^0=1$,

$$
S_t^0=e^{rt},
\qquad
S_t=S_0\exp\left((\mu-\tfrac12\sigma^2)t+\sigma B_t\right),
$$

so the risky asset is a [geometric Brownian motion](../../../../../../geometric-brownian-motion.md).
