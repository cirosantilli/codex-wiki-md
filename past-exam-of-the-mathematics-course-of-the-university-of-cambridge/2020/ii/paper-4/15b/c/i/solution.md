<h1 id="15b/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $H=H(p)$, [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) give

$$
p(t)=P,
\qquad
q(t)=Q+tH'(P).
$$

Thus $P=p$ and $Q=q-tH'(p)$. The [Poisson bracket](../../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) is $\{Q,P\}=1$, so the transformation is canonical for every $t$. A type-2 generator is

$$
\boxed{F_2(q,P,t)=qP-tH(P)}.
$$

Indeed $p=\partial_qF_2=P$, $Q=\partial_PF_2=q-tH'(P)$, and the transformed Hamiltonian is $K=0$, as expected because $Q,P$ are constants of motion.
