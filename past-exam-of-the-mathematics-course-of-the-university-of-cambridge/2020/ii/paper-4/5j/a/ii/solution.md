↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The full [F-test](../../../../../../../f-test.md) has $p<2.2\times10^{-16}$ and tests the [null hypothesis](../../../../../../../null-hypothesis.md) that both slope coefficients vanish. It decisively rejects that hypothesis, while $R^2=0.8126$ says the fitted model explains about $81\%$ of the sample variation. Together with the insignificant individual t-tests, this is characteristic of [multicollinearity](../../../../../../../multicollinearity.md): the two covariates explain the response jointly, but the output does not identify either one as having a separately estimable effect.
