<h1 id="31a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an equation $y''+p(x)y'+q(x)y=0$, infinity is a [regular singular point](../../../../../../regular-singular-point.md) only when $p(x)=O(x^{-1})$ and $q(x)=O(x^{-2})$. Here $p=-1$ and $q=-2/x-2/x^2$, so infinity is an [irregular singular point](../../../../../../irregular-singular-point.md).
