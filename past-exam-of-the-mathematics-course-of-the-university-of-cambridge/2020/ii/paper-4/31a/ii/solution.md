<h1 id="31a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the standard [elimination of the first derivative](../../../../../../elimination-of-the-first-derivative.md) by setting

$$
y(x)=e^{x/2}u(x).
$$

Then

$$
y''-y'=e^{x/2}\left(u''-\frac14u\right),
$$

so the transformed equation is

$$
\boxed{u''+q(x)u=0,\qquad q(x)=-\frac14-\frac2x-\frac2{x^2}}.
$$
