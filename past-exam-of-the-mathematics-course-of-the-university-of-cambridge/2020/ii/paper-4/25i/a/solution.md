<h1 id="25i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a compact oriented [regular surface](../../../../../../smooth-surface.md) $S\subset\mathbb R^3$ without boundary, the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](../../../../../../gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) states

$$
\int_S K\,dA=2\pi\chi(S).
$$

Here $K=k_1k_2$ is the [Gaussian curvature](../../../../../../gaussian-curvature.md), the product of the two [principal curvatures](../../../../../../principal-curvature.md); $dA$ is the area element induced by the [first fundamental form](../../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md); and $\chi(S)$ is the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../../euler-characteristic.md). If $S$ is connected and has [genus](../../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$, the [classification theorem for surfaces](../../../../../../classification-theorem-for-surfaces.md) gives $\chi(S)=2-2g$, so

$$
\boxed{\int_S K\,dA=4\pi(1-g).}
$$
