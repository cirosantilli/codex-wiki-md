For the tori in part (c), the distance from the origin is as large as $R_n+1\to\infty$, so condition (1) fails. Their [surface areas](../../../../../../../surface-area.md) are

$$
\operatorname{Area}(S_n)=4\pi^2R_n,
$$

so condition (2) fails as well.
