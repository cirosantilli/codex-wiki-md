↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $\omega=e^{2\pi i/N}$, the [quantum Fourier transform](../../../../../../quantum-fourier-transform.md) is

$$
\operatorname{QFT}_N|x\rangle
=\frac1{\sqrt N}\sum_{y=0}^{N-1}\omega^{xy}|y\rangle.
$$

Its matrix is unitary because [character orthogonality](../../../../../../character-orthogonality.md) for the cyclic group gives orthogonal rows.
