<h1 id="10c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The map $x\mapsto-x$ is an [involution](../../../../../../involution.md), so

$$
\operatorname{QFT}_N^4=I.
$$

If $\lambda$ is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $\operatorname{QFT}_N$, then $\lambda^4=1$. Hence every eigenvalue belongs to

$$
\{1,-1,i,-i\}.
$$

Because $X^4-1$ has distinct roots, the transform is [diagonalizable](../../../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md); which fourth roots occur and their multiplicities depend on $N$.
