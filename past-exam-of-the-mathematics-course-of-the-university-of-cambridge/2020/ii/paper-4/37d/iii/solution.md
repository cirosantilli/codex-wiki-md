<h1 id="37d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the [Dirac delta distribution](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) source $\rho(\mathbf r)=M\delta(\mathbf r)$, the stated [Green function of the Laplacian](../../../../../../green-function-of-the-laplacian.md) gives

$$
\Phi(\mathbf r)
=-\int\frac{4\pi M\delta(\mathbf r')}{4\pi|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}\,d^3r'
=\boxed{-\frac Mr}.
$$

This is the [Newtonian potential of a point mass](../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md); its negative [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) gives the inverse-square gravitational acceleration $-M\widehat{\mathbf r}/r^2$.
