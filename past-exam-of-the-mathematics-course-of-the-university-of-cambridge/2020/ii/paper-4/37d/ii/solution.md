<h1 id="37d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the [Newtonian limit of general relativity](../../../../../../newtonian-limit.md), time derivatives are negligible and $T_{00}=\rho$ is the only leading component. With signature $(-,+,+,+)$,

$$
T=\eta^{\mu\nu}T_{\mu\nu}=-\rho.
$$

The $00$ component of the trace-reversed equation becomes

$$
\nabla^2h_{00}=-16\pi\left(\rho-\frac12\eta_{00}(-\rho)\right)=-8\pi\rho.
$$

Identifying $h_{00}=-2\Phi$ therefore gives the [Poisson equation](../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) of [Newtonian gravity](../../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi\rho}.
$$
