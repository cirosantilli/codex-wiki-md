<h1 id="37d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Einstein tensor](../../../../../../einstein-tensor.md) is $G_{\alpha\beta}=R_{\alpha\beta}-\tfrac12g_{\alpha\beta}R$. Taking the [metric trace](../../../../../../metric-trace.md) of the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) in four dimensions gives

$$
-R=8\pi T,
$$

and substitution back gives the [Trace-reversed Einstein field equations in D dimensions](../../../../../../trace-reversed-einstein-field-equations-in-d-dimensions.md) specialized to $D=4$:

$$
\boxed{R_{\alpha\beta}=8\pi\left(T_{\alpha\beta}-\frac12g_{\alpha\beta}T\right)}.
$$

For [linearized gravity](../../../../../../linearized-gravity.md), the given equation and $G_{\mu\nu}=8\pi T_{\mu\nu}$ imply

$$
\Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}=-16\pi T_{\mu\nu}.
$$

Since $\bar h=-h$, its trace is $\Box h=16\pi T$. Using $\bar h_{\mu\nu}=h_{\mu\nu}-\tfrac12\eta_{\mu\nu}h$ then yields

$$
\boxed{\Box h_{\mu\nu}=-16\pi\left(T_{\mu\nu}-\frac12\eta_{\mu\nu}T\right)}.
$$
