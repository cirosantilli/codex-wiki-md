<h1 id="37d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Move the positive [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) to the source side and define

$$
T^{(\Lambda)}_{\alpha\beta}=-\frac{\Lambda}{8\pi}g_{\alpha\beta}.
$$

To first order about [Minkowski spacetime](../../../../../../minkowski-spacetime.md), $T^{(\Lambda)}=-\Lambda/(2\pi)$. Its contribution to the trace-reversed $00$ source is

$$
T^{(\Lambda)}_{00}-\frac12\eta_{00}T^{(\Lambda)}=-\frac{\Lambda}{8\pi}.
$$

Repeating the [Newtonian limit of general relativity](../../../../../../newtonian-limit.md) calculation gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi\rho-\Lambda}.
$$

For a point mass, $\nabla^2r^2=6$, so

$$
\Phi=-\frac Mr-Br^2,
\qquad -6B=-\Lambda,
\qquad
\boxed{B=\frac\Lambda6}.
$$

The radial acceleration is

$$
-\frac{d\Phi}{dr}=-\frac M{r^2}+\frac\Lambda3r.
$$

Thus positive $\Lambda$ supplies an outward acceleration and balances the point-mass attraction at the [static radius](../../../../../../static-radius.md)

$$
r_{\rm s}=\left(\frac{3M}{\Lambda}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

The [weak-field approximation](../../../../../../weak-field-approximation.md) requires both $M/r\ll1$ and $\Lambda r^2\ll1$, giving parametrically

$$
\boxed{M\ll r\ll\Lambda^{-1/2}}.
$$
