An [interval map](../../../../../../../interval-map.md) $F:I\to I$ has a [horseshoe](../../../../../../../horseshoe-for-an-interval-map.md) when there are an [open interval](../../../../../../../open-interval.md) $J\subseteq I$ and two disjoint [open intervals](../../../../../../../open-interval.md) $K_0,K_1\subseteq J$ such that

$$
\boxed{F(K_0)=F(K_1)=J.}
$$
