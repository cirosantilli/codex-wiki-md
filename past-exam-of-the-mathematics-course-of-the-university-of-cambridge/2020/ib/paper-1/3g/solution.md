<h1 id="3g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3G](../3g.md)

The boundary arcs meet at $0$ and $1$. The [Möbius transformation](../../../../../mobius-transformation.md)

$$
w=\frac{z}{1-z}
$$

sends these points to $0$ and infinity, sends the real boundary segment to the positive real axis, and sends the circular boundary arc to the ray of argument $-\pi/6$. Hence it maps $D\cap L$ onto the sector

$$
-\frac{\pi}{6}<\arg w<0.
$$

The power $\zeta=w^6$ maps this sector conformally onto the lower half-plane. Finally, the Cayley map $\zeta\mapsto(\zeta+i)/(\zeta-i)$ maps the lower half-plane onto the unit disc. Thus a requested [conformal equivalence](../../../../../conformal-equivalence.md) is

$$
\boxed{F(z)=
\frac{\left(\dfrac{z}{1-z}\right)^6+i}
{\left(\dfrac{z}{1-z}\right)^6-i}}.
$$
