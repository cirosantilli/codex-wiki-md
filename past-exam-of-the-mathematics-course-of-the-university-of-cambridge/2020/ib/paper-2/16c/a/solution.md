↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Equation (1) is [Laplace equation](../../../../../../laplace-equation.md) for the [velocity potential](../../../../../../velocity-potential.md). It expresses incompressibility, $\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0$, together with $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$. Equation (2) is the linearized dynamic free-surface condition: the unsteady [Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) and constant atmospheric pressure balance the potential acceleration against the gravitational restoring term $g\zeta$. Equation (3), with the printed $c$ understood as $\zeta$, is the kinematic free-surface condition: the surface moves with the vertical fluid velocity. Equations (4) and (5) are [no-penetration boundary conditions](../../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md) at the rigid bottom and cylindrical sidewall, respectively.
