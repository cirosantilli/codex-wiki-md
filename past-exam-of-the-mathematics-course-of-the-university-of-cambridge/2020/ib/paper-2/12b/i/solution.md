<h1 id="12b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md) is

$$
f(z)=-\frac1{2z}+\frac1{2(z-2)}.
$$

For $0<|z|<2$, expand the second term as a [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md):

$$
\frac1{2(z-2)}
=-\frac14\frac1{1-z/2}
=-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{z^n}{2^{n+2}}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{f(z)=-\frac1{2z}
-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{z^n}{2^{n+2}}},
\qquad 0<|z|<2.
$$
