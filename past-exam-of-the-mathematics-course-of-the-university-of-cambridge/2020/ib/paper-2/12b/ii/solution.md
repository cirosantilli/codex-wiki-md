<h1 id="12b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $|z|>2$, expand directly in inverse powers:

$$
f(z)=\frac1{z^2}\frac1{1-2/z}.
$$

Therefore the outer [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) is

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{2^n}{z^{n+2}}},
\qquad |z|>2.
$$
