<h1 id="10e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The identity map

$$
\operatorname{id}:
(\widehat{\mathcal P}_n,d_\infty)
\longrightarrow
(\widehat{\mathcal P}_n,d_1)
$$

is a continuous bijection by $d_1\le d_\infty$. Its domain is compact by part (i), and its codomain is a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) because every metric space is Hausdorff. The [compact-to-Hausdorff continuous bijection theorem](../../../../../../compact-to-hausdorff-continuous-bijection-theorem.md) therefore makes the identity a homeomorphism. Consequently

$$
\boxed{d_1\text{ and }d_\infty\text{ induce the same topology on }\widehat{\mathcal P}_n}.
$$
