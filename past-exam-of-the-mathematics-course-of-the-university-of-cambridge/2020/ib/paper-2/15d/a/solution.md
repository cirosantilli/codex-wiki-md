<h1 id="15d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Translation symmetry in the plane and reflection symmetry across it force the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) to be uniform on each side, parallel to $\mathbf e_y$, and opposite on the two sides. Apply [Ampère's law](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) to a rectangular loop perpendicular to the sheet whose two long sides have length $L$. The enclosed current is $KL$, so

$$
2BL=\mu_0KL.
$$

The right-hand rule fixes the directions:

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf B(z>0)=-\frac{\mu_0K}{2}\mathbf e_y,
\qquad
\mathbf B(z<0)=\frac{\mu_0K}{2}\mathbf e_y}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\mathbf e_z\times\mathbf B(0^+)
-\mathbf e_z\times\mathbf B(0^-)
=\frac{\mu_0K}{2}\mathbf e_x
-\left(-\frac{\mu_0K}{2}\mathbf e_x\right)
=\boxed{\mu_0\mathbf K},
$$

which is the magnetic jump condition for a [surface current density](../../../../../../surface-current-density.md).
