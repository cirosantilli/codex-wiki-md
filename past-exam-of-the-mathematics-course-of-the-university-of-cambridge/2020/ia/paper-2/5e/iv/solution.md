↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**False.** Compositions of scalings $z\mapsto\mu z$ and inversion $z\mapsto1/z$ reduce to one of the forms $\nu z$ or $\nu/z$; they preserve the unordered pair $\{0,\infty\}$. But

$$
f(z)=\frac{z}{z+1}
$$

is a [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) fixing $0$ and sends infinity to $1$, so it cannot be such a composition.
