↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** A Möbius transformation fixes infinity exactly when $c=0$, so it has the affine form

$$
z\longmapsto \alpha z+\beta,\qquad \alpha\ne0.
$$

This is the composition of the scaling $z\mapsto\alpha z$ and the translation $z\mapsto z+\beta$.
