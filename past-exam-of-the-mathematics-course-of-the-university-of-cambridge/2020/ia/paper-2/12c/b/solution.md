<h1 id="12c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the parent's rest frame, momentum conservation makes the massive daughter and photon momenta equal and opposite, of magnitude $p$. The [relativistic two-body decay](../../../../../../relativistic-two-body-decay.md) formulas give

$$
p=\frac{M^2-m^2}{2M}c,\qquad
E_R=\frac{M^2+m^2}{2M}c^2.
$$

Since $v=pc^2/E_R$ and $r=m/M$,

$$
\boxed{v=cf(r),\qquad f(r)=\frac{1-r^2}{1+r^2}}.
$$

The daughter's [Lorentz factor](../../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) is

$$
\gamma=\frac{E_R}{mc^2}=\frac{1+r^2}{2r}.
$$

If it decays after [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) $\tau$, [time dilation](../../../../../../time-dilation.md) gives $t=\gamma\tau$, while uniform motion gives $x=v\gamma\tau$. Therefore the event coordinates in the parent frame are

$$
\boxed{(ct,x)=
\left(\frac{1+r^2}{2r}c\tau,\,
\frac{1-r^2}{2r}c\tau\right)}.
$$
