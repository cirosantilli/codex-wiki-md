<h1 id="8d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $x$ is a [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) of $f$, then

$$
g(\sigma x)=\sigma f\sigma^{-1}(\sigma x)
=\sigma f(x)=\sigma x.
$$

Conversely, applying the same argument to $f=\sigma^{-1}g\sigma$ transports every fixed point of $g$ back to one of $f$. Thus the bijection $\sigma$ restricts to a bijection between the two fixed-point sets, so [conjugate functions](../../../../../../conjugate-functions.md) have the same number of fixed points.
