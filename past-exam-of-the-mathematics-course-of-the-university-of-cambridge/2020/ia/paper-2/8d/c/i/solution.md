<h1 id="8d/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a bijection $h:\mathbb N\to\mathbb Z$, and conjugate the translation of the integers:

$$
\boxed{f=h^{-1}\circ(z\mapsto z+1)\circ h}.
$$

Then $f$ is a bijection of $\mathbb N$. Its $k$th [iterate](../../../../../../../iterated-function.md) is $h^{-1}(z\mapsto z+k)h$, which has no fixed point for every $k\geq1$. Hence $f\in T$.
