<h1 id="4c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [central force](../../../../../../central-force.md) conserves [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md). The initial impact parameter gives $L=ub$, while at closest approach the velocity is transverse and $L=wp$. Thus $u=wp/b$. [Conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
\frac{u^2}{2}-\frac{k}{R}
=\frac{w^2}{2}-\frac{k}{p}.
$$

Under $u^2\gg2k/R$, neglect the initial potential term and substitute $u=wp/b$:

$$
\frac{w^2p^2}{2b^2}
=\frac{w^2}{2}-\frac{k}{p}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\frac{w^2}{k}
=\frac{2b^2}{p(b^2-p^2)}}.
$$
