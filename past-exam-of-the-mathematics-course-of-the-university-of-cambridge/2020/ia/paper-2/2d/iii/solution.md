<h1 id="2d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This relation is reflexive because choosing $n=1$ gives $|x/x|=1$. It need not be symmetric: take positive real numbers $y=e$ and $x=e^2$. Then $|x/y^2|=1$ is rational, so $x\sim y$. But

$$
\left|\frac{y}{x^m}\right|=e^{1-2m}
$$

is transcendental, hence irrational, for every integer $m\geq1$. Thus $y\not\sim x$, and the relation is not an equivalence relation.
