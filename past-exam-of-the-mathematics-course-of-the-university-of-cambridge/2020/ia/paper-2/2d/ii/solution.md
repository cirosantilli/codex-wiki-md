↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By the [equality condition in the complex triangle inequality](../../../../../../equality-condition-in-the-complex-triangle-inequality.md),

$$
|x+y|=|x|+|y|
$$

for nonzero $x,y$ exactly when $x/y$ is a positive real number. Having the same [complex argument](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive, so this is an equivalence relation. Its classes are the open rays from the origin.
