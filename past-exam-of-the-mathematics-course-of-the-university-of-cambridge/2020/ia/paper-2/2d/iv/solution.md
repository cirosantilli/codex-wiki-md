↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Set $F(x)=|x^3-x|$. The relation is exactly

$$
x\sim y\iff F(x)=F(y).
$$

It is therefore the [equivalence relation induced by a function](../../../../../../equivalence-relation-induced-by-a-function.md): equality of the two function values is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive. Hence this is an equivalence relation.
