↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md) is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive. Here

$$
|x-y|^2<|x|^2+|y|^2
\iff \operatorname{Re}(x\overline y)>0.
$$

The relation is reflexive and symmetric, but it is not transitive. For example, take

$$
x=1,\qquad y=e^{i\pi/3},\qquad z=e^{2i\pi/3}.
$$

The real parts of $x\overline y$ and $y\overline z$ are positive, whereas that of $x\overline z$ is negative. Hence this is not an equivalence relation.
