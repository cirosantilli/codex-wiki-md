<h1 id="6e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the rotation group $C_5$, the identity fixes all $n^5$ colorings. Because five is prime, each of the four nonidentity rotations is a single five-cycle and fixes exactly the $n$ constant colorings. [Burnside lemma](../../../../../../burnside-s-lemma.md) therefore gives the number of [coloring necklaces](../../../../../../necklace-combinatorics.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{n^5+4n}{5}}.
$$

For the [dihedral group](../../../../../../dihedral-group.md) $D_{10}$, the five reflections each fix one vertex and interchange two pairs of vertices, so each fixes $n^3$ colorings. Adding these to the identity and rotation contributions gives the number of [coloring bracelets](../../../../../../coloring-bracelet.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{n^5+5n^3+4n}{10}}.
$$
