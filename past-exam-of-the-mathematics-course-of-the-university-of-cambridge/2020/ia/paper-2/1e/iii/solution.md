<h1 id="1e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose the support of the four-cycle and then cyclically order it. A chosen set of four letters has $(4-1)!=6$ distinct cycles, so the answer is

$$
\boxed{6\binom n4}.
$$
