↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [product of two transpositions](../../../../../../product-of-two-transpositions.md) has three possible forms. Equal transpositions produce the identity, transpositions meeting in one letter produce a [three-cycle](../../../../../../three-cycle.md), and disjoint transpositions produce two disjoint two-cycles. There are two three-cycles on each chosen triple and three pairings of each chosen four-set. Therefore the number of distinct permutations representable this way is

$$
\boxed{1+2\binom n3+3\binom n4}.
$$
