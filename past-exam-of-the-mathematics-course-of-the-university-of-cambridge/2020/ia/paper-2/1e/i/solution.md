<h1 id="1e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) exchanges exactly two letters and fixes the rest. A [permutation cycle](../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) $(a_1\,a_2\,\ldots,a_k)$ sends each displayed letter to the next, sends $a_k$ to $a_1$, and fixes all other letters.

After imposing $\sigma(1)=2$, the remaining $n-1$ domain elements may be sent bijectively to the remaining $n-1$ values in arbitrary order. Hence the number is

$$
\boxed{(n-1)!}.
$$
