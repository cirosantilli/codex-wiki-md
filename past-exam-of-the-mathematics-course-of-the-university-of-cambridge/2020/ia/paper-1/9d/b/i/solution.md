<h1 id="9d/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate

$$
F(x)=s(x)c(a-x)+c(x)s(a-x).
$$

Using $s'=c$ and $c'=-s$ gives

$$
F'(x)=c(x)c(a-x)+s(x)s(a-x)
-s(x)s(a-x)-c(x)c(a-x)=0.
$$

By the [zero derivative on a connected open set](../../../../../../../zero-derivative-on-a-connected-open-set.md), $F$ is independent of $x$.
