The constant from part (i) is found by setting $x=0$:

$$
F(0)=s(a).
$$

Taking $a=x+y$ therefore gives the [sine addition formula](../../../../../../../sine-addition-formula.md)

$$
\boxed{s(x+y)=s(x)c(y)+c(x)s(y)}.
$$
