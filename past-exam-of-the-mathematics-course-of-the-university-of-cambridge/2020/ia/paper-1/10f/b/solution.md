<h1 id="10f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Boundedness of $(z_n)$ makes both real sequences $(x_n)$ and $(y_n)$ bounded. The [Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem](../../../../../../bolzano-weierstrass-theorem.md) first gives a subsequence $(z_{n_j})$ for which $(x_{n_j})$ converges. Applying it again to $(y_{n_j})$ gives a subsubsequence for which the imaginary parts also converge. Since convergence of [complex numbers](../../../../../../complex-number.md) is equivalent to convergence of their [real parts](../../../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary parts](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md), this subsubsequence converges in $\mathbb C$.
