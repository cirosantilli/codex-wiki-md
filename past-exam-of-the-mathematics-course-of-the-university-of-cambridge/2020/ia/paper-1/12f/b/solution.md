↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [linear combination of independent normal random variables](../../../../../../linear-combination-of-independent-normal-random-variables.md) is normal. Hence

$$
S_n\sim N(0,n),
\qquad
\boxed{U_n=\frac{S_n}{\sqrt n}\sim N(0,1)}.
$$

This also follows by multiplying the [moment-generating functions](../../../../../../moment-generating-function.md) of the [independent and identically distributed random variables](../../../../../../independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md).
