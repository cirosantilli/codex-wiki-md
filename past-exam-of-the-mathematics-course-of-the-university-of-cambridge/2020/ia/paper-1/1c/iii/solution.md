<h1 id="1c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $z\overline z=|z|^2=x^2+y^2$, the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) gives

$$
\frac1z-\frac1{\overline z}
=\frac{\overline z-z}{|z|^2}
=-\frac{2iy}{x^2+y^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}=0,
\qquad \operatorname{Im}=-\frac{2y}{x^2+y^2}}.
$$
