<h1 id="1c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using [Euler's formula](../../../../../../euler-s-formula.md), the [complex exponential function](../../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) satisfies

$$
e^{x+iy}=e^x(\cos y+i\sin y).
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}(e^z)=e^x\cos y,
\qquad \operatorname{Im}(e^z)=e^x\sin y}.
$$
