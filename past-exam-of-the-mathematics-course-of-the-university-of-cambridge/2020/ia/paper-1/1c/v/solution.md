<h1 id="1c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

All values of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) are

$$
w=\log|z|+i(\arg z+2\pi n),
\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Because $x>0$, one may take the [complex argument](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) $\arg z=\arctan(y/x)$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}w=\frac12\log(x^2+y^2),
\qquad
\operatorname{Im}w=\arctan\frac yx+2\pi n,quad n\in\mathbb Z}.
$$
