<h1 id="33d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $a=\hbar/2$. Operators on distinct [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md) factors commute. Acting with $\sigma_x\otimes\sigma_y\otimes\sigma_y$ on $|\uparrow\uparrow\uparrow\rangle$ gives $- |\downarrow\downarrow\downarrow\rangle$, while acting on $|\downarrow\downarrow\downarrow\rangle$ gives $- |\uparrow\uparrow\uparrow\rangle$. Therefore their difference is an eigenvector with eigenvalue $+1$. The same computation applies to the permutations $yxy$ and $yyx$. The product $xxx$ flips the two basis tensors without a phase and so sends their difference to its negative.

Restoring the three factors of $a$, the [GHZ state](../../../../../../greenberger-horne-zeilinger-state.md) has

$$
\boxed{s_x^{(1)}s_y^{(2)}s_y^{(3)}:\ +a^3,\quad s_y^{(1)}s_x^{(2)}s_y^{(3)}:\ +a^3,\quad s_y^{(1)}s_y^{(2)}s_x^{(3)}:\ +a^3,\quad s_x^{(1)}s_x^{(2)}s_x^{(3)}:\ -a^3.}
$$

These are certain measurement outcomes for the four product observables in this state.
