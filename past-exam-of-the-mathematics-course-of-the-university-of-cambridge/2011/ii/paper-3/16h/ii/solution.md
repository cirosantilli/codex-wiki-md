<h1 id="16h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

This class is not first-order [axiomatisable](../../../../../../first-order-axiomatizable-class.md). If a theory axiomatized the countable [dense total orders](../../../../../../dense-order.md), it would have $(\mathbb Q,<)$ as an infinite model. By the [Upward Lowenheim-Skolem theorem](../../../../../../upward-lowenheim-skolem-theorem.md) it would also have an uncountable model, contradicting the proposed restriction. The same argument applies whether countable means exactly countably infinite or at most countable.

$$
\boxed{\text{Countable dense total orders are not first-order axiomatizable.}}
$$
