<h1 id="16h/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This class is also not first-order [axiomatisable](../../../../../../first-order-axiomatizable-class.md). Suppose such a theory had an uncountable dense-order model $M$. Its language is finite. Starting with a countably infinite set of elements and repeatedly adjoining witnesses for all existential formulas with parameters already chosen yields a countable elementary substructure: there are only countably many formulas and parameter tuples at every stage. The induction-on-formulas argument from part (i) proves elementarity. The substructure satisfies every sentence of the theory, yet is countable, a contradiction. This is the witness construction underlying the [Downward Lowenheim-Skolem theorem](../../../../../../downward-lowenheim-skolem-theorem.md).

$$
\boxed{\text{Uncountable dense total orders are not first-order axiomatizable.}}
$$
