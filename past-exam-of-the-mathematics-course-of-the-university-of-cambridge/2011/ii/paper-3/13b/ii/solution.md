<h1 id="13b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a spatially homogeneous positive [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state.md), $\alpha n(n_0^2-n^2)=0$ and $\beta n-\gamma a=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{n=n_0,\qquad a=a_0=\beta n_0/\gamma.}
$$

Write $n=n_0+\eta$, $a=a_0+\xi$, and define $A=2\alpha n_0^2>0$. Homogeneous [linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md) gives

$$
\begin{pmatrix}\dot\eta\\\dot\xi\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-A&0\\\beta&-\gamma\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\eta\\\xi\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $-A,-\gamma$, both negative. Hence this steady state is stable to homogeneous disturbances, including the repeated-eigenvalue case, where a possible $te^{-At}$ factor still decays.
