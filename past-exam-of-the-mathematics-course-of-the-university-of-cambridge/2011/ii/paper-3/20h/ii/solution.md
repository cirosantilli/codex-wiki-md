<h1 id="20h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For [contiguous simplicial maps](../../../../../../contiguous-simplicial-maps.md), $|g|(x)$ and $|h|(x)$ lie in a common simplex of $L$ for every $x$. Its convexity makes

$$
\boxed{H(x,t)=(1-t)|g|(x)+t|h|(x)}
$$

a well-defined map into $|L|$, using the ambient Euclidean realization. The formula is continuous globally and agrees across simplex faces. At $t=0,1$ it equals $|g|,|h|$, so it is the desired [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md).
