<h1 id="23h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $K=k(X)$. The space of [Kähler differentials](../../../../../../kahler-differential.md) $\Omega^1_{K/k}$ is the $K$-vector space generated by symbols $df$, $f\in K$, with relations

$$
d(f+g)=df+dg,\qquad d(fg)=f\,dg+g\,df,\qquad dc=0\quad(c\in k).
$$

Its elements are [rational differentials](../../../../../../rational-differential-on-an-algebraic-curve.md). A local parameter $t$ is a nonconstant element of $K$, so $k(t)$ is a rational function field and $K/k(t)$ is finite. In characteristic zero this extension is separable.

For any $f\in K$ let $P(t,f)=0$ be its minimal polynomial over $k(t)$. Differentiating gives

$$
P_f(t,f)\,df+P_t(t,f)\,dt=0.
$$

Separability ensures $P_f(t,f)\ne0$, so $df$ is a multiple of $dt$, and hence $\Omega^1_{K/k}=Kdt$. To show $dt\ne0$, the derivation $d/dt$ on $k(t)$ extends uniquely to the separable extension $K$, by the same differentiated minimal-polynomial formula; it sends $t$ to $1$, so the universal differential $dt$ cannot vanish. Thus

$$
\boxed{\Omega^1_{k(X)/k}=k(X)\,dt\text{ is one dimensional}.}
$$

For a nonzero differential $\omega=f\,dt$, define its [valuation of a rational differential](../../../../../../valuation-of-a-rational-differential.md) at $p$ by

$$
\boxed{v_p(\omega)=\operatorname{ord}_p(f).}
$$

Concretely $\omega=t^m u\,dt$ with $u$ a unit in the local ring, and this valuation is $m$. For $\omega=0$ one conventionally assigns $+\infty$, not an integer.
