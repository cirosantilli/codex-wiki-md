↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The cyclotomic [Galois group](../../../../../../galois-group.md) theorem states

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\mu_n)/\mathbb Q)\cong(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times,\qquad \sigma_b(\zeta_n)=\zeta_n^b,}
$$

for $b$ coprime to $n$, with $[\mathbb Q(\mu_n):\mathbb Q]=\varphi(n)$, where $\varphi$ is the [Euler totient function](../../../../../../euler-totient-function.md).
