<h1 id="9c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9C](../9c.md)

[Noether theorem](../../../../../noether-theorem.md) associates a conserved quantity $\sum_i p_i\delta q_i$ to a continuous symmetry of the [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) (with the usual boundary-term correction if the Lagrangian changes by a total derivative). The angles $\psi$ and $\phi$ are [cyclic coordinates](../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md), so for the [heavy symmetric top](../../../../../heavy-symmetric-top.md)

$$
\boxed{p_\psi=I_3(\dot\psi+\dot\phi\cos\theta)=b,\qquad p_\phi=I_1\dot\phi\sin^2\theta+b\cos\theta=a.}
$$

Time-translation symmetry gives conserved [energy](../../../../../energy.md), whose [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) expression is

$$
\boxed{E=\frac{p_\theta^2}{2I_1}+\frac{(p_\phi-p_\psi\cos\theta)^2}{2I_1\sin^2\theta}+\frac{p_\psi^2}{2I_3}+Mgl\cos\theta.}
$$

It is quadratic in the momenta; $p_\theta=I_1\dot\theta$. Eliminating the cyclic velocities and putting $\mu=\cos\theta$, so $\dot\mu^2=\sin^2\theta\dot\theta^2$, yields

$$
\boxed{\dot\mu^2=\frac2{I_1}(1-\mu^2)\left(E-\frac{b^2}{2I_3}-Mgl\mu\right)-\frac{(a-b\mu)^2}{I_1^2}=F(\mu).}
$$

The first term has degree three and the second degree two, displaying the cubic governing [nutation](../../../../../nutation.md).
