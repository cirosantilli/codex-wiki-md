<h1 id="7c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Put $a=2/\sqrt3$. On $[-a,a]$, the extrema of $|x(1-x^2)|$ occur at $\pm1/\sqrt3$ or the endpoints. The corresponding values are $2/(3\sqrt3)$, including the endpoint value $a(a^2-1)$. Consequently

$$
|f(x)|\leq\frac{2\lambda}{3\sqrt3}<\frac2{\sqrt3}=a\qquad(\lambda<3,\ |x|\leq a).
$$

Thus $[-a,a]$ is a [trapping region](../../../../../../trapping-region.md) and

$$
\boxed{|x_n|<2/\sqrt3\text{ for all }n\geq1\text{ whenever }|x_0|\leq2/\sqrt3.}
$$

It contains a neighbourhood of the origin. Sign changes are possible because $f(x)$ has opposite sign to $x$ for $1<|x|<a$.
