<h1 id="7c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [iteration of a map](../../../../../../iterated-function.md) $f(x)=\lambda x(1-x^2)$, a [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) satisfies $x[1-\lambda+\lambda x^2]=0$. If $0<\lambda<1$, the bracket is strictly positive, so the sole fixed point is $0$. Its [multiplier of a periodic orbit of an iteration](../../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) is $f'(0)=\lambda$, whose modulus is below one. Thus $\boxed{x=0\text{ is locally asymptotically stable}}$ by [fixed point stability for an iteration](../../../../../../fixed-point-stability-for-an-iteration.md).
