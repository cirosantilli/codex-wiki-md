<h1 id="7c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\lambda>1$, the multiplier at the origin exceeds one, so it is unstable. The other [fixed points](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) are

$$
x_\pm=\pm\sqrt{1-\lambda^{-1}},\qquad f'(x_\pm)=\lambda(1-3x_\pm^2)=3-2\lambda.
$$

Using [fixed point stability for an iteration](../../../../../../fixed-point-stability-for-an-iteration.md), $|3-2\lambda|<1$ exactly when $1<\lambda<2$, and $|3-2\lambda|>1$ when $\lambda>2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{x_\pm\text{ stable for }1<\lambda<2,\quad\text{unstable for }\lambda>2.}
$$

The endpoint $\lambda=2$ has multiplier $-1$ and requires nonlinear analysis, which is not part of the asserted open intervals.
