<h1 id="18h/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Klein four-group](../../../../../../klein-four-group.md) $V_4$ is normal in $S_4$, and $S_4/V_4\cong S_3$ through the action on the three partitions into pairs. A [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) containing $V_4$ is the full preimage of its image in this quotient. Up to conjugacy the [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of $S_3$ have orders $1,2,3,6$. Their preimages give the complete list

$$
\boxed{V_4,\qquad D_8,\qquad A_4,\qquad S_4,}
$$

with respective orders $4,8,12,24$, where $D_8$ denotes the [dihedral group](../../../../../../dihedral-group.md) of order eight. The order-eight preimage is a Sylow two-subgroup of $S_4$, generated for example by a four-cycle and a reflection reversing it.
