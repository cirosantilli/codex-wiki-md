<h1 id="2f/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $z(t)=e^{2\pi it}$ and use the straight [homotopy](../../../../../../../homotopy.md) $H(s,t)=z(t)+s(\gamma(t)-z(t))$. For $0\le s<1$, the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) gives

$$
|H(s,t)|\ge1-s|\gamma(t)-z(t)|\ge1-s>0.
$$

At $s=1$ it is nonzero by the hypothesis. Thus this is a [homotopy](../../../../../../../homotopy.md) of closed loops avoiding the origin, even though the given distance bound is non-strict. The [homotopy invariance of winding number](../../../../../../../homotopy-invariance-of-winding-number.md) gives

$$
\boxed{w(\gamma;0)=w(e^{2\pi it};0)=1.}
$$
