<h1 id="19i/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The invariant [positive-definite bilinear form](../../../../../../positive-definite-bilinear-form.md) makes every $\rho(g)$ orthogonal in an orthonormal real basis. Every element of $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ has the form $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\-\overline b&\overline a\end{pmatrix}$ with $|a|^2+|b|^2=1$, so this group is homeomorphic to $S^3$ and connected. The [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $\det\rho(g)$ takes values in $\{-1,1\}$ and equals one at the identity. It is therefore identically one. Hence **$\boxed{\rho(\mathrm{SU}(2))\subseteq\mathrm{SO}(3)}$**.
