<h1 id="19i/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A traceless [skew-Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md) has the unique form

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}ix&y+iz\\-y+iz&-ix\end{pmatrix},\qquad x,y,z\in\mathbb R.
$$

This real-linear parametrization is bijective, proving **$\boxed{V\cong\mathbb R^3}$** as [real vector spaces](../../../../../../real-vector-space.md). It is not a [complex vector space](../../../../../../complex-vector-space.md): multiplication by $i$ generally turns a [skew-Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md) into a Hermitian one.
