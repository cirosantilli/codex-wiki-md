<h1 id="19i/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The pairing is real and symmetric: $\overline{\operatorname{tr}(AB)}=\operatorname{tr}(B^*A^*)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)=\operatorname{tr}(AB)$, and cyclicity of the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) exchanges $A,B$. For the coordinates in part (i), $A^2=-(x^2+y^2+z^2)I$, so

$$
\boxed{-\operatorname{tr}(A^2)=2(x^2+y^2+z^2)>0\quad(A\ne0).}
$$

Thus it is a positive-definite real [bilinear form](../../../../../../bilinear-form.md), hence nondegenerate. Finally

$$
-\operatorname{tr}[(gAg^{-1})(gBg^{-1})]=-\operatorname{tr}(gABg^{-1})=-\operatorname{tr}(AB),
$$

proving invariance under [conjugation](../../../../../../conjugation.md).
