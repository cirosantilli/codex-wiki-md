↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Poisson bracket](../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) in canonical coordinates is

$$
\boxed{\{f,g\}=\sum_i\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial q_i}\frac{\partial g}{\partial p_i}-\frac{\partial f}{\partial p_i}\frac{\partial g}{\partial q_i}\right).}
$$

Using [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) $\dot q_i=H_{p_i}$ and $\dot p_i=-H_{q_i}$ in the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{df}{dt}=\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}+\{f,H\}.}
$$

The partial time derivative holds phase-space coordinates fixed; the total derivative follows the actual trajectory.
