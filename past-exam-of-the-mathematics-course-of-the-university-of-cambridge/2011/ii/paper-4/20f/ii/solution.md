The [Dirichlet unit theorem](../../../../../../dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md) states that for a [number field](../../../../../../number-field.md) with $r_1$ real embeddings and $r_2$ conjugate pairs of nonreal embeddings,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal O_K^\times\cong\mu(K)\times\mathbb Z^{r_1+r_2-1},}
$$

where $\mu(K)$ is its finite cyclic group of [roots of unity](../../../../../../root-of-unity.md). Equivalently there exist $r_1+r_2-1$ multiplicatively independent [fundamental units](../../../../../../fundamental-unit-number-theory.md) such that every unit is a unique product of a root of unity and [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) powers of those units.
