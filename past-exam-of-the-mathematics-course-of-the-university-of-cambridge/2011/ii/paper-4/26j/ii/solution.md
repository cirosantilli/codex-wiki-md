↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put

$$
a_j=\int_{[0,\infty)}e^{-\lambda t}\frac{(\lambda t)^j}{j!}\,dG(t),\qquad j\ge0.
$$

The [transition probabilities](../../../../../../transition-probability.md) are

$$
\boxed{p_{0j}=a_j;\qquad p_{ij}=\begin{cases}a_{j-i+1},&j\ge i-1,\\0,&j<i-1,\end{cases}\quad i\ge1.}
$$

The first and second rows coincide because either an arrival to an empty queue or the single existing customer initiates the next service.
