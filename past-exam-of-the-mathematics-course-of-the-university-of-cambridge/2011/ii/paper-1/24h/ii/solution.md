<h1 id="24h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The coefficients of $\det(TI-A)$ are polynomial functions of the matrix entries. Its [polynomial discriminant](../../../../../../polynomial-discriminant.md) $\Delta(A)$ is therefore a polynomial in those entries, and it is nonzero exactly when all roots of the characteristic polynomial are distinct. Thus the required set is the [principal open subset](../../../../../../principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(\Delta)$ of affine space. It is nonempty: an algebraically closed field is infinite, so choose a diagonal matrix with $n$ pairwise distinct entries.

It is also affine as a variety. The map $A\mapsto(A,1/\Delta(A))$ identifies it with the closed hypersurface

$$
\boxed{\{(A,z):z\Delta(A)=1\}\subseteq\mathbb A^{n^2+1}.}
$$

Projection to $A$ is its regular inverse. This proves the requested affineness while retaining the open embedding into matrix space.
