↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [affine variety](../../../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md) is irreducible when it is nonempty and cannot be expressed as the union of two proper [Zariski-closed sets](../../../../../../zariski-closed-set.md). If a nonempty open subset $U$ were not dense, $X=\overline U\cup(X\setminus U)$ would be such a union: the first set is proper by assumption, and the second is proper because $U$ is nonempty. Hence **every nonempty open subset of an irreducible variety is dense**.
