<h1 id="16h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [16H](../16h.md)

The inductive definition of [ordinal addition](../../../../../ordinal-addition.md) is $\alpha+0=\alpha$, $\alpha+(\beta+1)=(\alpha+\beta)+1$, and $\alpha+\lambda=\sup_{\beta<\lambda}(\alpha+\beta)$ for a nonzero limit ordinal $\lambda$. The synthetic definition is the [order type](../../../../../order-type.md) of the [order sum](../../../../../order-sum.md) obtained by putting a copy of $\beta$ after a disjoint copy of $\alpha$.

These agree by [transfinite induction](../../../../../transfinite-induction.md) on the second argument. Appending no points gives $\alpha$; appending one new greatest point gives a successor. At a limit stage, the order sum is exhausted by the increasing initial segments obtained by appending each $\beta<\lambda$, so its order type is their supremum. Thus the synthetic construction satisfies all three recursion clauses, and uniqueness of transfinite recursion proves equivalence.
