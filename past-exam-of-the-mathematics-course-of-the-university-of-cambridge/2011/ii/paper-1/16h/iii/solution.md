**False.** Take $\alpha=\omega^2+1$, which satisfies the proposed lower bound. Associativity of [ordinal addition](../../../../../../ordinal-addition.md) and $1+\omega^2=\omega^2$ give

$$
\boxed{(\omega^2+1)+\omega^2=\omega^2\cdot2,\qquad
\omega^2+(\omega^2+1)=\omega^2\cdot2+1.}
$$

The final successor survives only in the second expression.
