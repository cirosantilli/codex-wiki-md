<h1 id="16h/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Always true.** In the order-sum interpretation of [ordinal multiplication](../../../../../../ordinal-multiplication.md), $\alpha(\beta+\gamma)$ consists of copies of $\alpha$ indexed first by $\beta$ and then by $\gamma$. The first portion has [order type](../../../../../../order-type.md) $\alpha\beta$ and the second $\alpha\gamma$, proving $\alpha(\beta+\gamma)=\alpha\beta+\alpha\gamma$. Equivalently, [transfinite induction](../../../../../../transfinite-induction.md) on $\gamma$ uses the successor rule, associativity of [ordinal addition](../../../../../../ordinal-addition.md), and continuity of both operations in the right argument at limit ordinals.
