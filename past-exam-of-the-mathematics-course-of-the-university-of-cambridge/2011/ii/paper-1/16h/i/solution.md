**False.** By the successor and limit clauses for [ordinal multiplication](../../../../../../ordinal-multiplication.md),

$$
\boxed{2\omega=\sup_{n<\omega}2n=\omega,\qquad
\omega2=\omega+\omega>\omega.}
$$

Multiplication is not commutative because it counts ordered copies, rather than merely the cardinality of their union.
