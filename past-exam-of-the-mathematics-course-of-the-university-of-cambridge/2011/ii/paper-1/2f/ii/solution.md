<h1 id="2f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each integer $n\geq0$, put $Z_n=\{z:f^{(n)}(z)=0\}$. Every [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) $f^{(n)}$ is an [entire function](../../../../../../entire-function.md) and cannot be identically zero: otherwise repeated integration would make $f$ a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md). By the [identity theorem for holomorphic functions](../../../../../../identity-theorem.md), its [zero set](../../../../../../zero-set.md) has empty [interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md), and [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) makes $Z_n$ a [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md). Thus $Z_n$ is [nowhere dense](../../../../../../nowhere-dense-set.md).

The complex plane with its usual [metric](../../../../../../metric.md) is a [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md), so the [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md) gives $z_0\notin\bigcup_{n\geq0}Z_n$. The [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) there has coefficients

$$
\boxed{\frac{f^{(n)}(z_0)}{n!}\ne0\quad\text{for every }n\geq0.}
$$
