↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The closed-set version of the [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md) says that if a nonempty [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) $X$ is a countable union $X=\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty F_n$ of [closed sets](../../../../../../closed-set.md), then at least one $F_n$ has nonempty [interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md). Equivalently, a nonempty [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) cannot be a countable union of closed [nowhere dense sets](../../../../../../nowhere-dense-set.md).
