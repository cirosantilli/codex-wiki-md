<h1 id="29j/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With $x=0$, the [budget constraint](../../../../../../budget-constraint.md) is $S_0^T\theta=w_0$. On this hyperplane the riskless term is constant, so maximize $\theta^T\mu-(\gamma/2)\theta^TV\theta$. The [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) equation gives $\theta=M(\mu-\eta S_0)$. If $S_0\ne0$, let $d=S_0^TMS_0>0$ and impose the budget constraint:

$$
\boxed{\eta=\frac{S_0^TM\mu-w_0}{S_0^TMS_0},\qquad\theta^*=M(\mu-\eta S_0),\qquad x^*=0.}
$$

Positive definiteness makes the objective strictly concave on the hyperplane, proving this stationary point is the global constrained optimum. If $S_0=0$, the cash-free constraint is feasible only when $w_0=0$; in that degenerate case the optimum is $M\mu$. The displayed quotient, including the one in the question, presupposes $S_0\ne0$.
