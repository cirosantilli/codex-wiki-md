<h1 id="9c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [canonical momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is $p_i=\partial L/\partial\dot x_i=m\dot x_i+qA_i$. Its time derivative contains the total derivative along the trajectory: $\dot p_i=m\ddot x_i+q\partial_tA_i+q\dot x_j\partial_jA_i$. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) therefore gives

$$
m\ddot x_i=q\left[-\partial_i\phi-\partial_tA_i+
\dot x_j(\partial_iA_j-\partial_jA_i)\right].
$$

The [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) is $\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A$, so the last term is $(\dot{\mathbf x}\times\mathbf B)_i$. With the [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md) $\mathbf E=-\nabla\phi-\partial_t\mathbf A$, this is precisely the [Lorentz force law](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md):

$$
\boxed{m\ddot{\mathbf x}=q(\mathbf E+\dot{\mathbf x}\times\mathbf B).}
$$
