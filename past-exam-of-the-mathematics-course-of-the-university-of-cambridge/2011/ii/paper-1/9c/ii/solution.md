<h1 id="9c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The specified [vector potential](../../../../../../vector-potential.md) gives $\mathbf B=(0,0,B)$. Put $\omega=qB/m$, retaining its sign. The [Lorentz force law](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) yields $\ddot x=\omega\dot y$, $\ddot y=-\omega\dot x$ and $\ddot z=0$. For initial velocities $(u,v,w)$ and $\omega\ne0$, integration gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
x(t)&=x_0+\frac u\omega\sin\omega t+\frac v\omega(1-\cos\omega t),\\
y(t)&=y_0+\frac v\omega\sin\omega t-\frac u\omega(1-\cos\omega t),\\
z(t)&=z_0+wt.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The transverse motion is a circle centred at $(x_0+v/\omega,y_0-u/\omega)$ with radius $\sqrt{u^2+v^2}/|\omega|$. Thus the full orbit is a [helix](../../../../../../helix.md) around a line parallel to the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md), with period $2\pi/|\omega|$ and signed axial advance $2\pi w/|\omega|$ per turn. The charge sign determines the sense of rotation. If $w=0$ it is a circle, if $u=v=0$ it is a straight line parallel to the field, and if $qB=0$ all motion is uniform rectilinear motion.
