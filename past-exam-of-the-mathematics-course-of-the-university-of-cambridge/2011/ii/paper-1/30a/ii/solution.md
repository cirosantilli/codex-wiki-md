<h1 id="30a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Every value of the solution is transported unchanged from its initial characteristic endpoint. Therefore the formula from (i) immediately gives

$$
\boxed{f_I\geq0\ \Longrightarrow\ f(x,v,t)=f_I(\Phi_{-t}(x,v))\geq0.}
$$

More generally the [Hamiltonian transport equation](../../../../../../hamiltonian-transport-equation.md) preserves the range of the initial values while its characteristic solution exists: it neither creates negative values nor changes values by a source term.
