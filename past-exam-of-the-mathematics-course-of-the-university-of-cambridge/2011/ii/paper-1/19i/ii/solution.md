<h1 id="19i/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\chi$ be the [character of a representation](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) of $\rho$. The [character orthogonality](../../../../../../character-orthogonality.md) identity for an [irreducible character](../../../../../../irreducible-character.md) is $\sum_{g\in G}|\chi(g)|^2=|G|=n$. For $g\in Z$, the scalar formula from part (i) gives $|\chi(g)|=d$. Keeping these $m$ nonnegative summands yields

$$
\boxed{md^2\leq n,\qquad d^2\leq n/m.}
$$
