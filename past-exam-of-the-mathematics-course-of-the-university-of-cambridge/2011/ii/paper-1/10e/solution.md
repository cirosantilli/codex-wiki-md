<h1 id="10e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10E](../10e.md)

For neighbouring [comoving observers](../../../../../comoving-observer.md) separated by a small physical distance $d\ell$, the recession velocity is $H(t)d\ell$, where $H=\dot a/a$ is the [Hubble parameter](../../../../../hubble-parameter.md). During the photon's travel time $dt=d\ell/c$, the local [Doppler effect](../../../../../doppler-effect.md) gives $d\lambda/\lambda=Hdt$. Integrating along the ray gives the [cosmological wavelength-redshift relation](../../../../../cosmological-wavelength-redshift-relation.md):

$$
\log\frac{\lambda_0}{\lambda_e}=\int_{t_e}^{t_0}\frac{\dot a}a\,dt,
\qquad\boxed{1+z=\frac{a(t_0)}{a(t_e)}.}
$$

The same ratio stretches the arrival interval of successive wave crests and of successive emitted pulses: $dt_0=(1+z)dt_e$. Each [photon](../../../../../photon.md) loses a factor $1+z$ in energy because $E=hc/\lambda$. Thus energy arriving through the sphere per unit observer time has one factor from [cosmological redshift](../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) and one from time dilation:

$$
\boxed{\frac{dE_0}{dt_0}=\frac L{(1+z)^2}.}
$$

This is the total power crossing the sphere; division by its area would give the observed [flux](../../../../../flux.md).
