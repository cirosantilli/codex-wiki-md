**False.** Changing the connecting path changes the isomorphism by an inner automorphism. Take $x=y$, one path constant and the other representing $a$ in the [Klein bottle group](../../../../../../klein-bottle-group.md) $\langle a,b:aba^{-1}=b^{-1}\rangle$. The first induced isomorphism is the identity, whereas the second sends $b$ to $b^{-1}\ne b$. The element $b$ has infinite order, as is also witnessed by mapping it to a translation and $a$ to a reflection in the infinite dihedral group. A fixed path to a different endpoint can be appended if distinct basepoints are desired.
