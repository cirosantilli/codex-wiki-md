↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**False.** Take a bouquet of two circles and the two-dimensional torus. Both are connected polyhedra and have [first homology group](../../../../../../first-homology.md) $\mathbb Z^2$. Their [fundamental groups](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) are respectively the nonabelian free group $F_2$ and the abelian group $\mathbb Z^2$, so they are not isomorphic. First homology only remembers the [abelianization](../../../../../../abelianization.md) of the fundamental group.
