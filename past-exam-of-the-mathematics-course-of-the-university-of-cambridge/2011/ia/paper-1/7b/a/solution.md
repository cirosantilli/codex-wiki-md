<h1 id="7b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expanding the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) gives

$$
\det(M-tI)=(2-t)\bigl[(1-t)(3-t)+2\bigr]-2(2-t)
=(2-t)(1-t)(3-t).
$$

Thus the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1,2,3$. Solving the corresponding [homogeneous linear systems](../../../../../../homogeneous-linear-system.md) gives

$$
\boxed{E_1=\operatorname{span}\{(-1,0,1)^T\},\quad
E_2=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T\},\quad
E_3=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,1)^T\}.}
$$

Every nonzero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) in the displayed [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) is an [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md). Direct multiplication verifies the three eigenvalue equations. The distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) imply these [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are linearly independent and form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of $\mathbb R^3$.
