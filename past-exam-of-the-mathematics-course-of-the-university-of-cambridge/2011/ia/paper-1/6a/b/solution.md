<h1 id="6a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At $\alpha=0$, $\Phi$ is the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto the plane perpendicular to $n$. Its [image of a linear map](../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) and [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{im}\Phi=n^\perp,\qquad \ker\Phi=\operatorname{span}\{n\}.}
$$

Every perpendicular [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is unchanged and every parallel [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is annihilated. The image plane is perpendicular to the kernel axis by the definition of $n^\perp$; their dimensions two and one also agree with the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md).
