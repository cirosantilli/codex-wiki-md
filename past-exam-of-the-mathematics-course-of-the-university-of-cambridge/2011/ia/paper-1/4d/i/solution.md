<h1 id="4d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) coefficient $c_n=n^2$, the [Cauchy-Hadamard theorem](../../../../../../cauchy-hadamard-theorem.md) gives

$$
R^{-1}=\limsup_{n\to\infty}|c_n|^{1/n}
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\exp\!\left(\frac{2\log n}{n}\right)=1.
$$

Thus the **[radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md) is $\boxed{R=1}$**. The [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) converges absolutely for $|z|<1$ and diverges for $|z|>1$. On $|z|=1$ its terms have modulus $n^2$, so they do not tend to zero and the series diverges there as well.
