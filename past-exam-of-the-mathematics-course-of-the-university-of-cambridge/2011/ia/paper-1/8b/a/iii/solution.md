<h1 id="8b/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose real [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $u,v$ with distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda,\mu$. Symmetry gives

$$
\lambda u^Tv=(Mu)^Tv=u^TMv=\mu u^Tv.
$$

Therefore $(\lambda-\mu)u^Tv=0$, and **$\boxed{u\cdot v=0}$**. For complex choices, replace transpose by [conjugate transpose](../../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md); the reality proved in part (i) gives the same conclusion for the [Hermitian inner product](../../../../../../../hermitian-form.md). Thus different [eigenspaces](../../../../../../../eigenspace.md) are [orthogonal](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md).
