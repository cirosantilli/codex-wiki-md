**No.** The [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $f(x)=(x+1)/2$ maps $(0,1)$ into $(0,1)$, but $f(x)-x=(1-x)/2>0$ throughout the domain. Its only possible [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) is the excluded endpoint one.
