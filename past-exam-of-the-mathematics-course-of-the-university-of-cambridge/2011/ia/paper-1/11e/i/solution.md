<h1 id="11e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At zero, $|f(x)-f(0)|=|x|\to0$, so the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). At a nonzero $x_0$, approach along rational and irrational [sequences](../../../../../../sequence.md); [density of the rational numbers](../../../../../../density-of-the-rational-numbers.md) and [density of the irrational numbers](../../../../../../density-of-the-irrational-numbers.md) give limiting values $x_0$ and $-x_0$, which are different. Thus the **[continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) set is $\boxed{\{0\}}$**.

At zero, the [difference quotient](../../../../../../difference-quotient.md) is one for rational nonzero increments and minus one for irrational increments, so the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) does not exist. Elsewhere [differentiability implies continuity](../../../../../../differentiability-implies-continuity.md), and continuity already fails. Thus the **[differentiability](../../../../../../differentiability.md) set is $\boxed{\varnothing}$**.
