<h1 id="11e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Away from zero, the product and composition of the displayed smooth [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) are [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) and [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md). At zero,

$$
|f(x)|\le|x|\longrightarrow0,
$$

so $f$ remains [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). Hence the **[continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) set is $\boxed{\mathbb R}$**.

The [difference quotient](../../../../../../difference-quotient.md) at zero is $f(h)/h=\sin(1/h)$. For $h_n=(\pi/2+2\pi n)^{-1}$ it equals one, and for $k_n=(3\pi/2+2\pi n)^{-1}$ it equals minus one. Both [sequences](../../../../../../sequence.md) tend to zero, so no [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) exists there. For $x\ne0$, the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) and [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) give

$$
\boxed{f'(x)=\sin(1/x)-\frac{\cos(1/x)}x.}
$$

Thus the **[differentiability](../../../../../../differentiability.md) set is $\boxed{\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}}$**.
