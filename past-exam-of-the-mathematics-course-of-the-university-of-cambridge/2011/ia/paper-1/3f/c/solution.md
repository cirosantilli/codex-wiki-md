<h1 id="3f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Define $a_{2k}=0$ and $a_{2k-1}=k$. The odd-indexed terms are unbounded, while the even-indexed [subsequence](../../../../../../subsequence.md) is identically zero and hence converges to zero. This gives **an unbounded [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) with a convergent [subsequence](../../../../../../subsequence.md)**.
