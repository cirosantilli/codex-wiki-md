The [Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem](../../../../../../bolzano-weierstrass-theorem.md) states that **every bounded [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) in $\mathbb R$ has a convergent [subsequence](../../../../../../subsequence.md)**. More generally, a bounded [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) in $\mathbb R^d$ has a [subsequence](../../../../../../subsequence.md) converging to a point of $\mathbb R^d$.
