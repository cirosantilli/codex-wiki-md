<h1 id="9b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the scalar product of [Newton's second law](../../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) with the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $\dot{\mathbf r}$. The [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) is perpendicular to its first factor, so

$$
\frac d{dt}\left(\frac12m|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2\right)
=\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf F
=-\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot\nabla\phi
+\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot(\dot{\mathbf r}\times\mathbf B)
=-\frac d{dt}\phi(\mathbf r).
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12m|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2+\phi(\mathbf r)\quad\text{is constant}.}
$$

This is [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md): the sum of [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) stays fixed. The magnetic-type [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) $\dot{\mathbf r}\times\mathbf B$ does no [work](../../../../../../work.md), because it is perpendicular to the instantaneous [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md). It may turn the trajectory but cannot directly change its speed, regardless of the spatial variation of $\mathbf B$.
