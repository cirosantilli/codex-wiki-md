[Fermat's little theorem](../../../../../fermat-little-theorem.md) states that for a [prime](../../../../../prime-number.md) $p$ and an integer $a$,

$$
a^p\equiv a\pmod p;
\qquad p\nmid a\ \Longrightarrow\ a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p.
$$

[Wilson theorem](../../../../../wilson-s-theorem.md) states that for a [prime](../../../../../prime-number.md) $p$,

$$
\boxed{(p-1)!\equiv-1\pmod p.}
$$

All the following [modular congruences](../../../../../modular-congruence.md) are taken modulo the indicated [prime](../../../../../prime-number.md).
