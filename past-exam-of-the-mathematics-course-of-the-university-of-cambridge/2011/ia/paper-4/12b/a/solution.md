<h1 id="12b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The relativistic energy of a particle of [rest mass](../../../../../../invariant-mass.md) $m$ is $E=\gamma mc^2$, where the [Lorentz factor](../../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) is $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. Using the [binomial series](../../../../../../binomial-series.md) at small $v/c$ gives

$$
\boxed{E=mc^2+\frac12mv^2+O\left(\frac{mv^4}{c^2}\right).}
$$

The second term is the familiar Newtonian [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). The first is the [rest energy](../../../../../../rest-energy.md), which remains when $v=0$. Thus the new physical idea is **mass-energy equivalence: a body's mass represents energy $mc^2$ even at rest**. The total energy is not merely energy due to motion.
