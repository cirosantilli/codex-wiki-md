<h1 id="11b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Without dust collection, $dM=-dm$. The [rocket equation](../../../../../../rocket-equation.md) reduces to

$$
M\,dv=u\,dm=-u\,dM.
$$

Integrating from $v=0$, $M=M_0$ gives

$$
\boxed{v=u\log\frac{M_0}{M}.}
$$

This is the usual [rocket equation](../../../../../../rocket-equation.md) with constant relative exhaust speed, valid while the mass remains positive and the prescribed ejection continues.
