<h1 id="1d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) preserves whether the two coordinates are equal. Consequently the diagonal and its complement are invariant under this [group action](../../../../../../group-action.md). Any $(i,i)$ can be carried to any $(j,j)$ by a [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) sending $i$ to $j$. Likewise, for $i\ne j$ and $k\ne l$, the assignment $i\mapsto k$, $j\mapsto l$ extends to a [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) of the remaining letters.

For $n\geq2$ the [equality-pattern orbits of ordered pairs](../../../../../../equality-pattern-orbits-of-ordered-pairs.md) are the two [orbits of a group action](../../../../../../orbit-of-a-group-action.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\{(i,i):1\leq i\leq n\},\qquad\{(i,j):i\ne j\},}
$$

with sizes $n$ and $n(n-1)$. For $n=1$ only the diagonal [orbit of a group action](../../../../../../orbit-of-a-group-action.md) exists.
