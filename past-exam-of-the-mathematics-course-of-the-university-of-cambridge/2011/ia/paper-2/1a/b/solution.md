<h1 id="1a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Eliminate the free constant by comparing consecutive values:

$$
y_{n+1}-2y_n=(a2^{n+1}-n-1)-2(a2^n-n)=n-1.
$$

Therefore an appropriate [inhomogeneous linear recurrence](../../../../../../inhomogeneous-linear-recurrence.md) is

$$
\boxed{y_{n+1}-2y_n=n-1.}
$$

Its homogeneous part has solution $a2^n$, and $-n$ is a particular solution. A first-order [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md) has one arbitrary initial value, so this gives exactly the desired general solution.
