<h1 id="6a/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At $(x,0,z)$ the [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) is $(2x,0,2z)$. The outward radial unit [vector](../../../../../../../vector.md) is $(x,0,z)/\sqrt{x^2+z^2}$ when $(x,z)\ne(0,0)$. Taking its scalar product with the [gradient](../../../../../../../gradient.md) gives the radial [directional derivative](../../../../../../../directional-derivative.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial f}{\partial r}=2\sqrt{x^2+z^2}.}
$$

Equivalently, on this plane $f=r^2$. At the origin the radial unit vector is undefined, but every unit [directional derivative](../../../../../../../directional-derivative.md) is zero. Differentiating along the unnormalized scaling path $(tx,0,tz)$ instead gives $2(x^2+z^2)$ at $t=1$; that is a rate per scaling parameter, not per unit radial distance.
