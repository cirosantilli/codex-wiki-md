↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [partial derivatives](../../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) of $f$ are $f_x=2x$, $f_y=4y(a-y^2)$ and $f_z=2z$. Therefore the three [implicit differentiation](../../../../../../../implicit-differentiation.md) formulas are

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial y}\right)_z=\frac{2y(y^2-a)}x,\qquad
\left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial z}\right)_x=\frac{z}{2y(y^2-a)},\qquad
\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right)_y=-\frac{x}z.}
$$

Their product is $-1$ wherever $xyz(y^2-a)\ne0$. The excluded factors identify exactly where these quotient formulas cannot all represent finite [partial derivatives](../../../../../../../partial-derivative.md).
