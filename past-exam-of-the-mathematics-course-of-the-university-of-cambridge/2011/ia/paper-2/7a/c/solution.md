<h1 id="7a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Both functions satisfy the constant-coefficient [homogeneous linear differential equation](../../../../../../homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{y'''+y'=0.}
$$

Its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\lambda(\lambda^2+1)$, so the general real solution is $y=A+B\cos x+C\sin x$. The three initial conditions give $A+B=0$, $C=1$, and $-B=0$, hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\sin x.}
$$

Here the equation has continuous coefficients and nonzero highest-derivative coefficient everywhere. The [uniqueness theorem for ordinary differential equations](../../../../../../uniqueness-theorem-for-ordinary-differential-equations.md), applied to its three-component first-order system, gives **a unique solution on the entire half-line**.
