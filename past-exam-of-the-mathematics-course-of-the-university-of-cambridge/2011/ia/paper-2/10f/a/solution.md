<h1 id="10f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For any nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $X$ with finite [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value.md) and any $a>0$, [Markov's inequality](../../../../../../markov-inequality.md) states

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X\ge a)\le\frac{\mathbb E X}{a}.}
$$

Indeed $X\ge a\,\mathbf1_{\{X\ge a\}}$, and taking [expectations](../../../../../../expected-value.md) proves the bound. Nonnegativity is essential.
