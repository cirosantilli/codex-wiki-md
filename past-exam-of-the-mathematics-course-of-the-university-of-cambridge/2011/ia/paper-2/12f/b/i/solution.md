<h1 id="12f/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Conditional on $\Phi=\phi$, the [uniform distribution](../../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) of the sector's centre angle gives a range of possible centre bearings of length $\phi$ that hit a specified house. Independence of the two angles therefore gives the [conditional probability](../../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) $\phi/(2\pi)$. By the [law of total expectation](../../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(H_1\text{ hit})=\mathbb E\left[\frac{\Phi}{2\pi}\right]=\frac13.}
$$
