<h1 id="12f/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The event that $H_1$ is hit but $H_2$ is not has probability $1/3-1/12=1/4$. Dividing by the [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) $1/3$ that $H_1$ is covered, using the definition of [conditional probability](../../../../../../../conditional-probability.md), gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(H_2\text{ not hit}\mid H_1\text{ hit})=
\frac{1/3-1/12}{1/3}=\frac34.}
$$

This conditioning concerns angular coverage of a sector; the two house-hit events are not independent.
