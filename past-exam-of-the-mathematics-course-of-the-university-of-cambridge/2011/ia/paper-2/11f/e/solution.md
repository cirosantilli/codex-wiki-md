↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Now the event is to hit $20$ before $1$, starting at $3$. Up to the first of those two [hitting times](../../../../../../first-passage-time.md), translate the interval by one unit; its endpoints become $0,19$ and the starting point becomes $2$. The [gambler's ruin](../../../../../../gambler-s-ruin.md) [hitting probability](../../../../../../hitting-probability.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P_3(T_{20}<T_1)=\frac{3-1}{20-1}=\frac2{19}.}
$$

Stopping at $1$ is essential: using the original absorbing endpoint $0$ would answer a different event.
