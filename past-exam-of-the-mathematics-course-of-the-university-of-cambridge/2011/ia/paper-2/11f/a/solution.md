<h1 id="11f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $h_k$ be the [hitting probability](../../../../../../hitting-probability.md) of the right endpoint before the left endpoint, starting at $k$. A surviving vase corresponds to that right-endpoint event. Conditioning on the first step of the [simple symmetric random walk](../../../../../../simple-symmetric-random-walk.md) gives

$$
\boxed{h_k=\frac12h_{k-1}+\frac12h_{k+1},\quad 1\le k\le19;\qquad h_0=0,\quad h_{20}=1.}
$$

The [Markov property](../../../../../../markov-property.md) makes the remaining probability depend only on the position after that step. These absorbing endpoint values distinguish the event of interest from its complement.
