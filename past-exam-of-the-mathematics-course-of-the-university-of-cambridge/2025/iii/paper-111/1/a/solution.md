A [root system](../../../../../../root-system.md) in the real [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) space $V$ is a finite spanning set $\Phi\subset V\setminus\{0\}$ such that

$$
\Phi\cap\mathbb R\alpha=\{\alpha,-\alpha\}
\quad\text{and}\quad
s_\alpha(\Phi)=\Phi
\qquad(\alpha\in\Phi),
$$

where the [orthogonal reflection](../../../../../../reflection-in-a-hyperplane.md)

$$
s_\alpha(v)=v-2\frac{(v,\alpha)}{(\alpha,\alpha)}\alpha.
$$

For a crystallographic root system one additionally requires $2(\beta,\alpha)/(\alpha,\alpha)\in\mathbb Z$; that condition is not needed for general finite reflection groups.

A [fundamental system of a root system](../../../../../../fundamental-system-of-a-root-system.md) is a basis $\Delta\subset\Phi$ such that every root has either all nonnegative or all nonpositive coordinates in this basis. Its associated [positive system of a root system](../../../../../../positive-system-of-a-root-system.md) is

$$
\Pi=\Phi\cap\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R_{\geq0}}\Delta,
$$

and $\Phi=\Pi\sqcup(-\Pi)$.
