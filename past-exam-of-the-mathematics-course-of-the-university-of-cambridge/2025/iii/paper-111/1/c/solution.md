<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the [positive-root criterion for Coxeter length](../../../../../../positive-root-criterion-for-coxeter-length.md): for a simple root $\alpha\in\Delta$,

$$
\ell(ws_\alpha)>\ell(w)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
w\alpha\in\Pi.
$$

The hypothesis $w\Delta\subseteq\Pi$ therefore says that every simple generator is a right ascent of $w$. If $w\ne1$, a [reduced expression in a Coxeter group](../../../../../../reduced-expression-in-a-coxeter-group.md) for $w$ has a final simple generator $s_\alpha$, and deleting it gives

$$
\ell(ws_\alpha)=\ell(w)-1,
$$

a contradiction. Hence $w=1$.

If $w$ stabilizes $\Delta$ setwise, then $w\Delta=\Delta\subseteq\Pi$, so the result just proved gives $w=1$. Thus the stabilizer of every fundamental system is trivial.
