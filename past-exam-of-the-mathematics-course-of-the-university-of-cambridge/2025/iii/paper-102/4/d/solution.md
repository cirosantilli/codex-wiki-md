↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbb C)$ there are two fundamental weights. The corresponding [Fundamental representations of sl3](../../../../../../fundamental-representations-of-sl3.md) are

$$
\boxed{V(\omega_1)=\mathbb C^3,\qquad
V(\omega_2)=\Lambda^2\mathbb C^3\cong(\mathbb C^3)^*}.
$$
