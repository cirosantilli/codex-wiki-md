<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For an irreducible representation $V(\lambda)$, write

$$
\lambda=\sum_i m_i\omega_i.
$$

Form

$$
T=\bigotimes_iV(\omega_i)^{\otimes m_i}.
$$

The tensor product of highest-weight vectors is killed by all positive-root spaces and has weight $\lambda$. It therefore generates a highest-weight constituent isomorphic to $V(\lambda)$. By [complete reducibility of semisimple Lie algebra representations](../../../../../../weyl-s-theorem-on-complete-reducibility.md), this constituent is a subrepresentation of $T$. This proves the [generation by fundamental representations](../../../../../../generation-by-fundamental-representations.md) statement.
