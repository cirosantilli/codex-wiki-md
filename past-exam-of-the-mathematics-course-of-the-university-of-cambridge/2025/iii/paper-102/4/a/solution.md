<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose positive roots $\Phi^+$ and let

$$
\rho=\frac12\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}\alpha
$$

be the [Weyl vector](../../../../../../half-sum-of-positive-roots.md). For a [dominant integral weight](../../../../../../dominant-integral-weight.md) $\lambda$, the [Weyl dimension formula](../../../../../../weyl-dimension-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dim V(\lambda)
=\prod_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}
\frac{\langle\lambda+\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}
{\langle\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle}}.
$$

Here $\alpha^\vee$ is the coroot and $\langle\ ,\ \rangle$ is the natural weight-coroot pairing.
