<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Weyl reflection](../../../../../../weyl-reflection.md) $s_{\alpha_1}$ swaps $\varepsilon_1$ and $\varepsilon_2$, whereas $s_{\alpha_2}$ changes the sign of $\varepsilon_2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
s_{\alpha_1}(\alpha_1)&=-\alpha_1,
&s_{\alpha_1}(\alpha_2)&=\alpha_1+\alpha_2,\\
s_{\alpha_2}(\alpha_1)&=\alpha_1+2\alpha_2,
&s_{\alpha_2}(\alpha_2)&=-\alpha_2.
\end{aligned}}
$$
