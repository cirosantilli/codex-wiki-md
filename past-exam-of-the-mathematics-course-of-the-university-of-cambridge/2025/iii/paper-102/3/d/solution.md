<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [highest root](../../../../../../highest-root.md) is

$$
\boxed{\beta=\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2
=\alpha_1+2\alpha_2}.
$$

Since $\beta$ is long and the $\varepsilon_i$ are orthonormal, $\beta^\vee=\beta$. The [coroot](../../../../../../coroot.md) pairing gives

$$
\boxed{\langle\alpha_1,\beta^\vee\rangle=0,
\qquad
\langle\alpha_2,\beta^\vee\rangle=1}.
$$
