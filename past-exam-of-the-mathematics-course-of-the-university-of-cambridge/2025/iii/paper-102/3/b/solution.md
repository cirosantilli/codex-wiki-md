↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose

$$
\boxed{\alpha_1=\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2,\qquad
\alpha_2=\varepsilon_2}.
$$

Here $\alpha_1$ is long and $\alpha_2$ is short. The [Dynkin diagram](../../../../../../dynkin-diagram.md) consists of two vertices joined by a double edge, with its arrow pointing from $\alpha_1$ toward the shorter root $\alpha_2$:

$$
\boxed{\alpha_1\Longrightarrow\alpha_2.}
$$
