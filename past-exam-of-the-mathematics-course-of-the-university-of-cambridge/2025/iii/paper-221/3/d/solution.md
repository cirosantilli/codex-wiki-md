<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Each factor $f_{j\mid\operatorname{pa}(j)}$ depends only on node $j$ and its parents. Those vertices form a clique in the [moral graph](../../../../../../moral-graph.md), because moralization joins every pair of parents and removes arrow directions. The positive joint density therefore factors into clique potentials of the moral graph. The [Hammersley-Clifford theorem](../../../../../../hammersley-clifford-theorem.md) then implies the global Markov property for that undirected graph.
