<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Given a finite coloring $c:\mathbb N\to[k]$, color each three-element subset $\{a<b<d\}$ by the color of

$$
a+2b+3d.
$$

[Ramsey's theorem](../../../../../../../ramsey-s-theorem.md) gives an infinite set $M$ on whose triples this induced coloring is constant. Enumerating $M$ increasingly as $x_1<x_2<\cdots$ gives a strictly increasing sequence for which every $x_i+2x_j+3x_k$, $i<j<k$, has the same color.
