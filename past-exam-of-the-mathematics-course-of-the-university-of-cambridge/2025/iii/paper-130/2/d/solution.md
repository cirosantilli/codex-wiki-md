↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The finite-witness version of part c can be iterated by the standard product argument: color one finite witness by the entire color pattern it induces on a second witness, find a diagonal triple with one pattern, and then find a diagonal triple inside that common pattern. This is the special four-point case of the [Gallai theorem for an integer lattice](../../../../../../gallai-theorem-for-an-integer-lattice.md). Apply that theorem to

$$
S=\{(0,0),(1,1),(2,2),(2,0)\}\subseteq\mathbb N^2.
$$

A monochromatic homothetic copy $(a,b)+rS$ is exactly

$$
\{(a,b),(a+r,b+r),(a+2r,b+2r),(a+2r,b)\},
$$

as required.
