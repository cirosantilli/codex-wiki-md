<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The uniform estimate gives a subsequence

$$
u_m\rightharpoonup u
\quad\hbox{weakly in }\widetilde V,
\qquad
\|u\|\leq R.
$$

The compact periodic embedding $\widetilde V\Subset\widetilde H$ improves this to

$$
u_m\to u
\quad\hbox{strongly in }\widetilde H.
$$

One may also take strong convergence in $L^3$ by the [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](../../../../../../../rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md). Combining this with weak convergence of the gradients and using the skew form when derivatives must be moved to a test function gives

$$
\widetilde B(u_m,u_m)
\rightharpoonup\widetilde B(u,u)
\quad\hbox{in }\widetilde V'.
$$

For any test function in a fixed finite-dimensional subspace, the Galerkin equation therefore passes to the limit. Density then gives

$$
\boxed{
\nu\widetilde Au+\widetilde B(u,u)=f
\quad\hbox{in }\widetilde V',
\qquad
\|u\|\leq R}.
$$

This is a weak solution of the [steady skew-symmetrized Navier-Stokes equation](../../../../../../../steady-skew-symmetrized-navier-stokes-equation.md).
