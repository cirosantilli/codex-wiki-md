↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The energy equality shows that

$$
t\longmapsto |u(t)|^2
=|u_0|^2-2\nu\int_0^t\|u(\tau)\|^2\,d\tau
$$

is continuous. Part i gives weak continuity in $H$. In a Hilbert space, weak convergence together with convergence of norms implies strong convergence. Applying this whenever $t_n\to t$ proves the [strong continuity from weak continuity and an energy equality](../../../../../../../strong-continuity-from-weak-continuity-and-an-energy-equality.md):

$$
\boxed{u\in C([0,T];H)}.
$$
