<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expand $P_Nw$ in the orthonormal [Stokes operator](../../../../../../../stokes-operator.md) eigenbasis:

$$
P_Nw=\sum_{k=1}^Nw_k\phi_k.
$$

The $H$ and $V$ norms satisfy

$$
|P_Nw|^2=\sum_{k=1}^N|w_k|^2,
\qquad
\|P_Nw\|^2=(AP_Nw,P_Nw)
=\sum_{k=1}^N\lambda_k|w_k|^2.
$$

Since $\lambda_k\leq\lambda_N$ on the projected space,

$$
\boxed{\|P_Nw\|\leq\lambda_N^{1/2}|P_Nw|}.
$$

This is the basic inverse estimate for a [spectral projection of the Stokes operator](../../../../../../../spectral-projection-of-the-stokes-operator.md).
