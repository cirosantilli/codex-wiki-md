<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $\Re s>1$, absolute convergence permits a shift of the complex variable:

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\Lambda(n)n^{iu}}{n^s}
=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n^{s-iu}}
=-\frac{\zeta'(s-iu)}{\zeta(s-iu)}.
$$
