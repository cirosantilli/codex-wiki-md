For arithmetic functions $f,g$ bounded in modulus by one, their [pretentious distance](../../../../../../pretentious-distance.md) up to $x$ is defined by

$$
\boxed{\mathbb D(f,g;x)^2
=\sum_{p\leq x}\frac{1-\Re(f(p)\overline{g(p)})}{p}.}
$$
