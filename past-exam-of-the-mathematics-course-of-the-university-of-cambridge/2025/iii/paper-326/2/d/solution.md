<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The compact self-adjoint operator $K$ has an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of eigenvectors $e_n$. Part (c) gives $\lambda_n>0$ and $\sum_n\lambda_n<\infty$, so $K$ is positive and trace class, exactly the required condition for a [covariance operator of a Gaussian measure](../../../../../../covariance-operator-of-a-gaussian-measure.md) on a Hilbert space.

For independent standard normal variables $\xi_n$, define the [Hilbert-space Gaussian series](../../../../../../hilbert-space-gaussian-series.md)

$$
\boxed{
U=m+\sum_{n=1}^\infty\sqrt{\lambda_n}\,\xi_ne_n}.
$$

Because $\mathbb E\|U-m\|^2=\sum_n\lambda_n<\infty$, the series converges in $L^2(\Omega;X)$ and almost surely. Its law is the [Gaussian measure](../../../../../../gaussian-measure.md) $N(m,K)$.
