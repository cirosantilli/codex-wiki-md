<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The operator $A^*A$ is positive, self-adjoint, and compact. Because $\operatorname{ran}A$ is infinite dimensional, the [spectral theorem for compact Hermitian operators](../../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-compact-hermitian-operators.md) gives positive spectral values tending to zero. Even if $\ker A=\{0\}$, zero remains in the spectrum as a limit point. Hence for every $\tau$,

$$
\|I-\tau A^*A\|
=\sup_{\lambda\in\sigma(A^*A)}|1-\tau\lambda|
\geq1.
$$

The strict inequality $\|I-\tau K\|<1$ needed for the operator-norm [Neumann series](../../../../../../neumann-series.md) is impossible, so formula (3) cannot be applied directly to invert $A^*A$.
