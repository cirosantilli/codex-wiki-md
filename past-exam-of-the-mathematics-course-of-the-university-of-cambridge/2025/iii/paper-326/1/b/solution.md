<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An element $u^\dagger$ is a $J$-minimizing solution when

$$
Au^\dagger=f,
\qquad
J(u^\dagger)=\inf\{J(u):Au=f\}.
$$

It satisfies the [source condition in variational regularization](../../../../../../source-condition-in-variational-regularization.md) when there is a $w^\dagger\in Y^*$ such that

$$
\boxed{p^\dagger=A^*w^\dagger\in\partial J(u^\dagger)},
$$

where $\partial J(u^\dagger)$ is the [subdifferential](../../../../../../subdifferential.md) of the [convex functional](../../../../../../convex-function.md) $J$.
