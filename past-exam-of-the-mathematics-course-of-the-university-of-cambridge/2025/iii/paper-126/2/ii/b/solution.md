<h1 id="2/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A smooth plane curve of degree $m$ has genus

$$
g=\frac{(m-1)(m-2)}2.
$$

The line bundle $\mathcal O_X(D)(t)$ has degree $d+mt$. The [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{P(X,\mathcal O_X(D),t)
=\chi(\mathcal O_X(D)(t))
=mt+d+1-g
=mt+d+1-\frac{(m-1)(m-2)}2.}
$$
