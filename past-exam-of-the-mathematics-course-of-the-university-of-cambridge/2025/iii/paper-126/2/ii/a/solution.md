<h1 id="2/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For every integer $t$,

$$
P(\mathbb P_k^2,\mathcal O,t)=\chi(\mathbb P_k^2,\mathcal O(t))
=\binom{t+2}{2}
=\frac{t^2+3t+2}{2}.
$$

For $t\geq0$ this is the dimension of the homogeneous polynomials of degree $t$, since the higher cohomology vanishes; polynomiality then identifies the [Hilbert polynomial](../../../../../../../hilbert-polynomial.md).
