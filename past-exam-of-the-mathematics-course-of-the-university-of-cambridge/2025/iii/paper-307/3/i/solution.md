<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Both terms in $B_\mu=W_\mu-\frac14\bar Q\bar\sigma_\mu Q$ commute with momentum. For the first,

$$
[W_\mu,P_\rho]=\frac12\epsilon_{\mu\nu\lambda\sigma}
[M^{\nu\lambda},P_\rho]P^\sigma=0,
$$

because the two resulting products of momenta are symmetric in indices contracted with the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md). For the second, $[P_\rho,Q]=[P_\rho,\bar Q]=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[B_\mu,P_\rho]=0}.
$$
