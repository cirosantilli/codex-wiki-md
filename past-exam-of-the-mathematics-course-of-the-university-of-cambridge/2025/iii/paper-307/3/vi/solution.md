<h1 id="3/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The antisymmetric $C_{\mu\nu}$ transforms as a [Lorentz tensor](../../../../../../lorentz-tensor.md), so its complete contraction

$$
\widetilde C_2=C_{\mu\nu}C^{\mu\nu}
$$

is a [Lorentz scalar](../../../../../../lorentz-scalar.md). Hence

$$
[M^{\rho\sigma},\widetilde C_2]=0.
$$

Parts ii and v give $[P_\rho,\widetilde C_2]=[Q_\alpha,\widetilde C_2]=[\bar Q_{\dot\alpha},\widetilde C_2]=0$. It therefore commutes with every generator of the [Super-Poincaré algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md) and is a [Casimir element](../../../../../../casimir-element.md), called the [superspin Casimir](../../../../../../superspin-casimir.md).
