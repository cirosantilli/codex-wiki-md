<h1 id="3/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector](../../../../../../pauli-lubanski-pseudovector.md) and the spinor Lorentz transformation give

$$
[W_\mu,Q_\alpha]=i(\sigma_{\mu\nu})_\alpha{}^\beta P^\nu Q_\beta.
$$

Combining this with part iii and the [Pauli matrix](../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md) identity $\sigma^\nu\bar\sigma_\mu=\eta^\nu{}_\mu\mathbf1+2i\sigma^\nu{}_\mu$ cancels the term containing $\sigma_{\mu\nu}P^\nu$. In these conventions,

$$
\boxed{[B_\mu,Q_\alpha]=-\frac12P_\mu Q_\alpha}.
$$

Simultaneously reversing the convention for $W_\mu$ reverses the final sign, but the proportionality to $P_\mu Q_\alpha$ is invariant and is what the next part needs.
