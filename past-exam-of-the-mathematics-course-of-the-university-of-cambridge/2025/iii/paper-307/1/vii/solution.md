<h1 id="1/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

The relation in part vi gives

$$
\Phi'{}^\dagger e^{2V'}\Phi'
=\Phi^\dagger e^{2i\Lambda^\dagger}
e^{-2i\Lambda^\dagger}e^{2V}e^{2i\Lambda}e^{-2i\Lambda}\Phi
=\Phi^\dagger e^{2V}\Phi,
$$

so the full-superspace term has [gauge invariance](../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md). The [chiral field-strength superfield](../../../../../../chiral-field-strength-superfield.md) transforms covariantly,

$$
W_\alpha' =e^{-2i\Lambda}W_\alpha e^{2i\Lambda}.
$$

Cyclicity of the [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) then gives $\operatorname{Tr}(W'{}^\alpha W'_\alpha)=\operatorname{Tr}(W^\alpha W_\alpha)$. Both superspace integrals, and hence the entire Lagrangian density, are invariant.
