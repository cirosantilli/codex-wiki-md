↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [vector superfield](../../../../../../vector-superfield.md) is a real [superfield](../../../../../../superfield.md),

$$
V=V^\dagger.
$$

For a non-Abelian gauge theory it is also Lie-algebra valued, $V=V^aT_a$. The reality condition permits the component expansion to be reduced to [Wess-Zumino gauge](../../../../../../wess-zumino-gauge.md), where the physical fields are a gauge field and gaugino together with a real auxiliary field.
