The [Boussinesq approximation](../../../../../../boussinesq-approximation.md) uses a constant reference density in inertia and continuity while retaining the small temperature-dependent density deficit in buoyancy. Here $(\rho_0-\rho)/\rho_0=(T-T_0)/T_0$, so the plume remains incompressible but rises under $g(T-T_0)/T_0$.

The [Batchelor entrainment hypothesis](../../../../../../batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md) sets the ambient inflow speed at the exposed plume edge to $E W$, where $E$ is a dimensionless entrainment coefficient. For this one-sided [heated wall plume](../../../../../../heated-wall-plume.md), it gives $dQ/dz=EW$.
