<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $H=\operatorname{Stab}_F(x)$. For $g,h\in G$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_x(g)=f_x(h)
&\iff F(g,x)=F(h,x)\\
&\iff F(h^{-1}g,x)=x\\
&\iff h^{-1}g\in H\\
&\iff g\in hH.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Thus $f_x$ is constant on every left [coset](../../../../../../../coset.md) of $H$ and takes different values on different cosets. Its oracle is therefore an oracle for the [hidden subgroup problem](../../../../../../../hidden-subgroup-problem.md), and the hidden subgroup is precisely the [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) $H$.**
