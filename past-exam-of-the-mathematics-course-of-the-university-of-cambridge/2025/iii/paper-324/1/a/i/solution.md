<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $H=\operatorname{Stab}_F(x)$. The identity belongs to $H$ because $F(e,x)=x$. If $g,h\in H$, the [group action](../../../../../../../group-action.md) law gives

$$
F(gh,x)=F(g,F(h,x))=F(g,x)=x,
$$

so $gh\in H$. Finally, if $g\in H$, then

$$
x=F(e,x)=F(g^{-1}g,x)=F(g^{-1},F(g,x))=F(g^{-1},x),
$$

so $g^{-1}\in H$. The [subgroup](../../../../../../../subgroup.md) criterion therefore proves that $\operatorname{Stab}_F(x)$ is the [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $x$.
