<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $\gcd(x,N)=1$, multiplication by $x$ modulo $N$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../../bijection.md) of $\mathbb Z_N$, with inverse multiplication by the [modular inverse](../../../../../../../modular-multiplicative-inverse.md) $x^{-1}$. Hence $U_x$ permutes the standard orthonormal basis. It preserves all inner products and satisfies

$$
U_x^\dagger=U_{x^{-1}},
\qquad
U_x^\dagger U_x=I,
$$

so $U_x$ is a [unitary operator](../../../../../../../unitary-operator.md).
