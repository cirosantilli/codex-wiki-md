<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an Earth-mass planet with a nitrogen-carbon-dioxide-water atmosphere, commonly quoted conservative Solar boundaries are approximately

$$
\boxed{a_{\rm in}\simeq0.95\,{\rm AU}},
\qquad
\boxed{a_{\rm out}\simeq1.67\,{\rm AU}}.
$$

The inner edge is governed by moist-greenhouse water loss and ultimately the [runaway greenhouse](../../../../../../runaway-greenhouse.md). The outer edge is the [maximum greenhouse outer habitable-zone limit](../../../../../../maximum-greenhouse-outer-habitable-zone-limit.md), where additional carbon dioxide condenses or increases reflected light faster than it increases infrared trapping. Optimistic empirical boundaries, based on recent Venus and early Mars, are broader, roughly $0.75$ to $1.77\,{\rm AU}$. The values depend on clouds, planetary mass, atmospheric inventory, and climate assumptions.
