<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume a spherical blackbody planet, uniform reradiation, constant [Bond albedo](../../../../../../bond-albedo.md) $A_B$, and negligible internal luminosity. Radiative equilibrium gives

$$
\sigma T_{\rm eq}^4
=\frac{(1-A_B)L_s}{16\pi a_H^2}.
$$

With $L_s=KM_s^\beta$,

$$
\boxed{a_H(M_s,T)=
\left[\frac{(1-A_B)K}{16\pi\sigma T^4}\right]^{1/2}
M_s^{\beta/2}}.
$$

The inner and outer [circumstellar habitable zone](../../../../../../circumstellar-habitable-zone.md) boundaries are obtained by setting $T=T_{\max}$ and $T=T_{\min}$ respectively:

$$
\boxed{a_{\rm in}\propto T_{\max}^{-2}M_s^{\beta/2}},
\qquad
\boxed{a_{\rm out}\propto T_{\min}^{-2}M_s^{\beta/2}}.
$$

Real boundaries require wavelength-dependent albedo, clouds, greenhouse effects, and the stellar spectrum, which this equilibrium-temperature model neglects.
