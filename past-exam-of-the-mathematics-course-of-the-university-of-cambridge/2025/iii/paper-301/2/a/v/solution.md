<h1 id="2/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Positivity selects

$$
\widetilde b=-\widetilde a,
$$

for which the kinetic terms combine, up to normalization and a total derivative, into the [Proca field](../../../../../../../proca-field.md) form $-F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}/4$. Taking the divergence of the equation of motion gives

$$
(\widetilde a+\widetilde b)\Box(\partial\cdot V)
+m^2\partial\cdot V=0.
$$

For the selected relation and $m\ne0$, this implies the [transverse vector field](../../../../../../../transverse-vector-field.md) condition $\partial_\mu V^\mu=0$. The remaining three massive polarizations have positive on-shell energy when $\widetilde a>0$.
