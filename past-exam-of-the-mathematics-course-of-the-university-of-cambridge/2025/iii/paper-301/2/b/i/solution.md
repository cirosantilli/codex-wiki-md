<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $\widetilde c=\widetilde a+\widetilde b$. Substituting the [transverse-longitudinal decomposition](../../../../../../../transverse-longitudinal-decomposition.md) $V_\mu=A_\mu+\partial_\mu\pi$ and integrating cross terms by parts gives

$$
\mathcal L\simeq
\widetilde a A_\mu\Box A^\mu+m^2A_\mu A^\mu
-\widetilde c(\Box\pi)^2+m^2\partial_\mu\pi\partial^\mu\pi.
$$

The equations are

$$
(\widetilde a\Box+m^2)A_\mu=0,
\qquad
\Box(\widetilde c\Box+m^2)\pi=0,
$$

together with $\partial_\mu A^\mu=0$.
