<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Iterating the Schwinger-Dyson equations once, the leading connected correlator is one scalar propagator and two Dirac propagators meeting at one [Yukawa interaction](../../../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) vertex. Amputation gives

$$
\langle p,s;q,s'\,\mathrm{out}|k\,\mathrm{in}\rangle_c
=(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(k-p-q)
\left[-i\lambda\bar u^s(p)v^{s'}(q)\right].
$$

**Thus the invariant amplitude, in the convention $S_{fi}=i(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}\mathcal M_{fi}$, is $\mathcal M_{s,s'}=-\lambda\bar u^s(p)v^{s'}(q)$.**
