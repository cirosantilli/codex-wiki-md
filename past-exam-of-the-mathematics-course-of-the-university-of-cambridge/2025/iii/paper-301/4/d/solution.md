<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

On shell, the contact vertex is $4i\lambda^2m^2$. At a cubic vertex with two external on-shell legs and internal momentum $k$, the rule reduces to $2i\lambda(k^2-m^2)$. The propagator cancels one such factor, so the three exchange diagrams sum to

$$
-4i\lambda^2[(s-m^2)+(t-m^2)+(u-m^2)].
$$

For equal-mass two-to-two scattering, $s+t+u=4m^2$, and the exchange sum is $-4i\lambda^2m^2$. It cancels the contact diagram exactly. Thus the connected on-shell amplitude vanishes, as required by the [equivalence theorem for field redefinitions](../../../../../../equivalence-theorem-for-field-redefinitions.md): an invertible local field redefinition cannot turn a free theory into a physically interacting one.
