<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For this metric, $\sqrt{-g}=a^2$, while $g^{\eta\eta}=-a^{-2}$, $g^{zz}=a^{-2}$ and $g^{ij}=\delta^{ij}$. The massless [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md) is consequently

$$
\Box\phi
=\frac1{a^2}(-\partial_\eta^2+\partial_z^2)\phi
+\delta^{ij}\partial_i\partial_j\phi=0.
$$

Insert the spatial [Fourier transform](../../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) given in the question. Each [wavenumber](../../../../../../../wavenumber.md) mode then satisfies

$$
\boxed{\Phi_{k_z\mathbf k}''
+\omega_{k_z\mathbf k}^2(\eta)\Phi_{k_z\mathbf k}=0},
\qquad
\boxed{\omega_{k_z\mathbf k}^2(\eta)=k_z^2+a(\eta)^2|\mathbf k|^2}.
$$

**Thus every field mode is a [simple harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) with a time-dependent [angular frequency](../../../../../../../angular-frequency.md).**
