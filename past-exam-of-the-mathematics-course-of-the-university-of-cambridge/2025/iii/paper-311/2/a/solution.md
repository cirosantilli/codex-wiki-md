<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose an auxiliary null vector $N^a$ with $U\mathbin\cdot N=-1$. The [screen-space projector](../../../../../../screen-space-projector.md) is

$$
P^a{}_b=\delta^a_b+U^aN_b+N^aU_b,
$$

and the [optical tensor](../../../../../../optical-tensor.md) is $\widehat B_{ab}=P_a{}^cP_b{}^dB_{cd}$ for $B_{ab}=\nabla_bU_a$. Because the screen is two-dimensional, its irreducible decomposition is

$$
\widehat B_{ab}=\frac12\theta P_{ab}+\widehat\sigma_{ab}+\widehat\omega_{ab},
$$

where

$$
\boxed{\theta=P^{ab}\widehat B_{ab}},
\qquad
\boxed{\widehat\sigma_{ab}=\widehat B_{(ab)}-\frac12\theta P_{ab}},
\qquad
\boxed{\widehat\omega_{ab}=\widehat B_{[ab]}}.
$$

These are respectively the [null expansion](../../../../../../null-expansion.md), [null shear](../../../../../../null-shear.md), and [null twist](../../../../../../null-twist.md), also called rotation.
