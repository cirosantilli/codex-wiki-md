<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a spacelike two-dimensional cross-section $S$ of the [null hypersurface](../../../../../../../null-hypersurface.md) $\mathcal N$ and coordinates $y^i$ on $S$. Extend $y^i$ along the null generators, and choose their parameter $\lambda$ to be affine, so $U=\partial_\lambda$ on $\mathcal N$. At every point choose the other null normal $L$ with $g(U,L)=1$, shoot out the affinely parametrized null geodesic with tangent $L$, and call its affine parameter $r$. Transport $(\lambda,y^i)$ along those transverse geodesics.

This construction gives [Gaussian null coordinates](../../../../../../../gaussian-null-coordinates.md). The hypersurface is $r=0$, and the coordinate conditions imply

$$
g_{rr}=g_{ri}=0,
\qquad
g_{r\lambda}=1,
\qquad
g_{\lambda\lambda}|_{r=0}=g_{\lambda i}|_{r=0}=0.
$$

Because $\lambda$ is affine on the generators, $\partial_rg_{\lambda\lambda}|_{r=0}=0$. Smoothness then factors the remaining components as $g_{\lambda\lambda}=r^2F$ and $g_{\lambda i}=rh_i$, giving

$$
\boxed{ds^2=2\,dr\,d\lambda+r^2F\,d\lambda^2+2rh_i\,d\lambda\,dy^i+h_{ij}\,dy^i\,dy^j}.
$$
