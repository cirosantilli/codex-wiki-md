<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Using $v=t+r_\star$ puts the [black string](../../../../../../black-string.md) metric into regular ingoing form,

$$
ds^2=-f\,dv^2+2\,dv\,dr+r^2d\Omega_3^2+dz^2.
$$

Its inverse metric gives

$$
\nabla^ar=(\partial_v)^a+f(\partial_r)^a,
\qquad
(\nabla r)^2=f.
$$

For $r<r_+$, $f<0$, so $\nabla r$ is timelike. It is future-directed by continuity from the future horizon, where it equals the future generator $\partial_v$. If $X$ is any future-directed causal tangent, then

$$
X(r)=g(X,\nabla r)<0.
$$

Thus $r$ decreases strictly along every future-directed causal curve in the interior. No such curve can cross back through $r=r_+$ or reach [future null infinity](../../../../../../future-null-infinity.md). The region $r<r_+$ is consequently outside the causal past of future null infinity and is part of the [black hole](../../../../../../black-hole.md) region.
