<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let independent increments $D_n$ equal $-1$ with probability $1-2^{-n}$ and $2^n-1$ with probability $2^{-n}$. Then $\mathbb ED_n=0$, so $X_n=\sum_{j=1}^nD_j$ is a martingale. Since $\sum_n2^{-n}<\infty$, the [Borel-Cantelli lemmas](../../../../../../../borel-cantelli-lemmas.md) say that only finitely many positive jumps occur almost surely. Thereafter every increment is $-1$, and hence $X_n\to-\infty$ almost surely.
