<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Conditional expectation is the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) from $L^2(\mathcal F)$ onto $L^2(\mathcal G)$. The difference $X-\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ is orthogonal to every $\mathcal G$-measurable square-integrable variable, including $\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G]-\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal H]$. The [Pythagorean theorem in an inner-product space](../../../../../../pythagorean-theorem-in-an-inner-product-space.md) applied to

$$
X-\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal H]
=(X-\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G])
+(\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G]-\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal H])
$$

gives the identity.
