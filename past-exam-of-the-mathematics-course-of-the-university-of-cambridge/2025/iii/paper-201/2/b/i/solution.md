<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The function $z\mapsto\log|z|$ is [harmonic](../../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) on the annulus $\varepsilon<|z|<R$. Hence $\log|B_{t\wedge\tau_\varepsilon\wedge\tau_R}|$ is a bounded martingale by [Itô formula](../../../../../../../ito-s-lemma.md). Optional stopping gives

$$
\log|x|=p\log\varepsilon+(1-p)\log R,
\qquad p=\mathbb P_x(\tau_\varepsilon<\tau_R).
$$

Solving yields

$$
\boxed{p=\frac{\log R-\log|x|}{\log R-\log\varepsilon}.}
$$
