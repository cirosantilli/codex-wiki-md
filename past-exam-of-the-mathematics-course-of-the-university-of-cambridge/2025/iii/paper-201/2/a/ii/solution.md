<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The process $Y_t=B_{T-t}-B_T$ is centered Gaussian and continuous. Its covariance is

$$
\mathbb E[Y_sY_t]=\min(s,t),
$$

as follows by expanding Brownian covariances, or by reading its increments backwards. The [Gaussian-process characterization of Brownian motion](../../../../../../../gaussian-process-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md) therefore shows that $(Y_t)_{0\leq t\leq T}$ has the same law as $(B_t)_{0\leq t\leq T}$.
