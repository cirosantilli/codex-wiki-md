<h1 id="6/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Almost-sure convergence plus [uniform integrability](../../../../../../../uniform-integrability.md) gives $X_n\to X$ in $L^1$. Conditional expectation is an $L^1$ contraction, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E|\mathbb E[X_n\mid\mathcal G]-\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G]|
\leq\mathbb E|X_n-X|\to0.}
$$
