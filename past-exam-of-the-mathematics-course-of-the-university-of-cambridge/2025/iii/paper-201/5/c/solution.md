<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Differentiability gives $\varphi(u)=1+iau+o(u)$ at zero. By the [characteristic function of a sum of independent variables](../../../../../../characteristic-function-of-a-sum-of-independent-variables.md),

$$
\mathbb E e^{itS_n/n}=\varphi(t/n)^n\longrightarrow e^{iat},
$$

the characteristic function of the constant $a$. The [Lévy continuity theorem](../../../../../../levy-continuity-theorem.md) yields $S_n/n\xrightarrow d a$, and convergence in distribution to a constant is equivalent to [convergence in probability](../../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md).
