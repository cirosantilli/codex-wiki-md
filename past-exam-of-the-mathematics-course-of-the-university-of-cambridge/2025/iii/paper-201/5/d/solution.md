<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A family $(\mu_n)$ of probability measures on a metric space is tight when for every $\eta>0$ there is a compact set $K$ such that $\sup_n\mu_n(K^c)<\eta$.
