↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Writing $c_i(t)=\langle\mathbf1_{[0,t]},f_i\rangle_{L^2}$ gives $X_n(t)=\sum_{i=1}^n\alpha_ic_i(t)$, hence

$$
X_n(t)\sim N\left(0,\sum_{i=1}^nc_i(t)^2\right).
$$

By [Parseval identity](../../../../../../../parseval-identity.md), the variance tends to $\lVert\mathbf1_{[0,t]}\rVert_2^2=t$. Thus $X_n(t)\xrightarrow dN(0,t)$.
