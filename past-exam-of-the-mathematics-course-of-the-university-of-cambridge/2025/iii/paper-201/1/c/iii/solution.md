<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For real $u_1,\ldots,u_j$, the preceding series construction gives

$$
\sum_ru_rX(t_r)
=\sum_{i\geq1}\alpha_i\left\langle\sum_ru_r\mathbf1_{[0,t_r]},f_i\right\rangle.
$$

It is centered Gaussian, and [Parseval identity](../../../../../../../parseval-identity.md) makes its variance

$$
\left\lVert\sum_ru_r\mathbf1_{[0,t_r]}\right\rVert_2^2
=\sum_{r,s}u_ru_s\min(t_r,t_s).
$$

This is the variance of $\sum_ru_rB_{t_r}$. The [Cramér–Wold theorem](../../../../../../../cramer-wold-theorem.md) proves equality of the two joint distributions.
