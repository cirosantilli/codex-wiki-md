<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $x\in\mathfrak m$ and choose any positive integer $n$ coprime to the residue characteristic. For

$$
f(Y)=Y^n-(1+x)
$$

one has $f(1)\equiv0\pmod{\mathfrak m}$ and $f'(1)=n\not\equiv0\pmod{\mathfrak m}$. The simple-root form of [Hensel lemma](../../../../../../../hensel-s-lemma.md) produces $y\in K$ with $y^n=1+x$. Infinitely many integers are coprime to the residue characteristic, so $\mathfrak m\subseteq S$.
