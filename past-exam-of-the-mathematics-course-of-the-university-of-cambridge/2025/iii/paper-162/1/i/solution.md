<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At the generic point $\eta_V$ of $V$, the local ring $R=\mathcal O_{W,\eta_V}$ is a one-dimensional Noetherian local domain. Write $r=a/b$ with nonzero $a,b\in R$ and define the [order of vanishing](../../../../../../order-of-vanishing.md)

$$
\operatorname{ord}_V(r)=\operatorname{length}_R(R/(a))-\operatorname{length}_R(R/(b)).
$$

This is independent of the representation. The associated principal $k$-cycle is

$$
[\operatorname{div}(r)]=\sum_{\substack{V\subseteq W\\\dim V=k}}\operatorname{ord}_V(r)[V]\in Z_k(X),
$$

where only finitely many terms are nonzero.

The subgroup $\operatorname{Rat}_k(X)\subseteq Z_k(X)$ is generated by these cycles as $W$ ranges over integral $(k+1)$-dimensional subvarieties and $r$ over $R(W)^\times$. Two $k$-cycles are [rationally equivalent](../../../../../../rational-equivalence.md) when their difference lies in this subgroup, and the [Chow group](../../../../../../chow-group.md) is

$$
\boxed{A_k(X)=Z_k(X)/\operatorname{Rat}_k(X).}
$$
