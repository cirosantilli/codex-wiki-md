<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Homotopy invariance gives

$$
A_k(\mathbb A^n)=
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z[\mathbb A^n],&k=n,\\
0,&k\ne n,
\end{cases}
$$

or, in cohomological grading, $A^*(\mathbb A^n)=\mathbb Z$ concentrated in degree zero.

Apply the localization sequence successively to $X_{i-1}\subseteq X_i$. Since the Chow group of each stratum is freely generated in the dimensions of its affine components, induction shows that $A_*(X_i)$ is generated by the closures of the cells appearing up to stage $i$. Hence $A_*(X)$ is generated by the classes $[V_{i,j}]$ of a [cellular decomposition of a scheme](../../../../../../cellular-decomposition-of-a-scheme.md).

The standard cells of $\mathbb P^r$ have one affine space in each dimension $0,\ldots,r$. Consequently

$$
A_k(\mathbb P^r)\cong\mathbb Z
$$

for $0\leq k\leq r$, generated by a linear $\mathbb P^k$, and zero otherwise. Equivalently, the [Chow ring of projective space](../../../../../../chow-ring-of-projective-space.md) is $\mathbb Z[H]/(H^{r+1})$.
