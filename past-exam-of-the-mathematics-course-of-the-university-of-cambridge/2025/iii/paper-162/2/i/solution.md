↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\zeta=c_1(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P(E)}(1))$. The [projective bundle formula for Chow groups](../../../../../../projective-bundle-formula-for-chow-groups.md) is the map

$$
\Theta_E:\bigoplus_{i=0}^{e}A_{k-e+i}(X)\longrightarrow A_k(\mathbb P(E)),
\qquad
(\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_e)\longmapsto
\sum_{i=0}^{e}\zeta^i\cap p^*\alpha_i.
$$

Flat pullback raises dimension by $e$, and intersection with $\zeta^i$ lowers it by $i$, so every summand has dimension $k$.
