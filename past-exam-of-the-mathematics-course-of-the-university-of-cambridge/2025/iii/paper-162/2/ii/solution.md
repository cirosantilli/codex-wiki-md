<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Compactify the total space of $E$ as $\overline E=\mathbb P(E\oplus\mathcal O_X)$; its hyperplane at infinity is $\mathbb P(E)$ and its open complement is $E$. Under the projective bundle formula for $\overline E$, the pushforward from that hyperplane spans the summands containing positive powers of $\zeta$. The localization quotient therefore retains the zeroth summand

$$
p^*A_k(X)\subseteq A_{k+e+1}(\overline E),
$$

whose restriction to $E$ is precisely the vector-bundle pullback $\pi^*:A_k(X)\to A_{k+e+1}(E)$. It survives injectively. In fact this proves the stronger [homotopy invariance of Chow groups](../../../../../../homotopy-invariance-of-chow-groups.md): $\pi^*$ is an isomorphism.
