<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

From the tautological exact sequence and the Whitney formula in the [Chern class](../../../../../../chern-class.md) formalism,

$$
c(\xi)=\frac{c(p^*E)}{c(\mathcal O(-1))}
=p^*c(E)(1+\zeta+\zeta^2+\cdots).
$$

Thus $c_i(\xi)$ is a sum of terms $\zeta^j p^*c_{i-j}(E)$ with $0\leq j\leq i$. The projective-bundle pushforward satisfies

$$
p_*(\zeta^j\cap p^*\gamma)=0\quad(j<e),
\qquad
p_*(\zeta^e\cap p^*\gamma)=\gamma.
$$

The projection formula now makes every term vanish when $i<e$. For $i=e$, only the term $\zeta^e p^*\alpha$ survives, giving

$$
p_*(c_i(\xi)\cap p^*\alpha)=
\begin{cases}
\alpha,&i=e,\\
0,&i<e.
\end{cases}
$$
