The [Euler sequence](../../../../../../euler-sequence.md) on $\mathbb P^m$ is

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^m}
\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^m}(1)^{\oplus(m+1)}
\longrightarrow T\mathbb P^m\longrightarrow0.
$$

The Whitney product formula for the [Total Chern class](../../../../../../total-chern-class.md) therefore gives

$$
c(T\mathbb P^m)=\frac{(1+H)^{m+1}}1=(1+H)^{m+1}
$$

in $A^*(\mathbb P^m)=\mathbb Z[H]/(H^{m+1})$.
