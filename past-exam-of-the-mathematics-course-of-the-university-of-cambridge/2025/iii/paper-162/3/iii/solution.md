<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $N=N_{X/\mathbb P^m}$ be the [normal bundle](../../../../../../normal-bundle.md), of rank $m-n$. Since $TX$ is trivial, its exact sequence and the Euler-sequence calculation give

$$
c(N)=c(T\mathbb P^m|_X)=(1+H)^{m+1}.
$$

If $m<2n$, put $i=m-n+1$, so $1\leq i\leq n$. Since $i$ exceeds the rank of $N$, $c_i(N)=0$, yet the formula gives

$$
0=c_i(N)=\binom{m+1}{i}H^i.
$$

Intersecting with $H^{n-i}$ and integrating over $X$ gives

$$
0=\binom{m+1}{i}\deg X,
$$

contradicting positivity of the degree. Hence $m\geq2n$.
