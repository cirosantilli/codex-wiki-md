<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Repeated measurements from one person can share persistent unobserved spending tendencies, violating model1's independent-error assumption and shifting that person's baseline. Model2 represents this [clustered data](../../../../../../clustered-data.md) through a common random intercept $b_i$, which induces within-person covariance $\operatorname{Cov}(Y_{ij},Y_{ik}\mid X)=\sigma^2$ for $j\ne k$.
