<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The residual deviance of a Gaussian GLM is its [residual sum of squares](../../../../../../residual-sum-of-squares.md), $154.30$. Thus the empirical training error for squared-error loss is

$$
\frac1{100}\sum_{i,j}(Y_{ij}-\widehat Y_{ij})^2
=\frac{154.30}{100}=1.543.
$$

If training error is defined as the unnormalized total loss, its value is $154.30$.
