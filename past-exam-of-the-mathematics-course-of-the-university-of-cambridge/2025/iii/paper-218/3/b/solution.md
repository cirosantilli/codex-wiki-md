↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The $L$-nearest-neighbour classifier finds the $L$ training predictors nearest to $x$ and returns the majority class among their labels. Increasing $L$ averages more labels and reduces [variance of an estimator](../../../../../../variance-of-an-estimator.md), but uses observations farther from $x$ and therefore increases [bias of an estimator](../../../../../../bias-of-an-estimator.md); decreasing $L$ reverses this [bias-variance tradeoff](../../../../../../bias-variance-tradeoff.md).
