The second fit is a [Quasi-Poisson regression](../../../../../../quasi-poisson-regression.md). It retains the [logarithmic link function](../../../../../../logarithmic-link-function.md) and mean model

$$
\log\mu_i=\beta_0+\beta_1x_{i1}+\beta_2x_{i2},
$$

but assumes only $\operatorname{Var}(Y_i)=\phi\mu_i$ for an unknown [dispersion parameter](../../../../../../dispersion-parameter.md) $\phi$, rather than a complete [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md).
