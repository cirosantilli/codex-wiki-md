↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Part c gives $x+B(\Gamma,\alpha)\subseteq A+A+A$ with $d=|\Gamma|\leq2\alpha^{-3}$. By the [arithmetic progression in a cyclic Bohr set](../../../../../../arithmetic-progression-in-a-cyclic-bohr-set.md), $B(\Gamma,\alpha)$ contains a centered progression of length at least

$$
\frac18\alpha N^{1/d}
\geq\frac18\alpha N^{\alpha^3/2}.
$$

Its translate by $x$ is the required [arithmetic progression](../../../../../../arithmetic-progression.md) in $A+A+A$.
