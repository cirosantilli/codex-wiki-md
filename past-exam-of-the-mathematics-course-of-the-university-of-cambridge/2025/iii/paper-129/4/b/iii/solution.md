<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) successively in the three shift variables to the correlation with the [quadratic phase](../../../../../../../quadratic-phase.md) $h$. At the third step the third additive derivative of its phase vanishes, leaving

$$
|\langle f,h\rangle|^8\leq\|f\|_{U^3}^8.
$$

Thus $|\langle f,h\rangle|\geq\delta$ implies

$$
\boxed{\|f\|_{U^3}\geq\delta.}
$$
