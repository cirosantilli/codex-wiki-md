<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $h(x)=e_p(q(x))$, the product in the $U^3$ cube average is

$$
e_p\!\left(
\sum_{\epsilon\in\{0,1\}^3}(-1)^{|\epsilon|}
q(x+\epsilon\mathbin\cdot h)
\right).
$$

The expression in parentheses is the third additive derivative of the [quadratic form](../../../../../../../quadratic-form.md) $q$, so it vanishes. Every cube contributes one and therefore the [quadratic phase](../../../../../../../quadratic-phase.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{\|h\|_{U^3}=1.}
$$
