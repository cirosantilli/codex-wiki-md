<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Repeated [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) shows that the $U^3$ norm dominates the absolute mean of a function:

$$
|\mathbb Ef|\leq\|f\|_{U^3}.
$$

Taking $f=1_A$ and using $\mathbb E1_A=\alpha$ gives

$$
\boxed{\|1_A\|_{U^3}\geq\alpha.}
$$
