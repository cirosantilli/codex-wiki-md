<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The hypothesis says that the normalized [additive energy](../../../../../../additive-energy.md) of $A$ is at least $\eta$. By the [Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](../../../../../../balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md), for an absolute $C_0$ there is $A'\subseteq A$ such that

$$
|A'|\geq\eta^{C_0}|A|,
\qquad
|A'+A'|\leq\eta^{-C_0}|A'|.
$$

The finite-field Bogolyubov-Ruzsa consequence of the [Freiman-Ruzsa theorem over a finite field](../../../../../../freiman-ruzsa-theorem-over-a-finite-field.md) says that a set of doubling at most $K$ has a [vector subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md)

$$
V\subseteq A'+A'-A'-A'
$$

with $|V|\geq p^{-K^{C_1}}|A'|$ for an absolute $C_1$. Taking $K=\eta^{-C_0}$ and enlarging the absolute exponent gives

$$
V\subseteq A+A-A-A,
\qquad
|V|\geq p^{-\eta^{-C}}|A|.
$$

This is an energy form of the [Bogolyubov lemma](../../../../../../bogolyubov-lemma.md): the usual lemma assumes positive density in an ambient group, whereas the [Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](../../../../../../balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md) first extracts a dense structured model from the many additive quadruples. The resulting bound depends on the energy parameter rather than on the possibly tiny ambient density of $A$.
