↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The restriction of $J$ to the $J$-invariant bundle $TY$ is an [almost complex structure](../../../../../almost-complex-manifold.md). Its [Nijenhuis tensor](../../../../../nijenhuis-tensor.md) is the restriction of the ambient Nijenhuis tensor because vector fields tangent to an [embedded submanifold](../../../../../embedded-submanifold.md) have tangent Lie bracket. The ambient tensor vanishes since $X$ is a [complex manifold](../../../../../complex-manifold.md), so the [Newlander-Nirenberg theorem](../../../../../newlander-nirenberg-theorem.md) makes the induced structure on $Y$ integrable. The inclusion has complex-linear differential and is therefore holomorphic; hence $Y$ is a [complex submanifold](../../../../../complex-submanifold.md).

For a complex submanifold, the [holomorphic normal bundle](../../../../../holomorphic-normal-bundle.md) is

$$
N_{Y/X}=TX|_Y/TY.
$$

If $Y$ is a smooth hypersurface, taking top exterior powers in the [holomorphic conormal sequence](../../../../../holomorphic-conormal-sequence.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow N_{Y/X}^*\longrightarrow\Omega_X^1|_Y\longrightarrow\Omega_Y^1\longrightarrow0
$$

gives

$$
K_X|_Y\cong N_{Y/X}^*\otimes K_Y.
$$

The normal bundle of a hypersurface is $N_{Y/X}\cong\mathcal O_X(Y)|_Y$, so the [adjunction formula](../../../../../adjunction-formula.md) is

$$
K_Y\cong(K_X\otimes\mathcal O_X(Y))|_Y.
$$

On $X=\mathbb{CP}^n\times\mathbb{CP}^m$, a bihomogeneous polynomial of bidegree $(d_1,d_2)$ is a section of the [holomorphic line bundle](../../../../../holomorphic-line-bundle.md) $\mathcal O(d_1,d_2)$. Its zero locus is smooth precisely when the section is [transverse](../../../../../transverse-intersection-theorem.md) to the zero section, equivalently when $F$ and all of its homogeneous first partial derivatives have no common projective zero. Since

$$
K_X\cong\mathcal O(-n-1,-m-1),
$$

the [adjunction formula](../../../../../adjunction-formula.md) yields

$$
K_Y\cong\mathcal O(d_1-n-1,d_2-m-1)|_Y.
$$

**Thus a smooth $F$ with $d_1=n+1$ and $d_2=m+1$ defines a [complex submanifold](../../../../../complex-submanifold.md) with trivial [canonical bundle](../../../../../canonical-bundle.md).**
