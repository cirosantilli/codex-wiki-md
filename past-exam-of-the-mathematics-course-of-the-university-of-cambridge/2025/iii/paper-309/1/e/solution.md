<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For a radial [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md), $k=0$ gives $\dot r^2=e^2$. Choose the affine parameter so that $r(0)=0$ and the outgoing branch has $\dot r=e>0$. Then

$$
r(\lambda)=e\lambda,
\qquad
\dot t=\frac e{1+r^2}.
$$

Using $dr/d\lambda=e$,

$$
t(\lambda)=\int_0^{r(\lambda)}\frac{dr}{1+r^2}
=\arctan r(\lambda).
$$

Consequently $r\to\infty$ while

$$
\boxed{t\longrightarrow\tau=\frac\pi2}.
$$

The conformal boundary is infinitely far away in affine parameter but is reached in finite static coordinate time.
