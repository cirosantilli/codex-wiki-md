<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [spherical coordinate system](../../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md), $|d\mathbf x|^2=dr^2+r^2d\Omega_2^2$ and $\mathbf x\mathbin\cdot d\mathbf x=r\,dr$. Hence

$$
|d\mathbf x|^2-\frac{(\mathbf x\mathbin\cdot d\mathbf x)^2}{1+r^2}
=\left(1-\frac{r^2}{1+r^2}\right)dr^2+r^2d\Omega_2^2
=\frac{dr^2}{1+r^2}+r^2d\Omega_2^2.
$$

Thus the [static coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../static-coordinates-on-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) have

$$
\boxed{w(r)=1+r^2},
$$

which is positive for every $r\geq0$.
