<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Affinely parametrized [geodesics](../../../../../../geodesic.md) are the critical curves of the [geodesic Lagrangian](../../../../../../geodesic-lagrangian.md)

$$
L=\frac12\left[-w\dot t^2+\frac{\dot r^2}{w}
+r^2\dot\theta^2+r^2\sin^2\theta\,\dot\phi^2\right].
$$

For $w=1+r^2$ and $w'=2r$, the nonzero [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md), up to symmetry in the lower indices, are

$$
\Gamma^t{}_{tr}=\frac{w'}{2w}=\frac r w,
\quad
\Gamma^r{}_{tt}=\frac{ww'}2=rw,
\quad
\Gamma^r{}_{rr}=-\frac{w'}{2w}=-\frac r w,
$$



$$
\Gamma^r{}_{\theta\theta}=-rw,
\quad
\Gamma^r{}_{\phi\phi}=-rw\sin^2\theta,
\quad
\Gamma^\theta{}_{r\theta}=\Gamma^\phi{}_{r\phi}=\frac1r,
$$



$$
\Gamma^\theta{}_{\phi\phi}=-\sin\theta\cos\theta,
\qquad
\Gamma^\phi{}_{\theta\phi}=\cot\theta.
$$

They follow either from the Euler-Lagrange equations or directly from the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) formula.
