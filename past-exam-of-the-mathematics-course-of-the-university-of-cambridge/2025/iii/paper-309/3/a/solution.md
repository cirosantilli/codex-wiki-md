<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}+\epsilon h_{\mu\nu}$ and retain first-order terms. The quadratic Christoffel products in the supplied Ricci formula drop out. The wave-coordinate condition becomes the [Lorenz gauge in linearized gravity](../../../../../../lorenz-gauge-in-linearized-gravity.md)

$$
\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0,
\qquad
\bar h_{\mu\nu}=h_{\mu\nu}-\frac12\eta_{\mu\nu}h.
$$

The [linearized Ricci tensor and scalar](../../../../../../linearized-ricci-tensor-and-scalar.md) then satisfy

$$
G^{(1)}_{\mu\nu}=-\frac12\mathop{\Box}\bar h_{\mu\nu}.
$$

Substitution into $G_{\mu\nu}=8\pi T_{\mu\nu}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\mathop{\Box}\bar h_{\mu\nu}=-16\pi T_{\mu\nu}},
\qquad
\boxed{\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0}.
$$

If the paper denotes the trace-reversed variable itself by $h_{\mu\nu}$ in its displayed equation, this is exactly that convention.
