<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The first vacuum [Maxwell equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations.md) equation is preserved for every vector field because part a gives

$$
d\widetilde F=d\mathcal L_XF=\mathcal L_X(dF)=0.
$$

On two-forms in four dimensions, the mixed volume tensor $T_{ab}{}^{cd}$ represents twice the [Hodge star operator](../../../../../../hodge-star-operator.md). Part b therefore says that a conformal Killing field commutes with the Hodge star:

$$
\mathcal L_X(\star F)=\star\mathcal L_XF.
$$

It follows that

$$
d(\star\widetilde F)
=d\mathcal L_X(\star F)
=\mathcal L_Xd(\star F)=0.
$$

**Hence $\widetilde F=\mathcal L_XF$ satisfies both vacuum Maxwell equations whenever $F$ does.**
