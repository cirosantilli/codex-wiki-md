<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At $\epsilon=0$ the metric is the constant [Minkowski metric](../../../../../../minkowski-metric.md), whose Christoffel symbols and curvature vanish, so

$$
R^{(0)}_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=0.
$$

Insert $R=\epsilon R^{(1)}+O(\epsilon^2)$ into the [Penrose wave equation](../../../../../../penrose-wave-equation.md). Every curvature-square term is $O(\epsilon^2)$, while the covariant wave operator reduces at first order to the flat [d'Alembert operator](../../../../../../d-alembert-operator.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\partial^\alpha\partial_\alpha
R^{(1)}_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=0}.
$$

The [linearized Riemann curvature operator](../../../../../../linearized-riemann-curvature-operator.md) is unchanged by $h_{\mu\nu}\mapsto h_{\mu\nu}+\partial_\mu\xi_\nu+\partial_\nu\xi_\mu$, because the resulting third derivatives cancel pairwise. The equation therefore requires no gauge choice for $h_{\mu\nu}$.
