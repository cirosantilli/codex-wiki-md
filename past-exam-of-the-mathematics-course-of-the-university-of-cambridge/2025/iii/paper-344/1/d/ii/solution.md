<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The two [binodal](../../../../../../../binodal.md) compositions $\phi_1<0<\phi_2$ are the contact points of the [common-tangent construction](../../../../../../../common-tangent-construction.md). Equivalently, they solve

$$
f'(\phi_1)=f'(\phi_2)=\mu,
\qquad
f(\phi_1)-\mu\phi_1=f(\phi_2)-\mu\phi_2.
$$

The first equality is equality of [chemical potential](../../../../../../../chemical-potential.md); the second is equality of [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md). Here

$$
(a-C)\phi_1+b\phi_1^3=a\phi_2+b\phi_2^3
$$

together with the intercept equation determines the two densities. As $C\downarrow a$, both contact points approach zero continuously with scale $|\phi_{1,2}|\propto\sqrt{C-a}$, so this mean-field onset of phase separation is continuous.
