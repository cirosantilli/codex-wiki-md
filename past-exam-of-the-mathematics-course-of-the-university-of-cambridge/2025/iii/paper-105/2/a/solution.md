<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $u,g\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$, the function $g$ is the $i$th [weak derivative](../../../../../../weak-derivative.md) $D_i u$ when

$$
\int_UuD_i\varphi=-\int_Ug\varphi
$$

for every [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) $\varphi\in C_c^\infty(U)$. This is the [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) identity with no boundary term and agrees with the ordinary derivative whenever $u$ is classically differentiable.

For $1\leq p\leq\infty$, the [first-order Sobolev space](../../../../../../first-order-sobolev-space.md) is

$$
W^{1,p}(U)=\{u\in L^p(U):D_i u\in L^p(U),\ 1\leq i\leq n\},
$$

with norm, for example,

$$
\|u\|_{W^{1,p}(U)}=\|u\|_{L^p(U)}+\sum_{i=1}^n\|D_i u\|_{L^p(U)}.
$$

Functions equal [almost everywhere](../../../../../../almost-everywhere.md) represent the same Sobolev element.
