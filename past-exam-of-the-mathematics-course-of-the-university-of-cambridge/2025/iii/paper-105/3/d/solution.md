<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [characteristic curves](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) are $x=\xi+at$. Along one of them, $U(t)=u(t,\xi+at)$ obeys the separable [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md)

$$
U'=U^2,
\qquad
U(0)=\cos\xi.
$$

Therefore

$$
U(t)=\frac{\cos\xi}{1-t\cos\xi},
\qquad
u(t,x)=\frac{\cos(x-at)}{1-t\cos(x-at)}.
$$

For $0\leq t<1$, the denominator is positive everywhere. At $t=1$, it first vanishes where $\cos\xi=1$, namely at $\xi=2\pi k$. The solution consequently has [finite-time blowup](../../../../../../finite-time-blowup.md) at time

$$
T_*=1
$$

along the points $x=a+2\pi k$. No finite classical solution can continue through that time.
