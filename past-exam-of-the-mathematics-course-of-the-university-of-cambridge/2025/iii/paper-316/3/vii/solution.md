<h1 id="3/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

Very near one planet, that planet dominates both the particle's free-precession coefficient and its forcing coefficient, with the leading terms arranged approximately as $\dot z\simeq iA(z-z_p)-z/\tau$. Since $|A|\tau\gg1$ close to the planet, the late-time forced solution approaches

$$
\boxed{z\simeq z_p}.
$$

The particle's orbit therefore tends toward the planet's eccentricity and apsidal direction. Extremely close to the planet, close encounters, co-orbital dynamics, and individual [mean-motion resonances](../../../../../../mean-motion-resonance.md) invalidate the orbit-averaged linear approximation.
