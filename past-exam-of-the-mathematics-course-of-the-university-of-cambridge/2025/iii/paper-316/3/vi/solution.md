<h1 id="3/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

For $t\gg\tau$, the free term has disappeared. Exactly at $A=g_1$, the resonant contribution tends to

$$
\boxed{z_1(t)=i\tau\nu_1e^{i(g_1t+\beta_1)}},
$$

with finite eccentricity $|\nu_1|\tau$ and a quarter-cycle phase shift. Without damping, exact resonance instead gives

$$
z_1(t)=e^{iAt}(C+i\nu_1t e^{i\beta_1}),
$$

whose [secular resonance](../../../../../../secular-resonance.md) amplitude grows linearly in the ideal linear theory.
