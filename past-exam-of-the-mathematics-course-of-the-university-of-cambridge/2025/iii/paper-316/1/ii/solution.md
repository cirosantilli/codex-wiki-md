<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The impactors' number density per diameter is $KD_{\rm im}^{-\alpha}/V$. With gravitational focusing neglected, the [geometric collision cross-section](../../../../../../geometric-collision-cross-section.md) is $\pi(D+D_{\rm im})^2/4$. The [planetesimal collision rate](../../../../../../planetesimal-collision-rate.md) is consequently

$$
R_{\rm col}(D,D_{\rm im})dD_{\rm im}
=\frac{K D_{\rm im}^{-\alpha}}{V}
\frac{\pi(D+D_{\rm im})^2}{4}v_{\rm rel}\,dD_{\rm im}
=A D_{\rm im}^{2-\alpha}\left(1+\frac D{D_{\rm im}}\right)^2dD_{\rm im},
$$

where

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{\pi K v_{\rm rel}}{4V}}.
$$
