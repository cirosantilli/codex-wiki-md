The definition of $X_c$ gives

$$
\frac Q{Q_D^*}=\left(\frac{D_{\rm im}}{X_cD}\right)^3.
$$

The [cratering collision](../../../../../../cratering-collision.md) prescription therefore removes the target-mass fraction

$$
\boxed{1-f_{\rm lr}=\frac12\frac Q{Q_D^*}
=\frac12\left(\frac{D_{\rm im}}{X_cD}\right)^3}.
$$
