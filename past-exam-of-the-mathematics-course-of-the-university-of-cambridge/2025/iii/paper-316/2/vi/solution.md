<h1 id="2/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

In a steady axisymmetric outflow, [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) makes $2\pi r\Sigma(r)v_r(r)$ independent of $r$. The [steady radial dust outflow](../../../../../../steady-radial-dust-outflow.md) therefore has

$$
\boxed{\Sigma(r)\propto\frac1{rv_r}
\propto\frac1{\sqrt{(x-1)[(2\beta-1)x+1]}}},
\qquad x=\frac r{a_p}.
$$

It has an integrable $(r-a_p)^{-1/2}$ pile-up just outside the source ring, where the radial speed starts from zero, and approaches $Sigma\propto r^{-1}$ at large radius.
