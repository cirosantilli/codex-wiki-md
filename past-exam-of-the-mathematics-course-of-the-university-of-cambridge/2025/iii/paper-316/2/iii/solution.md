<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The parent body's [circular Kepler orbit](../../../../../../circular-kepler-orbit.md) has speed $v_K=\sqrt{\mu/a_p}$. Release without a velocity impulse therefore gives

$$
\boxed{h=a_pv_K=\sqrt{\mu a_p}}.
$$

The release point is the dust orbit's point of closest approach, where $\theta-\varpi=\pi$. Substituting $r=a_p$ in the orbit equation gives

$$
a_p=\frac{a_p/(1-\beta)}{1-e},
\qquad
\boxed{e=\frac{\beta}{\beta-1}}.
$$

The [longitude of periapsis](../../../../../../longitude-of-periapsis.md) $\varpi$ specifies the symmetry axis of the conic. With this signed repulsive-force convention, it points opposite the release direction, so the closest point is at $\theta=\varpi+\pi$.
