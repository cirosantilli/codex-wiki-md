<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In polar coordinates in the orbital plane, $h=r^2\dot\theta$. Setting $u=1/r$, the [Binet equation](../../../../../../binet-equation.md) for the effective inverse-square force is

$$
u''+u=\frac{\mu(1-\beta)}{h^2}.
$$

Its general solution is

$$
u=\frac{\mu(1-\beta)}{h^2}
\left[1+e\cos(\theta-\varpi)\right],
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{h^2/[\mu(1-\beta)]}
{1+e\cos(\theta-\varpi)}}.
$$

This is a branch of a [hyperbolic Kepler orbit](../../../../../../hyperbolic-kepler-orbit.md); because $1-\beta<0$, its physical branch has a negative denominator.
