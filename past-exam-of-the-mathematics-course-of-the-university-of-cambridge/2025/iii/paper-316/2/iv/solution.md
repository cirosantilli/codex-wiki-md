<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

At release,

$$
C=\frac{\mu}{2a_p}-\frac{\mu(1-\beta)}{a_p}
=\frac{\mu}{a_p}\left(\beta-\frac12\right).
$$

At large radius the potential vanishes, so

$$
\boxed{v_\infty=\sqrt{2C}
=\sqrt{(2\beta-1)\frac{\mu}{a_p}}
\simeq\sqrt{\frac{2\beta\mu}{a_p}}}.
$$

The asymptote satisfies $1+e\cos f_\infty=0$, where $f=\theta-\varpi$. If the angular displacement from the release direction is $\Delta=\pi-f_\infty$, then

$$
\cos(\pi-\Delta)=-\frac1e=-1+\frac1\beta.
$$

For $\beta\gg1$, $-\cos\Delta\simeq-1+\Delta^2/2$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta\simeq\sqrt{\frac2\beta}}.
$$
