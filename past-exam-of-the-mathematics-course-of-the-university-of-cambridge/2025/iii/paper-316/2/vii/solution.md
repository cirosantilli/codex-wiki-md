<h1 id="2/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

The large-distance profile is proportional to $[\sqrt{2\beta-1},x]^{-1}$. At $x=2$, the exact profile is proportional to $(4\beta-1)^{-1/2}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Sigma_{\rm actual}(2a_p)}
{\Sigma_{r^{-1}\ {
m extrapolation}}(2a_p)}
=\frac{2\sqrt{2\beta-1}}{\sqrt{4\beta-1}}
\longrightarrow\sqrt2}
$$

as $\beta\to\infty$. The enhancement is the finite-radius remnant of the source-ring pile-up.
