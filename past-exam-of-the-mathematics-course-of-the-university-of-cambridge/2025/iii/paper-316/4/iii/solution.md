<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $v_K=\sqrt{GM_*/a_p}$. At periapsis the nearly parabolic planetesimal and planet have parallel speeds $\sqrt2v_K$ and $v_K$, so

$$
v_\infty\simeq(\sqrt2-1)v_K.
$$

For a passage just outside the planet, the [impact parameter](../../../../../../impact-parameter.md) is $b\simeq\delta a_p$. In the small-angle limit of the supplied [gravitational scattering angle](../../../../../../gravitational-scattering-angle.md),

$$
\frac\theta2\simeq\frac{GM_p}{bv_\infty^2}
=\frac{\mu}{\delta(\sqrt2-1)^2}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\theta\simeq A\frac\mu\delta,
\qquad A=\frac2{(\sqrt2-1)^2}=6+4\sqrt2}.
$$
