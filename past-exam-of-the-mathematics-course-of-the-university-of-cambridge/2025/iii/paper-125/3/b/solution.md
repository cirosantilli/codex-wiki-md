<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The Hessian determinant of $u^3+v^3+w^3$ is a nonzero scalar multiple of $uvw$. Hence the [inflection points](../../../../../../inflection-point-of-a-plane-cubic.md) are

$$
(1:-\zeta:0),
\qquad(1:0:-\zeta),
\qquad(0:1:-\zeta),
\qquad \zeta^3=1.
$$

There are nine of them. Since $O_C=(1:-1:0)$ is an inflection point, these are exactly $C[3]$. They are all defined over

$$
K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3}),
$$

so $C(K)[3]\cong(\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z)^2$.
