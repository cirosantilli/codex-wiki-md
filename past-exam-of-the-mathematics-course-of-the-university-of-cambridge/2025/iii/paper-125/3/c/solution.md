<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Weil pairing](../../../../../../weil-pairing.md) $e_3:C[3]\times C[3]\to\mu_3$ is nondegenerate and Galois equivariant. If all of $C[3]$ is rational over a field, pairing a basis produces a primitive cube root of unity in that field. Thus a quadratic field with full 3-torsion must contain $\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$ and must equal it.

More generally, if $E(\mathbb F_p)[\ell]\cong(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^2$, Frobenius acts as the identity on $E[\ell]$. Its characteristic polynomial $X^2-aX+p$ is therefore congruent to $(X-1)^2$ modulo $\ell$. Comparing coefficients gives

$$
a\equiv2\pmod\ell,
\qquad p\equiv1\pmod\ell.
$$

In particular $\#E(\mathbb F_p)=1+p-a$ with $a\equiv2\pmod\ell$.
