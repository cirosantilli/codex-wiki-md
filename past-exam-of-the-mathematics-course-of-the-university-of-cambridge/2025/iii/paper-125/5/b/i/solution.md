For $P=(1,5)$, the tangent slope is

$$
\lambda=\frac{3x^2+26x+11}{2y}=4,
$$

so

$$
2P=(\lambda^2-13-2,\,-5+\lambda(1-x(2P)))=(1,-5).
$$

For $Q=(-1,1)$, the secant slope is $2$, giving

$$
P+Q=(-9,15).
$$

Let $T=(0,0)$, the rational point of order two. Direct use of the chord-and-tangent law gives

$$
(x,y)+T=\left(\frac{11}{x},-\frac{11y}{x^2}\right).
$$

Translation by a torsion point sends torsion points to torsion points, proving the claim.
