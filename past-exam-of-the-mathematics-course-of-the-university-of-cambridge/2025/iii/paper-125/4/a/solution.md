<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $[x]\in\Delta$, choose $\alpha_x$ with $\alpha_x^n=x$ and define the [Kummer pairing](../../../../../../kummer-pairing.md)

$$
\langle\sigma,[x]\rangle=\frac{\sigma(\alpha_x)}{\alpha_x}\in\mu_n.
$$

Changing $x$ by an $n$th power or changing its root leaves the quotient unchanged because $\mu_n\subset K$. Multiplication of radicals proves bilinearity. If an automorphism pairs trivially with every class, it fixes all generating radicals and hence all of $L$. If a class pairs trivially with every automorphism, its radical lies in $K$, so the class is trivial. Thus the pairing is well-defined, bilinear, and nondegenerate on both sides.
