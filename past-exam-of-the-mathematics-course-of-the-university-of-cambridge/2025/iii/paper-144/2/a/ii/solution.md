An [ultrafilter](../../../../../../../ultrafilter.md) is a proper filter maximal under inclusion. Equivalently, for every $A\subseteq\mathbb N$, exactly one of $A$ and $\mathbb N\setminus A$ belongs to $\mathcal U$.
