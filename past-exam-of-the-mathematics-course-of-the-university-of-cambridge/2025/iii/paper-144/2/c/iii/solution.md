↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Yes.** Choose a [nonprincipal ultrafilter](../../../../../../../nonprincipal-ultrafilter.md) on $\mathbb N$ and let

$$
a=[(1\bmod i)_{i\in\mathbb N}]_{\mathcal U}\in\mathcal C.
$$

For each fixed positive integer $n$, the equality $na=0$ holds in the $i$th factor exactly when $i$ divides $n$. Only finitely many positive integers divide $n$, so the cofinite set of indices for which $na\ne0$ belongs to $\mathcal U$. [Łoś theorem](../../../../../../../los-theorem.md) gives $na\ne0$ in $\mathcal C$ for every $n>0$. Thus $a$ has infinite [order of a group element](../../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md).
