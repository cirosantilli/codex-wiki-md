A complete theory $T$ is [categorical theory](../../../../../../categorical-theory.md) in the infinite cardinal $\kappa$, or $\kappa$-categorical, when it has a model of cardinality $\kappa$ and any two of its models of cardinality $\kappa$ are [isomorphic](../../../../../../isomorphism.md). Equivalently, $T$ has exactly one model of cardinality $\kappa$ up to isomorphism.
