<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

One shuffle removes the ordered top $k$ cards and inserts them, in a uniformly random person-order, into successively chosen uniform slots. The transition rule depends on a deck only through relabeling of its cards, so its transition matrix on the [symmetric group](../../../../../../../symmetric-group.md) is doubly stochastic. Hence the uniform distribution on all $n!$ permutations is invariant.
