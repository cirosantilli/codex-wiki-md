<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Reverse time under the uniform invariant law. The reverse shuffle selects $k$ cards uniformly without replacement and moves them to the top in random order. Track cards once selected. The forward time $\tau$ at which the initially bottom card first lies among the next top $k$ corresponds in reverse to the first time every card has been selected. At that [coupon-collector](../../../../../../../coupon-collector-problem.md) time, the order of all marked cards is uniform and independent of the marking time, by induction over the random insertions. Reversing again shows that $X_\tau$ is uniform and independent of $\tau$, so part (a) makes $\tau$ a strong stationary time.
