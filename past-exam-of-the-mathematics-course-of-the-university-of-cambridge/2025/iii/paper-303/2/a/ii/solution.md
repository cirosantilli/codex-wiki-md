<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For couplings $g_i(\ell)$ under coarse-graining scale $b=e^\ell$, their [beta function](../../../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) is $\beta_i(g)=dg_i/d\ell$. At a [renormalization-group fixed point](../../../../../../../renormalization-group-fixed-point.md) $g_*$ all beta functions vanish. Linearization gives

$$
\frac{d}{d\ell}\delta g_i=M_{ij}\delta g_j,
\qquad
M_{ij}=\left.\frac{\partial\beta_i}{\partial g_j}\right|_{g_*}.
$$

The eigenvalues $y_a$ of the [stability matrix of a renormalization-group fixed point](../../../../../../../stability-matrix-of-a-renormalization-group-fixed-point.md) are the scaling dimensions of the associated coupling directions: $\delta g_a(\ell)=e^{y_a\ell}\delta g_a(0)$.
