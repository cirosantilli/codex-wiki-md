<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $\lambda_0=0$, the action has the exact discrete symmetry $\phi\mapsto-\phi$. Integrating out modes and rescaling preserve this symmetry, so no odd operator such as $\phi^3$ can be generated from the even quartic coupling. Thus $g_0$ corrects $\lambda$ at no order in perturbation theory: the hypersurface $\lambda=0$ is invariant under the [renormalization-group flow](../../../../../../../renormalization-group-flow.md).
