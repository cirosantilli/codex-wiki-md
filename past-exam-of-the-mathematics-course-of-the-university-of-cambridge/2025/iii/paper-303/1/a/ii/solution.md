↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [mean-field approximation](../../../../../../../mean-field-approximation.md) replaces

$$
S_iS_j=m(S_i+S_j)-m^2+(S_i-m)(S_j-m)
$$

by its first three terms. Since every site has $q$ neighbours,

$$
\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}S_iS_j\simeq qm\sum_iS_i-\frac{Nq}{2}m^2.
$$

The sites then decouple, and their three possible weights sum to

$$
1+e^{-\beta g+\beta(Jqm+B)}+e^{-\beta g-\beta(Jqm+B)}
=1+2\kappa\cosh\!\bigl(\beta(Jqm+B)\bigr).
$$

Hence $c_1=1$, $c_2=2$, and

$$
\boxed{Z=e^{-\beta NJqm^2/2}
\left[1+2\kappa\cosh\!\bigl(\beta(Jqm+B)\bigr)\right]^N.}
$$
