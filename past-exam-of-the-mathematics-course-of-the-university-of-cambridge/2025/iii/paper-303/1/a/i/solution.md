For the [partition function](../../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) $Z=\sum_{\{S\}}e^{-\beta E}$, differentiation with respect to the external field gives

$$
\frac{\partial\log Z}{\partial B}
=\frac1Z\sum_{\{S\}}\beta\!\left(\sum_iS_i\right)e^{-\beta E}
=\beta\sum_i\langle S_i\rangle.
$$

Therefore the [magnetization](../../../../../../../magnetization.md) per site is

$$
\boxed{m=\frac1{N\beta}\frac{\partial\log Z}{\partial B}}.
$$
