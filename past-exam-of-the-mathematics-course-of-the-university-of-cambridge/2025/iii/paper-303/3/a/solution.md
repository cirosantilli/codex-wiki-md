<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If the singular free-energy density is set by one correlated region, then $f_s\sim\xi^{-d}$. With reduced temperature $t$, the definitions $\xi\sim|t|^{-\nu}$ and $f_s\sim|t|^{2-\alpha}$ therefore give

$$
|t|^{2-\alpha}\sim|t|^{d\nu},
\qquad
\boxed{2-\alpha=d\nu}.
$$

This is the [hyperscaling relation](../../../../../../hyperscaling-relation.md); it assumes that no [dangerously irrelevant coupling](../../../../../../dangerously-irrelevant-coupling.md) introduces an additional scale.
