<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Introducing a [Feynman parameter](../../../../../../feynman-parameter.md) and subtracting at zero external momentum give

$$
\Gamma_4(s,t,u)=\lambda+\frac{\lambda^2}{32\pi^2}
\sum_{q^2=s,t,u}\int_0^1dx\,
\log\frac{m^2}{m^2-x(1-x)q^2-i\epsilon}+O(\lambda^3).
$$

The bare coupling $\lambda_0=\lambda+\delta_\lambda$ is independent of the [renormalization scale](../../../../../../renormalization-scale.md). Differentiating its logarithmic counterterm at fixed $\lambda_0$ gives the leading [beta function](../../../../../../beta-function-physics.md)

$$
\boxed{\beta_\lambda=\mu\frac{d\lambda}{d\mu}=\frac{3\lambda^2}{16\pi^2}+O(\lambda^3)}.
$$
