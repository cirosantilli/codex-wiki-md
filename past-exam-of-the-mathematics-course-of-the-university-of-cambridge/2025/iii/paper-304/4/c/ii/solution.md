<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Gauge invariance gives $Q\int\mathcal L=0$. Nilpotence gives $Q^2\Psi=0$, and therefore

$$
QS=Q\int\mathcal L+Q^2\Psi=0.
$$

**Thus writing the gauge-fixing contribution as a BRST-exact term makes the complete action [BRST invariant](../../../../../../../brst-symmetry.md).**
