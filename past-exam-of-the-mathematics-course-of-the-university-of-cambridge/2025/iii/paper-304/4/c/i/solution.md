<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the Grassmann-odd ghost $c$, the [BRST transformations](../../../../../../../brst-symmetry.md) are

$$
QA_\mu=D_\mu c=\partial_\mu c-ig[A_\mu,c],
\qquad
Q\phi=ig[c,\phi],
\qquad
Qc=igc^2,
$$

up to a simultaneous convention-dependent sign for $Q$ and $c$. The ghost transformation makes the [BRST charge](../../../../../../../brst-charge.md) nilpotent: $Q^2A_\mu=Q^2\phi=0$.
