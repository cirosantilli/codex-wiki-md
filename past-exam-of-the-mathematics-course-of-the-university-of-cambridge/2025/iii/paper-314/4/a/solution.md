<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [force-free magnetic field](../../../../../../force-free-magnetic-field.md) obeys

$$
(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\times\mathbf B=0,
$$

equivalently $\mathbf j\times\mathbf B=0$: its current is parallel to its magnetic field. In a very low-density exterior, material pressure and inertia are too small to balance a finite Lorentz force. Quasistatic force balance therefore drives the magnetic field toward a force-free configuration.
