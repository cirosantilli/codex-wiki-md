<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $u>0$ be the inward radial speed. Steady spherical [Bondi accretion](../../../../../../bondi-accretion.md) conserves mass:

$$
\dot M=4\pi r^2\rho u.
$$

For $\gamma=5/3$, $p=K\rho^{5/3}$ and the [adiabatic sound speed](../../../../../../adiabatic-sound-speed.md) satisfies $v_s^2=\gamma p/\rho\propto\rho^{2/3}$. Matching the reservoir therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\rho=\rho_0\left(\frac{v_s}{v_{s0}}\right)^3}.
$$

The Bernoulli integral is

$$
\frac{u^2}{2}+\frac{v_s^2}{\gamma-1}-\frac{GM}{r}
=\frac{3v_{s0}^2}{2}.
$$

Writing the [Mach number](../../../../../../mach-number.md) as $\mathcal M=u/v_s$ and eliminating $v_s$ with mass conservation gives

$$
r v_s^2
=\left(\frac{\dot Mv_{s0}^3}{4\pi\rho_0}\right)^{1/2}\mathcal M^{-1/2}.
$$

Multiplication of the Bernoulli equation by $r/2$ now yields

$$
\boxed{A\dot M^{1/2}
\left(\mathcal M^{3/2}+3\mathcal M^{-1/2}\right)=GM+Br},
$$

where

$$
\boxed{A=\left(\frac{v_{s0}^3}{16\pi\rho_0}\right)^{1/2}},
\qquad
\boxed{B=\frac32v_{s0}^2}.
$$
