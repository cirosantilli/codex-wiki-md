<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

As $r\to0$, the right-hand side of the Mach-number equation tends to $GM$. Since $f(\mathcal M)\geq4$, a real positive solution at arbitrarily small $r$ requires

$$
4A\dot M^{1/2}\leq GM.
$$

Using the value of $A$ gives

$$
\dot M\leq\frac{(GM)^2}{16A^2}
=\boxed{\frac{\pi\rho_0(GM)^2}{v_{s0}^3}}
=\dot M_{\rm crit}.
$$

This is the [critical Bondi accretion rate for gamma equals five thirds](../../../../../../critical-bondi-accretion-rate-for-gamma-equals-five-thirds.md).
