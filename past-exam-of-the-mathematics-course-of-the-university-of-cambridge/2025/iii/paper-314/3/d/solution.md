<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $\mathbf B=B\mathbf e_x$, the vertical magnetic perturbation is

$$
\delta B_z=ik_xB\,\widetilde\xi_z.
$$

The $z$ component of the Fourier-transformed equation of motion is therefore

$$
-\rho\omega^2\widetilde\xi_z
=-ik_z\widetilde{\delta\Pi}
-\rho v_A^2k_x^2\widetilde\xi_z.
$$

Using $\omega=vk_x$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\widetilde{\delta\Pi}}{\widetilde\xi_z}
=-i\rho\,\frac{k_x^2}{k_z}(v^2-v_A^2)}.
$$
