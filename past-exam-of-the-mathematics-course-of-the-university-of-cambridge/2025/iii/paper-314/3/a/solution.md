↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [adiabatic sound speed](../../../../../../adiabatic-sound-speed.md) and [Alfvén speed](../../../../../../alfven-speed.md) are

$$
\boxed{v_s^2=\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial\rho}\right)_s
=\frac{\gamma p}{\rho}},
\qquad
\boxed{\mathbf v_A=\frac{\mathbf B}{\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}},
\quad v_A=\frac{B}{\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}}}.
$$

The entropy is held fixed in the sound-speed derivative.
