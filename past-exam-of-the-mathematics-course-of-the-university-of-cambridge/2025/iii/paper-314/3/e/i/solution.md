<h1 id="3/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The interface is material in [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../../../../ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md), so fluid on it remains on it. Its normal displacement $\xi_z$ must consequently be the same when approached from either side. Integrating normal momentum balance through an infinitesimal pillbox shows that the Lagrangian total-pressure traction is continuous. Because the equilibrium total pressure is constant on each side and continuous at $z=0$, this reduces to continuity of the Eulerian perturbation $\delta\Pi$. Hence

$$
\boxed{[\xi_z]_-^+=0,\qquad[\delta\Pi]_-^+=0}.
$$
