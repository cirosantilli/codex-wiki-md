<h1 id="3/e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Define

$$
\Sigma_\pm=\frac{\rho_\pm}{|k_{z\pm}|}.
$$

Solving the matching condition gives the [magnetohydrodynamic interface wave](../../../../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-interface-wave.md) speed

$$
\boxed{v^2=
\frac{\Sigma_+v_{A+}^2+\Sigma_-v_{A-}^2}
{\Sigma_++\Sigma_-}}.
$$

The weights $\Sigma_\pm$ are positive, so $v^2$ is a weighted average of the two squared [Alfvén speeds](../../../../../../../alfven-speed.md). It therefore lies between them, and so does the positive phase speed $v$.
