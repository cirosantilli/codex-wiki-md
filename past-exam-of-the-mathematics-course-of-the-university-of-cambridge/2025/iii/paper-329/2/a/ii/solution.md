↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $C=1/2$,

$$
(W')^2=W^2+2W=(W+1)^2-1,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{W(Z)=\cosh Z-1,
\qquad A(Z)\propto\frac1{\cosh Z-1}.}
$$

The thread thins exponentially for large $Z$, and its tensile stress is proportional to $\sinh Z$.
