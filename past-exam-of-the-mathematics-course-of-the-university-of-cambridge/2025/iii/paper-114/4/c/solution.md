<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $0<k<d$ and $\alpha\in H^k(M;\mathbb Z)$. Its restrictions to the contractible sets $U$ and $V$ vanish. The [cap-product support lemma for a two-set cover](../../../../../../cap-product-support-lemma-for-a-two-set-cover.md), proved by representing $[M]$ with small simplices and replacing the restricted cocycles by coboundaries, therefore gives

$$
[M]\frown\alpha=0.
$$

[Poincare duality](../../../../../../poincare-duality.md) makes cap product with $[M]$ injective, so $H^k(M;\mathbb Z)=0$ for every intermediate degree. Applying duality again gives $H_i(M;\mathbb Z)=0$ for $0<i<d$. Finally the connected oriented closed manifold has

$$
H_i(M;\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&i=0,d,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
