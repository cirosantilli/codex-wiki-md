The [cap product](../../../../../../cap-product.md) is the chain operation

$$
C_p(M;R)\otimes C^k(M;R)\longrightarrow C_{p-k}(M;R)
$$

obtained by evaluating the cochain on the front $k$-face of a singular simplex and retaining its back $(p-k)$-face, with the standard sign convention. It descends to homology and cohomology.

A [fundamental class](../../../../../../fundamental-class.md) $[M]\in H_d(M;\mathbb Z)$ restricts at every $x\in M$ to the local generator of $H_d(M,M\setminus\{x\};\mathbb Z)$ selected by the orientation. [Poincare duality](../../../../../../poincare-duality.md) states that

$$
H^k(M;R)\xrightarrow{\cong}H_{d-k}(M;R),
\qquad
\alpha\longmapsto[M]\frown\alpha
$$

is an isomorphism for every $k$ and coefficient ring $R$.
