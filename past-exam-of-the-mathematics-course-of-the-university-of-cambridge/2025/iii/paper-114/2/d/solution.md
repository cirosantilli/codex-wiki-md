<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Suppose such maps existed, and let $u$ generate $H^4(S^4;\mathbb Z)$. Since $f^*g^*=1$, write $g^*u=ma$ and $f^*a=nu$; then $mn=1$, so $m=\pm1$. Naturality of the [cup product](../../../../../../cup-product.md) gives

$$
0=g^*(u^2)=(g^*u)^2=a^2=d,
$$

contradicting the nonzero degree-eight class computed in part c.
