<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $n>0$,

$$
H^*(\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z[x]/(x^{n+1}),
\qquad |x|=2.
$$

The CW structure with one cell in every even dimension gives the additive groups, and the generator $x=c_1(\gamma^*)$ restricts compatibly along $\mathbb{CP}^{n-1}\subset\mathbb{CP}^n$; its powers generate each even group.
