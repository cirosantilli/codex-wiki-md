<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With the indicated points as basepoints, $X$ is the [smash product](../../../../../../smash-product.md) $\mathbb{CP}^2\wedge\mathbb{CP}^2$. The quotient map identifies its positive-degree cohomology with the relative cohomology of the product modulo the wedge, which under the Künneth isomorphism is the ideal generated by $xy$. Thus

$$
\widetilde H^*(X;\mathbb Z)
=\mathbb Z\{a,b,c,d\},
\qquad
a=xy, b=x^2y, c=xy^2, d=x^2y^2,
$$

in degrees $4,6,6,8$. The only nonzero product of positive-degree basis elements is

$$
a^2=d.
$$

Together with the unit in degree zero, this determines the cohomology ring.
