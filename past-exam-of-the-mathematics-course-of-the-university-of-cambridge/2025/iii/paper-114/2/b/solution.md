↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The integral [Künneth theorem](../../../../../../kunneth-theorem.md) gives a natural short exact sequence containing tensor and Tor terms. Here all cohomology groups are free abelian, so the Tor terms vanish and the external cup product is a ring isomorphism. Thus

$$
\boxed{H^*(\mathbb{CP}^2\times\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)
\cong\mathbb Z[x,y]/(x^3,y^3),
\qquad |x|=|y|=2.}
$$
