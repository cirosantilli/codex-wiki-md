<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Suppose $\mathbb P_k^n$ had a cover by $n$ affine opens. Since projective space is separated, every finite intersection in this cover is affine. The [acyclic cover theorem](../../../../../../leray-s-theorem.md) would therefore compute the cohomology of every quasi-coherent sheaf by its Čech complex. A cover with only $n$ members has no degree-$n$ cochains, so it would imply

$$
H^n(\mathbb P_k^n,\mathcal O(-n-1))=0.
$$

But [top cohomology of projective space](../../../../../../top-cohomology-of-projective-space.md) gives

$$
H^n(\mathbb P_k^n,\mathcal O(-n-1))\cong k,
$$

a contradiction. Hence no such affine cover exists.
