<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [prime Weil divisor](../../../../../../prime-weil-divisor.md) is an integral closed subscheme of codimension one. If $Y$ has generic point $\eta_Y$, regularity in codimension one makes the local ring $\mathcal O_{X,\eta_Y}$ a [discrete valuation ring](../../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md) with fraction field $K(X)$. Its normalized [discrete valuation](../../../../../../discrete-valuation.md)

$$
\nu_Y:K(X)^*\longrightarrow\mathbb Z
$$

is the order of vanishing along $Y$: writing $f=u\pi^m$ for a unit $u$ and uniformizer $\pi$ gives $\nu_Y(f)=m$. Additivity of exponents makes this a group homomorphism.
