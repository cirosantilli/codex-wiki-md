<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $a,p\in M[G]$, and suppose a [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\psi(x,y,p)$ defines exactly one $y$ for every $x\in a$. Apply the [Lévy reflection theorem](../../../../../../levy-reflection-theorem.md) to the formulas needed to express this assertion, choosing an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal.md) $\alpha$ with $a,p\in V_\alpha^{M[G]}$ such that

$$
M[G]\models\exists y\,\psi(x,y,p)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
V_\alpha^{M[G]}\models\exists y\,\psi(x,y,p)
$$

for every $x\in a$. Therefore every required value lies in the [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $V_\alpha^{M[G]}$.

The already established [axiom schema of separation](../../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md) forms the set

$$
b=\{y\in V_\alpha^{M[G]}:\exists x\in a\,\psi(x,y,p)\}.
$$

Functionality makes $b$ exactly the range of the definable function on $a$. This proves every instance of the [Axiom schema of replacement](../../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) in the [generic extension](../../../../../../generic-extension.md) $M[G]$.
