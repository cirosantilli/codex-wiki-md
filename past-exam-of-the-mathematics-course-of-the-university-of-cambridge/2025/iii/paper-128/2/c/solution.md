<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let

$$
d=\bigcup\{s:(s,f)\in G\}.
$$

For each [natural number](../../../../../../natural-number.md) $m$, conditions whose stem has length at least $m$ form a [dense subset of a forcing order](../../../../../../dense-subset-of-a-forcing-order.md), so the [generic filter](../../../../../../generic-filter.md) meets all of them and $d\in\omega^\omega$.

Fix $h\in\omega^\omega\cap M$. The set

$$
D_h=\{(s,f):f(n)>h(n)\text{ for every }n\}
$$

is dense: from $(s,f)$ replace $f$ by $n\mapsto\max\{f(n),h(n)+1\}$. Choose $(s,f)\in G\cap D_h$. Every stronger condition must put each newly added stem value $d(n)$ above $f(n)$, so

$$
d(n)>h(n)
$$

for every $n\geq|s|$. Thus $d$ is a [dominating real](../../../../../../dominating-real.md) over $M$.
