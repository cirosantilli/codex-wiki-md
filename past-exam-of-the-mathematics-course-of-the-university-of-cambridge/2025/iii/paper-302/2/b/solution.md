<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Identify $x=(x^0,x^1,x^2)$ with the real symmetric matrix

$$
X=\phi(x)=\begin{pmatrix}x^0+x^1&x^2\\x^2&x^0-x^1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det X=(x^0)^2-(x^1)^2-(x^2)^2.
$$

For $A\in SL(2,\mathbb R)$, define $X\mapsto AXA^T$. This linear action preserves $\det X$, so it defines $\rho(A)\in SO^+(1,2)$, and $\rho(AB)=\rho(A)\rho(B)$. Its kernel is $\{\pm I\}$ and it is onto, hence it is a two-to-one [covering homomorphism](../../../../../../covering-homomorphism.md) rather than an [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md). Consequently $PSL(2,\mathbb R)=SL(2,\mathbb R)/\{\pm I\}\cong SO^+(1,2)$.
