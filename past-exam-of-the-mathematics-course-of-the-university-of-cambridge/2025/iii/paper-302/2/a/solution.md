<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1)$, the defining condition for the $(1+2)$-dimensional [Lorentz group](../../../../../../lorentz-group.md) is

$$
\Lambda^T\eta\Lambda=\eta.
$$

Taking [determinants](../../../../../../determinant.md) gives $(\det\Lambda)^2=1$, hence $\det\Lambda=\pm1$. The norm of the zeroth column gives

$$
(\Lambda^0{}_0)^2-(\Lambda^1{}_0)^2-(\Lambda^2{}_0)^2=1,
$$

so $|\Lambda^0{}_0|\geq1$. The [Proper orthochronous Lorentz group](../../../../../../proper-orthochronous-lorentz-group.md) is the component with $\det\Lambda=1$ and $\Lambda^0{}_0\geq1$.
