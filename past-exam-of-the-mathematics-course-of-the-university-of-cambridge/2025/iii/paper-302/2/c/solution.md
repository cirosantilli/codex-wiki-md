↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The connected $(1+2)$-dimensional [Poincaré group](../../../../../../poincare-group.md) is

$$
ISO^+(1,2)=\mathbb R^{1,2}\rtimes SO^+(1,2),
\qquad
(a,\Lambda)(b,M)=(a+\Lambda b,\Lambda M).
$$

The [semidirect product](../../../../../../semidirect-product.md) records that Lorentz transformations act nontrivially on translations, which is what organizes momentum orbits. For a representative momentum $p$, its [little group](../../../../../../little-group.md) is the stabilizer $G_p=\{\Lambda\in SO^+(1,2):\Lambda p=p\}$. [Wigner's classification](../../../../../../wigner-s-classification.md) constructs irreducible particle representations by inducing a unitary irreducible representation of $G_p$ along the Lorentz orbit of $p$.
