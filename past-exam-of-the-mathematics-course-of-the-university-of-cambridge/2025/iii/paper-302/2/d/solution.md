<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a massive positive-energy orbit, choose $p_*=(m,0,0)$ with $m>0$. Its [little group](../../../../../../little-group.md) is $SO(2)$, whose [unitary irreducible representations](../../../../../../unitary-irreducible-representation.md) are the characters $e^{is\theta}$. Induction gives one-particle states $|p,s\rangle$ on the mass shell $p^2=m^2$, $p^0>0$, labeled by mass and spin. For the Poincare group itself, single-valuedness gives $s\in\mathbb Z$; its double cover permits half-integers, and its universal cover permits any real $s$, producing [anyonic spin](../../../../../../anyon.md) in $(2+1)$ dimensions.
