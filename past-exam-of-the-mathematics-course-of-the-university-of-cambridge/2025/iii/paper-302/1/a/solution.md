A [Lie group](../../../../../../lie-group.md) is a [smooth manifold](../../../../../../smooth-manifold.md) $G$ equipped with smooth maps $m:G\times G\to G$, $m(g,h)=gh$, and $\iota:G\to G$, $\iota(g)=g^{-1}$, for which multiplication is associative, there is an identity $e$, and every $g$ has the inverse $g^{-1}$. In equations,

$$
(gh)k=g(hk),\qquad eg=ge=g,\qquad gg^{-1}=g^{-1}g=e.
$$

**Thus the [group axioms](../../../../../../group-axioms.md) and the smooth structure are compatible.**
