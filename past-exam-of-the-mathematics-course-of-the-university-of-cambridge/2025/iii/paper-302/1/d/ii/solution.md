↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The defining two-dimensional [group representation](../../../../../../../group-representation.md) is [reducible](../../../../../../../reducible-representation.md), because the line $\mathbb Re_1$ is an [invariant subspace](../../../../../../../invariant-subspace.md):

$$
\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&1\end{pmatrix}e_1=ae_1.
$$

For $b\ne0$, the complementary line $\mathbb Re_2$ is not invariant, so this representation need not split as a [direct sum](../../../../../../../direct-sum.md) of one-dimensional representations.
