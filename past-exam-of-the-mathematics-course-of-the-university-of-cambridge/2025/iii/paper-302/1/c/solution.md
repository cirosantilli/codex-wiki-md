<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $g(x)=I+x^iT_i+O(x^2)$ in a [matrix representation](../../../../../../matrix-representation.md) and $[T_i,T_j]=c^k{}_{ij}T_k$. Direct multiplication gives

$$
g(y)^{-1}g(x)^{-1}g(y)g(x)
=I+y^ix^j[T_i,T_j]+O(3),
$$

so

$$
w^k=c^k{}_{ij}y^ix^j+O(3)=-c^k{}_{ij}x^iy^j+O(3).
$$

The cancellation of the constant and linear terms explains why the [group commutator](../../../../../../group-commutator.md) first detects the [Lie bracket](../../../../../../lie-bracket.md) at quadratic order.
