<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [special unitary group](../../../../../../special-unitary-group.md) is $SU(2)=\{U\in M_2(\mathbb C):U^\dagger U=I,\ \det U=1\}$. Differentiating these equations at the identity shows that its [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) is

$$
\mathfrak{su}(2)=\{X:X^\dagger=-X,\ \operatorname{tr}X=0\}.
$$

For $T_i=-i\sigma_i/2$, the [Pauli-matrix](../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md) identity $[\sigma_i,\sigma_j]=2i\epsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k$ gives

$$
[T_i,T_j]=\epsilon_{ijk}T_k,
$$

so the [structure constants](../../../../../../structure-constant-of-a-lie-algebra.md) in this basis are $c^k{}_{ij}=\epsilon_{ijk}$.
