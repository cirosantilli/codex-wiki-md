<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Since $-\theta T_1=i\theta\sigma_1/2$, the same [disentangling identity](../../../../../../disentangling-identity.md) applies in every [Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md). The rightmost $E_+$ kills $|\Lambda\rangle$, the leftmost $E_-$ kills $\langle\Lambda|$, and the middle factor acts by its highest weight. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\langle\Lambda|e^{-\theta d(T_1)}|\Lambda\rangle
=\left(\cos\frac\theta2\right)^\Lambda.}
$$
