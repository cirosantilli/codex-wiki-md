<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Cartan-Weyl basis](../../../../../../cartan-weyl-basis.md) obeys

$$
[H,E_\pm]=\pm2E_\pm,\qquad [E_+,E_-]=H.
$$

For a highest-weight vector $|\Lambda\rangle$, $E_+|\Lambda\rangle=0$ and $H|\Lambda\rangle=\Lambda|\Lambda\rangle$. Successive nonzero [lowerings](../../../../../../lowering-operator.md) give the [weight basis](../../../../../../weight-vector.md)

$$
|\Lambda-2k\rangle\ \propto\ E_-^k|\Lambda\rangle,
\qquad k=0,1,\ldots,\Lambda.
$$

Finite dimensionality requires the [highest weight](../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\Lambda$ to be a nonnegative integer. The weights are $\Lambda,\Lambda-2,\ldots,-\Lambda$, each with multiplicity one, so $\dim d_\Lambda=\Lambda+1$.
