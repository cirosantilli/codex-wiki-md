The representation with highest weight $[0,0,1]$ is the [dual representation](../../../../../../../dual-representation.md) of $d_1$. Its weights are the negatives of those of $d_1$:

$$
[0,0,1],\quad[0,1,-1],\quad[1,-1,0],\quad[-1,0,0].
$$

**Thus $\dim d_2=4$.**
