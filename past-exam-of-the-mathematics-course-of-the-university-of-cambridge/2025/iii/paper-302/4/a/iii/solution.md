The [Dynkin labels](../../../../../../../dynkin-label.md) of a weight $\lambda$ are

$$
[\lambda^1,\ldots,\lambda^r],
\qquad
\lambda^i=\langle\lambda,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle,
$$

so $\lambda=\sum_i\lambda^i\omega_i$.
