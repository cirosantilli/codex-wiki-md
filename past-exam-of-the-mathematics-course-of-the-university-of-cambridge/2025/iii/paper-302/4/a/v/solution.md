The [highest weight of a representation](../../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) is the weight $\Lambda$ of a nonzero [weight vector](../../../../../../../weight-vector.md) annihilated by every positive-root operator. Every other weight is obtained from $\Lambda$ by subtracting a nonnegative integer combination of [simple roots](../../../../../../../simple-root.md). A finite-dimensional irreducible representation of a complex semisimple Lie algebra is determined by its dominant integral highest weight.
