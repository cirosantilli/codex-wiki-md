<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The tensor product of the defining representation and its dual is the endomorphism representation. Splitting an endomorphism into its [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) and traceless part gives

$$
d_1\otimes d_2\cong\mathbf4\otimes\overline{\mathbf4}
=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf{15}.
$$

In [Dynkin labels](../../../../../../dynkin-label.md), the two irreducible summands have highest weights $[0,0,0]$ and $[1,0,1]$.
