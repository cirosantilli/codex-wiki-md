<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Disconnected nodes in a [Dynkin diagram](../../../../../../../dynkin-diagram.md) represent orthogonal roots, so $\angle(\alpha_1,\alpha_3)=90^\circ$ in all three cases. A single edge gives $120^\circ$, hence $\angle(\alpha_1,\alpha_2)=120^\circ$ and, for $A_3$, also $\angle(\alpha_2,\alpha_3)=120^\circ$. The double edge in both $B_3$ and $C_3$ gives $\angle(\alpha_2,\alpha_3)=135^\circ$.
