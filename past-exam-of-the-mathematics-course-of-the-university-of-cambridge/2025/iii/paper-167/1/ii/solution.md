<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose algebra generators $f_1,\ldots,f_r$ of the finitely generated [coordinate ring](../../../../../../coordinate-ring.md) $k[G]$. The right regular action is locally finite: writing

$$
\Delta(f_i)=\sum_j f_{ij}\otimes h_{ij}
$$

shows that every right translate of $f_i$ lies in the finite span of the $f_{ij}$. Hence all the $f_i$ lie in some finite-dimensional translation-stable subspace $V\subseteq k[G]$.

This gives a rational representation $\rho:G\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$. If $g\in\ker\rho$, then $f_i(xg)=f_i(x)$ for every $i$ and $x$. Setting $x=e$ gives $f_i(g)=f_i(e)$ for every algebra generator, and therefore for every regular function on $G$. Regular functions separate closed points of an affine variety, so $g=e$. Thus $\rho$ is a [faithful representation of an affine algebraic group](../../../../../../faithful-representation-of-an-affine-algebraic-group.md).
