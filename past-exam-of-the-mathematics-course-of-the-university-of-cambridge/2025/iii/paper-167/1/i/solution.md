An [affine algebraic group](../../../../../../linear-algebraic-group.md) over $k$ is an [affine variety](../../../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md) $G$ equipped with multiplication $G\times G\to G$, inversion $G\to G$, and an identity element satisfying the group axioms, with multiplication and inversion both [regular maps](../../../../../../morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md). Dually, the [coordinate ring](../../../../../../coordinate-ring.md) $k[G]$ is a commutative [Hopf algebra](../../../../../../hopf-algebra.md): multiplication on $G$ induces the comultiplication $\Delta:k[G]\to k[G]\otimes k[G]$, inversion induces the antipode, and evaluation at the identity is the counit.
