<h1 id="4/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Put $i_0=0$ and $d_j=i_j-i_{j-1}$. The stabilizer

$$
P_I=\operatorname{Stab}_G(F_{i_1}<\cdots<F_{i_r})
$$

is the standard [parabolic subgroup](../../../../../../parabolic-subgroup.md) containing $B$ obtained by allowing arbitrary changes of basis inside the successive blocks. Its [Levi subgroup](../../../../../../levi-subgroup.md) is

$$
L_I\cong
\operatorname{GL}_{d_1}\times\cdots\times
\operatorname{GL}_{d_r}\times
\operatorname{Sp}_{2(n-i_r)}.
$$

The fiber of $\pi_I$ over the displayed partial flag is $P_I/B$. Choosing a complete refinement amounts to choosing complete flags in every quotient $F_{i_j}/F_{i_{j-1}}$ and a complete isotropic flag in $F_{i_r}^{\perp}/F_{i_r}$. Hence

$$
\pi_I^{-1}(F_I)
\cong\mathcal B(L_I),
$$

the [flag variety of an algebraic group](../../../../../../generalized-flag-variety.md) $L_I$; explicitly it is the product of the complete flag varieties of the listed general linear and symplectic factors.
