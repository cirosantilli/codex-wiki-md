<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the rational [Serre spectral sequence](../../../../../../serre-spectral-sequence.md) to the path-loop fibration of $K(\mathbb Z,3)$. Its fiber is

$$
\Omega K(\mathbb Z,3)\simeq K(\mathbb Z,2)\simeq\mathbb{CP}^{\infty},
$$

whose rational [cohomology ring](../../../../../../cohomology-ring.md) is $\mathbb Q[c]$ with $|c|=2$. Since the path space is contractible, $c$ must transgress to a nonzero class $x\in H^3(K(\mathbb Z,3);\mathbb Q)$. Multiplicativity gives

$$
d_3(c^m)=m c^{m-1}x.
$$

Over $\mathbb Q$ these differentials pair and kill every positive-degree class except $x$, while graded commutativity gives $x^2=0$. Hence

$$
H^i(K(\mathbb Z,3);\mathbb Q)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Q,&i=0,3,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
