<h1 id="1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Consider a lifting square with $\Lambda_1^3\hookrightarrow\Delta^3$ on the left, $Q\to N(\mathcal C)$ on the right, and prescribed bottom simplex $\sigma:\Delta^3\to N(\mathcal C)$. Since $Q$ is a [quasicategory](../../../../../../quasicategory.md), the top horn has some filler $\widetilde\sigma:\Delta^3\to Q$.

The two simplices $p\widetilde\sigma$ and $\sigma$ of the [nerve of a category](../../../../../../nerve-category-theory.md) restrict to the same inner horn. Inner horns in a category's nerve have unique fillers, because composition in a category is defined and unique. Hence $p\widetilde\sigma=\sigma$, so $\widetilde\sigma$ is the required lift.
