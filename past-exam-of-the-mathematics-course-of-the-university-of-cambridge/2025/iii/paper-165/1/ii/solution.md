↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [quasicategory](../../../../../../quasicategory.md) is a [simplicial set](../../../../../../simplicial-set.md) having the right lifting property against every [inner horn](../../../../../../inner-horn.md) inclusion

$$
\Lambda_i^n\hookrightarrow\Delta^n,
\qquad 0<i<n.
$$

A [Kan complex](../../../../../../kan-complex.md) has the right lifting property against every [simplicial horn](../../../../../../simplicial-horn.md) inclusion, including the two outer horns $i=0,n$.
