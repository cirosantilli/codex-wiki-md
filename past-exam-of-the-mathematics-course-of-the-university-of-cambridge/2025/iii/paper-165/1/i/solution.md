The [simplex category](../../../../../../simplex-category.md) $\Delta$ has objects $[n]=\{0<1<\cdots<n\}$ for $n\geq0$ and order-preserving maps as morphisms. A [simplicial set](../../../../../../simplicial-set.md) is a [functor](../../../../../../functor.md) $X:\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set}$.

The [standard simplex](../../../../../../standard-simplex.md) is the representable simplicial set

$$
\Delta^n_m=\operatorname{Hom}_\Delta([m],[n]).
$$

For $0\leq i\leq n$, the [simplicial horn](../../../../../../simplicial-horn.md) $\Lambda_i^n\subseteq\Delta^n$ is the union of the images of all coface maps $\Delta^{n-1}\to\Delta^n$ except the face opposite vertex $i$.
