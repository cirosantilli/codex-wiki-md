<h1 id="2/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Regard the given [short exact sequence](../../../../../../short-exact-sequence.md) as a degreewise short exact sequence of [chain complexes](../../../../../../chain-complex.md) concentrated in degree $n$. The inverse functor in the [Dold–Kan correspondence](../../../../../../dold-kan-correspondence.md) is exact, so it produces a degreewise short exact sequence of [simplicial abelian groups](../../../../../../simplicial-abelian-group.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow K(A_1,n)\longrightarrow K(A_2,n)\longrightarrow K(A_3,n)\longrightarrow0.
$$

The last map is degreewise surjective and hence a Kan fibration. Its strict fiber is $K(A_1,n)$, and a strict fiber of a fibration computes the homotopy fiber. This proves the asserted [homotopy fiber sequence](../../../../../../homotopy-fiber-sequence.md) of pointed [Kan complexes](../../../../../../kan-complex.md).
