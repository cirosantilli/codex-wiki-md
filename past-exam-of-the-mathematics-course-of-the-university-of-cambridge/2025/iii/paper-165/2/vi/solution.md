<h1 id="2/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Products of [Eilenberg–MacLane spaces](../../../../../../eilenberg-maclane-space.md) satisfy

$$
K(\mathbb Z/4,3)\times K(\mathbb Z/5,3)
\simeq K(\mathbb Z/4\oplus\mathbb Z/5,3)
\cong K(\mathbb Z/20,3),
$$

where the last isomorphism uses the [Chinese remainder theorem](../../../../../../chinese-remainder-theorem.md). This space is $2$-connected, so the [Hurewicz theorem](../../../../../../hurewicz-theorem.md) identifies

$$
H_3(-;\mathbb Z)\cong\pi_3(-)\cong\mathbb Z/20.
$$

The assumed surjection on $H_3$ is an isomorphism because the group is finite. Hence $f$ is an isomorphism on $\pi_3$; all other homotopy groups of the source and target vanish. Thus $f$ is a weak homotopy equivalence, and the [Whitehead theorem](../../../../../../whitehead-theorem.md) for [Kan complexes](../../../../../../kan-complex.md) makes it a [homotopy equivalence](../../../../../../homotopy-equivalence.md).
