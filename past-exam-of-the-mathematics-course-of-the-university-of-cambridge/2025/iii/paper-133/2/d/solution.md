<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Suppose $G=A*B$ were a nontrivial [free product](../../../../../../free-product.md). Its [Bass-Serre tree](../../../../../../bass-serre-tree.md) action has trivial edge stabilizers and no global fixed vertex. Part c makes $a$ elliptic. Since $a$ has infinite order, the fixed set of every nonzero power $a^r$ is a single vertex: it is nonempty, while fixing two vertices would fix the intervening edge and put the infinite-order element $a^r$ in a trivial edge stabilizer.

Let this vertex be $v$. The relation $ba^mb^{-1}=a^n$ gives

$$
b\operatorname{Fix}(a^m)=\operatorname{Fix}(a^n),
$$

and both sides are the singleton $\{v\}$. Thus $b$ also fixes $v$. Since $a$ and $b$ generate the [Baumslag-Solitar group](../../../../../../baumslag-solitar-group.md), the entire group fixes $v$, contradicting the Bass-Serre action of a nontrivial free product. Hence no such decomposition exists.
