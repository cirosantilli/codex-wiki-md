<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppose that $a$ acted as a [hyperbolic isometry of a tree](../../../../../../hyperbolic-isometry-of-a-tree.md), with [translation length](../../../../../../translation-length.md) $\tau>0$. Nonzero powers have the same axis and

$$
\ell(a^m)=m\tau,
\qquad
\ell(a^n)=n\tau.
$$

Translation length is invariant under conjugacy, whereas the defining relation in the [Baumslag-Solitar group](../../../../../../baumslag-solitar-group.md) says that $a^m$ and $a^n$ are conjugate. Hence $m\tau=n\tau$, contradicting $m\ne n$. Therefore $a$ acts elliptically in every combinatorial tree action.
