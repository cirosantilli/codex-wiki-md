<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $x,y\in X$, the [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) and the fact that $\phi$ is an [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md) give

$$
\begin{aligned}
|d_\phi(x)-d_\phi(y)|
&=|d(x,\phi x)-d(y,\phi y)|\\
&\leq d(x,y)+d(\phi x,\phi y)\\
&=2d(x,y).
\end{aligned}
$$

**Thus the [displacement function](../../../../../../displacement-function.md) is $2$-Lipschitz, hence [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md).**
