<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose $y_n\in X$ with $d_\phi(y_n)\to0$. By the [cocompact group action](../../../../../../cocompact-group-action.md), there is a compact set $K\subseteq X$ whose translates cover $X$. Choose $\gamma_n\in\Gamma$ such that

$$
x_n:=\gamma_ny_n\in K,
\qquad
\phi_n:=\gamma_n\phi\gamma_n^{-1}.
$$

Isometric invariance gives

$$
d_{\phi_n}(x_n)
=d(\gamma_ny_n,\gamma_n\phi y_n)
=d_\phi(y_n)\longrightarrow0.
$$

For a fixed basepoint $x_0$, compactness of $K$ gives $C=\max_{x\in K}d(x_0,x)<\infty$, so $d(x_0,x_n)\leq C$.
