<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take $G=\mathbb Z^2$ with the standard generating set $S=\{(1,0),(0,1)\}$, and let

$$
H=\langle(1,1)\rangle.
$$

Intrinsic distance in $H$ between $0$ and $(n,n)$ is $|n|$, while its ambient [word metric](../../../../../../word-metric.md) distance is $2|n|$, so $H$ is [quasi-isometrically embedded](../../../../../../quasi-isometrically-embedded-subgroup.md). However, the ambient geodesic from $(0,0)$ to $(n,n)$ that first travels to $(n,0)$ and then to $(n,n)$ contains $(n,0)$. Its distance from the diagonal subgroup $H$ is $n$. No uniform $K$ can contain every such geodesic in the $K$-neighborhood of $H$, so $H$ is not a [quasiconvex subgroup](../../../../../../quasiconvex-subgroup.md).
