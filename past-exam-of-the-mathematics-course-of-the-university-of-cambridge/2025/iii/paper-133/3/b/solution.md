<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $h\in H$ and let

$$
1=x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_N=h
$$

be a geodesic in the [Cayley graph](../../../../../../cayley-graph.md) $\operatorname{Cay}_S(G)$. By $K$-quasiconvexity choose $h_i\in H$ with $d_S(x_i,h_i)\leq K$, taking $h_0=1$ and $h_N=h$. Then

$$
|h_i^{-1}h_{i+1}|_S
\leq d_S(h_i,x_i)+1+d_S(x_{i+1},h_{i+1})
\leq2K+1.
$$

The elements $h_i^{-1}h_{i+1}$ telescope to $h$, so the finite set

$$
U=H\cap\{g:|g|_S\leq2K+1\}
$$

generates $H$.

Moreover $|h|_U\leq|h|_S$, while $|h|_S\leq(2K+1)|h|_U$. Thus the inclusion $(H,d_U)\to(G,d_S)$ is a [quasi-isometric embedding](../../../../../../quasi-isometric-embedding.md), and $H$ is quasi-isometrically embedded.
