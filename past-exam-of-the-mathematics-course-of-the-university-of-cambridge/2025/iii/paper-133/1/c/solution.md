<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [infinite dihedral group](../../../../../../infinite-dihedral-group.md) is $D_\infty\cong C_2*C_2$, with factors $A=\langle a\rangle$ and $B=\langle b\rangle$. Its [Bass-Serre tree](../../../../../../bass-serre-tree.md) has vertex set

$$
D_\infty/A\sqcup D_\infty/B
$$

and one edge indexed by each $g\in D_\infty$, joining $gA$ to $gB$. Since both factors have order two, every vertex has degree two. The connected tree is therefore a bi-infinite line.

The action is cocompact, and its vertex stabilizers are the finite conjugates of $A$ and $B$, so it is proper. By the [Milnor–Švarc lemma](../../../../../../milnor-svarc-lemma.md), an orbit map from $D_\infty$ with a [word metric](../../../../../../word-metric.md) to this line is a [quasi-isometry](../../../../../../quasi-isometry.md). A simplicial bi-infinite line is quasi-isometric to $\mathbb R$, hence so is $D_\infty$.
