<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Varying the [Polyakov action](../../../../../../polyakov-action.md) with respect to the worldsheet metric gives

$$
T_{\alpha\beta}=T\left(\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X
-\frac12g_{\alpha\beta}g^{\gamma\delta}\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X\right)=0.
$$

In [conformal gauge](../../../../../../conformal-gauge.md), variation of $X$ gives the wave equation

$$
(\partial_\tau^2-\partial_\sigma^2)X^\mu=0,
$$

while the [Virasoro constraints](../../../../../../virasoro-constraint.md) become $\dot X\cdot X'=0$ and $\dot X^2+X'^2=0$. The boundary variation is $-T\int d\tau,X'\cdot\delta X$ at each endpoint. It vanishes through a [Neumann boundary condition](../../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md) $X'^\mu=0$, a [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) $\delta X^\mu=0$, or a direction-by-direction mixture of the two.
