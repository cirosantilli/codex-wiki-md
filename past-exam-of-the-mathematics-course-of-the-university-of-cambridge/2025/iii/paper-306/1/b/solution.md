<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Translations $\delta X^\mu=a^\mu$ and Lorentz transformations $\delta X^\mu=\omega^\mu{}_{\nu}X^\nu$ leave the action invariant because it depends only on derivatives and Lorentz contractions. [Noether theorem](../../../../../../noether-theorem.md) gives the stated currents, whose equations are $\partial_\alpha P^{\alpha\mu}=0$ and $\partial_\alpha J^{\alpha\mu\nu}=0$. For $0\leq\sigma\leq\pi$, the conserved open-string charges are

$$
P^\mu=T\int_0^\pi d\sigma\,\dot X^\mu,
\qquad
J^{\mu\nu}=T\int_0^\pi d\sigma\,(X^\mu\dot X^\nu-X^\nu\dot X^\mu).
$$

The endpoint fluxes vanish for the allowed boundary conditions.
