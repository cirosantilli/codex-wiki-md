<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The string embedding coordinates form a [nonlinear sigma model](../../../../../../nonlinear-sigma-model.md) whose target-space metric is the spacetime metric $G_{\mu\nu}$. A consistent quantum string background must preserve worldsheet [Weyl invariance](../../../../../../weyl-transformation.md), so all sigma-model beta functions must vanish, and the total matter-plus-ghost central charge must cancel. For a bosonic string with only a metric, this requires $D=26$ and, to leading order in $\alpha'$, $\beta^G_{\mu\nu}=\alpha'R_{\mu\nu}+O(\alpha'^2)=0$; thus the target metric must be Ricci-flat at leading order.
