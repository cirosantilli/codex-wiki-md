<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let

$$
\perp^\mu{}_\alpha=\delta^\mu{}_\alpha+n^\mu n_\alpha,
\qquad n^\mu n_\mu=-1.
$$

The identity decomposes as

$$
\delta^\mu{}_\alpha=\perp^\mu{}_\alpha-n^\mu n_\alpha.
$$

Applying this decomposition to both indices of $T_{\mu\nu}$ gives

$$
T_{\alpha\beta}
=(\perp^\mu{}_\alpha-n^\mu n_\alpha)
(\perp^\nu{}_\beta-n^\nu n_\beta)T_{\mu\nu}.
$$

The four terms are, from the definitions, $S_{\alpha\beta}$, $j_\alpha n_\beta$, $n_\alpha j_\beta$, and $\rho n_\alpha n_\beta$. Hence the [3+1 decomposition of the stress-energy tensor](../../../../../../3-plus-1-decomposition-of-the-stress-energy-tensor.md) is

$$
\boxed{
T_{\alpha\beta}
=\rho n_\alpha n_\beta+j_\alpha n_\beta
+n_\alpha j_\beta+S_{\alpha\beta}}.
$$

It also makes explicit that

$$
n^\alpha j_\alpha=0,\qquad
n^\alpha S_{\alpha\beta}=n^\beta S_{\alpha\beta}=0.
$$
