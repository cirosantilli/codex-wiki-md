<h1 id="3/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In vacuum, neglecting $\widetilde A_{mn}\widetilde A^{mn}$ leaves

$$
\boxed{\frac{dK}{dt}=\frac13K^2}.
$$

Separating variables and imposing $K(t_0)=K_0>0$ gives

$$
-\frac1{K(t)}+\frac1{K_0}=\frac{t-t_0}{3},
$$

hence

$$
\boxed{
K(t)=\frac{K_0}
{1-\frac{K_0}{3}(t-t_0)}}.
$$

The denominator vanishes at

$$
\boxed{t_\infty=t_0+\frac3{K_0}}.
$$

This [finite-time blow-up of mean curvature in geodesic slicing](../../../../../../../finite-time-blow-up-of-mean-curvature-in-geodesic-slicing.md) is another direct expression of the gauge's singularity problem.
