<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If no mass is accreted, $M$ is constant and $\Sigma\sim M/R^2$. The viscous-time estimate becomes $t\propto R^{2-m+2n}$, while $J\sim M(GM_\star R)^{1/2}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{R\propto t^{1/(2-m+2n)}},
\qquad
\boxed{J\propto t^{1/[2(2-m+2n)]}}.
$$

The central torque supplies the [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) that allows the fixed disk mass to spread.
