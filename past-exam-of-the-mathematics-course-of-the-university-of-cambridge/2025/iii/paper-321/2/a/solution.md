<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The viscous time is

$$
t\sim\frac{R^2}{\bar\nu}
\sim A^{-1}R^{2-m}\Sigma^{-n},
\qquad \Sigma\sim\frac M{R^2}.
$$

With zero stellar torque, total [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is conserved and $J\sim M(GM_\star R)^{1/2}$, so $M\propto R^{-1/2}$. Hence

$$
t\propto R^{2-m+5n/2},
$$

and

$$
\boxed{R\propto t^{2/(4-2m+5n)}},
\qquad
\boxed{M\propto t^{-1/(4-2m+5n)}}.
$$

Accreting material loses specific angular momentum; conservation requires a diminishing fraction of the disk to spread outward and carry that angular momentum.
