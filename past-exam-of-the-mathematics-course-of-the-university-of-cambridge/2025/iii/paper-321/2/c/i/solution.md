<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $r=r_0x^2$, $\Sigma=\Sigma_0x^{-3}S$, and $\bar\nu=\bar\nu_0x^{2m-3n}S^n$. Then $\partial_r=(2r_0x)^{-1}\partial_x$. Substitution into the [viscous evolution of an accretion disk](../../../../../../../viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md) equation, with $\tau=3\bar\nu_0t/(4r_0^2)$, cancels the common dimensional factors and gives

$$
\boxed{\partial_\tau S=\partial_x^2\left(x^{2m-3n-2}S^{n+1}\right)}.
$$
