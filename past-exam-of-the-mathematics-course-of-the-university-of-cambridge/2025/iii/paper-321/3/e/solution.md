<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For $\nu=0$,

$$
s[s^2+\varpi^2(1+n^2)]=0.
$$

An exponentially growing root exists exactly when $1+n^2<0$, or

$$
\boxed{\Omega^2+N^2<0}.
$$

**Thus an adverse radial entropy gradient $N^2<0$ must overcome the epicyclic restoring effect of rotation. Rotation stabilizes gradients with $-\Omega^2<N^2<0$.**
