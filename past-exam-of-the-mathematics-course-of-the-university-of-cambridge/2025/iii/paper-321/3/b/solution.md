<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\mathbf u_0=-\tfrac32\Omega x\mathbf e_y$, advection and viscosity vanish, while its [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md) cancels $-\nabla\Phi_t$; constant pressure and $\theta=0$ complete the equilibrium. For axisymmetric perturbations,

$$
\partial_tu_x'-2\Omega u_y'=-\rho_0^{-1}\partial_xP'-N^2\theta'+\nu\nabla^2u_x',
$$



$$
\partial_tu_y'+\frac12\Omega u_x'=\nu\nabla^2u_y',\qquad
\partial_tu_z'=-\rho_0^{-1}\partial_zP'+\nu\nabla^2u_z',
$$



$$
\partial_t\theta'=u_x',\qquad
\partial_xu_x'+\partial_zu_z'=0.
$$
