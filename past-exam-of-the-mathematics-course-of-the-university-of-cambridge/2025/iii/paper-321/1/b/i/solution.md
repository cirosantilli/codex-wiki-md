<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The steady disk should end at the [innermost stable circular orbit](../../../../../../../innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md), $r_{\rm in}=3r_S$. Inside it, matter plunges inward on an orbital timescale and cannot maintain the nearly circular shear flow assumed by the viscous-disk equations. If the plunging region communicates negligible stress back across the ISCO, the natural boundary condition is the [zero-torque inner boundary condition](../../../../../../../zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathcal G(r_{\rm in})=0}.
$$
