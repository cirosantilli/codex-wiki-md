<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [Paczyński-Wiita potential](../../../../../../paczynski-wiita-potential.md), circular balance gives

$$
r\Omega^2=\frac{d\Phi}{dr}=\frac{GM}{(r-r_S)^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Omega(r)=\frac{\sqrt{GM}}{r^{1/2}(r-r_S)}},
\qquad
\boxed{h(r)=r^2\Omega=\frac{\sqrt{GMr^3}}{r-r_S}}.
$$

The squared [radial epicyclic frequency](../../../../../../radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) is

$$
\kappa_r^2=\frac1{r^3}\frac{d(h^2)}{dr}
=\frac{GM(r-3r_S)}{r(r-r_S)^3}.
$$

Circular orbits change from stable to unstable where $\kappa_r^2$ vanishes, so

$$
\boxed{r_{\rm ISCO}=3r_S}.
$$
