A [discrete valuation](../../../../../../discrete-valuation.md) on a field $K$ is a surjective group homomorphism

$$
v:K^\times\longrightarrow\mathbb Z
$$

satisfying

$$
v(x+y)\geq\min\{v(x),v(y)\}
$$

whenever $x+y\ne0$. Its [discrete valuation ring](../../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md) is

$$
\boxed{R_v=\{0\}\cup\{x\in K^\times:v(x)\geq0\}}.
$$

One standard characterization defines a [Dedekind domain](../../../../../../dedekind-domain.md) as a Noetherian integrally closed domain of Krull dimension one. Equivalently, all its localizations at nonzero prime ideals are discrete valuation rings.
