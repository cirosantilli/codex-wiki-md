<h1 id="1/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume tensor products of two nonzero modules never vanish. If $\mathfrak m\ne\mathfrak n$ were distinct [maximal ideals](../../../../../../../maximal-ideal.md), then $\mathfrak m+\mathfrak n=R$ and

$$
(R/\mathfrak m)\otimes_R(R/\mathfrak n)
\cong R/(\mathfrak m+\mathfrak n)=0,
$$

although both residue fields are nonzero. Hence $R$ has one maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ and is a [local ring](../../../../../../../local-ring.md).

For every module $M$,

$$
(R/\mathfrak m)\otimes_RM\cong M/\mathfrak mM.
$$

If $\mathfrak mM=M$, this tensor product vanishes. Since $R/\mathfrak m\ne0$, the assumed property forces $M=0$. Thus condition (a) implies condition (b).
