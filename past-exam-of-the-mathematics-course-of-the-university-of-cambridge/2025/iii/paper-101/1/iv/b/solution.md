<h1 id="1/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Conversely, assume condition (b), and let $M,N$ be nonzero. The stated property gives

$$
M/\mathfrak mM\ne0,
\qquad
N/\mathfrak mN\ne0.
$$

These are nonzero [vector spaces](../../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) over the [residue field](../../../../../../../residue-field.md) $k=R/\mathfrak m$, so their tensor product over $k$ is nonzero. Associativity and base change give

$$
\begin{aligned}
(M\otimes_RN)\otimes_Rk
&\cong(M\otimes_Rk)\otimes_k(N\otimes_Rk)\\
&\cong(M/\mathfrak mM)\otimes_k(N/\mathfrak mN)\ne0.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Therefore $M\otimes_RN\ne0$. This proves the reverse implication and the [local tensor nonvanishing criterion](../../../../../../../local-tensor-nonvanishing-criterion.md).**
