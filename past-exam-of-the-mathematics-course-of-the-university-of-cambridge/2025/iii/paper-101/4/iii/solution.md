<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The polynomial ring $\mathbb C[X,Y]$ is a two-dimensional [unique factorization domain](../../../../../../unique-factorization-domain.md). Let $\mathfrak p$ be a prime ideal containing $(f,g)$. Since $f\ne0$, the prime $\mathfrak p$ is nonzero. If it were not maximal, it would have height one, so the [height-one prime in a unique factorization domain](../../../../../../height-one-prime-in-a-unique-factorization-domain.md) would give $\mathfrak p=(h)$ for an irreducible polynomial $h$. Then $h$ would divide both $f$ and $g$, contrary to the hypothesis. Every prime of

$$
R=\mathbb C[X,Y]/(f,g)
$$

is therefore maximal.

The [Hilbert basis theorem](../../../../../../hilbert-basis-theorem.md) makes $R$ Noetherian, and it has Krull dimension zero by the preceding paragraph. The [Noetherian dimension-zero criterion for an Artinian ring](../../../../../../noetherian-dimension-zero-criterion-for-an-artinian-ring.md) now shows that

$$
\boxed{R\text{ is Artinian}}.
$$
