<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\mathfrak m$ be maximal in $\mathbb Q[T_1,\ldots,T_n]$ and put

$$
K=\mathbb Q[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/\mathfrak m.
$$

The [Zariski lemma](../../../../../../zariski-s-lemma.md) makes $K$ a finite extension of $\mathbb Q$. Base change gives

$$
\mathbb C[T_1,\ldots,T_n]/\mathfrak m\mathbb C[T_1,\ldots,T_n]
\cong\mathbb C\otimes_{\mathbb Q}K.
$$

This ring is nonzero because the field extension makes $\mathbb C$ a [faithfully flat module](../../../../../../faithfully-flat-module.md) over $\mathbb Q$. Choose a maximal ideal $\mathfrak n$ of the quotient, or equivalently a maximal ideal of $\mathbb C[T_1,\ldots,T_n]$ containing $\mathfrak m\mathbb C[T_1,\ldots,T_n]$. Its contraction to the rational polynomial ring contains $\mathfrak m$ and is proper, so maximality of $\mathfrak m$ forces

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak n\cap\mathbb Q[T_1,\ldots,T_n]=\mathfrak m.}
$$
