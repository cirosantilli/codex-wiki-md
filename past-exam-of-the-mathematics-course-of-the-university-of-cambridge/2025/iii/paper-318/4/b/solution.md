<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $e_m$ denote the $m$th [elementary symmetric polynomial](../../../../../../elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md) of $t_{i+1},\ldots,t_{i+k-1}$. Expanding both sides of the [Marsden identity](../../../../../../marsden-identity.md) in powers of $x$ and equating the coefficient of $x^{k-1-m}$ gives

$$
\boxed{a_{m,i}=
\frac{e_m(t_{i+1},\ldots,t_{i+k-1})}{\binom{k-1}{m}}},
\qquad0\leq m\leq k-1.
$$

**Thus $a_{0,i}=1$, while $a_{1,i}$ and $a_{2,i}$ are respectively the means of the interior knots and of their pairwise products.**
