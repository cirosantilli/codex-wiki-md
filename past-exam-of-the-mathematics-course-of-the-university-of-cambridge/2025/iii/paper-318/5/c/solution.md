<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $f=\mathbf1_{[-\pi,\pi)}$, its $2\pi$-periodization is one almost everywhere. The refinement mask is the periodic function equal to one on $[-\pi/2,\pi/2)$ and zero on the rest of $[-\pi,\pi)$. Fourier inversion gives the [Shannon scaling function](../../../../../../shannon-scaling-function.md)

$$
\boxed{\phi(x)=\frac{\sin\pi x}{\pi x}}.
$$

Since $m(t)=\tfrac12\sum_na_ne^{-int}$, its Fourier coefficients give

$$
\boxed{a_0=1,\qquad a_n=\frac{2\sin(n\pi/2)}{\pi n}\quad(n\ne0)}.
$$
