An [orthonormal wavelet](../../../../../../orthonormal-wavelet.md) is $\psi\in L^2(\mathbb R)$ such that $\{2^{j/2}\psi(2^jx-k)\}_{j,k\in\mathbb Z}$ is an orthonormal basis. A [multiresolution analysis](../../../../../../multiresolution-analysis.md) is a nested family of closed spaces $V_j$ with trivial intersection, dense union, dyadic scaling, integer-translation invariance of $V_0$, and a generator $\phi$ whose integer translates form an orthonormal basis of $V_0$. The [Meyer-Mallat theorem](../../../../../../meyer-mallat-theorem.md) says every such analysis has an orthonormal wavelet whose translates span $V_{j+1}\ominus V_j$.
