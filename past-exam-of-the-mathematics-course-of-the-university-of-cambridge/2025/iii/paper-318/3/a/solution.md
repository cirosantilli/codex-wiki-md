<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The explicit [divided difference](../../../../../../divided-difference.md) formula makes $M_i(t)$ a finite linear combination of $(t_j-t)_+^{k-1}$. It is therefore polynomial of degree at most $k-1$ between knots and globally $C^{k-2}$. For $t<t_i$, the nodal data come from a polynomial of degree $k-1$, whose order-$k$ divided difference vanishes; for $t\geq t_{i+k}$ all truncated powers vanish. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{supp}M_i=[t_i,t_{i+k}]},
$$

and normalization does not change the degree, smoothness, knots, or support.
