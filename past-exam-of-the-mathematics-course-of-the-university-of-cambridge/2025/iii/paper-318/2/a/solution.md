↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substituting the [Fourier coefficient](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md)s into the [Fourier partial sum](../../../../../../fourier-partial-sum.md) and summing the finite geometric series gives

$$
s_n(f,x)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb T}f(t)\sum_{k=-n}^ne^{ik(x-t)}dt
=\frac1\pi\int_{\mathbb T}D_n(x-t)f(t)dt,
$$

where

$$
\boxed{D_n(u)=\frac{\sin((n+\tfrac12)u)}{2\sin(u/2)}}.
$$
