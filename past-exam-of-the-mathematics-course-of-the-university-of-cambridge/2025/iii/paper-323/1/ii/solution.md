<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a fixed input, the [Holevo–Helstrom theorem](../../../../../../holevo-helstrom-theorem.md) gives the optimal error for the two output states. Optimizing the input and [quantum ancilla](../../../../../../quantum-ancilla.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{
P_{\mathrm{err}}^{\mathrm{anc}}
=\frac12\left(1-\left\|pT_1-(1-p)T_2\right\|_\diamond\right)}.
$$

The stabilization in the [diamond norm](../../../../../../diamond-norm.md) is exactly the optimization over ancillary systems. Without an ancilla the same argument instead gives

$$
\boxed{
P_{\mathrm{err}}^{\mathrm{no\ anc}}
=\frac12\left(1-\left\|pT_1-(1-p)T_2\right\|_1\right)},
$$

where $\|\cdot\|_1$ is the [induced trace norm](../../../../../../induced-trace-norm.md).
