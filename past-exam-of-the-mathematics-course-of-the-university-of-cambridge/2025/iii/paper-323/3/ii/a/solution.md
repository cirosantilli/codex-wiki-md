<h1 id="3/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A real function $f$ on $I=[0,1]$ is an [operator convex function](../../../../../../../operator-convex-function.md) when, for all [Hermitian operators](../../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) $X,Y$ whose spectra lie in $I$ and every $0\leq\lambda\leq1$,

$$
\boxed{
f(\lambda X+(1-\lambda)Y)
\preceq\lambda f(X)+(1-\lambda)f(Y)},
$$

where $\preceq$ is the [Loewner order](../../../../../../../loewner-order.md). Reversing the inequality defines an [operator concave function](../../../../../../../operator-concave-function.md), equivalently $-f$ is operator convex.
