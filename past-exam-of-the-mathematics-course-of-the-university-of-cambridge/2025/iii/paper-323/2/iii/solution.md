<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Each rank-one [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) is

$$
|z\rangle\langle z|
=\frac1d\sum_{j,k}z_jz_k^*|j\rangle\langle k|,
\qquad
|z^*\rangle\langle z^*|
=\frac1d\sum_{j',k'}z_{j'}^*z_{k'}|j'\rangle\langle k'|.
$$

Taking their [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md) and averaging over the $4^d$ independent choices of the fourth roots of unity gives

$$
\boxed{
\sigma
=\frac1{4^dd^2}
\sum_{j,k,j',k'}
\left(\sum_z z_jz_k^*z_{j'}^*z_{k'}\right)
|j\rangle\langle k|\otimes|j'\rangle\langle k'|}.
$$

This is the claimed expansion in [matrix elements](../../../../../../matrix-element.md).
