A bipartite [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) is a [separable quantum state](../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md) when it has a [convex combination](../../../../../../convex-combination.md) decomposition

$$
\rho_{AB}=\sum_rq_r\,\rho_r^A\otimes\rho_r^B,
\qquad q_r\geq0,\qquad\sum_rq_r=1,
$$

into [product states](../../../../../../product-state.md). A state for which no such decomposition exists is an [entangled state](../../../../../../entangled-state.md).
