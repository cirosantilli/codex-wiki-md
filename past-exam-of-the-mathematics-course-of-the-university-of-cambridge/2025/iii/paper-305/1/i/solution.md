<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [unitary gauge](../../../../../../unitary-gauge.md) and write $\phi=v+h/\sqrt2$. The quadratic Lagrangian contains

$$
\frac12(\partial h)^2-\lambda v^2h^2+e^2v^2A_\mu A^\mu.
$$

Thus the radial Higgs excitation is a spin-zero particle with $m_h^2=2\lambda v^2$, while the gauge boson is a massive spin-one particle with $m_A^2=2e^2v^2$. The phase of $\phi$ is the [Goldstone boson](../../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) eaten by the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md) to supply the longitudinal polarization of $A_\mu$.
