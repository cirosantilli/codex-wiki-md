<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Writing $\Phi=(\phi_1,\phi_2)^T$, the scalar sector has a global $SU(2)$ flavour symmetry in addition to the gauged common $U(1)$ phase. A vacuum can be chosen as $\Phi_0=(0,v)^T$. A diagonal combination of the gauge phase and the $SU(2)$ rotation generated by $\sigma_3$ leaves it invariant.

The radial scalar has $m_h^2=2\lambda v^2$, the gauge boson has $m_A^2=2e^2v^2$, and two real scalars remain massless. The four real scalar directions consist of one radial mode and three angular modes; one angular mode is eaten, leaving the two [Goldstone bosons](../../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) expected from the two broken physical global generators. The vacuum set before quotienting is $S^3$, and the manifold of gauge-inequivalent vacua is the [complex projective line](../../../../../../complex-projective-line.md)

$$
\boxed{S^3/U(1)=\mathbb{CP}^1\cong S^2.}
$$
