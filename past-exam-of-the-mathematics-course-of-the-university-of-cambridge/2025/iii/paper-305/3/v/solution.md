<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The adjoint fermion is a flavour singlet, so

$$
\mathcal A[SU(N_f)_L^3]=2,
\qquad
\mathcal A[SU(N_f)_L^2U(1)_X]=-2.
$$

There are $4N_f$ fundamental Weyl components of charge $-1$ and three adjoint components of charge $q=N_f/2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A[U(1)_X^3]=-4N_f+\frac{3N_f^3}{8}},
\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal A[U(1)_X\text{-gravity}]=-4N_f+\frac{3N_f}{2}=-\frac{5N_f}{2}}.
$$
