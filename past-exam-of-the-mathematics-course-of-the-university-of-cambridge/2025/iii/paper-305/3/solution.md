A [gauge anomaly](../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) makes gauge redundancy inconsistent and must cancel. A [chiral anomaly](../../../../../chiral-anomaly.md) is the quantum nonconservation of a classically conserved axial current and may be a physical effect. A ['t Hooft anomaly](../../../../../t-hooft-anomaly.md) is an obstruction to gauging a global symmetry; it is preserved by [renormalization-group flow](../../../../../renormalization-group-flow.md) and must be reproduced by the infrared theory, through massless degrees of freedom, symmetry breaking, or topological order.
