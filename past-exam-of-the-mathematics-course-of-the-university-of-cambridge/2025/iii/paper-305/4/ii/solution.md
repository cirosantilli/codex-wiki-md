<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Two complex $N\times N$ quark Yukawa matrices contain $4N^2$ real parameters. Flavour-basis transformations $U(N)_Q\times U(N)_u\times U(N)_d$ remove $3N^2-1$, because the common baryon-number phase remains unbroken. Thus there are

$$
N^2+1=2N+\frac{N(N-1)}2+\frac{(N-1)(N-2)}2
$$

physical parameters: $2N$ quark masses, $N(N-1)/2$ mixing angles, and $(N-1)(N-2)/2$ complex phases. A physical phase exists only for $N\geq3$ and produces [CP violation](../../../../../../cp-violation.md), equivalently time-reversal violation when CPT is conserved.
