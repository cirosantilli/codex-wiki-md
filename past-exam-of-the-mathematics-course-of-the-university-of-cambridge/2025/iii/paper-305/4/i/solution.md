<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A Dirac mass term directly pairs a left-handed weak doublet with a right-handed weak singlet and is therefore not electroweak gauge invariant. Introduce the [Higgs doublet](../../../../../../higgs-field.md) $H:(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{1/2}$ and $\widetilde H=i\sigma_2H^*:(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{-1/2}$. The gauge-invariant [Yukawa terms](../../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) are

$$
-\bar Q_LY_dHd_R-\bar Q_LY_u\widetilde Hu_R
-\bar L_LY_eHe_R-\bar L_LY_\nu\widetilde H\nu_R+\text{h.c.}
$$

When [electroweak symmetry breaking](../../../../../../electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md) gives $\langle H\rangle=(0,v_H/\sqrt2)^T$, these become Dirac mass matrices $M_f=Y_fv_H/\sqrt2$.
