<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The condensate transforms as $\langle\bar q_Lq_R\rangle\mapsto L\langle\bar q_Lq_R\rangle R^\dagger$, so its value proportional to the identity produces [chiral symmetry breaking](../../../../../../chiral-symmetry-breaking.md)

$$
SU(2)_L\times SU(2)_R\longrightarrow SU(2)_V.
$$

The vacuum manifold is the coset $(SU(2)_L\times SU(2)_R)/SU(2)_V\cong SU(2)$ and has three Goldstone modes. A field $U(x)\in SU(2)$ transforms as $U\mapsto LUR^\dagger$. Lorentz invariance and chiral symmetry leave, at two-derivative order, one invariant metric on this coset, so

$$
\mathcal L_\pi=\frac{f_\pi^2}{4}\operatorname{tr}(\partial_\mu U^\dagger\partial^\mu U).
$$

Since the Lagrangian density has dimension four and $U$ is dimensionless, the [pion decay constant](../../../../../../pion-decay-constant.md) $f_\pi$ has mass dimension one.
