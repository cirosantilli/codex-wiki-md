The pooled [Wald test](../../../../../../wald-test.md) statistic is

$$
T=\frac{p_1-p_0}
{\sqrt{\bar p(1-\bar p)(1/n_1+1/n_0)}}.
$$

In asymptotic calculations one replaces $\bar p$ by its probability limit $\bar\pi=(n_1\pi_1+n_0\pi_0)/(n_1+n_0)$.
