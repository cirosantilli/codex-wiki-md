<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Under frequency-dependent [mass-action kinetics](../../../../../../law-of-mass-action.md), if $I(t)$ of the $N(t)$ individuals are infectious, the force of infection is

$$
\lambda(t)=\beta\frac{I(t)}{N(t)}.
$$

One newly infectious person in an otherwise susceptible population transmits at total rate approximately $\beta$ for mean infectious duration $1/\gamma$. Hence the [basic reproduction number](../../../../../../basic-reproduction-number.md) is

$$
R_0=\frac\beta\gamma=\beta d_I,
$$

where $d_I$ is the infectious, rather than latent-plus-infectious, duration.
