The [log-rank test](../../../../../../log-rank-test.md) compares observed failures in each group with the numbers expected under equal hazards, conditioning at every event time on the risk set and total failures. Its score sums $O_{1j}-Y_{1j}d_j/Y_j$ and is standardized by its null hypergeometric variance. It is most powerful for approximately [proportional hazards](../../../../../../proportional-hazards-model.md). Strongly crossing survival curves can produce large positive and negative contributions that cancel, so very different distributions may yield a weak log-rank statistic.
