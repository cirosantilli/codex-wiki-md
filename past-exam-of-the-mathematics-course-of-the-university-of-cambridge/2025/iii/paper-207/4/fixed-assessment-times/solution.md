<h1 id="4/fixed-assessment-times/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed assessment times](../fixed-assessment-times.md)

Monthly assessment gives [interval censoring](../../../../../../interval-censoring.md), although recording failure at the visit treats it as exact. It also creates many tied times. Ordinary continuous-time log-rank calculations then require a tie convention or grouped-time method, and unequal or missed visits can induce informative observation.
