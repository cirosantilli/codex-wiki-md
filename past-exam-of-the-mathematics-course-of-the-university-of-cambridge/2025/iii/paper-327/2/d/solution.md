<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The heat operator

$$
\partial_t-\Delta_x
$$

is hypoelliptic: its symbol $i\tau+|\xi|^2$ satisfies the derivative estimates that yield local regularity by the argument in part (c). It is not elliptic as an operator of total order two, because its principal symbol is $|\xi|^2$, which vanishes at every nonzero covector $(\tau,0)$ with $\tau\ne0$. Hence it is a [hypoelliptic differential operator](../../../../../../hypoelliptic-operator.md) that is not an [elliptic differential operator](../../../../../../elliptic-differential-operator.md).
