↑ **Parent:** [Viii](../viii.md)

**True.** Fourier transformation turns $D^\alpha$ into multiplication by $\xi^\alpha$, and

$$
\langle\xi\rangle^{s-|\alpha|}|\xi^\alpha|
\leq\langle\xi\rangle^s.
$$

**Hence $D^\alpha:H^s\to H^{s-|\alpha|}$ is bounded.**
