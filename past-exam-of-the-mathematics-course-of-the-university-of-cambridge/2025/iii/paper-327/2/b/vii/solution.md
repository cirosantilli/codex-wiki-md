↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

**True.** The Fourier transform of a [compactly supported distribution](../../../../../../../compactly-supported-distribution.md) is a smooth function of at most polynomial growth. A sufficiently negative Sobolev weight makes its square integrable, so every $u\in\mathcal E'(\mathbb R^n)$ belongs to $H^t(\mathbb R^n)$ for some $t$.
