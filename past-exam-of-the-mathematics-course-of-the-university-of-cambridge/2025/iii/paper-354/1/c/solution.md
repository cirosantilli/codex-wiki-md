<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [state–operator correspondence](../../../../../../state-operator-correspondence.md) maps $\mathcal O$ to the lowest one-particle state on $S^4\times\mathbb R$. On a sphere of radius $r$, cylinder energy equals scaling dimension divided by $r$. The primary has $\Delta=5$.

A translation generator raises the dimension by one and transforms as a vector of $SO(5)$. States with no angular momentum arise from scalar descendant pairs $P^2$, so the allowed descendants are

$$
(P^2)^n\mathcal O,\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots.
$$

Their dimensions are $5+2n$, and hence the one-quantum, zero-angular-momentum energies are

$$
\boxed{E_n=\frac{5+2n}{r},\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots}.
$$

These are the $l=0$ normal-mode energies of a massless scalar in global [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md).
