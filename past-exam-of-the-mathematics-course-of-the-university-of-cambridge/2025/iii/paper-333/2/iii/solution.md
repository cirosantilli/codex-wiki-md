<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At small [Rossby number](../../../../../../rossby-number.md), $q=(\zeta+f_0)/H\simeq f_0/H$. For steady flow with $H=y^2$, the forced potential-vorticity equation becomes

$$
v\frac d{dy}\left(\frac{f_0}{y^2}\right)
=\frac{W_0}{y^2},
$$

so

$$
\boxed{v=-\frac{W_0}{2f_0}y.}
$$

Define a transport streamfunction by $Hu=-\psi_y$ and $Hv=\psi_x$. Choosing the eastern wall as $\psi=0$ gives the [topographic Sverdrup balance](../../../../../../topographic-sverdrup-balance.md) solution

$$
\boxed{
\psi=\frac{W_0}{2f_0}(L_X-x)y^3,}
$$

and therefore

$$
u=-\frac{3W_0}{2f_0}(L_X-x),
\qquad
v=-\frac{W_0}{2f_0}y.
$$

In each half-basin, the transport streamlines are the level curves $(L_X-x)y^3=\text{constant}$. They move westward and toward $y=0$. This interior solution treats the two sides of the degenerate line $H=0$ separately and requires boundary layers to enforce solid-wall conditions.
