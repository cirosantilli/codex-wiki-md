<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Linearization about $U\widehat{\mathbf x}$ changes the frequency to the intrinsic value $\omega-Uk$. Thus

$$
\boxed{
\omega=Uk-\frac{\beta k}{K^2+f_0^2m^2/N^2}.}
$$

A mountain-fixed disturbance has $\omega=0$, so its vertical wavenumber satisfies

$$
\boxed{
m^2=\frac{N^2}{f_0^2}\left(\frac\beta U-K^2\right).}
$$

The [quasi-geostrophic mountain wave](../../../../../../quasi-geostrophic-mountain-wave.md) propagates vertically only when $m^2>0$. For $U>0$, this requires

$$
\boxed{0<U<\frac\beta{k^2+l^2}.}
$$

Otherwise the disturbance is vertically evanescent.
