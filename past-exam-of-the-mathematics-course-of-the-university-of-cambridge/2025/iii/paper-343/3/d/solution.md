<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the [Affleck--Kennedy--Lieb--Tasaki state](../../../../../../affleck-kennedy-lieb-tasaki-state.md), take the dihedral subgroup of spin rotations generated by $\pi$ rotations about the $x$ and $z$ axes. On each physical spin-one site these generators commute and give an ordinary representation of $\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$. On the virtual spin-one-half space they may be represented, up to phases, by

$$
V_x=\sigma_x,
\qquad
V_z=\sigma_z.
$$

They anticommute,

$$
V_xV_z=-V_zV_x,
$$

so the virtual action is the nontrivial projective representation of $\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$.

The reduced density operator across a cut commutes with this irreducible projective action. By [Schur lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) it is proportional to the identity within each virtual doublet. Consequently every level in the [entanglement spectrum of a matrix product state](../../../../../../entanglement-spectrum-of-a-matrix-product-state.md) is at least twofold degenerate, the characteristic protected edge-spin degeneracy of the AKLT phase.
