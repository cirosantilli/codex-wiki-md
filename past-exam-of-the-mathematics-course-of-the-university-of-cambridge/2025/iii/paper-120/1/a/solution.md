A [Heyting algebra](../../../../../../heyting-algebra.md) is a bounded [distributive lattice](../../../../../../distributive-lattice.md) $L$ in which, for every $a,b\in L$, there is an element $a\Rightarrow b$ satisfying

$$
x\leq(a\Rightarrow b)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x\wedge a\leq b.
$$

**Thus $a\Rightarrow b$ is the greatest element whose meet with $a$ lies below $b$.**
