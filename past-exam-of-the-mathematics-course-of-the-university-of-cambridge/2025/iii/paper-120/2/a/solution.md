The [Church-Rosser theorem](../../../../../../church-rosser-theorem.md) states that if $M\twoheadrightarrow_\beta N_1$ and $M\twoheadrightarrow_\beta N_2$, then there is a term $P$ with $N_1\twoheadrightarrow_\beta P$ and $N_2\twoheadrightarrow_\beta P$. Equivalently, beta reduction is confluent.
