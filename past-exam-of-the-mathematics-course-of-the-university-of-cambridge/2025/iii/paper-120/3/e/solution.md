The [Tennenbaum theorem](../../../../../../tennenbaum-s-theorem.md) states that no countable nonstandard model of [Peano arithmetic](../../../../../../peano-arithmetic.md) has a presentation on $\mathbb N$ in which both its addition and multiplication operations are recursive.
