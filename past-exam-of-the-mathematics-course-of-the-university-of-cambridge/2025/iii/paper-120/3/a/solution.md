A [Sigma-1 formula](../../../../../../sigma-1-formula.md) is a formula equivalent in arithmetic to $\exists y_1\cdots\exists y_r\,\delta$, where $\delta$ is bounded. A [Pi-1 formula](../../../../../../pi-1-formula.md) is similarly equivalent to $\forall y_1\cdots\forall y_r\,\delta$ with $\delta$ bounded.
