For standard Gaussian measure $\gamma$, the [Gaussian Poincaré inequality](../../../../../../gaussian-poincare-inequality.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Var}_\gamma h\leq\int(h')^2d\gamma.
$$

The [Gaussian logarithmic Sobolev inequality](../../../../../../gaussian-logarithmic-sobolev-inequality.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Ent}_\gamma(h^2)
\leq2\int(h')^2d\gamma,
$$

where $\operatorname{Ent}_\gamma(G)=\int G\log G\,d\gamma-(\int Gd\gamma)\log\int Gd\gamma$.
