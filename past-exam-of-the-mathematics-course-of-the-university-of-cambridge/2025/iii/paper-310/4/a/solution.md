↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Canonical quantization imposes

$$
[a_{\mathbf k},a_{\mathbf k'}^\dagger]=(2\pi)^3\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf k-\mathbf k'),
\qquad [a_{\mathbf k},a_{\mathbf k'}]=[a_{\mathbf k}^\dagger,a_{\mathbf k'}^\dagger]=0.
$$

The [two-point correlation function](../../../../../../correlation-function.md) has mode power $P_{\delta\phi}=|f_k|^2/a^2$. After [cosmological horizon exit](../../../../../../cosmological-horizon-exit.md), $|k\tau|\ll1$, so

$$
|f_k|^2\simeq\frac1{2k^3\tau^2},
\qquad a^2=\frac1{H^2\tau^2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\Delta_{\delta\phi}^2=\frac{k^3}{2\pi^2}P_{\delta\phi}
=\boxed{\left(\frac H{2\pi}\right)^2}.
$$

This is the [scale-invariant inflationary power spectrum](../../../../../../scale-invariant-inflationary-power-spectrum.md) of a light canonical scalar.
