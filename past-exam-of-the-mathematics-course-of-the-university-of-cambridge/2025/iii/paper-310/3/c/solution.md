<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Neutrino free streaming](../../../../../../neutrino-free-streaming.md) gives $\delta_\nu\simeq0$ on the stated scales, so $\delta_m=(1-f_\nu)\delta_{cb}$. With derivatives with respect to $N=\log a$, the growth equation becomes

$$
\delta_{cb,NN}+\frac12\delta_{cb,N}
-\frac32(1-f_\nu)\delta_{cb}=0.
$$

For $\delta_{cb}\propto a^p$,

$$
p=\frac{-1+\sqrt{25-24f_\nu}}4
=1-\frac35f_\nu+O(f_\nu^2).
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{X(f_\nu)=1-\frac35f_\nu}
$$

to first order.
