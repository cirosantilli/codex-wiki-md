<h1 id="3/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Let $T=AB$ with $A,B$ [Hilbert--Schmidt](../../../../../../../hilbert-schmidt-operator.md), and write $T=U|T|$. Since $|T|=U^*AB$, part iii and the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) for series give

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|T\|_1
&=\sum_n\langle Be_n,A^*Ue_n\rangle\\
&\leq\left(\sum_n\|Be_n\|^2\right)^{1/2}
\left(\sum_n\|A^*Ue_n\|^2\right)^{1/2}\\
&\leq\|B\|_2\|A^*\|_2\|U\|
=\|A\|_2\|B\|_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

**Thus $T$ is trace class with the required bound.**
