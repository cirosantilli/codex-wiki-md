<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By the definition of the [trace norm](../../../../../../../trace-class-operator.md) and positivity of $|T|$,

$$
\|T\|_1
=\bigl\||T|^{1/2}\bigr\|_2^2
=\sum_n\bigl\||T|^{1/2}e_n\bigr\|^2
=\sum_n\langle|T|e_n,e_n\rangle.
$$
