↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [operator norm](../../../../../../../operator-norm.md) gives

$$
\|ST\|_2^2
=\sum_n\|STe_n\|^2
\leq\|S\|^2\sum_n\|Te_n\|^2
=\|S\|^2\|T\|_2^2.
$$

Using part i and the first inequality for the adjoints gives

$$
\boxed{\|TS\|_2=\|S^*T^*\|_2
\leq\|S^*\|\|T^*\|_2
=\|S\|\|T\|_2.}
$$
