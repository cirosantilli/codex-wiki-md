<h1 id="3/b/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Use the norm-attaining factorization $T=AB$ from part v. Then $ST=(SA)B$, parts ii and iv give

$$
\|ST\|_1
\leq\|SA\|_2\|B\|_2
\leq\|S\|\|A\|_2\|B\|_2
=\|S\|\|T\|_1.
$$

**Thus the trace-class operators form a left ideal. Applying the result to adjoints gives the corresponding right-ideal estimate as well.**
