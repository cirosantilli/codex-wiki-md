↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Twice applying [Parseval identity for a Hilbertian basis](../../../../../../../parseval-identity-for-a-hilbertian-basis.md) to the matrix coefficients gives

$$
\|T\|_2^2
=\sum_n\sum_m|\langle Te_n,e_m\rangle|^2
=\sum_m\sum_n|\langle e_n,T^*e_m\rangle|^2
=\|T^*\|_2^2.
$$

**Thus $\|T\|_2=\|T^*\|_2$.**
