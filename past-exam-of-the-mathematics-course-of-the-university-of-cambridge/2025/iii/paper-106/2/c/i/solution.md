<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $f\notin K'_\varepsilon$, some relative weak-star neighbourhood $U$ of $f$ in $K$ has $\operatorname{diam}U\leq\varepsilon$. Every $g\in U$ has that same $U$ as a neighbourhood of norm diameter at most $\varepsilon$, so $g\notin K'_\varepsilon$. Hence $K\setminus K'_\varepsilon$ is relatively weak-star open and the [Szlenk derivation](../../../../../../../szlenk-derivation.md) $K'_\varepsilon$ is weak-star closed in $K$.
