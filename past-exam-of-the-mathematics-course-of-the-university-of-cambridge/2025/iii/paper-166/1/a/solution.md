<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the general result, choose logarithms of nonzero [algebraic numbers](../../../../../../algebraic-number.md) $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_s$ and algebraic coefficients $\beta_0,\ldots,\beta_s$, and put

$$
\Lambda=\beta_0+\sum_{j=1}^s\beta_j\log\alpha_j.
$$

Let

$$
A_j=\max\{H(f_{\alpha_j}),e^{|\log\alpha_j|},10\},
$$

where $f_{\alpha_j}$ is the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) and its $H$ is the [naive polynomial height](../../../../../../naive-polynomial-height.md), and let

$$
B=\max\{H(f_{\beta_0}),\ldots,H(f_{\beta_s}),
\log A_1,\ldots,\log A_s\}.
$$

The [general lower bound for a linear form in logarithms](../../../../../../general-lower-bound-for-a-linear-form-in-logarithms.md) states that, if $\Lambda\ne0$, then

$$
|\Lambda|>
\exp\!\left(-C\log A_1\cdots\log A_s\log B\right),
$$

where the effective constant $C$ depends only on $s$ and the degree of the [number field](../../../../../../number-field.md) generated by all the data.

For the improved homogeneous result, take $b_1,\ldots,b_s\in\mathbb Z$ and

$$
\Lambda=\sum_{j=1}^sb_j\log\alpha_j.
$$

With the same $A_j$, after ordering the terms set

$$
B^*=\max\left\{
\frac{|b_1|}{\log A_s},\ldots,
\frac{|b_{s-1}|}{\log A_s},|b_s|,10
\right\}.
$$

The [Baker lower bound for a homogeneous linear form in logarithms](../../../../../../baker-lower-bound-for-a-homogeneous-linear-form-in-logarithms.md) gives

$$
\Lambda\ne0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
|\Lambda|>
\exp\!\left(-C\log A_1\cdots\log A_s\log B^*\right).
$$

Both constants are effective. The division by $\log A_s$ in $B^*$ is the improvement that matters when $\alpha_s$ has variable height.
