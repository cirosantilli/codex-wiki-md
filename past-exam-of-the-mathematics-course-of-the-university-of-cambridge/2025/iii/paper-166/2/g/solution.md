<h1 id="2/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Choose distinct [prime numbers](../../../../../../prime-number.md) $p,q$ so large that $pq>C(p+q)$, and set

$$
\alpha=\frac1p,
\qquad
\beta=\frac1q.
$$

The fraction $(p+q)/(pq)$ is reduced, because neither $p$ nor $q$ divides $p+q$. The [height of a rational number](../../../../../../height-of-a-rational-number.md) therefore gives

$$
H(\alpha+\beta)=pq,
\qquad
H(\alpha)+H(\beta)=p+q.
$$

The choice of $p,q$ proves the required strict inequality.
