<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For every $k\in\mathbb Z$,

$$
H(\alpha^k)=H(\alpha)^{|k|}.
$$

For $k\geq0$, this follows directly from

$$
\max(1,|\alpha^k|_v)=\max(1,|\alpha|_v)^k
$$

at each [place of a number field](../../../../../../place-of-a-number-field.md). The [product formula](../../../../../../product-formula.md) gives $H(\alpha^{-1})=H(\alpha)$, because

$$
\max(1,|\alpha|_v^{-1})
=\frac{\max(1,|\alpha|_v)}{|\alpha|_v},
$$

and this handles negative $k$ as well.
