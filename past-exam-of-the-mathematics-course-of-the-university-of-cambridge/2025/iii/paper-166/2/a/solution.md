<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $K$ be a [number field](../../../../../../number-field.md). Its [places](../../../../../../place-of-a-number-field.md) consist of its real embeddings, conjugate pairs of complex embeddings, and the finite places associated with nonzero [prime ideals](../../../../../../prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak p$ of its [ring of integers of a number field](../../../../../../ring-of-integers.md). At a real or complex place use the usual absolute value. If $\mathfrak p$ lies over the [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number.md) $p$ with [ramification index](../../../../../../ramification-index.md) $e_{\mathfrak p}$, normalize its [absolute value](../../../../../../non-archimedean-place-of-a-number-field.md) by

$$
|x|_{\mathfrak p}=p^{-\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak p}(x)/e_{\mathfrak p}}.
$$

These normalizations extend the standard absolute values on $\mathbb Q$.

Write $d_v=[K_v:\mathbb Q_v]$ for the [local degree of a place](../../../../../../local-degree-of-a-place.md). Thus $d_v$ is $1$ at a real place, $2$ at a complex place, and $e_vf_v$ at a finite place. The [Absolute multiplicative Weil height](../../../../../../absolute-multiplicative-weil-height.md) is

$$
H(\alpha)=
\prod_{v\in M_K}\max\{1,|\alpha|_v\}^{d_v/[K:\mathbb Q]}.
$$

The [product formula](../../../../../../product-formula.md) shows that this is unchanged when $K$ is replaced by a larger number field containing $\alpha$.
