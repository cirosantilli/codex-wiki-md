<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For coprime integers $p,q$ with $q>0$,

$$
H(p/q)=\max\{|p|,q\}.
$$

This follows either directly from the real and [p-adic absolute values](../../../../../../p-adic-absolute-value.md), or from the [height-Mahler measure formula](../../../../../../height-mahler-measure-formula.md) applied to the primitive [minimal polynomial](../../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) $qX-p$.
