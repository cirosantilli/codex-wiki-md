**No.** Let $n=7$, $r=3$, take $\mathcal A=\{\{1,2,3\}\}$, and let

$$
\mathcal B=\{B\in[7]^{(3)}:B\cap\{1,2,3\}\ne\varnothing\}.
$$

The families are nonempty and cross-intersecting. There are

$$
|\mathcal B|=\binom73-\binom43=31
$$

members, so

$$
|\mathcal A|+|\mathcal B|=32
>30=2\binom62.
$$
