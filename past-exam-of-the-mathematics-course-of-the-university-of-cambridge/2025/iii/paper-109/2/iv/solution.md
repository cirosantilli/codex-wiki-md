<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Fix a [prime number](../../../../../../prime-number.md) $p>r$. Distinct members of an intersecting $r$-uniform family have intersection size in

$$
L=\{1,2,\ldots,r-1\}\subset\mathbb F_p,
$$

whereas every member has size $r\notin L$ modulo $p$. The [Frankl-Wilson theorem](../../../../../../frankl-wilson-theorem.md) with $|L|=r-1$ therefore gives

$$
|\mathcal A|\leq\binom n{r-1}.
$$

For fixed $r$,

$$
\binom n{r-1}
=\binom{n-1}{r-1}\frac n{n-r+1}
=\binom{n-1}{r-1}\left(1+O(n^{-1})\right).
$$

This is the asserted asymptotic weakening of the [Erdős-Ko-Rado theorem](../../../../../../erdos-ko-rado-theorem.md).
