<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Partition the family into complementary pairs $\{A,[4p]\setminus A\}$. Choose at most one member from each pair to obtain $\mathcal A'$ with $|\mathcal A'|\geq|\mathcal A|/2$. Distinct members of $\mathcal A'$ are not disjoint, and the hypothesis excludes intersection size $p$. Since their intersection sizes lie between $1$ and $2p-1$, they therefore lie modulo $p$ in

$$
L=\{1,2,\ldots,p-1\}.
$$

Every member has size $2p\equiv0\pmod p$, which is outside $L$. The [Frankl-Wilson theorem](../../../../../../frankl-wilson-theorem.md) gives

$$
|\mathcal A'|\leq\binom{4p}{p-1},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{|\mathcal A|\leq2\binom{4p}{p-1}.}
$$
