**False.** Take $n=6$, $r=3$, and

$$
\mathcal A=\{A\in[6]^{(3)}:|A\cap\{1,2,3\}|\geq2\}.
$$

There are $1+3\cdot3=10$ members. Its lower shadow consists of the three pairs inside $\{1,2,3\}$ and the nine pairs having one point there and one in $\{4,5,6\}$, so $|\partial\mathcal A|=12$. Its upper shadow consists of the twelve four-sets containing at least two points of $\{1,2,3\}$, so $|\nabla\mathcal A|=12$. The sum is $24$.

The initial lex segment of length ten is the star of all triples containing $1$; its lower and upper shadows have sizes $15$ and $10$. The initial colex segment consists of all triples in $[5]$; its lower and upper shadows have sizes $10$ and $15$. Both sums are $25$, so neither canonical segment minimizes the sum.
