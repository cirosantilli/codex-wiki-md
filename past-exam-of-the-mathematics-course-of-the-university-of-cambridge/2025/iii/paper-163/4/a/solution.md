<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\Theta(R)$ partition an $R^{-1}$-neighborhood of the parabola into $R^{-1/2}\times R^{-1}$ caps, and suppose $\widehat f_\theta$ is supported in $\theta$. The [decoupling inequality for the parabola](../../../../../../decoupling-inequality-for-the-parabola.md) states that for $2\leq p<\infty$ and every $\epsilon>0$,

$$
\boxed{\left\|\sum_{\theta\in\Theta(R)}f_\theta\right\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^2)}
\lesssim_{p,\epsilon}R^\epsilon
\left(1+R^{1/2-3/p}\right)
\left(\sum_{\theta\in\Theta(R)}\|f_\theta\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^2)}^2\right)^{1/2}.}
$$
