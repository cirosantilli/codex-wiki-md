The [Khintchine inequality](../../../../../../khintchine-inequality.md) states that for independent Rademacher random variables $\varepsilon_n$ and every $0<p<\infty$, there are constants $A_p,B_p>0$ such that

$$
A_p\left(\sum_n|a_n|^2\right)^{1/2}
\leq\left(\mathbb E\left|\sum_n\varepsilon_na_n\right|^p\right)^{1/p}
\leq B_p\left(\sum_n|a_n|^2\right)^{1/2}.
$$

The constants depend only on $p$.
