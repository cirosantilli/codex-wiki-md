<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $v$ range over a bounded subset of $C^{1,\beta}$. All jets $(x,v,Dv)$ then lie in one compact set, so the composed coefficients have uniform $C^{0,\alpha\beta}$ bounds and one ellipticity constant. The [weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](../../../../../../weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md) bounds $T(v)$ in $C^0$ by the boundary data and the bounded forcing term. The [global Schauder estimate](../../../../../../global-schauder-estimate.md) consequently bounds $T(v)$ in $C^{2,\alpha\beta}$.

The [compact embedding of Hölder spaces](../../../../../../compact-embedding-of-holder-spaces.md)

$$
C^{2,\alpha\beta}(\overline\Omega)Subset C^{1,\beta}(\overline\Omega)
$$

then makes the image relatively compact. Hence $T$ is a compact operator on $C^{1,\beta}(\overline\Omega)$.
