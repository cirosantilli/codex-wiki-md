<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Seek a [radial function](../../../../../../radial-function.md) $u=u(r)$ on the unit ball. The equation and regularity at the origin give

$$
\left(r^{n-1}|u'|^{p-2}u'\right)'=r^{n-1},
\qquad
r^{n-1}|u'|^{p-2}u'=\frac{r^n}{n}.
$$

Hence $u'(r)=(r/n)^{1/(p-1)}$, and the zero [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) gives

$$
u(r)=\frac{p-1}{p}n^{-1/(p-1)}
\left(r^{p/(p-1)}-1\right).
$$

The power $|x|^q$ is twice differentiable at the origin exactly when $q\geq2$. Here $q=p/(p-1)$, so $u\in C^2$ at the origin exactly when $1<p\leq2$. Under the assumption $2<p<n$, this weak solution is never $C^2$ at the origin, exhibiting the regularity loss caused by [degenerate ellipticity](../../../../../../degenerate-elliptic-operator.md).
