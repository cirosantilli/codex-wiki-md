<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a bipartite [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) $\rho_{SA}$, the [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) of $S$ is

$$
\boxed{\rho_S=\operatorname{Tr}_A\rho_{SA}}.
$$

It is characterized by

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_S(M_S\rho_S)
=\operatorname{Tr}_{SA}[(M_S\otimes I_A)\rho_{SA}]
$$

for every observable $M_S$ on $S$. Equivalently, in any [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $\{|a_k\rangle\}$ of $A$, the [partial trace](../../../../../../partial-trace.md) is

$$
\rho_S=\sum_k(I_S\otimes\langle a_k|)\rho_{SA}
(I_S\otimes|a_k\rangle),
$$

and the result is independent of that basis.
