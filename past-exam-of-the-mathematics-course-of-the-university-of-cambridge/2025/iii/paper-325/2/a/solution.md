<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The outputs satisfy

$$
a\mathbin\oplus b=xy,
$$

and each allowed pair occurs with probability $1/2$. This is a [Popescu–Rohrlich box](../../../../../../popescu-rohrlich-box.md). For either party, summing over the remote output gives a uniform local bit:

$$
P(a=0|x,y)=P(a=1|x,y)=\frac12,
$$

independently of $y$, and similarly for Bob independently of $x$. The device is therefore a [no-signalling box](../../../../../../no-signalling-box.md) and does not necessarily permit superluminal signalling, despite correlations stronger than quantum theory allows.
