<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For input pairs other than $11$, either local output is the equiprobable ensemble $\{|+\rangle,|-\rangle\}$; for input $11$, it is $\{|0\rangle,|1\rangle\}$. Both ensembles have the same [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md),

$$
\frac12(|+\rangle\langle+|+|-\rangle\langle-|)
=\frac12(|0\rangle\langle0|+|1\rangle\langle1|)
=\frac I2.
$$

Every local [measurement in quantum mechanics](../../../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) therefore has the same statistics for every remote input. The joint outputs have unusual correlations, but observing them requires the parties to compare results through an ordinary causal channel. Hence this device also does not necessarily allow superluminal signalling.
