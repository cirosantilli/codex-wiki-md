<h1 id="2/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

Both $\{\#C_N\geq\theta\#H_N/2\}$ and $\{0\in C_N\}$ are increasing events. The first has probability at least $\theta/2$ by part (a2), while the second has probability at least $\theta$ because an infinite origin cluster reaches $h_N$. The [Harris-FKG inequality](../../../../../../harris-fkg-inequality.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(A_N)\geq\frac{\theta}{2}\theta=\frac{\theta^2}{2}.}
$$
