<h1 id="2/b2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B2](../b2.md)

A closed circuit of diameter at least $n$ through a fixed listed point contains a closed path from that point to graph distance at least $n/2$. [Exponential decay of subcritical percolation](../../../../../../exponential-decay-of-subcritical-percolation.md) bounds this probability by $Ce^{-cn}$. A union bound over the $n$ listed points gives

$$
\mathbb P(B_n)\leq Cn e^{-cn}\leq n e^{-u n}
$$

after reducing $u>0$ and adjusting finitely many small $n$.
