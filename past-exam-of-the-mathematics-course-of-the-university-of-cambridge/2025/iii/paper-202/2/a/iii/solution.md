Because $A_t^{(n)}=X_t^2-M_t^{(n)}$, the inequality $(x-y)^2\leq2x^2+2y^2$ and part (ii) yield

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(A_t^{(n)})^2
\leq2\mathbb EX_t^4+2\mathbb E(M_t^{(n)})^2
\leq2C^4+8C^4=10C^4.}
$$
