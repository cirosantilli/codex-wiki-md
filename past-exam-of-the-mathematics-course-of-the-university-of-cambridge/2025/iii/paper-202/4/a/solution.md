↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [strong solution of a stochastic differential equation](../../../../../../strong-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md) is an adapted process $X$ on a prescribed filtered probability space carrying a prescribed Brownian motion $W$, satisfying

$$
X_t=X_0+\int_0^tb(X_s)ds+\int_0^t\sigma(X_s)dW_s
$$

almost surely. A [weak solution of a stochastic differential equation](../../../../../../weak-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md) consists of a filtered probability space, a Brownian motion, and an adapted process on that space satisfying the same integral equation; the space and driving Brownian motion are part of what may be chosen.
