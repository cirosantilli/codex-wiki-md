[Pathwise uniqueness](../../../../../../pathwise-uniqueness.md) means that two solutions on the same filtered probability space, driven by the same Brownian motion and having the same initial value almost surely, are indistinguishable. [Uniqueness in law](../../../../../../uniqueness-in-law.md) means that any two weak solutions with the same initial distribution induce the same probability law on path space.
