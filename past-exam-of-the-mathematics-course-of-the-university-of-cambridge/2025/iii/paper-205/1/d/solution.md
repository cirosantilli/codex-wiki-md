<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Suppose $\widehat\beta_1$ and $\widehat\beta_2$ minimize $Q$. Their midpoint is also a minimizer because $Q$ is a [convex function](../../../../../../convex-function.md). The group penalty is convex, while the squared Euclidean norm is strictly convex in the fitted value. If $X\widehat\beta_1\ne X\widehat\beta_2$, strict convexity would make the midpoint objective strictly smaller than the minimum. Hence $X\widehat\beta_1=X\widehat\beta_2$, so the fitted values are unique.
