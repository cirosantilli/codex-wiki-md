↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [convex function](../../../../../../convex-function.md) $f:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R$, its [subdifferential](../../../../../../subdifferential.md) at $x$ is

$$
\partial f(x)=\{g\in\mathbb R^d:f(y)\geq f(x)+g^T(y-x)\text{ for every }y\in\mathbb R^d\}.
$$

Its elements are the subgradients of $f$ at $x$.
