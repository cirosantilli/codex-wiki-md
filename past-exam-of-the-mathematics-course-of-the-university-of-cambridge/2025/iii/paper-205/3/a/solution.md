↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A symmetric function $k:\mathcal X^2\to\mathbb R$ is a [positive-definite kernel](../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-kernel.md) when $\sum_{i,j}a_ia_jk(x_i,x_j)\geq0$ for every finite set of points and real coefficients. A [Reproducing kernel Hilbert space](../../../../../../reproducing-kernel-hilbert-space.md) $\mathcal H$ is a Hilbert space of functions whose evaluation maps are continuous. Its reproducing kernel satisfies $k(x,\cdot)\in\mathcal H$ and $f(x)=\langle f,k(x,\cdot)\rangle_{\mathcal H}$.
