↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write a filled vertex with $n$ incoming linear fields for the $n$th-order [peculiar-velocity divergence](../../../../../../../peculiar-velocity-divergence.md). Gaussian initial conditions require every linear field to be paired. Through sixth order in the linear density there are exactly five connected topologies: the tree diagram $B_{211}$ and the one-loop triangle $B_{222}$, vertex-correction diagram $B_{321}^{I}$, propagator-correction diagram $B_{321}^{II}$, and four-leg diagram $B_{411}$. These are the five diagrams of the [one-loop matter bispectrum](../../../../../../../one-loop-matter-bispectrum.md).
