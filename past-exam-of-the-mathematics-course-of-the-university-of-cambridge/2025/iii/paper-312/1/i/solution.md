↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md) obeys $dt=a\,d\eta$, so $\dot\phi=a^{-1}\phi'$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{S_{\rm int}=\frac{\lambda}{3!}\int d^3x\,d\eta\,a(\eta)(\phi')^3}.
$$

For expanding [de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../de-sitter-spacetime.md), $a=-1/(H\eta)$ with $-\infty<\eta<0$.
