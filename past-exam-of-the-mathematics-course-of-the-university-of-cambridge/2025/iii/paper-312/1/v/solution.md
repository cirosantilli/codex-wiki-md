<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

A late-time connected four-point function of a scale-invariant field in three spatial dimensions, after removing its momentum-conserving delta function, must obey

$$
B_4(\Lambda\mathbf k_1,\ldots,\Lambda\mathbf k_4)
=\Lambda^{-9}B_4(\mathbf k_1,\ldots,\mathbf k_4).
$$

In both contributions, $s/(k_1k_2k_3k_4)$ scales as $\Lambda^{-3}$ and every energy-denominator term scales as $\Lambda^{-6}$. Hence the complete $s$-channel [primordial trispectrum](../../../../../../primordial-trispectrum.md) scales as $\Lambda^{-9}$, as required by scale invariance.
