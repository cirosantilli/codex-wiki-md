<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the homogeneous background, $X=\dot{\bar\phi}^{,2}/2$. The second [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives

$$
\frac12P_{,X}\dot{\bar\phi}^{,2}
=-\dot H M_{\rm Pl}^2
=\epsilon H^2M_{\rm Pl}^2.
$$

Dividing by $H M_{\rm Pl}^2\dot{\bar\phi}$ shows that the coefficient found above is

$$
\boxed{C=\frac{H\epsilon}{\dot{\bar\phi}}}.
$$
