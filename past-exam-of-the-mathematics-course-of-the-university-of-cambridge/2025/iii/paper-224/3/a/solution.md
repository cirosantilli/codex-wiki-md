<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For feasible mass functions $Q_1,Q_2$ at distortion levels $d_1,d_2$, the mixture $Q=\lambda Q_1+(1-\lambda)Q_2$ has nonzero-symbol mass at most $\lambda d_1+(1-\lambda)d_2$. Concavity of [information entropy](../../../../../../information-entropy.md) gives

$$
H(Q)\geq\lambda H(Q_1)+(1-\lambda)H(Q_2).
$$

Taking maximizing sequences proves

$$
\phi(\lambda d_1+(1-\lambda)d_2)
\geq\lambda\phi(d_1)+(1-\lambda)\phi(d_2).
$$
