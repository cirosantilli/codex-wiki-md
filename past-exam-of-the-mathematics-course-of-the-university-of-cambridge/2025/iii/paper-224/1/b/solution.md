<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $0\leq\lambda\leq1$ and put $P=\lambda P_1+(1-\lambda)P_2$ and $Q=\lambda Q_1+(1-\lambda)Q_2$. For each alphabet symbol $x$, apply the [log-sum inequality](../../../../../../log-sum-inequality.md) to $a_1=\lambda P_1(x)$, $a_2=(1-\lambda)P_2(x)$ and the corresponding $b$ values. Summing over $x$ gives

$$
D(P\Vert Q)
\leq\lambda D(P_1\Vert Q_1)
+(1-\lambda)D(P_2\Vert Q_2),
$$

which is joint [convexity](../../../../../../convex-function.md) in $(P,Q)$.
