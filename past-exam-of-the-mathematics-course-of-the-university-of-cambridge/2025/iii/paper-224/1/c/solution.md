<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $Z=\mathbb E_Qe^g$, define the exponentially tilted mass function $Q_g(x)=Q(x)e^{g(x)}/Z$. [Gibbs inequality](../../../../../../gibbs-inequality.md) gives

$$
0\leq D_e(P\Vert Q_g)
=D_e(P\Vert Q)-\mathbb E_Pg+\log Z.
$$

Rearranging proves

$$
D_e(P\Vert Q)\geq\mathbb E_Pg-log\mathbb E_Qe^g.
$$
