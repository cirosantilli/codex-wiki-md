↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The preceding inequality supplies the upper bound on the supremum. If $P$ has full support relative to $Q$, choose $g(x)=\log(P(x)/Q(x))$; then $\mathbb E_Qe^g=1$ and the objective equals $D_e(P\Vert Q)$. If $P(x)=0$ at some symbols, use this choice on the support of $P$ and put $g(x)=-M$ elsewhere. Letting $M\to\infty$ gives the same value. Finiteness of $D(P\Vert Q)$ guarantees that $Q$ is positive on the support of $P$. This proves the [Gibbs variational principle for relative entropy](../../../../../../gibbs-variational-principle-for-relative-entropy.md).
