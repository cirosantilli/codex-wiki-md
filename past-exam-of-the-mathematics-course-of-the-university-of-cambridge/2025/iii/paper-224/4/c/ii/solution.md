<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For decreasing probabilities, the first $x$ symbols each have probability at least $P(x)$, so $xP(x)\leq1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{L^*(x)=\lfloor\log_2x\rfloor
\leq\log_2x\leq-\log_2P(x).}
$$
