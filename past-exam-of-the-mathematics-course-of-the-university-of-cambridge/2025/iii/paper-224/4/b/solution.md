<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the distribution $Q_C$ from part (a),

$$
L(x)=-\log_2Q_C(x)-\log_2K.
$$

Therefore

$$
\mathbb E_P L(X)
=H(P)+D(P\Vert Q_C)-\log_2K
\geq H(P),
$$

by [Gibbs inequality](../../../../../../gibbs-inequality.md) and $K\leq1$.
