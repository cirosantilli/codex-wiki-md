<h1 id="6d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $D_8=\langle r,s:r^4=s^2=1, srs=r^{-1}\rangle$, its normal [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) are

$$
1,\qquad\langle r^2\rangle,\qquad\langle r\rangle,\qquad
\langle r^2,s\rangle,\qquad\langle r^2,rs\rangle,\qquad D_8.
$$

The last three proper nontrivial examples have index two; the four individual reflection [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) are not normal.
