↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $g\in G$ and $x\in\phi^{-1}(N)$,

$$
\phi(gxg^{-1})=\phi(g)\phi(x)\phi(g)^{-1}\in N,
$$

so the preimage is normal.
