<h1 id="6d/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Given $h\in H$, choose $g\in G$ with $\phi(g)=h$. For $k\in K$,

$$
h\phi(k)h^{-1}=\phi(gkg^{-1})\in\phi(K),
$$

because $K$ is normal. Thus $\phi(K)\triangleleft H$.
