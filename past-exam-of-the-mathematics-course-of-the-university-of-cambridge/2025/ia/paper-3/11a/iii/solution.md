<h1 id="11a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Reflection symmetry makes the [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) zero for $i\ne j$. Rotational symmetry makes all diagonal [integrals](../../../../../../integral.md) equal, and their sum is $R^2A_{n-1}(R)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_{r=R}x_ix_j\,dS=\delta_{ij}\frac{R^2A_{n-1}(R)}n
=\delta_{ij}\frac{\pi^{n/2}R^{n+1}}{\Gamma(n/2+1)}.}
$$
