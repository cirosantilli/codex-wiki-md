<h1 id="5d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $N\triangleleft S_n$, then $N\cap A_n\triangleleft A_n$, hence the intersection is either $1$ or $A_n$. In the latter case $N$ is $A_n$ or $S_n$. In the former, $N$ injects into $S_n/A_n\cong C_2$, so $|N|\le2$. A [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) of order two would be central, but $Z(S_n)=1$ for $n\ge3$. Therefore the normal [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md) are

$$
\boxed{1,\quad A_n,\quad S_n.}
$$
