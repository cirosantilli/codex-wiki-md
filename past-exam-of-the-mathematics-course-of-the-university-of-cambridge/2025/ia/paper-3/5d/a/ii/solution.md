↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $y=gx$, then

$$
\operatorname{Stab}_G(y)=g\operatorname{Stab}_G(x)g^{-1}.
$$

Indeed, $h$ fixes $x$ exactly when $ghg^{-1}$ fixes $gx$. Conjugation by $g$ is therefore the required isomorphism.
