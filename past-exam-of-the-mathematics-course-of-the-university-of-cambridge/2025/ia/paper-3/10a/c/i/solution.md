<h1 id="10a/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $A=x(1-x)$, $B=y(1-y)$ and $C=z(1-z)$. The forcing is $AB+AC+BC$, while $\nabla^2(ABC)=-2(AB+AC+BC)$. Thus

$$
u=-\frac12x(1-x)y(1-y)z(1-z),
$$

which vanishes on every face.
