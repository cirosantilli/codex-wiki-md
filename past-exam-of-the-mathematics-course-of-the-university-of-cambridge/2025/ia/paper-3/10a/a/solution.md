<h1 id="10a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $p=\sum_{j=0}^na_jx^{n-j}y^j$. Equating the coefficient of $x^{n-j-2}y^j$ in $\nabla^2p$ gives

$$
(n-j)(n-j-1)a_j+(j+2)(j+1)a_{j+2}=0.
$$

**Thus all even coefficients are determined by $a_0$ and all odd coefficients by $a_1$, with no further constraints. These two choices give two independent harmonic homogeneous [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md).**
