<h1 id="2c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The fixed points are $0$ and, when $a\ge-1$, $1\pm\sqrt{1+a}$. For a [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md) autonomous equation, a simple fixed point is stable when $f'<0$. Since $f'(0)=a$ and $f'(r)=2r(1-r)$ at either nonzero root, excluding the bifurcation values as requested,

$$
0\text{ is stable for }a<0,
$$



$$
1+\sqrt{1+a}\text{ is stable for }a>-1,
$$

and

$$
1-\sqrt{1+a}\text{ is stable for }a>0.
$$
