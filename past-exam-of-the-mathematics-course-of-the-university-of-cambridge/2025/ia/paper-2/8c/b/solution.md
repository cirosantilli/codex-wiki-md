<h1 id="8c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $m=1$, $Y=(1-\eta^2)/2$. Expanding both sides of the perturbation equation gives

$$
(1-\eta^2)y''-2\eta y'+6\lambda y=0.
$$

For $y=\sum_{r\ge0}a_r\eta^r$, coefficient comparison gives

$$
a_{r+2}=\frac{r(r+1)-6\lambda}{(r+2)(r+1)}a_r.
$$

The even and odd [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) terminate exactly when $6\lambda=k(k+1)$ with the corresponding parity. Thus every positive integer $k$ gives the regular [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) solution, a Legendre [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) up to scale.
