<h1 id="9f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With $p=(1+\varepsilon)\log n/(n-1)$, the [union bound](../../../../../../boole-s-inequality.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(L=0)\le n(1-p)^{n-1}
\le ne^{-p(n-1)}=n^{-\varepsilon}.}
$$
