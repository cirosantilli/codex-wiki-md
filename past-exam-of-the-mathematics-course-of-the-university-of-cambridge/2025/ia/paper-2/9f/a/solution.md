<h1 id="9f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $T$ is the number of triads, then

$$
\mathbb ET=\binom n3\frac18<\frac{n^3}{48}.
$$

Markov's inequality therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(T\ge n^3/24)\le\frac12.}
$$
