<h1 id="6c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $y=tz$ and $u=(x,z)^T$. The system becomes

$$
tu'=\begin{pmatrix}4&-6\\3&-5\end{pmatrix}u+t\binom11+\binom21.
$$

The [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) has eigenpairs $1,(2,1)^T$ and $-2,(1,1)^T$. A particular solution is $(-t/3-2,-1)^T$. Therefore

$$
x=2C_1t+C_2t^{-2}-\frac t3-2,
$$



$$
y=C_1t^2+C_2t^{-1}-t.
$$
