<h1 id="6c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $k\ne m$, the required Euler-equation solution is

$$
x(t)=\frac{t^k-t^m}{k^2-m^2}.
$$

For $k=m>0$, the limiting resonant form is

$$
x(t)=\frac{t^m\log t}{2m}.
$$

Both extend continuously to zero and satisfy the two [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md). If $k=m=0$, integration gives

$$
x=\frac12(\log t)^2+C\log t+D,
$$

which cannot have a finite zero [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) at $t=0$, so no such solution exists.
