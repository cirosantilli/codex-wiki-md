<h1 id="11f/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $\sigma^2=\operatorname{Var}(2^{X_1})\le\ell2^b$. If $\sigma=0$ the result is immediate. Otherwise the [central limit theorem](../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) applies to $L$, while the displayed threshold is at least $\alpha\sigma/\sqrt n$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb P\left(\frac{|L-\ell|}{\ell}\ge\alpha\sqrt{\frac{2^b}{n\ell}}\right)
\le2-2\Phi(\alpha).}
$$
