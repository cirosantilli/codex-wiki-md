↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $Z$ and $a>0$, the pointwise inequality $Z\ge a\mathbf1_{\{Z\ge a\}}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(Z\ge a)\le\frac{\mathbb EZ}{a}.}
$$
