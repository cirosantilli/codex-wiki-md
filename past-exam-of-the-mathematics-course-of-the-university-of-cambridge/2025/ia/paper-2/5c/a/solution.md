<h1 id="5c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Wronskian](../../../../../../wronskian.md) is $W=y_1y_2'-y_2y_1'$. Abel's identity gives

$$
W'=-pW.
$$

If $W\ne0$, the two column [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) in the displayed representation form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of $\mathbb R^2$ at each $x$. Differentiating the first component and comparing it with the second gives

$$
u'y_1+v'y_2=0.
$$

Substitution in the differential equation gives

$$
u'y_1'+v'y_2'=f.
$$

Solving this two-by-two system yields

$$
\boxed{u'=-\frac{y_2f}{W},\qquad v'=\frac{y_1f}{W}.}
$$
