<h1 id="2e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Prime exponents must obey simultaneous congruences modulo $2,3,5$. The choices

$$
v_2(n)=15,\qquad v_3(n)=10,\qquad v_5(n)=6
$$

satisfy the required congruences. Thus one example is

$$
\boxed{n=2^{15}3^{10}5^6.}
$$
