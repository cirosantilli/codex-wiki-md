<h1 id="6e/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Choose a prime with $p^2\mid n$, and set $a=0$, $b=n/p$. Then $b\not\equiv0\pmod n$, but $b^2=n^2/p^2$ is divisible by $n$. Therefore $a^k\equiv b^k\equiv0\pmod n$ for every $k>1$.
