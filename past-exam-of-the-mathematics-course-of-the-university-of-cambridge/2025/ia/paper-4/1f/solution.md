<h1 id="1f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1F](../1f.md)

The [series](../../../../../series-mathematics.md) $\sum a_n$ converges when its partial sums tend to a finite real [limit](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md). Put $m=n+1$. Then

$$
a_n=\frac{2\sqrt m}{\sqrt{m-1}+\sqrt{m+1}}-1>0
$$

by strict concavity of the square root. Taylor expansion at $m$ gives

$$
\sqrt{m-1}+\sqrt{m+1}=2\sqrt m\left(1-\frac1{8m^2}+O(m^{-4})\right),
$$

so $m^2a_n\to1/8$. [Limit](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) comparison with $\sum n^{-2}$ proves convergence.
