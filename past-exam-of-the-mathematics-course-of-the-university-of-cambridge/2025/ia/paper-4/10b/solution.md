<h1 id="10b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10B](../10b.md)

Binet's equation gives

$$
r=\frac{r_0}{1+e\cos\theta},\qquad r_0=\frac{l^2}{k}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{l=\sqrt{kr_0},\qquad E=\frac{mk(e^2-1)}{2r_0}.}
$$
