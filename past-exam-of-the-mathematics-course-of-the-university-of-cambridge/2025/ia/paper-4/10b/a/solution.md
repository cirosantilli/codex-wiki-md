<h1 id="10b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At infinity the specific energy is $v^2/2$ and $l=bv$, so

$$
e=\sqrt{1+\frac{b^2v^4}{k^2}}.
$$

The angle through which the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is deflected between the incoming and outgoing asymptotes is

$$
\boxed{\chi=2\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{k}{bv^2}\right).}
$$
