<h1 id="8a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) $(1,0,1)^T,(0,1,0)^T,(1,0,-1)^T$ are [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $B$ with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $2,1,0$. Applying the exponential to these eigenspaces gives

$$
e^B=\begin{pmatrix}(1+e^2)/2&0&(e^2-1)/2\\0&e&0\\(e^2-1)/2&0&(1+e^2)/2\end{pmatrix}.
$$
