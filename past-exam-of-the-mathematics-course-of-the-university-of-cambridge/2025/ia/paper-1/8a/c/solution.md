<h1 id="8a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $A=S\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)S^{-1}$. The power [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) gives $e^A=S\operatorname{diag}(e^{\lambda_1},\ldots,e^{\lambda_n})S^{-1}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\det(e^A)=\prod_i e^{\lambda_i}=e^{\sum_i\lambda_i}=e^{\operatorname{tr}A}.}
$$
