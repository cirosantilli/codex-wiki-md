↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The removable definition gives the entire [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md)

$$
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{z^{2n}}{(2n+1)!}.
$$

Therefore

$$
f^{(k)}(0)=\begin{cases}0,&k\text{ odd},\\(-1)^{k/2}/(k+1),&k\text{ even}.
\end{cases}
$$
