<h1 id="7a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) equation is $u_{i+1}=\lambda u_i$, so

$$
u=c(1,\lambda,\lambda^2,\ldots)^T.
$$

This is a nonzero square-summable [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) exactly when $c\ne0$ and $|\lambda|<1$. Hence every point of the open unit disc is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), with the displayed one-dimensional eigenspace.
