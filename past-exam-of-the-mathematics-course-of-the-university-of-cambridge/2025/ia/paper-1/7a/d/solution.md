<h1 id="7a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the transpose shift, $(Cu)_1=0$ and $(Cu)_i=u_{i-1}$ for $i\ge2$. If $Cu=\lambda u$, the first equation and the subsequent recurrence force every component to vanish, both for $\lambda=0$ and for $\lambda\ne0$. Thus $C$ has no [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), illustrating that a bounded infinite-dimensional operator need not have one.
