↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$ is a [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md) for which $\ker(A-\lambda I)$ contains a nonzero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md); that kernel is its eigenspace. The characteristic [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has degree $n$, so the [fundamental theorem of algebra](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-algebra.md) supplies at least one complex root and hence an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md). Its eigenspace dimension can be any integer from $1$ to $n$.
