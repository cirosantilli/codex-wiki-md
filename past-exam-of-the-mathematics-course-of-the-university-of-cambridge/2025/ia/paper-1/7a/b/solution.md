<h1 id="7a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With $\chi_A(t)=\det(tI-A)$, direct expansion gives

$$
\chi_A(t)=(t-3)(t-1)^2(t+1)(t+2).
$$

**Thus the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $3,1,-1,-2$. Kernel calculation gives [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) dimensions $1,2,1,1$, respectively.**
