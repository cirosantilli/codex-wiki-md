↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The distinguished unit [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is $n=(1,1,1)/\sqrt3$. Comparing diagonal, symmetric off-diagonal, and antisymmetric parts gives

$$
\alpha=a-\frac{b+c}{2},\qquad \beta=\frac32(b+c),\qquad \gamma=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}(b-c),
$$

so $A_{ij}=\alpha\delta_{ij}+\beta n_in_j+\gamma\varepsilon_{ijk}n_k$.
