<h1 id="12e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For continuous $f$, the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $F(x)=\int_a^xf(t)dt$ is [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) with $F\prime=f$. Conversely, if $F\prime$ is continuous, then $\int_a^bF\prime(t)dt=F(b)-F(a)$.
