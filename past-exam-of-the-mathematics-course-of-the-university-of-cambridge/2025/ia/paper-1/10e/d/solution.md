<h1 id="10e/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Both [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) are continuous at every $x>0$. At zero, $f_1(1/(\pi/2+2\pi n))=1$ while $f_1(1/(3\pi/2+2\pi n))=-1$, so $f_1$ is discontinuous. Since $|x\sin(1/x)|\le x\to0$, $f_2$ is continuous at zero and hence everywhere.
