<h1 id="10e/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $S=\{0\}\cup\{1/n:n\ge1\}$ and let $D$ be the Dirichlet [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md), equal to $1$ on rationals and $0$ on irrationals. Then $f(x)=d(x,S)D(x)$ is continuous exactly on $S$: the distance factor squeezes it to zero on $S$, while away from $S$ it is a positive continuous factor times an everywhere-discontinuous [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md).
