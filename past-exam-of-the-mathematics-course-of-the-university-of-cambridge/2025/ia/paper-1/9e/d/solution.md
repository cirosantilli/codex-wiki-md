<h1 id="9e/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write

$$
\frac{j}{j^2+j+1}=\frac1j+O(j^{-2})
$$

uniformly for $j\ge1$. The accumulated error from $j=n+1$ to $2n$ tends to zero, while the corresponding [harmonic sum](../../../../../../harmonic-sum.md) tends to $\log2$. Hence $b_n\to\log2$.
