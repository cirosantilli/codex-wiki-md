<h1 id="4e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The Darboux–Riemann criterion says that a [bounded function](../../../../../../bounded-function.md) on $[a,b]$ is Riemann integrable exactly when, for every $\varepsilon>0$, some partition $P$ satisfies $U(f,P)-L(f,P)<\varepsilon$. Indeed, every lower sum is at most every upper sum. Taking the supremum of lower sums and infimum of upper sums, the criterion makes their difference smaller than every positive $\varepsilon$, so they are equal; this common value is the Riemann [integral](../../../../../../integral.md).
