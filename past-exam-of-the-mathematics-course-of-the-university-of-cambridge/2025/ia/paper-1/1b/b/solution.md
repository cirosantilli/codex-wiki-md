<h1 id="1b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The vertices satisfy

$$
(z+1)^N-1=0.
$$

After removing the root $z=0$, the product of the other roots has [modulus](../../../../../../modulus.md) equal to the constant term of $((z+1)^N-1)/z$, namely $N$. Hence the product of the $N-1$ chord lengths from one vertex of a unit [regular polygon](../../../../../../regular-polygon.md) is $N$. Multiplying this identity over all $N$ vertices counts every chord twice, so the product of all chord lengths is $N^{N/2}$. Scaling the circle by $R$ scales each of its $N(N-1)/2$ chords by $R$, giving

$$
\boxed{N^{N/2}R^{N(N-1)/2}.}
$$
