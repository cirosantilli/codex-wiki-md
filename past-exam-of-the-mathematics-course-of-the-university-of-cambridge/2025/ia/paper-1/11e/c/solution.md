<h1 id="11e/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $f\prime\ge0$, the [mean value theorem](../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) gives $f(y)-f(x)=f\prime(c)(y-x)\ge0$. Conversely, if $f$ is increasing, every difference quotient with positive or negative increment is nonnegative; taking its [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) gives $f\prime(x)\ge0$.
