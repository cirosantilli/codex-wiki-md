<h1 id="11e/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $x>0$, apply the [mean value theorem](../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) to $[0,x]$: $f(x)/x=f\prime(c)$ for some $c<x$. Since $f\prime$ is increasing, $f(x)/x\le f\prime(x)$. Therefore

$$
\left(\frac{f(x)}x\right)\prime=\frac{xf\prime(x)-f(x)}{x^2}\ge0,
$$

and part (c) proves that the quotient is increasing.
