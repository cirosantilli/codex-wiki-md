<h1 id="9f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $V=V_1\oplus V_2$ were a nontrivial invariant decomposition, each restricted minimal [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) would be a power of $x-\alpha$ of degree at most $\dim V_i<n$. Their least common multiple is the minimal [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of $\phi$, contradicting its degree $n$.

Since $m_\phi$ has degree $n$, $\phi$ has a [cyclic vector](../../../../../../cyclic-vector.md) $b_1$. Put $b_k=\phi^{k-1}b_1$. Then $\phi(b_k)=b_{k+1}$ for $k<n$. In this [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is the [companion matrix](../../../../../../companion-matrix.md) with ones immediately below the diagonal and last column

$$
c_{k+1}=(-1)^{n-k+1}\binom nk\alpha^{n-k},\qquad0\le k<n,
$$

obtained by expanding $(x-\alpha)^n$.
