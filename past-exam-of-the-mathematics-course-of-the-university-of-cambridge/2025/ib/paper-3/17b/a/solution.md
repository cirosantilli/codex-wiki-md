<h1 id="17b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If the monic [orthogonal polynomial](../../../../../../orthogonal-polynomial.md) $p_n$ had fewer than $n$ distinct sign-changing zeros in $(-1,1)$, multiply the factors corresponding to those zeros to form a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $q$ of degree below $n$. Then $p_nq$ has one sign and is not identically zero, so its positive-weight [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) cannot vanish, contradicting orthogonality. Thus all $n$ zeros are distinct and lie in the open interval.
