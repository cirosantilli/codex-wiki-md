↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The three-term recurrence theorem with the displayed coefficients identifies $P_n$ with the monic orthogonal [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $p_n$. Part (b) says its zeros are precisely the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the real symmetric [Jacobi matrix](../../../../../../jacobi-matrix.md) $A_n$, and part (a) says those zeros are distinct and all belong to $(-1,1)$.
