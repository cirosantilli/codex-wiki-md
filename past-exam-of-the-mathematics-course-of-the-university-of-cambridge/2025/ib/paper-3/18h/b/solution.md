<h1 id="18h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Rao–Blackwell says that if $U$ estimates a parameter and $T$ is sufficient, then $U^*=E(U\mid T)$ has the same mean and no larger variance, with strict improvement unless $U$ is already a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) of $T$ almost surely. The mean statement is the tower property, while

$$
\operatorname{Var}U=\operatorname{Var}E(U\mid T)+E\operatorname{Var}(U\mid T)
$$

proves the variance claim.
