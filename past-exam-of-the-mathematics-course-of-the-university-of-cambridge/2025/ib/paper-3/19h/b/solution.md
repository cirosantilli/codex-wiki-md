<h1 id="19h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every probability [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $x$, antisymmetry gives $x^TAx=0$. Applying the minimax inequalities with the same strategy on both sides shows the lower value is at most zero and the upper value at least zero; transposition also changes the value to its negative. Hence the value is zero.
