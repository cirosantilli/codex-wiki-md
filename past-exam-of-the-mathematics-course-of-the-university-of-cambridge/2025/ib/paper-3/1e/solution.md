<h1 id="1e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1E](../1e.md)

If a finite [group](../../../../../group-split.md) is not simple, choose a maximal proper normal [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) and repeat inside it. Orders strictly decrease, so the process terminates; maximality makes every quotient simple.

For $S_4$ one [composition series](../../../../../composition-series.md) is

$$
S_4\triangleright A_4\triangleright V_4
\triangleright\langle(12)(34)\rangle\triangleright1.
$$

The successive quotients have orders $2,3,2,2$, hence are simple. As [subgroups](../../../../../subgroup.md) of $S_4$, the [groups](../../../../../group-split.md) $S_4,A_4,V_4$, and $1$ are normal. The selected order-two [subgroup](../../../../../subgroup.md) is not normal, since conjugation permutes the three double transpositions.
