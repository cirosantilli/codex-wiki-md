<h1 id="3a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The poles inside $C_n$ are zero and $\pm k\pi$ for $1\le k\le n$. At a nonzero pole,

$$
\operatorname{Res}_{k\pi}\frac1{z^3\sin z}=\frac{(-1)^k}{k^3\pi^3},
$$

and the residue at $-k\pi$ is its negative. At zero, the [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) contains only even powers because the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is even, so its residue is zero. All residues cancel and therefore

$$
\boxed{I_n=0\qquad(n=0,1,2,\ldots).}
$$
