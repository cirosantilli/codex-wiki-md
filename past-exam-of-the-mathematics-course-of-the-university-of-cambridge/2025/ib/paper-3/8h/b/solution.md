<h1 id="8h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A return to zero is possible only at even times, and

$$
P_0(X_{2k}=0)=\binom{2k}{k}[p(1-p)]^k.
$$

Stirling's estimate makes this asymptotic to a positive constant times

$$
\frac{[4p(1-p)]^k}{\sqrt k}.
$$

A state is recurrent exactly when the sum of its return probabilities diverges. If $p=1/2$, the resulting $k^{-1/2}$ [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) diverges. If $p\ne1/2$, then $4p(1-p)<1$ and the [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) converges geometrically. Thus the walk is recurrent exactly when $p=1/2$.
