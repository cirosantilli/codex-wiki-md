<h1 id="12/12-2a/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $h'(0)=0$, let $a_kz^k$ be the first nonzero Taylor term. Then $k\ge2$ and $a_k$ is real. But $\operatorname{Im}(a_kr^ke^{ik\theta})$ changes sign as $0<\theta<\pi$, and for small $r$ the leading term controls the sign. This contradicts positivity in the upper half-plane. Hence $h'(0)\ne0$.
