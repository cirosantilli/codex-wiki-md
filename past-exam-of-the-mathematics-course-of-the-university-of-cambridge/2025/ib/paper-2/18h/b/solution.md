<h1 id="18h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

From state $i>0$ the chain moves deterministically to $i-1$. From zero it jumps to $j$ when the next lifetime is $j+1$. Thus

$$
p_{i,i-1}=1\ (i>0),\qquad p_{0,j}=q_{j+1}\ (0\le j<N),
$$

with all other probabilities zero.
