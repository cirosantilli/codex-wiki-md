↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) gives

$$
f_n(x)=2n\int_{x-1/n}^{x+1/n}h'(t)\,dt.
$$

**Thus $f_n(x)\to4h'(x)$. Uniform continuity of $h'$ makes the difference from $4h'(x)$ uniformly small over every interval of radius $1/n$, so convergence is uniform on all of $\mathbb R$.**
