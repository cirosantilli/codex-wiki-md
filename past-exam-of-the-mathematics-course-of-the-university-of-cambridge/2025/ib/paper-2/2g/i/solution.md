↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Pointwise, $\tanh(nx)$ tends to $-1,0,1$ according as $x<0,x=0,x>0$. This [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) is discontinuous, whereas a [uniform limit](../../../../../../uniform-limit.md) of [continuous functions](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) is continuous. Convergence is therefore not uniform.
