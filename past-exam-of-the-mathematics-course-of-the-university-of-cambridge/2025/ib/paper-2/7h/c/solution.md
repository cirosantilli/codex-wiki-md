<h1 id="7h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $z=tx+(1-t)y$. Apply the hypothesis with first argument $z$ and second argument successively $x,y$:

$$
f(z)-f(x)\le(1-t)\lambda(z)^T(y-x),
$$



$$
f(z)-f(y)\le t\lambda(z)^T(x-y).
$$

Multiply these inequalities by $t$ and $1-t$ respectively and add. The right sides cancel, leaving $f(z)\le tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)$.
