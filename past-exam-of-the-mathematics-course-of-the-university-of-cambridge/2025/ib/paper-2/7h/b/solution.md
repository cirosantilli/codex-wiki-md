↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take $(t,x),(s,y)$ and $0\le\alpha\le1$, and put $T=\alpha t+(1-\alpha)s$. Then

$$
\frac{\alpha x+(1-\alpha)y}{T}
=\frac{\alpha t}{T}\frac xt+\frac{(1-\alpha)s}{T}\frac ys.
$$

Convexity of $f$, followed by multiplication by $T$, proves convexity of its perspective $g(t,x)=tf(x/t)$.
