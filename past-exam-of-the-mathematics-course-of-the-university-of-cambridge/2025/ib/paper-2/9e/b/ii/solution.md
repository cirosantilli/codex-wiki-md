<h1 id="9e/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The class $a$ is nilpotent exactly when $\phi(a)=0$. Divisibility by $p$ makes $a^e$ zero modulo $p^e$. Conversely, the image of a nilpotent in the field $R/(p)$ is nilpotent and therefore zero.
