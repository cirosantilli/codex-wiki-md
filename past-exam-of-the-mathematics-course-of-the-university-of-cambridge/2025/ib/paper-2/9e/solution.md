A [euclidean domain](../../../../../euclidean-domain.md) is an [integral](../../../../../integral.md) domain with a [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $d$ on nonzero elements such that for $a,b\ne0$ there are $q,r$ with $a=bq+r$ and either $r=0$ or $d(r)<d(b)$.
