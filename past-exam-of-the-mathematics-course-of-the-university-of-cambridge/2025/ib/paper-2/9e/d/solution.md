<h1 id="9e/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A unit modulo $d$ has nonzero image modulo every $p_i$. Any representative therefore satisfies the criterion in part (c), so it is already a unit modulo $I$. Its image is the given unit modulo $d$, proving surjectivity on unit [groups](../../../../../../group-split.md).
