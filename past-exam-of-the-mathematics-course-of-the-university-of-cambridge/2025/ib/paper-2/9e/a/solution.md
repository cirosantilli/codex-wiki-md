<h1 id="9e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a nonzero [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) $I$, choose $a\in I$ of minimal Euclidean value. Divide any $x\in I$ by $a$: $x=qa+r$. Then $r\in I$, and minimality forces $r=0$. Hence $I=(a)$; the zero [ideal](../../../../../../ideal.md) is also principal.
