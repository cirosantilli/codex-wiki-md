<h1 id="18h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

One of the $m+n$ balance equations is redundant, and the remaining $m+n-1$ have full rank. A nondegenerate [basic feasible solution](../../../../../../basic-feasible-solution.md) therefore has exactly $m+n-1$ positive variables. Equivalently, its positive cells form a spanning tree of the complete bipartite row--column graph.
