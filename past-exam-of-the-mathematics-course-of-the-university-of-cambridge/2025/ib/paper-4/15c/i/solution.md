↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Here $L_3=-i\hbar(x_1\partial_{x_2}-x_2\partial_{x_1})$. The radial factor and $x_3^n$ are annihilated by the angular [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md), while

$$
L_3(x_1+ix_2)=\hbar(x_1+ix_2).
$$

The product rule therefore gives

$$
L_3\chi_{m,n}=m\hbar\chi_{m,n}.
$$

Replacing $i$ by $-i$ gives [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $-m\hbar$ for $(x_1-ix_2)^mx_3^nf(r)$.
