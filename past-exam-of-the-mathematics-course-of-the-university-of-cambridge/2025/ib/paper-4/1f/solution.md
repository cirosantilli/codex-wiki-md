<h1 id="1f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1F](../1f.md)

The dual space $V^*$ is the [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of [linear maps](../../../../../linear-map.md) $V\to\mathbb F$. If $B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$, its dual family satisfies $v_i^*(v_j)=\delta_{ij}$. Every functional $f$ obeys

$$
f=\sum_i f(v_i)v_i^*,
$$

so the family spans; evaluation on each $v_j$ proves linear independence. Thus it is a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) without any prior dimension argument.

For $p=a+bt+ct^2$,

$$
f_0(p)=a,\quad f_1(p)=a+b/2+c/3,\quad f_2(p)=a-b/2+c/3.
$$

The [basis](../../../../../basis.md) dual to $(f_0,f_1,f_2)$ is

$$
p_0=1-3t^2,\qquad p_1=t+\frac32t^2,\qquad p_2=-t+\frac32t^2,
$$

as direct substitution gives $f_i(p_j)=\delta_{ij}$. Hence the three functionals form a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $P_2^*$.
