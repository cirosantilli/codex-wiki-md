↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The prime [ideals](../../../../../../../ideal.md) of $\mathbb R[x]$ are $(0)$ and the maximal [ideals](../../../../../../../ideal.md) generated by the monic irreducibles

$$
x-a\quad(a\in\mathbb R),\qquad (x-a)^2+b^2\quad(a\in\mathbb R, b>0).
$$

For a nonzero prime $I=(p)$, use the residue classes of $1,x,\ldots,x^{N-1}$, where $N=n\deg p$. Multiplication by $x$ on $\mathbb R[x]/(p^n)$ is represented by the [companion matrix](../../../../../../../companion-matrix.md) of the monic [polynomial](../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $p^n$. In particular, for $p=x-a$ this may equivalently be written as one size-$n$ [Jordan block](../../../../../../../jordan-block.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $a$. The [ideal](../../../../../../../ideal.md) $(0)$ does not give a finite-dimensional quotient.
