<h1 id="17h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The unrestricted maximum-likelihood estimates are $(\bar X,\bar Y)$, while the null fixes both means at zero. Hence

$$
-2\log\Lambda=m\bar X^2+n\bar Y^2.
$$

Under the null this is $\chi^2_2$, so the size-$\alpha$ [generalized likelihood-ratio test](../../../../../../generalized-likelihood-ratio-test.md) rejects exactly when

$$
\boxed{m\bar X^2+n\bar Y^2>F_2^{-1}(1-\alpha).}
$$
