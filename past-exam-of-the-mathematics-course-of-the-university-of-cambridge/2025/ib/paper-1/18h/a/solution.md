<h1 id="18h/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $S_{xx}=\sum X_i^2$. The likelihood equations give

$$
\hat\beta=\frac{\sum X_iY_i}{S_{xx}},\qquad
\hat\sigma^2=\frac1n\sum(Y_i-X_i\hat\beta)^2.
$$

These are orthogonal projections of a Gaussian [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) onto the span of $X$ and its orthogonal complement, hence are independent.
