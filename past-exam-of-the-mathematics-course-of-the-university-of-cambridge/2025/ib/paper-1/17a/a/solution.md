<h1 id="17a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a unit [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $v$, a [Householder reflection](../../../../../../householder-transformation.md) is $H=I-2vv^T$. Clearly $H^T=H$, and $H^TH=(I-2vv^T)^2=I$.
