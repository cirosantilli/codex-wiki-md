<h1 id="17a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For symmetric $A$, apply at stage $k$ the same reflection on both sides, choosing it to zero entries below the first subdiagonal in column $k$. [Orthogonal similarity](../../../../../../orthogonal-similarity.md) preserves symmetry, so the corresponding row entries vanish too and previous zeros remain. After finitely many stages $Q A Q^T$ is symmetric tridiagonal. Constructing each reflector uses only arithmetic and one square root.
