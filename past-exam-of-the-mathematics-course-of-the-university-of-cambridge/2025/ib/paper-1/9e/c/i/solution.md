<h1 id="9e/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Sylow gives $n_7\mid24$ and $n_7\equiv1\pmod7$. Simplicity excludes $n_7=1$, so $n_7=8$. Distinct order-seven [subgroups](../../../../../../../subgroup.md) intersect trivially, hence there are

$$
8(7-1)=48
$$

elements of order seven.
