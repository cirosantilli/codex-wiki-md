<h1 id="9e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $k=|G/H|$. The [coset action](../../../../../../coset-action.md) gives a nontrivial homomorphism $G\to S_k$ whose kernel is normal. Simplicity makes it injective. The sign map is trivial on the nonabelian simple image, so $G$ embeds in $A_k$. For $k\le4$, $A_k$ is solvable, as are its [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md), whereas a nonabelian simple [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is not. Hence $k\ge5$.
