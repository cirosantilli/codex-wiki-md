<h1 id="4c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4C](../4c.md)

For every [tangent vector](../../../../../tangent-vector.md) $v\perp x_0$, stationarity of $Q$ on the sphere gives $2v^TAx_0=0$. Hence $Ax_0$ is parallel to $x_0$, say $Ax_0=Ex_0$. Taking the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product.md) with $x_0$ gives $E=Q(x_0)$. The spectral theorem shows that this is the largest [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md).

For $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\t&1\end{pmatrix}$ the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1\pm t$, so

$$
E(t)=1+|t|.
$$

Its graph is a V translated upward and is convex.
