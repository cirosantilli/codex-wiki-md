<h1 id="16d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the downstream tissue [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) as gauge zero. The two transverse [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) fluxes cancel. The force of the fluid on the junction is axial and equals

$$
F=\left[A(p_{\rm up}-p_{\rm down})+\rho q(U-v\cos\alpha)\right]e_x,
$$

where $U=q/A$, $v=q/(2a)$ and the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) difference is that in part (i).
