<h1 id="14c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [14C](../14c.md)

Write $E=-\hbar^2\kappa^2/(2m)$. An even [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md) is proportional to $\cos(kx)$ inside the well and to $e^{-\kappa|x|}$ outside, where

$$
k^2=\frac{m}{a\hbar^2}-\kappa^2.
$$

Continuity of the logarithmic [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) at $a$ gives

$$
\kappa=k\tan(ka).
$$

For sufficiently small $a$, $ka$ lies in the first monotone branch, so this equation has exactly one positive root and the even state is unique up to scale.

As $a\downarrow0$, $\kappa\sim k^2a\to m/\hbar^2$. Hence

$$
E_0=-\frac{m}{2\hbar^2}.
$$

The wells converge distributionally to $V_0(x)=-\delta(x)$, whose normalized even [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md) is

$$
\psi_0(x)=\sqrt{\frac m{\hbar^2}}e^{-m|x|/\hbar^2}.
$$

Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) jump is precisely the one imposed by the [delta potential](../../../../../delta-potential.md).
