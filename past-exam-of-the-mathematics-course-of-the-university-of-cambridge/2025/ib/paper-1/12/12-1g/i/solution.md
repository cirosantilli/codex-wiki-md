<h1 id="12/12-1g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $\csc^2z=z^{-2}+1/3+O(z^2)$, the principal parts cancel and $f_1=-1/3+O(z^2)$. The singularity is removable.
