↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Termwise integration of the exponential [series](../../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) gives

$$
f_2(z)=2\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n}{n!(2n+1)}z^{-2n}.
$$

There are infinitely many negative Laurent coefficients, so zero is an [essential singularity](../../../../../../../essential-singularity.md).
