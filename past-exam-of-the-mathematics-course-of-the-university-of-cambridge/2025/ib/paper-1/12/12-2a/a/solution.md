<h1 id="12/12-2a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For distinct $z,w$ in the convex disc, integrate along the segment:

$$
f(z)-f(w)=f'(z_0)(z-w)+\int_w^z(f'(\zeta)-f'(z_0))\,d\zeta.
$$

The second term has [modulus](../../../../../../../modulus.md) strictly below $|f'(z_0)||z-w|$, so the sum cannot vanish. Thus $f$ is one-to-one.
