<h1 id="12/12-2a/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose an entire $F$ with real part $u$. The bound implies that $e^F$ has at most [polynomial](../../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) growth, so Cauchy's estimates make it a [polynomial](../../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md). It has no zeros, hence that [polynomial](../../../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is constant. Therefore $F$, and in particular $u$, is constant.
