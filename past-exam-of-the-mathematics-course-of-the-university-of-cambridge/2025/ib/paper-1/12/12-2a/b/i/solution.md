<h1 id="12/12-2a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Harmonic means twice continuously [differentiable](../../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) with $u_{xx}+u_{yy}=0$. On the simply connected plane, choose an entire [harmonic conjugate](../../../../../../../../harmonic-conjugate.md) so $F=u+iv$ is entire. If $u\ge0$, then $e^{-F}$ is bounded; Liouville's theorem makes it, and hence $u$, constant.
