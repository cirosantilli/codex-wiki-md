<h1 id="41d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Constructing the reflected vector $y$ costs $O(N)$ assignments. Part (a) computes all of its Fourier coefficients in $O(N\log N)$ multiplications, and part (b) recovers the required coefficients $Z_0,\ldots,Z_{N-1}$ with one phase multiplication per coefficient. Hence the whole [discrete cosine transform](../../../../../../discrete-cosine-transform.md) costs $O(N\log N)$ multiplications.
