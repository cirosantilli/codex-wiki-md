<h1 id="38e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a covariant rank-two tensor,

$$
\nabla_\alpha g_{\mu\nu}
=\partial_\alpha g_{\mu\nu}
-\Gamma^\rho_{\alpha\mu}g_{\rho\nu}
-\Gamma^\rho_{\alpha\nu}g_{\mu\rho}.
$$

Impose [metric compatibility](../../../../../../metric-compatibility.md) $\nabla g=0$, write this equation and its two cyclic permutations in $\alpha,\mu,\nu$, and use the assumed torsion-free symmetry of the connection. Solving gives the unique Levi--Civita connection

$$
\boxed{
\Gamma^\gamma_{\alpha\beta}
=\frac12g^{\gamma\delta}
\left(\partial_\alpha g_{\beta\delta}
+\partial_\beta g_{\alpha\delta}
-\partial_\delta g_{\alpha\beta}\right).}
$$
