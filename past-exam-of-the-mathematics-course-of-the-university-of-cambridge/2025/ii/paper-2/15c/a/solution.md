<h1 id="15c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The order of $a$ modulo $N$ is the least positive integer $r$ such that

$$
\boxed{a^r\equiv1\pmod N.}
$$

It exists because $a$ is a unit in the finite group $(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times$.
