<h1 id="16h/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $T_G$ is inconsistent. By the [compactness theorem](../../../../../../../compactness-theorem.md), some finite subset $\Delta\subseteq T_G$ is inconsistent. Only finitely many added symbols $f_g$ occur in $\Delta$; let $X\subseteq G$ be the finite set of their indices. Every sentence of $\Delta$ belongs to the sublanguage $L_X$, so

$$
\Delta\subseteq T_G\cap L_X.
$$

**Consequently $T_G\cap L_X$ is inconsistent.**
