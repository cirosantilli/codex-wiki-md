<h1 id="28l/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In the stable regime, $L$ is exactly the queue-length process of an $M/M/1$ queue with arrival rate $\lambda$ and effective service rate $\mu(1-p)$. By [Burke theorem](../../../../../../../burke-s-theorem.md), when this queue is in equilibrium its external departure process is a Poisson process of rate

$$
\boxed{\lambda.}
$$
