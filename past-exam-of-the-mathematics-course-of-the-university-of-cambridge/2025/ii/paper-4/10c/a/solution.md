<h1 id="10c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Apply a [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) to Alice's qubit, followed by a [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md) from Alice to Bob and another from Alice to Charlie:

$$
|000\rangle
\longmapsto
\frac{|000\rangle+|100\rangle}{\sqrt2}
\longmapsto
\frac{|000\rangle+|110\rangle}{\sqrt2}
\longmapsto
\frac{|000\rangle+|111\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

**Thus this circuit gives the required [quantum circuit preparation of a three-qubit GHZ state](../../../../../../quantum-circuit-preparation-of-a-three-qubit-ghz-state.md).**
