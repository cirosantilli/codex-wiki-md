A [class in set theory](../../../../../../class-set-theory.md) is a collection

$$
M=\{x\in V:V\models\psi(x,a_1,\ldots,a_r)\}
$$

defined by a formula $\psi$, with set parameters if these are allowed by the chosen convention. It is a set-class if there is an $m\in V$ whose elements are exactly the members of $M$. Otherwise it is a [proper class](../../../../../../proper-class.md).
