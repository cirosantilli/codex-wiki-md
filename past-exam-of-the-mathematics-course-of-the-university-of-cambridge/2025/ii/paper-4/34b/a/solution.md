<h1 id="34b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [symmetric gauge](../../../../../../symmetric-gauge.md),

$$
\partial_iA_j+\partial_jA_i=0,
\qquad
\partial_iA_j-\partial_jA_i
=\varepsilon_{ijk}B_k.
$$

Together with $[p_i,f(x)]=-i\hbar\partial_i f$, this gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
[p_i+eA_i,p_j-eA_j]
&=-e[p_i,A_j]+e[A_i,p_j]\\
&=ie\hbar(\partial_iA_j+\partial_jA_i)=0,
\end{aligned}
$$

whereas

$$
\begin{aligned}
[p_i+eA_i,p_j+eA_j]
&=e[p_i,A_j]+e[A_i,p_j]\\
&=ie\hbar(\partial_jA_i-\partial_iA_j)\\
&=-ie\hbar\varepsilon_{ijk}B_k.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{[p_i+eA_i,p_j-eA_j]=0},
\qquad
\boxed{[p_i+eA_i,p_j+eA_j]
=-ie\hbar\varepsilon_{ijk}B_k}.
$$

These are the commutators of the [kinetic momentum and magnetic pseudomomentum](../../../../../../kinetic-momentum-and-magnetic-pseudomomentum.md).
