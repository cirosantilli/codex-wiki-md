<h1 id="32a/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The result follows by induction. It is true for $n=1$. If

$$
F^n(x)=2^nx\pmod1,
$$

then

$$
F^{n+1}(x)
=F(F^n(x))
=2(2^nx\pmod1)\pmod1
=2^{n+1}x\pmod1.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{F^n(x)=2^nx\pmod1}.
$$
