<h1 id="23h/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $x=0$, the claim is immediate. Otherwise weak convergence gives

$$
\lVert x\rVert^2
=\langle x,x\rangle
=\lim_{n\to\infty}\langle x_n,x\rangle.
$$

By Cauchy-Schwarz,

$$
\langle x_n,x\rangle
\leq\lVert x_n\rVert\lVert x\rVert.
$$

Taking the lower limit and dividing by $\lVert x\rVert$ proves the [weak lower semicontinuity of the Hilbert norm](../../../../../../../weak-lower-semicontinuity-of-the-hilbert-norm.md):

$$
\boxed{\lVert x\rVert\leq\liminf_n\lVert x_n\rVert}.
$$
