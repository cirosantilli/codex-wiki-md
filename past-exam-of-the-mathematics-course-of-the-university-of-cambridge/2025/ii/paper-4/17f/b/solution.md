<h1 id="17f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [graph Ramsey number](../../../../../../graph-ramsey-number.md) $R(G)$ is the least positive integer $n$ for which every red-blue colouring of $K_n$ contains a monochromatic copy of $G$. If $g=|V(G)|$, the Ramsey theorem gives a monochromatic $K_g$ in every colouring of $K_{R(g)}$, and that clique contains $G$. Hence

$$
\boxed{R(G)\leq R(g)<\infty.}
$$
