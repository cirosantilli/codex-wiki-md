<h1 id="8b/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $Q=p$ and $P=\lambda q$,

$$
\{Q,P\}_{q,p}=-\lambda.
$$

The map is therefore canonical only for

$$
\boxed{\lambda=-1}.
$$

Then $Q=p$ and $P=-q$. The [type-one generating function for a canonical transformation](../../../../../../../type-one-generating-function-for-a-canonical-transformation.md)

$$
\boxed{\Phi(q,Q)=qQ}
$$

gives

$$
p=\Phi_q=Q,
\qquad
P=-\Phi_Q=-q,
$$

as required.
