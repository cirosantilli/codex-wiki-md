<h1 id="39a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the frame in which the shock is stationary, the upstream gas approaches it with speed $V$, while the downstream gas recedes from it with speed $V-u_1$. The [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions for a perfect gas](../../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md) are therefore

$$
\rho_0V=\rho_1(V-u_1),
$$



$$
p_0+\rho_0V^2
=p_1+\rho_1(V-u_1)^2,
$$

and

$$
\frac{\gamma p_0}{(\gamma-1)\rho_0}+\frac12V^2
=\frac{\gamma p_1}{(\gamma-1)\rho_1}
+\frac12(V-u_1)^2.
$$

These equations express conservation of mass flux, momentum flux, and total specific enthalpy respectively.
