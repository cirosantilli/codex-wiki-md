<h1 id="39a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The momentum equation from part (c) is

$$
p_1-p_0=\frac13\rho_0V^2.
$$

Since $p_1-p_0=3p_0$, it follows that

$$
\rho_0V^2=9p_0,
\qquad
\boxed{V=3\sqrt{\frac{p_0}{\rho_0}}}.
$$

This completes the [piston-driven shock with specific-heat ratio three](../../../../../../piston-driven-shock-with-specific-heat-ratio-three.md) calculation.
