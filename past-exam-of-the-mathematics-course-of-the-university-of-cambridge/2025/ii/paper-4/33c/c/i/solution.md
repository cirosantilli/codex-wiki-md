<h1 id="33c/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The states $|n,\ell,m\rangle$ diagonalize $L^2$:

$$
L^2|n,\ell,m\rangle
=\hbar^2\ell(\ell+1)|n,\ell,m\rangle.
$$

The perturbation therefore introduces no mixing. The [orbital-angular-momentum-squared perturbation of hydrogen](../../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum-squared-perturbation-of-hydrogen.md) leaves

$$
|2,0,0\rangle
$$

at energy

$$
\boxed{E_2},
$$

while

$$
|2,1,-1\rangle,\quad |2,1,0\rangle,\quad |2,1,1\rangle
$$

remain triply degenerate at

$$
\boxed{E_2+2g\hbar^2}.
$$

These statements are exact because the unperturbed Hamiltonian commutes with $L^2$.
