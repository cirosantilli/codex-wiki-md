<h1 id="6a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The population map is

$$
x_{n+1}=g(x_n),
\qquad
g(x)=x\bigl(r+ke^{-\lambda x}\bigr).
$$

A positive equilibrium satisfies

$$
1=r+ke^{-\lambda x_*},
$$

and hence

$$
x_*=\frac1\lambda\log\left(\frac{k}{1-r}\right).
$$

It is strictly positive exactly when

$$
\boxed{k>1-r}.
$$

This is the nonzero equilibrium of the [survival-augmented Ricker map](../../../../../../survival-augmented-ricker-map.md).
