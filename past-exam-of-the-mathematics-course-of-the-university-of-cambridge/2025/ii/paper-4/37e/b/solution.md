<h1 id="37e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a slowly moving particle in a static weak field, the spatial part of the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) reduces to

$$
\frac{d^2x^i}{dt^2}=-\Gamma^i{}_{00}.
$$

To first order in the perturbation,

$$
\Gamma^i{}_{00}
=-\frac12\delta^{ij}\partial_jh_{00},
$$

and hence

$$
\frac{dv^i}{dt}=\frac12\delta^{ij}\partial_jh_{00}.
$$

Newtonian acceleration is $-\delta^{ij}\partial_j\Phi$, so $h_{00}=-2\Phi$. Substitution into part (a), followed by the [Poisson equation](../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) $\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho$, gives

$$
\kappa\rho=-\nabla^2h_{00}=2\nabla^2\Phi=8\pi G\rho.
$$

Thus the [Newtonian limit of general relativity](../../../../../../newtonian-limit.md) fixes

$$
\boxed{h_{00}=-2\Phi,\qquad \kappa=8\pi G}.
$$
