<h1 id="25i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An orientation of $S$ chooses a unit normal at every point. The [Gauss map](../../../../../../gauss-map.md) is

$$
N:S\longrightarrow S^2,
\qquad p\longmapsto N(p).
$$

For the unit tangent $T=\dot\alpha$, the [surface covariant derivative](../../../../../../surface-covariant-derivative.md) is the tangential projection

$$
D_sT=(\ddot\alpha)^{\top}.
$$

The signed [geodesic curvature](../../../../../../geodesic-curvature.md) is

$$
\boxed{\kappa_g=\langle D_sT,N\times T\rangle}.
$$

Because $|T|=1$, $D_sT$ is perpendicular to $T$ and hence

$$
D_sT=\kappa_g(N\times T).
$$

**Consequently $\kappa_g=0$ means $D_sT=0$: the tangent is parallel along $\alpha$, so $\alpha$ is a [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md).**
