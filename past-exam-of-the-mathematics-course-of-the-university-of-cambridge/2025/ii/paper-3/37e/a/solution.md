<h1 id="37e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write

$$
f(r)=1-\frac{2M}{r}.
$$

For a radial null ray, the [Schwarzschild spacetime](../../../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime.md) line element gives

$$
0=-f\,dt^2+f^{-1}dr^2,
\qquad
\frac{dt}{dr}=\pm f^{-1}.
$$

Outside the horizon an ingoing ray has $dr/dt<0$, so it takes the minus sign. Since the [Schwarzschild tortoise coordinate](../../../../../../schwarzschild-tortoise-coordinate.md) obeys $dr_*/dr=f^{-1}$,

$$
\frac{d}{dr}(t+r_*)=0.
$$

It follows that

$$
\boxed{v=t+r+2M\log\left|\frac{r}{2M}-1\right|}
$$

is constant along every ingoing radial light ray.
