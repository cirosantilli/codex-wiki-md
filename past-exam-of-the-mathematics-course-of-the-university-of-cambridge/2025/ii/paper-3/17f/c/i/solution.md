<h1 id="17f/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each crossing pair, delete one of its two edges. At most $t(G)$ distinct edges are deleted, and the graph left behind has a crossing-free drawing. The [planar graph edge bound](../../../../../../../planar-graph-edge-bound.md) therefore gives

$$
e-t(G)\leq3n-6,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{t(G)\geq e-3n+6}.
$$

This is the linear estimate underlying the [crossing lemma](../../../../../../../crossing-lemma.md).
