The [risk of a decision rule](../../../../../../../risk-of-a-decision-rule.md) is its expected loss at a fixed parameter value:

$$
R(\theta,\delta)
=\mathbb E_\theta\!\left[L(\delta(X),\theta)\right].
$$

For a prior $\pi$, a [Bayes estimator](../../../../../../../bayes-estimator.md) minimizes the integrated risk

$$
r(\pi,\delta)
=\int_\Theta R(\theta,\delta)\,\pi(d\theta),
$$

or equivalently minimizes posterior expected loss for almost every observation. A [minimax decision rule](../../../../../../../minimax-decision-rule.md) minimizes

$$
\sup_{\theta\in\Theta}R(\theta,\delta)
$$

over all decision rules.
