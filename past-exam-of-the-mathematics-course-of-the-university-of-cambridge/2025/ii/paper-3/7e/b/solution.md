<h1 id="7e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

As $z\to0$ within the right half-plane, the functional equation $\Gamma(1+z)=z\Gamma(z)$ and continuity at $1$ give

$$
\Gamma(z)\sim\frac1z,
\qquad
\Gamma(2z)\sim\frac1{2z}.
$$

Using part (a),

$$
B(z,z)=\frac{\Gamma(z)^2}{\Gamma(2z)}
\sim\frac{z^{-2}}{(2z)^{-1}}
=\boxed{\frac2z}.
$$
