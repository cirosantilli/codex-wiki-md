<h1 id="19j/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

If the eigenvalues of $\rho(g)$ are $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_d$, then those of $g$ on $\bigwedge^2V$ are $\lambda_i\lambda_j$ for $i<j$. Therefore the [Character of an exterior square](../../../../../../character-of-an-exterior-square.md) is

$$
\boxed{
\chi_{\wedge^2V}(g)
=\sum_{i<j}\lambda_i\lambda_j
=\frac12\left(\chi_V(g)^2-\chi_V(g^2)\right)}.
$$
