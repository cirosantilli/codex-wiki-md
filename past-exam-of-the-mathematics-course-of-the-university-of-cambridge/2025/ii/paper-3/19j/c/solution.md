<h1 id="19j/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For an irreducible $V$, [Schur lemma](../../../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) makes the central element $-I$ act by a scalar $\varepsilon$. Since $(-I)^2=I$, $\varepsilon=\mathord\pm1$, and on $V\otimes V$ it acts by $\varepsilon^2=1$. Thus $\{\mathord\pm I\}$ lies in the kernel and the action factors through $SU_2/\{\mathord\pm I\}$, as in [central parity on SU2 tensor products](../../../../../../central-parity-on-su2-tensor-products.md).

This fails for reducible representations. Take $V=V_0\oplus V_1$. On the summand $V_0\otimes V_1\subset V\otimes V$, the element $-I$ acts as $-1$, so the tensor-square action does not factor through the quotient.
