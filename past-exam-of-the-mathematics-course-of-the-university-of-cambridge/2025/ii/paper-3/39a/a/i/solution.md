<h1 id="39a/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a plane wave $\phi=e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$, the [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md) gives

$$
-\omega^2+c^2k^2+A^2c^2=0.
$$

Thus the positive-frequency dispersion relation is

$$
\boxed{\omega(k)=c\sqrt{k^2+A^2}}.
$$
