<h1 id="25i/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A unit-speed surface curve $\alpha$ is a [geodesic](../../../../../../../geodesic.md) when its acceleration is normal to the surface, equivalently when its covariant acceleration vanishes. In coordinates $u^1,u^2$, the [Christoffel symbol](../../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md)s of the Levi-Civita connection are

$$
\Gamma^k_{ij}
=\frac12g^{k\ell}
\left(\partial_i g_{j\ell}
+\partial_j g_{i\ell}
-\partial_\ell g_{ij}\right).
$$

The coordinate functions of a geodesic satisfy the [geodesic equation](../../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\ddot u^k+\Gamma^k_{ij}\dot u^i\dot u^j=0}.
$$
