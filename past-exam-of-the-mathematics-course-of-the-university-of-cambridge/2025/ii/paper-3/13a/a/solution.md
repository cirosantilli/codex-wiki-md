<h1 id="13a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

This [birth-death process](../../../../../../birth-death-process.md) has birth rates $b_n=\alpha+\beta n$ and death rates $d_n=\gamma n(n-1)$. With $P_{-1}=0$, its [birth-death master equation](../../../../../../birth-death-master-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{
\frac{dP_n}{dt}
=[\alpha+\beta(n-1)]P_{n-1}
+\gamma(n+1)nP_{n+1}
-[\alpha+\beta n+\gamma n(n-1)]P_n.}
$$

The first two terms are inflow by a birth from $n-1$ and a death from $n+1$; the bracketed term is the total outflow from $n$.
