<h1 id="15c/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $Uv=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$, unitarity gives

$$
\|v\|=\|Uv\|=\|\lambda v\|=|\lambda|\|v\|.
$$

**Hence $|\lambda|=1$. Every eigenvalue of a unitary matrix therefore lies on the unit circle.**
