<h1 id="40a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\rho'$ and $p'$ be the density and [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) perturbations. Homentropy gives $p'=c_0^2\rho'$. The linearized mass and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) equations are

$$
\rho'_t+\rho_0\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0,
\qquad
\rho_0\mathbf u_t=-\nabla p'.
$$

For [potential flow](../../../../../../potential-flow.md), $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$, and the [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) equation integrates to

$$
p'=-\rho_0\phi_t,
$$

where a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) of time has been absorbed into $\phi$. Substitution into mass conservation yields

$$
-\frac{\rho_0}{c_0^2}\phi_{tt}+\rho_0\nabla^2\phi=0,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{\phi_{tt}-c_0^2\nabla^2\phi=0.}
$$
