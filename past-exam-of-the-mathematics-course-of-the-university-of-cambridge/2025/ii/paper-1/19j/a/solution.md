<h1 id="19j/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Over $\mathbb C$, the operator representing $r$ has an [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $v$ with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda\ne0$. The relation $trt^{-1}=r^{-1}$ gives

$$
r(tv)=t(r^{-1}v)=\lambda^{-1}tv.
$$

**Therefore $\operatorname{span}\{v,tv\}$ is a nonzero $G$-invariant subspace of dimension at most two. If $V$ is irreducible it equals this subspace, so $\dim V\leq2$.**
