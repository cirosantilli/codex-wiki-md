<h1 id="39d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The steady Stokes equations are linear and contain no inertial term. If every imposed boundary [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) and body force is reversed, then the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) and [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) departure from any uniform reference [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) reverse sign. Thus every fluid particle retraces its path in the reversed experiment. This is the principle of kinematic reversibility.
