**Yes.** Define the probability measure

$$
\mu=\sum_{n=1}^\infty2^{-n}\nu_n.
$$

Countable additivity follows by Tonelli's theorem and $\mu(E)=\sum_n2^{-n}=1$. If $\mu(A)=0$, every nonnegative term $2^{-n}\nu_n(A)$ is zero, so $\nu_n(A)=0$ for every $n$. Hence every $\nu_n\ll\mu$.
