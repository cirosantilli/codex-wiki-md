↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $0<p\leq1$, the elementary bound $t^p\leq1+t$ gives

$$
\int_Ew^p,d\mu\leq\mu(E)+\int_Ew,d\mu<\infty.
$$

**No exponent $p>1$ is forced.** On $E=(0,1)$ with Lebesgue measure, take $w(x)=x^{-1/p}$. Then $w$ is positive and integrable, so $d\nu=w,d\mu$ defines a finite measure mutually absolutely continuous with $\mu$, but

$$
\int_0^1w(x)^pdx=\int_0^1\frac{dx}{x}=\infty.
$$

**Thus the required range is exactly $0<p\leq1$.**
