<h1 id="30k/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $X=\theta^TZ$,

$$
\mathbb EX=\theta^Tb,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=\theta^TV\theta.
$$

The objective has [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) $b-V\theta$ and Hessian $-V$, which is negative definite. Its unique maximizer is therefore

$$
\boxed{\theta_M=V^{-1}b}.
$$
