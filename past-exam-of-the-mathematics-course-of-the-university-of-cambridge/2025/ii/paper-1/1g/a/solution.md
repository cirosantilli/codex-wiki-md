↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Starting at $r=\lceil\sqrt{14351}\rceil=120$, one immediately finds

$$
r^2-N=120^2-14351=49=7^2.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{14351=(120-7)(120+7)=113\cdot127.}
$$
