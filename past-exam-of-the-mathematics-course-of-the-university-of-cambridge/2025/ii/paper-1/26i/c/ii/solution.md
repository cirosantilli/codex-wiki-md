**No.** The set is closed and bounded in the finite-dimensional space $S_3(\mathbb R)$, hence compact and nonempty. A global parametrization would be a homeomorphism from a nonempty open subset of $\mathbb R^2$ onto this compact surface, but no nonempty open subset of $\mathbb R^2$ is compact.
