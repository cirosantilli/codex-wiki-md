<h1 id="26i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\operatorname{Skew}_{2n}$ be the skew-symmetric $2n$ by $2n$ [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) and define

$$
F:M_{2n}(\mathbb R)\to\operatorname{Skew}_{2n},\qquad F(A)=A^TJA.
$$

Then $Sp_n(\mathbb R)=F^{-1}(J)$, and

$$
DF_A(H)=H^TJA+A^TJH.
$$

At a symplectic $A$, put $H=AX$. The [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) becomes $X^TJ+JX$. Given skew $K$, choosing $X=-\tfrac12JK$ gives $X^TJ+JX=K$, so the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is surjective.

The [preimage theorem](../../../../../../preimage-theorem.md) makes the level set a submanifold. Since

$$
\dim M_{2n}=4n^2,\qquad \dim\operatorname{Skew}_{2n}=\frac{(2n)(2n-1)}2=2n^2-n,
$$

its dimension is $2n^2+n$.
