<h1 id="33d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substituting the displayed [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) into $V_x-U_y+[U,V]$ makes every off-diagonal entry vanish. The two nonzero diagonal entries are opposites, the first being

$$
-u_{xy}+e^u-e^{-2u}.
$$

Thus compatibility is equivalent to the [Tzitzeica equation](../../../../../../tzitzeica-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{u_{xy}=F(u)=e^u-e^{-2u}}.
$$
