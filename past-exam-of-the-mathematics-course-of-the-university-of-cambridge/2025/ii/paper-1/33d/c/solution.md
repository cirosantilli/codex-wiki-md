<h1 id="33d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The flow of $x\partial_x-\alpha y\partial_y$ is

$$
G_\varepsilon:(x,y,u)\longmapsto(e^\varepsilon x,e^{-\alpha\varepsilon}y,u).
$$

Under the induced action on solutions, $u_{xy}$ acquires the factor $e^{(\alpha-1)\varepsilon}$, while $e^u-e^{-2u}$ is unchanged. The equation is invariant for every $\varepsilon$ exactly when

$$
\boxed{\alpha=1.}
$$
