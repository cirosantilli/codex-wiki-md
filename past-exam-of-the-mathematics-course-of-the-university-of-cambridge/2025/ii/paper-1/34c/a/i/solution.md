↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using $B^2=0$ and $BB^\dagger=1-B^\dagger B$,

$$
H^2=B^\dagger BB^\dagger B
=B^\dagger(1-B^\dagger B)B=H.
$$

**Thus $H$ is a projection and its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $0$ and $1$.**
