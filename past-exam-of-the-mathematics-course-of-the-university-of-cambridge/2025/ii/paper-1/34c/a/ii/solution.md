<h1 id="34c/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The state $B|1\rangle$ has norm

$$
\lVert B|1\rangle\rVert^2=\langle1|B^\dagger B|1\rangle=1
$$

and satisfies $H B|1\rangle=B^\dagger B^2|1\rangle=0$. Hence it is the normalized ground state, denoted $|0\rangle$. Also

$$
\lVert B^\dagger|1\rangle\rVert^2
=\langle1|BB^\dagger|1\rangle
=\langle1|(1-H)|1\rangle=0,
$$

so

$$
B|1\rangle=|0\rangle,\qquad B^\dagger|1\rangle=0.
$$
