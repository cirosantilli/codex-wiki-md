<h1 id="34c/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Define $|\pm\rangle_1=(|0\rangle_1\pm|1\rangle_1)/\sqrt2$. Since $B_1+B_1^\dagger$ is the Pauli $X$ operator on the first oscillator, the exact [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are

$$
|\pm\rangle_1\otimes|n_2\rangle_2,
$$

with energies

$$
E_{\pm,n_2}=\pm g+E_2n_2,\qquad n_2=0,1.
$$
