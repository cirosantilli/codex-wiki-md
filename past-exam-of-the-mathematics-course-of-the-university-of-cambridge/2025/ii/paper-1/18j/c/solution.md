<h1 id="18j/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For an irreducible cubic over $\mathbb Q$, the Galois [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is $A_3$ when its discriminant is a rational square and $S_3$ otherwise. The rational-root test shows that the first two cubics are irreducible.

For $X^3-21X-22$,

$$
\Delta=23976=2^3\,3^4\,37
$$

is not a square, so the Galois [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is $S_3$.

For $X^3-21X-28$,

$$
\Delta=15876=126^2,
$$

so the Galois [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is $A_3\cong C_3$.

Finally,

$$
X^3-21X-34=(X+2)(X^2-2X-17),
$$

whose remaining roots are $1\pm3\sqrt2$. Its splitting field is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$ and its Galois [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) is $C_2$.
