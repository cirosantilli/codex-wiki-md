<h1 id="24g/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The equation factors as

$$
zw-2iw-iz-2=(z-2i)(w-i).
$$

Thus $Y$ is the union of the two complex lines $z=2i$ and $w=i$, meeting at $(2i,i)$. A sufficiently small neighborhood of the intersection, with that point removed, has two connected components, whereas a punctured disc is connected. The intersection therefore has no neighborhood homeomorphic to an open subset of $\mathbb C$. Hence $Y$ cannot be a Riemann surface.
