<h1 id="12f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $K=\{w:w\in W_w\}$ and let $e_\varnothing$ be any index for the empty language. The proof of [Rice theorem](../../../../../../rice-s-theorem.md) gives

$$
e_\varnothing\notin I\implies K\leq_m I,
\qquad e_\varnothing\in I\implies\mathbb B\setminus K\leq_m I.
$$

In either case choose a language on the opposite side of the nontrivial property and switch from the empty enumerator to its enumerator if the simulated diagonal computation halts.
