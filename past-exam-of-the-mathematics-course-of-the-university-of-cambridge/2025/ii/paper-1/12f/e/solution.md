<h1 id="12f/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Since the empty language does not lie in

$$
\mathbf{Two}=\{w:|W_w|\geq2\},
$$

Rice's construction gives $K\leq_m\mathbf{Two}$. Conversely, $\mathbf{Two}$ is computably enumerable: dovetail the enumerator for $W_w$ until two distinct words appear, then accept. The diagonal halting set $K$ is many-one complete for computably enumerable sets, so $\mathbf{Two}\leq_mK$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{K\equiv_m\mathbf{Two}.}
$$
