<h1 id="12f/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $C,D\subseteq\mathbb B$, one writes $C\leq_mD$ if there is a total computable [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) $f:\mathbb B\to\mathbb B$ such that

$$
x\in C\iff f(x)\in D
$$

for every $x$. This is a computable [many-one reduction](../../../../../../../many-one-reduction.md).
