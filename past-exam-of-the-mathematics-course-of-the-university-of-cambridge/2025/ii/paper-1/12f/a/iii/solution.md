<h1 id="12f/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Fix an effective coding of machines and write $W_w$ for the computably enumerable language recognized by code $w$. A set $I$ of codes is an [index set](../../../../../../../index-set.md) when membership depends only on the recognized language:

$$
W_u=W_v\implies(u\in I\iff v\in I).
$$

It is nontrivial when it is neither empty nor all of $\mathbb B$.
