<h1 id="12f/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The set of codes of even length is not an [index set](../../../../../../index-set.md) because code length is syntactic rather than a property of $W_w$. By the [padding lemma](../../../../../../padding-lemma.md), every computably enumerable language has equivalent machine descriptions of arbitrarily many lengths, in particular descriptions of both parities. Hence equal recognized languages need not give equal membership.
