<h1 id="16h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The ordinal $\beta$ is the ordered concatenation of its initial segment $\alpha$ and its tail $\beta\setminus\alpha$. If the tail has order type $\gamma$, this concatenation has order type $\alpha+\gamma$. Hence $\alpha+\gamma=\beta$.
