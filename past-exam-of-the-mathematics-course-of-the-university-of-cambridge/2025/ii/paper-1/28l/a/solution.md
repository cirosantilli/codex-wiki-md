<h1 id="28l/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $p_n(t)=\mathbb P(X_t=n)$. The infinitesimal definition gives the forward equations

$$
p_0'=-\lambda p_0,\qquad p_n'=\lambda p_{n-1}-\lambda p_n\quad(n\geq1),
$$

with $p_0(0)=1$. Solving recursively gives

$$
\mathbb P(X_t=n)=e^{-\lambda t}\frac{(\lambda t)^n}{n!},
$$

so $X_t\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda t)$.
