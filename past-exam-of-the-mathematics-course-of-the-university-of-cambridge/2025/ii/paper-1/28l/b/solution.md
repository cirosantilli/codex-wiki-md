<h1 id="28l/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Conditionally on $X_t=n$, the $n$ arrival times are the order statistics of independent $\operatorname{Unif}[0,t]$ variables. For $n\geq1$, the last jump occurs before $3t/4$ exactly when every point lies before that time, so the probability is

$$
\left(\frac34\right)^n.
$$

The number falling in $(t/4,3t/4)$ is therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Binomial}\left(n,\frac12\right).}
$$
