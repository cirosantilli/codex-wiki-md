<h1 id="32a/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set

$$
Q(x,y)=12x^2+6y^2.
$$

Direct substitution of the system gives the exact factorization

$$
\dot Q=-2Q+24(x+y)(2x^2+y^2)
=2Q(2x+2y-1).
$$

Cauchy--Schwarz in the ellipsoidal norm gives

$$
x+y\leq\sqrt{(12x^2+6y^2)\left(\frac1{12}+\frac16\right)}
=\frac{\sqrt Q}{2}.
$$

**Thus if $0<Q<1$, then $x+y<1/2$ and $\dot Q<0$. Every trajectory beginning in $Q<1$ remains in a compact smaller sublevel set and tends to the only point where $Q=0$, namely the origin. Hence the basin contains the stated region.**
