<h1 id="10c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Immediately before the final Hadamard, the state is

$$
\frac1{\sqrt2}\left(|0\rangle|\psi\rangle-i|1\rangle U|\psi\rangle\right).
$$

The outcome-one component after that Hadamard is $(|\psi\rangle+iU|\psi\rangle)/2$. Thus, with $z=\langle\psi|U|\psi\rangle$,

$$
\boxed{p(1)=\frac14\lVert|\psi\rangle+iU|\psi\rangle\rVert^2
=\frac12(1-\operatorname{Im}z).}
$$
