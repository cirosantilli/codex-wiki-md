<h1 id="10c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every product [vector](../../../../../../vector.md),

$$
 (A\otimes B)(|x\rangle\otimes|y\rangle)=A|x\rangle\otimes B|y\rangle.
$$

Product [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) span the two-register state space, so the single $A\otimes B$ gate equals parallel application of $A$ to the first register and $B$ to the second. Unitarity guarantees that both descriptions are valid quantum circuits.
