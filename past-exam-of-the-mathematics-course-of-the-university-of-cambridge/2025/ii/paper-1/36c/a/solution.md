<h1 id="36c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For discrete states $s$ of energy $E_s$, the [canonical partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is

$$
Z(\beta)=\sum_s e^{-\beta E_s},\qquad \beta=(k_BT)^{-1}.
$$

For a classical continuous system the sum is replaced by the appropriately normalized phase-space [integral](../../../../../../integral.md), including the Gibbs factor $1/N!$ for identical particles.

It packages the equilibrium probabilities $P_s=e^{-\beta E_s}/Z$ and hence all canonical [thermodynamics](../../../../../../thermodynamics-split.md). In particular,

$$
F=-k_BT\log Z,\qquad
\langle E\rangle=-\frac{\partial\log Z}{\partial\beta},\qquad
(\Delta E)^2=\frac{\partial^2\log Z}{\partial\beta^2},
$$

and $S=k_B(\log Z+\beta\langle E\rangle)$.
