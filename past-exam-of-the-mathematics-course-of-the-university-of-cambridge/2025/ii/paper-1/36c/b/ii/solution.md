<h1 id="36c/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The normalized one-particle height density is exponential:

$$
f_Z(z)=\beta mg e^{-\beta mgz},\qquad z>0.
$$

Consequently

$$
\langle z\rangle=\frac{1}{\beta mg}=\frac{k_BT}{mg}.
$$

The number density and local ideal-gas [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure.md) are

$$
n(z)=\frac{N}{A\langle z\rangle}e^{-z/\langle z\rangle},
\qquad
p(z)=n(z)k_BT
=\frac{Nmg}{A}e^{-z/\langle z\rangle}.
$$

This also obeys hydrostatic balance $p'(z)=-mgn(z)$.
