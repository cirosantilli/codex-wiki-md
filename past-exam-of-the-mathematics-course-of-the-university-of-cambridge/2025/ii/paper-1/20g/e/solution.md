<h1 id="20g/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $\beta=a+b\alpha\in\mathcal O_L$. The nontrivial $K$-automorphism sends $\alpha$ to $-\alpha$, so

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(\beta)=2a\in\mathcal O_K.
$$

Also $\alpha$ is [integral](../../../../../../integral.md), hence $\alpha\beta$ is [integral](../../../../../../integral.md), and

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(\alpha\beta)=2b\alpha^2
=2b(4+\sqrt{17})\in\mathcal O_K.
$$

But $4+\sqrt{17}$ has norm $-1$, so it is a unit of $\mathcal O_K$. Multiplying by its inverse proves $2b\in\mathcal O_K$.
