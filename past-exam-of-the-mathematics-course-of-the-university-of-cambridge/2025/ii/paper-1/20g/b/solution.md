<h1 id="20g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $17$ is square-free and $17\equiv1\pmod4$, the standard quadratic-field integral-basis theorem gives

$$
\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z\left[\frac{1+\sqrt{17}}2\right].
$$

Indeed, the displayed generator has monic [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $x^2-x-4$, and the discriminant of this [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) is $17$, the [field discriminant](../../../../../../field-discriminant.md); no larger order is possible.
