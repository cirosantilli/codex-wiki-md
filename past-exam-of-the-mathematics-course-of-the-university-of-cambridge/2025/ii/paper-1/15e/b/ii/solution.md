<h1 id="15e/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The expanding branch obeys

$$
\dot a=\sqrt{\gamma a+\beta}.
$$

Integrating from $a=0$ at $t=0$ gives

$$
\frac2\gamma\left(\sqrt{\gamma a+\beta}-\sqrt\beta\right)=t.
$$

Solving for $a$,

$$
a(t)=\sqrt\beta\,t+\frac\gamma4t^2
=t\left(\sqrt\beta+\frac{\gamma t}{4}\right).
$$
