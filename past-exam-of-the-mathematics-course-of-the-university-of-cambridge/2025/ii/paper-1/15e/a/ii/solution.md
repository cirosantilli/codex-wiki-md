<h1 id="15e/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Define [conformal time](../../../../../../../conformal-time.md) by $d\tau=dt/a(t)$. The radial coordinate and the angular-radius [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
k&\chi(r)&f(\chi)\\ \hline
0&r&\chi\\
k>0&k^{-1/2}\sin^{-1}(\sqrt k\,r)&k^{-1/2}\sin(\sqrt k\,\chi)\\
k<0&(-k)^{-1/2}\sinh^{-1}(\sqrt{-k}\,r)&(-k)^{-1/2}\sinh(\sqrt{-k}\,\chi).
\end{array}
$$

Substitution yields the stated conformal metric. A radial null ray satisfies

$$
-c^2d\tau^2+d\chi^2=0,
$$

so $d\chi/d\tau=\pm c$ and $\chi=\chi_0\pm c(\tau-\tau_0)$.
