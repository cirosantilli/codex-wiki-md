<h1 id="10f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) satisfy $a,b,c,d\ge0$ and $a+b+c+d=1$. Since the variables are indicators,

$$
\mathbb EX=c+d,\quad\mathbb EY=b+d,\quad\mathbb E[XY]=d.
$$

Their [covariance](../../../../../../covariance.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=d-(c+d)(b+d)=ad-bc.
$$

Thus the necessary and sufficient condition for [uncorrelated random variables](../../../../../../uncorrelated-random-variables.md) is

$$
\boxed{ad=bc.}
$$

This uses zero [covariance](../../../../../../covariance.md) as the definition. If one variable is constant, a normalized correlation coefficient is undefined, but zero [covariance](../../../../../../covariance.md) and this criterion remain meaningful.
