<h1 id="1d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Separate variables and integrate on $|y|<1$:

$$
\int\frac{dy}{1-y^2}=x+C,\qquad
\operatorname{artanh}y=\frac12\log\frac{1+y}{1-y}=x+C.
$$

Hence the [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) in this region is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\tanh(x+C),\qquad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Every such solution increases from values near $-1$ towards $1$, remains strictly between the two [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), and is defined for all real $x$.
