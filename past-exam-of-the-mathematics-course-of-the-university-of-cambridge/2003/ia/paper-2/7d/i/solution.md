<h1 id="7d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Try a [particular integral](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) along each forcing [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md). Since $A\mathbf a_j=\lambda_j\mathbf a_j$,

$$
(\partial_t-A)\left(\frac{\mathbf a_j e^{i\omega_jt}}{i\omega_j-\lambda_j}\right)=\mathbf a_j e^{i\omega_jt}.
$$

With both denominators nonzero, add the two responses:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x_p=\frac{\mathbf a_1e^{i\omega_1t}}{i\omega_1-\lambda_1}
+\frac{\mathbf a_2e^{i\omega_2t}}{i\omega_2-\lambda_2}.}
$$

Only the forcing component's own [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) enters its denominator; a frequency matching a different eigenmode does not create resonance in this already diagonal forcing component.
