<h1 id="5b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A positive, counterclockwise [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) takes the standard [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) vectors $(1,0)^T$ and $(0,1)^T$ to $(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)^T$ and $(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta)^T$. These images are the columns of its [rotation matrix](../../../../../../rotation-matrix.md), so

$$
\boxed{R(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

It fixes the origin, preserves the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) and has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) one.
