<h1 id="8d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the stated angle, $q=\cos(2\pi/3)=-1/2$, so $\|u+v\|=\sqrt{2+2q}=1$. The minimizing [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) is therefore $w=-(u+v)$, and

$$
\boxed{\lambda=-1,\qquad S_{\min}=-\frac12-1=-\frac32.}
$$

Indeed all three pairwise [dot products](../../../../../../dot-product.md) equal $-1/2$, so the vectors lie in one plane, separated by $120$ degrees. This is also the global [minimum pairwise dot-product sum of three unit vectors](../../../../../../minimum-pairwise-dot-product-sum-of-three-unit-vectors.md): for any three unit vectors,

$$
0\le\|u+v+w\|^2=3+2S,
$$

so $S\ge-3/2$, with equality exactly when $u+v+w=0$.
