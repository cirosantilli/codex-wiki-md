<h1 id="8d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) for the two [unit vectors](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) gives $0<\|u+v\|\le2$. Taking reciprocals and using the negative sign in $\lambda=-1/\|u+v\|$ yields

$$
\boxed{\lambda\le-\frac12.}
$$

In fact equality would require $u=v$, by the equality condition in the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Since the two vectors here are [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md), the inequality is strict for every admissible pair.
