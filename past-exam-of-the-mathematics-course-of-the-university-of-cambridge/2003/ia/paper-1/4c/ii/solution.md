<h1 id="4c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The coefficient ratio is

$$
\frac{n+1+2^{-(n+1)}}{n+2^{-n}}\longrightarrow1.
$$

Thus the [ratio test](../../../../../../ratio-test.md) again gives [radius of convergence](../../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md) $\boxed{R=1}$. On $|z|=1$, however, the terms have absolute value $n+2^{-n}$, which does not tend to zero. The necessary term condition for a [convergent series](../../../../../../convergent-series.md) fails at every boundary point. Therefore this [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) **diverges everywhere on $|z|=1$**.
