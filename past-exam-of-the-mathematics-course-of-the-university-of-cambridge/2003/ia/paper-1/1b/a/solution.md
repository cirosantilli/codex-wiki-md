<h1 id="1b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Compose [permutations](../../../../../../permutation.md) from right to left. Tracking the four symbols through the two [permutation cycles](../../../../../../permutation-cycle.md) gives $1\mapsto2\mapsto1$ and $3\mapsto4\mapsto3$. Thus the [cycle decomposition of a permutation](../../../../../../cycle-decomposition-of-a-permutation.md) is

$$
\boxed{(123)(234)=(12)(34).}
$$

The disjoint transpositions commute, and squaring their product gives the [identity element](../../../../../../identity-element.md), while the product itself is nonidentity. Its [order of a group element](../../../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) is therefore **two**. Each transposition has negative [sign of a permutation](../../../../../../sign-of-a-permutation.md), so the product has sign $(-1)^2=\boxed{+1}$: it is an [even permutation](../../../../../../even-permutation.md).
