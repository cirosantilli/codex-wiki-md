**This is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md).** The difference $x-x=0$ is an even [integer](../../../../../../integer.md), proving [reflexivity](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md). If $x-y=2k$ then $y-x=-2k$, proving that the relation is [symmetric](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md). If $x-y=2k$ and $y-z=2\ell$ then $x-z=2(k+\ell)$, proving [transitivity](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md). Its [equivalence classes](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) are the translates $x+2\mathbb Z$; two [real numbers](../../../../../../real-number.md) need not be [integers](../../../../../../integer.md) themselves to belong to the same class.
