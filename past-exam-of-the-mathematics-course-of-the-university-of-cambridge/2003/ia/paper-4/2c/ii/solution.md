<h1 id="2c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For nonzero [complex numbers](../../../../../../complex-number.md), $x\overline y=(x/y)|y|^2$. Since $|y|^2$ is positive real, the condition is equivalent to $x/y\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$. **This is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md).** The ratio $x/x=1$ gives [reflexivity](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md); the reciprocal of a nonzero real ratio shows that the relation is [symmetric](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md); and $(x/y)(y/z)=x/z$ gives [transitivity](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md). An [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) is a real line through zero with zero removed, or equivalently all nonzero [complex numbers](../../../../../../complex-number.md) with the same [argument](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) modulo $\pi$.
