An [equivalence relation](../../../../../equivalence-relation.md) on a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $X$ is a [binary relation](../../../../../binary-relation.md) that is [reflexive](../../../../../reflexive-relation.md), [symmetric](../../../../../symmetric-relation.md) and [transitive](../../../../../transitive-relation.md): every $x\in X$ satisfies $x\sim x$; $x\sim y$ implies $y\sim x$; and $x\sim y$, $y\sim z$ imply $x\sim z$. Each of these quantifiers ranges over the entire specified [set](../../../../../set-split.md). The separate entries below test these three properties.
