<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [homotopy lifting theorem for a covering map](../../../../../../homotopy-lifting-theorem-for-a-covering-map.md): a [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) of [paths](../../../../../../continuous-path.md) has a unique lift once its initial [path](../../../../../../continuous-path.md) is lifted. Also use [uniqueness of lifts to a covering space](../../../../../../uniqueness-of-lifts-to-a-covering-space.md): a lift of a constant [path](../../../../../../continuous-path.md) with a specified starting point is constant.

Let $\widetilde\gamma$ be a loop based at $\widetilde x_0$ whose image under $p_*$ is trivial. There is a [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) $F:I\times I\to X$ from $p\widetilde\gamma$ to the constant loop at $x_0$, fixing both endpoints. Lift this [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) with initial [path](../../../../../../continuous-path.md) $\widetilde\gamma$. Both vertical boundary [paths](../../../../../../continuous-path.md) are constant lifts at $\widetilde x_0$. Its final [path](../../../../../../continuous-path.md) is a lift of the constant loop and hence is also constant at $\widetilde x_0$. Thus the lifted [homotopy](../../../../../../homotopy.md) contracts $\widetilde\gamma$ relative to its endpoints. The kernel of the induced [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) is trivial, proving

$$
\boxed{p_*:\pi_1(\widetilde X,\widetilde x_0)\hookrightarrow\pi_1(X,x_0)}.
$$

No universal-cover assumption is needed for this injection; it holds for any actual [covering map](../../../../../../covering-space.md).
