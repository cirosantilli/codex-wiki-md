For a [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix.md) $\rho$ on a finite-dimensional [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), its [Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md) in bits is

$$
\boxed{S(\rho)=-\operatorname{tr}(\rho\log_2\rho)=-\sum_j\lambda_j\log_2\lambda_j,}
$$

where $\lambda_j$ are its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and $0\log_20=0$. It is the [Shannon entropy](../../../../../information-entropy.md) of the spectrum, is zero for a [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md), and is invariant under unitary conjugation.
