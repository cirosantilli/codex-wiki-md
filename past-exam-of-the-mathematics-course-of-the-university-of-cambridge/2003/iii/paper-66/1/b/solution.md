<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use a radial [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) of the [FRW metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) in the form $ds^2=-dt^2+a(t)^2d\chi^2$ along a fixed spatial direction, with $c=1$. For an emitter and observer at fixed [comoving coordinates](../../../../../../comoving-coordinate.md), the comoving separation is

$$
\chi_o-\chi_e=\int_{t_e}^{t_o}\frac{dt}{a(t)}.
$$

Apply this to two adjacent wave crests. Since their endpoint separation is the same, subtraction to first order gives $\delta t_o/a(t_o)=\delta t_e/a(t_e)$. These intervals are the local periods measured by the [comoving observers](../../../../../../comoving-observer.md), so the received wavelength is stretched by $a(t_o)/a(t_e)$. Consequently the [cosmological redshift](../../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{1+z=\frac{\lambda_o}{\lambda_e}=\frac{a(t_o)}{a(t_e)}=\frac1{a(t_e)}}
$$

when $a(t_o)=1$. This relation assumes comoving emitter and observer; additional [peculiar velocities](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) supply kinematic redshift factors.
