<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Represent the leading real pattern by $A(T)e^{ik_cx}+\overline A(T)e^{-ik_cx}$. Before forcing, continuous [translation symmetry](../../../../../translational-symmetry.md) acts as $A\mapsto e^{i\theta}A$, and spatial [reflection](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) acts as $A\mapsto\overline A$. Equivariance excludes quadratic terms and permits a linear term and $|A|^2A$ at cubic order. Symmetry under [reflection](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) makes their coefficients real. A [supercritical bifurcation](../../../../../supercritical-bifurcation.md) has a saturating cubic coefficient; rescaling amplitude and time makes it $-1$. The imperfection has a Fourier component at the same [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md), so its resonant projection contributes an additive real coefficient. On the spatially uniform [wave envelope](../../../../../envelope-waves.md) subspace the [amplitude equation](../../../../../amplitude-equation.md) is therefore $A_T=\mu A-|A|^2A+\alpha$. Allowing modulations would additionally produce the spatial terms of the [real Ginzburg–Landau equation](../../../../../real-ginzburg-landau-equation.md).

For a forcing pattern moving in the positive $x$ direction, its laboratory-frame coefficient is $\alpha e^{-i\Omega T}$, where $\Omega$ is proportional to $k_cc$ after converting to slow time. The comoving amplitude is $B=e^{i\Omega T}A$, since the fast carrier becomes $e^{ik_c(x-ct)}$. Differentiation gives

$$
\boxed{B_T=(\mu+i\Omega)B-|B|^2B+\alpha.}
$$

A constant phase choice makes $\alpha>0$, and [complex conjugation](../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) reverses the sign of $\Omega$, so take $\Lambda=\Omega>0$. Put $B=\alpha^{1/3}a$ and $\tau=\alpha^{2/3}T$. The rescaled linear coefficients are $\mu/\alpha^{2/3}$ and $\Lambda/\alpha^{2/3}$, and the forcing becomes one. Below the rescaled variables are renamed $A,T,\mu,\Lambda$, giving the [directly forced cubic amplitude equation](../../../../../directly-forced-cubic-amplitude-equation.md).
