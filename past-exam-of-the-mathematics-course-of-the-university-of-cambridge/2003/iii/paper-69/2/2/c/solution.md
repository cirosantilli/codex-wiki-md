<h1 id="2/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For any [trigonometric polynomial](../../../../../../../trigonometric-polynomial.md) $t_n$ of degree at most $n$, reproduction from (a) and the [operator norm](../../../../../../../operator-norm.md) bound from (b) imply

$$
f-v_nf=(f-t_n)-v_n(f-t_n),\qquad \|f-v_nf\|_\infty\le4\|f-t_n\|_\infty.
$$

Taking the [infimum](../../../../../../../infimum.md) over $t_n$ gives

$$
\boxed{\|f-v_nf\|_\infty\le4E_n(f)}.
$$

This is the [polynomial reproduction error bound](../../../../../../../polynomial-reproduction-error-bound.md) with $\|v_n\|\le3$; attaining the [best uniform approximation](../../../../../../../best-uniform-approximation.md) is not needed for this inequality.
