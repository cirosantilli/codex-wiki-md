<h1 id="2/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Subtracting the two initial averages of [Fourier partial sums](../../../../../../../fourier-partial-sum.md) gives

$$
\boxed{v_n=2\sigma_{2n-1}-\sigma_{n-1}},
$$

because $2n\sigma_{2n-1}=\sum_{j=0}^{2n-1}s_j$ and $n\sigma_{n-1}=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}s_j$. The positive normalized [Fejér kernel](../../../../../../../fejer-kernel.md) gives the [uniform-norm contraction of Fejér summation](../../../../../../../fejer-summation-is-a-uniform-norm-contraction.md), namely $\|\sigma_mf\|_\infty\le\|f\|_\infty$. The [triangle inequality](../../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\|v_nf\|_\infty\le2\|\sigma_{2n-1}f\|_\infty+\|\sigma_{n-1}f\|_\infty\le3\|f\|_\infty.}
$$
