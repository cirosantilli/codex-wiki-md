↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [trigonometric polynomial](../../../../../../../trigonometric-polynomial.md) $t_n(x)=\sum_{|k|\le n}c_ke^{ikx}$, [orthogonality](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of the exponential [Fourier modes](../../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) gives $s_j(t_n)=t_n$ whenever $j\ge n$. Every term in the [de la Vallée Poussin sum](../../../../../../../de-la-vallee-poussin-sum.md) has such an index, so

$$
\boxed{v_n(t_n)=\frac1n\sum_{j=n}^{2n-1}t_n=t_n.}
$$

Here and below $n\ge1$.
