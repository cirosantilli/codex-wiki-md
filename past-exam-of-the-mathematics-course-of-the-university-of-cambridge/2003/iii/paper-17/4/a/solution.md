<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**False.** Give $E=TS^2\to S^2$ a fibrewise area form. This is a rank-two [symplectic vector bundle](../../../../../../symplectic-vector-bundle.md). A [Lagrangian subbundle](../../../../../../lagrangian-subbundle.md) would be a real line subbundle $L\subset TS^2$. Real [line bundles](../../../../../../line-bundle.md) are classified by their first [Stiefel–Whitney class](../../../../../../stiefel-whitney-class.md) in $H^1(B;\mathbb Z/2)$; since this group vanishes for $S^2$, $L$ would be trivial. A nonzero section of $L$ would therefore be a nowhere-vanishing tangent [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) on $S^2$. The [Hairy ball theorem](../../../../../../hairy-ball-theorem.md) rules this out: every continuous tangent [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) on an even-dimensional sphere has a zero. Hence $TS^2$ does not even have one [Lagrangian subbundle](../../../../../../lagrangian-subbundle.md), let alone a splitting into two. Fibrewise existence of [Lagrangian subspaces](../../../../../../lagrangian-subspace.md) does not guarantee a globally continuous choice.
