<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**True.** The [Künneth theorem](../../../../../../kunneth-theorem.md) gives the [Poincaré polynomial](../../../../../../poincare-polynomial.md)

$$
P_{S^4\times T^4}(t)=(1+t^4)(1+t)^4,
$$

so $b_2=6$ and $b_4=2$. The proposed equality also gives $b_0=b_8=1$. Even if [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) was not stated separately, these two numbers force the putative eight-manifold to be connected and compact: the top ordinary real [cohomology](../../../../../../cohomology-split.md) of a connected noncompact manifold is zero.

The auxiliary [Hard Lefschetz theorem on de Rham cohomology](../../../../../../hard-lefschetz-theorem-on-de-rham-cohomology.md) says that, on a compact [Kähler manifold](../../../../../../kahler-manifold.md) of complex dimension $n$, $L^{n-k}:H^k\to H^{2n-k}$ is an [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md), where $L\alpha=[\omega]\smile\alpha$. Here $n=4$, so $L^2:H^2\to H^6$ is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md). If $L\alpha=0$, then $L^2\alpha=0$ and therefore $\alpha=0$. Thus $L:H^2\to H^4$ is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md), forcing $b_2\leq b_4$. The inequality $6\leq2$ is impossible. Hence **these [Betti numbers](../../../../../../betti-number.md) cannot occur for a Kähler eight-manifold**.
