<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $E=e^{-ikx+w(k)t}$. Expanding the proposed [local relation](../../../../../../local-relation.md) gives $E[q_t+wq+X_x-ikX]$. Choose the derivative terms in $X$ to cancel the mixed spectral terms:

$$
\boxed{w(k)=i(k+k^3),\qquad X=(1+k^2)q-ikq_x-q_{xx}.}
$$

Indeed,

$$
X_x-ikX=q_x-q_{xxx}-i(k+k^3)q,
$$

so the resulting divergence is exactly $E(q_t+q_x-q_{xxx})$. This establishes the identity for every complex [spectral parameter](../../../../../../spectral-parameter.md) $k$, with the given differentiability. The cubic [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation.md) has the reverse sign from the usual $q_t+q_{xxx}=0$ convention; that sign affects both the spectral sectors and the number of required [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md).
