<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $A=\mathcal O(X)$ and $\neg a=a\to0$. The closure of the open $a$ is the [closed sublocale](../../../../../../closed-sublocale.md) with [closed nucleus](../../../../../../closed-nucleus.md) $c(\neg a)$; its interior is the open $\neg\neg a$. The closed [sublocale](../../../../../../sublocale.md) complementary to $\neg a$ is also open precisely when $\neg a$ is complemented, in which case its open representative is $\neg\neg a$. Thus the closure is clopen precisely when the [regular open element](../../../../../../regular-element-of-a-heyting-algebra.md) $\neg\neg a$ is complemented. Every [regular open element](../../../../../../regular-element-of-a-heyting-algebra.md) has this form, so **(i) and (ii) are equivalent**.
