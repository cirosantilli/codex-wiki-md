<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Conversely assume binary-join preservation by double negation. Since $\neg(a\vee\neg a)=0$, applying the assumption gives

$$
1=\neg\neg(a\vee\neg a)=\neg\neg a\vee\neg a.
$$

Also $\neg\neg a\wedge\neg a=0$. Thus $\neg\neg a$ is complemented, and every [regular open element](../../../../../../regular-element-of-a-heyting-algebra.md) is clopen. This proves **(iii) implies (ii)**. Double negation already preserves $0$ and all finite meets, so binary-join preservation here is equivalent to preservation of all finite joins.
