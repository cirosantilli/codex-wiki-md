↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [Booleanization of a locale](../../../../../../booleanization-of-a-locale.md) is the [sublocale](../../../../../../sublocale.md) with double-negation nucleus. A [flat sublocale](../../../../../../flat-sublocale.md) has a nucleus preserving finite joins, equivalently its fixed-element set is closed under ambient finite joins. For completeness, if a nucleus preserves finite joins then its fixed points are closed under them. Conversely, if fixed points are closed under joins, $j(a)\vee j(b)$ is fixed and bounds $a\vee b$, giving $j(a\vee b)\leq j(a)\vee j(b)$; monotonicity gives equality, and preservation of $0$ is precisely $j(0)=0$. Since $\neg\neg0=0$, condition (iv) is exactly (iii). Together the preceding implications prove **all four characterizations of an [extremally disconnected locale](../../../../../../extremally-disconnected-locale.md)**.
