<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose every [regular open element](../../../../../../regular-element-of-a-heyting-algebra.md) is complemented. In a [Heyting algebra](../../../../../../heyting-algebra.md), a complemented element is regular: if $a\vee b=1$ and $a\wedge b=0$, then $b=\neg a$ and $a=\neg b$. Finite joins of complemented elements are complemented. Hence $\neg\neg a\vee\neg\neg b$ is regular, and it is the least regular upper bound of $a\vee b$. Therefore

$$
\neg\neg(a\vee b)=\neg\neg a\vee\neg\neg b.
$$

This proves **(ii) implies (iii)**. The identification of the [regular open elements](../../../../../../regular-element-of-a-heyting-algebra.md) with interiors of closures was established in the preceding part.
