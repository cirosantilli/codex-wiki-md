<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The acceptance [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) is $1/C$, so

$$
\boxed{P(\text{reject})=1-\frac1C,\qquad E[\text{attempts per success}]=C.}
$$

For the given proposal [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $g$, every valid envelope must have $C\ge\sup f/g=M$. Rejection increases with $C$, so **the optimal envelope is $C=M$**, giving rejection [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) $1-1/M$. This optimizes the constant for the fixed proposal; it does not assert that $g$ is the best possible proposal. For densities specified only almost everywhere, an essential supremum is sufficient.
