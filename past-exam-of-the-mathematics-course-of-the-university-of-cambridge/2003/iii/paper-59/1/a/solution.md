<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At both points, $\cos\theta=0$ and $\sin2\phi=0$, so both components of the [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) vanish. Thus **both points are [equilibrium points](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) for every parameter value**. These are regular points of the [spherical coordinates](../../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) chart: the coordinate singularity at the pole plays no role in their [linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md).
