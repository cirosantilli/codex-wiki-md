<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the supplied [normal form](../../../../../../normal-form-dynamical-systems.md), the four local topological [phase portraits](../../../../../../phase-portrait.md) are as follows. When $\mu_2<0$, only the origin is present and it is a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md). When $\mu_2>0,\mu_1<0$, the origin is attracting, between the two outer [saddle equilibria](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md); inward-facing [separatrices](../../../../../../separatrix.md) enter its basin. When $\mu_2>0$ and $0<\mu_1<h(\mu_2)$, the origin is repelling and a stable [limit cycle](../../../../../../limit-cycle.md) surrounds it, inside the separatrix boundary formed by the outer [saddle equilibria](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md). Finally, when $\mu_2>0,\mu_1>h(\mu_2)$, the origin remains repelling but the local [limit cycle](../../../../../../limit-cycle.md) has disappeared, and trajectories leave the central region through the opened separatrix channels. Whether the origin is a [node](../../../../../../node-dynamical-systems.md) or a [focus](../../../../../../focus-dynamical-systems.md) within one of these regions does not change this orbit topology.

On the connection curve, the two outer [saddle equilibria](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) are joined in both directions; the period of the disappearing [limit cycle](../../../../../../limit-cycle.md) diverges. This is a [heteroclinic cycle](../../../../../../heteroclinic-cycle.md), not a pair of saddle [homoclinic orbits](../../../../../../homoclinic-orbit.md). The sketches below show the four open regions of the supplied system; they are not presented as phase portraits of the inequivalent printed spherical equations.
