<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) of $d_Y$ along $(f,g)$, with maps $e:E\to X$ and $k:E\to Y$ satisfying $(f,g)e=d_Yk$. Applying the two product projections gives $fe=k=ge$, so $e$ equalizes $f,g$.

Given $h:Z\to X$ with $fh=gh$, put $v=fh$. Then $(f,g)h=(v,v)=d_Yv$. The pullback property gives a unique $u:Z\to E$ with $eu=h$ and $ku=v$. Any map satisfying $eu=h$ automatically has $ku=feu=fh=v$, so uniqueness is exactly the [equalizer](../../../../../../equaliser.md) uniqueness. Hence **pulling back the diagonal constructs the [equalizer](../../../../../../equaliser.md) of $f,g$**.
