↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Genetic linkage](../../../../../../genetic-linkage.md) analysis asks whether a [genetic marker](../../../../../../genetic-marker.md) and a disease [genetic locus](../../../../../../genetic-locus.md) cosegregate within families more often than would be expected under [independent assortment](../../../../../../independent-assortment.md). It uses [pedigrees](../../../../../../pedigree.md) and [Mendelian segregation](../../../../../../mendelian-segregation.md) to estimate the [recombination fraction](../../../../../../recombination-fraction.md) $\theta$, with evidence for linkage arising when the data favor $\theta<1/2$. Even when founders are in linkage equilibrium, linked loci can show familial cosegregation.

[Genetic association](../../../../../../genetic-association.md) analysis instead asks whether observed [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) or [genotype](../../../../../../genotype.md) frequencies differ with disease status in a sampled population, often comparing cases with controls. It can detect a causal variant or a correlated [genetic marker](../../../../../../genetic-marker.md) through [linkage disequilibrium](../../../../../../linkage-disequilibrium.md); population structure and selection can also create association. **Linkage is about familial transmission; association is about population dependence.** Neither an association alone nor a low family recombination estimate establishes that the marker itself causes disease.
