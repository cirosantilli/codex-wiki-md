<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use individual [stratified randomization](../../../../../../stratified-randomization.md) within each prison, with concealed centrally generated [permuted-block randomization](../../../../../../permuted-block-randomization.md) and varying block sizes. Prison can affect both baseline risk and intervention delivery, so balancing assignments within prison protects against imbalance in that important [covariate](../../../../../../covariate.md). Varying block sizes and [allocation concealment](../../../../../../allocation-concealment.md) prevent staff from predicting the next allocation and selecting participants accordingly. Prespecify how incomplete final blocks are treated. To enforce the overall 4000:4000 allocation exactly, coordinate the stratum totals centrally, allocating odd final places through a concealed random rule; exact half allocation within every prison is impossible if some prison totals are odd.

**Individual randomization stratified by prison is preferable to randomizing only twenty whole prisons for this design.** A [cluster-randomized trial](../../../../../../cluster-randomized-trial.md) would have few independent units and could lose substantial precision through [intraclass correlation](../../../../../../intraclass-correlation-coefficient.md). Sharing or selling the intervention can cause [treatment contamination](../../../../../../treatment-contamination.md), so record it and retain an [intention-to-treat analysis](../../../../../../intention-to-treat-analysis.md); substantial spillover might justify a separately powered cluster design rather than being ignored.
