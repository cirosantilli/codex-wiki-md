<h1 id="6/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [full siblings](../../../../../../../full-sibling.md) $E,F$ have parents $G,D$. Since $G$ is noninbred, $\phi_{GG}=1/2$, whereas $\phi_{DD}=5/8$ and $\phi_{GD}=1/8$. Applying the parental [kinship coefficient](../../../../../../../kinship-coefficient.md) recursion to both siblings gives

$$
\boxed{\phi_{EF}=\frac{\phi_{GG}+2\phi_{GD}+\phi_{DD}}4=\frac{1/2+2(1/8)+5/8}{4}=\frac{11}{32}.}
$$

The familiar outbred-sibling value $1/4$ does not apply to this inbred [pedigree](../../../../../../../pedigree.md).
