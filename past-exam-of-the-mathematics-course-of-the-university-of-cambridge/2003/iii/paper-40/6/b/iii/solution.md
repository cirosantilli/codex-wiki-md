<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The self-[kinship coefficient](../../../../../../../kinship-coefficient.md) of the inbred individual $D$ is $\phi_{DD}=(1+F_D)/2=5/8$. Since $E$ receives a uniformly chosen parental [allele](../../../../../../../allele.md) from each of $D,G$,

$$
\boxed{\phi_{DE}=\tfrac12(\phi_{DD}+\phi_{DG})=\tfrac12(\tfrac58+\tfrac18)=\frac38.}
$$

The $\phi_{DD}$ term is essential: self-[kinship coefficient](../../../../../../../kinship-coefficient.md) is not zero, even for a noninbred individual.
