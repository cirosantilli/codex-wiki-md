<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The founder $C$ is unrelated to the descendants of $A,B$, while the [kinship coefficient](../../../../../../../kinship-coefficient.md) between $D$ and its parents' sibling $U$ is

$$
\phi_{DU}=\tfrac12(\phi_{VU}+\phi_{WU})=\frac14.
$$

As $G$ has parents $C,U$, the [kinship coefficient](../../../../../../../kinship-coefficient.md) between $D,G$ is

$$
\phi_{DG}=\tfrac12(\phi_{DC}+\phi_{DU})=\tfrac12(0+\tfrac14)=\frac18.
$$

The parents of $E$ are $D,G$, so

$$
\boxed{F_E=\phi_{DG}=\frac18.}
$$

The same calculation gives $F_F=1/8$.
