↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

To identify the unlabelled individuals in the original [pedigree](../../../../../../../pedigree.md), call the three children of $A,B$ from left to right $U,V,W$. Then $G$ has parents $C,U$, $D$ has parents $V,W$, and $E,F$ have parents $G,D$. The three [full siblings](../../../../../../../full-sibling.md) $U,V,W$ are noninbred because $A,B$ are unrelated noninbred [founders in a pedigree](../../../../../../../founder-in-a-pedigree.md). Their pairwise [kinship coefficients](../../../../../../../kinship-coefficient.md) are

$$
\phi_{UV}=\phi_{UW}=\phi_{VW}=\frac{\phi_{AA}+\phi_{BB}}4=\frac14.
$$

Since $D$ is the child of the sibling pair $V,W$, its [inbreeding coefficient](../../../../../../../inbreeding-coefficient.md) is

$$
\boxed{F_D=\phi_{VW}=\frac14.}
$$
