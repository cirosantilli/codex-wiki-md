<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [kinship coefficient](../../../../../../kinship-coefficient.md) $\phi_{XY}$ is the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) that one [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) copy sampled uniformly from each of individuals $X,Y$, independently conditional on their copies, is [identical by descent](../../../../../../identity-by-descent.md). The [inbreeding coefficient](../../../../../../inbreeding-coefficient.md) $F_X$ is the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) that the two homologous [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) copies within $X$ are [identical by descent](../../../../../../identity-by-descent.md). For distinct offspring and older individual, [Mendelian segregation](../../../../../../mendelian-segregation.md) gives

$$
\phi_{XY}=\tfrac12(\phi_{P_XY}+\phi_{M_XY}),\qquad F_X=\phi_{P_XM_X},\qquad \phi_{XX}=\tfrac12(1+F_X).
$$

For the last identity, two independent selections choose the same copy with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$, and choose distinct copies with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$, in which case identity has [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $F_X$. **An unrelated, noninbred founder has $F=0$ and self-kinship $1/2$**, while distinct such founders have zero mutual [kinship coefficient](../../../../../../kinship-coefficient.md). The source's statement of zero founder kinship must be read as zero kinship between distinct founders; zero self-kinship would contradict the definition.
