<h1 id="1/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Differentiate the [log-likelihood](../../../../../../../log-likelihood.md) again. For $d>0$, the positive interior [maximum-likelihood estimate](../../../../../../../maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\ell''(\widehat\theta)=-\frac{d}{\widehat\theta^2}.}
$$

The [observed information](../../../../../../../observed-fisher-information.md) is $-\ell''(\widehat\theta)=d/\widehat\theta^2$, giving the usual local [standard error](../../../../../../../standard-error.md) approximation $\widehat\theta/\sqrt d$. For $d=0$ there is no positive interior maximum, so the displayed substitution and this [standard error](../../../../../../../standard-error.md) approximation do not apply; at every positive $\theta$ the second derivative is zero.
