<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Replace the expected total [person-time](../../../../../../../person-time.md) and failure count in the preceding identity by their observed values, $E=\sum_i x_i$ and $d$. The resulting estimating equation is $\theta E=d$, so

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\theta=\frac{d}{\sum_i x_i}.}
$$

This [events divided by exposure estimator](../../../../../../../events-divided-by-exposure-estimator.md) is failures per unit observed [person-time](../../../../../../../person-time.md). We assume positive total exposure; if $d=0$, it gives the boundary estimate zero.
