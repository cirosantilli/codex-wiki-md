<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [baryon asymmetry](../../../../../baryon-asymmetry.md) is an excess of net [baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md) over zero, conveniently measured by $\eta_B=(n_B-n_{\bar B})/n_\gamma$. Ordinary matter contains abundant [baryons](../../../../../baryon.md), whereas no comparable primordial [antibaryon](../../../../../antibaryon.md) population is observed. Large nearby domains of matter and [antimatter](../../../../../antimatter.md) would produce radiation through [particle-antiparticle annihilation](../../../../../annihilation.md) at their boundaries. Antiparticles produced in energetic collisions are not evidence for a cosmological antimatter domain. The small nonzero [baryon-to-photon ratio](../../../../../baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) inferred from [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](../../../../../big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) is independently tested by [baryon-density consistency between nucleosynthesis and the microwave background](../../../../../baryon-density-consistency-between-nucleosynthesis-and-the-microwave-background.md).

Starting with zero net [baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md), the [Sakharov conditions](../../../../../sakharov-conditions.md) require **baryon-number violation, C and CP violation, and departure from thermal equilibrium**. Conservation of [baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md) would keep the initial value zero. [C symmetry](../../../../../charge-conjugation-symmetry.md) or [CP symmetry](../../../../../cp-symmetry.md) would pair baryon production with equal antibaryon production; [CP rate cancellation in baryogenesis](../../../../../cp-rate-cancellation-in-baryogenesis.md) explains why [C violation](../../../../../violation-of-charge-conjugation.md) alone does not suffice. In [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md) with zero imposed charge [chemical potentials](../../../../../chemical-potential.md), [CPT symmetry](../../../../../cpt-symmetry.md) forbids the required net [baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md). An asymmetric rate in one reaction must not be mistaken for net production after all reverse reactions are included.

A simple example is [heavy-boson decay baryogenesis](../../../../../heavy-boson-decay-baryogenesis.md). Take equally many heavy $X$ [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) and their [antiparticles](../../../../../antiparticle.md), with decay channels

$$
X\longrightarrow uu\quad(r),\qquad X\longrightarrow\bar d e^+\quad(1-r),
\qquad
\bar X\longrightarrow\bar u\bar u\quad(\bar r),\qquad
\bar X\longrightarrow d e^-\quad(1-\bar r).
$$

The symbols in parentheses are [branching fractions](../../../../../branching-fraction.md). The two $X$ channels carry [baryon numbers](../../../../../baryon-number.md) $2/3$ and $-1/3$, so both cannot conserve one assigned [baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md) for $X$. Their mean [baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md) is $r-1/3$, while the mean for $\bar X$ is $-\bar r+1/3$. Consequently,

$$
\boxed{\Delta B_{X\bar X}=r-\bar r,\qquad\epsilon=\frac{r-\bar r}{2},}
$$

where $\epsilon$ is the asymmetry per decaying particle. [CP violation](../../../../../cp-violation.md) permits $r\ne\bar r$ without violating the equality of total [decay widths](../../../../../decay-width.md) required by [CPT symmetry](../../../../../cpt-symmetry.md). Microscopically a [direct CP asymmetry](../../../../../direct-cp-asymmetry.md) requires interference between [decay amplitudes](../../../../../decay-amplitude.md) with both relative [weak phases](../../../../../weak-phase-cp-violation.md) and [absorptive phases](../../../../../absorptive-phase-of-a-decay-amplitude.md); specifying complex couplings alone would not establish unequal partial [decay widths](../../../../../decay-width.md).

For [radiation domination](../../../../../radiation-domination.md), a sufficient decay condition is $\Gamma_X\lesssim H(T=M_X)=1.66\sqrt{g_*}M_X^2/m_P$. The heavy population can then survive as the equilibrium abundance drops, before decaying while inverse production is suppressed. Its actual abundance differs from its [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md) value. If subsequent [baryon-number washout](../../../../../baryon-number-washout.md) is negligible and [cosmological entropy conservation](../../../../../cosmological-entropy-conservation.md) holds, an initial total abundance $Y_X=(n_X+n_{\bar X})/s$ produces

$$
\boxed{Y_B\equiv\frac{n_B-n_{\bar B}}s=\epsilon Y_X.}
$$

Here $s$ is the [entropy density](../../../../../entropy-density.md). Residual [baryon-number washout](../../../../../baryon-number-washout.md) multiplies this expression by an efficiency factor below one. The example realizes all three [Sakharov conditions](../../../../../sakharov-conditions.md); it illustrates a mechanism, rather than identifying the actual cosmological source of the [baryon asymmetry](../../../../../baryon-asymmetry.md).
