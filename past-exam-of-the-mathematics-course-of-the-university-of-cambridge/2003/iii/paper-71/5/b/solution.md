<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a three-dimensional [worm-like chain](../../../../../../worm-like-chain.md), the tangent correlation length is $l_p=B/(k_BT)$, where $B$ is the [filament bending modulus](../../../../../../filament-bending-modulus.md). The measured [persistence length](../../../../../../persistence-length.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{B=k_BT l_p=(4.14\times10^{-21})(2.0\times10^{-8})=8.28\times10^{-29}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2}.}
$$

Represent the [actin](../../../../../../actin.md) filament as an isotropic solid circular cylinder with diameter $d=10^{-9}\,\mathrm m$. Its [second moment of area](../../../../../../second-moment-of-area.md) is $I=\pi d^4/64=4.909\times10^{-38}\,\mathrm{m^4}$. Since $B=EI$,

$$
\boxed{E=\frac{64k_BT l_p}{\pi d^4}=1.69\times10^9\,\mathrm{Pa}.}
$$

This is an effective [Young's modulus](../../../../../../young-s-modulus.md) within the given continuum cross-sectional model; the numerical diameter and persistence length must not be replaced by unrelated typical biological values.

The same measurement corrects the independent-link approximation: the [Kuhn length](../../../../../../kuhn-length.md) is $b_K=2l_p=40\,\mathrm{nm}$, with $N_K=L/b_K=2.5\times10^9$. Thus the long-chain entropic stiffness is $3k_BT/(2l_pL)=3.105\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$, and the root-mean-square end-to-end distance is $2\,\mathrm{mm}$. For comparison, the material axial stiffness is $EA/L=1.325\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$, using $A=\pi d^2/4$. The corrected coil response remains much softer than axial backbone stretching.
