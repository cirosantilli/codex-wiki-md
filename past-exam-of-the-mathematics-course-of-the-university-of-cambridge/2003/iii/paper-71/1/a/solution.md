<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $b\simeq2l_p$ be the [Kuhn length](../../../../../../kuhn-length.md), $N=L/b$ the number of statistical segments, and $v$ their effective [excluded volume](../../../../../../excluded-volume.md). We first assume a good-solvent, strongly confined [self-avoiding walk](../../../../../../self-avoiding-walk.md), rather than an [ideal chain](../../../../../../ideal-chain.md). A confinement blob contains $g$ links and has diameter $D\sim bg^{3/5}$ in the simple $v\sim b^3$ scaling model. Therefore $g\sim(D/b)^{5/3}$. Distant blobs cannot freely pass through one another in a narrow tube, so the $N/g$ blobs form an axial string. The [de Gennes confinement scaling](../../../../../../de-gennes-confinement-scaling.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{X\sim\frac NgD\sim L\left(\frac bD\right)^{2/3}\sim L\left(\frac{l_p}{D}\right)^{2/3}.}
$$

The [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is of order $k_BT$ per blob, not the [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) of stretching a straight elastic rod.

The role of solvent and chain thickness can be made explicit by a [Flory approximation](../../../../../../flory-approximation.md): an axial extension $X$ costs an entropic term $k_BTX^2/(Nb^2)$, while the confined number density gives an interaction term $k_BTvN^2/(XD^2)$. Minimizing their sum gives $X^3\sim vN^3b^2/D^2$, hence

$$
\boxed{X\sim L\left(\frac{v}{bD^2}\right)^{1/3}.}
$$

For a thin statistical segment of effective diameter $d$, $v\sim b^2d$ instead gives $X\sim L(bd/D^2)^{1/3}$. Thus the simpler answer implicitly fixes the [excluded volume](../../../../../../excluded-volume.md); the [DNA](../../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) thickness and ionic environment can matter quantitatively.

Both blob arguments require $b\ll D\ll R_{\rm free}$ and $N/g\gg1$, where $R_{\rm free}\sim bN^{3/5}$ in the simple good-solvent model. The printed comparison with $L$ alone does not ensure confinement relative to the unconfined coil. If $D\gg R_{\rm free}$, the tube barely perturbs the coil. An [ideal chain](../../../../../../ideal-chain.md) without [excluded volume](../../../../../../excluded-volume.md) has axial spread of order $\sqrt{Lb}$ and does not acquire the extensive extension of a nonoverlapping blob string. These are distinct physical regimes, not changes to a numerical prefactor.
