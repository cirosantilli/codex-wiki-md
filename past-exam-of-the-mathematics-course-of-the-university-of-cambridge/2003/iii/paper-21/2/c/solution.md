<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [rational point](../../../../../../rational-point.md) $P=(0,1)$ lies on $E$ and is neither the identity nor $(-1,0)$. By [solution](../b/solution.md) these are all the rational [torsion points of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). Hence **$P$ has infinite order and $E(\mathbb Q)$ is infinite**: its multiples $nP$ are distinct. No assertion about its being a generator or about the exact [rank of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../rank-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) is needed here.
