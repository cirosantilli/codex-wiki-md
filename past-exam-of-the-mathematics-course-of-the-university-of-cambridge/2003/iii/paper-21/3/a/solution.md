<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [elliptic curve](../../../../../../elliptic-curve.md) over $\mathbb Q$ has [good reduction](../../../../../../good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) at $p$ if it admits an integral [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) over $\mathbb Z_p$ whose reduced projective cubic is a [smooth algebraic curve](../../../../../../smooth-algebraic-curve.md) over $\mathbb F_p$. Equivalently, its [Minimal Weierstrass equation](../../../../../../minimal-weierstrass-equation.md) has a unit [elliptic-curve discriminant](../../../../../../elliptic-curve-discriminant.md), or its smooth proper model is an [elliptic curve](../../../../../../elliptic-curve.md) over the [valuation ring](../../../../../../valuation-ring.md). The existence of an appropriate integral model matters: a nonminimal equation can have discriminant divisible by $p$ even when the curve has [good reduction](../../../../../../good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{E\text{ has good reduction at }p\iff v_p(\Delta_{\min})=0.}
$$
