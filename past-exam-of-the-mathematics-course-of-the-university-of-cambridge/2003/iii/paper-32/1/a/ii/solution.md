↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The same pricing relation gives $x^\top A_0\geq0$ whenever the terminal payment is nonnegative. Therefore negative initial cost also contradicts a strictly positive [state-price density](../../../../../../../state-price-density.md), even if the terminal payment is identically zero.

This second form is needed for directions invisible in the terminal payments. Let $L$ be the [linear span](../../../../../../../linear-span.md) of the essential values of $A_1$, equivalently

$$
L=\{u\in\mathbb R^r:u^\top A_1=0\text{ almost surely}\}^{\perp}.
$$

If $A_0\notin L$, let $v$ be its nonzero [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) on $L^\perp$ and set $x=-v$. Then $x^\top A_1=0$ [almost surely](../../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md) while $x^\top A_0=-\|v\|^2<0$. Hence the second form exists in this case. Part (b) handles all remaining cases.
