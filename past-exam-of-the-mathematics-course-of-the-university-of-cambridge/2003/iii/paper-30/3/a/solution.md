<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Order configurations coordinatewise: $\omega\le\eta$ when $\omega(e)\le\eta(e)$ for every coordinate. An [increasing event](../../../../../../increasing-event.md) $A$ is an upward-closed set: $\omega\in A$ and $\omega\le\eta$ imply $\eta\in A$.

The finite-lattice [FKG inequality](../../../../../../fkg-inequality.md) states that a [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) mass function satisfying the [FKG lattice condition](../../../../../../fkg-lattice-condition.md)

$$
\mu(\omega\vee\eta)\mu(\omega\wedge\eta)\ge\mu(\omega)\mu(\eta)
\quad\text{for every }\omega,\eta
$$

is [positively associated](../../../../../../positive-association-of-random-variables.md). Here [meet in a lattice](../../../../../../meet-in-a-lattice.md) and [join in a lattice](../../../../../../join-in-a-lattice.md) are coordinatewise minimum and maximum. Thus for [increasing observables](../../../../../../increasing-function-on-a-partially-ordered-set.md) $f,g$,

$$
E_\mu[fg]\ge E_\mu[f]E_\mu[g].
$$

Taking [indicator functions](../../../../../../indicator-function.md) gives **$\mu(A\cap B)\ge\mu(A)\mu(B)$ for all [increasing events](../../../../../../increasing-event.md)**. The strictly positive version suffices for the Ising weights here; the theorem also holds for nonnegative log-supermodular weights of positive total mass.
