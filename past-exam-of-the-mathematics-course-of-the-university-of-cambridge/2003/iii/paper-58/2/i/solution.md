<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $\mathcal H=a'/a$, where primes denote [conformal time](../../../../../../conformal-time.md). In the passive convention for a [cosmological gauge transformation](../../../../../../gauge-transformation-in-cosmological-perturbation-theory.md), retain terms linear in $h$ or $\xi$ and discard their products. The [linear metric gauge-transformation law](../../../../../../linear-metric-gauge-transformation-law.md) then gives

$$
\Delta h_{\mu\nu}=-\eta_{\mu\alpha}\partial_\nu\xi^\alpha
-\eta_{\nu\alpha}\partial_\mu\xi^\alpha
-2\mathcal H\eta_{\mu\nu}\xi^0.
$$

For a single nonzero [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md), suppress the common factor $e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}$. Its derivatives are

$$
\partial_0\xi^0=T',\quad \partial_i\xi^0=ik\widehat k_iT,\quad
\partial_0\xi^i=i\widehat k_iL',\quad
\partial_j\xi^i=-k\widehat k_i\widehat k_jL.
$$

Using $\eta_{00}=-1$ and $\eta_{ij}=\delta_{ij}$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Delta h_{00}&=2(T'+\mathcal HT),\\
\Delta h_{0i}&=i\widehat k_i(kT-L'),\\
\Delta h_{ij}&=2kL\widehat k_i\widehat k_j-2\mathcal HT\delta_{ij}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The term proportional to $\mathcal HT$ comes from the time dependence of the background [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md); omitting it would transform only the flat reference metric.
