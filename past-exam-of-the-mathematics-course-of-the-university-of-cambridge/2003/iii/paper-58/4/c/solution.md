<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the growing [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) as $\delta_{m,\mathbf k}=A_{\mathbf k}\tau^2$, with $A_{\mathbf k}$ having the indicated $k^{n/2}$ spectral amplitude. In the dust-comoving [synchronous gauge](../../../../../../synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md), $h'=-2\delta_m'$, and the supplied $h_s'=h'$ therefore implies $h_s''=-4A_{\mathbf k}$. On the same comoving density slicing, the [cosmological Poisson equation](../../../../../../cosmological-poisson-equation.md) is

$$
-k^2\Phi_{\mathbf k}=4\pi Ga^2\rho_m\delta_{m,\mathbf k}
=\frac32\mathcal H^2\delta_{m,\mathbf k}=6A_{\mathbf k},
$$

because $\mathcal H=2/\tau$. This is the comoving-density form of the relativistic scalar constraint, so it can be used on these large scales; replacing its density by the Newtonian-gauge density would not be justified there. Combining the two results gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{h_s''}{2k^2}=-\frac{2A_{\mathbf k}}{k^2}=\frac13\Phi_{\mathbf k}.}
$$

Thus the large-angle [Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../../../sachs-wolfe-effect.md) has source $\Phi/3$. The potential is constant for this matter-era growing mode, and its [power spectrum](../../../../../../power-spectrum.md) obeys $P_\Phi(k)\propto k^{n-4}$.
