<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For an [Einstein static universe](../../../../../../einstein-static-universe.md), both $a_t$ and $a_{tt}$ vanish. The [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives

$$
0=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}(\rho_m+2\rho_r-2\rho_\Lambda)a_E,
$$

and $a_E>0$ therefore implies

$$
\boxed{\rho_m+2\rho_r=2\rho_\Lambda.}
$$

The [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) additionally fixes the spatial curvature:

$$
\frac{k}{a_E^2}=\frac{8\pi G}{3}(\rho_m+\rho_r+\rho_\Lambda)>0.
$$

Thus the acceleration balance is necessary but must be accompanied by the static energy constraint.
