<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

During [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md), the spatially flat background has $a\propto\tau^2$ and $\mathcal H=2/\tau$. Let $\theta_m=\nabla\cdot\mathbf v_m$ denote the scalar velocity divergence. Linear [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) in [synchronous gauge](../../../../../../synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md) gives

$$
\delta_m'=-\theta_m-\frac12h',\qquad \theta_m'+\mathcal H\theta_m=0.
$$

The first equation follows from the perturbed volume element $a^3(1+h/2)$; the second states that peculiar dust velocity redshifts as $a^{-1}$. Differentiating the first and substituting the trace of the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md), with $w_m=0$ and $\Omega_m=1$, yields

$$
\delta_m''+\mathcal H\delta_m'-\frac32\mathcal H^2\delta_m
=-(\theta_m'+\mathcal H\theta_m)=0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\delta_m''+\frac2\tau\delta_m'-\frac6{\tau^2}\delta_m=0.
$$

Trying $\delta_m\propto\tau^p$ gives $p(p-1)+2p-6=(p-2)(p+3)=0$. The complete solution of this density equation is

$$
\boxed{\delta_m(\mathbf x,\tau)=A(\mathbf x)\tau^2+B(\mathbf x)\tau^{-3}.}
$$

These are the [matter-era growing and decaying density modes](../../../../../../matter-era-growing-and-decaying-density-modes.md), proportional to $a$ and $a^{-3/2}$. In a dust-comoving [synchronous gauge](../../../../../../synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md), $\theta_m=0$ and $h'=-2\delta_m'$. That rest-frame choice distinguishes physical mode coefficients from changes of density induced by [residual synchronous-gauge freedom](../../../../../../residual-synchronous-gauge-freedom.md).
