<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $A(M)=V(M)+hM$ be the zero-field [constrained order-parameter free energy](../../../../../../constrained-order-parameter-free-energy.md). At coexistence in a [conjugate field](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md) $h_c$, two global minima $M_1<M_2$ obey

$$
A'(M_1)=A'(M_2)=h_c,\qquad
A(M_2)-A(M_1)=h_c(M_2-M_1).
$$

Thus the same line of slope $h_c$ is tangent to $A$ at both endpoints: the [common-tangent construction for phase coexistence](../../../../../../common-tangent-construction-for-phase-coexistence.md). Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\int_{M_1}^{M_2}[A'(M)-h_c]\,dM=0.}
$$

This is the [Maxwell equal-area construction](../../../../../../maxwell-construction.md) on the equation-of-state curve $h=A'(M)$: the signed areas on either side of the horizontal coexistence line cancel. Equal slope alone finds stationary phases; the integral additionally enforces equal [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md).

If the mean [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) is constrained between the endpoints, the system can separate into two macroscopic phases. With $\lambda=(M-M_1)/(M_2-M_1)$, their bulk [free-energy density](../../../../../../free-energy-density.md) is $(1-\lambda)A(M_1)+\lambda A(M_2)$. The interface contributes subextensively in the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md). The resulting [constrained order-parameter free energy](../../../../../../constrained-order-parameter-free-energy.md) is the convex envelope of $A$, with a straight coexistence segment. This explains why a nonconvex uniform [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) must be convexified when describing stable constrained equilibrium, and why a [spinodal point](../../../../../../spinodal-point.md) is not the [Maxwell construction](../../../../../../maxwell-construction.md) boundary.
