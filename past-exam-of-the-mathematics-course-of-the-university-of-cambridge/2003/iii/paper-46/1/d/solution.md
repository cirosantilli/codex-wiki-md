<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At the [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md), both the quadratic and quartic even coefficients vanish, while the sextic coefficient remains positive. Two even control parameters must therefore be tuned, in addition to setting the symmetry-breaking [conjugate field](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md) to zero. The [continuous phase transition](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md) line and [first-order phase transition](../../../../../../first-order-phase-transition.md) line meet, the coexistence [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) jump shrinks to zero, and the [tricritical wings](../../../../../../tricritical-wing.md) end there. The balance governing the equation of state changes from quartic to sextic stabilization.

Along the tricritical thermal path $u=0$, $r=a t$, $h=0$, minimizing $V$ gives $M^4=-r/v$ on the ordered side. At $r=u=0$, the equation of state is $h=vM^5$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\beta_{\mathrm{tri}}=\frac14,\qquad \delta_{\mathrm{tri}}=5.}
$$

These [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md) require the tuned tricritical path; a path with fixed $u>0$ instead has the ordinary exponents derived above. The corresponding equilibrium [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) is $f_{\min}=-(-r)^{3/2}/(3\sqrt v)$ below the transition. Its first thermal derivative is continuous but its second derivative diverges, giving the tricritical [mean-field approximation](../../../../../../mean-field-approximation.md) a heat-capacity exponent $\alpha=1/2$. Fluctuations are negligible asymptotically only above the tricritical [upper critical dimension](../../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md) three; at three dimensions logarithmic corrections can accompany these powers.
