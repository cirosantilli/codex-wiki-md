↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) distinguishes equilibrium [thermodynamic phases](../../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md). For a scalar magnetic system, a slowly varying coarse-grained [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) $\phi(x)$ represents local [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md), and its uniform expectation $M=\langle\phi\rangle$ is the [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md). Under [spin inversion symmetry](../../../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md), $\phi\mapsto-\phi$ and $h\mapsto-h$. The disordered phase has $M=0$ at $h=0$, while a pure ordered phase has $M\ne0$. In a finite symmetric system the average still vanishes at $h=0$; [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) means taking the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md) before $h\to0^+$ or $h\to0^-$ to select a [pure thermodynamic phase](../../../../../../pure-thermodynamic-phase.md).

[Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md) assigns a local [free-energy functional](../../../../../../free-energy-functional.md)

$$
\mathcal F[\phi]=\int d^Dx\left[\frac\kappa2|\nabla\phi|^2+\frac r2\phi^2+\frac u4\phi^4+\frac v6\phi^6-h\phi\right],\qquad \kappa>0.
$$

[Spin inversion symmetry](../../../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md) forbids odd powers at zero [conjugate field](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md); locality and slow variation motivate the [gradient expansion](../../../../../../gradient-expansion.md). The [gradient energy](../../../../../../gradient-energy.md) penalizes interfaces and determines long-wavelength response. Integrating $e^{-\mathcal F/(k_BT)}$ over fields includes fluctuations, whereas the [Landau approximation](../../../../../../landau-approximation.md) replaces that integral by its stable stationary configuration. For a uniform phase this reduces to minimizing $V(M)$. If $f$ is the equilibrium [free-energy density](../../../../../../free-energy-density.md), then $M=-\partial f/\partial h$, so the [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) is also the thermodynamic response to its [conjugate field](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md).
