<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The unstressed shape has equal radii at $x=\pm L$, so its contributions cancel when subtracting the elastic end pressures. The [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) difference is therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta p=\alpha\frac{2Q}{U}\,[H(-\widetilde L)-H(\widetilde L)]}.
$$

The multiplier is a [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) scale: $\alpha$ has dimensions [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) per length and $2Q/U$ is a length.
