<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Retain fixed dimensionless structure constants and fixed $M$. Write the assumed [stellar homology](../../../../../../stellar-homology.md) relations as $P_c=C_PGM^2/R^4$ and $\rho_c=C_\rho M/R^3$. The [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) gives

$$
T_c=\frac{\mu_i}{\mathcal R}\left(\frac{P_c}{\rho_c}-K_{\rm nr}\rho_c^{2/3}\right)=\frac{\mu_i}{\mathcal R}\left(AG\frac{M}{R}-BK_{\rm nr}\frac{M^{2/3}}{R^2}\right),
$$

where $A=C_P/C_\rho$ and $B=C_\rho^{2/3}$. The first term is the temperature required for ordinary thermal [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) support; the second subtracts the support supplied by nonrelativistic [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md).

As a function of $u=1/R$, this is a concave [quadratic polynomial](../../../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md). Differentiation gives

$$
u_{\max}=\frac{AG}{2BK_{\rm nr}}M^{1/3},\qquad R_{\max}=\frac{2BK_{\rm nr}}{AG}M^{-1/3},
$$

and substitution proves

$$
\boxed{T_{\max}=\frac{A^2G^2}{4B\mathcal R K_{\rm nr}}\mu_iM^{4/3}\propto\mu_iM^{4/3}}.
$$

The scaling holds at fixed $K_{\rm nr}$ and homologous profile; changing the [mean molecular weight per electron](../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight-per-electron.md) also changes $K_{\rm nr}$. At the maximum the ion thermal [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) and [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) contribute equally. Subsequent contraction lets [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) carry more of the load without raising the temperature; eventually the assumed positive-temperature branch approaches a cold, degenerate configuration.

**A sufficiently low-mass object never reaches the temperature needed for sustained [hydrogen burning](../../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md), so it becomes a cooling [brown dwarf](../../../../../../brown-dwarf.md) rather than a hydrogen-burning [star](../../../../../../star.md) on the [main sequence](../../../../../../main-sequence.md).** The scaling explains a [hydrogen-burning minimum mass](../../../../../../hydrogen-burning-minimum-mass.md); its numerical value additionally requires the composition, detailed structure and nuclear ignition conditions, which are not specified here.
