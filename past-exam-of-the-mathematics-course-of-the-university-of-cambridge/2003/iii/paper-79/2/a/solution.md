<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Work in a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) $E$, for example a [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md). A [rate function](../../../../../../rate-function.md) is a [lower semicontinuous](../../../../../../lower-semicontinuity.md) map $I:E\to[0,\infty]$. A [good rate function](../../../../../../good-rate-function.md) additionally has [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) finite [sublevel sets](../../../../../../sublevel-set.md) $\{x:I(x)\leq r\}$.

A [large deviation principle](../../../../../../large-deviation-principle.md) at [large-deviation speed](../../../../../../large-deviation-speed.md) $L$ requires, for every [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $G$ and [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md) $F$,

$$
-\inf_{x\in G}I(x)\leq\liminf_{L\to\infty}\frac1L\log\mathbb P(X_L\in G),\qquad
\limsup_{L\to\infty}\frac1L\log\mathbb P(X_L\in F)\leq-\inf_{x\in F}I(x).
$$

The conventions are $\log0=-\infty$ and $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$. A [weak large deviation principle](../../../../../../weak-large-deviation-principle.md) imposes the same lower bound but only the upper bound for [compact sets](../../../../../../compact-space.md). [Exponential tightness](../../../../../../exponential-tightness.md) supplies [compact sets](../../../../../../compact-space.md) $K_M$ with $\limsup L^{-1}\log\mathbb P(X_L\notin K_M)<-M$ for every $M\geq0$. The strict inequality can equivalently be obtained from the common non-strict definition by choosing a larger containment level.
