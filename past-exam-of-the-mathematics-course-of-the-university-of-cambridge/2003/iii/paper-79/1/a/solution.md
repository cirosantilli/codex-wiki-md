At [large-deviation speed](../../../../../../large-deviation-speed.md) $L$, the [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) gives the [large deviation principle](../../../../../../large-deviation-principle.md) on $\mathbb R$ with [good rate function](../../../../../../good-rate-function.md)

$$
\boxed{J(b)=\begin{cases}\lambda b,&b\geq0,\\+\infty,&b<0.\end{cases}}
$$

In particular, $\mathbb P(B/L\geq b)=e^{-\lambda Lb}$ for $b\geq0$. Its finite [sublevel sets](../../../../../../sublevel-set.md) are the [compact sets](../../../../../../compact-space.md) $[0,r/\lambda]$.
