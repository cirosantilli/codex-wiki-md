<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

In the ordinary local two-derivative matter class, the general ungauged [supersymmetric action](../../../../../supersymmetric-action.md) for a [chiral superfield](../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) is

$$
\boxed{S=\int d^4x\,d^4\theta\,K(Z,\bar Z)
+\left[\int d^4x\,d^2\theta\,W(Z)+\mathrm{h.c.}\right].}
$$

The [Kähler potential](../../../../../kahler-potential.md) $K$ is real, and the [superpotential](../../../../../superpotential.md) $W$ is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function.md). Adding $f(Z)+\bar f(\bar Z)$ to $K$ changes the [action](../../../../../action.md) only by a [boundary term](../../../../../boundary-term.md): full [superspace integration](../../../../../superspace-integration.md) of a chiral or antichiral function vanishes modulo that term. This is a [Kähler transformation](../../../../../kahler-transformation.md). A positive [Kähler metric](../../../../../kahler-metric.md) $G=K_{z\bar z}$ is required for a nonsingular positive-norm kinetic term, not just for derivative counting.

To see the differential order, the bosonic component Lagrangian in mostly-plus signature contains

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{bos}}=-G\partial_mz\partial^m\bar z
+G F\bar F+W_zF+\overline{W_z}\bar F.
$$

The [auxiliary field](../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) equation is algebraic: $F=-G^{-1}\overline{W_z}$ on a purely bosonic configuration. It gives the [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) $U=G^{-1}|W_z|^2$. The scalar equations have at most two spacetime derivatives, the [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) equations from the sigma-model kinetic term have one, and the remaining interaction terms do not raise this order. The fermionic corrections to the auxiliary equation are still algebraic.

For four-dimensional [renormalizability](../../../../../renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md), assign [engineering dimensions](../../../../../engineering-dimension.md) $[Z]=1$, $[d^4\theta]=2$, and $[d^2\theta]=1$. Hence, up to a [Kähler transformation](../../../../../kahler-transformation.md) and a constant field normalization, the restrictions are

$$
\boxed{K=\bar Z Z,\qquad
W=w_0+aZ+\frac m2Z^2+\frac g{3!}Z^3.}
$$

A mixed higher-degree term in $K$ or a term of degree above three in $W$ introduces a negative-mass-dimension coupling. The constant $w_0$ has no local effect in global [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md), as in the [constant superpotential in global supersymmetry](../../../../../constant-superpotential-in-global-supersymmetry.md).

The boxed general-action statement uses the conventional two-derivative matter interpretation. It is not a classification of all derivative-dependent effective [superfield](../../../../../superfield.md) [actions](../../../../../action.md) by the order of their bosonic equations alone. For example, a scalar Lagrangian quartic in first derivatives still yields a second-order Euler–Lagrange equation, and [supersymmetric](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) derivative-dependent interactions can contain such terms. Their auxiliary and fermionic equations require separate checking. This distinction is explicit in [the original construction of supersymmetric derivative-dependent scalar actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.3748). Such [actions](../../../../../action.md) are outside the ordinary $K+W$ class used for the renormalizable answer here.
