<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The matrix $C\gamma^m$ is symmetric. Its contraction with two identical odd [Grassmann variables](../../../../../../grassmann-variable.md) is antisymmetric under their exchange, so

$$
\boxed{\bar\lambda\gamma^m\lambda=0.}
$$

This is the vector case of the [four-dimensional Majorana bilinear vanishing criterion](../../../../../../four-dimensional-majorana-bilinear-vanishing-criterion.md).
