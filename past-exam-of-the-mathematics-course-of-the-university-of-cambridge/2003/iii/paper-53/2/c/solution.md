<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Four-dimensional [Hodge duality](../../../../../../hodge-duality.md) identifies $\gamma^{mn}\gamma_5$ with a nonzero convention-dependent multiple of $\epsilon^{mnpq}\gamma_{pq}$. Every $C\gamma_{pq}$ is symmetric by the [four-dimensional Majorana bilinear vanishing criterion](../../../../../../four-dimensional-majorana-bilinear-vanishing-criterion.md); taking this linear combination preserves symmetry. Hence

$$
\boxed{\bar\lambda\gamma^{mn}\gamma_5\lambda=0.}
$$

The phase in the duality formula depends on the definition of $\gamma_5$ and orientation, but does not affect the vanishing result.
