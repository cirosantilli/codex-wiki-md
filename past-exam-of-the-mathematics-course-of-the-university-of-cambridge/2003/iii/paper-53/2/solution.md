↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Choose the [charge conjugation](../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) matrix so that $C^T=-C$ and $(\gamma^m)^T=-C\gamma^mC^{-1}$, and write $\bar\lambda=\lambda^TC$. Since the components are odd [Grassmann variables](../../../../../grassmann-variable.md),

$$
\lambda^TM\lambda=\frac12\lambda^T(M-M^T)\lambda.
$$

Thus a [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) gives zero, whereas a nonzero [antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) can give a nonzero bilinear. The [four-dimensional Majorana bilinear vanishing criterion](../../../../../four-dimensional-majorana-bilinear-vanishing-criterion.md) follows by transposing an antisymmetrized product and reversing its $n$ factors:

$$
\bigl(C\gamma^{m_1\ldots m_n}\bigr)^T
=-(-1)^{n(n+1)/2}C\gamma^{m_1\ldots m_n}.
$$

For $n=0,1,2,3$, the symmetry types are respectively antisymmetric, symmetric, symmetric and antisymmetric. These are the same-spinor selection rules of the [Majorana spinor](../../../../../majorana-spinor.md); they would be reversed for a commuting spinor. **The vanishing choices are (a) and (c).**
