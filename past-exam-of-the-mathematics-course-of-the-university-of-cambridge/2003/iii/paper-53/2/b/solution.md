<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The matrix $C$ is antisymmetric, so the scalar [Majorana spinor](../../../../../../majorana-spinor.md) bilinear is **not identically zero**. For example, choose two independent odd [Grassmann variables](../../../../../../grassmann-variable.md) $\lambda_\alpha,\lambda_\beta$ at a pair of indices with $C_{\alpha\beta}\ne0$. Their contribution is $2C_{\alpha\beta}\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\beta$, which is nonzero as a Grassmann polynomial. This is why a [Majorana mass term](../../../../../../majorana-mass-term.md) is possible.
