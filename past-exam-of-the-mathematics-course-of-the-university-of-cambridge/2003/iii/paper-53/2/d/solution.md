<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The three-gamma matrix $C\gamma^{mnp}$ is antisymmetric, and for three distinct indices it is nonzero. A pair of nonzero Grassmann components therefore produces a nonzero term exactly as in the scalar case. Consequently this three-form bilinear is **not identically zero**. Equivalently it is dual to the axial-vector [Majorana spinor](../../../../../../majorana-spinor.md) bilinear, which is allowed; it must not be confused with the vanishing vector in part (a).
