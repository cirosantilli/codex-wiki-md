<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Suppose $J$ is a [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) vanishing at both endpoints of a nonconstant [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) segment $[0,t_0]$. Its tangential component $h(t)=\langle J(t),T(t)\rangle$ satisfies

$$
h''(t)=\langle D_t^2J,T\rangle=-\langle R(J,T)T,T\rangle=0,
$$

because $D_tT=0$ and the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor.md) has the usual skew-adjoint symmetry. The two zero endpoint values imply $h\equiv0$. Both $J$ and $D_tJ$ are therefore perpendicular to $T$.

The [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) from these [Jacobi fields](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) to $T(0)^\perp$ given by $J\mapsto D_tJ(0)$ is injective: $J(0)=0$ together with $D_tJ(0)=0$ forces $J\equiv0$ by uniqueness for the linear [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) defining a [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md). Its target has dimension $n-1$, so

$$
\boxed{\text{multiplicity}\leq n-1.}
$$

This proves the [endpoint-vanishing normal Jacobi fields have dimension at most n-1](../../../../../../endpoint-vanishing-normal-jacobi-fields-have-dimension-at-most-n-1.md) bound. For a constant [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) the equation is $J''=0$, and two zero values instead force $J=0$, so there is no [conjugate point](../../../../../../conjugate-point.md).
