<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take unit-curvature [Real projective space](../../../../../../real-projective-space.md) $\mathbb{RP}^n=S^n/\{\pm1\}$ with $n\geq2$, and any point $p=[v]$. It is complete with positive [sectional curvature](../../../../../../sectional-curvature.md). A unit-speed [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) through $p$ lifts to

$$
\widetilde\gamma(t)=\cos t\,v+\sin t\,w,\qquad |v|=|w|=1,\quad\langle v,w\rangle=0.
$$

Since the two representatives of an endpoint are antipodal, [Riemannian distance](../../../../../../riemannian-distance.md) in the quotient is the smaller of the two spherical distances:

$$
d([v],[u])=\arccos|\langle v,u\rangle|,\qquad d(p,\gamma(t))=\arccos|\cos t|.
$$

This equals $t$ for $0\leq t\leq\pi/2$ and is strictly smaller just afterwards. Thus the [Riemannian cut point](../../../../../../riemannian-cut-point.md) occurs at time $\pi/2$ in every direction.

Along a curvature-one [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md), every normal [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) with initial value zero is $J(t)=\sin t\,P_ta$, where $P_t$ is [parallel transport](../../../../../../parallel-transport.md) and $a\perp\dot\gamma(0)$. Its first further zero is $t=\pi$. The quotient map is a [local isometry](../../../../../../local-isometry.md), so the [Jacobi field](../../../../../../jacobi-field.md) equation and conjugacy times are unchanged. Hence

$$
\boxed{t_{\mathrm{cut}}=\pi/2<\pi=t_{\mathrm{conj}}\quad\text{in every direction}.}
$$

This is [cut and conjugate times in round real projective space](../../../../../../cut-and-conjugate-times-in-round-real-projective-space.md).
