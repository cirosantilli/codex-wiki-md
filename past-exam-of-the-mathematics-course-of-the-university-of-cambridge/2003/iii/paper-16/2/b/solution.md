<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\pi:\widetilde M\to M$ be the [universal cover](../../../../../../universal-cover.md) and equip it with the pullback [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md) $\widetilde g=\pi^*g$. The [covering map](../../../../../../covering-space.md) is a [local isometry](../../../../../../local-isometry.md), so $\widetilde M$ has constant [sectional curvature](../../../../../../sectional-curvature.md) one.

We must check completeness of the lifted [Riemannian metric](../../../../../../riemannian-metric.md). A [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) on $\widetilde M$ projects to a [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) on $M$. By [geodesic completeness](../../../../../../geodesic-completeness.md) of $M$, the projected [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md) extends for all real time. Its unique lift with a specified initial point extends the original lifted [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md), because $\pi$ is a [local isometry](../../../../../../local-isometry.md). Hence $\widetilde M$ is geodesically complete, and the [Hopf-Rinow theorem](../../../../../../hopf-rinow-theorem.md) also gives metric completeness.

The allowed constant-curvature form of [Cartan-Ambrose-Hicks theorem](../../../../../../cartan-ambrose-hicks-theorem.md) says that a linear tangent-space [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md) between complete simply connected [Riemannian manifolds](../../../../../../riemannian-manifold.md) of the same constant [sectional curvature](../../../../../../sectional-curvature.md) extends to a global [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md). The unit round [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) $S^n$ is complete, simply connected for $n\geq2$, and has [sectional curvature](../../../../../../sectional-curvature.md) one. Comparing it with $\widetilde M$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{(\widetilde M,\widetilde g)\cong S^n.}
$$

This is the covering step in the [classification of complete positive constant-curvature manifolds](../../../../../../classification-of-complete-positive-constant-curvature-manifolds.md). As usual for this assertion, $n\geq2$ is understood: in dimension one there are no sectional two-planes, and the [universal cover](../../../../../../universal-cover.md) of the circle is the line, not the circle.
