<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Here the [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) of a finite-dimensional complex [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) is a maximal toral subalgebra: it is abelian, its adjoint operators are simultaneously diagonalizable, and its centralizer is itself. These standard properties give the [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\oplus\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Phi}\mathfrak g_\alpha,\qquad \mathfrak g_\alpha=\{X\in\mathfrak g:[H,X]=\alpha(H)X\ \forall H\in\mathfrak h\}.}
$$

The [roots of a root system](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) $\alpha$ are the nonzero [linear functionals](../../../../../../linear-functional.md) in $\mathfrak h^*$ for which $\mathfrak g_\alpha\ne0$; $\Phi$ is their finite set. Each [root space](../../../../../../root-space.md) is one-dimensional. The zero [weight space](../../../../../../weight-space.md) is the centralizer of $\mathfrak h$, hence exactly $\mathfrak h$. Thus the displayed sum is a direct sum of simultaneous eigenspaces of the [Adjoint representation](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). It is the Cartan root-space decomposition meant here.

The bracket respects weight addition: $[\mathfrak g_\alpha,\mathfrak g_\beta]\subseteq\mathfrak g_{\alpha+\beta}$, where $\mathfrak g_0=\mathfrak h$ and a missing weight means the zero space. This follows by applying the [Jacobi identity](../../../../../../jacobi-identity.md) with $H\in\mathfrak h$ to the bracket of two root vectors. It explains how the vector-space decomposition encodes the [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) structure.
