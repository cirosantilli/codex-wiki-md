<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

First show that the [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) is orthogonal, for the [Killing form](../../../../../../killing-form.md), to every nonzero [root space](../../../../../../root-space.md). For $H\in\mathfrak h$ and $X\in\mathfrak g_\alpha$, choose $H_0\in\mathfrak h$ with $\alpha(H_0)\ne0$. Invariance and the abelian nature of $\mathfrak h$ give

$$
\alpha(H_0)B(H,X)=B(H,[H_0,X])=B([H,H_0],X)=0.
$$

Hence $B(H,X)=0$.

Now let $H$ be in the radical of the restriction to $\mathfrak h$, so $B(H,H')=0$ for every $H'\in\mathfrak h$. The preceding argument also gives $B(H,\mathfrak g_\alpha)=0$ for every root. By the [root-space decomposition](../../../../../../root-space-decomposition.md), $H$ is orthogonal to all of $\mathfrak g$. The assumed nondegeneracy on $\mathfrak g$ forces $H=0$. Therefore **the Killing form restricted to the Cartan subalgebra is nondegenerate**.
