<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $E$ be the real span of the roots in $\mathfrak h^*$. The [weight lattice](../../../../../../weight-lattice.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Lambda_W=P=\{\mu\in E:\mu(h_\alpha)\in\mathbb Z\text{ for every root }\alpha\},}
$$

where $h_\alpha$ is the [coroot](../../../../../../coroot.md) in the root's [sl2 subalgebra associated with a root](../../../../../../sl2-subalgebra-associated-with-a-root.md), normalized by $\alpha(h_\alpha)=2$. It suffices to test the simple coroots. Equivalently, if $\omega_i(h_{\alpha_j})=\delta_{ij}$ defines the [fundamental weights](../../../../../../fundamental-weight.md), then $P=\bigoplus_i\mathbb Z\omega_i$.

To prove [integrality of representation weights on coroots](../../../../../../integrality-of-representation-weights-on-coroots.md), restrict a finite-dimensional module $V$ to the root's [sl2 Lie algebra](../../../../../../sl2-lie-algebra.md). By the [classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](../../../../../../classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md), the eigenvalues of its Cartan element $h_\alpha$ are integers. Thus every $\mu\in\Lambda_V$ has $\mu(h_\alpha)\in\mathbb Z$. The simple coroots form a basis of the real Cartan space, so these integral values also put $\mu$ in $E$. Therefore $\boxed{\Lambda_V\subseteq\Lambda_W}$ for every finite-dimensional representation in this setting. The weight lattice need not coincide with the smaller [root lattice](../../../../../../root-lattice.md).
