<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose a real linear functional $\ell:E\to\mathbb R$ nonzero on every root. Its positive system is $\boxed{\Phi^+=\{\alpha\in\Phi:\ell(\alpha)>0\}}$. The other roots form $\Phi^-=-\Phi^+$. Thus exactly one of $\alpha,-\alpha$ is positive, and a sum of positive roots that is a root is again positive because $\ell$ is additive.

The [simple roots](../../../../../../simple-root.md) are the positive roots not expressible as a sum of two positive roots. They form a [fundamental system of a root system](../../../../../../fundamental-system-of-a-root-system.md); every positive root is a nonnegative integral combination of them. Positivity is a choice of ordering, rather than a property of a root independent of that choice.
