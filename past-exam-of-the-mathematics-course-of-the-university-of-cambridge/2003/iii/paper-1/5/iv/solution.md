<h1 id="5/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the chosen simple roots $\Delta$, the open [fundamental chamber of a root system](../../../../../../fundamental-chamber-of-a-root-system.md) is

$$
\boxed{C=\{\lambda\in E:(\lambda,\alpha)>0\text{ for every }\alpha\in\Delta\}.}
$$

Equivalently $\lambda(h_\alpha)>0$ for every simple coroot. Since all positive roots are nonnegative combinations of simple roots, requiring the inequality for all positive roots gives the same chamber. It is a connected component of the complement of the root reflection hyperplanes; the [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) permutes those chambers simply transitively.

The [Closed dominant Weyl chamber](../../../../../../closed-dominant-weyl-chamber.md) is obtained by replacing the inequalities by $\ge0$. It is the cone generated by the [fundamental weights](../../../../../../fundamental-weight.md). When the fundamental Weyl chamber is taken to include its walls, this closed convention is intended; both conventions determine the same positive system and boundary rays.
