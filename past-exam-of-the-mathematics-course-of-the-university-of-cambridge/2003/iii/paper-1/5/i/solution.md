↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](../../../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md) $V$, a [weight of a representation](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) relative to $\mathfrak h$ is a linear functional $\mu\in\mathfrak h^*$ for which the simultaneous eigenspace

$$
V_\mu=\{v\in V:H\cdot v=\mu(H)v\text{ for every }H\in\mathfrak h\}
$$

is nonzero. Its elements are [weight vectors](../../../../../../weight-vector.md), and $\dim V_\mu$ is the [weight multiplicity](../../../../../../weight-multiplicity.md). The set of weights is $\boxed{\Lambda_V=\{\mu:V_\mu\ne0\}}$. For a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), the Cartan action on a finite-dimensional module is simultaneously diagonalizable, giving $V=\bigoplus_{\mu\in\Lambda_V}V_\mu$. The set of weights and their multiplicities are distinct information: several basis vectors can occupy the same point of a [weight diagram](../../../../../../weight-diagram.md).
