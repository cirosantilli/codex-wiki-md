<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The nine one-dimensional weight spaces from part (i) all lie in $\ker C$, because the dual index differs from every polynomial index. Their bases remain $T_i^{jj}$ at weight $2\epsilon_j-\epsilon_i$ for $i\ne j$, and $T_i^{jk}$ at weight $-2\epsilon_i$ for three distinct indices $i,j,k$.

At weight $\epsilon_i$, the full space has basis $T_i^{ii},T_j^{ij},T_k^{ik}$, where $j,k$ are the two other indices. Their contractions are $2e_i,e_i,e_i$. Thus the contraction kernel has the independent basis $K_{ij}=2T_j^{ij}-T_i^{ii}$ and $K_{ik}=2T_k^{ik}-T_i^{ii}$. Explicitly the [weight basis of the trace-free symmetric-square dual tensor module](../../../../../../../weight-basis-of-the-trace-free-symmetric-square-dual-tensor-module.md) is completed by

$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{weight}&\text{basis in }\ker C\\\hline
\epsilon_1&K_{12}=2T_2^{12}-T_1^{11},\quad K_{13}=2T_3^{13}-T_1^{11}\\
\epsilon_2&K_{21}=2T_1^{12}-T_2^{22},\quad K_{23}=2T_3^{23}-T_2^{22}\\
\epsilon_3&K_{31}=2T_1^{13}-T_3^{33},\quad K_{32}=2T_2^{23}-T_3^{33}
\end{array}
$$

Together with the nine single vectors, these give fifteen independent vectors. They are precisely the $T$ and $K$ labels on the fifteen-dimensional weight diagram.

The defining summand has only the following three weight spaces:

$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
\epsilon_1&S_1=T_1^{11}+T_2^{12}+T_3^{13}\\
\epsilon_2&S_2=T_1^{12}+T_2^{22}+T_3^{23}\\
\epsilon_3&S_3=T_1^{13}+T_2^{23}+T_3^{33}
\end{array}
$$

Each $S_i=s(e_i)$ is nonzero, has its indicated weight and satisfies $C(S_i)=4e_i$. These are the labels on the three-dimensional diagram. The [highest-weight vectors](../../../../../../../highest-weight-vector.md) in the two summands are

$$
\boxed{z^*\otimes x^2=T_3^{11}\text{ in }L(2,1),\qquad S_1=s(x)\text{ in }L(1,0).}
$$

At each repeated weight, the two kernel vectors together with $S_i$ span the original three-dimensional weight space, since the kernel has dimension two and $C(S_i)\ne0$. Thus the displayed diagrams specify all submodule weight-space bases.
