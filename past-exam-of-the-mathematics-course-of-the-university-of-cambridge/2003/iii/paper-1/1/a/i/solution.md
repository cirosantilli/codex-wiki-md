<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use $h=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$, $e=E_{12}$ and $f=E_{21}$ in the [sl2 Lie algebra](../../../../../../../sl2-lie-algebra.md), so $hx=x$, $hy=-y$, $ey=x$ and $fx=y$. On the [symmetric power](../../../../../../../symmetric-power.md), a monomial $x^{3-k}y^k$ has weight $3-2k$. The [tensor product of Lie algebra representations](../../../../../../../tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md) adds weights, because $h$ acts as $h\otimes I+I\otimes h$.

Put $A_k=x\otimes x^{3-k}y^k$ and $B_k=y\otimes x^{3-k}y^k$ for $0\le k\le3$. These eight vectors are a [basis](../../../../../../../basis.md) of $U$; their weights are $4-2k$ and $2-2k$. Thus the [weight-space decomposition](../../../../../../../weight-space-decomposition.md) and a [weight basis](../../../../../../../weight-basis.md) for each summand are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{weight}&\text{basis of its weight space}&\text{multiplicity}\\\hline
4&A_0&1\\
2&A_1,\ B_0&2\\
0&A_2,\ B_1&2\\
-2&A_3,\ B_2&2\\
-4&B_3&1
\end{array}
$$

In particular $\boxed{U=U_4\oplus U_2\oplus U_0\oplus U_{-2}\oplus U_{-4}}$, with dimensions summing to eight. Here the subscripts are the eigenvalues of the chosen Cartan element $h$.
