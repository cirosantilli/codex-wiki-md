<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose $b=(ad-1)/N\in\mathbb Z$, and take the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
\gamma=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\N&d\end{pmatrix}\in SL_2(\mathbb Z).
$$

At $w=\tau-d/N$ its denominator is $Nw+d=N\tau$, while its numerator is $aw+b=a\tau-1/N$. Consequently

$$
\gamma w=\frac aN-\frac1{N^2\tau}.
$$

Applying the weight-$k$ transformation law of the [cusp form](../../../../../../cusp-form.md) at $w\in\mathbb H$ proves the [rational-cusp inversion of a level-one cusp form](../../../../../../rational-cusp-inversion-of-a-level-one-cusp-form.md):

$$
\boxed{f\!\left(\frac aN-\frac1{N^2\tau}\right)=(N\tau)^kf\!\left(\tau-\frac dN\right).}
$$
