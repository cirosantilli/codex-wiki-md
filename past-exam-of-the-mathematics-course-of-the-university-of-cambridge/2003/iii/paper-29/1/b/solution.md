↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Equality of the [probability distributions](../../../../../../probability-distribution.md) implies equality of all integrals for which they are finite, in particular $\mathbb E[(\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G])^2]=\mathbb E[X^2]<\infty$. Part (a), applied to this [conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md), therefore gives $\boxed{X=\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G]\text{ almost surely}.}$ The second-moment assumption justifies the squared-norm argument; the next part removes it.
