<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Identify the ambient [Euclidean space](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $\mathbb R^{2m+2}$ with $\mathbb C^{m+1}$ and let $J$ be multiplication by $i$. Equivalently, in real coordinates,

$$
J(x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_{2m},x_{2m+1})=(-x_1,x_0,\ldots,-x_{2m+1},x_{2m}).
$$

This [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) preserves the norm and satisfies $\langle x,Jx\rangle=0$. On the unit [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) $S^{2m+1}$ define

$$
\boxed{V(x)=Jx.}
$$

It is continuous, tangent because it lies in $x^\perp=T_xS^{2m+1}$, and has norm one everywhere. It is therefore a [nowhere-zero section](../../../../../../nowhere-zero-section.md) of the [tangent bundle](../../../../../../tangent-bundle.md), including when $m=0$ and the [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) is $S^1$. This supplies a [nonvanishing tangent field on an odd-dimensional sphere](../../../../../../nonvanishing-tangent-field-on-an-odd-dimensional-sphere.md) explicitly.
