<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [continuous map](../../../../../../continuous-map.md) $f:S^2\times S^4\to\mathbb{CP}^3$, use the classes from part (a). Since $H^2(S^2\times S^4;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Zu$, there is an [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) $k$ with $f^*c=ku$. Naturality of the [cup product](../../../../../../cup-product.md) and $u^2=0$ yield

$$
f^*(c^3)=(f^*c)^3=k^3u^3=0.
$$

With the product [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) on the source and the complex [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) on the target, the [mapping degree](../../../../../../degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) $d$ is defined by $f_*[S^2\times S^4]=d[\mathbb{CP}^3]$. Evaluation on the [fundamental class](../../../../../../fundamental-class.md) gives

$$
d=\langle c^3,f_*[S^2\times S^4]\rangle
=\langle f^*(c^3),[S^2\times S^4]\rangle=0.
$$

Thus **the induced [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) on $H_6$ is zero**. This is a [cup-power obstruction to nonzero degree](../../../../../../cup-power-obstruction-to-nonzero-degree.md), rather than a consequence of the additive [homology groups](../../../../../../homology-group.md) alone.
