<h1 id="13e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $h=f-g$, whose [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) coefficients are $c_n=a_n-b_n$. Suppose some coefficient is nonzero, and let $N$ be the least such index. Inside the common convergence disc,

$$
h(z)=(z-z_0)^N\left(c_N+\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}c_{N+j}(z-z_0)^j\right).
$$

The parenthesized [analytic function](../../../../../../space-of-holomorphic-functions.md) tends to $c_N\ne0$ as $z\to z_0$, so it does not vanish in some neighborhood. Hence $h$ has no [zeros](../../../../../../zero-of-a-function.md) sufficiently near $z_0$ except possibly $z_0$ itself. This contradicts $h(z_k)=0$ with $z_k\ne z_0$ and $z_k\to z_0$. Thus **all coefficients agree**:

$$
\boxed{a_n=b_n\quad\text{for every }n\geq0.}
$$

This also proves the local isolation of [zeros](../../../../../../zero-of-a-function.md) of a nonzero [analytic function](../../../../../../space-of-holomorphic-functions.md).
