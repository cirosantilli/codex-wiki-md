<h1 id="3e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fix any $z_0\in\mathbb C$. The [Cauchy estimate](../../../../../../cauchy-estimate.md) on the circle $|z-z_0|=R$ applies for every $R>0$ because $f$ is an [entire function](../../../../../../entire-function.md). It gives

$$
|f'(z_0)|\leq\frac{\max_{|z-z_0|=R}|f(z)|}{R}\leq\frac{1+\sqrt{|z_0|+R}}R\longrightarrow0.
$$

Therefore $f'(z_0)=0$. Since $z_0$ was arbitrary and $\mathbb C$ is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md), **$f$ is constant**. This is the sublinear case of the [polynomial growth theorem for entire functions](../../../../../../polynomial-growth-theorem-for-entire-functions.md).
