<h1 id="6b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Lagrange basis polynomials](../../../../../../lagrange-cardinal-polynomial.md) satisfy $\ell_i(x_j)=\delta_{ij}$: for $j\ne i$, the product has a zero factor, whereas for $j=i$ every factor is one. The [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md)

$$
q(x)=p(x)-\sum_{i=0}^np(x_i)\ell_i(x)
$$

has [degree of a polynomial](../../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $n$ and has $n+1$ distinct [roots of a polynomial](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md). The [root bound for a polynomial](../../../../../../lagrange-root-bound-over-a-field.md) implies $q=0$. Hence **the interpolation identity holds for every polynomial of degree at most $n$**.
