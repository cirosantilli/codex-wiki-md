<h1 id="17g/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The displayed [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) are the three [Pauli matrices](../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md), in the order $\sigma_3,\sigma_1,\sigma_2$. They satisfy $\operatorname{tr}A_i=0$ and $\operatorname{tr}(A_iA_j)=2\delta_{ij}$. Every [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) has a unique expression $aI+\sum_iv_iA_i$ with all four [coefficients](../../../../../../coefficient.md) real. Therefore they form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) together with $I$.

The preceding [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) identities give $b(I,I)=-1$, $b(I,A_i)=0$, and $b(A_i,A_j)=\delta_{ij}$. The Gram [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1).}
$$

Consequently **[rank](../../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md) $=4$ and inertia signature $(3,1)$**. If signature is defined as the number of positive squares minus the number of negative squares, its value is $2$. This is the [Hermitian matrix representation of Minkowski four-vectors](../../../../../../hermitian-matrix-representation-of-minkowski-four-vectors.md), with the negative-[determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) sign convention.
