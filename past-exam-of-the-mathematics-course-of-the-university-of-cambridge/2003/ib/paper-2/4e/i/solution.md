<h1 id="4e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $f(n)\to\infty$, then for each $k$ the complement of $f^{-1}(I_k)$ contains only finitely many indices. Thus every nonempty open tail has a cofinite preimage, and the empty set has empty preimage. Hence **$f$ is continuous** from the cofinite space to the tail-[topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) space.
