<h1 id="15e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

By the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md), $\operatorname{nullity}A=n-\operatorname{rank}A\ge n-m>0$. Thus a first dependent column prefix exists. Minimality of $k$ makes the first $k-1$ columns independent, so $\operatorname{rank}A_{k-1}=k-1$. Adding one column cannot reduce this [rank](../../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md), while nonzero [nullity](../../../../../../nullity-of-a-linear-map.md) of $A_k$ implies [rank](../../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md) at most $k-1$. Hence

$$
\operatorname{rank}A_k=k-1,\qquad
\boxed{\operatorname{nullity}A_k=1.}
$$

For $k=1$ the earlier prefix is the zero-dimensional empty [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md), and the same argument applies.
