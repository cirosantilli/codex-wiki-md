<h1 id="15e/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose a fixed reference [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) and let $V,W$ have columns $v_i,w_i$. Both [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) are invertible. The vectors $v_i+\lambda w_i$ are dependent exactly when $\det(V+\lambda W)=0$. This is a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in $\lambda$ of degree $m$, whose leading [coefficient](../../../../../../coefficient.md) is $\det W\ne0$. A nonzero degree-$m$ [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has at most $m$ distinct real roots. Thus **there are at most $m$ exceptional parameters**. This is the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) argument for the [matrix pencil](../../../../../../matrix-pencil.md) $V+\lambda W$.
