<h1 id="14e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For every $v\in V$, $(\alpha+\beta)v=\alpha v+\beta v$, so $\operatorname{im}(\alpha+\beta)\subseteq\operatorname{im}\alpha+\operatorname{im}\beta$. Taking [dimensions](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) and applying the preceding [dimension formula for a sum of subspaces](../../../../../../dimension-formula-for-a-sum-of-subspaces.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{rank}(\alpha+\beta)\leq\dim(\operatorname{im}\alpha+\operatorname{im}\beta)\leq\operatorname{rank}\alpha+\operatorname{rank}\beta.
$$

This proves the upper [rank bounds for a sum of linear maps](../../../../../../rank-bounds-for-a-sum-of-linear-maps.md), without assuming the two images have zero intersection.
