<h1 id="14e/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply the preceding [rank bounds for a sum of linear maps](../../../../../../rank-bounds-for-a-sum-of-linear-maps.md) to $\alpha=(\alpha+\beta)+(-\beta)$. Since multiplying a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) by $-1$ does not change its image, $\operatorname{rank}\alpha\leq\operatorname{rank}(\alpha+\beta)+\operatorname{rank}\beta$. Thus $\operatorname{rank}(\alpha+\beta)\geq\operatorname{rank}\alpha-\operatorname{rank}\beta$. Interchanging the maps gives the other difference, proving

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}(\alpha+\beta)\geq|\operatorname{rank}\alpha-\operatorname{rank}\beta|.}
$$
