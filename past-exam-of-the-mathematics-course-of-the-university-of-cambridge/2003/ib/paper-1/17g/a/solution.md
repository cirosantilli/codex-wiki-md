<h1 id="17g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a nonzero residue $a$ modulo an odd [prime](../../../../../../prime-number.md) $p$, the [Legendre symbol](../../../../../../legendre-symbol.md) $(a/p)$ equals $+1$ if $a$ is a nonzero square modulo $p$ and $-1$ otherwise. The extension to multiples of $p$ has value zero. [Euler's criterion](../../../../../../euler-s-criterion.md) states

$$
\boxed{a^{(p-1)/2}\equiv\left(\frac ap\right)\pmod p,\qquad p\nmid a.}
$$
