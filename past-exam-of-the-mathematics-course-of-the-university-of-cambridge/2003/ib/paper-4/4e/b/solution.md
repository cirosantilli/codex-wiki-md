<h1 id="4e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [uniform limit theorem](../../../../../../uniform-limit-theorem.md) first gives [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of $f$ on $D$. For any triangle $\Gamma$ whose closed interior is in $D$, the [Cauchy integral theorem](../../../../../../cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) gives $\int_\Gamma f_n(z)\,dz=0$. Moreover,

$$
\left|\int_\Gamma(f-f_n)(z)\,dz\right|
\le\operatorname{length}(\Gamma)\sup_D|f-f_n|\longrightarrow0.
$$

Hence $\int_\Gamma f=0$, and [Morera's theorem](../../../../../../morera-s-theorem.md) proves that **$f$ is analytic on $D$**. In fact, the proof only needs [locally uniform convergence](../../../../../../locally-uniform-convergence.md) on compact subsets, since each triangle is compact; the stated [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) on the entire domain is stronger.
