<h1 id="8b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the middle annulus, expand the [pole](../../../../../../pole.md) at one in negative powers and the [pole](../../../../../../pole.md) at two in positive powers:

$$
-\frac1{z-1}=-\sum_{n=0}^\infty z^{-n-1},\qquad
\frac1{z-2}=-\sum_{n=0}^\infty2^{-n-1}z^n.
$$

Their [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) convergence conditions are $|z|>1$ and $|z|<2$, respectively. The required [Laurent series](../../../../../../laurent-series.md) is

$$
\boxed{f(z)=-\sum_{n=0}^\infty z^{-n-1}-\sum_{n=0}^\infty2^{-n-1}z^n},\qquad1<|z|<2.
$$
