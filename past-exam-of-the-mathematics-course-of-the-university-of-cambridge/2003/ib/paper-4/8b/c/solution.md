<h1 id="8b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Outside both [poles](../../../../../../pole.md), the two [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) use negative powers:

$$
-\frac1{z-1}=-\sum_{n=0}^\infty z^{-n-1},\qquad
\frac1{z-2}=\sum_{n=0}^\infty2^n z^{-n-1}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty(2^n-1)z^{-n-1}},\qquad |z|>2.
$$

The coefficient of $z^{-1}$ vanishes, as it must since the [rational function](../../../../../../rational-function.md) starts with $z^{-2}$ at infinity.
