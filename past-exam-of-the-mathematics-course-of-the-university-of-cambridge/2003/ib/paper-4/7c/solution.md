<h1 id="7c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7C](../7c.md)

For steady [inviscid flow](../../../../../inviscid-flow.md) at atmospheric [pressure](../../../../../pressure.md), [Bernoulli's equation](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) along a freely falling [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md) gives $u^2/2+gz=u_0^2/2+gH$. The downward speed is therefore

$$
\boxed{u=u_0\left[1+\frac{2g}{u_0^2}(H-z)\right]^{1/2}}.
$$

For the incompressible jet, [mass conservation](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) fixes its [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md): $\pi R^2u=\pi a^2u_0$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{R=a\left[1+\frac{2g}{u_0^2}(H-z)\right]^{-1/4}}.
$$

Far from impact, the [inviscid falling jet and radial impact film](../../../../../inviscid-falling-jet-and-radial-impact-film.md) is thin and nearly horizontal. Let $v$ be its radial speed and $h$ its thickness. The same [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) now crosses a cylindrical surface of area $2\pi rh$, giving $v=a^2u_0/(2rh)$. At the [free surface](../../../../../free-surface.md), atmospheric [pressure](../../../../../pressure.md) and [Bernoulli's equation](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) give $v^2=u_0^2+2g(H-h)$. Eliminate $v$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\frac{a^4}{4r^2h^2}=1+\frac{2g}{u_0^2}(H-h)}.
$$

This description assumes a steady incompressible jet, negligible surface tension, and negligible dissipation between the tube and the thin far-field film. It is a free-surface relation, not an assertion that [pressure](../../../../../pressure.md) is atmospheric throughout the film or that the impact region itself is thin.
