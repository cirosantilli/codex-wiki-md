<h1 id="2d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $v(r,t)=ru(r,t)$. Multiplication of the radial [wave equation](../../../../../../wave-equation-split.md) by $r$ gives $v_{tt}=c^2v_{rr}$ for $r>0$. In the [characteristic coordinates](../../../../../../characteristic-coordinate.md) $\xi=r+ct$, $\eta=r-ct$, this becomes $v_{\xi\eta}=0$. Integrating first in $\xi$ and then in $\eta$ gives $v=f(\xi)+g(\eta)$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{u(r,t)=\frac{f(r+ct)+g(r-ct)}r}\qquad(r>0).
$$

The two terms are incoming and outgoing radial disturbances. If the field is finite at the origin, their numerators must cancel there; arbitrary independent choices generally produce a $1/r$ singularity.
