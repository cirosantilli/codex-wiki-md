<h1 id="8a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For every integer $m$, [De Moivre's theorem](../../../../../../de-moivre-s-theorem.md) states $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^m=\cos(m\theta)+i\sin(m\theta)$. Taking the real part for $m=5$ gives

$$
\cos5\theta=\cos^5\theta-10\cos^3\theta\sin^2\theta+5\cos\theta\sin^4\theta.
$$

Replace $\sin^2\theta$ by $1-\cos^2\theta$ and expand:

$$
\boxed{\cos5\theta=16\cos^5\theta-20\cos^3\theta+5\cos\theta.}
$$

The resulting polynomial is the fifth [Chebyshev polynomial](../../../../../../chebyshev-polynomial.md).
