<h1 id="7b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $[v]_\times$ denote the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) matrix, with $[v]_\times x=v\times x$. Then

$$
\boxed{A=uu^T+[v]_\times,\qquad A_{ij}=u_iu_j-\epsilon_{ijk}v_k.}
$$

The two [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) calculations use an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) adapted to $u,v$.
