<h1 id="7b/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $u\cdot v=0$, use the positively oriented [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $(u,v\times u,v)$. The [cross product](../../../../../../../cross-product.md) identities give $Au=u+v\times u$, $A(v\times u)=-u$ and $Av=0$. In this basis,

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\lambda(\lambda^2-\lambda+1)$, so

$$
\boxed{\lambda=0,\quad\frac{1+i\sqrt3}{2},\quad\frac{1-i\sqrt3}{2}.}
$$
