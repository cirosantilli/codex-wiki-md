<h1 id="2a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In [spherical coordinates](../../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md), $z=r\cos\theta$ and $x^2+y^2=r^2\sin^2\theta$, so the [elliptic paraboloid](../../../../../../elliptic-paraboloid.md) has equation

$$
\boxed{r\cos\theta=r^2\sin^2\theta.}
$$

Away from the origin this is $r=\cos\theta/\sin^2\theta$, with $0<\theta<\pi/2$. The undivided equation also includes the origin, where angular coordinates are not unique.
