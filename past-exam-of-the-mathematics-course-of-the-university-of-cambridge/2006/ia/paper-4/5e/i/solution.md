↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By the inverse identity established in the root solution, the composite $T$ is a [symmetric relation](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md) precisely when

$$
T=T^{-1}=S\circ R.
$$

Thus condition (i) implies condition (iii). Conversely, if $S\circ R\subseteq R\circ S$, take inverse relations. Since $R,S$ are symmetric, this gives the reverse inclusion $R\circ S\subseteq S\circ R$. The two composites are equal, making $T$ symmetric. Therefore **(i) and (iii) are equivalent**, and either one [forces](../../../../../../force.md) commutation.
