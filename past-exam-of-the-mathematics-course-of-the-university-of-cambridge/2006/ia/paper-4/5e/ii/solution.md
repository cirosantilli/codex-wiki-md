<h1 id="5e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [binary relation](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) $T$ is transitive exactly when $T\circ T\subseteq T$. If condition (iii) holds, associativity and monotonicity of [composition of relations](../../../../../../composition-of-relations.md) give

$$
T\circ T=R\circ S\circ R\circ S
\subseteq R\circ R\circ S\circ S
=R\circ S=T.
$$

Hence (iii) implies (ii). Conversely, the inclusions $R,S\subseteq T$ imply

$$
S\circ R\subseteq T\circ T.
$$

If $T$ is a [transitive relation](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md), the right side lies in $T$, which is condition (iii). Thus **(ii) and (iii) are equivalent**.
