<h1 id="9a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the first leg $y=0$ and $x$ decreases from one to zero; on the second $x=0$ and $y$ increases from zero to one. Adding their [line integrals](../../../../../../line-integral.md) gives

$$
I_2=\alpha\int_1^0x^2\,dx+\beta\int_0^1y^2\,dy
=\boxed{\frac{\beta-\alpha}{3}}.
$$

The negative sign in the first term follows from the direction of traversal.
