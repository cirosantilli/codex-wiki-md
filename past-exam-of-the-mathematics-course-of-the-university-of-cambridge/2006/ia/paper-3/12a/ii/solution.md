<h1 id="12a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Decompose the [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md) into its orthogonal parts $\mathbf E=\mathbf E_\perp+\mathbf E_\parallel$, where $\mathbf E_\parallel=n(n\cdot\mathbf E)$. The [uniaxial conductivity tensor](../../../../../../uniaxial-conductivity-tensor.md) acts on them as

$$
\mathbf J=\alpha\mathbf E_\perp+(\alpha+\gamma)\mathbf E_\parallel.
$$

If $\mathbf J=0$, its perpendicular and parallel components each vanish. Since $\alpha\ne0$ and $\alpha+\gamma\ne0$, this forces both field components to be zero. The contrapositive gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E\ne0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\mathbf J\ne0}.
$$

Equivalently, the tensor has nonzero [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\alpha,\alpha,\alpha+\gamma$ and determinant $\alpha^2(\alpha+\gamma)\ne0$.
