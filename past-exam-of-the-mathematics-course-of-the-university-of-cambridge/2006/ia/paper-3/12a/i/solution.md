<h1 id="12a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Writing the [uniaxial conductivity tensor](../../../../../../uniaxial-conductivity-tensor.md) in vector form gives the [electric current density](../../../../../../current-density.md)

$$
\mathbf J=\alpha\mathbf E+\gamma n(n\cdot\mathbf E).
$$

Choose the plane through the origin perpendicular to $n$:

$$
\boxed{\Pi=\{\mathbf E:n\cdot\mathbf E=0\}}.
$$

For an [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field.md) in this plane, the anisotropic term vanishes and $\mathbf J=\alpha\mathbf E$. Thus every nonzero such field is parallel or antiparallel to its current according to the sign of the nonzero $\alpha$. The zero field gives zero current. This establishes collinearity throughout the plane without assuming that the tensor is isotropic.
