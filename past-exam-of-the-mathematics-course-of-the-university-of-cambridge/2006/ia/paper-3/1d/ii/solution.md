↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** Consider the real [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
A_1=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1&0\\0&c&-c\\0&c&c\end{pmatrix},\qquad c=\frac1{\sqrt2}.
$$

It is block upper triangular, so its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is the same as that of $A_0$ and it has the required [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md). Its second column, however, has squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $1+c^2+c^2=2$, whereas every column of an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) has norm one. Thus

$$
\boxed{A_1^{\mathsf T}A_1\ne I}.
$$

Unit-modulus [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) alone do not imply orthogonality; they do not control the geometry of the [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md).
