The preceding proofs show that this inclusion forces both [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) and [transitivity](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md) of $T$; its [reflexivity](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) already follows from that of $R,S$. It actually forces equality of the composites, not just a one-way inclusion:

$$
\boxed{S\circ R\subseteq R\circ S\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S\circ R=R\circ S.}
$$

This is the [composition of commuting equivalence relations](../../../../../../composition-of-commuting-equivalence-relations.md) criterion. It remains to establish the asserted smallest-extension characterization, rather than merely to identify an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md).
