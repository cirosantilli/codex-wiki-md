↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [binary relation](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) $T$ is [symmetric](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md) exactly when $T=T^{-1}$. By the reversed-chain identity, condition (i) therefore gives $R\circ S=S\circ R$, in particular condition (iii). Conversely, if $S\circ R\subseteq R\circ S$, reversing both sides gives $R\circ S\subseteq S\circ R$. Thus condition (iii) gives equality and therefore [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md). **Conditions (i) and (iii) are equivalent.**
