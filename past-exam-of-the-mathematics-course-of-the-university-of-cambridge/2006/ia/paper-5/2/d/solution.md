↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**False as a general performance claim.** A [microkernel](../../../../../../microkernel.md) moves services into separate protection domains, which can require extra messages, scheduling and address-space transitions. A [monolithic kernel](../../../../../../monolithic-kernel.md) can often call a service directly within its own privileged address space. The [microkernel](../../../../../../microkernel.md) gains isolation and modularity, but these do not imply greater speed. Efficient implementations and particular workloads can change the comparison; the architecture alone gives no universal ordering.
