<h1 id="8/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

With [direct memory access](../../../../../../../direct-memory-access.md), a device can initiate memory transfers, so the processor is no longer the only [computer bus](../../../../../../../computer-bus.md) master. Requests must be arbitrated to prevent conflicting transfers, and the processor may have to wait while the device owns a shared bus. The [operating system](../../../../../../../operating-system.md) must supply valid stable buffers and arrange safe completion before reusing them. On a noncoherent system, cached processor copies must be flushed or invalidated as appropriate so processor and device agree on data. These extra initiators also need suitable address and protection controls.
