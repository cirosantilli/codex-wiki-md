<h1 id="6b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) to $U(s,y)=u(s,y+s)$. Holding $y$ fixed while differentiating in $s$ gives $U_s=u_t+u_x$, while $U_y=u_x$ and $U_{yy}=u_{xx}$. Thus the [advection-diffusion equation](../../../../../../advection-diffusion-equation.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{U_s=U_{yy}+F(s,y),\qquad F(s,y)=f(s,y+s).}
$$

The moving coordinate follows the unit-speed advection, leaving the [heat equation](../../../../../../heat-equation.md) with a transformed source.
