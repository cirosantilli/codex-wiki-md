<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $Z=\sum_iY_i(\infty)$ and $\mathbf1=(1,\ldots,1)^T$. Sum the bounds in part (a) over the [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md), using [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) and the [Tonelli theorem](../../../../../../tonelli-theorem.md) for the nonnegative series:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E Z\leq
\mathbf1^T\sum_{k=0}^\infty(\beta A)^kX(0)}.
$$

The bound remains valid if the series diverges, although it then gives no finite estimate. When the [spectral radius](../../../../../../spectral-radius.md) of $\beta A$ is less than one, the series is a [Neumann series](../../../../../../neumann-series.md), giving the [resolvent bound for a discrete SIR epidemic](../../../../../../resolvent-bound-for-a-discrete-sir-epidemic.md).
