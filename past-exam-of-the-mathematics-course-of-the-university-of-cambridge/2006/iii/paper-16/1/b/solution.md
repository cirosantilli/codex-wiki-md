<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [closed orientable surface](../../../../../../closed-orientable-surface.md) $\Sigma_g$, use the [fundamental group](../../../../../../fundamental-group.md) presentation

$$
\pi_1(\Sigma_g)=\left\langle a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_g,b_g\mid\prod_{j=1}^g[a_j,b_j]=1\right\rangle.
$$

Since $g\ge1$, define a [homomorphism](../../../../../../homomorphism.md) to $\mathbb Z/n$ by sending $a_1$ to $1$ and every other displayed generator to zero. All commutators map to zero, so the relation is respected, and the map is surjective. Its kernel has index $n$. The [classification of connected covering spaces](../../../../../../classification-of-connected-covering-spaces.md) supplies a connected $n$-sheeted [covering space](../../../../../../covering-space.md), including the identity cover when $n=1$.

Every connected finite [covering space](../../../../../../covering-space.md) of $\Sigma_g$ is a [closed orientable surface](../../../../../../closed-orientable-surface.md): [compactness](../../../../../../compact-space.md) follows from finite degree, and the base orientation lifts through local covering charts. Lift a finite cell decomposition of the base. Every cell has $n$ lifts, so the [Euler characteristic under a finite covering](../../../../../../euler-characteristic-under-a-finite-covering.md) gives $\chi(\widetilde\Sigma)=n\chi(\Sigma_g)$. If the covering surface has genus $h$, this reads $2-2h=n(2-2g)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{h=1+n(g-1).}
$$

This [genus of a finite cover of a closed orientable surface](../../../../../../genus-of-a-finite-cover-of-a-closed-orientable-surface.md) depends only on the sheet number, not on the particular subgroup used to construct the cover.
