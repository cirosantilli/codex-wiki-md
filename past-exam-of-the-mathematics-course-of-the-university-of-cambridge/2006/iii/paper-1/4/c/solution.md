<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $H_{12}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)$ and $H_{23}=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,-1)$. On the [root space](../../../../../../root-space.md) spanned by $E_{ij}$, their adjoint [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $h_i-h_j$ and $k_i-k_j$; both act as zero on the [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md). Thus the [Killing form](../../../../../../killing-form.md) is the sum of their products over the six [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md). The pair of [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) $\pm(L_1-L_2)$ contributes $-4$, the pair $\pm(L_2-L_3)$ contributes $-4$, and $\pm(L_1-L_3)$ contributes $2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{B(H_{12},H_{23})=-6.}
$$

This also agrees with the [Killing form of the special linear Lie algebra](../../../../../../killing-form-of-the-special-linear-lie-algebra.md), $B(A,B)=6\operatorname{tr}(AB)$ for $\mathfrak{sl}_3$.
