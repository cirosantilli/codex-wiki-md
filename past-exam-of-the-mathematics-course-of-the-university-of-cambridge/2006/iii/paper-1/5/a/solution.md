<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Identify the real span of the [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) with its dual using the invariant [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md), and put $\alpha^\vee=2\alpha/(\alpha,\alpha)$. The [weight lattice](../../../../../../weight-lattice.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Lambda_W=\{\lambda:\langle\lambda,\alpha^\vee\rangle\in\mathbb Z\text{ for every root }\alpha\}.}
$$

The [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) is generated by the [root reflections](../../../../../../root-reflection.md) $s_\alpha$. Each reflection permutes the finite set of [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) and preserves their lengths, hence also permutes their coroots. Thus, for $\lambda\in\Lambda_W$,

$$
\langle s_\alpha\lambda,\beta^\vee\rangle=\langle\lambda,(s_\alpha\beta)^\vee\rangle\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Every generating reflection therefore maps the [weight lattice](../../../../../../weight-lattice.md) to itself, and so does every product. Since the inverse product has the same property, **every Weyl-group element acts bijectively on $\Lambda_W$**. The [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) span the ambient space, so the action on the finite [root](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) set is faithful; in particular the [Weyl group](../../../../../../weyl-group.md) is finite.
