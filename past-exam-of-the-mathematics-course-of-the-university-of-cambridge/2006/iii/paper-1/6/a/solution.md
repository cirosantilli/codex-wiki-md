↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A reduced crystallographic abstract [root system](../../../../../../root-system.md) in a finite-dimensional real [inner product space](../../../../../../inner-product-space.md) $E$ is a finite set $R$ such that: it spans $E$ and does not contain zero; the only scalar multiples of $\alpha\in R$ belonging to $R$ are $\pm\alpha$; every [root reflection](../../../../../../root-reflection.md)

$$
s_\alpha(x)=x-\frac{2(x,\alpha)}{(\alpha,\alpha)}\alpha
$$

permutes $R$; and each [Cartan integer](../../../../../../cartan-integer.md) $2(\beta,\alpha)/(\alpha,\alpha)$ is integral. These are the crystallographic and reduced conventions appropriate to [roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](../../../../../../semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md). Without crystallographic integrality, the numerical restrictions in the following subparts would not hold.
