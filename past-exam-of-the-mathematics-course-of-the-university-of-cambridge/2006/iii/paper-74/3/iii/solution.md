<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Set $s=0$ in the [horizontal-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation](../../../../../../horizontal-field-magnetoconvection-dispersion-relation.md). With positive $\sigma,\zeta$ and nonzero $a_h$, cancellation gives the stationary neutral curve

$$
R=\frac{\Lambda^3}{a_h^2}+\frac{Qm^2\Lambda}{a_h^2},\qquad
\Lambda=a_h^2+\pi^2.
$$

At fixed $a_h^2$, the [Chandrasekhar number](../../../../../../chandrasekhar-number.md) contributes a nonnegative penalty proportional to $m^2$. It can be eliminated by choosing $m=0$. It remains to minimize $f(x)=(x+\pi^2)^3/x$ for $x=k^2>0$:

$$
f'(x)=\frac{(x+\pi^2)^2(2x-\pi^2)}{x^2}.
$$

The function diverges at both endpoints and has its unique minimum at $x=\pi^2/2$. Thus the [magnetic-field-aligned rolls evade steady magnetic inhibition](../../../../../../magnetic-field-aligned-rolls-evade-steady-magnetic-inhibition.md):

$$
\boxed{m_c=0,\qquad |k_c|=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt2},\qquad R_c=\frac{27\pi^4}{4},}
$$

independently of $Q$. If higher vertical sine modes are included, replacing $\pi$ by $n\pi$ gives minimum $27n^4\pi^4/4$, so the displayed first vertical mode also gives the smallest steady threshold.

Physically, these [convection rolls](../../../../../../convection-roll.md) have axes along the imposed [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md). Their perturbations are independent of $y$, so $\partial_y\mathbf u=0$ in the [resistive induction equation](../../../../../../resistive-induction-equation.md) and no growing magnetic perturbation is generated. The flow does not bend the field; consequently [magnetic tension](../../../../../../magnetic-tension.md) cannot inhibit these rolls, however large the field strength. This conclusion concerns the requested steady disturbances.
