<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With negligible cold-dark-matter [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md), the [synchronous perfect-fluid density equation](../../../../../../synchronous-perfect-fluid-density-equation.md) gives $\delta_C'=-h'/2$. Differentiate and use the trace [Einstein equation](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md), taking the cold matter [sound speed](../../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) to vanish and its density to dominate:

$$
\delta_C''+\mathcal H\delta_C'=4\pi Ga^2\rho_C\delta_C.
$$

In a spatially flat [matter-dominated universe](../../../../../../matter-domination.md), $a\propto\tau^2$, $\mathcal H=2/\tau$, and the conformal [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) gives $4\pi Ga^2\rho_C=3\mathcal H^2/2=6/\tau^2$. Hence

$$
\delta_C''+\frac2\tau\delta_C'-\frac6{\tau^2}\delta_C=0.
$$

Put $\delta_C\propto\tau^s$. The [indicial equation](../../../../../../indicial-equation.md) is $s(s-1)+2s-6=(s-2)(s+3)=0$. These two independent modes span the solution space, so

$$
\boxed{\delta_C(\tau,x)=A(x)\tau^2+B(x)\tau^{-3}.}
$$

Equivalently the growing mode is proportional to $a$ and the decaying mode to $a^{-3/2}$. The coefficients are set by [initial data](../../../../../../initial-data-in-general-relativity.md). Since [synchronous gauge](../../../../../../synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md) has residual coordinate modes, interpreting the [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) physically requires fixing its residual freedom consistently, for example by the cold-matter [rest frame](../../../../../../rest-frame.md) and [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) [integration](../../../../../../integral.md) convention; the mode equation alone is not a gauge-invariant observable.
