<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use primes for conformal-time derivatives. The cold-dark-matter [Cosmological Euler equation in synchronous gauge](../../../../../../cosmological-euler-equation-in-synchronous-gauge.md) is $v_C'+\mathcal Hv_C=0$, so multiplying by $a$ gives $(av_C)'=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{v_C(\tau,x)=\frac{a(\tau_i)}{a(\tau)}v_C(\tau_i,x).}
$$

In an expanding universe $a$ increases, damping any initial [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) as $a^{-1}$. During [radiation domination](../../../../../../radiation-domination.md) $a\propto\tau$, so the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) scales as $\tau^{-1}$; during [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md) $a\propto\tau^2$, it scales as $\tau^{-2}$. This is the [nonrelativistic limit](../../../../../../nonrelativistic-limit.md) of the physical-momentum [redshift](../../../../../../redshift.md) derived in Q1. It concerns the bulk [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) in the synchronous coordinate frame, not a growing [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) sourced in another gauge. A cold-matter rest-frame choice also removes the residual synchronous time displacement relevant to that component. The decay statement assumes expanding evolution and the pressureless, linear approximation used by the equation.
