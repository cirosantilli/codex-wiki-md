<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) for $p+e^-\leftrightarrow H+\gamma$ implies $\mu_H=\mu_p+\mu_e$, because equilibrium [photons](../../../../../../photon.md) have zero [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md). Dividing the three nonrelativistic Maxwell-Boltzmann [number densities](../../../../../../number-density.md) therefore gives

$$
\frac{n_H}{n_pn_e}=\frac{g_H}{g_pg_e}\left(\frac{m_H}{m_pm_e}\right)^{3/2}\left(\frac{2\pi}T\right)^{3/2}\exp\!\left(\frac{m_p+m_e-m_H}T\right).
$$

The [Hydrogen binding energy](../../../../../../hydrogen-binding-energy.md) is $I=m_p+m_e-m_H=13.6\,\mathrm{eV}$ in [natural units](../../../../../../natural-units.md). For ground-state [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md), with hyperfine splittings unresolved, $g_H=4$ and $g_p=g_e=2$, so the degeneracy ratio is one. Equivalently one can omit nuclear-spin degeneracy consistently from both the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) and atom. Excited [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) levels are exponentially suppressed at [recombination temperatures](../../../../../../recombination-temperature.md). Also $m_H/m_p=1+O(m_e/m_p)$, so its effect in the prefactor is negligible. Thus

$$
\boxed{\frac{n_H}{n_pn_e}\simeq\left(\frac{2\pi}{m_eT}\right)^{3/2}e^{I/T}.}
$$

The sign of the exponential follows from the smaller [rest mass](../../../../../../invariant-mass.md) of the bound atom: cooling favours neutral [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) once translational [entropy](../../../../../../entropy.md) no longer compensates the binding [energy](../../../../../../energy.md).
