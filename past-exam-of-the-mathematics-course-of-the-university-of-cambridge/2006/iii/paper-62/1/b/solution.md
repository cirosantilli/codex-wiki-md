<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In proper-time gauge the particle [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) is $L=m(-\dot t^2+a^2\dot{\mathbf x}^{\,2}-1)/2$, now with dots referring to [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) $s$. The canonical spatial [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) is

$$
\boxed{p_i^C=\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot x^i}=ma^2\delta_{ij}\frac{dx^j}{ds}.}
$$

The [FRW metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) is independent of all spatial coordinates. Hence $\partial L/\partial x^i=0$ and the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives $dp_i^C/ds=0$. This [conserved quantity](../../../../../../conserved-quantity.md) is the [comoving momentum](../../../../../../comoving-momentum.md).

The mass-shell [mass shell](../../../../../../mass-shell.md) constraint gives $(dt/ds)^2-a^2|d\mathbf x/ds|^2=1$. For future-directed motion and proper [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) $\mathbf v=a\,d\mathbf x/dt$, it follows that $dt/ds=(1-v^2)^{-1/2}=\gamma$. Therefore

$$
\mathbf p^C=ma^2\gamma\frac{d\mathbf x}{dt}=a\gamma m\mathbf v=a\mathbf p^K,
\qquad \boxed{\mathbf p^K=\frac{\mathbf p^C}{a}.}
$$

The kinetic [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) here is the [physical momentum in an FRW universe](../../../../../../physical-momentum-in-an-frw-universe.md), measured in the [orthonormal frame](../../../../../../orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) of a [comoving observer](../../../../../../comoving-observer.md). It [redshifts](../../../../../../redshift.md) as $a^{-1}$ irrespective of whether the particle is relativistic or nonrelativistic.
