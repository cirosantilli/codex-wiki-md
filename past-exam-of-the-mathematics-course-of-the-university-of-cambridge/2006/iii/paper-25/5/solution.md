<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

All assertions of independence below are relative consistency assertions. We construct structures satisfying the other [ZF](../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) axioms but falsifying the indicated axiom; an ordinary model of [ZF](../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) supplies the positive side. The convenient ground theory is [ZFC](../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md), which has the same consistency strength as [ZF](../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) by the [constructible universe theorem](../../../../../constructible-universe-theorem.md).

For the [Axiom of union](../../../../../axiom-of-union.md), put $\lambda=\beth_\omega$ and use the [hereditarily locally small membership model](../../../../../hereditarily-locally-small-membership-model.md)

$$
B_\lambda=\{x:\forall y\in\operatorname{TC}(\{x\})\ (|y|<\lambda)\}.
$$

It is a [transitive class](../../../../../transitive-class.md). The condition requires each individual [set](../../../../../set-split.md) in the membership ancestry to have size below $\lambda$; it does not require its entire [transitive closure](../../../../../transitive-closure.md) to have size below $\lambda$. [Extensionality](../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md) and [foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) are inherited. Pairs and [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) of members remain in the class, giving [pairing](../../../../../axiom-of-pairing.md) and [separation](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md), and $\omega$ witnesses [infinity](../../../../../infinity.md). For $x\in B_\lambda$, the actual $\mathcal P(x)$ also belongs to $B_\lambda$: if $|x|=\mu<\lambda$, then $2^\mu<\lambda$ by the strong-limit property, while every [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of $x$ and every member further below it remains locally small. This verifies the full [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) axiom. If an internally definable functional relation has domain $x\in B_\lambda$, ground [replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md), applied to the relativized [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md), gives its range $r$. Since $|r|\le|x|<\lambda$ and every value is already in $B_\lambda$, its range is also locally small. Thus every [replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) instance holds.

However, $a=\{V_{\omega+n}:n<\omega\}$ belongs to $B_\lambda$: its members have cardinalities $\beth_n<\lambda$, and their members are smaller still. Its actual [union](../../../../../set-union.md) is $V_{\omega+\omega}$, of [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) $\beth_\omega=\lambda$, which is not in $B_\lambda$. Transitivity makes any internal [union](../../../../../set-union.md) witness equal to this actual [union](../../../../../set-union.md). Hence **[Union](../../../../../set-union.md) fails while all the other [ZF](../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) axioms hold.**

For the [Axiom of power set](../../../../../axiom-of-power-set.md), use the [hereditarily countable sets](../../../../../hereditarily-countable-set.md) $H_{\omega_1}$ in the ground choice universe. This is transitive and contains $\omega$. Pairs, [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) and [unions](../../../../../set-union.md) have [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) [transitive closure](../../../../../transitive-closure.md). For [replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md), a functional image of a [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) domain is [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md), and the [union](../../../../../set-union.md) of countably many [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) transitive closures is [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md); hence that image is again hereditarily [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md). These observations, with inherited [extensionality](../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md) and [foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md), verify all the remaining axioms. Every real, viewed as a [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of $\omega$, belongs to $H_{\omega_1}$. A power-set witness for $\omega$ would therefore contain every such real, but a hereditarily [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) [set](../../../../../set-split.md) has only countably many members, whereas $\mathcal P(\omega)$ is [uncountable](../../../../../uncountable-set.md) by [Cantor's theorem](../../../../../cantor-s-theorem.md). **[Power set](../../../../../power-set.md) fails.**

For the [Axiom schema of replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md), use $V_{\omega+\omega}$ with actual membership. This limit-rank structure contains $\omega$ and is closed under pairs, [unions](../../../../../set-union.md) and [power sets](../../../../../power-set.md): every relevant finite increase in rank stays below $\omega+\omega$. A separated [subset](../../../../../subset.md) has rank no higher than that of the original [set](../../../../../set-split.md). [Extensionality](../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md) and [foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) are inherited. The internally definable [function](../../../../../function-split.md)

$$
f:\omega\longrightarrow V_{\omega+\omega},\qquad f(n)=V_{\omega+n},
$$

is obtained by $n$ successive power-set operations starting with the parameter $V_\omega$. Each finite iteration, including its finite history, belongs to the structure. Its range has rank $\omega+\omega$ and is not an element of $V_{\omega+\omega}$. Thus the [replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) instance for this [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) fails, although every other [ZF](../../../../../zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) axiom holds.

For the [axiom of extensionality](../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md), take the well-founded cumulative [set](../../../../../set-split.md) universe over two distinct [urelements](../../../../../urelement.md), and forget the predicate distinguishing [urelements](../../../../../urelement.md) from [sets](../../../../../set-split.md). Each [urelement](../../../../../urelement.md) has no members, just as the ordinary [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md) has no members, so [extensionality](../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md) fails. Every other axiom survives in this pure membership language. [Pairing](../../../../../axiom-of-pairing.md), [union](../../../../../set-union.md), [separation](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md) and [replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) are witnessed by the corresponding ordinary [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) of [urelements](../../../../../urelement.md) and [sets](../../../../../set-split.md); [foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) follows from their well-founded ranks, and the pure $\omega$ supplies [infinity](../../../../../infinity.md). Here [foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md) is written with “has a member” as its nonemptiness antecedent, and disjointness means having no common member. Formulations using inequality to one designated empty object are not equivalent after removing [extensionality](../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md). There is also one necessary adjustment to [power set](../../../../../power-set.md): an [urelement](../../../../../urelement.md) is vacuously a [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of every object because it has no members. Thus the power-set witness for $x$ is the ordinary [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of all usual [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) of its extension, together with both [urelements](../../../../../urelement.md). This is still a [set](../../../../../set-split.md), and contains exactly the objects satisfying the unrestricted [subset](../../../../../subset.md) [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md). Consequently **the countermodel falsifies [Extensionality](../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md) alone**, without silently weakening [Power set](../../../../../power-set.md) to [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) only.

Finally, the [hereditarily finite sets](../../../../../hereditarily-finite-set.md) $V_\omega$ satisfy [extensionality](../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md), [foundation](../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md), [pairing](../../../../../axiom-of-pairing.md), [union](../../../../../set-union.md), [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) and [separation](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md). Every functional image of a finite domain is a [finite set](../../../../../finite-set.md) of hereditarily finite objects, hence also belongs to $V_\omega$, giving all [replacement](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-replacement.md) instances. No [finite set](../../../../../finite-set.md) contains $\varnothing$ and is closed under successor: it would then contain every finite von Neumann [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal.md). **[Infinity](../../../../../infinity.md) fails while the remaining axioms hold.**
