<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [projective completion](../../../../../../projective-completion.md) has identity $O=[0:1:0]$. For this [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](../../../../../../weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md), negation is

$$
-(x,y)=(x,-y-1).
$$

For distinct affine points $P,Q$, draw their chord; for $P=Q$, use the tangent with its [intersection multiplicity](../../../../../../intersection-multiplicity.md). If the third intersection is $R$, define $P\oplus Q=-R$. A vertical chord pairs $P$ with $-P$ and gives their sum $O$; also $P\oplus O=P$. A vertical tangent at a point satisfying $2y+1=0$ gives $2P=O$.

For a nonvertical line $y=mx+b$, substitution into the cubic gives

$$
x^3+(4-m^2)x^2+(-2-2mb-m)x-(b^2+b)=0.
$$

Thus if $P=(x_1,y_1)$ and $Q=(x_2,y_2)$, the sum has coordinates

$$
x_3=m^2-4-x_1-x_2,\qquad y_3=-mx_3-b-1.
$$

Here $m=(y_2-y_1)/(x_2-x_1)$ for a chord, and [implicit differentiation](../../../../../../implicit-differentiation.md) gives

$$
m=\frac{3x_1^2+8x_1-2}{2y_1+1}
$$

for a nonvertical tangent. These formulas include the repeated-root case. [Associativity](../../../../../../associative-property.md) can be seen without a lengthy coordinate calculation: a line cuts the cubic in a divisor equivalent to $3(O)$, and $P\mapsto[(P)-(O)]$ identifies the curve with its degree-zero [divisor class group](../../../../../../divisor-class-group.md). The chord-and-tangent operation becomes addition of [divisor classes](../../../../../../divisor-class.md), hence is associative.
