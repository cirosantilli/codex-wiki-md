<h1 id="2/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At the present epoch, define $s=1+z$ and $F(z)=\dot z/H_0$. For flat matter plus a [cosmological constant](../../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md), neglecting radiation,

$$
F(z)=s-E(z),\qquad E(z)=\frac{H(z)}{H_0}=\sqrt{\Omega_{m,0}s^3+\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}.
$$

In an [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), $E=s^{3/2}$, hence

$$
\boxed{F(z)=s-s^{3/2}<0\quad(z>0).}
$$

The [redshift drift](../../../../../../../redshift-drift.md) is zero at $z=0$, has initial slope $F'(0)=-1/2$, and decreases monotonically because $F'=1-3\sqrt s/2<0$. Its [second derivative](../../../../../../../second-derivative.md) is $-3/(4\sqrt s)<0$, so the sketch bends downward, with $F\sim-z^{3/2}$ at large positive [redshift](../../../../../../../redshift.md).
