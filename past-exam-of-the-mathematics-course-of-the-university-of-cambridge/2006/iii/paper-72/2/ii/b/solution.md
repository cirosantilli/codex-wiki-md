<h1 id="2/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A pure positive [cosmological constant](../../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) in a flat universe gives constant $H=H_0$, the expanding de Sitter model. Thus

$$
\boxed{\frac{\dot z}{H_0}=z.}
$$

The [redshift drift](../../../../../../../redshift-drift.md) is a [straight line](../../../../../../../straight-line.md) through the origin with positive slope. Here the source and observer expansion rates are equal; the factor $1+z$ in the observer contribution makes every positive-[redshift](../../../../../../../redshift.md) comoving source drift toward still larger [redshift](../../../../../../../redshift.md).
