↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For fixed [cosmological density parameters](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md), the [redshift drift](../../../../../../redshift-drift.md) has the form $\dot z=H_0F(z)$. Multiplying the [Hubble constant](../../../../../../hubble-constant.md) changes the vertical scale of all three sketches but leaves their zeros unchanged. Thus **the drift-zero [redshift](../../../../../../redshift.md) is independent of $H_0$ at fixed density fractions**. Every model has the trivial $z=0$ zero; only the specified mixed model has a further positive zero, $z_*\simeq2.089$. If instead physical matter and vacuum densities are held fixed while $H_0$ changes, their density fractions change too, so that is a different family of models.
