<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [cosmological principle](../../../../../../cosmological-principle.md) assumes statistical [spatial homogeneity](../../../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md) and [cosmological isotropy](../../../../../../cosmological-isotropy.md) on sufficiently large scales. [Spatial homogeneity](../../../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md) means that coarse-grained statistical properties do not depend on location; [cosmological isotropy](../../../../../../cosmological-isotropy.md) means that they do not depend on direction. The claim concerns a large-scale description, allowing individual [galaxies](../../../../../../galaxy-split.md), clusters and voids. It motivates the [FRW metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) and makes [cosmic expansion](../../../../../../expansion-of-the-universe.md) describable by a single [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) and a small number of matter variables.

Its adoption combines empirical large-scale regularity with the [Copernican principle](../../../../../../copernican-principle.md), the assumption that our location is typical. The resulting models provide a coherent account of [cosmic expansion](../../../../../../expansion-of-the-universe.md), the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background.md) and primordial abundances. Neither the [Einstein field equations](../../../../../../einstein-field-equations.md) alone nor [cosmological isotropy](../../../../../../cosmological-isotropy.md) around just one observer proves global [spatial homogeneity](../../../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md): a [spherically symmetric](../../../../../../spherical-symmetry.md) universe specially centred on that observer would also look isotropic. Observations of a finite [past light cone](../../../../../../past-light-cone.md) support the principle over sampled scales without establishing it beyond the observed region.

Three supporting observations probe complementary aspects. First, after the observer-motion dipole and local foregrounds are removed, the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background.md) is extremely isotropic, with intrinsic [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) fluctuations of order $10^{-5}$ and a nearly identical thermal spectrum across the sky. Second, suitably corrected all-sky counts of distant radio sources and extragalactic background intensities show no large preferred direction; Galactic obscuration and survey selection must be accounted for. Third, three-dimensional [galaxy](../../../../../../galaxy-split.md) [redshift](../../../../../../redshift.md) surveys show that clustering and voids average toward statistically similar distributions in sufficiently large regions: large-volume number counts approach volume scaling and correlations weaken on large separation scales. The first two primarily test [cosmological isotropy](../../../../../../cosmological-isotropy.md), while the third adds evidence for large-scale [spatial homogeneity](../../../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md). **The principle is a well-supported large-scale working hypothesis whose observational and typicality assumptions remain explicit.**
