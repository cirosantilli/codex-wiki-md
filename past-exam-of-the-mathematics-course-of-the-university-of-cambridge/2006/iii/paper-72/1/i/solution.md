<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md), the [Friedmann acceleration equation](../../../../../../friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) with vanishing [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) is

$$
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}\rho_m.
$$

Evaluating at the present [cosmic time](../../../../../../cosmic-time.md) and using the definition of the [deceleration parameter](../../../../../../deceleration-parameter.md) gives

$$
q_0=\frac{4\pi G\rho_{m,0}}{3H_0^2}
=\frac12\frac{\rho_{m,0}}{3H_0^2/(8\pi G)}.
$$

The denominator is the [critical density](../../../../../../critical-density.md), so the [cosmological density parameter](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md) satisfies **the dust relation**

$$
\boxed{\Omega_{m,0}=2q_0.}
$$

This uses [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) and $\Lambda=0$; spatial flatness has not been assumed.
