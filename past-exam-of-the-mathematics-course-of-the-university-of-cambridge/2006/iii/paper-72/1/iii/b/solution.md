<h1 id="1/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $q_0=1/2$, the expanding [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) reduces to $\dot y=H_0y^{-1/2}$. Separating variables and placing the [Big Bang](../../../../../../../big-bang.md) at $t=0$ gives

$$
H_0t=\int_0^y u^{1/2}\,du=\frac23y^{3/2}.
$$

Thus the flat pressureless [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) has

$$
\boxed{H_0t=\frac23\left(\frac a{a_0}\right)^{3/2},\qquad
\frac a{a_0}=\left(\frac32H_0t\right)^{2/3}.}
$$

In particular its present age is $2/(3H_0)$.
