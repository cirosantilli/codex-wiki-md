<h1 id="3/iv/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $r=|\boldsymbol\theta|$ and write the vector [thin gravitational lens equation](../../../../../../../thin-gravitational-lens-equation.md) as $\boldsymbol\beta=(1-\bar\kappa(r))\boldsymbol\theta$. A tangential displacement at fixed radius has [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_t=1-\bar\kappa$. A radial displacement has [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_r=1-\alpha'(r)$. From the definition of the mean [lensing convergence](../../../../../../../lensing-convergence.md),

$$
r^2\bar\kappa(r)=2\int_0^r\kappa(s)s\,ds,
\qquad 2\bar\kappa+r\bar\kappa'=2\kappa.
$$

Since $\alpha=r\bar\kappa$, this gives $\alpha'=2\kappa-\bar\kappa$. Consequently,

$$
\det A=(1-\bar\kappa)(1-2\kappa+\bar\kappa).
$$

The two requested [gravitational-lensing critical curve](../../../../../../../gravitational-lensing-critical-curve.md) conditions are therefore

$$
\boxed{\bar\kappa=1\quad\text{or}\quad 2\kappa-\bar\kappa=1.}
$$

They correspond respectively to a [tangential critical curve of an axisymmetric lens](../../../../../../../tangential-critical-curve-of-an-axisymmetric-lens.md) and a [radial critical curve of an axisymmetric lens](../../../../../../../radial-critical-curve-of-an-axisymmetric-lens.md).
