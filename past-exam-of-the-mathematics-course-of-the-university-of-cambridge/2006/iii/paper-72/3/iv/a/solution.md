<h1 id="3/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

It is useful to rewrite the local [lensing convergence](../../../../../../../lensing-convergence.md) as

$$
\kappa(\theta)=\theta_E\frac{\theta_c^2+\theta^2/2}{(\theta^2+\theta_c^2)^{3/2}}.
$$

For positive core radius, the requested limits are

$$
\boxed{\kappa(\theta)\sim\frac{\theta_E}{2|\theta|}\longrightarrow0
\quad(|\theta|\to\infty),\qquad
\kappa(0)=\frac{\theta_E}{\theta_c}<\infty.}
$$

A [singular isothermal sphere lens](../../../../../../../singular-isothermal-sphere-lens.md) instead has $\kappa(\theta)=\theta_E/(2|\theta|)$, which diverges at the centre. The finite core of the [softened isothermal lensing potential](../../../../../../../softened-isothermal-lensing-potential.md) regularizes this central [lensing convergence](../../../../../../../lensing-convergence.md); at large angular radius the two models have the same leading profile.
