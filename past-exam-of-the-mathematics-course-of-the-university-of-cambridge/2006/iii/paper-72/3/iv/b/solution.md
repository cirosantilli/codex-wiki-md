<h1 id="3/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an axisymmetric [gravitational lens](../../../../../../../gravitational-lens.md), the reduced deflection is $\alpha(\theta)=\bar\kappa(\theta)\theta$. The specified mean [lensing convergence](../../../../../../../lensing-convergence.md) therefore gives

$$
\beta(\theta)=\theta\left(1-\frac{\theta_E}{\sqrt{\theta^2+\theta_c^2}}\right),\qquad
\beta'(\theta)=1-\frac{\theta_E\theta_c^2}{(\theta^2+\theta_c^2)^{3/2}}.
$$

The [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) is smallest at the centre, where $\beta'(0)=1-\theta_E/\theta_c$. If $\theta_c\geq\theta_E$, the mapping is monotone and an offset source has only one image. If $0<\theta_c<\theta_E$, the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) is negative near zero and positive at large radius, producing two [stationary points](../../../../../../../stationary-point.md) and a range of source offsets with three images. Thus **the condition for a nonempty multiple-image region is**

$$
\boxed{0<\theta_c<\theta_E,\quad\text{equivalently }\kappa(0)>1.}
$$

This condition permits multiple images; a particular source must additionally lie inside the [multiple-image caustic of a softened isothermal lensing potential](../../../../../../../multiple-image-caustic-of-a-softened-isothermal-lensing-potential.md). At equality the critical radii collapse to the origin and there is no finite three-image region.
