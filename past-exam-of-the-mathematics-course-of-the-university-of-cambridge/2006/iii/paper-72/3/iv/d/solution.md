<h1 id="3/iv/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The tangential condition $\bar\kappa=1$ gives $\sqrt{\theta_t^2+\theta_c^2}=\theta_E$, hence

$$
\boxed{\theta_t=\sqrt{\theta_E^2-\theta_c^2}.}
$$

This is a nonzero [gravitational-lensing critical curve](../../../../../../../gravitational-lensing-critical-curve.md) precisely when $\theta_E>\theta_c$. It is also the radius of the [Einstein ring](../../../../../../../einstein-ring.md) for an aligned source in this cored model, so the strength parameter $\theta_E$ itself is not the cored model's ring radius.

For completeness the radial condition is equally explicit. Subtracting the mean [lensing convergence](../../../../../../../lensing-convergence.md) from twice the local one gives

$$
2\kappa-\bar\kappa=\frac{\theta_E\theta_c^2}{(\theta^2+\theta_c^2)^{3/2}}.
$$

Thus the [radial critical curve of an axisymmetric lens](../../../../../../../radial-critical-curve-of-an-axisymmetric-lens.md) has radius

$$
\boxed{\theta_r=\sqrt{(\theta_E\theta_c^2)^{2/3}-\theta_c^2},}
$$

again nonzero exactly when $\theta_E>\theta_c$.
