**False in general.** On $[5]$ take the rank-two star

$$
\mathcal A=\{12,13,14,15\}.
$$

It is an [intersecting family](../../../../../../intersecting-family.md) of four sets and $r=2\le5/2$. The first four rank-two sets in [colexicographic order](../../../../../../colexicographic-order.md) are

$$
\{12,13,23,14\}.
$$

The members $23$ and $14$ are disjoint. Thus [colexicographic replacement can destroy intersection](../../../../../../colexicographic-replacement-can-destroy-intersection.md), despite its shadow-minimizing property.
