<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The reverse-oriented [Complex projective plane](../../../../../../complex-projective-plane.md) has [intersection form](../../../../../../intersection-form.md) $(-1)$, so $b^+=0$, while $b_1=0$. The [Hodge star](../../../../../../hodge-star-operator.md) splits harmonic two-forms into self-dual and anti-self-dual subspaces, and their dimensions are the positive and negative indices of the [intersection form](../../../../../../intersection-form.md). Therefore every harmonic two-form is an [anti-self-dual two-form](../../../../../../anti-self-dual-differential-form.md) for every chosen metric.

By part (b), every [complex line bundle](../../../../../../complex-line-bundle.md) has a [unitary connection](../../../../../../unitary-connection.md) with harmonic real [vector-bundle curvature](../../../../../../curvature-form.md), so this connection is an [ASD connection](../../../../../../anti-self-dual-connection.md). Conversely, an [ASD connection](../../../../../../anti-self-dual-connection.md) on a [complex line bundle](../../../../../../complex-line-bundle.md) has $dF=0$ and $d*F=-dF=0$, so its [vector-bundle curvature](../../../../../../curvature-form.md) is harmonic. The uniqueness in part (b) therefore applies to all [ASD connections](../../../../../../anti-self-dual-connection.md) on that fixed [complex line bundle](../../../../../../complex-line-bundle.md). **Each [complex line bundle](../../../../../../complex-line-bundle.md) has exactly one gauge orbit of ASD connections.**
