<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Since the observed times are ordered, the final [risk set](../../../../../../risk-set.md) is $R_m=\{m\}$. Its [Cox partial likelihood](../../../../../../cox-partial-likelihood.md) factor is therefore

$$
\left(\frac{e^{\beta^Tz_m}}{e^{\beta^Tz_m}}\right)^{v_m}=1
$$

for either value of the last event indicator. Every earlier [risk set](../../../../../../risk-set.md) and earlier event factor is unaffected by changing $v_m$. Thus **the entire [partial likelihood](../../../../../../partial-likelihood.md) as a function of $\beta$ is unchanged**, not merely its value at one fitted coefficient. Its maximizing set, its [score function](../../../../../../informant-function.md) and its [Observed Fisher information](../../../../../../observed-fisher-information.md) are identical. In particular, any consistently chosen finite fitted $\widehat\beta$ is unchanged. The lone terminal event conveys no relative-hazard information because there is no other individual with whom to compare it.
