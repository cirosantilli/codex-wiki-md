<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Use the real three-dimensional space of traceless [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
X=\begin{pmatrix}x&t+y\\-t+y&-x\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det X=t^2-x^2-y^2.
$$

Thus $-\det X$ is a quadratic form of signature $(2,1)$. The adjoint action $X\mapsto AXA^{-1}$ of $SL(2,\mathbb R)$ preserves trace and [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md). Connectedness puts its image in $SO_0(2,1)$.

A kernel element commutes with every [traceless matrix](../../../../../../traceless-matrix.md) and with the identity, hence with every real [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md); it is therefore scalar. [Determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) one leaves $\pm I$. The derivative is injective and both groups have dimension three, so the image is open and equals the [identity component](../../../../../../identity-component.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{SO_0(2,1)\cong SL(2,\mathbb R)/\{\pm I\}.}
$$
