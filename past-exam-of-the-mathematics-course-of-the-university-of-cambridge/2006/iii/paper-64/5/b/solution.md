<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Identify a Minkowski vector $(t,x,y,z)$ with the [Hermitian matrix representation of Minkowski four-vectors](../../../../../../hermitian-matrix-representation-of-minkowski-four-vectors.md)

$$
X=\begin{pmatrix}t+z&x-iy\\x+iy&t-z\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\det X=t^2-x^2-y^2-z^2.
$$

For $A\in SL(2,\mathbb C)$, the real-linear action $X\mapsto AXA^\dagger$ preserves Hermiticity and the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md), so it preserves the Lorentz quadratic form. It also preserves the cone of positive-definite [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), hence the future timelike cone. The group $SL(2,\mathbb C)$ is connected, by [polar decomposition of an invertible complex matrix](../../../../../../polar-decomposition-of-an-invertible-complex-matrix.md) into $SU(2)$ and positive determinant-one [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). Thus its image lies in the [Proper orthochronous Lorentz group](../../../../../../proper-orthochronous-lorentz-group.md).

If $AXA^\dagger=X$ for every Hermitian $X$, taking $X=I$ makes $A$ unitary; the remaining equations then say it commutes with every [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). Their real span is all [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and their complex span is all complex [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md), so $A$ is scalar. The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) condition leaves precisely $A=\pm I$. A discrete kernel gives an injective differential, and the real dimensions of both groups are six. The image is therefore open and equals the connected [Lorentz group](../../../../../../lorentz-group.md):

$$
\boxed{SO_0(3,1)\cong SL(2,\mathbb C)/\{\pm I\}.}
$$

The quotient does not include the disconnected time-reversing component of $SO(3,1)$.
