<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Conjugation $\alpha_g(x)=gxg^{-1}$ is an [automorphism](../../../../../../automorphism.md) of $G$ sending $A$ to $gAg^{-1}$. It induces the bijection

$$
xA\longmapsto(gxg^{-1})(gAg^{-1})
$$

between their left [coset](../../../../../../coset.md) sets; conjugation by $g^{-1}$ gives the inverse. Therefore

$$
\boxed{[G:gAg^{-1}]=[G:A]=i.}
$$

This holds whether or not $A$ is normal, and whether the index is finite or infinite.
