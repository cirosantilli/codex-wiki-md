<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Construct the [tensor-product weight diagram](../../../../../../tensor-product-weight-diagram.md) by placing one octet diagram at each triplet [representation weight](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) $u,d,s$ and adding multiplicities where the shifted diagrams overlap. Its [highest weight](../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) is

$$
u+\alpha_3=\left(1,\frac2{\sqrt3}\right)=2\omega_1+\omega_2,
$$

so remove the $(2,1)$ diagram by [highest-weight character subtraction](../../../../../../highest-weight-character-subtraction.md). Its unequal-sided hexagon has nine boundary [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) of multiplicity one and three interior [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) of multiplicity two, giving [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $15$.

To see the remainder explicitly, measure diagram coordinates by $(a,b)=(2h_1,2\sqrt3h_2)$. After subtracting this $\mathbf{15}$, the residual [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) are

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
(a,b)&(0,4)&(-2,-2)&(2,-2)&(1,1)&(-1,1)&(0,-2)\\ \hline
\text{multiplicity}&1&1&1&2&2&2
\end{array}
$$

The next [highest weight](../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) is $(h_1,h_2)=(0,2/\sqrt3)=2\omega_2$, with [Dynkin labels](../../../../../../dynkin-label.md) $(0,2)$. The conjugate sextet $\overline{\mathbf6}$ is a triangular [weight diagram](../../../../../../weight-diagram.md) containing each of these six [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) once. Subtracting it leaves $(1,1),(-1,1),(0,-2)$ once each in the integer coordinates: precisely the triplet. The [SU(3) triplet-octet decomposition](../../../../../../su-3-triplet-octet-decomposition.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf3\otimes\mathbf8
=\mathbf{15}_{(2,1)}\oplus\overline{\mathbf6}_{(0,2)}
\oplus\mathbf3_{(1,0)}.}
$$

The dimensions add to $15+6+3=24$, and the figure below verifies the equality at every [representation weight](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md), including overlapping interior [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md).
