<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In a [tensor product of Lie algebra representations](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md), the Cartan generators act as sums on the tensor factors. Thus the [tensor-product weight diagram](../../../../../../tensor-product-weight-diagram.md) is formed by adding [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md), counting all ordered choices; multiplicities convolve.

For three triplets, the three vertices $3u,3d,3s$ each arise once. Each of the six [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) $2u+d$, $2u+s$, $2d+u$, $2d+s$, $2s+u$, $2s+d$ arises in three orders. The central [representation weight](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) $u+d+s=0$ arises in six orders. The resulting [weight diagram](../../../../../../weight-diagram.md) has total [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $3+6\cdot3+6=27$.

Its maximal [highest weight](../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) is $3u=3\omega_1$, with [Dynkin labels](../../../../../../dynkin-label.md) $(3,0)$. The corresponding triangular decuplet diagram has precisely these ten locations, all of [weight multiplicity](../../../../../../weight-multiplicity.md) one. Subtract it by [highest-weight character subtraction](../../../../../../highest-weight-character-subtraction.md). The six remaining nonzero [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) each have multiplicity two and are exactly the six octet [root](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md), while the origin has multiplicity five. Two octets account for multiplicity two on each [root](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) and multiplicity four at the origin. The remaining origin is one singlet. Therefore the [tensor cube of the defining SU(3) representation](../../../../../../tensor-cube-of-the-defining-su-3-representation.md) decomposes as

$$
\boxed{\mathbf3\otimes\mathbf3\otimes\mathbf3
=\mathbf{10}_{(3,0)}\oplus
\mathbf8_{(1,1)}\oplus\mathbf8_{(1,1)}\oplus\mathbf1_{(0,0)}.}
$$

The [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) check is $10+8+8+1=27$. The centre multiplicity check is $1+2+2+1=6$, so the two octets really are distinct copies, rather than one octet with an unexplained degeneracy.
