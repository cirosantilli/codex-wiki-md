<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A fundamental matrix realization compatible with the [root](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) normalization is

$$
H_1=\frac12\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0),\qquad
H_2=\frac1{2\sqrt3}\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2).
$$

The [weights of a representation](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) on the coordinate states $u,d,s$ are therefore

$$
\boxed{u=\left(\frac12,\frac1{2\sqrt3}\right),\qquad
d=\left(-\frac12,\frac1{2\sqrt3}\right),\qquad
s=\left(0,-\frac1{\sqrt3}\right).}
$$

Each has [weight multiplicity](../../../../../../weight-multiplicity.md) one. The [weight diagram of the defining SU(3) representation](../../../../../../weight-diagram-of-the-defining-su-3-representation.md) is the corresponding equilateral triangle. These are $(H_1,H_2)$ coordinates; the familiar vertical [flavor hypercharge](../../../../../../flavor-hypercharge.md) coordinate is $Y=2H_2/\sqrt3$.

For the [adjoint representation of SU(3)](../../../../../../adjoint-representation-of-su-3.md), the states are elements of the [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md). The two Cartan generators commute with both $H_i$, giving [representation weight](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) $(0,0)$ with multiplicity two. Each [root](../../../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) generator $E_\pm^r$ is an eigenvector of the Cartan adjoint action with [representation weight](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) $\pm\alpha_r$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\text{adjoint weights: }\,
\pm(1,0),\quad\pm(-1/2,\sqrt3/2),\quad
\pm(1/2,\sqrt3/2),\quad (0,0)\text{ twice}.}
$$

The six nonzero [representation weights](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) form a regular hexagon, with a doubly occupied centre. Its [highest weight](../../../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) is $\alpha_3=\omega_1+\omega_2$, so its [Dynkin labels](../../../../../../dynkin-label.md) are $(1,1)$ and its [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is eight. The triplet and octet panels in the preceding figure give these two [weight diagrams](../../../../../../weight-diagram.md) explicitly.
