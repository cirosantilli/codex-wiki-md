<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [Jacobson radical](../../../../../jacobson-radical.md) is $J(R)=\bigcap_L L$, where $L$ runs over the [maximal right ideals](../../../../../maximal-right-ideal.md). Every proper [right ideal](../../../../../right-ideal.md) $I$ lies in such an $L$: the union of a chain of proper [right ideals](../../../../../right-ideal.md) containing $I$ is a [right ideal](../../../../../right-ideal.md) and remains proper because it omits $1$. Thus [Zorn lemma](../../../../../zorn-s-lemma.md) applies.

The right-module form of [Nakayama lemma](../../../../../nakayama-lemma.md) says that a [finitely generated module](../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) $M$ with $MJ=M$ is zero. More generally, if $N$ is a [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) and $M=N+MJ$, then $M=N$. To prove the first statement, suppose $M\ne0$ and take a generating set $m_1,\ldots,m_s$ of minimal size. Since $MJ=M$, there are $a_i\in J$ with

$$
m_s=\sum_{i=1}^s m_i a_i,\qquad
m_s(1-a_s)=\sum_{i<s}m_i a_i.
$$

The [unit criterion for the Jacobson radical](../../../../../unit-criterion-for-the-jacobson-radical.md) makes $1-a_s$ a [unit](../../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md). Hence $m_s$ is generated by the other $m_i$, a contradiction. Apply this result to the [quotient module](../../../../../quotient-module.md) $M/N$ to obtain the general form.

For an [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md) [module endomorphism](../../../../../module-endomorphism.md) $f$ of an [Artinian module](../../../../../artinian-module.md) $M$, the [descending chain condition](../../../../../descending-chain-condition.md) gives

$$
f^n(M)=f^{n+1}(M)
$$

for some $n$. Given $x\in M$, write $f^n(x)=f^{n+1}(y)$; injectivity of $f^n$ gives $x=f(y)$. Thus $f$ is [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md), as asserted by [Artinian modules are co-Hopfian](../../../../../artinian-modules-are-co-hopfian.md).

Now $R$ is a [semilocal ring](../../../../../semi-local-ring.md) and $\overline V=V/VJ$ is a [finitely generated module](../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) over the [right Artinian ring](../../../../../right-artinian-ring.md) $\overline R=R/J$. A finite [direct sum](../../../../../direct-sum.md) of copies of $\overline R$, and hence its [quotient module](../../../../../quotient-module.md) $\overline V$, is an [Artinian module](../../../../../artinian-module.md). The [module endomorphism](../../../../../module-endomorphism.md) $\alpha$ induces $\overline\alpha$ on $\overline V$, and the given inverse-image condition says precisely that $\ker\overline\alpha=0$. The preceding argument makes $\overline\alpha$ [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md), so

$$
V=\alpha(V)+VJ.
$$

The [quotient module](../../../../../quotient-module.md) $W=V/\alpha(V)$ is finitely generated and satisfies $WJ=W$. Applying [Nakayama lemma](../../../../../nakayama-lemma.md) yields $W=0$. Therefore **$\alpha$ is surjective**, by [surjectivity from injectivity modulo the Jacobson radical](../../../../../surjectivity-from-injectivity-modulo-the-jacobson-radical.md).
