<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [connected correlation function](../../../../../../connected-correlation-function.md) $G(x)=\langle\phi(x)\phi(0)\rangle-\langle\phi\rangle^2$, so that the constant ordered contribution is removed. Away from a [thermodynamic critical point](../../../../../../thermodynamic-critical-point.md) its long-distance decay defines the [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md): $-\lim_{R\to\infty}R^{-1}\log|G(R)|=\xi^{-1}$ when this limit exists. In a massive rotation-invariant phase the [exponential decay](../../../../../../exponential-decay.md) is typically multiplied by an algebraic factor.

Expand the [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md) free energy about a stable uniform minimum $M$, writing $\phi=M+\delta\phi$. To quadratic order,

$$
\mathcal F_2=\frac12\int d^Dx\{\kappa|\nabla\delta\phi|^2+c(\delta\phi)^2\},\qquad c=V''(M)=r+3uM^2+5vM^4>0.
$$

integration of the [Gaussian field theory](../../../../../../gaussian-field-theory.md) gives the Fourier-space [connected correlation function](../../../../../../connected-correlation-function.md) $\widetilde G(q)=k_BT/(c+\kappa q^2)$. Thus the [Ornstein--Zernike correlation function](../../../../../../ornstein-zernike-correlation-function.md) has

$$
\boxed{\xi=\sqrt{\kappa/c}.}
$$

For an ordinary quartic transition, $M=0$ above the transition and $M^2=-r/u$ below, so $c=r$ and $c=-2r$, respectively. The [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) diverges as $|T-T_c|^{-1/2}$ on both sides, with different amplitudes. At criticality the mass vanishes and the Gaussian [correlation function](../../../../../../correlation-function.md) is proportional to $R^{-(D-2)}$ for $D>2$. Interactions can change this power to $R^{-(D-2+\eta)}$.
