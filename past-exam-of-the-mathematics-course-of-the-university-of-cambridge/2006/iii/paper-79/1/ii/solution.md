<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Local isotropy of turbulence](../../../../../../local-isotropy-of-turbulence.md) means that joint small-scale [velocity increment](../../../../../../velocity-increment.md) statistics are approximately unchanged by [rotations](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), even when the large-scale flow is anisotropic. The [universal equilibrium range](../../../../../../equilibrium-range.md) contains separations much smaller than the [integral scale of turbulence](../../../../../../integral-scale-of-turbulence.md): their adjustment times are short compared with the outer [eddy turnover time](../../../../../../eddy-turnover-time.md), and their normalized statistics are assumed independent of the detailed large-scale forcing. It includes the [inertial range](../../../../../../inertial-range.md) and the [dissipation range](../../../../../../dissipation-range.md).

The [Kolmogorov first similarity hypothesis](../../../../../../kolmogorov-first-similarity-hypothesis.md) says that these locally isotropic statistics depend only on $r$, mean [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) $\epsilon$ and [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu$. [Dimensional analysis](../../../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) therefore expresses the second-order [longitudinal structure function](../../../../../../longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md) in terms of the [Kolmogorov microscales](../../../../../../kolmogorov-microscales.md):

$$
\boxed{S_2(r)=\langle(\Delta v)^2\rangle=v^2F(r/\eta),\qquad r\ll\ell}.
$$

The universality of $F$ is an assumption of the theory, not a consequence of [dimensional analysis](../../../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) alone.

The [Kolmogorov second similarity hypothesis](../../../../../../kolmogorov-second-similarity-hypothesis.md) removes dependence on [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) when $\eta\ll r\ll\ell$. Only $\epsilon$ and $r$ remain, so the [Kolmogorov two-thirds law](../../../../../../kolmogorov-two-thirds-law.md) is

$$
\boxed{S_2(r)=\beta(\epsilon r)^{2/3}}.
$$

The same reasoning gives the predicted order-$P$ [longitudinal structure function](../../../../../../longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md):

$$
\boxed{\langle(\Delta v)^P\rangle=\beta_P(\epsilon r)^{P/3}}.
$$

These are signed [moments](../../../../../../moment.md) for integer $P$; for noninteger orders, use $\langle|\Delta v|^P\rangle$. In particular, the signed third-order coefficient is negative, $\beta_3=-4/5$, in the [Kolmogorov four-fifths law](../../../../../../kolmogorov-four-fifths-law.md).
