<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [zeroth law of turbulence](../../../../../../zeroth-law-of-turbulence.md) states that at fixed outer [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $u$ and [integral scale of turbulence](../../../../../../integral-scale-of-turbulence.md) $\ell$, the mean [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) per unit mass approaches a nonzero finite value as the [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md) $\mathrm{Re}=u\ell/\nu$ becomes large. In an established forward [energy cascade](../../../../../../energy-cascade.md), this gives $\epsilon\sim C_\epsilon u^3/\ell$, with $C_\epsilon$ of order one. The small-scale [velocity gradients](../../../../../../velocity-gradient.md) become large enough to offset decreasing [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md); this is the [turbulent dissipation anomaly](../../../../../../turbulent-dissipation-anomaly.md).

At the [Kolmogorov microscales](../../../../../../kolmogorov-microscales.md), the local [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md) is of order one, $v\eta/\nu\sim1$, and the [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) is $\epsilon\sim\nu(v/\eta)^2$. Solving these balances gives

$$
\boxed{\eta=(\nu^3/\epsilon)^{1/4},\qquad v=(\nu\epsilon)^{1/4}}.
$$

Substituting the outer-scale estimate, and omitting order-one constants, gives

$$
\boxed{\eta\sim\ell\,\mathrm{Re}^{-3/4},\qquad v\sim u\,\mathrm{Re}^{-1/4}}.
$$

The associated [eddy turnover time](../../../../../../eddy-turnover-time.md) is $\eta/v=(\nu/\epsilon)^{1/2}$.
