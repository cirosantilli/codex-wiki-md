<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

[External intermittency](../../../../../../external-intermittency.md) is the alternation of turbulent and non-turbulent fluid at a point as a fluctuating interface passes it. [Internal intermittency](../../../../../../internal-intermittency.md) in the [equilibrium range](../../../../../../equilibrium-range.md) instead describes uneven, bursty concentrations of small-scale [velocity gradients](../../../../../../velocity-gradient.md) and [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) within turbulent fluid. [Integral-scale intermittency](../../../../../../integral-scale-intermittency.md) is modulation of the energy-containing motions and their energy supply. It is non-universal because it retains information about forcing, boundary conditions and the history of the large-scale flow.

The [Landau intermittency counterexample](../../../../../../landau-intermittency-counterexample.md) challenges the unconditional universality asserted by [Kolmogorov 1941 theory](../../../../../../kolmogorov-1941-theory.md). Suppose turbulent populations have different conditional mean [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) rates $\epsilon_a$. Even if each obeys the same conditional scaling, averaging over populations gives

$$
S_P=\beta_Pr^{P/3}\langle\epsilon_a^{P/3}\rangle,
$$

which generally differs from $\beta_Pr^{P/3}\langle\epsilon_a\rangle^{P/3}$. Thus a coefficient normalized by the global mean depends on the large-scale intensity distribution. The third-order case escapes this particular objection because the power of $\epsilon_a$ is one.

The [Kolmogorov refined similarity hypothesis](../../../../../../kolmogorov-refined-similarity-hypothesis.md) replaces global mean [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) by [coarse-grained energy dissipation](../../../../../../coarse-grained-energy-dissipation.md) $\epsilon_{\mathrm{AV}}(r)$. It assumes that

$$
V=\frac{\Delta v}{[r\epsilon_{\mathrm{AV}}(r)]^{1/3}}
$$

has universal conditional statistics at sufficiently large local [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md). If the conditional $P$th [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) is $\beta_P$, the [law of total expectation](../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\langle(\Delta v)^P\rangle=\beta_P\langle\epsilon_{\mathrm{AV}}(r)^{P/3}\rangle r^{P/3}}.
$$

The [refined similarity](../../../../../../kolmogorov-refined-similarity-hypothesis.md) hypothesis does not itself prescribe the distribution of [coarse-grained energy dissipation](../../../../../../coarse-grained-energy-dissipation.md). Scale dependence of those [moments](../../../../../../moment.md) can change the predicted exponents. As before, noninteger orders require absolute [velocity increments](../../../../../../velocity-increment.md).
