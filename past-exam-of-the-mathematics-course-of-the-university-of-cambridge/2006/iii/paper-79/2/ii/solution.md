<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [principal strain rates](../../../../../../principal-strain-rates.md) $a,b,c$ are the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the symmetric [strain-rate tensor](../../../../../../strain-rate-tensor.md). [Incompressibility](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) makes its [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) zero, so $a+b+c=0$. The polynomial identity

$$
a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc
=(a+b+c)(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca)
$$

therefore gives $a^3+b^3+c^3=3abc$. Since the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of $S^3$ is the sum of the cubes of its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), the [Betchov relation](../../../../../../betchov-relation.md) becomes

$$
3\langle abc\rangle=-\frac34\langle\omega_i\omega_jS_{ij}\rangle,
\qquad
\boxed{\langle abc\rangle=-\frac14\langle\omega_i\omega_jS_{ij}\rangle}.
$$
