<h1 id="4/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Relative vertical [vorticity](../../../../../../vorticity.md) is the horizontal [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) of the [streamfunction](../../../../../../stream-function.md), not its full stretched three-dimensional [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md). Inside the [spherical potential-vorticity anomaly](../../../../../../spherical-potential-vorticity-anomaly.md), the quadratic solution gives

$$
\boxed{q_g=\psi_{xx}+\psi_{yy}=2Q_0/3>0.}
$$

Outside, in the equatorial plane $Z=0$, let $s=(x^2+y^2)^{1/2}>R$ and $C=Q_0R^3/3$. The azimuthal [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is $v_\theta=\psi_s=C/s^2$, so

$$
\boxed{q_g=\frac1s\frac d{ds}(s v_\theta)=-\frac{C}{s^3}=-\frac{Q_0R^3}{3s^3}<0.}
$$

The opposite signs thus follow directly from solid-body rotation inside and the exterior [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md)'s rapid radial decay. The azimuthal [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) itself has the same rotational sense on both sides; its radial derivative makes the exterior [relative vorticity](../../../../../../relative-vorticity.md) negative.

There is no contradiction with the zero exterior PV anomaly. At $Z=0$ the exterior solution has $\psi_{ZZ}=C/s^3$, so $q_g+\psi_{ZZ}=0$. The [stratification](../../../../../../density-stratification.md)/stretching contribution compensates the horizontal [relative vorticity](../../../../../../relative-vorticity.md). Inside it contributes $Q_0/3$, supplementing $2Q_0/3$ to give the prescribed excess $Q_0$.
