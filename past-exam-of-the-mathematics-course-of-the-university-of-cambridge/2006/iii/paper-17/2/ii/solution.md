<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [direct image sheaf](../../../../../../direct-image-sheaf.md) is defined by

$$
\boxed{(\phi_*\mathcal F)(U)=\mathcal F(\phi^{-1}U)}.
$$

Its restrictions are the restrictions of $\mathcal F$. Inverse images preserve covers and intersections, so the [sheaf gluing axiom](../../../../../../sheaf-gluing-axiom.md) holds immediately. The [morphism of ringed spaces](../../../../../../morphism-of-ringed-spaces.md) supplies

$$
\phi^\#:\mathcal O_X(U)\longrightarrow\mathcal O_Y(\phi^{-1}U).
$$

An element of $\mathcal O_X(U)$ acts on the displayed sections through this ring map and the original $\mathcal O_Y$-module action. This makes the [direct image sheaf](../../../../../../direct-image-sheaf.md) an $\mathcal O_X$-module; no additional [sheafification](../../../../../../sheafification.md) is required.
