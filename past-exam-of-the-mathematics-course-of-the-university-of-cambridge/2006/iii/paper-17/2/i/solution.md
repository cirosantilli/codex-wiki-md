<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Form the [presheaf](../../../../../../presheaf-of-sets-on-a-topological-space.md) whose value on $U\subseteq X$ is

$$
\mathcal G(U)\otimes_{\mathcal O_X(U)}\mathcal H(U),
$$

with restrictions induced from those of the two [sheaves of modules](../../../../../../sheaf-of-modules.md) and the [structure sheaf](../../../../../../structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md). Its [sheafification](../../../../../../sheafification.md) is the [tensor product of sheaves](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-sheaves.md) $\mathcal G\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal H$. Sheafification is needed because taking a [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md) on each [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) need not itself satisfy the gluing axiom. The resulting [sheaf](../../../../../../sheaf-mathematics.md) represents bilinear [sheaf](../../../../../../sheaf-mathematics.md) morphisms, and [localization](../../../../../../localization-of-a-ring.md) gives the [stalk](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) formula

$$
\boxed{(\mathcal G\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal H)_P
\cong\mathcal G_P\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,P}}\mathcal H_P}.
$$

Indeed, [sheafification](../../../../../../sheafification.md) preserves [stalks](../../../../../../stalk-of-a-sheaf.md), and filtered [direct limits](../../../../../../direct-limit-of-abelian-groups.md) commute with the [module](../../../../../../module-mathematics.md) [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md).
