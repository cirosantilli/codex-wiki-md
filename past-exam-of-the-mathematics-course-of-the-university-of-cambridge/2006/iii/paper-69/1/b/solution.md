<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Statistical isotropy](../../../../../../statistical-isotropy.md) and [statistical reflection symmetry](../../../../../../statistical-reflection-symmetry.md) allow the [spectral tensor](../../../../../../spectral-tensor.md) to contain only $\delta_{ij}$ and $k_i k_j$:

$$
\widehat C_{ij}(\mathbf k)=A(k)\delta_{ij}+D(k)k_i k_j.
$$

The [solenoidal magnetic-field constraint](../../../../../../solenoidal-magnetic-field-constraint.md) is essential here. Taking its [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) gives $k_i\widehat B_i=0$, hence $k_i\widehat C_{ij}=0$ and $A+Dk^2=0$. Thus the [spectral tensor](../../../../../../spectral-tensor.md) is a scalar multiple of the [transverse projector of a vector field](../../../../../../transverse-projector-of-a-vector-field.md). Its [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is $2A$, so the prescribed normalization gives

$$
\boxed{\widehat C_{ij}(\mathbf k)=H(k)\left(\delta_{ij}-\frac{k_i k_j}{k^2}\right),\qquad k\ne0.}
$$

This is the [solenoidal isotropic spectral tensor](../../../../../../solenoidal-isotropic-spectral-tensor.md). Without the [solenoidal](../../../../../../solenoidal-vector-field.md) condition, isotropy and reflection symmetry would leave two scalar functions. Without reflection symmetry, an antisymmetric term proportional to $i\epsilon_{ijm}k_m$ could additionally encode [magnetic helicity](../../../../../../magnetic-helicity.md).
