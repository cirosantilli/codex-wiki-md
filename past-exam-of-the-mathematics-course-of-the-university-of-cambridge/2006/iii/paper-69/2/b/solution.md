<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $b_l=\delta B_l/B_0$. The nonlinear term has two perpendicular spatial [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md), so its rate relative to the fluctuation amplitude is

$$
\tau_{\mathrm{nl}}^{-1}\sim\frac{v_Ad_i b_l}{l_\perp^2},\qquad \tau_{\mathrm{nl}}\sim\frac{l_\perp^2}{v_Ad_i b_l}.
$$

The [wave period](../../../../../../wave-period.md) from part (a), using $k\sim k_\perp$, is $\tau_w\sim l_\parallel l_\perp/(v_Ad_i)$. A [weak wave cascade](../../../../../../weak-wave-cascade.md) requires that one interaction change a packet only slightly:

$$
\chi=\frac{\tau_w}{\tau_{\mathrm{nl}}}\sim b_l\frac{l_\parallel}{l_\perp}\ll1.
$$

For interactions with decorrelated phases, successive fractional changes accumulate as a [random walk](../../../../../../random-walk.md). An order-one change needs $N\chi^2\sim1$, giving

$$
\boxed{\tau_l\sim N\tau_w\sim\frac{\tau_{\mathrm{nl}}^2}{\tau_w}=\frac{l_\perp^3}{v_Ad_i b_l^2l_\parallel}.}
$$

Constant [energy flux](../../../../../../energy-flux.md) per unit mass then gives $\epsilon\sim v_A^2b_l^2/\tau_l\sim v_A^3d_i b_l^4l_\parallel/l_\perp^3$, hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{\delta B_l}{B_0}\sim\left(\frac{\epsilon l_\perp^3}{v_A^3d_i l_\parallel}\right)^{1/4}.}
$$

The [weak electron-magnetohydrodynamic cascade](../../../../../../weak-electron-magnetohydrodynamic-cascade.md) scaling uses both locality in scale and the decorrelation assumption; a small amplitude by itself does not specify the cascade time.
