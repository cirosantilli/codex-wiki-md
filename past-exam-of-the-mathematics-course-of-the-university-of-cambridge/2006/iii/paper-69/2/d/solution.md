<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Critical balance](../../../../../../critical-balance.md) makes the nonlinear interaction time comparable to the [wave period](../../../../../../wave-period.md):

$$
\frac{l_\perp^2}{v_Ad_i b_l}\sim\frac{l_\parallel l_\perp}{v_Ad_i},\qquad l_\parallel\sim\frac{l_\perp}{b_l}.
$$

An order-one interaction transfers energy in $\tau_l\sim\tau_{\mathrm{nl}}$, so constant [energy flux](../../../../../../energy-flux.md) gives $\epsilon\sim v_A^3d_i b_l^3/l_\perp^2$. Thus the [critically balanced electron-magnetohydrodynamic cascade](../../../../../../critically-balanced-electron-magnetohydrodynamic-cascade.md) has

$$
\boxed{\frac{\delta B_l}{B_0}\sim\left(\frac{\epsilon}{v_A^3d_i}\right)^{1/3}l_\perp^{2/3},\qquad l_\parallel\sim\left(\frac{v_A^3d_i}{\epsilon}\right)^{1/3}l_\perp^{1/3}.}
$$

For the corresponding perpendicular [magnetic energy spectrum](../../../../../../magnetic-energy-spectrum.md), $\delta B_l^2\sim k_\perp E_B(k_\perp)$ gives $E_B(k_\perp)\propto k_\perp^{-7/3}$. The increasing ratio $l_\parallel/l_\perp\propto l_\perp^{-2/3}$ describes progressively more anisotropic fluctuations at smaller perpendicular scales, within the range in which the model and [critical balance](../../../../../../critical-balance.md) assumptions hold.
