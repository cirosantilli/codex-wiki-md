<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume statistically steady, three-dimensional [turbulence](../../../../../../turbulence-split.md) at large [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md), with local [statistical homogeneity](../../../../../../statistical-homogeneity.md) and [statistical isotropy](../../../../../../statistical-isotropy.md) at scales small compared with the forcing scale $L$. The [Kolmogorov 1941 theory](../../../../../../kolmogorov-1941-theory.md) additionally assumes a local [energy cascade](../../../../../../energy-cascade.md), constant mean [energy flux](../../../../../../energy-flux.md) per unit mass $\epsilon$, and negligible [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) within the [inertial range](../../../../../../inertial-range.md). Its dimensional form neglects corrections from [internal intermittency](../../../../../../internal-intermittency.md).

At separation $l$, let $\delta u_l$ be a typical [velocity increment](../../../../../../velocity-increment.md). The [eddy turnover time](../../../../../../eddy-turnover-time.md) is $\tau_l\sim l/\delta u_l$. Constant transfer rate then gives $\epsilon\sim\delta u_l^2/\tau_l\sim\delta u_l^3/l$, hence

$$
\boxed{\delta u_l\sim(\epsilon l)^{1/3},\qquad E(k)=C_K\epsilon^{2/3}k^{-5/3}.}
$$

Here $E(k)$ is the one-dimensional [turbulent energy spectrum](../../../../../../turbulent-energy-spectrum.md), obtained from $\delta u_l^2\sim kE(k)$ with $k\sim l^{-1}$. The scaling holds only for

$$
\boxed{\eta\ll l\ll L,\qquad L^{-1}\ll k\ll\eta^{-1},\qquad \eta=(\nu^3/\epsilon)^{1/4}.}
$$

The [Kolmogorov length scale](../../../../../../kolmogorov-length-scale.md) follows by setting the scale-dependent [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md) $\delta u_l l/\nu$ to one. The associated [Kolmogorov microscales](../../../../../../kolmogorov-microscales.md) give $\tau_\eta=(\nu/\epsilon)^{1/2}$. At larger scales the forcing matters; at smaller scales [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) matters.
