<h1 id="2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

If two distinct [hypergraph edges](../../../../../../edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) survived in $H$ and shared two vertices, they were an offending pair in $H_0$. The deletion set $D$ contains a chosen vertex from their union, so at least one of those [hypergraph edges](../../../../../../edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) could not have survived. This contradiction proves that **$H$ is a [linear hypergraph](../../../../../../linear-hypergraph.md)**. Deleting vertices leaves every surviving [hypergraph edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) of size three, so the construction meets the uniformity requirement as well as the chromatic-number requirement.
