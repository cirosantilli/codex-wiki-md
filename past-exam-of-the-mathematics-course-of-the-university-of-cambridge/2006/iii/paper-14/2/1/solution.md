<h1 id="2/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Every [hypergraph independent set](../../../../../../hypergraph-independent-set.md) in the induced [hypergraph](../../../../../../hypergraph-split.md) $H$ is also a [hypergraph independent set](../../../../../../hypergraph-independent-set.md) in $H_0$. Thus its maximum independent-set size is at most $s-1<n/(2k)<N/k$. In any proper [hypergraph colouring](../../../../../../hypergraph-colouring.md) with at most $k$ colours, one [colour class](../../../../../../colour-class.md) would have at least $N/k$ vertices, contradicting that bound. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\chi(H)>2005,\quad\text{hence}\quad\chi(H)\geq2006.}
$$

This is the weak [hypergraph colouring](../../../../../../hypergraph-colouring.md) convention: an [hypergraph edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) may use two colours, but it may not be [monochromatic](../../../../../../monochromatic-set.md).
