<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [density operator](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) represents a [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md) exactly when it has rank one, equivalently $\rho^2=\rho$ or $\operatorname{Tr}\rho^2=1$. The spectra just found give

$$
\boxed{\rho_1=|\psi_1\rangle\langle\psi_1|,\quad |\psi_1\rangle=\frac{|0\rangle-|1\rangle}{\sqrt2};\qquad
\rho_3=|\psi_3\rangle\langle\psi_3|,\quad |\psi_3\rangle=|1\rangle.}
$$

Their [wavefunctions](../../../../../../wave-function.md) are defined up to a [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md). The other two are [mixed states](../../../../../../mixed-state.md), with purity $(3/4)^2+(1/4)^2=5/8$, and have no single-wavefunction representation as rank-one projectors.
