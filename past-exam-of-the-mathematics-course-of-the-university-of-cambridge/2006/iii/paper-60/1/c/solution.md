<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Two finite-dimensional [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) are related by [unitary conjugation](../../../../../../unitary-conjugation.md) if and only if they have the same multiset of [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md). Necessity follows because conjugation preserves the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md); sufficiency follows by mapping orthonormal eigenbases in the [spectral theorem](../../../../../../spectral-theorem.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{\rho_0\sim\rho_2,\qquad \rho_1\sim\rho_3,}
$$

and no member of the first pair is equivalent to a member of the second. For an explicit example, the [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) $H$ gives $H\rho_0H^\dagger=\rho_2$ and $H\rho_3H^\dagger=\rho_1$. Multiplying $H$ by a scalar phase does not change either conjugation.
