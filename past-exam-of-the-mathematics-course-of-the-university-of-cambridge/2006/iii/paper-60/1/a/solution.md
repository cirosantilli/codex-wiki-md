<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Each operator has [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) one, so its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\lambda^2-\lambda+\det\rho$. The first and last are diagonal; for the others, the symmetric and antisymmetric vectors diagonalize the matrix. Their [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{spec}(\rho_0)=\operatorname{spec}(\rho_2)=\{3/4,1/4\},\qquad \operatorname{spec}(\rho_1)=\operatorname{spec}(\rho_3)=\{1,0\}.}
$$

For $\rho_1$, $(1,-1)^T/\sqrt2$ has [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) one and $(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$ has [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) zero. For $\rho_2$, those [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1/4$ and $3/4$, respectively. All four [density operators](../../../../../../density-matrix.md) are positive semidefinite, as these spectra confirm.
