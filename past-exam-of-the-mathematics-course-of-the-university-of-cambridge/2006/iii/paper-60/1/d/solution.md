<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Von Neumann equation](../../../../../../von-neumann-equation.md) integrates to $\rho(t)=U(t)\rho(0)U(t)^\dagger$, so the [spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) and [purity of a density operator](../../../../../../purity-of-a-density-operator.md) are invariant under any allowed [open-loop control](../../../../../../open-loop-control.md). These are the kinematic restrictions.

There is no additional orbit restriction here: the generators $i\sigma_z$ and $i\sigma_x$, together with their [matrix commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md), span the [special unitary Lie algebra](../../../../../../special-unitary-lie-algebra.md):

$$
[i\sigma_z,i\sigma_x]=-2i\sigma_y,\qquad \mathfrak g=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{i\sigma_x,i\sigma_y,i\sigma_z\}=\mathfrak{su}(2).
$$

For freely chosen durations and piecewise continuous real control values, this gives [density operator controllability](../../../../../../density-operator-controllability.md). The connected reachable group is $SU(2)$; its action by conjugation is the same as that of $U(2)$, because a [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md) cancels. Thus **exactly the pairs $\rho_0\leftrightarrow\rho_2$ and $\rho_1\leftrightarrow\rho_3$ can be interconverted**. No coherent control can turn either [mixed state](../../../../../../mixed-state.md) into either [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md), even though every orientation within each spectral orbit is reachable.
