<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Relative to a [filtration](../../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) satisfying the usual conditions, a [local martingale](../../../../../../local-martingale.md) is an [adapted](../../../../../../adapted-process.md) [càdlàg process](../../../../../../cadlag-process.md) $X$ for which there exist increasing [stopping times](../../../../../../stopping-time.md) $S_n\uparrow\infty$ almost surely such that every stopped process $X^{S_n}_t=X_{t\wedge S_n}$ is an integrable [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md). Thus, for $s\le t$,

$$
\mathbb E[X_{t\wedge S_n}\mid\mathcal F_s]=X_{s\wedge S_n}.
$$

The sequence is a [localizing sequence](../../../../../../localizing-sequence.md). A [continuous local martingale](../../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md) additionally has continuous sample paths. The usual definition entails integrability of the initial value; [local martingales](../../../../../../local-martingale.md) need not be integrable at later times without stopping.
