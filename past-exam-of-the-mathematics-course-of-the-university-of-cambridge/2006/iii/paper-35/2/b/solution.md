↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](../../../../../../dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) states: let $M$ be a [continuous local martingale](../../../../../../continuous-local-martingale.md) with $M_0=0$ and $[M]_\infty=\infty$ almost surely. Set

$$
\tau_u=\inf\{t\ge0:[M]_t>u\},\qquad
\mathcal G_u=\mathcal F_{\tau_u}.
$$

Then $W_u=M_{\tau_u}$ is standard [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) relative to $(\mathcal G_u)$ and

$$
\boxed{M_t=W_{[M]_t}.}
$$

Strict increase of the bracket is not required in the general theorem: $M$ is constant on intervals on which its bracket is constant. Continuity of $M$ is required. A nonzero initial value is handled by applying the theorem to $M-M_0$ and adding it back. If the terminal bracket is finite, the [finite-lifetime extension of the Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](../../../../../../finite-lifetime-extension-of-the-dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) supplies the [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) up to that bracket time and, if necessary, an independent continuation on an enlarged probability space.
