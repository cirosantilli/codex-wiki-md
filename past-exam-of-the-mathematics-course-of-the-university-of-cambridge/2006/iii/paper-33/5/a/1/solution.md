<h1 id="5/a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Expand the difference between the two [quantum mutual informations](../../../../../../../quantum-mutual-information.md):

$$
I(A:BC)-I(A:B)=S(BC)-S(ABC)-S(B)+S(AB).
$$

This is precisely the nonnegative expression in [Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../../../strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{I(A:B)\le I(A:BC).}
$$

The reduced state on $AB$ is obtained by the [partial trace](../../../../../../../partial-trace.md) over $C$, so this proves that discarding a system cannot increase its [quantum mutual information](../../../../../../../quantum-mutual-information.md) with the retained reference.
