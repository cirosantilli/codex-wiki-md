<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [unital quantum channel](../../../../../../unital-quantum-channel.md) preserves the identity operator: $\Phi(I)=I$. In equal input and output dimensions, it therefore preserves the [maximally mixed state](../../../../../../maximally-mixed-state.md). For [amplitude damping channel](../../../../../../amplitude-damping-channel.md),

$$
\mathcal A_p(I)=K_0K_0^\dagger+K_1K_1^\dagger=\begin{pmatrix}1+p&0\\0&1-p\end{pmatrix}=I+pZ.
$$

Hence **the channel is not unital for $p>0$; it is unital only in the identity case $p=0$**. Trace preservation involves the opposite products $K_i^\dagger K_i$ and does not imply unitality.
