<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [pedigree founder](../../../../../../founder-in-a-pedigree.md) factor $P(G_i)$ depends on population [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) and [haplotype](../../../../../../haplotype.md) frequencies, ancestry, mating assumptions and any population [linkage disequilibrium](../../../../../../linkage-disequilibrium.md). At one autosomal [genetic locus](../../../../../../genetic-locus.md), [Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium](../../../../../../hardy-weinberg-principle.md) with [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) frequencies $p,q$ gives [genotype](../../../../../../genotype.md) [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) $p^2,2pq,q^2$. For a specified ordered pair of independent [pedigree founder](../../../../../../founder-in-a-pedigree.md) [haplotypes](../../../../../../haplotype.md) $h_1,h_2$, the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is $p_{h_1}p_{h_2}$; an unordered distinct pair has twice that [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). [Pedigree founders](../../../../../../founder-in-a-pedigree.md) assumed to be related require a joint prior rather than a product of unrelated-founder factors.

The nonfounder factor depends on the parental [genotypes](../../../../../../genotype.md) and phases, [Mendelian segregation](../../../../../../mendelian-segregation.md), and the [recombination fractions](../../../../../../recombination-fraction.md) between [genetic loci](../../../../../../genetic-locus.md). In a two-locus [heterozygote](../../../../../../heterozygote.md) of known phase, the two parental [haplotypes](../../../../../../haplotype.md) have transmission [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) $(1-\theta)/2$ each, and the two recombinant [haplotypes](../../../../../../haplotype.md) $\theta/2$ each. Multiply the maternal and paternal gamete [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) and sum any gamete combinations producing the child's [genotype](../../../../../../genotype.md). Sex-specific [genetic recombination](../../../../../../genetic-recombination.md), mutation or segregation distortion can be incorporated by changing these gamete [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md). At one ordinary autosomal [genetic locus](../../../../../../genetic-locus.md), each parental copy is transmitted with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$.
