<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In the unrearranged [likelihood](../../../../../../likelihood-function.md) there are four summands. Each is a product of four supplied [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) and takes three multiplications. Summing the four results takes three additions. Thus

$$
\boxed{12\text{ multiplications},\qquad3\text{ additions}.}
$$

This count concerns direct evaluation without reusing repeated factors; zero-or-one [phenotype](../../../../../../phenotype.md) values could of course allow extra special-case savings.
