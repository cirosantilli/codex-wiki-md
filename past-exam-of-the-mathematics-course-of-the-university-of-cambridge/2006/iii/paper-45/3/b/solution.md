<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Conditional on both generation-2 siblings carrying the specified copy $q$, each passes it to each of their three children with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$. Their outside partners do not carry that ancestral copy. There are six independent [meioses](../../../../../../meiosis.md), one for each generation-3 individual forming the three cousin marriages. Consequently

$$
\boxed{P(\text{all six generation-3 partners carry }q\mid\text{both generation-2 siblings carry }q)=2^{-6}=1/64.}
$$

This calculation ignores [phenotype](../../../../../../phenotype.md) [ascertainment in a genetic study](../../../../../../genetic-study-ascertainment.md), as requested; conditioning on the later disease observations would force these transmissions rather than assign them their prior [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md).
