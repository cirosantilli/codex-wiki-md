<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $b$ for transmission of [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) 1 with [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) 2 untransmitted, and $c$ for the reverse. The informative transmissions give $b=20$, $c=30$. Under the fair-transmission null, conditional on $b+c=50$, $b$ is binomial with success [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$. Its standardized squared deviation is

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{TDT}=\frac{(b-c)^2}{b+c}=\frac{(20-30)^2}{50}=2.}
$$

The reference distribution is asymptotically chi-squared with one degree of freedom, giving a two-sided $p$-value about $0.157$. **[Allele](../../../../../../allele.md) 2 is overtransmitted and hence is the [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) more associated with disease in this sample**, although this result is not strong evidence against the null. The diagonal transmission cells contribute no discordant contrast.
