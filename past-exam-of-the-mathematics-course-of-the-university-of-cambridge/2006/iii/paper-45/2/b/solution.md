↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Only [heterozygous](../../../../../../heterozygosity.md) parents contribute an alternative-allele transmission contrast to the [transmission disequilibrium test](../../../../../../transmission-disequilibrium-test.md). Both parents are [homozygous](../../../../../../homozygosity.md) in rows 1, 13, 14 and 15, so **rows 1, 13, 14 and 15 are uninformative**. Their counts total 15 families. Every other row has at least one [heterozygous](../../../../../../heterozygosity.md) parent. When both parents are [heterozygous](../../../../../../heterozygosity.md) and the child is [heterozygous](../../../../../../heterozygosity.md), there is one transmission of each [allele](../../../../../../allele.md), regardless of the unresolved parent-specific phase; these transmissions contribute to both discordant cells.
