[Genetic linkage](../../../../../../genetic-linkage.md) analysis follows co-segregation of [genetic marker](../../../../../../genetic-marker.md) and disease [alleles](../../../../../../allele.md) within [pedigrees](../../../../../../pedigree.md). It estimates or tests a [recombination fraction](../../../../../../recombination-fraction.md), with [independent assortment](../../../../../../independent-assortment.md) at $\theta=1/2$ as the ordinary null. It can locate a disease [genetic locus](../../../../../../genetic-locus.md) even when different families have different [genetic marker](../../../../../../genetic-marker.md) [alleles](../../../../../../allele.md) on their disease chromosomes.

[Genetic association](../../../../../../genetic-association.md) analysis tests whether [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) or [genotype](../../../../../../genotype.md) frequencies differ with disease status. It can compare unrelated cases and controls, or compare transmitted and untransmitted parental [alleles](../../../../../../allele.md). A noncausal [genetic marker](../../../../../../genetic-marker.md) can be associated through population [linkage disequilibrium](../../../../../../linkage-disequilibrium.md) with a causal [genetic locus](../../../../../../genetic-locus.md). Association therefore concerns population-frequency relationships, whereas linkage concerns inheritance; [population stratification](../../../../../../population-stratification.md) can confound the former without producing genuine within-family linkage.
