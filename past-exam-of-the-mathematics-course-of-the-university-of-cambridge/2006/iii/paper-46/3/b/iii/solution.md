<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The first [principal component](../../../../../../../principal-component.md) has equal positive coefficients on the three academic subjects and zero coefficient on physical education. It measures overall academic performance in those subjects and explains $71.16\%$ of the [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md). The large positive academic covariances explain why these subjects vary predominantly together.

The second [principal component](../../../../../../../principal-component.md) has normalized coefficients approximately $(0.099,0,-0.099,0.990)$. It is mainly a physical-education score, with a small adjustment for the physics-versus-mathematics contrast, and explains $12.90\%$ of the [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md). The small physical-education covariances with the academic subjects make this direction close to a separate physical-education axis. This is an interpretation of the observed [covariance matrix](../../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md), not proof of distinct causal abilities or statistical independence.

The third [principal component](../../../../../../../principal-component.md) compares physics and mathematics together against biology: its coefficient vector is $(1,-2,1,0)/\sqrt6$. It captures subject balance rather than overall academic performance, accounting for $9.49\%$ of the [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md). The fourth [principal component](../../../../../../../principal-component.md) primarily contrasts physics with mathematics, with a smaller physical-education correction; its normalized coefficients are approximately $(0.700,0,-0.700,-0.140)$, and it accounts for the remaining $6.45\%$. Changing all signs in any component reverses its score without changing the interpretation of its contrast or its [explained variance of a principal component](../../../../../../../explained-variance-of-a-principal-component.md).

**A two-component summary separates broad academic performance from predominantly physical-education performance and retains about $84.06\%$ of the variation.** It still discards about $15.94\%$, including differences between academic subjects. Whether that loss is acceptable depends on the purpose: an overall summary may use two [principal components](../../../../../../../principal-component.md), whereas studying subject-specific strengths requires the later [principal components](../../../../../../../principal-component.md) too. A single [principal component](../../../../../../../principal-component.md) would lose most of the physical-education information.
