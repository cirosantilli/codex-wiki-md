Now the [prior odds](../../../../../../../prior-odds.md) are $\pi_f/\pi_a=2$. The fitted log posterior odds are $q(x)+\log 2$, so the [Gaussian Bayes classifier](../../../../../../../gaussian-bayes-classifier.md) assigns fattus when $q(x)>-\log 2$. The [prior-dependent Gaussian discriminant boundary](../../../../../../../prior-dependent-gaussian-discriminant-boundary.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{x_2=0.4x_1+4.4+0.8\log 2\simeq0.4x_1+4.9545.}
$$

It moves upwards parallel to the original line by $0.8\log 2\simeq0.5545$, expanding the fattus region. This is the dashed line in the sketch. The fifth individual's fitted log [likelihood ratio](../../../../../../../likelihood-ratio.md) remains zero, but its posterior odds become $2:1$, giving posterior fattus probability $2/3$.

**The fifth individual is now classified as fattus.** For completeness, the other four classifications remain apathus, fattus, apathus and fattus: their scores lie on the same side of the new threshold as before.
