<h1 id="6b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a normalized [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function.md), the [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) and [probability current](../../../../../../probability-current.md) are

$$
\rho=|\psi|^2,\qquad \mathbf J=\frac{\hbar}{2mi}\left(\psi^*\nabla\psi-\psi\nabla\psi^*\right).
$$

The [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) and its conjugate, for a real potential, are

$$
i\hbar\psi_t=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\psi+V\psi,\qquad
-i\hbar\psi_t^*=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2\psi^*+V\psi^*.
$$

Multiplying respectively by $\psi^*$ and $\psi$ and adding their expressions for the time derivatives cancels the potential terms. Hence

$$
\rho_t=\frac{i\hbar}{2m}\left(\psi^*\nabla^2\psi-\psi\nabla^2\psi^*\right)=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf J.
$$

The gradient cross-terms cancel when expanding the divergence. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\rho_t+\nabla\cdot\mathbf J=0.}
$$

This [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md) expresses local conservation of probability. Integrating over a region says that its probability decreases by the outward flux of the [probability current](../../../../../../probability-current.md).
