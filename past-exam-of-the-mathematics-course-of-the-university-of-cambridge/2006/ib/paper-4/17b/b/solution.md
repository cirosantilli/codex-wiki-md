In the javelin's [rest frame](../../../../../../rest-frame.md), the barn moves at speed $12c/13$. Applying [length contraction](../../../../../../length-contraction.md) to its proper length gives

$$
\boxed{L'_{\rm barn}=\frac3\gamma=\frac{15}{13}\ \mathrm m.}
$$

The lengths in the two frames use different pairs of simultaneous endpoint events.
