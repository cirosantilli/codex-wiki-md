<h1 id="17b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Lorentz factor](../../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) for $\beta=v/c=12/13$ is

$$
\gamma=\frac1{\sqrt{1-\beta^2}}=\frac{13}{5}.
$$

The javelin's length at simultaneous barn-frame times follows from [length contraction](../../../../../../length-contraction.md):

$$
\boxed{L_{\rm javelin}=\frac4\gamma=\frac{20}{13}\ \mathrm m.}
$$

Its proper length remains four metres.
