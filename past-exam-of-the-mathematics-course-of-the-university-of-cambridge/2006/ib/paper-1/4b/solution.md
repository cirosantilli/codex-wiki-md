<h1 id="4b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4B](../4b.md)

Interpret $m$ as the [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) of each initial ball and assume that no energy or [momentum](../../../../../momentum.md) escapes the collision. Let $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$ be its [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md). The total initial energy and [momentum](../../../../../momentum.md) are

$$
E=mc^2(\gamma+1),\qquad P=\gamma mv.
$$

Their conservation determines the final [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md). For a single lump, $P=\gamma'm'v'$ and $E=\gamma'm'c^2$, hence

$$
\boxed{v'=\frac{Pc^2}{E}=\frac{\gamma v}{\gamma+1}.}
$$

The final [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) follows from $m'^2c^4=E^2-c^2P^2$:

$$
m'^2=m^2\left[(\gamma+1)^2-\gamma^2\frac{v^2}{c^2}\right]
=m^2(2+2\gamma),\qquad
\boxed{m'=m\sqrt{2(1+\gamma)}.}
$$

This is the [rest mass of a coalescing relativistic collision](../../../../../rest-mass-of-a-coalescing-relativistic-collision.md). For $v>0$, $m'>2m$: some [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) becomes [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy.md) in the final rest frame. Setting $m'=2m$ would discard that energy and contradict conservation. At small speeds $v'\sim v/2$, while $v=0$ gives $m'=2m$.
