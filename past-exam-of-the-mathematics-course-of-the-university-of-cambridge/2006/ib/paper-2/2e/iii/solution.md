<h1 id="2e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Change the generators to $a=m_1$, $b=m_3-m_2$ and $c=m_2$. This is an invertible [integer](../../../../../../integer.md) change of generators, since $m_1=a$, $m_2=c$, $m_3=b+c$. The relations become $2b=0$ and $4c=0$, with no relation involving $a$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{M\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z.}
$$

This is already the invariant-factor order required by the [structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain](../../../../../../structure-theorem-for-finitely-generated-modules-over-a-principal-ideal-domain.md), because $2\mid4$. The inverse change of generators shows that there are no hidden extra relations.
