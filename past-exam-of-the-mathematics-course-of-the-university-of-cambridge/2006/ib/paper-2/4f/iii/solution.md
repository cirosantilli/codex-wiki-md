<h1 id="4f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The map $(x,y)\mapsto(x,y,x^2+y^2)$ is a continuous surjection from the [path-connected](../../../../../../path-connected-space.md) plane onto this surface. It is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../../homeomorphism.md) because coordinate projection is its continuous inverse. Thus **the paraboloid is [path-connected](../../../../../../path-connected-space.md) and [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md)**. Explicitly, lifting any straight planar segment by this map produces a path on the surface.
