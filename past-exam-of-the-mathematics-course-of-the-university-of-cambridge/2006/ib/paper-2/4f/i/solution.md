<h1 id="4f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The equation implies $|z|\geq1$. Its intersections with $\{z>0\}$ and $\{z<0\}$ are disjoint, nonempty subsets open in the [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md), and together cover the entire surface. They therefore form a separation, proving **the surface is disconnected**. More precisely its two sheets are $z=\pm\sqrt{1+x^2+y^2}$; each is homeomorphic to the [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) plane by projection, so these are exactly its two [connected components](../../../../../../connected-component.md).
