<h1 id="4f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Factorization gives $y=x$ or $y=-x$. Each point can be joined to the origin by a straight segment lying on its own line. Two such segments join any pair of points, so the union is [path-connected](../../../../../../path-connected-space.md) and hence **[connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md)**. Alternatively it is the union of two [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) lines with a common point.
