<h1 id="3f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The pointwise limit is the constant function $1$. For $0<x<1$, the inequality $1-e^{-u}\leq u$ for $u\geq0$ gives

$$
|e^{-x/n}-1|=1-e^{-x/n}\leq\frac xn<\frac1n.
$$

Hence **the sequence converges uniformly to one on $(0,1)$**. Its exact supremum error is $1-e^{-1/n}$, although the supremum is not attained on the open interval. Compactness of the interval is not needed for this [uniform convergence](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) bound.
