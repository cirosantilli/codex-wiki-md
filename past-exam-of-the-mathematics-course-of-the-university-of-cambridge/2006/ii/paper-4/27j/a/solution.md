The [strong law of large numbers](../../../../../../strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) states that for independent identically distributed real variables with $\mathbb E|X_1|<\infty$, the sample average converges almost surely to $\mathbb EX_1$. The [central limit theorem](../../../../../../central-limit-theorem.md) states that if additionally $0<\operatorname{Var}X_1=\sigma^2<\infty$, then

$$
\sqrt n(\overline X_n-\mathbb EX_1)\ \xrightarrow{d}\ N(0,\sigma^2).
$$

The vector form uses finite second moments and gives a centred multivariate Gaussian with the [covariance matrix](../../../../../../covariance-matrix.md) of one observation.
