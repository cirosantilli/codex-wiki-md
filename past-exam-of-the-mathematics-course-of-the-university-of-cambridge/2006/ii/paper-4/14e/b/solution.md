<h1 id="14e/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $t=x^2$, so $dx=dt/(2\sqrt t)$. Then

$$
I=\frac12\int_0^1t^{-1/3}(1-t)^{1/3}dt
=\frac12B(2/3,4/3).
$$

The [beta--gamma identity](../../../../../../beta-gamma-identity.md), the [Gamma function recurrence](../../../../../../gamma-function-recurrence.md) and the [gamma reflection formula](../../../../../../gamma-reflection-formula.md) give

$$
I=\frac12\Gamma(2/3)\Gamma(4/3)
=\frac16\Gamma(2/3)\Gamma(1/3)
=\boxed{\frac{\pi}{6\sin(\pi/3)}}.
$$
