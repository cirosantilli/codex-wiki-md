<h1 id="19f/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $z\in Z$, the [eigenspaces](../../../../../../../eigenspace.md) of $\rho(z)$ are invariant under $G$, since every representing matrix commutes with $\rho(z)$. Irreducibility forces one such [eigenspace](../../../../../../../eigenspace.md) to be all of $W$, so $\rho(z)=\chi(z)I$. The multiplication law gives $\chi(zz')=\chi(z)\chi(z')$, making $\chi$ a one-dimensional, hence irreducible, [group representation](../../../../../../../group-representation.md) of $Z$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Res}^G_ZW=(\dim W)\chi}.
$$
