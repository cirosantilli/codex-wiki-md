<h1 id="19f/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\chi$ be the central [one-dimensional character](../../../../../../../one-dimensional-character.md) of a proposed $W$. Since $Z$ is not cyclic, part (a) makes this [one-dimensional character](../../../../../../../one-dimensional-character.md) nonfaithful. Choose a nonidentity $z\in\ker\chi$. It acts as identity on every [tensor power](../../../../../../../tensor-power.md) $W^{\otimes n}$ and therefore on every summand. There is a [one-dimensional character](../../../../../../../one-dimensional-character.md) $\eta$ of $Z$ with $\eta(z)\ne1$: decompose the finite [abelian group](../../../../../../../abelian-group.md) into cyclic factors and choose a primitive [one-dimensional character](../../../../../../../one-dimensional-character.md) on a factor where $z$ has a nonidentity component. By part (b)(ii), some [irreducible representation](../../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) $V$ of $G$ has central [one-dimensional character](../../../../../../../one-dimensional-character.md) $\eta$ on $Z$. This $V$ cannot occur in any [tensor power](../../../../../../../tensor-power.md) of $W$, because $z$ acts nontrivially on it. Therefore **no such tensor-generating irreducible $W$ exists**.
