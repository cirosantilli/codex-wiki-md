<h1 id="19f/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [faithful representation](../../../../../../../faithful-representation.md) of dimension one embeds $A$ into $\mathbb C^\times$. Every image element is a $|A|$th root of unity by [Lagrange's theorem](../../../../../../../lagrange-s-theorem.md), so the image is a [subgroup](../../../../../../../subgroup.md) of the [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) of those roots. It is cyclic, and faithfulness makes $A$ cyclic. Conversely a generator of a [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) of order $m$ can be sent to $e^{2\pi i/m}$, giving an injective [one-dimensional character](../../../../../../../one-dimensional-character.md). Thus **such a [faithful representation](../../../../../../../faithful-representation.md) exists exactly when $A$ is a [cyclic group](../../../../../../../cyclic-group.md)**.
