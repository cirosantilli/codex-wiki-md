<h1 id="30a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Insert the uniformly convergent [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) of $g$ into the interval formula and integrate termwise. The constant mode contributes $tg_0$, while for $k\ne0$,

$$
\frac12\int_{x-t}^{x+t}e^{iks}ds=e^{ikx}\frac{\sin(kt)}k.
$$

The sum is exactly the expression from part (b), proving agreement of the transform and Fourier-series formulas.
