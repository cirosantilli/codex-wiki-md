<h1 id="25j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take the truncated dyadic approximation

$$
\boxed{g_n(x)=\min\{n,2^{-n}\lfloor2^nf(x)\rfloor\}}.
$$

It has finitely many values and measurable level sets, so is simple. Downward rounding and truncation ensure $0\le g_n\le f$. For a fixed finite $f(x)$, eventually $n>f(x)$, and then $0\le f(x)-g_n(x)<2^{-n}$. Thus $g_n(x)\to f(x)$ everywhere. If extended value infinity is permitted, define $g_n=n$ there, also giving the required limit.
