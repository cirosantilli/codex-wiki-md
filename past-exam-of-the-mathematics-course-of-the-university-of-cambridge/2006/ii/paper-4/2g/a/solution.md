↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The closed-set form of the [Baire category theorem](../../../../../../baire-category-theorem.md) says: if a nonempty [complete metric space](../../../../../../complete-metric-space.md) is a [countable union](../../../../../../countable-union.md) of [closed sets](../../../../../../closed-set.md), at least one of those sets has nonempty [interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md). Equivalently, a complete metric space cannot be a [countable union](../../../../../../countable-union.md) of [closed sets](../../../../../../closed-set.md) with empty interiors. In the application the space is $\mathbb R$ with its usual complete metric.
