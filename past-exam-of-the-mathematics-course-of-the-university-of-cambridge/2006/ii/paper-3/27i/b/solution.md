<h1 id="27i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With continuous dividend yield $D$, it is the discounted total gain from the stock, including dividends, that must be a [martingale](../../../../../../martingale-split.md). The ex-dividend stock drift under the [risk-neutral measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md) is therefore $r-D$. Replace $r-\sigma^2/2$ in the exponential representation by $r-D-\sigma^2/2$ and repeat the chain rule. The discount rate remains $r$, giving

$$
\boxed{V_t+\tfrac12\sigma^2S^2V_{SS}+(r-D)SV_S-rV=0.}
$$

The terminal payoff condition is unchanged.
