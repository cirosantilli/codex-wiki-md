<h1 id="9c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Near $\theta=0$, the linearized equation is $\ddot\theta+(g-a)\theta/\ell=0$. When $a<g$ this is an [harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md), so the downward configuration is stable. Writing $\theta=\pi+\varepsilon$ instead gives $\ddot\varepsilon-(g-a)\varepsilon/\ell=0$, with an exponentially growing solution. **Downward is stable; upward is unstable.**
