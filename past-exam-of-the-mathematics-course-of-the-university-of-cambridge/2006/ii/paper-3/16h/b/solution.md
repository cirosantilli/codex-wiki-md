<h1 id="16h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For each positive integer $n$, the finite ordered interval $[-n,n]\cap\mathbb Z$ has a greatest and a least element, and these intervals form a chain of [substructures](../../../../../../substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md). Their union is $\mathbb Z$, which has neither. An [inductive first-order theory](../../../../../../inductive-first-order-theory.md) must be preserved by such unions, as proved above. **The theory with both endpoints cannot be axiomatized inductively**, even though it has an ordinary first-order axiomatization with existential–universal endpoint sentences.
