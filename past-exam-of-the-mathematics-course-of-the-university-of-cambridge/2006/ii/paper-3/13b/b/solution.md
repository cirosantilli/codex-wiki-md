<h1 id="13b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [Fourier mode](../../../../../../fourier-mode.md) with $q=k^2\ge0$ evolves under $J-q\operatorname{diag}(\kappa_1,\kappa_2)$. Its [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) remains negative for a homogeneously stable positive branch. Its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is

$$
D(q)=\kappa_1\kappa_2q^2+(s\kappa_1-u_0\kappa_2)q+s(2u_0^2-u_0).
$$

If $u_0\kappa_2\le s\kappa_1$, this is positive for every $q\ge0$. Otherwise its minimum is attained at $q_*=(u_0\kappa_2-s\kappa_1)/(2\kappa_1\kappa_2)$. Stability requires $D(q_*)>0$, or

$$
u_0\kappa_2-s\kappa_1<2\sqrt{\kappa_1\kappa_2s(2u_0^2-u_0)}.
$$

In terms of $d=\sqrt{\kappa_2/\kappa_1}$, the boundary is the positive root of $u_0d^2-s=2d\sqrt{s(2u_0^2-u_0)}$:

$$
\boxed{d_c(u_0)=\sqrt{2s}+\frac{\sqrt{s(2u_0^2-u_0)}}{u_0}.}
$$

Within $1/2<u_0<s$, all modes decay for $d<d_c$; a finite band grows for $d>d_c$, giving a [Turing instability](../../../../../../turing-instability.md). Equality is neutral at one nonzero [wavenumber](../../../../../../wavenumber.md).

For $s=2$, the positive lower branch $0<u_0<1/2$ is unstable for every $d$. The upper branch is homogeneously stable for $1/2<u_0<2$: it is stable to all modes to the left of $d_c=2+\sqrt{4-2/u_0}$ and unstable to spatial modes to the right. For $u_0>2$ it is already homogeneously unstable. The zero branch is stable to every spatial mode when $r<0$, because its mode [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $r-\kappa_1k^2$ and $-s-\kappa_2k^2$.
