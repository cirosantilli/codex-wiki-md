<h1 id="15c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For fixed $\theta$, the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) for the meridional coordinate requires $\dot\phi^2\cos\theta=g/\ell$. Combining this with $v=\ell\sin\theta\dot\phi$ and $\cos\theta=D/\ell$ gives

$$
\boxed{v^2=\frac{g(\ell^2-D^2)}{D}.}
$$

The resulting motion is a [conical pendulum](../../../../../../conical-pendulum.md). The positive-depth condition is necessary for a nondegenerate constant-latitude orbit in gravity; the bottom point is the limiting zero-speed equilibrium.
