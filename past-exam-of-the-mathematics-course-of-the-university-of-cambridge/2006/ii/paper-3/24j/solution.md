↑ **Parent:** [24J](../24j.md)

The [characteristic function](../../../../../characteristic-function.md) is $\phi_X(u)=\mathbb E(e^{iuX})$. It satisfies $\phi_{-X}(u)=\phi_X(-u)=\overline{\phi_X(u)}$. Thus a real-valued [characteristic function](../../../../../characteristic-function.md) gives $\phi_X=\phi_{-X}$, and the [uniqueness theorem for characteristic functions](../../../../../uniqueness-theorem-for-characteristic-functions.md) implies equality of the two distributions. Conversely, equality in distribution gives equality with the complex conjugate, making the [characteristic function](../../../../../characteristic-function.md) real. **Reality for all $u$ is equivalent to symmetry of the distribution about zero.**
