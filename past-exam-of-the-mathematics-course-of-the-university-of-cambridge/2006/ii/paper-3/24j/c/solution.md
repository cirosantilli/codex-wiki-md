<h1 id="24j/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Condition on $X$. The normal [characteristic function](../../../../../../characteristic-function.md) gives $\mathbb E(e^{iuXY}\mid X)=e^{-u^2X^2/2}$, whose [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) is $(1+u^2)^{-1/2}$. A second [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) gives $\mathbb E(e^{-iuZ^2})=(1+2iu)^{-1/2}$, taking the square-root branch continuous from one at $u=0$. Independence of $(X,Y)$ and $Z$ therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\phi_\eta(u)=\frac1{\sqrt{1+u^2}\sqrt{1+2iu}}.}
$$
