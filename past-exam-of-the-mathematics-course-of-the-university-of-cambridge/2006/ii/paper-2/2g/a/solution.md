↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Chebyshev alternation theorem](../../../../../../equioscillation-theorem.md) says that a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $p$ of degree at most $n$ is a [best uniform approximation](../../../../../../best-uniform-approximation.md) to a real [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $f$ on a [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md) interval if and only if there are $n+2$ ordered points at which $f-p$ attains its [uniform norm](../../../../../../supremum-norm.md) with alternating signs. For nonzero error these are equal-magnitude alternating extrema; the zero-error case is understood separately. The best approximating [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is unique.
