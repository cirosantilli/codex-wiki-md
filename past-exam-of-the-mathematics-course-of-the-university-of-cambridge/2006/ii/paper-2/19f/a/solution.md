<h1 id="19f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [conjugacy classes](../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) are specified by [cycle type](../../../../../../cycle-type.md), with sizes $1,6,3,8,6$ for $1,(12),(12)(34),(123),(1234)$. The trivial and [sign representations](../../../../../../sign-representation.md) give the first two rows. The [permutation representation](../../../../../../permutation-representation.md) on four letters contains a trivial line; its [orthogonal complement](../../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) has [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) equal to the number of fixed letters minus one. Tensoring that [representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) by the sign gives another row. Finally the quotient $S_4/V_4\cong S_3$ supplies the two-dimensional standard [representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md). The resulting table is

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}
&1&(12)&(12)(34)&(123)&(1234)\\\hline
\text{class size}&1&6&3&8&6\\
\mathbf1&1&1&1&1&1\\
\mathrm{sgn}&1&-1&1&1&-1\\
V&3&1&-1&0&-1\\
V\otimes\mathrm{sgn}&3&-1&-1&0&1\\
W&2&0&2&-1&0
\end{array}
$$

With the class-size-weighted [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md), these [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) have [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) one and are pairwise orthogonal, so all are irreducible and inequivalent. Their squared dimensions sum to $1+1+9+9+4=24=|S_4|$, proving completeness.
