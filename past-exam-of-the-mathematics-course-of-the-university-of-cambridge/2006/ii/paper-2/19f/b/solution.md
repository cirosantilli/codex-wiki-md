<h1 id="19f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The classes of $A_4$ are the identity, the three double transpositions, and two classes of four three-cycles. Its quotient by $V_4$ is cyclic of order three, giving [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $\mathbf1,\chi,\chi^2$, with $\chi$ taking values $1,1,\omega,\omega^2$ on these classes. The restriction of $V$ has [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $(3,-1,0,0)$ and [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) $(9+3)/12=1$, so it is the three-dimensional [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) $T$. These four [representations](../../../../../../group-representation.md) are complete since $1+1+1+9=12$.

The sign is trivial on $A_4$, so

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Res}\mathbf1=\operatorname{Res}\mathrm{sgn}=\mathbf1,\quad \operatorname{Res}V=\operatorname{Res}(V\otimes\mathrm{sgn})=T,\quad \operatorname{Res}W=\chi\oplus\chi^2.}
$$

For the last equality the restricted [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is $(2,2,-1,-1)$, the sum of the two indicated [one-dimensional characters](../../../../../../one-dimensional-character.md). Equality of [characters](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) proves the decomposition.
