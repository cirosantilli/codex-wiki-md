<h1 id="11g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

All poles lie outside some circle $|z|=R$ with $R>1$. The [rational function](../../../../../../rational-function.md) is [holomorphic](../../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) in that disc, so its [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) at zero [converges uniformly](../../../../../../uniform-convergence.md) on $|z|\le1$. Its [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) [partial sums](../../../../../../partial-sum.md) therefore approximate uniformly on $K$. If there are no finite poles, the function is already a [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md).
