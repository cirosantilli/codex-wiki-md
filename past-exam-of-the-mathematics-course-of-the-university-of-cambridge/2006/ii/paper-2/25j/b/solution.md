<h1 id="25j/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The original PDF has height **$1/n$** on the interval $[0,e^n]$. For finite $p$,

$$
\|f_n\|_p=\frac{e^{n/p}}n\longrightarrow\infty,
$$

so the sequence cannot converge in $L^p$, since a norm-convergent sequence is bounded. For $p=\infty$, $\|f_n\|_\infty=1/n\to0$, so **it converges to zero in $L^\infty$**. It also converges pointwise, and hence [almost everywhere](../../../../../../almost-everywhere.md), to zero. Finally for each $\epsilon>0$, once $n>1/\epsilon$ the set $\{|f_n|>\epsilon\}$ is empty. Thus **it converges in [measure](../../../../../../measure.md) to zero**, even on this infinite-measure space.
