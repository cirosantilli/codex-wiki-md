<h1 id="24h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In [normal coordinates](../../../../../../normal-coordinates.md), each radial line $u(t)=tv$ is an affinely parametrized [geodesic](../../../../../../geodesic.md). Its coordinate [geodesic equation](../../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) gives $\Gamma^k_{ij}(tv)v^iv^j=0$. At $t=0$ this holds for every [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector.md) $v$. Since $\Gamma^k_{ij}=\Gamma^k_{ji}$, polarization of this [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form.md) gives every coefficient zero. Thus **all [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) vanish at the centre $p$**; they need not vanish elsewhere.
