<h1 id="24h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a parametrization $X(u^1,u^2)$, put $g_{ij}=X_i\cdot X_j$. Decompose the [second derivatives](../../../../../../second-derivative.md) into tangent and [normal components](../../../../../../normal-component.md):

$$
X_{ij}=\Gamma^k_{ij}X_k+b_{ij}\nu.
$$

These tangent coefficients are the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md). Differentiate $g_{ij}$ and combine the resulting three identities to obtain

$$
X_{ij}\cdot X_l=\frac12(\partial_i g_{jl}+\partial_jg_{il}-\partial_lg_{ij}),\qquad \boxed{\Gamma^k_{ij}=\frac12g^{kl}(\partial_i g_{jl}+\partial_jg_{il}-\partial_lg_{ij}).}
$$

Here $(g^{kl})$ is the [inverse matrix](../../../../../../matrix-inverse.md). The formula proves that these symbols depend only on the [first fundamental form](../../../../../../first-fundamental-form.md) and its first derivatives, not on the [second fundamental form](../../../../../../second-fundamental-form-split.md).
