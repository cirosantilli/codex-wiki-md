For strings $P_s/c^2=-\rho_s/3$, so $d\log\rho_s=-2\,d\log a$. Thus **$\rho_s=\rho_{s0}a^{-2}$**. These separate scalings assume no [energy](../../../../../../../energy.md) transfer between components.
