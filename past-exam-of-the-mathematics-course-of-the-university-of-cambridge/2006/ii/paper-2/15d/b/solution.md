<h1 id="15d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two scalings give $\rho=\rho_{r0}a^{-4}+\rho_{s0}a^{-2}$. Flatness and the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) give $H^2=8\pi G\rho/3$, with $H_0^2=(8\pi G/3)(\rho_{r0}+\rho_{s0})$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\dot a}{a}\right)^2=\frac{H_0^2}{a^4}\frac{a^2+\beta}{1+\beta}.}
$$

On the expanding branch, putting the [Big Bang](../../../../../../big-bang.md) at zero, separation gives

$$
t=\frac{\sqrt{1+\beta}}{H_0}\bigl(\sqrt{a^2+\beta}-\sqrt\beta\bigr),\qquad a^2=\frac{H_0^2t^2}{1+\beta}+\frac{2H_0t\sqrt\beta}{\sqrt{1+\beta}}.
$$

Its positive [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) is the required solution. If $\beta>0$, its early behaviour is $a\propto t^{1/2}$, as for radiation. The values $a=1$ and $a^2=\beta$ give

$$
\boxed{t_0=\frac{\sqrt{1+\beta}}{H_0}(\sqrt{1+\beta}-\sqrt\beta),\qquad t_{\rm eq}=\frac{\sqrt\beta\sqrt{1+\beta}}{H_0}(\sqrt2-1).}
$$

For $\beta=0$ there is only the string component, with linear expansion and no radiation era.
