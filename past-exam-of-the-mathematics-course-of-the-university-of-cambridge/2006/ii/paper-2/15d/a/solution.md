<h1 id="15d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiate $E=\rho c^2a^3V_0$ and use $dE=-P\,dV$, with $V=a^3V_0$. Division by $c^2a^3V_0$ gives

$$
\boxed{\dot\rho=-3\frac{\dot a}{a}\left(\rho+\frac{P}{c^2}\right).}
$$

This is homogeneous [adiabatic expansion](../../../../../../adiabatic-expansion.md) without [entropy production](../../../../../../entropy-production.md) or exchange across the comoving element.
