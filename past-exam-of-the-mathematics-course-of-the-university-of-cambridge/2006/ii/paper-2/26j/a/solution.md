↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For independent identically distributed positive [holding times](../../../../../../holding-time.md) $S_i$, define $T_0=0$, $T_n=\sum_{i=1}^nS_i$ and the renewal count $X_t=\max\{n:T_n\le t\}$. With $0<\mu=ES_1<\infty$, the renewal strong law is $X_t/t\to1/\mu$ [almost surely](../../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md). The [elementary renewal theorem](../../../../../../elementary-renewal-theorem.md) is $m(t)/t\to1/\mu$, where $m(t)=EX_t$. More generally the rate is zero when the mean [holding time](../../../../../../holding-time.md) is infinite, under the usual nonexplosion assumptions.
