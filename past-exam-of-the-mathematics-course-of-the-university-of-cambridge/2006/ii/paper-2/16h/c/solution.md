<h1 id="16h/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**True.** This is left distributivity of [ordinal multiplication](../../../../../../ordinal-multiplication.md). Fix $\alpha,\beta$ and induct on $\gamma$. The zero case is immediate. The successor step uses $\alpha(\eta+1)=\alpha\eta+\alpha$ and [associativity of ordinal addition](../../../../../../associativity-of-ordinal-addition.md). At a limit, multiplication and addition are [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) in their right argument, so taking suprema of the earlier equalities gives $\alpha(\beta+\gamma)=\alpha\beta+\alpha\gamma$.
