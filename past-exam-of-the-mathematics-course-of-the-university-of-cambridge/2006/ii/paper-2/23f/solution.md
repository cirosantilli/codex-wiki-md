A [Riemann surface](../../../../../riemann-surfaces.md) is a Hausdorff, second-countable, one-dimensional [complex manifold](../../../../../complex-manifold.md), with charts to [open subsets](../../../../../open-set.md) of $\mathbb C$ whose transition maps are [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md). Connectedness is often included in the convention. A map between [Riemann surfaces](../../../../../riemann-surfaces.md) is [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) when its expression in every compatible pair of charts is [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md). It is [biholomorphic](../../../../../biholomorphism.md) when it is bijective and both it and its inverse are [holomorphic](../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md).
