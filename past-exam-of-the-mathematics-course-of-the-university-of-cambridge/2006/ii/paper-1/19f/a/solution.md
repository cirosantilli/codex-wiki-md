↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [permutation representation](../../../../../../permutation-representation.md) $\mathbb C[X]$ has [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $e_x$, $x\in X$, with $g e_x=e_{gx}$. Its [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is a permutation [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md), whose diagonal entry is one exactly at [fixed points](../../../../../../fixed-point.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\chi_{\mathbb C[X]}(g)=|\{x\in X:gx=x\}|.}
$$
