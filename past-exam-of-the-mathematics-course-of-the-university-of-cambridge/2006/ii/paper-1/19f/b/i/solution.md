↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The translation subgroup $U$ is normal and $G/U\cong\mathbb F_p^\times$, via the upper-left entry $a$. Thus the [coset](../../../../../../../coset.md) [representation](../../../../../../../group-representation.md) is the [regular representation](../../../../../../../regular-representation.md) of that cyclic quotient. If $\chi$ runs through its $p-1$ complex one-dimensional [characters](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md), inflated to $G$, then

$$
\boxed{\mathbb C[G/U]\cong\bigoplus_{\chi\in\widehat{\mathbb F_p^\times}}\chi.}
$$

Each occurs once: the regular [character](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is $p-1$ at the quotient identity and zero elsewhere, which is also the sum of all quotient [characters](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) by finite [character orthogonality](../../../../../../../character-orthogonality.md).
