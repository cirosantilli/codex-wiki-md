<h1 id="19f/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $g=(a,b)$ for the affine map $z\mapsto az+b$. [Conjugation](../../../../../../../conjugation.md) by $(a,0)$ sends translation by $b$ to translation by $ab$, so it permutes transitively the nontrivial [characters](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) of $U$. Since $U$ is normal, the [induced character](../../../../../../../induced-character.md) is zero outside $U$. On a translation by $b$ it is the sum of all conjugates of $\psi$, hence

$$
\boxed{\chi_\rho(a,b)=\begin{cases}p-1,&a=1,b=0,\\-1,&a=1,b\ne0,\\0,&a\ne1.\end{cases}}
$$

For $b\ne0$ the sum of the nontrivial [additive characters](../../../../../../../additive-character.md) is minus one, since the sum including the trivial [character](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is zero. Its [character](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) [norm](../../../../../../../norm.md) is

$$
\langle\chi_\rho,\chi_\rho\rangle_G=\frac{(p-1)^2+(p-1)}{p(p-1)}=1.
$$

By the irreducibility criterion for [characters](../../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md), **$\rho=\operatorname{Ind}_U^G\psi$ is irreducible of dimension $p-1$**. All choices of nontrivial $\psi$ give the same [representation](../../../../../../../group-representation.md).
