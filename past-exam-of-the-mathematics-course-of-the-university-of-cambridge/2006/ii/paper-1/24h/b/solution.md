<h1 id="24h/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the homogeneity identity with respect to $t$ at one to obtain Euler's identity

$$
\sum_{j=1}^kx_j\partial_jp(x)=mp(x).
$$

On $X_a$, its right side is $ma\ne0$, so $dp_x\ne0$. Thus $a$ is a [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md). The [preimage theorem](../../../../../../preimage-theorem.md) says that the nonempty inverse image of a [regular value](../../../../../../regular-value.md) of a [smooth map](../../../../../../smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) $M^k\to N^l$ is a smooth submanifold of codimension $l$. Applied to $p:\mathbb R^k\to\mathbb R$, it gives **$\dim X_a=k-1$**.
