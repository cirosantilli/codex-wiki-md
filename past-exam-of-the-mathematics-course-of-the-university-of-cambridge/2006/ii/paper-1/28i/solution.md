<h1 id="28i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [28I](../28i.md)

The diagram has common multipliers $u=5/3,d=2/3$ on every branch, with gross riskless return $R=4/3$. The [martingale](../../../../../martingale-split.md) condition $pu+(1-p)d=R$ gives

$$
\boxed{p=\frac{R-d}{u-d}=\frac23,\qquad1-p=\frac13.}
$$

Since $d<R<u$, these [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) lie strictly between zero and one at each node, and the market is arbitrage-free. All valuations below use discounted [conditional expectations](../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) under this measure.
