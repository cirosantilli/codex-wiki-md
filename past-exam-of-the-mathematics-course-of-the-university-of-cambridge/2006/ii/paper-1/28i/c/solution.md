<h1 id="28i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Only paths whose first move is down have positive payoffs. On the down-up path the raised strike gives $992-960=32$, and on down-down it gives $992-384=608$. Their [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) are $2/9$ and $1/9$. Hence

$$
\boxed{V_0=R^{-2}\left(\frac29\cdot32+\frac19\cdot608\right)=42.}
$$

The payoff at the middle stock-price node is path dependent: the up-down path still has strike 864 and pays zero.
