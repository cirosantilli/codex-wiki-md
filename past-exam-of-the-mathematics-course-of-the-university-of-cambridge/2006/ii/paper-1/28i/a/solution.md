↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The terminal European put pays zero at the upper and middle nodes and $864-384=480$ at the lower one. Its [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is $(1-p)^2=1/9$. Thus

$$
\boxed{V_0=R^{-2}\frac{480}{9}=30.}
$$
