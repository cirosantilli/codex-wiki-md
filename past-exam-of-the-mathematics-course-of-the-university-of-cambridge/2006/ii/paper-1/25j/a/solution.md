Events $(A_n)$ are [independent events](../../../../../../independent-events.md) if for every finite list of distinct indices $i_1,\ldots,i_k$, $\mathbb P(\bigcap_jA_{i_j})=\prod_j\mathbb P(A_{i_j})$. Random variables are [independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) if for every such list and [Borel sets](../../../../../../borel-set.md) $B_j\subseteq\mathbb R$, $\mathbb P(X_{i_j}\in B_j\text{ for all }j)=\prod_j\mathbb P(X_{i_j}\in B_j)$. This is equivalent to independence of their generated [sigma-algebras](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md); [pairwise independence](../../../../../../pairwise-independent-events.md) alone is insufficient.
