The [tail sigma-algebra](../../../../../../tail-sigma-algebra.md) is

$$
\mathcal T=\bigcap_{m\geq1}\sigma(X_m,X_{m+1},\ldots).
$$

[Kolmogorov zero-one law](../../../../../../kolmogorov-s-zero-one-law.md) states that if the random variables are mutually independent, every event in $\mathcal T$ has [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) zero or one. No identical-distribution assumption is needed.
