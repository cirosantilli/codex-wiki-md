↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A conservative [Q-matrix](../../../../../../transition-rate-matrix.md) has $q_{ij}\geq0$ for $i\ne j$, finite exit rates $-q_{ii}=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}$, and hence row sums zero. It specifies the infinitesimal [transition probabilities](../../../../../../transition-probability.md) $P_{ij}(h)=\delta_{ij}+hq_{ij}+o(h)$. For a finite state space,

$$
\boxed{P(t)=e^{tQ},\quad P(0)=I,\quad P'(t)=QP(t)=P(t)Q.}
$$

The first [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) at zero is the generator. On infinite state spaces one must additionally address nonexplosion and the domains needed for the forward and backward equations.
