<h1 id="38c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Positive definiteness of $A$ implies $D>0$. Let $B=D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}$, a symmetric positive definite [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md). The additional hypothesis says $2I-B=D^{-1/2}(2D-A)D^{-1/2}>0$, so all [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$ of $B$ satisfy $0<\lambda<2$. The ordinary Jacobi error [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $I-D^{-1}A$ is similar to $I-B$ and therefore has [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1-\lambda\in(-1,1)$. Its [spectral radius](../../../../../../spectral-radius.md) is less than one. The convergence criterion for stationary linear iterations consequently gives **convergence from every starting vector**.
