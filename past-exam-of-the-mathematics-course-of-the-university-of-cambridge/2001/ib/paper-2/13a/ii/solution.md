<h1 id="13a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $F(z)=\exp(ig(z)^2)$. Since $\operatorname{Im}(g^2)=2uv$, its modulus is $e^{-2uv}\le1$. The [Liouville theorem](../../../../../../liouville-theorem.md) again makes $F$ constant, and $F'=2igg'F=0$ implies $(g^2)'=0$. Hence $g^2$ is a constant $c$. If $c=0$, then $g=0$. If $c\ne0$, then $g$ never vanishes and $gg'=0$ implies $g'=0$. In either case **$g$ is constant**. This is also an application of the [one-sided product bound for an entire function](../../../../../../one-sided-product-bound-for-an-entire-function.md) after rotating $g$ by $i$.
