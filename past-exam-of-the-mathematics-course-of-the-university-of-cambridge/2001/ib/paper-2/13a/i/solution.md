<h1 id="13a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $F(z)=\exp[-(1-i)g(z)]$. It is entire, and $\operatorname{Re}[(1-i)g]=u+v$ makes $|F|=e^{-(u+v)}\le1$. By the just-proved [Liouville theorem](../../../../../../liouville-theorem.md), $F$ is constant. Differentiation gives $0=F'=-(1-i)g'F$. The exponential never vanishes, so $g'=0$ everywhere and **$g$ is constant**. This is the [entire function confined to a half-plane is constant](../../../../../../entire-function-confined-to-a-half-plane-is-constant.md) principle.
