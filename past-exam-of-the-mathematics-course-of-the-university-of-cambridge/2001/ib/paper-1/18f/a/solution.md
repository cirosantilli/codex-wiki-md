<h1 id="18f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Infinite walls impose the [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) $\psi(-a)=\psi(a)=0$. Inside the [infinite square well](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md), the [Time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) is $-\hbar^2\psi''/(2m)=E\psi$. Its nonzero solutions satisfying both endpoint conditions have wave numbers $k_n=n\pi/(2a)$, $n\geq1$. Since

$$
\int_{-a}^a\sin^2\left(\frac{n\pi(x+a)}{2a}\right)dx=a,
$$

the normalized [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) are the sine modes with prefactor $1/\sqrt a$. They are orthonormal by sine [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) and complete by the [Fourier sine series](../../../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) on an interval of length $2a$. Differentiating twice gives

$$
\boxed{E_n=\frac{\hbar^2\pi^2n^2}{8ma^2}\quad(n=1,2,\ldots)}.
$$
