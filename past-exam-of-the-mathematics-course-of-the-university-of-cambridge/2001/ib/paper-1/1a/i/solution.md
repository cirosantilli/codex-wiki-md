↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Not uniformly continuous.** Take $x_n=n$, $y_n=n+1/n$. Then $|x_n-y_n|\to0$, but

$$
|y_n^2-x_n^2|=2+\frac1{n^2}\geq2.
$$

This contradicts the defining implication for [uniform continuity](../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md), for example with $\varepsilon=1$. The [sequential criterion for uniform continuity](../../../../../../sequential-criterion-for-uniform-continuity.md) makes explicit why arbitrarily close arguments at large distances from the origin matter.
