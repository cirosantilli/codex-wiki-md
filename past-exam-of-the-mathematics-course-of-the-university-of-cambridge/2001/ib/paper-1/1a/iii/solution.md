↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Uniformly continuous.** For $x\geq1$, the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) satisfies

$$
\left|\frac{d}{dx}\frac{\sin x}{x}\right|=\left|\frac{\cos x}{x}-\frac{\sin x}{x^2}\right|\leq2.
$$

The [mean value theorem](../../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) therefore gives a [Lipschitz condition](../../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) with constant two, hence [uniform continuity](../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md).
