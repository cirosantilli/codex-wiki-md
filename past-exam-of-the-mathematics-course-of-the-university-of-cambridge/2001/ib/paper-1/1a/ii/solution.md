↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Not uniformly continuous.** Choose

$$
x_n=\sqrt{2\pi n+\frac\pi2},\qquad y_n=\sqrt{2\pi n+\frac{3\pi}2}.
$$

Their separation is $\pi/(x_n+y_n)\to0$, while $\sin(x_n^2)=1$ and $\sin(y_n^2)=-1$. Thus the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) values stay distance two apart, contradicting [uniform continuity](../../../../../../uniform-continuity.md). Boundedness of the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) does not prevent increasingly rapid oscillations.
