<h1 id="9f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9F](../9f.md)

The [Einstein's principle of relativity](../../../../../einstein-s-principle-of-relativity.md) says that the laws of physics take the same form in every [inertial frame](../../../../../inertial-frame.md): no [inertial frame](../../../../../inertial-frame.md) is physically privileged, and uniform absolute motion cannot be detected by an internal experiment.

Assume $|u|<c$. Direct expansion of the transformed coordinates gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
x'^2-c^2t'^2
&=\gamma^2\left[(x-ut)^2-\left(ct-\frac{u}{c}x\right)^2\right]\\
&=\gamma^2(1-u^2/c^2)(x^2-c^2t^2)=x^2-c^2t^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $y'=y$ and $z'=z$, the full [Minkowski interval](../../../../../minkowski-interval.md) is invariant. Points of the outgoing light shell therefore satisfy

$$
\boxed{x'^2+y'^2+z'^2=c^2t'^2.}
$$

At each fixed $t'>0$ this is a sphere of radius $ct'$. The future branch remains future-directed: for a shell point with $t>0$, $|x|\le ct$ gives $t'=\gamma(t-ux/c^2)>0$.

A [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) is a linear coordinate transformation preserving the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md), equivalently $\Lambda^T\eta\Lambda=\eta$. The calculation establishes this property for all events, not only points on the light cone. The displayed map has [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) one and preserves [time orientation](../../../../../time-orientation.md), so it is the proper orthochronous boost along the $x$ direction.
