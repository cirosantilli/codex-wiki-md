<h1 id="18f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Conservation of momentum in the parent's rest frame gives $p_A+p_B=0$. Equal daughter masses and the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) then give equal daughter energies. Energy conservation fixes

$$
\boxed{E_A=E_B=\frac{Mc^2}{2}.}
$$

Using $E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4$ and the specified direction of A,

$$
\boxed{p_A=-\frac c2\sqrt{M^2-4m^2},\qquad
p_B=+\frac c2\sqrt{M^2-4m^2}.}
$$

The velocities follow from $v=pc^2/E$:

$$
\boxed{v_A=-V,\quad v_B=V,\qquad V=c\sqrt{1-4(m/M)^2}.}
$$

These are spatial momenta in the original [inertial frame](../../../../../../inertial-frame.md). For positive daughter rest mass, $m<M/2$ gives $0<V<c$.
