<h1 id="18f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Before its second decay A moves uniformly with $x=-Vt$. Its [Lorentz factor](../../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) is

$$
\gamma_A=\frac1{\sqrt{1-V^2/c^2}}=\frac{M}{2m}.
$$

The [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) along its [world line](../../../../../../world-line.md) satisfies $d\tau=dt/\gamma_A$. Since the first decay occurs at the spacetime origin, integration gives

$$
\boxed{t=\frac{M}{2m}\tau,\qquad x=-\frac{MV}{2m}\tau.}
$$

Equivalently these are the components of A's constant [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md) times $\tau$. The assumption $m>0$ is needed for a daughter rest frame and its proper-time clock; a massless daughter has neither.
