<h1 id="10f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10F](../10f.md)

In the observer's [rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md), $U=(c,0)$ and $P=(E'/c,\mathbf p')$. The [Lorentz invariance](../../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) of the [Minkowski inner product](../../../../../minkowski-inner-product.md) gives $P\cdot U=E'$ and

$$
P\cdot P=\frac{E'^2}{c^2}-|\mathbf p'|^2=m^2c^2.
$$

The relation between [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) and [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is $\mathbf p'=E'\mathbf v/c^2$, so

$$
\frac{v^2}{c^2}=\frac{c^2|\mathbf p'|^2}{E'^2}=1-\frac{c^2(P\cdot P)}{(P\cdot U)^2}.
$$

Taking the nonnegative square root proves

$$
\boxed{v=c\sqrt{1-\frac{c^2(P\cdot P)}{(P\cdot U)^2}}.}
$$

For a future-directed massive particle and observer, $P\cdot U>0$ and the expression lies in $[0,1)$; the massless limit, with nonzero energy, gives $v=c$.
