A [norm](../../../../../norm.md) on a real [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $V$ is a map $p:V\to[0,\infty)$ satisfying $p(x)=0$ exactly when $x=0$, $p(tx)=|t|p(x)$ for real $t$, and $p(x+y)\le p(x)+p(y)$. Its induced [metric](../../../../../metric.md) is $d_p(x,y)=p(x-y)$. The three axioms respectively give definiteness, compatibility with scaling, and the [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md).
