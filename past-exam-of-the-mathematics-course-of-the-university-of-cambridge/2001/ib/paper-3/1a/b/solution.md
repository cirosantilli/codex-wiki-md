<h1 id="1a/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expand using the bilinearity and symmetry of the real [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md):

$$
\|x+y\|^2=\langle x,x\rangle+2\langle x,y\rangle+\langle y,y\rangle,\qquad
\|x-y\|^2=\langle x,x\rangle-2\langle x,y\rangle+\langle y,y\rangle.
$$

Addition proves the [parallelogram law](../../../../../../parallelogram-law.md),

$$
\boxed{\|x+y\|^2+\|x-y\|^2=2\|x\|^2+2\|y\|^2.}
$$

For the maximum [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) on $\mathbb R^2$, take $x=(1,0)$ and $y=(0,1)$. All four vectors $x,y,x+y,x-y$ have maximum [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) one. The left side of the [parallelogram law](../../../../../../parallelogram-law.md) is then $2$, whereas the right side is $4$. **The maximum norm cannot come from an inner product.**
