<h1 id="2g/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Regularity at $r=0$ excludes every negative radial power and the logarithmic zero mode. Thus the general regular single-valued [harmonic function](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md) on the [disk](../../../../../../disk-mathematics.md) has the form

$$
\boxed{\phi(r,\theta)=A_0+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}r^n(A_n\cos n\theta+B_n\sin n\theta).}
$$

The coefficients must give a [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) converging locally in the disk; if finiteness and continuity on its boundary are required, the boundary [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) must represent the prescribed finite continuous boundary values. Conversely every such locally convergent expansion is [harmonic](../../../../../../harmonic-function.md), and the [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) on circles show that it includes every regular solution. There is no angular term linear in $\theta$, because it would be multivalued.
