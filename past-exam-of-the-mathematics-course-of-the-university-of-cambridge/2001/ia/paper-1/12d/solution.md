<h1 id="12d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [12D](../12d.md)

For a bounded function on a nondegenerate compact interval $[a,b]$, a partition $P$ has [Darboux sums](../../../../../darboux-sum.md)

$$
L(f,P)=\sum_i\inf_{[t_{i-1},t_i]}f\,(t_i-t_{i-1}),\qquad
U(f,P)=\sum_i\sup_{[t_{i-1},t_i]}f\,(t_i-t_{i-1}).
$$

The [Riemann integrability criterion](../../../../../riemann-integrability-criterion.md) is

$$
\boxed{\forall\epsilon>0\ \exists P:\ U(f,P)-L(f,P)<\epsilon.}
$$

Equivalently the supremum of lower sums and infimum of upper sums agree; their common value is the [Riemann integral](../../../../../riemann-integral.md). A bounded nonintegrable example is the [Dirichlet function](../../../../../dirichlet-function.md) $\boxed{f=1_{\mathbb Q}}$. Every positive-length interval contains rational and irrational points, so every lower sum is zero and every upper sum is $b-a>0$.
