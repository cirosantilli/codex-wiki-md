<h1 id="9d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $z\ne0$,

$$
|a_nz^n|^{1/n}=|a_n|^{1/n}|z|\longrightarrow R|z|.
$$

If $R|z|<1$, choose $q$ strictly between that limit and $1$. Eventually $|a_nz^n|\le q^n$, proving [absolute convergence](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) by the [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) comparison. If $R|z|>1$, choose $q>1$ below the limit; eventually $|a_nz^n|\ge q^n$, so the terms do not tend to zero and the series diverges. The point $z=0$ is harmless. This proves the [root test](../../../../../../root-test.md) in the needed case and gives

$$
\boxed{\rho=1/R.}
$$
