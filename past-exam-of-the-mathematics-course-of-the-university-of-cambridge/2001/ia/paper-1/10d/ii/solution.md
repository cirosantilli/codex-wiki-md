**The fixed-point property also fails on the real line.** The [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) $\boxed{f(t)=t+1}$ maps $\mathbb R$ into itself but has no [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md), since $t+1=t$ is impossible.
