↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The fixed-point property fails on an open interval.** The [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md)

$$
\boxed{f(t)=\frac{t+b}{2}}
$$

maps $(a,b)$ into itself, because $a<t<f(t)<b$. Its only possible [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) satisfies $t=(t+b)/2$, hence $t=b$, which is outside the domain. It is therefore a counterexample even with the self-map requirement retained.
