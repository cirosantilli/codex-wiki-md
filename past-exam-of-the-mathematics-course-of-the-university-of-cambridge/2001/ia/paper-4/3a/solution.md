<h1 id="3a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3A](../3a.md)

For an attractive [central force](../../../../../central-force.md), the transverse [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) vanishes. In [polar coordinates](../../../../../polar-coordinates.md),

$$
2\dot r\dot\theta+r\ddot\theta=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\frac d{dt}(r^2\dot\theta)=0.
$$

Thus the [specific angular momentum](../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) $h=r^2\dot\theta$ is constant. Assume $h\ne0$, so angle can parameterize the [orbit](../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md), and put $u=1/r$. Primes denote angle [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md). The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\dot\theta=hu^2,\qquad
\dot r=-hu',\qquad
\ddot r=-h^2u^2u'',\qquad
r\dot\theta^2=h^2u^3.
$$

The outward radial component of the attractive [force](../../../../../force.md) is $-f(u)$. Its [Newton's second law](../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) equation is therefore $m(\ddot r-r\dot\theta^2)=-f(u)$, giving the [Binet equation](../../../../../binet-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{u''+u=\frac{f(u)}{mh^2u^2}.}
$$

The sign reflects $f$ being the positive inward force magnitude. Zero [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) produces radial motion and is outside this angular parametrization.
