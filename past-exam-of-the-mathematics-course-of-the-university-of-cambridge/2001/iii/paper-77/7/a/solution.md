<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $F(t)=\prod_i(1-x_it)^{-1}$, its coefficients are precisely the [complete homogeneous symmetric functions](../../../../../../complete-homogeneous-symmetric-polynomial.md) $f_r=h_r(x)$. Thus the homomorphism sends every abstract generator $h_r$ to its ordinary value in the alphabet $x$. Equivalently the general product expansion is the ordinary [Cauchy identity for symmetric functions](../../../../../../cauchy-identity-for-symmetric-functions.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{s_\lambda^F=s_\lambda(x).}
$$

For every [symmetric function](../../../../../../symmetric-function.md) $u$, the same specialization gives $u^F=u(x)$.
