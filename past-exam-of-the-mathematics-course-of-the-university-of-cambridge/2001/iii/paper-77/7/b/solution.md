<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $F(t)=\prod_i(1+x_it)$ the coefficients are the [elementary symmetric functions](../../../../../../elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md), $f_r=e_r(x)$. The specialization is evaluation in $x$ after the [symmetric-function involution](../../../../../../symmetric-function-involution.md), because $\omega(h_r)=e_r$. Hence

$$
s_\lambda^F=\omega(s_\lambda)(x)=s_{\lambda'}(x),
\qquad
\boxed{s_\lambda^F=s_{\lambda'}(x).}
$$

One can also read this coefficient directly from the [dual Cauchy identity](../../../../../../dual-cauchy-identity-for-symmetric-functions.md), after exchanging its alphabets. For a general [symmetric function](../../../../../../symmetric-function.md), $u^F=(\omega u)(x)$; it is not simply $u(x)$.
