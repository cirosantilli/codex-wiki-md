<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a statistically homogeneous matter distribution, the [cosmological two-point correlation function](../../../../../../correlation-function-astronomy.md) of the mean-zero [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) $\delta=(\rho-\bar\rho)/\bar\rho$ is

$$
\boxed{\xi(r)=\langle\delta(\mathbf x)\delta(\mathbf x+\mathbf r)\rangle,
\qquad r=|\mathbf r|.}
$$

[Spatial homogeneity](../../../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md) removes dependence on $\mathbf x$, and [isotropy](../../../../../../isotropy.md) reduces the separation argument to its magnitude. For discrete [galaxies](../../../../../../galaxy-split.md), the [galaxy two-point correlation function](../../../../../../galaxy-two-point-correlation-function.md) equivalently specifies the excess pair probability at distinct positions:

$$
dP_{12}=\bar n^2[1+\xi(r)]\,dV_1dV_2.
$$

Consequently the mean density of other [galaxies](../../../../../../galaxy-split.md), conditioned on a [galaxy](../../../../../../galaxy-split.md) at the origin, is $\bar n[1+\xi(r)]$. The self-pair at zero separation is excluded from this definition; its [Dirac delta](../../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) contribution is the [galaxy power spectrum shot noise](../../../../../../galaxy-power-spectrum-shot-noise.md) of the unsmoothed number-density field.
