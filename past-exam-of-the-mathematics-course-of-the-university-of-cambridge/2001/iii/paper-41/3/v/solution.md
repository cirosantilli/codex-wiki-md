<h1 id="3/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Consider a logarithmic band of [wavenumbers](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) around $k\sim R^{-1}$, where $R$ is a comoving scale. The [dimensionless cosmological power spectrum](../../../../../../dimensionless-cosmological-power-spectrum.md) of the [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md) is $\Delta_\delta^2(k)=k^3P_\delta(k)/(2\pi^2)$. For $P_\delta=Ak$ this scales as $k^4$, so the rms density amplitude in such a band scales as $R^{-2}$.

The [cosmological Poisson equation](../../../../../../cosmological-poisson-equation.md) gives $-k^2\Phi_{\mathbf k}=4\pi Ga^2\bar\rho_m\delta_{\mathbf k}$, with $\Phi$ a dimensionful [peculiar gravitational potential](../../../../../../peculiar-gravitational-potential.md). Its spectrum and its [potential fluctuations per logarithmic wavenumber](../../../../../../potential-fluctuations-per-logarithmic-wavenumber.md) are therefore

$$
P_\Phi(k)=\frac{(4\pi Ga^2\bar\rho_m)^2A}{k^3},
\qquad
\boxed{\Delta_\Phi^2(k)=\frac{(4\pi Ga^2\bar\rho_m)^2A}{2\pi^2},}
$$

independent of $k$. Equivalently, $\Phi_R\sim G\bar\rho_m(aR)^2\delta_R$ is independent of $R$ because $\delta_R\propto R^{-2}$. This is the scale-local meaning of the [Harrison-Zeldovich spectrum](../../../../../../harrison-peebles-zeldovich-spectrum.md). An uncut spectrum has a divergent total potential [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md); equal amplitude per logarithmic band does not imply a finite unsmoothed [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) over all scales. For wavelengths beyond the [Hubble radius](../../../../../../hubble-radius.md), the statement is formulated using the corresponding conserved primordial curvature mode and its adiabatic matter-era potential, rather than treating the subhorizon Poisson approximation as universally valid.

On angular scales larger than roughly a degree, the [Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../../../sachs-wolfe-effect.md) relates the last-scattering potential to the observed [Cosmic microwave background anisotropy](../../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-anisotropy-split.md): $\Delta T/T\simeq\Phi/(3c^2)$ for adiabatic modes in [matter domination](../../../../../../matter-domination.md). Thus the observed large-angle amplitude constrains $A$. Scale-invariant potential bands produce a [Sachs-Wolfe plateau](../../../../../../sachs-wolfe-plateau.md), $\ell(\ell+1)C_\ell$ approximately constant, rather than constant $C_\ell$. Its amplitude and any tilt test the normalization and spectral shape. [Cosmic variance](../../../../../../cosmic-variance.md), [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../../../integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md) and assumptions about adiabatic initial conditions limit this inference; the density spectrum's late-time transfer must also be included when comparing it with galaxy clustering.
