<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [Type Ia supernova](../../../../../../type-ia-supernova.md) can be standardized as a [standard candle](../../../../../../standard-candle.md) by correcting its peak brightness using the [light curve](../../../../../../light-curve.md) shape and [colour index](../../../../../../color-index.md). Its host spectrum gives the [redshift](../../../../../../redshift.md); the corrected [apparent magnitude](../../../../../../apparent-magnitude.md) and calibrated [absolute magnitude](../../../../../../absolute-magnitude.md) give the [distance modulus](../../../../../../distance-modulus.md)

$$
\mu=m-M=5\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{d_L}{10\,\mathrm{pc}}\right).
$$

The resulting [Hubble diagram](../../../../../../hubble-diagram.md) measures the shape of the [luminosity distance](../../../../../../luminosity-distance.md) as a function of [redshift](../../../../../../redshift.md). A spatially flat matter-and-vacuum model, for example, predicts

$$
d_L(z)=(1+z)\frac{c}{H_0}
\int_0^z\frac{dz'}{\sqrt{\Omega_m(1+z')^3+\Omega_\Lambda}}.
$$

Nonzero [cosmological spatial curvature](../../../../../../spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) changes both the expansion rate and the conversion from radial to transverse [comoving distance](../../../../../../comoving-radial-distance.md). Comparing the observed [Hubble diagram](../../../../../../hubble-diagram.md) with these predictions constrains the [matter density parameter](../../../../../../matter-density-parameter.md), [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) and [cosmological spatial curvature](../../../../../../spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md); a single low-[redshift](../../../../../../redshift.md) observation cannot separate them. The nearby sample calibrates the common intercept, while distant [Type Ia supernovae](../../../../../../type-ia-supernova.md) supply the redshift-dependent shape. Relative faintness at a specified [redshift](../../../../../../redshift.md) means a greater inferred [luminosity distance](../../../../../../luminosity-distance.md).

[Type Ia supernova cosmology](../../../../../../type-ia-supernova-cosmology.md) requires correction for [interstellar extinction](../../../../../../interstellar-extinction.md), possible evolution of the standardized [luminosity](../../../../../../luminosity.md), and [selection bias](../../../../../../selection-bias.md). The [absolute magnitude–Hubble constant degeneracy](../../../../../../absolute-magnitude-hubble-constant-degeneracy.md) means that an uncalibrated sample constrains the shape rather than independently determining $H_0$.
