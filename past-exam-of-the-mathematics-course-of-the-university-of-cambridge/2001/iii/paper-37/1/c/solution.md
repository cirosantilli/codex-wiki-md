<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The radiative family is a stylized approximation to gas-pressure-supported [main sequence](../../../../../../main-sequence.md) stars with mostly radiative interiors and moderately temperature-sensitive hydrogen burning, such as the roughly solar-mass regime. Its opacity exponents are local approximations, not a universal composition-independent opacity law. The fully convective family is most relevant to the low-mass [main sequence](../../../../../../main-sequence.md), below the transition to a radiative core. Its steep surface temperature dependence resembles the role of [negative hydrogen ion opacity](../../../../../../negative-hydrogen-ion-opacity.md) in setting a [Hayashi track](../../../../../../hayashi-track.md); real surface opacity is not exactly the supplied density-linear tenth-power law everywhere.

At higher mass, the [CNO cycle](../../../../../../cno-cycle.md) replaces the [proton–proton chain](../../../../../../proton-proton-chain.md) as the dominant source of [stellar nuclear fusion](../../../../../../stellar-nuclear-fusion.md) and has a much larger local temperature exponent. This promotes a [convective core](../../../../../../convective-core.md). Hot highly ionized matter increasingly has [electron-scattering opacity](../../../../../../electron-scattering-opacity.md), rather than the specified density/temperature-dependent absorption. [Radiation pressure](../../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) becomes important in the [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md), changing $T_c\propto M/R$ and eventually making the [luminosity](../../../../../../luminosity.md) relation approach an [Eddington luminosity](../../../../../../eddington-luminosity.md) scaling. Composition gradients and strong [mass loss](../../../../../../mass-loss-astrophysics.md) also break strict homology.

At lower mass and effective temperature, partial [ionization](../../../../../../ionization.md) and [thermal dissociation](../../../../../../thermal-dissociation.md) change the heat capacity and [adiabatic index](../../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md); molecules and eventually condensates alter the surface [opacity](../../../../../../opacity.md). Increasing [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) invalidates pure ideal-gas support. Below the [hydrogen-burning minimum mass](../../../../../../hydrogen-burning-minimum-mass.md), sustained hydrogen-burning equilibrium is absent, so [brown dwarfs](../../../../../../brown-dwarf.md) cool instead of following a zero-age hydrogen-burning sequence. These changes explain why neither derived slope should be extrapolated indefinitely.
