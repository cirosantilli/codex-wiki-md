<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take homologous profiles and hold composition, hence [mean molecular weight](../../../../../../mean-molecular-weight.md), fixed. The [stellar mass conservation equation](../../../../../../stellar-mass-conservation-equation.md) and [stellar hydrostatic equation](../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure-support-equation.md) give $\rho_c\propto M/R^3$ and $P_c\propto GM^2/R^4$. With [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) pressure support, $T_c\propto M/R$. Integrating the specific nuclear rate through homologous profiles then gives

$$
L_{\rm nuc}\propto M\rho_cT_c^5\propto M^7R^{-8}.
$$

The [radiative diffusion in a star](../../../../../../radiative-diffusion-in-a-star.md) equation gives $L_{\rm rad}\propto RT_c^4/(\kappa_c\rho_c)$. Here $\kappa_c\propto\rho_c^{1/2}T_c^{-5/2}\propto M^{-2}R$, so

$$
L_{\rm rad}\propto M^5R^{-1}.
$$

Equality of generated and transported luminosity implies $M^7R^{-8}\propto M^5R^{-1}$, whence

$$
\boxed{R\propto M^{2/7},\qquad L\propto M^{33/7}.}
$$

This is the [radiative homology with density-half inverse-five-halves opacity](../../../../../../radiative-homology-with-density-half-inverse-five-halves-opacity.md) scaling.

The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) defines the effective temperature by $L=4\pi R^2\sigma_{\rm SB}T_{\rm eff}^4$. Therefore $T_{\rm eff}\propto M^{29/28}$. In theoretical [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](../../../../../../hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) coordinates, **the slope is**

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\log L}{d\log T_{\rm eff}}=\frac{132}{29}.}
$$

The usual graphical convention puts increasing temperature to the left, so the line descends as one moves right. If the horizontal coordinate is $-\log T_{\rm eff}$, its plotted slope is $-132/29$.
