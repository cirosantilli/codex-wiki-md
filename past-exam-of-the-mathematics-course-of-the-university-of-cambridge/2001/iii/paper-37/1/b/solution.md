<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A fully convective, monatomic [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) is approximately isentropic through its deep interior. Its [adiabatic index](../../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) is $5/3$, so $P=K\rho^{5/3}$ and $T\propto K\rho^{2/3}$. The $n=3/2$ [stellar polytrope](../../../../../../stellar-polytrope.md) scaling, or direct substitution of the central homology scalings into $K=P_c/\rho_c^{5/3}$, gives $K\propto RM^{1/3}$.

Near the photosphere, hydrostatic balance expressed in optical depth gives $P_{\rm ph}\sim g/\kappa_{\rm ph}$, with $g\propto M/R^2$. Treat $T_{\rm ph}$ as proportional to $T_{\rm eff}$ and match to the convective adiabat, neglecting a varying surface entropy jump in this homology approximation. Since $\rho\propto P/T$ and $\kappa_{\rm ph}\propto\rho T^{10}\propto PT^9$,

$$
P_{\rm ph}^2T_{\rm eff}^9\propto g.
$$

The adiabat gives $P\propto K^{-3/2}T^{5/2}$, so the surface condition yields

$$
T_{\rm eff}^{14}\propto gK^3\propto M^2R.
$$

The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) consequently gives $L_{\rm surf}\propto M^{4/7}R^{16/7}$. The same specific nuclear law as before still gives $L_{\rm nuc}\propto M^7R^{-8}$. Equating these scalings produces $R^{72/7}\propto M^{45/7}$. Thus the [fully convective homology with tenth-power surface opacity](../../../../../../fully-convective-homology-with-tenth-power-surface-opacity.md) is

$$
\boxed{R\propto M^{5/8},\qquad L\propto M^2.}
$$

Then $T_{\rm eff}\propto M^{3/16}$, and **the theoretical HR slope is**

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\log L}{d\log T_{\rm eff}}=\frac{32}{3}.}
$$

Its steepness expresses the relatively weak change of effective temperature with mass. The graph using $-\log T_{\rm eff}$ has the negative of this slope.
