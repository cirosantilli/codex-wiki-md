<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalize the [scale factor](../../../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) to $a(t_0)=1$, use $c=1$, and define the instantaneous [critical density](../../../../../../critical-density.md) by $\rho_{\rm cr}(t)=3H(t)^2/(8\pi G)$. Separately conserved [pressureless matter](../../../../../../pressureless-matter.md) and [radiation in cosmology](../../../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md) obey the [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md),

$$
\dot\rho+3H(\rho+p)=0,\qquad
\rho_M=\rho_{M0}a^{-3},\qquad
\rho_R=\rho_{R0}a^{-4}.
$$

The present [cosmological density parameters](../../../../../../cosmological-density-parameter.md) are

$$
\Omega_{M0}=\frac{8\pi G\rho_{M0}}{3H_0^2},\qquad
\Omega_{R0}=\frac{8\pi G\rho_{R0}}{3H_0^2},\qquad
\Omega_{\Lambda0}=\frac{\Lambda}{3H_0^2},\qquad
\Omega_0=\Omega_{M0}+\Omega_{R0}+\Omega_{\Lambda0}.
$$

The [cosmological constant](../../../../../../cosmological-constant.md) has equivalent [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) $\rho_\Lambda=\Lambda/(8\pi G)$. Substituting into the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) yields

$$
H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}(\rho_M+\rho_R)+\frac{\Lambda}{3}-\frac{k}{a^2}.
$$

At the present epoch this gives $k=H_0^2(\Omega_0-1)$, or curvature contribution $\Omega_{K0}=-k/H_0^2=1-\Omega_0$. Dividing each component by $H_0^2$ proves

$$
\boxed{\frac{H^2}{H_0^2}
=\Omega_{R0}a^{-4}+\Omega_{M0}a^{-3}
+\Omega_{\Lambda0}+(1-\Omega_0)a^{-2}.}
$$

Spatial curvature is not included in the total physical-density parameter $\Omega_0$. In particular, positive vacuum density does not have the radiation or matter scaling.
