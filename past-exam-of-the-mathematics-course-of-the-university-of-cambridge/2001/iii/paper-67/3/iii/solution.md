<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $H_\Lambda=\sqrt{\Lambda/3}$ and $\kappa=\sqrt{-k}>0$. With matter and radiation absent, the [Friedmann equation](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) is

$$
H^2=H_\Lambda^2+\frac{\kappa^2}{a^2},
\qquad
\dot a^2=H_\Lambda^2a^2+\kappa^2.
$$

Choose the expanding branch and put its coordinate origin at $a(0)=0$. Separating variables,

$$
t=\int_0^a\frac{db}{\sqrt{H_\Lambda^2b^2+\kappa^2}}
=\frac1{H_\Lambda}\operatorname{arsinh}\!\left(\frac{H_\Lambda a}{\kappa}\right),
$$

so the [open slicing of de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../open-slicing-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md) is

$$
\boxed{a(t)=\frac{\sqrt{-k}}{H_\Lambda}\sinh(H_\Lambda t).}
$$

For $a(t_0)=1$ the equivalent normalized form is

$$
\boxed{a(t)=\frac{\sinh(H_\Lambda t)}{\sinh(H_\Lambda t_0)},\qquad
\sinh(H_\Lambda t_0)=\frac{H_\Lambda}{\sqrt{-k}}.}
$$

It has $H(t)=H_\Lambda\coth(H_\Lambda t)$ and $H_0^2=H_\Lambda^2-k$, as required. In terms of a prescribed present [Hubble parameter](../../../../../../hubble-parameter.md),

$$
t_0=H_\Lambda^{-1}\operatorname{artanh}(H_\Lambda/H_0),
\qquad H_0>H_\Lambda.
$$

At early times $a\simeq\sqrt{-k}\,t$ resembles the [Milne universe](../../../../../../milne-model.md), while at late times it grows exponentially. The empty model still has vacuum [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md); the vanishing of $a$ at the open-patch origin is not a curvature singularity of [de Sitter spacetime](../../../../../../de-sitter-spacetime.md).
