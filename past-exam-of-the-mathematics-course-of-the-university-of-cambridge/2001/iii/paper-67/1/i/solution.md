<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use units $c=1$, and let $r$ be the areal radial coordinate of the [FRW metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), rather than the radial [comoving distance](../../../../../../comoving-radial-distance.md) when $k\ne0$. On a constant-[cosmic time](../../../../../../cosmic-time.md) slice the spatial [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) is

$$
d\ell^2=a(t)^2\left(\frac{dr^2}{1-kr^2}+r^2d\Omega^2\right),
\qquad
dV=a^3\frac{r^2\sin\theta}{\sqrt{1-kr^2}}\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi.
$$

The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of the spatial [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) supplies the factor $(1-kr^2)^{-1/2}$. Integrating over the [solid angle](../../../../../../solid-angle.md) gives $dV_{\rm shell}=4\pi a^3r^2|dr|/\sqrt{1-kr^2}$. An incoming radial [null geodesic](../../../../../../null-geodesic.md) obeys

$$
\left|\frac{dr}{dt}\right|=\frac{\sqrt{1-kr^2}}{a(t)}.
$$

Consequently the proper shell volume sampled by an emission-time interval of positive width $dt$ is $4\pi a^2r^2dt$. Multiplying by the proper [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) proves the [source counts on an FLRW past light cone](../../../../../../source-counts-on-an-flrw-past-light-cone.md):

$$
\boxed{dN=4\pi a(t)^2r(t)^2n(t)\,dt.}
$$

Here $r(t)$ is evaluated on the observer's [past light cone](../../../../../../past-light-cone.md), not along a fixed source worldline. The curvature factor cancels between the spatial volume and the radial light speed; it has not been set to one.
