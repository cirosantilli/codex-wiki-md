<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use $X_i=n_i/n_B$, so $X_D$ counts [deuterium](../../../../../../deuterium.md) nuclei per baryon, not baryons bound into [deuterium](../../../../../../deuterium.md). For the radiative capture reaction, [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) and the zero [photon chemical potential](../../../../../../photon-chemical-potential.md) give $\mu_D=\mu_n+\mu_p$. Dividing the three [nonrelativistic Maxwell--Boltzmann number densities](../../../../../../nonrelativistic-maxwell-boltzmann-number-density.md) removes all [chemical potentials](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md):

$$
\frac{n_D}{n_nn_p}
=\frac{g_D}{g_ng_p}
\left(\frac{m_D}{m_nm_p}\right)^{3/2}
\left(\frac{2\pi}{T}\right)^{3/2}
\exp\!\left[-\frac{m_D-m_n-m_p}{T}\right].
$$

Here $g_D=3$ and $g_n=g_p=2$. The binding convention in this PDF is a negative mass defect, $B_D=m_D-m_n-m_p<0$. The positive [nuclear binding energy](../../../../../../nuclear-binding-energy.md) is $\varepsilon_D=-B_D$, so the abundance is enhanced by $e^{-B_D/T}$, not by $e^{B_D/T}$.

Since $X_D/(X_nX_p)=n_Bn_D/(n_nn_p)$, insert the [baryon-to-photon ratio](../../../../../../baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) $n_B=\eta n_\gamma$ and the [photon number density](../../../../../../photon-number-density.md) $n_\gamma=2\zeta(3)T^3/\pi^2$. This gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{X_D}{X_nX_p}
=\eta\frac{2\zeta(3)}{\pi^2}T^3
\frac34\left(\frac{m_D}{m_nm_p}\right)^{3/2}
\left(\frac{2\pi}{T}\right)^{3/2}e^{-B_D/T}.}
$$

At equal nucleon mass $m_N$ in the prefactors, $m_D\simeq2m_N$, the [deuterium equilibrium abundance](../../../../../../deuterium-equilibrium-abundance.md) simplifies to

$$
\boxed{\frac{X_D}{X_nX_p}
\simeq\frac{12\zeta(3)}{\sqrt\pi}\,
\eta\left(\frac{T}{m_N}\right)^{3/2}e^{-B_D/T}.}
$$

The dimensionless prefactor is about $8.14$. These expressions describe [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md); residual primordial [deuterium](../../../../../../deuterium.md) after nuclear reactions cease must instead be obtained from the reaction kinetics.
