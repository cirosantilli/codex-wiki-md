<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In this [bufferless queue](../../../../../../bufferless-queue.md), [queue overflow](../../../../../../queue-overflow.md) is the event

$$
S_L>L\lambda+C L^{(1+\beta)/2},\quad\text{equivalently }Q_L>C.
$$

The [Poisson moderate deviation principle](../../../../../../poisson-moderate-deviation-principle.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_L\frac1{L^\beta}\log\mathbb P(\mathrm{overflow})
=-\inf_{x>C}\frac{x^2}{2\lambda}=-\frac{C^2}{2\lambda}.}
$$

The same infimum is obtained for $x\ge C$, so replacing $Q_L>C$ by $Q_L\ge C$ gives the same logarithmic exponent. Any integer rounding of the [service rate of a queue](../../../../../../service-rate-of-a-queue.md) changes the scaled threshold by a quantity tending to zero.
