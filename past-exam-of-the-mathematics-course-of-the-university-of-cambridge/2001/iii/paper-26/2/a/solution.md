<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [large deviation principle](../../../../../../large-deviation-principle.md) for random elements $X^L$ in a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) $E$, at [large-deviation speed](../../../../../../large-deviation-speed.md) $a_L\to\infty$, has a [lower semicontinuous](../../../../../../lower-semicontinuity.md) [rate function](../../../../../../rate-function.md) $I:E\to[0,\infty]$. For every [open set](../../../../../../open-set.md) $G$ and every [closed set](../../../../../../closed-set.md) $F$,

$$
-\inf_G I\le\liminf_L\frac1{a_L}\log\mathbb P(X^L\in G),
\qquad
\limsup_L\frac1{a_L}\log\mathbb P(X^L\in F)\le-\inf_F I.
$$

Equivalently, for every [Borel set](../../../../../../borel-set.md) $A$, the lower and upper bounds use its [interior](../../../../../../interior-topology.md) and [closure](../../../../../../closure-topology.md), respectively. The conventions are $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$ and $\log0=-\infty$. A [good rate function](../../../../../../good-rate-function.md) has [compact sublevel sets](../../../../../../compact-sublevel-set.md) $\{I\le r\}$ for every finite $r$. Here take $a_L=L$; the different speeds in the [moderate deviation principle](../../../../../../moderate-deviation-principle.md) are stated explicitly when used.
