<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The commutative [Laurent polynomial ring](../../../../../../laurent-polynomial-ring.md) $B$ is an [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md) and $\sigma$ is invertible. Write nonzero elements as

$$
r=\sum_{i=m_0}^{m}a_ix^i,\qquad s=\sum_{j=n_0}^{n}b_jx^j,\qquad a_m,b_n\ne0.
$$

The largest exponent in their product is $m+n$, with coefficient

$$
\boxed{a_m\sigma^m(b_n)\ne0}.
$$

There is only one pair of exponents contributing at this degree, and the coefficient cannot vanish in the [integral domain](../../../../../../integral-domain.md) $B$. Hence $rs\ne0$. Equivalently, **$rs=0$ forces $r=0$ or $s=0$.** This is [skew Laurent extensions of domains are domains](../../../../../../skew-laurent-extensions-of-domains-are-domains.md), and the argument works over the original [field](../../../../../../field.md) in any characteristic.
