<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Work over $\mathbb C$, or another [field](../../../../../field.md) of [characteristic zero](../../../../../characteristic-zero.md). The [Weyl algebra](../../../../../weyl-algebra.md) $A_n$ is generated by $x_1,\ldots,x_n,\partial_1,\ldots,\partial_n$ with commuting $x$'s, commuting $\partial$'s and $[\partial_i,x_j]=\delta_{ij}$. Its [ordered monomial basis of a Weyl algebra](../../../../../ordered-monomial-basis-of-a-weyl-algebra.md) consists of the [monomials](../../../../../monomial.md) $x^\alpha\partial^\beta$. One way to verify independence is to apply a finite operator relation to the formal exponential $e^{\mathbf t\cdot\mathbf x}$: after canceling that exponential the relation becomes a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) identity $\sum c_{\alpha\beta}\mathbf x^\alpha\mathbf t^\beta=0$, so every coefficient vanishes. The relations already reorder every word into that [basis](../../../../../basis.md).

Give both generators degree one. The [Bernstein filtration](../../../../../bernstein-filtration.md) is

$$
F_mA_n=\operatorname{span}\{x^\alpha\partial^\beta:|\alpha|+|\beta|\le m\},\qquad\operatorname{gr}A_n\cong\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n,\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n].
$$

Thus $\dim F_mA_n=\binom{m+2n}{2n}$. [Commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) terms have lower degree, accounting for the commutative symbol algebra. Since that algebra is [Noetherian](../../../../../noetherian-ring.md), leading-symbol generation followed by finite downward degree cancellation proves that $A_n$ is [Noetherian](../../../../../noetherian-ring.md) on both sides.

A left [finitely generated module](../../../../../finitely-generated-module.md) $M$ has a [good filtration](../../../../../good-filtration-of-a-module.md), for example $M_m=\sum_jF_mA_nm_j$ for a finite generating set of the [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md). More generally one allows fixed degree shifts of the generators. The [associated graded module](../../../../../associated-graded-module.md) is finite over the symbol algebra. Define

$$
\operatorname{Ch}(M)=\operatorname{Supp}(\operatorname{gr}M)=V(\operatorname{Ann}\operatorname{gr}M)\subseteq\mathbb A^{2n}\cong T^*\mathbb A^n.
$$

This is the [characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module](../../../../../characteristic-variety-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md) for the Bernstein algebra filtration. It records where the [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md)'s leading symbols remain nonzero and makes noncommutative [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) growth accessible to [commutative algebra](../../../../../commutative-algebra-split.md).

For a fixed algebra filtration it is independent of the [good filtration](../../../../../good-filtration-of-a-module.md). Any two such filtrations differ by bounded shifts $M_i\subseteq\widetilde M_{i+c}$ and conversely, since each finite set of generators occurs in bounded degree in the other filtration. If a degree-$d$ operator has symbol annihilating $\operatorname{gr}M$, its $r$th power maps $M_i$ into $M_{i+rd-r}$. For $r>2c$, bounded-shift comparison forces the power of that symbol to annihilate $\operatorname{gr}\widetilde M$. Reversing the argument gives equal [radicals of ideals](../../../../../radical-of-an-ideal.md) of [annihilators](../../../../../annihilator-ring-theory.md). This is [independence of characteristic support from a good module filtration](../../../../../independence-of-characteristic-support-from-a-good-module-filtration.md). Changing the algebra filtration itself, for example to the [order filtration of a Weyl algebra](../../../../../order-filtration-of-a-weyl-algebra.md) with the $x_i$ in degree zero, need not preserve the same variety; the convention matters.

The [Hilbert-Serre theorem](../../../../../hilbert-serre-theorem.md) gives an eventual cumulative [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md)

$$
\dim M_m=\frac{e(M)}{d!}m^d+O(m^{d-1}),\qquad d=\dim\operatorname{Ch}(M).
$$

Here $d$ is also the [Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of a module](../../../../../gelfand-kirillov-dimension-of-a-module.md), and $e(M)>0$ is an integer [multiplicity of a filtered module](../../../../../multiplicity-of-a-filtered-module.md) for nonzero $M$. Finite generation bounds $d\le2n$.

Here is a proof sketch of the essential lower bound, rather than an appeal to its name. For a nonzero $f\in F_mA_n$, choose a nonzero [monomial](../../../../../monomial.md) of greatest total degree in its ordered expansion, say $x^\alpha\partial^\beta$. The operations $\operatorname{ad}\partial_i$ and $\operatorname{ad}(-x_i)$ differentiate this expansion with respect to $x_i$ and $\partial_i$. Apply them $\alpha_i$ and $\beta_i$ times. Lower-degree terms die, and every other [monomial](../../../../../monomial.md) of the same degree also dies unless it has exactly those exponents. The result is a nonzero scalar multiple of the chosen coefficient. [Characteristic zero](../../../../../characteristic-zero.md) is essential for the factorials. Expanding the repeated [commutators](../../../../../commutator.md) therefore gives

$$
1=\sum_j u_jfv_j,\qquad u_j,v_j\in F_mA_n.
$$

Choose $0\ne v\in M_0$. If $f$ acted as zero on all $M_m$, every $fv_jv$ would vanish, contrary to $v=\sum_ju_jfv_jv$. Hence the linear action map

$$
F_mA_n\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb C}(M_m,M_{2m})
$$

is injective. It follows that $\dim F_mA_n\le(\dim M_m)(\dim M_{2m})$. Comparing [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) degrees gives $2n\le2d$. Thus

$$
\boxed{n\le d(M)\le2n}\qquad(M\ne0).
$$

This proves [Bernstein inequality for Weyl algebra modules](../../../../../bernstein-inequality-for-weyl-algebra-modules.md) through the [faithful finite-step action of a Weyl algebra](../../../../../faithful-finite-step-action-of-a-weyl-algebra.md).

The bound is sharp. The [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) representation $\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\cong A_n/\sum_iA_n\partial_i$ has the [zero section](../../../../../zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) as its [characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module](../../../../../characteristic-variety-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md), namely $\xi=0$ and dimension $n$. It is a [simple module](../../../../../irreducible-module.md): differentiate a nonzero [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) sufficiently to obtain a nonzero constant, then multiply by the $x_i$ to generate the whole [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md). The [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) $A_n/\sum_iA_nx_i$ similarly has [characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module](../../../../../characteristic-variety-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md) $x=0$, a fiber of the [cotangent bundle](../../../../../cotangent-bundle.md). A nonzero [free module](../../../../../free-module.md) has the whole [cotangent bundle](../../../../../cotangent-bundle.md) and dimension $2n$. No nonzero finite-dimensional [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) exists for $n\ge1$, also seen by taking the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of $[\partial_1,x_1]=I$.

[Modules](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) attaining dimension $n$ are [holonomic Weyl algebra modules](../../../../../holonomic-weyl-algebra-module.md) (with the zero [module](../../../../../module-mathematics.md) included conventionally). Every nonzero subquotient remains of dimension $n$ by the lower bound and monotonicity of growth. Compatible filtrations in a [short exact sequence](../../../../../short-exact-sequence.md) make the leading [multiplicity of a filtered module](../../../../../multiplicity-of-a-filtered-module.md) additive. Since each nonzero factor has positive integer [multiplicity of a filtered module](../../../../../multiplicity-of-a-filtered-module.md), the number of factors in any strict [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) chain is bounded by $e(M)$. Together with Noetherianity, this proves finite [composition length](../../../../../composition-length.md). It illustrates why [characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module](../../../../../characteristic-variety-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md) and [multiplicity of a filtered module](../../../../../multiplicity-of-a-filtered-module.md) provide useful structure beyond a list of presentations. In positive characteristic there are finite-dimensional Weyl [modules](../../../../../module-mathematics.md), so the characteristic-zero inequality and this discussion must not be asserted without its [field](../../../../../field.md) hypothesis.
