# Paper 28

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](split.md)

[https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/postgrad/part-iii/files/pastpapers/2001/Paper28.pdf](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/postgrad/part-iii/files/pastpapers/2001/Paper28.pdf)

The surviving four-page PDF does not include the separate S-Plus output sheets. Consequently it does not identify several fitted formulas, coefficients, test results or survival-table crossing times. The solutions below derive the identifiable results and explain the output interpretation; numerical conclusions requiring those absent sheets are distinguished explicitly from what the printed information establishes.

The official [PostScript edition](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/postgrad/part-iii/files/pastpapers/2001/Paper28.ps) has also been rendered and checked: it has the same four pages and contains no separate output sheets.
