<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Biot-Savart law](../../../../../../biot-savart-law.md) gives the [complex amplitude](../../../../../../complex-amplitude.md) of the field from the real wire as

$$
\mathbf B_{\rm wire}(x,y)=\frac{\mu_0J}{2\pi}\frac{-(y+b)\mathbf e_x+x\mathbf e_y}{x^2+(y+b)^2}.
$$

An oppositely directed image current $-J$ at $(0,b)$ gives

$$
\mathbf B_{\rm image}(x,y)=-\frac{\mu_0J}{2\pi}\frac{-(y-b)\mathbf e_x+x\mathbf e_y}{x^2+(y-b)^2}.
$$

The [method of images](../../../../../../method-of-images.md) makes the normal oscillating [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) vanish at $y=0$, as required when the alternating component cannot penetrate a [perfect conductor](../../../../../../perfect-conductor.md). The two tangential components reinforce one another there. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B(x,0^-)=C(x)\mathbf e_x,\qquad C(x)=-\frac{\mu_0Jb}{\pi(x^2+b^2)}.}
$$

Only the oscillating field is being excluded. A pre-existing steady [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md) is not erased by the [perfect conductor](../../../../../../perfect-conductor.md) limit.
