<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The central implication is that a helical conducting flow can continually regenerate a large-scale [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field.md), rather than merely stretch an existing component for a finite time. The [alpha effect](../../../../../../alpha-effect.md) competes with microscopic and [turbulent diffusivity](../../../../../../eddy-diffusivity.md), so [dynamo action](../../../../../../dynamo-action.md) requires a sufficiently strong regenerative response on a sufficiently large scale.

In a [planetary dynamo](../../../../../../planetary-dynamo.md), convection in an electrically conducting interior provides motion. Rotation through the [Coriolis force](../../../../../../coriolis-force.md), together with [density stratification](../../../../../../density-stratification.md) and boundaries, can give the motion a preferred handedness and nonzero [kinetic helicity](../../../../../../hydrodynamical-helicity.md). This makes the [alpha effect](../../../../../../alpha-effect.md) plausible, although its sign and magnitude vary spatially. A [geodynamo](../../../../../../geodynamo.md) must also satisfy the magnetic matching conditions at the core boundaries and the global excitation threshold. Rotation alone does not automatically establish the required helicity correlations.

For stellar and galactic fields, [differential rotation](../../../../../../differential-rotation.md) supplies the [Omega effect](../../../../../../omega-effect.md), converting a [poloidal magnetic field](../../../../../../poloidal-magnetic-field.md) into a [toroidal magnetic field](../../../../../../toroidal-magnetic-field.md). An [alpha effect](../../../../../../alpha-effect.md) can regenerate the poloidal component, closing an [alpha-Omega dynamo](../../../../../../alpha-omega-dynamo.md) loop. The [alpha-squared dynamo](../../../../../../alpha-squared-dynamo.md) provides another possibility when sufficiently helical motions dominate over shear. A large-scale nearly axisymmetric mean field is compatible with the [Cowling anti-dynamo theorem](../../../../../../cowling-anti-dynamo-theorem.md) because the fluctuating motions and fields that generate the [mean-field electromotive force](../../../../../../mean-field-electromotive-force.md) need not be axisymmetric.

The growing [Beltrami field](../../../../../../beltrami-field.md) calculation is a kinematic onset model, not a prediction of unlimited growth. The growing [Lorentz force density](../../../../../../lorentz-force-density.md) modifies the flow, leading to [dynamo quenching](../../../../../../dynamo-quenching.md) and saturation. In nearly ideal closed systems, conserved [magnetic helicity](../../../../../../magnetic-helicity.md) constrains the simultaneous large- and small-scale field evolution; helicity transport through boundaries and finite resistivity can therefore be important. Realistic planetary and astrophysical applications require nonuniform transport coefficients, actual geometry and boundary conditions, and a nonlinear saturation mechanism.
