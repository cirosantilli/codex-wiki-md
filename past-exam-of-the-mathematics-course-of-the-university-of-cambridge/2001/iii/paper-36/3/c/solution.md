<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The relevant competition is between energy reduction and [magnetic flux freezing](../../../../../../magnetic-flux-freezing.md). Ideal evolution transports the entire connectivity of the [magnetic field lines](../../../../../../magnetic-field-line.md), including the separation and linkage of flux regions. [Viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) can remove [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) but cannot permit [magnetic reconnection](../../../../../../magnetic-reconnection.md) between such regions. As each region relaxes toward lower [magnetic energy](../../../../../../magnetic-energy.md), neighboring flux regions can approach with different limiting tangential fields. A smooth transition can become increasingly thin; its field remains bounded while its [curl](../../../../../../curl.md), and therefore its [electric current density](../../../../../../current-density.md), becomes large.

The limiting object is a [current sheet](../../../../../../current-sheet.md). If $\mathbf n$ points from its minus to its plus side, its surface [electric current density](../../../../../../current-density.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf K=\frac1{\mu_0}\mathbf n\times(\mathbf B_+-\mathbf B_-).}
$$

For a tangential discontinuity, $\mathbf B_\pm\cdot\mathbf n=0$. The normal balance in [magnetostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../magnetostatic-equilibrium.md) requires

$$
\boxed{\left[p+\frac{|\mathbf B|^2}{2\mu_0}\right]_-^+=0.}
$$

Thus a finite jump of tangential field can coexist with equilibrium: the jump of [magnetic pressure](../../../../../../magnetic-pressure.md) is balanced by the ordinary [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) jump.

This need not happen for every initial field; a field already in smooth [magnetostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../magnetostatic-equilibrium.md) is an immediate exception. The expectation concerns topology-constrained relaxation for which a smooth limiting arrangement is obstructed. Current-sheet formation is consistent with the relaxation framework developed in [Moffatt's primary analysis](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/hkm2/PDFs/Moffatt_1985_JFM_159_359.pdf). Retaining complete frozen connectivity is essential; minimizing energy subject only to the value of total [magnetic helicity](../../../../../../magnetic-helicity.md) would generally permit field rearrangements forbidden by this evolution.
