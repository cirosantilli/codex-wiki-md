<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [hyperscaling relation](../../../../../../hyperscaling-relation.md) expresses the idea that the singular dimensionless [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) in one [correlation volume](../../../../../../correlation-volume.md) is of order one. A macroscopic volume $V$ contains of order $V/\xi^d$ such correlated regions, so

$$
f_s(t,h)\asymp\xi(t,h)^{-d}.
$$

Substitution of the homogeneous [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) gives

$$
\xi^{-d}=|t|^{d\nu}[g_{\xi,\pm}(h/|t|^\Delta)]^{-d}.
$$

This has exactly the required homogeneous field variable. Comparing its thermal power with that of the singular [free-energy density](../../../../../../free-energy-density.md) proves

$$
\boxed{d\nu=2-\alpha.}
$$

The argument concerns powers, not equality of the two scaling functions or their amplitudes. It assumes the [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) supplies the only singular length scale and that no [dangerously irrelevant coupling](../../../../../../dangerously-irrelevant-coupling.md) changes the free energy per correlated region. For ordinary short-range quartic theory, [hyperscaling](../../../../../../hyperscaling-relation.md) holds below the [upper critical dimension](../../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md) four, with the usual qualifications for marginal logarithms at four. Above four, [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md) $\alpha=0$, $\nu=1/2$ do not satisfy $d\nu=2-\alpha$; the quartic coupling is dangerously irrelevant. The stated homogeneous form of $\xi$ alone therefore does not prove hyperscaling without the correlation-volume assumption.
