<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A further derivative gives the [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) within a selected [pure thermodynamic phase](../../../../../../pure-thermodynamic-phase.md):

$$
\chi(t,h)=\frac{\partial m}{\partial h}
=|t|^{2-\alpha-2\Delta}g_{m,\pm}'(h/|t|^\Delta).
$$

Assuming a finite nonzero zero-field amplitude on the branch considered, comparison with $\chi(t,0)\asymp|t|^{-\gamma}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\gamma=2\Delta+\alpha-2.}
$$

Together with part (a), this proves the [Rushbrooke scaling relation](../../../../../../rushbrooke-scaling-relation.md) $\alpha+2\beta+\gamma=2$ and the [Widom scaling relation](../../../../../../widom-scaling-relation.md) $\gamma=\beta(\delta-1)$. Below the transition, use the one-sided response of an ordered phase; differentiating through the cusp of the equilibrium [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) at $h=0$ would include phase switching rather than this critical response.
