<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The no-borrowing [portfolio](../../../../../../investment-portfolio.md) constraint is $0\le x\le T$. Net [asset returns](../../../../../../financial-return.md) must be added to the original principal. Thus the two final [portfolio wealths](../../../../../../portfolio-wealth.md) are

$$
\boxed{w_g=x(1+r)+(T-x)(1+g)=T(1+g)-(g-r)x,\qquad w_b=x(1+r)+(T-x)(1+b)=T(1+b)+(r-b)x.}
$$

The [expected utility](../../../../../../expected-utility.md) is

$$
\boxed{U(x)=p\,u(w_g)+(1-p)u(w_b).}
$$

The probabilities are those of the investor's beliefs; no [risk-neutral measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md) is being used in this preference calculation.
