[Risk neutrality](../../../../../../risk-neutrality.md) means indifference between a random [portfolio wealth](../../../../../../portfolio-wealth.md) and its certain [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value.md). For increasing twice differentiable [expected utility](../../../../../../expected-utility.md) preferences, this corresponds to an [affine function](../../../../../../affine-function.md) $u(w)=Aw+C$ with $A>0$ on the relevant wealth interval. Maximizing [expected utility](../../../../../../expected-utility.md) then amounts to maximizing [expected return](../../../../../../expected-return.md):

$$
\mathbb Ew=T[1+pg+(1-p)b]+x[r-pg-(1-p)b].
$$

The coefficient of $x$ is strictly negative. Consequently **the unique optimum is $x_*=0$: invest all initial wealth in equity**. [Risk neutrality](../../../../../../risk-neutrality.md) here is a preference property, distinct from the pricing use of a [risk-neutral measure](../../../../../../risk-neutral-measure.md).
