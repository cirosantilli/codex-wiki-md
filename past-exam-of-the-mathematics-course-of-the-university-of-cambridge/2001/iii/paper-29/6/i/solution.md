The [recombination fraction](../../../../../../recombination-fraction.md) is the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) that a transmitted gamete is recombinant between the two loci in the ordinary two-locus [genetic linkage](../../../../../../genetic-linkage.md) model. Its conventional range is

$$
\boxed{0\leq\theta\leq\tfrac12.}
$$

Zero corresponds to complete linkage and $1/2$ to independent assortment. Multiple physical crossovers can restore parental phase, so the recombination fraction is not the expected number of crossovers or an unrestricted crossover [probability](../../../../../../probability.md).
