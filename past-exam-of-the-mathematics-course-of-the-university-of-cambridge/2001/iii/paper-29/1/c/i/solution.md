<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If both groups share the same [survival function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md) and the common fit is adequate, their complete [Cox–Snell residuals](../../../../../../../cox-snell-residual.md) have the same unit [exponential distribution](../../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md). Under [independent censoring](../../../../../../../independent-censoring.md) in each group, both groupwise [Kaplan–Meier estimators](../../../../../../../kaplan-meier-estimator.md) should therefore be close to $e^{-u}$ and to each other, subject to ordinary sampling variation and the number of subjects still at risk. Different [censoring](../../../../../../../censoring-statistics.md) patterns can change precision without changing the residual survival target.

**Both group curves should approximately follow the same unit-exponential survival curve.**
