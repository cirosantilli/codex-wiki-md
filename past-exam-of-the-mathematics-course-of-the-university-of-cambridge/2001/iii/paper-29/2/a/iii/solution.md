The continuous failure-time [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is the negative derivative of the [survival function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md):

$$
\boxed{f(t)=-F'(t)=h(t)F(t)=h(t)e^{-H(t)}.}
$$

The minus sign is essential because survivor [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) decreases with time.
