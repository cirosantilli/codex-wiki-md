Writing $F$ for the [survival function](../../../../../../../survival-function.md) here, the relation $F'(t)=-h(t)F(t)$ with $F(0)=1$ gives

$$
\boxed{F(t)=e^{-H(t)}.}
$$

This notation uses $F$ for survival rather than for the cumulative distribution function.
