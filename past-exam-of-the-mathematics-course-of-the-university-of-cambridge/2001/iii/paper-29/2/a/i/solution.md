↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a continuous nonnegative event time with [hazard function](../../../../../../../hazard-function.md) $h$, the [cumulative hazard function](../../../../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{H(t)=\int_0^t h(s)\,ds,\qquad H(0)=0.}
$$

Its derivative is the instantaneous hazard wherever the derivative exists.
