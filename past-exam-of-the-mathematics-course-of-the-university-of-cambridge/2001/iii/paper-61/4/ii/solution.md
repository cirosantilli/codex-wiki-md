<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Share the [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) $|\Phi_+\rangle$ between Alice and Bob, and allow their actions to be spacelike separated. The [projective measurement](../../../../../../projective-measurement.md) postulate gives the [remote preparation in conjugate bases](../../../../../../remote-preparation-in-conjugate-bases.md) identity

$$
({}_A\langle a|\otimes I_B)|\Phi_+\rangle
=\frac{|a^*\rangle_B}{\sqrt2}.
$$

If Alice measures in the computational basis, Bob is prepared in $|0\rangle$ or $|1\rangle$, each with probability one half. If she instead measures in the conjugate basis $U^*|0\rangle,U^*|1\rangle$, Bob is prepared in $U|0\rangle$ or $U|1\rangle$, again equally likely. The conjugation is essential for a general complex [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md).

Interpreting distinct states as distinct physical rays, the two candidate pairs are disjoint. A hypothetical perfect identifier would tell Bob which member he received and therefore which basis Alice chose. Alice could transmit a bit by that basis choice, without sending her outcome or any other message. Bob could read it while spacelike separated from Alice, violating the no-superluminal-signalling requirement of [special relativity](../../../../../../special-relativity-split.md). This establishes that [perfect two-basis discrimination would allow superluminal signalling](../../../../../../perfect-two-basis-discrimination-would-allow-superluminal-signalling.md) and rules out the device using the requested physical assumptions.

Ordinary [measurement in quantum measurements](../../../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) do not signal: the two equally weighted ensembles have identical [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md)

$$
\frac12\sum_{j=0}^1|j\rangle\langle j|
=\frac12\sum_{j=0}^1U|j\rangle\langle j|U^\dagger
=\frac I2.
$$

The contradiction arises from the hypothesized ability to label the ensemble's individual pure members. Global-phase variants of a vector do not count as distinct physical [pure states](../../../../../../pure-state.md); treating them as distinguishable labels would already introduce an unphysical extra readout.
