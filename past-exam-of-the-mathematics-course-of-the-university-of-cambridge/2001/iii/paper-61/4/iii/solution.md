<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A guaranteed exact overlap device would also distinguish two remotely prepared ensembles and permit a forbidden signal. Feed it a known reference $|\psi_1\rangle=|0\rangle$ and Bob's unknown state $|\psi_2\rangle$, obtained from Alice's [projective measurement](../../../../../../projective-measurement.md) on a shared [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md). A device promised to work for arbitrary unknown inputs must work on this fixed-reference subset.

Alice chooses either the computational basis or the diagonal basis $|+\rangle,|-\rangle$. By the same [remote preparation in conjugate bases](../../../../../../remote-preparation-in-conjugate-bases.md), Bob receives a uniformly random member of the chosen basis. The possible exact squared overlap outputs are

$$
|\langle0|\psi_2\rangle|^2=
\begin{cases}
0\ \text{or}\ 1,&\psi_2=|1\rangle\ \text{or}\ |0\rangle,\\
1/2,&\psi_2=|+\rangle\ \text{or}\ |-\rangle.
\end{cases}
$$

These output sets are disjoint. Bob would infer Alice's basis choice from one run, without her communicating the random measurement result. Spacelike operation would contradict [special relativity](../../../../../../special-relativity-split.md). Thus [exact pure-state overlap readout would allow superluminal signalling](../../../../../../exact-pure-state-overlap-readout-would-allow-superluminal-signalling.md), and **no guaranteed exact overlap device is compatible with the stated assumptions**.

This argument rules out even the phase-invariant magnitude squared. The raw complex inner product has an additional issue: replacing either [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md) representative by a [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md) changes that complex number without changing the physical input. The no-signalling contradiction therefore remains valid even if some phase convention is supplied. Statistical estimation from many identically prepared copies is not the promised exact single-pair readout.
