↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If an $m$-term [arithmetic progression](../../../../../../arithmetic-progression.md) contains no red pair, all of its unordered pairs are blue, since there are only two colours. This gives **the first alternative**, including the vacuous singleton case. Otherwise the position-pair [finite colouring](../../../../../../finite-coloring.md) in the root solution is defined everywhere, and the construction below forces the second alternative.
