<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $N$ parallel type-II [D-branes](../../../../../../d-brane.md), an oriented open string has endpoint labels $i,j$, its [Chan-Paton factors](../../../../../../chan-paton-factor.md). At coincidence, the massless states form $N\times N$ matrices, giving adjoint fields of $U(N)$. Tangential polarizations produce the [Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../../yang-mills-theory.md) gauge field; transverse polarizations produce $9-p$ adjoint [scalar fields](../../../../../../scalar-field.md). The leading low-energy action is the dimensional reduction of [ten-dimensional super Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../../ten-dimensional-super-yang-mills-theory.md) to $p+1$ dimensions. Expanding the [Dirac-Born-Infeld action](../../../../../../dirac-born-infeld-action.md) gives the gauge and scalar kinetic terms, while dimensional reduction supplies the [Hermitian commutator potential in D-brane Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../../hermitian-commutator-potential-in-d-brane-yang-mills-theory.md):

$$
V=-\frac1{4g_{\rm YM}^2}\sum_{I,J}\operatorname{Tr}[\phi^I,\phi^J]^2\ge0.
$$

The sign is important: for Hermitian $\phi$, the commutator is anti-Hermitian. Zero potential requires commuting scalars, whose simultaneous eigenvalues are brane positions through the [D-brane scalar-position normalization](../../../../../../d-brane-scalar-position-normalization.md)

$$
Y_i^I=2\pi\alpha'\langle\phi_i^I\rangle.
$$

This makes the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md) geometrical. Separating branes gives masses to strings joining different positions; bringing them together gives [Coincident-D-brane gauge enhancement](../../../../../../coincident-d-brane-gauge-enhancement.md).

Two parallel [D3-branes](../../../../../../d3-brane.md) in [type IIB superstring theory](../../../../../../type-iib-superstring-theory.md) give [four-dimensional N=4 super Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../../four-dimensional-n-4-super-yang-mills-theory.md) with gauge group $U(2)$, containing a gauge field, six real adjoint [scalar fields](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) and four adjoint [Weyl fermions](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md). On its [Coulomb branch](../../../../../../coulomb-branch.md), choose

$$
\langle\phi^I\rangle=\frac1{2\pi\alpha'}\begin{pmatrix}Y_1^I&0\\0&Y_2^I\end{pmatrix}.
$$

For distinct position vectors, $U(2)$ breaks to $U(1)\times U(1)$. The off-diagonal vector multiplets are the two orientations of a string joining the branes, and

$$
\boxed{m_W=\frac{|Y_1-Y_2|}{2\pi\alpha'}.}
$$

The same mass follows from the scalar covariant-derivative term, $m_W^2=\sum_I(\phi_1^I-\phi_2^I)^2$. The overall $U(1)$ describes center-of-mass motion; the relative $SU(2)$ sector is Higgsed to its Cartan $U(1)$. At coincidence the off-diagonal states become massless and the full $U(2)$ gauge symmetry is restored.
