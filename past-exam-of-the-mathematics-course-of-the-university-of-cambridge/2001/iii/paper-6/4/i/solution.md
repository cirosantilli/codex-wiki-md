<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume weakly null sequences have norm-null images. Let $(y_n)$ be any [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) in $T(K)$, where $K$ is weakly compact, and choose $x_n\in K$ with $y_n=Tx_n$. By the weak subsequence result, some $x_{n_j}\rightharpoonup x\in K$. Then $x_{n_j}-x\rightharpoonup0$, and the assumed property gives

$$
\|y_{n_j}-Tx\|=\|T(x_{n_j}-x)\|\longrightarrow0.
$$

Thus every sequence in $T(K)$ has a norm-convergent subsequence with limit in $T(K)$. In a [metric space](../../../../../../metric-space.md), sequential compactness is equivalent to compactness. Therefore **$\boxed{T(K)\text{ is norm compact}}$**. This is one characterization of a [completely continuous operator](../../../../../../completely-continuous-operator.md).
