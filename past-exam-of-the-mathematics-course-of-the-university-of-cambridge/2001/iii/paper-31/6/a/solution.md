↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [transferable utility game](../../../../../../transferable-utility-game.md), an [imputation](../../../../../../imputation-in-a-coalitional-game.md) is an efficient and individually rational [payoff](../../../../../../payoff.md) allocation:

$$
I(v)=\left\{x\in\mathbb R^3:\sum_i x_i=v(N),\quad x_i\ge v(\{i\})\right\}.
$$

Here it is

$$
\boxed{I(v)=\{x:x_1+x_2+x_3=12,\ x_1\ge1,\ x_2\ge2,\ x_3\ge3\}.}
$$

Writing $z_1=x_1-1$, $z_2=x_2-2$, $z_3=x_3-3$ identifies it with a scaled [standard simplex](../../../../../../standard-simplex.md) $z_1+z_2+z_3=6$. Its three vertices are $(7,2,3)$, $(1,8,3)$ and $(1,2,9)$.
