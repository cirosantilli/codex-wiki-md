<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A steady transverse [bifurcation](../../../../../../bifurcation.md) requires a zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), hence $\det M=0$. This gives

$$
\boxed{r_S=R^2=-\frac{|d|^2}{2Q},\qquad Q=\operatorname{Re}(\beta\overline d)<0.}
$$

The inequality is required for a positive [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md). Since $d_R>0$, the numerator cannot vanish. The zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is simple provided the other [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), the trace $-2(d_R+\beta_Rr_S)$, is nonzero. Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{Q\ne Q_*,\qquad Q_*:=\frac{\beta_R|d|^2}{2d_R}<0.}
$$

With $d,\beta$ fixed, $\partial_r\det M=2Q\ne0$, so the simple zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) crosses transversely. If this is to be the first instability of the stable pure branch, its other [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) must still be negative. Comparing with $r_H=-d_R/\beta_R$ gives the stronger condition

$$
\boxed{Q<Q_*\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad r_S<r_H.}
$$

For $Q_*<Q<0$, a simple steady threshold still exists, but it lies after the Hopf threshold and the pure branch is already unstable there. At $Q=Q_*$ both transverse [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) vanish, so the event is a double-zero degeneracy rather than an ordinary simple steady [bifurcation](../../../../../../bifurcation.md).

The reduced system has the [reflection](../../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) $C\mapsto-C$. At a generic simple steady threshold, the new mixed states therefore occur in a reflection-related pair, with small nonzero $B$ and a corresponding correction to the $A$ magnitude and common frequency. In the original variables they are **phase-locked periodic solutions involving both spatial modes**: $A=a e^{i\widetilde\Omega t}$, $B=b e^{i\widetilde\Omega t}$, with constant nonzero complex $a,b$. The odd spatial component breaks the [reflection](../../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of the pure even mode; the two branches are exchanged by $b\mapsto-b$. Determining whether the branches are supercritical or subcritical, and their stability, requires the reduced nonlinear [coefficient](../../../../../../coefficient.md) and is not determined by this linear threshold alone.
