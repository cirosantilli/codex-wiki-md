<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Submultiplicativity](../../../../../../submultiplicativity.md) and the [C-star identity](../../../../../../c-star-identity.md) give $\|x\|^2=\|x^*x\|\le\|x^*\|\|x\|$. For $x\ne0$ this implies $\|x\|\le\|x^*\|$. Apply the same argument to $x^*$ and use $x^{**}=x$ for the reverse inequality; $x=0$ is immediate. Hence

$$
\boxed{\|x^*\|=\|x\|}.
$$
