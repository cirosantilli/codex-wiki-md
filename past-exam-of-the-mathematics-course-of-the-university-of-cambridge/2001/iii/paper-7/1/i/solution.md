<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Intersect a [connected component](../../../../../../connected-component.md) $U$ of $\rho_A(x)$ with the [clopen](../../../../../../clopen-set.md) subset $\rho_B(x)$. [Connectedness](../../../../../../connected-space.md) makes the intersection either empty or all of $U$. In the empty case $U\subseteq\sigma_B(x)$. In the other case $U\subseteq\rho_B(x)$; a connected set in $\rho_B(x)$ containing $U$ is also connected in $\rho_A(x)$, so maximality prevents it from being larger. Thus **$U$ is a component of $\rho_B(x)$, or $U\subseteq\sigma_B(x)$**.
