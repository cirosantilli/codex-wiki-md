<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [genus-degree formula](../../../../../../genus-degree-formula.md) gives

$$
g(C)=p_a(C)=\frac{(3-1)(3-2)}2=1,
$$

because the [nonsingular plane cubic](../../../../../../nonsingular-plane-cubic.md) is already its own [normalization of an algebraic curve](../../../../../../normalization-of-an-algebraic-curve-split.md). If it were [birational](../../../../../../birational-variety.md) to the [projective line](../../../../../../projective-line.md), the two [smooth projective curves](../../../../../../smooth-projective-curve.md) would have isomorphic [function fields](../../../../../../function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md). The uniqueness of the [finite normalization model of a one-variable function field](../../../../../../finite-normalization-model-of-a-one-variable-function-field.md) would then make them isomorphic. But the [projective line](../../../../../../projective-line.md) has [genus of a smooth projective curve](../../../../../../genus-of-a-smooth-projective-curve.md) zero. Therefore **the cubic is not rational**. This obstruction works in the stated positive characteristics as well as in characteristic zero.
