<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [genus-degree formula](../../../../../../genus-degree-formula.md) gives [arithmetic genus](../../../../../../arithmetic-genus.md) three for a [plane quartic](../../../../../../plane-quartic.md). Each of its three double points has [multiplicity of a plane curve at a point](../../../../../../multiplicity-of-a-plane-curve-at-a-point.md) two, so the [arithmetic genus drop under a point blowup](../../../../../../arithmetic-genus-drop-under-a-point-blowup.md) is one at each point. The resulting [integral projective curve](../../../../../../integral-projective-curve.md) $C'$ has

$$
p_a(C')=3-3=0.
$$

For its finite [normalization of an algebraic curve](../../../../../../normalization-of-an-algebraic-curve-split.md) $\nu:\widetilde C\to C'$, the [arithmetic genus](../../../../../../arithmetic-genus.md) identity is

$$
p_a(C')=g(\widetilde C)+\operatorname{length}(\nu_*\mathcal O_{\widetilde C}/\mathcal O_{C'}).
$$

Both terms are nonnegative, so $g(\widetilde C)=0$. Over the [algebraically closed field](../../../../../../algebraically-closed-field.md), the [rationality of a smooth projective genus-zero curve](../../../../../../rationality-of-a-smooth-projective-genus-zero-curve.md) identifies $\widetilde C$ with the [projective line](../../../../../../projective-line.md): a point $P$ exists, and [Riemann-Roch theorem](../../../../../../riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives a degree-one map from the two-dimensional space $L(P)$. Consequently $C$ is [birational](../../../../../../birational-variety.md) to $\mathbb P^1$. This proves the [rationality of a plane quartic with three double points](../../../../../../rationality-of-a-plane-quartic-with-three-double-points.md) even when a double point is not an ordinary node.
