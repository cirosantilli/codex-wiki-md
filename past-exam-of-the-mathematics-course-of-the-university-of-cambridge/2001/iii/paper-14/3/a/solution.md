<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Every [projective line](../../../../../../projective-line.md) on the [smooth algebraic surface](../../../../../../smooth-algebraic-surface.md) through $P$ lies in the [projective tangent plane](../../../../../../projective-tangent-plane.md) $T_PS$, since its tangent direction is contained in the surface's [Zariski tangent space](../../../../../../zariski-tangent-space.md). The [plane section](../../../../../../plane-section.md) $S\cap T_PS$ is a nonzero [plane cubic](../../../../../../plane-cubic.md). It cannot be the entire plane, since a [smooth cubic surface](../../../../../../smooth-cubic-surface.md) is irreducible and has no plane component.

Each distinct [projective line](../../../../../../projective-line.md) through $P$ is therefore a distinct degree-one factor of the cubic restricted to $T_PS$. A degree-three [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has at most three such factors. Hence there are **at most three lines through any point**. This is the [lines through a point of a smooth cubic surface](../../../../../../lines-through-a-point-of-a-smooth-cubic-surface.md) bound; equality is allowed when the tangent section consists of three concurrent lines.
