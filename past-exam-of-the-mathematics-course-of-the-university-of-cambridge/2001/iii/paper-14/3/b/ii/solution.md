<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The two [projective lines](../../../../../../../projective-line.md) in the $i$th pair form the [fibre of a morphism](../../../../../../../fiber-of-a-morphism.md) $f^{-1}([s_i:t_i])$ of the extended [conic bundle from a line on a smooth cubic surface](../../../../../../../conic-bundle-from-a-line-on-a-smooth-cubic-surface.md) constructed above. Distinct points of the [projective line](../../../../../../../projective-line.md) have disjoint inverse images, so

$$
(l_i\cup l_i')\cap(l_j\cup l_j')=\varnothing\qquad(i\ne j).
$$

The extension of $f$ across $l$ matters: arguing only that distinct planes through $l$ meet along $l$ would not by itself exclude intersections there. The formula $f|_l=[-B:A]$ excludes them as well. Lines within a single pair do intersect; the assertion concerns different pairs.
