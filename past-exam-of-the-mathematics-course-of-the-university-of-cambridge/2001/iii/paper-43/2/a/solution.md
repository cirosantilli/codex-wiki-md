<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use complex amplitudes $(U,W,\Sigma,P)(z)e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$, with $k\ne0$ and $\omega\ne0$, and take physical real parts. The two-dimensional linearized [Boussinesq equations](../../../../../../boussinesq-equations.md) give

$$
ikU+W'=0,\quad -i\omega\Sigma=-N^2W,\quad
-i\omega U=-ikP/\rho_0,\quad -i\omega W=-P'/\rho_0+\Sigma.
$$

The first and third equations imply $U=iW'/k$ and $P=i\rho_0\omega W'/k^2$; the buoyancy equation gives $\Sigma=-iN^2W/\omega$. Insert these into the vertical momentum equation. The amplitude of the [internal gravity wave](../../../../../../internal-wave.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{W''+k^2\left(\frac{N^2(z)}{\omega^2}-1\right)W=0.}
$$

Oscillatory vertical propagation requires $N^2>\omega^2$; in a well-mixed region $N=0$, the bounded solution is [evanescent](../../../../../../evanescent-wave.md) rather than a vertically propagating internal wave.
