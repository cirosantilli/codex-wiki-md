<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Yoneda embedding](../../../../../../yoneda-embedding.md) sends $A$ to $h_A=\mathcal C(-,A)$ and $u:A\to B$ to postcomposition $h_u:h_A\to h_B$. Apply the [Yoneda lemma](../../../../../../yoneda-lemma.md) with $X=h_B$:

$$
\operatorname{Nat}(h_A,h_B)\cong h_B(A)=\mathcal C(A,B).
$$

The inverse sends $u$ precisely to $h_u$, because its component at $C$ maps $f:C\to A$ to $uf$. Hence the induced maps on all [hom-sets](../../../../../../hom-set.md) are [bijections](../../../../../../bijection.md). **The Yoneda embedding is full and faithful.**
