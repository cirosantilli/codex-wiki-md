↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**False.** For every deterministic $t>0$, [Brownian scaling](../../../../../../brownian-scaling.md) gives $B_t/\sqrt t\sim N(0,1)$. In particular, for any $\varepsilon>0$,

$$
\mathbb P(|B_t|/\sqrt t>\varepsilon)=2\bigl(1-\Phi(\varepsilon)\bigr)>0,
$$

independently of $t$. Almost-sure convergence would imply [convergence in probability](../../../../../../convergence-in-probability.md), but these fixed positive tail probabilities rule out convergence in [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) to zero. The square-root normalization retains fluctuations of order one.
