<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

For $R=\mathcal O_k$ and $S=\mathcal O_K$, define the [inverse different](../../../../../inverse-different.md), or [codifferent](../../../../../inverse-different.md), by the [trace-dual lattice](../../../../../trace-dual-lattice.md)

$$
\mathfrak D_{K/k}^{-1}=\{x\in K:\operatorname{Tr}_{K/k}(xS)\subseteq R\}.
$$

The [trace pairing](../../../../../trace-pairing.md) is nondegenerate in this [separable field extension](../../../../../separable-extension.md), so this is a full [fractional ideal](../../../../../fractional-ideal.md) of $S$. The relative [different ideal](../../../../../different-ideal.md) is its inverse:

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak D_{K/k}=(\mathfrak D_{K/k}^{-1})^{-1}.}
$$

Every integral $x$ has integral traces against $S$, so $S\subseteq\mathfrak D^{-1}$ and $\mathfrak D\subseteq S$.

For $k=\mathbb Q$, take an [integral basis](../../../../../integral-basis.md) $b_1,\ldots,b_n$ and its [trace pairing](../../../../../trace-pairing.md) matrix $T=(\operatorname{Tr}(b_ib_j))$. In these coordinates the [codifferent](../../../../../inverse-different.md) is $T^{-1}\mathbb Z^n$, and

$$
[\mathfrak D^{-1}:S]=|\det T|=|d_K|.
$$

The [ideal norm](../../../../../ideal-norm.md) of $\mathfrak D$ equals this index, by inversion of fractional ideal norms. Therefore the [norm of the different ideal](../../../../../norm-of-the-different-ideal.md) relation is

$$
\boxed{N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\mathfrak D_{K/\mathbb Q})=|d_K|,\qquad d_K=(-1)^{r_2}N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\mathfrak D_{K/\mathbb Q}).}
$$

Here $r_2$ is the number of conjugate pairs of complex embeddings. The different is an ideal of the upper field, whereas the relative [discriminant ideal](../../../../../discriminant-ideal.md) is its ideal norm in the base field; over $\mathbb Q$ the ordinary signed [field discriminant](../../../../../field-discriminant.md) also records the signature.
