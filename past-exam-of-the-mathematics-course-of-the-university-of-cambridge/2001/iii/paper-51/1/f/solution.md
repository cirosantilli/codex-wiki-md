<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

To include the [stable-variable lag in nilpotent center-manifold reduction](../../../../../../stable-variable-lag-in-nilpotent-center-manifold-reduction.md), let $v=2w+h_3(z,w)+O(5)$. Invariance of the [center manifold](../../../../../../center-manifold.md) requires

$$
w\partial_z h_3-{2\over9}(z-2w)^3=-{3\over2}h_3.
$$

For the [homogeneous polynomial](../../../../../../homogeneous-polynomial.md) $h_3=Az^3+Bz^2w+Czw^2+Dw^3$, comparison of coefficients successively gives

$$
A={4\over27},\quad B=-{32\over27},\quad C={272\over81},\quad D=-{832\over243}.
$$

Only $A$ is needed for the requested cubic [normal form](../../../../../../normal-form-dynamical-systems.md). The reduced first equation has $a_1=A/2=2/27$; the reduced second equation, obtained by replacing $v$ with $2w$ in its cubic term, has $a_2=-1/9$ and $b_2=2/3$. Therefore the [cubic elimination at a nilpotent double-zero point](../../../../../../cubic-elimination-at-a-nilpotent-double-zero-point.md) yields

$$
\boxed{P=-{1\over9},\qquad Q={2\over3}+3{2\over27}={8\over9}.}
$$

In particular, ignoring the lag and imposing $v=2w$ exactly would incorrectly give $Q=2/3$. The full cubic graph above provides a direct substitution check.
