<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md) of this reduction of the [FitzHugh-Nagumo model](../../../../../../fitzhugh-nagumo-model.md) at its trivial [equilibrium point](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is

$$
L=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\-1&-2&1\\-c/2&0&d/2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\det(mI-L)=m^3+(2-d/2)m^2+(1-d)m+(c-d)/2.
$$

A double zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) requires both the constant and linear coefficients to vanish. Hence **the codimension-two point is**

$$
\boxed{(c_*,d_*)=(1,1).}
$$

There the polynomial is $m^2(m+3/2)$. The [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $L$ is one-dimensional, so its zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) has a nontrivial size-two block in [Jordan normal form](../../../../../../jordan-normal-form.md). This is a reflection-symmetric double-zero point of [Bogdanov–Takens bifurcation](../../../../../../bogdanov-takens-bifurcation.md) type, with one stable direction; the nonlinear coefficients are computed below rather than assumed.
