<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $h=(h_1,h_2)$ for the homogeneous cubic correction and $A(x,y)^T=(y,0)^T$ for the nilpotent linear part. The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives the transformed cubic [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) as $f_3+Ah-Dh\,A(x,y)^T$. Thus the cubic coefficients in the first equation are

$$
a_1+\alpha_2,\quad b_1+\beta_2-3\alpha_1,\quad c_1+\gamma_2-2\beta_1,\quad d_1+\delta_2-\gamma_1,
$$

while those in the second equation are

$$
a_2,\quad b_2-3\alpha_2,\quad c_2-2\beta_2,\quad d_2-\gamma_2.
$$

Consequently one explicit [near-identity transformation](../../../../../../near-identity-transformation.md) is

$$
\alpha_2=-a_1,\quad\beta_2=c_2/2,\quad\gamma_2=d_2,\quad\delta_2=0,\qquad
\alpha_1={b_1+c_2/2\over3},\quad\beta_1={c_1+d_2\over2},\quad\gamma_1=d_1,\quad\delta_1=0.
$$

The transformed equations have no cubic correction in $\dot x$, and only $x^3,x^2y$ survive in $\dot y$. **The cubic coefficients are**

$$
\boxed{P=a_2,\qquad Q=b_2+3a_1.}
$$

This is the [cubic elimination at a nilpotent double-zero point](../../../../../../cubic-elimination-at-a-nilpotent-double-zero-point.md); the extra $3a_1$ is the [nilpotent cubic damping invariant](../../../../../../nilpotent-cubic-damping-invariant.md) contribution from the first equation. Higher-order terms generated by the substitution are outside the cubic truncation.
