<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Count incidences of a treatment with a block containing it. Summing over blocks gives $bk$, while summing over treatments gives $tr$. Hence the [balanced incomplete block design](../../../../../../balanced-incomplete-block-design.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{bk=tr.}
$$
