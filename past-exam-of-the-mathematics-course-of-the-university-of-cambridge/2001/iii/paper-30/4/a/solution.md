<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Symmetry forces $r=k$, and the pair-counting identity becomes $k(k-1)=2\cdot45=90$. Its only positive integral solution is $k=r=10$. Since the number of blocks is even, the [even-order symmetric design square obstruction](../../../../../../even-order-symmetric-design-square-obstruction.md) requires $r-\lambda=8$ to be a square. It is not. **No design with these parameters exists.**
