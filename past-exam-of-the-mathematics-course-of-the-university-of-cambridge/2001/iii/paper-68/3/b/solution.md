<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $g=\det(g_{ab})$. The [Jacobi formula](../../../../../../jacobi-s-formula.md) gives $\delta g=g\,g^{ab}\delta g_{ab}$. Since a [Lorentzian metric](../../../../../../lorentzian-metric.md) of signature $(+---)$ has negative [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md),

$$
\boxed{\delta\sqrt{-g}
=\frac12\sqrt{-g}\,g^{ab}\delta g_{ab}.}
$$

The [variation of metric volume density](../../../../../../variation-of-metric-volume-density.md) therefore has the opposite sign when expressed using the inverse [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md): $\delta\sqrt{-g}=-\sqrt{-g}\,g_{ab}\delta g^{ab}/2$. This follows immediately from the [variation of inverse metric](../../../../../../variation-of-inverse-metric.md) and explains the volume term in the field-equation derivation.
