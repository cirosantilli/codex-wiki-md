<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiate $g^{ac}g_{cb}=\delta^a_b$ while holding the coordinate system fixed. The [variation of inverse metric](../../../../../../variation-of-inverse-metric.md) obeys

$$
(\delta g^{ac})g_{cb}+g^{ac}\delta g_{cb}=0.
$$

Multiplication by the inverse [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\delta g^{cd}=-g^{ca}g^{db}\delta g_{ab}.}
$$

This is the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) inverse [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) written with [tensor](../../../../../../tensor.md) indices. A variation of the [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) is symmetric, so the right-hand side is symmetric in $c,d$.
