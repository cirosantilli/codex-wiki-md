<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Retain the identification with the [left regular action](../../../../../../left-regular-action.md). A [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) [group automorphism](../../../../../../group-automorphism.md) $\varphi$ of $G$ satisfies $\varphi L_g\varphi^{-1}=L_{\varphi(g)}$, so it normalizes $L(G)$ and fixes the identity point.

Conversely let a normalizing [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) $n$ fix the identity. Its conjugation on $L(G)$ induces a [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) [group automorphism](../../../../../../group-automorphism.md) $\varphi$ characterized by $nL_gn^{-1}=L_{\varphi(g)}$. Evaluating at the identity gives $n(g)=\varphi(g)$, so the whole identity stabilizer in the [normalizer](../../../../../../normalizer.md) is exactly $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$. For arbitrary $n$, put $t=n(1)$. Then $L_t^{-1}n$ fixes the identity and is an [group automorphism](../../../../../../group-automorphism.md); thus every element has a unique expression $L_t\varphi$.

The translation [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) is normal in its [normalizer](../../../../../../normalizer.md), its intersection with the [group automorphism](../../../../../../group-automorphism.md) [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) is trivial, and the multiplication rule is

$$
(L_t\varphi)(L_u\psi)=L_{t\varphi(u)}\varphi\psi.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{N_{\operatorname{Sym}(\Omega)}(G)\cong G\rtimes\operatorname{Aut}(G)=\operatorname{Hol}(G).}
$$

The action in this [semidirect product](../../../../../../semidirect-product.md) is the natural evaluation of [group automorphisms](../../../../../../group-automorphism.md) on $G$. This [normalizer](../../../../../../normalizer.md) is the [holomorph](../../../../../../holomorph.md), realized as all [permutations](../../../../../../permutation.md) $x\mapsto t\varphi(x)$. The decomposition is established from the [regular group action](../../../../../../regular-group-action.md) itself rather than only from an order comparison.
