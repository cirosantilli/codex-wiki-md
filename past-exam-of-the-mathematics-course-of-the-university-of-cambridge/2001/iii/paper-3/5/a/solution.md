<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The orbits of a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) form a [block system](../../../../../../block-system.md): $g(N\omega)=N(g\omega)$, so every [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) element permutes these orbits. By primitivity the orbits are either all singletons or one whole orbit. In the singleton case $N$ fixes every point, giving $N\leq G_{[\Omega]}$.

In the other case $N$ is transitive. For each $g\in G$ choose $n\in N$ with $n\alpha=g\alpha$. Then $n^{-1}g\in G_\alpha$, so $G=NG_\alpha$. Work in the quotient $G/N$. If $g=nh$ with $h\in G_\alpha$, the image of $A^g=g^{-1}Ag$ is the image of $A^h$, which equals the image $\overline A$ of $A$ because $A\triangleleft G_\alpha$. All conjugates of $A$ thus have the same image in $G/N$.

By hypothesis those conjugates generate $G$, so the quotient is generated by the single [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $\overline A$, and equals it. Since $A$ is abelian, $G/N$ is abelian. The [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) of a [group homomorphism](../../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) to an [abelian group](../../../../../../abelian-group.md) contains the [commutator subgroup](../../../../../../commutator-subgroup.md), yielding

$$
\boxed{G'\leq N\quad\text{or}\quad N\leq G_{[\Omega]}.}
$$

This is the normal-subgroup argument behind the [Iwasawa simplicity lemma](../../../../../../iwasawa-simplicity-lemma.md). Faithfulness is not needed for this version: retaining the pointwise [kernel of a group homomorphism](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) as the second alternative is essential. In a faithful [primitive group action](../../../../../../primitive-group-action.md) it says every nontrivial [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md) contains $G'$.
