<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Complex conjugation preserves the real [Kähler form](../../../../../../kahler-form.md) and interchanges $\partial,\bar\partial$ and their formal adjoints. Conjugating the permitted identity among the [Kähler identities](../../../../../../kahler-identities.md) therefore yields $[\Lambda,\bar\partial]=-i\partial^*$. The same nilpotence calculation as in part (a) gives $\bar\partial\partial^*+\partial^*\bar\partial=0$. Now expand $d=\partial+\bar\partial$ and $d^*=\partial^*+\bar\partial^*$:

$$
\Delta_d=\Delta_\partial+\Delta_{\bar\partial}
+\partial\bar\partial^*+\bar\partial^*\partial
+\bar\partial\partial^*+\partial^*\bar\partial.
$$

Both mixed anticommutators vanish, proving **$\boxed{\Delta_d=\Delta_\partial+\Delta_{\bar\partial}}$**.
