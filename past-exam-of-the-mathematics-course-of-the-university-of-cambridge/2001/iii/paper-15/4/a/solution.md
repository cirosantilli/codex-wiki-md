<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

On the [Kähler manifold](../../../../../../kahler-manifold.md) define $L\alpha=\omega\wedge\alpha$ using the [Kähler form](../../../../../../kahler-form.md); the operator $\Lambda=L^*$ is the [adjoint Lefschetz operator](../../../../../../adjoint-lefschetz-operator.md), the pointwise contraction adjoint to wedging with $\omega$. It lowers bidegree by $(1,1)$. Set

$$
\Delta_d=dd^*+d^*d,\qquad \Delta_\partial=\partial\partial^*+\partial^*\partial,
$$

and keep $\Delta_{\bar\partial}$ as above. These are the [Hodge Laplacian](../../../../../../hodge-laplacian.md) and the holomorphic and antiholomorphic Laplacians respectively. The permitted identity among the [Kähler identities](../../../../../../kahler-identities.md) $[\Lambda,\partial]=i\bar\partial^*$ gives $\bar\partial^*=-i[\Lambda,\partial]$. Expanding rather than assuming the desired anticommutation,

$$
\partial\bar\partial^*+\bar\partial^*\partial
=-i\bigl(\partial\Lambda\partial-\partial^2\Lambda+\Lambda\partial^2-\partial\Lambda\partial\bigr)=0,
$$

because $\partial^2=0$. Hence **$\boxed{\partial\bar\partial^*+\bar\partial^*\partial=0}$**.
