↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**Anticommuting-variable dependence alone is not the defining condition:** a [superfield](../../../../../../superfield.md) must carry a specified [Super-Poincaré group](../../../../../../super-poincare-group.md) transformation law. Any appropriately graded function on [superspace](../../../../../../superspace.md) becomes an unconstrained scalar [superfield](../../../../../../superfield.md) under coordinate pullback, but an arbitrary collection of coefficients in a [Grassmann variable](../../../../../../grassmann-variable.md) polynomial is not automatically a prescribed [supermultiplet](../../../../../../supermultiplet.md). For example, a [chiral superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) has $\Phi(y,\theta)=A(y)+\sqrt2\theta\psi(y)+\theta^2F(y)$, with $y^\mu=x^\mu+i\theta\sigma^\mu\bar\theta$ and $\bar D_{\dot\alpha}\Phi=0$; a [vector superfield](../../../../../../vector-superfield.md) obeys $V=V^\dagger$ and contains a gauge vector, [gaugino](../../../../../../gaugino.md) and [auxiliary field](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) in [Wess-Zumino gauge](../../../../../../wess-zumino-gauge.md). A polynomial with constant component fields can be a special [chiral superfield](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md); lack of explicit spacetime dependence alone does not disqualify it. This is [superfield covariance is not Grassmann dependence alone](../../../../../../superfield-covariance-is-not-grassmann-dependence-alone.md).
