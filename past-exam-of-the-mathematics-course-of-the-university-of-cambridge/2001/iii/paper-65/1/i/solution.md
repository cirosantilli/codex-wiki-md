<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Three distinct motivations are **stabilizing the weak scale, improving gauge-coupling unification, and supplying dark matter**. [Supersymmetric cancellation of quadratic divergences](../../../../../../supersymmetric-cancellation-of-quadratic-divergences.md) protects the [Higgs field](../../../../../../higgs-field.md) from the [hierarchy problem](../../../../../../hierarchy-problem.md); [soft supersymmetry breaking](../../../../../../soft-supersymmetry-breaking.md) near the weak scale keeps the residual corrections modest. The [MSSM](../../../../../../minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md) particle content improves [gauge coupling unification](../../../../../../gauge-coupling-unification.md) under the usual assumptions about intermediate thresholds. Conserved [R-parity](../../../../../../r-parity.md) can make a neutral [lightest supersymmetric particle](../../../../../../lightest-supersymmetric-particle.md), such as a [neutralino](../../../../../../neutralino.md), stable and a possible weak-scale [dark matter](../../../../../../dark-matter.md) candidate. These are motivations, not guaranteed predictions. If [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) is instead broken at much higher energies, it can still organize [string theory](../../../../../../string-theory-split.md) and [supergravity](../../../../../../supergravity.md), and it remains a powerful tool for nonperturbative [quantum field theory](../../../../../../quantum-field-theory-split.md), as illustrated by [Seiberg–Witten theory](../../../../../../seiberg-witten-theory.md).
