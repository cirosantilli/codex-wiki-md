<h1 id="1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Specify the [gauge group](../../../../../../gauge-group.md), its [gauge group representations](../../../../../../gauge-group-representation.md) and gaugings, a real [Kähler potential](../../../../../../kahler-potential.md) $K$, a [holomorphic superpotential](../../../../../../superpotential.md) $W$, and a symmetric [holomorphic](../../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) [gauge kinetic function](../../../../../../gauge-kinetic-function.md) $f_{ab}$, with positive kinetic matrices. The latter includes the [gauge couplings](../../../../../../gauge-coupling.md) and theta angles; allowed Abelian [Fayet–Iliopoulos terms](../../../../../../fayet-iliopoulos-term.md) require constants $\xi_a$. For local [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md), also specify the [Planck mass](../../../../../../planck-mass.md); [constant Fayet–Iliopoulos terms in conventional N=1 supergravity](../../../../../../constant-fayet-iliopoulos-terms-in-conventional-n-1-supergravity.md) require a compatible gauged [R-symmetry](../../../../../../r-symmetry.md) and transformation of $W$. **$W$ and $f$ are holomorphic; $K$ generally is not.** [Radiative corrections](../../../../../../radiative-correction.md) generally change $K$. The [Wilsonian effective action](../../../../../../wilsonian-effective-action.md) obeys [superpotential non-renormalization](../../../../../../non-renormalization-theorem.md) perturbatively, although physical couplings run through field normalization and nonperturbative corrections can change $W$. The [gauge kinetic function](../../../../../../gauge-kinetic-function.md) does receive corrections while remaining [holomorphic](../../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md); its perturbative Wilsonian running has [one-loop exactness of the holomorphic gauge kinetic function](../../../../../../one-loop-exactness-of-the-holomorphic-gauge-kinetic-function.md), not all-order constancy of the physical [gauge coupling](../../../../../../gauge-coupling.md).
