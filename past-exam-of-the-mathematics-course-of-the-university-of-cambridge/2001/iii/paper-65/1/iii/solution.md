The [super-Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../super-higgs-mechanism.md) is **the absorption of the goldstino by the gravitino when local supersymmetry is spontaneously broken**. In rigid [supersymmetry breaking](../../../../../../supersymmetry-breaking.md), the [goldstino](../../../../../../goldstino.md) is a physical massless [fermion](../../../../../../fermion.md). In [supergravity](../../../../../../supergravity.md), its transformation permits a gauge choice removing it; its two degrees of freedom become the longitudinal [helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md) $\pm\tfrac12$ states of the massive [gravitino](../../../../../../gravitino.md). The massless [gravitino](../../../../../../gravitino.md) has helicities $\pm\tfrac32$, whereas the massive [gravitino](../../../../../../gravitino.md) has four spin states. This is distinct from the [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md), which gives an internal gauge vector a mass and need not break [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md).
