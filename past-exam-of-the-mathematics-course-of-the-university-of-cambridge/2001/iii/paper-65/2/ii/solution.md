<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [massless supermultiplet](../../../../../../massless-supermultiplet.md), put the momentum along the third axis. The matrix $2\sigma\cdot p$ in the [supersymmetry algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md) has rank one, so only one fermionic oscillator acts nontrivially. It relates states whose [helicities](../../../../../../helicity.md) differ by $\tfrac12$. Including the antiparticles required by [CPT completion of a supermultiplet](../../../../../../cpt-completion-of-a-supermultiplet.md) gives the following complete spectra.

A [chiral multiplet](../../../../../../chiral-multiplet.md) contains a complex scalar $A$ and a [Weyl spinor](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) $\psi$. Its scalar particle and antiparticle have helicity $0$; its [fermion](../../../../../../fermion.md) particle and antiparticle have helicities $-\tfrac12$ and $+\tfrac12$. Therefore **$n_B=2=n_F$**, with maximum absolute [helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md) $\tfrac12$.

A [supersymmetric vector multiplet](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) contains a massless gauge vector $A_\mu$, with helicities $+1,-1$, and a [Majorana spinor](../../../../../../majorana-spinor.md) [gaugino](../../../../../../gaugino.md) $\lambda$, with helicities $+\tfrac12,-\tfrac12$. Again **$n_B=2=n_F$**, now with maximum absolute [helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md) $1$. The chiral $F$ and vector $D$ [auxiliary fields](../../../../../../auxiliary-field.md) contribute no on-shell states.

**The Higgs mechanism is compatible with unbroken supersymmetry.** A [supersymmetric vacuum](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vacuum.md) may have nonzero scalar [vacuum expectation values](../../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) that break the internal [gauge symmetry](../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md) while obeying both [F-flatness](../../../../../../f-flatness.md) and [D-flatness](../../../../../../d-flatness.md). For example, oppositely charged [chiral superfields](../../../../../../chiral-superfield.md) with $W=0$, no [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](../../../../../../fayet-iliopoulos-term.md) and equal nonzero scalar magnitudes have $F=D=0$ but break the Abelian [gauge group](../../../../../../gauge-group.md). In the [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](../../../../../../supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md), a massless [vector multiplet](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) combines with the [chiral multiplet](../../../../../../chiral-multiplet.md) along the broken gauge direction. One real scalar becomes the longitudinal vector polarization; the other remains a physical real scalar, and the [gaugino](../../../../../../gaugino.md) combines with the [Higgsino](../../../../../../higgsino.md) into a [Dirac spinor](../../../../../../dirac-spinor.md). The resulting massive [vector multiplet](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) has $3+1=4$ [bosonic](../../../../../../boson.md) and $4$ [fermionic](../../../../../../fermion.md) states with a common mass, as in part (i). Any other uneaten [chiral multiplets](../../../../../../chiral-multiplet.md) remain separately in the spectrum.
