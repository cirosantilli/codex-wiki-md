<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Each separately conserved component obeys the [constant-equation-of-state density scaling](../../../../../../constant-equation-of-state-density-scaling.md) $\rho\propto a^{-3(1+w)}$. For the flat [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), the [Friedmann equations](../../../../../../friedmann-equations.md) therefore give

$$
\frac{H(z)^2}{H_0^2}=\Omega_m(1+z)^3+\Omega_r(1+z)^4+\Omega_X(1+z)^{3(1+w)}.
$$

Write $H(z)/H_0=1+h_1z+O(z^2)$, where $h_1=[3\Omega_m+4\Omega_r+3(1+w)\Omega_X]/2$. Expanding the reciprocal inside the [luminosity distance](../../../../../../luminosity-distance.md) integral gives

$$
H_0d_L=(1+z)\left[z-\frac{h_1}2z^2+O(z^3)\right]
=z+\left(1-\frac{h_1}2\right)z^2+O(z^3).
$$

Matching its quadratic coefficient gives the [deceleration parameter](../../../../../../deceleration-parameter.md) $q_0=h_1-1$. Using flatness in this part,

$$
\boxed{q_0=\frac{\Omega_m}2+\Omega_r+\frac{1+3w}{2}\Omega_X
=\frac12\left(1+\Omega_r+3w\Omega_X\right).}
$$

A negative [deceleration parameter](../../../../../../deceleration-parameter.md) signifies accelerated expansion; negative pressure must overcome the positive matter and radiation terms. Restoring units replaces $H_0d_L$ by $H_0d_L/c$ in the expansion, leaving $q_0$ unchanged.
