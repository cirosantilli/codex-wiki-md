↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [SU(5) grand unified theory](../../../../../../../su-5-grand-unified-theory.md) has [simply connected space](../../../../../../../simply-connected-space.md) $G=SU(5)$ and a connected [Standard Model](../../../../../../../standard-model-split.md) subgroup. More precisely that subgroup is $(SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1))/\mathbb Z_6$; the product notation suppresses a finite central quotient. The [long exact sequence of homotopy groups of a fibration](../../../../../../../long-exact-sequence-of-homotopy-groups-of-a-fibration.md) $H\to G\to G/H$ gives

$$
\pi_2(G/H)\simeq\pi_1(H)\simeq\mathbb Z,\qquad
\pi_1(G/H)\simeq\pi_0(H)=0.
$$

Thus stable [magnetic monopoles](../../../../../../../magnetic-monopole.md) form, whereas this transition does not require walls or strings. The [monopole core and mass scales in an SU(5) transition](../../../../../../../monopole-core-and-mass-scales-in-an-su-5-transition.md) are set by the massive gauge and [scalar fields](../../../../../../../scalar-field.md):

$$
\boxed{r_V\sim(g\eta)^{-1},\qquad r_\phi\sim(\sqrt\lambda\eta)^{-1},\qquad
M_M\sim\frac{4\pi\eta}{g}\times\text{an order-one profile factor}.}
$$

For order-one couplings, the core size is of order $\eta^{-1}$ and the mass is of order $\eta$ up to the often substantial factor $4\pi/g$. These heavy objects are nonrelativistic soon after formation and can survive as stable relics.
