<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a vacuum [order parameter](../../../../../../../order-parameter.md) $\Phi_0$ and let $H$ be its [stabilizer](../../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) in $G$. Acting with $G$ produces all symmetry-related minima; two elements produce the same minimum precisely when they differ by an element of $H$. The [vacuum manifold](../../../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{M\simeq G/H.}
$$

This assumes a single transitive orbit of minima; additional independent vacuum sectors would require more than one orbit. For a local [gauge symmetry](../../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md), $G/H$ describes the vacuum directions used in finite-energy boundary conditions and defect classification, rather than distinct observable vacua after quotienting by every gauge redundancy.
