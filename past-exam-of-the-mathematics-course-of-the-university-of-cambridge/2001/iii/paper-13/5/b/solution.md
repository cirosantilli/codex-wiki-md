<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Identify $S^{2b-1}$ with the unit sphere in $\mathbb C^b$, and put $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/a}$. The generator of the [cyclic group](../../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z/a$ acts by

$$
(z_1,\ldots,z_b)\longmapsto(\zeta z_1,\ldots,\zeta z_b).
$$

It preserves the sphere because $|\zeta|=1$. If its $k$th power fixes a point, then $(\zeta^k-1)z_j=0$ for every $j$. Some coordinate is nonzero, so $\zeta^k=1$, meaning $k\equiv0\pmod a$. Thus **the scalar root-of-unity action is free**. The case $a=1$ is the trivial [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) and also satisfies freeness. The quotient is a standard [lens space](../../../../../../lens-space.md).
