<h1 id="1c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $f(a)=f(a')$, applying $g$ gives $(g\circ f)(a)=(g\circ f)(a')$. The [injectivity](../../../../../../injective-function.md) of $g\circ f$ implies $a=a'$, so $f$ is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md).
