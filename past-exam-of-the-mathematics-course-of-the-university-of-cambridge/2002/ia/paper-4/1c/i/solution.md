<h1 id="1c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $c\in C$. Since $g$ is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), choose $b\in B$ with $g(b)=c$. Since $f$ is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), choose $a\in A$ with $f(a)=b$. Then $(g\circ f)(a)=g(b)=c$. Thus every element of $C$ has a preimage, proving that $g\circ f$ is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md).
