<h1 id="1c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For any $c\in C$, the [surjectivity](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) of $g\circ f$ supplies $a\in A$ with $g(f(a))=c$. Therefore $f(a)\in B$ is a preimage of $c$ under $g$, proving [surjectivity](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) of $g$.

For the requested counterexample, take $A=C=\{0\}$, $B=\{0,1\}$, $f(0)=0$, and $g(0)=g(1)=0$. The [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) is the identity [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) of the singleton, but $f$ is not [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) and $g$ is not [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md).
