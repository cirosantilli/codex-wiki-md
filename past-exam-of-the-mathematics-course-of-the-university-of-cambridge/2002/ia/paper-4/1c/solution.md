An [injective function](../../../../../injective-function.md) satisfies $f(a)=f(a')\Rightarrow a=a'$ for all $a,a'\in A$. A [surjective function](../../../../../surjective-function.md) $g:A\to B$ satisfies: for every $b\in B$, some $a\in A$ has $g(a)=b$. The following arguments apply to [function composition](../../../../../function-composition.md) with the stated domains and codomains.
