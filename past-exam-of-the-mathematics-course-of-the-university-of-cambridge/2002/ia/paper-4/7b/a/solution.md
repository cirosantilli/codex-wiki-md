<h1 id="7b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For each $1\le j\le p-1$, [Fermat's little theorem](../../../../../../fermat-little-theorem.md) gives $j^p\equiv j\pmod p$. Since $p$ is odd,

$$
\boxed{\sum_{j=1}^{p-1}j^p\equiv\sum_{j=1}^{p-1}j=\frac{p(p-1)}2\equiv0\pmod p}.
$$
