<h1 id="6f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take the finite ambient [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $S=\bigcup_iA_i$ and the additive [set function](../../../../../../set-function.md) $f(A)=|A|$. Disjoint subsets have additive [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md), so (a) applies and gives

$$
\boxed{\left|\bigcup_{i=1}^nA_i\right|=\sum_{\varnothing\ne J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}}(-1)^{|J|+1}\left|\bigcap_{j\in J}A_j\right|}.
$$

This is the finite [inclusion-exclusion principle](../../../../../../inclusion-exclusion-principle.md).
