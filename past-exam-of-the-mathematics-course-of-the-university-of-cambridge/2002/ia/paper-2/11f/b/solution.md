<h1 id="11f/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Conditioning on $U=X+Y$, [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of $Z$ gives $\mathbb P(Z>u)=1-u$ for $0\le u\le1$ and zero for $u>1$. Using the preceding [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(Z>X+Y)=\int_0^1(1-u)u\,du=\frac12-\frac13=\frac16.}
$$
