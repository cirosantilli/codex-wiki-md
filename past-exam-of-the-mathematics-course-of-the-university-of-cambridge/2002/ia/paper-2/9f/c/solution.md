<h1 id="9f/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Two [independent events](../../../../../../independent-events.md) satisfy **$\mathbb P(A\cap B)=\mathbb P(A)\mathbb P(B)$**. If $\mathbb P(B)>0$, this is equivalent to $\mathbb P(A\mid B)=\mathbb P(A)$: learning that $B$ occurred does not change the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of $A$. The product definition also covers zero-probability events without dividing by zero.
