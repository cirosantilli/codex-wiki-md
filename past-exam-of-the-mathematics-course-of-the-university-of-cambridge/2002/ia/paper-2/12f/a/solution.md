↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [branching process](../../../../../../branching-process.md) models reproduction through successive generations. In the [Galton-Watson process](../../../../../../galton-watson-process.md) used here, every individual independently produces a nonnegative integer number of offspring with a common [offspring distribution](../../../../../../offspring-distribution-of-a-branching-process.md); these reproduction counts are independent across individuals and generations. If $X_n$ is the generation size, then

$$
X_{n+1}=\sum_{j=1}^{X_n}\xi_{n,j},
$$

where the $\xi_{n,j}$ have that distribution and the empty sum is zero. Thus **zero is absorbing**: once a generation is empty, every subsequent generation is empty.
