<h1 id="7b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take positive angles counterclockwise. The [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) sends the [standard basis](../../../../../../standard-basis.md) [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) to $(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha)$ and $(-\sin\alpha,\cos\alpha)$. These images form the columns of its [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md), so

$$
\boxed{R_2(\alpha)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\alpha&-\sin\alpha\\\sin\alpha&\cos\alpha\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The columns are an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) and the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is one, as required for a plane [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md).
