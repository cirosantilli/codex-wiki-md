<h1 id="1b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a finite [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) $G$ acting on a [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $X$, write $G_x=\{g\in G:gx=x\}$ for the [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) and $Gx=\{gx:g\in G\}$ for the [orbit](../../../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) of $x$. The [orbit-stabilizer theorem](../../../../../../orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{|G|=|G_x|\,|Gx|.}
$$

Indeed, $gG_x\mapsto gx$ is a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) from the left [cosets](../../../../../../coset.md) of $G_x$ onto $Gx$: two images agree precisely when $h^{-1}g\in G_x$. Each [coset](../../../../../../coset.md) has $|G_x|$ elements, proving the formula.
