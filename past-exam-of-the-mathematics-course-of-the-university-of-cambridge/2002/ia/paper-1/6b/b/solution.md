<h1 id="6b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) fixes $0,1,\infty$, but sends $i$ to $-i$, so it is not the identity. The preceding classification of [fixed points of a Möbius transformation](../../../../../../fixed-point-of-a-mobius-transformation.md) says that a nonidentity [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) has at most two [fixed points](../../../../../../fixed-point.md). Since [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) has at least three, **it cannot be a [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md)**.
