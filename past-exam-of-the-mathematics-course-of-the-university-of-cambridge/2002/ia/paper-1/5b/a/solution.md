<h1 id="5b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the scalene triangle vertex [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $T=\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,2)\}$. Its edge lengths are $1,2,\sqrt5$. Each vertex has a different unordered pair of incident edge lengths. A [Euclidean isometry](../../../../../../euclidean-isometry.md) preserving the [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) must preserve these pairs, and therefore fixes every vertex.

To justify [rigidity of a scalene triangle](../../../../../../rigidity-of-a-scalene-triangle.md) fully, [Euclidean distances](../../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) from an arbitrary plane point to these three [fixed points](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) determine that point uniquely: subtracting its squared [Euclidean distance](../../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) to the origin from the other two squared [Euclidean distances](../../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) determines its two coordinates. Thus an [isometry](../../../../../../isometry.md) fixing the three vertices fixes every plane point. **Only the identity preserves $T$.**

For the general union of [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) translates, let $h\in G$. Left multiplication by $h$ permutes $G$, so

$$
h(S)=\bigcup_{g\in G}hg(T)=\bigcup_{k\in G}k(T)=S.
$$

Therefore **$G\subseteq H$**. This containment uses only the [group action](../../../../../../group-action.md); the absence of symmetries of $T$ is not needed for the containment itself.
