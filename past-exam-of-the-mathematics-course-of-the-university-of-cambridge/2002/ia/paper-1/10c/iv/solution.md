<h1 id="10c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use the [reduced-denominator function unbounded on every interval](../../../../../../reduced-denominator-function-unbounded-on-every-interval.md):

$$
\boxed{f_4(x)=\begin{cases}q,&x=p/q\text{ in lowest terms},\ q>0,\\0,&x\notin\mathbb Q.\end{cases}}
$$

Every value is a finite [real number](../../../../../../real-number.md). In any nondegenerate interval $[a,b]\subseteq[0,1]$, its interior contains infinitely many [rational numbers](../../../../../../rational-number.md), whereas there are only finitely many reduced fractions in $[0,1]$ with denominator at most any fixed $M$. Some [rational number](../../../../../../rational-number.md) in $(a,b)$ therefore has denominator exceeding $M$. Hence the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is unbounded on every such interval. It reverses the positive values of the [Thomae function](../../../../../../thomae-function.md) rather than requiring an infinite value at any point.
