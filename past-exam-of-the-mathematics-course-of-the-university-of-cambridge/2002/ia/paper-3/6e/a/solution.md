<h1 id="6e/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set $w=(x-y)/2$. The sum condition then gives $x=a+w$ and $y=a-w$, and their [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) is

$$
x\cdot y=(a+w)\cdot(a-w)=|a|^2-|w|^2.
$$

Therefore $|w|^2=|a|^2-c$. Since $c<|a|^2$, this is a strictly positive squared radius. The complete solution is

$$
\boxed{x=a+Rn,\qquad y=a-Rn,\qquad R=\sqrt{|a|^2-c},\qquad |n|=1.}
$$

Every choice of [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) $n$ satisfies both conditions, and every solution has this form. The endpoints lie on the [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) with center $a$ and radius $R$, and their midpoint is $a$. They are therefore opposite ends of one diameter of that [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md).
