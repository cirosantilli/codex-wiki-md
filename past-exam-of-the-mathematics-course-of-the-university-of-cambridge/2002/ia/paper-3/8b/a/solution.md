<h1 id="8b/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The three known vertices have pairwise squared distances $8$, so the [regular tetrahedron](../../../../../../regular-tetrahedron.md) must have every squared edge length equal to $8$. Comparing the squared distance from $d$ to $b$ with that from $d$ to $a$ gives

$$
|d-b|^2-|d-a|^2
=4+(x+1)^2-(x-1)^2=4+4x.
$$

Equality forces $x=-1$. Similarly,

$$
|d-c|^2-|d-a|^2=4+(y+1)^2-(y-1)^2=4+4y,
$$

so $y=-1$. The resulting point has squared distance $8$ from each of the other three vertices. Therefore

$$
\boxed{x=y=-1,\qquad d=(1,-1,-1).}
$$
