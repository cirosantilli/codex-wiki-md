<h1 id="3a/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an [exact differential](../../../../../../exact-differential.md) $M\,d\theta+N\,d\phi=dF$, equality of mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) requires $M_\phi=N_\theta$. Here the two [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) are

$$
M_\phi=-\sinh\theta\sin\phi,\qquad N_\theta=\sinh\theta\sin\phi.
$$

They are not identically equal, so the [differential form](../../../../../../differential-form-split.md) is not a [closed differential form](../../../../../../closed-differential-form.md) and hence **is not an exact differential**. Equality along special lines such as $\theta=0$ does not supply a potential on a two-dimensional open neighborhood, because every such neighborhood contains points where the two [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) differ.
