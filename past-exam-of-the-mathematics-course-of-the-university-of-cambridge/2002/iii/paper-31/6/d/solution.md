<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The PDF's subscript is $0$, and the [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) starts from zero. Thus $B_0=0$ almost surely and the process is exactly $B$ at every time. **Its law is [Wiener measure](../../../../../../wiener-measure.md) itself, with density $1$.** There is no random future coefficient or anticipating transformation in this printed case.
