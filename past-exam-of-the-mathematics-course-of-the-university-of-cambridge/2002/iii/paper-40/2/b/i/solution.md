<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the [indicator function](../../../../../../../indicator-function.md) $\theta(x)=\mathbf1_{\{x\geq k\}}$. Its [expectation](../../../../../../../expected-value.md) under the standard [Cauchy distribution](../../../../../../../cauchy-distribution.md) is the desired upper-tail [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md). To use the proposed tail [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) in the remaining parts, **assume $k>0$**: a [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) proportional to $x^{-2}$ on $[k,\infty)$ is not normalizable when that interval contains zero.
