<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

From order $k+1$, set $z=a'_{k+1}$, remove that final coefficient, retain $a_i=a'_i$ for $1\leq i\leq k$, and restore the intercept by

$$
\boxed{a_0=a'_0+z\left(\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^{k+1}\right).}
$$

Keep $v$ fixed. This deterministically recovers the lower-order state and the auxiliary variable that would reproduce the higher-order state under a birth. The inverse map also has absolute [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) one. In particular, simply deleting the highest coefficient without restoring the intercept is not the reverse of the supplied move.
