<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Integrating out the second coordinate gives the [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) of the [marginal distribution](../../../../../../../marginal-distribution.md)

$$
f_{X_1}(x)=\int_0^{\sqrt{h(x)}}\frac{2s}{H}\,ds=\frac{h(x)}H.
$$

Thus **the ratio has the normalized target [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md)**. The factor $s$ from the [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is essential; merely transforming the rectangular coordinates as if they remained uniform would give a different [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md). This is the basis of the [ratio-of-uniforms method](../../../../../../../ratio-of-uniforms-method.md).
