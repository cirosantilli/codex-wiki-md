<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The [RNS string](../../../../../spinning-string.md) adds [worldsheet Majorana fermions](../../../../../worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md) $\psi^\mu$ to the coordinate fields $X^\mu$. The new fields are spinors on the [worldsheet](../../../../../worldsheet.md) but vectors under the target [Lorentz group](../../../../../lorentz-group.md); this distinction matters when interpreting their oscillator states. In a flat target, a convenient Lorentzian convention for the free gauge-fixed action is

$$
S=-\frac1{4\pi\alpha'}\int d^2\sigma\,
\left(\partial_aX^\mu\partial^aX_\mu-i\bar\psi^\mu\rho^a\partial_a\psi_\mu\right),
$$

where $\rho^a$ are two-dimensional [gamma matrices](../../../../../gamma-matrices.md). The bosonic and fermionic kinetic terms are related by [worldsheet supersymmetry](../../../../../worldsheet-supersymmetry.md). Before fixing local [worldsheet supersymmetry](../../../../../worldsheet-supersymmetry.md), one introduces a [worldsheet gravitino](../../../../../worldsheet-gravitino.md) along with the [worldsheet metric](../../../../../worldsheet-metric.md). After [superconformal gauge](../../../../../superconformal-gauge.md) fixing, the residual matter constraints are the [Virasoro constraints](../../../../../virasoro-constraint.md) and the constraints generated by the [superconformal current](../../../../../superconformal-current.md) $G\propto\psi\cdot\partial X$.

Each real [worldsheet Majorana fermion](../../../../../worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md) contributes $1/2$ to the chiral [central charge](../../../../../central-charge.md), while each coordinate contributes one. The reparameterization [bc system](../../../../../bc-system.md) contributes $-26$, and fixing local [worldsheet supersymmetry](../../../../../worldsheet-supersymmetry.md) also produces commuting [superconformal ghosts](../../../../../superconformal-ghost.md) of weights $(3/2,-1/2)$, with [central charge](../../../../../central-charge.md) $11$. Thus

$$
c_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac32D-26+11=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{D=10}.
$$

In [light-cone gauge in string theory](../../../../../light-cone-gauge-in-string-theory.md) there are eight physical transverse coordinate fields and eight transverse [worldsheet Majorana fermions](../../../../../worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md).

On the doubled spatial interval of an [open string](../../../../../open-string.md), the two allowed fermionic periodicities are

$$
\psi(\sigma+2\pi)=+\psi(\sigma)\quad\text{(Ramond)},\qquad
\psi(\sigma+2\pi)=-\psi(\sigma)\quad\text{(Neveu--Schwarz)}.
$$

They arise from the relative signs of the left-right fermion gluing conditions at the two ends. Thus the [Ramond sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md) has integer-mode oscillators $d_n$, including $d_0$, and the [Neveu–Schwarz sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-sector.md) has half-integer oscillators $b_r$, $r\in\mathbb Z+1/2$, without zero modes. In either sector,

$$
\{\psi_r^\mu,\psi_s^\nu\}=\eta^{\mu\nu}\delta_{r+s,0}.
$$

A [closed string](../../../../../closed-string.md) has independent left- and right-moving periodicities, giving four sector choices rather than two.

The [Ramond zero-mode Clifford algebra](../../../../../ramond-zero-mode-clifford-algebra.md) is

$$
\{d_0^\mu,d_0^\nu\}=\eta^{\mu\nu},\qquad
\Gamma^\mu=\sqrt2\,d_0^\mu,\qquad
\{\Gamma^\mu,\Gamma^\nu\}=2\eta^{\mu\nu}.
$$

Hence its [ground states](../../../../../ground-state.md) carry a [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](../../../../../spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md). The zero-mode supercurrent constraint reduces on a ground state to the [Dirac equation](../../../../../dirac-equation.md) $p_\mu\Gamma^\mu u=0$. In the [Neveu–Schwarz sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-sector.md) the [oscillator vacuum](../../../../../oscillator-vacuum.md) instead carries a scalar representation. Consequently the [open string](../../../../../open-string.md) [NS sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-sector.md) describes spacetime [bosons](../../../../../boson.md), while the [R sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md) describes spacetime [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md). Exciting a target-vector oscillator does not change this statistics assignment.

The [string intercepts](../../../../../normal-ordering-constant-of-a-string.md) follow from the transverse [zero-point energy](../../../../../zero-point-energy.md). Per boson the integer-mode contribution is $\frac12\zeta(-1)=-1/24$. Per fermion it is the negative of the corresponding half-sum. Since $\zeta(-1,1/2)=1/24$, the eight transverse pairs give

$$
E_0^{\mathrm{NS}}=8\left(-\frac1{24}-\frac1{48}\right)=-\frac12,
\qquad
E_0^{\mathrm R}=8\left(-\frac1{24}+\frac1{24}\right)=0.
$$

Thus $a_{\mathrm{NS}}=1/2$, $a_{\mathrm R}=0$, and the [open string](../../../../../open-string.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md) formula is $\alpha'M^2=N-a$. This distinguishes the lowest states explicitly:

- In the [NS sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-sector.md), the level-zero scalar is a [tachyon](../../../../../tachyon.md) with $M^2=-1/(2\alpha')$. At level $1/2$, $\epsilon_\mu b_{-1/2}^\mu|0;p\rangle$ is a massless vector. The supercurrent and [Virasoro constraints](../../../../../virasoro-constraint.md) give $p\cdot\epsilon=0$ and the equivalence $\epsilon\sim\epsilon+\lambda p$. There are eight physical vector polarizations.
- In the [R sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md), the level-zero states are massless spacetime [spinors](../../../../../spinor.md) satisfying the [Dirac equation](../../../../../dirac-equation.md). Before the [GSO projection](../../../../../gso-projection.md), the two chiralities together give 16 on-shell polarizations; they split into the two eight-dimensional transverse spinor representations.

The supersymmetric [GSO projection](../../../../../gso-projection.md) keeps odd oscillator fermion number in the [NS sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-sector.md). Defining $F_{\mathrm{osc}}$ so that the NS vacuum has $F_{\mathrm{osc}}=0$, its projector is

$$
P_{\mathrm{NS}}=\frac12\left(1-(-1)^{F_{\mathrm{osc}}}\right).
$$

It removes the scalar [tachyon](../../../../../tachyon.md) and keeps the massless vector. In the [R sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md), fermion parity contains the zero-mode chirality operator, so one may write

$$
P_{\mathrm R}=\frac12\left(1+\eta\Gamma_{11}(-1)^{F_{\mathrm{osc}}}\right),\qquad \eta=\pm1.
$$

Here $F_{\mathrm{osc}}$ counts nonzero-mode fermionic excitations. On the [ground states](../../../../../ground-state.md), this keeps one [Majorana-Weyl spinor](../../../../../majorana-weyl-spinor.md) chirality: 16 real covariant components, or eight on-shell polarizations after the [Dirac equation](../../../../../dirac-equation.md). **The projected massless open-string states are an eight-polarization gauge vector and an eight-polarization gaugino.** The compatible projected tower exhibits spacetime [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md); unprojected [worldsheet supersymmetry](../../../../../worldsheet-supersymmetry.md) by itself did not eliminate the tachyon or equalize these massless counts.

For a [closed string](../../../../../closed-string.md), both chiral zero-mode constraints must hold:

$$
\boxed{M^2=\frac4{\alpha'}(N_L-a_L)=\frac4{\alpha'}(N_R-a_R)},\qquad
\boxed{N_L-a_L=N_R-a_R}.
$$

This shifted [closed-string level matching](../../../../../closed-string-level-matching.md) is particularly important in the mixed sectors. The lowest states and their spacetime nature are as follows.

- The [NS-NS sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-neveu-schwarz-sector.md) is bosonic. Before projection, $(N_L,N_R)=(0,0)$ is a scalar [tachyon](../../../../../tachyon.md) with $M^2=-2/\alpha'$. The [GSO projection](../../../../../gso-projection.md) on each side removes it. The surviving lowest level is $(1/2,1/2)$, with transverse states $b_{-1/2}^i\widetilde b_{-1/2}^j|0;p\rangle$. Decomposing their tensor product into symmetric traceless, antisymmetric and trace parts gives the [graviton](../../../../../graviton.md), [Kalb–Ramond field](../../../../../kalb-ramond-field.md) and [dilaton](../../../../../dilaton.md), with $35+28+1=64$ physical polarizations.
- The [RR sector](../../../../../ramond-ramond-sector.md) is bosonic, since it combines two spacetime [spinors](../../../../../spinor.md). Its lowest states have $(N_L,N_R)=(0,0)$ and are massless. The two [Dirac equations](../../../../../dirac-equation.md) on the bispinor polarizations identify them with on-shell differential-form field strengths. After projection they supply 64 bosonic polarizations. The actual form degrees depend on the two chosen [Ramond sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md) chiralities.
- The [NS-R sector](../../../../../neveu-schwarz-ramond-sector.md) and [R-NS sector](../../../../../ramond-neveu-schwarz-sector.md) are fermionic. Their respective lowest level-matched pairs are $(1/2,0)$ and $(0,1/2)$, not two oscillator vacua. A vector tensored with a chiral spinor decomposes into its gamma-traceless vector-spinor and gamma trace. These give a massless [gravitino](../../../../../gravitino.md) and [dilatino](../../../../../dilatino.md), with $56+8=64$ polarizations in each mixed sector. The vacuum pair would fail the shifted [closed-string level matching](../../../../../closed-string-level-matching.md), so it cannot be counted as a mixed-sector tachyon.

For [type IIA superstring theory](../../../../../type-iia-superstring-theory.md), the two [R sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md) ground-state chiralities are opposite. The [Ramond–Ramond potentials](../../../../../ramond-ramond-potential.md) can be represented by $C_1,C_3$, with transverse counts $8+56=64$. The two mixed-sector [gravitinos](../../../../../gravitino.md) have opposite chiralities, and the ten-dimensional theory is nonchiral. For [type IIB superstring theory](../../../../../type-iib-superstring-theory.md), the two [R sector](../../../../../ramond-sector.md) chiralities agree; the [Ramond–Ramond potentials](../../../../../ramond-ramond-potential.md) are $C_0,C_2,C_4$, with self-dual five-form field strength, giving $1+28+35=64$. Its two [gravitinos](../../../../../gravitino.md) have the same chirality. Thus **both projected type II theories have 128 massless bosonic and 128 massless fermionic polarizations and no tachyon**, while their chiralities and form spectra differ.

If unoriented [open strings](../../../../../open-string.md) are included in the complete theory, the supersymmetric example is [type I string theory](../../../../../type-i-string-theory.md). Worldsheet orientation reversal projects the closed [type IIB superstring theory](../../../../../type-iib-superstring-theory.md) spectrum and identifies the two mixed sectors; the surviving closed fields include the [graviton](../../../../../graviton.md), [dilaton](../../../../../dilaton.md), a [Ramond–Ramond potential](../../../../../ramond-ramond-potential.md) $C_2$, and one [gravitino](../../../../../gravitino.md) and [dilatino](../../../../../dilatino.md). The open states form vector and gaugino multiplets with [Chan-Paton factors](../../../../../chan-paton-factor.md); tadpole and anomaly cancellation select $SO(32)$. This is an additional orientation projection, not part of the [GSO projection](../../../../../gso-projection.md) itself. The [heterotic string](../../../../../heterotic-string.md) instead pairs one supersymmetric chiral side with a bosonic chiral side carrying an internal gauge sector; it does not have a Ramond choice on both sides.
