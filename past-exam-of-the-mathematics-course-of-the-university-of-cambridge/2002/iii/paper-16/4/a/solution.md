<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\pi:T^*N\to N$ be the projection and $s_\alpha(q)=(q,\alpha_q)$ the section with image $\Gamma_\alpha$. For the [canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle](../../../../../../canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md),

$$
\theta_{(q,p)}(v)=p(d\pi(v)).
$$

Since $\pi\circ s_\alpha=\mathrm{id}$, evaluation on a tangent vector $v\in T_qN$ gives

$$
(s_\alpha^*\theta)_q(v)=\alpha_q(v),\qquad s_\alpha^*\theta=\alpha.
$$

Use the same position-first [standard symplectic form](../../../../../../standard-symplectic-form.md) as before, $\omega=-d\theta$. Pullback commutes with the [exterior derivative](../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md), so

$$
s_\alpha^*\omega=-d\alpha.
$$

The [graph of a differential one-form](../../../../../../graph-of-a-differential-one-form.md) is an embedded submanifold, because its projection is the inverse of the section on the graph. Its dimension is $\dim N$, half that of $T^*N$. Therefore the vanishing of the pulled-back symplectic form is exactly the [Lagrangian submanifold](../../../../../../lagrangian-submanifold.md) condition, and

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_\alpha\text{ is Lagrangian}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad d\alpha=0}.
$$

This proves that the [graph of a closed one-form is Lagrangian](../../../../../../graph-of-a-closed-one-form-is-lagrangian.md) using the intrinsic one-form rather than a coordinate guess.
