<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [cotangent fiber translation](../../../../../../cotangent-fiber-translation.md) $f(q,p)=(q,p+\alpha_q)$ is a smooth diffeomorphism with inverse $(q,p)\mapsto(q,p-\alpha_q)$, and $\pi\circ f=\pi$. For a tangent vector $v$ at $(q,p)$, the intrinsic definition of the [canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle](../../../../../../canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) gives

$$
(f^*\theta)_{(q,p)}(v)=(p+\alpha_q)(d\pi(v))=\theta_{(q,p)}(v)+(\pi^*\alpha)_{(q,p)}(v).
$$

Thus $f^*\theta=\theta+\pi^*\alpha$. Taking its [exterior derivative](../../../../../../exterior-derivative.md) with the fixed sign convention yields

$$
\boxed{f^*\omega=\omega-\pi^*(d\alpha)}.
$$

If $\alpha$ is a [closed differential one-form](../../../../../../closed-differential-one-form.md), the last term vanishes, proving that $f$ is a [symplectomorphism](../../../../../../symplectomorphism.md). In fact the converse holds too: the pullback by the zero section shows that $\pi^*(d\alpha)=0$ implies $d\alpha=0$.
