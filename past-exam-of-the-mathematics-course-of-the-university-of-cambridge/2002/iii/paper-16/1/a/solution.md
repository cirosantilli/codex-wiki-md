<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [Hamiltonian vector field](../../../../../../hamiltonian-vector-field.md) convention $\iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega=-dH_t$. A smooth time-dependent [Hamiltonian function](../../../../../../hamiltonian-function.md) $H:[0,1]\times M\to\mathbb R$ with flow defined over the whole interval generates the [Hamiltonian isotopy](../../../../../../hamiltonian-isotopy.md) determined by

$$
\frac d{dt}\phi_H^t=X_{H_t}\circ\phi_H^t,\qquad \phi_H^0=\mathrm{id}.
$$

The [group of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms](../../../../../../group-of-hamiltonian-diffeomorphisms.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Ham}(M,\omega)=\{\phi_H^1:H\text{ generates such a flow}\}}.
$$

These maps are [symplectomorphisms](../../../../../../symplectomorphism.md): by [Cartan's magic formula](../../../../../../cartan-s-magic-formula.md), $\mathcal L_{X_{H_t}}\omega=d\iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega=0$. Thus $d(\phi_H^t)^*\omega/dt=0$.

The term “group” is justified explicitly. If $\phi_t,\psi_t$ are generated by $H_t,K_t$, their product path is generated by $H_t+K_t\circ\phi_t^{-1}$, since the pushforward of $X_{K_t}$ by a [symplectomorphism](../../../../../../symplectomorphism.md) is $X_{K_t\circ\phi_t^{-1}}$. The inverse path is generated by $-H_t\circ\phi_t$. The zero Hamiltonian gives the identity.

On a closed [symplectic manifold](../../../../../../symplectic-manifold.md), all these flows exist on the finite time interval. On an open manifold one often means the [compactly supported Hamiltonian diffeomorphism group](../../../../../../compactly-supported-hamiltonian-diffeomorphism-group.md) $\operatorname{Ham}_c$, using Hamiltonians with compact support in $[0,1]\times M$. We use that subgroup for the finite-energy definitions on Euclidean space below. The unrestricted complete-flow group is needed for the global cotangent translations in Question 4; those translations are generally not compactly supported.
