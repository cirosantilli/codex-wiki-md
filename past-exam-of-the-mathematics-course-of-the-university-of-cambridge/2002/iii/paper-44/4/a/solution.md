<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Work at $r\gg R_{\rm sch}$ in the region where the central [black hole](../../../../../../black-hole.md) dominates the [gravitational potential](../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md). Let $J$ be a star's specific [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md). Near its very small pericentre, the centrifugal term balances the deep gravitational potential. For an approximately parabolic passage with capture pericentre $r_c=R_{\rm sch}$,

$$
\frac{J_{\rm lc}^2}{2r_c^2}\simeq\frac{GM_H}{r_c},
\qquad J_{\rm lc}^2\simeq2GM_HR_{\rm sch}.
$$

At radius $r$, a velocity making angle $\vartheta$ with the inward radial direction has $J=rv\sin\vartheta$. Therefore the [loss cone](../../../../../../loss-cone.md) half-angle is

$$
\boxed{\vartheta_{\rm lc}(r,v)\simeq
\frac{\sqrt{2GM_HR_{\rm sch}}}{rv}.}
$$

Taking the characteristic speed to be the [circular speed](../../../../../../circular-speed.md) $v^2\sim GM_H/r$ gives

$$
\boxed{\vartheta_{\rm lc}\sim\sqrt{\frac{R_{\rm sch}}r},}
$$

with Newtonian circular-speed normalization $\vartheta_{\rm lc}^2\simeq2R_{\rm sch}/r$. The inward cap occupies solid angle $\Omega_{\rm lc}\simeq\pi\vartheta_{\rm lc}^2$, or a fraction $\vartheta_{\rm lc}^2/4$ of an isotropic velocity sphere. Counting low-angular-momentum trajectories in both radial directions changes only an order-one factor; each such orbit is removed at its next pericentre.

The capture occurs in the strong-field region, so this Newtonian pericentre estimate is not an exact relativistic capture coefficient. The Schwarzschild capture calculation alters an order-one constant, while retaining the small-angle scaling $\vartheta_{\rm lc}\propto r^{-1/2}$ for these characteristic Keplerian speeds.
