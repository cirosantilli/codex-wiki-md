↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Now $B=\overline{\mathbb C[1,x]}$. Let $U$ be a bounded component of $\rho_A(x)$ and $\lambda\in U$. Its [boundary](../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) lies in $\sigma_A(x)$: an [open](../../../../../../open-set.md) disk about a [boundary](../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) point in the [algebra resolvent set](../../../../../../resolvent-set-of-a-banach-algebra-element.md) would meet $U$ and would belong to the same component, contradicting [boundary](../../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) membership. The [maximum modulus principle](../../../../../../maximum-modulus-principle.md) on the bounded domain $U$ therefore gives, for every complex [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) $p$,

$$
|p(\lambda)|\le\max_{\partial U}|p|
\le\max_{\sigma_A(x)}|p|=r_A(p(x))\le\|p(x)\|.
$$

Here [spectral mapping theorem](../../../../../../spectral-mapping-theorem.md) follows by factoring $p(z)-\mu$: its commuting factors are invertible exactly when every root avoids the [Banach algebra spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-of-an-element.md).

The rule $p(x)\mapsto p(\lambda)$ is well-defined by the displayed bound, multiplicative and bounded on the dense [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) [subalgebra](../../../../../../subalgebra.md). It extends to a [unital](../../../../../../unital-algebra.md) [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $\varphi_\lambda$ on $B$. Since $\varphi_\lambda(\lambda1-x)=0$, that element cannot have an inverse in $B$. Thus **$U\subseteq\sigma_B(x)$**. Together with part (iii), this proves that the [Banach algebra spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-of-an-element.md) of a polynomial-generated Banach [subalgebra](../../../../../../subalgebra.md) is exactly the original [Banach algebra spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-of-an-element.md) with all its bounded complementary components filled in.
