<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $|\lambda|>\|x\|$, the [Neumann series](../../../../../../neumann-series.md)

$$
(\lambda1-x)^{-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\lambda^{-n-1}x^n
$$

converges in $B$. The exterior of this disk is [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) and belongs to both [algebra resolvent sets](../../../../../../resolvent-set-of-a-banach-algebra-element.md). Since each [Banach algebra spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-of-an-element.md) is [compact](../../../../../../compact-space.md), each [algebra resolvent set](../../../../../../resolvent-set-of-a-banach-algebra-element.md) has exactly one unbounded component: any unbounded component meets that exterior, and two components meeting a common [connected](../../../../../../connected-space.md) exterior must coincide. Part (ii) says that the component of $\rho_A(x)$ meeting the exterior is also a whole component of $\rho_B(x)$. Hence **the unbounded components are identical**.
