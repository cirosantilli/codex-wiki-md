<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use $\sigma_A(x)$ and $\rho_A(x)$ for [Banach algebra spectrum](../../../../../../spectrum-of-an-element.md) and [algebra resolvent set](../../../../../../resolvent-set-of-a-banach-algebra-element.md). An inverse in the smaller [unital algebra](../../../../../../unital-algebra.md) is also an inverse in the larger one, so $\rho_B(x)\subseteq\rho_A(x)$.

If $\lambda\in\rho_B(x)$ and $r=(\lambda1-x)^{-1}\in B$, then for $|\mu-\lambda|\|r\|<1$ the [Neumann series](../../../../../../neumann-series.md) gives

$$
(\mu1-x)^{-1}=(1+(\mu-\lambda)r)^{-1}r\in B.
$$

Thus $\rho_B(x)$ is [open](../../../../../../open-set.md). To prove relative closedness, suppose $\lambda_n\in\rho_B(x)$ and $\lambda_n\to\lambda\in\rho_A(x)$. [Continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of inversion in $A$ gives $(\lambda_n1-x)^{-1}\to(\lambda1-x)^{-1}$. Since $B$ is [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) [closed](../../../../../../closed-set.md), the limiting inverse belongs to $B$, so $\lambda\in\rho_B(x)$. Consequently **$\rho_B(x)$ is relatively clopen in $\rho_A(x)$**.
