<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [contraction principle for large deviations](../../../../../../contraction-principle-for-large-deviations.md) states that if [random elements](../../../../../../random-element.md) $X^L$ in a space $E$ satisfy a [large deviation principle](../../../../../../large-deviation-principle.md) with speed $a_L\to\infty$ and [good rate function](../../../../../../good-rate-function.md) $I$, and $f:E\to F$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md), then $f(X^L)$ satisfies a [large deviation principle](../../../../../../large-deviation-principle.md) at the same speed with

$$
\boxed{J(y)=\inf\{I(x):f(x)=y\},\qquad \inf\varnothing=+\infty.}
$$

For example, take $E,F$ to be [Polish spaces](../../../../../../polish-space.md). The [rate function](../../../../../../rate-function.md) $J$ is good: its finite [sublevel sets](../../../../../../sublevel-set.md) are the [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) images $f(\{I\leq r\})$ of compact sublevel sets. Compactness and [lower semicontinuity](../../../../../../lower-semicontinuity.md) ensure that a finite fibre infimum relevant to such a sublevel set is attained. The probability bounds follow by taking preimages of open and closed sets.
