A finite [monomial group](../../../../../../monomial-group.md), also called an [M-group](../../../../../../monomial-group.md), is a [finite group](../../../../../../finite-group.md) for which every [irreducible character](../../../../../../irreducible-character.md) is a [monomial character](../../../../../../monomial-character.md): for every $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ there are a [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H\leq G$ and a [linear character](../../../../../../linear-character.md) $\lambda$ of $H$ such that $\chi=\operatorname{Ind}_H^G\lambda$. The inducing [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) and [linear character](../../../../../../linear-character.md) may depend on $\chi$.

[Taketa's theorem](../../../../../../taketa-s-theorem.md) states that **every finite monomial group is solvable**. Here being a [solvable group](../../../../../../solvable-group.md) means that the iterated [derived subgroups](../../../../../../commutator-subgroup.md) eventually reach the identity.

[Dade's embedding theorem for monomial groups](../../../../../../dade-s-embedding-theorem-for-monomial-groups.md) states that **every finite solvable group embeds as a [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) of a finite monomial group**. It does not say that every solvable group is itself monomial, nor that the embedding can always be taken normal. Together the results identify solvability as a necessary property of monomial groups without making monomiality closed under taking arbitrary [subgroups](../../../../../../subgroup.md).
