A [Frobenius group](../../../../../../frobenius-group.md) is a finite [transitive group action](../../../../../../transitive-group-action.md) on more than one point in which a nonidentity element fixes at most one point and a point stabilizer is nontrivial. Equivalently it has a nontrivial proper [subgroup](../../../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ with $H\cap H^g=1$ for every $g\notin H$. The [Frobenius complement](../../../../../../frobenius-complement.md) is such a point stabilizer $H$.

The [Frobenius kernel](../../../../../../frobenius-kernel.md) is the identity together with all fixed-point-free elements in this action, equivalently

$$
N=\{1\}\cup\left(G\setminus\bigcup_{g\in G}H^g\right).
$$

The [Frobenius kernel theorem](../../../../../../frobenius-kernel-theorem.md) asserts that this set is a [normal subgroup](../../../../../../normal-subgroup.md), acts regularly on the points, and gives $G=N\rtimes H$. In particular $C_N(h)=1$ for $1\ne h\in H$.

[Brauer's permutation lemma](../../../../../../brauer-s-permutation-lemma.md) states that an [automorphism](../../../../../../automorphism.md) of a [finite group](../../../../../../finite-group.md) fixes equally many [conjugacy classes](../../../../../../conjugacy-class.md) and [irreducible characters](../../../../../../irreducible-character.md). More generally a group of automorphisms has equal [permutation characters](../../../../../../permutation-character.md) on those two sets. Indeed, the [character table](../../../../../../character-table.md) is invertible and intertwines the two permutation matrices: simultaneous permutation of rows and columns preserves [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) evaluation. Similar matrices have equal traces, and a permutation-matrix trace counts its fixed points. The fixed objects on the group side are classes, not individual elements.
