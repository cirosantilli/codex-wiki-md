↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**No.** The [free Abelian lattice-ordered group](../../../../../../free-abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md) on two generators is [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md), since every element is represented by a finite term over a countable alphabet of operation symbols and the two generators. The additive [group](../../../../../../group-split.md) of all real-valued functions on the given half-plane is uncountable: it already contains a distinct constant function for every [real number](../../../../../../real-number.md). A [lattice-ordered group homomorphism](../../../../../../lattice-ordered-group-homomorphism.md) that is an isomorphism would be a bijection, contradicting these cardinalities. The concrete free-group model consists of specially constrained integer-linear term functions, not all real-valued functions on a domain.
