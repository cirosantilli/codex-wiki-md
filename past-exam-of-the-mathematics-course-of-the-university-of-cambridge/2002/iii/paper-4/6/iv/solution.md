↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**No.** Assign the two free generators to $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ in the [lexicographically ordered group](../../../../../../lexicographically-ordered-group.md) $\mathbb Z^2$. The [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md) gives a [lattice-ordered group homomorphism](../../../../../../lattice-ordered-group-homomorphism.md) onto this group: its image contains both ordinary group generators. It is not an [Archimedean ordered group](../../../../../../archimedean-ordered-group.md), since

$$
n(0,1)<(1,0)\qquad\text{for every positive integer }n.
$$

Thus a lattice-homomorphic image can contain a nonzero positive infinitesimal even when the original free object has an Archimedean pointwise function representation.
