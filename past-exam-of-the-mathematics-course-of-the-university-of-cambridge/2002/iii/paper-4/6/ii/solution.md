<h1 id="6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A concrete model of the two-generated [free Abelian lattice-ordered group](../../../../../../free-abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md) is the lattice subgroup of real-valued functions on $\mathbb R^2$ generated by the coordinate projections $X(x,y)=x$, $Y(x,y)=y$. Its elements are finite [joins](../../../../../../least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of finite [meets](../../../../../../greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of integer linear forms $ax+by$. Thus they are continuous [positively homogeneous](../../../../../../positively-homogeneous-function-degree-one.md) [piecewise linear functions](../../../../../../piecewise-linear-function.md), with pointwise addition and lattice operations. This is the [piecewise integer-linear representation of a free Abelian lattice-ordered group](../../../../../../piecewise-integer-linear-representation-of-a-free-abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md).

For the construction, first form the term algebra on two symbols using addition, negation, zero, [join](../../../../../../least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) and [meet](../../../../../../greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md), then identify terms that agree in every [Abelian lattice-ordered group](../../../../../../abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md). Evaluation under any assignment of the two generators factors through this quotient, proving its [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md). To obtain the concrete function model, use the identity-detection theorem that the real line generates the variety of Abelian lattice-ordered groups. Its proof passes a failed identity to a prime ordered quotient, chooses its finitely many active linear pieces, and uses the rational-inequality alternative underlying [Farkas' lemma](../../../../../../farkas-lemma.md) to find a real witness. This makes evaluation on $\mathbb R^2$ injective as well as surjective onto the generated function group.

**Its underlying group is not $\mathbb Z^n$ for any finite $n$.** The [integer-linear hinge functions have infinite independent rank](../../../../../../integer-linear-hinge-functions-have-infinite-independent-rank.md): $h_k(x,y)=(x-ky)\vee0$ are integer-linearly independent for distinct integers $k$. On $y=1$, only $h_k$ has a slope jump at $x=k$, so each coefficient in a finite relation must vanish. The underlying group therefore has infinite [rank of an abelian group](../../../../../../rank-of-an-abelian-group.md) despite having two lattice generators.
