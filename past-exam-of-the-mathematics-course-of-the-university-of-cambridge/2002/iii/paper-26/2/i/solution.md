<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\gamma=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in SL_2(\mathbb Z)$ and $j=c\tau+d$. Differentiating the transformation law of a weight-$k$ [modular form](../../../../../../modular-form.md) gives the [derivative transformation of a weak modular form](../../../../../../derivative-transformation-of-a-weak-modular-form.md)

$$
(Df)(\gamma\tau)=j^{k+2}Df(\tau)+\frac{kc}{2\pi i}j^{k+1}f(\tau).
$$

The corresponding anomaly for $Dg$ has coefficient $\ell c/(2\pi i)$. Consequently these anomalies cancel in $\ell gDf-kfDg$, which transforms with weight $k+\ell+2$. The [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) are holomorphic on the [complex upper half-plane](../../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). In a [Fourier expansion](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md), $D(\sum_{n\geq0}a_nq^n)=\sum_{n\geq1}na_nq^n$, so each term in the combination has zero constant coefficient and no negative powers. There is just one [modular cusp](../../../../../../cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) up to the full [modular group](../../../../../../modular-group.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\ell gDf-kfDg\in S_{k+\ell+2}.}
$$

This is, up to the chosen sign, the [first Rankin-Cohen bracket](../../../../../../first-rankin-cohen-bracket.md).
