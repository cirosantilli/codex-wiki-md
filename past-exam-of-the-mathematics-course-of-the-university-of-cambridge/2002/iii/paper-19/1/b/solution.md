↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**False.** The zero [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is already a counterexample. The [Jones polynomial evaluation at one](../../../../../../jones-polynomial-evaluation-at-one.md) of a nonempty [link](../../../../../../link.md) with $\ell$ components is

$$
V_L(1)=(-2)^{\ell-1}\ne0.
$$

To prove this, specialize the [Jones polynomial skein relation](../../../../../../jones-polynomial-skein-relation.md) at $t=1$: switching a crossing preserves the value. Switch crossings until the diagram is an [unlink](../../../../../../unlink.md) without changing its component count. The [Kauffman bracket](../../../../../../kauffman-bracket.md) loop rule, equivalently the [Jones polynomial of a split union](../../../../../../jones-polynomial-of-a-split-union.md), gives the displayed evaluation. Hence arbitrary Laurent [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) cannot occur.
