<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**False as written for arbitrary links.** The two-component [unlink](../../../../../../unlink.md) has a diagram with no crossings but

$$
\boxed{V_{U\sqcup U}(t)=-(t^{1/2}+t^{-1/2}),\qquad\operatorname{br}V_{U\sqcup U}=1>0.}
$$

The correct connected-diagram statement does hold, in particular for every [knot diagram](../../../../../../knot-diagram.md). If $s_A,s_B$ count the all-$A$ and all-$B$ [bracket smoothing state](../../../../../../bracket-smoothing-state.md) [circles](../../../../../../circle.md), changing $j$ smoothings increases the circle count by at most $j$. The maximum and minimum $A$ exponents therefore lie between $n+2s_A-2$ and $-n-2s_B+2$. This gives the [Kauffman bracket breadth bound](../../../../../../kauffman-bracket-breadth-bound.md) $\operatorname{br}_A\langle D\rangle\leq2n+2s_A+2s_B-4$. For a connected projection, the [Turaev surface](../../../../../../turaev-surface.md) has [Euler characteristic](../../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) $s_A+s_B-n\leq2$, so the bound is $4n$. [Writhe of a link diagram](../../../../../../writhe-of-a-link-diagram.md) normalization changes only a [monomial](../../../../../../monomial.md), and $t=A^{-4}$ divides the breadth by four, giving $\operatorname{br}V_L\leq n$.

For a diagram with $k$ projection components, sum the same [Euler characteristic](../../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) inequality over the components. Then $s_A+s_B\leq n+2k$ and the [Jones polynomial breadth bound for a disconnected diagram](../../../../../../jones-polynomial-breadth-bound-for-a-disconnected-diagram.md) is $\operatorname{br}V_L\leq n+k-1$. The extra $k-1$ is indispensable, as the [unlink](../../../../../../unlink.md) shows.
