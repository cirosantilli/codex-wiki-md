<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**True.** In a [connected sum of knots](../../../../../../connected-sum-of-knots.md), a [bracket smoothing state](../../../../../../bracket-smoothing-state.md) from each summand contributes a state of the joined diagram. Joining the two distinguished state [circles](../../../../../../circle.md) reduces their total count by one, so the factors $\delta^{s-1}$ multiply. The smoothing weights also multiply and the [writhe of a link diagram](../../../../../../writhe-of-a-link-diagram.md) adds. Thus the [Jones polynomial of a connected sum](../../../../../../jones-polynomial-of-a-connected-sum.md) is

$$
\boxed{V_{K_1\#K_2}(t)=V_{K_1}(t)V_{K_2}(t).}
$$
