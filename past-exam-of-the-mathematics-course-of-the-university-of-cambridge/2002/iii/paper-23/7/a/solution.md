<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Fagin's theorem](../../../../../../fagin-s-theorem.md) states that, for a finite relational signature, an isomorphism-invariant class of finite structures has its standard encoding language in [NP](../../../../../../np-complexity.md) if and only if it is definable by an [existential second-order logic](../../../../../../existential-second-order-logic.md) sentence

$$
\exists R_1\cdots\exists R_m\,\psi,
$$

where $\psi$ is first-order and the relation variables have fixed finite arities. The forward implication includes the substantive encoding of a polynomially bounded accepting computation by relations. In the reverse implication the relations are polynomial-length certificates and the fixed [first-order sentence](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) can be checked in [polynomial time](../../../../../../polynomial-time.md). This is a finite-structure characterization; no order need be named in the input signature.
