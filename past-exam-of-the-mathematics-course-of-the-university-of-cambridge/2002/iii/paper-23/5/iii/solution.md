<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Assume $A$ is a countable model of $T$ and its automorphism group is an [oligomorphic permutation group](../../../../../../oligomorphic-permutation-group.md). Complete types are constant on automorphism orbits, so only finitely many complete $n$-types are realized in $A$, for each $n$.

These realized types are dense in the [Stone topology on a type space](../../../../../../stone-topology-on-a-type-space.md) $S_n(T)$. Indeed, any nonempty basic [clopen set](../../../../../../clopen-set.md) $[\theta]$ is given by a formula consistent with $T$. Completeness implies $T\models\exists\mathbf x\,\theta$, and thus some tuple in $A$ realizes a type in $[\theta]$. A finite set of points is closed in a Hausdorff space; if it is also dense, it is the whole space. Therefore $S_n(T)$ equals the finite set of types realized in $A$. This proves (iii) implies (ii), and the previous part then yields (i). We have established

$$
\boxed{\omega\text{-categoricity}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \forall n\ |S_n(T)|<\infty\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \text{a countable oligomorphic model exists}.}
$$

In particular, the argument does not assume that an arbitrary countable model is saturated or that its type-orbits coincide before that coincidence has been proved.
