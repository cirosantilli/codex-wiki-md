The [compactness theorem](../../../../../../compactness-theorem.md) for [first-order logic](../../../../../../first-order-logic.md) says that a set $\Sigma$ of [first-order sentences](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) has a model if and only if every finite subset of $\Sigma$ has a model. It applies to arbitrary languages, including expansions by arbitrarily many new constants. Equivalently, if $\Sigma$ entails a sentence, some finite subset already entails it. We will use both formulations with the [diagram of a structure](../../../../../../diagram-mathematical-logic.md).
