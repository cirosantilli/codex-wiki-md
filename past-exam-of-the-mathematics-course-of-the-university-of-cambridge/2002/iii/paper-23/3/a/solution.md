<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [1-embedding](../../../../../../existential-embedding.md), or [existential embedding](../../../../../../existential-embedding.md), is a [structure embedding](../../../../../../structure-embedding.md) $\alpha:A\to B$ satisfying

$$
A\models\exists\mathbf y\,\theta(\mathbf a,\mathbf y)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
B\models\exists\mathbf y\,\theta(\alpha\mathbf a,\mathbf y)
$$

for every quantifier-free $\theta$ and every parameter tuple $\mathbf a$ from $A$. The forward direction is automatic for an embedding; reflection of [existential formulas](../../../../../../existential-formula.md) is the extra property. For an inclusion this says that $A$ is [simply closed](../../../../../../simple-closure.md) in $B$. This is weaker than requiring an [elementary embedding](../../../../../../elementary-embedding.md), which preserves all formulas.
