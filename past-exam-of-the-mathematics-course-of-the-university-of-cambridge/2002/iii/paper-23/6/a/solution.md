<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a relational [first-order structure](../../../../../../first-order-structure.md) $A$, form its [Gaifman graph](../../../../../../gaifman-graph.md): distinct elements are adjacent when they occur together in a tuple of some interpreted relation. Let $d_A$ be graph distance, with distance infinite between different components. For a tuple $\mathbf a=(a_1,\ldots,a_m)$, its radius-$r$ neighbourhood has universe

$$
N_r^A(\mathbf a)=\{b\in A:\min_i d_A(b,a_i)\le r\}.
$$

The neighbourhood structure is the induced relational structure on this set, expanded by constants marking the entries of $\mathbf a$ in their original order. Its isomorphism type thus records the centres, not just the unmarked ball. For one binary graph relation, this is the usual union of the radius-$r$ graph balls. This formulation assumes the finite relational signatures used in the finite-model setting.
