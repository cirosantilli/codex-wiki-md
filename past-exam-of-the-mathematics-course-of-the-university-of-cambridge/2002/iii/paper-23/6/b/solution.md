↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An exact-count form of the [Hanf locality lemma](../../../../../../hanf-locality-lemma.md) is as follows. For every [quantifier rank](../../../../../../quantifier-rank.md) $q$, put $r=3^q$. If finite relational structures $A,B$ admit a bijection $f:A\to B$ such that

$$
(N_r^A(a),a)\cong(N_r^B(f(a)),f(a))\qquad\text{for every }a\in A,
$$

then $A$ and $B$ agree on every [first-order sentence](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) of [quantifier rank](../../../../../../quantifier-rank.md) at most $q$. Equivalently, they have the same number of occurrences of every marked radius-$r$ one-point neighbourhood type. The individual local isomorphisms need not be restrictions of $f$.

A bounded-degree threshold version permits the type counts to be different when both exceed a sufficiently large bound depending on the degree bound and $q$. We need only the exact-count form. Its locality content is that sufficiently large matching neighbourhoods and their multiplicities determine all sentences up to the prescribed [quantifier rank](../../../../../../quantifier-rank.md).
