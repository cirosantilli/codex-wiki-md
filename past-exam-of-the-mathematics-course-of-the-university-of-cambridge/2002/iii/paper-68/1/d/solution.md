<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the **supersymmetric Higgs mechanism**, an internal [gauge symmetry](../../../../../../gauge-invariance.md) is broken by a charged [scalar field](../../../../../../scalar-field.md) expectation value while [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) can remain unbroken. A massless [vector multiplet](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) absorbs a [chiral multiplet](../../../../../../chiral-multiplet.md) to form a [massive N=1 vector multiplet](../../../../../../massive-n-1-vector-multiplet.md): the vector absorbs a scalar [Goldstone boson](../../../../../../goldstone-boson.md), and the [gaugino](../../../../../../gaugino.md) and matter [fermion](../../../../../../fermion.md) acquire the matching masses. In the **super-Higgs mechanism**, local [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) itself is spontaneously broken: the [gravitino](../../../../../../gravitino.md) absorbs the spin-$1/2$ [Goldstino](../../../../../../goldstino.md) and becomes a massive spin-$3/2$ particle. In a flat vacuum this increases its physical states from two to four.
