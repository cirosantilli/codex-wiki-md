<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Three independent motivations are **radiative stability, gauge unification and dark matter**. First, [supersymmetric cancellation of quadratic divergences](../../../../../../supersymmetric-cancellation-of-quadratic-divergences.md) protects the Higgs [scalar mass](../../../../../../scalar-mass.md) from the [hierarchy problem](../../../../../../hierarchy-problem.md); residual corrections are controlled by [soft supersymmetry breaking](../../../../../../soft-supersymmetry-breaking.md), so avoiding large cancellations favours a breaking scale near the weak scale. Second, [supersymmetric gauge coupling unification](../../../../../../supersymmetric-gauge-coupling-unification.md) can reconcile the running of the three [Standard Model](../../../../../../standard-model-split.md) couplings when [superpartner](../../../../../../sparticle.md) thresholds lie near that scale. Third, with conserved [R-parity](../../../../../../r-parity.md), a stable [lightest supersymmetric particle](../../../../../../lightest-supersymmetric-particle.md) can provide [dark matter](../../../../../../dark-matter.md), and weak-scale annihilation can produce an appropriate thermal abundance. These are conditional motivations, not proofs of a TeV threshold. Even without low-scale discovery, [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) remains important **as a structure in supergravity and superstring theory**, potentially broken at a higher scale, and **as an exact calculational tool** through [superpotential non-renormalization](../../../../../../non-renormalization-theorem.md), [BPS states](../../../../../../bps-state.md) and control of strongly coupled [quantum field theories](../../../../../../quantum-field-theory-split.md).
