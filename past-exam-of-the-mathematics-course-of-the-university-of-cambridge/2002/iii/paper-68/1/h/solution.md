<h1 id="1/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

At two derivatives, specify the [gauge group](../../../../../../gauge-group.md) and matter representations, a real [Kähler potential](../../../../../../kahler-potential.md) $K(\Phi,\bar\Phi)$, a holomorphic [superpotential](../../../../../../superpotential.md) $W(\Phi)$ and a symmetric holomorphic [gauge kinetic function](../../../../../../gauge-kinetic-function.md) $f_{ab}(\Phi)$. Gauge couplings and topological angles are contained in $f$; global theories can also have allowed [Fayet–Iliopoulos terms](../../../../../../fayet-iliopoulos-term.md) for Abelian factors. Local theories require the gravitational coupling and consistent gauging: a constant [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](../../../../../../fayet-iliopoulos-term.md) is not an independent addition to arbitrary [supergravity](../../../../../../supergravity.md) data. A constant in $W$ affects [supergravity](../../../../../../supergravity.md) although it is irrelevant in the global theory. **$K$ is not holomorphic or protected; $W$ has no perturbative Wilsonian renormalization; holomorphic $f$ is perturbatively one-loop exact.** Nonperturbative corrections can modify $W$ and $f$, and [wave-function renormalization](../../../../../../wave-function-renormalization.md) makes physical couplings run despite [superpotential non-renormalization](../../../../../../non-renormalization-theorem.md). Higher-derivative actions require further data.
