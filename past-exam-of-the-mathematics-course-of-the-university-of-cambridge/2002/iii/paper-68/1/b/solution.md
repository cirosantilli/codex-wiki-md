<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For physical finite-spin [one-particle states](../../../../../../one-particle-state.md), a positive-energy [Poincaré group](../../../../../../poincare-group.md) representation is labelled by mass and [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) $(m,j)$ when $m>0$, or mass and [helicity](../../../../../../helicity.md) $(0,h)$ when massless. The individual states also carry their [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md) and a spin projection. For [four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry](../../../../../../four-dimensional-n-1-supersymmetry.md), the irreducible labels become $(m,j_{
m super})$, with $j_{
m super}$ the [superspin](../../../../../../superspin.md), or $(0,h_{
m super})$, with a chosen [superhelicity](../../../../../../superhelicity.md) convention. The constituent representation labels are

$$
\boxed{\text{massive: }(m,j),(m,j),(m,j+\tfrac12),(m,j-\tfrac12),}
$$

with the last entry omitted for $j=0$, and

$$
\boxed{\text{massless: }(0,h),(0,h+\tfrac12).}
$$

A required [CPT completion of a supermultiplet](../../../../../../cpt-completion-of-a-supermultiplet.md) adds the conjugate labels $(0,-h),(0,-h-\tfrac12)$ and conjugates any internal charges.
