↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Unbroken extended supersymmetry obstructs the observed chiral matter structure.** In four dimensions, the matter [hypermultiplets](../../../../../../hypermultiplet.md) of $\mathcal N\ge2$ theories contain conjugate [gauge group representations](../../../../../../gauge-group-representation.md), so their [fermions](../../../../../../fermion.md) are vectorlike rather than the chiral [Weyl fermions](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) of the [Standard Model](../../../../../../standard-model-split.md). [Extended supersymmetry](../../../../../../extended-supersymmetry.md) also relates interactions more strongly, restricting independent [Yukawa couplings](../../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) and [superpotentials](../../../../../../superpotential.md). It can occur at higher energies and break to $\mathcal N=1$ or to no [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md); the objection is to its unbroken realization in an ordinary low-energy chiral particle theory.
