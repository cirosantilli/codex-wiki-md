↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**The supersymmetry algebra closes on spacetime translations.** Its [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) are [Weyl spinors](../../../../../../weyl-spinor.md), and

$$
\{Q_\alpha,\bar Q_{\dot\beta}\}=2\sigma^\mu_{\alpha\dot\beta}P_\mu.
$$

Thus the composition of two odd [supersymmetry transformations](../../../../../../supersymmetry-transformation.md) moves the spacetime argument. The generators also change [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) and connect [bosons](../../../../../../boson.md) with [fermions](../../../../../../fermion.md). They extend the [Poincaré group](../../../../../../poincare-group.md) to the [Super-Poincaré group](../../../../../../super-poincare-group.md), rather than forming an internal symmetry that commutes with spacetime transformations.
