<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At fixed nonzero [four-momentum](../../../../../../four-momentum.md), take the finite physical [supermultiplet](../../../../../../supermultiplet.md) and write $P_F=(-1)^F$ for [fermion parity](../../../../../../fermion-parity.md). Each [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) is odd, so $P_FQ_\alpha=-Q_\alpha P_F$ and the same holds for its adjoint. By the [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md),

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(P_FQ_\alpha Q_\alpha^\dagger)
=\operatorname{Tr}(Q_\alpha^\dagger P_FQ_\alpha)
=-\operatorname{Tr}(P_FQ_\alpha^\dagger Q_\alpha).
$$

Consequently $\operatorname{Tr}[P_F\{Q_\alpha,Q_\alpha^\dagger\}]=0$. Sum the two spinor components of the $\mathcal N=1$ [Super-Poincaré algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md). Since $\operatorname{Tr}_{2\times2}\sigma^i=0$, their sum is $4H$. On a fixed-energy representation, $H=EI$, yielding

$$
0=4E\operatorname{Tr}P_F=4E(n_B-n_F).
$$

For a nonzero physical one-particle momentum, $E>0$, including the massless case. Hence **the bosonic and fermionic physical state counts agree**:

$$
\boxed{n_B=n_F.}
$$

Equivalently a nonzero normalized [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) and its adjoint define an odd [involution](../../../../../../involution.md) pairing the two subspaces. This is [supertrace pairing at positive energy](../../../../../../supertrace-pairing-at-positive-energy.md); it counts spin polarizations, not merely distinct names of particles.

The phrase “every supermultiplet” requires this positive-energy qualification. A zero-energy [supersymmetric vacuum](../../../../../../supersymmetric-vacuum.md) with all [supercharges](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) acting as zero is a one-dimensional bosonic representation with no fermionic partner. It is a counterexample to the unrestricted statement, because division by $E$ is then impossible. It does not contradict the requested equality for particle [supermultiplets](../../../../../../supermultiplet.md).
