<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Sum the diagonal spinor entries of the $\mathcal N=1$ [Super-Poincaré algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md), obtaining

$$
\boxed{H=\frac14\sum_{\alpha=1}^2\{Q_\alpha,Q_\alpha^\dagger\}.}
$$

The expectation in any state is therefore

$$
\langle H\rangle=\frac14\sum_\alpha\bigl(\|Q_\alpha|v\rangle\|^2+\|Q_\alpha^\dagger|v\rangle\|^2\bigr)\ge0.
$$

This proves [energy positivity in global supersymmetry](../../../../../../energy-positivity-in-global-supersymmetry.md), with the energy origin fixed by the algebra.

If a [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) is invariant under [supersymmetry](../../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md), all its [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) variations vanish and its energy is zero. Conversely, zero energy makes every nonnegative squared norm above zero, so every [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) annihilates that vacuum. Thus **strictly positive vacuum energy implies supersymmetry breaking**: some [supercharge](../../../../../../supersymmetry-generator.md) fails to annihilate the vacuum. A finite-volume regulator can be used before passing to vacuum-energy density. This is a statement about the global [supersymmetry algebra](../../../../../../super-poincare-algebra.md); it is not a positivity theorem for the general [supergravity F-term potential](../../../../../../supergravity-f-term-potential.md).
