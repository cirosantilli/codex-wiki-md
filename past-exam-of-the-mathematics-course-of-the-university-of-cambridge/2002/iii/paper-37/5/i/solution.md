<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under [Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium](../../../../../../hardy-weinberg-principle.md), the two parental [allele](../../../../../../allele.md) copies are sampled independently from the same pool. Multiplying their frequencies, and adding the two orderings for a heterozygote, gives

$$
\boxed{\Pr(a/a)=p^2,\qquad\Pr(a/b)=2p(1-p),\qquad\Pr(b/b)=(1-p)^2.}
$$

Their sum is $(p+(1-p))^2=1$. The [genotypes](../../../../../../genotype.md) are unordered even though the paternal and maternal transmissions may be distinguished in the calculation.
