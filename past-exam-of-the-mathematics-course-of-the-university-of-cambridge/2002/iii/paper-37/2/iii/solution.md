<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The two event types are mutually exclusive as observed first outcomes. Summing their [cumulative incidence functions](../../../../../../cumulative-incidence-function.md) and using the [survivor function](../../../../../../survival-function.md) gives

$$
F_A(t)+F_B(t)=1-S(t).
$$

Taking the increasing limit in time therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\Pr(\text{an event ever occurs})=1-\exp\{-H_A(\infty)-H_B(\infty)\}.}
$$

The expression is one when the sum of the two [cumulative hazards](../../../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md) diverges, with $e^{-\infty}=0$. Finite total [cumulative hazard](../../../../../../cumulative-hazard-function.md) leaves a positive probability of never experiencing either event.
