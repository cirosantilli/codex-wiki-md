<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**The elements have several complementary origins: primordial light nuclei, stellar and explosive fusion products, and nuclei built by neutron capture. Chemical enrichment also requires their ejection, rather than merely their production inside a star.** [Nucleosynthesis](../../../../../nucleosynthesis.md) and transport into the [interstellar medium](../../../../../interstellar-medium.md) must therefore be considered together.

[Hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) and much of the [helium](../../../../../helium.md) are primordial. During [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](../../../../../big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md), a cooling, expanding plasma assembled surviving [neutrons](../../../../../neutron.md) and [protons](../../../../../proton.md) into [deuterium](../../../../../deuterium.md), helium-3 and especially helium-4, with small abundances of lithium-7 or its beryllium-7 progenitor. Unstable nuclei at mass numbers five and eight, together with the rapidly falling density and short available time, obstructed sustained production of heavier nuclei. The [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) and heavier material in later generations consequently require other sites. [Cosmic rays](../../../../../cosmic-ray.md) fragment heavier interstellar nuclei and contribute importantly to the light elements lithium, beryllium and boron, which ordinary stellar burning tends to destroy.

**Burning stages depend on initial stellar mass.** [Stellar nuclear fusion](../../../../../stellar-nuclear-fusion.md) can release energy when the products have greater [nuclear binding energy](../../../../../nuclear-binding-energy.md) than the reactants. The resulting [stellar thermostat](../../../../../stellar-thermostat.md) links nuclear burning to [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md): when fuel exhaustion lowers heating, contraction raises the central [temperature](../../../../../temperature.md) until another fuel can ignite, if the core becomes hot enough. Higher nuclear charges bring larger [Coulomb barriers](../../../../../coulomb-barrier.md), so later charged-particle burning generally requires progressively higher [temperatures](../../../../../temperature.md). Whether the core reaches them depends on its mass, [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md), mixing and envelope loss.

An approximately solar-composition $1M_\odot$ star first spends most of its life on the [main sequence](../../../../../main-sequence.md), converting [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) into [helium](../../../../../helium.md) mainly through the [proton–proton chain](../../../../../proton-proton-chain.md). The net conversion is four protons into one helium-4 nucleus, with positrons and [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md); the small [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md) contribution redistributes CNO catalyst abundances but is not a net source of carbon at this stage. Exhaustion of central [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) leaves a helium-rich core. A surrounding [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) continues to add [helium](../../../../../helium.md), while the core contracts and the envelope expands into a [red giant](../../../../../red-giant.md).

The low-mass helium core becomes supported substantially by [electron degeneracy pressure](../../../../../electron-degeneracy-pressure.md). At roughly $10^8\,\mathrm K$, the [Triple-alpha process](../../../../../triple-alpha-process.md) ignites, first making transient beryllium-8 and then carbon-12 through the [Hoyle state](../../../../../hoyle-state.md). Because degeneracy initially weakens the usual expansion response, this ignition is a [helium flash](../../../../../helium-flash.md). It heats and lifts the degeneracy of the core, leading to a stable phase of [core helium burning](../../../../../core-helium-burning.md). The [Triple-alpha process](../../../../../triple-alpha-process.md) makes [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md); competing [carbon-12 alpha capture](../../../../../carbon-12-alpha-capture.md) makes [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md):

$$
3\,{}^4\mathrm{He}\longrightarrow{}^{12}\mathrm C,\qquad
{}^{12}\mathrm C(\alpha,\gamma){}^{16}\mathrm O.
$$

After central [helium](../../../../../helium.md) exhaustion, the star develops a carbon-oxygen core, a [helium-burning shell](../../../../../helium-burning-shell.md) and a more external [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md). On the [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md), recurrent [thermal pulses of an asymptotic-giant-branch star](../../../../../thermal-pulse-of-an-asymptotic-giant-branch-star.md) arise from unstable helium-shell burning. [Stellar dredge-up](../../../../../stellar-dredge-up.md) can bring processed material toward the surface, with its efficiency depending on mass and composition. The eventual [stellar wind](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) removes the envelope; the hot exposed core can illuminate a [planetary nebula](../../../../../planetary-nebula.md), and the remnant becomes a [carbon-oxygen white dwarf](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-white-dwarf.md). A usual isolated $1M_\odot$ star does not ignite [carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md). In particular, an element synthesized in its core need not all escape: much of the carbon and oxygen remains locked in the [white dwarf](../../../../../white-dwarf.md).

An approximately $5M_\odot$ star burns central [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) predominantly through the [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md), usually in a convective core. The CNO nuclei act as catalysts; the slow nitrogen-14 proton-capture step tends to accumulate nitrogen-14 while converting [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) into [helium](../../../../../helium.md). After central fuel exhaustion, a [hydrogen-burning shell](../../../../../hydrogen-burning-shell.md) surrounds the contracting helium core. Unlike the $1M_\odot$ case, [core helium burning](../../../../../core-helium-burning.md) normally begins before strong electron degeneracy, without a classical [helium flash](../../../../../helium-flash.md). The same [Triple-alpha process](../../../../../triple-alpha-process.md) and [carbon-12 alpha capture](../../../../../carbon-12-alpha-capture.md) create a carbon-oxygen core.

The $5M_\odot$ star then enters the [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md), with hydrogen and helium burning in shells. Repeated thermal pulses and [stellar dredge-up](../../../../../stellar-dredge-up.md) can enrich its envelope with freshly made [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and [s-process](../../../../../s-process.md) nuclei. [Hot-bottom burning](../../../../../hot-bottom-burning.md) can occur when the base of the convective envelope is sufficiently hot: the [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md) processes dredged-up carbon into [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), so a massive AGB star need not become carbon-rich despite making carbon below its envelope. Helium-shell reactions on neon-22 can also provide neutrons for the [s-process](../../../../../s-process.md). For an ordinary $5M_\odot$ model, strong envelope loss generally precedes carbon ignition, leaving a [carbon-oxygen white dwarf](../../../../../carbon-oxygen-white-dwarf.md). The exact threshold for carbon ignition and the size of these yields depend on [metallicity](../../../../../metallicity.md), mass loss and mixing; they are not universal integers fixed solely by initial mass.

A $20M_\odot$ star reaches much more advanced stages. It starts with convective-core [CNO cycle](../../../../../cno-cycle.md) [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md), followed by [core helium burning](../../../../../core-helium-burning.md). After central [helium](../../../../../helium.md) exhaustion, contraction can ignite [carbon burning](../../../../../carbon-burning.md) at temperatures approaching $10^9\,\mathrm K$. Carbon-fusion channels make products including [neon](../../../../../neon.md), [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md) and [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md). [Neon burning](../../../../../neon-burning.md) then uses [photodisintegration](../../../../../nuclear-photodisintegration.md) of neon-20 to release oxygen-16 and an [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md), followed by capture of that alpha particle on another neon-20 nucleus; the combined network releases energy and produces magnesium-24. [Oxygen burning](../../../../../oxygen-burning.md) makes silicon-group nuclei through channels such as

$$
{}^{16}\mathrm O+{}^{16}\mathrm O\longrightarrow{}^{28}\mathrm{Si}+\alpha.
$$

At still higher [temperature](../../../../../temperature.md), [silicon burning](../../../../../silicon-burning.md) rearranges nuclei by [photodisintegration](../../../../../nuclear-photodisintegration.md) and captures of protons, neutrons and [alpha particles](../../../../../alpha-particle.md). It is not principally direct fusion of two silicon nuclei. Networks approach quasi-equilibrium and then [nuclear statistical equilibrium](../../../../../nuclear-statistical-equilibrium.md), producing an iron-group core whose precise mixture depends on the [electron fraction in stellar matter](../../../../../electron-fraction-in-stellar-matter.md). Outer shells retain different stages of burning, giving an approximately layered structure. Increasing [stellar neutrino energy loss](../../../../../stellar-neutrino-energy-loss.md) makes advanced burning stages much shorter than [hydrogen burning](../../../../../hydrogen-burning.md).

Near the iron group, [nuclear binding energy](../../../../../nuclear-binding-energy.md) per nucleon is close to its maximum. Further fusion cannot provide the sustained energy source required to support the core. As the core grows, [electron capture](../../../../../electron-capture.md) reduces electron-pressure support and [photodisintegration](../../../../../nuclear-photodisintegration.md) absorbs energy, assisting gravitational collapse. A successful [core-collapse supernova](../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) ejects some of the envelope and processed shells, and leaves a [neutron star](../../../../../neutron-star.md) or [black hole](../../../../../black-hole.md). The shock also causes explosive burning, changing the pre-existing abundances. A $20M_\odot$ initial mass does not uniquely fix the explosion or remnant: envelope loss, rotation, composition and fallback all matter, and some collapses fail to eject much material.

**Ejection determines the enrichment of the interstellar medium.** Low- and intermediate-mass stars return [helium](../../../../../helium.md), CNO-processed matter, dredged-up [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and [s-process](../../../../../s-process.md) products through [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) and envelope expulsion. Massive-star winds can expose hydrogen- or helium-burning products before collapse. Successful [core-collapse supernovae](../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) eject substantial oxygen and other alpha-capture products, together with some iron-group matter formed in hydrostatic and explosive burning. Material that falls back or remains in a [stellar remnant](../../../../../stellar-remnant.md) is withheld from the [interstellar medium](../../../../../interstellar-medium.md). Subsequent mixing, cooling and star formation distribute the escaped material into new stars. The distinction between production and ejection is why an internal burning inventory alone is not a stellar yield.

[Binary stars](../../../../../binary-star.md) provide an additional major iron-group source. In a [Type Ia supernova](../../../../../type-ia-supernova.md), a carbon-oxygen [white dwarf](../../../../../white-dwarf.md) in a binary undergoes thermonuclear disruption after an appropriate accretion or merger history. Explosive carbon and oxygen burning makes intermediate-mass and iron-group nuclei. Much initially synthesized nickel-56 decays through cobalt-56 into iron-56; this [radioactive decay](../../../../../radioactive-decay.md) also powers the [supernova](../../../../../supernova.md) light curve. Such explosions have delay times and yields different from those of massive-star collapse, and help explain why iron enrichment need not track oxygen enrichment exactly.

**Neutron capture builds much of the material beyond iron.** Charged-particle reactions encounter increasing [Coulomb barriers](../../../../../coulomb-barrier.md) as nuclear charge grows, but a neutral [neutron](../../../../../neutron.md) does not encounter that barrier. [Neutron capture](../../../../../neutron-capture.md) raises mass number, and subsequent [beta-minus decay](../../../../../beta-minus-decay.md) can turn a neutron into a proton and raise atomic number. The relative capture and decay times select different paths through the nuclear chart.

In the [slow neutron-capture process](../../../../../s-process.md), most unstable intermediates decay before the next capture, so the path stays near stable nuclei. In [Asymptotic giant branch](../../../../../asymptotic-giant-branch.md) stars, important neutron sources are

$$
{}^{13}\mathrm C(\alpha,n){}^{16}\mathrm O,\qquad
{}^{22}\mathrm{Ne}(\alpha,n){}^{25}\mathrm{Mg}.
$$

The carbon-13 source operates in suitable partially mixed layers, while the hotter helium-shell pulses can activate the neon-22 source. Captures on pre-existing seed nuclei produce elements such as strontium and barium and, under suitable conditions, lead. [Stellar dredge-up](../../../../../stellar-dredge-up.md) and [stellar winds](../../../../../stellar-wind.md) return these products to the [interstellar medium](../../../../../interstellar-medium.md). Massive stars also have a weak [s-process](../../../../../s-process.md), notably using neon-22 during helium- and carbon-burning phases; it need not produce the same abundance range as the main AGB contribution.

In the [rapid neutron-capture process](../../../../../r-process.md), very high neutron densities permit many captures before typical [beta-minus decays](../../../../../beta-minus-decay.md). The path moves to neutron-rich nuclei, which later decay toward stability, producing heavy nuclei including lanthanides and actinides. Neutron-rich ejecta from [neutron star mergers](../../../../../neutron-star-merger.md) provide an established site: a merger can eject dynamical material and disk outflows, and [radioactive decay](../../../../../radioactive-decay.md) of the newly made nuclei powers a [kilonova](../../../../../kilonova.md). Special neutron-rich outflows associated with some massive-star collapses are additional candidate sites. It would be unjustified to assume that every ordinary [core-collapse supernova](../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) produces the full heavy [r-process](../../../../../r-process.md) pattern; the required neutron richness and expansion history are restrictive.

The two neutron-capture processes do not make every isotope beyond iron. Some proton-rich isotopes bypass their main paths. [Gamma-process nucleosynthesis](../../../../../gamma-process-nucleosynthesis.md), for example, uses [photodisintegration](../../../../../nuclear-photodisintegration.md) of pre-existing heavy seed nuclei in hot explosive material. Other proton-rich reaction paths can matter in suitable environments. Thus “heavier than iron” describes a family of production mechanisms rather than a single further ordinary fusion stage.

**Binaries change both stellar evolution and the delivery of elements.** [Roche-lobe overflow](../../../../../roche-lobe-overflow.md), wind transfer and mergers can strip a donor, rejuvenate a [mass-gaining star](../../../../../mass-gaining-star.md), alter core and envelope masses, and change the timing and character of an eventual explosion. Binary stripping can remove the hydrogen envelope of a massive [core-collapse supernova](../../../../../core-collapse-supernova.md) progenitor. Accretion onto a [white dwarf](../../../../../white-dwarf.md) can cause a [classical nova](../../../../../classical-nova.md), ejecting hydrogen-burning products and particular isotopes while ordinarily leaving the [white dwarf](../../../../../white-dwarf.md) intact; this differs from the destructive [Type Ia supernova](../../../../../type-ia-supernova.md). Compact [binary stars](../../../../../binary-star.md) can lose orbital energy through [gravitational radiation](../../../../../gravitational-wave.md) and merge, providing the [neutron star merger](../../../../../neutron-star-merger.md) route to heavy [r-process](../../../../../r-process.md) material. Consequently chemical enrichment cannot be inferred entirely from isolated-star tracks, even when a simplified population calculation treats binary components as evolving independently.
