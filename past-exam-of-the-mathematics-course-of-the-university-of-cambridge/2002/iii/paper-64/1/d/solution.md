<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md) is the meeting of a line of [continuous phase transitions](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md) and a line of [first-order phase transitions](../../../../../../first-order-phase-transition.md). In the symmetric scalar [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md), it occurs at $r=u=0$ with $v>0$. Two independent controls are needed to tune both coefficients. The continuous transition lies at $r=0,u>0$, and the [tricritical three-phase line](../../../../../../tricritical-three-phase-line.md) found above has $r=3u^2/(16v),u<0$; both approach the [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md). At zero conjugate field the three coexisting states on this first-order line are the disordered state and the two opposite ordered states.

Setting $u=H=0$ makes the leading stabilizing interaction sextic. The nonzero minimum obeys $M^4=-r/v$, so the [order-parameter critical exponent](../../../../../../order-parameter-critical-exponent.md) along this tuned direction is $\beta_{\mathrm{tri}}=1/4$. Substitution gives

$$
f_{\min}=-\frac{|r|^{3/2}}{3\sqrt v}\quad(r<0),
$$

and two temperature derivatives give the [heat-capacity critical exponent](../../../../../../heat-capacity-critical-exponent.md) $\alpha_{\mathrm{tri}}=1/2$. At $r=u=0$, the [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) is $H=vM^5$, giving the [critical-isotherm exponent](../../../../../../critical-isotherm-exponent.md) $\delta_{\mathrm{tri}}=5$.

For [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md), differentiating $H=rM+vM^5$ gives $\chi^{-1}=r+5vM^4$. This is $r$ above the transition and $-4r$ below, hence $\gamma_{\mathrm{tri}}=1$. The quadratic fluctuation kernel still has a mass proportional to $|r|$, giving $\nu_{\mathrm{tri}}=1/2$ and $\eta_{\mathrm{tri}}=0$. Thus the [mean-field tricritical exponent calculation](../../../../../../mean-field-tricritical-exponent-calculation.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\beta_{\mathrm{tri}}=\frac14,\quad
\alpha_{\mathrm{tri}}=\frac12,\quad
\delta_{\mathrm{tri}}=5,\quad
\gamma_{\mathrm{tri}}=1,\quad\nu_{\mathrm{tri}}=\frac12.}
$$

Detuning $u$ gives [tricritical crossover scaling](../../../../../../tricritical-crossover-scaling.md); one must specify the approach to the [tricritical point](../../../../../../tricritical-point.md) when quoting these [critical exponents](../../../../../../critical-exponent.md). The sextic interaction has [upper critical dimension](../../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md) three, so sufficiently strong long-distance fluctuations change these mean-field predictions below three dimensions.
