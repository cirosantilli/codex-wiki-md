<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Critical exponents](../../../../../../critical-exponent.md) describe leading singular powers near a [continuous phase transition](../../../../../../continuous-phase-transition.md). Using the [reduced temperature](../../../../../../reduced-temperature.md) $t=(T-T_c)/T_c$, the standard definitions include

$$
M(t,0)\sim(-t)^\beta\quad(t<0),\qquad
\chi(t,0)\sim |t|^{-\gamma},\qquad
C_{\mathrm{sing}}(t)\sim |t|^{-\alpha},\qquad
\xi(t)\sim |t|^{-\nu}.
$$

These are the [order-parameter critical exponent](../../../../../../order-parameter-critical-exponent.md), [magnetic-susceptibility critical exponent](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility-critical-exponent.md), [heat-capacity critical exponent](../../../../../../heat-capacity-critical-exponent.md) and [correlation-length critical exponent](../../../../../../correlation-length-critical-exponent.md). The [critical-isotherm exponent](../../../../../../critical-isotherm-exponent.md) is defined by $M(0,H)\sim\operatorname{sgn}(H)|H|^{1/\delta}$. The [anomalous dimension](../../../../../../anomalous-dimension.md) $\eta$ gives the critical long-distance [connected correlation function](../../../../../../connected-correlation-function.md), $G_c(x)\sim |x|^{-(d-2+\eta)}$, when an ordinary scale-invariant critical point exists.

In the [Landau approximation](../../../../../../landau-approximation.md), minimize the [Landau free energy](../../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) to obtain the [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md), differentiate it with respect to the [field conjugate to an order parameter](../../../../../../field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md) to obtain [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md), and differentiate the minimized [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) with respect to temperature to obtain [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md). These operations determine $\beta,\delta,\gamma,\alpha$, respectively. Expanding the [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md) about its uniform minimum gives the quadratic fluctuation kernel $Kq^2+V''(M)$, whose [Fourier transform](../../../../../../fourier-transform.md) determines the [correlation length](../../../../../../correlation-length.md) and the [anomalous dimension](../../../../../../anomalous-dimension.md). At the ordinary quartic critical point this gives $\xi^2=K/V''(M)\propto |t|^{-1}$ and $\eta=0$.

For $u>0$, $r=0$ gives $H=uM^3$ and hence $\delta=3$; the equilibrium [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) $-r^2/(4u)$ gives a finite [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) discontinuity, conventionally $\alpha=0$. Together with the explicit calculation below, the ordinary [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md) are

$$
\boxed{\alpha=0,\quad\beta=\frac12,\quad\gamma=1,\quad
\delta=3,\quad\nu=\frac12,\quad\eta=0.}
$$

These describe neglect of fluctuation corrections, rather than the exact exponents of every low-dimensional [Landau-Ginzburg theory](../../../../../../landau-ginzburg-theory.md).
