<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Take the symmetric scalar [Landau free energy](../../../../../landau-free-energy.md) with $u>0$, $r=at$ for a positive constant $a$, and leading [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md)

$$
H=rM+uM^3.
$$

At zero conjugate field, minimization gives $M=0$ for $t>0$ and $M=\pm\sqrt{-r/u}$ for $t<0$. Selecting the positive [pure thermodynamic phase](../../../../../pure-thermodynamic-phase.md),

$$
M=\sqrt{\frac au}\,(-t)^{1/2}.
$$

Consequently the [order-parameter critical exponent](../../../../../order-parameter-critical-exponent.md) is $\boxed{\beta=1/2}$.

Differentiate the [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md) at fixed temperature, within the selected phase:

$$
\chi=\left.\frac{\partial M}{\partial H}\right|_t
=\frac1{r+3uM^2}
=\begin{cases}
(at)^{-1},&t>0,\\
(2a|t|)^{-1},&t<0.
\end{cases}
$$

Both sides diverge as $|t|^{-1}$, so the [magnetic-susceptibility critical exponent](../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility-critical-exponent.md) is $\boxed{\gamma=1}$. The leading above-to-below [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) amplitude ratio is two. These are the ordinary [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md), valid under the fluctuation qualification explained above.
