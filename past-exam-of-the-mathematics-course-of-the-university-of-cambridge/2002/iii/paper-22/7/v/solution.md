<h1 id="7/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The sheaf $A_n$ is noninitial: it contains $y_n$, whose value at $n$ includes $1_n$. In a [two-valued topos](../../../../../../two-valued-topos.md) its support, the image of $A_n\to1$, can therefore only be $1$. An image equal to $0$ would force $A_n=0$, since the [initial object](../../../../../../initial-object.md) of a topos is strict. Consequently **every $A_n\to1$ is an [epimorphism](../../../../../../epimorphism.md)**.

Products of sheaves on a small site exist and are computed as products of the underlying presheaves, since a product of unique matching-family amalgamations again uniquely amalgamates. For the countable product $P=\prod_{n\in\mathbb N}A_n$, its value at stage $m$ is $\prod_nA_n(m)$. The factor with $n=m+1$ is empty by the stronger statement proved in the preceding part. Thus $P(m)=\varnothing$ at every stage $m$, with the unique empty restriction maps. It is the empty presheaf, already an initial sheaf because no empty [sieve on a category](../../../../../../sieve-category-theory.md) covers. Hence

$$
\boxed{A_n\twoheadrightarrow1\text{ for every }n,\qquad\prod_{n\in\mathbb N}A_n\cong0.}
$$

This proves the final PDF-only request and exhibits the [separating tower site with vanishing product](../../../../../../separating-tower-site-with-vanishing-product.md). It is a genuine failure of countable choice in this Boolean two-valued [sheaf topos](../../../../../../grothendieck-topos.md); it does not contradict the classical external construction of the site.
