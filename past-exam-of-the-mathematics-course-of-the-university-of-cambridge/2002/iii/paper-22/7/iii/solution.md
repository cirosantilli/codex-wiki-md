<h1 id="7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $y_n=C(-,n)$. Suppose two sections $f,g\in y_n(m)$ have equal restrictions on a covering [sieve on a category](../../../../../../sieve-category-theory.md) $R$ on $m$. Such a [sieve on a category](../../../../../../sieve-category-theory.md) is nonempty, so choose $h:l\to m$ in it. Then $fh=gh$. Applying hypothesis (b) with the two right-hand factors both $h$ gives $f=g$, since $f,g$ have the same source and target. Hence every representable is separated for $J$.

In the associated-sheaf construction, sections of $F^+$ are matching families modulo agreement after covering refinement. The map $F\to F^+$ identifies two original sections only if they agree on a covering [sieve on a category](../../../../../../sieve-category-theory.md). Thus it is monic when $F$ is separated. The plus construction is itself separated, and applying it a second time gives the associated sheaf; its second unit is monic as well. Applying this to $y_n$ proves **the unit is a [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md)**

$$
\boxed{y_n\hookrightarrow A_n=a_Jy_n.}
$$

The same construction shows the unit image is dense: a section of $F^{++}$ is locally represented by sections of $F^+$, which are in turn locally represented by sections of $F$, and transitivity combines the two covering refinements.
