<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For every prime $p\ne37$, the [elliptic-curve discriminant](../../../../../../elliptic-curve-discriminant.md) is a unit modulo $p$, giving [good reduction](../../../../../../good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). [Hasse's theorem on elliptic curves](../../../../../../hasse-s-theorem-on-elliptic-curves.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\left|\#\widetilde E_p(\mathbb F_p)-(p+1)\right|\le2\sqrt p.}
$$

Equivalently, the [elliptic-curve point count over a finite field](../../../../../../elliptic-curve-point-count-over-a-finite-field.md) lies between $p+1-2\sqrt p$ and $p+1+2\sqrt p$, including for $p=2$.
