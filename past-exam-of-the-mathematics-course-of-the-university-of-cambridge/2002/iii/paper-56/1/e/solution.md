<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Write $F=\kappa_{\rm fold}(\lambda)$, $G=\kappa_{\rm hom}(\lambda)$ and $H=-\lambda$ for $\lambda<0$, so $0<F<G<H$ near the double-zero point. The six open regions are:

- I: $\lambda>0,\kappa<0$. The origin is the only [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) and is attracting; there is no [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md).
- II: $\lambda>0,\kappa>0$. The origin is repelling and is surrounded by an attracting [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md), born at its [supercritical Hopf bifurcation](../../../../../../supercritical-hopf-bifurcation.md).
- III: $\lambda<0,\kappa<F$. The origin is a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) and the two nonzero [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are [sink equilibria](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md). Its [stable manifold](../../../../../../stable-manifold.md) separates their basins; there is no [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md).
- IV: $\lambda<0,F<\kappa<G$. There are two [sink equilibria](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md) and the [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md), an unstable [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) surrounding all three, and a larger attracting [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md). Initial conditions inside the unstable [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) go to one of the [sink equilibria](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md), according to the [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md)'s basin boundary; those outside it approach the attracting [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md).
- V: $\lambda<0,G<\kappa<H$. The [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) and two [sink equilibria](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md) remain, but each [sink equilibrium](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md) is surrounded by its own unstable [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md). A larger attracting [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) surrounds the entire configuration. Each unstable inner [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) bounds its [sink equilibrium](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md)'s basin.
- VI: $\lambda<0,\kappa>H$. The two nonzero [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are repelling. The [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md)'s [stable manifold](../../../../../../stable-manifold.md) still leads to the [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md), while generic trajectories approach the attracting outer [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md).

The changes III–IV, IV–V and V–VI are respectively a fold of [periodic orbits](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md), the double homoclinic event, and the two [subcritical Hopf bifurcations](../../../../../../subcritical-hopf-bifurcation.md). Thus the unstable [periodic orbits](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) from the [subcritical Hopf bifurcations](../../../../../../subcritical-hopf-bifurcation.md), the expanding [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) passage, and the attracting [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) from the other [Hopf bifurcation](../../../../../../hopf-bifurcation.md) fit into one consistent portrait.<a id="1/e/image-the-six-local-phase-portrait-types-solid-cycles-attract-and-dashed-cycles-repel"></a>
