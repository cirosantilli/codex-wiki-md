<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The global curve is a homoclinic connection to the lower positive [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), which is the [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) created at the [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md). Near $\lambda=1/2$ on the side $\lambda>1/2$, place it at

$$
\boxed{\mu_G(\lambda)>\mu_H(\lambda)>-1/4,}
$$

tangent to $\mu=-1/4$ at the double-zero point. The stable [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) occupies the strip $\mu_H<\mu<\mu_G$ and approaches the [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) on the upper boundary. The dashed curve in the two-parameter figure indicates this local placement; its drawn numerical coefficient is schematic, not a computed global-bifurcation formula.

This placement is consistent with the supplied [normal form](../../../../../../normal-form-dynamical-systems.md). Its [centre equilibrium](../../../../../../center-equilibrium.md) has a [supercritical Hopf bifurcation](../../../../../../supercritical-hopf-bifurcation.md), and its [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) has negative [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) on the relevant loop branch, allowing a stable [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) to disappear in the [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) loop. No global-curve calculation is required here.
