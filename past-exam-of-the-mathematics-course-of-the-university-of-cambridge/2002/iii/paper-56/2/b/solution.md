<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At $\mu=-1/4$, the two branches meet at $(1/2,1/4)$. The [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) there are $0$ and $1/2-\lambda$. For $\lambda\ne1/2$, the nonzero quadratic term and transverse stability identify a [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md). If $\lambda>1/2$, it creates a [sink equilibrium](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md) and a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) as $\mu$ increases; if $\lambda<1/2$, it creates a [source equilibrium](../../../../../../source-equilibrium.md) and a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md). At $\lambda=1/2$, the zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is double and the ordinary saddle-node description needs the two-parameter unfolding.

At $\mu=0$, the origin and lower branch cross. Since $s_-=-\mu+O(\mu^2)$, the branches cross transversely in the $(\mu,u)$ diagram. The origin changes from a [sink equilibrium](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md) to a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) as $\mu$ increases through zero; the lower branch changes from a positive-$u$ [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) to a negative-$u$ [sink equilibrium](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md). This is a [transcritical bifurcation](../../../../../../transcritical-bifurcation.md). A local centre-manifold calculation also gives $\dot u=\mu u+u^2+\cdots$, because $v=O(u^2)$ and the transverse [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is $-\lambda<0$.

The figure uses a representative $\lambda>1/2$; the upper branch's additional Hopf change is indicated. For $\lambda<1/2$ the upper branch is unstable everywhere on its existence interval. The exact [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) criterion in part (a) specifies stability for every fixed positive $\lambda$.<a id="2/b/image-equilibrium-branches-and-the-two-parameter-diagram-of-the-quadratic-product-flow"></a>
