<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Only the upper [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) can have zero [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) with positive [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md). Setting $s_+=\lambda$ gives

$$
\boxed{\mu_H(\lambda)=\lambda(\lambda-1),\qquad \lambda>\frac12.}
$$

At this point the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is $\lambda^2(2\lambda-1)>0$, so the angular frequency is $\lambda\sqrt{2\lambda-1}$. Moreover $ds_+/d\mu=1/\sqrt{1+4\mu}>0$, so the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) crosses zero transversely as $\mu$ increases. The stipulated [supercritical Hopf bifurcation](../../../../../../supercritical-hopf-bifurcation.md) creates a stable [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) on the side $\mu>\mu_H$ where the upper [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) becomes unstable.

The steady-state curves are the vertical lines $\mu=-1/4$ and $\mu=0$ in the $(\mu,\lambda)$ [plane](../../../../../../plane.md). The Hopf parabola has its endpoint at

$$
\boxed{(\mu,\lambda)=(-1/4,1/2),\qquad (u,v)=(1/2,1/4).}
$$

There the Jacobian has [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) and [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) zero but is not the zero matrix: it has a nilpotent Jordan block. This is the codimension-two Takens-Bogdanov point. The intersection of the Hopf curve with $\mu=0$ at $\lambda=1$ concerns a different [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) from the transcritical crossing at the origin; it is not another double-zero [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md).
