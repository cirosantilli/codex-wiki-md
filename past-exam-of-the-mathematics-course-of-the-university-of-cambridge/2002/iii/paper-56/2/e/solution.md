↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The four nearby portrait types in $u>0$ can be organized by increasing $\mu$ at fixed $\lambda>1/2$ close to $1/2$:

- A: $\mu<-1/4$. There is no interior [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md); trajectories approach the stable boundary [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) at the origin.
- B: $-1/4<\mu<\mu_H$. The lower interior [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) and the upper one a [sink equilibrium](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md). The [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md)'s [stable manifold](../../../../../../stable-manifold.md) separates trajectories approaching the [sink equilibrium](../../../../../../sink-equilibrium.md) from those approaching the origin.
- C: $\mu_H<\mu<\mu_G$. The upper [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is repelling, enclosed by an attracting [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md). The [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md)'s [stable manifold](../../../../../../stable-manifold.md) separates the [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md)'s basin from the origin's basin.
- D: $\mu>\mu_G$, still in the local neighborhood with $\mu<0$. The [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) and repelling upper [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) remain, but the [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) has disappeared. Generic trajectories ultimately approach the origin.

The no-cycle region D continues around the double-zero point onto the $\lambda<1/2$ side. The sketches distinguish the boundary origin from the two interior [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) and show the [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) only in region C.<a id="2/e/image-four-portraits-near-the-double-zero-point-and-the-positive-parameter-attracting-cycle-regime"></a>
