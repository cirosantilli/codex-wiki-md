<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [easy lambda term](../../../../../../easy-lambda-term.md) must be equatable consistently with every closed [lambda term](../../../../../../lambda-term.md). Choose the proposed equation $I=K$. Applying it to $I$ gives $I=\lambda y.I$. Applying that equation to an arbitrary closed $V$ gives $V=I$. In particular the two [Church Booleans](../../../../../../church-boolean.md) become equal; then applying them to arbitrary $A,B$ gives $A=B$. The extended equational theory is trivial, hence inconsistent in the required sense. Therefore **$I$ is not easy**.
