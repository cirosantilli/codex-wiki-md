<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The syntax operation $(s,t)\mapsto\#(ST)$, where $s=\#S,t=\#T$, is a [total computable function](../../../../../../total-computable-function.md) on valid codes: decode the finite syntax trees, form their application, and encode it again. Extend it arbitrarily on invalid codes if necessary. Similarly $n\mapsto\#(c_n)$ is total computable because the finite numeral syntax can be generated effectively from $n$. The preceding characterization of [lambda-definable functions](../../../../../../lambda-definable-function.md) supplies closed representatives $A,C$ with

$$
\boxed{A d(S)d(T)\equiv_\beta d(ST),\qquad Cc_n\equiv_\beta d(c_n).}
$$

Here $d(S)=c_{\#S}$ is [lambda quotation](../../../../../../lambda-quotation.md), not a code of a [beta equivalence](../../../../../../beta-equivalence.md) class. In particular $C$ quotes the syntax of the numeral itself; it is not simply successor or an identity operation.
