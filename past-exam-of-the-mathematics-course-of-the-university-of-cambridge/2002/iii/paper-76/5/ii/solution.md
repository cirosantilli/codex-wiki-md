<h1 id="5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose $x$ fresh for $F$ and put $H=\lambda x.F(Ax(Cx))$, $Q=H d(H)$. The preceding coding equations give

$$
C d(H)\equiv_\beta d(d(H)),\qquad A d(H)(C d(H))\equiv_\beta d(H d(H))=d(Q).
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{Q\to_\beta F\bigl(A d(H)(C d(H))\bigr)\equiv_\beta Fd(Q).}
$$

This [self-quoting fixed point](../../../../../../self-quoting-fixed-point.md) is valid even if $F$ has [free variables](../../../../../../free-variable.md), since the effective [Gödel numbering](../../../../../../godel-numbering.md) codes open [lambda terms](../../../../../../lambda-term.md) too. Freshness of $x$ prevents variable capture.
