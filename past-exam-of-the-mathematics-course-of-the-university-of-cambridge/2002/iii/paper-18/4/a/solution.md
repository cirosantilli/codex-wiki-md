<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $v_{1,+}=e_1+ie_2$, $v_{1,-}=e_1-ie_2$, $v_{2,+}=e_3+ie_4$, $v_{2,-}=e_3-ie_4$ and $v_0=e_5$. Direct multiplication by the printed rotation blocks gives their torus eigenvalues

$$
e^{ix_1},\ e^{-ix_1},\ e^{ix_2},\ e^{-ix_2},\ 1.
$$

Thus the differentiated [weights of a representation](../../../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) are $ix_1,-ix_1,ix_2,-ix_2,0$, with this sign convention fixed by the given matrix blocks.

A wedge of two distinct eigenvectors has the product eigenvalue, so its differentiated weight is the sum. The ten vectors in a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of the [exterior square](../../../../../../exterior-square.md) split into three groups:

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{vectors}&\text{weights}&\text{number}\\
 v_{1,\sigma}\wedge v_{2,\tau}&i(\sigma x_1+\tau x_2),\ \sigma,\tau=\pm1&4\\
 v_{j,\sigma}\wedge v_0&i\sigma x_j,\ j=1,2,\ \sigma=\pm1&4\\
 v_{1,+}\wedge v_{1,-},\ v_{2,+}\wedge v_{2,-}&0&2
\end{array}
$$

The eight nonzero weights are distinct, so each has multiplicity one; the zero [weight space](../../../../../../weight-space.md) has dimension two. The multiplicities add to $\binom52=10$, accounting for the entire representation.
