<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) equation balances the gradient of the total [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) against gravity. In a [thin disk](../../../../../../thin-disk.md) with [Keplerian rotation](../../../../../../keplerian-disk.md), expanding the central gravitational field about the midplane gives vertical acceleration $-\Omega^2z$, so $p_z=-\rho\Omega^2z$. Disk self-gravity is neglected in this approximation.

The flux-gradient equation is local thermal balance: the upward [radiative flux](../../../../../../radiative-flux.md) increases with height because [differential rotation](../../../../../../differential-rotation.md) dissipates [energy](../../../../../../energy.md). For a Newtonian effective [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity.md) $\mu$, the volumetric heating rate is $\mu(r\Omega')^2$, which becomes $9\mu\Omega^2/4$ for [Keplerian rotation](../../../../../../keplerian-disk.md). The symbol $\mu$ here is [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity.md), not [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md); the latter is $\nu=\mu/\rho$.

The [radiative diffusion](../../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md) equation applies to an [optically thick medium](../../../../../../optically-thick-medium.md) in [local thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md). Its [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) coefficient is $c/(3\kappa\rho)$, and the equilibrium radiation [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md) is $u_{\rm rad}=4\sigma T^4/c$. Hence

$$
F=-\frac{c}{3\kappa\rho}\frac{du_{\rm rad}}{dz}=-\frac{16\sigma T^3}{3\kappa\rho}\frac{dT}{dz}.
$$

The minus sign means [energy](../../../../../../energy.md) flows down the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) gradient. Here $\sigma$ is the [Stefan-Boltzmann constant](../../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-constant.md), $\kappa$ the [opacity](../../../../../../opacity.md) per unit [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), and $c$ the [speed of light](../../../../../../speed-of-light.md). Constant [Thomson scattering](../../../../../../thomson-scattering.md) [opacity](../../../../../../opacity.md) is the adopted transport approximation; [local thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) is an additional assumption behind the radiation [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md).

Finally the [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) adds [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas.md) [gas pressure](../../../../../../gas-pressure.md) $p_g=\rho kT/(\mu_m m_H)$ and isotropic [radiation pressure](../../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) $p_r=u_{\rm rad}/3=4\sigma T^4/(3c)$. The [Boltzmann constant](../../../../../../boltzmann-constant.md) is $k$, and $\mu_m m_H$ is the mean [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) per gas particle. Both contributions supply the total [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) entering vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md).
