<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the limiting model with negligible [gas pressure](../../../../../../gas-pressure.md), $p=p_r=4\sigma T^4/(3c)$. Its gradient is $p_z=16\sigma T^3T_z/(3c)$. Combining [radiative diffusion](../../../../../../radiative-diffusion.md) and [hydrostatic equilibrium](../../../../../../hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives

$$
F=-\frac{c}{\kappa\rho}p_z=\frac{c\Omega^2}{\kappa}z.
$$

Because $\kappa$ and $\Omega$ are constant with height at the fixed disk radius,

$$
\frac{dF}{dz}=\frac{c\Omega^2}{\kappa}.
$$

Equating this to the viscous heating rate $9\mu\Omega^2/4$ forces

$$
\boxed{\mu=\frac{4c}{9\kappa}.}
$$

This proves [constant viscosity in radiation-supported vertical balance](../../../../../../constant-viscosity-in-radiation-supported-vertical-balance.md). It fixes the local effective [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity.md) uniquely, independent of height, [surface density](../../../../../../surface-density-of-a-disk.md) and radius within this ideal limiting model. It does not fix a height-independent [kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md): $\nu(z)=4c/(9\kappa\rho(z))$ still depends on the local [mass density](../../../../../../density.md).
