<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A loop [integral](../../../../../../integral.md) in a theory with dimensionless classical couplings can contain a logarithmic [ultraviolet divergence](../../../../../../ultraviolet-divergence.md). After subtraction, an expression such as $\log(-p^2/\mu^2)$ remains; one must specify the [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) scale at which the measured or [renormalized coupling](../../../../../../renormalized-coupling.md) is fixed. A logarithm of a dimensionful [momentum](../../../../../../momentum.md) alone is not meaningful. In [dimensional regularization](../../../../../../dimensional-regularization.md), the same necessity appears through $\lambda_{\rm bare}=\mu^{d_\lambda}\,Z_\lambda\lambda(\mu)$ in the continued dimension, with $d_\lambda$ tending to zero in the physical dimension.

Thus the [renormalization](../../../../../../renormalization.md) prescription introduces an arbitrary [renormalization scale](../../../../../../renormalization-scale.md), even when no [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) or length occurs in the classical action. Physical quantities cannot depend on its arbitrary choice: explicit logarithms cancel the implicit change of the [running coupling](../../../../../../running-coupling.md), governed by $\mu\,d\lambda/d\mu=\beta(\lambda)$. A nonzero [renormalization-group beta function](../../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) is a quantum scale anomaly. An integration constant may encode the same data as a dimensionful scale, which is [dimensional transmutation](../../../../../../dimensional-transmutation.md). **The scale labels a [renormalization](../../../../../../renormalization.md) condition, not a new arbitrary measurable parameter.** At a true [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) the coupling need not run; the use of a subtraction scale is then a convention rather than an intrinsic generated physical scale.
