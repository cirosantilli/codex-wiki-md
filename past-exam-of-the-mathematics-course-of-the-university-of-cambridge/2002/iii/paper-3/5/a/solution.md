<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The PDF has $q$-power exponents; the [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is a [linearized polynomial over a finite field](../../../../../../linearized-polynomial-over-a-finite-field.md), not the degree-$d$ [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) produced by the converted TeX. Let $V$ be its root set in an [algebraic closure](../../../../../../algebraic-closure.md). Because $(x+y)^{q^i}=x^{q^i}+y^{q^i}$ and $c^{q^i}=c$ for $c\in\mathbb F_q$,

$$
f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y),\qquad f(cx)=c f(x).
$$

Thus $V$ is an $\mathbb F_q$-vector space. Its derivative is $a_0\ne0$, and its degree is $q^d$, so it has exactly $q^d$ distinct roots. A finite [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of that size has dimension $d$.

Every splitting-field automorphism fixes $\mathbb F_q$ and carries roots to roots. Hence it acts linearly on $V$. The action is faithful because the [splitting field](../../../../../../splitting-field.md) is generated by the roots. Choosing a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) identifies this injection with

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f,\mathbb F_q(t))\hookrightarrow GL(d,q).}
$$

Different bases conjugate the matrix subgroup. The linear action itself is canonical, while a particular matrix realization is basis-dependent.
