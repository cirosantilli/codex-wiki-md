<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use $R=\mathbb F_2[t]$. At $t=1$ the specialization is $X^5+X^2+1$, already proved irreducible and separable. Because the original [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is monic, this proves irreducibility over $\mathbb F_2(t)$ and gives a transitive group on five roots.

At $t=0$ the specialization factors as

$$
X^5+X+1=(X^2+X+1)(X^3+X^2+1).
$$

Neither factor has a root in $\mathbb F_2$, so they are irreducible; the factors are coprime and the specialization is separable. For example its derivative is $(X+1)^4$ and its value at one is one. The unramified reduction test gives a $(2)(3)$ [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) whose cube is a [transposition](../../../../../../transposition-permutation.md). The same primitive prime-degree graph argument now gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(f,\mathbb F_2(t))=S_5.}
$$

No characteristic-zero discriminant-sign argument is being transferred to characteristic two: good specialization and the explicit cycle test are sufficient.
