<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fix ordered branch positions $P=(p_1,\ldots,p_r)$ and conjugacy classes $C_i$ in $G$. Form the generating product-one set

$$
\mathrm{Ni}(G;\mathbf C)=
\{(g_1,\ldots,g_r):g_i\in C_i,\ g_1\cdots g_r=1,
\ \langle g_1,\ldots,g_r\rangle=G\}.
$$

The [rigid ramification type](../../../../../../rigid-ramification-type.md) condition is that this set is nonempty and consists of one orbit under simultaneous inner conjugation by $G$. Rationality of classes is a further arithmetic condition and is not needed for this complex uniqueness statement.

Let $U=\mathbb P^1(\mathbb C)\setminus P$. Choose a base point and positively oriented peripheral loops $\gamma_i$ with sole relation $\gamma_1\cdots\gamma_r=1$. A connected Galois cover over $U$ gives a surjective [monodromy](../../../../../../monodromy.md) map $\pi_1(U)\to G$, and changing the labeling of one fiber conjugates it. Its peripheral images are exactly a tuple in the set above.

For two extensions of the same rigid type, rigidity conjugates their tuples to agree. The loops generate $\pi_1(U)$, so their entire [monodromy](../../../../../../monodromy.md) maps then agree. This constructs an isomorphism of the unramified covers directly: label fibers at the base point identically, and transport that matching along paths. Independence of path follows from equal [monodromy](../../../../../../monodromy.md). The resulting map is [holomorphic](../../../../../../complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) because the cover maps are local biholomorphisms.

Near each missing point, the cover has local form $u=\pi^{e_i}$. The isomorphism therefore extends over the added points, equivalently by the removable-singularity theorem in these local parameters. Pullback of meromorphic functions gives an isomorphism of the function [fields](../../../../../../field.md) fixing $\mathbb C(t)$. Hence **there is at most one extension of the prescribed rigid type**. This is uniqueness for fixed branch positions and peripheral conventions, not uniqueness after arbitrarily moving the branch points.
