<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Use finitely generated right [projective modules](../../../../../projective-module.md) throughout. The [group](../../../../../group-split.md) [K0 of a ring](../../../../../k0-of-a-ring.md) is the [Grothendieck group](../../../../../grothendieck-group.md) of their direct-sum [monoid](../../../../../monoid.md), with $[P\oplus Q]=[P]+[Q]$. The [group](../../../../../group-split.md) [K1 of a ring](../../../../../k1-of-a-ring.md) is $\mathrm{GL}_\infty(A)/E_\infty(A)$, equivalently the [abelianization](../../../../../abelianization.md) of the [stable general linear group](../../../../../stable-general-linear-group.md). Stabilization is $g\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(g,1)$; $E_\infty$ is generated by elementary [transvections](../../../../../transvection.md). The [stable elementary subgroup](../../../../../stable-elementary-subgroup.md) is the [commutator subgroup](../../../../../commutator-subgroup.md): elementary [transvections](../../../../../transvection.md) are commutators using a third index, and stable block interchange and elementary row identities make the quotient abelian.

Write $A_0$ for the lower-right [ring](../../../../../ring.md), printed as $A'$ in the source. A [Milnor square](../../../../../milnor-square.md) has unital commuting maps, is a [pullback](../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md)

$$
A=\{(a_1,a_2)\in A_1\times A_2:j_1(a_1)=j_2(a_2)\},
$$

and has at least one of $j_1,j_2$ surjective. Interchange the two sides if necessary so that $j_1$ is surjective.

We first establish the patching fact used in the proof. For projective $P_i$ and an [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md) $\phi:P_1\otimes A_0\to P_2\otimes A_0$, set

$$
M(P_1,P_2,\phi)=\{(x_1,x_2):\phi(\bar x_1)=\bar x_2\}.
$$

For [free modules](../../../../../free-module.md) denote the result by $M_g$. The block [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) identity

$$
\begin{pmatrix}g&0\\0&g^{-1}\end{pmatrix}=W(g)W(-I),\qquad W(g)=\begin{pmatrix}I&g\\0&I\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\-g^{-1}&I\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}I&g\\0&I\end{pmatrix}
$$

expresses $\operatorname{diag}(g,g^{-1})$ as [elementary matrices](../../../../../elementary-matrix.md). Their entries lift through $j_1$, and their elementary lifts remain invertible. A change of basis on the first side then trivializes this gluing. Thus $M_g\oplus M_{g^{-1}}\cong A^{2r}$, proving that $M_g$ is finitely generated projective. Notice that we lifted [elementary matrices](../../../../../elementary-matrix.md), not arbitrary invertible [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md).

For general $P_1,P_2$, choose $P_1\oplus Q_1\cong A_1^r$ and $P_2\oplus Q_2\cong A_2^s$. The reductions of $Q_1\oplus A_1^s$ and $Q_2\oplus A_2^r$ are isomorphic: insert the common reduced $P_i$ and reorder the two complements. Patch them by such an [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md). The sum with $M(P_1,P_2,\phi)$ now patches two [free modules](../../../../../free-module.md) of equal size, so is projective by the free case, making each summand projective. The same free change of basis followed by these direct-summand projections shows that extension to $A_i$ recovers $P_i$. Conversely, write an $A$-projective as the image of an [idempotent](../../../../../idempotent.md) over a finite [free module](../../../../../free-module.md). Since [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) over the [pullback](../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) are compatible pairs of [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md), applying the compatible [idempotents](../../../../../idempotent.md) to the free [pullback](../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) reconstructs this projective from its two reductions. Compatible module [isomorphisms](../../../../../isomorphism.md) patch as well. This proves [Milnor patching of projective modules](../../../../../milnor-patching-of-projective-modules.md) in the form needed below.

The diagonal maps in the sequence are [extension of scalars](../../../../../extension-of-scalars.md); the two maps to $A_0$ are differences $j_{1*}-j_{2*}$. Define the connecting map by

$$
\partial[g]=[M_g]-[A^r],\qquad g\in\mathrm{GL}_r(A_0).
$$

It is unchanged by stabilization or elementary multiplication: an elementary factor lifts through $j_1$ and changes a free basis in the gluing. The identity $\operatorname{diag}(g,h)=\operatorname{diag}(gh,I)\operatorname{diag}(h^{-1},h)$ and the preceding elementary factorization show $M_g\oplus M_h\cong M_{gh}\oplus A^r$ after matching sizes. Thus $\partial$ is an additive [homomorphism](../../../../../homomorphism.md) on $K_1(A_0)$.

**Exactness at $K_1(A_1)\oplus K_1(A_2)$.** If the two reduced classes agree, choose stabilized representatives $g_1,g_2$ of the same size. Their reductions differ by an [elementary matrix](../../../../../elementary-matrix.md) $e$ over $A_0$. Lift $e$ to an [elementary matrix](../../../../../elementary-matrix.md) over $A_1$ and adjust $g_1$ without changing its $K_1$ class. Its reduction now equals that of $g_2$. The compatible pair and its compatible inverse give an invertible [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) over $A$, so the original pair of classes is in the diagonal image. The reverse containment follows from commutativity of the square.

**Exactness at $K_1(A_0)$.** A gluing obtained from a change of basis over either upper [ring](../../../../../ring.md) patches a [free module](../../../../../free-module.md), so the difference-map image is killed by $\partial$. Conversely, $\partial[g]=0$ means that $M_g$ becomes free after adding a common projective summand. Add a complement of that summand to obtain an actual [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md) $M_g\oplus A^s\cong A^{r+s}$. Its two local bases express $g\oplus I_s=j_2(v_2)j_1(v_1)^{-1}$. Therefore $[g]=j_{2*}[v_2]-j_{1*}[v_1]$, which lies in the image of the difference map. This uses stable equality of projective classes, not cancellation of arbitrary projectives.

**Exactness at $K_0(A)$.** Connecting classes reduce to zero on both sides. Conversely, write a class in the kernel as $[P]-[A^r]$ by adding a complement to its negative projective term. Its reductions have the same classes as $A_i^r$. Equality in $K_0(A_i)$ becomes an [isomorphism](../../../../../isomorphism.md) after a finite common free summand, by taking a complement to the auxiliary projective. Use one sufficiently large free summand on both sides. The stabilized $P$ is then locally free with gluing $g$, so the original class is $[M_g]-[A^{r+s}]=\partial[g]$.

**Exactness at $K_0(A_1)\oplus K_0(A_2)$.** Write a kernel pair as $([P_1]-[A_1^r],[P_2]-[A_2^r])$, using complements and padding to make the free sizes equal. Their reduced positive classes agree. Adding a common finite free summand makes those reductions isomorphic. Patch the resulting projectives, and subtract the matching free $A$-module. Its restrictions are the desired pair. The reverse containment is immediate because both reductions to $A_0$ agree.

These arguments prove the [Mayer-Vietoris sequence in algebraic K-theory](../../../../../mayer-vietoris-sequence-in-algebraic-k-theory.md)

$$
\boxed{K_1A\to K_1A_1\oplus K_1A_2\to K_1A_0\xrightarrow{\partial}K_0A\to K_0A_1\oplus K_0A_2\to K_0A_0.}
$$

No zero is inserted at either end; this fragment does not assert injectivity at the first term or surjectivity at the last.

For the [group ring](../../../../../group-ring.md) of the [cyclic group](../../../../../cyclic-group.md), take the [cyclotomic Milnor square](../../../../../cyclotomic-milnor-square.md)

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}\mathbb Z[C_p]&\longrightarrow&\mathbb Z\\\downarrow&&\downarrow\\\mathbb Z[\zeta]&\longrightarrow&\mathbb F_p.\end{array}
$$

The maps send the cyclic generator to $1$ and to $\zeta$; the lower map sets $\zeta=1$, and the right map reduces modulo $p$. Since $\Phi_p(1)=p$, $\mathbb Z[\zeta]/(\zeta-1)=\mathbb F_p$. To verify the [pullback](../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md), represent an element by a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $p-1$. If both evaluations vanish, it is a constant multiple of the [minimal polynomial of an algebraic element](../../../../../minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) $\Phi_p$, and evaluation at one forces that constant to be zero. Conversely, represent $b\in\mathbb Z[\zeta]$ by $f$ of degree below $p-1$. For a compatible integer $a$, $a-f(1)$ is divisible by $p$; replacing $f$ by $f+[(a-f(1))/p]\Phi_p$ yields the pair $(a,b)$. The right map is surjective, so this is a [Milnor square](../../../../../milnor-square.md).

The hinted [cyclotomic units](../../../../../cyclotomic-unit.md) are $u_k=1+\zeta+\cdots+\zeta^{k-1}$. If $kl\equiv1\pmod p$ with $1\le l<p$, the geometric sum $\sum_{j=0}^{l-1}\zeta^{kj}$ is their inverse, because $\zeta^{kl}=\zeta$. Their residues are $k\in\mathbb F_p^\times$, covering every unit of the common [field](../../../../../field.md). [Gaussian elimination](../../../../../gaussian-elimination.md) identifies $K_1(\mathbb F_p)$ with $\mathbb F_p^\times$ by [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md). Thus $K_1(\mathbb Z[\zeta])\to K_1(\mathbb F_p)$ is surjective, and the connecting map vanishes.

The remaining exact sequence injects $K_0(\mathbb Z[C_p])$ into $\mathbb Z\oplus K_0(\mathbb Z[\zeta])$, with image $\{(r,x):r=\operatorname{rank}x\}$. Here $K_0(\mathbb Z)=K_0(\mathbb F_p)=\mathbb Z$, and reduction of a projective over the [integral domain](../../../../../integral-domain.md) $\mathbb Z[\zeta]$ has dimension its rank: localize at $(\zeta-1)$, where the projective is free, and compare its residue-field dimension with its fraction-field dimension. Projection onto the second factor is therefore a bijection on this kernel. Hence extension along the cyclotomic evaluation gives

$$
\boxed{K_0(\mathbb Z[C_p])\cong K_0(\mathbb Z[\zeta]).}
$$

The essential step is [reduction of cyclotomic units at the ramified prime](../../../../../reduction-of-cyclotomic-units-at-the-ramified-prime.md); surjectivity of a [ring](../../../../../ring.md) map alone would not imply surjectivity on $K_1$.
