<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [complete market](../../../../../../../complete-market.md) makes every state-contingent [consumption](../../../../../../../consumption.md) vector attainable. Its budget is $\sum_sp_sc_s=100$, and expected [logarithmic utility](../../../../../../../logarithmic-utility.md) under the equally likely states is $\tfrac14\sum_s\log c_s$, with $c_s>0$. Increasing [utility](../../../../../../../utility.md) makes the budget bind. For a [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) $\eta$, the [first-order conditions](../../../../../../../first-order-optimality-condition.md) are

$$
\frac1{4c_s}=\eta p_s,\qquad c_s=\frac1{4\eta p_s}.
$$

Substitution in the budget gives $1/\eta=100$, so $\eta=0.01$ and

$$
\boxed{c=(250,\ 125,\ \tfrac{250}{3},\ \tfrac{250}{3})^t.}
$$

Every state receives an expenditure $p_sc_s=25$, rather than equal [consumption](../../../../../../../consumption.md). [Strict concavity](../../../../../../../strict-concavity.md) of [logarithmic utility](../../../../../../../logarithmic-utility.md) makes this feasible stationary point the unique global maximizer. The physical probabilities enter the [utility](../../../../../../../utility.md) objective; the supporting prices enter the budget, and they must not be interchanged.
