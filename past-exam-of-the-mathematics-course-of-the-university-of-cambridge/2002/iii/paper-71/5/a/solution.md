<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $\mathcal H=a'/a$ and choose the residual synchronous frame comoving with [cold dark matter](../../../../../../cold-dark-matter.md), so $\mathbf v_C=0$. Its Euler equation preserves this choice. The continuity equation then gives $\delta_C'=h'/2$. Neglect background [baryon](../../../../../../baryon.md) [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) and all unenhanced corrections of order $c_s^2$, but retain $c_s^2k^2$ because a short wavelength can make it important. The trace equation becomes

$$
h''+\mathcal Hh'=3\mathcal H^2(\Omega_C\delta_C+\Omega_B\delta_B).
$$

Using $h'=2\delta_C'$ proves

$$
\boxed{\delta_C''+\mathcal H\delta_C'-\frac32\mathcal H^2(\Omega_C\delta_C+\Omega_B\delta_B)=0}.
$$

For [baryons](../../../../../../baryon.md) let $\theta_B=i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf v_B$. The retained continuity and Euler terms are $\delta_B'=-\theta_B+h'/2$ and $\theta_B'+\mathcal H\theta_B-c_s^2k^2\delta_B=0$. Differentiate the first and substitute the second:

$$
\delta_B''+\mathcal H\delta_B'+c_s^2k^2\delta_B=\frac12(h''+\mathcal Hh').
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\delta_B''+\mathcal H\delta_B'-\frac32\mathcal H^2(\Omega_C\delta_C+\Omega_B\delta_B)+c_s^2k^2\delta_B=0}.
$$

The [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) sign is stabilizing. The drag terms contain first derivatives of the [density](../../../../../../density.md) perturbations, as printed in the PDF; those primes are lost in the converted TeX. The approximation assumes slowly varying nonrelativistic background [baryon](../../../../../../baryon.md) [pressure](../../../../../../pressure.md) and the growing physical mode, not a residual [synchronous gauge](../../../../../../synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md) mode.
