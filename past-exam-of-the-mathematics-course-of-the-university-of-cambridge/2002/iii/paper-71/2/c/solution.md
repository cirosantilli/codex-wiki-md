<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Above the transition,

$$
\frac{\Gamma_{\rm int}}H=\frac{\alpha_Y^2m_{\rm pl}}{1.66\sqrt{g_*}\,T}=\frac{T_{\rm eq}}T,\qquad\boxed{T_{\rm eq}\sim7\times10^{11}\ {\rm GeV}}
$$

for $g_*\sim100$. The ratio increases during cooling, rather than at higher [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md). A GUT transition at $T_c\sim10^{15}$–$10^{16}$ GeV occurs well above this scale, so the interaction is too slow to establish or maintain [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md) throughout $T>T_c$. In fact its integrated interaction probability from an arbitrarily high [temperature](../../../../../../temperature.md) down to $T_c$ is at most of order $T_{\rm eq}/T_c\ll1$, since $dt=-dT/(HT)$.

An initial [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) with zero [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential.md) therefore cannot be assumed from this high-temperature rate alone. If imposed initially and collisionlessly redshifted while the particles are massless, its thermal-looking form can persist, but this is not dynamically maintained [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../../thermal-equilibrium.md). Other initial states can remain nonthermal. This is the [thermalization temperature for a massless gauge mediator](../../../../../../thermalization-temperature-for-a-massless-gauge-mediator.md).

The below-transition rate stipulated in the model has $\Gamma/H=(T/T_D)^3$. If $T_c\gg T_D$ and it includes efficient number-changing reactions, it can restore [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) after the transition and erase the initial-state memory, making the estimates in a and b applicable. If it only equilibrates momenta, or if [reheating](../../../../../../reheating.md) never populates the relevant range, the [thermal relic](../../../../../../thermal-relic.md) calculation must be reconsidered.
