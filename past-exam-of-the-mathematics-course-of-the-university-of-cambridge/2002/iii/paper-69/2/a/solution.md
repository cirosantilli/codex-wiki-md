<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose

$$
m(x_\parallel,\mathbf x_\perp)=\int\frac{dq_\parallel\,d^{d-1}q_\perp}{(2\pi)^d}\,
\widetilde m(q_\parallel,\mathbf q_\perp)e^{i(q_\parallel x_\parallel+\mathbf q_\perp\cdot\mathbf x_\perp)}.
$$

For a real scalar field $\widetilde m(-q)=\widetilde m(q)^*$. The parallel derivative becomes $iq_\parallel$, and the transverse [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) becomes $-q_\perp^2$. The latter is squared in the energy, producing $q_\perp^4$, not $q_\perp^2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\beta H=\frac12\int_q[Kq_\parallel^2+Lq_\perp^4+t]|\widetilde m(q)|^2-h\widetilde m(0).}
$$

The zero-momentum source is understood in a finite box or as a uniform-field distribution before taking the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md). With an orthonormal box transform the source is $-h\sqrt V\,m_0$. The positive coefficients and $t>0$ give a normalizable [Gaussian field theory](../../../../../../gaussian-field-theory.md), with mean uniform field $\overline m=h/t$ and connected Fourier [covariance](../../../../../../covariance.md) $[Kq_\parallel^2+Lq_\perp^4+t]^{-1}$.
