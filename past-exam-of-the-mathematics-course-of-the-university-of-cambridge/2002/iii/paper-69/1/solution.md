[Spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) occurs when the [statistical Hamiltonian](../../../../../statistical-hamiltonian.md) is invariant under a [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) but a chosen equilibrium pure state is not. A small selecting field or a [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) chooses one of several states related by the [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md). For a finite unconstrained system the symmetric ensemble generally has zero [order parameter](../../../../../order-parameter.md); spontaneous order is defined by taking the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md) before removing the selecting field. A ferromagnet with a selected magnetization direction is the standard example.

If the broken [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) is continuous, nearby symmetry-related states cost no energy for a uniform displacement along the manifold of states. Locality and a nonzero stiffness then make a slowly varying displacement cost [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) energy rather than a mass term. These gapless long-wavelength fluctuations are [Goldstone modes](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md). In the conventional relativistic internal-symmetry setting their count is the number of broken generators. Nonrelativistic dynamics can pair broken generators, as in a ferromagnet; gauge [symmetries](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) and broken space-time [symmetries](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) require additional qualifications. A discrete broken [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) alone does not produce a [Goldstone mode](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md).

For an interface with a selected mean height, the continuous displacement is a rigid translation normal to the mean plane. The tension gives a stiffness for spatially varying translations. The following calculation concerns this static Goldstone sector. It determines the excitation energy and equilibrium fluctuations, but a frequency dispersion requires a separate inertial or dissipative dynamics. In low dimensions thermal roughness also limits the existence of a strictly localized flat interface in an infinite system.
