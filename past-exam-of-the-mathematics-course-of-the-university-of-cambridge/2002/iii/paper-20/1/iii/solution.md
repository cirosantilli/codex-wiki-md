<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $f:A\to B$ is a [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md), each [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $\mathcal C(U,A)\to\mathcal C(U,B)$ given by postcomposition with $f$ is injective. Consequently, if two [natural transformations](../../../../../../natural-transformation.md) into $H_A$ become equal after $H_\bullet(f)$, their components are equal, and the transformations themselves are equal. This proves that $H_\bullet(f)$ is a [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md) in the [presheaf category](../../../../../../presheaf-category.md).

Conversely, suppose $H_\bullet(f)$ is a [monomorphism](../../../../../../monomorphism.md) and $u,v:U\to A$ satisfy $fu=fv$. Then $H_\bullet(f)H_\bullet(u)=H_\bullet(f)H_\bullet(v)$. Cancellation gives $H_\bullet(u)=H_\bullet(v)$, and faithfulness of the [Yoneda embedding](../../../../../../yoneda-embedding.md) gives $u=v$. Hence **$H_\bullet(f)$ is monic exactly when $f$ is monic**.
