<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

A [poset](../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) $P$ becomes a [category](../../../../../category-split.md) with the elements of $P$ as objects and exactly one [morphism](../../../../../morphism.md) $x\to y$ when $x\leq y$, with no [morphism](../../../../../morphism.md) otherwise. Reflexivity supplies the [identity morphisms](../../../../../identity-morphism.md) and transitivity supplies [composition in a category](../../../../../composition-in-a-category.md); uniqueness of [morphisms](../../../../../morphism.md) proves associativity. A [functor](../../../../../functor.md) $f:P\to Q$ is exactly a [monotone function](../../../../../monotonic-function.md): it must send $x\leq y$ to $f(x)\leq f(y)$, and this condition suffices because all the required [morphisms](../../../../../morphism.md) are unique. Antisymmetry says that isomorphic objects of this [category](../../../../../category-split.md) are equal.
