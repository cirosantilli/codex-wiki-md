<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $1$ be a [terminal object](../../../../../../terminal-object.md). The [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) of $X\to1\leftarrow Y$ has precisely the [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md) of the binary [product in a category](../../../../../../product-category-theory.md) $X\times Y$: every pair of [morphisms](../../../../../../morphism.md) to $X,Y$ is compatible over $1$. Repeated binary [products in a category](../../../../../../product-category-theory.md), together with $1$ as the empty product, give all finite [products in a category](../../../../../../product-category-theory.md).

For $f,g:X\rightrightarrows Y$, form the [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) of the [categorical diagonal](../../../../../../categorical-diagonal.md) $\Delta_Y:Y\to Y\times Y$ along $\langle f,g\rangle:X\to Y\times Y$. Its projection $e:E\to X$ satisfies $fe=ge$. If $h:T\to X$ satisfies $fh=gh$, the pair $h,fh$ gives a unique [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md) into this [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md). Conversely any such [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md) must project to that pair. Hence $e$ is the [equalizer](../../../../../../equaliser.md) of $f,g$. The preceding construction now gives **all [finite limits](../../../../../../finite-limit.md)**.
