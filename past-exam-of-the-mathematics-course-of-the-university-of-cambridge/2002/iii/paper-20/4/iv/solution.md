<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A precise [commutation of iterated categorical limits](../../../../../../commutation-of-iterated-categorical-limits.md) statement is the following. For small [categories](../../../../../../category-split.md) $\mathcal I,\mathcal J$ and $F:\mathcal I\times\mathcal J\to\mathcal D$, if every column [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) and the resulting $\mathcal J$-limit exist, then

$$
\lim_j\lim_iF(i,j)\cong\lim_{(i,j)}F(i,j).
$$

If every row [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) and the resulting $\mathcal I$-limit also exist, then

$$
\boxed{\lim_j\lim_iF(i,j)\cong\lim_{(i,j)}F(i,j)\cong\lim_i\lim_jF(i,j).}
$$

Each [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md) is the unique one identifying all projections to $F(i,j)$. Indeed, the preceding part proves that each iterated [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) has the [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md) of the whole product [diagram in a category](../../../../../../diagram-category-theory.md). Its uniqueness gives the canonical [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md). Equivalently, [categorical cones](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) with vertex $X$ over the product [diagram in a category](../../../../../../diagram-category-theory.md) are exactly compatible families, indexed by $j$, of [categorical cones](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) over $F(-,j)$; regrouping the same compatibility equations cannot change their representing object. In a [complete category](../../../../../../complete-category.md) all the stated existence hypotheses hold for small $\mathcal I,\mathcal J$. The theorem is about [categorical limits](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) with [categorical limits](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md), and makes no unconditional claim about commuting a [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) with a [colimit](../../../../../../colimit.md).
