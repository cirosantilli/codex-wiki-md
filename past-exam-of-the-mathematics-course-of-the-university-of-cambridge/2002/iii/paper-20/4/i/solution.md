<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $D:\mathcal I\to\mathcal C$, define the [functor](../../../../../../functor.md) $\operatorname{Cone}(-,D):\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set}$ by

$$
\operatorname{Cone}(X,D)=\operatorname{Nat}(\Delta X,D),
$$

where $\Delta X$ is the [constant diagram functor](../../../../../../constant-diagram-functor.md); a [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md) into $X$ acts by precomposition on every [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) leg. A [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) is a [representation of a functor](../../../../../../representation-of-a-functor.md) of the form

$$
\boxed{\mathcal C(-,L)\cong\operatorname{Cone}(-,D).}
$$

The image of $1_L$ is the universal [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) $\lambda:\Delta L\to D$. By [naturality](../../../../../../naturality.md) of the representing [isomorphism](../../../../../../isomorphism.md), the image of $h:X\to L$ is $\lambda\circ\Delta h$. Thus this definition is exactly existence and uniqueness of factorization of every [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) through $\lambda$. Conversely that [universal property](../../../../../../universal-property.md) gives the representing [natural bijection](../../../../../../natural-bijection.md).
