<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $M$ be a [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) of $L:\mathcal J\to\mathcal D$, with projections $p_j:M\to L_j$. [Set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $\gamma_{i,j}=\lambda_{i,j}p_j$. The equations for the [categorical cones](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) $\lambda$ and $p$, and the defining equations for $L(v)$, show that this family is a [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) over $F:\mathcal I\times\mathcal J\to\mathcal D$.

For any [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) $x_{i,j}:X\to F(i,j)$, fix $j$. Its $\mathcal I$-legs give a unique $h_j:X\to L_j$. For $v:j\to k$, both $L(v)h_j$ and $h_k$ have composite $x_{i,k}$ with every $\lambda_{i,k}$, so they are equal. Thus the $h_j$ form a [categorical cone](../../../../../../cone-over-a-diagram.md) over $L$, which factors uniquely through $h:X\to M$. The equalities $\gamma_{i,j}h=x_{i,j}$ follow. Any other such $h$ has the same composites with every $p_j$, because the projections $\lambda_{i,j}$ detect equality; uniqueness for $M$ then makes it equal to $h$. Therefore **$M$ is a [categorical limit](../../../../../../categorical-limit.md) of the whole product [diagram in a category](../../../../../../diagram-category-theory.md)**.
