↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Name the upper objects $A,B,C$ and the lower objects $D,E,F$, so the [morphisms](../../../../../../morphism.md) are $b:A\to B$, $c:B\to C$, $a:A\to D$, $g:B\to E$, $d:C\to F$, $f:D\to E$, and $e:E\to F$. The [commutative diagram](../../../../../../commutative-diagram.md) gives $gb=fa$ and $dc=eg$.

Take $u:T\to D$ and $v:T\to C$ with $efu=dv$. The right [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) gives a unique $w:T\to B$ with $gw=fu$ and $cw=v$. The left [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) then gives a unique $z:T\to A$ with $az=u$ and $bz=w$. It follows that $cbz=v$, giving the required factorization through the outside rectangle. Any other factorization has the same middle [morphism](../../../../../../morphism.md) by uniqueness in the right [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md), and therefore is the same $z$ by uniqueness in the left [pullback in a category](../../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md). **The outside rectangle is a pullback**.
