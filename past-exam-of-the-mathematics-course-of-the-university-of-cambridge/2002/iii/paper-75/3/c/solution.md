<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**The universal cover of maximally extended AdS is not globally hyperbolic.** Its timelike [conformal boundary](../../../../../../conformal-boundary.md) can send signals into the interior after any chosen initial slice, so bulk initial data alone do not determine evolution. In the conformal strip, a null ray can run from $\rho\to\pi/2$ to the center in global time $\pi/2$; it need not meet a sufficiently earlier constant-time slice within the interior spacetime.

The compact-diamond test makes the obstruction explicit. Choose two central events with times $-2$ and $2$ in units $L=1$. Events at $T=0$, $\rho\to\pi/2$ lie between them causally, because their radial coordinate separation is less than2. They form a sequence in the causal diamond with no limit in the AdS interior. The diamond is therefore noncompact. Boundary conditions, such as reflecting conditions on the conformal boundary, can supply a well-posed initial-boundary-value problem; they do not turn the boundary-free interior into a globally hyperbolic spacetime in the usual definition. The periodic-time hyperboloid is also non-globally-hyperbolic, already because it has closed timelike curves.
