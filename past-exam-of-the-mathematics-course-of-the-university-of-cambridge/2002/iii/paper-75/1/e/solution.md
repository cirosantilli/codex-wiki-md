<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Three distinct low-redshift nonlinear contributions are:

- The [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../../../thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) arises when hot cluster electrons inverse-Compton scatter the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background.md). Photons receive energy from the thermal electron distribution. With $x=h\nu/(k_BT_\gamma)$, the thermodynamic-temperature equivalent is $\Delta T/T=y[x\coth(x/2)-4]$, with [Compton y parameter](../../../../../../compton-y-parameter.md) $y=\int n_e\sigma_T k_BT_e/(m_ec^2)\,dl$. This is a spectral distortion: a low-frequency decrement, a high-frequency increment, and a nonrelativistic null at $x\simeq3.83$, about217GHz for the present CMB temperature. Relativistic electron corrections shift the detailed spectrum and null.
- The [kinetic Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../../../kinetic-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) is the Doppler shift caused by scattering off the bulk motion of an electron cloud. At lowest order, $\Delta T/T=-\int n_e\sigma_T(\mathbf v\cdot\widehat n/c)\,dl$. It can have either sign. Unlike the thermal effect, it has the spectrum of a small [blackbody](../../../../../../blackbody.md) temperature shift: frequency independent in thermodynamic temperature units, or proportional to $\partial B_\nu/\partial T$ in intensity units.
- The [Rees-Sciama effect](../../../../../../rees-sciama-effect.md) arises from potentials changing during nonlinear collapse, accretion or motion of structures. Energy gained when a photon enters a well need not equal energy lost on departure. Its gravitational line-of-sight source is the nonlinear evolving-potential counterpart of the [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../../../integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md). It too is achromatic in thermodynamic temperature units, not a Compton spectral distortion. Linear potentials can be constant in a matter era while nonlinear potentials still evolve.

Gravitational lensing supplies another nonlinear modification by remapping pre-existing temperature and polarization patterns. It preserves the local blackbody spectrum; it does not generate temperature differences by lensing a perfectly isotropic background.
