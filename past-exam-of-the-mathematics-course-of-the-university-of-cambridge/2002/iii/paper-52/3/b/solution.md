<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $g'=g(\rho_f-\rho_a)/\rho_f>0$. The [Saint-Venant dry-front condition](../../../../../../saint-venant-dry-front-condition.md) describes a liquid advancing over a dry bed, or into an ambient whose inertia is negligible, as when $\rho_a/\rho_f\ll1$. The edge has $h_f=0$; its [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is obtained by continuing the appropriate [shallow water](../../../../../../shallow-water-approximation.md) [Riemann invariant](../../../../../../riemann-invariant.md) to zero depth. There is no independent universal finite-depth head [Froude number](../../../../../../froude-number.md). In the triangular geometry a release of a resting layer gives $u_f=2\sqrt{2g'h_0}$, whereas a rectangular release gives $2\sqrt{g'h_0}$.

The [Benjamin deep-ambient front condition](../../../../../../benjamin-deep-ambient-front-condition.md) includes the inertia of an ambient that must turn around a finite-depth [gravity current](../../../../../../gravity-current.md) head. In the ideal deep-ambient [Boussinesq approximation](../../../../../../boussinesq-approximation.md), $\rho_a/\rho_f$ is close to one and

$$
\boxed{u_f=F\sqrt{g'h_f},\qquad F=\sqrt2.}
$$

Here $h_f$ is the current depth just behind its head, $u_f$ its speed relative to the far ambient, and $F$ the ideal head [Froude number](../../../../../../froude-number.md). The ambient is much deeper than $h_f$; a finite upper-layer depth changes the closure. The lower density must exceed the ambient density for a bottom current. A non-Boussinesq head requires an appropriate density-ratio correction rather than using $F=\sqrt2$ at every ratio. The Saint-Venant and Benjamin models therefore describe different front physics, even though both use the same long-wave equations in the interior.
