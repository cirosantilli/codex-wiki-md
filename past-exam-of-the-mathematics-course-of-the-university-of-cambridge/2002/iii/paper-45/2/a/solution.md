<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\delta=(\rho-\bar\rho)/\bar\rho$ be the [density contrast](../../../../../../density-contrast.md), let $\mathbf x$ be a [comoving coordinate](../../../../../../comoving-coordinate.md), and use $a_0=1$. Define $C=3H_0^2\Omega_0/2$, so $4\pi G\bar\rho=C/a^3$ for the pressureless background. The perturbation [Poisson equation](../../../../../../poisson-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla_x^2\varphi=4\pi Ga^2\bar\rho\,\delta=\frac Ca\delta.}
$$

For the comoving velocity field $\mathbf w=\dot{\mathbf x}$, local [mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\partial_t\delta+\nabla_x\cdot[(1+\delta)\mathbf w]=0.}
$$

One obtains this by subtracting $\dot{\bar\rho}+3H\bar\rho=0$ from $\partial_t\rho+3H\rho+\nabla_x\cdot(\rho\mathbf w)=0$. In the linear regime it reduces to $\partial_t\delta=-\nabla_x\cdot\mathbf w$, the [linearized cosmological continuity equation](../../../../../../linearized-cosmological-continuity-equation.md). The spatial derivatives are with respect to $\mathbf x$, and the time derivative in the conservative continuity equation is at fixed $\mathbf x$. In contrast the acceleration of an individual fluid element is a [material derivative](../../../../../../material-derivative.md). The physical [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) is $a\mathbf w$.
