<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [cosmological principle](../../../../../../cosmological-principle.md) asserts that, after averaging over sufficiently large scales, the universe is spatially homogeneous and isotropic on constant-[cosmic time](../../../../../../cosmic-time.md) slices. Homogeneity means no distinguished location; isotropy means no distinguished spatial direction. These are statistical large-scale statements, not assertions that individual galaxies, clusters or the [cosmic web](../../../../../../cosmic-web.md) are uniform, and they do not require the universe to be unchanging in time.

The nearly isotropic [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background.md) is strong evidence for large-scale isotropy about us. Its dominant dipole is explained by our [peculiar velocity](../../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md); the much smaller residual fluctuations provide the seeds and record of [structure formation](../../../../../../structure-formation.md), rather than invalidating the principle. Large-area galaxy and radio-source distributions and deep galaxy surveys support [statistical isotropy](../../../../../../statistical-isotropy.md) and a tendency towards [statistical homogeneity](../../../../../../statistical-homogeneity.md) after smoothing beyond the strongly clustered scales. An approximately isotropic [Hubble flow](../../../../../../hubble-flow.md) and broadly uniform background radiation supply complementary checks.

There is an important logical distinction: isotropy seen by one observer alone does not prove homogeneity. A spherically symmetric inhomogeneous universe centered on that observer could look isotropic. Combining the observations with the assumption that our location is not privileged, so comparable observers would see comparable large-scale isotropy, motivates the homogeneous [FLRW metric](../../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md). Selection effects, foregrounds, finite survey volumes and the fact that observations lie on our past light cone must be accounted for. **The evidence supports a large-scale statistical principle, not exact uniformity on every scale.**
