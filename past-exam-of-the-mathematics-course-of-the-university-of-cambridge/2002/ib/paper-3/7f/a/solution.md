<h1 id="7f/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**True**. Both [vector subspaces](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) contain zero, and if $u,v\in U\cap W$, every [linear combination](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) $\alpha u+\beta v$ belongs to each of $U$ and $W$. Thus it belongs to their intersection. This proves the [intersection of vector subspaces](../../../../../../intersection-of-vector-subspaces.md) is a [vector subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md).
