<h1 id="4g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The sets $Y\cap C$ and $Y\cap D$ are relatively open in the [subspace topology](../../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $Y$. They are disjoint and their union is $Y$. If both were nonempty, they would be a separation of $Y$, contradicting its [connectedness](../../../../../../connected-space.md). Therefore at least one is empty, and **$Y$ lies entirely in $C$ or entirely in $D$**. The assertion also holds for empty $Y$.
