<h1 id="18d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The shed moves relative to the javelin frame at the same speed, so its simultaneous length there is

$$
\boxed{L'_s=1.5/\gamma=0.9\,\mathrm m.}
$$

The javelin retains its proper length $2\,\mathrm m$. The two statements use different simultaneity slices, as prescribed by [relativity of simultaneity](../../../../../../relativity-of-simultaneity.md).
