<h1 id="18d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Lorentz factor](../../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) is $\gamma=(1-0.8^2)^{-1/2}=5/3$. [Length contraction](../../../../../../length-contraction.md) makes the moving javelin's length in the shed frame

$$
\boxed{L=2/\gamma=1.2\,\mathrm m.}
$$

This measures its two endpoints at the same shed-frame time.
