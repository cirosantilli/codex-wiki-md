<h1 id="18d/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose event $A$, when the trailing end reaches the entrance, as $(ct,x)=(0,0)$ and as the primed origin. Write $T=ct$, $T'=ct'$. Before impact, the shed-frame [worldlines](../../../../../../world-line.md) are entrance $x=0$, back $x=1.5$, trailing end $x=0.8T$, and leading end $x=0.8T+1.2$. The [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) is

$$
T'=\frac53(T-0.8x),\qquad x'=\frac53(x-0.8T).
$$

Thus in the javelin frame the ends have $x'=0$ and $x'=2$, while the entrance and back have $x'=-0.8T'$ and $x'=0.9-0.8T'$. These four lines in each frame produce the requested [spacetime diagrams](../../../../../../spacetime-diagram.md). The diagrams show initial motion; dashed portions extrapolate that motion beyond first impact and are not a model of collision dynamics.
