<h1 id="13g/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the local coordinate $w=1/z$ around infinity. A [pole](../../../../../../pole.md) at $w=0$ is isolated: for some $\varepsilon>0$, $g(w)=f(1/w)$ is holomorphic at every point $0<|w|<\varepsilon$. Taking $r=1/\varepsilon$, the change of variable maps this punctured disk to $r<|z|<\infty$. Therefore **there are no finite [poles](../../../../../../pole.md) outside that disk**. This is a property of the local meromorphic chart at infinity, not an assertion that infinity itself is regular.
