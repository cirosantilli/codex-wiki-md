<h1 id="17f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [normal operator](../../../../../../normal-operator.md) satisfies $\alpha^*\alpha=\alpha\alpha^*$. Moving an operator across the [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product.md) gives

$$
\|\alpha v\|^2=\langle v,\alpha^*\alpha v\rangle=\langle v,\alpha\alpha^*v\rangle=\|\alpha^*v\|^2.
$$

One of these norms is zero exactly when the other is zero. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\ker\alpha=\ker\alpha^*}.
$$
