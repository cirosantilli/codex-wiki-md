<h1 id="17f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Part (ii) shows that $\alpha^*v=\bar\lambda v$ for every $v\in E_\lambda$, so $E_\lambda$ is invariant under $\alpha^*$. Apply the [adjoint criterion for an invariant orthogonal complement](../../../../../../adjoint-criterion-for-an-invariant-orthogonal-complement.md) with $\alpha^*$ in place of $\alpha$, using $(\alpha^*)^*=\alpha$. It follows that

$$
\boxed{\alpha(E_\lambda^\perp)\subseteq E_\lambda^\perp}.
$$

The same argument interchanging $\alpha$ and $\alpha^*$ shows that $E_\lambda^\perp$ is invariant under both operators, a fact needed for the induction in part (iv).
