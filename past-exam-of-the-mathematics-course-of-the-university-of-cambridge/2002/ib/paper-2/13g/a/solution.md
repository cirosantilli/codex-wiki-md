↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Define $\mathcal T$ to be the family of all unions of members of $\mathcal B$, including the empty union. We will verify that this is a [topology](../../../../../../topology-split.md) and that $\mathcal B$ is a [basis of a topology](../../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md). The two conditions play different roles: the covering condition supplies the whole space, while the local intersection condition supplies closure under finite intersections.
