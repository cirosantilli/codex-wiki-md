<h1 id="7b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7B](../7b.md)

Write the [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function.md) as $f=u+iv$, with $u^2+v^2=C$. If $C=0$ then $f=0$. If $C>0$, differentiating the constant modulus with respect to both coordinates, and using the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) $u_y=-v_x$, $v_y=u_x$, gives

$$
u u_x+v v_x=0,\qquad v u_x-u v_x=0.
$$

The coefficient matrix has [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) $-(u^2+v^2)=-C\ne0$, so $u_x=v_x=0$. The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) then also give $u_y=v_y=0$. Along a straight segment inside the disk, both real components have zero derivative and hence are constant. **Thus $f$ is constant throughout the disk.**
