<h1 id="15g/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $D=P^{-1}D_0P$, conjugating $U=D^{-1}T$ gives

$$
PUP^{-1}=D_0^{-1}(D_0+N)=I+D_0^{-1}N.
$$

Because $D_0^{-1}$ commutes with $N$, and $N^n=0$,

$$
(D_0^{-1}N)^n=D_0^{-n}N^n=0.
$$

Thus $U-I$ is similar to a [nilpotent matrix](../../../../../../nilpotent-matrix.md), and is itself nilpotent. **Therefore $U$ is a unipotent matrix.**
