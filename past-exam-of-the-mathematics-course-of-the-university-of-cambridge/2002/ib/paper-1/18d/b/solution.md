<h1 id="18d/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a negative energy write $E=-\hbar^2\lambda^2/(2M)$, $\lambda>0$. Away from the delta interaction the decaying solutions are $Ce^{-\lambda x}$ for $x>0$ and $Ce^{\lambda x}$ for $x<0$, with the same $C$ by [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md). The derivative jump is $-2\lambda C=-C/\Delta$, giving $\lambda=1/(2\Delta)$. Thus the [bound state in a delta potential](../../../../../../bound-state-in-a-delta-potential.md) has

$$
\boxed{E=-\frac{\hbar^2}{8M\Delta^2},\qquad\chi(x)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\Delta}}e^{-|x|/(2\Delta)}}.
$$

The normalization follows from $\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-2\lambda|x|}dx=1/\lambda$. This is a genuine square-integrable negative-energy state.
