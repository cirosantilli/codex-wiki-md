# Merotelic attachment

↑ **Parent:** [Chromosome segregation](chromosome-segregation.md)

Attachment of one [kinetochore](kinetochore.md) to [microtubules](microtubule.md) connected to both spindle poles. It can evade an unattached-kinetochore checkpoint signal and produce lagging [chromosomes](chromosome.md).
