# Character space of C1 on a compact interval

↑ **Parent:** [Continuously differentiable functions on a compact interval form a Banach algebra](continuously-differentiable-functions-on-a-compact-interval-form-a-banach-algebra.md)

The coordinate function has spectrum $[0,1]$: outside the interval its difference from a scalar has a continuously differentiable reciprocal, and on the interval it has a zero. Every [character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md) therefore takes the coordinate to some $t\in[0,1]$. Polynomial density in the norm containing both function and derivative, together with [automatic continuity of characters](automatic-continuity-of-characters.md), forces evaluation at $t$ on every function. The [maximal ideals](maximal-ideal.md) are exactly $\{f:f(t)=0\}$.
