# Weighted Gauss valuation

↑ **Parent:** [Valuation](valuation.md)

For a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) over a [discretely valued field](discretely-valued-field.md) and a rational weight $t$, this coefficient-minimum function is multiplicative. Scale the variable by an element of [valuation](valuation.md) $t$ in a valued extension and reduce the initial terms; nonzero residue polynomials have nonzero product. For a monic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) factored into roots, $w_t(f)=\sum_r\min(t,v(r))$. Its supporting lines determine the lower [Newton polygon](newton-polygon.md), giving the [Newton polygon root valuation theorem](newton-polygon-root-valuation-theorem.md) and its multiplicities.
