# Bond stretching force constant

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md)

In a local harmonic approximation, the vibrational wavenumber obeys $\widetilde\nu=(2\pi c)^{-1}\sqrt{k/\mu}$. Comparisons at fixed [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) therefore use squared frequency ratios. A local stretching force constant is not identical to a [bond-dissociation energy](bond-dissociation-energy.md).
