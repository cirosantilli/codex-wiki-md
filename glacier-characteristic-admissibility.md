# Glacier characteristic admissibility

↑ **Parent:** [Method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md)

For $H_t+H^{n+1}H_x=1-2x$ with $H(\xi,0)=0$, [method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md) conserves $H^{n+2}/(n+2)-(x-x^2)$. A positive downstream branch therefore requires $0\leq\xi<x<1-\xi$. Initial points in the negative-accumulation region cannot create nonnegative [ice](ice.md) from a bare bed. [Characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) intersections and missing dry-bed physics prevent using the formal initial/boundary branches as an unrestricted global solution.
