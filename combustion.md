# Combustion

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combustion)

An exothermic [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) between a fuel and an oxidizing agent. [Oxygen](oxygen.md) is a common oxidizer, but neither visible flame nor oxygen is required by every broader chemical use of the term.
