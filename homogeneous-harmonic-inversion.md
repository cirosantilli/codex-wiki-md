# Homogeneous harmonic inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Irregular solid harmonic](irregular-solid-harmonic.md)

A degree-$n$ [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) satisfies [Euler homogeneous function theorem](euler-theorem-for-homogeneous-functions.md) $\mathbf r\cdot\nabla H_n=nH_n$. The [product rule](product-rule.md) gives $\Delta(r^sH_n)=r^s\Delta H_n+s(2n+s+1)r^{s-2}H_n$ in three dimensions. Taking $s=-(2n+1)$ gives the displayed identity and the equivalence of harmonicity on $r>0$. The origin is excluded; even $1/r$ has a distributional [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) source there.
