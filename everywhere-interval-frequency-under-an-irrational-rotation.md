# Everywhere interval frequency under an irrational rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Irrational rotation](irrational-rotation.md)

Every orbit of an [irrational rotation of the circle](irrational-rotation.md) visits a half-open interval $I$ with limiting frequency equal to its length. One proof sandwiches the orbit of an arbitrary point between inner and outer intervals along a nearby orbit for which the [Birkhoff ergodic theorem](birkhoff-ergodic-theorem.md) holds.
