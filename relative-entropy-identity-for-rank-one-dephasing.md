# Relative-entropy identity for rank-one dephasing

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-one dephasing](rank-one-dephasing.md)

Because $\log\Delta(\rho)$ is diagonal, $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\log\Delta(\rho))=\operatorname{Tr}(\Delta(\rho)\log\Delta(\rho))$. Hence $D_{\mathrm{rel}}(\rho\|\Delta(\rho))=S(\Delta(\rho))-S(\rho)$. [Support inclusion under rank-one dephasing](support-inclusion-under-rank-one-dephasing.md) handles zero probabilities, and [Klein's inequality](klein-s-inequality.md) makes the difference nonnegative. Equality holds exactly when the state was already diagonal.
