# Material Properties: OFHC Copper by National Institute of Standards and Technology

The specific-heat correlation covers 4–300 K and gives mass-specific [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) in $\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}}$. Its stated fit deviation is 10% below 15 K. The page distinguishes specific heat from its separate [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) correlation.
