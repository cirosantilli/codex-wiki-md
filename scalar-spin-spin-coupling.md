# Scalar spin-spin coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

Through-bond interaction between nuclear [spins](spin.md) that splits [NMR](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) resonances. In the weak-coupling limit, $n$ equivalent spin-$\tfrac12$ neighbours produce $n+1$ lines with [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient-split.md) intensities. Equivalent nuclei do not split each other.
