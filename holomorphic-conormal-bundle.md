# Holomorphic conormal bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic conormal sequence](holomorphic-conormal-sequence.md)

For a [complex submanifold](complex-submanifold.md) $Y\subset X$, the holomorphic conormal bundle is the annihilator of $T^{1,0}Y$ inside $\Omega_X^1|_Y$, or equivalently the dual of the [holomorphic normal bundle](holomorphic-normal-bundle.md). For a smooth analytic hypersurface with local equation $f$, it is generated by $df|_Y$. It is also the locally free analytic sheaf $\mathcal I_Y/\mathcal I_Y^2$.
