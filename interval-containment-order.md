# Interval containment order

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)

The [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md) on nonempty closed [integer intervals](integer-interval.md) given by [subset](subset.md) inclusion. The [least upper bound](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of two such [intervals](interval-mathematics.md) is their interval hull $[\min(l,l'),\max(u,u')]$. The hull can contain points absent from both original [intervals](interval-mathematics.md), so it need not equal their [union](set-union.md).
