# Carbon-oxygen white dwarf

↑ **Parent:** [White dwarf](white-dwarf.md)

A carbon-oxygen white dwarf is a [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) whose core is predominantly [carbon](carbon.md) and [oxygen](oxygen.md), retained after [core helium burning](core-helium-burning.md) when the star loses its envelope without sustained [carbon burning](carbon-burning.md).
