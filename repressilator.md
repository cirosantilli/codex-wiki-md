# Repressilator

↑ **Parent:** [Gene regulatory network](gene-regulatory-network.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repressilator)

A repressilator is a cyclic negative-feedback [gene regulatory network](gene-regulatory-network.md) in which three repressors inhibit one another in sequence. In a normalized [logic approximation for gene regulation](logic-approximation-for-gene-regulation.md), $x'=H(h-z)-x$, $y'=H(h-x)-y$, $z'=H(h-y)-z$. For $0<h<1$, threshold crossings successively switch production and can generate sustained [oscillations](oscillation.md). A rise from $v_0<h$ reaches threshold after $\ln[(1-v_0)/(1-h)]$; a decay from $v_0>h$ reaches it after $\ln(v_0/h)$ in units of the common decay time.
