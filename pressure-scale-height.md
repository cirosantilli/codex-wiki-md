# Pressure scale height

↑ **Parent:** [Scale height](scale-height.md)

A pressure scale height is the local distance over which [pressure](pressure.md) would change by a factor $e$ if its logarithmic gradient were constant. In [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), $H_P=P/(\rho g)$; for an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) it is $k_BT/(\mu m_ug)$. A small logarithmic pressure interval has depth approximately $H_P\,\Delta\log P$.
