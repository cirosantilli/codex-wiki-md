# Curie temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetization](magnetization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curie_temperature)

The Curie temperature is the transition temperature above which a ferromagnetic material loses spontaneous [magnetization](magnetization.md). A hot conducting planetary core can generate a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) through [dynamo action](dynamo-action.md) without behaving as a permanent magnet. The blocking temperature at which a mineral records crustal remanence also depends on its relaxation timescale and need not equal its Curie temperature.
