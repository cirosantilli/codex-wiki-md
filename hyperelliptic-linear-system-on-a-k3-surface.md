# Hyperelliptic linear system on a K3 surface

↑ **Parent:** [K3 surface](k3-surface.md)

For a basepoint-free nef big divisor $D$, this means that the morphism given by its complete system is generically two-to-one onto its image. Equivalently, its general smooth curve is [hyperelliptic](hyperelliptic-curve.md). A double cover of $\mathbb P^2$ branched over a smooth sextic, with the pullback of $\mathcal O(1)$, gives the degree-two example.
