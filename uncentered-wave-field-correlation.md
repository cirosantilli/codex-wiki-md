# Uncentered wave-field correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Wave propagation in a random medium](wave-propagation-in-a-random-medium.md)

For a complex random [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md), this two-point [expectation](expected-value.md) retains the [coherent field](coherent-wave-field.md) and uses a [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) on the second amplitude. It is not a normalized [wave-field correlation](uncentered-wave-field-correlation.md) coefficient. Subtracting $\mathbb E[E(\mathbf r)]\mathbb E[E(\mathbf r')]^*$ gives the centered [covariance function](covariance-function.md). At coincident points the uncentered [wave-field correlation](uncentered-wave-field-correlation.md) equals the [mean wave intensity](ensemble-averaged-wave-intensity.md). Under second-order [stationarity](stationary-process.md) it depends only on the separation and has a [spectral measure of a stationary random field](spectral-measure-of-a-stationary-random-field.md). A deterministic field is included by taking a degenerate ensemble.
