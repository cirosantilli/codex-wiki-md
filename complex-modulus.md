# Complex modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Complex number](complex-number.md)

The [complex modulus](complex-modulus.md) of $z=x+iy$ is $|z|=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=\sqrt{z\overline z}$. It is the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) of $(x,y)$ in the [complex plane](complex-plane.md), and satisfies $|zw|=|z||w|$ and the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md).
