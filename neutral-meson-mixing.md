# Neutral meson mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)

Transitions between a neutral [meson](meson.md) carrying flavour and its [antiparticle](antiparticle.md). Decays are described on this two-state subspace by an effective [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $M-i\Gamma/2$ with [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) $M,\Gamma$.
