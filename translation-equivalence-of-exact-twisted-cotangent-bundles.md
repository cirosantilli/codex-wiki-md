# Translation equivalence of exact twisted cotangent bundles

↑ **Parent:** [Twisted cotangent symplectic form](twisted-cotangent-symplectic-form.md)

If $\sigma=d\beta$, [cotangent fiber translation](cotangent-fiber-translation.md) by $+\beta$ gives a [symplectomorphism](symplectomorphism.md) from $(T^*X,-d\alpha)$ to $(T^*X,-d\alpha+\pi^*\sigma)$. Its pullback changes $\alpha$ by $\pi^*\beta$, so the extra two-form cancels with $-\pi^*d\beta$. The opposite translation gives the inverse direction, globally without compactness.
