# Travel coordinate for a conservative transport perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear transport equation](linear-transport-equation.md)

For $a_t+(V(s)a)_s=0$ with $V>0$, the [flux](flux.md) perturbation $b=Va$ solves $b_t+Vb_s=0$. The travel [coordinate](coordinate-linear-algebra.md) transforms it into unit-speed transport. Thus the [flux](flux.md) perturbation is constant on [characteristics](characteristic-of-a-field.md) while [area](surface-area.md) [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) changes as $1/V$. In a supplied power-law river, $A_{\rm eq}'=M/V$, giving travel [time](time-in-physics.md) $[A_{\rm eq}(l)-A_{\rm eq}(0)]/M$. At a zero-speed head, this [time](time-in-physics.md) can remain integrable even though the [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) formula is not uniform.
