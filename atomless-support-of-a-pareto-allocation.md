# Atomless support of a Pareto allocation

↑ **Parent:** [Price quasi-equilibrium with transfers](price-quasi-equilibrium-with-transfers.md)

For an [atomless measure](non-atomic-measure.md) of consumers with measurable, complete, [transitive](transitive-relation.md) and continuous [preference relations](preference-relation.md), every exactly feasible [Pareto efficient](pareto-efficiency.md) [consumption allocation](consumption-allocation.md) admits nonzero quasi-equilibrium supporting prices. Countably many rational strict-improvement directions remove all exceptional null sets simultaneously. If their [convex hull](convex-hull.md) contained zero, sufficiently small disjoint cohorts could make those improvements in exactly balancing proportions, violating [Pareto efficiency](pareto-efficiency.md). A [supporting hyperplane theorem](supporting-hyperplane-theorem.md) then supplies the prices. Individual [convexity](convex-function.md) of preferences is unnecessary.
