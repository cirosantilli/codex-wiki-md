# Back focal plane

↑ **Parent:** [Focal plane](focal-plane.md)

The plane one [focal length](focal-length.md) beyond a lens. In a paraxial imaging system, incident parallel rays of a given direction focus at one point in this plane; it is therefore an angular or diffraction plane distinct from a finite-distance object's image plane.
