# Isenthalpic process

↑ **Parent:** [Enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isenthalpic_process)

An [isenthalpic process](isenthalpic-process.md) preserves [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) for a fixed amount of material, or preserves [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) between inlet and outlet when comparing a flowing [fluid](fluid.md) of fixed composition. It need not preserve [temperature](temperature-split.md) or [entropy](entropy-split.md). An insulated throttle satisfying the [steady-flow energy balance](steady-flow-energy-balance.md) assumptions is an important example.
