# Dirichlet modified Helmholtz Green function on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [One-dimensional modified Helmholtz Green function](one-dimensional-modified-helmholtz-green-function.md)

For $\kappa>0$, the [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) of $D_x^2-\kappa^2$ on $(0,L)$ with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) is the displayed negative kernel. Here $x_<=\min(x,\xi)$ and $x_>=\max(x,\xi)$. The left and right homogeneous solutions are $\sinh(\kappa x)$ and $\sinh(\kappa(L-x))$, with [Wronskian](wronskian.md) $-\kappa\sinh(\kappa L)$. The kernel is continuous and its first [derivative](derivative.md) jumps by $+1$, giving $(D_x^2-\kappa^2)G=\delta(x-\xi)$. Reversing the operator sign reverses the kernel sign.
