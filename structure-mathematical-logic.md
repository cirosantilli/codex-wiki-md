# Structure (mathematical logic)

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_(mathematical_logic))

A [structure](structure-mathematical-logic.md) consists of a domain together with interpretations of the symbols of a formal language. In the usual finitary setting it interprets constant, function, and relation symbols of their declared arities. A [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) is such an interpretation for first-order logic.
