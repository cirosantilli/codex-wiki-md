# Bicylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Steinmetz solid](steinmetz-solid.md)

The solid intersection of two equal-radius infinite [circular cylinders](circular-cylinder.md) whose axes meet at a right angle. For radius $a$, the [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) at height $z$ along the common perpendicular coordinate is a [square](square.md) of [area](surface-area.md) $4(a^2-z^2)$, so its [volume](volume.md) is $16a^3/3$.
