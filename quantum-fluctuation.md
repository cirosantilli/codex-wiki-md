# Quantum fluctuation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_fluctuation)

A [quantum fluctuation](quantum-fluctuation.md) is a nonzero variance of an observable in a quantum state, even when its mean vanishes. A vacuum field mode has such zero-point variance. In an inflationary background, the expanding geometry transfers short-wavelength vacuum field fluctuations into [primordial perturbations](primordial-perturbation.md) as their wavelengths cross the [Hubble radius](hubble-radius.md).
