# Narayana number

↑ **Parent:** [Catalan number](catalan-number.md)

The number $N(n,k)=\frac1n\binom nk\binom n{k-1}$ counts [Dyck paths](dyck-path.md) of semilength $n$ with exactly $k$ peaks, for $1\le k\le n$. Summing over $k$ gives the $n$th [Catalan number](catalan-number.md).
