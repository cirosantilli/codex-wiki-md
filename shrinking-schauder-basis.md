# Shrinking Schauder basis

↑ **Parent:** [Dual sequence of a Schauder basis](dual-sequence-of-a-schauder-basis.md)

A Schauder basis is shrinking when its [dual sequence](dual-sequence-of-a-schauder-basis.md) is a Schauder basis of the whole dual space. Every Schauder basis of a [reflexive Banach space](reflexive-banach-space.md) is shrinking.
