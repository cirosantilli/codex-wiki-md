# Photon energy

↑ **Parent:** [Photon](photon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photon_energy)

The [energy](energy-split.md) carried by one [photon](photon.md) is proportional to its [frequency](frequency.md) $\nu$, with proportionality constant the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) $h$. The equivalent forms use [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega=2\pi\nu$ and vacuum [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda=c/\nu$. Different observers can measure different [photon energies](photon-energy.md) because they measure different [frequencies](frequency.md); the [photon](photon.md) remains massless. In signature $(+---)$, an observer with [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) $u^\mu$ measures $E=p_\mu u^\mu$, where $p^\mu$ is the photon's [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) and $u^\mu u_\mu=c^2$.
