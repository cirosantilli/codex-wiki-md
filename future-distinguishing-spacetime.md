# Future-distinguishing spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Strong causality](strong-causality.md)

A spacetime is future-distinguishing when $I^+(p)=I^+(q)$ implies $p=q$. Every [strongly causal](strong-causality.md) spacetime is future-distinguishing.
