# Howea forsteriana

↑ **Parent:** [Angiosperm](angiosperm.md)

One of the two [Howea](howea.md) palms native to Lord Howe Island. Contrasts in soil association and flowering time with [Howea belmoreana](howea-belmoreana.md) provide evidence relevant to [sympatric speciation](sympatric-speciation.md).
