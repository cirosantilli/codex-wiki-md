# Integer power of a group element

↑ **Parent:** [Group element](group-element.md)

For an element $x$ of a [group](group-split.md) with [identity element](identity-element.md) $e$, define $x^0=e$, $x^n$ as a product of $n$ copies of $x$ for positive $n$, and $x^{-n}=(x^{-1})^n$. [Associativity](associative-property.md) and cancellation of [inverse elements](inverse-element.md) give $x^{m+n}=x^mx^n$ and $(x^m)^n=x^{mn}$ for all [integers](integer-split.md) $m,n$. Consequently $n\mapsto x^n$ is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from the [additive group of integers](additive-group-of-integers.md) onto the [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md) generated by $x$.
