# Geodesic reparametrization under projective equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Projective equivalence of affine connections](projective-equivalence-of-affine-connections.md)

Let $\tau$ be an [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) for a [geodesic](geodesic.md) of $\nabla$ and suppose $\bar\nabla_XY-\nabla_XY=V(X)Y+V(Y)X$. Then $\bar\nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma=2V(\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma$. For the new velocity $d\gamma/dt=\dot\gamma/t'$, the acceleration is $(t')^{-2}[\bar\nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma-(t''/t')\dot\gamma]$, which vanishes under the displayed [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md). Its nonzero solution $t'=C\exp(2\int V(\dot\gamma)\,d\tau)$ changes the parameter while preserving the curve.
