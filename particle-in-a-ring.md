# Particle in a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_in_a_ring)

A quantum particle constrained to a circle has integer angular-momentum eigenvalues and periodic position eigenfunctions. Its propagator can be written either as a sum over angular momenta or as a sum over [winding number](winding-number.md) sectors.
