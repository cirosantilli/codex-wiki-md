# Terminal scaling inequality for stochastic exponentials

↑ **Parent:** [Doléans-Dade exponential](doleans-dade-exponential.md)

For a convergent [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) and $r>1$, factor $\mathcal E(X)$ into $\mathcal E(rX)^{1/r^2}$ and an ordinary exponential. Apply the [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) and then the concave [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) to the fractional power $2/(r+1)$. The displayed lower bound is informative when the terminal exponential moment is finite.
