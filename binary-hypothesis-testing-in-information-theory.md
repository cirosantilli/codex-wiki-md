# Binary hypothesis testing in information theory

↑ **Parent:** [Information theory](information-theory-split.md)

For two [probability mass functions](probability-mass-function.md) $P,Q$, a decision region $B_n$ accepts $P^{\otimes n}$. Its two error probabilities are $P^{\otimes n}(B_n^c)$ and $Q^{\otimes n}(B_n)$.
