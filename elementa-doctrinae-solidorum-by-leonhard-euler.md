# Elementa doctrinae solidorum by Leonhard Euler

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Leonhard Euler](work-by-leonhard-euler.md)

By [Leonhard Euler](leonhard-euler.md). Latin journal article, written in 1750 and published in 1758 in Novi Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae 4, 109–140; Eneström number E230. The Euler Archive at the University of the Pacific supplies the bibliographic record.

Institutional record: [https://scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/euler-works/230/](https://scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/euler-works/230/)
