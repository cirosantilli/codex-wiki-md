# Numerical Calculations of the Dynamics of a Collapsing Proto-Star by Richard B. Larson

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Richard B. Larson](work-by-richard-b-larson.md)

The primary numerical study follows spherical protostellar collapse. A small central core forms before most of the envelope reaches it and subsequently grows by [accretion](accretion.md). The abstract also describes infrared reprocessing by the dusty envelope. Spherical symmetry and the chosen radiative and thermodynamic treatment are model assumptions; rotation, [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md) and non-spherical [accretion](accretion.md) require additional physics.
