# Yukawa fermion triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion loop](fermion-loop.md)

Three scalar [Yukawa interactions](yukawa-interaction.md) on a closed [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) loop give two cyclic orientations. Odd [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) traces vanish, while $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)=4g^{\mu\nu}$ gives the displayed numerator. For external invariant mass squares $M^2,\mu^2,\mu^2$, write $D_a=a^2-m^2$ and similarly for the other two lines. The numerator becomes $4m[D_a+D_b+D_c+4m^2-\mu^2-M^2/2]$, reducing the [fermion loop](fermion-loop.md) to three scalar bubbles and a [scalar triangle Feynman integral](scalar-triangle-feynman-integral.md). The [closed fermion loop sign](closed-fermion-loop-sign.md) must be included. A massive [fermion](fermion.md) produces a logarithmic local divergence.
