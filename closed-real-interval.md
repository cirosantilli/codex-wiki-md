# Closed real interval

↑ **Parent:** [Interval (mathematics)](interval-mathematics.md)

A bounded closed real interval has the form $[a,b]=\{x\in\mathbb R:a\leq x\leq b\}$.

This is a closed instance of a [real interval](interval-mathematics.md); the general interval concept also includes open and half-open intervals.
