# Logarithmic utility

↑ **Parent:** [Constant relative risk aversion utility](constant-relative-risk-aversion-utility.md)

[Logarithmic utility](logarithmic-utility.md) on positive [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md) has [derivative](derivative.md) $1/x$ and relative risk aversion one. Its [utility conjugate](utility-conjugate.md) is $V(y)=-\log y-1$, since the maximizing wealth is $1/y$. It satisfies the [Inada conditions](inada-conditions.md).
