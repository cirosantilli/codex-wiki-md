# Space-filling SLE above parameter eight

↑ **Parent:** [Phase classification of the SLE trace](phase-classification-of-the-sle-trace.md)

For $\kappa>8$, the [Loewner trace](trace-of-a-loewner-chain.md) visits every point of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) almost surely. Choose $\rho=\kappa-8$ in the [SLE interior-point martingale](sle-interior-point-martingale.md) to obtain $M_t=\Upsilon_t^{(\kappa-8)/8}S_t^{-(\kappa-8)/\kappa}$. The [maximal inequality for a nonnegative supermartingale](maximal-inequality-for-a-nonnegative-supermartingale.md) bounds its running supremum. If a fixed [interior](interior-topology.md) ball were avoided, its center would have $\Upsilon_t\geq c_1>0$ before swallowing, forcing $S_t\geq c_2>0$. But the [Chordal Loewner equation](chordal-loewner-equation.md) gives $dY_t^2/dt=-4S_t^2$, forcing a finite lifetime with $Y_t\to0$, and $d\log\Upsilon_t/d\log Y_t=2S_t^2$ then forces $\Upsilon_t\to0$, a contradiction. Countably many rational balls give a dense trace; [continuity](continuous-function.md) and [Transience of chordal SLE](transience-of-chordal-sle.md) make its image relatively closed, so it is all of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). This argument does not cover the critical parameter eight.
