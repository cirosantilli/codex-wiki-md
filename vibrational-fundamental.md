# Vibrational fundamental

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)

Excitation of one quantum of a [normal mode](normal-mode.md) from the vibrational ground state. For an infrared-active mode in the linear-dipole [harmonic approximation](harmonic-approximation.md), this is the allowed absorption underlying the usual fundamental band. A [vibrational overtone](vibrational-overtone.md) excites two or more quanta from the ground state.
