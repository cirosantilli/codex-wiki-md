# Safety behavior

↑ **Parent:** [Avoidance learning](avoidance-learning.md)

A precaution intended to prevent a feared outcome or conceal [anxiety](anxiety.md). It can maintain fear when safety is attributed to the precaution, when it blocks informative experience, or when it produces misleading symptoms. Whether a behavior has this function depends on context; objectively useful precautions are not inherently pathological.
