# Massless Fierz-Pauli action

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized gravity](linearized-gravity.md)

A [massless Fierz-Pauli action](massless-fierz-pauli-action.md) is the quadratic flat-background action for a symmetric metric perturbation that yields the vacuum [Linearized Einstein equations](linearized-einstein-equations.md). One normalization, up to a total derivative, is

$$
\mathcal L=-\frac14\partial_\mu h^{\rho\sigma}\partial^\mu h_{\rho\sigma}
+\frac12A_\mu A^\mu-\frac12A^\mu\partial_\mu h
+\frac14\partial_\mu h\partial^\mu h,\qquad A_\mu=\partial_\nu h^\nu{}_\mu.
$$

Varying the symmetric field with compact support gives the displayed linearized [Einstein tensor](einstein-tensor.md) coefficient. Its linearized coordinate-gauge symmetry is consistent with the contracted [Bianchi identity](bianchi-identity.md). It is the massless gravitational action, distinct from imposing the massive [Fierz-Pauli equations](fierz-pauli-equations.md) with five polarizations.
