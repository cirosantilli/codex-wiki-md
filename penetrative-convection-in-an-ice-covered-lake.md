# Penetrative convection in an ice-covered lake

↑ **Parent:** [Penetrative convection](penetrative-convection.md)

In an ice-covered freshwater lake, a stagnant layer colder than the density-maximum temperature can cap a warmer turbulent region. Maximizing the boundary-layer [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) obtained from a [Local Rayleigh-number closure](local-rayleigh-number-closure.md) gives an interface temperature $T_h$ satisfying

$$
T_m-T_h=\frac35(T_w-T_m)
$$

and a maximum upward [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) proportional to $(T_w-T_m)^{5/3}$.
