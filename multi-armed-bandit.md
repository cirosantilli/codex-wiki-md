# Multi-armed bandit

↑ **Parent:** [Online learning](online-learning.md)

A sequential decision model in which each round selects an action and observes its reward, balancing information gathering against immediate reward. For fixed action means $m(x)$, the [pseudoregret](pseudoregret.md) compares selected actions with an action maximizing $m$. In models with a [link function](link-function.md) and parameter $\theta$, rewards may have means $m_\theta(x)=\mu(\langle x,\theta\rangle)$.
