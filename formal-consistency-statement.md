# Formal consistency statement

↑ **Parent:** [Consistent first-order theory](consistent-first-order-theory.md)

After fixing a [Gödel numbering](godel-numbering.md), the formal consistency statement $\operatorname{Con}(T)$ asserts that no natural number codes a $T$-proof of a contradiction. For a recursively axiomatized theory, this is an arithmetical sentence expressible inside any sufficiently strong base theory.
