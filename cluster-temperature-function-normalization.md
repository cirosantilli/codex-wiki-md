# Cluster temperature function normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md)

The [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) and [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) relate a cluster's gas temperature to $GM/R$, while $M=(4\pi/3)\Delta_v\rho_cR^3$. A calibrated mass-temperature relation converts the [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md) into $dn/dT=(dn/dM)|dM/dT|$. Its exponential rare-cluster tail is sensitive to the [density contrast](density-contrast.md) variance on cluster scales, hence to the power-spectrum amplitude, often parameterized by $\sigma_8$. Mass calibration, temperature scatter, nonthermal pressure, sample selection and the matter density must be included rather than treating temperature counts as an independent measurement of amplitude alone.
