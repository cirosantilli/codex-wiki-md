# Spherical capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor](capacitor.md)

Two concentric conducting spherical shells of radii $a<b$ with charges $Q,-Q$ have an [electric field](electric-field-split.md) only in the intervening vacuum: $E_r=Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^2)$ by [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md). Integrating gives a potential difference $Q(1/a-1/b)/(4\pi\epsilon_0)$, and hence the displayed [capacitance](capacitance.md). The [electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md) is $Q^2/(2C)$, either by charging work or by integrating $\epsilon_0|E|^2/2$ over the gap.
