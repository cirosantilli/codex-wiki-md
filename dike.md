# Dike

↑ **Parent:** [Magma](magma.md)

A geological dike is a sheet-like body or conduit formed when [magma](magma.md) enters a fracture cutting across the surrounding rock. A vertical narrow dike can be modeled as a two-dimensional channel for coupled flow, [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) and wall [melting](melting.md).
