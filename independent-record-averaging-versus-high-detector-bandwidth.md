# Independent-record averaging versus high detector bandwidth

↑ **Parent:** [Langevin dynamics](langevin-dynamics.md)

Zero-[mean](expected-value-split.md) random forces vanish in an [ensemble average](expected-value-split.md), while independent finite-bandwidth sample averages have [variance](variance-split.md) proportional to the inverse sample count. Merely increasing detector bandwidth does not suppress physical noise; it can expose more fluctuations. Independence, filtering and averaging are separate assumptions.
