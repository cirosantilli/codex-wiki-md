# Excess hazard

↑ **Parent:** [Relative survival](relative-survival.md)

An [excess hazard](excess-hazard.md) measures mortality above the [background hazard](background-hazard.md). When it is common across individuals, each observed [survivor function](survival-function.md) factors into its individual background [survivor function](survival-function.md) and a common [relative survivor function](relative-survivor-function.md). A nonnegative [excess hazard](excess-hazard.md) gives a nonincreasing [relative survivor function](relative-survivor-function.md); a negative [excess hazard](excess-hazard.md) can instead reflect better survival than expected in the reference population.
