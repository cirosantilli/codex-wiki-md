# Biased van der Pol relaxation period

↑ **Parent:** [Singular perturbation](singular-perturbation-split.md)

For $\ddot x+k(x^2-1)\dot x+x=\lambda$, with fixed $|\lambda|<1$ and $k\to\infty$, the Liénard coordinate $y=\dot x/k+x^3/3-x$ has fast horizontal jumps and slow motion on $y=x^3/3-x$. The slow time is $t/k$. Integrating $(x^2-1)dx/dT=\lambda-x$ along $[-2,-1]$ and $[2,1]$ gives the displayed [period](period-of-a-function.md). The two jumps take negligible time at this scale. For fixed $\lambda>1$, the right attracting branch contains the stable [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) and the large relaxation cycle disappears.
