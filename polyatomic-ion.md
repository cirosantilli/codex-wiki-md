# Polyatomic ion

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyatomic_ion)

A [polyatomic ion](polyatomic-ion.md) consists of a bound group of two or more [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) with nonzero total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). [Hydronium](hydronium.md), $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$, contains three [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and one [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md); its net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) is $+e$, where $e$ is the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md). This is a discrete charged group, while an [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md) can contain an extended arrangement of [ions](ion.md).[polyatomic ions, introductory paragraph and Table 2.5, hydronium](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/2-6-ionic-and-molecular-compounds.md)
