# Mellin continuation of a noncuspidal modular form

↑ **Parent:** [Modular form](modular-form.md)

For a level-one [modular form](modular-form.md) of positive even weight $k$ and constant coefficient $a_0$, put $I_f(s)=\int_1^\infty(f(iy)-a_0)y^{s-1}\,dy$. This is an [entire function](entire-function.md). Splitting the [Mellin transform](mellin-transform.md) at one and using $f(iy)=i^k y^{-k}f(i/y)$ proves the displayed continuation of $(2\pi)^{-s}\Gamma(s)L(f,s)$. It obeys $\Lambda_f(s)=i^k\Lambda_f(k-s)$ and has possible simple [poles](pole.md) only at zero and $k$, with residues $-a_0,i^ka_0$. For a [cusp form](cusp-form.md) both disappear.
