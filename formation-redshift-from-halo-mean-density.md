# Formation redshift from halo mean density

↑ **Parent:** [Virial radius of a dark-matter halo](virial-radius-of-a-dark-matter-halo.md)

In a spherical-overdensity halo convention, $3M/(4\pi R^3)=\Delta_f\rho_0(1+z_f)^3$, so $1+z_f$ follows from the displayed cube root. At fixed measured $M,R,\rho_0$, changing the adopted overdensity changes $1+z_f\propto\Delta_f^{-1/3}$. It does not change the hydrostatic [temperature](temperature-split.md) estimate from the same measured [mass](mass.md) and radius. A [redshift](redshift.md) inferred this way is a model formation epoch, not a direct age measurement.
