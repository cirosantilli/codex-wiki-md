# Clamped biharmonic problem

↑ **Parent:** [Biharmonic operator](biharmonic-operator.md)

The clamped problem uses the [weak solution](weak-solution.md) identity $\int\Delta u\Delta v=\int fv$ for every $v$ in the [clamped second-order Sobolev space](clamped-second-order-sobolev-space.md). For $f\in L^2(U)$, the [clamped Hessian identity](clamped-hessian-identity.md) and the [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) make this a bounded [coercive bilinear form](coercive-bilinear-form.md). The [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) gives a unique [weak solution](weak-solution.md) without a mean-zero condition on $f$.
