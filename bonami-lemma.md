# Bonami lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis of Boolean functions](analysis-of-boolean-functions.md)

The Bonami lemma states that a function of [Fourier degree](fourier-walsh-transform.md) at most $k$ satisfies $\lVert f\rVert_4\leq3^{k/2}\lVert f\rVert_2$.
