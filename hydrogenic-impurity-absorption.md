# Hydrogenic impurity absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Impurity level](impurity-level.md)

A sequence of bound-to-bound optical transitions of a [hydrogenic donor state](hydrogenic-donor-state.md) or [hydrogenic acceptor state](hydrogenic-acceptor-state.md), converging on a photoionization threshold $R^*$. For an initially occupied $1s$ state, ordinary [electric dipole transitions](electric-dipole-transition.md) reach $np$ states; above the threshold, the final carrier can enter a band continuum.
