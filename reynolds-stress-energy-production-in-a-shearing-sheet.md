# Reynolds-stress energy production in a shearing sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md)

For shear $\mathbf u_0=-2Ax\mathbf e_y$, the spatially averaged perturbation [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) obeys $dE/dt=2A\rho\langle u_x'u_y'\rangle-\rho\nu\langle|\nabla\mathbf u'|^2\rangle$. [Pressure](pressure.md) and the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) do no net work. Thus positive radial-azimuthal velocity correlation extracts mean-flow [energy](energy.md). A leading two-dimensional [shearing wave](shearing-wave.md) has $\operatorname{Re}(v_xv_y^*)=-k_xk_y|Z|^2/k^4>0$.
