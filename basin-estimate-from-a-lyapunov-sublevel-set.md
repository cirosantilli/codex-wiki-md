# Basin estimate from a Lyapunov sublevel set

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant sublevel set](invariant-sublevel-set.md)

Suppose a compact connected component $C$ of $\{x:V(x)\leq c\}$ contains an equilibrium $x_*$ and $\dot V<0$ throughout $C\setminus\{x_*\}$. Then $C$ is an [invariant sublevel set](invariant-sublevel-set.md), and every trajectory in $C$ converges to $x_*$ by the [LaSalle invariance principle](lasalle-s-invariance-principle.md). Thus the interior of $C$ lies in the [basin of attraction](basin-of-attraction.md) of $x_*$. The largest such component supplies the strongest basin estimate available from that Lyapunov function alone.
