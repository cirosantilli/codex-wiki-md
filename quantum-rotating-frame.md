# Quantum rotating frame

↑ **Parent:** [Interaction picture](interaction-picture.md)

A time-dependent [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) $R(t)$ defines transformed [quantum states](quantum-state.md) by $|\psi_{\mathrm r}\rangle=R^\dagger|\psi\rangle$. Substituting $|\psi\rangle=R|\psi_{\mathrm r}\rangle$ into the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) gives the displayed transformed [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), in units $\hbar=1$. If $R=e^{-itH_0}$ for constant $H_0$, this becomes $H_{\mathrm r}=R^\dagger HR-H_0$. Removing a common nuclear [Zeeman energy](zeeman-energy.md) with $H_0=\omega(ZI+IZ)/2$ leaves an isotropic [Heisenberg model](heisenberg-model.md) coupling unchanged, because it commutes with the generator of the common rotation. Removing two unequal nuclear frequencies instead makes their exchange terms oscillate at the difference frequency.
