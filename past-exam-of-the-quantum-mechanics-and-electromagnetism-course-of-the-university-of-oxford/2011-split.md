# 2011

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the quantum mechanics and electromagnetism course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This is the combined Part B paper for Quantum Mechanics and Electromagnetism and Quantum Theory and Quantum Computers. Its cover explicitly prints Part B and the joint label B7.1/C7.1. Section A belongs to the former course; section B belongs to the latter and is also present, with question numbers 1–3, in the separate Part C paper C7.1b, formal code `DMAC 2A78`. The combined paper is organized here under Quantum Mechanics and Electromagnetism; the standalone Part C paper belongs to Quantum Theory and Quantum Computers.

Source release: [Oxford examination archive release thread](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), Part B bundle pages 291–296. The sitting was Wednesday 15 June 2011, 9.30am–12.30pm.

Answer availability checked on 28 October 2026: bounded searches of official Oxford sources by year, course titles, module labels and both papers' formal codes found no advertisement of exact full-paper or selected-part solutions. Availability for these 2011 papers was checked independently of the later even-year policy described in the [departmental examination overview](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/members/students/undergraduate-courses/examinations-assessments/overview-examinations).
