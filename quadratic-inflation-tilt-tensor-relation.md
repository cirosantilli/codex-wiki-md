# Quadratic-inflation tilt-tensor relation

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic-potential slow-roll solution](quadratic-potential-slow-roll-solution.md)

The quadratic potential has equal [potential slow-roll parameters](potential-slow-roll-parameter.md), $\epsilon_V=\eta_V=1/(2N+1)$. Consequently $n_s=1-4/(2N+1)$ and $r=16/(2N+1)$ at leading order. The relation $r=4(1-n_s)$ is independent of the [inflaton](inflaton.md) mass, so a satisfactory scalar tilt does not by itself guarantee a satisfactory tensor prediction.
