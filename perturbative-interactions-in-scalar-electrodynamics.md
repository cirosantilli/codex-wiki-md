# Perturbative interactions in scalar electrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)

Expanding the covariant kinetic term of [scalar electrodynamics](scalar-electrodynamics.md) gives a one-photon two-scalar vertex and a two-photon two-scalar [seagull vertex](seagull-vertex.md). Their tree amplitudes and [Ward identities](ward-identity.md) depend on a consistent choice of charge-flow and [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md) convention.
