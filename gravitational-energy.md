# Gravitational energy

↑ **Parent:** [Potential energy](potential-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_energy)

The potential energy associated with gravitational interactions. For a self-gravitating spherical body with a fixed dimensionless density profile, $E_{\rm grav}=-C GM^2/R$ for a positive profile-dependent constant $C$; a uniform sphere has $C=3/5$. The $R^{-1}$ scaling competes with the same radius dependence of ultrarelativistic [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) in a white dwarf, giving the [Chandrasekhar mass](chandrasekhar-limit.md) scale.
