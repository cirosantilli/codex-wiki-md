# Longest common prefix

↑ **Parent:** [String](string.md)

The longest common prefix of two [strings](string.md) is the longest [string](string.md) that is a [string prefix](string-prefix.md) of both. Its length is the number of matching initial characters before the first mismatch or the end of a [string](string.md). A direct scan maintains equality of the processed prefixes and takes time linear in that length plus one. For suffixes represented by indices in a shared [array](array-data-structure.md), no character copying is needed.
