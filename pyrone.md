# Pyrone

↑ **Parent:** [Heterocyclic compound](heterocyclic-compound.md)

A six-membered [oxygen](oxygen.md) [heterocycle](heterocyclic-compound.md) containing an exocyclic [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md). A charge-separated contributor with [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](oxygen.md) negative and a pyrylium-like ring clarifies why aromatic character is weaker and more polarized than in [benzene](benzene.md).
