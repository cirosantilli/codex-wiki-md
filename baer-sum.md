# Baer sum

↑ **Parent:** [Extension group](extension-group.md)

To add two extensions of $M$ by $N$, take their [direct sum](direct-sum.md), form a [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md) along the diagonal $M\to M\oplus M$, and form a [pushout in a category](pushout-in-a-category.md) along addition $N\oplus N\to N$. This produces another extension of $M$ by $N$ and defines addition in $\operatorname{Ext}^1_R(M,N)$. In a [quiver representation](representation-of-a-quiver.md), vertexwise splittings express the two extensions by off-diagonal arrow matrices, and the Baer sum adds those matrices modulo the [extension complex of quiver representations](extension-complex-of-quiver-representations.md) coboundaries.
