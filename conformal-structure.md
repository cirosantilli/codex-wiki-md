# Conformal structure

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal geometry](conformal-geometry.md)

An equivalence class of [Riemannian metrics](riemannian-metric.md) under multiplication by positive smooth functions. Such rescalings preserve angles. On an oriented smooth surface, a conformal structure is equivalent to a [complex structure](complex-structure.md), with local holomorphic coordinates in which its metrics are positive multiples of $dx^2+dy^2$.
