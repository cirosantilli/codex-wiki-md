# Bessel density modes during exponential expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Matter density modes during cosmological-constant domination](matter-density-modes-during-cosmological-constant-domination.md)

For constant positive [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) $\mu$ and a four-thirds [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md), a [linear cosmological density perturbation](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md) obeys $\ddot\delta+2\mu\dot\delta+\eta^2e^{-3\mu t}\delta=0$. Put $\delta=e^{-\mu t}y$ and $x=2|\eta|e^{-3\mu t/2}/(3\mu)$. The resulting [Bessel differential equation](bessel-differential-equation.md) has order $2/3$; use ordinary [Bessel functions](bessel-function.md) for $\eta^2>0$ and [modified Bessel functions](modified-bessel-function.md) for $\eta^2<0$. Their small-$x$ expansions give one constant limiting mode and one $e^{-2\mu t}$ mode. The constant limit need not exceed the initial amplitude without an initial-velocity condition.
