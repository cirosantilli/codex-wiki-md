# Density-depth degeneracy of free-free radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md)

For a uniform ionized hydrogen column with known [temperature](temperature-split.md) and fixed projected angular area, free-free brightness and [optical depth](optical-depth.md) depend on $\mathrm{EM}=n_e^2L$. The displayed rescaling leaves the whole ideal spectrum unchanged. [density](density.md) and path length cannot be separated without additional information. A known geometry/distance, or a distinct column diagnostic proportional to $n_eL$, can break the degeneracy; [gas clumping factor](gas-clumping-factor.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md) structure complicate that inference.
