# Conserved-mean convection amplitude equations

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md)

The [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) $T_t=-\mu\Delta T-\Delta^2T+\nabla\cdot(|\nabla T|^2\nabla T)$ on a $2\pi$-periodic square conserves the spatial mean. On a fixed-mean subspace its first instability is at $\mu=1$ and has four real critical [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md). With $\mu=1+\varepsilon^2\nu$ and $T=\varepsilon(Ae^{ix}+Be^{iy}+\text{complex conjugate})+\cdots$, projection onto the critical [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) gives the displayed equation and its $A,B$ interchange. For $\nu>0$, squares have $|A|^2=|B|^2=\nu/5$, [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $-2\nu,-2\nu/5$, and neutral translation [phases](phase-waves.md). Rolls have a positive transverse growth rate $\nu/3$.
