<h1 id="jordan-holder-theorem-for-modules">Jordan–Hölder theorem for modules</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Composition series of a module](composition-series-of-a-module.md)

Every two finite [composition series of a module](composition-series-of-a-module.md) have the same multiset of simple [composition factors](composition-factor.md), up to isomorphism and order. One proves this by induction on the length: if the first simple submodules differ, their intersection is zero, and the quotient by their sum identifies the remaining common comparison problem; if they coincide, pass directly to the quotient. The theorem makes the multiplicity of a simple module independent of a chosen composition series. It does not assert that the module is a direct sum of its composition factors.
