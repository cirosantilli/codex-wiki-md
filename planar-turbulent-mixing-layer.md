# Planar turbulent mixing layer

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent mixing](turbulent-mixing.md)

A [planar turbulent mixing layer](planar-turbulent-mixing-layer.md) develops between two parallel streams of different mean [velocities](velocity.md). Their [shear flow](shear-flow.md) generates [turbulence](turbulence-split.md) that transports streamwise [linear momentum](momentum-split.md) across the interface. A thin-layer [eddy viscosity](eddy-viscosity.md) model with $\nu_T=C\Delta U\delta(x)$ has thickness proportional to downstream distance. For $\Delta U/U_c\ll1$, its leading mean [velocity](velocity.md) transition is an [error function](error-function.md).
