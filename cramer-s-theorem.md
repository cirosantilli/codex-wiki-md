<h1 id="cramer-s-theorem">Cramér's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cramér's_theorem_(large_deviations))

For [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with a [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) finite on an open interval containing zero, the empirical mean satisfies a [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) with rate function given by the [Legendre transform of a cumulant-generating function](legendre-transform-of-a-cumulant-generating-function.md). The exponential-moment hypothesis is part of this theorem; mere integrability does not guarantee this full large-deviation principle.
