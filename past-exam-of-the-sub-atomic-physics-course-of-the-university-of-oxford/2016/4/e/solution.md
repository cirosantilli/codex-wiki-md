<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The new light active leptons open one additional $W\to\sigma N$ channel and two $Z$ channels. At the stated equal-partial-width approximation,

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_W'=2085+232=2317\ \mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

**The quoted $501$ MeV is the total invisible width into the three known [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) flavors**, not the width into one flavor. The [invisible Z width per active neutrino](../../../../invisible-z-width-per-active-neutrino.md) fixes this interpretation. The notation in the numerical input is ambiguous; the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) normalization fixes the new active-neutrino contribution to $501/3=167$ MeV. Adding the new charged-lepton width therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_Z'=2495+167+84=2746\ \mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

This interpretation is consistent with the usual determination of the effective number of active [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) from the invisible width divided by the predicted width of a single species.[definition of the effective [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) count from the total invisible width and the single-species [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) width](../../../../number-of-neutrino-types-by-particle-data-group.md)

Keep the original light-fermion production and observed-decay [partial decay widths](../../../../partial-decay-width.md), masses and prefactors fixed. New leptonic channels alter their [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md) through the total widths. From the peak formula in [d](../d.md),

$$
\frac{R'}{R}=\left(\frac{\Gamma_Z'}{\Gamma_Z}\frac{\Gamma_W}{\Gamma_W'}\right)^2
=\left(\frac{2746}{2495}\frac{2085}{2317}\right)^2
=0.9808893.
$$

Consequently,

$$
\boxed{\frac{R'-R}{R}=-0.0191107\simeq-1.91\%.}
$$

The relatively small change is a cancellation between the increase in the two total widths; each separately changes substantially more. This assumes that the new [quarks](../../../../quark.md) do not contribute and that adding leptons leaves the old [partial decay widths](../../../../partial-decay-width.md) unchanged.

If the source's $501$ MeV were instead imposed literally as the width of a single [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) flavor, the algebra would give $\Gamma_Z'=3080$ MeV and $R'/R=1.23401$. That would be a different numerical premise, inconsistent with the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) interpretation of the supplied invisible-width value. It is recorded to make the normalization issue explicit, rather than silently treating the three-flavor sum as a one-flavor [partial decay width](../../../../partial-decay-width.md).
