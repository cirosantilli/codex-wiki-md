<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Under the stipulated peak-energy approximation, set $E=m_V$ separately for $V=W,Z$ in the supplied [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](../../../../breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md). For a spin-one resonance,

$$
\sigma_V(m_V)\propto\frac{3B_{\rm in}^VB_{\rm out}^V}{m_V^2}
=\frac{3\Gamma_{\rm in}^V\Gamma_{\rm out}^V}{m_V^2\Gamma_V^2}.
$$

Define the measured ratio as $R=\sigma(W\to\ell\nu)/\sigma(Z\to\ell\ell)$. With the known production prefactor ratio neglected as instructed, the spin factors cancel and

$$
R=\frac{m_Z^2}{m_W^2}\frac{\Gamma_{\rm in}^W\Gamma_{\rm out}^W}
{\Gamma_{\rm in}^Z\Gamma_{\rm out}^Z}\frac{\Gamma_Z^2}{\Gamma_W^2}.
$$

Solving for the positive physical width gives

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_W=\Gamma_Z\frac{m_Z}{m_W}
\sqrt{\frac{\Gamma_{\rm in}^W\Gamma_{\rm out}^W}
{R\,\Gamma_{\rm in}^Z\Gamma_{\rm out}^Z}}.}
$$

If the production prefactors are retained as $C_W,C_Z$, multiply the quantity under the square root by $C_W/C_Z$. The [partial decay widths](../../../../partial-decay-width.md) in this extraction are calculated inputs, while the [total decay width](../../../../total-decay-width.md) being inferred need not be fixed to its [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) value.

The [peak and integrated resonance width dependence](../../../../peak-and-integrated-resonance-width-dependence.md) explains why the square root is essential to the specified **peak** approximation. An energy-integrated narrow-width cross section contains one inverse power of the [total decay width](../../../../total-decay-width.md), because integrating over the resonance supplies a factor proportional to its width. That different observable leads to a different extraction formula. Real hadron-collider analyses also include [parton distribution functions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) and the energy convolution; the present result follows the explicit approximation in the source.
