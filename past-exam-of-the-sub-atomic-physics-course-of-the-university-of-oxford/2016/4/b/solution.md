<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using the measured single-flavor leptonic [partial decay width](../../../../partial-decay-width.md) as one unit,

$$
\boxed{N_{\rm eff}=\frac{\Gamma_W}{\Gamma(W\to e\nu)}
=\frac{2085}{232}=8.987\simeq9.}
$$

This agrees with three leptonic channels plus six color-distinguishable [quark](../../../../quark.md) channels in the leading massless approximation. The six [quark](../../../../quark.md) units are two accessible up-type flavors times three [color charges](../../../../color-charge.md), with the sums of squared [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) elements over down-type partners approximately unity. They are not nine generations or nine new flavors. Small mass and [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) radiative corrections prevent the ratio from being an exactly integer channel count.
