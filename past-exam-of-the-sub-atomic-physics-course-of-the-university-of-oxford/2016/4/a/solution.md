<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the first generation the flavor-diagonal [weak neutral current](../../../../neutral-current.md) permits

$$
Z\longrightarrow e^-e^+,\quad\nu_e\bar\nu_e,\quad u\bar u,\quad d\bar d.
$$

Each [quark](../../../../quark.md) channel has three possible matching [color charges](../../../../color-charge.md). The [weak charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) permits

$$
W^+\longrightarrow e^+\nu_e,\ u\bar d;\qquad
W^-\longrightarrow e^-\bar\nu_e,\ d\bar u.
$$

The two charges have equal [partial decay widths](../../../../partial-decay-width.md) to charge-conjugate channels to the accuracy needed here. [Lepton universality](../../../../lepton-universality.md) and [color multiplicity in a decay width](../../../../color-multiplicity-in-a-decay-width.md) give one unit of width for a massless lepton pair and three for a [quark](../../../../quark.md) pair, apart from [quark](../../../../quark.md) mixing and radiative corrections.

**If only the first generation is included**, the [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md) are $1/(1+3)=1/4$ to the lepton channel and $3/4$ to [quarks](../../../../quark.md). For the actual accessible [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) channels, there are three lepton units and two accessible up-type [quark](../../../../quark.md) rows, $u$ and $c$, each with three colors. The top [quark](../../../../quark.md) is too heavy. Unitarity of the [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) sums over accessible down-type partners, giving nine units in total:

$$
\boxed{B(W\to\ell\nu)\simeq\frac19\ \text{for one lepton flavor},\quad
B(W\to\text{any leptons})\simeq\frac13,\quad
B(W\to\text{any quarks})\simeq\frac23.}
$$

These fractions concern a charged lepton together with its appropriate [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md); “lepton pair” does not mean two charged leptons in a $W$ decay. The [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) has different vector and axial couplings for different fermions, so the same simple equal-unit rule cannot be transferred to all its [partial decay widths](../../../../partial-decay-width.md).
