<h1 id="2/c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $c=1$ and let $E$ denote the **total** center-of-momentum energy, not one beam's energy. Opposite equal momenta give $E=2E_{\rm beam}=498$ MeV, so the [kaon](../../../../../kaon.md) is at its stipulated resonance mass $m_K=498$ MeV. Write the unspecified selected exit [branching fractions](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) as $b_\rho$ and $b_K$. The supplied line shape gives

$$
\mathcal R(E)\equiv\frac{\sigma_\rho}{\sigma_K}
=\frac{3b_\rho}{0.69b_K}\frac{\Gamma_\rho^2}{\Gamma_K^2}
\frac{(E^2-m_K^2)^2+E^2\Gamma_K^2}
{(E^2-m_\rho^2)^2+E^2\Gamma_\rho^2}.
$$

The distinction between [inclusive and exclusive resonance branching factors](../../../../../inclusive-and-exclusive-resonance-branching-factors.md) matters here. For inclusive resonance formation, sum over all decays, so $b_\rho=b_K=1$. At $E=m_K$ the small [kaon](../../../../../kaon.md) width cancels from the peak value:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal R_{\rm inclusive}(498)=\frac3{0.69}
\frac{498^2\,147^2}{(498^2-769^2)^2+498^2\,147^2}
=0.1890.}
$$

If instead the measured cross section is specifically resonant elastic $\pi^+\pi^-\to\pi^+\pi^-$, its exit [branching fractions](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) equal the entrance ones: $b_\rho=1$, $b_K=0.69$. Then

$$
\boxed{\mathcal R_{\pi\pi}(498)=0.1890/0.69=0.2740.}
$$

The source says production without specifying an observed decay channel, so both conventions are made explicit rather than silently squaring a [branching fraction](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) for an inclusive measurement. The quoted [kaon](../../../../../kaon.md) width and two-pion [branching fraction](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) identify the short-lived propagation state $K_S$; a pure flavor state $K^0$ is a superposition of short- and long-lived states. This exercise uses the stipulated $K_S$ resonance model and equal omitted proportionality factors.
