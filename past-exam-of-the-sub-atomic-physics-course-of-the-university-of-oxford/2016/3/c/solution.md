<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Interpret the displayed [semi-empirical mass formula](../../../../semi-empirical-mass-formula.md) as an equation for mass energy, using $c=1$ for the mass terms. Its seven contributions are as follows.

The term $Z(m_p+m_e)$ is the rest energy of the $Z$ [protons](../../../../proton.md) and $Z$ atomic [electrons](../../../../electron.md); $(A-Z)m_n$ is the rest energy of the [neutrons](../../../../neutron-split.md). Including $Zm_e$ makes this an atomic-mass approximation. Small atomic electronic binding energies are neglected.

The volume contribution $-a_vA$ represents the short-range attractive [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md). Saturation gives each nucleon roughly a fixed number of neighbors, so the [nuclear binding energy](../../../../nuclear-binding-energy.md) is approximately proportional to $A$; binding lowers the mass. The surface contribution $+a_sA^{2/3}$ restores the binding missing from [nucleons](../../../../nucleon.md) near the surface. Approximately constant nuclear density gives radius proportional to $A^{1/3}$ and area proportional to $A^{2/3}$, explaining that power and the positive mass correction.

The [Coulomb potential energy](../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md) contribution $+a_cZ^2/A^{1/3}$ is positive because [proton](../../../../proton.md) charges repel. A sphere's electrostatic self-energy scales as $Z^2/R$, and $R\propto A^{1/3}$. The more refined proton-pair count $Z(Z-1)$ differs from the $Z^2$ approximation explicitly used here.

The [nuclear symmetry energy](../../../../symmetry-energy.md) contribution $+a_a(Z-A/2)^2/A$ penalizes unequal [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) and [proton](../../../../proton.md) populations. By the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md), filling one species' higher-momentum states is energetically more expensive than balancing the two [Fermi seas](../../../../fermi-sea.md); nuclear interactions also contribute to the empirical coefficient. The stated convention is equivalent to $23.285(N-Z)^2/A$ MeV, since $Z-A/2=(Z-N)/2$. It must not be combined with the usual coefficient near $23$ MeV without this factor of four.

Finally, [nuclear pairing](../../../../nuclear-pairing.md) favors paired [protons](../../../../proton.md) and paired [neutrons](../../../../neutron-split.md). In the mass formula,

$$
\boxed{\Delta M_{\rm pair}c^2=
\begin{cases}
-a_pA^{-1/2}&Z,N\text{ both even},\\
0&A\text{ odd},\\
+a_pA^{-1/2}&Z,N\text{ both odd}.
\end{cases}}
$$

The signs reverse when this correction is written as a contribution to positive [nuclear binding energy](../../../../nuclear-binding-energy.md). This smooth [liquid-drop model](../../../../liquid-drop-model-of-the-nucleus.md) omits detailed nuclear shell corrections.
