<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first step of the [proton–proton chain](../../../../proton-proton-chain.md) is

$$
\boxed{p+p\longrightarrow d+e^++\nu_e,\qquad Q\simeq0.420\ \mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

Here $d$ is the [deuteron](../../../../deuteron.md). A [weak charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) converts one [proton](../../../../proton.md) into a [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md), while the resulting [proton](../../../../proton.md)–[neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) pair becomes bound. The [Q value](../../../../q-value.md) follows from the difference of initial and final rest energies:

$$
Q=(2m_p-m_d-m_e)c^2
=B_d-(m_n-m_p)c^2-m_ec^2
\simeq2.2246-1.2933-0.5110=0.4203\ \mathrm{MeV}.
$$

This includes the emitted [Positron](../../../../positron.md)'s rest energy in the final-state mass and excludes its subsequent [electron-positron annihilation](../../../../electron-positron-annihilation.md). The [nuclear binding energy](../../../../nuclear-binding-energy.md) of the [deuteron](../../../../deuteron.md) makes this reaction possible although the isolated conversion $p\to n+e^++\nu_e$ is energetically forbidden. The released energy is shared by final-state kinetic energies, including the escaping [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md).
