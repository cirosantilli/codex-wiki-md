<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The radiation registered in the stated [solar luminosity](../../../../solar-luminosity.md) includes two [Positron](../../../../positron.md) annihilations but excludes the two escaping [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md). Thus the visible energy per completed helium-producing chain is

$$
\boxed{Q_{\rm vis}=24.68+2(1.02)-2(0.26)=26.20\ \mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

The signs in this energy ledger matter: adding the [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) energy would overestimate the energy available to sustain the measured radiation. Using $1\ \mathrm{MeV}=1.602176634\times10^{-13}$ J, the chain rate and hydrogen-consumption rate are

$$
\dot N_{\rm chain}=\frac{3.86\times10^{26}}{26.20(1.602176634\times10^{-13})}
=9.196\times10^{37}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}},\qquad
\dot N_H=4\dot N_{\rm chain}=3.678\times10^{38}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}.
$$

With a year taken as $365.25$ days, the stated elapsed time is $1.4516496\times10^{17}$ s. Consequently,

$$
\boxed{N_{H,\rm used}=5.34\times10^{55},\qquad
N_{H,\rm remaining}=8.47\times10^{56}.}
$$

At the same fixed [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md), the remaining time would be

$$
\boxed{t_{\rm remaining}=\frac{9\times10^{56}-5.3395\times10^{55}}{3.6782\times10^{38}}
=2.302\times10^{18}\ \mathrm{s}=7.29\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{yr}.}
$$

Only about $5.93\%$ of the stipulated initial reservoir has been consumed under these assumptions. This is a calculation for an entirely accessible hydrogen reservoir with constant [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md). It is not a prediction of the Sun's remaining [main-sequence lifetime](../../../../main-sequence-lifetime.md): only part of the hydrogen participates in core burning, and stellar structure and [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md) evolve.
