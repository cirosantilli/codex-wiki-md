<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) has valence content $udd$ and total [spin](../../../../spin.md) $J=1/2$; its ground-state parity is positive. The two [down quarks](../../../../down-quark.md) contribute charge $-2/3$ and the [up quark](../../../../up-quark.md) contributes $+2/3$, giving net charge zero. “Three valence [quarks](../../../../quark.md)” specifies the net flavor quantum numbers, rather than excluding [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) or additional sea [quark](../../../../quark.md)–[antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) components from the full [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) state.

In the approximate light-flavor SU(3) classification, the [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) belongs to the ground-state [baryon octet](../../../../baryon-octet.md), containing eight [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md):

$$
\boxed{p,n;\quad\Sigma^+,\Sigma^0,\Sigma^-;\quad\Lambda^0;\quad\Xi^0,\Xi^-.}
$$

These are eight particle species, without counting their [antiparticles](../../../../antiparticle.md) again as extra members of the same octet. The corresponding [antibaryons](../../../../antibaryon.md) form the conjugate particle set. Within the smaller SU(2) [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) classification, the [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) instead belongs to the two-member nucleon doublet $(p,n)$. Stating the symmetry distinguishes the usual eight-member flavor multiplet answer from the also valid two-member [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) multiplet. The [baryon decuplet](../../../../baryon-decuplet.md) has different ground-state spin and is not the [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md)'s octet.
