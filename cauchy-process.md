# Cauchy process

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy process](levy-process.md)

The standard symmetric [Cauchy process](cauchy-process.md) is the [Lévy process](levy-process.md) with [Lévy characteristic exponent](characteristic-exponent-of-a-levy-process.md) $\Psi(u)=|u|$. Its time-$t$ distribution for $t>0$ is the [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) of location zero and scale $t$. It can be constructed by [subordination of a Lévy process](subordination-of-a-levy-process.md): evaluate an independent standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) at the [Brownian first-passage subordinator](brownian-first-passage-subordinator.md), whose Laplace exponent is $\sqrt{2\lambda}$.
