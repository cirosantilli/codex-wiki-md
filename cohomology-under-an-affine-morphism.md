# Cohomology under an affine morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Affine morphism](affine-morphism.md)

For an [affine morphism](affine-morphism.md) $f:X\to Y$ and a [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$ on $X$, the [direct image sheaf](direct-image-sheaf.md) has the same [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) on $Y$ as $\mathcal F$ on $X$. To prove this, take a [flasque resolution](flasque-resolution.md) $\mathcal F\to\mathcal I^\bullet$. Its direct images are [flasque sheaves](flasque-sheaf.md). On each affine open $V\subset Y$, the complex of sections of the direct images is $\Gamma(f^{-1}V,\mathcal I^\bullet)$. By [vanishing of quasi-coherent cohomology on an affine scheme](vanishing-of-quasi-coherent-cohomology-on-an-affine-scheme.md), this augmented complex is exact. Checking on the affine basis proves that $f_*\mathcal I^\bullet$ resolves $f_*\mathcal F$. Its global sections are identical to those of the original resolution.
