# Disk algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform algebra](uniform-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disk_algebra)

The [disk algebra](disk-algebra.md) consists of functions continuous on the closed unit disk and holomorphic in its interior, with the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md). Polynomials are dense: dilations $f_r(z)=f(rz)$ converge uniformly to $f$ as $r\uparrow1$, and each $f_r$ is uniformly approximated on the closed disk by its Taylor polynomials. A [character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md) is therefore determined by its value $\lambda$ at the coordinate function; boundedness gives $|\lambda|\leq1$, and polynomial approximation yields $\varphi(f)=f(\lambda)$. Consequently the [disk algebra](disk-algebra.md) is a [natural uniform algebra](natural-uniform-algebra.md) on the closed disk.
