# Dimensionless electric displacement in DBI theory

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac-Born-Infeld action](dirac-born-infeld-action.md)

With $\mathbf E_i=2\pi\alpha'F_{0i}$, this normalization separates the nonlinear constitutive relation from the [D3-brane](d3-brane.md) tension. It is the normalized canonical electric momentum. The [Gauss law constraint in gauge theory](gauss-law-constraint-in-gauge-theory.md) requires its divergence to vanish away from string endpoints. On the electric [BIon](born-infeld-soliton.md) locus it equals the scaled electric field, but this equality is not true for arbitrary configurations.
