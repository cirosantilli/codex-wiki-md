# Additive genetic covariance matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

The [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) of additive inherited contributions to a collection of [phenotypic traits](phenotypic-trait.md). Its diagonal entries are additive [genetic variances](genetic-variance.md); off-diagonal entries describe additive [genetic covariance](genetic-covariance.md). It summarizes available local directions of response but can change as [allele frequencies](allele-frequency.md) and associations change.
