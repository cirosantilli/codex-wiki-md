# Eta mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Eta and eta prime mesons](eta-and-eta-prime-mesons.md)

Mixing of the [Eta octet state](eta-octet-state.md) and [eta singlet state](eta-singlet-state.md) to produce physical neutral pseudoscalar [mass](mass.md) eigenstates. It limits [mass](mass.md) identities derived by treating the physical [eta meson](eta-meson.md) as a pure octet state.
