# Secondary cosmic ray

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md)

A [secondary cosmic ray](secondary-cosmic-ray.md) here is a particle produced by an atmospheric interaction of a [primary cosmic ray](primary-cosmic-ray.md) or its descendants. A [muon](muon.md) at ground level need not have traveled from the original astronomical source.
