# Deterministic two-qubit entanglement dilution

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement dilution](entanglement-dilution.md)

For nonnegative $u,v$ with $u^2+v^2=1$, Alice applies [Kraus operators](kraus-operator.md) $M_0=\operatorname{diag}(u,v)$ and $M_1=\operatorname{diag}(v,u)$ to one half of a [Bell state](bell-state-split.md). Their squared effects sum to the identity, and both outcomes have probability one half. The first branch is the target; the second becomes the target after both parties apply [Pauli X gates](pauli-x-gate.md). Sending the outcome by [classical communication](classical-communication.md) makes this exact deterministic [entanglement dilution](entanglement-dilution.md), rather than a postselected conversion.
