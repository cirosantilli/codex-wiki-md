# Unconfined aquifer with depth-dependent permeability

↑ **Parent:** [Unconfined aquifer](unconfined-aquifer.md)

With [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) $k(z)=k_0(1+\beta z)$, the [Dupuit approximation](dupuit-approximation.md) gives

$$
q=-u_b\left(h+\frac\beta2h^2\right)h_x,\qquad \phi h_t+q_x=R,\qquad u_b=\frac{k_0\rho g}{\mu}.
$$

Here $\phi$ is [porosity](porosity.md) and $R$ is recharge. The low-height limit has mobility proportional to $h$, whereas large heights have mobility proportional to $h^2$. This changes both the [forced filling similarity for power-law diffusion](forced-filling-similarity-for-power-law-diffusion.md) and the [separable draining profile for power-law diffusion](separable-draining-profile-for-power-law-diffusion.md).
