# Cutoff independence of an oscillatory integral

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillatory integral](oscillatory-integral.md)

For an amplitude of symbol order $N$ in $k$ frequency variables, applying a phase integration operator $r>N+k$ times makes the pairing with a [test function](test-function.md) absolutely integrable. Derivatives of a large-frequency cutoff are supported where $|\theta|\asymp R$ and give an error bounded by $CR^{N-r+k}$ times finitely many test derivatives. That error tends to zero, making the resulting [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) independent of the cutoff and of the admissible number of integrations.
