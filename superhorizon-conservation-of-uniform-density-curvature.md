# Superhorizon conservation of uniform-density curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform-density curvature perturbation](uniform-density-curvature-perturbation.md)

With the convention $\zeta=\Phi-\delta\rho/[3(\bar\rho+\bar P)]$, energy conservation gives $\zeta'=\mathcal H\delta P_{\rm nad}/(\bar\rho+\bar P)+(\nabla\cdot\mathbf v)/3$. The [non-adiabatic pressure perturbation](non-adiabatic-pressure-perturbation.md) vanishes for [adiabatic cosmological perturbations](adiabatic-initial-conditions.md), and regular velocity gradients are suppressed on [superhorizon scales](superhorizon-scale.md). Curvature is then conserved to leading gradient order, allowing primordial initial conditions to be propagated between cosmological eras.
