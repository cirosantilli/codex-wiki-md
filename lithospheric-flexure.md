# Lithospheric flexure

↑ **Parent:** [Lithosphere](lithosphere.md)

[Lithospheric flexure](lithospheric-flexure.md) is bending of a mechanically strong outer shell under loads. A flat [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) approximation balances bending, buoyancy and applied loading. In the Fourier domain, bending contributes a term proportional to the [flexural rigidity](bending-stiffness.md) times the fourth power of horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md); it therefore suppresses short-wavelength deflection particularly strongly.
