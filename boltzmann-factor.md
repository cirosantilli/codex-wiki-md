# Boltzmann factor

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann_factor)

At inverse temperature $\beta=1/(k_BT)$, the Boltzmann factor $e^{-\beta E}$ is the relative [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md) weight of a [microstate](microstate.md) with energy $E$.
