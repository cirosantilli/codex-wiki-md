# Stationary points of a cosine-modulated Gaussian

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary point](stationary-point.md)

The [gradient](gradient.md) vanishes only when $y=0$ and $\tan x=-2x$. On each tangent branch, $\tan x+2x$ increases strictly from negative to positive infinity, so it has exactly one root $x_s$. With $z_s=e^{-x_s^2}\cos x_s$, the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is diagonal with entries $-(4x_s^2+3)z_s$ and $-2z_s$. Its determinant is positive, so every stationary point is a strict maximum when $z_s>0$ or a strict minimum when $z_s<0$. Signs alternate between successive tangent branches. This gives infinitely many nondegenerate extrema under a decaying [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) envelope.
