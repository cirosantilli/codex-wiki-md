# Normal matrices have equal adjoint norms

↑ **Parent:** [Normal matrix](normal-matrix.md)

For a [normal matrix](normal-matrix.md), $N^\dagger N=NN^\dagger$. Consequently $\|Nx\|^2=x^\dagger N^\dagger Nx=x^\dagger NN^\dagger x=\|N^\dagger x\|^2$. In particular, $N$ and its [Hermitian conjugate](hermitian-conjugation.md) have the same [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md).
