# First-order transition in a cubic-quartic Landau potential

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)

For a real unrestricted scalar [order parameter](order-parameter.md) and $A=A_2m^2/2+A_3m^3/3+A_4m^4/4$ with $A_3\ne0$ and $A_4>0$, the disordered [global minimum](global-minimum.md) is replaced discontinuously by a nonzero minimum at the displayed [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) condition. At coexistence $A=A_4m^2[m+2A_3/(3A_4)]^2/4$. Ordered [stationary points](stationary-point.md) first appear at the [spinodal point](spinodal-point.md) $A_2=A_3^2/(4A_4)$, while the disordered point loses local stability at $A_2=0$; neither local limit is the equilibrium transition condition.
