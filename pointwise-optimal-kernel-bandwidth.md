# Pointwise optimal kernel bandwidth

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel density estimation](kernel-density-estimation.md)

For a second-order [kernel density estimator](kernel-density-estimation.md), if $f(x)>0$ and $f''(x)\ne0$, the leading pointwise [asymptotic mean squared error](asymptotic-mean-squared-error.md) is $R(K)f(x)/(nh)+\mu_2(K)^2f''(x)^2h^4/4$. Differentiation gives the displayed interior optimum. At a zero of $f$, the leading variance term vanishes and this two-term approximation has no positive interior minimizer; at a zero of $f''$, a higher-order bias expansion is needed.
