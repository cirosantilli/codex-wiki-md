# Source dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Point source](point-source.md)

A source dipole is the limit of an equal source and sink brought together with their source-strength times separation vector fixed at $\mathbf M$. Differentiating the [three-dimensional point source](three-dimensional-point-source.md) field gives $\mathbf u=-(\mathbf M\cdot\nabla)[\mathbf r/(4\pi r^3)]$. It has [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) $-\mathbf M\cdot\mathbf r/(4\pi r^3)$, zero total [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md), and $r^{-3}$ far-field decay. Unlike a [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md), it is an irrotational potential singularity rather than a [force-dipole flow](force-dipole-flow.md).
