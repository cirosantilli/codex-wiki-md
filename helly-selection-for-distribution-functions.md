# Helly selection for distribution functions

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform tightness](uniform-tightness.md)

A tight sequence of [probability measures](probability-measure.md) on the real line has a subsequence whose [cumulative distribution functions](cumulative-distribution-function.md) converge at every continuity point of a [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md). Choose a diagonal subsequence converging at every rational $q$, write those limits as $f(q)$, and define $F(x)=\inf_{q>x}f(q)$. Monotonicity gives right continuity, and [tightness of probability measures](uniform-tightness.md) gives limits zero and one at the two ends of the real line. Bracketing by rational continuity approximations proves convergence at the continuity points of $F$. The corresponding [probability measure](probability-measure.md) is the weak subsequential limit: approximate a bounded continuous test function by step functions on a compact interval, and use tightness to bound the tails.
