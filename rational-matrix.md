# Rational matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

A rational matrix is a [matrix](matrix.md) whose entries are [rational numbers](rational-number.md). Multiplying by a common denominator produces an [integer](integer.md) matrix and preserves its homogeneous solution set.
