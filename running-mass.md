# Running mass

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

A renormalized mass changes with the reference scale according to its mass [anomalous dimension](anomalous-dimension.md) $\delta(g)$. In a dimensionless correlator at increasing physical energy, its effective argument includes the canonical factor $e^{-t}$. This distinguishes the [running mass](running-mass.md) itself from its ratio to the growing energy scale.
