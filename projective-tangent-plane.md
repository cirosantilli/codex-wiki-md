# Projective tangent plane

↑ **Parent:** [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md)

For a [smooth algebraic surface](smooth-algebraic-surface.md) defined by a [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) $F$ in [projective space](projective-space-split.md) of dimension three, its tangent plane at $P$ is the displayed plane. Every [projective line](projective-line.md) on the surface through $P$ lies in this plane.
