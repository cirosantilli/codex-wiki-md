# Cyclopentadienyl ligand orbital interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclopentadienyl ligand](cyclopentadienyl-ligand.md)

The five ring p orbitals form one nondegenerate and two doubly degenerate symmetry-adapted sets. About the ring normal, these match [metal](metal.md) $d_{z^2}$, $(d_{xz},d_{yz})$, and $(d_{xy},d_{x^2-y^2})$ by sigma, pi and delta symmetry respectively. Matching symmetry permits interaction, but spatial overlap and energy separation determine its strength.
