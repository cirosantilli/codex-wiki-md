# Canonical energy variance

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md)

Differentiating the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) at fixed external parameters gives $\langle E\rangle=-\partial_\beta\ln Z$ and $\partial_\beta^2\ln Z=\langle E^2\rangle-\langle E\rangle^2$. Consequently the [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) is

$$
C_V=\frac{\operatorname{Var}(E)}{k_{\rm B}T^2}\ge0.
$$

The identity assumes an [energy](energy-split.md) spectrum independent of [temperature](temperature-split.md) and the appropriate fixed-volume [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md). It relates a thermal response to equilibrium [variance](variance-split.md).
