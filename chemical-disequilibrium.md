# Chemical disequilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md)

A chemical system is out of local [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) when reaction adjustment cannot keep pace with transport, imposed changes or external driving. Comparing the [chemical relaxation time](chemical-relaxation-time.md) with transport and irradiation times identifies plausible departures. [Disequilibrium chemistry in an exoplanet atmosphere](disequilibrium-chemistry-in-an-exoplanet-atmosphere.md) combines this principle with mixing, horizontal advection and [atmospheric photochemistry](atmospheric-photochemistry.md).
