# Cone angle

↑ **Parent:** [Flat cone](flat-cone.md)

The total angle around the tip of a Euclidean cone. For a [translation surface](translation-surface.md) zero of order $m$ it equals $2\pi(m+1)$, while for a [holomorphic quadratic differential](holomorphic-quadratic-differential.md) zero of order $m$ it equals $(m+2)\pi$. An angle $2\pi$ is a regular point.
