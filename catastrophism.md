# Catastrophism

↑ **Parent:** [Geology](geology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catastrophism)

Explanations assigning major geological changes to exceptional or rapid events. This does not logically entail that every formation was produced by one biblical flood.
