# Hausdorff locale

↑ **Parent:** [Locale](locale.md)

A [locale](locale.md) is Hausdorff when its diagonal into its localic square is a [closed sublocale](closed-sublocale.md). Its complement is the join of rectangles $a\times b$ with $a\wedge b=0$. This is stronger than the underlying space being a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md), because the localic square need not be spatial.
