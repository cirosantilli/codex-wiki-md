# Mycielskian triangle preservation

↑ **Parent:** [Mycielskian](mycielskian.md)

A finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md) $G$ is a [triangle-free graph](triangle-free-graph.md) if and only if its [Mycielskian](mycielskian.md) is a [triangle-free graph](triangle-free-graph.md). No [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) can contain the [Mycielski apex](mycielski-apex.md), whose neighbours are an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md), or two [Mycielski shadow vertices](mycielski-shadow-vertex.md). A [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) containing one shadow $u_i$ and two originals $v_j,v_\ell$ requires all three original [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) $v_iv_j,v_iv_\ell,v_jv_\ell$, hence an original [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md). Conversely every original [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) remains. In fact each original [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) gives four in $\mu(G)$: itself and the three choices of replacing one of its [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) by its shadow. Thus triangle-freeness is preserved, but a positive [triangle count](triangle-count.md) is multiplied by four.
