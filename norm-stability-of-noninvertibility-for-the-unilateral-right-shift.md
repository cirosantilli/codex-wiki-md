# Norm stability of noninvertibility for the unilateral right shift

↑ **Parent:** [Unilateral shift operator](unilateral-shift-operator.md)

On $\ell_1$, the [unilateral shift operator](unilateral-shift-operator.md) $R$ and [left shift operator](left-shift-operator.md) $L$ satisfy $LR=I$ and $\|L\|=1$, while $L$ has nontrivial kernel. If $\|A\|<1$, the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md) makes $L(R+A)=I+LA$ invertible. Were $R+A$ invertible, this identity would express $L$ as a product of invertible operators, a contradiction. Thus the open unit ball about $R$ contains no invertible operator.
