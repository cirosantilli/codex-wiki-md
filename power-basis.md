# Power basis

↑ **Parent:** [Simple field extension](simple-field-extension.md)

If $K(\alpha)/K$ is a simple algebraic [field extension](field-extension.md) of degree $n$, the displayed powers form a [basis](basis.md). The minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ reduces every higher power to a [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of them, while a nontrivial linear relation among the first $n$ powers would give a polynomial of degree below the minimal polynomial vanishing at $\alpha$. If $\alpha$ is an [algebraic integer](algebraic-integer.md) over the rational numbers, this is a basis of the order $\mathbb Z[\alpha]$ as a free abelian group; it need not be an [integral basis](integral-basis.md) of the whole [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md).
