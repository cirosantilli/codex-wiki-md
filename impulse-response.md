# Impulse response

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impulse_response)

The impulse response of a linear system is its response to a [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) input, under specified initial or boundary conditions. Time-translation invariance makes the response to a general forcing the [convolution](convolution.md) of that forcing with the impulse response. A causal response is zero before the impulse.
