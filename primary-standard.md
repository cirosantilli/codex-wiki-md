# Primary standard

↑ **Parent:** [Titrant standardization](titrant-standardization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_standard)

A [primary standard](primary-standard.md) supplies a reliably known reacting inventory from which a [titrant](titrant.md) [concentration](concentration.md) can be determined.

The defining advantage is known high purity: the weighed portion can be assigned a known [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md). A [titrant](titrant.md) whose [concentration](concentration.md) is established against that inventory is a secondary standard solution in IUPAC terminology.[IUPAC Compendium of Chemical Terminology, fifth edition (Gold Book) by International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry; Gold Book, standard solution, S05924; definitions of primary standard and secondary standard solution; underlying source Orange Book, second edition, p. 48](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/standard-solution.md)

An accurate balance determines total [mass](mass.md), including any unwanted [water](water.md) or other material. To infer reacting inventory, one also needs known composition, stability during storage and weighing, complete transfer and dissolution, and a sufficiently complete [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) of known [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md). A finely resolved balance cannot establish these chemical facts. [Potassium hydrogen phthalate](potassium-hydrogen-phthalate.md) is useful because a suitably prepared portion provides a known acid inventory for [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md) [titrant standardization](titrant-standardization.md); the [KHP standardization protocol](khp-standardization-protocol.md) below shows how that claim is put into practice.

Preparation belongs to the particular material and method. For example, the MIT handout specifies drying [potassium hydrogen phthalate](potassium-hydrogen-phthalate.md) at $110\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ for $1.5$ hours or overnight, cooling in a desiccator, and weighing by difference; its footnote identifies removal of superficial moisture as the purpose. This is a condition of that [potassium hydrogen phthalate](potassium-hydrogen-phthalate.md) procedure, not a universal instruction to heat every [primary standard](primary-standard.md) in the same way.[Printed Titration-5, §IV.2 and footnote 5: drying, cooling, weighing by difference, and superficial moisture](the-potentiometric-titration-of-an-acid-mixture-mit-5-310-laboratory-handout.md)
