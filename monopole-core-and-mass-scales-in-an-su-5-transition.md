<h1 id="monopole-core-and-mass-scales-in-an-su-5-transition">Monopole core and mass scales in an SU(5) transition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(5) grand unified theory](su-5-grand-unified-theory.md)

For [simply connected space](simply-connected-space.md) $SU(5)$ broken to the connected [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) subgroup, the exact homotopy sequence gives $\pi_2(G/H)\cong\pi_1(H)\cong\mathbb Z$. Stable [magnetic monopoles](magnetic-monopole.md) form. Their gauge and scalar cores have scales $(g\eta)^{-1}$ and $(\sqrt\lambda\eta)^{-1}$; their masses are $M_M\sim4\pi\eta/g$ times a dimensionless profile factor. The physical subgroup is $(SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1))/\mathbb Z_6$, which retains an infinite-cyclic fundamental group.
