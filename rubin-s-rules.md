<h1 id="rubin-s-rules">Rubin's rules</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple imputation](multiple-imputation.md)

For $M>1$ completed-data estimates $Q_m$ with variances $U_m$, pool using $\overline Q=M^{-1}\sum_mQ_m$, $\overline U=M^{-1}\sum_mU_m$, $B=(M-1)^{-1}\sum_m(Q_m-\overline Q)^2$, and $T=\overline U+(1+M^{-1})B$. The total [variance](variance-split.md) includes within-imputation uncertainty, between-imputation uncertainty and finite-$M$ simulation uncertainty.
