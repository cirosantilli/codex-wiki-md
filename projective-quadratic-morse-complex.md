# Projective quadratic Morse complex

↑ **Parent:** [Morse-Smale complex](morse-smale-complex.md)

For distinct ordered $a_0<\cdots<a_m$, the quotient $\sum a_jx_j^2/\sum x_j^2$ on [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) has one [critical point](critical-point.md) $[e_i]$ of index $i$. Its descending flow is $[x_j(t)]=[e^{-2a_jt}x_j(0)]$. The unstable cell at $[e_i]$ is $\mathbb{RP}^i\setminus\mathbb{RP}^{i-1}$. Between adjacent [critical points](critical-point.md) there are two trajectories, whose relative orientation is the degree $(-1)^i$ of the [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) on $S^{i-1}$. Thus, up to generator orientation, the differential is two in even degrees and zero in odd degrees. This gives the integral [cellular homology of real projective space](cellular-homology-of-real-projective-space.md) rather than only its mod-two ranks.
