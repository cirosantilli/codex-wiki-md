# Radical polymerization

↑ **Parent:** [Chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md)

A [chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md) whose propagating active centres are [free radicals](radical-chemistry.md). Initiation creates active chains, propagation adds monomers while regenerating an active centre, and termination or transfer changes the population of growing chains.[radical polymerization, term R05075: radical kinetic-chain carriers](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry.md)
