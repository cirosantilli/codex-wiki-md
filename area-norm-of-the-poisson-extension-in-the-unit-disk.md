# Area norm of the Poisson extension in the unit disk

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson kernel for the unit disk](poisson-kernel-for-the-unit-disk.md)

For square-integrable periodic boundary data and the [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) used in the [Poisson kernel for the unit disk](poisson-kernel-for-the-unit-disk.md), the displayed identity gives the squared area norm of its Poisson extension $u$. At fixed radius $r$, [Parseval's identity](parseval-identity.md) gives

$$
\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}|u(r,\theta)|^2\,d\theta
=\pi\left[\frac{|a_0|^2}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^\infty r^{2n}(|a_n|^2+|b_n|^2)\right].
$$

Multiply by the [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) [area element](area-element-of-a-surface.md) factor $r\,dr$ and integrate from zero to one. The constant term contributes $\pi|a_0|^2/4$, and $\int_0^1r^{2n+1}\,dr=1/[2(n+1)]$. Interchanging the nonnegative sum and radial [integral](integral-split.md) is justified by the [monotone convergence theorem](monotone-convergence-theorem.md). The resulting factor $1/(n+1)$ shows how higher angular frequencies contribute less to the interior area norm.
