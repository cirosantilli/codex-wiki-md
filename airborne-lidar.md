# Airborne lidar

↑ **Parent:** [Lidar](lidar.md)

[Lidar](lidar.md) acquired from an aircraft with surveyed trajectory and orientation. Ground-return classification removes canopy and structure returns before constructing a bare-ground [digital elevation model](digital-elevation-model.md). Penetration through forest is sampling through canopy gaps, not unrestricted transmission through leaves. Areas with few or no ground returns require uncertainty assessment rather than treating interpolated heights as observations.[Point-cloud returns from vegetation, structures and ground, and filtering to a bare-earth model](what-is-the-difference-between-lidar-data-and-a-digital-elevation-model-by-united-states-geological-survey.md)
