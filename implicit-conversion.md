# Implicit conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language](programming-language-split.md)

A conversion inserted by the language's typing rules rather than an explicit conversion call in source code. Its availability depends on scope and the conversion rules; converting a method receiver and converting an argument are distinct uses. In [Scala](scala.md), an imported implicit conversion can supply a rational-number receiver for an integer expression lacking an applicable multiplication overload.
