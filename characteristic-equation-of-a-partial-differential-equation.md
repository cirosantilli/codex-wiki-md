# Characteristic equation of a partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic direction](characteristic-direction.md)

For principal part $Au_{xx}+2Bu_{xy}+Cu_{yy}$, the displayed quadratic relation determines the [characteristic directions](characteristic-direction.md). Curves tangent to them are [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md). Lower-order terms do not enter this relation, but they may be singular where the principal coefficients alone suggest an otherwise ordinary classification. In particular, classifying a principal part on a singular line does not extend the full differential equation onto that line.
