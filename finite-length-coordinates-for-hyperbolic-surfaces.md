# Finite length coordinates for hyperbolic surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates](fenchel-nielsen-coordinates.md)

For a fixed [pants decomposition](pants-decomposition.md) of a closed [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-$g$ [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md), its $3g-3$ cuff lengths determine all its [Y-pieces](hyperbolic-y-piece.md). Two suitably chosen transverse simple closed curves at each cuff, one obtained from the other by a [Dehn twist](dehn-twist.md), determine the corresponding twist: hyperbolic seam identities express their lengths through shifted hyperbolic cosines of that twist, removing the sign ambiguity of just one transversal. Thus finitely many marked lengths determine the [Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates](fenchel-nielsen-coordinates.md). The unmarked [length spectrum](length-spectrum.md) lacks these labels, which is why this fact alone does not imply global spectral uniqueness.
