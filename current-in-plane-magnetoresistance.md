# Current-in-plane magnetoresistance

↑ **Parent:** [Giant magnetoresistance](giant-magnetoresistance.md)

The measuring [electric current](electric-current.md) flows along the layer planes. This is the geometry of the [Fe-Cr giant magnetoresistance experiments](fe-cr-giant-magnetoresistance-experiments.md). Scattering and current paths through an extended stack require a transport description; treating successive layers as ordinary series [resistors](resistor.md) would not follow from this geometry.
