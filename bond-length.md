# Bond length

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bond_length)

A [bond length](bond-length.md) describes the separation of the [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) of two bonded [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). Distinguish an equilibrium geometry from an instantaneous or experimentally averaged separation. Methods of determining [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md) can use different averaging conventions, so reported [bond lengths](bond-length.md) need their measurement context. See the [IUPAC Gold Book: bond length](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/BT07003). Changing a [bond length](bond-length.md) is one possible [reaction coordinate](reaction-coordinate.md).
