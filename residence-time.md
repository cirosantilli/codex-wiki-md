# Residence time

↑ **Parent:** [Residence time distribution](residence-time-distribution.md)

The time a material element spends in a system. In ideal [plug flow](plug-flow.md) it equals the volume divided by volumetric flow rate; in a mixed vessel that ratio is a mean and does not guarantee the age of every discharged element.
