# Filtered colimit in a category

↑ **Parent:** [Filtered category](filtered-category.md)

A filtered colimit in a category is a [colimit](colimit.md) whose indexing category is a small [filtered category](filtered-category.md). In the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md), [filtered colimits commute with finite limits in sets](filtered-colimits-commute-with-finite-limits-in-sets.md). A [filtered colimit of modules](filtered-colimit-of-modules.md) is an example of this general categorical construction.
