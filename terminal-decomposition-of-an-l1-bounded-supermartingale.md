# Terminal decomposition of an L1-bounded supermartingale

↑ **Parent:** [Supermartingale](supermartingale.md)

An $L^1$-bounded [supermartingale](supermartingale.md) has an [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md) limit $X_\infty$ in $L^1$. The [conditional-expectation martingale](conditional-expectation-martingale.md) $M_n=\mathbb E[X_\infty\mid\mathcal F_n]$ has [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md) and converges to $X_\infty$ both [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) and in $L^1$. To identify its limit, test against every event in every finite-time [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) and use uniqueness on their generated [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md). Then $Y=X-M$ is a [supermartingale](supermartingale.md) tending to zero [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). There is no general nonnegativity conclusion for $Y$, nor general [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md) to zero: the negative of the [fair-coin doubling martingale](fair-coin-doubling-martingale.md) is a counterexample to both stronger assertions.
