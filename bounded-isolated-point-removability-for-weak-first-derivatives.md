# Bounded isolated-point removability for weak first derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Weak derivative](weak-derivative.md)

A bounded function which is $C^1$ away from one point in dimension $n\geq2$, and whose classical gradient is locally integrable across that point, has that gradient as its [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md). Cut a [test function](test-function.md) off within distance $\varepsilon$ of the point. The error from differentiating the cutoff is bounded by $C\|u\|_\infty\varepsilon^{n-1}$ and vanishes. The other terms converge by local integrability. In dimension one the [Heaviside step function](heaviside-step-function.md) is a counterexample: its classical derivative off the point is zero, but its distributional derivative is a [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md).
