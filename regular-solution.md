# Regular solution

↑ **Parent:** [Solution (chemistry)](solution-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_solution)

A regular solution keeps the ideal combinatorial [entropy of mixing](entropy-of-mixing.md) while allowing a nonzero energetic preference for unlike contacts. For a symmetric binary [mixture](mixture-split.md) with equal molecular sizes, the displayed free energy per site combines mixing entropy with a contact penalty $\chi\phi(1-\phi)$. Its [spinodal](spinodal.md) satisfies $1/\phi+1/(1-\phi)-2\chi=0$. At the symmetric [thermodynamic critical point](thermodynamic-critical-point.md), the third composition derivative also vanishes, giving $\phi_c=1/2$ and $\chi_c=2$. If $\chi=A/T$, the critical temperature is $A/2$. This is the equal-size limit of [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md).
