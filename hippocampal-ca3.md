# Hippocampal CA3

↑ **Parent:** [Hippocampus](hippocampus.md)

A [hippocampal](hippocampus.md) subfield with recurrent collateral connectivity, motivating [autoassociative memory](autoassociative-memory.md) accounts of [pattern completion](pattern-completion.md). Its computations also depend on the input conditions, inhibition and [synaptic plasticity](synaptic-plasticity.md); it is not exclusively a retrieval circuit.
