# Meisenheimer complex

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophilic aromatic substitution](nucleophilic-aromatic-substitution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meisenheimer_complex)

A [Meisenheimer complex](meisenheimer-complex.md) is a sigma-bonded anionic addition species associated with suitable [nucleophilic aromatic substitution](nucleophilic-aromatic-substitution.md) reactions. Delocalization into ortho or para nitro groups can stabilize its negative charge.
