# Unique readability theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Syntax tree of a formula](syntax-tree-of-a-formula.md)

The [unique readability theorem](unique-readability-theorem.md) asserts uniqueness of the outer [formation rule](formal-grammar.md) and its immediate components in a [logical formula](logical-formula.md). For the fully parenthesized language with [negation](negation.md) and [implication](logical-implication.md), every [formula](logical-formula.md) is uniquely a [variable](logical-variable.md), a [negation](negation.md) of one [formula](logical-formula.md), or an [implication](logical-implication.md) between an [ordered pair](ordered-pair.md) of [formulas](logical-formula.md). It gives a well-defined [syntax tree](syntax-tree-of-a-formula.md) and licenses recursive definitions and [structural induction](structural-induction.md) on [formulas](logical-formula.md).
