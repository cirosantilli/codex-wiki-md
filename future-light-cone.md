# Future light cone

↑ **Parent:** [Light cone](light-cone.md)

The [future light cone](future-light-cone.md) is the future-directed part of the [light cone](light-cone.md) of a [spacetime event](event-relativity.md). Its boundary consists of the events reached by outgoing light rays. Events reached by slower causal signals lie inside the boundary; [relativistic causality](relativistic-causality.md) forbids a controllable signal from reaching the spacelike exterior.
