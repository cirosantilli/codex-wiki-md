# Graph property

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

A [graph](graph-split.md) property is a class of finite [graphs](graph-split.md) closed under isomorphism. Its labelled order-$n$ slice consists of members on [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) set $[n]$. Closure under taking arbitrary subgraphs and closure under taking only induced subgraphs lead respectively to [subgraph-closed graph properties](subgraph-closed-graph-property.md) and [hereditary graph properties](hereditary-graph-property.md).
