# Hypergeometric connection formula at one

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss hypergeometric equation](gauss-hypergeometric-equation.md)

Put $d=c-a-b\notin\mathbb Z$ and choose a branch of $(1-z)^d$. The solution regular at zero is

$$
{}_2F_1(a,b;c;z)=\frac{\Gamma(c)\Gamma(d)}{\Gamma(c-a)\Gamma(c-b)}{}_2F_1(a,b;1-d;1-z)+\frac{\Gamma(c)\Gamma(-d)}{\Gamma(a)\Gamma(b)}(1-z)^d{}_2F_1(c-a,c-b;1+d;1-z).
$$

The two [characteristic exponents at a regular singular point](characteristic-exponent-at-a-regular-singular-point.md) are $0,d$. The [Euler integral for the hypergeometric function](euler-integral-for-the-hypergeometric-function.md) determines the first coefficient by a finite endpoint limit and the second by scaling the singular endpoint; [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) extends the parameter ranges.
