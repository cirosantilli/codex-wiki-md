# Canonical scroll of a trigonal curve

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonal curve](trigonal-curve.md)

The rank-two bundle of fibre spans is globally generated and splits as $\mathcal O(a_1)\oplus\mathcal O(a_2)$ with $a_1+a_2=g-2$. On its [rational normal scroll](rational-normal-scroll.md), $L|_C$ is the degree-three pencil and $M|_C=K_C$. The [adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md) then forces the displayed divisor class. Writing $a=a_2-a_1$ and $M=B+a_2L$ on the [Hirzebruch surface](hirzebruch-surface.md) gives $C\in|3B+(a+a_2+2)L|$.
