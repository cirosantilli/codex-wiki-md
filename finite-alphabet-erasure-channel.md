# Finite-alphabet erasure channel

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete memoryless channel](discrete-memoryless-channel.md)

A finite-alphabet erasure channel transmits the input symbol unchanged with probability $1-f$ and replaces it by a distinct erasure flag with probability $f$, independently of the symbol. Given the flag, the posterior remains the input distribution; otherwise the input is known. Thus its [mutual information](mutual-information.md) is $(1-f)H(X)$.
