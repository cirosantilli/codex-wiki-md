# Closed point

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

A point of a [topological space](topological-space.md) is closed when its singleton is a [closed set](closed-set.md). In a [T1 space](t1-space.md) every point is closed. The closed points of an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}A$ are its [maximal ideals](maximal-ideal.md); the closure of the point corresponding to $\mathfrak p$ is $V(\mathfrak p)$.
