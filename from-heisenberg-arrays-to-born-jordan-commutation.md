# From Heisenberg arrays to Born-Jordan commutation

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix mechanics](matrix-mechanics.md)

In 1925, [Werner Heisenberg](werner-heisenberg.md) proposed replacing classical orbital quantities with arrays of transition amplitudes and frequencies. His paper's opening demanded relations between quantities observable in principle; section 1 built products by summing over an intermediate state and noted their dependence on order. The formulas above express that construction in modern [linear operator](linear-operator.md) notation.[Heisenberg, 1925; introduction and section 1, equations (5), (8), (15), (20), (24)–(25); Doria translation](on-the-quantum-reinterpretation-of-kinematical-and-mechanical-relationships-by-werner-heisenberg.md)

Pumping and a heated bath prepared [Mercury](mercury-planet.md) vapor around a hot [platinum](platinum.md) wire, mesh and collector in the original [Franck-Hertz experiment](franck-hertz-experiment.md). A [galvanometer](galvanometer.md) read collector [electric current](electric-current.md) while accelerating [voltage](voltage.md) varied at fixed retarding [voltage](voltage.md). Retarding scans distinguished outgoing [electron](electron.md) energies.[1914, pp. 459–461, Figs. 1–2](uber-zusammenstosse-zwischen-elektronen-und-den-molekulen-des-quecksilberdampfes-und-die-ionisierungsspannung-desselben-by-james-franck-and-gustav-ludwig-hertz.md) The losses constrain excitation of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md); they do not determine every optical coupling. The [Franck-Hertz experiment](franck-hertz-experiment.md) explains the original mistaken [ionization](ionization.md) interpretation.

Such excitation and [spectral line](spectral-line.md) evidence motivated a mechanics of discrete [energy levels](energy-level.md) and transitions. Replacing one representation by another was a theoretical construction; the [Franck-Hertz experiment](franck-hertz-experiment.md) does not measure a [matrix product](matrix-product.md) or establish a unique representation of an [observable](observable.md).

Later in 1925, [Max Born](max-born.md) and [Pascual Jordan](pascual-jordan.md) developed the array calculus systematically. Their section 3 states $pq-qp=hI/(2\pi i)$, equivalently the modern [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md)

$$
[X,P]=i\hbar I.
$$

This relates [position](position.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) through an intrinsically noncommuting product.[Born and Jordan, Zur Quantenmechanik, 1925; section 3, equation (38), translation p. 14](zur-quantenmechanik-by-max-born-and-pascual-jordan.md)

For the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), the [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) and [creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) give $H=\hbar\omega(a^\dagger a+I/2)$, recovering both the spacing and [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) algebraically. One must retain infinitely many [number states](number-state.md) for the exact relation: the finite cutoff below reveals the obstruction explicitly.
