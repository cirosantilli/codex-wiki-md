# Alternative routing

↑ **Parent:** [Loss network](loss-network.md)

Alternative routing lets a call try another resource path when its preferred route is blocked. Overflow onto longer paths can create feedback and multiple solutions of the [Erlang fixed point approximation](erlang-fixed-point-approximation.md), even though an exact finite irreducible occupancy [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) has a unique [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md).
