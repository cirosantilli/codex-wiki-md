# Hamming space

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md)

The Hamming space of length $n$ over a [finite field](finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_q$ is the set $\mathbb F_q^n$ equipped with [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md), the number of coordinates at which two words differ. The [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md) of a word is its distance from zero.
