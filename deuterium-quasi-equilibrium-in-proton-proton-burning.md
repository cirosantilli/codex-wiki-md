# Deuterium quasi-equilibrium in proton-proton burning

↑ **Parent:** [Effective proton-proton reaction network](effective-proton-proton-reaction-network.md)

[deuterium](deuterium.md) [proton](proton.md) capture is much faster than the weak reaction that produces [deuterium](deuterium.md). Its abundance therefore adjusts so that $\lambda_{21}n_2n_1\simeq\lambda_{11}n_1^2/2$. Substituting this balance eliminates the fast intermediate from the slower [proton](proton.md) and [helium-3](helium-3.md) evolution equations. This is a [quasi-steady approximation](quasi-steady-approximation.md), not an assumption that [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) itself is in nuclear equilibrium.
