# Euler pentagonal identity

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi triple product](jacobi-triple-product.md)

The identity holds for $|q|<1$, with both sides locally uniformly convergent. In the [character theta proof of the eta product](character-theta-proof-of-the-eta-product.md), pair the terms indexed by $n$ and $-n$ and then select $n=6r+1$; their [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) signs are $(-1)^r$. Removing $q^{1/24}$ gives the displayed product identity.
