# Softening Rayleigh-Duffing phase portrait

↑ **Parent:** [Softening Rayleigh-Duffing oscillator](softening-rayleigh-duffing-oscillator.md)

With $a=-A>0$, a stable cycle grows from the origin. At $a_h\simeq0.33144619$, symmetric saddle connections form a repelling [heteroclinic cycle](heteroclinic-cycle.md), while the stable inner cycle persists. Just above it an unstable outer cycle coexists with the stable one; they merge at $a_f\simeq0.33147066$ in a [saddle-node bifurcation of periodic orbits](saddle-node-bifurcation-of-periodic-orbits.md). These approximate values follow from the half-return equations $H_a(p)=p$, with $H_a'(p)=1$ at the fold, and saddle-manifold shooting at the connection. A coarse parameter scan can miss the narrow coexistence interval. The saddle [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) ratio is below one at the connection, proving that the loop is repelling and preventing identification of the stable cycle's disappearance with that connection.
