# Shallow-water specific energy

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

For a steady rectangular-channel flow with discharge $Q=bhu$, the [shallow-water specific energy](shallow-water-specific-energy.md) relative to the bed is $E=h+Q^2/(2gb^2h^2)$. At fixed discharge and width, $E_h=1-F^2$, where the [Froude number](froude-number.md) satisfies $F^2=Q^2/(gb^2h^3)$. The minimum is $E_c=3h_c/2$, with $h_c=(Q^2/(gb^2))^{1/3}$. Without dissipation, $E+H$ is the conserved [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) head for bed elevation $H$.
