# Neutrino mass ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Mass-squared difference](mass-squared-difference.md)

The normal ordering has the third light [neutrino](neutrino.md) above the solar pair in [mass](mass.md); the inverted ordering has it below that pair. [Oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md) experiments determine differences of squared [masses](mass.md), not the common absolute [mass](mass.md) scale. A pure vacuum electron-survival expression uses squared sines and does not by itself fix the signs of individual [phase](phase-waves.md) differences.
