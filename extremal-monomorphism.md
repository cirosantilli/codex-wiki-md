# Extremal monomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Monomorphism](monomorphism.md)

A [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) $m$ is extremal if every factorization $m=he$ with $e$ an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md) forces $e$ to be an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). A [strong monomorphism](strong-monomorphism.md) has this property by its lifting property. In a [complete category](complete-category.md) that is a [well-powered category](well-powered-category.md), intersecting all strong [subobjects](subobject.md) containing a given morphism yields an epimorphism–strong-monomorphism factorization; applying it to an extremal monomorphism proves that the extremal monomorphism is strong. The factorization definition and lifting definition should be kept distinct until the needed ambient hypotheses are supplied.
