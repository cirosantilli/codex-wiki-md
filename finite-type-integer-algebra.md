# Finite-type integer algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative algebra](commutative-algebra-split.md)

A unital commutative [ring](ring.md) generated by finitely many elements under addition and multiplication, starting from the image of the integers. Equivalently it is a quotient of a finite-variable [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) over $\mathbb Z$. [localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md) at one element remains finite type, since $R[1/r]\cong R[T]/(rT-1)$. Generation here is as an algebra, not merely as a [field extension](field-extension.md) when the [ring](ring.md) happens to be a [field](field.md).
