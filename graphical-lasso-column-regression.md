# Graphical Lasso column regression

↑ **Parent:** [Graphical Lasso](graphical-lasso.md)

For a [Graphical Lasso](graphical-lasso.md) solution with inverse $\widehat\Sigma$, the unique minimizer of $\tfrac12\|Wb-W^{-1}S_{-j,j}\|_2^2+\lambda\|b\|_1$, $W^2=\widehat\Sigma_{-j,-j}\succ0$, is $b=-\widehat\Omega_{-j,j}/\widehat\Omega_{jj}$. The inverse identity gives $\widehat\Sigma_{-j,j}=\widehat\Sigma_{-j,-j}b$, and the off-diagonal [Graphical-Lasso Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](graphical-lasso-karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) become the column [Lasso](lasso.md) conditions because the corresponding signs are opposite.
