# A-DNA

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

[A-DNA](a-dna.md) is a right-handed duplex family with predominantly C3′-endo [sugar puckering](sugar-puckering.md), tilted and displaced base pairs, and a deep narrow [major groove](major-groove.md). Double-stranded RNA and many [DNA-RNA hybrids](dna-rna-hybrid.md) have related A-form geometry.
