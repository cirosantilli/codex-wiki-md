# Two-dimensional hydrogenic donor

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogenic donor state](hydrogenic-donor-state.md)

An electron confined to a plane in the screened [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) $-\kappa/r$, $\kappa=e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0\epsilon_r)$, has the radial ground state $\psi=Ae^{-br}$. The planar radial Laplacian is $(b^2-b/r)\psi$. Cancelling the $1/r$ term in the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) gives $b=2m^*\kappa/\hbar^2$ and $E=-\hbar^2b^2/(2m^*)$. The binding magnitude is four times the three-dimensional effective Rydberg. Normalization in the plane gives $A=b\sqrt{2/\pi}$. The ordinary three-dimensional electric field is retained; a logarithmic two-dimensional interaction is a different problem.
