# Torque-controlled homopolar dynamo equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Homopolar disc dynamo](homopolar-disc-dynamo.md)

For steady rotation at nonzero current, the electromagnetic braking [torque](torque.md) is $CI^2$ and balances applied [torque](torque.md) $G$. Thus $I^2=G/C$, while the circuit requires $\omega=R/C$. With $H=NI/l$, these give the displayed field and $G\omega=I^2R$. This is a steady [energy](energy-split.md) balance, not a general nonlinear stability claim.
