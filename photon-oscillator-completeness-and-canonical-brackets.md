# Photon oscillator completeness and canonical brackets

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant photon Fock space](covariant-photon-fock-space.md)

With real complete [polarization vectors](polarization-vector.md), the [covariant photon Fock space](covariant-photon-fock-space.md) oscillator [commutators](commutator.md) and $\sum_{\lambda,\lambda'}\epsilon_\mu^\lambda\epsilon^{\nu\lambda'}\eta_{\lambda\lambda'}=\delta_\mu{}^\nu$ imply the equal-time [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) $[A_\mu(\mathbf x),-\dot A^\nu(\mathbf y)]=i\delta_\mu{}^\nu\delta^3(\mathbf x-\mathbf y)$. The two mixed creation-annihilation terms add; the two coordinate-coordinate or momentum-momentum terms cancel after their polarization sums. The sign of the time-component oscillator is essential to the identity. [Boundary-induced canonical transformation in Feynman gauge](boundary-induced-canonical-transformation-in-feynman-gauge.md) explains the momenta for the unsubtracted density.
