# Collisionless Jeans dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Collisionless Boltzmann equation](collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md)

Linearize the [Collisionless Boltzmann equation](collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md) and the perturbation Poisson equation about a homogeneous [velocity](velocity.md) distribution $f_0$. A [plane wave](plane-wave.md) gives $f_1=\phi_1\mathbf k\cdot\nabla_vf_0/(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf v-\omega)$. Integrating this density and using $-k^2\phi_1=4\pi G\int f_1d^3v$ yields $1+(4\pi G/k^2)\int[\mathbf k\cdot\nabla_vf_0/(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf v-\omega)]d^3v=0$. Growing modes have $\operatorname{Im}\omega>0$ and no real-velocity pole; real and damped frequencies require the causal continuation prescription. The Newtonian homogeneous background requires the [Jeans swindle](jeans-swindle.md).
