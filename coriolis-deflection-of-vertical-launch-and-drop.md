# Coriolis deflection of vertical launch and drop

↑ **Parent:** [Coriolis acceleration in local Earth coordinates](coriolis-acceleration-in-local-earth-coordinates.md)

At first order in [Earth](earth.md)'s rotation, vertical [velocity](velocity.md) gives east [acceleration](acceleration.md) $-2\Omega\cos\lambda\,\dot z$. A launch returning to its initial height lands westward, while a drop from the launch's maximum height lands eastward with one quarter the displacement magnitude. These results assume constant effective gravity, no air [drag](drag-physics.md) and no horizontal [velocity](velocity.md) in the Coriolis term. The northern component vanishes at this order.
