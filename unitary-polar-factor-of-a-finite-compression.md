# Unitary polar factor of a finite compression

↑ **Parent:** [Polar decomposition of a bounded operator](polar-decomposition-of-a-bounded-operator.md)

If $T_n=P_nAP_n^*$ is a square finite compression with [singular value decomposition](singular-value-decomposition.md) $T_n=V_n\Sigma_nW_n^*$, then $U_n=V_nW_n^*$ is a unitary extension of its polar factor. When $A$ is unitary and $P_n^*P_n\to I$ strongly, $|T_n|\to I$ strongly and hence $U_nP_n\to A$ strongly.
