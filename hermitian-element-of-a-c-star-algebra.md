# Hermitian element of a C-star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md)

An element $x$ of a [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) is Hermitian when $x=x^*$. Its [spectrum](spectrum-of-an-element.md) is real, its norm equals its spectral radius, and it is the difference of two [positive elements](positive-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md).
