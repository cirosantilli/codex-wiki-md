# Razor-thin disk Poisson kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Razor-thin disk approximation](razor-thin-disk-approximation.md)

For a horizontal Fourier mode of [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k>0$, the midplane gravitational potential of a razor-thin disk is

$$
\widehat\Phi_m(\mathbf k)=-\frac{2\pi G}{k}\widehat\Sigma(\mathbf k).
$$
