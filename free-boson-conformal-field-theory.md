# Free boson conformal field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional conformal field theory](two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md)

The free boson conformal field theory has action quadratic in a scalar $X$ and central charge one. Its oscillator partition function is expressed through the [Dedekind eta function](dedekind-eta-function.md).
