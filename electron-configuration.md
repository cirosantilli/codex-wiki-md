# Electron configuration

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chemistry](quantum-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron_configuration)

An [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md) records the occupation of [electron shells](electron-shell.md) and [electron subshells](electron-subshell.md) by [Electrons](electron.md). For neutral [sodium](sodium.md) in its [ground state](ground-state.md), $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^1$ gives $2+2+6+1=11$ [Electrons](electron.md); adding the occupations within each [electron shell](electron-shell.md) gives $(2,8,1)$. A superscript counts [Electrons](electron.md), rather than multiplying the preceding labels. Removing the outer [Electron](electron.md) gives the [sodium](sodium.md) [ion](ion.md) $\mathrm{Na}^{+}$ with $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6$, or $(2,8)$. See [Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, electron configurations of atoms and ions](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/6-4-electronic-structure-of-atoms-electron-configurations).
