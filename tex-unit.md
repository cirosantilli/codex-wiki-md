# Tex unit

↑ **Parent:** [Textile linear density](textile-linear-density.md)

A unit of [mass](mass.md) per length, equal to $1\,\mathrm{g/km}$. [Force](force-split.md) divided by a linear [density](density.md) stated in tex gives tensile tenacity in newtons per tex.
