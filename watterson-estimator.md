# Watterson estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Segregating site](segregating-site.md)

For a neutral sample of size $n\ge2$ under the [infinite sites mutation model](infinite-sites-mutation-model.md), the Watterson estimator is $\widehat\theta_W=S/a_{n-1}$, where $a_{n-1}=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}1/i$. Since the coalescent has $ES=\theta a_{n-1}$, this estimator is unbiased. It matches the observed segregating-site count to its expected value, but need not maximize the exact [segregating-site likelihood under the neutral coalescent](segregating-site-likelihood-under-the-neutral-coalescent.md).
