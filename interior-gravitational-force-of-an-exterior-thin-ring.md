# Interior gravitational force of an exterior thin ring

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md)

A thin ring of mass $m$ and radius $a$ has interior relative potential $Gm[1+R^2/(4a^2)+\cdots]/a$. Its leading radial acceleration is outward, $GmR/(2a^3)$, unlike the zero interior force of a spherical shell. Exterior disc annuli therefore affect a [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md).
