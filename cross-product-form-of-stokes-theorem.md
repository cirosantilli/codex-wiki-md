# Cross-product form of Stokes theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md)

For a continuously differentiable [vector field](vector-field.md) on an oriented surface with compatibly oriented boundary, this vector identity follows by dotting both sides with an arbitrary constant vector $a$ and applying [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) to $\mathbf F\times a$. The [curl](curl.md) identity $\nabla\times(a\times\mathbf F)=a\,\nabla\cdot\mathbf F-(a\cdot\nabla)\mathbf F$ gives the surface integrand. In components, $[(d\mathbf S\times\nabla)\times\mathbf F]_i=dS_j\partial_iF_j-dS_i\partial_jF_j$, with the derivatives acting on $\mathbf F$. For the [position vector](position-vector.md) field $\mathbf F=\mathbf r$, it equals $-2d\mathbf S$; hence the line integral is minus twice the oriented vector area.
