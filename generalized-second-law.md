# Generalized second law

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole thermodynamics](black-hole-thermodynamics.md)

The generalized second law states that the sum of [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md) and the entropy of matter and radiation outside black holes does not decrease.
