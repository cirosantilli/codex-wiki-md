# Nonorthogonal vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Inner product](inner-product.md)

Two [vectors](vector.md) are nonorthogonal when their [inner product](inner-product.md) is nonzero. In the [power method](power-method.md), the starting vector must be nonorthogonal to a dominant [eigenvector](eigenvector.md).
