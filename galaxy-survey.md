# Galaxy survey

↑ **Parent:** [Large-scale structure of the universe](large-scale-structure-of-the-universe-split.md)

A galaxy survey measures positions and other properties of a selected galaxy sample. A spectroscopic survey uses [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) to estimate radial distance, with corrections for [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md). Its volume, selection, [galaxy bias](galaxy-bias.md), and sampling determine which [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md) modes can be inferred.
