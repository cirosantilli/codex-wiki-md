# Laplace approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace's method](laplace-s-method.md)

At a unique interior maximum with positive-definite negative [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md), a quadratic expansion and rescaling by $\sqrt n$ give the displayed Gaussian integral, provided the tails are negligible. In a regular [Bayesian inference](bayesian-statistics.md) it approximates the integrated [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) by the [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) at its dominant point times the prior density and inverse square root of local curvature. Boundary modes, singular Hessians, and multiple equally dominant modes need modified formulas.
