# CP violation in neutral-meson mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral meson mixing](neutral-meson-mixing.md)

For [mass eigenstates](mass-eigenstate.md) $p|M\rangle\pm q|\bar M\rangle$, unequal forward and reverse [flavour](flavor-eigenstate.md) transition probabilities occur when $|q/p|\ne1$. They are proportional to $|q/p|^2|g_-|^2$ and $|p/q|^2|g_-|^2$. This diagnostic is invariant under rephasing the two [flavour eigenstates](flavor-eigenstate.md).
