# Projective bundle formula for complex vector bundles

↑ **Parent:** [Projective bundle](projective-bundle.md)

For a rank-$r$ complex [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) and $u=c_1(\lambda^*)$, the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of its [projective bundle](projective-bundle.md) is free over the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of the base on $1,u,\ldots,u^{r-1}$. Consequently pullback is injective, which yields the [splitting principle for complex vector bundles](splitting-principle-for-complex-vector-bundles.md) by iteration.
