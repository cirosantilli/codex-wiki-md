# Fractional flow

↑ **Parent:** [Two-phase porous-media flow](two-phase-porous-media-flow.md)

The fraction of total advective [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) carried by one phase when capillary and gravity corrections are omitted. For two phases it is $\lambda_w/(\lambda_w+\lambda_n)$. This quantity lies between zero and one for nonnegative [phase mobilities](phase-mobility.md). Its nonlinear dependence on [fluid saturation](fluid-saturation.md) determines [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) and shocks in the [Buckley-Leverett equation](buckley-leverett-equation.md).
