# Vinogradov mean value

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential sum](exponential-sum.md)

By [orthogonality of integer Fourier modes](orthogonality-of-integer-fourier-modes.md), this integral counts pairs of $k$-tuples with the same first $r$ power sums. Diagonal pairs give $J_{k,r}(Z)\gg Z^k$. There are $O_{k,r}(Z^{r(r+1)/2})$ possible moment vectors; the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $J_{k,r}(Z)\gg Z^{2k-r(r+1)/2}$. Upper bounds measure how much arithmetic coincidence remains beyond these necessary contributions, and enter the [Vinogradov mean-value method for a bilinear exponential sum](vinogradov-mean-value-method-for-a-bilinear-exponential-sum.md).
