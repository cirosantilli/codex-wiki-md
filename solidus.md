# Solidus

↑ **Parent:** [Phase diagram](phase-diagram.md)

The solidus bounds the entirely solid equilibrium region of a [phase diagram](phase-diagram.md). For a linear [liquidus](liquidus.md) $T_L(C_l)=-mC_l$ and a constant [segregation coefficient](solid-liquid-segregation-coefficient.md) $C_s=k_D C_l$, the corresponding solidus is $T_S(C_s)=-mC_s/k_D$. The [concentration](concentration.md) argument is the solid [concentration](concentration.md), not the neighboring liquid [concentration](concentration.md).
