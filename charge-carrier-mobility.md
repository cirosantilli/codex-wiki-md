# Charge carrier mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Charge carrier](charge-carrier.md)

[Charge carrier mobility](charge-carrier-mobility.md) is the magnitude of steady [drift velocity](drift-velocity.md) per unit weak [electric field](electric-field-split.md) in a scalar transport model. In the [Drude model](drude-model.md), $\mathbf v_d=\operatorname{sgn}(q)\mu\mathbf E$ and $\sigma=n|q|\mu$. The positive [charge carrier mobility](charge-carrier-mobility.md) separates ease of motion from the sign of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md); its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is $\mathrm{m^2/(V\,s)}$.
