# Linear canonical transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Integral transform](integral-transform-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_canonical_transformation)

A family of [integral transforms](integral-transform-split.md) associated with a two-by-two real [matrix](matrix.md) of determinant one. For $B\ne0$, one optical normalization has kernel proportional to $\exp[ik(Ax^2+Dx'^2-2xx')/(2B)]$. It includes free-space [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md), quadratic phase multiplication and the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md). The optical amplitude normalization in one transverse dimension is $1/\sqrt{i\lambda B}$ with a consistent phase branch.
