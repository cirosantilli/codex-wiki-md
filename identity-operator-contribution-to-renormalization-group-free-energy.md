# Identity-operator contribution to renormalization-group free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized blocking kernel](normalized-blocking-kernel.md)

If an energy per site $C$ multiplies the identity operator, blocking sends $C$ to $b^DC+c(u)$. For [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per site $F=C+f(u)$, exact [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) invariance gives the displayed inhomogeneous equation with $g=b^{-D}c$. It records eliminated-mode [entropy](entropy.md), vacuum contributions and measure normalization. Removing a suitable regular background leaves homogeneous singular scaling; additive logarithmic resonances can obstruct a strictly analytic subtraction.
