# Electrostatic reciprocity identity

↑ **Parent:** [Electric potential](electric-potential.md)

For two [electrostatic potentials](electric-potential.md) satisfying the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) $\Delta\phi_i=-\rho_i/\varepsilon_0$, [Green's second identity](green-second-identity.md) yields the displayed reciprocity formula. The [product rule for divergence](product-rule-for-divergence.md) applied to $\phi_1\nabla\phi_2-\phi_2\nabla\phi_1$ cancels the cross-gradient terms. The normal points outward from the integration region; for an exterior region this means inward toward the excluded object on its inner boundary. Boundary terms may be removed only under suitable decay or boundary conditions.
