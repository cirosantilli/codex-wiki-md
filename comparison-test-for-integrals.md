# Comparison test for integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Integral](integral-split.md)

If [measurable functions](measurable-function.md) satisfy $|f|\leq g$ almost everywhere and $g$ is nonnegative and [Lebesgue integrable](lebesgue-integrable-function.md), then $f$ is [Lebesgue integrable](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) and $\int|f|\leq\int g$. This follows from [monotonicity of the Lebesgue integral](monotonicity-of-the-lebesgue-integral.md). Conversely, $0\leq g\leq|f|$ with $\int g=\infty$ rules out [Lebesgue integrability](integrability.md) of $f$. For nonnegative [improper integrals](improper-integral.md), the same comparison applies after taking increasing finite-interval limits.
