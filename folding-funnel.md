# Folding funnel

↑ **Parent:** [Protein folding](protein-folding.md)

A folding funnel is a schematic [energy landscape](energy-landscape.md) in which increasingly native-like [protein](protein-split.md) conformations tend to have lower [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md), while the many unfolded conformations occupy a broad region. Roughness represents barriers and possible kinetic traps. The funnel is an organizing description, not a claim that every protein follows one unique geometrical trajectory or possesses a rigid isolated native state.
