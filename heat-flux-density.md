# Heat flux density

↑ **Parent:** [Heat](heat.md)

Heat flux density is the rate of [heat](heat.md) transfer through unit area. Its SI unit is watts per square metre, and [Fourier's law](fourier-s-law.md) gives its conductive contribution.
