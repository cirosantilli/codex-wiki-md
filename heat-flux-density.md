# Heat flux density

↑ **Parent:** [Heat](heat.md)

[Heat flux density](heat-flux-density.md) is a [vector](vector.md) $\mathbf q$ describing the rate of [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) per unit [area](surface-area.md), measured in $\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$. Through a planar face of [area](surface-area.md) $A$ with uniform normal component $q$, the signed rate is $\dot Q=Aq$ in $\mathrm W$; positive $q$ means [heat](heat.md) travels in the chosen positive normal direction. [Fourier's law](fourier-s-law.md) determines the conductive contribution. In a bar with a constant cross-sectional [area](surface-area.md), steady [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) without internal sources or side losses makes both $q$ and $\dot Q$ constant along the bar. Constant $\dot Q$ need not mean constant $q$ when the cross-sectional [area](surface-area.md) varies.
