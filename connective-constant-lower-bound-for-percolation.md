# Connective-constant lower bound for percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation critical probability](percolation-critical-probability.md)

For independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on a transitive locally finite lattice, let $c_n$ count rooted [self-avoiding walks](self-avoiding-walk.md) and let $\mu=\lim c_n^{1/n}$ be the [connective constant](connective-constant.md). An infinite open [percolation cluster](percolation-cluster.md) supplies an open walk of every length. The [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) gives $\theta(p)\leq c_np^n$, which tends to zero when $p\mu<1$. Thus $p_c\geq1/\mu$.
