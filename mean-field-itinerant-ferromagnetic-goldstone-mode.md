# Mean-field itinerant ferromagnetic Goldstone mode

↑ **Parent:** [Itinerant ferromagnetism](itinerant-ferromagnetism.md)

An isotropic spin-polarized metal has a degenerate magnetization direction and a long-wavelength transverse magnon with quadratic dispersion. Nonzero spin density pairs the two transverse broken generators into one [type-B Goldstone boson](type-b-goldstone-boson.md). Exchange-split [Fermi surfaces](fermi-surface.md) also support fermionic low-energy excitations; the magnon is distinct from longitudinal amplitude motion and the spin-flip continuum.
