# Scattering charge selection rule from global gauge symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Global symmetry in field theory](global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md)

For a conserved charge $Q$ and invariant vacuum, its global symmetry commutes with the [S-matrix](s-matrix.md). Matrix elements between charge eigenstates give the displayed selection rule. Constant electromagnetic phases cancel at every QED vertex, and nonzero physical amplitudes conserve total charge. Local [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) strengthens this global rule to the [Ward-Takahashi identity](ward-identity.md).
