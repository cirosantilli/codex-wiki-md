# Sine infinite product

↑ **Parent:** [Sine](sine.md)

The paired zero factors converge locally uniformly because $\sum n^{-2}$ converges. [Hadamard factorization](hadamard-factorization-theorem.md) for the order-one entire sine function gives a possible exponential multiplier; oddness removes its linear exponent and the derivative at zero fixes its constant. This product turns a [Dirichlet oscillator determinant ratio](dirichlet-oscillator-determinant-ratio.md) into an elementary sine ratio.
