# Ghost number

↑ **Parent:** [Faddeev-Popov ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md)

The additive grading assigns ghost number $+1$ to $c$ and $-1$ to $\bar c$. Physical observables have ghost number zero. This grading is distinct from spin or the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
