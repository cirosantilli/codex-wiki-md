# Convective wave-packet instability

↑ **Parent:** [Spatiotemporal wave-packet stability](spatiotemporal-wave-packet-stability.md)

A localized disturbance grows along some moving rays but decays at its original fixed location. This wave-packet meaning differs from buoyancy-driven stellar convection and is therefore given a distinct canonical title. Changing the observation frame can change the absolute/convective classification. The [Briggs-Bers criterion](briggs-bers-criterion.md) uses the laboratory-frame impulse response to distinguish this case from [absolute wave-packet instability](absolute-wave-packet-instability.md).
