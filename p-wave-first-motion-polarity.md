# P-wave first-motion polarity

↑ **Parent:** [Focal mechanism](focal-mechanism.md)

The sign of the initial direct [P wave](p-wave.md) motion after instrument, component and ray-direction conventions are calibrated. It constrains the sign of $\hat r^{\mathsf T}M\hat r$ at the source takeoff direction. Near-nodal arrivals and uncertain takeoff angles need special treatment.
