# Circular polarizer

↑ **Parent:** [Polarizer](polarizer.md)

A reciprocal optical [circular polarizer](circular-polarizer.md) can be assembled from a linear [polarizer](polarizer.md) followed by a [quarter-wave plate](quarter-wave-plate.md) whose axes make $45^\circ$ to the transmitted linear state. Forward passage converts [unpolarized light](unpolarized-light.md) into circular [light](light.md) of half the ideal incident [intensity](intensity-physics.md). Reverse passage encounters the retarder first; it leaves [unpolarized light](unpolarized-light.md) unpolarized until the linear [polarizer](polarizer.md) transmits half as linear [light](light.md). Thus the one-pass [intensity](intensity-physics.md) can be equal in both directions even though the outgoing polarization differs. Use a [coherency matrix](coherency-matrix.md), not a single [Jones vector](jones-vector.md), for unpolarized input.
