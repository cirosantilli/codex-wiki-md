# Killed geometric Brownian heat kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric Brownian motion](geometric-brownian-motion.md)

The [differential operator](differential-operator.md) $L=\tfrac12x^2\partial_{xx}+\tfrac12x\partial_x-\tfrac12$ has solution operator $P_tg(x)=e^{-t/2}\mathbb E[g(xe^{B_t})]$. For $x\ne0$ its [integral kernel](integral-kernel.md) with respect to [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) is $p(t,x,y)=e^{-t/2}(|y|\sqrt{2\pi t})^{-1}\exp[-\log^2(|y/x|)/(2t)]$ when $xy>0$, and zero otherwise. For $x=0$ the kernel is the [measure](measure.md) $e^{-t/2}\delta_0$, not a density. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) applied to $e^{-s/2}u(t-s,X_s)$ proves this [Feynman-Kac formula](feynman-kac-formula.md).
