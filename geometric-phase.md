# Geometric phase

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum phase](quantum-phase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_phase)

A [geometric phase](geometric-phase.md) is a [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) associated with the geometry of a path of state rays, after separating the [dynamical phase](dynamical-phase.md). The [Berry phase](berry-phase.md) is its cyclic case governed by the [adiabatic theorem](adiabatic-theorem.md); more general cyclic evolution can also have a [geometric phase](geometric-phase.md).
