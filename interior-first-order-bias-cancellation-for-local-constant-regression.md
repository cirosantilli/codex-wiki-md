# Interior first-order bias cancellation for local constant regression

↑ **Parent:** [Nadaraya–Watson estimator](nadaraya-watson-estimator.md)

At an interior point where the [regression function](regression-function.md) is differentiable, a symmetric compactly supported [regression kernel](kernel-for-nonparametric-regression.md) makes the first-order [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md) vanish asymptotically. In regular [fixed-design nonparametric regression](fixed-design-nonparametric-regression.md) with $h\to0$ and $nh\to\infty$, the [local constant estimator](nadaraya-watson-estimator.md) then has squared [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md) $o(h^2)$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $R(K)/(nh)+o((nh)^{-1})$ for a unit-integral [regression kernel](kernel-for-nonparametric-regression.md). The [differentiability](differentiability.md) remainder is pointwise and need not hold uniformly over a function class.
