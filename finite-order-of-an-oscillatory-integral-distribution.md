# Finite order of an oscillatory integral distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillatory integral](oscillatory-integral.md)

An [oscillatory integral](oscillatory-integral.md) with a degree-one homogeneous [phase function](phase-function.md), $k$ frequency variables, and [oscillatory integral amplitude](amplitude-of-an-oscillatory-integral.md) in fixed [symbol class](symbol-class.md) order $N$ has [order of a distribution](order-of-a-distribution.md) at most any nonnegative integer $r>N+k$. The integer works on every [compact set](compact-space.md); the continuity constant can vary with the [compact set](compact-space.md).
