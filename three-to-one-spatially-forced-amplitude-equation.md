# Three-to-one spatially forced amplitude equation

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md)

A critical pattern [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) transforms under a spatial translation as $A\mapsto e^{i\phi}A$. A forcing at three times the critical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) transforms as $F\mapsto e^{3i\phi}F$. Their lowest resonant product with phase weight one is $F\overline A^{\,2}$. This determines the form of the leading weak-forcing [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md), while projection onto an [adjoint eigenfunction](adjoint-eigenfunction.md) determines its coefficient. Symmetry permits this coupling but does not ensure that it is nonzero. Travelling forcing gives a time-dependent phase to $F$.
