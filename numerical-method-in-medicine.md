# Numerical method in medicine

↑ **Parent:** [History of medicine](history-of-medicine.md)

Use of systematically recorded groups of cases and numerical comparisons to evaluate [treatment](treatment.md), associated especially with [Pierre Charles Alexandre Louis](pierre-charles-alexandre-louis.md). Such comparisons need not be [randomized controlled trials](randomized-controlled-trial.md) and remain vulnerable to [confounding](confounding.md). [1835, pp. 70–76](research-on-the-effects-of-bloodletting-by-pierre-charles-alexandre-louis.md)
