# Kashiwara operator

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal basis](crystal-basis.md)

On a crystal, $\widetilde f_i$ follows an edge of color $i$ and $\widetilde e_i$ follows that edge backwards.

These maps are the raising and lowering operations of a [crystal basis](crystal-basis.md).
