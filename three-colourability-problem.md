# Three-colourability problem

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring.md)

This [decision problem](decision-problem.md) asks whether a finite [undirected graph](undirected-graph.md) has a [graph colouring](graph-coloring.md) with three colours. It is [NP-complete](np-completeness.md): a colouring certifies membership in [NP](np-complexity.md), while [3-SAT](3-sat.md) reduces using [Boolean-pair colouring gadgets](boolean-pair-colouring-gadget.md) and [three-colour clause gadgets](three-colour-clause-gadget.md).
