<h1 id="artin-schreier-extension">Artin–Schreier extension</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Artin–Schreier theory](artin-schreier-theory.md)

An Artin–Schreier extension is a field extension generated by a root $\alpha$ of an irreducible [Artin–Schreier polynomial](artin-schreier-polynomial.md). It is a cyclic [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of degree $p$, with automorphisms $\alpha\mapsto\alpha+c$ for $c\in\mathbb F_p$.
