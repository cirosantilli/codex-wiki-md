# Strain-dominated mush temperature profile

↑ **Parent:** [Strain-driven steady mush](strain-driven-steady-mush.md)

Set $\delta=\sqrt{2\kappa/\alpha}$, $R=\alpha h/V$ and $S=L/[c_p(T_0-T_E)]$. In the regular limit $R\gg S\gg1$, at fixed finite thermal strain number $\alpha h^2/\kappa$ and bounded phase fraction, the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) reduces to $\kappa T''+\alpha zT'=0$. Integration gives the displayed [error function](error-function.md) profile. Matching the roof [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md) $G$ yields

$$
G=\frac{2(T_0-T_E)}{\sqrt{\pi}\delta}\frac{e^{-(h/\delta)^2}}{\operatorname{erf}(h/\delta)}.
$$

The product composition is $C_s=C_0-\alpha(T_0-T_E)\delta[1-e^{-(h/\delta)^2}]/[mV\sqrt\pi\operatorname{erf}(h/\delta)]$. The asymptotic reduction requires admissible phase fractions. If the thermal strain number grows singularly with $R$, the two ordering inequalities alone do not control boundary-layer errors; the full [strain-driven steady mush](strain-driven-steady-mush.md) equations retain the latent-heat term.
