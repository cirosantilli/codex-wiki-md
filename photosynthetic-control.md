# Photosynthetic control

↑ **Parent:** [Photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md)

[Photosynthetic control](photosynthetic-control.md) refers to regulation of electron flow, especially lumen-pH-dependent restriction at the cytochrome b6f complex. Slowing electron delivery can protect downstream acceptors from excessive reduction.
