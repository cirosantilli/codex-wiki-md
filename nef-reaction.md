# Nef reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Conversion of a primary or secondary nitro-derived [nitronate](nitronate.md) into a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) compound by suitable hydrolytic conditions. The nitro-bearing [carbon](carbon.md) becomes an aldehyde [carbon](carbon.md) for a primary nitroalkane or a [ketone](ketone.md) [carbon](carbon.md) for a secondary one.
