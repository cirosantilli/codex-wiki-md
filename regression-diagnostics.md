# Regression diagnostics

↑ **Parent:** [Linear regression](linear-regression-split.md)

Regression diagnostics check assumptions and influential observations in a fitted [regression function](regression-function.md). [Residual-versus-fitted plots](residual-versus-fitted-plot.md) can reveal omitted mean structure or nonconstant [variance](variance-split.md); a [quantile-quantile plot](q-q-plot.md) checks a specified error distribution. [Regression leverage](regression-leverage.md) measures unusual predictor configurations, and [Cook's distance](cook-s-distance.md) combines leverage and residual size to measure coefficient sensitivity. Independence may require checking collection order and the study design in addition to residual plots.
