# Henri Becquerel

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Becquerel)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md) whose [uranium](uranium.md) experiments helped establish [radioactivity](radioactive-decay.md). His 1896 [photographic plate](photographic-plate.md) tests separated exposure to the material from immediate illumination; the cautious interpretation in his report is part of the story of [Becquerel's uranium-radiation experiment](becquerel-s-uranium-radiation-experiment.md). He held the chair of applied [physics](physics-split.md) at the Paris Museum of Natural History.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1901–1921; Biographical identity and professional specialization](nobel-lectures-physics-1901-1921/henri-becquerel-biographical.md)
