# Cobordism Morse function

↑ **Parent:** [Morse function](morse-function.md)

The incoming and outgoing boundaries are the regular levels zero and one, and all [critical points](critical-point.md) lie in the interior. Near the boundary use a collar coordinate as the function. The [Morse handle-attachment theorem](morse-handle-attachment-theorem.md) produces a relative chain group freely generated by the interior [critical points](critical-point.md). Thus $\chi(W,M_0)$ is the alternating sum of their [Morse indices](morse-index.md), regardless of how their [critical values](critical-value.md) are ordered.
