# Diisopropylethylamine

↑ **Parent:** [Amine](amine.md)

[Diisopropylethylamine](diisopropylethylamine.md) is a sterically hindered tertiary [amine](amine.md) base. It can remove [protons](proton.md) and trap acid while competing relatively weakly as a [nucleophile](nucleophile.md).
