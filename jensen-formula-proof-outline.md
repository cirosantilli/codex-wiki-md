# Jensen formula proof outline

↑ **Parent:** [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md)

Divide $f$ by its finitely many interior zero factors, with each factor repeated according to its [multiplicity of a zero](multiplicity-of-a-zero.md). The resulting [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $g$ has no [zeros of a function](zero-of-a-function.md) in the closed [disk](disk-mathematics.md), so $\log|g|$ is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) and its circle average is $\log|g(0)|$ by the [mean value property for harmonic functions](mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md). For each removed factor $z-a$, with $0<|a|<R$, the circle average of $\log|Re^{i\theta}-a|$ is $\log R$: factor out $Re^{i\theta}$ and apply the [mean value property for harmonic functions](mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md) to $\log|1-(a/R)w|$ on a neighbourhood of the closed unit [disk](disk-mathematics.md). Reassembling the factors gives $\log(R/|a|)$ for each zero, proving [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md).
