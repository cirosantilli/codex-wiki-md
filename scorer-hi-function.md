# Scorer Hi function

↑ **Parent:** [Scorer function](scorer-function.md)

For real $x$, the integral representation is

$$
\operatorname{Hi}(x)=\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty e^{xt-t^3/3}\,dt.
$$

Using [differentiation under the integral sign](differentiation-under-the-integral-sign.md) twice and then [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) shows $\operatorname{Hi}''-x\operatorname{Hi}=1/\pi$. The cubic decay makes the integral converge for every real $x$.
