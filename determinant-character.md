# Determinant character

↑ **Parent:** [Character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md)

For a [group representation](group-representation.md) $\rho$ with character $\theta$, its determinant character sends $g$ to $\det\rho(g)$. Multiplicativity makes it a [linear character](linear-character.md), independent of the equivalent matrix realization of $\rho$.
