# Degenerate Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

A [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is degenerate when its Hessian with respect to [velocities](velocity.md) is singular. The [momentum](momentum-split.md)–[velocity](velocity.md) relation then cannot be inverted for every [velocity](velocity.md). Some Euler–Lagrange equations can become algebraic constraints rather than independent second-order equations. A massless limit can produce this behaviour; disappearance of an equation at quadratic order does not establish an unconstrained mode of the exact nonlinear model.
