# Rademacher sum

↑ **Parent:** [Rademacher distribution](rademacher-distribution.md)

A Rademacher sum is a linear combination of independent [Rademacher random variables](rademacher-distribution.md), with scalar or normed-space-valued coefficients. Its moments are controlled by the [Khintchine inequality](khintchine-inequality.md) in the scalar case and the [Kahane-Khintchine inequality](kahane-khintchine-inequality.md) in a general [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md).
