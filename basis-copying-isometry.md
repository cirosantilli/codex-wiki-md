# Basis-copying isometry

↑ **Parent:** [No-cloning theorem](no-cloning-theorem.md)

Relative to a fixed [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), the displayed linear map is an [isometry](isometry.md) because its [basis](basis.md) images are orthonormal. Its adjoint obeys $C^*|k\rangle|\ell\rangle=\delta_{k\ell}|k\rangle$. If [vectors](vector.md) are described by coefficient [functions](function-split.md), this adjoint maps $|f\rangle|g\rangle$ to $|fg\rangle$, where the product is pointwise. Although $C$ copies every [basis](basis.md) [quantum state](quantum-state.md), it does not copy arbitrary [superpositions](superposition-principle.md). For example it sends $(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ to $(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$, whereas two copies would also contain the cross terms $|01\rangle$ and $|10\rangle$.
