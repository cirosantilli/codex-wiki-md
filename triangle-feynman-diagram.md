# Triangle Feynman diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A triangle [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) has a loop of three propagators joining three vertices. In [six-dimensional cubic scalar field theory](six-dimensional-cubic-scalar-field-theory.md) it gives a one-loop three-point proper vertex. Attaching a cubic tree vertex makes a four-point connected diagram, but the connecting propagator is a bridge, so the latter is a [one-particle-reducible Feynman diagram](one-particle-reducible-feynman-diagram.md).
