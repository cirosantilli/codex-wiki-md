# Coefficient ideals of a formal power series ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Noetherianity of a formal power series ring](noetherianity-of-a-formal-power-series-ring.md)

For an [ideal](ideal.md) $H\subseteq R[[X]]$, let $A_n$ consist of coefficients of $X^n$ in members of $H$ with all lower coefficients zero. These are [ideals](ideal.md) of $R$ and $A_n\subseteq A_{n+1}$, by multiplication by $X$. If $R$ is [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md), this chain stabilizes. Choose series whose leading coefficients generate the finitely many distinct coefficient [ideals](ideal.md), then cancel coefficients successively. The accumulated multipliers are [formal power series](formal-power-series.md), giving an ordinary finite [ideal](ideal.md) generating set for $H$, rather than merely a dense subideal.
