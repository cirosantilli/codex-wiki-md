# Metachloroperbenzoic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Metachloroperbenzoic acid](metachloroperbenzoic-acid.md) is a peroxycarboxylic acid used for concerted [oxygen](oxygen.md) transfer to [alkenes](alkene.md). A neighboring allylic hydroxyl can direct its approach through [hydrogen bonding](hydrogen-bond.md).
