# Pair immigration with linear deaths

↑ **Parent:** [Immigration--death process](immigration-death-process.md)

A population jumps from $n$ to $n+2$ at a constant immigration rate $\lambda$ and from $n$ to $n-1$ at rate $\beta n$. Applying its [Markov jump-process generator](markov-jump-process-generator.md) to $n$ and $n^2$ gives the displayed mean and variance equations. For $\beta>0$ and finite initial second moment, the stationary mean and variance are $2\lambda/\beta$ and $3\lambda/\beta$. Interpreting the pair-arrival rate as proportional to population instead would produce a different branching process.
