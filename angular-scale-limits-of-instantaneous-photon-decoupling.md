# Angular-scale limits of instantaneous photon decoupling

↑ **Parent:** [Recombination (cosmology)](recombination-cosmology.md)

Approximating decoupling by a single time and retaining only a photon monopole and Doppler dipole omits the finite [cosmological visibility function](cosmological-visibility-function.md) width, diffusion or [Silk damping](cosmic-microwave-background-diffusion-damping.md), quadrupole anisotropy and polarization generated as [tight coupling](tight-coupling-approximation.md) fails. The [photon-baryon acoustic oscillator](photon-baryon-acoustic-oscillator.md) also make the emission density and velocity strongly scale dependent. They can be included in the initial monopole and dipole, so their existence alone does not invalidate the subsequent collisionless [line-of-sight solution for free-streaming photons](line-of-sight-solution-for-free-streaming-photons.md). Smaller angles correspond to larger angular multipole $\ell$.
