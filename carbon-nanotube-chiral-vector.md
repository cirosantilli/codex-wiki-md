# Carbon nanotube chiral vector

↑ **Parent:** [Single-walled carbon nanotube](single-walled-carbon-nanotube.md)

The [graphene](graphene.md) translation $\mathbf C_h=n\mathbf a_1+m\mathbf a_2$ that becomes a tube circumference when its endpoints are identified. It determines wrapping indices and diameter $|\mathbf C_h|/\pi$; it is perpendicular to the tube axis.
