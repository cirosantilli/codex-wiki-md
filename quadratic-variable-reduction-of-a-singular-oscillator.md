# Quadratic-variable reduction of a singular oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order linear differential equation](second-order-linear-differential-equation.md)

The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) with $y(x)=Y(x^2)$ gives $xy''-y'=4x^3Y''$ and transforms the equation, away from zero, into $Y''+Y=0$. Thus its solution space is spanned by $\cos(x^2)$ and $\sin(x^2)$. Both extend smoothly through the [regular singular point](regular-singular-point.md) at zero. Their even [power series](power-series.md) allow the independent data $y(0)$ and $y''(0)$ to select the two solutions; every smooth solution has $y'(0)=0$.
