# Additive identity

↑ **Parent:** [Identity element](identity-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Additive_identity)

An [identity element](identity-element.md) for [addition](addition.md): $0+a=a+0=a$. In a [vector space](vector-space-split.md), it is the [zero vector](zero-vector.md); among [matrices](matrix.md) of fixed dimensions, it is the [zero matrix](zero-matrix.md).
