# Net survival

↑ **Parent:** [Competing risks](competing-risks.md)

For [cause-specific hazard](cause-specific-hazard.md) $\lambda_k$, the [net survival](net-survival.md) is $S_k^{\rm net}(t)=\exp[-\int_0^t\lambda_k(u)\,du]$. Censoring all competing events in a cause-specific [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md) targets this quantity under appropriate observation-censoring assumptions. Its complement generally exceeds the actual [cumulative incidence function](cumulative-incidence-function.md) $\int_0^tS(u)\lambda_k(u)\,du$, because all-cause survival $S(u)$ also includes the other hazards. Identifying [net survival](net-survival.md) with survival after a hypothetical elimination of competing events requires extra causal assumptions.
