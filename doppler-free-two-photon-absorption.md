# Doppler-free two-photon absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Two-photon absorption](two-photon-absorption.md)

Absorbing one [photon](photon.md) from each of two opposing equal-frequency beams cancels the first-order Doppler shift through their nearly zero summed [wavevector](wavevector.md). Absorbing both [photons](photon.md) from the same beam does not share this cancellation and can create a broad background.
