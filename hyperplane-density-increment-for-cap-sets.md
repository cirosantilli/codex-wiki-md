# Hyperplane density increment for cap sets

↑ **Parent:** [Density increment](density-increment.md)

If a [cap set](cap-set.md) $A\subseteq\mathbb F_3^d$ has [subset density](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) $\alpha>0$ and $3^d\alpha^2\geq2$, some [affine subspace](affine-subspace.md) of codimension one has relative [subset density](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) at least $\alpha+\alpha^2/2$. The zero-sum count and the [Parseval identity on a finite group](parseval-identity-on-a-finite-group.md) give a nonzero [finite abelian Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient-on-a-finite-abelian-group.md) of magnitude at least $\alpha^2/2$. The three slice densities are $\alpha+2\operatorname{Re}(z\omega^j)$, where $z$ is this [finite abelian Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient-on-a-finite-abelian-group.md) and $\omega$ a primitive cube [root of unity](root-of-unity.md); one slice has increment at least $|z|$. Translation preserves the zero-sum condition because the [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) is three.
