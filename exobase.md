# Exobase

↑ **Parent:** [Exosphere](exosphere.md)

The exobase marks the transition between a collisional atmosphere and its [exosphere](exosphere.md). For a neutral, approximately hydrostatic [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md), take [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) $\ell\simeq1/(n\sigma_c)$ and [atmospheric scale height](atmospheric-scale-height.md) $H=k_BT/(mg)$. The criterion $\ell\sim H$ gives

$$
n_e\sim\frac1{\sigma_c H},\qquad P_e\sim\frac{mg}{\sigma_c}.
$$

An order-one collision convention can change this coefficient. The [pressure](pressure.md) is not universal: the [collision cross-section](collision-cross-section.md), composition, local gravity, and ionization must be specified. A strongly escaping, non-hydrostatic atmosphere requires an appropriate density-gradient length instead of a hydrostatic $H$.
