# Generalized-eigenspace bracket lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Derivation of a Lie algebra](derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

If $D$ is a [derivation of a Lie algebra](derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), then its [generalized eigenspaces](generalized-eigenspace.md) satisfy

$$
[L_\lambda,L_\mu]\subseteq L_{\lambda+\mu}.
$$

Apply a sufficiently large power of $D-(\lambda+\mu)I$ and use the [binomial theorem](binomial-theorem.md) together with the derivation rule.
