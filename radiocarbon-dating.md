# Radiocarbon dating

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometric dating](radiometric-dating.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiocarbon_dating)

[Radiocarbon dating](radiocarbon-dating.md) uses the loss of [carbon-14](carbon-14.md) relative to stable carbon after carbon exchange stops. It can measure either an isotope ratio by [accelerator mass spectrometry](accelerator-mass-spectrometry.md) or [radioactive activity](radioactive-activity.md) per fixed carbon content. Interpreting an age requires [radiocarbon calibration](radiocarbon-calibration.md) and control of contamination and reservoir differences.
