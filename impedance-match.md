# Impedance match

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic impedance](acoustic-impedance.md)

Equality of the relevant wave impedances across an interface. In the elementary normal-incidence model this makes the [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) vanish, even when other material properties differ.
