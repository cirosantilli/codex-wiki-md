# Cn Weyl group

↑ **Parent:** [Cn root system](cn-root-system.md)

The [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) of the [Cn root system](cn-root-system.md) is the [signed symmetric group](hyperoctahedral-group.md). A [Weyl reflection](weyl-reflection.md) in $2\varepsilon_i$ changes the sign of coordinate $i$; one in $\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j$ interchanges coordinates $i,j$; one in $\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j$ interchanges and negates both coordinates. These generate all independent sign changes and all [permutations](permutation.md), giving order $2^nn!$.
