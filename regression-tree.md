# Regression tree

↑ **Parent:** [Decision tree learning](decision-tree-learning.md)

A [regression tree](regression-tree.md) is the numerical-response case of [decision tree learning](decision-tree-learning.md). A regression tree recursively partitions predictor space by axis-aligned splits. At each leaf, candidate coordinates and thresholds are compared by the residual sum of squares after replacing that leaf by its two children; the selected split minimizes the total criterion. The fitted value in each terminal region is the sample mean there.
