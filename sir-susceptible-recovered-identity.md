# SIR susceptible-recovered identity

↑ **Parent:** [SIR model](sir-model.md)

For the closed deterministic [SIR model](sir-model.md) with $S(0)=S_0$, $R(0)=0$, infection rate $\beta SI$ and recovery rate $\gamma I$, division of the two flow equations gives $S(t)=S_0e^{-\beta R(t)/\gamma}$. Population conservation then expresses $I$ in terms of $R$.
