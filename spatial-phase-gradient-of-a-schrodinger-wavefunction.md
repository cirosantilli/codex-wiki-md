# Spatial phase gradient of a Schrodinger wavefunction

↑ **Parent:** [Probability current](probability-current.md)

Where a nonzero [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) can be written as $\psi=\sqrt\rho e^{i\theta}$, and there is no [vector potential](vector-potential.md), substituting into the Schrodinger [probability current](probability-current.md) gives $\mathbf j=\rho\hbar\nabla\theta/m$. The direction of [probability](probability.md) flow is determined by the [phase](phase-waves.md) gradient rather than the slope of the [real part](real-part.md). A [plane wave](plane-wave.md) and its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) have the same [real part](real-part.md) and density but opposite currents. In a [vector potential](vector-potential.md) the gauge-invariant kinetic [momentum](momentum-split.md) replaces $\hbar\nabla\theta$.
