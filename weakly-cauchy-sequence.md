# Weakly Cauchy sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Weak topology](weak-topology-split.md)

A sequence $(x_n)$ is weakly Cauchy when $(f(x_n))$ is a [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md) for every continuous linear functional $f$. Its consecutive differences form a [weakly null sequence](weakly-null-sequence.md).
