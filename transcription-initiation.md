# Transcription initiation

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription (biology)](transcription-biology.md)

The initial stage of [transcription](transcription-biology.md), including [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md) association with a [promoter](promoter-genetics.md), local [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) opening, and the first [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) synthesis events before productive elongation. Open-complex formation, abortive synthesis and [promoter escape](promoter-escape.md) are distinct transitions; an unwound bubble need not disappear when escape occurs.
