# Closest point theorem for a closed convex subset of Lp

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

For $2\le p<\infty$, a nonempty [closed](closed-set.md) [convex set](convex-set.md) $K$ in an [Lp space](lp-space.md) has exactly one nearest point to every $f$. A [minimizing sequence](minimizing-sequence.md) $g_j$ is a [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md): the [Clarkson inequality](clarkson-s-inequalities.md) bounds $\|g_j-g_k\|_p^p$ by $2^{p-1}(\|g_j-f\|_p^p+\|g_k-f\|_p^p)-2^pd^p$, where $d$ is the infimum of the distances. [Completeness of Lp spaces](completeness-of-lp-spaces.md) and the [closed set](closed-set.md) property give a limit in $K$. Applying the same inequality to two minimizers proves uniqueness. Nonemptiness is essential.
