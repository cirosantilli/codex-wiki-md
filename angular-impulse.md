# Angular impulse

↑ **Parent:** [Torque](torque.md)

The [time integral](time-integral.md) of [torque](torque.md) about a specified origin equals the change in [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) about that origin under the usual fixed-origin [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) balance. Its units are $\mathrm{N\,m\,s}$, equivalently $\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$. For rotation about a fixed [principal axis](principal-axis.md), the magnitude is $I\Delta\omega$.
