# Time-dependent Ginzburg-Landau equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md)

A phenomenological near-transition relaxation equation for a charged complex [order parameter](order-parameter.md), with a positive real relaxation coefficient in its simplest dissipative form. Under $\Psi\mapsto e^{iq\chi/\hbar}\Psi$, $\mathbf A\mapsto\mathbf A+\nabla\chi$ and $\phi\mapsto\phi-\partial_t\chi$, the displayed time derivative transforms covariantly, as does the spatial derivative in the [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md). Microscopic applicability depends on regime and scattering processes; this equation is distinct from a universal conservative condensate wave equation.
