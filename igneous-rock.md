# Igneous rock

↑ **Parent:** [Petrology](petrology.md)

A [rock](rock-geology.md) formed by crystallization of [magma](magma.md) or lava. Intrusive bodies crystallize below the surface, while extrusive rocks solidify at or near the surface. [Granite](granite.md) is a common intrusive example and [basalt](basalt.md) a common extrusive example.
