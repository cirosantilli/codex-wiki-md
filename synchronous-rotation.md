# Synchronous rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal locking](tidal-locking.md)

Synchronous rotation has spin period equal to orbital period. A synchronously rotating [exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md) on a circular orbit has a persistent illuminated hemisphere and a persistent dark hemisphere, making [day-night heat redistribution](day-night-heat-redistribution.md) crucial.
