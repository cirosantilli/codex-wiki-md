# Scalar resonant Friedel-pair intensity difference

↑ **Parent:** [Anomalous X-ray scattering](anomalous-x-ray-scattering.md)

For positional [structure factors](structure-factor.md) $A,B$, a real scattering factor $a$, and a complex factor $b+ib''$, the amplitudes are $F_+=aA+(b+ib'')B$ and $F_-=aA^*+(b+ib'')B^*$. Expanding their squared moduli gives the displayed difference. Resonance and lack of inversion allow a difference but do not force one: proportional positional sums with a real proportionality factor give zero.
