# Inelastic neutron scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron scattering](neutron-scattering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inelastic_neutron_scattering)

[Inelastic neutron scattering](inelastic-neutron-scattering.md) resolves $E_i-E_f\ne0$. Through [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md), a peak at positive $\hbar\omega$ identifies an allowed excitation [energy](energy-split.md); through [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md), its location in $\mathbf Q$ constrains the spatial variation of that excitation. A [phonon](phonon.md) is a quantum of lattice vibration; a [magnon](magnon.md) is a quantum of a [spin wave](spin-wave.md). Their different couplings make [energy](energy-split.md) transfer alone insufficient to identify which was created.

[Bertram Neville Brockhouse](bertram-neville-brockhouse.md)'s [aluminium](aluminium.md) experiment rotated a large single [crystal](crystal.md), scanning analyzer angle for neutron groups. His [vanadium](vanadium.md) comparison exposed the elastic resolution. Later [magnetite](magnetite.md) measurements used a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) intensity test to identify [magnons](magnon.md).[1994 lecture, pp. 115–121, Figs. 3–6](slow-neutron-spectroscopy-and-the-grand-atlas-of-the-physical-world-by-bertram-neville-brockhouse.md)

Successive orientations sample different [wave vectors](wavevector.md); analyzer settings distinguish outgoing [energies](energy-split.md). This supplies points on a [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md), rather than only an angular diffraction pattern. Finite resolution can merge neighboring excitations; background, multiple scattering and processes involving several [phonons](phonon.md) can complicate interpretation. A lattice model is a testable explanation of selected features, not a guarantee that every spectral peak has the same origin.
