# Left-semicircle harmonic measure from an off-center point

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md)

For $0<a<1$ in the unit disk, the [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $(z-a)/(1-az)$ takes $a$ to zero and the left semicircle to the arc centered at $-1$ with half-angle $\arctan((1-a^2)/(2a))$. Brownian exit from the centered disk is uniform in angle, so [conformal invariance of harmonic measure](conformal-invariance-of-harmonic-measure.md) gives the displayed probability. Scaling and the [reflection identity for Brownian exit from a half-disc](reflection-identity-for-brownian-exit-from-a-half-disc.md) give probability $4\arctan(1/r)/\pi$ of exiting the radius-$r$ circle from start one before reaching the imaginary axis.
