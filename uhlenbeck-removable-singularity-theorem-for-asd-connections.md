# Uhlenbeck removable singularity theorem for ASD connections

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-self-dual connection](anti-self-dual-connection.md)

A smooth [anti-self-dual connection](anti-self-dual-connection.md) with compact structure group on a punctured four-ball and finite $L^2$ curvature extends smoothly over the puncture after changing gauge and extending the bundle. The gauge on the punctured ball can carry nontrivial clutching degree; the extended bundle need not agree globally with the original one in a bubbling sequence.
