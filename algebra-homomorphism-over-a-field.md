# Algebra homomorphism over a field

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md)

For a [field](field.md) $k$, a homomorphism of $k$-algebras is a unital [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) preserving scalar multiplication by every element of $k$. For unital algebras it is equivalently a [ring](ring.md) homomorphism commuting with their structural maps from $k$. Pullback by a [morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) gives such a homomorphism between their [coordinate rings](coordinate-ring.md).
