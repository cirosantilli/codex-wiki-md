# Quasi-compact morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md)

A [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) is quasi-compact if the inverse image of every [quasi-compact](compact-space.md) [open subset](open-set.md) is [quasi-compact](compact-space.md). It suffices to test [affine open subschemes](affine-open-subscheme.md) of the target. This is a relative condition: the source need not be a [quasi-compact scheme](quasi-compact-scheme.md) when the target is not one.
