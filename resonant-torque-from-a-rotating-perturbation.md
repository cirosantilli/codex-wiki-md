# Resonant torque from a rotating perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

The leading second-order specific [torque](torque.md) on a collisionless distribution from a weak steadily rotating perturbation with $\Phi_{\mathbf k}=\Psi_{\mathbf k}e^{-im\Omega_pt}$ and $m=k_\phi$. The sum includes conjugate harmonics. [Linear response in action-angle variables](linear-response-in-action-angle-variables.md) gives the finite-time kernel $\sin(\Delta t)/\Delta$; its integrated [long-time resonance kernel](long-time-resonance-kernel.md) selects orbital [resonances](resonance.md). This limit assumes a smooth continuum, regular resonant surfaces and negligible secular redistribution during the response calculation.
