# Screened Rutherford multiple-scattering scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple Coulomb scattering](multiple-coulomb-scattering.md)

For $d\sigma/d\Omega=K/[p^2\sin^4(\theta/2)]$ between angular cutoffs, the leading total [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) is $16\pi K/(p^2\theta_{\min}^2)$. Multiply its mean count $\rho_nx\sigma$ by the one-scatter second moment $2\theta_{\min}^2\ln(\theta_{\max}/\theta_{\min})$ to obtain the displayed total angular [variance](variance-split.md). A single-plane projected [variance](variance-split.md) is half as large. Comparing the scattering angle to a magnetic bend explains the approximately [momentum](momentum-split.md)-independent scattering contribution to relative track uncertainty.
