<h1 id="greene-nijenhuis-wilf-hook-walk">Greene–Nijenhuis–Wilf hook walk</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md)

At a noncorner cell of a [Young diagram](young-diagram.md), this random walk chooses uniformly one of the other cells in its [hook of a Young diagram](hook-of-a-young-diagram.md). It stops at a corner. Every step moves right or down, so it terminates. Its corner probabilities provide a probabilistic proof of the [hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md).
