# SL2R action on differentials

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmüller theory](teichmuller-theory.md)

For nonzero [holomorphic one-forms](holomorphic-one-form.md) and [holomorphic quadratic differentials](holomorphic-quadratic-differential.md), apply $A\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ to every [flat coordinate](natural-coordinate-of-a-holomorphic-differential.md). Translation and sign transition maps remain of the same type, defining a new [complex structure](complex-structure.md) and differential. Composition gives a [group action](group-action.md), [zero orders](order-of-a-zero-of-a-differential.md) persist and area is preserved. The zero section has no such atlas; fixing it gives only a set-theoretic extension that is generally discontinuous.
