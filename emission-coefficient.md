# Emission coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md)

The [emission coefficient](emission-coefficient.md) $j_\nu$ measures emitted [energy](energy-split.md) per unit volume, time, [solid angle](solid-angle.md) and [frequency](frequency.md). Its SI units are $\mathrm{W\,m^{-3}\,sr^{-1}\,Hz^{-1}}$. Along a ray it contributes $j_\nu ds$ to [specific intensity](spectral-radiance.md). For an [isotropic](isotropy.md) [spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md) transition with a normalized [spectral line profile](spectral-line-profile.md), $j_\nu=h\nu_{21}n_2A_{21}\varphi(\nu)/(4\pi)$; integration over [solid angle](solid-angle.md) removes the $4\pi$ denominator.
