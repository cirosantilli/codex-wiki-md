# Finite-depth Eady dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Eady model](eady-model.md)

For constant shear $U=\Lambda z$ between impermeable horizontal walls at $0,D$, with constant [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) and no interior [potential-vorticity gradient](potential-vorticity-gradient.md), define $m=NK/|f_0|$. The boundary conditions yield

$$
\left(c-\frac{\Lambda D}{2}\right)^2=\frac{\Lambda^2}{m^2}\left[\frac{(mD)^2}{4}-(mD)\coth(mD)+1\right].
$$

The bracket is negative for $0<mD<2.399\ldots$, giving [baroclinic instability](baroclinic-instability.md) for nonzero zonal wavenumber. For large separation the boundary waves decouple; the lower-wave speed tends to $\Lambda/m$, matching the [dispersion relation of a semi-infinite Eady edge wave](dispersion-relation-of-a-semi-infinite-eady-edge-wave.md).
