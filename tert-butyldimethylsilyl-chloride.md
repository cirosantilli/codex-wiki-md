# Tert-butyldimethylsilyl chloride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

The silylating reagent $\mathrm{tBuMe_2SiCl}$, commonly abbreviated [TBSCl](tert-butyldimethylsilyl-chloride.md). [nucleophilic substitution](nucleophilic-substitution.md) at silicon converts an [alcohol](alcohol.md) into a [silyl ether](silyl-ether.md).
