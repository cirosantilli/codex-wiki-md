# Complete integral of the Hamilton-Jacobi equation

↑ **Parent:** [Hamilton-Jacobi equation](hamilton-jacobi-equation.md)

A complete integral depends on as many independent parameters as configuration coordinates and supplies a locally invertible [canonical transformation](canonical-transformation.md) to constants of motion. For a type-one generating function $G(q,Q,t)$, require a nondegenerate mixed-derivative matrix in the relevant local chart. Solving $G_t+H(q,G_q,t)=0$ makes the transformed [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) zero, so both new canonical coordinates and new [canonical momenta](canonical-momentum.md) are constant. An arbitrary parameter-deficient solution does not ensure such a transformation.
