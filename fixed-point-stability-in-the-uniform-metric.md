# Fixed-point stability in the uniform metric

↑ **Parent:** [Contraction mapping theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md)

For two [contraction mappings](contraction-mapping.md) of a nonempty [complete metric space](complete-metric-space.md), their [fixed points](fixed-point.md) obey $d(x_f,x_g)\leq q_fd(x_f,x_g)+d_\infty(f,g)$. Rearranging proves the displayed bound whenever the [uniform metric](uniform-metric.md) is finite. It establishes continuity of the fixed-point map at each $f$ without assuming a contraction constant shared by all maps near $f$.
