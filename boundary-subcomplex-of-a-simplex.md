# Boundary subcomplex of a simplex

↑ **Parent:** [Simplex](simplex.md)

The boundary subcomplex $\partial\Delta^n$ consists of all proper faces of the $n$-simplex. Its [geometric realization of a simplicial complex](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) is homeomorphic to the [sphere](sphere.md) $S^{n-1}$, while $|\Delta^n|$ is homeomorphic to the [closed disk](closed-disc.md) $D^n$.
