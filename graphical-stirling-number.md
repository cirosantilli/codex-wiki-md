# Graphical Stirling number

↑ **Parent:** [Stirling numbers of the second kind](stirling-numbers-of-the-second-kind.md)

The graphical Stirling number $\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}n\\k\end{smallmatrix}\right\}_G$ counts partitions of the vertices of a graph $G$ into $k$ nonempty [independent sets](independent-set-graph-theory.md). Equivalently, it counts proper colourings with $k$ unlabeled nonempty colour classes.
