# Cluster baryon-fraction estimate of matter density

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic baryon fraction](cosmic-baryon-fraction.md)

If a measured cluster baryon fraction represents the [cosmic baryon fraction](cosmic-baryon-fraction.md) $\Omega_b/\Omega_m$, combining it with the [cosmological baryon density](cosmological-baryon-density.md) inferred from [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) gives the displayed estimate. Gas and stellar baryons must both be counted, and all distance or [Hubble constant](hubble-constant.md) dependence must be consistent. Baryon depletion or feedback modifies the identification: with $f_{b,\rm cluster}=b\Omega_b/\Omega_m$, the corrected answer is $\Omega_m=b\Omega_b/f_{b,\rm cluster}$. The method measures total matter rather than vacuum or radiation density.
