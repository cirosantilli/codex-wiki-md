# One-point value region of a holomorphic disc map

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz-Pick theorem](schwarz-pick-theorem.md)

For [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D$ with $f(z_0)=w_0$, put $r=|(z-z_0)/(1-\overline z_0z)|$. Their possible values at $z$ form the closed disc with centre $(1-r^2)w_0/(1-r^2|w_0|^2)$ and radius $r(1-|w_0|^2)/(1-r^2|w_0|^2)$. Indeed $\psi_{w_0}\circ f=\psi_{z_0}h$ with [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) $|h|\le1$; all constant choices of $h$ in the closed [unit disc](unit-disc.md) are admissible. Completing the square in the displayed pseudohyperbolic inequality gives the centre and radius.
