# Collective cell migration

↑ **Parent:** [Cell migration](cell-migration.md)

In [collective cell migration](collective-cell-migration.md), neighbouring [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) move with coordinated trajectories while retaining interactions that influence their motion. Mechanical coupling, contact-dependent signals and soluble cues can each contribute. A coherent group need not consist of identically behaving cells: leaders and followers may perform different roles.
