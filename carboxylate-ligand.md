# Carboxylate ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [carboxylate](carboxylate.md) bound through one or both [oxygen](oxygen.md) atoms. It can be monodentate, chelating or bridging; its charge is $-1$ regardless of whether one or two [oxygen](oxygen.md) donors bind.
