# Disconnected space

↑ **Parent:** [Connected space](connected-space.md)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is [disconnected space](disconnected-space.md) if it can be written as the union of two disjoint nonempty [open subsets](open-set.md). Such a pair is a [separation of a topological space](separation-of-a-topological-space.md). Each of the subsets is also closed, and conversely a nontrivial subset that is both open and closed gives a [separation of a topological space](separation-of-a-topological-space.md) with its complement.
