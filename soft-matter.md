# Soft matter

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_matter)

[Soft matter](soft-matter.md) studies materials whose large-scale structures respond strongly to modest mechanical or thermal disturbances, including [polymers](polymer-split.md), [liquid crystals](liquid-crystal.md) and [colloids](colloid.md). [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md) connects chain conformations to material response; the [coil-stretch transition](coil-stretch-transition.md) is one example.
