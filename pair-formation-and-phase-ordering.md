# Pair formation and phase ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Superconducting transition temperature](superconducting-transition-temperature.md)

Pairing can establish a local binding or gap scale before a system develops phase-coherent transport. An actual [superconducting transition temperature](superconducting-transition-temperature.md) depends on both the pair scale and the [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md). Low carrier density or weak interlayer coupling can make phase ordering the lower scale even when the pairing amplitude is appreciable. A minimum or smooth interpolation of two estimated scales is a qualitative guide, not a substitute for a self-consistent theory of the crossover.
