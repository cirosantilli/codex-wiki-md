# Absorbing Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md)

A [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md) with at least one [absorbing state](absorbing-state.md), such that from each state an [absorbing state](absorbing-state.md) can be reached. In a finite such chain, absorption occurs with [probability](probability.md) 1. Expected times to absorption satisfy a [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md) obtained by conditioning on the first transition.
