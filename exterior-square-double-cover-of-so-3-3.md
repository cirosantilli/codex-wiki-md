<h1 id="exterior-square-double-cover-of-so-3-3">Exterior-square double cover of SO(3,3)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Spin group](spin-group.md)

A fixed volume form makes wedge pairing on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$ a symmetric form of signature $(3,3)$. The exterior-square action of [special linear group](special-linear-group.md) [matrices](matrix.md) preserves it. Its kernel is $\{\pm I\}$: a transformation acting trivially on every bivector preserves every two-plane and hence every line, so is scalar, with scalar square one. The derivative is injective and both groups have dimension fifteen, so the connected image is the full [identity component](identity-component.md). This is a concrete real spin-group cover.
