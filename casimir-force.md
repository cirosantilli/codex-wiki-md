# Casimir force

↑ **Parent:** [Fluctuation-induced force](fluctuation-induced-force.md)

Two parallel ideal conducting plates at zero [temperature](temperature-split.md) have separation-dependent electromagnetic energy per area $-\pi^2\hbar c/(720d^3)$ and [pressure](pressure-split.md) $-\pi^2\hbar c/(240d^4)$. The negative sign denotes attraction in this geometry. Finite [temperature](temperature-split.md), material response and boundary geometry change the ideal result; an absolute divergent vacuum energy is not the measured quantity.
