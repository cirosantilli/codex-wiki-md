# Infinitary logic

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinitary_logic)

[Infinitary logic](infinitary-logic.md) permits infinitary syntactic operations, such as infinite conjunctions, together with an interpretation of its formulas. The [infinitary language](infinitary-language.md) $L_{\kappa,\lambda}$ specifies the permitted conjunction and quantifier lengths.
