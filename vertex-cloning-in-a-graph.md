# Vertex cloning in a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph blow-up](graph-blow-up.md)

Cloning a vertex replaces it by several nonadjacent vertices with identical neighbourhoods. In [homomorphism density](homomorphism-density.md), conditioning on the images of all other vertices reduces cloning to the inequality $\mathbb E p^t\ge(\mathbb E p)^t$ from the [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md). Iterated cloning shows that positive clique homomorphism density forces any fixed complete multipartite blow-up.
