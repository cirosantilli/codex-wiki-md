# Three-dimensional Bessel process

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel process](bessel-process.md)

The radial part of three-dimensional [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started away from the origin is a [Bessel process](bessel-process.md) of dimension three. The radial [Laplacian](laplacian.md) is $\Delta|x|=2/|x|$, so the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives the displayed equation. The radial martingale has unit [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) rate and is [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) by the [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md). The reciprocal $1/R$ is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). Stopping it on $(\varepsilon,M)$ gives $\mathbb P_x(T_\varepsilon<T_M)=\varepsilon(M-x)/(x(M-\varepsilon))$. Letting $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ shows that zero is not hit.
