# Carbon-14

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon](carbon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon-14)

[Carbon-14](carbon-14.md) has six [protons](proton.md) and eight [neutrons](neutron-split.md). Atmospheric production is dominated by $^{14}\mathrm N(n,p){}^{14}\mathrm C$ using secondary [cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md) [neutrons](neutron-split.md). It undergoes [beta-minus decay](beta-minus-decay.md) to [nitrogen-14](nitrogen-14.md), with a physical [half-life](half-life.md) of approximately $5730$ years.
