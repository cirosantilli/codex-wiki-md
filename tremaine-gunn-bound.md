# Tremaine-Gunn bound

↑ **Parent:** [Hot dark matter](hot-dark-matter.md)

The maximum primordial occupation of a collisionless [fermion](fermion.md) distribution limits the coarse-grained phase-space density attainable in a halo. Combining that bound with an observed [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) and [mass density](density.md) gives a lower candidate [mass](mass.md). This tests light-fermion halo models independently of whether the candidate is optically visible.[Physical Review Letters 42, pp. 407–410](dynamical-role-of-light-neutral-leptons-in-cosmology-by-scott-tremaine-and-james-edward-gunn.md)
