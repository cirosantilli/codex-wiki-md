# Somatic nervous system

↑ **Parent:** [Nervous system](nervous-system.md)

The division of the [nervous system](nervous-system.md) associated with bodily sensation and control of [skeletal muscle](skeletal-muscle.md). [Facial electromyography](facial-electromyography.md) records activity of [skeletal muscle](skeletal-muscle.md); it is not itself an autonomic recording.
