# Oort constants

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy dynamics](galaxy-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oort_constants)

The Oort constants describe local differential galactic rotation: $A=-R\Omega'/2$ and $B=-\Omega-R\Omega'/2$. Thus $\Omega=A-B$, the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) obeys $\kappa^2=-4\Omega B$, and the [epicyclic ellipse](epicyclic-ellipse.md) has axis ratio $\sqrt{-B/(A-B)}$.
