# Atmospheric extinction

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric optics](atmospheric-optics.md)

Atmospheric extinction attenuates starlight through absorption and scattering. It depends on [wavelength](wavelength.md) and path length, and is described by an [optical depth](optical-depth.md) or an extinction coefficient in astronomical magnitudes per airmass.
