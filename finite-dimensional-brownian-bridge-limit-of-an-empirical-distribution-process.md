# Finite-dimensional Brownian bridge limit of an empirical distribution process

↑ **Parent:** [Empirical distribution process](empirical-distribution-process.md)

For fixed $x_1,\ldots,x_d$, the vector $(\alpha_n(x_j))_{j=1}^d$ converges in distribution to $(B(G(x_j)))_{j=1}^d$, where $B$ is a standard [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md). To prove it, write the vector as $n^{-1/2}$ times the sum of independent centred bounded indicator vectors. Their covariance matrix is $G(\min(x_i,x_j))-G(x_i)G(x_j)$. The [multivariate central limit theorem](multivariate-central-limit-theorem.md) gives a centred [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) with this covariance, exactly the bridge vector. Singular covariance matrices, including endpoint times or repeated values of $G$, cause no problem. This is a finite-dimensional assertion; a full function-space limit additionally requires tightness.
