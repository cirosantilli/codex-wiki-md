# Pullback of a sheaf of modules

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse image sheaf](inverse-image-sheaf.md)

For a morphism of [ringed spaces](ringed-space-split.md), the pullback of an $\mathcal O_Y$-module is

$$
f^*\mathcal E=\mathcal O_X\otimes_{f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y}f^{-1}\mathcal E.
$$

The [inverse image functor](inverse-image-functor.md) supplies the sheaf $f^{-1}\mathcal E$; tensoring with the source structure sheaf supplies the module scalar extension.
