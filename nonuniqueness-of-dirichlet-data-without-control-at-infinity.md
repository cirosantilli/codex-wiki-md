# Nonuniqueness of Dirichlet data without control at infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md)

Unbounded-domain [Dirichlet problems](dirichlet-problem.md) can need a condition at infinity. On all of $\mathbb R^n$, constant functions have identical empty boundary data and are distinct finite-energy stationary points of $\frac12\int|Du|^2$. With nonempty boundary, on the exterior of the unit ball in dimension at least three, $1-|x|^{2-n}$ is a nonzero harmonic function vanishing on the sphere, with finite [Dirichlet energy](dirichlet-energy.md) and a nonzero limit at infinity. It is stationary for compactly supported variations. The permitted class of variations and the behaviour at infinity must be specified for a global variational uniqueness claim.
