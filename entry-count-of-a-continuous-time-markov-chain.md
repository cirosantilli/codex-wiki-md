# Entry count of a continuous-time Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

The entry count $N_s(t)$ counts jumps of a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) into state $s$ by time $t$, excluding initial occupation. It is a [counting process](counting-process.md). For homogeneous [transition intensities](transition-intensity.md), its mean has derivative $\sum_{i\ne s}p_{ri}(t)q_{is}$ conditional on initial state $r$, so its [expected value](expected-value.md) is the sum of incoming rates times the expected [occupation times of a continuous-time Markov chain](occupation-time-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md). This density is an expected count rate, not necessarily a normalized [probability density function](probability-density-function.md).
