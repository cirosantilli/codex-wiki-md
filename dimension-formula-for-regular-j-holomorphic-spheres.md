# Dimension formula for regular J-holomorphic spheres

↑ **Parent:** [J-holomorphic sphere](j-holomorphic-sphere.md)

For a regular nonconstant [J-holomorphic sphere](j-holomorphic-sphere.md) in a $2n$-dimensional target, the parametrized [Fredholm index](fredholm-index.md) is $2n+2c_1(A)$. Removing the six-dimensional [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) reparametrization group gives the displayed local moduli dimension. The quotient is a manifold for a [simple J-holomorphic sphere](simple-j-holomorphic-sphere.md) and may be an orbifold for a multiple cover with finite stabilizer. In dimension four the formula is $2c_1(A)-2$.
