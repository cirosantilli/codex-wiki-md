# Octahedral quasirandomness of a three-uniform hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md)

For a tripartite three-[uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) on nonempty finite parts $X,Y,Z$, let $h:X\times Y\times Z\to\{0,1\}$ be its [hypergraph edge](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) indicator, let $p=\mathbb Eh$ and put $g=h-p$. In the eighth-power convention, $\alpha$-quasirandomness means

$$
\|g\|_{\square^3}^8=\mathbb E_{x_0,x_1,y_0,y_1,z_0,z_1}\prod_{i,j,k\in\{0,1\}}g(x_i,y_j,z_k)\leq\alpha.
$$

All averages are uniform and independent, with repeated sampled [vertices of a hypergraph](vertex-of-a-hypergraph.md) allowed. This expression is nonnegative because it equals $\mathbb E_{x_0,x_1,y_0,y_1}(\mathbb E_z\prod_{i,j}g(x_i,y_j,z))^2$. It measures correlations across the eight faces of a tripartite octahedral configuration. If instead the [norm](norm.md) itself is used as the quasirandomness parameter, the parameter in this definition is its eighth power.
