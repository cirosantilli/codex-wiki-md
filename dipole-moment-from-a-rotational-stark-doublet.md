# Dipole moment from a rotational Stark doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic Stark shift of a polar linear rotor](quadratic-stark-shift-of-a-polar-linear-rotor.md)

For $J=0\to1$, the $m=0$ and $|m|=1$ [frequency](frequency.md) shifts are $4(pE)^2/(15Bh)$ and $7(pE)^2/(60Bh)$. Their difference is $3(pE)^2/(20Bh)$, so $p=(h/E)\sqrt{(20/3)(B/h)\Delta\nu}$. The common lower-state shift cancels from the splitting but not from either absolute transition [frequency](frequency.md).
