# Lorentz four-force

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

The covariant force on a charged particle is its charge times the electromagnetic field-strength tensor contracted with its [four-velocity](four-velocity.md). Antisymmetry keeps the four-force orthogonal to that velocity and preserves the rest mass.
