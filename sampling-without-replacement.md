# Sampling without replacement

↑ **Parent:** [Probability sampling](probability-sampling.md)

A sample from a finite population is drawn without replacement when an item, once selected, cannot be selected again. Under uniform sequential sampling, every unordered subset of a fixed sample size has equal [probability](probability.md), though the individual draws are generally dependent. Counting subsets with [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md) gives the [hypergeometric distribution](hypergeometric-distribution.md) for the number of selected items of a specified type.
