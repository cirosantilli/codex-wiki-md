# Reciprocal-polynomial root identities

↑ **Parent:** [Partial fraction decomposition](partial-fraction-decomposition.md)

Let $P$ be a monic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of positive degree $m\ge1$ with distinct [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) $r_j$. The [partial fraction decomposition](partial-fraction-decomposition.md)

$$
\frac1{P(z)}=\sum_{j=1}^m\frac1{P'(r_j)(z-r_j)}
$$

follows by matching the simple pole residues; the difference is entire and vanishes at infinity. Comparing its expansion there gives $\sum_jr_j^\ell/P'(r_j)=0$ for $0\le\ell<m-1$ and $\sum_jr_j^{m-1}/P'(r_j)=1$. These identities give the continuity and derivative jumps needed for constant-coefficient [Green functions](green-s-function.md).
