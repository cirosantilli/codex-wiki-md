# Basis-independent conversion of qubit Bloch radii

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum channel](quantum-channel.md)

Fix $0<a\leq1$ and $-1\leq b\leq1$. A single deterministic [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) sending every state of vector $a\mathbf n$ to the state of vector $b\mathbf n$ exists exactly under the displayed condition. Add the requirements for $\mathbf n$ and $-\mathbf n$ to show that the channel fixes $I$. Subtract and take the three coordinate directions to get its action on each [Pauli matrix](pauli-matrices.md), namely multiplication by $b/a$. Linearity thus uniquely forces the [isotropic qubit map](isotropic-qubit-map.md) $\mathcal E_{b/a}$. Its complete-positivity range proves necessity and sufficiency. A basis-dependent unitary may reverse one known vector without implementing a universal inversion, so knowing the basis changes the conversion problem.
