# Basis of a topology

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

A basis of a topology is a family of open sets such that every open set is a union of members of that family. Equivalently, every point of every open set lies in a basis member contained in it. The rectangles $U\times V$ form a basis for the [product topology](product-topology.md) of two spaces. This topological meaning differs from a linear-algebra [basis](basis.md).
