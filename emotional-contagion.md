# Emotional contagion

↑ **Parent:** [Emotion](emotion.md)

An interpersonal influence in which a recipient comes to experience affect resembling another person's affect. [Facial mimicry](facial-mimicry.md), shared circumstances and [social appraisal](social-appraisal.md) are possible contributors, but the presence of one does not establish the others.
