# Regular matrix summability method

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix summability method](matrix-summability-method.md)

A matrix summability method is regular if it preserves the ordinary limit of every [convergent sequence](convergent-sequence.md). The [Silverman-Toeplitz theorem](silverman-toeplitz-theorem.md) characterizes regularity by uniformly bounded absolute row sums, entrywise convergence to zero down every fixed column, and convergence of the row sums to one.
