# Grassmannian of locally free quotients

↑ **Parent:** [Grassmannian](grassmannian.md)

For a finite [free module](free-module.md) $M$ of rank $n$, this [scheme](scheme.md) represents rank-$r$ [locally free module](locally-free-module.md) quotients of $M$ after [base change](base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md), with quotient maps identified up to commuting [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). It carries a universal [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) $0\to\mathcal S\to M\otimes\mathcal O\to\mathcal Q\to0$, of ranks $n-r,n,r$. Its [standard affine charts of the quotient Grassmannian](standard-affine-charts-of-the-quotient-grassmannian.md) prove representability over any base [ring](ring.md). Over a field, taking kernels gives the usual [Grassmannian](grassmannian.md) of $(n-r)$-planes.
