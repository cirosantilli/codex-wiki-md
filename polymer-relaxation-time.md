# Polymer relaxation time

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

A characteristic [time](time-in-physics.md) for a chosen [polymer](polymer-split.md) deformation to relax after forcing stops. Its value depends on the measured quantity and the chain model; one [polymer](polymer-split.md) can have multiple relaxation modes.
