# Long-wave approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

A long-wave approximation assumes that a layer thickness $H$ is much smaller than its streamwise length $L$. The small aspect ratio $H/L$ makes transverse gradients dominate longitudinal gradients and is the geometric basis of [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md).
