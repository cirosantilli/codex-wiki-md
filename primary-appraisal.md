# Primary appraisal

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive appraisal](cognitive-appraisal.md)

Evaluation of whether an event matters to well-being or goals, and whether it involves benefit, harm or threat. The distinction from [secondary appraisal](secondary-appraisal.md) concerns evaluative content; it need not imply two consciously experienced, strictly successive stages.
