# Photon-only matter-radiation equality

↑ **Parent:** [Matter-radiation equality](matter-radiation-equality.md)

Nonrelativistic matter density grows as $(1+z)^3$, whereas freely redshifting [photon](photon.md) [energy density](energy-density.md) grows as $(1+z)^4$. Their equality gives the displayed redshift. Radiation inferred solely from a [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md) excludes the relic [neutrino](neutrino.md) [energy density](energy-density.md), so the resulting redshift differs from total matter-radiation equality. Comparing density parameters at one epoch is equivalent to comparing the densities because their common critical-density denominator cancels.
