# Magnetostriction

↑ **Parent:** [Elasticity (physics)](elasticity-physics-split.md)

[Magnetostriction](magnetostriction.md) is a change of a material's shape or dimensions associated with its [magnetization](magnetization.md). A [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) can therefore produce [strain](strain.md) without a contact [force](force-split.md). The resulting [magnetoelastic coupling](magnetoelastic-coupling.md) lets a [ferromagnet](ferromagnet.md) convert changes of magnetic state into mechanical motion; a constraint can instead turn the preferred deformation into [stress](stress.md). [James Prescott Joule](james-prescott-joule.md) published his study of [iron](iron.md) and [steel](steel.md) bars in 1847.[1847, volume 30, pp. 76–87](on-the-effects-of-magnetism-upon-the-dimensions-of-iron-and-steel-bars-by-james-prescott-joule.md)

The surprising point is that reversing the [magnetization](magnetization.md) need not reverse the deformation. A material can prefer a longer axis along either $+\mathbf m$ or $-\mathbf m$: an axis has no arrow. [Isotropic linear magnetostriction vanishes](isotropic-linear-magnetostriction-vanishes.md) excludes one particular [linear](linearity.md) constitutive term, while [saturated isotropic magnetostrictive strain](saturated-isotropic-magnetostrictive-strain.md) supplies a nonzero response with a [quadratic dependence](quadratic-dependence.md) on the magnetic direction. The [pulsed-field optical magnetostriction experiment](pulsed-field-optical-magnetostriction-experiment.md) shows how an actual length change can be resolved, and [quadratic magnetostriction and frequency doubling](quadratic-magnetostriction-and-frequency-doubling.md) separates an illustrative response law from a measured material curve.
