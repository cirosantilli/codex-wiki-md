# Hot Big Bang model

↑ **Parent:** [Big Bang](big-bang.md)

The hot Big Bang model describes an initially hot expanding universe whose standard radiation- and matter-dominated eras are governed by decelerating Friedmann expansion. Without an earlier inflationary era it exhibits the [horizon problem](horizon-problem.md) and [flatness problem](flatness-problem.md).
