# Scalar shear potential

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar cosmological perturbation](scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md)

For the negative [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md) convention and a [shift vector](shift-vector.md) entering the line element as $dx^i-N^i dt$, the trace-free scalar curvature perturbation is $\delta K^i{}_j{}_{\rm TF}=-(\partial^i\partial_j-\delta^i_j\Delta/3)\chi$. Here spatial raised derivatives use $a^{-2}\delta^{ij}$, so $\Delta=a^{-2}\nabla^2$. Changing the sign of the metric [shift vector](shift-vector.md) changes the corresponding definition of $\chi$.
