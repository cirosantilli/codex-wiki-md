# Cardinalities of monotone natural-number functions

↑ **Parent:** [Countable set](countable-set.md)

[Nondecreasing functions](nondecreasing-function.md) $\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ form an uncountable set: every subset $A$ is encoded by the strictly increasing function $n+\sum_{k=1}^{n}\mathbf1_A(k)$. In contrast, [nonincreasing functions](nonincreasing-function.md) form a countable set because each is determined by a finite initial segment and its eventual constant value.
