# Partial sum

↑ **Parent:** [Series (mathematics)](series-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_sum)

For terms $(a_n)$, the $n$th partial sum is $S_n=\sum_{j=1}^n a_j$. A [series](series-mathematics.md) converges when its sequence of partial sums converges.
