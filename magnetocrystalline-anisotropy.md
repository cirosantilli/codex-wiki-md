# Magnetocrystalline anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic anisotropy](magnetic-anisotropy.md)

Dependence of a crystal's magnetic energy on magnetization direction relative to its lattice, commonly generated by spin-orbit coupling and the crystal environment. A half-filled orbital shell with $L=0$ suppresses first-order orbital anisotropy, although smaller admixture and dipolar effects can remain.
