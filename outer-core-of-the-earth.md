# Outer core of the Earth

↑ **Parent:** [Solid Earth](solid-earth.md)

The [Earth](earth.md)'s outer core is liquid. [P waves](p-wave.md) slow strongly across the mantle-to-outer-core transition, while ordinary shear waves do not propagate through the liquid. This produces major changes in seismic ray paths and shadow ranges.
