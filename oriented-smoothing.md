# Oriented smoothing

↑ **Parent:** [Skein relation](skein-relation.md)

The oriented smoothing replaces a crossing by two disjoint arcs whose orientations agree with the four oriented ends. It is the zero-resolution in an oriented [skein relation](skein-relation.md).
