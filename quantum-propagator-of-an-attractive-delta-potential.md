# Quantum propagator of an attractive delta potential

↑ **Parent:** [Delta potential](delta-potential.md)

For $H=p^2/(2m)-g\delta(x)$ with $g>0$, put $\kappa=mg/\hbar^2$ and $T>0$. The [bound state in a delta potential](bound-state-in-a-delta-potential.md) has squared amplitude $\kappa$ at the origin. The even normalized continuum states have squared amplitude $k^2/[\pi(k^2+\kappa^2)]$ for $k>0$, and the odd states vanish there. The [spectral resolution of a quantum propagator](spectral-resolution-of-a-quantum-propagator.md) gives the displayed result. At long times, the continuum stationary point at $k=0$ is suppressed by its quadratic spectral weight: expanding that weight gives a continuum term $e^{-3i\pi/4}(2m/\hbar T)^{3/2}/(4\sqrt\pi\kappa^2)+O(T^{-5/2})$. The [bound state](bound-state.md) term persists with constant modulus. This [stationary phase](stationary-phase-method.md) expansion requires $\hbar\kappa^2T/(2m)\gg1$.
