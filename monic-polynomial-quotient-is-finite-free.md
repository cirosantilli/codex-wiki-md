# Monic polynomial quotient is finite free

↑ **Parent:** [Monic polynomial division over a ring](monic-polynomial-division-over-a-ring.md)

For any commutative [ring](ring.md) $A$ and a [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) $F$ of positive degree $D$, [monic polynomial division over a ring](monic-polynomial-division-over-a-ring.md) gives unique representatives of degree less than $D$. Thus the classes of $1,T,\ldots,T^{D-1}$ form a [basis of a module](basis-of-a-module.md) over $A$. This is a finite free algebra, hence flat, and the map $A\to A[T]/(F)$ is injective. The monic hypothesis makes leading-degree arguments valid even when $A$ has zero divisors.
