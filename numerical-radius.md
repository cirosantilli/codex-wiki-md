# Numerical radius

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical range of an operator](numerical-range-of-an-operator.md)

The radius is a scalar derived from the [numerical range of an operator](numerical-range-of-an-operator.md), rather than the range set itself.

For a bounded [linear operator](linear-operator.md) on a complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), the numerical radius is the supremum of the absolute values in its [numerical range of an operator](numerical-range-of-an-operator.md). It is a [norm](norm.md) equivalent to the [operator norm](operator-norm.md), with $w(T)\leq\|T\|\leq2w(T)$. For a self-adjoint operator the two [norms](norm.md) agree.
