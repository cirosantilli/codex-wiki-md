# Free-surface image of a rotlet dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Rotlet dipole](rotlet-dipole.md)

At a flat shear-free impermeable plane, a tangential [torque](torque.md) is reflected with opposite sign because [torque](torque.md) is an axial vector. A parallel axial [rotlet dipole](rotlet-dipole.md) therefore has an opposite signed moment at the reflected source. Its [velocity](velocity.md) vanishes at the point directly beneath the image, but its [velocity](velocity.md) gradient can turn the swimmer.
