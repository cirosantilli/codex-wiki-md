# Grazing clearance of an impulsively struck rod

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

A thin straight rod of [length](length.md) $l$, [mass](mass.md) $m$, central [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I=\beta ml^2$ and midpoint [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) starts beside a pointlike pole at that midpoint. An end [impulse](impulse.md) perpendicular to the rod gives translation $V=J/m$ and rotation $\Omega=J/(2\beta ml)$. At the first quarter-turn the trailing end is at horizontal distance $\pi\beta l-l/2$ from the pole. Strict clearance requires $\beta>1/(2\pi)$; equality is the grazing limit. The criterion is for free motion before contact, a negligibly thin rod and pole, and an impulse directed away from the pole.
