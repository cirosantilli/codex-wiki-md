# Invariant bilinear forms of odd dihedral doublets

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible complex representations of a finite dihedral group](irreducible-complex-representations-of-a-finite-dihedral-group.md)

For the [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of order $2m$ with odd $m$, let $D_k(r)=\operatorname{diag}(\zeta^k,\zeta^{-k})$ and $D_k(s)=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, where $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/m}$ and $1\leq k\leq(m-1)/2$. An [invariant tensor](invariant-tensor.md) defining a bilinear form satisfies $D_k(g)^TLD_k(g)=L$. The rotation forces $L_{11}=L_{22}=0$ and the reflection forces $L_{12}=L_{21}$. Thus the [invariant tensor](invariant-tensor.md) is a multiple of $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, and a singlet [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md) gives two equal physical masses by [singular value decomposition](singular-value-decomposition.md). Distinct inequivalent doublets admit no such bilinear form, by [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) and their self-duality.
