# Resonant transmission through a square well

↑ **Parent:** [Finite square well](finite-square-well.md)

For an attractive [finite square well](finite-square-well.md) of width $a$, outside wave number $k$ and inside wave number $q$, matching the [wavefunction](wave-function.md) and its first [derivative](derivative.md) at both endpoints gives the displayed [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md). It equals one when $qa$ is an integer multiple of $\pi$: the internal reflections cancel. For $q=2k$ the coefficient of $\sin^2(qa)$ is $9/16$.
