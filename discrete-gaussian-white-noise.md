# Discrete Gaussian white noise

↑ **Parent:** [White noise](white-noise.md)

Discrete [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) [white noise](white-noise.md) is a jointly [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) sequence with [expected value](expected-value.md) zero and [covariance](covariance.md) $\sigma^2$ at lag zero and zero at other lags. Joint normality makes the components [independent](independent-random-variables.md). This is a sequence of ordinary [random variables](random-variable-split.md), unlike the generalized continuous-time white-noise process.
