# Equivalence principle does not uniquely fix field equations

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein equivalence principle](einstein-equivalence-principle.md)

Universal metric coupling and local special-relativistic nongravitational physics do not select a unique dynamical metric [action](action.md). Additional assumptions are needed to obtain the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations-split.md). A local freely falling coordinate choice can remove connection coefficients at a point but cannot remove curvature or tidal effects over a finite region. Tests of [redshift](redshift.md), [free fall](free-fall.md) and self-gravitating dynamics therefore address related but distinct claims.
