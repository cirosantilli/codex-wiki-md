# Spin-one singlet state

↑ **Parent:** [Singlet state](singlet-state.md)

In the product basis $|m_1,m_2\rangle$ for two spin-one particles, total magnetic quantum number zero allows only $|1,-1\rangle$, $|0,0\rangle$ and $|-1,1\rangle$. Applying the total [ladder operators](ladder-operator.md) to $\alpha|1,-1\rangle+\beta|0,0\rangle+\gamma|-1,1\rangle$ gives

$$
S_+|\psi\rangle=\sqrt2\hbar[(\alpha+\beta)|1,0\rangle+(\beta+\gamma)|0,1\rangle].
$$

A [singlet state](singlet-state.md) is annihilated by $S_+$ and $S_-$, so $\alpha=\gamma=-\beta$. [Wavefunction normalization](wavefunction-normalization.md) gives the displayed state, up to an overall phase. Here the ket on the left of the title formula has total-spin labels, while its right-hand kets have individual magnetic labels. The state is symmetric under exchanging the particles, although no exchange constraint is needed for distinguishable particles.
