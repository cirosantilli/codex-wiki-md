# p-elementary group

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary group](elementary-group.md)

A p-elementary group is a [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) of a [p-group](p-group.md) and a cyclic group whose order is coprime to $p$. Either factor may be trivial. The coprime cyclic factor is central, a stronger requirement than merely having a cyclic subgroup with p-group quotient.
