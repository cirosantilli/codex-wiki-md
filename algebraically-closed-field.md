# Algebraically closed field

↑ **Parent:** [Field](field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraically_closed_field)

A field is algebraically closed when every nonconstant [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) over it has a root in it, equivalently when every such polynomial splits into linear factors.
