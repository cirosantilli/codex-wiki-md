# Eta and eta prime mesons

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoscalar meson nonet](pseudoscalar-meson-nonet.md)

These neutral isospin-zero [pseudoscalar mesons](pseudoscalar-meson.md) have $J^{PC}=0^{-+}$. Their light-quark components mix the [Eta octet state](eta-octet-state.md) and [eta singlet state](eta-singlet-state.md). The singlet axial anomaly contributes to the unusually large eta-prime mass, while flavour breaking also affects both masses and the mixing. The simple orthogonal one-angle flavour model is useful for explaining the nonet; detailed decay-constant descriptions can require more than one parameter.
