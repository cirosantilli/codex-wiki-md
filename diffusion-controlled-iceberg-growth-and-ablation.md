# Diffusion-controlled iceberg growth and ablation

↑ **Parent:** [Saline Stefan problem](saline-stefan-problem.md)

For a planar ice-ocean interface with no fluid motion, similarity places the boundary at $a(t)=2\lambda\sqrt{Dt}$. The sign of $\lambda$ distinguishes ice growth from ablation, while rejected salt during growth can cause [constitutional supercooling](constitutional-supercooling.md) in the adjacent ocean.
