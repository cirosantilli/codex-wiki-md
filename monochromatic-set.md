# Monochromatic set

↑ **Parent:** [Finite coloring](finite-coloring.md)

A [subset](subset.md) $A\subseteq X$ is monochromatic for a [finite coloring](finite-coloring.md) $c:X\to C$ if there is a color $i\in C$ such that

$$
c(a)=i\qquad\text{for every }a\in A.
$$

Equivalently, the [restriction of a function](restriction-mathematics.md) $c\restriction A$ is a [constant function](constant-function.md), or $A$ lies in one [colour class](colour-class.md) $c^{-1}(i)$. The word describes agreement of colors; it does not require $A$ to equal an entire [colour class](colour-class.md).

For example, color the [integers](integer-split.md) by [integer parity](parity-mathematics.md), using red for even and blue for odd. The [set](set-split.md) $\{2,4,8\}$ is [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md), whereas $\{2,3\}$ is not. If the colored objects are [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md), a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) means that its three [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) form a [monochromatic set](monochromatic-set.md). In a coloring of $r$-element [subsets](subset.md) of a [set](set-split.md) $S$, a [homogeneous set for a colouring](homogeneous-set-for-a-colouring.md) $H\subseteq S$ means that the collection of all $r$-element [subsets](subset.md) of $H$ is [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md). For pairs, this is a [complete graph](complete-graph.md) on $H$ whose [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) all have one color, rather than a coloring of the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in $H$.
