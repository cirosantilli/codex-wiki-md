# Stress characteristics for an anisotropic yield curve

↑ **Parent:** [Slip-line field theory](slip-line-field-theory.md)

Parameterize a fixed [yield surface](yield-surface.md) by stress-space [arc length](arc-length.md) $l$ with $\xi_{,l}=-\cos2\phi$ and $\tau_{,l}=-\sin2\phi$. For mean normal stress $\sigma$, [force balance](force-balance.md) gives $\nabla\sigma=M\nabla l$, where $M=\begin{pmatrix}\cos2\phi&\sin2\phi\\\sin2\phi&-\cos2\phi\end{pmatrix}$. Its eigenvectors along angles $\phi$ and $\phi+\pi/2$ have [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $+1$ and $-1$, proving the displayed [slip line](slip-line.md) invariants.
