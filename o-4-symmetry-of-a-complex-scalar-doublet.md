<h1 id="o-4-symmetry-of-a-complex-scalar-doublet">O(4) symmetry of a complex scalar doublet</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Internal symmetry of a classical field theory](internal-symmetry-of-a-classical-field-theory.md)

Two canonically normalized [complex scalar fields](complex-scalar-field.md) with potential $V=m^2\Phi^\dagger\Phi+\lambda(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)^2$ are four real fields with a radial potential. Consequently the isolated action is invariant under the [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) $O(4)$, not only under complex-linear $U(2)$ transformations. Writing $\phi_1=(q_1+iq_2)/\sqrt2$ and $\phi_2=(q_3+iq_4)/\sqrt2$ makes the kinetic term $\sum_A(\partial q_A)^2/2$ and the potential a function of $\sum_Aq_A^2$. The six real-plane rotations give currents $j_{AB}^\mu=q_B\partial^\mu q_A-q_A\partial^\mu q_B$. Gauge or Yukawa couplings can break this larger symmetry, so it should not be asserted for an arbitrary interacting doublet.
