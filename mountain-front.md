# Mountain front

↑ **Parent:** [Geomorphology](geomorphology.md)

The boundary between a mountain block and adjacent lower terrain. Its geometry records interacting [tectonic uplift](tectonic-uplift.md), [faulting](faulting.md), erosion and deposition. A straight front, [triangular facets](triangular-facet.md) and displaced [alluvial fans](alluvial-fan.md) together can support an active [normal fault](normal-fault.md) interpretation.
