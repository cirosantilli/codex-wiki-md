# Scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Locally ringed space](locally-ringed-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheme_(mathematics))

A scheme is a [locally ringed space](locally-ringed-space.md) covered by open subsets isomorphic to spectra of [commutative rings](commutative-ring.md). It retains both the points defined by prime ideals and the local algebra of functions around them.
