↑ **Parent:** [Bézier curve](bezier-curve.md)

[De Casteljau's algorithm](de-casteljau-s-algorithm.md) repeatedly replaces neighboring control points by $(1-t)B_j+tB_{j+1}$ until a single point remains. The same triangular construction gives both subcurves split at $t$.
