<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [amphiphile](../../../../../amphiphile.md) contains a solvent-compatible head and a solvent-avoiding tail. In water, aggregation hides hydrocarbon tails while leaving heads exposed. This reduces the free-energy cost of tail–water contact, competing with loss of [translational entropy](../../../../../translational-entropy.md), headgroup repulsion and chain packing. A [micelle](../../../../../micelle.md) is a finite equilibrium aggregate, not necessarily a permanently bound particle.

Equality of molecular [chemical potentials](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) controls aggregation. For a large preferred aggregation number, $\mu_1=\mu_1^0+k_BT\ln x_1$ approaches an aggregate value $\mu_*^0$, giving

$$
\boxed{x_{\rm CMC}\simeq\exp[(\mu_*^0-\mu_1^0)/(k_BT)].}
$$

Above the [critical micelle concentration](../../../../../critical-micelle-concentration.md), added [surfactant](../../../../../surfactant.md) mainly increases aggregate population while [monomer](../../../../../monomer.md) activity changes relatively little. Finite aggregation numbers give a smooth crossover rather than an exact bulk phase transition. The [amphiphile packing parameter](../../../../../amphiphile-packing-parameter.md) $p=v/(a_0l_c)$ compares tail volume, preferred head area and chain length; small $p$ favours spherical [micelles](../../../../../micelle.md), while larger values favour less curved aggregates. These geometric criteria are approximate, not universal shape phase boundaries.[1976, packing/free-energy model of generic hydrocarbon surfactant aggregates](../../../../../theory-of-self-assembly-of-hydrocarbon-amphiphiles-by-jacob-n-israelachvili-and-others.md)

Surface-tension or conductivity changes with [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) locate an operational CMC; scattering probes size and aggregation number. [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), impurities and salt must be controlled, and independent methods help distinguish aggregation from precipitation. In an ionic [surfactant](../../../../../surfactant.md), salt screens headgroup repulsion and can change both the CMC and aggregate shape. This discussion concerns generic synthetic [surfactants](../../../../../surfactant.md) only.
