<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Young's equation](../../../../../young-s-equation.md) gives $\gamma_{SV}-\gamma_{SL}=\gamma_{LV}\cos\theta$. The [interfacial-energy difference from two contact angles](../../../../../interfacial-energy-difference-from-two-contact-angles.md) follows by subtracting the two equilibrium contact-angle equations, which eliminates the common solid–air energy:

$$
\boxed{\gamma_{S,\mathrm{water}}-\gamma_{S,\mathrm{decane}}
=\gamma_{\mathrm{decane},V}\cos25^\circ-\gamma_{\mathrm{water},V}\cos102^\circ
\simeq3.67\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-2}}.}
$$

The water–solid interface has the larger energy. This assumes the same clean solid surface and equilibrium angles, without swelling or strong [contact-angle hysteresis](../../../../../contact-angle-hysteresis.md).
