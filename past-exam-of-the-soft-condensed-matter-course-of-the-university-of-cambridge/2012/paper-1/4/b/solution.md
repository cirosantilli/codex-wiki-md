<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [relaxation modulus](../../../../../relaxation-modulus.md) is measured by imposing a step of [strain](../../../../../strain.md) and then holding that [strain](../../../../../strain.md) fixed. Let its amplitude be $\epsilon_0$, so the initial [stress](../../../../../stress.md) jump is $\sigma_0=G_0\epsilon_0$. For $t>0$, $\dot\epsilon=0$ and the [Maxwell model](../../../../../linear-maxwell-fluid.md) gives

$$
\dot\sigma=-\frac{G_0}{\eta}\sigma,
\qquad\sigma(t)=\sigma_0e^{-t/\tau},\qquad
\boxed{G(t)=\frac{\sigma(t)}{\epsilon_0}=G_0e^{-t/\tau},\quad\tau=\eta/G_0.}
$$

The initial [stress](../../../../../stress.md) in the wording must be understood as that produced by the initial deformation. If an external apparatus instead maintains $\sigma=\sigma_0$, the [stress](../../../../../stress.md) does not decay: the experiment is creep, considered below. Merely specifying an initial [stress](../../../../../stress.md) without a subsequent constraint would not uniquely prescribe the relaxation experiment.
