<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For a thermally elastic network, estimate one elastically active element per mesh [volume](../../../../../volume.md). Each contributes an elastic free-energy scale of order $k_BT$, giving the [entropic network modulus](../../../../../entropic-network-modulus.md)

$$
\boxed{G_0\sim\frac{k_BT}{m^3}\simeq3.3\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Pa}}
$$

at $T=298\,\mathrm K$. Mesh size alone would not determine the modulus of an arbitrary bending-dominated or athermal network; this is the entropic scaling assumption needed for the estimate.

The formal elastic intercept in the given model is consequently

$$
\boxed{\langle r^2\rangle_{\rm plateau}\sim\frac{m^3}{\pi a}
=3.98\times10^{-14}\,\mathrm{m^2}=0.0398\,\mu\mathrm m^2.}
$$

The corresponding three-dimensional root-mean-square [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) is about $0.20\,\mu\mathrm m$. The [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) cancels from this estimate because the assumed modulus is itself proportional to $T$. This is the extrapolated plateau from the preceding part, not a nonzero exact zero-lag [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md). Here $m/a=1/2$, so a continuum estimate is only approximate.

In [passive microrheology](../../../../../passive-microrheology.md), track dilute inert probe spheres by video microscopy and average squared lag [displacements](../../../../../displacement.md) over starting [times](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) and probes. An isotropic two-dimensional image gives $\langle\Delta r^2\rangle_{3d}=(3/2)\langle\Delta r^2\rangle_{2d}$. Alternatively, [dynamic light scattering](../../../../../dynamic-light-scattering.md) probes ensemble [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) statistics; the original optical [microrheology](../../../../../microrheology.md) study compared inferred storage and loss moduli with mechanical measurements.[Physical Review Letters 74 (1995), p. 1250, abstract: dynamic light scattering and comparison with mechanical moduli](../../../../../optical-measurements-of-frequency-dependent-linear-viscoelastic-moduli-of-complex-fluids-by-t-g-mason-and-d-a-weitz.md)

Remove drift, calibrate spatial scale and frame timing, estimate localization noise using an immobilized-bead control, and account for motion blur. Keep probes away from walls and check that changing probe [radius](../../../../../radius.md) or tracer concentration does not change the inferred bulk response unexpectedly. Noise alone can generate a false short-time intercept, while adsorption or confinement can mimic [elasticity](../../../../../elasticity-physics-split.md). No numerical long-time slope is fixed because a numerical [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) is not supplied.
