<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In a dilute solution, separate coils diffuse through solvent with long-ranged hydrodynamic coupling among their segments. The [Zimm model](../../../../../zimm-model.md) gives whole-chain diffusion

$$
\boxed{D_{\rm dilute}\sim\frac{k_BT}{\eta_sR_H}\propto N^{-\nu},\qquad\nu\simeq3/5,}
$$

where $R_H$ is a [hydrodynamic radius](../../../../../hydrodynamic-radius.md) and $\eta_s$ the solvent [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md). The coil continually changes shape while its [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) performs long-time [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md); treating it as a rigid sphere is only a scaling analogy.

At high concentration, solvent-mediated [hydrodynamic interactions](../../../../../hydrodynamic-interaction.md) are screened on sufficiently large scales and [times](../../../../../time-in-physics.md). If chains are not entangled, additive local or blob friction gives [Rouse model](../../../../../rouse-model.md) behaviour, $D\propto N^{-1}$ at fixed concentration. For long entangled chains, neighboring chains prevent rapid transverse escape. In the [reptation](../../../../../reptation.md) tube picture the chain moves mainly along its contour until it renews the surrounding tube. Contour diffusion scales as $N^{-1}$, the tube-renewal [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) as $N^3$, and the random-coil size squared as $N$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{D_{\rm entangled}\sim\frac{R^2}{\tau_d}\propto N^{-2}.}
$$

Both cases ultimately give diffusive centre-of-mass motion, but entangled concentrated chains move much more slowly and retain constraints for longer. The inequality $\phi\gg\phi^*$ establishes strong overlap, not by itself entanglement: the latter also requires sufficiently many segments relative to an entanglement [length](../../../../../length.md). No numerical entanglement scale or concentrated-solution [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) is supplied, so only the conditional regimes and scaling exponents can be fixed here.
