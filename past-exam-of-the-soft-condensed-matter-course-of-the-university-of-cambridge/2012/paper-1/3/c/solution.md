<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Overlap means roughly one coil per coil [volume](../../../../../volume.md). With segment [volume](../../../../../volume.md) $a^3$, the corresponding [polymer overlap volume fraction](../../../../../polymer-overlap-volume-fraction.md) is

$$
\phi^*\sim\frac{Na^3}{R_g^3}.
$$

For a strong [good solvent](../../../../../good-solvent.md), $R_g\sim aN^{3/5}$, and hence

$$
\boxed{\phi^*\sim N^{-4/5}=(10^4)^{-4/5}\simeq6.3\times10^{-4},}
$$

or about **0.063% by [volume](../../../../../volume.md)** in the unit-prefactor scaling convention. The segment [length](../../../../../length.md) cancels. A coil has no sharply defined boundary, so using $4\pi R_g^3/3$ or retaining a size amplitude changes an order-one prefactor. This calculation is a scaling estimate, not an exact volume-fraction measurement.
