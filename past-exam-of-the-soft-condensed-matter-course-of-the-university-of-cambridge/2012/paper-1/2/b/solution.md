<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a clean liquid interface at fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and composition, [surface tension](../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) is the reversible free-energy cost per added [area](../../../../../surface-area.md), $\gamma=(\partial F/\partial A)_{T,V,N}$, so $dF=\gamma\,dA$. Its units are both $\mathrm{J\,m^{-2}}$ and $\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$. Molecules at an interface have a different environment from those in the bulk; creating more interface changes their interactions and [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md). For constant $\gamma$, the interfacial [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is $\gamma A$ up to a reference constant. This is not generally identical to the internal [surface energy](../../../../../surface-energy.md): the interfacial [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) density is $-d\gamma/dT$, giving internal-energy density $\gamma-T\,d\gamma/dT$.

A line of [length](../../../../../length.md) $\ell$ in one interface transmits a tangential [force](../../../../../force-split.md) $\gamma\ell$, perpendicular to the line. A soap film has two interfaces, giving [force](../../../../../force-split.md) $2\gamma\ell$ on a sliding rim. Quasistatic extension by $dx$ creates total [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) $2\ell dx$, recovering that [force](../../../../../force-split.md) from $dF=2\gamma\ell dx$. Uniform positive [surface tension](../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) favors minimum [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) at fixed [volume](../../../../../volume.md), hence an isolated spherical droplet when gravity is negligible. [curvature](../../../../../curvature.md) gives the [Young–Laplace equation](../../../../../young-laplace-equation.md), with outward normal chosen consistently:

$$
\boxed{\Delta p=\gamma\left(\frac1{R_1}+\frac1{R_2}\right).}
$$

A spherical liquid drop has $\Delta p=2\gamma/R$; a thin soap bubble with two similar interfaces has approximately $4\gamma/R$. The [capillary length](../../../../../capillary-length.md) $\sqrt{\gamma/(\Delta\rho g)}$ compares gravity with capillarity. [surface tension](../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) also restores [capillary waves](../../../../../capillary-wave.md), rather than providing a bulk static [shear modulus](../../../../../shear-modulus.md).

Interfacial contamination and added [surfactants](../../../../../surfactant.md) can change $\gamma$ substantially; a nonuniform value produces tangential [Marangoni stress](../../../../../marangoni-effect.md). A rim-force measurement should therefore control [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), cleanliness and the number of interfaces. Capillary-rise methods additionally need the [contact angle](../../../../../contact-angle.md), because the vertical component is proportional to $\gamma\cos\theta$. An independent frame-force measurement can distinguish a contact-angle change from a true change of [surface tension](../../../../../surface-tension-split.md).
