<h1 id="section-b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Flory–Huggins solution theory](../../../../../flory-huggins-solution-theory.md) treats a [polymer solution](../../../../../polymer-solution.md) as an incompressible lattice mixture, with one [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md) occupying one site and one chain occupying $N$ connected sites. Thus the supplied $f$ is naturally the mixing [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per lattice site; if a site has volume $v_0$, the free-energy density is $f/v_0$.

The term $k_BT(\phi/N)\ln\phi$ is the [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) translational [entropy of mixing](../../../../../entropy-of-mixing.md) contribution. There are only $1/N$ as many independently translating chains as [monomers](../../../../../monomer.md), which explains the factor $1/N$. The term $k_BT(1-\phi)\ln(1-\phi)$ is the [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) [entropy of mixing](../../../../../entropy-of-mixing.md) contribution. Both lower the mixing [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) for $0<\phi<1$.

The term $k_BT\chi\phi(1-\phi)$ is the mean-field excess interaction contribution from replacing like contacts with [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md)–[solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) contacts. In the simplest energetic lattice model,

$$
\chi=\frac{z}{k_BT}\left[\epsilon_{ps}-\frac{\epsilon_{pp}+\epsilon_{ss}}2\right],
$$

where $z$ counts neighboring contacts and the $\epsilon_{ij}$ are pair contact energies. Positive $\chi$ penalizes unlike contacts and can favor demixing. Because chain connectivity suppresses the [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md)'s translational mixing [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md), relatively modest unfavorable interactions can cause separation of long chains. More general effective $\chi$ can also include entropic effects.
