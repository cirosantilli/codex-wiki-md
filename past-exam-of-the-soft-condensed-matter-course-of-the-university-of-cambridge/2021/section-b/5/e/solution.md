<h1 id="section-b/5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

One method is to give every particle a sufficiently large [surface charge density](../../../../../surface-charge-density.md) of the same sign. For example, attach ionizable sulfonate groups covalently to the surface. Dissociation leaves negatively charged surface groups and releases mobile positive [counterions](../../../../../counterion.md) into the water. A [surfactant](../../../../../surfactant.md) with a strongly bound head group can provide another charging route, provided it does not desorb during the experiment.

Each charged particle is surrounded by an [electric double layer](../../../../../electric-double-layer.md). As two such particles approach, overlap of these ionic clouds raises the electrostatic and ionic [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md), producing repulsion. In a dilute electrolyte, the range is set by the [Debye–Hückel screening length](../../../../../debye-huckel-screening-length.md),

$$
\lambda_D=\kappa^{-1},\qquad
\kappa^2=\frac{e^2}{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r k_BT}\sum_i z_i^2 n_i.
$$

For identical weakly charged spheres a representative [screened Coulomb pair potential](../../../../../screened-coulomb-pair-potential.md) is

$$
U_{\mathrm{el}}(R)\simeq\frac{Q^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r(1+\kappa a)^2R}
\,e^{-\kappa(R-2a)},\qquad R\geq2a.
$$

Its sum with the [Van der Waals force](../../../../../van-der-waals-force.md) attraction is the [DLVO theory](../../../../../dlvo-theory.md) pair potential. Choose the charging level so that the repulsive barrier is many $k_BT$ high; particles then rarely reach the primary attractive well during the observation time. This is kinetic stabilization rather than removal of all attraction.

Deionized water is useful because low added [ionic strength](../../../../../ionic-strength.md) gives a long-ranged repulsion. It does not mean that the charged dispersion contains no [ions](../../../../../ion.md): the surface's own [counterions](../../../../../counterion.md) are unavoidable and contribute to screening. Surface dissociation, contamination and particle concentration therefore matter even without added salt. An alternative valid route is a dense, firmly attached layer of water-soluble [polyethylene glycol](../../../../../polyethylene-glycol.md), producing [steric stabilization](../../../../../steric-stabilization.md) without relying on a large electrostatic barrier.
