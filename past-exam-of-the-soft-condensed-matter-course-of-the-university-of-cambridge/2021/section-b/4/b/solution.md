<h1 id="section-b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [dynamic modulus](../../../../../dynamic-modulus.md), or [complex viscoelastic modulus](../../../../../dynamic-modulus.md), is the ratio of complex [stress](../../../../../stress.md) amplitude to complex [strain](../../../../../strain.md) amplitude in the periodic steady response:

$$
\sigma(t)=\Re[\widehat\sigma e^{i\omega t}],\qquad
\epsilon(t)=\Re[\widehat\epsilon e^{i\omega t}],\qquad
\boxed{G^*(\omega)=\frac{\widehat\sigma}{\widehat\epsilon}=G'+iG''.}
$$

This defines the sign convention; for passive response the [loss modulus](../../../../../loss-modulus.md) is nonnegative at positive frequency. A [Newtonian fluid](../../../../../newtonian-fluid.md) obeys $\sigma=\eta\dot\epsilon$, while a [Hooke's law](../../../../../hooke-s-law.md) solid obeys $\sigma=G_0\epsilon$. Hence

$$
\boxed{G^*_{\mathrm{Newtonian}}=i\omega\eta,\qquad
G^*_{\mathrm{Hookean}}=G_0.}
$$

The first is purely dissipative and the second purely elastic. Here $\epsilon$ denotes the scalar deformation used in the problem, with any shear-versus-extension geometric factors already included in its modulus and viscosity.
