The ordinary physics is [Stokes settling velocity](../../../../../stokes-settling-velocity.md). For a generic isolated rigid [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) of radius $a$, particle [mass density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho_p$ and surrounding-fluid [mass density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho_f$, balance the buoyancy-corrected body [force](../../../../../force-split.md) against [Stokes law](../../../../../stokes-s-law.md):

$$
\frac{4\pi a^3}{3}(\rho_p-\rho_f)g_{\rm eff}=6\pi\eta aU,
\qquad U=\frac{2a^2(\rho_p-\rho_f)g_{\rm eff}}{9\eta}.
$$

Here $\eta$ is the [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md). If the effective acceleration is approximately constant over the displacement and the terminal regime has been reached, $s=Ut$. In an actual centrifuge $g_{\rm eff}=\Omega^2r$, so a sufficiently large radial change requires integrating $dr/dt\propto r$ instead of assuming constant $U$. This generic relation does not resolve the deferred biological application.
