<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [amphiphile packing parameter](../../../../../amphiphile-packing-parameter.md) $P=v/(a_0\ell_c)$, where $v$ is tail [volume](../../../../../volume.md), $a_0$ the effective head-group [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) and $\ell_c$ the maximum tail extension. The conventional cylindrical-[micelle](../../../../../micelle.md) window is $1/3\lesssim P\lesssim1/2$. Its upper limit follows directly from [cylindrical micelle geometry](../../../../../cylindrical-micelle-geometry.md): a long core of radius $R$ has volume-to-area ratio $R/2$, so $v/a_0=R/2\leq\ell_c/2$. The spherical packing limit is $P=1/3$; above it a filled spherical core cannot accommodate the assumed head-group [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) and chain length.

Inverting both positive inequalities gives

$$
\frac{2v}{\ell_c}\lesssim a_0\lesssim\frac{3v}{\ell_c},\qquad
\boxed{0.246\,\mathrm{nm^2}\lesssim a_0\lesssim0.369\,\mathrm{nm^2}.}
$$

Thus the useful estimate is **a head-group area of approximately $0.25$–$0.37\,\mathrm{nm^2}$**. If phase-boundary configurations are excluded, use strict inequalities. These endpoints are geometric morphology crossovers, rather than exact thermodynamic phase boundaries: chain conformations and head-group interactions also affect which [micelle](../../../../../micelle.md) minimizes the [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). The supplied molecular [volume](../../../../../volume.md) is interpreted as the tail-core [volume](../../../../../volume.md) required by this packing model.[1976, aggregate packing theory; cylindrical and spherical core constraints](../../../../../theory-of-self-assembly-of-hydrocarbon-amphiphiles-by-jacob-n-israelachvili-and-others.md)
