<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an equilibrium [contact angle](../../../../../contact-angle.md) measured through the [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md), [Young's equation](../../../../../young-s-equation.md) balances the tangential interfacial forces:

$$
\gamma_{SV}-\gamma_{SL}=\gamma_{LV}\cos\theta.
$$

The pure-[ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) measurement therefore fixes the solid–vapour [surface energy](../../../../../surface-energy.md) as

$$
\gamma_{SV}=(21.0+21.4\cos23^\circ)\times10^{-3}
=40.699\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}.
$$

**The inferred surface energy is $40.7\,\mathrm{mJ\,m^{-2}}$**, equivalently $40.7\,\mathrm{mN\,m^{-1}}$. The equality of these units follows because creating interfacial [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) costs [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).

For the equal-fraction [mixture](../../../../../mixture-split.md), the stated linear relations give $\gamma_{SL}=20.5\,\mathrm{mN\,m^{-1}}$ and $\gamma_{LV}=28.5\,\mathrm{mN\,m^{-1}}$. Holding the solid–vapour [surface energy](../../../../../surface-energy.md) fixed,

$$
\cos\theta_{1/2}=\frac{40.699-20.5}{28.5}=0.70873,
\qquad \boxed{\theta_{1/2}\simeq44.9^\circ.}
$$

The small decrease in $\gamma_{SL}$ increases the numerator of [Young's equation](../../../../../young-s-equation.md), but the increase in $\gamma_{LV}$ has the larger relative effect and reduces $\cos\theta$. Consequently **the contact angle increases and the mixture wets the surface less well**. This inference uses equilibrium [wetting](../../../../../wetting.md) on an unchanged smooth solid; a changing surface composition or [contact-angle hysteresis](../../../../../contact-angle-hysteresis.md) would require additional measurements.
