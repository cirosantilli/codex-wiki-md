<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) [permittivity](../../../../../permittivity-split.md), the [Debye–Hückel screening length](../../../../../debye-huckel-screening-length.md) satisfies $\lambda_D^{-2}\propto\sum_i c_i z_i^2=2I$, where $I=\tfrac12\sum_i c_i z_i^2$ is [ionic strength](../../../../../ionic-strength.md). Fully dissociated [sodium chloride](../../../../../sodium-chloride.md) has two monovalent [ion](../../../../../ion.md) species, giving $I_1=0.01$ M. Fully dissociated [magnesium sulfate](../../../../../magnesium-sulfate.md) has two divalent species, giving

$$
I_2=\tfrac12[0.1(2)^2+0.1(-2)^2]=0.40\,\mathrm M.
$$

The [multivalent-electrolyte screening-length ratio](../../../../../multivalent-electrolyte-screening-length-ratio.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\lambda_{D,2}=3\,\mathrm{nm}\sqrt{\frac{0.01}{0.40}}=0.474\,\mathrm{nm}\simeq0.47\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

The squared valences are essential: counting only the total [ion](../../../../../ion.md) [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) misses a factor of four in the screening strength per formula unit. This is the comparable linear-screening estimate, assuming unchanged dielectric response and full dissociation. [ion pairs](../../../../../ion-pair.md) and concentrated-electrolyte nonideality can make a real divalent-salt solution depart from it; no such correction is specified.
