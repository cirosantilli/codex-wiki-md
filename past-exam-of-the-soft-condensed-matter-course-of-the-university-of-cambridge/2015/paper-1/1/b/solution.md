<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a long [ideal chain](../../../../../ideal-chain.md) of $N$ statistical segments of [length](../../../../../length.md) $b$, $R_G^2=Nb^2/6$. At fixed repeat chemistry and statistical segment [length](../../../../../length.md), [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md) is proportional to $N$. The [molecular-weight scaling of an ideal-chain gyration radius](../../../../../molecular-weight-scaling-of-an-ideal-chain-gyration-radius.md) thus gives

$$
\boxed{R_G(10^6)=120\,\mathrm{nm}\sqrt{\frac{10^6}{10^5}}=379.5\,\mathrm{nm}\simeq3.8\times10^2\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

The supplied size and ideal statistics are used as stipulated; an independently tabulated coil size would not replace the given baseline.

[solvent quality](../../../../../solvent-quality.md) changes the balance of segment–segment and segment–[solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) contacts. In a [good solvent](../../../../../good-solvent.md), excluded-volume repulsion swells a sufficiently long isolated chain, with $R_G\propto N^\nu$, $\nu\simeq0.588$ in three dimensions; the [Flory approximation](../../../../../flory-approximation.md) gives $\nu=3/5$. At theta conditions the effective two-segment [excluded volume](../../../../../excluded-volume.md) vanishes and the ideal exponent $1/2$ is recovered for long chains. A [poor solvent](../../../../../poor-solvent.md) favors a compact [polymer globule](../../../../../polymer-globule.md), whose large-chain size scales approximately as $N^{1/3}$ at fixed internal [density](../../../../../density.md). [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) can also change local [persistence length](../../../../../persistence-length.md) and hence the prefactor. These different conformations need not have the same radius at the same [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md).
