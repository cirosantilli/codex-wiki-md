<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

By definition, the [relaxation modulus](../../../../relaxation-modulus.md) is the [stress](../../../../stress.md) generated by a unit step of [strain](../../../../strain.md) from an initially relaxed state. Set $\epsilon(t)=H(t)$, where $H$ is the [Heaviside step function](../../../../heaviside-step-function.md), and write $\sigma(t)=G(t)$. Integrating the [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) across the step shows that neither $G$ nor $G'$ can jump: a jump would produce a [Dirac delta function](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) or its [derivative](../../../../derivative.md), absent from the forcing. Hence $G(0^+)=G'(0^+)=0$.

For $t>0$, a particular solution is $1$. The [characteristic equation](../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) factors as

$$
15\lambda^2+8\lambda+1=(5\lambda+1)(3\lambda+1)=0,
$$

so $G=1+A e^{-t/5}+B e^{-t/3}$. The initial conditions give $A+B=-1$ and $A/5+B/3=0$, whence

$$
\boxed{G(t)=\left(1-\frac52e^{-t/5}+\frac32e^{-t/3}\right)H(t).}
$$

The time and modulus units are those implicit in the printed coefficients. A [Laplace transform](../../../../laplace-transform.md) check gives $\widehat G(s)=1/[s(5s+1)(3s+1)]$.

The original PDF really does put undifferentiated [strain](../../../../strain.md) on the right-hand side. The resulting response rises from zero to one, with $G'(t)=\tfrac12(e^{-t/5}-e^{-t/3})>0$, rather than exhibiting ordinary [stress relaxation](../../../../stress-relaxation.md). There is a stronger physical problem if this is intended as a passive material law: with the convention $e^{i\omega t}$ the [stress](../../../../stress.md)-to-[strain](../../../../strain.md) amplitude ratio is

$$
G^*(\omega)=\frac1{1-15\omega^2+8i\omega},\qquad
G''(\omega)=\frac{-8\omega}{(1-15\omega^2)^2+64\omega^2}<0\quad(\omega>0).
$$

The [loss modulus](../../../../loss-modulus.md) would give negative cycle-averaged supplied power $\langle\sigma\dot\epsilon\rangle=\omega G''|\epsilon_0|^2/2$, contrary to [passivity in linear viscoelasticity](../../../../passivity-in-linear-viscoelasticity.md). The boxed expression is therefore the formal [relaxation modulus](../../../../relaxation-modulus.md) of the equation as printed, not a claim that this equation describes an ordinary passive [viscoelasticity](../../../../viscoelasticity.md) model. No derivative of the input has been silently inserted.
