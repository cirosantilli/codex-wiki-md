<h1 id="section-b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a [magnesium chloride](../../../../../magnesium-chloride.md) [chemical solution](../../../../../solution-chemistry.md), the principal pair interactions are [hard sphere](../../../../../hard-sphere.md) exclusion, repulsion of overlapping [electric double layers](../../../../../electric-double-layer.md), and attractive [Van der Waals forces](../../../../../van-der-waals-force.md). The positive surface attracts [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md) [counterions](../../../../../counterion.md) and repels [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md) [coions](../../../../../coion.md); mobile [ions](../../../../../ion.md) screen the bare [Coulomb's law](../../../../../coulomb-s-law.md) interaction. The competition between screened electrostatic repulsion and [Van der Waals force](../../../../../van-der-waals-force.md) attraction is described by [DLVO theory](../../../../../dlvo-theory.md).

For well-separated identical weakly charged spheres, the approximate repulsive pair [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md), at centre separation $R\geq2a$, is

$$
U_{\mathrm{el}}(R)\simeq\frac{Q^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r}
\frac{e^{-\kappa(R-2a)}}{(1+\kappa a)^2R}.
$$

It is a [screened Coulomb pair potential](../../../../../screened-coulomb-pair-potential.md). An attractive [Hamaker constant](../../../../../hamaker-constant.md) adds a negative short-range contribution, while overlap is forbidden. Very near contact, [solvation](../../../../../solvation.md) and molecular surface structure can also matter. No polymer-induced interaction should be assumed before polymer is added in part (f).
