<h1 id="section-b/6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

First hold the surface [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) fixed and increase the bulk salt concentration. Since $\kappa^2\propto c$, the [Debye–Hückel screening length](../../../../../debye-huckel-screening-length.md) decreases as $c^{-1/2}$. For surface gap $h=R-2a\geq0$,

$$
U_{\mathrm{el}}(R)\propto\frac{e^{-\kappa h}}{(1+\kappa a)^2},
\qquad
\frac{\partial\log U_{\mathrm{el}}}{\partial\kappa}
=-h-\frac{2a}{1+\kappa a}<0.
$$

Thus both the range and the strength of repulsion decrease. In the repulsive [phase diagram](../../../../../phase-diagram.md), the sample moves downwards at fixed [volume fraction](../../../../../volume-fraction.md) and can melt from a [crystal](../../../../../crystal.md) into a fluid. Because screening length is already a diagram axis, changing salt moves the state through that diagram rather than independently changing its fixed-charge coexistence curves. If [Van der Waals forces](../../../../../van-der-waals-force.md) become dominant, however, adding salt may instead destabilize the dispersion and cause aggregation. These are distinct physical regimes.

Second increase $\sigma$ at fixed salt concentration and particle size. The [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) is $Q=4\pi a^2\sigma$, so the pair repulsion increases approximately as $\sigma^2$ within the [Debye–Hückel approximation](../../../../../debye-huckel-approximation.md), while the reservoir screening length remains unchanged. Freezing then becomes possible at lower [volume fraction](../../../../../volume-fraction.md) or shorter screening length. At fixed $\lambda_D$, the coexistence curves shift towards lower $\eta$; a previously fluid sample may crystallize.

These statements assume fixed, uniform charge and a salt reservoir dominating the screening. Strong charging requires nonlinear screening and possibly charge regulation. At very low added salt or appreciable particle concentration, particle-released [counterions](../../../../../counterion.md) also contribute to [ionic strength](../../../../../ionic-strength.md), so the screening length is no longer independently fixed by the added salt alone.
