<h1 id="section-b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The gap is not a fixed mechanical separation. [Thermal fluctuations](../../../../../thermal-fluctuation.md) of the wall compete with drift towards the [microtubule](../../../../../microtubule.md) under the opposing [force](../../../../../force-split.md). A [monomer](../../../../../monomer.md) can be incorporated only after the gap reaches its length. The [Smoluchowski equation](../../../../../smoluchowski-equation.md) describes this overdamped drift and [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) of gap [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md).

For gap potential [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $U(x)=Fx$, the equation and its [probability current](../../../../../probability-current.md) are

$$
\partial_t p=-\partial_xJ,
\qquad J=-D\left(\partial_xp+\beta U'(x)p\right)
=-D(p_x+\beta Fp),\qquad \beta=(k_BT)^{-1}.
$$

The drift mobility is $D\beta$, the overdamped consequence of the [fluctuation-dissipation relation for a Langevin particle](../../../../../fluctuation-dissipation-relation-for-a-langevin-particle.md). Equivalently, this coefficient is fixed by requiring the zero-current solution to be the [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) $p\propto e^{-\beta U}$. Solve this equation with the appropriate exclusion, incorporation and probability-normalization conditions. The steady flux through the incorporation threshold gives the addition rate $j_0$; the elongation speed is $v=\delta j_0$.

There is a regime distinction in the printed problem. The absorbing boundary imposed in part (c) means instantaneous incorporation once a gap opens, and leads to the [diffusion-limited Brownian ratchet with constant load](../../../../../diffusion-limited-brownian-ratchet-with-constant-load.md). A genuinely [reaction-limited polymerization Brownian ratchet](../../../../../reaction-limited-polymerization-brownian-ratchet.md) instead assumes fast gap equilibration followed by a finite chemical addition rate, giving an exponential gating factor multiplying that chemical rate. The equations requested in the subsequent parts specify the former model, which is the one solved here.
