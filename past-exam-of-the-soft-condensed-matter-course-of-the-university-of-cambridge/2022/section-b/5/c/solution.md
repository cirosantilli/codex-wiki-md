<h1 id="section-b/5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In this [polymerization Brownian ratchet](../../../../../polymerization-brownian-ratchet.md) model, a gap of width $\delta$ immediately accommodates one [monomer](../../../../../monomer.md). That event advances the tip by $\delta$ and resets the gap to zero. The threshold is therefore an [absorbing boundary condition for a diffusion](../../../../../absorbing-boundary-condition-for-a-diffusion.md),

$$
\boxed{p(\delta)=0.}
$$

All waiting-time [probability](../../../../../probability.md) lies within $0\leq x<\delta$, so the steady occupation [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{\int_0^\delta p(x)\,dx=1.}
$$

There is a reinjection flux $j_0$ at $x=0$ equal to the absorption flux at $x=\delta$. The nonpenetration constraint at zero is a [reflecting boundary condition for a diffusion](../../../../../reflecting-boundary-condition-for-a-diffusion.md) during each individual waiting episode. For the stationary reset process it must not be confused with setting the net current $J(0^+)$ to zero: the source at reset supplies $J(0^+)=j_0$. This is how a normalized steady density can coexist with a nonzero exit flux.
