<h1 id="section-b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the [steady state](../../../../../steady-state.md), conservation of [probability](../../../../../probability.md) requires $J_x=0$, so $J=j_0$ is constant. The sign is positive because increasing gap reaches the incorporation boundary, whereas the deterministic drift points towards smaller gap. Thus

$$
p'(x)+\beta Fp(x)=-\frac{j_0}{D}.
$$

An [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) $e^{\beta Fx}$ gives

$$
\boxed{p(x)=A e^{-\beta Fx}-\frac{j_0}{D\beta F}\quad(F>0).}
$$

Substitution makes the check particularly transparent:

$$
p'=-\beta F A e^{-\beta Fx},\qquad
-D(p'+\beta Fp)=j_0,
$$

so $\partial_t p=-J_x=0$. The constant offset is necessary for nonzero transport; a pure [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) would have $J=0$ and could not describe a steady succession of addition events. The $F=0$ limit is taken after applying the boundary conditions, avoiding the apparent singularity of the displayed parametrization.
