<h1 id="section-b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Here $T$ is the absolute [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and $k_B$ the [Boltzmann constant](../../../../../boltzmann-constant.md). An [adsorption blob](../../../../../adsorption-blob.md) contains $g\sim(\xi/b)^2$ [monomers](../../../../../monomer.md), so there are $N/g\sim N(b/\xi)^2$ such blobs. Confining a blob removes an amount of conformational [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) of order $k_B$; thus the positive confinement contribution to the [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) is

$$
F_{\mathrm{conf}}\simeq k_BT\frac Ng=k_BT N\left(\frac b\xi\right)^2.
$$

The statement about one blob's [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) is used as an [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) _loss_ relative to the free chain, rather than as an absolute [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md).

In the assumed slab model, contacts occur within a monomer-scale distance $b$ of the substrate. The contact fraction is approximately $b/\xi$, giving $N_c\simeq Nb/\xi$. Multiplying this by the negative contact [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) yields

$$
F_{\mathrm{contact}}=-\epsilon k_BT\frac{Nb}{\xi},\qquad
\boxed{F(\xi)=Nk_BT\left[\left(\frac b\xi\right)^2-\epsilon\frac b\xi\right].}
$$

The two contributions express the competition between loss of conformational [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) and gain of [adsorption](../../../../../adsorption.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). The numerical coefficients are the ones stipulated by this blob approximation.
