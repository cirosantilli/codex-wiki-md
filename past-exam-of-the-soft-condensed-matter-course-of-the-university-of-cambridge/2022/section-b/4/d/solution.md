<h1 id="section-b/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The stretched segment is a [Gaussian chain](../../../../../gaussian-chain.md) of $n$ [Kuhn length](../../../../../kuhn-length.md) steps, with unperturbed root-mean-square end-to-end distance $l=b\sqrt n$. Its end-to-end [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) is proportional to $\exp[-3z^2/(2nb^2)]$, so its leading elastic [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) is $3k_BTz^2/(2nb^2)$. Adding the remaining segment's [adsorption](../../../../../adsorption.md) [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) gives

$$
\boxed{F(n,z)=k_BT(N-n)\left[\left(\frac b\xi\right)^2-\epsilon\frac b\xi\right]
+\frac{3k_BTz^2}{2nb^2}.}
$$

An additive constant independent of $n,z$ is irrelevant. At the optimal adsorption height this becomes

$$
F(n,z)=-(N-n)\mu+\frac{3k_BTz^2}{2nb^2},\qquad \mu=\frac14k_BT\epsilon^2.
$$

The original PDF's hint labels the positive quantity proportional to $k_BT R^2/l^2$ an [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md). It has units of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and is the stretching [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). The corresponding change of conformational [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) is $\Delta S=-3k_B R^2/(2l^2)$. This dimensional and sign correction is essential to the minimization.

This is the leading extensive [Gaussian chain](../../../../../gaussian-chain.md) approximation: endpoint normalization and junction corrections give subleading terms. It requires many stretched [monomers](../../../../../monomer.md) and $z\ll nb$, rather than extension near the full contour length.
