<h1 id="section-b/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Minimize the [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) at fixed $N,T$. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
\frac{\partial F}{\partial\xi}=Nk_BT\left(-\frac{2b^2}{\xi^3}+\frac{\epsilon b}{\xi^2}\right).
$$

The physical [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) for $\epsilon>0$ is

$$
\boxed{\xi_* =\frac{2b}{\epsilon},\qquad F_{\mathrm{ads},*}=-\frac14Nk_BT\epsilon^2.}
$$

To check the minimum directly, put $x=b/\xi$. Then $F/(Nk_BT)=(x-\epsilon/2)^2-\epsilon^2/4$, which is globally minimized at $x=\epsilon/2$. The binding [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) gain per [monomer](../../../../../monomer.md) is consequently $\mu=k_BT\epsilon^2/4>0$.

The weak-[adsorption](../../../../../adsorption.md) blob picture requires $b\ll\xi_*\ll b\sqrt N$, or $2/\sqrt N\ll\epsilon\ll2$ in this model. If $\epsilon$ is too large, the layer saturates at a monomer-scale thickness; if too small, a many-blob adsorbed layer has not formed.
