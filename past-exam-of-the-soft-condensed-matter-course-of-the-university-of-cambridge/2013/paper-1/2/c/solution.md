<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [coil-globule transition](../../../../../coil-globule-transition.md) is the contraction of an isolated flexible [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) as [solvent quality](../../../../../solvent-quality.md) worsens. A swollen chain in a [good solvent](../../../../../good-solvent.md) has size $R\sim aN^{3/5}$ in the [Flory approximation](../../../../../flory-approximation.md). At the [theta temperature](../../../../../theta-temperature.md) the effective two-segment [excluded volume](../../../../../excluded-volume.md) vanishes and $R\sim aN^{1/2}$. A [poor solvent](../../../../../poor-solvent.md) favors a dense [polymer globule](../../../../../polymer-globule.md), with $R\propto N^{1/3}$ at fixed internal [density](../../../../../density.md).

The competition is visible in the scaling [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md)

$$
\frac{F}{k_BT}\sim\frac{R^2}{Na^2}+\frac{vN^2}{R^3}+\frac{uN^3}{R^6},\qquad u>0.
$$

For $v>0$, elastic cost balances two-segment repulsion to give the swollen-coil law. At $v=0$, three-segment repulsion stabilizes ideal-chain scaling; for $v<0$, attraction balanced by the positive three-segment term gives $R^3\sim uN/|v|$. The dense [polymer globule](../../../../../polymer-globule.md) also has an interfacial [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). The scaling expression explains regimes, not all fluctuation corrections or a detailed finite-chain transition curve. A finite chain crosses over smoothly; the sharp theta-point limit refers to indefinitely long chains.

A dilute-solution light-scattering experiment can follow [radius of gyration](../../../../../radius-of-gyration.md) while changing [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) or [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) composition. Measure at several concentrations and extrapolate toward isolated chains; check molecular-weight distribution, reversibility and the absence of growing multi-chain aggregates. [dynamic light scattering](../../../../../dynamic-light-scattering.md) measures [hydrodynamic size](../../../../../hydrodynamic-size.md), which is related to but distinct from [radius of gyration](../../../../../radius-of-gyration.md). A falling scattering-derived size accompanied by a rising aggregate population cannot by itself establish single-chain collapse. **[solvent quality](../../../../../solvent-quality.md) controls conformation through the balance of segment interactions and [conformational entropy](../../../../../conformational-entropy.md).**
