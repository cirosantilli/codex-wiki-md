<h1 id="born-infeld-soliton">Born–Infeld soliton</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac-Born-Infeld action](dirac-born-infeld-action.md)

A supersymmetric spike of a [D-brane](d-brane.md) combines a worldvolume gauge field with a transverse scalar profile. Its geometry and charge represent strings ending on the brane. Electric spikes represent [fundamental strings](fundamental-string.md); magnetic spikes on a [D3-brane](d3-brane.md) represent [D1-branes](d1-brane.md). The original construction is [Brane Dynamics From the Born-Infeld Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9708147).
