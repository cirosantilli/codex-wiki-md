<h1 id="einstein-metric-condition-in-f-r-gravity">Einstein metric condition in f(R) gravity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [f(R) gravity](f-r-gravity.md)

Every four-dimensional Einstein metric with $R_{ab}=\Lambda g_{ab}$ has constant [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) $R=4\Lambda$. The vacuum metric [f(R) gravity](f-r-gravity.md) tensor reduces to $(\Lambda f'(4\Lambda)-f(4\Lambda)/2)g_{ab}$, giving the displayed necessary and sufficient condition for a fixed [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md). Both function and derivative may vanish, so division by $f'$ is unnecessary. Requiring this for all real $\Lambda$ and a smooth $f$ instead yields $f(R)=CR^2$.
