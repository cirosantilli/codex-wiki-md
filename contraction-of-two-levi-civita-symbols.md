# Contraction of two Levi-Civita symbols

↑ **Parent:** [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md)

Contracting one index gives

$$
\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{ipq}
=\delta_{jp}\delta_{kq}-\delta_{jq}\delta_{kp}.
$$
