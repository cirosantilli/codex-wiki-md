# SLE does not hit a fixed nonzero boundary point

↑ **Parent:** [SLE boundary-point logarithmic separation diffusion](sle-boundary-point-logarithmic-separation-diffusion.md)

For every $x\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$, the probability that a chordal $\operatorname{SLE}_\kappa$ trace in $(\mathbb H,0,\infty)$ passes through $x$ is zero. The [SLE boundary-point logarithmic separation diffusion](sle-boundary-point-logarithmic-separation-diffusion.md) shows that the chance for $x$ to be swallowed strictly before a nearby point tends to zero as that point approaches $x$; [Scaling invariance of SLE](scaling-invariance-of-sle.md) and reflection then handle every nonzero $x$.
