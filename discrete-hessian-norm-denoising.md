# Discrete Hessian-norm denoising

↑ **Parent:** [Variational regularization](variational-regularization.md)

Replacing a discrete [gradient](gradient.md) by a second-difference operator $H:X\to X^4$ gives the global-norm penalty $\alpha\|Hu\|_2$. Its reconstruction is $g-P_Cg$ for $C=H^*B_\alpha$. Components in $\ker H$ are preserved; affine-image membership of this kernel depends on the boundary stencil. Pixelwise Hessian penalties instead use a product of four-component balls.
