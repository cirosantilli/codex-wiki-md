# Beta sampling by gamma ratios

↑ **Parent:** [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md)

Independent [gamma distributions](gamma-distribution.md) $S\sim\Gamma(\alpha,r)$ and $T\sim\Gamma(\beta,r)$ with a common rate yield a [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) $S/(S+T)\sim\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\beta)$. The [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) $S=wu$, $T=w(1-u)$ has [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $w$, and factors the joint [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) into a [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) density in $u$ and a [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) density in $w$. In particular, independent [chi-squared distributions](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $2\alpha$ and $2\beta$ degrees of freedom give the required ratio.
