# Monotonicity of random-cluster critical probability in cluster weight

↑ **Parent:** [Random-cluster critical probability](random-cluster-critical-probability.md)

The finite-volume [random-cluster weight monotonicity](random-cluster-weight-monotonicity.md) passes to consistently wired or free infinite-volume measures for finite increasing connection events. Decreasing these events to origin-to-infinity connectivity yields $\theta(p,q_1)\ge\theta(p,q_2)$ for $q_1\le q_2$. The corresponding supercritical parameter sets are nested, and hence $p_c(q_1)\le p_c(q_2)$.
