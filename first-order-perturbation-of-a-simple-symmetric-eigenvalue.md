# First-order perturbation of a simple symmetric eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md)

Let $B,E$ be fixed real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md), with $Bx=\lambda x$, $\|x\|_2=1$, and $\lambda$ a [simple eigenvalue](simple-eigenvalue.md). Complete $x$ to an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) $x,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda,\lambda_2,\ldots,\lambda_n$. Put $a=x^TEx$ and $v=-\sum_{j=2}^n(x_j^TEx)/(\lambda_j-\lambda)\,x_j$. Then $(B+\varepsilon E)(x+\varepsilon v)-(\lambda+\varepsilon a)(x+\varepsilon v)=\varepsilon^2(E-aI)v$. The [symmetric eigenvalue residual bound](symmetric-eigenvalue-residual-bound.md) produces an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda+\varepsilon a+O(\varepsilon^2)$ in [Big O notation](big-o-notation.md).
