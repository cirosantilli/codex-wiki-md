# Prequantum line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Prequantization](prequantization.md)

A [prequantum line bundle](prequantum-line-bundle.md) is a Hermitian [line bundle](line-bundle.md) over a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) with unitary [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) of the displayed curvature. With $\omega=-d\theta$, a local connection is $\nabla=d-i\theta/\hbar$. Reversing the Hamiltonian-vector-field convention or passing to the dual [line bundle](line-bundle.md) reverses the corresponding curvature convention. Integrality of $\omega/(2\pi\hbar)$ on closed two-cycles is the topological existence condition.
