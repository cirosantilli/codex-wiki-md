# Alternating ultrapowers give isomorphic ultralimits

↑ **Parent:** [Model-theoretic ultralimit](model-theoretic-ultralimit.md)

The [Frayne embedding lemma](frayne-embedding-lemma.md) embeds $B$ into an [ultrapower](ultrapower.md) $A_1$ of $A$. Its relative version embeds $A_1$ into an ultrapower $B_1$ of $B$, fixing the diagonal copy of $B$. Continue alternately over the previously fixed elementary substructure. After coherent identifications one has $B\preceq A_1\preceq B_1\preceq A_2\preceq B_2\preceq\cdots$, while each $A_{n+1}$ is an ultrapower of $A_n$ along its diagonal map, and similarly for $B$. The two cofinal subsequences have the same elementary union. This proves the ultralimit assertion without the stronger single-ultrapower isomorphism theorem.
