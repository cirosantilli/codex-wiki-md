# Eigenvariable condition

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

An [eigenvariable condition](eigenvariable-condition.md) ensures that a variable chosen as arbitrary or as an existential witness cannot smuggle extra information into a [quantifier](quantifier.md) inference. For the [universal quantifier introduction rule](universal-quantifier-introduction-rule.md), the generalized variable must not occur free in any undischarged assumption. For the [existential quantifier elimination rule](existential-quantifier-elimination-rule.md), the fresh witness must not occur free in the resulting conclusion or in the other undischarged assumptions. [Temporary assumptions](temporary-assumption.md) may contain it if they are discharged before the final inference.
