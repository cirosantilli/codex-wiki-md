# Computational psychiatry

↑ **Parent:** [Psychiatry](psychiatry-split.md)

The use of [mathematical models](mathematical-model.md) to investigate processes contributing to [mental disorder](mental-disorder.md) and its [treatment](treatment.md). Model fitting, [parameter recovery](parameter-recovery.md) and experimental intervention have different evidential roles; an estimated parameter is not automatically a diagnosis or a biological mechanism.
