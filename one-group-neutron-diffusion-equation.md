# One-group neutron diffusion equation

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion approximation](diffusion-approximation.md)

A [diffusion approximation](diffusion-approximation.md) for a scalar [neutron flux](neutron-flux.md), with one prescribed energy group. Its terms balance current loss, absorption, [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md) production and a source. A solution also requires appropriate [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
