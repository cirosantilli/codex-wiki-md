# Sensible heat

↑ **Parent:** [Heat](heat.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensible_heat)

[Sensible heat](sensible-heat.md) changes [temperature](temperature.md) without changing the phase. For constant [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md), its change per unit mass is $c_p\Delta T$, whereas [latent heat](latent-heat.md) accompanies a [phase transition](phase-transition.md).
