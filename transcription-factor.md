# Transcription factor

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcription_factor)

A [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md) is a [protein](protein.md) that regulates [transcription](transcription-biology.md). Combinations of [transcription factors](transcription-factor.md) help select [gene expression](gene-expression.md) patterns during [cell differentiation](cellular-differentiation.md).
