# Graded commutator

↑ **Parent:** [Graded algebra](graded-algebra.md)

The bracket of homogeneous elements in a parity-graded associative algebra. It is a commutator if at least one element is even and an [anticommutator](anticommutator.md) if both are odd. This is the natural bracket for the action of an odd [BRST charge](brst-charge.md) on quantum operators.
