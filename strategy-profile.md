# Strategy profile

↑ **Parent:** [Normal-form game](normal-form-game.md)

A [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md) specifies one strategy for every player. A [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md) [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md) lies in the [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) $S_1\times\cdots\times S_n$ of the players' strategy [sets](set-split.md). A [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md) specifies one [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) $\sigma_i$ over each $S_i$. Write $s_{-i}$ or $\sigma_{-i}$ for the choices of all players except player $i$.
