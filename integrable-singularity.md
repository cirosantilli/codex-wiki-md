# Integrable singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Improper integral](improper-integral.md)

A [function](function-split.md) has an [integrable singularity](integrable-singularity.md) at a point if it becomes unbounded there but its [absolute value](absolute-value.md) has a finite [improper integral](improper-integral.md) over a sufficiently small surrounding interval, one-sided at an endpoint. The endpoint behavior of the [arcsine distribution](arcsine-distribution.md)'s [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is an example.
