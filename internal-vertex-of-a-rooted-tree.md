# Internal vertex of a rooted tree

↑ **Parent:** [Rooted tree](rooted-tree.md)

A [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) with at least one [child in a rooted tree](child-in-a-rooted-tree.md). Internal [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and [leaves of a rooted tree](leaf-of-a-rooted-tree.md) partition the [vertex set](vertex-set.md). The ancestors of an internal [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) are also internal; consequently the internal [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) of a nontrivial [rooted tree](rooted-tree.md) induce a connected [subgraph](subgraph.md) containing its root.
