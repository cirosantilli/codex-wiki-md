# Finite one-sided generator of an invertible system forces zero entropy

↑ **Parent:** [One-sided generator](one-sided-generator.md)

If an invertible probability [measure-preserving system](measure-preserving-system.md) has a finite [one-sided generator](one-sided-generator.md) $\xi$, then its future sigma-algebra $\mathcal F_1=T^{-1}\mathcal B$ equals $\mathcal B$ modulo null sets. Hence $H_\mu(\xi\mid\mathcal F_1)=0$, and the [infinite-future formula for partition entropy rate](infinite-future-formula-for-partition-entropy-rate.md) and [Kolmogorov-Sinai generator theorem](kolmogorov-sinai-generator-theorem.md) give $h_\mu(T)=0$. Invertibility matters: a fair binary one-sided [Bernoulli shift](bernoulli-shift.md) has entropy $\log2$ and a finite one-sided generator.
