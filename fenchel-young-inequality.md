<h1 id="fenchel-young-inequality">Fenchel–Young inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenchel–Young_inequality)

For a proper convex function $f$ and its [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md) $f^*$,

$$
f(x)+f^*(p)\geq p\mathbin\cdot x.
$$

Equality holds exactly when $p\in\partial f(x)$, or equivalently $x\in\partial f^*(p)$.
