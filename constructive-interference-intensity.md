# Constructive interference intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)

For mutually coherent [monochromatic waves](monochromatic-wave.md) with the same [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) in the scalar convention of [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md), [constructive interference](constructive-interference.md) reaches its maximum at [phase difference](phase-difference.md) $\delta=2\pi m$, with [integer](integer.md) $m$. The [wave amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) add, giving $I_{\max}=I_1+I_2+2\sqrt{I_1I_2}$. Equal individual [wave intensities](intensity-physics.md) $I_0$ give $4I_0$; individual [wave intensities](intensity-physics.md) $1$ and $0.25$ give $2.25$ in their common unit. This maximum assumes the [optical coherence](optical-coherence.md) and detection conditions stated in [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md).
