# Steady-Hopf merger in vertical-field magnetoconvection

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillatory marginality in vertical-field magnetoconvection](oscillatory-marginality-in-vertical-field-magnetoconvection.md)

For thermal-time [magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md), a physical oscillatory neutral mode has $\omega^2=\sigma(1-\zeta)Q_\kappa\pi^2/(1+\sigma)-\zeta^2(k^2+\pi^2)^2>0$. The steady and oscillatory neutral curves meet when this frequency tends to zero. Thus $0<\zeta<1$ is necessary and the displayed relation locates the merger. A formal oscillatory-curve minimum with negative frequency squared is not a physical onset.
