# Least-prime divisibility constraint for a finite simple group

↑ **Parent:** [Finite nonabelian simple group](finite-nonabelian-simple-group.md)

Let $p$ be the smallest prime divisor of the order of a [finite nonabelian simple group](finite-nonabelian-simple-group.md). A cyclic [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) would give a [normal p-complement](normal-p-complement.md), so if $p^3$ does not divide the order, the [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) must be $C_p^2$. [Conjugation](conjugation.md) embeds its [normalizer](normalizer.md) modulo [centralizer](centralizer.md) into $GL_2(p)$, and this quotient has order prime to $p$. For odd $p$, every other prime divisor of $|GL_2(p)|=p(p-1)^2(p+1)$ is smaller than $p$, again forcing trivial [conjugation](conjugation.md) and a contradiction to the [Burnside transfer theorem](burnside-transfer-theorem.md). For $p=2$, nontrivial [conjugation](conjugation.md) must have order three, giving $12\mid |G|$. The [groups](group-split.md) $PSL_2(5^{2m+1})$ give infinitely many examples with exactly two factors of two in their orders.
