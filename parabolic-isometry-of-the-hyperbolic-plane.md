# Parabolic isometry of the hyperbolic plane

↑ **Parent:** [Fuchsian group](fuchsian-group.md)

A nonidentity orientation-preserving isometry of the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) is parabolic when it has one fixed point on the ideal boundary and none in the plane. In the [upper half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md), every parabolic isometry is conjugate to a nonzero horizontal translation.

This is one type of [isometry](isometry.md) of the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md), classified by its fixed points.
