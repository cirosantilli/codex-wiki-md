<h1 id="radial-kahler-metric-with-euclidean-volume-in-complex-dimension-two">Radial Kähler metric with Euclidean volume in complex dimension two</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler potential (complex geometry)](kahler-potential-complex-geometry.md)

For $s=|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2>0$ and $a>0$, a [geometric Kähler potential](kahler-potential-complex-geometry.md) with derivative $\varphi'(s)=\sqrt{s^2+a^2}/(2s)$ defines a [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md) on $\mathbb C^2\setminus\{0\}$. Its tangential and radial [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\sqrt{s^2+a^2}/(2s)$ and $s/(2\sqrt{s^2+a^2})$. Their product is $1/4$, giving the same [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md) as $\omega_0=(i/2)\sum dz_j\wedge d\bar z_j$, although the metric is different. This construction makes preservation of volume compatible with anisotropic stretching.
