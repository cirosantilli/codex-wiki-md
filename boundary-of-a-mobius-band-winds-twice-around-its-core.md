<h1 id="boundary-of-a-mobius-band-winds-twice-around-its-core">Boundary of a Möbius band winds twice around its core</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius band](mobius-band.md)

In the [quotient space](quotient-topological-space.md) description of the [Möbius band](mobius-band.md), follow $v=+1$ once along the rectangle. The seam sends its endpoint to the start of $v=-1$, which must also be traversed before returning to the original point. Under the [deformation retraction](deformation-retraction.md) onto the [core circle of a Möbius band](core-circle-of-a-mobius-band.md), these two stretches each travel once around the same [circle](circle.md). Orienting both circles by increasing longitudinal parameter gives a map of [topological degree](topological-degree.md) two. Equivalently, the [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) on [fundamental groups](fundamental-group.md) induced by inclusion of the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) sends a chosen generator to twice the core generator, or to $c^2$ in multiplicative notation. Reversing the chosen boundary generator changes the sign, not the fact that it winds twice.
