# Profile likelihood for a Gaussian precision matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Precision matrix](precision-matrix.md)

For independent [multivariate normal](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) observations with unknown mean and nonsingular covariance, profiling the mean at its sample value gives negative log-likelihood proportional to $-\log\det\Omega+\operatorname{tr}(S\Omega)$ over $\Omega\succ0$. The [sample covariance matrix](sample-covariance-matrix.md) uses divisor $n$. Its minimizer exists exactly when $S\succ0$, and is then $S^{-1}$.
