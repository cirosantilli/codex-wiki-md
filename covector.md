# Covector

↑ **Parent:** [Dual space](dual-space.md)

A [covector](covector.md) is an element of the [dual space](dual-space.md), hence a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on a [vector space](vector-space-split.md). In finite-dimensional coordinates, it acts as a row multiplied by a [coordinate vector](coordinate-vector.md) column. The row $\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\end{pmatrix}$ acts on $\begin{pmatrix}3\\1\end{pmatrix}$ to give five. The terminology emphasizes its role as a scalar-valued measurement and its behavior under a [change of basis](change-of-basis.md).

For example, the [total derivative](total-derivative.md) of $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$ at $(1,2)$ sends a small displacement $h$ to the linear change $df(h)=2h_x+4h_y$. This [covector](covector.md) describes the first-order response. The [gradient](gradient.md) $\nabla f=(2,4)$ represents the same rule through the chosen Euclidean [inner product](inner-product.md). In [differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md), the [cotangent space](cotangent-space.md) collects these scalar-valued measurements of [tangent vectors](tangent-vector.md).
