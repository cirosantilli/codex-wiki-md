# Transmittance

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometry](radiometry-split.md)

[Transmittance](transmittance.md) is the fraction of incident [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) transmitted through a specified object or boundary. Specify [wavelength](wavelength.md), [angle of incidence](angle-of-incidence.md), [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) and which outgoing [light](light.md) is collected.
