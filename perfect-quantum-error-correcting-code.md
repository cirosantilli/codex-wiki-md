# Perfect quantum error-correcting code

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hamming bound](quantum-hamming-bound.md)

A [nondegenerate quantum code](nondegenerate-quantum-error-correcting-code.md) is perfect at correction radius $t$ if its distinct correctable [Pauli error](pauli-operator.md) images exhaust the physical [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). Each image has dimension $2^k$, and their [orthogonal direct sum](orthogonal-direct-sum.md) is the whole $2^n$-dimensional space precisely when the [quantum Hamming bound](quantum-hamming-bound.md) is an equality. This perfectness concerns unknown-location errors of weight at most $t$; it does not imply correction of every higher-weight error.
