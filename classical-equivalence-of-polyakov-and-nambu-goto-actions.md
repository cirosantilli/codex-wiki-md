<h1 id="classical-equivalence-of-polyakov-and-nambu-goto-actions">Classical equivalence of Polyakov and Nambu–Goto actions</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Polyakov action](polyakov-action.md)

Varying the independent metric in the [Polyakov action](polyakov-action.md) sets the [worldsheet stress tensor](worldsheet-stress-energy-tensor.md) to zero. For a nondegenerate timelike embedding in two [worldsheet](worldsheet.md) dimensions, the metric is proportional to the [induced worldsheet metric](induced-worldsheet-metric.md). The conformal factor cancels from the action, which becomes the [Nambu–Goto action](nambu-goto-action.md). This elimination is classical; the quantum measure and [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) require separate treatment.
