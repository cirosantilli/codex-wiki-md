# Berry phase

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berry_phase)

A [Berry phase](berry-phase.md) is the [geometric phase](geometric-phase.md) acquired when a nondegenerate [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) follows a closed cycle of parameters of a [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) in the ideal limit of the [adiabatic theorem](adiabatic-theorem.md). The [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) must stay separated from other [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) by a nonzero [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md), and the parameters must change sufficiently slowly. Returning the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) to its starting value returns the state to the same ray, but its [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) can remember the journey. The total [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) contains both the [dynamical phase](dynamical-phase.md) and the [Berry phase](berry-phase.md).

The beauty of the [Berry phase](berry-phase.md) is that, within the [adiabatic theorem](adiabatic-theorem.md)'s limit, it depends on the oriented path of the parameters rather than how long the journey takes. For a [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) [qubit](qubit.md), the [Berry phase of a Bloch-sphere latitude](berry-phase-of-a-bloch-sphere-latitude.md) turns that memory into half a [solid angle](solid-angle.md). [Berry-phase interference with a reference path](berry-phase-interference-with-a-reference-path.md) converts this geometry into a detection [probability](probability.md).

Michael Berry's 1984 paper formulated the [geometric phase](geometric-phase.md) for cycles governed by the [adiabatic theorem](adiabatic-theorem.md) and derived the [solid angle](solid-angle.md) result for [spin](spin.md). Its bibliographic details are Proceedings of the Royal Society of London A, volume 392, pages 45–57, DOI 10.1098/rspa.1984.0023. [Berry's authored publication list, entry 120: Quantal phase factors accompanying adiabatic changes (1984)](https://michaelberryphysics.wordpress.com/publications/); [Berry's original paper, available through MIT](https://stuff.mit.edu/afs/athena/course/8/8.06/spring06/handouts/Berry_1984.pdf). The wider concept of [geometric phase](geometric-phase.md) does not require every cycle to obey the [adiabatic theorem](adiabatic-theorem.md): Aharonov and Anandan formulated a [geometric phase](geometric-phase.md) for general cyclic quantum evolution in 1987. [Aharonov and Anandan, Phase change during a cyclic quantum evolution, Physical Review Letters 58, 1593–1596 (1987)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.58.1593).
