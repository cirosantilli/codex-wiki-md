<h1 id="alfven-wave">Alfvén wave</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic wave](magnetohydrodynamic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfvén_wave)

An [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) is a [magnetohydrodynamic wave](magnetohydrodynamic-wave.md) in which [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) restores a sideways disturbance of a conducting [fluid](fluid.md). In the small-[amplitude](wave-amplitude.md), uniform-background [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md) model, its shear [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) has [displacement](displacement.md) perpendicular to both the background [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and the [wavevector](wavevector.md). It is an [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) disturbance: the [mass density](density.md) and gas [pressure](pressure-split.md) have no first-order fluctuation. Its [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) is $\omega^2=(\mathbf k\mathbin\cdot\mathbf v_A)^2$, where $\mathbf v_A$ is the background [Alfvén velocity](alfven-velocity.md).

The mechanism resembles a plucked taut string: [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md) makes sideways [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md) bend [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md), and their [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) accelerates neighboring fluid. The fluid supplies inertia and the bent [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) supplies the restoring stress. This permits [energy](energy-split.md) transport without a first-order compression, unlike an [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md). [Hannes Alfvén](hannes-olof-gosta-alfven.md)'s 1942 paper, Existence of Electromagnetic-Hydrodynamic Waves, considered precisely a uniform axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), infinite [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) and sideways [velocity](velocity.md) depending on axial position and time; it proposed a solar application. This was a theoretical prediction, followed by the distinct [Lundquist liquid-metal Alfvén wave experiment](lundquist-liquid-metal-alfven-wave-experiment.md).[1942, pp. 405–406; ideal-conductor plane-wave argument, reproduced on slide 4 of Kinetic Alfvén waves in space plasmas](existence-of-electromagnetic-hydrodynamic-waves-by-hannes-olof-gosta-alfven.md)

**[Figure 1](#image-magnetic-tension-restores-a-transverse-alfven-wave-displacement). Magnetic tension restores a transverse Alfvén-wave displacement**. An original schematic of a small sideways bend in a uniform background [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The [displacement](displacement.md) and restoring [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) point in opposite directions at the crest; the transverse [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) follows the slope, so it vanishes at that crest. With [fluid Lagrangian displacement](lagrangian-displacement-fluid-mechanics.md) $\boldsymbol\xi$, [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md) gives $\mathbf b_\perp=B_0\partial_z\boldsymbol\xi_\perp$. The drawing exaggerates the bend for readability.

For oblique propagation, the shear [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) has [velocity](velocity.md) normal to the plane containing the [wavevector](wavevector.md) and background [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The [fast magnetosonic wave](fast-magnetosonic-wave.md) and [slow magnetosonic wave](slow-magnetosonic-wave.md) instead have [velocity](velocity.md) in that plane and generally involve compression, with gas [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) contributing to their restoring forces. For exactly parallel propagation the plane is not unique: either transverse [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) is allowed, and some modal speeds coincide. For $k_\parallel=0$ the shear branch has $\omega=0$, while the [fast magnetosonic wave](fast-magnetosonic-wave.md) can still propagate perpendicular to the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). One should identify the [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) as well as the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md). The statement that the shear mode is incompressible concerns this linear mode; a finite linearly polarized disturbance can drive a quadratic compressive response, as in the [magnetic-pressure obstruction to a linearly polarized Alfvén wave](magnetic-pressure-obstruction-to-a-linearly-polarized-alfven-wave.md).
