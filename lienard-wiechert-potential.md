<h1 id="lienard-wiechert-potential">Liénard–Wiechert potential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Retarded potential](retarded-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liénard–Wiechert_potential)

The potentials of a point charge $q$ are

$$
\phi=\frac{q}{4\pi\epsilon_0}
\frac1{R-\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf R/c},
\qquad
\mathbf A=\frac{\mathbf v}{c^2}\phi,
$$

with every source quantity evaluated at the [retarded time](retarded-time.md).
