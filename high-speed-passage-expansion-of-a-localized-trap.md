# High-speed passage expansion of a localized trap

↑ **Parent:** [Passage through a translating optical trap](passage-through-a-translating-optical-trap.md)

If the maximum force-induced speed is smaller in magnitude than $v_T$, expand the passage denominator as a uniformly convergent [geometric series](geometric-series.md). For a localized conservative well with equal outside potential levels, the first force moment vanishes and displacement starts at order $v_T^{-2}$. An odd force has no cubic moment, giving the next correction at order $v_T^{-4}$. Compact force support alone does not guarantee a vanishing first moment or positive displacement.
