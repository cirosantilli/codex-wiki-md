# Discrete Neumann mode selection

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md)

On $0<x<\ell$, homogeneous [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) give spatial [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) $\cos(m\pi x/\ell)$, $m=0,1,\ldots$, with squared [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md) $q_m$. Differentiating twice proves that their [Laplacian](laplacian.md) [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $-q_m$, and their endpoint [derivatives](derivative.md) vanish. A [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $\sigma(q)$ therefore predicts the fastest permitted growing mode by maximizing $\operatorname{Re}\sigma(q_m)$ over integers $m$, rather than over all real [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md). A continuous unstable band is insufficient if it contains no $q_m$.
