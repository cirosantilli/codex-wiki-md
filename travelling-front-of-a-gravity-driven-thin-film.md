# Travelling front of a gravity-driven thin film

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-driven thin film on an incline](gravity-driven-thin-film-on-an-incline.md)

For $h_t=\partial_x(-Ah^3+Bh^3h_x)$, $A,B>0$, a front $h=F(x-ct)$ joining $h_0$ behind it to zero at its tip has $c=Ah_0^2$. Integrating once gives $F'=(A/B)(1-h_0^2/F^2)$ and

$$
\xi=\frac BA\left[F+\frac{h_0}{2}\log\frac{h_0-F}{h_0+F}\right].
$$

Near the tip, $F\sim(-3c\xi/B)^{1/3}$. Its unbounded slope signals the failure of the small-slope [lubrication approximation](lubrication-theory.md) sufficiently close to the [contact line](contact-line.md).
