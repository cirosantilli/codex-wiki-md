# Driven two-state rotary motor

↑ **Parent:** [Bacterial flagellar motor](bacterial-flagellar-motor.md)

A minimal [bacterial flagellar motor](bacterial-flagellar-motor.md) model combines [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega>0$ with bound and detached [probabilities](probability.md) $P_A+P_B=1$. With detachment rate $k_0$ in the first half-turn and attachment rate $k_0$ in the second, the periodic stationary bound probability is $P_A=\alpha e^{-\beta\theta}$ on $0\le\theta\le\pi$, where $\alpha=(1+e^{-\pi\beta})^{-1}$, and $P_A(\theta+\pi)=1-P_A(\theta)$. The one-way switching windows describe externally driven kinetics rather than [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) with a passive heat bath.
