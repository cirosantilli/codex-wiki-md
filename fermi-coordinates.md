# Fermi coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Normal coordinates](normal-coordinates.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi_coordinates)

[Fermi coordinates](fermi-coordinates.md) transport a local frame along a timelike [worldline](world-line.md). Along a [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md), a parallel-transported orthonormal frame defines [Fermi normal coordinates](fermi-coordinates.md) in which the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) is Minkowskian and the [Christoffel symbols](christoffel-symbol.md) vanish on the worldline. Curvature first appears in terms quadratic in the spatial distance from it, so finite laboratories retain [tidal forces](tidal-force.md).
