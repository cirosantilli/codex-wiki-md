# Transit duration of a small planet

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md)

For a small [planet](planet.md) on a nearly circular [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md), a stellar chord at normalized [transit impact parameter](transit-impact-parameter.md) $b$ has length $2R_\star\sqrt{1-b^2}$. Dividing by transverse orbital speed $2\pi a/P$ gives the duration. A central transit has the longest duration within this approximation. Eliminating $a$ with [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md) yields $P/D^3\ge\pi^2G\rho_\star/3$ for known mean stellar [mass density](density.md). Finite planet radius, appreciable [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md), and $R_\star/a$ corrections change the bound.
