# Relative risk bounds from rounded group compositions

↑ **Parent:** [Relative risk from group compositions](relative-risk-from-group-compositions.md)

Suppose $0<p_L\leq p\leq p_U<1$ and $0<a_L\leq a\leq a_U<1$. The formula for [relative risk from group compositions](relative-risk-from-group-compositions.md) increases with $a$ and decreases with $p$, since $a/(1-a)$ increases and $(1-p)/p$ decreases. This [monotonicity](monotonic-function.md) proves the displayed bounds. If percentages are rounded to the nearest whole percentage point, each corresponding proportion lies within $0.005$ of its displayed value. These bounds describe rounding uncertainty; they are not a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) for sampling uncertainty.
