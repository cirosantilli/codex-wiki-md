# Magnetically driven spin-blockade lifting

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli spin blockade](pauli-spin-blockade.md)

A resonantly driven local electron-spin rotation can give a blocked two-dot triplet a singlet component, allowing spin-conserving charge transfer to a lowest-orbital doubly occupied dot. A common rotation of two perfectly identical [spins](spin.md) does not mix singlet and triplet sectors, so relative [spin](spin.md) rotation or an additional mixing mechanism is essential. Leakage-current spectroscopy tests resonant lifting; pulse-duration oscillations add evidence for coherent control.
