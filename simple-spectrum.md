# Simple spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md)

A finite-dimensional [linear operator](linear-operator.md) has simple spectrum when each [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) has [algebraic multiplicity](algebraic-multiplicity.md) one. For a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md), this means its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are all distinct, so [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) chosen for different [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are automatically [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md).
