# Photon quadrupole in tight coupling with polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Tight-coupling approximation](tight-coupling-approximation.md)

For the expansion $\Theta(\mu)=\sum_\ell(-i)^\ell\Theta_\ell P_\ell(\mu)$ without a $(2\ell+1)$ factor, the leading polarization relation is $E_2=-\sqrt6\,\Theta_2/4$. The [photon Boltzmann hierarchy](photon-boltzmann-hierarchy.md) then gives $\Theta_2=-8k\Theta_1/(9\dot\tau)$. This small [photon quadrupole](photon-quadrupole.md) supplies the shear-viscosity part of [Silk damping](cosmic-microwave-background-diffusion-damping.md).
