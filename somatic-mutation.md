# Somatic mutation

↑ **Parent:** [Mutation](mutation.md)

A [somatic mutation](somatic-mutation.md) occurs in a body-cell lineage outside the [germline mutation](germline-mutation.md) route. Its descendant [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) can inherit it through [DNA replication](dna-replication.md), even though it is not transmitted through [gametes](gamete.md). Thus heritability within a [cell lineage](cell-lineage.md) differs from inheritance between sexually reproducing [organisms](organism-split.md).
