# Marginal circular orbit under an inverse-cube force

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse-cube central orbit classification](inverse-cube-central-orbit-classification.md)

At $B=-L^2/(2m)$ the centrifugal and [force](force-split.md) potentials cancel. With zero radial [speed](speed.md), any radius is a circular solution, but there is no radial restoring [force](force-split.md); radial-velocity perturbations are not confined.
