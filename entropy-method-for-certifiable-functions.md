# Entropy method for certifiable functions

↑ **Parent:** [Certifiable function](certifiable-function.md)

The entropy method combines a coordinatewise certificate with [tensorization of entropy](tensorization-of-entropy.md) to bound the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) of a certifiable random quantity and hence its tails.
