# Lacunary power series

↑ **Parent:** [Power series](power-series.md)

A power series is lacunary when only a sparse sequence of powers has nonzero coefficients. For $\sum_jc_jz^{m_j}$ with strictly increasing integer exponents, the [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) satisfies $R^{-1}=\limsup_j|c_j|^{1/m_j}$ by the [Cauchy-Hadamard theorem](cauchy-hadamard-theorem.md). The exponent $m_j$, rather than the term index $j$, controls the coefficient root.
