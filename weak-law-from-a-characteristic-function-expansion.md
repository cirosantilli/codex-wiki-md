# Weak law from a characteristic-function expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy continuity theorem](levy-continuity-theorem.md)

If [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) have [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) $\varphi(t)=1+iat+o(|t|)$ at zero, then their [sample mean](sample-mean.md) has [convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md) to $a$: its [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) is $\varphi(t/n)^n\to e^{iat}$. This is also [convergence in probability](convergence-in-probability.md) by [convergence in distribution to a constant implies convergence in probability](convergence-in-distribution-to-a-constant-implies-convergence-in-probability.md).
