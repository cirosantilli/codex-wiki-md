# Image charges between concentric grounded spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Image charge](image-charge.md)

For radii $a<b$ and a [point charge](point-charge.md) $q$ at $r_0\mathbf e_z$, $a<r_0<b$, set $k=b/a$. The [method of images](method-of-images.md) gives the two families $(qk^n,r_0k^{2n}\mathbf e_z)$ and $(-qa k^n/r_0,a^2k^{2n}\mathbf e_z/r_0)$ for $n\in\mathbb Z$. The first family's $n=0$ term is the physical [point charge](point-charge.md). All other [image charges](image-charge.md) lie outside the gap. Sum their [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md) [electric potentials](electric-potential.md) with factor $1/(4\pi\epsilon_0)$.

For a short proof, reflection of $(Q,z)$ in a grounded sphere of radius $R$ gives $(-RQ/z,R^2/z)$. On $r=a$ this pairs first-family index $n$ with second-family index $-n$; on $r=b$ it pairs $n$ with $1-n$. Each pair has zero boundary [electric potential](electric-potential.md). The negative-index [electric potential](electric-potential.md) tails are $O(k^n)$ and the positive-index tails are $O(k^{-n})$, uniformly on the closed gap after excluding the physical singularity. These [geometric series](geometric-series.md) establish [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md), boundary cancellation, and termwise differentiation away from the physical [point charge](point-charge.md). [Uniqueness of Poisson equation](uniqueness-of-poisson-equation.md) identifies the solution. A spherical [Gaussian surface](gaussian-surface.md) just outside $a$ encloses the first family with $n\leq-1$ and the second with $n\leq0$, so [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) and the [geometric series](geometric-series.md) give $Q_a=-qa(b-r_0)/(r_0(b-a))$.
