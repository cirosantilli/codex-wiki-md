# Charged weak box contribution to kaon mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral kaon mixing](neutral-kaon-mixing.md)

A [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) charged-current [box diagram](box-diagram.md) converts $d\bar s$ into $s\bar d$. Internal [quarks](quark.md) run over $u,c,t$, and the flavor sum contains $\lambda_i\lambda_j$ with $\lambda_i=V_{is}^*V_{id}$. The loop function depends on internal masses; [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) unitarity gives $\sum_i\lambda_i=0$, producing the [GIM mechanism](gim-mechanism.md) cancellation of flavor-independent terms. The amplitude is of order $g^4$ and second order in the [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md).
