# Polar decomposition of an invertible real matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Principal square root of a positive semidefinite matrix](principal-square-root-of-a-positive-semidefinite-matrix.md)

Every invertible real matrix has the polar decomposition

$$
M=RP,
\qquad
P=\sqrt{M^TM},
\qquad
R=MP^{-1},
$$

where $P$ is symmetric positive definite and $R$ is orthogonal.

This is the invertible real-matrix case of [polar decomposition](polar-decomposition.md).
