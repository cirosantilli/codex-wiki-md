# Short-relative-time optimal state for triangular shear

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal initial state for triangular stable shear](optimal-initial-state-for-triangular-stable-shear.md)

In the [optimal initial state for triangular stable shear](optimal-initial-state-for-triangular-stable-shear.md), expansion of $e^{t/R}$ gives the displayed leading parameter with relative error $O(t/R)$. Keeping both terms distinguishes a large-$R$ shear regime from the limit $t\to0$ at fixed $R$. The latter direction is the largest-[eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) direction of the Hermitian part of $M$; the former can instead have large absolute time and substantial shear amplification even though $t/R$ is small.
