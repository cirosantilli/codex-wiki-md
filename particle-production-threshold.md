# Particle-production threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic scattering cross-section](relativistic-scattering-cross-section.md)

The lowest initial [centre-of-mass frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md) [energy](energy-split.md) at which a specified final state can be made. For free massive final particles in [natural units](natural-units.md), the minimum is the sum of their [masses](mass.md), attained when their relative [momenta](momentum-split.md) vanish. The [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) at the exact threshold can vanish even though the channel becomes kinematically accessible there.
