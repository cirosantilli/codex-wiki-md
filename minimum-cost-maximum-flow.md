# Minimum-cost maximum flow

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum-cost flow problem](minimum-cost-flow-problem.md)

A maximum-value feasible network flow chosen to have the least total cost among all maximum-value flows. It combines a maximum-flow objective with a [minimum-cost flow](minimum-cost-flow-problem.md) objective.
