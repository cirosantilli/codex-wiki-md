# Lunar crustal dichotomy

↑ **Parent:** [Moon](moon.md)

The hemispheric contrast involving thicker, generally higher feldspathic crust on the [far side of the Moon](far-side-of-the-moon.md), compared with generally thinner crust, more extensive [lunar maria](lunar-mare.md) and the concentration of [KREEP](kreep.md)-rich material on the [near side of the Moon](near-side-of-the-moon.md). Crustal mass, topography, chemistry and later volcanic deposits are different observables. A proposed mechanism must explain their relationship rather than identify the contrast solely with visible dark maria.
