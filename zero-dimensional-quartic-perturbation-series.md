# Zero-dimensional quartic perturbation series

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-dimensional scalar field theory](zero-dimensional-scalar-field-theory.md)

For $m^2>0$, the [zero-dimensional scalar field theory](zero-dimensional-scalar-field-theory.md) with [Euclidean action](euclidean-action.md) $m^2x^2/2+\lambda x^4/4!$ has a [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) normalization $I(0)=\sqrt{2\pi/m^2}$. Its [Feynman rules](feynman-rule.md) assign $1/m^2$ to each [propagator](propagator.md), $-\lambda$ to each quartic vertex, and the reciprocal [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) to each [vacuum diagram](vacuum-feynman-diagram.md). The integral itself includes disconnected [vacuum diagrams](vacuum-feynman-diagram.md); its logarithm selects connected ones. At second order the three possible numbers of edges joining the two vertices are zero, two and four, with [Feynman-diagram symmetry factors](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) $128,16,48$ respectively. Their weights add to $35\lambda^2/(384m^8)$.

The [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md) give the general coefficient $I(0)(-1)^v(4v-1)!!/[v!(4!)^v m^{4v}]$. Successive absolute coefficients have ratio $(4v+3)(4v+1)/[24(v+1)m^4]$, which tends to infinity. Thus this is an [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) as $\lambda\downarrow0$, rather than a convergent [power series](power-series.md). For every fixed truncation the [Taylor theorem](taylor-theorem.md) remainder estimate for $e^{-u}$, $u\geq0$, can be integrated against the [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md) to justify the corresponding remainder bound.
