# Itinerant ferromagnetism

↑ **Parent:** [Hubbard model](hubbard-model.md)

Ferromagnetic spin polarization carried by mobile electrons rather than a separate fixed-spin lattice. A homogeneous spin field splits the electron bands and competes with a positive field cost. Mean-field instability is governed by the [Stoner criterion](stoner-criterion.md); the ordered metal supports transverse spin waves as well as fermionic particle-hole excitations.
