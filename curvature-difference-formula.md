# Curvature difference formula

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature form](curvature-form.md)

If two objects $\nabla_1$ and $\nabla_2$ are each a [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) and satisfy $\nabla_1=\nabla_2+a$ for an $\operatorname{End}(E)$-valued one-form $a$, then

$$
F_{\nabla_1}=F_{\nabla_2}+d_{\nabla_2}a+a\wedge a.
$$

This follows by expanding $(\nabla_2+a)^2$ with the [covariant exterior derivative](exterior-covariant-derivative.md) on the [endomorphism bundle connection](endomorphism-bundle-connection.md).
