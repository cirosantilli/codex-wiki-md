# Oscillatory convection

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal convection](thermal-convection.md)

[oscillatory convection](oscillatory-convection.md) is time-dependent convection emerging through nonzero-frequency [overstability](overstability.md) or a [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md). At onset a complex-conjugate growth-rate pair crosses the imaginary axis. Travelling and standing [convection rolls](convection-roll.md) are distinct nonlinear possibilities, and their stability cannot be decided from the linear [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) alone.
