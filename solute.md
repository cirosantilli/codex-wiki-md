# Solute

↑ **Parent:** [Solution (chemistry)](solution-chemistry.md)

A [solute](solute.md) is a component of a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) distinguished from its [solvent](solvent.md). Its [concentration](concentration.md) describes how much is present relative to a specified quantity of [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) or [solvent](solvent.md).
