# Tunnel-rate-selective spin readout

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-to-charge conversion](spin-to-charge-conversion.md)

Map states with unequal escape rates $\Gamma_f\gg\Gamma_s$ onto distinct charge outcomes after an interval between their characteristic escape times. Both removal energies can lie above the reservoir [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md), so their splitting need not be thermally resolved. A useful rate difference must have a physical origin: a demonstrated single-dot example uses a two-electron [singlet state](singlet-state.md) and [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) with different occupied orbitals. A bare single-electron Zeeman pair in the same orbital does not automatically provide the required rate contrast.[pp. 1–2, Figs. 1–2](single-shot-readout-of-electron-spin-states-in-a-quantum-dot-using-spin-dependent-tunnel-rates-by-r-hanson-and-others.md)
