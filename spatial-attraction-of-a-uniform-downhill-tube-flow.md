# Spatial attraction of a uniform downhill tube flow

↑ **Parent:** [Collapsible tube flow](collapsible-tube-flow.md)

For $p=P_0+\rho c_0^2\alpha^2/2$ and constant flux $c_0A_0q$, a downhill [collapsible tube flow](collapsible-tube-flow.md) obeys the displayed steady equation. Uniform area satisfies $qR(\alpha_1)=\beta$. Since $R'<0$, linearizing about this root gives downstream exponential decay precisely when $\alpha_1^2<q$. This is spatial stability of a steady inlet-value problem, not a guarantee of temporal stability of the driven flow. The [wave speed](wave-speed.md) is $c_0\alpha$, so the stable spatial branch is supercritical.
