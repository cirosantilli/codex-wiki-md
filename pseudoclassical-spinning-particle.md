# Pseudoclassical spinning particle

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic particle phase-space action](relativistic-particle-phase-space-action.md)

A pseudoclassical spinning particle supplements ordinary position and momentum by odd [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md). An even multiplier imposes $p^2=0$ and an odd multiplier imposes $p\cdot\zeta=0$. The odd symplectic term gives $\{\zeta^m,\zeta^n\}_{D}=-i\eta^{mn}$. Quantization represents these variables by a [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md), producing a spinor wavefunction.
