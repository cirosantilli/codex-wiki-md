# Exponentially weighted L2 space

↑ **Parent:** [L2 space is a Hilbert space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md)

The exponentially weighted [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) consists of measurable functions with $\int_{\mathbb R}|u(x)|^2e^{2\gamma x}\,dx<\infty$. Multiplication by $e^{\gamma x}$ is a unitary map onto [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md). Requiring unweighted square integrability as well produces an intersection that is incomplete under the weighted norm alone. For example, $\mathbf1_{[-N,0]}$ is a weighted [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md) for $\gamma>0$, but its weighted limit $\mathbf1_{(-\infty,0]}$ is not in unweighted [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md).
