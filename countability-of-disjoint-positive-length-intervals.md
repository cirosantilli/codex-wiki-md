# Countability of disjoint positive-length intervals

↑ **Parent:** [Countable set](countable-set.md)

A family of pairwise disjoint real intervals of positive length is [countable](countable-set.md). Each interval contains a [rational number](rational-number.md) in its interior by the [density of the rational numbers](density-of-the-rational-numbers.md). Fix an enumeration of the [rational numbers](rational-number.md) and select the first member lying inside each interval. Disjointness makes this selection an [injection](injective-function.md) into a [countable set](countable-set.md). The argument also applies to disjoint nonempty open subsets of the real line.
