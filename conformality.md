# Conformality

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

The local angle-preservation property of a [conformal map](conformal-map.md). At a regular point its [derivative](derivative.md) is a positive scaling times an orthogonal linear map. Consequently composition multiplies [gradient](gradient.md) lengths by that scaling, as follows directly from the [chain rule](chain-rule.md).
