# Unit-root autoregressive process

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md)

An autoregressive polynomial with a root at one gives a unit-root model. The basic example $X_t=X_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$ is a [random walk](random-walk.md), whose variance grows with time when the increments have positive variance. [Differencing](differencing.md) removes this root and recovers its [white noise](white-noise.md) increments.
