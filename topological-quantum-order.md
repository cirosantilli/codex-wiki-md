# Topological quantum order

↑ **Parent:** [Topological quantum matter](topological-quantum-matter-split.md)

The local-pair condition for [topological quantum order](topological-quantum-order.md) asks for an orthogonal partner state with [local indistinguishability](local-indistinguishability.md) on supports of diameter at most a fixed fraction of the system diameter, with a fixed error smaller than one. This criterion captures a form of macroscopic information hidden from local observables; it is not a complete definition of every topological phase.
