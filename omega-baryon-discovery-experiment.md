# Omega baryon discovery experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Omega baryon](omega-baryon.md)

The 1964 report exposed Brookhaven's 80-inch hydrogen [bubble chamber](bubble-chamber.md) to a mass-separated $5.0\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$ negative-[kaon](kaon.md) beam. A photographed decay chain, track [momenta](momentum-split.md) and bubble density supported the [particle identification](particle-identification.md). Two converted [photons](photon.md) helped reconstruct a neutral [pion](pion.md). Alternative assignments were tested kinematically. The authors reported an [Omega baryon](omega-baryon.md) candidate with $Q=-1$, $S=-3$ and [mass](mass.md) $1686\pm12\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$, reserving a detailed systematic-error discussion for further events.[Original Barnes et al. letter, Phys.Rev.Lett.12(1964), pp.204–206: Fig.2, TableI and final identification](observation-of-a-hyperon-with-strangeness-minus-three-by-v-e-barnes.md)

This is an inference from several linked observations. A [charged particle](charged-particle.md) can leave a visible [bubble-chamber track](bubble-chamber-track.md), while a neutral segment must be reconstructed from its production and decay products. Agreement between possible [decay channels](decay-channel.md), [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) and the reconstructed [mass](mass.md) is stronger evidence than a suggestive track shape alone. The event tests a predicted [hadron](hadron.md) pattern; it does not photograph the three [strange quarks](strange-quark.md) individually.

For example, an independently constructed two-body kinematic check uses

$$
M^2c^4=(E_1+E_2)^2-c^2|\mathbf p_1+\mathbf p_2|^2.
$$

In a parent rest frame with equal and opposite daughter [momenta](momentum-split.md), the momentum sum vanishes, giving $Mc^2=E_1+E_2$. In units $c=1$, stipulated daughter [masses](mass.md) $m_1=1$, $m_2=1/4$ and momentum magnitude $p=3/4$ give $E_1=5/4$, $E_2=\sqrt{10}/4$ and $M=(5+\sqrt{10})/4\simeq2.04057$. A common [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) changes the daughter [energies](energy-split.md) and [momenta](momentum-split.md) while preserving this reconstructed [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md). These chosen units and numbers are an illustration, not a reanalysis of the 1964 event.
