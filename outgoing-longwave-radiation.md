# Outgoing longwave radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative flux](radiative-flux.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outgoing_longwave_radiation)

[Outgoing longwave radiation](outgoing-longwave-radiation.md) is the outward thermal [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) at the top of the atmosphere, integrated over the specified thermal spectrum and outward directions. It differs from a directional spectral [radiance](radiance.md).
