# Trace formula for periodic points of a subshift

↑ **Parent:** [Subshift of finite type](subshift-of-finite-type.md)

A [fixed point](fixed-point.md) of the $n$th [iteration of a map](iterated-function.md) of a [subshift of finite type](subshift-of-finite-type.md) is determined uniquely by its length-$n$ block with a permitted closing transition. Summing the counts of length-$n$ closed walks over their starting symbols gives the displayed [trace](matrix-trace.md). This counts points whose least period divides $n$. If $P_n$ counts points of least period exactly $n$, then $\operatorname{tr}(A^n)=\sum_{d\mid n}P_d$, and [Möbius inversion](mobius-inversion-formula.md) gives $P_n=\sum_{d\mid n}\mu_{\mathrm M}(n/d)\operatorname{tr}(A^d)$. The number of distinct least-period-$n$ [periodic orbits](periodic-orbit.md) is $P_n/n$.
