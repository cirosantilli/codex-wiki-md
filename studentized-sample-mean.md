# Studentized sample mean

↑ **Parent:** [Sample mean](sample-mean.md)

For [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) observations with finite positive [variance](variance-split.md), the [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md), consistency of the [sample variance](sample-variance.md) and [Slutsky's theorem](slutsky-theorem.md) give $T_n\xrightarrow{d}N(0,1)$. Under an independent [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) sample it instead has the exact finite-sample [Student t-distribution](student-s-t-distribution.md) with $n-1$ degrees of freedom, as described by the [studentized normal sample mean](studentized-normal-sample-mean.md). The asymptotic statement does not require normal individual observations.
