# Electrical network

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted graph](weighted-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_network)

An electrical network is a [weighted graph](weighted-graph.md) whose edge weights are interpreted as electrical conductances. Random-walk hitting quantities can then be expressed through voltages, currents, and [effective resistance](effective-resistance.md).
