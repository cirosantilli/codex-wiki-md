# Quantum dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Dual pair in a monoidal category](dual-pair-in-a-monoidal-category.md)

Closing an identity strand using compatible [evaluation morphisms](evaluation-morphism.md) and [coevaluation morphisms](coevaluation-morphism.md) gives the categorical dimension in a pivotal or ribbon setting. For the standard two-dimensional [quantum enveloping algebra of sl2](quantum-enveloping-algebra-of-sl2.md) [module](module-mathematics.md), the weighted contraction has diagonal coefficients $q^{-1},q$, giving [quantum dimension](quantum-dimension.md) $q+q^{-1}=[2]_q$. The chosen duality and framing conventions matter.
