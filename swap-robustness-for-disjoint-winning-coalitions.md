# Swap robustness for disjoint winning coalitions

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted voting game](weighted-voting-game.md)

A zero-one [coalitional game](transferable-utility-game.md) has this property if exchanging one player each way between two disjoint winning [coalitions](coalition-game-theory.md) leaves at least one winning. Any [weighted voting game](weighted-voting-game.md) satisfies it: the exchange preserves their aggregate weight, which is at least twice the quota, whereas two losing coalitions would have aggregate weight strictly below twice the quota.

The disjointness restriction is substantive. If every winning [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) contains a common player, the condition is vacuous. It is consequently weaker than restrictions allowing exchanges between overlapping winning [coalitions](coalition-game-theory.md), and does not characterize [weighted voting games](weighted-voting-game.md).
