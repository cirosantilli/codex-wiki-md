# Thermal Doppler broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler broadening](doppler-broadening.md)

If nonrelativistic emitters of mass $m$ have rest-frame angular frequency $\omega_0$, then the one-dimensional [Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-velocity-distribution.md) produces a Gaussian line with standard deviation

$$
\sigma_\omega=\frac{\omega_0}{c}\sqrt{\frac{k_BT}{m}}.
$$

Measuring the line width therefore determines the gas temperature when nonthermal broadening is negligible or separately modeled.
