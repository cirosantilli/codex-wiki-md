# Internal quantum efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Light-emitting diode efficiency](light-emitting-diode-efficiency.md)

For the convention used in [light-emitting diode efficiency](light-emitting-diode-efficiency.md), the internally generated [photon](photon.md) rate divided by the rate of [electron](electron.md) and [electron hole](electron-hole.md) pairs reaching the emitting layer. Its denominator must be stated when comparing measurements.
