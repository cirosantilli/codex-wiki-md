# Commutative ring spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Ring spectrum](ring-spectrum.md)

A [commutative ring spectrum](commutative-ring-spectrum.md), in the homotopy-category convention, satisfies $\mu\tau=\mu$, where $\tau$ interchanges the factors of the [smash product of spectra](smash-product-of-spectra.md). This condition alone does not assert an $E_\infty$ refinement.
