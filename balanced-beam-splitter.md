# Balanced beam splitter

↑ **Parent:** [Beam splitter](beam-splitter.md)

A lossless [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md) that gives equal output [probabilities](probability.md) for a photon incident in one input port. Balance constrains the magnitudes of the [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md); the [beam splitter scattering matrix](beam-splitter-scattering-matrix.md) also specifies their relative [wave phases](phase-waves.md).
