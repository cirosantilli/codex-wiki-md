# Napkin ring problem

↑ **Parent:** [Solid geometry](solid-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napkin_ring_problem)

Removing a central cylindrical hole from a sphere leaves a solid whose [volume](volume.md) depends only on its remaining height $h$. With $a=h/2$, sphere radius $R$ and hole radius $r_0$, the rim gives $R^2-r_0^2=a^2$. Every horizontal slice is an annulus of area $\pi(a^2-z^2)$, so integrating from $-a$ to $a$ gives $V=4\pi a^3/3=\pi h^3/6$. This slice equality explains why changing $R$ while retaining $h$ leaves the volume unchanged.
