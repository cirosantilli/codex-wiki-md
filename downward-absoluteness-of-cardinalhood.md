# Downward absoluteness of cardinalhood

↑ **Parent:** [Set-theoretic absoluteness](set-theoretic-absoluteness.md)

If a larger transitive model regards an ordinal $\kappa$ as a [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md), then a smaller transitive model does too: any bijection in the smaller model witnessing otherwise would remain in the larger one. The converse can fail when the larger model contains a new bijection between $\kappa$ and a smaller ordinal.
