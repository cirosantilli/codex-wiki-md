# Gaussian density kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel for density estimation](kernel-for-density-estimation.md)

The Gaussian density kernel is the standard [normal probability density](normal-density.md) used as a unit-integral [kernel for density estimation](kernel-for-density-estimation.md). Its second moment is one and $R(K_N)=1/(2\sqrt\pi)$. Its [canonical kernel](canonical-kernel-for-density-estimation.md) has scale $(2\sqrt\pi)^{-1/5}$. It is a function of one smoothing displacement, distinct from the two-argument [Gaussian kernel](gaussian-kernel.md) used as a [positive-semidefinite kernel](positive-semidefinite-kernel.md).
