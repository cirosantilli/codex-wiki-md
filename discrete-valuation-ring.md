# Discrete valuation ring

↑ **Parent:** [Valuation ring](valuation-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_valuation_ring)

A discrete valuation ring is a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md) with exactly one nonzero maximal ideal. Every nonzero element of its [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) is a unit times a unique integer power of a uniformizer.
