# Atomic operation

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronization (computing)](synchronization-computing.md)

An operation that takes effect indivisibly relative to the specified competing accesses. An atomic increment protects the whole read-modify-write; separate atomic loads and stores do not make their combined sequence an [atomic operation](atomic-operation.md).
