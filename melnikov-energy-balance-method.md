# Melnikov energy-balance method

↑ **Parent:** [Nearly Hamiltonian system](nearly-hamiltonian-system.md)

For a weak perturbation of a planar [Hamiltonian system](hamiltonian-system.md), integrate the first-order [orbital derivative](orbital-derivative.md) of the Hamiltonian along an unperturbed homoclinic orbit. This measures the first-order separation of stable and unstable manifolds on a transverse section. A simple zero as a parameter varies permits a nearby homoclinic connection by the implicit function theorem. The resulting leading balance is not a claim that the unperturbed separatrix remains an exact orbit after perturbation.
