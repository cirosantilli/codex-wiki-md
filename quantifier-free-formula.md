# Quantifier-free formula

↑ **Parent:** [First-order formula](first-order-formula.md)

A quantifier-free [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) is built from [atomic formulas](atomic-formula.md) using logical connectives, with no quantifiers. An embedding of [first-order structures](first-order-structure.md) preserves and reflects its truth, by [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) on the [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md). Both positive and negated atomic information are needed for this conclusion in a relational language.
