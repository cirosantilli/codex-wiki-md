# Reflecting boundary condition for a diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Fokker-Planck probability current](fokker-planck-probability-current.md)

A reflecting boundary confines a diffusion without losing probability. Its forward condition is zero outward [Fokker-Planck probability current](fokker-planck-probability-current.md), $J\mathbin\cdot n=0$, and its backward condition is a zero conormal derivative.
