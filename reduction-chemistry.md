# Reduction (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Redox](redox.md)

A [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) decreases an [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md), recording gain in the formal [electron](electron.md) assignment. For $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}+2e^-\to\mathrm{Cu}$, the decrease is $+2\to0$. The reduced [chemical species](chemical-species.md) serves as the [oxidizing agent](oxidizing-agent.md) in the coupled [redox](redox.md).[§4.2, “Oxidation-Reduction Reactions”: reduction as decreasing oxidation number](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

In the [electrochemistry](electrochemistry-split.md) examples here, [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) is gain of [electrons](electron.md) by a [chemical species](chemical-species.md); it occurs at the [cathode](cathode.md). That [electrode](electrode.md) definition holds in both a [galvanic cell](galvanic-cell.md) and an [electrolytic cell](electrolytic-cell.md); it does not fix terminal polarity.
