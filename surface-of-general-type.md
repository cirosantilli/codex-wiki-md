# Surface of general type

↑ **Parent:** [Kodaira dimension](kodaira-dimension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_of_general_type)

A smooth projective surface is of general type when its [Kodaira dimension](kodaira-dimension.md) is two, equivalently when its canonical divisor is big.
