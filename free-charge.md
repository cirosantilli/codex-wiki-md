# Free charge

↑ **Parent:** [Electric charge](electric-charge-split.md)

In macroscopic [electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md), [free charge](free-charge.md) is the part of the [charge density](charge-density.md) represented explicitly as $\rho_f$, separately from [bound charge](bound-charge.md) associated with the [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) of matter. The [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) satisfies $\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\rho_f$. The word free does not imply zero [force](force-split.md) or zero [energy](energy-split.md): it distinguishes this source from the polarization response in the chosen material description.
