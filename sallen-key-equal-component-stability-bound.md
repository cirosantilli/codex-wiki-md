# Sallen-Key equal-component stability bound

↑ **Parent:** [Sallen-Key low-pass filter](sallen-key-low-pass-filter.md)

For equal frequency-setting [resistors](resistor.md) $R$ and [capacitors](capacitor.md) $C$, write $\tau=RC$. The denominator is $1+(3-K)s\tau+s^2\tau^2$. With positive $\tau$, [asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md) requires $K<3$; at $K=3$ the ideal [poles](pole.md) are imaginary, and larger gain gives right-half-plane [poles](pole.md). In particular $K=5$ gives $(1-s\tau)^2$, a repeated unstable [pole](pole.md). A finite formal gain curve must not be confused with stable filter operation.
