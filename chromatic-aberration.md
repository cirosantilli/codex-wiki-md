# Chromatic aberration

↑ **Parent:** [Optical aberration](optical-aberration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_aberration)

Chromatic aberration is wavelength-dependent imaging caused by [optical dispersion](dispersion-optics.md). Axial chromatic aberration changes [focal length](focal-length.md) with [wavelength](wavelength.md); lateral chromatic aberration changes image scale.
