# Half-integer open-string oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [String oscillator](string-oscillator.md)

For a [Neumann-Dirichlet open-string boundary condition](neumann-dirichlet-open-string-boundary-condition.md), the transverse modes have frequencies $r\in\mathbb Z+1/2$. The [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) give $[\alpha_r^i,\alpha_s^j]=r\delta^{ij}\delta_{r+s,0}$ and $\alpha_r^\dagger=\alpha_{-r}$. One derivation expands $X-y=\sum_{r>0}q_r\cos(r\sigma)$: spatial [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) reduces the [Polyakov action](polyakov-action.md) to $(8\alpha')^{-1}\int\sum_r(\dot q_r^2-r^2q_r^2)d\tau$. These are [harmonic oscillators](simple-harmonic-motion.md) of [mass](mass.md) $1/(4\alpha')$, whose normalized [creation operators](creation-operator.md) satisfy $\alpha_{-r}=i\sqrt r\,a_r^\dagger$ in the conventional phased expansion.
