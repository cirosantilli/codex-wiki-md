# Lepton universality

↑ **Parent:** [Charged current](charged-current.md)

The [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) assigns the same [gauge coupling](gauge-coupling.md) to each lepton generation in the [weak charged current](charged-current.md). Different decay rates still arise from masses and phase space. Ratios of [leptonic pion decay](leptonic-pion-decay.md) rates cancel the common hadronic decay constant and quark mixing coefficient, isolating universality and [helicity suppression](helicity-suppression.md). Electromagnetic [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md) must be included for precision comparisons to inclusive measured decay rates.
