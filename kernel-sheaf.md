# Kernel sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of sheaves](morphism-of-sheaves.md)

For a [morphism of sheaves](morphism-of-sheaves.md) of abelian groups or modules, the kernel sheaf has sections $\ker(\mathcal F(U)\to\mathcal G(U))$. These already satisfy the [sheaf gluing axiom](sheaf-gluing-axiom.md). Its [stalk](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) at $x$ is the kernel of $\mathcal F_x\to\mathcal G_x$, since filtered colimits of modules preserve exactness. A sheaf morphism is injective exactly when its kernel sheaf is zero.
