# Reflection at a density step on a string

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic impedance of a stretched string](characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md)

For a massless join between two ideal strings with a common [tension](tension.md) $T$, require continuous transverse [displacement](displacement.md) and continuous transverse force $T\partial_x\Psi$. With $Z_j=\sqrt{T\rho_j}$ for [linear mass densities](linear-mass-density.md) $\rho_j$, the displacement [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) and transmission amplitude are

$$
r=\frac{Z_1-Z_2}{Z_1+Z_2},\qquad t=\frac{2Z_1}{Z_1+Z_2}.
$$

The reflected and transmitted mean-power fractions are $\mathcal R=|r|^2$ and $\mathcal T=(Z_2/Z_1)|t|^2$, with $\mathcal R+\mathcal T=1$. Reflection from the heavier side reverses [phase](phase-waves.md); reflection from the lighter side does not. Total reflection occurs in the limits $Z_2/Z_1\to\infty$ or zero, while a finite positive impedance mismatch transmits some [energy](energy-split.md). Equal [mechanical impedances](mechanical-impedance.md) remove reflection.
