# Minimum companion mass from radial velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md)

For a companion of negligible [mass](mass.md) compared with its host, $M_p\sin i=K[P/(2\pi G)]^{1/3}M_\star^{2/3}\sqrt{1-e^2}$. The observable supplies a projected [mass](mass.md); the minimum at a specified eccentricity occurs for an edge-on orbit. A [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md) can constrain inclination, while low-inclination stars or brown dwarfs can otherwise mimic a lower-[mass](mass.md) companion. The exact relation retains $(M_\star+M_p)^{2/3}$ in the denominator.
