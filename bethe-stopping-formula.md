# Bethe stopping formula

↑ **Parent:** [Collision stopping power](collision-stopping-power.md)

A high-speed approximation for electronic [stopping power](stopping-power.md) of charged particles. Its leading heavy-projectile dependence is $z^2\beta^{-2}[\ln(2m_ec^2\beta^2\Gamma^2/I)-\beta^2]$; low-speed charge exchange and binding effects require corrections.
