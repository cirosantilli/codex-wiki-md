# Hyperbolic zero sequences on a punctured disk

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic metric on the punctured disk](hyperbolic-metric-on-the-punctured-disk.md)

The curvature-minus-one [hyperbolic metric on the punctured disk](hyperbolic-metric-on-the-punctured-disk.md) assigns distance $2\log n$ along the radial geodesic from $e^{-1}$ to $e^{-n^2}$. Its exponential distances therefore sum to a finite value; the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) gives the same conclusion for any fixed basepoint. Nevertheless no nonzero bounded [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) has these zeros: the [Riemann removable singularity theorem](riemann-removable-singularity-theorem.md) extends it at zero, and the [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md) then makes it identically zero. Simple connectivity is necessary in the hyperbolic characterization of bounded analytic zero sets.
