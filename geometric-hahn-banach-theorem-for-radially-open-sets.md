# Geometric Hahn-Banach theorem for radially open sets

↑ **Parent:** [Hahn-Banach separation theorem](hahn-banach-separation-theorem.md)

For a nonempty [radially open convex set](radially-open-convex-set.md) $C$ disjoint from a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $U$, there is a nonzero [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $L$ whose [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) contains $U$ and avoids $C$. Choose $a\in C$ and the [Minkowski functional](minkowski-functional.md) $p$ of $C-a$. On $U+\mathbb Ra$, set $g(u+ta)=-t$. Disjointness implies $p(u-a)\geq1$, which gives $g\leq p$ for negative $t$; nonnegativity of $p$ gives it for nonnegative $t$. The algebraic [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) extends $g$ to $L\leq p$. For $x\in C$, $L(x)+1=L(x-a)<1$, so $L(x)<0$. Also $L(a)=-1$ and $L|_U=0$.
