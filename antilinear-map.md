# Antilinear map

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antilinear_map)

For complex [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md), an antilinear map satisfies $A(\alpha u+\beta v)=\overline\alpha Au+\overline\beta Av$. It is linear over the [real numbers](real-number.md) and conjugates complex [scalar multiplication](scalar-multiplication.md). This differs from two [conjugate linear operators](conjugate-linear-operators.md), which are related by similarity rather than conjugate-linearity.
