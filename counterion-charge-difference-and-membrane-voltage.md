# Counterion charge difference and membrane voltage

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitance](capacitance.md)

For an ideal two-plate [capacitor](capacitor.md) with opposite [charges](electric-charge-split.md), a stated inside-minus-outside [charge](electric-charge-split.md) difference is twice the inner plate [charge](electric-charge-split.md). The [voltage](voltage.md) is inner plate [charge](electric-charge-split.md) divided by [capacitance](capacitance.md). Confusing a plate [charge](electric-charge-split.md) with the difference of plate [charges](electric-charge-split.md) creates a factor-two error in inferred [membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) [voltage](voltage.md).
