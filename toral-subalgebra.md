# Toral subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md)

A toral subalgebra of a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$ is a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak a$ such that $\operatorname{ad}a$ is [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md) for every $a\in\mathfrak a$. Here [diagonalizability](diagonalizable-matrix.md) is over the stated ground [field](field.md), and refers to the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). In arguments that use a simultaneous [weight decomposition](weight-decomposition.md), commutativity of $\mathfrak a$ is stated as well. For a [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) of [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md), [Cartan subalgebras](cartan-subalgebra.md) are examples. Semisimplicity on the [defining representation](defining-representation-of-a-matrix-lie-algebra.md) of a [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md) follows from [adjoint semisimplicity in the symplectic Lie algebra](adjoint-semisimplicity-in-the-symplectic-lie-algebra.md).
