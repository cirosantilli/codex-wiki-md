# Neutral-hydrogen column-density distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Lyman-alpha absorption](lyman-alpha-absorption.md)

The neutral-hydrogen column-density distribution counts [Lyman-alpha absorption](lyman-alpha-absorption.md) systems per unit [column density](column-density.md) and [redshift](redshift.md). A count in logarithmic column bins is $N\ln(10)f(N,z)$. At low forest columns an approximate power law $f\propto N^{-\beta}$ with $\beta$ near $1.5$ is useful, but the slope and normalization depend on column range and epoch. [Lyman limit systems](lyman-limit-system.md) are optically thick at the hydrogen photoionization edge; [damped Lyman-alpha systems](damped-lyman-alpha-system.md) have strong radiative damping wings. A distribution per [absorption distance](absorption-distance.md) instead removes the geometrical path factor.
