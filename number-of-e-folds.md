# Number of e-folds

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)

The number of e-folds during an interval of expansion is the logarithmic growth of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md):

$$
N=\log\frac{a_f}{a_i}=\int_{t_i}^{t_f}H\,dt.
$$
