# Restriction of monad algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Monad morphism](monad-morphism.md)

A [monad morphism](monad-morphism.md) $\alpha:S\Rightarrow T$ induces a [functor](functor.md) $\mathcal C^T\to\mathcal C^S$ between [Eilenberg-Moore categories](eilenberg-moore-category.md), identical on underlying objects and arrows. Its unit equation gives the restricted algebra's unit law. Naturality and the multiplication equation give $(a\alpha_A)S(a\alpha_A)=aTa\,\alpha_{TA}S\alpha_A=a\mu_A^T T\alpha_A\alpha_{SA}=a\alpha_A\mu_A^S$. Naturality also preserves algebra [morphisms](morphism.md).
