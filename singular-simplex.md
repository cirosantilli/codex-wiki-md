# Singular simplex

↑ **Parent:** [Singular homology](singular-homology.md)

A singular $n$-simplex in a space $X$ is a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $\sigma:\Delta^n\to X$ from the standard $n$-simplex. Its alternating restrictions to the faces form its [boundary](boundary-operator.md).
