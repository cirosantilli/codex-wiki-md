# Memory consolidation

↑ **Parent:** [Memory in animals](memory-in-animals.md)

Processes that stabilize or reorganize information after acquisition. [Synaptic consolidation](synaptic-consolidation.md) concerns cellular changes, whereas [systems consolidation](systems-consolidation.md) concerns changing interactions among [brain](brain.md) regions. Better later performance need not mean a literal transfer of a complete memory between two stores.
