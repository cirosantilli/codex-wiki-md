# Gerstenhaber algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Graded algebra](graded-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerstenhaber_algebra)

A Gerstenhaber algebra has an associative product making it a [graded commutative algebra](graded-commutative-algebra.md) and a degree-minus-one [graded Lie bracket](graded-lie-bracket.md). In the left convention, $[u,vw]=[u,v]w+(-1)^{(|u|-1)|v|}v[u,w]$. The shifted degrees $|u|-1$ determine the signs of antisymmetry and the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md). [Hochschild cohomology](hochschild-cohomology.md) and the [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md) of polynomial [derivations](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) are examples.
