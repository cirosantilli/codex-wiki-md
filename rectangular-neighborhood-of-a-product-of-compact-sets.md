# Rectangular neighborhood of a product of compact sets

↑ **Parent:** [Compact space](compact-space.md)

If $K$ and $L$ are [compact subsets](compact-space.md) of two spaces and $W$ is an [open neighborhood](open-neighbourhood.md) of their product, there are [open sets](open-set.md) $U,V$ with $K\times L\subseteq U\times V\subseteq W$. First use [compactness](compact-space.md) of $L$ to produce a rectangle around each slice $\{x\}\times L$, intersecting finitely many first factors and uniting second factors. Then use [compactness](compact-space.md) of $K$, uniting finitely many resulting first factors and intersecting second factors. No [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) assumption is required.
