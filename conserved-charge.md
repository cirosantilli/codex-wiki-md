# Conserved charge

↑ **Parent:** [Conserved current](conserved-current.md)

An integral of the time component of a [conserved current](conserved-current.md) is a [conserved charge](conserved-charge.md) when the spatial boundary flux vanishes. Charges need not come from an immediately apparent elementary symmetry; a field theory can have an infinite higher-derivative current hierarchy. A [Noether charge](noether-charge.md) is obtained from the symmetry current supplied by the [Noether theorem](noether-theorem.md).
