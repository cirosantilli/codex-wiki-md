# First-cousin identity-by-descent probabilities

↑ **Parent:** [Identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md)

For [first cousins](first-cousin.md) in an otherwise [outbred pedigree](outbred-pedigree.md), compare the copies transmitted from their related parents, who are [full siblings](full-sibling.md). These transmissions trace to the same shared grandparent with [probability](probability.md) $1/2$ and then to the same homologous copy of that grandparent with probability $1/2$, giving $k_1=1/4$. The unrelated parental contributions cannot match, so two shared copies have probability zero. Selecting one random copy from each cousin chooses those two potentially shared copies with probability $1/4$, giving [kinship coefficient](kinship-coefficient.md) $k_1/4=1/16$.
