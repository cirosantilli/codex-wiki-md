# Symmetry enlargement under group translates

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action.md)

For a [group](group-split.md) of [Euclidean isometries](euclidean-isometry.md), every element preserves the displayed union of images of a [set](set-split.md), because left multiplication permutes its [group](group-split.md) indices. Its full setwise [isometry group](isometry-group.md) can be larger even when $T$ itself has no nonidentity symmetry. Take $G$ to be the [integer](integer.md) [translations](translation-geometry.md) of the plane and $T=\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,2)\}$. By [rigidity of a scalene triangle](rigidity-of-a-scalene-triangle.md), $T$ has trivial [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md). Yet $S=\mathbb Z^2$, whose quarter-turn about the origin is a symmetry outside the [translation](translation-geometry.md) [group](group-split.md).
