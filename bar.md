# Bar

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure-split.md)

A [pressure](pressure-split.md) unit defined by $1\ \mathrm{bar}=10^5\ \mathrm{Pa}$. In a logarithmic gas [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md), a pressure expressed in [bars](bar.md) must still be divided by the same-unit reference [pressure](pressure-split.md) to form a dimensionless ratio.
