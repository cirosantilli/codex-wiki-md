# Perfect Morse function

↑ **Parent:** [Morse function](morse-function.md)

A [Morse function](morse-function.md) is perfect over a specified field when its numbers of [critical points](critical-point.md) equal the [Betti numbers](betti-number.md) over that field. Over the rationals this forces every integer [matrix](matrix.md) in its [Morse chain complex](morse-chain-complex.md) to have rank zero, hence to vanish. Consequently its integral [homology](homology-split.md) is free of rank $c_i$ in every degree; a [manifold](topological-manifold.md) with integral [homology](homology-split.md) torsion cannot admit such an integral-critical-count perfect function.
