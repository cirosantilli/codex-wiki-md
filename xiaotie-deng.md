# Xiaotie Deng

↑ **Parent:** [List of computer scientists](list-of-computer-scientists.md)

A [computer scientist](computer-scientist.md) working on [game theory](game-theory-split.md), [algorithms](algorithm-split.md) and [computational complexity theory](computational-complexity-theory.md). Co-author with [Christos Papadimitriou](christos-papadimitriou.md) of [On the Complexity of Cooperative Solution Concepts by Xiaotie Deng and Christos Papadimitriou](on-the-complexity-of-cooperative-solution-concepts-by-xiaotie-deng-and-christos-papadimitriou.md), which studies [induced subgraph games](induced-subgraph-game.md).

Primary profile: [Peking University profile identifying Xiaotie Deng and his research interests](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/info/1211/1974.htm).
