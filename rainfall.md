# Rainfall

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric precipitation](atmospheric-precipitation.md)

Liquid-water precipitation reaching the surface. In a catchment water balance its rate is an input measured as water depth per time; only the part not absorbed or otherwise lost becomes [rainfall](rainfall.md) excess feeding overland flow.
