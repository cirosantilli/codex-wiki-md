# Velocity field of a dense coaxial vortex-ring array

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex ring](vortex-ring.md)

A long array of equal co-oriented [vortex rings](vortex-ring.md), spaced by $l\ll r$, approximates an azimuthal [vortex sheet](vortex-sheet.md) on a cylinder of radius $r$. The [circulation](circulation-physics.md) per unit axial [length](length.md) is $\Gamma/l$. The conditions $\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0$ and $\nabla\times\mathbf u=\boldsymbol\omega$ are mathematically analogous to the magnetostatic equations for a [solenoid](solenoid.md). Away from its ends, an array long compared with $r$ produces nearly uniform axial flow $\Gamma/l$ inside and weak flow outside. A finite array has return flow outside connecting its two ends. The uniform-core approximation fails close to individual vortex cores and near the ends, and the field requires a stated [circulation](circulation-physics.md) orientation and background flow.
