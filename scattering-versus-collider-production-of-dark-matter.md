# Scattering versus collider production of dark matter

↑ **Parent:** [Dark-matter direct detection](dark-matter-direct-detection.md)

A low-momentum dark-matter–[nucleon](nucleon.md) elastic [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) and a high-energy collider pair-production [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) are different observables. Crossing of amplitudes does not equate their numerical values. Mediator [masses](mass.md), couplings, [nuclear matrix elements](nuclear-matrix-element.md), parton distributions and kinematics enter the relation. Collider sensitivity further requires a trigger, visible recoil, acceptance and background treatment. Multiplying a direct-detection [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) by collider [luminosity](luminosity.md) is therefore only a hypothetical dimensional estimate unless a production model justifies the substitution.
