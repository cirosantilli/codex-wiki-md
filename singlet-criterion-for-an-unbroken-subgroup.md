# Singlet criterion for an unbroken subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md)

An unbroken [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$ must fix the [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) $v$ of a [scalar field](scalar-field.md) in a [group representation](group-representation.md) $V$. Thus a nonzero such $v$ exists exactly when $V^H\ne\{0\}$. For a [compact group](compact-group.md), [complete reducibility of compact-group representations](complete-reducibility-of-compact-group-representations.md) identifies this fixed [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) with the sum of the [trivial representations](trivial-representation.md) in the [restriction of a representation](restriction-of-a-representation.md) $V|_H$. Every $v\in V^H$ has $H\subseteq\operatorname{Stab}(v)$; equality requires a separate [stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md) calculation. The criterion neither determines the full unbroken [group](group-split.md) nor guarantees that a given [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) has its minimum at that direction.
