# Gell-Mann and Zweig quark models

↑ **Parent:** [Quark model](quark-model.md)

In his 1964 paper, [Murray Gell-Mann](murray-gell-mann.md) proposed fractional-charge [quarks](quark.md), constructing [baryons](baryon.md) and [mesons](meson.md) from them. He also distinguished a mathematical constituent model from the hypothesis of finite-mass physical particles. The successful bookkeeping did not itself settle that distinction.[pp.214–215: triplet construction and discussion of physical versus mathematical quarks](a-schematic-model-of-baryons-and-mesons-by-murray-gell-mann.md)

[George Zweig](george-zweig.md)'s 1964 CERN model used three constituents called aces, with fractional [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and [baryon number](baryon-number.md) $1/3$. Its abstract also proposed an experimental search.[Version2 title page and abstract, dated21February1964](an-su3-model-for-strong-interaction-symmetry-and-its-breaking-version-2-by-george-zweig.md) The theoretical construction and the experiment that might discriminate between interpretations are separate parts of the argument. The [Omega baryon discovery experiment](omega-baryon-discovery-experiment.md) illustrates the power of testing an incomplete particle pattern against new observations.
