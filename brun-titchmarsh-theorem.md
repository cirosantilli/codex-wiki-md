<h1 id="brun-titchmarsh-theorem">Brun–Titchmarsh theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Upper-bound sieve](upper-bound-sieve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brun–Titchmarsh_theorem)

For $x>q$ and $(a,q)=1$, the [prime-counting function](prime-counting-function.md) in an [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) satisfies

$$
\pi(x;q,a)\leq\frac{2x}{\phi(q)\log(x/q)}.
$$

The denominator involves the [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md) and the length-to-modulus ratio. In particular, for $q\leq x^{1-\eta}$ with fixed $\eta>0$, this is $O_\eta(x/(\phi(q)\log x))$.
