Here $B(p)=\sqrt{p^2+4\lambda}$ and $S_j$ are the [Fourier sine transforms](fourier-sine-transform.md) of the boundary values. The [modified Helmholtz closed spectral one-form](modified-helmholtz-closed-spectral-one-form.md) and its reflected [global relations](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md) remove the unknown normal traces. Equivalently, four reflected [fundamental solutions](fundamental-solution-of-a-linear-differential-operator.md) give a zero-boundary [Green function](green-s-function.md) in the quadrant. Its inward derivatives give the displayed formula. The two terms recover the bottom and left traces respectively; matching corner values ensure continuity at the corner.
