# Brownian representation replication in a local volatility market

↑ **Parent:** [Local volatility model](local-volatility-model.md)

For a square-integrable discounted claim martingale $dM=h\,dW$ and discounted stock $d\widetilde S=\widetilde S\sigma\,dW$, choose stock holdings $h/(\widetilde S\sigma)$. The remaining wealth lies in the bond. A nonnegative claim makes this replication admissible; discounted admissible wealth is a [supermartingale](supermartingale.md), establishing the minimal initial cost.
