# Instrumental variable

↑ **Parent:** [Causal inference](causal-inference-split.md)

An instrumental variable changes an [exposure](exposure.md) or [treatment](treatment.md) but affects the outcome only through that exposure. Together, [instrument relevance](instrument-relevance.md), [instrumental-variable independence](instrumental-variable-independence.md), and the [exclusion restriction](exclusion-restriction.md) identify causal effects under suitable structural assumptions.
