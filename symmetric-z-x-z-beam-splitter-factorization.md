# Symmetric z-x-z beam splitter factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Beam splitter scattering matrix](beam-splitter-scattering-matrix.md)

Since $HZH=X$, [unitary conjugation](unitary-conjugation.md) gives $H e^{-i\beta Z/2}H=e^{-i\beta X/2}$. Multiplying by the two outer $R_z(\alpha)$ rotations gives

$$
U=\begin{pmatrix}e^{-i\alpha}\cos(\beta/2)&-i\sin(\beta/2)\\-i\sin(\beta/2)&e^{i\alpha}\cos(\beta/2)\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The case $\alpha=\beta=\pi/2$ equals $-iH$. Equal output probabilities for one input do not by themselves identify an optical component with the [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md); relative [wave phases](phase-waves.md) also matter.
