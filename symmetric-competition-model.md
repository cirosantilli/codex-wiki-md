# Symmetric competition model

↑ **Parent:** [Population dynamics](population-dynamics.md)

Two populations grow at the same per-capita rate and each loses members at a rate proportional to their product. On the invariant diagonal $x=y=z$, the [logistic differential equation](logistic-differential-equation.md) gives $z(t)=\lambda/[\mu+(\lambda-\mu)e^{-\lambda t}]$ for $z(0)=1$ and positive rates. The positive symmetric equilibrium $(\lambda/\mu,\lambda/\mu)$ is stable along that diagonal but unstable transversely: $(x-y)'=\lambda(x-y)$, while the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has eigenvalues $-\lambda,+\lambda$. Thus an exactly symmetric deterministic solution can relax toward coexistence while a small imbalance grows. A corresponding [density-dependent Markov jump process](density-dependent-markov-jump-process.md) has a controlled [fluid limit](fluid-limit.md) on each fixed finite interval, rather than uniform control for all time.
