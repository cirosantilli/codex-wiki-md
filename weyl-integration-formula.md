# Weyl integration formula

↑ **Parent:** [Compact Lie group](compact-lie-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_integration_formula)

Let $G$ be a [connected](connected-space.md) [compact Lie group](compact-lie-group.md), $T$ a [maximal torus](maximal-torus.md), and $W$ its [Weyl group](weyl-group.md). With normalized [Haar measures](haar-measure.md) and $D(t)=\prod_{\alpha>0}(1-e^{-\alpha}(t))$, every [continuous](continuous-function.md) [class function](class-function.md) satisfies $\int_G f(g)\,dg=|W|^{-1}\int_T f(t)|D(t)|^2\,dt$. For a general [continuous function](continuous-function.md), replace $f(t)$ by its average $\int_{G/T}f(gtg^{-1})\,d(gT)$. [Conjugation](conjugation.md) from $G/T\times T$ covers the regular elements $|W|$ times, and its real [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $|D(t)|^2$.
