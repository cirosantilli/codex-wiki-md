# Triangle count in a binomial random graph

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial random graph](binomial-random-graph.md)

If $X$ counts triangles in $G(n,p)$, then

$$
\mathbb EX=\binom n3p^3
$$

and

$$
\operatorname{var}X
\leq\binom n3p^3
+2\binom n2\binom{n-2}2p^5.
$$

Thus $np\to\infty$ implies $\operatorname{var}X/(\mathbb EX)^2\to0$, and the [second moment method](second-moment-method.md) gives $\mathbb P(X>0)\to1$.
