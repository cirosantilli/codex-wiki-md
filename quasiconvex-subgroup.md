# Quasiconvex subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiconvex subset](quasiconvex-subset.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasiconvex_subgroup)

A subgroup is quasiconvex when it is a [quasiconvex subset](quasiconvex-subset.md) of a Cayley graph. In a [hyperbolic group](hyperbolic-group.md), a finitely generated subgroup is quasiconvex exactly when it is [quasi-isometrically embedded](quasi-isometrically-embedded-subgroup.md).
