# Dynamical diffraction

↑ **Parent:** [Electron diffraction](electron-diffraction.md)

[diffraction](diffraction.md) in which repeated scattering and coupling between transmitted and diffracted beams significantly affect their amplitudes. Electron-diffraction intensity can then vary strongly with thickness and excitation error; it is not generally just the squared single-scattering [crystal basis structure factor](crystal-basis-structure-factor.md).
