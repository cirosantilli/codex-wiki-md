# Ammonia molecular orbital diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital diagram](molecular-orbital-diagram.md)

In pyramidal [ammonia](ammonia.md), the hydrogen [symmetry orbitals](symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md) span $A_1\oplus E$ of the [C3v molecular point group](c3v-molecular-point-group.md). Nitrogen $2s$ and $2p_z$ both have $A_1$ symmetry and $(2p_x,2p_y)$ span $E$. In the planar [D3h molecular point group](d3h-molecular-point-group.md), these become $A_1'$, $A_2''$ and $E'$, respectively, while the hydrogen combinations span $A_1'\oplus E'$. Thus the planar out-of-plane [lone pair](lone-pair.md) is a symmetry-isolated [nonbonding orbital](nonbonding-orbital.md); pyramidalization permits mixing with the other $a_1$ functions.[The ammonia molecule, Figures 7–9 and Tables 4–5: orbital symmetries in both geometries](explaining-the-geometry-of-simple-molecules-using-molecular-orbital-energy-level-diagrams-built-by-using-symmetry-principles-by-sergio-p-machado-and-roberto-b-faria.md)
