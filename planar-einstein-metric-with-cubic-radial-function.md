# Planar Einstein metric with cubic radial function

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md)

This metric has $R_{ab}=-3g_{ab}$ and [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) $\Lambda=-3$ in unit curvature-radius normalization. Its regular static coframe is real when $F>0$ and $z\ne0$. Setting $q=\alpha z^3$, its six independent [curvature 2-form](curvature-2-form.md) coefficients are $-(1-q)$ in the $03$ and $12$ planes and $-(1+q/2)$ in the other four planes. Their Ricci contractions cancel $q$. The [Weyl tensor](weyl-tensor.md) squared invariant is $12\alpha^2z^6$, so nonzero $\alpha$ does not give constant sectional curvature despite the Einstein equation.
