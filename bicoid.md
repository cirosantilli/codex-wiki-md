# Bicoid

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A [Drosophila](drosophila.md) maternal [homeodomain](homeodomain.md) [protein](protein-split.md) concentrated anteriorly. It activates zygotic [transcription](transcription-biology.md) in a [concentration](concentration.md)-dependent context and also represses translation of [Caudal](caudal.md) [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md).
