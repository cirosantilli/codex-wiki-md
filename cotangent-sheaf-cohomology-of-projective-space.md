# Cotangent-sheaf cohomology of projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of relative Kähler differentials](sheaf-of-relative-kahler-differentials.md)

For $n\geq1$, the cotangent form of the [Euler sequence](euler-sequence.md) is $0\to\Omega^1_{\mathbb P^n/k}\to\mathcal O(-1)^{\oplus(n+1)}\to\mathcal O\to0$. The [cohomology of twisting sheaves on projective space](cohomology-of-twisting-sheaves-on-projective-space.md) and the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) give $H^1(\mathbb P_k^n,\Omega^1)\cong k$, with every other cohomology group zero. Therefore the [Euler characteristic of a coherent sheaf](euler-characteristic-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md) is $-1$. The nonzero class is the connecting image of the constant section $1$.
