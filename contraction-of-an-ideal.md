# Contraction of an ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Extension and contraction of ideals](extension-and-contraction-of-ideals.md)

For a ring homomorphism $f:R\to A$ and an ideal $J\subseteq A$, its contraction is $J^c=f^{-1}(J)$.
