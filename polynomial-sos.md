# Polynomial SOS

↑ **Parent:** [Nonnegative polynomial](nonnegative-polynomial.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_SOS)

A [sum of squares polynomial](polynomial-sos.md) is a real polynomial expressible as $p=\sum_iq_i^2$ for real polynomials $q_i$. In the homogeneous degree-$2m$ case the summands may be taken homogeneous of degree $m$. Every such polynomial is nonnegative on real inputs; the converse fails for multivariate polynomials in general.

A real [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $p$ is a sum of squares polynomial if $p=\sum_\ell q_\ell^2$ for real [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) $q_\ell$. Such a representation certifies global nonnegativity. If $m(z)$ is the vector of relevant [monomials](monomial.md), the identity $p(z)=m(z)^TGm(z)$ with a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) $G$ encodes a sum of squares through a [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md). Equating coefficients is linear in $G$, so finding a certificate is a [semidefinite program](semidefinite-programming.md).
