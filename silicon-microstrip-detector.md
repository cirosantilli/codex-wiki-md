# Silicon microstrip detector

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detector](particle-detector.md)

A [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) [particle detector](particle-detector.md) uses narrow conducting strips on depleted silicon to measure the position of an ionizing particle. The collecting [electric field](electric-field-split.md) transports the generated [electrons](electron.md) and [holes](electron-hole.md); the induced signals identify the traversed strips.
