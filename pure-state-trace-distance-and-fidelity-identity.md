# Pure-state trace distance and fidelity identity

↑ **Parent:** [Fidelity of quantum states](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md)

For pure states $\rho=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ and $\sigma=|\varphi\rangle\langle\varphi|$, the unsquared [quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) is $F=|\langle\psi|\varphi\rangle|$. In their two-dimensional span, $\rho-\sigma$ has [trace](matrix-trace.md) zero and [determinant](determinant.md) $-(1-F^2)$, hence [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\pm\sqrt{1-F^2}$. Its [trace norm](trace-norm.md) is twice the positive [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md), giving [trace distance](trace-distance.md) $D=\sqrt{1-F^2}$.
