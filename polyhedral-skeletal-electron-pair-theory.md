# Polyhedral skeletal electron pair theory

↑ **Parent:** [Multicentre bonding](multicentre-bonding.md)

An electron-counting approach relating [skeletal electron pairs](skeletal-electron-pair.md) to a cluster vertex count $n$. A [closo](closo-cluster.md) deltahedron has $n+1$ pairs; [nido](nido-cluster.md) and [arachno](arachno-cluster.md) frameworks have $n+2$ and $n+3$. The count excludes electrons in externally directed bonds or [lone pairs](lone-pair.md). It is a structural guide with a defined domain, not a rule that every molecule with the same total valence count has the same shape.
