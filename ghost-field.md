# Ghost field

↑ **Parent:** [Quantized field](quantized-field.md)

A ghost field in the general dynamical sense has a kinetic term with the wrong sign and makes the Hamiltonian unbounded below or produces negative-norm states. This use is distinct from a [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](faddeev-popov-ghost.md), which is an auxiliary Grassmann field for gauge fixing.
