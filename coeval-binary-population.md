# Coeval binary population

↑ **Parent:** [Binary star](binary-star.md)

A coeval binary has components formed at the same time. A population of such [binary stars](binary-star.md) can still have a distribution of system ages. If component masses have [independence](independent-random-variables.md) and the state at age $t$ is determined separately for each component, the states have [conditional independence](conditional-independence.md) given $t$. If $g(t)$ and $w(t)$ are the conditional [probabilities](probability.md) of two states, the unconditional unordered pair fractions are $\mathbb E[g(t)^2]$ and $2\mathbb E[g(t)w(t)]$, not generally products of marginal state fractions.
