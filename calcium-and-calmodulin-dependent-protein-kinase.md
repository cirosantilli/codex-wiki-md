# Calcium and calmodulin-dependent protein kinase

↑ **Parent:** [Protein kinase](protein-kinase.md)

The plant symbiotic kinase CCaMK integrates calcium binding and [calmodulin](calmodulin.md)-dependent regulation. Its activation can connect nuclear [calcium signaling](calcium-signaling.md) to symbiotic developmental programs.
