# Reflected Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflected_Brownian_motion)

One-dimensional reflected Brownian motion on $[0,\infty)$ has the same law as $|B_t|$. The [Tanaka formula](tanaka-s-formula.md) represents it as a Brownian motion plus a nondecreasing [local time of a semimartingale](local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md) that grows only at zero.
