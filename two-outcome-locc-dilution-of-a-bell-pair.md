# Two-outcome LOCC dilution of a Bell pair

↑ **Parent:** [Local operations and classical communication](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md)

Let $c,s\geq0$ and $c^2+s^2=1$. Starting from the [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) $(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$, Alice applies the [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) with [Kraus operators](kraus-operator.md) $M_0,M_1$ displayed above. Completeness follows from $M_0^\dagger M_0+M_1^\dagger M_1=I$. Each outcome has [probability](probability.md) $1/2$. The normalized outputs are $c|00\rangle+s|11\rangle$ and $s|00\rangle+c|11\rangle$. Communicating the outcome allows both parties to apply a [Pauli X gate](pauli-x-gate.md) in the second branch, making both outputs equal to the first. Thus this [LOCC](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md) protocol deterministically converts one [Bell pair](bell-pair.md) into any two-[qubit](qubit.md) [pure state](pure-state.md) with these [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md), after appropriate [local unitary operations](local-unitary-operation.md). It includes the [product state](product-state.md) endpoint without [postselection](postselection.md).
