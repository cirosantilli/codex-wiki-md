# Kelvin-Voigt model

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)

The [Kelvin-Voigt model](kelvin-voigt-model.md) puts a spring and dashpot in parallel. Their common displacement makes their forces add. With the convention $e^{i\omega t}$, the complex stiffness is $k+i\omega\mu$, giving storage stiffness $k$ and loss stiffness $\omega\mu$. Under a force step, $x=(F_0/k)(1-e^{-t/\tau})$ with $\tau=\mu/k$. The series spring–dashpot is a [Maxwell fluid](linear-maxwell-fluid.md) analogue and has different relaxation and creep behavior.
