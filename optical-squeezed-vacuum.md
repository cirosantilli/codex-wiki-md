# Optical squeezed vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Squeezed vacuum state](squeezed-vacuum-state.md)

A single optical mode in a [squeezed vacuum state](squeezed-vacuum-state.md). In principal [optical quadratures](optical-quadrature.md), its variances are $e^{-2r}/4$ and $e^{2r}/4$, while its mean [photon](photon.md) number is $\sinh^2r$. Its mean field vanishes. A degenerate [optical parametric amplifier](optical-parametric-amplifier.md) generates the state by pair creation with phase fixed by the pump. Loss admixes unsqueezed vacuum and limits the measured variance reduction.
