# Optical momentum of a ray

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics-split.md)

For a [light ray](light-ray.md) parametrized by [arc length](arc-length.md) $s$, with [unit tangent vector](unit-tangent-vector.md) $t$ in a smooth positive [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $\mu$, the optical momentum is $p=\mu t$. This is the momentum for optical path-length normalization. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) for [Fermat principle](fermat-principle.md), $\int\mu(r)|r'(\tau)|d\tau$, gives $d(\mu t)/ds=\nabla\mu$. Therefore an index independent of $z$ conserves $p_z=\mu\,dz/ds$. In coordinates $r(z),\theta(z)$ this is $\mu/\sqrt{1+r'^2+r^2\theta'^2}$, the first integral supplied by the [Beltrami identity for several dependent variables](beltrami-identity-for-several-dependent-variables.md).
