<h1 id="characteristic-exponent-of-a-levy-process">Characteristic exponent of a Lévy process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy process](levy-process.md)

Use the convention

$$
\mathbb E e^{iuX_t}=e^{-t\Psi(u)}.
$$

Then $\Psi$ is the characteristic exponent of the [Lévy process](levy-process.md). Some authors instead write $e^{t\psi(u)}$, with $\psi=-\Psi$. The sign convention should always be specified. The [Lévy–Khintchine formula](levy-khintchine-formula.md) describes all possible exponents.
