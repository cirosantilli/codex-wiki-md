# Signed two-flux composite-fermion filling

↑ **Parent:** [Composite fermion](composite-fermion.md)

Attach two statistical flux quanta $\Phi_0=h/e$ per [electron](electron.md) at density $n_s$. For $B>0$, $B^*=B-2n_s\Phi_0=B(1-2\nu)$ and the signed effective filling is $\nu^*=n_s\Phi_0/B^*=\nu/(1-2\nu)$. Inversion gives $\nu=\nu^*/(2\nu^*+1)$. Integer $\nu^*=p$ gives $p/(2p+1)$, while $\nu^*=-p$ gives $p/(2p-1)$. Using nonnegative effective filling instead requires explicitly tracking the effective-field sign. At half filling the mean effective field vanishes.
