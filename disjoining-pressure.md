# Disjoining pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film equation](thin-film-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disjoining_pressure)

Disjoining pressure is the thickness-dependent normal stress caused by intermolecular interactions between nearby interfaces. An attractive law $\Pi(h)=V/h^3$ grows as a film thins and can drive rupture.
