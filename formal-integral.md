# Formal integral

↑ **Parent:** [Formal power series](formal-power-series.md)

For a [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) over a [field](field.md) of [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md), the displayed coefficientwise operation chooses constant coefficient zero. Its [formal derivative](formal-derivative.md) is the original [formal power series](formal-power-series.md): differentiating the term with exponent $n+1$ cancels its denominator. Conversely, formally integrating a [formal derivative](formal-derivative.md) removes the original constant term. These are algebraic identities; analytic [convergence](convergence.md) is not required.
