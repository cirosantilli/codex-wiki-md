# Inverting low-pass amplifier

↑ **Parent:** [Inverting amplifier](inverting-amplifier.md)

An ideal [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md) with a grounded noninverting input, input [resistor](resistor.md) $R_i$, and parallel feedback [resistor](resistor.md) $R_f$ and [capacitor](capacitor.md) $C$ implements a [low-pass filter](low-pass-filter.md). The [virtual short](virtual-short.md) and [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) give $V_{\rm out}/V_{\rm in}=-Z_f/R_i$, where $Z_f=R_f/(1+i\omega R_fC)$. The DC gain magnitude is $R_f/R_i$ and the [cutoff frequency](cutoff-frequency.md) is $1/(2\pi R_fC)$. The [capacitance](capacitance.md) reduces high-[frequency](frequency.md) feedback [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md), causing the gain magnitude to fall.
