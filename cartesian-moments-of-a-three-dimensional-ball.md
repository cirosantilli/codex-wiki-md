# Cartesian moments of a three-dimensional ball

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic tensor integral](isotropic-tensor-integral.md)

[Spherical symmetry](spherical-symmetry.md) makes moments over a ball [isotropic tensors](isotropic-tensor.md). Odd moments vanish under $x\mapsto-x$, while the second and fourth moments are

$$
\int_{|x|\leq a}x_ix_j\,dV=\frac{4\pi a^5}{15}\delta_{ij},\qquad
\int_{|x|\leq a}x_ix_jx_kx_l\,dV=\frac{4\pi a^7}{105}(\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}).
$$

For the second moment, a [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md) gives $3\alpha=\int r^2\,dV=4\pi a^5/5$. For the fourth, complete index symmetry makes the three coefficients equal and a double [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md) gives $15\beta=\int r^4\,dV=4\pi a^7/7$. These identities turn polynomial [volume integrals](volume-integral.md) into scalar radial integrals and are useful for inertia and rotational averages.
