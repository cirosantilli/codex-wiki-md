# Observational unit

↑ **Parent:** [Experimental unit](experimental-unit.md)

An [observational unit](observational-unit.md) is the object or occasion supplying a recorded response. Several [observational units](observational-unit.md) can belong to one [experimental unit](experimental-unit.md); counting them as independent treatment [replicates](replicate.md) creates [pseudoreplication](pseudoreplication.md).
