# Electric-dipole radar cross section of a small conducting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Radar cross section](radar-cross-section.md)

Retaining only the [electric dipole](electric-dipole.md) induced in a small sphere gives $\mathbf p=4\pi\epsilon_0a^3\mathbf E_{\rm inc}$. In the backward direction its [differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md) is $k^4a^6$, so the [radar cross section](radar-cross-section.md) is $4\pi k^4a^6$. This response model omits the sphere's induced [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md); it must be distinguished from [perfect-conductor sphere dipole interference](perfect-conductor-sphere-dipole-interference.md).
