# Critical galvanometer transfer function

↑ **Parent:** [Critical step response of a galvanometer](critical-step-response-of-a-galvanometer.md)

With $e^{i\omega t}$ [phasors](phasor.md), [critical damping](critical-damping.md) gives $\Theta(\omega)=F_0/(\omega_0+i\omega)^2$, [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $F_0/(\omega_0^2+\omega^2)$ and [phase](phase-waves.md) $-2\arctan(\omega/\omega_0)$. The response strongly suppresses [alternating currents](alternating-current.md) well above the mechanical [frequency](frequency.md).
