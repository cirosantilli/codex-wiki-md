# Disk flaring

↑ **Parent:** [Disk aspect ratio](disk-aspect-ratio.md)

A disk flares when its [disk aspect ratio](disk-aspect-ratio.md) increases outward. This is stronger than the statement that its height increases: a disk with $H\propto r$ has constant aspect ratio and is not flaring by this definition. The radial [temperature](temperature.md) and [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) profiles determine flaring in a specified vertical equilibrium model.
