# Random walk on a multigraph

↑ **Parent:** [Random walk on a graph](random-walk-on-a-graph.md)

An undirected graph may have repeated edges. Choosing an incident edge uniformly gives transition probability equal to the edge multiplicity divided by the vertex degree. Its stationary probability is proportional to degree and its stationary fluxes are symmetric, making it a [reversible Markov chain](reversible-markov-chain.md).
