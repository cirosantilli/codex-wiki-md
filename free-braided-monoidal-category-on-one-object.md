# Free braided monoidal category on one object

↑ **Parent:** [Braided monoidal category](braided-monoidal-category.md)

The [braided monoidal category](braided-monoidal-category.md) generated by one object: objects are parenthesized tensor expressions in that object and the unit, and [morphisms](morphism.md) are structural isomorphisms and crossings subject to precisely the [monoidal category](monoidal-category.md) and [braiding](braiding.md) axioms.
