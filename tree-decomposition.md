# Tree decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

A [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md) of bags of [graph](graph-split.md) [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) such that every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) occurs in a bag, both endpoints of every [graph](graph-split.md) [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) occur together in a bag, and the bags containing any fixed [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) form a connected [connected subgraph of a tree](connected-subgraph-of-a-tree.md). Its width is $\max_t|B_t|-1$. The connected-occurrence condition lets subproblems agree on shared [variables](variable-mathematics.md) while solving a [constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md); an arbitrary cover of [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) by small bags is insufficient.
