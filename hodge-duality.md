# Hodge duality

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge number](hodge-number.md)

On a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) of complex dimension $n$, the [conjugate-linear Hodge star](conjugate-linear-hodge-star.md) gives a bijection of harmonic forms in complementary bidegrees. The [Dolbeault Hodge decomposition](dolbeault-hodge-decomposition-on-a-compact-hermitian-manifold.md) identifies these harmonic spaces with [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md) and yields the displayed equality.
