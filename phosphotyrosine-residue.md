# Phosphotyrosine residue

↑ **Parent:** [Tyrosine residue](tyrosine-residue.md)

A [phosphotyrosine residue](phosphotyrosine-residue.md) is a [tyrosine residue](tyrosine-residue.md) modified by [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md). Its location and surrounding [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md) help determine which [SH2 domains](sh2-domain.md) can recognize it.
