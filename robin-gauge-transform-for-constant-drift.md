# Robin gauge transform for constant drift

↑ **Parent:** [Advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md)

Writing $u=e^{-\beta x/2}v$ transforms $u_t=u_{xx}+\beta u_x$ into $v_t=v_{xx}-\beta^2v/4$. A prescribed coordinate derivative $u_x=g$ at an endpoint becomes $v_x-(\beta/2)v=e^{\beta x/2}g$ there. Thus a [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) becomes a [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md). The signs are coordinate-derivative signs at both endpoints; outward normal derivatives require a sign change at the left endpoint.
