# Depolarizing noise weight above one

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum depolarizing channel](quantum-depolarizing-channel.md)

The displayed qubit [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) is completely positive for $0\leq q\leq4/3$. Indeed its [Pauli channel](pauli-channel.md) probabilities are $1-3q/4$ for the identity and $q/4$ for each nonidentity Pauli operator. In the range $q>1$ the coefficient $1-q$ is negative, so the displayed expression is not a convex mixture of the unchanged and maximally mixed states, even though the Pauli representation is a valid [random unitary channel](random-unitary-channel.md). Its [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) is contracted and reversed by the factor $1-q$. The [Holevo capacity of a qubit depolarizing channel](holevo-capacity-of-a-qubit-depolarizing-channel.md) remains $1-h_2(q/2)$ throughout this interval.
