# Strong subadditivity of quantum entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](subadditivity-of-von-neumann-entropy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_subadditivity_of_quantum_entropy)

For a tripartite [density operator](density-matrix.md) $\rho_{ABC}$,

$$
S(\rho_{ABC})+S(\rho_B)\leq S(\rho_{AB})+S(\rho_{BC}).
$$

Equivalently, conditioning on an additional quantum system cannot increase [quantum conditional entropy](quantum-conditional-entropy.md).
