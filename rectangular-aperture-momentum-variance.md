# Rectangular-aperture momentum variance

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md)

A normalized hard rectangular spatial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) has a sinc-squared [momentum density](momentum-density.md). Its central lobe has finite first-zero width $h/d$, but its second [momentum](momentum-split.md) moment diverges. Thus that diffraction width is not a finite rms uncertainty. Smooth-edge states can have finite moments and satisfy the usual uncertainty inequality without this idealization.
