# Haken sum

↑ **Parent:** [Normal surface coordinates](normal-surface-coordinates.md)

The Haken sum of compatible [normal surfaces](normal-surface.md) is obtained by resolving their intersection curves in the way that preserves the normal [disk](disk-mathematics.md) types. It adds [normal surface coordinates](normal-surface-coordinates.md), [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) and mod-two [homology](homology-split.md) classes. The alternative local resolution can create returning face arcs and need not be normal.
