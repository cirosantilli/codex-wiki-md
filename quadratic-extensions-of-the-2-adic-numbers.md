# Quadratic extensions of the 2-adic numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Square-class group of the 2-adic numbers](square-class-group-of-the-2-adic-numbers.md)

The three generators $-1,2,5$ of the [square-class group of the 2-adic numbers](square-class-group-of-the-2-adic-numbers.md) give these seven distinct extensions. In characteristic different from two, completing the square shows every quadratic extension is of this form, and comparing coefficients in $\sqrt e=a+b\sqrt d$ proves that two such fields coincide precisely when $e/d$ is a square. The only unramified member is $\mathbb Q_2(\sqrt5)$: $(1+\sqrt5)/2$ is integral with irreducible reduction $X^2+X+1$. Conversely any unramified quadratic extension has that residue field, and [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) lifts a residue root of $X^2-X-1$, recovering the same field.
