<h1 id="optimal-forster-distance-for-two-state-contrast">Optimal Förster distance for two-state contrast</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [FRET efficiency](fret-efficiency.md)

For [FRET efficiency](fret-efficiency.md) $E(R)=1/[1+(R/R_0)^6]$ and distinct positive reporter distances A and B, the absolute contrast is maximized at the geometric mean [Förster distance](forster-distance.md) $R_0=\sqrt{AB}$. Substitution of $x=R_0^6$ makes the derivative have the sign of $A^6B^6-x^2$. The maximum is $|B^3-A^3|/(B^3+A^3)$ and the two efficiencies then sum to one. If $A=B$, every choice gives zero contrast and there is no unique optimum.
