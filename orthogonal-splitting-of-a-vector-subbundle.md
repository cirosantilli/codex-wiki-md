# Orthogonal splitting of a vector subbundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector subbundle](vector-subbundle.md)

A [fiber metric](fiber-metric.md) on a smooth [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E$ gives a smooth [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) $P:E\to F$ onto any [vector subbundle](vector-subbundle.md). In a [local frame](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) of $E$, let $G$ be the metric [matrix](matrix.md) and $A$ the columns of a frame of $F$. Then $P=A(A^tGA)^{-1}A^tG$, proving smoothness. The complementary subbundle $F^\perp=\ker P$ maps isomorphically onto the [quotient vector bundle](quotient-vector-bundle.md). Explicitly $v\mapsto(Pv,[v])$ is a [vector bundle isomorphism](vector-bundle-isomorphism.md) $E\to F\oplus(E/F)$, with inverse $(f,[v])\mapsto f+(I-P)v$. This inverse is independent of the representative $v$, but the splitting depends on the chosen metric.
