# Skeleton of a CW complex

↑ **Parent:** [CW complex](cw-complex.md)

The $q$-skeleton of a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) is the [subcomplex](simplicial-subcomplex.md) consisting of its [topological cells](topological-cell.md) of dimension at most $q$. A [cellular map](cellular-map.md) preserves these skeleta. The [cellular approximation theorem](cellular-approximation-theorem.md) lets continuous maps respect this filtration up to [homotopy](homotopy.md).
