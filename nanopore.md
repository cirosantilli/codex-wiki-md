# Nanopore

↑ **Parent:** [Microfluidics](microfluidics.md)

A [nanopore](nanopore.md) is a channel or aperture with a nanometre-scale constriction. A solid-state realization can connect conducting liquid reservoirs, permitting ionic current to report particle passage through [resistive-pulse sensing](resistive-pulse-sensing.md). A geometrical pore diameter alone does not determine its conductance: axial shape, reservoir access, surface charge, solution conductivity and interfacial effects can all matter. This definition concerns a physical aperture, without a biological application.
