# Killing-form Yang-Mills Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

For a real compact semisimple algebra, $B=-\kappa$ is a positive internal metric. With signature $(+---)$ and connection $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+A_\mu$, the healthy Lagrangian is $\mathcal L=-B(F_{\mu\nu},F^{\mu\nu})/(4g^2)=\kappa(F_{\mu\nu},F^{\mu\nu})/(4g^2)$. [Gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) follows from Killing-form invariance and $\delta_XF=\epsilon[X,F]$. The physical [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) density is $[B(E_i,E_i)+B(B_i,B_i)]/(2g^2)$ after imposing the [Gauss law constraint in gauge theory](gauss-law-constraint-in-gauge-theory.md) and treating the spatial boundary flux appropriately. An Abelian factor needs a separate positive invariant metric because its [Killing form](killing-form.md) vanishes.
