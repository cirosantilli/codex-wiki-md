# Maxwell dissipation under controlled stress and strain

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Maxwell fluid](linear-maxwell-fluid.md)

For a series [Maxwell model](linear-maxwell-fluid.md) with $\tau=\eta/k$, $E''=k\omega\tau/(1+\omega^2\tau^2)$. At fixed [strain](strain.md) amplitude the energy dissipated per cycle is $\pi E''\varepsilon_0^2$ and peaks at $\omega\tau=1$. At fixed [stress](stress.md) amplitude it is instead $\pi\sigma_0^2/(\eta\omega)$, which has no finite-frequency maximum. Specifying the imposed variable and whether loss means energy per cycle or [power](power.md) is essential.
