# Power-product map to a slit half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz-Christoffel mapping](schwarz-christoffel-mapping.md)

For $0<k<1$, principal upper-half-plane branches give a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) onto the upper half-plane cut along $[0,\tau]$, where $\tau=2k^k(1-k)^{1-k}e^{i\pi k}$. Its [derivative](derivative.md) is $(z-1+2k)(z-1)^{k-1}(z+1)^{-k}$. The two prevertices $-1,1$ map to the slit base, and $1-2k$ maps to its tip. Boundary traversal goes out along the slit and back, while $g(z)=z+1-2k+O(1/z)$ at infinity. The [argument principle](argument-principle.md) gives one preimage of every point off the slit.
