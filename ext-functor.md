# Ext functor

↑ **Parent:** [Hom functor](hom-functor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ext_functor)

The Ext functors are the right derived functors of the [Hom functor](hom-functor.md). They can be computed by applying $\operatorname{Hom}_R(-,N)$ to a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) of $M$ and taking [cohomology](cohomology-split.md).
