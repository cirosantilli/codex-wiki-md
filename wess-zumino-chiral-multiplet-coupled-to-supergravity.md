# Wess-Zumino chiral multiplet coupled to supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Wess–Zumino model](wess-zumino-model.md)

Localizing the rigid [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) of a free [Wess–Zumino model](wess-zumino-model.md) produces a derivative of the supersymmetry parameter multiplying the [supercurrent](supercurrent.md). The [Noether gauging procedure](noether-gauging-procedure.md) introduces a [gravitino](gravitino.md) coupled to this current. Closure also requires a [vierbein](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md), the [Einstein-Hilbert action](einstein-hilbert-action.md), covariant matter kinetic terms and higher-order interactions. The leading gravitino-matter coupling contains the derivative of the scalar, not its undifferentiated value.
