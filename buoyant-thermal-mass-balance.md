# Buoyant thermal mass balance

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyant thermal](buoyant-thermal.md)

If a [buoyant thermal](buoyant-thermal.md) receives volume inflow $J$ with [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $g_p$ and receives no other [fluid entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md), its mass and volume balances imply $\dot V=J$ and $d(g_tV)/dt=g_pJ$. If $V\propto t^{9/4}$ and $g_t\propto t^{-5/4}$, then $g_t=9g_p/4$.
