# Spherical subset of a Coxeter system

↑ **Parent:** [Standard parabolic subgroup](standard-parabolic-subgroup.md)

A subset $T\subseteq S$ is spherical when its [standard parabolic subgroup](standard-parabolic-subgroup.md) $W_T$ is finite.
