# Formal associative deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

A formal associative deformation is a unital $k[[t]]$-bilinear product continuous for the [adic topology](adic-topology.md) on $A[[t]]$ of the form $a*b=ab+\sum_{r\geq1}t^r\mu_r(a,b)$. Each coefficient $\mu_r$ is a $k$-bilinear map $A\otimes A\to A$, and the whole product satisfies [associativity](associative-property.md). For an infinite-dimensional [algebra](algebra-split.md), $A[[t]]$ is the completed [tensor product](tensor-product.md) $\varprojlim_nA\otimes k[t]/(t^n)$ and generally differs from the ordinary $A\otimes k[[t]]$.
