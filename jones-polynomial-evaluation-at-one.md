# Jones polynomial evaluation at one

↑ **Parent:** [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md)

For a nonempty [link](link.md) with $\ell$ components, the [Jones polynomial skein relation](jones-polynomial-skein-relation.md) at $t=1$ makes a crossing change preserve the value. Crossing changes turn the [link](link.md) into an [unlink](unlink.md) with the same number of components. Repeated [Jones polynomial of a split union](jones-polynomial-of-a-split-union.md) gives the formula. In particular the [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md) is never the zero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md).
