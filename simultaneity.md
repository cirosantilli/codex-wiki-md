# Simultaneity

↑ **Parent:** [Relativity of simultaneity](relativity-of-simultaneity.md)

Two [spacetime events](event-relativity.md) are simultaneous in a specified [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) when their time coordinates are equal. The [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) changes this condition for spatially separated events. A [spacelike separation](spacelike-separation.md) admits an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) in which the two events are simultaneous; a nonzero [null separation](null-separation.md) does not.
