# Precipitation (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precipitation_(chemistry))

[Precipitation](precipitation-chemistry.md) is formation of a solid from a solution. A newly formed solid can create a surface for [heterogeneous catalysis](heterogeneous-catalysis.md); observing it does not establish the composition or catalytic role of that surface.
