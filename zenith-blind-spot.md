# Zenith blind spot

↑ **Parent:** [Altazimuth mount](altazimuth-mount.md)

A [zenith blind spot](zenith-blind-spot.md) is a region in which an [altazimuth mount](altazimuth-mount.md) cannot follow a target because the required axis motion exceeds a drive constraint. A finite slew across an exact [zenith](zenith.md) crossing gives a lost-track interval; a full forbidden footprint additionally depends on the target trajectory, acceleration and the control strategy. It need not be circular.
