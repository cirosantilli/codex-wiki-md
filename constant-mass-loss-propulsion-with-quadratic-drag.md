# Constant mass-loss propulsion with quadratic drag

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

A body ejecting [mass](mass.md) at constant rate $Q>0$ and relative exhaust [speed](speed.md) $V>0$, against [quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md) $\alpha u^2$, has remaining [mass](mass.md) $m=m_0-Qt$. A short-interval [momentum](momentum.md) balance gives $m\dot u=QV-\alpha u^2$. From rest, [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) gives

$$
u(t)=\sqrt{\frac{QV}{\alpha}}\tanh\left(\sqrt{\frac{\alpha V}{Q}}\log\frac{m_0}{m_0-Qt}\right).
$$

For final [mass](mass.md) $m_f>0$, this formula applies until $t=(m_0-m_f)/Q$. The zero-drag limit is the [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) $u_f=V\log(m_0/m_f)$. Two [dimensionless variables](dimensionless-variable.md) are $\alpha V/Q$ and $m_0/m_f$.
