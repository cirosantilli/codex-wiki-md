# General linear group modulo the unitary group

↑ **Parent:** [Principal bundle](principal-bundle.md)

Let $H(n)$ be the real [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md). The [polar decomposition of an invertible complex matrix](polar-decomposition-of-an-invertible-complex-matrix.md) gives a diffeomorphism

$$
H(n)\times U(n)\longrightarrow GL(n,\mathbb C),
\qquad (B,u)\longmapsto e^Bu.
$$

Its inverse sends $g$ to

$$
B=\frac12\log(gg^*),
\qquad u=e^{-B}g.
$$

Right multiplication by $U(n)$ changes only $u$, so the orbit space is smoothly $H(n)$ and the quotient map is a globally trivial [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md).
