# Laplace expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)

Expand the [determinant](determinant.md) along any fixed row $i$ using the displayed formula, where $A^{(i,j)}$ deletes row $i$ and column $j$. The signed [matrix minors](minor-linear-algebra.md) are its [cofactors](cofactor.md). Applying the same formula to the [transpose](transpose.md) gives the column expansion. The row formula follows from the [Leibniz formula for determinants](leibniz-formula-for-determinants.md) by grouping [permutations](permutation.md) according to the selected column in row $i$. For a [lower triangular matrix](lower-triangular-matrix.md), expanding along the first row reduces the determinant to $A_{11}$ times a smaller triangular determinant; induction gives the product of all diagonal entries, even when some vanish.
