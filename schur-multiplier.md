# Schur multiplier

↑ **Parent:** [Group homology](group-homology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schur_multiplier)

The Schur multiplier of a group $G$ is its second integral [group homology](group-homology.md) group:

$$
M(G)=H_2(G,\mathbb Z).
$$
