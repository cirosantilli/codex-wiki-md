# Halocline

↑ **Parent:** [Density stratification](density-stratification.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halocline)

A layer with a pronounced vertical change in [salinity](salinity.md) is a halocline. When relatively fresh surface water lies over saltier water, its contribution to [density stratification](density-stratification.md) inhibits vertical exchange. A cold Arctic halocline can separate [sea ice](sea-ice.md) from warmer Atlantic water; increased heat at depth does not imply an equally increased [ocean heat flux](ocean-heat-flux.md) at the ice unless the intervening exchange also changes.
