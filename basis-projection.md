# Basis projection

↑ **Parent:** [Schauder basis](schauder-basis.md)

The nth basis projection associated with a [Schauder basis](schauder-basis.md) is

$$
P_n\left(\sum_{k=1}^\infty a_ke_k\right)=\sum_{k=1}^na_ke_k.
$$
