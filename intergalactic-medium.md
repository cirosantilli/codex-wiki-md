# Intergalactic medium

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md)

The intergalactic medium is the gas between [galaxies](galaxy-split.md). Its diffuse, ionized component contains much of the ordinary matter outside stars. [Lyman-alpha absorption](lyman-alpha-absorption.md) measures its small neutral-hydrogen fraction, while hotter phases require other tracers.
