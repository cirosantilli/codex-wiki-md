# Limits of Meissner screening

↑ **Parent:** [Meissner effect](meissner-effect.md)

For a bulk [type-II superconductor](type-ii-superconductor.md) with negligible [demagnetizing field](demagnetizing-field.md), raising the applied [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md) past the lower critical value $H_{c1}$ favors a [superconducting mixed state](superconducting-mixed-state.md) containing [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md) lines; [superconductivity](superconductivity.md) survives until the upper critical value $H_{c2}$. The [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) extends around each vortex over a penetration scale, not solely inside its core. Thus a nonzero average [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field.md) can coexist with [superconductivity](superconductivity.md). Below $H_{c1}$, a [type-II superconductor](type-ii-superconductor.md) also supports a [Meissner state](meissner-state.md); a vortex-free state above the equilibrium entry field may instead be [metastable](metastability.md). [§9.8, type-II critical-field and mixed-state paragraphs; the source uses B\_c1 and B\_c2 for applied-field scales](university-physics-volume-3-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/9-8-superconductivity.md)

Real preparation histories may leave [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md) lines held in place by material inhomogeneities, retaining [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) and producing [hysteresis](hysteresis.md). This [flux pinning](flux-pinning.md) restricts access to equilibrium: it is different from the arbitrary frozen interior [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) of the ideal [perfect conductor](perfect-conductor.md) model. Harvard's vortex-imaging work illustrates strong pinning to bulk disorder in a [type-II superconductor](type-ii-superconductor.md). [Vortex Imaging in Iron Pnictide Superconductor, Fig. 2 and the discussion of disordered vortices and bulk pinning](scanning-tunneling-microscopy-research-by-hoffman-laboratory.md) Separately, [flux conservation in a superconducting loop](flux-conservation-in-a-superconducting-loop.md) and [fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor](fluxoid-and-flux-quantization-in-a-superconductor.md) explain why a hole can retain [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md). Neither bore retention, vortex penetration nor weak screening in a thin sample should be silently identified with the equilibrium [Planar London screening](planar-london-screening.md) calculation.
