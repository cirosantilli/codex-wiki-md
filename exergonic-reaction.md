# Exergonic reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exergonic_reaction)

An [exergonic reaction](exergonic-reaction.md) has negative molar reaction change in [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) under the specified conditions. At fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md), its forward direction is thermodynamically favourable; its [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md) remains a separate question. See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 6.2: exergonic reactions](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/6-2-potential-kinetic-free-and-activation-energy).
