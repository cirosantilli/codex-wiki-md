# Par swap rate

↑ **Parent:** [Interest rate swap](interest-rate-swap.md)

The fixed rate making an [interest rate swap](interest-rate-swap.md) have zero initial value. For unit accrual periods, unit notional and payments $r_t-s$, the floating-leg value is $1-P_0(T)$ and the fixed-leg annuity is $\sum_{t=1}^TP_0(t)$. The formula changes when accrual lengths or payment schedules differ.
