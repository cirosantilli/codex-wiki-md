# Proton separation energy

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear separation energy](nuclear-separation-energy.md)

With bare nuclear [masses](mass.md), the [proton separation energy](proton-separation-energy.md) is the energy needed to remove a [proton](proton.md). A negative value means the daughter plus proton has lower rest [energy](energy-split.md). Proton emission can still have a finite lifetime because of a Coulomb or centrifugal barrier.
