# Piezoelectric coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Direct piezoelectric effect](direct-piezoelectric-effect.md)

A tensor coefficient relating an increment of electrical displacement to [stress](stress.md), or the corresponding converse [strain](strain.md) response to electric field, under stated constitutive conventions. Charge-per-force units $\mathrm{C\,N^{-1}}$ are equivalent to displacement-per-voltage units $\mathrm{m\,V^{-1}}$ for the usual $d$ coefficients.
