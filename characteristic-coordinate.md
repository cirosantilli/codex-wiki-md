# Characteristic coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic hypersurface](characteristic-hypersurface.md)

A characteristic coordinate is a function constant along one family of [characteristics](characteristic-hypersurface.md). For a second-order equation in two variables, two independent characteristic coordinates reduce the principal part to a mixed derivative.
