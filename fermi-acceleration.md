# Fermi acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi_acceleration)

[Fermi acceleration](fermi-acceleration.md) transfers bulk-flow [energy](energy-split.md) to fast charged particles through encounters with moving magnetic scattering structures. Scattering is approximately elastic in each structure's rest frame; transformations between these frames produce a systematic [energy](energy-split.md) change.
