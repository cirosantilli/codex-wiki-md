# Allowed beta-decay momentum spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Allowed beta decay](allowed-beta-decay.md)

With a momentum-independent allowed nuclear [matrix element](matrix-element.md), negligible [nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md) and negligible [neutrino mass](neutrino-mass.md), the [electron](electron.md) spectrum in [natural units](natural-units.md) is $d\Gamma/dp=C F(Z,E)p^2(E_0-E)^2$, where $E=\sqrt{p^2+m_e^2}$. Transforming to an [energy](energy-split.md) spectrum requires the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $dp/dE=E/p$.
