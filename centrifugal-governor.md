# Centrifugal governor

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrifugal_governor)

A mechanism in which rotating weights move outward as [angular speed](angular-speed.md) increases, allowing their displacement to indicate or regulate a machine's speed. The steady configuration follows the balance of actual applied [forces](force-split.md) and [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md), or equivalently of applied [forces](force-split.md) and [centrifugal force](centrifugal-force.md) in a [rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md). Geometry and the restoring mechanism determine the speed-displacement relation.
