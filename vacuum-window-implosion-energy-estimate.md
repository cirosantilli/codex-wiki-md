# Vacuum-window implosion energy estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure-volume work](pressure-volume-work.md)

When a [vacuum](vacuum.md) vessel's window shatters, a nearly atmospheric initial pressure difference gives inward pressure [force](force-split.md) $\Delta p A$ on frontal [area](surface-area.md) $A$. Dividing by the glass [mass](mass.md) $M$ estimates its initial collective [acceleration](acceleration.md). An ideal atmospheric pressure-work budget $p_0V$ for vessel [volume](volume.md) $V$ would give a collective [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) estimate $Mv_{\mathrm{rms}}^2/2\lesssim p_0V$ if assigned entirely to the fragments. This is a model-dependent energy budget, not a universal bound on every fragment's [speed](speed.md): air motion, fragmentation, pressure equalization and wall impacts alter the transfer, and unequal sharing can accelerate a small fragment faster than the collective estimate.
