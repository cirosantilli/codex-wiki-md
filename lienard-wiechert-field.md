<h1 id="lienard-wiechert-field">Liénard-Wiechert field</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Liénard–Wiechert potential](lienard-wiechert-potential.md)

The [Liénard-Wiechert field](lienard-wiechert-field.md) is the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) obtained from the retarded potentials of a moving point charge. In the nonrelativistic radiation zone, the electric radiation field is $\mathbf E_{\rm rad}=q\widehat{\mathbf n}\times(\widehat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf a)/(4\pi\epsilon_0c^2R)$ at [retarded time](retarded-time.md). Its outgoing [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) integrates to the [Larmor formula](larmor-formula.md). Coulomb and induction fields must also be retained when a near-field calculation is required.
