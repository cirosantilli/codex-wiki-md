# Irreducible representations of G6n

↑ **Parent:** [Character table](character-table.md)

For

$$
G_{6n}=\langle a,b:a^{2n}=b^3=1,\ a^{-1}ba=b^{-1}\rangle,
$$

there are $2n$ [linear characters](linear-character.md), given by $a\mapsto\xi^j$, $b\mapsto1$ for a primitive $(2n)$th [root of unity](root-of-unity.md) $\xi$, and $n$ two-dimensional irreducible representations

$$
a\mapsto\begin{pmatrix}0&\xi^k\\\xi^k&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
b\mapsto\begin{pmatrix}\omega&0\\0&\omega^2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad 0\leq k<n,
$$

where $\omega^3=1$ and $\omega\ne1$. The two-dimensional representations parametrized by $\varepsilon$ and $-\varepsilon$ are isomorphic. The [sum of squares of irreducible degrees](sum-of-squares-of-irreducible-degrees.md) is $2n+4n=6n$, so this list is complete.
