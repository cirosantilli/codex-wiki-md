# Congruence modulo an ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient ring](quotient-ring.md)

Two elements of a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) are congruent modulo an [ideal](ideal.md) if their difference lies in that [ideal](ideal.md), equivalently if their images in the [quotient ring](quotient-ring.md) agree. This is an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) compatible with [addition](addition.md) and [multiplication](multiplication.md). For $R=\mathbb Z_p[[T]]$ and $I=p^kR$, it means every coefficient difference is divisible by $p^k$.
