# Radial critical curve of an axisymmetric lens

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational-lensing critical curve](gravitational-lensing-critical-curve.md)

The local and mean [lensing convergence](lensing-convergence.md) obey $2\kappa=2\bar\kappa+r\bar\kappa'$. Hence the radial [derivative](derivative.md) of the lens mapping is $1-\alpha'(r)=1-2\kappa+\bar\kappa$. Its zeros are radial [gravitational-lensing critical curves](gravitational-lensing-critical-curve.md), where radial stretching diverges and images merge on crossing the mapped [gravitational-lensing caustic](gravitational-lensing-caustic.md).
