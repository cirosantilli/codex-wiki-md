<h1 id="newton-s-lens-formula">Newton's lens formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thin lens](thin-lens.md)

For a converging [thin lens](thin-lens.md) forming a [real image](real-image.md) of a real object, let $u,v$ be positive distances from the lens and $f$ the [focal length](focal-length.md). Set $u'=u-f$ and $v'=v-f$, measuring from the neighboring [focal points](focal-point-of-a-hypersurface.md). Substituting into $1/u+1/v=1/f$ gives $(f+u')(f+v')=f(2f+u'+v')$, so $u'v'=f^2$. Other object-image geometries require an explicitly consistent signed-distance convention.
