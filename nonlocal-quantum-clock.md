# Nonlocal quantum clock

↑ **Parent:** [Computational history state](computational-history-state.md)

A [nonlocal quantum clock](nonlocal-quantum-clock.md) records [quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md) time in an abstract $T+1$ dimensional register. A binary encoding uses logarithmically many qubits, but transitions $|t\rangle\langle t-1|$ need not have bounded qubit locality. Extra diagonal penalties may exclude unused binary labels. A [unary quantum clock](unary-quantum-clock.md) provides a different encoding for fixed-locality constructions.
