# Uniform quantum superposition

↑ **Parent:** [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md)

The uniform superposition of all $n$-bit strings is

$$
\frac1{\sqrt{2^n}}\sum_{x\in\{0,1\}^n}|x\rangle
=H^{\otimes n}|0\rangle^{\otimes n}.
$$
