# Divergent series

↑ **Parent:** [Series (mathematics)](series-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divergent_series)

A [series](series-mathematics.md) whose [partial sums](partial-sum.md) do not have a finite [limit of a sequence](limit-of-a-sequence.md). They may grow without bound or oscillate. Some [divergent series](divergent-series.md) belong to the domain of a [summation method](summation-method.md), such as [Cesàro summation](cesaro-summation.md).
