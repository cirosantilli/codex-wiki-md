# Malate synthase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[malate synthase](malate-synthase.md) combines [glyoxylate](glyoxylate.md) with an acetyl unit from acetyl-CoA to form malate. Together with [isocitrate lyase](isocitrate-lyase.md) it provides the characteristic carbon-conserving route of the [glyoxylate shunt](glyoxylate-shunt.md).
