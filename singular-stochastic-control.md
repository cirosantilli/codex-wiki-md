# Singular stochastic control

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic control](stochastic-control.md)

In singular [stochastic control](stochastic-control.md), a control is a [finite-variation process](finite-variation-process.md) rather than only a rate integrated against time. Its increments can have atoms or a part singular with respect to time. The [Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation](hamilton-jacobi-bellman-equation.md) becomes a variational inequality with a constraint on directional derivatives of the [value function](value-function.md).
