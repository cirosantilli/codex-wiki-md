# 2-factor

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

A [spanning subgraph](spanning-subgraph.md) in which every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) has [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) two. In a finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md), its [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md) are [graph cycles](cycle-in-a-graph.md). Removing a [perfect matching](perfect-matching.md) from a [cubic graph](cubic-graph.md) leaves a [2-factor](2-factor.md).
