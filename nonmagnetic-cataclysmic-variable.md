# Nonmagnetic cataclysmic variable

↑ **Parent:** [Cataclysmic variable](cataclysmic-variable.md)

A [cataclysmic variable](cataclysmic-variable.md) whose [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) does not dominate the large-scale [accretion](accretion.md) flow. It normally supports an [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) fed through [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md). The term does not rule out a magnetized [donor star](donor-star.md) or [magnetic braking of a binary star](magnetic-braking-of-a-binary-star.md).
