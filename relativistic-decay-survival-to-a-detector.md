# Relativistic decay survival to a detector

↑ **Parent:** [Time dilation](time-dilation.md)

A freely moving unstable particle of speed $v$ travels a laboratory distance $R$ in time $R/v$, accumulating [proper time](proper-time.md) $R/(\gamma v)$. For rest-frame [half-life](half-life.md) $T$ and [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md), its survival probability is the displayed expression. At least half survive exactly when $\gamma vT\geq R$. The minimum speed is therefore

$$
v_{\min}=\frac{cR}{\sqrt{R^2+c^2T^2}}.
$$

The relevant flight length is $\gamma vT$, rather than $\gamma cT$ for a particle whose speed is appreciably less than $c$.
