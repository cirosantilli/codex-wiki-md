<h1 id="molybdenum-iii-chloride">Molybdenum(III) chloride</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

In the alpha form, MoCl3 has edge-sharing octahedral layers distorted by Mo–Mo dimer formation. Metal-centred [electron](electron.md) pairing contrasts with the more localized d electrons of CrCl3.
