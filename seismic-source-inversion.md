# Seismic source inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)

[Seismic source inversion](seismic-source-inversion.md) estimates source location, origin time and source strength or mechanism from [seismograms](seismogram.md). A symmetric [seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md) has six independent entries. At fixed location and origin time, its predicted [seismograms](seismogram.md) are [linear](linearity.md) in those entries, allowing [variable projection](variable-projection.md) to reduce the nonlinear search.
