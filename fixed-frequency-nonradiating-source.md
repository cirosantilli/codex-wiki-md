# Fixed-frequency nonradiating source

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic inverse source problem](acoustic-inverse-source-problem.md)

A source is nonradiating at frequency $k$ if its outgoing field has zero [far-field pattern](far-field-pattern.md). For every $\chi\in C_c^\infty(A)$, the source $Q=(\Delta+k^2)\chi$ has outgoing field $\psi=\chi$ and is invisible outside $A$. Integration by parts shows that its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) vanishes on $|\boldsymbol\xi|=k$, so it belongs to the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) defined by the [source-to-far-field operator at fixed frequency](source-to-far-field-operator-at-fixed-frequency.md). This family proves nonuniqueness independently of the instability of the inverse.
