# Retarded time

↑ **Parent:** [Retarded potential](retarded-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retarded_time)

For a source point $y(s)$ and field event $(x,t)$, the retarded time solves

$$
t_{\rm ret}+\frac{|x-y(t_{\rm ret})|}{c}=t.
$$
