# Inductance of a toroid with an oblique narrow air gap

↑ **Parent:** [Inductance of a toroidal winding](inductance-of-a-toroidal-winding.md)

Let a slender [toroid](toroid.md) have core cross-section $A$, mean length $\ell$ and large relative [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) $\mu$. Cut a narrow gap of perpendicular width $t$ whose faces make angle $\theta$ with the local core direction. Continuity of normal [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) gives $B_{g,n}=B\sin\theta$; continuity of tangential [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md) gives $H_{g,t}=B\cos\theta/(\mu\mu_0)$. The gap field is nearly normal to the faces for large $\mu$. Its effective flux area is $A/\sin\theta$, so its leading [magnetic reluctance](magnetic-reluctance.md) is $t\sin\theta/(\mu_0A)$. Adding core reluctance gives the displayed [self-inductance](self-inductance.md). At $45^\circ$, $L\simeq\mu_0N^2A/(\ell/\mu+t/\sqrt2)$ and $H_g\simeq NI/(t+\sqrt2\ell/\mu)$. This retains $\mu t/\ell$ while neglecting fringing and corrections of relative order $t/\ell$ or $1/\mu$.
