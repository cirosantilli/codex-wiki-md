# Boxcar averaging of Poisson shot noise

↑ **Parent:** [Shot noise](shot-noise.md)

Independent arrivals of charges $q$ at rate $\lambda$ give a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) for the number $N$ in time $\tau$, with mean and [variance](variance-split.md) $\lambda\tau$. Since $\overline I_\tau=qN/\tau$ and $I=q\lambda$, its [variance](variance-split.md) is $qI/\tau$. This agrees with integrating the one-sided [shot noise](shot-noise.md) [power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md) $2qI$ through the averaging filter: its [effective noise bandwidth](effective-noise-bandwidth.md) is $1/(2\tau)$. A two-sided convention instead assigns density $qI$ to each sign of [frequency](frequency.md).
