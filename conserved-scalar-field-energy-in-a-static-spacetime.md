# Conserved scalar-field energy in a static spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Static spacetime](static-spacetime.md)

The [stress-energy current from a Killing vector](stress-energy-current-from-a-killing-vector.md) associated with the static time translation gives a positive conserved energy for a massive real scalar. Its hypersurface independence follows from current conservation and the divergence theorem.
