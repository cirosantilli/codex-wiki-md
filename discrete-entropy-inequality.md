# Discrete entropy inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy solution](entropy-solution.md)

For a scalar conservative monotone update with flux $F$, define $Q(a,b;c)=F(a\vee c,b\vee c)-F(a\wedge c,b\wedge c)$. Monotonicity and preservation of constant states give $|U_j^{n+1}-c|\leq|U_j^n-c|-(k/d)(Q_{j+1/2}-Q_{j-1/2})$. At equal states $Q(u,u;c)=\operatorname{sgn}(u-c)(f(u)-f(c))$, the [Kruzhkov entropy flux](kruzhkov-entropy-flux.md). Such discrete inequalities connect stability of a [monotone conservative scheme](monotone-conservative-scheme.md) with the admissibility of its limiting [entropy solution](entropy-solution.md).
