# Interpolating metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Metric exponential candidate](metric-exponential-candidate.md)

A metric space $X$ is interpolating when $d(x,y)=r+s$ implies that some $z$ satisfies $d(x,z)=r$ and $d(z,y)=s$. Interpolation makes the [metric exponential candidate](metric-exponential-candidate.md) satisfy the triangle inequality, so every bounded interpolating metric space is an [exponentiable object](exponentiable-object.md) of $\mathbf{Met}_b$.
