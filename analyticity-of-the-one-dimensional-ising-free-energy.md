# Analyticity of the one-dimensional Ising free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Transfer matrix for the one-dimensional Ising model](transfer-matrix-for-the-one-dimensional-ising-model.md)

At real field and nonzero [temperature](temperature-split.md), every entry of the finite-range Ising transfer matrix is positive. Its largest eigenvalue is simple by the [Perron–Frobenius theorem](perron-frobenius-theorem.md) and depends smoothly on field and temperature. The thermodynamic [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per site is its negative logarithm times $k_BT$. In the nearest-neighbour chain it is analytic and the zero-field [magnetization](magnetization.md) vanishes, despite exponentially long low-temperature correlations. Finite-volume spin-inversion symmetry alone would not suffice to exclude spontaneous thermodynamic order.
