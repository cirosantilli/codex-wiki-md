# Hypercontractive inequality on the Boolean hypercube

↑ **Parent:** [Bonami lemma](bonami-lemma.md)

One form of the hypercontractive inequality is $\lVert T_{1/\sqrt3}f\rVert_4\leq\lVert f\rVert_2$, where $T_\rho$ is the [noise operator on the Boolean hypercube](noise-operator-on-the-boolean-hypercube.md).
