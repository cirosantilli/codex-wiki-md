# Total nonnegativity of a matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

A real rectangular [matrix](matrix.md) is totally nonnegative if every square minor, with row and column indices in increasing order, is nonnegative. A nonnegative bidiagonal [matrix](matrix.md) is totally nonnegative, and products of compatible [totally nonnegative matrices](total-nonnegativity-of-a-matrix.md) have the same property by the [Cauchy–Binet formula](cauchy-binet-formula.md). This controls signs of inverse entries and underlies stability and shape-preserving properties of [B-spline](b-spline.md) collocation. Total nonnegativity permits zero minors; strict total positivity requires every such minor to be positive.
