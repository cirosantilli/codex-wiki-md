# Monodromy group of z squared plus z to the minus two

↑ **Parent:** [Monodromy group of a covering](monodromy-group-of-a-covering.md)

The degree-four [rational map](rational-map-complex-analysis.md) $F(z)=z^2+z^{-2}$ has branch values $-2,2,\infty$. Away from their inverse images, the transformations

$$
z\mapsto-z,
\qquad z\mapsto z^{-1}
$$

are commuting deck involutions. They generate a [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md) acting transitively on each fibre

$$
\{z,-z,z^{-1},-z^{-1}\}.
$$

Consequently the monodromy group is $V_4$, acting by the identity and the three double transpositions.
