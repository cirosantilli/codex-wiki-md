# Routhian mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Routhian_mechanics)

A reduction of [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md) that fixes conserved momenta of [cyclic coordinates](cyclic-coordinate.md) while keeping an Euler-Lagrange description for the remaining coordinates. It is named for [Edward Routh](edward-routh.md). The [Routhian](routhian.md) applies a partial [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md) to the eliminated velocities.
