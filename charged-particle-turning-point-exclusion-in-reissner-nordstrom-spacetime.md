# Charged-particle turning-point exclusion in Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md)

For $0<|Q|<M$, the [charged-particle radial equation in Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](charged-particle-radial-equation-in-reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md) implies that any radial [turning point](turning-point.md) obeys

$$
F\left(1+\frac{l^2}{r^2}\right)=\frac{(E-eQ/r)^2}{m^2}\ge0.
$$

Since $1+l^2/r^2>0$, it follows that $F\ge0$. Thus no massive [test particle](test-particle.md), regardless of its [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), can have a radial [turning point](turning-point.md) in $r_-<r<r_+$, where $F<0$. At a regular horizon crossing, a [timelike vector](timelike-vector.md) cannot lie in the tangent space of a [null hypersurface](null-hypersurface.md), so a massive particle has $\dot r\ne0$ there as well. The algebraic inequalities $r\ge r_+$ or $r\le r_-$ are necessary conditions, not an existence theorem for radial [turning points](turning-point.md).
