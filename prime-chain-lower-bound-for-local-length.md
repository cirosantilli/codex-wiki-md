# Prime-chain lower bound for local length

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert–Samuel growth dimension](hilbert-samuel-growth-dimension.md)

A [prime chain](prime-chain.md) of length $s$ in a [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md) forces its maximal-[ideal](ideal.md) length function to be at least $ct^s$ for large $t$. Quotient by the initial prime to reduce to a domain, choose a nonzero $a$ in the next prime, and use the [Artin-Rees lemma](artin-rees-lemma.md) to obtain $H_R(t)\geq H_R(t-c_0)+H_{R/(a)}(t)$. Induction on chain length and summing about $t/(2c_0)$ terms prove the bound. This yields $\dim R\leq d(R)$ without assuming the [Krull height theorem](krull-height-theorem.md).
