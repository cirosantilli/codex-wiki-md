# Serre duality for compact complex manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Serre duality](serre-duality.md)

For a compact complex $n$-manifold and a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) $E$, analytic [Serre duality](serre-duality.md) gives

$$
H^q(M,E)^*\cong H^{n-q}(M,K_M\otimes E^*).
$$

The pairing integrates the wedge product of bundle-valued [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md) representatives, contracting $E$ with its dual.
