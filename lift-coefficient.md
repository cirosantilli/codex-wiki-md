# Lift coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Lift (force)](lift-force-split.md)

The signed [lift](lift-force-split.md) divided by [dynamic pressure](dynamic-pressure.md) times a stated reference area. For a two-dimensional [airfoil](airfoil.md), use $c_l=L'/(q_\infty c)$ instead. Reference area and experimental flow conditions are part of the definition of a reported coefficient; see [lift](lift-force-split.md) for a worked scale and a pressure/balance experiment.
