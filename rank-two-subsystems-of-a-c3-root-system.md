# Rank-two subsystems of a C3 root system

↑ **Parent:** [C3 root system](c3-root-system.md)

In the realization $\Phi(C_3)=\{\pm2e_i,\ \pm e_i\pm e_j:i<j\}$, each coordinate two-plane cuts out a [C2 root system](c2-root-system.md). Each plane $\pm x_1\pm x_2\pm x_3=0$ cuts out an [A2 root system](a2-root-system.md); there are four such planes after identifying opposite normals. Thus the irreducible full rank-two [root subsystems](root-subsystem.md) comprise three of type $C_2$ and four of type $A_2$. For an $A_2$ simple pair $p,q$, equal lengths and angle $120$ degrees give roots $\pm p,\pm q,\pm(p+q)$. For a mixed-length simple pair $s,l$ with $\|l\|^2=2\|s\|^2$ and $s\cdot l=-\|s\|^2$, the [B2 root system](b2-root-system.md) has roots $\pm s,\pm l,\pm(s+l),\pm(2s+l)$. These lists also verify that the selected pairs are simple, rather than simply independent.
