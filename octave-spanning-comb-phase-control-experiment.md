# Octave-spanning comb phase-control experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Comb self-referencing](comb-self-referencing.md)

The 2000 experiment prepared [optical pulses](optical-pulse.md) with a [mode-locked laser](mode-locked-laser.md) and broadened them in an air-silica microstructure [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md). A wavelength-selective [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md) separated red and blue light. A nonlinear crystal doubled the red branch; the blue branch received a known acousto-optic shift. A filter selected recombined light for an avalanche [photodiode](photodiode.md), supplying an electronic [beat](beat.md). Mirror translation controlled repetition [frequency](frequency.md); mirror tilt controlled offset with coupled effects. Separate delayed-pulse [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) tested programmed [phase](phase-waves.md) changes. Alternative envelope fits and unmatched optical reflections were examined as checks and limitations.[Science 288, pp.636–638, Figs.2,4–5: apparatus and temporal check; Tokushima mirror](carrier-envelope-phase-control-of-femtosecond-mode-locked-lasers-and-direct-optical-frequency-synthesis-by-david-j-jones-and-others.md)

These readouts answer different questions. The electronic [beat](beat.md) closes a feedback loop; changing its setpoint and checking the delayed-pulse [phase](phase-waves.md) tests whether that control reaches the optical waveform. Neither readout alone establishes the accuracy of an unknown optical [frequency](frequency.md). That additionally requires a calibrated time reference, the correct tooth number and [beat](beat.md) sign, and checks for missed cycles and drift. The ideal unshifted diagram above omits the experiment's known acousto-optic shift; its actual raw detector [frequency](frequency.md) must be corrected for that shift before identifying $f_0$.
