# Areal velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Areal_velocity)

Areal velocity measures how fast a [position](position.md) [vector](vector.md) from a chosen origin sweeps [signed swept area](signed-swept-area.md). It connects a geometric picture of motion to [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md): a long [position](position.md) [vector](vector.md) with slow transverse motion can sweep the same [area](surface-area.md) per unit [time](time-in-physics.md) as a short one with fast transverse motion. Constancy requires a dynamical condition, given by the [torque criterion for constant areal velocity](torque-criterion-for-constant-areal-velocity.md).

Choose a fixed origin $O$ in an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) and a fixed [plane](plane.md) through $O$, with constant [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf n$ normal to it. For a differentiable trajectory in that [plane](plane.md), let $\mathbf r(t)$ be [position](position.md) relative to $O$ and $\mathbf v=\dot{\mathbf r}$ its [velocity](velocity.md). Positive swept [area](surface-area.md) follows the [right-hand rule](right-hand-rule.md) about $\mathbf n$. In positively oriented Cartesian coordinates in the [plane](plane.md), and in [plane polar coordinates](plane-polar-coordinates.md) with [angle](angle.md) $\theta$ in [radians](radian.md),

$$
\dot A=\frac12\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf r\times\mathbf v)
=\frac12(x\dot y-y\dot x)
=\frac12r^2\dot\theta.
$$

Here the [dot product](dot-product.md) selects the normal component of the [cross product](cross-product.md). Motion away from or towards $O$ alone contributes no [area](surface-area.md). The signed [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) component $h_n=\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf r\times\mathbf v)$ obeys

$$
\dot A=\frac{h_n}{2}=\frac{L_n}{2m}
$$

for a constant [mass](mass.md) $m$. If $\mathbf n$ is chosen along a nonzero conserved [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) [vector](vector.md), then $h_n=h=|\mathbf r\times\mathbf v|$ and the familiar positive formula is $\dot A=h/2$. Reversing the orientation changes the sign; the instantaneous unsigned swept-[area](surface-area.md) rate is $|\dot A|$.
