# Effusive molecular energy flux

↑ **Parent:** [Effusion](effusion.md)

An equilibrium Maxwell-Boltzmann gas has number flux $n\overline v/4$ onto a plane. Molecules crossing the plane are weighted by their positive normal [velocity](velocity.md), so their mean translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $2k_BT$, rather than the bulk value $3k_BT/2$. The translational [energy](energy-split.md) flux is therefore $F_E=n\overline v k_BT/2$. Internal degrees of freedom contribute their mean [energy](energy-split.md) per crossing molecule when independent of translational [velocity](velocity.md).
