# Noether current for a constant shift with second derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Noether current](noether-current.md)

If a [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) depending on first and second derivatives has no undifferentiated field, the [scalar-field shift symmetry](scalar-field-shift-symmetry.md) $\delta\phi=\epsilon$ gives the displayed [Noether current](noether-current.md). Its [divergence](divergence.md) is minus the [Euler-Lagrange field equation with second derivatives](euler-lagrange-field-equation-with-second-derivatives.md), so it is conserved [on shell](on-shell.md). The spatial integral of $j^0$ is constant only if the boundary current vanishes. For $\mathcal L=\rho\phi_t^2/2+\alpha\phi_x^2/2-\beta\phi_{xx}^2/2$, it gives $j^0=\rho\phi_t$, $j^x=\alpha\phi_x+\beta\phi_{xxx}$. Fixed transverse endpoint positions break the global shift symmetry of the boundary problem; support forces can then change the total transverse momentum even though the bulk continuity equation still holds.
