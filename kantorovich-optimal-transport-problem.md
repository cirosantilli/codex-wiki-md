# Kantorovich optimal transport problem

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal transport](optimal-transport.md)

The Kantorovich problem minimizes $\int c(x,y)\,d\pi(x,y)$ over [transport plans](transport-plan.md) $\pi$ with [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md) $\mu,\nu$. It relaxes the [Monge optimal transport problem](monge-optimal-transport-problem.md) by allowing source mass to split among destinations.
