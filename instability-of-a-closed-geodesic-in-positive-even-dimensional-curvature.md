# Instability of a closed geodesic in positive even-dimensional curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Second variation of geodesic energy](second-variation-of-geodesic-energy.md)

On an oriented even-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) of strictly positive [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md), a nonconstant closed geodesic has a length-decreasing smooth variation. Its [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) fixes the tangent and acts on the odd-dimensional normal space by a [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md) element. An [odd-dimensional special orthogonal transformation has a fixed vector](odd-dimensional-special-orthogonal-transformation-has-a-fixed-vector.md), so there is a nonzero periodic parallel normal field $V$. The [second variation of geodesic energy](second-variation-of-geodesic-energy.md) is then strictly negative. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) converts lower energy into strictly lower length because the original geodesic has constant speed. For an embedded geodesic the small variation is a [smooth isotopy](smooth-isotopy.md); otherwise it is a deformation through immersed loops.
