# Moore space (algebraic topology)

↑ **Parent:** [CW complex](cw-complex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moore_space_(algebraic_topology))

For an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $G$ and an integer $m\geq1$, a Moore space $M(G,m)$ is a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) whose reduced [homology](homology-split.md) is $G$ in degree $m$ and zero in every other degree.
