<h1 id="wright-fisher-binomial-sampling-chain">Wright–Fisher binomial sampling chain</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Wright–Fisher diffusion](wright-fisher-diffusion.md)

An urn with $j$ red balls among $n$ is sampled independently $n$ times with replacement, and the next generation's red count is the number of red draws. Its proportion is a bounded discrete-time [martingale](martingale-split.md). Accelerating generation time by $n$ gives a [Wright–Fisher diffusion](wright-fisher-diffusion.md) limit: the one-step variance of the proportion is $x(1-x)/n$, and the higher moments make Taylor remainders negligible.
