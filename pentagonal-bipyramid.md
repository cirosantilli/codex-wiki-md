# Pentagonal bipyramid

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)

A seven-vertex [polyhedron](polyhedron.md) consisting of a five-membered equatorial ring and two axial vertices, each joined to every ring vertex. Removing an equatorial vertex leaves the six-vertex pentagonal-pyramidal skeleton used for some [nido clusters](nido-cluster.md).
