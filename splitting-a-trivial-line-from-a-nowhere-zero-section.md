# Splitting a trivial line from a nowhere-zero section

↑ **Parent:** [Nowhere-zero section](nowhere-zero-section.md)

A unit [nowhere-zero section](nowhere-zero-section.md) $\sigma$ of a real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) with a [fiber metric](fiber-metric.md) defines a rank-one [trivial vector bundle](trivial-vector-bundle.md). Its fiberwise [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) is a subbundle: locally complete $\sigma$ to an orthonormal frame. The [vector bundle isomorphism](vector-bundle-isomorphism.md) $(v,t)\mapsto v+t\sigma$ has inverse $w\mapsto(w-\langle w,\sigma\rangle\sigma,\langle w,\sigma\rangle)$. For an oriented bundle, orient the complement so that its positive frame followed by $\sigma$ is positive.
