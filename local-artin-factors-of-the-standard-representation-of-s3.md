# Local Artin factors of the standard representation of S3

↑ **Parent:** [Local factor of an Artin L-function](local-factor-of-an-artin-l-function.md)

For the [standard representation of the symmetric group](standard-representation-of-the-symmetric-group.md) $S_3$, the unramified [local factor of an Artin L-function](local-factor-of-an-artin-l-function.md), with $t=p^{-s}$, is respectively the displayed factor for identity, transposition, and three-cycle [Frobenius cycle types](frobenius-cycle-type.md). These follow by removing the trivial summand from the three-point [permutation representation](permutation-representation.md). If the [inertia group](inertia-group.md) contains a three-cycle, its invariant subspace in the standard representation is zero, so the ramified local factor is one.
