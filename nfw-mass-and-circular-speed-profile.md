# NFW mass and circular-speed profile

↑ **Parent:** [Navarro--Frenk--White profile](navarro-frenk-white-profile.md)

For an [NFW profile](navarro-frenk-white-profile.md), $x=r/r_s$ and $v_c^2=GM(r)/r$. The [circular speed](circular-speed.md) rises as $r^{1/2}$ at small radius and eventually decreases as $[\log(r/r_s)/r]^{1/2}$. Its maximum occurs at $x\simeq2.16258$, with $v_{c,\max}^2\simeq2.71706G\rho_s r_s^2$.
