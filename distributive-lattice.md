# Distributive lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice](lattice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributive_lattice)

A distributive lattice is a [lattice](lattice.md) in which meet distributes over join and, equivalently, join distributes over meet.
