# Boundary-decaying Green kernel for an inverse-square differential operator

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function.md)

For $L=d^2/dx^2-2/x^2$ on $x>0$, the homogeneous solutions $x^2,x^{-1}$ satisfy respectively the zero and infinity [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). Their [Wronskian](wronskian.md) is $-3$. The displayed [Green's function](green-s-function.md) is continuous at $x=\xi$ and its first [derivative](derivative.md) has jump one, so $L_xG=\delta(x-\xi)$ in the [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) sense. Integration against a compactly supported forcing gives the unique solution decaying at both boundaries.
