# First-order Fermi acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi acceleration](fermi-acceleration.md)

In [first-order Fermi acceleration](first-order-fermi-acceleration.md), converging scattering media produce a mean fractional [energy](energy-split.md) gain linear in their relative [speed](speed.md) divided by the particle [speed](speed.md). At a nonrelativistic [shock wave](shock-wave.md), an almost [isotropic](isotropy.md) ultrarelativistic population gains $2(u_1-u_2)/(3c)$ per crossing and $4(u_1-u_2)/(3c)$ per complete return cycle. The distinction between a crossing and a cycle must also be respected when assigning an escape probability.
