# Constructive theory

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of physics](philosophy-of-physics.md)

An account that builds macroscopic processes from specified constituent systems and their interactions. [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md) explaining thermal behavior through molecular motion is a standard example. A [constructive theory](constructive-theory.md) and a [principle theory](principle-theory.md) can address the same phenomena at different explanatory levels.[1919 contrast between molecular kinetic theory and thermodynamic constraints](what-is-the-theory-of-relativity-by-albert-einstein.md)
