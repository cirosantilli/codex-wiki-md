# Methods of detecting exoplanets

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet](exoplanet-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methods_of_detecting_exoplanets)

Methods of detecting exoplanets infer or resolve a planet through its effects on stellar light or its own emission. They include [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md), transit photometry, gravitational microlensing and direct imaging. An [exoplanet secondary eclipse](exoplanet-secondary-eclipse.md) measures the loss of planetary light when the planet passes behind its star.
