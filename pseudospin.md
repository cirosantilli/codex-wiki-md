# Pseudospin

↑ **Parent:** [Tight binding](tight-binding.md)

A two-state degree of freedom represented mathematically like [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md), without necessarily being physical [spin](spin.md). The [sublattice pseudospin in graphene](sublattice-pseudospin-in-graphene.md) labels amplitudes on two sites per primitive cell.
