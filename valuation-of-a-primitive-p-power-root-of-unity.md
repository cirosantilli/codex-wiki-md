# Valuation of a primitive p-power root of unity

↑ **Parent:** [Roots of unity in a p-adic field](roots-of-unity-in-a-p-adic-field.md)

In a mixed-characteristic [discretely valued field](discretely-valued-field.md), a primitive $p^a$th [root of unity](root-of-unity.md) reduces to one. For every $j$ prime to $p$, the ratio $(\zeta^j-1)/(\zeta-1)$ is a unit, so all factors of $\Phi_{p^a}(1)=p$ have the same valuation. Taking valuations gives the formula. Because the left side is a positive integer for a normalized valuation, the existence of such a root forces $p^{a-1}(p-1)\mid v(p)$, bounding the possible $p$-power torsion.
