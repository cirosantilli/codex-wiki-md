# Radiation-era density modes without a pressure-gradient term

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological density perturbation](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md)

For $a\propto t^{1/2}$ and $\rho=3/(32\pi Gt^2)$, the specified equation $\ddot\Delta+2H\dot\Delta=(32\pi G\rho/3)\Delta$ reduces to $\ddot\Delta+t^{-1}\dot\Delta=t^{-2}\Delta$. Its two modes are $t$ and $t^{-1}$. This is a property of that equation; pressure-gradient and gauge choices must be specified before extending it to general radiation perturbations.
