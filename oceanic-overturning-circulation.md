# Oceanic overturning circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean circulation](ocean-circulation.md)

A large-scale circulation with upper and deep return branches and exchanges between them. Different branch [temperatures](temperature-split.md) give [meridional heat transport](meridional-heat-transport.md) even if their net volume transport cancels. The [Atlantic meridional overturning circulation](atlantic-meridional-overturning-circulation.md) is an important regional component. Wind forcing, surface [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) exchange and interior mixing all matter, so a purely thermally driven picture is incomplete.
