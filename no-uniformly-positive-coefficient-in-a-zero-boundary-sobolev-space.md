# No uniformly positive coefficient in a zero-boundary Sobolev space

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-boundary Sobolev space](zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md)

On a nonempty bounded domain where the [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) holds, no $a\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ can satisfy $a\geq a_->0$ almost everywhere. For $g(s)=\min(\max(s,0),a_-)$, [Lipschitz truncation preserves zero-boundary Sobolev spaces](lipschitz-truncation-preserves-zero-boundary-sobolev-spaces.md), so $g(a)\in H_0^1(\Omega)$. But $g(a)$ would be the nonzero constant $a_-$, whose zero gradient contradicts the [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md). In a uniformly elliptic [divergence-form elliptic operator](divergence-form-elliptic-operator.md), the homogeneous boundary condition belongs to the unknown and test functions, not to a coefficient bounded below by a positive constant.
