# Complex logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_logarithm)

For $z=re^{i\theta}\ne0$, the complex logarithm has the values $\log r+i(\theta+2\pi k)$ for $k\in\mathbb Z$.
