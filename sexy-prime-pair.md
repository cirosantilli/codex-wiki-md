# Sexy prime pair

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number-split.md)

Two [primes](prime-number-split.md) whose difference is $6$, for example $5$ and $11$. The name refers to the Latin word for six. The [primes](prime-number-split.md) need not be consecutive.
