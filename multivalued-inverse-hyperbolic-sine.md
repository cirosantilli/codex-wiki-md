# Multivalued inverse hyperbolic sine

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md)

The solutions of $\sinh w=z$ are

$$
w=\operatorname{Arcsinh}z+2\pi ik
\quad\hbox{or}\quad
w=-\operatorname{Arcsinh}z+(2k+1)\pi i,
\qquad k\in\mathbb Z,
$$

for any chosen local branch $\operatorname{Arcsinh}$.

It is the complex multivalued sine member of the [inverse hyperbolic functions](inverse-hyperbolic-functions.md).
