# Full-film eccentric journal-bearing rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Journal bearing](journal-bearing.md)

For inner radius $a$, mean gap $\Delta\ll a$, fixed eccentricity parameter $|\alpha|<1$, gap $h=\Delta(1+\alpha\sin\theta)$, and inner [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\Omega$, the constant circumferential flux is

$$
q=\frac{\Omega a\Delta}{2}\frac{1-\alpha^2}{1+\alpha^2/2}.
$$

It follows by integrating the [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) relation $p_s=6\mu\Omega a/h^2-12\mu q/h^3$ over a full period. The leading holding [force](force.md) is horizontal:

$$
F_x=-\frac{6\pi\mu\Omega a^3}{\Delta^2}\frac{\alpha}{\sqrt{1-\alpha^2}(1+\alpha^2/2)},\qquad F_z=0.
$$

[Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) computes this [force](force.md) without a closed-form [pressure](pressure.md). The fluid couples about the separate cylinder axes obey $C_i+C_o=\alpha\Delta F_x$; moments about a common axis balance exactly at this order.
