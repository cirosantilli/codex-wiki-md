# Diagonal boundedness of operator families over real and complex Hilbert spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md)

For operators on a complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), [pointwise](pointwise-convergence.md) boundedness of $\langle Tx,x\rangle$ over a family implies uniform boundedness of their [operator norms](operator-norm.md). The complex [polarization identity](polarization-identity.md) recovers each $\langle Tx,y\rangle$ from four diagonal values. Apply the [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md) first to the functionals in $y$ for fixed $x$, then to the operators in $x$. On a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) the claim fails: multiples of a nonzero skew-symmetric operator have zero diagonal forms but unbounded norms. For example, rotations through a quarter turn on $\mathbb R^2$ multiplied by integers suffice.
