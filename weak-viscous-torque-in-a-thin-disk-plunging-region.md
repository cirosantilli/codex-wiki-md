# Weak viscous torque in a thin-disk plunging region

↑ **Parent:** [Plunging region of a black-hole accretion disk](plunging-region-of-a-black-hole-accretion-disk.md)

A [thin disk](thin-disk.md) with [alpha disk](alpha-disk.md) viscosity $\nu\sim\alpha H^2\Omega$ has a [viscous timescale](viscous-timescale.md) much longer than its dynamical infall time in the [plunging region of a black-hole accretion disk](plunging-region-of-a-black-hole-accretion-disk.md). At fixed nonzero [accretion rate](accretion-rate.md), rapid inflow reduces its [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md). The resulting [viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) is smaller by the displayed factor than in the slowly drifting circular disk. This estimate concerns radii outside the narrow matching layers.
