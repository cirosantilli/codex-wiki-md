# Two-dimensional Bessel transition law

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel process](bessel-process.md)

A dimension-two [Bessel process](bessel-process.md) starting at $r>0$ has the displayed marginal law, where $G_1,G_2$ are independent standard [normal random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md). Take the norm of a [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) starting at $(r,0)$. [Planar Brownian motion avoids a fixed point](planar-brownian-motion-avoids-a-fixed-point.md), so the norm stays positive; [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives drift $1/(2R)$ and a martingale term of bracket $t$. The [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md) identifies that term as its driving Brownian motion. The squared radius divided by $t$ has a [noncentral chi-squared distribution](noncentral-chi-squared-distribution.md) with two degrees of freedom and noncentrality $r^2/t$.
