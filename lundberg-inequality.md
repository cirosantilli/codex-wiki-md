# Lundberg inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md)

For a [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md) with [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) $R>0$, the ultimate ruin probability from capital $u\geq0$ satisfies $\psi(u)\leq e^{-Ru}$. Stop the exponential [continuous-time martingale](continuous-time-martingale.md) at ruin or a finite horizon, bound its value on the ruin event, and then increase the horizon.
