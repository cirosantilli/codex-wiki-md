# Lyapunov moment inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Probability inequality](probability-inequality-split.md)

For a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $Z$ and exponents $0<r<s$, the absolute [moment](moment.md) norms satisfy $(\mathbb E|Z|^r)^{1/r}\leq(\mathbb E|Z|^s)^{1/s}$, allowing infinity. For a finite $s$th [moment](moment.md), apply the [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) to the [concave function](concave-function.md) $t^{r/s}$ and $|Z|^s$. The formula is for positive exponents; $r=0$ requires a separately defined limiting quantity.
