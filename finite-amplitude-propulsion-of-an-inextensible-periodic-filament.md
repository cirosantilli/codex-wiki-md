# Finite-amplitude propulsion of an inextensible periodic filament

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive-force theory](resistive-force-theory.md)

With drag ratio $\rho=c_\parallel/c_\perp$, squared-tangent average $\beta=\Lambda^{-1}\int x_s^2ds$, and axial wave speed $V$, [resistive-force theory](resistive-force-theory.md) gives the displayed axial swimming ratio. Pure axial free swimming additionally requires vanishing cross-tangent average, as in reflection-symmetric waveforms; otherwise a transverse [force](force.md) or translation is present.
