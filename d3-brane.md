# D3-brane

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

A [D3-brane](d3-brane.md) has three spatial dimensions. The low-energy fields of an isolated flat [D3-brane](d3-brane.md) form an abelian theory with sixteen linearly realized [supersymmetry generators](supersymmetry-generator.md). Its transverse scalar fields describe its geometric position, and its gauge field can carry the charges of strings ending on it.
