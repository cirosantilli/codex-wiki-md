# Nash bargaining solution

↑ **Parent:** [Nash bargaining problem](nash-bargaining-problem.md)

For an essential [Nash bargaining problem](nash-bargaining-problem.md), maximizing the [Nash product](nash-product.md) gives a unique vector: on positive gains its logarithm is a [strictly concave function](strictly-concave-function.md). The rule satisfies the four bargaining axioms, and the [supporting triangle for Nash bargaining](supporting-triangle-for-nash-bargaining.md) proves their converse characterization. The nonessential case requires additional conventions, since a zero product may have many maximizers.
