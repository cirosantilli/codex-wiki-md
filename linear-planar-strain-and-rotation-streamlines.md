# Linear planar strain and rotation streamlines

↑ **Parent:** [Stream function](stream-function.md)

The incompressible planar field $(\alpha x-\beta y,\beta x-\alpha y)$ has the displayed [stream function](stream-function.md) under $(u,v)=(\psi_y,-\psi_x)$ and vorticity $2\beta$. In rotated coordinates $s=(x+y)/\sqrt2$, $t=(x-y)/\sqrt2$, $\psi=((\alpha-\beta)s^2-(\alpha+\beta)t^2)/2$. For positive parameters its nonstationary [streamlines](streamline.md) are closed ellipses if $\alpha<\beta$, hyperbolas and straight separatrices if $\alpha>\beta$, and parallel straight lines with a stationary diagonal if $\alpha=\beta$.
