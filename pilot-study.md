# Pilot study

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments-split.md)

A preliminary investigation used to examine feasibility, procedures or measurement behavior before a larger study. A pilot can expose ambiguous [questionnaire](questionnaire.md) items without providing a stable final estimate of the underlying measurement structure.
