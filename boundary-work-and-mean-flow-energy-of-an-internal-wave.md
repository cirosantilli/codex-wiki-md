# Boundary work and mean-flow energy of an internal wave

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave momentum deposition](internal-wave-momentum-deposition.md)

For a traveling sinusoidal boundary forcing with horizontal phase speed $c_p$, the [pressure](pressure.md) and horizontal [velocity](velocity.md) polarization gives the displayed relation between upward [energy](energy.md) input and vertical [wave momentum flux](wave-momentum-flux.md). A slowly established outgoing wave envelope deposits horizontal mean [momentum](momentum.md) as its front advances. In the frame moving at $c_p$, the order-amplitude-squared change of mean [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $-\rho_0c_p\int\overline u\,dz$. Its loss rate equals the boundary work in the laboratory frame. The comparison uses a finite perturbation of the infinite uniform background [energy](energy.md).
