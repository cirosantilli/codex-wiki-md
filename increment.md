# Increment

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md)

An increment of a real- or [vector](vector.md)-valued [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) is the difference $X_t-X_s$ between its values at two times $s<t$. A [random walk](random-walk-split.md) accumulates its one-step increments; a [Poisson process](poisson-process-split.md) has nonnegative integer increments counting arrivals in $(s,t]$. A process can have [independent increments](independent-increments.md) even though its values at different times are dependent.
