# Degree by integration of a pullback volume form

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md)

For a smooth map $f:M\to N$ between oriented connected [closed manifolds](closed-manifold.md) of the same dimension, a normalized [volume form](volume-form.md) on the target gives $\deg f=\int_Mf^*\omega$. The formula agrees with the [degree as a sum of local degrees](degree-as-a-sum-of-local-degrees.md). A change of normalized top-form adds an exact form by top-dimensional [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md), whose pullback has zero integral by [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md).
