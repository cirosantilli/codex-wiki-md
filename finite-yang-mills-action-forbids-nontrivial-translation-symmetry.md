# Finite Yang-Mills action forbids nontrivial translation symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills instanton](yang-mills-instanton.md)

A smooth [gauge field](gauge-field.md) of finite positive Euclidean [Yang-Mills action](yang-mills-action.md) cannot be invariant under a nonzero translation, even up to [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md). The positive gauge-invariant action density would be periodic along that translation. A ball on which the density is bounded below by a positive constant has infinitely many disjoint translated copies, making the action infinite. Continuous translation invariance is a special case. Consequently a nonflat translation-invariant field satisfying the [Anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations](anti-self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md) on $\mathbb R^3\times\mathbb R$ is not a finite-action [Yang-Mills instanton](yang-mills-instanton.md). Flat connections are the zero-action exception. Translations still act on instanton moduli, sending a localized instanton to a distinct centered configuration.
