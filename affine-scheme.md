# Affine scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of a commutative ring](spectrum-of-a-commutative-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_scheme)

An affine scheme is a [scheme](scheme.md) isomorphic to $\operatorname{Spec}A$ for some [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $A$. Homomorphisms $A\to B$ correspond contravariantly to morphisms $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$.
