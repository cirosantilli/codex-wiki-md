<h1 id="lusternik-schnirelmann-category">Lusternik–Schnirelmann category</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusternik–Schnirelmann_category)

Use the unnormalized convention: the least number of open subsets covering $X$ whose inclusions into $X$ are [null-homotopic](null-homotopic-map.md). The subsets need not themselves be contractible. This number is one for a nonempty contractible space. If $r$ positive-degree classes have nonzero product, a cover by $r$ categorical sets would make that product zero by relative [cup products](cup-product.md); hence $\operatorname{cat}(X)\geq r+1$.
