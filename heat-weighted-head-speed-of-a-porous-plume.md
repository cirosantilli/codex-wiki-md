# Heat-weighted head speed of a porous plume

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic-secant porous plume profile](hyperbolic-secant-porous-plume-profile.md)

Truncating the steady [porous thermal plume](porous-thermal-plume.md) profile below a head height and conserving supplied heat gives $\dot Z_h\simeq\int w^2dx/\int wdx=(2/3)W(Z_h)$. Thus $Z_h\propto t^{3/4}$. This is an integral bulk-head estimate, while fastest centreline parcels have characteristic speed $W$; the approximation does not resolve the transient nose.
