# Coleman trace operator

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius substitution on cyclotomic power series](frobenius-substitution-on-cyclotomic-power-series.md)

In the unique decomposition $f=\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}(1+T)^i\varphi(f_i)$, define $\psi(f)=f_0$. Averaging translates over the p-th [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) annihilates every nonzero basis index and retains the zeroth, proving the displayed identity and its integrality. Thus $\psi\varphi=1$ and $\psi(\varphi(g)f)=g\psi(f)$. Under the [Amice transform](amice-transform.md), $\psi=0$ characterizes measures supported on $\mathbb Z_p^\times$.
