# Symmetrized exponential-splitting defect identity

↑ **Parent:** [Lie product formula](lie-product-formula.md)

For bounded [matrices](matrix.md), differentiating the symmetrized product gives $F'-(A+B)F=\tfrac12([e^{tB},A]e^{tA}+[e^{tA},B]e^{tB})$. The [variation-of-constants formula](variation-of-constants-formula.md) turns this into an integral expression for $F-e^{t(A+B)}$. This retains the noncommutativity of the factors explicitly; cancellation of their leading opposite [commutators](commutator.md) explains why the averaged product is more accurate than either ordering alone.
