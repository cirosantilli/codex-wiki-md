# Average-linkage clustering

↑ **Parent:** [Agglomerative hierarchical clustering](agglomerative-hierarchical-clustering.md)

Average linkage merges the pair with the smallest mean cross-pair dissimilarity. This differs from the minimum used by [single-linkage clustering](single-linkage-clustering.md) and the maximum used by [complete-linkage clustering](complete-linkage-clustering.md). The convention here weights individual cross-pairs equally; weighting already formed clusters equally is a different algorithm.
