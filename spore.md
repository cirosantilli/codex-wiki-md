# Spore

↑ **Parent:** [Sexual reproduction](sexual-reproduction.md)

A reproductive or dispersal cell capable of initiating growth without fusion with another cell. In the ordinary land-plant sexual cycle, [haploid](haploid.md) [spores](spore.md) formed by [meiosis](meiosis.md) initiate [gametophytes](gametophyte.md); they are distinct from fusing [gametes](gamete.md).
