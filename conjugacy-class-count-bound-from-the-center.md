# Conjugacy class count bound from the center

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md)

For a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$, write $k(G)$ for its number of [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md). Exactly the elements of the [center of a group](center-of-a-group.md) $Z(G)$ have [singleton](singleton-mathematics.md) [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md); every other [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) has at least two elements. Since the [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) form a [partition of a set](partition-of-a-set.md) $G$, $k(G)-|Z(G)|\leq(|G|-|Z(G)|)/2$. If $G$ is a [nonabelian group](non-abelian-group.md), the [cyclic quotient by the center](cyclic-quotient-by-the-center.md) criterion and the fact that every [group](group-split.md) of [group order](order-of-a-finite-group.md) at most three is a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) give $[G:Z(G)]\geq4$, hence $k(G)\leq5|G|/8$. The [quaternion group](quaternion-group.md) attains this bound, with five [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) among eight elements.
