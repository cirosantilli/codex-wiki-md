# Casimir eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Casimir element](casimir-element.md)

The [Casimir operator](casimir-element.md) formed from the [Killing form](killing-form.md) acts on an [Irreducible Lie algebra representation](irreducible-lie-algebra-representation.md) with [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\lambda$ by the scalar $(\lambda,\lambda+2\rho)$, where $\rho$ is the [half-sum of positive roots](half-sum-of-positive-roots.md). It is zero on the [trivial Lie algebra representation](trivial-lie-algebra-representation.md) and nonzero on every nontrivial finite-dimensional irreducible complex representation of a [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md).
