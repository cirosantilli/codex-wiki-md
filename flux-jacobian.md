# Flux Jacobian

↑ **Parent:** [System of conservation laws](system-of-conservation-laws.md)

The flux Jacobian is the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of the flux in a [system of conservation laws](system-of-conservation-laws.md). Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are the local [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md), and its left and right [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) select the corresponding wave variables and directions in state space.
