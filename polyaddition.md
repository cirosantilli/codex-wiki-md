# Polyaddition

↑ **Parent:** [Step-growth polymerization](step-growth-polymerization.md)

[Polyaddition](polyaddition.md) is [step-growth polymerization](step-growth-polymerization.md) by joining reactions without elimination of small [molecules](molecule.md). Compatible [functional groups](functional-group.md) on different chain lengths remain eligible to join; the word addition alone does not imply [chain-growth polymerization](chain-growth-polymerization.md).[polyaddition, P04720, definition and notes 1–2](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry.md)
