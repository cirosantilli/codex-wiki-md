# Closure of separable algebraic elements under addition

↑ **Parent:** [Separable algebraic element](separable-algebraic-element.md)

If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are [separable algebraic elements](separable-algebraic-element.md), put $K=F(\alpha)$ and $L=K(\beta)$. The [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) of $\beta$ over $K$ divides its [separable polynomial](separable-polynomial.md) over $F$, and is therefore separable. The [extension count for field embeddings](extension-count-for-field-embeddings.md) gives $[K:F]$ embeddings of $K$ into an [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md), each with $[L:K]$ extensions to $L$. Hence $L$ has $[L:F]$ embeddings. For $E=F(\alpha+\beta)$, each embedding of $E$ has at most $[L:E]$ extensions, so there are at least $[E:F]$ embeddings of $E$ and hence exactly that number. Since $E$ is a simple [field extension](field-extension.md), its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) has $[E:F]$ distinct roots. Thus $\alpha+\beta$ is a [separable algebraic element](separable-algebraic-element.md).
