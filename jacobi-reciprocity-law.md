# Jacobi reciprocity law

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi symbol](jacobi-symbol.md)

For coprime positive odd integers $a,b$, the [Jacobi symbol](jacobi-symbol.md) satisfies $(a/b)(b/a)=(-1)^{(a-1)(b-1)/4}$. It follows by factoring $a$ and $b$ into primes and multiplying the [quadratic reciprocity](quadratic-reciprocity.md) for each prime pair; the parity of the sums of prime half-differences agrees with $(a-1)/2$ and $(b-1)/2$. A [Jacobi symbol](jacobi-symbol.md) equal to one does not imply that its numerator is a [quadratic residue](quadratic-residue.md) for a composite denominator.
