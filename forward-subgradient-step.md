# Forward subgradient step

↑ **Parent:** [Subdifferential](subdifferential.md)

For a [convex function](convex-function.md) and $\tau>0$, the forward step is the set-valued explicit update $F_{\tau f}(x)=\{x-\tau p:p\in\partial f(x)\}$. It reduces to the usual gradient step when differentiable. In contrast, a backward step uses a [subgradient](subgradient.md) at the new point and is the [proximal operator](proximal-operator.md) for a proper closed convex function.
