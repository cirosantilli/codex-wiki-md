# Gravitational slip

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological perturbation theory](linear-cosmological-perturbation-theory-split.md)

A difference between the two scalar [metric](metric.md) potentials in a specified cosmological convention. Nonrelativistic motions probe the potential in the time-time [metric](metric.md) component, while [weak gravitational lensing](weak-gravitational-lensing.md) probes their sum. Joint tests can constrain [modified gravity](modified-gravity.md), but ordinary anisotropic stress can also generate slip and observational bias must be controlled.
