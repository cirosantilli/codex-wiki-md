# Hankel function

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel function](bessel-function-split.md)

The two Hankel functions $H_\nu^{(1)}=J_\nu+iY_\nu$ and $H_\nu^{(2)}=J_\nu-iY_\nu$ solve the [Bessel differential equation](bessel-differential-equation.md). Their large-argument forms distinguish outgoing and incoming oscillations and have sector-dependent [asymptotic expansions](asymptotic-expansion-split.md).
