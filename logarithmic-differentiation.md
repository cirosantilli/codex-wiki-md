# Logarithmic differentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)

Apply the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) to the [logarithm](logarithm.md) of a differentiable nonzero real-valued [function](function-split.md). This converts products into sums and powers into multiplicative coefficients. In particular, for positive $f=C\prod_i x_i^{a_i}$, $df/f=\sum_i a_i\,dx_i/x_i$. It is useful for [monomial uncertainty propagation](monomial-uncertainty-propagation.md) and for finding relative rates of change.
