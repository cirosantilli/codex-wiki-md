# Fox derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Fox calculus](fox-calculus.md)

The Fox derivative of a word in a [free group](free-group.md) obeys $\partial(uv)/\partial x=\partial u/\partial x+u\,\partial v/\partial x$ and $\partial x^{-1}/\partial x=-x^{-1}$.
