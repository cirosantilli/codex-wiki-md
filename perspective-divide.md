# Perspective divide

↑ **Parent:** [Perspective projection](perspective-projection.md)

Conversion of a transformed column of [homogeneous coordinates](homogeneous-coordinate.md) $(X,Y,Z,W)$ with $W\ne0$ to $(X/W,Y/W,Z/W)$. This is the nonlinear step in an otherwise matrix-based [perspective projection](perspective-projection.md) pipeline.
