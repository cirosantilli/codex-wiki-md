# Clock cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Clock signal](clock-signal.md)

One complete repetition of a periodic [clock signal](clock-signal.md), for example from one rising [clock edge](clock-edge.md) to the next. Its duration is the [clock period](clock-period.md). A [synchronous sequential circuit](synchronous-sequential-circuit.md) can update its stored state once per designated [clock edge](clock-edge.md); a one-cycle output pulse remains asserted for one such interval.
