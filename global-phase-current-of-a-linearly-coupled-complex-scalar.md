# Global phase current of a linearly coupled complex scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Linear-in-potential charged-scalar Lagrangian](linear-in-potential-charged-scalar-lagrangian.md)

For $\delta\phi=-i\epsilon\phi$, the conserved current is $J^\mu=i(\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-\phi\partial^\mu\phi^*)-2eA^\mu|\phi|^2$. The conjugate field equations imply

$$
\phi^*\Box\phi-\phi\Box\phi^*
=-2ie\,\partial_\mu(A^\mu|\phi|^2),
$$

which directly cancels the [divergence](divergence.md) of the explicit background term. Conservation requires no [Lorenz gauge condition](lorenz-gauge-condition.md); constancy of total charge additionally requires vanishing boundary flux.
