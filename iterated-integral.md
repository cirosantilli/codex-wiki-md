# Iterated integral

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple integral](multiple-integral.md)

An [iterated integral](iterated-integral.md) performs successive one-variable [integrals](integral-split.md). In the displayed expression, $y$ is held fixed during the inner [integration](integral-split.md) over $x$, and the result is then integrated over $y$. The inner bounds may depend on that fixed outer variable. By contrast, an inner bound depending on an already integrated variable does not specify such a nested operation without further interpretation. For example, the [triangle](triangle.md) $0\leq y\leq1$, $2y\leq x\leq2$ gives $\int_0^1\int_{2y}^2f(x,y)\,dx\,dy$, or, in the other order, $\int_0^2\int_0^{x/2}f(x,y)\,dy\,dx$. [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) and [Tonelli's theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) supply conditions under which these iterated expressions equal the same [multiple integral](multiple-integral.md).
