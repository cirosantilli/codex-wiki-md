# Dielectric screening

↑ **Parent:** [Relative permittivity](relative-permittivity.md)

Modification of the interaction between [electric charges](electric-charge.md) by a medium's electrical response. In the simplest [Wannier-Mott exciton](wannier-mott-exciton.md) model, the [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) is reduced by a constant [relative permittivity](relative-permittivity.md); actual screening can depend on spatial and temporal scales.
