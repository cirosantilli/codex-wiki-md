# Asymptotic scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [LSZ reduction formula](lsz-reduction-formula.md)

An [asymptotic scalar field](asymptotic-scalar-field.md) is a free field describing stable incoming or outgoing particles of an interacting [scalar field theory](scalar-field-theory-split.md). With a field that overlaps the stable one-particle state, the scattering asymptotic condition is $\phi\to\sqrt Z\phi_{\rm in}$ in the remote past and $\phi\to\sqrt Z\phi_{\rm out}$ in the remote future. These are scattering limits of suitably smeared matrix elements, not unrestricted strong pointwise limits of local fields. With covariant state normalization $\langle p'|p\rangle=(2\pi)^3 2E_p\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf p')$, the free-field vacuum-to-one-particle matrix element is one. [Translation invariance](translation-invariance.md) and the asymptotic condition then give $\langle p|\phi(0)|0\rangle=\sqrt Z$ after choosing its phase positive. The [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) residue $Z$ measures that overlap.
