# Separation axiom

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separation_axiom)

A separation axiom is a condition on a [topological space](topological-space.md) expressing how points or closed subsets can be distinguished by [open sets](open-set.md). The [Kolmogorov space](kolmogorov-space.md) condition distinguishes distinct points by some open set; the [T1 space](t1-space.md) condition makes every point closed; the stronger [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) condition gives disjoint neighbourhoods to distinct points.
