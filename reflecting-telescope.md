# Reflecting telescope

↑ **Parent:** [Optical telescope](optical-telescope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflecting_telescope)

A reflecting telescope forms an image using curved mirrors. Its primary and secondary shapes, spacing and aperture stop determine its [optical aberrations](optical-aberration.md), access to the focus, and obstruction.
