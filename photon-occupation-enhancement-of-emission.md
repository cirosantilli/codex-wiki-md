# Photon occupation enhancement of emission

↑ **Parent:** [Stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md)

The creation-operator [matrix element](matrix-element.md) $\langle n+1|a^\dagger|n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}$ makes emission into a radiation mode proportional to $n+1$. The $n$ part is [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) and the remaining part is [spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md). Absorption has the corresponding occupation factor $n$.
