# Product of linear projective subspaces in complex cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Complex cobordism](complex-cobordism.md)

The displayed product applies when $k+m\geq n$. Move one projective subspace by a unitary [isotopy](isotopy.md) into transverse position. The intersection is a linear projective subspace of dimension $k+m-n$, with normal complex bundle the sum of the restricted input [normal bundles](normal-bundle.md). If $k+m<n$, representatives can be made disjoint and the product is zero.
