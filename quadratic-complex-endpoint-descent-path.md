# Quadratic complex endpoint descent path

↑ **Parent:** [Method of steepest descent](method-of-steepest-descent.md)

For $e^{-\lambda z^2}$, the [saddle point](saddle-point.md) descent is the real axis and ascent is the imaginary axis. A descent path from a nonzero endpoint $b$ has $z(s)^2=b^2+s$, $s\geq0$, with the continuous square-root branch from $b$. It ends at positive or negative real infinity according to the sign of $\operatorname{Re}b$. The oriented outward [endpoint contribution in steepest descent](endpoint-contribution-in-steepest-descent.md) is $e^{-\lambda b^2}/(2\lambda b)$.
