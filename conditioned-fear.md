# Conditioned fear

↑ **Parent:** [Classical conditioning](classical-conditioning.md)

A defensive response learned through a relation between a cue and an aversive outcome. Laboratory cue responses and the complex [fears](fear.md) involved in a clinical [anxiety disorder](anxiety-disorder.md) require an explicit translational bridge.
