# Spherical Darcy injection

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy law](darcy-law.md)

For a sphere of radius $a$ injecting an [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) into an infinite homogeneous isotropic [porous medium](porous-medium.md), [Darcy's law](darcy-law.md) and [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) give $r^2q_r=C$. Integrating $p_r=-\mu C/(\Pi r^2)$ with $p(a)-p(\infty)=\Delta p$ gives $C=\Pi a\Delta p/\mu$. The [hydraulic resistance](hydraulic-resistance.md) is $\Delta p/Q=\mu/(4\pi\Pi a)$. A common background [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) cancels [gravity](gravity.md) if the imposed excess [pressure](pressure-split.md) is measured relative to that background.
