# Easy-plane XXZ antiferromagnetic spin-wave spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md)

For $0<\Delta<1$, choose a planar Néel reference and rotate local axes to align its longitudinal [spins](spin.md). One phase convention gives the displayed full-zone spectrum, linear near $k=0$ with slope $JS\sqrt{2(1+\Delta)}$, and gapped at $k=\pi$ by $2JS\sqrt{2(1-\Delta)}$. Shifting a sublattice phase can interchange the [momentum](momentum-split.md) locations. Folding the magnetic zone separates the gapless in-plane mode from the gapped out-of-plane polarization. An easy-axis reference in this regime is unstable and does not supply this physical expansion.
