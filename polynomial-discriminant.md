# Polynomial discriminant

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)

For a monic polynomial with roots $r_i$, its [discriminant](discriminant.md) is $\prod_{i<j}(r_i-r_j)^2$ and vanishes exactly for a repeated root.
