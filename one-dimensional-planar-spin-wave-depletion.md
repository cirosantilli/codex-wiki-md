# One-dimensional planar spin-wave depletion

↑ **Parent:** [Field-polarized planar ferromagnetic spin wave](field-polarized-planar-ferromagnetic-spin-wave.md)

The [Bogoliubov vacuum](bogoliubov-vacuum.md) has original-boson [occupation](bosonic-occupation-number.md) $v_k^2=(\alpha_k/\epsilon_k-1)/2$, reducing the ordered [spin](spin.md) by its average over [momenta](momentum-split.md). For the zero-field planar ferromagnetic chain, $v_k^2\sim1/(2\sqrt2|k|)$. Even after excluding the exact uniform [zero mode](zero-mode.md), the sum grows as $(2\pi\sqrt2)^{-1}\log L$. It invalidates a thermodynamic fixed-orientation expansion with finite dilute [depletion](one-dimensional-planar-spin-wave-depletion.md); it does not represent a negative physical [magnetization](magnetization.md).
