# Expected value premium principle

↑ **Parent:** [Actuarial statistics](actuarial-statistics-split.md)

The expected value principle prices a nonnegative insurance loss $L$ by its [expected value](expected-value.md) times one plus a positive loading. In the [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md), annual expected claim cost $\lambda\mu$ gives premium rate $(1+\theta)\lambda\mu$. Under [quota share reinsurance](quota-share-reinsurance.md), the reinsurer prices its ceded fraction $(1-\alpha)S$ using its own loading, which need not equal the direct insurer's loading.
