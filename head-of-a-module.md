# Head of a module

↑ **Parent:** [Projective cover](projective-cover.md)

The head, or top, of a finite-length module $M$ is its largest semisimple quotient. It is $M/J(M)$, where $J(M)$ is the [radical of a module](radical-of-a-module.md).
