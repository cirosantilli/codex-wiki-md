# Higgs chiral doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal supersymmetric Standard Model](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md)

A [Higgs chiral doublet](higgs-chiral-doublet.md) is a weak-doublet [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md) containing a Higgs [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md), a [higgsino](higgsino.md) and an [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md). In the [MSSM](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md), $H_u$ has [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) $+1/2$ and $H_d$ has [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) $-1/2$. Their paired charges enable [higgsino anomaly cancellation](higgsino-anomaly-cancellation.md) and the [holomorphic need for two Higgs chiral doublets](holomorphic-need-for-two-higgs-chiral-doublets.md).
