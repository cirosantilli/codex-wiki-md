# Pollen tube

↑ **Parent:** [Pollen](pollen.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pollen_tube)

A growing extension from a [pollen](pollen.md) grain. In a compatible [flower](flower.md), the [pollen tube](pollen-tube.md) provides a route for [sperm](sperm.md) to the [angiosperm female gametophyte](angiosperm-female-gametophyte.md).[Biology 2e, §32.2 Double Fertilization, Figure 32.18](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/32-2-pollination-and-fertilization.md)
