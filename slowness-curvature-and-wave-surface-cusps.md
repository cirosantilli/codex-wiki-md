# Slowness curvature and wave-surface cusps

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave surface](elastic-wave-surface.md)

In a symmetry-plane cross-section, write $\mathbf p=r(\theta)\mathbf n(\theta)$, $v=1/r$, and $\mathbf t=\mathbf n\prime$. The [elastic wave surface](elastic-wave-surface.md) is $\mathbf g=v\mathbf n+v\prime\mathbf t$. Its derivative vanishes when $v+v\prime\prime=(r^2+2r\prime^2-r r\prime\prime)/r^3=0$, exactly an [inflection point](inflection-point.md) of the regular [slowness surface](elastic-slowness-surface.md). At a simple zero the second and third derivatives of $\mathbf g$ are linearly independent, giving an ordinary [cusp](cusp-algebraic-geometry.md). Smooth nonconvex [slowness surface](elastic-slowness-surface.md) sheets can therefore generate cusped [wavefronts](wavefront.md).
