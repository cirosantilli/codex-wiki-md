# Strengthened Legendre condition

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre necessary condition](legendre-necessary-condition.md)

Strict positivity of the highest-[derivative](derivative.md) coefficient on the closed parameter interval is the strengthened Legendre condition. Continuity then gives a uniform positive lower bound and makes the [Jacobi equation](jacobi-equation.md) a regular second-order equation. It permits the [Jacobi positivity criterion](jacobi-positivity-criterion.md). It is not necessary for mere strict positivity of a [second variation](second-variation.md): $\int\varphi^2$ is strictly positive on nonzero variations even though its [derivative](derivative.md) coefficient vanishes.
