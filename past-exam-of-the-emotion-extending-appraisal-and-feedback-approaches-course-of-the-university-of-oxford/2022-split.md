# 2022

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the emotion: extending appraisal and feedback approaches course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all six essay options in the [Emotion: extending appraisal and feedback approaches course of the University of Oxford](../emotion-extending-appraisal-and-feedback-approaches-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) examination held on 25 May 2022. The cover allowed candidates to choose three; every option is addressed here. The arguments use evidence published before the examination. The mathematical examples and diagrams are explanatory constructions, not new experimental findings.

Source: [Oxford examination release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/experimental_psychology_past_exa/response/2757831/attach/3/New%20Compressed%20zipped%20Folder.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member a16467w1.pdf. The original cover supplies the code and date; the audited course identification supplies Part II.
