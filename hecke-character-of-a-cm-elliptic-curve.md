# Hecke character of a CM elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke character](hecke-character.md)

Suppose $F$ contains the CM field $K$ and all CM endomorphisms are defined over $F$. [CM reciprocity](cm-reciprocity-theorem.md) compares the torus with its idele-transformed lattice and gives $a(s)\in K^\times$ for finite ideles. Then $\psi_E(s)=a(s_f)/N_{F/K}(s)_\infty$. On principal ideles, $a(b_f)=N_{F/K}(b)$, so the character descends to the [idèle class group](idele-class-group.md). At a good prime its ideal value reduces to the [Frobenius isogeny](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). If the original field omits $K$, construct the character over $FK$ and induce the corresponding representation down to the original field.
