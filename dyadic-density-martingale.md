# Dyadic density martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue decomposition theorem](lebesgue-decomposition-theorem.md)

For a finite positive [measure](measure.md) on $[0,1)$, average its mass over each half-open [dyadic interval](dyadic-interval.md). The resulting nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md) has constant [expectation](expected-value.md) $\mu([0,1))$: a parent interval's mass is the sum of its children's masses. Its almost-sure limit is integrable but the [martingale](martingale-split.md) need not be [uniformly integrable](uniform-integrability.md). The limit recovers the density of the absolutely continuous component in the [Lebesgue decomposition theorem](lebesgue-decomposition-theorem.md).
