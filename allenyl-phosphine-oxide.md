# Allenyl phosphine oxide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

An [allenyl phosphine oxide](allenyl-phosphine-oxide.md) contains an [allene](allene.md) attached to a phosphine-oxide group. Attack at the central [allene](allene.md) [carbon](carbon.md) can generate a carbanion on the adjacent [phosphorus](phosphorus.md)-bearing [carbon](carbon.md), which is stabilized by the phosphoryl substituent.
