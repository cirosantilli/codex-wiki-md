# Klainerman-Sobolev inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field method for wave equations](vector-field-method-for-wave-equations.md)

In three spatial dimensions, with $Z$ the eleven translations, spatial rotations, boosts and scaling [commutation vector fields for the wave equation](commutation-vector-field-for-the-wave-equation.md), a sufficiently decaying [smooth function](smooth-function.md) satisfies $|u(t,x)|\leq C(1+t+|x|)^{-1}(1+|t-|x||)^{-1/2}\sum_{|I|\leq2}\|Z^Iu(t)\|_2$. This weighted [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md) converts control of [commuted wave energy](commuted-wave-energy.md) into decay. It holds independently of any [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) satisfied by $u$.
