# Principal diffusion coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear diffusion equation](nonlinear-diffusion-equation.md)

For flux $c(|\nabla u|)\nabla u$, the [derivative](derivative.md) matrix is $c(s)I+c'(s)\nabla u\otimes\nabla u/s$ with $s=|\nabla u|>0$. Its tangential and normal coefficients are $c(s)$ and $c(s)+sc'(s)$. Positivity of the scalar conductance alone therefore does not guarantee forward parabolicity.
