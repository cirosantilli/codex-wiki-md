# Frobenius isogeny of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md)

For an elliptic curve over $\mathbb F_q$, the Frobenius isogeny sends $(x,y)$ to $(x^q,y^q)$. If $a=q+1-\#E(\mathbb F_q)$, then

$$
\pi^2-[a]\pi+[q]=0.
$$
