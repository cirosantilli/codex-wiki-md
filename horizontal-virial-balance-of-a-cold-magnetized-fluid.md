# Horizontal virial balance of a cold magnetized fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetized-fluid tensor virial theorem](magnetized-fluid-tensor-virial-theorem.md)

At an initially resting instant with negligible [pressure](pressure.md), sum the horizontal components of the [magnetized-fluid tensor virial theorem](magnetized-fluid-tensor-virial-theorem.md). The horizontal magnetic trace is $-B_z^2/\mu_0$ and the gravitational force-stress trace is $(\partial_z\Phi)^2/(4\pi G)$. Their negatives give the displayed radial second-moment balance. Vacuum field stresses contribute too; being initially at rest does not imply zero acceleration.
