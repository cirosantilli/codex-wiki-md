# Prism graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prism_graph)

A [prism graph](prism-graph.md) joins two copies of a [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md) by matching corresponding vertices. The [triangular prism graph](triangular-prism-graph.md) is the three-cycle case, with six vertices and nine edges.
