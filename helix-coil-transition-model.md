# Helix-coil transition model

↑ **Parent:** [Biopolymer mechanics](biopolymer-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helix–coil_transition_model)

A helix-coil transition model assigns local states of a [biopolymer](biopolymer.md) to an ordered helical conformation or a disordered coil conformation. Their competing [free energies](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) determine the tendency to form a helix, while the cost of boundaries between regions gives cooperativity: forming a new helical run can be less favorable than extending an existing one. A coil state is a class of disordered conformations, not a claim that the whole molecule is an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md). The [Zimm-Bragg model](zimm-bragg-model.md) is a simple nearest-neighbour statistical model of this competition.
