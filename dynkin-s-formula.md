<h1 id="dynkin-s-formula">Dynkin's formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Infinitesimal generator (stochastic processes)](infinitesimal-generator-stochastic-processes.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynkin's_formula)

For a [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) with [infinitesimal generator](infinitesimal-generator-stochastic-processes.md) $L$ and a suitable test function $f$, the displayed process is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). With integrability it is a true [martingale](martingale-split.md) and yields the corresponding expectation identity at admissible stopping times. For bounded $f,Lf$ in the semigroup generator domain, $P_tf-f=\int_0^tP_sLf\,ds$. The [Markov property](markov-property.md) applies this identity conditionally over each interval, proving the martingale increments have zero conditional expectation. Localization gives the local version. For a continuous diffusion, these identities are the [martingale problem](martingale-problem.md) for $L$.
