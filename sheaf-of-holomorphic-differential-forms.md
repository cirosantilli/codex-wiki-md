# Sheaf of holomorphic differential forms

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic de Rham complex](holomorphic-de-rham-complex.md)

On a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md), $\Omega_X^p$ is the [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) of holomorphic degree-$p$ forms. It is the kernel of the [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md) on $\mathcal A^{p,0}$. The [Dolbeault theorem](dolbeault-theorem.md) computes its [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) using smooth forms.
