# Translational burst size

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

The expected number of [proteins](protein-split.md) produced by one [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) before its removal. With translation rate $r$ and exponential removal rate $\alpha_R$, $b=r/\alpha_R$. Independent Poisson translation during an exponential lifetime yields a geometric burst count on nonnegative integers, with [variance](variance-split.md) $b(1+b)$. In the short-message-lifetime limit with independent unit protein removal, the protein [Fano factor](fano-factor.md) tends to $1+b$.
