# Bipartite sign rounding bound

↑ **Parent:** [Krivine rounding scheme](krivine-rounding-scheme.md)

For [bipartite binary quadratic optimization](bipartite-binary-quadratic-optimization.md), if $v^*$ is the sign optimum and $p^*_{\rm SDP}$ its [semidefinite relaxation of binary quadratic optimization](semidefinite-relaxation-of-binary-quadratic-optimization.md) value, then $c_Kp^*_{\rm SDP}\leq v^*\leq p^*_{\rm SDP}$. The upper bound follows from rank-one lifting. For the lower bound, [Krivine rounding scheme](krivine-rounding-scheme.md) preprocessing followed by [Gaussian hyperplane rounding](gaussian-hyperplane-rounding.md) has expected objective exactly $c_Kp^*_{\rm SDP}$, and every rounded [vector](vector.md) is feasible. Some outcome reaches at least this [expectation](expected-value.md). No [independence](independent-random-variables.md) of rounded coordinates or [positive semidefiniteness](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) of the original bipartite objective is needed.
