# Debye heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Debye model](debye-model.md)

The three-dimensional [Debye model](debye-model.md) normalizes its [phonon](phonon.md) [density of states](density-of-states.md) to $3N$ modes: $g(\omega)=9N\omega^2/\omega_D^3$ below the [Debye frequency](debye-frequency.md) $\omega_D$. The thermal [energy](energy-split.md) is $U=\int_0^{\omega_D}g(\omega)\hbar\omega/[e^{\hbar\omega/(k_BT)}-1]d\omega$. Differentiate at fixed [volume](volume.md) and substitute $x=\hbar\omega/(k_BT)$ to obtain the displayed [heat capacity at constant volume](heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md).

At $T\ll\Theta_D$, integration by parts gives $\int_0^\infty x^4e^x/(e^x-1)^2dx=4\int_0^\infty x^3/(e^x-1)dx=4\pi^4/15$. Therefore $C_V\sim(12\pi^4/5)Nk_B(T/\Theta_D)^3$. At $T\gg\Theta_D$, each mode approaches the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md) value $k_B$ of [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), so $C_V\to3Nk_B$, the [Dulong-Petit law](dulong-petit-law.md). Fixed harmonic [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) does not contribute to either limit.
