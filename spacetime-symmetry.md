# Spacetime symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry (physics)](symmetry-physics.md)

An automorphism preserving the physically specified background structure of [spacetime](spacetime.md). With only a [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) and fixed orientations, the appropriate external symmetries are the corresponding [Poincaré group](poincare-group.md) elements. An added preferred [vector field](vector-field.md) reduces this group. This is distinct from merely changing coordinates.
