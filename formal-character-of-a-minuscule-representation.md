# Formal character of a minuscule representation

↑ **Parent:** [Minuscule representation](minuscule-representation.md)

The [formal character of a weight module](formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md) for a [minuscule representation](minuscule-representation.md) of [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\lambda$ is $\operatorname{ch}V(\lambda)=\sum_{\mu\in W\lambda}e^\mu$. The sum is over distinct points of the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) orbit, so $\dim V(\lambda)=|W\lambda|$.
