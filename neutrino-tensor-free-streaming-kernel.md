# Neutrino tensor free-streaming kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino tensor anisotropic stress](neutrino-tensor-anisotropic-stress.md)

Projecting free-streaming [neutrinos](neutrino.md) onto the tensor [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) gives

$$
K(x)=\frac1{16}\int_{-1}^{1}(1-\mu^2)^2e^{-ix\mu}\,d\mu
=\frac{j_2(x)}{x^2},\qquad K(0)=\frac1{15}.
$$

Here $j_2$ is a [Spherical Bessel function](spherical-bessel-function.md). The continuous value at zero follows by integrating $(1-\mu^2)^2$, whose integral is $16/15$.
