# Mean free path

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md)

The mean free path is the average distance travelled between [collisions](collision.md). For dilute targets with [number density](number-density.md) $n$ and effective [collision cross-section](collision-cross-section.md) $\sigma_c$, $\ell\sim1/(n\sigma_c)$; identical-particle conventions introduce factors such as $\sqrt2$.
