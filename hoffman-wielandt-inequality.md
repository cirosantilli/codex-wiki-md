<h1 id="hoffman-wielandt-inequality">Hoffman–Wielandt inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Normal matrix](normal-matrix.md)

For two [normal matrices](normal-matrix.md) of the same size, a pairing of their [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) has squared Euclidean discrepancy at most the squared [Frobenius norm](frobenius-norm.md) of their difference. For [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md), the increasing real [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) order gives such a pairing, because it minimizes squared discrepancy. For real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md), the norm square is $\operatorname{Tr}(A-B)^2$.
