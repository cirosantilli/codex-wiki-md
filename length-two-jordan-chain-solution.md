# Length-two Jordan chain solution

↑ **Parent:** [Jordan chain](jordan-chain.md)

If $(M-\lambda I)e=0$ and $(M-\lambda I)v=e$, the vector $v$ is a [generalized eigenvector](generalized-eigenvector.md) and $e^{\lambda t}(v+te)$ solves $\dot x=Mx$. The polynomial factor distinguishes a nontrivial [Jordan block](jordan-block.md) from an ordinary repeated eigenvalue with a full eigenvector [basis](basis.md).
