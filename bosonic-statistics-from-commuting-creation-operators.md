# Bosonic statistics from commuting creation operators

↑ **Parent:** [Bosonic exchange symmetry](bosonic-exchange-symmetry.md)

Commuting [creation operators](creation-operator.md) make multiparticle states invariant under exchange of their labels. With $[a,a^\dagger]=1$, the normalized number states $(a^\dagger)^r|0\rangle/\sqrt{r!}$ exist for all $r\ge0$, giving unrestricted [bosonic occupation numbers](bosonic-occupation-number.md). The single-mode thermal sum is $Z=\sum_{r\ge0}e^{-\beta Er}=(1-e^{-\beta E})^{-1}$ and the mean occupation is $(e^{\beta E}-1)^{-1}$. Thus the exchange symmetry and the [Bose-Einstein distribution](bose-einstein-distribution.md) are two expressions of the same bosonic algebra.
