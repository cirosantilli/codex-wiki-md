# Growth function of a discrete metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)

For a locally finite discrete [metric space](metric-space.md) $X$ with basepoint $x_0$, its growth function is

$$
\beta_{X,x_0}(r)=\#\{x\in X:d(x,x_0)\leq r\}.
$$

Changing the basepoint changes the argument by at most an additive constant. In a vertex-transitive metric space, including every [Cayley graph](cayley-graph.md), the growth function is exactly independent of the basepoint.
