# Primitive unit cell

↑ **Parent:** [Unit cell](unit-cell.md)

A fundamental cell of a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md), of volume equal to the absolute [determinant](determinant.md) of any primitive lattice basis. Direct and [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) primitive cell volumes multiply to $(2\pi)^d$ in dimension $d$ with the angular-wavenumber convention.
