# Intuitionistic first-order logic

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

[Intuitionistic first-order logic](intuitionistic-first-order-logic.md) has the usual introduction and elimination rules for logical connectives, quantifiers and [logical equality](logical-equality.md), but no unrestricted [double-negation elimination](double-negation-elimination.md) or [law of excluded middle](law-of-excluded-middle.md). Its proofs retain the witness and [logical disjunction](logical-disjunction.md) information that can be lost in [classical first-order logic](classical-first-order-logic.md).
