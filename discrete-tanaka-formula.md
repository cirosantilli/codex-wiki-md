# Discrete Tanaka formula

↑ **Parent:** [Tanaka's formula](tanaka-s-formula.md)

For integer paths with increments in $\{-1,0,1\}$, the [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) obeys a telescoping identity with slope $f(x)=\mathbf1_{\{x>0\}}+\frac12\mathbf1_{\{x=0\}}$ and a correction $\frac12\mathbf1_{\{S_{t-1}=K\}}(\Delta S_t)^2$ at the kink. For a [martingale](martingale-split.md), the slope term is a [martingale transform](martingale-transform.md).
