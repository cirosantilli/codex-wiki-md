# Exponential ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian closed category](cartesian-closed-category.md)

A full subcategory $\mathcal D$ of a [Cartesian closed category](cartesian-closed-category.md) $\mathcal C$ is an exponential ideal when $B^A$ belongs to $\mathcal D$ for every $B\in\mathcal D$ and every $A\in\mathcal C$.
