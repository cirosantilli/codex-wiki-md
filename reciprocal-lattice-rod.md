# Reciprocal lattice rod

↑ **Parent:** [Reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md)

An elongated reciprocal-space reflection caused by finite extent in the corresponding real-space direction. A foil of thickness $t$ has reflection breadth of order $1/t$ along its normal, increasing the range over which a reflection can intersect an [Ewald sphere](ewald-sphere.md). The exact profile depends on the specimen shape and scattering model.
