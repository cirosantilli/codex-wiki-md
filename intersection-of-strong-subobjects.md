# Intersection of strong subobjects

↑ **Parent:** [Strong monomorphism](strong-monomorphism.md)

Whenever the relevant pullback exists, the intersection of two [strong subobjects](strong-monomorphism.md) of an object is strong. More generally, an arbitrary small intersection of strong subobjects is strong when it exists: lift into each subobject and use their common composite into the ambient object to obtain a map into the limit.
