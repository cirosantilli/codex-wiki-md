# Anti-aliasing filter

↑ **Parent:** [Aliasing](aliasing.md)

An [anti-aliasing filter](anti-aliasing-filter.md) suppresses signal components outside the intended acquisition band before sampling. For voltage recording it is an analogue filter ahead of the [analog-to-digital converter](analog-to-digital-converter.md), with a transition band allowed below half the sampling rate. Once two frequencies have become indistinguishable through [aliasing](aliasing.md), a digital filter generally cannot recover the lost distinction. The [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md) concerns an already [bandlimited function](bandlimited-function.md); a physical filter approximates that prerequisite.
