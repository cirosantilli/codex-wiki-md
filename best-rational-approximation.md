# Best rational approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine approximation](diophantine-approximation.md)

A reduced fraction $p/q$ is a best rational approximation of the first kind to a [real number](real-number.md) $\alpha$ when no rational number with denominator at most $q$ is closer to $\alpha$.
