# p-variation of a path

↑ **Parent:** [Total variation of a function](total-variation-of-a-function.md)

For $p\ge1$, the displayed quantity measures the accumulated oscillation of a path in a [metric space](metric-space.md). For $p=1$ it is total variation. A [Hölder continuous](holder-condition.md) path of exponent $\alpha$ has finite $p$-variation on a compact interval if $p\alpha\ge1$. Applying this construction to the [Carnot-Carathéodory distance](carnot-caratheodory-distance.md) gives the defining regularity of a [weak geometric p-rough path](weak-geometric-p-rough-path.md).
