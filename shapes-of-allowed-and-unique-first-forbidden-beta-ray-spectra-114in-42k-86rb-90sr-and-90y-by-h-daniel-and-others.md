Physical Review 136, B1240. Primary experimental study with a double-lens beta spectrometer. The abstract distinguishes the allowed indium spectrum from the unique first-forbidden potassium, rubidium, strontium and yttrium spectra, and reports deviations from their simplest shape models. This is not an experimental measurement of yttrium-91 or the source exam's plotted points.
