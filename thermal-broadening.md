# Thermal broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Energy level](energy-level.md)

[temperature](temperature-split.md) can smear measured spectral or transport features through the spread of occupied energies and through temperature-dependent scattering. Fermi-edge occupation broadening and phonon-induced [lifetime broadening](lifetime-broadening.md) are different mechanisms and should not be equated.
