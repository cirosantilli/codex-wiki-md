# CM Tate module stabilizer

↑ **Parent:** [Tate module](tate-module.md)

Let $R=K\cap\operatorname{End}_k(E)$. If $\alpha\in R$ maps the [Tate module](tate-module.md) into $\ell^nT_\ell(E)$, it kills $E[\ell^n]$ and factors through the quotient isogeny $[\ell^n]$. The resulting [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) is $\alpha/\ell^n$, belongs to $K$ and is defined over $k$, hence belongs to $R$. Approximation in $R\otimes\mathbb Z_\ell$ now identifies the displayed stabilizer. This does not assume that the integral [Tate module](tate-module.md) is free over a possibly nonmaximal CM order.
