# Euler characteristic and holes

↑ **Parent:** [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md)

For a finite [CW complex](cw-complex.md), $b_0$ counts [connected components](connected-component.md): its [connected components](connected-component.md) are its [path components](path-component.md), as recorded by the [zeroth homology group](zeroth-homology-group.md). For a finite [graph](graph-split.md) regarded as a one-dimensional [CW complex](cw-complex.md), no higher [homology groups](homology-group.md) occur, so $b_1=E-V+b_0$ counts independent [graph cycles](cycle-in-a-graph.md), and $\chi=b_0-b_1$. For a connected planar [surface](topological-surface-split.md) obtained by removing $h$ disjoint open [disks](disk-mathematics.md) from the interior of a closed [disk](disk-mathematics.md), $(b_0,b_1,b_2)=(1,h,0)$ and $\chi=1-h$; the [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) is $h=1$.

The word "hole" needs its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). A [two-sphere](two-sphere.md) encloses a cavity but has $b_1=0$ and $b_2=1$. A closed [orientable](orientable-surface.md) [surface](topological-surface-split.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$ instead has $(b_0,b_1,b_2)=(1,2g,1)$ and $\chi=2-2g$: increasing the [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) by one adds two independent one-dimensional [homology classes](homology-class.md). In higher [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md), further [homology groups](homology-group.md) enter with alternating signs; there is no universal "components minus handles" rule.
