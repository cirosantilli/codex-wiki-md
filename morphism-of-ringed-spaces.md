# Morphism of ringed spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Ringed space](ringed-space-split.md)

A morphism of ringed spaces $f:(X,\mathcal O_X)\to(Y,\mathcal O_Y)$ consists of a continuous map $f:X\to Y$ and a morphism of [sheaves of rings](sheaf-of-rings.md) $f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y\to\mathcal O_X$.
