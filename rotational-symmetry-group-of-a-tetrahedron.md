# Rotational symmetry group of a tetrahedron

↑ **Parent:** [Tetrahedral symmetry](tetrahedral-symmetry.md)

The orientation-preserving symmetries of a [regular tetrahedron](regular-tetrahedron.md) form the alternating [group](group-split.md) on its four vertices. There are twelve elements: the identity, eight [rotational symmetries](rotational-symmetry.md) of order three, and three half-turns.
