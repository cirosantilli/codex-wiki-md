# Coordinate subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Vector subspace](vector-subspace.md)

For a chosen [basis](basis.md) $(e_1,\ldots,e_n)$, the [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) spanned by a specified collection of [basis vectors](basis-vector.md). In the standard [basis](basis.md) of [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), its [vectors](vector.md) have zero coordinates outside $\mathcal B$. The [block coordinate gradient](block-coordinate-gradient.md) is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) of the full [gradient](gradient.md) onto this subspace. The [fixed coordinate subspace](fixed-coordinate-subspace.md) consisting of the first $k$ coordinates is a particular example.
