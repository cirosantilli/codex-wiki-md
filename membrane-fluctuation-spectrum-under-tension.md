# Membrane fluctuation spectrum under tension

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal membrane undulation](thermal-membrane-undulation.md)

For small-slope height fluctuations of a nearly planar membrane, $A$ is projected area, $\sigma$ is [membrane tension](membrane-tension.md), $\kappa$ is [membrane bending modulus](membrane-bending-modulus.md), and $q$ is a [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). With a Fourier convention in which the mode amplitude $h_{\mathbf q}$ has units of length, the quadratic [Helfrich membrane energy](thermal-membrane-undulation.md) and the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md) give the displayed thermal variance. Bending dominates for $q\gg\sqrt{\sigma/\kappa}$, giving a $q^{-4}$ spectrum. Tension dominates at smaller $q$, giving $q^{-2}$. The corresponding [membrane elastic length](membrane-elastic-length.md) is $\sqrt{\kappa/\sigma}$ when length is measured as $1/q$; a wavelength convention multiplies it by $2\pi$.
