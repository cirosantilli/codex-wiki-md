# One-particle distribution function

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic kinetic theory](relativistic-kinetic-theory.md)

A scalar function on the future mass shell describing the expected particle count in a [phase-space distribution](phase-space-distribution-function.md) cell. In a local [orthonormal tetrad](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md), the number crossing a spacelike surface element is $f\,p^\mu d\Sigma_\mu\,d^3p/E$. On a constant-time slice it becomes $f\,d^3x\,d^3p$. Including degeneracy and phase-space normalization in $f$ gives the [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) $T^{\mu\nu}=\int(d^3p/E)fp^\mu p^\nu$. Other conventions may put the degeneracy or $(2\pi)^{-3}$ explicitly in the measure.
