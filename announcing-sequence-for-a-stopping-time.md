# Announcing sequence for a stopping time

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping time](stopping-time.md)

An announcing sequence consists of [stopping times](stopping-time.md) increasing to $T$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), strictly smaller than $T$ on $\{T>0\}$. Such a lifetime can be approached through stopped intervals on which a [locally defined stochastic process](locally-defined-stochastic-process.md) is an ordinary [adapted process](adapted-process.md). For the usual [maximal local solution of a stochastic differential equation](maximal-local-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md), the exit times $\tau_n$ from a nested [compact exhaustion](compact-exhaustion.md) of the open domain, capped as $T_n=\tau_n\wedge n$, supply this sequence. The existence of such a sequence is an extra lifetime convention; arbitrary [stopping times](stopping-time.md) need not admit one.
