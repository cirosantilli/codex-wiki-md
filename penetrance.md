# Penetrance

↑ **Parent:** [Phenotype](phenotype.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penetrance)

Penetrance is the probability of an expressed [phenotype](phenotype.md) conditional on a specified [genotype](genotype.md). It can depend on age, sex and environment. A fully penetrant recessive model assigns disease probability one to the recessive homozygote and zero to other genotypes; incomplete penetrance or phenocopies replace these indicators by intermediate probabilities.
