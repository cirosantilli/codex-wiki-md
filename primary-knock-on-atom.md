# Primary knock-on atom

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement damage](displacement-damage.md)

A lattice atom given a recoil by an incident particle. Its subsequent collisions can generate a [displacement cascade](displacement-cascade.md); its energy distribution depends on the incident radiation and material.
