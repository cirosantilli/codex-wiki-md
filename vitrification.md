# Vitrification

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)

[vitrification](vitrification.md) produces a noncrystalline glassy state. Rapid freezing of thin aqueous specimens is used to preserve hydrated biological structures for [cryo-electron microscopy](cryo-electron-microscopy.md); vitrification does not prevent all subsequent [radiation damage](radiation-damage.md).
