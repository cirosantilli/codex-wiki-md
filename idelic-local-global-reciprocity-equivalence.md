# Idelic local-global reciprocity equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Artin reciprocity law](artin-reciprocity-law.md)

For a finite [abelian extension](abelian-extension.md) of [number fields](number-field.md), local reciprocity maps into the local [decomposition groups](decomposition-group.md) combine to a continuous map on the [idele group](idele-group.md): outside finitely many places they kill units, and an idele has nonunit components only finitely often. The [Artin reciprocity law](artin-reciprocity-law.md) is equivalent to the compatibility that this product kills every [principal idele](principal-idele.md). Conversely, restrict a global continuous Artin homomorphism to each single-place embedding of $K_v^*$. These maps reconstruct the global map, by density of finite-component ideles and triviality on a basic open subgroup of units. Merely having unrelated local maps omits the essential principal-idele compatibility.
