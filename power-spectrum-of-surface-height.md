# Power spectrum of surface height

↑ **Parent:** [Small-height Dirichlet scattering](small-height-dirichlet-scattering.md)

For a real zero-mean stationary height process, its [power spectrum of surface height](power-spectrum-of-surface-height.md) is the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of the height [covariance function](covariance-function.md). It is even and nonnegative, and $\sigma^2=(2\pi)^{-1}\int S_h(q)dq$. More general stationary processes have a spectral measure rather than a density. Weighted spectral moments control the existence of boundary derivatives and second-order mean scattering corrections.
