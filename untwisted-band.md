# Untwisted band

↑ **Parent:** [Topological surface](topological-surface-split.md)

An untwisted band is the [topological surface](topological-surface-split.md) obtained from a rectangle by identifying $(0,v)$ with $(1,v)$. It is the [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) of a [circle](circle.md) and a [closed interval](closed-real-interval.md), and is [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to a closed [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md). A finite portion of the lateral surface of a [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) realizes it in three-dimensional space. Its two [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) circles are $S^1\times\{-1\}$ and $S^1\times\{1\}$, and the matching seam directions make it an [orientable surface](orientable-surface.md). The [Möbius band](mobius-band.md) changes just one identification sign and loses both this two-component boundary and its [orientability](orientability.md).
