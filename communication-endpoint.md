# Communication endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Communication protocol](communication-protocol.md)

A [communication endpoint](communication-endpoint.md) is a participant at the boundary of a specified communication function. Always identify the level: a [TLS](transport-layer-security.md) peer, a transport [endpoint](communication-endpoint.md) and an application [endpoint](communication-endpoint.md) need not represent the same boundary of correctness or trust.
