# Local volatility model

↑ **Parent:** [Local volatility](local-volatility.md)

The stock diffusion $dS_t=S_t(rdt+\sigma(t,S_t)dW_t)$ has spot-dependent [local volatility](local-volatility.md). With positive volatility and the Brownian filtration, [Brownian martingale representation theorem](brownian-martingale-representation-theorem.md) converts discounted payoff martingales into stock gains.
