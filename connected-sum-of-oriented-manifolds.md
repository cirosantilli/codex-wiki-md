# Connected sum of oriented manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Closed manifold](closed-manifold.md)

For connected oriented closed $d$-dimensional [manifolds](topological-manifold.md) with $d\geq2$, their connected sum removes an open $d$-dimensional ball from each and identifies the resulting boundary [spheres](sphere.md) by an [orientation-reversing diffeomorphism](orientation-reversing-diffeomorphism.md). The remaining orientations fit together. Collapsing the separating sphere gives a [pinch map](pinch-map.md) to the [wedge sum](wedge-sum.md) of the two closed manifolds, and projection to either summand has [degree of a continuous mapping](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) one. In intermediate positive degrees the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) is the direct sum of the summand groups. Products of classes from different summands vanish; products landing in top degree use the single common [orientation class](fundamental-class.md). These statements follow from [excision](excision-theorem.md), the [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) and the degree-one projections.
