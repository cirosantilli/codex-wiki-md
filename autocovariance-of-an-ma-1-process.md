<h1 id="autocovariance-of-an-ma-1-process">Autocovariance of an MA(1) process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-average process of order one](moving-average-process-of-order-one.md)

Only observations at lag one share a noise term. Thus $\gamma_0=\sigma^2(1+\theta^2)$, $\gamma_{\pm1}=\sigma^2\theta$, and every other lag has zero [covariance](covariance.md). This tridiagonal finite [covariance](covariance.md) structure makes the [finite-sample innovations of an MA(1) process](finite-sample-innovations-of-an-ma-1-process.md) particularly simple.
