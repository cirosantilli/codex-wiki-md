# ICD (Pseudomonas aeruginosa)

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

In the [Pseudomonas aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) branchpoint discussed here, [ICD](icd-pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md) is the [isocitrate](isocitrate.md) dehydrogenase susceptible to AceK-dependent [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md). It must be distinguished from the separate IDH activity, which is not regulated by that [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) route.
