# Perfect secrecy

↑ **Parent:** [One-time pad](one-time-pad.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_secrecy)

An encryption scheme has perfect secrecy when the ciphertext and plaintext are [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md), so observing the ciphertext does not change the plaintext's [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md).
