# Central idempotent

↑ **Parent:** [Center of an associative algebra](center-of-an-associative-algebra.md)

A central idempotent is an [idempotent](idempotent.md) in the [center of an associative algebra](center-of-an-associative-algebra.md). It gives a decomposition $A=Ae\oplus A(1-e)$ into two-sided [ideals](ideal.md), with products between the summands zero. The identities of these summands are $e$ and $1-e$.
