# Smooth-mask vanishing-moment criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Vanishing moment](vanishing-moment.md)

Suppose an integrable [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) [scaling function](scaling-function.md) has a [low-pass filter of a multiresolution analysis](low-pass-filter-of-a-multiresolution-analysis.md) that is $C^{p-1}$ near $\pi$, and its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) is $C^{p-1}$ near zero. If the associated [wavelet](wavelet.md) has $p$ integrable [vanishing moments](vanishing-moment.md), then $m^{(k)}(\pi)=0$ for $k<p$. Indeed, [moment differentiation of the Fourier transform](moment-differentiation-of-the-fourier-transform.md) makes $\widehat\psi^{(k)}(0)=0$, while $|\widehat\varphi(0)|=1$. In $\widehat\psi(2t)=e^{-it}\overline{m(t+\pi)}\widehat\varphi(t)$, division by the nonzero [smooth](smooth-function.md) factor proves the conclusion. With only [continuity](continuous-function.md) of that factor one still obtains a [Peano zero](peano-zero.md), but not automatically higher ordinary [derivatives](derivative.md).
