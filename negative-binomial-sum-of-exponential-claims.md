# Negative-binomial sum of exponential claims

↑ **Parent:** [Random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md)

Let $p+q=1$ and let the original independent count have [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md) with [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) $\binom{n+k-1}{k}p^nq^k$ on $k\ge0$. With unit-rate [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) severities, its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is $[p+qp/(p-t)]^n$ for $t<p$. Thus it also has a [random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md) representation with independent [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) count $B$ and rate-$p$ [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) steps. For $n=2$ the law is $p^2\delta_0+2pq\operatorname{Exp}(p)+q^2\operatorname{Erlang}(2,p)$. The [hurdle decomposition of a positive random sum](hurdle-decomposition-of-a-positive-random-sum.md) therefore has zero mass $p^2$ and positive density $p^2e^{-px}(2q+q^2x)/(1-p^2)$.
