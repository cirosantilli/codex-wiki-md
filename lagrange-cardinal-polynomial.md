# Lagrange cardinal polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange polynomial](lagrange-polynomial.md)

For distinct [interpolation nodes](interpolation-node.md) $x_0,\ldots,x_n$, the polynomial $\ell_i(x)=\prod_{j\ne i}(x-x_j)/(x_i-x_j)$ has $\ell_i(x_j)=\delta_{ij}$. These cardinal polynomials form a [basis](basis.md) of the [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $n$, and give $p(x)=\sum_i p(x_i)\ell_i(x)$.
