# Integer polynomial ring is not a principal ideal domain

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial ring over a unique factorization domain](polynomial-ring-over-a-unique-factorization-domain.md)

The [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) $\mathbb Z[X]$ is a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md) by [Gauss lemma for polynomials](gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md), but its [ideal](ideal.md) $(2,X)$ is not principal. Any generator would divide both 2 and $X$, hence be a unit: a divisor of 2 has degree zero, and a constant dividing $X$ is $\pm1$. Yet evaluation at zero followed by reduction modulo 2 annihilates the ideal and not 1. It embeds in the [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md) $\mathbb Q[X]$, illustrating that the property need not pass to a subring.
