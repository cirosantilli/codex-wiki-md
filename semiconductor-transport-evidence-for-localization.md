# Semiconductor transport evidence for localization

↑ **Parent:** [Anderson localization](anderson-localization.md)

[Nevill Francis Mott](nevill-francis-mott.md)'s 1977 lecture discusses [silicon](silicon.md) deposited in a glow discharge. Injected [electrons](electron.md) provided a drift-mobility readout; [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md) dependence tested localized-state transport and excitation toward a [mobility edge](mobility-edge.md). Varying [temperature](temperature-split.md) distinguishes transport regimes. The lecture gives no contact geometry or timing circuit here, so none is inferred. Its Figure 2 is schematic, not measured data.[Electrons in Glass, pp.404–406, Fig.2 and discussion of glow-discharge-deposited silicon; inspected institutional mirror [https://www.nevis.columbia.edu/~haas/documents/physics_nobel_lectures/physics71-80-2.pdf](https://www.nevis.columbia.edu/~haas/documents/physics_nobel_lectures/physics71-80-2.pdf)](electrons-in-glass-by-nevill-francis-mott.md)

This evidence motivates a transport question beyond the closed-chain [eigenvalue problem](eigenvalue-problem.md): how does a prepared charge disturbance move under the conditions of a real sample? The numerical figures above supply neither drift-mobility measurements nor a model of the deposition process. Finite-size concentration alone cannot reproduce an experimental [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) curve.

Finite [temperature](temperature-split.md) permits [phonon](phonon.md)-assisted hopping between [localized eigenstates](localized-eigenstate.md) or thermal excitation toward a [mobility edge](mobility-edge.md); localized states therefore need not imply zero measured [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) at every nonzero [temperature](temperature-split.md).[Electrons in Glass, pp.405,407, localized-state hopping and thermal excitation](electrons-in-glass-by-nevill-francis-mott.md)

Real [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) destroys the relative [wave phases](phase-waves.md) needed for long-path [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md). Observing coherent confinement requires the distance over which those [wave phases](phase-waves.md) survive to be long enough to explore the localized region, and the sample to be large enough to resolve it. [Temperature](temperature-split.md) alone is not an automatic switch: the actual inelastic processes set the relevant scales. Static elastic [wave scattering](wave-scattering.md) preserves coherence within each realization. Loss of the ensemble-averaged [coherent field](coherent-wave-field.md) through random [wave phases](phase-waves.md) is therefore not, by itself, physical [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) of an individual sample.
