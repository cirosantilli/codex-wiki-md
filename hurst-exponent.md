# Hurst exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Fractional Brownian motion](fractional-brownian-motion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurst_exponent)

For [fractional Brownian motion](fractional-brownian-motion.md), $H\in(0,1)$ controls the [variance](variance-split.md) $|t-s|^{2H}$ of increments and the scaling of its [finite-dimensional distributions](finite-dimensional-distribution.md). Larger $H$ means smaller increment [variance](variance-split.md) on intervals shorter than one and greater sample-path [Hölder continuity](holder-condition.md).
