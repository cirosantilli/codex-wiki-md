# Meagre set meeting every Ramsey cone in both colours

↑ **Parent:** [Gap-doubling closed family of infinite subsets](gap-doubling-closed-family-of-infinite-subsets.md)

A closed [nowhere dense](nowhere-dense-set.md) family can meet every cone $[M]^\omega$ in continuum many sets, as the [gap-doubling closed family of infinite subsets](gap-doubling-closed-family-of-infinite-subsets.md) does. Well-order the cones and choose two fresh candidates in each, permanently reserving one of each colour. The red family meets every cone and its complement contains every reserved blue candidate, so it is not Ramsey. As a subset of the closed nowhere dense family, it is still nowhere dense and has the [Baire property in the ordinary infinite-subset topology](baire-property-in-the-ordinary-infinite-subset-topology.md). This recursion requires no regularity assumption on the continuum cardinal.
