# Transverse miss on a rotating platform

↑ **Parent:** [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md)

A projectile thrown across a rotating disk's diameter at [speed](speed.md) $v$ relative to the thrower inherits the thrower's tangential [velocity](velocity.md). During its approximate flight time $2a/v$, a receiver at the opposite rim moves tangentially in the opposite direction. The relative sideways [displacement](displacement.md) is consequently $4\omega a^2/v$ to leading order in $a\omega/v$. Integrating the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) in the disk's frame gives the same result.
