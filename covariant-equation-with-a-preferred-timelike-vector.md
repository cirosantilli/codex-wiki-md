# Covariant equation with a preferred timelike vector

↑ **Parent:** [General covariance](general-covariance.md)

In units $c=1$ and [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, take a fixed unit timelike [vector field](vector-field.md) $n$ and $\alpha>0$. The displayed equation for a [scalar field](scalar-field.md) is tensorial; in arbitrary coordinates use the flat [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) in place of ordinary second derivatives. In the rest [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) of $n$ it becomes $-(1+\alpha)\phi_{tt}+\nabla^2\phi=0$, with characteristic [speed](speed.md) $1/\sqrt{1+\alpha}$. Its fixed $n$ selects a preferred frame. A boost that does not preserve $n$ is not a symmetry with this background held fixed, although transforming the components of every quantity gives an equivalent coordinate description. This separates [general covariance](general-covariance.md) from a substantive relativity principle.
