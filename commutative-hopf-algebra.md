# Commutative Hopf algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md)

A [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md) is commutative when its multiplication is commutative. This is independent of whether its coproduct is cocommutative; function algebras of finite nonabelian groups give commutative but noncocommutative examples.
