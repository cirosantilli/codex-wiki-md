# Nuclear emulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Photographic emulsion](photographic-emulsion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_emulsion)

A [nuclear emulsion](nuclear-emulsion.md) is a [photographic emulsion](photographic-emulsion.md) prepared for recording [charged particles](charged-particle.md). The sensitive material serves as both target and record: a particle may slow, stop, decay or cause a [nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md) inside it. Reading that record requires [track reconstruction](track-reconstruction.md), not recognizing a miniature picture of a particle. Unlike a [cloud chamber](cloud-chamber.md) photograph, the exposed [nuclear emulsion](nuclear-emulsion.md) itself retains the record until it is developed and examined.
