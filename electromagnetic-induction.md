# Electromagnetic induction

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_induction)

[Electromagnetic induction](electromagnetic-induction.md) includes an induced [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) associated with a changing [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) or motion of a conductor in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). [Faraday's law of induction](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) and [motional electromotive force](motional-electromotive-force.md) specify the relevant contributions.
