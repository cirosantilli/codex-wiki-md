# Coding sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Gene](gene.md)

A [coding sequence](coding-sequence.md) is the part of a nucleotide sequence that specifies a [polypeptide](polypeptide.md) through the [genetic code](genetic-code.md). In a processed eukaryotic transcript it excludes untranslated regions; a genomic region may require removal of [introns](intron.md) before bacterial expression.
