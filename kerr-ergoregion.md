# Kerr ergoregion

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md)

The Kerr ergoregion is the region where the asymptotically timelike stationary Killing field becomes spacelike. Its outer boundary is $r=M+\sqrt{M^2-a^2\cos^2\theta}$.

The Kerr realization of an [ergosphere](ergosphere.md) lies outside the outer horizon where the stationary Killing vector is spacelike; its outer boundary is $r=M+\sqrt{M^2-a^2\cos^2\theta}$.
