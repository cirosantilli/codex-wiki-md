# Rubidium-87 clock qubit

↑ **Parent:** [Rubidium-87 clock transition](rubidium-87-clock-transition.md)

A [qubit](qubit.md) encoded in the two $m_F=0$ [ground state](ground-state.md) [hyperfine levels](hyperfine-level.md) of [rubidium-87](rubidium-87.md). The electronic $J=1/2$ and [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md) $I=3/2$ combine to $F=1,2$. At zero magnetic field both levels have vanishing linear [Zeeman shift](zeeman-shift.md), suppressing first-order magnetic [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md); the [quadratic Zeeman shift of a clock transition](quadratic-zeeman-shift-of-a-clock-transition.md) remains. Coherent [microwave](microwave.md) [magnetic dipole transitions](magnetic-dipole-transition.md) or [stimulated Raman transitions](stimulated-raman-transition.md) implement single-[qubit](qubit.md) [rotation gates](rotation-gate.md). These are coupled electron-nucleus states, not bare nuclear-spin states.
