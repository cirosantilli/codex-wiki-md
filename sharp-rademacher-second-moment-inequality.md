# Sharp Rademacher second-moment inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Kahane-Khintchine inequality](kahane-khintchine-inequality.md)

For $S=\sum_i a_i\epsilon_i$ and $F=\|S\|$, the [even-function spectral gap on a hypercube](even-function-spectral-gap-on-a-hypercube.md) gives $2\operatorname{Var}(F)\leq-\mathbb E[FLF]$. A norming functional supplied by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) and convexity of the [norm](norm.md) gives $LF\geq-F$, because $LS=-S$. Hence the Dirichlet form is at most $\mathbb EF^2$, proving the inequality. Two equal scalar coefficients attain equality, so the constant is sharp.
