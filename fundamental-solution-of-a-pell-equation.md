# Fundamental solution of a Pell equation

↑ **Parent:** [Pell equation](pell-equation.md)

For $x^2-Dy^2=1$ with nonsquare [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $D$, the solution with $x>1$, $y>0$ and smallest $x$ is the fundamental solution. It generates other positive solutions through powers of $x+y\sqrt D$ and can be found using [continued fraction convergents](continued-fraction-convergent.md) of $\sqrt D$.
