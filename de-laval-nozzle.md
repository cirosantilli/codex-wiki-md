# de Laval nozzle

↑ **Parent:** [Isentropic nozzle flow](isentropic-nozzle-flow.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/de_Laval_nozzle)

A converging-diverging [nozzle](nozzle.md) that can accelerate a [compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md) from subsonic to supersonic speed through a sonic throat. The [nozzle area-velocity relation](nozzle-area-velocity-relation.md) requires acceleration in a converging subsonic section and a diverging supersonic section. Reservoir and downstream [pressures](pressure-split.md) determine whether this [transonic branch](transonic-branch.md) operates; the shape alone does not ensure [choked flow](choked-flow.md).
