# p-adic logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic exponential function](p-adic-exponential-function.md)

The $p$-adic logarithm is the convergent series $\log(1+x)=\sum_{n\geq1}(-1)^{n+1}x^n/n$ on a sufficiently small group of principal units.  
It is locally inverse to the [p-adic exponential function](p-adic-exponential-function.md).
