# Epimorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epimorphism)

An epimorphism $e:A\to B$ is right-cancellative: $fe=ge$ implies $f=g$. In the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md) and in [abelian groups](abelian-group.md), epimorphisms are precisely the surjective homomorphisms.
