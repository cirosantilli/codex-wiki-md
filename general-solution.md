# General solution

↑ **Parent:** [Linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

The general solution of a [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) is a family containing every solution, usually parameterized by arbitrary constants or initial data. For a forced [linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation.md), it is the sum of one [particular solution](particular-solution.md) and the general [homogeneous solution](homogeneous-solution.md).
