# Periodic stability criterion for a conserved-field uniform amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Conserved-field real amplitude equation](conserved-field-real-amplitude-equation.md)

A nonzero uniform [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) of the [conserved-field real amplitude equation](conserved-field-real-amplitude-equation.md) obeys $\mu=R^2-\alpha R$. Fixed zero mean excludes the constant $B$ perturbation. Its homogeneous amplitude [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $-r$. For a nonzero Fourier [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$, the [stability matrix](stability-matrix.md) has [trace](matrix-trace.md) $-r-(1+\sigma)k^2$ and [determinant](determinant.md) $\sigma k^2(k^2+r-2\delta R^2)$. The displayed conditions are necessary and sufficient for strict [linear stability](linear-stability.md) on a period $2L$. In an infinite-domain limit the nonzero-mode condition becomes $r\geq2\delta R^2$; a long-wave instability need not occur in a sufficiently short domain.
