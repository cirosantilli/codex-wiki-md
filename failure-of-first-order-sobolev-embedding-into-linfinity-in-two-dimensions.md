# Failure of first-order Sobolev embedding into Linfinity in two dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev embedding theorem](sobolev-embedding-theorem.md)

On a two-dimensional domain containing the origin, a logarithmic singularity such as $u(r)=(\log(1/r))^\alpha$ with $0<\alpha<1/2$ belongs locally to $H^1$ but is unbounded. Thus the critical first-order [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) does not embed into $L^\infty$.
