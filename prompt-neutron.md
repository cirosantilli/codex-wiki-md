# Prompt neutron

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron](neutron-split.md)

A [neutron](neutron-split.md) released on the short timescale associated with the fission event, rather than after a precursor radioactive transformation. Prompt describes emission timing, rather than the neutron speed after subsequent scattering.
