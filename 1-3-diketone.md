<h1 id="1-3-diketone">1,3-diketone</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ketone](ketone.md)

Two [ketone](ketone.md) [carbonyls](carbonyl-group.md) separated by one [carbon](carbon.md). The intervening C–H bonds are relatively acidic, and the [enol](enol.md) can be stabilized by [pi conjugation](pi-conjugation.md) and an intramolecular [hydrogen bond](hydrogen-bond.md).
