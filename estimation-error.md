# Estimation error

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical estimation](statistical-estimation.md)

An [estimation error](estimation-error.md) is the difference between an [estimator](estimator.md) and the [statistical parameter](statistical-parameter.md) it estimates. It is a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) before observing the sample. Its [mean](expected-value-split.md) is the [bias](bias-of-an-estimator.md) of the [estimator](estimator.md), and its mean square is the sum of the [variance](variance-split.md) and squared [bias](bias-of-an-estimator.md). Standardizing an [estimation error](estimation-error.md) by its [standard error](standard-error.md) often yields an approximate [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md), allowing construction of a [Wald confidence interval](wald-confidence-interval.md).
