# Normal surface

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

Relative to a [triangulation](triangulation.md), a [normal surface](normal-surface.md) meets every [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md) in disjoint normal [disks](disk-mathematics.md): four types of triangles cutting off vertices and three types of quadrilaterals separating opposite pairs of edges. A normal [disk](disk-mathematics.md) meets any edge at most once; its face arcs join distinct edges. Two different quadrilateral types cannot coexist in a [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md).
