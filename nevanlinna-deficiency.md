# Nevanlinna deficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Nevanlinna theory](nevanlinna-theory.md)

For a nonconstant [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md), its deficiency at a point of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) is the displayed asymptotic proportion missing from the full [Nevanlinna integrated counting function](nevanlinna-integrated-counting-function.md). The [Nevanlinna first main theorem](nevanlinna-first-main-theorem.md) bounds $N(r;a)$ by $T_f(r)+O(1)$ and identifies deficiency with the lower limiting normalized proximity. An omitted value has deficiency one.
