# Transcription (biology)

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcription_(biology))

[Transcription](transcription-biology.md) is synthesis of [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) using one [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) strand as a template by an [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md). During elongation the [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md) reads that template $3'\to5'$ while extending [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) $5'\to3'$. Only the selected region is transcribed; other regions need not be active, and the template strand can differ between [genes](gene.md). See [Alberts et al.: RNA polymerase and selection of transcribed DNA regions](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26887/).

For the displayed example, align these three [nucleotide sequences](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) in the same left-to-right positions:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{coding DNA:}\quad&5'\text{-}\mathrm{ATGGCTTAA}\text{-}3',\\
\text{template DNA:}\quad&3'\text{-}\mathrm{TACCGAATT}\text{-}5',\\
\text{RNA product:}\quad&5'\text{-}\mathrm{AUGGCUUAA}\text{-}3'.
\end{aligned}
$$

The first template triplet TAC selects AUG: T pairs with A, A with U, and C with G. The next CGA selects GCU, and ATT selects UAA. The [coding strand](coding-strand.md) therefore matches the [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) lettering except for T versus U, whereas the template is complementary. Reading that template in its own $5'\to3'$ direction gives $5'\text{-}\mathrm{TTAAGCCAT}\text{-}3'$; orientation cannot be discarded. The [gene expression](gene-expression.md) diagram keeps all three rows aligned. See [Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e: template and coding strands in transcription](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/15-2-prokaryotic-transcription).

This correspondence makes [nucleotide sequences](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) interpretable, but replacing T by U is valid only after identifying the [coding strand](coding-strand.md). Moreover, [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) may be processed after [transcription](transcription-biology.md), so the initial product and mature [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) need not have identical uninterrupted [nucleotide sequences](nucleic-acid-sequence.md). [Transcription](transcription-biology.md) also makes functional [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) that will not undergo [biological translation](translation-biology.md). See [Alberts et al.: RNA processing and RNA as the final gene product](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26887/).
