# Einstein-frame metric

↑ **Parent:** [String-frame metric](string-frame-metric.md)

For $D>2$, rescale the [string-frame metric](string-frame-metric.md) by $g^{(E)}=e^{-4\varphi/(D-2)}g^{(s)}$. The [Weyl transformation](weyl-transformation.md) of the [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) makes the coefficient of the Einstein-frame Ricci term independent of the [dilaton](dilaton.md). Indeed $g^{(s)}=e^{4\varphi/(D-2)}g^{(E)}$ contributes factors $e^{2D\varphi/(D-2)}$ from the volume, $e^{-2\varphi}$ from the original coupling and $e^{-4\varphi/(D-2)}$ from the Ricci term; their product is one. Derivative terms supply the transformed dilaton kinetic term. The two-dimensional case is excluded from this particular rescaling.
