# Expectation of a product of independent random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Expected value](expected-value.md)

For independent integrable variables, $\mathbb E\prod_iX_i=\prod_i\mathbb E X_i$. [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) first gives $\mathbb E\prod_i|X_i|=\prod_i\mathbb E|X_i|<\infty$; then [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) factors the signed joint integral.
