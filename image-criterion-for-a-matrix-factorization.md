# Image criterion for a matrix factorization

↑ **Parent:** [System of linear equations](system-of-linear-equations.md)

For [matrices](matrix.md) representing [linear maps](linear-map.md) on finite-dimensional [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md), $AX=B$ has a solution precisely when the [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) represented by $B$ is contained in that represented by $A$. Necessity follows by applying the equation to vectors. For sufficiency choose an $A$-preimage of each column of $B$ and assemble those vectors as the columns of $X$. All solutions are $X_0+N$ with $AN=0$, equivalently every column of $N$ lies in the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) represented by $A$. When $B$ has at least one column, a solution is unique precisely when $A$ has trivial [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md).
