# Nonvanishing of Kirby stabilization factors

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss sums of Jones-Wenzl colors](gauss-sums-of-jones-wenzl-colors.md)

Let $H$ be the real [Hopf pairing of Jones-Wenzl colors](hopf-pairing-of-jones-wenzl-colors.md) [matrix](matrix.md), $d$ the dimension vector and $T$ the diagonal [matrix](matrix.md) of [Jones-Wenzl twist eigenvalues](jones-wenzl-twist-eigenvalue.md). Twist balancing on the [fusion of Jones-Wenzl colors](fusion-of-jones-wenzl-colors.md) gives $HTd=p_+T^{-1}d$, using $\sum_i d_iN_{ij}^k=d_jd_k$. Complex conjugation gives $HT^{-1}d=p_-Td$. Apply $H$ again and use finite sine orthogonality, $H^2=\mathcal D^2I$. Since $Td\ne0$, the displayed identity follows. Thus both factors used to normalize the [Jones polynomial surgery invariant](jones-polynomial-surgery-invariant.md) are nonzero.
