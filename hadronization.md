# Hadronization

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadronization)

[Hadronization](hadronization.md) is the conversion of colored quarks and gluons into color-neutral [hadrons](hadron.md). It affects exclusive final-state distributions even when an inclusive observable can be approximated through a short-distance partonic calculation. A tree-level partonic [decay width](decay-width.md) does not by itself model this conversion.
