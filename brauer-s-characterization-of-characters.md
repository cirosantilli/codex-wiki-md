<h1 id="brauer-s-characterization-of-characters">Brauer's characterization of characters</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Virtual character](virtual-character.md)

A complex [class function](class-function.md) $f$ on a finite group is a [generalized character](virtual-character.md) if and only if $f_E$ is a generalized character of every [elementary subgroup](elementary-group.md). Equivalently, $\langle f_E,\alpha\rangle_E\in\mathbb Z$ for every such $E$ and $\alpha\in\operatorname{Irr}(E)$. Ordinary characters additionally have nonnegative global irreducible multiplicities; integrality of the virtual-character lattice is the conclusion of this criterion.
