# Spatial rotation vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Commutation vector field for the wave equation](commutation-vector-field-for-the-wave-equation.md)

The [vector field](vector-field.md) $\Omega_{ij}=x_i\partial_j-x_j\partial_i$ is an infinitesimal spatial rotation. It commutes with the flat [d'Alembert operator](d-alembert-operator.md) and supplies angular derivatives in the [Klainerman-Sobolev inequality](klainerman-sobolev-inequality.md).
