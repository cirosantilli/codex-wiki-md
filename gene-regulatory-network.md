# Gene regulatory network

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_regulatory_network)

A network describing how gene products affect the production of other gene products. Positive and negative regulatory interactions can generate [multistability](multistability.md), oscillations, and noise-sensitive transitions.
