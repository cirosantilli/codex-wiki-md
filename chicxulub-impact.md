# Chicxulub impact

↑ **Parent:** [Cretaceous–Palaeogene boundary](cretaceous-palaeogene-boundary.md)

The large impact associated with the [Cretaceous–Palaeogene boundary](cretaceous-palaeogene-boundary.md) and its [mass extinction](mass-extinction.md). Atmospheric dust, aerosols and soot can connect an impact to reduced sunlight, suppressed [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md) and disruption of [food webs](food-web.md); these environmental mechanisms distinguish the trigger from its biological effects.
