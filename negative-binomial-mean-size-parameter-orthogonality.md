# Negative binomial mean-size parameter orthogonality

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal statistical parameters](orthogonal-statistical-parameters.md)

For the [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md) with [mean](expected-value.md) $\mu>0$ and size $\theta>0$, the one-observation [score function](informant-function.md) in the mean is $u_\mu=\theta(y-\mu)/[\mu(\mu+\theta)]$. Its size derivative is $(y-\mu)/(\mu+\theta)^2$, whose [expectation](expected-value.md) is zero. Thus the expected [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md) is diagonal in $(\mu,\theta)$. Regular interior [maximum-likelihood estimators](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) have zero asymptotic cross covariance, without necessarily being independent in finite samples.
