# Absolutely continuous probability distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous probability distribution](continuous-probability-distribution-split.md)

An [absolutely continuous probability distribution](absolutely-continuous-probability-distribution.md) on the [real line](real-line.md) has a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $f$ with respect to [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) $\lambda$: for every [Borel set](borel-set.md) $A$, $\mathbb P(X\in A)=\int_A f(x)\,dx$. Its [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is an [absolutely continuous function](absolutely-continuous-function.md), with [derivative](derivative.md) $f$ [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md). The [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) and [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) example under [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) illustrates this class.
