# Gnathostome

↑ **Parent:** [Vertebrate](vertebrate.md)

A member of the jawed-vertebrate lineage. Crown usage includes the ancestor of living cartilaginous and bony [vertebrates](vertebrate.md) and all its descendants; a total-group usage additionally includes jawless stem relatives. The usage must be stated when discussing early [fossils](fossil.md).
