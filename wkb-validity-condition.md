# WKB validity condition

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md)

For $p(x)=\sqrt{2m[E-V(x)]}$ in a [classically allowed region](classically-allowed-region.md), a local validity condition is $|\hbar p^\prime/p^2|\ll1$. The [momentum](momentum-split.md) changes little during a local oscillation. It fails at an ordinary [turning point](turning-point.md) where $p=0$, requiring an [Airy turning-point connection formula](airy-turning-point-connection-formula.md).
