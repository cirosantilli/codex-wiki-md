# Riemann bilinear relations for a compact surface

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md)

For a [compact Riemann surface](compact-riemann-surface.md) with a [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md) $a_i,b_i$ of its first [homology group](homology-group.md), cutting along that basis and integrating a primitive gives

$$
\int_C\omega\wedge\eta=\sum_i\left(\int_{a_i}\omega\int_{b_i}\eta-\int_{b_i}\omega\int_{a_i}\eta\right)
$$

for closed smooth one-forms. For a [holomorphic differential form](holomorphic-differential-form.md) $\omega$ and a [differential of the third kind](differential-of-the-third-kind.md) $\eta$, the corresponding boundary calculation gives

$$
\sum_i\left(\int_{a_i}\omega\int_{b_i}\eta-\int_{b_i}\omega\int_{a_i}\eta\right)=2\pi i\sum_P\operatorname{res}_P(\eta)\int_{P_0}^P\omega.
$$

The paths and representatives of the cycles are chosen in the same cut surface. Normalized [holomorphic differential forms](holomorphic-differential-form.md) consequently have a symmetric period matrix with positive definite imaginary part.
