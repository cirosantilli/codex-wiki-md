# Rubidium-87 ground-state hyperfine structure

↑ **Parent:** [Rubidium-87](rubidium-87.md)

Ground-state electronic [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J=1/2$ couples to nuclear [spin](spin.md) $I=3/2$, giving $F=1,2$. For contact [hyperfine interaction](hyperfine-interaction.md) $A\mathbf I\cdot\mathbf J/\hbar^2$, their zero-field energies are $-5A/4$ and $3A/4$. The splitting is $2A$. Electronic weak-field contributions give $g_{F=1}\simeq-1/2$ and $g_{F=2}\simeq+1/2$; the small nuclear contribution refines these numbers.
