# Teichmuller expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmuller representative](teichmuller-representative.md)

If $K$ is a complete [discretely valued field](discretely-valued-field.md) whose residue field $k$ is a [perfect field](perfect-field.md), and $\pi$ is a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md), the multiplicative Teichmuller lift $[\mathord\cdot]:k\to\mathcal O_K$ gives each $x\in\mathcal O_K$ a unique expansion

$$
x=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}[a_i]\pi^i,
\qquad a_i\in k.
$$
