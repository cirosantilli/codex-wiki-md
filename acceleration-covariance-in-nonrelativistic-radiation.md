# Acceleration covariance in nonrelativistic radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Larmor formula](larmor-formula.md)

For a nonrelativistic point charge, let $C_{ij}=\langle a_i a_j\rangle$ be the time-averaged second moment of [acceleration](acceleration.md), without subtracting its mean. The outgoing [radiation zone](radiation-zone.md) fields satisfy $\mathbf B=\mathbf n\times\mathbf E/c$ and $\mathbf E=q\mathbf n\times(\mathbf n\times\mathbf a)/(4\pi\epsilon_0c^2r)$. Their [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) gives the displayed [power](power.md) per [solid angle](solid-angle.md). Its integral is $\langle P\rangle=q^2\operatorname{tr}C/(6\pi\epsilon_0c^3)$, the averaged [Larmor formula](larmor-formula.md).

For periodic motion the mean [acceleration](acceleration.md) vanishes and $C$ is also its [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md). More generally the second moment, rather than the centered [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md), is the relevant quantity. The angular formula is valid to leading nonrelativistic order and evaluates source motion at [retarded time](retarded-time.md).
