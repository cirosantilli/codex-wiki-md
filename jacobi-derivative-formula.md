# Jacobi derivative formula

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi theta function](jacobi-theta-function.md)

Use period-one spatial normalization and $Q=e^{\pi i\tau}$. The conventional odd function is $\theta_1=-\theta[1/2,1/2]$ with the characteristic convention of [theta function with characteristics](theta-function-with-characteristics.md). Differentiating its [Jacobi triple product](jacobi-triple-product.md) gives $\theta_1'(0)=2\pi Q^{1/4}\prod_{m\geq1}(1-Q^{2m})^3$. The three even constant products have product $2Q^{1/4}\prod_{m\geq1}(1-Q^{2m})^3$, proving the identity. The sign changes if one instead calls the characteristic series itself theta1.
