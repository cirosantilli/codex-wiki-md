# Tangent coordinate transition

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md)

A [coordinate chart](manifold-chart.md) $x$ trivializes the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) by expressing $v\in T_pM$ in the coordinate [basis](basis.md). For $y=f(x)$, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives the displayed transition. Smoothness and fibrewise linear invertibility of the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) construct the natural smooth [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) structure. The chain rule also supplies the transition cocycle.
