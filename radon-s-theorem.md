<h1 id="radon-s-theorem">Radon's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Convex hull](convex-hull.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon's_theorem)

Every [set](set-split.md) of at least $d+2$ points in $\mathbb R^d$ can be partitioned into two nonempty parts with intersecting [convex hulls](convex-hull.md). To see this, take a nonzero [affine dependence](affine-dependence.md) on $d+2$ of its points. Its positive and negative coefficient sums have the same magnitude $L>0$. Dividing by $L$ expresses one point as a [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of the positive-coefficient points and also as a [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of the negative-coefficient points. Distribute zero-coefficient and remaining points arbitrarily between the parts. This theorem obstructs arbitrary labels for [affine linear classifiers](affine-linear-classifier.md).
