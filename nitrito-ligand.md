# Nitrito ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [nitrito ligand](nitrito-ligand.md) is O-bound [nitrite](nitrite.md), $\mathrm{M{-}O{-}N{=}O}$. Its [oxygen](oxygen.md) donor provides [sigma donation](sigma-donation.md) and can provide pi donation; an N-bound [nitro ligand](nitro-ligand.md) is its [linkage isomer](linkage-isomer.md).
