# Survival under a linear force ramp

↑ **Parent:** [Survival probability](survival-probability.md)

For an initially intact link with no rebinding, [survival probability](survival-probability.md) obeys $dp/dt=-k(F)p$. A [force-tilted activation barrier](force-tilted-activation-barrier.md) with [force](force-split.md) $F=ft$, constant $f>0$ and $p(0)=1$ integrates to the displayed law. The rupture-force [probability density](probability-density.md) is $k(F)p(F)/f$, not $p(F)$ itself. The no-rebinding approximation requires separated partners to leave the capture region before appreciable reassociation; it is not guaranteed by the rate equation alone.
