# Semistable holomorphic vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Slope of a holomorphic vector bundle](slope-of-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

A positive-rank [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) on a [compact Riemann surface](compact-riemann-surface.md) is semistable when every nonzero proper [holomorphic subbundle](holomorphic-subbundle.md) has slope at most its own. Equivalently one tests coherent subsheaves: saturation on a smooth curve preserves rank, increases degree, and gives a subbundle with torsion-free quotient. Strict inequality defines stability, which is stronger.
