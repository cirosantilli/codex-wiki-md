# Turbulence closure

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)

A turbulence closure approximates the unclosed higher-order [moments](moment.md) in evolution equations for lower-order turbulent statistics. For example, an equation for the [velocity correlation tensor](velocity-correlation-tensor.md) involves triple correlations, whose evolution involves fourth-order correlations and pressure. A closure must be checked against [conservation laws](conservation-law.md), realizability and boundary conditions; matching an energy spectrum alone does not establish all these properties.
