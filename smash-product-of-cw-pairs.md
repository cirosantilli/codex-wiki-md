# Smash product of CW pairs

↑ **Parent:** [Smash product](smash-product.md)

The quotient of the displayed [CW pair](cw-pair.md) is $(X/A)\wedge(Y/B)$. In a multiplicative [generalized cohomology theory](generalized-cohomology-theory.md), external products of relative classes of degrees $k,m$ take values in degree $k+m$ of this pair. Pullback along a diagonal produces the [relative cup product](relative-cup-product.md).
