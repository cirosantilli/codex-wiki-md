# Derived length

↑ **Parent:** [Derived series](derived-series.md)

For a [solvable group](solvable-group.md), its derived length is the least nonnegative [integer](integer-split.md) $r$ at which the [derived series](derived-series.md) reaches the trivial [group](group-split.md), with $G^{(0)}=G$ and $G^{(r+1)}$ the [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) of $G^{(r)}$. A nontrivial [abelian group](abelian-group.md) has derived length one; a nonabelian [group](group-split.md) with nontrivial abelian [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) has derived length two.
