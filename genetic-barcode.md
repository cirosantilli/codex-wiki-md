# Genetic barcode

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic lineage tracing](genetic-lineage-tracing.md)

An inherited sequence label used to distinguish [cell](biological-cell-split.md) ancestries. Independent acquisition of the same barcode, loss of the readout and nonuniform barcode frequencies can all confound a clonal inference.
