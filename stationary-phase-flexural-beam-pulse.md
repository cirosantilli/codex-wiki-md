# Stationary-phase flexural-beam pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md)

For $\eta_{tt}+\lambda^2\eta_{xxxx}=0$ with Lorentzian displacement $\epsilon a/[\pi(x^2+\epsilon^2)]$ and zero initial velocity, the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) is $ae^{-\epsilon|k|}\cos(\lambda k^2t)$. Along a fixed ray $x=Vt$, the two phases $Vk\pm\lambda k^2$ have saddles $k=\mp V/(2\lambda)$ and curvatures $\pm2\lambda$. Applying [stationary phase](stationary-phase-method.md) and adding the conjugate contributions gives the displayed leading term. The finite pulse width supplies the attenuation factor; it may be dropped only if $\epsilon|V|/\lambda\ll1$. The expansion is an additive $o(t^{-1/2})$ statement, which remains meaningful at zeros of the leading cosine.
