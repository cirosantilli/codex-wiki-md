# Dopamine

↑ **Parent:** [Neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md)

A neurotransmitter involved in multiple neural functions. Some phasic dopamine-neuron responses are consistent with reward-prediction-error signalling, and timed interventions can affect learning. This does not mean that every [dopamine](dopamine.md) [neuron](neuron-split.md) or every timescale carries one identical scalar error.[Abstract and Figures 1–4: associative blocking, extinction and reward-timed dopamine-neuron stimulation](a-causal-link-between-prediction-errors-dopamine-neurons-and-learning-by-elizabeth-e-steinberg-and-others.md)
