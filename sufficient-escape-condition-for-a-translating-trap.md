# Sufficient escape condition for a translating trap

↑ **Parent:** [Passage through a translating optical trap](passage-through-a-translating-optical-trap.md)

In the trap frame, a particle under [linear drag](linear-drag.md) has relative speed $F(y)/\zeta-v_T$. A uniform strictly negative relative speed guarantees finite passage through a [compact support](compact-support.md). For a bounded continuous force, $v_T>\sup F/\zeta$ supplies that condition. For ordinary smooth forces, an encountered zero of the relative speed is an equilibrium or a passage bottleneck, not a finite crossing. Negative forces do not impede forward trap passage.
