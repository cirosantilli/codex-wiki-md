# Chlorophyll

↑ **Parent:** [Light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chlorophyll)

[Chlorophyll](chlorophyll.md) is a family of [molecules](molecule-split.md) that absorb [light](light.md) for [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md). In [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md), [chlorophyll](chlorophyll.md) works with [proteins](protein-split.md) in [thylakoid](thylakoid.md) membranes; absorbing [light](light.md) initiates [energy](energy-split.md) capture rather than directly constructing a [sugar](sugar.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 8.2: photosynthetic pigments](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/8-2-the-light-dependent-reactions-of-photosynthesis).
