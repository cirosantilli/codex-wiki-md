# Moreau envelope of the Euclidean norm

↑ **Parent:** [Moreau envelope](moreau-envelope.md)

For $r=\|x\|_2$, the [Moreau envelope](moreau-envelope.md) of the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) is $r^2/(2\tau)$ when $r\leq\tau$ and $r-\tau/2$ otherwise. Its gradient is $x/\tau$ in the core and $x/r$ outside. The approximation error is between zero and $\tau/2$.
