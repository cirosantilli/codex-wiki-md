# Harmonic analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic_analysis)

Harmonic analysis decomposes functions into elementary oscillations, generalizing [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) and the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md). On a locally compact Abelian group the elementary oscillations are [continuous unitary characters](continuous-unitary-character.md), and [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) supplies translation-invariant integration.
