# Accidental diffraction extinction from equal atomic scattering factors

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal basis structure factor](crystal-basis-structure-factor.md)

A vanishing [crystal basis structure factor](crystal-basis-structure-factor.md) can depend on approximate equality of [atomic scattering factors](atomic-scattering-factor.md), rather than on an exact [space group](space-group.md) condition. For two inequivalent site classes, an amplitude $F=C(f_A-f_B)$ vanishes when $f_A=f_B$ but becomes weakly observable if bonding [electron densities](electron-density.md) or thermal displacements differ. Chemical identity alone does not imply identical local [atomic scattering factors](atomic-scattering-factor.md). The [diffraction intensity](diffraction-intensity.md) is quadratic in the small difference.
