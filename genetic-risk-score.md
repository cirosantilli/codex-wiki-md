# Genetic risk score

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

A genetic risk score combines allele counts from several [genetic variants](genetic-variant.md), usually using specified weights. In [Mendelian randomization](mendelian-randomization.md) it can act as a combined [instrumental variable](instrumental-variable.md) for an [exposure](exposure.md); interpretation still requires [instrument relevance](instrument-relevance.md), [instrumental-variable independence](instrumental-variable-independence.md), and the [exclusion restriction](exclusion-restriction.md).
