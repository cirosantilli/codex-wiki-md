# Spectral-radius three-circle inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral radius](spectral-radius.md)

For a holomorphic [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)-valued function, put $M_r(f)=\sup_{|z|=r}r(f(z))$. Apply the [Hadamard three-circle theorem](hadamard-three-circle-theorem.md) to the holomorphic powers $f^{2^n}$. The continuous functions $\|f(z)^{2^n}\|^{1/2^n}$ decrease to the [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md). [Supremum convergence for decreasing continuous functions](supremum-convergence-for-decreasing-continuous-functions.md) passes the boundary suprema to the limit, proving the inequality with the same logarithmic interpolation parameter. In particular, at the geometric-mean radius, the square of the middle spectral radius is at most the product of the two boundary suprema.
