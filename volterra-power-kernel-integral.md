# Volterra power-kernel integral

↑ **Parent:** [Volterra operator](volterra-operator.md)

For nonnegative [Lebesgue measurable](lebesgue-measurable-set.md) $f$ on $(0,1)$, the displayed [Volterra power-kernel integral](volterra-power-kernel-integral.md) is defined for every $0<x<1$ as a nonnegative extended [Lebesgue integral](lebesgue-integral.md). The factor is finite and continuous on the open integration [interval](interval-mathematics.md) $(0,x)$, and an endpoint value can be assigned arbitrarily. The result may initially be infinite; for example $f(y)=1/y$ makes every value infinite. At $\beta=1$, it reduces to the ordinary [Volterra operator](volterra-operator.md).

For joint [integration](integral-split.md), use the [integral kernel](integral-kernel.md) $(x-y)^{\beta-1}$ on $0<y<x<1$ and zero elsewhere. This piecewise prescription avoids the singular diagonal and never raises a negative number to a noninteger power. The [L1 bound for a Volterra power-kernel integral](l1-bound-for-a-volterra-power-kernel-integral.md) gives an integrable output, finite [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md), whenever $f$ is integrable.
