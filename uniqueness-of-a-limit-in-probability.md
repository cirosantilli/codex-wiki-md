# Uniqueness of a limit in probability

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence in probability](convergence-in-probability.md)

If $X_n$ converges in probability to both $X$ and $Y$, then $X=Y$ almost surely. Indeed, the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) and a [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) show that $\mathbb P(|X-Y|>\varepsilon)=0$ for every $\varepsilon>0$.
