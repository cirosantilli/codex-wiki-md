# Thermal effusivity

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_effusivity)

A measure of how rapidly a material exchanges [heat](heat-split.md) with another body in transient contact. Here k is [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md), $\rho$ is [mass density](density.md) and $c_p$ is specific [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). Perceived coldness at equal actual [temperature](temperature-split.md) can depend on this contact heat transfer rather than on an electronic [Fermi temperature](fermi-temperature.md).
