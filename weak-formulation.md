# Weak formulation

↑ **Parent:** [Weak solution](weak-solution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_formulation)

A weak formulation multiplies a differential equation by a [test function](test-function.md), integrates, and uses [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) to move derivatives away from the unknown function. Natural boundary conditions appear in the resulting boundary terms.
