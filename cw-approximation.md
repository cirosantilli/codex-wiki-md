# CW approximation

↑ **Parent:** [CW complex](cw-complex.md)

A CW approximation of a space $X$ is a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) $X_{\mathrm{cw}}$ with a [weak homotopy equivalence](weak-homotopy-equivalence.md) $X_{\mathrm{cw}}\to X$. The realization of the [singular simplicial set](singular-simplicial-set.md) of $X$ supplies one functorially.
