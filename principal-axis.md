# Principal axis

↑ **Parent:** [Principal body frame](principal-body-frame.md)

A principal axis is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) direction of the [inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md). Choosing three orthogonal principal axes diagonalizes the inertia tensor.
