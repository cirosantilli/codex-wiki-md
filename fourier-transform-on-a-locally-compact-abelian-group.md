# Fourier transform on a locally compact abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md)

For $f\in L^1(G)$ the displayed integral defines a bounded continuous function on the [Pontryagin dual group](pontryagin-dual-group.md). It takes [convolution](convolution.md) to pointwise multiplication and left translation $T_x$ to multiplication by $\overline{\chi(x)}$. The transform depends on the scale of [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) on $G$; the compatible [dual Haar measure](dual-haar-measure.md) scales reciprocally.
