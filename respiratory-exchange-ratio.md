# Respiratory exchange ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Respirometry](respirometry.md)

The ratio of outward [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) transfer to inward [oxygen](oxygen.md) transfer measured at the mouth. It can differ from the cellular [respiratory quotient](respiratory-quotient.md) during transient changes in body gas stores.
