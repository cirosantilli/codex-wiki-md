# Orthogonal completion of mixed neutral lepton kinetic terms

↑ **Parent:** [Charged current](charged-current.md)

If a [chiral fermion](weyl-spinor.md) triplet uses one normalized linear combination of two neutral [leptons](lepton.md), adding the perpendicular combination as a [gauge singlet](gauge-singlet.md) completes their canonical [kinetic terms](kinetic-term.md). The two combinations are related to the original fields by a real [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md). Hence their summed kinetic quadratic form is exactly that of the two original fields, with no mixed terms. The singlet carries the same chirality; no right-handed component is created for a purely left-handed massless [neutrino](neutrino.md).
