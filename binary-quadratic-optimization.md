# Binary quadratic optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic optimization](quadratic-optimization.md)

Optimization of a [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) over binary choices, here using signs $x_i\in\{-1,1\}$. Lifting $xx^T$ turns the objective into a [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md); dropping the rank-one condition gives a [semidefinite relaxation of binary quadratic optimization](semidefinite-relaxation-of-binary-quadratic-optimization.md).
