# Ion milling

↑ **Parent:** [Sputtering](sputtering.md)

Removal of material by bombardment with energetic ions. Directional [sputtering](sputtering.md) can transfer a mask pattern with less undercut than an isotropic wet etch; mask erosion, redeposition and surface damage remain possible.
