# Dark radiation on a cosmological brane

↑ **Parent:** [High-energy braneworld expansion](high-energy-braneworld-expansion.md)

Projected bulk [Weyl tensor](weyl-tensor.md) can contribute a radiation-scaling term to the brane [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md), even without ordinary brane photons. For an anti-de Sitter black-hole bulk the integration constant is related to its mass. This [dark radiation on a cosmological brane](dark-radiation-on-a-cosmological-brane.md) changes the expansion rate tested by [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) and [Cosmic microwave background anisotropy](cosmic-microwave-background-anisotropy-split.md); it should not automatically be identified with a freely specifiable thermal particle species.
