# Actomyosin contractility

↑ **Parent:** [Cytoskeleton](cytoskeleton.md)

[actomyosin contractility](actomyosin-contractility.md) generates force through interactions of [myosin](myosin.md) motors with [actin](actin.md) networks. The resulting load depends on network organization, attachments and resistance from the surroundings.
