# Unramified Iwasawa torsion theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Unramified Iwasawa module](unramified-iwasawa-module.md)

The [unramified Iwasawa module](unramified-iwasawa-module.md) is finitely generated torsion over the [Iwasawa algebra of a Zp-extension](iwasawa-algebra-of-a-zp-extension.md), for every [Zp-extension](zp-extension.md) of a [number field](number-field.md). After a finite shift all ramified primes are totally ramified and their number $s$ is constant. [Class field theory](class-field-theory.md) bounds finite-layer [coinvariant modules](coinvariant-module.md) by $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z_p}Y_{\Gamma_n}\leq s-1$. The [Compact Nakayama lemma](compact-nakayama-lemma.md) proves finite generation, and a positive [Iwasawa-module rank](iwasawa-module-rank.md) would force ranks at least $r p^n$, a contradiction. No [Leopoldt conjecture](leopoldt-conjecture.md) is required.
