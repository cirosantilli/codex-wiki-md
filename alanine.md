# Alanine

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid](amino-acid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alanine)

Alanine is an [amino acid](amino-acid.md) represented by Ala or A in [protein](protein.md) sequence notation. GCU is one of its [codons](codon.md) in the standard [genetic code](genetic-code.md), and supplies the second residue in the worked [gene expression](gene-expression.md) example. [OpenStax Biology 2e, 15.1: alanine codons](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/15-1-the-genetic-code)
