# Alanine

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid](amino-acid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alanine)

Alanine is an [amino acid](amino-acid.md) represented by Ala or A in [protein](protein-split.md) sequence notation. GCU is one of its [codons](codon.md) in the standard [genetic code](genetic-code.md), and supplies the second residue in the worked [gene expression](gene-expression.md) example.
