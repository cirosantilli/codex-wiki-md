# Partial inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product](tensor-product.md)

A [partial inner product](partial-inner-product.md) contracts one factor of a bipartite state against a fixed bra, leaving a [vector](vector.md) in the other factor. For $|\Psi\rangle=\sum_{i,j}c_{ij}|i\rangle\otimes|j\rangle$ and $|a\rangle=\sum_i a_i|i\rangle$, the result is $\sum_{i,j}\overline{a_i}c_{ij}|j\rangle$. It is linear in $|\Psi\rangle$ and conjugate-linear in $|a\rangle$. For a normalized rank-one projective outcome on the first factor, its squared [norm](norm.md) is the outcome [probability](probability.md); divide by that [norm](norm.md) to obtain the conditional [pure state](pure-state.md) when it is nonzero.
