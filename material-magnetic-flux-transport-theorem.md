# Material magnetic-flux transport theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md)

Combine the [material surface element evolution](material-surface-element-evolution.md) law with the [product rule](product-rule.md) for $\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf S$. The result is

$$
\frac d{dt}\int_{S(t)}\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf S
=\int_{S(t)}\left[\frac{D\mathbf B}{Dt}+\mathbf B\nabla\cdot\mathbf u-(\mathbf B\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u\right]\cdot d\mathbf S.
$$

Expanding the [curl](curl.md) gives the equivalent title formula. The [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md) and [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) make the integrand zero and prove [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md), without requiring [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md).
