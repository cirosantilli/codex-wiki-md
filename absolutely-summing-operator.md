# Absolutely summing operator

↑ **Parent:** [Absolutely p-summing operator](absolutely-p-summing-operator.md)

An absolutely summing operator is an [absolutely p-summing operator](absolutely-p-summing-operator.md) with $p=1$. The identity on the infinite-dimensional [l2 sequence space](l2-sequence-space.md) is not absolutely summing: the first $n$ coordinate vectors have sum of norms $n$, while their weak 1-norm is $\sqrt n$.
