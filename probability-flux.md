# Probability flux

↑ **Parent:** [Probability density](probability-density.md)

A current in a [probability continuity equation](probability-continuity-equation.md). For overdamped drift-diffusion, $\mathbf J=\mathbf v p-D\nabla p$. This transport definition does not require the quantum-mechanical [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) current formula.
