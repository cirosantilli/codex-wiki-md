# Molar-mass dispersity

↑ **Parent:** [Polydispersity](polydispersity.md)

The [weight-average molar mass](weight-average-molar-mass.md) divided by the [number-average molar mass](number-average-molar-mass.md). Its conventional written symbol is Đ. With number weighting it equals $1+\operatorname{Var}(M)/\langle M\rangle^2$, so it is at least one and equals one only for a uniform [molar mass](molar-mass.md). It measures one aspect of distribution breadth, not the full shape of a [molar mass](molar-mass.md) distribution.
