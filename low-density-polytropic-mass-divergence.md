# Low-density polytropic mass divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar polytrope](stellar-polytrope.md)

For $p=K\rho^{1+1/n}$, the [Lane-Emden mass formula](lane-emden-mass-formula.md) gives $M\propto\rho_c^{(3-n)/(2n)}$ at fixed $K$, while $M/R\propto\rho_c^{1/n}$. For $3<n<5$, the [Lane-Emden equation](lane-emden-equation.md) has a finite-radius solution, yet $M\to\infty$ as $\rho_c\to0$. The relativistic [Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equation](tolman-oppenheimer-volkoff-equation.md) approaches this limit since $p_c/\rho_c\to0$. Monotone pressure and density alone therefore do not impose a finite stellar maximum mass.
