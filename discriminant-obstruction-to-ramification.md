# Discriminant obstruction to ramification

↑ **Parent:** [Field discriminant](field-discriminant.md)

A ramified prime makes the finite algebra $\mathcal O_K/p\mathcal O_K$ have a nonzero [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md). Multiplication by its product with any other element is nilpotent and has trace zero, so the reduced [trace pairing](trace-pairing.md) is degenerate. Therefore the [field discriminant](field-discriminant.md) is divisible by $p$. This proves finiteness of the ramified rational primes without requiring explicit prime factorization in the field.
