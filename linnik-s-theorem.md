<h1 id="linnik-s-theorem">Linnik's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic number theory](analytic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linnik's_theorem)

There is an absolute finite exponent $L$ bounding the least [prime](prime-number.md) in any reduced arithmetic progression by a constant times $q^L$. The [multiplicative large sieve inequality](character-large-sieve.md) is one of the tools in proofs of this theorem.
