# Difference of independent Poisson processes

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md)

For independent [Poisson processes](poisson-process.md) with rates $r_+,r_-\geq0$, their difference is a [Lévy process](levy-process.md) with [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) $\exp(t[r_+(e^{iu}-1)+r_-(e^{-iu}-1)])$. When the combined rate is positive, its jumps occur at rate $r_++r_-$, with direction probabilities proportional to the two rates. Equal unit rates give $\exp(2t(\cos u-1))$ and a rate-two [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md) with [Rademacher distribution](rademacher-distribution.md) jumps. Its paths are integer-valued, [càdlàg](cadlag.md), and of [finite variation](total-variation-of-a-function.md) on compact intervals.
