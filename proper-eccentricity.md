# Proper eccentricity

↑ **Parent:** [Secular perturbation](secular-perturbation.md)

The proper eccentricity is the magnitude of the homogeneous part of the [complex eccentricity](complex-eccentricity.md) in linear [secular perturbation](secular-perturbation.md) theory. It remains constant in conservative theory, while its [proper longitude of periapsis](proper-longitude-of-periapsis.md) advances at the free precession frequency.
