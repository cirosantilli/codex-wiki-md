# Degree matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Laplacian matrix](laplacian-matrix.md)

The [degree matrix](degree-matrix.md) is the [diagonal matrix](diagonal-matrix.md) whose $i$th diagonal entry is the [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md) $i$. For a finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md), $d_i=\sum_jA_{ij}$, where $A$ is its [adjacency matrix](adjacency-matrix.md). For a [Weighted graph Laplacian](weighted-graph-laplacian.md), use the sum of incident [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) weights instead of the number of incident [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md).
