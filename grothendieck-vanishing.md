# Grothendieck vanishing

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md)

For a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) on a [Noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md), its [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) vanishes in degrees greater than its support dimension. In particular, a zero-dimensional projective scheme has no positive-degree coherent cohomology.
