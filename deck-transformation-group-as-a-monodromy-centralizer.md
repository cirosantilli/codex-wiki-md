# Deck transformation group as a monodromy centralizer

↑ **Parent:** [Deck transformation group](deck-transformation-group.md)

For a connected covering, restriction to one fibre identifies the [deck transformation group](deck-transformation-group.md) with the centralizer in the fibre's symmetric group of the [monodromy action of a covering space](monodromy-action-of-a-covering-space.md). A deck transformation commutes with every lifted loop, and every commuting fibre permutation extends uniquely over the covering by path lifting.
