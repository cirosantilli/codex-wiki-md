# Diabatic heating

↑ **Parent:** [First law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md)

Heat addition changes parcel entropy and departs from an [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md). In an atmosphere, radiation, latent heating and turbulent heat transfer are examples. Spatially varying dust absorption can supply a thermal forcing for atmospheric waves; the heating distribution must project onto the wave's spatial structure to excite it efficiently.
