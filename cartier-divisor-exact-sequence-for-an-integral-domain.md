# Cartier divisor exact sequence for an integral domain

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

For an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $A$ with [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) $K$, realize [Cartier divisors](cartier-divisor-split.md) as [invertible fractional ideals](invertible-fractional-ideal.md), with local divisor equation $f$ corresponding to the ideal $fA$. This is the $\mathcal O(-D)$ convention. The maps in the displayed [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) are unit inclusion, $f\mapsto fA$, and $I\mapsto[I]$. A principal fractional ideal is trivial as a divisor exactly when its generator is a unit. An invertible fractional ideal is trivial in the [Picard group of a ring](picard-group-of-a-ring.md) exactly when it is principal. Every rank-one projective module embeds in $K$ and produces an invertible fractional ideal, proving surjectivity. Local multiplication identifies $I\otimes_AJ$ with $IJ$, so these are group homomorphisms.
