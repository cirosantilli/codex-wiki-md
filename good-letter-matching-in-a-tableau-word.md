# Good-letter matching in a tableau word

↑ **Parent:** [Pair of partitions for a generalized Specht module](pair-of-partitions-for-a-generalized-specht-module.md)

Read a word of type $b$ from the left. Every $1$ is good; an $i+1$ is good when the number of previous good $i$'s exceeds the number of previous good $(i+1)$'s. The set $s(a,b)$ contains words with at least $a_i$ good $i$'s for each $i$. Their matched chains yield independent marked-column [polytabloids](polytabloid.md); column antisymmetrization and the add-or-raise recursion give $\dim S^{a,b}=|s(a,b)|$ over every [field](field.md).
