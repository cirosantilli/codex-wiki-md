# Van der Waals critical point

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md)

The [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md) of the [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md) occurs where its two subcritical stationary points merge into a horizontal [inflection point](inflection-point.md). The conditions are $(\partial p/\partial v)_T=(\partial^2p/\partial v^2)_T=0$. The short calculation is

$$
-\frac{k_BT_c}{(v_c-b)^2}+\frac{2a}{v_c^3}=0,\qquad
\frac{2k_BT_c}{(v_c-b)^3}-\frac{6a}{v_c^4}=0.
$$

Dividing these relations gives $2/(v_c-b)=3/v_c$, hence

$$
v_c=3b,\qquad T_c=\frac{8a}{27bk_B},\qquad p_c=\frac{a}{27b^2}.
$$

Substitution gives the [Van der Waals law of corresponding states](van-der-waals-law-of-corresponding-states.md) and the parameter-independent ratio $p_cv_c/(k_BT_c)=3/8$. That ratio belongs to this [equation of state](equation-of-state.md); reducing a real [fluid](fluid.md)'s variables does not enforce it.[§5.1.3, equations 5.7–5.8: critical values, reduced equation and compressibility ratio](statistical-physics-by-david-tong.md)

An exact rearrangement of the reduced critical [Van der Waals isotherm](van-der-waals-isotherm.md) makes its flatness visible:

$$
q(x,1)-1=-\frac{3(x-1)^3}{x^2(3x-1)}.
$$

The first nonzero departure near $x=1$ is $-3(x-1)^3/2$. On the critical particle [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) approached from $t>1$, $(\partial q/\partial x)_{x=1}=-6(t-1)$ and

$$
\kappa_T=\frac{1}{6p_c(t-1)}.
$$

The divergent [isothermal compressibility](isothermal-compressibility.md) and cubic flattening explain why the critical region is mechanically soft. These are [mean-field theory](mean-field-theory.md) predictions; [thermal fluctuations](thermal-fluctuation.md) limit their use for real [fluids](fluid.md) very close to a [thermodynamic critical point](thermodynamic-critical-point.md).
