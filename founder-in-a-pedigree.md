# Founder in a pedigree

↑ **Parent:** [Pedigree](pedigree.md)

A founder is an individual whose parents are outside the recorded [pedigree](pedigree.md). The standard [pedigree likelihood](pedigree-likelihood.md) assigns unrelated founders independent genotype distributions based on population allele or haplotype frequencies. If founders are related, their joint prior must instead account for that relationship.
