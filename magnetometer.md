# Magnetometer

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetism](magnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetometer)

A [magnetometer](magnetometer.md) measures a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) or a specimen's [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). A [vibrating-sample magnetometer](vibrating-sample-magnetometer.md) determines the latter from the [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) induced in pickup coils by specimen motion.
