# Product of commuting elements of coprime order

↑ **Parent:** [Order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md)

Let commuting elements $a,b$ have finite coprime orders $m,n$. Their [cyclic subgroups](cyclic-subgroup.md) have trivial intersection by [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md). If $(ab)^k=1$, then $a^k=b^{-k}$ belongs to this intersection, so $m\mid k$ and $n\mid k$. Thus $mn\mid k$. Conversely, $(ab)^{mn}=1$ because the elements commute. This proves the displayed order formula.
