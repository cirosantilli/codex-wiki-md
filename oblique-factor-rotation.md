# Oblique factor rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Factor rotation](factor-rotation.md)

A representation of a factor solution allowing correlated [latent factors](latent-factor.md). If $X=LF+\epsilon$ and $H$ is invertible, replacing $L$ by $LH$ and $F$ by $H^{-1}F$ preserves the shared covariance when the factor [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) changes accordingly. Report factor correlations as well as loadings.
