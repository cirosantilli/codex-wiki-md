<h1 id="fano-s-inequality">Fano's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional entropy](conditional-entropy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fano's_inequality)

Let $X$ take values in an alphabet of size $m$, let $\widehat X$ be an estimate determined by $Y$, and put $p_e=\mathbb P(\widehat X\ne X)$. Then

$$
H(X\mid Y)\leq h_2(p_e)+p_e\log_2(m-1),
$$

where $h_2$ is the [binary entropy function](binary-entropy-function.md). The error indicator costs at most $h_2(p_e)$ bits, and after an error there are at most $m-1$ possible values of $X$.
