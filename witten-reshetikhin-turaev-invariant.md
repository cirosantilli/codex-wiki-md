# Witten-Reshetikhin-Turaev invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Topological quantum field theory](topological-quantum-field-theory.md)

A [topological quantum field theory](topological-quantum-field-theory.md) [TQFT partition function](topological-quantum-field-theory-partition-function.md) for a closed oriented [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) presented by [Dehn surgery on a framed link](dehn-surgery-on-a-framed-link.md). With [normalized Kirby color](normalized-kirby-color.md) on each surgery component and the convention $Z(S^3)=1/\mathcal D$, it is $\mathcal D^{-1}e^{-2\pi i c\sigma/8}$ times the colored [link](link.md) evaluation. Here $\sigma$ is the [signature](signature-of-a-quadratic-form.md) of the [linking matrix](linking-matrix.md) and $c$ is the [chiral central charge](chiral-central-charge.md).
