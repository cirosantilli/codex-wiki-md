# Fixation probability

↑ **Parent:** [Allele fixation](allele-fixation.md)

The probability that an [allele](allele.md) eventually occupies every copy at the specified [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) under a population model. For a rare favorable variant in a suitable large-population, small-effect branching approximation, $P_{\mathrm{fix}}\approx2s$; inheritance, reproduction and competition assumptions matter.
