# Volumetric heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volumetric_heat_capacity)

Volumetric heat capacity is [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) per unit [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md), with units $\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}\,K^{-1}}$. For homogeneous material of [mass density](density.md) $\rho=m/V$, it equals $\rho c$, using [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md) $c$ for the same constraint. It measures the [heat](heat.md) required per small [temperature](temperature.md) increase for a chosen occupied [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). In a material model using [specific heat capacity at constant pressure](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md), [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md) is $\alpha=k/(\rho c_p)$, where $k$ is [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md).
