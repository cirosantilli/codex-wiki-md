# Troposphere

↑ **Parent:** [Terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troposphere)

The troposphere is the lowest main layer of the [terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md), with weather and generally decreasing [temperature](temperature-split.md) with altitude. Local inversions and moist convection complicate a single lapse-rate description.
