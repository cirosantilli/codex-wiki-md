# Promotive factor

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological resilience](psychological-resilience.md)

A resource associated with more favorable functioning across the [exposure](exposure.md) levels under study. This describes a general favorable association, distinguished here from an [exposure](exposure.md)-dependent [stress-buffering interaction](stress-buffering-interaction.md). Terminology varies across the literature, so a study should specify its statistical and causal meaning.
