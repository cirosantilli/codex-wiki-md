# Locally Cartesian closed category

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian closed category](cartesian-closed-category.md)

A category with finite limits is locally Cartesian closed when every [slice category](slice-category.md) is a [Cartesian closed category](cartesian-closed-category.md). Covariant set-valued functor categories on small categories have this property, using the equivalence between their slices and functor categories on categories of elements.
