<h1 id="moser-s-trick">Moser's trick</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moser's_trick)

Let $M$ be compact and let $\omega_t$ be a smooth family of symplectic forms with constant [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) class. Choose $\sigma_t$ smoothly so that $\dot\omega_t=d\sigma_t$, solve

$$
\iota_{X_t}\omega_t=-\sigma_t,
$$

and let $\phi_t$ be the flow of $X_t$. Then

$$
\frac d{dt}(\phi_t^*\omega_t)
=\phi_t^*(\dot\omega_t+\mathcal L_{X_t}\omega_t)=0,
$$

so $\phi_t^*\omega_t=\omega_0$.
