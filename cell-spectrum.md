# Cell spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (topology)](spectrum-topology.md)

A [cell spectrum](cell-spectrum.md) is obtained by attaching stable cells, with successive [cofibers of spectra](cofiber-of-spectra.md) wedges of [suspensions of spectra](suspension-of-spectra.md) of the [sphere spectrum](sphere-spectrum.md). Cellular models provide the cofibrant objects needed to compute derived [maps of topological spectra](map-of-topological-spectra.md). Stable cells may occur in arbitrary integer degrees.
