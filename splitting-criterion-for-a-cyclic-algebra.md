# Splitting criterion for a cyclic algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic algebra](cyclic-algebra.md)

If $a=N_{L/K}c$, let $L$ act on itself by multiplication and let the extra generator act by $c\sigma$. Its $n$th power is multiplication by $a$, and these operators give the [cyclic algebra](cyclic-algebra.md) as $\operatorname{End}_K(L)$. Conversely, if the algebra is a [matrix algebra](matrix-algebra.md) on an $n$-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), its embedded degree-$n$ field $L$ makes that space one-dimensional over $L$. The extra generator is then $c\sigma$ in an $L$-basis, so its $n$th power forces $a=Nc$.
