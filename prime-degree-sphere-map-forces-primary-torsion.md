# Prime-degree sphere map forces primary torsion

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md)

If $S^n\to N$ has prime degree $p$ and $N$ is a closed connected oriented $n$-manifold, every [integral homology](integral-homology.md) group of $N$ in degrees strictly between zero and $n$ is a finite $p$-primary [abelian group](abelian-group.md). Consequently one power of $p$ annihilates all these groups. The proof combines [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) over each field $\mathbb F_q$, $q\ne p$, with the [universal coefficient theorem for homology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-homology.md) and finite generation.
