# Capacitance measurement calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric capacitance measurement](dielectric-capacitance-measurement.md)

Characterize the instrument and fixture before inferring [permittivity](permittivity-split.md): use the prescribed open and short measurements to compensate residual parallel response and series [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md), and check a known reference [capacitor](capacitor.md) or the required load standard. Keep cables and electrode geometry reproducible. An open fixture measurement is distinct from the empty-gap reference [capacitor](capacitor.md); blindly subtracting its entire [capacitance](capacitance.md) would remove the desired reference signal. Compensation cannot determine an incorrectly measured specimen thickness or eliminate all specimen-dependent [fringing field of a capacitor](fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) errors.
