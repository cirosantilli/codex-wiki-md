# Place value

↑ **Parent:** [Positional notation](positional-notation.md)

The [place value](place-value.md) is the weight of a position in [positional notation](positional-notation.md). A [numeral digit](numerical-digit.md) $d_j$ at that position contributes $d_jb^j$ to the represented [number](number.md).
