# Chemoreceptor

↑ **Parent:** [Physiology](physiology-split.md)

A [chemoreceptor](chemoreceptor.md) is a sensory cell or receptor system responding to chemical composition. In respiratory regulation, carbon-dioxide-related chemical signals contribute to feedback controlling [ventilation](pulmonary-ventilation.md). Blood transport and neural response introduce delays, so a lumped [ventilation](pulmonary-ventilation.md) model may respond to past rather than present [carbon-dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md).
