# Radius of convergence of the p-adic exponential

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic exponential function](p-adic-exponential-function.md)

On $\mathbb C_p$, the power series $\exp(x)=\sum_{n\geq0}x^n/n!$ has radius of convergence

$$
p^{-1/(p-1)}.
$$

Indeed, [Legendre formula](legendre-s-formula.md) gives $v_p(n!)/n\to1/(p-1)$, and the [Cauchy-Hadamard theorem](cauchy-hadamard-theorem.md) gives the stated radius.
