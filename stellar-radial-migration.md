# Stellar radial migration

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

A change in a star's mean orbital radius through exchange of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), particularly at a [corotation resonance](corotation-resonance.md) with a rotating perturbation. For the $k_R=0$ harmonic in [action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md), the leading change of [radial action](radial-action.md) vanishes even while azimuthal [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) changes. Migration can therefore rearrange a [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md) with much less heating than exchange at a [Lindblad resonance](lindblad-resonance.md).
