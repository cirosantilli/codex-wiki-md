# Magnetic moment inferred from an entropy derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell relations](maxwell-relations.md)

With magnetic [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) convention $dF=-S\,dT-m\,dB$, a measured $(\partial S/\partial B)_T=-\alpha B/T^2$ fixes $(\partial m/\partial T)_B$ to the same value. Integrating determines the displayed inverse-temperature term but leaves a temperature-independent function $m_0(B)$. An independent constitutive or boundary condition is needed to fix it. Requiring zero moment at infinite [temperature](temperature-split.md) recovers [Curie's law](curie-s-law.md) without that offset.
