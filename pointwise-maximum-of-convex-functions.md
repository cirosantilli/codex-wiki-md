# Pointwise maximum of convex functions

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

The pointwise maximum of finitely many convex functions is convex because

$$
\max_i f_i(tx+(1-t)y)
\leq t\max_i f_i(x)+(1-t)\max_i f_i(y).
$$
