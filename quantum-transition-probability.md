# Quantum transition probability

↑ **Parent:** [Born rule](born-rule.md)

For a prepared normalized [pure state](pure-state.md) $|i\rangle$ and a final [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) onto the normalized state $|f\rangle$, [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md) gives the displayed [probability](probability.md), by the [Born rule](born-rule.md). This depends on the preparation, final measurement, and [time-evolution operator](time-evolution-operator.md); it does not assume a classical [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md) of transitions.
