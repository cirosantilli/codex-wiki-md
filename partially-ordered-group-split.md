# Partially ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partially_ordered_group)

A [group](group-split.md) with a [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md) preserved by multiplication on both sides: $a\le b$ implies $xay\le xby$ for all $x,y$. In additive notation this says $a\le b\Rightarrow x+a+y\le x+b+y$. Preserving only one side is a different orderability condition.
