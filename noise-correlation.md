# Noise correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Population coding](population-coding.md)

Statistical dependence between neural responses remaining after the modeled task-related mean has been removed. Residual dependence can reflect shared state or omitted task variables as well as biological noise, so its interpretation depends on the conditioning model.
