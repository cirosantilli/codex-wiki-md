# Orthogonal dynamical symmetry in quantum control

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical Lie algebra](dynamical-lie-algebra.md)

Let $J$ be a symmetric unitary matrix and every skew-Hermitian control generator obey $x^TJ+Jx=0$. The condition is closed under real linear combinations and [commutators](commutator.md), and evolution preserves $U^TJU=J$. In a unitary basis where $J$ becomes the identity, the generators are real antisymmetric, so the [Dynamical Lie algebra](dynamical-lie-algebra.md) is contained in $\mathfrak{so}(N)$. The state quantity $|\psi^TJ\psi|$ is invariant under the reachable evolution and insensitive to global phase, obstructing unrestricted [pure-state controllability](pure-state-controllability.md). This is a bilinear-form symmetry; it does not assert that $J$ commutes with the Hamiltonians.
