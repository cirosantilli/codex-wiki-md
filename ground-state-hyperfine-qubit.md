# Ground-state hyperfine qubit

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperfine level](hyperfine-level.md)

A [qubit](qubit.md) encoded in two resolved [hyperfine levels](hyperfine-level.md) of the same electronic [ground state](ground-state.md). Such states avoid optical excited-state decay and can support coherent radiofrequency or microwave control through a [magnetic dipole transition](magnetic-dipole-transition.md). Technical magnetic noise and differential shifts can still dephase them; first-order field insensitivity requires additional choices of states and operating point.
