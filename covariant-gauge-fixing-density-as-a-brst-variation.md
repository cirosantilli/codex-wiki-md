# Covariant gauge-fixing density as a BRST variation

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-fixing fermion](gauge-fixing-fermion.md)

For the left [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) and $s\bar c=-b$, $sb=0$, the displayed [gauge-fixing fermion](gauge-fixing-fermion.md) gives $b\,\partial\cdot A+\xi b^2/2+\bar c\,\partial\cdot Dc$. The ghost signs follow from odd parity. [BRST nilpotence](brst-nilpotence.md) implies invariance of this density without using field equations. Eliminating the [Nakanishi-Lautrup field](nakanishi-lautrup-field.md) gives $-(\partial\cdot A)^2/(2\xi)$ for nonzero $\xi$.
