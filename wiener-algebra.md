# Wiener algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

The Wiener algebra consists of periodic [functions](function-split.md) with absolutely summable [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md), with [norm](norm.md) $\sum_k|a_k|$. Multiplication corresponds to [discrete convolution](discrete-convolution.md) of coefficients, so the [norm](norm.md) is submultiplicative. Consequently $e^f=\sum_{n\ge0}f^n/n!$ also belongs to the algebra when $f$ does. Every such [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) is uniformly convergent and defines a [continuous function](continuous-function.md).
