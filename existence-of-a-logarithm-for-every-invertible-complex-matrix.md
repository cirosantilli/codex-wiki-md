# Existence of a logarithm for every invertible complex matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix logarithm](matrix-logarithm.md)

An invertible complex [Jordan block](jordan-block.md) $\lambda I+N$, with $N^s=0$, has logarithm $\ell I+\sum_{k=1}^{s-1}(-1)^{k+1}(N/\lambda)^k/k$, where $e^\ell=\lambda$. Applying this construction blockwise and conjugating back proves surjectivity of the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) on the complex [general linear group](general-linear-group.md). There is no globally single-valued continuous choice of logarithm.
