# Social appraisal theory

↑ **Parent:** [Appraisal theory](appraisal-theory.md)

The account that another person's reaction changes an observer's evaluation of an event, and thereby the observer's [emotion](emotion.md). The process can be rapid and need not involve consciously naming the other's feeling. It permits complementary outcomes, such as another person's [anger](anger.md) contributing to the observer's [fear](fear.md).
