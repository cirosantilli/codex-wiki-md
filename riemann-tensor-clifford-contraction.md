# Riemann tensor Clifford contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Lichnerowicz spinor-square formula](lichnerowicz-spinor-square-formula.md)

The four-gamma product decomposes into fully antisymmetric, two-gamma and scalar parts. The first part vanishes on contraction by the [first Bianchi identity](first-bianchi-identity.md), and the two-gamma parts vanish because they contract symmetric [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md) against antisymmetric gamma matrices. The scalar contractions are $-g^{ac}g^{bd}R_{abcd}+g^{ad}g^{bc}R_{abcd}=-2R$. Here $R=g^{ac}g^{bd}R_{abcd}$ fixes the curvature-slot convention.
