# Orbit polynomial under a finite automorphism group

↑ **Parent:** [Artin fixed-field theorem](artin-fixed-field-theorem.md)

For $\alpha\in L$ and a finite automorphism group $G$, the polynomial

$$
f(t,\alpha)=\prod_{g\in G}(t-g(\alpha))
$$

has coefficients in the [fixed field](fixed-field.md) $L^G$, because every element of $G$ permutes its factors.
