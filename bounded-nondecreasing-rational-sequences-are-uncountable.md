# Bounded nondecreasing rational sequences are uncountable

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone sequence](monotone-sequence.md)

Map a [binary sequence](bitstream.md) $(\epsilon_j)$ to the rational partial sums $s_n=\sum_{j=1}^n\epsilon_j3^{-j}$. They are nondecreasing and bounded above by $1/2$. A first different digit makes the corresponding partial sums different at that index, so the map is injective. [Cantor's diagonal argument](cantor-s-diagonal-argument.md) then makes this family uncountable despite every individual term being rational.
