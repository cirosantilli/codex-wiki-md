# Integrated squared bias from a density jump

↑ **Parent:** [Bias of a kernel density estimator](bias-of-a-kernel-density-estimator.md)

An uncorrected nonnegative symmetric [kernel density estimator](kernel-density-estimation.md) can leak an order-one amount across a jump in a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md). If its transition region has width proportional to the [smoothing bandwidth](smoothing-bandwidth.md) $h$, the [integrated mean squared error](integrated-mean-squared-error.md) includes squared [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md) of order $h$, rather than the order $h^4$ familiar for twice-smooth interior densities. For the unit-width uniform window and the [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of rate one, the integrated squared [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md) is $h/12+o(h)$.
