# Linear pressure equation for rotating stratified flow

↑ **Parent:** [Inertia-gravity wave](inertia-gravity-wave.md)

With downward buoyancy $b=g\rho\prime/\rho_0$ in a uniform rotating [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), $q=\zeta-fb_z/N^2$ is time-independent. The linear pressure obeys $\nabla^2p_{tt}+f^2p_{zz}+N^2\nabla_H^2p=\rho_0fN^2q$. The initial [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) supplies the steady pressure source; nonzero-frequency [inertia-gravity waves](inertia-gravity-wave.md) have zero perturbation PV.
