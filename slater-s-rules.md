<h1 id="slater-s-rules">Slater's rules</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Shielding constant](shielding-constant.md)

An empirical orbital-screening prescription. Other electrons in an $ns,np$ group count $0.35$ each; electrons in shell $n-1$ count $0.85$, deeper electrons $1.00$, and outer electrons zero. For $1s$, the partner contributes $0.30$. d and f groups use separate rules.[1930, printed pp. 58–59](atomic-shielding-constants-by-john-c-slater.md)
