# Transient distribution of an M-M-infinity queue

↑ **Parent:** [M/M/∞ queue](m-m-%E2%88%9E-queue.md)

If $X_0=n$ and $p_t=e^{-\mu t}$, then

$$
X_t\overset{d}{=}
\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p_t)
+\operatorname{Poisson}\!\left(\frac\lambda\mu(1-p_t)\right),
$$

with independent summands. The binomial term counts surviving initial customers; [Poisson thinning](poisson-thinning.md) gives the surviving later arrivals.
