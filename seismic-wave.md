# Seismic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seismic_wave)

A propagating disturbance in or along the solid [Earth](earth.md), including body [P waves](p-wave.md), body [S waves](s-wave.md) and surface modes. A [water](water.md) layer supports compressional acoustic motion but not a bulk shear restoring force.
