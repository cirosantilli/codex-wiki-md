# Lense-Thirring precession

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lense–Thirring_precession)

Lense-Thirring precession is the rotation of orbital angular momentum caused by the gravitational field of a spinning mass. In the secular weak-field picture, the precession vector is parallel to the central spin. Thus an annulus has [torque](torque.md) proportional to $\mathbf J_h\times\delta\mathbf J_d$, perpendicular to $\mathbf J_h$. Summing such torques preserves this orthogonality even if their radial coefficients differ. Dissipation and the internal response of a warped [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) can supplement precession with an alignment torque.
