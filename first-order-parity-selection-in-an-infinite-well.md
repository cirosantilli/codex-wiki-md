# First-order parity selection in an infinite well

↑ **Parent:** [Electric-dipole selection rule](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md)

For $0<x<w$, normalized states $\psi_n=\sqrt{2/w}\sin[(n+1)\pi x/w]$ have centre-reflection eigenvalue $(-1)^n$. Since $x-w/2$ is odd, off-diagonal electric-dipole matrix elements vanish between equal-parity states. Ground state n=0 cannot reach n=2 at first order; an initial n=1 can reach even labels including n=0. This does not impose an all-orders prohibition: two dipole interactions can restore the original parity. The general nonzero elements follow from [position matrix elements in a symmetric infinite square well](position-matrix-elements-in-a-symmetric-infinite-square-well.md) after shifting the origin.
