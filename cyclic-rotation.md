# Cyclic rotation

↑ **Parent:** [String](string.md)

A [cyclic rotation](cyclic-rotation.md) of a [string](string.md) $xy$ is the [string](string.md) $yx$, where $x$ and $y$ are consecutive pieces. For example, rotating ABC left once gives BCA. A length-$n$ [string](string.md) has $n$ rotation positions, though repeated letters can make some resulting [strings](string.md) identical.
