# Fivefold-twinned silver nanowire

↑ **Parent:** [Silver nanowire](silver-nanowire.md)

A [silver](silver.md) wire composed of five twinned fcc domains around its axis, commonly with a pentagonal cross section. Sidewall passivation and atom addition at ends can favour one-dimensional growth from a multiply twinned seed. Cross-sectional microscopy and [diffraction](diffraction.md) are stronger evidence for this structure than the outer wire outline alone.[Microtomed cross sections and fivefold-twin evidence](polyol-synthesis-of-uniform-silver-nanowires-a-plausible-growth-mechanism-and-the-supporting-evidence-by-yugang-sun-and-others.md)
