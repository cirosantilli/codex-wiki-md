# Unit of an adjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Unit and counit of an adjunction](unit-and-counit-of-an-adjunction.md)

For $F\dashv G$, the unit is the [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) $\eta:1\Rightarrow GF$ corresponding to the identity of $FX$ under the [adjunction](adjoint-functors.md) at each $X$. It participates in the [triangle identities for an adjunction](triangle-identities-for-an-adjunction.md).
