# Quasi-geostrophic potential-vorticity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Three-dimensional quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md)

The quasi-geostrophic potential-vorticity equation states that [three-dimensional quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) is materially conserved in inviscid, adiabatic flow. On a [beta plane](beta-plane.md) with constant [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md),

$$
\frac{D_g}{Dt}\left(
\nabla_h^2\psi+\frac{f_0^2}{N^2}\psi_{zz}
\right)+\beta\psi_x=0.
$$
