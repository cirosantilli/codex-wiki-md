# Residual of an inverse problem

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)

The residual is the difference between predicted and observed data. Its [norm](norm.md) measures data misfit and can determine an iteration stopping rule for [regularization of an inverse problem](regularization-of-an-inverse-problem.md).
