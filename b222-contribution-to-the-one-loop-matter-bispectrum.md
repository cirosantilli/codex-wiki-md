# B222 contribution to the one-loop matter bispectrum

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop matter bispectrum](one-loop-matter-bispectrum.md)

The connected correlation of three second-order [density contrasts](density-contrast.md) forms a triangle of three linear [power spectra](power-spectrum.md). It has eight [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md). In one dimension, with $k_1+k_2+k_3=0$ and $P_L$ the power at the evaluation time,

$$
B_{222}=8\int\frac{dq}{2\pi}F_2(q,k_1-q)F_2(-q,k_2+q)F_2(q-k_1,-k_2-q)P_L(q)P_L(k_1-q)P_L(k_2+q).
$$

For the [one-dimensional cosmological density kernel](one-dimensional-cosmological-density-kernel.md), its hard-loop integrand starts as $-k_1^2k_2^2k_3^2P_L(q)^3/q^6$.
