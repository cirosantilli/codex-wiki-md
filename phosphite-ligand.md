# Phosphite ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [phosphite ligand](phosphite-ligand.md) such as $\mathrm{P(OR)_3}$ binds through a [phosphorus](phosphorus.md) [lone pair](lone-pair.md) and can accept [pi backbonding](pi-backbonding.md). Its donation and acceptance differ from those of both [phosphine ligands](phosphine-ligand.md) and [carbonyl ligands](carbonyl-ligand.md).
