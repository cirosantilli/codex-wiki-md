# Sampling rate

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic time-of-flight speed measurement](acoustic-time-of-flight-speed-measurement.md)

The sampling rate is the number of recorded samples per unit [time](time-in-physics.md). A sample-index difference $N$ represents a delay $N/f_s$ when the acquisition clock is correctly calibrated.
