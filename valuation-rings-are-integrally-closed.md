# Valuation rings are integrally closed

↑ **Parent:** [Valuation ring](valuation-ring.md)

If an element $x$ of the [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) is absent from a [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) $V$, its inverse $y$ belongs to the [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md). A monic equation for $x$, multiplied by a power of $y$, would put $1$ in that [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md). Thus $V$ is an [integrally closed domain](integrally-closed-domain.md), without a discrete or [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md) hypothesis.
