# Burn-in total variation comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric ergodicity](geometric-ergodicity.md)

Applying a [Markov kernel](markov-kernel.md) to two initial laws cannot increase their [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md), even for the distribution of an entire subsequent finite trajectory. If a bounded summand has absolute value at most $B$, the [variance](variance-split.md) of a length-$N$ path sum differs between these laws by at most $6B^2N^2$ times their [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md). Thus a geometrically long enough burn-in makes this difference negligible relative to $N$.
