# Elliptic point

↑ **Parent:** [Supporting-sphere curvature bound](supporting-sphere-curvature-bound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_point)

An elliptic point of a surface is a point at which the [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) is positive, equivalently where the two [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) have the same nonzero sign.
