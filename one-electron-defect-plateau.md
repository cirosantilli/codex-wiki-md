# One-electron defect plateau

↑ **Parent:** [Single-orbital interacting defect](single-orbital-interacting-defect.md)

For a repulsive single orbital at [temperatures](temperature-split.md) small compared with U, [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) strictly between the first and second addition energies select a singly occupied defect. Its mean occupation is nearly one while its charge-number [variance](variance-split.md) is exponentially small away from the thermally broadened step regions. The remaining [spin degeneracy](spin-degeneracy.md) does not itself produce charge-number fluctuations.
