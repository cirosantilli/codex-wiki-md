# Direct-gap semiconductor gain condition

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor laser](semiconductor-laser.md)

For a vertical optical transition between parabolic [energy bands](energy-band.md) with common carrier [temperature](temperature-split.md), net [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) has occupation factor $f_c(E_c)-f_v(E_v)$. The monotonic [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) makes this positive exactly when the [quasi-Fermi-level splitting](quasi-fermi-level-splitting.md) exceeds the transition [photon energy](photon-energy.md). An allowed transition must also lie above the [band gap](band-gap.md). Broadening, band degeneracy and a zero-temperature occupation window require their own qualifications; this condition alone does not establish [laser threshold](laser-threshold.md).
