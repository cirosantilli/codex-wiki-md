# Exact symplectic manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exact_symplectic_manifold)

An exact symplectic manifold has a [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) that is an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md), written here $\omega=-d\lambda$. A positive-dimensional example without boundary cannot be [compact](compact-space.md), by the [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md) applied to the top [wedge product of differential forms](wedge-product-of-differential-forms.md).
