# Axial repulsion of pusher stresslets

↑ **Parent:** [Force-dipole flow](force-dipole-flow.md)

Two equal parallel axial point [stresslets](force-dipole-flow.md) with positive strength $S$ and separation $\ell$ have relative [velocity](velocity.md) $S/(2\pi\mu\ell^2)$. Identical self-propulsion cancels in their relative motion. The solution is the displayed cubic separation law while the far-field approximation is valid.
