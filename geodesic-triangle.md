# Geodesic triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)

A geodesic triangle is bounded by three geodesic segments. On a surface its angle excess is the integral of the [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) over the triangle.

It is the three-sided instance of a [geodesic polygon](geodesic-polygon.md).
