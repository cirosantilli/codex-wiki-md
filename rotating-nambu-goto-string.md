<h1 id="rotating-nambu-goto-string">Rotating Nambu–Goto string</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu–Goto action](nambu-goto-action.md)

A straight rigidly rotating classical string has [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) at a free endpoint. Its [energy](energy.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) satisfy a [Regge trajectory](regge-trajectory.md). A segment with one anchored and one free end has $J=E^2/(\pi T)$, twice the slope of the string with two free ends.
