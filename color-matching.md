# Color matching

↑ **Parent:** [Color vision](color-vision.md)

An observer adjusts lights until they appear to match a comparison under specified viewing and adaptation conditions. The settings and range of accepted matches constrain the available spectral signals. Matching differs from detecting a difference and from assigning a [color](color.md) name.
