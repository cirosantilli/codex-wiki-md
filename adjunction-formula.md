# Adjunction formula

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunction_formula)

For a smooth hypersurface $X$ in a complex manifold $Y$,

$$
K_X\cong(K_Y\otimes\mathcal O_Y(X))|_X.
$$
