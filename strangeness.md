# Strangeness

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strangeness)

An additive [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) quantum number: a strange [quark](quark.md) has $S=-1$, its [antiquark](antiquark.md) has $S=+1$, and up and down [quarks](quark.md) have $S=0$. The [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md) conserves net strangeness. Together with [baryon number](baryon-number.md), it determines [flavor hypercharge](flavor-hypercharge.md) $Y=B+S$.
