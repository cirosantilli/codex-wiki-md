# Homology of the antipodal sphere mapping torus

↑ **Parent:** [Wang sequence](wang-sequence.md)

For $n\geq2$, the [Wang sequence](wang-sequence.md) and the [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) $(-1)^{n+1}$ of the [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) give $H_0=H_1=\mathbb Z$. If $n$ is odd, $H_n=H_{n+1}=\mathbb Z$; if $n$ is even, $H_n=\mathbb Z/2$ and $H_{n+1}=0$. All other groups vanish. At $n=1$ the [mapping torus](mapping-torus.md) is a torus with $H_1=\mathbb Z^2$. At $n=0$, the two-point swapping mapping torus is a circle. Every example is already a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md); for $n\geq1$ it is orientable exactly when $n$ is odd.
