# Difference equation

↑ **Parent:** [Recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md)

A [difference equation](difference-equation.md) relates values of a [sequence](sequence.md) at different indices, or its finite differences. A first-order autonomous system has the displayed form, with a scalar or vector state. Writing $z_{n+1}-z_n=F(z_n)-z_n$ gives its equivalent finite-difference form. Its [fixed point](fixed-point.md) stability is determined by the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of the update and the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), whereas a continuous-time [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) uses the real parts of its linearized [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md).
