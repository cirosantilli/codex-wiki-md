# Reversible isothermal expansion of a photon gas

↑ **Parent:** [Photon gas](photon-gas.md)

At fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), a [photon gas](photon-gas.md) has constant [energy density](energy-density.md) $u$ and [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) $p=u/3$. An increase $\Delta V$ in [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) requires both $\Delta U=u\Delta V$ and expansion [work](work.md) $p\Delta V$, giving absorbed [heat](heat-split.md) $4u\Delta V/3$.
