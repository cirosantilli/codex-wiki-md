# Solution surface

↑ **Parent:** [Solution of a partial differential equation](solution-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md)

The [function graph](graph-of-a-function.md) of a scalar [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) with two independent variables. For a [first-order partial differential equation](first-order-partial-differential-equation.md), the [method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md) constructs it from lifted [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md); invertibility of the projected parametrization is required for the surface to define a single-valued [function](function-split.md).
