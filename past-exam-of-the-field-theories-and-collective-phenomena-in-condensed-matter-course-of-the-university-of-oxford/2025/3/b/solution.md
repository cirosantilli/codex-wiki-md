<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md) and its [Hermitian conjugate](../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md) read

$$
a=uc+vd^\dagger,\quad a^\dagger=uc^\dagger+vd,
\qquad
b=-vc^\dagger+ud,\quad b^\dagger=-vc+ud^\dagger.
$$

Using the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) of $c,d$, the nonzero same-mode [anticommutators](../../../../anticommutator.md) are

$$
\{a,a^\dagger\}=u^2\{c,c^\dagger\}+v^2\{d^\dagger,d\}=u^2+v^2,
\qquad
\{b,b^\dagger\}=u^2+v^2.
$$

The equal-type [anticommutators](../../../../anticommutator.md) satisfy $\{a,a\}=\{b,b\}=0$, since every contributing equal-type or mixed-mode [anticommutator](../../../../anticommutator.md) of $c,d$ vanishes. The potentially nontrivial cross term cancels:

$$
\{a,b\}=-uv\{c,c^\dagger\}+uv\{d^\dagger,d\}
=-uv+uv=0.
$$

Also $\{a,b^\dagger\}=0$, and the other cross relations follow by [Hermitian conjugation](../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md). Therefore the necessary and sufficient condition is

$$
\boxed{u^2+v^2=1.}
$$

The real transformation [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) is then an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), with inverse

$$
\boxed{c=ua-vb^\dagger,\qquad d=va^\dagger+ub.}
$$

This preservation of the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) makes it a canonical [Bogoliubov transformation](../../../../bogoliubov-transformation.md).
