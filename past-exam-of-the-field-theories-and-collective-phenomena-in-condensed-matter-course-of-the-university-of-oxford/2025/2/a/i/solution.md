<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $J>0$ each bond minimizes its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) when its two [Ising spins](../../../../../ising-spin-variable.md) are opposite, giving bond [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $-J$. An even ring allows every bond to do this simultaneously, including the closing bond required by the [periodic boundary conditions](../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md). Its two [ground states](../../../../../ground-state.md) are

$$
\boxed{S_j=\sigma(-1)^{j-1},\quad\sigma=\pm1;
\qquad E_0=-NJ,\quad g_0=2.}
$$

The two configurations are related by [spin inversion symmetry](../../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md). There is no [geometrical frustration](../../../../../geometrical-frustration.md).
