<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $k$ count parallel bonds. Then $N-k$ bonds are antiparallel and the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is

$$
H=Jk-J(N-k)=-NJ+2Jk.
$$

The [periodic boundary conditions](../../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) impose

$$
\prod_{j=1}^N(S_jS_{j+1})=\prod_{j=1}^NS_j^2=1.
$$

Hence $N-k$ must be even. For odd $N$, $k$ is odd, so its minimum is one: one bond must be parallel. All other bonds alternate. Choosing that bond in $N$ ways and then choosing either value of $S_1$ determines every [Ising spin](../../../../../ising-spin-variable.md), giving

$$
\boxed{E_0=-(N-2)J,\qquad g_0=2N.}
$$

This is an [odd antiferromagnetic Ising ring](../../../../../odd-antiferromagnetic-ising-ring.md): [geometrical frustration](../../../../../geometrical-frustration.md) forces one defect, whose location is unrestricted. The [ground-state degeneracy](../../../../../ground-state-degeneracy.md) includes both its possible locations and global [spin inversion symmetry](../../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md). The formula also includes the self-bond case $N=1$ under the stated [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) convention.
