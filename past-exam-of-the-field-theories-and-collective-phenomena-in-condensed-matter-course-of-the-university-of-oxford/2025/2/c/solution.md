<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $0<K<\infty$, $|q|=\tanh K<1$. Taking the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md) $N\to\infty$ at fixed separation $n$ removes $q^N$ and $q^{N-n}$ from the [finite antiferromagnetic Ising-ring correlation](../../../../finite-ring-antiferromagnetic-ising-correlation.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{C_\infty(n)=(-\tanh K)^n=(-1)^ne^{-n/\xi},
\qquad \xi=-\frac1{\ln\tanh(\beta J)}=\frac1{\ln\coth(\beta J)}.}
$$

The [correlation length](../../../../correlation-length.md) $\xi$ is measured in lattice spacings. If the lattice spacing is $a$, its physical value is $a\xi$. The factor $(-1)^n$ expresses [antiferromagnetism](../../../../antiferromagnetism.md), while $e^{-n/\xi}$ measures the decay of the staggered [two-point correlation function](../../../../two-point-correlation-function.md).

At every positive [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), $\xi$ is finite. At low [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), $\tanh K=1-2e^{-2K}+O(e^{-4K})$, giving $\boxed{\xi\sim\tfrac12e^{2\beta J}}$. At infinite [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), $K=0$, distinct [Ising spins](../../../../ising-spin-variable.md) are uncorrelated; the limiting [correlation length](../../../../correlation-length.md) is zero.
