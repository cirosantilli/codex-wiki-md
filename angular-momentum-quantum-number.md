# Angular momentum quantum number

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum number](quantum-number.md)

Labels an [angular momentum eigenstate](angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) through the [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\hbar^2j(j+1)$ of the squared [angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator-split.md). The allowed projections are $m\hbar$, with $m=-j,\ldots,j$. [Orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) has integer $j$; [spin](spin.md) can also have half-integer $j$.
