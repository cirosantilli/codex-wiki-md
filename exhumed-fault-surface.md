# Exhumed fault surface

↑ **Parent:** [Fault scarp](fault-scarp.md)

An exposed [fault plane](fault-plane.md) revealed as [fault slip](fault-slip.md), differential motion and removal of cover bring it to the surface. On a [normal fault](normal-fault.md), lowering the [hanging wall](hanging-wall.md) can expose a planar [footwall](footwall.md) face. Continued exposure may preserve a bedrock scarp while [weathering](weathering.md), [erosion](erosion.md) and collapse progressively modify its older portions. A hillslope following the plane therefore need not be an independent erosional surface that accidentally has the same orientation.
