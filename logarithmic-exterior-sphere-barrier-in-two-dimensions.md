# Logarithmic exterior-sphere barrier in two dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior sphere condition](exterior-sphere-condition.md)

If $B(y,R)$ is an exterior ball tangent to a planar domain at $x_0$, then

$$
w(x)=\log\frac{|x-y|}{R}
$$

is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) in the domain, vanishes at $x_0$, and is positive at every other point of its closure. It is therefore a [barrier for the Dirichlet problem](barrier-for-the-dirichlet-problem.md) and proves that $x_0$ is a [regular boundary point](regular-boundary-point.md).
