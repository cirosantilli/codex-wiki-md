# Neutrino matter potential

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md)

Coherent forward weak scattering in ordinary matter changes [neutrino](neutrino.md) propagation [phases](phase-waves.md). The [charged-current](charged-current.md) [electron](electron.md) contribution gives the displayed potential difference, with the opposite sign for [antineutrinos](antineutrino.md). A common [neutral-current](neutral-current.md) term can be removed from active-flavour [oscillations](neutrino-oscillation.md). A sufficiently large effect requires propagation with the matter Hamiltonian rather than the vacuum formula.
