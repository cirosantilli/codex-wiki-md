# Morse handle-attachment theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Morse theory](morse-theory.md)

Crossing one nondegenerate [critical point](critical-point.md) of [Morse index](morse-index.md) $\lambda$ in a compact sublevel interval attaches a [handle](handle.md) $D^\lambda\times D^{m-\lambda}$. Between critical levels a suitably normalized [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md) identifies the sublevels. The [Morse lemma](morse-lemma.md) supplies the local quadratic model for the attachment. Retracting each [handle](handle.md) to its [core disk](core-disk-of-a-handle.md) gives a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) model with one $\lambda$-cell per [critical point](critical-point.md). Closed [manifolds](topological-manifold.md) have finite such decompositions; a noncompact [manifold](topological-manifold.md) requires a proper bounded-below exhaustion for the corresponding statement.
