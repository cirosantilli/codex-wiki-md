# Hydrostatic pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrostatic_pressure)

[Hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) is the [pressure](pressure-split.md) distribution in a resting [fluid](fluid.md), balancing its [body force](body-force.md) from [gravity](gravity.md). Take $z$ upward and a uniform downward [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) $\mathbf g=-g\mathbf e_z$, where $g>0$. The vertical [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) satisfies

$$
\frac{dp}{dz}=-\rho_f g.
$$

A short derivation balances the vertical [forces](force-split.md) on a thin horizontal layer of [area](surface-area.md) $A$ and thickness $\Delta z$: $p(z)A-p(z+\Delta z)A-\rho_fgA\Delta z=0$. Dividing by $A\Delta z$ and taking the [derivative](derivative.md) gives the equation above. For constant [mass density](density.md) $\rho_f$, [integration](integral-split.md) from the surface at $z_s$ gives

$$
p(z)=p_{\mathrm{surface}}+\rho_fg(z_s-z)
=p_{\mathrm{surface}}+\rho_fgh,\qquad h=z_s-z.
$$

Thus [pressure](pressure-split.md) increases with depth $h$. For the stipulated [water](water.md) with [mass density](density.md) $1000\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $9.81\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$, depths $0.20\ \mathrm m$ and $0.30\ \mathrm m$ give [gauge pressures](gauge-pressure.md) $1962\ \mathrm{Pa}$ and $2943\ \mathrm{Pa}$. Their difference produces the [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) of the example [cube](cube.md). [Feynman Lectures, II §40-1: hydrostatics](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/II_40.html#Ch40-S1)
