# Squarefree-part bound for smooth numbers

↑ **Parent:** [S-smooth number](s-smooth-number.md)

If $S$ is a finite set of [prime numbers](prime-number.md), every [S-smooth number](s-smooth-number.md) has a unique expression $n=ab^2$, where $a$ is [squarefree](squarefree-integer.md) and its [prime factors](prime-factor.md) lie in $S$. There are at most $2^{|S|}$ choices for $a$ and at most $\lfloor\sqrt x\rfloor$ choices for $b$ when $n\le x$. Consequently $f_S(x)\le2^{|S|}\sqrt x$.
