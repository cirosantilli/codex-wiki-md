# Single-photon state

↑ **Parent:** [Photon](photon.md)

A [single-photon state](single-photon-state.md) lies in the one-excitation sector of the quantized [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md). It can be a [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md) over spatial ports or temporal modes while its total [photon number](photon-number.md) remains one. Squared coefficients give exclusive event probabilities only when the corresponding wavepacket modes are orthonormal; overlapping temporal histories must be added coherently.
