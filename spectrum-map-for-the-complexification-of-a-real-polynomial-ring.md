# Spectrum map for the complexification of a real polynomial ring

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of a commutative ring](spectrum-of-a-commutative-ring.md)

Contraction sends the zero [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) to zero. A complex closed point $(x-z)$ contracts to $(x-z)$ when $z$ is real, and to $((x-\operatorname{Re}z)^2+(\operatorname{Im}z)^2)$ otherwise. Thus the map on the [spectrum of a commutative ring](spectrum-of-a-commutative-ring.md) is surjective and identifies each nonreal conjugate pair. Both spectra have only finite proper [Zariski-closed sets](zariski-closed-set.md), so this is also the topological quotient by complex conjugation.
