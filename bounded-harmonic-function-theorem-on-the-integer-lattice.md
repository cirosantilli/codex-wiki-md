# Bounded harmonic function theorem on the integer lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete harmonic function](discrete-harmonic-function.md)

Every bounded [discrete harmonic function](discrete-harmonic-function.md) on $\mathbb Z^d$ is constant. After rescaling its range to $[0,1]$, place it in the compact convex set of all such functions; translation invariance and the mean-value identity force every extreme point to be constant, and the [Krein-Milman theorem](krein-milman-theorem.md) finishes the proof.
