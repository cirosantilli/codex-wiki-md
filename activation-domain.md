# Activation domain

↑ **Parent:** [Protein domain](protein-domain.md)

An [activation domain](activation-domain.md) is a protein region that promotes transcription when appropriately recruited, often through cofactor interactions. Tethering experiments can distinguish activation capacity from the sequence specificity supplied by a [DNA-binding domain](dna-binding-domain.md).
