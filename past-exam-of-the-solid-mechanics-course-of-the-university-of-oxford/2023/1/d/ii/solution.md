<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Only the $k=0$ term of the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) contributes to the limit:

$$
\frac{M(\tau)}\tau
=\frac{A^4}{2}(\mu_1+3^n\mu_2)+O(\tau^2).
$$

Thus the requested [torsional stiffness per unit twist](../../../../../torsional-stiffness-per-unit-twist.md) is

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{A^4}{2}(\mu_1+3^n\mu_2).}
$$

The small-[strain](../../../../../strain.md) [shear modulus](../../../../../shear-modulus.md) is $G=2\hat w'(3)=2(\mu_1+3^n\mu_2)$. Therefore $\alpha=GA^4/4$, consistently with the usual circular-shaft [torque](../../../../../torque.md) relation after restoring the $2\pi$ factor. If the prescribed end rotation is the [angle](../../../../../angle.md) $\varphi=\tau L$, the physical [torsional stiffness](../../../../../torsional-stiffness.md) is $dN/d\varphi=2\pi\alpha/L$ at zero twist. This also explains why $L$ does not enter the requested $\alpha$.
