<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Cauchy momentum equation](../../../../../cauchy-momentum-equation.md) in [static elastic equilibrium](../../../../../static-elastic-equilibrium.md), with zero [body force](../../../../../body-force.md), is $\operatorname{div}\mathbf T=\mathbf0$. The [Cauchy stress tensor](../../../../../cauchy-stress-tensor.md) is symmetric. [Material isotropy](../../../../../material-isotropy.md) and the equal tangential [principal stretches](../../../../../principal-stretch.md) imply $t_\theta=t_\phi$, so its radial equation is

$$
\boxed{\frac{dt_r}{dr}+\frac2r(t_r-t_\theta)=0,\qquad
\text{equivalently}\quad
\frac{dt_r}{dr}=\frac2r(t_\theta-t_r).}
$$

All [stresses](../../../../../stress.md) and spatial derivatives in this equation are measured in the [current configuration](../../../../../current-configuration.md).
