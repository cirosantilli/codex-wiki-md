<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $R>0$, differentiation gives $\partial_jR=\theta_j$ and $\partial_j\theta_i=(\delta_{ij}-\theta_i\theta_j)/R$. Hence

$$
\boxed{Dy=\frac rR(I-\theta\otimes\theta)+r'\theta\otimes\theta.}
$$

The two matrices are the [orthogonal projections](../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto the tangent plane of the reference sphere and its radial line. The [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) multiplies the radial direction by $r'$ and each tangent direction by $r/R$. With positive $r,r'$, it is itself symmetric positive definite, so its [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are its principal stretches:

$$
\boxed{\lambda_1=r',\qquad\lambda_2=\lambda_3=r/R.}
$$
