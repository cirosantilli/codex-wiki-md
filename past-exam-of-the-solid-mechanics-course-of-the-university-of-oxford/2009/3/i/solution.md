<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At every point the [deformation gradient](../../../../deformation-gradient.md) attains the differentiable energy's minimum, so $DW(D\mathbf y)=0$ and it is a natural state. By the strict Baker-Ericksen argument just proved, all its principal stretches are equal. In the radial formula this gives

$$
r'(R)=r(R)/R,\qquad\frac d{dR}\left(\frac{r(R)}R\right)=0.
$$

Thus $r(R)=vR$ for one constant $v>0$ on the [connected](../../../../connected-space.md) radial interval, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbf y(\mathbf x)=v\mathbf x.}
$$

The constancy is global through the annulus, not a separately chosen dilation at each point.
