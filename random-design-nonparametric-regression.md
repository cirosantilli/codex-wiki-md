# Random-design nonparametric regression

↑ **Parent:** [Nonparametric regression](nonparametric-regression.md)

The predictor values are random, and the [regression function](regression-function.md) is the conditional mean of the response. For independent pairs and uniformly bounded conditional error [variances](variance-split.md), kernel-window occupancies govern the stochastic part of a [local polynomial regression](local-polynomial-regression.md) risk bound. This differs from [fixed-design nonparametric regression](fixed-design-nonparametric-regression.md), where the predictor values are deterministic.
