# Linear inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Inequality (mathematics)](inequality-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_inequality)

A linear inequality compares a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) of a [vector](vector.md) of unknowns with a constant, for example $a^Tx\leq b$ or $a^Tx<b$ over the [real numbers](real-number.md). For $a\ne0$, its [solution set](solution-set.md) is a closed or open [half-space](half-space.md), respectively. The inequality $x+y\leq1$ allows all points on one side of the line $x+y=1$, including the line. Multiplication by a negative [real number](real-number.md) reverses the [inequality](inequality-mathematics.md), so $x\geq1$ is equivalently $-x\leq-1$.
