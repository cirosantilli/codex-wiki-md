# Hill coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Hill equation](hill-equation.md)

The slope of $\log[\theta/(1-\theta)]$ against $\log[L]$ in a [Hill equation](hill-equation.md) description. A value above one supports positive [cooperative binding](cooperative-binding.md) under the model's assumptions. It does not by itself count occupied sites or identify a molecular mechanism.
