# Klein paradox

↑ **Parent:** [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md)

In a sufficiently strong electrostatic step, the positive-energy incident channel overlaps a negative-kinetic-energy channel on the far side. For a charged [scalar field](scalar-field.md) the outgoing [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) branch has negative charge current, allowing reflected particle current larger than the incident current while signed charge is conserved. The external source supplies [energy](energy-split.md) for particle-antiparticle creation. This requires a multiparticle interpretation; negative transmitted charge current is not a negative [probability](probability.md).
