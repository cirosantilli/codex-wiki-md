# Sleep slow oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Non-rapid eye movement sleep](non-rapid-eye-movement-sleep.md)

A slow alternation in cortical population activity, conventionally below about one cycle per second. Its phase can organize other sleep events; a scalp electrical rhythm alone does not reveal a particular represented episode.
