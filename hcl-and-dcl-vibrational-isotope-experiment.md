# HCl and DCl vibrational isotope experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Isotope scaling of diatomic vibration](isotope-scaling-of-diatomic-vibration.md)

An actual [infrared spectroscopy](infrared-spectroscopy.md) route is to compare gaseous [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md) containing ordinary [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) with its [deuterium](deuterium.md) counterpart. A documented teaching experiment uses a stopcock-equipped gas cell with potassium bromide windows and a Nicolet Avatar instrument for [Fourier transform spectroscopy](fourier-transform-spectroscopy.md). A nitrogen-purged flask and drying tube supply the mixture; the sample also contains sulfur dioxide. An empty sample-chamber background precedes the filled-cell scan. The [absorbance](absorbance.md) trace and peak-picked [spectroscopic wavenumbers](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) are the readout. The analysis assigns and indexes $^{1}\mathrm H^{35}\mathrm{Cl}$ and $^{2}\mathrm H^{35}\mathrm{Cl}$ transitions separately and propagates transition uncertainties. The instructions distinguish resolvable [chlorine](chlorine.md) [isotope](isotope.md) peaks from adequate precision for the intended parameter analysis. [McCormick, Introduction; Experimental, Laboratory Component, Figures 1–2 and background/peak-picking paragraphs; Analysis and Discussion, first three paragraphs](vibrational-rotational-spectroscopy-of-hcl-by-james-m-mccormick.md)

A separate published teaching dataset reports a Nicolet Nexus 670 instrument, a $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ gas cell at $20\,\mathrm{Torr}$, and [chlorine](chlorine.md) [isotope](isotope.md) peaks resolved to baseline. These apparatus details describe that dataset, not the preceding experiment. [Gutow, HCl and DCl IR absorption spectra, dataset description: instrument, path length, pressure and resolved chlorine isotope peaks](hcl-and-dcl-ir-absorption-spectra-by-jonathan-gutow.md)

The inference connects several models. Fit correctly assigned transitions to separate [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md) from [molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md), then compare the inferred vibrational scales using [isotope scaling of diatomic vibration](isotope-scaling-of-diatomic-vibration.md). Agreement supports the approximate separation between a nearly unchanged [chemical bond](chemical-bond.md) stiffness and a changed nuclear [mass](mass.md). It does not make a real [molecule](molecule.md) an exact classical [spring](spring.md): [anharmonicity](anharmonicity.md) shifts the fundamental transition away from the local [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) scale, and coupling to [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md) shifts individual lines.

For an independent test, calculate the [reduced masses](reduced-mass.md) from the specified [isotopes](isotope.md) and compare the inferred values $k=\mu(2\pi c\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm})^2$. Agreement within uncertainty tests the common-curvature assumption; choosing separate $k$ values just to fit each [frequency](frequency.md) would not test it. The documented background addresses instrument and ambient contributions, but does not by itself subtract window absorption, identify contaminating species, or calibrate the [spectroscopic wavenumber](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) scale. Those require separate controls. Finite spectral resolution, blended peaks, incorrect [isotope](isotope.md) assignments, and departure from the [harmonic approximation near a potential minimum](harmonic-approximation-near-a-potential-minimum.md) limit the comparison. No numerical measured line positions are assumed here.
