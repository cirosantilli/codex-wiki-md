# Gap opening by a planet

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary migration](planetary-migration.md)

A [planet](planet.md) transfers [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) to disk material through its spiral wakes, tending to push [gas](gas.md) away from its orbital annulus. A persistent deep gap requires this clearing to compete with [pressure](pressure-split.md) and disk transport. A gap can reduce local supply without completely shutting off [gas](gas.md) streams or fixing the final [planet](planet.md) [mass](mass.md).
