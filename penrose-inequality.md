# Penrose inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)

The [Penrose inequality](penrose-inequality.md) compares asymptotic energy and an appropriate horizon or enclosing area in [geometrized units](geometrized-units.md). Its physical motivation uses censorship, positive radiated energy, settling to a [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md), and [Hawking's area theorem](hawking-s-area-theorem.md). The area variable requires care: the area of an arbitrary [apparent horizon](apparent-horizon.md) on non-time-symmetric data does not give a universally valid inequality. The [Riemannian Penrose inequality](riemannian-penrose-inequality.md) uses the relevant outermost [minimal surface](minimal-surface.md) under nonnegative [scalar curvature](scalar-curvature.md).
