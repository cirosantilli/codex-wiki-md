# Pseudoholomorphic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoholomorphic_curve)

For almost complex manifolds $(\Sigma,j)$ and $(X,J)$, a smooth map $u:\Sigma\to X$ is J-holomorphic when $du\circ j=J\circ du$, equivalently $\bar\partial_Ju=0$.
