# Axisymmetric stagnation strain

↑ **Parent:** [Stagnation point](stagnation-point.md)

A regular axisymmetric [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) near a stagnation point on an impermeable tangent plane has equal tangential strain rates. The [incompressibility condition](incompressible-flow.md) forces the normal rate to be their negative double. Thus the leading [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) has the displayed quadratic form. Its streamline invariants are the azimuth and $z(x^2+y^2)$; for incoming flow from positive $z$, $W>0$.
