# Balanced spectrum of a nonidentity stabilizer

↑ **Parent:** [Stabilizer group](stabilizer-group.md)

A [stabilizer group](stabilizer-group.md) excludes $-I$, so each element squares to $I$ and is Hermitian; an imaginary-phase Pauli element would square to $-I$. A nonidentity stabilizer contains at least one nonidentity local [Pauli matrix](pauli-matrices.md) and has zero trace, since the trace of a tensor product is the product of local traces. Its eigenvalues are therefore $\pm1$ with equal multiplicities. This trace fact also gives $\dim\mathcal X_S=2^n/|S|$ from the [stabilizer-projector formula](stabilizer-projector-formula.md).
