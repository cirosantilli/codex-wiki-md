# Glass electrode

↑ **Parent:** [Weak acid dissociation measurement](weak-acid-dissociation-measurement.md)

A [glass electrode](glass-electrode.md) is an [electrode](electrode.md) whose glass membrane responds to [hydrogen ion](hydrogen-ion.md) [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md). A meter measures its [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) against a [reference electrode](reference-electrode.md); calibration with standard [buffer solutions](buffer-solution.md) converts this response to operational [pH](ph.md).
