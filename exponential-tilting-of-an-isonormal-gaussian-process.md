# Exponential tilting of an isonormal Gaussian process

↑ **Parent:** [Isonormal Gaussian process](isonormal-gaussian-process.md)

For an [isonormal Gaussian process](isonormal-gaussian-process.md) $X$ on a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $H$ and $k\in H$, the positive density $\exp(X(k)-\|k\|^2/2)$ has expectation one and defines an [equivalent probability measure](equivalent-probability-measure.md). Under this measure, $X(h)$ has mean $\langle h,k\rangle$ and the same [covariance](covariance.md) as before. The shifted family $X(h)-\langle h,k\rangle$ is again an [isonormal Gaussian process](isonormal-gaussian-process.md). This follows by evaluating the joint Gaussian exponential formula for any finite collection of arguments. It is the Hilbert-space version of [exponential tilting](exponential-tilting.md).
