# Angular speed

↑ **Parent:** [Angular velocity](angular-velocity.md)

Angular speed is the nonnegative magnitude of [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md): $\omega=|\boldsymbol\omega|$. It measures the rate of rotation about an instantaneous axis without specifying its direction. For uniform [circular motion](circular-motion.md) of radius $R$, the ordinary [speed](speed.md) is $v=R\omega$.
