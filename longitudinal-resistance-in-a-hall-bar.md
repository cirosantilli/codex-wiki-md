# Longitudinal resistance in a Hall bar

↑ **Parent:** [Hall bar](hall-bar.md)

Voltage contacts separated along the current direction measure longitudinal resistance, even when the device is called a [Hall bar](hall-bar.md). For an approximately uniform rectangular strip, $R_{xx}\simeq\rho_{xx}L/W$. Transverse contacts measure $R_{xy}$ instead. [Shubnikov-de Haas effect](shubnikov-de-haas-effect.md) oscillations in $R_{xx}$ need not be Hall-resistance plateaus; in the integer [quantum Hall effect](quantum-hall-effect.md), longitudinal minima accompany quantized transverse plateaus.
