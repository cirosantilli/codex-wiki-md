# Double-averaged circular tidal potential

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar quadrupole disturbing function](stellar-quadrupole-disturbing-function.md)

For two circular [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md) with [mutual inclination](mutual-inclination.md) $I_m$, independently average the [stellar quadrupole disturbing function](stellar-quadrupole-disturbing-function.md) over both [mean anomalies](mean-anomaly.md). A circular ring with unit normal $\boldsymbol k$ has $\langle\widehat r_i\widehat r_j\rangle=(\delta_{ij}-k_i k_j)/2$. Contracting the two ring tensors gives $\langle\cos^2\psi\rangle=[1+(\boldsymbol k\cdot\boldsymbol k_p)^2]/4$, proving the displayed exact angular factor. For small [orbital inclinations](orbital-inclination.md) relative to a common plane, $I_m^2=I^2+I_p^2-2II_p\cos(\Omega_p-\Omega)+O(I^4)$. Thus the secular angular factor is $1/4-3(I^2+I_p^2)/8+3II_p\cos(\Omega_p-\Omega)/4$. This potential generates the slow stellar contribution to satellite [nodal precession](nodal-precession.md).
