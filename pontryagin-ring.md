# Pontryagin ring

↑ **Parent:** [Loop space](loop-space.md)

The [homology](homology-split.md) ring formed from loop concatenation and the [homology cross product](homology-cross-product.md). [Homotopy](homotopy.md) associativity is enough for associativity in [homology](homology-split.md), and the constant loop is the unit. More generally the same construction applies to a homotopy-associative H-space.
