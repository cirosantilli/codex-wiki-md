# Soap titration of water hardness

↑ **Parent:** [Water hardness](water-hardness.md)

A historical quantitative test in which a standardized [soap](soap.md) solution is added to a measured water sample until a specified persistent lather appears. A reagent blank accounts for the amount needed to form the lather itself.
