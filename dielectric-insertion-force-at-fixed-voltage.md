# Dielectric insertion force at fixed voltage

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-voltage capacitor energy balance](fixed-voltage-capacitor-energy-balance.md)

At fixed [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V$, a change of [capacitance](capacitance.md) has electrical work input $V\,dQ=V^2dC$ and field-energy increase $dU=V^2dC/2$. The remaining half is mechanical [work](work.md) done by the field, so the force in the direction of increasing overlap is $F_x=V^2C'(x)/2$. Equivalently the effective mechanical potential is $-CV^2/2$. For a [partially filled coaxial capacitor](partially-filled-coaxial-capacitor.md), this gives $F_x=\pi\epsilon_0(\epsilon_r-1)V^2/\ln(b/a)$, directed inward when $\epsilon_r>1$. The [fringing field of a capacitor](fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) supplies the local field gradient and transmits this force even when its small correction to total capacitance is neglected.
