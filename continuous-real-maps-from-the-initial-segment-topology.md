# Continuous real maps from the initial-segment topology

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

Give $\mathbb N$ the [topology](topology-split.md) whose nonempty [open sets](open-set.md) are $\mathbb N$ and the finite initial segments. Every nonempty [open set](open-set.md) contains $1$, so any two nonempty [open sets](open-set.md) intersect. A [continuous map](continuous-map.md) from this [topological space](topological-space.md) into a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) is constant: distinct image points would have disjoint [open](open-set.md) [neighbourhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md), whose inverse images would be disjoint nonempty [open sets](open-set.md). This includes [continuous](continuous-function.md) real-valued maps.
