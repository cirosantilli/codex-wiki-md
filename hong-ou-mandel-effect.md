# Hong-Ou-Mandel effect

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum interference](quantum-interference.md)

Two indistinguishable [bosons](boson.md) entering separate inputs of an ideal balanced [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md) have zero [probability](probability.md) of leaving one per output. The two alternatives for that occupancy interfere destructively. Distinguishability in temporal, spatial or internal modes weakens the cancellation.
