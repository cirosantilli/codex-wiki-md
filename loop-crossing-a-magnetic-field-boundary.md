# Loop crossing a magnetic field boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic braking](magnetic-braking.md)

A square resistive loop of side $a$, moving normally across a sharp field edge at [speed](speed.md) $v$, has induced current magnitude $Bav/R$ and drag magnitude $B^2a^2v/R$ while partially overlapping the field. Completely enclosed in a uniform static field it has no changing flux and, with neglected [self-inductance](self-inductance.md), no current.
