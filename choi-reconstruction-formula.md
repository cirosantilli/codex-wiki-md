# Choi reconstruction formula

↑ **Parent:** [Choi matrix](choi-matrix.md)

With normalized [Choi state](choi-state.md) $C_\Lambda$ and the output factor first,

$$
\Lambda(X)=d\operatorname{Tr}_{\rm in}[C_\Lambda(I_{\rm out}\otimes X^T)].
$$

Expand $C_\Lambda=d^{-1}\sum_{i,j}\Lambda(|i\rangle\langle j|)\otimes|i\rangle\langle j|$. The [partial trace](partial-trace.md) multiplies each coefficient by $\operatorname{Tr}(|i\rangle\langle j|X^T)=X_{ij}$, reconstructing $\Lambda(X)$ by linearity.
