# BRST nilpotence

↑ **Parent:** [BRST symmetry](brst-symmetry.md)

A [BRST charge](brst-charge.md) is nilpotent when $Q^2=0$, making [BRST cohomology](brst-cohomology.md) well-defined. For the critical [bosonic string theory](bosonic-string-theory.md), the matter and ghost [central charges](central-charge.md) cancel, $D-26=0$, and the intercept is one. In a ghost convention with a zero-mode linear shift, the shifted total constraint generators, rather than the unshifted ones, obey the centerless [Witt algebra](witt-algebra.md).
