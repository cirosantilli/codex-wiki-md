# DNA supercoiling

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

Twisting of the axis of double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) associated with torsional constraint. Supercoiling can influence the energetic cost of local strand opening.
