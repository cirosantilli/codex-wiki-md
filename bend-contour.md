# Bend contour

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction contrast](diffraction-contrast.md)

A band of [diffraction contrast](diffraction-contrast.md) where local bending brings a [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) into a particular [Bragg diffraction](bragg-diffraction.md) condition. Its position changes with specimen tilt.
