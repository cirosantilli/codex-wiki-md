# Restriction of a Hamiltonian group action

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian group action](hamiltonian-group-action.md)

Restriction to a [Lie subgroup](lie-subgroup.md) with [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) inclusion $i:\mathfrak h\to\mathfrak g$ has [moment map](moment-map.md) $i^*\mu$. The [dual map](transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) $i^*$ composes with the original [moment map](moment-map.md).
