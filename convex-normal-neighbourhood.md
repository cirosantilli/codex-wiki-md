# Convex normal neighbourhood

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential map (Riemannian geometry)](exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md)

A convex normal neighbourhood is an open set whose points have unique smoothly varying joining [geodesics](geodesic.md), with the joining geodesic contained in the set. The corresponding star-shaped restriction of each [exponential map](exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md) is a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) onto the neighbourhood. Hence its differential is invertible and no such joining geodesic admits a nonzero [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) vanishing at both endpoints. Every point of a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) has arbitrarily small such neighbourhoods. This is stronger than merely requiring the existence of a minimizing geodesic in the set.
