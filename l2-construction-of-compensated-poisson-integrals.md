# L2 construction of compensated Poisson integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md)

For deterministic square-integrable $f$ and a [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md) of intensity $\nu$, first define the integral for simple functions on disjoint finite-intensity sets. [Independence](independent-random-variables.md) and the equality of [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) mean and [variance](variance-split.md) prove the displayed [isometry](isometry.md). Completion defines the integral for all $f\in L^2(\nu)$, even when $\int|f|d\nu=\infty$. A small-jump compensated [martingale](martingale-split.md) is thereby defined through [convergence in Lp](convergence-in-lp.md), not by subtracting two divergent ordinary integrals.
