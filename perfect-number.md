# Perfect number

↑ **Parent:** [Positive integer](positive-integer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_number)

A [positive integer](positive-integer.md) whose [proper-divisor sum](proper-divisor-sum.md) equals itself; equivalently, its [sum-of-divisors function](sum-of-divisors-function.md) satisfies $\sigma(n)=2n$. For example, $28=1+2+4+7+14$.
