# Scalar surface integral

↑ **Parent:** [Surface integral](surface-integral.md)

A [scalar surface integral](scalar-surface-integral.md) integrates a [scalar field](scalar-field.md) against the unsigned [surface area element](surface-area-element.md). For a regular [parametrization](parametric-equation.md) $\mathbf r(u,v)$, it is $\int f(\mathbf r(u,v))\|\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v\|\,du\,dv$. Reversing orientation does not change it. For example, the [scalar surface integral](scalar-surface-integral.md) of the constant one is the [surface area](surface-area.md); a signed [flux](flux.md) instead integrates the [normal component](normal-component.md) of a [vector field](vector-field.md).
