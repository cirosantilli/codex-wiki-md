# Uniform prior

↑ **Parent:** [Prior probability](prior-probability.md)

A [uniform prior](uniform-prior.md) on a finite-volume parameter region assigns constant [probability](probability.md) density there and zero outside. On $[0,1]$ it is $\operatorname{Beta}(1,1)$, giving a [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) posterior after [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) data. Uniformity depends on the coordinate: a nonlinear reparametrization introduces a Jacobian and generally does not preserve a uniform density.
