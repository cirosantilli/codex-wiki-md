# Unbounded element orders in imaginary quadratic ideal class groups

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md)

For every positive [integer](integer-split.md) $n$, some [imaginary quadratic field](imaginary-quadratic-field.md) has an [ideal class](ideal-class.md) of [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) greater than $n$. Specifically, take a [prime number](prime-number-split.md) $p\equiv1\pmod{20}$ with $p>5^n$. In $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-p}]$, the [Dedekind factorization theorem](dedekind-factorization-theorem.md) gives $(5)=P\overline P$, with distinct [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md) $P=(5,\sqrt{-p}-2)$ and $\overline P=(5,\sqrt{-p}+2)$ of [ideal norm](ideal-norm.md) five. If $P^k=(a+b\sqrt{-p})$ for $1\leq k\leq n$, the [field norm](field-norm.md) equation $5^k=a^2+pb^2$ forces $b=0$. Then $k$ is even and $(a)=(5)^{k/2}=P^{k/2}\overline P^{k/2}$, contradicting [unique factorization of ideals in a number field](unique-factorization-of-ideals-in-a-number-field.md). Thus $[P]$ has [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) greater than $n$. The existence of suitable $p$ follows from [Dirichlet's theorem on arithmetic progressions](dirichlet-s-theorem-on-arithmetic-progressions.md).
