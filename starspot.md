# Starspot

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit photometry](exoplanet-transit-photometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starspot)

A [starspot](starspot.md) is a relatively cool, dark region on a [star](star.md)'s surface associated with a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). [Starspots](starspot.md) change the [light curve](light-curve.md) as the [star](star.md) rotates. A [planet](planet.md) crossing a [starspot](starspot.md) blocks less light than it would from an unspotted region, producing a local upward anomaly during an [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md); unocculted [starspots](starspot.md) can instead deepen the normalized [exoplanet transit depth](exoplanet-transit-depth.md). They can also distort [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) and imitate a [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md) signal.
