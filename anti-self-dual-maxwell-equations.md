# Anti-self-dual Maxwell equations

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum Maxwell equations](vacuum-maxwell-equations.md)

In the [two-component spinor](two-component-spinor.md) convention with primed symmetric [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) self-dual, an [Abelian](abelian-group.md) [Maxwell field](electromagnetic-field.md) is anti-self-dual when its primed symmetric [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) [two-component spinor](two-component-spinor.md) vanishes. For a locally exact field $F=dA$, the [Bianchi identity](bianchi-identity.md) gives $dF=0$; the duality relation then also gives $d{*}F=0$. Thus it obeys the source-free [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations.md). In complexified Lorentzian signature the [Hodge star](hodge-star-operator.md) [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\pm i$; the [two-component spinor](two-component-spinor.md) condition specifies which component is meant without imposing a Euclidean eigenvalue on that signature.
