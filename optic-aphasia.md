# Optic aphasia

↑ **Parent:** [Aphasia](aphasia.md)

A disproportionate naming difficulty for visually presented objects despite evidence of recognition. Nonverbal knowledge and naming through other input routes help distinguish it from [visual agnosia](visual-agnosia.md).
