# Chemical shift

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

A [chemical shift](chemical-shift.md) measures an [NMR](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) resonance position relative to a reference. Electronic environments alter local magnetic shielding, so shifts can report a changed molecular environment without uniquely specifying a structure.
