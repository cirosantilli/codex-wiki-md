# Multiplicativity of the algebraic norm

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic norm](algebraic-norm.md)

For algebraic elements $\alpha$ and $\beta$,

$$
N(\alpha\beta)=N(\alpha)N(\beta).
$$

This follows because multiplication by $\alpha\beta$ is the composition of multiplication by $\beta$ and multiplication by $\alpha$, and determinants are multiplicative.
