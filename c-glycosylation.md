# C-glycosylation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Formation of a [carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) bond at an [anomeric carbon](anomeric-carbon.md). In [electrophilic aromatic substitution](electrophilic-aromatic-substitution.md) on a suitable heteroarene, [carbon](carbon.md) attack on an activated glycosyl donor is followed by [deprotonation](deprotonation.md) to restore [aromaticity](aromaticity.md).
