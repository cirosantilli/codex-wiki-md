# Norbornane

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

A seven-[carbon](carbon.md) bicyclic framework with three paths between its bridgeheads containing two, two and one internal atoms. Mapping these paths is useful when checking a tandem Michael cyclization product.
