# Small-limit preservation is insufficient for the large elements-colimit criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Colimit criterion for representability of a presheaf](colimit-criterion-for-representability-of-a-presheaf.md)

Let $\mathcal C$ be the ordered [category](category-split.md) of all [ordinals](ordinal.md) with an added greatest object $\infty$. The [categorical presheaf](presheaf-category-theory.md) with $X(\alpha)=\{*\}$ at ordinals and $X(\infty)=\varnothing$ preserves every small [categorical limit](categorical-limit.md): a small supremum is an ordinal unless its diagram contains $\infty$. Its [category of elements](category-of-elements.md) consists of all ordinals, and their projection has the large [colimit](colimit.md) $\infty$. Nevertheless $X$ is not [representable](representable-functor.md): an ordinal representer has bounded support, while the representer $\infty$ has a nonempty value at $\infty$. Thus preservation must include the possibly large opposite elements diagram. The dual functor $X^{\mathrm{op}}:\mathcal C\to\mathbf{Set}^{\mathrm{op}}$ also disproves the comma-projection criterion for a right adjoint under small-colimit preservation alone.
