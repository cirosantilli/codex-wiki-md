# Density gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Density stratification](density-stratification.md)

The density gradient is the [gradient](gradient.md) of a spatial [mass density](density.md) field. With an upward vertical coordinate in the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), the squared [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) is $N^2=-(g/\rho_*)\partial_z\rho_0$. A negative vertical density gradient therefore gives [stable density stratification](stable-density-stratification.md).
