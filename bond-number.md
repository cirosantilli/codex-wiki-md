# Bond number

↑ **Parent:** [Surface tension](surface-tension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bond_number)

The Bond number compares hydrostatic stress over a length $\ell$ with capillary stress. For a gas bubble of radius $a$ and negligible density, $\mathrm{Bo}=\rho ga^2/\gamma$. Small Bond number is the usual sufficient criterion for capillarity to resist gravity-scale deformations. For the clean-bubble terminal speed, the [capillary number](capillary-number.md) is $\mathrm{Ca}=\mu U/\gamma=\mathrm{Bo}/3$.
