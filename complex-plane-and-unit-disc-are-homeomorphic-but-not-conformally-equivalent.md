# Complex plane and unit disc are homeomorphic but not conformally equivalent

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal equivalence](conformal-equivalence.md)

The radial map $z\mapsto z/(1+|z|)$ is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) from $\mathbb C$ to the unit disc, with inverse $w\mapsto w/(1-|w|)$. A conformal equivalence in that direction would be a nonconstant bounded entire function, contradicting the [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md).
