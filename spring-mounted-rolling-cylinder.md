# Spring-mounted rolling cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

Attach a [spring](spring.md) of [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k$ to the axle of a round body of [mass](mass.md) $m$, radius $R$ and axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$. Assume [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md), a horizontal [drag force](drag-physics.md) $-b\dot x$ applied through the axle, and no additional resisting [torque](torque.md). The translational [force](force-split.md) equation is $m\ddot x=-kx-b\dot x+f$, where $f$ is signed [static friction](static-friction.md). The rotational equation and rolling constraint give $f=-I\ddot x/R^2$, yielding the displayed equation. Equivalently, the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $(m+I/R^2)\dot x^2/2$. This added effective inertia lowers the undamped [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) to $\sqrt{k/(m+I/R^2)}$. A distributed viscous [torque](torque.md) would add a separate damping term and cannot be inferred from the drag force alone.
