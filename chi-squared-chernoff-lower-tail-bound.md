# Chi-squared Chernoff lower-tail bound

↑ **Parent:** [Chi-squared concentration inequality](chi-squared-concentration-inequality.md)

For $V\sim\chi_n^2$ and $0<a<1$, apply the [Chernoff bound](chernoff-bound.md) to $e^{-sV}$ using $\mathbb E e^{-sV}=(1+2s)^{-n/2}$. Optimization at $s=(a^{-1}-1)/2$ proves the displayed estimate. It remains useful when the additive lower threshold in the [chi-squared concentration inequality](chi-squared-concentration-inequality.md) is nonpositive. For $0<r\leq1$, choosing $a=e^{-4r}$ gives a tail at most $e^{-nr^2}$ because $e^{-4r}-1+4r\geq2r^2$.
