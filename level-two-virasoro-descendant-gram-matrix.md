# Level-two Virasoro descendant Gram matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro Verma module](virasoro-verma-module.md)

For a normalized [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md) with $L_0|h\rangle=h|h\rangle$, $L_{n>0}|h\rangle=0$ and $L_n^\dagger=L_{-n}$, the level-two descendants $L_{-2}|h\rangle,L_{-1}^2|h\rangle$ have the displayed [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md). It follows from the [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md): $\langle L_2L_{-2}\rangle=4h+c/2$, $\langle L_2L_{-1}^2\rangle=6h$ and $\langle L_1^2L_{-1}^2\rangle=4h(2h+1)$. At $h=-1,c=26$ its null direction is $(1,3/2)$, giving the [level-two scalar Virasoro null state](level-two-scalar-virasoro-null-state.md). Nullity of a descendant is a metric statement; satisfying all physical constraints must be checked separately.
