# Finitely generated subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Subgroup](subgroup.md)

A subgroup is finitely generated when finitely many of its elements generate it as a [group](group-split.md). Even inside a [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md), subgroups need not be finitely generated. Finite generation of a witness subgroup, rather than of the original intersection, is what makes centralizing [HNN extensions](hnn-extension.md) usable for a [benign subgroup](benign-subgroup.md).
