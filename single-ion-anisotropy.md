# Single-ion anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Spin model](spin-model-split.md)

Single-ion anisotropy is an orientation-dependent local [spin](spin.md) energy, commonly $D(S_j^z)^2$. It differs from a linear Zeeman [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) term. For a classical planar [spin](spin.md), positive $D$ penalizes the out-of-plane component; for a spin-half quantum degree of freedom this particular square is constant.
