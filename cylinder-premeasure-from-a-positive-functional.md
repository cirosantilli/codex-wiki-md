# Cylinder premeasure from a positive functional

↑ **Parent:** [Premeasure](premeasure.md)

A normalized [positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md) on $C(\Omega)$ for [Cantor space](cantor-space.md) gives a finitely additive set function on the algebra of [clopen sets](clopen-set.md). If a countable disjoint union of such sets is itself clopen, [compactness](compact-space.md) gives a finite subcover; all other terms are empty. Finite additivity therefore gives the required [countable additivity](countable-additivity.md), making $\ell$ a [premeasure](premeasure.md). The [Caratheodory extension theorem](caratheodory-s-extension-theorem.md) produces a unique [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md).
