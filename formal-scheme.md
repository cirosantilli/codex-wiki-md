# Formal scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_scheme)

A [formal scheme](formal-scheme.md) is a [locally ringed space](locally-ringed-space.md) with a [sheaf of topological rings](sheaf-of-topological-rings.md) that is locally a [formal spectrum](formal-spectrum.md) of a complete adic [ring](ring.md). An ordinary [scheme](scheme.md) is the special case of the discrete topology. A [formal completion of a scheme](formal-completion-of-a-scheme.md) retains all [infinitesimal neighbourhoods](infinitesimal-neighbourhood-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) of a [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md), rather than just that subscheme.
