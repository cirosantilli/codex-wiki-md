# Weakly mixing measure-preserving transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodicity](ergodicity.md)

A probability [measure-preserving transformation](measure-preserving-transformation.md) $T$ is weakly mixing when, for every pair of measurable sets $A,B$, the average absolute correlation discrepancy tends to zero:

$$
\frac1N\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}|\mu(T^{-n}A\cap B)-\mu(A)\mu(B)|\longrightarrow0.
$$

Equivalently, the correlations converge to their products in [convergence in density of a sequence](convergence-in-density-of-a-sequence.md), its [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) with itself is an [ergodic transformation](ergodicity.md), or its product with every [ergodic transformation](ergodicity.md) is ergodic. The absolute values are essential: signed [Cesaro convergence of a sequence](cesaro-convergence-of-a-sequence.md) of the correlations to their products characterizes [ergodic transformations](ergodicity.md) and does not imply weak mixing. For example, the transformation interchanging two equally weighted points has discrepancy $(-1)^n/4$ for a singleton $A=B$: its signed average tends to zero while its average absolute discrepancy stays $1/4$.
