# Equal-angle elastic scattering with unequal masses

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

Equal laboratory recoil angles constrain outgoing [momenta](momentum-split.md), not equality of masses. In units $c=1$, take an incoming mass $m$ with [energy](energy-split.md) $2m$ striking a stationary mass $2m$. The final [momenta](momentum-split.md) $m(13/8,\sqrt3/2,\sqrt{57}/8,0)$ and $m(19/8,\sqrt3/2,-\sqrt{57}/8,0)$ conserve [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) and have mass shells $m^2$ and $4m^2$. Both trajectories make angle $\arctan(\sqrt{19}/4)$ with the incident axis. This is a concrete counterexample to inferring equal masses from equal outgoing angles alone.
