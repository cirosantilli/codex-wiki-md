↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**The defensible core of [William Odling](../../../../../william-odling.md)'s statement is a transfer of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md): sunlight helps build reduced food material, and its subsequent [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) can supply animal [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) and [work](../../../../../work.md).** Here solar force means solar [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), and [eremacausis](../../../../../eremacausis.md) means slow [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md), rather than a special vital agent. The historical wording, from his 1866 lectures, must be qualified where it identifies the fuel with the substance of the working [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) or contrasts plant and animal [metabolism](../../../../../metabolism-split.md) too absolutely.[Lecture IV, discussion of solar energy and animal oxidation; see also the concluding recapitulation](../../../../../lectures-on-animal-chemistry-by-william-odling.md)

In [oxygenic photosynthesis](../../../../../oxygenic-photosynthesis.md), light absorbed by [chlorophyll](../../../../../chlorophyll.md) supports conversion of [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and [water](../../../../../water.md) into reduced organic material, with release of [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). An overall account, written for one [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) unit, is

$$
6\mathrm{CO_2}+6\mathrm{H_2O}+\text{light energy}
\longrightarrow \mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6}+6\mathrm{O_2}.
$$

This summarizes several stages, rather than describing a single elementary [chemical reaction](../../../../../chemical-reaction-split.md). The evolved [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) comes from [water](../../../../../water.md). The product can furnish [starch](../../../../../starch.md), other [carbohydrates](../../../../../carbohydrate.md) and precursors for [fat](../../../../../fat.md) and [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md); the latter additionally requires a source of [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md). A human obtains these materials directly from plants or through animals that have eaten plants. Thus a grain meal and the flesh of a grain-fed animal can trace much of their chemical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to the same original sunlight.[Main Structures and Summary of Photosynthesis; photosynthetic products and their passage through food chains](../../../../../biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/8-1-overview-of-photosynthesis.md)

The reverse overall balance for complete aerobic utilization of [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) is

$$
\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6}+6\mathrm{O_2}
\longrightarrow 6\mathrm{CO_2}+6\mathrm{H_2O}+\text{released energy}.
$$

Similarly a representative [fatty acid](../../../../../fatty-acid.md), [palmitic acid](../../../../../palmitic-acid.md), has the complete [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) balance

$$
\mathrm{C_{16}H_{32}O_2}+23\mathrm{O_2}
\longrightarrow16\mathrm{CO_2}+16\mathrm{H_2O}+\text{released energy}.
$$

Both equations conserve each chemical element. The relevant [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) difference belongs to the complete set of reactants and products, including [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md); merely breaking a bond does not supply net [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). In modern terms, controlled [cellular respiration](../../../../../cellular-respiration.md) couples part of the available chemical [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) to [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) formation. [ATP hydrolysis](../../../../../atp-hydrolysis.md) can then support [muscle contraction](../../../../../muscle-contraction.md), active transport and [biosynthesis](../../../../../biosynthesis.md); some [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is transferred to [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md). The process occurs through enzyme-mediated steps, without a flame, although complete combustion in a [calorimeter](../../../../../calorimeter.md) can help measure the corresponding chemical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) change.

[Glands](../../../../../gland.md) and [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) continually replace constituents, and their [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) can be degraded. [Amino acids](../../../../../amino-acid.md) can contribute carbon skeletons to [energy metabolism](../../../../../energy-metabolism.md), while their [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) is disposed of largely as [urea](../../../../../urea.md). But ordinary [muscle contraction](../../../../../muscle-contraction.md) does not require proportional destruction and rebuilding of the contractile [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) at every stroke. [Carbohydrates](../../../../../carbohydrate.md) and [fats](../../../../../fat.md) are major fuels for [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md), and a [gland](../../../../../gland.md) can use those fuels to support secretion. The historical picture of burning the organ's own substance is therefore an inadequate general explanation.[Protein as metabolic fuel; amino-acid carbon skeletons and the urea cycle](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/24-4-protein-metabolism.md)

This correction was already an experimental issue before the exam. [Edward Frankland](../../../../../edward-frankland.md)'s 1866 discussion compared the mechanical [work](../../../../../work.md) of climbing with the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) that could be assigned to [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) destruction estimated from urinary [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md). The latter was insufficient to account for all the [work](../../../../../work.md). His combustion measurements also distinguished the [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) from burning a sample completely from the smaller amount available when nitrogenous material leaves the animal as still-combustible [urea](../../../../../urea.md). Measuring external [work](../../../../../work.md), urinary products and calorific values gives the hypothesis a quantitative test; it is more informative than simply pointing out that an active animal gets warm.[pp. 394–395, criticism of tissue-consumption doctrine; pp. 401–406, calorific measurements and comparison with climbing work, 1866](../../../../../on-the-source-of-muscular-power-by-edward-frankland.md)

Finally, plants themselves carry out [cellular respiration](../../../../../cellular-respiration.md), and not every ecosystem obtains its input from sunlight: [chemolithotrophy](../../../../../chemolithotrophy.md) is an alternative. The plant-animal contrast is useful for following [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) through an ordinary food chain, but it is not an exhaustive division of biochemical capabilities. [Conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) also requires the bookkeeping form

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm food}=W_{\rm external}+Q_{\rm outward}+\Delta E_{\rm body}}
$$

for an idealized interval in which $E_{\rm food}$ is the metabolizable food input, with energy lost in excreta already subtracted and other material-energy exchanges neglected. Here $W_{\rm external}$ is external [work](../../../../../work.md), $Q_{\rm outward}$ is net outward [heat transfer](../../../../../heat-transfer.md), and $\Delta E_{\rm body}$ includes changes in stored chemical and other [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). The living body transforms [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md); it does not create it.
