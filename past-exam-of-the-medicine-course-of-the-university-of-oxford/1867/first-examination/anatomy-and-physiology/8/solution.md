<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

**[Entozoa](../../../../../entozoa.md) is a historical grouping of internal animal [parasites](../../../../../parasite.md), especially parasitic worms; it is not a single natural modern [clade](../../../../../clade.md).** The core human examples belong principally to the [flatworms](../../../../../flatworm.md), including [Cestoda](../../../../../cestoda.md) and [Trematoda](../../../../../trematoda.md), and to the [roundworms](../../../../../nematoda.md), or [Nematoda](../../../../../nematoda.md). The distinction is structural and developmental, not merely between inhabitants of the [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md) and inhabitants of other organs.

The nineteenth-century higher arrangement was unsettled. Schemes placed these animals within [Vermes](../../../../../vermes.md), or within annuloid assemblages that included other supposedly related simple invertebrates. For a specifically pre-exam example, the 1865 geological-museum classification put [Entozoa](../../../../../entozoa.md) in [Annuloida](../../../../../annuloida.md), within [Annulosa](../../../../../annulosa.md). These historical groups must not be equated with the modern [Annelida](../../../../../annelida.md): an [Ascaris lumbricoides](../../../../../ascaris-lumbricoides.md) is not an earthworm. A more useful subdivision for this answer is the four orders treated in [Thomas Spencer Cobbold](../../../../../thomas-spencer-cobbold.md)'s 1864 [Entozoa, an Introduction to the Study of Helminthology by Thomas Spencer Cobbold](../../../../../entozoa-an-introduction-to-the-study-of-helminthology-by-thomas-spencer-cobbold.md): [Trematoda](../../../../../trematoda.md), [Nematoda](../../../../../nematoda.md), [Acanthocephala](../../../../../acanthocephala.md) and [Cestoda](../../../../../cestoda.md).[p. lxxvi, classification table: Annulosa, Annuloida, Entozoa](../../../../../catalogue-of-the-collection-of-fossils-in-the-museum-of-practical-geology-1865.md)[contents and order headings: Trematoda p. 14, Nematoda, Acanthocephala p. 97, Cestoda p. 104](../../../../../entozoa-an-introduction-to-the-study-of-helminthology-by-thomas-spencer-cobbold.md)

The leading distinctions are as follows:

- [Cestoda](../../../../../cestoda.md), the [tapeworms](../../../../../cestoda.md), have a flattened adult body with an attachment [scolex](../../../../../scolex.md) and, in the familiar human forms, a chain of reproductive [proglottids](../../../../../proglottid.md). They lack an alimentary canal and absorb nutrients across the [tegument](../../../../../tegument.md). The [proglottids](../../../../../proglottid.md) are repeated reproductive units, not annelid segments. Typical development passes through an egg and one or more larval stages, commonly in an [intermediate host](../../../../../intermediate-host.md), before the adult reaches a [definitive host](../../../../../definitive-host.md). A bladder-like [cysticercus](../../../../../cysticercus.md) and its adult [tapeworm](../../../../../cestoda.md) are stages of one life cycle.
- [Trematoda](../../../../../trematoda.md), the [flukes](../../../../../trematoda.md), have flattened unsegmented bodies, attachment suckers and a mouth leading to a usually branched, blind-ending [digestive system](../../../../../digestive-system.md). Most are [hermaphrodites](../../../../../hermaphrodite.md), but [Schistosoma](../../../../../schistosoma.md) has separate sexes. Typical human-infecting [flukes](../../../../../trematoda.md) use a molluscan [intermediate host](../../../../../intermediate-host.md), undergo multiplication in larval stages there, and produce dispersing [cercariae](../../../../../cercaria.md). The route from that stage into the human varies by species; there is no universal requirement to swallow an adult worm.
- [Nematoda](../../../../../nematoda.md) have cylindrical unsegmented bodies, a resistant [cuticle](../../../../../cuticle.md), a fluid-filled [pseudocoelom](../../../../../pseudocoelom.md), longitudinal [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) and a complete alimentary canal with mouth and anus. The sexes are usually separate. Development includes [moults](../../../../../moult.md); some life cycles are direct, while others need a vector or another host. Human infection may follow swallowing infective eggs, penetration by larvae, ingestion of tissue larvae or transmission by a vector, depending on the species.
- [Acanthocephala](../../../../../acanthocephala.md), the thorny-headed worms, possess a retractile hooked [proboscis](../../../../../proboscis.md) and lack an alimentary canal. Their development normally involves an arthropod [intermediate host](../../../../../intermediate-host.md). They belong in a general survey of historical [Entozoa](../../../../../entozoa.md), but are not among the usual common human intestinal worms.

For a British medical answer of this period, the ordinary intestinal list should begin with the following species; the modern life-cycle details clarify their identities rather than claiming that every step was already established in 1867:

- [Ascaris lumbricoides](../../../../../ascaris-lumbricoides.md), the large intestinal [roundworm](../../../../../nematoda.md). Adults inhabit the [small intestine](../../../../../small-intestine.md); infective eggs are acquired from the environment, and larvae migrate through the [liver](../../../../../liver.md) and [lungs](../../../../../lung.md) before returning to mature in the [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md).[Causal Agents; Life Cycle](../../../../../dpdx-ascariasis.md)
- [Enterobius vermicularis](../../../../../enterobius-vermicularis.md), historically commonly named [Oxyuris vermicularis](../../../../../enterobius-vermicularis.md), the small pinworm or threadworm. Adults chiefly occupy the caecal region; females deposit eggs on the perianal [skin](../../../../../skin.md), explaining spread through contaminated hands and objects and the usefulness of perianal sampling rather than reliance on ordinary stool samples.[Causal Agent; Life Cycle; Laboratory Diagnosis](../../../../../dpdx-enterobiasis.md)
- [Trichuris trichiura](../../../../../trichuris-trichiura.md), historically [Trichocephalus dispar](../../../../../trichuris-trichiura.md), the whipworm. The thin anterior end is embedded in the caecal or colonic mucosa; eggs passed in faeces must develop outside the host before becoming infective.[Causal Agent; Life Cycle](../../../../../dpdx-trichuriasis.md)
- [Taenia solium](../../../../../taenia-solium.md), the pork [tapeworm](../../../../../cestoda.md), with an armed [scolex](../../../../../scolex.md), and [Taenia saginata](../../../../../taenia-saginata.md), the beef [tapeworm](../../../../../cestoda.md), historically often [Taenia mediocanellata](../../../../../taenia-saginata.md), with an unarmed [scolex](../../../../../scolex.md). Intestinal adults follow ingestion of tissue [cysticerci](../../../../../cysticercus.md) in infected meat. Their eggs alone cannot reliably distinguish the two species by ordinary [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md).[Causal Agents; Life Cycle; Taenia spp. scoleces; Laboratory Diagnosis](../../../../../dpdx-taeniasis.md)
- The broad fish [tapeworm](../../../../../cestoda.md), historically [Bothriocephalus latus](../../../../../dibothriocephalus-latus.md), later [Diphyllobothrium latum](../../../../../dibothriocephalus-latus.md), now [Dibothriocephalus latus](../../../../../dibothriocephalus-latus.md). It is especially relevant in freshwater-fish-eating populations and has a life cycle involving a copepod and fish before the human definitive stage. Its attachment organs are longitudinal grooves, rather than the four rounded suckers typical of [Taenia](../../../../../taenia.md).[Causal Agents; Life Cycle](../../../../../dpdx-diphyllobothriasis.md)

Important tissue examples extend the answer beyond intestinal adults. The historical [Trichina spiralis](../../../../../trichinella-spiralis.md) is [Trichinella spiralis](../../../../../trichinella-spiralis.md): ingested infected meat carries larvae, adults develop in the [small intestine](../../../../../small-intestine.md), and new larvae migrate to [skeletal muscle](../../../../../skeletal-muscle.md). This is a distinct developmental pattern from the egg-acquired intestinal [Ascaris lumbricoides](../../../../../ascaris-lumbricoides.md) infection.[Causal Agents; Life Cycle](../../../../../dpdx-trichinellosis.md) Human hydatids are the larval [hydatid cysts](../../../../../hydatid-cyst.md) of [Echinococcus](../../../../../echinococcus.md), classically the [Echinococcus granulosus](../../../../../echinococcus-granulosus.md) complex; the small adult [tapeworm](../../../../../cestoda.md) is in a canid, while humans accidentally occupy the [intermediate host](../../../../../intermediate-host.md) role, commonly with [liver](../../../../../liver.md) or [lung](../../../../../lung.md) cysts.[Causal Agents; Life Cycle, Cystic Echinococcosis](../../../../../dpdx-echinococcosis.md)

A crucial distinction is that **human [Taenia solium](../../../../../taenia-solium.md) [cysticercosis](../../../../../cysticercosis.md) follows ingestion of eggs; eating pork containing [cysticerci](../../../../../cysticercus.md) produces intestinal [taeniasis](../../../../../taeniasis.md)**. The historical name [Cysticercus cellulosae](../../../../../cysticercus-cellulosae.md) designates a larval form, rather than an additional independent species to add to the list.[Causal Agent; Life Cycle](../../../../../dpdx-cysticercosis.md)

Geographical qualification matters: [Ancylostoma duodenale](../../../../../ancylostoma-duodenale.md), [Dracunculus medinensis](../../../../../dracunculus-medinensis.md) and [Schistosoma haematobium](../../../../../schistosoma-haematobium.md) are important in their endemic settings; [Fasciola hepatica](../../../../../fasciola-hepatica.md) is a familiar liver [fluke](../../../../../trematoda.md) but is not an ordinary common human worm in Britain. An enumeration should not turn this into a claim that all named species are equally common in every population.[Causal Agents; Geographic Distribution](../../../../../dpdx-intestinal-hookworm.md)[Causal Agent; Geographic Distribution](../../../../../dpdx-dracunculiasis.md)[Causal Agents; Geographic Distribution](../../../../../dpdx-schistosomiasis-infection.md)[Causal Agents; Geographic Distribution](../../../../../dpdx-fascioliasis.md) Conversely, calling only the familiar intestinal adults [Entozoa](../../../../../entozoa.md) would omit the instructive tissue stages and their transmission.

The decisive experimental insight is continuity of development. Comparing eggs, tissue larvae and adults, and tracing transfer between appropriate hosts, demonstrates a life cycle rather than spontaneous generation within a patient. In diagnostic material, an adult attachment organ, a [proglottid](../../../../../proglottid.md), an egg and a tissue cyst answer different identification questions. Identification therefore depends on the stage and the material actually supplied.
