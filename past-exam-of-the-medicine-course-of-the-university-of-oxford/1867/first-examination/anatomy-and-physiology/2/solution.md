**The two [pneumogastric nerves](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md) are the right and left [vagus nerves](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md), the tenth [cranial nerves](../../../../../cranial-nerve.md): mixed sensory, branchial motor and [parasympathetic](../../../../../parasympathetic.md) pathways from the [medulla oblongata](../../../../../medulla-oblongata.md) to the neck, [thorax](../../../../../thorax.md) and abdomen.** A useful description separates their apparent origin at the surface of the [brain](../../../../../brain.md) from the origins and terminations of their component fibres.

Rootlets emerge in the groove behind the [inferior olive](../../../../../inferior-olive.md), between the [glossopharyngeal nerve](../../../../../glossopharyngeal-nerve.md) above and the accessory rootlets below. Branchial motor fibres arise chiefly in the [nucleus ambiguus](../../../../../nucleus-ambiguus.md); preganglionic [parasympathetic](../../../../../parasympathetic.md) fibres arise in the [dorsal motor nucleus of the vagus](../../../../../dorsal-motor-nucleus-of-the-vagus.md) and, particularly for cardiac control, the [nucleus ambiguus](../../../../../nucleus-ambiguus.md). Visceral sensory fibres have their cell bodies chiefly in the [inferior vagal ganglion](../../../../../inferior-vagal-ganglion.md) and terminate centrally in the [nucleus of the solitary tract](../../../../../nucleus-of-the-solitary-tract.md); somatic sensory fibres associated with the [superior vagal ganglion](../../../../../superior-vagal-ganglion.md) project to the [spinal trigeminal nucleus](../../../../../spinal-trigeminal-nucleus.md). Consequently a sensory fibre is not accurately described as originating in a motor nucleus simply because its [peripheral nerve](../../../../../peripheral-nerve.md) emerges nearby.[Introduction and Structure and Function: branchial motor output and cardiac vagal control](../../../../../neuroanatomy-nucleus-ambiguus-statpearls.md)

Each [vagus nerve](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md) leaves the [skull](../../../../../skull.md) through the [jugular foramen](../../../../../jugular-foramen.md), with the [glossopharyngeal nerve](../../../../../glossopharyngeal-nerve.md) and [accessory nerve](../../../../../accessory-nerve.md). It bears the small [superior vagal ganglion](../../../../../superior-vagal-ganglion.md) at the foramen and the larger [inferior vagal ganglion](../../../../../inferior-vagal-ganglion.md) below it. Connections include communications with adjacent [cranial nerves](../../../../../cranial-nerve.md) and the cervical [sympathetic nervous system](../../../../../sympathetic-nervous-system.md). Traditional descriptions assign fibres joining the [vagus nerve](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md) from the cranial part of the [accessory nerve](../../../../../accessory-nerve.md) to the latter [peripheral nerve](../../../../../peripheral-nerve.md); their modern classification is more nuanced and does not change the peripheral branches being described. In the neck the [vagus nerve](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md) descends in the [carotid sheath](../../../../../carotid-sheath.md), behind and between the [internal jugular vein](../../../../../internal-jugular-vein.md) and the internal, then common, [carotid artery](../../../../../carotid-artery.md).

Its meningeal and auricular branches supply small sensory territories of the cranial covering and external ear. Pharyngeal branches contribute to the [pharyngeal plexus](../../../../../pharyngeal-plexus.md), supplying much of the [soft palate](../../../../../soft-palate.md) and [pharynx](../../../../../pharynx.md); the [stylopharyngeus muscle](../../../../../stylopharyngeus-muscle.md) belongs to the [glossopharyngeal nerve](../../../../../glossopharyngeal-nerve.md) and the [tensor veli palatini muscle](../../../../../tensor-veli-palatini-muscle.md) to the mandibular division of the [trigeminal nerve](../../../../../trigeminal-nerve.md). The internal branch of the [superior laryngeal nerve](../../../../../superior-laryngeal-nerve.md) supplies sensation above the [vocal folds](../../../../../vocal-fold.md); its external branch supplies the [cricothyroid muscle](../../../../../cricothyroid-muscle.md). The [recurrent laryngeal nerve](../../../../../recurrent-laryngeal-nerve.md) supplies the other intrinsic [laryngeal muscles](../../../../../laryngeal-muscle.md) and sensation below the [vocal folds](../../../../../vocal-fold.md). The right [recurrent laryngeal nerve](../../../../../recurrent-laryngeal-nerve.md) loops beneath the [right subclavian artery](../../../../../right-subclavian-artery.md); the left loops under the [aortic arch](../../../../../aortic-arch.md) beside the [ligamentum arteriosum](../../../../../ligamentum-arteriosum.md). Both return towards the [larynx](../../../../../larynx.md) near the groove between [trachea](../../../../../trachea.md) and [oesophagus](../../../../../oesophagus.md).[Structure and Function; Vagus Nerve Branches; Muscles](../../../../../neuroanatomy-cranial-nerve-10-vagus-nerve-statpearls.md)

In the [thorax](../../../../../thorax.md), both [vagus nerves](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md) pass behind the roots of the [lungs](../../../../../lung.md), contrasting with the anterior course of the [phrenic nerves](../../../../../phrenic-nerve.md). Cardiac branches join the [cardiac plexus](../../../../../cardiac-plexus.md); pulmonary branches join the [pulmonary plexus](../../../../../pulmonary-plexus.md). The trunks break up into the [oesophageal plexus](../../../../../oesophageal-plexus.md), then collect principally as an anterior trunk derived from the left [vagus nerve](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md) and a posterior trunk derived from the right. Through the [oesophageal hiatus](../../../../../oesophageal-hiatus.md) they reach the [stomach](../../../../../stomach.md); hepatic and coeliac branches distribute to abdominal viscera. Their [parasympathetic](../../../../../parasympathetic.md) territory includes foregut and midgut derivatives, extending into the proximal [colon](../../../../../colon.md), rather than every abdominal and pelvic organ.[Structure and Function: thoracic course and oesophageal plexus](../../../../../neuroanatomy-cranial-nerve-10-vagus-nerve-statpearls.md) This extensive distribution explains why the level of experimental section is indispensable information.

The main consequences of section can be organized by the interrupted functions:

- [Heart](../../../../../heart.md): cutting both cervical [vagus nerves](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md) removes tonic inhibition and usually increases [heart rate](../../../../../heart-rate.md). The [heart](../../../../../heart.md) continues beating because its rhythm is intrinsic. Electrical stimulation of the peripheral cut end can slow the [heart](../../../../../heart.md), whereas stimulation of the central end tests reflex effects through the [brainstem](../../../../../brainstem.md). Cutting a pathway and stimulating its intact output are different experiments.
- [Breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md): bilateral cervical section commonly produces slower, deeper breaths in experimental mammals. Sensory information from the [lungs](../../../../../lung.md) has been interrupted; the [respiratory diaphragm](../../../../../respiratory-diaphragm.md) is not directly paralysed, because its motor [peripheral nerves](../../../../../peripheral-nerve.md) are the [phrenic nerves](../../../../../phrenic-nerve.md). Persistence of [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) establishes that the [vagus nerves](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md) modify the respiratory rhythm rather than being its sole generator. The subsequently named [Hering-Breuer inflation reflex](../../../../../hering-breuer-inflation-reflex.md) provides a later explanation of one component of this feedback; its formal description belongs to 1868, after this examination.[Discussion of the 1868 lung inflation and deflation experiments](../../../../../reflexes-from-the-lungs-and-airways-historical-perspective.md)
- [Larynx](../../../../../larynx.md) and [lungs](../../../../../lung.md): section proximal to the [recurrent laryngeal nerves](../../../../../recurrent-laryngeal-nerve.md) can impair opening and closure of the [vocal folds](../../../../../vocal-fold.md), causing altered voice, obstruction and impaired airway protection. Classical bilateral cervical experiments sometimes ended in death with pulmonary congestion, [oedema](../../../../../oedema.md) or [aspiration](../../../../../aspiration.md) changes. Such outcomes must be distinguished from an immediate necessary arrest of [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md): species, age, lesion level and airway management affect them.
- [Stomach](../../../../../stomach.md): bilateral section can reduce vagally driven [gastric acid secretion](../../../../../gastric-acid-secretion.md) and disturb gastric motor coordination and emptying. Nevertheless [digestion](../../../../../digestion.md) and [nutrient absorption](../../../../../nutrient-absorption.md) are not abolished as chemical possibilities: [enteric nervous system](../../../../../enteric-nervous-system.md) activity, local stimuli and [hormones](../../../../../hormone.md) remain. Likewise, interruption of gastric sensory fibres does not establish that the entire experience of hunger originates in them.

A discriminating historical experiment would record respiratory movements and [heart rate](../../../../../heart-rate.md) before and after section, distinguish unilateral from bilateral section, and compare an intact airway with an airway bypass. A [tracheal cannula](../../../../../tracheal-cannula.md) can separate obstruction at the [glottis](../../../../../glottis.md) from changes in the respiratory rhythm, while the central-versus-peripheral stimulation comparison separates incoming reflex traffic from outgoing motor effects. Later separate-lung recordings confirmed that a change following unilateral section could affect both [lungs](../../../../../lung.md), supporting a central reflex interpretation rather than simple local paralysis.[Summary and Conclusions, points 1–4; separate recordings of the two lungs, 1927](../../../../../a-study-of-the-hering-breuer-reflex.md) The gastric distinction is also borne out by measurements of persistent acidity after surgical [vagotomy](../../../../../vagotomy.md), despite major reductions in stimulated [gastric acid secretion](../../../../../gastric-acid-secretion.md).[Abstract: hourly intragastric sampling, seven patients, four years after operation](../../../../../twenty-four-hour-gastric-acidity-after-vagotomy.md)
