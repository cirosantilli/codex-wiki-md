**The [liver](../../../../../liver.md) is both a metabolic processing organ and the producer of [bile](../../../../../bile.md).** These functions must be distinguished: much of what the [liver](../../../../../liver.md) contributes to the body enters the [blood](../../../../../blood.md), whereas [bile](../../../../../bile.md) is its secretion into the [digestive system](../../../../../digestive-system.md).

[Portal venous blood](../../../../../portal-venous-blood.md) brings absorbed materials from the alimentary organs to the [liver](../../../../../liver.md), while the [hepatic artery](../../../../../hepatic-artery.md) supplies oxygenated [blood](../../../../../blood.md). Within the [hepatic sinusoids](../../../../../hepatic-sinusoid.md), the incoming [blood](../../../../../blood.md) encounters [hepatocytes](../../../../../hepatocyte.md) and scavenging [Kupffer cells](../../../../../kupffer-cell.md). [Hepatocytes](../../../../../hepatocyte.md) take up, transform, store and release substances; their [bile canaliculi](../../../../../bile-canaliculi.md) conduct [bile](../../../../../bile.md) towards the [bile ducts](../../../../../bile-duct.md). The organ's position therefore makes it a useful regulator between absorption from a meal and supply to the rest of the body.

Its main non-biliary functions include the following:

- [Carbohydrate metabolism](../../../../../carbohydrate-metabolism.md): after a meal, [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) can be stored as [glycogen](../../../../../glycogen.md) by [glycogenesis](../../../../../glycogenesis.md); during fasting, [glycogenolysis](../../../../../glycogenolysis.md) and [gluconeogenesis](../../../../../gluconeogenesis.md) help maintain [blood glucose](../../../../../blood-sugar.md). The [liver](../../../../../liver.md) therefore both removes and supplies [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md), depending on the physiological state.
- [Lipid metabolism](../../../../../lipid-metabolism.md): the [liver](../../../../../liver.md) handles [fatty acids](../../../../../fatty-acid.md), synthesizes and exports [triglycerides](../../../../../triglyceride.md) in [lipoproteins](../../../../../lipoprotein.md), makes [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md), and can produce [ketone bodies](../../../../../ketone-body.md) during fasting. Storage of [fat](../../../../../fat.md) is not its sole function in this domain.
- [Amino acid metabolism](../../../../../amino-acid-metabolism.md): [transamination](../../../../../transamination.md) and [deamination](../../../../../deamination.md) redistribute or remove amino groups; the [urea cycle](../../../../../urea-cycle.md) converts much potentially toxic [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) nitrogen into [urea](../../../../../urea.md) for excretion by the [kidneys](../../../../../kidney.md). The [liver](../../../../../liver.md) synthesizes major [plasma proteins](../../../../../plasma-protein.md), including [albumin](../../../../../albumin.md) and many [coagulation factors](../../../../../coagulation-factor.md).
- Storage and regulation: stores include [glycogen](../../../../../glycogen.md), several [vitamins](../../../../../vitamin.md) and [iron](../../../../../iron.md). The [liver](../../../../../liver.md) also modifies many [hormones](../../../../../hormone.md) and administered chemicals. Such [biotransformation](../../../../../biotransformation.md) can aid elimination, but the word detoxification must not imply that every product is less toxic than its precursor.
- Clearance and development: [Kupffer cells](../../../../../kupffer-cell.md) remove material from the incoming [blood](../../../../../blood.md) by [phagocytosis](../../../../../phagocytosis.md). The fetal [liver](../../../../../liver.md) is an important site of [haematopoiesis](../../../../../haematopoiesis.md); this is a developmental function, rather than the ordinary principal source of adult [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md).

This modern list extends beyond the knowledge and biochemical vocabulary of 1867. It is consistent with the organ's combined metabolic, storage and clearance roles, rather than treating [bile](../../../../../bile.md) production as the whole explanation.[Introduction; Mechanism; Related Testing: metabolic, synthetic, storage and clearance functions](../../../../../physiology-liver-statpearls.md)

[Bile](../../../../../bile.md) contains [water](../../../../../water.md), [electrolytes](../../../../../electrolyte.md), [bile salts](../../../../../bile-salt.md), [phospholipids](../../../../../phospholipid.md), [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md) and [bilirubin](../../../../../bilirubin.md) conjugates. [Hepatocytes](../../../../../hepatocyte.md) form the primary secretion; ductal [epithelium](../../../../../epithelium.md) modifies it. The [gallbladder](../../../../../gallbladder.md) stores and concentrates [bile](../../../../../bile.md) between meals and releases it into the [duodenum](../../../../../duodenum.md). Production of [bile](../../../../../bile.md) must therefore be distinguished from its intermittent delivery to a meal.

Its two central functions are **assistance with [lipid digestion](../../../../../lipid-digestion.md) and [nutrient absorption](../../../../../nutrient-absorption.md), and excretion of selected waste substances**. Amphipathic [bile salts](../../../../../bile-salt.md) help disperse dietary [fat](../../../../../fat.md) and form [mixed micelles](../../../../../mixed-micelle.md) carrying products of [lipid digestion](../../../../../lipid-digestion.md) to the absorptive surface of the [small intestine](../../../../../small-intestine.md). They thereby promote uptake of [fatty acids](../../../../../fatty-acid.md), [monoglycerides](../../../../../monoglyceride.md), [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md) and [fat-soluble vitamins](../../../../../fat-soluble-vitamin.md). [Bile](../../../../../bile.md) is not itself a source of [pancreatic lipase](../../../../../pancreatic-lipase.md), nor does emulsification chemically hydrolyse [triglycerides](../../../../../triglyceride.md). Biliary [bilirubin](../../../../../bilirubin.md), produced through [haem](../../../../../haem.md) breakdown, provides an excretory route; [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md) and selected other substances also leave in [bile](../../../../../bile.md). Most secreted [bile salts](../../../../../bile-salt.md) are recovered, especially in the terminal [ileum](../../../../../ileum.md), and return through the [portal vein](../../../../../portal-vein.md): the [enterohepatic circulation](../../../../../enterohepatic-circulation.md) conserves the useful detergent.[The Liver; Composition of Bile; The Gallbladder](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/23-6-accessory-organs-in-digestion-the-liver-pancreas-and-gallbladder.md)

Two comparisons reveal why both sorts of function matter. First, compare a fresh [liver](../../../../../liver.md) extract with an extract prepared after an appropriate controlled interval: testing for reducing sugar and isolating [glycogen](../../../../../glycogen.md) distinguishes a stored precursor from newly liberated [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md). A reliable interpretation needs prompt sampling and controls because post-mortem [glycogenolysis](../../../../../glycogenolysis.md) can change the result after the animal has died; detecting sugar in an organ alone does not prove where all of it was made. Secondly, in classical [biliary fistula](../../../../../biliary-fistula.md) observations, diverting [bile](../../../../../bile.md) away from the [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md) while collecting it separately allowed its output to be measured and the loss of normal [fat](../../../../../fat.md) assimilation to be examined. Comparing faecal [fat](../../../../../fat.md) with and without delivery of [bile](../../../../../bile.md) distinguishes a digestive contribution from a merely excretory one. The modern explanation is that loss of intestinal [bile salts](../../../../../bile-salt.md) impairs [mixed micelle](../../../../../mixed-micelle.md) formation, while [jaundice](../../../../../jaundice.md) from retained biliary pigment is a separate consequence of failed pigment elimination.
