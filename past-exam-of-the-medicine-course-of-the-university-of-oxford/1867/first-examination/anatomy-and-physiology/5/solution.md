**The [fourth ventricle](../../../../../fourth-ventricle.md) is the cavity of the hindbrain between the dorsal surfaces of the [pons](../../../../../pons.md) and upper [medulla oblongata](../../../../../medulla-oblongata.md) anteriorly and the [cerebellum](../../../../../cerebellum.md) posteriorly.** It is continuous above with the [cerebral aqueduct](../../../../../cerebral-aqueduct.md) and below, at the [obex](../../../../../obex.md), with the [central canal of the spinal cord](../../../../../central-canal-of-the-spinal-cord.md). Its floor is diamond-shaped when viewed from behind, while its roof rises dorsally towards the [fastigium](../../../../../fastigium.md).

The floor, the [rhomboid fossa](../../../../../rhomboid-fossa.md), consists of an upper pontine triangle and a lower medullary triangle. Its upper apex points towards the [cerebral aqueduct](../../../../../cerebral-aqueduct.md); its lower apex is the [obex](../../../../../obex.md); the two lateral angles continue into the [lateral recesses of the fourth ventricle](../../../../../lateral-recesses-of-the-fourth-ventricle.md). A median groove divides it into right and left halves. On each side, the [medial eminence](../../../../../medial-eminence.md) lies medial to the [sulcus limitans](../../../../../sulcus-limitans.md), which separates predominantly motor territories medially from sensory territories laterally. The [striae medullares of the fourth ventricle](../../../../../striae-medullares-of-the-fourth-ventricle.md) cross the floor near the junction of its pontine and medullary portions; their extent varies.

Several surface features identify deeper relations. In the pontine part, the [facial colliculus](../../../../../facial-colliculus.md) overlies the [abducens nucleus](../../../../../abducens-nucleus.md) and the internal looping course of fibres of the [facial nerve](../../../../../facial-nerve.md); it is not the exposed [facial motor nucleus](../../../../../facial-motor-nucleus.md). The upper part of the [sulcus limitans](../../../../../sulcus-limitans.md) has the [superior fovea](../../../../../superior-fovea.md), and near its rostral lateral region lies the [locus coeruleus](../../../../../locus-coeruleus.md). Laterally, the [vestibular area](../../../../../vestibular-area.md) overlies the [vestibular nuclei](../../../../../vestibular-nucleus.md). In the lower triangle, the [hypoglossal trigone](../../../../../hypoglossal-trigone.md) overlies the [hypoglossal nucleus](../../../../../hypoglossal-nucleus.md), and the [vagal trigone](../../../../../vagal-trigone.md), lateral to it, overlies the [dorsal motor nucleus of the vagus](../../../../../dorsal-motor-nucleus-of-the-vagus.md). The [area postrema](../../../../../area-postrema.md) lies still farther caudolaterally, separated from the [vagal trigone](../../../../../vagal-trigone.md) by the [funiculus separans](../../../../../funiculus-separans.md). These are landmarks and underlying relations, not claims that the [cranial nerve nuclei](../../../../../cranial-nerve-nucleus.md) lie loose within the cavity.[Introduction; Structure and Function; Surgical Considerations: floor and relations](../../../../../neuroanatomy-fourth-ventricle-statpearls.md)

The upper lateral boundaries are formed by the [superior cerebellar peduncles](../../../../../superior-cerebellar-peduncle.md). The lower lateral boundaries relate to the [inferior cerebellar peduncles](../../../../../inferior-cerebellar-peduncle.md) and adjacent dorsal medullary margins. The lateral recess on each side curves around the [inferior cerebellar peduncle](../../../../../inferior-cerebellar-peduncle.md) towards its external opening near the [flocculus](../../../../../flocculus.md).

The roof has superior and inferior slopes. Superiorly the thin [superior medullary velum](../../../../../superior-medullary-velum.md) spans the [superior cerebellar peduncles](../../../../../superior-cerebellar-peduncle.md), with the [lingula of the cerebellum](../../../../../lingula-of-the-cerebellum.md) on its dorsal surface. Inferiorly, the roof relates to the [inferior medullary velum](../../../../../inferior-medullary-velum.md), [nodule of the cerebellum](../../../../../nodule-of-the-cerebellum.md) and thin [tela choroidea](../../../../../tela-choroidea.md). Vascular folds of the [tela choroidea](../../../../../tela-choroidea.md) carry the [choroid plexus of the fourth ventricle](../../../../../choroid-plexus-of-the-fourth-ventricle.md), with median and lateral extensions. The cavity has an [ependymal](../../../../../ependyma.md) lining; the [choroid plexus](../../../../../choroid-plexus.md) has its specialized epithelial covering and produces [cerebrospinal fluid](../../../../../cerebrospinal-fluid.md).

There are three important exits to the [subarachnoid space](../../../../../subarachnoid-space.md): one [median aperture of the fourth ventricle](../../../../../median-aperture-of-the-fourth-ventricle.md) through the inferior roof, opening towards the [cisterna magna](../../../../../cisterna-magna.md), and two [lateral apertures of the fourth ventricle](../../../../../lateral-apertures-of-the-fourth-ventricle.md) at the ends of the [lateral recesses of the fourth ventricle](../../../../../lateral-recesses-of-the-fourth-ventricle.md). These must be distinguished from the inlet through the [cerebral aqueduct](../../../../../cerebral-aqueduct.md) and the inferior continuity with the [central canal of the spinal cord](../../../../../central-canal-of-the-spinal-cord.md). Most [cerebrospinal fluid](../../../../../cerebrospinal-fluid.md) leaving the [fourth ventricle](../../../../../fourth-ventricle.md) reaches the [subarachnoid space](../../../../../subarachnoid-space.md), not the narrow adult [central canal of the spinal cord](../../../../../central-canal-of-the-spinal-cord.md).

The three-dimensional arrangement explains the method of examination: reflecting the [cerebellum](../../../../../cerebellum.md) and opening the delicate roof exposes the [rhomboid fossa](../../../../../rhomboid-fossa.md), but destroys part of the structure one is trying to describe. Studying an intact roof, then the exposed floor, and using a median section to locate the [fastigium](../../../../../fastigium.md) avoids interpreting a dissected cavity as naturally open behind. The modern nomenclature is useful here; not every fine landmark or physiological interpretation in this account should be read as an assertion about what an 1867 examiner required.[Gross-dissection study: dorsal brainstem, rhomboid fossa and fourth-ventricular approaches](../../../../../morphometric-assesment-of-the-external-anatomy-of-fourth-ventricle-and-dorsal-brainstem-in-fresh-cadavers.md)
