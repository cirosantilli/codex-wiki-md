<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Equal [pitch](../../../../../pitch.md) need not mean equal [frequency spectrum](../../../../../frequency-spectrum.md). For an ideal periodic [sound wave](../../../../../acoustic-wave.md) of fundamental [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) $f$, a [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) has the form

$$
p(t)=\sum_{n\ge1}A_n\cos(2\pi nft+\phi_n).
$$

Changing relative amplitudes $A_n$ changes the waveform and generally its [timbre](../../../../../timbre.md) without changing its fundamental period. Attack, decay and the time evolution of the [frequency spectrum](../../../../../frequency-spectrum.md) also matter for real musical notes.

The [middle ear](../../../../../middle-ear.md) transfers vibration into the [cochlea](../../../../../cochlea.md). The [basilar membrane](../../../../../basilar-membrane.md) supports a frequency-selective travelling wave: higher-frequency responses occur nearer the base and lower-frequency responses nearer the apex. [Hair cells](../../../../../hair-cell.md) in the [organ of Corti](../../../../../organ-of-corti.md) transduce mechanical stimulation into signals transmitted through the [auditory nerve](../../../../../auditory-nerve.md). Different spectral and temporal patterns therefore produce different sensory-activity patterns.[Steps 3–6: cochlear travelling wave, hair cells and auditory nerve](../../../../../how-do-we-hear-by-the-national-institute-on-deafness-and-other-communication-disorders.md)

This supplies a physical basis for [timbre](../../../../../timbre.md) discrimination. It does not make the [cochlea](../../../../../cochlea.md) a set of independent strings, nor does excitation position alone explain every perceptual distinction: the [cochlear amplifier](../../../../../cochlear-amplifier.md) and temporal neural coding also contribute.
