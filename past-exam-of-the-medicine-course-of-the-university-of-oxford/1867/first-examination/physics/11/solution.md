<h1 id="11/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [11](../11.md)

The coils form a [mutual induction](../../../../../mutual-induction.md) apparatus. Changing primary [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) changes secondary [magnetic flux linkage](../../../../../magnetic-flux-linkage.md) $\Lambda_2$. [Faraday's law of induction](../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E_2=-\frac{d\Lambda_2}{dt}.}
$$

On closing the primary circuit, increasing [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) produces a transient secondary [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) and [galvanometer](../../../../../galvanometer.md) deflection. By [Lenz's law](../../../../../lenz-s-law.md) its direction opposes the increasing [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md). Once primary [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) and core [magnetization](../../../../../magnetization.md) are steady, the derivative vanishes: the [galvanometer](../../../../../galvanometer.md) returns to zero despite the continuing [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md).

On opening the circuit, the decreasing [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) induces a secondary [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) tending to sustain that flux. The [galvanometer](../../../../../galvanometer.md) deflects oppositely, then returns to zero. **Making and breaking produce opposite transient deflections; steady primary current produces no sustained induced current.**

For fixed linear coils, $\Lambda_2=MI_1$ and $\mathcal E_2=-M\,dI_1/dt$, with [mutual inductance](../../../../../mutual-inductance.md) $M$. Opposite equal changes of primary [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) give opposite equal areas under the induced-[electromotive force](../../../../../electromotive-force.md) curves in that model. Peak sizes can differ with switching times. Real iron-core [magnetic hysteresis](../../../../../magnetic-hysteresis.md) and remanent [magnetization](../../../../../magnetization.md) can also change the flux excursion, so equality of the actual peaks is not required.
