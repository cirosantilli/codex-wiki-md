<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

[Boyle's law](../../../../../boyle-s-law.md) states that a fixed amount of [gas](../../../../../gas.md) at constant [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{pV=\text{constant},}
$$

where $p$ is absolute [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), not [gauge pressure](../../../../../gauge-pressure.md). It is an ideal-gas approximation, not an assertion that every rapid compression is isothermal.

The [respiratory diaphragm](../../../../../respiratory-diaphragm.md) contracts and lowers its dome, increasing thoracic volume. Mechanical coupling through the pleural space lets the [lungs](../../../../../lung.md) expand; alveolar [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) falls below atmospheric [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), drawing air in. Relaxation of the [respiratory diaphragm](../../../../../respiratory-diaphragm.md) and elastic recoil drive quiet expiration. These [respiratory mechanics](../../../../../respiratory-mechanics.md) describe ventilation, distinct from chemical [cellular respiration](../../../../../cellular-respiration.md).[“Breathing in” and “Breathing out”: diaphragm movement, expansion and recoil](../../../../../how-the-lungs-work-what-breathing-does-for-the-body-by-the-national-heart-lung-and-blood-institute.md)

For the stipulated sealed, isothermal gas, initial and final volumes are $1600$ and $2000\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. Expressing absolute [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) as a mercury-column height,

$$
p_f=760\frac{1600}{2000}=608\,\mathrm{mmHg}.
$$

The mercury column is supported by the atmospheric-to-internal [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) difference:

$$
\boxed{h=760-608=152\,\mathrm{mm}=15.2\,\mathrm{cm}.}
$$

The specified volume change belongs to the historical idealized model. Real tube-air volume, temperature changes and limits on respiratory-muscle pressure generation would affect a physical apparatus.
