<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

[Specific heat capacity](../../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md) is [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md). For a specified thermodynamic process without a [phase transition](../../../../../phase-transition.md),

$$
\boxed{c=\frac1m\frac{dQ}{dT}.}
$$

The constraint matters: constant-[pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) and constant-volume [heat capacities](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) need not coincide. Here the supplied iron value and water value are treated as constants.

At the stipulated barometer reading, iron left in boiling water reaches $100^\circ\mathrm C$. Taking water's [specific heat capacity](../../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md) as one in the given units, the iron has [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) $100(0.114)=11.4$. The adhering gramme of hot water adds another one. Thus the hot material has total [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) $12.4$, compared with $150$ for the initially cool water.

The no-loss [calorimetry](../../../../../calorimetry-split.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) balance is

$$
100(0.114)(100-T)+1(100-T)=150(T-10),
$$

so

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{12.4(100)+150(10)}{162.4}
=\frac{3425}{203}\,{}^\circ\mathrm C\simeq16.8719^\circ\mathrm C.}
$$

The adhering water enters on the hot side, not as extra water initially at $10^\circ\mathrm C$. The vessel's [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) is neglected, as none is supplied.
