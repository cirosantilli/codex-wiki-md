↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

**A direct lightning strike is unnecessary.** A nearby strike can produce a [ground current](../../../../../ground-current.md), putting the feet at different [electric potentials](../../../../../electric-potential.md) and driving current through the body by a [step voltage](../../../../../step-voltage.md). A side flash from a struck object or conduction along a contacted conductor also provides an indirect current path.[“Ground Current”, “Side Flash” and “Conduction”: indirect electrical exposure](../../../../../lightning-science-five-ways-lightning-strikes-people-by-the-national-weather-service.md)

Rapid air heating by a nearby [lightning](../../../../../lightning.md) discharge also produces an expanding pressure disturbance. Its blast can impart a mechanical [impulse](../../../../../impulse.md), causing a fall or blunt injury without current passing through the person.[p. 1, “How Lightning Hurts Us”: expanding air and blast-related blunt trauma](../../../../../frontcountry-lightning-risk-management.md)

The absence of a direct strike therefore does not distinguish electrical incapacity from mechanical displacement. Observations would be needed to identify the mechanism in a particular event.
