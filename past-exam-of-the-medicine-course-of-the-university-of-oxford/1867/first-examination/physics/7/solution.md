<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

In the paraxial [thin lens](../../../../../thin-lens.md) approximation, let a converging [lens](../../../../../lens-optics.md) have [focal length](../../../../../focal-length.md) $f>0$, real-object distance $u>0$, and signed image distance $v$, positive beyond the [lens](../../../../../lens-optics.md). Then

$$
\boxed{\frac1u+\frac1v=\frac1f,\qquad m=-\frac vu.}
$$

A ray parallel to the axis emerges through the far focus; a ray through the optical centre is approximately undeviated. Their intersection gives a real, inverted image for $u>f$: reduced for $u>2f$, equal-sized at $u=2f$, and enlarged for $f<u<2f$. At $u=f$ the emerging rays are parallel. For $u<f$ their backward extensions meet on the object side: $v<0$ and the image is virtual and upright.

The [retina](../../../../../retina.md) remains at an approximately fixed distance from the refracting apparatus. [Ocular accommodation](../../../../../accommodation-vertebrate-eye.md) changes the [optical power](../../../../../optical-power.md). A nearer object increases $1/u$, so a fixed retinal image distance requires larger $1/f$.

For near vision, the [ciliary muscle](../../../../../ciliary-muscle.md) contracts and reduces tension in the [zonular fibres](../../../../../zonular-fibre.md). The elastic [crystalline lens](../../../../../crystalline-lens.md) becomes more rounded, increasing its [optical power](../../../../../optical-power.md). For distant vision the [ciliary muscle](../../../../../ciliary-muscle.md) relaxes, zonular tension flattens the [crystalline lens](../../../../../crystalline-lens.md), and less power is needed. The [eye](../../../../../eye.md)'s several refracting surfaces make the single [thin lens](../../../../../thin-lens.md) a qualitative model rather than its full optical prescription.
