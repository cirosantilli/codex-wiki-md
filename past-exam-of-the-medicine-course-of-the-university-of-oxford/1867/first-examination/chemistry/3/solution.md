<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**[Bleaching powder](../../../../../bleaching-powder.md) is made by passing [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) over [slaked lime](../../../../../slaked-lime.md), exposing a large surface of the powder to the [gas](../../../../../gas.md).** The contemporary name “chlorinated lime” is more informative than treating it as pure [calcium chloride](../../../../../calcium-chloride.md). The 1867 [British Pharmacopoeia (1867)](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-1867.md) describes this manufacture and a dull-white, partly [water](../../../../../water.md)-soluble powder with bleaching and disinfecting properties.[Calx Chlorata, printed p. 65](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-1867.md)

An idealized overall [chemical equation](../../../../../chemical-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{2\mathrm{Ca(OH)}_2+2\mathrm{Cl}_2\longrightarrow\mathrm{Ca(OCl)}_2+\mathrm{CaCl}_2+2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}.}
$$

The [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) undergoes [disproportionation](../../../../../disproportionation.md): one part becomes [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md), with [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) $-1$, and another becomes [hypochlorite](../../../../../hypochlorite.md), with [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) $+1$. Commercial [bleaching powder](../../../../../bleaching-powder.md) contains residual [calcium hydroxide](../../../../../calcium-hydroxide.md), [water](../../../../../water.md) and chlorine-bearing [solid](../../../../../solid.md) phases; the [chemical equation](../../../../../chemical-equation.md) is a useful composition balance, not a claim that the powder is one pure molecular substance or simply a fully characterized mechanical mixture. The old formula $\mathrm{CaOCl}_2$ is an abbreviated representation, not the formula of pure [calcium hypochlorite](../../../../../calcium-hypochlorite.md), $\mathrm{Ca(OCl)}_2$.

Its chief properties follow from the [hypochlorite](../../../../../hypochlorite.md) chemistry. The aqueous extract is alkaline and oxidizing. The [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md)

$$
\mathrm{OCl}^-+\mathrm{H}^+\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{HOCl}
$$

produces [hypochlorous acid](../../../../../hypochlorous-acid.md) on lowering the [pH](../../../../../ph.md). Oxidation of coloured [organic compounds](../../../../../organic-compound.md) can destroy the [chromophores](../../../../../chromophore.md) responsible for their colour. This is irreversible chemical alteration of a dye, rather than merely washing it away. The historical description that bleaching works through “nascent [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md)” is not a necessary modern mechanism: [hypochlorous acid](../../../../../hypochlorous-acid.md) and related reactive chlorine species themselves react with substrates.

With sufficient [hydrochloric acid](../../../../../hydrochloric-acid.md), the [hypochlorite](../../../../../hypochlorite.md) and [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md) can generate [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md):

$$
\mathrm{OCl}^-+\mathrm{Cl}^-+2\mathrm{H}^+\longrightarrow\mathrm{Cl}_2+\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}.
$$

This explains acid-induced [gas](../../../../../gas.md) evolution and why acidification is not an innocuous way to use a bleaching material. Exposure, moisture and heating can reduce the stored oxidizing strength. Its useful strength is measured as [available chlorine](../../../../../available-chlorine.md), rather than inferred from the powder's total [mass](../../../../../mass.md) or its smell; inert material and residual [lime](../../../../../lime.md) do not provide the same bleaching capacity.
