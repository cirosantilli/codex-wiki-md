<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**Take dry [air](../../../../../air.md) as unity, and compare each [gas](../../../../../gas.md) at the same [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md).** In the [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) approximation, $\rho=pM/(RT)$, so the [relative density](../../../../../relative-density.md) is

$$
\boxed{d_{\rm air}=\frac{\rho_{\rm gas}}{\rho_{\rm air}}\simeq\frac{M_{\rm gas}}{28.97\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}}.}
$$

Here $M$ is [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md). The following values use modern approximate [molar masses](../../../../../molar-mass.md); rounding and historical [atomic weight](../../../../../relative-atomic-mass.md) conventions can shift the last figures.

| Historical name and modern identification | Formula | $M/(\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}})$ | [Relative density](../../../../../relative-density.md), [air](../../../../../air.md) $=1$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carbonic acid: [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) [gas](../../../../../gas.md) | $\mathrm{CO}_2$ | 44.01 | **1.519** |
| Carbonic oxide: [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) | $\mathrm{CO}$ | 28.01 | **0.967** |
| [Nitrous oxide](../../../../../nitrous-oxide.md) | $\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}$ | 44.01 | **1.519** |
| Dry [air](../../../../../air.md) | Mixture | 28.97 | **1.000** |
| [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) | $\mathrm{NH}_3$ | 17.03 | **0.588** |
| Olefiant [gas](../../../../../gas.md): [Ethylene](../../../../../ethylene.md) | $\mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{H}_4$ | 28.05 | **0.968** |
| Alcohol vapour: [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) | $\mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{H}_6\mathrm{O}$ | 46.07 | **1.590** |
| Ether vapour: [diethyl ether](../../../../../diethyl-ether.md) | $\mathrm{C}_4\mathrm{H}_{10}\mathrm{O}$ | 74.12 | **2.559** |

“Olefiant [gas](../../../../../gas.md)” is confirmed as a historical synonym of [Ethylene](../../../../../ethylene.md) in the [NIST Chemistry WebBook, SRD 69](../../../../../nist-chemistry-webbook-srd-69.md). In this [gas](../../../../../gas.md) list, “carbonic acid” means [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), not an isolated vapour of aqueous $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{CO}_3$. “Alcohol” and “ether” refer to [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) and [diethyl ether](../../../../../diethyl-ether.md), rather than their entire modern chemical classes.[Ethylene entry, Formula, Molecular weight and Other names; [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) entry, Formula and Molecular weight](../../../../../nist-chemistry-webbook-srd-69.md)

The order is therefore [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md), then [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) and [Ethylene](../../../../../ethylene.md) nearly tied, then [air](../../../../../air.md), then [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and [nitrous oxide](../../../../../nitrous-oxide.md) nearly tied, then [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) vapour, then [diethyl ether](../../../../../diethyl-ether.md) vapour. Nearly equal [molar masses](../../../../../molar-mass.md) do not imply equal chemical behaviour.

If instead [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md) is chosen as unity, divide each [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md) by about $2.016\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ and state that convention. In the older nominal $\mathrm H_2=2$ approximation, the corresponding values are about $22,14,22,14.5,8.5,14,23,37$ in the source order. These are a different reference scale, not different substances.

The vapour comparisons are not comparisons of the [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) [densities](../../../../../density.md). Pure [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) and [diethyl ether](../../../../../diethyl-ether.md) must be considered at conditions where they remain gaseous, or by a low-[pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) [gas](../../../../../gas.md) approximation. Their [liquids](../../../../../liquid.md) can be lighter than [water](../../../../../water.md) while their vapours are heavier than [air](../../../../../air.md).
