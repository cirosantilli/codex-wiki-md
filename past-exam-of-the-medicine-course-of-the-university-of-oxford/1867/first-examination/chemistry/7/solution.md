**Examples heavier than [water](../../../../../water.md) are [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md), [glycerol](../../../../../glycerol.md) and concentrated [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md); examples lighter than [water](../../../../../water.md) are [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) and [diethyl ether](../../../../../diethyl-ether.md).** “Heavier” here means greater [mass](../../../../../mass.md) for the same [volume](../../../../../volume.md), rather than greater total [mass](../../../../../mass.md) in arbitrarily sized samples.

Representative room-[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) [relative densities](../../../../../relative-density.md), with [water](../../../../../water.md) as unity, are

| [Liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) | Approximate [relative density](../../../../../relative-density.md) |
| --- | --- |
| [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md) | 13.5 |
| Concentrated [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) | 1.84 |
| [Glycerol](../../../../../glycerol.md) | 1.26 |
| [Water](../../../../../water.md) | 1.00 |
| [Ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) | 0.79 |
| [Diethyl ether](../../../../../diethyl-ether.md) | 0.71 |

These are illustrative values, not exact constants independent of [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) or composition. Dilution changes the [density](../../../../../density.md) of [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md), [glycerol](../../../../../glycerol.md) and [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) solutions.[Glycerin, Sulfuric acid, Ethyl alcohol and Ethyl ether entries, Specific Gravity fields](../../../../../niosh-pocket-guide-to-chemical-hazards.md)[Agent Properties, [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) density at 25 °C](../../../../../mercury-agent-properties-by-niosh.md)

A distinction from simply observing a floating layer is essential: [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) is miscible with [water](../../../../../water.md), so it mixes rather than remaining as a permanent upper layer. [Diethyl ether](../../../../../diethyl-ether.md) has limited [water](../../../../../water.md) solubility and can form an upper layer. More broadly, [gases](../../../../../gas.md) are also [fluids](../../../../../fluid.md), but the natural comparison intended here is between [liquids](../../../../../liquid.md); [air](../../../../../air.md) at ordinary conditions is much less dense than [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) [water](../../../../../water.md).
