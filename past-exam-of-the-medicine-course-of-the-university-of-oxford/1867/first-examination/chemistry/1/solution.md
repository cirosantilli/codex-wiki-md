**A [combustible substance](../../../../../combustible-substance.md) can sustain a sufficiently rapid, heat-producing [chemical reaction](../../../../../chemical-reaction-split.md) with a suitable [oxidizing agent](../../../../../oxidizing-agent.md).** In ordinary [air](../../../../../air.md) this usually means combination with [molecular oxygen](../../../../../molecular-oxygen.md), producing enough [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) to maintain the reaction after ignition. A visible flame is not essential: [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) can glow as it undergoes [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md).

[Combustibility](../../../../../combustibility.md) depends on the surroundings and experimental conditions. A cold block of [wax](../../../../../wax.md) does not ignite merely because it meets [air](../../../../../air.md): heating must first provide combustible vapour and overcome the kinetic barrier to reaction. Finely divided [iron](../../../../../iron.md) burns much more readily than a massive bar because more surface is exposed per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Nor is [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) universally required: [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md) can undergo [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) in [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md). The expression “supports [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md)” identifies the surrounding reactant in a particular experiment; it does not establish an absolute division between two kinds of [chemical substance](../../../../../chemical-substance-split.md).
