**The conventional nineteenth-century answer is [diamond](../../../../../diamond.md), [graphite](../../../../../graphite.md) and so-called [amorphous carbon](../../../../../amorphous-carbon.md).** These exhibit strikingly different physical properties while sharing the same [chemical element](../../../../../chemical-element.md), [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md).

[Diamond](../../../../../diamond.md) is a crystalline, usually transparent form, very hard and ordinarily an electrical insulator. Its three-dimensional [covalent bond](../../../../../covalent-bond-split.md) network joins each [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) [chemical atom](../../../../../atom-physics.md) to four neighbours. [Graphite](../../../../../graphite.md) is a dark, soft, crystalline form with layered bonding; easy shear between layers makes it useful for marking and lubrication. Its electronic structure permits much greater [electrical conductivity](../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) than that of ordinary [diamond](../../../../../diamond.md).

The historical “amorphous” class includes [charcoal](../../../../../charcoal.md), [coke](../../../../../coke.md), lampblack and [soot](../../../../../soot.md), as well as [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)-rich materials such as [gas](../../../../../gas.md)-retort [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). Such names describe preparation and texture as well as structure: restricted-[air](../../../../../air.md) heating of wood gives [charcoal](../../../../../charcoal.md), destructive distillation of coal gives [coke](../../../../../coke.md), and incomplete [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) of a [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)-bearing [fuel](../../../../../fuel.md) produces [soot](../../../../../soot.md). Modern structural examination shows that these materials can contain small, imperfect graphitic regions. They are not necessarily one distinct pure [allotrope](../../../../../allotrope.md). [Coal](../../../../../coal.md) itself is a chemically complex, impure [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)-rich material, rather than a pure form of the [chemical element](../../../../../chemical-element.md).

A common chemical connection is that pure samples of these [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) forms can yield [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) upon complete [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) in excess [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathrm C+\mathrm O_2\longrightarrow\mathrm{CO}_2.}
$$

Their different [hardness](../../../../../hardness.md), appearance and [electrical conductivity](../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) arise from structure and impurities, rather than from being different [chemical elements](../../../../../chemical-element.md). This is the explanatory role of [allotropy](../../../../../allotropy.md).

Modern lists also include [fullerenes](../../../../../fullerene.md), [carbon nanotubes](../../../../../carbon-nanotube.md) and [graphene](../../../../../graphene.md). These are useful present-day extensions, not discoveries to credit to the 1867 candidate.[Appearance; Uses, charcoal and coke as impure [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md); discussion of modern [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) forms](../../../../../periodic-table-by-royal-society-of-chemistry/carbon-element-information-properties-and-uses.md)
