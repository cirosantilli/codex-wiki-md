**[Steel](../../../../../steel.md) is an [iron](../../../../../iron.md)-based [alloy](../../../../../alloy.md) with controlled [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) content and impurities; its manufacture requires extracting [iron](../../../../../iron.md) from an [ore](../../../../../ore.md) and then adjusting the composition.** In a modern plain-[carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) classification, [steel](../../../../../steel.md) generally contains less than about $2\%$ [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) by [mass](../../../../../mass.md), whereas [pig iron](../../../../../pig-iron.md) is more [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)-rich. The historical practical contrast was with low-[carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) [wrought iron](../../../../../wrought-iron.md) and readily cast but relatively brittle [cast iron](../../../../../cast-iron.md). Hardening by quenching is characteristic of suitable [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)-rich steels, but not every grade hardens in the same way.

Begin, for example, with an [iron oxide](../../../../../iron-oxide.md) [ore](../../../../../ore.md). Preparation removes some earthy matter and, where appropriate, roasting expels moisture or converts a carbonate-bearing [ore](../../../../../ore.md) to oxide. A [blast furnace](../../../../../blast-furnace.md) receives the [ore](../../../../../ore.md), [coke](../../../../../coke.md) and a flux such as [limestone](../../../../../limestone.md). The hot [air](../../../../../air.md) blast sustains [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) of [coke](../../../../../coke.md), generating [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) and the [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) needed for much of the oxide [reduction](../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md). Representative overall balances are

$$
\mathrm C+\mathrm O_2\longrightarrow\mathrm{CO}_2,\qquad
\mathrm{CO}_2+\mathrm C\longrightarrow2\mathrm{CO},
$$



$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Fe}_2\mathrm O_3+3\mathrm{CO}\longrightarrow2\mathrm{Fe}+3\mathrm{CO}_2.}
$$

[Calcination](../../../../../calcination.md) of [limestone](../../../../../limestone.md) provides [calcium oxide](../../../../../calcium-oxide.md). Combination with acidic gangue, represented by [silica](../../../../../silica.md), gives a [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) [slag](../../../../../slag.md):

$$
\mathrm{CaCO}_3\longrightarrow\mathrm{CaO}+\mathrm{CO}_2,\qquad
\mathrm{CaO}+\mathrm{SiO}_2\longrightarrow\mathrm{CaSiO}_3.
$$

Actual [slag](../../../../../slag.md) is a multicomponent melt, rather than necessarily pure [calcium silicate](../../../../../calcium-silicate.md). The descending [iron](../../../../../iron.md) picks up [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and other impurities and collects as molten [pig iron](../../../../../pig-iron.md). This first extraction product is not already finished [steel](../../../../../steel.md).

Two important routes available in 1867 proceed from this stage. In the older indirect route, refining and [puddling](../../../../../puddling.md) oxidize much of the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and remove slag-associated impurities to obtain [wrought iron](../../../../../wrought-iron.md). Bars packed with [charcoal](../../../../../charcoal.md) are then heated for a prolonged period in the [cementation process](../../../../../cementation-process.md). [Carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) enters the bars, giving [blister steel](../../../../../blister-steel.md); forging and welding can make [shear steel](../../../../../shear-steel.md), while melting suitable stock in covered crucibles produces more uniform [crucible steel](../../../../../crucible-steel.md). The bar can acquire [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) through [gas](../../../../../gas.md)-mediated surface reactions and [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md); the process should not be imagined as intact charcoal grains travelling through the [iron](../../../../../iron.md).

In the [Bessemer process](../../../../../bessemer-process.md), an [air](../../../../../air.md) blast passes through molten [pig iron](../../../../../pig-iron.md) in a converter. [Oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) of [silicon](../../../../../silicon.md), [manganese](../../../../../manganese.md) and [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) supplies [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md), while lowering the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) content. Composition can be corrected by adding a controlled [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)- and manganese-bearing material such as [spiegeleisen](../../../../../spiegeleisen.md). [Henry Bessemer](../../../../../henry-bessemer.md)'s 1856 patent describes the central idea of maintaining the conversion by oxidizing constituents of the molten metal, without [fuel](../../../../../fuel.md) for reheating during that stage.[US16082A, specification and final claim, 11 November 1856](../../../../../improvement-in-the-manufacture-of-iron-and-steel-by-henry-bessemer.md)

The early acidic converter demanded suitable feed: it did not solve removal of [phosphorus](../../../../../phosphorus.md) from every [ore](../../../../../ore.md). Later basic converter practice must not be silently imported into a December 1867 account. Final casting, working and [heat treatment](../../../../../heat-treatment.md) determine the [steel microstructure](../../../../../steel-microstructure.md) and properties as well as composition. The useful manufacturing chain is therefore **[ore](../../../../../ore.md) → extracted [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)-rich [iron](../../../../../iron.md) → controlled composition → worked and appropriately heat-treated [steel](../../../../../steel.md)**.
