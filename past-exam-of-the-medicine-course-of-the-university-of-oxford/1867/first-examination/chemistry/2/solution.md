<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**The [wick](../../../../../wick.md) supplies [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) [wax](../../../../../wax.md) by [capillary action](../../../../../capillary-action.md); [wax](../../../../../wax.md) vapour reacts with atmospheric [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), releasing the [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) that keeps the supply going.** The [wick](../../../../../wick.md) is a delivery system, rather than the principal [fuel](../../../../../fuel.md).

First, an external flame warms the [wick](../../../../../wick.md) and adjacent [wax](../../../../../wax.md). [Melting](../../../../../melting.md) produces a small [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) reservoir, and [capillary action](../../../../../capillary-action.md) draws it into the spaces between the [wick](../../../../../wick.md) fibres. Near the hot tip, [evaporation](../../../../../evaporation.md) and some [pyrolysis](../../../../../pyrolysis.md) supply gaseous [fuel](../../../../../fuel.md). [Melting](../../../../../melting.md) and [evaporation](../../../../../evaporation.md) are physical changes; [pyrolysis](../../../../../pyrolysis.md) and [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) change the [chemical substances](../../../../../chemical-substance-split.md) present. Once established, the [candle flame](../../../../../candle-flame.md) transfers enough [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) back to the [wax](../../../../../wax.md) to maintain this sequence.[Lecture I, melting reservoir, [wick](../../../../../wick.md), capillary attraction and combustible vapour](../../../../../the-chemical-history-of-a-candle-by-michael-faraday.md)

The [candle flame](../../../../../candle-flame.md) is a [diffusion flame](../../../../../diffusion-flame.md). Its relatively dark inner region contains newly supplied [fuel](../../../../../fuel.md) with limited access to [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). In the yellow luminous region, incomplete conversion of the [hydrocarbons](../../../../../hydrocarbon.md) produces small [soot](../../../../../soot.md) particles, which become incandescent. Farther out, mixing supplies enough [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) to consume most of the remaining [fuel](../../../../../fuel.md), [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) and [soot](../../../../../soot.md). Thus the yellow light is largely thermal emission from hot particles, rather than a yellow [gas](../../../../../gas.md).[Lecture II, deposition of [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) on a cold surface and the luminosity of heated [solid](../../../../../solid.md) particles](../../../../../the-chemical-history-of-a-candle-by-michael-faraday.md)

For a representative [alkane](../../../../../alkane.md) component of [paraffin wax](../../../../../paraffin-wax.md), complete [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) has the [chemical equation](../../../../../chemical-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{C}_n\mathrm{H}_{2n+2}+\frac{3n+1}{2}\mathrm{O}_2\longrightarrow n\mathrm{CO}_2+(n+1)\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}.}
$$

Real candle materials are mixtures, and some, such as fatty materials, already contain [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md); the displayed [chemical equation](../../../../../chemical-equation.md) is an illustrative [wax](../../../../../wax.md) model. The principal products of complete [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) are [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and [water vapour](../../../../../water-vapor.md). [Convection](../../../../../convection-split.md) carries the hot [gases](../../../../../gas.md) upward and brings fresh [air](../../../../../air.md) into the flame. A cold collector can reveal condensed [water](../../../../../water.md); collecting the [gases](../../../../../gas.md) and passing them into [limewater](../../../../../limewater.md) gives a [calcium carbonate](../../../../../calcium-carbonate.md) cloudiness from [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md).

Removing the [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) supply, cooling the reaction zone, or cutting off [wax](../../../../../wax.md) delivery extinguishes the flame. The smoking [wick](../../../../../wick.md) immediately afterwards can still emit combustible vapour; without sufficient [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) at the mixing zone, that vapour need not sustain a flame.
