<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The historical names identify [potassium chlorate](../../../../../potassium-chlorate.md), [ammonium nitrate](../../../../../ammonium-nitrate.md) and [calcium carbonate](../../../../../calcium-carbonate.md). Their expected net [thermal decomposition](../../../../../thermal-decomposition.md) [chemical equations](../../../../../chemical-equation.md), written in modern formulae, are

$$
\boxed{2\mathrm{KClO}_3\xrightarrow{\Delta}2\mathrm{KCl}+3\mathrm{O}_2,}
$$



$$
\boxed{\mathrm{NH}_4\mathrm{NO}_3\xrightarrow{\Delta}\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}+2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O},}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{CaCO}_3\xrightarrow{\Delta}\mathrm{CaO}+\mathrm{CO}_2.}
$$

The first gives [potassium chloride](../../../../../potassium-chloride.md) and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). A [potassium perchlorate](../../../../../potassium-perchlorate.md) intermediate can occur during uncatalysed heating; the first [chemical equation](../../../../../chemical-equation.md) gives the final overall balance. The second represents the familiar [nitrous oxide](../../../../../nitrous-oxide.md) pathway, with hot [water](../../../../../water.md) initially as vapour. It does not mean that [ammonium nitrate](../../../../../ammonium-nitrate.md) has only one decomposition pathway: conditions, impurities and confinement can produce different [gases](../../../../../gas.md) and violent decomposition.[Decomposition Chemistry: dependence on heating, contamination and confinement](../../../../../chemical-advisory-on-ammonium-nitrate-by-united-states-environmental-protection-agency.md)

The third is [calcination](../../../../../calcination.md) of [limestone](../../../../../limestone.md) to [quicklime](../../../../../calcium-oxide.md). Its extent depends on [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and the [partial pressure](../../../../../partial-pressure.md) of [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md); removing [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) favours further decomposition. Reversing the process gives carbonation of [calcium oxide](../../../../../calcium-oxide.md).

For dissolution in [hydrochloric acid](../../../../../hydrochloric-acid.md), the corresponding balances are

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Zn}+2\mathrm{HCl}\longrightarrow\mathrm{ZnCl}_2+\mathrm{H}_2,}
$$



$$
\boxed{\mathrm{MgO}+2\mathrm{HCl}\longrightarrow\mathrm{MgCl}_2+\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O},}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Al}_2\mathrm{O}_3+6\mathrm{HCl}\longrightarrow2\mathrm{AlCl}_3+3\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}.}
$$

“Magnesia” here means [magnesium oxide](../../../../../magnesium-oxide.md), and “alumina” means [aluminium oxide](../../../../../aluminium-oxide.md). [Zinc](../../../../../zinc.md) undergoes an [oxidation-reduction reaction](../../../../../redox.md), reducing [hydrogen ions](../../../../../hydrogen-ion.md) to [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md). The oxides instead undergo [acid-base reactions](../../../../../acid-base-reaction.md): [hydrogen ions](../../../../../hydrogen-ion.md) convert oxide into [water](../../../../../water.md), without [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) evolution. The chlorides are dissolved species in [water](../../../../../water.md), rather than anhydrous molecular salts necessarily existing intact in the solution. Strongly ignited [aluminium oxide](../../../../../aluminium-oxide.md) may dissolve slowly; the balance does not determine the rate.
