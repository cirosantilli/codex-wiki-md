<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**Expose the [orbit (anatomy)](../../../../../orbit-anatomy.md) in layers, preserving the [eye](../../../../../eye.md), [extraocular muscles](../../../../../extraocular-muscle.md), [nerves](../../../../../nerve.md) and [blood vessels](../../../../../blood-vessel.md) so that their relations can be demonstrated.** This is a general explanation of the practical exercise, using present-day [anatomy](../../../../../anatomy-split.md) terminology. It does not assert findings in the individual head supplied in 1867. The essential result is an intelligible [dissection](../../../../../dissection.md), with structures traced to their attachments or continuations, rather than a collection of detached parts.

Begin by orienting the head and choosing a superior approach through the [anterior cranial fossa](../../../../../anterior-cranial-fossa.md). Useful apparatus consists of a small saw or [bone](../../../../../bone.md)-cutting forceps, a fine chisel, fine scissors, dissecting forceps and a blunt probe. The coarse instruments remove [bone](../../../../../bone.md); the fine instruments separate [connective tissue](../../../../../connective-tissue.md). Remove the [dura mater](../../../../../dura-mater.md) from the orbital part of the [frontal bone](../../../../../frontal-bone.md), then carefully remove the roof of the [orbit (anatomy)](../../../../../orbit-anatomy.md) while leaving the [periorbita](../../../../../periorbita.md) intact. Extend the exposure towards the apex without driving the instrument into the [optic canal](../../../../../optic-canal.md) or the [superior orbital fissure](../../../../../superior-orbital-fissure.md). Preserve the delicate medial wall and its [anterior ethmoidal nerve](../../../../../anterior-ethmoidal-nerve.md), [posterior ethmoidal nerve](../../../../../posterior-ethmoidal-nerve.md) and accompanying [blood vessels](../../../../../blood-vessel.md). Open the [periorbita](../../../../../periorbita.md) under direct vision. This establishes a continuous view from the anterior margin towards the apex.[Step 1, dorsal approach: removal of the roof, preservation of the medial ethmoidal structures, and gentle removal of fat](../../../../../dissection-of-the-orbit-by-loyola-university-chicago.md)

In the superficial plane, identify the [frontal nerve](../../../../../frontal-nerve.md) above the [levator palpebrae superioris](../../../../../levator-palpebrae-superioris.md), and follow its division into the [supraorbital nerve](../../../../../supraorbital-nerve.md) and [supratrochlear nerve](../../../../../supratrochlear-nerve.md). On the lateral side identify the [lacrimal gland](../../../../../lacrimal-gland.md), [lacrimal nerve](../../../../../lacrimal-nerve.md) and accompanying [lacrimal artery](../../../../../lacrimal-artery.md). On the medial side find the [superior oblique muscle](../../../../../superior-oblique-muscle.md) and the [trochlear nerve](../../../../../trochlear-nerve.md) entering it. Separate small amounts of [adipose tissue](../../../../../adipose-tissue.md) with a blunt probe instead of stripping out the contents in a single mass. Preserve the [superior ophthalmic vein](../../../../../superior-ophthalmic-vein.md) where recognizable. Before reflecting any structure, show its course in place: removal of an overlying structure should answer a particular question about what lies underneath it.[Procedure 6 and opening of procedure 7: superficial nerves, superior ophthalmic vein, and reflection of levator and superior rectus](../../../../../lab-manual-eye-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso.md)

Next isolate the [levator palpebrae superioris](../../../../../levator-palpebrae-superioris.md) and trace it forward towards the upper [eyelid](../../../../../eyelid.md). Its expanded [tendon](../../../../../tendon.md) reaches the upper [tarsal plate](../../../../../tarsal-plate.md) and adjacent [eyelid](../../../../../eyelid.md) tissues; distinguish this from an attachment to the [sclera](../../../../../sclera.md). Divide the [levator palpebrae superioris](../../../../../levator-palpebrae-superioris.md) across its belly and reflect the ends, keeping its [oculomotor nerve](../../../../../oculomotor-nerve.md) supply attached where practicable. The [superior rectus muscle](../../../../../superior-rectus-muscle.md) is now exposed. Isolate and reflect it in turn, preserving the superior division of the [oculomotor nerve](../../../../../oculomotor-nerve.md) entering the deep surface. This successive exposure demonstrates why the superficial [frontal nerve](../../../../../frontal-nerve.md) should be located before either [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) is divided.[Procedure 7 commentary: reflect the levator and superior rectus at their mid-bellies and identify the superior division of III; levator attachment is detailed in the anatomy tables](../../../../../reflection-of-the-orbital-muscles-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso.md)[Levator palpebrae superioris row, insertion](../../../../../anatomy-tables-muscles-of-the-head-and-neck-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso.md)

Trace the four rectus [extraocular muscles](../../../../../extraocular-muscle.md) from the [common tendinous ring](../../../../../common-tendinous-ring.md) at the apex to the anterior [sclera](../../../../../sclera.md), behind the margin of the [cornea](../../../../../cornea.md). Demonstrate the [medial rectus muscle](../../../../../medial-rectus-muscle.md) and [lateral rectus muscle](../../../../../lateral-rectus-muscle.md) along the corresponding sides of the [eye](../../../../../eye.md), and the [inferior rectus muscle](../../../../../inferior-rectus-muscle.md) below it. Trace the [superior oblique muscle](../../../../../superior-oblique-muscle.md) forwards to the [trochlea of superior oblique](../../../../../trochlea-of-superior-oblique.md); its [tendon](../../../../../tendon.md) turns backwards and laterally beneath the [superior rectus muscle](../../../../../superior-rectus-muscle.md) to reach the [sclera](../../../../../sclera.md) behind the equator of the [eye](../../../../../eye.md). The [inferior oblique muscle](../../../../../inferior-oblique-muscle.md) is the important exception to an apical origin: it begins on the anterior medial orbital floor of the [maxilla](../../../../../maxilla.md), lateral to the [lacrimal sac](../../../../../lacrimal-sac.md), and passes backwards and laterally beneath the [inferior rectus muscle](../../../../../inferior-rectus-muscle.md) to the posterior lateral [sclera](../../../../../sclera.md).[Rows for rectus and oblique muscles: origins, insertions, and the superior oblique pulley](../../../../../anatomy-tables-muscles-of-the-head-and-neck-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso.md)

The following functional explanation makes the [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) display meaningful. The [medial rectus muscle](../../../../../medial-rectus-muscle.md) produces [adduction of the eye](../../../../../adduction-of-the-eye.md), and the [lateral rectus muscle](../../../../../lateral-rectus-muscle.md) produces [abduction of the eye](../../../../../abduction-of-the-eye.md). The [superior rectus muscle](../../../../../superior-rectus-muscle.md) elevates and the [inferior rectus muscle](../../../../../inferior-rectus-muscle.md) depresses, with additional [adduction of the eye](../../../../../adduction-of-the-eye.md); the [superior rectus muscle](../../../../../superior-rectus-muscle.md) also produces [intorsion of the eye](../../../../../intorsion-of-the-eye.md), and the [inferior rectus muscle](../../../../../inferior-rectus-muscle.md) produces [extorsion of the eye](../../../../../extorsion-of-the-eye.md). The [superior oblique muscle](../../../../../superior-oblique-muscle.md) produces [intorsion of the eye](../../../../../intorsion-of-the-eye.md), depression and [abduction of the eye](../../../../../abduction-of-the-eye.md); the [inferior oblique muscle](../../../../../inferior-oblique-muscle.md) produces [extorsion of the eye](../../../../../extorsion-of-the-eye.md), elevation and [abduction of the eye](../../../../../abduction-of-the-eye.md). Depression by the [superior oblique muscle](../../../../../superior-oblique-muscle.md) is demonstrated most clearly with the [eye](../../../../../eye.md) adducted. Gentle traction on an isolated [tendon](../../../../../tendon.md), with the [eye](../../../../../eye.md) supported, can illustrate its line of pull in the preparation. This mechanical demonstration does not test living [nerve](../../../../../nerve.md) function, and no observed movement in the historical specimen is supplied.[Learning objective 3: rectus and oblique actions and clinical testing positions](../../../../../dissector-answers-eye-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso.md)

In the deeper plane, identify the [optic nerve](../../../../../optic-nerve.md) entering the posterior [eye](../../../../../eye.md) and trace it back to the [optic canal](../../../../../optic-canal.md). Identify the [nasociliary nerve](../../../../../nasociliary-nerve.md) crossing above it towards the medial wall; follow the [long ciliary nerves](../../../../../long-ciliary-nerves.md) towards the [eye](../../../../../eye.md) and the [anterior ethmoidal nerve](../../../../../anterior-ethmoidal-nerve.md) and [posterior ethmoidal nerve](../../../../../posterior-ethmoidal-nerve.md) towards the medial [bone](../../../../../bone.md). On the lateral side of the [optic nerve](../../../../../optic-nerve.md), between it and the [lateral rectus muscle](../../../../../lateral-rectus-muscle.md), seek the small [ciliary ganglion](../../../../../ciliary-ganglion.md). Preserve the [short ciliary nerves](../../../../../short-ciliary-nerves.md) that pass from its anterior aspect to the [eye](../../../../../eye.md); an indiscriminate sweep through the surrounding [adipose tissue](../../../../../adipose-tissue.md) would destroy precisely these small connections.[Nerves table: optic, nasociliary, long ciliary, ethmoidal, ciliary ganglion and short ciliary entries](../../../../../anatomy-tables-eye-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso.md)

Trace the inferior division of the [oculomotor nerve](../../../../../oculomotor-nerve.md) towards the [medial rectus muscle](../../../../../medial-rectus-muscle.md), [inferior rectus muscle](../../../../../inferior-rectus-muscle.md) and [inferior oblique muscle](../../../../../inferior-oblique-muscle.md), including its connection to the [ciliary ganglion](../../../../../ciliary-ganglion.md). Trace the [abducens nerve](../../../../../abducens-nerve.md) to the deep surface of the [lateral rectus muscle](../../../../../lateral-rectus-muscle.md), and the [trochlear nerve](../../../../../trochlear-nerve.md) to the [superior oblique muscle](../../../../../superior-oblique-muscle.md). **The [lateral rectus muscle](../../../../../lateral-rectus-muscle.md) receives the [abducens nerve](../../../../../abducens-nerve.md), the [superior oblique muscle](../../../../../superior-oblique-muscle.md) receives the [trochlear nerve](../../../../../trochlear-nerve.md), and the remaining listed [extraocular muscles](../../../../../extraocular-muscle.md) receive the [oculomotor nerve](../../../../../oculomotor-nerve.md).** The [ciliary ganglion](../../../../../ciliary-ganglion.md) is a relay for fibres of the [parasympathetic nervous system](../../../../../parasympathetic-nervous-system.md) carried with the [oculomotor nerve](../../../../../oculomotor-nerve.md) towards the [sphincter pupillae muscle](../../../../../sphincter-pupillae-muscle.md) and [ciliary muscle](../../../../../ciliary-muscle.md). Sensory fibres connected with the [nasociliary nerve](../../../../../nasociliary-nerve.md), and already postganglionic fibres of the [sympathetic nervous system](../../../../../sympathetic-nervous-system.md), traverse the [ciliary ganglion](../../../../../ciliary-ganglion.md) without another relay.[Nerves table: oculomotor and nerve to inferior oblique entries; ciliary ganglion and short ciliary entries distinguish the three fibre components](../../../../../anatomy-tables-eye-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso.md)

Trace the [ophthalmic artery](../../../../../ophthalmic-artery.md) from the [optic canal](../../../../../optic-canal.md) and its continuity with the [internal carotid artery](../../../../../internal-carotid-artery.md), distinguishing it from the [optic nerve](../../../../../optic-nerve.md). Follow recognizable branches: the [central retinal artery](../../../../../central-retinal-artery.md) towards and into the [optic nerve](../../../../../optic-nerve.md), the [posterior ciliary arteries](../../../../../posterior-ciliary-arteries.md) towards the posterior [sclera](../../../../../sclera.md), the [lacrimal artery](../../../../../lacrimal-artery.md) towards the [lacrimal gland](../../../../../lacrimal-gland.md), and the [supraorbital artery](../../../../../supraorbital-artery.md), [anterior ethmoidal artery](../../../../../anterior-ethmoidal-artery.md) and [posterior ethmoidal artery](../../../../../posterior-ethmoidal-artery.md) towards their exits. The usual [ophthalmic artery](../../../../../ophthalmic-artery.md) crosses above the [optic nerve](../../../../../optic-nerve.md) to reach the medial side, but an individual course must be established by tracing, not imposed on the preparation. [Variability in relationships of arteries and nerves in the human orbit (1984), abstract: direct dissections found an inferior ophthalmic-artery crossing in some orbits](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6507890/) Follow the [superior ophthalmic vein](../../../../../superior-ophthalmic-vein.md) towards the [superior orbital fissure](../../../../../superior-orbital-fissure.md) and [cavernous sinus](../../../../../cavernous-sinus.md). The [inferior ophthalmic vein](../../../../../inferior-ophthalmic-vein.md) also has communications towards the [pterygoid plexus](../../../../../pterygoid-plexus.md) through the [inferior orbital fissure](../../../../../inferior-orbital-fissure.md).[Dissection section, ophthalmic artery and superior ophthalmic vein; branching pattern is variable](../../../../../lab-27-dissection-orbit-and-ear-by-washington-state-university.md)

Finally, show the relations at the apex. The [optic nerve](../../../../../optic-nerve.md) and [ophthalmic artery](../../../../../ophthalmic-artery.md) pass through the [optic canal](../../../../../optic-canal.md) within the [common tendinous ring](../../../../../common-tendinous-ring.md). The divisions of the [oculomotor nerve](../../../../../oculomotor-nerve.md), [nasociliary nerve](../../../../../nasociliary-nerve.md) and [abducens nerve](../../../../../abducens-nerve.md) enter through the part of the [superior orbital fissure](../../../../../superior-orbital-fissure.md) enclosed by the [common tendinous ring](../../../../../common-tendinous-ring.md); the [frontal nerve](../../../../../frontal-nerve.md), [lacrimal nerve](../../../../../lacrimal-nerve.md) and [trochlear nerve](../../../../../trochlear-nerve.md) enter outside it. Keep the [eye](../../../../../eye.md) attached until these relations have been displayed. A final labelled account should distinguish structures actually preserved from structures damaged or too small to demonstrate. That qualification matters in a practical [dissection](../../../../../dissection.md): an expected structure is a guide to search, not an observation.
