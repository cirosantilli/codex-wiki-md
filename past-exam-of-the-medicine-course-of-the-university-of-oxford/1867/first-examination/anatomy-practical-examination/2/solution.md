With the actual [bone](../../../../../bone.md), first establish its identity and [anatomical position](../../../../../anatomical-position.md) from its shape and characteristic landmarks. Where applicable, establish the side by combining several landmarks rather than choosing a side from an isolated prominence. Describe the ends or principal parts, surfaces, borders, processes, grooves and openings in a reproducible order. Identify the surfaces forming each [anatomical joint](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md), their partners and any covering of [cartilage](../../../../../cartilage.md) evident or inferred from the normal [anatomy](../../../../../anatomy-split.md). Do not claim that a dry preparation retains all the soft parts.

Then give each named [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md), its exact attachment landmark and whether this is its [muscle origin](../../../../../muscle-origin.md) or [muscle insertion](../../../../../muscle-insertion.md). Name the attached [ligaments](../../../../../ligament.md) and any passing or attached [tendons](../../../../../tendon.md), distinguishing a groove transmitting a [tendon](../../../../../tendon.md) from a true [muscle insertion](../../../../../muscle-insertion.md). A [tendon](../../../../../tendon.md) is the means by which many [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) attach; it should not be counted as an unrelated second attachment. An appropriate completed record would therefore associate every attachment with a named landmark, rather than merely list neighbouring [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md). No such specimen-specific record can be filled in here without inventing the missing [bone](../../../../../bone.md).
