Original volume: [Examination papers for the Degree of Bachelor of Medicine by the University of Oxford (1864–1867)](../../../examination-papers-for-the-degree-of-bachelor-of-medicine-by-the-university-of-oxford-1864-1867.md). [Open the original paper in the scanned volume, PDF page 79](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3e/Examination_papers_for_the_Degree_of_Bachelor_of_medicine_-_1864-67_(IA_b24757810).pdf#page=79).

Original questions are transcribed from the public-domain edition. Credit: Wellcome Collection; original supplied by Royal College of Physicians, London. [Source and public-domain rights statement](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/d7b9zgnx).

The answers distinguish explanations available in December 1867 from later discoveries. In particular, the historical expressions [pneumogastric nerve](../../../vagus-nerve.md), [eremacausis](../../../eremacausis.md) and [Entozoa](../../../entozoa.md) require interpretation rather than unqualified adoption.
