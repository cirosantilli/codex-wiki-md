## 1

↑ **Parent:** [Anatomy practical examination](anatomy-practical-examination.md)

**Original question.**

Dissect the orbit in the head before you.

<h3 id="1/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [1](#1)

**Expose the [orbit (anatomy)](../../../anatomy.md#orbit-anatomy) in layers, preserving the [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye), [extraocular muscles](../../../anatomy.md#extraocular-muscle), [nerves](../../../neuroscience.md#nerve) and [blood vessels](../../../physiology.md#blood-vessel) so that their relations can be demonstrated.** This is a general explanation of the practical exercise, using present-day [anatomy](../../../anatomy.md) terminology. It does not assert findings in the individual head supplied in 1867. The essential result is an intelligible [dissection](../../../anatomy.md#dissection), with structures traced to their attachments or continuations, rather than a collection of detached parts.

Begin by orienting the head and choosing a superior approach through the [anterior cranial fossa](../../../anatomy.md#anterior-cranial-fossa). Useful apparatus consists of a small saw or [bone](../../../anatomy.md#bone)-cutting forceps, a fine chisel, fine scissors, dissecting forceps and a blunt probe. The coarse instruments remove [bone](../../../anatomy.md#bone); the fine instruments separate [connective tissue](../../../anatomy.md#connective-tissue). Remove the [dura mater](../../../anatomy.md#dura-mater) from the orbital part of the [frontal bone](../../../anatomy.md#frontal-bone), then carefully remove the roof of the [orbit (anatomy)](../../../anatomy.md#orbit-anatomy) while leaving the [periorbita](../../../anatomy.md#periorbita) intact. Extend the exposure towards the apex without driving the instrument into the [optic canal](../../../anatomy.md#optic-canal) or the [superior orbital fissure](../../../anatomy.md#superior-orbital-fissure). Preserve the delicate medial wall and its [anterior ethmoidal nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#anterior-ethmoidal-nerve), [posterior ethmoidal nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#posterior-ethmoidal-nerve) and accompanying [blood vessels](../../../physiology.md#blood-vessel). Open the [periorbita](../../../anatomy.md#periorbita) under direct vision. This establishes a continuous view from the anterior margin towards the apex.[Step 1, dorsal approach: removal of the roof, preservation of the medial ethmoidal structures, and gentle removal of fat](../../../anatomy.md#dissection-of-the-orbit-by-loyola-university-chicago)

In the superficial plane, identify the [frontal nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#frontal-nerve) above the [levator palpebrae superioris](../../../anatomy.md#levator-palpebrae-superioris), and follow its division into the [supraorbital nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#supraorbital-nerve) and [supratrochlear nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#supratrochlear-nerve). On the lateral side identify the [lacrimal gland](../../../anatomy.md#lacrimal-gland), [lacrimal nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#lacrimal-nerve) and accompanying [lacrimal artery](../../../physiology.md#lacrimal-artery). On the medial side find the [superior oblique muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-oblique-muscle) and the [trochlear nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#trochlear-nerve) entering it. Separate small amounts of [adipose tissue](../../../physiology.md#adipose-tissue) with a blunt probe instead of stripping out the contents in a single mass. Preserve the [superior ophthalmic vein](../../../physiology.md#superior-ophthalmic-vein) where recognizable. Before reflecting any structure, show its course in place: removal of an overlying structure should answer a particular question about what lies underneath it.[Procedure 6 and opening of procedure 7: superficial nerves, superior ophthalmic vein, and reflection of levator and superior rectus](../../../anatomy.md#lab-manual-eye-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso)

Next isolate the [levator palpebrae superioris](../../../anatomy.md#levator-palpebrae-superioris) and trace it forward towards the upper [eyelid](../../../anatomy.md#eyelid). Its expanded [tendon](../../../anatomy.md#tendon) reaches the upper [tarsal plate](../../../anatomy.md#tarsal-plate) and adjacent [eyelid](../../../anatomy.md#eyelid) tissues; distinguish this from an attachment to the [sclera](../../../anatomy.md#sclera). Divide the [levator palpebrae superioris](../../../anatomy.md#levator-palpebrae-superioris) across its belly and reflect the ends, keeping its [oculomotor nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#oculomotor-nerve) supply attached where practicable. The [superior rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-rectus-muscle) is now exposed. Isolate and reflect it in turn, preserving the superior division of the [oculomotor nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#oculomotor-nerve) entering the deep surface. This successive exposure demonstrates why the superficial [frontal nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#frontal-nerve) should be located before either [muscle](../../../anatomy.md#muscle) is divided.[Procedure 7 commentary: reflect the levator and superior rectus at their mid-bellies and identify the superior division of III; levator attachment is detailed in the anatomy tables](../../../anatomy.md#reflection-of-the-orbital-muscles-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso)[Levator palpebrae superioris row, insertion](../../../anatomy.md#anatomy-tables-muscles-of-the-head-and-neck-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso)

Trace the four rectus [extraocular muscles](../../../anatomy.md#extraocular-muscle) from the [common tendinous ring](../../../anatomy.md#common-tendinous-ring) at the apex to the anterior [sclera](../../../anatomy.md#sclera), behind the margin of the [cornea](../../../anatomy.md#cornea). Demonstrate the [medial rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#medial-rectus-muscle) and [lateral rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#lateral-rectus-muscle) along the corresponding sides of the [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye), and the [inferior rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#inferior-rectus-muscle) below it. Trace the [superior oblique muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-oblique-muscle) forwards to the [trochlea of superior oblique](../../../anatomy.md#trochlea-of-superior-oblique); its [tendon](../../../anatomy.md#tendon) turns backwards and laterally beneath the [superior rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-rectus-muscle) to reach the [sclera](../../../anatomy.md#sclera) behind the equator of the [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye). The [inferior oblique muscle](../../../anatomy.md#inferior-oblique-muscle) is the important exception to an apical origin: it begins on the anterior medial orbital floor of the [maxilla](../../../anatomy.md#maxilla), lateral to the [lacrimal sac](../../../anatomy.md#lacrimal-sac), and passes backwards and laterally beneath the [inferior rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#inferior-rectus-muscle) to the posterior lateral [sclera](../../../anatomy.md#sclera).[Rows for rectus and oblique muscles: origins, insertions, and the superior oblique pulley](../../../anatomy.md#anatomy-tables-muscles-of-the-head-and-neck-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso)

The following functional explanation makes the [muscle](../../../anatomy.md#muscle) display meaningful. The [medial rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#medial-rectus-muscle) produces [adduction of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#adduction-of-the-eye), and the [lateral rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#lateral-rectus-muscle) produces [abduction of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#abduction-of-the-eye). The [superior rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-rectus-muscle) elevates and the [inferior rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#inferior-rectus-muscle) depresses, with additional [adduction of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#adduction-of-the-eye); the [superior rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-rectus-muscle) also produces [intorsion of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#intorsion-of-the-eye), and the [inferior rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#inferior-rectus-muscle) produces [extorsion of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#extorsion-of-the-eye). The [superior oblique muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-oblique-muscle) produces [intorsion of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#intorsion-of-the-eye), depression and [abduction of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#abduction-of-the-eye); the [inferior oblique muscle](../../../anatomy.md#inferior-oblique-muscle) produces [extorsion of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#extorsion-of-the-eye), elevation and [abduction of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#abduction-of-the-eye). Depression by the [superior oblique muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-oblique-muscle) is demonstrated most clearly with the [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye) adducted. Gentle traction on an isolated [tendon](../../../anatomy.md#tendon), with the [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye) supported, can illustrate its line of pull in the preparation. This mechanical demonstration does not test living [nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#nerve) function, and no observed movement in the historical specimen is supplied.[Learning objective 3: rectus and oblique actions and clinical testing positions](../../../anatomy.md#dissector-answers-eye-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso)

In the deeper plane, identify the [optic nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#optic-nerve) entering the posterior [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye) and trace it back to the [optic canal](../../../anatomy.md#optic-canal). Identify the [nasociliary nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#nasociliary-nerve) crossing above it towards the medial wall; follow the [long ciliary nerves](../../../neuroscience.md#long-ciliary-nerves) towards the [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye) and the [anterior ethmoidal nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#anterior-ethmoidal-nerve) and [posterior ethmoidal nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#posterior-ethmoidal-nerve) towards the medial [bone](../../../anatomy.md#bone). On the lateral side of the [optic nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#optic-nerve), between it and the [lateral rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#lateral-rectus-muscle), seek the small [ciliary ganglion](../../../neuroscience.md#ciliary-ganglion). Preserve the [short ciliary nerves](../../../neuroscience.md#short-ciliary-nerves) that pass from its anterior aspect to the [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye); an indiscriminate sweep through the surrounding [adipose tissue](../../../physiology.md#adipose-tissue) would destroy precisely these small connections.[Nerves table: optic, nasociliary, long ciliary, ethmoidal, ciliary ganglion and short ciliary entries](../../../anatomy.md#anatomy-tables-eye-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso)

Trace the inferior division of the [oculomotor nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#oculomotor-nerve) towards the [medial rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#medial-rectus-muscle), [inferior rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#inferior-rectus-muscle) and [inferior oblique muscle](../../../anatomy.md#inferior-oblique-muscle), including its connection to the [ciliary ganglion](../../../neuroscience.md#ciliary-ganglion). Trace the [abducens nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#abducens-nerve) to the deep surface of the [lateral rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#lateral-rectus-muscle), and the [trochlear nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#trochlear-nerve) to the [superior oblique muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-oblique-muscle). **The [lateral rectus muscle](../../../anatomy.md#lateral-rectus-muscle) receives the [abducens nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#abducens-nerve), the [superior oblique muscle](../../../anatomy.md#superior-oblique-muscle) receives the [trochlear nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#trochlear-nerve), and the remaining listed [extraocular muscles](../../../anatomy.md#extraocular-muscle) receive the [oculomotor nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#oculomotor-nerve).** The [ciliary ganglion](../../../neuroscience.md#ciliary-ganglion) is a relay for fibres of the [parasympathetic nervous system](../../../neuroscience.md#parasympathetic-nervous-system) carried with the [oculomotor nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#oculomotor-nerve) towards the [sphincter pupillae muscle](../../../anatomy.md#sphincter-pupillae-muscle) and [ciliary muscle](../../../anatomy.md#ciliary-muscle). Sensory fibres connected with the [nasociliary nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#nasociliary-nerve), and already postganglionic fibres of the [sympathetic nervous system](../../../physiology.md#sympathetic-nervous-system), traverse the [ciliary ganglion](../../../neuroscience.md#ciliary-ganglion) without another relay.[Nerves table: oculomotor and nerve to inferior oblique entries; ciliary ganglion and short ciliary entries distinguish the three fibre components](../../../anatomy.md#anatomy-tables-eye-by-texas-tech-university-health-sciences-center-el-paso)

Trace the [ophthalmic artery](../../../physiology.md#ophthalmic-artery) from the [optic canal](../../../anatomy.md#optic-canal) and its continuity with the [internal carotid artery](../../../physiology.md#internal-carotid-artery), distinguishing it from the [optic nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#optic-nerve). Follow recognizable branches: the [central retinal artery](../../../physiology.md#central-retinal-artery) towards and into the [optic nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#optic-nerve), the [posterior ciliary arteries](../../../physiology.md#posterior-ciliary-arteries) towards the posterior [sclera](../../../anatomy.md#sclera), the [lacrimal artery](../../../physiology.md#lacrimal-artery) towards the [lacrimal gland](../../../anatomy.md#lacrimal-gland), and the [supraorbital artery](../../../physiology.md#supraorbital-artery), [anterior ethmoidal artery](../../../physiology.md#anterior-ethmoidal-artery) and [posterior ethmoidal artery](../../../physiology.md#posterior-ethmoidal-artery) towards their exits. The usual [ophthalmic artery](../../../physiology.md#ophthalmic-artery) crosses above the [optic nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#optic-nerve) to reach the medial side, but an individual course must be established by tracing, not imposed on the preparation. [Variability in relationships of arteries and nerves in the human orbit (1984), abstract: direct dissections found an inferior ophthalmic-artery crossing in some orbits](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6507890/) Follow the [superior ophthalmic vein](../../../physiology.md#superior-ophthalmic-vein) towards the [superior orbital fissure](../../../anatomy.md#superior-orbital-fissure) and [cavernous sinus](../../../physiology.md#cavernous-sinus). The [inferior ophthalmic vein](../../../physiology.md#inferior-ophthalmic-vein) also has communications towards the [pterygoid plexus](../../../physiology.md#pterygoid-plexus) through the [inferior orbital fissure](../../../anatomy.md#inferior-orbital-fissure).[Dissection section, ophthalmic artery and superior ophthalmic vein; branching pattern is variable](../../../anatomy.md#lab-27-dissection-orbit-and-ear-by-washington-state-university)

Finally, show the relations at the apex. The [optic nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#optic-nerve) and [ophthalmic artery](../../../physiology.md#ophthalmic-artery) pass through the [optic canal](../../../anatomy.md#optic-canal) within the [common tendinous ring](../../../anatomy.md#common-tendinous-ring). The divisions of the [oculomotor nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#oculomotor-nerve), [nasociliary nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#nasociliary-nerve) and [abducens nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#abducens-nerve) enter through the part of the [superior orbital fissure](../../../anatomy.md#superior-orbital-fissure) enclosed by the [common tendinous ring](../../../anatomy.md#common-tendinous-ring); the [frontal nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#frontal-nerve), [lacrimal nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#lacrimal-nerve) and [trochlear nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#trochlear-nerve) enter outside it. Keep the [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye) attached until these relations have been displayed. A final labelled account should distinguish structures actually preserved from structures damaged or too small to demonstrate. That qualification matters in a practical [dissection](../../../anatomy.md#dissection): an expected structure is a guide to search, not an observation.

**Original question.**

Write a detailed description of the bone on the table ; mention the muscles, ligaments, and tendons attached to it, with their places of origin or insertion.

<h3 id="2/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [2](#2)

**TODO: The displayed [bone](../../../anatomy.md#bone) is unavailable and unidentified; its name, side and individual attachments cannot be determined from the paper.** This leaf remains incomplete.

With the actual [bone](../../../anatomy.md#bone), first establish its identity and [anatomical position](../../../anatomy.md#anatomical-position) from its shape and characteristic landmarks. Where applicable, establish the side by combining several landmarks rather than choosing a side from an isolated prominence. Describe the ends or principal parts, surfaces, borders, processes, grooves and openings in a reproducible order. Identify the surfaces forming each [anatomical joint](../../../anatomy.md#anatomical-joint), their partners and any covering of [cartilage](../../../anatomy.md#cartilage) evident or inferred from the normal [anatomy](../../../anatomy.md). Do not claim that a dry preparation retains all the soft parts.

Then give each named [muscle](../../../anatomy.md#muscle), its exact attachment landmark and whether this is its [muscle origin](../../../anatomy.md#muscle-origin) or [muscle insertion](../../../anatomy.md#muscle-insertion). Name the attached [ligaments](../../../anatomy.md#ligament) and any passing or attached [tendons](../../../anatomy.md#tendon), distinguishing a groove transmitting a [tendon](../../../anatomy.md#tendon) from a true [muscle insertion](../../../anatomy.md#muscle-insertion). A [tendon](../../../anatomy.md#tendon) is the means by which many [muscles](../../../anatomy.md#muscle) attach; it should not be counted as an unrelated second attachment. An appropriate completed record would therefore associate every attachment with a named landmark, rather than merely list neighbouring [muscles](../../../anatomy.md#muscle). No such specimen-specific record can be filled in here without inventing the missing [bone](../../../anatomy.md#bone).
