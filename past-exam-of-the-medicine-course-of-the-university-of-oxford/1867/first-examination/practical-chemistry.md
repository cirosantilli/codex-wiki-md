**Original question.**

Examine the solution F for oxalic acid, and the solution G for antimony or arsenic.

<h3 id="3/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

**TODO: Testing F for [oxalic acid](../../../chemical-substance.md#oxalic-acid) remains incomplete.** The [chemical solution](../../../mixture.md#solution-chemistry) and its observed test results are unavailable, so neither the presence nor the absence of [oxalic acid](../../../chemical-substance.md#oxalic-acid) can be reported.

**TODO: Testing G for [antimony](../../../chemical-substance.md#antimony) or [arsenic](../../../chemical-substance.md#arsenic) remains incomplete.** The [chemical solution](../../../mixture.md#solution-chemistry) and its observed test results are unavailable, so neither [antimony](../../../chemical-substance.md#antimony) nor [arsenic](../../../chemical-substance.md#arsenic) can be identified or excluded. The alternatives named in the paper specify what to investigate; they do not establish what G contains.
