↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**The required sequence is fresh [flowers](../../../../../flower.md); sliced and dried [bulb](../../../../../bulb.md); unexpanded [petals](../../../../../petal.md); [heartwood](../../../../../heartwood.md); [seeds](../../../../../seed.md); expressed seed [fat](../../../../../fat.md); expressed seed [oil](../../../../../oil.md); and fresh and dried [root](../../../../../root-botany.md).** The [plant](../../../../../plant.md) organ, its state, and the actual official material must all be specified.

- **[Sambucus nigra](../../../../../sambucus-nigra.md): fresh [flowers](../../../../../flower.md).** The official article is _Sambuci flores_, elder flowers, gathered from the native [plant](../../../../../plant.md). The flower stalks are separated when making elder-flower water; the preparation also allows an equivalent quantity of flowers preserved fresh with common salt. Neither elder berries nor elder bark answers the botanical part asked here.[“Sambuci flores”, p. 274; “Aqua sambuci”, p. 45](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)
- **[Squill](../../../../../squill.md): the sliced and dried [bulb](../../../../../bulb.md).** The historical botanical source is _Urginea scilla_, also identified there as _Scilla maritima_. The medicinal slices consist of the fleshy bulb scales; this is an underground shoot structure, not a [root](../../../../../root-botany.md). The official article is _Scilla_.[“Scilla”, pp. 279–280](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)
- **[Rosa gallica](../../../../../rosa-gallica.md): the fresh and dried unexpanded [petals](../../../../../petal.md).** These are _Rosæ gallicæ petala_, the red-rose material. “Unexpanded” refers to collection before the flower has opened fully. The [petals](../../../../../petal.md) must be distinguished from the [rose hip](../../../../../rose-hip.md) used for a different official preparation.[“Rosæ gallicæ petala”, red-rose monograph](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)
- **[Pterocarpus santalinus](../../../../../pterocarpus-santalinus.md): the sliced or rasped [heartwood](../../../../../heartwood.md).** This is _Pterocarpi lignum_, red sandalwood. The answer is the wood, rather than [bark](../../../../../bark.md) or the exudate known as [kino](../../../../../kino.md). Botanical relationship between two medicinal materials does not make their official definitions interchangeable.[“Pterocarpi lignum”, p. 261](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)
- **[Physostigma venenosum](../../../../../physostigma-venenosum.md): the [seed](../../../../../seed.md).** _Physostigmatis faba_, the [Calabar bean](../../../../../calabar-bean.md), is botanically a [seed](../../../../../seed.md), not the entire pod. Its two large [cotyledons](../../../../../cotyledon.md) lie inside a hard seed coat. The official alcoholic extract is made from the powdered [seed](../../../../../seed.md).[“Physostigmatis faba”, pp. 233–234; “Extractum physostigmatis”, p. 126](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)
- **[Theobroma cacao](../../../../../theobroma-cacao.md): the concrete [oil](../../../../../oil.md) obtained by expression and heat from the ground [seeds](../../../../../seed.md).** The official article is _Oleum theobromæ_, [cocoa butter](../../../../../cocoa-butter.md). Naming the [seed](../../../../../seed.md) gives its source organ, but omits the important fact that the official pharmaceutical material is the separated [fat](../../../../../fat.md). It is distinct from [coconut oil](../../../../../coconut-oil.md).[“Oleum theobromæ”, p. 229](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)
- **[Croton tiglium](../../../../../croton-tiglium.md): the [oil](../../../../../oil.md) expressed from the [seeds](../../../../../seed.md).** The official material is _Oleum crotonis_, [croton oil](../../../../../croton-oil.md), rather than the whole [plant](../../../../../plant.md) or an unspecified [leaf](../../../../../leaf.md) preparation. The [seeds](../../../../../seed.md) are its source; the expressed [oil](../../../../../oil.md) is the article used in the official formulations.[“Oleum crotonis”, p. 222](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)
- **[Taraxacum officinale](../../../../../taraxacum-officinale.md): the fresh and dried [root](../../../../../root-botany.md).** The official article is _Taraxaci radix_, dandelion root, called _Taraxacum dens leonis_ in the 1867 standard, gathered between September and February from British meadows and pastures. The collection season and fresh/dried distinction belong to the historical standard; the answer is not the [leaves](../../../../../leaf.md) or flowers.[“Taraxaci radix”, p. 317](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)

The names in the paper sometimes use the shorter terminology of the first [British Pharmacopoeia](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia.md), whereas the 1867 edition explicitly renamed many entries to identify the medicinal organ. Its alteration list, for example, maps _Sambucus_ to _Sambuci flores_, _Pterocarpus_ to _Pterocarpi lignum_, and _Taraxacum_ to _Taraxaci radix_.[“Articles the names of which have been altered”, pp. xix–xxi](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md) That is a nomenclature change, not permission to choose any part of the named [plant](../../../../../plant.md).
