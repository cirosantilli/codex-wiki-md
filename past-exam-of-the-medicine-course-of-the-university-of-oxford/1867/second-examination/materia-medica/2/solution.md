<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**Prepare [quinine sulfate](../../../../../quinine-sulfate.md) by acidic extraction of [cinchona bark](../../../../../cinchona-bark.md), alkaline precipitation of the [quinine](../../../../../quinine.md) base, conversion into the [sulfate](../../../../../sulfate.md), and purification by [crystallization](../../../../../crystallization.md).** “[Quinia](../../../../../quinine.md)” is the historical name for [quinine](../../../../../quinine.md).

The 1867 process starts with one pound of yellow [cinchona bark](../../../../../cinchona-bark.md) in coarse powder, three fluid ounces of [hydrochloric acid](../../../../../hydrochloric-acid.md), four pints of solution of [sodium hydroxide](../../../../../sodium-hydroxide.md), and sufficient [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) and diluted [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md). The [hydrochloric acid](../../../../../hydrochloric-acid.md) is diluted with ten pints of [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md). The bark is first moistened with diluted [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md), macerated for twenty-four hours with occasional stirring, and then exhausted in a [percolator](../../../../../percolator.md) with the diluted [hydrochloric acid](../../../../../hydrochloric-acid.md). The historical endpoint is disappearance of the bitter character of the emerging liquid. This describes the period's test, not an instruction to taste an unidentified medicinal material.[“Quiniæ sulphas”, pp. 268–269, ingredients and extraction](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)

The soluble [alkaloid](../../../../../alkaloid.md) salts enter the percolate. Adding solution of [sodium hydroxide](../../../../../sodium-hydroxide.md) liberates the much less water-soluble [quinine](../../../../../quinine.md) base. After settling, the liquid is decanted and the precipitate collected on a filter and washed with cold [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) until the washings cease to be coloured. The precipitate is then treated warm with [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) and diluted [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md), added gradually to obtain a neutral liquid with nearly all the precipitate dissolved. The hot solution is filtered through paper, the filter washed with [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md), and the filtrate concentrated until a surface film appears. It is then set aside for [crystallization](../../../../../crystallization.md); the crystals are dried on filter paper without applied heat. This is a separation of a naturally occurring [alkaloid](../../../../../alkaloid.md), not a synthesis of the [quinine](../../../../../quinine.md) skeleton.

Writing $B$ for the [quinine](../../../../../quinine.md) base, the central changes are:

$$
\mathrm{BH^+ + OH^- \longrightarrow B\downarrow + H_2O},\qquad
\mathrm{2B+H_2SO_4\longrightarrow (BH)_2SO_4}.
$$

The successive changes in [solubility](../../../../../solubility.md) explain the apparatus: a [percolator](../../../../../percolator.md) washes soluble salts out of the powder; a filter retains the liberated base; a heated porcelain vessel dissolves the crude base as its [sulfate](../../../../../sulfate.md); cooling then recovers the less soluble crystals. Other [cinchona alkaloids](../../../../../cinchona-alkaloid.md) can accompany the product, which is why appearance and bitterness are insufficient evidence of purity.

The 1867 “Characters and Tests” combine identity and purity checks. They must be read as a group:

- **Appearance and [solubility](../../../../../solubility.md)**: filiform, silky, white crystals, sparingly soluble in [water](../../../../../water.md), with the characteristic bluish appearance of the solution. A little dilute acid increases [solubility](../../../../../solubility.md). The visible blue phenomenon is associated with [quinine fluorescence](../../../../../quinine-fluorescence.md).
- **[Sulfate](../../../../../sulfate.md) identity**: [barium chloride](../../../../../barium-chloride.md) gives a white precipitate insoluble in [nitric acid](../../../../../nitric-acid.md). The precipitate is [barium sulfate](../../../../../barium-sulfate.md).
- **[Quinine](../../../../../quinine.md) identity**: treating the solution first with chlorine water and then with [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) gives an emerald-green colour, the [thalleioquin test](../../../../../thalleioquin-test.md). A positive colour reaction establishes a characteristic chemical response, rather than proving that no other [alkaloid](../../../../../alkaloid.md) is present.
- **Strong-acid colour test**: dissolution in pure [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) gives only a faint yellowish tint, with no further colour change on gentle warming. Marked colouring suggests foreign organic matter, such as [sucrose](../../../../../sucrose.md) or other readily altered material.
- **Acid dissolution and liberation of the base**: ten grains with ten minims of diluted [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) and half a fluid ounce of [water](../../../../../water.md) form a complete solution; [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) then gives a white precipitate. Material insoluble under the acid conditions would fail the dissolution requirement.
- **Ether separation**: the precipitated base must pass into [diethyl ether](../../../../../diethyl-ether.md) on shaking the whole with half a fluid ounce of [diethyl ether](../../../../../diethyl-ether.md), without crystalline matter remaining at the boundary between the two liquid layers. Less ether-soluble [cinchona alkaloids](../../../../../cinchona-alkaloid.md), particularly [cinchonine](../../../../../cinchonine.md), can leave material at this boundary. This is a period screening test, not a modern quantitative determination of every related impurity.
- **[Water of crystallization](../../../../../water-of-crystallization.md)**: twenty-five grains lose 3.6 grains on drying at $212\,{}^\circ\mathrm{F}$, equivalent to $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. This checks excessive moisture or a materially different degree of hydration.

The historical quantity and endpoint requirements belong to the 1867 monograph.[“Quiniæ sulphas”, pp. 268–269, “Characters and Tests”](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md) Its formula represents the then-described heptahydrate; the current [“Quinine sulfate”](../../../../../international-pharmacopoeia-by-the-world-health-organization.md) instead specifies a dihydrate and modern tests for related [cinchona alkaloids](../../../../../cinchona-alkaloid.md). The modern specification explains that distinction; the exam's historical tests remain those listed above.[12th edition, 2024, “Quinine sulfate (Quinini sulfas)”, formula, loss on drying and related cinchona alkaloids](../../../../../international-pharmacopoeia-by-the-world-health-organization.md)
