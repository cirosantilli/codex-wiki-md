**Ordinary cantharides blisters owe their activity to [cantharidin](../../../../../cantharidin.md); other blistering methods act through different chemical irritants or through physical injury.** There is no single substance common to every way of raising a [blister](../../../../../blister.md).

In the standard historical preparations, the dried [blister beetle](../../../../../blister-beetle.md) supplies [cantharidin](../../../../../cantharidin.md). A plaster, blistering paper, blistering liquid, or cantharides vinegar is a different vehicle for delivering the same [vesicant](../../../../../vesicant.md) to the [skin](../../../../../skin.md). Extracting the beetle into a liquid does not make the solvent the specific active principle. The 1867 [British Pharmacopoeia](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia.md) includes _Emplastrum cantharidis_, _Charta epispastica_, _Liquor epispasticus_ and _Acetum cantharidis_.[“Cantharis”; “Acetum cantharidis”; “Charta epispastica”; “Emplastrum cantharidis”; “Liquor epispasticus”](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md)

Other historical means need separate explanations. Moist [mustard](../../../../../mustard.md) develops an irritant volatile product, principally [allyl isothiocyanate](../../../../../allyl-isothiocyanate.md) from black mustard; its usual intended effect is reddening, with prolonged exposure capable of more destructive injury. This differs from the fixed [mustard](../../../../../mustard.md) seed [oil](../../../../../oil.md): the irritant volatile product develops after moistening the powdered [seeds](../../../../../seed.md).[“Sinapis” and “Oleum sinapis”; mustard seed preparations and production of the volatile oil](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md) Strong [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) acts as an alkaline irritant. [Croton oil](../../../../../croton-oil.md) can produce an inflammatory and pustular eruption, which is not the same lesion as a clean cantharides [blister](../../../../../blister.md). A sufficiently hot object or hot liquid raises a [blister](../../../../../blister.md) through thermal damage, without a medicinal active molecule. These differences matter because [erythema](../../../../../erythema.md), a fluid-filled [blister](../../../../../blister.md), a [pustule](../../../../../pustule.md), and tissue destruction are not interchangeable endpoints.

For [cantharidin](../../../../../cantharidin.md), the modern tissue explanation involves loss of adhesion between [epidermal cells](../../../../../epidermal-cell.md), including disruption of [desmosomes](../../../../../desmosome.md), with separation within the [epidermis](../../../../../epidermis.md) and accumulation of fluid. The [blister](../../../../../blister.md) is therefore a local lesion of cell adhesion accompanied by [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md), rather than a pocket containing disease drawn from a remote organ. The FDA review describes this [acantholysis](../../../../../acantholysis.md) and distinguishes it from the incompletely understood mechanism of clinical benefit in a particular modern indication.[NDA 212905, §5.3, “Primary pharmacology”, p. 32](../../../../../fda-multidisciplinary-review-of-ycanth.md)

A nineteenth-century answer would usually describe two supposed routes to benefit:

- **[Counterirritation](../../../../../counterirritation.md).** A new superficial irritation was believed to relieve a deeper painful or inflamed part by altering nervous activity and the distribution of [blood](../../../../../blood.md). A physiologically intelligible component is that input from [sensory nerves](../../../../../sensory-nerve.md) can change [pain](../../../../../pain.md) processing and [reflex](../../../../../reflex.md) output. That does not establish the stronger historical claim that creating an artificial [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) cures an unrelated internal [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md).
- **[Serous drainage](../../../../../serous-drainage.md) or “depletion”.** Fluid escaping from the raised [blister](../../../../../blister.md) was thought to remove fluid from the circulation and reduce local fullness. Fluid does indeed leave damaged [blood vessels](../../../../../blood-vessel.md) and collect in the [blister](../../../../../blister.md), but the observation alone does not demonstrate beneficial selective removal of a disease-producing substance. Maintaining a draining sore was a historical practice, not a consequence required by current [physiology](../../../../../physiology-split.md).

Thus **the demonstrable processes are local injury, [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md), fluid exudation, and sensory stimulation; the broad historical claims of remote benefit require separate evidence.** Current approved topical [cantharidin](../../../../../cantharidin.md) use for [molluscum contagiosum](../../../../../molluscum-contagiosum.md) does not validate nineteenth-century blistering for diseases of internal organs. The modern label explicitly states that the mechanism of action in [molluscum contagiosum](../../../../../molluscum-contagiosum.md) is unknown and documents local [skin](../../../../../skin.md) reactions.[§§5.2 and 12.1–12.3; vesicant action, local reactions, unknown mechanism and measured exposure](../../../../../ycanth-prescribing-information.md)
