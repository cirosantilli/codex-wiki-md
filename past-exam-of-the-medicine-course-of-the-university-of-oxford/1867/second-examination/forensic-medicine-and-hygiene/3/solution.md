<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**The strongest evidence is a genuine reaction of living [tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md), particularly established [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) or [wound healing](../../../../../wound-healing.md); bleeding, gaping and a clot are supporting observations, not individually decisive proofs.**

Examine the whole body before disturbing the [wound](../../../../../wound.md), recording its situation, dimensions, margins and relation to damaged [blood vessels](../../../../../blood-vessel.md). Then expose the [wound](../../../../../wound.md) track by careful dissection. An injury received while the [blood circulation](../../../../../blood-circulation.md) was active may show substantial [hemorrhage](../../../../../hemorrhage.md), [blood](../../../../../blood.md) infiltrating the surrounding [tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md), and adherent [blood clots](../../../../../blood-clot.md). Infiltration is more informative than loose [blood](../../../../../blood.md) merely lying on the surface: serial cuts can show whether [blood](../../../../../blood.md) has penetrated the [tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md) rather than simply coated it.

The interval of survival changes what can reasonably be expected. If the person survived long enough, surrounding [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md), cellular reaction, formation of [granulation tissue](../../../../../granulation-tissue.md), or an established [scar](../../../../../scar.md) is evidence of a process that began during life. Such findings can make the conclusion much stronger than the appearance of a fresh cut. They do not furnish a universal clock: [wound healing](../../../../../wound-healing.md) varies with site, severity, health and environment.

A post-mortem cut will often have relatively pale margins and little infiltration, although it can gape because [skin](../../../../../skin.md) and other [tissues](../../../../../tissue-biology.md) are elastic. [Blood](../../../../../blood.md) may still escape from a recently dead body, particularly from dependent or congested parts, and [coagulation](../../../../../coagulation.md) can occur around the time of death. [Livor mortis](../../../../../livor-mortis.md) can resemble bruising, while drying and [putrefaction](../../../../../putrefaction.md) alter colour and consistency. A body moved, compressed or subjected to attempts at resuscitation presents further opportunities for artefact. Therefore neither “it bled” nor “the edges retracted” establishes that the [wound](../../../../../wound.md) was inflicted during life.

For modern corroboration, sample the margin, underlying [tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md) and an uninjured comparison site for [histology](../../../../../histology.md), interpreting [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) and other markers in the context of the whole examination. A primary study of decomposed forensic cases illustrates the use of [immunohistochemistry](../../../../../immunohistochemistry.md) while explicitly warning that extravasated [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) alone do not reliably establish [wound vitality](../../../../../wound-vitality.md).[2021, Introduction and Discussion: ante-mortem injuries, decomposition and limitations of erythrocyte extravasation](../../../../../wound-vitality-in-decomposed-bodies-new-frontiers-through-immunohistochemistry.md)

Consequently, **a rapidly fatal [wound](../../../../../wound.md) may be impossible to distinguish confidently from one inflicted immediately after death by gross aspect alone**. Absence of developed [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) is expected when survival was extremely brief; it is not proof of post-mortem injury. The answer should express the degree of support, rather than force an unwarranted yes-or-no conclusion.
