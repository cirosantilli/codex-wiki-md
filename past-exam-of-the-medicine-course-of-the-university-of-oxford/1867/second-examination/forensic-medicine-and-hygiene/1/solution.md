<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**Establish that the stain contains [blood](../../../../../blood.md); then state separately whether its human origin can be established. In 1867, a positive [blood](../../../../../blood.md) test could not reliably distinguish human [blood](../../../../../blood.md) from that of another mammal.**

First record the position, extent, colour and underlying material of the stain. Keep a portion untouched, take a small scraping or cutting for examination, and test an unstained portion of the same material alongside it. The comparison is important: a coloured fabric, [iron oxide](../../../../../iron-oxide.md) or a vegetable colouring substance may resemble dried [blood](../../../../../blood.md), and the material itself can interfere with a chemical test. Brown colour alone proves nothing.

For an examination practicable in 1867, use complementary observations:

- Moisten a small portion with an appropriate aqueous medium and examine it by [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md). Recognisable [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) support the identification. Oval, nucleated [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) would point towards certain non-mammalian sources; human [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) are normally non-nucleated discs. Drying and extraction distort the cells, however, and their measured diameters overlap those of other mammals. An apparent match with human [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) therefore does not establish human origin.
- Apply the historical [haemin crystal test](../../../../../haemin-crystal-test.md) to a separate portion: gently heat the material with [acetic acid](../../../../../acetic-acid.md) and a little [sodium chloride](../../../../../sodium-chloride.md) on a covered microscope slide, then look for the characteristic brown crystalline product. A positive result supports the presence of a [hemoglobin](../../../../../hemoglobin.md) derivative. It does not identify the [species](../../../../../species.md).
- Where a suitable [spectroscope](../../../../../spectroscope.md) is available, examine an extract for the characteristic [absorption spectrum](../../../../../absorption-spectrum.md) of [hemoglobin](../../../../../hemoglobin.md) and the changes produced by altering its chemical state. This adds a different kind of evidence, but the [absorption spectrum](../../../../../absorption-spectrum.md) is again shared by the [blood](../../../../../blood.md) of different [species](../../../../../species.md). [Spectroscopy](../../../../../spectroscopy.md) of [blood](../../../../../blood.md) was already developing before this examination; it should not be confused with later [DNA profiling](../../../../../dna-profiling.md).[Unit II, §§3–6: historical development, microscopic examination, crystal tests and spectral methods](../../../../../identification-of-blood-by-the-national-institute-of-justice.md)

These observations should agree before the examiner expresses a positive conclusion. Failure to recover intact [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) does not exclude an old [bloodstain](../../../../../bloodstain.md); conversely, a colour reaction without suitable controls is weak evidence. A defensible period report would distinguish “[blood](../../../../../blood.md) identified, consistent with mammalian origin” from “human [blood](../../../../../blood.md) proved.” The second conclusion does not follow from the first.

For comparison, later [precipitin tests](../../../../../precipitin-test.md) use [antibodies](../../../../../antibody.md) against human [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) to investigate [species](../../../../../species.md) of origin. Modern [DNA profiling](../../../../../dna-profiling.md) can investigate the donor, subject to preservation and contamination limits. These are later developments, not procedures to attribute to an Oxford candidate in 1867.[“Confirmatory Tests”: crystal formation versus human-origin testing with specific antisera](../../../../../confirmatory-blood-tests-by-the-national-institute-of-justice.md)
