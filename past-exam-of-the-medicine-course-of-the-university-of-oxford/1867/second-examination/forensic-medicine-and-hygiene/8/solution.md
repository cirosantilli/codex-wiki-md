<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

**Distinguish diseases caused by inhaled [air pollutants](../../../../../air-pollutant.md) from [infections](../../../../../infection.md) transmitted through air. The principal impurity groups are gases and vapours, suspended particles and biological material; detection must identify the particular group and, where possible, measure its concentration.**

The nineteenth-century phrase “impure air” mixed several different mechanisms. Irritant smoke and dust can damage the [respiratory system](../../../../../respiratory-system.md); toxic gases can poison without causing an [infection](../../../../../infection.md); crowded, poorly ventilated rooms can increase exposure to infectious material. The historical [miasma theory](../../../../../miasma-theory.md) treated foul emanations much more broadly as generators of disease. In particular, the smell of sewage does not itself establish the cause of [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md), [cholera](../../../../../cholera.md) or [malaria](../../../../../malaria.md).

For the first part, important real consequences include the following:

- [Particulate matter](../../../../../particulate-matter.md) and irritant gases aggravate [asthma](../../../../../asthma.md) and contribute to chronic [respiratory disease](../../../../../respiratory-disease.md). Fine [particulate matter](../../../../../particulate-matter.md) is also associated causally with [cardiovascular disease](../../../../../cardiovascular-disease.md) and [lung cancer](../../../../../lung-cancer.md); these are modern conclusions, not a list of mechanisms all established in 1867.[“Overview” and “Health effects”: particulate exposure, cardiovascular and respiratory disease and cancer](../../../../../ambient-outdoor-air-pollution-by-the-world-health-organization.md)
- [Carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) from incomplete [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) can cause [carbon monoxide poisoning](../../../../../carbon-monoxide-poisoning.md), with headache, weakness, confusion and potentially fatal impairment of [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) delivery. Accumulated [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and reduced [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) in confined spaces present different hazards; a high [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) reading does not identify every other contaminant.[“About carbon monoxide”: combustion sources, symptoms and severe effects](../../../../../carbon-monoxide-poisoning-basics-by-the-centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention.md)
- [Sulfur dioxide](../../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md), [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) and [hydrogen sulfide](../../../../../hydrogen-sulfide.md) can injure the eyes and [respiratory system](../../../../../respiratory-system.md); sufficiently high [hydrogen sulfide](../../../../../hydrogen-sulfide.md) exposure can rapidly incapacitate or kill. Industrial dusts create additional substance-specific hazards, so “dust” is not one chemically uniform [poison](../../../../../poison.md).[Pocket Guide, “Symptoms” and “Target Organs”: eye and respiratory irritation, central effects and respiratory arrest](../../../../../hydrogen-sulfide-by-the-national-institute-for-occupational-safety-and-health.md)
- Airborne infectious material can transmit particular [infections](../../../../../infection.md), including [tuberculosis](../../../../../tuberculosis.md). Ventilation changes dilution and removal; it does not create or abolish a pathogen's identity. This is a different process from [carbon monoxide poisoning](../../../../../carbon-monoxide-poisoning.md) or irritation by smoke.[“How it spreads”: airborne material from infectious pulmonary or throat disease, especially indoors with poor air circulation](../../../../../tuberculosis-causes-and-how-it-spreads-by-the-centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention.md)

For the second part, ordinary outdoor air already contains [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), a small amount of [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), and variable [water vapour](../../../../../water-vapor.md). Their presence alone is not evidence of pollution. In a smoky industrial town, [soot](../../../../../soot.md), mineral dust, products of fuel [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) and [sulfur dioxide](../../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md) would be particularly relevant. In occupied rooms, expired [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), [water vapour](../../../../../water-vapor.md) and suspended biological material increase unless removed. Decomposing wastes may contribute [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md), [hydrogen sulfide](../../../../../hydrogen-sulfide.md) and other volatile products. The actual mixture depends on sources, ventilation and weather.

For the third part, begin with a defined sampling site and time, record occupancy or industrial activity, and compare with a suitable reference. A volume of air, not simply an unrecorded sniff or a discoloured paper, is the basis of quantitative comparison. Methods available by 1867 include:

- **[Carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md): absorption followed by measurement of the reagent consumed.** In the [baryta absorption method](../../../../../baryta-absorption-method.md), expose a known volume of air to a measured amount of standardized [barium hydroxide](../../../../../barium-hydroxide.md) solution. [Carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) forms [barium carbonate](../../../../../barium-carbonate.md); compare the residual [alkalinity](../../../../../alkalinity.md) with a reagent blank by [titration](../../../../../titration.md). A contemporary Manchester investigation used 7–10 litre globes for this approach and checked it against gravimetric absorption in potash. This is a useful example of two different measurement principles checking each other.[report of the Manchester Literary and Philosophical Society, “Note on the Amount of Carbonic Acid contained in the Air of Manchester”: globe method and independent weight determinations](../../../../../chemical-news-volume-9-1864.md)
- **Sulfur-containing gases: use chemically distinct tests.** [Hydrogen sulfide](../../../../../hydrogen-sulfide.md) blackens paper moistened with a soluble [lead](../../../../../lead.md) salt by forming [lead sulfide](../../../../../lead-sulfide.md). Acidic [sulfur dioxide](../../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md) changes moist indicator paper and has reducing properties; a coloured reagent's loss of colour needs controls because other reducing substances interfere. A positive result establishes a reaction, not by itself a measured safe or dangerous concentration.
- **[Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md): capture and demonstrate alkalinity or a characteristic chemical reaction.** Draw air through clean [water](../../../../../water.md) or a suitable absorbing liquid, test the collected material and compare it with an unexposed blank. Moist red [litmus](../../../../../litmus.md) turning blue is a simple indication of an alkaline gas, but cannot by itself distinguish [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) from every other base.
- **Suspended material: collect before examining.** Deposit particles on a clean surface or capture them by filtration, then use [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) to distinguish obvious [soot](../../../../../soot.md), fibres, mineral particles, pollen and other material. A measured volume and collected mass permit a dust burden to be estimated. Visible particles are not a direct measurement of infectiousness, and a nineteenth-century microscopic inspection cannot exclude all pathogenic material.
- **[Carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md): recognise the limitation of smell and generic air tests.** It is colourless and odourless. Its interaction with [hemoglobin](../../../../../hemoglobin.md) provides a basis for chemical and spectral investigation, while modern calibrated instruments permit direct monitoring. An acceptable [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) result or a lack of offensive smell does not rule it out.

The chemical equations underlying two of these qualitative tests are

$$
\mathrm{Ba(OH)_2+CO_2\longrightarrow BaCO_3\downarrow+H_2O},
\qquad
\mathrm{Pb^{2+}+H_2S\longrightarrow PbS\downarrow+2H^+}.
$$

Here the precipitates are [barium carbonate](../../../../../barium-carbonate.md) and [lead sulfide](../../../../../lead-sulfide.md). For the [baryta absorption method](../../../../../baryta-absorption-method.md), in an ideal, fully captured sample with no interfering acid gases, the difference between blank and sample acid requirements satisfies

$$
\boxed{n(\mathrm{CO_2})=\frac{c_{\mathrm{H^+}}(V_{\mathrm{blank}}-V_{\mathrm{sample}})}{2}},
$$

where $c_{\mathrm{H^+}}$ is the amount concentration of titratable acid equivalents and the volumes refer to titration of the entire corresponding absorbent portions; any aliquot requires its scale factor. The factor two follows from the two acid equivalents neutralized per [mole](../../../../../mole-unit.md) of [barium hydroxide](../../../../../barium-hydroxide.md). This calculation assumes that the [barium carbonate](../../../../../barium-carbonate.md) precipitate is excluded from the residual-base determination; dissolving it again with the titrant would invalidate the inference.

Odour, visible smoke and room stuffiness are useful prompts to investigate, but **no single smell, [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) value, microscopic field or chemical colour test establishes that air is free of harmful impurities**. Specify which material was measured and which uncertainty remains.
