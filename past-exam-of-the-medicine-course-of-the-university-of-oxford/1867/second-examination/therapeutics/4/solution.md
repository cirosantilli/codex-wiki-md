<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**Recommend [thoracentesis](../../../../../thoracentesis.md) when removing [pleural fluid](../../../../../pleural-fluid.md) is likely to relieve important respiratory compromise or answer a consequential diagnostic question; the size, course, cause and accessibility of the [pleural effusion](../../../../../pleural-effusion.md) govern the decision.** “Paracentesis thoracis” is the older name. Merely detecting some fluid is insufficient.

At the bedside in 1867, the assessment would combine the history with asymmetry of chest movement, enlargement of the affected side, [percussion](../../../../../percussion.md) dullness, diminished breath sounds on [auscultation](../../../../../auscultation.md) and displacement of the [heart](../../../../../heart.md). A large or increasing [pleural effusion](../../../../../pleural-effusion.md) with oppressive [dyspnoea](../../../../../dyspnoea.md), inability to lie comfortably, a displaced [heart](../../../../../heart.md) or failure to improve was a stronger indication than a small resolving collection. The opposite [lung](../../../../../lung.md)'s condition also mattered: someone with limited reserve could tolerate compression poorly. These signs have to be interpreted together because extensive [lung](../../../../../lung.md) disease can also produce dullness or abnormal breath sounds.

The distinction between a serous [pleural effusion](../../../../../pleural-effusion.md) and [empyema](../../../../../empyema.md) changes the object of the operation. In the former, the immediate object is decompression and allowing the compressed [lung](../../../../../lung.md) to expand. In [empyema](../../../../../empyema.md), evacuation of infected material is required and continuing drainage may be necessary. Recurrent [pleural fluid](../../../../../pleural-fluid.md) implies a persisting cause. Conversely, an improving small collection could be watched while treating the associated disease. A period report describes waiting for absorption, then confirming fluid with a grooved needle and evacuating it through a trocar and cannula when the signs and displacement persisted. It illustrates contemporary instruments and reasoning; it is not a safe modern procedural template.[British Medical Journal, 27 April 1867, Case II; serial assessment and trocar-and-cannula evacuation](../../../../../pleuritic-effusion-paracentesis-thoracis-recovery-1867.md)

**Modern correction: separate diagnostic aspiration, therapeutic aspiration and drainage of [pleural infection](../../../../../pleural-infection.md).** Use imaging to establish the collection and a safe route. A new unexplained unilateral [pleural effusion](../../../../../pleural-effusion.md) may need sampling even without severe [dyspnoea](../../../../../dyspnoea.md). [Pleural fluid](../../../../../pleural-fluid.md) testing can include biochemical analysis, [cytology](../../../../../cytology.md) and [microbiological culture](../../../../../microbiological-culture.md), selected for the suspected cause. With suspected [pleural infection](../../../../../pleural-infection.md), frank [pus](../../../../../pus.md) or sufficiently low [pleural fluid](../../../../../pleural-fluid.md) [pH](../../../../../ph.md) supports chest drainage rather than repeated simple taps. For example, in the relevant suspected-infection pathway, [pH](../../../../../ph.md) at or below 7.2 signifies high risk and supports drainage if an accessible volume can be drained safely. A typical bilateral collection responding to treatment of [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md) can be approached differently from an unexplained unilateral one.[Investigation of undiagnosed unilateral pleural effusion; Pleural infection, recommendations on pleural-fluid pH and intercostal drainage](../../../../../british-thoracic-society-guideline-for-pleural-disease-2023.md)

Risk assessment includes [bleeding](../../../../../bleeding.md) risk, ability to cooperate and the availability of an accessible fluid pocket. A very small or inaccessible collection may make aspiration inappropriate. [Thoracic ultrasonography](../../../../../thoracic-ultrasonography.md) is required for [pleural effusion](../../../../../pleural-effusion.md) aspiration. For therapeutic aspiration, drain slowly with an appropriate catheter; generally do not exceed 1.5 litres in one attempt and stop earlier for chest pain, tightness, persistent [cough](../../../../../cough.md) or worsening [dyspnoea](../../../../../dyspnoea.md). Complications include [pneumothorax](../../../../../pneumothorax.md), [bleeding](../../../../../bleeding.md) and [re-expansion pulmonary oedema](../../../../../re-expansion-pulmonary-oedema.md). Repeated accumulation requires a plan for its underlying cause and, where indicated, a longer-term drainage strategy.[Pleural aspiration: indications, relative contraindications, ultrasound guidance and drainage volume; summary of clinical practice points](../../../../../british-thoracic-society-clinical-statement-on-pleural-procedures-2023.md) The durable principle is to compare the harm caused by the collection with the benefit and risk of the proposed procedure, rather than using a single physical sign as an automatic command to operate.
