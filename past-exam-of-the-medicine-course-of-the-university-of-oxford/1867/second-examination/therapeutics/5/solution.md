↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**No single dose of [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) can be inferred from the word [fever](../../../../../fever.md). In 1867, wine or spirits were used variably for supposed exhaustion; the quantities describe disputed historical practice, not a beneficial modern treatment.** It is essential to distinguish ounces of wine or spirits from ounces of absolute [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md). Equal beverage volumes do not contain equal [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) quantities, and the historical sources do not provide a universal standardized strength.

The intended historical distinction was between an uncomplicated febrile illness in a person maintaining circulation and food intake, for which a stimulant might be omitted, and a low or “adynamic” state with increasing prostration. In [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md), prescribers who used [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) as a stimulant looked for deterioration of the [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), cold extremities, weakness, tremulousness, inability to take nourishment and failing responsiveness. In [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md), the same general assessment had to be combined with attention to abdominal complications; a new collapse might signify [gastrointestinal bleeding](../../../../../gastrointestinal-bleeding.md) or [intestinal perforation](../../../../../intestinal-perforation.md), rather than an abstract need for more wine. A [fever](../../../../../fever.md) with a hot [skin](../../../../../skin.md) and rapid but well-sustained [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) did not by itself establish a need for [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md). Nor did [delirium](../../../../../delirium.md) distinguish exhaustion from drug effects.

A useful dose answer must supply actual contemporary quantities while admitting their limits. The 1867 Dundee [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md) report records marked variation: only 44.7% of 2476 cases in its stimulant table received them. Among treated cases, the reported averages of each individual's maximum daily quantity were 3.19 ounces of wine and 2.6 ounces of spirits. Its corresponding figures below age 25 were 3.5 and 1.09 ounces; above age 50, 2.1 and 5.7 ounces. These are beverage amounts in historical ounces, not amounts of pure [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md), and not a recommendation to combine both in every case. The author expressly warns that “stimulation” could describe very different quantities and that averages can mislead.[1867, pp. 154–157, Table V and explanation of average maximum daily quantities](../../../../../typhus-statistics-of-the-dundee-royal-infirmary-with-remarks-by-thomas-john-maclagan.md) **The defensible period rule is small divided quantities, if used at all, reassessed by effect rather than a fixed allowance for every disease name.** Those observational figures cannot establish an advantageous dose for [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md), [scarlet fever](../../../../../scarlet-fever.md) or another distinct illness.

A second report published in October 1867 stresses individual response: even half an ounce of port could produce flushing or sleep, while an occasional adult two-ounce whisky draught was described as a sedating intervention. These examples span very different physiological effects; they are not interchangeable routine doses. The author rejects a fixed schedule that continues unchanged despite a changing condition.[1867, pp. 204–207, variable response, stimulation versus narcosis and rejection of fixed schedules](../../../../../clinical-study-of-stimulation-in-typhus-by-james-burn-russell.md)

The signs a historical prescriber hoped to see after a trial were a fuller, steadier [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), warmer extremities, clearer awareness, more restful sleep and improved ability to take food. Increasing flushing, excitement, worsening [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md), stupor or a deteriorating [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) argued against simply escalating it. These are a reconstruction of the period's claimed bedside test, not validated endpoints proving that [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) treats the underlying [infection](../../../../../infection.md).[1867, pp. 204–207, response-dependent administration, harm from continued dosing and limitations of apparent symptom improvement](../../../../../clinical-study-of-stimulation-in-typhus-by-james-burn-russell.md) Natural improvement, fluid intake and selection of the sickest people for treatment all complicate causal interpretation of the reports.

**Modern correction: there is no therapeutic beverage-alcohol dose for [fever](../../../../../fever.md) or [sepsis](../../../../../sepsis.md).** Measure severity and identify the cause, maintain appropriate fluid and nutritional support, and treat relevant [infection](../../../../../infection.md). In suspected [sepsis](../../../../../sepsis.md), assessment of [blood pressure](../../../../../blood-pressure.md), consciousness, [pulmonary ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) and perfusion guides escalation; current guidance specifies assessed [intravenous fluid](../../../../../intravenous-fluid.md) resuscitation and reassessment, not wine as a circulatory treatment.[NG253, Managing suspected sepsis; high-risk management and intravenous fluids](../../../../../suspected-sepsis-in-people-aged-16-or-over-by-nice.md) If habitual heavy [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) exposure introduces a risk of [alcohol withdrawal](../../../../../alcohol-withdrawal.md), that is a separate diagnosis. Monitored [benzodiazepine](../../../../../benzodiazepine.md) treatment and indicated [thiamine](../../../../../thiamine.md) address that risk; administering wine for the [fever](../../../../../fever.md) is not its modern management.[CG100, 1.1 acute alcohol withdrawal and 1.2 Wernicke's encephalopathy](../../../../../alcohol-use-disorders-diagnosis-and-management-of-physical-complications-by-nice.md)
