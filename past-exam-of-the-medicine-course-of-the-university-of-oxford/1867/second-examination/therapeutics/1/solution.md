<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**An 1867 answer would distinguish general depletion to relieve a supposed dangerous circulatory overload from local depletion intended to relieve a circumscribed inflammatory condition.** General [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) meant opening a [vein](../../../../../vein.md) by [venesection](../../../../../venesection.md). Local [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) meant [medicinal leeches](../../../../../medicinal-leech.md) or [wet cupping](../../../../../wet-cupping.md); ordinary dry cupping did not remove [blood](../../../../../blood.md). In [wet cupping](../../../../../wet-cupping.md), superficial incisions and a partially evacuated cup withdrew [blood](../../../../../blood.md) from the [skin](../../../../../skin.md). The apparatus and the quantity removed mattered: a supposedly local procedure could still produce substantial systemic [blood loss](../../../../../blood-loss.md).

In the period's vocabulary, a robust person with an early “sthenic” attack, a strong full [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), severe oppression of breathing and an apparently engorged [lung](../../../../../lung.md) might be considered for general [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md). The intention was to lessen vascular distension and the work imposed on the [heart](../../../../../heart.md), or to arrest an advancing [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md). Local [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) over a painful region was intended to moderate local [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) without the full depletion of [venesection](../../../../../venesection.md). These were therapeutic theories, not proof that [blood](../../../../../blood.md) beneath the [skin](../../../../../skin.md) drained an inflamed organ selectively. A contemporary account of [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) explicitly preferred early [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) in severe “sthenic” cases and distinguished them from debilitated “asthenic” cases.[1866, pp. 23–29, distinctions between sthenic and asthenic cases and indications for early bloodletting](../../../../../on-the-treatment-of-pneumonia-by-alexander-smith.md)

Even within that framework, the decision needed the stage of illness, previous [blood loss](../../../../../blood-loss.md), age, general strength and response to any previous depletion. A weak rapid [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), pallor, faintness or exhausted convalescence argued against further [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md). “There is [fever](../../../../../fever.md)” or “the [blood](../../../../../blood.md) looks inflammatory” was not an adequate indication. A hot flushed face alone could not establish excess circulating volume. The period evidence also needs scrutiny: selecting the most severe cases for [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md), then comparing their recovery with mild unbled cases, confounds treatment with initial severity. The reported recoveries establish what was done; they do not isolate its causal effect.[1866, pp. 4–9, allocation by clinical severity and comparison of treatment periods](../../../../../on-the-treatment-of-pneumonia-by-alexander-smith.md)

**Modern correction: remove [blood](../../../../../blood.md) only for a defined indication, not to treat ordinary [fever](../../../../../fever.md), [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) or a presumed surplus of “inflammatory” [blood](../../../../../blood.md).** In [haemochromatosis](../../../../../haemochromatosis.md), controlled [venesection](../../../../../venesection.md) removes [iron](../../../../../iron.md) contained in [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md); repeated replacement of those [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) draws down the body's [iron](../../../../../iron.md) stores. The amount and frequency depend on [haemoglobin](../../../../../hemoglobin.md) and [ferritin](../../../../../ferritin.md) monitoring.[Phlebotomy; adjustment of frequency using iron and ferritin levels](../../../../../treatment-of-hemochromatosis-by-niddk.md) In [polycythaemia vera](../../../../../polycythaemia-vera.md), [venesection](../../../../../venesection.md) reduces excessive [red blood cell](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) mass and the associated risk of [thrombosis](../../../../../thrombosis.md).[Treatment; reduction of blood thickness and clotting risk](../../../../../polycythemia-vera-by-medlineplus.md) These measurable targets are different from the historical idea of “plethora”. In an exhausted person, indiscriminate [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) can worsen [anaemia](../../../../../anaemia.md) and circulatory failure; modern care identifies and treats the cause of the [respiratory distress](../../../../../respiratory-distress.md) or [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) instead.
