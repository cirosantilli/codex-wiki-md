<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**The historical common principle was to identify and destroy a superficial fungal parasite, remove material harbouring it and prevent reinfection. Modern diagnosis preserves this principle for genuine [fungal infection](../../../../../fungal-infection.md), but rejects its indiscriminate application to “tinea decalvans”.** The three labels must therefore be discussed separately.

In [pityriasis versicolor](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor.md), look for finely scaling patches of altered [skin pigmentation](../../../../../skin-pigmentation.md), commonly on the trunk. Examine superficial [skin](../../../../../skin.md) scrapings by [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) when uncertain. Historically, washing, removal of loose scale and a topical “parasiticide” aimed to reach the organism in the outer [epidermis](../../../../../epidermis.md). Today [pityriasis versicolor](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor.md) is associated with overgrowth of [Malassezia](../../../../../malassezia.md) [yeasts](../../../../../yeast.md) already present on the [skin](../../../../../skin.md); it is not ordinary contagious [ringworm](../../../../../ringworm.md) and is not evidence of poor cleanliness.[Clinical description and Patient advice; normal skin yeast, noncontagious condition](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor-by-nottinghamshire-area-prescribing-committee.md) Topical [ketoconazole](../../../../../ketoconazole.md) or [selenium sulfide](../../../../../selenium-sulfide.md) can control it. Extensive or recurrent disease sometimes needs other treatment. Loss of scale and control of the [yeast](../../../../../yeast.md) need not immediately restore [skin pigmentation](../../../../../skin-pigmentation.md); residual colour can persist for weeks or months. A [Wood's lamp](../../../../../wood-s-lamp.md) can assist examination, but is not a substitute for the whole clinical assessment.[Diagnosis; topical treatment; Outcome](../../../../../tinea-versicolor-diagnosis-and-treatment-by-the-american-academy-of-dermatology.md)

“Tinea decalvans” is the critical historical trap. Some nineteenth-century writers interpreted patchy baldness as parasitic and advocated extraction of affected hairs followed by prolonged topical treatment. A January–June 1867 digest explicitly calls the condition contagious and reports a [mercury(II) chloride](../../../../../mercury-ii-chloride.md) preparation. That is evidence of a contemporary belief, not evidence that the proposed organism caused every case of patchy baldness.[Article on Tinea decalvans immediately preceding Art. 65; report from Medical Press and Circular, 10 April 1867](../../../../../half-yearly-abstract-of-the-medical-sciences-january-june-1867.md) The label often covered what is now called [alopecia areata](../../../../../alopecia-areata.md), an immune-mediated form of [hair loss](../../../../../hair-loss.md). Smooth bald patches require differentiation from scaly patches with broken hairs and demonstrated [fungal infection](../../../../../fungal-infection.md). Confirm the diagnosis before treating. In established [alopecia areata](../../../../../alopecia-areata.md), a period “parasiticide” is inappropriate: observation or diagnosis-specific treatment, including local [corticosteroids](../../../../../corticosteroid.md) in suitable cases, is considered instead. Neither [mercury(II) chloride](../../../../../mercury-ii-chloride.md) nor destructive epilation is a modern treatment for [alopecia areata](../../../../../alopecia-areata.md).[Diagnostic assessment; limited recent patches; topical and injected corticosteroids](../../../../../alopecia-areata-diagnosis-and-treatment-by-the-american-academy-of-dermatology.md)

For contagious [ringworm](../../../../../ringworm.md), establish the site and extent. An enlarging scaly ring on bare [skin](../../../../../skin.md) suggests [tinea corporis](../../../../../tinea-corporis.md); scaly bald areas with broken hairs suggest [tinea capitis](../../../../../tinea-capitis.md). Examine scrapings or hairs by [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) and, where appropriate, [fungal culture](../../../../../fungal-culture.md). Clean clothing, towels, combs and brushes; avoid sharing them and investigate relevant human or animal sources. These measures address transmission, while an [antifungal drug](../../../../../antifungal-drug.md) treats the affected tissue.[Diagnosis; Treatment; Prevention, especially shared personal items and infected animals](../../../../../ringworm-by-who.md) Limited [tinea corporis](../../../../../tinea-corporis.md) can often be treated topically. [Tinea capitis](../../../../../tinea-capitis.md) needs an oral [antifungal drug](../../../../../antifungal-drug.md), such as [griseofulvin](../../../../../griseofulvin.md) or [terbinafine](../../../../../terbinafine.md) chosen for the circumstances, because a cream alone does not clear the infected hair-bearing site. An adjunctive shampoo can reduce spread but does not replace systemic treatment. Unsupervised [corticosteroid](../../../../../corticosteroid.md) cream can mask or worsen [ringworm](../../../../../ringworm.md).[Ringworm on the skin; Ringworm on the scalp; Things to avoid](../../../../../treatment-of-ringworm-by-cdc.md)[Scalp; antifungal shampoo as an adjunct and prevention of household reinfection](../../../../../ringworm-diagnosis-and-treatment-by-the-american-academy-of-dermatology.md)

Thus the useful unifying idea is **cause-directed treatment that reaches the affected tissue**, with transmission control when infection is transmissible. It does not follow that all three nineteenth-century names denote the same class of disease, or that every bald patch requires killing a parasite.
