<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**Severe [haemoptysis](../../../../../haemoptysis.md) in a person described historically as “phthisical” requires immediate attention to breathing and circulatory danger; the treatment of chronic wasting cannot wait in place of control of the acute [bleeding](../../../../../bleeding.md).** The historical label suggests [pulmonary tuberculosis](../../../../../pulmonary-tuberculosis.md), but does not itself prove that diagnosis or identify the bleeding vessel.

In period terms, the sensible supportive aims were quiet, reduced exertion and observation of the [blood loss](../../../../../blood-loss.md) and general condition. The intent was to avoid provoking further [cough](../../../../../cough.md) or dislodging a clot, while preserving the person's remaining strength. A depleted person with chronic wasting was a poor candidate for reflex general [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md). The historical pharmacological literature included [astringents](../../../../../astringent.md), mineral salts and external cold, but the mere sequence “remedy administered, [bleeding](../../../../../bleeding.md) stopped” could not establish efficacy: [haemoptysis](../../../../../haemoptysis.md) often varied or stopped temporarily on its own.

This uncertainty was recognized before the examination. An 1862 discussion lists [astringents](../../../../../astringent.md) such as [lead acetate](../../../../../lead-acetate.md), emetic drugs and external ice among competing treatments, and questions the evidential value of events following their use. It also records opposing fashions of severe dietary restriction and frequent feeding with stimulants.[1862, pp. 553–557, opening discussion of competing specifics and the limitations of apparent therapeutic success](../../../../../on-the-treatment-of-tubercular-hemoptysis-by-william-brinton.md) The same author advocated a [digitalis preparation](../../../../../digitalis-preparation.md) with close [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) observation for severe attacks, hoping to influence the circulation; he emphasized its dangers. This is a period proposal, not modern emergency treatment. The historical explanation is why rest and supposed haemostatic remedies were attempted, not a claim that they reliably closed a ruptured vessel. No toxic nineteenth-century mixture is recommended here.

**Modern correction: life-threatening [haemoptysis](../../../../../haemoptysis.md) is an [airway](../../../../../airway.md) emergency, often killing through obstruction and loss of gas exchange.** Its severity depends on respiratory and circulatory effects, not just the estimated volume in a basin. Obtain emergency specialist support, maintain oxygenation and protect the unaffected [lung](../../../../../lung.md); when the bleeding side is known, positioning that side down can limit contamination of the other side. Secure suitable [intravenous access](../../../../../intravenous-access.md), assess [haemoglobin](../../../../../hemoglobin.md) and [coagulation](../../../../../coagulation.md), prepare for needed [blood transfusion](../../../../../blood-transfusion.md) and correct important [coagulopathy](../../../../../coagulopathy.md). In unstable ongoing [bleeding](../../../../../bleeding.md), securing the [airway](../../../../../airway.md) and [bronchoscopy](../../../../../bronchoscopy.md) can take precedence over transport for imaging.[2023, initial management of life-threatening hemoptysis; airway isolation, positioning and haemodynamic stabilization](../../../../../society-of-chest-imaging-and-interventions-consensus-guidelines-for-the-interventional-radiology-management-of-hemoptysis.md)

Once sufficiently stable, [computed tomography angiography](../../../../../computed-tomography-angiography.md) helps locate the source and plan treatment. [Bronchial artery embolisation](../../../../../bronchial-artery-embolisation.md) is a principal means of controlling severe or recurrent [haemoptysis](../../../../../haemoptysis.md) from abnormal systemic vessels. [Bronchoscopy](../../../../../bronchoscopy.md) can assist localization and temporary control; selected lesions or failed endovascular control may require [surgery](../../../../../surgery.md). Do not assume every [tuberculosis](../../../../../tuberculosis.md)-associated haemorrhage comes from the same vascular bed. The intervention must match the demonstrated source. Modern management also treats the underlying disease; stopping [bleeding](../../../../../bleeding.md) alone does not cure [pulmonary tuberculosis](../../../../../pulmonary-tuberculosis.md).[2022, Table 1; indications; diagnostic and treatment pathways](../../../../../cirse-standards-of-practice-on-bronchial-artery-embolisation.md) Suspected active [pulmonary tuberculosis](../../../../../pulmonary-tuberculosis.md) warrants appropriate respiratory infection-control measures, diagnostic sampling when safe and a susceptibility-informed multidrug [antibiotic](../../../../../antibiotic.md) regimen once the diagnosis and treatment plan are established.[Choice of regimen; drug susceptibility, coexisting conditions and interactions](../../../../../treatment-for-drug-susceptible-tuberculosis-disease-by-cdc.md) Quiet surroundings can still help, but neither ice nor a historical [astringent](../../../../../astringent.md) substitutes for these measures.
