**The chief physiological routes are abrupt failure of [blood circulation](../../../../../blood-circulation.md), failure of [pulmonary ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) or [gas exchange](../../../../../gas-exchange.md), or catastrophic failure of the central control of those functions.** Historical clinical accounts used labels such as [syncope](../../../../../syncope.md), [asphyxia](../../../../../asphyxia.md) and [medical coma](../../../../../coma-medicine.md) in explaining deaths; these terms describe broad physiological failures rather than identifying the underlying [diseases](../../../../../disease.md).[February 25, p. 142; March 10, pp. 181–183, contrasting cardiac failure, obstructed circulation and respiratory failure](../../../../../hospital-reports-and-lectures-on-modes-of-death-in-the-british-medical-journal-1860.md) A brief faint or ordinary [medical coma](../../../../../coma-medicine.md) is not itself necessarily fatal.

In primary circulatory failure, effective delivery of [blood](../../../../../blood.md) to the [brain](../../../../../brain.md) stops. Modern examples include [ventricular fibrillation](../../../../../ventricular-fibrillation.md), pulseless [ventricular tachycardia](../../../../../ventricular-tachycardia.md), severe [bradyarrhythmia](../../../../../bradyarrhythmia.md) and [asystole](../../../../../asystole.md). They may arise in [coronary artery disease](../../../../../coronary-artery-disease.md), after [myocardial infarction](../../../../../myocardial-infarction.md), in [cardiomyopathy](../../../../../cardiomyopathy.md), or from inherited [cardiac ion channel disorders](../../../../../cardiac-ion-channel-disorder.md). An electrical cause may leave little distinctive gross evidence at [autopsy](../../../../../autopsy.md). These modern mechanisms refine the broad historical description of cardiac [syncope](../../../../../syncope.md).[Dangerous heart rhythms, scarred or thickened myocardium, and inherited electrical abnormalities](../../../../../what-causes-cardiac-arrest-by-the-american-heart-association.md)

Mechanical interruption of [blood circulation](../../../../../blood-circulation.md) gives another group: massive [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md), rupture of an [aortic aneurysm](../../../../../aortic-aneurysm.md), [cardiac tamponade](../../../../../cardiac-tamponade.md), and large [pulmonary embolism](../../../../../pulmonary-embolism.md). The immediate deficit differs in each. [Haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md) removes circulating volume; [cardiac tamponade](../../../../../cardiac-tamponade.md) prevents adequate filling; [pulmonary embolism](../../../../../pulmonary-embolism.md) obstructs flow through the [pulmonary circulation](../../../../../pulmonary-circulation.md). All can end with critically low [cardiac output](../../../../../cardiac-output.md) even if some electrical activity persists.[Etiology, noncardiac shock including pulmonary embolism and hemorrhage; Pathophysiology and reversible causes](../../../../../cardiac-arrest-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

Respiratory failure includes obstruction of the [airway](../../../../../airway.md) by a foreign body, [laryngeal oedema](../../../../../laryngeal-oedema.md) or [aspiration](../../../../../aspiration.md); drowning; and depression of [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) by drugs or major [brainstem](../../../../../brainstem.md) injury. [Oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) delivery then fails and secondary [cardiac arrest](../../../../../cardiac-arrest.md) follows. [Carbon monoxide poisoning](../../../../../carbon-monoxide-poisoning.md) and [cyanide poisoning](../../../../../cyanide-poisoning.md) illustrate related failure of [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) transport or use: not every hypoxic death is caused by physical obstruction of an [airway](../../../../../airway.md).[Overview, impaired oxygen delivery and cellular oxygen utilization](../../../../../clinical-guidance-for-carbon-monoxide-poisoning-by-cdc.md)[Background, effects on the body's ability to use oxygen](../../../../../cyanide-chemical-fact-sheet-by-cdc.md)[Other medical conditions and events, respiratory arrest, poisoning and drug overdose](../../../../../cardiac-arrest-causes-and-risk-factors-by-the-national-heart-lung-and-blood-institute.md)

A major [intracranial haemorrhage](../../../../../intracranial-haemorrhage.md) or rapidly developing [brain herniation](../../../../../brain-herniation.md) can destroy or compress the pathways maintaining [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md) and [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md). A severe disturbance of [electrolytes](../../../../../electrolyte.md), especially [potassium](../../../../../potassium.md), may instead act directly on the [heart](../../../../../heart.md)'s electrical function. A fatal [seizure](../../../../../seizure.md) can involve respiratory and circulatory mechanisms; [sudden unexpected death in epilepsy](../../../../../sudden-unexpected-death-in-epilepsy.md) remains a diagnosis requiring exclusion of other causes, not merely recognition of a history of [epilepsy](../../../../../epilepsy.md).[Heart problems; Other medical conditions and events, electrolytes and seizure disorders](../../../../../cardiac-arrest-causes-and-risk-factors-by-the-national-heart-lung-and-blood-institute.md)

The practical distinction is between **mode, immediate mechanism and underlying cause**. For example, rupture of an [aneurysm](../../../../../aneurysm.md) can cause [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md), then circulatory failure, then irreversible [hypoxic-ischaemic brain injury](../../../../../hypoxic-ischaemic-brain-injury.md). A complete explanation identifies that chain. Inspection, [histology](../../../../../histology.md), examination of the [airway](../../../../../airway.md) and [heart](../../../../../heart.md), and appropriately interpreted [toxicology](../../../../../toxicology.md) may establish it; an unexplained sudden death cannot be assigned a precise cause from its suddenness alone.
