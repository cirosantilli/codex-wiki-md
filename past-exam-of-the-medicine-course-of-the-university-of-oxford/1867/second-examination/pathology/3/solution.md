**Historical “amyloid disease” denotes the waxy or lardaceous visceral change, especially in the [spleen](../../../../../spleen.md), [liver](../../../../../liver.md) and [kidneys](../../../../../kidney.md); modern [amyloidosis](../../../../../amyloidosis.md) denotes deposition of misfolded [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) fibrils.** The old descriptive appearance and the modern chemical explanation must be kept distinct.

In nineteenth-century usage the affected organs could be enlarged, firm and unusually translucent or wax-like on section. The name [amyloid](../../../../../amyloid.md) means starch-like. The historical chemical argument relied on colour reactions with [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) and [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md), suggesting affinity with [starch](../../../../../starch.md) or [cellulose](../../../../../cellulose.md). Such a reaction was a clue to recognition, not a modern identification of the deposited substance. [Rudolf Virchow](../../../../../rudolf-virchow.md)'s 1856 account explicitly discusses the waxy change of the [spleen](../../../../../spleen.md), [liver](../../../../../liver.md) and [kidneys](../../../../../kidney.md), and its occurrence with longstanding destructive disease.[1856 English abstract, pp. 380–383, opening account of visceral sites and chronic disease](../../../../../on-the-course-of-the-amyloid-degeneration-by-rudolf-virchow.md)

The three principal visceral examples can be described more precisely:

- In the [spleen](../../../../../spleen.md), deposits in the [splenic white pulp](../../../../../splenic-white-pulp.md) may produce translucent granules, the so-called [sago spleen](../../../../../sago-spleen.md). More diffuse involvement gives a widespread lardaceous appearance.
- In the [liver](../../../../../liver.md), deposits along the [hepatic sinusoids](../../../../../hepatic-sinusoid.md) can compress adjacent [hepatocytes](../../../../../hepatocyte.md); extensive deposition may produce a large firm [liver](../../../../../liver.md).[Abstract, autopsy examination of 286 livers; sinusoidal and portal patterns](../../../../../hepatic-amyloidosis-morphologic-differences-between-systemic-al-and-aa-types.md)
- In the [kidney](../../../../../kidney.md), deposits in [glomeruli](../../../../../glomerulus.md), [blood vessels](../../../../../blood-vessel.md) and sometimes the interstitium interfere with function. Involvement of the [glomeruli](../../../../../glomerulus.md) explains the [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md) of Question 1; it may progress to [nephrotic syndrome](../../../../../nephrotic-syndrome.md) and [kidney failure](../../../../../kidney-failure.md).

The distribution is not confined to those three organs. [Amyloidosis](../../../../../amyloidosis.md) can also affect the [gastrointestinal tract](../../../../../gastrointestinal-tract.md), [heart](../../../../../heart.md), [blood vessels](../../../../../blood-vessel.md) and other sites; the pattern depends on the precursor [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). Modern examples include [AA amyloidosis](../../../../../aa-amyloidosis.md) associated with sustained [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md), [AL amyloidosis](../../../../../al-amyloidosis.md) involving [immunoglobulin light chains](../../../../../immunoglobulin-light-chain.md), and [transthyretin amyloidosis](../../../../../transthyretin-amyloidosis.md). This classification is a present-day explanation, not a list of molecular diagnoses asserted for unnamed 1867 patients.[Introduction; Etiology, systemic types and common sites of deposition](../../../../../amyloidosis-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

Modern confirmation uses [histology](../../../../../histology.md) with [Congo red](../../../../../congo-red.md) and examination with a [polarizing microscope](../../../../../polarizing-microscope.md), followed by identification of the deposited [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md), for example with [mass spectrometry](../../../../../mass-spectrometry.md). The characteristic birefringence supports identification of [amyloid](../../../../../amyloid.md); typing distinguishes biologically different diseases. These are later laboratory methods, not apparatus available to the candidate in 1867.[§82.2 Diagnosis, Congo red, polarized light and amyloid subtyping](../../../../../systemic-light-chain-amyloidosis-in-the-ebmt-handbook.md)

Thus an adequate historical list starts with **[spleen](../../../../../spleen.md), [liver](../../../../../liver.md) and [kidneys](../../../../../kidney.md)**, while the modern explanation is extracellular [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) deposition rather than conversion of an organ into [starch](../../../../../starch.md).
