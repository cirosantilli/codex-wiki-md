**An albuminous [urine](../../../../../urine.md) specimen can reflect renal leakage, altered renal function, or added [blood plasma](../../../../../blood-plasma.md) [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) below the [kidney](../../../../../kidney.md).** Finding [serum albumin](../../../../../serum-albumin.md) in [urine](../../../../../urine.md) identifies a phenomenon, not a single [disease](../../../../../disease.md).

In the historical setting, the important group was the renal disorders gathered under [Bright's disease](../../../../../bright-s-disease.md): acute inflammatory disease, including that following [scarlet fever](../../../../../scarlet-fever.md), and chronic diseased or contracted [kidneys](../../../../../kidney.md). In current terms, [glomerulonephritis](../../../../../glomerulonephritis.md) damages the [glomerular filtration barrier](../../../../../glomerular-filtration-barrier.md), permitting increased passage of [serum albumin](../../../../../serum-albumin.md) into the filtrate. [Red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) and [urinary casts](../../../../../urinary-cast.md) may accompany the loss. Different modern [glomerular diseases](../../../../../glomerular-disease.md) can produce the same finding; the nineteenth-century umbrella does not identify one modern microscopic diagnosis.[History, the association of dropsy, albuminuria and renal disease](../../../../../bright-s-disease-by-the-university-of-leeds-library.md)

In [renal amyloidosis](../../../../../renal-amyloidosis.md), deposition in the [glomeruli](../../../../../glomerulus.md) disrupts their filtering structure; heavy [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md) and [nephrotic syndrome](../../../../../nephrotic-syndrome.md) may result. [Diabetic kidney disease](../../../../../diabetic-kidney-disease.md) and [hypertensive kidney disease](../../../../../hypertensive-kidney-disease.md) provide further modern examples of chronic injury to the filtering apparatus. These modern classifications distinguish mechanisms.[Etiology, glomerular causes including amyloidosis, diabetic nephropathy and postinfectious glomerulonephritis](../../../../../proteinuria-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

The [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md) group includes renal venous [congestion](../../../../../congestion.md) and impaired effective [kidney](../../../../../kidney.md) [perfusion](../../../../../perfusion.md). Altered pressures and injury to the filtering apparatus can increase [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md); an albuminous specimen does not by itself prove destructive [nephritis](../../../../../nephritis.md). [Fever](../../../../../fever.md) and severe acute illness can also produce transient [proteinuria](../../../../../proteinuria.md) through reversible changes in renal function. In [pre-eclampsia](../../../../../pre-eclampsia.md), injury to the filtering apparatus, including [glomerular endotheliosis](../../../../../glomerular-endotheliosis.md), can cause [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md).[Abstract, endothelial swelling and disruption of the filtration apparatus](../../../../../the-glomerular-injury-of-preeclampsia.md) Pregnancy alone does not make [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md) normal, and pressure from a gravid [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md) is not an adequate general explanation of this disorder.[Etiology, preeclampsia; Pathophysiology, functional and tubular proteinuria](../../../../../proteinuria-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

Finally distinguish renal loss from addition below the filtering apparatus. [Haematuria](../../../../../haematuria.md), [pyelonephritis](../../../../../pyelonephritis.md), [cystitis](../../../../../cystitis.md) and contamination by inflammatory or genital material can add [blood plasma](../../../../../blood-plasma.md) [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), including [serum albumin](../../../../../serum-albumin.md), to a collected specimen. [Microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) and a clean sample help distinguish these possibilities. [Tubulointerstitial nephritis](../../../../../tubulointerstitial-nephritis.md) reduces [proximal tubule](../../../../../proximal-tubule.md) recovery of filtered [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), but predominantly causes loss of smaller [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md). Likewise, overflow of [immunoglobulin light chains](../../../../../immunoglobulin-light-chain.md) in [multiple myeloma](../../../../../multiple-myeloma.md) is [proteinuria](../../../../../proteinuria.md), not necessarily [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md). [Haematuria](../../../../../haematuria.md) alone can contribute [blood plasma](../../../../../blood-plasma.md) [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), so modest protein positivity need not locate the bleeding in the [kidney](../../../../../kidney.md).[Clinical Significance, serum proteins accompanying haematuria](../../../../../hematuria-in-clinical-methods.md) The distinction matters because an old protein-precipitation test did not identify the molecular species.[Definition and Technique, heat and acid precipitation; Basic Science, glomerular, tubular and overflow proteins; Clinical Significance, urinary sediment](../../../../../proteinuria-in-clinical-methods.md)

A useful diagnostic comparison is repeated [urinalysis](../../../../../urinalysis.md), with [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) of the sediment and a modern [urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio](../../../../../urine-albumin-to-creatinine-ratio.md). Persistence, [red blood cell casts](../../../../../red-blood-cell-cast.md), and the clinical setting distinguish sustained [glomerular disease](../../../../../glomerular-disease.md) from a transient change or lower-tract addition. A positive protein test alone cannot make that distinction.[How is albuminuria detected?, urine albumin and creatinine measurement](../../../../../albuminuria-albumin-in-the-urine-by-niddk.md)
