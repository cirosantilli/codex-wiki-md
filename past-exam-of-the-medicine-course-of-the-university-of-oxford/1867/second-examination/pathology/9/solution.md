↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

**In infants, [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) often has a patchy airway-centred pattern and less typical localizing symptoms, while collapse and systemic illness complicate its recognition.** The classical comparison is with an adult's acute [lobar pneumonia](../../../../../lobar-pneumonia.md), but age does not impose an absolute anatomical division.

The adult reference pattern is relatively continuous consolidation of a lobe. In the traditional pathological sequence, [congestion](../../../../../congestion.md) is followed by red [hepatization](../../../../../hepatization.md), with [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md), [neutrophils](../../../../../neutrophil.md) and [fibrin](../../../../../fibrin.md) in the [alveoli](../../../../../alveolus.md); later breakdown of the [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) contributes to grey [hepatization](../../../../../hepatization.md), followed by clearance or organization. These stages are a descriptive model, can overlap, and are not a mandatory timetable for every adult infection.[Histopathology, lobar pneumonia and the four classical stages](../../../../../pneumonia-pathology-in-statpearls.md)

By comparison, an infant may have [bronchopneumonia](../../../../../bronchopneumonia.md): separate foci centred on small [bronchi](../../../../../bronchus.md) and [bronchioles](../../../../../bronchiole.md), extending into adjacent [alveoli](../../../../../alveolus.md), sometimes in both [lungs](../../../../../lung.md). Airway secretions and inflammatory material coexist with patches of [atelectasis](../../../../../atelectasis.md); lesions of different ages can be present together. Coalescent foci can eventually resemble a larger area of consolidation. The distinction is anatomical rather than proof of one specific infecting organism.[Bronchopneumonia, Pathologic Features, airway-centred exudation and coalescence](../../../../../pulmonary-infections-in-the-normal-host.md)

Question 8 supplies an essential historical qualification: some lesions labelled infantile “lobular pneumonia” were actually collapse. Distinguishing [atelectasis](../../../../../atelectasis.md) from inflammatory [exudate](../../../../../exudate.md) prevents that diagnostic inflation. The two conditions can nevertheless coexist.[pp. 111–112, secondary collapse formerly described as lobular pneumonia](../../../../../lectures-on-the-diseases-of-infancy-and-childhood-review-1855.md)

Clinically, an infant cannot report pleuritic chest pain and usually swallows rather than expectorates sputum. The adult combination of rigors, chest pain and conspicuous rusty sputum is therefore a poor required checklist. More useful infant signs include rapid [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md), lower chest indrawing, nasal flaring, grunting, poor feeding, reduced activity and sometimes [cyanosis](../../../../../cyanosis.md). [Fever](../../../../../fever.md) can be present, but the youngest infants may instead show temperature instability and nonspecific illness. Physical signs can be scattered when the lesions are scattered; absence of one large area of dullness does not exclude [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md).[Infant symptoms and signs of breathing difficulty](../../../../../pneumonia-symptoms-by-the-national-heart-lung-and-blood-institute.md)[Symptoms and Signs, respiratory distress and temperature instability](../../../../../neonatal-pneumonia-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

In current practice, the age group also changes the differential. Early neonatal [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) can be part of generalized [sepsis](../../../../../sepsis.md) acquired around birth; later infant infections include both viral and bacterial causes. Assessment integrates the illness history, [oxygen saturation](../../../../../oxygen-saturation.md), examination and selected laboratory or imaging evidence rather than assigning a diagnosis from age alone. A positive airway culture can reflect colonization, so the organism must be interpreted with the clinical and inflammatory evidence.[Etiology; Diagnosis, blood cultures, tracheal aspirates and colonization](../../../../../neonatal-pneumonia-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

The decisive comparison is therefore **patchy and mixed pathology, greater diagnostic difficulty, and frequent systemic presentation in infancy**, with [lobar pneumonia](../../../../../lobar-pneumonia.md) possible in children and [bronchopneumonia](../../../../../bronchopneumonia.md) possible in adults.
