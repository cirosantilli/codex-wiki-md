**[Vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md) is a coordinated nervous motor act that can be initiated from the [digestive system](../../../../../digestive-system.md), circulating emetic substances, the [vestibular system](../../../../../vestibular-system.md), or higher and lower [brain](../../../../../brain.md) pathways.** A cause need not lie in the [stomach](../../../../../stomach.md).

Local causes include irritant food or poisons, [gastritis](../../../../../gastritis.md), [gastroenteritis](../../../../../gastroenteritis.md), [gastric outlet obstruction](../../../../../gastric-outlet-obstruction.md) and [intestinal obstruction](../../../../../intestinal-obstruction.md). Chemical irritation, [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) or distension activates visceral sensory pathways, notably afferents of the [vagus nerve](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md), which relay to the [medulla oblongata](../../../../../medulla-oblongata.md). In obstruction, retention and distension provide continuing stimulation; merely seeing food return does not imply excessive gastric contractile strength. [Peritonitis](../../../../../peritonitis.md) and painful disease elsewhere in the abdomen can also recruit visceral afferent pathways.[Etiology; table Some Causes of Nausea and Vomiting, gastrointestinal disorders](../../../../../nausea-and-vomiting-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

Circulating emetic agents, including some drugs and metabolic products accumulating in [kidney failure](../../../../../kidney-failure.md), act partly through the [area postrema](../../../../../area-postrema.md), a chemosensitive region with unusually direct access to substances in the [blood](../../../../../blood.md). Gastrointestinal and blood-borne inputs converge through the [nucleus tractus solitarius](../../../../../nucleus-tractus-solitarius.md) and connected medullary networks. It is more accurate to describe interacting groups of [neurons](../../../../../neuron-split.md) than one isolated “vomiting centre”.[Abstract, area postrema, abdominal vagal afferents, nucleus tractus solitarius and distributed pattern generation](../../../../../central-neurocircuitry-associated-with-emesis.md)

Other important groups are [motion sickness](../../../../../motion-sickness.md), where information from the [vestibular system](../../../../../vestibular-system.md) conflicts with other sensory information; [migraine](../../../../../migraine.md) and intracranial disease, including [raised intracranial pressure](../../../../../raised-intracranial-pressure.md) from a [brain tumour](../../../../../brain-tumour.md) or [intracranial haemorrhage](../../../../../intracranial-haemorrhage.md); and higher-centre influences such as a repulsive sight, smell, fear or learned association. [Pregnancy-associated vomiting](../../../../../pregnancy-associated-vomiting.md) belongs in the differential, with endocrine and central contributions rather than an explanation that it is simply mechanical pressure by the [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md) upon the [stomach](../../../../../stomach.md). These causes engage different inputs to an overlapping final motor programme.[Etiology; table Some Causes of Nausea and Vomiting, CNS and systemic causes](../../../../../nausea-and-vomiting-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

The act itself requires several coordinated changes. The proximal [stomach](../../../../../stomach.md) and [lower oesophageal sphincter](../../../../../lower-oesophageal-sphincter.md) relax; retrograde activity can bring contents of the upper [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md) into the [stomach](../../../../../stomach.md); the [diaphragm](../../../../../diaphragm.md) and abdominal [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) generate a pressure gradient that expels the contents. During expulsion the upper passage opens while coordinated laryngeal closure helps protect the [airway](../../../../../airway.md). [Retching](../../../../../retching.md) and actual expulsion are related stages, and neither should be confused with effortless [regurgitation](../../../../../regurgitation.md).[Digestive tract correlates and neurophysiology; relaxation, retrograde giant contraction, retching and expulsion](../../../../../physiology-of-the-digestive-tract-correlates-of-vomiting.md)

This mechanism explains the diversity of causes: **many sensory and chemical inputs can recruit the same coordinated output**. A pressure recording and observation of gastrointestinal movements during an emetic response can distinguish the patterned act from a simple spontaneous return of contents; they would not, without further evidence, identify the original cause in an individual.
