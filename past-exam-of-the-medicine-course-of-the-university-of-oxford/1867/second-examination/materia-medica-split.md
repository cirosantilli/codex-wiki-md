# Materia medica

↑ **Parent:** [Second examination](split.md)

Original volume: [Examination papers for the Degree of Bachelor of Medicine by the University of Oxford (1864–1867)](../../../examination-papers-for-the-degree-of-bachelor-of-medicine-by-the-university-of-oxford-1864-1867.md). [Open the original paper in the scanned volume, PDF page 89](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3e/Examination_papers_for_the_Degree_of_Bachelor_of_medicine_-_1864-67_(IA_b24757810).pdf#page=89).

Original questions are transcribed from the public-domain edition. Credit: Wellcome Collection; original supplied by Royal College of Physicians, London. [Source and public-domain rights statement](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/d7b9zgnx).

The historical standard used below is the [British Pharmacopoeia by the General Medical Council (1867)](../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1867.md). A contemporary July publication notice records official publication notices dated 14 June 1867, before this December examination. Later reprints containing the additions of 1874 must be distinguished from this original issue.[July 1867, p. 99; notice of publication in the three Gazettes on 14 June](../../../british-pharmacopoeia-publication-notice-1867.md) These are explanations of historical [materia medica](materia-medica-split.md), with present [physiology](../../../physiology-split.md) identified separately where it changes the interpretation.
