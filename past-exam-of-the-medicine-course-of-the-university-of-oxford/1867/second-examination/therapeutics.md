## 1

↑ **Parent:** [Therapeutics](therapeutics.md)

**Original question.**

Under what circumstances would you resort to blood letting, general or local, and with what intention ?

<h3 id="1/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [1](#1)

**An 1867 answer would distinguish general depletion to relieve a supposed dangerous circulatory overload from local depletion intended to relieve a circumscribed inflammatory condition.** General [bloodletting](../../../biology.md#bloodletting) meant opening a [vein](../../../physiology.md#vein) by [venesection](../../../biology.md#venesection). Local [bloodletting](../../../biology.md#bloodletting) meant [medicinal leeches](../../../biology.md#medicinal-leech) or [wet cupping](../../../biology.md#wet-cupping); ordinary dry cupping did not remove [blood](../../../physiology.md#blood). In [wet cupping](../../../biology.md#wet-cupping), superficial incisions and a partially evacuated cup withdrew [blood](../../../physiology.md#blood) from the [skin](../../../physiology.md#skin). The apparatus and the quantity removed mattered: a supposedly local procedure could still produce substantial systemic [blood loss](../../../biology.md#blood-loss).

In the period's vocabulary, a robust person with an early “sthenic” attack, a strong full [pulse](../../../wave.md#pulse), severe oppression of breathing and an apparently engorged [lung](../../../anatomy.md#lung) might be considered for general [bloodletting](../../../biology.md#bloodletting). The intention was to lessen vascular distension and the work imposed on the [heart](../../../physiology.md#heart), or to arrest an advancing [inflammation](../../../biology.md#inflammation). Local [bloodletting](../../../biology.md#bloodletting) over a painful region was intended to moderate local [inflammation](../../../biology.md#inflammation) without the full depletion of [venesection](../../../biology.md#venesection). These were therapeutic theories, not proof that [blood](../../../physiology.md#blood) beneath the [skin](../../../physiology.md#skin) drained an inflamed organ selectively. A contemporary account of [pneumonia](../../../biology.md#pneumonia) explicitly preferred early [bloodletting](../../../biology.md#bloodletting) in severe “sthenic” cases and distinguished them from debilitated “asthenic” cases.[1866, pp. 23–29, distinctions between sthenic and asthenic cases and indications for early bloodletting](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#on-the-treatment-of-pneumonia-by-alexander-smith)

Even within that framework, the decision needed the stage of illness, previous [blood loss](../../../biology.md#blood-loss), age, general strength and response to any previous depletion. A weak rapid [pulse](../../../wave.md#pulse), pallor, faintness or exhausted convalescence argued against further [bloodletting](../../../biology.md#bloodletting). “There is [fever](../../../physiology.md#fever)” or “the [blood](../../../physiology.md#blood) looks inflammatory” was not an adequate indication. A hot flushed face alone could not establish excess circulating volume. The period evidence also needs scrutiny: selecting the most severe cases for [bloodletting](../../../biology.md#bloodletting), then comparing their recovery with mild unbled cases, confounds treatment with initial severity. The reported recoveries establish what was done; they do not isolate its causal effect.[1866, pp. 4–9, allocation by clinical severity and comparison of treatment periods](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#on-the-treatment-of-pneumonia-by-alexander-smith)

**Modern correction: remove [blood](../../../physiology.md#blood) only for a defined indication, not to treat ordinary [fever](../../../physiology.md#fever), [pneumonia](../../../biology.md#pneumonia) or a presumed surplus of “inflammatory” [blood](../../../physiology.md#blood).** In [haemochromatosis](../../../biology.md#haemochromatosis), controlled [venesection](../../../biology.md#venesection) removes [iron](../../../chemical-substance.md#iron) contained in [red blood cells](../../../biological-cell.md#red-blood-cell); repeated replacement of those [red blood cells](../../../biological-cell.md#red-blood-cell) draws down the body's [iron](../../../chemical-substance.md#iron) stores. The amount and frequency depend on [haemoglobin](../../../protein.md#hemoglobin) and [ferritin](../../../protein.md#ferritin) monitoring.[Phlebotomy; adjustment of frequency using iron and ferritin levels](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#treatment-of-hemochromatosis-by-niddk) In [polycythaemia vera](../../../biology.md#polycythaemia-vera), [venesection](../../../biology.md#venesection) reduces excessive [red blood cell](../../../biological-cell.md#red-blood-cell) mass and the associated risk of [thrombosis](../../../biology.md#thrombosis).[Treatment; reduction of blood thickness and clotting risk](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#polycythemia-vera-by-medlineplus) These measurable targets are different from the historical idea of “plethora”. In an exhausted person, indiscriminate [bloodletting](../../../biology.md#bloodletting) can worsen [anaemia](../../../biology.md#anaemia) and circulatory failure; modern care identifies and treats the cause of the [respiratory distress](../../../biology.md#respiratory-distress) or [inflammation](../../../biology.md#inflammation) instead.

↑ **Parent:** [Therapeutics](therapeutics.md)

**Original question.**

Describe a typical case of chorea. What are the usual causes of this complaint, and what would be your treatment under various circumstances ?

<h3 id="2/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [2](#2)

**The typical childhood picture is [Sydenham's chorea](../../../neurology.md#sydenham-s-chorea): irregular involuntary movements, impaired sustained voluntary actions, variable weakness and emotional disturbance.** A hypothetical schoolchild might gradually become unusually clumsy, drop utensils, spill drinks and produce deteriorating handwriting. Unpredictable movements pass between the face, trunk and limbs, giving facial grimaces and an unsteady gait. The [chorea](../../../neurology.md#chorea) may be asymmetric. Asking the child to maintain a handgrip can reveal alternating tightening and relaxation; an outstretched hand or protruded [tongue](../../../anatomy.md#tongue) may not remain still. These are examples of [motor impersistence](../../../neurology.md#motor-impersistence), not wilful disobedience. Speech and feeding can become difficult. Marked weakness may accompany severe [chorea](../../../neurology.md#chorea). Anxiety or emotional lability may be conspicuous. This is a pattern to look for, not a claim that the unspecified person in the question has any particular finding.[Clinical features, Chorea; abrupt nonrhythmic movements, muscle weakness and emotional lability](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#clinical-guidance-for-acute-rheumatic-fever-by-cdc)[Chapter 7, Table 7.6, functional impairment and motor impersistence](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#australian-guideline-for-acute-rheumatic-fever-and-rheumatic-heart-disease-2025)

An 1867 account would associate childhood [chorea](../../../neurology.md#chorea) with the period around puberty, preceding “rheumatism”, fright and general debility. It might also investigate supposed peripheral irritants such as [intestinal parasites](../../../biology.md#intestinal-parasite). These explanations were not equally secure. Fright could precede or aggravate the visible movements without establishing their fundamental cause, and finding [intestinal parasites](../../../biology.md#intestinal-parasite) did not prove that they caused the [chorea](../../../neurology.md#chorea). Contemporary discussion already recognized spontaneous recovery and disputed whether reported drug cures exceeded it.[1865, pp. 32–35, age and sex distribution, attributed triggers, mineral tonics and debate over spontaneous recovery](../../../neuroscience-bibliography.md#on-chorea-and-on-nature-and-art-in-the-treatment-by-james-turnbull)

A reasonable historical treatment outline begins with quiet surroundings, rest, adequate food, assistance with feeding and protection from injury in violent [chorea](../../../neurology.md#chorea). Genuine associated illness, including [anaemia](../../../biology.md#anaemia), required attention. Preparations of [iron](../../../chemical-substance.md#iron) were common “tonics”; other remedies of the period included experimental mineral drugs. Their use should be reported as historical practice, not as an effective modern recipe. A recovery after weeks of treatment does not distinguish a pharmacological effect from the natural course of [Sydenham's chorea](../../../neurology.md#sydenham-s-chorea).[1865, discussion of diet and regimen, preparations of iron and the evidential problem of therapeutic claims](../../../neuroscience-bibliography.md#on-chorea-and-on-nature-and-art-in-the-treatment-by-james-turnbull)

**Modern correction: the usual cause of the classic childhood syndrome is an immune-mediated consequence of preceding group A [streptococcal infection](../../../biology.md#streptococcal-infection), not fright, moral weakness or an intestinal “irritation”.** [Sydenham's chorea](../../../neurology.md#sydenham-s-chorea), named after [Thomas Sydenham](../../../physician.md#thomas-sydenham), can be the sole late manifestation of [acute rheumatic fever](../../../biology.md#acute-rheumatic-fever). Assess for [carditis](../../../biology.md#carditis) even without an obvious [heart murmur](../../../psychology.md#heart-murmur): [echocardiography](../../../psychology.md#echocardiography) can reveal otherwise silent valve disease. Treat residual group A [streptococcal infection](../../../biology.md#streptococcal-infection) and institute appropriate secondary [antibiotic prophylaxis](../../../pharmacology.md#antibiotic-prophylaxis); these measures prevent further attacks rather than instantly stopping established movements.[Central nervous system manifestations; subclinical carditis; Antibiotics; Prevention of recurrent attacks](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#clinical-guidance-for-acute-rheumatic-fever-by-cdc)

Management varies with function. Mild [Sydenham's chorea](../../../neurology.md#sydenham-s-chorea) may need supportive measures alone. When eating, walking or self-care is substantially impaired, specialist care can add a movement-suppressing drug such as [carbamazepine](../../../pharmacology.md#carbamazepine). Severe disease may justify [corticosteroid](../../../pharmacology.md#corticosteroid) treatment; very severe or refractory disease may need further specialist [immunotherapy](../../../pharmacology.md#immunotherapy). Evidence and practice differ between guidelines, and improvement is not necessarily immediate. [sodium valproate](../../../pharmacology.md#sodium-valproate) is another option in selected circumstances, but its reproductive and other adverse effects are material limitations.[Chapter 7, Anticonvulsant agents, Corticosteroids for chorea and Table 7.6](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#australian-guideline-for-acute-rheumatic-fever-and-rheumatic-heart-disease-2025)[Recommendations 8.5–8.10 and 9.1–9.4; immunotherapy and symptomatic treatment](../../../neuroscience-bibliography.md#evaluation-diagnosis-and-treatment-of-sydenham-chorea-consensus-guidelines-2025) Atypical onset, progression, exposure to relevant drugs or other focal [neurological signs](../../../psychology.md#neurological-sign) calls for reconsideration of the cause of the [chorea](../../../neurology.md#chorea), rather than automatic assignment to [acute rheumatic fever](../../../biology.md#acute-rheumatic-fever).

**Original question.**

What would you do in cases of _Pityriasis versicolor_, _Tinea decalvans_, and _contagious Ringworm_, and what common principle runs through their rational treatment ?

<h3 id="3/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

**The historical common principle was to identify and destroy a superficial fungal parasite, remove material harbouring it and prevent reinfection. Modern diagnosis preserves this principle for genuine [fungal infection](../../../biology.md#fungal-infection), but rejects its indiscriminate application to “tinea decalvans”.** The three labels must therefore be discussed separately.

In [pityriasis versicolor](../../../biology.md#pityriasis-versicolor), look for finely scaling patches of altered [skin pigmentation](../../../biology.md#skin-pigmentation), commonly on the trunk. Examine superficial [skin](../../../physiology.md#skin) scrapings by [microscopy](../../../optics.md#microscopy) when uncertain. Historically, washing, removal of loose scale and a topical “parasiticide” aimed to reach the organism in the outer [epidermis](../../../physiology.md#epidermis). Today [pityriasis versicolor](../../../biology.md#pityriasis-versicolor) is associated with overgrowth of [Malassezia](../../../organism.md#malassezia) [yeasts](../../../organism.md#yeast) already present on the [skin](../../../physiology.md#skin); it is not ordinary contagious [ringworm](../../../biology.md#ringworm) and is not evidence of poor cleanliness.[Clinical description and Patient advice; normal skin yeast, noncontagious condition](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#pityriasis-versicolor-by-nottinghamshire-area-prescribing-committee) Topical [ketoconazole](../../../pharmacology.md#ketoconazole) or [selenium sulfide](../../../pharmacology.md#selenium-sulfide) can control it. Extensive or recurrent disease sometimes needs other treatment. Loss of scale and control of the [yeast](../../../organism.md#yeast) need not immediately restore [skin pigmentation](../../../biology.md#skin-pigmentation); residual colour can persist for weeks or months. A [Wood's lamp](../../../psychology.md#wood-s-lamp) can assist examination, but is not a substitute for the whole clinical assessment.[Diagnosis; topical treatment; Outcome](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#tinea-versicolor-diagnosis-and-treatment-by-the-american-academy-of-dermatology)

“Tinea decalvans” is the critical historical trap. Some nineteenth-century writers interpreted patchy baldness as parasitic and advocated extraction of affected hairs followed by prolonged topical treatment. A January–June 1867 digest explicitly calls the condition contagious and reports a [mercury(II) chloride](../../../chemical-substance.md#mercury-ii-chloride) preparation. That is evidence of a contemporary belief, not evidence that the proposed organism caused every case of patchy baldness.[Article on Tinea decalvans immediately preceding Art. 65; report from Medical Press and Circular, 10 April 1867](../../../biology-bibliography.md#half-yearly-abstract-of-the-medical-sciences-january-june-1867) The label often covered what is now called [alopecia areata](../../../biology.md#alopecia-areata), an immune-mediated form of [hair loss](../../../biology.md#hair-loss). Smooth bald patches require differentiation from scaly patches with broken hairs and demonstrated [fungal infection](../../../biology.md#fungal-infection). Confirm the diagnosis before treating. In established [alopecia areata](../../../biology.md#alopecia-areata), a period “parasiticide” is inappropriate: observation or diagnosis-specific treatment, including local [corticosteroids](../../../pharmacology.md#corticosteroid) in suitable cases, is considered instead. Neither [mercury(II) chloride](../../../chemical-substance.md#mercury-ii-chloride) nor destructive epilation is a modern treatment for [alopecia areata](../../../biology.md#alopecia-areata).[Diagnostic assessment; limited recent patches; topical and injected corticosteroids](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#alopecia-areata-diagnosis-and-treatment-by-the-american-academy-of-dermatology)

For contagious [ringworm](../../../biology.md#ringworm), establish the site and extent. An enlarging scaly ring on bare [skin](../../../physiology.md#skin) suggests [tinea corporis](../../../biology.md#tinea-corporis); scaly bald areas with broken hairs suggest [tinea capitis](../../../biology.md#tinea-capitis). Examine scrapings or hairs by [microscopy](../../../optics.md#microscopy) and, where appropriate, [fungal culture](../../../biology.md#fungal-culture). Clean clothing, towels, combs and brushes; avoid sharing them and investigate relevant human or animal sources. These measures address transmission, while an [antifungal drug](../../../pharmacology.md#antifungal-drug) treats the affected tissue.[Diagnosis; Treatment; Prevention, especially shared personal items and infected animals](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#ringworm-by-who) Limited [tinea corporis](../../../biology.md#tinea-corporis) can often be treated topically. [Tinea capitis](../../../biology.md#tinea-capitis) needs an oral [antifungal drug](../../../pharmacology.md#antifungal-drug), such as [griseofulvin](../../../pharmacology.md#griseofulvin) or [terbinafine](../../../pharmacology.md#terbinafine) chosen for the circumstances, because a cream alone does not clear the infected hair-bearing site. An adjunctive shampoo can reduce spread but does not replace systemic treatment. Unsupervised [corticosteroid](../../../pharmacology.md#corticosteroid) cream can mask or worsen [ringworm](../../../biology.md#ringworm).[Ringworm on the skin; Ringworm on the scalp; Things to avoid](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#treatment-of-ringworm-by-cdc)[Scalp; antifungal shampoo as an adjunct and prevention of household reinfection](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#ringworm-diagnosis-and-treatment-by-the-american-academy-of-dermatology)

Thus the useful unifying idea is **cause-directed treatment that reaches the affected tissue**, with transmission control when infection is transmissible. It does not follow that all three nineteenth-century names denote the same class of disease, or that every bald patch requires killing a parasite.

**Original question.**

What circumstances would guide your recommendation of Paracentesis thoracis in a case of fluid in the pleura ?

<h3 id="4/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [4](#4)

**Recommend [thoracentesis](../../../biology.md#thoracentesis) when removing [pleural fluid](../../../biology.md#pleural-fluid) is likely to relieve important respiratory compromise or answer a consequential diagnostic question; the size, course, cause and accessibility of the [pleural effusion](../../../biology.md#pleural-effusion) govern the decision.** “Paracentesis thoracis” is the older name. Merely detecting some fluid is insufficient.

At the bedside in 1867, the assessment would combine the history with asymmetry of chest movement, enlargement of the affected side, [percussion](../../../psychology.md#percussion) dullness, diminished breath sounds on [auscultation](../../../psychology.md#auscultation) and displacement of the [heart](../../../physiology.md#heart). A large or increasing [pleural effusion](../../../biology.md#pleural-effusion) with oppressive [dyspnoea](../../../biology.md#dyspnoea), inability to lie comfortably, a displaced [heart](../../../physiology.md#heart) or failure to improve was a stronger indication than a small resolving collection. The opposite [lung](../../../anatomy.md#lung)'s condition also mattered: someone with limited reserve could tolerate compression poorly. These signs have to be interpreted together because extensive [lung](../../../anatomy.md#lung) disease can also produce dullness or abnormal breath sounds.

The distinction between a serous [pleural effusion](../../../biology.md#pleural-effusion) and [empyema](../../../biology.md#empyema) changes the object of the operation. In the former, the immediate object is decompression and allowing the compressed [lung](../../../anatomy.md#lung) to expand. In [empyema](../../../biology.md#empyema), evacuation of infected material is required and continuing drainage may be necessary. Recurrent [pleural fluid](../../../biology.md#pleural-fluid) implies a persisting cause. Conversely, an improving small collection could be watched while treating the associated disease. A period report describes waiting for absorption, then confirming fluid with a grooved needle and evacuating it through a trocar and cannula when the signs and displacement persisted. It illustrates contemporary instruments and reasoning; it is not a safe modern procedural template.[British Medical Journal, 27 April 1867, Case II; serial assessment and trocar-and-cannula evacuation](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#pleuritic-effusion-paracentesis-thoracis-recovery-1867)

**Modern correction: separate diagnostic aspiration, therapeutic aspiration and drainage of [pleural infection](../../../biology.md#pleural-infection).** Use imaging to establish the collection and a safe route. A new unexplained unilateral [pleural effusion](../../../biology.md#pleural-effusion) may need sampling even without severe [dyspnoea](../../../biology.md#dyspnoea). [Pleural fluid](../../../biology.md#pleural-fluid) testing can include biochemical analysis, [cytology](../../../biology.md#cytology) and [microbiological culture](../../../biology.md#microbiological-culture), selected for the suspected cause. With suspected [pleural infection](../../../biology.md#pleural-infection), frank [pus](../../../biology.md#pus) or sufficiently low [pleural fluid](../../../biology.md#pleural-fluid) [pH](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#ph) supports chest drainage rather than repeated simple taps. For example, in the relevant suspected-infection pathway, [pH](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#ph) at or below 7.2 signifies high risk and supports drainage if an accessible volume can be drained safely. A typical bilateral collection responding to treatment of [heart failure](../../../biology.md#heart-failure) can be approached differently from an unexplained unilateral one.[Investigation of undiagnosed unilateral pleural effusion; Pleural infection, recommendations on pleural-fluid pH and intercostal drainage](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#british-thoracic-society-guideline-for-pleural-disease-2023)

Risk assessment includes [bleeding](../../../biology.md#bleeding) risk, ability to cooperate and the availability of an accessible fluid pocket. A very small or inaccessible collection may make aspiration inappropriate. [Thoracic ultrasonography](../../../biology.md#thoracic-ultrasonography) is required for [pleural effusion](../../../biology.md#pleural-effusion) aspiration. For therapeutic aspiration, drain slowly with an appropriate catheter; generally do not exceed 1.5 litres in one attempt and stop earlier for chest pain, tightness, persistent [cough](../../../biology.md#cough) or worsening [dyspnoea](../../../biology.md#dyspnoea). Complications include [pneumothorax](../../../biology.md#pneumothorax), [bleeding](../../../biology.md#bleeding) and [re-expansion pulmonary oedema](../../../biology.md#re-expansion-pulmonary-oedema). Repeated accumulation requires a plan for its underlying cause and, where indicated, a longer-term drainage strategy.[Pleural aspiration: indications, relative contraindications, ultrasound guidance and drainage volume; summary of clinical practice points](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#british-thoracic-society-clinical-statement-on-pleural-procedures-2023) The durable principle is to compare the harm caused by the collection with the benefit and risk of the proposed procedure, rather than using a single physical sign as an automatic command to operate.

## 5

↑ **Parent:** [Therapeutics](therapeutics.md)

**Original question.**

What are the doses of Alcohol which may be advantageously given in various forms of Fever? Describe the symptoms which regulate its administration.

<h3 id="5/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [5](#5)

**No single dose of [ethanol](../../../metabolism.md#ethanol) can be inferred from the word [fever](../../../physiology.md#fever). In 1867, wine or spirits were used variably for supposed exhaustion; the quantities describe disputed historical practice, not a beneficial modern treatment.** It is essential to distinguish ounces of wine or spirits from ounces of absolute [ethanol](../../../metabolism.md#ethanol). Equal beverage volumes do not contain equal [ethanol](../../../metabolism.md#ethanol) quantities, and the historical sources do not provide a universal standardized strength.

The intended historical distinction was between an uncomplicated febrile illness in a person maintaining circulation and food intake, for which a stimulant might be omitted, and a low or “adynamic” state with increasing prostration. In [typhus](../../../biology.md#typhus), prescribers who used [ethanol](../../../metabolism.md#ethanol) as a stimulant looked for deterioration of the [pulse](../../../wave.md#pulse), cold extremities, weakness, tremulousness, inability to take nourishment and failing responsiveness. In [typhoid fever](../../../biology.md#typhoid-fever), the same general assessment had to be combined with attention to abdominal complications; a new collapse might signify [gastrointestinal bleeding](../../../biology.md#gastrointestinal-bleeding) or [intestinal perforation](../../../biology.md#intestinal-perforation), rather than an abstract need for more wine. A [fever](../../../physiology.md#fever) with a hot [skin](../../../physiology.md#skin) and rapid but well-sustained [pulse](../../../wave.md#pulse) did not by itself establish a need for [ethanol](../../../metabolism.md#ethanol). Nor did [delirium](../../../neurology.md#delirium) distinguish exhaustion from drug effects.

A useful dose answer must supply actual contemporary quantities while admitting their limits. The 1867 Dundee [typhus](../../../biology.md#typhus) report records marked variation: only 44.7% of 2476 cases in its stimulant table received them. Among treated cases, the reported averages of each individual's maximum daily quantity were 3.19 ounces of wine and 2.6 ounces of spirits. Its corresponding figures below age 25 were 3.5 and 1.09 ounces; above age 50, 2.1 and 5.7 ounces. These are beverage amounts in historical ounces, not amounts of pure [ethanol](../../../metabolism.md#ethanol), and not a recommendation to combine both in every case. The author expressly warns that “stimulation” could describe very different quantities and that averages can mislead.[1867, pp. 154–157, Table V and explanation of average maximum daily quantities](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#typhus-statistics-of-the-dundee-royal-infirmary-with-remarks-by-thomas-john-maclagan) **The defensible period rule is small divided quantities, if used at all, reassessed by effect rather than a fixed allowance for every disease name.** Those observational figures cannot establish an advantageous dose for [typhoid fever](../../../biology.md#typhoid-fever), [scarlet fever](../../../biology.md#scarlet-fever) or another distinct illness.

A second report published in October 1867 stresses individual response: even half an ounce of port could produce flushing or sleep, while an occasional adult two-ounce whisky draught was described as a sedating intervention. These examples span very different physiological effects; they are not interchangeable routine doses. The author rejects a fixed schedule that continues unchanged despite a changing condition.[1867, pp. 204–207, variable response, stimulation versus narcosis and rejection of fixed schedules](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#clinical-study-of-stimulation-in-typhus-by-james-burn-russell)

The signs a historical prescriber hoped to see after a trial were a fuller, steadier [pulse](../../../wave.md#pulse), warmer extremities, clearer awareness, more restful sleep and improved ability to take food. Increasing flushing, excitement, worsening [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting), stupor or a deteriorating [pulse](../../../wave.md#pulse) argued against simply escalating it. These are a reconstruction of the period's claimed bedside test, not validated endpoints proving that [ethanol](../../../metabolism.md#ethanol) treats the underlying [infection](../../../biology.md#infection).[1867, pp. 204–207, response-dependent administration, harm from continued dosing and limitations of apparent symptom improvement](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#clinical-study-of-stimulation-in-typhus-by-james-burn-russell) Natural improvement, fluid intake and selection of the sickest people for treatment all complicate causal interpretation of the reports.

**Modern correction: there is no therapeutic beverage-alcohol dose for [fever](../../../physiology.md#fever) or [sepsis](../../../biology.md#sepsis).** Measure severity and identify the cause, maintain appropriate fluid and nutritional support, and treat relevant [infection](../../../biology.md#infection). In suspected [sepsis](../../../biology.md#sepsis), assessment of [blood pressure](../../../physiology.md#blood-pressure), consciousness, [pulmonary ventilation](../../../physiology.md#pulmonary-ventilation) and perfusion guides escalation; current guidance specifies assessed [intravenous fluid](../../../biology.md#intravenous-fluid) resuscitation and reassessment, not wine as a circulatory treatment.[NG253, Managing suspected sepsis; high-risk management and intravenous fluids](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#suspected-sepsis-in-people-aged-16-or-over-by-nice) If habitual heavy [ethanol](../../../metabolism.md#ethanol) exposure introduces a risk of [alcohol withdrawal](../../../neurology.md#alcohol-withdrawal), that is a separate diagnosis. Monitored [benzodiazepine](../../../pharmacology.md#benzodiazepine) treatment and indicated [thiamine](../../../biology.md#thiamine) address that risk; administering wine for the [fever](../../../physiology.md#fever) is not its modern management.[CG100, 1.1 acute alcohol withdrawal and 1.2 Wernicke's encephalopathy](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#alcohol-use-disorders-diagnosis-and-management-of-physical-complications-by-nice)

## 6

↑ **Parent:** [Therapeutics](therapeutics.md)

**Original question.**

How do you treat severe Hæmoptysis in a Phthisical person ?

<h3 id="6/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [6](#6)

**Severe [haemoptysis](../../../biology.md#haemoptysis) in a person described historically as “phthisical” requires immediate attention to breathing and circulatory danger; the treatment of chronic wasting cannot wait in place of control of the acute [bleeding](../../../biology.md#bleeding).** The historical label suggests [pulmonary tuberculosis](../../../biology.md#pulmonary-tuberculosis), but does not itself prove that diagnosis or identify the bleeding vessel.

In period terms, the sensible supportive aims were quiet, reduced exertion and observation of the [blood loss](../../../biology.md#blood-loss) and general condition. The intent was to avoid provoking further [cough](../../../biology.md#cough) or dislodging a clot, while preserving the person's remaining strength. A depleted person with chronic wasting was a poor candidate for reflex general [bloodletting](../../../biology.md#bloodletting). The historical pharmacological literature included [astringents](../../../pharmacology.md#astringent), mineral salts and external cold, but the mere sequence “remedy administered, [bleeding](../../../biology.md#bleeding) stopped” could not establish efficacy: [haemoptysis](../../../biology.md#haemoptysis) often varied or stopped temporarily on its own.

This uncertainty was recognized before the examination. An 1862 discussion lists [astringents](../../../pharmacology.md#astringent) such as [lead acetate](../../../chemical-substance.md#lead-acetate), emetic drugs and external ice among competing treatments, and questions the evidential value of events following their use. It also records opposing fashions of severe dietary restriction and frequent feeding with stimulants.[1862, pp. 553–557, opening discussion of competing specifics and the limitations of apparent therapeutic success](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#on-the-treatment-of-tubercular-hemoptysis-by-william-brinton) The same author advocated a [digitalis preparation](../../../pharmacology.md#digitalis-preparation) with close [pulse](../../../wave.md#pulse) observation for severe attacks, hoping to influence the circulation; he emphasized its dangers. This is a period proposal, not modern emergency treatment. The historical explanation is why rest and supposed haemostatic remedies were attempted, not a claim that they reliably closed a ruptured vessel. No toxic nineteenth-century mixture is recommended here.

**Modern correction: life-threatening [haemoptysis](../../../biology.md#haemoptysis) is an [airway](../../../physiology.md#airway) emergency, often killing through obstruction and loss of gas exchange.** Its severity depends on respiratory and circulatory effects, not just the estimated volume in a basin. Obtain emergency specialist support, maintain oxygenation and protect the unaffected [lung](../../../anatomy.md#lung); when the bleeding side is known, positioning that side down can limit contamination of the other side. Secure suitable [intravenous access](../../../biology.md#intravenous-access), assess [haemoglobin](../../../protein.md#hemoglobin) and [coagulation](../../../biology.md#coagulation), prepare for needed [blood transfusion](../../../biology.md#blood-transfusion) and correct important [coagulopathy](../../../biology.md#coagulopathy). In unstable ongoing [bleeding](../../../biology.md#bleeding), securing the [airway](../../../physiology.md#airway) and [bronchoscopy](../../../biology.md#bronchoscopy) can take precedence over transport for imaging.[2023, initial management of life-threatening hemoptysis; airway isolation, positioning and haemodynamic stabilization](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#society-of-chest-imaging-and-interventions-consensus-guidelines-for-the-interventional-radiology-management-of-hemoptysis)

Once sufficiently stable, [computed tomography angiography](../../../biology.md#computed-tomography-angiography) helps locate the source and plan treatment. [Bronchial artery embolisation](../../../biology.md#bronchial-artery-embolisation) is a principal means of controlling severe or recurrent [haemoptysis](../../../biology.md#haemoptysis) from abnormal systemic vessels. [Bronchoscopy](../../../biology.md#bronchoscopy) can assist localization and temporary control; selected lesions or failed endovascular control may require [surgery](../../../biology.md#surgery). Do not assume every [tuberculosis](../../../biology.md#tuberculosis)-associated haemorrhage comes from the same vascular bed. The intervention must match the demonstrated source. Modern management also treats the underlying disease; stopping [bleeding](../../../biology.md#bleeding) alone does not cure [pulmonary tuberculosis](../../../biology.md#pulmonary-tuberculosis).[2022, Table 1; indications; diagnostic and treatment pathways](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#cirse-standards-of-practice-on-bronchial-artery-embolisation) Suspected active [pulmonary tuberculosis](../../../biology.md#pulmonary-tuberculosis) warrants appropriate respiratory infection-control measures, diagnostic sampling when safe and a susceptibility-informed multidrug [antibiotic](../../../biology.md#antibiotic) regimen once the diagnosis and treatment plan are established.[Choice of regimen; drug susceptibility, coexisting conditions and interactions](../../../microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography.md#treatment-for-drug-susceptible-tuberculosis-disease-by-cdc) Quiet surroundings can still help, but neither ice nor a historical [astringent](../../../pharmacology.md#astringent) substitutes for these measures.

## 7

↑ **Parent:** [Therapeutics](therapeutics.md)

**Original question.**

What guides you in the treatment of chronic Vomiting—on what may it depend—and in what manner do you proceed according to your diagnosis ?

<h3 id="7/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [7](#7)

**The guide is the diagnosis and the physiological cost of persistent [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting), not the symptom alone. Establish whether material is truly vomited, search for local and extra-gastric causes, correct depletion and then treat the cause.** A distinction between [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting), passive [regurgitation](../../../physiology.md#regurgitation) and [rumination syndrome](../../../biology.md#rumination-syndrome) prevents several different disorders being forced into one category. First assess inability to retain fluid, weight loss, [dehydration](../../../chemical-reaction.md#dehydration), [gastrointestinal bleeding](../../../biology.md#gastrointestinal-bleeding), severe abdominal pain or distension and [neurological signs](../../../psychology.md#neurological-sign). Significant danger requires prompt investigation and support before a prolonged trial of symptom remedies.[2025, diagnostic algorithm; exclusion of secondary causes; distinction from regurgitation and rumination](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#european-guideline-on-chronic-nausea-and-vomiting-by-ueg-and-esnm)

The history should establish duration, relation to meals, retained undigested food, pain, [nausea](../../../biology.md#nausea), the character of the material, bowel function, weight change, pregnancy possibility and exposure to drugs or [ethanol](../../../metabolism.md#ethanol). Delayed large-volume [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting) of food with weight loss suggests retention, but does not distinguish [gastric outlet obstruction](../../../physiology.md#gastric-outlet-obstruction) from impaired emptying by itself. Early postprandial discomfort, [haematemesis](../../../biology.md#haematemesis) or an abdominal mass changes the investigation. [Bilious vomiting](../../../biology.md#bilious-vomiting) with distension and failure to pass stool raises concern for [gastrointestinal obstruction](../../../biology.md#gastrointestinal-obstruction). Headache, visual disturbance or focal [neurological signs](../../../psychology.md#neurological-sign) directs attention beyond the [stomach](../../../physiology.md#stomach); [kidney](../../../anatomy.md#kidney) disease, metabolic disturbances and pregnancy are also possible causes. None can be chosen as the diagnosis from the short source question alone.[2025, secondary causes, obstruction, medication effects, metabolic and central nervous system causes](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#european-guideline-on-chronic-nausea-and-vomiting-by-ueg-and-esnm)

An 1867 physician could already distinguish suspected [gastric ulcer](../../../biology.md#gastric-ulcer), chronic [gastritis](../../../physiology.md#gastritis), [cancer](../../../biology.md#cancer), obstruction near the [pylorus](../../../physiology.md#pylorus), pregnancy-related [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting), [uraemia](../../../biology.md#uraemia) and “sympathetic” [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting) accompanying disease elsewhere. The therapeutic reasoning was to remove a provoking dietary or medicinal cause, adapt food to what could be retained, investigate an obstructive or destructive lesion and address the associated systemic disease. Symptom suppression alone could conceal continued starvation. A 1867 digest reproduces an account of opium controlling [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting) in advanced consumption. It establishes an example of period palliation, not proof of cure and not a general prescription for chronic [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting).[Art. 41, Vomiting, extract from The Indigestions; symptomatic palliation in advanced pulmonary disease](../../../biology-bibliography.md#half-yearly-abstract-of-the-medical-sciences-january-june-1867)

Modern investigations are chosen by that differential: examination and appropriate [blood tests](../../../psychology.md#blood-test) for depletion or systemic disease, a [pregnancy test](../../../psychology.md#pregnancy-test) where relevant, [endoscopy](../../../psychology.md#endoscopy) for a suspected upper digestive lesion, and imaging when [gastrointestinal obstruction](../../../biology.md#gastrointestinal-obstruction) is possible. [Gastroparesis](../../../biology.md#gastroparesis) specifically means delayed [gastric emptying](../../../biology.md#gastric-emptying) without mechanical obstruction. After excluding obstruction, demonstrate abnormal emptying rather than diagnosing it from [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting) alone. A standardized [gastric emptying scintigraphy](../../../biology.md#gastric-emptying-scintigraphy) follows a labelled meal with a [gamma camera](../../../physics.md#gamma-camera); serial measurements test how much remains in the [stomach](../../../physiology.md#stomach). This apparatus addresses a physiological question that simple inspection of vomited food cannot answer.[Exclusion of blockage; gastric-emptying scan and measurement after a labelled meal](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#diagnosis-of-gastroparesis-by-niddk)

Treatment then branches with the diagnosis. **Obstruction needs decompression and treatment of the obstructing lesion; escalating an [antiemetic](../../../pharmacology.md#antiemetic) does not remove it.** A confirmed [peptic ulcer](../../../biology.md#peptic-ulcer) may require [Helicobacter pylori](../../../organism.md#helicobacter-pylori) eradication, [proton pump inhibitor](../../../pharmacology.md#proton-pump-inhibitor) treatment and withdrawal of an ulcer-provoking [nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug](../../../pharmacology.md#nonsteroidal-anti-inflammatory-drug) where possible. A suspected malignant lesion needs tissue diagnosis and appropriate specialist treatment.[CG184, 1.7, interventions for peptic ulcer disease and exclusion of malignancy in unhealed ulcer](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#gastro-oesophageal-reflux-disease-and-dyspepsia-in-adults-by-nice) In confirmed [gastroparesis](../../../biology.md#gastroparesis), smaller suitable meals, nutritional planning, correction of a contributing [diabetes](../../../biology.md#diabetes) problem and selected prokinetic or [antiemetic](../../../pharmacology.md#antiemetic) treatment have distinct purposes: an [antiemetic](../../../pharmacology.md#antiemetic) may reduce [nausea](../../../biology.md#nausea) without accelerating [gastric emptying](../../../biology.md#gastric-emptying). Persistent nutritional failure may require feeding beyond the [stomach](../../../physiology.md#stomach).[Changing eating habits; Medicines; Oral or nasal tube feeding and Jejunostomy tube feeding](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#treatment-for-gastroparesis-by-niddk)

Medication-induced [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting), metabolic disease, pregnancy-related illness and central causes require their own treatment pathways. Fluid and [electrolyte](../../../electrochemistry.md#electrolyte) losses need assessment and correction throughout. Reassess intake, weight and function as well as the frequency of [vomiting](../../../physiology.md#vomiting). If no structural or metabolic explanation is established, investigate the relevant disorder of motility or gut–brain interaction on positive criteria; “nothing found yet” does not justify either a fabricated diagnosis or a dismissal of the symptom.
