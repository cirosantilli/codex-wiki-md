**Original question.**

What are the varieties of Dislocation of the Shoulder-joint, and what are the principles of treatment.

<h3 id="1/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [1](#1)

**The chief historical varieties are subglenoid, subcoracoid, subclavicular and posterior [shoulder dislocation](../../../biology.md#shoulder-dislocation); treatment restores the [humeral head](../../../anatomy.md#humeral-head) gently, checks the [axillary nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#axillary-nerve) and circulation, and restores useful movement.**

The [glenohumeral joint](../../../anatomy.md#glenohumeral-joint) combines a large [humeral head](../../../anatomy.md#humeral-head) with a shallow [glenoid cavity](../../../anatomy.md#glenoid-cavity). Its range of movement depends on the comparatively loose [joint capsule](../../../anatomy.md#joint-capsule); the [glenoid labrum](../../../anatomy.md#glenoid-labrum), [rotator cuff](../../../physiology.md#rotator-cuff) and surrounding [muscles](../../../anatomy.md#muscle) provide stability. An injury can therefore lever the [humeral head](../../../anatomy.md#humeral-head) out of the [glenoid cavity](../../../anatomy.md#glenoid-cavity) while tearing the [joint capsule](../../../anatomy.md#joint-capsule) and sometimes the [glenoid labrum](../../../anatomy.md#glenoid-labrum) or adjacent [bone](../../../anatomy.md#bone).

The historical names describe where the [humeral head](../../../anatomy.md#humeral-head) is found relative to the [scapula](../../../anatomy.md#scapula) and [clavicle](../../../anatomy.md#clavicle):

- In subglenoid [shoulder dislocation](../../../biology.md#shoulder-dislocation), the [humeral head](../../../anatomy.md#humeral-head) lies below the [glenoid cavity](../../../anatomy.md#glenoid-cavity), toward the [axilla](../../../anatomy.md#axilla). Old descriptions of displacement “downwards into the axilla” need not mean the persistently overhead arm position now called [luxatio erecta](../../../biology.md#luxatio-erecta).
- In subcoracoid [shoulder dislocation](../../../biology.md#shoulder-dislocation), the [humeral head](../../../anatomy.md#humeral-head) lies in front of the [glenoid cavity](../../../anatomy.md#glenoid-cavity), beneath the [coracoid process](../../../anatomy.md#coracoid-process). This is the familiar anteroinferior pattern.
- In subclavicular [shoulder dislocation](../../../biology.md#shoulder-dislocation), the [humeral head](../../../anatomy.md#humeral-head) has passed farther forwards and medially, beneath the [clavicle](../../../anatomy.md#clavicle); this is a rarer, more extensive anterior displacement.
- In posterior [shoulder dislocation](../../../biology.md#shoulder-dislocation), historically also called subspinous displacement, the [humeral head](../../../anatomy.md#humeral-head) lies behind the [glenoid cavity](../../../anatomy.md#glenoid-cavity), toward the [scapular spine](../../../anatomy.md#scapular-spine). Modern assessment is particularly alert to this pattern after a [seizure](../../../neurology.md#seizure) or electrical injury. True inferior [shoulder dislocation](../../../biology.md#shoulder-dislocation), [luxatio erecta](../../../biology.md#luxatio-erecta), and exceptional superior displacement complete the directional classification; they should not be forced into the common anterior pattern.[Sections 3–4: anterior and posterior displacement and their mechanisms](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#shoulder-dislocations-emergency-department-by-the-royal-children-s-hospital-melbourne)

An 1867 answer would stress early recognition, relaxation of the [muscles](../../../anatomy.md#muscle), and controlled traction with suitable countertraction or gentle leverage to guide the [humeral head](../../../anatomy.md#humeral-head) through the torn [joint capsule](../../../anatomy.md#joint-capsule). [Anaesthesia](../../../biology.md#anaesthesia) made relaxation practicable. These are principles, not a reason to reproduce old forceful manoeuvres or indiscriminate pulley traction: force can produce a [fracture](../../../biology.md#fracture) or damage the [axillary artery](../../../physiology.md#axillary-artery) and [brachial plexus](../../../neuroscience.md#brachial-plexus).

Current assessment establishes the direction and associated [fracture](../../../biology.md#fracture), usually with [radiography](../../../electromagnetic-radiation.md#radiography) in appropriate views. Before and after [joint reduction](../../../biology.md#joint-reduction), record [pulse](../../../wave.md#pulse), [perfusion](../../../physiology.md#perfusion), distal movement and sensation, including sensation over the lateral [deltoid muscle](../../../physiology.md#deltoid-muscle) supplied by the [axillary nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#axillary-nerve). A [nerve injury](../../../neuroscience.md#nerve-injury) accompanying [joint dislocation](../../../biology.md#joint-dislocation) makes prompt restoration of alignment especially important.[BOASt, response to identification of peripheral nerve injury, recommendation 5](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#peripheral-nerve-injury-by-the-british-orthopaedic-association) [Joint reduction](../../../biology.md#joint-reduction) is performed by trained clinicians with suitable [analgesia](../../../neuroscience.md#analgesia) and, when needed, [sedation](../../../biology.md#sedation) or [anaesthesia](../../../biology.md#anaesthesia). Repeated forceful attempts are inappropriate; irreducibility or associated complex [fracture](../../../biology.md#fracture) requires specialist management.[Treatment for a dislocated shoulder](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#dislocated-shoulder-by-the-nhs)

After [joint reduction](../../../biology.md#joint-reduction), reassess the [axillary nerve](../../../neuroscience.md#axillary-nerve) and circulation and confirm alignment and relevant [fractures](../../../biology.md#fracture) with [radiography](../../../electromagnetic-radiation.md#radiography). A temporary [sling](../../../biology.md#sling) relieves pain, followed by progressive [physiotherapy](../../../biology.md#physiotherapy) to restore movement and [rotator cuff](../../../physiology.md#rotator-cuff) function. The balance is between recurrent instability and stiffness: prolonged immobilisation is not a universal solution. Recurrent instability, significant structural injury and persistent weakness require further assessment.[Sections 7–10: gentle reduction, repeat neurological examination, imaging and follow-up; this source specifically addresses children and adolescents](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#shoulder-dislocations-emergency-department-by-the-royal-children-s-hospital-melbourne)

**Original question.**

Distinguish between Hydrocele and Scrotal Hernia.

<h3 id="2/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [2](#2)

**A [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele) contains fluid around the [testis](../../../anatomy.md#testis); a [scrotal hernia](../../../biology.md#scrotal-hernia) contains abdominal contents that have descended through the [inguinal canal](../../../anatomy.md#inguinal-canal).** Their distinction rests on a combination of location, continuity with the [inguinal canal](../../../anatomy.md#inguinal-canal), behaviour and light transmission.

The usual acquired [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele) is a collection between the layers of the [tunica vaginalis](../../../anatomy.md#tunica-vaginalis). A [scrotal hernia](../../../biology.md#scrotal-hernia) is generally an indirect [inguinal hernia](../../../biology.md#inguinal-hernia): a sac of [peritoneum](../../../anatomy.md#peritoneum), often containing [intestine](../../../physiology.md#intestine) or [omentum](../../../anatomy.md#omentum), follows the [spermatic cord](../../../anatomy.md#spermatic-cord) into the [scrotum](../../../anatomy.md#scrotum). Persistence of the [processus vaginalis](../../../anatomy.md#processus-vaginalis) can permit either a communicating [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele) or an indirect [inguinal hernia](../../../biology.md#inguinal-hernia), depending on the communication and its contents. Thus the underlying developmental route is related even though the usual clinical appearances differ.[“Exams and Tests”: examination, light transmission, changes in swelling and imaging](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#hydrocele-by-medlineplus)

- An uncomplicated [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele) is typically smooth, fluctuant or tense, and confined to the [scrotum](../../../anatomy.md#scrotum). One can usually feel normal [spermatic cord](../../../anatomy.md#spermatic-cord) above it: one can “get above” the swelling. The [testis](../../../anatomy.md#testis) may be difficult to feel separately and is usually behind the fluid collection. A [scrotal hernia](../../../biology.md#scrotal-hernia) extends upwards toward the [inguinal canal](../../../anatomy.md#inguinal-canal); its upper limit cannot usually be encircled within the [scrotum](../../../anatomy.md#scrotum).[Chapter 7, p. 155, distinguishing continuity into the groin from a swelling confined to the scrotum](../../../biology-bibliography.md#the-ship-captain-s-medical-guide-by-the-maritime-and-coastguard-agency)
- A typical acquired [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele) has neither an expansile cough impulse nor ready reduction into the abdomen. A reducible [scrotal hernia](../../../biology.md#scrotal-hernia) enlarges on standing, coughing or straining, and diminishes on lying down or with gentle clinical reduction; [intestine](../../../physiology.md#intestine) may give a gurgle. An irreducible [hernia](../../../biology.md#hernia) is an important exception, so failure of reduction does not establish [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele).
- For [transillumination](../../../physics.md#transillumination), a bright light is applied to one side of the swelling in a darkened setting while the examiner views the other side. Clear fluid in a thin-walled [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele) usually transmits a diffuse glow. Most [scrotal hernias](../../../biology.md#scrotal-hernia) do not. Blood, thick walls or an underlying [tumour](../../../biology.md#tumour) can prevent a [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele) from transmitting light; apparent transmission alone cannot exclude a [hernia](../../../biology.md#hernia), especially in a child.
- A longstanding uncomplicated [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele) commonly produces heaviness rather than acute systemic illness. A painful, tender, irreducible [scrotal hernia](../../../biology.md#scrotal-hernia) with vomiting or abdominal distension raises concern for [intestinal obstruction](../../../physiology.md#intestinal-obstruction) or [strangulated hernia](../../../biology.md#strangulated-hernia), in which the blood supply of the trapped contents is compromised. That requires urgent surgical assessment.

If examination leaves doubt, [ultrasonography](../../../physics.md#ultrasonography) can establish whether the swelling contains fluid, [intestine](../../../physiology.md#intestine) or a solid [tumour](../../../biology.md#tumour), assess the [testis](../../../anatomy.md#testis) and show continuity with the [inguinal canal](../../../anatomy.md#inguinal-canal). A collection may obscure a diseased [testis](../../../anatomy.md#testis); demonstrating fluid does not finish the diagnostic task. Blind puncture is not an appropriate diagnostic shortcut when a [hernia](../../../biology.md#hernia) remains possible. Even an established [hydrocele](../../../biology.md#hydrocele) usually reaccumulates after simple aspiration, which also risks [infection](../../../biology.md#infection).[September 2024 leaflet, “Key points” and “What are the alternatives?”](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#repair-of-a-scrotal-hydrocele-by-the-british-association-of-urological-surgeons)

**Original question.**

Classify the Tumours called ‘Malignant.’

<h3 id="3/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

**An historically appropriate classification distinguishes the hard, soft, gelatinous, pigmented and epithelial malignant growths, together with recurrent and destructive growths whose place in “cancer” was disputed. Modern classification instead distinguishes [tumours](../../../biology.md#tumour) by cellular lineage and biological behaviour.**

The quotation marks around “Malignant” matter. In 1867 the clinical grouping did not have a single settled boundary in [histology](../../../anatomy.md#histology). Infiltration, destructive growth, return after removal, spread to distant sites and progressive constitutional illness were warning features; hardness or rapid growth alone was not a sufficient definition. A contemporary review of an 1866 textbook explicitly disputed confused descriptions of hard and soft [cancer](../../../biology.md#cancer), illustrating that this was already a live diagnostic controversy.[Discussion under “tumours”, contrasting scirrhus with encephaloid, melanotic and colloid growths and rejecting an indiscriminate use of “cancer”](../../../anatomy-and-physiology-bibliography.md#review-of-demonstrations-of-microscopic-anatomy-1866-book)

A useful period classification, without pretending that every contemporary author drew the same boundaries, is:

- **[Scirrhous carcinoma](../../../biology.md#scirrhous-carcinoma), or hard [cancer](../../../biology.md#cancer).** Abundant firm fibrous supporting tissue makes a hard, often contracted growth, classically in the [breast](../../../anatomy.md#breast). Retraction and fixation reflect invasion and contraction of that supporting tissue. “Hard” describes the gross appearance; it is not a separate modern cellular lineage.
- **[Encephaloid cancer](../../../biology.md#encephaloid-cancer), or medullary/soft [cancer](../../../biology.md#cancer).** The name refers to soft, brain-like tissue, often richly cellular and prone to rapid growth, breakdown and [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage). The historical label could include more than one modern diagnosis; it does not mean a [tumour](../../../biology.md#tumour) of the [brain](../../../neuroscience.md#brain).
- **[Colloid cancer](../../../biology.md#colloid-cancer).** A gelatinous appearance associated with abundant [mucin](../../../biochemistry.md#mucin). Some such lesions would now be classified as [mucinous carcinomas](../../../biology.md#mucinous-carcinoma); appearance alone cannot retrospectively determine their modern identity.
- **[Melanotic tumour](../../../biology.md#melanotic-tumour).** Dark pigment supplied a gross category. Many malignant examples belong to modern [melanoma](../../../biology.md#melanoma), arising from [melanocytes](../../../biological-cell.md#melanocyte), but pigmentation is neither necessary nor sufficient for [melanoma](../../../biology.md#melanoma). Pigmented examples could also be treated as varieties within other period categories.
- **[Epithelioma](../../../biology.md#epithelioma), or epithelial [cancer](../../../biology.md#cancer).** A growth exhibiting epithelial structure, especially at [skin](../../../physiology.md#skin) and [mucous membrane](../../../anatomy.md#mucous-membrane) surfaces. Many examples correspond to [squamous cell carcinoma](../../../biology.md#squamous-cell-carcinoma). A locally destructive [rodent ulcer](../../../biology.md#rodent-ulcer), now generally [basal cell carcinoma](../../../biology.md#basal-cell-carcinoma), was sometimes separated because distant spread was unusual.
- **[Recurrent fibroid tumour](../../../biology.md#recurrent-fibroid-tumour) and related “fibroplastic” or other [sarcomas](../../../biology.md#sarcoma).** These could return repeatedly and invade locally despite differing from the period's typical [cancer](../../../biology.md#cancer). They were sometimes called semi-malignant. A reported 1866 [recurrent fibroid tumour](../../../biology.md#recurrent-fibroid-tumour) developed distant deposits after apparently successful local treatment: a vivid reason that a purely local category could fail.[Case of recurrent fibroid tumour of the thigh, subsequent constitutional disease and post-mortem deposits; terminology is the report's historical terminology](../../../biology-bibliography.md#st-george-s-hospital-reports-1866)

Those are overlapping historical descriptive groups, not six mutually exclusive modern diagnoses. Nor does every growth historically called “sarcoma” meet the modern definition of [sarcoma](../../../biology.md#sarcoma).

The modern organising principle asks what cells are malignant. [Carcinoma](../../../biology.md#carcinoma) arises from [epithelium](../../../anatomy.md#epithelium); [sarcoma](../../../biology.md#sarcoma) arises in [bone](../../../anatomy.md#bone) or [soft tissue](../../../anatomy.md#soft-tissue); [melanoma](../../../biology.md#melanoma) arises from [melanocytes](../../../biological-cell.md#melanocyte); [lymphoma](../../../biology.md#lymphoma) and [leukaemia](../../../biology.md#leukaemia) concern blood-forming or immune cells. Other families include central [nervous system](../../../neuroscience.md#nervous-system) and [germ cell tumours](../../../biology.md#germ-cell-tumour). A solid lump is not essential for [cancer](../../../biology.md#cancer). Within a family, [histology](../../../anatomy.md#histology), [immunohistochemistry](../../../anatomy.md#immunohistochemistry) and appropriate [molecular diagnostics](../../../biology.md#molecular-diagnostics) refine the diagnosis. [Tumour grade](../../../biology.md#tumour-grade) describes features of aggressiveness, whereas [tumour stage](../../../biology.md#tumour-stage) describes extent of disease.[“Types of Cancer”, especially carcinoma, sarcoma, leukaemia, lymphoma, melanoma and other tumour types](../../../oncology-bibliography.md#what-is-cancer-by-the-national-cancer-institute)

This also corrects the old inference that recurrence must reflect a mysterious constitutional deposit: local residual disease and [metastasis](../../../biology.md#metastasis) are distinct mechanisms, and an apparently circumscribed growth may already have disseminated.

**Original question.**

What are the modes adopted for the cure of Hæmorrhoids? Describe the most frequent causes of the affection.

<h3 id="4/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [4](#4)

**Treat symptomatic [haemorrhoids](../../../biology.md#haemorrhoid) by correcting disordered defaecation and choosing an intervention suited to their position and severity. The main provoking factors are straining, [constipation](../../../biology.md#constipation) and disturbed bowel habits; [pregnancy](../../../biology.md#pregnancy) is another important setting.**

Normal [anal cushions](../../../anatomy.md#anal-cushion) contain vascular channels and supporting tissue and help seal the [anal canal](../../../anatomy.md#anal-canal). [Haemorrhoidal disease](../../../biology.md#haemorrhoid) is symptomatic enlargement, bleeding or displacement of these cushions, not simply the presence of normal [veins](../../../physiology.md#vein). Internal [haemorrhoids](../../../biology.md#haemorrhoid) originate above the [dentate line](../../../anatomy.md#dentate-line); external [haemorrhoids](../../../biology.md#haemorrhoid) lie below it in sensitive [skin](../../../physiology.md#skin). This explains why an uncomplicated internal lesion can bleed painlessly, while an acutely thrombosed external lesion can be very painful.[“Hemorrhoids”, definitions: normal cushions, position relative to the dentate line and symptoms](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#management-of-benign-anorectal-disorders-by-the-american-college-of-gastroenterology-2021)

[Constipation](../../../biology.md#constipation) with hard stools and repeated straining increases pressure and shearing stress in the [anal canal](../../../anatomy.md#anal-canal). Prolonged sitting on the toilet and frequent abnormal bowel movements can aggravate symptoms. During [pregnancy](../../../biology.md#pregnancy), pelvic venous congestion and [constipation](../../../biology.md#constipation) may contribute. Historical explanations often emphasised “congestion” from sedentary habits, abdominal pressure or obstructed venous return. These contain plausible mechanical observations but do not establish every old dietary or constitutional theory. In particular, [portal hypertension](../../../biology.md#portal-hypertension) can produce [rectal varices](../../../biology.md#rectal-varices), which must not be equated with ordinary [haemorrhoids](../../../biology.md#haemorrhoid).

An 1867 surgical answer should distinguish relief of irritation from destruction or removal of the troublesome tissue. Measures then used included regulation of the bowels, avoidance of straining, local cleansing and soothing or astringent applications, and reduction of a prolapsed internal mass where appropriate. Operative approaches included tying the base with a [ligature](../../../biology.md#ligature) so that the tissue sloughed, excision with control of [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage), and destruction by a heated cautery or clamp-and-cautery apparatus. Historical chemical cauterisation belongs to the same destructive principle. These describe period practice; indiscriminate caustics, cutting without haemostasis and tissue strangulation below the [dentate line](../../../anatomy.md#dentate-line) are not a modern treatment scheme.[p. 369: the third lecture on haemorrhoids and prolapse describes the clamp method, confirming its use before this exam](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#review-of-the-surgery-of-the-rectum-1865-book)

In current care, increased dietary [fibre](../../../function.md#fiber-of-a-function), adequate fluid and better toilet habits are first-line measures; medicines may provide temporary symptom relief. Persistent internal [haemorrhoids](../../../biology.md#haemorrhoid) can be treated by [rubber band ligation](../../../biology.md#rubber-band-ligation), [sclerotherapy](../../../biology.md#sclerotherapy) or [infrared coagulation](../../../biology.md#infrared-coagulation). [Rubber band ligation](../../../biology.md#rubber-band-ligation) interrupts the blood supply of a small portion above the [dentate line](../../../anatomy.md#dentate-line) and produces healing that fixes the displaced tissue. Advanced prolapse, substantial external disease or failure of less invasive treatment may justify [haemorrhoidectomy](../../../biology.md#haemorrhoidectomy); the operation must preserve enough intervening tissue to avoid [anal stenosis](../../../biology.md#anal-stenosis).[Recommendations 3, 5 and 7; dietary measures, office procedures and excisional surgery](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#management-of-hemorrhoids-by-the-american-society-of-colon-and-rectal-surgeons-2024)

The usual internal severity grades describe prolapse: grade I does not prolapse; grade II returns spontaneously; grade III needs manual replacement; grade IV cannot be reduced. These grades help select treatment but do not measure every patient's burden.[Table 1, classification by prolapse](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#diagnosis-and-treatment-of-hemorrhoids-by-the-american-gastroenterological-association-2026) Selected acutely thrombosed external [haemorrhoids](../../../biology.md#haemorrhoid) benefit from early excision; others settle with conservative treatment. A blanket promise of a permanent “cure” is inappropriate because recurrent provoking conditions and recurrent symptoms remain possible.[Recommendations 5–7 and section on thrombosed external haemorrhoids](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#management-of-hemorrhoids-by-the-american-society-of-colon-and-rectal-surgeons-2024)

Finally, [rectal bleeding](../../../biology.md#rectal-bleeding) must be investigated on its own merits. Examination, including inspection and suitable assessment of the [anal canal](../../../anatomy.md#anal-canal), determines whether [haemorrhoids](../../../biology.md#haemorrhoid) explain the symptoms; other causes include [colorectal cancer](../../../biology.md#colorectal-cancer) and [inflammatory bowel disease](../../../biology.md#inflammatory-bowel-disease). Persistent bleeding must not simply be labelled “piles”.[Anorectal examination, anoscopy and assessment of rectal bleeding](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#diagnosis-and-treatment-of-hemorrhoids-by-the-american-gastroenterological-association-2026)

↑ **Parent:** [Surgery and midwifery](surgery-midwifery.md)

**Original question.**

What is Astigmatism? how is it detected? and how remedied ?

<h3 id="5/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [5](#5)

**[Astigmatism](../../../geometrical-optics.md#astigmatism-optical-systems) is a [refractive error](../../../biology.md#refractive-error) in which the [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye)'s focusing power differs with direction. Detect its magnitude and axis by comparing directional focus; correct regular [astigmatism](../../../geometrical-optics.md#astigmatism-optical-systems) with a suitably oriented [cylindrical lens](../../../geometrical-optics.md#cylindrical-lens), adding a [spherical lens](../../../geometrical-optics.md#spherical-lens) when necessary.**

For regular [astigmatism](../../../geometrical-optics.md#astigmatism-optical-systems), the two principal [meridians of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#meridian-of-the-eye) are perpendicular and have different [optical powers](../../../geometrical-optics.md#optical-power), commonly because the [cornea](../../../anatomy.md#cornea) has unequal curvatures. The [crystalline lens](../../../anatomy.md#crystalline-lens) can also contribute. A point object consequently does not form one sharp point image: the idealised [paraxial optics](../../../geometrical-optics.md#paraxial-optics) gives two focal lines at different distances, perpendicular to one another, with an intervening region of blur. An ordinary [spherical lens](../../../geometrical-optics.md#spherical-lens) changes the focus in both meridians together and cannot by itself remove their difference. Irregular [astigmatism](../../../geometrical-optics.md#astigmatism-optical-systems), for example with a scarred [cornea](../../../anatomy.md#cornea), lacks this simple pair of uniform principal powers.[Introduction and sections on regular and irregular astigmatism](../../../neuroscience-bibliography.md#physiology-of-astigmatism-by-the-american-academy-of-ophthalmology)

Several complementary observations reveal it:

- With a distant point or suitable letter chart, an [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye) with [astigmatism](../../../geometrical-optics.md#astigmatism-optical-systems) gives blur that cannot be completely removed with different [spherical lenses](../../../geometrical-optics.md#spherical-lens). In a chart of equally dark radial lines, some directions can appear clearer or darker than others; unequal appearance suggests unequal directional focus, but does not alone supply a full prescription.
- A narrow [stenopaic slit](../../../biology.md#stenopaic-slit) admits rays through a restricted [meridian of the eye](../../../anatomy.md#meridian-of-the-eye). Rotate the slit and determine the [spherical lens](../../../geometrical-optics.md#spherical-lens) giving best distant vision in each principal direction. A difference between the best powers demonstrates regular [astigmatism](../../../geometrical-optics.md#astigmatism-optical-systems). This is an especially natural optical explanation for an 1867 answer: it isolates directions rather than simply asking whether vision improves through a small round aperture.
- Modern objective [retinoscopy](../../../biology.md#retinoscopy) observes the moving light reflex from the [retina](../../../neuroscience.md#retina) while trial lenses neutralise it in each meridian. [Keratometry](../../../biology.md#keratometry) measures reflected images from the anterior [cornea](../../../anatomy.md#cornea); [corneal topography](../../../biology.md#corneal-topography) maps a larger area and helps detect irregularity. These measure different things: [keratometry](../../../biology.md#keratometry) does not by itself include all contributions from the [crystalline lens](../../../anatomy.md#crystalline-lens). Subjective [refraction examination](../../../biology.md#refraction-examination) refines the prescription with trial lenses, often using a [crossed-cylinder test](../../../biology.md#crossed-cylinder-test).[Unit of Competence 7, objective retinoscopy and subjective refraction, including power and cylinder axis](../../../neuroscience-bibliography.md#assessment-of-visual-function-by-the-college-of-optometrists-2025)

The prescription must specify both the difference in [optical power](../../../geometrical-optics.md#optical-power) and its orientation. If the required correcting powers in the two principal directions are $R_1$ and $R_2$, choose the cylinder axis along direction 1. In the ideal [thin lens](../../../geometrical-optics.md#thin-lens) model:

$$
\boxed{S=R_1,\qquad C=R_2-R_1,\qquad R(\theta)=S+C\sin^2(\theta-\alpha)}.
$$

Here $S$ is the spherical correcting power, $C$ the cylindrical correcting power and $\alpha$ the cylinder axis, all powers measured in [dioptres](../../../geometrical-optics.md#dioptre). A [cylindrical lens](../../../geometrical-optics.md#cylindrical-lens) contributes no extra power along its axis and its full power perpendicular to that axis. For example, required corrections of $-1$ and $-3$ [dioptres](../../../geometrical-optics.md#dioptre) are supplied by a sphere of $-1$ [dioptre](../../../geometrical-optics.md#dioptre) and a cylinder of $-2$ [dioptres](../../../geometrical-optics.md#dioptre), with its axis in the direction requiring the smaller negative correction. These are correcting powers, not the uncorrected [eye](../../../anatomy.md#eye)'s intrinsic powers; confusing the two reverses the intended correction.

Spectacles with this sphere-and-cylinder combination are the central historical remedy. Present options also include [toric contact lenses](../../../anatomy.md#toric-contact-lens), appropriate rigid [contact lenses](../../../anatomy.md#contact-lens) for some irregular cases, and selected [refractive surgery](../../../biology.md#refractive-surgery). The suitability of these depends on the cause and condition of the [cornea](../../../anatomy.md#cornea); a spectacle cylinder cannot generally cancel all irregularities of an irregular [cornea](../../../anatomy.md#cornea).[Treatment options: spectacles, contact lenses and surgery; modern additions are not attributed to 1867](../../../neuroscience-bibliography.md#astigmatism-by-the-national-eye-institute)

## 6

↑ **Parent:** [Surgery and midwifery](surgery-midwifery.md)

**Original question.**

What are the causes of Phlegmasia dolens, and how would you treat it ?

<h3 id="6/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [6](#6)

**[Phlegmasia alba dolens](../../../biology.md#phlegmasia-alba-dolens), the historical “white” or “milk” leg, is a painful, swollen limb associated with extensive [deep vein thrombosis](../../../biology.md#deep-vein-thrombosis), particularly around childbirth. The modern priority is to identify [venous thrombosis](../../../biology.md#venous-thrombosis) and prevent [pulmonary embolism](../../../biology.md#pulmonary-embolism), not to treat an imagined accumulation of milk.**

Obstruction of major pelvic or thigh [veins](../../../physiology.md#vein) raises pressure in the channels draining the limb; fluid then accumulates in the tissues as [oedema](../../../biology.md#oedema). Severe outflow obstruction can also impede lymphatic drainage. The pale, tense appearance accounts for “alba”. It differs from [phlegmasia cerulea dolens](../../../biology.md#phlegmasia-cerulea-dolens), in which more complete obstruction, including collateral channels, produces a congested blue limb and can threaten arterial [perfusion](../../../physiology.md#perfusion). A historical case description cannot automatically be assigned one modern diagnosis solely from its name.

The central causal mechanisms are slowed venous flow, injury to the vessel wall and increased tendency of the blood to clot. [Pregnancy](../../../biology.md#pregnancy) promotes [hypercoagulability](../../../biology.md#hypercoagulability) and pelvic venous compression; childbirth can add tissue injury, immobility and [infection](../../../biology.md#infection). Prior [venous thrombosis](../../../biology.md#venous-thrombosis), [thrombophilia](../../../biology.md#thrombophilia), prolonged immobility, operative delivery and severe systemic illness further increase risk. It is not confined to the puerperium or even to women. The serious consequences are extension of the [thrombus](../../../biology.md#thrombus), detachment into the [pulmonary arteries](../../../physiology.md#pulmonary-artery) as a [pulmonary embolism](../../../biology.md#pulmonary-embolism), and chronic obstruction or valvular damage producing [post-thrombotic syndrome](../../../biology.md#post-thrombotic-syndrome).[“Why is a DVT serious?” and risk factors before, during and after pregnancy](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#diagnosis-and-treatment-of-venous-thrombosis-in-pregnancy-and-after-birth-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists)

An historically informed explanation should recognise that nineteenth-century arguments concerned the relative importance of venous obstruction, [phlebitis](../../../physiology.md#phlebitis), lymphatic disease and puerperal illness. Rest, support and elevation of the limb, pain relief and local soothing applications belonged to the period's conservative repertoire. These could ease symptoms but did not provide effective [anticoagulation](../../../biology.md#anticoagulation). Neither the “milk leg” name nor the observation of puerperal [infection](../../../biology.md#infection) establishes that every case is caused by milk displacement or by infected clot. The thrombotic and infective questions must be distinguished.

Current management begins with clinical assessment and [compression ultrasonography](../../../physics.md#compression-ultrasonography). A normally patent [vein](../../../physiology.md#vein) collapses under appropriate transducer pressure; a thrombosed segment often fails to compress. [Doppler ultrasonography](../../../physics.md#doppler-ultrasonography) adds information about flow, and suspected pelvic disease may require further imaging. A negative initial study does not close a strongly suspicious case; repeat or additional imaging may be needed. Fever and systemic illness prompt assessment for [infection](../../../biology.md#infection), while breathlessness, chest pain or collapse require urgent assessment for [pulmonary embolism](../../../biology.md#pulmonary-embolism).[“How are DVT and PE diagnosed during pregnancy?”](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#diagnosis-and-treatment-of-venous-thrombosis-in-pregnancy-and-after-birth-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists)

Unless contraindicated, therapeutic [low-molecular-weight heparin](../../../biology.md#low-molecular-weight-heparin) is the usual treatment during [pregnancy](../../../biology.md#pregnancy). It limits further clot formation while the body's own processes deal with the existing [thrombus](../../../biology.md#thrombus); it is not an instant mechanical solvent. Management also provides [analgesia](../../../neuroscience.md#analgesia), elevation and appropriate mobilisation once stable, with fitted compression for symptoms when suitable. Prolonged compulsory bed rest is not a general modern remedy. A threatened limb or suspected massive [pulmonary embolism](../../../biology.md#pulmonary-embolism) needs immediate specialist escalation; routine [thrombolysis](../../../biology.md#thrombolysis) for every swollen leg would expose patients to unjustified [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage).

For confirmed pregnancy-associated [venous thromboembolism](../../../biology.md#venous-thromboembolism), treatment usually continues through the rest of [pregnancy](../../../biology.md#pregnancy) and for at least six weeks after birth, with at least three months' treatment in total; the delivery plan must account for bleeding and [anaesthesia](../../../biology.md#anaesthesia). [Antibiotics](../../../biology.md#antibiotic) address a demonstrated or strongly suspected infective complication, not uncomplicated [venous thrombosis](../../../biology.md#venous-thrombosis) itself.[Green-top Guideline 37b, sections 4–8 and 10: imaging, heparin, supportive measures, mobilisation and duration; 2015 guideline, still listed while an update is in development](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#thrombosis-and-embolism-during-pregnancy-and-the-puerperium-acute-management-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists)

## 7

↑ **Parent:** [Surgery and midwifery](surgery-midwifery.md)

**Original question.**

There is occasional Hæmorrhage from the Vagina during the last half of Pregnancy. Upon what causes may this depend? State the treatment to be adopted during the later months, and at or near the full term.

<h3 id="7/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [7](#7)

**Recurrent bleeding in the second half of [pregnancy](../../../biology.md#pregnancy) particularly suggests [placenta praevia](../../../biology.md#placenta-praevia), but [placental abruption](../../../biology.md#placental-abruption), [vasa praevia](../../../biology.md#vasa-praevia), labour and local disease must also be considered. Treatment depends on the source, severity, maternal condition, fetal condition and gestational age.**

The source's “last half” includes a wider interval than the definition of [antepartum haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#antepartum-haemorrhage) from 24 weeks used by the cited British guideline. No diagnosis follows from intermittency alone.

In [placenta praevia](../../../biology.md#placenta-praevia), the [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) covers the internal opening of the [cervix](../../../anatomy.md#cervix). Development of the lower [uterine segment](../../../anatomy.md#uterine-segment) and opening of the [cervix](../../../anatomy.md#cervix) disturb attachment at the placental margin, exposing maternal vessels. Bleeding is often painless and recurrent and can become severe after apparently minor episodes. In [placental abruption](../../../biology.md#placental-abruption), a normally situated [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) separates prematurely. Bleeding into the placental bed reduces exchange with the [fetus](../../../biology.md#fetus); pain, tenderness and increased tone of the [uterus](../../../anatomy.md#uterus) are suggestive, but their absence does not exclude it. Some blood can remain concealed, making visible vaginal loss a poor measure of total loss.

In [vasa praevia](../../../biology.md#vasa-praevia), vulnerable fetal vessels run through the membranes near the internal cervical opening. Rupture, particularly when the membranes break, can cause rapid fetal blood loss while the mother remains comparatively stable. Other causes include a blood-stained cervical “show” in labour, [cervical ectropion](../../../anatomy.md#cervical-ectropion), a [cervical polyp](../../../anatomy.md#cervical-polyp), local [infection](../../../biology.md#infection), trauma, and occasionally a [tumour](../../../biology.md#tumour) of the [cervix](../../../anatomy.md#cervix) or [vagina](../../../anatomy.md#vagina). These must be distinguished from placental bleeding rather than presumed harmless from a small volume alone.[Green-top Guideline 63, sections 1–2 and 6–8: scope, causes, assessment and concealed loss](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#antepartum-haemorrhage-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists)

For an 1867 answer, the classic contrast is between “unavoidable” [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage) from [placenta praevia](../../../biology.md#placenta-praevia) and “accidental” [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage) from premature separation of a normally implanted [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta). The adjectives are historical classifications, not claims that either outcome is inevitable or that every [placental abruption](../../../biology.md#placental-abruption) follows an accident. Period management sought to conserve maternal strength when slight bleeding settled, then accomplish delivery when recurrent loss or labour made waiting dangerous. For partial [placenta praevia](../../../biology.md#placenta-praevia), rupture of membranes was used in suitable labour to bring the presenting part down against the bleeding region. For more extensive [placenta praevia](../../../biology.md#placenta-praevia), vaginal plugging, dilation and turning to deliver the child were debated and used. Contemporary discussion shows disagreement about how long it was safe to temporise: an 1864 report describes both advocates of early delivery and arguments about plugs, dilating bags and turning.[“On placenta praevia”, pp. 262–268, discussion of expectant intervals, premature delivery, plugging, membrane rupture and version](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#midwifery-in-the-edinburgh-medical-journal-september-1864)

Those measures belong to the history of managing [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage) before modern operative safety, imaging and transfusion services. They must not be carried over as a modern algorithm: a vaginal plug can conceal continuing loss; attempted separation of the [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) or turning through [placenta praevia](../../../biology.md#placenta-praevia) can aggravate bleeding and endanger the [fetus](../../../biology.md#fetus).

In current practice, the initial response to significant bleeding is hospital assessment with maternal [resuscitation](../../../biology.md#resuscitation), monitoring, intravenous access and blood tests, with blood products available according to severity. Determine placental position by [ultrasonography](../../../physics.md#ultrasonography), assess the [fetus](../../../biology.md#fetus) according to gestational age, and look for local causes with appropriate examination. **Do not perform a digital vaginal examination while [placenta praevia](../../../biology.md#placenta-praevia) remains a possible explanation until it has been excluded by [ultrasonography](../../../physics.md#ultrasonography).** A carefully performed [speculum examination](../../../biology.md#speculum-examination) and a skilled [transvaginal ultrasound](../../../physics.md#transvaginal-ultrasound) are different procedures; the latter is a safe, useful way to locate the placental edge. A normal [ultrasound](../../../fluid-mechanics.md#ultrasound) does not exclude [placental abruption](../../../biology.md#placental-abruption).[Sections 7.2–8.3: examination, avoidance of digital examination before excluding praevia, and limits of ultrasound for abruption](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#antepartum-haemorrhage-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists)

**During the later preterm months**, a stable mother and [fetus](../../../biology.md#fetus) after bleeding has stopped may be managed expectantly under specialist supervision. The plan includes surveillance, correction of [anaemia](../../../biology.md#anaemia), readiness to return urgently, and individual decisions about admission and timing of birth. [Corticosteroids](../../../pharmacology.md#corticosteroid) are considered when preterm delivery is likely; they do not treat the bleeding. Ongoing major [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage), maternal instability or serious fetal compromise can require delivery regardless of immaturity. This is not a policy of waiting for full term at any cost.

**At or near term**, the cause determines the route. Persistent [placenta praevia](../../../biology.md#placenta-praevia) covering the internal cervical opening requires [caesarean birth](../../../biology.md#caesarean-birth); repeated bleeding may require earlier delivery. Current 2026 guidance individualises timing for uncomplicated low-lying [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) or [placenta praevia](../../../biology.md#placenta-praevia) and advises considering planned birth no later than 37 weeks and 6 days. A low-lying placental edge that does not cover the opening requires individual assessment of whether vaginal birth is appropriate. These timings are present guidance, not an 1867 rule.[Green-top Guideline 27a, key recommendations on placental location, timing and place of birth; fifth edition, published June 2026](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#placenta-praevia-and-placenta-accreta-spectrum-diagnosis-and-management-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists-2026)

With [placental abruption](../../../biology.md#placental-abruption), expedite delivery when indicated by maternal or fetal condition; [caesarean birth](../../../biology.md#caesarean-birth) is often required for a live compromised [fetus](../../../biology.md#fetus) when vaginal delivery is not imminent, while vaginal delivery may be appropriate in other circumstances. A local cervical lesion requires cause-specific management. Suspected rupture of [vasa praevia](../../../biology.md#vasa-praevia) is an emergency for urgent [caesarean birth](../../../biology.md#caesarean-birth) and neonatal [resuscitation](../../../biology.md#resuscitation); an antenatally detected [vasa praevia](../../../biology.md#vasa-praevia) calls for planned birth before labour or membrane rupture.[“What is vasa praevia?”: fetal origin of blood loss and urgent birth after suspected rupture](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#placenta-praevia-placenta-accreta-and-vasa-praevia-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists) The central distinction is therefore safe observation of a settled, stable preterm episode versus timely delivery for an ongoing hazard, not a single remedy for every late-pregnancy bleed.

## 8

↑ **Parent:** [Surgery and midwifery](surgery-midwifery.md)

**Original question.**

What are the causes, consequences, and treatment of retained Placenta ?

<h3 id="8/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [8](#8)

**A [retained placenta](../../../biology.md#retained-placenta) has failed to separate, has separated but become trapped, or is abnormally attached. It can cause [postpartum haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#postpartum-haemorrhage) and [infection](../../../biology.md#infection); treatment combines timely assessment, control of bleeding and safe removal, with a different strategy when [placenta accreta spectrum](../../../biology.md#placenta-accreta-spectrum) is suspected.**

After the [fetus](../../../biology.md#fetus) is born, contraction and retraction of the [myometrium](../../../anatomy.md#myometrium) reduce the area of the placental bed. The [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) cannot shrink with it and normally separates through the maternal [decidua](../../../biology.md#decidua). Continued contraction compresses the maternal vessels crossing the [myometrium](../../../anatomy.md#myometrium), providing an important mechanism of [haemostasis](../../../physiology.md#haemostasis). A fragment left attached can both expose bleeding vessels and interfere with effective contraction.

Three mechanisms organise the causes:

- **Failure of separation**, often associated with [uterine atony](../../../anatomy.md#uterine-atony). Exhaustion after prolonged labour, overdistension of the [uterus](../../../anatomy.md#uterus) and a full [urinary bladder](../../../anatomy.md#urinary-bladder) may impair effective contraction or expulsion. A detached [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) can also remain in a poorly contracting [uterus](../../../anatomy.md#uterus).
- **Entrapment after separation** behind a closing [cervix](../../../anatomy.md#cervix) or constricted lower portion of the [uterus](../../../anatomy.md#uterus). Nineteenth-century texts discussed “hour-glass contraction”, but the diagnosis was contested even then; not every trapped [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) proves that an hourglass-shaped partition has formed in the body of the [uterus](../../../anatomy.md#uterus).
- **Abnormal attachment**, now classified within [placenta accreta spectrum](../../../biology.md#placenta-accreta-spectrum). Deficiency of the normal [decidua](../../../biology.md#decidua) at the attachment site allows abnormal adherence to, or invasion of, the [myometrium](../../../anatomy.md#myometrium). Previous uterine surgery is an important risk factor. A [succenturiate placental lobe](../../../biology.md#succenturiate-placental-lobe) or fragment can also remain after the main [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) has been delivered, so an apparently expelled organ does not exclude retained tissue.

Consequences include immediate or delayed [postpartum haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#postpartum-haemorrhage), [hypovolaemic shock](../../../biology.md#hypovolaemic-shock), [anaemia](../../../biology.md#anaemia), [endometritis](../../../anatomy.md#endometritis) and [sepsis](../../../biology.md#sepsis). Retained fragments may delay normal shrinking of the [uterus](../../../anatomy.md#uterus). Forceful pulling on the [umbilical cord](../../../biology.md#umbilical-cord) adds the risks of cord rupture and [uterine inversion](../../../anatomy.md#uterine-inversion). Conversely, indiscriminate delay while bleeding continues can be fatal.

An 1867 answer would assess bleeding, the size and contraction of the [uterus](../../../anatomy.md#uterus), whether separation had occurred and whether the outlet was obstructed. Emptying the [urinary bladder](../../../anatomy.md#urinary-bladder), encouraging effective contraction and supporting the [uterus](../../../anatomy.md#uterus) were rational preliminary principles. If the [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) remained attached or trapped and the circumstances demanded intervention, manual removal with external support of the [uterus](../../../anatomy.md#uterus) was the principal operative resource. The historical difficulty was to distinguish a removable attachment from a dangerously adherent one. A September 1867 report describes an apparent “hour-glass” retention actually relieved by loosening a tight abdominal binder; it is useful evidence for examining the mechanism rather than trusting the diagnostic label.[British Medical Journal, 7 September 1867, p. 206: retained placenta after external constriction, followed by expulsion when the binder was loosened](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#hour-glass-contraction-of-the-uterus-simulated-by-the-pressure-of-a-narrow-binder-by-william-t-greene-1867) The case is an observation, not proof that all retention has an external cause.

Current care monitors blood loss and maternal condition, secures intravenous access and obtains skilled obstetric help. With active third-stage management, failure to complete the [third stage of labour](../../../biology.md#third-stage-of-labour) by 30 minutes is prolonged; with physiological management, the cited NICE threshold is 60 minutes. These thresholds do not justify waiting through active [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage) or instability. A trained clinician may use [controlled cord traction](../../../biology.md#controlled-cord-traction) with uterine counterpressure under appropriate conditions; unopposed pulling is not the same technique.[NG235, recommendations 1.10.20–1.10.28: prolonged third stage, intravenous access, uterotonics and anaesthesia for removal](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#intrapartum-care-by-nice)

When removal is required, examination and manual removal take place with appropriate [anaesthesia](../../../biology.md#anaesthesia), aseptic technique, external support and readiness to manage [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage). The [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) and membranes are checked for completeness; persistent bleeding prompts assessment for retained fragments, [uterine atony](../../../anatomy.md#uterine-atony), trauma and disordered clotting. [Antibiotic prophylaxis](../../../pharmacology.md#antibiotic-prophylaxis) accompanies manual removal. Give [uterotonics](../../../pharmacology.md#uterotonic) for associated [postpartum haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#postpartum-haemorrhage) and to maintain contraction after removal, rather than repeatedly giving them as a substitute for removing a retained organ in a non-bleeding patient. WHO's 2025 recommendations advise preparing for manual removal after 30 minutes if the [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) remains undelivered and distinguish this decision from treatment of established [postpartum haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#postpartum-haemorrhage).[Recommendations 30–31: uterotonics for retained placenta in the presence of haemorrhage, preparation for removal, and routine antibiotic prophylaxis](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#consolidated-guidelines-for-the-prevention-diagnosis-and-treatment-of-postpartum-haemorrhage-by-the-world-health-organization-2025)

If no safe separation plane exists and [placenta accreta spectrum](../../../biology.md#placenta-accreta-spectrum) is suspected, repeated forceful attempts to peel off the [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) can precipitate catastrophic [haemorrhage](../../../biology.md#haemorrhage). Escalate to an experienced multidisciplinary team and a haemorrhage plan. Definitive [surgery](../../../biology.md#surgery), including [hysterectomy](../../../anatomy.md#hysterectomy), may be necessary; selected conservative management leaving the [placenta](../../../biology.md#placenta) in place requires specialist selection and follow-up. Current guidance explicitly advises avoiding attempted separation in women with a high probability of [placenta accreta spectrum](../../../biology.md#placenta-accreta-spectrum).[Key recommendations on avoiding placental separation, specialist multidisciplinary care and follow-up when the placenta is left in situ](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#placenta-praevia-and-placenta-accreta-spectrum-diagnosis-and-management-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists-2026) Thus the word “retained” identifies an urgent clinical problem, but the cause determines whether ordinary manual separation is safe.
