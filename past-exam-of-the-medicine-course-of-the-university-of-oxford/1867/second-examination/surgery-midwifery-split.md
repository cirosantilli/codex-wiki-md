# Surgery and midwifery

↑ **Parent:** [Second examination](split.md)

Original volume: [Examination papers for the Degree of Bachelor of Medicine by the University of Oxford (1864–1867)](../../../examination-papers-for-the-degree-of-bachelor-of-medicine-by-the-university-of-oxford-1864-1867.md). [Original scanned volume, PDF page 99](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3e/Examination_papers_for_the_Degree_of_Bachelor_of_medicine_-_1864-67_(IA_b24757810).pdf#page=99). Oxford, December 1867.

Original questions are transcribed from the public-domain edition. Credit: Wellcome Collection; original supplied by Royal College of Physicians, London. [Source and public-domain rights statement](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/d7b9zgnx).

The answers distinguish the terminology and therapeutic possibilities of 1867 from present [medicine](../../../medicine.md). They explain general [anatomy](../../../anatomy-split.md), [physiology](../../../physiology-split.md) and clinical reasoning; no individual patient or examination findings are assumed.
