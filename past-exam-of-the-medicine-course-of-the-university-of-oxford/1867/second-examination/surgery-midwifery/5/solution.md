<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**[Astigmatism](../../../../../astigmatism-optical-systems.md) is a [refractive error](../../../../../refractive-error.md) in which the [eye](../../../../../eye.md)'s focusing power differs with direction. Detect its magnitude and axis by comparing directional focus; correct regular [astigmatism](../../../../../astigmatism-optical-systems.md) with a suitably oriented [cylindrical lens](../../../../../cylindrical-lens.md), adding a [spherical lens](../../../../../spherical-lens.md) when necessary.**

For regular [astigmatism](../../../../../astigmatism-optical-systems.md), the two principal [meridians of the eye](../../../../../meridian-of-the-eye.md) are perpendicular and have different [optical powers](../../../../../optical-power.md), commonly because the [cornea](../../../../../cornea.md) has unequal curvatures. The [crystalline lens](../../../../../crystalline-lens.md) can also contribute. A point object consequently does not form one sharp point image: the idealised [paraxial optics](../../../../../paraxial-optics.md) gives two focal lines at different distances, perpendicular to one another, with an intervening region of blur. An ordinary [spherical lens](../../../../../spherical-lens.md) changes the focus in both meridians together and cannot by itself remove their difference. Irregular [astigmatism](../../../../../astigmatism-optical-systems.md), for example with a scarred [cornea](../../../../../cornea.md), lacks this simple pair of uniform principal powers.[Introduction and sections on regular and irregular astigmatism](../../../../../physiology-of-astigmatism-by-the-american-academy-of-ophthalmology.md)

Several complementary observations reveal it:

- With a distant point or suitable letter chart, an [eye](../../../../../eye.md) with [astigmatism](../../../../../astigmatism-optical-systems.md) gives blur that cannot be completely removed with different [spherical lenses](../../../../../spherical-lens.md). In a chart of equally dark radial lines, some directions can appear clearer or darker than others; unequal appearance suggests unequal directional focus, but does not alone supply a full prescription.
- A narrow [stenopaic slit](../../../../../stenopaic-slit.md) admits rays through a restricted [meridian of the eye](../../../../../meridian-of-the-eye.md). Rotate the slit and determine the [spherical lens](../../../../../spherical-lens.md) giving best distant vision in each principal direction. A difference between the best powers demonstrates regular [astigmatism](../../../../../astigmatism-optical-systems.md). This is an especially natural optical explanation for an 1867 answer: it isolates directions rather than simply asking whether vision improves through a small round aperture.
- Modern objective [retinoscopy](../../../../../retinoscopy.md) observes the moving light reflex from the [retina](../../../../../retina.md) while trial lenses neutralise it in each meridian. [Keratometry](../../../../../keratometry.md) measures reflected images from the anterior [cornea](../../../../../cornea.md); [corneal topography](../../../../../corneal-topography.md) maps a larger area and helps detect irregularity. These measure different things: [keratometry](../../../../../keratometry.md) does not by itself include all contributions from the [crystalline lens](../../../../../crystalline-lens.md). Subjective [refraction examination](../../../../../refraction-examination.md) refines the prescription with trial lenses, often using a [crossed-cylinder test](../../../../../crossed-cylinder-test.md).[Unit of Competence 7, objective retinoscopy and subjective refraction, including power and cylinder axis](../../../../../assessment-of-visual-function-by-the-college-of-optometrists-2025.md)

The prescription must specify both the difference in [optical power](../../../../../optical-power.md) and its orientation. If the required correcting powers in the two principal directions are $R_1$ and $R_2$, choose the cylinder axis along direction 1. In the ideal [thin lens](../../../../../thin-lens.md) model:

$$
\boxed{S=R_1,\qquad C=R_2-R_1,\qquad R(\theta)=S+C\sin^2(\theta-\alpha)}.
$$

Here $S$ is the spherical correcting power, $C$ the cylindrical correcting power and $\alpha$ the cylinder axis, all powers measured in [dioptres](../../../../../dioptre.md). A [cylindrical lens](../../../../../cylindrical-lens.md) contributes no extra power along its axis and its full power perpendicular to that axis. For example, required corrections of $-1$ and $-3$ [dioptres](../../../../../dioptre.md) are supplied by a sphere of $-1$ [dioptre](../../../../../dioptre.md) and a cylinder of $-2$ [dioptres](../../../../../dioptre.md), with its axis in the direction requiring the smaller negative correction. These are correcting powers, not the uncorrected [eye](../../../../../eye.md)'s intrinsic powers; confusing the two reverses the intended correction.

Spectacles with this sphere-and-cylinder combination are the central historical remedy. Present options also include [toric contact lenses](../../../../../toric-contact-lens.md), appropriate rigid [contact lenses](../../../../../contact-lens.md) for some irregular cases, and selected [refractive surgery](../../../../../refractive-surgery.md). The suitability of these depends on the cause and condition of the [cornea](../../../../../cornea.md); a spectacle cylinder cannot generally cancel all irregularities of an irregular [cornea](../../../../../cornea.md).[Treatment options: spectacles, contact lenses and surgery; modern additions are not attributed to 1867](../../../../../astigmatism-by-the-national-eye-institute.md)
