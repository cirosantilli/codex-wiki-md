<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**The chief historical varieties are subglenoid, subcoracoid, subclavicular and posterior [shoulder dislocation](../../../../../shoulder-dislocation.md); treatment restores the [humeral head](../../../../../humeral-head.md) gently, checks the [axillary nerve](../../../../../axillary-nerve.md) and circulation, and restores useful movement.**

The [glenohumeral joint](../../../../../glenohumeral-joint.md) combines a large [humeral head](../../../../../humeral-head.md) with a shallow [glenoid cavity](../../../../../glenoid-cavity.md). Its range of movement depends on the comparatively loose [joint capsule](../../../../../joint-capsule.md); the [glenoid labrum](../../../../../glenoid-labrum.md), [rotator cuff](../../../../../rotator-cuff.md) and surrounding [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) provide stability. An injury can therefore lever the [humeral head](../../../../../humeral-head.md) out of the [glenoid cavity](../../../../../glenoid-cavity.md) while tearing the [joint capsule](../../../../../joint-capsule.md) and sometimes the [glenoid labrum](../../../../../glenoid-labrum.md) or adjacent [bone](../../../../../bone.md).

The historical names describe where the [humeral head](../../../../../humeral-head.md) is found relative to the [scapula](../../../../../scapula.md) and [clavicle](../../../../../clavicle.md):

- In subglenoid [shoulder dislocation](../../../../../shoulder-dislocation.md), the [humeral head](../../../../../humeral-head.md) lies below the [glenoid cavity](../../../../../glenoid-cavity.md), toward the [axilla](../../../../../axilla.md). Old descriptions of displacement “downwards into the axilla” need not mean the persistently overhead arm position now called [luxatio erecta](../../../../../luxatio-erecta.md).
- In subcoracoid [shoulder dislocation](../../../../../shoulder-dislocation.md), the [humeral head](../../../../../humeral-head.md) lies in front of the [glenoid cavity](../../../../../glenoid-cavity.md), beneath the [coracoid process](../../../../../coracoid-process.md). This is the familiar anteroinferior pattern.
- In subclavicular [shoulder dislocation](../../../../../shoulder-dislocation.md), the [humeral head](../../../../../humeral-head.md) has passed farther forwards and medially, beneath the [clavicle](../../../../../clavicle.md); this is a rarer, more extensive anterior displacement.
- In posterior [shoulder dislocation](../../../../../shoulder-dislocation.md), historically also called subspinous displacement, the [humeral head](../../../../../humeral-head.md) lies behind the [glenoid cavity](../../../../../glenoid-cavity.md), toward the [scapular spine](../../../../../scapular-spine.md). Modern assessment is particularly alert to this pattern after a [seizure](../../../../../seizure.md) or electrical injury. True inferior [shoulder dislocation](../../../../../shoulder-dislocation.md), [luxatio erecta](../../../../../luxatio-erecta.md), and exceptional superior displacement complete the directional classification; they should not be forced into the common anterior pattern.[Sections 3–4: anterior and posterior displacement and their mechanisms](../../../../../shoulder-dislocations-emergency-department-by-the-royal-children-s-hospital-melbourne.md)

An 1867 answer would stress early recognition, relaxation of the [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md), and controlled traction with suitable countertraction or gentle leverage to guide the [humeral head](../../../../../humeral-head.md) through the torn [joint capsule](../../../../../joint-capsule.md). [Anaesthesia](../../../../../anaesthesia.md) made relaxation practicable. These are principles, not a reason to reproduce old forceful manoeuvres or indiscriminate pulley traction: force can produce a [fracture](../../../../../fracture.md) or damage the [axillary artery](../../../../../axillary-artery.md) and [brachial plexus](../../../../../brachial-plexus.md).

Current assessment establishes the direction and associated [fracture](../../../../../fracture.md), usually with [radiography](../../../../../radiography.md) in appropriate views. Before and after [joint reduction](../../../../../joint-reduction.md), record [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), [perfusion](../../../../../perfusion.md), distal movement and sensation, including sensation over the lateral [deltoid muscle](../../../../../deltoid-muscle.md) supplied by the [axillary nerve](../../../../../axillary-nerve.md). A [nerve injury](../../../../../nerve-injury.md) accompanying [joint dislocation](../../../../../joint-dislocation.md) makes prompt restoration of alignment especially important.[BOASt, response to identification of peripheral nerve injury, recommendation 5](../../../../../peripheral-nerve-injury-by-the-british-orthopaedic-association.md) [Joint reduction](../../../../../joint-reduction.md) is performed by trained clinicians with suitable [analgesia](../../../../../analgesia.md) and, when needed, [sedation](../../../../../sedation.md) or [anaesthesia](../../../../../anaesthesia.md). Repeated forceful attempts are inappropriate; irreducibility or associated complex [fracture](../../../../../fracture.md) requires specialist management.[Treatment for a dislocated shoulder](../../../../../dislocated-shoulder-by-the-nhs.md)

After [joint reduction](../../../../../joint-reduction.md), reassess the [axillary nerve](../../../../../axillary-nerve.md) and circulation and confirm alignment and relevant [fractures](../../../../../fracture.md) with [radiography](../../../../../radiography.md). A temporary [sling](../../../../../sling.md) relieves pain, followed by progressive [physiotherapy](../../../../../physiotherapy.md) to restore movement and [rotator cuff](../../../../../rotator-cuff.md) function. The balance is between recurrent instability and stiffness: prolonged immobilisation is not a universal solution. Recurrent instability, significant structural injury and persistent weakness require further assessment.[Sections 7–10: gentle reduction, repeat neurological examination, imaging and follow-up; this source specifically addresses children and adolescents](../../../../../shoulder-dislocations-emergency-department-by-the-royal-children-s-hospital-melbourne.md)
