<h1 id="latin-translation/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Latin translation](../latin-translation.md)

**Translation.** For rapidly developing, or [acute diseases](../../../../../acute-disease.md), also resolve of their own accord, sometimes through favourable chance and sometimes through nature's favour. Consequently, ignorant people, carried away by this, often avoid [physicians](../../../../../physician-split.md) and attribute these favourable outcomes to new incantations and amulets, or to chance; for these rapidly developing [diseases](../../../../../disease.md) are driven out either by a flow of [sweat](../../../../../sweat.md), by [blood](../../../../../blood.md) discharged through the nose, or by a discharge from the [intestines](../../../../../intestine.md). But [chronic diseases](../../../../../chronic-disease.md), or [diseases](../../../../../disease.md) of slow development, which have already gained possession of the body with a kind of prior claim, call for the skill of the [physician](../../../../../physician-split.md) alone, since they are resolved neither by nature nor by chance.

**Interpretation.** [Caelius Aurelianus](../../../../../caelius-aurelianus.md) contrasts spontaneous resolution in [acute disease](../../../../../acute-disease.md) with the entrenched course of [chronic disease](../../../../../chronic-disease.md). Here _passiones_ means affections or [diseases](../../../../../disease.md), not emotions; _ligamenta_ means protective bindings or amulets, not anatomical [ligaments](../../../../../ligament.md); _proventus_ means favourable outcomes. The metaphor _praejudicio quodam_ gives the established [disease](../../../../../disease.md) something like a prior claim on the body. The final assertion is the author's defence of professional [medicine](../../../../../medicine.md), not a present-day law that every [chronic disease](../../../../../chronic-disease.md) requires effective medical [treatment](../../../../../treatment.md) or that none can remit spontaneously. The account of [sweating](../../../../../sweating.md), [nosebleeds](../../../../../nosebleed.md) and [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md) discharge describes his historical explanation, not evidence that deliberately producing these discharges cures [acute disease](../../../../../acute-disease.md).
