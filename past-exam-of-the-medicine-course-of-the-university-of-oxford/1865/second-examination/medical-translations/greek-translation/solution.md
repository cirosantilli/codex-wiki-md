<h1 id="greek-translation/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Greek translation](../greek-translation.md)

**Translation.** Open the hollow [vein](../../../../../vein.md) at the [elbow](../../../../../elbow.md), but withdraw the [blood](../../../../../blood.md) through a small opening, so that the patient's strength is not markedly affected: a sudden, large withdrawal puts the constitution to the test. Take away much less than for other indications. For, in a case of collapse, even a small mistake readily sends the patient to the realm of the dead. Give [food](../../../../../food.md) immediately, therefore, to restore strength; for nature takes pleasure both in getting rid of the old material and in receiving the new.

If the patient's strength rules out [venesection](../../../../../venesection.md), but there are [inflammations](../../../../../inflammation.md), apply a [cupping vessel](../../../../../cupping-vessel.md) at that site well before the crisis of the [disease](../../../../../disease.md). For collapse occurs on the critical days, since it is at the same periods that nature brings matters to a decision and [diseases](../../../../../disease.md) kill. Even if the patient reaches the point of needing [wine](../../../../../wine.md), it is not altogether safe to drink [wine](../../../../../wine.md) while [inflammations](../../../../../inflammation.md) are present. [Wine](../../../../../wine.md) increases [pain](../../../../../pain.md) in those with [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md), but strengthens the constitution in those without it.

One or two days before applying the [cupping vessel](../../../../../cupping-vessel.md), a [poultice](../../../../../poultice.md) is needed both to relax the parts and to encourage a free flow of [blood](../../../../../blood.md). In some patients, poulticing should also continue after cupping into the second day. Here too there must be restraint: with cupping, excessive loss of [blood](../../../../../blood.md) carries the same danger. Use [enemas](../../../../../enema.md) only for old, retained [faeces](../../../../../faeces.md), and spare the patient's strength.

**Critical interpretation.** [Aretaeus of Cappadocia](../../../../../aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md) couples depletory interventions with concern for impending collapse. The assessment requires judging each measure and its indications, not simply approving or rejecting the whole paragraph. The detailed comparison below separates what could reasonably have been argued in 1865 from present-day [pathology](../../pathology-split.md) and practice.

**What condition is being treated?** The source is [On the Treatment of Acute Diseases by Aretaeus of Cappadocia](../../../../../on-the-treatment-of-acute-diseases-by-aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md), II.3, concerning the [cardiac affections of Aretaeus](../../../../../cardiac-affections-of-aretaeus.md). The surrounding chapter describes a dangerous febrile illness with impending collapse; it argues that apparent weakness can arise either from deficiency or from what the author regards as excess. This explains his apparently paradoxical recommendation to bleed someone at risk of collapse. It does not establish that this patient category corresponds to one present-day [heart](../../../../../heart.md) disorder. In particular, the passage's _συγκοπή_ must not automatically be identified with modern [syncope](../../../../../syncope.md), which denotes a brief loss of consciousness from inadequate cerebral [perfusion](../../../../../perfusion.md), with spontaneous recovery. [II.3, opening account of impending collapse and distinction between deficient and oppressed strength](../../../../../on-the-treatment-of-acute-diseases-by-aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md) [§3.1, definition of syncope](../../../../../syncope-guidelines-by-the-european-society-of-cardiology-2018.md)

The “hollow [vein](../../../../../vein.md) at the [elbow](../../../../../elbow.md)” is an ancient designation used here for [antecubital venesection](../../../../../antecubital-venesection.md); it cannot mean opening the deeply situated [vena cava](../../../../../vena-cava.md). _Δύναμις_ means the patient's strength or reserve, rather than a measured numerical quantity. The _κρίσις_ is the decisive turn of the illness, and the [critical days in ancient medicine](../../../../../critical-days-in-ancient-medicine.md) belong to the author's prognostic framework. No specific critical-day schedule or numerical volume of [blood](../../../../../blood.md) is supplied in this extract, and neither should be invented. The one- or two-day interval actually mentioned concerns preparation with a [poultice](../../../../../poultice.md).

**What could be endorsed in 1865?** The warning that rapid depletion or excessive [blood loss](../../../../../blood-loss.md) could precipitate collapse is sound clinical caution, and it applies to [wet cupping](../../../../../wet-cupping.md) as well as [venesection](../../../../../venesection.md). The emphasis on preserving strength, providing suitable [nutrition](../../../../../nutrition.md), and avoiding unnecessary evacuation could also be endorsed. [Bennett](../../../../../john-hughes-bennett.md)'s 1864 argument offers an especially clear contemporary comparison: supporting recovery was preferable to routine lowering measures, and limited bleeding that did not exhaust the patient should at most be considered palliative rather than assumed curative. A local reduction of vascular congestion could be considered as an exceptional palliative indication within that contemporary argument; it was not a reason to bleed every patient with [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md). [pp. 142–143: local congestion, nutritive support, and concluding distinction between palliation and cure](../../../../../treatment-of-inflammation-by-john-hughes-bennett.md)

**Where the recommendation should be opposed.** In an exhausted patient with a weak circulation after substantial [blood loss](../../../../../blood-loss.md), removing more [blood](../../../../../blood.md) would intensify the very danger being feared. The small incision changes the rate of removal, but does not by itself guarantee a tolerable total loss. Equally, [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) or another inflammatory [disease](../../../../../disease.md) does not establish an excess of removable [blood](../../../../../blood.md) merely because [fever](../../../../../fever.md) and a forceful pulse accompany it. These are grounds for dissenting from the indication, rather than merely demanding a smaller dose of the same intervention. The reservation is already intelligible from [Aretaeus of Cappadocia](../../../../../aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md)'s own warning, and the rejection of routine depletion is supported by the contemporary argument just cited.

**Present-day examples clarify the distinction.** In [hypovolaemic shock](../../../../../hypovolaemic-shock.md), the relevant issue is insufficient circulating volume and inadequate [perfusion](../../../../../perfusion.md); assessment, replacement of deficits and repeated reassessment are appropriate principles, rather than therapeutic [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md). Conversely, a patient with [acute heart failure](../../../../../acute-heart-failure.md) and [pulmonary oedema](../../../../../pulmonary-oedema.md) may require removal of excess fluid, but current guidance uses carefully monitored [diuretic therapy](../../../../../diuretic-therapy.md) rather than routine loss of [blood](../../../../../blood.md). These opposite clinical examples show why “collapse” alone cannot choose between depletion and support. Modern assessment follows [blood pressure](../../../../../blood-pressure.md), the pulse, peripheral [perfusion](../../../../../perfusion.md), fluid losses and signs of fluid overload; it does not wait for an ancient critical day. [CG174, §§1.2.1–1.2.3 and 1.5: assessment, deficit versus excess, and reassessment](../../../../../intravenous-fluid-therapy-in-adults-in-hospital-by-nice.md) [CG187, §§1.3.3–1.3.5: intravenous diuretics and monitoring](../../../../../acute-heart-failure-by-nice.md)

Modern [venesection](../../../../../venesection.md) nevertheless has specific indications, including [haemochromatosis](../../../../../haemochromatosis.md) and [polycythaemia vera](../../../../../polycythaemia-vera.md). In the former it reduces excess [iron](../../../../../iron.md); in the latter it can reduce an excessive [red blood cell](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) burden. These are defined abnormalities, not confirmation of the ancient rule about bleeding in [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) or impending collapse. They are present-day qualifications, not diagnoses or knowledge attributed to the 1865 candidate. [Venesection: removal of iron-containing red blood cells](../../../../../haemochromatosis-treatment-by-the-nhs.md) [Treatment, Venesection: use in polycythaemia vera](../../../../../erythrocytosis-by-the-nhs.md)

**Food, wine and local applications must be judged separately.** Suitable [food](../../../../../food.md) can help an alert, depleted convalescent who can swallow safely, so the restorative aim deserves qualified endorsement. Immediate oral feeding of someone who cannot protect the [airway](../../../../../airway.md) is different: the route must be appropriate, and oral [food](../../../../../food.md) does not by itself reverse circulatory failure. Current nutritional guidance explicitly considers swallowing safety and alertness. The idea that removal of old material makes new [food](../../../../../food.md) beneficial does not provide a modern mechanism for restoring lost [blood](../../../../../blood.md). [CG32, §§1.6.3 and 1.6.6: alertness, swallowing safety and indications for oral support](../../../../../nutrition-support-for-adults-by-nice.md)

The claim about [wine](../../../../../wine.md) also requires dissent in its universal form: absence of [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) is not sufficient evidence that [wine](../../../../../wine.md) will restore the patient's circulation. An 1865 discussion could question indiscriminate stimulation as well as indiscriminate depletion; [Bennett](../../../../../john-hughes-bennett.md) expressly distinguished nutritive support from overstimulation. Present-day resuscitation assesses the cause and circulatory state instead of treating [wine](../../../../../wine.md) as a general restorative. [p. 142, distinction between restoring nutritive processes and overstimulation](../../../../../treatment-of-inflammation-by-john-hughes-bennett.md) [CG174, §§1.2–1.3: assessment and resuscitation framework](../../../../../intravenous-fluid-therapy-in-adults-in-hospital-by-nice.md)

A gentle warm local application may be considered for comfort, as warmth and relief of [pain](../../../../../pain.md) were also discussed in the 1864 contemporary argument. That is a palliative aim, distinct from preparing to extract supposedly harmful [blood](../../../../../blood.md). There is no basis here for endorsing a [poultice](../../../../../poultice.md) or [wet cupping](../../../../../wet-cupping.md) as a cure for deep internal [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md); the caution about excessive bleeding remains applicable. [p. 142, local warmth and relief of pain](../../../../../treatment-of-inflammation-by-john-hughes-bennett.md) [Evidence and Safety: uncertain efficacy and adverse effects](../../../../../cupping-by-nccih.md)

Finally, the restriction of [enemas](../../../../../enema.md) to retained [faeces](../../../../../faeces.md) is preferable to routine purging of a weak patient. A concrete modern counterpart is a selected case of [faecal impaction](../../../../../faecal-impaction.md), for which an [enema](../../../../../enema.md) is one possible treatment after assessment. It does not follow that every older stool requires an [enema](../../../../../enema.md), or that an [enema](../../../../../enema.md) treats the cause of a febrile collapse. Thus the defensible parts of the passage are conserving reserve and using a measure for a specific indication; the unsupported parts are universal depletion, harmlessness, and treatment timed by [critical days in ancient medicine](../../../../../critical-days-in-ancient-medicine.md).
