<h1 id="latin-translation/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Latin translation](../latin-translation.md)

**Translation.** A [cupping vessel](../../../../../cupping-vessel.md) may also be needed in [chronic diseases](../../../../../chronic-disease.md), even when they have already continued for some time, whether because the bodily material is corrupted or because the wind is in a disordered condition; likewise in some [acute diseases](../../../../../acute-disease.md), if the body needs to be relieved but the patient's strength does not permit [blood](../../../../../blood.md) to be let from a [vein](../../../../../vein.md). This remedy is less forceful and therefore safer; nor is it ever dangerous, even if used in the midst of an attack of [fever](../../../../../fever.md), or while [food](../../../../../food.md) remains undigested. Accordingly, where [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) is needed, if opening a [vein](../../../../../vein.md) presents an immediate danger, or if the disorder is also situated in a particular part of the body, one should resort to this instead. We must nevertheless understand that, just as there is no danger here, so the protection is weaker, and that a violent malady cannot be met except by an equally forceful remedy.

**Critical interpretation.** [Celsus](../../../../../aulus-cornelius-celsus.md) is comparing local cupping with general [venesection](../../../../../venesection.md), rather than distinguishing a scientifically established safe treatment from a dangerous one. His absolute claim of harmlessness and his rule that severe [disease](../../../../../disease.md) requires an equally forceful intervention need criticism, even though the concern for the patient's limited reserve is sensible.

**The instrument and the historical explanation.** _Cucurbitula_ means a [cupping vessel](../../../../../cupping-vessel.md), not a vegetable prescribed as [food](../../../../../food.md). Earlier in the same chapter, [Celsus](../../../../../aulus-cornelius-celsus.md) describes bronze cups using burning material and horn cups from which air is withdrawn through an opening. Reduced internal [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) holds the cup against the [skin](../../../../../skin.md). He distinguishes [wet cupping](../../../../../wet-cupping.md), over incised [skin](../../../../../skin.md), from [dry cupping](../../../../../dry-cupping.md), over intact [skin](../../../../../skin.md); the present comparison with [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) chiefly concerns the former, while the reference to disordered wind belongs to his broader account of cupping. _Materia_ and _spiritus_ are his explanatory categories: “corrupted material” and [pathological wind in ancient medicine](../../../../../pathological-wind-in-ancient-medicine.md) should not be silently relabelled as demonstrated [bacterial infection](../../../../../bacterial-infection.md) or a modern measurement of gas. _Cruditas_ means a state of incomplete [digestion](../../../../../digestion.md), not a specific modern [disease](../../../../../disease.md). [II.11.1–6: cup construction, scarified versus intact skin, and the assigned comparison](../../../../../on-medicine-by-aulus-cornelius-celsus.md)

**Criticism available in 1865.** A cautious practitioner could agree that the patient's condition limits the use of depletory measures, and that a local complaint does not automatically require general [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md). But neither premise proves that local [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) is needed. The important distinction is between comparing the burdens of two interventions and establishing a benefit from either. By 1864, [John Hughes Bennett](../../../../../john-hughes-bennett.md) was arguing that [venesection](../../../../../venesection.md) could not remove established inflammatory exudation and that routine depletion in [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) weakened the processes needed for recovery. His account supplies contemporary grounds for dissenting from the final maxim: a severe inflammatory [disease](../../../../../disease.md) might call for support rather than an equally violent intervention. This was an argument available before the examination, although his particular theory of inflammatory repair is itself historical. [1864, pp. 142–143, arguments against depletion and for supporting nutritive processes](../../../../../treatment-of-inflammation-by-john-hughes-bennett.md)

**Present-day assessment.** The absolute statement “never dangerous” is false. [Wet cupping](../../../../../wet-cupping.md) can cause [blood loss](../../../../../blood-loss.md) and [anaemia](../../../../../anaemia.md), and both forms of cupping can cause [burns](../../../../../burn.md), [scars](../../../../../scar.md) and [infection](../../../../../infection.md). Current research does not establish cupping as an effective general remedy for [fever](../../../../../fever.md) or internal inflammatory [disease](../../../../../disease.md). A skin mark or extracted [blood](../../../../../blood.md) is an observed local effect, not by itself evidence that the cause of an internal [disease](../../../../../disease.md) has been removed. The defensible part of the paragraph is the concern for the patient's reserve, not its assurance of harmlessness or its equation of severity with therapeutic violence. [Evidence and Safety: limited quality of efficacy evidence; burns, scars, infections and anaemia from repeated wet cupping](../../../../../cupping-by-nccih.md)
