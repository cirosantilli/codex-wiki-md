<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**The historical priorities were rest, relief of painful [arthritis](../../../../../arthritis.md), and repeated examination for [carditis](../../../../../carditis.md); modern treatment also eradicates preceding [streptococcal infection](../../../../../streptococcal-infection.md) and prevents recurrences.** In an 1865 account, the patient would be nursed in bed, the painful [anatomical joints](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) supported, drinks and tolerable nourishment supplied, and temperature, pulse, breathing and [urine](../../../../../urine.md) observed. Moist flannel applications and [opium](../../../../../opium.md) preparations were used for severe pain and sleeplessness. Alkaline salts were commonly prescribed under a theory that the disease involved an abnormal acid material; attempts to make the [urine](../../../../../urine.md) alkaline were a therapeutic practice, not proof that an acid caused the disease. There was already disagreement about the value of specific drugs and [blistering agents](../../../../../blistering-agent.md). A contemporary critique describes these competing treatments and stresses the inadequacy of small uncontrolled case series.[14 January, p. 31, ordinary management and criticism of blistering](../../../../../remarks-on-the-treatment-of-acute-rheumatism-in-the-british-medical-journal-1865.md)

The important complications are [rheumatic carditis](../../../../../rheumatic-carditis.md), including [valvulitis](../../../../../valvulitis.md) and sometimes [pericarditis](../../../../../pericarditis.md), with possible [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md); [Sydenham's chorea](../../../../../sydenham-s-chorea.md) is another manifestation. New [dyspnoea](../../../../../dyspnoea.md), a changed [heart murmur](../../../../../heart-murmur.md), chest pain, a [pericardial friction rub](../../../../../pericardial-friction-rub.md), or signs of circulatory failure require reassessment rather than stronger treatment of the painful [anatomical joint](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) alone. Historically, local bleeding, [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md) and [counterirritation](../../../../../counterirritation.md) were employed against supposed cardiac inflammation. Their historical use does not justify them now. A heart examination also matters when [arthritis](../../../../../arthritis.md) improves, because improvement of pain does not establish freedom from cardiac injury.[clinical manifestations: carditis, arthritis and chorea](../../../../../clinical-guidance-for-acute-rheumatic-fever-by-cdc.md)

Present management confirms the diagnosis and cardiac involvement with appropriate clinical criteria, [electrocardiography](../../../../../electrocardiography.md) and [echocardiography](../../../../../echocardiography.md). [Penicillin](../../../../../penicillin.md) or an appropriate alternative eradicates residual group A [streptococci](../../../../../streptococcus.md), even if the throat symptoms have resolved. An [anti-inflammatory drug](../../../../../anti-inflammatory-drug.md) relieves [arthritis](../../../../../arthritis.md); severe [carditis](../../../../../carditis.md), [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md) or disabling [Sydenham's chorea](../../../../../sydenham-s-chorea.md) needs specialist management. Fluid-retaining [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md) can require a [diuretic](../../../../../diuretic.md) with monitoring of renal function and [electrolytes](../../../../../electrolyte.md); treatment is selected according to haemodynamic findings. The 2025 guideline allows [corticosteroids](../../../../../corticosteroid.md) for selected severe [carditis](../../../../../carditis.md) or [pericarditis](../../../../../pericarditis.md) with effusion, under specialist care. Mild [Sydenham's chorea](../../../../../sydenham-s-chorea.md) may need only rest and a calm environment; greater functional impairment can justify [carbamazepine](../../../../../carbamazepine.md) or another carefully selected symptomatic medicine. These measures accompany, rather than replace, [antibiotic prophylaxis](../../../../../antibiotic-prophylaxis.md). [Secondary antibiotic prophylaxis](../../../../../secondary-antibiotic-prophylaxis.md) reduces future attacks and further valve injury. Neither [aspirin](../../../../../aspirin.md) nor a later [salicylate](../../../../../salicylate.md) regimen should be presented as ordinary Oxford practice in 1865. These are historical explanations and general medical principles, not a treatment plan for an individual.[acute management, cardiac assessment, management of chorea and secondary prophylaxis](../../../../../australian-guideline-for-acute-rheumatic-fever-and-rheumatic-heart-disease-2025.md)
