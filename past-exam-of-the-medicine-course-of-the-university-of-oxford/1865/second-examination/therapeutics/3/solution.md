<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Local treatment is useful when there is an accessible lesion or a defined symptom to relieve; its concentration, delivered quantity and route must be specified separately.** Historical indications included chronic [pharyngitis](../../../../../pharyngitis.md) with excessive secretion, ulceration, enlarged or unhealthy tonsillar surfaces, and some forms of chronic [laryngitis](../../../../../laryngitis.md). The intended effects were cleansing, soothing, reducing secretion, or cauterising a circumscribed unhealthy surface. Merely having a sore throat was not sufficient reason for a caustic application.

The accessible [pharynx](../../../../../pharynx.md) could be treated by a gargle, a wash, a lozenge, or a carefully directed brush or sponge. A gargle does not reliably medicate the [larynx](../../../../../larynx.md): during normal swallowing the entrance is protected, and aspiration is an unwanted event. For laryngeal painting, the period apparatus included a light source, reflector, small warmed laryngeal mirror and a suitably bent brush. Illumination and the mirror image allowed inspection of the lesion and guidance of the instrument, instead of blind probing. A small measured loading reduced uncontrolled flow into the [airway](../../../../../airway.md). An [atomiser](../../../../../atomiser.md) instead broke liquid into droplets with a jet of air or steam; the concentration in its reservoir was not the amount retained by the patient. A contemporary review discusses brushes, mirrors, caustic holders and the limitations of spraying caustic solutions.[pp. 310–319, local treatment and equipment](../../../../../recent-works-on-the-laryngoscope-1865-review.md)

For a numerical period example, the 1865 account of medicated inhalation gives **[silver nitrate](../../../../../silver-nitrate.md), 3–5 grains, sometimes 10 grains, per Imperial fluid ounce of distilled [water](../../../../../water.md)**. These are historical reservoir concentrations, approximately 0.7–2.3 percent mass per volume, not oral doses and not modern recommendations for inhaling a corrosive. Other concentrations in the same 1865 account, each per fluid ounce of [water](../../../../../water.md), are **[tannic acid](../../../../../tannic-acid.md), 3–10 grains; [alum](../../../../../alum.md), 4–15 grains; [zinc sulfate](../../../../../zinc-sulfate.md), 1/2–5 grains; and [opium tincture](../../../../../opium-tincture.md), 1–10 minims**. The first three were intended chiefly as local astringent applications, and the last as a soothing or narcotic application. These are reports of historical practice, including hazardous applications, rather than evidence that atomising these drugs is beneficial or safe today.[vol. 52, pp. 102–106, especially p. 105, numbered list of drugs and silver-nitrate strengths](../../../../../medicated-inhalation-in-the-retrospect-of-medicine-1865.md)

Direct historical cauterisation used still stronger [silver nitrate](../../../../../silver-nitrate.md) solutions or the solid salt for selected ulcers and growth bases. A contemporary review records disagreement over aggressive treatment and reports a recommendation of 40 grains per fluid ounce in one of the works reviewed. That disagreement is part of the answer: **40 grains per ounce for local painting must not be confused with a dilute gargle, an inhalation concentration or a swallowed dose**. For a less destructive mode of local medication, the period dosage table lists **one to four or more [tannic acid lozenges](../../../../../tannic-acid-lozenge.md)**, illustrating again the distinction between strength per item and the number administered.[discussion of strong silver solutions and missing dose information](../../../../../recent-works-on-the-laryngoscope-1865-review.md)[tannic acid lozenges and posological table](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

Modern uncomplicated acute sore throat usually receives symptom relief, adequate drinking, and selective [antibiotic](../../../../../antibiotic.md) treatment when indicated by the assessed cause and severity. Medicated lozenges can give a small reduction in pain in adults; that modest benefit does not validate the historical corrosive sprays. Persistent hoarseness, a focal ulcer or a suspected growth requires diagnosis rather than repeated empirical painting. [Stridor](../../../../../stridor.md), difficulty managing secretions or respiratory distress raises an [airway](../../../../../airway.md) problem, for which irritating instrumentation can be hazardous. Local treatment of a syphilitic or other specific lesion cannot replace systemic treatment of its cause.[NG84, self-care, antibiotics and referral recommendations](../../../../../sore-throat-acute-antimicrobial-prescribing-by-nice.md)[persistent voice symptoms, assessment and emergency breathing symptoms](../../../../../laryngitis-by-nhs.md)
