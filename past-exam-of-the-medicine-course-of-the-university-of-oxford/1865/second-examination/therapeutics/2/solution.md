**The [delirium](../../../../../delirium.md) of [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md) calls for treatment of systemic illness and careful nursing; severe [diarrhoea](../../../../../diarrhoea.md) in [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md) additionally demands replacement of losses and vigilance for intestinal injury.** These are different [infections](../../../../../infection.md), despite the similar names.

For an 1865 description of [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md), provide a quiet, ventilated room, continuous attentive nursing, fluid and nourishment, mouth care, and protection from falls or wandering. Examine for retention of [urine](../../../../../urine.md), constipation, chest complications and exhaustion. A restless sleepless patient was sometimes given a cautiously selected [opium](../../../../../opium.md) preparation; wine or other alcoholic stimulants were also employed in supposed exhaustion. Such practice was contested: contemporary observations warned that recovery after a drug did not establish that the drug had caused recovery. Sedation was especially questionable with deepening stupor or respiratory impairment. Routine depletion of an already prostrate patient was not a rational substitute for support.[1865, discussion of stimulants, nursing and inference from recovery](../../../../../typhus-statistics-of-the-dundee-royal-infirmary-with-remarks-by-thomas-john-maclagan.md)

Modern epidemic [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md) is caused by [Rickettsia prowazekii](../../../../../rickettsia-prowazekii.md) and requires prompt [doxycycline](../../../../../doxycycline.md), alongside management of [hypoxia](../../../../../hypoxia.md), [dehydration](../../../../../dehydration.md), organ dysfunction and the precipitating [infection](../../../../../infection.md). Testing should not postpone treatment when the clinical suspicion is strong. A low-stimulation environment and observation remain useful, while historical alcohol stimulation and indiscriminate [opioid](../../../../../opioid.md) sedation are not disease-specific therapy. Control of the transmitting body lice is part of preventing further [infection](../../../../../infection.md).[treatment, severe manifestations and transmission](../../../../../clinical-overview-of-epidemic-typhus-by-cdc.md)

In the historical treatment of severe [typhoid diarrhoea](../../../../../typhoid-diarrhoea.md), rest, readily tolerated nourishment and measured replacement of drinks were accompanied by attempts to quiet the bowel with [opium](../../../../../opium.md), sometimes with an [astringent](../../../../../astringent.md) such as chalk or a lead-containing preparation. These aimed at reducing stool frequency; they did not eliminate the cause. An apparent reduction of stools could conceal deterioration, and intestinal pain, distension, bleeding or collapse required renewed examination. Contemporary criticism of routine [opium](../../../../../opium.md) use in febrile bowel disease shows that this was never a uniformly established cure.[lecture read 1 December 1865, published February 1866, pp. 76–81; adverse effects and disputed indications](../../../../../opium-in-certain-forms-of-fever-and-in-diarrhoea-and-dysentery-1865-lecture.md)

Today [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md) requires an effective [antibiotic](../../../../../antibiotic.md) selected with travel history and local [antimicrobial resistance](../../../../../antimicrobial-resistance.md) in mind, then refined by [bacterial culture](../../../../../bacterial-culture.md) and susceptibility results. Assess fluid balance, circulation, [electrolytes](../../../../../electrolyte.md) and kidney function, using oral or intravenous replacement as severity requires. [Gastrointestinal bleeding](../../../../../gastrointestinal-bleeding.md), increasing abdominal tenderness, guarding, distension, [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md) or a sudden change in condition can indicate [intestinal perforation](../../../../../intestinal-perforation.md) or other severe complications and need urgent hospital assessment. Historical lead preparations are toxic; suppressing [diarrhoea](../../../../../diarrhoea.md) is not a substitute for treating the [infection](../../../../../infection.md) and its complications.[diagnosis, antibiotic use and complications](../../../../../clinical-guidance-for-typhoid-fever-and-paratyphoid-fever-by-cdc.md)
