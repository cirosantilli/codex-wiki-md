↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Compare the pattern and sequence of findings; neither a red [rash](../../../../../rash.md) nor [stridor](../../../../../stridor.md) is a diagnosis.** Historical disease names also need care: nineteenth-century “true” or membranous [croup](../../../../../croup.md) can overlap with laryngeal [diphtheria](../../../../../diphtheria.md), whereas common modern [croup](../../../../../croup.md) usually means viral [laryngotracheitis](../../../../../laryngotracheitis.md).

**[Scarlet fever](../../../../../scarlet-fever.md).** An acute throat illness with [fever](../../../../../fever.md), a fine rough or sandpaper-like [rash](../../../../../rash.md), a characteristic red tongue and subsequent peeling suggests [scarlet fever](../../../../../scarlet-fever.md). Modern [scarlet fever](../../../../../scarlet-fever.md) is associated with toxin-producing group A [Streptococcus](../../../../../streptococcus.md); symptoms alone do not uniquely establish the organism. The main comparisons are:

- **[Measles](../../../../../measles.md):** prominent [cough](../../../../../cough.md), [coryza](../../../../../coryza.md) and [conjunctivitis](../../../../../conjunctivitis.md), followed by a more blotchy eruption spreading from the head downwards, favour [measles](../../../../../measles.md). Small characteristic lesions inside the mouth can help. Their absence does not, by itself, rule it out. [Clinical features: respiratory prodrome, oral lesions and spreading maculopapular rash](../../../../../clinical-overview-of-measles-by-cdc.md)
- **[Rubella](../../../../../rubella.md) and other viral exanthems:** an acute [rash](../../../../../rash.md) may resemble the early eruption. Exposure history, associated findings and the course matter; [rash](../../../../../rash.md) colour alone cannot distinguish them from [scarlet fever](../../../../../scarlet-fever.md).
- **A drug eruption or [urticaria](../../../../../urticaria.md):** a relevant exposure and a different evolution or fleeting raised lesions favour these explanations. Coexisting throat symptoms still require assessment.
- **Ordinary [pharyngitis](../../../../../pharyngitis.md) or [tonsillitis](../../../../../tonsillitis.md):** throat redness, exudate and [fever](../../../../../fever.md) without the characteristic general eruption are insufficient for [scarlet fever](../../../../../scarlet-fever.md). Conversely, [pharyngitis](../../../../../pharyngitis.md) with a [rash](../../../../../rash.md) has several possible causes. [Differential diagnosis and confirmation by rapid antigen test, nucleic acid amplification or throat culture](../../../../../clinical-guidance-for-scarlet-fever-by-cdc.md)
- **[Diphtheria](../../../../../diphtheria.md):** a firmly adherent membrane on the pharyngeal or laryngeal mucosa, sometimes with neck swelling and systemic illness, favours [diphtheria](../../../../../diphtheria.md). The membrane can bleed when forcibly disturbed; removal is not an appropriate diagnostic experiment. It differs from the usual scarlet-fever exanthem and tongue changes. [Clinical features: adherent pseudomembrane, bleeding on attempted removal and airway obstruction](../../../../../diphtheria-in-the-cdc-pink-book.md)

A modern throat test can confirm group A streptococcal [infection](../../../../../infection.md); it was unavailable in 1865. The useful historical answer therefore rests on careful examination and the clinical sequence, while admitting uncertain or overlapping cases.

**[Croup](../../../../../croup.md) and its mimics.** A harsh barking [cough](../../../../../cough.md), hoarseness and [stridor](../../../../../stridor.md) suggest narrowing of the upper [airway](../../../../../airway.md). In common viral [croup](../../../../../croup.md), the child may have an antecedent respiratory illness without the marked toxicity of a severe bacterial process. In the historical question, however, distinguishing “[croup](../../../../../croup.md)” from [diphtheria](../../../../../diphtheria.md) cannot mean assuming they were mutually exclusive labels. A membrane extending from the throat into the [larynx](../../../../../larynx.md) could form part of what was called membranous [croup](../../../../../croup.md).

- **A laryngeal spasm:** brief episodic obstruction with intervals of recovery and without a sustained inflammatory illness suggests the historical category [laryngismus stridulus](../../../../../laryngismus-stridulus.md), rather than progressive membranous disease.
- **An inhaled foreign body:** abrupt choking and [cough](../../../../../cough.md) during eating or play, a very sudden onset, or asymmetric chest findings favour an obstruction by material rather than ordinary viral [croup](../../../../../croup.md).
- **[Epiglottitis](../../../../../epiglottitis.md):** a very unwell child with drooling, difficulty swallowing, a muffled voice and little barking [cough](../../../../../cough.md) needs a different interpretation from ordinary [croup](../../../../../croup.md).
- **[Bacterial tracheitis](../../../../../bacterial-tracheitis.md):** marked systemic illness, progressive obstruction and thick secretions suggest a bacterial tracheal process.
- **A [retropharyngeal abscess](../../../../../retropharyngeal-abscess.md):** neck pain or stiffness, swallowing difficulty, drooling and a different pharyngeal picture point towards a deep collection rather than uncomplicated [croup](../../../../../croup.md). [Differential diagnoses and distinguishing clinical features](../../../../../acute-upper-airway-obstruction-by-the-royal-children-s-hospital-melbourne.md)

The practical priority is the severity of obstruction, not winning a verbal distinction at the bedside. Current guidance minimizes distress and unnecessary throat examination, obtains skilled airway support for deterioration, and does not require routine radiography or swabs in typical [croup](../../../../../croup.md). These modern precautions also explain why a child should not be forcibly examined merely to demonstrate a membrane. [Assessment, investigations and management](../../../../../croup-by-the-royal-children-s-hospital-melbourne.md)
