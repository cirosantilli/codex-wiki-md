**Tapping must follow identification of the fluid-containing cavity: [abdominal paracentesis](../../../../../abdominal-paracentesis.md) for [ascites](../../../../../ascites.md), [thoracentesis](../../../../../thoracentesis.md) for a [pleural effusion](../../../../../pleural-effusion.md).** An enlarged abdomen or a dull area of chest is not, by itself, a sufficient indication for blind puncture. The object may be relief of pressure or obtaining fluid for diagnosis; neither necessarily removes the underlying cause.

**Abdomen: states in which tapping is useful.** Tense or troublesome [ascites](../../../../../ascites.md) can interfere with breathing, eating and comfort, particularly when it persists despite treatment of its cause. Causes include [cirrhosis](../../../../../cirrhosis.md) with [portal hypertension](../../../../../portal-hypertension.md), [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md), malignant peritoneal disease and some inflammatory or infective conditions. New or unexplained [ascites](../../../../../ascites.md) can also require diagnostic sampling. General swelling without a drainable peritoneal collection is a different problem. An ovarian cyst, a gravid [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md), a distended [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md) and gaseous distension must not be mistaken for free [ascites](../../../../../ascites.md). [Diagnostic paracentesis and large-volume paracentesis](../../../../../guidelines-on-the-management-of-ascites-by-the-bsg-and-basl.md) [Initial diagnosis: cirrhosis and alternative causes, imaging and diagnostic paracentesis](../../../../../ascites-practice-guidance-by-aasld-2021.md)

**Abdomen: operative account.** The characteristic historical apparatus is a sharp [trocar](../../../../../trocar.md) within a hollow [cannula](../../../../../cannula.md). The patient is supported, the presence and distribution of fluid established, and the [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md) emptied to keep it away from the lower abdominal puncture route. Traditional descriptions use an appropriate abdominal site, often in the midline below the umbilicus; a landmark is not sufficient if an enlarged organ, a vessel or adherent [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md) is in the way. Once the instrument has entered fluid, withdrawal of the [trocar](../../../../../trocar.md) leaves the [cannula](../../../../../cannula.md) as the drainage channel. The patient and the flow are observed, and the opening is dressed after removal. This describes the operation's principle, not a universally safe blind puncture point.

Modern performance uses sterile preparation, appropriate local [anaesthesia](../../../../../anaesthesia.md), a selected fluid pocket and a [catheter](../../../../../catheter.md) system rather than assuming that every distended abdomen can be tapped in the same place. [Ultrasound](../../../../../ultrasound.md) helps identify a safe pocket and avoid organs and vessels. Samples may be sent for cell counts, [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) and [albumin](../../../../../albumin.md) measurements, culture or [cytology](../../../../../cytology.md) according to the clinical question. Therapeutic drainage requires reassessment because [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md), intestinal injury, leakage, [infection](../../../../../infection.md) and circulatory or renal disturbance can occur. In cirrhotic large-volume drainage, [albumin](../../../../../albumin.md) replacement has a specific modern role; it is not an 1865 routine to be projected backwards. [Recommendations 1–2 and 5–6: fluid assessment, ultrasound guidance, drainage and albumin](../../../../../guidelines-on-the-management-of-ascites-by-the-bsg-and-basl.md)

**Thorax: states in which tapping is useful.** A large [pleural effusion](../../../../../pleural-effusion.md) producing respiratory difficulty can be relieved, and an unexplained effusion can be sampled to identify its cause. Serous fluid, [blood](../../../../../blood.md) and [pus](../../../../../pus.md) have different implications: [pleural effusion](../../../../../pleural-effusion.md) associated with [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md), malignant disease or inflammation is not identical to [empyema](../../../../../empyema.md). A purulent collection may require continuing drainage and treatment of the [infection](../../../../../infection.md), not merely one palliative aspiration. Contemporary experience already included tapping in hydrothorax; an 1864 report with subsequent anatomical examination illustrates how removal of fluid could coexist with serious underlying disease. [Opening report: hydrothorax, thoracic paracentesis, subsequent death and anatomical examination](../../../../../paracentesis-report-in-the-medical-society-of-the-county-of-kings-1864.md)

**Thorax: operative account.** The supported position exposes a suitable part of the chest; the operator first distinguishes fluid from consolidated [lung](../../../../../lung.md) and identifies its upper extent and the [diaphragm](../../../../../diaphragm.md). An appropriate intercostal route enters the fluid above a [rib](../../../../../rib.md), avoiding the principal vessels and [nerve](../../../../../nerve.md) running along its lower border. The drainage apparatus should permit controlled outflow without freely introducing air. The position of the [lung](../../../../../lung.md), [diaphragm](../../../../../diaphragm.md), [liver](../../../../../liver.md) and [spleen](../../../../../spleen.md) makes an arbitrarily low puncture unsafe. Historic [trocar](../../../../../trocar.md)-and-[cannula](../../../../../cannula.md) entry and modern small [catheter](../../../../../catheter.md) aspiration share the need to enter the collection without injuring these structures.

Current guidance requires thoracic [ultrasound](../../../../../ultrasound.md) for aspiration of a [pleural effusion](../../../../../pleural-effusion.md), a suitable needle or [catheter](../../../../../catheter.md), and slow controlled drainage. Chest tightness, [pain](../../../../../pain.md), persistent [cough](../../../../../cough.md) or increasing breathlessness are reasons to stop and reassess, not simply to continue until no more fluid runs. Complications include [pneumothorax](../../../../../pneumothorax.md), [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md), injury to an abdominal organ, [infection](../../../../../infection.md) and [re-expansion pulmonary oedema](../../../../../re-expansion-pulmonary-oedema.md). These precautions belong to current practice; they must not be represented as technology available in 1865.
