**Classify the bleeding by anatomical source and by its relation to [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md), childbirth and [menstruation](../../../../../menstruation.md); treatment must address that source.** The amount of visible genital [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md) does not measure all the loss: a ruptured [ectopic pregnancy](../../../../../ectopic-pregnancy.md) or concealed [placental abruption](../../../../../placental-abruption.md) can cause major internal [blood loss](../../../../../blood-loss.md). Initial assessment of severe bleeding concerns the circulation, [anaemia](../../../../../anaemia.md) and the need for resuscitation while the cause is established. Bleeding from the [urethra](../../../../../urethra.md) or [rectum](../../../../../rectum.md) must first be distinguished from genital bleeding.

**Local sources outside the uterine cavity.** The [vulva](../../../../../vulva.md) and [vagina](../../../../../vagina.md) may bleed from lacerations after childbirth or other trauma, an inflamed lesion, a foreign body, a vascular lesion or [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md). Examination localizes the loss; treatment is local compression and repair of a significant laceration, removal of the foreign body, cause-directed treatment of [infection](../../../../../infection.md), or specialist management of the lesion. A [cervical polyp](../../../../../cervical-polyp.md), [cervical ectropion](../../../../../cervical-ectropion.md), [cervicitis](../../../../../cervicitis.md) or [cervical cancer](../../../../../cervical-cancer.md) can cause bleeding from the [cervix](../../../../../cervix.md), often noticed between periods or after intercourse. A suitable [polyp](../../../../../polyp.md) can be removed and examined; inflammation requires its cause to be established, while a suspicious lesion requires diagnostic assessment rather than indiscriminate cauterization. After menopause, thin, fragile vulvovaginal tissues can also bleed; this does not remove the need to investigate a suspicious lesion. These are different conditions, not one disease called “uterine [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md)”. [Etiology, examination and cause-directed treatment of vulvar, vaginal and cervical bleeding](../../../../../vaginal-bleeding-by-merck-manual.md)

**Bleeding from the [endometrium](../../../../../endometrium.md).** Heavy or irregular [menstruation](../../../../../menstruation.md) may accompany [ovulatory dysfunction](../../../../../ovulatory-dysfunction.md), [uterine fibroids](../../../../../uterine-fibroid.md), [endometrial polyps](../../../../../endometrial-polyp.md), [adenomyosis](../../../../../adenomyosis.md), a [coagulopathy](../../../../../coagulopathy.md), medication effects or [endometrial cancer](../../../../../endometrial-cancer.md). Modern assessment includes the menstrual and drug history, a full [blood count](../../../../../blood-count.md), and selected [ultrasound](../../../../../ultrasound.md), [hysteroscopy](../../../../../hysteroscopy.md) or tissue sampling according to the suspected cause. Where appropriate, menstrual bleeding can be reduced with [tranexamic acid](../../../../../tranexamic-acid.md) or hormonal treatment; an intracavitary [polyp](../../../../../polyp.md) or [uterine fibroid](../../../../../uterine-fibroid.md) may instead need targeted removal. Persistent postmenopausal bleeding must not simply be treated as an ordinary period. [NG88 §§1.2–1.5: assessment, blood count, selected investigation and medical or surgical treatment](../../../../../heavy-menstrual-bleeding-by-nice.md)

**Early [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md).** A [miscarriage](../../../../../miscarriage.md) may bleed while [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md) tissue separates or remains in the [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md). In a stable, confirmed case, expectant, medical or surgical management may be appropriate; heavy continuing loss or [infection](../../../../../infection.md) changes the urgency. [Ectopic pregnancy](../../../../../ectopic-pregnancy.md) requires a separate plan: selected stable cases may be monitored or treated medically, but rupture with haemorrhagic collapse requires urgent operative control, not treatment of the [vagina](../../../../../vagina.md) as though it were the principal source. A [hydatidiform mole](../../../../../hydatidiform-mole.md) is another [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md)-related source of abnormal bleeding; current management includes removal by suction evacuation and subsequent [human chorionic gonadotropin](../../../../../human-chorionic-gonadotropin.md) surveillance for persistent disease. [Hydatidiform moles: bleeding, evacuation and biochemical follow-up](../../../../../gestational-trophoblastic-disease-treatment-by-nci.md) [Early pregnancy loss, ectopic pregnancy and the distinction between vaginal and internal bleeding](../../../../../bleeding-during-pregnancy-by-acog.md) [NG126, management of tubal ectopic pregnancy and diagnostic precautions](../../../../../ectopic-pregnancy-and-miscarriage-by-nice.md)

**Later [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md) and labour.** [Placenta praevia](../../../../../placenta-praevia.md) commonly produces relatively painless bleeding, whereas [placental abruption](../../../../../placental-abruption.md) more often causes pain and uterine tenderness; these are useful patterns, not infallible rules. Management depends on the mother's circulation, the [fetus](../../../../../fetus.md), the placental lesion and the stage of [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md), with urgent delivery when required. A blind digital cervical examination is inappropriate before a possible [placenta praevia](../../../../../placenta-praevia.md) has been excluded. Ruptured [vasa praevia](../../../../../vasa-praevia.md) causes fetal rather than maternal bleeding and demands a different interpretation. A small [blood](../../../../../blood.md)-stained mucous “show” during cervical change is not equivalent to these pathological losses. [Green-top 63, history, examination and management; exclusion of placenta praevia before digital examination](../../../../../antepartum-haemorrhage-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists.md) [Vasa praevia and fetal bleeding](../../../../../placenta-praevia-placenta-accreta-and-vasa-praevia-by-the-royal-college-of-obstetricians-and-gynaecologists.md)

**After delivery.** The principal categories are failure of [uterine contraction](../../../../../uterine-contraction.md), retained [placental tissue](../../../../../placental-tissue.md), trauma and disordered [coagulation](../../../../../coagulation.md). For [uterine atony](../../../../../uterine-atony.md), the aim is to restore contraction with uterine massage and an appropriate [uterotonic](../../../../../uterotonic.md). Retained tissue, a genital laceration or [uterine inversion](../../../../../uterine-inversion.md) requires correction of that particular cause; a [coagulopathy](../../../../../coagulopathy.md) requires haemostatic support. With [placenta accreta spectrum](../../../../../placenta-accreta-spectrum.md), attempted forcible separation can provoke severe [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md). Current care combines resuscitation with cause-directed measures, and can require [tranexamic acid](../../../../../tranexamic-acid.md), balloon tamponade, embolisation or [surgery](../../../../../surgery.md) when initial measures fail. Later postpartum bleeding also raises retained tissue and [endometritis](../../../../../endometritis.md). [Causes and treatment, including retained placenta, uterine contraction, trauma and escalation](../../../../../heavy-bleeding-after-birth-by-rcog.md)

**The 1865 distinction.** An answer of the period could distinguish menstrual loss, local lesions, [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md)-related [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md) and post-delivery failure of contraction through the history and physical examination. Compression, local plugging, removal of an accessible lesion and obstetric intervention were among the available approaches; their use was debated. The 1864 discussion of [placenta praevia](../../../../../placenta-praevia.md) documents such disagreement. Modern imaging, [blood](../../../../../blood.md) banking, the present drug regimens and current operative pathways must not be attributed to that practice. Nor should historical astringents or depletion be substituted for identifying the source. [pp. 262–268, discussion of placenta praevia and competing methods of management](../../../../../midwifery-in-the-edinburgh-medical-journal-september-1864.md)
