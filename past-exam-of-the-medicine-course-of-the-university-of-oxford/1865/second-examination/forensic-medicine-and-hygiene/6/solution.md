<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**The best general measures for a sickroom are removal of the infectious source, cleaning and adequate [building ventilation](../../../../../building-ventilation.md), with targeted [disinfection](../../../../../disinfection.md) of contaminated objects. A chemical that suppresses smell is not thereby a means of making occupied room air safe.** Choice of an agent depends on the material, the organism and the conditions of use.

This distinction is available within the historical period. In [Notes on Nursing by Florence Nightingale](../../../../../notes-on-nursing-by-florence-nightingale.md), [Florence Nightingale](../../../../../florence-nightingale.md) warned against reliance on fumigations and disinfecting fluids in place of removing the offending material and supplying fresh [air](../../../../../air.md). Its advice supplies a contemporary limit on indiscriminate chemical use.[“Ventilation and Warming”, passage headed “Fumigations”: removal of the source rather than dependence on fumigating fluids](../../../../../notes-on-nursing-by-florence-nightingale.md)

An 1865 tract also rejected a universally applicable disinfectant and put removal and cleanliness first. Its further chemical and “miasm” claims require separate judgement.[Opening paragraphs: source removal and burning sugar as odour masking](../../../../../health-tract-no-154-disinfectants-1865.md)

[Chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) preparations, including chlorinated lime and chlorinated soda, were important period choices for contaminated material. In present terms their activity comes from reactive chlorine species, including [hypochlorous acid](../../../../../hypochlorous-acid.md), which damage microbial constituents through [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) and related reactions. Use on suitable cleaned surfaces or receptacles is a different matter from releasing irritating gas about the patient. Abundant organic dirt consumes the active reagent, so effective contact with the object matters more than an impressive smell.[“Chlorine and chlorine compounds”, mode of action, organic demand and practical limitations](../../../../../chemical-disinfectants-by-the-centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention.md)

[Potassium permanganate](../../../../../potassium-permanganate.md) and related preparations were promoted as oxidizing purifiers. Consumption of oxidizable dirt or odour does not establish destruction of all infectious organisms. The 1865 tract lists the class; its claim of refreshing room air by liberating [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) is a historical belief, not a substitute for ventilation.[Final paragraph: hypochlorites and manganate preparations, including claims concerning miasm and oxygen](../../../../../health-tract-no-154-disinfectants-1865.md)

[Zinc chloride](../../../../../zinc-chloride.md) and [iron(II) sulfate](../../../../../iron-ii-sulfate.md) were used to suppress decomposition and offensive discharges. Neither altered smell nor acidity proves microbial destruction. [Charcoal](../../../../../charcoal.md) provides a contrast: adsorption can retain odorous substances without reliably disinfecting an object. The tract lists these agents, but listing them does not establish equal effectiveness against infection.[Discussion of copperas, zinc chloride, chlorinated lime and charcoal; the mixing recipes and health claims are not adopted here](../../../../../health-tract-no-154-disinfectants-1865.md)

[Heat disinfection](../../../../../heat-disinfection.md) is valuable where the material can tolerate it: washable linen or utensils can receive a controlled hot process after suitable cleaning. The process must reach the whole object; a warm exterior does not show that a bulky interior received the same exposure. This is distinct from heating a room containing a patient. Burning a disposable contaminated object destroys the material altogether, but is not a general prescription for all bedding, equipment or room contents.

The usefulness of chemical [disinfection](../../../../../disinfection.md) is therefore substantial when applied correctly to a defined source, and limited when used indiscriminately. Remove excreta and soiled linen promptly, clean spills and reusable objects, protect attendants from transferring contamination, and separate flows of clean and contaminated material. Concentration, exposure time, temperature, compatibility and the amount of organic soil affect success. Modern guidance explicitly explains the shielding effect of soil and consumption of germicide by organic contamination.[“Organic and inorganic matter”, “Concentration and potency” and “Duration of exposure”](../../../../../factors-affecting-the-efficacy-of-disinfection-and-sterilization-by-the-centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention.md)

For the room itself, clean accessible surfaces and provide suitable air replacement. Controlled treatment of an empty room or an article is not the same intervention as spreading a pungent vapour in an occupied sickroom. A tolerable smell does not demonstrate efficacy, and an intolerable concentration can injure the patient and staff. Current guidance retains specific roles for disinfectants while emphasizing the separate importance of [building ventilation](../../../../../building-ventilation.md) against infectious [aerosols](../../../../../aerosol.md).[Indoor air and infection: fresh-air supply and ventilation as a distinct control](../../../../../ventilation-to-reduce-the-spread-of-respiratory-infections-by-the-uk-health-security-agency.md)

Consequently, **disinfect the contaminated material where a suitable process can act; do not use deodorization as a substitute for source removal, cleaning or fresh air**. The value and the limits of the agents depend on what they actually do to the source of infection.
