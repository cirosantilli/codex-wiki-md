**“Carbonic acid” in this question means gaseous [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), not liquid acid poisoning. Dangerous accumulation causes [hypercapnia](../../../../../hypercapnia.md), impaired consciousness and potentially death; prevention requires controlling its production and providing effective fresh-air replacement.** At high concentrations, displacement of [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) can contribute, but lack of [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) is not the only mechanism.

Early symptoms can include headache, dizziness, discomfort, restlessness, sweating, rapid or laboured [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) and an increased pulse. Greater exposure can cause confusion, loss of coordination, profound drowsiness, collapse, [convulsions](../../../../../convulsion.md), [coma](../../../../../coma-optics.md) and death. In a heavily contaminated space, collapse may occur before a person can recognize the danger or escape. [Carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) is colourless and odourless, so absence of an offensive smell provides no reassurance.[Pocket Guide, physical description, exposure route, symptoms and target organs](../../../../../carbon-dioxide-by-the-national-institute-for-occupational-safety-and-health.md)

The physiological point matters. Increased inspired [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) impairs elimination of the body's own [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and alters the acid–base state of the [blood](../../../../../blood.md). A hazardous atmosphere can therefore remain dangerous even when enough [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) is present to sustain a flame. The modern institutional discussion of [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) fire suppression distinguishes the gas's direct physiological effects from mere dilution of [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md).[“Life Safety Considerations of Carbon Dioxide”: physiological effects and oxygen concentration](../../../../../carbon-dioxide-as-a-fire-suppressant-examining-the-risks-by-the-united-states-environmental-protection-agency.md)

The characteristic settings are poorly ventilated spaces in which [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) is continuously generated or accumulates: brewery or wine-fermentation vessels, cellars, pits, wells, vaults and places containing decomposing organic material. Fermentation and [cellular respiration](../../../../../cellular-respiration.md) generate the gas; combustion is another source. In a badly ventilated occupied room, human [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) and unflued fires or lamps add to the burden. A dangerous combustion atmosphere may also contain [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md), which is a separate poison and requires a separate assessment. [Carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) is denser than [air](../../../../../air.md), so low or poorly swept spaces deserve attention; currents and mixing mean that one cannot assume a sharp, motionless layer with safe air above it.[Gas density and properties](../../../../../carbon-dioxide-by-the-national-institute-for-occupational-safety-and-health.md)

For prevention, remove or enclose the source where practicable and maintain [building ventilation](../../../../../building-ventilation.md) that actually sweeps the occupied and low-lying spaces. Exhaust contaminated [air](../../../../../air.md) to a safe place and bring replacement [air](../../../../../air.md) from a clean source. Opening a small window elsewhere in a building does not establish an adequate supply at the worker's position. Fermentation vessels, pits and similar spaces should be arranged for inspection and cleaning without entry where possible.

The nineteenth-century emphasis on fresh air and caution before entering such spaces remains sound. Current practice adds atmosphere testing for [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) deficiency and other relevant gases, together with controlled entry and rescue arrangements. A burning candle is an inadequate safety test: flame behaviour neither measures [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) nor rules out a different toxic gas. A rescuer who enters an unknown atmosphere without appropriate protection can become a second casualty. Trained rescue with suitable supplied breathing air and retrieval arrangements must be distinguished from simply holding one's breath and climbing in.[29 CFR 1910.146, atmosphere evaluation, ventilation and rescue provisions; cited as a present technical comparison, not English law in 1865](../../../../../permit-required-confined-spaces-by-the-occupational-safety-and-health-administration.md)

For ordinary rooms and wards, prevention also means limiting overcrowding, maintaining reliable air inlets and outlets, and removing products of combustion through proper flues. These principles concern continuing air exchange: a large room merely delays contamination if its [air](../../../../../air.md) is never renewed. They explain why the following ward question asks separately about cubic space and the rate of change of [air](../../../../../air.md).
