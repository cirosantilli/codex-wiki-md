**The strongest period examples are [cholera](../../../../../cholera.md), [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md), [dysentery](../../../../../dysentery.md) and other diarrhoeal illness associated with contaminated supplies. Other diseases were attributed to water on weaker grounds; the particular contaminant and route of exposure must be specified.** A chemical impurity, an infectious agent and an organism that uses water during its life cycle are different kinds of cause.

For an answer situated in 1865, [John Snow](../../../../../john-snow.md)'s work already provided evidence connecting [cholera](../../../../../cholera.md) with swallowed material from an affected person's intestinal discharges, including contaminated [drinking water](../../../../../drinking-water.md). His 1855 second edition compared populations supplied with different waters, as well as investigating local outbreaks. The persuasive feature is an exposure comparison, not simply proximity to a bad smell. Neither a single chemical analysis nor the later course of an epidemic can by itself settle every link in transmission.[Second edition, 1855, pp.55–98, comparison of water supplies and cholera mortality](../../../../../on-the-mode-of-communication-of-cholera-by-john-snow.md)

For [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md), the association with sewage-contaminated wells or supplies should be explained through infectious excreta reaching food or drink. [William Budd](../../../../../william-budd.md)'s 1861 discussion placed the infectious material in the intestinal discharges and opposed spontaneous generation from ordinary decay. That contemporary reasoning gives a much more precise answer than saying that any foul [water](../../../../../water.md) creates a fever. Modern knowledge identifies a specific [bacterium](../../../../../bacteria.md), [Salmonella Typhi](../../../../../salmonella-typhi.md), rather than an unspecified product of [putrefaction](../../../../../putrefaction.md).[1861, Part III, p.575: infectious discharges and rejection of spontaneous origin](../../../../../observations-on-typhoid-or-intestinal-fever-the-pythogenic-theory-by-william-budd.md)

[Diarrhoea](../../../../../diarrhoea.md) and [dysentery](../../../../../dysentery.md) name clinical patterns with several possible causes. Sewage contamination can convey organisms producing them, but not every episode is a [waterborne disease](../../../../../waterborne-disease.md). Current authoritative guidance recognizes transmission through unsafe [drinking water](../../../../../drinking-water.md) of [cholera](../../../../../cholera.md), [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md), dysenteric and other diarrhoeal infections, [hepatitis A](../../../../../hepatitis-a.md) and [polio](../../../../../poliomyelitis.md). The last two are present-day additions to the explanation, not an assertion that their viral agents were identified in 1865.[“Key facts” and “Water and health”: diseases associated with faecally contaminated supplies](../../../../../drinking-water-by-the-world-health-organization.md)

Parasites need the same care about route. [Guinea-worm disease](../../../../../dracunculiasis.md) can follow swallowing infected tiny crustaceans in unsafe [drinking water](../../../../../drinking-water.md); it is not caused by a generic chemical property of stagnant [water](../../../../../water.md). That life-cycle account is modern corroboration. Other water-associated infections can be acquired through contact rather than drinking, and diseases carried by insects breeding near [water](../../../../../water.md) form another category.[“Transmission, life cycle and incubation period”: ingestion of infected copepods in drinking water](../../../../../dracunculiasis-by-the-world-health-organization.md)

An important example of a nineteenth-century attribution is [goitre](../../../../../goitre.md) and the developmental disorder then called “cretinism”. An 1861 review linked their distribution to lime-rich [drinking water](../../../../../drinking-water.md) and treated this as a strong causal demonstration. This is evidence of what was supposed, not a conclusion to repeat as established medicine. Endemic [goitre](../../../../../goitre.md) is closely associated with [iodine deficiency](../../../../../iodine-deficiency.md), and severe deficiency during development can have profound neurological consequences; water hardness alone does not identify the relevant cause in a population.[pp.43–46, comparison of districts and the asserted lime-water explanation of goitre and cretinism](../../../../../the-british-soldier-in-india-in-the-british-and-foreign-medico-chirurgical-review-1861.md)[Health Professional Fact Sheet, “Iodine deficiency”: goitre and fetal neurodevelopmental consequences](../../../../../iodine-by-the-national-institutes-of-health-office-of-dietary-supplements.md)

Similarly, [malaria](../../../../../malaria.md) should not be explained as poisoning from a mouthful of stagnant [water](../../../../../water.md). Its ordinary transmission is through infected mosquitoes; aquatic breeding conditions can affect vector populations without making ingestion the route of infection. Modern public-health accounts distinguish such water-related vector exposure from enteric transmission.[Risks of epidemics: enteric disease versus mosquito-borne disease associated with breeding sites](../../../../../water-sanitation-and-health-in-humanitarian-emergencies-by-the-world-health-organization.md)

There are also genuine noninfectious hazards. [Lead](../../../../../lead.md) can enter [drinking water](../../../../../drinking-water.md) from plumbing and cause [lead poisoning](../../../../../lead-poisoning.md); other sources can introduce toxic [arsenic](../../../../../arsenic.md). These are poisonings by particular substances, not evidence that every mineralized supply is harmful. Current guidance explicitly distinguishes microbial contamination from naturally occurring or infrastructure-derived chemicals.[“Water and health”: arsenic, fluoride and lead leaching from water-system components](../../../../../drinking-water-by-the-world-health-organization.md)

Thus the sound list is accompanied by a causal distinction: diseases transmitted by contaminated [water](../../../../../water.md), diseases merely associated with living near [water](../../../../../water.md), specific chemical poisonings, and historical claims requiring correction. Clear, pleasant-tasting [water](../../../../../water.md) may transmit infection, while a conspicuous harmless mineral constituent may have nothing to do with an outbreak. Epidemiological comparison and investigation of the source are essential to deciding which relation is present.
