**The classical sequence in [lobar pneumonia](../../../../../lobar-pneumonia.md) is [congestion](../../../../../congestion.md), red [hepatization](../../../../../hepatization.md), grey [hepatization](../../../../../hepatization.md), and resolution.** These are overlapping morphological phases, not a clock that every [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) follows. An affected [lung](../../../../../lung.md) may contain adjacent areas in different phases; [bronchopneumonia](../../../../../bronchopneumonia.md) has scattered airway-centred foci rather than uniform involvement of a whole lobe. Modern [antibiotic](../../../../../antibiotic.md) treatment often interrupts the textbook sequence.[Histopathology: lobar pneumonia and its four stages](../../../../../pneumonia-pathology-in-statpearls.md)

During [congestion](../../../../../congestion.md), the [lung](../../../../../lung.md) is heavy, dark red and boggy but still contains some [air](../../../../../air.md). Its cut surface gives a blood-stained watery fluid. [Microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) shows distended [alveolar capillaries](../../../../../alveolar-capillary.md), [oedema](../../../../../oedema.md) within [alveoli](../../../../../alveolus.md), and an early cellular [exudate](../../../../../exudate.md), before dense [pulmonary consolidation](../../../../../pulmonary-consolidation.md) has developed. Reduced [alveolar ventilation](../../../../../alveolar-ventilation.md) begins as fluid replaces the normal air space.

In red [hepatization](../../../../../hepatization.md), the affected lobe is enlarged, firm, red and substantially airless. Its granular cut surface resembles [liver](../../../../../liver.md), which explains the name; it is not a transformation into [liver](../../../../../liver.md) tissue. The [alveoli](../../../../../alveolus.md) are occupied by a mesh of [fibrin](../../../../../fibrin.md) containing abundant [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) and [neutrophils](../../../../../neutrophil.md), while the [alveolar capillaries](../../../../../alveolar-capillary.md) remain congested. Filling of neighbouring air spaces produces continuous [pulmonary consolidation](../../../../../pulmonary-consolidation.md). Associated [pleurisy](../../../../../pleurisy.md) may put a [fibrinous exudate](../../../../../fibrinous-exudate.md) on the overlying [pleura](../../../../../pleura.md).

In grey [hepatization](../../../../../hepatization.md), [pulmonary consolidation](../../../../../pulmonary-consolidation.md) remains, but the colour becomes grey, grey-brown or yellowish as intact [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) disappear and the [fibrin](../../../../../fibrin.md) and inflammatory [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md) dominate. [Microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) shows degenerating [neutrophils](../../../../../neutrophil.md), debris and persistent intra-alveolar [fibrin](../../../../../fibrin.md); the conspicuous vascular engorgement of the preceding phase diminishes. Grey colour by itself does not establish a separate pathogen or mean that an [abscess](../../../../../abscess.md) has formed.[Histopathology: red and grey hepatization](../../../../../typical-bacterial-pneumonia-in-statpearls.md)

In resolution, the intra-alveolar [exudate](../../../../../exudate.md) is broken down, ingested by [macrophages](../../../../../macrophage.md), removed through airways and cleared by absorption. The lobe softens and becomes aerated again. Where the [alveolar septa](../../../../../alveolar-septum.md) have survived, the architecture can return nearly to normal; an air-space [exudate](../../../../../exudate.md) need not become a permanent scar. Failure of clearance may instead lead to organisation and [fibrosis](../../../../../fibrosis.md); destructive infection can produce [abscess](../../../../../abscess.md) or [empyema](../../../../../empyema.md). These are possible outcomes, not obligatory further stages.

A useful examination compares a cut section and a thin [histological section](../../../../../histological-section.md) from each region, following the [alveolar septa](../../../../../alveolar-septum.md) to distinguish filling of existing air spaces from destruction of the framework. A dense airless piece may sink in water, but this physical test only shows reduced [air](../../../../../air.md) content: [atelectasis](../../../../../atelectasis.md) can also sink. The distribution and the [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) establish why the tissue is airless.
