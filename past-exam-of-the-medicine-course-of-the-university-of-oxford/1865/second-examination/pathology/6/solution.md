**[Gallstones](../../../../../gallstone.md) contain chiefly [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md) and/or [bile pigment](../../../../../bile-pigment.md), with variable [calcium salts](../../../../../calcium-salt.md) and an organic matrix.** They are mixtures with different predominant components, not one compound with a universal formula. The period word [cholesterin](../../../../../cholesterol.md) denotes [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md).

Cholesterol-rich stones contain crystalline [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md), often with a smaller pigmented nucleus and layers of other material. Mixed stones combine [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md), pigment, [calcium salts](../../../../../calcium-salt.md) and an organic matrix including [mucin](../../../../../mucin.md). Pigment stones contain much less [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md); a major constituent is [calcium bilirubinate](../../../../../calcium-bilirubinate.md), related to the [bile pigment](../../../../../bile-pigment.md) [bilirubin](../../../../../bilirubin.md). Minor inorganic constituents can include [calcium carbonate](../../../../../calcium-carbonate.md) and [calcium phosphate](../../../../../calcium-phosphate.md). Not every visibly dark stone has the same composition, and no fixed percentage follows from the question.[Cholelithiasis: cholesterol, black pigment and brown pigment stone composition](../../../../../gallstone-composition-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

The useful modern subdivision of pigment stones is into black and brown types. Black stones commonly contain polymerised pigment with [calcium salts](../../../../../calcium-salt.md); brown stones include [calcium bilirubinate](../../../../../calcium-bilirubinate.md) and calcium salts of fatty acids, particularly in infected stagnant duct bile. These refinements explain why neither “all stones are [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md)” nor “all stones are lime salts” is adequate.[Definition and facts; symptoms and causes: cholesterol, bilirubin and bile composition](../../../../../gallstones-by-niddk.md)

Chemical examination should distinguish the fractions rather than infer them merely from the external colour. [Cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md) can be extracted with a suitable organic solvent and recognised in recrystallised material; microscopy can show characteristic plate-like crystals. A mineral residue after removal or destruction of organic matter indicates an inorganic fraction, but residue alone cannot identify a particular salt. Layering, facets and colour describe growth and packing and are not chemical analyses. In particular, [calcium bilirubinate](../../../../../calcium-bilirubinate.md) is not interchangeable with [calcium carbonate](../../../../../calcium-carbonate.md), and [cholesterol](../../../../../cholesterol.md) is not a [bile salt](../../../../../bile-salt.md).
