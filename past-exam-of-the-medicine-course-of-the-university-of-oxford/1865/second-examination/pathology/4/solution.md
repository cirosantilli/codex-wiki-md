**The historical name [pityriasis](../../../../../pityriasis.md) grouped finely scaling eruptions; its varieties were not all one [fungal infection](../../../../../fungal-infection.md).** A useful period list is simple or scalp [pityriasis](../../../../../pityriasis.md), [pityriasis rubra](../../../../../pityriasis-rubra.md), [pityriasis versicolor](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor.md) and [pityriasis nigra](../../../../../pityriasis-nigra.md). The names describe distribution or colour, and classifications differed. In particular, “nigra” was sometimes a very dark form of “versicolor,” sometimes a pigmentation disorder with little true scaling. It cannot safely be translated into one present species of [fungus](../../../../../fungus.md).[Pityriasis: capitis, rubra, versicolor and nigra; limits of the nigra category](../../../../../the-practice-of-medicine-by-robley-dunglison-1844.md)

Simple [pityriasis](../../../../../pityriasis.md), often called [pityriasis capitis](../../../../../pityriasis-capitis.md) on the scalp, gives loose, whitish, bran-like scales with little or variable redness. [Microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) of a scale principally shows flattened keratinised [epithelial cells](../../../../../epithelial-cell.md) and debris. An oily or inflamed scalp can also contain organisms normally living there; this does not make every case identical to [pityriasis versicolor](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor.md). Present descriptions of [dandruff](../../../../../dandruff.md) and [seborrhoeic dermatitis](../../../../../seborrhoeic-dermatitis.md) explain many such appearances, but the historical label cannot retrospectively specify the cause of each case.[Clinical features and relationship to seborrhoeic dermatitis](../../../../../seborrhoeic-dermatitis-by-dermnet.md)

[Pityriasis rubra](../../../../../pityriasis-rubra.md) denoted a conspicuously red, scaling eruption, sometimes extensive. Detached scales are again principally epidermal material. A modern [skin biopsy](../../../../../skin-biopsy.md) may show an inflammatory pattern, but there is no single microscopical organism that defines the historical category. It must not automatically be renamed [pityriasis rubra pilaris](../../../../../pityriasis-rubra-pilaris.md), a distinct follicular disorder. An extensive red and peeling [skin](../../../../../skin.md) is a pattern requiring causal diagnosis, rather than proof of one infection.[Definition, causes and nonspecific biopsy findings](../../../../../erythroderma-by-dermnet.md)

[Pityriasis versicolor](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor.md) produces irregular pale, yellowish-brown or brown patches, commonly on the upper [anatomical trunk](../../../../../anatomical-trunk.md), with a fine surface scale. Here the [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) is especially discriminating: a superficial scraping, cleared with [potassium hydroxide](../../../../../potassium-hydroxide.md), shows clusters of round yeast forms and short curved [hyphae](../../../../../hypha.md) within the [stratum corneum](../../../../../stratum-corneum.md). Modern classification places the organisms in [Malassezia](../../../../../malassezia.md). The older “Microsporon furfur” terminology should not be read as identification of a modern [Microsporum](../../../../../microsporum.md) dermatophyte. On a [histological section](../../../../../histological-section.md) the organisms occupy the horny surface layer and are made conspicuous by suitable [fungal stains](../../../../../fungal-stain.md).[Histology and special stains: yeasts and short hyphae in the stratum corneum](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor-pathology-by-dermnet.md)

[Pityriasis nigra](../../../../../pityriasis-nigra.md) calls first for verification of what is actually being described. If it is dark [pityriasis versicolor](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor.md), the same superficial yeast and [hyphae](../../../../../hypha.md) may be found; if the lesion is pigmentation without a fungal eruption, this finding cannot be assumed. A contemporary discussion explicitly recorded the overlap of nigra and versicolor and disputes over the significance of the supposed parasite.[pp. 208–209 and 218–219: versicolor, nigra and conflicting fungal interpretations](../../../../../on-the-phytopathology-of-the-skin-review-1864.md) Thus **epidermal scales are common to the description, but demonstrable fungal structures particularly distinguish [pityriasis versicolor](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor.md)**. Examine actual scrapings with a [optical microscope](../../../../../optical-microscope.md), compare several fields and use appropriate controls before attributing a different scaling disorder to a [fungus](../../../../../fungus.md).
