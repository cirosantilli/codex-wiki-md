<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

The strong evidence is the reproducible conversion between mechanical [work](../../../../../work.md) and thermal changes. In [Joule's paddle-wheel experiment](../../../../../joule-s-paddle-wheel-experiment.md), descending masses drive vanes in a liquid, and a measured input of mechanical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) produces a measured rise in [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). Allowing for the vessel and thermal losses gives a quantitative mechanical equivalent. Sustained frictional heating, including [Rumford's cannon-boring experiment](../../../../../rumford-s-cannon-boring-experiment.md), also challenged a simple account in which rubbing merely released a fixed stored supply of a material heat fluid.[1850, friction experiments and calorimetric corrections](../../../../../on-the-mechanical-equivalent-of-heat-by-james-prescott-joule.md)

Electrical and compressional experiments reinforce the connection. A resistor warms under [Joule heating](../../../../../joule-heating.md), with electrical input $\int VI\,dt$. A gas compressed with little [heat transfer](../../../../../heat-transfer.md) can rise in [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), showing that mechanical [work](../../../../../work.md) can increase its [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md). Conversely an expanding gas can perform [work](../../../../../work.md) while cooling. Such experiments connect several kinds of change through a conserved [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) balance.

The [kinetic theory of gases](../../../../../kinetic-theory-of-gases.md) supplies a microscopic model. Translational molecular motion produces momentum transfer to walls; for a dilute monatomic ideal gas,

$$
p=\frac13\frac NV m\langle v^2\rangle,
\qquad U=\frac32Nk_{\rm B}T.
$$

Thus increasing molecular kinetic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is reflected in [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and pressure changes. These formulas describe a specified model, not a proof that every substance's [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) consists only of translational motion. Rotations, vibrations and intermolecular potential [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) also matter.

In modern terminology, **[heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) is energy transferred because of a temperature difference**. The body has [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md), and microscopic motion accounts for part of it. Calling heat “a form of motion” captures the historical rejection of a conserved material caloric, but should not obscure that distinction between stored [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and an energy-transfer process.
