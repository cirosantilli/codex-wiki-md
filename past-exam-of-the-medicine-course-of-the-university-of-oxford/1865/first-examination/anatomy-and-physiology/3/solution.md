<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Exhaled carbonic acid here means [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md); its amount per unit time must be distinguished from its percentage in expired air.** A person can breathe a larger volume of more dilute expired gas and still exhale more total [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md).

At metabolic steady state, pulmonary [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) output approximately reflects its production by [cellular respiration](../../../../../cellular-respiration.md). Increased muscular activity is therefore a major physiological cause of increased output: walking, climbing and strenuous exercise increase substrate oxidation and usually increase both ventilation and total [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) elimination. Rest lowers the requirement; during ordinary sleep, reduced activity and metabolic demand generally lower output relative to the waking active state. A comparison needs the same time interval and should not confuse a short transient with a sustained resting measurement.

Food affects output in two ways. Digestion, absorption and processing increase metabolic expenditure after a meal, with a time course depending on the meal. The material oxidized also matters. The [respiratory quotient](../../../../../respiratory-quotient.md) is the ratio of metabolic [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) production to [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) use. For complete oxidation of [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md),

$$
\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6}+6\mathrm{O_2}\longrightarrow6\mathrm{CO_2}+6\mathrm{H_2O},
\qquad \boxed{\mathrm{RQ}=1}.
$$

A typical [fatty acid](../../../../../fatty-acid.md) gives less [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) per unit [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) consumed; for [palmitic acid](../../../../../palmitic-acid.md),

$$
\mathrm{C_{16}H_{32}O_2}+23\mathrm{O_2}\longrightarrow16\mathrm{CO_2}+16\mathrm{H_2O},
\qquad \boxed{\mathrm{RQ}=\frac{16}{23}\simeq0.70}.
$$

These are substrate comparisons, not a rule that any high-fat meal immediately reduces a person's total daily output. Fasting changes fuel selection as well as metabolic rate, so both processes must be considered.[Absorptive and Postabsorptive States: storage, mobilization and changing fuel use](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/24-5-metabolic-states-of-the-body.md)

Cold exposure increases output when maintaining body [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) requires extra [thermogenesis](../../../../../thermogenesis.md), especially [shivering](../../../../../shivering.md). Moving from cold to a thermally comfortable environment can reduce that extra production. Heating does not simply increase or decrease output in proportion to air [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md): passive warming, heat stress, activity and the thermoregulatory response differ. Growth, body size, age, pregnancy and lactation can change whole-body metabolic requirements. Comparisons between people therefore need to specify whether output is absolute or normalized to [body mass](../../../../../body-mass.md) or another appropriate size measure. Biological sex alone is not an explanation independent of these factors. Daily variation also mixes circadian physiology with sleep, meals and activity.

Ventilatory changes require a separate account. Voluntary [hyperventilation](../../../../../hyperventilation.md) can initially wash [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) out of body stores faster than it is being produced; breath holding temporarily prevents its pulmonary escape while production continues. Subsequent recovery changes the output again. The ratio measured at the mouth, the [respiratory exchange ratio](../../../../../respiratory-exchange-ratio.md), need not equal the tissue [respiratory quotient](../../../../../respiratory-quotient.md) during those transitions or during intense exercise with acid buffering. Reduced expired output for a few seconds is therefore not evidence that tissue [metabolism](../../../../../metabolism-split.md) has stopped.[Carbon-dioxide production, transport in dissolved and bicarbonate forms, and pulmonary elimination](../../../../../physiology-carbon-dioxide-transport-statpearls.md)

A measurement should collect total expired gas over a known time and determine its dry [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) fraction, allowing for inspired [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md). With inspired and expired dry gas volumes expressed under the same reference conditions,

$$
\boxed{\dot V_{\mathrm{CO_2}}=\dot V_EF_{E,\mathrm{CO_2}}-\dot V_IF_{I,\mathrm{CO_2}}}.
$$

Here $\dot V_E$ and $\dot V_I$ are expired and inspired volume rates and the $F$ terms their respective fractions. Recording food intake, physical activity, ambient [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and time since the preceding intervention makes the comparison interpretable. [Carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) output is a measured flux, not merely an observation that an animal makes a gas collection vessel cloudy.
