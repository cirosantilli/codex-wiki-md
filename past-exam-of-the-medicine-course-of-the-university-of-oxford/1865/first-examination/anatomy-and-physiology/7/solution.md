↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

**Section and stimulation show that the cervical [sympathetic](../../../../../sympathetic.md) pathway carries influences controlling head-and-neck vascular tone and several ocular functions.** The effects of cutting the pathway must be compared with those of stimulating its peripheral continuation, and neither should be confused with direct heating of the [nerve](../../../../../nerve.md).

In the classical unilateral experiment, dividing the cervical [sympathetic trunk](../../../../../sympathetic-trunk.md) produces dilation of vessels on the same side of the head, particularly visible in the rabbit's ear, with redness and increased [skin](../../../../../skin.md) [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). The opposite side provides a useful simultaneous comparison, though it is not immune to systemic changes. The observation establishes that an intact pathway normally contributes a constricting influence. More warm [blood](../../../../../blood.md) reaching a relatively cool ear explains the warming without requiring a special heat-producing material conducted by the [nerve](../../../../../nerve.md).

Electrical stimulation of the cranial or peripheral cut end produces the complementary effect: vessels narrow, the ear becomes paler and its [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) tends to fall. When stimulation stops, dilation returns. A stimulating circuit and appropriately placed electrodes supply a controlled perturbation, while direct observation of vessel calibre and a [thermometer](../../../../../thermometer.md) provide separate readouts. Stimulation of the central end instead can engage pathways through the [central nervous system](../../../../../central-nervous-system.md) and is not the same local test. These comparisons distinguish an efferent vasomotor mechanism from a mere correlation between an operation and warmth.

[Claude Bernard](../../../../../claude-bernard.md)'s section experiments in 1851 and the subsequent work of [Charles-Édouard Brown-Séquard](../../../../../charles-edouard-brown-sequard.md) helped establish this interpretation. A contemporary report published before the examination records the reversal of ocular and vascular signs on galvanizing the upper end of the divided cervical sympathetic. The report also describes recovery differences after section and removal of a segment, warning against treating an early and a late observation as the same experiment.[Account of section, removal of a segment and repeated galvanization; ocular and vascular effects](../../../../../observations-on-the-experiments-of-professor-budge-upon-the-influence-of-the-great-sympathetic-1853.md)

The ocular effects include a smaller [pupil](../../../../../pupil.md) after interruption and dilation with stimulation of the surviving peripheral pathway. Modern anatomy explains this through the sympathetic supply to the radial [iris dilator muscle](../../../../../iris-dilator-muscle.md). Interruption can also narrow the palpebral opening by reducing activity in sympathetically supplied eyelid [smooth muscle](../../../../../smooth-muscle.md). In species with a conspicuous [nictitating membrane](../../../../../nictitating-membrane.md), its position changes; apparent recession or protrusion of the eye is species-dependent and should not be transferred uncritically to humans. The [pupil](../../../../../pupil.md)'s constrictor supply through the [parasympathetic nervous system](../../../../../parasympathetic-nervous-system.md) is a different pathway, so sympathetic section does not amount to paralysis of every ocular [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md).

The cervical pathway includes ascending preganglionic fibres reaching cervical ganglia, notably the [superior cervical ganglion](../../../../../superior-cervical-ganglion.md), and postganglionic routes towards cranial targets, including periarterial plexuses. The source of this sympathetic output is thoracic spinal circuitry; it is not generated merely because the chain lies in the neck. Modern extension of the account includes sympathetic control of [sweat glands](../../../../../sweat-gland.md) in relevant territories. The distribution of lost sweating depends on the lesion and the species; a rabbit-ear [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) experiment is not itself a demonstration of the entire human sweating pathway.[Structure and Function: ascending pathway, cranial distribution and ocular/vasomotor functions](../../../../../neuroanatomy-superior-cervical-ganglion-statpearls.md)

The experimental lesson is therefore specific. The same intervention can alter calibre, [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pupil](../../../../../pupil.md) size through different effector structures. Measuring those outcomes separately, documenting stimulus timing, and distinguishing section from stimulation establish more than naming a general nutritive or calorific function of the sympathetic. Modern [neurotransmitter](../../../../../neurotransmitter.md) and [receptor](../../../../../receptor-biochemistry.md) descriptions can explain these observations further, but they were not available in their present form in 1865.
