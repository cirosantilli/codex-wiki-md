<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**A lymphatic gland is a [lymph node](../../../../../lymph-node.md); a Lieberkühnian gland is an [intestinal crypt](../../../../../intestinal-crypt.md); a [Brunner gland](../../../../../brunner-gland.md) is a predominantly mucous duodenal gland.** The first is a lymphoid organ with traversing lymphatic channels; the other two are epithelial glands opening towards the intestinal lumen. Similar historical terminology must not erase that difference.

A [lymph node](../../../../../lymph-node.md) has a fibrous [connective tissue](../../../../../connective-tissue.md) capsule sending trabeculae into the organ and an internal reticular framework supporting mobile [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Its outer cortex contains lymphoid follicles, whose appearance changes with immune activity; activated secondary follicles have paler [germinal centres](../../../../../germinal-centre.md) surrounded by more densely packed [lymphocytes](../../../../../lymphocyte.md). The deeper cortex or [paracortex](../../../../../paracortex.md) lies between follicular cortex and medulla. The medulla contains branching cellular cords separated by medullary sinuses. A [hilum](../../../../../hilum.md) provides the entry and exit of the main [blood vessels](../../../../../blood-vessel.md) and the exit of efferent lymphatics.

Afferent [lymphatic vessels](../../../../../lymphatic-vessel.md) enter at the convex surface and deliver [lymph](../../../../../lymph.md) to the subcapsular sinus. Flow then passes through cortical or trabecular channels into medullary sinuses and out through efferent vessels near the [hilum](../../../../../hilum.md). [Macrophages](../../../../../macrophage.md) and the reticular arrangement bring carried material into contact with the cellular apparatus. The node's [blood circulation](../../../../../blood-circulation.md) is distinct from that lymphatic route. In modern terms, follicles are predominantly [B cell](../../../../../b-cell.md) territories, the [paracortex](../../../../../paracortex.md) is rich in [T cells](../../../../../t-cell.md), and medullary cords contain many antibody-producing [plasma cells](../../../../../plasma-cell.md). Those cellular classifications go beyond an ordinary 1865 microscopic description.[Structure; Function; Histochemistry and Cytochemistry: cortex, paracortex and medulla](../../../../../histology-lymph-nodes-statpearls.md)

Thin stained sections show the overall architecture, while a silver stain highlights the supporting reticular fibres rather than every [lymphocyte](../../../../../lymphocyte.md). The fibres include [type III collagen](../../../../../type-iii-collagen.md); the thicker capsule has a different connective-tissue organization. One must not interpret the meshwork itself as an epithelial secretory tube, or assume a section missing the [hilum](../../../../../hilum.md) proves that the node has no efferent vessel.[Slides 22 and 31: silver-stained reticulum, sinuses, capsule, hilum and postcapillary venules](../../../../../lymphatic-tissues-lymph-nodes-by-columbia-university.md)

The [intestinal crypts](../../../../../intestinal-crypt.md) are simple tubular epithelial invaginations opening between the [intestinal villi](../../../../../intestinal-villus.md) in the [small intestine](../../../../../small-intestine.md). Their bases approach the [muscularis mucosae](../../../../../muscularis-mucosae.md), remaining within the mucosa and surrounded by [lamina propria](../../../../../lamina-propria.md). Each gland has a lumen continuous with the intestinal surface and a lining supported by a [basement membrane](../../../../../basement-membrane.md). It contains columnar epithelial [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) at different stages of differentiation, [goblet cells](../../../../../goblet-cell.md) producing mucus, endocrine [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) and, in small-[intestinal crypt](../../../../../intestinal-crypt.md) bases, conspicuous granular [Paneth cells](../../../../../paneth-cell.md). A proliferative compartment supplies replacement [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) that move towards the surface; this modern [cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md)-renewal account explains why dividing [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) are common in the crypts. Crypts also occur in the [colon](../../../../../colon.md), whose mucosa lacks ordinary small-intestinal villi. One should therefore specify the region rather than make villi part of every crypt's definition.

A [Brunner gland](../../../../../brunner-gland.md) lies chiefly in the submucosa of the proximal [duodenum](../../../../../duodenum.md), below the [muscularis mucosae](../../../../../muscularis-mucosae.md). It is a branched, compound tubular gland with pale mucus-rich secretory [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md), basal nuclei and small lumina. Its ducts pass through the [muscularis mucosae](../../../../../muscularis-mucosae.md) and commonly join overlying [intestinal crypts](../../../../../intestinal-crypt.md) before opening into the lumen. Its alkaline, mucus-rich secretion helps protect the mucosa from acidic gastric contents. The position below the [muscularis mucosae](../../../../../muscularis-mucosae.md) distinguishes it particularly well from a [Lieberkühn gland](../../../../../intestinal-crypt.md) in the mucosa.[Small Intestine; regional differences: crypts, cell types and submucosal Brunner glands with ducts joining crypts](../../../../../gi-tract-notes-by-duke-university.md)

The most useful comparison is consequently one of compartments: a reticular lymphoid organ containing sinuses, a mucosal epithelial tube, and a branched submucosal secretory system. Recognition of the capsule, epithelial lining, [muscularis mucosae](../../../../../muscularis-mucosae.md) and duct connections establishes those distinctions more securely than a name alone.
