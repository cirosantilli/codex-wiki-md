**The historical [Marsh test](../../../../../marsh-test.md) separates arsenic into a volatile hydride and then into a visible deposit, allowing it to be examined apart from much of the original organic matrix.** It requires controls and confirmation because a dark deposit is not unique to [arsenic](../../../../../arsenic.md). The test is associated with [James Marsh](../../../../../james-marsh.md); its primary account explicitly addresses mixtures containing animal and vegetable matter.[Opening pages, analytical purpose and hydrogen-generating apparatus](../../../../../separation-of-arsenic-by-james-marsh.md)

Retain representative material and a control portion before destructive preparation. A complex organic sample can be clarified and, where needed, subjected to a validated wet oxidation that destroys interfering organic matter while retaining the arsenic in the analytical preparation. Avoid uncontrolled heating to dryness or conditions that volatilize an arsenic compound; successful mineralization cannot simply be assumed. The reagent sequence must leave the arsenic in a reducible form and must not leave enough oxidant to defeat the subsequent reduction. A recovery experiment with a known addition is a check on the entire preparation, not merely on the last colour reaction.

In the hydrogen-generating stage, arsenic-free [zinc](../../../../../zinc.md) reacts with a suitable dilute acid. The sample's reducible arsenic is converted to [arsine](../../../../../arsine.md), which passes with the carrier gas out of the generating vessel. A representative net reduction balance for [arsenic(III)](../../../../../arsenic-iii.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathrm H_3\mathrm{AsO}_3+3\mathrm{Zn}+6\mathrm H^+\longrightarrow\mathrm{AsH}_3+3\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}+3\mathrm H_2\mathrm O.}
$$

This is a reaction balance, not a claim that the old phrase “nascent hydrogen” identifies one uniquely established microscopic mechanism.

The apparatus needs controlled reagent entry, an unobstructed gas path and a means of excluding droplets carried from the sample. In the later heated-tube form, the hydride decomposes at a hot section:

$$
\boxed{2\mathrm{AsH}_3\longrightarrow2\mathrm{As}+3\mathrm H_2.}
$$

Arsenic collects as a mirror on the cooler glass beyond it. Marsh's original flame method deposited material on a cold surface held in relation to the burning jet. The original arrangement and the heated-tube improvement should not be treated as identical apparatus. In either case the useful analytical separation is transfer of the arsenic away from the nonvolatile matrix, not just darkening of that matrix.

Run the complete reagent blank first: contaminated zinc or acid can otherwise make an arsenic-free sample appear positive. A positive known control checks that hydride production and collection work. [Antimony](../../../../../antimony.md) can form [stibine](../../../../../stibine.md) and a similar dark deposit, so mirror formation alone is not conclusive. Confirm with independent chemistry, for example controlled oxidation of the collected deposit followed by appropriate arsenic tests; the greater susceptibility of an arsenic mirror to suitable hypochlorite treatment supplies an additional historical distinction from an antimony deposit. No single visual resemblance should replace these discriminations.

A negative test is meaningful only relative to the validated preparation, detection limit and controls. Modern arsenic analysis uses instrumental methods with defined recovery and sensitivity; the historical demonstration is not itself a contemporary forensic certification. [Arsine](../../../../../arsine.md) is acutely toxic, and the hydrogen-bearing gas stream introduces further apparatus hazards: this account explains the analytical chemistry, not a household procedure.[Physical Description, Symptoms and Target Organs](../../../../../arsine-in-the-niosh-pocket-guide.md)
