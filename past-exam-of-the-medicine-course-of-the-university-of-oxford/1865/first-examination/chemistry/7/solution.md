<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

**“Carbonic oxide” means [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md), $\mathrm{CO}$: a combustible, reducing, colourless gas distinct from [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md).** Several preparations remove part of the oxygen from a carbon–oxygen compound or prevent complete oxidation of carbon.

Passing [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) over sufficiently hot [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{CO}_2+\mathrm C\rightleftharpoons2\mathrm{CO}.}
$$

High temperature favours substantial conversion. The reaction is important in furnaces, but gas emerging from such a process need not be pure [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md); residual [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and other gases must be considered. Incomplete [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md) of carbonaceous fuel is another source, with the ideal net balance $2\mathrm C+\mathrm O_2\to2\mathrm{CO}$. It is a source principle rather than a selective method guaranteeing a pure sample.

For a historical laboratory preparation, [formic acid](../../../../../formic-acid.md) can be dehydrated by a suitable strongly dehydrating medium:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{HCOOH}\longrightarrow\mathrm{CO}+\mathrm H_2\mathrm O.}
$$

[Oxalic acid](../../../../../oxalic-acid.md) under an appropriate dehydrating treatment yields a mixture:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm H_2\mathrm C_2\mathrm O_4\longrightarrow\mathrm{CO}+\mathrm{CO}_2+\mathrm H_2\mathrm O.}
$$

An alkaline absorption stage removes much of the [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), while [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) passes on. A generator, droplet trap, purifier and collecting vessel illustrate why preparation and purification are separate operations. The gas is only slightly soluble in [water](../../../../../water.md), so collection over water is possible in principle. Concentrated [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) acting on carbonaceous material can also give mixed carbon oxides and [sulfur dioxide](../../../../../sulfur-dioxide.md); that route requires additional purification and should not be presented as clean CO evolution without by-products.

The [relative density](../../../../../relative-density.md) of [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) against dry [air](../../../../../air.md) is approximately $28.01/28.97\simeq0.967$. It has no reliable warning odour. It burns with a blue flame when supplied with suitable [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md):

$$
\boxed{2\mathrm{CO}+\mathrm O_2\longrightarrow2\mathrm{CO}_2.}
$$

A CO sample itself does not ordinarily support combustion of a taper. Nor does it immediately give the usual limewater test of [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md); oxidation of the CO first gives a gas that does. This distinguishes fuel behaviour from the property of supporting combustion of another material.

Its reducing power is illustrated by

$$
\mathrm{CuO}+\mathrm{CO}\longrightarrow\mathrm{Cu}+\mathrm{CO}_2
$$

and by reduction of suitable iron oxides in metallurgy. The products reflect transfer of [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) from the oxide to [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md). Its ability to bind metal centres is also chemically important. In biological exposure, binding to [haemoglobin](../../../../../hemoglobin.md) and other effects disrupt oxygen delivery and use; it is a serious poison, not merely an innocuous replacement of atmospheric oxygen. This modern explanation must not turn an historical gas-preparation answer into a proposed exposure experiment.[Sources and the colourless, odourless character of carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide-poisoning-basics-by-the-centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention.md)[How carbon monoxide affects the body](../../../../../clinical-guidance-for-carbon-monoxide-poisoning-by-cdc.md)
