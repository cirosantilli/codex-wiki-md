**The identities of a, b, c and d remain undetermined.** The assertion that each tube holds a single [chemical substance](../../../../../chemical-substance-split.md) narrows the type of problem but gives no [chemical test](../../../../../chemical-test.md) result from which to choose a name.

A useful examination would give each specimen its own labelled record and preserve an untouched portion. Record physical appearance and behaviour in suitable [solvents](../../../../../solvent.md) before choosing tests. A soluble inorganic [salt](../../../../../salt-chemistry.md) calls for separate investigation of its [cation](../../../../../cation.md) and [anion](../../../../../anion.md); an insoluble material requires a justified preparation step before solution tests can be interpreted. A colour change, [precipitate](../../../../../precipitate.md) or evolved [gas](../../../../../gas.md) must be recorded as an observation before it is used as evidence for a composition. Fresh portions prevent one [reagent](../../../../../reagent.md) from introducing the ion sought by the next test.

In [qualitative chemical analysis](../../../../../qualitative-chemical-analysis.md), a discriminating result is followed by an independent confirmation and comparison with a [reagent blank](../../../../../reagent-blank.md) and a known reference. For example, the behaviour of a metal [hydroxide](../../../../../hydroxide.md) depends on both the [cation](../../../../../cation.md) and the chemical conditions; “a precipitate formed” is not a complete identification.[Metal hydroxides and negative-ion tests: colour, behaviour in excess reagent and separate anion tests](../../../../../testing-salts-for-anions-and-cations-by-the-royal-society-of-chemistry.md) The record supplied here contains none of those observations, so no particular reaction or identity can be assigned to any of the four letters.
