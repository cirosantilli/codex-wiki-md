**Neither the gas nor the conditions required to obtain it from g can be identified.** The word “fixed” does not supply a chemical formula, and the existence of a labelled solid does not establish that it is a [carbonate](../../../../../carbonate.md) or any other particular [chemical compound](../../../../../chemical-compound.md).

A defensible investigation would first characterise g and distinguish physical release of an absorbed [gas](../../../../../gas.md) from a [chemical reaction](../../../../../chemical-reaction-split.md) that produces a new [gas](../../../../../gas.md). Whether [heating](../../../../../heat-transfer.md), contact with a [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md), or a selected [reagent](../../../../../reagent.md) is relevant depends on that characterisation. An arbitrary [thermal decomposition](../../../../../thermal-decomposition.md) equation would therefore assume the very composition that is missing.

If an identified reaction produced a [gas](../../../../../gas.md), the apparatus would be chosen for its actual properties: a reaction vessel and delivery system, a means of distinguishing the product from initially enclosed [air](../../../../../air.md), and a compatible collection or detection method. Collection over [water](../../../../../water.md) would be unsuitable when dissolution or reaction with [water](../../../../../water.md) materially changes the product; a [gas syringe](../../../../../gas-syringe.md) is an alternative only when compatible with the substances and conditions. The gas would then require discriminating [chemical tests](../../../../../chemical-test.md), and the residue could help check the proposed reaction. No evolution, odour, combustion behaviour, [precipitation](../../../../../precipitation-chemistry.md) result or residue is reported for g, so none of those observations can be supplied as its answer.
