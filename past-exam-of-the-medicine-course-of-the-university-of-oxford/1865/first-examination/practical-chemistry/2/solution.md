**The presence or absence of [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) in e, and of [iron](../../../../../iron.md) or [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md) in f, remains unestablished.** The named substances are targets of the examination. They are not positive findings.

The period name “hydrocyanic acid” refers to aqueous [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md). “Magnesia” is the historical name for [magnesium oxide](../../../../../magnesium-oxide.md); examination of a [chemical solution](../../../../../solution-chemistry.md) for magnesia normally concerns dissolved [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md) compounds, rather than a claim that intact [magnesium oxide](../../../../../magnesium-oxide.md) particles are dissolved unchanged. The modern [ionic equations](../../../../../ionic-equation.md) below express test principles and are not observations made on f.

For e, the analytical task is to obtain a response attributable to [cyanide](../../../../../cyanide.md), distinguish it from responses of other constituents, and check the entire procedure against a [reagent blank](../../../../../reagent-blank.md) and a known reference. The distinction between free [cyanide](../../../../../cyanide.md) and [cyanide](../../../../../cyanide.md) held in a [coordination complex](../../../../../coordination-complex.md) matters: a test for one fraction need not measure the other. As a modern comparison, the [Cyanide in Waters and Extracts, Method 9014 by United States Environmental Protection Agency](../../../../../cyanide-in-waters-and-extracts-method-9014-by-united-states-environmental-protection-agency.md) provides [titrimetric analysis](../../../../../titration.md) and [spectrophotometric analysis](../../../../../spectrophotometric-analysis.md), with specified sample preparation, interferences and quality controls. This 2014 procedure illustrates how a result is validated; it is not being attributed to the 1865 candidate.[§§1–2: analytical scope and measurement principles; §§4 and 9: interferences and controls](../../../../../cyanide-in-waters-and-extracts-method-9014-by-united-states-environmental-protection-agency.md) No numerical [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md), positive colour or negative result can be extracted from the label e.

An odour is neither an acceptable identifying experiment nor a reliable negative test. [Hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) is volatile and highly toxic, and many people cannot detect its supposed characteristic smell. Any actual analysis belongs in a suitably contained laboratory method; uncontrolled acidification of a possible [cyanide](../../../../../cyanide.md) solution can release [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md).[Agent Characteristics: volatility, toxicity and unreliable odour warning](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide-emergency-response-information-by-niosh.md)[§5.2: hydrogen cyanide release on acidification](../../../../../cyanide-in-waters-and-extracts-method-9014-by-united-states-environmental-protection-agency.md)

For f, distinguish [iron(II)](../../../../../iron-ii.md) from [iron(III)](../../../../../iron-iii.md) when describing a test. On suitable separate portions, [hydroxide](../../../../../hydroxide.md) can produce a green [iron(II) hydroxide](../../../../../iron-ii-hydroxide.md) [precipitate](../../../../../precipitate.md) or a brown hydrated [iron(III) hydroxide](../../../../../iron-iii-hydroxide.md) [precipitate](../../../../../precipitate.md). [Iron(II)](../../../../../iron-ii.md) products can oxidise during handling, so the time at which a colour is observed matters. The simplified precipitation balances are:

$$
\mathrm{Fe^{2+}+2OH^-\longrightarrow Fe(OH)_2(s)},\qquad
\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+3OH^-\longrightarrow Fe(OH)_3(s)}.
$$

These equations describe possible chemical responses, not a choice between them for the missing f. Tests intended for a particular [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) cannot be treated as universal tests of every form of [iron](../../../../../iron.md).

Dissolved [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md) can give a white [magnesium hydroxide](../../../../../magnesium-hydroxide.md) [precipitate](../../../../../precipitate.md):

$$
\mathrm{Mg^{2+}+2OH^-\longrightarrow Mg(OH)_2(s)}.
$$

The white solid does not characteristically dissolve in excess [sodium hydroxide](../../../../../sodium-hydroxide.md) under the usual qualitative-test conditions. This differentiates it from some amphoteric [hydroxides](../../../../../hydroxide.md), but is not uniquely specific for [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md).[Metal hydroxides: iron(II), iron(III), magnesium and the distinction from aluminium in excess sodium hydroxide](../../../../../testing-salts-for-anions-and-cations-by-the-royal-society-of-chemistry.md) If [iron](../../../../../iron.md) and [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md) were together, a single mixed [precipitate](../../../../../precipitate.md) could obscure their separate behaviour. An analytical separation with controlled [pH](../../../../../ph.md), followed by confirmation on the separated fractions, would be needed; indiscriminately adding excess alkali is not proof of separation.[printed II & III-15–17: hydroxide concentration, magnesium interference and iron confirmation](../../../../../qualitative-analysis-of-metal-ions-in-solution-by-williams-college.md) The actual [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) of any [iron](../../../../../iron.md), the presence of [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md), and the outcome of such a separation all remain unknown.
