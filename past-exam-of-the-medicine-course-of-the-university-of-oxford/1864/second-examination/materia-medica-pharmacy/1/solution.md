**The 1864 preparations contain either crude [opium](../../../../../opium.md) or its aqueous [Extractum Opii](../../../../../extractum-opii.md); those quantities must be distinguished from a quantity of isolated [morphine](../../../../../morphine.md).** The following are the officinal [opium](../../../../../opium.md) preparations. Ratios for solids mean the [mass fraction](../../../../../mass-fraction.md) in the compounded preparation; liquid strengths specify [mass](../../../../../mass.md) per [volume](../../../../../volume.md) or the actual ingredients used.

- [Emplastrum Opii](../../../../../emplastrum-opii.md): powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) in resin plaster: **1 in 10**.
- [Enema Opii](../../../../../enema-opii.md): [opium tincture](../../../../../opium-tincture.md) 30 [minims](../../../../../minim-unit.md) with starch [decoction](../../../../../decoction.md) 2 [fluid ounces](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md). Thus the tincture is **1 volume to 32 volumes of vehicle**, before mixing.
- [Extractum Opii](../../../../../extractum-opii.md): aqueous [solvent extraction](../../../../../solvent-extraction.md) of 1 [avoirdupois pound](../../../../../avoirdupois-pound.md) of sliced [opium](../../../../../opium.md) with 6 [Imperial pints](../../../../../imperial-pint.md) of [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md), in three successive portions, followed by [evaporation](../../../../../evaporation.md). The prescribed starting quantities do not impose a fixed yield or a universal crude-drug-to-extract potency ratio.
- [Extractum Opii Liquidum](../../../../../extractum-opii-liquidum.md): [Extractum Opii](../../../../../extractum-opii.md) 1 [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) 17 [fluid ounces](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md), [rectified spirit](../../../../../rectified-spirit.md) 3 [fluid ounces](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md): **1 ounce of extract to the pint of liquid ingredients**.
- [Linimentum Opii](../../../../../linimentum-opii.md): equal [volumes](../../../../../volume.md) of [opium tincture](../../../../../opium-tincture.md) and [soap liniment](../../../../../soap-liniment.md): **half the tincture's nominal [opium](../../../../../opium.md) strength**.
- [Pilula Opii](../../../../../pilula-opii.md): powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) with hard [soap](../../../../../soap.md): **1 in 5** of the nominal pill ingredients.
- [Pilula Plumbi cum Opio](../../../../../pilula-plumbi-cum-opio.md): powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) with [lead acetate](../../../../../lead-acetate.md) and confection of roses: **1 in 8**.
- [Pulvis Cretae Aromaticus cum Opio](../../../../../pulvis-cretae-aromaticus-cum-opio.md): powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) with aromatic chalk powder: **1 in 40**.
- [Pulvis Ipecacuanhae cum Opio](../../../../../pulvis-ipecacuanhae-cum-opio.md): powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) with [ipecacuanha](../../../../../ipecacuanha.md) and [potassium sulfate](../../../../../potassium-sulfate.md): **1 in 10**.
- [Pulvis Kino cum Opio](../../../../../pulvis-kino-cum-opio.md): powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) with [kino](../../../../../kino.md) and [cinnamon](../../../../../cinnamon.md): **1 in 20**.

[Papaverceae, part 2, British formulas outside the bracketed American additions; underlying revision dated May 1864](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

- [Tinctura Camphorae cum Opio](../../../../../tinctura-camphorae-cum-opio.md): [opium](../../../../../opium.md) 40 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md) to 1 [Imperial pint](../../../../../imperial-pint.md) of [proof spirit](../../../../../proof-spirit.md), with [benzoic acid](../../../../../benzoic-acid.md), [camphor](../../../../../camphor.md) and [anise oil](../../../../../anise-oil.md): **2 grains of [opium](../../../../../opium.md) per [fluid ounce](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md)**.
- [Opium tincture](../../../../../opium-tincture.md), _Tinctura Opii_: powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) 1½ [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md) to 1 [Imperial pint](../../../../../imperial-pint.md) of [proof spirit](../../../../../proof-spirit.md). Nominally this is **32.8125 grains per fluid ounce**, or approximately 1 [grain](../../../../../grain-unit.md) per 14.63 [minims](../../../../../minim-unit.md).
- [Trochisci Opii](../../../../../trochisci-opii.md): 72 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md) of [Extractum Opii](../../../../../extractum-opii.md) in 720 lozenges: **one tenth of a grain of extract per lozenge**.
- [Unguentum Gallae cum Opio](../../../../../unguentum-gallae-cum-opio.md): powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) 32 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md) added to 1 [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md) of gall ointment. The actual nominal total is $437.5+32=469.5$ [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md), giving **32/469.5 by mass, approximately 1 in 14.67**; “32 grains to the ounce of ointment taken” is not 32 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md) per final ounce.
- [Vinum Opii](../../../../../vinum-opii.md): powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) 1½ [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), [sherry](../../../../../sherry.md) 1 [Imperial pint](../../../../../imperial-pint.md): **the tincture's nominal [opium](../../../../../opium.md) proportion**, in a different solvent. This formula uses crude [opium](../../../../../opium.md), not [Extractum Opii](../../../../../extractum-opii.md).

[Papaverceae, part 3, British tincture, lozenge, ointment and wine formulas](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

The arithmetic uses the new [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md) of 437.5 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md), not the old apothecaries' ounce of 480 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md). These are historical formulation proportions, not modern assays of [morphine](../../../../../morphine.md) delivered after [solvent extraction](../../../../../solvent-extraction.md). [Confectio Opii](../../../../../confectio-opii.md) was omitted in 1864, and this edition's [Vinum Opii](../../../../../vinum-opii.md) contains no spices. The separate [morphine hydrochloride](../../../../../morphine-hydrochloride.md) preparations—[Liquor Morphiae Hydrochloratis](../../../../../liquor-morphiae-hydrochloratis.md), [Suppositoria Morphiae](../../../../../suppositoria-morphiae.md), [Trochisci Morphiae](../../../../../trochisci-morphiae.md), and [Trochisci Morphiae et Ipecacuanhae](../../../../../trochisci-morphiae-et-ipecacuanhae.md)—contain an isolated [alkaloid](../../../../../alkaloid.md) salt rather than crude [opium](../../../../../opium.md). [pp. 11–12, 39 and 75–76](../../../../../the-prescriber-s-analysis-of-the-british-pharmacopoeia-by-john-birkbeck-nevins-1864.md)
