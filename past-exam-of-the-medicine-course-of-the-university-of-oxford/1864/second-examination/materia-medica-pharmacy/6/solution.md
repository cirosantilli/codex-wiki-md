<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**The 1864 iron list includes metallic, ferrous, ferric and compound preparations; its wet [Ferri Peroxidum Hydratum](../../../../../ferri-peroxidum-hydratum.md) was used historically as an [antidote](../../../../../antidote.md) to [arsenic poisoning](../../../../../arsenic-poisoning.md).** It must be distinguished from dried [Ferri Peroxidum](../../../../../ferri-peroxidum.md) and from later editions' reassignment of similar Latin names.

The officinal articles and preparations are:

- metallic forms: [iron wire](../../../../../iron-wire.md), _Ferrum_, and [Ferrum Redactum](../../../../../ferrum-redactum.md);
- simple salts and oxides: [Ferri Arsenias](../../../../../ferri-arsenias.md), [Ferri Carbonas Saccharata](../../../../../ferri-carbonas-saccharata.md), [Ferri Iodidum](../../../../../ferri-iodidum.md), [Ferri Oxidum Magneticum](../../../../../ferri-oxidum-magneticum.md), [Ferri Peroxidum](../../../../../ferri-peroxidum.md), [Ferri Peroxidum Hydratum](../../../../../ferri-peroxidum-hydratum.md), [Ferri Phosphas](../../../../../ferri-phosphas.md), [Ferri Sulphas](../../../../../ferri-sulphas.md), [Ferri Sulphas Exsiccata](../../../../../ferri-sulphas-exsiccata.md) and [Ferri Sulphas Granulata](../../../../../ferri-sulphas-granulata.md);
- complex salts: [Ferri et Ammoniae Citras](../../../../../ferri-et-ammoniae-citras.md), [Ferri et Quiniae Citras](../../../../../ferri-et-quiniae-citras.md) and [Ferrum Tartaratum](../../../../../ferrum-tartaratum.md);
- solutions: [Liquor Ferri Perchloridi](../../../../../liquor-ferri-perchloridi.md), [Liquor Ferri Pernitratis](../../../../../liquor-ferri-pernitratis.md) and the preparative [Liquor Ferri Persulphatis](../../../../../liquor-ferri-persulphatis.md);
- compounded forms: [Mistura Ferri Composita](../../../../../mistura-ferri-composita.md), [Pilula Ferri Carbonatis](../../../../../pilula-ferri-carbonatis.md), [Pilula Ferri Iodidi](../../../../../pilula-ferri-iodidi.md), [Syrupus Ferri Iodidi](../../../../../syrupus-ferri-iodidi.md), [Syrupus Ferri Phosphatis](../../../../../syrupus-ferri-phosphatis.md), [Tinctura Ferri Perchloridi](../../../../../tinctura-ferri-perchloridi.md) and [Vinum Ferri](../../../../../vinum-ferri.md).

[Iron and successive British iron monographs; Solution of Persulphate of Iron, Appendix A; British compounds distinguished from American additions and older London preparations](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md) [pp. 43–45, iron additions, omissions and names; pp. 55, 63, 67 and 72, compound forms](../../../../../the-prescriber-s-analysis-of-the-british-pharmacopoeia-by-john-birkbeck-nevins-1864.md)

**Prepare the hydrated peroxide by inducing [precipitation](../../../../../precipitation-chemistry.md) from the ferric solution with [sodium hydroxide](../../../../../sodium-hydroxide.md), collecting and washing the wet precipitate, and preserving it without drying in a covered vessel.** The starting solution is [Liquor Ferri Persulphatis](../../../../../liquor-ferri-persulphatis.md), whose “persulphate” here means ferric sulfate, not a modern peroxydisulfate salt. Add the appropriately diluted ferric solution to the alkaline liquor with stirring, allow the [precipitate](../../../../../precipitate.md) to settle, collect it on a calico [filter](../../../../../filter-set-theory.md), and wash with [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) until the washings no longer give a [precipitate](../../../../../precipitate.md) with [barium chloride](../../../../../barium-chloride.md). This tests removal of soluble [sulfate](../../../../../sulfate.md). The resulting fresh, hydrated material is kept wet; drying gives the separate dried preparation. [Ferri Peroxidum Hydratum; Ferri Peroxidum; Solution of Persulphate of Iron](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

In modern chemical notation the precipitation can be idealised as

$$
\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+3OH^-\longrightarrow Fe(OH)_3(s)}.
$$

The fresh solid is a hydrated ferric oxide material with variable water content; the equation is not a claim that every pharmaceutical batch is one pure, crystalline [iron hydroxide](../../../../../iron-hydroxide.md). Its historical use depended on binding dissolved [arsenic](../../../../../arsenic.md) into less available material in the digestive tract, especially the freshly precipitated, wet state. It was not a universal antidote to every metal or every stage of absorbed [arsenic poisoning](../../../../../arsenic-poisoning.md). Contemporary discussion explicitly identified the new hydrated peroxide as an arsenic antidote. [6 February 1864, p. 65, iron preparations and freshly precipitated antidote](../../../../../the-british-pharmacopoeia-in-chemical-news-1864.md) [§1231, preparation, washings tested with barium chloride, preservation without drying and recent preparation](../../../../../a-manual-of-practical-therapeutics-by-edward-john-waring-1865.md)

Modern treatment of significant [arsenic poisoning](../../../../../arsenic-poisoning.md) concerns systemic toxicity and may involve specialist-directed [chelation therapy](../../../../../chelation-therapy.md); a nineteenth-century ferric paste is not the modern treatment standard.
