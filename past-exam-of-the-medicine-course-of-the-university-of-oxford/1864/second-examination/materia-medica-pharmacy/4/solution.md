<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**The compositions must be specified ingredient by ingredient; the two alkaloidal solutions contain 4 grains per nominal fluid ounce, but one contains a [morphine](../../../../../morphine.md) salt and the other [strychnine](../../../../../strychnine.md).**

[Pulvis Jalapae Compositus](../../../../../pulvis-jalapae-compositus.md) contains powdered [jalap](../../../../../jalap.md) 5 [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), [potassium hydrogen tartrate](../../../../../potassium-hydrogen-tartrate.md) 9 [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), and powdered [ginger](../../../../../ginger.md) 1 [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md). Thus the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) proportions are **5:9:1**, and [jalap](../../../../../jalap.md) supplies one third of the powder's [mass](../../../../../mass.md). This is a compound [purgative](../../../../../purgative.md), not pure [jalap resin](../../../../../jalap-resin.md). [Convolvulaceae, Jalapa, compound powder](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

[Pilula Scillae Composita](../../../../../pilula-scillae-composita.md) contains powdered [squill](../../../../../squill.md) 1¼ [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), powdered [ginger](../../../../../ginger.md), [ammoniacum](../../../../../ammoniacum.md) and hard [soap](../../../../../soap.md) 1 [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md) each, with [treacle](../../../../../treacle.md) 2 [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), or sufficient to make a pill mass. Using the specified 2 [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md) of [treacle](../../../../../treacle.md), the whole is 6¼ [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md) and contains **one fifth [squill](../../../../../squill.md) by mass**. The allowance of sufficient [treacle](../../../../../treacle.md) makes consistency a compounding requirement, not a claim that every moist pill batch has an analytically exact 20% [squill](../../../../../squill.md) content. [Liliaceae, British compound squill pill, distinguished from bracketed American formula](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

[Liquor Morphiae Hydrochloratis](../../../../../liquor-morphiae-hydrochloratis.md) contains [morphine hydrochloride](../../../../../morphine-hydrochloride.md) 4 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md), dilute [hydrochloric acid](../../../../../hydrochloric-acid.md) 8 [minims](../../../../../minim-unit.md), [rectified spirit](../../../../../rectified-spirit.md) 2 [fluid drachms](../../../../../fluid-drachm.md) and [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) 6 [fluid drachms](../../../../../fluid-drachm.md). Its conventional strength is **½ grain of [morphine hydrochloride](../../../../../morphine-hydrochloride.md) per fluid drachm**, not ½ [grain](../../../../../grain-unit.md) of free [morphine](../../../../../morphine.md). The small quantity of [hydrochloric acid](../../../../../hydrochloric-acid.md) maintains an acid solution. [Papaverceae, part 3, Liquor Morphiae Hydrochloratis](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

[Liquor Strychniae](../../../../../liquor-strychniae.md) contains [strychnine](../../../../../strychnine.md) 4 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md), dilute [hydrochloric acid](../../../../../hydrochloric-acid.md) 6 [minims](../../../../../minim-unit.md), [rectified spirit](../../../../../rectified-spirit.md) 2 [fluid drachms](../../../../../fluid-drachm.md) and [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) 6 [fluid drachms](../../../../../fluid-drachm.md). Dissolve the [strychnine](../../../../../strychnine.md) in the acid mixed with part of the [water](../../../../../water.md), using the prescribed assistance of heat, then add the spirit and remaining [water](../../../../../water.md). Its conventional strength is **½ grain of [strychnine](../../../../../strychnine.md) per fluid drachm**. Acid promotes [solubility](../../../../../solubility.md) by forming a soluble [salt](../../../../../salt-chemistry.md) of the [alkaloid](../../../../../alkaloid.md). [Loganiaceae, Liquor Strychniae](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

For the last two solutions, the stated historical strengths use the nominal 8 [fluid drachms](../../../../../fluid-drachm.md) of spirit and [water](../../../../../water.md). The extra few [minims](../../../../../minim-unit.md) of dilute acid must still appear in the actual recipe; treating all ingredient [volumes](../../../../../volume.md) as perfectly additive would not give an exact modern analytical [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md).
