<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

**[Volumetric analysis](../../../../../volumetric-analysis.md) determines an amount of substance from the measured volume of a reagent of known strength that reacts with it in a known proportion.** A measured [aliquot](../../../../../aliquot.md) is placed in a suitable vessel, the [standard solution](../../../../../standard-solution.md) is delivered from a [burette](../../../../../burette.md), and the volume required to reach the chosen [titration endpoint](../../../../../titration-endpoint.md) is read. A [pipette](../../../../../pipette.md) measures the [aliquot](../../../../../aliquot.md); a [volumetric flask](../../../../../volumetric-flask.md) prepares or dilutes the [standard solution](../../../../../standard-solution.md).

For a reaction $aA+bB\longrightarrow\text{products}$, if the [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) of the [titrant](../../../../../titrant.md) $B$ is $c_B$ and the corrected volume delivered is $V_B$, then

$$
\boxed{n_A=\frac{a}{b}c_BV_B}.
$$

With volume in [litres](../../../../../litre.md) and concentration in [moles](../../../../../mole-unit.md) per litre, the answer is an amount in [moles](../../../../../mole-unit.md). The corresponding [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is $m_A=M_A n_A$, using the appropriate [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md). For an unknown solution of aliquot volume $V_A$,

$$
\boxed{c_A=\frac{a\,c_BV_B}{b\,V_A}}.
$$

These are modern symbols for the same proportional reasoning that a nineteenth-century analyst could express with equivalent weights and a specified standard solution.

[titration; volumetric titration, terms T06387 and 09055](../../../../../iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry.md)

A suitable reaction is sufficiently rapid, complete and selective, with a recognisable endpoint. In an [acid-base titration](../../../../../acid-base-titration.md), an [indicator](../../../../../indicator-function.md) can change colour near [neutralization](../../../../../neutralization.md); other reactions use [precipitation](../../../../../precipitation-chemistry.md), [redox](../../../../../redox.md) or a colour intrinsic to a reagent. The observed [titration endpoint](../../../../../titration-endpoint.md) must represent the required [equivalence point](../../../../../equivalence-point.md) closely enough. The reagent's nominal preparation alone is insufficient if its [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) changes in storage; [standardisation of a solution](../../../../../standardisation-of-a-solution.md), a [reagent blank](../../../../../reagent-blank.md) where needed, and correct reading of the [meniscus](../../../../../meniscus-liquid.md) control [systematic error](../../../../../systematic-error.md). A [titration](../../../../../titration.md) measures the intended [analyte](../../../../../analyte.md) only insofar as interfering substances have been excluded or accounted for.
