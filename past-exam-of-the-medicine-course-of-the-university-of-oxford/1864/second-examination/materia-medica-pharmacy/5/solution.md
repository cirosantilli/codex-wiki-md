↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**The chief historical examples are [rhubarb](../../../../../rhubarb.md), [senna](../../../../../senna.md), [santonin](../../../../../santonin.md) and [logwood](../../../../../logwood.md) for [urine](../../../../../urine.md); [iron preparations](../../../../../iron-preparation.md), [bismuth preparations](../../../../../bismuth-preparation.md) and [charcoal](../../../../../charcoal.md) for darkening the [faeces](../../../../../faeces.md).** There are two different processes: a substance absorbed and subsequently excreted can colour a secretion, while material remaining in the [intestinal lumen](../../../../../intestinal-lumen.md) can colour the stools without demonstrating systemic [drug absorption](../../../../../drug-absorption.md).

- [Rhubarb](../../../../../rhubarb.md) colours [urine](../../../../../urine.md) yellow to yellow-brown; the colour becomes redder in an [alkaline solution](../../../../../alkaline-solution.md). The contemporary account also reports coloration of [sweat](../../../../../sweat.md) and [breast milk](../../../../../breast-milk.md). This is a historical observation about passage of colouring matter, not a claim that every constituent passes unchanged into every secretion. [Labiatae, continued, Rheum: colouring constituent, alkaline solutions, and coloration of urine, perspiration and milk](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)
- [Senna](../../../../../senna.md) can give [urine](../../../../../urine.md) a yellow-brown or red-brown colour. The effect is still recognised in current medicinal information. [Side effects, coloration of urine](../../../../../senna-by-the-nhs.md)
- [Santonin](../../../../../santonin.md), used historically as an [anthelmintic](../../../../../anthelmintic.md), can make [urine](../../../../../urine.md) conspicuously yellow. This must be distinguished from the drug's separate effect on colour vision: a yellow-looking visual field is not itself excretion of a yellow pigment. [§2416, physiological effects and yellow urine](../../../../../a-manual-of-practical-therapeutics-by-edward-john-waring-1865.md)
- [Logwood](../../../../../logwood.md) can give [urine](../../../../../urine.md) a pink or reddish colour, particularly when it is an [alkaline solution](../../../../../alkaline-solution.md); the historical description notes that addition of [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) brings out the colour in a previously acid specimen. [Cisalpinae, Haematoxylum, urine coloration](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)
- [Iron preparations](../../../../../iron-preparation.md) can darken [faeces](../../../../../faeces.md); [bismuth preparations](../../../../../bismuth-preparation.md) also cause dark or black stools. In these examples the result need not be a pre-existing vegetable dye simply passing unchanged through the body. Current information confirms black stools with oral [iron](../../../../../iron.md) and with the modern [bismuth](../../../../../bismuth.md) medicine [bismuth subsalicylate](../../../../../bismuth-subsalicylate.md); the latter is corroboration of the colour effect, not an identification of that modern formulation with the 1864 bismuth articles. [Dark stools and iron tablets](../../../../../bleeding-from-the-bottom-by-the-nhs.md) [Side effects: black stools and black tongue](../../../../../pepto-bismol-by-the-nhs.md)
- [Charcoal](../../../../../charcoal.md) supplies a black material directly to the bowel contents. Its appearance in the stools does not require its [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) and subsequent [renal excretion](../../../../../renal-excretion.md).

**Colour alone does not identify either the drug or a disease.** A medicinal pigment can resemble [blood](../../../../../blood.md) or a [bile pigment](../../../../../bile-pigment.md); [pH](../../../../../ph.md), timing after administration and appropriate chemical or microscopic examination distinguish possibilities. Conversely, a history of taking a colouring medicine does not prove that an observed specimen contains no [blood](../../../../../blood.md). These examples describe pharmacological and historical observations, not results from an unseen specimen.
