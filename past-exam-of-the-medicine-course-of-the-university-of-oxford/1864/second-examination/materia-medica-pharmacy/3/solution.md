↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**The seven named [mixtures](../../../../../mixture-split.md) of the 1864 [British Pharmacopoeia](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia.md) are [Mistura Ammoniaci](../../../../../mistura-ammoniaci.md), [Mistura Amygdalae](../../../../../mistura-amygdalae.md), [Mistura Creasoti](../../../../../mistura-creasoti.md), [Mistura Cretae](../../../../../mistura-cretae.md), [Mistura Ferri Composita](../../../../../mistura-ferri-composita.md), [Mistura Guaiaci](../../../../../mistura-guaiaci.md) and [Mistura Scammonii](../../../../../mistura-scammonii.md).** Their compositions are as follows; [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md) are used for solids and [fluid ounces](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md) for liquids.

- [Mistura Ammoniaci](../../../../../mistura-ammoniaci.md): [ammoniacum](../../../../../ammoniacum.md) ¼ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) 8 [fluid ounces](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md), rubbed together and strained. Its own [gum](../../../../../gum.md) helps disperse its [resin](../../../../../resin.md); [ammoniacum](../../../../../ammoniacum.md) is not [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md). [Umbelliferae, part 2, Ammoniacum](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)
- [Mistura Amygdalae](../../../../../mistura-amygdalae.md): compound almond powder 2½ [avoirdupois ounces](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) 1 [Imperial pint](../../../../../imperial-pint.md). The compound powder is made from sweet [almonds](../../../../../almond.md) 8 parts, [sugar](../../../../../sugar.md) 4 parts and [gum arabic](../../../../../gum-arabic.md) 1 part by [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Trituration produces an [emulsion](../../../../../emulsion.md). [Rosaceae, continued, almond preparations](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)
- [Mistura Creasoti](../../../../../mistura-creasoti.md): [creosote](../../../../../creosote.md) 16 [minims](../../../../../minim-unit.md), [glacial acetic acid](../../../../../glacial-acetic-acid.md) 16 [minims](../../../../../minim-unit.md), [Spiritus Juniperi](../../../../../spiritus-juniperi.md) ½ [fluid drachm](../../../../../fluid-drachm.md), [syrup](../../../../../syrup.md) 1 [fluid ounce](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md), [distilled water](../../../../../distilled-water.md) 15 [fluid ounces](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md). Here the spirit has the particularly strong 1864 [juniper oil](../../../../../juniper-oil.md) content. [Hydrocarbons from the destruction of wood by fire, Mistura Creosoti](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)
- [Mistura Cretae](../../../../../mistura-cretae.md): prepared [chalk](../../../../../chalk.md) and powdered [gum arabic](../../../../../gum-arabic.md), ¼ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md) each; [syrup](../../../../../syrup.md) ½ [fluid ounce](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md); [cinnamon water](../../../../../cinnamon-water.md) 7½ [fluid ounces](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md). The [chalk](../../../../../chalk.md) is suspended, not dissolved. [Calcis Carbonas Praecipitata, Creta Praeparata, British mixture](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)
- [Mistura Ferri Composita](../../../../../mistura-ferri-composita.md): powdered [myrrh](../../../../../myrrh.md) 60 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md), [potassium carbonate](../../../../../potassium-carbonate.md) 25 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md), [rose water](../../../../../rose-water.md) 8 [fluid ounces](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md), [ferrous sulfate](../../../../../ferrous-sulfate.md) 30 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md), [Spiritus Myristicae](../../../../../spiritus-myristicae.md) 1 [fluid drachm](../../../../../fluid-drachm.md), [sugar](../../../../../sugar.md) 60 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md). Triturate the [myrrh](../../../../../myrrh.md) with the spirit and [potassium carbonate](../../../../../potassium-carbonate.md), then add the [rose water](../../../../../rose-water.md) with [sugar](../../../../../sugar.md), followed by [ferrous sulfate](../../../../../ferrous-sulfate.md); keep the resulting preparation stoppered. Formation of [ferrous carbonate](../../../../../ferrous-carbonate.md) and its subsequent [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) explain why the order and storage matter. [Ferri Carbonas Saccharata, British Mistura Ferri Composita formula](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)
- [Mistura Guaiaci](../../../../../mistura-guaiaci.md): powdered [guaiacum resin](../../../../../guaiacum-resin.md) ½ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), [sugar](../../../../../sugar.md) ½ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), powdered [gum arabic](../../../../../gum-arabic.md) ¼ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../../../avoirdupois-ounce.md), [cinnamon water](../../../../../cinnamon-water.md) 1 [Imperial pint](../../../../../imperial-pint.md). The [gum](../../../../../gum.md) assists dispersion of the poorly water-soluble [resin](../../../../../resin.md). [Zygophyllaceae, resin preparations](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)
- [Mistura Scammonii](../../../../../mistura-scammonii.md): [scammony resin](../../../../../scammony-resin.md) 4 [grains](../../../../../grain-unit.md) with unskimmed [milk](../../../../../milk.md) 2 [fluid ounces](../../../../../fluid-ounce.md), triturated into a uniform dispersion. [Convolvulaceae, Scammonium, Mistura Scammonii](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

The category is an official preparation name, not every possible bedside combination of medicines. Camphor water and acacia [mucilage](../../../../../mucilage.md) are not additional entries under _Mistura_: the 1864 names are _Aqua Camphorae_ and _Mucilago Acaciae_. [pp. 30 and 52–53, mixture names and alterations](../../../../../the-prescriber-s-analysis-of-the-british-pharmacopoeia-by-john-birkbeck-nevins-1864.md)
