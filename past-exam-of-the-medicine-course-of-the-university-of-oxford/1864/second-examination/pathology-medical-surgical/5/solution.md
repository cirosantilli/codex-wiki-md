↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**Urinary [albumin](../../../../../albumin.md), [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) and [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md) represent different abnormalities, and none alone identifies one disease.** A fresh, appropriately collected specimen and confirmation of the constituent are essential before interpreting it.

Persistent [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md) commonly reflects increased passage of [albumin](../../../../../albumin.md) across an injured [glomerular filtration barrier](../../../../../glomerular-filtration-barrier.md). Examples include [glomerulonephritis](../../../../../glomerulonephritis.md), [diabetic kidney disease](../../../../../diabetic-kidney-disease.md) and other protein-losing glomerular disorders. Renal vascular congestion and severe systemic illness can also increase excretion. Transient [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md) can accompany fever, strenuous exercise or a [urinary tract infection](../../../../../urinary-tract-infection.md); an upright-posture-related pattern may disappear in an early-morning specimen. Blood or inflammatory material added downstream can contribute protein, so a protein-positive specimen need not mean that every protein molecule crossed a diseased [glomerulus](../../../../../glomerulus.md).[Causes: temporary and persistent albuminuria; importance of repeat testing](../../../../../albuminuria-by-the-national-kidney-foundation.md)

[Albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md) should be distinguished from the broader term [proteinuria](../../../../../proteinuria.md). Reduced tubular recovery and overproduction of other small proteins can produce different urinary protein patterns. A modern urine [albumin](../../../../../albumin.md)-to-[creatinine](../../../../../creatinine.md) measurement helps quantify albumin loss and reduce the effect of variable dilution.[Detection: urine albumin measurement and albumin-to-creatinine ratio](../../../../../albuminuria-albumin-in-the-urine-by-niddk.md) The historical “albumen” category does not imply that every old heat or precipitation test was chemically specific for [albumin](../../../../../albumin.md).

[Glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md) results either from an excessive filtered [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) load or reduced renal recovery. [Diabetes](../../../../../diabetes.md) with [hyperglycaemia](../../../../../hyperglycaemia.md) is the major systemic example. Transient hyperglycaemic states may also cause it; [renal glycosuria](../../../../../renal-glycosuria.md), pregnancy-related changes and proximal tubular dysfunction can produce [glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md) without sustained diabetic [hyperglycaemia](../../../../../hyperglycaemia.md). Modern drugs that deliberately reduce renal [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) recovery provide another mechanism, but should not be attributed to 1864 practice.[Definition and etiology: glucose without hyperglycaemia; isolated or associated tubular defects](../../../../../renal-glucosuria-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md) [Chapter 139, Basic Science: excessive filtered load, diminished tubular recovery and pregnancy](../../../../../glucosuria-in-clinical-methods.md) Urinary sugar is therefore a reason to examine [blood glucose](../../../../../blood-sugar.md), not proof of one diabetes subtype. Historical reducing-sugar reactions also require care because chemical reduction is not uniquely specific to [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md).

“Pus globules” correspond chiefly to [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md), especially [neutrophils](../../../../../neutrophil.md). [Pyuria](../../../../../pyuria.md) occurs with [cystitis](../../../../../cystitis.md), [pyelonephritis](../../../../../pyelonephritis.md), urethral or prostatic infection and suppuration draining into the urinary tract. It can also occur with stones, [urinary tuberculosis](../../../../../urinary-tuberculosis.md), [interstitial nephritis](../../../../../interstitial-nephritis.md) and other inflammatory disorders, or through contamination by genital secretions. Previous antimicrobial treatment or organisms not recovered by a routine culture can leave apparently sterile [pyuria](../../../../../pyuria.md).[Urine testing: leukocytes, casts, noninfective causes and contamination](../../../../../bacterial-urinary-tract-infections-by-merck-manual.md)[§5.13, Pyuria: sterile pyuria and alternative causes](../../../../../living-donor-kidney-transplantation-guidelines-by-the-british-transplantation-society-and-renal-association-2011.md)

A wet sediment examined with a [microscope](../../../../../microscope.md) can show [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md), [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) and [urinary casts](../../../../../urinary-cast.md); [urine culture](../../../../../urine-culture.md) addresses a different question about detectable organisms. White-cell casts suggest inflammation within the kidney rather than simply pus added in the bladder, but are not unique to bacterial [pyelonephritis](../../../../../pyelonephritis.md). **“Pus in urine” neither localises every lesion nor proves an ordinary bacterial infection.**
