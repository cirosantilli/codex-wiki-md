**[Typhus](../../../../../typhus.md) and [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md) are distinct infections, despite their overlapping fever, prostration and delirium.** In the British historical comparison, typhus chiefly means epidemic louse-borne [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md), not every disease now called a typhus fever.

Classical [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md) often begins relatively abruptly with severe headache, fever, prostration and a conspicuously ill mental state. Its eruption is commonly a more extensive dusky maculopapular rash on the [anatomical trunk](../../../../../anatomical-trunk.md) and limbs and may become petechial. Crowded conditions and infestation with body lice support the epidemiological distinction. Modern knowledge identifies [Rickettsia prowazekii](../../../../../rickettsia-prowazekii.md) and transmission involving infected louse material. Injury to small-vessel [endothelium](../../../../../endothelium.md) helps explain the widespread illness; it is not chiefly a local ulcerating disease of the ileal lymphoid tissue.[Transmission; clinical characteristics; rash and small-vessel injury](../../../../../clinical-overview-of-epidemic-typhus-by-cdc.md)

[Typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md) more often develops insidiously, with persistent fever, headache and progressive weakness, together with abdominal discomfort, distension and either constipation or [diarrhoea](../../../../../diarrhoea.md). Sparse, transient rose spots may occur on the [anatomical trunk](../../../../../anatomical-trunk.md). Splenic enlargement and intestinal complications are important. The characteristic pathological process involves intestinal lymphoid tissue, especially [Peyer's patches](../../../../../peyer-s-patch.md) in the terminal [ileum](../../../../../ileum.md), with enlargement, [necrosis](../../../../../necrosis.md) and ulceration. Severe ulcers can bleed or perforate.[Pathophysiology and histopathology: intestinal lymphoid lesions and complications](../../../../../typhoid-fever-in-statpearls.md)

Modern causation separates [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md), due to [Salmonella Typhi](../../../../../salmonella-typhi.md), from louse-borne [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md). [Typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md) spreads through food or water contaminated by human excreta, including material from carriers; it is not acquired merely because an air space is crowded. A prolonged course, abdominal findings and intestinal haemorrhage favour it, whereas abrupt severe illness with a widespread dusky rash and louse exposure favour [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md).[Infectious agent; transmission; clinical presentation and complications](../../../../../typhoid-and-paratyphoid-fever-in-the-cdc-yellow-book.md)

Neither the rash nor the bowel habit is invariable, and the old contrast between an approximately two-week typhus and a longer enteric illness is a tendency, not a diagnostic clock. An absent rose spot does not exclude [typhoid fever](../../../../../typhoid-fever.md), and an absent rash does not exclude [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md). Modern confirmation uses organism-appropriate investigations, including [blood culture](../../../../../blood-culture.md) for enteric fever and appropriate serological or molecular testing for [typhus](../../../../../typhus.md). The important historical achievement is the separation of the syndromes; the later microbiological explanation should not be projected into every 1864 diagnostic account.
