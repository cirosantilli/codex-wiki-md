**Abdominal [hernias](../../../../../hernia.md) are classified primarily by the opening through which tissue protrudes; groin and scrotal swellings have a substantially wider differential diagnosis.** Reducibility, obstruction and strangulation describe the state of a [hernia](../../../../../hernia.md), rather than different anatomical sites.

The principal external varieties are [indirect inguinal hernia](../../../../../indirect-inguinal-hernia.md) and [direct inguinal hernia](../../../../../direct-inguinal-hernia.md), [femoral hernia](../../../../../femoral-hernia.md), [umbilical hernia](../../../../../umbilical-hernia.md) and other anterior abdominal-wall hernias, including [epigastric hernia](../../../../../epigastric-hernia.md) and [incisional hernia](../../../../../incisional-hernia.md). An [indirect inguinal hernia](../../../../../indirect-inguinal-hernia.md) enters through the deep inguinal ring lateral to the inferior epigastric vessels and can follow the canal into the [scrotum](../../../../../scrotum.md); a [direct inguinal hernia](../../../../../direct-inguinal-hernia.md) passes through the posterior wall medial to those vessels. A [femoral hernia](../../../../../femoral-hernia.md) passes through the femoral canal below the [inguinal ligament](../../../../../inguinal-ligament.md), rather than along the inguinal canal.[Types of hernia: inguinal, femoral, incisional, ventral, umbilical and hiatal](../../../../../surgical-mesh-used-for-hernia-repair-by-the-fda.md)

Less common wall or pelvic varieties include [lateral ventral hernia](../../../../../lateral-ventral-hernia.md), [lumbar hernia](../../../../../lumbar-hernia.md), [obturator hernia](../../../../../obturator-hernia.md), [sciatic hernia](../../../../../sciatic-hernia.md) and [perineal hernia](../../../../../perineal-hernia.md). [Diaphragmatic hernia](../../../../../diaphragmatic-hernia.md) defects allow abdominal contents into the thorax. [Internal hernias](../../../../../internal-hernia.md) can pass through a mesenteric or peritoneal aperture while remaining within the abdominal cavity, including paraduodenal recesses or the epiploic foramen. Contents may be [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md), [omentum](../../../../../omentum.md) or other viscera; a developmental route and an acquired wall weakness can yield different forms at the same broad site.[Anatomical varieties and distinction of inguinal and femoral defects](../../../../../hernias-of-the-abdominal-wall-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)[Classification by aperture and internal location](../../../../../internal-hernias-a-difficult-diagnostic-challenge-review-of-ct-signs-and-clinical-findings.md)

In the groin, alternatives to an inguinoscrotal [hernia](../../../../../hernia.md) include enlarged or inflamed [lymph nodes](../../../../../lymph-node.md), an [abscess](../../../../../abscess.md), [lipoma](../../../../../lipoma.md) of the [spermatic cord](../../../../../spermatic-cord.md) or other soft tissue, a retained or undescended [testis](../../../../../testis.md), a [hydrocele](../../../../../hydrocele.md) of the cord, a [saphena varix](../../../../../saphena-varix.md), and a [femoral aneurysm](../../../../../femoral-aneurysm.md) or pseudoaneurysm. A [femoral hernia](../../../../../femoral-hernia.md) is also an important separate diagnosis. A fluid collection along a persistent female inguinal peritoneal process can produce a corresponding inguinal swelling. These lesions are not interchangeable merely because they lie near the inguinal opening.[Differential diagnosis of groin swelling](../../../../../inguinal-hernias-diagnosis-and-management-by-the-american-academy-of-family-physicians.md)

In the [scrotum](../../../../../scrotum.md), consider [hydrocele](../../../../../hydrocele.md), [haematocele](../../../../../haematocele.md), [spermatocele](../../../../../spermatocele.md) or an epididymal cyst, [varicocele](../../../../../varicocele.md), [epididymitis](../../../../../epididymitis.md), [orchitis](../../../../../orchitis.md), [abscess](../../../../../abscess.md), traumatic swelling and a [testicular tumour](../../../../../testicular-tumour.md). Torsion of the [testis](../../../../../testis.md) or its appendages can cause acute painful swelling. General [oedema](../../../../../oedema.md) or [lymphoedema](../../../../../lymphoedema.md) can enlarge the scrotal wall diffusely rather than create one discrete lump.[Etiology: hydrocele, varicocele, cystic masses, haematocele, oedema and tumour](../../../../../painless-scrotal-mass-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)[Differential diagnosis: torsion, trauma, infection and other acute swelling](../../../../../the-acute-scrotum-by-the-american-urological-association.md)

The discriminating examination locates the swelling relative to the [inguinal ligament](../../../../../inguinal-ligament.md) and to the [testis](../../../../../testis.md), asks whether it is cystic or solid, and notes posture-related change, reducibility, cough impulse and transillumination when relevant. A cough impulse is not exclusive to a [hernia](../../../../../hernia.md); a communicating fluid collection or a venous swelling may vary with pressure. Modern [ultrasonography](../../../../../ultrasonography.md), including assessment of [blood flow](../../../../../blood-flow.md) when indicated, can clarify uncertain anatomy. **The site and character of a swelling must be established before it is called an inguinoscrotal hernia.**
