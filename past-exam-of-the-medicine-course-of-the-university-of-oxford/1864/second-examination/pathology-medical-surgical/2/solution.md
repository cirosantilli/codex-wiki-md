**[Leucocythaemia](../../../../../leucocythaemia.md) was the historical description of a persistent excess of white corpuscles in the [blood](../../../../../blood.md), commonly accompanied by enlargement of blood-forming or lymphoid organs.** The corresponding modern disease group is [leukaemia](../../../../../leukaemia.md), but a raised [leukocyte](../../../../../leukocyte.md) count alone is not a sufficient modern diagnosis.

A period investigation would compare the proportions and appearances of the white and red corpuscles in a fresh blood preparation and examine the [spleen](../../../../../spleen.md), [lymph nodes](../../../../../lymph-node.md) and [liver](../../../../../liver.md). Splenic enlargement could be striking, and enlarged lymphatic glands supplied another important pattern. The white material was not simply [pus](../../../../../pus.md) circulating from an unnoticed [abscess](../../../../../abscess.md); microscopic resemblance between a [leukocyte](../../../../../leukocyte.md) and a pus corpuscle does not establish suppuration. A persistent excess must also be distinguished from a temporary reactive rise during [infection](../../../../../infection.md) or [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md).

In present terms, [leukaemia](../../../../../leukaemia.md) comprises malignant disorders of blood-forming cells. Acute and chronic forms, and myeloid and lymphoid lineages, differ in the maturity and identity of their abnormal cells and in their course. [Bone marrow](../../../../../bone-marrow.md) disease can interfere with normal production of [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md), [platelets](../../../../../platelet.md) and functioning [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md). Thus pallor, fatigue and breathlessness may reflect [anaemia](../../../../../anaemia.md); bruising or bleeding may reflect inadequate [platelets](../../../../../platelet.md); recurrent [infection](../../../../../infection.md) can occur despite a large total white count. Infiltration or enlargement of the [spleen](../../../../../spleen.md), [lymph nodes](../../../../../lymph-node.md) and [liver](../../../../../liver.md) can produce swelling, early satiety or abdominal discomfort.[Definition, major classifications, symptoms and organ enlargement](../../../../../leukaemia-by-cancer-council-australia.md)

The useful modern examination includes a complete [blood count](../../../../../blood-count.md) and differential, a stained [blood film](../../../../../blood-film.md), and investigation of [bone marrow](../../../../../bone-marrow.md) and cellular markers when indicated. The morphology and proportions of mature and immature cells matter. A count can be normal or low in some [leukaemias](../../../../../leukaemia.md), while a high count can have a non-malignant cause.[Interpretation: a count alone cannot confirm a diagnosis; differential, film and marrow investigations](../../../../../white-blood-count-by-medlineplus.md) **The historical appearance suggests a disorder of blood formation, but classification requires more than counting white cells.**
