**Examine the specimen systematically: establish its condition, inspect it, assess reaction and concentration, investigate important solutes, examine the sediment, and interpret the findings together.** This is a written plan; the particular appearances and analytical results for A are not supplied.

The physical, chemical and microscopical divisions are complementary. [Urinalysis in Clinical Methods by David M. Roxe](../../../../../urinalysis-in-clinical-methods-by-david-m-roxe.md) supplies a modern framework for them; its 1990 account includes [urine reagent strips](../../../../../urine-reagent-strip.md), which must not be attributed to an 1864 examination. A period account instead uses visual examination, indicator paper, a [urinometer](../../../../../urinometer.md), separate wet-chemical tests and an [optical microscope](../../../../../optical-microscope.md). Microscopy of urinary deposits was already established before this paper.[pp. 128–129: microscopical examination even when no deposit is visible to the unaided eye](../../../../../urinary-deposits-review-1853.md)

**Check the sample before testing it.** Record the label, collection time, whether it is a single void or part of a timed collection, and any known contamination or storage. The volume in one bottle does not establish daily [urine](../../../../../urine.md) output. Retain an unchanged portion and use separate clean vessels for different tests. Examine promptly: standing changes the specimen through microbial growth, chemical change and the formation or disappearance of particles. A current investigation would use appropriate collection and preservation procedures; a fresh sample and one altered by prolonged storage are not interchangeable.[Executive summary, Patient preparation and Specimen collection; preanalytical sample quality and preservation](../../../../../eflm-european-urinalysis-guideline-2023.md)

**Describe what is visible.** Note colour, clarity, apparent sediment and any surface film. Compare a well-mixed portion with material allowed to settle. Cloudiness may reflect [urinary crystals](../../../../../urinary-crystal.md), [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) or [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md), microorganisms or contamination, so it should be described before a cause is assigned. The plan does not infer “pus” from opacity or [jaundice](../../../../../jaundice.md) from colour alone. Record actual observations and the conditions under which they were made; neither tasting nor deliberate inhalation is a necessary diagnostic step.

**Assess reaction and concentration.** Indicator paper such as [litmus](../../../../../litmus.md) supplies a period-appropriate assessment of acidity or alkalinity; modern [pH](../../../../../ph.md) measurement gives a numerical result. Measure [specific gravity](../../../../../relative-density.md) with a freely floating [urinometer](../../../../../urinometer.md) in sufficient fluid, avoiding contact with the vessel and following its calibration and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) requirements. Report the instrument and conditions. [Specific gravity](../../../../../relative-density.md) concerns dissolved material as well as [water](../../../../../water.md), so a dense specimen is not by itself proof of either normal renal function or [glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md).[Basic Science: specific gravity, urinometer temperature correction and the effects of dissolved constituents](../../../../../urinalysis-in-clinical-methods-by-david-m-roxe.md)

**Investigate protein on a separate portion.** For a historical account, a controlled heat-coagulation test with attention to the reaction of the fluid, together with an appropriate acid-precipitation test, provides a way to investigate the older category “albumen.” Compare any turbidity before and after the specified reagent, since heating can also bring inorganic salts out of solution. In a present laboratory, a [urine reagent strip](../../../../../urine-reagent-strip.md) result is followed, where appropriate, by quantitative [albumin](../../../../../albumin.md) or total [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) measurement. These tests have different sensitivities and chemical selectivities; one negative screen does not exclude every urinary [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md).[Technique: heat and precipitation methods, strip selectivity, confirmation and quantification](../../../../../proteinuria-in-clinical-methods.md)

**Investigate sugar independently.** A period chemical approach tests reduction of an alkaline [copper(II)](../../../../../copper-ii.md) reagent, while a modern enzyme-based method tests [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) more selectively. A positive reducing reaction does not identify [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) uniquely, and the amount of reduction is not a diagnosis of [diabetes](../../../../../diabetes.md). Confirm the substance sought and relate confirmed [glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md) to [blood glucose](../../../../../blood-sugar.md) and renal handling before drawing a clinical conclusion. A sample can contain other reducing substances, and impaired tubular recovery can permit [glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md) without persistent [hyperglycaemia](../../../../../hyperglycaemia.md).[Technique and Basic Science: copper reduction versus glucose-specific methods; filtered load and renal threshold](../../../../../glucosuria-in-clinical-methods.md) A [reagent blank](../../../../../reagent-blank.md) and a suitable known reference help distinguish specimen behaviour from an unsuccessful or contaminated test.

**Examine the formed elements.** Keep an unmodified portion for [urine microscopy](../../../../../urine-microscopy.md). Let a suitable portion settle, or in a modern standardised laboratory prepare a measured sediment by controlled [centrifugation](../../../../../centrifugation-split.md). Place a drop under a coverslip, survey several fields at low magnification, then use higher magnification to characterise cells, casts and crystals. Record the method and magnification so that reported abundance has a meaning.[Particle analysis and microscopy procedures; sample preparation and reporting](../../../../../eflm-european-urinalysis-guideline-2023.md) Do not acidify the entire specimen merely to clear it before recording its original particles: the treatment may change the structures being sought.

Distinguish [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md), [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md), epithelial elements, [urinary casts](../../../../../urinary-cast.md), [urinary crystals](../../../../../urinary-crystal.md), microorganisms and extraneous fibres or other artefacts. A cast's shape and contents provide different information from free cells. Hyaline casts are not invariably pathological; a red-cell cast is more significant for a renal source of bleeding than a few free [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md). White-cell casts indicate renal inflammation but are not uniquely diagnostic of bacterial [pyelonephritis](../../../../../pyelonephritis.md).[Hyaline, red-cell and white-cell casts; interpretation and pseudocasts](../../../../../urinary-casts-by-merck-manual.md) Crystal morphology, the specimen's reaction and storage conditions should be considered together; crystals in a bottle do not alone demonstrate a [urinary stone](../../../../../urinary-stone.md).

**Confirm and report the pattern.** If the findings raise a question about [bilirubin](../../../../../bilirubin.md), blood pigment, infection or another constituent, perform the appropriate discriminating test on a fresh separate portion. Modern blood-pigment, [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) and leukocyte/nitrite strip tests are later additions, not an 1864 instrument list. [Urine culture](../../../../../urine-culture.md) is a separate modern investigation when infection is suspected. Neither [pyuria](../../../../../pyuria.md) nor organisms seen in a possibly contaminated specimen settle that diagnosis on their own.[Urine testing: limitations of microscopy, contamination, culture and leukocyte findings](../../../../../bacterial-urinary-tract-infections-by-merck-manual.md)

The final account should distinguish the observations, the tests and their limitations, and the physiological or pathological interpretation. Reconcile apparently conflicting results, repeat a doubtful test on a fresh portion, and identify what further clinical information is needed. **The deliverable here is the examination plan; no colour, density, chemical result, sediment finding or diagnosis is assigned to A.**
