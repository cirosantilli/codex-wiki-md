## 2

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical examination](clinical-examination.md)

**Original question.**

_At the Museum._

Examine (writing a methodical account of your plan of examination) the Urine marked A.

<h3 id="2/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [2](#2)

**Examine the specimen systematically: establish its condition, inspect it, assess reaction and concentration, investigate important solutes, examine the sediment, and interpret the findings together.** This is a written plan; the particular appearances and analytical results for A are not supplied.

The physical, chemical and microscopical divisions are complementary. [Urinalysis in Clinical Methods by David M. Roxe](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#urinalysis-in-clinical-methods-by-david-m-roxe) supplies a modern framework for them; its 1990 account includes [urine reagent strips](../../../biology.md#urine-reagent-strip), which must not be attributed to an 1864 examination. A period account instead uses visual examination, indicator paper, a [urinometer](../../../fluid-mechanics.md#urinometer), separate wet-chemical tests and an [optical microscope](../../../optical-instrument.md#optical-microscope). Microscopy of urinary deposits was already established before this paper.[pp. 128–129: microscopical examination even when no deposit is visible to the unaided eye](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#urinary-deposits-review-1853)

**Check the sample before testing it.** Record the label, collection time, whether it is a single void or part of a timed collection, and any known contamination or storage. The volume in one bottle does not establish daily [urine](../../../physiology.md#urine) output. Retain an unchanged portion and use separate clean vessels for different tests. Examine promptly: standing changes the specimen through microbial growth, chemical change and the formation or disappearance of particles. A current investigation would use appropriate collection and preservation procedures; a fresh sample and one altered by prolonged storage are not interchangeable.[Executive summary, Patient preparation and Specimen collection; preanalytical sample quality and preservation](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#eflm-european-urinalysis-guideline-2023)

**Describe what is visible.** Note colour, clarity, apparent sediment and any surface film. Compare a well-mixed portion with material allowed to settle. Cloudiness may reflect [urinary crystals](../../../biology.md#urinary-crystal), [red blood cells](../../../biological-cell.md#red-blood-cell) or [leukocytes](../../../biological-cell.md#leukocyte), microorganisms or contamination, so it should be described before a cause is assigned. The plan does not infer “pus” from opacity or [jaundice](../../../biology.md#jaundice) from colour alone. Record actual observations and the conditions under which they were made; neither tasting nor deliberate inhalation is a necessary diagnostic step.

**Assess reaction and concentration.** Indicator paper such as [litmus](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#litmus) supplies a period-appropriate assessment of acidity or alkalinity; modern [pH](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#ph) measurement gives a numerical result. Measure [specific gravity](../../../fluid-mechanics.md#relative-density) with a freely floating [urinometer](../../../fluid-mechanics.md#urinometer) in sufficient fluid, avoiding contact with the vessel and following its calibration and [temperature](../../../temperature.md) requirements. Report the instrument and conditions. [Specific gravity](../../../fluid-mechanics.md#relative-density) concerns dissolved material as well as [water](../../../chemical-substance.md#water), so a dense specimen is not by itself proof of either normal renal function or [glycosuria](../../../biology.md#glycosuria).[Basic Science: specific gravity, urinometer temperature correction and the effects of dissolved constituents](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#urinalysis-in-clinical-methods-by-david-m-roxe)

**Investigate protein on a separate portion.** For a historical account, a controlled heat-coagulation test with attention to the reaction of the fluid, together with an appropriate acid-precipitation test, provides a way to investigate the older category “albumen.” Compare any turbidity before and after the specified reagent, since heating can also bring inorganic salts out of solution. In a present laboratory, a [urine reagent strip](../../../biology.md#urine-reagent-strip) result is followed, where appropriate, by quantitative [albumin](../../../physiology.md#albumin) or total [protein](../../../protein.md) measurement. These tests have different sensitivities and chemical selectivities; one negative screen does not exclude every urinary [protein](../../../protein.md).[Technique: heat and precipitation methods, strip selectivity, confirmation and quantification](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#proteinuria-in-clinical-methods)

**Investigate sugar independently.** A period chemical approach tests reduction of an alkaline [copper(II)](../../../chemical-substance.md#copper-ii) reagent, while a modern enzyme-based method tests [glucose](../../../chemical-substance.md#glucose) more selectively. A positive reducing reaction does not identify [glucose](../../../chemical-substance.md#glucose) uniquely, and the amount of reduction is not a diagnosis of [diabetes](../../../biology.md#diabetes). Confirm the substance sought and relate confirmed [glycosuria](../../../biology.md#glycosuria) to [blood glucose](../../../physiology.md#blood-sugar) and renal handling before drawing a clinical conclusion. A sample can contain other reducing substances, and impaired tubular recovery can permit [glycosuria](../../../biology.md#glycosuria) without persistent [hyperglycaemia](../../../physiology.md#hyperglycaemia).[Technique and Basic Science: copper reduction versus glucose-specific methods; filtered load and renal threshold](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#glucosuria-in-clinical-methods) A [reagent blank](../../../chemistry.md#reagent-blank) and a suitable known reference help distinguish specimen behaviour from an unsuccessful or contaminated test.

**Examine the formed elements.** Keep an unmodified portion for [urine microscopy](../../../biology.md#urine-microscopy). Let a suitable portion settle, or in a modern standardised laboratory prepare a measured sediment by controlled [centrifugation](../../../centrifugation.md). Place a drop under a coverslip, survey several fields at low magnification, then use higher magnification to characterise cells, casts and crystals. Record the method and magnification so that reported abundance has a meaning.[Particle analysis and microscopy procedures; sample preparation and reporting](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#eflm-european-urinalysis-guideline-2023) Do not acidify the entire specimen merely to clear it before recording its original particles: the treatment may change the structures being sought.

Distinguish [red blood cells](../../../biological-cell.md#red-blood-cell), [leukocytes](../../../biological-cell.md#leukocyte), epithelial elements, [urinary casts](../../../biology.md#urinary-cast), [urinary crystals](../../../biology.md#urinary-crystal), microorganisms and extraneous fibres or other artefacts. A cast's shape and contents provide different information from free cells. Hyaline casts are not invariably pathological; a red-cell cast is more significant for a renal source of bleeding than a few free [red blood cells](../../../biological-cell.md#red-blood-cell). White-cell casts indicate renal inflammation but are not uniquely diagnostic of bacterial [pyelonephritis](../../../anatomy.md#pyelonephritis).[Hyaline, red-cell and white-cell casts; interpretation and pseudocasts](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#urinary-casts-by-merck-manual) Crystal morphology, the specimen's reaction and storage conditions should be considered together; crystals in a bottle do not alone demonstrate a [urinary stone](../../../biology.md#urinary-stone).

**Confirm and report the pattern.** If the findings raise a question about [bilirubin](../../../physiology.md#bilirubin), blood pigment, infection or another constituent, perform the appropriate discriminating test on a fresh separate portion. Modern blood-pigment, [ketone](../../../chemical-substance.md#ketone) and leukocyte/nitrite strip tests are later additions, not an 1864 instrument list. [Urine culture](../../../biology.md#urine-culture) is a separate modern investigation when infection is suspected. Neither [pyuria](../../../biology.md#pyuria) nor organisms seen in a possibly contaminated specimen settle that diagnosis on their own.[Urine testing: limitations of microscopy, contamination, culture and leukocyte findings](../../../reproductive-and-urinary-medicine-bibliography.md#bacterial-urinary-tract-infections-by-merck-manual)

The final account should distinguish the observations, the tests and their limitations, and the physiological or pathological interpretation. Reconcile apparently conflicting results, repeat a doubtful test on a fresh portion, and identify what further clinical information is needed. **The deliverable here is the examination plan; no colour, density, chemical result, sediment finding or diagnosis is assigned to A.**
