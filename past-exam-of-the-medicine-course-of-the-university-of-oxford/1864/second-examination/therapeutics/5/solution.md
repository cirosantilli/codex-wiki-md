<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**Resuscitate significant [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md) while identifying its cause; pregnancy, timing in relation to birth, and the condition of the [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md) determine the definitive treatment.** A vaginal loss need not originate in the [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md), and the visible volume may underestimate concealed bleeding. Assess [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md), [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), [blood pressure](../../../../../blood-pressure.md) and ongoing loss; serious bleeding calls for urgent obstetric or gynaecological help, [intravenous access](../../../../../intravenous-access.md), blood sampling and access to [blood transfusion](../../../../../blood-transfusion.md). Investigations must not delay control of a life-threatening source.

Outside pregnancy, distinguish excessive menstrual bleeding from irregular, postcoital or postmenopausal bleeding. Causes include [uterine fibroids](../../../../../uterine-fibroid.md), [endometrial polyps](../../../../../endometrial-polyp.md), disturbed [ovulation](../../../../../ovulation.md), [coagulopathy](../../../../../coagulopathy.md) and [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md). A [speculum](../../../../../speculum.md) examination may reveal a cervical or vaginal source. History, examination, [blood count](../../../../../blood-count.md), and appropriately selected [ultrasonography](../../../../../ultrasonography.md), [hysteroscopy](../../../../../hysteroscopy.md) or tissue sampling establish the cause. Current treatment for [heavy menstrual bleeding](../../../../../heavy-menstrual-bleeding.md) may use [tranexamic acid](../../../../../tranexamic-acid.md) or hormonal treatment where suitable; removal of a [polyp](../../../../../polyp.md) or treatment of a [uterine fibroid](../../../../../uterine-fibroid.md) addresses a structural cause. Suspected [cancer](../../../../../cancer.md) needs a diagnostic and specialist pathway, rather than repeated empirical suppression of bleeding.[NG88, investigation according to history and examination, and cause-directed treatment](../../../../../heavy-menstrual-bleeding-by-nice.md)

In early pregnancy, consider threatened or established [miscarriage](../../../../../miscarriage.md) and retained pregnancy tissue. Establish the location and viability of the pregnancy with [ultrasonography](../../../../../ultrasonography.md) and, where appropriate, serial [human chorionic gonadotropin](../../../../../human-chorionic-gonadotropin.md) measurements. Stable cases permit a choice among expectant, medical or surgical management according to the diagnosis and circumstances; heavy ongoing bleeding or instability changes the urgency. An [ectopic pregnancy](../../../../../ectopic-pregnancy.md) may cause vaginal bleeding together with dangerous internal [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md), even though the bleeding pregnancy is outside the [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md). Pain, collapse or instability therefore demands urgent assessment rather than an assumption that all bleeding represents a harmless menstrual or uterine event.[NG126, §§1.3–1.7: emergency assessment, diagnosis and management](../../../../../ectopic-pregnancy-and-miscarriage-by-nice.md)

Later pregnancy requires distinction between [placenta praevia](../../../../../placenta-praevia.md), [placental abruption](../../../../../placental-abruption.md) and other genital-tract sources. [Placenta praevia](../../../../../placenta-praevia.md) may cause heavy painless bleeding; [placental abruption](../../../../../placental-abruption.md) may involve pain, a tense [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md) and concealed loss. Stabilise the mother, assess the fetus and locate the [placenta](../../../../../placenta.md) by [ultrasonography](../../../../../ultrasonography.md). Avoid a digital examination that could disturb a covering [placenta](../../../../../placenta.md) before [placenta praevia](../../../../../placenta-praevia.md) has been excluded. The timing and route of birth depend on severity, placental position, gestation and maternal and fetal condition. A [placenta](../../../../../placenta.md) covering the cervical opening usually requires [Caesarean section](../../../../../caesarean-section.md); the diagnosis is not an instruction to apply a vaginal plug and wait through major continuing loss.[Bleeding, ultrasound assessment and planning birth](../../../../../placenta-praevia-placenta-accreta-and-vasa-praevia-by-rcog.md)[Green-top Guideline 63, §7.3: digital examination precaution](../../../../../antepartum-haemorrhage-by-rcog.md)

During and after birth, organise [postpartum haemorrhage](../../../../../postpartum-haemorrhage.md) causes as poor [uterine contraction](../../../../../uterine-contraction.md), retained tissue, genital-tract trauma and defective clotting. A [uterine rupture](../../../../../uterine-rupture.md) is a surgical emergency; a contracting [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md) does not exclude a traumatic source. For [uterine atony](../../../../../uterine-atony.md), uterine massage and a suitable [uterotonic](../../../../../uterotonic.md) accompany resuscitation; emptying a distended [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md) may help contraction. Examine for tears and repair them; deal with retained [placental tissue](../../../../../placental-tissue.md) with skilled obstetric care, and do not forcibly detach an abnormally adherent [placenta](../../../../../placenta.md). Correct relevant [coagulopathy](../../../../../coagulopathy.md) and use [blood products](../../../../../blood-product.md) as indicated. Current early treatment includes [tranexamic acid](../../../../../tranexamic-acid.md) within the recommended interval. Continuing bleeding may require balloon tamponade or surgery; a [uterine inversion](../../../../../uterine-inversion.md) requires urgent skilled replacement and resuscitation, not treatment as simple atony. Delayed bleeding after birth suggests retained tissue or infection and requires renewed assessment.[First-response treatment bundle and cause-directed management of refractory bleeding](../../../../../consolidated-guidelines-for-the-prevention-diagnosis-and-treatment-of-postpartum-haemorrhage-by-the-world-health-organization-2025.md)

An 1864 practitioner could recognise miscarriage, abnormal placental attachment or separation, retained [placenta](../../../../../placenta.md), atony and traumatic bleeding, and could attempt pressure, manual measures and delivery appropriate to the cause. Cold applications and vaginal plugging appeared in period practice, but were not interchangeable cures. Contemporary obstetric discussion already distinguished several mechanisms of bleeding.[pp. 549–556: uterine disease, pregnancy and obstetric complications](../../../../../quarterly-report-on-midwifery-april-1864.md) In particular, a [uterotonic](../../../../../uterotonic.md) cannot repair a tear, and obstructing the outflow does not establish that internal [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md) has ceased. The modern diagnostic and resuscitative measures above explain why treatment must follow the mechanism rather than the symptom alone.
