<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

**There is no single reliably poisonous dose for each [opium](../../../../../opium.md) preparation. Identify the preparation and exposure, but diagnose and treat poisoning from its effects, especially depressed breathing.** Toxicity depends on the actual alkaloid content, route, age, [opioid tolerance](../../../../../opioid-tolerance.md), coexisting illness and other depressants. A quantity tolerated by one person may produce dangerous [respiratory depression](../../../../../respiratory-depression.md) in another. In particular, neither a historical ordinary dose nor a published fatal quantity defines a boundary below which every person is safe.[Risk factors and the characteristic combination of impaired consciousness, depressed breathing and pinpoint pupils](../../../../../opioid-overdose-by-who.md)

For the historical numerical part, separate formulation strength from a poisonous threshold. The relevant British formulations are those associated with the [British Pharmacopoeia by the General Medical Council (1864)](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia-by-the-general-medical-council-1864.md), not a later edition or the bracketed American recipes in [The Essentials of Materia Medica and Therapeutics by Alfred Baring Garrod (1865)](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md). In the following comparisons, a [grain](../../../../../grain-unit.md) is a mass unit and a [minim](../../../../../minim-unit.md) a volume unit; a fluid ounce is 480 [minims](../../../../../minim-unit.md). These comparisons describe period preparations and are not administration instructions.

- [Opium tincture](../../../../../opium-tincture.md) and the 1864 [Vinum Opii](../../../../../vinum-opii.md) are each described as containing the soluble constituents of one grain of dry [opium](../../../../../opium.md) in approximately 14½ minims. [Tinctura Camphorae cum Opio](../../../../../tinctura-camphorae-cum-opio.md) contains one grain in half a fluid ounce, or 240 minims. Confusing these liquids therefore creates a large error in exposure.
- [Extractum Opii](../../../../../extractum-opii.md) is an aqueous extract with potency dependent on extraction and starting material. The contemporary comparison gives about half a grain of dry extract as equivalent to one grain of raw [opium](../../../../../opium.md). [Extractum Opii Liquidum](../../../../../extractum-opii-liquidum.md) contains about one grain of extract in 22 minims. This is an approximate raw-drug equivalence, not an assay of [morphine](../../../../../morphine.md).
- [Pilula Opii](../../../../../pilula-opii.md) contains one part [opium](../../../../../opium.md) in five parts pill mass; [Pilula Plumbi cum Opio](../../../../../pilula-plumbi-cum-opio.md) one in eight. The latter also carries the independent hazard of a [lead](../../../../../lead.md) compound.
- [Pulvis Ipecacuanhae cum Opio](../../../../../pulvis-ipecacuanhae-cum-opio.md), [Pulvis Kino cum Opio](../../../../../pulvis-kino-cum-opio.md) and [Pulvis Cretae Aromaticus cum Opio](../../../../../pulvis-cretae-aromaticus-cum-opio.md) contain one part [opium](../../../../../opium.md) in ten, twenty and forty parts respectively. Other ingredients do not abolish [opioid](../../../../../opioid.md) toxicity.

These strengths make clear how to compare exposures once their quantities are known. For example, if a preparation contains a mass fraction $f$ of dry [opium](../../../../../opium.md) and a mass $m$ of it was taken, its nominal raw-drug content is

$$
\boxed{m_{\mathrm{opium}}=fm.}
$$

That equation cannot supply an unknown toxic threshold or account for all differences in absorption.[Posological Table, final Opium comparison: British strengths and approximate extract equivalence](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

Two entries require explicit textual caution. The monograph for [Trochisci Opii](../../../../../trochisci-opii.md) specifies 72 grains of [Extractum Opii](../../../../../extractum-opii.md) divided into 720 lozenges, hence **one tenth of a grain of extract per lozenge**; its summary table instead says one tenth of a grain of [opium](../../../../../opium.md). Those are not interchangeable descriptions. Similarly, the [Enema Opii](../../../../../enema-opii.md) recipe specifies 30 minims of [opium tincture](../../../../../opium-tincture.md) with two fluid ounces of starch decoction, whereas the summary table describes one grain of [opium](../../../../../opium.md) in two fluid ounces. At the stated tincture strength the recipe contributes about two grains to the mixture, so the discrepancy should not be silently turned into a precise rectal poisonous dose.[Papaverceae, Parts 2–3: Enema Opii recipe and Trochisci Opii recipe; compare Posological Table](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

The external preparations also belong to the officinal list: [Emplastrum Opii](../../../../../emplastrum-opii.md), [Linimentum Opii](../../../../../linimentum-opii.md) and [Unguentum Gallae cum Opio](../../../../../unguentum-gallae-cum-opio.md). The plaster contains one part powdered [opium](../../../../../opium.md) to nine parts resin plaster; the liniment mixes equal volumes of [opium tincture](../../../../../opium-tincture.md) and soap liniment. The galls ointment is described as approximately one part [opium](../../../../../opium.md) in 14½ parts of ointment. These are not oral medicines. Exposure over damaged skin, contact with mucosa or accidental swallowing cannot be evaluated by assigning a universal number of spoonfuls or applications.[Papaverceae, Part 2: plaster and liniment; Part 3: galls and opium ointment](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

If preparations derived from the purified alkaloid are included, distinguish them from raw [opium](../../../../../opium.md): [Liquor Morphiae Hydrochloratis](../../../../../liquor-morphiae-hydrochloratis.md) contains four grains of [morphine hydrochloride](../../../../../morphine-hydrochloride.md) per fluid ounce; [Suppositoria Morphiae](../../../../../suppositoria-morphiae.md) contain a quarter grain each; [Trochisci Morphiae](../../../../../trochisci-morphiae.md) and [Trochisci Morphiae et Ipecacuanhae](../../../../../trochisci-morphiae-et-ipecacuanhae.md) contain one thirty-sixth of a grain of the salt per lozenge. A raw-drug fraction is not the same thing as a mass of purified [morphine hydrochloride](../../../../../morphine-hydrochloride.md).[Papaverceae, Part 3, Of Hydrochlorate of Morphia: solution, suppositories and the two lozenges](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md)

Deep historical narcotism means profound [opioid poisoning](../../../../../opioid-poisoning.md), with depressed [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md) and dangerously slow or ineffective breathing, often with small [pupils](../../../../../pupil.md). Current priorities are emergency help, an open [airway](../../../../../airway.md), assisted ventilation and [oxygen therapy](../../../../../oxygen-therapy.md) as required, and prompt [naloxone](../../../../../naloxone.md). If there is [cardiac arrest](../../../../../cardiac-arrest.md), start appropriate [cardiopulmonary resuscitation](../../../../../cardiopulmonary-resuscitation.md). Repeat [naloxone](../../../../../naloxone.md) or use an infusion when clinically required, and continue observation because the [opioid](../../../../../opioid.md) may outlast the reversal. A transient awakening is not proof of sustained recovery.[Emergency responses: basic life support and timely naloxone](../../../../../opioid-overdose-by-who.md)[Emergency response, naloxone and continuing assistance](../../../../../responding-to-a-suspected-opioid-overdose-by-the-national-institute-for-occupational-safety-and-health.md)

Older attempts to empty the [stomach](../../../../../stomach.md) or to rouse an unconscious person must not displace respiratory support. Do not give oral drinks or an [emetic](../../../../../emetic.md) to an unconscious person, and do not perform unprotected gastric instrumentation: [aspiration](../../../../../aspiration.md) can add a second cause of respiratory failure. The concise therapeutic answer is therefore **support ventilation and reverse the [opioid](../../../../../opioid.md) effect, rather than waiting for a calculated poisonous dose to be exceeded**.
