<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

The four agents have different proposed purposes. **Their indications must be tied to a mechanism and a diagnosis, and their historical use must not be converted into a general modern recommendation.** In particular, a historical effect on a discharge, pulse or bowel movement is not proof of cure of the disease causing it.

[Elaterium](../../../../../elaterium.md) was a drastic hydragogue [purgative](../../../../../purgative.md), the dried deposit from the juice of squirting cucumber fruit. The intended use was to remove fluid through the [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md) in severe [dropsy](../../../../../dropsy.md), particularly some cardiac cases. The same action explains the danger: violent [diarrhoea](../../../../../diarrhoea.md), abdominal irritation, [dehydration](../../../../../dehydration.md) and collapse. Frailty, existing fluid depletion, inflammatory disease of the [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md), suspected [bowel obstruction](../../../../../bowel-obstruction.md) and pregnancy make such treatment particularly objectionable. Variable potency further undermines a fixed-dose rule.[Cucurbitaceae, Elaterium: preparation, hydragogue action, dropsy and irritant effects](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md) In current care, identify the cause of [oedema](../../../../../oedema.md); cardiac fluid overload may require monitored [diuretic](../../../../../diuretic.md) treatment, not deliberate drastic purging. [Elaterium](../../../../../elaterium.md) is not a substitute for a modern [diuretic](../../../../../diuretic.md).

[Digitalis preparations](../../../../../digitalis-preparation.md), historically obtained from foxglove leaves, were used to slow and strengthen cardiac action and promote [diuresis](../../../../../diuresis.md) in selected forms of cardiac [dropsy](../../../../../dropsy.md). An appropriate indication therefore requires assessment of the [heart](../../../../../heart.md) and [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md), not the mere presence of swelling. Modern [digoxin](../../../../../digoxin.md) is a standardised [cardiac glycoside](../../../../../cardiac-glycoside.md), used in selected [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md) and [atrial fibrillation](../../../../../atrial-fibrillation.md); it is not equivalent to an arbitrary amount of crude leaf. Important contraindications include certain [atrioventricular blocks](../../../../../atrioventricular-block.md), [ventricular tachycardia](../../../../../ventricular-tachycardia.md), [ventricular fibrillation](../../../../../ventricular-fibrillation.md) and particular accessory-pathway arrhythmias. [Renal impairment](../../../../../renal-impairment.md), [hypokalaemia](../../../../../hypokalaemia.md) and interacting medicines increase the risk of toxicity and demand attention to dosing and monitoring. A very slow [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), new [cardiac arrhythmia](../../../../../cardiac-arrhythmia.md), nausea or visual disturbance during treatment calls for assessment, not additional doses to obtain more [diuresis](../../../../../diuresis.md).[§§4.1–4.5: indications, conduction disease, arrhythmias, renal function, electrolytes and interactions](../../../../../digoxin-tablets-summary-of-product-characteristics-emc.md)

[Aloes](../../../../../aloes.md) means the dried aloe leaf juice used as a stimulant [laxative](../../../../../laxative.md), not the relatively different leaf gel. Historically it was used for sluggish bowel action and sometimes as an [emmenagogue](../../../../../emmenagogue.md), on theories of pelvic stimulation. The latter is not a present treatment for an unexplained absent period. The recognised current herbal indication is short-term occasional [constipation](../../../../../constipation.md), with limits on duration and age. Contraindications include [bowel obstruction](../../../../../bowel-obstruction.md), inflammatory bowel disease, unexplained abdominal [pain](../../../../../pain.md), severe [dehydration](../../../../../dehydration.md), pregnancy and breastfeeding. Repeated purgation can disturb water and [electrolyte](../../../../../electrolyte.md) balance; constipation caused by a serious obstruction should not be treated by progressively stronger [laxatives](../../../../../laxative.md).[Public summary: dried leaf juice, short-term use and contraindications](../../../../../aloes-folii-succus-siccatus-by-the-european-medicines-agency.md)

[Copaiba](../../../../../copaiba.md) is an [oleoresin](../../../../../oleoresin.md), historically given for [gonorrhoea](../../../../../gonorrhoea.md) and persistent urethral discharge, and sometimes other mucosal complaints. Period selection tended to favour a less acutely inflamed stage; irritation of the [stomach](../../../../../stomach.md) and urinary tract, nausea, [diarrhoea](../../../../../diarrhoea.md) or a rash argued against continuing it. The contemporary monograph also warns against its use in febrile states. Existing marked urinary irritation is poor ground for an irritant empirical remedy.[Cisalpinae, Copaiba: balsam and oil, urinary uses and unwanted effects](../../../../../the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865.md) Modern [gonorrhoea](../../../../../gonorrhoea.md) requires appropriate microbiological diagnosis, effective [antibiotic](../../../../../antibiotic.md) treatment and partner management. Current CDC guidance uses [ceftriaxone](../../../../../ceftriaxone.md)-based treatment; [copaiba](../../../../../copaiba.md) is not an alternative to eradication of the infection. Apparent reduction of discharge cannot establish microbiological cure.[Diagnostic considerations, recommended regimens and management of partners](../../../../../gonococcal-infections-among-adolescents-and-adults-by-cdc.md)

Thus the period shorthand is fluid removal by [elaterium](../../../../../elaterium.md), selected cardiac treatment by [digitalis preparations](../../../../../digitalis-preparation.md), bowel stimulation by [aloes](../../../../../aloes.md), and a disputed treatment of mucosal discharge by [copaiba](../../../../../copaiba.md). Present interpretation preserves the useful distinction between actions while rejecting the assumption that all four historical indications remain valid.
