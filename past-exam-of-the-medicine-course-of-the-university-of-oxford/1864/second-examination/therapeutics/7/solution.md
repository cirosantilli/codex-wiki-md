<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

**Relieve [urinary retention](../../../../../urinary-retention.md) promptly and gently, while distinguishing transient [spasm](../../../../../spasm.md) from a fixed [urethral stricture](../../../../../urethral-stricture.md) or another obstruction.** The historical label [spasmodic urethral stricture](../../../../../spasmodic-urethral-stricture.md) describes a functional component of obstruction; it does not prove that the [urethra](../../../../../urethra.md) contains a permanent fibrotic narrowing.

Ask about the onset, previous attacks, instrumentation, injury, discharge, medications and previous difficulty passing [urine](../../../../../urine.md). Examine for a distended painful [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md), infection and trauma. Current [bladder ultrasonography](../../../../../bladder-ultrasonography.md) can confirm retention and estimate distension without repeatedly attempting instrumentation. A history of easy passage at other times may support a transient component, but neither that history nor failure to pass a catheter excludes an underlying anatomical lesion.

For the 1864 therapeutic framework, reassurance, warmth, a warm bath and relief of [pain](../../../../../pain.md) were intended to relax muscular [spasm](../../../../../spasm.md). An earlier clinical account documents [opium](../../../../../opium.md) and the warm bath, while warning that the bath was unreliable.[Discussion of acute retention and spasmodic obstruction](../../../../../diseases-of-the-urinary-organs-1842.md) Period use of [opium](../../../../../opium.md) belongs to that historical account; it must not be assumed to be harmless, since [opioids](../../../../../opioid.md) can themselves contribute to [urinary retention](../../../../../urinary-retention.md) and sedation. Such measures should not postpone drainage of a persistently overfilled [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md). A lubricated catheter, handled by a skilled operator, should be advanced gently rather than forced through resistance. Producing bleeding or a false passage can convert an initially functional problem into substantial injury.

Current guidance calls for immediate catheterisation in acute [urinary retention](../../../../../urinary-retention.md), with attention to contraindications and the cause. Suspected [urethral trauma](../../../../../urethral-trauma.md), major resistance or repeated failure calls for specialist help, not escalating force. Depending on the findings, guided urethral catheterisation or [suprapubic catheterisation](../../../../../suprapubic-catheterisation.md) may be appropriate. The latter requires a suitably localised distended [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md) and assessment of contraindications; it is not an instruction for blind puncture.[CG97, §1.7.1: immediate catheterisation for acute retention](../../../../../lower-urinary-tract-symptoms-in-men-by-nice.md)[§§4.3–4.6: urethral risks and suprapubic indications and contraindications](../../../../../indwelling-catheterisation-in-adults-by-eaun-2024.md)

After decompression, address the precipitant: infection, painful local inflammation, medication effects, a fixed [urethral stricture](../../../../../urethral-stricture.md) or another outlet disorder. Recurrent attacks merit investigation rather than repeated labelling as [spasm](../../../../../spasm.md). An incision or a course of repeated forceful dilatation is not justified merely by the word spasmodic. [Discussion of retention: opium and the warm bath, with contemporary reservations about the bath](../../../../../diseases-of-the-urinary-organs-1842.md) The principle common to the historical and modern reasoning is to remove the obstruction with the least injury; modern assessment makes the anatomical and infective distinctions more reliable.
