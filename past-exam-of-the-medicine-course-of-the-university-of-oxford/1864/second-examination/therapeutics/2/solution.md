<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**This combination warrants a grave prognosis and closely supervised treatment of both the [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) and the [delirium tremens](../../../../../delirium-tremens.md); recovery cannot be promised or ruled out from the diagnosis alone.** The immediate conflict is that the agitation and sleeplessness of [alcohol withdrawal](../../../../../alcohol-withdrawal.md) call for effective control, while [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) reduces respiratory reserve. Excessive sedation can then worsen [hypoventilation](../../../../../hypoventilation.md) and [aspiration](../../../../../aspiration.md), whereas uncontrolled [delirium tremens](../../../../../delirium-tremens.md) can itself be life-threatening.

An historically reasoned 1864 approach would emphasise continuous nursing, a quiet environment, nourishment and fluids that the person could safely swallow, and repeated examination of the [thorax](../../../../../thorax.md) and [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md). Suppressing a cough or inducing sleep would not demonstrate resolution of the [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md). The traditional strongly depleting approach to inflammation, including [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) and large doses of [tartar emetic](../../../../../potassium-antimony-tartrate.md), would be especially hazardous in an exhausted person. [Opium](../../../../../opium.md) and alcoholic stimulation were debated contemporary remedies; escalating [opium](../../../../../opium.md) simply because sleep had not occurred could deepen [respiratory depression](../../../../../respiratory-depression.md). Period discussion of [delirium tremens](../../../../../delirium-tremens.md) explicitly recognised that treatment response varied and that intercurrent disease altered the outlook.[Medicinal-treatment discussion, pp. 411–413, and discussion of complications and prognosis](../../../../../practical-notes-on-diagnosis-prognosis-and-treatment-in-cases-of-delirium-tremens-by-thomas-laycock.md) These are historical treatment controversies, not present instructions to administer alcohol or [opium](../../../../../opium.md).

Modern management is in hospital, with an appropriate level of observation and early critical-care assessment if breathing or [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md) deteriorates. Assess the [airway](../../../../../airway.md), [respiratory rate](../../../../../respiratory-rate.md), [oxygen saturation](../../../../../oxygen-saturation.md), [blood pressure](../../../../../blood-pressure.md), [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [blood glucose](../../../../../blood-sugar.md) and [electrolytes](../../../../../electrolyte.md). Establish the severity and likely cause of the [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md), including possible [aspiration](../../../../../aspiration.md); a [chest radiograph](../../../../../chest-radiograph.md) and microbiological samples are used as indicated. Give timely [antibiotics](../../../../../antibiotic.md) selected for the setting, severity, likely organisms and local resistance patterns. Provide [oxygen therapy](../../../../../oxygen-therapy.md) or more advanced respiratory support when indicated, while reassessing the response.[NG250, §§1.4–1.7 and 1.9: investigations, antibiotic treatment and respiratory support](../../../../../pneumonia-diagnosis-and-management-by-nice.md)

Treat the [delirium tremens](../../../../../delirium-tremens.md) using an appropriate [benzodiazepine](../../../../../benzodiazepine.md) regimen with frequent reassessment; the respiratory illness makes skilled monitoring particularly important, rather than making untreated withdrawal acceptable. Provide [thiamine](../../../../../thiamine.md), with the parenteral route when indicated by malnutrition or suspected [Wernicke encephalopathy](../../../../../wernicke-encephalopathy.md), and correct relevant deficiencies. [Hypoglycaemia](../../../../../hypoglycaemia.md) requires prompt treatment. [Antipsychotics](../../../../../antipsychotic.md) are not a replacement for treatment of the underlying [alcohol withdrawal](../../../../../alcohol-withdrawal.md).[CG100, §§1.1.4 and 1.2: delirium tremens and thiamine](../../../../../alcohol-use-disorders-diagnosis-and-management-of-physical-complications-by-nice.md)

Historically the absence of effective [antibiotics](../../../../../antibiotic.md) made the additional [pneumonia](../../../../../pneumonia.md) particularly ominous. Today the outlook depends on respiratory failure, [sepsis](../../../../../sepsis.md), coexisting organ disease and treatment response. No severity findings, age or organ-function results are supplied, so neither a mortality percentage nor an inevitable fatal outcome follows. A poor result would also not, by itself, prove that a particular historical remedy caused or prevented death.
