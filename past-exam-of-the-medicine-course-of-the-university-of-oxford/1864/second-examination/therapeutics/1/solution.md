<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**Support the acute illness, relieve painful [arthritis](../../../../../arthritis.md), and repeatedly assess the [heart](../../../../../heart.md); modern treatment also eradicates the antecedent [streptococcal infection](../../../../../streptococcal-infection.md) and prevents recurrence.** [Acute rheumatic fever](../../../../../acute-rheumatic-fever.md) is an inflammatory consequence of preceding [Group A streptococcal infection](../../../../../group-a-streptococcal-infection.md), rather than infection of each painful [anatomical joint](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) by the organism. The distinction matters because an [antibiotic](../../../../../antibiotic.md) and an [anti-inflammatory drug](../../../../../anti-inflammatory-drug.md) have different purposes.[Cause, clinical features, treatment and secondary prevention](../../../../../clinical-guidance-for-acute-rheumatic-fever-by-cdc.md)

In an 1864 answer, the defensible foundation is rest during the febrile illness, attentive nursing, adequate drinks and food, comfortable support of painful [anatomical joints](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md), and relief of [pain](../../../../../pain.md). The physician would follow the [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), breathing, ability to drink and [urine](../../../../../urine.md) output. Examination with a [stethoscope](../../../../../stethoscope.md) should be repeated for a new [heart murmur](../../../../../heart-murmur.md), a [pericardial friction rub](../../../../../pericardial-friction-rub.md) or signs of [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md); improvement of the [arthritis](../../../../../arthritis.md) does not prove that the [rheumatic carditis](../../../../../rheumatic-carditis.md) has ended. Historical alkaline treatment with [potassium bicarbonate](../../../../../potassium-bicarbonate.md) or other alkalis was intended to counter a supposed acid state. [Opium](../../../../../opium.md) was used for severe [pain](../../../../../pain.md) and sleeplessness, with risks of [constipation](../../../../../constipation.md) and [respiratory depression](../../../../../respiratory-depression.md). Neither that acid theory nor abolition of [pain](../../../../../pain.md) establishes prevention of damage to the [heart valves](../../../../../heart-valve.md). Contemporary discussion already disputed whether the apparent benefit of alkalis exceeded that of rest and nourishment, and recognised the difficulty of comparing unlike cases.[Practice of Medicine, pp. 179–183: alkaline treatment and discussion of case comparisons](../../../../../treatment-of-acute-rheumatism-in-the-edinburgh-medical-journal-1862.md)

Current assessment includes an [electrocardiogram](../../../../../electrocardiogram.md) and [echocardiography](../../../../../echocardiography.md), since [valvulitis](../../../../../valvulitis.md) may be present without an obvious [heart murmur](../../../../../heart-murmur.md). A suitable [penicillin](../../../../../penicillin.md) regimen eradicates residual [Group A streptococcal infection](../../../../../group-a-streptococcal-infection.md); subsequent [secondary antibiotic prophylaxis](../../../../../secondary-antibiotic-prophylaxis.md) reduces further attacks. An [anti-inflammatory drug](../../../../../anti-inflammatory-drug.md) such as [naproxen](../../../../../naproxen.md) or [ibuprofen](../../../../../ibuprofen.md) treats the established inflammatory symptoms, with choice and monitoring appropriate to age and illness. These measures should not be described as reversing an already scarred [heart valve](../../../../../heart-valve.md). [Heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md) from severe [rheumatic carditis](../../../../../rheumatic-carditis.md) needs specialist management; severe [Sydenham's chorea](../../../../../sydenham-s-chorea.md) may require specific treatment as well as protection, rest and support. Activity is adjusted to the clinical and cardiac findings rather than prescribed as indefinite immobility. [Bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md), deliberately induced [dehydration](../../../../../dehydration.md) and historical [counterirritation](../../../../../counterirritation.md) provide no substitute for these measures.[Chapter 7, especially Table 7.1: acute manifestations, carditis, chorea and continuing care](../../../../../australian-guideline-for-acute-rheumatic-fever-and-rheumatic-heart-disease-2025.md)

The treatment outline therefore has two time scales: control the present inflammatory illness and its complications, then prevent another immune-mediated attack. Modern [salicylate](../../../../../salicylate.md) treatment should not be projected backwards as the settled Oxford practice of December 1864.
