## 1

↑ **Parent:** [Materia medica, pharmacy](materia-medica-pharmacy.md)

**Original question.**

What officinal preparations of the British Pharmacopœia contain Opium, and in what proportions ?

<h3 id="1/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [1](#1)

**The 1864 preparations contain either crude [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) or its aqueous [Extractum Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#extractum-opii); those quantities must be distinguished from a quantity of isolated [morphine](../../../pharmacology.md#morphine).** The following are the officinal [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) preparations. Ratios for solids mean the [mass fraction](../../../mixture.md#mass-fraction) in the compounded preparation; liquid strengths specify [mass](../../../classical-mechanics.md#mass) per [volume](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#volume) or the actual ingredients used.

- [Emplastrum Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#emplastrum-opii): powdered [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) in resin plaster: **1 in 10**.
- [Enema Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#enema-opii): [opium tincture](../../../pharmacology.md#opium-tincture) 30 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit) with starch [decoction](../../../pharmacology.md#decoction) 2 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce). Thus the tincture is **1 volume to 32 volumes of vehicle**, before mixing.
- [Extractum Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#extractum-opii): aqueous [solvent extraction](../../../engineering.md#solvent-extraction) of 1 [avoirdupois pound](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-pound) of sliced [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) with 6 [Imperial pints](../../../physics.md#imperial-pint) of [distilled water](../../../chemical-substance.md#distilled-water), in three successive portions, followed by [evaporation](../../../critical-phenomenon.md#evaporation). The prescribed starting quantities do not impose a fixed yield or a universal crude-drug-to-extract potency ratio.
- [Extractum Opii Liquidum](../../../pharmacology.md#extractum-opii-liquidum): [Extractum Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#extractum-opii) 1 [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce), [distilled water](../../../chemical-substance.md#distilled-water) 17 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce), [rectified spirit](../../../pharmacology.md#rectified-spirit) 3 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce): **1 ounce of extract to the pint of liquid ingredients**.
- [Linimentum Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#linimentum-opii): equal [volumes](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#volume) of [opium tincture](../../../pharmacology.md#opium-tincture) and [soap liniment](../../../pharmacology.md#soap-liniment): **half the tincture's nominal [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) strength**.
- [Pilula Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#pilula-opii): powdered [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) with hard [soap](../../../surface-tension.md#soap): **1 in 5** of the nominal pill ingredients.
- [Pilula Plumbi cum Opio](../../../pharmacology.md#pilula-plumbi-cum-opio): powdered [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) with [lead acetate](../../../chemical-substance.md#lead-acetate) and confection of roses: **1 in 8**.
- [Pulvis Cretae Aromaticus cum Opio](../../../pharmacology.md#pulvis-cretae-aromaticus-cum-opio): powdered [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) with aromatic chalk powder: **1 in 40**.
- [Pulvis Ipecacuanhae cum Opio](../../../pharmacology.md#pulvis-ipecacuanhae-cum-opio): powdered [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) with [ipecacuanha](../../../pharmacology.md#ipecacuanha) and [potassium sulfate](../../../chemical-substance.md#potassium-sulfate): **1 in 10**.
- [Pulvis Kino cum Opio](../../../pharmacology.md#pulvis-kino-cum-opio): powdered [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) with [kino](../../../pharmacology.md#kino) and [cinnamon](../../../pharmacology.md#cinnamon): **1 in 20**.

[Papaverceae, part 2, British formulas outside the bracketed American additions; underlying revision dated May 1864](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

- [Tinctura Camphorae cum Opio](../../../pharmacology.md#tinctura-camphorae-cum-opio): [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) 40 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit) to 1 [Imperial pint](../../../physics.md#imperial-pint) of [proof spirit](../../../pharmacology.md#proof-spirit), with [benzoic acid](../../../chemical-substance.md#benzoic-acid), [camphor](../../../chemical-substance.md#camphor) and [anise oil](../../../pharmacology.md#anise-oil): **2 grains of [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) per [fluid ounce](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce)**.
- [Opium tincture](../../../pharmacology.md#opium-tincture), _Tinctura Opii_: powdered [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) 1½ [avoirdupois ounces](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce) to 1 [Imperial pint](../../../physics.md#imperial-pint) of [proof spirit](../../../pharmacology.md#proof-spirit). Nominally this is **32.8125 grains per fluid ounce**, or approximately 1 [grain](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit) per 14.63 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit).
- [Trochisci Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#trochisci-opii): 72 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit) of [Extractum Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#extractum-opii) in 720 lozenges: **one tenth of a grain of extract per lozenge**.
- [Unguentum Gallae cum Opio](../../../pharmacology.md#unguentum-gallae-cum-opio): powdered [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) 32 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit) added to 1 [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce) of gall ointment. The actual nominal total is $437.5+32=469.5$ [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit), giving **32/469.5 by mass, approximately 1 in 14.67**; “32 grains to the ounce of ointment taken” is not 32 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit) per final ounce.
- [Vinum Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#vinum-opii): powdered [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) 1½ [avoirdupois ounces](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce), [sherry](../../../physiology.md#sherry) 1 [Imperial pint](../../../physics.md#imperial-pint): **the tincture's nominal [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) proportion**, in a different solvent. This formula uses crude [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium), not [Extractum Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#extractum-opii).

[Papaverceae, part 3, British tincture, lozenge, ointment and wine formulas](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

The arithmetic uses the new [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce) of 437.5 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit), not the old apothecaries' ounce of 480 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit). These are historical formulation proportions, not modern assays of [morphine](../../../pharmacology.md#morphine) delivered after [solvent extraction](../../../engineering.md#solvent-extraction). [Confectio Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#confectio-opii) was omitted in 1864, and this edition's [Vinum Opii](../../../pharmacology.md#vinum-opii) contains no spices. The separate [morphine hydrochloride](../../../pharmacology.md#morphine-hydrochloride) preparations—[Liquor Morphiae Hydrochloratis](../../../pharmacology.md#liquor-morphiae-hydrochloratis), [Suppositoria Morphiae](../../../pharmacology.md#suppositoria-morphiae), [Trochisci Morphiae](../../../pharmacology.md#trochisci-morphiae), and [Trochisci Morphiae et Ipecacuanhae](../../../pharmacology.md#trochisci-morphiae-et-ipecacuanhae)—contain an isolated [alkaloid](../../../chemical-substance.md#alkaloid) salt rather than crude [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium). [pp. 11–12, 39 and 75–76](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-prescriber-s-analysis-of-the-british-pharmacopoeia-by-john-birkbeck-nevins-1864)

## 2

↑ **Parent:** [Materia medica, pharmacy](materia-medica-pharmacy.md)

**Original question.**

Mention the chief Diuretics used in England, and give the doses of their several preparations.

<h3 id="2/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [2](#2)

**The principal period groups were [digitalis preparations](../../../pharmacology.md#digitalis-preparation), [squill](../../../pharmacology.md#squill), [broom](../../../pharmacology.md#broom), [juniper oil](../../../pharmacology.md#juniper-oil), [buchu](../../../pharmacology.md#buchu), [nitrous ether spirit](../../../pharmacology.md#spiritus-aetheris-nitrosi), and several soluble [potassium salts](../../../ionic-bonding.md#potassium-salt).** They were grouped by observed promotion of [urine](../../../physiology.md#urine) flow, rather than by one common mechanism. The following are historical adult quantities per administration, expressed in the contemporary units; they are not present-day prescriptions.

- [Digitalis preparations](../../../pharmacology.md#digitalis-preparation): powdered leaves, ½–2 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit); [Infusum Digitalis](../../../pharmacology.md#infusum-digitalis), 2–4 [fluid drachms](../../../physics.md#fluid-drachm); [Tinctura Digitalis](../../../pharmacology.md#tinctura-digitalis), 5–40 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit); [digitalin](../../../pharmacology.md#digitalin), 1/40–1/10 [grain](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit) in the period table. Historical [digitalin](../../../pharmacology.md#digitalin) was not simply a modern pure [digoxin](../../../pharmacology.md#digoxin) product.
- [Squill](../../../pharmacology.md#squill): powder, 1–2 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit); [Pilula Scillae Composita](../../../pharmacology.md#pilula-scillae-composita), 5–10 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit); [Tinctura Scillae](../../../pharmacology.md#tinctura-scillae), 10–20 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit); [Syrupus Scillae](../../../pharmacology.md#syrupus-scillae), ½–1½ [fluid drachms](../../../physics.md#fluid-drachm).
- [Broom](../../../pharmacology.md#broom): [Decoctum Scoparii](../../../pharmacology.md#decoctum-scoparii), 1–3 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce); [Succus Scoparii](../../../pharmacology.md#succus-scoparii), 1–4 [fluid drachms](../../../physics.md#fluid-drachm).
- [Buchu](../../../pharmacology.md#buchu): powdered leaves, 20–40 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit); [Infusum Bucco](../../../pharmacology.md#infusum-bucco), 1–2 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce); [Tinctura Bucco](../../../pharmacology.md#tinctura-bucco), 1–2 [fluid drachms](../../../physics.md#fluid-drachm).
- [Juniper oil](../../../pharmacology.md#juniper-oil): [Spiritus Juniperi](../../../pharmacology.md#spiritus-juniperi), 10–30 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit) in the 1864 commentary. This unusually concentrated spirit contains one volume of [juniper oil](../../../pharmacology.md#juniper-oil) in ten; its dose must not be borrowed from a later, weaker formula.
- [Nitrous ether spirit](../../../pharmacology.md#spiritus-aetheris-nitrosi), _Spiritus Aetheris Nitrosi_: 30 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit) to 2 [fluid drachms](../../../physics.md#fluid-drachm).
- [Potassium acetate](../../../chemical-substance.md#potassium-acetate): 10–20 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit); [potassium citrate](../../../chemical-substance.md#potassium-citrate): 20–60 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit); [potassium nitrate](../../../chemistry.md#potassium-nitrate): 5–20 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit); [potassium tartrate](../../../chemical-substance.md#potassium-tartrate): 10–60 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit); [potassium hydrogen tartrate](../../../chemical-substance.md#potassium-hydrogen-tartrate): 20–60 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit).
- [Ammonium acetate solution](../../../pharmacology.md#ammonium-acetate-solution), _Liquor Ammoniae Acetatis_: 10–40 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit) of the concentrated British preparation, diluted for administration.

[Posological table: Digitalis, Infusum Digitalis, Scoparius preparations, Bucco preparations, Spiritus Aetheris Nitrosi, potassium salts and Liquor Ammoniae Acetatis; Liliaceae: squill preparations](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865) [p. 61, Spiritus Juniperi, 1 in 10 and 10–30 minims](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-prescriber-s-analysis-of-the-british-pharmacopoeia-by-john-birkbeck-nevins-1864)

Other recognised period agents included [dandelion](../../../pharmacology.md#dandelion) and [copaiba](../../../pharmacology.md#copaiba). The [dandelion](../../../pharmacology.md#dandelion) [decoction](../../../pharmacology.md#decoction) was given in quantities of 1–2 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce), its soft extract in quantities of 10–30 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit), and its preserved [juice](../../../pharmacology.md#juice) in quantities of ½–2 [fluid drachms](../../../physics.md#fluid-drachm). [Copaiba](../../../pharmacology.md#copaiba) was given in quantities of 15–30 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit), and [copaiba oil](../../../pharmacology.md#copaiba-oil) in quantities of 5–20 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit). Their historical inclusion does not establish that they are suitable substitutes for current [diuretic therapy](../../../biology.md#diuretic-therapy). [Posological table, Taraxacum preparations, Copaiba and Copaibae Oleum](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

The distinction between preparations matters more than a single “dose of the plant”: an [infusion](../../../pharmacology.md#infusion), [tincture](../../../pharmacology.md#tincture), [juice](../../../pharmacology.md#juice) and powder have different [concentrations](../../../mathematical-physics.md#concentration). Likewise, a salt's small historical [diuretic](../../../pharmacology.md#diuretic) dose is not its larger [purgative](../../../pharmacology.md#purgative) dose. A modern explanation also separates a direct effect on [renal tubules](../../../anatomy.md#renal-tubule) from improved [cardiac output](../../../physiology.md#cardiac-output) in appropriate [heart failure](../../../biology.md#heart-failure): [digitalis preparations](../../../pharmacology.md#digitalis-preparation) cannot be understood simply as agents that make every [kidney](../../../anatomy.md#kidney) excrete more [water](../../../chemical-substance.md#water). Modern [digoxin](../../../pharmacology.md#digoxin) is used for selected cardiac indications, rather than as a general treatment for low [urine](../../../physiology.md#urine) output. [Why is this medication prescribed?](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#digoxin-by-medlineplus)

## 3

↑ **Parent:** [Materia medica, pharmacy](materia-medica-pharmacy.md)

**Original question.**

Enumerate the “mixtures” for which directions are given in the British Pharmacopœia and state their composition.

<h3 id="3/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

**The seven named [mixtures](../../../mixture.md) of the 1864 [British Pharmacopoeia](../../../pharmacology.md#british-pharmacopoeia) are [Mistura Ammoniaci](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-ammoniaci), [Mistura Amygdalae](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-amygdalae), [Mistura Creasoti](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-creasoti), [Mistura Cretae](../../../ionic-bonding.md#mistura-cretae), [Mistura Ferri Composita](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-ferri-composita), [Mistura Guaiaci](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-guaiaci) and [Mistura Scammonii](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-scammonii).** Their compositions are as follows; [avoirdupois ounces](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce) are used for solids and [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce) for liquids.

- [Mistura Ammoniaci](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-ammoniaci): [ammoniacum](../../../pharmacology.md#ammoniacum) ¼ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce), [distilled water](../../../chemical-substance.md#distilled-water) 8 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce), rubbed together and strained. Its own [gum](../../../pharmacology.md#gum) helps disperse its [resin](../../../chemical-substance.md#resin); [ammoniacum](../../../pharmacology.md#ammoniacum) is not [Ammonia](../../../chemical-substance.md#ammonia). [Umbelliferae, part 2, Ammoniacum](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)
- [Mistura Amygdalae](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-amygdalae): compound almond powder 2½ [avoirdupois ounces](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce), [distilled water](../../../chemical-substance.md#distilled-water) 1 [Imperial pint](../../../physics.md#imperial-pint). The compound powder is made from sweet [almonds](../../../pharmacology.md#almond) 8 parts, [sugar](../../../chemical-substance.md#sugar) 4 parts and [gum arabic](../../../pharmacology.md#gum-arabic) 1 part by [mass](../../../classical-mechanics.md#mass). Trituration produces an [emulsion](../../../mixture.md#emulsion). [Rosaceae, continued, almond preparations](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)
- [Mistura Creasoti](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-creasoti): [creosote](../../../pharmacology.md#creosote) 16 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit), [glacial acetic acid](../../../chemical-substance.md#glacial-acetic-acid) 16 [minims](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit), [Spiritus Juniperi](../../../pharmacology.md#spiritus-juniperi) ½ [fluid drachm](../../../physics.md#fluid-drachm), [syrup](../../../pharmacology.md#syrup) 1 [fluid ounce](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce), [distilled water](../../../chemical-substance.md#distilled-water) 15 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce). Here the spirit has the particularly strong 1864 [juniper oil](../../../pharmacology.md#juniper-oil) content. [Hydrocarbons from the destruction of wood by fire, Mistura Creosoti](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)
- [Mistura Cretae](../../../ionic-bonding.md#mistura-cretae): prepared [chalk](../../../ionic-bonding.md#chalk) and powdered [gum arabic](../../../pharmacology.md#gum-arabic), ¼ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce) each; [syrup](../../../pharmacology.md#syrup) ½ [fluid ounce](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce); [cinnamon water](../../../pharmacology.md#cinnamon-water) 7½ [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce). The [chalk](../../../ionic-bonding.md#chalk) is suspended, not dissolved. [Calcis Carbonas Praecipitata, Creta Praeparata, British mixture](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)
- [Mistura Ferri Composita](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-ferri-composita): powdered [myrrh](../../../pharmacology.md#myrrh) 60 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit), [potassium carbonate](../../../ionic-bonding.md#potassium-carbonate) 25 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit), [rose water](../../../pharmacology.md#rose-water) 8 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce), [ferrous sulfate](../../../chemical-substance.md#ferrous-sulfate) 30 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit), [Spiritus Myristicae](../../../pharmacology.md#spiritus-myristicae) 1 [fluid drachm](../../../physics.md#fluid-drachm), [sugar](../../../chemical-substance.md#sugar) 60 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit). Triturate the [myrrh](../../../pharmacology.md#myrrh) with the spirit and [potassium carbonate](../../../ionic-bonding.md#potassium-carbonate), then add the [rose water](../../../pharmacology.md#rose-water) with [sugar](../../../chemical-substance.md#sugar), followed by [ferrous sulfate](../../../chemical-substance.md#ferrous-sulfate); keep the resulting preparation stoppered. Formation of [ferrous carbonate](../../../chemical-substance.md#ferrous-carbonate) and its subsequent [oxidation](../../../chemical-reaction.md#oxidation) explain why the order and storage matter. [Ferri Carbonas Saccharata, British Mistura Ferri Composita formula](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)
- [Mistura Guaiaci](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-guaiaci): powdered [guaiacum resin](../../../pharmacology.md#guaiacum-resin) ½ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce), [sugar](../../../chemical-substance.md#sugar) ½ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce), powdered [gum arabic](../../../pharmacology.md#gum-arabic) ¼ [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce), [cinnamon water](../../../pharmacology.md#cinnamon-water) 1 [Imperial pint](../../../physics.md#imperial-pint). The [gum](../../../pharmacology.md#gum) assists dispersion of the poorly water-soluble [resin](../../../chemical-substance.md#resin). [Zygophyllaceae, resin preparations](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)
- [Mistura Scammonii](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-scammonii): [scammony resin](../../../pharmacology.md#scammony-resin) 4 [grains](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit) with unskimmed [milk](../../../organism.md#milk) 2 [fluid ounces](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce), triturated into a uniform dispersion. [Convolvulaceae, Scammonium, Mistura Scammonii](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

The category is an official preparation name, not every possible bedside combination of medicines. Camphor water and acacia [mucilage](../../../pharmacology.md#mucilage) are not additional entries under _Mistura_: the 1864 names are _Aqua Camphorae_ and _Mucilago Acaciae_. [pp. 30 and 52–53, mixture names and alterations](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-prescriber-s-analysis-of-the-british-pharmacopoeia-by-john-birkbeck-nevins-1864)

## 4

↑ **Parent:** [Materia medica, pharmacy](materia-medica-pharmacy.md)

**Original question.**

What is the composition of the Pulvis Jalapæ compositus, of the Pilula Scillæ composita, of the Liquor Morphiæ hydrochloratis, and of the Liquor Strychniæ of the British Pharmacopœia ?

<h3 id="4/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [4](#4)

[Liliaceae, British compound squill pill, distinguished from bracketed American formula](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

## 5

↑ **Parent:** [Materia medica, pharmacy](materia-medica-pharmacy.md)

**Original question.**

Name the medicines which impart colouring matter to the excretions.

<h3 id="5/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [5](#5)

**The chief historical examples are [rhubarb](../../../pharmacology.md#rhubarb), [senna](../../../pharmacology.md#senna), [santonin](../../../pharmacology.md#santonin) and [logwood](../../../pharmacology.md#logwood) for [urine](../../../physiology.md#urine); [iron preparations](../../../pharmacology.md#iron-preparation), [bismuth preparations](../../../pharmacology.md#bismuth-preparation) and [charcoal](../../../chemical-substance.md#charcoal) for darkening the [faeces](../../../physiology.md#faeces).** There are two different processes: a substance absorbed and subsequently excreted can colour a secretion, while material remaining in the [intestinal lumen](../../../physiology.md#intestinal-lumen) can colour the stools without demonstrating systemic [drug absorption](../../../pharmacology.md#drug-absorption).

- [Rhubarb](../../../pharmacology.md#rhubarb) colours [urine](../../../physiology.md#urine) yellow to yellow-brown; the colour becomes redder in an [alkaline solution](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#alkaline-solution). The contemporary account also reports coloration of [sweat](../../../physiology.md#sweat) and [breast milk](../../../organism.md#breast-milk). This is a historical observation about passage of colouring matter, not a claim that every constituent passes unchanged into every secretion. [Labiatae, continued, Rheum: colouring constituent, alkaline solutions, and coloration of urine, perspiration and milk](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)
- [Senna](../../../pharmacology.md#senna) can give [urine](../../../physiology.md#urine) a yellow-brown or red-brown colour. The effect is still recognised in current medicinal information. [Side effects, coloration of urine](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#senna-by-the-nhs)
- [Santonin](../../../pharmacology.md#santonin), used historically as an [anthelmintic](../../../pharmacology.md#anthelmintic), can make [urine](../../../physiology.md#urine) conspicuously yellow. This must be distinguished from the drug's separate effect on colour vision: a yellow-looking visual field is not itself excretion of a yellow pigment. [§2416, physiological effects and yellow urine](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#a-manual-of-practical-therapeutics-by-edward-john-waring-1865)
- [Logwood](../../../pharmacology.md#logwood) can give [urine](../../../physiology.md#urine) a pink or reddish colour, particularly when it is an [alkaline solution](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#alkaline-solution); the historical description notes that addition of [Ammonia](../../../chemical-substance.md#ammonia) brings out the colour in a previously acid specimen. [Cisalpinae, Haematoxylum, urine coloration](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)
- [Iron preparations](../../../pharmacology.md#iron-preparation) can darken [faeces](../../../physiology.md#faeces); [bismuth preparations](../../../pharmacology.md#bismuth-preparation) also cause dark or black stools. In these examples the result need not be a pre-existing vegetable dye simply passing unchanged through the body. Current information confirms black stools with oral [iron](../../../chemical-substance.md#iron) and with the modern [bismuth](../../../chemical-substance.md#bismuth) medicine [bismuth subsalicylate](../../../chemical-substance.md#bismuth-subsalicylate); the latter is corroboration of the colour effect, not an identification of that modern formulation with the 1864 bismuth articles. [Dark stools and iron tablets](../../../clinical-medicine-bibliography.md#bleeding-from-the-bottom-by-the-nhs) [Side effects: black stools and black tongue](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#pepto-bismol-by-the-nhs)
- [Charcoal](../../../chemical-substance.md#charcoal) supplies a black material directly to the bowel contents. Its appearance in the stools does not require its [absorption](../../../radiometry.md#absorption-electromagnetic-radiation) and subsequent [renal excretion](../../../anatomy.md#renal-excretion).

**Colour alone does not identify either the drug or a disease.** A medicinal pigment can resemble [blood](../../../physiology.md#blood) or a [bile pigment](../../../physiology.md#bile-pigment); [pH](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#ph), timing after administration and appropriate chemical or microscopic examination distinguish possibilities. Conversely, a history of taking a colouring medicine does not prove that an observed specimen contains no [blood](../../../physiology.md#blood). These examples describe pharmacological and historical observations, not results from an unseen specimen.

## 6

↑ **Parent:** [Materia medica, pharmacy](materia-medica-pharmacy.md)

**Original question.**

What are the preparations of Iron in the British Pharmacopœia ? For what purpose is Ferri Peroxidum Hydratum used ? How is it ordered to be made ?

<h3 id="6/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [6](#6)

**The 1864 iron list includes metallic, ferrous, ferric and compound preparations; its wet [Ferri Peroxidum Hydratum](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-peroxidum-hydratum) was used historically as an [antidote](../../../biology.md#antidote) to [arsenic poisoning](../../../biology.md#arsenic-poisoning).** It must be distinguished from dried [Ferri Peroxidum](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-peroxidum) and from later editions' reassignment of similar Latin names.

The officinal articles and preparations are:

- metallic forms: [iron wire](../../../chemical-substance.md#iron-wire), _Ferrum_, and [Ferrum Redactum](../../../pharmacology.md#ferrum-redactum);
- simple salts and oxides: [Ferri Arsenias](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-arsenias), [Ferri Carbonas Saccharata](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-carbonas-saccharata), [Ferri Iodidum](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-iodidum), [Ferri Oxidum Magneticum](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-oxidum-magneticum), [Ferri Peroxidum](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-peroxidum), [Ferri Peroxidum Hydratum](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-peroxidum-hydratum), [Ferri Phosphas](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-phosphas), [Ferri Sulphas](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-sulphas), [Ferri Sulphas Exsiccata](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-sulphas-exsiccata) and [Ferri Sulphas Granulata](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-sulphas-granulata);
- complex salts: [Ferri et Ammoniae Citras](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-et-ammoniae-citras), [Ferri et Quiniae Citras](../../../pharmacology.md#ferri-et-quiniae-citras) and [Ferrum Tartaratum](../../../pharmacology.md#ferrum-tartaratum);
- solutions: [Liquor Ferri Perchloridi](../../../pharmacology.md#liquor-ferri-perchloridi), [Liquor Ferri Pernitratis](../../../pharmacology.md#liquor-ferri-pernitratis) and the preparative [Liquor Ferri Persulphatis](../../../pharmacology.md#liquor-ferri-persulphatis);
- compounded forms: [Mistura Ferri Composita](../../../pharmacology.md#mistura-ferri-composita), [Pilula Ferri Carbonatis](../../../pharmacology.md#pilula-ferri-carbonatis), [Pilula Ferri Iodidi](../../../pharmacology.md#pilula-ferri-iodidi), [Syrupus Ferri Iodidi](../../../pharmacology.md#syrupus-ferri-iodidi), [Syrupus Ferri Phosphatis](../../../pharmacology.md#syrupus-ferri-phosphatis), [Tinctura Ferri Perchloridi](../../../pharmacology.md#tinctura-ferri-perchloridi) and [Vinum Ferri](../../../pharmacology.md#vinum-ferri).

[Iron and successive British iron monographs; Solution of Persulphate of Iron, Appendix A; British compounds distinguished from American additions and older London preparations](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865) [pp. 43–45, iron additions, omissions and names; pp. 55, 63, 67 and 72, compound forms](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-prescriber-s-analysis-of-the-british-pharmacopoeia-by-john-birkbeck-nevins-1864)

**Prepare the hydrated peroxide by inducing [precipitation](../../../chemical-reaction.md#precipitation-chemistry) from the ferric solution with [sodium hydroxide](../../../chemical-substance.md#sodium-hydroxide), collecting and washing the wet precipitate, and preserving it without drying in a covered vessel.** The starting solution is [Liquor Ferri Persulphatis](../../../pharmacology.md#liquor-ferri-persulphatis), whose “persulphate” here means ferric sulfate, not a modern peroxydisulfate salt. Add the appropriately diluted ferric solution to the alkaline liquor with stirring, allow the [precipitate](../../../chemical-reaction.md#precipitate) to settle, collect it on a calico [filter](../../../set-theory.md#filter-set-theory), and wash with [distilled water](../../../chemical-substance.md#distilled-water) until the washings no longer give a [precipitate](../../../chemical-reaction.md#precipitate) with [barium chloride](../../../chemical-substance.md#barium-chloride). This tests removal of soluble [sulfate](../../../chemistry.md#sulfate). The resulting fresh, hydrated material is kept wet; drying gives the separate dried preparation. [Ferri Peroxidum Hydratum; Ferri Peroxidum; Solution of Persulphate of Iron](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

In modern chemical notation the precipitation can be idealised as

$$
\mathrm{Fe^{3+}+3OH^-\longrightarrow Fe(OH)_3(s)}.
$$

The fresh solid is a hydrated ferric oxide material with variable water content; the equation is not a claim that every pharmaceutical batch is one pure, crystalline [iron hydroxide](../../../chemical-substance.md#iron-hydroxide). Its historical use depended on binding dissolved [arsenic](../../../chemical-substance.md#arsenic) into less available material in the digestive tract, especially the freshly precipitated, wet state. It was not a universal antidote to every metal or every stage of absorbed [arsenic poisoning](../../../biology.md#arsenic-poisoning). Contemporary discussion explicitly identified the new hydrated peroxide as an arsenic antidote. [6 February 1864, p. 65, iron preparations and freshly precipitated antidote](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-british-pharmacopoeia-in-chemical-news-1864) [§1231, preparation, washings tested with barium chloride, preservation without drying and recent preparation](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#a-manual-of-practical-therapeutics-by-edward-john-waring-1865)

Modern treatment of significant [arsenic poisoning](../../../biology.md#arsenic-poisoning) concerns systemic toxicity and may involve specialist-directed [chelation therapy](../../../biology.md#chelation-therapy); a nineteenth-century ferric paste is not the modern treatment standard. [Patient management, acute exposure and specialist consultation for chelation](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#clinician-brief-arsenic-by-atsdr)

## 7

↑ **Parent:** [Materia medica, pharmacy](materia-medica-pharmacy.md)

**Original question.**

What classes of medicines are fitted for preparation by means of decoction and of infusion respectively ? How are liquid extracts made ?

<h3 id="7/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [7](#7)

**[Decoction](../../../pharmacology.md#decoction) suits material whose useful constituents are water-soluble and withstand boiling; [infusion](../../../pharmacology.md#infusion) avoids sustained boiling and can better preserve delicate or volatile constituents.** The choice depends on both the plant's structure and the chemistry of the constituent being extracted.

Hard [bark](../../../organism.md#bark), [wood](../../../organism.md#wood) and many kinds of [root](../../../organism.md#root-botany) need comminution and more prolonged contact with hot [water](../../../chemical-substance.md#water) to penetrate their tissues; a [decoction](../../../pharmacology.md#decoction) can assist extraction when the desired material is stable. Boiling is unsuitable when it drives off a needed [volatile oil](../../../pharmacology.md#volatile-oil), decomposes the active constituent, or extracts an unwanted excess of [starch](../../../chemical-substance.md#starch) or other material. A hard [root](../../../organism.md#root-botany) is therefore not automatically an instruction to boil. Period examples suitable for [decoction](../../../pharmacology.md#decoction) include [oak bark](../../../pharmacology.md#oak-bark), [logwood](../../../pharmacology.md#logwood) and [pareira root](../../../pharmacology.md#pareira-root); appropriate [infusions](../../../pharmacology.md#infusion) include aromatic [cloves](../../../pharmacology.md#clove) and [chamomile](../../../pharmacology.md#chamomile). Cold [infusion](../../../pharmacology.md#infusion) of [calumba](../../../pharmacology.md#calumba) avoids dissolving its [starch](../../../chemical-substance.md#starch), illustrating that suitability depends on the desired selectivity of [solvent extraction](../../../engineering.md#solvent-extraction), not merely on whether a drug is a leaf or a root. [Relation of measures to weights, part 2, Infusa and cold calumba; corresponding British plant monographs](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

For an [infusion](../../../pharmacology.md#infusion), the appropriately divided drug is left in a measured solvent for a prescribed time, commonly in a covered vessel. Hot [water](../../../chemical-substance.md#water) assists extraction, while a cover reduces loss of volatile matter. Cold [infusion](../../../pharmacology.md#infusion) can be chosen when heat is undesirable. The result must be strained or filtered; time, temperature, particle size and solvent quantity affect its strength. Neither method guarantees that every constituent is extracted in the same proportion.

**A [liquid extract](../../../pharmacology.md#liquid-extract) is made by extracting the specified drug with a suitable solvent, separating the liquid from the exhausted material, and bringing the extract to its prescribed concentration and volume.** [Maceration](../../../pharmacology.md#maceration) can precede [percolation](../../../percolation-theory.md); expression or washing recovers retained liquid. A percolator passes fresh solvent through a packed bed, rather than indefinitely soaking the drug in an increasingly saturated liquid. Where concentration is necessary, controlled evaporation limits damage; an alcoholic fraction can be reserved or recombined as the monograph directs. Different [liquid extracts](../../../pharmacology.md#liquid-extract) can use aqueous, alcoholic or ethereal processes. For example, the [liquid extract](../../../pharmacology.md#liquid-extract) of [male fern](../../../pharmacology.md#male-fern) is an ethereal extraction of a resinous and oily fraction, whereas the [liquid extract](../../../pharmacology.md#liquid-extract) of [opium](../../../pharmacology.md#opium) is compounded from a previously made aqueous extract. These are chemically different operations. There is no warrant for assigning all 1864 extracts one modern universal drug-to-extract ratio. [pp. 20–21 and 42–43, differing liquid extracts and strengths; p. 15, percolation](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-prescriber-s-analysis-of-the-british-pharmacopoeia-by-john-birkbeck-nevins-1864)

**Original question.**

What is the temperature at which Linseed, Mustard, Charcoal, and Yeast Poultices are to be made ?

<h3 id="8/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [8](#8)

**For the contemporary formulary, use boiling water for the [linseed](../../../pharmacology.md#linseed), [charcoal](../../../chemical-substance.md#charcoal) and [mustard](../../../pharmacology.md#mustard) [poultices](../../../biology.md#poultice), and water at 100°F for the [yeast](../../../organism.md#yeast) [poultice](../../../biology.md#poultice).** These are preparation temperatures, not temperatures at which boiling material is placed on [skin](../../../physiology.md#skin).

For [Cataplasma Lini](../../../biology.md#cataplasma-lini), hot [water](../../../chemical-substance.md#water) hydrates the ground [linseed](../../../pharmacology.md#linseed) into a cohesive moist mass; the contemporary account also notes the addition of [olive oil](../../../pharmacology.md#olive-oil) when oil has been removed from the meal. For [Cataplasma Carbonis](../../../biology.md#cataplasma-carbonis), the hot bread-and-[linseed](../../../pharmacology.md#linseed) basis is made with boiling [water](../../../chemical-substance.md#water), then the [charcoal](../../../chemical-substance.md#charcoal) is incorporated and part used at the surface. At ordinary atmospheric pressure the boiling-water preparation temperature is approximately $\boxed{212\,{}^\circ\mathrm{F}=100\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}}$. [Relation of measures to weights of the British Pharmacopoeia, Cataplasmata; Carbo Ligni, charcoal cataplasm](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

For [Cataplasma Fermenti](../../../biology.md#cataplasma-fermenti), the stated $\boxed{100\,{}^\circ\mathrm{F}\approx37.8\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}}$ permits the [yeast](../../../organism.md#yeast) to ferment the flour mixture. Boiling would injure its fermenting activity. The flour, [yeast](../../../organism.md#yeast) and warm [water](../../../chemical-substance.md#water) are mixed and kept warm while the mass rises. This is a historical compounding process, not evidence that a fermented dressing treats every [wound](../../../biology.md#wound). [Cataplasmata, exception for Cataplasma Fermenti](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

The [mustard](../../../pharmacology.md#mustard) entry requires a historical qualification. The contemporary British recipe reproduced by the text directs powdered [mustard](../../../pharmacology.md#mustard) and [linseed](../../../pharmacology.md#linseed) to be mixed, then stirred into boiling [water](../../../chemical-substance.md#water); the same text observes that excessive heat impairs development of the pungent [volatile oil](../../../pharmacology.md#volatile-oil). Thus **“boiling” is the contemporary formula's instruction, while lukewarm preparation better preserves development of mustard's pungent [volatile oil](../../../pharmacology.md#volatile-oil)**. These are different claims. The later practical preference for lukewarm [water](../../../chemical-substance.md#water) should not silently replace the 1864 recipe, nor should that recipe be mistaken for optimal modern biochemical practice. [Cruciferae or Brassicaceae, Cataplasma Sinapis and effect of excessive heat](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-essentials-of-materia-medica-and-therapeutics-by-alfred-baring-garrod-1865)

## 9

↑ **Parent:** [Materia medica, pharmacy](materia-medica-pharmacy.md)

**Original question.**

Explain the principle of Volumetric Analysis.

<h3 id="9/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [9](#9)

**[Volumetric analysis](../../../chemistry.md#volumetric-analysis) determines an amount of substance from the measured volume of a reagent of known strength that reacts with it in a known proportion.** A measured [aliquot](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#aliquot) is placed in a suitable vessel, the [standard solution](../../../mixture.md#standard-solution) is delivered from a [burette](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#burette), and the volume required to reach the chosen [titration endpoint](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#titration-endpoint) is read. A [pipette](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#pipette) measures the [aliquot](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#aliquot); a [volumetric flask](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#volumetric-flask) prepares or dilutes the [standard solution](../../../mixture.md#standard-solution).

For a reaction $aA+bB\longrightarrow\text{products}$, if the [concentration](../../../mathematical-physics.md#concentration) of the [titrant](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#titrant) $B$ is $c_B$ and the corrected volume delivered is $V_B$, then

$$
\boxed{n_A=\frac{a}{b}c_BV_B}.
$$

With volume in [litres](../../../physics.md#litre) and concentration in [moles](../../../physics.md#mole-unit) per litre, the answer is an amount in [moles](../../../physics.md#mole-unit). The corresponding [mass](../../../classical-mechanics.md#mass) is $m_A=M_A n_A$, using the appropriate [molar mass](../../../chemistry.md#molar-mass). For an unknown solution of aliquot volume $V_A$,

$$
\boxed{c_A=\frac{a\,c_BV_B}{b\,V_A}}.
$$

These are modern symbols for the same proportional reasoning that a nineteenth-century analyst could express with equivalent weights and a specified standard solution.

[titration; volumetric titration, terms T06387 and 09055](../../../chemistry-bibliography.md#iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry)

A suitable reaction is sufficiently rapid, complete and selective, with a recognisable endpoint. In an [acid-base titration](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#acid-base-titration), an [indicator](../../../measure-theory.md#indicator-function) can change colour near [neutralization](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#neutralization); other reactions use [precipitation](../../../chemical-reaction.md#precipitation-chemistry), [redox](../../../chemical-reaction.md#redox) or a colour intrinsic to a reagent. The observed [titration endpoint](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#titration-endpoint) must represent the required [equivalence point](../../../acid-base-chemistry.md#equivalence-point) closely enough. The reagent's nominal preparation alone is insufficient if its [concentration](../../../mathematical-physics.md#concentration) changes in storage; [standardisation of a solution](../../../chemistry.md#standardisation-of-a-solution), a [reagent blank](../../../chemistry.md#reagent-blank) where needed, and correct reading of the [meniscus](../../../surface-tension.md#meniscus-liquid) control [systematic error](../../../physics.md#systematic-error). A [titration](../../../chemistry.md#titration) measures the intended [analyte](../../../chemistry.md#analyte) only insofar as interfering substances have been excluded or accounted for.

## 10

↑ **Parent:** [Materia medica, pharmacy](materia-medica-pharmacy.md)

**Original question.**

Give the equivalents of the weights and measures employed in the British Pharmacopœia, in French weights and measures.

<h3 id="10/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [10](#10)

**The 1864 solid units are the [avoirdupois pound](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-pound), [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce) and [grain](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit); the liquid units are the Imperial measures.** The [grain](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit) is unchanged, while the old apothecaries' pound and ounce are not the units of the new formulary. [pp. 16–17, change of weights and unchanged liquid measures](../../../pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md#the-prescriber-s-analysis-of-the-british-pharmacopoeia-by-john-birkbeck-nevins-1864)

The relations for solids are

$$
1\,\mathrm{lb}=16\,\mathrm{oz}=7000\,\mathrm{gr},\qquad
1\,\mathrm{oz}=437.5\,\mathrm{gr}.
$$

Their approximate French metric equivalents are:

- one [grain](../../../classical-mechanics.md#grain-unit): $\boxed{0.0648\,\mathrm{g}}$, about $64.8\,\mathrm{mg}$;
- one [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce): $\boxed{28.35\,\mathrm{g}}$;
- one [avoirdupois pound](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-pound): $\boxed{453.59\,\mathrm{g}}$.

For liquids,

$$
1\,\mathrm{gal}=8\,\mathrm{pt},\qquad
1\,\mathrm{pt}=20\,\mathrm{fl\,oz},\qquad
1\,\mathrm{fl\,oz}=8\,\mathrm{fl\,dr},\qquad
1\,\mathrm{fl\,dr}=60\,\mathrm{min}.
$$

The approximate metric volumes are:

- one [minim](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit): $\boxed{0.0592\,\mathrm{mL}}$;
- one [fluid drachm](../../../physics.md#fluid-drachm): $\boxed{3.552\,\mathrm{mL}}$;
- one [fluid ounce](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce): $\boxed{28.41\,\mathrm{mL}}$;
- one [Imperial pint](../../../physics.md#imperial-pint): $\boxed{568.26\,\mathrm{mL}}$;
- one [Imperial gallon](../../../physics.md#imperial-gallon): $\boxed{4.5461\,\mathrm{L}}$.

A [millilitre](../../../physics.md#millilitre) is a cubic centimetre, and a [litre](../../../physics.md#litre) is a cubic decimetre. These displayed equivalents are rounded; small differences from a period table's last digits reflect rounding and historical calibration conventions. A [fluid ounce](../../../physics.md#fluid-ounce) is a [volume](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#volume) and an [avoirdupois ounce](../../../physics.md#avoirdupois-ounce) is a [mass](../../../classical-mechanics.md#mass). They cannot be interchanged for an arbitrary liquid without its [density](../../../fluid-mechanics.md#density). Likewise, a drop is not a defined [minim](../../../geometry-and-topology.md#minim-unit): its volume depends on the liquid and the dropper.
