**Poisoning by [belladonna](../../../../../belladonna.md) characteristically combines delirium with dry secretions and dilated [pupils](../../../../../pupil.md); poisoning by hydrocyanic acid can cause very rapid collapse with failure of [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md).** The two patterns are distinctive descriptions, not infallible diagnoses from one symptom.

With [belladonna](../../../../../belladonna.md), expect dryness of the mouth and throat, difficulty swallowing, blurred vision and [mydriasis](../../../../../mydriasis.md). The skin may become hot, dry and flushed; the [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) is often rapid. Restlessness, confusion, visual [hallucinations](../../../../../hallucination.md) and agitated [delirium](../../../../../delirium.md) may be prominent: the person may talk incoherently or pick at imaginary objects. Reduced intestinal activity and [urinary retention](../../../../../urinary-retention.md) can accompany the dryness. Severe poisoning can proceed to [seizures](../../../../../seizure.md), depressed [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md), [coma](../../../../../coma-optics.md) and circulatory failure. This is now understood chiefly as an [antimuscarinic poisoning](../../../../../antimuscarinic-poisoning.md) syndrome produced by alkaloids including [atropine](../../../../../atropine.md), which oppose the action of [acetylcholine](../../../../../acetylcholine.md) at [muscarinic receptors](../../../../../muscarinic-acetylcholine-receptor.md).[Background; characteristic central and peripheral signs; severity table](../../../../../anticholinergic-syndrome-by-the-royal-children%E2%80%99s-hospital-melbourne.md)

Hydrocyanic acid is [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md), historically also called prussic acid. Depending on the route and amount, early complaints may include dizziness, [headache](../../../../../headache.md), anxiety, [nausea](../../../../../nausea.md), weakness and a sense of suffocation. Rapid [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) may give way to slow or failing [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md), followed by [seizures](../../../../../seizure.md), [coma](../../../../../coma-optics.md), circulatory collapse and death. A sufficiently large exposure may be so rapid that an elaborate sequence of preliminary symptoms is not observed. There is no single obligatory interval from exposure to collapse.[How cyanide works; signs and symptoms; severity and route of exposure](../../../../../cyanide-chemical-fact-sheet-by-cdc.md)

In modern terms, [cyanide](../../../../../cyanide.md) impairs cellular use of [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), including inhibition of [cytochrome c oxidase](../../../../../cytochrome-c-oxidase.md) in [cellular respiration](../../../../../cellular-respiration.md). An apparently oxygenated colour therefore cannot exclude serious poisoning. A bitter-almond odour is an unreliable clue: it may be absent or not perceived, and no examiner should intentionally smell a suspect sample. The historical term “narcotic poison” does not mean that either [belladonna](../../../../../belladonna.md) or [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) acts like an [opioid](../../../../../opioid.md). The receptor and cellular explanations above are present scientific interpretations, rather than molecular explanations available in 1864.[Signs/symptoms and chemical identification: odour is an inadequate warning](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide-emergency-response-information-by-niosh.md)
