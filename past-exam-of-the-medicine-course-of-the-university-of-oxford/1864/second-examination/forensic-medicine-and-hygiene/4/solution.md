<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**[Oxalic acid poisoning](../../../../../oxalic-acid-poisoning.md) may produce corrosive gastrointestinal injury, early circulatory collapse and later [kidney](../../../../../kidney.md) injury; no single post-mortem appearance proves the diagnosis.** The severity of local injury and systemic poisoning need not be identical in every case.

After ingestion, burning pain in the mouth, throat or upper abdomen may be followed by [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md), sometimes containing [blood](../../../../../blood.md), abdominal tenderness and [diarrhoea](../../../../../diarrhoea.md). Weakness, a small rapid [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), cold clammy skin and collapse may develop. A dilute preparation may produce less obvious surface injury than concentrated [oxalic acid](../../../../../oxalic-acid.md), while still giving systemic poisoning. Current chemical safety information recognizes both corrosive effects and disturbance of [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) balance.[Ingestion hazards and effects of short-term exposure](../../../../../oxalic-acid-international-chemical-safety-card-0529.md)

The absorbed [oxalate](../../../../../oxalate.md) can bind [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) and form [calcium oxalate](../../../../../calcium-oxalate.md). A fall in ionized [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) can contribute to neuromuscular disturbance and cardiac instability, but [hypocalcaemia](../../../../../hypocalcaemia.md) must not be asserted to occur in every case or inferred from a symptom alone. If the person survives the early illness, [acute kidney injury](../../../../../acute-kidney-injury.md) can follow. In modern pathological investigations, [calcium oxalate](../../../../../calcium-oxalate.md) crystals in renal tubules and associated tubular damage provide a mechanism for this later complication.[1967, five patients: plasma oxalate, calcium and tissue deposits](../../../../../plasma-oxalic-acid-and-calcium-levels-in-oxalate-poisoning.md)[Clinical course and renal biopsy: tubular injury with oxalate crystals](../../../../../acute-renal-failure-following-oxalic-acid-poisoning-a-case-report.md)

At [autopsy](../../../../../autopsy.md), examine the mouth, [oesophagus](../../../../../oesophagus.md) and [stomach](../../../../../stomach.md) for corrosive changes. Depending on concentration and survival, the [mucous membrane](../../../../../mucous-membrane.md) may be pale or discoloured, inflamed, eroded, haemorrhagic or softened; blood-stained contents and severe gastric damage may be present. Perforation is possible, but is not a necessary finding. The pre-examination report [Case of Criminal Poisoning with Oxalic Acid, in Which Perforation of the Stomach Took Place by Henry Duncan Littlejohn](../../../../../case-of-criminal-poisoning-with-oxalic-acid-in-which-perforation-of-the-stomach-took-place-by-henry-duncan-littlejohn.md) documents a particular severe example with marked softening and perforation. It is evidence that these changes can occur, rather than a description that every fatal case must reproduce.[1861, pp.13–20: examination of the damaged stomach](../../../../../case-of-criminal-poisoning-with-oxalic-acid-in-which-perforation-of-the-stomach-took-place-by-henry-duncan-littlejohn.md)

Examine the [kidneys](../../../../../kidney.md) as well as the gastrointestinal tract; histological tubular changes and crystals may matter especially after delayed death. Nevertheless, [calcium oxalate](../../../../../calcium-oxalate.md) deposition is not unique to direct ingestion of [oxalic acid](../../../../../oxalic-acid.md), and gross corrosive damage is not unique to this poison. Preserve material for controlled [toxicological analysis](../../../../../toxicological-analysis.md), and interpret the chemical findings alongside the history and pathological distribution. The older classification of [oxalic acid](../../../../../oxalic-acid.md) as a “narcotic” does not adequately explain the corrosive, electrolyte and renal mechanisms now recognized.
