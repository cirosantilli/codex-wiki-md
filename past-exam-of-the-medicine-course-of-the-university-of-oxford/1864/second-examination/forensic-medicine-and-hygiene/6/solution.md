<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**The several methods are natural ventilation by wind or buoyancy, mechanical supply, mechanical extraction, and combinations of these.** Each must provide both a route for clean outdoor [air](../../../../../air.md) to enter and a route for used [air](../../../../../air.md) to leave.

For [natural ventilation](../../../../../natural-ventilation.md), opening windows on different sides permits [cross ventilation](../../../../../cross-ventilation.md) when the [wind](../../../../../wind.md) creates a suitable [pressure difference](../../../../../pressure-difference.md). Even one window can exchange [air](../../../../../air.md), but the amount and distribution are more dependent on conditions. Openings at different heights use the [stack effect](../../../../../stack-effect.md): warmer, less dense indoor [air](../../../../../air.md) tends to rise and leave through an upper opening, drawing replacement [air](../../../../../air.md) through a lower one. A flue or ventilating shaft can extend this route. A fireplace may assist extraction through its chimney, provided replacement [air](../../../../../air.md) enters and combustion products are carried safely away.

Permanent inlets, adjustable window openings and outlet shafts can make these principles more controllable. Their positions should distribute fresh [air](../../../../../air.md) through the occupied part of the ward instead of allowing it to pass directly from inlet to outlet. An open door into an adjoining foul space is not necessarily a source of clean [air](../../../../../air.md). [Florence Nightingale](../../../../../florence-nightingale.md)'s pre-1864 discussions emphasize outside [air](../../../../../air.md), openings and the relation between ventilation and warmth; admitting fresh [air](../../../../../air.md) need not mean exposing a patient to an avoidable cold draught.[Ventilation and warming](../../../../../notes-on-nursing-by-florence-nightingale.md)[Third edition, 1863, discussion of ward ventilation and window arrangements](../../../../../notes-on-hospitals-by-florence-nightingale.md)

With mechanical extraction, a fan or other air-moving apparatus removes indoor [air](../../../../../air.md), while planned inlets admit replacement [air](../../../../../air.md). With mechanical supply, the apparatus introduces outdoor [air](../../../../../air.md), and outlets allow displacement of the used [air](../../../../../air.md). A balanced installation provides both mechanically. Natural openings can also be combined with mechanical assistance. Heating the incoming [air](../../../../../air.md) can reduce chilling without making it necessary to recirculate all the used [air](../../../../../air.md).

What matters is the actual [airflow](../../../../../airflow.md) through the room, including its direction. Outlet flow can be investigated with an [anemometer](../../../../../anemometer.md); short-circuiting between nearby openings and stagnant regions must be considered. Moving the same indoor [air](../../../../../air.md) around does not by itself supply fresh [air](../../../../../air.md). Nor does a perfume remove a source of contamination. Source removal, sufficient exchange and reasonable temperature control belong together; windows or fans are means to those ends, not proof that they have been achieved.
