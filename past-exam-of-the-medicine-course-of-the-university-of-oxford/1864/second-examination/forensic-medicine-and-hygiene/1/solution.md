**Neither the smell of [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) nor a drunken appearance excludes [concussion](../../../../../concussion.md) or a more serious [head injury](../../../../../head-injury.md).** The useful distinction comes from the history, a complete examination and repeated assessment; [alcohol intoxication](../../../../../alcohol-intoxication.md) and [head injury](../../../../../head-injury.md) may occur together.

First establish the order of events. Witnesses may describe drinking followed by progressive slurring, unsteadiness and drowsiness, or a blow or fall followed immediately by altered [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md), confusion or a gap in [human memory](../../../../../human-memory.md). Ask about the amount and timing of the drink, the mechanism of injury and any interval of apparent recovery. A person may have fallen because of [alcohol intoxication](../../../../../alcohol-intoxication.md), and subsequently have suffered a [head injury](../../../../../head-injury.md). Conversely, an injured person may be mistakenly described as drunk because of slurred speech and an unsteady gait.

Examine the level of [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md), orientation, speech, [human memory](../../../../../human-memory.md) and ability to follow commands. In [alcohol intoxication](../../../../../alcohol-intoxication.md), disinhibition, slurred speech, incoordination, [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md) and increasing drowsiness can occur; severe poisoning can progress to [coma](../../../../../coma-optics.md) and impaired [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md). After [concussion](../../../../../concussion.md), confusion, [amnesia](../../../../../amnesia.md), [headache](../../../../../headache.md), dizziness, [nausea](../../../../../nausea.md) and [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md) may occur, with or without an observed loss of [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md). Thus [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md) or drowsiness alone does not decide the diagnosis.[Symptoms of alcohol poisoning](../../../../../alcohol-poisoning-by-the-national-health-service.md)[Symptoms and warning signs after a head injury](../../../../../head-injury-and-concussion-by-the-national-health-service.md)

Look for a scalp wound, swelling or other evidence of impact, and examine the [pupils](../../../../../pupil.md), their reaction to light, limb movements and any focal [neurological deficit](../../../../../neurological-deficit.md). Unequal [pupils](../../../../../pupil.md), one-sided weakness, a worsening level of [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md) or a [seizure](../../../../../seizure.md) should prompt concern for significant [traumatic brain injury](../../../../../traumatic-brain-injury.md), rather than be dismissed as ordinary drunkenness or uncomplicated [concussion](../../../../../concussion.md). The absence of an external wound does not exclude an internal injury. Nor are a flushed face, a particular [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) or the smell of drink sufficiently specific to settle the question.

The defensible diagnostic conclusion may initially be uncertainty requiring observation and investigation. Current guidance specifically requires important [traumatic brain injury](../../../../../traumatic-brain-injury.md) to be excluded before depressed [consciousness](../../../../../consciousness.md) is attributed to intoxication. Modern [computed tomography](../../../../../computed-tomography.md) and measurement of [blood glucose](../../../../../blood-sugar.md) can resolve problems that an 1864 bedside examination could not; these are later diagnostic aids, not equipment to attribute to the examination's period.[Recommendations 1.4.1–1.4.2: initial assessment and intoxication](../../../../../head-injury-assessment-and-early-management-by-nice.md)
