<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

**[Glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) can be obtained by [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) of [starch](../../../../../starch.md) and detected as a [reducing sugar](../../../../../reducing-sugar.md) by a heated alkaline [copper(II)](../../../../../copper-ii.md) test. A positive copper test alone does not uniquely identify [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md).**

Suspend [starch](../../../../../starch.md) in [water](../../../../../water.md), warm it to make a paste, and heat with a dilute [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md), such as dilute [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md), to cause [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) of the [glycosidic bonds](../../../../../glycosidic-bond.md). The [chemical reaction](../../../../../chemical-reaction-split.md) passes through shorter [carbohydrates](../../../../../carbohydrate.md); sufficient [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) ultimately gives [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md). The usual repeat-unit accounting is

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{(C_6H_{10}O_5)_n+nH_2O\longrightarrow nC_6H_{12}O_6}.}
$$

This idealized [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) formula omits end-group details. The [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) catalyses cleavage and is not consumed in the displayed equation. Merely using [amylase](../../../../../amylase.md) from malt should not be described as necessarily producing pure [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md); it can leave [maltose](../../../../../maltose.md) and shorter [polysaccharides](../../../../../polysaccharide.md).

When [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) is used, neutralization with [calcium carbonate](../../../../../calcium-carbonate.md) removes the acidity and much of the [sulfate](../../../../../sulfate.md) as [calcium sulfate](../../../../../calcium-sulfate.md). Filter out the insoluble residue, then concentrate the [chemical solution](../../../../../solution-chemistry.md) gently and crystallize the [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md); further [recrystallization](../../../../../recrystallization.md) is required to distinguish a reasonably pure [chemical substance](../../../../../chemical-substance-split.md) from a crude syrup. Overheating and unnecessarily prolonged strong [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) treatment degrade the [sugar](../../../../../sugar.md). [Hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) of [sucrose](../../../../../sucrose.md) is a less selective preparative answer because it gives [fructose](../../../../../fructose.md) alongside [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md).

For the historical copper test, use freshly mixed [Fehling's reagent](../../../../../fehling-s-reagent.md): an aqueous [copper(II) sulfate](../../../../../copper-ii-sulfate.md) portion and an alkaline [tartrate](../../../../../tartrate.md) portion. The [tartrate](../../../../../tartrate.md) keeps [copper(II)](../../../../../copper-ii.md) in [coordination complexes](../../../../../coordination-complex.md) instead of allowing immediate bulk precipitation of [copper(II) hydroxide](../../../../../copper-ii-hydroxide.md). Add a suitably prepared sample to a small test portion and warm in a hot [water bath](../../../../../water-bath.md). A yellow-orange to brick-red [precipitate](../../../../../precipitate.md) of [copper(I) oxide](../../../../../copper-i-oxide.md) indicates [reduction](../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) of the [copper(II)](../../../../../copper-ii.md) reagent. Run a known [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) sample and a [water](../../../../../water.md) blank alongside it. In a simple sample, a visible difference between these controls helps distinguish a true positive from a deteriorated reagent or contamination.[Theory, uses of Fehling's solution and formation of red copper(I) oxide](../../../../../estimation-of-glucose-by-amrita-vishwa-vidyapeetham.md)

The key [redox](../../../../../redox.md) accounting on the [copper](../../../../../copper.md) side is

$$
\mathrm{2Cu^{2+}+2OH^-+2e^-\longrightarrow Cu_2O+H_2O}.
$$

The required [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) come from [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) of the [sugar](../../../../../sugar.md). This half-equation deliberately omits the changing [tartrate](../../../../../tartrate.md) [ligands](../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) and does not assign a unique organic product. The often printed claim that hot alkaline [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) simply becomes [gluconate](../../../../../gluconate.md) in this test is too simple: competing [isomerization](../../../../../isomerization.md), [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) and fragmentation make the organic chemistry more complicated.[§2.3, interpretation of copper tests and product complexity](../../../../../the-iodine-test-for-reducing-sugars-by-holger-fleischer.md)

[Fructose](../../../../../fructose.md), [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md), [maltose](../../../../../maltose.md) and various other [reducing substances](../../../../../reducing-substance.md) can also give a positive [Fehling's test](../../../../../fehling-s-test.md). In particular, [fructose](../../../../../fructose.md) can form [aldoses](../../../../../aldose.md) by [tautomerization](../../../../../tautomerization.md) in [alkaline solution](../../../../../alkaline-solution.md); an initial [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) functional group does not make it reliably negative.[Oxidation of Monosaccharides; fructose rearrangement](../../../../../organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/25-6-reactions-of-monosaccharides.md) Fermentation by suitable [yeast](../../../../../yeast.md), with [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) formation, and [optical rotation](../../../../../optical-rotation.md) can supply further historical evidence, but neither is individually specific to [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md). These are anticipated diagnostic observations, not measurements supplied by the paper. In present laboratory practice an appropriately validated [enzymatic glucose assay](../../../../../enzymatic-glucose-assay.md) can establish [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) more selectively; the old reducing-sugar test is not by itself a diagnosis of [diabetes](../../../../../diabetes.md).[Monitoring: Diagnosis, blood tests and glucose-oxidase assay explanation](../../../../../diagnosis-of-diabetes-in-pdb-101.md)
