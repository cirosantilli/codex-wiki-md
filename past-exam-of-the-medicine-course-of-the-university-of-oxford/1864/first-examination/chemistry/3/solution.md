<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Observe each gas volume, temperature, pressure and moisture condition, and reduce both volumes to the same temperature, pressure and dry-gas basis.** Equal scale readings alone do not establish equal amounts of [gas](../../../../../gas.md).

Read the calibrated [volume](../../../../../volume.md) of each [gas](../../../../../gas.md) in a graduated tube or [eudiometer](../../../../../eudiometer.md), with the eye at the [meniscus](../../../../../meniscus-liquid.md) to avoid [parallax](../../../../../parallax.md). Allow the [gas](../../../../../gas.md) and the surrounding bath to reach [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md), then read a [thermometer](../../../../../thermometer.md) close to the [gas](../../../../../gas.md). Heating expands a fixed amount of [gas](../../../../../gas.md), so a larger reading could reflect a higher [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) rather than more [gas](../../../../../gas.md). The [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) used in a [gas law](../../../../../gas-law.md) must be an [thermodynamic temperature](../../../../../thermodynamic-temperature.md).

Read the atmospheric [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) with a [barometer](../../../../../barometer.md). If a [gas](../../../../../gas.md) is confined over a [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md), also observe the difference between the inside and outside [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) levels. With $h$ the internal surface height above the external surface, the total internal [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) is

$$
p_g=p_{\mathrm{atm}}-\rho_\ell gh.
$$

Here $\rho_\ell$ is the confining [density](../../../../../density.md) of the [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) and $g$ the [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md); the sign reverses automatically when $h$ is negative. Equalizing the levels before taking the [volume](../../../../../volume.md) reading removes this [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) correction. A [mercury barometer](../../../../../mercury-barometer.md) can itself require [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and calibration corrections for accurate work.

If the [gas](../../../../../gas.md) is saturated with [water vapor](../../../../../water-vapor.md), subtract the [vapor pressure](../../../../../vapor-pressure.md) of [water](../../../../../water.md) at its measured [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md):

$$
p_{\mathrm{dry}}=p_g-p_{\mathrm{H_2O}}(T).
$$

The observed [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) includes both the test [gas](../../../../../gas.md) and [water vapor](../../../../../water-vapor.md); counting it all as test [gas](../../../../../gas.md) overestimates the amount. The saturation assumption needs time for equilibration. For a dry [gas](../../../../../gas.md), no [water vapor](../../../../../water-vapor.md) subtraction is made; another collecting [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) may require its own [vapor pressure](../../../../../vapor-pressure.md) correction. A collecting [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md) must also be chosen so that it neither appreciably dissolves nor reacts with the [gas](../../../../../gas.md): [water](../../../../../water.md), for instance, is unsuitable for preserving the whole amount of freely soluble [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md).

For dilute [gases](../../../../../gas.md) obeying the [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md), the [volume](../../../../../volume.md) $V_0$ at a common chosen $T_0,p_0$ is

$$
\boxed{V_0=V\frac{p_{\mathrm{dry}}}{p_0}\frac{T_0}{T}.}
$$

Consequently the corrected comparison is

$$
\boxed{\frac{V_{1,0}}{V_{2,0}}=\frac{V_1}{V_2}\frac{p_{1,\mathrm{dry}}}{p_{2,\mathrm{dry}}}\frac{T_2}{T_1}.}
$$

This is also the ratio of [amounts of substance](../../../../../amount-of-substance.md) in the [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) approximation. Check for leaks, trapped [air](../../../../../air.md), changing composition and incomplete transfer. At high [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) or near [condensation](../../../../../condensation.md), [real gas](../../../../../real-gas.md) behaviour may require [compressibility factors](../../../../../compressibility-factor.md); with $pV=ZnRT$, the inferred amount ratio contains the further factor $Z_2/Z_1$.
