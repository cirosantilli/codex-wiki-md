<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**[Cow's milk](../../../../../cow-s-milk.md) contains [water](../../../../../water.md), dispersed [fat](../../../../../fat.md), [casein](../../../../../casein.md), soluble [milk proteins](../../../../../milk-protein.md), [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md) and [mineral](../../../../../mineral.md) constituents, with smaller amounts of other substances.** These constituents occupy different physical states, which makes their separation possible: the [fat](../../../../../fat.md) is present as an [emulsion](../../../../../emulsion.md), much of the [casein](../../../../../casein.md) as [colloidal](../../../../../colloid.md) [casein micelles](../../../../../casein-micelle.md), and the [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md) and many [salts](../../../../../salt-chemistry.md) in [chemical solution](../../../../../solution-chemistry.md). [Vitamins](../../../../../vitamin.md), [enzymes](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) and small nitrogenous constituents accompany these main fractions. The proportions vary between samples and are not fixed by the name [cow's milk](../../../../../cow-s-milk.md).[chapter 2, principal constituents and Tables 2.1–2.2](../../../../../the-chemistry-of-milk-in-the-dairy-processing-handbook.md)

First let fresh unhomogenized [milk](../../../../../milk.md) stand so that the less dense [fat globules](../../../../../fat-globule.md) rise as [cream](../../../../../cream.md); skimming gives a [fat](../../../../../fat.md)-enriched layer and [skimmed milk](../../../../../skimmed-milk.md) beneath. This separates fractions rather than pure [chemical substances](../../../../../chemical-substance-split.md): [cream](../../../../../cream.md) still contains [water](../../../../../water.md) and other constituents. Churning [cream](../../../../../cream.md) coalesces the [fat globules](../../../../../fat-globule.md) into butter; extraction of a separately prepared dried portion with a suitable organic [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) provides another way to isolate [fat](../../../../../fat.md). A modern [centrifugal separator](../../../../../centrifugal-separator.md) accelerates the same density-based separation, but it is not needed to explain an 1864 bench separation.

Next add dilute [acetic acid](../../../../../acetic-acid.md) carefully to [skimmed milk](../../../../../skimmed-milk.md). Lowering its [pH](../../../../../ph.md) toward the [isoelectric point](../../../../../isoelectric-point.md) of [casein](../../../../../casein.md), about 4.6, produces a curd that can be collected by [filtration](../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) and washed. Excess [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md), retained [fat](../../../../../fat.md), and shifts of [calcium phosphate](../../../../../calcium-phosphate.md) between curd and liquid limit purity. [Rennet](../../../../../rennet.md) offers another route to a curd, but its [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md)-catalysed action and the [mineral](../../../../../mineral.md) content of the resulting curd differ from simple [acid precipitation](../../../../../acid-precipitation.md); the two should not be treated as identical mechanisms.[chapter 2, Casein, precipitation and acidification](../../../../../the-chemistry-of-milk-in-the-dairy-processing-handbook.md)

The liquid after removal of the curd is [whey](../../../../../whey.md). Heat this fraction under suitable acidity to induce [protein denaturation](../../../../../denaturation-biochemistry.md) and aggregate much of its residual [whey protein](../../../../../whey-protein.md); collect the coagulum by [filtration](../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md). Older descriptions commonly grouped this fraction under “albumin”, but modern [milk chemistry](../../../../../milk-chemistry.md) distinguishes several [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md). Heating alone does not make every minor constituent fall into a single pure fraction.[chapter 18, Recovery of denatured whey proteins](../../../../../milk-and-whey-fractionation-in-the-dairy-processing-handbook.md)

Concentrate the deproteinized [whey](../../../../../whey.md) and cool it to crystallize [lactose](../../../../../lactose.md). Filter and wash the crystals, then purify them by [recrystallization](../../../../../recrystallization.md); dissolved [salts](../../../../../salt-chemistry.md) and residual [organic matter](../../../../../organic-matter.md) remain substantially in the mother liquor. This provides a separation from most [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md), not an automatic complete separation from all [minerals](../../../../../mineral.md).[chapter 18, Lactose recovery; Crystallisation; Lactose separation](../../../../../milk-and-whey-fractionation-in-the-dairy-processing-handbook.md)

Finally use separate measured portions for [water](../../../../../water.md) and [mineral](../../../../../mineral.md) determinations. Controlled drying gives an operational [total solids](../../../../../total-solids.md) measurement; subtraction from the initial [mass](../../../../../mass.md) estimates the [water](../../../../../water.md) lost. Ignition of a dried portion destroys most [organic matter](../../../../../organic-matter.md) and leaves [ash](../../../../../ash.md) for [mineral analysis](../../../../../mineral-analysis.md). [Ash](../../../../../ash.md) is a transformed residue: it does not preserve all original [salts](../../../../../salt-chemistry.md) unchanged, and some constituents can volatilize. [Calcium](../../../../../calcium.md), [phosphorus](../../../../../phosphorus.md), [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md), [potassium](../../../../../potassium.md) and [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md) are among the important [mineral](../../../../../mineral.md) constituents. **Skimming, curd formation, heat coagulation, lactose crystallization, drying and ashing separate or determine the principal fractions; none alone constitutes a complete pure-component analysis.**
