<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**A suitable historical answer gives both the chemical transformation and the means of isolating a reasonably pure product.** The following are preparative principles, with modern [chemical formulas](../../../../../chemical-formula.md); they are not instructions for self-preparation of medicines. In particular, [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md), [mercury compounds](../../../../../mercury-compound.md) and [arsenic compounds](../../../../../arsenic-compound.md) present serious [toxicity](../../../../../toxicity.md) hazards. [Hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) is extremely volatile, can be absorbed by several routes, and its odour is an unreliable warning.[Agent Characteristics; Routes of Exposure; warning that smell does not provide adequate protection](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide-emergency-response-information-by-niosh.md)

For [hydrocyanic acid](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md), a customary early nineteenth-century route is the controlled [distillation](../../../../../distillation.md) of [potassium ferrocyanide](../../../../../potassium-ferrocyanide.md) with dilute [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) and [water](../../../../../water.md). [Hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) passes into the distillate; nonvolatile [salts](../../../../../salt-chemistry.md) remain behind. A generating flask, efficient [laboratory condenser](../../../../../laboratory-condenser.md) and chilled receiver are needed because [hydrogen cyanide](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) is readily lost. The contemporary account of [hydrocyanic acid](../../../../../hydrogen-cyanide.md) preparation in [Pharmacopoeia Castrensis Ruthenica in The British and Foreign Medical Review (1842)](../../../../../pharmacopoeia-castrensis-ruthenica-in-the-british-and-foreign-medical-review-1842.md) specifically describes the [ferrocyanide](../../../../../ferrocyanide.md)–[sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) method and its losses on imperfect condensation. The [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) must be determined analytically before any defined dilution: a nominal starting mixture does not establish the final strength.[Processes for obtaining Hydrocyanic Acid](../../../../../pharmacopoeia-castrensis-ruthenica-in-the-british-and-foreign-medical-review-1842.md) At the level of the released [cyanide](../../../../../cyanide.md), the essential [acid-base reaction](../../../../../acid-base-reaction.md) is $\mathrm{CN^-+H^+\rightarrow HCN}$. This equation alone is not a complete equation for decomposing the stable [ferrocyanide](../../../../../ferrocyanide.md) [coordination complex](../../../../../coordination-complex.md). Concentrated [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) can lead to different decomposition chemistry, so the specified dilution is meaningful.

For [formic acid](../../../../../formic-acid.md), gently heat [oxalic acid](../../../../../oxalic-acid.md) with [glycerol](../../../../../glycerol.md) and some [water](../../../../../water.md), then separate the aqueous [formic acid](../../../../../formic-acid.md) by [distillation](../../../../../distillation.md). The useful net transformation is

$$
\mathrm{H_2C_2O_4\longrightarrow HCOOH+CO_2}.
$$

The [glycerol](../../../../../glycerol.md) participates through intermediate [esters](../../../../../ester.md) and is regenerated rather than consumed in this net equation. [Marcellin Berthelot](../../../../../marcellin-berthelot.md)'s 1856 account describes the [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) evolution, subsequent [water](../../../../../water.md) addition and [distillation](../../../../../distillation.md), and reuse of the residual [glycerol](../../../../../glycerol.md). Overheating is disadvantageous because the [formic acid](../../../../../formic-acid.md) can decompose further to [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) and [water](../../../../../water.md). The method therefore separates a volatile [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) from a much less volatile carrier; it is not merely the dry destructive heating of [oxalic acid](../../../../../oxalic-acid.md).[Polytechnisches Journal 140 (1856), p.78, report of the March 1856 communication](../../../../../verfahren-zur-darstellung-der-ameisensaure-by-marcellin-berthelot.md)

For [oxalic acid](../../../../../oxalic-acid.md), oxidize a [carbohydrate](../../../../../carbohydrate.md), such as [sucrose](../../../../../sucrose.md) or [starch](../../../../../starch.md), with [nitric acid](../../../../../nitric-acid.md); concentrate the resulting [chemical solution](../../../../../solution-chemistry.md) appropriately and cool it to obtain crystals, followed by [recrystallization](../../../../../recrystallization.md). [Nitrogen oxides](../../../../../nitrogen-oxides.md) evolve, and excessive [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) can carry [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) onward to [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), so an idealized single-product equation would conceal losses. The use of [starch](../../../../../starch.md) and [nitric acid](../../../../../nitric-acid.md) for [oxalic acid](../../../../../oxalic-acid.md) production is explicitly documented in an 1850 account of [nitric oxide](../../../../../nitrogen-monoxide.md) recovery.[p.466, “Verfahren das Salpetergas in Salpetersäure zu verwandeln”, opening example](../../../../../polytechnisches-journal-volume-117-1850.md) An alternative already established before 1864 is alkaline treatment of [sawdust](../../../../../sawdust.md), producing an [oxalate](../../../../../oxalate.md) which can be purified through sparingly soluble [calcium oxalate](../../../../../calcium-oxalate.md).[report on chemical manufacture in South Lancashire, Oxalsäure section: alkaline sawdust route](../../../../../polytechnisches-journal-volume-162-1861.md) Decomposing the isolated [calcium oxalate](../../../../../calcium-oxalate.md) with [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) separates much of the [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) as [calcium sulfate](../../../../../calcium-sulfate.md):

$$
\mathrm{CaC_2O_4+H_2SO_4\longrightarrow H_2C_2O_4+CaSO_4}.
$$

Filter off the [calcium sulfate](../../../../../calcium-sulfate.md) and crystallize the [oxalic acid](../../../../../oxalic-acid.md) from the filtrate. The usual crystals contain two [water of crystallization](../../../../../water-of-crystallization.md); a calculation for the anhydrous [acid](../../../../../acid-chemistry.md) must not treat their entire [mass](../../../../../mass.md) as $\mathrm{H_2C_2O_4}$.

For [calomel](../../../../../mercury-i-chloride.md), intimately combine [mercury(II) sulfate](../../../../../mercury-ii-sulfate.md), metallic [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md) and dry [sodium chloride](../../../../../sodium-chloride.md), then collect the volatile [mercury(I) chloride](../../../../../mercury-i-chloride.md) in a cooler receiving chamber. The net change is

$$
\mathrm{HgSO_4+Hg+2NaCl\longrightarrow Hg_2Cl_2+Na_2SO_4}.
$$

The extra metallic [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md) lowers the average [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) from +2 to +1. The product is purified by appropriate [sublimation](../../../../../sublimation.md) and washing to remove soluble contamination, particularly [mercury(II) chloride](../../../../../mercury-ii-chloride.md). Both the [chemical composition](../../../../../chemical-composition.md) and the fine subdivision of the product matter: condensation through a chamber can give a powder rather than an adherent crystalline crust. This physical part of [calomel](../../../../../mercury-i-chloride.md) preparation is described in the 1843 primary account in [Polytechnisches Journal volume 87 (1843)](../../../../../polytechnisches-journal-volume-87-1843.md); the account's historical therapeutic claims are not adopted here.[pp.209 onward, “Ueber Darstellung des Calomels als höchst zartes Pulver”, condensation and collecting chamber](../../../../../polytechnisches-journal-volume-87-1843.md)

For [corrosive sublimate](../../../../../mercury-ii-chloride.md), heat dry [mercury(II) sulfate](../../../../../mercury-ii-sulfate.md) with [sodium chloride](../../../../../sodium-chloride.md) and collect the [mercury(II) chloride](../../../../../mercury-ii-chloride.md) in the cooler part of a suitable [sublimation](../../../../../sublimation.md) apparatus:

$$
\mathrm{HgSO_4+2NaCl\longrightarrow HgCl_2+Na_2SO_4}.
$$

Here no metallic [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md) is added. Nonvolatile [sodium sulfate](../../../../../sodium-sulfate.md) stays behind, while [mercury(II) chloride](../../../../../mercury-ii-chloride.md) is transferred and condensed. Further [sublimation](../../../../../sublimation.md) or [recrystallization](../../../../../recrystallization.md) separates impurities. [Mercury, p.141, bichloride preparation and the following calomel account](../../../../../dictionary-of-arts-manufactures-and-mines-by-andrew-ure.md) The crucial distinction from [calomel](../../../../../mercury-i-chloride.md) preparation is whether [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md) remains in the +2 [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) or is converted to +1.

For the solid called [arsenious acid](../../../../../arsenic-trioxide.md), roast an [arsenic](../../../../../arsenic.md)-bearing [ore](../../../../../ore.md) in [air](../../../../../air.md) and collect the volatile [arsenic trioxide](../../../../../arsenic-trioxide.md) in cooler chambers; purify by resublimation. The essential [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) is represented by

$$
\mathrm{4As+3O_2\longrightarrow2As_2O_3}.
$$

Actual [ores](../../../../../ore.md) also contain other [chemical elements](../../../../../chemical-element.md), so their roasting equations and gaseous by-products depend on composition. A contemporary 1860 report describes roasting arseniferous [ores](../../../../../ore.md), collecting the volatile [arsenic trioxide](../../../../../arsenic-trioxide.md) in chambers and refining it by another [sublimation](../../../../../sublimation.md). It also demonstrates that apparently good material could contain [antimony(III) oxide](../../../../../antimony-iii-oxide.md): physical appearance alone does not establish purity.[p.77, report on the antimony content of glassy arsenious acid from Andreasberg](../../../../../polytechnisches-journal-volume-156-1860.md) The product intended here is dry $\mathrm{As_2O_3}$; dissolving it does not make the bottled solid a pure crystalline hydrate $\mathrm{H_3AsO_3}$.

For [tartar emetic](../../../../../potassium-antimony-tartrate.md), react [antimony(III) oxide](../../../../../antimony-iii-oxide.md) with [potassium hydrogen tartrate](../../../../../potassium-hydrogen-tartrate.md) in [water](../../../../../water.md). A small amount of [water](../../../../../water.md) first gives intimate contact in a paste; subsequent heating with more [water](../../../../../water.md) completes formation of the soluble [potassium antimony tartrate](../../../../../potassium-antimony-tartrate.md). Filter away unreacted insoluble matter and cool the clear filtrate to crystallize the product. This sequence is directly described in a January 1864 discussion of the newly issued [British Pharmacopoeia](../../../../../british-pharmacopoeia.md), rather than inferred from its later editions.[30 January 1864, p.53, Antimonium Tartaratum: paste, standing, boiling and the oxide–bitartrate route](../../../../../chemical-news-volume-9-1864.md) In the traditional antimonyl notation the underlying reaction, before assigning crystal [water of crystallization](../../../../../water-of-crystallization.md), is

$$
\mathrm{Sb_2O_3+2KHC_4H_4O_6\longrightarrow2K(SbO)C_4H_4O_6+H_2O}.
$$

This notation expresses the overall composition; [tartrate](../../../../../tartrate.md) [coordination complexes](../../../../../coordination-complex.md) and [water of crystallization](../../../../../water-of-crystallization.md) need the qualification given in [4](../4.md). The historical pharmaceutical use of [tartar emetic](../../../../../potassium-antimony-tartrate.md) does not make it a safe contemporary emetic.[Toxicity; gastrointestinal effects and systemic poisoning](../../../../../antimony-potassium-tartrate-by-the-national-poisons-information-service.md)

For [potassium chlorate](../../../../../potassium-chlorate.md), pass [chlorine](../../../../../chlorine.md) into hot concentrated [potassium hydroxide](../../../../../potassium-hydroxide.md). The [disproportionation](../../../../../disproportionation.md) gives [potassium chloride](../../../../../potassium-chloride.md) together with [potassium chlorate](../../../../../potassium-chlorate.md):

$$
\mathrm{3Cl_2+6KOH\longrightarrow KClO_3+5KCl+3H_2O}.
$$

Concentration and cooling permit [fractional crystallization](../../../../../fractional-crystallization.md) of the much less soluble cold-water [potassium chlorate](../../../../../potassium-chlorate.md), leaving much of the [potassium chloride](../../../../../potassium-chloride.md) in the mother liquor; [recrystallization](../../../../../recrystallization.md) improves purity. Cold dilute [alkali](../../../../../alkali.md) instead favours [hypochlorite](../../../../../hypochlorite.md), so the heating condition is essential. An economical contemporary manufacturing alternative uses hot [calcium hydroxide](../../../../../calcium-hydroxide.md) and [potassium chloride](../../../../../potassium-chloride.md); the overall accounting is

$$
\mathrm{6Cl_2+6Ca(OH)_2+2KCl\longrightarrow2KClO_3+6CaCl_2+6H_2O}.
$$

The 1850 account in [Polytechnisches Journal volume 118 (1850)](../../../../../polytechnisches-journal-volume-118-1850.md) describes a steam-heated stirred vessel and recovery by cooling, rather than an electrical manufacturing process introduced retrospectively.[pp.424 onward, “Die neue Bereitungsart des chlorsauren Kalis”](../../../../../polytechnisches-journal-volume-118-1850.md)
