<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

**At the same temperature and pressure, one volume of HCl corresponds to $\tfrac12$ volume of hydrogen and $\tfrac12$ volume of chlorine; one of steam to one of hydrogen and $\tfrac12$ of oxygen; one of $\mathrm{N_2O}$ to one of nitrogen and $\tfrac12$ of oxygen; one of NO to $\tfrac12$ of each element; and one of ammonia to $\tfrac12$ of nitrogen and $\tfrac32$ of hydrogen.** All elemental [volumes](../../../../../volume.md) here refer to their ordinary diatomic [gases](../../../../../gas.md).

The simplest way to avoid an erroneous factor of two is to balance each [chemical reaction](../../../../../chemical-reaction-split.md) using $\mathrm{H_2}$, $\mathrm{Cl_2}$, $\mathrm{N_2}$ and $\mathrm{O_2}$. Under [Avogadro's law](../../../../../avogadro-s-law.md), their [stoichiometric coefficients](../../../../../stoichiometric-coefficient.md) are also [gas volume](../../../../../gas-volume.md) ratios, provided all [gases](../../../../../gas.md) are compared at the same [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) and [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) behaviour is a good approximation.

- For gaseous [hydrogen chloride](../../../../../hydrogen-chloride.md), $\mathrm{H_2+Cl_2\rightarrow2HCl}$. Thus $\boxed{V_{\mathrm{HCl}}:V_{\mathrm{H_2}}:V_{\mathrm{Cl_2}}=2:1:1}$. There is no change in total [gas volume](../../../../../gas-volume.md) in this idealized combination.
- For [water vapor](../../../../../water-vapor.md), $\mathrm{2H_2+O_2\rightarrow2H_2O(g)}$. Thus $\boxed{V_{\mathrm{H_2O(g)}}:V_{\mathrm{H_2}}:V_{\mathrm{O_2}}=2:2:1}$: three reactant [volumes](../../../../../volume.md) give two product [volumes](../../../../../volume.md).
- “Protoxide of nitrogen” is [nitrous oxide](../../../../../nitrous-oxide.md), $\mathrm{N_2O}$. The composition equation $\mathrm{2N_2+O_2\rightarrow2N_2O}$ gives $\boxed{V_{\mathrm{N_2O}}:V_{\mathrm{N_2}}:V_{\mathrm{O_2}}=2:2:1}$. This equation expresses composition; it does not assert that direct mixing gives an easy preparative reaction.
- “Binoxide of nitrogen” in the historical equivalent nomenclature is [nitric oxide](../../../../../nitrogen-monoxide.md), modern $\mathrm{NO}$, rather than modern [nitrogen dioxide](../../../../../nitrogen-dioxide.md). The equation $\mathrm{N_2+O_2\rightarrow2NO}$ gives $\boxed{V_{\mathrm{NO}}:V_{\mathrm{N_2}}:V_{\mathrm{O_2}}=2:1:1}$. Older $\mathrm{NO_2}$ notation with [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) equivalent 8 describes the same oxygen-to-nitrogen mass ratio as modern $\mathrm{NO}$ with [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) atomic weight 16; reading its subscript by modern rules changes the substance.[Nitrogen, p.115, “Binoxide of Nitrogen; Nitric Oxide”, older formula and equal elemental volumes](../../../../../review-of-chemistry-for-students-by-john-g-murphy.md)
- For [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md), $\mathrm{N_2+3H_2\rightarrow2NH_3}$ gives $\boxed{V_{\mathrm{NH_3}}:V_{\mathrm{N_2}}:V_{\mathrm{H_2}}=2:1:3}$: four reactant [volumes](../../../../../volume.md) correspond to two product [volumes](../../../../../volume.md).

If the common chosen [volume](../../../../../volume.md) of each compound is $V$, the corresponding ordered elemental [volumes](../../../../../volume.md) are therefore $(V/2,V/2)$, $(V,V/2)$, $(V,V/2)$, $(V/2,V/2)$ and $(V/2,3V/2)$. The ratios concern molecular counts and hypothetical free elemental [gases](../../../../../gas.md), not separate compartments of elemental [gas](../../../../../gas.md) still present inside a compound.

“Hydrochloric acid” must here mean gaseous [hydrogen chloride](../../../../../hydrogen-chloride.md), rather than its aqueous [chemical solution](../../../../../solution-chemistry.md); [water](../../../../../water.md) must remain as [steam](../../../../../steam.md). If [water](../../../../../water.md) condenses or [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) dissolves in the collecting [liquid](../../../../../liquid.md), measured [volumes](../../../../../volume.md) no longer show these simple ratios. For an experimental comparison, dry-gas and [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) corrections are those discussed in [3](../3.md).
