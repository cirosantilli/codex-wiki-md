<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**The molecular formula is $\boxed{\mathrm{C_4H_8O_2}}$, interpreting the vapour density as relative to air.** The [organic elemental analysis](../../../../../organic-elemental-analysis.md) first determines a simpler [empirical formula](../../../../../empirical-formula.md); the [vapor-density molar mass measurement](../../../../../vapor-density-molar-mass-measurement.md) then fixes its multiple.

Take 100 mass units of the [chemical substance](../../../../../chemical-substance-split.md) and divide each elemental contribution by its rounded [relative atomic mass](../../../../../relative-atomic-mass.md):

$$
 n_{\mathrm C}:n_{\mathrm H}:n_{\mathrm O}
 =\frac{54.35}{12}:\frac{9.11}{1}:\frac{36.54}{16}
 =4.5292:9.1100:2.2838
 \simeq 2:4:1.
$$

The [empirical formula](../../../../../empirical-formula.md) is therefore $\mathrm{C_2H_4O}$, with [empirical formula mass](../../../../../empirical-formula-mass.md) approximately 44. Its calculated [mass percentages](../../../../../mass-percentage.md) are 54.55% [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), 9.09% [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) and 36.36% [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). The small discrepancies from the given [organic elemental analysis](../../../../../organic-elemental-analysis.md) are consistent with rounded experimental data; they should not be converted into large, meaningless integer subscripts.

For equal [volumes](../../../../../volume.md) of [ideal gases](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) at equal [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), the [density](../../../../../density.md) ratio equals the [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md) ratio. On the [air](../../../../../air.md) convention,

$$
M\simeq 3.05\times28.97\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}=88.36\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}},\qquad
\frac{M}{44\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}}\simeq2.
$$

Doubling $\mathrm{C_2H_4O}$ gives $\mathrm{C_4H_8O_2}$; its rounded [relative molecular mass](../../../../../relative-molecular-mass.md) of 88 predicts an [air](../../../../../air.md)-relative [vapor density](../../../../../vapor-density.md) of approximately $88/28.97=3.04$, close to 3.05. If the stated [vapor density](../../../../../vapor-density.md) were instead relative to [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md), it would imply a [relative molecular mass](../../../../../relative-molecular-mass.md) near 6.1, smaller than the [empirical formula mass](../../../../../empirical-formula-mass.md) and incompatible with the analysis. That interpretation cannot describe a pure, undecomposed molecular [vapor](../../../../../vapor.md) with the stated composition.

Under the older [equivalent weight](../../../../../equivalent-weight.md) convention $\mathrm C=6$, $\mathrm O=8$, $\mathrm H=1$, the same composition and formula weight could be written $\mathrm{C_8H_8O_4}$. This is a change of notation, not eight modern [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) [chemical atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) in the [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md). The result specifies composition and [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md); it does not distinguish among [structural isomers](../../../../../structural-isomer.md), such as [ethyl acetate](../../../../../ethyl-acetate.md) and [butyric acid](../../../../../butyric-acid.md).
