**[Rigor mortis](../../../../../rigor-mortis.md) is a temporary postmortem loss of muscular extensibility caused principally by depletion of [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) and persistent [actin](../../../../../actin.md)–[myosin](../../../../../myosin.md) attachment.** Its onset, intensity and disappearance depend on the previous state of the [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) and its environment; it is not a fixed clock.

After an initial period of relaxation, surviving [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) [metabolism](../../../../../metabolism-split.md) briefly maintains some [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md). When supplies and regeneration fail, cross-bridges cannot detach normally, while disturbed [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) handling facilitates attachment and tension. The resulting stiffness is a local physicochemical change in [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md), not a continuing voluntary contraction and not evidence that its motor [nerves](../../../../../nerve.md) remain alive. Later degradation of [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) structures removes the rigidity. This [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md)-dependent explanation is modern; an 1864 answer could describe observations and their dependence on muscular condition without possessing the present cross-bridge model.[Rigor mortis: ATP depletion, calcium disturbance and eventual secondary relaxation](../../../../../postmortem-changes-statpearls.md)

Prior exertion, prolonged convulsive activity, fever or exhausting disease can leave smaller [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) reserves and alter the interval before stiffness becomes evident. Starvation, wasting, age and [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) mass can influence how pronounced it appears. A well-nourished resting [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) and an exhausted [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) need not behave alike after death. Cause of death matters through such conditions, through [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), and through injury or chemicals affecting [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) [metabolism](../../../../../metabolism-split.md); a named cause alone does not impose one universal timetable.

Warm conditions generally hasten biochemical changes, often bringing earlier rigor and faster subsequent loss through decomposition. Cooling generally slows the overall sequence and can prolong its persistence. There are qualifications: [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) shortening caused by low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) before rigor, freezing of tissue, and heat-induced [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) coagulation are not interchangeable with ordinary rigor. Controlled [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) experiments measuring extensibility, [pH](../../../../../ph.md), [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) demonstrate that shortening and completed rigor need not occur at the same stage.[Abstract: controlled temperature, pH, ATP and separation of shortening from loss of extensibility](../../../../../the-influence-of-temperature-on-shortening-and-rigor-onset-in-beef-muscle.md)

The stiffness becomes noticeable unevenly because [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) differ in size, condition and the way their resistance is tested. The customary progression from small facial and jaw [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) to larger limb groups is a practical generalization, not a biological wave proving that all other [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) were chemically unchanged until reached. Mechanical movement can break established resistance in a region, and the later appearance depends on how far the underlying process had progressed. Repeated manipulation therefore changes what the observer is trying to use as evidence.

A defensible observation records the environment, body condition, which [anatomical joints](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) resist passive movement, whether the resistance is local or widespread, and evidence of decomposition. It is interpreted alongside other observations rather than converted into an exact elapsed time. Rigor alone cannot establish an exact elapsed time.
