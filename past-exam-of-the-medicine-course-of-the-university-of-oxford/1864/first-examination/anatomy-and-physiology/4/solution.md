↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**[Plants](../../../../../plant.md) generally incorporate combined [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) into organic compounds, whereas [animals](../../../../../animal.md) eliminate the surplus [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) of metabolized compounds chiefly as [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md), [urea](../../../../../urea.md) or [uric acid](../../../../../uric-acid.md).** The two processes connect [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) chemistry to the organism's [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) supply, [water](../../../../../water.md) economy and environment.

Roots take up [nitrate](../../../../../nitrate.md) and [ammonium](../../../../../ammonium.md) from their surroundings. [Nitrate](../../../../../nitrate.md) must be reduced before its [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) can be incorporated in the usual amino form: [nitrate reductase](../../../../../nitrate-reductase.md) produces [nitrite](../../../../../nitrite.md), and [nitrite reductase](../../../../../nitrite-reductase.md) produces [ammonium](../../../../../ammonium.md). The balanced reduction, written without implying a single elementary reaction, is

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{NO_3^-}+10\mathrm{H^+}+8e^-\longrightarrow\mathrm{NH_4^+}+3\mathrm{H_2O}}.
$$

Reducing equivalents and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) must be supplied by [metabolism](../../../../../metabolism-split.md); [nitrate](../../../../../nitrate.md) is not simply deposited unchanged inside every [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md).

In the modern biochemical account, [glutamine synthetase](../../../../../glutamine-synthetase.md) incorporates [ammonium](../../../../../ammonium.md) into [glutamine](../../../../../glutamine.md), using [ATP](../../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md); [glutamate synthase](../../../../../glutamate-synthase.md) transfers that [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) to a [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) skeleton derived from [2-Oxoglutarate](../../../../../2-oxoglutarate.md), producing [glutamate](../../../../../glutamate.md). Further transfer and synthesis distribute [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) into other [amino acids](../../../../../amino-acid.md), [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), [nucleotides](../../../../../nucleotide.md) and other [cell](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) constituents. [Carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) [metabolism](../../../../../metabolism-split.md) therefore provides both [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and skeletons for [nitrogen assimilation](../../../../../nitrogen-assimilation.md). These named enzymes and reactions explain the process today; they were not a completed molecular scheme in 1864.[Nitrogen Assimilation: nitrate reduction and the GS/GOGAT cycle](../../../../../nitrogen-uptake-assimilation-and-remobilization-in-plants.md)

Ordinary higher-plant [cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md) do not themselves reduce atmospheric [molecular nitrogen](../../../../../molecular-nitrogen.md) merely because the gas surrounds the leaves. Biological [nitrogen fixation](../../../../../nitrogen-fixation.md) is performed by particular microorganisms; symbiotic associations can supply fixed [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) to a host plant. The later elucidation of root-nodule symbiosis should be distinguished from what the examiner could have expected. Organic [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) can also be taken up in suitable circumstances, so [nitrate](../../../../../nitrate.md) and [ammonium](../../../../../ammonium.md) are important routes rather than an exhaustive prohibition on every other source.[The Nitrogen Cycle: fixation, ammonification, nitrification and return to the atmosphere](../../../../../biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/46-3-biogeochemical-cycles.md)

A useful assimilation experiment grows comparable seedlings in a defined medium containing all required [minerals](../../../../../mineral.md) except combined [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), with parallel [nitrate](../../../../../nitrate.md)- or [ammonium](../../../../../ammonium.md)-supplied cultures. Initial seed reserves, contaminating [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), [water](../../../../../water.md) and microbial growth must be allowed for. Growth and final [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) content are more informative than green appearance alone. A plant initially growing in a [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md)-free solution may simply be consuming seed reserves; that observation does not demonstrate fixation of atmospheric [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md).

Animals obtain most of their [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) from organic [food](../../../../../food.md). [Amino acid metabolism](../../../../../amino-acid-metabolism.md) separates [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) handling from oxidation or reuse of the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) skeleton. Free [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) is toxic at high concentrations, and mammals convert much of their waste [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) into [urea](../../../../../urea.md) in the [liver](../../../../../liver.md); the [kidneys](../../../../../kidney.md) then eliminate it in [urine](../../../../../urine.md). [Uric acid](../../../../../uric-acid.md) is also produced from [purine](../../../../../purine.md) breakdown. Renal [ammonium](../../../../../ammonium.md) excretion has a separate important connection with acid-base balance. **[Urea](../../../../../urea.md) formation and urinary excretion are different processes in different organs.**[Ammonia; Urea; Uric Acid: toxicity, water requirements and comparative routes](../../../../../biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/41-4-nitrogenous-wastes.md)

Many aquatic animals can release [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) directly into abundant surrounding [water](../../../../../water.md). Birds, many reptiles and many insects eliminate much [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) as relatively insoluble [uric acid](../../../../../uric-acid.md), conserving [water](../../../../../water.md); mammals generally depend more on soluble [urea](../../../../../urea.md). These are broad physiological strategies with exceptions, not a rule that all animals have human [urine](../../../../../urine.md). Faecal [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) includes unabsorbed [food](../../../../../food.md) as well as endogenous material, while smaller losses can occur through [skin](../../../../../skin.md) and secretions. For a stated interval the accounting identity is

$$
\boxed{N_{\rm intake}-N_{\rm total\ loss}=\Delta N_{\rm body}}.
$$

Positive retention during growth and negative balance during tissue loss need not be errors of excretion. [Nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) is redistributed or lost in compounds; it is not simply breathed out as all the atmospheric [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) passing through the [lungs](../../../../../lung.md).
