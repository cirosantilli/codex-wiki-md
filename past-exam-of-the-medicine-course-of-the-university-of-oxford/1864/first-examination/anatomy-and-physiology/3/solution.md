↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**A [food](../../../../../food.md) supplies material or usable chemical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) that contributes to the organism's maintenance, growth or normal function after ingestion and physiological processing.** It must be usable in the amount and form supplied, without its harmful effects defeating that nutritive role. Mere combustibility, a pleasant taste, or the presence of [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) is insufficient.

An edible material may need [digestion](../../../../../digestion.md) before its constituents can be absorbed. A [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) need not enter the [blood](../../../../../blood.md) unchanged to nourish: [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) supplies absorbable [amino acids](../../../../../amino-acid.md). Conversely, a substance can burn in a laboratory yet furnish little accessible [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to human [metabolism](../../../../../metabolism-split.md). The nutritional value of a mixture depends on availability, proportions and the organism consuming it. No requirement says that each separate [food](../../../../../food.md) must contain every constituent of a complete diet.

A useful nineteenth-century division separates nitrogenous or plastic [foods](../../../../../food.md) from non-nitrogenous or respiratory [foods](../../../../../food.md). The first includes animal and plant [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md), which supply [amino acids](../../../../../amino-acid.md) for tissue renewal. The second includes [carbohydrates](../../../../../carbohydrate.md) and [fats](../../../../../fat.md), whose oxidation supplies much of the body's [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). But the division is functional rather than absolute: [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) can also be oxidized, and [lipids](../../../../../lipid.md) and [carbohydrates](../../../../../carbohydrate.md) supply material for cellular structures as well as fuel. The modern account follows [anabolism](../../../../../anabolism.md) and [catabolism](../../../../../catabolism.md) rather than assigning each class one exclusive destination.[Anabolic and Catabolic Reactions: synthesis, breakdown and energy transfer](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/24-1-overview-of-metabolic-reactions.md)

A fuller classification includes [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md); [carbohydrates](../../../../../carbohydrate.md), including sugars and [starch](../../../../../starch.md); [lipids](../../../../../lipid.md), including [fats](../../../../../fat.md) and essential fatty components; [water](../../../../../water.md); and required [mineral](../../../../../mineral.md) constituents. [Water](../../../../../water.md) provides solvent, transport medium and a participant in many reactions. [Mineral](../../../../../mineral.md) constituents supply, among other needs, ions for electrical and osmotic functions and material for mineralized tissues. Neither class has to supply combustible [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to be indispensable. Meat, milk, grain and legumes are mixtures spanning several classes, rather than chemically pure examples of just one.

In modern classification, [vitamins](../../../../../vitamin.md) and other required micronutrients must also be included. Their specific deficiency roles and present classification should not be attributed wholesale to 1864. [Dietary fibre](../../../../../dietary-fibre.md) is not equivalent to digestible [starch](../../../../../starch.md); it can have important intestinal functions, and fermentable fractions can contribute metabolites through the microbiota. The adequacy of a diet concerns the whole combination of nutrients and the person's requirements, not a single measure of heat released on burning it.[Macronutrients; Vitamins; Minerals: energy-yielding and non-energy-yielding nutritional requirements](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/24-7-nutrition-and-diet.md)

[Oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) is essential to ordinary aerobic life, but in the usual dietary sense it is a respiratory input rather than an ingested [food](../../../../../food.md) class. Likewise, a drug that changes appetite or stimulates a physiological response is not thereby a substitute for nutritive material. A convincing experiment on [food](../../../../../food.md) value must distinguish failure to eat, failure to digest or absorb, and a genuine missing dietary constituent; maintaining equal gross [food](../../../../../food.md) mass alone does not control those differences.
