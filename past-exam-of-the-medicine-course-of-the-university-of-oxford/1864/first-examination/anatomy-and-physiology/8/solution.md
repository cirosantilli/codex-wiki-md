**Both the [aorta](../../../../../aorta.md) and a [vena cava](../../../../../vena-cava.md) have an endothelial lining and three broadly corresponding tunics; the [aorta](../../../../../aorta.md) is dominated by an elastic muscular media, whereas a caval [vein](../../../../../vein.md) has a relatively thin media and a prominent collagenous adventitia.** Their appearance reflects their different mechanical conditions, not simply the [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) content of their [blood](../../../../../blood.md).

The [tunica intima](../../../../../tunica-intima.md) of each begins with a continuous [endothelium](../../../../../endothelium.md) on its [basement membrane](../../../../../basement-membrane.md), supported by subendothelial [connective tissue](../../../../../connective-tissue.md). A normal section must be distinguished from age-related or pathological thickening. In the [aorta](../../../../../aorta.md), the intima merges towards a very thick [tunica media](../../../../../tunica-media.md). Unlike a typical muscular [artery](../../../../../artery.md) with one conspicuous internal elastic boundary, an elastic [artery](../../../../../artery.md) contains so many [elastic lamellae](../../../../../elastic-lamella.md) that selecting one line as the uniquely dominant elastic membrane is less useful.

The aortic media contains repeated, approximately concentric fenestrated [elastic lamellae](../../../../../elastic-lamella.md), with [smooth muscle cells](../../../../../smooth-muscle-cell.md), [collagen](../../../../../collagen.md) and other matrix components between them. The elastic elements extend during ventricular ejection and recoil between beats; [smooth muscle](../../../../../smooth-muscle.md) participates in matrix maintenance and regulation of wall properties. The outer [tunica adventitia](../../../../../tunica-adventitia.md) contains [collagen](../../../../../collagen.md), [elastic fibres](../../../../../elastic-fibre.md), [nerves](../../../../../nerve.md) and [vasa vasorum](../../../../../vasa-vasorum.md). Those small vessels serve the outer wall, which is too thick to be supplied entirely by diffusion from luminal [blood](../../../../../blood.md).[Slides MH 065 and MH 066, aorta: elastic lamellae, smooth muscle and outer wall](../../../../../blood-vessels-in-the-histology-guide.md)

A [vena cava](../../../../../vena-cava.md) has a wide lumen relative to its wall and is easily distorted or collapsed in a fixed empty preparation. Its intima is relatively thin. The media contains fewer circular smooth-[muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) and elastic elements than the aortic media and is much less dominant. Its adventitia is often the thickest coat, with substantial [collagen](../../../../../collagen.md), elastic tissue and conspicuous longitudinal bundles of [smooth muscle](../../../../../smooth-muscle.md), particularly in the inferior vena cava. The relative amount varies with the sampled caval region; a thin-walled [vein](../../../../../vein.md) must not be described as a vessel without any [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md). [Vasa vasorum](../../../../../vasa-vasorum.md) and [nerves](../../../../../nerve.md) occur in its outer wall too.[Shared Structures; Arteries; Veins: tunics, elastic arteries and venous wall proportions](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/20-1-structure-and-function-of-blood-vessels.md)

Ordinary peripheral venous valves are folds of intima, but a valve is not a necessary feature of every large [vein](../../../../../vein.md) or every caval section. The [aorta](../../../../../aorta.md) likewise has no series of valves distributed down its wall; the aortic valve is at its cardiac origin. Thus two circular profiles alone should be compared by wall organization, not diagnosed by assuming the [vein](../../../../../vein.md) must display a valve.

An elastic-fibre stain makes the aortic lamellae conspicuous, while ordinary stained sections show nuclei and the relative distribution of [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) and [connective tissue](../../../../../connective-tissue.md). A transverse section cuts longitudinal caval [muscle](../../../../../muscle.md) bundles across, whereas a longitudinal section displays their length; recognizing orientation prevents confusing those bundles with glands or isolated cellular nodules. The low-pressure, highly compliant venous reservoir and the pressure-bearing elastic arterial conduit are consequently different uses of the same broad tissue classes.
