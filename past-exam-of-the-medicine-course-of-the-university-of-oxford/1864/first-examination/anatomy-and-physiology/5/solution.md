↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**[Starch granules](../../../../../starch-granule.md) can often be distinguished by their shape, size distribution, hilum and lamellae; [dextrin](../../../../../dextrin.md) and [inulin](../../../../../inulin.md) are distinguished chiefly by their molecular constitution, solubility and chemical reactions.** An isolated particle of uncertain origin cannot always be identified uniquely by one feature.

In a [water](../../../../../water.md)-mounted preparation of unheated [starch](../../../../../starch.md), potato granules are usually relatively large, oval or pear-shaped, often with an eccentric hilum near the narrower end and conspicuous curved growth markings. Wheat gives a characteristic mixture of larger flattened lenticular granules and small rounded granules; the hilum and rings are less conspicuous. Rice [starch](../../../../../starch.md) consists of very small angular or polyhedral granules, often still associated into compound groups. These are distributions rather than a guarantee that every potato granule is larger than every wheat granule.[Microscopic identification: potato, wheat and rice granule shape, hilum and striations](../../../../../starches-in-the-international-pharmacopoeia.md)

Maize commonly gives polygonal or rounded granules with a central point or fissured hilum; oats characteristically include aggregates of small angular granules. Arrowroot commonly has oval or elongated granules, sometimes with a transverse fissure towards one end. Preparation, variety and processing change the apparent population. Rotation between crossed polarizers shows the organized granule structure through a characteristic extinction cross in many intact granules. Swelling and loss of birefringence on heating with sufficient [water](../../../../../water.md) help demonstrate [starch gelatinization](../../../../../starch-gelatinization.md), although the precise [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) depends on the [starch](../../../../../starch.md) source.

Cold [water](../../../../../water.md) normally leaves native [starch granules](../../../../../starch-granule.md) as a suspension rather than a clear molecular solution. On heating, granules swell and a paste develops. With [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) in iodide solution, ordinary [starch](../../../../../starch.md) rich in sufficiently long [amylose](../../../../../amylose.md) helices produces an intense blue or blue-black complex. Its intensity depends on chain structure and conditions; the response can weaken on heating and return on cooling if the chains remain suitable. Continued acid or enzymatic [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) shortens chains and eventually destroys this characteristic blue response.

[Dextrin](../../../../../dextrin.md) denotes a range of shorter or modified [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) polymers derived from [starch](../../../../../starch.md), rather than one pure substance. Many preparations dissolve or disperse in [water](../../../../../water.md) more readily and no longer show intact native granules. Their [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) reactions vary with chain length: some give violet or reddish-brown colours, while extensively shortened material gives no characteristic colour. It is therefore too absolute to say that every [dextrin](../../../../../dextrin.md) is chemically identical or that no [dextrin](../../../../../dextrin.md) can react with [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md). The modern international specifications themselves distinguish [starch](../../../../../starch.md) modifications and their variable [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) colours.[Definition: mixtures of glucose polymers produced by starch or glycogen hydrolysis](../../../../../dextrins-in-mesh.md)[Identification; iodine colours and hydrolysis test in the specifications summary](../../../../../modified-starches-specifications-by-the-joint-fao-and-who-expert-committee-on-food-additives.md)

[Inulin](../../../../../inulin.md) is principally a [fructose](../../../../../fructose.md)-based [fructan](../../../../../fructan.md), with a different linkage pattern from [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md)-based [starch](../../../../../starch.md). It occurs, for example, in chicory and related storage organs. It does not give [starch](../../../../../starch.md)'s blue [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) complex. It dissolves more readily in hot [water](../../../../../water.md), and may separate as deposits on cooling or precipitation, but such deposits are not the characteristic native [starch granules](../../../../../starch-granule.md). [Hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) yields mainly [fructose](../../../../../fructose.md), whereas complete [starch](../../../../../starch.md) or [dextrin](../../../../../dextrin.md) [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) yields [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md). A copper-reduction test after [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) detects reducing products in both cases and is therefore insufficient by itself to distinguish them. Their different [hydrolysis](../../../../../hydrolysis.md) products, interpreted with [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) reaction and [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md), give a more specific comparison.[CHEBI:15443: beta-(2→1)-fructan identity](../../../../../inulin-in-chebi.md)

A sound comparison uses separately labelled reference preparations, equal [iodine](../../../../../iodine.md) conditions and separate unheated and heated mounts. Inspecting an unknown mixture only after boiling destroys much of the morphological evidence. The combined observations distinguish source-related morphology from the effects of processing.
