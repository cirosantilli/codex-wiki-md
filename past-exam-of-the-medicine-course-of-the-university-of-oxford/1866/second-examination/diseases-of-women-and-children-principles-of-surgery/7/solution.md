<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

**Suspected strangulation means threatened or impaired blood supply to herniated tissue; it is more serious than irreducibility alone and can occur without complete intestinal obstruction.** The diagnostic assessment must combine the local swelling, abdominal symptoms, general condition and time course.

Ask about a previous [hernia](../../../../../hernia.md), whether it was reducible, a new painful swelling, abrupt failure of reduction, the onset of pain and [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md), bowel function and prior attempts at manipulation. Examine the relevant sites, including both groins and the femoral regions, rather than attributing unexplained [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md) and abdominal pain solely to a gastric illness. A previously familiar painless lump can become dangerous after years of intermittent reduction. A small femoral [hernia](../../../../../hernia.md) may be less conspicuous than its systemic consequences suggest.

Locally, new tenderness, tension, irreducibility and loss of a usual cough impulse raise concern. Erythematous or discoloured overlying skin is more alarming, but can be absent early. Abdominal distension, persistent [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md) and failure to pass stool or flatus support [intestinal obstruction](../../../../../intestinal-obstruction.md). Increasing pain, [tachycardia](../../../../../tachycardia.md), [fever](../../../../../fever.md), dehydration, [peritonitis](../../../../../peritonitis.md) or shock suggest advancing compromise. Do not wait for every late sign: prolonged delay can lead to [intestinal ischaemia](../../../../../intestinal-ischaemia.md), [necrosis](../../../../../necrosis.md) and [sepsis](../../../../../sepsis.md).[Timing of intervention; predictors and consequences of bowel strangulation](../../../../../wses-guidelines-for-emergency-repair-of-complicated-abdominal-wall-hernias-2017.md)

Several negative observations are unsafe reassurances. Passage of stool may merely empty bowel below the obstruction. If only part of the bowel wall is trapped, as in a [Richter hernia](../../../../../richter-hernia.md), complete obstruction may never occur. A very ill person may describe less pain late in the course. Disappearance of the external swelling after a forceful manipulation does not prove safe restoration of viable bowel: persistence of pain or obstruction demands reassessment, including possible [reduction en masse](../../../../../reduction-en-masse.md).[2021 manual, chapter 23, Richter hernia and diagnostic considerations](../../../../../management-of-incarcerated-and-strangulated-abdominal-wall-hernias-by-the-american-college-of-surgeons.md)

An 1866 surgeon relied heavily on these bedside features and on the distinction from an inflamed [lymph node](../../../../../lymph-node.md), [hydrocele](../../../../../hydrocele.md), another groin mass or non-strangulated [hernia](../../../../../hernia.md). Contemporary reports describe sequences of attempted reduction, baths, enemata and [chloroform](../../../../../chloroform.md) before operation. They document period practice; they must not be transferred into a rule that strangulation begins only when repeated reduction has failed.[Published 1866, p. 329, historical taxis and operative decision sequence](../../../../../surgical-ward-clinical-report-from-the-royal-infirmary-1864-1865.md)

**Modern correction: suspected strangulation needs immediate surgical assessment and timely operative treatment.** Selected [blood tests](../../../../../blood-test.md) and imaging, commonly contrast-enhanced [computed tomography](../../../../../computed-tomography.md) when useful and feasible, support assessment but should not delay action in a convincing emergency. Normal early laboratory results do not exclude local ischaemia. Current guidance distinguishes an acutely irreducible [hernia](../../../../../hernia.md) without evidence of bowel compromise, where supervised reduction may be considered, from suspected strangulation, where emergency surgery is indicated. Ongoing symptoms after apparent reduction also require observation and reassessment.[Acute groin hernias, manual reduction contraindications, observation and emergency surgery](../../../../../update-of-the-international-herniasurge-guidelines-for-groin-hernia-management-2023.md) The diagnostic goal is not merely to name the lump, but to recognize endangered tissue before irreversible injury.
