**Early [congenital syphilis](../../../../../congenital-syphilis.md) can affect the skin, mucosa, bones, viscera and nervous system, yet an infected infant may look well at birth.** The symptoms are a variable constellation, not a compulsory sequence seen in every child.

The classical infantile picture includes persistent nasal discharge or “snuffles”, difficulty feeding, poor growth and a rash that can involve the palms and soles. The eruption may be maculopapular or blistering, with desquamation; colour descriptions such as “coppery” are neither universal nor diagnostic alone. Fissures around the mouth and other mucocutaneous lesions may occur. Look also for [hepatosplenomegaly](../../../../../hepatosplenomegaly.md), [jaundice](../../../../../jaundice.md), [anaemia](../../../../../anaemia.md), enlarged [lymph nodes](../../../../../lymph-node.md) and evidence of systemic illness.[Clinical criteria for children younger than two years; rhinitis, rash and systemic features](../../../../../congenital-syphilis-2026-case-definition-by-cdc.md)

Bone disease is particularly important when interpreting an apparently motionless limb. [Osteochondritis](../../../../../osteochondritis.md) or [periostitis](../../../../../periostitis.md) can make movement painful, producing [pseudoparalysis](../../../../../pseudoparalysis.md). This is distinct from the loss of motor function discussed under infantile paralysis. The nervous system can also be involved, including [meningitis](../../../../../meningitis.md) or seizures. Severe disease can be associated with prematurity, hydrops, stillbirth or neonatal death, although a living infant with mild signs may present much later.[Neonatal examination, pseudoparalysis, systemic manifestations and evaluation](../../../../../congenital-syphilis-treatment-guidance-by-cdc.md)

The period term “hereditary syphilis” should not be read as a modern account of Mendelian inheritance. **Modern science identifies infection by [Treponema pallidum](../../../../../treponema-pallidum.md), transmitted to the fetus during [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md), rather than an inherited constitutional taint.** Absence of obvious maternal symptoms does not exclude transmission. Equally, a rash or nasal discharge alone is not enough to prove [syphilis](../../../../../syphilis.md) or justify an allegation about a parent's behaviour.[Transmission and absence of symptoms at birth](../../../../../about-congenital-syphilis-by-cdc.md)

Later disease is useful for completing the history, but is not the usual neonatal picture. It can include notched permanent incisors or abnormal molars, a saddle-shaped nose, anterior bowing of the shins, [interstitial keratitis](../../../../../interstitial-keratitis.md) and [sensorineural hearing loss](../../../../../sensorineural-hearing-loss.md). These permanent dental signs cannot be demanded of a young infant before the relevant teeth have developed.[Clinical criteria in older children, two years and above](../../../../../congenital-syphilis-2026-case-definition-by-cdc.md)

**Modern correction: examine and test in the context of maternal infection and treatment, even when the baby has no symptoms.** Infant and maternal quantitative nontreponemal results, examination, treatment history and selected direct or additional tests guide assessment. Passive maternal [antibodies](../../../../../antibody.md) complicate interpretation of infant serology; a positive test does not have the same meaning in every setting. Appropriate [penicillin](../../../../../penicillin.md) treatment and follow-up are determined by the clinical scenario, not by waiting for a characteristic rash. Historical mercurial treatment is not its modern substitute.[Interpretation of neonatal tests; scenario-based evaluation, penicillin treatment and follow-up](../../../../../congenital-syphilis-treatment-guidance-by-cdc.md)
