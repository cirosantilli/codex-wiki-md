<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**Suspect [uterine retroversion](../../../../../uterine-retroversion.md) from examination and the clinical setting, while distinguishing an incidental position from symptomatic disease or an incarcerated pregnant uterus.** Pelvic pressure, backache, painful [menstruation](../../../../../menstruation.md), pain with intercourse, difficulty voiding or rectal pressure can prompt assessment, but none proves that the uterine position is the cause.

In retroversion the [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md) tilts backwards as a whole; [uterine retroflexion](../../../../../uterine-retroflexion.md) instead describes bending between its body and [cervix](../../../../../cervix.md). The two can coexist. On an appropriate pelvic examination, the uterine body may be felt posteriorly while the [cervix](../../../../../cervix.md) points more anteriorly. Mobility, tenderness, a pelvic mass and the possibility of [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md) affect interpretation. Symptoms may come from associated [endometriosis](../../../../../endometriosis.md), adhesions or another pelvic disorder rather than the tilt itself.[Anatomy, symptoms, associated conditions and diagnosis](../../../../../retroverted-uterus-by-cleveland-clinic.md)

The especially important historical presentation is early or mid-[pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md) with increasing pelvic pressure and [urinary retention](../../../../../urinary-retention.md). An enlarged backward-lying [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md) can remain trapped beneath the pelvic brim, distorting the [cervix](../../../../../cervix.md) and bladder outlet. Dribbling [urine](../../../../../urine.md) can represent overflow from retention, not adequate emptying. A distended [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md) and a posterior pelvic mass require careful assessment rather than treatment with a diuretic. A pre-1866 account of retroversion after conception emphasized retention as a principal presenting problem.[17 November 1860, p. 889, diagnostic signs and retention of urine](../../../../../retroversio-uteri-post-conceptionem-in-the-british-medical-journal-1860.md)

Historical treatment aimed first to empty an overdistended [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md) with a catheter, then to restore position where appropriate and prevent recurrence, sometimes with positional measures or a [vaginal pessary](../../../../../vaginal-pessary.md). A fixed, painful or possibly pregnant mass made blind forceful manipulation particularly hazardous. Describing the aim of a period manoeuvre does not make repeated unassessed attempts at repositioning acceptable.

**Modern correction: an asymptomatic retroverted [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md) commonly needs no treatment.** Symptoms call for investigation of their actual cause; retroversion alone does not establish infertility or explain every miscarriage. For selected symptomatic mobile cases, conservative support or a fitted [vaginal pessary](../../../../../vaginal-pessary.md) may be considered, while associated disease is managed on its merits.[Management, fertility and distinction from rare uterine incarceration](../../../../../retroverted-uterus-by-cleveland-clinic.md) Suspected incarceration in [pregnancy](../../../../../pregnancy.md), especially with [urinary retention](../../../../../urinary-retention.md), needs urgent obstetric assessment. [Ultrasonography](../../../../../ultrasonography.md) can clarify the anatomy; [magnetic resonance imaging](../../../../../magnetic-resonance-imaging.md) may help when the relation of the [uterus](../../../../../uterus.md), [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md) and [cervix](../../../../../cervix.md) remains uncertain. Drainage of the [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md) and any attempt at reduction are specialist decisions guided by gestation, anatomy and complications. Published evidence is largely case reports and series, so a single successful method is not a universal rule.[Presentation, diagnostic imaging and management; limitations of case-based evidence](../../../../../incarcerated-gravid-uterus-a-systematic-review-2023.md)
