<h1 id="greek-translation/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Greek translation](../greek-translation.md)

**Translation.** [Gout](../../../../../gout.md), then, does not readily become continuous, but sometimes leaves a long interval; for it is slight. Indeed, a man suffering from [gout](../../../../../gout.md) won a footrace at the Olympic games during a remission. Men are more readily affected, but less severely than women; women are affected less frequently than men, but more severely. For what is neither customary nor natural to a person, if it gains the upper hand through compulsion, produces a more violently oppressive affliction. As regards age, it generally begins from thirty-five years onwards, but earlier or later according to each person's constitution and way of life. The [pains](../../../../../pain.md) are terrible, and their accompaniments worse than the [pains](../../../../../pain.md): [faintness](../../../../../faintness.md), inability to move when touched, [loss of appetite](../../../../../loss-of-appetite.md), [thirst](../../../../../thirst.md) and [sleeplessness](../../../../../insomnia.md). But if they recover, on the one hand, as though they had escaped death, they live without restraint: uncontrolled, frank, cheerful, receptive to gifts and luxurious in their way of life; on the other, as though they would escape death once again, they make abundant use of the life they presently have. In many people [gout](../../../../../gout.md) has settled into [dropsy](../../../../../dropsy.md), and sometimes into [asthma](../../../../../asthma.md); and there is no escaping the succession of these conditions.

**The passage describes an ancient conception of [gout](../../../../../gout.md), not a modern diagnostic definition.** Its source is [Aretaeus of Cappadocia](../../../../../aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md)'s [On the Causes and Signs of Chronic Diseases by Aretaeus of Cappadocia](../../../../../on-the-causes-and-signs-of-chronic-diseases-by-aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md), II.12, on arthritic and sciatic affections. Here _ποδάγρη_ is rendered [gout](../../../../../gout.md), with the more specific name [podagra](../../../../../podagra.md) referring to the [foot](../../../../../foot.md). The conspicuous clinical observation is intermittency: the account contrasts severe attacks with intervals in which someone might run competitively. The Olympic story is the author's illustration, not an independently verified case history. [PDF volume p. 73, complete Greek extract and its source attribution](../../../../../examination-papers-for-the-degree-of-bachelor-of-medicine-by-the-university-of-oxford-1864-1867.md)

Several details matter for a faithful rendering. _λεπτὴ γάρ_ says “for it is slight”, despite the later description of severe [pain](../../../../../pain.md); it should be understood in the context of the preceding intermittent course, rather than turned into a claim that every attack is mild. _δίαιτα_ includes the whole way of life, not simply [food](../../../../../food.md). _ἁπλοί_ means frank or straightforward here. The printed _δωροδόκοι_ literally concerns receiving gifts, which is why the translation says “receptive to gifts”; silently replacing that with “generous” would reverse the ordinary direction of the word. The closing _ὡς ἐκ θανάτου αὖθις ἐσόμενοι_ is rendered as an expectation of escaping death again: the printed clause contains no negative. The comparison edition punctuates the symptom list so that touch provokes faintness; the Oxford punctuation instead leaves “when touched” next to immobility, as reflected above. [II.12, final paragraphs: intermittency, the symptom list, dispositions following recovery and the closing prognosis](../../../../../on-the-causes-and-signs-of-chronic-diseases-by-aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md) [δωροδόκος: taking presents; lexical support for the literal direction of the printed word](../../../../../greek-vocabulary-at-perseus.md)

**The assertions about women's greater severity, unusual conditions necessarily being more violent, and the patient's moral character are historical claims, not established consequences of [gout](../../../../../gout.md).** Modern [gout](../../../../../gout.md) is an inflammatory [arthritis](../../../../../arthritis.md) caused by deposits of [urate crystals](../../../../../urate-crystal.md) in [anatomical joints](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md). It is more common in men, and risk in women increases after [menopause](../../../../../menopause.md); that does not establish the text's proposed universal severity rule or its portrait of indulgent behaviour. Diagnosis concerns clinical findings and, where needed, examination of a [synovial fluid](../../../../../synovial-fluid.md) sample, rather than moral judgements about the sufferer. [Who gets gout; cause in uric-acid crystals; diagnostic testing including fluid taken from an affected joint](../../../../../gout-by-the-nhs.md)

Likewise, [dropsy](../../../../../dropsy.md) is a historical term for abnormal fluid accumulation, overlapping with [oedema](../../../../../oedema.md); it does not name one specific modern [disease](../../../../../disease.md). The ancient _ἄσθμα_ can describe difficult breathing and should not automatically be equated with present-day [asthma](../../../../../asthma.md). The translation retains the historical word, but the closing progression from [gout](../../../../../gout.md) to [dropsy](../../../../../dropsy.md) or [asthma](../../../../../asthma.md), with no escape, is the author's prognosis, not a current inevitable natural history. Modern diagnostic categories must therefore be kept separate from the ancient list. [II.1, dropsy as a systemic condition rather than merely one collection of fluid; II.12, final sentence of the assigned passage](../../../../../on-the-causes-and-signs-of-chronic-diseases-by-aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md)
