<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

**The sound historical indication was selected dropsical states associated with disordered cardiac action; the modern uses of particular [cardiac glycosides](../../../../../cardiac-glycoside.md) are narrower and explicitly rhythm-dependent.** A [digitalis preparation](../../../../../digitalis-preparation.md) was not simply a drug for every slow urinary flow or rapid [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md).

Before 1866, [William Withering](../../../../../william-withering.md)'s account had already demonstrated both the possible relief of dropsy and the danger of careless use. He recorded successes and failures and warned about [nausea](../../../../../nausea.md), [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md), visual disturbance and excessive slowing of the [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md). These observations support careful assessment of effect and toxicity, not a method of administering foxglove until poisoning occurs.[1785, Preface; Effects, Rules, and Cautions](../../../../../an-account-of-the-foxglove-and-some-of-its-medical-uses-by-william-withering.md) The historical assessment would follow breathing, swelling, [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), [urine](../../../../../urine.md) and general condition. Increased [urine](../../../../../urine.md) and reduced [oedema](../../../../../oedema.md) could represent relief while the original cardiac disorder persisted.

Calling the drug a cardiac “sedative” from the slowing of the [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) could obscure a different effect on contraction. Likewise, apparent diuresis did not establish it as a universal remedy for renal dropsy. Period applications extended into febrile or haemorrhagic illnesses, but such claims should not be promoted into modern indications merely because the [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) changed. The problem is to distinguish treatment of a useful physiological target from suppression of a sign.

**Modern correction: [digoxin](../../../../../digoxin.md) increases myocardial contractility and slows conduction through the [atrioventricular node](../../../../../atrioventricular-node.md).** In selected [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md) it can improve symptoms and reduce hospitalization. A randomized, placebo-controlled trial in people with chronic [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md) and normal [sinus rhythm](../../../../../sinus-rhythm.md) found fewer hospitalizations but no reduction in overall mortality.[1997, main trial, Methods, Results and Conclusions](../../../../../the-effect-of-digoxin-on-mortality-and-morbidity-in-patients-with-heart-failure-by-the-digitalis-investigation-group.md) It can also control resting ventricular rate in selected adults with chronic [atrial fibrillation](../../../../../atrial-fibrillation.md). It is not a general treatment for a fast [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), and the presence of an accessory conduction pathway can make its use hazardous. [Ventricular fibrillation](../../../../../ventricular-fibrillation.md) is a contraindication.[Indications 1.1 and 1.3; Contraindications; warning 5.1; mechanism of action 12.1](../../../../../digoxin-prescribing-information-on-dailymed.md)

The margin between useful and toxic exposure is small. Renal function, age, body composition, interactions and [electrolyte](../../../../../electrolyte.md) disturbances influence risk. Monitor the clinical response, [electrocardiogram](../../../../../electrocardiogram.md), relevant laboratory results and drug concentrations when indicated. [Nausea](../../../../../nausea.md), [vomiting](../../../../../vomiting.md), visual symptoms, marked [bradycardia](../../../../../bradycardia.md) or new [cardiac arrhythmias](../../../../../cardiac-arrhythmia.md) should raise concern for toxicity, not be treated as confirmation that the drug is beginning to work.[Sections 2.1 and 2.5; 5.2–5.3; 8.6, renal impairment](../../../../../digoxin-prescribing-information-on-dailymed.md) Historical leaf or tincture preparations must therefore not be substituted for a modern standardized preparation, or converted into modern doses from a shared name alone.
