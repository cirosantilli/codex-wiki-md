↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**A [carbuncle](../../../../../carbuncle.md) is a deep infection involving connected adjacent hair follicles. Local damage, bacterial carriage and impaired host defence can favour its development; a drainable focus needs drainage.** It is more extensive than a single superficial follicular pustule.

Relevant predisposing circumstances include [diabetes](../../../../../diabetes.md), friction or injury at the skin, close contact or shared contaminated articles, and impaired immunity. Ask about recurrent [skin](../../../../../skin.md) infections, medication and systemic health. A lesion on the nape in someone with [diabetes](../../../../../diabetes.md) is a familiar pattern, but the source supplies neither its site nor a test result, so neither may be asserted as an observation. Evaluate for [fever](../../../../../fever.md), spreading [cellulitis](../../../../../cellulitis.md), severe pain and [sepsis](../../../../../sepsis.md), and consider whether a lesion labelled a “carbuncle” is actually another disorder.[Purulent SSTIs; Furuncles and Carbuncles, anatomy, common organism and association with diabetes](../../../../../skin-and-soft-tissue-infection-guideline-by-idsa.md)

In historical language, “debility”, age, poor nourishment and a disordered constitutional state were often invoked. Some of these descriptions might correspond to a recognizable predisposition; others were vague explanations after the event. Period local treatments included poulticing, incisions to relieve tension and evacuate dead or suppurating tissue, and attempts at destruction with caustics. A November 1866 letter on boils and [carbuncles](../../../../../carbuncle.md) is evidence that both their origin and treatment were actively disputed. Its proposed “parasiticide” treatment must not be read as proof of a fungal cause or as a modern instruction to apply corrosive chemicals.[British Medical Journal, 10 November 1866, pp. 534–535, historical local treatment and proposed parasitic interpretation](../../../../../treatment-of-boils-and-carbuncles-by-james-startin.md)

**Modern correction: the usual process is bacterial, commonly involving [Staphylococcus aureus](../../../../../staphylococcus-aureus.md), and a fungal theory inferred from apparent response to a caustic is inadequate.** Assess for a drainable collection; incision and drainage is the core treatment of a [carbuncle](../../../../../carbuncle.md). [Microbiological culture](../../../../../microbiological-culture.md) of [pus](../../../../../pus.md) can guide therapy, especially in difficult or recurrent cases. Systemic [antibiotics](../../../../../antibiotic.md) are added according to illness severity, surrounding infection, host factors and the suspected or demonstrated organism. Severe illness requires escalation, not delay while waiting for the lesion to “ripen”.[Recommendations for incision and drainage, culture and adjunctive antibiotics; impaired host defences and systemic illness](../../../../../skin-and-soft-tissue-infection-guideline-by-idsa.md)

Address [diabetes](../../../../../diabetes.md) or other identified predispositions, dress draining lesions and reduce transmission through hand hygiene and avoiding shared personal articles. Repeated lesions call for reassessment of diagnosis and sources of reinfection. A destructive historical incision or caustic is not equivalent to controlled drainage with appropriate analgesia, infection control and follow-up.
