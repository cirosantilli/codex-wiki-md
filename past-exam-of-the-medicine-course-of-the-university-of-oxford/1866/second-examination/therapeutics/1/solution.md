↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**The treatment depends on the cause of [acute pericarditis](../../../../../acute-pericarditis.md), the person's strength and the presence of [pericardial effusion](../../../../../pericardial-effusion.md), [myocarditis](../../../../../myocarditis.md) or circulatory failure.** In an 1866 answer, rest, relief of pain and repeated assessment of the [heart](../../../../../heart.md) are more defensible starting points than an indiscriminate attempt to suppress every sign of [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) by depletion.

The bedside assessment would include the history of rheumatic illness, chest pain, a [pericardial friction rub](../../../../../pericardial-friction-rub.md), the character of the [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md), breathing, the extent of cardiac dullness on [percussion](../../../../../percussion.md) and changes in the [heart sounds](../../../../../heart-sound.md). Serial examination matters: disappearance of a [pericardial friction rub](../../../../../pericardial-friction-rub.md) need not mean recovery if fluid is separating the inflamed surfaces. Increasing oppression of breathing, weakness or a failing [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) would require reconsidering both the disease and the treatment. [Auscultation](../../../../../auscultation.md) with a [stethoscope](../../../../../stethoscope.md) and careful repeated [percussion](../../../../../percussion.md) supplied information that modern imaging now refines.

Period practice included local [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) with [medicinal leeches](../../../../../medicinal-leech.md), counterirritation with a [blistering agent](../../../../../blistering-agent.md), [opium](../../../../../opium.md) for pain and rest, and disputed [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md) treatment intended to alter inflammatory exudation. A robust adult early in an apparently vigorous inflammatory attack was treated differently by depletion advocates from an exhausted person with [anaemia](../../../../../anaemia.md), previous [blood loss](../../../../../blood-loss.md) or advanced [kidney](../../../../../kidney.md) disease. Even in that framework, a rapid weak [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) was not a command to remove more [blood](../../../../../blood.md). Nor was [Mercury](../../../../../mercury-planet.md) an uncontested standard: a medical address published in December 1866 specifically listed its use in [pericarditis](../../../../../pericarditis.md) among unresolved therapeutic questions.[p. 294, therapeutic uncertainties concerning mercury in pericarditis and diuretics in Bright's disease](../../../../../introductory-address-at-the-public-opening-of-the-medical-session-1866-67-by-william-tennant-gairdner.md)

**Modern correction: establish the diagnosis, identify complications and use cause-directed treatment rather than [bloodletting](../../../../../bloodletting.md) or mercurialization.** [Electrocardiography](../../../../../electrocardiography.md), inflammatory markers, [troponin](../../../../../troponin.md) where myocardial injury is suspected and [echocardiography](../../../../../echocardiography.md) help distinguish uncomplicated [pericarditis](../../../../../pericarditis.md) from important associated disease. A typical uncomplicated inflammatory episode is treated with [aspirin](../../../../../aspirin.md) or a suitable [nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug](../../../../../nonsteroidal-anti-inflammatory-drug.md), usually with [colchicine](../../../../../colchicine.md) and appropriate gastroprotection. Renal impairment, [gastrointestinal bleeding](../../../../../gastrointestinal-bleeding.md), interactions and a specific infectious or systemic cause can modify this plan. [Corticosteroids](../../../../../corticosteroid.md) have selected indications and are not the routine first choice. Activity is restricted during active disease and return to exertion follows reassessment.[Diagnostic assessment; section 6.2.2, pharmacological therapy for pericarditis](../../../../../2025-esc-guidelines-for-the-management-of-myocarditis-and-pericarditis.md)

A large [pericardial effusion](../../../../../pericardial-effusion.md) requires attention to its haemodynamic effect, not merely its size. **[Cardiac tamponade](../../../../../cardiac-tamponade.md) requires urgent specialist drainage and stabilization.** Purulent disease requires treatment of the [infection](../../../../../infection.md) and drainage; associated [myocarditis](../../../../../myocarditis.md) or a systemic cause changes management further.[Pericardial effusion and cardiac tamponade management pathways](../../../../../2025-esc-guidelines-for-the-management-of-myocarditis-and-pericarditis.md) These distinctions explain why treatment cannot be fixed solely by the label “acute”.
