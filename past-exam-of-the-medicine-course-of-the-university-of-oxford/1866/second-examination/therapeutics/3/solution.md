<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Established [tetanus](../../../../../tetanus.md) is an emergency: minimize provoked [muscle spasms](../../../../../muscle-spasm.md), protect breathing, manage the wound and provide sustained supportive care.** The source does not identify a wound, so none should be invented; a search for a plausible portal of entry is part of the assessment.

In historical practice, a quiet, darkened environment and gentle handling aimed to reduce reflex provocation of painful [muscle spasms](../../../../../muscle-spasm.md). [Trismus](../../../../../trismus.md) made oral feeding difficult, while generalized rigidity and [opisthotonus](../../../../../opisthotonus.md) could threaten breathing and exhaust the person. A wound or retained material needed inspection and appropriate local care. Nutrition and fluid support were essential because the illness could outlast a single dramatic paroxysm. Forcefully attempting to open the jaws or repeatedly provoking [muscle spasms](../../../../../muscle-spasm.md) to “test” them would add danger.

Period treatment tried to suppress the motor manifestations with [opium](../../../../../opium.md), [chloroform](../../../../../chloroform.md) and other sedating remedies. The literature contains reports of apparent success, including a report published in November 1866 describing traumatic [tetanus](../../../../../tetanus.md). A single survival cannot establish that its mixture of interventions neutralized the cause, especially when case severity and the natural course varied.[pp. 336–337, clinical record and treatment; period evidence, not a modern protocol](../../../../../a-case-of-traumatic-tetanus-with-anaesthesia-of-the-trunk-and-upper-extremities-treated-successfully-1866.md) Apparent relaxation during [anaesthesia](../../../../../anaesthesia.md) also does not prove eradication of the process producing the [muscle spasms](../../../../../muscle-spasm.md).

**Modern correction: the problem is toxin-mediated loss of inhibitory control, so treating visible [muscle spasms](../../../../../muscle-spasm.md) alone is incomplete.** [Clostridium tetani](../../../../../clostridium-tetani.md) in a suitable wound can produce [tetanospasmin](../../../../../tetanospasmin.md). Treatment includes [tetanus immune globulin](../../../../../tetanus-immune-globulin.md) to neutralize toxin that has not yet bound to neural tissue, wound cleaning and removal of devitalized tissue, and appropriate [antibiotics](../../../../../antibiotic.md). The immune globulin does not reverse toxin already bound at [nerve](../../../../../nerve.md) endings, which explains the need for prolonged supportive care despite prompt treatment.[TIG; wound management; antibiotic therapy](../../../../../clinical-care-of-tetanus-by-cdc.md)

Sedation and muscle-relaxing therapy control [muscle spasms](../../../../../muscle-spasm.md), with [airway](../../../../../airway.md) management and assisted [pulmonary ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) when needed. Marked autonomic instability may require additional intensive care. Feeding, hydration and prevention of complications must continue during recovery. **Clinical recovery from [tetanus](../../../../../tetanus.md) does not confer dependable immunity: vaccination is still required.**[Control muscle spasms; Maintain an airway; Vaccination during recovery](../../../../../clinical-care-of-tetanus-by-cdc.md) For established disease, prophylactic wound advice alone is therefore insufficient, and neither historical [chloroform](../../../../../chloroform.md) inhalation nor an opiate mixture substitutes for the modern treatment bundle.
