<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

**[Gout](../../../../../gout.md) is a crystal-deposition disorder; “[rheumatism](../../../../../rheumatism.md)” was a broader historical label that must be separated into acute rheumatic fever and several chronic joint disorders.** In particular, chronic rheumatism is not a precise synonym for modern [rheumatoid arthritis](../../../../../rheumatoid-arthritis.md).

In **acute [gout](../../../../../gout.md)**, a sudden, intensely painful, red, hot and swollen [anatomical joint](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) is characteristic. The first metatarsophalangeal [anatomical joint](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) is a classic site, and early attacks often affect one [anatomical joint](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) with intervals of recovery. The material responsible is [monosodium urate](../../../../../monosodium-urate.md) deposited in or around the [anatomical joint](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md), with an acute inflammatory reaction. High [serum urate](../../../../../serum-urate.md) supports the diagnosis but neither guarantees clinical [gout](../../../../../gout.md) nor excludes it when a sample during a flare is ordinary.[Introduction; crystal deposition; Diagnosis, serum urate](../../../../../gout-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

In **chronic [gout](../../../../../gout.md)**, persistent deposits can form [tophi](../../../../../tophus.md), including periarticular nodules and deposits in the ear or other cooler sites. Surrounding inflammatory [tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md) and [fibrosis](../../../../../fibrosis.md) accompany the deposits; recurrent damage can erode articular structures and cause deformity and persistent symptoms. More than one [anatomical joint](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) may then be affected. The chalk-like material is a deposit of urate, not new [bone](../../../../../bone.md), and not merely an accumulation of ordinary [pus](../../../../../pus.md).[Pathophysiology; chronic tophaceous gout](../../../../../gout-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

The classical **acute rheumatism** of this comparison is usually [acute rheumatic fever](../../../../../acute-rheumatic-fever.md). In its typical articular presentation, [fever](../../../../../fever.md) accompanies painful inflammation that migrates between large [anatomical joints](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md), rather than repeatedly selecting one small distal site. In modern terms it is a delayed immune-mediated sequel of [Group A streptococcal infection](../../../../../group-a-streptococcal-infection.md). [Carditis](../../../../../carditis.md), especially involvement of cardiac [cardiac valves](../../../../../heart-valve.md), is a major distinguishing concern; chorea and characteristic skin manifestations may also occur. The articular illness usually subsides without the progressive urate deposits of chronic [gout](../../../../../gout.md). The later understanding of the streptococcal cause must be distinguished from nineteenth-century constitutional theories.[Cause; Clinical features, migratory arthritis and carditis](../../../../../clinical-guidance-for-acute-rheumatic-fever-by-cdc.md)

For **chronic rheumatism**, distinguish at least two important modern patterns. [Rheumatoid arthritis](../../../../../rheumatoid-arthritis.md) causes persistent inflammatory [synovitis](../../../../../synovitis.md), commonly affecting corresponding small [anatomical joints](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) on both sides. Thickened inflamed [synovium](../../../../../synovium.md) and invasive [pannus](../../../../../pannus.md) can damage [cartilage](../../../../../cartilage.md) and [bone](../../../../../bone.md), producing lasting deformity. [Osteoarthritis](../../../../../osteoarthritis.md), also historically included among chronic or deforming rheumatic complaints, instead emphasizes loss of articular [cartilage](../../../../../cartilage.md), altered underlying [bone](../../../../../bone.md) and [osteophytes](../../../../../osteophyte.md). These conditions cannot be identified merely by saying that the [anatomical joints](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) have been painful for a long time.[Pathophysiology; Symptoms and Signs; Differential diagnosis](../../../../../rheumatoid-arthritis-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)[Pathophysiology and structural changes](../../../../../osteoarthritis-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

A modern discriminating examination for suspected [gout](../../../../../gout.md) examines aspirated [synovial fluid](../../../../../synovial-fluid.md) with a [polarizing microscope](../../../../../polarizing-microscope.md). Needle-like, strongly negatively birefringent [monosodium urate](../../../../../monosodium-urate.md) crystals support the diagnosis; a suitable compensator helps assess their optical behaviour.[Crystal morphology, polarized examination and preservation in alcohol](../../../../../gouty-tophus-by-weill-cornell-medicine.md) This is later diagnostic technique, not a test claimed to have been supplied in 1866. [Microbiological culture](../../../../../microbiological-culture.md) addresses a different alternative, [septic arthritis](../../../../../septic-arthritis.md); crystals and infection can coexist. Clinical distribution and duration remain useful, but neither appearance nor a single [serum urate](../../../../../serum-urate.md) value settles every case.[NG219, recommendations 1.1.3–1.1.9, differential diagnosis, serum urate and aspiration](../../../../../gout-diagnosis-and-management-by-nice.md)

**Acute versus chronic describes the course; gout versus a particular rheumatic disease identifies a mechanism.** The two distinctions must be made separately.
