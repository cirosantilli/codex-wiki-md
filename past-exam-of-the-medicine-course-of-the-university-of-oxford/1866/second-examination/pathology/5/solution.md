**A ring-shaped patch is a clinical shape, not one histological disease.** The historical category included several named scalp and skin affections whose relationship was disputed. In particular, old claims that [alopecia areata](../../../../../alopecia-areata.md) was a fungal disease cannot be carried into modern [pathology](../../pathology-split.md). A contemporary review actually records competing classifications of favus, scalp ringworm, circinate eruptions, beard disease and patchy baldness, and disagreement about whether their fungi were identical.[Discussion of proposed parasites of favus, herpes tonsurans, herpes circinatus, sycosis, tinea decalvans and pityriasis versicolor](../../../../../on-the-parasitic-affections-of-the-skin-review-1864.md)

The microscopic distinctions should be organized by the structure infected or injured:

- **Ordinary scalp [ringworm](../../../../../ringworm.md), or [tinea capitis](../../../../../tinea-capitis.md).** The [fungus](../../../../../fungus.md) invades keratinized [hair](../../../../../hair.md) and adjacent surface material. [Hyphae](../../../../../hypha.md) and [arthroconidia](../../../../../arthroconidium.md) can occur within the shaft, the [endothrix](../../../../../endothrix.md) pattern, or around its exterior, the [ectothrix](../../../../../ectothrix.md) pattern. The weakened shaft breaks, producing short stumps or dark dots. Inflammation varies from limited scale to a severe follicular reaction. Modern species cannot reliably be assigned from the shape of the bald patch alone.[Classification by invasion of the hair shaft; clinical variants and microscopy](../../../../../tinea-capitis-by-dermnet.md)
- **[Favus](../../../../../favus.md).** The characteristic yellow cups, or [scutula](../../../../../scutulum.md), consist of a mass of fungal filaments and keratinous debris around a [hair follicle](../../../../../hair-follicle.md). Hyphae may also involve the hair. Longstanding destructive disease can scar the follicle and cause permanent [hair loss](../../../../../hair-loss.md). The historical name of its organism was Achorion schoenleinii; the usual modern designation is [Trichophyton schoenleinii](../../../../../trichophyton-schoenleinii.md). Those names describe the history of classification, not two different appearances inferred from an unseen specimen.[Introduction and Etiology, scutula and fungal cause](../../../../../favus-in-statpearls.md)
- **[Tinea corporis](../../../../../tinea-corporis.md) and fungal beard disease, [tinea barbae](../../../../../tinea-barbae.md).** On smooth [skin](../../../../../skin.md), superficial hyphae occupy the [stratum corneum](../../../../../stratum-corneum.md), with variable epidermal reaction; the advancing margin can be the most productive site for scrapings. In beard infection the hair-bearing [hair follicles](../../../../../hair-follicle.md) are involved, and the reaction may be follicular, pustular or granulomatous. The historical word [sycosis](../../../../../sycosis.md) also covered nonfungal follicular inflammation, so its name alone does not establish a dermatophyte.[Histology and special stains](../../../../../tinea-corporis-pathology-by-dermnet.md)[Microscopy of scrapings and hairs; inflammatory follicular disease](../../../../../tinea-barbae-by-dermnet.md)
- **[Pityriasis versicolor](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor.md).** This is associated with [Malassezia](../../../../../malassezia.md), not the usual dermatophytes of true [ringworm](../../../../../ringworm.md). Numerous rounded yeast forms and short fungal filaments occur predominantly in the [stratum corneum](../../../../../stratum-corneum.md); a hair shaft filled by arthroconidia is not the defining lesion. This explains why the older name “tinea versicolor” should not imply identity with scalp [ringworm](../../../../../ringworm.md).[Histology, yeast and short hyphae in the stratum corneum](../../../../../pityriasis-versicolor-pathology-by-dermnet.md)
- **[Alopecia areata](../../../../../alopecia-areata.md), historically entangled with “tinea decalvans”.** The modern lesion is immune-mediated disturbance of hair follicles, with characteristic lymphocytic inflammation around and sometimes within the bulb in active disease and changes in the hair-growth cycle. The inflammatory infiltrate may be inconspicuous in chronic disease. Finding short or altered hairs is not proof of fungi.[Histopathological diagnostics, active peribulbar inflammation and chronic-stage variation](../../../../../s3-guideline-diagnostics-and-therapy-of-alopecia-areata-part-1.md)

Annular [eczema](../../../../../eczema.md) or [psoriasis](../../../../../psoriasis.md) supplies a further clinical mimic: an inflammatory epidermal pattern alone is not evidence of an invading [fungus](../../../../../fungus.md). For an actual microscopic investigation, collect scale from an active margin and affected hairs when hair is involved, mount the material on a [microscope slide](../../../../../microscope-slide.md) with [potassium hydroxide](../../../../../potassium-hydroxide.md) to clear keratin, and examine under a [microscope](../../../../../microscope.md). A modern [biopsy](../../../../../biopsy.md) can be examined with [Periodic acid-Schiff staining](../../../../../periodic-acid-schiff-staining.md) to highlight fungal structures. Distribution within the surface layer, hair shaft, follicle or bulb matters as much as the presence of particles. [Fungal culture](../../../../../fungal-culture.md) or molecular identification can resolve species when morphology cannot.[B 39, issue 3.2, Microscopy and specimen examination](../../../../../investigation-of-dermatological-specimens-for-superficial-mycoses-by-the-uk-standards-for-microbiology-investigations.md) **Distinguish the organism, its anatomical location and the host reaction; do not diagnose all circular lesions as one infection.**
