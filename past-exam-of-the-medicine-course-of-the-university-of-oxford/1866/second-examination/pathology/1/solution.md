**The historical material was a tuberculous or caseous product, whose formation was associated with constitutional susceptibility, poor nourishment and unhealthy living conditions; its anatomical origin was disputed.** The word [scrofulous material](../../../../../scrofulous-material.md) must not be taken to identify a chemically uniform substance deposited from the [blood](../../../../../blood.md).

In the period's constitutional account, an inherited [scrofulous diathesis](../../../../../scrofulous-diathesis.md), defective nourishment, overcrowding, ill-ventilated accommodation and prolonged debilitating illness were thought to favour its formation. Damp or cold surroundings were also blamed, but are not in themselves a sufficient cause of [tuberculosis](../../../../../tuberculosis.md). A contemporary clinical discussion explicitly linked inadequate diet and crowded, badly ventilated dormitories with scrofulous illness.[p. 913, discussion preceding the Margate infirmary diet lists](../../../../../scrofulous-diet-and-ventilation-in-the-british-medical-journal-24-november-1860.md) Familial clustering could reflect shared exposure and living conditions as well as host susceptibility; it did not establish inheritance of a ready-made morbid deposit.

The modern explanation separates acquisition of [Mycobacterium tuberculosis](../../../../../mycobacterium-tuberculosis.md) from progression after infection. Infectious airborne particles can reach the [alveoli](../../../../../alveolus.md); close exposure and poor indoor air circulation increase transmission. Impaired [immunity](../../../../../immunity-medical.md), [malnutrition](../../../../../malnutrition.md) and some chronic illnesses increase the risk of progression. Thus some old observations about housing and nutrition concerned real risks, while the old constitutional explanation did not identify the infectious cause.[Risk factors; How it spreads](../../../../../clinical-overview-of-tuberculosis-by-cdc.md)[Airborne transmission and progression from inactive infection](../../../../../tuberculosis-causes-and-how-it-spreads-by-the-centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention.md)

The primary sites considered were the [pulmonary interstitium](../../../../../pulmonary-interstitium.md), including supporting [connective tissue](../../../../../connective-tissue.md) around small [airways](../../../../../airway.md) and [blood vessels](../../../../../blood-vessel.md), and the lining or contents of the [alveoli](../../../../../alveolus.md). A cellular nodule in the supporting [tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md) is structurally different from degenerated inflammatory material filling air spaces. Contemporary authors disputed formation in exuded fluid versus origin from local tissue elements.[1863 lecture, discussion of tubercle-corpuscles, pp. 895–897](../../../../../the-present-aspect-of-the-doctrine-of-cellular-pathology-by-william-turner.md)

Under the [microscope](../../../../../microscope.md), a cellular nodule and its degenerated centre look different. In present terminology a [tuberculous granuloma](../../../../../tuberculous-granuloma.md) commonly contains [epithelioid macrophages](../../../../../epithelioid-macrophage.md), [lymphocytes](../../../../../lymphocyte.md) and sometimes multinucleated [giant cells](../../../../../giant-cell.md), with central [caseous necrosis](../../../../../caseous-necrosis.md). The caseous portion is granular, largely structureless debris with loss of recognizable viable [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md); lipid droplets and remnants may be seen. Old descriptions of small granular “tubercle corpuscles” did not establish a uniquely diagnostic cell. Caseation can occur in other processes, and a severely degraded specimen may conceal its origin.[Histopathological diagnosis, pulmonary granulomas and caseous necrosis](../../../../../diagnosis-of-tuberculosis-a-consensus-statement-from-the-brazilian-thoracic-association.md)

A discriminating examination therefore samples both the centre and the advancing margin, preserves the relation to [alveoli](../../../../../alveolus.md) and interstitium in sections, and examines adjoining [lung](../../../../../lung.md). Modern [acid-fast staining](../../../../../acid-fast-staining.md), [microbiological culture](../../../../../microbiological-culture.md) and molecular tests address the infectious cause; they are later methods, not observations supplied by this 1866 paper. **Architecture and causal evidence are more informative than the appearance of the debris alone.**
