**“Leukhæmia” denotes [leukaemia](../../../../../leukaemia.md): historically a persistent excess of colourless corpuscles in the [blood](../../../../../blood.md), now understood as a group of malignant disorders of blood-forming [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md).** It is distinct from a temporary rise of ordinary [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md) during [inflammation](../../../../../inflammation.md) or [infection](../../../../../infection.md).

The historical laboratory finding was an unusually large proportion of colourless [blood](../../../../../blood.md) corpuscles on [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md), often accompanied by enlargement of the [spleen](../../../../../spleen.md) or [lymph nodes](../../../../../lymph-node.md). Such a blood appearance did not mean that ordinary [pus](../../../../../pus.md) had simply entered the circulation. The present classification separates acute and chronic disorders and myeloid and lymphoid lineages. The abnormal population can interfere with production of normal [red blood cells](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md), [platelets](../../../../../platelet.md) and effective [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md) in the [bone marrow](../../../../../bone-marrow.md). A high total white-cell count is common, but some acute [leukaemias](../../../../../leukaemia.md) present with a normal or low count; the count alone is neither a complete definition nor a modern subtype diagnosis.[Introduction; Classification; Findings at Diagnosis table; Leukemoid reaction](../../../../../overview-of-leukemia-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

The symptoms follow several mechanisms:

- Reduced normal [red blood cell](../../../../../red-blood-cell.md) production causes [anaemia](../../../../../anaemia.md), with pallor, fatigue, weakness, exertional [dyspnoea](../../../../../dyspnoea.md) and sometimes palpitations.
- Reduced [platelet](../../../../../platelet.md) production can cause easy bruising, pinpoint [petechiae](../../../../../petechiae.md), nosebleeds, bleeding gums or other [haemorrhage](../../../../../haemorrhage.md). Bleeding is a tendency rather than an inevitable finding in every subtype.
- Defective production or function of effective [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md) predisposes to [infection](../../../../../infection.md), [fever](../../../../../fever.md), mouth lesions and slow recovery from infections. A large number of abnormal [leukocytes](../../../../../leukocyte.md) does not guarantee normal defence.
- Expansion or infiltration of the [bone marrow](../../../../../bone-marrow.md) can produce bone pain. Enlargement of [lymph nodes](../../../../../lymph-node.md), the [spleen](../../../../../spleen.md) and sometimes the [liver](../../../../../liver.md) may produce visible swellings, abdominal discomfort or early fullness after eating.
- Systemic illness can cause diminished appetite, loss of body mass and night sweats. Conversely, some chronic [leukaemias](../../../../../leukaemia.md) are initially detected in apparently well people.

These are possible manifestations, not a required syndrome in every affected person.[What is leukemia?; Symptoms; Diagnosis](../../../../../leukemia-by-medlineplus.md)[How leukemia starts; Features and symptoms of acute and chronic leukemia](../../../../../leukemia-by-the-american-cancer-society.md)

For an 1866 explanation, the important connection is between the persistent abnormal [blood](../../../../../blood.md) picture, enlargement of blood-forming or lymphatic organs, progressive debility and bleeding. Modern diagnosis goes further: a [complete blood count](../../../../../complete-blood-count.md) with differential and a stained [blood film](../../../../../blood-film.md) establish which cellular populations are abnormal; selected [bone marrow](../../../../../bone-marrow.md) studies, [immunophenotyping](../../../../../immunophenotyping.md) and genetic tests identify the disease. A microscope image of numerous white corpuscles is the beginning of that distinction, not proof of every later molecular category. **The dominant clinical problem can be failure of normal blood formation despite an apparent abundance of white cells.**
