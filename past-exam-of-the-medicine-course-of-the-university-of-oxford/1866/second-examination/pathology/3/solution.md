**The significance of [glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md) and [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md) depends on persistence, amount, mechanism and accompanying findings.** The older words “sugar” and “albumen” do not guarantee that a nineteenth-century test identified one particular molecule.

For urinary sugar, distinguish the following situations:

- In [diabetes](../../../../../diabetes.md), an excessive filtered [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) load can exceed recovery by the [proximal tubule](../../../../../proximal-tubule.md). Persistent [glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md) with measured [hyperglycaemia](../../../../../hyperglycaemia.md), thirst, excessive urination and loss of body mass has much greater diagnostic significance than one weak urinary reaction.
- Transient [hyperglycaemia](../../../../../hyperglycaemia.md) during acute stress or illness can produce transient [glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md). Changes after a large carbohydrate load depend on the individual's renal handling; a positive sample does not by itself establish chronic [diabetes](../../../../../diabetes.md).
- In [renal glycosuria](../../../../../renal-glycosuria.md), impaired tubular recovery permits loss despite an ordinary [blood glucose](../../../../../blood-sugar.md) level. This may be an isolated inherited trait or accompany wider [proximal tubule](../../../../../proximal-tubule.md) dysfunction, as in [Fanconi syndrome](../../../../../fanconi-syndrome.md) or tubular injury. Pregnancy can also lower the effective threshold for urinary loss.
- A specimen can contain other [reducing substances](../../../../../reducing-substance.md), including sugars other than [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md). A general reducing test therefore needs chemical confirmation before its result is labelled [glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md).

Modern glucose-specific assays and concurrent [blood glucose](../../../../../blood-sugar.md) measurements distinguish excess filtered load from reduced recovery.[Technique, nonspecific copper reduction; Basic Science, glucose load, tubular defects and pregnancy](../../../../../glucosuria-in-clinical-methods.md)[Definition and causes, isolated transport defect versus generalized tubular dysfunction](../../../../../renal-glucosuria-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

For “albumen”, the principal pathological groups are injury to the [glomerular filtration barrier](../../../../../glomerular-filtration-barrier.md) in [glomerulonephritis](../../../../../glomerulonephritis.md), [renal amyloidosis](../../../../../renal-amyloidosis.md), [diabetic kidney disease](../../../../../diabetic-kidney-disease.md) and other [glomerular diseases](../../../../../glomerular-disease.md); changes associated with [heart failure](../../../../../heart-failure.md) and renal venous [congestion](../../../../../congestion.md); and pregnancy-associated injury such as [pre-eclampsia](../../../../../pre-eclampsia.md). [Fever](../../../../../fever.md) and severe exertion may produce transient [proteinuria](../../../../../proteinuria.md), while [orthostatic proteinuria](../../../../../orthostatic-proteinuria.md) can disappear during recumbency. Impaired tubular recovery chiefly increases smaller urinary [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md); it should not automatically be called a selective loss of [serum albumin](../../../../../serum-albumin.md).[Pathophysiology; Etiology; functional and orthostatic proteinuria](../../../../../proteinuria-by-merck-manual-professional-edition.md)

A collected specimen can also receive [blood plasma](../../../../../blood-plasma.md) [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) from [haematuria](../../../../../haematuria.md) or inflammatory material below the filtering apparatus. Moreover, [immunoglobulin light chains](../../../../../immunoglobulin-light-chain.md) in [multiple myeloma](../../../../../multiple-myeloma.md) can give a positive general protein test without being [serum albumin](../../../../../serum-albumin.md). Historical heat and acid precipitation tested a physical property of material in a tube, not a modern molecular diagnosis.[Technique and Basic Science, precipitation versus protein identification](../../../../../proteinuria-in-clinical-methods.md)[Clinical Significance, serum proteins accompanying haematuria](../../../../../hematuria-in-clinical-methods.md)

The diagnostic value consequently rises with reproducibility and coherent accompanying evidence. Persistent heavy [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md), especially with [oedema](../../../../../oedema.md) and abnormal sediment, strongly suggests significant renal disease; [red blood cell casts](../../../../../red-blood-cell-cast.md) support a renal rather than merely lower-tract source of bleeding. Repeated [urinalysis](../../../../../urinalysis.md), sediment [microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) and a [urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio](../../../../../urine-albumin-to-creatinine-ratio.md) help quantify and localize the problem.[Detection by dipstick and urine albumin and creatinine measurement](../../../../../albuminuria-albumin-in-the-urine-by-niddk.md) Coexisting [glycosuria](../../../../../glycosuria.md) and [albuminuria](../../../../../albuminuria.md) may reflect [diabetes](../../../../../diabetes.md) with renal injury, or another combination of mechanisms. **Neither their coexistence nor either isolated result is sufficient to establish the whole diagnosis.**
