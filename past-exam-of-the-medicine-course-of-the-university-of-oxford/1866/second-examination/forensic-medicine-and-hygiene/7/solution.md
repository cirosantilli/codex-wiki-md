<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

**Take representative samples of a measured volume, identify the impurity by an appropriate reaction, and express the blank-corrected amount per volume of ward [air](../../../../../air.md).** Excess [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), moisture, organic contamination, [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) and suspended material are the principal groups to investigate; combustion or defective drains may introduce additional gases. Smell alone measures none of them reliably.

Record occupancy, the position and height of sampling, open doors or windows, heating and lighting, temperature and sampling time. Compare indoor and outdoor samples collected under comparable conditions. Avoid breathing into the collecting vessel. A sample near an open window may miss conditions at the beds, and a sample during cleaning may contain unusually disturbed dust. Explain the sampling conditions with the result.

For [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), “carbonic acid” in period terminology, collect a known volume in a calibrated vessel and introduce a measured portion of standardized [barium hydroxide](../../../../../barium-hydroxide.md) solution. Shake to promote absorption. [Carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) consumes the alkaline reagent and forms [barium carbonate](../../../../../barium-carbonate.md):

$$
\mathrm{CO_2+Ba(OH)_2\longrightarrow BaCO_3\downarrow+H_2O}.
$$

Determine the residual alkali in the clear liquid, excluding the carbonate precipitate from an acid determination that would redissolve it. Compare with an equal unexposed reagent portion. An 1864 report describes 7–10 litre globes and checks against gravimetric absorption, so this is an available period method rather than an instrument projected backwards.[p.80, “Note on the Amount of Carbonic Acid contained in the Air of Manchester”: standardized baryta in globes and gravimetric comparison](../../../../../chemical-news-volume-9-1864.md)

If $c$ is the [hydrogen ion](../../../../../hydrogen-ion.md) equivalent concentration of the titrating acid, and $v_b$ and $v_s$ are the respective blank and exposed-solution titration volumes, then, for the entire absorption charge,

$$
\boxed{n_{\mathrm{CO_2}}=\frac{c(v_b-v_s)}{2},\qquad x_{\mathrm{CO_2}}=\frac{n_{\mathrm{CO_2}}RT}{PV_{\mathrm{air}}}.}
$$

The factor two arises because one [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) molecule consumes two alkali equivalents. If only an aliquot is titrated, multiply by the ratio of total solution volume to aliquot volume. Here $P$, $T$ and $V_{\mathrm{air}}$ describe the same measured [gas](../../../../../gas.md) sample, with consistent wet- or dry-gas conventions; $R$ is the [molar gas constant](../../../../../molar-gas-constant.md). Report $10^6x_{\mathrm{CO_2}}$ as [parts per million](../../../../../parts-per-million.md) by volume. Other absorbed acidic gases are potential interferences. The calculation is a stoichiometric and [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) interpretation, not a direct measure of infectiousness.

For [water vapour](../../../../../water-vapor.md), compare dry- and wet-bulb thermometers to estimate [relative humidity](../../../../../relative-humidity.md), or pass a known volume through a suitable preweighed drying absorber and determine its mass gain. The latter gives collected [water](../../../../../water.md) mass divided by sampled [air](../../../../../air.md) volume, with a blank and appropriate control of other absorbable vapours. Humidity is relevant to ward conditions, but is not by itself evidence that the air contains a particular disease agent.

For [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md), moist red [litmus](../../../../../litmus.md) becoming blue is a screening reaction, not a selective quantitative analysis. Absorb a measured volume of [air](../../../../../air.md) into a known amount of dilute acid; the reduction in residual acidity measures absorbed volatile bases. Other alkaline vapours interfere, so do not call the result pure [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) without further discrimination. A modern comparison is the validated acid-treated sorbent collection and [ion chromatography](../../../../../ion-chromatography.md) of the institutional air-sampling method; it too requires calibrated flow, blanks and checks for sample loss.[Sampling, measurement and calculations: acid-treated collection medium, blanks, sample volume and ion chromatography; explicitly a later comparison](../../../../../ammonia-by-ion-chromatography-method-6016-by-the-national-institute-for-occupational-safety-and-health.md)

For suspended dust, collect material on a preweighed filter, condition and reweigh it, and divide the blank-corrected mass increase by sampled volume. [Microscopy](../../../../../microscopy.md) can describe fibres, cells and other particles, but cannot turn an unspecified speck into an identified pathogen. A modern gravimetric sampling method illustrates the importance of controlled filter handling and air-volume measurement.[Sampling and measurement: preweighed filter, gravimetric determination, field blanks and sampled air volume](../../../../../particulates-not-otherwise-regulated-total-method-0500-by-the-national-institute-for-occupational-safety-and-health.md)

Period investigators also assessed oxidizable organic contamination through the consumption of an oxidizing reagent such as [potassium permanganate](../../../../../potassium-permanganate.md). Such a result is an operational measure of chemical consumption, dependent on the procedure; it does not reveal a unique substance or count living organisms. An 1865 review already warned that inorganic reducing substances also decolourized the reagent, making supposed quantitative organic estimates unreliable.[Review of air contamination in mines: discussion of permanganate decolourization and rejection of quantitative organic-matter estimates](../../../../../chemical-news-volume-11-1865.md) Similarly, a lead-salt darkening reaction may support the presence of [hydrogen sulfide](../../../../../hydrogen-sulfide.md) where drains are implicated, but this gas need not be present in every ward. Investigate [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) if combustion is suspected: it is odourless and cannot be excluded because the room smells fresh.[Sources and properties: combustion-related exposure and lack of warning odour](../../../../../carbon-monoxide-poisoning-basics-by-the-centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention.md)

A useful report therefore separates measured [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), humidity, chemical contamination and particles, states detection limits and interferences, and avoids declaring ward air safe from a single result. Infectious [aerosols](../../../../../aerosol.md) require a separate assessment; settling plates or a few microscopic fields do not quantitatively characterize everything that patients inhale. Current infection-control guidance likewise warns about the limitations of nonspecific environmental sampling.[Air sampling: lack of general interpretive standards, sampling-method limitations and the restricted role of settling plates](../../../../../environmental-sampling-by-the-centers-for-disease-control-and-prevention.md)
