# Therapeutics

↑ **Parent:** [Second examination](split.md)

Original volume: [Examination papers for the Degree of Bachelor of Medicine by the University of Oxford (1864–1867)](../../../examination-papers-for-the-degree-of-bachelor-of-medicine-by-the-university-of-oxford-1864-1867.md). [Open the original paper in the scanned volume](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3e/Examination_papers_for_the_Degree_of_Bachelor_of_medicine_-_1864-67_(IA_b24757810).pdf#page=65), PDF pages 65–65.

Original questions are transcribed from the public-domain edition. Credit: Wellcome Collection; original supplied by Royal College of Physicians, London. [Source and public-domain rights statement](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/d7b9zgnx).

These solutions reconstruct therapeutic reasoning available in December 1866 and distinguish it from present medical conclusions. They are not an official answer key. Clinical patterns are explanatory examples, not observations of an actual patient. Historical drugs and procedures are discussed as historical practices, not current prescriptions.
