Original volume: [Examination papers for the Degree of Bachelor of Medicine by the University of Oxford (1864–1867)](../../../examination-papers-for-the-degree-of-bachelor-of-medicine-by-the-university-of-oxford-1864-1867.md). [Open the original paper in the scanned volume](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3e/Examination_papers_for_the_Degree_of_Bachelor_of_medicine_-_1864-67_(IA_b24757810).pdf#page=69), PDF pages 69–69.

Original questions are transcribed from the public-domain edition. Credit: Wellcome Collection; original supplied by Royal College of Physicians, London. [Source and public-domain rights statement](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/d7b9zgnx).

These historical educational solutions distinguish reasoning available in December 1866 from present scientific conclusions. They are not an official answer key. Clinical examples describe possible patterns, not an actual patient's observations. Historical procedures and drugs are identified as period practices, not current prescriptions.
