↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A [lever](../../../../../lever.md) is a body free to rotate about a [fulcrum](../../../../../fulcrum.md). Its usefulness comes from a balance of [torques](../../../../../torque.md), not from producing [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). For a stationary ideal [lever](../../../../../lever.md), let $d_E$ and $d_L$ be the perpendicular [lever arms](../../../../../lever-arm.md) of the effort $E$ and load $L$. Then

$$
E d_E=L d_L,\qquad \boxed{\mathrm{MA}=\frac{L}{E}=\frac{d_E}{d_L}.}
$$

The [mechanical advantage](../../../../../mechanical-advantage.md) uses perpendicular distances to the lines of action, which need not equal distances measured along a bent or obliquely loaded arm. In an ideal slow motion, the effort moves through the larger distance when its [force](../../../../../force-split.md) is the smaller: [work](../../../../../work.md) is conserved.

For the three familiar straight arrangements, the [fulcrum](../../../../../fulcrum.md) lies between effort and load in a first-class [lever](../../../../../lever.md), the load lies between [fulcrum](../../../../../fulcrum.md) and effort in a second-class [lever](../../../../../lever.md), and the effort lies between [fulcrum](../../../../../fulcrum.md) and load in a third-class [lever](../../../../../lever.md). The second class has ideal [mechanical advantage](../../../../../mechanical-advantage.md) greater than one; the third has it less than one. The first can have either, depending on the [lever arms](../../../../../lever-arm.md).

The forearm bends by [elbow flexion](../../../../../elbow-flexion.md). The [biceps brachii](../../../../../biceps-brachii.md) and [brachialis](../../../../../brachialis.md) pull through short [tendons](../../../../../tendon.md) close to the [elbow](../../../../../elbow.md), while a load in the hand acts farther away. This is predominantly a third-class arrangement: a large [muscle force](../../../../../muscle-force.md) buys a comparatively large and rapid hand displacement. The [biceps brachii](../../../../../biceps-brachii.md) also contributes to [forearm supination](../../../../../forearm-supination.md). [Elbow extension](../../../../../elbow-extension.md) is produced chiefly by the [triceps brachii](../../../../../triceps-brachii.md), whose [tendon](../../../../../tendon.md) acts on the [olecranon](../../../../../olecranon.md) behind the [elbow](../../../../../elbow.md). The extensor insertion and forearm load lie on opposite sides of the effective pivot, giving the corresponding first-class analogy. Actual [anatomical joint](../../../../../anatomical-joint.md) motion has changing [muscle moment arms](../../../../../muscle-moment-arm.md), and the [joint reaction force](../../../../../joint-reaction-force.md) also enters the full [force](../../../../../force-split.md) balance; the body is not a set of frictionless pins.
