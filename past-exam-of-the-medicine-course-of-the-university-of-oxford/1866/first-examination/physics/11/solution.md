<h1 id="11/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [11](../11.md)

With the electrodes disconnected, the copper has no sustained external route through which to receive electrons from the zinc. It is not appreciably attacked by a clean, dilute nonoxidizing [sulfuric acid](../../../../../sulfuric-acid.md) solution. Zinc can in principle reduce acid while dissolving, but amalgamation suppresses much of the local action associated with impurities and promotes a high kinetic barrier for hydrogen evolution on the amalgamated surface. Thus the intended historical observation is **little visible action with the circuit open**, not a thermodynamic assertion that zinc cannot react with acid.

Closing the external wire supplies an electron path. At the zinc [anode](../../../../../anode.md),

$$
\mathrm{Zn}\longrightarrow\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}+2e^-;
$$

at the copper [cathode](../../../../../cathode.md),

$$
2\mathrm H^++2e^-\longrightarrow\mathrm H_2.
$$

The net [redox](../../../../../redox.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Zn}+2\mathrm H^+\longrightarrow\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}+\mathrm H_2.}
$$

The zinc dissolves, and bubbles of [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) appear chiefly at the copper. Electrons flow through the wire from zinc to copper; conventional [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) flows oppositely there. Ionic transport through the [electrolyte](../../../../../electrolyte.md) completes the circuit and prevents indefinite charge accumulation in the solution. The copper primarily provides a conducting surface: there is no stipulated copper-ion solution from which metallic copper must be deposited, so this is not the [Daniell cell](../../../../../daniell-cell.md) reaction.

The [chemical energy](../../../../../chemical-energy.md) supplies the electrical [work](../../../../../work.md). Accumulation of hydrogen and changing interfacial conditions can polarize the cell, reducing the current. A complete description therefore distinguishes the initial closed-circuit response from an indefinitely constant-current idealization. [Electrochemical overpotential](../../../../../overpotential.md) explains why a favorable reaction and its observable rate are different questions.
