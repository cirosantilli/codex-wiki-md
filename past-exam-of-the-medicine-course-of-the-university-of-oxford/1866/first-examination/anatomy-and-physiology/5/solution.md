↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**[Chyle](../../../../../chyle.md) is intestinal [lymph](../../../../../lymph.md) enriched with absorbed dietary [lipids](../../../../../lipid.md); its usual route ends in the venous circulation at the left jugulosubclavian junction.** The milky appearance after a fat-containing meal is chiefly due to suspended lipid-rich particles, now called [chylomicrons](../../../../../chylomicron.md).

Formation begins with [lipid digestion](../../../../../lipid-digestion.md) in the [small intestine](../../../../../small-intestine.md). [Pancreatic lipase](../../../../../pancreatic-lipase.md) helps hydrolyse dietary [triglycerides](../../../../../triglyceride.md); [bile salts](../../../../../bile-salt.md) help the digestion products reach the intestinal absorptive surface in [mixed micelles](../../../../../mixed-micelle.md). [Enterocytes](../../../../../enterocyte.md) take up the products, reconstruct much of the long-chain [triglyceride](../../../../../triglyceride.md), and package it with other [lipids](../../../../../lipid.md) and [apolipoproteins](../../../../../apolipoprotein.md) into [chylomicrons](../../../../../chylomicron.md). These leave the basolateral side of the [enterocytes](../../../../../enterocyte.md) and enter the central [lacteals](../../../../../lacteal.md) of the [intestinal villi](../../../../../intestinal-villus.md). Thus the particles carried in [chyle](../../../../../chyle.md) are not simply intact globules of a meal passing through an open hole from the intestinal lumen.[Lipid Absorption: micelles, enterocytes, chylomicrons and lacteals](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/23-7-chemical-digestion-and-absorption-a-closer-look.md)

From the [lacteals](../../../../../lacteal.md), [chyle](../../../../../chyle.md) enters lymphatic channels in the intestinal wall and then collecting vessels in the [mesentery](../../../../../mesentery.md). These vessels pass through [mesenteric lymph nodes](../../../../../mesenteric-lymph-node.md), with afferent vessels entering and efferent vessels leaving the nodes. Successive collectors join the intestinal lymphatic trunk or trunks. They usually communicate with the [cisterna chyli](../../../../../cisterna-chyli.md), an expanded origin of the [thoracic duct](../../../../../thoracic-duct.md) in the upper lumbar region, near the bodies of L1–L2 and to the right of the [aorta](../../../../../aorta.md). The [cisterna chyli](../../../../../cisterna-chyli.md) also receives lymph from lumbar trunks; the stream beyond it therefore contains material from more than the [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md). A conspicuous sac is not present in every individual.

The [thoracic duct](../../../../../thoracic-duct.md) passes through the [aortic hiatus](../../../../../aortic-hiatus.md) of the [respiratory diaphragm](../../../../../respiratory-diaphragm.md) and ascends in the posterior [mediastinum](../../../../../mediastinum.md), typically between the [aorta](../../../../../aorta.md) and [azygos vein](../../../../../azygos-vein.md), behind the [oesophagus](../../../../../oesophagus.md). It crosses towards the left in the middle or upper [thorax](../../../../../thorax.md), continues to the root of the neck and arches to its terminal venous opening. The usual endpoint is the left venous angle, where the [left internal jugular vein](../../../../../left-internal-jugular-vein.md) joins the [left subclavian vein](../../../../../left-subclavian-vein.md); terminal branches and the precise opening show anatomical variation. It is not normally the right venous angle or a direct entrance into the [portal vein](../../../../../portal-vein.md).[Structure and Course: cisterna chyli, thoracic course, left cervical arch and variable venous termination](../../../../../anatomy-thorax-thoracic-duct-statpearls.md)

The flow is promoted by intrinsic contraction of collecting lymphatic vessels, movements of surrounding [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) and respiratory changes of [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). [Lymphatic valves](../../../../../lymphatic-valve.md) limit backward movement. [Lymph nodes](../../../../../lymph-node.md) are interposed biological structures, not pumps that propel every particle independently.[Lymphatic Capillaries; Larger Lymphatic Vessels, Trunks, and Ducts; lymph transport](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/21-1-anatomy-of-the-lymphatic-and-immune-systems.md)

This route applies particularly to the long-chain dietary [lipids](../../../../../lipid.md) carried by [chylomicrons](../../../../../chylomicron.md). Most absorbed [glucose](../../../../../glucose.md) and [amino acids](../../../../../amino-acid.md), and much shorter-chain [fatty acid](../../../../../fatty-acid.md) material, follow portal vascular routes instead. Confusing these pathways would obscure the reason for asking separately about [chyle](../../../../../chyle.md). In a classical anatomical demonstration, the mesenteric channels of an appropriately fed animal appear white, and an injected or dissected preparation can trace their continuity towards the [thoracic duct](../../../../../thoracic-duct.md). The timing after a fatty meal matters: an empty-looking channel in a fasted preparation does not prove absence of a [lymphatic vessel](../../../../../lymphatic-vessel.md). The molecular account of [chylomicrons](../../../../../chylomicron.md) is a modern explanation of the older gross observation.
