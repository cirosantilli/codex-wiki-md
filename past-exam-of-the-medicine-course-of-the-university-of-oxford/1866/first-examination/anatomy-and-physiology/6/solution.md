**[Cowper's glands](../../../../../bulbourethral-gland.md) are the paired [bulbourethral glands](../../../../../bulbourethral-gland.md), deep to the [perineal membrane](../../../../../perineal-membrane.md) on either side of the membranous [urethra](../../../../../urethra.md).** The relevant dissection is of the male anterior [perineum](../../../../../perineum.md), or [urogenital triangle](../../../../../urogenital-triangle.md). A broad exposure reveals structures at different lateral positions; they are not all piled one above another along a single central line.

From superficial to deep, the principal layers and contents are:

- **[Skin](../../../../../skin.md) and superficial [fascia](../../../../../fascia.md).** The superficial fascia includes a fatty layer and a deeper membranous layer, [Colles' fascia](../../../../../colles-fascia.md). The latter attaches laterally to the ischiopubic rami and posteriorly towards the posterior border of the [perineal membrane](../../../../../perineal-membrane.md) and [perineal body](../../../../../perineal-body.md); anteriorly it is continuous with fascia of the scrotum and lower abdominal wall. Superficial vessels and [nerves](../../../../../nerve.md) accompany these layers. Cutaneous arterial supply includes branches of the [perineal artery](../../../../../perineal-artery.md) and, more anteriorly, contributions associated with the external pudendal circulation.
- **The superficial perineal compartment.** Removing the membranous fascia exposes the superficial pouch and the investing fascia over its [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md). Centrally, [bulbospongiosus](../../../../../bulbospongiosus.md) covers the bulb of the penis and proximal [corpus spongiosum](../../../../../corpus-spongiosum.md). On either side, [ischiocavernosus](../../../../../ischiocavernosus.md) covers a crus of the penis. Posteriorly, the [superficial transverse perineal muscle](../../../../../superficial-transverse-perineal-muscle.md) runs towards the [perineal body](../../../../../perineal-body.md). Older descriptions call these the accelerator urinae, erector penis and transversus perinei respectively. The names should be matched to the structures rather than counted as additional [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md).
- **Superficial perineal vessels.** The [perineal artery](../../../../../perineal-artery.md), a branch of the [internal pudendal artery](../../../../../internal-pudendal-artery.md), supplies this region and gives posterior scrotal and muscular branches; accompanying [veins](../../../../../vein.md) drain it. These vessels lie in relation to the pouch and its [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md), rather than constituting a complete vascular sheet. The bulb and crura are the associated [erectile tissues](../../../../../erectile-tissue.md), not additional membranes.
- **The [perineal membrane](../../../../../perineal-membrane.md).** This dense fibrous sheet spans the anterior pelvic outlet between the ischiopubic rami. It is the structure traditionally described as the triangular ligament's inferior layer. It is pierced by the [urethra](../../../../../urethra.md) and by the ducts of the [bulbourethral glands](../../../../../bulbourethral-gland.md), as well as related vascular passages. The [artery of the bulb of the penis](../../../../../artery-of-the-bulb-of-the-penis.md) passes to the bulb in close relation to the membrane. The deep and dorsal penile arterial routes lie more anteriorly; they should be identified where the exposure reaches them rather than imagined to cross every central dissection plane.
- **The deep perineal structures.** Beyond the [perineal membrane](../../../../../perineal-membrane.md) are the [external urethral sphincter](../../../../../external-urethral-sphincter.md) complex, traditionally described deep transverse perineal fibres, the membranous [urethra](../../../../../urethra.md) and the [bulbourethral glands](../../../../../bulbourethral-gland.md). The glands lie in close relation to sphincteric [skeletal muscle](../../../../../skeletal-muscle.md). Their ducts run forwards through the [perineal membrane](../../../../../perineal-membrane.md) and open into the bulbar [urethra](../../../../../urethra.md), so the position of a duct's opening is not the position of its gland.

The superficial [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md), erectile bodies and pudendal branches identify the shallow compartment; the [perineal membrane](../../../../../perineal-membrane.md) separates it from the gland-bearing deeper region.[Structure and Function; Blood Supply and Lymphatics; Muscles](../../../../../anatomy-abdomen-and-pelvis-superficial-perineal-space-statpearls.md) The deep space contains the sphincter complex and male [bulbourethral glands](../../../../../bulbourethral-gland.md), with their ducts traversing the membrane.[Introduction; Structure and Function; Blood Supply and Lymphatics](../../../../../anatomy-abdomen-and-pelvis-deep-perineal-space-statpearls.md)

The main [internal pudendal vessels](../../../../../internal-pudendal-vessels.md) themselves run laterally in the [pudendal canal](../../../../../pudendal-canal.md), in relation to the fascia over [obturator internus](../../../../../obturator-internus.md), before their terminal distribution. A median approach to a gland does not necessarily expose their whole trunks. The [deep dorsal vein of the penis](../../../../../deep-dorsal-vein-of-the-penis.md) has a distinct anterior route beneath the pubic arch and should not be confused with the small accompanying perineal [veins](../../../../../vein.md). These positional qualifications are necessary for an accurate enumeration of the vessels one may meet.

Nineteenth-century descriptions often arranged the deep structures as a [urogenital diaphragm](../../../../../urogenital-diaphragm.md) enclosed between two neat fascial layers, the superior and inferior layers of the triangular ligament. Modern dissection finds a more complex sphincteric and connective-tissue arrangement. The historically named layers explain the older vocabulary, but the [perineal membrane](../../../../../perineal-membrane.md) and surrounding [muscles](../../../../../muscle.md) should not be made into a fictitious uniform sandwich. Nor does reaching the [bulbourethral glands](../../../../../bulbourethral-gland.md) require dissecting through the whole [pelvic diaphragm](../../../../../pelvic-diaphragm.md) above them.
