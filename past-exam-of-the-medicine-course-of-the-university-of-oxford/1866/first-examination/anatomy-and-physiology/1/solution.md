**The [nervous system](../../../../../nervous-system.md) regulates visceral movement, [glandular secretion](../../../../../glandular-secretion.md), [blood flow](../../../../../blood-flow.md) and coordinated bodily responses, while also influencing [nutrition](../../../../../nutrition.md) indirectly through those functions.** Organic or vegetative life here means activities maintaining the body, such as [blood circulation](../../../../../blood-circulation.md), [digestion](../../../../../digestion.md), [pulmonary ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md), secretion and excretion. It does not designate an independent form of living substance.

One influence is directly motor. [Autonomic nerves](../../../../../autonomic-nerve.md) alter the rate and force of the [heart](../../../../../heart.md), contract or relax [smooth muscle](../../../../../smooth-muscle.md) in the [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md), [urinary bladder](../../../../../urinary-bladder.md), airways and [blood vessels](../../../../../blood-vessel.md), and coordinate movement with the opening and closure of sphincters. Their effect need not be excitatory: an active [vagus nerve](../../../../../vagus-nerve.md) can slow the [heart](../../../../../heart.md), while other pathways accelerate it. In the modern account, [sympathetic](../../../../../sympathetic.md) and [parasympathetic](../../../../../parasympathetic.md) effects depend on the target and its [receptors](../../../../../receptor-biochemistry.md); they are not respectively universal accelerator and brake systems. Some organs receive both divisions, while others do not have a simple balanced double supply.[Opening comparison; Sympathetic Division; Parasympathetic Division; target-specific actions](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/15-1-divisions-of-the-autonomic-nervous-system.md)

A second influence is secretomotor. Activity in appropriate [nerves](../../../../../nerve.md) can increase [salivary secretion](../../../../../salivary-secretion.md), alter [gastric acid secretion](../../../../../gastric-acid-secretion.md) and promote [sweating](../../../../../sweating.md). This is distinguishable from moving a duct or changing its calibre. A convincing experiment collects a secretion from a cannulated duct while a [nerve](../../../../../nerve.md) is stimulated, rather than inferring secretion from a change of colour or organ size. For example, stimulation of the [chorda tympani](../../../../../chorda-tympani.md) can evoke fluid from the [submandibular gland](../../../../../submandibular-gland.md). The response depends on functioning secretory [biological cells](../../../../../biological-cell-split.md); the [nerve](../../../../../nerve.md) communicates a command rather than supplying the material constituents of saliva.

A third influence is vasomotor. Changing the calibre of [arterioles](../../../../../arteriole.md) changes local [vascular resistance](../../../../../vascular-resistance.md) and [perfusion](../../../../../perfusion.md). The classic comparison of an ear before and after interruption of its cervical [sympathetic](../../../../../sympathetic.md) supply illustrates loss of vasoconstrictor tone: the vessels dilate and the ear becomes warmer because more warm [blood](../../../../../blood.md) reaches it. Stimulation of the peripheral cut pathway can produce the converse vascular effect. Warmth therefore need not mean that the local [nerves](../../../../../nerve.md) themselves generate more [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md). Vascular control also changes the delivery of [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) and [nutrients](../../../../../nutrient.md) to a [gland](../../../../../gland.md) or [tissue](../../../../../tissue-biology.md), so it can modify function indirectly.

These outputs are organized by sensory feedback and [reflexes](../../../../../reflex.md). Distension of the [intestine](../../../../../intestine.md) can evoke coordinated movement; changes in [blood pressure](../../../../../blood-pressure.md) or blood-gas composition influence [heart rate](../../../../../heart-rate.md) and [breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md). Local circuits in the [enteric nervous system](../../../../../enteric-nervous-system.md) permit some digestive activity without an intact connection to the [brain](../../../../../brain.md), while longer [reflexes](../../../../../reflex.md) involve the [spinal cord](../../../../../spinal-cord.md) or [brainstem](../../../../../brainstem.md). Central states also matter: the sight or expectation of food can initiate preparatory digestive responses, and fear can alter [heart rate](../../../../../heart-rate.md), [sweating](../../../../../sweating.md) and visceral movement.[Long and Short Reflexes; control of cardiovascular and digestive functions](../../../../../anatomy-and-physiology-2e-by-j-gordon-betts-and-others/15-2-autonomic-reflexes-and-homeostasis.md)

Somatic motor activity also supports vegetative functions. [Breathing](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) uses the [respiratory diaphragm](../../../../../respiratory-diaphragm.md) and other [skeletal muscles](../../../../../skeletal-muscle.md); swallowing and continence use coordinated [skeletal muscle](../../../../../skeletal-muscle.md) as well as [smooth muscle](../../../../../smooth-muscle.md). Thus organic function cannot be identified solely with the anatomical distribution of the [sympathetic nervous system](../../../../../sympathetic-nervous-system.md).

The historical idea of a direct trophic influence requires discrimination. A [denervation](../../../../../denervation.md) region may change through loss of movement, altered [perfusion](../../../../../perfusion.md), loss of protective sensation or changed secretion. Those observations alone do not establish a special nutritive fluid flowing down the [nerve](../../../../../nerve.md). Modern biology recognizes molecular signalling between [nerves](../../../../../nerve.md) and their targets, as well as [neuroendocrine signalling](../../../../../neuroendocrine-signalling.md) pathways: the [hypothalamus](../../../../../hypothalamus.md) can influence [hormone](../../../../../hormone.md) release, and [sympathetic](../../../../../sympathetic.md) input can evoke [adrenal medulla](../../../../../adrenal-medulla.md) secretion. These are mechanisms added to, rather than evidence for an undefined historical nutritive force. A useful experimental design measures the motor, vascular and secretory consequences separately before assigning a residual effect to direct trophic action.
