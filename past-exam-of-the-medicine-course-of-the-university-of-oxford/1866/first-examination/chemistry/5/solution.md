<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**Purified [coal gas](../../../../../coal-gas.md) is mainly [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md), [methane](../../../../../methane.md) and [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md), with smaller quantities of other [hydrocarbons](../../../../../hydrocarbon.md) and diluent gases.** It is made by heating [coal](../../../../../coal.md) in retorts with little or no admitted [air](../../../../../air.md), not by completely burning the [coal](../../../../../coal.md).

The hot products include permanent gases, condensable organic vapours and [water vapour](../../../../../water-vapor.md), leaving [coke](../../../../../coke.md) in the retort. Cooling removes much of the [coal tar](../../../../../coal-tar.md) and condenses [water](../../../../../water.md), which captures [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) as [ammoniacal liquor](../../../../../ammoniacal-liquor.md). Further washing and purification remove much of the remaining [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md), [hydrogen sulfide](../../../../../hydrogen-sulfide.md) and other objectionable constituents before the [gas](../../../../../gas.md) is distributed. A crude retort product and purified household [coal gas](../../../../../coal-gas.md) therefore have different compositions.

[Molecular hydrogen](../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md) is commonly the largest component by [volume](../../../../../volume.md). [Methane](../../../../../methane.md) is another major combustible constituent, while [carbon monoxide](../../../../../carbon-monoxide.md) contributes fuel value and serious toxicity. [Ethylene](../../../../../ethylene.md) and other unsaturated [hydrocarbons](../../../../../hydrocarbon.md), together with vapours such as [benzene](../../../../../benzene.md), contribute strongly to the luminous flame. Their thermal breakdown can produce incandescent [soot](../../../../../soot.md), explaining why a small fraction of heavier [hydrocarbons](../../../../../hydrocarbon.md) can disproportionately affect lighting quality. Smaller or variable quantities of [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and [molecular nitrogen](../../../../../molecular-nitrogen.md) dilute the combustible mixture. [Molecular oxygen](../../../../../molecular-oxygen.md) is not an intended major constituent; air ingress can change the mixture.

There is no single balanced [chemical equation](../../../../../chemical-equation.md) for making [coal gas](../../../../../coal-gas.md): [coal](../../../../../coal.md) is a complex feed and produces many products simultaneously. Nor is one exact percentage composition universal; it depends on the [coal](../../../../../coal.md), retort [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), operating conditions and purification. The main distinction is between the light combustible gases, the smaller illuminating hydrocarbon fraction, and contaminants or diluents. Modern natural gas, usually rich in [methane](../../../../../methane.md), should not be used as a synonym for historical [coal gas](../../../../../coal-gas.md).
