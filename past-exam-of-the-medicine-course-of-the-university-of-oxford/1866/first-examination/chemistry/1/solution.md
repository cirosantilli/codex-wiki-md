**[Chemical elements](../../../../../chemical-element.md) are the constituents from which ordinary [chemical compounds](../../../../../chemical-compound.md) are built; many familiar materials are [mixtures](../../../../../mixture-split.md) of those [chemical compounds](../../../../../chemical-compound.md) and sometimes free [chemical elements](../../../../../chemical-element.md).** In the historical operational definition, a [chemical element](../../../../../chemical-element.md) could not be resolved into simpler substances by chemical means. In the modern definition, its [chemical atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) all have the same [atomic number](../../../../../atomic-number.md). Ordinary [chemical reactions](../../../../../chemical-reaction-split.md) rearrange those [chemical atoms](../../../../../atom-physics.md) rather than creating a new [chemical element](../../../../../chemical-element.md).

[Air](../../../../../air.md) is a [mixture](../../../../../mixture-split.md), chiefly of [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), whose components can be separated or selectively consumed. For example, burning [phosphorus](../../../../../phosphorus.md) in a suitable enclosed sample removes most of the [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) and leaves chiefly [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md); the residual [gas](../../../../../gas.md) has different properties from the original [air](../../../../../air.md). [Air](../../../../../air.md) also contains [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), variable [water vapour](../../../../../water-vapor.md) and smaller amounts of other gases. Its variable composition is incompatible with treating it as one elementary substance.

[Water](../../../../../water.md) is a [chemical compound](../../../../../chemical-compound.md): [electrolysis](../../../../../electrolysis.md) produces [molecular hydrogen](../../../../../molecular-hydrogen.md) and [molecular oxygen](../../../../../molecular-oxygen.md), approximately in the [volume](../../../../../volume.md) ratio $2:1$ at the same [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). Their recombination produces [water](../../../../../water.md) again:

$$
\boxed{2\mathrm H_2+\mathrm O_2\longrightarrow2\mathrm H_2\mathrm O.}
$$

Thus [water](../../../../../water.md) has fixed composition but is chemically decomposable, whereas [air](../../../../../air.md) has no fixed single [chemical formula](../../../../../chemical-formula.md). Neither belongs with the [chemical elements](../../../../../chemical-element.md) merely because it is common and widespread on the Earth's surface.
