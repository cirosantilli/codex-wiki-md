↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**[Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) is the volatile [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md)–[hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) [chemical compound](../../../../../chemical-compound.md) $\mathrm{NH}_3$, obtained historically from ammoniacal salts and from the decomposition of nitrogen-containing organic matter.** Sources include the decay of animal and vegetable material, the conversion of [urea](../../../../../urea.md) in stale urine, and the destructive distillation of [coal](../../../../../coal.md), bones or horn. In gasworks, much of the [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) was captured in the aqueous [ammoniacal liquor](../../../../../ammoniacal-liquor.md) during cooling and washing of crude [coal gas](../../../../../coal-gas.md). These sources do not mean that nitrogen-free constituents of [coal](../../../../../coal.md) spontaneously produce [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md).

An [ammonium](../../../../../ammonium.md) salt releases [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) when heated with a suitable [chemical base](../../../../../base-chemistry.md). For [ammonium chloride](../../../../../ammonium-chloride.md) and [slaked lime](../../../../../slaked-lime.md),

$$
\boxed{2\mathrm{NH}_4\mathrm{Cl}+\mathrm{Ca(OH)}_2\longrightarrow2\mathrm{NH}_3+\mathrm{CaCl}_2+2\mathrm H_2\mathrm O.}
$$

The ionic principle is $\mathrm{NH}_4^++\mathrm{OH}^-\to\mathrm{NH}_3+\mathrm H_2\mathrm O$. A generating vessel supplies a drying stage and then an inverted dry collecting vessel in which the lighter [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) displaces [air](../../../../../air.md). It is unsuitable for ordinary collection over [water](../../../../../water.md) because it dissolves so readily. [Quicklime](../../../../../calcium-oxide.md) can serve as a drying agent; a strongly acidic drying material would instead consume the [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md).

[Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) is a colourless [gas](../../../../../gas.md) with a penetrating, irritating odour. Its approximate [relative density](../../../../../relative-density.md) against [air](../../../../../air.md) is $17.03/28.97\simeq0.59$. It is readily liquefied under suitable cooling or [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), and its dissolution in [water](../../../../../water.md) releases [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md). It is toxic and corrosive at sufficient exposure; direct smelling is not an analytical requirement.[Physical Description, Molecular Weight, Solubility and Symptoms](../../../../../ammonia-in-the-niosh-pocket-guide.md)

The aqueous [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{NH}_3+\mathrm H_2\mathrm O\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{NH}_4^++\mathrm{OH}^-}
$$

explains the alkaline reaction and the change of moist red [litmus](../../../../../litmus.md) to blue. The historical name “ammonium hydroxide” for the solution should not imply that every dissolved molecule has become a discrete molecule of $\mathrm{NH}_4\mathrm{OH}$. With [hydrochloric acid](../../../../../hydrochloric-acid.md), [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) forms [ammonium chloride](../../../../../ammonium-chloride.md):

$$
\mathrm{NH}_3+\mathrm{HCl}\longrightarrow\mathrm{NH}_4\mathrm{Cl}.
$$

The familiar white cloud where the gases meet consists of fine solid salt particles, rather than liquid [water](../../../../../water.md) alone. In solution, [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) also acts as a [ligand](../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md); an excess can produce a deep-blue copper–ammine [coordination complex](../../../../../coordination-complex.md) from suitable [copper(II)](../../../../../copper-ii.md) solutions. Although [Ammonia](../../../../../ammonia.md) does not readily sustain an ordinary flame in [air](../../../../../air.md), it is not intrinsically incapable of [combustion](../../../../../combustion.md); sufficiently favourable mixtures burn.
