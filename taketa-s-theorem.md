<h1 id="taketa-s-theorem">Taketa's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Monomial group](monomial-group.md)

Every finite [monomial group](monomial-group.md) is a [solvable group](solvable-group.md). Thus expressing every [irreducible character](irreducible-character.md) as induction from a [linear character](linear-character.md) imposes a group-theoretic solvability condition.
