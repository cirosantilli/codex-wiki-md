# First variation

↑ **Parent:** [Variation](variation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_variation)

The first variation of a [functional](functional.md) $L$ at $y$ in the direction $\eta$ is

$$
\delta L[y;\eta]
=\left.\frac d{d\varepsilon}L[y+\varepsilon\eta]\right|_{\varepsilon=0}.
$$
