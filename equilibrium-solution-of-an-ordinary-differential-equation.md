# Equilibrium solution of an ordinary differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

A constant [function](function-split.md) $y(x)=c$ is an equilibrium solution of $y'=F(x,y)$ on an interval when $F(x,c)=0$ throughout that interval. Dividing by a factor such as $y-c$ during [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) excludes this solution, so it must be checked separately. For an [autonomous differential equation](autonomous-system-mathematics.md), the constant state is an [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md).
