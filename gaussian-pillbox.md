# Gaussian pillbox

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)

A Gaussian pillbox is a thin cylindrical [Gaussian surface](gaussian-surface.md) crossing an interface. Shrinking its height converts a divergence law into a jump condition for the field's normal component.
