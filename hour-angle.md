# Hour angle

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial coordinate system](equatorial-coordinate-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hour_angle)

The [hour angle](hour-angle.md) of a direction is the westward [angle](angle.md) from the upper [celestial meridian](celestial-meridian.md) to its hour circle. In a consistent equinox convention, $h=\mathrm{LST}-\alpha$ modulo one turn, where $\alpha$ is [right ascension](right-ascension.md) and LST is local [sidereal time](sidereal-time.md).
