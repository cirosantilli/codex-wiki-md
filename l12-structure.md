# L12 structure

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)

An ordered cubic $A_3B$ structure with B at cube corners and A at face centres. The chemically labelled [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) is primitive cubic; its [superlattice reflections](superlattice-reflection.md) distinguish it from a chemically disordered [face-centered cubic lattice](face-centered-cubic-lattice.md).
