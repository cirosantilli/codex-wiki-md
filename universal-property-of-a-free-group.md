# Universal property of a free group

↑ **Parent:** [Free group](free-group.md)

For every function $f:S\to G$ from a set into a [group](group-split.md), there is a unique [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $\bar f:F(S)\to G$ whose restriction to $S$ is $f$.
