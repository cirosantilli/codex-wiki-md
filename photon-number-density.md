# Photon number density

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature scaling of thermal photon number and energy densities](temperature-scaling-of-thermal-photon-number-and-energy-densities.md)

In natural units, a thermal photon gas with two polarizations has

$$
n_\gamma=\frac{2\zeta(3)}{\pi^2}T^3.
$$
