# Open slicing of anti-de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

An [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) with $p=-\varrho_0$ and constant negative [energy density](energy-density.md) $\varrho_0$ has $\alpha^2=-8\pi G\varrho_0/3>0$ and obeys $\dot a^2+k=-\alpha^2a^2$. Positive solutions exist only for $k=-1$. They are $a=\alpha^{-1}\sin[\alpha(t-t_B)]$ on $0<\alpha(t-t_B)<\pi$. This is an open slicing of [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md), whose [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) is $-12\alpha^2$ and [Kretschmann scalar](kretschmann-scalar.md) is $24\alpha^4$. The zeros mark boundaries of this slicing rather than [curvature singularities](curvature-singularity.md).
