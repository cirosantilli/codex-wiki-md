# Total divergences in a variational principle

↑ **Parent:** [Total divergence](total-divergence.md)

Adding $dF(q,t)/dt$ to a mechanical [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) changes its [action](action.md) by $F(q_f,t_f)-F(q_i,t_i)$. Fixed endpoint [variations](variation.md) therefore leave its bulk [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) unchanged. For fields, adding $\partial_\mu K^\mu$ changes the action by a boundary flux. The same conclusion holds for compactly supported variations or appropriate fixed boundary data. If $K$ depends on field derivatives, those boundary data must also control their variations. Such terms can still change canonical momenta, boundary observables, and representatives of [Noether currents](noether-current.md); equivalence of bulk equations does not mean that every boundary-value problem is identical.
