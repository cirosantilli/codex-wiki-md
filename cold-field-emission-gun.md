# Cold field emission gun

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscope](electron-microscope.md)

A [cold field emission gun](cold-field-emission-gun.md) produces an [electron](electron.md) beam by field emission without the high emitter temperature of a thermal source. A narrow energy spread can improve temporal coherence and high-frequency contrast in [cryo-electron microscopy](cryo-electron-microscopy.md).
