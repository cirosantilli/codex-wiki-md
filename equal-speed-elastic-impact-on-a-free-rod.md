# Equal-speed elastic impact on a free rod

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

A [point mass](point-mass.md) $m$ strikes a free rod of equal [mass](mass.md), initially at rest, perpendicularly at distance $h$ from its [center of mass](center-of-mass.md). Assume a smooth [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md) and rod [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$ about its centre. The final translational [speeds](speed.md) are equal precisely when $h^2=2I/m$. Indeed, [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) then fixes both final [velocities](velocity.md) to $v_0/2$; angular [impulse](impulse.md) gives $I\omega=mv_0h/2$, and conservation of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) gives $I\omega^2=mv_0^2/2$. Eliminating $\omega$ proves the formula. A uniform rod has $I=ml^2/12$, hence $|h|=l/\sqrt6$, which lies within the rod.
