# Shifted addition on odd integers

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)

The [odd integers](odd-integer.md) form an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) under the displayed operation. The [identity element](identity-element.md) is $-1$ and the [inverse element](inverse-element.md) of $a$ is $-a-2$. The [bijection](bijection.md) $a\mapsto(a+1)/2$ is a [group isomorphism](group-isomorphism.md) to $(\mathbb Z,+)$, since it converts the shifted operation into addition. This illustrates how an unfamiliar identity and inverse can arise from ordinary addition by a change of coordinates.
