# Wall shear stress

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary layer](boundary-layer.md)

For a stationary flat plate in a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md), the streamwise [shear stress](shear-stress.md) exerted by the [fluid](fluid.md) on its upper face is

$$
\tau_w=\mu\left.\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right|_{y=0}.
$$

The sign here is positive when the [fluid](fluid.md) pulls the plate downstream; the plate exerts the opposite tangential [traction](traction.md) on the [fluid](fluid.md). In a [Blasius boundary layer](blasius-boundary-layer.md), $\tau_w\simeq0.332057\rho U^2/\sqrt{\mathrm{Re}_x}$. A growing [boundary layer thickness](boundary-layer-thickness.md) therefore accompanies decreasing local [wall shear stress](wall-shear-stress.md), because its near-wall [velocity](velocity.md) [gradient](gradient.md) becomes gentler. The formal divergence as $x\to0$ is integrable, but that leading-edge limit lies outside the thin-layer hypothesis.[Boundary-layer Thickness, Skin friction, and Energy dissipation](modules-on-high-reynolds-number-flows/boundary-layer-thickness-skin-friction-and-energy-dissipation.md)
