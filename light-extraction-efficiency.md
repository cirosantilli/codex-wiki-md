# Light extraction efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Light-emitting diode efficiency](light-emitting-diode-efficiency.md)

The fraction of internally emitted [photons](photon.md) that escape a [light-emitting diode](light-emitting-diode.md) across the specified optical boundary. Collection by a particular detector adds another factor unless the measurement accounts for all escaping [light](light.md).
