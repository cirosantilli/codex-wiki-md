# Alternating proximal-gradient operator

↑ **Parent:** [Proximal gradient method](proximal-gradient-method.md)

For a jointly smooth [convex function](convex-function.md) $f(x,y)$, update $y$ implicitly by its proximal minimization at fixed $x$, then update $x$ explicitly with the gradient at the mixed point $(x,y^+)$. The combined map is $(\tau L/2)$-averaged for $0<\tau L<2$, as follows by applying [cocoercivity](cocoercivity.md) at two mixed points. Its fixed points are precisely the minimizers. If a minimizer exists, averaged iteration proves convergence.
