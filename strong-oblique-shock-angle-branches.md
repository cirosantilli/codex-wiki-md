# Strong oblique-shock angle branches

↑ **Parent:** [Oblique shock](oblique-shock.md)

Measure shock angle $\beta$ from the incident flow to the shock front, and deflection $\theta$ towards it. The strong normal-compression ratio and continuity of tangential [velocity](velocity.md) give $\tan\beta=[(\gamma+1)/(\gamma-1)]\tan(\beta-\theta)$. Angle-addition identities reduce this to $\sin(2\beta-\theta)=\gamma\sin\theta$. The two title branches exist for $0<\theta<\arcsin(1/\gamma)$ and merge at $\beta=\pi/4+\theta/2$. As $\theta\to0$, their formal limits are $(\gamma+1)\theta/2$ and $\pi/2-(\gamma-1)\theta/2$. The weak-branch expansion requires $M\sin\beta\gg1$; at finite [Mach number](mach-number.md) its true zero-deflection limit is the [Mach angle](mach-angle.md).
