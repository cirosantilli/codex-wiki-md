# Premetastatic niche

↑ **Parent:** [Medicine](medicine.md)

A tissue environment altered before arrival of malignant cells in a way that can favour subsequent metastatic establishment. Conditioning must be distinguished from effects of [tumour](tumour.md) cells already present.
