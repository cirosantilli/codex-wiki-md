# Scalar transport

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

[Scalar transport](scalar-transport.md) follows a material quantity through [advection](advection.md) and [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) terms, for example $\Theta_t+\mathbf U\cdot\nabla\Theta=\kappa\Delta\Theta$. A [passive scalar](passive-scalar.md) does not affect the [velocity field](velocity-field.md); an [active scalar](active-scalar.md) feeds back through forces such as [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). With [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) bounded by impermeable walls and homogeneous [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md), the spatial mean is conserved.
