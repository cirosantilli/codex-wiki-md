# Frobenius series for the Laguerre differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Laguerre differential equation](laguerre-differential-equation.md)

A [Frobenius solution](frobenius-solution.md) $y=\sum_{k\geq0}a_kx^{k+\sigma}$ has the repeated [indicial root](indicial-root.md) $\sigma=0$. Equating coefficients gives the displayed [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md). The resulting analytic solution is an [entire function](entire-function.md): its consecutive term ratio has magnitude asymptotic to $|x|/k$. For $\nu=n\geq0$ an integer, the recurrence terminates and $L_n(x)=\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k\binom nkx^k/k!$. The second local solution is independent and has a logarithmic term at zero; repeated [indicial roots](indicial-root.md) do not supply two independent analytic series.
