# CPT constraints on particle properties

↑ **Parent:** [CPT theorem](cpt-theorem.md)

In a [CPT](cpt-symmetry.md)-invariant vacuum, a [subatomic particle](subatomic-particle.md) and its [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) have equal masses and total [decay widths](decay-width.md), equal spin magnitudes and opposite conserved additive charges. [CPT](cpt-symmetry.md) exchanges initial and final states in a scattering experiment; it relates a process to the reversed process with antiparticles and reversed spins. It does not force equality of every pair of conjugate partial [decay widths](decay-width.md) when [CP violation](cp-violation.md) is present.
