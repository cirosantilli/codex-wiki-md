# H1 non-tangential maximal inequality on the disk

↑ **Parent:** [H1 space on the unit disk](h1-space-on-the-unit-disk.md)

For an analytic [H1 space on the unit disk](h1-space-on-the-unit-disk.md) function, its fixed-aperture [non-tangential maximal function](non-tangential-maximal-function.md) obeys the displayed strong $L^1$ bound. First replace $f$ by $f_R(z)=f(Rz)$, $R<1$. The fact that [modulus powers of holomorphic functions are subharmonic](modulus-powers-of-holomorphic-functions-are-subharmonic.md) gives $|f_R|^{1/2}\le P[|f(R\xi)|^{1/2}]$. Since a [Poisson maximal function is dominated by the Hardy-Littlewood maximal function](poisson-maximal-function-is-dominated-by-the-hardy-littlewood-maximal-function.md), the strong $L^2$ [Hardy-Littlewood maximal inequality](hardy-littlewood-maximal-inequality.md) yields $\int N_Af_R\le C_A\int|f(R\xi)|\le C_A\|f\|_1$. Pointwise interior convergence and the [Fatou lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) remove $R$. Analyticity permits this endpoint estimate; general $L^1$ boundary data have only a weak $L^1$ maximal bound.
