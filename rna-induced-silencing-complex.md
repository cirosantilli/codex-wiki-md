# RNA-induced silencing complex

↑ **Parent:** [RNA interference](rna-interference.md)

An [Argonaute](argonaute.md)-containing effector complex guided by small [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md). Target binding can produce cleavage, [translational repression](translational-repression.md) or [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) destabilization depending on the guide, effector and target.
