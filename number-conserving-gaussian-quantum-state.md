# Number-conserving Gaussian quantum state

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian quantum state](gaussian-quantum-state.md)

Here number-conserving means invariant under a global particle-number [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) rotation: the field mean and anomalous averages $\langle\psi\psi\rangle$ vanish. It does not mean a sharp total particle number. All normal correlations are obtained by Wick pairings of the [one-body density matrix](one-body-density-matrix.md). Independent thermal bosonic modes satisfy this rule; generic nonvacuum bosonic [number states](number-state.md) do not. A [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md) is the analogous fermionic pairing state.
