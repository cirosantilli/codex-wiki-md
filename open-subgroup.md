# Open subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)

An open [subgroup](subgroup.md) is also closed, since its complement is the union of its other open [cosets](coset.md). Conversely a closed subgroup need not be open, as $\mathbb Z\subset\mathbb R$ shows.
