## Plant morphology

↑ **Parent:** [Botany](botany.md)

Study of the form and organization of [plant](organism.md#plant) structures.

### Shoot

↑ **Parent:** [Plant morphology](#plant-morphology)

A [plant stem](#plant-stem) with its associated [leaves](organism.md#leaf) and [buds](#bud).

### Peduncle

↑ **Parent:** [Plant morphology](#plant-morphology)

The stalk supporting an [inflorescence](#inflorescence) or a solitary [flower](organism.md#flower).

### Inflorescence

↑ **Parent:** [Plant morphology](#plant-morphology)

The arrangement of [flowers](organism.md#flower) on a flowering axis, or the flowering system itself.

#### Flower head

↑ **Parent:** [Inflorescence](#inflorescence)

An [inflorescence](#inflorescence) of closely packed [flowers](organism.md#flower) on a common expanded support, as in the [common daisy](organism.md#bellis-perennis).

#### Pedicel

↑ **Parent:** [Inflorescence](#inflorescence)

The stalk of one [flower](organism.md#flower) within an [inflorescence](#inflorescence).

#### Umbel

↑ **Parent:** [Inflorescence](#inflorescence)

An [inflorescence](#inflorescence) with stalks arising together at the top of a [peduncle](#peduncle).[T–U, entry “umbel”](#glossary-of-botanical-terms-by-plantnet)

##### Compound umbel

↑ **Parent:** [Umbel](#umbel)

An [inflorescence](#inflorescence) whose primary branches arise together and each end in an [umbellet](#umbellet).

###### Umbellet

↑ **Parent:** [Compound umbel](#compound-umbel)

A secondary [umbel](#umbel) within a [compound umbel](#compound-umbel).

#### Spadix

↑ **Parent:** [Inflorescence](#inflorescence)

An [inflorescence](#inflorescence) with a thickened axis bearing closely attached [flowers](organism.md#flower), commonly associated with a [spathe](#spathe).

### Bract

↑ **Parent:** [Plant morphology](#plant-morphology)

A modified [leaf](organism.md#leaf) associated with a [flower](organism.md#flower) or [inflorescence](#inflorescence).

#### Involucre

↑ **Parent:** [Bract](#bract)

A ring or several rings of [bracts](#bract) surrounding a [flower](organism.md#flower) or [inflorescence](#inflorescence).[I–L, entry “involucre”, sense 1](#glossary-of-botanical-terms-by-plantnet)

#### Spathe

↑ **Parent:** [Bract](#bract)

A sheathing [bract](#bract) enclosing a [flower](organism.md#flower) or [inflorescence](#inflorescence), especially a [spadix](#spadix).[S, entry “spathe”](#glossary-of-botanical-terms-by-plantnet)

### Bud

↑ **Parent:** [Plant morphology](#plant-morphology)

An undeveloped [shoot](#shoot) or [flower](organism.md#flower), often enclosed by protective [leaves](organism.md#leaf).

### Plant stem

↑ **Parent:** [Plant morphology](#plant-morphology)

An axis of a [plant](organism.md#plant) bearing [leaves](organism.md#leaf) at [stem nodes](#stem-node), separated by [internodes](#internode). A [rhizome](#rhizome) is a specialized [plant stem](#plant-stem).

#### Rhizome

↑ **Parent:** [Plant stem](#plant-stem)

A persistent, usually horizontal [plant stem](#plant-stem) at or beneath the ground surface, bearing [stem nodes](#stem-node), [buds](#bud) and often reduced [leaves](organism.md#leaf). [Ginger](organism.md#ginger) provides an example.[Plant description](#ginger-by-the-royal-botanic-gardens-kew)

#### Internode

↑ **Parent:** [Plant stem](#plant-stem)

The portion of a [plant stem](#plant-stem) between successive [stem nodes](#stem-node).

#### Stem node

↑ **Parent:** [Plant stem](#plant-stem)

A point on a [plant stem](#plant-stem) where a [leaf](organism.md#leaf) is attached.

## Plant physiology

↑ **Parent:** [Botany](botany.md)

Study of the functions of [plants](organism.md#plant), including [photosynthesis](metabolism.md#photosynthesis), transport, growth and [signal transduction](signal-transduction.md).

### Nitrogen assimilation

↑ **Parent:** [Plant physiology](#plant-physiology)

Incorporation of inorganic or organic [nitrogen](chemical-substance.md#nitrogen) into cellular compounds. In [plants](organism.md#plant), nitrate reduction and ammonium incorporation connect nitrogen uptake with carbon metabolism.

### Root pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Plant physiology](#plant-physiology)

Positive hydrostatic pressure associated with roots, capable under suitable conditions of producing sap exudation.

### Transpiration

↑ **Parent:** [Plant physiology](#plant-physiology)

### Flavonoid

↑ **Parent:** [Plant physiology](#plant-physiology)

A class of plant metabolites. Selected root-exuded flavonoids help induce rhizobial symbiotic signaling; this is one role among many.

### Phototropin

↑ **Parent:** [Plant physiology](#plant-physiology)

A blue-light-activated receptor kinase family controlling responses including chloroplast relocation, phototropism and stomatal opening.

<h3 id="nrt1-1">NRT1.1</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [Plant physiology](#plant-physiology)

An Arabidopsis nitrate transporter and sensor, also called CHL1 or NPF6.3. Its nitrate-regulated auxin transport connects local nutrient conditions to lateral-root development.

### Cryptochrome

↑ **Parent:** [Plant physiology](#plant-physiology)

A blue-light photoreceptor family with roles including plant development, circadian entrainment and photoperiodic responses.

### Photoperiodism

↑ **Parent:** [Plant physiology](#plant-physiology)

Dependence of a biological response on the daily pattern of light and darkness, often mediated by coincidence between light and an internally timed sensitive phase.

#### CONSTANS

↑ **Parent:** [Photoperiodism](#photoperiodism)

An Arabidopsis transcriptional regulator whose timed expression and light-dependent protein regulation contribute to photoperiodic activation of [FLOWERING LOCUS T](#flowering-locus-t).

#### External coincidence model

↑ **Parent:** [Photoperiodism](#photoperiodism)

A model in which a light-sensitive internal phase produces a response when it coincides with the external light interval. Equal total light need not produce equal responses if timing differs.

### Guard cell

↑ **Parent:** [Plant physiology](#plant-physiology)

One of a pair of specialized epidermal cells controlling a [stoma](organism.md#stoma). Changes in solute content and [turgor pressure](biological-cell.md#turgor-pressure) alter pore aperture.

## Plant immunity

↑ **Parent:** [Botany](botany.md)

### Growth-defense trade-off

↑ **Parent:** [Plant immunity](#plant-immunity)

Allocation and regulatory conflicts between growth and defence; the sign and magnitude of the relationship depend on environment and [genotype](genetics.md#genotype).

### PAD4

↑ **Parent:** [Plant immunity](#plant-immunity)

A component of a [plant](organism.md#plant) immune signaling pathway whose evolutionary conservation and loss are coupled to other pathway members.

### EDS1

↑ **Parent:** [Plant immunity](#plant-immunity)

A component of a [plant](organism.md#plant) immune signaling pathway whose evolutionary conservation and loss are coupled to other pathway members.

### Hybrid necrosis

↑ **Parent:** [Plant immunity](#plant-immunity)

A deleterious hybrid [phenotype](genetics.md#phenotype) that can arise from incompatible immune-gene interactions and inappropriate defence activation.

### Gene-for-gene interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Plant immunity](#plant-immunity)

A host resistance and pathogen avirulence specificity relationship; it is not the complete architecture of all [plant](organism.md#plant) disease resistance.

### Nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Plant immunity](#plant-immunity)

An intracellular immune receptor class in [plants](organism.md#plant), commonly shortened to [NLR](#nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor).

#### Pan-NLRome

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat receptor](#nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor)

The species-wide collection of [NLR](#nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor) genes and alleles across accessions, rather than the inventory in a single reference genome.

#### NLR gene cluster

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat receptor](#nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor)

A genomic group of related receptor genes shaped by duplication, deletion and sequence exchange; not every [NLR](#nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor) is clustered.

#### Integrated decoy

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat receptor](#nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor)

A domain incorporated into an immune receptor that mimics an effector target and links its perturbation to immune recognition.

#### Paired NLR

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat receptor](#nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor)

Functionally coupled receptors whose sensor and signaling activities may be partitioned between two genes. Physical linkage can help preserve their compatible inheritance.

##### RRS1-RPS4 receptor pair

↑ **Parent:** [Paired NLR](#paired-nlr)

An [Arabidopsis thaliana](biology.md#arabidopsis-thaliana) paired immune-receptor example associated with a WRKY integrated domain in RRS1.

### Pattern recognition receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Plant immunity](#plant-immunity)

A receptor recognizing conserved microbial or damage-associated molecular patterns.

### Resistance gene

↑ **Parent:** [Plant immunity](#plant-immunity)

A host gene contributing to reduced disease; not all [resistance genes](#resistance-gene) encode [NLR](#nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor) receptors.

## Plant genetics

↑ **Parent:** [Botany](botany.md)

### FLOWERING LOCUS T

↑ **Parent:** [Plant genetics](#plant-genetics)

A flowering regulator that can also link maternal temperature history in fruit tissues to progeny dormancy.

### Ph1 locus

↑ **Parent:** [Plant genetics](#plant-genetics)

A wheat locus promoting meiotic stability and limiting crossover between homoeologous chromosomes.

### Plant breeding

↑ **Parent:** [Plant genetics](#plant-genetics)

#### Seed priming

↑ **Parent:** [Plant breeding](#plant-breeding)

A controlled preparatory treatment that changes subsequent germination or stress responses; benefits and storage costs depend on the [crop](#crop) and treatment.

#### Linkage drag

↑ **Parent:** [Plant breeding](#plant-breeding)

Retention of unfavorable linked material during introgression of a desired allele.

#### Somaclonal variation

↑ **Parent:** [Plant breeding](#plant-breeding)

Variation among [plants](organism.md#plant) regenerated from cultured somatic cells; it can have genetic or epigenetic causes.

##### Karma epiallele

↑ **Parent:** [Somaclonal variation](#somaclonal-variation)

Methylation variation at an intronic [retrotransposon](genetics.md#retrotransposon) associated with normal versus mantled oil-palm development; loss of methylation is associated with altered splicing and reduced yield.

### Plant epigenetics

↑ **Parent:** [Plant genetics](#plant-genetics)

#### FWA

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

An [Arabidopsis thaliana](biology.md#arabidopsis-thaliana) gene whose ectopic expression and flowering [phenotype](genetics.md#phenotype) can be associated with a stably unmethylated promoter state.

<h4 id="vte3-1">VTE3(1)</h4>

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

A [tomato](organism.md#tomato) vitamin E biosynthetic gene whose expression can be influenced by methylation of a nearby promoter [retrotransposon](genetics.md#retrotransposon).

#### Stress priming

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

A prior stimulus changes the response to a subsequent challenge. Persistence may involve metabolites, proteins, regulatory feedback or [chromatin](molecular-genetics.md#chromatin); priming alone does not establish [epigenetic inheritance](genetics.md#epigenetic-inheritance).

##### HSFA2

↑ **Parent:** [Stress priming](#stress-priming)

A heat-shock [transcription](molecular-genetics.md#transcription-biology) factor involved in sustained transcriptional stress memory in [Arabidopsis thaliana](biology.md#arabidopsis-thaliana).

##### Somatic stress memory

↑ **Parent:** [Stress priming](#stress-priming)

Persistence of a response state within an individual after the initial stimulus has ended, distinguished from inheritance through reproduction.

#### Vernalization

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

Acquisition of competence to flower after prolonged cold; the molecular implementation and persistence vary among [plant](organism.md#plant) lineages.

##### PEP1

↑ **Parent:** [Vernalization](#vernalization)

The [Arabis alpina](organism.md#arabis-alpina) [FLC](#flowering-locus-c) orthologue, whose seasonal reactivation contributes to perennial flowering behavior.

##### FLOWERING LOCUS C

↑ **Parent:** [Vernalization](#vernalization)

An [Arabidopsis thaliana](biology.md#arabidopsis-thaliana) floral repressor whose stable silencing contributes to memory of prolonged cold.

#### Nucleolar dominance

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

Epigenetic silencing of one parental set of ribosomal RNA genes in a hybrid; it is not simply the deletion of those genes.

#### RNA-directed DNA methylation

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

A [plant](organism.md#plant) silencing pathway in which small RNAs guide methylation at homologous [chromatin](molecular-genetics.md#chromatin). Canonical [RdDM](#rna-directed-dna-methylation) involves [Pol IV](molecular-genetics.md#rna-polymerase-iv), [RDR2](molecular-genetics.md#rdr2), [DCL3](enzyme.md#dcl3), Argonaute and [Pol V](molecular-genetics.md#rna-polymerase-v) scaffold [transcription](molecular-genetics.md#transcription-biology).

#### Epigenome editing

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

Targeted alteration of an [epigenetic state](genetics.md#epigenetic-state), distinguished from cutting or rewriting the target DNA sequence. Persistence after removal of the targeting factor is locus dependent.

#### Epigenome-wide association study

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

An association analysis relating epigenomic measurements to a [phenotype](genetics.md#phenotype). Association alone does not establish causal direction or independence from genetic variation.

#### Epigenetic recombinant inbred line

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

A line from a mapping population segregating experimentally induced epigenetic differences in a comparatively uniform genetic background. New transposon insertions and residual sequence differences still need to be assessed.

#### Epiallele

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](#plant-epigenetics)

An alternative regulatory state of a locus associated with an epigenetic difference. Its stability and independence from DNA sequence variation must be tested.

##### Epimutation

↑ **Parent:** [Epiallele](#epiallele)

A gain or loss of an inherited [epigenetic state](genetics.md#epigenetic-state) rather than a change in nucleotide sequence.
