# Spherical top-hat window function

↑ **Parent:** [Density contrast](density-contrast.md)

A spherical top-hat window function averages a field uniformly inside a [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $R$ and gives zero weight outside it. Its normalized real-space kernel is $W_R(\mathbf x)=3/(4\pi R^3)$ for $|\mathbf x|\leq R$, and its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) is

$$
\widetilde W_R(k)=3\frac{\sin(kR)-kR\cos(kR)}{(kR)^3}.
$$
