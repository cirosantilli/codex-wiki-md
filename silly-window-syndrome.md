# Silly window syndrome

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission Control Protocol](transmission-control-protocol.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silly_window_syndrome)

[Silly window syndrome](silly-window-syndrome.md) is inefficient transmission in tiny [TCP segments](tcp-segment.md), arising through small writes or small increments of advertised receiving space. Sender and receiver measures address different causes.
