# p-adic zeta pseudomeasure

↑ **Parent:** [Kubota-Leopoldt p-adic L-function](kubota-leopoldt-p-adic-l-function.md)

On $G=\mathbb Z_p^\times/\{\pm1\}$, this element of the total quotient ring of the [Iwasawa algebra](iwasawa-algebra.md) becomes an integral measure after multiplication by every difference $[a]-[1]$. Its even moments interpolate the displayed Euler-corrected zeta values. Its trivial-character component has a pole, so the pseudomeasure itself must not be treated as an element of the integral group algebra. Multiplication by the [augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md) removes the pole.
