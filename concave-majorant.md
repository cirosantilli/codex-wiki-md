# Concave majorant

↑ **Parent:** [Concave function](concave-function.md)

A concave majorant of $F$ is a [concave function](concave-function.md) lying everywhere above $F$ on the specified domain. The least concave majorant, when it exists, is the pointwise smallest such function. A common tangent can replace an upward derivative kink by a linear segment joining two contact points.
