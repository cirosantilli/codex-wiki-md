# Gaussian sign-correlation identity

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian random vector](gaussian-random-vector.md)

For centered, unit-variance jointly [Gaussian random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md) with [correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) $\rho$, the displayed identity holds. Realize the pair as projections onto unit [vectors](vector.md) of angle $\theta=\arccos\rho$. Rotational symmetry of the two-dimensional [Gaussian random vector](gaussian-random-vector.md) direction makes sign disagreement have [probability](probability.md) $\theta/\pi$. The [expectation](expected-value.md) is therefore $1-2\theta/\pi=(2/\pi)\arcsin\rho$. Perfectly correlated and anticorrelated endpoints follow directly; the sign at zero does not affect the result.
