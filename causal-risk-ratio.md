# Causal risk ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Causal effect](causal-effect.md)

For binary potential outcomes, the causal risk ratio compares intervention risks:

$$
\operatorname{CRR}=\frac{\mathbb E[Y(1)]}{\mathbb E[Y(0)]}.
$$
