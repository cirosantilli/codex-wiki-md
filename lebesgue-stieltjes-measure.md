# Lebesgue-Stieltjes measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure theory](measure-theory-split.md)

An increasing right-continuous function $F:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ determines a measure by

$$
\mu_F((a,b])=F(b)-F(a).
$$

The [Radon-Nikodym theorem](radon-nikodym-theorem.md) and [Lebesgue differentiation theorem](lebesgue-differentiation-theorem.md) identify the density of the absolutely continuous part of this measure with $F'$ almost everywhere.
