# Seifert longitude

↑ **Parent:** [Knot theory](knot-theory-split.md)

The Seifert longitude is the zero-linking parallel of a knot on the boundary of its tubular neighborhood. It is the framing induced by a [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md).
