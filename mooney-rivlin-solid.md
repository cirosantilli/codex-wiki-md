# Mooney-Rivlin solid

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mooney–Rivlin_solid)

An incompressible [Mooney-Rivlin solid](mooney-rivlin-solid.md) has [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) $W=C_1(I_1-3)+C_2(I_2-3)$, with [right Cauchy-Green deformation tensor](right-cauchy-green-deformation-tensor.md) $C=F^TF$, $I_1=\operatorname{tr}C$ and $I_2=\operatorname{tr}C^{-1}$ when $\det C=1$. It extends the [neo-Hookean solid](neo-hookean-solid.md) by a second invariant. A convention $W=\mu_1(I_1-3)/2-\mu_2(I_2-3)/2$ means $C_1=\mu_1/2$, $C_2=-\mu_2/2$; the minus sign must be retained when comparing material constants.
