# Born non-scattering contrasts at one incident direction

↑ **Parent:** [Born approximation for scalar wave scattering](born-approximation-for-scalar-wave-scattering.md)

For real $h\in C_c^\infty(D)$, the displayed real compactly supported contrast has [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) $\widehat V(\boldsymbol\kappa)=[|\boldsymbol\kappa|^4-4(\mathbf k_i\cdot\boldsymbol\kappa)^2]\widehat h(\boldsymbol\kappa)$. This vanishes on the shifted [Ewald sphere](ewald-sphere.md) because $|\boldsymbol\kappa|^2+2\mathbf k_i\cdot\boldsymbol\kappa=0$ there. Thus nonzero contrasts can have zero fixed-frequency, fixed-incidence [Born approximation](born-approximation.md) data. A small amplitude preserves weak contrast and a positive [refractive index](refractive-index.md).
