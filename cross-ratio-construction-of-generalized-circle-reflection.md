# Cross-ratio construction of generalized-circle reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md)

Normalize three distinct points on a [generalized circle](generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md) by a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $f$ taking them to $1,0,\infty$. The inverse image of the extended real line is their generalized circle, and $J=f^{-1}\circ c\circ f$, with $c(z)=\overline z$, fixes that circle pointwise and squares to the identity. Changing the three normalizing points replaces $f$ by $M\circ f$ with a real-coefficient Möbius map $M$. Since $M$ commutes with conjugation, $J$ is unchanged. Thus this construction depends only on the circle. It is [inversion in a circle](inversion-in-a-circle.md) for an ordinary circle and [Euclidean reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) for a straight line, each extended to the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md).
