# Alternating knot

↑ **Parent:** [Knot](knot.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternating_knot)

An [alternating knot](alternating-knot.md) is a [knot](knot.md) admitting an [alternating knot diagram](alternating-knot-diagram.md). A nonalternating diagram can still represent an alternating knot; the property of the knot is existential over its diagrams.
