# Displacement interpolation

↑ **Parent:** [Wasserstein distance](wasserstein-distance.md)

In Euclidean space, push an optimal [transport plan](transport-plan.md) $\pi$ forward by $(x,y)\mapsto(1-t)x+ty$. The resulting [probability measures](probability-measure.md) form a constant speed curve for the [p-Wasserstein distance](p-wasserstein-distance.md):

$$
W_p(\mu_s,\mu_t)=|t-s|W_p(\mu_0,\mu_1).
$$

If the plan is induced by a [transport map](transport-map.md) $T$, this becomes the displayed title formula.
