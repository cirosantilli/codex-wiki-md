# Carrier-turnover saturation of passive transport

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating-access carrier transport](alternating-access-carrier-transport.md)

With fixed interior [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) and finite [carrier protein](carrier-protein.md), symmetric alternating-access kinetics produce a monotone [concave](concave-function.md) transport curve of the displayed form, with $A,C,D>0$. The [flux](flux.md) reverses at equal free [concentrations](concentration.md) and approaches the finite limit $C/A$ for abundant exterior [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md). Its plateau reflects finite carrier abundance and finite turnover, rather than unlimited transport proportional to the [concentration](concentration.md) difference. The [protein](protein-split.md) total multiplying the [flux](flux.md) is distinct from the total [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md).
