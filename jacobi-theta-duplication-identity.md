# Jacobi theta duplication identity

↑ **Parent:** [Theta function with characteristics](theta-function-with-characteristics.md)

For the binary characteristic convention, each [theta function with characteristics](theta-function-with-characteristics.md) has one simple zero per lattice cell. The four zeros of the numerator, at the four half-periods, cancel those of $\vartheta_{11}(2z)$. Their quotient is an entire [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md), hence constant. Taking the limit at zero determines the factor one half. The product identity remains valid at the zeros, where the initial quotient requires removable extension.
