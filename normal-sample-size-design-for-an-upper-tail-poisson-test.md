# Normal sample-size design for an upper-tail Poisson test

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly most powerful test](uniformly-most-powerful-test.md)

For a Poisson sample tested against mean $\mu_0=1$, the exact uniformly most powerful one-sided test thresholds the sample sum and randomizes on its boundary if necessary. A [normal approximation](normal-approximation.md) gives critical sum $n+z_{1-\alpha}\sqrt n$. Equating its standardized alternative threshold to $-z_{1-\beta}$ gives the displayed design for approximate power $1-\beta$ at mean $\mu_1>1$. At size $0.05$ and target power $0.9$, the two quantiles are $1.645$ and $1.282$. Rounding up supplies an [integer](integer-split.md) design; exact Poisson power still requires discrete evaluation.
