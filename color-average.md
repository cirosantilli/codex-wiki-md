# Color average

↑ **Parent:** [Color charge](color-charge.md)

A uniform incoherent initial mixture of two independently prepared fundamental [color charges](color-charge.md) requires an average over their $N^2$ initial possibilities, together with a sum over unobserved final [color charges](color-charge.md). This differs from preparing a coherent [color singlet](colour-singlet.md). For quark-antiquark annihilation through one vector in the adjoint [representation](group-representation.md), $c_s=T^a_{ji}T^a_{kl}$ gives $N^{-2}\sum|c_s|^2=(N^2-1)/(4N^2)$. For the exchange contraction $c_t=T^a_{ki}T^a_{jl}$, $N^{-2}\sum c_sc_t^*=-(N^2-1)/(4N^3)$ by the [SU(N) fundamental completeness relation](su-n-fundamental-completeness-relation.md). The negative overlap reverses the sign of the corresponding Abelian [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) term.
