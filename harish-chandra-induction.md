# Harish-Chandra induction

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group of Lie type](finite-group-of-lie-type.md)

For a rational [parabolic subgroup](parabolic-subgroup.md) $P=L\ltimes U$, use [inflation of a group representation](inflation-of-a-group-representation.md) for a complex $L^F$-module across $P^F\to L^F$ and induce it to $G^F$. This is Harish-Chandra induction. Its adjoint, [Harish-Chandra restriction](harish-chandra-restriction.md), takes $U^F$-invariants. These are ordinary finite-group [group representation](group-representation.md) functors and should not be confused with general [Deligne-Lusztig induction](deligne-lusztig-induction.md) from a nonrational Borel.
