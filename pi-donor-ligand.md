# Pi-donor ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [pi-donor ligand](pi-donor-ligand.md) has an occupied orbital that can overlap sideways with a metal orbital. In the standard [octahedral splitting](octahedral-splitting.md) picture it raises the metal $t_{2g}$-derived levels, tending to reduce their separation from $e_g$.
