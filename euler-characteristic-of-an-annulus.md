# Euler characteristic of an annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md)

Build a closed [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) from two nested $n$-sided [polygons](polygon.md), $n\geq3$, with $n$ radial [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) joining corresponding [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). There are $2n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), $3n$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and $n$ quadrilateral [topological cells](topological-cell.md), giving $\chi=2n-3n+n=0$. The central missing region and the exterior are not faces of the [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md). A [deformation retraction](deformation-retraction.md) onto a [circle](circle.md) gives $(b_0,b_1,b_2)=(1,1,0)$, agreeing with the cell count. This is a useful reduction: the [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) is two-dimensional, yet its [homology](homology-split.md) is that of a one-dimensional [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md).
