# Efficiency upper bound from Eddington-limited growth

↑ **Parent:** [Salpeter time](salpeter-time.md)

For fixed efficiency, growth from $M_i$ to $M_f$ within $\Delta t$ at no more than the [Eddington luminosity](eddington-luminosity.md) requires $[\eta/(1-\eta)]t_E\log(M_f/M_i)\leq\Delta t$. Solving gives an upper bound, because greater radiative efficiency reduces retained [mass](mass.md) growth at a fixed [luminosity](luminosity.md). A duty cycle below unity tightens the requirement; mergers and super-Eddington episodes invalidate its stated premises.
