[Author's authorized chapter previews](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~jrn10/Markov/). Sections 1.7, 1.8 and 1.10 develop invariant [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md), convergence to equilibrium and the [countable-state Markov chain ergodic theorem](countable-state-markov-chain-ergodic-theorem.md) for [Markov chains](markov-chain-split.md). The distinction between a normalizable invariant measure and an invariant measure of infinite mass is crucial on a countably infinite [state space](state-space.md).
