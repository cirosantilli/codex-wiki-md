# Serocatalytic model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical epidemiology](mathematical-epidemiology.md)

An irreversible [serocatalytic model](serocatalytic-model.md) describes the transition from seronegative to seropositive after infection. If the [force of infection](force-of-infection.md) is $\lambda(a)$ and detectable [antibodies](antibody.md) persist, the seronegative fraction satisfies $s'(a)=-\lambda(a)s(a)$, hence [seroprevalence](seroprevalence.md) is $1-\exp[-\int_0^a\lambda(u)\,du]$ when $s(0)=1$. Antibody loss needs a reverse transition, and imperfect testing needs a separate observation model.
