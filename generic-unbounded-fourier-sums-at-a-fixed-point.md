# Generic unbounded Fourier sums at a fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier partial sum](fourier-partial-sum.md)

For each integer $m\geq1$, the set of continuous functions satisfying $|S_N(f,0)|\leq m$ for every $N$ is closed in the [uniform norm](supremum-norm.md). If it contained a ball of radius $r$, subtracting two members of that ball would bound every partial-sum functional norm by $4m/r$. The [Dirichlet kernel harmonic lower bound](dirichlet-kernel-harmonic-lower-bound.md) excludes this, so these sets are [nowhere dense](nowhere-dense-set.md). By the [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md), their complement is a dense $G_\delta$ set. Every function in that complement has unbounded Fourier partial sums at zero. Translation gives the same conclusion at any prescribed point. This is a direct [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md) argument on the real [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) of continuous functions on the circle.
