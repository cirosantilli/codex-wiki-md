# Atmospheric precipitation

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)

Condensed [water](water.md) falling from [clouds](cloud.md) or deposited from the atmosphere as liquid or solid particles. It can efficiently scavenge soluble gases and [aerosol](aerosol.md) particles. Gas conversion into [sulfate](sulfate.md) and subsequent [atmospheric precipitation](atmospheric-precipitation.md) removal are separate stages of an atmospheric sulfur budget.
