# Orthogonal polynomial contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Treatment contrast](treatment-contrast.md)

At equally replicated quantitative treatment levels, evaluations of [orthogonal polynomials](orthogonal-polynomial.md) generate pairwise [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) [treatment contrasts](treatment-contrast.md), using the replication-weighted [inner product](inner-product.md). Five equally spaced levels give four contrast directions representing degrees one through four, besides the constant direction. This decomposes treatment variation without assuming the response has low polynomial degree.
