# Asteroid

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary science](planetary-science-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asteroid)

An [asteroid](asteroid.md) is a small rocky or metallic body orbiting a [star](star.md). [Asteroid](asteroid.md) dynamics can involve [mean-motion resonances](mean-motion-resonance.md), [planetary scattering](planetary-scattering.md) and [secular perturbations](secular-perturbation.md). Solar-system [asteroids](asteroid.md) illustrate how phase protection can prevent close encounters even when orbital paths approach a [planet](planet.md).
