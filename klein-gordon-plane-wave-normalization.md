# Klein-Gordon plane-wave normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Positive-frequency solution](positive-frequency-solution.md)

For $p^0=\sqrt{\mathbf p^2+m^2}>0$ in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), these modes obey $i\partial_t\psi_{\mathbf p}=p^0\psi_{\mathbf p}$ and have [Klein-Gordon inner product](klein-gordon-inner-product.md) $(\psi_{\mathbf p},\psi_{\mathbf q})=\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$. Their conjugates have the opposite norm and are orthogonal to the positive-frequency modes. Plane waves are a generalized, delta-normalized basis; normalizable wave packets have positive norm $\int |a(\mathbf p)|^2d^3p$. Completeness follows by applying the spatial [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) to the [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md), which gives the two branches $e^{\mp ip^0t}$ for each momentum.
