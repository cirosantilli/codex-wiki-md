# Syndrome

↑ **Parent:** [Parity-check matrix](parity-check-matrix.md)

The syndrome of a received word $y$ with respect to a [parity-check matrix](parity-check-matrix.md) $H$ is $s=Hy^T$. It vanishes exactly when $y$ is a codeword and otherwise identifies the coset containing the error pattern.
