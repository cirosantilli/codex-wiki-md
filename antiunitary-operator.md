# Antiunitary operator

↑ **Parent:** [Antilinear map](antilinear-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiunitary_operator)

An antiunitary operator between complex [Hilbert spaces](hilbert-space-split.md) is a surjective [antilinear map](antilinear-map.md) satisfying the displayed [inner product](inner-product.md) identity. It preserves [norms](norm.md) but conjugates scalars. In a chosen [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) it is $UK$, with $U$ a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) and $K$ coefficientwise [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md). Conversely any such $UK$ is antiunitary. Antilinearity by itself does not preserve norms.
