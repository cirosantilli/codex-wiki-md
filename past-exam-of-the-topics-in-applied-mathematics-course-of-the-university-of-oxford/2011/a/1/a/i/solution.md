<h1 id="a/1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $f(N)=\alpha N^2/(\beta N^2+S)$. Its nonnegative [fixed points](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) are $N=0$ and, when $\alpha^2\geq4\beta S$,

$$
\boxed{N_\pm=\frac{\alpha\pm\sqrt{\alpha^2-4\beta S}}{2\beta}.}
$$

Indeed a positive [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) satisfies $\beta N^2-\alpha N+S=0$. The [linear stability](../../../../../../linear-stability.md) multiplier is

$$
f'(N)=\frac{2\alpha SN}{(\beta N^2+S)^2},\qquad f'(0)=0.
$$

At a positive [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md), $\beta N^2+S=\alpha N$, hence

$$
\boxed{f'(N_\pm)=1\mp\frac{\sqrt{\alpha^2-4\beta S}}{\alpha}.}
$$

For strict discriminant positivity, $N_+$ is attracting and $N_-$ repelling; the extinction state is superstable. At equality the positive point has multiplier one. Since

$$
f(N)-N=-\frac{N(\beta N^2-\alpha N+S)}{\beta N^2+S},
$$

it attracts from above and repels from below at equality. This is a semistable [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md), whose one-sided behavior requires the nonlinear calculation.
