<h1 id="a/1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The term $ru$ is unrestricted growth of species $u$, and $-ru^2$ represents its density-dependent self-limitation, with scaled [carrying capacity](../../../../../../carrying-capacity.md) one. The interaction $-ruv$ suppresses $u$ in the presence of species $v$.

Species $v$ has intrinsic growth $v$ and suppression $-\beta uv$ by $u$. Thus both interspecific effects are harmful: this is a [competition model](../../../../../../competition-model.md), with no self-limitation term for $v$. Its unbounded growth when $u$ disappears is a consequence of that modeling choice. The parameter $r$ is the growth-time ratio, while $\beta$ measures the effect of $u$ on $v$.
