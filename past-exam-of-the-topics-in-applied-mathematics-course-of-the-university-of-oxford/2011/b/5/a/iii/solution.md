↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A period-$k$ orbit of a [Poincaré map](../../../../../../poincare-map.md) corresponds to a closed trajectory, hence a [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) of the underlying flow, meeting the chosen section at the $k$ distinct successive return points. Its time period is the sum of the $k$ return times, not necessarily $k$ times a single common time. Stability of the return-map orbit describes transverse stability of that closed trajectory; the flow itself also has the neutral time-translation direction.
