<h1 id="b/5/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the primitive repeating word $\boxed{\ldots001001.001001\ldots}$. One shift changes the sequence and three shifts return it, so the least period is three. A word such as $000$ repeated would instead have least period one; primitivity is what distinguishes a true three-cycle from a periodic notation for a [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md).
