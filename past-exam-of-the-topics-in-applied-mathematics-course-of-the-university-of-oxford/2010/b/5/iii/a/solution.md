In the two-symbol shift representation, $\boxed{\ldots000.000\ldots}$ is fixed under a shift and codes the [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) labelled $0$. The constant all-ones sequence supplies the analogous example. The dot only marks the present position; shifting it advances one iterate. The assertion uses the faithful orbit coding assumed in the question.
