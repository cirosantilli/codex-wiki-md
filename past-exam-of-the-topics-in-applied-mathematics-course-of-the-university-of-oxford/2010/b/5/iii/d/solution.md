<h1 id="b/5/iii/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose $\boxed{\ldots00000.11111\ldots}$, with zeros on the entire left tail and ones on the entire right tail. Forward shifts converge in the symbolic metric to the all-one [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md), while backward shifts converge to the all-zero [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md). Its two limiting invariant states differ, so it is a [heteroclinic orbit](../../../../../../heteroclinic-orbit.md). Reversing the two tails reverses these asymptotic roles.
