<h1 id="b/4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [equilibria](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) satisfy $y=0$ and $x-x^3=0$, giving $(0,0)$ and $(\pm1,0)$. At the origin,

$$
J_0=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&-\Omega\end{pmatrix},\qquad\lambda_\pm=\frac{-\Omega\pm\sqrt{\Omega^2+4}}2.
$$

Their product is $-1$, so **the origin is a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) for every real $\Omega$**.

At either nonzero [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md),

$$
J_\pm=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-2&-(\Omega+1)\end{pmatrix},\qquad\lambda^2+(\Omega+1)\lambda+2=0.
$$

The [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is positive and the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is $-(\Omega+1)$. Thus both [equilibria](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are attracting for $\Omega>-1$, repelling for $\Omega<-1$, and have purely imaginary [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\pm i\sqrt2$ at $\Omega=-1$. More precisely they are [stable foci](../../../../../stable-spiral.md) for $-1<\Omega<-1+2\sqrt2$ and [stable nodes](../../../../../stable-node.md) for $\Omega>-1+2\sqrt2$; they are un[stable foci](../../../../../stable-spiral.md) for $-1-2\sqrt2<\Omega<-1$ and un[stable nodes](../../../../../stable-node.md) for $\Omega<-1-2\sqrt2$. At the two discriminant-zero endpoints they are repeated-eigenvalue [degenerate nodes](../../../../../degenerate-node.md), with a nondiagonalizable Jacobian. At $\Omega=-1$, [linearization](../../../../../linearization.md) alone does not determine nonlinear stability or prove a nonlinear centre.
