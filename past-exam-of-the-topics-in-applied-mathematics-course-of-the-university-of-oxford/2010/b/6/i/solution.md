<h1 id="b/6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The Lorenz [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) has constant [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) $\nabla\cdot F=-\sigma-1-b$. [Liouville formula for a fundamental matrix](../../../../../liouville-formula-for-a-fundamental-matrix.md) for the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the flow therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\det D\Phi_t=e^{-(\sigma+1+b)t},\qquad V(t)=V(0)e^{-(\sigma+1+b)t}.}
$$

This concerns transported three-dimensional phase volume, not the Euclidean distance between each pair of trajectories.

A quasiperiodic orbit with the usual smooth two-frequency [torus](../../../../../torus.md) closure would lie on an invariant embedded two-torus. That [torus](../../../../../torus.md) bounds a solid [torus](../../../../../torus.md) of positive finite volume in $\mathbb R^3$. Since the [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) is tangent to its boundary, its outward flux is zero, contradicting the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md), whose volume integral is $-(\sigma+1+b)V<0$. This [negative-divergence obstruction to invariant tori](../../../../../negative-divergence-obstruction-to-invariant-tori.md) excludes such quasiperiodic tori. The argument uses global negative [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) and the enclosed volume; saying only that an attracting [torus](../../../../../torus.md) has zero volume would not exclude it.

At an [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) the sum of [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is $-(\sigma+1+b)<0$, so they cannot all have positive real parts: no [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is a fully repelling source. For a [periodic orbit](../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) of period $T$, one [Floquet multiplier](../../../../../floquet-multiplier.md) equals one in the flow direction. The product of the other two is $e^{-(\sigma+1+b)T}<1$, so they cannot both have modulus greater than one: no [periodic orbit](../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) is fully repelling either. Saddle [equilibria](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) and saddle [periodic orbits](../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) remain possible; contraction of volume does not imply contraction in every direction.
