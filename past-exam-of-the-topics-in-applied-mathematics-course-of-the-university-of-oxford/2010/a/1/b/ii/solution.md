<h1 id="a/1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The four nonnegative [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are $0,a_1,a_2,a_3$. Since all factors are positive below $a_1$, the sign of $h(N)$ on consecutive intervals is

$$
(0,a_1):+,qquad(a_1,a_2):- ,qquad(a_2,a_3):+,qquad(a_3,\infty):-.
$$

The [phase line](../../../../../../phase-line.md) therefore points away from zero, towards $a_1$, away from $a_2$, and towards $a_3$. Equivalently $h'(0)=a_1a_2a_3>0$, and at each simple positive root $a_i$, $h'(a_i)=-a_i\prod_{j\ne i}(a_j-a_i)$. Their signs alternate negative, positive, negative.

Hence **extinction and $a_2$ are unstable, while $a_1$ and $a_3$ are [asymptotically stable](../../../../../../asymptotic-stability.md)**. Initial positive populations below $a_2$ tend to $a_1$; those above $a_2$ tend to $a_3$; an initial population exactly at $a_2$ remains there. This is [bistability](../../../../../../bistability.md) with a threshold separating the two basins.
