<h1 id="a/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose $X_*=k_2A_0/(2k_1)$, $t_*=1/(k_2A_0)$, $\ell_*=(D_X/(k_2A_0))^{1/2}$, and an independently selected positive $Y_*$ representing its reference or supplied [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md). Set $X=X_*u$, $Y=Y_*v$, $t=t_*\tau$, $x=\ell_*\xi$. Dividing the first equation by $X_*/t_*$ and the second by $Y_*/t_*$ yields the stated system with

$$
\boxed{\beta=\frac{k_3Y_*}{k_2A_0},\quad\alpha=\frac{2k_1k_4}{k_2k_3},\quad\gamma=\frac{k_4}{k_2},\quad\delta=\frac{k_3}{2k_1},\quad D=\frac{D_Y}{D_X}.}
$$

The parameter $\beta$ can be changed through the chosen or physically supplied reference $Y$ [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) while these other combinations remain fixed. The combinations satisfy $\alpha\delta=\gamma$ for this reaction mechanism, so they need not be algebraically independent. The spatial scale makes the $u$ [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) one, and the time and $X$ scales balance linear autocatalytic growth against the quadratic loss.
