<h1 id="a/3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A diffusion-driven instability means that the homogeneous reaction [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is [asymptotically stable](../../../../../../asymptotic-stability.md) against spatially uniform perturbations, but adding [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) makes at least one admissible spatially nonuniform mode grow. Thus the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $J$ must have negative real parts, while $J-k^2\mathcal D$ has an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) with positive real part for some positive [Neumann Laplacian](../../../../../../neumann-laplacian.md) [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $k^2$ on the actual domain.

The instability is driven by different [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) responses acting together with reaction feedback. It is not merely an [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) that was already unstable without [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md). On a bounded domain the permitted [wave numbers](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) are discrete, so an algebraically unstable continuous band must also contain an actual spatial eigenmode.
