<h1 id="a/3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

After relabelling the chemicals if necessary, take $f_u>0$ and $g_v<0$. At least one diagonal entry must be positive because $D_vf_u+D_ug_v>0$, whereas their sum is negative. Since their product is negative and $\det J>0$, the off-diagonal product must be negative and sufficiently large in magnitude. The two possibilities are

$$
\boxed{J\sim\begin{pmatrix}+&-\\+&-\end{pmatrix}\quad\hbox{or}\quad J\sim\begin{pmatrix}+&+\\-&-\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

In the first, $u$ activates its own production and stimulates $v$, while $v$ suppresses $u$: an activator-inhibitor mechanism. In the [substrate-depletion pattern mechanism](../../../../../../substrate-depletion-pattern-mechanism.md), $v$ promotes the autocatalytic species $u$, which consumes or suppresses $v$: a substrate-depletion mechanism. These are different biological interaction signs, not merely two drawings of the same feedback. Suitable relative magnitudes and sufficiently different diffusivities are still required; the sign pattern alone does not guarantee a [Turing instability](../../../../../../turing-instability.md).
