↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [Hopf bifurcation](../../../../../hopf-bifurcation.md) occurs when a simple pair of [complex conjugate](../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) crosses the imaginary axis at nonzero frequency. A supercritical [normal form of a dynamical system](../../../../../normal-form-dynamical-systems.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dot z=(\mu+i\omega_0)z-|z|^2z,\qquad\omega_0\ne0.}
$$

Writing $z=re^{i\theta}$ gives $\dot r=\mu r-r^3$ and $\dot\theta=\omega_0$. A stable [periodic orbit](../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) of radius $\sqrt\mu$ is born for $\mu>0$ as the equilibrium loses stability. In contrast, a subcritical Hopf has an unstable small [periodic orbit](../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) on the side where the equilibrium is still stable.
