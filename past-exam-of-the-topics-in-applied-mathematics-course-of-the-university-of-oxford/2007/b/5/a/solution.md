<h1 id="b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) creates or destroys two equilibria when a real [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) passes through zero with a nonzero quadratic normal-form term. With a [parameter](../../../../../parameter.md) orientation chosen so the pair appears for $\mu>0$, use

$$
\boxed{\dot q=\mu-q^2.}
$$

There are no equilibria for $\mu<0$, a double nonhyperbolic equilibrium at zero, and equilibria $q=\pm\sqrt\mu$ for $\mu>0$. The positive branch is stable and the negative branch unstable in the active direction; additional hyperbolic directions can make them a node and a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md). “Supercritical” for a fold depends on this chosen [parameter](../../../../../parameter.md) orientation; unlike the pitchfork and Hopf cases it is not an intrinsic distinction between the stability of two possible cubic branches.
