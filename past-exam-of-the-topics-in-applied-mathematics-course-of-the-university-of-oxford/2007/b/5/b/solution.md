↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The supercritical pitchfork [normal form of a dynamical system](../../../../../normal-form-dynamical-systems.md) is

$$
\boxed{\dot q=\mu q-q^3.}
$$

The symmetric equilibrium $q=0$ is stable for $\mu<0$ and unstable for $\mu>0$. Two new stable equilibria $q=\pm\sqrt\mu$ emerge on the unstable side of the original branch. Reflection [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) $q\mapsto-q$ preserves the pair. The new equilibria approach zero continuously as the [parameter](../../../../../parameter.md) reaches the threshold.
