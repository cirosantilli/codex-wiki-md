<h1 id="b/7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A hyperbolic **[sink fixed point of a map](../../../../../sink-fixed-point-of-a-map.md)** has both Jacobian [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) strictly inside the [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md). Its iterates locally attract nearby initial states; complex [fixed-point multipliers](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) can make the approach spiral. Zero [fixed-point multipliers](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) are strongly contracting and can occur for [noninvertible maps](../../../../../noninvertible-map.md). A [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) of modulus one requires nonlinear or exact analysis.
