<h1 id="b/7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A hyperbolic **[saddle fixed point of a map](../../../../../saddle-fixed-point-of-a-map.md)** has one [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of modulus less than one and one greater than one. The corresponding stable and unstable directions persist locally as [invariant sets](../../../../../invariant-set-of-a-measure-preserving-transformation.md) or manifolds under the usual smoothness and invertibility assumptions. For a [noninvertible map](../../../../../noninvertible-map.md) a zero stable [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) is possible, and the word [saddle fixed point of a map](../../../../../saddle-fixed-point-of-a-map.md) describes forward contraction in one direction and expansion in the other; backward dynamics may not be unique.
