<h1 id="b/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At a differentiable [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md), linear perturbations advance by the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md). A hyperbolic **[source fixed point of a map](../../../../../source-fixed-point-of-a-map.md)** has both [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of modulus greater than one, so nearby perturbations expand under forward [iteration of a map](../../../../../iterated-function.md). Real signs can cause alternating directions and a complex pair can cause spiral expansion. [Eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) on the [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) are excluded from this linear classification.
