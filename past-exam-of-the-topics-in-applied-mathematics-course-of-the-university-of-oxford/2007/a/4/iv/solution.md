<h1 id="a/4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Put $q=k_n^2$. The mode matrix has [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) $-1/2-(1+D)q<0$, so a growing [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) occurs exactly when its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is negative:

$$
\boxed{\Delta(q)=Dq^2+(D-1/2)q+1<0.}
$$

This upward quadratic has two positive roots only if $D<1/2$ and $D^2-5D+1/4>0$. Within $0<D<1$ this is

$$
\boxed{0<D<D_c=\frac{5-\sqrt{24}}2.}
$$

For such $D$, define

$$
\boxed{q_\pm=\frac{1/2-D\pm\sqrt{D^2-5D+1/4}}{2D}.}
$$

The unstable modes satisfy $\boxed{\sqrt{q_-}<k_n<\sqrt{q_+}}$. These are strict inequalities: at either endpoint the leading [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is zero rather than positive.
