<h1 id="a/4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The reaction Jacobian is

$$
\boxed{J=\begin{pmatrix}-b^2&-2ab\\b^2&-1+2ab\end{pmatrix}_{(3/4,1)}
=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-3/2\\1&1/2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is $-1/2$ and [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is $1$, so both [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) have negative real parts. Explicitly they are $(-1\pm i\sqrt{15})/4$. Therefore the homogeneous reaction equilibrium is a [stable focus](../../../../../stable-spiral.md) in the absence of diffusion.
