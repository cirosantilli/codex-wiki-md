<h1 id="a/4/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

If $D\geq1/2$, both nonconstant coefficients of $\Delta(q)$ are nonnegative, so it is positive for $q\geq0$. If $D_c<D<1/2$, its [discriminant](../../../../../discriminant.md) is negative, so it is again strictly positive. Therefore $\boxed{D>D_c\implies\text{no diffusion-driven instability}}$.

At $D=D_c$ the double root can only give a neutral zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) if it coincides with an allowed spatial mode; it still gives no positive growth rate. This is the finite-[wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md) onset of a [Turing instability](../../../../../turing-instability.md), not an oscillatory crossing since the mode [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) stays negative.
