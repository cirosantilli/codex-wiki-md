<h1 id="a/3/i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A nonconstant trajectory approaching a [stable focus](../../../../../../stable-spiral.md) winds around it infinitely often. Its $a$ coordinate therefore changes sign repeatedly near zero. Since the [biological population](../../../../../../population-biology.md) profile must remain nonnegative, such a trajectory cannot represent the wave. The only nonnegative trajectory avoiding that winding at the origin would be the equilibrium itself, which cannot join the populated state. Thus $\boxed{\text{no nonnegative front for }0<c<2\eta}$.
