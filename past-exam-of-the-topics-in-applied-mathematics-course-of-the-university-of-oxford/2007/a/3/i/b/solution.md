<h1 id="a/3/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The phase-plane equations are $a'=b$, $b'=-cb-f(a)$. Nonnegative equilibria require $b=0$ and $f(a)=0$, hence only $\boxed{(0,0),\ (\eta,0)}$. At the origin $f'(0)=\eta^2$, so the linear [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) solve

$$
\lambda^2+c\lambda+\eta^2=0,\qquad
\boxed{\lambda_\pm=(-c\pm\sqrt{c^2-4\eta^2})/2.}
$$

For $0<c<2\eta$ the roots are complex with negative real part, giving a [stable focus](../../../../../../stable-spiral.md). For $c>2\eta$ both are negative real numbers, giving a [stable node](../../../../../../stable-node.md). Equality is the repeated-root threshold and is not covered by either strict classification.
