↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Law of mass action](../../../../../law-of-mass-action.md) assigns net [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) gain $k_0s$ to autocatalytic replication, [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md)-[enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) binding flux $k_1se$, dissociation flux $k_{-1}c$ and product-formation flux $k_2c$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\dot s=k_0s-k_1se+k_{-1}c,\quad
\dot c=k_1se-(k_{-1}+k_2)c,\quad
\dot e=-k_1se+(k_{-1}+k_2)c.}
$$

Complex breakdown to product restores free [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) but does not restore [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md). Product [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md), if required, satisfies $\dot p=k_2c$.
