<h1 id="a/1/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

A [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) of $F$ is also a [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) of $F\circ F$. By the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md),

$$
\boxed{(F\circ F)'(u_*)=F'(F(u_*))F'(u_*)=[F'(u_*)]^2.}
$$

If the original [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) has modulus greater than one, its square also exceeds one, so the same [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) is unstable for the twice-iterated map. New stable [fixed points](../../../../../fixed-point.md) of the twice-iterated map, if present, must be different points; they can represent a genuine [two-cycle](../../../../../period-two-orbit.md) of the original map.
