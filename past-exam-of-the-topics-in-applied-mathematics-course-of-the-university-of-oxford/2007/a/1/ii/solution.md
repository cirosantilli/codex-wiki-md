<h1 id="a/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A positive [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) requires $\beta+u_*^\gamma=\alpha$, so it exists exactly when $\boxed{\alpha>\beta}$ and is $u_*=(\alpha-\beta)^{1/\gamma}$. Differentiating the map at it gives

$$
\boxed{F'(u_*)=1-\gamma\left(1-\frac\beta\alpha\right).}
$$

For $1<\gamma\leq2$ and $0<\beta/\alpha<1$, this [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) is strictly between $-1$ and $1$. Hence the positive [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md) is linearly asymptotically stable, including $\gamma=2$; positivity of $\beta$ makes the lower bound strict.
