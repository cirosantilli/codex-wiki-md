<h1 id="a/1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [fixed-point multiplier](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) decreases continuously with $\gamma$ and first reaches the [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) at $-1$. Solving $1-\gamma_*(1-\beta/\alpha)=-1$ gives

$$
\boxed{\gamma_*=\frac{2\alpha}{\alpha-\beta}>2.}
$$

It is stable for $1<\gamma<\gamma_*$ and unstable for $\gamma>\gamma_*$. At the critical value [linear stability](../../../../../linear-stability.md) is inconclusive. The crossing at $-1$ is the local signature of a [period-doubling bifurcation](../../../../../period-doubling-bifurcation.md), rather than a crossing through $+1$.
