# 2008

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the topics in applied mathematics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part B paper is cross-listed for Mathematics and Mathematics and Statistics. Section A concerns [mathematical biology](../mathematical-biology-split.md), and section B concerns [dynamical systems](../dynamical-systems-split.md). [Source release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3) The archive contains the complete editable eight-page transcription. Bounded searches of official sources found no advertised answers to this exact paper. The original solutions cover every source leaf and every unnumbered task in questions 5–8.
