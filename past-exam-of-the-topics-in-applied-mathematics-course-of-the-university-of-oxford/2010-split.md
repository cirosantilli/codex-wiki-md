# 2010

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the topics in applied mathematics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part B paper covers Mathematical Ecology and Biology in section A and Nonlinear Systems in section B, and is cross-listed for the Mathematics, Mathematics and Computer Science, and Mathematics and Statistics degrees. [Source release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3) A bounded search of official sources found no advertised solutions for this exact paper. The complete seven-page archive transcription preserves the printed notation; the solutions explain the initial-data restrictions, omitted Neumann zero modes and zero-frequency exception where needed.
