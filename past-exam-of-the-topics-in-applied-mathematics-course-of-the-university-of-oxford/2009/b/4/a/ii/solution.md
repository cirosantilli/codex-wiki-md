<h1 id="b/4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\nu=1$, the [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are $(0,0)$ and $(\pm1,0)$. At the origin the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1,-1$, so it is a saddle for every $\kappa$.

At either nonzero point the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) is $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-2&\kappa\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
\boxed{\lambda_\pm=\frac{\kappa\pm\sqrt{\kappa^2-8}}2.}
$$

Both points are attracting for $\kappa<0$ and repelling for $\kappa>0$. For $-2\sqrt2<\kappa<0$ they are [stable foci](../../../../../../stable-spiral.md); for $\kappa<-2\sqrt2$ [stable nodes](../../../../../../stable-node.md). For $0<\kappa<2\sqrt2$ they are [unstable foci](../../../../../../unstable-spiral.md); for $\kappa>2\sqrt2$ [unstable nodes](../../../../../../unstable-node.md). At the two repeated-root boundaries they are [degenerate nodes](../../../../../../degenerate-node.md). At $\kappa=0$, [linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md) has purely imaginary [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md); the conserved [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) in the next part shows that they are actual nonlinear [centres](../../../../../../center-equilibrium.md). Thus the zero-real-part case is resolved by an additional [first integral](../../../../../../first-integral.md), rather than by the linear test alone.
