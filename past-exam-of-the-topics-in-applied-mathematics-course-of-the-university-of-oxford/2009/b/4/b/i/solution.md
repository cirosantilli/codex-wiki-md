↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The cubic terms vanish in the [linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md), leaving $J_0=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&-2\\2&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1\pm2i$, so **the origin is a repelling [focus equilibrium](../../../../../../focus-dynamical-systems.md)**. The nonzero imaginary part gives [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), while the positive real part makes small amplitudes grow. In particular it cannot be the forward limit of nearby nonzero trajectories, which motivates looking for an attracting [periodic orbit](../../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) away from the origin.
