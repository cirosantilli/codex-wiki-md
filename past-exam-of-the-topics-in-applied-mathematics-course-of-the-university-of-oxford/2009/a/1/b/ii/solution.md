↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the same vertical-then-horizontal cobweb construction to $H(u)=9ue^{-u}$. Since no numerical value of the large initial [biological population](../../../../../../population-biology.md) is prescribed, an exact five-generation list is $u_1$, $H(u_1)$, $H^2(u_1)$, $H^3(u_1)$, $H^4(u_1)$. For the plotted example $u_1=5$, these values are

$$
\boxed{5,\quad0.303208,\quad2.015121,\quad2.417618,\quad1.939421.}
$$

The initial slump is followed by rapid growth and an alternating overshoot about $\log9\simeq2.197225$, which is now unstable because its [periodic-orbit multiplier](../../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) $1-\log9<-1$. A still larger initial [biological population](../../../../../../population-biology.md) can remain extremely small for more than these four updates; the example illustrates the requested construction without imposing an unstated universal numerical starting value.
