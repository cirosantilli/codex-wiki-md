<h1 id="a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [Ricker population map](../../../../../ricker-population-map.md) $H(u)=rue^{-u}$, [fixed points](../../../../../fixed-point.md) satisfy $u=0$ or $re^{-u}=1$. Thus zero exists for all $r>0$, while $u_*=\log r$ is positive exactly when $r>1$. Since $H'(u)=re^{-u}(1-u)$, the respective [periodic-orbit multipliers](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) are $r$ and $1-\log r$.

Consequently **zero is linearly attracting for $0<r<1$ and repelling for $r>1$; the positive [biological population](../../../../../population-biology.md) is linearly attracting for $1<r<e^2$ and repelling for $r>e^2$**. The [periodic-orbit multiplier](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) of the positive point is positive below $r=e$ and negative above it, distinguishing monotone from alternating local convergence. At $r=1$, the coincident zero state has unit [periodic-orbit multiplier](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) but $0<H(u)<u$ for every $u>0$, giving nonlinear attraction from the physical right side. At $r=e^2$ the [periodic-orbit multiplier](../../../../../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) is $-1$ and [linearization](../../../../../linearization.md) is inconclusive. Writing $u=2+\eta$, the second iterate has expansion $\eta\mapsto\eta-\eta^3/3+O(\eta^4)$, so the critical point is weakly attracting; beyond it a period-doubling instability occurs.
