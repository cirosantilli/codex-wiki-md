↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use $t=aT$ and scale each count by the susceptible [infection](../../../../../../infection.md) threshold $a/r$:

$$
(I,S,V,R)=\frac ar(q,s,v,m),\qquad\eta=\frac pr.
$$

For example $I_T=(a^2/r)q_t$, while $rIS=(a^2/r)qs$. Dividing all equations by $a^2/r$ therefore gives

$$
\boxed{q_t=q(s-1),\quad s_t=-(1+\eta)qs,\quad v_t=\eta qs,\quad m_t=q.}
$$

Thus $\eta$ measures the [vaccination](../../../../../../vaccination.md) response relative to transmission in this mass-action model. The total dimensionless count $q+s+v+m$ is conserved, and the instantaneous [infection](../../../../../../infection.md) growth threshold is $s=1$.
