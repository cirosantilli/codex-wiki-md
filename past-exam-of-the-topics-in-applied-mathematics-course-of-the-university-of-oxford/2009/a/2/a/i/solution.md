↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [law of mass action](../../../../../../law-of-mass-action.md) assigns [infection](../../../../../../infection.md) rate $rIS$, death rate $aI$, and [vaccination](../../../../../../vaccination.md) rate $pIS$. [Vaccination](../../../../../../vaccination.md) consumes a susceptible animal and produces a vaccinated one while leaving the triggering infected animal present. In dimensional time $T$,

$$
\boxed{I_T=rIS-aI,\quad S_T=-(r+p)IS,\quad V_T=pIS,\quad R_T=aI.}
$$

All compartment counts remain nonnegative. Adding the equations gives $(I+S+V+R)_T=0$; deaths are retained in the bookkeeping compartment rather than removed from that conserved count. There is no recovery class of live immune survivors in the [infection](../../../../../../infection.md) reaction: in this particular model every animal that becomes infected ultimately dies.
