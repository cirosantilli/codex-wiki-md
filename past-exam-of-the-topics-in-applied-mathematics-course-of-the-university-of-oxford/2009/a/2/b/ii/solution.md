<h1 id="a/2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Rearrange the final-size equation as $s_\infty=s_0\exp[s_\infty-s_0-(1+\eta)q_0]$. With fixed $q_0>0,s_0>0$ and $\eta\to\infty$, the selected root is exponentially small:

$$
s_\infty\sim s_0e^{-s_0-(1+\eta)q_0}.
$$

Substitution into the death count yields

$$
\boxed{m_\infty=q_0+\frac{s_0}{\eta}+O(\eta^{-2})+O(e^{-\eta q_0}/\eta).}
$$

Thus strong [vaccination](../../../../../../vaccination.md) suppresses most secondary fatal infections but cannot save the $q_0$ animals already infected, as required by the stated reaction scheme. The limit holds for the fixed positive initial [infection](../../../../../../infection.md) assumed here; it need not be uniform if $q_0$ is allowed to shrink with increasing $\eta$.
