<h1 id="a/3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In coordinate order $(n,m)$ the [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) is

$$
J(n,m)=\begin{pmatrix}1-2n-m/2&-n/2\\-qm/2&q(1-2m-n/2)\end{pmatrix}.
$$

At the origin it is $\operatorname{diag}(1,q)$, giving a repelling [node equilibrium](../../../../../../node-dynamical-systems.md); when $q=1$ the repeated positive [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is semisimple. At coexistence it is

$$
J_*=-\frac13\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\q&2q\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is $-2(1+q)/3$, [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $q/3$, and characteristic [discriminant](../../../../../../discriminant.md) $4(q^2-q+1)/9>0$. Hence both [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are real and negative, proving a stable [node equilibrium](../../../../../../node-dynamical-systems.md) for every $q>0$.

The other [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$. Their triangular Jacobians have [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $(-1,q/2)$ and $(1/2,-q)$ respectively. Thus **both single-species [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are saddles**, with their attracting directions on the corresponding coordinate axes and unstable directions allowing invasion by the other species. There are no further nonnegative [equilibria](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) because the two non-axis [nullclines](../../../../../../nullcline.md) intersect only at $(2/3,2/3)$.
