<h1 id="a/3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The coexistence [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) calculated in part (a) has negative diagonal entries and positive [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md). For any spatial mode with squared [wave number](../../../../../../wavenumber.md) $k^2\geq0$, the linearized [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is $J_*-k^2\operatorname{diag}(1,\eta)$. Its [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is more negative than before, and its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac q3+\frac{2(\eta+q)}3k^2+\eta k^4>0.}
$$

Hence both [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) retain negative real parts for every mode and every $\eta>0$. This is the mechanism by which [negative self-response excludes two-species Turing instability](../../../../../../negative-self-response-excludes-two-species-turing-instability.md), excluding diffusion-driven instability using only the reaction [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) already computed, together with the diagonal diffusive [damping](../../../../../../damping.md). In particular unequal [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) is not enough to produce a [Turing pattern](../../../../../../turing-pattern.md) when both local self-responses are negative in this two-species competitive model.
