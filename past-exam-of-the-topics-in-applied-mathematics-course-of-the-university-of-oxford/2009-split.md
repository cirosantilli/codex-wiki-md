# 2009

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the topics in applied mathematics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part B paper covers Mathematical Ecology and Biology in section A and Nonlinear Systems in section B. It is cross-listed for Mathematics, Mathematics and Computer Science, and Mathematics and Statistics. [Source release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3) A bounded official-source search found no advertised answers for this exact paper. The complete archive transcription retains its supplied graph and printed map; the solutions include the antipodal [two-cycle](../period-two-orbit.md) lost by the proposed quadratic reduction.
