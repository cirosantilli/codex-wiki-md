<h1 id="a/1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Yes: there is a quasistatic [hysteresis](../../../../../../hysteresis.md) loop.** [Equilibrium points](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) lie on the [mathematical model parameter](../../../../../../mathematical-model-parameter.md) curve $a=-P(N)$. Its two [saddle-node bifurcations](../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) occur at the [stationary points](../../../../../../stationary-point.md) computed in part (a), at [mathematical model parameter](../../../../../../mathematical-model-parameter.md) values

$$
a_{\rm down}=-P(N_M)=-\frac5{54}-\frac{7\sqrt7}{108}\simeq-0.264076,
$$



$$
a_{\rm up}=-P(N_m)=-\frac5{54}+\frac{7\sqrt7}{108}\simeq0.0788913.
$$

Between these values there are two [attracting](../../../../../../attraction-in-a-dynamical-system.md) branches separated by a [repelling](../../../../../../repulsion-in-a-dynamical-system.md) branch. Starting below $a_{\rm down}$ and increasing $a$ slowly, a solution follows the lower [attracting](../../../../../../attraction-in-a-dynamical-system.md) branch. This branch persists through the [bistability](../../../../../../bistability.md) interval and ends only at $a_{\rm up}$, where it meets the [repelling](../../../../../../repulsion-in-a-dynamical-system.md) branch in a [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md). The solution then moves to the upper [attracting](../../../../../../attraction-in-a-dynamical-system.md) branch.

If the direction of [mathematical model parameter](../../../../../../mathematical-model-parameter.md) change is reversed, the upper branch persists until $a_{\rm down}$, where a different [saddle-node bifurcation](../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md) forces the downward transition. The two transition values differ, and both lie inside the allowed range $[-1/2,1/2]$. Thus the [biological population](../../../../../../population-biology.md) selected at an intermediate [mathematical model parameter](../../../../../../mathematical-model-parameter.md) value depends on its prior branch. This is the [equilibrium point](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) [hysteresis](../../../../../../hysteresis.md) shown in the branch diagram; finite-rate passage can additionally delay the jumps near the [saddle-node bifurcations](../../../../../../saddle-node-bifurcation.md).
