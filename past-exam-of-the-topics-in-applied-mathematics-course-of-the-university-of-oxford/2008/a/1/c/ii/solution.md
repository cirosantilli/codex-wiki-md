<h1 id="a/1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With three distinct positive [roots of an equation](../../../../../../root-of-an-equation.md), the negative-leading [cubic polynomial](../../../../../../cubic-polynomial.md) has the sign pattern

$$
+\quad N_*\quad-\quad N_{**}\quad+\quad N_{***}\quad-.
$$

Equivalently its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is negative at the first [root of an equation](../../../../../../root-of-an-equation.md), positive at the middle [root of an equation](../../../../../../root-of-an-equation.md), and negative at the final [root of an equation](../../../../../../root-of-an-equation.md). The [phase line](../../../../../../phase-line.md) arrows therefore point toward the two outer [equilibrium points](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) and away from the middle one:

$$
\boxed{N_*\text{ stable},\qquad N_{**}\text{ unstable},\qquad N_{***}\text{ stable}.}
$$

The middle [equilibrium point](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) separates the two [basin of attraction](../../../../../../basin-of-attraction.md) in the [bistability](../../../../../../bistability.md) [mathematical model parameter](../../../../../../mathematical-model-parameter.md) interval.
