<h1 id="a/2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The delayed influx $rI(t-\tau)S(t-\tau)$ represents animals exposed one [incubation period](../../../../../../incubation-period.md) earlier who enter the [infective individual](../../../../../../infective-individual.md) [compartment](../../../../../../compartment.md) now. They die at the instantaneous rate $aI(t)$ once in that [compartment](../../../../../../compartment.md). The displayed bookkeeping removes an animal from $S$ at the same delayed time that it enters $I$, preserving $S+I+R$.

For this bookkeeping, $S$ must include the exposed animals which have not yet completed [incubation period](../../../../../../incubation-period.md). If $S$ were instead defined as strictly unexposed [susceptible individuals](../../../../../../susceptible-individual.md), its loss term would be the current exposure rate and a separate latent compartment would be needed. The calculations here follow the printed [delay differential equation](../../../../../../delay-differential-equation.md). A full initial-value problem for a [delay differential equation](../../../../../../delay-differential-equation.md) also needs a history on $[-\tau,0]$; the following [small-delay expansion of an epidemic transfer rate](../../../../../../small-delay-expansion-of-an-epidemic-transfer-rate.md) concerns a smooth compatible history and the regular regime assumed by the question.
