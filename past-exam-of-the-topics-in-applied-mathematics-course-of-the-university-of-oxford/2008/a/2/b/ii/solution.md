<h1 id="a/2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the first-order [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) correction it suffices to use the zeroth-order [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md). They give

$$
\dot I\,S+I\dot S=(rIS-aI)S+I(-rIS)=IS\{r(S-I)-a\}.
$$

The delayed product is consequently

$$
I(t-\tau)S(t-\tau)=IS+\tau IS\{a+r(I-S)\}+O(\tau^2).
$$

Multiplying by $r$ and substituting into the two transfer equations produces

$$
\boxed{f(I,S,r,a)=r\{a+r(I-S)\},\qquad g(I,S,r,a)=-r\{a+r(I-S)\}.}
$$

The opposite signs of $f$ and $g$ are also required by [conservation law](../../../../../../conservation-law.md) of $S+I+R$: this correction changes the timing of the transfer between $S$ and $I$, while leaving the instantaneous death term unchanged.
