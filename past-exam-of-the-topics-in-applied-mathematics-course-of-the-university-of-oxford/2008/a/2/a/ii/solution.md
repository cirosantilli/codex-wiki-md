<h1 id="a/2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $S_0=S_{initial}$ and $I_0=I_{initial}$. Until [quarantine](../../../../../../quarantine.md) and while $I,S>0$, division of the equations gives

$$
\frac{dI}{dS}=-1+\frac{a}{rS}.
$$

[Integration](../../../../../../integral-split.md) from the initial state yields the [first integral](../../../../../../first-integral.md)

$$
I+S-\frac ar\log S=I_0+S_0-\frac ar\log S_0.
$$

Let $S_*$ be the [susceptible](../../../../../../susceptible-individual.md) count at the first time $I=I_*$. It is the [root of an equation](../../../../../../root-of-an-equation.md) reached on the decreasing-$S$ outbreak trajectory of

$$
\boxed{I_*+S_*-\frac ar\log S_*=I_0+S_0-\frac ar\log S_0.}
$$

For a strictly rising threshold crossing this is the larger admissible [root of an equation](../../../../../../root-of-an-equation.md), with $a/r<S_*<S_0$; if the threshold equals the peak, $S_*=a/r$. The assumption that [quarantine](../../../../../../quarantine.md) is reached selects the physically relevant branch and excludes a merely hypothetical declining-branch crossing after an earlier one.

Since there is no [incubation period](../../../../../../incubation-period.md) here, all removals from $S$ have already become [infection cases](../../../../../../infection-case.md) by [quarantine](../../../../../../quarantine.md). No further [susceptible individuals](../../../../../../susceptible-individual.md) are [infected](../../../../../../infection.md) after isolation. The requested number among the initially [susceptible individuals](../../../../../../susceptible-individual.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{S_0-S_*.}
$$
