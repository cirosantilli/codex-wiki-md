<h1 id="a/2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The term $rIS$ is a mass-action incidence rate: each [infective individual](../../../../../../infective-individual.md) makes potentially transmitting contacts with [susceptible individuals](../../../../../../susceptible-individual.md), and the total rate is proportional to the product of the two [compartment](../../../../../../compartment.md) sizes. An [infection](../../../../../../infection.md) transfers one animal from $S$ to $I$, accounting for opposite signs in their equations. The [per-capita mortality rate](../../../../../../per-capita-mortality-rate.md) of an [infective individual](../../../../../../infective-individual.md) is $a$, giving transfer $aI$ from $I$ to $R$.

There are no births, recoveries or additional deaths in this model. Adding the three equations gives $\dot S+\dot I+\dot R=0$, so the total initial [biological population](../../../../../../population-biology.md) is conserved when the dead [compartment](../../../../../../compartment.md) is included. Before [quarantine](../../../../../../quarantine.md), $S$ decreases whenever $I,S>0$; after effective isolation no further transmission is permitted.
