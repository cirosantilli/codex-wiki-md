↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The term $\alpha\nabla^2q$ is cell [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) arising from random movement. [Chemotaxis](../../../../../chemotaxis.md) up the [concentration gradient](../../../../../concentration-gradient.md) gives the cell [flux](../../../../../flux.md) $\beta q\nabla c$, whose negative [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is the transport contribution $-\beta\nabla\cdot(q\nabla c)$. The reaction term $q(1-q)$ gives growth governed by the [logistic differential equation](../../../../../logistic-differential-equation.md) with [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) [carrying capacity](../../../../../carrying-capacity.md) one.

The [chemoattractant](../../../../../chemoattractant.md) itself diffuses, is produced by cells at rate $\kappa\eta q$, and decays at rate $\kappa c$. The homogeneous [Neumann boundary conditions](../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md) suppress the normal [diffusive](../../../../../diffusion.md) [flux](../../../../../flux.md) of each quantity. They also suppress the normal [chemotactic cell flux](../../../../../chemotactic-flux.md) because the normal [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of $c$ vanishes. Thus the rectangular boundary is impermeable, and the spatially integrated cell count changes through [cell proliferation](../../../../../cell-proliferation.md) rather than transport across that boundary.
