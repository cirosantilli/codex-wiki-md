<h1 id="a/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the modal [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $M(s)$, $s\ge0$, its [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is always negative:

$$
\operatorname{tr}M(s)=-(1+\kappa)-(\alpha+1)s<0.
$$

Its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is

$$
D(s)=(1+\alpha s)(\kappa+s)-\beta\kappa\eta s
=\alpha s^2+(1+\alpha\kappa-\beta\kappa\eta)s+\kappa.
$$

The proposed inequality is exactly $\beta\kappa\eta<\alpha\kappa+1$. It makes the coefficient of $s$ positive, while the leading and constant coefficients are already positive. Therefore $D(s)>0$ for every $s\ge0$, independent of which discrete wave numbers the domain allows.

A real two-by-two [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) with negative [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) and positive [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) has both [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) with negative [real part](../../../../../real-part.md): if they are real they have positive product and negative sum, and if [nonreal](../../../../../nonreal-complex-number.md) their common [real part](../../../../../real-part.md) is half the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md). Every mode therefore decays, proving **[linear stability](../../../../../linear-stability.md) for all domain sizes** under the stated sufficient condition.
