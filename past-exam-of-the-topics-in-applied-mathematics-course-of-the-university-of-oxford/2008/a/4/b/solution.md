<h1 id="a/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For spatially homogeneous [perturbations](../../../../../perturbation.md) write $q=1+u$ and $c=\eta+v$. The linearized reaction equations are

$$
\begin{pmatrix}\dot u\\\dot v\end{pmatrix}
=J\begin{pmatrix}u\\v\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\boxed{J=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\\kappa\eta&-\kappa\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

This [triangular matrix](../../../../../triangular-matrix.md) has [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $-1$ and $-\kappa$, both negative. Hence the [homogeneous steady state](../../../../../homogeneous-equilibrium.md) is linearly [asymptotically stable](../../../../../asymptotic-stability.md) against homogeneous [perturbations](../../../../../perturbation.md). Even when $\kappa=1$, where the two [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) coincide and a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) factor may occur in the [matrix exponential](../../../../../matrix-exponential.md), all such [perturbations](../../../../../perturbation.md) still decay.
