↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Here $L_1=1$ and $L_2=1/2$, so a [perturbation](../../../../../perturbation.md) varying in both coordinates has $n,m\ge1$ and

$$
s=\pi^2(n^2+4m^2)\ge5\pi^2.
$$

With the given unit coefficients the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) becomes $D(s)=(1+s)^2-\beta s$. The [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) remains negative, so [instability](../../../../../instability.md) occurs precisely when $D(s)<0$, or

$$
\beta>s+2+\frac1s.
$$

The right-hand side is strictly increasing for $s>1$, since its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is $1-s^{-2}>0$. Its smallest allowed value is attained by the mode $(n,m)=(1,1)$, giving the onset threshold

$$
\boxed{\beta_{\rm crit}=5\pi^2+2+\frac1{5\pi^2}\simeq51.3683.}
$$

At this value the first mixed mode has a zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md); it becomes [unstable](../../../../../instability.md) for $\beta>\beta_{\rm crit}$. Thus the critical value is the infimum of the strictly [unstable](../../../../../instability.md) range, rather than a strictly [unstable](../../../../../instability.md) point itself. Its spatial factor is $\cos(\pi x)\cos(2\pi y)$.
