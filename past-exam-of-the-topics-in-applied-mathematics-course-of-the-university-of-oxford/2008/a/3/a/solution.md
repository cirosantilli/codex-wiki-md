<h1 id="a/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An unbiased [random walk](../../../../../random-walk-split.md) of [bacteria](../../../../../bacteria.md) leads, on the continuum scale, to [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) with coefficient $D$, represented by $DB_{XX}$. The [cell proliferation](../../../../../cell-proliferation.md) rate $\nu GB$ is proportional both to the available [biological growth factor](../../../../../biological-growth-factor.md) concentration and to the [bacterial density](../../../../../bacterial-density.md). Consumption of [biological growth factor](../../../../../biological-growth-factor.md) at rate $\mu GB$ gives its negative reaction term.

There is no [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) term for $G$ because the [biological growth factor](../../../../../biological-growth-factor.md) is attached to the substrate rather than freely transported. There is also no [chemotactic drift](../../../../../chemotactic-drift.md) in this model: the specified motion is random, while the [biological growth factor](../../../../../biological-growth-factor.md) changes [cell proliferation](../../../../../cell-proliferation.md) rather than directional movement. Both reaction terms vanish if either [bacteria](../../../../../bacteria.md) or [biological growth factor](../../../../../biological-growth-factor.md) are absent.
