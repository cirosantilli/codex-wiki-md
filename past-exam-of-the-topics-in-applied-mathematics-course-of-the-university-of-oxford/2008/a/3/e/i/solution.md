<h1 id="a/3/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At the leading edge $b\to0$ and $g\to1$. The linearized [bacterial](../../../../../../bacteria.md) equation is

$$
b''+cb'+b=0,
$$

with [characteristic exponents](../../../../../../characteristic-exponent.md)

$$
\lambda_\pm=\frac{-c\pm\sqrt{c^2-4}}2.
$$

For $0<c<2$ they form a [nonreal](../../../../../../nonreal-complex-number.md) [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) pair. A nonzero profile approaching the [travelling front](../../../../../../travelling-front.md) then has an exponentially decaying oscillatory leading term, and repeatedly changes sign. This is incompatible with the assumed positive [bacterial density](../../../../../../bacterial-density.md). The leading edge must therefore have real [characteristic exponents](../../../../../../characteristic-exponent.md), requiring

$$
\boxed{c\ge2.}
$$

At $c=2$ the repeated [characteristic exponent](../../../../../../characteristic-exponent.md) is $-1$ and [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) factors in the asymptotic profile may occur. The argument supplies a necessary lower bound; it is not an independent existence proof for every speed satisfying it.
