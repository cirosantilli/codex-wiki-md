<h1 id="a/3/e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the modified strain the [travelling wave](../../../../../../travelling-wave.md) equations are

$$
b''+cb'+gb(1-\eta b)=0,\qquad cg'=gb.
$$

Integrating the second across the entire [travelling wave](../../../../../../travelling-wave.md) gives $\int_{\mathbb R}gb\,dy=c$, since the [biological growth factor](../../../../../../biological-growth-factor.md) profile still goes from $0$ to $1$. Integrating the first, using the constant asymptotic states and vanishing [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) at infinity, gives

$$
cb_1=\int_{\mathbb R}gb(1-\eta b)\,dy
=c-\eta\int_{\mathbb R}gb^2\,dy.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{b_1=1-\frac\eta c\int_{\mathbb R}g(y)b(y)^2\,dy<1=b_0.}
$$

The subtracted [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) is strictly positive because the profiles are positive and $\eta>0$. It is finite: $b$ is bounded by continuity and its finite endpoint limits, and $\int gb=c$. The additional density-dependent inhibition therefore reduces the [bacterial density](../../../../../../bacterial-density.md) left behind; positivity of $b_1$ is part of the assumed positive-wave solution.
