<h1 id="a/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Substitute $gb=cg'$ into the [bacterial](../../../../../bacteria.md) profile equation. This gives

$$
b''+cb'+cg'=0,
$$

which integrates once to

$$
b'+c(b+g)=C.
$$

At the asymptotic constant states the profile [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) tends to zero. The state ahead of the [travelling wave](../../../../../travelling-wave.md), where $b\to0$ and $g\to1$, therefore gives $C=c$. The state behind it, where $g\to0$ and $b\to b_0$, gives $C=cb_0$. Since $c>0$,

$$
\boxed{b_0=1,\qquad b'+c(b+g)=c.}
$$

This [first integral](../../../../../first-integral.md) expresses the matching between biomass produced and [biological growth factor](../../../../../biological-growth-factor.md) consumed after the chosen [nondimensionalization](../../../../../nondimensionalization.md).
