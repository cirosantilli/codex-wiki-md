<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose a positive reference [biological growth factor](../../../../../biological-growth-factor.md) concentration $G_{ref}$, for example its [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) ahead of the [travelling wave](../../../../../travelling-wave.md). Set

$$
T=T_0t,\quad X=X_0x,\quad G=G_{ref}g,\quad B=B_0b,
$$

where

$$
\boxed{T_0=\frac1{\nu G_{ref}},\qquad X_0=\sqrt{\frac D{\nu G_{ref}}},\qquad B_0=\frac{\nu G_{ref}}\mu.}
$$

The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) then transforms the equations into

$$
b_t=\frac{DT_0}{X_0^2}b_{xx}+\nu G_{ref}T_0gb,\qquad g_t=-\mu B_0T_0gb.
$$

Each displayed coefficient is one with the chosen scales. Hence the [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) system is

$$
\boxed{b_t=b_{xx}+gb,\qquad g_t=-gb.}
$$

The free choice of $G_{ref}$ expresses the freedom to normalize the as-yet unspecified [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) scale.
