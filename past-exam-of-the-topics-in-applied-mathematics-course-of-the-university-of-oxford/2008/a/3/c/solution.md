<h1 id="a/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take profiles $b=b(y)$ and $g=g(y)$ with $y=x-ct$, and use a prime for [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) with respect to $y$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $b_t=-cb'$, $b_{xx}=b''$ and $g_t=-cg'$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{b''+cb'+gb=0,\qquad cg'=gb.}
$$

These are coupled [ordinary differential equations](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) for a right-moving [travelling wave](../../../../../travelling-wave.md) with speed $c>0$. A first-order formulation, using $v=b'$, is $b'=v$, $v'=-cv-gb$, $g'=gb/c$. In particular a positive profile has $g'>0$, consistent with depleted substrate behind the [travelling front](../../../../../travelling-front.md) and fresh [biological growth factor](../../../../../biological-growth-factor.md) ahead.
