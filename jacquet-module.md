# Jacquet module

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth representation of a locally profinite group](smooth-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacquet_module)

The unnormalized [Jacquet module](jacquet-module.md) is the space of coinvariants of a unipotent subgroup $U$. If a subgroup $T$ normalizes $U$, it acts on this quotient. Every [linear functional](linear-functional.md) transforming by a [linear character](linear-character.md) trivial on $U$ factors through it. Normalized Jacquet functors include an additional modulus twist; that convention must be specified.
