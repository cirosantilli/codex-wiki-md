# Expected payoff

↑ **Parent:** [Payoff](payoff.md)

For a [finite game](finite-game.md), let $s=(s_1,\ldots,s_n)$ range over all [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md) [strategy profiles](strategy-profile.md), and let $\sigma_j(s_j)$ be player $j$'s [probability](probability.md) of choosing $s_j$. Under [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [mixed strategies](mixed-strategy.md), the [probability](probability.md) of $s$ is $\prod_j\sigma_j(s_j)$. The displayed sum is the [expected value](expected-value.md) of player $i$'s [payoff](payoff.md). Holding everyone else's [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) fixed makes $U_i$ a [linear function](linear-function.md) of $\sigma_i$; this is why checking [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md) deviations suffices to check all [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) deviations.
