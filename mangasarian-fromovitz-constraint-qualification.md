<h1 id="mangasarian-fromovitz-constraint-qualification">Mangasarian–Fromovitz constraint qualification</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint qualification](constraint-qualification.md)

At a feasible point for smooth $h=0$, $g\geq0$, the equality gradients must be independent and there must be a direction $v$ with $Dh\,v=0$ and $Dg_i\,v>0$ for every active inequality. It supplies a first-order direction strictly into all active inequality constraints while respecting the equality tangent space. This [constraint qualification](constraint-qualification.md) ensures the usual [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) are necessary for local optimality. For a strictly convex quadratic inequality restricted to an affine equality space, it holds at every feasible point exactly when a strictly feasible point exists; moving from a boundary point toward a strictly feasible point supplies the required direction.
