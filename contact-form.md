# Contact form

↑ **Parent:** [Contact structure](contact-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_form)

A contact form on a $(2n+1)$-manifold is a one-form satisfying $\alpha\wedge(d\alpha)^n\ne0$ everywhere.
