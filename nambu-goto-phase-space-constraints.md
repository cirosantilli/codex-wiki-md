<h1 id="nambu-goto-phase-space-constraints">Nambu–Goto phase-space constraints</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu-Goto phase-space action](nambu-goto-phase-space-action.md)

The two constraints are $P^2+T^2X'^2=0$ and $P\cdot X'=0$. They are [first-class constraints](first-class-constraint.md) associated with [worldsheet diffeomorphisms](worldsheet-diffeomorphism.md). Their [Lagrange multipliers](lagrange-multiplier.md) are arbitrary gauge functions rather than propagating fields.
