# Natal kick binding criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Supernova kick in a binary star](supernova-kick-in-a-binary-star.md)

For an initially circular binary losing mass instantaneously, let $f$ be the retained total mass fraction and $q$ the kick speed in units of the initial relative orbital speed. A kick exactly parallel or opposite to the exploding star's [velocity](velocity.md) leaves negative relative [orbital energy](orbital-energy.md) precisely for the title inequality. Equality is marginal escape. For an opposite kick $q=1$, the relative [velocity](velocity.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) vanish: negative [energy](energy-split.md) then produces a radial collision, rather than a persistent finite-pericentre binary.
