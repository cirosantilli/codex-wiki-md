# Hyperpolarization

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane potential](membrane-potential.md)

[Hyperpolarization](hyperpolarization.md) makes a neuronal [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md) more negative relative to the exterior. It is a voltage description; inhibition need not produce [hyperpolarization](hyperpolarization.md), as [shunting inhibition](shunting-inhibition.md) illustrates.
