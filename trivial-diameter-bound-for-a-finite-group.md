# Trivial diameter bound for a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Diameter of a finite group with respect to a generating set](diameter-of-a-finite-group-with-respect-to-a-generating-set.md)

A connected [Cayley graph](cayley-graph.md) on a finite group $G$ has $|G|$ vertices, and a shortest path repeats no vertex. Therefore

$$
\operatorname{diam}_S(G)\leq|G|-1.
$$
