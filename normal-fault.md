# Normal fault

↑ **Parent:** [Normal faulting](normal-faulting.md)

A [fault](geological-fault.md) on which the [hanging wall](hanging-wall.md) moves downward relative to the [footwall](footwall.md). Local bending or collapse can create small normal faults even during a regional reverse-faulting event.
