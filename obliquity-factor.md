# Obliquity factor

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction](diffraction.md)

The [obliquity factor](obliquity-factor.md) weights the directional contribution of an aperture element in a scalar [diffraction](diffraction.md) integral. In a fixed observation direction in the [Fraunhofer diffraction](fraunhofer-diffraction.md) regime, its variation across a sufficiently small aperture can be neglected along with the variation of the geometric amplitude denominator. Its angular dependence can still matter when comparing widely separated observation directions.
