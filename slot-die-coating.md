# Slot-die coating

↑ **Parent:** [Thin film](thin-film.md)

Metered delivery of a liquid film from a slot onto a moving substrate. It can support continuous web production, but uniform wet-film thickness, ink rheology, drying, substrate [wetting](wetting.md) and particle orientation affect the final coating.
