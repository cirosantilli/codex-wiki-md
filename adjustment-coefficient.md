# Adjustment coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md)

The adjustment coefficient is a positive root of $\lambda(M_X(r)-1)=cr$ in the [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md). When the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is finite at $R$, the process $\exp(R[\sum_{j=1}^{N_t}X_j-ct])$ is a [continuous-time martingale](continuous-time-martingale.md). It yields the [Lundberg inequality](lundberg-inequality.md) and, under the relevant tilted integrability, the [Cramér–Lundberg ruin asymptotic](cramer-lundberg-ruin-asymptotic.md).
