# Two-stack encoding of a Turing tape

↑ **Parent:** [Turing machine](turing-machine.md)

Encode the cells immediately left of the head in a reversed positional stack $L$, and the current and right cells in $R$. Quotient and remainder by the alphabet base read and remove cells; multiplication by that base pushes a cell. The resulting local tape transitions are [primitive recursive](primitive-recursive-function.md), which makes them directly implementable on [Church numerals](church-numeral.md).
