# Kelvin-Voigt harmonic compliance

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin-Voigt model](kelvin-voigt-model.md)

Under harmonic [stress](stress.md), [strain](strain.md) amplitude is $\sigma_0/\sqrt{E^2+\omega^2\eta^2}$ and its lag is $\arctan(\omega\eta/E)$. Low frequencies are nearly elastic; high-frequency stress-controlled deformation is small and nearly a quarter cycle behind [stress](stress.md).
