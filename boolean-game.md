# Boolean game

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

A finite game represented succinctly by [propositional logic](propositional-logic.md). Each player controls a disjoint collection of [Boolean variables](boolean-variable.md) and chooses their truth values. Together those choices determine a [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md). A player's goal is a [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md), with [payoff](payoff.md) one if satisfied and zero otherwise. The control sets normally form a [set partition](set-partition.md) of all variables.

A [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md) [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md) is a [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md) if every unsatisfied player remains unsatisfied after every change to that player's controlled variables. Checking a candidate can therefore require solving a [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md), even though evaluating its current [payoffs](payoff.md) is easy.
