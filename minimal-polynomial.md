# Minimal polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Operator theory](linear-operator-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_polynomial_(linear_algebra))

For a [square matrix](square-matrix.md) over a [field](field.md), the minimal polynomial $m_A$ is the unique [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) of least degree satisfying $m_A(A)=0$. The [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](cayley-hamilton-theorem-split.md) guarantees at least one such [polynomial](polynomial-split.md): the [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md). The minimal polynomial divides every [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) that annihilates $A$, so reducing [matrix powers](matrix-power.md) modulo $m_A$ can need fewer coefficients than reducing modulo the [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md).

For example, $2I_2$ has [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) $t-2$, whereas the [Jordan block](jordan-block.md) $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ has [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) $(t-2)^2$. Both have the same [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md). Their different [matrix powers](matrix-power.md) are illustrated in [repeated eigenvalues in the Cayley-Hamilton theorem](repeated-eigenvalues-in-the-cayley-hamilton-theorem.md).
