# Cartesian comonad

↑ **Parent:** [Comonad](comonad.md)

In the finite-limit setting, a Cartesian comonad is a [comonad](comonad.md) whose underlying endofunctor preserves [finite limits](finite-limit.md). Its [category of coalgebras for a comonad](category-of-coalgebras-for-a-comonad.md) has finite limits created by the [forgetful functor](forgetful-functor.md). On an [elementary topos](elementary-topos.md), equalizer constructions of [exponentials in a coalgebra topos](exponentials-in-a-coalgebra-topos.md) and the [subobject classifier of a coalgebra topos](subobject-classifier-of-a-coalgebra-topos.md) make that coalgebra category an elementary topos.
