# Gauss equation

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss–Codazzi equations](gauss-codazzi-equations.md)

For a Euclidean embedded submanifold,

$$
\langle R(X,Y)Z,W\rangle
=\langle A(X,W),A(Y,Z)\rangle-\langle A(X,Z),A(Y,W)\rangle.
$$

It expresses intrinsic curvature in terms of the [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md).
