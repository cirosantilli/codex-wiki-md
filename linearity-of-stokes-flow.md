# Linearity of Stokes flow

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

The [Stokes equation](stokes-equation.md) is linear in velocity, pressure, body force, and boundary data. Solutions may therefore be superposed, and rigid-body velocities depend linearly on applied forces and torques.
