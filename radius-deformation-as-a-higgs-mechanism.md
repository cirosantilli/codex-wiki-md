# Radius deformation as a Higgs mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Self-dual circle compactification](self-dual-circle-compactification.md)

At the [self-dual circle](self-dual-circle-compactification.md), left and right dimension-one currents generate $SU(2)_L\times SU(2)_R$. The compact-coordinate vertex $\partial Y_L\bar\partial Y_R$ is a Cartan–Cartan component of a [scalar field](scalar-field.md) in the $(3,3)$ representation. Its [expectation value](expectation-value.md) changes the circle radius and leaves $U(1)_L\times U(1)_R$ unbroken. The four extra charged vectors acquire the displayed mass, and four [scalar field](scalar-field.md) polarizations become their longitudinal modes. This realizes the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md) geometrically. In a bosonic string the ever-present [tachyon](tachyon.md) and additional [scalar field](scalar-field.md) instabilities prevent interpreting the whole family as a stable vacuum.
