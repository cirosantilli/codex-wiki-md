# Factorial of a Poisson random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md)

If $X$ has a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) with parameter $\lambda>0$, then the ordinary [factorial](factorial.md) $X!$ has finite [expectation](expected-value-split.md) exactly when $\lambda<1$. Indeed, its nonnegative expectation [series](series-mathematics.md) is $e^{-\lambda}\sum_{k\geq0}\lambda^k$, since $k!$ cancels the denominator of the [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md). The [geometric series](geometric-series.md) gives the formula and proves divergence for $\lambda\geq1$. This differs from a falling-factorial [moment](moment.md) of fixed order, which is finite for every finite [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) parameter.
