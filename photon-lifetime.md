# Photon lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Optical cavity](optical-cavity.md)

The [photon lifetime](photon-lifetime.md) is the exponential decay time of stored [energy](energy-split.md) in an unpumped [optical cavity](optical-cavity.md). If a round trip of duration $T$ retains an energy fraction $\rho$, then $\tau_c=-T/\log\rho$ and the [quality factor](q-factor.md) is $Q=\omega\tau_c$. Field-amplitude decay takes twice this time. Output coupling contributes to the loss as well as absorption and scattering.
