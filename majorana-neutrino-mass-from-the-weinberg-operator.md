# Majorana neutrino mass from the Weinberg operator

↑ **Parent:** [Weinberg operator](weinberg-operator.md)

For the gauge-singlet composite $\chi=\widetilde\phi^\dagger L$, the interaction $G\chi^TC^{-1}\chi-G^*\bar\chi C\bar\chi^T$ is electroweak [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md). At the [Higgs field](higgs-field.md) vacuum, $\chi=v\nu_L/\sqrt2$, giving $\tfrac12(Gv^2\nu_L^TC^{-1}\nu_L-G^*v^2\bar\nu_L C\bar\nu_L^T)$. The [Grassmann variation of a chiral Majorana mass term](grassmann-variation-of-a-chiral-majorana-mass-term.md) therefore identifies the mass parameter as $Gv^2$, with physical mass $|G|v^2$. The dimension-five interaction has a coupling of mass dimension minus one. Under [lepton number](lepton-number.md) phase rotation, its two terms acquire factors $e^{\pm2i\alpha}$, so it violates continuous lepton number by two units. It is an effective extension of the minimal renormalizable [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md).
