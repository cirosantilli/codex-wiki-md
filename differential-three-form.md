# Differential three-form

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form](differential-form-split.md)

A differential three-form is a smooth, totally antisymmetric covariant tensor of rank three. For a [Killing-Yano two-form](killing-yano-two-form.md), its [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) is such a tensor, because it is antisymmetric in the first two and last two slots.
