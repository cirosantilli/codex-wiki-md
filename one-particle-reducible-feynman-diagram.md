# One-particle-reducible Feynman diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Connected Feynman diagram](connected-feynman-diagram.md)

A connected [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) is one-particle reducible if cutting one internal propagator disconnects it. These diagrams occur in [connected correlation functions](connected-correlation-function.md) but do not contribute to a [quantum effective action](effective-action.md) defined by a [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md). For a strict [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md), a bridge also cannot be a high-momentum propagator when the sum of external momenta on one side lies below the integrated shell.
