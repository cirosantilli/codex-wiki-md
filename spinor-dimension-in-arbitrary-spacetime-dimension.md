# Spinor dimension in arbitrary spacetime dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Spin representation](spin-representation.md)

An irreducible complex [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) module in even spacetime dimension $2k$ has dimension $2^k$ and splits under the connected [Spin group](spin-group.md) into two [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) modules of dimension $2^{k-1}$. In odd dimension $2k+1$, an irreducible spinor still has dimension $2^k$ but has no Weyl splitting. Reality conditions depend on signature and are additional to these complex counts.
