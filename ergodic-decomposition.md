# Ergodic decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodicity](ergodicity.md)

For a [measure-preserving system](measure-preserving-system.md) on a standard probability space, an invariant probability measure is an integral of ergodic invariant probability measures. Its conditional measures given the [invariant sigma-algebra](invariant-sigma-algebra.md) are the ergodic components.
