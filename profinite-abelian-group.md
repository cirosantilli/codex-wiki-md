# Profinite abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Profinite group](profinite-group.md)

A profinite abelian group is an inverse limit of finite [abelian groups](abelian-group.md). Its topology is compact and totally disconnected. An abelian [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) acquires scalar multiplication by [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md) by taking limits of integer multiples.
