# Repeated-point Abel differential formula

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative of the Abelian sum map](derivative-of-the-abelian-sum-map.md)

At a multiplicity-$r$ point, choose a local parameter and elementary symmetric coordinates $e_1,\ldots,e_r$ for the nearby roots. The tangent [principal part](principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md) is $v=\sum_{k=1}^r(-1)^{k-1}\delta e_k z^{-k}$. [Newton's identities](newton-s-identities.md) give $d(\sum z_i^k)=(-1)^{k-1}k\,d e_k$. Expanding the primitive of a [holomorphic one-form](holomorphic-one-form.md) proves the [residue](residue.md) formula, including its sign. The dual map is restriction to the length-$r$ scheme, so it sees all jets through order $r-1$.
