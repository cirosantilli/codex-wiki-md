# Spatial metric sign for a unit timelike normal

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial projection tensor](spatial-projection-tensor.md)

With signature $+---$ and $n^\mu n_\mu=1$, the [spatial projection tensor](spatial-projection-tensor.md) is $P^\mu{}_\nu=\delta^\mu{}_\nu-n^\mu n_\nu$. Projecting the spacetime [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) gives $q_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}-n_\mu n_\nu$, which is negative definite on spatial vectors. The positive spatial [induced metric](induced-metric.md) is $h_{\mu\nu}=-q_{\mu\nu}$. A plus sign in $g_{\mu\nu}+n_\mu n_\nu$ belongs to a different normal/signature convention and is not transverse in this one. Fully projected [covariant derivatives](covariant-derivative.md) of either spatial metric vanish by [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md).
