# Binormal vector

↑ **Parent:** [Frenet frame](frenet-frame.md)

The binormal is $B=T\times N$, the [cross product](cross-product.md) of the [unit tangent vector](unit-tangent-vector.md) and [principal normal vector](principal-normal-vector.md). It is a unit [vector](vector.md) perpendicular to both factors and completes the oriented [Frenet frame](frenet-frame.md).
