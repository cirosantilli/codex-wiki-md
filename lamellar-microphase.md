# Lamellar microphase

↑ **Parent:** [Microphase separation](microphase-separation.md)

Alternating sheet-like domains of the components of a microphase-separated material. In diblock systems the two layer thicknesses can be unequal and reflect unequal block fractions. Confinement, surface [wetting](wetting.md) and kinetic trapping can produce layered films outside the [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) bulk lamellar region.
