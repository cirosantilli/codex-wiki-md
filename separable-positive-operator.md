# Separable positive operator

↑ **Parent:** [Separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md)

A separable positive operator is a finite sum $\eta_{AB}=\sum_y\alpha_y\otimes\beta_y$ with both factors [positive operators](positive-operator.md). If its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is one, normalizing the nonzero factors gives a [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md). Conversely, a nonnegative scalar multiple of a [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md) is a separable positive operator.
