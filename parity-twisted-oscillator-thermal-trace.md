# Parity-twisted oscillator thermal trace

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal trace](thermal-trace.md)

For two equal-frequency [quantum harmonic oscillators](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md), the ordinary [thermal trace](thermal-trace.md) is $r/(1-r)^2$ and the [trace](matrix-trace.md) with the [parity operator](parity-operator.md) is $r/(1+r)^2$, where $r=e^{-\beta\omega}$. Total excitation $N$ has degeneracy $N+1$ and parity $(-1)^N$. Therefore the traces expand as $\sum_{n\ge1}(\pm1)^{n-1}nr^n$. The alternating weights come from parity insertion in a bosonic state space, not fermionic statistics.
