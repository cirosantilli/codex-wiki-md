# Equal modal energies for inverse-harmonic string amplitudes

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md)

For a uniform ideal fixed-end string, a real [normal mode](normal-mode.md) of displacement amplitude $A_n$ has total [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) $T\pi^2n^2A_n^2/(4L)$. This follows by integrating the squared sine velocity and cosine slope over the length; each squared trigonometric function integrates to $L/2$. Thus finitely many excited modes with $|A_n|$ proportional to $1/n$ have equal total modal energies. Their instantaneous kinetic energies need not agree, because their time factors oscillate at different frequencies.
