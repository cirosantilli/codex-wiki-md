# K-isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Field isomorphism](field-isomorphism.md)

A K-isomorphism between extensions of $K$ is a [field isomorphism](field-isomorphism.md) fixing $K$ pointwise. Simple algebraic extensions $K(\alpha)$ and $K(\beta)$ admit one sending $\alpha$ to $\beta$ exactly when their [minimal polynomials](minimal-polynomial.md) agree: both then identify with the same quotient $K[X]/(m)$.
