# Twistor correspondence

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor theory](twistor-theory.md)

The [flag manifold](flag-manifold.md) of a line $\ell$ contained in a two-plane $W$ has projections $(\ell,W)\mapsto W$ and $(\ell,W)\mapsto\ell$. Their fibers are respectively $\mathbb{CP}^1$ and $\mathbb{CP}^2$. This is the flat [twistor correspondence](twistor-correspondence.md): [complexified compactified Minkowski space](complexified-compactified-minkowski-space.md) is the [Grassmannian](grassmannian.md) of two-planes in [twistor space](twistor-space.md), while [projective twistor space](projective-twistor-space.md) is its projective space of lines.
