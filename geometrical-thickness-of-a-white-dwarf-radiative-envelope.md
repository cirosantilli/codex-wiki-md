# Geometrical thickness of a white-dwarf radiative envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative-zero white-dwarf envelope](radiative-zero-white-dwarf-envelope.md)

Integrating the [hydrostatic pressure support equation](hydrostatic-pressure-support-equation.md) with the [radiative-zero white-dwarf envelope](radiative-zero-white-dwarf-envelope.md) law yields $T=4GM(1/r-1/R)/(17\mathcal R)$. Matching $T=T_c$ at $r_b$ gives $s=R-r_b$ as displayed. A strongly degenerate bulk core has thermal energy small compared with its [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md), making $q\ll1$ and hence $s\ll R$. The inequality $T_c\gg T_{\rm eff}$ alone does not establish geometrical thinness.
