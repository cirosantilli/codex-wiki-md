# Poisson approximation with dependency neighborhoods

↑ **Parent:** [Stein-Chen method](stein-chen-method.md)

For indicators $I_a$ with means $p_a$, let $B_a$ contain $a$ and suppose $I_a$ is independent of the entire family outside $B_a$. Put $\mu=\sum_ap_a$, $b_1=\sum_a\sum_{b\in B_a}p_ap_b$ and $b_2=\sum_a\sum_{b\in B_a\setminus\{a\}}\mathbb E(I_aI_b)$. The [Poisson Stein equation](poisson-stein-equation.md) and its bound $\|\Delta f\|_\infty\le1$ give the displayed [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md) estimate. Stronger Stein factors can improve the bound when $\mu$ is large.
