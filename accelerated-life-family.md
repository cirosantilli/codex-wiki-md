# Accelerated life family

↑ **Parent:** [Accelerated failure time model](accelerated-failure-time-model.md)

An accelerated life family consists of [survival distributions](survival-distribution.md) related by positive time scaling: $T_z$ has the distribution of $c_zT_0$. Therefore the [survivor function](survival-function.md) is $S_z(t)=S_0(t/c_z)$ and, for densities, the [hazard function](hazard-function.md) is $h_z(t)=c_z^{-1}h_0(t/c_z)$. This need not be a constant hazard ratio.
