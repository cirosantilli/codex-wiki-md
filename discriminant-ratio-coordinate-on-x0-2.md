# Discriminant-ratio coordinate on X0 2

↑ **Parent:** [X0 2 modular curve](x0-2-modular-curve.md)

The ratio of [modular discriminants](modular-discriminant.md) has weight zero and level $\Gamma_0(2)$. It has one [simple pole](simple-pole.md) at infinity and one simple zero at the zero [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md), with no zeros or [poles](pole.md) in the half-plane. It is a spherical coordinate by the [single-pole criterion for a spherical coordinate](single-pole-criterion-for-a-spherical-coordinate.md). The [Klein j-invariant](klein-j-invariant.md) is $(t+256)^3/t^2$, with $t(e^{2\pi i/3})=-256$.
