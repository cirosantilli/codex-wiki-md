# Free electron model

↑ **Parent:** [Electron gas](electron-gas.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_electron_model)

The [free electron model](free-electron-model.md) approximates conduction [electrons](electron.md) by noninteracting particles with the displayed parabolic [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md), filling one-particle states according to [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md). A uniform positive background supplies [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md). [Periodic boundary conditions](periodic-boundary-conditions.md) give one spatial state per $k$-space volume $(2\pi)^d/V_d$ in $d$ dimensions; the physical [spin](spin.md) degeneracy is two. The model captures basic state counting and degeneracy but omits the periodic lattice potential, interaction corrections and detailed [electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md).
