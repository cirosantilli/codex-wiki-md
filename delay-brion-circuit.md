# Delay-Brion circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Neural circuit](neural-circuit.md)

A historical account linking medial temporal and diencephalic [amnesia](amnesia.md) through an extended hippocampal system, associated with [Jean Delay](jean-delay.md) and [Serge Brion](serge-brion.md). Important structures include the [hippocampus](hippocampus.md), [fornix](fornix.md), [mammillary bodies](mammillary-body.md) and [anterior thalamic nuclei](anterior-thalamic-nuclei.md). It overlaps the [Papez circuit](papez-circuit.md), but an explanatory claim about memory is not identical to the original proposal about emotion.[Abstract: historical antecedent and proposed extended system](episodic-memory-amnesia-and-the-hippocampal-anterior-thalamic-axis-by-john-p-aggleton-and-others.md)
