# Natural cubic spline interpolant

↑ **Parent:** [Natural cubic spline](natural-cubic-spline.md)

For at least two distinct knots $x_1<\cdots<x_n$ and values $v_i$, the unique [natural cubic spline](natural-cubic-spline.md) matching those values and linear beyond $x_1,x_n$. Its interpolation map is a [linear map](linear-map.md) by uniqueness. Over a larger domain $[a,b]$, the two exterior pieces remain linear. With only one knot, arbitrary slopes of [affine functions](affine-function.md) make uniqueness false.
