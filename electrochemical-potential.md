# Electrochemical potential

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrochemical_potential)

The molar [electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md) combines the molar [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) $\mu_{\rm m}$ with the electrical contribution $zF_{\rm Far}\psi$, where $z$ is the signed charge number of an [ion](ion.md), $F_{\rm Far}=N_{\rm A}e$ is the [Faraday constant](faraday-constant.md) and $\psi$ is [electric potential](electric-potential.md). Both terms have units $\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}$. In the particle convention the corresponding expression is $\widetilde\mu_{\rm particle}=\mu_{\rm particle}+ze\psi$, with [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md) $e$; multiplying it by the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) gives the molar form. Transfer between locations at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md) is driven by differences in [electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md). The [membrane transport](membrane-transport.md) examples use it to distinguish neutral [solute](solute.md) transfer from [ion](ion.md) transfer.
