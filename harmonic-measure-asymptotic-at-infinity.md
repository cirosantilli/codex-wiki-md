# Harmonic-measure asymptotic at infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md)

For a [compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md) and a Borel subset of the [intrinsic boundary of a simply connected domain](intrinsic-boundary-of-a-simply-connected-domain.md), [hydrodynamic normalization at infinity](hydrodynamic-normalization-at-infinity.md) and the [Poisson kernel for the upper half-plane](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md) give this limit as $y\to\infty$ with $x/y\to0$. After multiplying the kernel by $\pi y$, it tends pointwise to one and is eventually uniformly bounded. [Dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) proves the finite-measure case and [Fatou's lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) proves the infinite-measure case.
