# Slack variable

↑ **Parent:** [Linear programming](linear-programming-split.md)

A slack variable turns an inequality constraint $a^Tx\le b$ into the equality $a^Tx+s=b$ with $s\ge0$. Its value measures the unused amount of the constraint. In the [simplex algorithm](simplex-algorithm.md), slack variables often supply the initial [basic feasible solution](basic-feasible-solution.md).
