# Strict hyperbolicity

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolicity](hyperbolicity.md)

A first-order system $U_t+A(U)U_x=C(U)$ is strictly hyperbolic at a state when $A$ has distinct real [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). These separate its [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) families and permit independent compatibility equations from [left eigenvectors](left-eigenvector.md). At a repeated [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) [strict hyperbolicity](strict-hyperbolicity.md) fails; if the [matrix](matrix.md) is also defective, even [hyperbolicity](hyperbolicity.md) in the complete-[eigenvector](eigenvector.md) sense fails. For $A=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}u&2\\2v&u\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $u\pm2\sqrt v$ are distinct precisely when $v>0$.
