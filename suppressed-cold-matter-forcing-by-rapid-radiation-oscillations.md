# Suppressed cold-matter forcing by rapid radiation oscillations

↑ **Parent:** [Subhorizon radiation acoustic oscillations](subhorizon-radiation-acoustic-oscillations.md)

In deep [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md), an oscillatory radiation density source in the matter equation has order $\Delta_r/\tau^2$ and frequency of order $k$. Its rapidly varying particular response is therefore of order $\Delta_r/(k\tau)^2$. For $k\tau\gg1$ it does not control the secular matter growth, though forcing near horizon entry sets the constant and logarithmic homogeneous amplitudes.
