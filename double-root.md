# Double root

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md)

A [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) is double when its [multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md) is exactly two. Equivalently, $p(x)=(x-x_0)^2q(x)$ with $q(x_0)\ne0$. A generic small perturbation splits it according to [square-root splitting of a double polynomial root](square-root-splitting-of-a-double-polynomial-root.md).
