# Geopotential surface

↑ **Parent:** [Geopotential](geopotential.md)

A surface on which [geopotential](geopotential.md) is constant. Effective [gravity](gravity.md) is perpendicular to it. In a local constant-gravity approximation these surfaces are horizontal; comparing their slopes with [isopycnals](isopycnal.md) identifies the geometry permitting an [adiabatic parcel exchange](adiabatic-parcel-exchange.md) to release [available potential energy](available-potential-energy.md).
