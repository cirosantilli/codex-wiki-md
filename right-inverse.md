# Right inverse

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse function](inverse-function.md)

A right inverse of a [function](function-split.md) $f:X\to Y$ is a function $g:Y\to X$ satisfying $f\circ g=\operatorname{id}_Y$. It forces $f$ to be a [surjective function](surjective-function.md). A [surjective linear map](surjective-linear-map.md) onto a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) has a linear right inverse: lift a [basis](basis.md) of the target and extend by [linearity](linearity.md).
