# Unitary sphere quotient and stability range

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary group](unitary-group.md)

The last-column map has fibre the subgroup fixing the last standard unit vector. Unitary completion gives surjectivity, and [compactness](compact-space.md) gives the quotient [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md). Projecting the other standard vectors onto the [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) of a nearby unit vector and applying Gram-Schmidt gives a local section, so the projection is a fiber bundle. The bundle $U(n)\to U(n+1)\to S^{2n+1}$ then gives $\pi_iU(n)\cong\pi_iU(n+1)$ for $i\le2n-1$ because both adjacent [sphere](sphere.md) [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) vanish.
