# High-mass X-ray binary

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray binary](x-ray-binary.md)

A high-mass [X-ray binary](x-ray-binary.md) has a massive [donor star](donor-star.md) supplying an accreting [neutron star](neutron-star.md) or [black hole](black-hole.md). A strong [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md) often feeds it, although [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md) and donor equatorial outflows also occur. The short lifetime of the donor associates these systems with young stellar populations.
