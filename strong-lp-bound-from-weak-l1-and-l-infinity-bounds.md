# Strong Lp bound from weak L1 and L-infinity bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-type operator](weak-type-operator.md)

Let $S$ be a [sublinear operator](sublinear-operator.md) with positive constants and $\mu\{Sf>a\}\leq C_1\|f\|_1/a$ and $\|Sf\|_\infty\leq C_\infty\|f\|_\infty$. Split $f$ into its part above $a/(2C_\infty)$ and its bounded remainder. The latter contributes at most $a/2$, so the weak bound controls the superlevel measure by $2C_1a^{-1}\int_{|f|>a/(2C_\infty)}|f|$. Integrating this estimate with [layer cake representation](layer-cake-representation.md) proves the displayed bound for $1<p<\infty$ and $q=p/(p-1)$. In particular it gives the strong $L^p$ estimate for the [Hardy-Littlewood maximal function](hardy-littlewood-maximal-function.md).
