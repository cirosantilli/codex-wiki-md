# Direct image of a coherent sheaf under a closed immersion

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md)

If $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ is a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md) of [Noetherian schemes](noetherian-scheme.md) and $\mathcal F$ is a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) on $Z$, then $i_*\mathcal F$ is coherent on $X$. Affine-locally this regards a finitely generated $A/I$-module as a finitely generated $A$-module.
