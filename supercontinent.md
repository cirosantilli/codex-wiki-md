# Supercontinent

↑ **Parent:** [Plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supercontinent)

A continental assembly containing most of the [Earth](earth.md)'s continental area or major [cratons](craton.md). Its reconstruction requires age-compatible geological connections and [paleomagnetism](paleomagnetism.md); similarity of modern continental outlines alone is insufficient.
