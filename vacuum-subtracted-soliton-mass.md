# Vacuum-subtracted soliton mass

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field-theory soliton](classical-field-theory-soliton-split.md)

The rest mass of a stable [soliton](soliton.md) is the lowest energy in its [topological sector](topological-sector.md) minus the vacuum energy, in the infinite-volume limit. If Euclidean kernels $K_Q(\tau)$ have nonzero overlap with the sector's lowest-energy states, then $M=-\lim_{\tau\to\infty}\tau^{-1}\log[K_1(\tau)/K_0(\tau)]$, followed by the infinite-volume limit. A [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md) represents these kernels, with boundary wavefunctionals included if necessary.
