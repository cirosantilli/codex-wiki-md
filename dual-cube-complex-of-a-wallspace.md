# Dual cube complex of a wallspace

↑ **Parent:** [Wallspace](wallspace.md)

The vertices of the dual cube complex are consistent choices of one halfspace of every wall that differ from a principal choice on only finitely many walls. Two vertices are joined when their choices differ on one wall, and higher cubes fill the resulting hypercubes. The result is a [CAT(0) cube complex](cat-0-cube-complex.md).
