# Rytov approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse scattering problem](inverse-scattering-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rytov_approximation)

The Rytov approximation writes a total wave field as $\psi=\psi_i e^\phi$ and linearizes the equation for the complex logarithmic perturbation $\phi$. At first order, $\psi_i\phi$ equals the first [Born approximation](born-approximation.md) scattered field, so

$$
\psi_R=\psi_i\exp(\psi_{s,B}/\psi_i).
$$

It can remain useful when phase accumulation is appreciable but local scattering is weak.
