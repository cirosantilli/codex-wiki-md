# Derivations of a semisimple Lie algebra are inner

↑ **Parent:** [Inner derivation of a Lie algebra](inner-derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

For a finite-dimensional complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), every derivation is an [inner derivation of a Lie algebra](inner-derivation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Adjoin a derivation $\delta$ as a one-dimensional acting algebra, obtaining $M=L\rtimes K\delta$ with $[\delta,l]=\delta(l)$. The [semisimple Lie ideal has a commuting ideal complement](semisimple-lie-ideal-has-a-commuting-ideal-complement.md) result supplies a one-dimensional complementary ideal commuting with $L$. Its vector with derivation component $\delta$ is $\delta-x$ for some $x\in L$. Commuting this with arbitrary $l\in L$ gives $\delta(l)=[x,l]$. The zero derivation is represented by $x=0$.
