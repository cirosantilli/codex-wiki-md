# Superconducting transition temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity.md)

The [superconducting transition temperature](superconducting-transition-temperature.md) $T_c$ is the [temperature](temperature.md) at which the material enters [superconductivity](superconductivity.md) on cooling in zero applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) and with negligible measuring [electric current](electric-current.md). A measured transition at nonzero [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) or appreciable [electric current](electric-current.md) need not occur at this zero-field $T_c$. If a [resistance-versus-temperature experiment for superconductivity](resistance-versus-temperature-experiment-for-superconductivity.md) gives a transition spread over several [temperatures](temperature.md), report whether its quoted value is the onset, midpoint, or disappearance of detectable [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md), together with the experimental conditions. This operational distinction matters when comparing samples.
