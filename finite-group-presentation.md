# Finite group presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Group presentation](group-presentation.md)

A finite group presentation has finitely many generators and finitely many relators. It defines the quotient of the [free group](free-group.md) on $X$ by the [normal closure](normal-closure.md) of $R$. [Free products](free-product.md) of finitely presented groups and [HNN extensions](hnn-extension.md) along explicitly finitely generated associated subgroups have finite presentations obtained by adjoining finitely many generators and relations.
