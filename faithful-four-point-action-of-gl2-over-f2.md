# Faithful four-point action of GL2 over F2

↑ **Parent:** [General linear group over a finite field](general-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md)

The [general linear group over a finite field](general-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md) $GL_2(\mathbb F_2)$ acts faithfully on the four [vectors](vector.md) of $\mathbb F_2^2$, since a matrix fixing all vectors fixes the standard basis. The zero vector is fixed by every element; the induced action on the three nonzero vectors identifies this six-element group with $S_3$. Swapping the two coordinates fixes $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$ and exchanges the other two vectors, so its four-point [permutation](permutation.md) is odd. This action therefore embeds the group into $S_4$ with nontrivial composite [sign homomorphism](sign-homomorphism.md).
