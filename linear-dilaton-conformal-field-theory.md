# Linear dilaton conformal field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Free boson conformal field theory](free-boson-conformal-field-theory.md)

A linear dilaton conformal field theory improves the free-boson stress tensor by a second derivative, $T=-\alpha'^{-1}:\!\partial X\partial X\!:-q\partial^2X$. The improvement shifts the [central charge](central-charge.md) to $c=1+6\alpha'q^2$ and corresponds to a dilaton linear in the target coordinate $X$.
