# Molecular orbital correlation diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital theory](molecular-orbital-theory.md)

A diagram linking reactant and product orbitals by a [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) conserved along a stipulated reaction path. Orbitals of the same [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) avoid crossing in a generic one-parameter problem; orbitals of different [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) can cross. Correlation constrains connectivity, not quantitative barrier heights.
