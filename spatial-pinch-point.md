# Spatial pinch point

↑ **Parent:** [Briggs-Bers criterion](briggs-bers-criterion.md)

A collision of spatial branches which pinches the spatial inversion contour as the temporal contour is lowered from above all singularities. The branches must originate on opposite sides of the spatial contour. A collision of two upper-half-plane branches or two lower-half-plane branches can solve the double-root equations without causing [absolute wave-packet instability](absolute-wave-packet-instability.md).
