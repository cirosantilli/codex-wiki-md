# Spot interest rate

↑ **Parent:** [Interest rate](interest-rate.md)

The time-$t$ one-period spot interest rate is the rate available for investment from $t$ to $t+1$. For a unit-face-value [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md),

$$
1+r_t=\frac1{P_t^{t+1}}.
$$
