# Beta-hydride elimination

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

[Beta-hydride elimination](beta-hydride-elimination.md) transfers hydrogen from the beta [carbon](carbon.md) of a [metal](metal.md)-bound alkyl to the [metal](metal.md) while forming an [alkene](alkene.md). It can release a growing [polymer](polymer-split.md) chain and compete with [migratory insertion](migratory-insertion.md). Its accessibility depends on geometry, available sites and bond energetics.
