# Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

Awarded to [Albert Einstein](albert-einstein.md) for theoretical physics, with particular recognition of the law of the [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md). He received the award in 1922.[Prize motivation and delayed presentation](albert-einstein-facts-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)

The [Millikan photoelectric experiment](millikan-photoelectric-experiment.md) makes the stopping-voltage test and its experimental limitations explicit.
