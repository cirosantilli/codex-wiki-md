# Fano point-line stabilizer intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Fano plane](fano-plane.md)

There are 21 incident point-line pairs and 28 nonincident pairs in the [Fano plane](fano-plane.md), with $GL_3(2)$ transitive on each type. [Orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives the two orders as $168/21=8$ and $168/28=6$.
