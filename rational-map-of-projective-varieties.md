# Rational map of projective varieties

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)

A rational map is a morphism on a dense open subset, considered up to agreement on a smaller dense open subset.

It is the projective-source-and-target case of a [rational map of algebraic varieties](rational-map-of-algebraic-varieties.md).
