# Lecture Notes on General Relativity by Sean M. Carroll

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Sean M. Carroll](work-by-sean-m-carroll.md)

Author's graduate lecture notes, arXiv:gr-qc/9712019, 1997. Section 2 develops [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md), [covectors](covector.md) and the failure of ordinary [tensor](tensor.md) derivatives under nonlinear coordinate changes; section 3 constructs covariant differentiation, [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) and [curvature](curvature.md). Metric and [curvature](curvature.md) signs must be translated when combining conventions.
