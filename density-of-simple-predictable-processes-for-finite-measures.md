# Density of simple predictable processes for finite measures

↑ **Parent:** [Simple predictable process](simple-predictable-process.md)

For every [finite measure](finite-measure.md) $\mu$ on the [predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md), bounded [simple predictable processes](simple-predictable-process.md) are dense in $L^2(\mu)$. Include a bounded $\mathcal F_0$-measurable value supported at time zero when $\mu$ may charge that slice. Indicators of the generating rectangles and the [Pi-lambda theorem](pi-lambda-theorem.md) prove density. This gives the completion step in the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) construction.
