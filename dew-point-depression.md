# Dew-point depression

↑ **Parent:** [Dew point](dew-point.md)

The difference between air [temperature](temperature-split.md) and its [dew point](dew-point.md). For unsaturated air it is positive. Near terrestrial surface conditions, a closed ascending parcel's [lifting condensation level](lifting-condensation-level.md) is approximately $125\,\mathrm{m\,K^{-1}}$ times this difference, because its [temperature](temperature-split.md) decreases about $8\,\mathrm{K\,km^{-1}}$ faster than its [dew point](dew-point.md).
