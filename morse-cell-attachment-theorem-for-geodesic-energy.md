# Morse cell-attachment theorem for geodesic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Morse theory](morse-theory.md)

On a complete compact [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), nondegenerate critical points of fixed-endpoint [geodesic energy](energy-of-a-curve.md) yield a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) model of the [path space](path-space.md), with one cell per geodesic and dimension equal to its [Morse index](morse-index.md). Finite-dimensional broken-geodesic models prove the assertion at bounded energy, and exhaustion gives the full path space. On $S^n$ with distinct nonantipodal endpoints the cell dimensions are $j(n-1)$, one in each such dimension.
