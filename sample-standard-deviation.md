# Sample standard deviation

↑ **Parent:** [Sample variance](sample-variance.md)

The sample standard deviation is the nonnegative square root of the usual [sample variance](sample-variance.md) for $n\ge2$. The displayed convention uses divisor $n-1$. Although the [sample variance](sample-variance.md) is an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of the population [variance](variance-split.md) under independent identically distributed sampling with finite [variance](variance-split.md), taking its square root does not in general give an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of the population [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md).
