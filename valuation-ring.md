# Valuation ring

↑ **Parent:** [Local ring](local-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valuation_ring)

A valuation ring is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $R$ such that, for every nonzero element $x$ of its [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) $K$, either $x\in R$ or $x^{-1}\in R$. Equivalently, its ideals are totally ordered by inclusion.
