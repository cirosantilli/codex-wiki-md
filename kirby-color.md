# Kirby color

↑ **Parent:** [Colored link](colored-link.md)

The Kirby color is the weighted sum of annular closures of the surviving [Jones-Wenzl idempotents](jones-wenzl-idempotent.md) at a root of unity. Its weights are their closed bracket traces. The [fusion of Jones-Wenzl colors](fusion-of-jones-wenzl-colors.md) gives $a\Omega=d_a\Omega$ in the annular fusion ring. Inserting the corresponding fusion resolutions along a band gives the [Kirby color handle-slide identity](kirby-color-handle-slide-identity.md), the central ingredient of surgery invariance.
