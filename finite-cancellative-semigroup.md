# Finite cancellative semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Semigroup](semigroup.md)

A nonempty [finite set](finite-set.md) with an [associative](associative-property.md) [binary operation](binary-operation.md) satisfying both [cancellation laws](cancellation-law.md) is a [group](group-split.md). For fixed $a$, cancellation makes $x\mapsto ax$ and $x\mapsto xa$ [injective](injective-function.md), hence [surjective](surjective-function.md). Choose $e$ with $ae=a$. Then $a(ex)=ax$ gives $ex=x$, and $(xe)x=xx$ gives $xe=x$. Choose $ab=e=ca$; [associativity](associative-property.md) gives $c=c(ab)=(ca)b=b$, so $a$ has a two-sided [inverse element](inverse-element.md).
