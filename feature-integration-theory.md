# Feature integration theory

↑ **Parent:** [Attention](attention.md)

A theory proposing early registration of separable visual features and a role for focused [attention](attention.md) in correctly binding their conjunctions into objects. Visual-search slopes and binding errors are complementary tests, not interchangeable proofs of one serial architecture. The original model already recognized possible top-down constraints on unattended feature combinations.[Cognitive Psychology 12, pp. 97–136](a-feature-integration-theory-of-attention-by-anne-treisman-and-others.md)
