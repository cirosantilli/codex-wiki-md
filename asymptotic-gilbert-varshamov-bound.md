<h1 id="asymptotic-gilbert-varshamov-bound">Asymptotic Gilbert–Varshamov bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gilbert–Varshamov bound](gilbert-varshamov-bound.md)

For $0\le\delta\le1-1/q$, take logarithms of the [Gilbert–Varshamov bound](gilbert-varshamov-bound.md) and apply the [Hamming ball volume exponent](hamming-ball-volume-exponent.md). The [Varshamov bound for linear codes](varshamov-bound-for-linear-codes.md) gives the same achievable lower bound within linear codes for prime-power $q$.
