# Umbilical point

↑ **Parent:** [Principal curvature](principal-curvature.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umbilical_point)

An umbilical point of a surface is a point where the two [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) are equal, equivalently where the [shape operator](shape-operator.md) is a scalar multiple of the identity.
