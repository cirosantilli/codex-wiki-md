# Fibre of absolute Frobenius over a rational point of the affine line

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme-theoretic fiber](scheme-theoretic-fiber.md)

Over a [perfect field](perfect-field.md) $k$ of characteristic $p$, the [scheme-theoretic fiber](scheme-theoretic-fiber.md) of the [Absolute Frobenius morphism](absolute-frobenius-morphism.md) of $\mathbb A_k^1$ over a rational point $a$ is

$$
\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(k[t]\otimes_{k[t],\,t\mapsto t^p}k\bigr)
\cong\operatorname{Spec}k[t]/(t-a)^p.
$$

Its underlying space is one point, but its coordinate ring has a nonzero [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md), so the fibre is a length-$p$ [nonreduced scheme](nonreduced-scheme.md).
