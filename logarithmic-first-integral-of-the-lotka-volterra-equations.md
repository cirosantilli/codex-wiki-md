# Logarithmic first integral of the Lotka-Volterra equations

↑ **Parent:** [Lotka-Volterra equations](lotka-volterra-equations.md)

For the normalized equations $\dot x=x(1-y)$ and $\dot y=ry(x-1)$ on the [positive quadrant](positive-quadrant.md),

$$
V(x,y)=r(x-\log x-1)+y-\log y-1
$$

is a [first integral](first-integral.md). It is nonnegative, [strictly convex](strictly-convex-function.md), and tends to infinity at the boundary and at infinity. Every positive regular level is therefore a compact simple closed curve traversed by one [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md).
