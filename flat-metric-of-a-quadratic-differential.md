# Flat metric of a quadratic differential

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic differential](quadratic-differential.md)

For $q=\phi(z)\,dz^2$, the length element is $|\phi(z)|^{1/2}|dz|$, locally Euclidean in [flat coordinates](natural-coordinate-of-a-holomorphic-differential.md). Zeros give cone points, not punctures. Its infimal length in a [homotopy class](homotopy-class.md) need not determine the hyperbolic length of that class.
