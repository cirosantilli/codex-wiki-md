# Potential-vorticity generation by heating and friction

↑ **Parent:** [Dry atmospheric Ertel potential vorticity](dry-atmospheric-ertel-potential-vorticity.md)

For a composition-fixed equation of state, let $\dot\theta=D\theta/Dt$ and let $\mathbf F$ be nonconservative force per unit mass. The vorticity and scalar-gradient evolution equations give the source terms in the title. Spatially varying heating projected along absolute vorticity and the curl of friction can generate or destroy [Ertel potential vorticity](ertel-potential-vorticity.md). Heating that changes potential temperature does not automatically change PV everywhere: its spatial gradient and orientation matter. Unresolved irreversible mixing can also alter the parcelwise conservation picture.
