# Zassenhaus butterfly lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Subnormal series](subnormal-series.md)

Let $A'\trianglelefteq A$ and $B'\trianglelefteq B$ be [subgroups](subgroup.md) of one [group](group-split.md). Put $C=A\cap B$ and $D=(A'\cap B)(A\cap B')$. Then $D\trianglelefteq C$ and the [first isomorphism theorem](first-isomorphism-theorem.md) identifies both

$$
\frac{A'C}{A'(A\cap B')}\quad\text{and}\quad\frac{B'C}{B'(B\cap A')}
$$

with $C/D$. Indeed $C$ maps onto either displayed [quotient group](quotient-group.md), and its kernel in either case is $D$. This gives the matching factors used in the [Schreier refinement theorem](schreier-refinement-theorem.md).
