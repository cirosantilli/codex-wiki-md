# Lessons in Elementary Chemistry by Henry Enfield Roscoe

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry bibliography](chemistry-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Henry Enfield Roscoe](work-by-henry-enfield-roscoe.md)

Textbook by [Henry Enfield Roscoe](henry-enfield-roscoe.md). The inspected 1868 printing, Lesson XV, printed pp. 131–133, describes the bone-ash and acid-phosphate route to [phosphorus](phosphorus.md). Its printing date is retained, and its account is not identified with a pharmacopoeial formula or claimed to establish every detail of an 1866 official process.

[https://darwin-online.org.uk/converted/pdf/1868_Roscoe_elementary_chemistry_A5727.pdf](https://darwin-online.org.uk/converted/pdf/1868_Roscoe_elementary_chemistry_A5727.pdf)

The 1866 first publication is separately recorded in the publisher-facing edition record: [Other editions lists the 1866 edition](https://books.google.com/books/about/Lessons_in_elementary_chemistry.html?id=8QcFAAAAQAAJ).
