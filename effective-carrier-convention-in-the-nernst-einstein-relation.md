# Effective carrier convention in the Nernst-Einstein relation

↑ **Parent:** [Nernst-Einstein relation](nernst-einstein-relation.md)

The [density](density.md) and diffusivity in $\sigma=nq^2D/(k_BT)$ must refer to the same carrier species and correlation convention. [Oxygen vacancy](oxygen-vacancy.md) diffusivity belongs with vacancy [density](density.md); [tracer diffusion](tracer-diffusion.md) of oxygen atoms is not automatically the vacancy diffusivity. A transport inversion may determine an effective [density](density.md) without uniquely determining a chemical defect concentration.
