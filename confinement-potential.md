# Confinement potential

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal transport](optimal-transport.md)

A [function](function-split.md) describing the energy of placing a particle at a position in a fixed external field. Its associated [confinement energy](confinement-energy.md) is linear in the [probability measure](probability-measure.md) representing the particle distribution. A common example is $V(x)=|x|^2/2$.
