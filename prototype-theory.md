# Prototype theory

↑ **Parent:** [Categorization](categorization.md)

An account in which category judgments rely on similarity to an abstract or representative summary. It naturally explains centrality and generalization effects, but must be distinguished from [exemplar theory](exemplar-theory.md) and from a descriptive typicality gradient.
