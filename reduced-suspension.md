# Reduced suspension

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy](homotopy.md)

The based suspension collapses both ends and the basepoint cylinder of $X\times I$ to one basepoint. The [loop space](loop-space.md) and reduced suspension are adjoint in based [homotopy](homotopy.md) theory. For a connected based [CW complex](cw-complex.md), the [James reduced product](james-reduced-product.md) models the loop space of its reduced suspension.
