# Class number one for discriminant minus sixty-seven

↑ **Parent:** [Enumeration of reduced binary quadratic forms](enumeration-of-reduced-binary-quadratic-forms.md)

For negative discriminant $-67$, a [reduced positive definite binary quadratic form](reduced-positive-definite-binary-quadratic-form.md) satisfies $1\le a\le\sqrt{67/3}<5$ and $b$ odd with $|b|\le a$. Checking $4a\mid b^2+67$ leaves only $(a,b,c)=(1,1,17)$ under the boundary convention. The [reduction algorithm for a positive definite binary quadratic form](reduction-algorithm-for-a-positive-definite-binary-quadratic-form.md) therefore puts every form in this one [proper equivalence of binary quadratic forms](proper-equivalence-of-binary-quadratic-forms.md) class. Its unimodular shears give infinitely many distinct forms $x^2+(2n+1)xy+(n^2+n+17)y^2$.
