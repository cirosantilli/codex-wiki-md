# Exchange option

↑ **Parent:** [European contingent claim](european-contingent-claim.md)

An exchange option permits delivery of one asset in exchange for another at maturity, producing the positive-part payoff $(S_1(T)-S_2(T))^+$. In a complete finite market its price is the discounted [expected value](expected-value-split.md) under the unique [risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md). For a two-period joint binomial model, evaluating this payoff at all four terminal paths and weighting by their risk-neutral path probabilities gives its price directly.
