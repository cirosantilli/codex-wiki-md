# Killing vector preserves scalar curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Second covariant derivative of a Killing vector](second-covariant-derivative-of-a-killing-vector.md)

Every [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) $K$ preserves the [scalar curvature](scalar-curvature.md): $\mathcal L_KR=K^\alpha\nabla_\alpha R=0$. One index proof rewrites this derivative as a doubly contracted curvature commutator acting on the antisymmetric tensor $\nabla_{[\mu}K_{\lambda]}$; both terms vanish by symmetry of the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md).
