# Isospin ladder strength in analogue beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi transition](fermi-transition.md)

The [Fermi transition](fermi-transition.md) operator is an [isospin](isospin.md) ladder operator. Acting between normalized members of one [isospin multiplet](isospin-multiplet.md) gives the displayed squared [matrix element](matrix-element.md). An edge transition in a $T=3/2$ multiplet has Fermi strength three, whereas the transition between the two $T=1/2$ mirror members has strength one. This fixes the vector contribution; nonzero-spin analogue transitions can also have a [Gamow-Teller transition](gamow-teller-transition.md) contribution that is not fixed by this counting.
