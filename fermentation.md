# Fermentation

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolism](metabolism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermentation)

In its biochemical sense, [fermentation](fermentation.md) is [metabolism](metabolism.md) that obtains [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) from breakdown of organic [substrates](substrate-biochemistry.md) without a respiratory electron transport chain. The alcoholic route is one example; not every [fermentation](fermentation.md) produces [ethanol](ethanol.md). See [Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e, 7.5: fermentation versus anaerobic respiration](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/7-5-metabolism-without-oxygen).
