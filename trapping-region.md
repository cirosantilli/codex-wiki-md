# Trapping region

↑ **Parent:** [Positively invariant set](positively-invariant-set.md)

A trapping region is a compact region across whose boundary the vector field points inward. It is positively invariant and confines every forward trajectory that enters it.

This is a dynamical example of an [invariant](invariant-mathematics.md); forward invariance and strict inward trapping are additional conditions.
