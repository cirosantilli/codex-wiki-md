# Line fraction estimate of phase volume

↑ **Parent:** [Microstructure](microstructure.md)

Estimating a phase [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) from the proportion of a representative line assigned to that phase. Sampling, orientation and phase identification must be appropriate. The width of a height feature on a differentially etched surface need not equal the phase intersection on the original flat section.
