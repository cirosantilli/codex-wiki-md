<h1 id="alfven-frequency">Alfvén frequency</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md)

The Alfvén frequency is the frequency of an [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) with [wavevector](wavevector.md) $\mathbf k$ in a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) $\mathbf B$ and [mass density](density.md) $\rho$. For a vertical field and vertical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$, its square is $\omega_a^2=k^2B_z^2/(\mu_0\rho)$. Its competition with orbital shear determines the [ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation](ideal-magnetorotational-dispersion-relation.md).
