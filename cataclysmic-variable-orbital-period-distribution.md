# Cataclysmic-variable orbital-period distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Cataclysmic variable](cataclysmic-variable.md)

Ordinary hydrogen-rich [cataclysmic variables](cataclysmic-variable.md) occur on both sides of a [cataclysmic-variable period gap](cataclysmic-variable-period-gap.md) at approximately two to three hours, with a [cataclysmic-variable period minimum](cataclysmic-variable-period-minimum.md) near 82 minutes. Selection effects change the observed proportions. [Knigge, Baraffe and Patterson's donor-based evolutionary study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.2440) summarizes these empirical landmarks.
