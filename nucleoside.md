# Nucleoside

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleoside)

A [nucleoside](nucleoside.md) combines a [nucleobase](nucleotide-base.md) with a [sugar](sugar.md), without the [phosphate](phosphate.md) groups of a [nucleotide](nucleotide.md). [Adenosine](adenosine.md) is the [nucleoside](nucleoside.md) built from [adenine](adenine.md) and [ribose](ribose.md). See [Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell: nucleosides and nucleotides](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26883/).
