# Fiducial volume

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

A selected inner detector volume in which the response and background rejection meet specified criteria. Position cuts can suppress surface backgrounds, but the selected target [mass](mass.md) and position-reconstruction uncertainty must be calibrated.
