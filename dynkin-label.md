# Dynkin label

↑ **Parent:** [Weight lattice](weight-lattice.md)

The Dynkin labels of a weight $\lambda$ are its simple-coroot coordinates $\lambda^i=\langle\lambda,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle$. Thus $\lambda=\sum_i\lambda^i\omega_i$ in the basis of [fundamental weights](fundamental-weight.md).
