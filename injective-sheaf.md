# Injective sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of abelian groups](sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md)

A sheaf of abelian groups is injective if every morphism to it from a subsheaf extends to the containing sheaf. Every such sheaf embeds in an injective sheaf. Injective sheaves are [flasque](flasque-sheaf.md): the inclusion of [extension by zero](extension-by-zero.md) constant-integer sheaves associated to $U\subseteq V$ turns the extension property into surjectivity of restriction from $V$ to $U$.
