# Transmission electron microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transmission_electron_microscopy)

[Transmission electron microscopy](transmission-electron-microscopy.md) images [electrons](electron.md) transmitted through a thin specimen. [Electron diffraction](electron-diffraction.md) from a selected region supplies complementary information about its atomic arrangement. A useful transmitted signal requires suitable specimen thickness.[JEOL, Another characteristic electron diffraction: thin specimens, images and diffraction patterns](transmission-electron-microscope-tem-by-jeol.md)

The image also depends on which outgoing [electron](electron.md) directions reach the detector. [Transmission electron microscopy contrast](transmission-electron-microscopy-contrast.md) makes this selection explicit: changing the [objective aperture](objective-aperture.md) can change an image without changing the specimen. Interpreting the result requires [electron diffraction](electron-diffraction.md), a specified detector response and [measurement calibration](measurement-calibration.md).
