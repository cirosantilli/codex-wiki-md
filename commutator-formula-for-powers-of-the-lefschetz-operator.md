# Commutator formula for powers of the Lefschetz operator

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz commutator](lefschetz-commutator.md)

For the [Hermitian Lefschetz operator](lefschetz-operator-on-a-hermitian-manifold.md) on a complex $n$-manifold, the [Lefschetz commutator](lefschetz-commutator.md) and the [commutator derivation identity](commutator-derivation-identity.md) imply this formula. When expanding a product commutator, one must evaluate $[L,\Lambda]$ on the degree after the powers of $L$ to its right have acted.
