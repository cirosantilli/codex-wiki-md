<h1 id="lubin-tate-tower">Lubin–Tate tower</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lubin–Tate extension](lubin-tate-extension.md)

Choose compatible primitive [Lubin–Tate torsion](lubin-tate-torsion.md) points with $[\pi]\lambda_{n+1}=\lambda_n$. Restriction of the [Lubin–Tate Galois action on primitive torsion](lubin-tate-galois-action-on-primitive-torsion.md) is reduction of [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) classes modulo $\pi^n$. The [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Gal}(K_\pi/K)\simeq\varprojlim_n(\mathcal O_K/\pi^n)^{\times}\simeq\mathcal O_K^{\times}
$$

as topological groups. This describes the [Galois group](galois-group.md) without invoking local reciprocity.
