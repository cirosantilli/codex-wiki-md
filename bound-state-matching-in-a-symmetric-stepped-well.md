# Bound-state matching in a symmetric stepped well

↑ **Parent:** [Wavefunction matching at a finite potential step](wavefunction-matching-at-a-finite-potential-step.md)

In an even, piecewise constant potential, integrate outward from the centre with either even [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) $\psi'(0)=0$ or odd [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) $\psi(0)=0$. Match the [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) and its [derivative](derivative.md) at every finite step and impose [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) outside. The resulting discrete [energies](energy-split.md) alternate in [parity](parity.md), with the fourth [bound state](bound-state.md) odd and containing three [nodes of a wavefunction](node-of-a-wavefunction.md). There is no hard-wall zero at a finite step; the second [derivative](derivative.md) changes according to $\psi''=2m(V-E)\psi/\hbar^2$.
