# Integrated squared density curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Second derivative kernel density estimator](second-derivative-kernel-density-estimator.md)

The integrated squared second derivative measures a smooth density's curvature in L2. It supplies the leading integrated squared bias constant of a second-order [kernel density estimator](kernel-density-estimation.md). For nonzero second moment $\mu_2(K)$, the [asymptotic mean integrated squared error](asymptotic-mean-integrated-squared-error.md) has bias term $h^4\mu_2(K)^2R(f'')/4$, so greater curvature calls for a smaller [smoothing bandwidth](smoothing-bandwidth.md).
