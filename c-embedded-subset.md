# C-embedded subset

↑ **Parent:** [Topological embedding](topological-embedding.md)

A [subset](subset.md) $Y\subseteq X$ is [C-embedded](c-embedded-subset.md) if every [continuous](continuous-function.md) [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) on its [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md) extends continuously to $X$. [Closed subsets](closed-set.md) of [normal spaces](normal-space.md) have this property by the [Tietze extension theorem](tietze-extension-theorem.md). The condition also makes sense for nonclosed [subsets](subset.md).
