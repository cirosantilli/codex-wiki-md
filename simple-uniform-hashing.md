# Simple uniform hashing

↑ **Parent:** [Separate chaining](separate-chaining.md)

A probabilistic model assigning distinct keys independent bucket indices with a [uniform distribution on a finite set](discrete-uniform-distribution.md). It supplies an [expected value](expected-value-split.md) bound for [separate chaining](separate-chaining.md), not a guarantee for every deterministic [hash function](hash-function.md) or workload.
