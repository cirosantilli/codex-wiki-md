# Complementary DNA

↑ **Parent:** [Reverse transcription](reverse-transcription.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complementary_DNA)

[Complementary DNA](complementary-dna.md) is [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) made from an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) template by [reverse transcription](reverse-transcription.md). When its template is mature [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md), its sequence reflects the processed transcript rather than every intervening [intron](intron.md) in the corresponding [gene](gene.md).
