# John von Neumann

↑ **Parent:** [List of mathematicians](list-of-mathematicians.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_von_Neumann)

A [mathematician](mathematician.md) whose work includes the [minimax theorem](minimax-theorem.md) and, with [Oskar Morgenstern](oskar-morgenstern.md), the foundations of [expected utility](expected-utility.md) and [game theory](game-theory-split.md).
