# Product manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md)

Products of [manifold charts](manifold-chart.md) form a [smooth atlas](smooth-atlas.md) on the topological product of two [smooth manifolds](smooth-manifold.md). The transition maps are products of the two smooth transition maps, so the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is the sum of the dimensions. The smooth structure is independent of the chosen compatible atlases.
