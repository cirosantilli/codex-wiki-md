# Primitive central idempotent

↑ **Parent:** [Central idempotent](central-idempotent.md)

A nonzero [central idempotent](central-idempotent.md) is primitive central if it is not a sum of two nonzero orthogonal [central idempotents](central-idempotent.md). For an [Artinian ring](artinian-ring.md), it determines a [block of an Artinian algebra](block-of-an-artinian-algebra.md). This differs from a [primitive idempotent](primitive-idempotent.md) in the whole [ring](ring.md): for a [matrix algebra](matrix-algebra.md) of size greater than one over a [field](field.md), $1$ is primitive central but is a sum of diagonal [idempotents](idempotent.md).
