<h1 id="su-2-matrix">SU(2) matrix</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(2) group](su-2-group.md)

An SU(2) matrix is a two-by-two complex [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) with [determinant](determinant.md) one. It has the form $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\-\bar b&\bar a\end{pmatrix}$ with $|a|^2+|b|^2=1$. Its action on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) is a rotational [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md); opposite matrices induce the same sphere rotation.
