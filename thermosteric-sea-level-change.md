# Thermosteric sea level change

↑ **Parent:** [Steric sea level change](steric-sea-level-change.md)

Thermosteric sea level change is the [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md) part of [steric sea level change](steric-sea-level-change.md). For a uniform layer of depth $H$, [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) gives $\rho H=(\rho+\Delta\rho)(H+\Delta\eta)$, hence $\Delta\eta\simeq-H\Delta\rho/\rho=H\alpha\Delta T$.
