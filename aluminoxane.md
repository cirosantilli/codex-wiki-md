# Aluminoxane

↑ **Parent:** [Organoaluminium compound](organoaluminium-compound.md)

An [aluminium](aluminium.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md) compound containing Al–O–Al connectivity, often represented compositionally as $\mathrm{(RAlO)_n}$. Electron-deficient [aluminium](aluminium.md) can achieve four-coordinate environments through cage assembly and multiply bridging [oxygen](oxygen.md).
