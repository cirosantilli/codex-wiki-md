# Supercritical Hopf bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md)

A stable small [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) emerges on the side where the equilibrium has become unstable. In the displayed radial [Hopf normal form](hopf-normal-form.md), its radius is $r=\sqrt\mu$ for $\mu>0$. Nondegeneracy requires a transverse imaginary-eigenvalue crossing and a nonzero negative cubic radial coefficient.
