↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green's_identities)

Green's identities relate volume integrals involving gradients and Laplacians to boundary values and normal derivatives. They follow from the divergence theorem and include [Green's first identity](green-s-first-identity.md), [Green second identity](green-second-identity.md) and [Green's third identity](green-s-third-identity.md).
