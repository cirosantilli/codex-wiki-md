# Coincident-point bunching factors

↑ **Parent:** [Normally ordered density correlation](normally-ordered-density-correlation.md)

A single-component chaotic Gaussian bosonic field has normalized coincident normally ordered correlation $n!$: every pairing contributes the same product of densities. A same-component fermionic field instead has zero coincident correlations for $n\geq2$ by exclusion. These are state-dependent statements: a coherent bosonic field has factor one, and a single-mode [number state](number-state.md) has $N^{\underline n}/N^n$. Ratios to distant unnormalized correlations additionally require the compared densities to agree and distant correlations to factorize.
