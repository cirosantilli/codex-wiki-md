# Past exam of the MPhil in nuclear energy course of the University of Cambridge

↑ **Parent:** [MPhil in nuclear energy course of the University of Cambridge](../mphil-in-nuclear-energy-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge.md)

The 2019 and 2021 NE2 papers match Engineering 4I10 papers with public worked cribs: [2019 crib](https://teaching.eng.cam.ac.uk/system/files/Crib_4I10_2019.pdf) and [2021 crib](https://teaching.eng.cam.ac.uk/system/files/4I10_Crib_2021.pdf). The 2020 NE1 cover explicitly crosslists Engineering 4M16; [Engineering advertises student access to examination cribs for all years in its library](https://teaching.eng.cam.ac.uk/content/teaching-opportunities). These three papers are skipped for new solutions. Student-answer availability for the other held papers remains unverified.
