<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Nuclear fuel cladding](../../../../../nuclear-fuel-cladding.md) has to retain the [nuclear fuel](../../../../../nuclear-fuel.md) and its [fission products](../../../../../fission-product.md), transfer heat to the [coolant](../../../../../coolant.md), and preserve a usable mechanical geometry throughout [irradiation](../../../../../irradiation.md). Those functions impose several coupled requirements.

Its [neutron absorption cross section](../../../../../neutron-absorption-cross-section.md) should be small: absorption in a structural tube removes neutrons without producing useful fuel fission. This is a major attraction of [zirconium alloys](../../../../../zirconium-alloys.md) in a thermal-spectrum [nuclear reactor](../../../../../nuclear-reactor.md). Low absorption alone is insufficient, because the material must also tolerate its [coolant](../../../../../coolant.md) and [irradiation](../../../../../irradiation.md) environment.

The tube needs adequate [yield strength](../../../../../yield-strength.md), [fracture toughness](../../../../../fracture-toughness.md) and [ductility](../../../../../ductility.md) to withstand [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) differences, handling, thermal cycling and fuel expansion. Resistance to [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) and [mechanical fatigue](../../../../../mechanical-fatigue.md) limits progressive deformation and cracking. The design must accommodate changes in [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) and contact with the expanding fuel, including [pellet-cladding mechanical interaction](../../../../../pellet-cladding-mechanical-interaction.md).

The coolant-facing surface should resist [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md) and loss of protective oxide integrity. Hydrogen uptake and [hydrogen embrittlement](../../../../../hydrogen-embrittlement.md) matter for [zirconium alloys](../../../../../zirconium-alloys.md); chemical compatibility with the fuel and released [fission products](../../../../../fission-product.md) matters on the inner surface. Resistance to [stress corrosion cracking](../../../../../stress-corrosion-cracking.md) is particularly important when chemical attack and tensile loading act together.

The material should transfer heat without an excessive [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) drop, retain mechanical integrity at the relevant temperatures, and remain dimensionally acceptable under [radiation damage in materials](../../../../../radiation-damage-in-materials.md). [Irradiation growth](../../../../../irradiation-growth.md), [irradiation creep](../../../../../irradiation-creep.md), [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) and [irradiation hardening](../../../../../irradiation-hardening.md) can alter clearance, [stresses](../../../../../stress.md) and [coolant](../../../../../coolant.md) passages even when the unirradiated tube meets its specification.

Finally, the alloy should be manufacturable as thin, reproducible, leak-tight tubing with reliable end joints and inspectable defects. Material selection balances these requirements: increasing strength by alloying, for example, can change [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md), neutron absorption and [irradiation](../../../../../irradiation.md) behavior. A high melting point by itself does not establish suitability.
