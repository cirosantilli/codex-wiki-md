<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Reducing [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) means delaying stable cavity growth and reducing its rate after incubation. Several approaches act on different parts of that mechanism.

Choose an alloy and initial [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md) with favorable [defect sink](../../../../../defect-sink.md) populations. [Grain boundaries](../../../../../grain-boundary.md), stable interfaces, precipitates and a controlled [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) structure can remove mobile defects and promote recombination before they feed voids. The relevant quantity is sink efficiency and bias, rather than the number of visible microstructural features alone.

[Cold working](../../../../../cold-working.md) can increase the starting [dislocation density](../../../../../dislocation-density.md) and prolong incubation in suitable austenitic steels. [Irradiation](../../../../../irradiation.md) and thermal recovery can later change that structure, so an initial benefit need not persist indefinitely. Solute additions and controlled precipitation can also change [vacancy](../../../../../vacancy.md) mobility, defect trapping and cavity nucleation. Their effectiveness must be assessed together with [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md), [ductility](../../../../../ductility.md) and irradiation-assisted changes in composition.

Where other constraints permit, choose a more swelling-resistant material family, such as an appropriate ferritic-martensitic steel, rather than assuming every stainless steel has the response of an austenitic grade. This introduces its own tradeoffs in toughness, [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) window and compatibility.

Manage the operating [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and accumulated dose to avoid the most unfavorable swelling regime, and account for transmutation-gas production. Neither simply minimizing [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) nor adding gas traps guarantees an improved component: the former can increase hardening and embrittlement, and gas-stabilized cavities can become nuclei for later swelling.

Useful comparisons require specimens with controlled composition and heat treatment, measured dose and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) histories, and both dimensional measurements and [transmission electron microscopy](../../../../../transmission-electron-microscopy.md). Matching final dose alone does not establish that two [irradiation](../../../../../irradiation.md) conditions produce the same swelling.
