<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The traditional threefold classification is **[immediate dismantling](../../../../../immediate-dismantling.md), [deferred dismantling](../../../../../deferred-dismantling.md) and [entombment of a nuclear facility](../../../../../entombment-of-a-nuclear-facility.md)**. It appears in the older IAEA WS-R-5 classification.[2006, paragraph 1.5, historical descriptions of the three approaches](../../../../../decommissioning-of-facilities-using-radioactive-material-by-the-international-atomic-energy-agency.md)

With [immediate dismantling](../../../../../immediate-dismantling.md), [decontamination](../../../../../decontamination.md) and removal follow permanent shutdown without a prolonged safe-storage interval. Operating staff, drawings and knowledge remain available, the project can reach its end state sooner, and the site can return to use earlier. Against this, substantial funds and waste-management routes are needed early, and less time has elapsed for short-lived activation products to [radioactive decay](../../../../../radioactive-decay.md). Exposure and remote-handling requirements may therefore be greater for a particular task.

With [deferred dismantling](../../../../../deferred-dismantling.md), the facility is put into a maintained safe condition before later dismantling. [radioactive decay](../../../../../radioactive-decay.md) can reduce some later radiation fields and permit better tools or disposal routes to become available. However, deferral does not remove the waste or responsibility: surveillance, security, building maintenance and preservation of records continue. Deterioration or loss of institutional knowledge can offset the benefit. Long-lived constituents may obtain little useful reduction over a practicable delay.

[entombment of a nuclear facility](../../../../../entombment-of-a-nuclear-facility.md) encases radioactive structures within a long-lived barrier. It can reduce the need for immediate intrusive work in exceptional circumstances, but leaves a persistent in-situ inventory, restricts site use and requires confidence in barriers, groundwater behaviour and long-term institutional arrangements. It transfers management obligations rather than simply completing ordinary unrestricted site release.

There is an important qualification to the historical wording: **GSR Part 6, published in 2014 and therefore already applicable to interpreting a 2021 question, recognises the first two planned strategies and excludes [entombment of a nuclear facility](../../../../../entombment-of-a-nuclear-facility.md) for planned permanent shutdown.** Paragraph 1.10 allows it to be considered only exceptionally, such as after a severe accident. It would therefore be misleading to present all three historical labels as equally acceptable normal planned strategies.[GSR Part 6, paragraphs 1.9–1.10, particularly the explicit entombment qualification](../../../../../decommissioning-of-facilities-by-the-international-atomic-energy-agency.md)
