<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An economic threshold requires all unit costs to have an explicit mass basis. Let $p_U$ be the concentrate price per kilogram of [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md), and let $C_{\rm net}$ denote the annual incremental fabrication-and-disposal cost of the closed cycle relative to the open cycle. The break-even annual cash balance is

$$
p_U(41342.2969)+50(1486.7266)=1000M_{\rm rep}+C_{\rm net},
$$

where $M_{\rm rep}$ is the charged [nuclear fuel reprocessing](../../../../../nuclear-fuel-reprocessing.md) throughput in kilograms of initial heavy metal. Use $M_{\rm rep}=35393.9394$: the stated $33$ tonnes is the remaining [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) inventory, not the whole initial heavy-metal throughput. Thus

$$
\boxed{p_U=\frac{35393939.4+C_{\rm net}-74336.3}{41342.2969} \mathrm{dollars\,kg_U^{-1}}}.
$$

If the quoted combined $400$ dollars per kilogram is a **net additional charge on that same reprocessed-HM basis**, $C_{\rm net}=400M_{\rm rep}$, giving **1,196.77 dollars per kg U**. Under this explicit convention, the saving in purchased separative work is small and the resource saving must pay nearly all the additional [nuclear fuel reprocessing](../../../../../nuclear-fuel-reprocessing.md) cost.

The wording does not identify the mass basis or counterfactual sign of the combined recycled-fuel and spent-fuel-disposal term. If instead $400$ dollars is a net charge per kilogram of the $4469.4375$ kg recycled product, the same balance gives **897.56 dollars per kg U**. If it denotes a net avoided charge of $400$ dollars per kilogram of discharged HM, the threshold is **511.87 dollars per kg U**. These are alternative accounting conventions, not three simultaneous economic thresholds; the general balance makes the ambiguity visible.

All prices here are per [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) mass, not per gross concentrate mass. This simplified comparison assigns no [plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md) credit, financing, conversion-cost difference or change in repository liability, and takes the quoted charges as incremental. A closed fuel cycle can save natural [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) without being the cheaper option: at a price below the threshold under the chosen cost convention, the additional charges exceed the resource credit. No contemporary uranium-market price is needed for that conclusion.
