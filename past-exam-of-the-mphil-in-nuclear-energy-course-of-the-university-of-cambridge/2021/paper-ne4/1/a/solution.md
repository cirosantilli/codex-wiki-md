<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The distinction is between **partitioning reusable fuel constituents from fission-product waste**, and subsequently **partitioning [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) from [plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md)**. In the first operation, chemical selectivity transfers selected [actinides](../../../../../actinide.md) into a different stream while most [fission products](../../../../../fission-product.md) remain in the waste stream. In the second, differences in [chemical speciation](../../../../../chemical-speciation.md) allow the two recovered elements to follow different streams. Neither operation separates [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) [isotopes](../../../../../isotope.md): [uranium enrichment](../../../../../uranium-enrichment.md) and chemical element separation are different tasks.

At a conceptual level, [solvent extraction](../../../../../solvent-extraction.md) exploits unequal affinities of dissolved species for immiscible liquid phases. [complexation](../../../../../complexation.md) changes those affinities, while a change in [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) can change an element's preferred phase without changing its identity. This explains why a stream bearing [actinides](../../../../../actinide.md) can first be separated from much of its accompanying waste, and why [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) and [plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md) need not subsequently remain together. Dissolved and undissolved residues, contaminated solvents and off-gases remain waste-management obligations; a recovery percentage alone is not a complete material balance.

Equipment must provide phase contact and phase disengagement within shielded, confined systems. Remotely maintained contactors and settlers are equipment categories serving those functions; sealed transfer systems, shielded cells and monitored off-gas handling limit contamination and exposure. Cooling manages residual [decay heat](../../../../../decay-heat.md), and [nuclear material accountancy](../../../../../nuclear-material-accountancy.md) follows inventories and losses. [criticality control in radioactive waste](../../../../../criticality-control-in-radioactive-waste.md) is essential because [solvent extraction](../../../../../solvent-extraction.md) can redistribute fissile constituents. **Scoped limitation:** this answer gives the chemical principles and equipment functions; operational fissile-separation recipes, reagent sequences, contactor design details and process conditions are intentionally not supplied.
