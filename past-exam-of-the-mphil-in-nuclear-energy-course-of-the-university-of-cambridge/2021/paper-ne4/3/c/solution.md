<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**[gadolinium](../../../../../gadolinium.md) is primarily a strong [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) absorber.** The supplied thermal cross section for [gadolinium-157](../../../../../gadolinium-157.md) is very large, so capture removes [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md) that could otherwise sustain [nuclear fission](../../../../../nuclear-fission.md). This is a criticality-control role rather than a requirement to complete the host formula. The isotope-specific number must not be assigned unchanged to natural [gadolinium](../../../../../gadolinium.md).

**[hafnium](../../../../../hafnium.md) supplies an additional [neutron poison](../../../../../neutron-poison.md) with favourable retention chemistry.** Its supplied natural-abundance thermal cross section is much smaller than that of [gadolinium-157](../../../../../gadolinium-157.md) but still appreciable. Chemically, tetravalent [hafnium](../../../../../hafnium.md) resembles [zirconium](../../../../../zirconium.md) and can remain associated with a durable titanate host. Keeping the absorber with the fissile inventory during alteration is as important as its initial [neutron capture](../../../../../neutron-capture.md) strength: a highly absorbing constituent offers poor long-term protection if it is preferentially lost. These considerations explain using complementary absorbers rather than ranking them solely by their initial thermal cross sections.

**[uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) addition chiefly addresses isotopic dilution, including the long-term daughter inventory.** [plutonium-239](../../../../../plutonium-239.md) [alpha decay](../../../../../alpha-decay.md) produces [uranium-235](../../../../../uranium-235.md). Added [uranium-238](../../../../../uranium-238.md) lowers the [uranium-235](../../../../../uranium-235.md) fraction of that future [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) inventory; [depleted uranium](../../../../../depleted-uranium.md) adds less [uranium-235](../../../../../uranium-235.md) initially than natural [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md). [uranium-238](../../../../../uranium-238.md) also absorbs some [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md), but its small listed cross section does not make it a substitute for the strong absorbers. The long-term daughter issue and the retention of [gadolinium](../../../../../gadolinium.md) and [hafnium](../../../../../hafnium.md) are explicit concerns in [ceramic](../../../../../ceramic.md) waste-form development.[Section 2, plutonium-immobilisation ceramic development, uranium-235 ingrowth and neutron-absorber retention](../../../../../immobilisation-of-higher-activity-wastes-from-nuclear-reactor-production-of-99mo-by-m-w-a-stewart-and-others.md)

These are complementary physical purposes, **not proof of [criticality control in radioactive waste](../../../../../criticality-control-in-radioactive-waste.md) from three cross-section values**. [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) energy, isotopic composition, geometry, moderation, absorber distribution and their coupled evolution matter. Incorporation in the same durable waste form helps maintain association, but neither [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) addition nor chemical immobilisation makes deliberate element separation physically impossible.
