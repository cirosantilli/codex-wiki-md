<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The host is **[zirconolite](../../../../../zirconolite.md)**, with ideal end-member composition $\mathrm{CaZrTi_2O_7}$. In the proposed formula, the substitution is formally

$$
\mathrm{Zr^{4+}\longleftrightarrow Pu^{4+}}.
$$

It is an [isovalent substitution](../../../../../isovalent-substitution.md): the mean charge of the mixed [zirconium](../../../../../zirconium.md)/[plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md) site remains $+4$, so

$$
2+\big[0.2(4)+0.8(4)\big]+2(4)=14=7(2).
$$

No compensating oxygen vacancy or aliovalent co-substituent is required by this particular charge account. Suitable [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) is essential; the same formula interpreted with only trivalent [plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md) would not be charge balanced.

In [zirconolite-2M](../../../../../zirconolite-2m.md) the [zirconium](../../../../../zirconium.md) site has an oxygen [coordination number](../../../../../coordination-number.md) of seven. The [ionic radius](../../../../../ionic-radius.md) of [plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md)(IV) is larger than that of [zirconium](../../../../../zirconium.md)(IV), so increasing its occupancy changes local strain and the preferred arrangement of the [cation](../../../../../cation.md) layers. The observed progression in the investigated substitution series was **[zirconolite-2M](../../../../../zirconolite-2m.md) → [zirconolite-4M](../../../../../zirconolite-4m.md) → [pyrochlore structure](../../../../../pyrochlore-structure.md)**, rather than unlimited substitution in one unchanged monoclinic lattice. The primary study found a 2M [solid-solution limit](../../../../../solid-solution-limit.md) near $x=0.15$, a narrow 4M range near $x=0.41$, and a pyrochlore composition at $x=0.60$ in $\mathrm{CaZr}_{1-x}\mathrm{Pu}_x\mathrm{Ti_2O_7}$. These are experimental observations for that series, not universal boundaries independent of preparation and [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md).[MRS Proceedings 663, 259; abstract, seven-coordinate site and reported polytype transitions](../../../../../structural-effect-of-pu-substitutions-on-the-zr-site-in-zirconolite-by-b-d-begg-and-others.md)

Consequently the nominal $x=0.20$ composition identifies a zirconolite-based waste-form design, but **does not by itself prove a pure 2M product**. Diffraction and phase-composition measurements are needed. At larger substitution, the fluorite-related pyrochlore arrangement accommodates a different [cation](../../../../../cation.md) distribution and coordination environment.
