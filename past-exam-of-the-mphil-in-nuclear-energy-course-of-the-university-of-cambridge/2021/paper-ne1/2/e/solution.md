<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

After the short prompt adjustment, the [prompt jump approximation](../../../../../prompt-jump-approximation.md) takes the [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) amplitude to remain nearly in balance with the slowly changing [delayed neutron precursor](../../../../../delayed-neutron-precursor.md) reservoir. For the source-free model,

$$
0\simeq\frac{\rho-\beta}{\Lambda}n+\lambda c,
\qquad n\simeq\frac{\Lambda\lambda}{\beta-\rho}c.
$$

Substitute this quasi-steady relation into the precursor equation:

$$
\frac{dc}{dt}=\frac{\beta\lambda}{\beta-\rho}c-\lambda c
=\frac{\rho\lambda}{\beta-\rho}c.
$$

For constant coefficients,

$$
\boxed{c(t)=c_0\exp\!\left(\frac{\rho\lambda}{\beta-\rho}t\right).}
$$

The approximation is meaningful in the stated delayed-[neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) regime with $\rho<\beta$ and a separated fast transient. The [delayed neutron precursor](../../../../../delayed-neutron-precursor.md) reservoir remains continuous across an idealized instantaneous [reactivity](../../../../../reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md) step; the approximation does not resolve the short prompt adjustment itself.
