<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At equilibrium, precursor production balances precursor decay:

$$
0=\frac\beta\Lambda n_0-\lambda c_0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{c_0}{n_0}=\frac{\beta}{\lambda\Lambda}
=\frac{0.007}{(0.1)(0.5\times10^{-3})}=140.}
$$

The [delayed neutron precursor](../../../../../delayed-neutron-precursor.md) population can exceed the instantaneous [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) population even when the [delayed neutron fraction](../../../../../delayed-neutron-fraction.md) is small: the [delayed neutron precursor](../../../../../delayed-neutron-precursor.md) reservoir persists much longer than an individual prompt-[neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) generation.
