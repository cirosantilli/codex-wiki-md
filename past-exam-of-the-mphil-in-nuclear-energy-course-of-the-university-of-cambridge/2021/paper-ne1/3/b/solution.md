<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the prescribed homogeneous one-group model, number balance is

$$
\frac{\partial n}{\partial t}=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf J+(\eta-1)\Sigma_a\phi+S.
$$

Use steady state, $\partial n/\partial t=0$, and the [diffusion approximation](../../../../../diffusion-approximation.md) $\mathbf J=-D\nabla\phi$. A spatially constant $D$ gives

$$
D\nabla^2\phi+(\eta-1)\Sigma_a\phi+S=0.
$$

For a spherically symmetric scalar field,

$$
\nabla^2\phi=\frac1{r^2}\frac d{dr}\left(r^2\frac{d\phi}{dr}\right).
$$

Dividing by $D$ reproduces the requested radial equation. The assumptions are a steady source, [spherical symmetry](../../../../../spherical-symmetry.md), homogeneous effective coefficients, a single energy-group description and a [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) closure. In particular, replacing the general production term by the supplied $(\eta-1)\Sigma_a\phi$ is a model assumption, not a general identity for heterogeneous reactor materials.
