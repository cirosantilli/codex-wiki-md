<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

[Alpha decay](../../../../../alpha-decay.md) releases energy in charged particles with short stopping ranges in matter, so much of their [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) becomes local [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md). An appropriate [radioisotope thermoelectric generator](../../../../../radioisotope-thermoelectric-generator.md) can use a predictable [radioactive decay](../../../../../radioactive-decay.md) [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) source without requiring sunlight or a sustained [nuclear chain reaction](../../../../../nuclear-chain-reaction.md). Low penetrating photon and [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) output is desirable, but being an alpha emitter does not by itself establish the complete radiation spectrum.

**The isotope's [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md) must suit the mission.** A short [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md) gives high initial specific activity but a rapid decline in useful thermal [power](../../../../../power.md); a much longer one gives a steadier output but less activity per atom. Selection also depends on recoverable decay energy, specific [power](../../../../../power.md), daughter emissions, chemical and thermal stability, compatibility with the [power](../../../../../power.md)-conversion system, availability and cost. Practical containment and radiological acceptability remain separate system-level requirements.

A [thermoelectric generator](../../../../../thermoelectric-generator.md) converts a [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference into electrical output through the [Seebeck effect](../../../../../seebeck-effect.md); not all decay [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) becomes electricity. NASA's operational account describes this heat-to-electricity distinction and the need to match source lifetime to long space missions.[What is an RTG, and the discussion of isotope-selection and mission-duration criteria](../../../../../radioisotope-power-systems-faq-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)

For the assumed three-[half-life](../../../../../half-life.md) mission, the thermal [power](../../../../../power.md) falls by a factor of eight. That large decline is the important disadvantage of this particular [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md) even though the initial specific thermal [power](../../../../../power.md) is high.
