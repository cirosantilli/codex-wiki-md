<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**The lighter product receives the fraction $1/(1+m/M)$ of the two massive products' [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md).** Neglecting photon [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), [conservation of momentum](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives equal and opposite daughter momenta, $\mathbf p_m=-\mathbf p_M$. In the nonrelativistic approximation,

$$
K_m=\frac{p^2}{2m},\qquad K_M=\frac{p^2}{2M},\qquad\frac{K_M}{K_m}=\frac mM.
$$

Hence $K_{\rm products}=K_m(1+m/M)$ and

$$
\boxed{K_m=\frac{K_{\rm products}}{1+m/M}.}
$$

This partitions [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) after any stipulated photon energy has been removed from the available [Q value](../../../../../q-value.md). It does not assign the complete mass-defect energy to the light particle.
