<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

After three [half-lives](../../../../../../half-life.md), $P(T)=P_0/8$. Therefore the minimum initial thermal [power](../../../../../../power.md) is

$$
\boxed{P_0=8(250\ \mathrm W)=2000\ \mathrm W.}
$$

Equality gives the stated terminal [power](../../../../../../power.md) exactly in the idealized [exponential decay](../../../../../../exponential-decay.md) model. The associated duration is $415.2$ days. No thermal-to-electrical efficiency is needed because both specified powers are thermal.
