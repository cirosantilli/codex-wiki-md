**Conceptual scope:** the safe account below describes the functions of [spent nuclear fuel](../../../../../spent-nuclear-fuel.md) management and the chemical basis of partitioning. It does not supply an ordered fissile-separation flowsheet, reagents, process conditions or purification instructions. In particular, the requested operational eight-step separation scheme is outside this answer's scope.

Eight useful functional objectives are: receiving and identifying the [spent nuclear fuel](../../../../../spent-nuclear-fuel.md); managing residual [decay heat](../../../../../decay-heat.md); providing [radioactive material confinement](../../../../../radioactive-material-confinement.md) and [radiation shielding](../../../../../radiation-shielding.md); establishing the chemical and radiological inventory; partitioning reusable constituents from waste at a conceptual level; maintaining accountable control of recovered materials; conditioning solid and liquid wastes; and retaining or treating [radioactive effluents](../../../../../radioactive-effluent.md). These are **management and containment functions**, not a claim that the question specifies one universal eight-stage plant design. Several occur throughout a facility's operation rather than sequentially.

The chemical principle of [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md)/[plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md) partitioning is that an element's [oxidation state](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) and [complexation](../../../../../complexation.md) affect its [chemical speciation](../../../../../chemical-speciation.md), [solubility](../../../../../solubility.md) and relative affinity for immiscible phases. [solvent extraction](../../../../../solvent-extraction.md) can therefore separate elements whose chemistry differs, even though it does not distinguish [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) [isotopes](../../../../../isotope.md) by this chemical selectivity. A recovery operation must account for the accompanying [fission products](../../../../../fission-product.md), contaminated process materials and secondary wastes; the recovered material is only one output. [radioactive material confinement](../../../../../radioactive-material-confinement.md), remote handling and [nuclear material accountancy](../../../../../nuclear-material-accountancy.md) remain necessary because chemical partitioning redistributes both radioactive and fissile inventories.
