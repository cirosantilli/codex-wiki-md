<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**Resource savings do not by themselves establish a financial saving.** The one-recycle case avoids purchasing $34374.04$ kg U in natural feed and $1592.76$ kg-SWU per year. Those are the benefits to compare with the incremental costs, not the whole value of the recovered [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) or the gross avoided fresh-product separative work.

Let $p_U$ be the concentrate price per kilogram of [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md), $p_S$ the price per kg-SWU, and $C_{\rm add}$ the annual net extra cost of recovery, recycle-specific conversion and fabrication, waste management and financing, relative to the once-through alternative. Recycling is cheaper under this simplified boundary only if

$$
\boxed{C_{\rm add}<34374.04\,p_U+1592.76\,p_S}.
$$

Equivalently, if $p_S$ and $C_{\rm add}$ were supplied, the break-even [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) price would be

$$
p_U^*=\frac{C_{\rm add}-1592.76p_S}{34374.04}.
$$

An avoided spent-fuel-disposal charge belongs as a credit in $C_{\rm add}$; a new [radioactive waste conditioning](../../../../../radioactive-waste-conditioning.md) or disposal liability belongs as a cost. They must not both be assigned the same sign simply because each concerns waste.

The natural-uranium saving is appreciable, but the fractional separative-work saving is small. Recovery losses and the [uranium-236](../../../../../uranium-236.md) compensation limit the value of recycling. Additional radiological handling, separate fabrication campaigns, secondary waste streams, capital tied up during storage and recovery, and uncertain future disposal costs can matter. Conversely, a higher natural-uranium price, a lower incremental recovery cost or an independently justified disposal credit can favour reuse. The calculation gives a conditional comparison, **not a uniquely determined economic verdict or market-price threshold**, because no costs are supplied. [plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md) utilisation is a different option and has not been silently credited to this uranium-only recycle.
