<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A parent initially at rest emits an [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) and a recoiling heavy daughter. [momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives equal and opposite momenta, so their nonrelativistic [kinetic energies](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) satisfy

$$
\frac{T_d}{T_\alpha}=\frac{m_\alpha}{M_d},\qquad
T_\alpha=Q\frac{M_d}{M_d+m_\alpha},\qquad
T_d=Q\frac{m_\alpha}{M_d+m_\alpha}.
$$

Here $Q$ is the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) available to this branch; excitation of the daughter reduces it and may be followed by photon emission. For an illustrative $Q=5$ MeV, $m_\alpha=4\,\mathrm u$ and $M_d=236\,\mathrm u$, the energies are **$4.917$ MeV in the [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) and $83.3$ keV in the daughter**. The heavy daughter receives much less energy, but that does not imply much less atomic damage.

The fast [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) loses most of its energy through [electronic stopping](../../../../../electronic-stopping.md): [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md) and [electronic excitation](../../../../../electronic-excitation.md) along a relatively long path. It also undergoes nuclear collisions, particularly towards the end of its range, and can produce [displacement damage](../../../../../displacement-damage.md). The slower, heavy, highly charged recoil couples strongly to surrounding nuclei and generates a compact [displacement cascade](../../../../../displacement-cascade.md). Successive knock-on atoms create [vacancies](../../../../../vacancy.md), [interstitial defects](../../../../../interstitial-defect.md) and clusters. Some [Frenkel pairs](../../../../../frenkel-pair.md) recombine as the cascade relaxes; the initial collision count is therefore not the number of defects that remain.

A quantitative illustration comes from [brannerite](../../../../../brannerite.md) rather than an assumed universal [ceramic](../../../../../ceramic.md). Simulations in the primary natural-mineral study used a $4.5$ MeV [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) and an $80$ keV daughter recoil. The corresponding ranges were about **$13$ micrometres and $23$ nanometres**. At the study's assumed $50$ eV [threshold displacement energy](../../../../../threshold-displacement-energy.md), its tabulated results give roughly $560$–$571$ recoil [atomic displacements](../../../../../atomic-displacement.md) and $91$–$100$ [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) [atomic displacements](../../../../../atomic-displacement.md) per [radioactive decay](../../../../../radioactive-decay.md). The numbers depend strongly on the displacement threshold and material, but the shorter recoil path contains the dominant and much denser [ballistic radiation damage](../../../../../ballistic-radiation-damage.md).[Chemical Geology 291, section 2.3 and Table 5; simulated ranges and threshold-dependent displacement counts](../../../../../chemistry-microstructure-and-alpha-decay-damage-of-natural-brannerite-by-g-r-lumpkin-and-others.md)

In a susceptible [ceramic](../../../../../ceramic.md), cascade cores can accumulate into an [amorphous fraction](../../../../../amorphous-fraction.md); defects and associated relaxation can also change volume, [thermal conductivity](../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md) and mechanical response. [molecular dynamics](../../../../../molecular-dynamics.md) simulations of [zirconolite](../../../../../zirconolite.md) found a strongly disordered core surrounded by more isolated [point defects](../../../../../point-defect.md), supporting this distinction between local cascade damage and an entirely transformed specimen.[Journal of Nuclear Materials 306, 61–72, abstract and cascade-core/point-defect results](../../../../../molecular-dynamics-simulation-of-the-alpha-recoil-nucleus-displacement-cascade-in-zirconolite-by-l-veiller-and-others.md)

The [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) eventually becomes a [helium](../../../../../helium.md) atom after slowing and [charge exchange](../../../../../charge-exchange.md). [helium](../../../../../helium.md) accumulation can have later microstructural effects distinct from the initial knock-on cascade. Material-dependent recombination and [annealing](../../../../../annealing-materials-science.md) limit surviving disorder; a change in [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) or [radiation-induced swelling](../../../../../radiation-induced-swelling.md) is not automatically [radiation-induced amorphization](../../../../../radiation-induced-amorphization.md), and [radiation-induced amorphization](../../../../../radiation-induced-amorphization.md) is not automatically [dissolution](../../../../../dissolution.md) or release of the contained radionuclide.
