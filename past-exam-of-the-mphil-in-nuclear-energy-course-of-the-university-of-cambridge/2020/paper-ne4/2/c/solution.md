<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The assumption is a **leading approximation for concentrated [ballistic radiation damage](../../../../../ballistic-radiation-damage.md)**, not an exact statement that the [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) displaces no atoms. The daughter has only about $m_\alpha/M_d$ times the [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md)'s [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), but it is much slower and loses a much larger fraction of that energy in collisions with target nuclei. Those collisions initiate closely packed recoil cascades. The [alpha particle](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) deposits most of its much larger energy into the electronic system over a path hundreds of times longer, with a less concentrated displacement contribution.

Thus the dense region susceptible to one-event loss of crystalline order can reasonably be associated chiefly with the daughter recoil. This explains why the effective damaged volume in the [direct-impact amorphization model](../../../../../direct-impact-amorphization-model.md) can be estimated or fitted from recoil-dominated cascades. It is the **spatial density and stopping channel of the damage**, not simply which particle carries more energy, that matters.

The approximation has a measurable limit. Turcotte's comparison of actinide-dioxide self-damage and external alpha irradiation found a nonzero alpha contribution, reporting about $19\%$ of total damage in the studied case. Comparison of diffraction-derived lattice changes with internal-reference measurements helps separate real [radiation-induced swelling](../../../../../radiation-induced-swelling.md) from instrument shifts, while the distinction between self-decay and external-alpha irradiation probes the omitted recoil channel.[BNWL-SA-5333, summary and experimental/results discussion; nonzero alpha damage and diffraction measurements](../../../../../alpha-radiation-damage-in-the-actinide-dioxides-by-r-p-turcotte.md)

Consequently “all [ballistic radiation damage](../../../../../ballistic-radiation-damage.md)” is useful as the stated model assumption but should not be promoted to an exact material-independent law. [electronic excitation](../../../../../electronic-excitation.md) can also couple to structural change, and recovery alters what survives. The derivation in part (b) remains valid for an effective one-event volume fitted consistently to the relevant regime; it does not establish that all [ceramics](../../../../../ceramic.md) or spent fuels share the same $V_\alpha$ or the same eventual [amorphous fraction](../../../../../amorphous-fraction.md).
