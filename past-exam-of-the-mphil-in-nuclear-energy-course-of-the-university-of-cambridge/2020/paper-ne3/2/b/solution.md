↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**[Irradiation creep](../../../../../irradiation-creep.md) adds stress-directed, time-dependent strain driven by the irradiation-generated defect population.** It can be appreciable where ordinary thermal [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) would be slow, because [irradiation](../../../../../irradiation.md) maintains mobile [point defects](../../../../../point-defect.md) far above their equilibrium populations.

Absorption or emission of [vacancies](../../../../../vacancy.md) and [self-interstitials](../../../../../self-interstitial.md) permits [dislocation climb](../../../../../dislocation-climb.md). Applied [stress](../../../../../stress.md) can make differently oriented [dislocations](../../../../../dislocation.md) or [dislocation loops](../../../../../dislocation-loop.md) absorb defects at different rates: [stress-induced preferential absorption](../../../../../stress-induced-preferential-absorption.md) then produces a directional strain. Climb can also allow a pinned [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) to bypass an obstacle and continue [dislocation glide](../../../../../dislocation-glide.md), providing a second route to strain.

A useful small-strain empirical form, over a limited range of conditions, is

$$
\dot\varepsilon_{\rm irr}=B\sigma\dot d,
\qquad
\varepsilon_{\rm irr}\simeq B\sigma d
$$

for constant [stress](../../../../../stress.md) $\sigma$ and approximately constant irradiation-creep compliance $B$. Here $d$ is the accumulated [displacements per atom](../../../../../displacements-per-atom.md) and $\dot d$ the [displacement dose rate](../../../../../displacement-dose-rate.md); $B$ is measured per unit [stress](../../../../../stress.md) and per unit dpa. This relation explains why creep can scale approximately with both [stress](../../../../../stress.md) and [neutron flux](../../../../../neutron-flux.md) when the damage spectrum is fixed. It is not a constitutive law valid at every dose or [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).

The total strain can also include thermal [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md), [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md), [irradiation growth](../../../../../irradiation-growth.md) and changes due to precipitation or recovery. Stress-free swelling is not itself [irradiation creep](../../../../../irradiation-creep.md). Experiments on pressurized type316 tubes separated these contributions and found that correcting stress-enhanced swelling materially affected comparisons between annealed and specimens strengthened by [cold working](../../../../../cold-working.md).[PNNL publication abstract; Journal of Nuclear Materials 367–370, pp.954–959, experimental separation of creep, swelling and precipitation strains](../../../../../the-influence-of-cold-work-level-on-the-irradiation-creep-and-swelling-of-aisi-316-stainless-steel-by-e-r-gilbert-and-f-a-garner.md)

At sufficiently high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), thermal [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) can dominate. Changes in [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md) caused by [irradiation hardening](../../../../../irradiation-hardening.md), softening, bubbles and cavities can either alter the creep rate or shorten rupture life. Thus enhanced defect-driven creep is the central effect, while the net rate and lifetime remain material- and history-dependent.
