<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**[Void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) is generally favored by an intermediate [irradiation temperature](../../../../../irradiation-temperature.md) window.** The balance involves defect mobility, stable [nucleation](../../../../../nucleation.md), absorption at [defect sinks](../../../../../defect-sink.md) and thermal emission; a larger [vacancy](../../../../../vacancy.md) population alone does not guarantee a larger void.

At low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [vacancy](../../../../../vacancy.md) migration is slow. [Self-interstitials](../../../../../self-interstitial.md) may migrate and form [dislocation loops](../../../../../dislocation-loop.md), and small clusters can cause considerable [irradiation hardening](../../../../../irradiation-hardening.md), while vacancies cannot readily feed large growing cavities. Defects retained in the matrix can evolve during later heating.

At intermediate [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [vacancies](../../../../../vacancy.md) can migrate far enough to reach stable cavities. [Interstitial bias](../../../../../interstitial-bias.md) and the competition among [defect sinks](../../../../../defect-sink.md) sustain a net vacancy flux into these cavities. Once the incubation period has produced suitable nuclei and sink populations, substantial [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) can develop.

At high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), the [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md) vacancy population and vacancy emission from small cavities rise. A small void's curvature further favors emission, so maintaining stable nuclei becomes harder. Recovery changes the [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) population, and recombination, gas transport and evolving precipitates also change the flux balance. The irradiation-driven [vacancy supersaturation](../../../../../vacancy-supersaturation.md) can fall, suppressing void [nucleation](../../../../../nucleation.md) and net swelling. Existing cavities may still coarsen, and gas bubbles, [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) and other damage can remain important.

Thus **low-temperature defect retention, intermediate-temperature cavity growth and high-temperature recovery/emission are different regimes**. The boundaries depend on [alloy](../../../../../alloy.md) composition, initial [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md), gas production, accumulated [displacements per atom](../../../../../displacements-per-atom.md) and [displacement dose rate](../../../../../displacement-dose-rate.md). No alloy-independent peak [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) can be inferred from the paper's data.
