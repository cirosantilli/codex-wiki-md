↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**The characteristic effect is an irradiation-induced upward shift of the [ductile-to-brittle transition](../../../../../ductile-to-brittle-transition.md) in ferritic [steel](../../../../../steel.md).** A component previously used above its transition can then fail by [cleavage fracture](../../../../../cleavage-fracture.md) at the same service [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), with much less warning from plastic deformation.

[Ferrite](../../../../../ferrite.md) has a [body-centered cubic lattice](../../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md). At low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [dislocation glide](../../../../../dislocation-glide.md) becomes difficult enough that brittle crack propagation can precede substantial plastic relaxation. [Irradiation hardening](../../../../../irradiation-hardening.md) raises the [yield strength](../../../../../yield-strength.md) by introducing obstacles to [dislocation glide](../../../../../dislocation-glide.md); the competition with [cleavage fracture](../../../../../cleavage-fracture.md) consequently shifts toward brittle failure. [Austenite](../../../../../austenite.md), with its [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md), does not normally show this same sharp cleavage transition. This does not make austenitic [steel](../../../../../steel.md) immune to loss of [ductility](../../../../../ductility.md), [helium embrittlement](../../../../../helium-embrittlement.md) or other [radiation damage in materials](../../../../../radiation-damage-in-materials.md).

Examine the fracture surface for [cleavage fracture](../../../../../cleavage-fracture.md) facets and river patterns, and compare [Charpy impact test](../../../../../charpy-impact-test.md) energy-versus-[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) curves from exposed material and an unirradiated reference of the same composition and heat treatment. The reference is important: a low impact energy from one failed specimen cannot establish an irradiation-induced shift. Transition curves can also use lateral expansion or the proportion of ductile fracture appearance.[§2, Background: absorbed energy, lateral expansion, shear fracture appearance and transition curves](../../../../../nist-software-package-for-obtaining-charpy-transition-curves-by-enrico-lucon-and-others.md)

A direct [fracture toughness](../../../../../fracture-toughness.md) test over the transition region complements the [Charpy impact test](../../../../../charpy-impact-test.md); the two measurements are not interchangeable. Combine these observations with exposure history, local [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md) and crack location. Brittle appearance supports the failure mechanism, while a measured transition shift and suitable controls support its attribution to [irradiation](../../../../../irradiation.md).
