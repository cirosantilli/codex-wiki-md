**Neutron capture by boron-10 can generate [helium](../../../../../helium.md), leading to [helium embrittlement](../../../../../helium-embrittlement.md), especially at elevated [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).** The relevant [nuclear reaction](../../../../../nuclear-reaction.md) is

$$
{}^{10}_{5}\mathrm B+{}^{1}_{0}n\longrightarrow{}^{7}_{3}\mathrm{Li}+{}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He}.
$$

The emitted alpha particle eventually becomes a [helium](../../../../../helium.md) atom in the metal. The product branch can also involve gamma emission, without changing this helium-producing balance. The production depends on boron isotopic content and the neutron energy spectrum; a thermal component is particularly relevant to this reaction.

[Helium](../../../../../helium.md) has very low solubility in [steel](../../../../../steel.md) and can be trapped at [vacancies](../../../../../vacancy.md), interfaces and [grain boundaries](../../../../../grain-boundary.md). Boron already segregated near [grain boundaries](../../../../../grain-boundary.md) can therefore create a locally unfavorable distribution of [helium](../../../../../helium.md). Stabilized helium–vacancy clusters develop into bubbles or cavities. Under tensile [stress](../../../../../stress.md), boundary cavities can grow and link, reducing [ductility](../../../../../ductility.md), [fracture toughness](../../../../../fracture-toughness.md) and creep-rupture life through [intergranular fracture](../../../../../intergranular-fracture.md). Early thin-foil observations and mechanical tests associated high-temperature ductility loss with helium-containing boundary bubbles and their coalescence.[Abstract and opening discussion, Nature 211, pp.291–292: boron-10 reaction, electron microscopy and boundary-bubble mechanism](../../../../../high-temperature-embrittlement-of-stainless-steel-irradiated-in-fast-fluxes-by-a-s-fraser-and-others.md)

[Irradiation temperature](../../../../../irradiation-temperature.md) controls the transport and clustering of both [helium](../../../../../helium.md) and [vacancies](../../../../../vacancy.md). At lower [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), reduced long-range transport can retain small trapped clusters in the matrix. These can contribute to [irradiation hardening](../../../../../irradiation-hardening.md), but extensive boundary-cavity growth is kinetically less favorable. At higher [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), migration, cavity growth and [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) become faster, making [intergranular fracture](../../../../../intergranular-fracture.md) and loss of high-temperature [ductility](../../../../../ductility.md) more likely. Trap distribution and prior [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md) can redistribute the gas, so there is no composition-independent critical [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) or universal monotonic law.

The mechanical-test [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) also matters. Material irradiated cold can become embrittled during subsequent hot testing or service when trapped gas and defects become mobile. Distinguish gas production during [irradiation](../../../../../irradiation.md) from the later conditions under which its mechanical consequences are expressed.
