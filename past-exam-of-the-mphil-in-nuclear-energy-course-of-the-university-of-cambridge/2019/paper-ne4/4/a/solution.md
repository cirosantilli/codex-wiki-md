<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**[glass](../../../../../glass.md) accommodates a chemically diverse waste inventory without requiring every constituent to fit one specific crystal site.** A borosilicate network can incorporate many constituents while producing a dense monolithic waste form. An established melt-and-pour approach permits remote processing and a reproducible package geometry; suitable formulations combine useful waste loading with low aqueous release rates. The absence of crystalline [grain boundaries](../../../../../grain-boundary.md) also removes one possible preferential path, although cracks or other heterogeneity can still occur.

The flexibility has limits. Some constituents have limited solubility or can separate into undesired phases, and increasing waste loading can impair processability or durability. Melting can release volatile constituents that require capture and generate secondary wastes. Cooling [stresses](../../../../../stress.md) may crack the product and enlarge its accessible surface; [devitrification](../../../../../devitrification.md) or phase separation can change chemical durability. Heat and radiation can change [density](../../../../../density.md), bonding or [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md) even though the starting [glass](../../../../../glass.md) is already noncrystalline.

Most importantly, **[glass](../../../../../glass.md) is an immobilisation medium, not an assertion of zero reaction with [water](../../../../../water.md)**. Durability depends on composition, [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), solution chemistry and the evolution of alteration products. A low initial leach rate does not alone demonstrate long-term behaviour, and an amorphous structure does not make the material inert. Container and repository barriers complement rather than duplicate the [glass](../../../../../glass.md)'s role. A suitable choice therefore balances processing, composition tolerance, radiation response and aqueous durability instead of maximising only one property.
