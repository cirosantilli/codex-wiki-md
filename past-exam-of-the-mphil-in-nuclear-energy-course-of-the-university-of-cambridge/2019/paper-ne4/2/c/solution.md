<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Delay cavity nucleation and prevent a large sustained [vacancy](../../../../../vacancy.md) flux to growing cavities.** Select an austenitic alloy composition and heat treatment demonstrated to have low [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) in the intended spectrum and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) range; modest composition changes can alter [precipitation](../../../../../precipitation-chemistry.md), segregation and sink evolution. The grade label alone is insufficient.

[cold working](../../../../../cold-working.md) supplies a [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) population that can alter defect capture and extend the low-swelling transient. Stable fine precipitates or other engineered sinks can promote defect removal, but capture bias matters as well as sink count. Irradiation and thermal recovery can erase the starting [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md), so an initially favourable state must be checked at relevant dose. Controlled impurity and helium-producing constituent contents can reduce harmful gas effects, subject to [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md) resistance and phase-stability requirements.

Operating outside a strong-swelling regime, reducing accumulated exposure or replacing components before their qualified limit are complementary measures. Each trades against other constraints such as [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md), [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md), [ductility](../../../../../ductility.md) and heat removal. **“More [cold working](../../../../../cold-working.md)” or “more sinks” is not an unlimited prescription.** Measurements must separate [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) from [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md); reducing one does not establish that the other was reduced. The pressurised-tube study is a particularly useful control because, after separating stress-enhanced [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md), its derived [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) [strains](../../../../../strain.md) were similar for annealed and cold-worked specimens.[PNNL abstract, comparison after correction for stress-enhanced swelling](../../../../../the-influence-of-cold-work-level-on-the-irradiation-creep-and-swelling-of-aisi-316-stainless-steel-by-e-r-gilbert-and-f-a-garner.md)
