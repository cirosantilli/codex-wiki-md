↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) has a **[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) window**, rather than increasing monotonically with irradiation [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). At sufficiently low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [vacancy](../../../../../vacancy.md) migration and cavity growth are slow. At intermediate [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), mobile defects, stable nuclei and unequal sink capture can support rapid cavity growth. At higher [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), increased [vacancy](../../../../../vacancy.md) emission, recombination and changing sink populations can reduce the [vacancy supersaturation](../../../../../vacancy-supersaturation.md) and net [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md). The precise peak depends on alloy, spectrum, dose rate and starting [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md); lower [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) at high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) does not mean that [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) or [helium embrittlement](../../../../../helium-embrittlement.md) is harmless there.

Distinguish [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) $S=\Delta V/V_0$ from its slope. With displacement dose $D$, a simple schematic description is

$$
S(D)\simeq0\quad(D<D_0),\qquad
S(D)\simeq k(T)(D-D_0)\quad(D>D_0),
$$

where $D_0$ represents incubation and $k(T)$ an approximately constant post-transient slope over a limited range. Real nucleation and sink evolution make the transition smooth; neither $D_0$ nor $k$ is a universal material constant. At larger dose, microstructural or compositional evolution may change the slope again.

[neutron fluence](../../../../../neutron-fluence.md) is an integrated exposure, $\Phi=\int\phi\,dt$. Under an unchanged spectrum it can track displacement dose, but equal fluence does not necessarily imply equal damage or [helium](../../../../../helium.md) production. Even equal dpa can give different [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) at different dose rates because recombination and transport compete in real time. Thus compare specimens at specified [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), spectrum, dose rate and metallurgical condition; do not transfer one [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) curve using fluence alone.
