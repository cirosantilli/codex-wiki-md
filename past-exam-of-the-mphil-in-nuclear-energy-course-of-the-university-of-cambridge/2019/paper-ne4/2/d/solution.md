↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Both terms concern **time-dependent [strain](../../../../../strain.md) under [stress](../../../../../stress.md)**. Here [radiation-enhanced creep](../../../../../radiation-enhanced-creep.md) means irradiation accelerating a mechanism that also operates thermally, whereas [radiation-induced creep](../../../../../radiation-induced-creep.md) emphasises deformation driven by irradiation-specific defect fluxes. The literature sometimes uses [irradiation creep](../../../../../irradiation-creep.md) as an umbrella, so identifying the mechanism is more informative than treating the names as mutually exclusive experimental categories.

[radiation-enhanced diffusion](../../../../../radiation-enhanced-diffusion.md) maintains mobile point-defect concentrations above their thermal equilibrium values. Under a load, this can accelerate diffusion-mediated deformation or [dislocation climb](../../../../../dislocation-climb.md) and thereby enhance a [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) mechanism. The effect depends on [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), defect production, recombination and sinks; simply raising the specimen [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) by radiation heating is a different explanation and must be controlled.

[radiation-induced creep](../../../../../radiation-induced-creep.md) can remain important where unirradiated [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) is small. [stress-induced preferential absorption](../../../../../stress-induced-preferential-absorption.md) gives differently oriented [dislocations](../../../../../dislocation.md) unequal capture of irradiation-produced defects, producing directional climb. Climb can also enable further glide by relieving obstacles. A schematic low-stress relation is

$$
\frac{d\varepsilon_{\rm irr}}{dD}\simeq B_{\rm irr}\sigma,
\qquad
\dot\varepsilon_{\rm irr}\simeq B_{\rm irr}\sigma\dot D,
$$

with a condition-dependent compliance $B_{\rm irr}$, not a universal constitutive law. Coupling to [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) and evolving sinks can add further terms.

Compare stressed and unstressed specimens in and out of irradiation at matched actual [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), and resolve longitudinal, transverse and volumetric [strains](../../../../../strain.md). Unstressed [irradiation growth](../../../../../irradiation-growth.md) or [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) is not itself evidence of [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md), and stress-enhanced [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) must not be included automatically in a fitted [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) [strain](../../../../../strain.md). These controls distinguish a mechanical response to irradiation defects from ordinary [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) or a different dimensional-change mechanism.
