<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**A [reactor coolant](../../../../../reactor-coolant.md) must remove heat reliably while remaining compatible with the core.** Desirable properties include adequate [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) and heat transfer, manageable viscosity and pumping demand, stability over the operating range, limited [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md) of fuel and structures, acceptable [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) absorption and activation, and a tolerable leakage or chemical-reaction hazard. **[water](../../../../../water.md)** is a standard example in light-water reactors: it transfers heat well and also moderates, but pressure, boiling behaviour, [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md) chemistry and loss of inventory must be managed. No single [reactor coolant](../../../../../reactor-coolant.md) is best independently of reactor design.

**A control-rod material must absorb [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md) effectively and retain mechanical and chemical integrity.** It needs a suitable absorption cross section over the relevant spectrum, adequate absorber inventory over life, manageable capture heating and transmutation products, and compatibility with its sheath and [reactor coolant](../../../../../reactor-coolant.md). **[boron carbide](../../../../../boron-carbide.md)** is an example. Depletion, gas generation, [radiation-induced swelling](../../../../../radiation-induced-swelling.md) and cracking must be allowed for; a large initial cross section alone does not establish lifetime performance.

**A [neutron moderator](../../../../../neutron-moderator.md) must slow [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md) efficiently while absorbing relatively few.** Light nuclei provide effective energy transfer per collision, but absorption, [density](../../../../../density.md), radiation stability, purity and temperature-dependent properties determine the usable moderation. **[heavy water](../../../../../heavy-water.md)** is an example combining effective slowing with low parasitic absorption; [graphite](../../../../../graphite.md) is another possible material in different designs. Heat removal and compatibility remain necessary even though moderation is its principal neutronic role.

**[nuclear fuel cladding](../../../../../nuclear-fuel-cladding.md) must retain fuel and [fission products](../../../../../fission-product.md) while transferring heat and carrying load.** Desirable properties include [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md) resistance, low [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) absorption, adequate [thermal conductivity](../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md), strength and [ductility](../../../../../ductility.md), resistance to [irradiation growth](../../../../../irradiation-growth.md), [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) and [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md), and compatibility with fuel and [reactor coolant](../../../../../reactor-coolant.md). **A [zirconium alloy](../../../../../zirconium-alloy.md) such as [Zircaloy](../../../../../zircaloy.md)** is a light-water-reactor example. Hydrogen uptake, [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md) and irradiation response constrain service; the low absorption benefit does not eliminate these materials problems.
