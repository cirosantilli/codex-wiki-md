<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A useful fuel combines **neutronic suitability, heat removal, containment and dimensional stability**. A sufficient fissile inventory and acceptable [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) economy permit the intended power and exposure, while unnecessary parasitic absorption wastes [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md). These requirements are reactor-specific rather than a recommendation for one universal composition.

A high melting margin and stable phases help preserve geometry. Good [thermal conductivity](../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md) reduces the centre-to-surface [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) rise and associated thermal [stress](../../../../../stress.md); [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) helps moderate transient [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) changes. Chemical stability and compatibility with [reactor coolant](../../../../../reactor-coolant.md) and [nuclear fuel cladding](../../../../../nuclear-fuel-cladding.md) reduce [corrosion](../../../../../corrosion.md) and reactions at the interface with [nuclear fuel cladding](../../../../../nuclear-fuel-cladding.md). Oxygen chemistry and other composition variables can affect both transport and interface reactions.

Under irradiation the fuel should tolerate [displacement damage](../../../../../displacement-damage.md), restructuring, [radiation-induced swelling](../../../../../radiation-induced-swelling.md) and [fission gas](../../../../../fission-gas.md) accumulation without unacceptable cracking, gap closure or pressure. Retention of radionuclides and controlled release of [fission gas](../../../../../fission-gas.md) support the [nuclear fuel cladding](../../../../../nuclear-fuel-cladding.md)'s containment function. Predictable [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) can relieve [stress](../../../../../stress.md), but excessive deformation may compromise geometry. Fabrication reproducibility, inspectable [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md), resource availability and the practicability of subsequent waste management also matter.

**[uranium dioxide](../../../../../uranium-dioxide.md) illustrates the compromises:** it is a chemically robust, high-melting ceramic fuel, but its relatively limited [thermal conductivity](../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md), irradiation-dependent [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md) and gas behaviour require explicit thermal and mechanical design margins. A desirable individual property does not establish the performance of the coupled [nuclear fuel](../../../../../nuclear-fuel.md) and [nuclear fuel cladding](../../../../../nuclear-fuel-cladding.md) system.
