↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The two historically dominant commercial processes are **[gaseous diffusion enrichment](../../../../../gaseous-diffusion-enrichment.md) and [gas centrifuge enrichment](../../../../../gas-centrifuge-enrichment.md)**. The first exploits differences in molecular transport through a barrier; the second exploits mass-dependent redistribution in a rotating gas. Both are isotope-separation principles rather than chemical separation of [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) from another element.

**[gas centrifuge enrichment](../../../../../gas-centrifuge-enrichment.md) had become predominant by the time of this examination.** Its much smaller electricity demand reduces a major operating cost of [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) technology. Modular capacity and the replacement of ageing [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) plants contributed to commercial adoption. This historical conclusion does not require machine dimensions, operating conditions or a cascade design. The United States' final operating [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) plant closed in 2013, consistent with the earlier transition rather than a claim based on current plant counts.[Institutional historical sections on gas centrifuges, electricity demand and diffusion-plant closures](../../../../../backgrounder-on-uranium-enrichment-by-the-united-states-nuclear-regulatory-commission.md)
