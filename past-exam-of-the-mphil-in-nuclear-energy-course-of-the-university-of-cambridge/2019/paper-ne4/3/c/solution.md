<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md) introduces **[decay heat](../../../../../decay-heat.md), self-irradiation and composition-sensitive thermochemical behaviour**, as well as demanding radiological handling and material accountancy. [isotopic composition](../../../../../isotopic-composition.md) affects heat and [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) production, so total [plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md) mass alone does not specify the handling environment. The elemental metal also has inconvenient allotropic and thermal behaviour; using an appropriate stable ceramic fuel form avoids treating bare metal as a straightforward substitute for [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) oxide.

In [mixed oxide fuel](../../../../../mixed-oxide-fuel.md), [plutonium dioxide](../../../../../plutonium-dioxide.md) and [uranium dioxide](../../../../../uranium-dioxide.md) form the engineered ceramic system. Important challenges are composition uniformity, [thermal conductivity](../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md) and melting margin, oxygen chemistry, restructuring and [fission gas](../../../../../fission-gas.md) behaviour, and compatibility with [nuclear fuel cladding](../../../../../nuclear-fuel-cladding.md). A local composition variation can produce a local power or [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) variation. During service, [actinide](../../../../../actinide.md) redistribution can change the initial spatial distribution; its direction cannot be inferred from the [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) gradient alone. Autoradiographic examination of irradiated mixed-oxide pins directly investigated the competing transport effects.[Journal of Nuclear Materials 47, 187–197, abstract: irradiated-pin observations, thermal diffusion, evaporation–condensation and oxygen chemistry](../../../../../plutonium-redistribution-in-fast-reactor-mixed-oxide-fuel-pins-by-m-bober-and-others.md)

Mitigation is to use a characterised fuel form and isotopic inventory, qualify composition and [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md) uniformity, retain suitable thermal margins, and establish irradiation performance and interface compatibility. [radioactive material confinement](../../../../../radioactive-material-confinement.md), remote or protected handling and waste management address the accompanying contamination and exposure problems. These are **materials requirements and verification objectives**; no [plutonium](../../../../../plutonium.md) preparation, separation or fuel-manufacturing procedure is needed to answer them.
