<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**[thermal cycling growth](../../../../../thermal-cycling-growth.md) is permanent dimensional change remaining after a [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) cycle; [irradiation growth](../../../../../irradiation-growth.md) is a radiation-driven change of shape that need not require an applied load.** Neither is simply the reversible increase in dimensions of a warm specimen.

For a crystal in its principal axes, a small unconstrained thermal [strain](../../../../../strain.md) is

$$
\boldsymbol\varepsilon^{\rm th}=\boldsymbol\alpha\Delta T.
$$

[anisotropic thermal expansion](../../../../../anisotropic-thermal-expansion.md) means that the principal components of $\boldsymbol\alpha$ differ. Adjacent [crystal grains](../../../../../grain-in-a-polycrystal.md) with different orientations therefore demand different [strain](../../../../../strain.md) tensors during the same [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) change. Their bonded boundaries impose compatibility and generate internal [stresses](../../../../../stress.md). [plasticity](../../../../../plasticity-physics.md), [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md), boundary motion or microcracking can accommodate this mismatch irreversibly; when the accommodation fails to cancel on cooling, a residual [strain](../../../../../strain.md) remains. Repeated cycles can accumulate directional [thermal ratcheting](../../../../../thermal-ratcheting.md), surface roughening or distortion. [crystallographic texture](../../../../../crystallographic-texture.md), constraints, cycle amplitude and the [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence of yielding and recovery determine the outcome.

**Anisotropy alone is insufficient.** A freely expanding [single crystal](../../../../../single-crystal.md) under spatially uniform, fully reversible heating returns to its original dimensions. Even cyclic [plasticity](../../../../../plasticity-physics.md) can settle into a stabilised response rather than ratchet indefinitely. Demonstrating [thermal cycling growth](../../../../../thermal-cycling-growth.md) means comparing dimensions at the same reference [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) after successive cycles, while controlling external loads, [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) gradients and phase changes. [uranium](../../../../../uranium.md) dimensional instability under cycling was investigated directly in the early reactor-materials programme.[ORNL-1386, 1952, opening discussion of dimensional instability, preferred orientation and observed distortion](../../../../../thermal-cycling-of-uranium-by-nathaniel-stetson-and-melbourne-l-winton.md)

[irradiation growth](../../../../../irradiation-growth.md) has a different source of irreversibility. [displacement cascades](../../../../../displacement-cascade.md) create [vacancies](../../../../../vacancy.md) and [self-interstitials](../../../../../self-interstitial.md); anisotropic migration, clustering and capture at oriented [dislocations](../../../../../dislocation.md) and boundaries can produce unequal [strains](../../../../../strain.md) along crystal directions. For approximately volume-conserving growth,

$$
\varepsilon_1^{g}+\varepsilon_2^{g}+\varepsilon_3^{g}\simeq0,
$$

although a rod may elongate substantially in one direction. The effect can occur at constant [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) without an external [stress](../../../../../stress.md). Distinguish it from **[void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md)**, which increases volume through cavities, and **[irradiation creep](../../../../../irradiation-creep.md)**, whose directional deformation is stress-dependent. Experiments should therefore measure axial and transverse dimensions, [density](../../../../../density.md) and the applied [stress](../../../../../stress.md) rather than identifying every increase in length as one mechanism.
