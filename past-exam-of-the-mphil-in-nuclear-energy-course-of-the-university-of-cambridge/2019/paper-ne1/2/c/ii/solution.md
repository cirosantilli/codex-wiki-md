<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Substitute the separated solution into the [one-group neutron diffusion equation](../../../../../../one-group-neutron-diffusion-equation.md). The radial and axial [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) contributions are $-\alpha^2\phi$ and $-\beta^2\phi$. A nonzero solution therefore requires

$$
\boxed{\alpha^2+\beta^2
=\frac{(\eta-1)\Sigma_a}{D}
=\frac{\nu\Sigma_f-\Sigma_a}{D}.}
$$

The left side is the [geometric buckling](../../../../../../geometric-buckling.md); the right side is fixed by the stipulated homogeneous material. Their equality is the model's [nuclear criticality](../../../../../../nuclear-criticality.md) condition, expressing the balance of production, absorption and leakage. Both sides have inverse-length-squared units.
