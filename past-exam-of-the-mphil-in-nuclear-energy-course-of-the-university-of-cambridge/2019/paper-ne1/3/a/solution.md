<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**Each equation is stored thermal energy changing at the rate “input minus output”.** Define the three [heat capacities](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) $C_j=M_jc_j$, and introduce the internal heat-transfer rates

$$
Q_{FM}=\frac{\overline T_F-\overline T_M}{R_1},
\qquad
Q_{MC}=\frac{\overline T_M-\overline T_C}{R_2}.
$$

In the fuel balance, $C_F\dot{\overline T}_F$ is accumulation, $P$ is thermal [power](../../../../../power.md) generation and $Q_{FM}$ is transfer to the [neutron moderator](../../../../../neutron-moderator.md). In the moderator balance, $Q_{FM}$ enters, $Q_{MC}$ leaves and $C_M\dot{\overline T}_M$ is accumulation. In the coolant balance, $Q_{MC}$ enters, $C_C\dot{\overline T}_C$ is accumulation and $2W_Cc_C(\overline T_C-T_i)$ is net energy carried away by flowing [coolant](../../../../../coolant.md).

The factor two follows the model's arithmetic-mean approximation $\overline T_C=(T_i+T_o)/2$, since the advective energy removal is $W_Cc_C(T_o-T_i)$. The parameters $R_1$ and $R_2$ are effective [thermal resistances](../../../../../thermal-resistance.md), in $\mathrm{K\,W^{-1}}$, for fuel-to-moderator and moderator-to-coolant transfer. They summarize the relevant geometry and heat-transfer mechanisms; they are not [heat capacities](../../../../../heat-capacity.md).
