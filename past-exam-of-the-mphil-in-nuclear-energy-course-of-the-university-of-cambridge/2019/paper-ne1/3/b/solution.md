<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At a [steady state](../../../../../steady-state.md), all three accumulation terms vanish. The fuel balance gives $Q_{FM}=P$, the moderator balance then gives $Q_{MC}=P$, and the coolant balance equates that input to its advective output. Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\overline T_C&=T_i+\frac{P}{2W_Cc_C},\\
\overline T_M&=T_i+P\left(R_2+\frac1{2W_Cc_C}\right),\\
\overline T_F&=T_i+P\left(R_1+R_2+\frac1{2W_Cc_C}\right).
\end{aligned}}
$$

Each successive region adds the temperature drop needed to carry the same thermal [power](../../../../../power.md) through its [thermal resistance](../../../../../thermal-resistance.md). Summing the original balances cancels both internal transfers, providing the independent total energy balance $P=W_Cc_C(T_o-T_i)$.
