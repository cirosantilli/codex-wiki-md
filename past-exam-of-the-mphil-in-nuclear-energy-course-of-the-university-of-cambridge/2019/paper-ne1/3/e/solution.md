<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**The power coefficient is approximately $-2.28\times10^{-11}\ \mathrm{W^{-1}}$, or $-2.28\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{MW^{-1}}$.** From part (c),

$$
R_1=R_2=\frac{75}{236\times10^6}
=3.17797\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{K\,W^{-1}},
\qquad
\frac1{2W_Cc_C}=2.11864\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{K\,W^{-1}}.
$$

Substituting in the [power coefficient of reactivity](../../../../../power-coefficient-of-reactivity.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_P={}&(-2.0\times10^{-5})(3.17797\times10^{-7})\\
&+(-1.5\times10^{-5})(3.17797\times10^{-7})\\
&+(-5.5\times10^{-5})(2.11864\times10^{-7})\\
={}&-2.27754\times10^{-11}\ \mathrm{W^{-1}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The negative sign means that a slow increase in thermal [power](../../../../../power.md) produces a decrease in [reactor reactivity](../../../../../reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md) along this family. This is a restoring thermal [feedback control](../../../../../closed-loop-control.md) effect despite the positive moderator coefficient. **A negative quasistatic slope alone does not prove stability of every transient:** the separate [heat capacities](../../../../../heat-capacity.md), transport delays and neutron-population dynamics matter for a time-dependent stability analysis.
