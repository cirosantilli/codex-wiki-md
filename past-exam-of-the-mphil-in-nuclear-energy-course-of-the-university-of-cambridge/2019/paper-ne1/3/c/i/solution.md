↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The coolant mass flow rate is approximately $983\ \mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$.** All thermal [power](../../../../../../power.md) leaves in the steady coolant energy rise:

$$
P=W_Cc_C(T_o-T_i).
$$

With the stated constant [specific heat capacity](../../../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md),

$$
\boxed{W_C=\frac{236\times10^6}
{2400(700-600)}
=983.33\ \mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ difference equals a $100\ \mathrm K$ difference. Thermal [power](../../../../../../power.md) is used directly; no electrical conversion efficiency enters.
