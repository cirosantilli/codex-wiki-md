↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The average moderator temperature is $725\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$.** First the mean coolant temperature in the [lumped thermal model](../../../../../../lumped-thermal-model.md) is

$$
\overline T_C=\frac{600+700}{2}=650\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.
$$

At a [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state.md), the same thermal [power](../../../../../../power.md) crosses both [thermal resistances](../../../../../../thermal-resistance.md). If $R_1=R_2$, then

$$
\overline T_F-\overline T_M
=\overline T_M-\overline T_C.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\overline T_M=\frac{800+650}{2}=725\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.}
$$

In particular $R_1=R_2=(800-650)/(2P)=3.17797\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm{K\,W^{-1}}$ at the specified state.
