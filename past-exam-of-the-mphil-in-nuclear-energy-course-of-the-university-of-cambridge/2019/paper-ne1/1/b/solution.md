<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**The cobalt-60 mass is approximately $5.73\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{kg}$.** The [nuclear decay constant](../../../../../nuclear-decay-constant.md) follows from the [half-life](../../../../../half-life.md), using a $365.25$-day year:

$$
\lambda=\frac{\ln2}{5.27(365.25)(86400)}
=4.16784\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}.
$$

Since [radioactive activity](../../../../../radioactive-activity.md) is $A=\lambda N$, the parent population at certification is

$$
N=\frac{2.4\times10^9}{4.16784\times10^{-9}}
=5.75838\times10^{17}.
$$

Convert the supplied [atomic mass](../../../../../atomic-mass.md) into [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md), $M=0.05993382\ \mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}$, and use the [Avogadro constant](../../../../../avogadro-constant.md):

$$
\boxed{m=\frac{NM}{N_A}
=\frac{(5.75838\times10^{17})(0.05993382)}
{6.02214076\times10^{23}}
=5.73088\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{kg}.}
$$

This is about $57.3\ \mathrm{\mu g}$ of the specified [isotope](../../../../../isotope.md). It is not the total [mass](../../../../../mass.md) of the pellets or capsule, which can also contain other material.
