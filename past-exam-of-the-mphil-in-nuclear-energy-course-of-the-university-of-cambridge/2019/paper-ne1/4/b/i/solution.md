<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $G=\gamma_I\Sigma_f\phi$ be the constant precursor production rate per volume. Here $\gamma_I$ is the precursor yield per [nuclear fission](../../../../../../nuclear-fission.md), and $I$ and $X$ are [number densities](../../../../../../number-density.md). At a [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state.md),

$$
I_0=\frac{G}{\lambda_I},
\qquad
X_0=\frac{G}{\lambda_X+\phi\sigma_{aX}}.
$$

For fixed homogeneous coefficients and spatial shape, the reactivity contribution of an added absorption coefficient $\Sigma_{aX}=\sigma_{aX}X_0$ is $-\Sigma_{aX}/(\nu\Sigma_f)$. Thus the model gives

$$
\Delta\rho_X
=-\frac{\gamma_I}{\nu}
\frac{\phi\sigma_{aX}}{\lambda_X+\phi\sigma_{aX}}.
$$

When capture removal dominates xenon [radioactive decay](../../../../../../radioactive-decay.md), $\phi\sigma_{aX}\gg\lambda_X$, and hence

$$
\boxed{\Delta\rho_X\longrightarrow-\frac{\gamma_I}{\nu}.}
$$

The limit is independent of the xenon [neutron absorption cross section](../../../../../../neutron-absorption-cross-section.md): at high [neutron flux](../../../../../../neutron-flux.md), nearly every xenon atom supplied by the precursor ultimately consumes a [neutron](../../../../../../neutron-split.md). The assumptions include constant pre-shutdown [neutron flux](../../../../../../neutron-flux.md), sufficient time for precursor and xenon equilibrium, the supplied single-precursor model with no direct xenon fission source, and fixed parameters for translating absorption into [reactor reactivity](../../../../../../reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md).
