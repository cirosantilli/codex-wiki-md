# Density stratification

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

Density stratification is variation of a fluid's [mass density](density.md) with height. Its stable or unstable character depends on whether the [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) force restores or amplifies a displaced parcel.
