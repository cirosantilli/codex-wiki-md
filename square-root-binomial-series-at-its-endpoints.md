# Square-root binomial series at its endpoints

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial series](binomial-series.md)

Write $a_n=\binom{2n}{n}/4^n$, $a_0=1$. The [binomial series](binomial-series.md) for $\sqrt{1-u}$ has positive numbers $b_n=a_{n-1}-a_n=\binom{2n}{n}/[4^n(2n-1)]$ as the magnitudes of its nonconstant coefficients. The product $a_n=\prod_{j=1}^n[1-1/(2j)]$ tends to zero, since $\ln a_n\leq-\tfrac12\sum_{j=1}^n1/j\to-\infty$. Thus $\sum b_n=1$ by telescoping. The [Weierstrass M-test](weierstrass-m-test.md) gives [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) and [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md) on $[-1,1]$. Continuity extends the interior identity to both endpoints. Its [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) is one; outside this interval its terms do not tend to zero.
