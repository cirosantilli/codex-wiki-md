<h1 id="levy-characterization-of-multidimensional-brownian-motion">Lévy characterization of multidimensional Brownian motion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md)

A continuous vector-valued [local martingale](local-martingale.md) $M$ starting at zero is standard multidimensional [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) if its [quadratic covariations](quadratic-covariation.md) satisfy $[M^i,M^j]_t=\delta_{ij}t$. For each deterministic vector $\xi$, the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) makes $\exp(i\xi\cdot M_t+|\xi|^2t/2)$ a complex [local martingale](local-martingale.md). Its absolute value is bounded on each finite horizon, so it is a true [martingale](martingale-split.md). Its [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) identity gives

$$
\mathbb E[e^{i\xi\cdot(M_t-M_s)}\mid\mathcal F_s]=e^{-|\xi|^2(t-s)/2}.
$$

This conditional [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) identifies a centered [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) with covariance $(t-s)I$, independent of the past. Thus the coordinates are independent [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md). The converse follows immediately from the standard coordinate [quadratic covariations](quadratic-covariation.md).
