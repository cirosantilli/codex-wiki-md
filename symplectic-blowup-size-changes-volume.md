# Symplectic blowup size changes volume

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic blowup](symplectic-blowup.md)

For a closed $2n$-dimensional [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md), a [symplectic blowup](symplectic-blowup.md) of size $\varepsilon=\pi R^2$ removes the [symplectic volume](symplectic-volume.md) of the radius-$R$ ball. Its exceptional divisor has lower dimension and contributes no ambient volume. The displayed volume loss shows that different size choices can give forms that are not related by a [symplectomorphism](symplectomorphism.md).
