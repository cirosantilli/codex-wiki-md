# Loss-driven onset of condensate stirring

↑ **Parent:** [Local sound-speed criterion for circular condensate stirring](local-sound-speed-criterion-for-circular-condensate-stirring.md)

For a [two-dimensional Thomas–Fermi condensate](two-dimensional-thomas-fermi-condensate.md) at fixed coupling, $N=\pi n_0R^2/2\propto\mu^2$, so $R\propto N^{1/4}$. Slow exponential atom loss shrinks the cloud while a fixed stirring radius becomes a larger fraction of its size. At $\Omega=\omega_r/\sqrt2$ and initial $D/R=1/2$, the local-sound-speed threshold is reached at $t_c=2\tau\ln2$, before the shrinking cloud edge reaches the beam.
