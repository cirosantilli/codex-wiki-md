# Inductive bias

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical learning](statistical-learning-split.md)

Constraints or preferences that guide [generalization](generalization-in-statistical-learning.md) beyond observed training examples. Local [convolution](convolution.md), [weight sharing](weight-sharing.md), a compositional grammar and a prior over programs supply different inductive biases. Their usefulness depends on the structure of the target problem.
