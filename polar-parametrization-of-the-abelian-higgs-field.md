# Polar parametrization of the Abelian Higgs field

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian Higgs model](abelian-higgs-model.md)

Near a nonzero [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md), the radial variable $h$ describes the [Higgs mode](higgs-mode.md) and the angular variable $\pi$ describes the would-be [Goldstone boson](goldstone-boson.md). For $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+ieA_\mu$, their local [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) is $h\mapsto h$, $\pi\mapsto\pi+f\alpha$, and $A_\mu\mapsto A_\mu-\partial_\mu\alpha/e$. The scalar kinetic term is $(\partial h)^2/2+(1+h/f)^2(\partial\pi+efA)^2/2$. These coordinates simplify [gauge orbits](gauge-orbit.md) but obscure ordinary [power counting](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) through higher-dimensional derivative vertices and fail at $\phi=0$.
