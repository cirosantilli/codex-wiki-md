# Weyl character formula

↑ **Parent:** [Highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_character_formula)

For a dominant integral weight $\lambda$, the irreducible character is

$$
\operatorname{ch}L_\lambda=
\frac{\sum_{w\in W}(-1)^{\ell(w)}e^{w(\lambda+\rho)}}
{\sum_{w\in W}(-1)^{\ell(w)}e^{w\rho}}.
$$
