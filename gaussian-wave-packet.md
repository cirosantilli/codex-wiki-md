# Gaussian wave packet

↑ **Parent:** [Wave function](wave-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_wave_packet)

A Gaussian wave packet is a [wavefunction](wave-function.md) whose spatial amplitude is a [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md). Free quantum evolution preserves its Gaussian form while changing its complex width and spreading its [probability density](probability-density.md).
