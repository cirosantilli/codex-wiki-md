# Disease-ascertained transmission probability

↑ **Parent:** [Genotype relative risk](genotype-relative-risk.md)

Let $q_g=P(g\mid g_f,g_m)$ be the [Mendelian segregation](mendelian-segregation.md) probability, and assume the child's disease status depends on the parental [genotypes](genotype.md) only through its own genotype, with [penetrance](penetrance.md) proportional to $R_g$. [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) gives the displayed conditional law. For a diallelic parent pair there are four equally likely labelled transmission outcomes. If an unordered genotype occurs in $m_g$ of them, $q_g=m_g/4$, so its probability is $m_gR_g/\sum_hm_hR_h$. Dropping multiplicities would, for example, misweight a heterozygous child of two heterozygotes.
