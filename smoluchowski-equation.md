# Smoluchowski equation

↑ **Parent:** [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md)

For overdamped [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) at uniform [temperature](temperature-split.md) in a [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $U$, constant [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$ gives the displayed [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md), with $\beta=(k_BT)^{-1}$. The [probability current](probability-current.md) is $\boldsymbol J=-D(\nabla p+\beta p\nabla U)$. Zero current recovers the [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md); absorbing boundaries instead permit transport and [mean first-passage times](mean-first-passage-time.md). This diffusion equation should be distinguished from the similarly named coagulation equation.
