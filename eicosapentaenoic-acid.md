# Eicosapentaenoic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Fatty acid](fatty-acid.md)

[eicosapentaenoic acid](eicosapentaenoic-acid.md) is a 20-carbon omega-3 fatty acid with five double bonds. Engineering its production requires coordinated desaturation, elongation and lipid-carrier handling.
