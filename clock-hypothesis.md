# Clock hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Twin paradox with finite acceleration](twin-paradox-with-finite-acceleration.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Proper time](proper-time.md)

The [clock hypothesis](clock-hypothesis.md) says that an ideal clock following a [timelike curve](timelike-curve.md) measures its [proper time](proper-time.md): its instantaneous rate depends on [velocity](velocity.md) through $d\tau=dt\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}$, with no separate [acceleration](acceleration.md) term. [Acceleration](acceleration.md) still changes the [world line](world-line.md) and hence the accumulated [proper time](proper-time.md). A physical clock may suffer disturbances under forces or environmental changes, so experiments must distinguish its mechanism's errors from the geometric prediction.
