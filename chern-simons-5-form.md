# Chern-Simons 5-form

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field](gauge-field.md)

For a matrix-valued [gauge field](gauge-field.md) $A$, let $F=dA+A\wedge A$ be its [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md); products below combine matrix multiplication with the [wedge product of differential forms](wedge-product-of-differential-forms.md). The five-form can be written $Q_5=\operatorname{Tr}(A(dA)^2+\tfrac32A^3dA+\tfrac35A^5)$. Applying the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md), using its graded product rule and the graded cyclic property of the [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md), gives $dQ_5=\operatorname{Tr}(F^3)$. The six-form is an invariant polynomial in the [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md), while its local potential depends on the connection.
