# Sphere center from three tetrahedron face distances

↑ **Parent:** [Tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md)

For a positively oriented [basis](basis.md) $\mathbf a,\mathbf b,\mathbf c$, put $\tau=\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)>0$. The center of an interior [sphere](sphere.md) at distance $r$ from each of the three [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md) face planes through the origin has the displayed expression. The three inward [unit vectors](unit-vector.md) are the normalized [cross products](cross-product.md) $\mathbf b\times\mathbf c$, $\mathbf c\times\mathbf a$, $\mathbf a\times\mathbf b$. Taking [inner products](inner-product.md) with these vectors solves three independent signed-distance equations. Reversing some signs instead produces centers on exterior sides. Existence of a sphere contained in the full [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md) also requires clearance from the fourth face.
