<h1 id="euler-s-critical-load">Euler's critical load</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Bernoulli beam equation](euler-bernoulli-beam-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler's_critical_load)

A straight slender [elastic beam](elastic-beam.md) under axial compression loses stability when bending can decrease its total [potential energy](potential-energy.md). With pinned ends a distance $L$ apart, its quadratic potential is $\tfrac12\int_0^L[EI_A(y'')^2-P(y')^2]dx$. Stationarity gives $EI_Ay''''+Py''=0$, with $y=y''=0$ at each end. The first nontrivial mode is $\sin(\pi x/L)$, at $P_{\rm cr}=\pi^2EI_A/L^2$. More generally $P_{\rm cr}=\pi^2EI_A/(K L)^2$, with an effective-length factor $K$ determined by endpoint [constraints](constraint-mechanics.md): $K=1$ for pinned-pinned, $2$ for fixed-free and $1/2$ for fixed-fixed. Real imperfections remove the perfectly sharp onset, and short columns can yield before this elastic instability.
