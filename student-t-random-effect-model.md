# Student t random-effect model

↑ **Parent:** [Random-effects meta-analysis](random-effects-meta-analysis.md)

A [Student t random-effect model](student-t-random-effect-model.md) assigns [Student's t-distributions](student-s-t-distribution.md) to exchangeable study effects, allowing heavier tails than a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) hierarchy. An equivalent [Gaussian scale mixture](gaussian-scale-mixture.md) is $\lambda_j\sim\chi^2_\nu$, $\beta_j\mid\lambda_j,\mu,\psi\sim N(\mu,\nu\psi^2/\lambda_j)$, with independent latent draws. For $\nu>2$ the [variance](variance-split.md) is $\nu\psi^2/(\nu-2)$, so $\psi$ is a scale rather than a [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md). Small latent precisions weaken shrinkage for atypical studies while retaining [partial pooling](partial-pooling.md) for the rest.
