# Particle-conserving displacement of a Fermi circle

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi circle](fermi-circle.md)

Translating a [Fermi circle](fermi-circle.md) of radius $p_F$ by $p_0\hat{\mathbf x}$ preserves its area. For $p_0<p_F$, its radial displacement from the original centre is the displayed expression. Its [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md) is $\nu=p_0\cos\theta-p_0^2\sin^2\theta/(2p_F)+O(p_0^4/p_F^3)$. The second-order term cancels the apparent area increase from the first-order shape: $\int_0^{2\pi}(p_F\nu+\nu^2/2)\,d\theta=0$. This distinction matters when converting a grand-potential variation into a fixed-[particle number](particle-number.md) [energy](energy-split.md) change.
