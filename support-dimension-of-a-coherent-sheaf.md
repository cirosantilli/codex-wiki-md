# Support dimension of a coherent sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md)

The support dimension is the [dimension of a scheme](dimension-of-a-scheme.md) of the closed support of a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md). Tensoring with a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) leaves its support unchanged. Quotienting by a section that avoids its [associated points](associated-point-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md) reduces the support dimension by at least one.
