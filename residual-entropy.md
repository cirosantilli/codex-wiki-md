# Residual entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy](entropy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Residual_entropy)

[Residual entropy](residual-entropy.md) is [entropy](entropy-split.md) remaining in the zero-temperature limit. An equilibrium ground-state degeneracy $g_0$ gives $S_0=k_B\ln g_0$ for the complete system; an extensive [residual entropy](residual-entropy.md) requires a correspondingly large state count. Frozen configurational disorder can also leave an experimentally inferred [residual entropy](residual-entropy.md) when a sample fails to equilibrate. The zero-entropy perfect-crystal form of the [third law of thermodynamics](third-law-of-thermodynamics.md) assumes a unique, fully ordered equilibrium [ground state](ground-state.md).
