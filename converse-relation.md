# Converse relation

↑ **Parent:** [Binary relation](binary-relation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converse_relation)

Reversing every [ordered pair](ordered-pair.md) of a [binary relation](binary-relation.md). Swapping the two components is its own inverse and therefore preserves distinctness of a representation by a [list](list-abstract-data-type.md) of distinct pairs.
