# Regular sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative ring](commutative-ring.md)

A regular sequence in a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $A$ is a finite list such that $(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$ is proper and $f_j$ is a [non-zero-divisor](non-zero-divisor.md) on $A/(f_1,\ldots,f_{j-1})$ for each $j$. In a graded ring with homogeneous $f_j$, injectivity can be checked on homogeneous elements because components of distinct degrees cannot cancel. It gives [short exact sequences](short-exact-sequence.md) $0\to R_{j-1}(-\deg f_j)\to R_{j-1}\to R_j\to0$ for the successive graded quotients.
