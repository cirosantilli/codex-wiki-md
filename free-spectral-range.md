# Free spectral range

↑ **Parent:** [Physical optics](physical-optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_spectral_range)

The [free spectral range](free-spectral-range.md) is the [wavelength](wavelength.md) or [frequency](frequency.md) spacing between adjacent orders or resonances of an [optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md). It identifies when different orders can overlap. Always specify the coordinate: a uniform spacing in [frequency](frequency.md) need not be uniform in [wavelength](wavelength.md). For an ideal [Fabry–Pérot interferometer](fabry-perot-interferometer.md) at [normal incidence](normal-incidence.md), with mirror separation $d$ and constant [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n$, [Fabry–Pérot resonance spacing](fabry-perot-resonance-spacing.md) gives $\Delta\nu_{\rm FSR}=c/(2nd)$, where $c$ is the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) in [vacuum](vacuum.md). The [free spectral range of an echelle grating](free-spectral-range-of-an-echelle-grating.md) follows a different order condition.
