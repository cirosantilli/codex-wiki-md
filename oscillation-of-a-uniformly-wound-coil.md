# Oscillation of a uniformly wound coil

↑ **Parent:** [Small-angle magnetic dipole oscillation](small-angle-magnetic-dipole-oscillation.md)

For a light nonmagnetic rod of length $l$ wound uniformly with $N$ thin turns of mean radius $a$, the [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) is $m_B=NI\pi a^2$. Wire of cross-sectional [area](surface-area.md) $A_w$ and [density](density.md) $\rho$ has [mass](mass.md) approximately $M=2\pi aN\rho A_w$. The transverse central [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) is $J=M(l^2/12+a^2/2)$ in the thin-winding approximation. Consequently the oscillation period in a transverse [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) does not depend on the number of turns: wire inertia and magnetic moment both scale with $N$.
