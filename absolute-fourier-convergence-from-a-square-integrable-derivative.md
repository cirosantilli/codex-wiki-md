# Absolute Fourier convergence from a square-integrable derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md)

For a periodic absolutely continuous [function](function-split.md) with derivative in $L^2$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $\widehat{f'}(n)=in\widehat f(n)$. [Bessel's inequality](bessel-s-inequality.md) and the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) prove the displayed bound in the normalized circle norm. Adding the constant coefficient gives an absolutely and uniformly convergent [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md). Merely bounding individual coefficients by $O(1/|n|)$ would not establish absolute convergence.
