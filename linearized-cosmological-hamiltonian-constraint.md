# Linearized cosmological Hamiltonian constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian constraint](hamiltonian-constraint.md)

For a spatially flat [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), negative [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md), and $K=-3H+\kappa$, the [Hamiltonian constraint](hamiltonian-constraint.md) gives the displayed scalar perturbation equation. Indeed $K^2=9H^2-6H\kappa$ and $K^i{}_jK^j{}_i=3H^2-2H\kappa$ to first order. The spatial [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) is $4\Delta\Phi$; subtract the background equation $6H^2=16\pi G\bar\rho$ and divide by four.
