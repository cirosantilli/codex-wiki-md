# Ordinarily nowhere-dense sets need not be completely Ramsey

↑ **Parent:** [Cone on an infinite coinfinite ground set](cone-on-an-infinite-coinfinite-ground-set.md)

The cone $[E]^\omega$ on the even [integers](integer.md) is nonempty and [Ellentuck topology](ellentuck-topology.md) open, while it is closed and [nowhere dense](nowhere-dense-set.md) in the [ordinary topology on infinite subsets](ordinary-topology-on-infinite-subsets.md). Choose a [finite-symmetric-difference parity colouring](finite-symmetric-difference-parity-colouring.md) on that cone and take one colour class. The class is still ordinarily [nowhere dense](nowhere-dense-set.md) because its [closure](closure-topology.md) lies in the cone, but every stem-free refinement inside the cone meets both colours. Hence it is not [completely Ramsey](completely-ramsey-set.md).
