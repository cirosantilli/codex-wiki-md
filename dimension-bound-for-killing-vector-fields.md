# Dimension bound for Killing vector fields

↑ **Parent:** [Killing transport](killing-transport.md)

On a connected $n$-dimensional [pseudo-Riemannian manifold](pseudo-riemannian-manifold.md), evaluation of a [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) and its first derivative is injective by [Killing transport](killing-transport.md). The value has $n$ components and the [Killing equation](killing-equation.md) makes the derivative an antisymmetric two-tensor with $n(n-1)/2$ components. Their sum gives the bound. Flat metrics attain it through $\xi_a=A_a+B_{ab}x^b$ with $B_{ab}=-B_{ba}$. Connectedness is essential for a uniform global bound in terms of $n$: independent fields can be supported on different connected components.
