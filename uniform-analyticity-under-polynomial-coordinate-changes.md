# Uniform analyticity under polynomial coordinate changes

↑ **Parent:** [Real analytic function](real-analytic-function.md)

Suppose a family of [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) coordinate maps has a fixed bound on its degrees and coefficients depending continuously on a compact parameter set, and sends a fixed real compact set into a compact subset of the domain of a [real analytic function](real-analytic-function.md). A common complex neighborhood can then be chosen so the compositions are holomorphic and uniformly bounded there. The multivariable [Cauchy estimate](cauchy-estimate.md) gives uniform factorial [derivative](derivative.md) bounds. For the change $\tilde x=x+y^2-\varepsilon$, the coefficient pullback uses its inverse: $f_\varepsilon(\tilde x,\tilde y)=f(\tilde x-\tilde y^2+\varepsilon,\tilde y)$.
