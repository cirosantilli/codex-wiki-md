# Maximal invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Transformation model](transformation-model.md)

A statistic is invariant if $A(gy)=A(y)$; it is maximal when it also separates different [group orbits](orbit-of-a-group-action.md). Consequently every invariant procedure factors through the [maximal invariant](maximal-invariant.md), subject to the usual measurability conditions on the orbit space. Standardizing a sample by an [equivariant estimator](equivariant-estimator.md) of location and positive scale gives a [maximal invariant](maximal-invariant.md) for the positive affine [group](group-split.md).
