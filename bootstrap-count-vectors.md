# Bootstrap count vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Bootstrap sample](bootstrap-sample.md)

An unordered nonparametric [bootstrap sample](bootstrap-sample.md) from $n$ distinct observations is specified by its $n$ nonnegative multiplicities. [Stars and bars](stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md) gives $\binom{2n-1}{n}$ such vectors. They are not equiprobable: a vector has [probability](probability.md) $n!/(n^n\prod_iN_i!)$, because this counts its ordered sequences of sampled indices.
