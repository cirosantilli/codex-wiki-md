# Left bit shift

↑ **Parent:** [Bit shift](bit-shift.md)

A [bit shift](bit-shift.md) toward more significant positions, filling low positions with zeros. On an unbounded nonnegative [integer](integer-split.md), shifting left by $k$ places multiplies it by $2^k$; fixed-width representations may discard overflowing high bits.
