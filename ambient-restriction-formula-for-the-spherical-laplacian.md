# Ambient restriction formula for the spherical Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of the Laplacian on a sphere](spectrum-of-the-laplacian-on-a-sphere.md)

For a smooth ambient function near the unit [sphere](sphere.md), the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) along the [great circles](great-circle.md) $\gamma_i(t)=p\cos t+e_i\sin t$ gives $(F\circ\gamma_i)''(0)=D^2F(e_i,e_i)-DF(p)$. Sum over an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of the [tangent space](tangent-space.md) and supplement it by the radial vector $p$. The [geodesic trace formula for the Laplace-Beltrami operator](geodesic-trace-formula-for-the-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) then gives the displayed formula, evaluated at radius one. In particular a degree-$\ell$ [harmonic polynomial](harmonic-polynomial.md) restricts to a [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) with positive [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\ell(\ell+d-1)$.
