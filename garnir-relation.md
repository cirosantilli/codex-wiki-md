# Garnir relation

↑ **Parent:** [Standard polytabloid basis](standard-polytabloid-basis.md)

Take two adjacent columns of a numbered [Young tableau](young-tableau.md), with a bottom segment $A$ of the left column and a top segment $B$ of the right column such that $|A|+|B|$ exceeds the height of the left column. Alternating over [permutations](permutation.md) of $A\cup B$, modulo [permutations](permutation.md) preserving $A$ and $B$ separately, gives a signed relation among [polytabloids](polytabloid.md). The full alternation vanishes because each [tabloid](tabloid.md) has two of these entries in one row. Solving for a nonstandard [polytabloid](polytabloid.md) replaces its offending row inversion by earlier column-standard tableaux. The relation is proved over the free integral [tabloid](tabloid.md) lattice, where cancellation and division by nonzero integer factors are valid; reduction then makes it valid in every characteristic. Together with column antisymmetry these relations give the [standard polytabloid basis](standard-polytabloid-basis.md).
