# Pigeon breast muscle evidence for a metabolic cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Citric acid cycle](citric-acid-cycle.md)

[Hans Adolf Krebs](hans-adolf-krebs.md) described the experimental reasoning in his 1953 Nobel lecture. Pigeon breast [skeletal muscle](skeletal-muscle.md) retained strong oxidative activity after preparation in a Latapie mincing mill and suspension in aqueous medium. Small [fumarate](fumarate.md) additions increased [oxygen](oxygen.md) uptake by more than complete [oxidation](oxidation.md) of the added [fumarate](fumarate.md) could explain; added four-[carbon](carbon.md) acids could still be detected afterward. His Table 1 compares the increase in uptake with that calculated from complete consumption of the addition. These comparisons challenge the interpretation that the addition merely supplied extra fuel.[The citric acid cycle, Nobel Lecture, 11 December 1953, printed pp. 400–401 and Table 1: minced-muscle preparation, uptake comparison and retained acid; authored transcript also available at [https://studyres.com/doc/1333386/hans-a.-krebs---nobel-lecture](https://studyres.com/doc/1333386/hans-a.-krebs---nobel-lecture)](the-citric-acid-cycle-by-hans-adolf-krebs.md)

The lecture identifies the preparation apparatus but does not describe the gas-measuring instrument behind Table 1. The result supports regeneration, not a unique complete [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md). Its later discussion combines [citrate](citrate.md) formation from [oxaloacetate](oxaloacetate.md) with breakdown back toward the four-[carbon](carbon.md) intermediates to close the cycle. [Competitive inhibition](competitive-inhibition.md) supplies another comparison: [malonate](malonate.md) inhibits [succinate dehydrogenase](succinate-dehydrogenase.md), and [succinate](succinate.md) accumulates. Such evidence links a blocked step to the surrounding [metabolic pathway](metabolic-pathway.md); damaged tissue and restricted inhibitor access can complicate interpretation.[The citric acid cycle, printed pp. 401–404, Figures 1–2 and malonate discussion: cyclic interpretation and inhibitor-access limitation; p. 399, preparation damage](the-citric-acid-cycle-by-hans-adolf-krebs.md)

An increase above the added fuel's own [oxygen](oxygen.md) demand is a particularly revealing control on the explanation: it compares the proposed cause with the largest effect that simple consumption could produce. The modern [mitochondrial oxygen electrode assay](mitochondrial-oxygen-electrode-assay.md) asks a different question about a prepared [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md), testing [respiratory control](respiratory-control.md) through [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) additions. Neither an [oxygen](oxygen.md) uptake signal nor an accumulating intermediate is by itself a direct measurement of [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) production.
