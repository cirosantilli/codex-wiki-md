# Isothermal vertical structure in stellar gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical structure of an astrophysical disk](vertical-structure-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md)

For a [thin disk](thin-disk.md) in the harmonic vertical [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) $\Omega_K^2z^2/2$, [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) and $p=c_s^2\rho$ with height-independent [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md) give the displayed Gaussian density. For perturbations with [adiabatic index](heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma$, the [Brunt–Väisälä frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) is $N^2=(1-1/\gamma)\Omega_K^2z^2/H^2$. An isothermal background can therefore support buoyancy for adiabatic perturbations; isothermal perturbations instead have $\gamma=1$ and zero buoyancy frequency.
