# Odds

↑ **Parent:** [Probability](probability.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odds)

For an [event](event.md) of [probability](probability.md) $0<p<1$, its odds in favor are $o=p/(1-p)$. They compare the [probability](probability.md) of the [event](event.md) with that of its [complement](complement-of-a-set.md). Conversely $p=o/(1+o)$. For example, [probability](probability.md) $1/4$ gives [odds](odds.md) $1/3$, or one to three in favor. The same transformation applies to a [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md); an [odds ratio](odds-ratio.md) compares these transformed [probabilities](probability.md) in two groups, while a [risk ratio](risk-ratio.md) compares the [probabilities](probability.md) themselves.
