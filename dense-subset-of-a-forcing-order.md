# Dense subset of a forcing order

↑ **Parent:** [Forcing](forcing-split.md)

A subset $D$ of a [forcing](forcing-split.md) order $P$ is dense if every $p\in P$ has a stronger condition $q\leq p$ in $D$. This order-density condition is the one used by a [generic filter](generic-filter.md); it is distinct from density in an arbitrarily specified topology.
