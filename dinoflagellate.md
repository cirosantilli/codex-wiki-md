# Dinoflagellate

↑ **Parent:** [Eukaryote](eukaryote.md)

A [dinoflagellate](dinoflagellate.md) belongs to a eukaryotic lineage notable for unusual nuclear chromosome organization. Many species have persistently condensed DNA with low bulk histone abundance, while retained histones and alternative nucleoproteins caution against a universal histone-free description.
