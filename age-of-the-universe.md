# Age of the universe

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_the_universe)

The [age of the universe](age-of-the-universe.md) is elapsed cosmic time since the early hot Big Bang phase, inferred within a cosmological model from expansion and matter-content measurements. The [age of an FLRW universe](age-of-an-flrw-universe.md) is the corresponding model computation from its scale-factor evolution.
