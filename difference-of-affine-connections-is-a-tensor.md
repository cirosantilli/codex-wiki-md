# Difference of affine connections is a tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Affine connection](affine-connection.md)

The difference of two [affine connections](affine-connection.md) is a smooth [tensor field](tensor-field.md) of type $(1,2)$. Linearity over [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) holds in the first slot by the connection axioms. In the second slot, the two extra terms $X(f)Y$ cancel, so $\Delta(X,fY)=f\Delta(X,Y)$. This [tensoriality](tensoriality.md) makes the value depend only on $X_p,Y_p$. In coordinates its components are the differences of the [Christoffel symbols](christoffel-symbol.md), although either collection of connection coefficients individually is not a tensor.
