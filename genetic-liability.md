# Genetic liability

↑ **Parent:** [Psychiatric genetics](psychiatric-genetics.md)

The inherited component of vulnerability to an outcome under a specified model and environment. Liability is probabilistic; genetic association does not imply inevitable disease or identify an individual's exact onset time.
