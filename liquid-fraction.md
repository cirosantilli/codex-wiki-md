# Liquid fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Solid fraction](solid-fraction.md)

The liquid fraction is the volume fraction occupied by liquid in a solid–liquid mixture. For a saturated rigid [mushy layer](mushy-layer.md), it is the [porosity](porosity.md) available to [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md).
