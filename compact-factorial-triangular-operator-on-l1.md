# Compact factorial triangular operator on l1

↑ **Parent:** [Compact operator](compact-operator-split.md)

The operator in the title is bounded and compact on the [absolutely summable sequence space](absolutely-summable-sequence-space.md). The [norm](norm.md) of a [matrix](matrix.md) operator on $\ell^1$ is the supremum of its absolute column sums: the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) gives one bound and coordinate unit vectors give the other. Here the column sum is $c_m=\sum_{n=1}^{m-1}n!/m!\leq2/m$, using $\sum_{n=1}^kn!\leq2k!$. The latter inequality follows by induction. Retaining the first $N$ columns gives a [finite-rank operator](finite-rank-operator.md) and a remainder of [norm](norm.md) at most $2/(N+1)$. A [norm](norm.md) limit of finite-rank operators is a [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md), proving the assertion. This column-decay method can prove compactness even when each row has infinitely many nonzero entries.
