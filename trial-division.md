# Trial division

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_division)

[Trial division](trial-division.md) searches for [divisors](divisor.md) of an [integer](integer.md) by testing candidate divisions. For [primality testing](primality-testing.md) of an [integer](integer.md) $n>1$, testing the [prime numbers](prime-number.md) up to $\sqrt n$ suffices: any [composite number](composite-number.md) has a [prime factor](prime-factor.md) in that range. After finding a [prime factor](prime-factor.md), repeated [division](division-mathematics.md) removes its [prime power](prime-power.md), and testing the remaining quotient gives an elementary [integer factorization](integer-factorization.md) method. This is useful for small inputs, but the number of candidates can grow rapidly with the input's digit count.
