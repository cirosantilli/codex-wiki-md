# Euclidean worldline path integral

↑ **Parent:** [Path integral](path-integral.md)

A thermal [path integral](path-integral.md) represents the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) by paths periodic in Euclidean time of circumference $\beta$. For $H=p^2/2+V(x)$, its formal action is $S_E[x]=\int_0^\beta(\dot x^2/2+V(x))\,d\tau$. The [periodic boundary condition](periodic-boundary-conditions.md) results from taking the trace of the evolution kernel. The continuum measure requires a regularization and normalization; finite-dimensional measures make both choices explicit.
