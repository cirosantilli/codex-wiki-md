# Laplacian spectrum of a complete graph

↑ **Parent:** [Laplacian matrix](laplacian-matrix.md)

The [Graph Laplacian](laplacian-matrix.md) of the [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $K_n$ has eigenvalue zero on the constant vector and eigenvalue $n$ on the $(n-1)$-dimensional subspace whose coordinates sum to zero.
