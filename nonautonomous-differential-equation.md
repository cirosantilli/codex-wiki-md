# Nonautonomous differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

A [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) whose evolution rule depends explicitly on time. A time-varying coefficient or [forcing term](forcing-term.md) can make a system nonautonomous. Unlike an [autonomous differential equation](autonomous-system-mathematics.md), the state alone need not determine its future without the current time.
