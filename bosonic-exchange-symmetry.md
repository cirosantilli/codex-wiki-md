# Bosonic exchange symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Boson](boson.md)

[Bosonic exchange symmetry](bosonic-exchange-symmetry.md) is the symmetric exchange statistics of identical [bosons](boson.md). Commuting [creation operators](creation-operator.md) build the symmetric [bosonic Fock space](bosonic-fock-space.md); a normalized one-particle mode has occupation states $(a^\dagger)^n|0\rangle/\sqrt{n!}$ for every nonnegative integer $n$. At thermal equilibrium this symmetry leads to [Bose-Einstein statistics](bose-einstein-statistics.md) and the [Bose-Einstein distribution](bose-einstein-distribution.md). Exchange symmetry itself does not require thermal equilibrium.
