# Parabolic partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order partial differential equation](second-order-partial-differential-equation.md)

In two independent variables, a scalar second-order [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) with a nonzero rank-one [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md). Its two characteristic directions coincide. The [heat equation](heat-equation.md) is the basic example.
