# Thermal free-free emissivity

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md)

The emission coefficient per volume, [frequency](frequency.md) and [solid angle](solid-angle.md) of a Maxwellian [plasma](plasma-physics.md) has $j_\nu\propto n_e\sum_i Z_i^2 n_i\,T^{-1/2}e^{-h\nu/(k_BT)}\bar g_{\rm ff}$. Define the angular convention before supplying a numerical coefficient. The high-frequency cutoff measures [temperature](temperature-split.md), while the normalization measures a density-squared emission integral. The radio spectrum is almost flat when optically thin and rises as $\nu^2$ when optically thick at fixed projected area.
