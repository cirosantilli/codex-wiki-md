# Polynomial sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Factorization of polynomials](factorization-of-polynomials.md)

A sieve that marks reducible polynomials by their divisibility by smaller irreducible polynomials. It is the polynomial-ring analogue of a [number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md).
