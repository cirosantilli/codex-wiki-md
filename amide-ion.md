# Amide ion

↑ **Parent:** [Anion](anion.md)

The [anion](anion.md) $\mathrm{NH_2^-}$ is the conjugate base of [ammonia](ammonia.md). It is chemically distinct from an organic [amide](amide.md) functional group and can deprotonate suitable [carbon acids](carbon-acid.md).
