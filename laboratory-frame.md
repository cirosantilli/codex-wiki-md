# Laboratory frame

↑ **Parent:** [Reference frame](reference-frame.md)

The [laboratory frame](laboratory-frame.md) is the [reference frame](reference-frame.md) in which an experiment's measuring apparatus is at rest. It need not be the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md): a target at rest in the laboratory can be struck by a projectile carrying nonzero total [momentum](momentum-split.md).
