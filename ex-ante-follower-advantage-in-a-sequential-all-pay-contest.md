# Ex ante follower advantage in a sequential all-pay contest

↑ **Parent:** [Sequential private-value all-pay contest](sequential-private-value-all-pay-contest.md)

Every optimal leader bid satisfies $b(v)\leq v$, because bidding zero guarantees payoff zero and winning probability is at most one. Hence $F(b(v))\leq F(v)$. With identically distributed independent continuous valuations, the [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md) gives $\mathbb E F(V)=1/2$. The leader's unconditional winning probability is therefore at most $1/2$, even when some high leader types have a conditional advantage. This argument does not depend on selecting a unique optimum.
