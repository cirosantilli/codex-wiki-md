<h1 id="conic-caratheodory-theorem">Conic Carathéodory theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Carathéodory's theorem (convex hull)](caratheodory-s-theorem-convex-hull.md)

In an $N$-dimensional real [vector space](vector-space-split.md), each member of a [conic hull](conic-hull.md) has a representation using at most $N$ generators. To prove it, take a finite positive-coefficient representation with more than $N$ terms. Its generators are linearly dependent, say $\sum_j\mu_js_j=0$, with some $\mu_j>0$ after reversing the relation if necessary. Subtract $t\mu_j$ from each [coefficient](coefficient.md), taking $t=\min_{\mu_j>0}\lambda_j/\mu_j$. All [coefficients](coefficient.md) stay nonnegative and at least one vanishes. Iterate. The bound differs from the $N+1$ bound for a [convex hull](convex-hull.md) because the [coefficient](coefficient.md) sum is unrestricted.
