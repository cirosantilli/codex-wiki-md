# Semi-infinite Eady edge wave

↑ **Parent:** [Semi-infinite Eady model](semi-infinite-eady-model.md)

A lower-boundary [Eady edge wave](eady-edge-wave.md) in the [semi-infinite Eady model](semi-infinite-eady-model.md) has a smooth exponentially decaying vertical structure and a real frequency. It is supported by the horizontal boundary [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) gradient while its interior [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) anomaly vanishes. Its speed equals the basic velocity at one penetration depth.
