# Direct band gap

↑ **Parent:** [Band gap](band-gap.md)

A [band gap](band-gap.md) whose [conduction band](conduction-band.md) minimum and [valence band](valence-band.md) maximum occur at the same [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md), allowing an optical transition without a momentum-balancing [phonon](phonon.md).
