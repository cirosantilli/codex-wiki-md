# Genetic background

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)

The [genetic background](genetic-background.md) is the rest of the [genotype](genotype.md) against which a focal [allele](allele.md) or [gene deletion](gene-deletion.md) is studied. Other [genes](gene.md) can influence the resulting [phenotype](phenotype.md), so comparisons should specify which background is held fixed.
