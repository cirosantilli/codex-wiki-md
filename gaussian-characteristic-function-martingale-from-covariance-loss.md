# Gaussian characteristic-function martingale from covariance loss

↑ **Parent:** [SLE4 coupling with a Gaussian free field](sle4-coupling-with-a-gaussian-free-field.md)

If a continuous mean [martingale](martingale-split.md) $M$ and a nonnegative finite-variation process $V$ satisfy $d\langle M\rangle=-dV$, then the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) shows $\exp(iM-V/2)$ is a bounded complex [martingale](martingale-split.md). Its terminal expectation establishes an entire [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md) with the initial mean and variance. This is stronger than matching only the first two moments in a random-domain field construction.
