# Precipitation hardening

↑ **Parent:** [Alloy](alloy.md)

Heat treatment that develops a fine dispersion of a new [solid](solid.md) phase in an [alloy](alloy.md), impeding [dislocation](dislocation.md) motion. It is distinct from precipitation from a liquid [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). A [solid solution](solid-solution.md) may first be formed at high temperature, quenched to retain supersaturation, and then aged to promote solid-state precipitation.
