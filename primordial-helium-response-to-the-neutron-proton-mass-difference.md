# Primordial helium response to the neutron-proton mass difference

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial helium mass fraction](primordial-helium-mass-fraction.md)

At fixed [cosmological weak freeze-out](cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md) temperature $T_f$ and fixed neutron survival factor, [neutron-proton chemical equilibrium](neutron-proton-chemical-equilibrium.md) gives $r_f=e^{-Q/T_f}$ and [neutron-limited helium synthesis](neutron-limited-helium-synthesis.md) gives $Y_p\propto2r_f/(1+r_f)$. Differentiating yields the displayed sensitivity. A finite change $Q\mapsto(1+\alpha)Q$ gives $Y_p'/Y_p=(1+e^{Q/T_f})/(1+e^{(1+\alpha)Q/T_f})$. Increasing the positive mass difference reduces helium production. Changes to the weak conversion rates, neutron lifetime or nuclear history must be supplied separately for a full abundance prediction.
