# Failed supernova

↑ **Parent:** [Core-collapse supernova](core-collapse-supernova.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Failed_supernova)

Core collapse can fail to produce an ordinary successful ejecting [supernova](supernova.md), leaving a [black hole](black-hole.md) and retaining much of the processed core. Some envelope material can still escape, depending on the event; failure is not equivalent to a universal zero-yield prescription.
