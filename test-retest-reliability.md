# Test-retest reliability

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement reliability](measurement-reliability.md)

Consistency of scores obtained from the same units on different occasions, under a justified interval and conditions. A low value may reflect measurement error, genuine change or both; the intended stability of the construct matters.
