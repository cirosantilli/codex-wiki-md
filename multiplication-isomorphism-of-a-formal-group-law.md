# Multiplication isomorphism of a formal group law

↑ **Parent:** [Invertible morphism criterion for formal group laws](invertible-morphism-criterion-for-formal-group-laws.md)

For a one-dimensional [formal group law](formal-group-law.md) $F$ over a ring $R$, the multiplication series satisfies $[n]_F(T)=nT+O(T^2)$. It is an isomorphism exactly when $n$ is a unit of $R$.
