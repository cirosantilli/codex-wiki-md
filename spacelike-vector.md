# Spacelike vector

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md)

With [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, a nonzero [vector](vector.md) $v$ is spacelike when its [Lorentzian inner product](lorentzian-inner-product.md) with itself satisfies $g(v,v)>0$. In [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), this means its spatial components have larger squared Euclidean length than its time component. The classification is preserved by [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md). A [timelike vector](timelike-vector.md) has negative squared [Lorentzian inner product](lorentzian-inner-product.md), and a [null vector](null-vector.md) has zero squared [Lorentzian inner product](lorentzian-inner-product.md).
