# Lace expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation theory](percolation-theory.md)

An iterative expansion of long-range connectivity functions that separates an approximately random-walk contribution from corrections caused by loops and intersections. In suitable high-dimensional [percolation](percolation-theory.md) models it controls these corrections and proves [mean-field percolation exponents](mean-field-percolation-exponents.md). It also applies to other models, including [self-avoiding walks](self-avoiding-walk.md); its use always requires model-specific estimates.
