# Zig-zag step

↑ **Parent:** [Splaying](splaying.md)

When a node and its parent occupy opposite left/right directions, rotate the selected node above its parent and then above its grandparent. These two [tree rotations](tree-rotation.md) move the selected node two levels upward while preserving the [binary search tree](binary-search-tree.md) ordering.
