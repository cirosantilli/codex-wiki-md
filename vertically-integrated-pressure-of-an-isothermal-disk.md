# Vertically integrated pressure of an isothermal disk

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal vertical structure in stellar gravity](isothermal-vertical-structure-in-stellar-gravity.md)

A locally isothermal disk in vertically harmonic external gravity has [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) $c_s^2\rho_z=-\Omega_z^2z\rho$, hence $\rho=\rho_0e^{-z^2/(2H^2)}$ with $H=c_s/\Omega_z$. Because $c_s$ is independent of height, integrating $p=c_s^2\rho$ gives the displayed pressure. The harmonic-gravity approximation has the accuracy of the [thin disk](thin-disk.md) expansion.
