# Conditional entropy of finite measurable partitions

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy of a finite measurable partition](entropy-of-a-finite-measurable-partition.md)

For finite partitions $\xi,\eta$,

$$
H_\mu(\xi\mid\eta)
=\sum_{B\in\eta}\mu(B)H_\mu(\xi\mid B)
=H_\mu(\xi\vee\eta)-H_\mu(\eta).
$$

[Conditioning reduces entropy](conditioning-reduces-entropy.md), so $H_\mu(\xi\mid\eta)\leq H_\mu(\xi)$.
