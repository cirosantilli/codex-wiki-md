# Stirling correction by Gaussian moments

↑ **Parent:** [Stirling formula](stirling-formula.md)

In [Laplace method](laplace-s-method.md) for the [Gamma function](gamma-function.md), scaling $t=x(1+s/\sqrt x)$ gives a standard [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md). The first even correction polynomial is $s^2-7s^4/12+s^6/18$. Its expectation under a unit-variance centered Gaussian is $1-7\cdot3/12+15/18=1/12$. The amplitude expansion contributes to this coefficient as well as the phase expansion; expanding only the latter gives the wrong [Stirling formula](stirling-formula.md) correction.
