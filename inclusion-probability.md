# Inclusion probability

↑ **Parent:** [Probability sampling](probability-sampling.md)

An [inclusion probability](inclusion-probability.md) is the [probability](probability.md) that a particular unit belongs to the selected sample. For [sample size](sample-size.md) $n$ in [simple random sampling](simple-random-sampling.md) from $N$ labels it is $n/N$. It differs from the selection [probability](probability.md) at a particular draw position.
