# Standard basis

↑ **Parent:** [Basis](basis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_basis)

The standard basis of the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $\mathbb R^n$ consists of the [vectors](vector.md) $e_i$ whose $i$th coordinate is one and whose other coordinates are zero. Every [vector](vector.md) $x$ has the unique expression $x=\sum_i x_ie_i$ in this [basis](basis.md).
