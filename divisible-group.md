# Divisible group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisible_group)

A divisible group is an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $G$ in which, for every positive integer $n$ and every $g\in G$, some $h\in G$ satisfies $nh=g$.
