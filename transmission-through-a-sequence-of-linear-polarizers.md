# Transmission through a sequence of linear polarizers

↑ **Parent:** [Malus's law](malus-s-law.md)

For ideal [polarizers](polarizer.md) with successive transverse transmission directions $\theta_j$, the [Jones matrix](jones-matrix.md) is the rank-one [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) $P_j=\mathbf u_j\mathbf u_j^{\mathsf T}$. The identity $\mathbf u_j^{\mathsf T}\mathbf u_{j-1}=\cos(\theta_j-\theta_{j-1})$ reduces the transmitted [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) analogue, here the optical [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md), to a product of projections. For [unpolarized light](unpolarized-light.md), the first element transmits half the incident [light intensity](light-intensity.md), giving the displayed product. For a specified incident [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md), replace the first half-factor with its actual projection.
