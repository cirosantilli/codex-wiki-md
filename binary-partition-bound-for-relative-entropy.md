# Binary partition bound for relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md)

For a measurable event $A$, applying [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) to $u\log u$ separately on $A$ and its complement shows that the [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) between two [probability measures](probability-measure.md) is at least the divergence between the two event indicators. This reduces a general [probability measure](probability-measure.md) comparison to a binary one and is the key step in a proof of [Pinsker's inequality](pinsker-s-inequality.md).
