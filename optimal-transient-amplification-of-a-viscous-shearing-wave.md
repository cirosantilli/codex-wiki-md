# Optimal transient amplification of a viscous shearing wave

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional viscous shearing wave](two-dimensional-viscous-shearing-wave.md)

For the shearing-wave [energy](energy.md) profile, stationary points satisfy $-2\mathrm{Re}\,T=(1+T^2)^2$. At large [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md), the initial minimum is at $T_-\sim-(2\mathrm{Re})^{1/3}$ and the subsequent maximum is at $T_+\sim-1/(2\mathrm{Re})$. Their [energy](energy.md) ratio is $G_{\max}\sim(2\mathrm{Re})^{2/3}e^{-2/3}$. The [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) here uses $A$, half the background shear rate; using $2A$ instead changes the coefficient convention.
