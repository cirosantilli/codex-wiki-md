# Separation of a two-dimensional central-potential eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational invariance of a central-potential Hamiltonian](rotational-invariance-of-a-central-potential-hamiltonian.md)

An energy and [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) for a real two-dimensional [central potential](central-potential.md) can be chosen with integer $k$ and real radial function $f$. Its radial equation is

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\left(f''+r^{-1}f'-k^2r^{-2}f\right)+Vf=Ef,\qquad \int_0^\infty r f^2\,dr=1.
$$

[Quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md) permits other [stationary states](stationary-state.md), including $f(r)\cos(k\phi)/\sqrt\pi$, which are not single angular exponentials. [Separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) supplies a basis rather than describing every superposition in a degenerate eigenspace.
