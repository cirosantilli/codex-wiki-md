# Lifting property of a free module

↑ **Parent:** [Free module](free-module.md)

For a [free module](free-module.md) $F$, a [surjective](surjective-function.md) [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) $\phi:P\to M$, and a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) $\psi:F\to M$, there is a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) $q:F\to P$ satisfying $\phi q=\psi$. Choose a preimage under $\phi$ of the image of each element of a [basis of a module](basis-of-a-module.md) of $F$, and extend by linearity. Thus every [free module](free-module.md) is a [projective module](projective-module.md).
