# Baryon-asymmetry washout

↑ **Parent:** [Baryogenesis](baryogenesis.md)

Inverse decays or number-violating scatterings can erase an asymmetry after its generation. A viable mechanism must generate a surviving charge combination before the relevant washout processes cease to be effective.
