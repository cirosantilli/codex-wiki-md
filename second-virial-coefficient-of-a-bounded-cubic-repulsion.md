# Second virial coefficient of a bounded cubic repulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Second virial coefficient](second-virial-coefficient.md)

For a classical dilute [gas](gas.md) with [pair potential](pair-potential.md) $u(r)=\epsilon[1-(r/a)^3]$ for $r<a$ and zero otherwise, put $x=\epsilon/(k_BT)>0$. Substitution $t=(r/a)^3$ in the [Mayer f-function](mayer-f-function.md) formula gives

$$
B_2=\frac{2\pi a^3}{3}\int_0^1[1-e^{-x(1-t)}]dt
=\frac{2\pi a^3}{3}\left[1-\frac{1-e^{-x}}x\right].
$$

It is positive for finite positive [temperature](temperature-split.md), tends to $2\pi a^3/3$ as $T\to0$ in the classical model, and behaves as $\pi a^3\epsilon/(3k_BT)$ at high [temperature](temperature-split.md). The low-temperature mathematical limit need not remain in the dilute classical regime for a real molecular [gas](gas.md).
