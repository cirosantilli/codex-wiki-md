# Residual mean circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Transformed Eulerian mean](transformed-eulerian-mean.md)

The residual mean circulation is the meridional and vertical circulation used by the [transformed Eulerian mean](transformed-eulerian-mean.md). It combines the Eulerian-mean circulation with an eddy-induced contribution so that mean tracer evolution is advected by a single nondivergent flow.
