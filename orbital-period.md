# Orbital period

↑ **Parent:** [Mean motion](mean-motion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_period)

The orbital period is the time required to complete one orbit. It is related to [mean motion](mean-motion.md) by $T=2\pi/n$.
