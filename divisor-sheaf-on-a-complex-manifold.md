# Divisor sheaf on a complex manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of meromorphic functions on a complex manifold](sheaf-of-meromorphic-functions-on-a-complex-manifold.md)

The quotient sheaf $\mathcal K_X^*/\mathcal O_X^*$ records the zero and pole orders of local nonzero [meromorphic functions](meromorphic-function.md). Each of its [global sections](global-section.md) is naturally a [divisor on a complex manifold](divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md) on $X$.
