# Stable-variable lag in nilpotent center-manifold reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Center manifold](center-manifold.md)

For $\dot z=v/2$, $\dot v=-3v/2+3w$, $\dot w=-(z-v)^3/9$, the stable coordinate is $v-2w$ and the [center manifold](center-manifold.md) begins with $v=2w$. A cubic correction $h_3$ satisfies $w\partial_z h_3-(2/9)(z-2w)^3=-3h_3/2$, giving $h_3=4z^3/27-32z^2w/27+272zw^2/81-832w^3/243$. The derivative of the graph contributes at the same order as the cubic forcing. Simply setting $\dot v=0$ omits this lag and incorrectly changes the cubic damping coefficient of the reduced [normal form](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md).
