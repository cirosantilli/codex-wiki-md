# Decimal digit-sum divisibility test

↑ **Parent:** [Divisibility rule](divisibility-rule.md)

For a nonnegative [integer](integer-split.md) with decimal expansion $a=\sum_i a_i10^i$, the congruence $10\equiv1\pmod d$ gives $a\equiv\sum_i a_i\pmod d$ whenever $d\mid9$. Thus [divisibility](divisibility-split.md) by three or nine is equivalent to [divisibility](divisibility-split.md) of the sum of its [decimal digits](decimal-digit.md) by the same number. More generally a base-$b$ expansion has the analogous rule for every divisor of $b-1$. This follows by repeated addition and multiplication in [modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic-split.md); it does not require an empirical rule about decimal notation.
