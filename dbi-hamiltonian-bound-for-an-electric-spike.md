# DBI Hamiltonian bound for an electric spike

↑ **Parent:** [Born–Infeld soliton](born-infeld-soliton.md)

For a static [D3-brane](d3-brane.md) with one scalar and no magnetic field, taking the Legendre transform of the [DBI action](dirac-born-infeld-action.md) gives the displayed identity. It bounds energy by the electric scalar charge and is saturated at $\mathbf d=\nabla X$. The opposite orientation uses the analogous identity with reversed signs. On saturation, the constitutive relation gives $\mathbf E=\mathbf d$ and the [Gauss law constraint in gauge theory](gauss-law-constraint-in-gauge-theory.md) reduces to the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md) for $X$.
