# Clock jitter

↑ **Parent:** [Signal processing](signal-processing.md)

A fluctuation in the time at which a [signal](signal.md) is sampled produces an amplitude error proportional to its local time [derivative](derivative.md). For a [sinusoid](sinusoid.md), the characteristic fractional error grows with the product of [frequency](frequency.md) and timing uncertainty. Sampling-time uncertainty is distinct from [uniform quantization error](uniform-quantization-error.md).
