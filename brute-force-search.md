# Brute-force search

↑ **Parent:** [Algorithm](algorithm-split.md)

A search [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) that tests candidates directly, without exploiting enough structure to avoid their enumeration. A small brute-force implementation is often useful for checking an optimized [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) independently.
