# General position for projective lines

↑ **Parent:** [General linear position](general-linear-position.md)

Distinct lines in the [projective plane](projective-plane.md) are in general position when no three are concurrent. Their union is a [reduced plane curve](reduced-plane-curve.md) with exactly $\binom d2$ [singular points](singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md), all ordinary pairwise crossings, if there are $d$ lines. An explicit family is $y=t_i x+t_i^2z$ for distinct complex $t_i$: the intersection of the $i$th and $j$th lines is $[-t_i-t_j:-t_it_j:1]$, and substituting it into a third equation gives $-(t_k-t_i)(t_k-t_j)\ne0$.
