# Tomography

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomography)

Tomography reconstructs an interior distribution from measurements along multiple lines or paths through it. A linear unattenuated model is the [Radon transform](radon-transform.md); absorption changes it to an [attenuated Radon transform](attenuated-radon-transform.md).
