# Logic and Proof examination paper of the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The four-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) Finals [computer science](computer-science-split.md) examination held on 10 June 2015, Trinity Term 2015. Public primary-source archive: [Lawrence Attachment.zip](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `2015-landp.pdf`. Printed p. 4, Question 3(d), permits an arbitrary [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md) in an asserted [least Herbrand model](least-herbrand-model.md) equivalence; that assertion fails when negative atoms are allowed, even for a satisfiable [Horn clause](horn-clause.md).
