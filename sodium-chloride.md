# Sodium chloride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodium_chloride)

[Sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) contains [sodium](sodium.md) [cations](cation.md) $\mathrm{Na}^+$ and [chlorine](chlorine.md) [anions](anion.md) $\mathrm{Cl}^-$ in a $1:1$ ratio; its [formula unit](formula-unit.md) has zero net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md).
