# Insulin receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Receptor tyrosine kinase](receptor-tyrosine-kinase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insulin_receptor)

The mature [insulin receptor](insulin-receptor.md) already contains two extracellular $\alpha$ chains and two membrane-spanning $\beta$ chains, joined through [protein disulfide bonds](protein-disulfide-bond.md), before [insulin](insulin.md) binds. [Insulin](insulin.md) induces rearrangement of this $(\alpha\beta)_2$ assembly. Partner-mediated [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) of the inhibitory activation loop permits increased [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) activity. Ligand-induced creation of a previously absent dimer is therefore an unsuitable universal explanation.[“Mechanisms of Receptor Activation” and “TKD autoinhibition by the activation loop: insulin and FGF receptors”, Figure 2E](cell-signaling-by-receptor-tyrosine-kinases-by-mark-a-lemmon-and-joseph-schlessinger.md)
