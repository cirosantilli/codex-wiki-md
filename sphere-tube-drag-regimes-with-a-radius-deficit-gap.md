# Sphere-tube drag regimes with a radius-deficit gap

↑ **Parent:** [Three drag regimes for a nearly occluding sphere](three-drag-regimes-for-a-nearly-occluding-sphere.md)

Use radius-deficit $\epsilon$, $F^*=F/(6\pi\mu aU)$ and $\delta=3a/(4L)$. The [control-volume drag formula for a sphere in a tube](control-volume-drag-formula-for-a-sphere-in-a-tube.md) and the [radius-deficit normalization of sphere-tube lubrication integrals](radius-deficit-normalization-of-sphere-tube-lubrication-integrals.md) give three leading resistances: $c_3\epsilon^{-5/2}$ for $\delta\ll\epsilon^{5/2}$, $1/\delta$ for $\epsilon^{5/2}\ll\delta\ll\epsilon^{1/2}$, and $2\pi\sqrt2/(3\sqrt\epsilon)$ for $\delta\gg\epsilon^{1/2}$. They correspond to gap bypass with almost no tube throughflow, [pressure](pressure.md) resistance of tube throughflow, and local annular resistance, respectively.
