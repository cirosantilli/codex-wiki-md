# Brownian portfolio exposures

↑ **Parent:** [Investment portfolio](investment-portfolio.md)

Brownian portfolio exposures are the coefficients $y_j$ of independent [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md) in the wealth equation. An invertible asset volatility matrix lets one optimize directly over these exposures. The drift risk premium is then the dot product of exposures with the [market price of risk](market-price-of-risk.md) vector.
