# Fold point of a critical manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Critical manifold](critical-manifold.md)

For one fast and one slow variable, a nondegenerate [fold point of a critical manifold](fold-point-of-a-critical-manifold.md) satisfies $F=F_x=0$ and $F_{xx}F_y\ne0$. An attracting and a repelling branch of fast [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) meet there. A slow trajectory reaching the fold generally leaves by a rapid jump when its slow drift crosses the fold transversely. The fold is defined relative to the fast direction: if fast trajectories are slanted in the plotted coordinates, their tangency to the [critical manifold](critical-manifold.md) need not be an extremum of its plotted height.
