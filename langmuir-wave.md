# Langmuir wave

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma oscillation](plasma-oscillation.md)

A longitudinal electron [plasma oscillation](plasma-oscillation.md) whose long-wavelength [frequency](frequency.md) approaches the electron [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md) when ions can be treated as stationary. Its [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) and [Landau damping](landau-damping.md) depend on the electron [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md). Compact velocity support can permit exactly undamped branches outside the support.
