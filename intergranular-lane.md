# Intergranular lane

↑ **Parent:** [Solar granulation](solar-granulation.md)

An intergranular lane is a region between [solar granules](solar-granule.md), commonly associated with cooler material and descending flow. Horizontal convergence can concentrate approximately vertical [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) there. The field's geometry, diffusivity and feedback determine the actual concentration rather than the lane label alone.
