# Fourier transform of a tempered distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md)

With $\widehat\varphi(\xi)=\int e^{-ix\cdot\xi}\varphi(x)\,dx$, the Fourier transform of a [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md) is defined by

$$
\langle\widehat u,\varphi\rangle=\langle u,\widehat\varphi\rangle.
$$

Continuity of the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) on the [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md) makes this well defined. For regular integrable functions, [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) shows that it agrees with the ordinary transform. The inverse convention has factor $(2\pi)^{-n}$ and the positive exponential.
