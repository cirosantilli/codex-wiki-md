# Synchronous coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

In [synchronous coordinates](synchronous-coordinates.md), fixed spatial-coordinate worldlines are [timelike geodesics](timelike-geodesic.md), and $t$ is their [proper time](proper-time.md). The metric has unit [lapse function](lapse-function.md) and zero [shift vector](shift-vector.md) in time units consistent with the displayed factor $c^2$. Indeed $g_{tt}=c^2$ and $g_{ti}=0$ give $\Gamma^\mu{}_{tt}=0$. Locally, an irrotational freely falling congruence permits this construction before crossings of the coordinate worldlines. [Comoving observers](comoving-observer.md) in a [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) provide the standard cosmological example.
