# Statistical equilibrium of atomic level populations

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)

An atomic population is in [statistical equilibrium](statistical-equilibrium-of-atomic-level-populations.md) when the total rate into each level equals the total rate out, subject to conservation of the total number of atoms or ions. The rates can include electron-impact transitions and [spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md). This is weaker than thermodynamic equilibrium: a collisionally excited, radiatively decaying dilute gas generally does not have Boltzmann level populations.
