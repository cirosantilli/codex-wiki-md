# Ds meson

↑ **Parent:** [D meson](d-meson.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D_s_meson)

A charmed strange [meson](meson.md). Its ground-state charged particle has valence content $c\bar s$ and spin-parity $0^-$. Its [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) has the conjugate valence content. Nonleptonic decays are governed by [weak charged currents](charged-current.md) and hadronic transition matrix elements.
