# Weierstrass gap

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md)

At a point $p$ of a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md), a positive integer $n$ is a Weierstrass gap when no [rational function](rational-function.md) has a pole of order exactly $n$ at $p$ and no other poles. Equivalently,

$$
\ell(np)=\ell((n-1)p).
$$
