# Weyl theorem for compact self-adjoint perturbations

↑ **Parent:** [Essential spectrum of a bounded self-adjoint operator](essential-spectrum-of-a-bounded-self-adjoint-operator.md)

If $L$ is bounded and self-adjoint on a complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) and $K$ is compact and self-adjoint, then

$$
\Sigma_{\mathrm e}(L+K)=\Sigma_{\mathrm e}(L).
$$

A [singular Weyl sequence](singular-weyl-sequence.md) for $L$ stays singular for $L+K$, because [compact operators send weak convergence to norm convergence](compact-operators-send-weak-convergence-to-norm-convergence.md) and therefore $Kf_n\to0$. Applying the same argument with $-K$ proves the reverse inclusion. Finite-multiplicity isolated [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) can move under such perturbations; the [essential spectrum of a bounded self-adjoint operator](essential-spectrum-of-a-bounded-self-adjoint-operator.md) is unchanged.
