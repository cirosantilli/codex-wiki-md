# Cubic stationary endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md)

If the first two phase [derivatives](derivative.md) vanish at an endpoint but the third does not, an oscillatory contribution is concentrated on a width proportional to $N^{-1/3}$. For a smooth nonzero endpoint [oscillatory integral amplitude](amplitude-of-an-oscillatory-integral.md), rescaling reduces the leading contribution to a cubic oscillatory integral. Its constant follows from the [Gamma function](gamma-function.md) Fourier integral. This is a degenerate form of the [stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md), and a quadratic formula is insufficient.
