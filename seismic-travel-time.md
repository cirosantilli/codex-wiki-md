# Seismic travel time

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic ray theory](seismic-ray-theory.md)

The time for a specified [seismic phase](seismic-phase.md) to propagate along its ray. Arrival time additionally includes [earthquake origin time](earthquake-origin-time.md); those quantities must not be interchanged on an absolute-clock [seismogram](seismogram.md).
