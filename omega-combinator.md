# Omega combinator

↑ **Parent:** [Combinator](combinator.md)

The omega combinator is the divergent [untyped lambda calculus](untyped-lambda-calculus.md) term

$$
\Omega=(\lambda x.xx)(\lambda x.xx),
$$

whose only [beta reduction](beta-reduction.md) reproduces $\Omega$.
