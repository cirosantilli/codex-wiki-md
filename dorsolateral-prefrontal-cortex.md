# Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex

↑ **Parent:** [Prefrontal cortex](prefrontal-cortex.md)

A lateral prefrontal region studied extensively in delayed-response tasks. Activity can carry remembered stimulus and anticipated response information; a task must separate them before assigning a uniquely mnemonic function.
