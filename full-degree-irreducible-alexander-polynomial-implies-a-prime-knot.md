# Full-degree irreducible Alexander polynomial implies a prime knot

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander breadth bound on Seifert genus](alexander-breadth-bound-on-seifert-genus.md)

Assume $g_s(K)>0$. A nontrivial [connected sum of knots](connected-sum-of-knots.md) has positive genus summands, and [additivity of Seifert genus](additivity-of-seifert-genus.md) applies. Its [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md) is the product of the summand [polynomials](polynomial-split.md). If the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is irreducible, one summand has unit [polynomial](polynomial-split.md); the other must then have [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) breadth $2g_s(K)$, exceeding twice its strictly smaller genus. This contradicts the [Alexander breadth bound on Seifert genus](alexander-breadth-bound-on-seifert-genus.md). Irreducibility without the full-degree hypothesis alone does not imply primeness.
