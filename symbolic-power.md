# Symbolic power

↑ **Parent:** [Prime ideal](prime-ideal.md)

The $n$th symbolic power of a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $P\subset R$ is

$$
P^{(n)}=P^nR_P\cap R,
$$

where the intersection denotes [contraction of an ideal](contraction-of-an-ideal.md). It is always a $P$-[primary ideal](primary-ideal.md). The ordinary power $P^n$ equals $P^{(n)}$ precisely when $P^n$ is $P$-primary; this assertion does not require $R$ to be [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md).
