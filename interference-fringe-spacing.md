# Interference fringe spacing

↑ **Parent:** [Interference fringe](interference-fringe.md)

The distance between adjacent bright [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md), or between adjacent dark [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md), measured along a specified direction. For the symmetric crossing [plane waves](plane-wave.md) in [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md), measured along $x$, $\Lambda=\lambda/(2\sin\theta)$. Bright-to-dark separation is $\Lambda/2$. As $\theta$ tends to zero the spacing diverges; at $\theta=0$ the [phase difference](phase-difference.md) is spatially constant and there are no periodic bands.
