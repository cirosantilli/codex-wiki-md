# Oxygen forbidden-line temperature diagnostic

↑ **Parent:** [Forbidden emission line](forbidden-emission-line.md)

For low-density gas with Maxwellian electrons, the excitation-energy difference between the ${}^1S_0$ and ${}^1D_2$ levels of doubly ionized oxygen makes $(I_{4959}+I_{5007})/I_{4363}$ temperature sensitive. A constant-collision-strength approximation is $R\simeq7.90e^{32900\,{\rm K}/T_e}$. Increasing temperature strengthens the higher-excitation auroral line and lowers $R$. Density, [extinction](extinction.md) and nonthermal electrons require separate treatment; the $5007/4959$ pair shares one upper level and is primarily a branching ratio rather than this thermometer.
