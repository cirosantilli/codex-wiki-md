# Secular tidal torque

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal locking](tidal-locking.md)

A [secular tidal torque](secular-tidal-torque.md) is the [torque](torque.md) remaining after averaging the changing tidal interaction over fast orbital and rotational motion. The misaligned [tidal deformation](tidal-deformation.md) lets the companion's [gravity](gravity.md) change the spin [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md); the orbit receives the opposite [torque](torque.md). [Tidal dissipation](tidal-dissipation.md) converts [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) into [heat](heat-split.md), while total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is conserved in an isolated system. Thus the spin-orbit coupling exchanges [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) rather than destroying it. [Fuller, Guillot, Mathis and Murray (2024): tidal dissipation in giant planets](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11214-024-01055-4).

For intuition near [synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md) on a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md), hold [mean motion](mean-motion.md) $n$ and [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$ fixed and adopt the illustrative linear dissipative law

$$
I\dot\omega=\langle\tau\rangle=-K(\omega-n),\qquad K>0,\qquad \omega=\dot\theta.
$$

Here $\omega$ is spin [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md), $\theta$ is a marked surface direction in an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md), and $K$ is a response coefficient, not a universal material constant. A faster spin is slowed and a slower prograde spin is accelerated. The solution and the associated mechanical power loss are

$$
\omega(t)-n=[\omega(0)-n]e^{-Kt/I},\qquad
\dot E_{\rm mech}=\langle\tau\rangle(\omega-n)=-K(\omega-n)^2\le0.
$$

The orbital contribution to this power is $-n\langle\tau\rangle$, and the spin contribution is $\omega\langle\tau\rangle$; their sum becomes [heat](heat-split.md). Holding $n$ fixed is a local approximation to the coupled evolution. Actual [secular tidal torque](secular-tidal-torque.md) depends on the deformation response and [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md), so this simple law does not establish which [spin-orbit resonance](spin-orbit-resonance.md) an eccentric body captures into.
