# Semisimple representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_representation)

A representation is semisimple when it is a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md). Equivalently, every invariant subspace has an invariant complement.
