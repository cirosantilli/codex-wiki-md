# Locally finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion group](torsion-group.md)

A [group](group-split.md) is locally finite when every [subgroup](subgroup.md) that is a [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) is finite. It is a [torsion group](torsion-group.md), since the cyclic subgroup generated by one element must be finite. A [restricted direct sum of groups](restricted-direct-sum-of-groups.md) with finite factors is locally finite. A locally finite group can be infinite, while a locally finite [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) is finite by definition.
