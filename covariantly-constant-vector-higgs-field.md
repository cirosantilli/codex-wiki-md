# Covariantly constant vector Higgs field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector Higgs field](vector-higgs-field.md)

If $D_\mu\Phi=0$ in the [SO(3) vector Higgs model](so-3-vector-higgs-model.md), antisymmetry of the [gauge potential](gauge-field.md) implies $\partial_\mu|\Phi|^2=0$. On a local patch with nonzero constant norm, [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md) aligns the field with a fixed axis. The connection and [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) then take values in the axis stabilizer; curvature can remain nonzero. Global alignment can require several bundle patches.
