# Hydraulic opening of a mineral vein

↑ **Parent:** [Mineral vein](mineral-vein.md)

A fluid-filled fracture can open perpendicular to the least compressive [principal stress](principal-stress.md). In a compression-positive contact approximation, [effective stress](effective-stress.md) is $\sigma_n'=\sigma_n-P_f$. Opening an initially intact plane of [tensile strength](tensile-strength.md) $T_0$ requires

$$
\sigma_n-P_f<-T_0.
$$

A sufficiently weak pre-existing crack can open near zero effective compression. Increasing [isotropic](isotropy.md) [pore fluid pressure](pore-fluid-pressure.md) translates all effective [principal stresses](principal-stress.md) without rotating their directions, so an opening fracture and a frictional [normal fault](normal-fault.md) can record compatible stress axes at different [pressure](pressure-split.md) episodes. Subsequent mineral precipitation creates a [mineral vein](mineral-vein.md); white appearance alone does not identify its filling mineral.
