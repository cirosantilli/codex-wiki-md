# The Essentials of Materia Medica and Therapeutics by Alfred Baring Garrod (1865)

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology and toxicology bibliography](pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography-split.md)

American edition, William Wood, with British entries distinguished from bracketed United States additions. The preface dates the underlying British revision to May 1864. [Edition and contents](https://chestofbooks.com/health/materia-medica-drugs/The-Essentials-Therapeutics/index.html). [Posological table and preparation strengths](https://chestofbooks.com/health/materia-medica-drugs/The-Essentials-Therapeutics/Posological-Table.html). Historical doses describe period practice, not current prescribing.
