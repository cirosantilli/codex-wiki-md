# Critical manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Singular perturbation](singular-perturbation-split.md)

For a system $\varepsilon\dot x=F(x,y)$, $\dot y=G(x,y)$ with fast variable $x$ and slow variable $y$, the [critical manifold](critical-manifold.md) is the set of [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) of the fast problem $dx/ds=F(x,y)$ with $y$ frozen. It is attracting where the fast [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) with negative real parts and repelling where those real parts are positive. A smooth graph can cease to be attracting at a [fold point of a critical manifold](fold-point-of-a-critical-manifold.md).
