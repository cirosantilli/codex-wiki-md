# Cyclicity of a product of two finite cyclic groups

↑ **Parent:** [Direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md)

Generators $a,b$ of the two [cyclic groups](cyclic-group.md) give a pair of order $\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)$. It generates the product precisely when this equals $mn$, namely when the orders are [coprime](coprime-integers.md). If their greatest common divisor is larger than one, every element has order at most this smaller least common multiple, so no generator exists.
