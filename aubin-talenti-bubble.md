# Aubin-Talenti bubble

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Lane-Emden equation](generalized-lane-emden-equation.md)

At the energy-critical exponent $p=(d+2)/(d-2)$, the normalized Aubin-Talenti bubble

$$
W(x)=\left(1+\frac{|x|^2}{d(d-2)}\right)^{-(d-2)/2}
$$

solves $\Delta W+W^p=0$ and optimizes the critical [Sobolev embedding theorem](sobolev-embedding-theorem.md).
