# Normal modes of a double pendulum with a two-to-one length ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Normal modes of a double pendulum](normal-modes-of-a-double-pendulum.md)

For equal point masses and upper/lower massless rods of length $2l,l$, angular coordinates from the downward vertical give mass matrix $ml^2\begin{pmatrix}8&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$ and stiffness matrix $mgl\operatorname{diag}(4,1)$. The [generalized eigenvalue problem](generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md) reduces to $s^2-3s+1=0$, where $s=\omega^2l/g$. The slow mode has angular-amplitude ratio $\theta_2/\theta_1=\sqrt5-1$, and the fast mode has ratio $-1-\sqrt5$.
