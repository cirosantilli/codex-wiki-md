# Invariance equation for a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant manifold](invariant-manifold.md)

For $\dot x=F(x,y)$ and $\dot y=G(x,y)$, a [differentiable](differentiable-function.md) graph $y=h(x)$ is invariant precisely when $Dh(x)F(x,h(x))=G(x,h(x))$. This is the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) along a trajectory on the graph. The equation determines coefficients in a local power-series approximation of a [stable manifold](stable-manifold.md) or [unstable manifold](unstable-manifold.md); checking tangent directions alone does not ensure invariance.
