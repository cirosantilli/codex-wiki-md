# Fundamental representations of sl3

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md)

The two fundamental representations of $\mathfrak{sl}_3$ are the defining representation $V(\omega_1)=\mathbb C^3$ and its dual

$$
V(\omega_2)=\Lambda^2\mathbb C^3\cong(\mathbb C^3)^*.
$$
