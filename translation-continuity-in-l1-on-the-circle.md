# Translation continuity in L1 on the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

For $f\in L^1(\mathbb T,m)$ with $m$ normalized [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md), translation on the [circle group](circle-group.md) is continuous in the [L1 norm](l1-norm.md): $\|f(\cdot+t)-f\|_1\to0$ as $t\to0$ modulo one. Approximate $f$ in the [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) by a continuous function, use its [uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md), and use translation invariance to preserve the approximation error. In particular, $m(B\mathbin\triangle(B-t))\to0$ for every measurable $B$.
