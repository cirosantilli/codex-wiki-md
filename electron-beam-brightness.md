# Electron beam brightness

↑ **Parent:** [Electron optics](electron-optics.md)

The [electric current](electric-current.md) delivered per transverse probe area per [solid angle](solid-angle.md). For a uniform circular probe of diameter $d$ and a small convergence semiangle $\alpha$, one convention is $\beta=4I/(\pi^2d^2\alpha^2)$. State the accelerating [voltage](voltage.md), angular convention and whether brightness is reduced by a [voltage](voltage.md) factor before comparing sources. A smaller aperture changes current as well as convergence; finite [electron beam brightness](electron-beam-brightness.md) prevents arbitrary current in an arbitrarily small probe.
