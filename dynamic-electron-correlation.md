# Dynamic electron correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Electron correlation](electron-correlation.md)

The correlated instantaneous avoidance of [electrons](electron.md) beyond a single [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md). It can remain important even when one configuration dominates, as for an isolated closed-shell helium atom; it is distinct from the near-degeneracy problem of [static electron correlation](static-electron-correlation.md).
