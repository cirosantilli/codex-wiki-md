# Clock

↑ **Parent:** [Time in physics](time-in-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clock)

A device that supplies a reproducible measure of elapsed [time](time-in-physics.md). An ideal relativistic [clock](clock.md) measures [proper time](proper-time.md) along its [world line](world-line.md); the [clock hypothesis](clock-hypothesis.md) separates this geometric ideal from disturbances of a particular physical mechanism.
