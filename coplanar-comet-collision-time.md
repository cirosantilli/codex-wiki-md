# Coplanar comet collision time

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary scattering](planetary-scattering.md)

The [geometric collision cross-section](geometric-collision-cross-section.md) in a coplanar calculation is a length $2R_p$, rather than an area. For one [comet](comet.md) with local surface probability $\Sigma_c$ and [relative velocity](relative-velocity.md) $v_{\rm rel}$, the rare-event rate is $2R_p\Sigma_c v_{\rm rel}$. Without [gravitational focusing](gravitational-focusing.md), the resulting mean collision time scales as $Q^{3/2}M_p^{-1/3}a_p$ for deep planet-crossing orbits at fixed stellar mass and planetary [mass density](density.md).
