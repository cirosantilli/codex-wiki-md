# Chemical branching fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md)

For competing irreversible pseudo-first-order channels with rates $k_j$, the fraction following channel $j$ is $f_j=k_j/\sum_l k_l$ when those conditions stay fixed during the intermediate's lifetime. A photochemical destruction rate must count only branches that actually remove the chosen species after fast recycling. For excited [oxygen](oxygen.md), collisional deactivation followed by [ozone](ozone.md) reformation is different from reaction with [water](water.md) to make [hydroxyl radicals](hydroxyl-radical.md).
