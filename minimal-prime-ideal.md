# Minimal prime ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Prime ideal](prime-ideal.md)

A minimal prime ideal is a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) containing no strictly smaller prime ideal. If $A$ is a [reduced ring](reduced-ring.md) and $\mathfrak p$ is minimal, the [localization at a prime ideal](localization-at-a-prime-ideal.md) $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is a field: its only prime is its maximal ideal, and reducedness makes that ideal its zero [nilradical](nilradical.md). Every nonzero commutative ring has a minimal prime.
