# GIM mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor-changing neutral current](flavor-changing-neutral-current.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIM_mechanism)

Unitarity of the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) cancels flavour-independent contributions to weak flavour-changing neutral amplitudes. In a quark loop, equal internal masses would give a common contribution whose off-diagonal CKM sum vanishes; unequal masses leave a suppressed nonzero amplitude. This explains why [neutral kaon mixing](neutral-kaon-mixing.md) occurs through loops rather than a tree neutral-current vertex.
