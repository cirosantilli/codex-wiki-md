# Polynomial skew-sumset expansion over a prime field

↑ **Parent:** [Closure bounds for small skew-sumset scalars](closure-bounds-for-small-skew-sumset-scalars.md)

For positive $\alpha,\beta$, suppose $p^\alpha\leq|A|\leq p^{1-\alpha}$ and $|B|\geq p^\beta$. Repeatedly replace a [scalar](scalar.md) set $S$ by $3SS-3SS$. [Quadratic growth of a sixfold product difference set](quadratic-growth-of-a-sixfold-product-difference-set.md) reaches size at least $p/2$ in a number of steps depending only on $\beta$. The [closure bounds for small skew-sumset scalars](closure-bounds-for-small-skew-sumset-scalars.md) transfer a putative bound $|A+bA|\leq K|A|$ to all these [scalars](scalar.md) with exponent depending only on the number of steps. Averaging collisions over the resulting large [scalar](scalar.md) set then forces $K^L\geq\frac14\min(|A|,p/|A|)\geq p^\alpha/4$. This supplies a positive exponent depending only on $\alpha,\beta$. The finitely many smaller primes are covered by the strict inequality $|A+bA|>|A|$ for every nonzero $b$ when $1<|A|<p$.
