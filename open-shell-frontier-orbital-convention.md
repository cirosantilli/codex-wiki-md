# Open-shell frontier orbital convention

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md)

For an open-shell species, distinguish a singly occupied spatial orbital from a vacant [spin](spin.md) orbital. Adding an [electron](electron.md) to an [oxygen](oxygen.md) molecule can fill the opposite-spin vacancy in a pi-star [singly occupied molecular orbital](singly-occupied-molecular-orbital.md) even though the next completely empty spatial orbital is sigma-star.
