# Finite quotient of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient group](quotient-group.md)

A finite quotient is the image of a [group](group-split.md) under a surjective [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) to a [finite group](finite-group.md). A group has no nontrivial finite quotients exactly when every homomorphism from it to a finite group is trivial. This property does not prohibit finite or finite-quotient-bearing subgroups.
