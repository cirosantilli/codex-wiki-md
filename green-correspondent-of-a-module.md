# Green correspondent of a module

↑ **Parent:** [Green correspondence](green-correspondence.md)

The Green correspondent is the unique indecomposable summand of restriction or induction having the selected [vertex of an indecomposable module](vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md), in the setting of [Green correspondence](green-correspondence.md). The two correspondence maps are inverse and preserve [sources of an indecomposable module](source-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) up to the appropriate normalizer conjugacy.
