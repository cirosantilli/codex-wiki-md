# Gill-arch hypothesis of paired fin origins

↑ **Parent:** [Paired fin](paired-fin.md)

The proposal that paired appendages evolved through modification of branchial structures. Evidence for a pharyngeal contribution to the shoulder is distinguishable from evidence that the whole [fin](fin.md) arose by conversion of a [gill](gill.md) arch.
