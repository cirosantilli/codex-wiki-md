# Baire space of sequences

↑ **Parent:** [Descriptive set theory](descriptive-set-theory-split.md)

The set-theoretic Baire space is $\omega^\omega$, the set of all infinite [sequences](sequence.md) of [natural numbers](natural-number.md), with the product topology obtained from discrete $\omega$.
