# Antisymmetric superpotential improvement

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md)

If $B^{\rho\mu\nu}=-B^{\mu\rho\nu}$, commuting partial derivatives gives $\partial_\mu\partial_\rho B^{\rho\mu\nu}=0$. Such an improvement preserves conservation of a [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) and changes integrated charges only by a spatial boundary term. The [Belinfante-Rosenfeld stress-energy tensor](belinfante-rosenfeld-stress-energy-tensor.md) is obtained through this type of improvement.
