# Pauli spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli equation](pauli-equation.md)

A two-component complex [wave function](wave-function-split.md) describing the two spin states of a nonrelativistic spin-$1/2$ particle. Under a spatial rotation by angle $\theta$ about $\widehat{\mathbf n}$ it transforms as $\psi\mapsto e^{-i\theta\widehat{\mathbf n}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma/2}\psi$, with [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) $\boldsymbol\sigma$. A [Pauli spinor](pauli-spinor.md) may describe a massive particle; two components alone do not imply the massless [Weyl equation](weyl-equation.md).
