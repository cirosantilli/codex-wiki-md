# Rational equilibrium curve at a degenerate pitchfork

↑ **Parent:** [Pitchfork bifurcation normal form](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md)

For $k>1$, the rational [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) curve in the display has expansion $r=1+q+[1-q(k-1)]u+qk(k-1)u^2+\cdots$. Its cubic [pitchfork bifurcation](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md) coefficient vanishes at $q=1/(k-1)$, leaving $r-(1+q)=ku^2+\cdots$. For larger $q$, the nonzero branch folds at $u=[\sqrt{q(k-1)}-1]/k$ and $r=[k-1+q+2\sqrt{q(k-1)}]/k$. If the other [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) remain stable, the two folds bound a bistable wedge between stable zero and stable nonzero states, separated by an unstable branch. If another [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is critical, a higher-dimensional [centre manifold](center-manifold.md) is required instead.
