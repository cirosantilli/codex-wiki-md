# Eulerian time derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Material derivative](material-derivative.md)

The [Eulerian time derivative](eulerian-time-derivative.md) measures a continuum field's rate of change at a fixed [Eulerian coordinate](eulerian-coordinate.md). For a [velocity field](velocity-field.md) $\mathbf u$, the [material derivative](material-derivative.md) instead follows a [fluid element](fluid-element.md) and satisfies $Df/Dt=\partial_t f+\mathbf u\cdot\nabla f$. A nonuniform [steady flow](steady-flow.md) has zero [Eulerian time derivative](eulerian-time-derivative.md) of [velocity](velocity.md) but can have nonzero [material acceleration](material-acceleration.md).
