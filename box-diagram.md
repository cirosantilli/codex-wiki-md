# Box diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A [box diagram](box-diagram.md) has a one-loop cycle of four propagator edges and four vertices connecting external legs. Its internal particle types depend on the theory. In a [charged weak box contribution to kaon mixing](charged-weak-box-contribution-to-kaon-mixing.md), two charged weak-boson propagators connect two up-type [quark](quark.md) lines, with flavor-changing charged-current vertices.
