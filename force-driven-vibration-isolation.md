# Force-driven vibration isolation

↑ **Parent:** [Forced harmonic oscillator](forced-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For a supported [mass](mass.md) $m$, total [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k$, and viscous coefficient $b$, a harmonic applied [force](force-split.md) has response $|x|=F_0/\sqrt{(k-m\omega^2)^2+b^2\omega^2}$. Far above the natural [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), light damping gives $|x|\simeq F_0/(m\omega^2)$. For a prescribed displacement bound $x_{\max}$, this estimates $m\gtrsim F_0/(\omega^2x_{\max})$, but the finite spring term can make the estimated equality slightly violate the bound. If $F_0/x_{\max}>b\omega$, the high-mass exact threshold is

$$
m_{\min}=\frac{k+\sqrt{(F_0/x_{\max})^2-b^2\omega^2}}{\omega^2}.
$$

This model describes a specified external [force](force-split.md). Motion transmitted through a moving support instead has a base-displacement forcing term and a different dependence on damping.
