# Compulsory miss

↑ **Parent:** [Cache miss](cache-miss.md)

A [cache miss](cache-miss.md) on the first access to a [cache block](cache-line.md) in an initially empty [memory cache](cache-computing.md) with no fetching ahead of requests. Larger capacity cannot eliminate these first fetches; subsequent [cache misses](cache-miss.md) can have other causes.
