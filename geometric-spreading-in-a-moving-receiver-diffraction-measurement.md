# Geometric spreading in a moving-receiver diffraction measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Knife-edge diffraction](knife-edge-diffraction.md)

A diffraction-loss ratio is normalized to the unobstructed [intensity](intensity-physics.md) at the same receiver position. With a point source that unobstructed [intensity](intensity-physics.md) changes as inverse source–receiver distance squared. Moving the receiver can therefore decrease the transmission ratio while increasing its absolute received [intensity](intensity-physics.md). The two curves require separate normalization; a Cornu-chord plot alone omits geometric spreading.
