# Arcuate fasciculus

↑ **Parent:** [White matter](white-matter.md)

A dorsal association-fiber system linking temporal and frontal territories. Its exact subdivision and functional interpretation depend on the anatomical and reconstruction framework; it should not be equated with the entire [dorsal language pathway](dorsal-language-pathway.md).
