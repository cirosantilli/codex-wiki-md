↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Both dimers have two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) in a valence sigma-bonding orbital and a formal valence [bond order](../../../../../../bond-order.md) of one.** For [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) the minimal-basis configuration is $(1\sigma_g)^2$, with the $1s$-derived $1\sigma_g$ as [highest occupied molecular orbital](../../../../../../highest-occupied-molecular-orbital.md) and $1\sigma_u^*$ as [LUMO](../../../../../../lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md). The bonding combination has no internuclear [node](../../../../../../node-dynamical-systems.md); the antibonding combination does.

For [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md), include the filled core pair of levels as well as the valence levels:

$$
\mathrm{Li_2}:\ (1\sigma_g)^2(1\sigma_u)^2(2\sigma_g)^2.
$$

The first two orbitals are predominantly core $1s$ combinations whose bonding and antibonding occupations cancel in a simple bond-order count. The [highest occupied molecular orbital](../../../../../../highest-occupied-molecular-orbital.md) is predominantly $\sigma_g(2s)$, denoted $2\sigma_g$; the [LUMO](../../../../../../lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md) is $2\sigma_u$, predominantly the antibonding $2s$ combination but modified by mixing with compatible $2p$ contributions. The nearby empty $2p$-derived sigma and pi levels must also be shown. Only orbitals of matching symmetry mix, so this does not mean that every $2s$ combination mixes with every $2p$ combination.

On a common qualitative energy reference, [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) $1s$ is much lower than [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md)'s valence $2s$, while [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md)'s core $1s$ levels lie much deeper. The Li valence bonding–antibonding separation is smaller, consistent with its longer and weaker bond. The figure uses a compressed core region and schematic gaps, not measured orbital energies. The role of symmetry-compatible [s–p orbital mixing](../../../../../../s-p-orbital-mixing.md) in the first-row diatomics is illustrated in institutional molecular-orbital notes.[pp. 1–2: s–p mixing and first-row orbital ordering](../../../../../../diatomic-mos-by-timothy-hughbanks.md)
