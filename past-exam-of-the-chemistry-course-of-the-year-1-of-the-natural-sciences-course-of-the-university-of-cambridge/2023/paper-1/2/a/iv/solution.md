<h1 id="2/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**[ethanol](../../../../../../ethanol.md) undergoes [proton transfer](../../../../../../proton-transfer.md) with the [methoxide](../../../../../../methoxide.md) produced.** The [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) of [methoxide](../../../../../../methoxide.md) attacks the hydroxyl [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) of [ethanol](../../../../../../ethanol.md), while the [ethanol](../../../../../../ethanol.md) O–H bond pair returns to its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md):

$$
\mathrm{MeO^-+EtOH\rightleftharpoons MeOH+EtO^-}.
$$

The [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) acidities are similar, so this is an equilibrium, not an overwhelmingly one-way [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) determined solely by intrinsic acidity. The large [ethanol](../../../../../../ethanol.md) reservoir and initially small [methanol](../../../../../../methanol.md) [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) nevertheless matter. This exchange means that [lithium ethoxide](../../../../../../lithium-ethoxide.md) need not be consumed stoichiometrically in the net transesterification. Neutral [ethanol](../../../../../../ethanol.md) can also participate in [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) shuttling; it is not acting as a reducing reagent or as a substrate for elimination under these conditions.
