<h1 id="2/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**Excess [ethanol](../../../../../../ethanol.md) drives a reversible [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) exchange mainly through the actual mixture composition, not through a large new bond-energy advantage.** The net reaction exchanges one [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) and one [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) for a closely related [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) and [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md). It preserves the main bond types and has no obvious large exothermicity. Solvation and [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) pairing can contribute to its [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md), but there is no basis here for asserting a large negative standard reaction [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md).

There are two [molecules](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) on each side, so an increase in particle count is not the [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) explanation. Instead use

$$
\Delta_rG=\Delta_rG^\circ+RT\ln Q,\qquad
Q=\frac{a_{\rm ethyl\ acetate}a_{\rm methanol}}{a_{\rm methyl\ acetate}a_{\rm ethanol}}.
$$

With [ethanol](../../../../../../ethanol.md) as the solvent and little [methanol](../../../../../../methanol.md) initially, the [reaction quotient](../../../../../../reaction-quotient.md) is small; product formation and mixing lower the [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) until the exchange equilibrium is reached. [ethanol](../../../../../../ethanol.md) can be treated approximately as having fixed activity near its pure-solvent value, rather than assigned an arbitrarily enormous activity, while [methanol](../../../../../../methanol.md) remains dilute. This composition-dependent chemical-potential contribution is the appropriate entropic/mixing argument. It explains forward conversion under these conditions without claiming that $\Delta_rS^\circ$ must be positive, or that complete conversion follows without an [equilibrium constant](../../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) or removal of [methanol](../../../../../../methanol.md).
