<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $G$ be [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md), $H$ [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) and $T$ [thermodynamic temperature](../../../../../thermodynamic-temperature.md) for a fixed amount and composition of material, with the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) taken at constant [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). Apply the [Gibbs–Helmholtz equation](../../../../../gibbs-helmholtz-equation.md) to the standard molar reaction quantities, obtained by summing product minus reactant contributions with their stoichiometric coefficients:

$$
\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dT}\left(\frac{\Delta_rG^\circ}{T}\right)=-\frac{\Delta_rH^\circ}{T^2}.
$$

Define $K(T)$ as the dimensionless thermodynamic [equilibrium constant](../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) expressed using activities in specified standard states. Since $\Delta_rG^\circ=-RT\ln K$, where $R$ is the [molar gas constant](../../../../../molar-gas-constant.md),

$$
-R\frac{\mathrm d\ln K}{\mathrm dT}=-\frac{\Delta_rH^\circ}{T^2},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{\mathrm d\ln K}{\mathrm dT}=\frac{\Delta_rH^\circ}{RT^2}.}
$$

If the reaction [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) is constant over the interval, [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\ln\frac{K(T_2)}{K(T_1)}=-\frac{\Delta_rH^\circ}{R}\left(\frac1{T_2}-\frac1{T_1}\right).}
$$

This is the requested [Van't Hoff equation](../../../../../van-t-hoff-equation.md). For an exothermic reaction, increasing [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) decreases $K$. Although the name “isochore” is often used in this context, the displayed [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) form follows from standard Gibbs energies and the stated constant-pressure Gibbs–Helmholtz identity; the analogous ideal-gas [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) form involves [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) when the [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence of standard [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) is retained.
