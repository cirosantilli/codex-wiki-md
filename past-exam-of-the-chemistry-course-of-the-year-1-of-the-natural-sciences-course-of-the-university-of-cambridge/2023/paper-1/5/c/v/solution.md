<h1 id="5/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Using bonds broken minus bonds formed, the enthalpies per mole of elementary events are

$$
\begin{array}{c|r}
\text{step}&\Delta H_i/\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\\\hline
1&+242\\
2&436-431=+5\\
3&242-431=-189\\
4&-242
\end{array}
$$

Under the [steady-state approximation in chemical kinetics](../../../../../../steady-state-approximation-in-chemical-kinetics.md), initiation and termination have equal event rates, so their [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) contributions cancel. The two propagation steps also have equal flux, and their combined [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) is $5-189=\boxed{-184\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}}$ per overall reaction producing two moles of HCl. **Step 3 dominates the [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) release**; the small endothermic contribution of step 2 only slightly offsets it.

An insulated vessel cannot remove this released energy as [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md), so its [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) rises as reaction proceeds. In a rigid vessel the precise energy balance uses reaction [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) and constant-volume [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) capacities, whereas a constant-pressure adiabatic balance uses [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md); the strong exothermic conclusion is the same. Warming changes the [rate constants](../../../../../../rate-constant.md), and activated initiation and propagation can accelerate, producing positive thermal feedback and potentially a rapid [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) rise. A numerical final [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) or a claim of an inevitable explosion is not justified without composition, [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) capacities and heat-loss information. The initially assumed 298-K [rate constants](../../../../../../rate-constant.md) cannot remain fixed throughout an appreciable adiabatic [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) rise.
