<h1 id="5/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Apply the [steady-state approximation in chemical kinetics](../../../../../../steady-state-approximation-in-chemical-kinetics.md) to the [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) and [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) [free radicals](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md).** After a short initiation transient their formation and consumption can balance while the stable reactants continue to be depleted and [hydrogen chloride](../../../../../../hydrogen-chloride.md) accumulates. Accordingly set $\mathrm d[\mathrm H]/\mathrm dt\simeq0$ and $\mathrm d[\mathrm{Cl}]/\mathrm dt\simeq0$.

This does not mean the [free radicals](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) are absent or that the whole mixture is at equilibrium. [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) and [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) [molecules](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) and the [hydrogen chloride](../../../../../../hydrogen-chloride.md) product generally cannot all be assigned steady concentrations over appreciable conversion. The inert-body [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) can be treated as an imposed or slowly varying bath quantity; it is not an intermediate to which a [free radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) balance must be applied.
