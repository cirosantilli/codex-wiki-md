<h1 id="5/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Step 1 is initiation; steps 2 and 3 are propagation; step 4 is termination.** Initiation dissociates a [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) into two [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) [free radicals](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md). Each propagation step consumes one [free radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) and creates another, carrying the chain forward without destroying its [free radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) carrier overall. Termination removes two [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) [free radicals](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) by recombination.

The inert collision partner accepts or supplies energy as needed and is regenerated. It is not a [free radical](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) chain carrier or a net reagent. Adding the two propagation steps cancels the [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) and [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) [free radicals](../../../../../../radical-chemistry.md) and gives one complete conversion of [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) plus [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) to two [molecules](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) of [hydrogen chloride](../../../../../../hydrogen-chloride.md).
