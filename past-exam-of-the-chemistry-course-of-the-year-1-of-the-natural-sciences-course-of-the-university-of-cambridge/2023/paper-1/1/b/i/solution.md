↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**B gives spectrum 4.** Number the unsaturated acyl chain from its [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). For $\mathrm{MeO-C(=O)-CH=CH-CH_3}$, the assignments are

$$
\delta_C(\mathrm{C=O})=166.9,\quad
\delta_C(\alpha\text{-CH})=122.5,\quad
\delta_C(\beta\text{-CH})=144.6,\quad
\delta_C(\mathrm{CH_3})=17.9,\quad
\delta_C(\mathrm{OCH_3})=51.3\ \mathrm{ppm}.
$$

The strongly deshielded beta [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and relatively shielded alpha [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) reflect polarization of the [conjugated ester](../../../../../../conjugated-ester.md). All four protonated carbons here are CH or methyl environments and share one [attached proton test](../../../../../../attached-proton-test.md) phase, whereas the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) has the opposite phase. The methoxy [resonance](../../../../../../resonance.md) is decisive: B has an O-bound methyl, not an O-bound methylene.
