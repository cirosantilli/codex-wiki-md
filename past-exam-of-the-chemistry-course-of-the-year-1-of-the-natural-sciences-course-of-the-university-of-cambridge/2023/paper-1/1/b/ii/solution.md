**C gives spectrum 3.** For [allyl acetate](../../../../../../allyl-acetate.md), $\mathrm{CH_3C(=O)OCH_2CH=CH_2}$, assign

$$
\delta_C(\mathrm{C=O})=170.6,\quad
\delta_C(\mathrm{COCH_3})=20.9,\quad
\delta_C(\mathrm{OCH_2})=65.1,\quad
\delta_C(\mathrm{CH=})=132.3,\quad
\delta_C(\mathrm{=CH_2})=118.1\ \mathrm{ppm}.
$$

The 65.1-ppm [resonance](../../../../../../resonance.md) is the oxygen-bound methylene. Its [attached proton test](../../../../../../attached-proton-test.md) phase matches the [terminal alkene](../../../../../../terminal-alkene.md) methylene and the nonprotonated [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md), while the [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) CH and acetyl methyl have the opposite phase. The [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) is not conjugated with the [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) because [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and a saturated methylene interrupt the carbon–carbon conjugated path. This explains its higher [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) shift relative to B without requiring the two [esters](../../../../../../ester.md) to have identical electronic environments.
