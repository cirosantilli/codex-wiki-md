<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**B should have a lower [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) stretching frequency because its [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) is conjugated with a C=C bond.** A typical diagnostic comparison is a [conjugated ester](../../../../../../conjugated-ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) around $1715$–$1730\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ for B versus a nonconjugated [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) around $1740$–$1750\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ for C; the precise positions depend on the measurement conditions. [conjugation](../../../../../../conjugation.md) distributes the pi bonding over the acyl fragment and lowers the effective [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [bond force constant](../../../../../../bond-force-constant.md).

C also has a [terminal alkene](../../../../../../terminal-alkene.md), giving characteristic out-of-plane C–H bending features near 990 and $910\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. B has an internal, trans-disubstituted [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md), for which a band near $965\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ is useful. Both compounds contain [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) C–O bonds and [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) C–H bonds, so merely observing those general features would not distinguish them. The [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) comparison plus the [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) substitution pattern is the stronger identification.
