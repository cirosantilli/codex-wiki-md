<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use $a$ for the acetyl methyl, $b$ for the O-bound [methylene group](../../../../../../methylene-group.md) pair, $c$ for the internal vinyl [proton](../../../../../../proton.md), and $d,e$ for the terminal vinyl [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) respectively trans and cis to $c$. The assignments are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|l}
\text{protons}&\delta_H/\mathrm{ppm}&\text{integral}&\text{resolved pattern}\\\hline
a&2.081&3&\text{singlet}\\
b&4.570&2&\text{doublet of triplets}\\
c&5.919&1&\text{doublet of doublets of triplets}\\
d&5.314&1&\text{doublet of apparent quartets}\\
e&5.235&1&\text{doublet of apparent quartets}
\end{array}
$$

The two terminal [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) are [diastereotopic](../../../../../../diastereotopic.md): one is cis and the other trans to $c$, so they have different shifts and different [vicinal couplings](../../../../../../vicinal-coupling.md). Each terminal [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) also couples through [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) to the other and through [long-range scalar coupling](../../../../../../long-range-scalar-coupling.md) to the two $b$ [protons](../../../../../../proton.md). When those small couplings coincide, an underlying [doublet of doublets](../../../../../../doublet-of-doublets.md) of [NMR triplets](../../../../../../nmr-triplet.md) collapses to the observed [NMR doublet](../../../../../../nmr-doublet.md) of apparent [NMR quartets](../../../../../../nmr-quartet.md). The O-bound [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) are [enantiotopic](../../../../../../enantiotopicity.md) and have [chemical equivalence in NMR](../../../../../../chemical-equivalence-in-nmr.md) in this achiral environment; they are split by $c$ and by the two terminal [protons](../../../../../../proton.md). The integrated terminal envelope contains two [protons](../../../../../../proton.md), not two equivalent [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) at one shift.
