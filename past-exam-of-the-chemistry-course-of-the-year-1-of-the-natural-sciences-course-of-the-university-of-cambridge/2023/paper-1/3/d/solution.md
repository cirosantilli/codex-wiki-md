<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**Build the phenethyl [grignard reagent](../../../../../grignard-reagent.md), use one portion to make a phenethyl-substituted [ester](../../../../../ester.md), and add two further equivalents to that [ester](../../../../../ester.md).** Write $R=\mathrm{PhCH_2CH_2}$; the target is $\mathrm{R_3COH}$, so all three [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) substituents must be phenethyl, not benzyl.

[hydroboration](../../../../../hydroboration.md) followed by [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) of [styrene](../../../../../styrene.md) gives $\mathrm{PhCH_2CH_2OH}$: use $\mathrm{BH_3}$ in a suitable [ether](../../../../../ether.md), followed by alkaline [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md) peroxide. The [Anti-Markovnikov addition](../../../../../anti-markovnikov-addition.md) regiochemistry is essential; acid-catalysed [Markovnikov addition](../../../../../markovnikov-addition.md) of water would give the wrong [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md). Convert the primary [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) to $\mathrm{PhCH_2CH_2Br}$ with [phosphorus tribromide](../../../../../phosphorus-tribromide.md), then form $\mathrm{RMgBr}$ with [magnesium](../../../../../magnesium.md) in dry [ether](../../../../../ether.md). The organomagnesium reagent must be prepared and used without a [protic solvent](../../../../../protic-solvent.md).

[carboxylate](../../../../../carboxylate.md) one portion with [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) and acidify to obtain $\mathrm{RCO_2H}$, then esterify with [ethanol](../../../../../ethanol.md) under acid catalysis to give $\mathrm{RCO_2Et}$. Treat the isolated [ester](../../../../../ester.md) with at least two equivalents of another portion of $\mathrm{RMgBr}$ and then perform an aqueous acidic work-up:

$$
\mathrm{RCO_2Et\xrightarrow[\text{then aqueous acid}]{2\,RMgBr}R_3COH}.
$$

The first addition to the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) gives a [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) after [ethoxide](../../../../../ethoxide.md) elimination; the second addition gives the tertiary [alkoxide](../../../../../alkoxide.md). Work-up supplies its OH [proton](../../../../../proton.md). The extra central [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) comes from [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md), and each original [styrene](../../../../../styrene.md) contributes one intact eight-carbon phenethyl unit. The resulting [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) count is $1+3\times8=25$, matching the target skeleton.
