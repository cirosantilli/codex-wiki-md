<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**CO and CO$_2$ are discrete [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md), whereas the other oxides gain stability from extended bonding or ionic lattices.** [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md)'s compact 2p orbitals overlap effectively with [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) 2p orbitals, allowing strong multiple bonds that satisfy the valence requirements within small [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md). Intermolecular forces between CO or CO$_2$ [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md) are much weaker than those internal bonds, so low condensation temperatures and gaseous states at room conditions are unsurprising. CO$_2$ is nonpolar overall but still has [London dispersion forces](../../../../../london-dispersion-force.md) and interactions between [electric quadrupoles](../../../../../electric-quadrupole.md); “nonpolar” does not mean no attraction.

For larger Group 14 atoms, overlap with [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) 2p orbitals is less effective for strong localized multiple bonding. SiO$_2$ instead forms an extended network of SiO$_4$ [tetrahedra](../../../../../tetrahedron.md) with each [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) bridging two [silicon](../../../../../silicon.md) atoms. Breaking or melting the network involves much more than separating intact SiO$_2$ [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md). The Si–O [electronegativity](../../../../../electronegativity-split.md) difference is $3.61-1.92=1.69$, so these are strongly polar [covalent bonds](../../../../../covalent-bond-split.md) rather than evidence for a molecular solid.

For Pb–O the difference is $3.61-1.85=1.76$, and ionic Pb$^{2+}$/O$^{2-}$ or Pb$^{4+}$/O$^{2-}$ descriptions provide useful starting points, with appreciable covalent contributions. PbO has layered structures with asymmetric [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) coordination consistent with a stereochemically active 6s$^2$ pair. A common PbO$_2$ structure is rutile-like, with PbO$_6$ octahedra linked through [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). Extended lattice interactions account for their solid states.[Geometry and Electronic Structure: rutile PbO2, six-coordinate Pb and three-coordinate O](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.107.246402) The similar Si–O and Pb–O [electronegativity](../../../../../electronegativity-split.md) differences show why a single difference threshold cannot determine the entire bonding type, [coordination number](../../../../../coordination-number.md) or structure.
