<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**A suitable representative structure is a three-dimensional network of corner-sharing CO$_4$ [tetrahedra](../../../../../tetrahedron.md), with C–O single bonds.** Each [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) is four-coordinate; every bridging [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) is bonded to two carbons. Sharing four oxygens between two [tetrahedra](../../../../../tetrahedron.md) gives $4/2=2$ oxygens per [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), so the network retains empirical formula CO$_2$ without containing separate O=C=O [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md).

High [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) favours a compact, highly coordinated network through its smaller molar volume: at fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), increasing [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) changes relative Gibbs energies according to $\mathrm d(\Delta G)=\Delta V\,\mathrm dp$. Heating helps overcome the large reconstructive barrier to breaking and redistributing molecular pi bonding and establishing an extended single-bond network. It should not be argued that high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) by itself favours the lower-entropy network. Nor is merely freezing molecular CO$_2$ sufficient: dry ice remains a molecular solid.

The sketch shows connectivity and local tetrahedral coordination, not a unique predicted [crystal structure](../../../../../crystal-structure.md). Real [amorphous carbonia](../../../../../amorphous-carbonia.md) need not have perfect periodic order or exclusively one coordination environment. The original [carbonia](../../../../../amorphous-carbonia.md) experiments combined high-pressure preparation with infrared, Raman and diffraction evidence for loss of molecular character and formation of an amorphous network.[Abstract: spectroscopy and diffraction evidence for amorphous nonmolecular CO2](../../../../../amorphous-silica-like-carbon-dioxide-by-mario-santoro-and-others.md)
