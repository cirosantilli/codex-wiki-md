<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**Amine [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) binds the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) end of CO$_2$; Mg$^{2+}$ binds through [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md).** The polarized C=O bonds make [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) electrophilic and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) electron rich. An amine [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) therefore donates toward [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), potentially giving a carbamate-like adduct after [proton transfer](../../../../../proton-transfer.md). The hard [Lewis acid](../../../../../lewis-acid.md) Mg$^{2+}$ accepts an [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md), either from coordinated CO$_2$ or from an [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) of a captured [carbamate](../../../../../carbamate.md) species. Simply reversing these assignments because [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) and [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) both have [lone pairs](../../../../../lone-pair.md) would put two electron-pair donors together without accounting for the electrophilic site.

The local binding pattern does not by itself determine the full proton-transfer state or whether cooperative chains form; those depend on the framework and amine arrangement. A documented diamine-appended framework shows cooperative carbon-dioxide insertion and [ammonium carbamate chain](../../../../../ammonium-carbamate-chain.md) formation, illustrating one possible implementation.[Adsorption mechanism and ammonium-carbamate-chain assignment](../../../../../cooperative-insertion-of-co2-in-diamine-appended-metal-organic-frameworks-by-thomas-m-mcdonald-and-others.md)

To regenerate the material, lower the CO$_2$ [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) using reduced [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) or a CO$_2$-free purge, and, where binding is exothermic, [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) the framework to favour [desorption](../../../../../desorption.md). The [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) must remain compatible with framework stability. This reverses [adsorption](../../../../../adsorption.md) or reversible [chemisorption](../../../../../chemisorption.md); it does not require permanently destroying the [metal–organic framework](../../../../../metal-organic-framework.md).
