**[carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) uses strong 2s/2p-derived covalent bonding, whereas retention or involvement of the low-energy Pb 6s pair distinguishes the two [lead](../../../../../../lead.md) oxides.** In CO, [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) has an approximately triple-bonded description with one [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md); the latter has substantial 2s character, while the sigma and two pi bonding combinations involve the valence shell, particularly [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) 2p orbitals for the pi bonds. In CO$_2$, [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is formally in [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) +4 and its four [valence electrons](../../../../../../valence-electron.md) participate in two strongly covalent C=O double-bond frameworks. A localized account uses sp hybrids for sigma bonding and two [p orbitals](../../../../../../p-orbital.md) for the pi systems. [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) states are bookkeeping devices here: neither [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) contains a bare C$^{2+}$ or C$^{4+}$ [ion](../../../../../../ion.md).

In PbO, the Pb(II) description removes or employs mainly the two 6p [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md), retaining a relatively low-energy 6s$^2$ pair. In PbO$_2$, the Pb(IV) description additionally involves those two 6s [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). The stabilization and poor availability of that pair underlie the [inert-pair effect](../../../../../../inert-pair-effect.md) and the relative preference for Pb(II) chemistry. “Inert” does not mean that the 6s [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) have zero influence on bonding or shape: mixing with [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) states and empty Pb states can make the Pb(II) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) stereochemically active. The more ionic formal electron counts in the [lead](../../../../../../lead.md) oxides and the covalent molecular-orbital description of the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) oxides are complementary models, not literal assignments of individual [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) to immutable [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md).[Abstract and electronic-density analysis: oxygen-dependent lone-pair activity in PbO](../../../../../../the-origin-of-the-stereochemically-active-pb-ii-lone-pair-dft-calculations-on-pbo-and-pbs-by-aron-walsh-and-graeme-w-watson.md)
