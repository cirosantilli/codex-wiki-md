**B is 4-hydroxy-1-methylpyrrolidin-2-one; C is 1-methylpyrrolidin-3-ol.** Their ring connectivities are shown below. Each ring remains five membered; the attached N-methyl group is retained.

**[Figure 4](#3/b/i/image-original-cyclic-product-structures-and-hydride-electron-pair-arrows-for-ketone-and-lactam-reduction). Original cyclic product structures and hydride electron-pair arrows for ketone and lactam reduction**.

For B, a B–H bond of [sodium borohydride](../../../../../sodium-borohydride.md) supplies a [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) equivalent to the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) carbonyl carbon, and the carbonyl $\pi$ pair moves to [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). This gives an [alkoxide](../../../../../alkoxide.md), whose [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md) during the reaction or workup gives the secondary [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md). The [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md) carbonyl remains. Converting one $\mathrm{C{=}O}$ to $\mathrm{CH(OH)}$ adds two H atoms and gives $\mathrm{C_5H_9NO_2}$.

For C, [lithium aluminium hydride](../../../../../lithium-aluminium-hydride.md) reduces that [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) in the same net way and also reduces the [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md). Coordination of the amide [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) to [aluminium](../../../../../aluminium.md) assists [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) attack at its carbonyl carbon, giving a tetrahedral intermediate. The N [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) then forms a C=N bond while the C–O bond breaks to an aluminium-bound oxygen group. A resulting [iminium ion](../../../../../iminium.md) accepts a second [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) at carbon, yielding the cyclic tertiary [amine](../../../../../amine.md). Aqueous workup protonates the ketone-derived [alkoxide](../../../../../alkoxide.md).

The [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md) conversion replaces $\mathrm{N-C({=}O)}$ by $\mathrm{N-CH_2}$; its [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) is removed rather than retained as another [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md). Together with [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) reduction, the net change is $+4\mathrm H-\mathrm O$, giving $\mathrm{C_5H_{11}NO}$. No stereochemistry is specified: the new [alcohol](../../../../../alcohol.md) stereocentre is formed as a [racemic mixture](../../../../../racemic-mixture.md) in an achiral environment.
