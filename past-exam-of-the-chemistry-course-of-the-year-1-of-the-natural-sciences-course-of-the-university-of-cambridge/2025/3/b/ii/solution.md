<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**[Sodium borohydride](../../../../../sodium-borohydride.md) is chemoselective for the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md); [lithium aluminium hydride](../../../../../lithium-aluminium-hydride.md) reduces both the [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) and the [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md).** In an [amide](../../../../../amide.md), donation of the N [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) into the carbonyl $\pi$ system reduces the carbonyl carbon's [electrophilicity](../../../../../electrophilicity.md). It is therefore substantially less susceptible to the milder [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) donor.

[Lithium aluminium hydride](../../../../../lithium-aluminium-hydride.md) supplies more strongly reducing [hydride](../../../../../hydride.md) equivalents, and the strong affinity of [aluminium](../../../../../aluminium.md) for [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) assists removal of the amide [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) through the [iminium](../../../../../iminium.md) pathway. This difference explains both the unchanged [lactam](../../../../../lactam.md) carbonyl in B and the absence of that [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) in C. Both reactions are interpreted after the usual protonating workup.
