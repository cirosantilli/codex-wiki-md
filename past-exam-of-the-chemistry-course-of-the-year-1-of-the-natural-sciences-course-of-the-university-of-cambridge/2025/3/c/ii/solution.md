<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Basic [methanol](../../../../../methanol.md) exchanges the [ester](../../../../../ester.md) alkoxy group while retaining the [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md); dilute aqueous acid removes the [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) while retaining the ethyl [ester](../../../../../ester.md) on the stated reaction timescale.** An [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) lacks an electrophilic carbonyl and is stable to ordinary base: expelling [methoxide](../../../../../methoxide.md) directly would require a poor leaving group. The [ester](../../../../../ester.md) can instead undergo [nucleophilic acyl substitution](../../../../../nucleophilic-acyl-substitution.md) through a carbonyl-derived tetrahedral intermediate.

Under acid, protonating an [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) converts an alkoxy substituent into departing [methanol](../../../../../methanol.md), and the resulting [oxocarbenium](../../../../../oxocarbenium-ion.md) is stabilized by its remaining [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md). [Water](../../../../../water.md) then drives [acetal hydrolysis](../../../../../acetal-hydrolysis.md) toward the [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md). [Ester hydrolysis](../../../../../ester-hydrolysis.md) is also possible under acid, but is slower under the conditions implied by the product data. The retained ethyl group and six distinct [carbon-13 NMR](../../../../../carbon-13-nmr.md) environments support E rather than the carboxylic acid that prolonged hydrolysis could produce.
