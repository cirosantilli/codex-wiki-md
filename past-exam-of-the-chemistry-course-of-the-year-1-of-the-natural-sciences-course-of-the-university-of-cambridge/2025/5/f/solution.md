<h1 id="5/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Adding the initiation step to the net forward trapping step cancels the nitrate-radical [reaction intermediate](../../../../reaction-intermediate.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{2NO_2+O_3\longrightarrow N_2O_5+O_2}.}
$$

The net [stoichiometry](../../../../stoichiometry.md) agrees with $-d[\mathrm{NO_2}]/dt\simeq2\,d[\mathrm{N_2O_5}]/dt$ under the [quasi-steady-state approximation](../../../../quasi-steady-state-approximation.md).
