<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Nitrogen dioxide is consumed in reactions 1 and 2 and regenerated in reaction $-2$, so

$$
\frac{d[\mathrm{NO_2}]}{dt}=-r_1-r_2+r_{-2}.
$$

Using $r_2-r_{-2}\simeq r_1$ from the nitrate-radical [quasi-steady-state approximation](../../../../quasi-steady-state-approximation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{d[\mathrm{NO_2}]}{dt}\simeq-2k_1[\mathrm{NO_2}][\mathrm{O_3}].}
$$

**The factor of two is essential:** an initiating event uses one nitrogen dioxide [molecule](../../../../molecule-split.md), and the ensuing net radical-trapping event uses a second one. The exact nitrogen balance before making this approximation is $d([\mathrm{NO_2}]+[\mathrm{NO_3}]+2[\mathrm{N_2O_5}])/dt=0$.
