<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

This labelled part introduces the reversible reaction pair without a separate calculation to perform. For the following derivations, define its forward and reverse [elementary reaction](../../../../elementary-reaction.md) rates as

$$
r_2=k_2[\mathrm{NO_2}][\mathrm{NO_3}],\qquad r_{-2}=k_{-2}[\mathrm{N_2O_5}],
$$

and the initiating rate as $r_1=k_1[\mathrm{NO_2}][\mathrm{O_3}]$. The [law of mass action](../../../../law-of-mass-action.md) gives $k_2$ bimolecular units and $k_{-2}$ first-order units. **Reversibility does not require $r_2=r_{-2}$ while the complete network is reacting**: that equality would impose [chemical equilibrium](../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) on the pair, an extra assumption not needed here.
