<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Arrhenius equation](../../../../arrhenius-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{k_1(T)=A\exp\!\left(-\frac{E_a}{RT}\right).}
$$

Here $E_a$ is the molar [activation energy](../../../../activation-energy.md), $R$ the [molar gas constant](../../../../molar-gas-constant.md), $T$ the absolute [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), and $A$ the [pre-exponential factor](../../../../pre-exponential-factor.md), taken approximately constant over the measured range. For this bimolecular [elementary reaction](../../../../elementary-reaction.md), $A$ and $k_1$ both have units $\mathrm{cm^3\,mol^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$.
