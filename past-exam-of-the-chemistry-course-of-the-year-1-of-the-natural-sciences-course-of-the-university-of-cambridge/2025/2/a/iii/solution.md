<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An emitted [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has positive [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md)

$$
E_\gamma=\frac{hc}{\lambda}
=\frac{(6.626\times10^{-34})(2.998\times10^8)}{18.2\times10^{-9}}
=1.09147\times10^{-17}\,\mathrm J.
$$

For [spontaneous emission](../../../../../spontaneous-emission.md) into $n_f=2$, the [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) equation is

$$
E_\gamma=E_{n_i}-E_2=36R_H\left(\frac14-\frac1{n_i^2}\right).
$$

Consequently

$$
\frac1{n_i^2}=\frac14-\frac{1.09147\times10^{-17}}{7.848\times10^{-17}}=0.110924,
\qquad n_i=3.0025.
$$

The [principal quantum number](../../../../../principal-quantum-number.md) must be an integer, so **the transition is $\boxed{n_i=3\longrightarrow n_f=2}$.** Its calculated [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) is approximately $18.22\,\mathrm{nm}$, agreeing with the rounded observation.
