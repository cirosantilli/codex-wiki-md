<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [hydrogen-like ion](../../../../../hydrogen-like-ion.md), the [Coulomb potential](../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md) is stronger by the nuclear [atomic number](../../../../../atomic-number.md) $Z$, and the [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md) scale as $Z^2$:

$$
\boxed{E_n=-\frac{Z^2R_H}{n^2},\qquad n=1,2,\ldots.}
$$

Here $Z$ is the number of [protons](../../../../../proton.md) in the [atomic nucleus](../../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md); the ion has exactly one [electron](../../../../../electron.md), so its net [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) is $(Z-1)e$. The zero of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is a separated stationary [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [atomic nucleus](../../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) at infinite distance. This is the nonrelativistic hydrogenic approximation using the supplied $R_H$; a more precise isotope-specific result also corrects the [reduced mass](../../../../../reduced-mass.md).
