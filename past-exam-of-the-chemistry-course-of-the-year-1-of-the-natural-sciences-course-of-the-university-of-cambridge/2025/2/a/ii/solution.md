↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The lowest two [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md) have $n=1$ and $n=2$.** For [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), $Z=6$, even though the ion's net charge is $5+$:

$$
\boxed{E_1=-36(2.180\times10^{-18})=-7.848\times10^{-17}\,\mathrm J,\qquad E_2=\frac{E_1}{4}=-1.962\times10^{-17}\,\mathrm J.}
$$

Increasing $n$ raises the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) toward zero; the most negative value is the [ground-state energy](../../../../../ground-state-energy.md).
