↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

One consistent localized [orbital hybridization](../../../../../orbital-hybridization.md) description uses the [Lewis structures](../../../../../lewis-structure.md)

$$
\mathrm{H_3C-N^+\equiv N},\qquad
\mathrm{H_2C=N^+=N^-}\ \leftrightarrow\ \mathrm{H_2C^--N^+\equiv N}.
$$

**For [methyldiazonium](../../../../../methyldiazonium.md), take C as $sp^3$ and both N atoms as $sp$. For [diazomethane](../../../../../diazomethane.md), the double-bond [resonance contributor](../../../../../resonance-structure.md) suggests C as $sp^2$, central N as $sp$, and terminal N as $sp^2$.** The central N uses two opposite $sp$ hybrids for the linear C–N–N [sigma bond](../../../../../sigma-bond.md) framework. In the localized double-bond picture, terminal N uses one $sp^2$ hybrid for its [sigma bond](../../../../../sigma-bond.md), two for [lone pairs](../../../../../lone-pair.md), and its remaining p [atomic orbital](../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) for a [pi bond](../../../../../pi-bond.md).

These labels specify an approximate bonding construction, not uniquely measurable atomic properties. An alternative representation takes terminal N as $sp$, with one $sp$ [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) and one p [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md); the latter can participate in the delocalized C–N–N $\pi$ system. It describes the same [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md) more explicitly. Neither construction requires an unpaired [electron](../../../../../electron.md) or a bent central N.
