↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**On carbon [protonation](../../../../../protonation.md), the C–N [bond length](../../../../../bond-length.md) increases and the N–N [bond length](../../../../../bond-length.md) decreases.** Forming the fourth C [sigma bond](../../../../../sigma-bond.md) changes the carbon from approximately planar $sp^2$ to tetrahedral $sp^3$, removing its effective C–N [pi bonding](../../../../../pi-bond.md). The product is described by $\mathrm{H_3C-N^+\equiv N}$: C–N becomes predominantly single and N–N predominantly triple. The [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md) of the unprotonated species makes its actual [bond orders](../../../../../bond-order.md) intermediate rather than exact integers.
