<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**There are $\boxed{4+2(1)+2(5)=16}$ [valence electrons](../../../../../valence-electron.md), all paired, so [diazomethane](../../../../../diazomethane.md) exhibits [diamagnetism](../../../../../diamagnetism.md).** A localized orbital occupancy consistent with the double-bond construction in 2(c)(i) is

$$
\begin{array}{l|r|l}
\text{occupied orbitals}&\text{electrons}&\text{atomic-orbital ingredients}\\\hline
2\ \mathrm{C-H}\ \sigma&4&\mathrm C(sp^2)+\mathrm H(1s)\\
\mathrm{C-N}\ \sigma&2&\mathrm C(sp^2)+\mathrm N_c(sp)\\
\mathrm{N-N}\ \sigma&2&\mathrm N_c(sp)+\mathrm N_t(sp^2)\\
\mathrm{C-N}\ \pi&2&\mathrm C(2p)+\mathrm N_c(2p)\\
\mathrm{N-N}\ \pi&2&\mathrm N_c(2p_\perp)+\mathrm N_t(2p)\\
2\ \mathrm N_t\text{ lone pairs}&4&2\ \mathrm N_t(sp^2)
\end{array}
$$

Here $c,t$ mean central and terminal. Each listed occupied spatial orbital contains two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) of opposite [spin](../../../../../spin.md). The central N's two p [atomic orbitals](../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) are perpendicular, so they can support the two perpendicular [pi bonds](../../../../../pi-bond.md). No central-N [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) is left over: its positive [formal charge](../../../../../formal-charge.md) accompanies four bonding pairs in this [Lewis structure](../../../../../lewis-structure.md).

Allowing [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md) mixes a terminal-N [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) into the C–N–N $\pi$ orbitals. In the alternative terminal-$sp$ basis, the same electron count is four occupied $\sigma$ orbitals ($8$ electrons), one terminal-$sp$ [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) ($2$), one perpendicular N–N $\pi$ orbital ($2$), and two occupied orbitals of a three-centre C–N–N $\pi$ system ($4$). The $16$-electron count and the paired ground state survive this change of description; the localized picture does not assert two perfectly isolated double bonds.
