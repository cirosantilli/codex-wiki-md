**The labels apply when the [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md) has an [inversion centre](../../../../../inversion-centre.md)**, as a homonuclear diatomic [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md) does. The label $g$ means $\psi(-\mathbf r)=\psi(\mathbf r)$, and $u$ means $\psi(-\mathbf r)=-\psi(\mathbf r)$, with the origin at that centre. In a heteronuclear diatomic [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md), inversion exchanges different [atomic nuclei](../../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) and these are generally not valid symmetry labels.

For the p-derived combinations above,

$$
\boxed{\sigma_g,\qquad\sigma_u^*,\qquad\pi_u,\qquad\pi_g^*.}
$$

Inversion both exchanges the two centres and changes the sign of each individual p [atomic orbital](../../../../../atomic-orbital.md). Applying both operations to each sum or difference gives these labels. The star denotes [antibonding molecular orbitals](../../../../../antibonding-orbital.md), independently of [parity](../../../../../parity.md): a bonding [molecular orbital](../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) need not always be $g$.
