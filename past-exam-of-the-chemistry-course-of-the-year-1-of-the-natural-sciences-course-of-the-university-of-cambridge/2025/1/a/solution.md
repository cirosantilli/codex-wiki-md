**The diagnostic [infrared spectroscopy](../../../../infrared-spectroscopy.md) absorptions are the conjugated [ester](../../../../ester.md) carbonyl near $1720\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ and the [alkene](../../../../alkene.md) stretch near $1650\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$.** Reasonable approximate ranges are $1715$–$1730$ and $1620$–$1660\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, respectively. [Conjugated system](../../../../conjugated-system.md) lowers the carbonyl stretching [spectroscopic wavenumber](../../../../spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) relative to an unconjugated [ester](../../../../ester.md).

The [ester](../../../../ester.md) C–O stretches and strong C–F stretches lie mainly in the $1000$–$1300\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ region. Vinylic C–H stretching is just above $3000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, and saturated C–H stretching is about $2850$–$3000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. There is no [hydroxy group](../../../../hydroxy-group.md) to give a broad O–H band.
