↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**One [fluorine-19 NMR](../../../../fluorine-19-nmr.md) resonance, integrating to three equivalent [fluorine](../../../../fluorine.md) nuclei, appears as a doublet of doublets with $J=6.6$ and $1.8\,\mathrm{Hz}$.** This predicts four principal lines of equal intensity, from coupling to $\mathrm H_a$ and $\mathrm H_b$, respectively. Relative to the unknown resonance centre, their offsets are $\pm4.2$ and $\pm2.4\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

Equivalent [fluorine](../../../../fluorine.md) nuclei do not split one another. This prediction assumes ordinary [proton](../../../../proton.md)-coupled acquisition; [proton decoupling](../../../../proton-decoupling.md) would collapse these four principal lines to a singlet. The proton and carbon spectra do not determine the numerical [fluorine](../../../../fluorine.md) [chemical shift](../../../../chemical-shift.md). Weak natural-abundance [carbon-13](../../../../carbon-13.md) satellites could accompany the principal pattern.
