↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The six carbon environments are assigned as follows**, with [chemical shifts](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) at the centres of the [fluorine](../../../../../fluorine.md)-split multiplets:

$$
\begin{array}{c|r|l}
\text{label}&\delta/\mathrm{ppm}&\text{multiplicity}\\\hline
\mathrm C_e\ (\mathrm{CF_3})&122.11&\text{quartet}\\
\mathrm C_f\ (\mathrm{CF_3CH{=}})&131.335&\text{quartet}\\
\mathrm C_g\ (\mathrm{{=}CHCO_2})&128.97&\text{quartet}\\
\mathrm C_h\ (\mathrm{C{=}O})&163.9&\text{singlet}\\
\mathrm C_j\ (\mathrm{OCH_2})&61.7&\text{singlet}\\
\mathrm C_k\ (\mathrm{CH_3})&14.1&\text{singlet}
\end{array}
$$

The supplied [carbon-13](../../../../../carbon-13.md) spectrum is interpreted as [proton-decoupled NMR](../../../../../proton-decoupled-nmr.md), as indicated by its unsplit ethoxy signals. [Proton decoupling](../../../../../proton-decoupling.md) removes C–H splitting but leaves C–F [scalar spin-spin coupling](../../../../../scalar-spin-spin-coupling.md). Three equivalent spin-$\tfrac12$ [fluorine](../../../../../fluorine.md) nuclei give each resolved quartet its $1:3:3:1$ intensity pattern. The carbonyl's much smaller four-bond C–F interaction is unresolved in the plotted spectrum; “singlet” describes what is observed, rather than proving an exactly zero interaction.

The wide quartet is centred at $(127.48+116.74)/2=122.11$ ppm. The other two centres are $(132.04+130.63)/2=131.335$ and $(129.09+128.85)/2=128.97$ ppm. The signal near $77$ ppm is the [deuterated chloroform](../../../../../deuterated-chloroform.md) [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md), not another carbon of the analyte. The structure labels skip $\mathrm C_i$: the ethoxy methylene is $\mathrm C_j$.
