↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The assignments are $\mathrm H_a$: $6.787$ ppm, $1$H, doublet of quartets; $\mathrm H_b$: $6.496$ ppm, $1$H, doublet of quartets; $\mathrm H_c$: $4.281$ ppm, $2$H, quartet; $\mathrm H_d$: $1.331$ ppm, $3$H, triplet.** The [chemical shift](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) of a multiplet is its centre, rather than its most downfield line.

Each vinylic [proton](../../../../../proton.md) has [scalar spin-spin coupling](../../../../../scalar-spin-spin-coupling.md) to the other vinylic [proton](../../../../../proton.md) and to three equivalent spin-$\tfrac12$ [fluorine](../../../../../fluorine.md) nuclei. The first interaction produces a doublet, and the second a $1:3:3:1$ quartet: eight lines overall, partly interleaved for $\mathrm H_a$. The two [chemical shifts](../../../../../chemical-shift.md) differ because the vinylic sites have different substituents.

The ethoxy $\mathrm{OCH_2}$ [protons](../../../../../proton.md) are deshielded by [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) and split into a $1:3:3:1$ quartet by the three methyl [protons](../../../../../proton.md). The methyl [protons](../../../../../proton.md) are split into a $1:2:1$ triplet by the two equivalent methylene [protons](../../../../../proton.md). The relative [NMR signal integrals](../../../../../nmr-signal-integral.md) are $1:1:2:3$.
