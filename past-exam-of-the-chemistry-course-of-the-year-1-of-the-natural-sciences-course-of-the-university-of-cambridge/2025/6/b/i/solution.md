<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**[Gallium phosphide](../../../../../gallium-phosphide.md) forms a tetrahedral network of [polar covalent bonds](../../../../../polar-covalent-bond.md), with electron density displaced toward [phosphorus](../../../../../phosphorus.md).** The alternating diamond-related structure is the [zinc blende structure](../../../../../zinc-blende-structure.md): each Ga has four P neighbours and each P has four Ga neighbours. A local [orbital hybridization](../../../../../orbital-hybridization.md) picture combines outward-pointing s,p-derived orbitals into four approximately $sp^3$ bond directions; in the crystal these interactions form extended occupied bonding bands.

Since $\chi_{\rm Allen}(\mathrm P)-\chi_{\rm Allen}(\mathrm{Ga})=2.25-1.76=0.49>0$, P attracts shared [electron density](../../../../../electron-density.md) more strongly. Write $\mathrm{Ga}^{\delta+}-\mathrm P^{\delta-}$ to indicate an ionic contribution superposed on [covalent bonds](../../../../../covalent-bond-split.md). Formal [oxidation states](../../../../../oxidation-state.md) Ga($+3$) and P($-3$) are electron-assignment labels, not proof of completely transferred charges or a purely ionic crystal.

**Neither atom requires $3d$ hybrids for these four bonds.** The Ga $3d^{10}$ shell is filled and relatively core-like; the relevant Ga valence orbitals are $4s,4p$. P uses principally $3s,3p$; its empty $3d$ orbitals are too high in energy to be a necessary bonding set. Detailed band calculations may include d-state mixing or core–valence effects, but that does not turn the tetrahedral bonding into an expanded-valence d-hybrid construction.
