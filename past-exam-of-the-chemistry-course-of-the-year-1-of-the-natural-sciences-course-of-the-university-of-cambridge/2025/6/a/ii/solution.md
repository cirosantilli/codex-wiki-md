<h1 id="6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**[Photoelectron spectroscopy](../../../../../photoemission-spectroscopy.md) measures orbital-dependent binding energies from which an effective charge can be inferred using a stated model.** Ultraviolet [photoelectron spectroscopy](../../../../../photoemission-spectroscopy.md) accesses outer-shell ionizations; [X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy](../../../../../x-ray-photoelectron-spectroscopy.md) accesses deeper core states. For an isolated gas-phase atom and a calibrated threshold, measure emitted-electron kinetic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and use $I=h\nu-E_{\rm kin}$, with the appropriate energy reference. Optical atomic term spectra and series limits also constrain valence ionization energies.

A hydrogenic inference would set $I\simeq R_\infty Z_{\rm eff}^2/n_{\rm eff}^2$, hence $Z_{\rm eff}\simeq n_{\rm eff}\sqrt{I/R_\infty}$, with $R_\infty$ here an energy of approximately $13.6\,\mathrm{eV}$ and $n_{\rm eff}$ a chosen effective shell parameter. **The measured binding energies are experimental; the extracted $Z_{\rm eff}$ values remain model dependent.** They need not equal Slater screening parameters fitted to radial functions.

Unlike the equal $ns,np$ values in [Slater's rules](../../../../../slater-s-rules.md), spectroscopic values distinguish s from p because their penetration and shielding differ; the more tightly bound s states usually imply larger binding-energy-derived effective charges at a common shell parameter. Subshell, atomic-term and, especially for heavier atoms, [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) structure give further distinctions. Electron relaxation following ionization and electron correlation also matter. Thus real binding energies vary across the heavier group-15 atoms even where the simple Slater outer-shell estimates plateau; no universal constant correction to the Slater values follows.[1981, binding-energy measurements and resolved atomic and chemical environments](../../../../../electron-spectroscopy-for-atoms-molecules-and-condensed-matter-by-kai-manne-borje-siegbahn.md)
