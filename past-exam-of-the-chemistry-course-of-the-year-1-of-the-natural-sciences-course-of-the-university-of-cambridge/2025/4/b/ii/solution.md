↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Nernst equation](../../../../../nernst-equation.md) for the six-electron cell reaction is

$$
\boxed{E=E^\circ-\frac{RT}{6F}\ln Q,\qquad
Q=\frac{a_{\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}}^3a_{\mathrm{NO}}^2}{a_{\mathrm{NO_3^-}}^2a_{\mathrm{H^+}}^8}.}
$$

Here pure solid [zinc](../../../../../zinc.md) and liquid [water](../../../../../water.md) have unit [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md). For ideal nitric oxide gas, $a_{\mathrm{NO}}=p_{\mathrm{NO}}/p^\circ$ with $p^\circ=1\,\mathrm{bar}$; for ideal dissolved species, $a_i=[i]/c^\circ$. The [reaction quotient](../../../../../reaction-quotient.md) is dimensionless. The [platinum](../../../../../platinum.md) supplies an electronically conducting surface and is absent from the net [chemical equation](../../../../../chemical-equation.md).
