<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Bulk [electroneutrality](../../../../../electroneutrality.md) gives $\boxed{[\mathrm{NO_3^-}]=[\mathrm{H^+}]+2[\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}]}$.** Each dissolved [zinc](../../../../../zinc.md) ion contributes two positive elementary charges, whereas each nitrate contributes one negative charge.

This assumes complete [nitric acid](../../../../../nitric-acid.md) dissociation, only $\mathrm H^+$ and $\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}$ as significant cations, and only nitrate as a significant anion. It neglects $\mathrm{OH^-}$ at acidic [pH](../../../../../ph.md), other dissolved salts, zinc complexes and additional ionic nitrogen products. It is a bulk charge balance; a thin charged electrode interface does not invalidate bulk electroneutrality.
