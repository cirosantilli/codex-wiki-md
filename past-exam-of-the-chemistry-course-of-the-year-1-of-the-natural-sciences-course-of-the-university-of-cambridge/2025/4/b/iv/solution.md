<h1 id="4/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

At [pH](../../../../../ph.md) $3$, $[\mathrm{H^+}]=10^{-3}\,\mathrm M$, so the nitrate [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) is $0.001+2(0.1)=0.201\,\mathrm M$. With ideal [thermodynamic activities](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md),

$$
Q=\frac{(0.1)^3(0.1)^2}{(0.201)^2(10^{-3})^8}=2.47519\times10^{20}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{E=1.719-\frac{8.3145(298)}{6(96485)}\ln(2.47519\times10^{20})=1.5180\,\mathrm V.}
$$

The activity of nitric oxide is $0.1$ because the supplied pressure is $0.1\,\mathrm{bar}$ and the gas standard is $1\,\mathrm{bar}$. This numerical result additionally approximates zinc and nitrate [thermodynamic activities](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) by their standard-concentration ratios; without their [activity coefficients](../../../../../activity-coefficient.md), a more precise nonideal value is not specified.
