<h1 id="4/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For the cell reaction normalized as in 4(b)(i), six moles of [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) are transferred per mole of reaction. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta_rG=-6FE=-6(96485)(1.5180208)=-8.78797\times10^5\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}\approx-879\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.}
$$

This is the [Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) at the stated composition and gas pressure, not the [standard Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../standard-gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md). Its negative sign confirms thermodynamic favourability in the written direction under those conditions.
