<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Oxidize [zinc](../../../../../zinc.md) at the [anode](../../../../../anode.md) and reduce nitrate to nitric oxide at the inert [platinum](../../../../../platinum.md) [cathode](../../../../../cathode.md). Multiplying the oxidation [half-reaction](../../../../../half-reaction.md) by three and the reduction [half-reaction](../../../../../half-reaction.md) by two cancels six [electrons](../../../../../electron.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{3Zn(s)+2NO_3^-(aq)+8H^+(aq)\longrightarrow3Zn^{2+}(aq)+2NO(g)+4H_2O(l)}.}
$$

The [standard cell potential](../../../../../standard-cell-potential.md) is the cathode reduction potential minus the anode reduction potential:

$$
\boxed{E^\circ=0.957-(-0.762)=1.719\,\mathrm V.}
$$

**The written direction is spontaneous under standard conditions**, since $\Delta_rG^\circ=-6FE^\circ<0$. Multiplying a [half-reaction](../../../../../half-reaction.md) changes its extensive [Gibbs energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md), but does not multiply its intensive [electrode potential](../../../../../electrode-potential.md).
