The [standard Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../standard-gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) gives

$$
\ln K_a=-\frac{\Delta_rG^\circ}{RT}
=-\frac{41133}{8.3145(298)}=-16.6011,
\qquad\boxed{K_a=6.17\times10^{-8}.}
$$

The positive [standard Gibbs energy of reaction](../../../../../standard-gibbs-energy-of-reaction.md) means that dissociation is unfavourable when every [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) equals one; it does not forbid partial dissociation. The corresponding $\mathrm pK_a=-\log_{10}K_a$ is about $7.21$.
