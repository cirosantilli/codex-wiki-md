<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**The equilibrium molecular-acid concentration is $\boxed{[\mathrm{H_2N_2O_2}]=0.162\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}}$.** From the supplied [pH](../../../../../ph.md) convention,

$$
[\mathrm{H^+}]=c^\circ10^{-4}=10^{-4}\,\mathrm M.
$$

With only these monovalent [ions](../../../../../ion.md) appreciably present, [electroneutrality](../../../../../electroneutrality.md) requires $[\mathrm{HN_2O_2^-}]=[\mathrm{H^+}]$. Substitution in the [acid dissociation constant](../../../../../acid-dissociation-constant.md) gives

$$
[\mathrm{H_2N_2O_2}]
=\frac{(10^{-4}\,\mathrm M)^2}{(6.1690\times10^{-8})(1\,\mathrm M)}
=0.16210\,\mathrm M.
$$

This calculation assumes ideal [thermodynamic activities](../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) for the acid and its conjugate base as well as for $\mathrm H^+$, negligible water [autoionization](../../../../../autoionization.md), negligible second dissociation, and no additional electrolyte. The acid's analytical concentration would include its dissociated fraction and be approximately $0.16220\,\mathrm M$, rather than exactly the molecular-acid concentration requested.
