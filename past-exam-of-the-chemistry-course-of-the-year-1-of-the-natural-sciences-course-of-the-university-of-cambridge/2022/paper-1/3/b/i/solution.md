<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The two starting [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) groups are adjacent on the [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) ring. Select one [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) as the site receiving both incoming [methyl groups](../../../../../../methyl-group.md). A first methyl addition gives a [tetrahedral intermediate](../../../../../../tetrahedral-intermediate.md); its collapse expels [methoxide](../../../../../../methoxide.md) to give the ortho-substituted [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) $\mathrm{o\!-!MeO_2C-C_6H_4-C(=O)Me}$. A second methyl addition produces $\mathrm{o\!-!MeO_2C-C_6H_4-C(O^-)Me_2}$, with the [alkoxide](../../../../../../alkoxide.md) bound to [magnesium](../../../../../../magnesium.md).

The newly generated [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) is now positioned to attack the remaining [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) intramolecularly. Draw an arrow from its [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) to that [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and another from the C=O [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md) to its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md). Collapse of the resulting [tetrahedral intermediate](../../../../../../tetrahedral-intermediate.md) reforms the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) and expels [methoxide](../../../../../../methoxide.md), closing a five-membered [lactone](../../../../../../lactone.md). Protonation of magnesium-bound species in the work-up gives **[3,3-Dimethylphthalide](../../../../../../3-3-dimethylphthalide.md)**. Equivalently, protonation can first give the ortho hydroxy [ester](../../../../../../ester.md), followed by acid-assisted [lactonization](../../../../../../lactonization.md) with loss of methanol; both descriptions show the same required bond formation.

The [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) in the new ring is the [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) of the first [ester](../../../../../../ester.md)'s original [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md); the surviving [lactone](../../../../../../lactone.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) comes from the second [ester](../../../../../../ester.md). The product retains the six ring carbons and both original acyl carbons, loses the two [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) methoxy carbons, and gains two carbons from [methylmagnesium bromide](../../../../../../methylmagnesium-bromide.md). The favourable intramolecular five-membered closure accounts for the displayed product. It does not make the isolated [lactone](../../../../../../lactone.md) universally inert to a large excess of [grignard reagent](../../../../../../grignard-reagent.md); stoichiometry and trapping/work-up conditions matter.
