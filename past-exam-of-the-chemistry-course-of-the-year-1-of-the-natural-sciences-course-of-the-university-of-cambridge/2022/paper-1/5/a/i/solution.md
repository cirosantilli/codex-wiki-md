<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With the forward direction defined as cis-to-trans, the elementary steps have rates $k_1[\mathrm{cis}]$ and $k_{-1}[\mathrm{trans}]$. One trans molecule is gained per forward event and lost per reverse event, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{\mathrm d[\mathrm{trans}]}{\mathrm dt}=k_1[\mathrm{cis}]-k_{-1}[\mathrm{trans}].}
$$

Each [rate constant](../../../../../../rate-constant.md) has units $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, and the right side has units of [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) per unit [time](../../../../../../time-in-physics.md). This net rate can have either sign, even though the individual event rates are nonnegative.
