<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $C_{\rm tot}=[\mathrm{cis}]+[\mathrm{trans}]$, which is conserved for this closed one-to-one [isomerization](../../../../../../isomerization.md) at fixed volume. Substitute $[\mathrm{cis}]_{298}=C_{\rm tot}-[\mathrm{trans}]_{298}$ into the equilibrium ratio:

$$
[\mathrm{trans}]_{298}=K_{\rm eq}(C_{\rm tot}-[\mathrm{trans}]_{298}).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{[\mathrm{trans}]_{298}=\frac{K_{\rm eq}}{1+K_{\rm eq}}C_{\rm tot},\qquad[\mathrm{cis}]_{298}=\frac{1}{1+K_{\rm eq}}C_{\rm tot}.}
$$

The equilibrium split is independent of having started with pure cis, provided equilibration is complete and no side reaction changes the total [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md).
