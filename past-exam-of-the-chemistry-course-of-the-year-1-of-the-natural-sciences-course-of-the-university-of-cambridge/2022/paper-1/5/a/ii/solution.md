<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At [chemical equilibrium](../../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md), the forward and reverse event rates are equal, rather than both reactions stopping:

$$
k_1[\mathrm{cis}]_{\rm eq}=k_{-1}[\mathrm{trans}]_{\rm eq}.
$$

For the dilute-solution [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) model,

$$
\boxed{K_{\rm eq}=\frac{[\mathrm{trans}]_{\rm eq}}{[\mathrm{cis}]_{\rm eq}}=\frac{k_1}{k_{-1}}.}
$$

The ratio is dimensionless because both [concentrations](../../../../../../concentration.md) and both [rate constants](../../../../../../rate-constant.md) have matching units. An exact thermodynamic [equilibrium constant](../../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) uses activities; here the common standard [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) and ideal-solution activity coefficients cancel.
