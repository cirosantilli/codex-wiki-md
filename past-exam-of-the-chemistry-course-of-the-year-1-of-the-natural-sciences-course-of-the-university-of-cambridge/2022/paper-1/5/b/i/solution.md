<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The forward and reverse pathways pass through the same [transition state](../../../../../../transition-state.md). Measured from the cis minimum, the forward barrier is $E_{a,1}$; measured from the trans minimum, the reverse barrier is $E_{a,-1}$. Since $E_{a,-1}=E_{a,1}+2.23\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$, the trans minimum lies **$2.23\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ below the cis minimum** in the elementary energy-profile model:

$$
E_{\rm trans}-E_{\rm cis}=E_{a,1}-E_{a,-1}=-2.23\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.
$$

The absolute barrier heights cannot be deduced from their difference. The following original profile uses an arbitrary transition-state height and labels that distinction explicitly.
