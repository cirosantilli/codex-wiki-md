↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) increase shifts the equilibrium towards cis.** The ratio $[\mathrm{trans}]/[\mathrm{cis}]$ decreases from about $2.46$ to $2.31$, so the trans fraction falls from $0.71095$ to $2.31/3.31=0.69789$. Trans remains the more abundant isomer; “favours cis” describes the direction of the change, rather than requiring cis to become the majority.
