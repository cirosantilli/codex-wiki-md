<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In molecular [boron trichloride](../../../../../../boron-trichloride.md), [boron](../../../../../../boron.md) uses three approximately $sp^2$ hybrids to form three equivalent B–Cl [sigma bonds](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md) in a trigonal plane. Its perpendicular empty $2p$ orbital can accept [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) density from the compatible combination of filled [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) $3p$ [lone pairs](../../../../../../lone-pair.md). The resulting [pi donation](../../../../../../pi-donation.md) is delocalized over the three B–Cl connections.

In the elementary model, one net [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md) is distributed over three equivalent [sigma bonds](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md), giving **formal average B–Cl [bond order](../../../../../../bond-order.md) $1+1/3=4/3$**. The corresponding [resonance contributors](../../../../../../resonance-structure.md) each put a B=Cl connection in a different position, with formal negative [boron](../../../../../../boron.md) and positive donating [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md). They do not mean one experimentally distinct double bond and two single bonds. The $4/3$ value is the idealized sigma-plus-delocalized-pi bookkeeping value; a computed bond index need not equal that exact rational number.
