<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Dimerization lowers [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) by letting the coordinatively unsaturated [aluminium](../../../../../../aluminium.md) centres accept [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) lone-pair donation. Effective intramolecular Cl-to-Al pi stabilization of the monomer is relatively weak, while [aluminium](../../../../../../aluminium.md)'s larger size accommodates four-coordinate geometry and two [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) bridges. At room [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) this bonding gain can outweigh the [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) penalty of associating two molecules.

In [boron trichloride](../../../../../../boron-trichloride.md), the compact short B–Cl framework supports appreciable ligand-to-boron [pi donation](../../../../../../pi-donation.md), stabilizing the planar monomer. Its smaller [boron](../../../../../../boron.md) centre also makes the crowded four-coordinate bridged arrangement less favourable. Thus dimerization must be assessed from the balance of monomer stabilization, bridge-bond formation, structural strain and [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md); merely counting six electrons around each monomeric centre would incorrectly predict identical association for both. [orbital overlap](../../../../../../orbital-overlap.md) depends on radial extent, bond length and energy matching, not just whether two orbitals share the same principal quantum number.
