↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The dimer has four terminal chlorides and two [bridging chloride ligands](../../../../../../bridging-chloride-ligand.md). Each [aluminium](../../../../../../aluminium.md) is approximately tetrahedrally surrounded by two terminal and two bridging chlorines. A [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) from one $\mathrm{AlCl_3}$ fragment donates into an empty orbital of the other [Lewis acid](../../../../../../lewis-acid.md) centre; reciprocal donation closes the four-membered $\mathrm{Al_2Cl_2}$ ring.

The localized [coordinate covalent bond](../../../../../../coordinate-covalent-bond.md) model has **single Al–Cl bonds, formal [bond order](../../../../../../bond-order.md) one, for both terminal and bridging connections**. Donation describes the origin of a pair, not half an electron-pair bond. A bridge [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) has two bonded pairs and two remaining [lone pairs](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) in this all-single-bond Lewis accounting; the original terminal chlorines have three [lone pairs](../../../../../../lone-pair.md). Bridging contacts are longer and weaker than terminal ones, so equal formal order does not imply equal strength or equal numerical quantum-chemical bond indices. In particular, these [chloride](../../../../../../chloride.md) bridges should not be assigned the electron-deficient methyl-bridge half-bonds of (b).
