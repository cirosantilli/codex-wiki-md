<h1 id="2/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

The four [sigma bonds](../../../../../sigma-bond.md) are the two C–H connections, C–N and N–N, containing eight electrons. A terminal-nitrogen axial [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md) contains two more. Of the remaining six electrons, two occupy an N–N [pi bond](../../../../../pi-bond.md) lying in the molecular plane, while four occupy a perpendicular [three-centre four-electron pi bond](../../../../../three-centre-four-electron-pi-bond.md) extending over C–N–N. There are therefore 16 [valence electrons](../../../../../valence-electron.md) in total.

For orientation, a three-site model with equal on-site energy $\alpha$ and adjacent interaction $\beta<0$ gives perpendicular pi levels at $\alpha+\sqrt2\beta$, $\alpha$, and $\alpha-\sqrt2\beta$. The lower two are doubly occupied and the upper is empty. The middle level has a terminal-end combination with little central-atom amplitude; unequal C and N orbital energies change its coefficients and make it only approximately nonbonding.

The **[highest occupied molecular orbital](../../../../../highest-occupied-molecular-orbital.md) is the upper occupied perpendicular pi orbital**, with substantial [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) amplitude, broadly above the strongly bonding sigma framework and the lower occupied pi orbitals. It is not the terminal axial [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md). Real [diazomethane](../../../../../diazomethane.md) has C–N bonding and N–N antibonding character in this frontier orbital, rather than being an exactly nonbonding equal-site model. Photoelectron and Rydberg-state experiments support this assignment.[Abstract and discussion of ionization: removal from a C–N-bonding, N–N-antibonding pi orbital](../../../../../vibronic-structure-and-ion-core-interactions-in-rydberg-states-of-diazomethane-an-experimental-and-theoretical-investigation-by-igor-fedorov-and-others.md)
