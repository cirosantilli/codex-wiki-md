<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The framework has a planar $\mathrm{H_2C}$ group attached to a straight C–N–N axis. Two useful octet [resonance contributors](../../../../../resonance-structure.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{H_2C=N^+=N^-\ \leftrightarrow\ H_2C^--N^+\equiv N}.}
$$

In the left contributor, the terminal [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) has two [lone pairs](../../../../../lone-pair.md); in the right, the terminal [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) and [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) each have one. Each formula has 16 valence [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and zero net [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md). The double-headed resonance arrow changes the electronic allocation while retaining the same nuclear framework; these are not two different equilibrium species.
