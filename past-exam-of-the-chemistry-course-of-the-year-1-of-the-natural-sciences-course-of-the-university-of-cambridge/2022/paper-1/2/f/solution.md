<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

An internally consistent elementary [orbital hybridisation](../../../../../orbital-hybridization.md) model is **$sp^2$ at [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) and $sp$ at both [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) atoms; the hydrogens use $1s$ orbitals**. The three [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) hybrids form two C–H [sigma bonds](../../../../../sigma-bond.md) and one C–N [sigma bond](../../../../../sigma-bond.md), leaving one perpendicular [p orbital](../../../../../p-orbital.md). The central [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) uses opposite $sp$ hybrids for its two collinear sigma connections, leaving two perpendicular [p orbitals](../../../../../p-orbital.md) for the two pi systems.

At terminal [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md), one $sp$ hybrid points toward the central [nitrogen](../../../../../nitrogen.md) and the other houses an axial [lone pair](../../../../../lone-pair.md). One perpendicular [p orbital](../../../../../p-orbital.md) forms the second, N–N-only pi system; the other participates in the three-atom C–N–N pi system. This description remains the same across both [resonance contributors](../../../../../resonance-structure.md); counting lone-pair domains in just one drawing and assigning a different terminal hybridisation to each contributor would obscure the delocalization. [orbital hybridisation](../../../../../orbital-hybridization.md) is a useful choice of localized basis, not a uniquely measurable label: advanced treatments of [diazomethane](../../../../../diazomethane.md) require a superposition of bonding patterns.[Abstract: limitations of single bonding patterns and multiconfigurational description](../../../../../on-the-electronic-structure-of-the-diazomethane-molecule-by-andre-g-h-barbosa-and-joao-g-s-monteiro.md)
