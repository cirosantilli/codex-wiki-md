↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

An intense band near $2088\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ is consistent with the high-frequency coupled C–N–N stretching motion of a [diazo group](../../../../../diazo-group.md), with substantial N–N multiple-bond character. It lies above a typical isolated C=N double-bond stretch, and supports contributions with a strong N–N connection. It does not establish that the molecule is a literal localized C–N single bond plus an unperturbed N≡N triple bond: a [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) moves the coupled framework, and the [resonance contributors](../../../../../resonance-structure.md) describe one electronic structure.

Protonation at the nucleophilic [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) gives **[methyldiazonium](../../../../../methyldiazonium.md), $\mathrm{CH_3-N^+\equiv N}$**. The carbon-rich [highest occupied molecular orbital](../../../../../highest-occupied-molecular-orbital.md) and the carbon-negative [resonance contributor](../../../../../resonance-structure.md) rationalize this site. Subsequent displacement of [molecular nitrogen](../../../../../molecular-nitrogen.md) can be strongly favourable; the initial protonation product still contains both nitrogens.
