↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The one-electron species are $\mathrm{C^{5+}}$ and $\mathrm{N^{6+}}$, so there is no [electron](../../../../../electron.md)–[electron](../../../../../electron.md) screening and the nuclear charges remain $Z=6$ and $7$.

$$
\boxed{E_{n=2}(\mathrm{C^{5+}})=-13.6\frac{6^2}{4}=-122.4\,\mathrm{eV},\qquad E_{n=2}(\mathrm{N^{6+}})=-13.6\frac{7^2}{4}=-166.6\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

Within the stated [hydrogen-like ion](../../../../../hydrogen-like-ion.md) model, $2s$ and all three $2p$ [atomic orbitals](../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) of a given species have the same energy. The energy zero is a separated [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and the residual nucleus.
