**B is [2-Methylpropanoyl chloride](../../../../../../2-methylpropanoyl-chloride.md), $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHC(=O)Cl}$.** The single index of [index of hydrogen deficiency](../../../../../../degree-of-unsaturation.md) is supplied by the [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). Its relatively high-frequency [infrared spectroscopy](../../../../../../infrared-spectroscopy.md) band identifies an [acyl chloride](../../../../../../acyl-chloride.md), while two equivalent [methyl groups](../../../../../../methyl-group.md) explain why four [carbons](../../../../../../carbon.md) produce only three [carbon-13 NMR](../../../../../../carbon-13-nmr.md) resonances.

Assign $\delta_C=178.1$ to the [acyl chloride](../../../../../../acyl-chloride.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md), $46.3$ to the methine and $18.9$ ppm to both [methyl groups](../../../../../../methyl-group.md). Their [chemical equivalence in NMR](../../../../../../chemical-equivalence-in-nmr.md) follows from the symmetry of the isopropyl fragment in this achiral environment.
