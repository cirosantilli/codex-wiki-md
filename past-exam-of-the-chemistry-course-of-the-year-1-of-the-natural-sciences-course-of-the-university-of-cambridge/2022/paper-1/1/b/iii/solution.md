<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The two [methyl groups](../../../../../../methyl-group.md) give one six-[proton](../../../../../../proton.md) [NMR doublet](../../../../../../nmr-doublet.md) centred at $(1.319+1.296)/2=1.3075$ ppm. The methine gives a one-[proton](../../../../../../proton.md) [NMR septet](../../../../../../nmr-septet.md) centred near $2.976$ ppm. The latter is split by six equivalent methyl [protons](../../../../../../proton.md), producing the first-order $1:6:15:20:15:6:1$ intensity pattern.

$$
\boxed{{}^3J_{\mathrm{CH,CH_3}}\simeq6.9\,\mathrm{Hz}.}
$$

This follows exactly from the methyl doublet spacing, $300(0.023)$, and approximately from the successive septet spacings, which alternate between $0.022$ and $0.024$ ppm within the precision of the printed labels. There is no methylene triplet or quartet: the six-[proton](../../../../../../proton.md) integral and septet establish the branched skeleton.
