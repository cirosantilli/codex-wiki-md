↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [vinyl ether](../../../../../../vinyl-ether.md) has an important [resonance contributor](../../../../../../resonance-structure.md)

$$
\mathrm{CH_2=CH-O-R\ \leftrightarrow\ {}^-CH_2-CH=O^+-R},\qquad R=\mathrm{CH_2CH_2Cl}.
$$

Donation from [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) into the [conjugated system](../../../../../../conjugated-system.md) increases [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) density at the terminal unsaturated [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), helping explain its unusually shielded $\delta_C=87.4$ ppm and its terminal [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) near 4.1–4.2 ppm. The directly oxygen-bound unsaturated [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) is strongly deshielded at $151.1$ ppm; its [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) at $6.480$ ppm also experiences the [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) substituent and [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) magnetic anisotropy. These trends are consistent with the whole delocalized electronic structure; a [chemical shift](../../../../../../chemical-shift.md) is not simply a meter for an atom's [partial atomic charge](../../../../../../partial-atomic-charge.md).

For the saturated part, the stronger local deshielding by [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) places $\mathrm{OCH_2}$ at $\delta_C=67.8$, $\delta_H\simeq3.948$, while $\mathrm{CH_2Cl}$ lies at $41.9$ and $3.708$ ppm. The two terminal vinylic [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) have different geometrical relationships to the [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) substituent and therefore different [chemical shifts](../../../../../../chemical-shift.md) as well as different [vicinal couplings](../../../../../../vicinal-coupling.md).
