<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**C is [2-Chloroethyl vinyl ether](../../../../../../2-chloroethyl-vinyl-ether.md), $\mathrm{CH_2=CH-O-CH_2-CH_2Cl}$.** There is one [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) and no [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). Assign the [carbon-13 NMR](../../../../../../carbon-13-nmr.md) resonances as

$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{site}&\delta_C/\mathrm{ppm}\\\hline
\mathrm{CH_2=}&87.4\\
\mathrm{=CH-O}&151.1\\
\mathrm{OCH_2}&67.8\\
\mathrm{CH_2Cl}&41.9
\end{array}
$$

The strongly differentiated pair of [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) shifts is characteristic of a [vinyl ether](../../../../../../vinyl-ether.md); interpreting $151.1$ ppm as a [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) would leave the unusually shielded unsaturated $87.4$ ppm [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and the three distinct vinylic [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) unexplained.
