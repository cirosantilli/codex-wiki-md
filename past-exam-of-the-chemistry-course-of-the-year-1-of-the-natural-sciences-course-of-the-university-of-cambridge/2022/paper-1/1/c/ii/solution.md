↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The $1621\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ absorption is predominantly the [carbon–carbon double-bond stretching vibration](../../../../../../carbon-carbon-double-bond-stretching-vibration.md) of the electron-rich [vinyl ether](../../../../../../vinyl-ether.md). [resonance donation](../../../../../../resonance-donation.md) from an [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) changes both its frequency and its [electric dipole moment](../../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md) variation during stretching. There is no [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) in the assigned structure, consistent with the absence of a [carbon-13 NMR](../../../../../../carbon-13-nmr.md) signal in the usual [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md) or [acyl chloride](../../../../../../acyl-chloride.md) region.
