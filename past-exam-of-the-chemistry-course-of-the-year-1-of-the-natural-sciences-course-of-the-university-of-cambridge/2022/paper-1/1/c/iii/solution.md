<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $a$ be the internal vinylic [proton](../../../../../../proton.md), $b$ the terminal [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) trans to $a$, $c$ the terminal [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) cis to $a$, $d$ the oxygen-bound methylene and $e$ the chlorine-bound methylene. The assignments are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{site}&\delta_H/\mathrm{ppm}&\text{integral}&\text{multiplicity}\\\hline
a&6.480&1&\text{doublet of doublets}\\
b&4.220&1&\text{doublet of doublets}\\
c&4.072&1&\text{doublet of doublets}\\
d&3.948&2&\text{triplet}\\
e&3.708&2&\text{triplet}
\end{array}
$$

For the $a$ [doublet of doublets](../../../../../../doublet-of-doublets.md), pairing corresponding lines gives $J_{ab}\simeq300[(6.516-6.468)+(6.492-6.445)]/2=14.25$ Hz and $J_{ac}\simeq300[(6.516-6.492)+(6.468-6.445)]/2=7.05$ Hz. The larger value identifies [trans vinylic coupling](../../../../../../trans-vinylic-coupling.md); the smaller identifies [cis vinylic coupling](../../../../../../cis-vinylic-coupling.md). The $b$ resonance confirms the large splitting, approximately $14.25$ Hz, and has a small [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) to $c$.

The small spacings are not perfectly reciprocal if treated as exact [First-order NMR multiplets](../../../../../../first-order-nmr-multiplet.md): the $b$ lines imply $|J_{bc}|\simeq2.55$ Hz, whereas the $c$ lines imply $|J_{bc}|\simeq2.10$ Hz and $J_{ac}\simeq6.60$ Hz. Thus report **$J_{ab}\simeq14.3$ Hz, $J_{ac}\simeq7$ Hz and $|J_{bc}|\simeq2$–$3$ Hz**, rather than asserting incompatible exact constants. Printed precision and departures from a simple first-order pattern limit this line-picking estimate; a full spin-system fit would be needed for refined values. Splittings alone do not establish the signs of these couplings.

The two [methylene groups](../../../../../../methylene-group.md) split one another into approximate [NMR triplets](../../../../../../nmr-triplet.md), with $J_{de}\simeq300(3.727-3.708)=5.7$ Hz; the oxygen-bound triplet gives approximately the same value from its rounded line labels. The three vinylic [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) plus two methylenes give the required seven-[proton](../../../../../../proton.md) integral. The terminal vinylic [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) are [diastereotopic](../../../../../../diastereotopic.md), because replacing one or the other creates different alkene geometries.
