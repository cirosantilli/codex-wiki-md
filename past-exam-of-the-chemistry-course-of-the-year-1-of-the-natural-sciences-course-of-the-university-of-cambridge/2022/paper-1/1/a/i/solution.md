↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**A is [3-Chlorobutan-2-one](../../../../../../3-chlorobutan-2-one.md), $\mathrm{CH_3C(=O)CH(Cl)CH_3}$.** Counting [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md) as one [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) in the index of [index of hydrogen deficiency](../../../../../../degree-of-unsaturation.md) gives $(2\times4+2-7-1)/2=1$. A single [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) accounts for that unsaturation; the resonance at $203.2$ ppm identifies a [ketone](../../../../../../ketone.md), while the $59.0$ ppm methine bears [chlorine](../../../../../../chlorine.md).

The [carbon-13 NMR](../../../../../../carbon-13-nmr.md) assignments are $\delta_C=203.2$ for $\mathrm{C=O}$, $25.7$ for $\mathrm{CH_3CO}$, $59.0$ for $\mathrm{CHCl}$ and $20.1$ ppm for $\mathrm{CH(Cl)CH_3}$. The four distinct [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) environments agree with the unsymmetrical structure. The small signal near 77 ppm belongs to the usual deuterated chloroform [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md), rather than to a fifth [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) of A.
