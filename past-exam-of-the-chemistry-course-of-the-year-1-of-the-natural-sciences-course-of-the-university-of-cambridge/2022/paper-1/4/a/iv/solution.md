<h1 id="4/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [Clausius statement of the second law](../../../../../../clausius-statement-of-the-second-law.md) forbids a cyclic device whose sole effect is transfer of [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) from a colder [thermal reservoir](../../../../../../heat-bath.md) to a hotter one. The [Kelvin-Planck statement of the second law](../../../../../../kelvin-planck-statement-of-the-second-law.md) forbids a cyclic device whose sole effect is complete conversion of [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) drawn from a single [thermal reservoir](../../../../../../heat-bath.md) into [work](../../../../../../work.md). These are equivalent formulations of the [Second law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md); an ordinary refrigerator is allowed because supplied [work](../../../../../../work.md) is an additional effect.
