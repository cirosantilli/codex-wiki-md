The **[standard enthalpy of formation](../../../../../../standard-enthalpy-of-formation.md)** is the [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) change on forming exactly one [mole](../../../../../../mole-unit.md) of the specified compound in its stated [standard state](../../../../../../standard-state.md) from its constituent [chemical elements](../../../../../../chemical-element.md) in their reference states at the same specified [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). The reference states are normally the stable elemental forms at the standard [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md). A [standard state](../../../../../../standard-state.md) does not itself mean 298 K; [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) must be stated separately.
