↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For $\mathrm{C_6H_6(l)\rightarrow6C(graphite)+3H_2(g)}$, reversing the standard formation reaction gives $\Delta_rG^\circ=-124.3\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. Thus decomposition is thermodynamically favoured under the corresponding standard conditions. **[benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) persists because it is kinetically stable on the laboratory timescale.** Breaking and reorganizing its bonding framework requires access to high-[activation energy](../../../../../../activation-energy.md) pathways, and suitable reactive intermediates or catalysts are absent under ordinary storage conditions. A negative [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) change specifies the favoured thermodynamic direction, not a rapid [reaction rate](../../../../../../reaction-rate.md); thermal energy does not automatically overcome a large barrier.
