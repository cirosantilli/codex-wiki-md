<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For red-to-yellow conversion, subtract the red values from the yellow values:

$$
\Delta_rH^\circ=(-90.2)-(-90.7)=+0.5\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}},\qquad
\Delta_rS^\circ=73.0-72.0=+1.0\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}\,mol^{-1}}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\Delta_rG^\circ(T)=500-T\quad\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}},\qquad\Delta_rG^\circ(298\,\mathrm K)=+202\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}\simeq+0.20\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.}
$$

The positive value makes the red form the lower-[Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) form at 298 K within the supplied bulk-phase model. The calculation uses 298 K as the conventional reference [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md); the paper supplies no separate numerical [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) for this subpart. Keeping [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) and [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) in the same energy units is essential.
