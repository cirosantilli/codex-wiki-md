**The assignments are A → spectrum 3, B → spectrum 4, C → spectrum 1, and D → spectrum 2.** The useful [infrared spectroscopy](../../../../../infrared-spectroscopy.md) evidence is the combination of functional-group absorptions, rather than every feature of the fingerprint region.

A is a saturated diketone: spectrum 3 has a strong [ketone](../../../../../ketone.md) C=O band near $1710\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ without the very broad acid O–H absorption. Its two symmetry-related carbonyls do not require two well-separated diagnostic bands.

B is a conjugated dienoic acid: spectrum 4 combines a broad [carboxylic acid](../../../../../carboxylic-acid.md) O–H envelope, approximately $2500$–$3300\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, with a conjugated C=O band near $1690$–$1700\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ and C=C absorptions around $1600$–$1650\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$.

C is an alkynoic acid: spectrum 1 contains the broad acid O–H absorption, a carbonyl near $1700\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, and a conspicuous C≡C stretch near $2250\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. Its [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) is internal, so no terminal ≡C–H stretch is expected.

D is a conjugated vinyl [ester](../../../../../ester.md): spectrum 2 has a strong ester C=O absorption around $1730$–$1740\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, alkene features near $1640\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, and strong C–O fingerprint absorptions, but no broad acid O–H envelope. These are approximate readings of the original axes, not exact assignments of every vibrational mode.
