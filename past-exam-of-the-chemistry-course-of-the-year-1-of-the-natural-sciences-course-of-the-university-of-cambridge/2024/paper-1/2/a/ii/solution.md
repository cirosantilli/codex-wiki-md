**[Ozone](../../../../../../ozone.md) is bent, with two equivalent O–O bonds of approximate [bond order](../../../../../../bond-order.md) $1.5$.** Its useful localized [orbital hybridization](../../../../../../orbital-hybridization.md) description is $sp^2$ at all three oxygens. Two hybrids at the central oxygen form sigma bonds and a third contains a [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md); the three perpendicular p orbitals form a [three-centre four-electron pi bond](../../../../../../three-centre-four-electron-pi-bond.md).

The three pi orbitals are bonding, nonbonding and antibonding, filled as $\pi_{\rm b}^2\pi_{\rm nb}^2\pi_*^0$. The terminal-centred nonbonding orbital is the [Homo](../../../../../../homo.md), conventionally $1a_2$; the delocalized antibonding orbital is the [LUMO](../../../../../../lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md), $2b_1$. The two principal [resonance structures](../../../../../../resonance-structure.md), $\mathrm{^-O-O^+=O}$ and $\mathrm{O=O^+-O^-}$, distribute the extra pi bond across the two links.

The 18 valence electrons comprise four sigma-bond electrons, ten in five sigma-framework lone pairs, and four in the pi system. This gives a paired ground-state model. More accurate treatments include electron correlation; the simple bond-order picture is not an exact charge-density calculation.[2021, §2.3 and Table 9: ozone HOMO and LUMO](../../../../../../the-structures-molecular-orbital-properties-and-vibrational-spectra-of-the-homo-and-heterodimers-of-sulphur-dioxide-and-ozone-an-ab-initio-study-by-thomas-a-ford.md)
