<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Methyl addition gives 1-methylcyclohexanol, whose acid-catalysed dehydration yields the shown 1-methylcyclohexene.** The methyl end of [methyllithium](../../../../../../methyllithium.md) attacks the cyclohexanone carbonyl carbon, and the C=O pi electrons move onto oxygen, giving a lithium alkoxide. Protonation gives the tertiary alcohol.

Protonate OH to make $\mathrm{OH_2^+}$, then cleave its C–O bond with both electrons going to departing water. A base removes a beta hydrogen from either adjacent ring methylene, and that C–H pair forms the C=C bond to the tertiary cationic centre. This [E1 elimination](../../../../../../e1-elimination.md) regenerates the acid catalyst. The two adjacent ring positions are equivalent, and the displayed endocyclic alkene is more substituted than the possible exocyclic methylene alternative.
