<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For each ideal gas, the [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) relative to the pure gas at standard pressure is $\Delta\mu_i=RT\ln(p_i/p^\circ)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{F_2}}&=-78.45\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}},\\
\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{ClF}}&=-0.2611\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}},\\
\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{ClF_3}}&=-5.705\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

All three shifts are negative because their partial pressures are below $1\,\mathrm{bar}$. As a check, $\Delta_rG^\circ+\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{ClF_3}}-\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{ClF}}-\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{F_2}}=0$ at equilibrium, within rounding.
