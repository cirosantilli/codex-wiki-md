<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Subtract the second supplied reaction from the first. Chlorine trifluoride and aqueous HCl cancel, while one water and two HF remain on their respective sides, giving the required reaction. By [Hess's law](../../../../../hess-s-law.md) for [Gibbs energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md),

$$
\boxed{\Delta_rG^\circ=-438.96-(-32.13)=-406.83\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.}
$$

The subtraction reverses the second reaction before adding it; adding both supplied Gibbs energies without changing its sign would correspond to a different chemical equation.
