The [standard entropy of reaction](../../../../../standard-entropy-of-reaction.md) is

$$
\Delta_rS^\circ=282.15-217.94-202.79=-138.58\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}\,mol^{-1}}.
$$

Using $\Delta_rG^\circ=\Delta_rH^\circ-T\Delta_rS^\circ$,

$$
\Delta_rH^\circ=-73.01+298(-0.13858)=-114.30684\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.
$$

The formation enthalpy of elemental fluorine gas is zero in its standard state. Thus

$$
\boxed{\Delta_fH^\circ(\mathrm{ClF_3})=\Delta_rH^\circ+\Delta_fH^\circ(\mathrm{ClF})=-164.60\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.}
$$

The large negative entropy change is consistent with converting two gas molecules to one; it is already included in the Gibbs-energy relation.
