The decadic [Beer-Lambert law](../../../../../../beer-lambert-law.md) can be written

$$
\boxed{I=I_0\,10^{-\varepsilon lc},\qquad \log_{10}(I_0/I)=\varepsilon lc.}
$$

Here $l$ is the path length in metres, $c$ is concentration in $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ or M, and $\varepsilon$ is the decadic [molar attenuation coefficient](../../../../../../molar-absorption-coefficient.md) in $\mathrm{M^{-1}\,m^{-1}}$. The intensity ratio and the exponent are dimensionless. Equivalently $I=I_0e^{-\kappa lc}$ with $\kappa=(\ln10)\varepsilon$ in the same units. Defining the logarithm convention avoids a missing factor of $\ln10$.
