<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a [first-order reaction](../../../../../../first-order-reaction.md) with no appreciable reverse contribution in this decay experiment,

$$
\frac{dc}{dt}=-kc,\qquad c(t)=c_0e^{-kt}.
$$

With fixed path length and attenuation coefficient, and absorbance attributable to ONOO after blank/background correction, the [Beer-Lambert law](../../../../../../beer-lambert-law.md) gives $A(t)=\varepsilon lc_0e^{-kt}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\ln A(t)=\ln(\varepsilon lc_0)-kt.}
$$

**The gradient is $-k$**, in $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ when time is measured in seconds. Constant illumination wavelength, homogeneous sampling and negligible product absorbance at that wavelength are required for this direct interpretation.
