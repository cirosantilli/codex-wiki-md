<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The intermediate balance is

$$
\frac{d[\mathrm{ONOO}]}{dt}=k_1[\mathrm{NO}][\mathrm{O_2}]-k_{-1}[\mathrm{ONOO}]-k_2[\mathrm{NO}][\mathrm{ONOO}].
$$

Setting this derivative approximately to zero under the [quasi-steady-state approximation](../../../../../../quasi-steady-state-approximation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{[\mathrm{ONOO}]_{\rm ss}=\frac{k_1[\mathrm{NO}][\mathrm{O_2}]}{k_{-1}+k_2[\mathrm{NO}]}.}
$$

Both dissociation and reaction with NO remove the intermediate. Its steady concentration is not zero and does not require the first reversible pair to be at equilibrium.
