<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The last [elementary reaction](../../../../../../elementary-reaction.md) creates two nitrogen dioxide molecules per event, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{d[\mathrm{NO_2}]}{dt}=2k_2[\mathrm{NO}][\mathrm{ONOO}]_{\rm ss}
=\frac{2k_1k_2[\mathrm{NO}]^2[\mathrm{O_2}]}{k_{-1}+k_2[\mathrm{NO}]}.}
$$

The factor of two is required for the production rate requested. The conventional reaction rate for $\mathrm{2NO+O_2\to2NO_2}$ would be one half of this production rate.
