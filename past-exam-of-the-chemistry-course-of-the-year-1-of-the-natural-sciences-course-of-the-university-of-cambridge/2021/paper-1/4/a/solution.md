<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write both [electrode](../../../../../electrode.md) reactions first as [reductions](../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md):

$$
\mathrm{CH_3COO^-+5H^++4e^-\longrightarrow C_2H_5OH+H_2O},
$$



$$
\mathrm{CeO_2+4H^++e^-\longrightarrow Ce^{3+}+2H_2O}.
$$

The **conventional cell reaction** runs with [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) on the left and [reduction](../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) on the right. Reverse the first equation and multiply the second by four:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{C_2H_5OH+4CeO_2+11H^+
\longrightarrow CH_3COO^-+4Ce^{3+}+7H_2O}}.
$$

Four [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) pass through the external circuit per reaction as written. This direction consumes the oxide; deposition requires the reverse [electrolysis](../../../../../electrolysis.md) direction.
