<h1 id="4/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The fixed nonproton [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) ratio is $(0.1)^4(0.3)/(3\times10^{-6})=10$. Hence

$$
Q=10\,10^{11\,{\rm pH}},\qquad
E=1.945-\frac{RT\ln10}{4F}(1+11\,{\rm pH}).
$$

Using the supplied constants at $298\,\mathrm K$,

$$
\boxed{E\simeq1.9302-0.1626\,{\rm pH}\quad\mathrm V}.
$$

At [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) 7, **$E\simeq0.792\,\mathrm V$**. This is the formal fixed-thermodynamic activity calculation requested; actual cerium speciation and thermodynamic activities would need to remain compatible with the chosen [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) to realize it experimentally.
