<h1 id="2/a/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

BeLi has **three [valence electrons](../../../../../../valence-electron.md)**: two occupy a bonding sigma orbital and the third its higher partner. The elementary [bond order](../../../../../../bond-order.md) is therefore **$1/2$**, with one [unpaired electron](../../../../../../unpaired-electron.md): expect a relatively weak, paramagnetic, doublet [diatomic molecule](../../../../../../diatomic-molecule.md). Because Be attracts valence charge more strongly than Li, the bond is polar toward Be. This is a qualitative prediction, not a numerical dissociation energy.

This is the elementary picture of the [lithium beryllium diatomic molecule](../../../../../../lithium-beryllium-diatomic-molecule.md).
