**N$_2$:** [Homo](../../../../../../homo.md) $\sigma_{2p_z}$; [LUMO](../../../../../../lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md) the degenerate $\pi_{2p}^*$ pair.

**O$_2$:** the two $\pi_{2p}^*$ orbitals are the highest occupied, singly occupied spatial orbitals. The next completely empty spatial orbital is $\sigma_{2p_z}^*$. An added [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) can instead occupy the opposite-spin vacancy in a singly occupied pi-star orbital; this distinction matters if “[LUMO](../../../../../../lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md)” is being used for an electron-addition [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) orbital.

**Li$_2$:** [Homo](../../../../../../homo.md) $\sigma_{2s}$; next empty sigma orbital conventionally denoted $\sigma_{2s}^*$ in this elementary diagram. **Be$_2$:** [Homo](../../../../../../homo.md) $\sigma_{2s}^*$; the low empty 2p manifold supplies the [LUMO](../../../../../../lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md) candidates, drawn as $\pi_{2p}$ in the stated light-diatomic ordering. Mixing makes these labels descriptions of dominant atomic-orbital character, not strictly unmixed atomic functions.

This distinction is the [open-shell frontier orbital convention](../../../../../../open-shell-frontier-orbital-convention.md).
