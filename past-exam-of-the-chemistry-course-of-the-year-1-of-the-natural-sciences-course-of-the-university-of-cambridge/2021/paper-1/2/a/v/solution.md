**No ordinary [covalent bond](../../../../../../covalent-bond-split.md) is expected.** He's filled 1s orbital is very low in energy, whereas Be supplies another filled shell and much higher empty valence orbitals. Mixing the two occupied s combinations gives no net elementary bond-order gain, and their large energy mismatch limits useful shared covalent character. A very weak dispersion-bound encounter is a different possibility; the answer concerns a conventional [covalent bond](../../../../../../covalent-bond-split.md).
