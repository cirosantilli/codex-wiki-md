↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The reacting **[Homo](../../../../../../homo.md) is the tertiary-amine [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md)**. The relevant **[LUMO](../../../../../../lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md) is the C-I [antibonding orbital](../../../../../../antibonding-orbital.md), $\sigma^*_{\mathrm{C-I}}$, of MeI**. Backside lone-pair donation into that orbital forms N-C while weakening C-I. The neutral OH group is not first converted to an alkoxide in the stated reaction.
