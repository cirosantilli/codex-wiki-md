**[Figure 5](#2/b/ii/image-nitrogen-lone-pair-attack-and-iodide-departure-form-choline-iodide). Nitrogen lone-pair attack and iodide departure form choline iodide**.

Draw a curly arrow from the [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) to methyl [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), and a second from the C-I bond to I:

$$
\mathrm{HOCH_2CH_2NMe_2+MeI
\longrightarrow[HOCH_2CH_2NMe_3]^+I^-}.
$$

This is concerted **[SN2](../../../../../../sn2-reaction.md) quaternization**. The product is [choline](../../../../../../choline.md) iodide; [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) has four [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) substituents and positive charge, while the [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) remains OH.
