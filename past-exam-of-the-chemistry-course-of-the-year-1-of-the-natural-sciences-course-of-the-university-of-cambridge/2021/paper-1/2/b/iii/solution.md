<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**[Acetonitrile](../../../../../../acetonitrile.md)** is suitable: it dissolves the organic reagents, is polar enough to stabilize the developing ionic products and is an aprotic, relatively unreactive medium. It does not strongly hydrogen-bond-solvate the amine [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) or compete efficiently as the methylating [nucleophile](../../../../../../nucleophile.md). Sufficient solubility and chemical compatibility matter as well as polarity.
