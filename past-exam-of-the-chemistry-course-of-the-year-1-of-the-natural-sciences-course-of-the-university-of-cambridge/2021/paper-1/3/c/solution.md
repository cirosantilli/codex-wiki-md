<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\mathrm{Ar}=p\!-\!\mathrm{MeOC_6H_4}$. One standard route is:

$$
\mathrm{ArBr}
\xrightarrow[\text{dry ether or THF}]{Mg}\mathrm{ArMgBr}
\xrightarrow[\text{then aqueous workup}]{CH_3CHO}
\mathrm{ArCH(OH)CH_3}
\xrightarrow[\Delta]{\text{catalytic }p\text{-TsOH}}
\mathrm{ArCH{=}CH_2}
\xrightarrow{mCPBA}\mathrm{ArCH{-}CH_2}
$$

where the last two [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md) are joined through [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) to form the **[epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md)** shown in the product drawing.

![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-chemistry-course-of-the-year-1-of-the-natural-sciences-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2021/paper-1-q3-synthetic-route.png)

**[Figure 8](#3/c/image-grignard-addition-dehydration-and-epoxidation-furnish-the-methoxy-substituted-styrene-oxide). Grignard addition, dehydration and epoxidation furnish the methoxy-substituted styrene oxide**.

Use rigorously dry conditions for Grignard formation/addition; quench only after the [aldehyde](../../../../../aldehyde.md) addition. [Dehydration](../../../../../dehydration.md) gives the styrene, and peracid [oxidation](../../../../../oxidation.md) transfers [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) across its double bond. The methoxy substituent is compatible with this sequence. No asymmetric reagent is specified, so the target [epoxide](../../../../../epoxide.md) is racemic.
