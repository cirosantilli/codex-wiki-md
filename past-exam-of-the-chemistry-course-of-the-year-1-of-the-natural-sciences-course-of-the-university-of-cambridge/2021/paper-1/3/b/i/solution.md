**[Figure 6](#3/b/i/image-bromonium-formation-and-intramolecular-opening-give-the-six-membered-cyclic-bromoether). Bromonium formation and intramolecular opening give the six-membered cyclic bromoether**.

The [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [pi bond](../../../../../../pi-bond.md) attacks Br$_2$ as the Br-Br bond breaks to bromide, forming a bridged, unsymmetrical **[bromonium ion](../../../../../../bromonium-ion.md)**. The [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) bearing the two [methyl groups](../../../../../../methyl-group.md) has greater carbocation-like character.

The tethered [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) attacks that [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) from the side opposite the bromine bridge. Its [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) forms the new O-C bond while the attacked C-Br bridge bond opens, giving a six-membered cyclic oxonium ion. Bromide or [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) removes the O-H [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) to give the neutral bromoether.

This explains why O becomes attached at the more substituted [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) while Br remains at the other [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). Polar [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) assists charge separation, and intramolecular capture competes effectively with external bromide attack. Opening is anti; attack on the two faces of the achiral starting material gives a racemic product at the newly formed CHBr stereocentre.

The ring-forming step is [bromoetherification](../../../../../../bromoetherification.md).
