**[Figure 7](#3/b/ii/image-benzylidene-acetal-hydrolysis-releases-a-diol-that-closes-to-an-unsaturated-gamma-lactone). Benzylidene-acetal hydrolysis releases a diol that closes to an unsaturated gamma-lactone**.

First hydrolyze the benzylidene [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md). Protonate one [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md) [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) and break its bond to the Ph-CH centre, giving one free [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) and a benzylic [oxocarbenium ion](../../../../../../oxocarbenium-ion.md). Water attacks that centre; [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) transfers give a [hemiacetal](../../../../../../hemiacetal.md). Protonate the remaining acetal-derived OR group and cleave it to release the second [alcohol](../../../../../../alcohol.md) and **[benzaldehyde](../../../../../../benzaldehyde.md)**.

The resulting unsaturated hydroxy [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) is

$$
\mathrm{HO-CH(CH_2CH{=}CH_2)-C(CH_2OH){=}CH-C(O)OMe}.
$$

The OH on the allyl-substituted methine is gamma to the [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md). [Protonation](../../../../../../protonation.md) of that [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) enables intramolecular OH attack to give a five-membered [tetrahedral intermediate](../../../../../../tetrahedral-intermediate.md). [Proton](../../../../../../proton.md) transfer to methoxy and collapse expel [methanol](../../../../../../methanol.md), followed by [deprotonation](../../../../../../deprotonation.md) to give the **alpha,beta-unsaturated gamma-lactone**.

The other liberated OH remains CH$_2$OH. Both C=C units survive: one is the allyl substituent and the other becomes the [lactone](../../../../../../lactone.md)'s conjugated double bond. [Hydrolysis](../../../../../../hydrolysis.md) to a hydroxy acid followed by [lactonization](../../../../../../lactonization.md) is an alternative sequence under the aqueous acid; direct acid-catalysed intramolecular [ester](../../../../../../ester.md) exchange gives the same atom mapping.
