<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**A is dimethyl [ether](../../../../../../ether.md), $\mathrm{CH_3OCH_3}$**, with NaCl as the corresponding salt byproduct. Backside methoxide attack on methyl chloride gives an [SN2](../../../../../../sn2-reaction.md) substitution:

$$
v=k[\mathrm{CH_3Cl}][\mathrm{MeO^-}].
$$

The rate is bimolecular because both reactants participate in the elementary bond-forming/bond-breaking event.
