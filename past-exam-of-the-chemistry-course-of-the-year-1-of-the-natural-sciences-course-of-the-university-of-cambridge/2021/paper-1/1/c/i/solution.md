**[Proton nuclear magnetic resonance](../../../../../../proton-nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) spectra: 4 = A, 5 = C, 6 = B.**

Spectrum 4 has one vinyl [proton](../../../../../../proton.md), two alkene-methyl groups and an [ethoxy group](../../../../../../ethoxy-group.md). Spectrum 5 has one [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md) [proton](../../../../../../proton.md), one terminal-alkyne [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) and two equivalent [ethoxy groups](../../../../../../ethoxy-group.md). Spectrum 6 has the three-proton terminal-alkene pattern and the allylic OCH$_2$ group, together with the butanoate chain.
