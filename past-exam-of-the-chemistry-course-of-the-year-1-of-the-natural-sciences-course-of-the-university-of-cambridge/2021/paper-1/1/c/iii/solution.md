**[Figure 3](#1/c/iii/image-coupling-partners-and-approximate-coupling-constants-with-line-separations-converted-using-300-hz-per-ppm). Coupling partners and approximate coupling constants, with line separations converted using 300 Hz per ppm**.

**A:** ethoxy CH$_2$ ↔ CH$_3$, $J\simeq7\,\mathrm{Hz}$; vinyl CH ↔ each [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) methyl, small $^4J\simeq1$–$1.5\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

**C:** ethoxy CH$_2$ ↔ CH$_3$, about $7\,\mathrm{Hz}$; the two OCH$_2$ [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) types have a [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) of about $9$–$10\,\mathrm{Hz}$; [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md) CH ↔ C≡CH has $^4J\simeq2\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

**B:** internal vinyl CH ↔ trans terminal H, about $17\,\mathrm{Hz}$; ↔ cis terminal H, about $10\,\mathrm{Hz}$; terminal CH$_2$ [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) about $1$–$2\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Vinyl CH ↔ OCH$_2$ is about $6\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Both vicinal links along the butanoate alkyl chain are about $7$–$8\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

Use $J=300\,\Delta\delta$ when a separation is measured in ppm. Thus $0.057\,\mathrm{ppm}$ corresponds to about $17\,\mathrm{Hz}$, whereas $0.023\,\mathrm{ppm}$ corresponds to about $7\,\mathrm{Hz}$. These are approximate readings, not a fitted [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) simulation.

The small four-bond splittings illustrate [allylic scalar coupling](../../../../../../allylic-scalar-coupling.md) and [acetal-alkyne long-range coupling](../../../../../../acetal-alkyne-long-range-coupling.md).
