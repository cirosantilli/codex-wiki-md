**[Figure 2](#1/c/ii/image-approximate-proton-shifts-and-coupling-patterns-derived-from-the-printed-spectra). Approximate proton shifts and coupling patterns derived from the printed spectra**.

**A, spectrum 4:** ethoxy CH$_3$ at about $1.3$ is a [NMR triplet](../../../../../../nmr-triplet.md), and OCH$_2$ at about $4.15$ is a [NMR quartet](../../../../../../nmr-quartet.md). The two [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) methyls at about $1.9$ and $2.2$ each integrate to three [protons](../../../../../../proton.md); they are apparent [NMR singlets](../../../../../../nmr-singlet.md) on the overview but have small allylic [NMR doublet](../../../../../../nmr-doublet.md) splitting. The vinyl CH at about $5.7$ is correspondingly a narrow [NMR multiplet](../../../../../../nmr-multiplet.md), formally a [quartet of quartets](../../../../../../quartet-of-quartets.md) when its two methyl couplings are unequal, or approximately a [NMR septet](../../../../../../nmr-septet.md) if they coincide. A broadened overview must not be used to declare all those small couplings exactly zero.

**C, spectrum 5:** the [acetal](../../../../../../acetal.md) CH at about $5.25$ and terminal C≡CH at about $2.55$ each integrate to one [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) and show their small mutual long-range [NMR doublet](../../../../../../nmr-doublet.md) splitting. The ethoxy methyls give a six-proton [NMR triplet](../../../../../../nmr-triplet.md) near $1.25$. The OCH$_2$ region has two sets near $3.6$ and $3.75$, each integrating to two [protons](../../../../../../proton.md). These are an [ABX3 spin system](../../../../../../abx3-spin-system.md): [geminal coupling](../../../../../../geminal-coupling.md) and coupling to CH$_3$ produce approximately one [doublet of quartets](../../../../../../doublet-of-quartets.md) for each [proton](../../../../../../proton.md) type, with second-order distortion in the expanded trace. Treating the whole region as one ideal four-proton [NMR quartet](../../../../../../nmr-quartet.md) misses the printed structure.

**B, spectrum 6:** the internal [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) CH near $5.95$ is a [doublet of doublets of triplets](../../../../../../doublet-of-doublets-of-triplets.md) from trans/cis coupling to CH$_2$ and [vicinal coupling](../../../../../../vicinal-coupling.md) to OCH$_2$. The two terminal [alkene](../../../../../../alkene.md) [protons](../../../../../../proton.md) near $5.2$–$5.35$ are distinct [doublet of doublets](../../../../../../doublet-of-doublets.md)-type signals, with possible additional small allylic splitting. OCH$_2$ near $4.6$ is principally a [NMR doublet](../../../../../../nmr-doublet.md). The acyl-alpha CH$_2$ near $2.3$ is a [NMR triplet](../../../../../../nmr-triplet.md), the middle CH$_2$ near $1.65$ an approximate [NMR sextet](../../../../../../nmr-sextet.md) or overlapping [NMR triplet](../../../../../../nmr-triplet.md) of quartets, and the terminal methyl near $0.95$ a [NMR triplet](../../../../../../nmr-triplet.md).
