**A: conjugated [ester](../../../../../ester.md); B: unconjugated [ester](../../../../../ester.md); C: terminal [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md).**

A has a strong [ester](../../../../../ester.md) C=O band at roughly $1715$–$1730\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, lowered by [conjugation](../../../../../conjugation.md) with C=C. B's unconjugated [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) is nearer $1735$–$1750\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$; it also has terminal-alkene features, including the approximately $990/910\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ pair.

C has **no [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) band**. Its terminal [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) gives a relatively sharp sp C-H stretch near $3300\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ and a usually weaker C≡C feature near $2100$–$2150\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. C-O stretching alone is less diagnostic because [esters](../../../../../ester.md) and the [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) all contain C-O bonds. The numbers are characteristic ranges, not exact frequencies inferred from an unprinted IR trace.
