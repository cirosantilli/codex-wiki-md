**[Carbon-13 NMR](../../../../../carbon-13-nmr.md) spectra: 1 = C, 2 = A, 3 = B.** Approximate shifts, read from the printed axes, are shown in the annotated structures.

![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-chemistry-course-of-the-year-1-of-the-natural-sciences-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2021/paper-1-q1-carbon-assignments.png)

**[Figure 1](#1/b/image-derived-carbon-assignments-for-the-three-isomers-excluding-the-solvent-signal-near-77-ppm). Derived carbon assignments for the three isomers, excluding the solvent signal near 77 ppm**.

- **C:** $\mathrm{HC{\equiv}C-CH(OCH_2CH_3)_2}$ has five [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) environments: [acetal](../../../../../acetal.md) CH near $91$; substituted/terminal [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) [carbons](../../../../../carbon.md) near $79$ and $74$; OCH$_2$ near $61$; and methyl near $15\,\mathrm{ppm}$. One [alkyne](../../../../../alkyne.md) signal is close to the CDCl$_3$ [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) region, which must not be counted as a new molecular environment.
- **A:** $\mathrm{EtOCO-CH=C(CH_3)_2}$ has [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) near $167$, substituted [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) C near $157$, vinyl CH near $116$, OCH$_2$ near $60$, two nonequivalent [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) methyls near $28$ and $21$, and ethoxy methyl near $14\,\mathrm{ppm}$.
- **B:** $\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CH_2-OCO-CH_2-CH_2-CH_3}$ has [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) near $173$, vinyl CH near $133$, terminal vinyl CH$_2$ near $118$, OCH$_2$ near $65$, acyl-alpha CH$_2$ near $36$, the next CH$_2$ near $19$, and terminal methyl near $14\,\mathrm{ppm}$.

The two methyls in A are chemically distinct because one is cis and the other trans to each substituent at the other [alkene](../../../../../alkene.md) [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). Their two measured shifts are secure; assigning an individual methyl to a particular side of the drawn double bond requires an additional stereospecific shift argument or experiment, so their spatial interchange is left explicit.
