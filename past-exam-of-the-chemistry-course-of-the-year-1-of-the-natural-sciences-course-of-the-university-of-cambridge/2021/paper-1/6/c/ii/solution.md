<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Potassium](../../../../../../potassium.md) intercalates between [graphite](../../../../../../graphite.md) layers and **donates [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) density to the graphitic pi system**. Occupation of formerly less populated electronic states raises the mobile-carrier density, increasing [electrical conductivity](../../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) under the stated comparison.

The gain is electronic doping of the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) framework, rather than a claim that [potassium](../../../../../../potassium.md) strengthens every C-C bond or simply acts as a separate wire through the sample. Intercalation also changes layer separation and scattering, so conductivity depends on both carrier population and [mobility](../../../../../../order-parameter-mobility.md).

The material is [potassium graphite](../../../../../../potassium-graphite.md), a [graphite intercalation compound](../../../../../../graphite-intercalation-compound.md).
