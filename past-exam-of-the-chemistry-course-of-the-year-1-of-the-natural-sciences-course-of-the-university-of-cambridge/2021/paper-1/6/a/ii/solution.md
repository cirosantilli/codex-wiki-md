<h1 id="6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The expected order is **B-F \> C-F \> N-F**.

B-F has a large favorable polar/ionic contribution because electron-deficient [boron](../../../../../../boron.md) and [fluorine](../../../../../../fluorine.md) have a large [electronegativity](../../../../../../electronegativity-split.md) difference. [Fluorine](../../../../../../fluorine.md) lone-pair donation into [boron](../../../../../../boron.md)'s empty 2p orbital can additionally strengthen bonding in planar BF$_3$. C-F is a strong, polar second-row [sigma bond](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md), but tetrahedral CF$_4$ has no equivalent empty [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) 2p acceptor for that pi donation.

N-F has less favorable bond polarity and substantial repulsion between [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) and [fluorine](../../../../../../fluorine.md) [lone pairs](../../../../../../lone-pair.md), making it weaker. Invoking occupied [boron](../../../../../../boron.md) d orbitals is unnecessary and inappropriate for this second-row comparison.
