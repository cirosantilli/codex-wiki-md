↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**C-C is strongest; N-N is much weaker despite [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md)'s smaller size.** [Carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md)'s effective nuclear attraction and good overlap of compact second-row valence orbitals favor a strong C-C [sigma bond](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md). The larger, more electron-deficient [boron](../../../../../../boron.md) centres give less effective B-B bonding in the stated molecule, despite its relatively high-s-character trigonal bonding.

In hydrazine, the two neighboring [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) [lone pairs](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) strongly repel one another, destabilizing the N-N bond. Bond strength therefore cannot be predicted from atomic size alone; lone-pair repulsion explains the especially low N-N value.

This illustrates [bonding trends in second-period single bonds](../../../../../../bonding-trends-in-second-period-single-bonds.md).
