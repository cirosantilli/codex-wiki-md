<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $x=[\mathrm{MeX}]$ and $x_0=10^{-4}\,\mathrm M$.

- **A:** OH$^-$ starts in 100-fold excess, so its consumption changes its [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) by at most about 1%. Set $h\simeq h_0=0.01\,\mathrm M$: $-dx/dt=k h_0x$, a [pseudo-first-order reaction](../../../../../../pseudo-first-order-reaction.md) law.
- **B:** initially $h_0=x_0$. [Stoichiometry](../../../../../../stoichiometry.md) preserves $h=x$ while [hydroxide](../../../../../../hydroxide.md) dominates water autoionization, giving $-dx/dt=kx^2$. It is **not** [pseudo-first-order reaction](../../../../../../pseudo-first-order-reaction.md) merely because its initial [pH](../../../../../../ph.md) equals C's.
- **C:** adequate buffer capacity keeps $h=10^{-4}\,\mathrm M$ constant despite consumption: $-dx/dt=khx$, again [pseudo-first-order reaction](../../../../../../pseudo-first-order-reaction.md). Assume buffer components do not react with MeX or change the effective [rate constant](../../../../../../rate-constant.md) appreciably.
