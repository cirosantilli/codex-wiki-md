At $25^\circ\mathrm C$, $[\mathrm{OH^-}]=10^{{\rm pH}-14}\,\mathrm{mol\,dm^{-3}}$ in the usual dilute-solution [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) approximation. Therefore **A: $10^{-2}\,\mathrm M$; B: $10^{-4}\,\mathrm M$; C: $10^{-4}\,\mathrm M$**. These are initial [concentrations](../../../../../../concentration.md); only C is actively buffered at its stated value.
