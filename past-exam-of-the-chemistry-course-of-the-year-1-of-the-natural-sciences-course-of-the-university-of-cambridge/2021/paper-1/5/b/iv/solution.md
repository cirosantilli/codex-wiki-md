↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Set $x=0.1x_0$:

$$
\boxed{t_{90,A}\simeq\frac{\ln10}{k(10^{-2}\,\mathrm M)}},
\qquad
\boxed{t_{90,B}=\frac9{k(10^{-4}\,\mathrm M)}},
$$



$$
\boxed{t_{90,C}=\frac{\ln10}{k(10^{-4}\,\mathrm M)}}.
$$

The corresponding numerical [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) factors are approximately $230.3$, $90000$ and $23026\,\mathrm{M^{-1}}$, each divided by $k$. Thus A is about 100 times faster than C at this conversion, while B takes about $9/\ln10=3.91$ times as long as C because its [hydroxide](../../../../../../hydroxide.md) is depleted.
