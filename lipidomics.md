# Lipidomics

↑ **Parent:** [Biochemistry](biochemistry-split.md)

[lipidomics](lipidomics.md) measures the identities and relative or absolute amounts of [lipids](lipid.md) in a sample. Extraction, standards, response factors and isomer discrimination constrain inference; bulk composition alone does not determine leaflet distribution or local membrane properties.
