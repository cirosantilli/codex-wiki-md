# Fermi circle

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi contour](fermi-contour.md)

The circular zero-temperature occupation boundary of an isotropic two-dimensional [electron](electron.md) gas. The occupied disk has area $\pi k_F^2$; converting its area to particle number requires the reciprocal-space state density and a separately specified [spin degeneracy](spin-degeneracy.md).
