# Ladder paradox

↑ **Parent:** [Relativity of simultaneity](relativity-of-simultaneity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladder_paradox)

The apparent conflict between fitting a moving rod inside a shorter stationary enclosure in one frame and observing an even shorter moving enclosure in the rod's frame. [Length contraction](length-contraction.md) compares endpoints simultaneous in the measuring frame, and [relativity of simultaneity](relativity-of-simultaneity.md) means the two frames do not compare the same pairs of events. Transforming the enclosure-entry and enclosure-exit events resolves the apparent contradiction.
