# Angular-momentum flux

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md)

The rate at which [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) crosses a surface, including advection and stresses. With positive inward [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md), the net inward flux in a steady [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) is $\dot M h-\mathcal G$.
