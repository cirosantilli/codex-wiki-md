# Zeeman discovery experiment of 1896

↑ **Parent:** [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md)

In August 1896, [Pieter Zeeman](pieter-zeeman.md) observed broadened [sodium](sodium.md) [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) at Leiden, initially without resolved components. His lecture describes a slit and concave [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md), then compares the broadening with the [sodium](sodium.md) doublet separation: about one thirtieth, roughly $0.2$ [ångström](angstrom.md). This supplied a spectral reference scale, not an absolute [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) calibration. Later [cadmium](cadmium.md) photographs displayed resolved transverse triplets and axial doublets.[Pieter Zeeman, Light Radiation in a Magnetic Field, Nobel lecture delivered May 2, 1903; University of Maryland mirror PDF pp. 2–3, paragraphs beginning “In August, 1896”, “The change was however very small” and the cadmium photograph discussion](light-radiation-in-a-magnetic-field-by-pieter-zeeman.md)

The fuller report specifies a concave [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md) of ten-foot radius with 14,438 lines per inch, an adjustable slit and a micrometer eyepiece with vertical crosswire. Salt in a flame between [electromagnet](electromagnet.md) poles supplied the [sodium](sodium.md) [spectral lines](spectral-line.md). Repeated switching broadened and restored them. To test changes in flame [temperature](temperature-split.md) or vapour [density](density.md), [Pieter Zeeman](pieter-zeeman.md) examined [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) through a heated porcelain tube with water-cooled glass windows. The arc lamp was displaced from the magnet and its [light](light.md) redirected by a mirror to avoid disturbing the arc. Rotating a narrower tube made the absorption [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) equally broad at different heights; the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) still broadened their whole length.[Pieter Zeeman, On the influence of magnetism on the nature of the light emitted by a substance, Part I, §§2–3 and 7–9, printed pp. 4–8: grating, eyepiece, arc-lamp control and rotating tube](on-the-influence-of-magnetism-on-the-nature-of-the-light-emitted-by-a-substance-part-i-by-pieter-zeeman.md)

For the axial test, pierced [electromagnet](electromagnet.md) poles let the [light](light.md) travel along the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). A [quarter-wave plate](quarter-wave-plate.md) and [polarizer](polarizer.md) selected one broadened edge; rotating the analyser by $90^\circ$ or reversing the magnet current exchanged the visible edge. The crosswire made its displacement detectable. This tested [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) as well as line width. For its rough estimate the report compared each side's widening with the [sodium](sodium.md) doublet separation, obtaining about one fortieth, and stated an approximate [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) intensity in historical units. These passages give no absolute field-calibration procedure; the inferred charge-to-[mass](mass.md) ratio was explicitly a first approximation.[Pieter Zeeman, On the influence of magnetism on the nature of the light emitted by a substance, Part II, §§14, 20 and 23, printed pp. 10, 16–18; scan is a corrected reprint as its footnote to §24 explains](on-the-influence-of-magnetism-on-the-nature-of-the-light-emitted-by-a-substance-part-ii-by-pieter-zeeman.md)

[Hendrik Antoon Lorentz](hendrik-antoon-lorentz.md)'s charged-oscillator theory connected the observed splitting to moving [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). In modern language, a line-position measurement needs a [wavelength](wavelength.md) or [frequency](frequency.md) calibration and an independent [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) calibration before it determines a [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). Resolving the individual components adds the transition information used in the worked example below.
