# Treatment expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Placebo effect](placebo-effect.md)

A [belief](belief.md) about the likely consequences of [treatment](treatment.md) that can influence [attention](attention.md), interpretation, behaviour and symptom experience. Experimental changes in expectation at constant drug exposure can distinguish contextual modulation from differences in administered dose.[Science Translational Medicine 3, 70ra14; fixed remifentanil concentration, heat pain and expectation manipulation](the-effect-of-treatment-expectation-on-drug-efficacy-by-ulrike-bingel-and-others.md)
