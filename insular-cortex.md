# Insular cortex

↑ **Parent:** [Cerebral cortex](cerebral-cortex.md)

A region of [cerebral cortex](cerebral-cortex.md) folded within the lateral sulcus. Named anatomical regions can contain heterogeneous populations; activation of the [insular cortex](insular-cortex.md) is not uniquely diagnostic of one mental process.
