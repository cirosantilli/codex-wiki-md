# Marginal-sea overflow

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean circulation](ocean-circulation.md)

A dense current that leaves a marginal sea over a sill and descends into a deeper basin. [Entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md) increases its [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) while reducing its [density](density.md) anomaly; the resulting mixed water can contribute to a deep overturning branch.
