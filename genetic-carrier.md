# Genetic carrier

↑ **Parent:** [Recessive inheritance](recessive-inheritance.md)

A genetic [genetic carrier](genetic-carrier.md) has one disease-associated recessive [allele](allele.md) and one normal [allele](allele.md) at a [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md), and is unaffected under a fully penetrant recessive model. Two [genetic carriers](genetic-carrier.md) have affected-offspring [probability](probability.md) $1/4$ under [Mendelian segregation](mendelian-segregation.md); a [genetic carrier](genetic-carrier.md) and an individual with no disease [allele](allele.md) have affected-offspring [probability](probability.md) zero under that same mutation-free model.
