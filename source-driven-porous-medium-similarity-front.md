# Source-driven porous-medium similarity front

↑ **Parent:** [Porous medium equation](porous-medium-equation.md)

For the nonlinear [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) $u_t=(uu_x)_x+u/(2t)$ on $x>0,t>0$, the fixed boundary value $u(0,t)=1$ and zero slope at $x=0$ admit the displayed compact-support [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md), with front $x_F=2\sqrt t$. Substitution $u=f(x/\sqrt t)$ reduces the equation to $(ff'+\eta f/2)'=0$. The boundary data make the integration constant zero. While $f>0$, division gives $f'=-\eta/2$, hence $f=1-\eta^2/4$. At its first zero the diffusive flux $uu_x$ vanishes, so extension by zero creates no singular flux term. The solution is smooth inside its positive region and is a [weak solution](weak-solution.md) at the moving front.
