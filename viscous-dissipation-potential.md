# Viscous dissipation potential

↑ **Parent:** [Rheology](rheology-split.md)

A convex scalar function $\Omega(D)$ on symmetric [rate-of-strain tensors](strain-rate-tensor.md) generates [stress](stress.md) by $S=\partial\Omega/\partial D$. For [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), its argument is trace free and a [pressure](pressure.md) reaction must be added to the [stress](stress.md). At nondifferentiable points the law is $S\in\partial\Omega(D)$, where the [subdifferential](subdifferential.md) is defined by $\Omega(E)-\Omega(D)\geq S:(E-D)$ for every admissible $E$. This is a strain-rate potential, in contrast with potentials parameterized by thermodynamic forces.
