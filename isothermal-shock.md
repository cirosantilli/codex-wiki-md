# Isothermal shock

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

An isothermal shock joins two states at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md). Mass and [momentum](momentum-split.md) conservation imply $u_1u_2=c_s^2$ for a nontrivial planar jump, so the compression ratio is the upstream isothermal Mach number squared. [Heat](heat-split.md) exchange replaces adiabatic energy conservation across the gas alone.
