# Uniform integrability of a stopped uniformly integrable martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly integrable martingale convergence theorem](uniformly-integrable-martingale-convergence-theorem.md)

Stopping a [uniformly integrable](uniform-integrability.md) discrete-time [martingale](martingale-split.md) at any [stopping time](stopping-time.md) preserves [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md), including when the [stopping time](stopping-time.md) can be infinite. For $C=\sup_n\mathbb E|X_n|$, the [optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) and the [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) give

$$
\mathbb E[|X_{n\wedge T}|\mathbf1_{\{|X_{n\wedge T}|>K\}}]\leq\sup_j\mathbb E[|X_j|\mathbf1_{\{|X_j|>R\}}]+RC/K.
$$

Choose $R$ first, then $K$, to make this uniformly small.
