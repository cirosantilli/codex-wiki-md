# Sine addition formula

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)

The sine addition formula evaluates the vertical coordinate after two successive [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md):

$$
\boxed{\sin(x+y)=\sin x\cos y+\cos x\sin y.}
$$

Here $x,y$ are real [angles](angle.md) measured in [radians](radian.md); the identity also extends to complex arguments. It is useful when a new [angle](angle.md) can be assembled from familiar ones. For example,

$$
\sin(5\pi/12)=\sin(\pi/6+\pi/4)
=\frac12\frac{\sqrt2}{2}+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\frac{\sqrt2}{2}
=\frac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}.
$$

The [cosine addition formula](cosine-addition-formula.md) gives the horizontal coordinate of the same rotated point. Both formulas follow by multiplying a [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) by the [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $(\cos x,\sin x)$, rather than by adding the individual coordinate values. [NIST DLMF, §4.21(i): trigonometric addition formulas](https://dlmf.nist.gov/4.21.i)
