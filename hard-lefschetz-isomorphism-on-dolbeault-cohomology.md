# Hard Lefschetz isomorphism on Dolbeault cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md)

On a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), the [Lefschetz operator preserves harmonic forms](lefschetz-operator-preserves-harmonic-forms.md), so the indicated power acts on harmonic representatives of [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md). It is injective by [injectivity of powers of the Lefschetz operator](injectivity-of-powers-of-the-lefschetz-operator.md); [Hodge symmetry](hodge-symmetry.md) and [Hodge duality](hodge-duality.md) make its finite-dimensional source and target have equal dimension, so it is an isomorphism.
