# Bromonium ion

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

A [bromonium ion](bromonium-ion.md) bridges both carbons of an [alkene](alkene.md)-derived three-membered ring. Bromide opening from the opposite face gives an anti vicinal dibromide and avoids a freely rotating open carbocation.
