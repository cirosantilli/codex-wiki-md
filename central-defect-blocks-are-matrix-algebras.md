# Central defect blocks are matrix algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md)

Let $k$ be an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) and let a [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) $B$ have a central [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) $D$. Its quotient by $J(kD)B$ is a defect-zero block, hence a full [matrix algebra](matrix-algebra.md) over $k$. The [algebra](algebra-split.md) $B$ is free over the central [local ring](local-ring.md) $kD$. Lift a primitive idempotent from this matrix quotient; its corner is free of rank one over $kD$, and is therefore $kD$. Lifting matrix units gives $B\cong\operatorname{Mat}_r(kD)$.
