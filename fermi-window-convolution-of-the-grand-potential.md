# Fermi-window convolution of the grand potential

↑ **Parent:** [Grand potential](grand-potential.md)

For noninteracting [fermions](fermion.md) with [density of states](density-of-states.md) $g$, define $\Phi_0(x)=-\int_0^x(x-y)g(y)dy$. The [grand potential](grand-potential.md) is $\int_0^\infty g(\epsilon)F(\epsilon)d\epsilon$, where $F=k_BT\ln(1-n_F)$ has derivative $F'=n_F$. Integrating by parts twice gives the title expression, since $\Phi_0(0)=\Phi'_0(0)=0$ and upper boundary terms vanish for ordinary polynomially growing [densities](density.md) of states. The [Fermi window](fermi-window.md) therefore thermally averages the zero-temperature [grand potential](grand-potential.md) as a function of trial [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md).
