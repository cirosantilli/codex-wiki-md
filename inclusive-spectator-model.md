# Inclusive spectator model

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)

An approximation to a heavy-hadron decay in which one heavy [quark](quark.md) undergoes a weak decay and the other constituents are treated as spectators. The inclusive leading width is approximated by a free-quark width, summed over allowed final states. [confinement](confinement.md), final-state masses, strong-interaction corrections and interactions with spectator constituents limit quantitative lifetime predictions.
