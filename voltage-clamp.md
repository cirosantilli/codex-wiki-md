# Voltage clamp

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscience](neuroscience.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage_clamp)

A [voltage clamp](voltage-clamp.md) uses electrical feedback to hold a preparation's [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md) near a commanded value while measuring the [electric current](electric-current.md) required to maintain it. Holding voltage separates changes in [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) from changes in electrical driving force. Imperfect voltage control and finite recording bandwidth limit the inference. The muscle experiment under [ionotropic receptor](ionotropic-receptor.md) used separate intracellular electrodes for voltage control and a sealed patch pipette for recording local [electric current](electric-current.md).[pp. 33–35; Figure 1](elementary-steps-in-synaptic-transmission-revealed-by-currents-through-single-ion-channels-by-bert-sakmann.md)
