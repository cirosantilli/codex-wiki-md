# Hopf-Rinow theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic completeness](geodesic-completeness.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopf–Rinow_theorem)

For a connected finite-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), completeness of the [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md), [geodesic completeness](geodesic-completeness.md), and [compactness](compact-space.md) of every closed bounded subset are equivalent. These conditions imply that any two points are joined by a [minimizing geodesic](minimizing-geodesic.md). Pairwise existence of [minimizing geodesics](minimizing-geodesic.md) alone does not imply completeness: an open [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md) is incomplete, yet the [line segment](line-segment.md) between any two of its points stays in the ball and minimizes length.
