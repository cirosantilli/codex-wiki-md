<h1 id="erwin-schrodinger">Erwin Schrödinger</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md) who developed [wave mechanics](wave-mechanics.md). In 1926, [Erwin Schrödinger](erwin-schrodinger.md) made the energies of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) problem, rather than prescribing a separate quantization rule for each classical [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md). He shared the [Nobel Prize in Physics in 1933](nobel-prize-in-physics-in-1933.md) with [Paul Dirac](paul-dirac.md).[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; Zurich period and 1926 wave-equation work; joint 1933 award; birth and death dates](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/erwin-schrodinger-biographical.md)

The [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) turns a proposed [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) into predictions for a [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) and its evolution. [Boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) can select discrete [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md), while [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md) makes their relative [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md) observable through [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md). The [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) gives a transparent worked example; a [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) supplies a physical application with a different [potential energy](potential-energy.md).

The [Davisson–Germer experiment](davisson-germer-experiment.md) gives a complementary experimental route into [matter waves](matter-wave.md): an accelerated [electron](electron.md) beam scattered from a [nickel](nickel.md) crystal produces direction-dependent collected [electric current](electric-current.md). Its apparatus and angular scans make the wave prediction testable. Interpreting a [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) as a [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) also requires the [Born rule](born-rule.md); that statistical interpretation deserves its own history alongside [Erwin Schrödinger](erwin-schrodinger.md)'s dynamics.
