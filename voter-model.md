# Voter model

↑ **Parent:** [Interacting particle system](interacting-particle-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voter_model)

At each site, a rate-one clock rings and the site copies the state of a randomly chosen neighbor. The model is a [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) on [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) configurations, interpreted through its local update [infinitesimal generator](infinitesimal-generator-stochastic-processes.md). All constant configurations are [absorbing state](absorbing-state.md). Tracing copying arrows backwards gives the [voter-model duality](voter-model-duality.md) with [coalescing random walks](coalescing-random-walks.md).
