# Cantor-space representation of positive functionals

↑ **Parent:** [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md)

Extend the [cylinder premeasure from a positive functional](cylinder-premeasure-from-a-positive-functional.md) to a [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md) and use [cylinder-function density in Cantor space](cylinder-function-density-in-cantor-space.md). The integral and functional agree on cylinder [simple functions](simple-function.md), and both are continuous in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md), so they agree on all of $C(\Omega)$. Uniqueness on the generating cylinder algebra gives uniqueness of the Borel [measure](measure.md).
