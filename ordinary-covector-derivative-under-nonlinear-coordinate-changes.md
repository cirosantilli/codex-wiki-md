# Ordinary covector derivative under nonlinear coordinate changes

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md)

Let $A'_b=J^d{}_b A_d$, where $J^d{}_b=\partial x^d/\partial x'^b$. Then

$$
\partial'_aA'_b=J^c{}_aJ^d{}_b\partial_cA_d+
\frac{\partial^2x^d}{\partial x'^a\partial x'^b}A_d.
$$

The second term prevents the ordinary derivative from being a generally [covariant tensor](covariant-tensor.md). It vanishes for affine coordinate changes, including Cartesian [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md), so the restricted claim of [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md) is valid. Antisymmetrization cancels the same term for arbitrary smooth coordinate changes and gives the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) of a [one-form](one-form.md).
