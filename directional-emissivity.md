# Directional emissivity

↑ **Parent:** [Emissivity](emissivity.md)

[Directional emissivity](directional-emissivity.md) is the ratio of emitted [radiance](radiance.md) to [blackbody](blackbody.md) [radiance](radiance.md) at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) in a specified outgoing direction. It can be total over [wavelength](wavelength.md) or additionally spectrally resolved. Normal-direction [emissivity](emissivity.md) is not automatically [total hemispherical emissivity](total-hemispherical-emissivity.md): the latter integrates all directions with the [projected area](projected-area.md) weighting. A [diffuse thermal emitter](diffuse-thermal-emitter.md) removes this directional dependence of emitted [radiance](radiance.md).
