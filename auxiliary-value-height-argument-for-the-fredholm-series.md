# Auxiliary-value height argument for the Fredholm series

↑ **Parent:** [Fredholm lacunary series](fredholm-lacunary-series.md)

A nonzero [integer](integer.md) [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of bidegree at most $N$ can cancel the first $(N+1)^2-1$ Taylor coefficients of the [Fredholm series](fredholm-lacunary-series.md). Functional transcendence makes the resulting [analytic function](space-of-holomorphic-functions.md) nonzero. If $\alpha$ and $f_l(\alpha)$ were both algebraic, the relation $f_l(\alpha^{l^r})=f_l(\alpha)-\sum_{j<r}\alpha^{l^j}$ would keep the auxiliary values in one fixed [number field](number-field.md). Their [Absolute logarithmic Weil height](absolute-logarithmic-weil-height.md) is at most $N l^r h(\alpha)(1+1/(l-1))+O_N(r+1)$, whereas their chosen absolute value is $O_N(|\alpha|^{T_Nl^r})$. The [Liouville height inequality](liouville-height-inequality.md) forces $T_N=O(N)$, contradicting its quadratic lower bound for sufficiently large $N$.
