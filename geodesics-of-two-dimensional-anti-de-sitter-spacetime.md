# Geodesics of two-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime](two-dimensional-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

A geodesic of the unit embedded hyperboloid lies in the plane spanned by its initial point and tangent. The constraint $X^2=1$ forces the displayed ambient acceleration. Timelike geodesics are $X=P\cos s+V\sin s$, null ones are $X=P+sV$, and spacelike ones are $X=P\cosh s+V\sinh s$. Timelike geodesics are closed on the hyperboloid but lift to complete nonclosed curves on its [universal cover](universal-cover.md). In static coordinates, conserved $E=\cosh^2r\,dt/ds$ gives $(dr/ds)^2=E^2/\cosh^2r-1$ and $\sinh r=\sqrt{E^2-1}\sin(s-s_0)$. Null affine parameters obey $\lambda=\pm\sinh r/E+\text{constant}$, proving completeness at both spatial ends.
