# Conjugate of an infimal convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Infimal convolution](infimal-convolution.md)

For proper [convex functions](convex-function.md) whose [infimal convolution](infimal-convolution.md) is well defined, $(f\mathbin\square g)^*=f^*+g^*$. The proof separates a supremum over independent variables after writing $x=u+y$. No attainment of the infimum is needed.
