# Cubic nilpotency from a mixed chiral constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Chiral superfield constrained by a nilpotent superfield](chiral-superfield-constrained-by-a-nilpotent-superfield.md)

On the invertible-$F_X$ branch, use $y=(G\chi)/F_X-(GG)F_Y/(2F_X^2)$ and the two-component [Grassmann algebra](grassmann-algebra.md) identity $(G\chi)^2=-\tfrac12(GG)(\chi\chi)$. Then $y^2=-(GG)(\chi\chi)/(2F_X^2)$, $y^3=y^2\chi=0$ and $y^2F_Y-y\chi\chi=0$. These are the components of $Y^3=0$. Generally $y^2$ is nonzero, so cubic nilpotency must not be replaced by quadratic nilpotency. The allowed analytic holomorphic monomials are $1,X,Y,Y^2$.
