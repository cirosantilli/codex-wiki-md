# Spectroradiometer

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)

An instrument measuring a radiometric quantity as a function of [wavelength](wavelength.md) under a stated collection geometry and calibration. Measurement at the relevant viewpoint helps connect scene or display output to observer input.
