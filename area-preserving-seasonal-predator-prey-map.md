# Area-preserving seasonal predator-prey map

↑ **Parent:** [Annual pulse-breeding predator-prey model](annual-pulse-breeding-predator-prey-model.md)

The [annual pulse-breeding predator-prey model](annual-pulse-breeding-predator-prey-model.md) is an [area-preserving map](area-preserving-map.md) in logarithmic population coordinates: $u'=u+\log r-\kappa e^v$, then $v'=v+\log s+\log(1+be^{u'})$. Each step is a shear of [determinant](determinant.md) one. At a positive [fixed point](fixed-point.md), the ordinary-population [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has [determinant](determinant.md) one and [trace](matrix-trace.md) $2-(1-s)\log r$. [Eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) lie on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) for $0<(1-s)\log r<4$, but this linear neutrality does not prove nonlinear stability at every resonance. Area preservation excludes an isolated attracting positive periodic cycle.
