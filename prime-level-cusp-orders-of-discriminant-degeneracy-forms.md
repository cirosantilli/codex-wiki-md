# Prime-level cusp orders of discriminant degeneracy forms

↑ **Parent:** [Degeneracy map for cusp forms](degeneracy-map-for-cusp-forms.md)

For prime $p$, the two cusps of $\Gamma_0(p)$ have widths one and $p$. At infinity the [modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md) product gives the first pair of orders. At zero use $S\tau=-1/\tau$ and the parameter $q_0=e^{2\pi i\tau/p}$. The weight-twelve transforms are $\Delta|_{12}S=\Delta(\tau)$ and $\Delta(p\tau)|_{12}S=p^{-12}\Delta(\tau/p)$, giving orders $p$ and one in $q_0$.
