# Viscous-thread drawing

↑ **Parent:** [Slender viscous thread](slender-viscous-thread.md)

In steady viscous-thread drawing the conserved volume flux $Aw=Q$ couples thinning directly to acceleration. The axial tensile stress is $3\mu w_z$, so solutions with larger positive $w_z$ thin more rapidly.
