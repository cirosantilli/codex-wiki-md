<h1 id="gross-pitaevskii-solitary-wave">Gross–Pitaevskii solitary wave</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md)

For the normalized conservative equation $-2i\psi_t=\nabla^2\psi+(1-|\psi|^2)\psi$, a localized disturbance translating at speed $v$ obeys $\nabla^2\psi+(1-|\psi|^2)\psi-2iv\psi_x=0$ in translating coordinates. Its complex-field boundary is $\psi\to1$. This permits zero-net-winding vortex pairs, but excludes a lone [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md). The usual subsonic far field has $\psi-1=O(r^{-1})$ in two dimensions.
