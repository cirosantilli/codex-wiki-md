# Cubic Weyl inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl differencing](weyl-differencing.md)

If $S(\theta)=\sum_{x\le n}e(\theta x^3)$ and $|\theta-a/q|\le q^{-2}$ with $(a,q)=1$, the displayed estimate holds. Twice applying [Weyl differencing](weyl-differencing.md) bounds $|S|^4$ by $O(n^3+n\sum_{0<|h|,|l|<n}\min(n,\|6\theta hl\|^{-1}))$. Group by $6hl$ and use the [subpower bound for the divisor function](subpower-bound-for-the-divisor-function.md). The [reciprocal fractional-part sum near a rational](reciprocal-fractional-part-sum-near-a-rational.md) bounds the resulting sum by $O(\log(2q)(n^3/q+n^2+n+q))$. Take fourth roots, absorbing logarithms into $n^\eta$ when $q\le n^3$; for larger $q$ the trivial bound suffices. [Leiden lecture notes, Section 7.2](https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~evertsejh/ant20-7.pdf) develop this cubic differencing estimate.
