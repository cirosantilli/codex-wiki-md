# Positive definiteness of a Lyapunov function

↑ **Parent:** [Lyapunov function](lyapunov-function.md)

A [Lyapunov function](lyapunov-function.md) is positive definite at an equilibrium $x_*$ when $V(x_*)=0$ and $V(x)>0$ for nearby $x\ne x_*$. Thus $x_*$ is a strict local minimum of $V$.
