# PLU factorization

↑ **Parent:** [LU decomposition](lu-decomposition.md)

A [matrix factorization](matrix-decomposition.md) incorporating a [permutation matrix](permutation-matrix.md), a [unit lower triangular matrix](unit-lower-triangular-matrix.md) and an [upper triangular matrix](upper-triangular-matrix.md). The convention $A=PLU$ differs from $PA=LU$ by replacing $P$ with its inverse. When pivoting during elimination, previously stored lower-triangular multipliers must be permuted consistently with the rows.
