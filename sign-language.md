# Sign language

↑ **Parent:** [Language](language.md)

A natural [language](language.md) using structured visual-manual and other bodily signals. [Sign languages](sign-language.md) have phonological organisation, meaning and [syntax](syntax.md); they are not universal pantomime or merely a manual transcription of [speech](speech.md).
