# Doubly connected domain

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces-split.md)

A planar [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) whose complement in the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) has exactly two [connected components](connected-component.md). An [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) is the standard example. If neither complementary component is a single point, the domain is conformally equivalent to a circular [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) with finite positive [conformal modulus of an annulus](conformal-modulus-of-an-annulus.md); punctured-disc and punctured-plane cases account for degenerate complementary components.
