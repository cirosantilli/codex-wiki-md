# Molar heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)

Molar heat capacity is [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) divided by [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) $n$, with units $\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$. Its constraint must still be specified: $C_{V,\mathrm m}=C_V/n$ and $C_{p,\mathrm m}=C_p/n$. For the classical [monatomic gas](monatomic-gas.md) example in [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), these are $3R/2$ and $5R/2$, where $R$ is the [molar gas constant](molar-gas-constant.md). Some textbooks use $C_V$ and $C_p$ for these per-[mole](mole-unit.md) quantities; the convention here reserves them for the whole sample.
