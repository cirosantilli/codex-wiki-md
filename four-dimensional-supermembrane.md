# Four-dimensional supermembrane

↑ **Parent:** [Brane](brane.md)

The four-dimensional minimal [supersymmetric](supersymmetry-split.md) membrane has embedding coordinates $(X^m,\theta^\alpha)$ with four real odd components. Its area term uses the [supertranslation-invariant superspace coframe](supertranslation-invariant-superspace-coframe.md), and a [Wess-Zumino brane coupling](wess-zumino-brane-coupling.md) integrates a superspace three-form potential with closed four-form curvature. Equal charge magnitude and tension permit [kappa symmetry](kappa-symmetry.md). After [worldvolume](worldvolume.md) reparametrization and kappa fixing, one transverse scalar and one three-dimensional Majorana [fermion](fermion.md) remain. This gives the long-wavelength description of the translation and [fermion](fermion.md) zero modes of a [supersymmetric domain wall](supersymmetric-domain-wall.md); it is distinct from restricting the eleven-dimensional M2-brane to a four-dimensional target.
