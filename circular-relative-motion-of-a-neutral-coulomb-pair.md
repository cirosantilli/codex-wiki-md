# Circular relative motion of a neutral Coulomb pair

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral Coulomb pair in a uniform magnetic field](neutral-coulomb-pair-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md)

In the zero-pseudomomentum sector, a planar relative circular orbit has two signed angular-frequency branches. Here $\mu=m_1m_2/(m_1+m_2)$, $e^*=e(m_1-m_2)/[2(m_1+m_2)]$, $R$ is separation, and the Coulomb constant is absorbed into $e^2$. The [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) also circles: $\mathbf R_{\rm cm}=\mathbf R_0+eB\mathbf r/[(m_1+m_2)\omega]$. This distinction is essential when reconstructing individual particle orbits.
