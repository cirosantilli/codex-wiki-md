# Divergence of a cross product

↑ **Parent:** [Vector calculus](vector-calculus.md)

For differentiable vector fields $A$ and $B$,

$$
\nabla\cdot(A\times B)=B\cdot(\nabla\times A)-A\cdot(\nabla\times B).
$$
