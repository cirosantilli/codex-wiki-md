# Locally Noetherian scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

A [scheme](scheme.md) is locally Noetherian if it has an [affine open cover](affine-open-cover.md) by spectra of [Noetherian rings](noetherian-ring.md). It is a [Noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md) exactly when it is also a [quasi-compact scheme](quasi-compact-scheme.md): a finite subcover then supplies the required finite [affine open cover](affine-open-cover.md). An infinite gluing of spectra of [Noetherian rings](noetherian-ring.md) along [open subschemes](open-subscheme.md) is still locally Noetherian, but need not be a [quasi-compact scheme](quasi-compact-scheme.md).
