# Local exponential chart

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential map of a Lie group](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md)

Since the differential of the [Exponential map of a Lie group](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md) at zero is the identity, the [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) makes it a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) on a sufficiently small neighborhood of zero. Its local inverse is a logarithm near the group identity. It need not extend to a global logarithm, even for the real [general linear group](general-linear-group.md).
