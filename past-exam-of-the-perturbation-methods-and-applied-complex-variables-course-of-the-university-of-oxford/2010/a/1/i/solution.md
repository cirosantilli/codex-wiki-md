<h1 id="a/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $\delta=\sqrt\epsilon$ and write the outer [asymptotic expansion](../../../../../asymptotic-expansion-split.md) as $y_0+\delta y_{1/2}+\delta^2y_1+\cdots$. Equating powers in the [differential equation](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) gives

$$
y_0'-xy_0=0,\qquad y_{1/2}'-xy_{1/2}=0,\qquad y_1'-xy_1=y_0^{(3)}.
$$

The first two equations give $y_0=Ae^{x^2/2}$ and $y_{1/2}=Be^{x^2/2}$. Since $y_0^{(3)}=A(x^3+3x)e^{x^2/2}$, an [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) yields

$$
\boxed{y_0=Ae^{x^2/2},\quad y_{1/2}=Be^{x^2/2},\quad
 y_1=e^{x^2/2}\left[C+A\left(\frac{x^4}4+\frac{3x^2}2\right)\right].}
$$

Here $A,B,C$ are matching constants; boundary conditions have not yet been imposed.
