<h1 id="a/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [asymptotic sequence](../../../../../asymptotic-sequence.md) $a_{n+1}(\epsilon)=o(a_n(\epsilon))$, the [asymptotic expansion](../../../../../asymptotic-expansion-split.md) condition is that every fixed truncation has remainder smaller than its last retained term:

$$
\boxed{f(\epsilon)-\sum_{n=0}^Na_n(\epsilon)=o(a_N(\epsilon))\quad\text{for every fixed }N.}
$$

This limit is taken as $\epsilon\to0$ in the specified direction or sector. The number $N$ stays fixed while taking that limit. The infinite series need not converge for any nonzero parameter.
