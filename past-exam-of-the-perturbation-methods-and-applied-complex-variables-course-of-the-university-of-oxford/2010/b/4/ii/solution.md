<h1 id="b/4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the branch of $\sqrt{1-Z^2}$ that is positive for $Z=iY$, $Y>0$. The [right-half-plane slit map](../../../../../right-half-plane-slit-map.md)

$$
\boxed{f(Z)=(1-Z^2)^{-1/2}}
$$

is holomorphic in the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), with [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $Z(1-Z^2)^{-3/2}$, the required [Schwarz-Christoffel formula](../../../../../schwarz-christoffel-mapping.md) form. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) has no interior zero.

To verify the image and global injectivity, factor the construction. Squaring maps the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) bijectively onto $\mathbb C\setminus[0,\infty)$. Thus $1-Z^2$ ranges over $\mathbb C\setminus(-\infty,1]$. The positive-real-part square root maps this onto the [right half-plane](../../../../../right-half-plane.md) with the interval $(0,1]$ removed; reciprocation maps it onto the [right half-plane](../../../../../right-half-plane.md) slit along $[1,\infty)$. This also identifies the slit tip as a boundary point; the strict inequality in the printed slit description must be understood with that boundary convention.

Approaching the real axis from above gives

$$
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
Z&(-\infty,-1)&(-1,0)&(0,1)&(1,\infty)\\\hline
f(Z)&\text{negative imaginary axis}&\text{lower slit bank}&\text{upper slit bank}&\text{positive imaginary axis}.
\end{array}
$$

In particular $Z=-1$ maps to the lower infinite prime end $A$, $Z=0$ to the tip $B=1$, and $Z=1$ to the upper infinite prime end $C$. The real-axis point at infinity maps to zero on the vertical wall.
