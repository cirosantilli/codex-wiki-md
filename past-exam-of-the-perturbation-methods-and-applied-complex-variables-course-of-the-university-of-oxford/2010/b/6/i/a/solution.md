<h1 id="b/6/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Glue the two analytic functions on their overlap, where their values agree. Since $\alpha<\beta$, the two half-planes cover the whole complex plane. The gluing therefore defines an [entire function](../../../../../../entire-function.md). It is bounded outside a sufficiently large disk by the two given infinity bounds, and bounded on the remaining compact disk by continuity. The [Liouville theorem](../../../../../../liouville-theorem.md) then gives **one common constant**.
