<h1 id="a/1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The ratio can be evaluated directly:

$$
\frac{e^{f(x)}}{e^{g(x)}}=\exp(f(x)-g(x)).
$$

The assumption $|f-g|\to0$ gives $f-g\to0$, so [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md) gives

$$
\boxed{e^{f(x)}\sim e^{g(x)}.}
$$

The additional premise $f\sim g$ is not needed for this implication. The useful requirement is additive control of the exponents, as explained by [asymptotic comparison of exponential functions](../../../../../asymptotic-comparison-of-exponential-functions.md).
