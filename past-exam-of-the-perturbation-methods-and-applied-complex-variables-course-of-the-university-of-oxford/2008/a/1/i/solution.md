<h1 id="a/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For each fixed $n$, the next term must be negligible relative to its predecessor:

$$
\boxed{a_{n+1}(\epsilon)=o(a_n(\epsilon))
\quad(\epsilon\to0).}
$$

The ratios are understood where their denominators are nonzero near the limiting point, or equivalently through the [little o notation](../../../../../little-o-notation.md) definition. For example $1,\epsilon,\epsilon^2,\ldots$ form an [asymptotic sequence](../../../../../asymptotic-sequence.md).
