<h1 id="a/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For every fixed truncation index $N$, the remainder must be smaller than the last retained term:

$$
\boxed{f(\epsilon)-\sum_{n=0}^{N}a_n(\epsilon)
=o(a_N(\epsilon))\quad(\epsilon\to0).}
$$

This is an [asymptotic expansion](../../../../../asymptotic-expansion-split.md) relative to the [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) in part (i). The number of retained terms is fixed before taking the limit. The associated [series](../../../../../series-mathematics.md) need not converge for any nonzero parameter; its role is to give successively smaller truncation errors. If $a_n$ is an [asymptotic sequence](../../../../../asymptotic-sequence.md), the same condition can be stated as a remainder $O(a_{N+1})$ when the next expansion condition is available, but the displayed [little o notation](../../../../../little-o-notation.md) formulation is the defining one.
