<h1 id="b/8/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The two [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) agree throughout their [overlap region](../../../../../../overlap-region.md) strip, so they define a single [holomorphic function](../../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the union of their domains, which is the whole complex plane. The resulting [entire function](../../../../../../entire-function.md) is bounded outside a sufficiently large disk by the assumptions at infinity. It is bounded on the disk by [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) and compactness. [Liouville theorem](../../../../../../liouville-theorem.md) therefore makes it constant:

$$
\boxed{w_+=w_-=C.}
$$

Agreement on an open [overlap region](../../../../../../overlap-region.md) is what permits [analytic continuation](../../../../../../analytic-continuation.md); equality of unspecified boundary values alone would need additional hypotheses.
