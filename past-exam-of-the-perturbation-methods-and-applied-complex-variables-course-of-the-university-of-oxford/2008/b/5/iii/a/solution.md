<h1 id="b/5/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A uniform horizontal [velocity potential](../../../../../../velocity-potential.md) in the upper $Z$-plane is a real constant times $Z$. Since $dz/dZ=(2/\pi)R/Z$, choose

$$
\boxed{w(z)=\frac2\pi Z(z)}
$$

up to an additive constant. Its [complex velocity](../../../../../../complex-velocity.md) is

$$
\frac{dw}{dz}=\frac ZR.
$$

As $Z\to\infty$ this tends to one, giving physical [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $(1,0)$. As $Z\to0$, $R\to i$ and $Z/R\to0$, giving zero [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) far down the channel. The [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) of $w$ is constant on the real $Z$-axis, so the physical boundary is a [streamline](../../../../../../streamline.md) and the impermeability condition holds. The usual [complex velocity](../../../../../../complex-velocity.md) convention is $dw/dz=u-iv$.
