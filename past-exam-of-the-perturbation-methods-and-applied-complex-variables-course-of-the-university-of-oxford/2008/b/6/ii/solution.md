<h1 id="b/6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) strip, $e^{\pi w/a}$ maps to the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). The real [Möbius transformation](../../../../../mobius-transformation.md)

$$
\zeta=-\frac1{1+e^{\pi w/a}}
$$

preserves that [half-plane](../../../../../half-plane.md) and sends its marked endpoints to $-1,\infty,0$ in the order $A/E,D,B/C$.

For the [hodograph plane](../../../../../hodograph-plane.md), first put $r=(q-1)/(q+1)$. Its image is the second quadrant, bounded by the negative real ray and positive imaginary ray. Squaring takes that quadrant to the lower [half-plane](../../../../../half-plane.md), and negating restores the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). Thus

$$
\zeta=-\left(\frac{q-1}{q+1}\right)^2
$$

has exactly the same three marked values. Uniqueness of a [conformal map](../../../../../conformal-map.md) to a [half-plane](../../../../../half-plane.md) with three assigned boundary values makes these maps equal. Cancel the minus signs to obtain

$$
\boxed{\frac1{e^{\pi w/a}+1}
=\left(\frac{w'-1}{w'+1}\right)^2.}
$$

The sign in the intermediate map is important for identifying the upper, rather than lower, [half-plane](../../../../../half-plane.md).
