<h1 id="b/6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

On the [fluid free boundary](../../../../../fluid-free-boundary.md) write $q=e^{-i\theta}$, with $-\pi<\theta<0$: the lip has $q=-1$ and the downstream limit has $q=1$. Then

$$
\frac{q-1}{q+1}=-i\tan(\theta/2).
$$

Substitution into the relation from part (ii) gives

$$
e^{\pi w/a}=-\csc^2(\theta/2).
$$

The surface lies on the top of the [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) strip, so its [logarithm](../../../../../logarithm.md) has [imaginary part](../../../../../imaginary-part.md) $\pi$, giving

$$
w=\frac a\pi\log\csc^2(\theta/2)+ia,\qquad
\frac{dw}{d\theta}=-\frac a\pi\cot(\theta/2).
$$

Finally $dz/dw=1/q=e^{i\theta}$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{dz}{d\theta}
=-\frac a\pi e^{i\theta}\cot(\theta/2).}
$$

The source's typeset $\cot\theta/2$ is interpreted as $\cot(\theta/2)$, as the derivation requires.
