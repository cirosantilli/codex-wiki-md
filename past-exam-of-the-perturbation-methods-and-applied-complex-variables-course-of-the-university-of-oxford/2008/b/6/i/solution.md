<h1 id="b/6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The lower wall has [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) zero. The [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md) through the lip and along the upper [fluid free boundary](../../../../../fluid-free-boundary.md) has [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) equal to the downstream [volumetric flow rate](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md), namely $a$ because the downstream speed is one and its depth is $a$. The [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) therefore fills

$$
\boxed{0<\operatorname{Im}w<a.}
$$

The chosen [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) origin gives $w(D)=ia$. The remote reservoir endpoints $A,E$ have [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) tending to $-\infty$; the downstream endpoints $B,C$ have [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) tending to $+\infty$.

Put $q=dw/dz=u-iv$. On the floor its values run from zero to one along the positive [real axis](../../../../../real-axis.md). Above the plate the flow goes left toward the lip, so the plate [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) run along the negative [real axis](../../../../../real-axis.md) from zero at $E$ to $-1$ at $D$. On the [fluid free boundary](../../../../../fluid-free-boundary.md) [Bernoulli's equation](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) and its constant [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) give $|q|=1$. Its downward [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) has $v<0$, hence $\operatorname{Im}q>0$. Consequently the [hodograph plane](../../../../../hodograph-plane.md) domain is

$$
\boxed{|q|<1,\quad\operatorname{Im}q>0,}
$$

the upper unit [semicircle](../../../../../semicircle.md). Its marked boundary values are $q(A)=q(E)=0$, $q(D)=-1$, and $q(B)=q(C)=1$.
