# Psychological research methods

↑ **Parent:** [Psychology](psychology-split.md)

Methods for defining questions, obtaining observations and evaluating claims in [psychology](psychology-split.md). [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments-split.md) can identify effects of assigned conditions; an [observational study](observational-study.md) measures existing variation. [Qualitative research methods](qualitative-research-methods.md) examine meanings and processes in context. The method must fit the question and the intended inference.
