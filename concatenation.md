# Concatenation

↑ **Parent:** [String](string.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concatenation)

[Concatenation](concatenation.md) joins two [strings](string.md) end to end, retaining the order of their symbols. For example, joining $ab$ and $ba$ gives $abba$. It has the [associative property](associative-property.md): joining $(uv)w$ or $u(vw)$ gives the same [word over an alphabet](string.md). The [empty word](empty-word.md) is its [identity element](identity-element.md). All finite [words over an alphabet](string.md) with this [binary operation](binary-operation.md) form a [free monoid](free-monoid.md).
