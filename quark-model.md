# Quark model

↑ **Parent:** [Hadron](hadron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quark_model)

Ordinary [baryons](baryon.md) have three valence [quarks](quark.md), and ordinary [mesons](meson.md) have a [quark](quark.md) and an [antiquark](antiquark.md). Additive quantum numbers such as [electric charge](electric-charge.md), [baryon number](baryon-number.md) and [strangeness](strangeness.md) are sums of constituent values. This valence description organizes [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) multiplets while allowing sea constituents and gluons.
