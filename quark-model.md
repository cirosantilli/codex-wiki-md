# Quark model

↑ **Parent:** [Hadron](hadron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quark_model)

Ordinary [baryons](baryon.md) have three valence [quarks](quark.md), and ordinary [mesons](meson.md) have a [quark](quark.md) and an [antiquark](antiquark.md). Additive quantum numbers such as [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), [baryon number](baryon-number.md) and [strangeness](strangeness.md) are sums of constituent values. This valence description organizes [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) multiplets while allowing sea constituents and gluons.

The [quark model](quark-model.md) makes a large catalogue manageable: the same few [quark flavors](quark-flavor.md) generate many different [hadrons](hadron.md). It predicts relations between [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), [strangeness](strangeness.md) and [isospin](isospin.md), rather than assigning these independently to each new particle. It is especially useful for asking which combinations are possible and which additional measurements could distinguish them. A constituent list alone does not determine a [mass](mass.md), a decay rate or whether a proposed combination forms a [bound state](bound-state.md).
