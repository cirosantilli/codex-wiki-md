# Scattering in a repulsive inverse-square potential

↑ **Parent:** [Repulsive inverse-square potential](repulsive-inverse-square-potential.md)

For $V(r)=c/r^2$ with $c>0$, nonzero physical [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $h$ and positive [energy](energy.md) $E$, put $\beta=\sqrt{1+2mc/h^2}>1$ and $r_{\min}=\sqrt{(c+h^2/(2m))/E}$. Direct integration of the [effective potential](effective-potential.md) radial equation gives the displayed [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md). It has one radial minimum and two straight-line asymptotes separated by polar angle $\pi/\beta$. The repulsive scattering deflection is $\pi-\pi/\beta$; no bounded or circular orbit exists at finite positive energy.
