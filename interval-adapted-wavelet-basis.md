# Interval-adapted wavelet basis

↑ **Parent:** [Wavelet](wavelet.md)

An interval-adapted wavelet basis is an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of $L^2[0,1]$ made of a finite coarse [scaling function](scaling-function.md) family and resolution-indexed [wavelets](wavelet.md). A localized construction modifies only a bounded number of functions near each endpoint at every level. Boundary modification must preserve nested refinement spaces and the required [vanishing moments](vanishing-moment.md); simple restriction of a whole-line basis does not do so.
