# Optical mode matching

↑ **Parent:** [Optical cavity](optical-cavity.md)

[Optical mode matching](optical-mode-matching.md) makes the spatial field of an incident beam equal to a desired [optical cavity](optical-cavity.md) [normal mode](normal-mode.md). For fundamental circular [Gaussian beams](gaussian-beam.md), matching the [complex beam parameter](complex-beam-parameter.md) fixes the [beam waist](beam-waist.md) size and location; the [optical axis](optical-axis.md), [polarization](polarization-waves.md) and [frequency](frequency.md) must also match. A single converging [optical lens](lens-optics.md) connects waist [Rayleigh ranges](rayleigh-length.md) $z_A,z_B$ only when $f^2\geq z_Az_B$. With $s=\sqrt{f^2/(z_Az_B)-1}$, waist-to-lens distances obey $u=f\pm z_As$, $v=f\pm z_Bs$ with correlated signs, followed by physical distance constraints.
