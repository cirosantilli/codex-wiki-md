# Coleman interpolation theorem for cyclotomic local units

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md)

For odd $p$ and a [norm-compatible sequence of local units](norm-compatible-sequence-of-local-units.md) in $\mathbb Q_p(\mu_{p^{n+1}})$, there is a unique $f_u\in\mathbb Z_p[[T]]^\times$ interpolating all its entries, and the [Coleman norm operator](coleman-norm-operator.md) fixes it. To construct it, choose integral unit polynomials $F_j$ interpolating entry $u_j$, and take a convergent subsequence of $N^jF_{2j}$ in the compact $(p,T)$-adic unit space. The congruence $NF\equiv F\pmod p$ and [Coleman norm contraction](coleman-norm-contraction.md) show that its evaluation at any fixed level tends to the desired entry. Uniqueness follows from the [Weierstrass preparation theorem](weierstrass-preparation-theorem.md): a nonzero integral power series has only finitely many zeros in the open unit disk. The norm relation on evaluations then proves norm fixation. Principal units correspond to $f_u(0)\equiv1\pmod p$.
