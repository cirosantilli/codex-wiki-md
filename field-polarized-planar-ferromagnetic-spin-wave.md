# Field-polarized planar ferromagnetic spin wave

↑ **Parent:** [Linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md)

For the [XY model](xy-model.md) with equal ferromagnetic exchange $J$ in its plane and [field](magnetic-field-split.md) term $-hS\sum_j S_j^x$, rotate the local quantization axis along the [Classical spin](classical-spin.md). The [Holstein–Primakoff transformation](holstein-primakoff-transformation.md) gives the number coefficient $\alpha_k=S(|h|+2J-J\cos k)$ and anomalous coefficient $\Delta_k=JS\cos k$ with one transverse-axis convention. [Bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization](bosonic-bogoliubov-diagonalization.md) gives $\epsilon_k=\sqrt{\alpha_k^2-\Delta_k^2}$ and [field](magnetic-field-split.md) [gap](energy-gap.md) $S\sqrt{|h|(|h|+2J)}$. A nonzero [field](magnetic-field-split.md) stabilizes every oscillator mode.
