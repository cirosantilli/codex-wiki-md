# Composition-dependent CNO homology

↑ **Parent:** [Homology relations for radiative stars](homology-relations-for-radiative-stars.md)

For homologous, chemically uniform, gas-pressure-supported radiative stars with specific energy generation $\epsilon\propto X\rho T^{13}$ and composition-dependent constant [opacity](opacity.md), [stellar homology](stellar-homology.md) gives $L_{\mathrm{nuc}}\propto X\mu^{13}M^{15}R^{-16}$ and $L_{\mathrm{rad}}\propto\mu^4M^3/\kappa$. Equating them gives the displayed scalings. [Hydrogen](hydrogen.md) depletion changes both [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md) and [electron-scattering opacity](electron-scattering-opacity.md); holding composition fixed gives $R\propto M^{3/4}$ and $L\propto M^3$. The power-law burning approximation applies only while [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) fuel remains.
