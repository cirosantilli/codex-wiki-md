# Electron hole

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron_hole)

An electron hole is the absence of an electron in an otherwise filled band, described as a mobile positive-charge excitation. It can form a [bound state](bound-state.md) with an electron, giving an [exciton](exciton.md).
