# Hyperbolic cosine

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic function](hyperbolic-function.md)

The [hyperbolic cosine](hyperbolic-cosine.md) is the [even function](even-function.md)

$$
\cosh x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2.
$$

It has [derivative](derivative.md) $\sinh x$ and second [derivative](derivative.md) $\cosh x>0$, so it is a strictly [convex function](convex-function.md). Its unique [global minimum](global-minimum.md) is $\cosh0=1$, and its [image](image-of-a-function.md) is $[1,\infty)$. For example, $\cosh(-1)=\cosh1\approx1.543081$. On the unit [hyperbola](hyperbola.md), the [hyperbolic cosine](hyperbolic-cosine.md) is the horizontal coordinate; as a graph against $x$, it is a symmetric bowl.

Its [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) begins

$$
\cosh x=1+\frac{x^2}{2!}+\frac{x^4}{4!}+\cdots.
$$

The positive fourth-order term explains why the bowl resembles a [parabola](parabola.md) near its minimum yet differs from one away from it. Restricting to $x\geq0$ gives the [inverse hyperbolic cosine](inverse-hyperbolic-cosine.md).
