# Option (finance)

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent claim](contingent-claim.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Option_(finance))

A [financial option](option-finance.md) grants a right, without a corresponding obligation to exercise, to transact at specified terms. A [call option](call-option.md) is a right to buy and a [put option](put-option.md) is a right to sell. The contract specifies its permitted exercise times and [strike price](strike-price.md); the value of that right differs from the [exercise payoff](exercise-payoff.md) at any particular time.
