# Counterterm closure

↑ **Parent:** [Counterterm](counterterm.md)

A family of actions is closed under [counterterms](counterterm.md) when all required divergent local terms can be absorbed into that family. This does not imply that the full [quantum effective action](effective-action.md) contains no additional finite effective [interaction vertices](interaction-vertex.md) or terms in its [derivative expansion](derivative-expansion.md).
