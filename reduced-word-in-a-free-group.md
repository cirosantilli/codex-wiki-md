# Reduced word in a free group

↑ **Parent:** [Free group](free-group.md)

A [reduced word](reduced-word-in-a-free-group.md) contains no adjacent generator and inverse-generator pair. Cancel such pairs to obtain the unique reduced representative in a [free group](free-group.md). One proof constructs the free [group](group-split.md) itself from [reduced words](reduced-word-in-a-free-group.md), with multiplication given by concatenation followed by cancellation; its action by these operations on words distinguishes distinct reduced representatives.
