# Simple-knot B-spline regularity

↑ **Parent:** [B-spline](b-spline.md)

For distinct increasing knots and $k\ge2$, the explicit [divided difference](divided-difference.md) formula writes an order-$k$ [B-spline](b-spline.md) as a finite sum of [truncated power functions](truncated-power-function.md) of degree $k-1$. It is a [piecewise polynomial function](piecewise-polynomial-function.md) and is globally $C^{k-2}$. Below its first knot the order-$k$ [divided difference](divided-difference.md) annihilates a degree-$k-1$ polynomial; above its last knot all values vanish. Nonzero first and last pieces give the exact closed support. Order one gives interval indicators instead of continuous splines.
