# Lebesgue outer measure

↑ **Parent:** [Outer measure](outer-measure.md)

The resulting [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) is the restriction of this outer measure to the sets satisfying the measurability criterion.

Lebesgue outer measure on $\mathbb R$ is

$$
m^*(E)=\inf\left\{\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|I_n|:E\subseteq\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}I_n\right\},
$$

where the infimum is over countable interval covers. The [Caratheodory measurability criterion](caratheodory-s-criterion.md) selects the Lebesgue-measurable sets.
