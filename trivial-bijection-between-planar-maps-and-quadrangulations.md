# Trivial bijection between planar maps and quadrangulations

↑ **Parent:** [Planar quadrangulation](planar-quadrangulation.md)

The trivial bijection sends a rooted [planar map](planar-map.md) with $n$ edges to a rooted [planar quadrangulation](planar-quadrangulation.md) with $n$ faces. Put one new vertex in each face and, in each corner, connect that face vertex to the incident original vertex. Each original edge then lies inside one quadrangular face; deleting the original edges gives the quadrangulation. The face bipartition recovers the original vertices and hence the inverse map.
