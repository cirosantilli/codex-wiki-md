# Five-mode rotating-convection truncation

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rotating-rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

A [Galerkin method](galerkin-method.md) retaining one [stream function](stream-function.md) mode, two temperature modes and two transverse-velocity modes produces five coupled amplitudes. A useful normalization gives $a'=-\sigma a+\sigma rb-\sigma^2qd$, $b'=a-b-ac$, $c'=h(-c+ab)$, $d'=a-\sigma d-ae$, $e'=-(4-h)\sigma e+had$. Here $\sigma>0$ is the [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md), $0<h<4$, and $q$ measures squared rotation. The trivial [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) has characteristic polynomial $(s+\sigma)^2(s+1)-\sigma r(s+\sigma)+\sigma^2q(s+1)$ in its three coupled linear modes; the remaining two modes decay. [Stationary bifurcations](stationary-bifurcation.md) and oscillatory onset meet at $q=(1+\sigma)/(1-\sigma)$, $r=2/(1-\sigma)$ when $\sigma<1$.
