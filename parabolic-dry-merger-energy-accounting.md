# Parabolic dry-merger energy accounting

↑ **Parent:** [Dry galaxy merger](dry-galaxy-merger.md)

In a [dry galaxy merger](dry-galaxy-merger.md), zero orbital energy at infinite separation and retention of all mass and energy give $E_F=E_I+E_A$. If the progenitors and remnant satisfy the [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md), their [gravitational radii](gravitational-radius.md) obey $R_F/R_I=(1+\eta)^2/(1+\epsilon\eta)$, where $\eta=M_A/M_I$ and $\epsilon=\langle v_A^2\rangle/\langle v_I^2\rangle$. The latter average is weighted by satellite mass. Nonzero orbital energy, escaping stars, or dissipative losses change the energy accounting.
