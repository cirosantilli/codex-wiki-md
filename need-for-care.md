# Need for care

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical formulation](clinical-formulation.md)

A requirement for support or treatment assessed through difficulties, distress, impairment, risk, context and preferences. Actual service contact is an imperfect proxy because access, willingness and [discrimination](discrimination.md) affect who receives care.
