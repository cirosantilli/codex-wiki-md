# Enthalpy change of solution

↑ **Parent:** [Thermochemistry](thermochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enthalpy_change_of_solution)

An [enthalpy of solution](enthalpy-change-of-solution.md) is an [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) difference for dissolution into a specified [solvent](solvent.md) at stated [temperature](temperature.md) and final [concentration](concentration.md). Keep a measured finite-dilution value distinct from an infinite-dilution reference value.
