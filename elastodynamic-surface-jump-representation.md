# Elastodynamic surface-jump representation

↑ **Parent:** [Elastodynamic Green tensor](elastodynamic-green-tensor.md)

Choose a surface normal $\mathbf n$ from its minus to its plus side and jumps $[u]=u^+-u^-$, $[t]=(\sigma^+-\sigma^-)\mathbf n$. The equivalent force density is $-[t]$ and the dipole density is $M_{jk}=c_{jk\ell m}[u_\ell]n_m$. This follows by applying the elastic wave operator to a piecewise smooth [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md): its distributional source is $-[t_i]\delta_\Sigma-\partial_j(c_{ijkl}[u_k]n_l\delta_\Sigma)$. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) against the [elastodynamic Green tensor](elastodynamic-green-tensor.md) gives the displayed signs. At a material interface the dipole term is interpreted through the continuous reciprocal Green [traction](traction.md), rather than an undefined product of a discontinuous stiffness with a surface delta.
