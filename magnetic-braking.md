# Magnetic braking

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic induction](electromagnetic-induction.md)

Braking caused by induced currents whose magnetic [force](force-split.md) opposes motion. [energy](energy-split.md) removed from mechanical motion can become resistive [heat](heat-split.md); the [force](force-split.md) depends on magnetic-field gradients or boundary crossing, resistance and [inductance](inductance.md).
