# Vectorlike gauge spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Chirality (physics)](chirality-physics.md)

A vectorlike gauge spectrum pairs complex [gauge group representations](gauge-group-representation.md) $R$ and $\bar R$ among independent left-handed [Weyl spinors](weyl-spinor.md). Such a pair permits a gauge-invariant [fermion](fermion.md) mass and occurs in a full charged [hypermultiplet](hypermultiplet.md) of four-dimensional $\mathcal N=2$ [extended supersymmetry](extended-supersymmetry.md).
