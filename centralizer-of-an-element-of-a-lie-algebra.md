# Centralizer of an element of a Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

For an element $x$ of a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$, its centralizer is the [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) $Z_{\mathfrak g}(x)=\{y\in\mathfrak g:[y,x]=0\}$, equivalently the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $\operatorname{ad}x$ in the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
