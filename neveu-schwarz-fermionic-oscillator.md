<h1 id="neveu-schwarz-fermionic-oscillator">Neveu–Schwarz fermionic oscillator</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md)

Antiperiodic [worldsheet Majorana fermions](worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md) give transverse modes $b_r^I$, $r\in\mathbb Z+\tfrac12$, with $\{b_r^I,b_s^J\}=\delta^{IJ}\delta_{r+s,0}$. Positive modes annihilate the [oscillator vacuum](oscillator-vacuum.md); negative modes are [fermionic creation operators](fermionic-creation-operator.md).
