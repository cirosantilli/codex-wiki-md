# Complex permittivity

↑ **Parent:** [Permittivity](permittivity-split.md)

For harmonic [electric fields](electric-field-split.md) with time convention $e^{i\omega t}$, write $\mathbf D=\epsilon^*(\omega)\mathbf E$ for their complex amplitudes, where $\omega$ is [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). The [real part](real-part.md) $\epsilon'$ describes the in-phase [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md); the minus [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) $\epsilon''$ describes the loss component. The sign reverses with the opposite time convention. [Complex permittivity](complex-permittivity.md) can also be a [tensor](tensor.md) in a [linear anisotropic dielectric](linear-anisotropic-dielectric.md).
