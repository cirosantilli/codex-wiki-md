# Capital asset pricing model

↑ **Parent:** [Market portfolio](market-portfolio.md)

The expected excess return obeys $\mathbb E(R_i-r)=\beta_i\mathbb E(R_M-r)$ in the mean-variance market model. The [beta of an asset](beta-of-an-asset.md) is a covariance-to-market-variance ratio.
