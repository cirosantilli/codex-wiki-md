# Naturality

↑ **Parent:** [Natural transformation](natural-transformation.md)

For a [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) $\theta:F\Rightarrow G$, naturality is the equation $G(f)\theta_A=\theta_BF(f)$ for each [morphism](morphism.md) $f:A\to B$. Thus transforming and then applying a [functor](functor.md) gives the same result as applying that functor and then transforming. It makes the component maps a coherent transformation of [functors](functor.md).
