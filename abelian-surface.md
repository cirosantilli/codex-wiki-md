# Abelian surface

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal algebraic surface](minimal-algebraic-surface.md)

An abelian surface is a two-dimensional [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md). It contains no rational curve: a morphism from $\mathbb P^1$ to an abelian variety is constant. Consequently every abelian surface is a [minimal algebraic surface](minimal-algebraic-surface.md).
