# Conformal radius under a chordal Loewner flow

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal radius](conformal-radius.md)

Before the [Loewner swallowing time](interior-point-swallowing-time-for-a-loewner-chain.md) of $z$, set $Z_t=g_t(z)-U_t=X_t+iY_t$ and $\Upsilon_t=Y_t/|g_t'(z)|$. Since the [conformal radius](conformal-radius.md) of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) at $Z_t$ is $2Y_t$, the transformation rule gives $2\Upsilon_t=\operatorname{crad}_{D_t}(z)$. The [Koebe quarter theorem](koebe-quarter-theorem.md) and the interior-disc comparison give $\operatorname{dist}(z,\partial D_t)/2\leq\Upsilon_t\leq2\operatorname{dist}(z,\partial D_t)$. Differentiating the [Chordal Loewner equation](chordal-loewner-equation.md) gives $\boxed{\partial_t\log\Upsilon_t=-4Y_t^2/|Z_t|^4}$. Thus this quantity decreases as the remaining [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) shrinks.
