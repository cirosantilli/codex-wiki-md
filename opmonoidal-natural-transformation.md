# Opmonoidal natural transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Opmonoidal functor](opmonoidal-functor.md)

A [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) $\tau:F\Rightarrow G$ satisfying $G_2\tau_{X\otimes Y}=(\tau_X\otimes\tau_Y)F_2$ and $G_0\tau_I=F_0$.
