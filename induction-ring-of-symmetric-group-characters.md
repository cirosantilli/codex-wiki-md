# Induction ring of symmetric-group characters

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory of the symmetric group](representation-theory-of-the-symmetric-group-split.md)

The graded abelian group $\bigoplus_{n\ge0}R(S_n)$ of ordinary virtual [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) has product $\chi\star\psi=\operatorname{Ind}_{S_m\times S_n}^{S_{m+n}}(\chi\boxtimes\psi)$ for degrees $m,n$. Its unit is the trivial degree-zero character. Associativity follows by transitivity of [induced representations](induced-representation.md) through the three-factor Young subgroup; commutativity follows by conjugating the block positions. The [Frobenius characteristic map](frobenius-characteristic-map.md) identifies this ring with the ring of [symmetric functions](symmetric-function.md), sending irreducible Specht characters to [Schur functions](schur-polynomial.md). This product is different from pointwise multiplication of characters of the same symmetric group.
