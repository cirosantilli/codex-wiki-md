# Image edge enhancement

↑ **Parent:** [Image edge](image-edge.md)

[image edge](image-edge.md) enhancement increases the apparent sharpness or contrast of an [image edge](image-edge.md). [Unsharp masking](unsharp-masking.md) gives bounded linear high-frequency amplification; backward normal diffusion in the [Perona-Malik equation](perona-malik-equation.md) gives formal nonlinear sharpening with [ill-posedness](ill-posed-problem.md) risks. Positive diffusion tensors chiefly preserve or connect structure rather than perform unbounded backward diffusion.
