# Spherical mean of a Poisson solution outside its source

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical mean](spherical-mean.md)

In three dimensions let $v\in H_0^1(B_R)$ solve $-\Delta v=f$, where $f\in L^{6/5}(B_R)$ is supported in $B_a$ and $0<a<R$. Write $M=\int f$ and $\bar v(s)=(4\pi)^{-1}\int_{S^2}v(s\omega)\,d\omega$. Outside $B_a$, the [weak solution](weak-solution.md) is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md). The [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md) against radial [test functions](test-function.md) gives $-4\pi s^2\bar v'(s)=M$ for $a<s<R$. The zero [Sobolev trace](trace-operator.md) gives $\bar v(R)=0$; integration proves the displayed formula, without assuming the source is a [radial function](radial-function.md).

[Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) on spheres gives $\|v\|_2^2\geq M^2(4\pi)^{-1}\int_a^R(1-s/R)^2\,ds$. For fixed nonzero $M$ and fixed $a$, this grows linearly in $R$. Such solutions on expanding [balls](ball-mathematics.md) cannot remain bounded in the full $H^1(\mathbb R^3)$ [norm](norm.md) after [zero extension of H01](zero-extension-of-h01.md).
