# Unitary group as a regular level set

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary group](unitary-group.md)

The smooth map $F(A)=A^*A$ from complex matrices, regarded as a real vector space, to Hermitian matrices has derivative $DF_A(K)=A^*K+K^*A$. At a unitary $A$, taking $K=AH/2$ gives any prescribed Hermitian $H$. Thus $I$ is a [regular value](regular-value.md) and $F^{-1}(I)=U(n)$ is a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) of real dimension $n^2$.
