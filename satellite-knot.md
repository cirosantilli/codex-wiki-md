# Satellite knot

↑ **Parent:** [Knot](knot.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_knot)

A satellite knot $P(K)$ is obtained by embedding a patterned solid torus into a tubular neighborhood of a companion knot $K$.
