# Active systems consolidation hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Systems consolidation](systems-consolidation.md)

An account in which reactivation of recently encoded representations during offline states modifies distributed [neural circuits](neural-circuit.md). Coordination of [hippocampal sharp-wave ripples](hippocampal-sharp-wave-ripple.md), [sleep spindles](sleep-spindle.md) and cortical [sleep slow oscillations](sleep-slow-oscillation.md) provides a candidate timing mechanism. Reactivation, strengthened behaviour and permanent cortical storage are distinct claims requiring different evidence.
