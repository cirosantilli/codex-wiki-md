# Hail

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hail)

Ice precipitation grown within thunderstorms, chiefly by collection and freezing of supercooled liquid droplets. A strong [updraft](updraft.md) can retain stones in a growth region; changing growth conditions can create clear and opaque layers without requiring one complete up-and-down trip per layer.[Growth and layering](severe-weather-101-hail-basics-by-noaa.md)
