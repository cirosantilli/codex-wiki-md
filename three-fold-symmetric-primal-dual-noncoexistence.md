# Three-fold symmetric primal-dual noncoexistence

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating arms argument at the self-dual percolation parameter](alternating-arms-argument-at-the-self-dual-percolation-parameter.md)

For independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on a periodic plane lattice with three-fold rotation symmetry, the primal model at $p$ and the [dual bond percolation](dual-bond-percolation.md) model at $1-p$ cannot both percolate. To adapt [Zhang's argument](alternating-arms-argument-at-the-self-dual-percolation-parameter.md), divide a large boundary circle into six arcs, repeating the division in each $120$-degree sector. Vary the division point until the failure probabilities $a,b$ of the two alternating primal arc types are comparable within a factor $(1-p)^{-2}$. Moving past a single crossing edge changes a failure probability by at most a factor $(1-p)^{-1}$, by the [FKG inequality](fkg-inequality.md). If the probability of no primal exterior arm is at most $\varepsilon$, then $a^3b^3\le\varepsilon$ and both failures are at most $(1-p)^{-1}\varepsilon^{1/6}$. For the dual model, at least one arc type has failure at most $\varepsilon^{1/6}$; its three rotations have the same bound. Four alternating primal/dual arms now occur with positive probability, contradicting the [alternating exterior-arm separation lemma](alternating-exterior-arm-separation-lemma.md) and uniqueness after a finite modification. This extends the four-fold argument to the [martini lattice](martini-lattice.md).
