# Nilradical commutes with localization

↑ **Parent:** [Nilradical](nilradical.md)

For any [multiplicative subset](multiplicatively-closed-set.md) $S$ of a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$, fractions with [nilpotent](nilpotent.md) numerators are [nilpotent](nilpotent.md) in $S^{-1}R$. Conversely, if $(a/s)^n=0$, the [vanishing criterion in a module localization](vanishing-criterion-in-a-module-localization.md) gives $t\in S$ with $ta^n=0$. Then $(ta)^n=t^{n-1}ta^n=0$ and $a/s=ta/(ts)$ has a [nilpotent](nilpotent.md) numerator. This proves the formula for the [nilradical](nilradical.md) without any finiteness assumption.
