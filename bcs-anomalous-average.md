# BCS anomalous average

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

The BCS anomalous average is the pair expectation $\langle b_k\rangle=\langle c_{-k\downarrow}c_{k\uparrow}\rangle$. In the [BCS ground state](bcs-ground-state.md) it is $u_kv_k=\Delta_k/(2E_k)$ for real gap at zero temperature. At temperature $T$ it becomes $\Delta_k\tanh(E_k/(2k_BT))/(2E_k)$. Substitution into the pairing-field definition gives the [BCS gap equation](bcs-gap-equation.md).
