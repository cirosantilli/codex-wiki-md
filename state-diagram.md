# State diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Unified Modeling Language](unified-modeling-language.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_diagram)

Describe the possible states of a system and transitions triggered by events, optionally constrained by guards and accompanied by actions. The diagram emphasizes a component's lifecycle and permitted reactions. It can describe many interaction traces, while a [sequence diagram](sequence-diagram.md) focuses on the temporal ordering of messages in an interaction.
