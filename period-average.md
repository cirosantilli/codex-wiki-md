# Period average

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic function](periodic-function.md)

For an integrable [periodic function](periodic-function.md) $f$ with period $T$, its period average is $\langle f\rangle=T^{-1}\int_{t_0}^{t_0+T}f(t)\,dt$, independent of $t_0$. In particular a sinusoidal square has period average $1/2$.
