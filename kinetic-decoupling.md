# Kinetic decoupling

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal decoupling in cosmology](thermal-decoupling-in-cosmology.md)

Kinetic decoupling occurs when momentum-exchange collisions cease to maintain the [temperature](temperature.md) and [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) of a particle species in equilibrium with a surrounding bath. Afterwards, a freely moving nonrelativistic particle has momentum proportional to $a^{-1}$ and effective kinetic [temperature](temperature.md) proportional to $a^{-2}$, where $a$ is the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md).
