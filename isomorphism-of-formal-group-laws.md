# Isomorphism of formal group laws

↑ **Parent:** [Formal group law](formal-group-law.md)

An isomorphism from [formal group law](formal-group-law.md) $F$ to $G$ over $R$ is a formal series $h(T)=a_1T+a_2T^2+\cdots$ with $a_1\in R^*$ and $h(F(X,Y))=G(h(X),h(Y))$. Coefficient recursion gives a compositional inverse over $R$. An isomorphism of formal series need not converge on an arbitrary prescribed analytic domain; convergence must be checked separately.
