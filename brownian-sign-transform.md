# Brownian sign transform

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

The sign transform of standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) is itself standard Brownian motion. The integrand is [predictable](predictable-process.md) because it is a Borel function of a continuous adapted process. Brownian motion spends zero Lebesgue time at a specified level: integrate $\mathbb P(B_s=0)=0$ over positive times. Consequently the transform's [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) is $t$, regardless of the chosen sign value at zero. The [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md) applies.
