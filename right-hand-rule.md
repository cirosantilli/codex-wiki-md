# Right-hand rule

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right-hand_rule)

The right-hand rule fixes the orientation of a [cross product](cross-product.md) and relates the circulation direction of an [electric current](electric-current.md) to the direction of its [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).
