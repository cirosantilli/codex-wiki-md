# Genome-wide association study

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic association](genetic-association.md)

A study testing associations between genomic variants and a measured outcome across many genomic positions. Relatedness, [population stratification](population-stratification.md), multiple testing and independent replication matter; an association can tag a neighboring causal change through [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md).
