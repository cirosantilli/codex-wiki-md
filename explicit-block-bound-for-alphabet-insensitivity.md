# Explicit block bound for alphabet insensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Alphabet insensitivity lemma](alphabet-insensitivity-lemma.md)

For r colours, k letters and d variable blocks, set $P_i=\sum_{j<i}L_j$ and $L_i=r^{k^{P_i+d-i}}$. Process blocks from right to left. In block i, the $L_i+1$ words $a^t b^{L_i-t}$ have [colour profiles](colour-profile-of-a-finite-block.md) indexed by all $k^{P_i}$ earlier words and $k^{d-i}$ later variable assignments. Two profiles agree, giving a nonempty variable interval insensitive to exchanging a and b. Previously obtained insensitivity holds for all earlier words and survives their restriction. This constructs the [combinatorial subspace](combinatorial-subspace.md) required by the [alphabet insensitivity lemma](alphabet-insensitivity-lemma.md).
