# Atmospheric blast-wave range

↑ **Parent:** [Blast wave](blast-wave.md)

For an explosion confined approximately to a thin atmospheric layer with column [mass density](density.md) $\Sigma=\int\rho\,dz$, an optimistic conserved-energy scaling is $E\sim\pi D^2\Sigma U^2$. The strong [blast wave](blast-wave.md) approximation becomes invalid near front speed $U\sim c_a$, where $c_a$ is the ambient [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md), giving the displayed distance scale. Its numerical coefficient depends on the flow profiles, geometry and [heat capacity ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md). Vertical energy escape makes full confinement an optimistic approximation. The criterion estimates survival of a strong [shock wave](shock-wave.md), rather than whether any later [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) is detectable.
