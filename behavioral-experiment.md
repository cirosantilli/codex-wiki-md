# Behavioral experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive behavioral therapy](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md)

A planned test of a specific prediction relevant to a psychological difficulty. The design specifies what would be expected under competing interpretations and reviews the observed outcome, including alternative explanations; it is not simply repeating a feared activity without a [learning](learning.md) question.
