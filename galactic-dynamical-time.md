# Galactic dynamical time

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy dynamics](galaxy-dynamics.md)

A [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) of characteristic radius $R$ and [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) $\sigma$ has crossing time $R/\sigma$. Gravitational balance gives $GM/R\sim\sigma^2$, hence $t_{\rm dyn}\sim GM/\sigma^3$. A numerical coefficient depends on the radius definition, density profile, and whether one uses a crossing, orbital, or free-fall time; these are not identical clocks.
