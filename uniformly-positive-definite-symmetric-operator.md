# Uniformly positive definite symmetric operator

↑ **Parent:** [Positive definite symmetric operator](positive-definite-symmetric-operator.md)

A [positive definite symmetric operator](positive-definite-symmetric-operator.md) is uniformly positive definite if $\langle Lv,v\rangle\geq\gamma\|v\|^2$ for some $\gamma>0$. For a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) defined on a whole [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), this is a symmetric [coercive operator](coercive-operator.md), and the [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) supplies a unique solution to $Lu=f$ for every $f$. For a differential operator, the corresponding bounded coercive form is used on its form space.
