# Boolean formula

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md)

A Boolean formula is a finite expression built from [Boolean variables](boolean-variable.md) and [Boolean operations](boolean-operation.md). A choice of truth values determines its value. The [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md) asks whether some choice makes it true.
