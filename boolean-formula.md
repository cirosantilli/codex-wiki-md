# Boolean formula

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md)

A [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md) is a finite expression built from [Boolean variables](boolean-variable.md) and [Boolean operations](boolean-operation.md), equivalently a [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) with two-valued semantics. A [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) determines its [truth value](truth-value.md). In $(x\lor y)\land\neg x$, [logical negation](negation.md) acts only on $x$, and the outer [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md) combines $x\lor y$ with $\neg x$. Occurrences of the same [Boolean variable](boolean-variable.md) share one [truth value](truth-value.md). The [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md) asks whether some [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) makes the [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md) true.
