# Lie bracket from local group commutators

↑ **Parent:** [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md)

In a [local exponential chart](local-exponential-chart.md), the mixed second differential of the [group commutator](group-commutator.md) defines a [bilinear map](bilinear-map.md) on the [tangent space](tangent-space.md) at the identity. Inversion after swapping the two group elements proves antisymmetry. Differentiating conjugation gives $[X,Y]=\operatorname{ad}(X)Y$; applying [naturality of the Lie bracket](differential-of-a-lie-group-homomorphism-preserves-lie-brackets.md) to the [Adjoint representation of a Lie group](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md) then proves the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md). For a matrix group this recovers $XY-YX$ by expanding through the mixed term.
