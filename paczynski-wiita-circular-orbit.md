<h1 id="paczynski-wiita-circular-orbit">Paczyński-Wiita circular orbit</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Paczyński-Wiita potential](paczynski-wiita-potential.md)

Let $r_S=2GM/c^2$. In the [Paczyński-Wiita potential](paczynski-wiita-potential.md), [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) balance and the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) formula give

$$
\Omega^2=\frac{GM}{r(r-r_S)^2},\quad
h=\frac{\sqrt{GM}\,r^{3/2}}{r-r_S},\quad
\kappa_r^2=\frac{GM(r-3r_S)}{r(r-r_S)^3}.
$$

Thus the [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md) lies at $r=3r_S$. Its [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) is $-c^2/16$, giving a zero-torque [radiative efficiency of black-hole accretion](radiative-efficiency-of-black-hole-accretion.md) of $1/16$. The model reproduces the Schwarzschild inner orbit exactly but approximates its relativistic energy and frequency. [Abramowicz's derivation](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.0913) explains the potential's relation to the Schwarzschild effective potential.
