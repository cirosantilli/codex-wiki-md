# Diffusion creep

↑ **Parent:** [Creep (deformation)](creep-deformation.md)

Permanent shape change driven by stress-biased atomic or [vacancy diffusion](vacancy-diffusion.md). Different [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) at differently stressed grain surfaces drive material transfer, requiring grain-shape accommodation. Bulk and boundary transport give different grain-size dependencies.
