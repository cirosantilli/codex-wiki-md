# Uniform-current toroidal-field curl reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Toroidal magnetic field](toroidal-magnetic-field.md)

For constant-density incompressible [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md) about $\mathbf B=Jr\mathbf e_\phi$, the cylindrical basis contributions make the linearized [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) equal to $imJ\mathbf b+2J\mathbf e_z\times\mathbf b$, apart from a [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) gradient. The induction equation reduces to $i\omega\mathbf b=imJ\mathbf u$. Taking the curl after eliminating velocity gives the displayed relation, with $\widetilde J=J/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$. A second curl gives a vector Helmholtz equation and squared frequencies $\omega^2=\widetilde J^2[m^2\pm2|mk|/(\alpha^2+k^2)^{1/2}]$. The lower $|m|=1$ branch grows when $3k^2>\alpha^2$.
