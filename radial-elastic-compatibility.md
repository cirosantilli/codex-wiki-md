# Radial elastic compatibility

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

In a homogeneous isotropic solid without [body force](body-force.md), a radial [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) satisfies $(\lambda+2\mu)(u'+2u/r)'=0$. For nonzero $\lambda+2\mu$, its [volumetric strain](volumetric-strain.md) is constant. Since [Hooke's law](hooke-s-law.md) gives $\operatorname{tr}\tau=(3\lambda+2\mu)(u'+2u/r)$, its [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) has constant [trace](matrix-trace.md). After [plasticity in continuum mechanics](plasticity-physics.md) has occurred, this conclusion applies to purely elastic increments during [elastic unloading](elastic-unloading.md), and need not apply to the total [residual stress](residual-stress.md).
