# Modulo-six counter

↑ **Parent:** [Binary counter](binary-counter.md)

A [digital counter](digital-counter.md) cycling through six stored values. With ordinary [binary representation](binary-number.md), the valid three-[bit](bit.md) states are $000,001,010,011,100,101$. The unused encodings require either an initialization assumption or specified recovery transitions.
