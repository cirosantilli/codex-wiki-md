# Connected shell-contraction expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md)

The [cumulant expansion](cumulant-expansion.md) of the shell integral keeps precisely [connected Feynman diagrams](connected-feynman-diagram.md) built from [shell-restricted scalar propagators](shell-restricted-scalar-propagator.md). The external low fields are backgrounds, not propagators. Connected graphs with bridges can contribute, so the expansion is not limited to [one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagrams](one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md).
