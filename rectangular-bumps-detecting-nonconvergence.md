# Rectangular bumps detecting nonconvergence

↑ **Parent:** [Separate continuity](separate-continuity.md)

Let $(u_n,v_n)\to(0,0)$ with both coordinates nonzero, $r_n=|u_n|/4$, $s_n=|v_n|/4$, and $\phi(t)=\max(1-|t|,0)$. The displayed [function](function-split.md) equals one at every $(u_n,v_n)$ and zero on both coordinate axes. Each coordinate line off the corresponding axis meets only finitely many supports, so its restriction is a [finite maximum](maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) of [continuous functions](continuous-function.md). On the axes it is zero. Hence $B$ is [separately continuous](separate-continuity.md) and disproves [convergence](convergence.md) of this [sequence](sequence.md) in any [topology](topology-split.md) making every [separately continuous](separate-continuity.md) [function](function-split.md) [continuous](continuous-function.md). Isotropic radial bumps need not have this property when one coordinate approaches zero much faster than the other.
