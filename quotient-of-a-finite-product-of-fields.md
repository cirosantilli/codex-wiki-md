# Quotient of a finite product of fields

↑ **Parent:** [Direct product of rings](direct-product-of-rings.md)

A [quotient ring](quotient-ring.md) of a finite [direct product of rings](direct-product-of-rings.md) whose factors are [fields](field.md) is a product of a subset of those factors. By [ideals of a finite product of rings](ideals-of-a-finite-product-of-rings.md), write $J=\prod_jJ_j$. Since a [field](field.md) has only the zero [ideal](ideal.md) and the whole [field](field.md) as [ideals](ideal.md), every $J_j$ is one of these. The [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $(x_j)\mapsto(x_j+J_j)$ is surjective with [kernel of a ring homomorphism](kernel-of-a-ring-homomorphism.md) $J$, so the [first isomorphism theorem for rings](first-isomorphism-theorem-for-rings.md) gives $(\prod_jK_j)/J\cong\prod_j(K_j/J_j)$. Discard the [zero ring](zero-ring.md) factors. If no factors survive, the result is the [zero ring](zero-ring.md).
