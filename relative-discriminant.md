# Relative discriminant

↑ **Parent:** [Discriminant ideal](discriminant-ideal.md)

The relative discriminant $\mathfrak d_{L/K}$ of a finite extension of [number fields](number-field.md) is the [discriminant ideal](discriminant-ideal.md) of $\mathcal O_L$ over $\mathcal O_K$. It satisfies

$$
|d_L|=|d_K|^{[L:K]}N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\mathfrak d_{L/K}),
$$

and its prime divisors are exactly the ramified finite primes.
