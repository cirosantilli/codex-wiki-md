# Monge gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Static gauge](static-gauge.md)

For a string, this local [static gauge](static-gauge.md) identifies two embedding coordinates with the worldsheet coordinates, $X^0=t$ and $X^1=\sigma$. The remaining coordinates describe a graph over that coordinate plane. Folded embeddings may require more than one patch.
