<h1 id="lower-semicontinuity-of-a-holder-seminorm">Lower semicontinuity of a Hölder seminorm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder seminorm](holder-seminorm.md)

If $f_j\to f$ uniformly and the [Hölder seminorms](holder-seminorm.md) are uniformly bounded, each fixed difference quotient converges. Taking the supremum over point pairs gives $[f]_\mu\leq\liminf_j[f_j]_\mu$. The [supremum norms](supremum-norm.md) converge, so the full [Hölder norm](holder-norm.md) has the same [lower semicontinuity of a Hölder seminorm](lower-semicontinuity-of-a-holder-seminorm.md).
