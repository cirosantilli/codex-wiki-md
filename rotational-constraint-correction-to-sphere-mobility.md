# Rotational constraint correction to sphere mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Method of reflections for Stokes flow](method-of-reflections-for-stokes-flow.md)

In the preceding two-sphere geometry, holding the second sphere's angular velocity at zero needs a torque $\mathbf G=-4\pi\mu a^3\boldsymbol\omega_\infty$. Its [rotlet](rotlet.md) adds

$$
\delta\mathbf U_{\rm rot}=-\frac{3a^4}{4R^4}
\left[\mathbf U_0-(\mathbf U_0\cdot\widehat{\mathbf R})\widehat{\mathbf R}\right]
$$

to the first sphere's velocity, in addition to the [self-mobility correction from a distant force-free sphere](self-mobility-correction-from-a-distant-force-free-sphere.md). This leading constraint correction is transverse to the line of centres.
