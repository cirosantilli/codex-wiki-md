# Residue and signature group of a modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Ray class exact sequence](ray-class-exact-sequence.md)

This group records finite unit residues and signs at the selected real places of a [modulus of a number field](modulus-of-a-number-field.md). Omit the residue factor for trivial finite modulus. Its quotient by the image of the [unit group](unit-group.md) is naturally the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of the map from the [ray class group](ray-class-group.md) to the ordinary [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md). The [weak approximation for number fields](weak-approximation-for-number-fields.md) theorem supplies elements realizing the specified data, and units account for changing a generator of a [principal fractional ideal](principal-fractional-ideal.md).
