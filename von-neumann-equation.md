# Von Neumann equation

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_equation)

The density operator of a closed quantum system obeys

$$
i\hbar\dot\rho=[H,\rho].
$$
