# Asymptotic relative speed of a disrupted binary

↑ **Parent:** [Kick-direction binary survival criterion](kick-direction-binary-survival-criterion.md)

A disrupted [binary star](binary-star.md) with positive post-impulse [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) $\varepsilon'$ has relative speed $V=\sqrt{2\varepsilon'}$ at infinite separation, because the [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md) tends to zero. With the notation of the [kick-direction binary survival criterion](kick-direction-binary-survival-criterion.md), $V=v\sqrt{1+2\alpha\cos\psi+\alpha^2-2\beta}$. This is not a systemic recoil velocity. A marginal [parabolic Kepler orbit](parabolic-trajectory.md) has $V=0$ at infinity.
