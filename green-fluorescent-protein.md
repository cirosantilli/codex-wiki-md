# Green fluorescent protein

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescent protein](fluorescent-protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_fluorescent_protein)

[GFP](green-fluorescent-protein.md) is a fluorescent [protein](protein-split.md) used to report cellular location or [gene expression](gene-expression.md). Fluorophore maturation and protein turnover introduce delays and filtering, so measured intensity does not in general reproduce an instantaneous transcription rate.
