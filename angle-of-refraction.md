# Angle of refraction

↑ **Parent:** [Refraction](refraction.md)

The [angle of refraction](angle-of-refraction.md) is the [angle](angle.md) between the transmitted [light ray](light-ray.md) and the surface [normal vector](normal-vector.md), measured on the transmitted side. With positive [refractive indices](refractive-index.md), a propagating transmitted [light ray](light-ray.md) satisfies $n_i\sin\theta_i=n_t\sin\theta_t$ in [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md). The incident and transmitted [angles](angle.md) are measured from the same normal line, not from the surface and not from the emergent [light ray](light-ray.md) at a second surface.
