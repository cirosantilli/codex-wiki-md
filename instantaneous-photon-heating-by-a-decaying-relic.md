# Instantaneous photon heating by a decaying relic

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological entropy conservation](cosmological-entropy-conservation.md)

If a nonrelativistic relic decays entirely into promptly thermalized photons, energy conservation at fixed [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) gives $\rho_\gamma^+=\rho_\gamma^-+\rho_\chi^-$. Since thermal photons have $\rho_\gamma\propto T_\gamma^4$, their temperature rises by the displayed factor while an already decoupled [Cosmic neutrino background](cosmic-neutrino-background.md) is not heated. Before decay the relic-to-photon density ratio grows as $a$.
