# Hankel inversion of a rotation-measure correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Long-path projection of a magnetic correlation](long-path-projection-of-a-magnetic-correlation.md)

Invert the two-dimensional [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) in the [long-path projection of a magnetic correlation](long-path-projection-of-a-magnetic-correlation.md), then perform the angular integration in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md). The angular integral is $2\pi J_0(kr)$, a [Bessel function of the first kind](bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md), giving the displayed [Hankel transform](hankel-transform.md) with the stated [Fourier-Hankel normalization for an isotropic spectrum](fourier-hankel-normalization-for-an-isotropic-spectrum.md). The result recovers the scalar tensor coefficient $H$, whose shell-integrated [magnetic energy spectrum](magnetic-energy-spectrum.md) has an additional factor $k^2$.
