# Fixed-point iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

Fixed-point iteration seeks a [fixed point](fixed-point.md) of a map $F$ by repeatedly setting $x_{k+1}=F(x_k)$. Convergence requires additional hypotheses; for example, a [contraction mapping](contraction-mapping.md) on a complete metric space has a unique fixed point reached by this iteration.
