# Mantle viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Earth mantle](earth-mantle.md)

An effective relation between deviatoric [stress](stress.md) and deformation rate in mantle rocks. [temperature](temperature-split.md), [stress](stress.md), grain size, water and time scale can affect it; a constant Newtonian [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) is a model parameter, not a universal material number.
