# Ellipse perimeter expansion near a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipse](ellipse.md)

For an [ellipse](ellipse.md) with semiaxes $1,x$, the perimeter integral has integrand $\sqrt{1-t\cos^2\theta}$. The uniformly convergent [binomial series](binomial-series.md) near $t=0$ permits termwise integration. Integrals of $\cos^2\theta$ and $\cos^4\theta$ over a full period give the displayed first corrections. The same coefficients follow from the [Frobenius method](frobenius-method.md) for the transformed perimeter equation.
