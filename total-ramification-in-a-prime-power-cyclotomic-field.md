# Total ramification in a prime-power cyclotomic field

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

The prime $\ell$ is totally ramified in $\mathbb Q(\zeta_{\ell^r})$, whose degree is $\varphi(\ell^r)$. The displayed equality is an equality of ideals in its [ring of integers of a number field](ring-of-integers.md), and the residue field is $\mathbb F_\ell$.
