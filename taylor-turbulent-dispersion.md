# Taylor turbulent dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent mixing](turbulent-mixing.md)

Taylor turbulent dispersion describes the absolute displacement of a marked [fluid element](fluid-element.md) in stationary [homogeneous turbulence](homogeneous-turbulence.md). Here $R_L$ is its Lagrangian velocity [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md). Continuous correlation at zero gives ballistic spreading at short times, and an integrable correlation gives diffusive spreading at long times. This is distinct from shear-induced [Taylor dispersion](taylor-dispersion.md) and from [Richardson pair dispersion](richardson-pair-dispersion.md).
