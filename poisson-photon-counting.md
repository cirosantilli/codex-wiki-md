# Poisson photon counting

↑ **Parent:** [Shot noise](shot-noise.md)

For independent [photon](photon.md) detections with a fixed mean rate, counts in a fixed exposure have a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md), so the [variance](variance-split.md) equals the mean. Multiplying by a representative [photon energy](photon-energy.md) gives $\sigma_E=h\nu\sqrt N$. A source-only signal-to-noise threshold $q$ therefore needs an expected $N=q^2$ [photons](photon.md). Background, detector noise, variable illumination and [photon](photon.md) correlations can invalidate that source-only formula.
