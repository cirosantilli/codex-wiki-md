<h1 id="elliptic-element-of-psl2-r">Elliptic element of PSL2(R)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [PSL2(R)](psl2-r.md)

A real [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) represented by $A\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ with $|\operatorname{tr}A|<2$. It fixes a point of the [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md); an integral matrix of this type has [trace](matrix-trace.md) $0$ or $\pm1$. In the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md), moving its fixed point to zero makes it a rotation about zero.
