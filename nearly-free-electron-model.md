# Nearly-free electron model

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch's theorem](bloch-s-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nearly-free_electron_model)

The nearly-free electron model treats a weak [periodic potential](periodic-potential.md) by [degenerate perturbation theory](degenerate-perturbation-theory.md). At a Bragg crossing, a Fourier coefficient $V_n$ couples the two degenerate plane waves and opens an energy gap of width $2|V_n|$.
