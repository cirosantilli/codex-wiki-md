# Oriented classification of lens spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Lens space](lens-space.md)

With a consistent surgery orientation convention, $L(p,q)$ and $L(p,q')$ are orientation-preservingly related by a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) exactly when $q'\equiv q^{\pm1}\pmod p$. An orientation-reversing [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) instead requires $q'\equiv-q^{\pm1}\pmod p$.
