# Galois cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_cohomology)

Galois cohomology is the [group cohomology](group-cohomology.md) of a Galois group acting on a module. For a discrete $G$-module $M$, the first group is the quotient of crossed homomorphisms $c(\sigma\tau)=c(\sigma)+\sigma c(\tau)$ by maps $c(\sigma)=\sigma m-m$.
