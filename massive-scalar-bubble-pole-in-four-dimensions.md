# Massive scalar bubble pole in four dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean massive loop integral](euclidean-massive-loop-integral.md)

For positive $m^2$, a [Feynman parameter](feynman-parameter.md) and a momentum shift turn the integral into $\int_0^1I_{d,2}(\sqrt{m^2+x(1-x)p^2})\,dx$. The [Euclidean massive loop integral](euclidean-massive-loop-integral.md) has factor $\Gamma(\epsilon/2)=2/\epsilon+O(1)$, while its other factors tend to $(4\pi)^{-2}$. The pole is independent of the external [momentum](momentum.md) and the [mass](mass.md), so it is removable by a local [counterterm](counterterm.md).
