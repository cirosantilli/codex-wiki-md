<h1 id="plucker-coordinates">Plücker coordinates</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Plücker embedding](plucker-embedding.md)

A line in real [projective space](projective-space-split.md) $\mathbb P^3$ is the span of two independent homogeneous vectors $A,B$. Its Plücker coordinates are the entries of the displayed rank-two [antisymmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md), determined up to a nonzero scalar. They satisfy $L^{01}L^{23}-L^{02}L^{13}+L^{03}L^{12}=0$. With a chosen volume orientation, its dual plane form is $L_{ij}=\tfrac12\varepsilon_{ijkl}L^{kl}$. This is projective duality using the volume form, not lowering indices with a Euclidean metric.
