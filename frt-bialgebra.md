# FRT bialgebra

↑ **Parent:** [RTT construction](rtt-construction.md)

Given an invertible [braiding](braiding.md) $C$ on $V\otimes V$, the universal [bialgebra](bialgebra.md) coacting on $V$ for which $C$ is a [comodule](comodule.md) map is generated by the matrix coefficients $t_{ij}$. Its [comultiplication](comultiplication.md) is $\Delta t_{ij}=\sum_k t_{ik}\otimes t_{kj}$. Colinearity gives quadratic relations. Writing $R=\tau C$ turns the relation into the familiar [RTT construction](rtt-construction.md) with the appropriate ordered tensor factors. A [Yang-Baxter R-matrix](yang-baxter-r-matrix.md) supplies a scalar [coquasitriangular structure](coquasitriangular-structure.md).
