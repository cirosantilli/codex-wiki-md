# Strong-shock accretion temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Strong-shock Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](strong-shock-rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md)

A strong, nonradiative [shock](shock-wave.md) in a monatomic ideal gas gives $p_{\mathrm d}=3\rho_{\mathrm u}v_{\mathrm u}^2/4$ and $\rho_{\mathrm d}=4\rho_{\mathrm u}$. The [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md) therefore gives the [temperature](temperature-split.md) in the title, with $\mu$ the mean particle mass. If the upstream speed is a Newtonian free-fall speed, $k_{\mathrm B}T_{\mathrm d}=3\mu GM/(8R)$. Mass alone does not determine this [temperature](temperature-split.md) without a radius and composition; electron-ion disequilibrium and radiation can also make the emergent spectrum differ from the ideal gas [temperature](temperature-split.md).
