<h1 id="stuart-s-tau-c">Stuart's tau-c</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal categorical variable](ordinal-categorical-variable.md)

For an $r\times c$ [contingency table](contingency-table.md) of two [ordinal categorical variables](ordinal-categorical-variable.md), write $m=\min(r,c)>1$, $n$ for the [sample size](sample-size.md) and $C,D$ for [concordant pairs](concordant-pair.md) and [discordant pairs](discordant-pair.md). The coefficient is

$$
\tau_c=\frac{2m(C-D)}{n^2(m-1)}.
$$

It provides an ordinal association measure whose normalization accounts for the table dimensions. It generally differs from [Kendall's tau-b](kendall-s-tau-b.md) and [Goodman and Kruskal's gamma](goodman-and-kruskal-s-gamma.md) on a table with [tied pairs](tied-pair-ordinal-data.md). An association sign presupposes the chosen category ordering and is not a causal direction. The eponym is associated with [Alan Stuart](alan-stuart.md).[StuartTauC, Details: formula, minimum table dimension and ordinal-data requirement](ordinal-association-measures-in-the-desctools-documentation.md)
