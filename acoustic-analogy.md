# Acoustic analogy

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustics](acoustics.md)

An acoustic analogy rearranges fluid conservation equations into a chosen linear acoustic propagation operator plus effective forcing. The rearrangement can be exact before source approximations are made. A term classified as forcing can also contain propagation effects omitted from the reference operator; this produces [propagation-source ambiguity in an acoustic analogy](propagation-source-ambiguity-in-an-acoustic-analogy.md). The [Lighthill acoustic analogy](lighthill-acoustic-analogy.md) is a familiar homogeneous reference-medium example.
