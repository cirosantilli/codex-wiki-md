<h1 id="tensorization-of-a-poincare-inequality">Tensorization of a Poincaré inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré inequality in probability theory](poincare-inequality-in-probability-theory.md)

If each probability measure $\mu_i$ has Poincaré constant $C_i$, then the product measure $\bigotimes_i\mu_i$ has Poincaré constant at most $\max_iC_i$. This follows by iterating the [law of total variance](law-of-total-variance.md) and applying [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) to derivatives of conditional expectations.
