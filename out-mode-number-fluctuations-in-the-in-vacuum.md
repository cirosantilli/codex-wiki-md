# Out-mode number fluctuations in the in-vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Particle number from Bogoliubov coefficients](particle-number-from-bogoliubov-coefficients.md)

If $b_s=\sum_j(A_{sj}a_j+B_{sj}a_j^\dagger)$ and the state is the [in-vacuum](in-vacuum.md), put $n_s=\sum_j|B_{sj}|^2$ and $c_s=\sum_j A_{sj}B_{sj}$. The [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) give $N_s^2=b_s^{\dagger2}b_s^2+N_s$. Acting with $b_s^2$ on the vacuum produces $c_s|0\rangle$ and a two-particle vector whose squared norm is $2n_s^2$, because its two possible vacuum contractions have equal contributions. This proves the displayed second moment and $\operatorname{Var}(N_s)=n_s(n_s+1)+|c_s|^2$. A thermal-mode formula requires $c_s=0$; a general [squeezed vacuum state](squeezed-vacuum-state.md) need not satisfy that restriction.
