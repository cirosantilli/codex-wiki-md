# Alexander breadth bound on Seifert genus

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander polynomial](alexander-polynomial.md)

A minimal-[genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md) has a $2g_s(K)$-by-$2g_s(K)$ [Seifert matrix](seifert-matrix.md) $V$. Each entry of $tV-V^T$ has degree at most one, so its [determinant](determinant.md) has [breadth of a Laurent polynomial](breadth-of-a-laurent-polynomial.md) at most $2g_s(K)$. This lower bound on [Seifert genus](seifert-genus.md) need not be sharp: an untwisted [Whitehead double](whitehead-double.md) can have [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md) $1$ and positive [Seifert genus](seifert-genus.md).
