# Weierstrass elliptic differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

The [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) satisfies

$$
\wp'(z)^2=4\wp(z)^3-g_2\wp(z)-g_3.
$$

Writing $G_{2r}=\sum_{\omega\in\Lambda\setminus\{0\}}\omega^{-2r}$, the [Laurent coefficients of the Weierstrass elliptic function](laurent-coefficients-of-the-weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) give

$$
\wp(z)=z^{-2}+3G_4z^2+5G_6z^4+O(z^6),
\qquad
\wp'(z)=-2z^{-3}+6G_4z+20G_6z^3+O(z^5).
$$

Thus $g_2=60G_4$ and $g_3=140G_6$ cancel every nonremovable term in the [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) of $\wp'^2-4\wp^3+g_2\wp+g_3$ at each point of the [period lattice](period-lattice.md). The resulting [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md) is an [entire function](entire-function.md) and is bounded, hence is zero by [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md).
