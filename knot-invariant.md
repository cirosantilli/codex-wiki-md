# Knot invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Knot theory](knot-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_invariant)

A knot invariant assigns the same value to isotopic [knots](knot.md). Examples include the [knot group](knot-group.md), [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md), [Seifert genus](seifert-genus.md), [knot signature](signature-of-a-knot.md) and [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md). Different [knot invariants](knot-invariant.md) can retain different information: isomorphic [knot groups](knot-group.md) do not alone imply isotopic [knots](knot.md).
