# Currying

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential object](exponential-object.md)

Currying transforms a morphism of two variables into a morphism taking values in an [exponential object](exponential-object.md). The [evaluation map of an exponential object](evaluation-map-of-an-exponential-object.md) recovers the original morphism. Its categorical universal property requires naturality and uniqueness, not just an underlying set bijection.
