# Conservation of quantum probability

↑ **Parent:** [Probability continuity equation](probability-continuity-equation.md)

If the [probability current](probability-current.md) of a [normalizable wavefunction](normalizable-wavefunction.md) vanishes at spatial infinity, integrating the [probability continuity equation](probability-continuity-equation.md) over all space shows that the total probability $\int |\psi|^2$ is constant in time.
