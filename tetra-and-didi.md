# Tetra and Didi

↑ **Parent:** [Isospectral manifolds](isospectral-manifolds.md)

A pair of closed orientable flat three-manifolds with equal scalar [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) spectra but different first [Betti numbers](betti-number.md), respectively one and zero. Consequently they are not [homeomorphic](local-homeomorphism.md). They cannot be isospectral for the [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md) on one-forms, whose zero-eigenvalue multiplicity is the first [Betti number](betti-number.md). This shows why the spectrum on functions must not be silently replaced by the spectra on all differential forms.
