# Periodic common refinement of floor-quotient partitions

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection of equivalence relations](intersection-of-equivalence-relations.md)

For positive [integers](integer-split.md) $a,b$, partition the [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md) by equality of $\lfloor n/a\rfloor$ and separately by equality of $\lfloor n/b\rfloor$. The [intersection of equivalence relations](intersection-of-equivalence-relations.md) gives nonempty intersections of these consecutive blocks. Its boundaries occur at multiples of either $a$ or $b$.

Let $\ell=\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$. Translating by $\ell$ increases the two floor quotients by the fixed [integers](integer-split.md) $\ell/a$ and $\ell/b$, so it preserves all within-block equalities and all block boundaries. Thus the refinement repeats in intervals of length $\ell$. If $0=c_0<c_1<\cdots<c_r=\ell$ lists the multiples of $a$ or $b$ within one period, its blocks are exactly $\{j\ell+c_i,\ldots,j\ell+c_{i+1}-1\}$ for $j\ge0$ and $0\le i<r$. The pattern repeats, but blocks in different periods remain distinct [equivalence classes](equivalence-class.md).
