# Split equalizers associated with a monad

↑ **Parent:** [Split equalizer](split-equalizer.md)

For a [monad](monad.md) $(T,\eta,\mu)$, the map $\eta_{TA}:TA\to TTA$ is a [split equalizer](split-equalizer.md) of $\eta_{TTA},T\eta_{TA}$ with retraction $\mu_A$ and splitting $T\mu_A$. The map $T\eta_A:TA\to TTA$ is a split equalizer of $TT\eta_A,T\eta_{TA}$ with retraction $\mu_A$ and splitting $\mu_{TA}$. Each assertion follows from the two unit laws and [naturality](naturality.md) of the unit or multiplication. No assumption that $T$ preserves arbitrary equalizers is needed.
