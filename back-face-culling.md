# Back-face culling

↑ **Parent:** [Computer graphics](computer-graphics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back-face_culling)

Discarding surfaces viewed from their back side under a chosen vertex-winding and outward-[normal vector](normal-vector.md) convention. For an outward [normal vector](normal-vector.md) $n$ and an approaching [ray](ray-geometry.md) direction $r$, a front-facing hit has $n\cdot r<0$.
