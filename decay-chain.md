# Decay chain

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decay_chain)

A succession of [radioactive decays](radioactive-decay.md) in which a daughter can itself be unstable. For a closed unit-branching chain $a\to b\to c$ with stable $c$, the population [rate equations](rate-equation.md) are $\dot N_a=-\lambda_aN_a$, $\dot N_b=\lambda_aN_a-\lambda_bN_b$ and $\dot N_c=\lambda_bN_b$. They conserve $N_a+N_b+N_c$. [Sequential radioactive decay](sequential-radioactive-decay.md) gives the two-member transient solution; coincident [nuclear decay constants](nuclear-decay-constant.md) require the continuous limit containing $te^{-\lambda t}$.
