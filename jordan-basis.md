# Jordan basis

↑ **Parent:** [Jordan normal form](jordan-normal-form.md)

A Jordan basis is a [basis](basis.md) consisting of [Jordan chains](jordan-chain.md) of [generalized eigenvectors](generalized-eigenvector.md) for a [linear operator](linear-operator.md). Its [matrix](matrix.md) is in [Jordan normal form](jordan-normal-form.md). Over the complex numbers every finite-dimensional [linear operator](linear-operator.md) has such a [basis](basis.md); a real matrix with nonreal [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) requires complexification for this form.
