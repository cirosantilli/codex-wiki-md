# Affine minorant

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

An affine minorant of $f$ is a function $x\mapsto\langle a,x\rangle+b$ that is everywhere at most $f(x)$. Every proper lower-semicontinuous [convex function](convex-function.md) has such a minorant, by separating a point below its closed [epigraph](epigraph.md). Adding a positive quadratic then yields a [coercive function](coercive-function.md), which establishes existence in proximal minimization.
