# Virial pressure from pair correlations

↑ **Parent:** [Radial distribution function](radial-distribution-function.md)

For a differentiable central [pair potential](pair-potential.md) in a classical homogeneous three-dimensional fluid, the mechanical [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) gives the displayed pressure. The [radial distribution function](radial-distribution-function.md) accounts for the probability of each pair separation. In the dilute limit, $g(r)=e^{-v(r)/(k_BT)}+O(n)$, and integration by parts yields $B_2(T)=-2\pi\int_0^\infty[e^{-v(r)/(k_BT)}-1]r^2dr$. Thus the [second virial coefficient](second-virial-coefficient.md) controls $p/(nk_BT)=1+B_2n+\cdots$. A few virial terms are a low-density approximation, not generally a converged description of a dense liquid. A discontinuous hard core needs its contact form or a limiting argument.
