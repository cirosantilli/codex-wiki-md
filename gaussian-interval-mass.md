# Gaussian interval mass

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

The normalized Gaussian mass of an interval is

$$
I_\alpha(\theta,b,w)=\frac1{\sqrt{4\pi\alpha w}}\int_{-b}^{b}e^{-(\varphi-\theta)^2/(4\alpha w)}d\varphi,\qquad\alpha,w>0.
$$

It is the convolution of an interval indicator with the [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md), and has an [error function](error-function.md) representation. As the variance tends to zero it converges to the interval indicator away from its endpoints, with value one half at an endpoint. This is a concrete [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md).
