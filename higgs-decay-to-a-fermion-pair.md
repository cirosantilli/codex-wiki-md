# Higgs decay to a fermion pair

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md)

A scalar [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md) $-m_fH\bar f f/v$ gives

$$
\Gamma=\frac{N_cm_f^2m_H}{8\pi v^2}\left(1-\frac{4m_f^2}{m_H^2}\right)^{3/2}
$$

above threshold. The [fermion spin sum](fermion-spin-sum.md) provides the additional factor of momentum squared that turns the phase-space threshold factor into its third power. The [color multiplicity in a decay width](color-multiplicity-in-a-decay-width.md) is $N_c=3$ for a quark pair and one for a lepton pair.
