# Binary cyclotomic coset modulo an odd integer

↑ **Parent:** [BCH code](bch-code.md)

For odd $n$, multiplication by two permutes the residues modulo $n$. Its orbit through $i$ is the [binary cyclotomic coset modulo an odd integer](binary-cyclotomic-coset-modulo-an-odd-integer.md). If $\alpha$ has order $n$ in a [finite field](finite-field.md) whose [field characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) is two, the [Frobenius automorphism](frobenius-automorphism.md) sends $\alpha^i$ to $\alpha^{2i}$. Hence the [minimal polynomial of an algebraic element](minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) $\alpha^i$ over $\mathbb F_2$ is $\prod_{j\in C_i}(X-\alpha^j)$. Its coefficients are fixed by Frobenius and lie in $\mathbb F_2$, and its degree is the orbit size. Thus a binary [cyclic code](cyclic-code.md) containing one [defining zero of a cyclic code](zero-of-a-cyclic-code.md) must contain the full orbit as zeros.
