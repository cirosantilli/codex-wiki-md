# Qubit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state](quantum-state.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qubit)

A qubit is a two-dimensional [quantum system](quantum-system.md). Its pure state is a ray in a two-dimensional complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) and may be written $\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle$ with $|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1$.
