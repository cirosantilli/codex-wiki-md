# Differentiation operator

↑ **Parent:** [Operator theory](linear-operator-theory-split.md)

The differentiation operator sends a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) to its [derivative](derivative.md); its domain and the [norms](norm.md) must be specified. On $C^1([-1,1])$ with values in $C([-1,1])$, using the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) on both, it is an unbounded [linear operator](linear-operator.md): $f_n(x)=\sin(nx)$ satisfies $\|f_n\|_\infty\leq1$ and $\|Df_n\|_\infty=n$. Its [graph norm](graph-norm.md) is $\|f\|_\infty+\|f'\|_\infty$, which is [complete](completeness.md). Indeed, the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) consists of constants and is [complete](completeness.md), while $Sg(x)=\int_0^xg(t)\,dt$ is a bounded [right inverse](right-inverse.md) by the [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md); apply [completeness of a graph norm from a bounded right inverse](completeness-of-a-graph-norm-from-a-bounded-right-inverse.md).
