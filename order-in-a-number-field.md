# Order in a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md)

An order in a [number field](number-field.md) $K$ is a subring $A\subseteq\mathcal O_K$ containing $1$ and having full rank $[K:\mathbb Q]$ as an additive [abelian group](abelian-group.md). Its finite index in the [ring of integers of a number field](ring-of-integers.md) is related to its [discriminant](discriminant.md) by the [discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice](discriminant-index-formula-for-an-integral-lattice.md).
