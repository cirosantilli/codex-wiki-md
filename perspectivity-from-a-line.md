# Perspectivity from a line

↑ **Parent:** [Desargues's theorem](desargues-s-theorem.md)

Two [projective triangles](projective-triangle.md) are in perspectivity from a line when the three intersections of corresponding sides lie on one [projective line](projective-line.md), called their axis of perspectivity. In the nondegenerate setting this is dual to [perspectivity from a point](perspectivity-from-a-point.md) under [point-line duality](point-line-duality.md).
