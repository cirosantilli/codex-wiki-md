# Leading high-temperature density kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix-split.md)

Neglecting [commutators](commutator.md) between kinetic and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) permits a leading product approximation to the imaginary-time evolution operator. The kinetic factor is a Gaussian Fourier [integral](integral-split.md); the potential factor multiplies it in position space. The unsymmetrized product places the potential at one endpoint and need not be exactly [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md) at the discarded order. Splitting the potential equally between both endpoints gives a [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md) approximation with exponent $-\beta[V(x)+V(x')]/2$ and the same leading diagonal normalization.
