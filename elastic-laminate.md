# Elastic laminate

↑ **Parent:** [Composite material](composite-material.md)

An [elastic laminate](elastic-laminate.md) has planar perfectly bonded layers with a common normal. Its piecewise constant [strains](strain.md) differ by $\operatorname{sym}(a\otimes n)$, while its normal [traction](traction.md) is common to every layer. For positive [elastic stiffness tensors](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md), these conditions and the mean [strain](strain.md) uniquely determine the layer [strains](strain.md): the difference of two solutions has zero mean and zero total elastic work, and positivity forces it to vanish.
