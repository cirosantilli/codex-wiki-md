# Spacetime metric

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md)

A spacetime metric is the [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md) on a [spacetime](spacetime.md). It determines [proper time](proper-time.md) along timelike paths and the null directions that describe local light propagation.
