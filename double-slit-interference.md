# Double-slit interference

↑ **Parent:** [Double-slit experiment](double-slit-experiment.md)

[Double-slit interference](double-slit-interference.md) depends on the relative [wave phase](phase-waves.md) and [first-order optical coherence](first-order-optical-coherence.md) of two collected fields. For equal separately measured intensities, the [interferometric visibility](interferometric-visibility.md) is $|\gamma_{12}|$. A small source direction change $\vartheta_x$ introduces [phase difference](phase-difference.md) $2\pi D\vartheta_x/\lambda$ for projected [interferometric baseline](interferometric-baseline.md) $D$.
