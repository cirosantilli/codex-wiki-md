# Mean squared displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md)

A mean squared displacement is the [expected value](expected-value-split.md) of a squared position change over a specified time lag. For isotropic free [diffusion](diffusion.md) in $d$ dimensions, it equals $2dD\tau$. A nonzero mean [drift](drift-coefficient.md) contributes its squared displacement as well as the displacement [variance](variance-split.md); specifying the tracked coordinates and whether the mean is subtracted is essential.
