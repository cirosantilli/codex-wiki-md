# Shift domination criterion for a Banach limit

↑ **Parent:** [Banach limit](banach-limit.md)

Suppose $q$ is a [sublinear functional](sublinear-function.md) on the bounded real sequences, dominated by the [limit superior](limit-superior.md) and nonpositive on every shift difference $x-Sx$. The [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) then yields a [Banach limit](banach-limit.md). Indeed $0\leq q(x)+q(-x)$ implies $q(x)\geq-q(-x)\geq\liminf x_n$, so $q$ agrees with the ordinary limit on convergent sequences. Extend this linear limit functional by Hahn-Banach to a linear $\psi\leq q$. Domination on $x-Sx$ and on its negative forces $\psi(x)=\psi(Sx)$. The two-sided limit bounds give positivity, [norm](norm.md) one and agreement with ordinary limits. In finite-shift averaging constructions, the needed bound on shift differences follows by telescoping consecutive averages to $(x_{n+1}-x_{n+k+1})/k$.
