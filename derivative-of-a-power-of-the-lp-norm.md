# Derivative of a power of the Lp norm

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

For $1<p<\infty$ and $u,v$ in an [Lp space](lp-space.md), the derivative with respect to the real scalar $t$ has the displayed form; the integrand is zero where $u=0$. The pointwise [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives the derivative, while the [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) makes $(|u|+|v|)^{p-1}|v|$ integrable and permits the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md). This variational derivative characterizes nearest points to [closed](closed-set.md) [convex sets](convex-set.md) and constructs the [Lp duality](lp-duality-on-an-arbitrary-measure-space.md) density of a [bounded linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md).
