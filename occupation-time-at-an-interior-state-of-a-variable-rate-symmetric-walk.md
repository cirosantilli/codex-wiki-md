# Occupation time at an interior state of a variable-rate symmetric walk

↑ **Parent:** [Occupation time of a continuous-time Markov chain](occupation-time-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

For a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) with absorbing endpoints $0,N$ and rates $q_{j,j\pm1}=\lambda_j/2$, the jump chain is a [simple symmetric random walk](simple-symmetric-random-walk.md). On leaving $i$, absorption before returning has probability $p_i=\tfrac12(1/i+1/(N-i))$, by [gambler's ruin](gambler-s-ruin.md) on each side. The [geometric return count before absorption](geometric-return-count-before-absorption.md) and independent rate-$\lambda_i$ [holding times](holding-time.md) give $\mathbb E_iI=1/(p_i\lambda_i)$. The rates at other states affect elapsed excursion times but not their return probabilities.
