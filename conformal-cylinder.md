# Conformal cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal modulus of an annulus](conformal-modulus-of-an-annulus.md)

The quotient of a horizontal strip by a horizontal translation. The model $(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)\times(0,M)$ has [conformal modulus of an annulus](conformal-modulus-of-an-annulus.md) $M$. Averaging horizontal loop lengths and applying [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) proves that the core-loop [extremal length](extremal-length.md) equals $1/M$.
