# Global regularity for one-dimensional wave maps

↑ **Parent:** [Wave map](wave-map.md)

For smooth localized compatible [wave map Cauchy data](wave-map-cauchy-data.md) and compact Riemannian target, one-dimensional [wave maps](wave-map.md) stay smooth globally. In null coordinates, the equation is $D_v\phi_u=D_u\phi_v=0$. Compatibility of the target metric with the [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) makes $|\phi_u|^2$ independent of $v$ and $|\phi_v|^2$ independent of $u$. These transported derivative bounds and higher [energy estimates](energy-estimate.md) prevent finite-time breakdown.
