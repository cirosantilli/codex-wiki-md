# Torsion subgroup killed by an integer

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion subgroup](torsion-subgroup.md)

For an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $G$ and an integer $m\geq1$, its $m$-torsion subgroup is $G[m]=\{x\in G:mx=0\}$, the kernel of multiplication by $m$. In a [finite abelian group](finite-abelian-group.md), every nonempty fiber of this homomorphism has [cardinality](cardinality.md) $|G[m]|$, so $|G:mG|=|G[m]|$. This follows directly: if $mx=my$, then $x-y\in G[m]$, and conversely translating by an element of $G[m]$ leaves the image unchanged. On the [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z_N$, the solutions of $mx=0$ number $\gcd(m,N)$; divide both integers by their greatest common divisor to see that $x$ must be a multiple of $N/\gcd(m,N)$.
