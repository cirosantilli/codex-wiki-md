# Interference fringe

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)

An [interference fringe](interference-fringe.md) is a spatial band of enhanced or reduced [wave intensity](intensity-physics.md) caused by a spatially varying [phase difference](phase-difference.md). Uniform crossing [plane waves](plane-wave.md) produce straight parallel [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md); curved [wavefronts](wavefront.md) can give curved bands. The [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md) mark the relative [wave phase](phase-waves.md), rather than the individual instantaneous [wavefronts](wavefront.md).
