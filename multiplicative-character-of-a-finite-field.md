# Multiplicative character of a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Character group of a finite abelian group](character-group-of-a-finite-abelian-group.md)

A multiplicative [character](character-of-a-representation.md) is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from a [finite field](finite-field.md)'s [multiplicative group of a finite field](multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field.md) to the complex unit circle. A generator of the cyclic [multiplicative group of a finite field](multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field.md) produces a [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of every order dividing $q-1$. Extend every such [character](character-of-a-representation.md) to zero by value zero, including the trivial multiplicative [character](character-of-a-representation.md), when using cyclotomic indicator formulas.
