# Units of the fifth cyclotomic field are real up to roots of unity

↑ **Parent:** [Unit group of the fifth cyclotomic field](unit-group-of-the-fifth-cyclotomic-field.md)

Let $L=\mathbb Q(\zeta_5)$ and let complex conjugation be denoted by a bar. For $u\in\mathcal O_L^\times$, every conjugate of $u/\bar u$ has modulus one, so the [kernel of the logarithmic unit embedding](kernel-of-the-logarithmic-unit-embedding.md) shows that $u/\bar u$ is a root of unity. Reduction modulo $1-\zeta_5$ gives $u/\bar u\equiv1$, so this root belongs to $\mu_5$. Squaring permutes $\mu_5$, hence multiplication by a suitable root of unity makes $u$ real. The real unit belongs to $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$ and the [units of the quadratic field Q square root of five](units-of-the-quadratic-field-q-square-root-of-five.md) finish the calculation.
