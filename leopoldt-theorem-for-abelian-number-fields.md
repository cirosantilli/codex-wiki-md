# Leopoldt theorem for abelian number fields

↑ **Parent:** [Leopoldt conjecture](leopoldt-conjecture.md)

The [Leopoldt conjecture](leopoldt-conjecture.md) holds for every finite abelian extension of $\mathbb Q$. The proof uses linear independence of $p$-adic logarithms of algebraic numbers. In cyclotomic towers it supplies the ranks of the global-unit terms in the [class-field unit sequence](class-field-unit-sequence.md); it is not needed for the [unramified Iwasawa torsion theorem](unramified-iwasawa-torsion-theorem.md) in arbitrary towers.
