# Form birefringence

↑ **Parent:** [Birefringence](birefringence.md)

[Birefringence](birefringence.md) produced by subwavelength structure rather than solely by intrinsic crystalline anisotropy. For ideal dielectric lamellae, continuity of tangential [electric field](electric-field-split.md) gives $\epsilon_\parallel=f\epsilon_1+(1-f)\epsilon_2$, while continuity of normal [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) gives $\epsilon_\perp^{-1}=f/\epsilon_1+(1-f)/\epsilon_2$. The unequal effective [permittivities](permittivity-split.md) give different principal [refractive indices](refractive-index.md).
