# Material symmetry group

↑ **Parent:** [Material isotropy](material-isotropy.md)

The [symmetry group](symmetry-group.md) of a [hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md) consists of invertible reference transformations $H$ preserving its [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md): $W(FH)=W(F)$ for every admissible [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) $F$. A convention restricting reference transformations to determinant one restricts $H$ to the [special linear group](special-linear-group.md). [Material isotropy](material-isotropy.md) requires all proper [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) in this group. This right action differs from the left action in [material frame indifference](material-frame-indifference.md).
