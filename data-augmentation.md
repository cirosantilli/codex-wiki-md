# Data augmentation

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian statistics](bayesian-statistics.md)

[Data augmentation](data-augmentation.md) enlarges a [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) with [latent variables](latent-variable.md) whose removal by [marginalization](marginalization.md) recovers the original [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md). Convenient [full conditional distributions](full-conditional-distribution.md) then permit a [Gibbs sampler](gibbs-sampler.md) on the enlarged space.
