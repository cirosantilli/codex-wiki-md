# Helly property

↑ **Parent:** [Group action on a tree](group-action-on-a-tree.md)

Finite families of convex subtrees of a tree have the Helly property: if every pair intersects, then their total intersection is nonempty.

In the terminology of a [Helly family](helly-family.md), convex subtrees form a family of Helly order two.
