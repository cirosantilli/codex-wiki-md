# Latent-to-sensible heat ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan number](stefan-number.md)

The latent-to-sensible heat ratio is the reciprocal of the [Stefan number](stefan-number.md) defined by $\mathrm{Ste}=c_p\Delta T/L$. Large $S$ means that phase change uses much more energy than changing temperature by $\Delta T$.
