# Conjugation representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

If $G$ acts on a vector space $W$, it acts on $\operatorname{End}(W)$ by conjugation:

$$
g\cdot X=\rho(g)X\rho(g)^{-1}.
$$

Under $\operatorname{End}(W)\cong W\otimes W^*$, this is the [tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) $W\otimes W^*$.
