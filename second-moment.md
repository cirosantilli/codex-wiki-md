# Second moment

↑ **Parent:** [Moment](moment.md)

The second [moment](moment.md) of a real [random variable](random-variable-split.md) is $\mathbb E[X^2]$. Its [variance](variance-split.md) is $\mathbb E[X^2]-(\mathbb EX)^2$ when these quantities are finite.
