# Bracket connection on a Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Affine connection](affine-connection.md)

Specify the displayed rule on [left-invariant vector fields](left-invariant-vector-field.md) and extend by the [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) to an [affine connection](affine-connection.md) on the [Lie group](lie-group.md). In a [left-invariant vector field](left-invariant-vector-field.md) frame $L_i$, the extension is $\nabla_{L_i}(z^jL_j)=L_i(z^j)L_j+\lambda z^j[L_i,L_j]$. Applying the bare bracket formula to arbitrary vector fields instead would violate the [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) unless $\lambda=1$. With $R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z$, the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) gives

$$
T(X,Y)=(2\lambda-1)[X,Y],\qquad R(X,Y)Z=\lambda(\lambda-1)[[X,Y],Z],\qquad \operatorname{Ric}(X,Y)=\lambda(\lambda-1)B(X,Y),
$$

for left-invariant arguments, where $B$ is the [Killing form](killing-form.md) and $\operatorname{Ric}(X,Y)=\operatorname{tr}(Z\mapsto R(Z,X)Y)$. These identities determine the [tensor fields](tensor-field.md) everywhere. The endpoints $\lambda=0,1$ have zero curvature, with respectively left- and right-invariant [parallel frames](parallel-frame-along-a-curve.md), and opposite [torsion tensors](torsion-tensor.md). The midpoint $\lambda=1/2$ is torsion-free but need not have zero curvature.
