# Pontryagin-Thom collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md)

A compact smooth embedding has a [tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md) identified with its [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md). Collapse the complement of its open disk neighborhood to the basepoint and identify the remaining quotient with the [Thom space](thom-space.md) of that normal bundle. Pulling back its [Thom class](thom-class.md) constructs a Gysin class. For a graph embedding into a product with Euclidean space, desuspending the auxiliary coordinates gives the corresponding wrong-way map.
