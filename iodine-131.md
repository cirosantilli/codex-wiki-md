# Iodine-131

↑ **Parent:** [Radiopharmaceutical](radiopharmaceutical.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iodine-131)

An iodine [radionuclide](radionuclide.md) with approximately eight-day [half-life](half-life.md), emitting [beta particles](beta-particle.md) and [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md). Its uptake and retention depend strongly on the carrier and tissue physiology.
