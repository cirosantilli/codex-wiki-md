# Sine index-form bound for positive Ricci curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian index form](riemannian-index-form.md)

Along a unit-speed [minimizing geodesic](minimizing-geodesic.md) of positive length $L$, take perpendicular parallel orthonormal fields $E_i$ and set $V_i=\sin(\pi t/L)E_i$. The [second variation of geodesic energy](second-variation-of-geodesic-energy.md) makes each index form nonnegative, while $\operatorname{Ric}\geq(n-1)\kappa g$ bounds their sum as displayed. For $n\geq2$ and $\kappa>0$, lengths greater than $\pi/\sqrt\kappa$ are impossible. Completeness is what supplies a [minimizing geodesic](minimizing-geodesic.md) between arbitrary points in the [Bonnet-Myers theorem](myers-s-theorem.md).
