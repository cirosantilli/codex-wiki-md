# Electron pairing energy

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

The [electron pairing energy](electron-pairing-energy.md) represents the energetic penalty for putting [electrons](electron.md) into already occupied orbitals rather than suitable separate orbitals. A simple comparison with [octahedral splitting](octahedral-splitting.md) explains high-[spin](spin.md) and low-[spin](spin.md) alternatives.
