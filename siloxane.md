# Siloxane

↑ **Parent:** [Organosilicon compound](organosilicon-compound.md)

An Si–O–Si linkage, or a compound containing such linkages. Cyclic and polymeric [siloxanes](siloxane.md) have strongly polar bonds and flexible bond angles; their bonding should not be reduced to a dominant oxygen-2p/silicon-3d pi interaction.
