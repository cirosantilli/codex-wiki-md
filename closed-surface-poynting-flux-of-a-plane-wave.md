# Closed-surface Poynting flux of a plane wave

↑ **Parent:** [Poynting theorem](poynting-theorem.md)

For a real linearly polarized monochromatic vacuum [plane wave](plane-wave.md), the local [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) is proportional to $\cos^2(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r-\omega t)\widehat{\mathbf k}$. Its flux through a closed surface generally oscillates at $2\omega$, with zero cycle average, although particular volume geometries can cancel it identically. For a circularly polarized plane wave of constant amplitude the [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) is spatially and temporally constant, so the net flux through every closed surface is exactly zero. A nonzero constant local energy flux must not be confused with the net closed-surface flux.
