# Lefschetz operator on a Hermitian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian manifold](hermitian-manifold.md)

Exterior multiplication by the fundamental $(1,1)$-form of a [Hermitian manifold](hermitian-manifold.md) raises bidegree by $(1,1)$. Its pointwise [formal adjoint](formal-adjoint.md) lowers bidegree by $(1,1)$. On a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) this is the [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md), but its pointwise algebraic properties do not require the form to be closed.
