# Hyperbolas with prescribed asymptotes

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbola](hyperbola.md)

For two distinct intersecting [straight lines](straight-line.md) through the origin, with independent real linear equations $L_1=0$ and $L_2=0$, the nondegenerate centred [hyperbolas](hyperbola.md) having exactly those [asymptotes](asymptote.md) are

$$
L_1(x,y)L_2(x,y)=c,\qquad c\ne0.
$$

Indeed, the homogeneous [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) of a centred [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) vanishes in its two asymptotic directions and hence is a nonzero scalar multiple of $L_1L_2$. Its equation has no linear terms because the origin is its centre, and its constant term is nonzero by nondegeneracy. Conversely, the invertible [linear transformation](linear-map.md) $(x,y)\mapsto(L_1,L_2)$ takes the displayed curve to $uv=c$, a [hyperbola](hyperbola.md), and its ends approach $u=0$ or $v=0$. Distinct values of $c$ give disjoint [level sets](level-set.md). In particular, the [rectangular hyperbolas](rectangular-hyperbola.md) with [asymptotes](asymptote.md) $y=\pm x$ are $x^2-y^2=c$, $c\ne0$.
