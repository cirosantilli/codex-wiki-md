# Receive window

↑ **Parent:** [Sliding window protocol](sliding-window-protocol.md)

A [receive window](receive-window.md) bounds the numbered data a receiver is prepared to accept. In [TCP](transmission-control-protocol.md), the receiver advertises a window that limits the sender independently of the sender's [congestion window](congestion-window.md).
