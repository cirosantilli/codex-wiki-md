# Longitudinal Brownian field with transverse covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

A longitudinal Brownian field $\mathcal B_x(z)$ is a centered [Gaussian random field](gaussian-random-field.md) with

$$
 \mathbb E[\mathcal B_x(z)\mathcal B_{x'}(z')]=\min(x,x')B(z-z'),\qquad x,x'\geq0,
$$

where $B$ is a valid transverse [covariance kernel](covariance-kernel.md). For each fixed $z$ it is a scaled [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), and increments over disjoint longitudinal intervals are independent. Such a field drives a Markov model of [wave propagation in a random medium](wave-propagation-in-a-random-medium.md). Its formal longitudinal derivative is [Gaussian white noise](gaussian-white-noise.md) in $x$, with transverse correlations described by $B$; it is not in general a Brownian sheet.
