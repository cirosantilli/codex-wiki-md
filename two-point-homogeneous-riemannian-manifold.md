# Two-point homogeneous Riemannian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous Riemannian manifold](homogeneous-riemannian-manifold.md)

A connected [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) is two-point homogeneous if its [isometry](isometry.md) group is transitive on ordered pairs at each fixed [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md). The equal-distance condition is necessary because [isometries](isometry.md) preserve distance. Round spheres and Euclidean spaces are examples. The [unit tangent transitivity characterizes two-point homogeneity](unit-tangent-transitivity-characterizes-two-point-homogeneity.md) lemma shows that this pair condition is equivalent to transitivity on the [unit tangent bundle](unit-tangent-bundle.md).
