# Orbital elements from Hill coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Free solution of Hill equations](free-solution-of-hill-equations.md)

The oscillatory integration constants determine the [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) vector, the constant radial offset determines the [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md), and the constant along-track offset fixes the [mean longitude](mean-longitude.md). The radial and tangential epicycle amplitudes have ratio $1:2$.
