# Markov chain variational inference

↑ **Parent:** [Variational inference](variational-inference.md)

[Markov chain variational inference](markov-chain-variational-inference.md) optimizes an initial parametric [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) $\nu_\eta$ by minimizing $\operatorname{KL}(K^t\nu_\eta\Vert\pi)$ after a fixed number of [Markov kernel](markov-kernel.md) updates. The best initial [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) can depend on $t$; [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) contraction does not imply that optimization commutes with applying the [Markov kernel](markov-kernel.md).
