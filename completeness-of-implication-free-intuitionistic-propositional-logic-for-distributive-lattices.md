# Completeness of implication-free intuitionistic propositional logic for distributive lattices

↑ **Parent:** [Implication-free fragment of intuitionistic propositional logic](implication-free-fragment-of-intuitionistic-propositional-logic.md)

An implication-free formula is provable exactly when every valuation into every bounded [distributive lattice](distributive-lattice.md) assigns it the top element.
