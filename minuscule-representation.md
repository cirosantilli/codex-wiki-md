# Minuscule representation

↑ **Parent:** [Highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minuscule_representation)

A finite-dimensional irreducible representation of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) is minuscule if its weights form one [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) orbit. Every [weight multiplicity](weight-multiplicity.md) is then one, because multiplicities are invariant under the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) and the [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) has multiplicity one.
