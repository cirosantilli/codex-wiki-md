# IRP2

↑ **Parent:** [Iron regulatory protein](iron-regulatory-protein.md)

An iron-responsive [RNA-binding protein](rna-binding-protein.md) whose abundance is controlled through regulated degradation, including by [FBXL5](fbxl5.md). It does not use the [aconitase](aconitase.md) switch of [IRP1](irp1.md).
