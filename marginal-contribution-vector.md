# Marginal contribution vector

↑ **Parent:** [Shapley value](shapley-value.md)

For a player ordering $\pi$, $P_i$ is the set before player $i$, and the displayed entries form its [marginal contribution](marginal-contribution.md) vector. Their sum is $v(N)-v(\varnothing)$. The [Shapley value](shapley-value.md) is the average of these vectors. In a [convex cooperative game](convex-cooperative-game.md), increasing [marginal contributions](marginal-contribution.md) make each such vector satisfy every [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) constraint of the [core of a cooperative game](core-game-theory.md).
