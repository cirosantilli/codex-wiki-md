# Illusory conjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Feature integration theory](feature-integration-theory.md)

A reported or measured binding of features into a combination not present in the display, such as assigning one item's [color](color.md) to another item's shape. Such errors support a distinction between feature availability and correct assignment, while their perceptual, memory and response contributions require task-specific controls.
