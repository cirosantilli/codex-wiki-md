# Outer automorphism of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Outer automorphism group](outer-automorphism-group.md)

An outer [group automorphism](group-automorphism.md) is not [conjugation](conjugation.md) by any element of the [group](group-split.md). Its nonidentity class belongs to $\operatorname{Out}(G)=\operatorname{Aut}(G)/\operatorname{Inn}(G)$. A permutation-group example is the exceptional automorphism of $S_6$ that swaps its natural and transitive copies of $S_5$; it sends a [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) to a product of three disjoint [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md) and hence cannot be inner.
