# Small subgroups of tetrahedral symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Tetrahedral symmetry](tetrahedral-symmetry.md)

The full [symmetry group](symmetry-group.md) of a [regular tetrahedron](regular-tetrahedron.md) is the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_4$ acting on its vertices. Its [subgroups](subgroup.md) with at most six elements have eight types up to [conjugation](conjugation.md), with the following representative [subgroups](subgroup.md) and counts:

$$
\begin{array}{c|l|r}
\text{abstract group}&\text{representative}&\text{count}\\\hline
C_1&\{e\}&1\\
C_2&\langle(12)\rangle&6\\
C_2&\langle(12)(34)\rangle&3\\
C_3&\langle(123)\rangle&4\\
C_4&\langle(1234)\rangle&3\\
C_2\times C_2&\{e,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}&1\\
C_2\times C_2&\langle(12),(34)\rangle&3\\
S_3&\{\sigma:\sigma(4)=4\}&4
\end{array}
$$

A [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) is a [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) through the plane containing the two fixed vertices and the midpoint of the exchanged edge. A double [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) is a half-turn about the axis joining the midpoints of the two exchanged edges. A three-cycle is a one-third-turn about a vertex-to-opposite-face axis. A four-cycle is a quarter-turn composed with a [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) perpendicular to the opposite-edge midpoint axis; it is an [improper orthogonal transformation](improper-orthogonal-transformation.md).

The unique [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md) containing only double [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md) consists of the three opposite-edge half-turns and the [identity element](identity-element.md). The other three [Klein four-groups](klein-four-group.md) consist of two perpendicular mirror [reflections](reflection-mathematics.md), their half-turn product, and the [identity element](identity-element.md). Each $S_3$ is a [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of a vertex, including the three [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) and three mirror [reflections](reflection-mathematics.md) preserving that vertex.

For completeness, a [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md) containing $(12)$ must contain the commuting [involution](involution.md) $(34)$ or $(12)(34)$, yielding the displayed [subgroup](subgroup.md); one containing no [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md) must contain all three double [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md). An $S_3$ [subgroup](subgroup.md) has a unique [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md) of [cardinality](cardinality.md) three, whose unique fixed vertex must therefore be preserved by the entire $S_3$. These observations and the [disjoint cycle decomposition](disjoint-cycle-decomposition.md) exhaust the types allowed by [groups of order at most six](groups-of-order-at-most-six.md). The counts sum to $25$.
