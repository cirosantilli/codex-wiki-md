# Complex projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_projective_line)

The complex projective line is the space of complex lines in $\mathbb C^2$. It is diffeomorphic to the two-sphere and is the quotient $S^3/S^1$ in the [Hopf fibration](hopf-fibration.md).
