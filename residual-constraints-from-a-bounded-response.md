# Residual constraints from a bounded response

↑ **Parent:** [Regression residual](regression-residual.md)

If observed responses satisfy $L\le y_i\le U$, their [regression residuals](regression-residual.md) $e_i=y_i-\widehat y_i$ necessarily obey the displayed inequalities. Subtracting the corresponding [fitted values](fitted-values.md) proves both bounds, without a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) or a fitted-model assumption. On a [residual-versus-fitted plot](residual-versus-fitted-plot.md), all points must lie between two parallel lines of slope $-1$. This is also a consistency check when $L,U$ are merely the reported observed minimum and maximum, rather than theoretical response limits. A bounded response does not itself prove that a straight [conditional mean](conditional-expectation.md) is inappropriate.
