# Octahedral molecular geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md)

In [octahedral molecular geometry](octahedral-molecular-geometry.md), six donor directions point toward the vertices of an octahedron. Three mutually cis donors can occupy one face; three coplanar donor directions can occupy a meridian.
