# Coplanar qubit realization of the chained Bell inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Chained modular Bell inequality](chained-modular-bell-inequality.md)

On $|\Phi^+\rangle$, measure $\sigma_z\cos\theta+\sigma_x\sin\theta$, assigning [bit](bit.md) $a$ to eigenvalue $(-1)^a$. Choose Alice's angles $2(i-1)\delta$ and Bob's angles $(2i-1)\delta$, with $\delta=\pi/(2N)$. The [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) correlation is $\cos(\theta-\phi)$. Each adjacent disagreement term and the last agreement term therefore has [probability](probability.md) $\sin^2(\pi/(4N))$. For $N\geq2$, their total violates the [chained modular Bell inequality](chained-modular-bell-inequality.md) bound one, and it tends to zero as $N\to\infty$. At $N=1$ the value is exactly one.
