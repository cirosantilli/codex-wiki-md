# Regular-axis alignment of steady poloidal ideal flow

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic mass loading](magnetohydrodynamic-mass-loading.md)

For steady axisymmetric ideal flow with only poloidal components, induction implies $\mathbf u\times\mathbf B=C\mathbf e_\phi/R$. Axis regularity, or zero toroidal [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md), sets $C=0$. Continuity then gives $\rho\mathbf u=k(\psi)\mathbf B$, locally on connected magnetic surfaces of the [poloidal magnetic flux function](poloidal-magnetic-flux-function.md). Without the zero-circulation condition, annular domains can support steady cross-field flow.
