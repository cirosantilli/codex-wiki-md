# Relative constructible universe

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible universe](constructible-universe.md)

For a [transitive set](transitive-set.md) $X$, the relative constructible universe $L(X)$ is the union of the [relative constructible hierarchy](relative-constructible-hierarchy.md), starting at $L_0(X)=X$, applying the [definable power set](definable-power-set-split.md) at successors and taking unions at [limit ordinals](limit-ordinal.md). It is the smallest [inner model](inner-model.md) of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) containing $X$ and all its members. For an arbitrary [set](set-split.md) $A$, take the base $X$ to be the [transitive closure](transitive-closure.md) of $\{A\}$, so that $A\in X$. [Axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md) need not hold for an arbitrary base; the construction uses initial parameters rather than a predicate relativization.
