# Sign compatibility of LAR and Lasso

↑ **Parent:** [Least angle regression](least-angle-regression.md)

If every nonzero active coefficient has the sign of its residual score along the [least angle regression](least-angle-regression.md) path, the [LAR active correlation invariant](lar-active-correlation-invariant.md) and inactive bounds give the [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions for the Lasso](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions-for-the-lasso.md). A unique [Lasso](lasso.md) solution then equals the LAR path. Zero coefficients use a [subgradient](subgradient.md) interval; literal sign equality with sign(0)=0 is not appropriate for a newly entering coefficient at a positive knot.
