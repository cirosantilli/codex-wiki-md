# Protein glycosylation

↑ **Parent:** [Glycosylation](glycosylation.md)

[protein glycosylation](protein-glycosylation.md) attaches a carbohydrate group to a [protein](protein-split.md) directly or extends a protein-associated glycan. Donor supply, acceptor accessibility, [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) specificity and compartmentation jointly determine the product.
