# Fold caustic for a cubic Charpit equation

↑ **Parent:** [Charpit's equations](charpit-s-equations.md)

For initial data $u(x,0)=1+x^3/4$ on $0\leq x\leq1$, the [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) surface has $x=se^r$, $y=3s^2(1-e^{-2r})/2$, $u=1+s^3(e^{-3r}-3e^{-r}/4)$. Its projection determinant is $3s^2e^r(2e^{-2r}-1)$, so the sheet connected to the initial data loses local invertibility at $r=\log2/2$. The regular sheet has $r<\log2/2$ and projects to $0<x<\sqrt2$, $3(1-x^{-2})/2<y<3x^2/8$. Its upper boundary is a [fold point of a critical manifold](fold-point-of-a-critical-manifold.md) [caustic](wave-caustic.md). Solving the inverse yields $s^2=[x^2+\sqrt{x^4-(8/3)x^2y}]/2$; the other square-root branch lies on the folded sheet and cannot simply be joined as a unique smooth solution. The initial point $s=0$ is already [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md), illustrating the need to inspect endpoints as well as the interior of a Cauchy curve.
