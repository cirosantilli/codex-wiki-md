# Bi-Lipschitz map

↑ **Parent:** [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md)

A bi-Lipschitz map between [metric spaces](metric-space.md) satisfies the displayed bounds for all input pairs, with constants $0<m\leq M<\infty$. The lower bound makes it [injective](injective-function.md); its inverse on its image is [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) with bound $1/m$, while the map itself has [Lipschitz bound](lipschitz-bound.md) $M$. Thus it controls both stretching and compression. It is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) onto its image, preserves [Hausdorff dimension](hausdorff-dimension.md), and has [bi-Lipschitz distortion](bi-lipschitz-distortion.md) at most $M/m$. Uniformly multiplying every distance by the same positive factor has distortion one, as explained in [metric embedding](metric-embedding.md).
