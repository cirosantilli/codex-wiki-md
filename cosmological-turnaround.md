# Cosmological turnaround

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical-collapse model](spherical-collapse-model.md)

An initially expanding overdense region reaches a maximum physical [radius](radius.md) before contracting. In a spherical pressureless model its radial expansion velocity vanishes there. The background universe can continue expanding throughout the local turnaround and subsequent collapse.
