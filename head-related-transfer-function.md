# Head-related transfer function

↑ **Parent:** [Hearing](hearing.md)

A direction-dependent acoustic transfer function from an external source to one ear, including effects of the head, pinna and torso under stated measurement conditions. Individual geometry changes the spectral cues. A pair of such functions predicts binaural signals for a known source; inferring direction from unknown sounds is a harder [inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md).
