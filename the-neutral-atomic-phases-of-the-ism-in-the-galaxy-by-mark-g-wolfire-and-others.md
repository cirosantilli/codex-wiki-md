# The Neutral Atomic Phases of the ISM in the Galaxy by Mark G. Wolfire and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Mark G. Wolfire](work-by-mark-g-wolfire.md)

The primary model computes cold and warm atomic equilibrium branches and the [pressure](pressure-split.md) interval where they coexist. Its heating and cooling depend on the radiation field, [ionization](ionization.md) and gas abundances. This is a thermal-phase model for the Milky Way disk, not a claim that all interstellar phases share one [pressure](pressure-split.md) or a perfectly static configuration.
