# Rank of the fundamental group of a finite connected graph

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental group](fundamental-group.md)

For a finite [connected graph](connected-graph.md), collapse a [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md). Its $v-1$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) disappear, and each of the remaining $e-v+1$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) gives a [circle](circle.md) in a [bouquet of circles](bouquet-of-circles.md). The [fundamental group of a bouquet of circles](fundamental-group-of-a-bouquet-of-circles.md) is [free group](free-group.md), with one member of a [free basis of a group](free-basis-of-a-group.md) for each [circle](circle.md). This gives the formula in terms of the [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) $\chi(X)=v-e$.
