# The Cell by Oscar Hertwig

↑ **Parent:** [Cell biology bibliography](cell-biology-bibliography-split.md)

[https://embryology.med.unsw.edu.au/embryology/index.php?title=Book_-_The_Cell_-_outlines_of_general_anatomy_and_physiology_%281895%29](https://embryology.med.unsw.edu.au/embryology/index.php?title=Book_-_The_Cell_-_outlines_of_general_anatomy_and_physiology_%281895%29)  
[https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/59252](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/59252)

English edition of the primary textbook. Chapters I–II discuss investigation and organization; chapter VI discusses multiplication; chapter VII examines fertilization, including echinoderm eggs; chapter IX discusses the cell as the basis of heredity. The UNSW transcription has substantial OCR errors; the facsimile is authoritative for exact wording.
