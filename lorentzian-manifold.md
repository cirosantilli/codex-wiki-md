# Lorentzian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold](topological-manifold.md)

A smooth [manifold](topological-manifold.md) carrying a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) on each [tangent space](tangent-space.md), with one negative and all remaining positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), or the overall reversed-sign convention. Its nonzero [null vectors](null-vector.md) have vanishing metric [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md).
