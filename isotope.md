# Isotope

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical element](chemical-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isotope)

Isotopes are atoms of the same [chemical element](chemical-element.md), with the same number of [protons](proton.md) but different numbers of [neutrons](neutron.md). Their abundance ratios can distinguish material sources and biological or chemical fractionation, while radioactive isotopes can additionally carry chronological information.
