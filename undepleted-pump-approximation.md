# Undepleted-pump approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md)

Treat the strong input pump amplitudes as prescribed while solving for weaker fields. It is self-consistent only when conversion removes a small fraction of pump [energy](energy-split.md) and the assumed pump phases remain valid. An apparent divergent gain is evidence of breakdown of this approximation, not unlimited [energy](energy-split.md) generation.
