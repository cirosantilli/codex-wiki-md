# Target normalization in Rutherford scattering rates

↑ **Parent:** [Rutherford differential cross-section](rutherford-differential-cross-section.md)

At fixed incident flux, projectile energy and detector geometry, the [Rutherford differential cross-section](rutherford-differential-cross-section.md) gives count rate $S\propto\mathcal N Z^2$, where $\mathcal N$ is the target nuclei per unit area. Equal $\mathcal N$ gives a $Z^2$ rate ratio. Equal foil mass per area instead gives $Z^2/A$, with $A$ the atomic mass number, while equal physical thickness also requires the material [mass density](density.md). An empirical [log-log plot](log-log-plot.md) with slope $s$ supplies a different, experimentally normalized relation $S\propto A^s$. These normalizations must be specified before comparing different target elements.
