# Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Model fermion](standard-model-fermion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabibbo–Kobayashi–Maskawa_matrix)

The Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix is the unitary mismatch between the left-handed transformations that diagonalize the up- and down-quark Yukawa matrices. It appears in charged weak currents.
