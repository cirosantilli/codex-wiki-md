# Strict dual certificate for basis pursuit

↑ **Parent:** [Basis pursuit](basis-pursuit.md)

For a real [vector](vector.md) $x$ with [support of a vector](support-of-a-vector.md) $S$, this certificate is a [vector](vector.md) $h$ whose measurements under the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) match the active [sign function](sign-function.md) values and lie strictly between minus one and one on the inactive coordinates. If $A_S$ is [injective](injective-function.md), such a certificate proves that $x$ is the unique [basis pursuit](basis-pursuit.md) [minimizer](global-minimizer.md). For a nonzero $v\in\ker A$, [injectivity](injective-function.md) ensures $v_{S^c}\ne0$, and $0=\langle A^*h,v\rangle$ implies the [fixed-sign null space condition](fixed-sign-null-space-condition.md). The strict inequality is interpreted coordinatewise if $S^c$ is empty. The condition with the [sign function](sign-function.md) written here is for real variables; complex [basis pursuit](basis-pursuit.md) uses the unit phases of active coordinates instead.
