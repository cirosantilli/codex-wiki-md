# Spontaneous symmetry breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spontaneous_symmetry_breaking)

Spontaneous symmetry breaking occurs when the action and equations have a symmetry but a chosen [ground state](ground-state.md) is invariant only under a proper subgroup. Acting with the broken symmetry generates a degenerate [vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md).
