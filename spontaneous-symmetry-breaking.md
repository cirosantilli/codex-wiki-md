# Spontaneous symmetry breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spontaneous_symmetry_breaking)

[Spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) occurs when the [action](action.md) and equations have a [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) but a chosen [ground state](ground-state.md) is invariant only under a proper [subgroup](subgroup.md). Acting with the broken [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) generates a degenerate [vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md). An [order parameter](order-parameter.md) distinguishes the selected states: the equations do not prefer its direction, yet a particular state does. This lets one use the [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) of the laws to predict excitations around a state that has less [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md), notably the [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md) of a broken [continuous symmetry](continuous-symmetry.md).

A finite system with [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md)-preserving [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) can have a symmetric [ground state](ground-state.md); a symmetric statistical mixture of broken states also has vanishing mean [order parameter](order-parameter.md). A nonzero mean therefore requires a stated selection procedure. The [symmetry-breaking order of limits](symmetry-breaking-order-of-limits.md) takes the [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) in a small applied source before removing that source. The [finite-volume angular source selection](finite-volume-angular-source-selection.md) example makes the distinction explicit for a [continuous symmetry](continuous-symmetry.md).
