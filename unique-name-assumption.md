# Unique name assumption

↑ **Parent:** [Description logic](description-logic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unique_name_assumption)

The assumption that distinct individual names denote distinct domain elements. Standard [ALC](alc.md) semantics does not impose it; [Herbrand interpretations](herbrand-structure.md) do treat distinct ground terms as distinct elements.
