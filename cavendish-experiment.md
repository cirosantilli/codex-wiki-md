# Cavendish experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavendish_experiment)

The Cavendish experiment measures the weak [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md) between laboratory masses with a torsion balance, and can be used to determine the [gravitational constant](gravitational-constant.md).
