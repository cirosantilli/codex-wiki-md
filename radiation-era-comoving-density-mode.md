# Radiation-era comoving density mode

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-equation-of-state comoving density equation](constant-equation-of-state-comoving-density-equation.md)

For [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md), the [comoving-gauge density contrast](comoving-gauge-density-contrast.md) satisfies $\Delta_k''+(k^2/3-2/\eta^2)\Delta_k=0$. The displayed basis solves it. The first mode is regular and grows as $Ax^2/3$ outside the [sound horizon](sound-horizon.md); the second diverges as $B/x$ toward the initial singularity. At large $x$, the regular mode approaches $-A\cos x$ and has constant-amplitude acoustic oscillations.
