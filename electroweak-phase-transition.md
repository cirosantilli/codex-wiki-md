# Electroweak phase transition

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md)

A thermal change in the electroweak Higgs sector as the early Universe cools. Its order and strength depend on the particle content, couplings and effective description. A [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) [landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) is a simplified model and does not, by itself, establish a model-independent Higgs-mass bound.
