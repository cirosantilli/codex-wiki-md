# Kripke completeness of implicational intuitionistic logic

↑ **Parent:** [Implicational fragment of intuitionistic propositional logic](implicational-fragment-of-intuitionistic-propositional-logic.md)

For implicational formulas $\Gamma$ and $\varphi$,

$$
\Gamma\vdash_{\mathrm{IPC}(\to)}\varphi
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Gamma\models_{\mathrm{Kripke}}\varphi.
$$

The reverse implication follows from the [canonical Kripke model for implicational intuitionistic logic](canonical-kripke-model-for-implicational-intuitionistic-logic.md) and its truth lemma.
