# Helium metastability

↑ **Parent:** [Helium atom](helium-atom.md)

The low $1s2s$ levels of a [helium atom](helium-atom.md) lack an allowed direct electric dipole decay to the ground $1s^2\,{}^1S_0$ level. The triplet is excluded by the [spin selection rule](spin-selection-rule.md), and the singlet-to-singlet $S$ transition is excluded by [parity](parity.md) and orbital selection. Weaker radiative channels and collisions can still de-excite these states; metastable does not mean infinitely lived.
