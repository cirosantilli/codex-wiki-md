# Lucas closed form

↑ **Parent:** [Lucas number](lucas-number.md)

For $\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$ and $\psi=(1-\sqrt5)/2$, each power sequence satisfies the [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md) $x_{n+2}=x_{n+1}+x_n$, because both bases solve $t^2=t+1$. Their sum starts at $2,1$, so uniqueness of a recursively defined sequence proves that it is the [Lucas number](lucas-number.md) sequence. Alternatively, diagonalize the [companion matrix](companion-matrix.md) $\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ and use $(1,2)^T=(\varphi,1)^T+(\psi,1)^T$. The formula explains the exponential growth governed by the [golden ratio](golden-ratio.md) and the alternating decaying correction $\psi^n$.
