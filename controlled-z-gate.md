# Controlled-Z gate

↑ **Parent:** [Controlled phase gate](controlled-phase-gate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Controlled-Z_gate)

The controlled-Z gate is $CP(\pi)=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,-1)$. Conjugating the target of a [controlled-NOT gate](controlled-not-gate.md) by [Hadamard gates](hadamard-gate.md) turns controlled-NOT into controlled-Z.
