# Acoustic startle reflex

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscience](neuroscience-split.md)

A rapid response to an abrupt sound. Its amplitude can be modulated by threat, [attention](attention.md), motor capacity and drugs; baseline startle and potentiation should be examined separately.
