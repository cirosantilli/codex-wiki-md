# Isovolumetric contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Cardiac cycle](cardiac-cycle.md)

During this stage of the [cardiac cycle](cardiac-cycle.md), the ventricular inlet and outlet valves are both closed. Ventricular volume is fixed at its end-filling value $V^+$, while increasing [ventricular elastance](ventricular-elastance.md) raises [pressure](pressure-split.md) as $p_{LV}=E_{LV}(t)(V^+-V_0)$. Arterial and venous pressures remain approximately fixed when the stage is short relative to their systemic resistance–compliance times.
