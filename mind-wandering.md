# Mind-wandering

↑ **Parent:** [Attention](attention.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind-wandering)

Thought disengaged from the current external task. Its content, intentionality, associated awareness and effect on task performance should be measured separately. An off-task thought can occur with or without [meta-awareness](meta-awareness.md).
