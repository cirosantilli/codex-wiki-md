# Stern model

↑ **Parent:** [Electric double layer](electric-double-layer.md)

A model separating a compact interfacial layer from a diffuse [ion](ion.md) distribution. Specified [adsorption](adsorption.md) and boundary conditions determine the potential at the outer compact-layer boundary; a diffuse-layer equation alone does not determine specific [adsorption](adsorption.md).
