# Nagata criterion for unique factorization domains

↑ **Parent:** [Unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md)

Let $A$ be an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) that is a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), and let $x\in A$ generate a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md). If the [localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md) $A_x$ is a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md), then $A$ is a unique factorization domain. Indeed, integrality of $a/x^n$ over $A$, with $n$ minimal, forces $x\mid a$ and hence proves that $A$ is an [integrally closed domain](integrally-closed-domain.md). The [Nagata theorem for divisor class groups](nagata-theorem-for-divisor-class-groups.md) then says that $\operatorname{Cl}(A)$ is generated by the height-one primes containing $x$; the only one is the principal prime $(x)$, so $\operatorname{Cl}(A)=0$.
