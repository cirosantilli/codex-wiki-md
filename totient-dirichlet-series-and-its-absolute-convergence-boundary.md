# Totient Dirichlet series and its absolute convergence boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md)

The [totient divisor-sum identity](totient-divisor-sum-identity.md) and multiplication of absolutely convergent [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md) give the quotient for $\operatorname{Re}s>2$. The bound $\phi(n)\leq n$ proves convergence there. The [simple pole](simple-pole.md) of the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md) at one makes the quotient unbounded as real $s\downarrow2$. Since the coefficients are nonnegative, convergence at two would bound all these larger-real-part values, a contradiction. Thus the [abscissa of absolute convergence of a Dirichlet series](abscissa-of-absolute-convergence-of-a-dirichlet-series.md) is two.
