# Denominator criterion for Newton polygon irreducibility

↑ **Parent:** [Newton polygon](newton-polygon.md)

If a monic degree-$n$ polynomial over a discretely valued field with normalized valuation has all its roots of valuation $a/n$, where $\gcd(a,n)=1$, then it is irreducible. Each irreducible factor of degree $d$ has constant term of valuation $da/n\in\mathbb Z$, forcing $n\mid d$. Equivalently, a single [Newton polygon](newton-polygon.md) segment of relatively prime horizontal and vertical lengths gives irreducibility.
