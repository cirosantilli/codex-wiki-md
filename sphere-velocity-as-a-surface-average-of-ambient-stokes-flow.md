# Sphere velocity as a surface average of ambient Stokes flow

↑ **Parent:** [Faxén's first law](faxen-s-first-law.md)

For a no-slip sphere in a smooth ambient [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) whose [body forces](body-force.md) lie outside the sphere and are unchanged by insertion, use the translating sphere as a reciprocal auxiliary field. Its uniform surface [traction](traction.md) selects the ambient surface average and annihilates a rigid rotation term. The ambient stress has zero net surface force because its divergence vanishes inside the ball. Thus the sphere's hydrodynamic [force](force-split.md) is $-6\pi\mu a(\mathbf V-\langle\mathbf u_\infty\rangle)$; mechanical balance gives the displayed formula. The surface-average-only expression requires a force-free inertialess sphere. The [spherical mean of a biharmonic function](spherical-mean-of-a-biharmonic-function.md) makes this average exactly $[1+(a^2/6)\nabla^2]\mathbf u_\infty(0)$.
