# Regulated calibration bath

↑ **Parent:** [Heat bath](heat-bath.md)

A regulated bath holds its [temperature](temperature-split.md) near a chosen setting through [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md), while stirring reduces internal [temperature gradients](temperature-gradient.md). Its finite [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) buffers disturbances; external heating and cooling replace the ideal [heat bath](heat-bath.md)'s unlimited capacity. This is useful for [thermometer calibration](thermometer-calibration.md) and for maintaining a [blackbody](blackbody.md) calibration source at a known [temperature](temperature-split.md).

[Joel B. Fowler](joel-b-fowler.md)'s A Third Generation Water Bath Based Blackbody Source reports a NIST test of a stirred water bath containing a copper [blackbody](blackbody.md) cavity. A bottom plate supplied heating and cooling; a double stirrer circulated water. Electronic [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md) regulated heating. An electronic [thermometer](thermometer.md) with a [thermistor](thermistor.md) probe provided the readout. The preparation was to approach a selected setting, wait for stabilization, adjust against the measured [temperature](temperature-split.md) and then log continuously.[A Third Generation Water Bath Based Blackbody Source, §§2–3, printed pp. 592–593: bath construction, controller and setpoint procedure](a-third-generation-water-bath-based-blackbody-source-by-joel-b-fowler.md)

To test spatial uniformity, one [thermistor](thermistor.md) stayed near the cavity tip while another visited ten locations at three heights. The simultaneous reference distinguished spatial differences from temporal drift. An insulated dome reduced [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) along probe casings. Table 1 reports nonzero departures at several settings: circulation did not make every location identical. The [measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md) analysis separately included uniformity, stability, setting, immersion and readout contributions. These tests supported a bounded [temperature](temperature-split.md) assignment around the cavity, rather than infinite [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) or perfect [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) throughout the apparatus.[A Third Generation Water Bath Based Blackbody Source, §4.1 and Tables 1–2, printed pp. 594–595: fixed and movable probes, immersion control, measured nonuniformity and uncertainty components](a-third-generation-water-bath-based-blackbody-source-by-joel-b-fowler.md)
