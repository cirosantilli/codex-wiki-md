# Denticity

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

The number of donor atoms from one [ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) bonded to a particular [metal](metal.md) centre. Unlike [hapticity](hapticity.md), it counts distinct donor sites rather than a contiguous bonded fragment.
