# Optical theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md)

Write the [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) as $S=1+iT$. [Unitarity](unitary-operator.md) gives $-i(T-T^\dagger)=T^\dagger T$, so the diagonal forward matrix element obeys $2\operatorname{Im}T_{ii}=\sum_f\int d\Phi_f\,|T_{fi}|^2$. Thus the imaginary part of a forward [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) measures the total inclusive transition rate. For an electromagnetic source, the [hadronic electromagnetic-current spectral density](hadronic-electromagnetic-current-spectral-density.md) supplies the inclusive spectral sum.
