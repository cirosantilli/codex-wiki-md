# Stop loss variance minimization principle

↑ **Parent:** [Aggregate stop loss reinsurance](aggregate-stop-loss-reinsurance.md)

Among retained payouts $g(S)$ with $0\leq g(x)\leq x$ and the same [expected value](expected-value.md) as $\min(S,M)$, [aggregate stop loss reinsurance](aggregate-stop-loss-reinsurance.md) minimizes the [variance](variance-split.md). Pointwise $(g(x)-M)^2\geq(\min(x,M)-M)^2$, and subtracting the identical squared distance of their common [expected value](expected-value.md) from $M$ proves the claim. Equality requires equal payouts almost surely.
