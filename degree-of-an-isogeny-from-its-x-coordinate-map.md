# Degree of an isogeny from its x-coordinate map

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of an isogeny](degree-of-an-isogeny.md)

For an [isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md) $\phi:E_1\to E_2$, write $x_2\circ\phi=A(x_1)/B(x_1)$ with [coprime](coprime-integers.md) [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) $A,B$. Each $x_i:E_i\to\mathbb P^1$ is a two-to-one [finite morphism](finite-morphism.md), and [function field](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) degrees multiply in a tower. Consequently $2\deg\phi=2\max(\deg A,\deg B)$. This computes the total degree, including an inseparable factor. The zero homomorphism is assigned degree zero separately.
