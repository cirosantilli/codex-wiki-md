# Greatest fixed point

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed point](fixed-point.md)

A [fixed point](fixed-point.md) of a [function](function-split.md) on a [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) that is greater than or equal to every other [fixed point](fixed-point.md). For the [order-preserving function](order-preserving-function.md) $H(X)=A\cap\{s:\operatorname{Post}(s)\subseteq X\}$ on the [power set](power-set.md) of a finite [transition system](transition-system.md) with no [terminal states](terminal-state.md), its [greatest fixed point](greatest-fixed-point.md) is the [set](set-split.md) of [states](state-computer-science.md) from which every infinite [path in a transition system](path-in-a-transition-system.md) remains in $A$. Iteration from the whole [state space](state-space.md) decreases to this [set](set-split.md); every strict decrease removes at least one [state](state-computer-science.md).
