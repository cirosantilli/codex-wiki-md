# Heisenberg uncertainty relation

↑ **Parent:** [Robertson uncertainty principle](robertson-uncertainty-principle.md)

For a normalized state with the required position and momentum moments, $[x,p]=i\hbar$ and the [Robertson uncertainty principle](robertson-uncertainty-principle.md) give $\Delta x\,\Delta p\geq\hbar/2$. In a [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md), the [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) then bounds the variance contribution to energy below by $\hbar\omega/2$.
