# Rapid eye movement sleep

↑ **Parent:** [Sleep](sleep.md)

A sleep state identified by rapid eye movements and characteristic electrophysiological and muscle-tone patterns. Its contribution to a particular learned task must be tested rather than inferred from the existence of dreams.
