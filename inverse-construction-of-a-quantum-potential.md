# Inverse construction of a quantum potential

↑ **Parent:** [Time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md)

For a real, sufficiently smooth, nonvanishing [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) of a particle with constant [mass](mass.md), the [time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md) determines the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) through the displayed logarithmic-curvature formula. An additive choice of [energy](energy-split.md) origin fixes the remaining constant. For $\psi=e^{-ax^4}$ with $a>0$ and $V(0)=0$, it gives $E=0$ and $V=(\hbar^2/2m)(16a^2x^6-12ax^2)$. At a node the quotient must instead be interpreted by a limit; an arbitrary trial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) can produce singularities rather than a regular potential.
