# Cohomology ring of the antipodal two-sphere mapping torus

↑ **Parent:** [Homology of the antipodal sphere mapping torus](homology-of-the-antipodal-sphere-mapping-torus.md)

The [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) on the two-sphere reverses [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md), so its [mapping torus](mapping-torus.md) is a nonorientable closed three-manifold. The [Wang sequence](wang-sequence.md) gives $H_0=H_1=\mathbb Z$, $H_2=\mathbb Z/2$ and $H_3=0$. The [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) therefore gives the [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md) $\mathbb Z[t,u]/(t^2,tu,u^2,2u)$ with $|t|=1$, $|u|=3$. The base-circle class satisfies $t^2=0$ by pullback; the other positive-degree products vanish for dimension reasons.
