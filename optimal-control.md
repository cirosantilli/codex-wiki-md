# Optimal control

↑ **Parent:** [Control theory](control-theory-split.md)

Choice of a [control input](control-input.md) to minimize an objective subject to [differential equations](differential-equation-split.md) for a state and constraints on the control, state or terminal time. Necessary conditions such as the [Pontryagin maximum principle](pontryagin-maximum-principle.md) require regularity hypotheses and do not by themselves prove global optimality.
