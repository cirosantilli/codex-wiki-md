# Construction of a closed subscheme from a quasi-coherent ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md)

A [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) of ideals $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ gives a [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md) by gluing $\operatorname{Spec}(A/J)$ on [affine open subschemes](affine-open-subscheme.md) where $\mathcal I=\widetilde J$. Compatibility follows from [exactness of localization](exactness-of-localization.md). On the closed set $Z=\{x:\mathcal I_x\ne\mathcal O_{X,x}\}$, the [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) is $i^{-1}(\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I)$, where $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ is the inclusion. Its pushforward is $\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I$.
