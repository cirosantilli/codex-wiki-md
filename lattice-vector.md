# Lattice vector

↑ **Parent:** [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md)

A direct lattice vector is an integer [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of primitive translation vectors $\mathbf a_i$ of a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md). Translating by such a vector preserves the lattice. Its dot product with every [reciprocal lattice vector](reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) is an integer multiple of $2\pi$ in the angular-wavevector convention.
