# Conic combination

↑ **Parent:** [Conic hull](conic-hull.md)

A finite sum $\sum_j\lambda_js_j$ with $\lambda_j\geq0$. Unlike a [convex combination](convex-combination.md), the [coefficients](coefficient.md) need not sum to one. Zero [coefficients](coefficient.md) and the empty sum are allowed.
