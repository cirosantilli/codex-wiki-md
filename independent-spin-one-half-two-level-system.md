# Independent spin-one-half two-level system

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md)

For $N$ distinguishable noninteracting spins, let a fraction $\alpha$ occupy the level $+\epsilon$ and the remainder occupy $-\epsilon$. The [Stirling formula](stirling-formula.md) gives

$$
S=-Nk_B\bigl[\alpha\log\alpha+(1-\alpha)\log(1-\alpha)\bigr],
\qquad
E=N\epsilon(2\alpha-1).
$$

The relation $1/T=\partial S/\partial E$ yields

$$
\alpha=\frac1{1+e^{2\epsilon/(k_BT)}},
\qquad
E=-N\epsilon\tanh\left(\frac\epsilon{k_BT}\right).
$$

Positive temperature corresponds to $0\leq\alpha<1/2$; a population inversion $1/2<\alpha\leq1$ has [negative temperature](negative-temperature.md).
