# Special Euclidean group

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean group](euclidean-group.md)

The [group](group-split.md) of [Euclidean isometries](euclidean-isometry.md) preserving [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) of $\mathbb R^n$. Its elements have the form $x\mapsto Ax+b$, with $A$ in the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md) and $b\in\mathbb R^n$. The [function composition](function-composition.md) law is $(A,b)(B,c)=(AB,Ac+b)$, the [identity element](identity-element.md) is $(I,0)$, and the [inverse element](inverse-element.md) is $(A^{-1},-A^{-1}b)$. These formulas prove closure and inverses; [function composition](function-composition.md) supplies [associativity](associative-property.md). For $n=2$, $A$ is a [planar rotation](planar-rotation.md).
