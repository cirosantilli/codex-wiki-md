# Monofunctor

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor.md)

A functor $F:\mathcal C\to\mathbf{Set}$ is a monofunctor when $F(f)$ is an [injective function](injective-function.md) for every [morphism](morphism.md) $f$ of $\mathcal C$.
