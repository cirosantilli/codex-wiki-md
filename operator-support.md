# Operator support

↑ **Parent:** [Local Hamiltonian](local-hamiltonian.md)

The [operator support](operator-support.md) is the set of subsystems on which an operator may act nontrivially; it acts as the identity outside that set. [Unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md) can enlarge the support, while a [Lieb-Robinson bound](lieb-robinson-bound.md) controls the error of replacing the evolved operator by one with smaller support. The support diameter and the number of sites in the support are different quantities.
