# Symmetric absorbing-interval survival probability

↑ **Parent:** [Survival probability from an absorbing transition density](survival-probability-from-an-absorbing-transition-density.md)

For [diffusion](diffusion.md) starting at the centre of $(-L,L)$ with absorbing endpoints, only even-parity spatial [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) contribute. Integrating their cosine expansion gives the displayed series. The leading late-time term has prefactor $4/\pi$ and decay rate $\pi^2D/(4L^2)$; its first relative correction is $-\frac13e^{-2\pi^2Dt/L^2}$.
