# Ice-albedo feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Climate feedback](climate-feedback.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice–albedo_feedback)

Replacing bright ice or snow with darker water or ground decreases [surface albedo](surface-albedo.md), increases absorbed sunlight and can cause further melting. Locally the extra absorbed [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) is $(\alpha_{\rm ice}-\alpha_{\rm water})S_\downarrow$ on the newly exposed surface. The effect depends on season, cloud cover and incident sunlight.
