# Closed system

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md)

A [closed system](closed-system.md) exchanges no matter with its surroundings, but can exchange [energy](energy-split.md) through [heat](heat-split.md) or [work](work.md). A sealed cylinder with a moving piston is a typical idealized example. “Closed” fixes the material inventory, rather than the [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) or the [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md). An insulated cylinder can receive compression [work](work.md); a rigid sealed vessel can receive [heat](heat-split.md). Neither is necessarily an [isolated system](isolated-system.md).
