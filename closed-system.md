# Closed system

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md)

A closed thermodynamic system exchanges no matter with its surroundings, but can exchange [energy](energy.md) through [heat](heat.md) or [work](work.md). A sealed cylinder with a moving piston is a typical idealized example.
