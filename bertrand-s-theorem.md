<h1 id="bertrand-s-theorem">Bertrand's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Central force](central-force.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand's_theorem)

[Joseph Bertrand](joseph-bertrand.md)'s theorem states that, among smooth attractive [central potentials](central-potential.md) admitting stable [circular orbits](circular-orbit.md), requiring every [bound orbit](bound-orbit.md) to be closed singles out the inverse-distance [potential energy](potential-energy.md) and the isotropic quadratic [potential energy](potential-energy.md), up to additive constants. Rational radial-to-angular [frequency](frequency.md) ratios near a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) provide only a necessary local closure test; finite-amplitude closure is the additional content. In $U=Br^n$, the radial-to-angular [frequency](frequency.md) ratio near a stable circle is $\sqrt{n+2}$, and stability requires $nB>0$ and $n>-2$. The all-bound-orbit cases are $n=-1$ and $n=2$.
