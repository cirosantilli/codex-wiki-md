# CW subcomplex

↑ **Parent:** [CW complex](cw-complex.md)

A [CW subcomplex](cw-subcomplex.md) is a union of cells of a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) containing the attaching boundary of every included cell. In a combinatorial [2-complex](2-complex.md), including a two-cell therefore includes every edge in its boundary. A connected [CW subcomplex](cw-subcomplex.md) has a connected [1-skeleton](1-skeleton.md), whose intrinsic path metric may differ from the ambient graph metric.
