# MSc in quantum technologies course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-quantum-technologies).

Core: introduction to quantum technologies; quantum technologies and their applications; hardware for quantum technologies; industrial seminar; practical training. Students requiring the quantum-mechanics bridging course take it in place of one option. Others take three option modules. Four-month supervised project in a university or industry research team, followed by a dissertation.
