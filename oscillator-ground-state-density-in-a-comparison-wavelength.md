# Oscillator ground-state density in a comparison wavelength

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

A free particle whose [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) equals the ground-state total [energy](energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega/2$ has [momentum](momentum-split.md) $\hbar/a$ and [de Broglie wavelength](de-broglie-wavelength.md) $\lambda=2\pi a$, where $a=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$. Substitution into the normalized bound-state [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) yields the title density. The comparison [wavelength](wavelength.md) is a useful [length](length.md) scale; it is not a claim that the [bound state](bound-state.md) has a single free [momentum](momentum-split.md) or spatially uniform [probability density](probability-density.md).
