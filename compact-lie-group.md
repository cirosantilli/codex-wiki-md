# Compact Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Lie_group)

A compact Lie group is a [Lie group](lie-group.md) whose underlying manifold is [compact](compact-space.md). Averaging any positive inner product over its [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) supplies an invariant inner product on each finite-dimensional representation, including the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
