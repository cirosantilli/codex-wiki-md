# Phonetic category

↑ **Parent:** [Phonetics](phonetics.md)

A grouping of speech sounds defined by a relevant perceptual or articulatory organisation. Experimental discrimination of two endpoints does not, by itself, establish a complete inventory of linguistic categories.
