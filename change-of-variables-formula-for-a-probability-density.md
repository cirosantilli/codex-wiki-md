# Change-of-variables formula for a probability density

↑ **Parent:** [Probability density function](probability-density-function.md)

If $Y=g(X)$ for a differentiable bijection, then

$$
f_Y(y)=f_X(g^{-1}(y))\left|\det Dg^{-1}(y)\right|.
$$

This is the [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) applied to probability density.
