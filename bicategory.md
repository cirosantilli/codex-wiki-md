# Bicategory

↑ **Parent:** [Category theory](category-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicategory)

A bicategory has objects, hom-categories, composition functors and identity 1-cells. Associativity and unit laws hold through coherent invertible 2-cells, satisfying pentagon and triangle identities. A [monoidal category](monoidal-category.md) is exactly a bicategory with a single object: its objects are the 1-cells, its morphisms the 2-cells, and its tensor product the horizontal composition.
