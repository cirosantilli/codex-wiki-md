# Benzvalene

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md)

A cage isomer of [benzene](benzene.md) with one [carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) [double bond](double-bond.md), conventionally described as tricyclo\[3.1.0.0(2,6)\]hex-3-ene. Its ideal cage geometry belongs to the [C2v molecular point group](c2v-molecular-point-group.md). [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md) depends on the full three-dimensional cage rather than the apparent [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) of a planar skeletal projection.
