# Inverse-cube central orbit classification

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse-cube central force](inverse-cube-central-force.md)

For nonzero [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $L$, put $u=1/r$ and $\lambda=1+2mB/L^2$. The [Binet equation](binet-equation.md) is $u_{\theta\theta}+\lambda u=0$. Positive $\lambda$ gives cosine scattering branches, zero gives linear reciprocal radius, and negative gives exponential combinations, with [energy](energy-split.md) distinguishing sinh, exponential and cosh branches. Only intervals with $u>0$ are physical.
