# Kleene theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Regular expression](regular-expression.md)

This theorem characterizes a [regular language](regular-language.md) by [regular expressions](regular-expression.md) and acceptance by a [finite-state automaton](finite-state-machine.md). Kleene's theorem states that a language is denoted by a regular expression exactly when it is accepted by a finite automaton.
