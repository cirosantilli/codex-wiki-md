# Eukaryotic transcriptional activator

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A [eukaryotic transcriptional activator](eukaryotic-transcriptional-activator.md) increases productive transcription through interactions with chromatin regulators, coactivators or the transcription machinery. Its effect depends on regulatory-site occupancy and cellular context, not just the presence of a DNA-binding protein.
