# Cut locus

↑ **Parent:** [Minimizing geodesic](minimizing-geodesic.md)

On a complete connected [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), let $c(v)$ for a unit tangent vector $v$ be the supremum of times for which $t\mapsto\exp_p(tv)$ minimizes [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md) from $p$. The cut locus consists of the endpoints $\exp_p(c(v)v)$ with $c(v)<\infty$. Radial minimization fails beyond this first cut time; distance spheres can cease to be smooth there.
