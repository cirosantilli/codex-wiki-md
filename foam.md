# Foam

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

A dispersion of [gas](gas.md) bubbles in a [liquid](liquid.md) or solid matrix. Thin intervening films and interfacial forces govern stability; bubble diameter need not itself lie below a micrometre.
