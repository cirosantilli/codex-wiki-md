# Amplification factor

↑ **Parent:** [von Neumann stability analysis](von-neumann-stability-analysis.md)

For a one-step translation-invariant recurrence, the amplification factor is the multiplier satisfying

$$
\widehat u^{\,n+1}(\theta)=H(\theta)\widehat u^{\,n}(\theta).
$$

The [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md) converts the pointwise bound $|H|\leq1$ into non-growth of the discrete $2$-norm.
