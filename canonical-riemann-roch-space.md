# Canonical Riemann-Roch space

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical divisor](canonical-divisor.md)

If $K_X=(\omega)$ is represented by a nonzero [rational differential](rational-differential-on-an-algebraic-curve.md), multiplication by $\omega$ identifies the [Riemann-Roch space](riemann-roch-space.md) $L(K_X)$ with the vector space of [holomorphic differential forms](holomorphic-differential-form.md). The [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives $\ell(K_X)=g$ for a smooth projective curve of genus $g$.
