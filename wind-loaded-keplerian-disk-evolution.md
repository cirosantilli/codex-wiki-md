# Wind-loaded Keplerian disk evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

Subtract local [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) times [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) from [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) including wind losses S,T. With outward radial mass flux $\mathcal F$ and outward [viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) $\mathcal G=-2\pi\bar\nu\Sigma r^3\Omega'$, this gives $\mathcal Fh'+\mathcal G'=2\pi r(Sh-T)$. For [Keplerian rotation](keplerian-disk.md) and $T=qSh$, substitution in mass conservation yields the displayed equation. A wind with q=1 removes local angular momentum but supplies no additional drift term; q\>1 can drive accretion even with zero viscosity.
