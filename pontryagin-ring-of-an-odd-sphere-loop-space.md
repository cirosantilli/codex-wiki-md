# Pontryagin ring of an odd-sphere loop space

↑ **Parent:** [Pontryagin ring](pontryagin-ring.md)

For $n\geq1$, the [Bott–Samelson theorem](bott-samelson-theorem.md) gives a single homology generator of degree $2n$ with no word relations. Its [Pontryagin product](pontryagin-product-on-loop-space-homology.md) satisfies $x^i*x^j=x^{i+j}$. Unlike this polynomial [homology](homology-split.md) product, the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) cup product has divided-power coefficients.
