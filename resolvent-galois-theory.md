# Resolvent (Galois theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resolvent_(Galois_theory))

A [resolvent](resolvent-galois-theory.md) is an auxiliary polynomial built from expressions in the roots of a polynomial. The action of its [Galois group](galois-group.md) on those expressions gives information about solvability and intermediate field extensions. A [Lagrange resolvent for a cubic](lagrange-resolvent-for-a-cubic.md) uses Fourier combinations of the three roots.
