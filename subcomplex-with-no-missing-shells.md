# Subcomplex with no missing shells

↑ **Parent:** [Small cancellation theory](small-cancellation-theory.md)

A [CW subcomplex](cw-subcomplex.md) has no missing shells if a cell with a shell-length boundary arc contained in the [CW subcomplex](cw-subcomplex.md) is itself contained in the [CW subcomplex](cw-subcomplex.md). For $C'(1/6)$ it suffices that every excluded cell have a complementary boundary arc of length at least half its perimeter with no edge in the [CW subcomplex](cw-subcomplex.md): an arc lying in the [CW subcomplex](cw-subcomplex.md) then has length at most half and cannot be a shell's exterior arc.
