# Conventional current

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)

[Conventional current](conventional-current.md) uses the direction in which positive [electric charge](electric-charge.md) flows. A moving negative [electric charge](electric-charge.md) contributes [electric current](electric-current.md) in the opposite direction to its [velocity](velocity.md). Thus [Electrons](electron.md) drifting left produce [conventional current](conventional-current.md) to the right; the convention describes signed [electric charge](electric-charge.md) transport, without requiring positive [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md). [Ling, Sanny and Moebs, §9.1: conventional current direction](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/9-1-electrical-current).
