# Diol

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

A [diol](diol.md) has two [hydroxy groups](hydroxy-group.md). Its two groups can serve as joining ends in [polyaddition](polyaddition.md); the rest of its [molecule](molecule.md) can already be a long chain.
