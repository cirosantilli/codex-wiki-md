# Toric surface

↑ **Parent:** [Toric variety](toric-variety.md)

A toric surface is a two-dimensional normal [toric variety](toric-variety.md), described by a [toric fan](fan-in-toric-geometry.md) in a rank-two [toric lattice](lattice-in-toric-geometry.md). Primitive consecutive rays bound its affine charts; those charts are smooth exactly when the two ray generators form a [lattice basis](basis-of-a-toric-lattice.md).
