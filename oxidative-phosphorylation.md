# Oxidative phosphorylation

↑ **Parent:** [Energy metabolism](energy-metabolism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxidative_phosphorylation)

[Oxidative phosphorylation](oxidative-phosphorylation.md) couples respiratory [redox](redox.md) conversion to [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) formation through [chemiosmosis](chemiosmosis.md). [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) uses inward [proton](proton.md) flow to join [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) and [inorganic phosphate](inorganic-phosphate.md).[Chapter 14, Electron-Transport Chains and Their Proton Pumps, Summary: ATP synthase and chemiosmotic coupling](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

The intermediate [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) explains why [respiratory uncoupling](respiratory-uncoupling.md) can preserve [oxygen](oxygen.md) consumption while reducing [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) formation. An [electron](electron.md) ledger alone cannot determine the recovery of useful [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md).
