# Minimax sample mean for a nonnegative normal location

↑ **Parent:** [Minimax decision rule](minimax-decision-rule.md)

For independent $X_i\sim N(\theta,1)$ with $\theta\ge0$, the sample mean has constant squared-error risk $1/n$ and is minimax. Smooth priors supported on $[0,L]$ whose prior Fisher information is $O(L^{-2})$ give, through the [Van Trees inequality](van-trees-inequality.md), a lower bound tending to $1/n$ for every rule's worst-case risk.
