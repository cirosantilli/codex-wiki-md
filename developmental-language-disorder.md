# Developmental language disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Language acquisition](language-acquisition.md)

Persistent developmental difficulty with [language](language.md) associated with functional impact. It is not interchangeable with motor [speech](speech.md) apraxia or [stuttering](stuttering.md), and a rare single-family genetic finding does not establish a universal cause.
