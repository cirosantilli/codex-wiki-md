# NOx-limited ozone-production regime

↑ **Parent:** [Photochemical ozone production](photochemical-ozone-production.md)

A regime in which additional [nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md) promote net [tropospheric ozone](tropospheric-ozone.md) production because NO availability limits peroxy-radical oxidation of NO. Reducing NOx then generally lowers [ozone](ozone.md) production, subject to transport and chemical history. At much larger NOx burdens, OH removal by nitric-acid formation can instead produce a [VOC-limited ozone-production regime](voc-limited-ozone-production-regime.md).
