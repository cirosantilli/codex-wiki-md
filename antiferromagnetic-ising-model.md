# Antiferromagnetic Ising model

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)

With the convention $H=J\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}\sigma_i\sigma_j-h\sum_i\sigma_i$, positive $J$ favors opposite neighboring [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md). On a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md), the [Néel order parameter](neel-order-parameter.md) measures opposite sublattice [magnetizations](magnetization.md); it is distinct from the uniform [magnetization](magnetization.md). Changing the signs of spins on one sublattice maps the zero-field model to the ferromagnetic [Ising model](ising-model.md).
