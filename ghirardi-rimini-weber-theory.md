# Ghirardi-Rimini-Weber theory

↑ **Parent:** [Objective-collapse theory](objective-collapse-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghirardi–Rimini–Weber_theory)

The Ghirardi--Rimini--Weber theory modifies [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md) by random Gaussian position localizations. Each constituent collapses at a small fixed rate, while the rate for an entangled macroscopic body grows with its number of constituents.
