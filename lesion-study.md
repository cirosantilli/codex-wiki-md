# Lesion study

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscientific methods](neuroscientific-methods.md)

A study relating damage to altered or preserved performance. Damage may affect several neighboring areas and connections, and chronic compensation can change the relationship between a lesion and behavior. Deficits are evidence about a network's contribution under the tested conditions, not automatic localization of one abstract faculty.
