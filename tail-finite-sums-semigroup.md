# Tail finite-sums semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Idempotent ultrafilter](idempotent-ultrafilter.md)

For a sequence of positive integers, the nested closures of its tail [finite-sums sets](finite-sums-set.md) have nonempty compact intersection in the [Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers](stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers.md). This intersection is a [subsemigroup](subsemigroup.md). For a sum $s$ from one tail, all sums supported beyond a chosen finite representation of $s$ lie in that tail translated by $-s$. The [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) addition formula then establishes closure under addition. The [Ellis–Numakura lemma](ellis-numakura-lemma.md) supplies an [idempotent ultrafilter](idempotent-ultrafilter.md) containing every tail finite-sums set and hence any set containing the full [finite-sums set](finite-sums-set.md).
