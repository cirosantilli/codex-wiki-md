# Soft magnetic material

↑ **Parent:** [Ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md)

A [soft magnetic material](soft-magnetic-material.md) changes its [magnetization](magnetization.md) relatively easily and has a low coercive field. High useful permeability and small [hysteresis](hysteresis.md) losses favour such materials in electromagnets and alternating-field cores. “Soft” describes magnetic response rather than mechanical softness; electrically resistive [ferrimagnets](ferrimagnetism.md) can also be magnetically soft.
