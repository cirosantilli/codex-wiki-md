↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Hill radius](../../../../../hill-radius.md) compares planetary [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) with the differential stellar tide. Expanding the stellar tide near a circular orbit gives $GM_p/r_H^2\simeq3\Omega^2r_H$, so

$$
\boxed{r_H=a\left(\frac{M_p}{3M_\star}\right)^{1/3}.}
$$

It sets the size of the [planet](../../../../../planet.md)'s tidal neighbourhood, controls encounter and [feeding zone](../../../../../feeding-zone-of-a-planetary-embryo.md) scales, and limits the region within which material can remain bound against stellar shear. It is not itself a solid surface or a guarantee of capture.

The [Bondi radius](../../../../../bondi-radius.md) compares planetary potential energy per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md) with the [gas](../../../../../gas.md) thermal scale,

$$
\boxed{r_B=\frac{GM_p}{c_s^2}.}
$$

This convention uses the ambient [sound speed](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md); definitions based on escape speed can differ by a factor of two. Inside $r_B$, [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) can substantially perturb thermal [gas](../../../../../gas.md). [Gas](../../../../../gas.md) still needs a way to dissipate energy and [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) to accrete; a geometric boundary alone does not supply a [gas](../../../../../gas.md)-capture rate.

For an embedded hydrostatic envelope a useful outer scale is $r_{\rm out}\sim\min(r_B,r_H)$, subject to disk thickness and [gas](../../../../../gas.md) recycling. Let $h=H/a=c_s/(a\Omega)$ and $q=M_p/M_\star$. The [Bondi-Hill crossover in a protoplanetary disk](../../../../../bondi-hill-crossover-in-a-protoplanetary-disk.md) obeys

$$
\frac{r_B}{a}=\frac q{h^2},\qquad\frac{r_H}{a}=\left(\frac q3\right)^{1/3},\qquad\boxed{r_B=r_H\ \Longrightarrow\ q=\frac{h^3}{\sqrt3}.}
$$

Small cores are usually thermally limited, whereas larger ones become tidally limited. These radii also organize [pebble accretion](../../../../../pebble-accretion.md): [gas](../../../../../gas.md) [drag](../../../../../drag-physics.md) can turn an encounter into capture, with drift-dominated and shear-dominated regimes depending on particle stopping and encounter times. There is no universal accretion efficiency fixed by $r_H$ or $r_B$ alone.[Version1, sectionIII.C.1, pages32–34: [core accretion](../../../../../core-accretion.md) and envelope boundaries](../../../../../lecture-notes-on-the-formation-and-early-evolution-of-planetary-systems-by-philip-j-armitage.md)
