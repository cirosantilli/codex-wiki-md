# Range straggling

↑ **Parent:** [Emulsion range measurement](emulsion-range-measurement.md)

[Range straggling](range-straggling.md) is the event-to-event spread in [particle range](particle-range.md) for otherwise similar incident particles, caused by fluctuations in their energy loss and transport through matter. It must be distinguished from [measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md) and from the incomplete recording of particles that escape.
