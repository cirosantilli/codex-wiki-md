# Kondo lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Kondo effect](kondo-effect.md)

A periodic array of local moments coupled to conduction [electrons](electron.md). Competition between screening, coherent hybridization and intersite magnetic interactions can produce a [heavy fermion material](heavy-fermion-material.md), magnetic order or a correlated insulating state.
