# Helical symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous symmetry](continuous-symmetry.md)

A [helical symmetry](helical-symmetry.md) combines an axial [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) and a proportional axial [translation](translation-geometry.md). A [helix](helix.md) with $z=b\theta$ is unchanged as a set by this transformation. If a mechanical [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is invariant, the [Noether conserved quantity for a mechanical point symmetry](noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) is $L_z+bP_z$, where the two momenta include all moving components. Pure axial [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) and pure axial [translation](translation-geometry.md) need not separately be [symmetries](symmetry-physics.md). The [helical symmetry of a potential](helical-symmetry-of-a-potential.md) is a related special case in which the constraint is replaced by an invariant potential.[online chapter 2, §2.4.1, conserved momentum for a continuous coordinate transformation](classical-dynamics-by-david-tong.md)
