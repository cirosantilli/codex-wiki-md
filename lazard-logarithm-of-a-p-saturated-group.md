# Lazard logarithm of a p-saturated group

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa algebra](iwasawa-algebra.md)

Inside the completed rational Iwasawa algebra, the convergent series

$$
\log g=\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}n(g-1)^n
$$

sends a finite-rank p-saturated group to a $\mathbb Z_p$-Lie algebra. The [Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula](baker-campbell-hausdorff-formula.md) and its commutator expansion prove closure under addition and the [commutator](commutator.md) bracket.
