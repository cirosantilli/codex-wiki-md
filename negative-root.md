# Negative root

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

After choosing [positive roots](positive-root.md) in a [root system](root-system.md), a negative root is the negative of a [positive root](positive-root.md). Equivalently, for a regular linear functional defining the positive roots, its value on a negative root is negative. Relative to the associated [simple roots](simple-root.md), the coefficients of a negative root are all nonpositive integers.
