# Fantope

↑ **Parent:** [Positive semidefinite cone](positive-semidefinite-cone.md)

For an integer $0\leq k\leq n$, the fantope is the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of rank-$k$ [orthogonal projection matrices](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) in $\mathbb R^n$. Equivalently, it consists of real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) in $[0,1]$ and [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) $k$. To prove the equivalence, apply the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md) and express the [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) vector as a [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of the zero-one [extreme points](extreme-point.md) of the [capped simplex](capped-simplex.md). The [sum of the largest eigenvalues](sum-of-the-largest-eigenvalues.md) is its [support function](support-function.md).
