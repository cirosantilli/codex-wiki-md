# Corner compatibility for constant-speed transport

↑ **Parent:** [Inflow transport boundary condition](inflow-transport-boundary-condition.md)

For the [transport equation](transport-equation.md) $u_t+a u_x=0$ on the quadrant with $a>0$, smooth initial and inflow data glue across $x=at$ to order $m$ precisely when $f^{(j)}(0)=(-a)^j u_0^{(j)}(0)$ for $0\le j\le m$. Matching all orders yields a smooth solution; matching orders zero and one yields a [classical solution](classical-solution.md) up to the two boundaries.
