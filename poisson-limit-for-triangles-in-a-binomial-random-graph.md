# Poisson limit for triangles in a binomial random graph

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle count in a binomial random graph](triangle-count-in-a-binomial-random-graph.md)

Convergence holds in [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md). A triangle indicator depends only on its three edges, so its dependency neighborhood consists of itself and the $3(n-3)$ triangles sharing an edge. The [Poisson approximation with dependency neighborhoods](poisson-approximation-with-dependency-neighborhoods.md) gives errors $b_1=O(n^4p^6)$ and $b_2=O(n^4p^5)$. Under the displayed scaling $p$ is of order $n^{-1}$, so both vanish. The probability of at least one triangle therefore tends to $1-e^{-\alpha}$.
