# Koszul complex

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koszul_complex)

The Koszul complex on $f_1,\ldots,f_r$ has term $K_i=\bigwedge^iR^r$ and differential $\partial(e_{j_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge e_{j_i})=\sum_a(-1)^{a-1}f_{j_a}e_{j_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\widehat{e_{j_a}}\wedge\cdots\wedge e_{j_i}$. Its degree-zero [homology](homology-split.md) is $R/(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$. For a [regular sequence](regular-sequence.md) it is exact in positive degrees, hence a [Koszul resolution](koszul-resolution.md) of that quotient. The variables of a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) form a [regular sequence](regular-sequence.md), so this resolves the coefficient [field](field.md) in length equal to the number of variables.
