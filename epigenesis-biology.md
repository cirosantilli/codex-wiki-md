# Epigenesis (biology)

↑ **Parent:** [Embryology](embryology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epigenesis_(biology))

Progressive formation and differentiation of an organism during [biological development](biological-development.md), historically contrasted with [preformationism](preformationism.md). It must not be confused with the modern concept of [epigenetics](epigenetics.md).
