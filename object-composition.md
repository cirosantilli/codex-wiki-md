# Object composition

↑ **Parent:** [Object-oriented programming](object-oriented-programming.md)

Object composition builds behavior by storing component [software objects](software-object.md) and delegating operations to them. A containing [software object](software-object.md) can expose a distinct [object interface](object-interface.md) and control which operations affect additional state. Exclusive ownership or a specified reference-aliasing discipline may be needed to protect its [representation invariant](representation-invariant.md). Unlike subclass overrides, [object methods](object-method.md) of a separate component do not dynamically dispatch into the containing wrapper.
