# Nucleic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleic_acid)

A [nucleic acid](nucleic-acid.md) is a [polymer](polymer-split.md) built from linked [nucleotides](nucleotide.md). The two principal kinds are [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md). Each [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) contributes a [sugar](sugar.md), a [nucleobase](nucleotide-base.md), and [phosphate](phosphate.md) to the chain; the [nucleobase](nucleotide-base.md) is only the letter-bearing component, not the entire [nucleotide](nucleotide.md). [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) uses [deoxyribose](deoxyribose.md), whereas [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) uses [ribose](ribose.md). A chain of six [nucleotides](nucleotide.md) is therefore not six free [nucleobases](nucleotide-base.md) placed beside one another. See [NHGRI: nucleic acids](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Nucleic-Acids).

The chemical scaffold and the variable lettering do different jobs. [Covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md) maintain the [sugar](sugar.md)–[phosphate](phosphate.md) backbone, while the order of attached [nucleobases](nucleotide-base.md) specifies the [nucleic acid sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md). A [phosphodiester bond](phosphodiester-bond.md) links the $3'$ position of one [sugar](sugar.md) to the $5'$ position of the next through [phosphate](phosphate.md). These position numbers refer to [carbon](carbon.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) in the [sugar](sugar.md); they define a strand's direction even when it bends or forms a circle. See [Brown, Genomes, 2nd edition: the human genome](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21134/).

This division is useful: the backbone supplies a connected physical record, and [base pairing](base-pairing.md) allows its letters to recognize partners. [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) copies that record, and [transcription](transcription-biology.md) uses a [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) template to make [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md). [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) can also fold into functional shapes through [base pairing](base-pairing.md) within one chain; a [nucleic acid sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) specifies chemical order, not a requirement for a straight shape. The beauty is that the same kind of [polymer](polymer-split.md) can carry a message and form a structure that acts on messages.
