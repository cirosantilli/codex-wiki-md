# Transverse decomposition of an unpolarized hadronic tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Hadronic tensor](hadronic-tensor.md)

For electromagnetic [deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md) with an unpolarized target, [Lorentz invariance](lorentz-invariance.md), parity and [current conservation](conserved-current.md) leave two independent [deep-inelastic structure functions](deep-inelastic-structure-function.md). With $T^{\mu\nu}=g^{\mu\nu}-q^\mu q^\nu/q^2$ and $\widetilde P=P-(P\cdot q/q^2)q$, both tensor structures are transverse to $q$. Terms with a free $q$ index disappear on contraction with a conserved [leptonic tensor](leptonic-tensor.md), although such terms can still be necessary to make the full [hadronic tensor](hadronic-tensor.md) transverse.
