# Determinant of the minimum-index matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)

Let $L$ be the [triangular matrix](triangular-matrix.md) with $L_{ij}=1$ for $j\leq i$ and zero otherwise. Then $(LL^T)_{ij}=\min(i,j)$ and $\det L=1$, so the [determinant](determinant.md) of $LL^T$ is one. More generally, for $0=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n$, the [matrix](matrix.md) with entries $\min(t_i,t_j)$ factors as $L\operatorname{diag}(t_k-t_{k-1})L^T$ and has [determinant](determinant.md) $\prod_k(t_k-t_{k-1})$. This is the finite-dimensional structure behind the [Brownian covariance kernel](brownian-covariance-kernel.md).
