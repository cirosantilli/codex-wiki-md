# Stackelberg competition

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stackelberg_competition)

A sequential model in [game theory](game-theory-split.md) in which a leader commits to an action before a follower observes it and chooses a [best response](best-response.md). The leader anticipates the follower's response when optimizing. With private follower information, the leader maximizes its expected payoff over follower types. The resulting solution is a [Stackelberg equilibrium](stackelberg-equilibrium.md).
