# Asymptotic freedom

↑ **Parent:** [Beta function (physics)](beta-function-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_freedom)

An asymptotically free theory has a [running coupling](running-coupling.md) that tends to zero at arbitrarily high energy. Its negative weak-coupling beta function makes short-distance perturbation theory increasingly accurate.
