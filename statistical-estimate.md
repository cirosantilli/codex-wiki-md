# Statistical estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical estimation](statistical-estimation.md)

A statistical estimate is the realized numerical value of an [estimator](estimator.md) after observing a [statistical sample](statistical-sample.md). Before observation, the [estimator](estimator.md) is a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) or [random vector](random-vector.md); its realized value is the estimate. Both are commonly written $\widehat\theta$, so context distinguishes them.
