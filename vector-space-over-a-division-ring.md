# Vector space over a division ring

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)

A [module](module-mathematics.md) over a [division ring](division-ring.md) has the usual [basis](basis.md) and [linear independence](linear-independence.md) theory of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md), with the side of scalar multiplication specified. For a right [module](module-mathematics.md), a finite relation has the form $\sum_i v_i a_i=0$. Every nonzero [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) of a one-dimensional right [module](module-mathematics.md) is invertible. If $V$ is a [simple module](irreducible-module.md) on the left over $R$, evaluation makes it a right vector space over the [opposite ring](opposite-ring.md) $\operatorname{End}_R(V)^{\mathrm{op}}$, a [division ring](division-ring.md) by [Schur's lemma](schur-s-lemma.md). This is the scalar structure used by the [Jacobson density theorem](jacobson-density-theorem.md).
