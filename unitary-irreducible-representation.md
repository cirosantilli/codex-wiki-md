# Unitary irreducible representation

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary representation](unitary-representation.md)

A unitary irreducible representation is both [unitary](unitary-representation.md) and [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md). Such representations are the building blocks in the harmonic analysis of groups and in quantum symmetry classifications.
