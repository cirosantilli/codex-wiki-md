# Perceptual sensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Signal detection theory](signal-detection-theory.md)

The ability to discriminate stimulus classes, conceptually distinct from the [decision criterion](decision-criterion.md) determining which response is selected. A higher frequency of [fear](fear.md) judgments can reflect a criterion shift without better discrimination.
