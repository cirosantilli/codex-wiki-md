# Magnetic curvature drift

↑ **Parent:** [Drift kinetics](drift-kinetics.md)

The transverse [guiding center](guiding-center.md) drift associated with [magnetic field-line curvature](magnetic-field-line-curvature.md). The signed [cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md) $\Omega_s=q_sB/(m_sc)$ makes oppositely charged species drift oppositely at fixed [velocity](velocity.md). The resulting species-dependent response is retained in slow drift-scale instabilities.
