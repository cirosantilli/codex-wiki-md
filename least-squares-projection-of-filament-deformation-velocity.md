# Least-squares projection of filament deformation velocity

↑ **Parent:** [First-order free swimming of a planar filament](first-order-free-swimming-of-a-planar-filament.md)

At first order, the transverse drag depends on $g_t+V_1+\Omega_1x$. Its zero force and torque conditions make this residual orthogonal to $1$ and $x$. Thus $-V_1-\Omega_1x$ is the [linear least-squares projection](linear-least-squares-projection.md) of the prescribed transverse deformation velocity onto the span of $1$ and $x$. This recasts a force-balance calculation as a projection.
