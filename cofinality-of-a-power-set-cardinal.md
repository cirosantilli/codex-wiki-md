# Cofinality of a power-set cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Cofinality](cofinality.md)

For an infinite [cardinal number](cardinal-number-split.md) $\kappa$, use [cardinal arithmetic](cardinal-arithmetic.md) with the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md). If $\lambda=2^\kappa$ had [cofinality](cofinality.md) $\mu\leq\kappa$, choose cofinal [ordinals](ordinal-split.md) $\alpha_i<\lambda$, $i<\mu$. Their [cardinalities](cardinality.md) $a_i$ satisfy $\sum_i a_i=\lambda$. The [König theorem for cardinal numbers](konig-s-theorem-set-theory.md) gives $\lambda<\prod_i a_i^+\leq\lambda^\mu\leq(2^\kappa)^\kappa=\lambda$, a contradiction.
