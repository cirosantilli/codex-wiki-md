# Ext from a principal non-zero-divisor quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Ext functor](ext-functor.md)

For a non-zero-divisor $a\in A$, the free resolution $0\to A\xrightarrow{a}A\to A/(a)\to0$ gives degree-zero kernel and degree-one cokernel, and all higher [Ext functors](ext-functor.md) vanish. Directly in an [injective resolution](injective-resolution.md) of the second variable, multiplication by $a$ is surjective in every term: the map $aA\to I$ taking $ax$ to $xm$ extends to $A\to I$. Thus $0\to\operatorname{Hom}_A(A/(a),I^\bullet)\to I^\bullet\xrightarrow{a}I^\bullet\to0$ is a short exact sequence of complexes and gives the same result. The displayed Hom functor is covariant in $M$; changing it to $\operatorname{Hom}_A(M,A/(a))$ changes the variable that is resolved.
