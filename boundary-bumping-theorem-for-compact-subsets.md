# Boundary-bumping theorem for compact subsets

↑ **Parent:** [Connected component](connected-component.md)

Every [connected component](connected-component.md) $C$ of a nonempty [compact](compact-space.md) proper subset $D$ of a [connected](connected-space.md) [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) meets its [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md). If not, the [Šura-Bura lemma](sura-bura-lemma.md) identifies $C$ as a [quasi-component](quasi-component.md) in the [compact](compact-space.md) [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) subspace $D$. Finitely many [clopen](clopen-set.md) [neighborhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) separate it from the [compact](compact-space.md) [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md), giving a nonempty relatively [clopen](clopen-set.md) $H\subseteq\operatorname{int}D$. Such $H$ is open in the ambient space, and is also closed there because $D$ is closed. It is proper because $D$ is proper. This contradicts [connectedness](connected-space.md).
