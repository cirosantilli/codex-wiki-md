# Viscous gravity current on a cone

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-driven thin film on an incline](gravity-driven-thin-film-on-an-incline.md)

Let $x$ be slant distance from an upward apex, and let the surface slope down at angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. Axisymmetric [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) gives

$$
h_t+\frac Kx\partial_x\left[xh^3(\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha\,h_x)\right]=0,
\qquad K=\frac{\rho g}{3\mu}.
$$

For conserved volume $V$ and $x_N^3\gg V/\sin\alpha$, the leading [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) has $h=\sqrt{x/(5K\sin\alpha\,t)}$ in $0<x<x_N$, with

$$
x_N^5=\frac{125K\sin\alpha\,V^2t}{16\pi^2\cos^2\alpha}.
$$

The outer front is a discontinuity in this reduced conservation law; the hydrostatic-gradient term and the full local flow resolve the front and apex regions where the outer approximation fails.
