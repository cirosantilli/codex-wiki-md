# Convex simple games are unanimity games

↑ **Parent:** [Convex cooperative game](convex-cooperative-game.md)

Every nontrivial [simple cooperative game](simple-cooperative-game.md) that is a [convex cooperative game](convex-cooperative-game.md) is a [unanimity game](unanimity-game.md). If two [coalitions](coalition-game-theory.md) win, the [supermodular](supermodular-set-function.md) inequality forces their intersection to win as well. Intersecting the finite nonempty family of [winning coalitions](winning-coalition.md) gives a winning [set](set-split.md) $K\ne\varnothing$. Every winning [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) contains $K$; conversely monotonicity makes every superset of $K$ win. Thus the game is $u_K$. Conversely, every [unanimity game](unanimity-game.md) is a [supermodular set function](supermodular-set-function.md), as a direct check of the winning and losing cases shows.
