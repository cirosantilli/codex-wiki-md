# Strain-rate tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strain-rate_tensor)

The rate-of-strain tensor is the symmetric part of the velocity gradient,

$$
e=\frac12\left(\nabla u+(\nabla u)^T\right).
$$
