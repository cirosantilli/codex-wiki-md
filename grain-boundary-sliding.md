# Grain-boundary sliding

↑ **Parent:** [Creep (deformation)](creep-deformation.md)

Relative tangential motion of neighbouring [crystal grains](grain-in-a-polycrystal.md) along their boundary, requiring accommodation to avoid incompatibility and damage. It can contribute to high-temperature [creep](creep-deformation.md).
