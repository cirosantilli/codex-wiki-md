# Second vertical moment of a solid ring torus

↑ **Parent:** [Ring torus](ring-torus.md)

A [solid torus](solid-torus.md) of major radius $A$ and minor radius $R<A$, centred on the horizontal plane, has volume $2\pi^2AR^2$. In [circular torus coordinates](circular-torus-coordinates.md), $z=r\cos\alpha$. Integrating $z^2$ with the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $r(A+r\sin\alpha)$ gives $\int_Vz^2dV=(\pi^2/2)AR^4$; the part odd in $\sin\alpha$ vanishes. Thus the mean squared height is $R^2/4$. The identity is useful for [moments of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) and [flux](flux.md) calculations using the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md).
