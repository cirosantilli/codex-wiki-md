# Inbreeding

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inbreeding)

Inbreeding is reproduction between genetically related individuals. It increases the chance that an offspring's maternal and paternal copies at a locus are [identical by descent](identity-by-descent.md). Such homozygosity by descent is autozygosity.
