# Corners theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Corner in an integer grid](corner-in-an-integer-grid.md)

For every $\delta>0$, all sufficiently large integers $n$ have the property that every $A\subseteq[n]^2$ with $|A|\geq\delta n^2$ contains a [corner in an integer grid](corner-in-an-integer-grid.md). The [tripartite graph encoding of a grid](tripartite-graph-encoding-of-a-grid.md) turns the absence of a corner into a family of many [edge-disjoint triangles](edge-disjoint-triangles.md) but only quadratically many total [triangles in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md), contradicting the [triangle removal lemma](triangle-removal-lemma.md).
