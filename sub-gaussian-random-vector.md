# Sub-Gaussian random vector

↑ **Parent:** [Sub-Gaussian distribution](sub-gaussian-distribution.md)

A centered random vector is sub-Gaussian with variance proxy $\tau^2$ when the displayed inequality holds for every deterministic vector $a$. Equivalently every scalar inner product is a [sub-Gaussian random variable](sub-gaussian-distribution.md) with proxy $\tau^2\|a\|_2^2$. Independence of coordinates is not required. For vectors $a_j$ with [Euclidean norms](euclidean-norm.md) at most one, the [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) gives $\max_j|\langle a_j,g\rangle|\leq\tau\sqrt{2\log(2m/\delta)}$ with [probability](probability.md) at least $1-\delta$. This assumption alone is weaker than concentration for all Lipschitz functions.
