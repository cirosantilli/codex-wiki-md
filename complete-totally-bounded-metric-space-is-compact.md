# Complete totally bounded metric space is compact

↑ **Parent:** [Compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md)

A metric space is compact exactly when it is both [complete](completeness.md) and [totally bounded](totally-bounded-space.md). For the nontrivial direction, successively choose a point from a nested sequence of finite $2^{-n}$-nets; a diagonal construction gives a Cauchy subsequence of every sequence, and completeness gives its limit.
