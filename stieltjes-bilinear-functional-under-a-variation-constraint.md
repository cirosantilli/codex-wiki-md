# Stieltjes bilinear functional under a variation constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Hadamard differentiability](hadamard-differentiability.md)

On bounded continuously differentiable functions with uniformly bounded [total variation of a function](total-variation-of-a-function.md), the map $T$ is [Hadamard differentiable](hadamard-differentiability.md) in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) with [derivative](derivative.md) $\dot T_{F,G}(a,b)=\int_0^ubF^{\prime}+G(u)a(u)-G(0)a(0)-\int_0^uG^{\prime}a$. Use [weak convergence of bounded-variation integrators](weak-convergence-of-bounded-variation-integrators.md) for the first term and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) for the remaining terms. No [derivatives](derivative.md) of the limiting continuous directions are required.
