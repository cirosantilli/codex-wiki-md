# Normal surface singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Normal variety](normal-variety.md)

A singular point on a normal two-dimensional [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) has an integrally closed local domain which is not a regular local ring. A [rational double point](rational-double-point.md) is an important isolated example with a crepant minimal resolution.
