# Indexed lambda term

↑ **Parent:** [Graph model of lambda calculus](graph-model-of-lambda-calculus.md)

An indexed [lambda term](lambda-term.md) assigns a natural number to every subterm occurrence; one also admits a constant $\bot$ with empty interpretation. In the [graph model of lambda calculus](graph-model-of-lambda-calculus.md), every syntax constructor is interpreted as usual and then intersected with $\{0,\ldots,n\}$ at its occurrence index $n$. All indexed interpretations are finite subsets of the unindexed interpretation, and their directed union recovers that interpretation.
