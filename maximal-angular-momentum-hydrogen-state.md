# Maximal-angular-momentum hydrogen state

↑ **Parent:** [Radial Schrodinger equation for the hydrogen atom](radial-schrodinger-equation-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md)

A [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) state with principal quantum number $n=l+1$ has no radial nodes and radial factor $R(r)=Ar^l e^{-r/[a(l+1)]}$, where $a$ is the [Bohr radius](bohr-radius.md). Its energy is $-\hbar^2/[2ma^2(l+1)^2]$. With unit-normalized [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md), the radial normalization gives $|A|^2=2^{2l+3}/[a^{2l+3}(l+1)^{2l+3}(2l+2)!]$.
