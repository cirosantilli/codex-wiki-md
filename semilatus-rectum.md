# Semilatus rectum

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)

The distance from a focus to a [conic section](conic-section.md) along a line perpendicular to its symmetry axis. In focus-centred [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md), an [ellipse](ellipse.md) may be written $r(1-e\cos\theta)=\ell$, where $e$ is its [eccentricity of a conic](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md).

The distance from a focus of a [conic section](conic-section.md) to the curve measured perpendicular to its principal axis. In the focus-based [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) equation $r=p/(1+e\cos\phi)$ it is the [length](length.md) $p$, where $e$ is the [eccentricity](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md). For an [ellipse](ellipse.md), $p=a(1-e^2)=b^2/a$. For a Newtonian [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md), $p=j^2/(GM)$ with $j$ the [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md).
