# Semilatus rectum

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)

The distance from a focus to a [conic section](conic-section.md) along a line perpendicular to its symmetry axis. In focus-centred [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md), an [ellipse](ellipse.md) may be written $r(1-e\cos\theta)=\ell$, where $e$ is its [eccentricity of a conic](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md).
