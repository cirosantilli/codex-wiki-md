# Exponential map of a matrix Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix Lie group](matrix-lie-group.md)

For a [Matrix Lie group](matrix-lie-group.md) $G$ with Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, its exponential map is the restriction of the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md), $X\mapsto e^X$. It maps $\mathfrak g$ into $G$ and is a local diffeomorphism at zero, but it need not be surjective globally.
