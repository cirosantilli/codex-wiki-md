# Frequency-modulation atomic force microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

Dynamic [atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md) using the resonance-frequency change of an oscillating cantilever as the interaction-sensitive signal. Small-amplitude oscillations and weak force gradients give the [small-amplitude AFM frequency shift](small-amplitude-afm-frequency-shift.md); larger amplitudes require an averaged interaction analysis.
