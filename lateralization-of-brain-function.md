# Lateralization of brain function

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscience](neuroscience-split.md)

A functional asymmetry between [cerebral hemispheres](cerebral-hemisphere.md), measured for a stated task, contrast or operation. Population-level left dominance does not imply complete right-side inactivity or an identical pattern in every individual.
