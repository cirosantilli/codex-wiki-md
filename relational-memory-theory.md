# Relational memory theory

↑ **Parent:** [Relational memory](relational-memory.md)

An account assigning the [hippocampus](hippocampus.md) a role in organizing related experiences and permitting their flexible expression. The proposed computation can operate on spatial and nonspatial information. It is a theory whose parts require tests, rather than a synonym for every task affected by [hippocampal](hippocampus.md) damage.[1999, proposed organization and flexible expression of relational representations](the-hippocampus-and-mechanisms-of-declarative-memory-by-howard-eichenbaum.md)
