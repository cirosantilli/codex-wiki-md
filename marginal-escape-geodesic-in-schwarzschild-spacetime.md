# Marginal escape geodesic in Schwarzschild spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Local radial speed of a Schwarzschild geodesic](local-radial-speed-of-a-schwarzschild-geodesic.md)

The outgoing radial [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) with zero [speed](speed.md) at infinity has $k=1$ and $dr/d\tau=\sqrt{2GM/r}$. Thus $r(\tau)=[r_0^{3/2}+(3/2)\sqrt{2GM}\tau]^{2/3}$ and the local static-observer [speed](speed.md) is $\sqrt{2GM/r}$.
