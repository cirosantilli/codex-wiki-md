# Mixing length

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)

A mixing length is the characteristic transverse displacement over which a turbulent parcel retains its streamwise momentum before mixing with its surroundings. Taylor expansion of a mean velocity profile gives a fluctuation of magnitude $l|U_y|$. Combining comparable transverse fluctuations with down-gradient [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md) transport gives the closure $\overline{u'v'}=-l^2|U_y|U_y$. This is a phenomenological approximation, not a consequence of the [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md). In a free jet, a prescribed local [mixing length](mixing-length.md) must also be compatible with centreline regularity and vanishing outer momentum flux.
