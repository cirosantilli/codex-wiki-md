# Sago spleen

↑ **Parent:** [Amyloidosis](amyloidosis.md)

A gross pattern of [amyloidosis](amyloidosis.md) of the [spleen](spleen.md) in which translucent deposits in the [splenic white pulp](splenic-white-pulp.md) resemble sago grains. The pattern can accompany different forms of [amyloidosis](amyloidosis.md).[Abstract, sago pattern and limits of anatomical typing](hepatic-amyloidosis-morphologic-differences-between-systemic-al-and-aa-types.md)
