# Locally connected space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_connected_space)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) has [local connectedness](locally-connected-space.md) when every point has a [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) basis of [connected](connected-space.md) [open sets](open-set.md). In particular, a planar [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) has [local connectedness](locally-connected-space.md), but its [domain boundary](boundary-of-a-domain.md) need not be. [Local connectedness](locally-connected-space.md) of the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) is the condition governing a continuous extension of a [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md) to the parametrizing circle.
