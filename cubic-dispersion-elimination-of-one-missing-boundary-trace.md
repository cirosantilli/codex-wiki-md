# Cubic-dispersion elimination of one missing boundary trace

↑ **Parent:** [Dispersion symmetry elimination of a boundary trace](dispersion-symmetry-elimination-of-a-boundary-trace.md)

For the [backward-sign Airy half-line global relation](backward-sign-airy-half-line-global-relation.md), suppose exactly one trace of derivative order $j\in\{0,1,2\}$ is unknown. Its spectral multiplier is a constant times $k^r$, $r=2-j$. Use the [rotated null contours for half-range Fourier inversion](rotated-null-contours-for-half-range-fourier-inversion.md) with the displayed coefficients. The unknown contribution becomes an integral over the boundary of the middle upper sector $\pi/3<\arg k<2\pi/3$. There $\operatorname{Im}k^3<0$, so

$$
e^{-ik^3t}F_j(k,t)=\int_0^t e^{-ik^3(t-s)}f_j(s)\,ds
$$

is bounded, while $e^{ikx}$ decays exponentially. The [Cauchy integral theorem](cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) therefore removes that entire unknown contribution. The resulting solution depends on the initial transform and the two prescribed boundary transforms only.
