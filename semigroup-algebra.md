# Semigroup algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)

For an additive [semigroup](semigroup.md) $D$ with identity zero, the semigroup algebra $k[D]$ has a $k$-basis of formal symbols $t^q$, $q\in D$, and multiplication $t^qt^{q'}=t^{q+q'}$. For $D=\mathbb Z[1/2]_{\ge0}$ it is the [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $\bigcup_{h\ge0}k[t^{1/2^h}]$. Its positive-exponent [ideal](ideal.md) is a nonzero [idempotent ideal](idempotent-ideal.md), and the strictly ascending chain $(t)\subsetneq(t^{1/2})\subsetneq\cdots$ shows that the algebra is not a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md).
