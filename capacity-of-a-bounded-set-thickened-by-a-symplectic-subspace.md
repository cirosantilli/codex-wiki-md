# Capacity of a bounded set thickened by a symplectic subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic cylinder](symplectic-cylinder.md)

If $U$ is nonempty, bounded and open in standard symplectic Euclidean space, and $W$ is a codimension-two [symplectic subspace](symplectic-subspace.md), a linear symplectic change of coordinates makes $W=\{q_1=p_1=0\}$. Boundedness of the remaining coordinate projection places $U+W$ inside a finite-radius [symplectic cylinder](symplectic-cylinder.md). Openness puts a small ball inside it. Monotonicity and normalization of a [symplectic capacity](symplectic-capacity.md) give the displayed strict bounds.
