# Vlasov equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vlasov_equation)

The [Vlasov equation](vlasov-equation.md) describes collisionless evolution of a phase-space [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) in a self-consistent force field: $\partial_t f+\mathbf v\cdot\nabla_xf+\mathbf F\cdot\nabla_pf=0$. The force can be electromagnetic or gravitational; the [Collisionless dark-matter Vlasov equation](collisionless-dark-matter-vlasov-equation.md) is the cosmological gravitational specialization.
