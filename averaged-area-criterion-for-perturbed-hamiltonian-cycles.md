# Averaged area criterion for perturbed Hamiltonian cycles

↑ **Parent:** [Averaged first-integral obstruction to persistence of a periodic orbit](averaged-first-integral-obstruction-to-persistence-of-a-periodic-orbit.md)

For a planar [Hamiltonian system](hamiltonian-system.md) with vector field $(H_y/2,-H_x/2)$ perturbed by $\varepsilon(\widehat\mu-r^2)(x,y)$, the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) expresses the leading drift of the [first integral](first-integral.md) around a closed [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) as

$$
\oint H_t\,dt=4\varepsilon\left[\widehat\mu|\mathcal D_h|-2\int_{\mathcal D_h}r^2\,dA\right].
$$

A vanishing drift is a necessary first-order selection condition for a persisting [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md). A simple zero with outward drift on its inner side and inward drift on its outer side yields an attracting [limit cycle](limit-cycle.md). In the [Hamiltonian limit of three-to-one forcing](hamiltonian-limit-of-three-to-one-forcing.md), the separatrix triangle has mean $r^2=1/4$, so the leading separatrix flux vanishes at $\widehat\mu=1/2$. A homoclinic or heteroclinic transition still requires the separatrix splitting and higher-order corrections to be controlled.
