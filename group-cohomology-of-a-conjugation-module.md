# Group cohomology of a conjugation module

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugation module of a group ring](conjugation-module-of-a-group-ring.md)

For a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$ and representatives $g_i$ of its [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md), [Shapiro's lemma](shapiro-s-lemma.md) gives

$$
H^n(G,\mathbb ZG_{\mathrm{conj}})
\cong\bigoplus_iH^n(C_G(g_i),\mathbb Z).
$$
