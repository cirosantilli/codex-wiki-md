# Hot band

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)

A transition starting from a thermally populated vibrationally excited state. Its intensity depends on the lower-state population through the [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md). [Anharmonicity](anharmonicity.md) and coupling between [normal modes](normal-mode.md) can shift it away from the corresponding [vibrational fundamental](vibrational-fundamental.md).
