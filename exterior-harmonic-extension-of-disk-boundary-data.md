# Exterior harmonic extension of disk boundary data

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic extension](harmonic-extension.md)

For bounded piecewise continuous [Dirichlet boundary data](dirichlet-boundary-data.md) $f$ on a circle of radius $R$, write its [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) as $a_0+\sum_{n\geq1}[a_n\cos(n\theta)+b_n\sin(n\theta)]$. The displayed series gives the bounded exterior [harmonic extension](harmonic-extension.md) for $r>R$. The radial [inversion in a circle](inversion-in-a-circle.md) $r\mapsto R^2/r$ converts it into the ordinary interior [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel.md) solution. Values at discontinuities are understood through the usual boundary trace, with midpoint radial limits at jumps; changing the assigned values at finitely many boundary points does not change the extension.

The limit at infinity is forced to equal the angular mean $a_0=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(\theta)d\theta$. Indeed the angular mean of any exterior [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) solves $(r\overline u')'=0$, and boundedness excludes its logarithmic term. Equivalently, inversion makes infinity an isolated removable singularity of a bounded planar [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md). Uniqueness follows from interior uniqueness after inversion, including the [logarithmic cutoff proof of harmonic uniqueness with finitely many boundary exceptions](logarithmic-cutoff-proof-of-harmonic-uniqueness-with-finitely-many-boundary-exceptions.md) when needed.
