<h1 id="two-site-support-of-the-pauli-matrix-affleck-kennedy-lieb-tasaki-tensor">Two-site support of the Pauli-matrix Affleck--Kennedy--Lieb--Tasaki tensor</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli-matrix representation of the Affleck--Kennedy--Lieb--Tasaki state](pauli-matrix-representation-of-the-affleck-kennedy-lieb-tasaki-state.md)

Because $\sigma_i\sigma_j=\delta_{ij}I+i\varepsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k$, the two-site image of the Pauli-matrix AKLT tensor is the direct sum of the scalar and antisymmetric subspaces, which have total spin zero and one. Its orthogonal complement is the symmetric traceless total-spin-two subspace.
