# Occupation number

↑ **Parent:** [Fock space](fock-space.md)

The eigenvalue of a mode number operator counts excitations in that mode. [Bosonic occupation numbers](bosonic-occupation-number.md) range over all nonnegative integers; [fermionic occupation numbers](fermionic-occupation-number.md) are zero or one.
