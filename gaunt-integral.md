# Gaunt integral

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md)

The integral of three [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md) on the sphere. In the conventional complex basis it is real and equals a product of two [Wigner 3j symbols](3-j-symbol.md) and $\sqrt{(2\ell_1+1)(2\ell_2+1)(2\ell_3+1)/(4\pi)}$. It vanishes unless the multipoles satisfy a triangle condition, $\ell_1+\ell_2+\ell_3$ is even, and $m_1+m_2+m_3=0$. It carries the angular geometry of the [reduced CMB bispectrum](reduced-cmb-bispectrum.md).
