# Factorization of polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factorization_of_polynomials)

A [polynomial factorization](factorization-of-polynomials.md) expresses a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) as a product of other [polynomials](polynomial-split.md). The allowed [coefficients](coefficient.md) matter: $X^2-2$ has no nonconstant factorization over the [rational numbers](rational-number.md), but factors as $(X-\sqrt2)(X+\sqrt2)$ over the [real numbers](real-number.md). [Difference of squares](difference-of-two-squares.md) gives $X^2-Y^2=(X-Y)(X+Y)$ over any [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md).
