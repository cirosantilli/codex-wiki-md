# Schwarzschild circular-orbit local speed

↑ **Parent:** [Timelike circular Schwarzschild geodesic](timelike-circular-schwarzschild-geodesic.md)

A [Schwarzschild static observer](schwarzschild-static-observer.md) at a circular orbit measures speed $v_{\rm loc}=r(d\phi/dt)/\sqrt{1-2\mu/r}$. Combining the circular [geodesic](geodesic.md) [frequency](frequency.md) with this clock rate gives the displayed formula. The observer-relative [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) is $\gamma=\sqrt{(r-2\mu)/(r-3\mu)}$, and $r\,d\phi/d\tau=\gamma v_{\rm loc}$. Thus the local speed is $c/2$ at the Schwarzschild [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md). Coordinate and locally measured speeds are distinct.
