# Ornstein-Uhlenbeck moment equations

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Fokker-Planck equation](ornstein-uhlenbeck-fokker-planck-equation.md)

[Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) against $1$, $v_i$ and $|v|^2/2$ gives these equations for [mass](mass.md), unnormalized [momentum](momentum.md) and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). With $M_0\ne0$, the mean is $u(t)=e^{-t}u_0$, and $E(t)=dM_0/2+(E_0-dM_0/2)e^{-2t}$. Normalizing by [mass](mass.md) is invalid if that [mass](mass.md) is zero.
