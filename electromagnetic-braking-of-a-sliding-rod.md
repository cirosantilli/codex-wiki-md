# Electromagnetic braking of a sliding rod

↑ **Parent:** [Motional electromotive force](motional-electromotive-force.md)

A conducting rod sliding on rails separated by $\ell$ in a transverse magnetic field has induced current magnitude $B\ell v/R$ when self-inductance is neglected and resistance is $R$. The [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) opposes motion with magnitude $B^2\ell^2v/R$, so free motion decays exponentially on the displayed time constant. [Joule heating](joule-heating.md) integrates to the rod's lost kinetic energy. Current direction follows the [Lenz's law](lenz-s-law.md).
