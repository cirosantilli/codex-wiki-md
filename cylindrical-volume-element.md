# Cylindrical volume element

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical coordinate system](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md)

For $x=r\cos\phi$, $y=r\sin\phi$ and unchanged $z$, the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) of Cartesian coordinates with respect to [cylindrical coordinates](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) is $r$. Hence a [volume integral](volume-integral.md) acquires the displayed factor for $r\geq0$. The inverse determinant is $1/r$ where $r>0$; the axis is a coordinate degeneracy.
