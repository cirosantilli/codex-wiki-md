# Pancreatic secretory-protein pulse-chase experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Secretory protein routing](secretory-protein-routing.md)

In 1967, [James D. Jamieson](james-d-jamieson.md) and [George E. Palade](george-e-palade.md) incubated guinea pig [pancreas](pancreas.md) slices, following a three-minute pulse of tritium-labeled [leucine](leucine.md) with excess unlabeled [leucine](leucine.md). This [pulse-chase analysis](pulse-chase-analysis.md) marked newly made [proteins](protein-split.md) while reducing subsequent incorporation. Fixed, Epon-embedded thin sections were mounted on coated grids, covered with Ilford L-4 photographic emulsion, developed and stained for [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md). [Autoradiography](autoradiography.md) located photographic grains relative to [pancreatic acinar cell](pancreatic-acinar-cell.md) structures.[Jamieson and Palade, Intracellular Transport of Secretory Proteins in the Pancreatic Exocrine Cell. II. Transport to Condensing Vacuoles and Zymogen Granules (1967), p. 598 Methods; Figure 10: three-minute pulse](intracellular-transport-of-secretory-proteins-in-the-pancreatic-exocrine-cell-ii-transport-to-condensing-vacuoles-and-zymogen-granules-by-james-d-jamieson-and-george-e-palade.md)

Pulse-end samples provided a temporal reference: label lay mainly over [rough endoplasmic reticulum](rough-endoplasmic-reticulum.md); later samples shifted toward peripheral [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md) elements, condensing vacuoles and [secretory granules](secretory-granule.md). As a contamination check, isolated granule pellets were also examined by [autoradiography](autoradiography.md): early pellet labeling chiefly marked condensing vacuoles. Grids from the compared pellet samples were coated together to control photographic processing. This supported transport of a previously labeled cohort rather than separate synthesis at each location. Fixed samples did not track individual [proteins](protein-split.md); localization was finite, and labeling was not absolutely exclusive to secretory cargo. The result concerns this preparation, not every [protein](protein-split.md) in every [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md).[pp. 603–606 and Figures 10–13: changing localization; pp. 610–611 and Figures 14–15, Table III: pellet check and matched emulsion processing; p. 611 General Premises: nonexportable-protein labeling](intracellular-transport-of-secretory-proteins-in-the-pancreatic-exocrine-cell-ii-transport-to-condensing-vacuoles-and-zymogen-granules-by-james-d-jamieson-and-george-e-palade.md)
