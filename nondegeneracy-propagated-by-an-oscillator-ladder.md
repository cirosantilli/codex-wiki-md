# Nondegeneracy propagated by an oscillator ladder

↑ **Parent:** [Creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md)

For the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) with $a_-=P-im\omega X$, $a_+=a_-^*$ and $\gamma=2m\hbar\omega$, factorization gives $a_+a_-=2m(H-E_0)$, where $E_0=\hbar\omega/2$. For an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) $\psi$ at $E_n=E_0+n\hbar\omega$,

$$
\|(a_-)^n\psi\|^2=\gamma^n n!\|\psi\|^2,\qquad (a_+)^n(a_-)^n\psi=\gamma^n n!\psi.
$$

The first identity shows that lowering to the [ground state](ground-state.md) is injective on the $E_n$ [eigenspace](eigenspace.md); the second shows how to invert it by raising. If the ground [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) is one-dimensional, $(a_-)^n\psi=c\psi_0$ and therefore $\psi=c(a_+)^n\psi_0/(\gamma^n n!)$. Thus every level is also nondegenerate. This proof explicitly rules out a second independent [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) at an excited level rather than merely constructing one ladder of states.
