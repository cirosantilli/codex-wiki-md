# Least-number principle

↑ **Parent:** [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md)

Every nonempty definable set of natural numbers has a least element. Over the usual arithmetic language, this principle is equivalent to the induction scheme.

The [well-ordering principle](well-ordering-principle-for-the-natural-numbers.md) quantifies over all nonempty subsets of the natural numbers; in first-order arithmetic this entry is its definable-set schema.
