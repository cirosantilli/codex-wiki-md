# Tensor-product weight diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Weight diagram](weight-diagram.md)

For a [tensor product of Lie algebra representations](tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md) with [weight-space decompositions](weight-space-decomposition.md), the [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) add and their [weight multiplicities](weight-multiplicity.md) convolve. A diagram must sum contributions landing at the same weight, rather than treating coincident points as distinct positions.
