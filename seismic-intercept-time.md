# Seismic intercept time

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic ray parameter in horizontal layering](seismic-ray-parameter-in-horizontal-layering.md)

A ray's intercept time subtracts its tangent travel-time contribution at the receiver range. For a smooth branch, $dT/dx=p$ implies $d\tau/dp=-x$. Thus an intercept-time curve encodes range as its negative slope. Separate turning branches around a [low-velocity zone](low-velocity-zone.md) must be retained rather than joined by fictitious rays.
