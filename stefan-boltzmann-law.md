<h1 id="stefan-boltzmann-law">Stefan–Boltzmann law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Heat](heat.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan–Boltzmann_law)

The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives the thermal-radiation flux emitted by a surface as $F=\epsilon\sigma T^4$, where $\epsilon$ is its [emissivity](emissivity.md) and $\sigma$ is the Stefan–Boltzmann constant.
