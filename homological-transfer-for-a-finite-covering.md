# Homological transfer for a finite covering

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

For an $n$-sheeted [covering space](covering-space.md) $f:X\to Y$, define the transfer of a [singular simplex](singular-simplex.md) as the sum of its $n$ lifts. Restriction of these lifts to any face gives exactly the lifts of that face, so transfer is a [chain map](chain-map.md). Its induced map $\operatorname{tr}:H_i(Y;R)\to H_i(X;R)$ satisfies $f_*\operatorname{tr}=n\,\mathrm{id}$ for any commutative coefficient ring $R$. If $n$ is invertible in $R$, then $n^{-1}\operatorname{tr}$ is a right inverse of $f_*$. In particular a finite covering surjects onto the base's rational [homology](homology-split.md).
