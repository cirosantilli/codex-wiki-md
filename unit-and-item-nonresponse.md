# Unit and item nonresponse

↑ **Parent:** [Missing data](missing-data.md)

Item nonresponse leaves a particular measurement unobserved while retaining other measurements on that individual. Unit nonresponse leaves all survey measurements unavailable. Adjusting for an observed auxiliary variable can address item nonresponse under [missing at random](missing-at-random.md), but does not identify the auxiliary-variable distribution among unit nonresponders. Extrapolation to them needs further assumptions or external information.
