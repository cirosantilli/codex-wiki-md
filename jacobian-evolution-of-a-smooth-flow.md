# Jacobian evolution of a smooth flow

↑ **Parent:** [Flow map](flow-map.md)

For a smooth [flow map](flow-map.md) $\varphi_t$ generated by a [vector field](vector-field.md) $F$, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) and $\det(I+tDF)=1+t\operatorname{tr}(DF)+o(t)$ give $\partial_tJ[\varphi_t](x)=(\nabla\cdot F)(\varphi_t(x))J[\varphi_t](x)$. With initial value one, its solution is the exponential of the time integral of this [divergence](divergence.md). Thus a divergence-free generator preserves Euclidean volume and orientation. This connects the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) with a [volume-preserving vector field](volume-preserving-vector-field.md).
