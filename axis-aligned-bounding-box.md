# Axis-aligned bounding box

↑ **Parent:** [Bounding volume](bounding-volume.md)

An [axis-aligned bounding box](axis-aligned-bounding-box.md) is the product of coordinate intervals containing an object. Overlap requires overlap in every coordinate interval.
