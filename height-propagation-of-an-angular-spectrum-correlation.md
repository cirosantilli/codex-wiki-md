# Height propagation of an angular-spectrum correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Outgoing angular spectrum](outgoing-angular-spectrum.md)

Use $\widehat\psi(\nu)=\int\psi(x,0)e^{-i\nu x}dx$ and $q(\nu)=\sqrt{k^2-\nu^2}$ on the upward branch. If the uncentered field correlation is translationally homogeneous and $\langle\widehat\psi(\nu)\widehat\psi(\nu')^*\rangle=2\pi S(\nu)\delta(\nu-\nu')$, its correlation at height $z$ is the displayed integral. The propagating spectrum is unchanged; evanescent contributions decrease as $e^{-2\sqrt{\nu^2-k^2}z}$. A [spectral measure of a stationary random field](spectral-measure-of-a-stationary-random-field.md) permits discrete coherent components. Without correlation homogeneity a double spectral covariance integral depends on both observation coordinates rather than only their separation.
