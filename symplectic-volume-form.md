# Symplectic volume form

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic volume](symplectic-volume.md)

The top-degree [differential form](differential-form-split.md) $\omega^n/n!$ on a $2n$-dimensional [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md). Every [canonical transformation](canonical-transformation.md) preserves this form. For $n>1$, preservation of this volume alone does not imply preservation of the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md).
