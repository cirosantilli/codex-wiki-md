# Dual conic

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth plane conic](smooth-plane-conic.md)

The tangent lines of a [smooth plane conic](smooth-plane-conic.md) form a [smooth plane conic](smooth-plane-conic.md) in the [dual projective space](dual-projective-space.md). If the original equation is $v^{\mathsf T}Qv=0$, its tangent has coefficient vector $Qv$, giving the displayed equation. The matrix $Q$ is symmetric and invertible, so this correspondence is a projective linear isomorphism of the conics.
