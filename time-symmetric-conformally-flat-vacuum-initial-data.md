# Time-symmetric conformally flat vacuum initial data

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian constraint](hamiltonian-constraint.md)

For a spatial initial slice with vanishing [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md), the vacuum [momentum constraint](momentum-constraint.md) is automatic and the [Hamiltonian constraint](hamiltonian-constraint.md) requires zero [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md). In three spatial dimensions, the indicated [conformally flat metric](conformally-flat-metric.md) has $\mathcal R=-8\psi^{-5}\Delta\psi$, with a nonzero [conformal factor](conformal-factor.md). Thus a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) supplies the data on its domain. A positive example is $\psi=1+a/r$ on a punctured or exterior region. There is no nonconstant globally smooth example on all of Euclidean three-space tending to one at infinity, by the [maximum principle for harmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md). Time symmetry of an initial slice does not imply that its entire evolved spacetime is static.
