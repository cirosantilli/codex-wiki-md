# Independent sets in every large subset of a dense random graph

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic number of a binomial random graph](chromatic-number-of-a-binomial-random-graph.md)

For fixed $p$ and $\gamma>0$, [Janson inequality](janson-inequality.md) bounds the failure probability for a given large [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) subset by $\exp(-c|U|^2/(\log n)^4)$. A union bound over all at most $2^n$ subsets still tends to zero. This uniform statement is essential because [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) sets left by a greedy colouring procedure depend on the [graph](graph-split.md); one cannot assume each adaptively chosen remainder is an independent fresh random [graph](graph-split.md).
