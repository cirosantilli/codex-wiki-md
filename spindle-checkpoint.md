# Spindle checkpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Mitosis](mitosis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spindle_checkpoint)

The [spindle checkpoint](spindle-checkpoint.md) can delay [anaphase](anaphase.md) while [kinetochore](kinetochore.md) attachment is incomplete. This couples progression to the state of the [spindle apparatus](spindle-apparatus.md), rather than requiring every [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) to separate its [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) after a fixed waiting time. Alignment in a picture alone is not a demonstration that this control is satisfied.[Biology 2e, §10.3, Regulation at Internal Checkpoints, Figure 10.11 and The M Checkpoint](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/control-of-the-cell-cycle.md)
