# Effective gravity in a rotating frame

↑ **Parent:** [Effective potential](effective-potential.md)

Effective gravity in a uniformly rotating frame is minus the [gradient](gradient.md) of the sum of the gravitational and centrifugal potentials. A fluid surface in rotational equilibrium is an [equipotential surface](equipotential-surface.md) and is therefore normal to this effective gravity.
