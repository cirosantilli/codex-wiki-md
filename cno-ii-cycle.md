# CNO-II cycle

↑ **Parent:** [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md)

A branch of the [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md) proceeds through proton captures on nitrogen-15 and oxygen-16, [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md) of fluorine-17, and ${}^{17}\mathrm O(p,\alpha){}^{14}\mathrm N$. Returning through oxygen-15 and nitrogen-15 closes the cycle. It also catalyzes the conversion of four protons into helium-4, with branch-dependent [neutrino](neutrino.md) loss.
