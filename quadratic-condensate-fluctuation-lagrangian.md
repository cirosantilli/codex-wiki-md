# Quadratic condensate fluctuation Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md)

Expand a repulsive [Bose-Einstein condensate](bose-einstein-condensate.md) around the real uniform saddle $\psi_0=\sqrt{\mu/g}$ as $\psi=\psi_0+\chi$. After removing a constant and a temporal [boundary term](boundary-term.md), the quadratic [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) is the displayed expression. Its equation $i\hbar\dot\chi=-\hbar^2\nabla^2\chi/(2M)+\mu(\chi+\chi^*)$ mixes a fluctuation and its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md). Writing $\chi=\chi_1+i\chi_2$ gives canonically paired amplitude and phase fluctuations and one positive [Bogoliubov quasiparticle dispersion](bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md) branch, $\hbar\omega=\sqrt{\epsilon_k(\epsilon_k+2\mu)}$ with $\epsilon_k=\hbar^2k^2/(2M)$. The phase direction is a [Goldstone mode](goldstone-boson.md); its zero uniform restoring force does not produce a second independent positive-frequency amplitude branch in this first-order-in-time theory.
