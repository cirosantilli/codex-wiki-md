# Amplification polynomial of a multistep method

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic polynomials of a linear multistep method](characteristic-polynomials-of-a-linear-multistep-method.md)

Applying a [linear multistep method](linear-multistep-method.md) to the [Dahlquist test equation](dahlquist-test-equation.md) with $z=h\lambda$ produces a constant-coefficient recurrence whose modes satisfy $P_z(w)=0$. [Absolute stability](linear-stability-domain.md) requires the resulting [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) to lie in the unit disk, with unit-modulus roots simple. Thus multistep stability depends on every recurrence mode, rather than a single one-step [stability function](stability-function.md).
