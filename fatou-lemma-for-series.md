# Fatou lemma for series

↑ **Parent:** [Fatou's lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md)

For nonnegative numbers $a_{n,k}$, [Fatou lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) applied to counting measure gives

$$
\sum_k\liminf_{n\to\infty}a_{n,k}
\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}\sum_ka_{n,k}.
$$
