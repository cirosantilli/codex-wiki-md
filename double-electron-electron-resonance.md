# Double electron-electron resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

[double electron-electron resonance](double-electron-electron-resonance.md) is a pulsed [electron paramagnetic resonance](electron-paramagnetic-resonance.md) method that uses spin–spin coupling to constrain distances between labels. Label conformation, distance distributions and background correction affect the structural interpretation.
