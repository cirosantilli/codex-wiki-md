# First integral of polytropic spherical-bubble motion

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh equation for an inviscid spherical bubble](rayleigh-equation-for-an-inviscid-spherical-bubble.md)

For gas pressure depending only on radius and initial speed zero, multiplying the [Rayleigh equation for an inviscid spherical bubble](rayleigh-equation-for-an-inviscid-spherical-bubble.md) by $2R^2\dot R$ integrates to the displayed identity. A polytropic pressure $p_g=K/R^{3\kappa}$ gives elementary power integrals when $\kappa\ne1$, and a logarithm when $\kappa=1$. Turning radii are the positive zeros of this first integral. At $\kappa=2$ and $p_g(R_0)=p_\infty/2$, they are $R_0$ and $R_0/2^{1/3}$; the inward and outward accelerations at them have opposite signs, giving bounded undamped oscillation in the [ideal](ideal.md) model.
