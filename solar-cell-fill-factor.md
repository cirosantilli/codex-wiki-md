# Solar-cell fill factor

↑ **Parent:** [Solar cell](solar-cell.md)

The [solar-cell fill factor](solar-cell-fill-factor.md) compares the maximum delivered electrical [power](power.md) with the rectangle formed by the open-circuit [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) and short-circuit [electric current](electric-current.md). It measures the shape of the illuminated [current-voltage characteristic](current-voltage-characteristic.md). The conversion efficiency is $\eta=V_{\rm oc}I_{\rm sc}\mathrm{FF}/P_{\rm in}$.
