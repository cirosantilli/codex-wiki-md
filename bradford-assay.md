# Bradford assay

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

A dye-binding estimate of [protein](protein-split.md) concentration using a calibration series and absorbance near $595\,\mathrm{nm}$. Protein-dependent dye response, buffer interference and blank treatment limit how directly a [bovine serum albumin](bovine-serum-albumin.md) standard estimates another [protein](protein-split.md).
