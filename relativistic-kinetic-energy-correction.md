# Relativistic kinetic-energy correction

↑ **Parent:** [Fine structure](fine-structure.md)

Expanding the [relativistic kinetic energy](relativistic-kinetic-energy.md) $\sqrt{m^2c^4+c^2p^2}-mc^2$ for $|p|\ll mc$ gives $p^2/(2m)-p^4/(8m^3c^2)+\cdots$. The first correction is negative and contributes to atomic [fine structure](fine-structure.md). Its perturbative use requires small momentum compared with mc; the truncated negative quartic term is not a globally stable exact Hamiltonian.
