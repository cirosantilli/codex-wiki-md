# Capillary pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Surface tension](surface-tension-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capillary_pressure)

Capillary pressure is the pressure jump produced by a curved interface. For constant surface tension it is proportional to the interface curvature according to the [Young–Laplace equation](young-laplace-equation.md).
