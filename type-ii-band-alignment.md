# Type-II band alignment

↑ **Parent:** [Heterojunction](heterojunction.md)

A staggered alignment in which the lowest electron states and most favorable hole states occur in different materials. Unlike [type-I band alignment](type-i-band-alignment.md), it can reduce electron–hole spatial overlap. Comparing the two [band gaps](band-gap.md) alone does not determine whether this alignment occurs.
