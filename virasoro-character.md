# Virasoro character

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md)

A [Virasoro character](virasoro-character.md) records the graded dimensions of a positive-energy [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md) module. A state of weight $h+N$ contributes $q^{h+N-c/24}$, and its multiplicity is the dimension of that energy eigenspace. The shift accounts for the [Casimir energy of a two-dimensional conformal field theory](casimir-energy-of-a-two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md). For a [Virasoro Verma module](virasoro-verma-module.md), the character is $q^{h-c/24}\prod_{n\ge1}(1-q^n)^{-1}$, by counting the ordered descendant basis.
