<h1 id="cramer-model">Cramér model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilistic number theory](probabilistic-number-theory.md)

The [Cramér model](cramer-model.md) selects integers $n\ge3$ independently with probability $1/\log n$, and conventionally sets $U_1=0$, $U_2=1$. It is a random model for the distribution of [primes](prime-number.md), not an assertion of independence for actual [prime numbers](prime-number.md).
