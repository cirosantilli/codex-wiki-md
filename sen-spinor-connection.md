# Sen spinor connection

↑ **Parent:** [Witten spinor equation](witten-spinor-equation.md)

Restrict the spacetime [spin connection](spin-connection.md) to a spacelike hypersurface. With future unit normal $n$ and convention $k_{ij}=-g(\nabla_i n,e_j)$, it differs from the intrinsic spin connection by the displayed [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md) term. Its spatial [Dirac operator](dirac-operator.md) is the operator in the [Witten spinor equation](witten-spinor-equation.md); replacing it by the intrinsic operator would omit the ADM momentum contribution.
