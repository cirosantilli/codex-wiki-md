# Diffusion occupation time

↑ **Parent:** [Itô diffusion](ito-diffusion.md)

The time spent by a [Itô diffusion](ito-diffusion.md) in a measurable set $I$ up to time $T$ is $A_T(I)=\int_0^T\mathbf1_I(X_t)\,dt$. Under [stationarity](stationary-process.md) its [expected value](expected-value.md) is $T\mu(I)$, where $\mu$ is the [Invariant distribution of an Itô diffusion](invariant-distribution-of-an-ito-diffusion.md). Occupation times form the denominator of a local [drift estimator](drift-estimator.md).
