# Tensorization of entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration inequality](concentration-inequality.md)

For a nonnegative [function](function-split.md) $Z$ of [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md), entropy is at most the sum of its conditional coordinate entropies. This tensorization is a basic step of the [entropy method](entropy-method-for-certifiable-functions.md).
