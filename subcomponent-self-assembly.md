# Subcomponent self-assembly

↑ **Parent:** [Supramolecular chemistry](supramolecular-chemistry.md)

Reversible formation of covalent ligands and metal coordination bonds from simpler components in the same mixture. [imine](imine.md) condensation around a metal template is a common example.
