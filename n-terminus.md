# N-terminus

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-terminus)

The N-terminus is the amino end of a [protein](protein-split.md) or [amino acid](amino-acid.md) chain. Sequence notation lists residues from the [N-terminus](n-terminus.md) toward the [C-terminus](c-terminus.md), the same direction in which [biological translation](translation-biology.md) builds a chain. The two-residue example under [gene expression](gene-expression.md) places [methionine](methionine.md) at the N-terminal end before any subsequent processing. [OpenStax Biology 2e, 3.4: amino acids and polypeptide direction](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-4-proteins)
