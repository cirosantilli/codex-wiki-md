# Infant-directed speech

↑ **Parent:** [Language acquisition](language-acquisition.md)

Speech addressed to an [infant](infant.md), often differing in [prosody](prosody.md) from adult-directed speech. A listening preference is an experimentally measurable tendency whose magnitude can depend on the stimuli, the [infant](infant.md)’s experience and the measurement method; it is not a universal individual trait.[Advances in Methods and Practices in Psychological Science 3, 24–52; Participants, preregistered analyses and Discussion](quantifying-sources-of-variability-in-infancy-research-using-the-infant-directed-speech-preference-by-the-manybabies-consortium.md)
