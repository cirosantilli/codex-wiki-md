# Weyl lensing potential

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar cosmological perturbation](scalar-cosmological-perturbation.md)

The average of the two [conformal Newtonian gauge](newtonian-gauge.md) potentials controls scalar [gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing.md). A conformal rescaling makes the spatial optical metric flat and its time component $1+4\Psi$ to first order. Vanishing [scalar anisotropic stress](scalar-anisotropic-stress.md) makes $\phi=\psi$ in general relativity, but lensing depends on their sum even when they differ.
