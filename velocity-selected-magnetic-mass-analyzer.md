# Velocity-selected magnetic mass analyzer

↑ **Parent:** [Mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md)

Reuse the [velocity selector](velocity-selector.md) analysis in the [Thomson charge-to-mass experiment](thomson-charge-to-mass-experiment.md), but now send positive [ions](ion.md) with $q=ze$ into a separate magnetic analyzer. The selector fields $E_s$ and $B_s$ give $v_0=E_s/B_s$. If the analyzer field $B_a$ is perpendicular to the selected [velocity](velocity.md), its [gyroradius](gyroradius.md) gives

$$
\frac{m}{q}=\frac{B_a r}{v_0}=\frac{B_aB_s r}{E_s},\qquad D=2r
$$

for a semicircular return to the entrance plane. The displayed impact separation $D$ is a diameter. This formula is linear in $r$ at fixed selected [speed](speed.md); the fixed-[electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) model above instead gives $m/q\propto r^2$. They impose different experimental conditions.

For a chosen example, take $E_s=2.00\times10^4\,\mathrm{V/m}$, $B_s=0.200\,\mathrm T$, and $B_a=0.500\,\mathrm T$. Thus $v_0=1.00\times10^5\,\mathrm{m/s}$. Approximating an [ion](ion.md) [mass](mass.md) by $24\,\mathrm u$ and setting $q=e$ gives $r\approx49.75\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $D\approx99.50\,\mathrm{mm}$. Here $\mathrm u$ denotes the [unified atomic mass unit](dalton-unit.md). These are stipulated teaching inputs, not a reported Aston measurement or an exact [magnesium](magnesium.md) [atomic mass](atomic-mass.md).

A finite accepted [speed](speed.md) range broadens the radius distribution. At fixed $m/q$ and $B_a$, $\delta r/r=\delta v/v$. At fixed [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and [mass](mass.md), $K=mv^2/2$ gives $\delta v/v\simeq\delta K/(2K)$ for a small [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) spread. A one-percent [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) spread therefore produces roughly a half-percent radius spread before other broadening is included. Its width convention must be specified before converting that statement into a [mass resolving power](mass-resolution.md).
