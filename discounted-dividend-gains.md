# Discounted dividend gains

↑ **Parent:** [Self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md)

If $B$ is a positive riskless [numéraire](numeraire.md) of finite variation, $S$ the ex-dividend price vector and $D$ its cash-dividend rate, a [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) with risky holdings $\pi$ satisfies $d(X/B)=\pi\cdot dY$. This follows by differentiating $X/B$, substituting $dX=\phi\,dB+\pi\cdot(dS+D\,dt)$ and using $X=\phi B+\pi\cdot S$. If $Y$ is a [martingale](martingale-split.md) under an equivalent measure, discounted wealth is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). A deterministic lower bound on discounted wealth makes it a [supermartingale](supermartingale.md), ruling out [arbitrage](arbitrage.md). The cumulative dividends here are discounted at their own payment times.
