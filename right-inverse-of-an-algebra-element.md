# Right inverse of an algebra element

↑ **Parent:** [Group of invertible elements of a Banach algebra](group-of-invertible-elements-of-a-banach-algebra.md)

A right inverse of an element $a$ is an element $b$ with $ab=1$. A [left inverse of an algebra element](left-inverse-of-an-algebra-element.md) and a [right inverse of an algebra element](right-inverse-of-an-algebra-element.md), when both exist, coincide. For the [unilateral shift operator](unilateral-shift-operator.md) $S$, $S^*S=1$ but $SS^*\ne1$, exhibiting the distinction in the [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) of bounded operators.
