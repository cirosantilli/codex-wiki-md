# Spectral data cube

↑ **Parent:** [Integral field spectrograph](integral-field-spectrograph.md)

A spectral data cube stores intensity $I(x,y,\lambda)$ with two spatial coordinates and one spectral coordinate. The third coordinate is [wavelength](wavelength.md), not an additional spatial dimension.
