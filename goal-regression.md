# Goal regression

↑ **Parent:** [Classical planning](classical-planning.md)

For a positive conjunctive goal $G$, an action with $\operatorname{Del}(a)\cap G=\varnothing$ can serve as the final step. Before it executes, the remaining goals and its [preconditions](precondition.md) must hold, giving the displayed regressed set. For [precondition-free STRIPS planning](precondition-free-strips-planning.md), only deletion of already supplied requirements remains.
