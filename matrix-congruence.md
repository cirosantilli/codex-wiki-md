# Matrix congruence

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md)

Two square [matrices](matrix.md) $A,C$ are congruent when $C=P^TAP$ for an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md) $P$. This is the transformation of the [matrix of a bilinear form](matrix-of-a-bilinear-form.md) under [change of basis](change-of-basis.md), and also transforms a [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) under an invertible [linear map](linear-map.md) on its [coordinates](coordinate-linear-algebra.md). The [matrices](matrix.md) need not be symmetric. It differs from [matrix similarity](matrix-similarity.md), which uses $P^{-1}AP$ for a [linear operator](linear-operator.md). For real [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md), [completing the square](completing-the-square.md) diagonalizes the corresponding [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) by [matrix congruence](matrix-congruence.md).
