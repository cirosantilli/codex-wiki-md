# Fixed point of a group action

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action.md)

A fixed point of a [group action](group-action.md) is an element fixed by every group element. The set $A^G=\{a:g\cdot a=a\text{ for all }g\in G\}$ is the [categorical limit](categorical-limit.md) of the action viewed as a [functor](functor.md) from the one-object [category](category-split.md) associated with $G$ to the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md).
