# Lefschetz number of a doubled map

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md)

Let $F$ be the self-map of the double $D(N)=N\cup_{\partial N}N$ induced by a self-map $f$ preserving the boundary. Naturality of the [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) and alternating-trace additivity give

$$
L(F)=2L(f)-L(f|_{\partial N}).
$$
