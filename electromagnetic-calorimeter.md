# Electromagnetic calorimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Calorimeter (particle physics)](calorimeter-particle-physics.md)

A [particle calorimeter](calorimeter-particle-physics.md) designed to measure [electron](electron.md) and [photon](photon.md) [energies](energy-split.md) through [particle showers](particle-shower.md). [Track reconstruction](track-reconstruction.md) helps distinguish charged [electrons](electron.md) from neutral [photons](photon.md).
