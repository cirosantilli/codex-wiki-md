# Currying adjunction for small categories

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential object](exponential-object.md)

A [functor](functor.md) $\mathcal D\times\mathcal C\to\mathcal E$ is uniquely a [functor](functor.md) $\mathcal D\to[\mathcal C,\mathcal E]$: fix its first coordinate and use its first-coordinate arrows as [natural transformations](natural-transformation.md). Uncurrying evaluates those transformations and second-coordinate arrows. The [adjunction unit](unit-of-an-adjunction.md) inserts a fixed first coordinate, and the [adjunction counit](counit-of-an-adjunction.md) is evaluation. This makes the [category of small categories](category-of-small-categories.md) cartesian closed.
