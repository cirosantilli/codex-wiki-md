# Coefficient of kinetic friction

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md)

The [coefficient of kinetic friction](coefficient-of-kinetic-friction.md) is the [dimensionless quantity](dimensionless-quantity.md) $\mu_k=|f_k|/N$ for sliding contact with positive [normal force](normal-force.md) $N$ in the ideal [kinetic friction](kinetic-friction.md) model. It characterizes the pair of contacting surfaces and their condition. A constant $\mu_k$ means the model neglects dependence on [contact slip](contact-slip.md) speed; unlike the [coefficient of static friction](coefficient-of-static-friction.md), it specifies a sliding [force](force.md) magnitude rather than a no-slip upper bound.
