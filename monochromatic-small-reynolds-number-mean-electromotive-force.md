# Monochromatic small-Reynolds-number mean electromotive force

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md)

Suppose a steady [solenoidal](solenoidal-vector-field.md) velocity satisfies $\Delta\mathbf u=-\mathbf u$, and spatial averaging permits [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) without boundary terms. For a uniform test [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) $\mathbf B$, the small-[magnetic Reynolds number](magnetic-reynolds-number.md) expansion gives $\mathbf b_1=(\mathbf B\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u$ and $\Delta\mathbf b_2=-\nabla\times(\mathbf u\times\mathbf b_1)$. Using the [self-adjointness](self-adjoint-operator.md) of the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) inside the average,

$$
\mathcal E^{(1)}=\langle\mathbf u\times(\mathbf B\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u\rangle,\qquad
\mathcal E^{(2)}=\langle\mathbf u\times\nabla\times[\mathbf u\times(\mathbf B\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u]\rangle.
$$

The second equality concerns the averaged [mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md); it does not assert that the quadratic forcing itself is monochromatic.
