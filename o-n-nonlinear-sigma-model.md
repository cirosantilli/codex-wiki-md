<h1 id="o-n-nonlinear-sigma-model">O(N) nonlinear sigma model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear sigma model](nonlinear-sigma-model.md)

The $O(N)$ nonlinear sigma model has an $N$-component field constrained to a sphere, commonly $\mathbf n^2=N$ or $1$. An auxiliary Lagrange-multiplier field turns the constraint into a self-consistent mass gap in the [Large-N expansion](large-n-expansion.md).
