# Cell-attached recording

↑ **Parent:** [Patch clamp](patch-clamp.md)

In [cell-attached recording](cell-attached-recording.md), the membrane under the [patch pipette](patch-pipette.md) remains intact and the [cytosol](cytosol.md) is retained. If bath voltage is zero, cell-interior voltage is $V_{\mathrm{cell}}$ and pipette voltage is $V_p$, the patch's inside-minus-outside [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) is $V_{\mathrm{cell}}-V_p$. Commanding $V_p$ alone does not determine that difference when $V_{\mathrm{cell}}$ is unknown or changing. This is especially relevant to [single-channel current-voltage relations](single-channel-current-voltage-relation.md).
