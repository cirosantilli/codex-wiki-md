# Monotonicity theorem for a J-holomorphic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoholomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md)

On a compact region with controlled compatible almost complex structure, there are $c,r_0>0$ such that a nonconstant J-holomorphic curve through the center of a ball of radius $r<r_0$, with boundary outside the ball, has symplectic area at least $cr^2$. For the standard complex structure the sharp local constant is $\pi$.
