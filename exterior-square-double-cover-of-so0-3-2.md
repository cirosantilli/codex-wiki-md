<h1 id="exterior-square-double-cover-of-so0-3-2">Exterior-square double cover of SO0(3,2)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Spin group](spin-group.md)

Fix a volume form on a four-dimensional real [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md). Wedge pairing on its [exterior square](exterior-square.md) has signature $(3,3)$. The [symplectic group](symplectic-group.md) fixes the bivector inverse to the symplectic form. Its orthogonal complement is five-dimensional with signature $(3,2)$ after choosing the appropriate overall sign. The induced representation has kernel $\{\pm I\}$: identity action on the entire exterior square preserves every two-plane and thus every line, forcing a scalar transformation with square one. Its derivative is injective and both group dimensions are ten, so its image is the [identity component](identity-component.md) of $SO(3,2)$.
