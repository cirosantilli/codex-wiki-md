# Cognitive behavioral therapy

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical psychology](clinical-psychology.md)

A family of psychological treatments using explicit formulations and structured cognitive and behavioral procedures. Treatment tests and modifies relevant [learning](learning.md), interpretations and behavior; its content depends on the problem and the evidence rather than on one universal technique.
