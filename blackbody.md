# Blackbody

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackbody)

A [blackbody](blackbody.md) absorbs all incident [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md). In [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) it emits the universal [Planck law](planck-s-law.md) spectrum determined solely by its [temperature](temperature-split.md).
