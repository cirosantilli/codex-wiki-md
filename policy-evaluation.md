# Policy evaluation

↑ **Parent:** [Markov decision process](markov-decision-process.md)

Estimating the expected return under a specified control policy. It is distinct from improving or optimizing that policy. A [Bellman expectation equation](bellman-expectation-equation.md) describes the fixed-policy value, whereas a Bellman optimality equation compares admissible actions.
