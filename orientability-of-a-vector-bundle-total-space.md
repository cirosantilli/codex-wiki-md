# Orientability of a vector bundle total space

↑ **Parent:** [Orientation of a vector bundle](orientation-of-a-vector-bundle.md)

The total space of a real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E\to M$ has the exact sequence $0\to\pi^*E\to TE\to\pi^*TM\to0$. Taking [determinant line bundles](determinant-line-bundle.md) gives the displayed identity. If $M$ is an [orientable smooth manifold](orientable-smooth-manifold.md), the total space is orientable exactly when $\det E$ is trivial. Sufficiency follows by pullback; necessity follows by restricting the total-space determinant identity to the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md). For the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md), base and fibre coordinate determinants cancel, giving an orientation even when the base is not orientable.
