# Rigid-lid resonance of stationary topographic internal waves

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary topographic internal gravity wave](stationary-topographic-internal-gravity-wave.md)

For a sinusoidal small-amplitude bed, constant current $U>0$ and [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) $N$, put $m=\sqrt{N^2/U^2-k^2}>0$. The linear vertical [velocity](velocity.md) amplitude is $W(z)=ikU\eta_0\sin[m(H-z)]/\sin(mH)$. The upward-wave coefficient is $A_+=-kU\eta_0e^{-imH}/[2\sin(mH)]$. At $mH=n\pi$, the prescribed nonzero bed motion and homogeneous rigid-lid condition are incompatible with a bounded stationary inviscid solution. With angle $\theta$ between intrinsic [group velocity](group-velocity.md) and vertical, $\tan\theta=m/k$, giving $kH\tan\theta=n\pi$.
