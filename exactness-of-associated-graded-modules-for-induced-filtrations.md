# Exactness of associated graded modules for induced filtrations

↑ **Parent:** [Associated graded module](associated-graded-module.md)

Give $N$ the [subspace filtration](subspace-filtration.md) and $M/N$ the [quotient filtration](quotient-filtration.md). If the degree-$i$ class of $m\in F_iM$ maps to zero, then $m=n+m'$ with $n\in N$ and $m'\in F_{i-1}M$. Thus $n\in N\cap F_iM$ and the class comes from $\operatorname{gr}N$. Inclusion is [injective](injective-function.md) in every degree and the quotient map is [surjective](surjective-function.md), proving the displayed [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) of [associated graded modules](associated-graded-module.md). Exactness can fail for arbitrarily chosen incompatible filtrations.
