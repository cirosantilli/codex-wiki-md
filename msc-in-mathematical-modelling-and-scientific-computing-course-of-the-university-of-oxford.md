# MSc in mathematical modelling and scientific computing course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-mathematical-modelling-and-scientific-computing).

Required applied [partial differential equations](partial-differential-equation-split.md) and perturbation methods; choose two or three numerical-analysis cores from numerical solution of [partial differential equations](partial-differential-equation-split.md), numerical [linear algebra](linear-algebra-split.md) and continuous optimisation. At least two special-topic units, including modelling/methods and computation. One scientific-computing case study in Michaelmas term and one mathematical-modelling case study in Hilary term. Compulsory four-unit dissertation, normally written in Trinity term and the summer.
