# Radial disintegration of an orthogonally invariant measure

↑ **Parent:** [Pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md)

An [orthogonally invariant random vector](orthogonally-invariant-random-vector.md) has a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) determined by its radial [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md). Here $\sigma$ is normalized [surface measure on a sphere](surface-measure-on-a-sphere.md), and the formula holds for bounded measurable $f$ and for nonnegative measurable $f$. It includes an [atom of a measure](atom-measure-theory.md) at zero: the inner integral there is simply $f(0)$.

To prove the formula first for continuous bounded $f$, average $f(Qx)$ over normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) of the compact [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md). Right invariance makes this average depend only on $|x|$; averaging also over [surface measure on a sphere](surface-measure-on-a-sphere.md) identifies it with the spherical average. Invariance of $\mu$ and [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) then give the identity. Uniqueness of finite [Borel measures](borel-measure.md) and measurable approximation extend it to the stated classes of $f$. No extra sphere-area factor belongs in an average against normalized [surface measure on a sphere](surface-measure-on-a-sphere.md).
