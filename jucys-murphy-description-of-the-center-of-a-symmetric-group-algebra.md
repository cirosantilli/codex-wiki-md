<h1 id="jucys-murphy-description-of-the-center-of-a-symmetric-group-algebra">Jucys–Murphy description of the center of a symmetric-group algebra</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Jucys–Murphy element](jucys-murphy-element.md)

The [Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](jucys-murphy-element.md) commute, and [symmetric polynomials](symmetric-polynomial.md) in them are central. The full center theorem holds integrally as well as over [fields](field.md), so it survives modular reduction. On an ordinary [Specht module](specht-module.md), the scalar of such a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is its value on the cell-content multiset. The integral generation step is stronger than knowing this description only over a characteristic-zero [field](field.md) and is essential in the modular block argument.
