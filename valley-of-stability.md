# Valley of stability

↑ **Parent:** [Beta stability](beta-stability.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valley_of_stability)

The [valley of stability](valley-of-stability.md) is the region of nuclear [masses](mass.md) near the minima with respect to proton-neutron imbalance. Its beta-stable band bends toward neutron excess for heavier [nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md), as [nuclear Coulomb energy](nuclear-coulomb-energy.md) competes with the cost of neutron-proton asymmetry. The band is distinct from the particle-emission boundaries set by [nuclear separation energies](nuclear-separation-energy.md).
