# Highest-weight weak-isospin selection in kaon decay

↑ **Parent:** [Isospin](isospin.md)

Suppose a transition operator obeys $[I_3,H]=H/2$ and $[I_+,H]=0$, and acts on the lower component of an isospin-half [kaon](kaon.md). Its output has third component zero, and $I_+^2H|K\rangle=HI_+^2|K\rangle=0$. This excludes an isospin-two output. A spinless parent decaying to two spinless [pions](pion.md) has zero relative [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md), so [Bose-Einstein statistics](bose-einstein-statistics.md) excludes the antisymmetric isospin-one sector. The output is therefore the [two-isovector singlet](two-isovector-singlet.md). In the equal-mass isospin limit, charged and neutral two-pion decay widths have ratio two, including the identical-particle counting.
