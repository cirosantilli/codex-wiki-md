# Spline knot

↑ **Parent:** [Spline approximation](spline-approximation.md)

A junction between adjacent [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) pieces of a [cubic spline](cubic-spline.md) or other spline. [Continuity](continuous-function.md) conditions at these junctions determine which piecewise [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) functions are splines.
