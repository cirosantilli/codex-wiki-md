[DPPH](2-2-diphenyl-1-picrylhydrazyl.md) is a neutral organic [radical](radical-chemistry.md) used as a [spin](spin.md) reference in electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR), also called electron spin resonance (ESR). Its unpaired [Electron](electron.md) remains bound within the [chemical species](chemical-species.md): “free radical” does not mean a freely moving [Electron](electron.md). The [DPPH electron spin resonance experiment](dpph-electron-spin-resonance-experiment.md) makes its [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) observable.
