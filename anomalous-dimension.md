# Anomalous dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Engineering dimension](engineering-dimension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anomalous_dimension)

An anomalous dimension is the interaction-generated difference between a full [scaling dimension](scaling-dimension.md) and its [engineering dimension](engineering-dimension.md). For a scalar field, momentum-dependent self-energy corrections require [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) and produce $\Delta_\phi=(d-2+\eta)/2$.
