# Binary classification

↑ **Parent:** [Classification in statistical learning](classification-in-statistical-learning.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_classification)

[Classification in statistical learning](classification-in-statistical-learning.md) with two possible labels. Given the [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md) $\eta(x)=\mathbb P(Y=1\mid X=x)$, a [Bayes classifier](bayes-classifier.md) under [zero-one loss](misclassification-loss.md) predicts one exactly when $\eta(x)\geq1/2$, with arbitrary decisions at ties.
