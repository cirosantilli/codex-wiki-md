# Affected-sib-pair analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Nonparametric linkage analysis](nonparametric-linkage-analysis.md)

An affected-sib-pair analysis examines marker [identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md) between affected [full siblings](full-sibling.md). Without [genetic linkage](genetic-linkage.md) to the disease locus, the probabilities of sharing zero, one and two copies are $1/4,1/2,1/4$. Excess sharing supplies evidence for linkage under disease models that preferentially transmit a common risk allele to both siblings.
