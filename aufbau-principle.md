# Aufbau principle

↑ **Parent:** [Electron configuration](electron-configuration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aufbau_principle)

An approximate scheme for constructing an atomic [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md) by filling lower-energy [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) before higher-energy ones, subject to the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md). Orbital ordering can change on ionization and has exceptions in interacting [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). Selecting the free-ion ground term within a specified open-shell configuration additionally requires [Hund's rules](hund-s-rules.md) or a more detailed interaction calculation.
