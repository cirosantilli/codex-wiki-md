# Semistrip Laplace spectral global relation

↑ **Parent:** [Global relation for a linear boundary value problem](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md)

Let $q$ be a real [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) in a semistrip, $g_0(x)=q(x,0)$, $g_l(x)=q(x,l)$ and $h(y)=q(0,y)$. Let $G_j,N_j$ be the negative-exponential [Half-range Fourier transforms](half-range-fourier-transform.md) of $g_j$ and $q_y(x,j)$, and put $H(k)=\int_0^l e^{ky}h(y)\,dy$, $W(k)=\int_0^l e^{ky}q_x(0,y)\,dy$. The [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) $e^{-ikz}q_z\,dz$ has zero boundary integral. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) of tangential derivatives cancels all corner values and gives the displayed [global relation](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md), for $\operatorname{Im}k\leq0$.
