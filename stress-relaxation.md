# Stress relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stress_relaxation)

Stress relaxation is decay of stored stress after a deformation is imposed and then held fixed. It probes a material's memory through its [relaxation modulus](relaxation-modulus.md). An instantaneous [generalized Newtonian fluid](generalized-newtonian-fluid.md) has no stored shear stress once the shear rate is zero and cannot describe a nontrivial relaxation tail.
