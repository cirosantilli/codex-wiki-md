# Subgroup of index the smallest prime divisor is normal

↑ **Parent:** [Index of a subgroup](index-of-a-subgroup.md)

Let $G$ be finite and let $H$ have index $p$, the smallest prime divisor of $|G|$. The [group action](group-action-split.md) on [left cosets](left-coset.md) gives $\Phi:G\to S_p$, with $\ker\Phi\subseteq H$. The image order divides both $|G|$ and $p!$. By the choice of $p$, the only possible nontrivial prime factor common to these orders is $p$, and $p!$ contains only one factor of $p$. Thus the image order is $1$ or $p$. The action is [transitive](transitive-relation.md) on $p>1$ cosets, so its image is nontrivial and has order $p$. The [first isomorphism theorem](first-isomorphism-theorem.md) gives $|\ker\Phi|=|G|/p=|H|$, hence $\ker\Phi=H$. A [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md), proving the claim. For example, every subgroup of order $25$ in a group of order $75$ is normal.
