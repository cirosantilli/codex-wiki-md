# Stress-energy tensor of a nonminimally coupled scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md)

For action $S=\int\sqrt{-g}[-\frac12(\nabla\Phi)^2-\xi R\Phi^2]d^4x$, [metric variation](metric-variation.md) defines $\delta S=\frac12\int\sqrt{-g}T^{ab}\delta g_{ab}d^4x$ and gives the displayed tensor. The [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md) and the scalar derivative commutator yield $\nabla^aT_{ab}=(\Box\Phi-2\xi R\Phi)\nabla_b\Phi$, so the [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) is conserved on the field equation.
