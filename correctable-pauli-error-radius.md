# Correctable Pauli error radius

↑ **Parent:** [Distance of a quantum error-correcting code](distance-of-a-quantum-error-correcting-code.md)

The guaranteed radius is the largest integer $t$ for which all unknown-location [Pauli operators](pauli-operator.md) of weight at most $t$ can be corrected together. Pairwise products have weight at most $2t$, so $2t<d$ suffices by the [Knill--Laflamme condition](knill-laflamme-condition.md). Conversely, split a minimum-weight undetectable Pauli operator between two subsets of its support. If $d\leq2t$, this gives two errors of weight at most $t$ whose pairwise product violates that condition. Consequently $d$ is either $2t+1$ or $2t+2$; parity cannot be recovered from $t$ alone.
