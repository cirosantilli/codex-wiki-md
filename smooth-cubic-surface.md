# Smooth cubic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Cubic surface](cubic-surface.md)

A [cubic surface](cubic-surface.md) with empty [singular locus](singular-locus.md). Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), it is irreducible: distinct positive-degree hypersurface components would meet, and every point of their intersection would be singular in their product equation.
