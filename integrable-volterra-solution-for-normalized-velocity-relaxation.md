# Integrable Volterra solution for normalized velocity relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized velocity-reset collision operator](normalized-velocity-reset-collision-operator.md)

For $E=L^1_{x,v}$, let $(\tau f)(t)=\int_0^tU_{t-s}(P-I)f(s)ds$. The [factorial bound for a Volterra iterate](factorial-bound-for-a-volterra-iterate.md) is $\|\tau^nf(t)\|_1\leq(2t)^n\sup_{s\leq t}\|f(s)\|_1/n!$. Thus the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md) converges in $C([0,T];E)$ for every finite $T$ and solves $f=U_\cdot f_0+\tau f$. The same estimate applied to a difference proves uniqueness among locally time-bounded [integral](integral.md) solutions. The whole collision term remains inside this undamped [integral](integral.md), rather than absorbing loss into the free propagator.
