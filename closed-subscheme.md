# Closed subscheme

↑ **Parent:** [Closed immersion](closed-immersion.md)

A closed subscheme of $X$ is a [scheme](scheme.md) $Z$ together with a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md) $Z\hookrightarrow X$, usually identified with its image and its quotient structure sheaf.
