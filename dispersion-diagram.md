# Dispersion diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md)

A dispersion diagram plots the branches of a [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md), usually with [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) on the horizontal axis and [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) on the vertical axis. Its slopes, intersections, and gaps display [group velocities](group-velocity.md), mode conversion, and forbidden frequency ranges.
