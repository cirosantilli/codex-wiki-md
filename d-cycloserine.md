# D-cycloserine

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology](pharmacology-split.md)

A compound studied as a partial [agonist](agonist.md) at the glycine site of the [NMDA receptor](nmda-receptor.md) and as a possible facilitator of [learning](learning.md). An experimental adjunct to a [learning](learning.md) session is not equivalent to a general [anxiety](anxiety.md) suppressant.
