# Uniaxial optical medium

↑ **Parent:** [Birefringence](birefringence.md)

A transparent, nonmagnetic [linear anisotropic dielectric](linear-anisotropic-dielectric.md) is uniaxial when two principal [permittivities](permittivity-split.md) agree. With the [optic axis](optic-axis.md) along $z$, its [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) [tensor](tensor.md) has the displayed [diagonal matrix](diagonal-matrix.md) form. Here $n_o$ and $n_e$ are principal [refractive indices](refractive-index.md) at a specified [frequency](frequency.md); $n_e$ is not the [extraordinary wave](extraordinary-wave.md)'s index in every direction.[§2.8.1, printed pp. 154–156, equations 2.406–2.413: uniaxial tensor and direction-dependent extraordinary index](fundamentals-of-photonics-and-quantum-electronics-lecture-notes-by-franz-x-kartner.md)
