# Speed-radius attractor of an accreting raindrop

↑ **Parent:** [Accreting raindrop with quadratic drag](accreting-raindrop-with-quadratic-drag.md)

For the [accreting raindrop with quadratic drag](accreting-raindrop-with-quadratic-drag.md), set $q=v^2/a$ and $B=7c/8+3k/4$. Its exact equation is $\dot q=2(v/a)(g-Bq)$. Dividing by $\dot a=cv/4$ gives $q(a)=g/B+(q(a_0)-g/B)(a_0/a)^{7+6k/c}$. Thus $q$ stays between its positive initial value and positive equilibrium. Since $d\sqrt a/dt=c\sqrt q/8$, the radius grows without finite-time divergence and tends to infinity. The power correction then vanishes, proving both limits. Positive drag makes the limiting [acceleration](acceleration.md) strictly less than $g/7$.
