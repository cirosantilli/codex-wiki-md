# Resonator

↑ **Parent:** [Resonance](resonance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resonator)

A [resonator](resonator.md) stores oscillatory [energy](energy-split.md) in one or more [normal modes](normal-mode.md). Driving near a mode's [frequency](frequency.md) can produce [resonance](resonance.md). Losses limit the response and determine its [quality factor](q-factor.md). After the drive is removed, an isolated weakly damped mode decays approximately exponentially; an observed room response may combine many modes.
