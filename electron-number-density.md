# Electron number density

↑ **Parent:** [Number density](number-density.md)

Electron number density is the number of free electrons per unit volume. In fully ionized stellar matter, $n_e=\rho/(\mu_em_u)$, with [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ and [mean molecular weight per electron](mean-molecular-weight-per-electron.md) $\mu_e$.
