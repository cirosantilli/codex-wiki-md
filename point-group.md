# Point group

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_group)

The [point group](point-group.md) of a Euclidean crystallographic group is the group of its orthogonal linear parts, equivalently its quotient by the translation subgroup. It preserves the translation [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md). In an arbitrary dimension it is a finite orthogonal group; for a [plane crystallographic group](wallpaper-group.md) this lattice preservation restricts the allowed rotation orders.
