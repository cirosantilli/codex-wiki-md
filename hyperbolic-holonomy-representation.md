# Hyperbolic holonomy representation

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md)

For a marked closed [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md), the representation of its [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) whose image acts as the deck group on the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md). It is discrete and faithful, determined up to conjugation by the marking and the metric, and gives $X=\mathbb H^2/\rho(\pi_1\Sigma_g)$. For a closed orientable surface it admits determinant-one lifts. It is different from the tangent-space action of the [Riemannian holonomy group](riemannian-holonomy-group.md).
