# TATA-binding protein

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A [protein](protein-split.md) that recognizes a [TATA box](tata-box.md) and participates in [transcription](transcription-biology.md) initiation complexes. Its roles are not restricted to [gene promoters](promoter-genetics.md) with an easily identifiable consensus [TATA box](tata-box.md).
