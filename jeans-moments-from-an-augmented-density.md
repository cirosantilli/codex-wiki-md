# Jeans moments from an augmented density

↑ **Parent:** [Augmented stellar density](augmented-stellar-density.md)

With radial pressure zero at $\psi=0$, an [augmented density](augmented-stellar-density.md) supplies $\rho\langle v_r^2\rangle=\int_0^\psi\rho(r,q)dq$ and $\rho\langle v_\theta^2\rangle=(2r)^{-1}\int_0^\psi\partial_r[r^2\rho(r,q)]dq$. The derivative holds $q$ fixed. Their chain-rule combination satisfies the [Spherical Jeans equation](spherical-jeans-equation.md), but the physical density alone does not choose an augmented extension.
