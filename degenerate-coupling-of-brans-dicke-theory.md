# Degenerate coupling of Brans-Dicke theory

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar equation of Brans-Dicke theory](scalar-equation-of-brans-dicke-theory.md)

At $\omega=-3/2$, the combined [scalar equation of Brans-Dicke theory](scalar-equation-of-brans-dicke-theory.md) becomes the matter consistency condition $T=0$. It does not determine $\Box\Phi$ by division, since the wave coefficient has vanished. The original [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) and [scalar](scalar.md) [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) must be retained. Introducing scalar-dependent matter or a [scalar](scalar.md) potential changes the consistency condition.
