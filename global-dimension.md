# Global dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Projective dimension](projective-dimension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_dimension)

The global dimension of a [ring](ring.md) is the supremum of the [projective dimensions](projective-dimension.md) of all its [modules](module-mathematics.md), allowing infinity. It measures the longest [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) needed by any [module](module-mathematics.md). For a [field](field.md) it is zero, since every [module](module-mathematics.md) is a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) and hence free.
