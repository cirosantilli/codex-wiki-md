# Wall hindrance in Brownian diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Perrin sedimentation experiment](perrin-sedimentation-experiment.md)

[Buoyancy](buoyancy.md) changes the effective gravitational [mass](mass.md); a nearby no-slip wall changes the [hydrodynamic resistance matrix](hydrodynamic-resistance-matrix.md) and therefore the [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md). These are distinct corrections. Write the horizontal resistance as $\zeta_\parallel=6\pi\eta a/f$, with $0<f<1$ representing wall hindrance at a specified height. Applying the [Stokes–Einstein relation](stokes-einstein-relation.md) with that resistance gives

$$
D_\parallel=f\frac{k_BT}{6\pi\eta a},\qquad
\frac{N_{A,\,\mathrm{inferred}}^{\rm diff}}{N_{A,\,\mathrm{true}}}=\frac1f
$$

if one mistakenly uses the bulk resistance in the inversion. A chosen $f=0.8$ would overestimate $N_A$ by $25\%$; this is a sensitivity example, not a correction measured in [Jean Baptiste Perrin](jean-baptiste-perrin.md)'s apparatus. Normal and parallel coefficients of the [hydrodynamic resistance matrix](hydrodynamic-resistance-matrix.md) need not coincide. [Stokes settling velocity](stokes-settling-velocity.md) near a wall can likewise bias a radius inferred using an unbounded-fluid formula.

For a passive dilute [particle suspension](suspension-chemistry-split.md) at uniform [temperature](temperature-split.md), a change of mobility alone does not change the [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md). In the absence of additional wall forces the correct local vertical flux is $j_z=-D_z(z)[dn/dz+m_bgn/(k_BT)]$, so zero flux still gives the same [sedimentation scale height](sedimentation-scale-height.md). Walls can nevertheless invalidate that simple inference through adsorption, additional interaction energies, or sampling of inaccessible grain-centre positions. Optical position errors and exposure averaging also affect displacement readout; varying distance from the wall, observation lag and optical depth tests different possible errors.
