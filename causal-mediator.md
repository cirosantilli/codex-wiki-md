# Causal mediator

↑ **Parent:** [Causal mediation](causal-mediation.md)

A [random variable](random-variable-split.md) on a proposed causal pathway from a cause to an outcome. A [causal mediator](causal-mediator.md) transmits part of a [causal effect](causal-effect.md); [statistical association](statistical-association.md) with the cause and outcome alone does not establish that role.
