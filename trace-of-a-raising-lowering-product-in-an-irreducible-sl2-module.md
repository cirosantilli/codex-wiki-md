# Trace of a raising-lowering product in an irreducible sl2 module

↑ **Parent:** [Sl2 highest-weight lowering formula](sl2-highest-weight-lowering-formula.md)

For a highest-weight vector $v$ of weight $k$ in an irreducible finite-dimensional [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md) module, the [basis](basis.md) $v,Yv,\ldots,Y^kv$ diagonalizes $XY$, with eigenvalue $(j+1)(k-j)$ on $Y^jv$. Summing those eigenvalues gives $\operatorname{tr}(XY)=k(k+1)(k+2)/6$. The formula $XY^nv=n(k-n+1)Y^{n-1}v$ requires $v$ to be a [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md), not an arbitrary vector of the module.
