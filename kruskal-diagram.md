# Kruskal diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Kruskal spacetime](kruskal-spacetime.md)

This radial spacetime diagram uses [Kruskal–Szekeres coordinates](kruskal-szekeres-coordinates.md) to display both exteriors, the black hole and the white hole of maximally extended [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md). Each point represents a symmetry two-sphere. Radial [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) have slopes $\pm1$ in the displayed coordinates; the horizons are $T_K=\pm X_K$ and the singularities satisfy $T_K^2-X_K^2=1$ in the usual normalization. A further compactification gives the corresponding [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md).
