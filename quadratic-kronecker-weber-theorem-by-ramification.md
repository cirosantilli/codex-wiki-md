<h1 id="quadratic-kronecker-weber-theorem-by-ramification">Quadratic Kronecker–Weber theorem by ramification</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kronecker–Weber theorem](kronecker-weber-theorem.md)

For an odd prime $\ell$, the unique [quadratic subfield of a prime cyclotomic field](quadratic-subfield-of-a-prime-cyclotomic-field.md) has no finite [ramification](ramification-mathematics.md) outside $\ell$. The classification of [quadratic discriminants](quadratic-discriminant.md) identifies it with $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{\ell^*})$, where $\ell^*=(-1)^{(\ell-1)/2}\ell$. A fundamental [discriminant](discriminant.md) factors as $D=D_2\prod_{\ell\mid D,\ \ell\text{ odd}}\ell^*$ with $D_2\in\{1,-4,8,-8\}$. The remaining [quadratic number field](quadratic-field.md) is generated by $i$, $\sqrt2$, or $\sqrt{-2}$ and lies in $\mathbb Q(\zeta_8)$. Taking the corresponding [compositum](field-compositum.md) proves the indicated quadratic case of the [Kronecker–Weber theorem](kronecker-weber-theorem.md).
