# Data assimilation

↑ **Parent:** [Weather forecasting](weather-forecasting.md)

Combining observations with a dynamical prior estimates an initial atmospheric state and its uncertainty. In a linear Gaussian update, analysis increments are $K(y-Hx_b)$ with $K=BH^T(HBH^T+R)^{-1}$, where $B$ and $R$ are prior and observation error [covariance matrices](covariance-matrix.md). Observation noise, sparse coverage and incorrect model or covariance assumptions can all leave forecast errors.
