# Brahmagupta-Fibonacci identity

↑ **Parent:** [Sum of two squares theorem](sum-of-two-squares-theorem.md)

The identity $(a^2+b^2)(c^2+d^2)=(ac-bd)^2+(ad+bc)^2$ shows that a product of two sums of two squares is another sum of two squares. It follows by multiplying [Gaussian integers](gaussian-integer.md) and taking their squared absolute values.
