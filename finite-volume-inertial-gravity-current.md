# Finite-volume inertial gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity current](gravity-current.md)

For a one-sided fixed-volume [gravity current](gravity-current.md) in a unit-width channel with a closed rear wall, use front closure $\dot L=F\sqrt{g'h_f}$ with $0<F<2$. An exact late-time [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) has $u=\dot L x/L$, $h=h_f[1-F^2/4+(F^2/4)(x/L)^2]$ and $h_fL=V/(1-F^2/6)$. Substituting into the [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md) proves the radius law. The [Benjamin deep-ambient front condition](benjamin-deep-ambient-front-condition.md) has $F=\sqrt2$. The virtual time origin $t_v$ is fixed by matching to the finite release, not by volume alone.
