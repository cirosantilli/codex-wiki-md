# Harmonic pressure and vorticity in Stokes flow

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

Divergence and curl of $-\nabla p+\mu\nabla^2u=0$ with $\nabla\cdot u=0$ give $\nabla^2p=0$ and $\nabla^2\omega=0$.
