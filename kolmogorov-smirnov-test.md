# Kolmogorov-Smirnov test

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

The [Kolmogorov-Smirnov test](kolmogorov-smirnov-test.md) compares an empirical distribution with a specified continuous distribution using the supremum of their discrepancy. Under a simple continuous null, the [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md) makes its distribution independent of the specified distribution. Fitting parameters generally changes this null law.
