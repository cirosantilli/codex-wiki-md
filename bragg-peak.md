# Bragg peak

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping power](stopping-power.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bragg_peak)

A maximum near the end of the depth-dose curve of a charged-particle beam, associated with increasing [stopping power](stopping-power.md) during slowing. Its precise width and distal tail depend on beam spread and particle transport.
