# Rankine half-body

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

The region bounded by the dividing [streamlines](streamline.md) of a two-dimensional [point source](point-source.md) superposed on uniform flow. Its stagnation point is $-Q/(2\pi U)$ upstream and its far downstream width is $Q/U$. The boundary encloses fluid originating at the source rather than a rigid obstacle; the upstream stagnation point is reached only asymptotically by the upstream source trajectory.
