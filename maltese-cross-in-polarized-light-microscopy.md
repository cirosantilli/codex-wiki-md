# Maltese cross in polarized-light microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Polarizing microscope](polarizing-microscope.md)

An extinction pattern with four dark arms produced, for example, by a radial arrangement of local optical principal directions between crossed [polarizers](polarizer.md). The local intensity factor $\sin^2(2\varphi)$ vanishes along the polarizer axes. Such a radial-material pattern need not be a [conoscopic interference pattern](conoscopic-interference-pattern.md).
