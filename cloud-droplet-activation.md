# Cloud-droplet activation

↑ **Parent:** [Cloud condensation nucleus](cloud-condensation-nucleus.md)

An [aerosol](aerosol.md) particle becomes a freely growing [cloud droplet](cloud-droplet.md) after crossing its [Köhler theory](kohler-theory.md) growth barrier under the parcel's [atmospheric supersaturation](atmospheric-supersaturation.md) history. Counting particles whose [critical supersaturation](critical-supersaturation.md) is below a specified peak gives an equilibrium-threshold estimate; calculating that peak requires cooling, [water vapor](water-vapor.md) depletion and condensational-growth kinetics. Neither total [aerosol](aerosol.md) number nor dry size alone determines activation.
