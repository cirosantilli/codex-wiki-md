# First-year sea ice

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)

First-year sea ice is ice that has formed since the preceding summer melt season and has not yet survived a summer. It is usually thinner than older ice, although deformation can create thick [sea-ice pressure ridges](sea-ice-pressure-ridge.md) in a young cover.
