# Amice transform

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic measure](p-adic-measure.md)

The coefficient of $T^n$ is $\int\binom{x}{n}\,d\mu(x)$. The [Mahler expansion of continuous p-adic functions](mahler-expansion-of-continuous-p-adic-functions.md) identifies $I$-valued [p-adic measures](p-adic-measure.md) on $\mathbb Z_p$ with $I[[T]]$. If $D=(1+T)d/dT$, then $D^k\mathcal A_\mu(0)=\int x^k\,d\mu(x)$. A measure supported on $\mathbb Z_p^\times$ has $\sum_{\zeta^p=1}\mathcal A_\mu(\zeta(1+T)-1)=0$, because the sum of $\zeta^x$ is zero for $p\nmid x$.
