# Rotating-frame reduction of circular viscoelastic shear

↑ **Parent:** [Upper-convected derivative](upper-convected-derivative.md)

For azimuthal velocity $v(r)e_\theta$, the [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md) in the rotating polar basis is $\begin{pmatrix}0&-v/r\\v'&0\end{pmatrix}$. The basis rotates along a particle at angular speed $v/r$. Accounting for that rotation in the [material derivative](material-derivative.md) replaces $L$ by $L-(v/r)\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ in the [upper-convected derivative](upper-convected-derivative.md). Its remaining shear rate is $v'-v/r$, so the local viscoelastic [normal-stress difference](normal-stress-difference.md) follows the same constitutive algebra as [simple shear flow](simple-shear-flow.md).
