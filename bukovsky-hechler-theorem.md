<h1 id="bukovsky-hechler-theorem">Bukovský-Hechler theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Singular cardinal](singular-cardinal.md)

If the power-set [function](function-split.md) is eventually constant below a [singular cardinal](singular-cardinal.md) $\kappa$, its constant value persists at $\kappa$. Writing $\theta=\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)$ gives $2^\kappa=(2^{<\kappa})^\theta$. On the plateau choose a [cardinal](cardinal-number.md) $\mu\ge\theta$, so $(2^\mu)^\theta=2^{\mu\cdot\theta}=2^\mu$.
