# Cumulant expansion of a coarse-grained free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum-shell renormalization group](momentum-shell-renormalization-group.md)

Writing the interaction as $V$ and averaging over the fast modes, which have a [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md), gives the [cumulant expansion](cumulant-expansion.md) $F_{\mathrm{eff}}=F_0+\langle V\rangle-\tfrac12(\langle V^2\rangle-\langle V\rangle^2)+\cdots$. The second cumulant selects [connected Feynman diagrams](connected-feynman-diagram.md).
