# Traceless symmetric square of the defining so5 representation

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl dimension formula for B2](weyl-dimension-formula-for-b2.md)

For the five-dimensional defining representation $V=V(\omega_2)$ of $\mathfrak{so}_5$, the invariant quadratic form splits

$$
S^2V\cong\mathbb C\oplus V(2\omega_2).
$$

Thus the traceless symmetric square is irreducible of dimension $15-1=14$.
