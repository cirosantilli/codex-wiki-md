# Polynomial growth of iterated sumsets

↑ **Parent:** [Doubling constant](doubling-constant.md)

For a finite nonempty subset of an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) with [doubling constant](doubling-constant.md) at most $K$, the [Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality](plunnecke-ruzsa-inequality.md) gives $|3A-2A|\leq K^5|A|$. Applying the [Ruzsa covering lemma](ruzsa-covering-lemma.md) to $2(A-A)$ using $A$ gives $2T\subseteq D+T$ for $T=A-A$ and $|D|=q\leq K^5$. Thus $\ell T\subseteq(\ell-1)D+T$, and counting multiplicities in the finite set $D$ proves the displayed polynomial bound. For fixed $K>1$, it is eventually at most $K^{\epsilon\ell}|A|$ for every $\epsilon>0$.
