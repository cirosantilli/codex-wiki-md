# Source (quantum field theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Generating functional](generating-functional.md)

An external [source](source-quantum-field-theory.md) is a prescribed field coupled to a dynamical field or operator in the action, for example $S_J=S_0+\int J_i(x)\phi_i(x)\,d^dx$. Functional derivatives of the [generating functional](generating-functional.md) with respect to $J$ insert the corresponding fields into correlation functions. The source is varied to generate these insertions, rather than integrated over as a dynamical degree of freedom. Transforming it in the appropriate representation maintains an invariant source coupling when deriving a [Ward identity](ward-identity.md).
