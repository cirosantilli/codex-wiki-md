# Bohr-Mollerup theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma function](gamma-function.md)

On $(0,\infty)$, the [Gamma function](gamma-function.md) is the unique positive [function](function-split.md) $f$ satisfying $f(1)=1$, $f(x+1)=xf(x)$, and the condition that $\log f$ is a [convex function](convex-function.md). This last condition concerns the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) of $f$, rather than $f$ itself, and removes the interpolation freedom left by the [Gamma function recurrence](gamma-function-recurrence.md).[NIST DLMF, Section 5.5(iv): Bohr-Mollerup theorem](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.5#iv)
