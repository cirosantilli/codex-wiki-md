# Grounding line

↑ **Parent:** [Ice shelf](ice-shelf.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grounding_line)

The grounding line is the transition where an ice sheet detaches from its bed and begins to float as an [ice shelf](ice-shelf.md). Ice thickness there obeys the hydrostatic flotation condition.
