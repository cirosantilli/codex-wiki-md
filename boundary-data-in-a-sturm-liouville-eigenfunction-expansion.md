# Boundary data in a Sturm-Liouville eigenfunction expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Sturm-Liouville eigenfunction expansion](sturm-liouville-eigenfunction-expansion.md)

Let $Ay=(py')'+qy$ have real [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) $u_n$ obeying $Au_n=\lambda_nwu_n$ and homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), with $N_n=\int_a^bwu_n^2>0$. Suppose $Ay+\mu wy=f$, with possibly nonzero endpoint values. The [Lagrange identity for a Sturm-Liouville operator](lagrange-identity-for-a-sturm-liouville-operator.md) gives

$$
\int_a^bu_nf\,dx-(\lambda_n+\mu)\int_a^bwu_ny\,dx=[p(u_ny'-u_n'y)]_a^b.
$$

Division by $(\lambda_n+\mu)N_n$ gives the displayed coefficient whenever the denominator is nonzero. At [resonance in a differential equation](resonance-in-a-differential-equation.md), equality of the forcing projection and the boundary term is the [Fredholm solvability condition](fredholm-solvability-condition.md). The [Sturm-Liouville eigenfunction expansion](sturm-liouville-eigenfunction-expansion.md) represents the interior solution in the [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md) sense; its zero endpoint terms do not reproduce nonzero [boundary traces](boundary-trace-of-a-function.md) by pointwise substitution. A smooth lift of the endpoint data reduces the calculation to homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md).
