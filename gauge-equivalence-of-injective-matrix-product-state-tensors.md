# Gauge equivalence of injective matrix product state tensors

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental theorem of matrix product states](fundamental-theorem-of-matrix-product-states.md)

The virtual [similarity transformation](similarity-transformation.md) $A^i\mapsto XA^iX^{-1}$ leaves every periodic MPS amplitude invariant by cyclicity of the [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md). The [fundamental theorem of matrix product states](fundamental-theorem-of-matrix-product-states.md) says that, up to an overall phase, this is the only ambiguity for injective uniform tensors.
