# Exponential martingale from a jump generator

↑ **Parent:** [Markov jump-process generator](markov-jump-process-generator.md)

For a positive function $g$ in the domain of a [Markov jump-process generator](markov-jump-process-generator.md), the generator martingale for $g(X_t)$ and [semimartingale integration by parts](semimartingale-integration-by-parts.md) give the displayed [local martingale](local-martingale.md). Suitable finite-horizon deterministic bounds on $g$ and $Lg/g$ make it a true [martingale](martingale-split.md). For a [single marked exponential jump process](single-marked-exponential-jump-process.md), take $g(x)=e^{\theta x}$. Then the exponent's compensating process is $r_\theta(t\wedge T)$, where $r_\theta=(e^\theta-1)/\theta-1$ for $\theta\ne0$ and $r_0=0$; it is continuous adapted and hence [predictable](predictable-process.md).
