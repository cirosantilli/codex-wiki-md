# Junction tree

↑ **Parent:** [Probabilistic graphical model](probabilistic-graphical-model.md)

A junction tree connects the [maximal cliques](maximal-clique.md) of a [chordal graph](chordal-graph.md) into a [tree](tree-graph-theory.md) satisfying the [running intersection property](running-intersection-property.md). Its edges carry separators equal to the intersections of the endpoint cliques. Assigning original factors to containing cliques permits exact [sum-product belief propagation](sum-product-belief-propagation.md) without summing independently over the entire collection of variables.
