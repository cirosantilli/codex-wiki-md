# Luminosity variability size bound

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic jet](relativistic-jet.md)

Coherent intrinsic [luminosity](luminosity.md) variation on observed time $\Delta t_{\rm obs}$ bounds the characteristic emitting radius by a light-crossing argument. [relativistic beaming](relativistic-beaming.md) shortens the observed interval by [Doppler factor](doppler-factor.md) $D$, while [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) lengthens it by $1+z$. This bound assumes that the variation samples the emitting region causally; geometry, independent subregions and propagation-driven variation can change its interpretation.
