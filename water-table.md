# Water table

↑ **Parent:** [Unconfined aquifer](unconfined-aquifer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_table)

The [water table](water-table.md) is the upper surface of the saturated zone in an [unconfined aquifer](unconfined-aquifer.md), where liquid [pressure](pressure-split.md) equals [atmospheric pressure](atmospheric-pressure.md) in the simplest drainage model. Under the [Dupuit approximation](dupuit-approximation.md), its height determines [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) throughout the saturated region and therefore the lateral [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md).
