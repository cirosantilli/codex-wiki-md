# Removal of the first derivative from a second-order differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md)

For

$$
y''+P(x)y'+Q(x)y=0,
$$

the substitution

$$
y=e^{-\frac12\int P(x)\,dx}v
$$

gives the normal form

$$
v''+\left(Q-\frac12P'-\frac14P^2\right)v=0.
$$
