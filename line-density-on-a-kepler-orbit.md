# Line density on a Kepler orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-mixed orbit](phase-mixed-orbit.md)

Uniform [mean anomaly](mean-anomaly.md) implies a line probability inversely proportional to the orbital speed. For small [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) and [true anomaly](true-anomaly.md) $f$, it is proportional to $1-e\cos f$.
