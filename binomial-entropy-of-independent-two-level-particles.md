# Binomial entropy of independent two-level particles

↑ **Parent:** [Two-level system](two-level-system.md)

For independently labelled particles with nondegenerate levels, choosing which $N_+$ particles occupy the upper level gives a binomial [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md). With $x=(N_+-N_-)/N$, its large-number [entropy](entropy-split.md) is $Nk_B[\ln2-(1+x)\ln(1+x)/2-(1-x)\ln(1-x)/2]$. This counting assumes distinguishable or localized degrees of freedom, not indistinguishable particles sharing one pair of quantum orbitals.
