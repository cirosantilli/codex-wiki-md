# Internal-force cancellation in system momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's third law](newton-s-third-law.md)

For a fixed collection of two constant-[mass](mass.md) bodies in an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md), separate their mutual [forces](force-split.md) from the resultant [external force](external-force.md) on each:

$$
\frac{d\mathbf p_A}{dt}=\mathbf F_{B\mathrm{\ on\ }A}+\mathbf F_{A,\mathrm{ext}},\qquad
\frac{d\mathbf p_B}{dt}=\mathbf F_{A\mathrm{\ on\ }B}+\mathbf F_{B,\mathrm{ext}}.
$$

Adding the two [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) equations and using [Newton's third law](newton-s-third-law.md) gives

$$
\mathbf P=\mathbf p_A+\mathbf p_B,\qquad
\frac{d\mathbf P}{dt}=\mathbf F_{A,\mathrm{ext}}+\mathbf F_{B,\mathrm{ext}}.
$$

This is the proof of [internal-force cancellation in system momentum](internal-force-cancellation-in-system-momentum.md). For more bodies, each [internal force](internal-force.md) cancels with its partner once both appear in the sum. A boundary that excludes one interacting body leaves its [force](force-split.md) in the [external force](external-force.md) sum. A massive connecting spring must also be included, or its own changing [momentum](momentum-split.md) accounted for; its two endpoint [forces](force-split.md) are not automatically one [third-law interaction pair](third-law-interaction-pair.md) between the carts.

Zero resultant [external force](external-force.md) gives [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md); complete absence of [external forces](external-force.md) is sufficient but unnecessary. For constant total [mass](mass.md) $M$, the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) has [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf V=\mathbf P/M$ and obeys $M\,d\mathbf V/dt=\mathbf F_{\mathrm{ext}}$. Internal interactions can move the parts apart while leaving the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) moving uniformly.

Additional [external forces](external-force.md) do not invalidate the identified mutual pair. At the $4\,\mathrm N$ instant above, suppose a hand also exerts $+3\,\mathrm N$ on $A$. The mutual [forces](force-split.md) remain $-4\,\mathrm N$ and $+4\,\mathrm N$, but the net [forces](force-split.md) become $-1\,\mathrm N$ on $A$ and $+4\,\mathrm N$ on $B$. Consequently $a_A=-1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $a_B=+2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, $dP/dt=+3\,\mathrm N$, and the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) has [acceleration](acceleration.md) $+1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Pair equality by itself does not promise constant total [momentum](momentum-split.md).
