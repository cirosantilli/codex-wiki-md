# Left-continuous cumulative function of an atomic measure

↑ **Parent:** [Monotonic function](monotonic-function.md)

For positive summable weights $w_j$ at points $q_j$, the strict cumulative function $F(x)=\sum_{q_j<x}w_j$ is nondecreasing and left-continuous. A finite-head/tail argument proves left-continuity, and its right jump at an atom is the mass there. This strict convention differs at atoms from the usual right-continuous [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md).
