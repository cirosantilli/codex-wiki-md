# Cosine perturbation of the ground level in a one-dimensional box

↑ **Parent:** [Nondegenerate perturbation theory](nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md)

For the normalized sine eigenfunctions of a [particle in a box](infinite-square-well.md) on $(0,a)$, multiplying the lowest one by $\epsilon\cos(2\pi x/a)$ gives $\epsilon(\phi_3-\phi_1)/2$. Thus the first-order diagonal shift is $-\epsilon/2$, and the only second-order coupling is to level three. With $E_3=9E_1$, its contribution is $(\epsilon^2/4)/(E_1-E_3)=-\epsilon^2/(32E_1)$, giving the displayed expansion. This simple selection rule makes the full second-order [perturbation theory](perturbation-theory.md) sum a single term.
