# Hermite interpolating polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Hermite interpolation](hermite-interpolation.md)

At distinct [interpolation nodes](interpolation-node.md) $x_0,\ldots,x_n$, prescribed values $y_i$ and first [derivatives](derivative.md) $d_i$ determine exactly one [Hermite interpolating polynomial](hermite-interpolating-polynomial.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $2n+1$. The homogeneous data force a double [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) at every [interpolation node](interpolation-node.md). Hence the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) given by these $2n+2$ conditions is zero: a nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) with those double [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) would have [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at least $2n+2$. Equal finite [dimensions of a vector space](dimension-vector-space.md) then give existence as well as uniqueness. This [interpolation polynomial](interpolation-polynomial.md) is distinct from the [Hermite polynomials](hermite-polynomial.md) forming a classical [orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md) family.
