# OASIS insomnia trial

↑ **Parent:** [Contributory-causal treatment development](contributory-causal-treatment-development.md)

A trial of digital [CBT](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md) for insomnia versus usual practice in university students with insomnia. Changes in psychotic experiences support investigation of sleep as a contributory process, but the population and comparator constrain application to clinical [delusions](delusion.md).[Lancet Psychiatry 4, 749–758; 3755 university students with insomnia, digital CBT versus usual practice and population limits](the-effects-of-improving-sleep-on-mental-health-oasis-a-randomised-controlled-trial-with-mediation-analysis-by-daniel-freeman-and-others.md)
