# Simple J-holomorphic sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Simple J-holomorphic curve](simple-j-holomorphic-curve.md)

A simple J-holomorphic sphere is a [J-holomorphic sphere](j-holomorphic-sphere.md) that is a [simple J-holomorphic curve](simple-j-holomorphic-curve.md). Its automorphism group as a parametrized map is trivial, because any automorphism fixing the map fixes its locally injective points.
