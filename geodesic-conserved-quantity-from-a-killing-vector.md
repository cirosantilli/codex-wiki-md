# Geodesic conserved quantity from a Killing vector

↑ **Parent:** [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md)

If $U$ is the tangent to an affinely parametrized geodesic and $K$ is a Killing vector, then $g(K,U)$ is constant along the geodesic.
