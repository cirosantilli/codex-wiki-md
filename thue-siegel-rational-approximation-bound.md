# Thue-Siegel rational approximation bound

↑ **Parent:** [Thue-Siegel auxiliary polynomial](thue-siegel-auxiliary-polynomial.md)

If a real algebraic number $\alpha$ has degree $d\geq3$, then for every $\varepsilon>0$ there are only finitely many reduced fractions $p/q$ such that

$$
\left|\alpha-\frac pq\right|<q^{-d/2-1-\varepsilon}.
$$

The [Thue-Siegel auxiliary polynomial](thue-siegel-auxiliary-polynomial.md) proof chooses two such approximations, constructs an auxiliary polynomial nonzero at their pair, and compares its denominator lower bound with the upper bound supplied by its high-order zero at $(\alpha,\alpha)$.
