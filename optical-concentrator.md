# Optical concentrator

↑ **Parent:** [Etendue](etendue.md)

An [optical concentrator](optical-concentrator.md) redirects [light](light.md) from an entrance [area](surface-area.md) $A_{\rm in}$ to a smaller receiver [area](surface-area.md) $A_{\rm out}$. Its geometric concentration ratio is $C=A_{\rm in}/A_{\rm out}$. Specify the admitted angular distribution and losses before interpreting $C$ as an [irradiance](irradiance.md) gain: if a fraction $\eta$ of incident [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) reaches the receiver, the ratio of mean receiver to mean entrance [irradiance](irradiance.md) is $\eta C$.
