<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The preceding [Gödel sentence](../../../../godel-sentence.md) argument gives $S\vdash G\to\operatorname{Con}_S$. The permitted arithmetized positive half of the [Rosser sentence](../../../../rosser-sentence.md) argument is

$$
S\vdash\operatorname{Con}_S\to\neg BR.
$$

Pure first-order logic gives $\neg BR\to R$: if there is no proof of $R$, each antecedent in its defining universal implication is false. Therefore

$$
\boxed{S\vdash G\to\operatorname{Con}_S\to\neg BR\to R,\qquad S\vdash G\to R.}
$$

This uses the supplied internal Rosser implication, rather than replacing an external nonprovability fact by an unproved internal [first-order sentence](../../../../first-order-sentence.md).
