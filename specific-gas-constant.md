# Specific gas constant

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md)

The gas constant per unit [mass](mass.md) for a specified composition. An [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) obeys $p=\rho\mathcal RT$. With dimensionless [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md) $\mu$ relative to an atomic [mass](mass.md) unit, $\mathcal R=k_B/(\mu m_u)$; the common astrophysical notation $R^*/\mu$ uses a modified mass-based constant rather than the molar gas constant without a [mass](mass.md) conversion.
