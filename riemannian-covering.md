# Riemannian covering

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

A [covering map](covering-space.md) between [Riemannian manifolds](riemannian-manifold.md) that is a [local isometry](local-isometry.md). The metric on the covering manifold is the pullback of the base metric; its [deck transformations](deck-transformation.md) are [isometries](isometry.md). This local metric agreement makes the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) commute with pullback of smooth functions.
