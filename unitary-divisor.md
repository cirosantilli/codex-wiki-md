# Unitary divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor](divisor.md)

A [divisor](divisor.md) $d$ of a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$ is unitary when $\gcd(d,n/d)=1$. Each [prime power](prime-power.md) in the [prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) of $n$ must therefore be included in $d$ completely or omitted completely. Consequently, the sum of the $k$-th powers of unitary divisors is $\prod_{p^e\parallel n}(1+p^{ek})$.
