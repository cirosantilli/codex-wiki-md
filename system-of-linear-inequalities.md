# System of linear inequalities

↑ **Parent:** [Linear inequality](linear-inequality.md)

A system of linear inequalities requires several [linear inequalities](linear-inequality.md) to hold simultaneously. The notation $Ax\leq b$ compares coordinates componentwise, giving one [inequality](inequality-mathematics.md) per row of the [matrix](matrix.md) $A$. Its [solution set](solution-set.md) is the [intersection](set-intersection.md) of the corresponding [half-spaces](half-space.md). For non-strict inequalities it is a [linear polyhedron](linear-polyhedron.md), which may be empty. For example, $x\geq1$, $y\geq1$, $x+y\leq1$ are inconsistent: adding their equivalent forms $-x\leq-1$, $-y\leq-1$, $x+y\leq1$ gives $0\leq-1$. A [Farkas certificate for linear inequalities](farkas-certificate-for-linear-inequalities.md) generalizes this cancellation.
