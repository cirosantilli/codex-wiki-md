# Three drag regimes for a nearly occluding sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Control-volume drag formula for a sphere in a tube](control-volume-drag-formula-for-a-sphere-in-a-tube.md)

Use $F^*=F/(6\pi\mu aU)$ and $L^*=4L/(3a)$. The [control-volume drag formula for a sphere in a tube](control-volume-drag-formula-for-a-sphere-in-a-tube.md) gives the displayed uniform leading drag. If $L^*\ll\epsilon^{-1/2}$, $F^*\sim(4\pi/3)\epsilon^{-1/2}$ and local annular shear dominates. If $\epsilon^{-1/2}\ll L^*\ll\epsilon^{-5/2}$, $F^*\sim L^*$ and the [Hagen-Poiseuille flow](hagen-poiseuille-equation.md) resistance of the long tube dominates. If $L^*\gg\epsilon^{-5/2}$, $F^*\sim3\pi\epsilon^{-5/2}$ and pressure-driven bypass through the narrow gap dominates. The laboratory flow is piston-like in the first two limits and negligible in the third.
