# Haematoidin

↑ **Parent:** [Bilirubin](bilirubin.md)

A historical tissue-pigment name associated with iron-free [bilirubin](bilirubin.md), often encountered as crystals in older haemorrhages. Appearance alone need not establish the detailed composition of every tissue deposit.
