# Material surface

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A material surface is transported with its [fluid elements](fluid-element.md), including its boundary when open. Its oriented [material surface element](material-surface-element.md) evolves by tangent transport. [Magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md) conserves the flux through such an open surface.
