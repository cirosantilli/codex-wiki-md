# Chevalley basis

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)

A Chevalley basis of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) consists of suitable coroot elements and one vector $e_\alpha$ in each [root space](root-space.md), chosen so all [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) structure constants are integers. In particular $[e_\alpha,e_\beta]=N_{\alpha,\beta}e_{\alpha+\beta}$ when the sum is a [root](root-of-a-root-system.md), with $N_{\alpha,\beta}=\pm(p+1)$ where $\beta-p\alpha$ is the last negative-direction root in the [root string](root-string.md). For independent roots with no root sum the bracket is zero. The integral lattice makes reduction to other fields possible.
