# RKKY interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Indirect exchange](indirect-exchange.md)

A local moment induces conduction-electron spin polarization, which mediates a second-order coupling to another moment. The static electronic spin susceptibility fixes the spatial interaction. For a three-dimensional spherical Fermi surface its leading large-distance form oscillates as $\cos(2k_FR)/R^3$, up to the overall sign convention and prefactor. The sign alternates with separation. Other band structures and dimensions change this form.
