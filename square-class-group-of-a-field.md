# Square-class group of a field

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer theory](kummer-theory.md)

The square-class group identifies two nonzero elements of a [field](field.md) when their quotient is a square. Multiplication makes it an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) of exponent two, hence a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) over $\mathbb F_2$. It records quadratic extensions and the images of [Kummer theory](kummer-theory.md) for $n=2$.
