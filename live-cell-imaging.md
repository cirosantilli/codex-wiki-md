# Live-cell imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence microscopy](fluorescence-microscopy.md)

[Live-cell imaging](live-cell-imaging.md) follows living [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) over time. Labels, illumination and immobilization must be checked for effects on the process being measured; an apparently sharp image is not sufficient evidence that the cell remains physiologically unperturbed.
