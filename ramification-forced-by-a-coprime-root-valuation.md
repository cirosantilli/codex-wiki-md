# Ramification forced by a coprime root valuation

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification index](ramification-index.md)

Suppose $\alpha^m=a\in\mathbb Q^\times$ in a [number field](number-field.md) and $\mathfrak p$ lies above $p$. Its normalized integral [valuation](valuation.md) satisfies $m v_{\mathfrak p}(\alpha)=e_{\mathfrak p/p}v_p(a)$. If $m$ and $v_p(a)$ are coprime, then $m\mid e_{\mathfrak p/p}$. In a degree-$m$ field the [fundamental identity for prime decomposition](fundamental-identity-for-prime-decomposition.md) forces a unique prime, [ramification index](ramification-index.md) $m$, and [residue degree](residue-degree.md) one. This detects total [ramification of a prime](ramification-of-a-prime.md) even when the displayed defining polynomial is not [Eisenstein](eisenstein-criterion.md) at $p$.
