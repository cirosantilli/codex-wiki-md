# Duality of sequence spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md)

Coordinate pairing gives $(\ell^p)^*=\ell^q$ for $1<p<\infty$, $(\ell^1)^*=\ell^\infty$, and $(c_0)^*=\ell^1$.

These are continuous-dual identifications under coordinate pairing, related to but distinct from the definition of an [Lp space](lp-space.md).
