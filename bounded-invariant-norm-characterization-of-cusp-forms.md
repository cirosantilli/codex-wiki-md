# Bounded invariant norm characterization of cusp forms

↑ **Parent:** [Invariant norm of a modular form](invariant-norm-of-a-modular-form.md)

For $k>0$, a meromorphic function satisfying the modular transformation law is a [cusp form](cusp-form.md) exactly when its invariant norm is bounded. The bound first excludes interior poles. At each cusp a genuine periodic slash translate is bounded by $Cy^{-k/2}$, which tends to zero. Its punctured-disk expression therefore has a removable singularity with zero value. Conversely cusp Fourier expansions decay exponentially, and finitely many compact core regions control the rest. Positive weight is essential: a nonzero constant in weight zero satisfies the bound but is not a cusp form.
