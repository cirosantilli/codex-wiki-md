# Putamen

↑ **Parent:** [Striatum](striatum.md)

A striatal nucleus participating in motor and learning-related circuits. Its interaction with cortical timing systems is relevant to hypotheses about fluent [speech](speech.md), but a connectivity difference does not by itself establish a cause of [stuttering](stuttering.md).
