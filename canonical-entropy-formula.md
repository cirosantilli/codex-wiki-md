# Canonical entropy formula

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md)

For $Z=\sum_i e^{-\beta E_i}$ and $U=\langle E\rangle$, the [entropy](entropy-split.md) is $S=k_B\ln Z+U/T$. It follows directly by substituting canonical probabilities into $S=-k_B\sum_i p_i\ln p_i$.
