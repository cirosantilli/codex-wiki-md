# Fluid membrane shear response

↑ **Parent:** [Helfrich energy](helfrich-energy.md)

A fluid surface has no equilibrium static in-plane [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md), because material can rearrange after shear. This does not set its [membrane tension](membrane-tension.md) or [membrane bending modulus](membrane-bending-modulus.md) to zero. Time-dependent deformation can also produce viscous in-plane [shear stress](shear-stress.md); absence of a static elastic coefficient is not absence of dissipation.
