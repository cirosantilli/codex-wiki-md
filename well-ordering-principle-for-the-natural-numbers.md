# Well-ordering principle for the natural numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Natural number](natural-number.md)

Every nonempty subset of the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) has a least element in their usual order. Applying this principle to positive integer linear combinations proves [Bézout's identity](bezout-identity.md). This principle for the usual natural-number order is distinct from the general [well-ordering theorem](well-ordering-theorem.md) asserting that every set can be equipped with some well-order.
