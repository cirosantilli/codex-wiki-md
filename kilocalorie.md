# Kilocalorie

↑ **Parent:** [Energy](energy-split.md)

A unit of [energy](energy-split.md) equal to one thousand thermochemical calories, or $4184\,\mathrm J$. In dietary usage the capitalized Calorie commonly denotes a kilocalorie.
