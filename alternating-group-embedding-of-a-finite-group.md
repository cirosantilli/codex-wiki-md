# Alternating-group embedding of a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Group embedding](group-embedding.md)

[Cayley theorem](cayley-s-theorem.md) embeds a [finite group](finite-group.md) of order $n$ into $S_n$. Extend $\sigma\in S_n$ to $n+2$ letters and multiply it by the disjoint [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) $(n+1\ n+2)$ exactly when $\sigma$ is odd. Additivity of parity makes this an injective [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) into the [alternating group](alternating-group.md) $A_{n+2}$. Thus every [finite group](finite-group.md) is isomorphic to a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of some [alternating group](alternating-group.md).
