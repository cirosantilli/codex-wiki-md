# Off-shell nilpotence of the Yang-Mills BRST quartet

↑ **Parent:** [BRST nilpotence](brst-nilpotence.md)

Using the [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md), $s^2A=D(sc)+g(Dc)\times c=0$ because $D(c\times c)=2(Dc)\times c$. The [graded Jacobi identity](graded-jacobi-identity.md) gives $s^2c=g^2(c\times c)\times c/2=0$. [BRST nilpotence](brst-nilpotence.md) on the antighost and [Nakanishi-Lautrup field](nakanishi-lautrup-field.md) is immediate. No field equations are required.
