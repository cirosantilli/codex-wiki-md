# Scaling identity for greatest common divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md)

For positive integers $m,n,k$, the [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) scales by the common multiplier $k$. If $d=\gcd(m,n)$, then $kd$ is a common divisor of $km,kn$. Meanwhile [Bézout's identity](bezout-identity.md) expresses $kd$ as an integer linear combination of $km,kn$, so their [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) divides $kd$. These two comparisons prove the identity without prime factorization.
