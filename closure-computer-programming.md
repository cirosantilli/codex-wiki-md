# Closure (computer programming)

↑ **Parent:** [Functional programming](functional-programming.md)

A [function](function-split.md) together with the bindings of variables from its defining environment. A mutable captured variable makes successive calls stateful. Sharing such a closure between copied [iterators](iterator.md) can violate the promised independent enumeration even when their current values are copied separately.
