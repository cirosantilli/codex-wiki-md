# Photon-baryon sound speed

↑ **Parent:** [Tight-coupling approximation](tight-coupling-approximation.md)

For baryon-loading ratio $R=3\rho_b/(4\rho_\gamma)$, the photon-baryon sound speed is $c_s^2=1/[3(1+R)]$.
