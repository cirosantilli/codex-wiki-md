# Optimal importance density for a single integral

↑ **Parent:** [Importance sampling](importance-sampling.md)

For estimating $I=\int g(x)f(x)\,dx$ by the unbiased importance estimator $g(Y)f(Y)/q(Y)$, the proposal minimizing variance is

$$
q^*(x)=\frac{|g(x)|f(x)}{\int |g(u)|f(u)\,du}.
$$

This follows from the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Its normalizing constant and dependence on the particular integrand often make it impractical.
