<h1 id="poincare-duality-for-noncompact-manifolds">Poincaré duality for noncompact manifolds</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md)

Cap product with the locally finite [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) of an $n$-manifold without boundary gives the displayed duality, including a possibly nontrivial [orientation local system](orientation-local-system.md) $\mathcal O_M$. It is the noncompact version of [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md); for a compact manifold [Borel-Moore homology](borel-moore-homology.md) is ordinary homology. Locally it is the relative fundamental-class calculation in an oriented coordinate ball; compatibility on overlaps and the [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) give the global statement. Since the chain complex of locally finite homology is zero in negative degrees, this duality implies $H^k(M;\mathbb Z)=0$ for $k>n$.
