# K correction

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute magnitude](absolute-magnitude.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K_correction)

The K-correction converts an observed [photometric passband](photometric-passband.md) to a specified emitted [photometric passband](photometric-passband.md), accounting for [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) and the source spectrum. For a narrow same-band measurement, $K_R=-2.5\log_{10}[(1+z)L_\nu((1+z)\nu_R)/L_\nu(\nu_R)]$. Thus its sign depends on the spectrum, not on [redshift](redshift.md) alone. For $L_\nu\propto\nu^\alpha$, it is $-2.5(1+\alpha)\log_{10}(1+z)$. Finite-band corrections use the actual detector response and [magnitude zero point](magnitude-zero-point.md).
