# Poynting flux

↑ **Parent:** [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md)

The [Poynting flux](poynting-flux.md) through an oriented surface is the normal component of the electromagnetic energy-flow [vector](vector.md). Integrating it over area gives outward electromagnetic power. A scale estimate of the form [energy density](energy-density.md) [times](time-in-physics.md) propagation speed must still specify the field and geometric regime.
