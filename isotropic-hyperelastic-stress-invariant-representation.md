# Isotropic hyperelastic stress invariant representation

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)

For a [material isotropy](material-isotropy.md) and [material frame indifference](material-frame-indifference.md) [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) $W(F)=h(I_1,I_2,I_3)$, take $B=FF^T$, $I_1=\operatorname{tr}B$, $I_2=((\operatorname{tr}B)^2-\operatorname{tr}B^2)/2$, $I_3=\det B=J^2$, $J=\det F>0$. The chain rule and $d(\det F)=J F^{-T}:dF$ give $DW=2h_1F+2h_2(I_1I-B)F+2I_3h_3F^{-T}$. Multiplying by $F^T/J$ yields the displayed [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md). [Material isotropy](material-isotropy.md) allows an identity term and a quadratic term in $B$, as well as the term proportional to $B$.
