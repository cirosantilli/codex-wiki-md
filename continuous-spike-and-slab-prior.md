# Continuous spike-and-slab prior

↑ **Parent:** [Prior probability](prior-probability.md)

A [continuous spike-and-slab prior](continuous-spike-and-slab-prior.md) mixes a narrow continuous [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) around zero with a much wider component. It permits practical effect selection while retaining a continuous [posterior density](posterior-density.md). Its narrow component is not the exact-zero mass of a [point-null mixture prior](point-null-mixture-prior.md). A latent component indicator aids computation; a wide-component draw can still have small magnitude, so posterior effect thresholds and component membership answer different questions.
