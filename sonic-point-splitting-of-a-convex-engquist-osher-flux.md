# Sonic-point splitting of a convex Engquist-Osher flux

↑ **Parent:** [Engquist-Osher flux](engquist-osher-flux.md)

For a flux that is a differentiable [convex function](convex-function.md) with unique minimum at $s_*$, define $f^+(u)=f(u)-f(s_*)$ for $u>s_*$ and zero otherwise, and $f^-(u)=f(u)-f(s_*)$ for $u<s_*$ and zero otherwise. The [Engquist-Osher flux](engquist-osher-flux.md) is $F(a,b)=f(s_*)+f^+(a)+f^-(b)$. Thus transsonic [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md) states $a\le s_*\le b$ give $F=f(s_*)$, while reversed transsonic states give $F=f(a)+f(b)-f(s_*)$. This follows by integrating the positive and negative parts of $f'$.
