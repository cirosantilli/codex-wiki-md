# Neutron moderation

↑ **Parent:** [Slow neutron](slow-neutron.md)

[Neutron moderation](neutron-moderation.md) reduces the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) of fast [neutrons](neutron-split.md) through repeated collisions in a [neutron moderator](neutron-moderator.md). In a simple [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md) model, [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) is especially effective because a [proton](proton.md) and a [neutron](neutron-split.md) have nearly equal [masses](mass.md): the [maximum elastic recoil energy fraction](maximum-elastic-recoil-energy-fraction.md) can approach one. The [mean logarithmic energy decrement](mean-logarithmic-energy-decrement.md) describes typical accumulated slowing, whereas the maximum transfer describes a single endpoint.

Slowing, absorption and escape compete. A material can remove [neutrons](neutron-split.md) by [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md) before they become [thermal neutrons](thermal-neutron.md), and some [neutrons](neutron-split.md) escape its boundaries. Describing a [neutron moderator](neutron-moderator.md) therefore requires more than collision kinematics. Near [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md), target thermal motion can also give [energy](energy-split.md) back to a [neutron](neutron-split.md); the initially stationary target approximation must then be replaced.
