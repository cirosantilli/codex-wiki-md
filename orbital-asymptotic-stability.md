# Orbital asymptotic stability

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital stability](orbital-stability.md)

A periodic orbit has [orbital asymptotic stability](orbital-asymptotic-stability.md) if nearby trajectories remain close and their distance to the orbit tends to zero. A persistent phase shift along the orbit is allowed.
