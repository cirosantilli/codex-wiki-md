# Bondi-Hill crossover in a protoplanetary disk

↑ **Parent:** [Bondi radius](bondi-radius.md)

With [planet](planet.md)-to-[star](star.md) [mass](mass.md) ratio $q$ and [disk aspect ratio](disk-aspect-ratio.md) $h$, $r_B/a=q/h^2$ and $r_H/a=(q/3)^{1/3}$. Equating the two gives $q=h^3/\sqrt3$. The thermally limited [radius](radius.md) grows faster with [planet](planet.md) [mass](mass.md) than the tidally limited [radius](radius.md); an envelope outer scale is often the smaller of the two.
