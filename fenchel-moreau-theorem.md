# Fenchel-Moreau theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md)

A proper lower-semicontinuous convex functional on a real [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) equals its biconjugate, with the canonical embedding in the bidual understood. One inequality follows from the [Fenchel–Young inequality](fenchel-young-inequality.md); separation of a point below the closed convex epigraph gives the opposite inequality. This is the closed-convex analogue of recovering a function from all its affine supporting functions.
