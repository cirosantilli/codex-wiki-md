# Thermal bead drag and heat-bath power

↑ **Parent:** [Drag dissipation of a bead-spring pair](drag-dissipation-of-a-bead-spring-pair.md)

If the relative bead velocity $\mathbf v$ is independent of its separation and has a centered [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md) with $\langle v_iv_j\rangle=(2k_BT/m)\delta_{ij}$, averaging the [drag dissipation of a bead-spring pair](drag-dissipation-of-a-bead-spring-pair.md) gives the displayed gross loss. The cross term vanishes by the zero conditional mean. At zero [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md), this remains $3n\zeta k_BT/m>0$, although mechanical stress power is zero. A thermal bath replenishes microscopic kinetic energy at equilibrium. Equating this gross loss to polymer stress power omits heat-bath input and cannot follow from the independently specified Maxwellian velocity distribution. The overdamped [free-energy balance of a Hookean dumbbell](free-energy-balance-of-a-hookean-dumbbell.md) uses a [probability current velocity](probability-current-velocity.md) instead.
