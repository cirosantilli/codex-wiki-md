# Propagation delay

↑ **Parent:** [Combinational logic](combinational-logic.md)

The elapsed time between a change at a [logic gate](logic-gate.md)'s input and the corresponding settled change at its output, under specified loading and transition conditions. Dependent gate delays add along a path. The [combinational critical path](combinational-critical-path.md) gives the longest such input-to-output path; reconvergent paths of unequal delay can produce a [logic hazard](hazard-logic.md) even when the final [Boolean function](boolean-function.md) value does not change.
