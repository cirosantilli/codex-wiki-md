# Bockstein factorization through integral cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Bockstein homomorphism](bockstein-homomorphism.md)

The [Bockstein homomorphism](bockstein-homomorphism.md) for $0\to\mathbb Z/n\to\mathbb Z/n^2\to\mathbb Z/n\to0$, with injection $[a]\mapsto[na]$, is coefficient reduction $\rho$ after the [integral Bockstein homomorphism](integral-bockstein-homomorphism.md). The commuting maps of coefficient sequences prove this by naturality of the [connecting homomorphism](connecting-homomorphism.md).
