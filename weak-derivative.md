# Weak derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_derivative)

A locally integrable function $g$ is the $i$th weak derivative of $u$ when

$$
\int_UuD_i\varphi=-\int_Ug\varphi
$$

for every [test function](test-function.md) $\varphi\in C_c^\infty(U)$. Thus a weak derivative is a [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) that is represented by a locally integrable function.
