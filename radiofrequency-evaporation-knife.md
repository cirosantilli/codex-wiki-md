# Radiofrequency evaporation knife

↑ **Parent:** [Forced evaporative cooling](forced-evaporative-cooling.md)

A radiofrequency field induces transitions from trapped weak-field-seeking [magnetic sublevels](magnetic-sublevel.md) to untrapped states at a resonance surface. Atoms with sufficient [energy](energy-split.md) reach that surface and leave. Sweeping the [frequency](frequency.md) changes the [energy](energy-split.md) cutoff without equating every lost atom with the sample-average [energy](energy-split.md).
