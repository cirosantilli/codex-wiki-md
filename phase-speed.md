# Phase speed

↑ **Parent:** [Phase velocity](phase-velocity.md)

The magnitude of the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) of a real-frequency plane [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) is $|\omega|/|\mathbf k|$. In an anisotropic medium it depends on the direction of the [wave vector](wavevector.md) and need not equal the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) magnitude.
