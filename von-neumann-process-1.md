# von Neumann process 1

↑ **Parent:** [Projective measurement](projective-measurement.md)

The projection step in the traditional measurement account. For outcome projectors $P_j$, the nonselective state is $\sum_jP_j\rho P_j$; conditional on outcome $j$ it is $P_j\rho P_j/\operatorname{Tr}(P_j\rho)$. Distinguishing these from the coherent state produced by [quantum premeasurement](quantum-premeasurement.md) avoids identifying a pure correlated projector with its dephased mixture.
