# Resistor-referenced parallel-resonator response

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel RLC circuit](parallel-rlc-circuit.md)

When $R_s$ is in series with a [parallel RLC circuit](parallel-rlc-circuit.md), the ratio of parallel-network [voltage](voltage.md) to [voltage](voltage.md) across $R_s$ is its impedance divided by $R_s$. Its peak is $R/R_s$, and its [phase](phase-waves.md) passes from $+\pi/2$ through zero to $-\pi/2$ as [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) rises through $1/\sqrt{LC}$. This is not the transfer ratio relative to the total input [voltage](voltage.md), which includes the additional series [voltage](voltage.md) drop and has peak $R/(R+R_s)$. Identifying [voltage](voltage.md) endpoints is necessary before using the [voltage](voltage.md) divider formula.
