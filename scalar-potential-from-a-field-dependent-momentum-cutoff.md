# Scalar potential from a field-dependent momentum cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum energy density](vacuum-energy-density.md)

If a free real [scalar field](scalar-field.md)'s three-[momentum](momentum-split.md) [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md) is identified with the absolute [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) of another [scalar field](scalar-field.md), the displayed [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) is even and has its unique [global minimum](global-minimum.md) at zero. It grows as $\varphi^4/(16\pi^2)$ for $|\varphi|\gg m$, but as $m|\varphi|^3/(12\pi^2)$ for $|\varphi|\ll m$ with $m>0$. The positive derivative with respect to $|\varphi|$ proves that this contribution alone does not produce [spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md). This cutoff identification is a model assumption, distinct from deriving a complete [effective potential](effective-potential.md) for a dynamical [field](field.md).
