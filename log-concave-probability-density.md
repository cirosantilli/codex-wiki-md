# Log-concave probability density

↑ **Parent:** [Probability density function](probability-density-function.md)

A probability density is log-concave when its logarithm is a [concave function](concave-function.md) on its support. Tangent lines to a differentiable concave log density lie above its graph, giving exponential envelopes for [rejection sampling](rejection-sampling.md).
