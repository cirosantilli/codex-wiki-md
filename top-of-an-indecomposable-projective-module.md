# Top of an indecomposable projective module

↑ **Parent:** [Indecomposable module](indecomposable-module.md)

For a nonzero finitely generated [indecomposable module](indecomposable-module.md) $P$ that is a [projective module](projective-module.md) over a [right Artinian ring](right-artinian-ring.md) $A$, its top $P/PJ(A)$ is a [simple module](irreducible-module.md). The [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) $J(A)$ is a [nilpotent ideal](nilpotent-ideal.md), so the top is nonzero; the quotient is a [semisimple module](semisimple-module.md) over the [semisimple ring](semisimple-ring.md) $A/J(A)$. If it decomposed, a nontrivial [idempotent](idempotent.md) of its [endomorphism ring](endomorphism-ring.md) would lift by projectivity to an [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) of $P$. The [Hopkins-Levitzki theorem](hopkins-levitzki-theorem.md) gives finite [composition length](composition-length.md), and the [Fitting lemma](fitting-lemma.md) makes the lift either invertible or nilpotent. Its induced map on the top would have the same property, impossible for that nontrivial [idempotent](idempotent.md).
