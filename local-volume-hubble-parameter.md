# Local volume Hubble parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md)

For the normal flow with zero [shift vector](shift-vector.md), $a_{\mathrm{loc}}=(\det h)^{1/6}$ obeys $H_{\mathrm{loc}}=N^{-1}\partial_t\log a_{\mathrm{loc}}=-K/3$ in the negative [extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface](extrinsic-curvature-of-a-spatial-hypersurface.md) convention. The [expansion scalar](expansion-scalar.md) is $\theta=-K=3H_{\mathrm{loc}}$.
