# Rossiter-McLaughlin effect

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar obliquity](stellar-obliquity.md)

A transiting body hides different velocity regions of a rotating stellar disc and causes an anomalous measured [Doppler shift](doppler-effect.md). Its time dependence constrains the sky-projected spin-orbit angle, which is not generally the full [stellar obliquity](stellar-obliquity.md).
