# Coercive Neumann energy with a positive reaction term

↑ **Parent:** [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md)

A symmetric uniformly elliptic divergence-form energy with zeroth-order coefficient at least $\mu>0$ controls the full $H^1$ [norm](norm.md), including constants. Its quadratic energy minus a [bounded](boundedness.md) load has a unique [minimizer](global-minimizer.md) by the direct method, and vanishing [first variation](first-variation.md) gives the natural [Neumann](neumann-boundary-condition.md) weak equation. Unlike a pure [Neumann](neumann-boundary-condition.md) [Laplacian](laplacian.md), this problem needs no zero-mean compatibility condition. The exact energy difference from the [weak solution](weak-solution.md) is half the bilinear energy of the difference.
