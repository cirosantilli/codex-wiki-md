# Iodine-cell Doppler calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md)

An [iodine](iodine.md) cell places reference absorption on the same beam as stellar lines. A template and known cell transmission constrain stellar shift and instrumental line shape together. The observed spectrum is modelled by multiplying the stellar and cell spectra before instrumental [convolution](convolution.md).
