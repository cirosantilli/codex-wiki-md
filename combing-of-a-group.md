# Combing of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)

Choose a finite symmetric generating alphabet $\Sigma$ and one word $\sigma(g)$ representing each group element, with $\sigma(1)$ the empty word. The associated [paths](continuous-path.md) in the [Cayley graph](cayley-graph.md) start at the identity and traverse one edge per unit time, remaining at their endpoint after completion. A synchronous [combing](combing-of-a-group.md) has a constant $K$ such that [paths](continuous-path.md) to adjacent endpoints stay within $K$ at equal times. An asynchronous version permits increasing reparametrizations. The [formal language](formal-language.md) need not be a [regular language](regular-language.md); regular synchronous representative languages define [automatic groups](automatic-group.md).
