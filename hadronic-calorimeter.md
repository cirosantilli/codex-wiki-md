# Hadronic calorimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Calorimeter (particle physics)](calorimeter-particle-physics.md)

A [particle calorimeter](calorimeter-particle-physics.md) designed to contain hadronic showers and estimate incident hadron [energy](energy-split.md). Noncompensating response, invisible nuclear breakup energy and leakage complicate its calibration relative to an [electromagnetic calorimeter](electromagnetic-calorimeter.md).
