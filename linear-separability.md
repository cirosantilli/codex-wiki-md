# Linear separability

↑ **Parent:** [Binary classification](binary-classification.md)

Two subsets of a [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) are weakly linearly separable when a [hyperplane](hyperplane.md) with nonzero normal vector places them in opposite closed [half-spaces](half-space.md). Points on the separating [hyperplane](hyperplane.md) may belong to either subset. Strict separation instead requires strictly opposite signs, excluding all boundary ties.
