# Sine-Gordon equation

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable systems](integrable-systems-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sine-Gordon_equation)

In light-cone coordinates, the sine-Gordon equation can be written $u_{XT}=\sin u$. Its [inverse scattering transform](inverse-scattering-transform.md), [solitons](soliton.md), breathers, and scaling symmetries make it an [integrable system](integrable-systems-split.md).
