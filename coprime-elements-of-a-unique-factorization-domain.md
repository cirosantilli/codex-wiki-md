# Coprime elements of a unique factorization domain

↑ **Parent:** [Unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md)

Two nonzero elements with no common nonunit irreducible factor. In a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md) this is equivalent to their greatest common divisor being a unit. Coprimality of numerator and denominator makes a fraction reduced, so simultaneous restrictions on its denominator can force it to be a [regular function](regular-function.md).
