# Effusion

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effusion)

[Effusion](effusion.md) is molecular passage through a small opening whose dimensions, including its thickness, are much smaller than the intermolecular [mean free path](mean-free-path.md). For an equilibrium [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md), the one-way particle rate through a thin hole of area $A$ is $\dot N=A p/\sqrt{2\pi m k_BT}$. This weights molecules by their wall-normal [velocity](velocity.md), rather than just counting all molecules in the reservoir. [Fitzpatrick, Molecular Effusion, equations 5.284–5.285](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/355/Surveyhtml/node224.html)
