# 1973 plasmid construction experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Plasmid](plasmid.md)

In his account of the 1973 work, [Stanley Norman Cohen](stanley-norman-cohen.md) describes purified R6-5 [plasmid](plasmid.md) [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) cut with [EcoRI](ecori.md), reassociated and joined by [DNA ligase](dna-ligase.md). The mixture entered [calcium chloride](calcium-chloride.md)-treated [Escherichia coli](escherichia-coli.md); plates containing [antibiotics](antibiotic.md) selected [biological colonies](colony-biology.md) showing [antibiotic resistance](antibiotic-resistance.md). The recovered pSC102 carried three R6-5 fragments and [kanamycin](kanamycin.md) [antibiotic resistance](antibiotic-resistance.md). The team then joined material from pSC102 to the carrier pSC101 providing [tetracycline](tetracycline.md) [antibiotic resistance](antibiotic-resistance.md), recovering pSC105 with both forms of [antibiotic resistance](antibiotic-resistance.md).[Cohen (2013), The Hawaii Meeting and the Initial DNA Cloning Experiments, Figure 2 and Figure 3 caption; pSC102, pSC101 and pSC105 identities](dna-cloning-a-personal-view-after-40-years-by-stanley-norman-cohen.md)

Stained [agarose gel electrophoresis](agarose-gel-electrophoresis.md), gradient [centrifugation](centrifugation.md) and [electron microscope](electron-microscope.md) comparisons characterized recovered [plasmids](plasmid.md). Parental and mixture lanes in Figure 3 supplied fragment comparisons; pSC101 alone gave one [EcoRI](ecori.md) fragment, while pSC105 combined its carrier fragment with the resistance-bearing fragment. This supported joined, propagating genetic material beyond selection alone. The comparisons did not determine every [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) or establish a universal construction efficiency.[Cohen (2013), paragraphs describing the two laboratories' analyses and Figure 3, top and bottom: parental, mixed and recovered DNA comparisons](dna-cloning-a-personal-view-after-40-years-by-stanley-norman-cohen.md)

The interesting inference combines two kinds of evidence: a recoverable [phenotype](phenotype.md) identifies descendants worth examining, and the extracted [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) tests a structural explanation. The fragment comparisons are molecular [scientific controls](scientific-control.md); they answer a different objection from a control for background growth. The hypothetical diagrams here illustrate that logic without reproducing the historical fragment lengths or [biological colony](colony-biology.md) counts.
