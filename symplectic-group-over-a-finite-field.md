# Symplectic group over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [General linear group over a finite field](general-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md)

This group preserves a nondegenerate [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md) on $\mathbb F_q^{2m}$. Choosing a nonzero vector and a partner of pairing one, then recursing on their orthogonal complement, gives $|Sp_{2m}(q)|=q^{m^2}\prod_{i=1}^m(q^{2i}-1)$. Its center consists of scalars with $\lambda^2=1$.
