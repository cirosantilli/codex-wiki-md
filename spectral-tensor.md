# Spectral tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Power spectrum](power-spectrum.md)

For a statistically homogeneous vector [random field](random-field.md), its spectral tensor is the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of the componentwise two-point [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) $C_{ij}(\mathbf r)=\mathbb E[B_i(\mathbf x+\mathbf r)B_j(\mathbf x)]$. It distributes cross-correlations between components over [wavevectors](wavevector.md). With $\widehat B(\mathbf k)=\int B(\mathbf r)e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}d^dr$, homogeneity gives $\mathbb E[\widehat B_i(\mathbf k)\widehat B_j(\mathbf k')]=(2\pi)^d\delta(\mathbf k+\mathbf k')\widehat C_{ij}(\mathbf k)$, understood as an identity of [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) for an infinite stationary field.
