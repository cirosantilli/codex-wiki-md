# Influence-function representer

↑ **Parent:** [Pathwise differentiability of a statistical functional](pathwise-differentiability-of-a-statistical-functional.md)

An influence-function representer is a centered [square-integrable function](square-integrable-function.md) representing [derivatives](derivative.md) of a [statistical functional](statistical-functional.md) along all admissible [score functions](informant-function.md). Representers may differ by a [function](function-split.md) orthogonal to the [statistical tangent space](statistical-tangent-space.md). This pathwise definition is distinct from defining an [influence function](influence-function.md) solely by point-mass contamination paths.
