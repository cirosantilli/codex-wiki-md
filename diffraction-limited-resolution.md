# Diffraction-limited resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction-limited system](diffraction-limited-system.md)

For a circular illuminated aperture of diameter $D$, the characteristic angular [diffraction](diffraction.md) scale is $\lambda/D$. The first zero of the ideal intensity pattern lies at approximately $1.22\lambda/D$.
