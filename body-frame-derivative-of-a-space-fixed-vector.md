# Body-frame derivative of a space-fixed vector

↑ **Parent:** [Principal body frame](principal-body-frame.md)

If a vector $k$ is fixed in space while a body frame rotates with [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega$, its body-frame components satisfy

$$
\left(\frac{dk}{dt}\right)_{\rm body}=-\omega\times k.
$$
