# Pressure-temperature path-work identity

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)

Suppose a small [volume](volume.md) change obeys $dV=-V_1\kappa_TdP+V_1\alpha dT$, with its displayed coefficients held constant. Work on the body is $\delta W=V_1\kappa_TP\,dP-V_1\alpha P\,dT$. Two rectangular pressure-temperature paths between the same endpoints differ by $W_a-W_b=V_1\alpha\Delta P\Delta T$, where path a changes [temperature](temperature-split.md) first. The straight interpolation samples the mean [pressure](pressure-split.md) during its [temperature](temperature-split.md) change, giving $W_c=(W_a+W_b)/2$. Variable material coefficients need not preserve that average relation.
