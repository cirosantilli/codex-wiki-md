# Hydrogen-helium free-free composition factor

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal free-free emissivity](thermal-free-free-emissivity.md)

For fully ionized [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and [helium](helium.md) with hydrogen [mass fraction](mass-fraction.md) $x_H$, $n_e=(1+x_H)\rho/(2m_p)$ and $\sum_i Z_i^2n_i=\rho/m_p$. With the same unit [Gaunt factor](gaunt-factor.md) for both species, [thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) at fixed [electron](electron.md) [number density](number-density.md) is multiplied by $2/(1+x_H)$ relative to pure ionized [hydrogen](hydrogen.md). The factor is one for pure [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and two for pure [helium](helium.md).
