# Homotopy invariance of de Rham cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md)

Smoothly homotopic maps induce the same map on de Rham cohomology. Integrating [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md) along a homotopy gives a cochain homotopy

$$
F_1^*-F_0^*=dK+Kd.
$$
