# Volume averages in a shearing box

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing-periodic boundary condition](shearing-periodic-boundary-condition.md)

Periodic azimuthal and vertical boundary terms vanish. Radial boundary terms cancel after the azimuthal translation prescribed by the [shearing-periodic boundary condition](shearing-periodic-boundary-condition.md). Consequently the [volume average](volume-average.md) of a divergence is zero, enabling integration by parts for the smooth shearing-periodic fields.
