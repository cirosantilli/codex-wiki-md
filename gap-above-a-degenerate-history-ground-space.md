# Gap above a degenerate history ground space

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman-Kitaev Hamiltonian](feynman-kitaev-hamiltonian.md)

After undoing [quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md) propagation, the Hamiltonian without output penalty is $Q\otimes|0\rangle\langle0|+I\otimes E$. The common kernel is the valid input space times the uniform clock vector. On its orthogonal complement, the [smallest angle between two subspaces](smallest-angle-between-two-subspaces.md) obeys $\cos\vartheta=\sqrt{T/(T+1)}$. The [Kitaev geometrical lemma](kitaev-geometrical-lemma.md) and path gap $2/(T+1)^2$ give $\Delta\geq(T+1)^{-3}$. Degenerate valid [quantum witnesses](quantum-witness.md) must be removed together when computing this angle.
