# Threshold stopping of an independent Gaussian series

↑ **Parent:** [Almost sure convergence criterion for an independent Gaussian series](almost-sure-convergence-criterion-for-an-independent-gaussian-series.md)

Let $M_n=\sum_{k\leq n}a_kZ_k$, and stop on first reaching a fixed positive threshold. If the coefficient squares are summable, [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md) gives the stopped mean zero, with the terminal limit used on $T=\infty$. If the coefficient squares diverge, $\mathbb P(M_n\geq R)\to1/2$ for each fixed $R$. Thus $\mathbb P(\sup_nM_n=\infty)\geq1/2$. This event is unaffected by changing finitely many of the [independent](independent-random-variables.md) variables, so the [Kolmogorov zero-one law](kolmogorov-s-zero-one-law.md) makes its probability one. Hence $T<\infty$ and $M_T$ is at least the positive threshold; its [expectation](expected-value.md), finite or infinite, cannot be zero.
