# Massive supermultiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md)

A [massive supermultiplet](massive-supermultiplet.md) is a [unitary irreducible representation](unitary-irreducible-representation.md) of the [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md) with positive timelike momentum. At rest, its nonzero [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) become [fermionic creation operators](fermionic-creation-operator.md) and [fermionic annihilation operators](fermionic-annihilation-operator.md). Without [central charges in supersymmetry](central-charge-in-supersymmetry.md), four-dimensional extended [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) has $2\mathcal N$ complex oscillators; a spin-$j$ [Clifford vacuum](clifford-vacuum.md) gives $(2j+1)2^{2\mathcal N}$ states.
