<h1 id="codimension-one-stiefel-whitney-immersion-obstruction">Codimension-one Stiefel–Whitney immersion obstruction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md)

An [immersion](immersion.md) of a smooth $d$-manifold in $\mathbb R^{d+1}$ supplies a rank-one [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) $\nu$ with $TM\oplus\nu\cong\varepsilon^{d+1}$. The [Whitney product formula for Stiefel–Whitney classes](whitney-product-formula-for-stiefel-whitney-classes.md) therefore requires $w(\nu)=w(TM)^{-1}$. A [real line bundle](real-line-bundle.md) has no positive-degree classes beyond $w_1$, so every higher-degree part of the inverse total class must vanish. For $\mathbb{RP}^2\times\mathbb{RP}^2$, the [stabilized tangent bundle of real projective space](stabilized-tangent-bundle-of-real-projective-space.md) gives $w(TM)^{-1}=(1+a)(1+b)$ in $\mathbb F_2[a,b]/(a^3,b^3)$. Its nonzero degree-two part $ab$ excludes an [immersion](immersion.md) in $\mathbb R^5$ even though the normal line need not be orientable.
