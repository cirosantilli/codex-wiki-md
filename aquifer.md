# Aquifer

↑ **Parent:** [Porous-media flow](porous-media-flow-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquifer)

An aquifer is a permeable geological formation containing and transmitting groundwater. A confined aquifer lies beneath a confining layer; an [unconfined aquifer](unconfined-aquifer.md) instead has a free water table.
