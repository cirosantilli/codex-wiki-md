<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $\rho=\varphi^\dagger\varphi$. The heavy-field [equation of motion](../../../../equation-of-motion.md) is

$$
(\Box+M^2)\phi=-g\rho,
\qquad
\phi=-\frac g{M^2}\rho+\frac g{M^4}\Box\rho+O(M^{-6}).
$$

This solution describes the low-energy sector without an independent incoming heavy-particle wave. It is valid when all external [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) invariants and light masses squared are small compared with $M^2$.

After an integration by parts, the terms involving the heavy [field](../../../../field.md) are $-\tfrac12\phi(\Box+M^2)\phi-g\rho\phi$. Substituting the solution into both terms, rather than the source term alone, gives

$$
\Delta\mathcal L_{\rm eff}=\frac{g^2}{2}\rho(\Box+M^2)^{-1}\rho
=\frac{g^2}{2M^2}\rho^2-\frac{g^2}{2M^4}\rho\Box\rho+O(M^{-6}).
$$

Hence, with the question's sign convention for the local [quartic interaction](../../../../quartic-interaction.md),

$$
\boxed{\lambda=-\frac{g^2}{2M^2},\qquad
\mathcal L_{\rm eff}=\partial_\mu\varphi^\dagger\partial^\mu\varphi-m^2\rho-\lambda\rho^2+O(M^{-4}).}
$$

This is [integrating out a field](../../../../integrating-out-a-field.md) followed by a [derivative expansion](../../../../derivative-expansion.md). The negative quartic means attraction. It also makes the truncated potential unbounded at large fields; minimizing the original potential over $\phi$ reveals the same negative quartic. Thus neither displayed model, without extra stabilizing interactions, supplies a globally stable nonperturbative vacuum. The matching describes perturbative low-energy scattering about the local vacuum. The simple equation-of-motion matching fixes the leading tree-level coefficient; precision quantum matching requires consistent loop renormalization and the additional effective operators at the requested accuracy.
