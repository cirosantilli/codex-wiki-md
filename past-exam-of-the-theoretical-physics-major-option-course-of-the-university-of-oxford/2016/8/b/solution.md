<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first-order [Dyson series](../../../../dyson-series.md) contains $-ig\int d^4w\,\varphi^\dagger\varphi\phi$. For an amputated three-point [Feynman diagram](../../../../feynman-diagram.md), each of the three distinct field types contracts in exactly one way. Writing each external plane wave in momentum space produces

$$
\int d^4w\,e^{-i(p_1+p_2+p_3)\cdot w}=(2\pi)^4\delta^4(p_1+p_2+p_3).
$$

Thus the momentum-space [Feynman rule](../../../../feynman-rule.md), with all [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md) incoming, is

$$
\boxed{-ig\,(2\pi)^4\delta^4(p_1+p_2+p_3).}
$$

After stripping the conservation delta factor, it is simply $-ig$. There is no $3!$ combinatorial factor because the interaction has three distinct field slots. The charged lines carry opposite charge-flow orientations and the real heavy line is neutral.
