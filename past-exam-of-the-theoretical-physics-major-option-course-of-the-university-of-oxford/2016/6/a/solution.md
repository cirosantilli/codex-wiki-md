<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The continuous [spacetime symmetries](../../../../spacetime-symmetry.md) are [translations](../../../../translation-geometry.md), spatial [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) and [Lorentz boosts](../../../../lorentz-boost.md), together the connected [Poincaré group](../../../../poincare-group.md). The theory also has local electromagnetic [U(1) gauge symmetry](../../../../u-1-gauge-symmetry.md), which is an internal symmetry rather than a spacetime transformation. A nonzero mass and a dimensionful interaction do not give an additional exact [scale invariance](../../../../scale-invariance.md).

In four dimensions the [action](../../../../action.md) is dimensionless and $[\partial]=1$. The two [kinetic terms](../../../../kinetic-term.md) give

$$
\boxed{[a]=1,\quad[A_\mu]=1,\quad[F_{\mu\nu}]=2,\quad[m_a]=1,\quad[g_a]=-1.}
$$

The axion-photon operator has [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) five, so this is an [effective field theory](../../../../effective-field-theory.md). Its dimensionless interaction strength grows with energy as $|g_a|E$. Without information about an ultraviolet completion, a conservative parametric validity condition is $E\ll\Lambda$ with $\Lambda\sim|g_a|^{-1}$, and $m_a$ must also lie below that scale for the axion to be a degree of freedom in the effective theory. Numerical factors such as $4\pi$ depend on the ultraviolet dynamics and on the chosen perturbative criterion; the Lagrangian alone does not fix them.
