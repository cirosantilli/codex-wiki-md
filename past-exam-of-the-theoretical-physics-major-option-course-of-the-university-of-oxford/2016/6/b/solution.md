<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [axion](../../../../axion.md) variation gives $\partial\mathcal L/\partial a=-m_a^2a+g_aF_{\mu\nu}\widetilde F^{\mu\nu}/4$, so

$$
\boxed{(\Box+m_a^2)a=\frac{g_a}{4}F_{\mu\nu}\widetilde F^{\mu\nu}.}
$$

For the [photon](../../../../photon.md) variation, the [dual electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../dual-electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) obeys $\delta(F_{\rho\sigma}\widetilde F^{\rho\sigma})=2\widetilde F^{\rho\sigma}\delta F_{\rho\sigma}$. Antisymmetry then gives

$$
\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu A_\nu)}=-F^{\mu\nu}+g_a a\widetilde F^{\mu\nu}.
$$

There is no undifferentiated $A_\nu$, hence

$$
\boxed{\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=g_a\partial_\mu(a\widetilde F^{\mu\nu})
=g_a(\partial_\mu a)\widetilde F^{\mu\nu}.}
$$

The last equality uses the electromagnetic [Bianchi identity](../../../../bianchi-identity.md) $\partial_\mu\widetilde F^{\mu\nu}=0$. These signs follow from the positive $aF\widetilde F$ interaction in the source; reversing that interaction reverses both source terms. The induced electromagnetic current is identically conserved: its divergence contracts a symmetric second derivative of $a$ with an antisymmetric tensor, while the remaining term vanishes by the same [Bianchi identity](../../../../bianchi-identity.md).
