<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Impose the discrete [Z2 symmetry](../../../../z2-symmetry.md) $\boxed{\Phi\mapsto-\Phi}$. The quadratic and quartic [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) invariants are unchanged, while $\operatorname{tr}\Phi^3$ changes sign. Invariance therefore forbids a nonzero coefficient of that cubic operator. This is an additional symmetry of the [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md), not a consequence of [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md) conjugation alone; cubic traces are permitted by the latter.
