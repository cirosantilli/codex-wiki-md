Here the imaginary-time [Heisenberg picture](../../../../heisenberg-picture.md) position operator is $\bar x(\tau)=e^{\tau H_0}\hat x e^{-\tau H_0}$, where $H_0=H(0,0)$. Bosonic [time ordering](../../../../time-ordering.md) $T_\tau$ places the larger imaginary time to the left without an exchange sign. The brackets denote the normalized [thermal expectation value](../../../../thermal-expectation-value.md)

$$
\langle O\rangle_\beta=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-\beta H_0}O)}{Z_0},
\qquad Z_0=\operatorname{Tr}e^{-\beta H_0}.
$$

The trace is cyclic and $H_0$ is time independent, so the [two-point correlation function](../../../../two-point-correlation-function.md) depends only on the time difference and extends periodically with period $\beta$. This periodicity is a property of the thermal [correlation function](../../../../correlation-function.md), not the operator identity $\bar x(\tau+\beta)=\bar x(\tau)$.
