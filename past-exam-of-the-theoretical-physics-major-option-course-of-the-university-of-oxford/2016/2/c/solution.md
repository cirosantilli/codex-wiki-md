<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Holstein–Primakoff transformation](../../../../holstein-primakoff-transformation.md) replaces each finite-dimensional [spin](../../../../spin.md) algebra by a bosonic [creation operator](../../../../creation-operator.md) and [annihilation operator](../../../../annihilation-operator.md), while restricting the local [occupation number](../../../../occupation-number.md) to $0\le n_j\le2S$. The number $n_j=a_j^\dagger a_j$ counts deviations from the fully polarized reference [quantum state](../../../../quantum-state.md). Its zero-boson [Fock vacuum](../../../../fock-vacuum.md) has $\widetilde S^z=S$.

The representation is exact on this physical subspace: the ordered square root gives $\widetilde S^+|n\rangle=\sqrt{n(2S-n+1)}|n-1\rangle$ and $\widetilde S^-|n\rangle=\sqrt{(n+1)(2S-n)}|n+1\rangle$, reproducing the [spin commutation relations](../../../../spin-commutation-relations.md). For $n_j\ll S$,

$$
\widetilde S^+_j=\sqrt{2S}\left(1-\frac{n_j}{4S}+\cdots\right)a_j,
$$

so the leading [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) is quadratic in bosons and describes [spin waves](../../../../spin-wave.md).

The square root generates boson interactions beyond leading order and its operator ordering matters. Truncation also enlarges the [Hilbert space](../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) to unphysical occupations unless the low-density approximation remains self-consistent. Large fluctuations, particularly near a gap closing or in low dimensions, can invalidate the [linear spin-wave approximation](../../../../linear-spin-wave-approximation.md) even when its algebraic manipulation is straightforward.
