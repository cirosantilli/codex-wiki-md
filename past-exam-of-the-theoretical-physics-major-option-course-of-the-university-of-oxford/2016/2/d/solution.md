<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose the positive-$x$ reference [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) and use the cyclic [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md)

$$
\widetilde S^z=S^x,\qquad\widetilde S^x=S^y,\qquad\widetilde S^y=S^z.
$$

This is a right-handed permutation, so it preserves the [spin commutation relations](../../../../spin-commutation-relations.md). It puts the ordered direction along the [Holstein–Primakoff transformation](../../../../holstein-primakoff-transformation.md)'s quantization axis. To the required order,

$$
\widetilde S^z_j=S-n_j,\quad
\widetilde S^x_j=\sqrt{\frac S2}(a_j+a_j^\dagger),\quad
\widetilde S^y_j=-i\sqrt{\frac S2}(a_j-a_j^\dagger).
$$

The longitudinal exchange contributes $-LJ^xS^2+2SJ^x\sum_jn_j+O(S^0)$. The transverse terms contribute

$$
-\frac S2\sum_j\left[(J^y+J^z)(a_j^\dagger a_{j+1}+a_{j+1}^\dagger a_j)
+(J^y-J^z)(a_ja_{j+1}+a_j^\dagger a_{j+1}^\dagger)\right].
$$

Dropping the constant and all terms of order $S^0$ or smaller gives the stated [linear spin-wave approximation](../../../../linear-spin-wave-approximation.md), with

$$
\boxed{A=-\frac S2(J^y+J^z),\qquad B=-\frac S2(J^y-J^z),\qquad C=2SJ^x.}
$$

The anomalous pair terms are present because the two transverse couplings differ. A different right-handed choice of transverse axes can change the sign of $B$ by a boson phase convention, while leaving the [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) unchanged.
