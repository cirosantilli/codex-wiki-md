<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $S=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ be the base-[spin](../../../../spin.md) insertion matrix. In the [eigenbasis](../../../../eigenbasis.md) from [solution](../c/solution.md), its diagonal matrix elements vanish: $\langle+|S|+\rangle=\langle-|S|-\rangle=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\langle\sigma_1\rangle=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(ST^L)}{\operatorname{Tr}T^L}=0,}
$$

not only at large $L$ but for every finite ring at zero field.

This vanishing is not merely an averaging artifact hiding a finite-temperature ordered phase. The two-point [correlation function](../../../../correlation-function.md) is $\langle\sigma_1\sigma_{1+r}\rangle=(\lambda_-^r\lambda_+^{L-r}+\lambda_+^r\lambda_-^{L-r})/Z_L$. In the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md) it becomes $(\lambda_-/\lambda_+)^r$, with finite [correlation length](../../../../correlation-length.md) $\xi^{-1}=\log(\lambda_+/\lambda_-)$ for every $T>0$ and $J>0$. Thermal [domain walls](../../../../domain-wall.md) destroy long-range alignment in this one-dimensional short-range system. At zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), the two fully aligned [ground states](../../../../ground-state.md) are degenerate; that limiting case must be distinguished from any fixed positive [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md).
