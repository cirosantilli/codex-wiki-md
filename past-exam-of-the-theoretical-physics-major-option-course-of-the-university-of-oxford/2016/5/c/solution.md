<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) is [Lorentz invariant](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md), so a scalar-valued static solution can be composed with a [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md). Let $X=\gamma(x-vt-x_0)$, with $\gamma=(1-v^2)^{-1/2}$ and $|v|<1$. If $\phi(x,t)=f(X)$, the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
(\partial_t^2-\partial_x^2)f(X)=\gamma^2(v^2-1)f''(X)=-f''(X).
$$

The boosted [equation of motion](../../../../equation-of-motion.md) is therefore exactly the static one already verified in [solution](../b/solution.md), and

$$
\boxed{\phi(x,t)=\frac4b\arctan\exp\left[k\gamma(x-vt-x_0)\right]}
$$

is a solution. The center moves as $x=x_0+vt$: $v$ is the physical [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) in units where the limiting speed is one. The [kink](../../../../scalar-field-kink.md) width contracts from $k^{-1}$ to $(\gamma k)^{-1}$, while its [topological charge](../../../../topological-charge.md) remains unchanged.
