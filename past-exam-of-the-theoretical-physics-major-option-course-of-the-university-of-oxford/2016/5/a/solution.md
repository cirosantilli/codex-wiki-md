<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Varying the [real scalar field](../../../../real-scalar-field.md) gives the [Euler-Lagrange field equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) $\partial_t^2\phi-\partial_x^2\phi+V'(\phi)=0$. Since $V'(\phi)=a\sin(b\phi)$, the answer is

$$
\boxed{\partial_t^2\phi-\partial_x^2\phi+a\sin(b\phi)=0.}
$$

For $b\ne0$, introducing $u=b\phi$ gives $u_{tt}-u_{xx}+ab\sin u=0$, the [Sine-Gordon equation](../../../../sine-gordon-equation.md). The proposed real localized [kink](../../../../scalar-field-kink.md) requires $ab>0$; arbitrary real $a,b$ do not guarantee that condition. In what follows put $k=\sqrt{ab}>0$. The displayed potential itself requires $b\ne0$, unless a limiting prescription is specified.
