<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $V(\phi)=m^2\phi^2/2+\sum_{n>2}\lambda_n\phi^n/n!$. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) for the [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md) is $\Box\phi+V'(\phi)=0$, or explicitly

$$
\boxed{(\Box+m^2)\phi+\sum_{n>2}\frac{\lambda_n}{(n-1)!}\phi^{n-1}=0.}
$$

The derivative of $\phi^n/n!$ is $\phi^{n-1}/(n-1)!$; all interaction terms therefore enter with the displayed plus sign for the chosen [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md).
