<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In the classically scale-invariant case from [solution](../d/solution.md), use $\partial\mathcal L/\partial(\partial_\mu\phi)=\partial^\mu\phi$, $\bar\delta\phi=\alpha(\phi+x^\nu\partial_\nu\phi)$ and $\delta x^\mu=-\alpha x^\mu$. Dividing the supplied [Noether current](../../../../noether-current.md) expression by $\alpha$ gives

$$
j^\mu=\phi\partial^\mu\phi+\partial^\mu\phi\,x^\nu\partial_\nu\phi-x^\mu\mathcal L.
$$

Since $\theta^{\mu\nu}=\partial^\mu\phi\partial^\nu\phi-\eta^{\mu\nu}\mathcal L$, this is

$$
\boxed{j^\mu=\phi\partial^\mu\phi+\theta^{\mu\nu}x_\nu.}
$$

The first term in this [dilatation current](../../../../dilatation-current.md) comes from the intrinsic weight of the [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md), while the second comes from moving its spacetime argument. Using the canonical [energy-momentum tensor](../../../../stress-energy-tensor-split.md) alone without the first term would not give the stated conserved current.
