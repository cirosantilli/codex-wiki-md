<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The real [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md) action has spacetime translations and [Lorentz invariance](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md), together giving [Poincaré symmetry](../../../../poincare-symmetry.md). With a real scalar transformation law it also has parity and [time reversal](../../../../t-symmetry.md). A generic polynomial potential has no continuous internal [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md). The additional field reversal $\phi\mapsto-\phi$ is a [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) precisely when every nonzero odd-power coupling vanishes. The free massless special case also admits a constant shift of $\phi$; generic interactions remove it.

In mass units, $[\partial_\mu]=1$ and the dimensionless [action](../../../../action.md) requires $[\mathcal L]=D$. Normalizing the kinetic term fixes the classical [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) of the [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md):

$$
2+2[\phi]=D,\qquad[\phi]=\frac{D-2}{2},\qquad[m]=1.
$$

For each interaction, $[\lambda_n]+n[\phi]=D$, so

$$
\boxed{[\lambda_n]=D-\frac{n(D-2)}2.}
$$

These are classical or engineering [scaling dimensions](../../../../scaling-dimension.md); quantum corrections can change the scaling of operators and couplings. The scale transformation prescribed later has field weight one, which equals this canonical weight only at $D=4$.
