<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Introduce the electromagnetic [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) $F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu$ and the charged vector field strength $\mathcal G_{\mu\nu}=D_\mu B_\nu-D_\nu B_\mu$, with $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+ieA_\mu$. [Minimal coupling](../../../../minimal-coupling.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_{\rm tot}=-\frac14F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}-\frac12\mathcal G_{\mu\nu}^*\mathcal G^{\mu\nu}+M^2B_\mu^*B^\mu.}
$$

Under $B_\mu'=e^{i\alpha(x)}B_\mu$ and $A_\mu'=A_\mu-\partial_\mu\Lambda$,

$$
D_\mu'B_\nu'=e^{i\alpha}\left[D_\mu B_\nu+i(\partial_\mu\alpha-e\partial_\mu\Lambda)B_\nu\right].
$$

Consequently the required relation is

$$
\boxed{\partial_\mu\alpha=e\partial_\mu\Lambda,\qquad\alpha=e\Lambda+\text{constant}.}
$$

Then $\mathcal G_{\mu\nu}'=e^{i\alpha}\mathcal G_{\mu\nu}$, its complex conjugate transforms oppositely, the mass bilinear is invariant, and $F_{\mu\nu}'=F_{\mu\nu}$. Each term in the total [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) is therefore invariant under the electromagnetic [U(1) gauge symmetry](../../../../u-1-gauge-symmetry.md). This phase [gauge symmetry](../../../../gauge-invariance.md) does not reinstate the broken gradient-shift [gauge symmetry](../../../../gauge-invariance.md) of the massive field itself.
