<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $\delta x^0=\epsilon^0$ the [Noether charge](../../../../noether-charge.md) is $Q=-\epsilon^0H$, with the [Hamiltonian density](../../../../hamiltonian-density.md) $\mathcal H=\pi_a v_a-\mathcal L(\phi,v,\nabla\phi)$ and $v_a=\dot\phi_a$. Invertibility of the momentum-velocity relation allows $v_a=v_a(\phi,\pi,\nabla\phi)$. When taking a [functional derivative](../../../../functional-derivative.md) at fixed $\phi$, the implicit dependence of $v$ cancels in the [Legendre transform](../../../../convex-conjugate.md):

$$
\frac{\delta H}{\delta\pi_a}=v_a+\left(\pi_b-\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial v_b}\right)\frac{\partial v_b}{\partial\pi_a}=v_a.
$$

It follows that

$$
\boxed{\{\phi_a,Q\}_{\rm PB}=\frac{\delta Q}{\delta\pi_a}=-\epsilon^0\dot\phi_a=\bar\delta\phi_a.}
$$

This proves the same generator relation for time translations. In a constrained theory the assumed inversion fails, so the appropriate reduced variables or [Dirac brackets](../../../../dirac-bracket.md) are needed instead. That is precisely the issue encountered for the [Proca field](../../../../proca-field.md) in Question 4.
