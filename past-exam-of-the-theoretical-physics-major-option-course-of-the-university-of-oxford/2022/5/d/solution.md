<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a first-derivative [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) with no explicit spacetime dependence, introduce the canonical [energy-momentum tensor](../../../../stress-energy-tensor-split.md)

$$
T^\rho{}_{\mu}=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\rho\phi_a)}\partial_\mu\phi_a-\delta^\rho{}_{\mu}\mathcal L.
$$

Direct differentiation gives

$$
\partial_\rho T^\rho{}_{\mu}=\left[\partial_\rho\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\rho\phi_a)}-\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\phi_a}\right]\partial_\mu\phi_a=0
$$

on solutions of the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md). The four [Noether currents](../../../../noether-current.md) are the four columns of this tensor, whereas their spatial integrals are the conserved [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) components:

$$
\boxed{P_\mu=\int d^3x\,T^0{}_{\mu}=\int d^3x\,[\pi_a\partial_\mu\phi_a-\eta_{0\mu}\mathcal L].}
$$

For $\mu=0$ this is the [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md); for a spatial lower index it is $P_k=\int\pi_a\partial_k\phi_a$. Under the paper's passive-translation convention, the corresponding generator is $Q=-\epsilon^\mu P_\mu$. Conservation of the integrated quantities also uses the stated vanishing boundary flux.
