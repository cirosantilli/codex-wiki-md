<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The continuous internal [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) is a common [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) around $z$:

$$
S_j^\pm\longmapsto e^{\pm i\theta}S_j^\pm,\qquad S_j^z\longmapsto S_j^z.
$$

Both the transverse exchange and the [Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interaction](../../../../antisymmetric-exchange.md) contain one raising and one lowering [spin operator](../../../../spin-operator.md), so the phases cancel. Equivalently, $[H,\sum_jS_j^z]=0$ and the continuous group is [U(1)](../../../../circle-group.md).

The discrete internal [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) is [time reversal](../../../../t-symmetry.md), $\mathcal T\mathbf S_j\mathcal T^{-1}=-\mathbf S_j$, together with $\mathcal T i\mathcal T^{-1}=-i$. Each term in $H$ contains two [spin operators](../../../../spin-operator.md), so it is invariant. The operation is [antiunitary](../../../../antiunitary-operator.md); treating the reversal of all three [spin](../../../../spin.md) components as a unitary [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) would violate the [spin commutation relations](../../../../spin-commutation-relations.md). It reverses the [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) and interchanges the two easy-axis ordered orientations. A convenient equivalent representative, composed with a $z$ [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), is $R_z(\pi)\mathcal T$, which reverses $S^z$ while preserving $S^x,S^y$ as operators.

The statement about one continuous and one discrete internal [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) describes generic parameters. At $D=0$, additional unitary $\pi$ [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) about transverse axes are allowed; at $D=u=0$, the continuous internal [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) enlarges to full [spin](../../../../spin.md) [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) symmetry. Spatial translations of the uniform chain are separate [symmetries](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of the sites.
