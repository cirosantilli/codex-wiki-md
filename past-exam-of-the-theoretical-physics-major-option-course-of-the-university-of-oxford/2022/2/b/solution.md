<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Holstein–Primakoff transformation](../../../../holstein-primakoff-transformation.md) maps the finite [spin](../../../../spin.md) basis $|S,m\rangle$ to bosonic [Fock states](../../../../fock-state.md) $|n\rangle$ with $n=S-m$. Thus $n=0$ is the fully polarized reference state, and creating a [boson](../../../../boson.md) lowers $S^z$ by one. The physical subspace has the [Holstein–Primakoff occupation constraint](../../../../holstein-primakoff-occupation-constraint.md) $0\le n\le2S$, not the whole infinite oscillator [Hilbert space](../../../../hilbert-space-split.md).

The specified ordering of the square root gives

$$
S^+|n\rangle=\sqrt{n(2S-n+1)}\,|n-1\rangle,\qquad S^-|n\rangle=\sqrt{(n+1)(2S-n)}\,|n+1\rangle.
$$

These are the exact [spin ladder operator](../../../../spin-ladder-operator.md) matrix elements; in particular, the lowering amplitude vanishes at $n=2S$. The underlying oscillator obeys its usual [canonical commutation relation](../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) on the enlarged space, while the [spin operators](../../../../spin-operator.md) preserve the constrained physical subspace. Projecting the oscillator operators themselves to a finite space would change their commutator at its upper endpoint.

Expanding $\sqrt{2S-n}=\sqrt{2S}(1-n/(4S)+\cdots)$ converts an ordered [spin](../../../../spin.md) problem to approximately free [magnons](../../../../magnon.md), with interactions in higher orders of $1/S$. Two complications are the nonpolynomial, order-sensitive square roots, which generate interacting [bosons](../../../../boson.md), and loss of the occupation bound when a truncated expansion uses the full bosonic [Fock space](../../../../fock-space.md). Large depletion, high [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), or soft modes near a [phase transition](../../../../phase-transition.md) can invalidate the assumption $\langle n\rangle\ll S$ even when $S$ is large.
