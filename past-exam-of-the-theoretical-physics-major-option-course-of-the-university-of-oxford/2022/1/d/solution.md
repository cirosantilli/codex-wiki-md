<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For one triangle, equal base [spins](../../../../spin.md) $s=s'=\sigma$ give [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $J+2\bar J\sigma\bar s$. Their minimum occurs at $\bar s=-\sigma$ and is $J-2\bar J$. Opposite base [spins](../../../../spin.md) give [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $-J$, independently of the apex [spin](../../../../spin.md). The all-equal configurations have [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $J+2\bar J$.

When $\bar J=J$, the six configurations with precisely one [spin](../../../../spin.md) different from the other two all have minimum [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $-J$. For a fixed first base [spin](../../../../spin.md), there is one minimum choice if the next base [spin](../../../../spin.md) is the same, and two choices of apex if it is opposite. The ground-configuration counting [transfer matrix](../../../../transfer-matrix.md) is therefore

$$
C=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \Omega_L=\operatorname{Tr}C^L=3^L+(-1)^L.
$$

This [ground-state counting of the frustrated sawtooth Ising chain](../../../../ground-state-counting-of-the-frustrated-sawtooth-ising-chain.md) proves the extensive [geometric frustration](../../../../geometrical-frustration.md) and the [residual entropy](../../../../residual-entropy.md) $k_B\lim_{L\to\infty}\log\Omega_L/(2L)=(k_B/2)\log3$.

When $\bar J>J$, $J-2\bar J<-J$. Every triangle must have equal base [spins](../../../../spin.md) and the opposite apex [spin](../../../../spin.md). Shared base [spins](../../../../spin.md) propagate that choice around the chain, leaving just two global [ground states](../../../../ground-state.md), of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $L(J-2\bar J)$. Their [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) per [spin](../../../../spin.md) is $k_B\log2/(2L)\to0$.

For couplings favoring [ferromagnetism](../../../../ferromagnetism.md), write $J=-K$, $\bar J=-\bar K$ with $K,\bar K>0$. Both orientations of an all-equal triangle minimize every bond simultaneously. The common base [spins](../../../../spin.md) then make the whole chain uniform. There are two global [ground states](../../../../ground-state.md) for either $K=\bar K$ or $K\ne\bar K$, so no corresponding distinction in [residual entropy](../../../../residual-entropy.md) occurs. This statement assumes all bonds remain nonzero.
