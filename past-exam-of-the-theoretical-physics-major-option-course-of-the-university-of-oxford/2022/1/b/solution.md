<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the $s=+1,-1$ basis the [transfer matrix](../../../../transfer-matrix.md) and its [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&a\end{pmatrix},\qquad a=2e^{-\beta J}\cosh(2\beta\bar J),\quad b=2e^{\beta J},\qquad \lambda_\pm=a\pm b.
$$

For finite positive [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), $a,b>0$ and $\lambda_+>|\lambda_-|$. Consequently $Z=\lambda_+^L+\lambda_-^L$ and $L^{-1}\log Z\to\log\lambda_+$ in the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md). There are $2L$ [spins](../../../../spin.md), so the [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) per [spin](../../../../spin.md) is

$$
\boxed{f(\beta,J,\bar J)=-\frac1{2\beta}\log\left[2e^{-\beta J}\cosh(2\beta\bar J)+2e^{\beta J}\right].}
$$

The factor $1/2$ is essential: one [transfer matrix](../../../../transfer-matrix.md) step counts two [spins](../../../../spin.md). For completeness, if the undefined $r$ is retained, set $a_r=A^{(r)}_{-1,-1}$, $d_r=A^{(r)}_{r+1,r+1}$ and $b_r=A^{(r)}_{-1,r+1}$. The same [thermodynamic limit](../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md) gives $f_r=-(2\beta)^{-1}\log[(a_r+d_r+\sqrt{(a_r-d_r)^2+4b_r^2})/2]$ whenever these are two distinct [spin](../../../../spin.md) values.
