<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The continuous internal [global symmetry in field theory](../../../../global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md) is

$$
\boxed{\varphi(x)\longmapsto e^{i\alpha}\varphi(x),\qquad\alpha\in\mathbb R\text{ constant},\qquad G=U(1).}
$$

Both $|\varphi|^2$ and $\partial_\mu\varphi^*\partial^\mu\varphi$ are unchanged. The group is the [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md), acting by phase [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md). The ordinary derivatives do not make it a local [gauge symmetry](../../../../gauge-invariance.md): spacetime-dependent $\alpha$ would add derivative terms. There is also the discrete [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of [complex conjugation](../../../../complex-conjugation.md); in the two-real-field description the full internal invariance is $O(2)$, whose connected component is this $U(1)$.
