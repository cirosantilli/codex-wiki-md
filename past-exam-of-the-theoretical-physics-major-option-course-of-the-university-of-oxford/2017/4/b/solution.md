<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md) is $V=-m^2|\varphi|^2+\lambda|\varphi|^4/4$. Completing the square gives

$$
V=\frac\lambda4\left(|\varphi|^2-\frac{2m^2}{\lambda}\right)^2-\frac{m^4}{\lambda}.
$$

The [Hamiltonian density](../../../../hamiltonian-density.md) also contains the nonnegative terms $|\dot\varphi|^2+|\nabla\varphi|^2$. Its lowest-energy configurations therefore have no time or spatial variation and minimize the displayed square. Thus

$$
\boxed{|\varphi|^2=v^2=\frac{2m^2}{\lambda},\qquad v=\frac{\sqrt2\,m}{\sqrt\lambda}>0.}
$$

The minima form a circle of phases. Selecting the real positive representative $\varphi=v$ chooses one [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md) and spontaneously breaks the continuous [global symmetry in field theory](../../../../global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md). The stationarity at $\varphi=0$ is a local maximum of the radial potential, not the [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md).
