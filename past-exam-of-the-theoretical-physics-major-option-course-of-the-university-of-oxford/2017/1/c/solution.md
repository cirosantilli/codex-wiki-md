<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Insert $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ at the first undecorated site. In the [eigenbasis](../../../../eigenbasis.md) of the [transfer matrix](../../../../transfer-matrix.md), $D$ exchanges $v_+$ and $v_-$, and both diagonal [matrix elements](../../../../matrix-element.md) vanish. Explicitly,

$$
\boxed{\langle\sigma_1\rangle_L=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(DV^L)}{\operatorname{tr}V^L}
=\frac{\lambda_+^L v_+^{\mathsf T}Dv_++\lambda_-^L v_-^{\mathsf T}Dv_-}{\lambda_+^L+\lambda_-^L}=0.}
$$

Thus its large-$L$ limit is zero at every fixed $T>0$. The result is exact even before taking that limit. Equivalently, simultaneous reversal of all [spins](../../../../spin.md) pairs configurations of equal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and opposite $\sigma_1$. Positivity of the [transfer matrix](../../../../transfer-matrix.md) at fixed [positive temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) prevents a degenerate dominant [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) and [spontaneous magnetization](../../../../spontaneous-magnetization.md) in this one-dimensional zero-field chain. Selecting one aligned state at strictly [zero temperature](../../../../absolute-zero.md) is a different order of limits.
