<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Interpret the two displayed exchange components as belonging to the common nearest-neighbour sum with coefficient $-J$. Write $H_f=|h|$ and, for $h\ne0$, $s=\operatorname{sgn}h$. The source explicitly fixes $J>0$ but does not separately constrain $h$ in this question; keeping $H_f$ covers either [field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) direction.

For [classical vectors](../../../../vector.md) of length $S$, define $\mathbf q_j=(S_j^x,S_j^y)$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\mathbf q_j\cdot\mathbf q_{j+1}\le|\mathbf q_j||\mathbf q_{j+1}|\le S^2$, and $hS_j^x\le H_fS$. Each term is therefore bounded below, so

$$
E_{\mathrm{cl}}\ge-LJS^2-LH_fS^2.
$$

Uniform alignment along the [field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) saturates every bound:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf S_j=(sS,0,0),\qquad E_{\mathrm{cl,min}}=-L(J+H_f)S^2\quad(h\ne0).}
$$

Equality requires all planar [vectors](../../../../vector.md) to be parallel with length $S$, and the nonzero [field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) then fixes their direction. At $h=0$, every uniformly oriented [vector](../../../../vector.md) in the $xy$ plane is a [classical ground state](../../../../ground-state.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf S_j=S(\cos\phi,\sin\phi,0),\qquad E_{\mathrm{cl,min}}=-LJS^2.}
$$

A nonuniform configuration cannot improve these simultaneously saturated bond bounds, including on the periodic ring.
