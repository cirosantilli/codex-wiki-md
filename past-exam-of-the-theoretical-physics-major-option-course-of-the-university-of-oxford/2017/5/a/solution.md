<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Proca action](../../../../proca-action.md) is invariant under spacetime translations and proper continuous [Lorentz transformations](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md), together forming the connected [Poincaré group](../../../../poincare-group.md). The fixed nonzero [mass](../../../../mass.md) excludes scale invariance. In four dimensions, a dimensionless [action](../../../../action.md) and the canonical [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) imply

$$
\boxed{[A^\mu]=1,\qquad[F^{\mu\nu}]=2,\qquad[m_A]=1.}
$$

These are [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md). The field-strength term is invariant under $A_\mu\to A_\mu+\partial_\mu\chi$, but the [mass](../../../../mass.md) term is not: its change contains $m_A^2 A^\mu\partial_\mu\chi+\tfrac12m_A^2(\partial\chi)^2$. Thus the displayed massive-vector theory has no Maxwell-type local gauge invariance. The massless limit has a different constraint structure.
