<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use one common [ultraviolet cutoff](../../../../ultraviolet-cutoff.md) for all physical degrees of freedom, with positive sign for each [bosonic](../../../../boson.md) degree of freedom and negative sign for each [fermionic](../../../../fermion.md) degree of freedom. For the [mass-moment cancellation of vacuum energy divergences](../../../../mass-moment-cancellation-of-vacuum-energy-divergences.md), define the weighted [supertrace](../../../../supertrace.md) $\operatorname{Str}f(m)=\sum_Bf(m_B)-\sum_Ff(m_F)$. The large-[momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) expansion is

$$
\sum_B\sqrt{p^2+m_B^2}-\sum_F\sqrt{p^2+m_F^2}
=p\,\operatorname{Str}1+\frac{\operatorname{Str}m^2}{2p}-\frac{\operatorname{Str}m^4}{8p^3}+O(p^{-5}).
$$

After multiplication by the radial measure $p^2dp$, these three terms produce quartic, quadratic and logarithmic [ultraviolet divergences](../../../../ultraviolet-divergence.md). Therefore the necessary and sufficient cancellations for the displayed _unsubtracted_ finite-species [vacuum energy](../../../../vacuum-energy.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Str}1=0,\qquad\operatorname{Str}m^2=0,\qquad\operatorname{Str}m^4=0.}
$$

They respectively mean equal numbers of [bosonic](../../../../boson.md) and [fermionic](../../../../fermion.md) physical degrees of freedom, equal sums of squared [masses](../../../../mass.md), and equal sums of fourth powers of [masses](../../../../mass.md). Once they hold, the remaining radial integrand falls as $p^{-3}$ and is integrable at infinity. Nonnegative [masses](../../../../mass.md) introduce no lower-end divergence.

Pairing every [bosonic](../../../../boson.md) degree of freedom with a [fermionic](../../../../fermion.md) one of the same [mass](../../../../mass.md), as in an unbroken [supersymmetric](../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) spectrum, is sufficient and cancels the entire expression. It is stronger than these three moment conditions. For a concrete non-paired example, take bosonic squared [masses](../../../../mass.md) $\{0,3,3\}m_*^2$ and fermionic squared [masses](../../../../mass.md) $\{1,1,4\}m_*^2$. Both have three degrees of freedom, squared-[mass](../../../../mass.md) sum $6m_*^2$ and fourth-power-[mass](../../../../mass.md) sum $18m_*^4$, but their spectra differ. If a [vacuum energy](../../../../vacuum-energy.md) [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) is instead permitted, a renormalized finite answer need not satisfy them; that is a different prescription from finiteness of the source's bare sum.
