<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The breaking of the internal [global symmetry in field theory](../../../../global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md) $SU(n)\times SU(n)$ to $SU(n)$ removes $2(n^2-1)-(n^2-1)$ generators. The relativistic [Goldstone theorem](../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) therefore requires

$$
\boxed{n^2-1\text{ massless Goldstone bosons}.}
$$

Other states fall into multiplets of the unbroken [special unitary group](../../../../special-unitary-group.md), but their [masses](../../../../mass.md) are not fixed by this counting. For the usual diagonal embedding $SU(n)_V\subset SU(n)_L\times SU(n)_R$, the [Goldstone bosons](../../../../goldstone-boson.md) form its adjoint multiplet.

If small explicit symmetry-breaking terms are added, the broken-generator directions generally acquire small curvature, producing light [pseudo-Goldstone bosons](../../../../pseudo-goldstone-boson.md) rather than exactly massless states. Their squared [masses](../../../../mass.md) tend to zero with those breaking parameters. Small breaking can also split multiplet degeneracies. These claims concern a small perturbation of the broken phase; approximate [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) alone does not establish which [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md) is selected.
