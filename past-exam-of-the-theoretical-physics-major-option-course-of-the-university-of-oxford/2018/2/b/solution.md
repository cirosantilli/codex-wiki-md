<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Both the canonical [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) and $V(\phi^{\mathsf T}\phi)$ are invariant under the [orthogonal group](../../../../orthogonal-group.md) $O(n)$. The stated nonzero [vacuum expectation value](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) has length $|v|$. Its stabilizer is the [orthogonal group](../../../../orthogonal-group.md) $O(n-1)$ acting on the perpendicular subspace. Hence

$$
\boxed{N_{\mathrm{GB}}=\frac{n(n-1)}2-\frac{(n-1)(n-2)}2=n-1.}
$$

The two nonzero coordinates of this particular [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md) do not break more generators than an axis-aligned [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md): the two choices are related by a common [orthogonal transformation](../../../../orthogonal-transformation.md).

Explicitly, with $s=\phi^{\mathsf T}\phi$,

$$
\mathsf M^2=2V'(v^2)\mathsf I+4V''(v^2)vv^{\mathsf T}.
$$

[stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md) at nonzero $v$ requires $V'(v^2)=0$. The $n-1$ transverse [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) vanish, while the radial squared [mass](../../../../mass.md) is $4v^2V''(v^2)$. If this curvature also vanishes, there is an additional accidental massless radial mode; the number of symmetry-protected [Goldstone bosons](../../../../goldstone-boson.md) remains $n-1$. If $v=0$, the [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) is unbroken and none is required.
