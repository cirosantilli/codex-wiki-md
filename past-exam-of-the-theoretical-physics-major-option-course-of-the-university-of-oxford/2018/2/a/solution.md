<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Goldstone's theorem](../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) states that a spontaneously broken continuous internal [global symmetry in field theory](../../../../global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md) of a local relativistic [quantum field theory](../../../../quantum-field-theory-split.md) produces massless [Goldstone bosons](../../../../goldstone-boson.md). For a [Lorentz invariance](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md) breaking $G$ to its stabilizer $H$, there are $\dim G-\dim H$ independent symmetry-required massless directions. Additional accidentally massless modes are possible. A gauge redundancy does not obey this physical-state counting, and nonrelativistic systems need not have one independent mode per broken generator.

For the classical proof, consider real [scalar fields](../../../../scalar-field.md) $\phi_i$ with a positive canonical [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) and an invariant smooth [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md) $V$. An infinitesimal generator $T^a$ satisfies

$$
\frac{\partial V}{\partial\phi_i}(T^a\phi)_i=0.
$$

Let $v$ be a stationary [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md) and differentiate this identity with respect to $\phi_j$. At $v$, the term proportional to $\partial_iV$ vanishes, leaving

$$
\boxed{\mathsf M^2_{ji}(T^av)_i=0,\qquad \mathsf M^2_{ji}=\left.\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial\phi_j\partial\phi_i}\right|_v.}
$$

The [Hessian matrix](../../../../hessian-matrix.md) $\mathsf M^2$ is the [scalar mass matrix](../../../../scalar-mass-matrix.md). An unbroken generator has $T^av=0$; broken generators span the tangent space of the [vacuum manifold](../../../../vacuum-manifold.md) $G/H$. Each independent nonzero tangent vector is a zero [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) of $\mathsf M^2$. The quadratic [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) in these directions are $\Box\pi^a=0$, giving the massless [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega^2=\mathbf p^2$. This proves the classical result and explains why the [Goldstone bosons](../../../../goldstone-boson.md) describe motion along the degenerate minima rather than against a restoring curvature.
