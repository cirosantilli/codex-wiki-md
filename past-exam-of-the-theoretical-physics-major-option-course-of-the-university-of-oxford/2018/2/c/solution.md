<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Assume $\mu^2,\lambda>0$. At zero source, the [vacuum manifold](../../../../vacuum-manifold.md) is the sphere of radius $v_0=\mu/\sqrt\lambda$. The source tilts the [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md) by $-\epsilon\phi_1$. Its force should be small compared with the radial restoring-force scale $\mu^2v_0$, so a useful quantitative condition is

$$
\boxed{|\epsilon|\ll\mu^2v_0=\frac{\mu^3}{\sqrt\lambda}.}
$$

For nonzero $\epsilon$, the exact [global symmetry in field theory](../../../../global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md) of the [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) is $O(n-1)$, rotating $\phi_2,\ldots,\phi_n$. The global [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md) aligns with the source:

$$
\langle\phi\rangle=(\operatorname{sgn}(\epsilon)w,0,\ldots,0),\qquad
\lambda w^3-\mu^2w=|\epsilon|,\qquad
w=v_0+\frac{|\epsilon|}{2\mu^2}+O\!\left(\frac{\epsilon^2}{\mu^4v_0}\right).
$$

This [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md) preserves the entire remaining $O(n-1)$ [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md). There is therefore no exact [Goldstone boson](../../../../goldstone-boson.md) for $\epsilon\ne0$. Expanding the [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md) to quadratic order gives

$$
\boxed{m_{\perp}^2=-\mu^2+\lambda w^2=\frac{|\epsilon|}{w},\qquad
m_{\parallel}^2=-\mu^2+3\lambda w^2=2\mu^2+\frac{3|\epsilon|}{w}.}
$$

Thus there are $n-1$ degenerate light [pseudo-Goldstone bosons](../../../../pseudo-goldstone-boson.md) and one heavy radial [scalar](../../../../scalar.md). The small-source condition makes their squared [masses](../../../../mass.md) parametrically smaller than the radial squared [mass](../../../../mass.md). Sending $\epsilon$ to zero restores the original massless modes.
