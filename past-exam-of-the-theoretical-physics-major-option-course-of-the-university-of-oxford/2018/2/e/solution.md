<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The light states have the counting $3+4+1=8$. This suggests approximate [chiral symmetry breaking](../../../../chiral-symmetry-breaking.md)

$$
\boxed{SU(3)_L\times SU(3)_R\longrightarrow SU(3)_V,\qquad 2(3^2-1)-(3^2-1)=8,}
$$

with a nonzero [quark chiral condensate](../../../../quark-chiral-condensate.md) $\langle\bar q_R^{\,j}q_L^{\,i}\rangle\propto\delta^{ij}$ for the three light flavours $u,d,s$. The three [pions](../../../../pion.md), four [kaons](../../../../kaon.md) and predominantly octet [eta meson](../../../../eta-meson.md) are the resulting [pseudo-Goldstone bosons](../../../../pseudo-goldstone-boson.md). Their small nonzero [masses](../../../../mass.md) reflect explicit [chiral symmetry](../../../../chiral-symmetry.md) breaking by the light-quark [masses](../../../../mass.md); for example, $m_\pi^2$ is proportional to $m_u+m_d$ at leading order. The larger strange-quark [mass](../../../../mass.md) makes the [kaons](../../../../kaon.md) and octet [eta meson](../../../../eta-meson.md) heavier than the [pions](../../../../pion.md), while [electromagnetic interactions](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) help split charged and neutral partners.[Chapter 5, chiral symmetry breaking and section 5.3 on adding quark masses](../../../../gauge-theory-by-david-tong.md)

Although the massless strong-interaction terms admit independent rotations of all six flavours, the charm, bottom and top [masses](../../../../mass.md) are too large to treat six-flavour [chiral symmetry](../../../../chiral-symmetry.md) as a small explicit breaking at the light-hadron scale. Full approximate $SU(6)_L\times SU(6)_R$ breaking would suggest 35 light modes, rather than this octet. The classical axial $U(1)$ is also broken by the [chiral anomaly](../../../../chiral-anomaly.md), so it does not supply a ninth equally light [Goldstone boson](../../../../goldstone-boson.md).

**Terminology qualification:** these mesons have [spin](../../../../spin.md) zero but negative [parity](../../../../parity.md): they are [pseudoscalar mesons](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md), with $J^P=0^-$, rather than positive-parity [scalar](../../../../scalar.md) [particles](../../../../particle-physics.md) with $J^P=0^+$. Calling the listed states “[scalar](../../../../scalar.md)” is acceptable only if it is being used loosely to mean [spin](../../../../spin.md) zero.
