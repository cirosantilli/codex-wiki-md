<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a coarse-grained [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) $m(\mathbf x)$ as the [order parameter](../../../../order-parameter.md): it vanishes in the [paramagnetism](../../../../paramagnetism.md) phase and develops a spontaneous nonzero value in the [ferromagnetism](../../../../ferromagnetism.md) phase. In zero applied [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), [spin](../../../../spin.md) reversal sends $m\mapsto-m$, so the local [Landau free energy](../../../../landau-free-energy.md) contains only even powers. A minimal spatially inhomogeneous [free-energy functional](../../../../free-energy-functional.md) is

$$
\mathcal F[m]=F_{\mathrm{reg}}(T)+\int d^dx\left[\frac{a(T)}2m^2+\frac b4m^4+\frac c6m^6
+\frac\kappa2|\nabla m|^2-hm+\cdots\right],\qquad\kappa>0.
$$

The positive [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) coefficient penalizes rapid spatial variations. Near an ordinary transition take $a(T)=a_0(T-T_0)$, $a_0>0$, and positive $b$; retain the sextic term when $b$ can approach or cross zero. The source $h$ is conjugate to the [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) and explicitly breaks [spin](../../../../spin.md) reversal.

Determine equilibrium by minimizing this [free-energy functional](../../../../free-energy-functional.md), imposing the appropriate [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md). Its [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{-\kappa\nabla^2m+a(T)m+bm^3+cm^5-h=0.}
$$

For a uniform system at $h=0$, compare the values of all stable stationary solutions, rather than accepting every solution of this equation. The selected [global minimum](../../../../global-minimum.md) gives the equilibrium [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md); derivatives of its [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) give [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md), [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) and [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md). At an ordinary transition the two ordered minima emerge continuously from zero. Spatial dependence also permits [domain walls](../../../../domain-wall.md) and an estimate of the [correlation length](../../../../correlation-length.md).

This is a [mean-field approximation](../../../../mean-field-approximation.md): it selects a minimizing profile and neglects statistical fluctuations around it. The [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) constrains the expansion, but its numerical coefficients are phenomenological. Close enough to a transition, [thermal fluctuations](../../../../thermal-fluctuation.md) near a [critical point](../../../../critical-point.md) can invalidate the resulting [mean-field critical exponents](../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md).[Chapter 1, the Landau-Ginzburg free-energy functional, spatial gradients and mean-field phase transitions](../../../../statistical-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
