<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the sextic polynomial to be bounded below at large $|m|$ while allowing either sign of $b$, its leading coefficient must obey

$$
\boxed{c>0.}
$$

If $c<0$, the [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) tends to $-\infty$ as $|m|\to\infty$, so it cannot define an equilibrium within this truncation. The exceptional case $c=0$ is stable for $b>0$ and gives the ordinary quartic [Landau theory](../../../../landau-theory.md); it cannot stabilize the negative-$b$ region required for the full sextic [phase diagram](../../../../phase-diagram.md). Higher positive powers could stabilize a negative $c$, but they are absent from this model.
