<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write $a=a_0(T-T_c)$, $a_0>0$, at fixed $b\geq0$, and define the [critical exponent](../../../../critical-exponent.md) by $|m|\sim(T_c-T)^\beta$ on the ordered side. For fixed $b>0$ and small negative $a$, the stationary equation gives $m^2=-a/b+O(a^2)$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\beta=\frac12\quad\text{on the ordinary line }a=0,\ b>0.}
$$

At the [tricritical point](../../../../tricritical-point.md) approached along the tuned path $b=0$, the stationary equation instead gives $m^4=-a/c$, so

$$
\boxed{\beta_{\mathrm{tri}}=\frac14\quad\text{at }a=b=0\text{ along }b=0.}
$$

For fixed $b<0$, the transition occurs before $a$ reaches zero and the [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) jumps; it is not a second-order transition and has no vanishing-order-parameter exponent of this form. A path that does not keep the quartic coefficient tuned to zero generally crosses over to the ordinary result.
