<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Work in the parent [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md), $p=(M,\mathbf0)$. For massless daughters, the spatial [Dirac delta](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) fixes $\mathbf q_2=-\mathbf q_1$ and $E_1=E_2=q$. The [Lorentz invariance](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) two-body [Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space-measure.md) becomes

$$
\begin{aligned}
d\Pi_2&=\frac{(2\pi)^4}{(2\pi)^6}\frac{q^2dq\,d\Omega}{4q^2}\,\delta(M-2q)\\
&=\frac{dq\,d\Omega}{16\pi^2}\,\delta(M-2q)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{d\Pi_2=\frac{d\Omega}{32\pi^2}.}
\end{aligned}
$$

The factor $1/2$ in the last step is the Jacobian of the energy-conservation [Dirac delta](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md). Consequently the labelled two-body [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{\mathrm{labelled}}=\frac1{64\pi^2M}\int d\Omega\,|\mathcal A|^2.}
$$

For two identical final [particles](../../../../particle-physics.md), the full angular integral counts each unordered final state twice, so the physical [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) includes an additional factor $1/2!$. This factor is needed for both channels in part e; it is separate from any vertex combinatorics already included in the [Feynman rule](../../../../feynman-rule.md).
