<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The massless two-body [kinematics](../../../../kinematics.md) gives $q_1\cdot q_2=M^2/2$. The [scalar](../../../../scalar.md) [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) therefore has constant magnitude $|\mathcal A_{aa}|=|\lambda_a|M^2/2$. Use $\int d\Omega=4\pi$ and the identical-particle factor from part d:

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{aa}=\frac1{2!}\frac{4\pi}{64\pi^2M}\frac{|\lambda_a|^2M^4}{4}
=\frac{|\lambda_a|^2M^3}{128\pi}.}
$$

For the [photons](../../../../photon.md), the [two-photon vertex of a scalar field-strength interaction](../../../../two-photon-vertex-of-a-scalar-field-strength-interaction.md) following from $\Phi F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}$ is, up to an irrelevant overall phase,

$$
\mathcal A_{\gamma\gamma}=\lambda_\gamma\left[(q_1\cdot q_2)(\epsilon_1\cdot\epsilon_2)
-(q_1\cdot\epsilon_2)(q_2\cdot\epsilon_1)\right].
$$

**Vertex qualification:** the source prints same-index [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) contractions in the second product, whereas the contractions must be crossed as above. The printed expression fails the [Ward identity](../../../../ward-identity.md). For a concrete counterexample take $q_1=(E,0,0,E)$, $q_2=(E,0,0,-E)$ and $\epsilon_2=(1,1,0,-1)$. This is a transverse unit spacelike [photon polarization vector](../../../../photon-polarization-vector.md), since $q_2\cdot\epsilon_2=0$ and $\epsilon_2^2=-1$. Replacing $\epsilon_1$ by $q_1$ in the printed expression leaves $4\lambda_\gamma E^3$ instead of zero. The corrected crossed expression vanishes under either such replacement.

Choose physical spatial transverse [photon polarizations](../../../../photon-polarization.md) along $x$ and $y$ in the parent [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md). Then both crossed products vanish; the two equal-polarization combinations have $|\epsilon_1\cdot\epsilon_2|^2=1$ and the two orthogonal combinations give zero. There is no initial-spin average for a spin-zero parent. Hence

$$
\sum_{\mathrm{pol}}|\mathcal A_{\gamma\gamma}|^2=2|\lambda_\gamma|^2\left(\frac{M^2}{2}\right)^2
=\frac{|\lambda_\gamma|^2M^4}{2},
\qquad
\boxed{\Gamma_{\gamma\gamma}=\frac{|\lambda_\gamma|^2M^3}{64\pi}.}
$$

The calculation in this spatial transverse gauge also yields the intended result from the misprinted expression, but only the corrected vertex makes its [gauge invariance](../../../../gauge-invariance.md) explicit. For equal-magnitude [couplings](../../../../coupling.md), the [photon](../../../../photon.md) [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) is twice the two-scalar [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) because of its two nonzero [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) combinations.
