<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

All daughter [particles](../../../../../particle-physics.md) are massless, so the parent [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $M$ supplies the only kinematic scale at [tree level](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md). Each vertex has two derivatives, making its [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) scale as $\lambda_iM^2$. For the four-$b$ vertex, symmetrizing over the derivative-bearing legs yields a sum of pairwise [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) products, which is proportional to $M^2$ by [four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md).

For $n$ massless daughters, the [Lorentz invariance](../../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) [Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure](../../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space-measure.md) scales as $M^{2n-4}$: each $d^3q/(2E)$ contributes two powers and the four-dimensional [Dirac delta](../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) removes four powers. Including the parent factor $1/(2M)$ gives $\Gamma\sim|\lambda_i|^2M^{2n-1}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{aa}\propto|\lambda_a|^2M^3,\qquad
\Gamma_{bbbb}\propto|\lambda_b|^2M^7,\qquad
\Gamma_{\gamma\gamma}\propto|\lambda_\gamma|^2M^3.}
$$

The unspecified factors are dimensionless angular integrals and identical-particle factors. Each result has [mass dimension](../../../../../mass-dimension.md) one, as a [decay width](../../../../../decay-width.md) must in [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md).
