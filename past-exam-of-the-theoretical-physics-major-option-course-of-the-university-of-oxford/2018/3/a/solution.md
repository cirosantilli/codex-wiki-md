<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [action](../../../../action.md) is dimensionless and $[d^4x]=-4$, so $[\mathcal L]=4$. A canonical [scalar](../../../../scalar.md) [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) then requires $2+2[\phi]=4$, and the electromagnetic [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) requires $2[F_{\mu\nu}]=4$. Thus

$$
\boxed{[\Phi]=[a]=[b]=1,\qquad[F_{\mu\nu}]=2.}
$$

The brackets here denote [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md). The [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) has no explicit coordinate dependence, and all indices are contracted as [Lorentz scalars](../../../../lorentz-scalar.md). Its continuous spacetime [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) is the [Poincaré group](../../../../poincare-group.md). [Noether's theorem](../../../../noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) gives conserved [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) from translations and conserved [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md), including the boost generators, from [Lorentz transformations](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md). The nonzero [mass](../../../../mass.md) $M$ and dimensionful [couplings](../../../../coupling.md) prevent a dilation [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md).

**Sign qualification:** the printed electromagnetic [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) has the opposite healthy relative sign to the [scalar](../../../../scalar.md) [kinetic terms](../../../../kinetic-term.md). With the stated mostly-minus [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md), a physical [Maxwell Lagrangian](../../../../maxwell-lagrangian.md) is $-F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}/4$. The printed plus sign instead gives [photon](../../../../photon.md) [Hamiltonian density](../../../../hamiltonian-density.md) $-(\mathbf E^2+\mathbf B^2)/2$; a nonzero purely electric configuration already has negative [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), unbounded below as its amplitude grows. The physical [decay widths](../../../../decay-width.md) below use the healthy Maxwell sign with the given interaction normalization. The dimensional and spacetime-symmetry conclusions themselves do not depend on this sign correction.
