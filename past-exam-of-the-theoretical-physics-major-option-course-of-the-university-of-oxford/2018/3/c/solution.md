<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Every interaction contains an even number of $b$ [fields](../../../../field.md), so the exact discrete [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) $b\mapsto-b$ forbids $\Phi\to bbb$ to every perturbative order. There is no such prohibition for $\Phi\to bb$.

The latter [decay channel](../../../../decay-channel.md) is absent at [tree level](../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) in the displayed interactions, but contracting two $b$ legs at a $\Phi b^2(\partial b)^2$ vertex produces a [tadpole diagram](../../../../tadpole-diagram.md) with two external $b$ legs. For example, contracting the undifferentiated pair leaves the local operator $\Phi(\partial b)^2$ multiplied by an integral proportional to $\int d^4\ell/(\ell^2+i0)$. Contracting the differentiated pair instead gives a local $\Phi b^2$ contribution. Both are allowed [effective field theory](../../../../effective-field-theory.md) operators. With a [ultraviolet cutoff](../../../../ultraviolet-cutoff.md), these local terms generically carry quadratic or quartic cutoff dependence and require matching coefficients or [counterterms](../../../../counterterm.md).

There is a relevant qualification to a claim that the one-loop [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) must be nonzero. Since $b$ is exactly massless here, the one-vertex [tadpole integrals](../../../../tadpole-integral.md) have no external scale and are [scaleless integrals](../../../../scaleless-integral.md). They vanish in [dimensional regularization](../../../../dimensional-regularization.md). Thus the correct conclusion is **allowed, and generically generated by quantum corrections or matching**, not a regulator-independent nonzero one-loop prediction from $\lambda_b$ alone. No exact [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) protects the two-$b$ channel; its numerical rate requires the renormalized coefficients of the allowed operators.
