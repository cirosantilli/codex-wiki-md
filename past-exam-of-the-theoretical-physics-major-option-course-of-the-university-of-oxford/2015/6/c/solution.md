<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At a single interaction insertion, the [Dyson series](../../../../dyson-series.md) supplies $i\mathcal L_{\rm int}=-ig\varphi^\dagger\varphi\phi$. Amputating the three external propagators in [b](../b.md) gives the momentum-space rule

$$
\boxed{\varphi^\dagger\!\text{--}\varphi\!\text{--}\phi\ \text{vertex}:\quad -ig.}
$$

If the momentum-conservation factor is included in the vertex convention, append $(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(p_\phi+p_\varphi+p_{\varphi^\dagger})$, with all momenta incoming. There is one charged line entering and one leaving the vertex, and a neutral real-scalar line. The absence of a factorial in the interaction is appropriate because its three fields are distinct. The amputated decay amplitude has magnitude $|g|$ with the usual convention that a graph computes $i\mathcal A$.
