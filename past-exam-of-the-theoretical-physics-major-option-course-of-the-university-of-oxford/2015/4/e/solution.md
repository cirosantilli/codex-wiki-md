<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

At $B=0$ a global reversal $\sigma_j\mapsto-\sigma_j$ leaves the energy and statistical weight unchanged but changes the sign of the one-spin observable. Pairing reversed configurations gives

$$
\boxed{\langle\sigma_j\rangle_\beta=0.}
$$

Equivalently, $\langle\pm|\sigma^z|\pm\rangle=0$ in the transfer [eigenbasis](../../../../eigenbasis.md), so $\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma^zT^N)=0$. This holds for every finite even ring and all finite temperatures, and for the zero-field Gibbs limit. A symmetry-selected zero-temperature alternating state can have nonzero **staggered** [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md); its uniform [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) is still zero. The classical spin variable in this model has no separate quantum spin-operator dynamics despite the conventional matrix notation $\sigma^z$ used for the insertion.
