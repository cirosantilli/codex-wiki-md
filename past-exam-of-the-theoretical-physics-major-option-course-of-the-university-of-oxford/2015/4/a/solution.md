<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\beta=(k_BT)^{-1}$, and sum over all $2^N$ configurations satisfying the periodic identification. The [canonical partition function](../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) and finite-size [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per site are

$$
\boxed{Z_N=\sum_{\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_N=\pm1}
\exp\!\left[-\beta J\sum_j\sigma_j\sigma_{j+1}+\beta B\sum_j\sigma_j\right],\qquad
f_N=-\frac{1}{\beta N}\log Z_N.}
$$

The plus sign multiplying $J$ in the energy means that $J>0$ favors opposite neighboring spins: this is an [antiferromagnetic Ising model](../../../../antiferromagnetic-ising-model.md). Even $N$ permits an alternating configuration to close without frustration. The finite-size formula does not require taking a [thermodynamic limit](../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md); that limit is taken only after evaluating $Z_N$.
