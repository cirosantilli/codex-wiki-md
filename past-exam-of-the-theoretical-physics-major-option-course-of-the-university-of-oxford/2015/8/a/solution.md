<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The continuous internal symmetry is local $U(1)$ [gauge invariance](../../../../gauge-invariance.md). With the source's [gauge covariant derivative](../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) convention it acts as

$$
\boxed{\varphi\mapsto e^{i\alpha(x)}\varphi,\qquad
A_\mu\mapsto A_\mu+\frac1e\partial_\mu\alpha,}
$$

so $D_\mu\varphi$ transforms like $\varphi$ and $F_{\mu\nu}$ is unchanged. Constant phases are its global subgroup, not an additional independent internal rotation group.

In flat four-dimensional space-time the continuous external symmetry is the [Poincaré group](../../../../poincare-group.md): four translations and six [Lorentz transformations](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md). Noether's theorem associates translations with conserved energy and momentum, spatial rotations with [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md), and boosts with the relativistic center-of-energy/boost charges. In tensor notation the latter two are the six components of the conserved total angular-momentum tensor $M^{\mu\nu}$, including field spin contributions where appropriate. The nonzero dimensionful parameter $\mu^2$ prevents exact scale invariance. Discrete transformations, such as parity, are outside the requested continuous-symmetry list.
