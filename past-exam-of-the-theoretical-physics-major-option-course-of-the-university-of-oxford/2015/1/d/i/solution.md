<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Condition on the molecule being empty. Its instantaneous incoming-charge jump rate is $f_1$, so the unconditional stationary number of arrivals per unit time is

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm in}=f_1p_0^{\rm st}=\frac{f_1f_3f_4}{f_3f_4+f_1f_3+f_1f_2}.}
$$

This [stationary jump traffic at a single contact](../../../../../stationary-jump-traffic-at-a-single-contact.md) is a unidirectional jump count, not a signed net current. Stationarity also gives $R_{\rm out}=f_4p_A^{\rm st}=R_{\rm in}$. Multiplying their difference by the charge gives zero net stationary electrical current through the molecule's single contact, even though individual arrival and departure events continue.
