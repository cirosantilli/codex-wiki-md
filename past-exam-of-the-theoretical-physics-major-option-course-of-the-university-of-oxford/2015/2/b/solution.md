<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The field term explicitly selects the positive $x$ direction. For $B>0$ the uniform minimizing state is $\mathbf S_j=S\hat{\mathbf x}$, with no remaining continuous degeneracy of its orientation. Rotations about the $x$ axis still leave the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) invariant, and the selected state respects that residual symmetry.

Tilting the [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) now costs a field energy. Accordingly the transverse [spin wave](../../../../spin-wave.md) acquires a gap

$$
\boxed{\Delta=BS,}
$$

as confirmed directly in [d](../d.md). The factor $S$ is present because the source couples the field as $-BS\sum_jS_j^x$ rather than $-B\sum_jS_j^x$. Gapless zero-field [Goldstone modes](../../../../goldstone-boson.md) need not survive an explicit symmetry-breaking field.
