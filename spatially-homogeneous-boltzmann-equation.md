# Spatially homogeneous Boltzmann equation

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md)

When the velocity distribution is independent of position and there are no forces, the [free transport equation](free-transport-equation.md) contributes zero and only collisions evolve $f$. For a bounded cutoff kernel, $\|Q(f,g)\|_1\leq C\|f\|_1\|g\|_1$ gives local existence and uniqueness by the [Banach fixed-point theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md). Positivity and mass conservation bound the norm and permit global continuation in this particular model. Stronger kernels require moment or weighted estimates.
