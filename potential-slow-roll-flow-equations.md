# Potential slow-roll flow equations

↑ **Parent:** [Potential slow-roll parameter](potential-slow-roll-parameter.md)

At horizon exit during single-field [slow-roll inflation](slow-roll-approximation.md), $d/d\log k\simeq-M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2(V'/V)d/d\Phi$. Applying this operator to the [potential slow-roll parameter](potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) and the [second potential slow-roll parameter](second-potential-slow-roll-parameter.md) yields the displayed leading-order flow equations. Corrections to the horizon-exit derivative contribute only at higher slow-roll order here.
