# Internal force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

An [internal force](internal-force.md) is a [force](force-split.md) between members inside the chosen system boundary. Changing that boundary can change which [forces](force-split.md) are internal. Pairwise [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics-split.md) interactions cancel through [third-law interaction pairs](third-law-interaction-pair.md) in the system's total [momentum](momentum-split.md) balance.
