# Tautology

↑ **Parent:** [Classical propositional logic](classical-propositional-logic.md)

A [tautology](tautology.md) is a [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) true under every [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) in [classical propositional logic](classical-propositional-logic.md), such as the [law of excluded middle](law-of-excluded-middle.md) $p\lor\neg p$. A primitive [proposition](proposition.md) $p$ is not a [tautology](tautology.md), even if it represents a true statement. See [forall x: Calgary, section 12.1](https://forallx.openlogicproject.org/html/Ch12.html).
