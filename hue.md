# Hue

↑ **Parent:** [Color appearance](color-appearance.md)

The [color](color.md) attribute distinguishing appearances such as red, yellow, green or blue and their combinations. A hue need not identify a single [wavelength](wavelength.md), and cone-class labels do not directly specify it.
