# Quartet of quartets

↑ **Parent:** [NMR multiplet](nmr-multiplet.md)

A first-order [NMR multiplet](nmr-multiplet.md) produced by coupling to two distinct sets of three equivalent spin-one-half nuclei. Sixteen lines are possible; equal couplings can collapse the pattern to a septet.
