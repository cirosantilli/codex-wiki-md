# Tolman electronic parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Phosphine ligand](phosphine-ligand.md)

The [Tolman electronic parameter](tolman-electronic-parameter.md) compares ligand electronic influence using the symmetric CO stretching band of a matched metal-carbonyl probe, classically $\mathrm{Ni(CO)_3L}$. Lower frequency commonly indicates greater net [electron](electron.md) release into the remaining CO ligands. It combines donation and acceptance and is not a direct measurement of [octahedral splitting](octahedral-splitting.md).
