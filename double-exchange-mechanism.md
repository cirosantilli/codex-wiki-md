# Double-exchange mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-exchange_mechanism)

Double exchange couples localized magnetic moments through actual carrier hopping between sites of different valence. Strong local [Hund's coupling](hund-s-coupling.md) makes hopping energetically easier when neighboring core spins align, lowering the carrier [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). In the classical strong-coupling limit, a common effective hopping is $t_{\rm eff}=t\cos(\theta/2)$ for relative spin angle $\theta$; aligned cores maximize its magnitude. Unlike insulating [superexchange](superexchange.md), this mechanism directly connects carrier motion and magnetic alignment.[1951 abstract: the observed connection between conduction and ferromagnetism in manganese perovskites motivates the double-exchange mechanism](interaction-between-the-d-shells-in-the-transition-metals-ii-ferromagnetic-compounds-of-manganese-with-perovskite-structure-by-clarence-zener.md)
