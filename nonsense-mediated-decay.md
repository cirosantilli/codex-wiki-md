# Nonsense-mediated decay

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

An [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) surveillance pathway that removes certain transcripts after a termination event interpreted in an abnormal context. It can alter observed [transcript isoform](transcript-isoform.md) abundance after [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md), so mature abundance is not always a direct measure of the production ratio.
