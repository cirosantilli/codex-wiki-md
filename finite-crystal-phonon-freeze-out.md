# Finite-crystal phonon freeze-out

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon](phonon.md)

An internal harmonic vibration spectrum with lowest positive frequency $\omega_{\min}$ has the displayed low-[temperature](temperature-split.md) thermal [phonon](phonon.md) population, where $g_{\min}$ is its degeneracy. The continuum bulk law fails when thermally occupied wavelengths reach the sample size. Mechanical boundary conditions determine $\omega_{\min}$; rigid translation of the entire solid is not an internal [phonon](phonon.md) mode.
