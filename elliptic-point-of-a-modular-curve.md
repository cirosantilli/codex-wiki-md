# Elliptic point of a modular curve

↑ **Parent:** [Modular curve](modular-curve.md)

An [elliptic point of a modular curve](elliptic-point-of-a-modular-curve.md) is the image of a point of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) with a nontrivial [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) in the effective modular subgroup. For the full [modular group](modular-group.md) the [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) orders are two and three, represented by $i$ and $e^{2\pi i/3}$. A local disk coordinate $u$ upstairs descends as $u^e$ when the [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) order is $e$. The central [matrices](matrix.md) $\pm I$ are factored out before counting this order.
