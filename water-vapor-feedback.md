# Water-vapor feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Climate feedback](climate-feedback.md)

[Water-vapor feedback](water-vapor-feedback.md) is the [climate feedback](climate-feedback.md) from changing atmospheric [water vapor](water-vapor.md) as [temperature](temperature-split.md) changes. Fixed [relative humidity](relative-humidity.md) allows more [water vapor](water-vapor.md) in a warmer column, altering [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md) and often amplifying warming. Fixing absolute humidity suppresses this response. The net restoring coefficient must still include the other [climate feedbacks](climate-feedback.md); this individual positive contribution does not establish instability.
