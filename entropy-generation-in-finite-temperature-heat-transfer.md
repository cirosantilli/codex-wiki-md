# Entropy generation in finite-temperature heat transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md)

When a system at instantaneous [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T$ absorbs [heat](heat-split.md) $dQ$ from a reservoir at $T_R$, with no other [entropy](entropy-split.md) production in the system, its [entropy](entropy-split.md) increases by $dQ/T$ while the reservoir changes by $-dQ/T_R$. The total is positive for spontaneous [heat](heat-split.md) transfer across a finite [temperature](temperature-split.md) difference. A quasistatic mechanical path can therefore be thermally irreversible.
