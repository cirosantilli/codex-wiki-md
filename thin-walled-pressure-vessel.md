# Thin-walled pressure vessel

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanical engineering](mechanical-engineering.md)

A pressure-containing shell whose thickness is small relative to its curvature radius, allowing a membrane-stress approximation. For a cylindrical closed-end tube, pressure [force](force-split.md) balance gives [hoop stress](hoop-stress.md) $Pr/t$ and axial [stress](stress.md) $Pr/(2t)$, with the same radius convention and neglected end disturbances.
