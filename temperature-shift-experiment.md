# Temperature-shift experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature-sensitive mutation](temperature-sensitive-mutation.md)

Changing between permissive and restrictive temperatures at defined stages tests when a conditional [gene](gene.md) product is required. Reciprocal shifts and short pulses separate initiation from maintenance, subject to inactivation kinetics and [protein perdurance](protein-perdurance.md).
