# Generalized Pareto distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Extreme value theory](extreme-value-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_Pareto_distribution)

For scale $\sigma>0$ and shape $\xi$, the generalized Pareto distribution on nonnegative excesses has [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md)

$$
G_{\xi,\sigma}(y)=1-(1+\xi y/\sigma)^{-1/\xi},\qquad y\geq0,\quad1+\xi y/\sigma>0.
$$

The case $\xi=0$ is the continuous limit $1-e^{-y/\sigma}$. Positive $\xi$ gives a power-law tail, zero gives an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md), and negative $\xi$ gives a finite upper endpoint $-\sigma/\xi$. Its role in modelling high-threshold excesses is explained by the [Pickands-Balkema-de Haan theorem](pickands-balkema-de-haan-theorem.md).
