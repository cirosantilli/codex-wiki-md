# Surface temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Atmosphere](atmosphere.md)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) of a planetary surface at a specified location or averaging scale. It can differ from overlying air temperature, effective radiating temperature and spectral [brightness temperature](brightness-temperature.md).
