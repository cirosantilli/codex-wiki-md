# Power-law nonlinear Klein-Gordon pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear Klein-Gordon equation](nonlinear-klein-gordon-equation.md)

For $f(w)=-aw+bw^n$, with $a,b>0$ and integer $n>1$, a positive localized static pulse has $C^{n-1}=a(n+1)/(2b)$ and $B=(n-1)\sqrt a/2$. Writing the same profile as $[A\cosh^2(Bx)]^{1/(1-n)}$ instead gives $A=2b/[a(n+1)]$. These are different amplitude conventions for the same solution. A [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) moves the pulse at [speed](speed.md) $\tanh\beta$, preserves its peak height and contracts its width by $1/\cosh\beta$. Existence alone does not imply dynamical stability.
