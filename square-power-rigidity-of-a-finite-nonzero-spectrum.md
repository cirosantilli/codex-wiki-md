# Square-power rigidity of a finite nonzero spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Root of unity](root-of-unity.md)

Let $S$ be a finite multiset of nonzero complex numbers such that taking the $m^2$th powers permutes it for every positive integer $m$. Taking $m=2$ and iterating the resulting permutation shows that each $z\in S$ obeys $z^{4^r}=z$ for some $r\geq1$, hence is a [root of unity](root-of-unity.md). Let $d$ be a common multiple of their orders. The $d^2$th powers are all one, and the assumed permutation then forces every original element to be one. For a [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md), the [spectral theorem for normal operators](spectral-theorem-for-normal-operators.md) further forces the matrix itself to be the identity.
