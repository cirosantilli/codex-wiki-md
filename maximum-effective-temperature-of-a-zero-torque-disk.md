# Maximum effective temperature of a zero-torque disk

↑ **Parent:** [Effective-temperature profile of a zero-torque disk](effective-temperature-profile-of-a-zero-torque-disk.md)

For the [effective-temperature profile of a zero-torque disk](effective-temperature-profile-of-a-zero-torque-disk.md), differentiation of $y^{-3}(1-y^{-1/2})$ with $y=r/r_{\rm in}$ gives $y_{\max}=49/36$. Thus $T_{\max}=(6/7)^{3/2}7^{-1/4}T_{\rm in}\simeq0.488T_{\rm in}$. This distinguishes a genuine maximum [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md) from the normalization used in a power-law approximation.
