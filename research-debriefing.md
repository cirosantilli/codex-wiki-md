# Research debriefing

↑ **Parent:** [Research ethics](research-ethics.md)

An explanation after participation of the study's purpose and relevant procedures, including justified deception, with an opportunity for questions and appropriate follow-up. Its form should fit what participants experienced and the study's risks.
