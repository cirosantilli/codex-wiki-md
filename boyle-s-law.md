<h1 id="boyle-s-law">Boyle's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal process](isothermal-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyle's_law)

For a fixed amount of an ideal gas at constant temperature, pressure times volume is constant. Equivalently its density is proportional to pressure. In a dilute bubbly flow, this allows bubble volume and its contribution to [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) to increase as hydrostatic pressure decreases, provided gas mass is retained and dissolution is negligible.
