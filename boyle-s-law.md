<h1 id="boyle-s-law">Boyle's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal process](isothermal-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyle's_law)

[Boyle's law](boyle-s-law.md) states that a fixed [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) at constant absolute [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md) has constant product of [absolute pressure](absolute-pressure.md) and total [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md):

$$
pV=C,\qquad p_1V_1=p_2V_2,\qquad p_2=p_1\frac{V_1}{V_2}.
$$

Here $p$ is measured relative to a [vacuum](vacuum.md), not relative to the surrounding [atmospheric pressure](atmospheric-pressure.md). Halving the [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) doubles the [absolute pressure](absolute-pressure.md); doubling the [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) halves it. A [real gas](real-gas.md) approximately follows [Boyle's law](boyle-s-law.md) when it is sufficiently dilute and far from a [phase transition](phase-transition.md).[§2.1, The Gas Laws, Figure 2.4, and equations 2.1–2.2: fixed temperature and particle number, absolute pressure, and the dilute-gas limit](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/2-1-molecular-model-of-an-ideal-gas.md)

For an original calculated example, suppose a sealed [gas](gas.md) sample has $p_1=100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $V_1=100\,\mathrm{cm^3}$. After [isothermal compression](isothermal-compression.md) to $V_2=50\,\mathrm{cm^3}$,

$$
p_2=(100\,\mathrm{kPa})\frac{100\,\mathrm{cm^3}}{50\,\mathrm{cm^3}}
=200\,\mathrm{kPa}.
$$

Both states have $C=10\,000\,\mathrm{kPa\,cm^3}=10\,\mathrm J$, since $1\,\mathrm{kPa\,cm^3}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm J$. The [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) ratio is dimensionless, so using the same [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) units in numerator and denominator leaves the [pressure](pressure-split.md) in kilopascals. Although $pV$ has units of [energy](energy-split.md), its value is not the compression [work](work.md): [isothermal ideal-gas work](isothermal-ideal-gas-work.md) involves an [integral](integral-split.md) along the path.

The blue curves show calculated [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) states, not measurements. The left panel is a branch of a [hyperbola](hyperbola.md); the right panel plots exactly the same [absolute pressures](absolute-pressure.md) against $1/V$. The dashed line uses [gauge pressure](gauge-pressure.md) with a stipulated constant [atmospheric pressure](atmospheric-pressure.md) of $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Its displaced intercept explains why [absolute pressure in Boyle's law](absolute-pressure-in-boyle-s-law.md) matters.

A short derivation starts from the [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md), $pV=nRT$. Fixing the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) $n$ and absolute [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md) $T$ fixes $C=nRT$, where $R$ is the [molar gas constant](molar-gas-constant.md). The microscopic picture in [ideal gas pressure from wall collisions](ideal-gas-pressure-from-wall-collisions.md) is equally appealing: at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), the typical particle [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) stays fixed, while compression increases the [number density](number-density.md) and wall [momentum](momentum-split.md) transfer per unit [surface area](surface-area.md) and time. A larger [absolute pressure](absolute-pressure.md) follows without requiring hotter particles.

The constraints are essential. A leak changes the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md). Rapid compression can raise the [temperature](temperature-split.md) before [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) restores [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md), as in the [isothermal gas syringe experiment](isothermal-gas-syringe-experiment.md). Molecular size and interactions in a dense [real gas](real-gas.md) invalidate the [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) approximation; the [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md) describes one such correction. Equal endpoint [temperatures](temperature-split.md) allow an endpoint test of [Boyle's law](boyle-s-law.md), but do not establish that every intervening state lies on an [isothermal process](isothermal-process.md).

For fixed [mass](mass.md) $m$, [mass density](density.md) satisfies $\rho=m/V=mp/C$, so it is proportional to [absolute pressure](absolute-pressure.md). In a dilute bubbly flow, [Boyle's law](boyle-s-law.md) allows bubble [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) and its contribution to [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) to increase as [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) decreases, provided [gas](gas.md) [mass](mass.md) is retained, dissolution is negligible, and the [temperature](temperature-split.md) stays approximately fixed. Its usefulness is that a simple ratio predicts a substantial size change without tracking individual [molecules](molecule-split.md).
