# Near-horizon limit

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

A limit retaining the geometry near a horizon while discarding terms describing its distant asymptotic region. For a regular extremal brane throat, removing the constant from its harmonic function can yield an [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) factor. Local symmetries and supersymmetries of the limiting geometry need not extend to the full original spacetime.
