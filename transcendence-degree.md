# Transcendence degree

↑ **Parent:** [Transcendence basis](transcendence-basis.md)

The transcendence degree is the cardinality of a [transcendence basis](transcendence-basis.md). It counts independent transcendental parameters. In a finitely generated separably generated [field extension](field-extension.md), it equals the dimension of the [Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md) as a vector space over the extension field.
