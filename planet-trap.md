# Planet trap

↑ **Parent:** [Type I migration](type-i-migration.md)

A location where the net disk torque on a [planet](planet.md) vanishes and, for a stable trap, restoring torque changes sign across the location. A torque zero alone does not guarantee stable trapping as the disk or [planet](planet.md) evolves.
