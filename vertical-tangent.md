# Vertical tangent

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent line](tangent-line.md)

A [tangent line](tangent-line.md) parallel to the vertical coordinate axis. For a regular [level set](level-set.md) $\Phi(x,y)=C$, the conditions $\partial\Phi/\partial y=0$ and $\partial\Phi/\partial x\ne0$ make the [tangent line](tangent-line.md) vertical: the relation $d\Phi=0$ forces $dx=0$. The [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md) then expresses $x$ locally as a differentiable function of $y$, although a graph $y(x)$ has no finite derivative there. The [curve](curve.md) $x=y^2$ at the origin is a simple example.
