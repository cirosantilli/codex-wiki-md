# Digit lifting for square roots of 2-adic units

↑ **Parent:** [2-adic unit](2-adic-unit.md)

An odd square is congruent to one modulo eight. Conversely, for $u\equiv1\pmod8$, begin with $x_3=1$ and $x_n^2\equiv u\pmod{2^n}$. For $n\ge3$, adding $\varepsilon_n2^{n-1}$ changes the square by $\varepsilon_n2^nx_n$ modulo $2^{n+1}$. Since $x_n$ is odd, one choice of $\varepsilon_n\in\{0,1\}$ gives the next congruence. The corrections tend to zero 2-adically, so completeness produces an odd square root. This directly proves the unit square criterion without needing a unit derivative in [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md).
