# Ground-state representation of a Sturm-Liouville energy

↑ **Parent:** [Sturm-Liouville theory](sturm-liouville-theory-split.md)

Suppose $u>0$ on an interval solves the [Sturm-Liouville equation](sturm-liouville-equation.md) $-(pu')'+qu=\lambda wu$. Writing $y=uv$ and expanding derivatives gives

$$
py'^2+(q-\lambda w)y^2=pu^2v'^2+(puu'v^2)'.
$$

If the endpoint term $[pu'y^2/u]$ vanishes, integration yields the displayed identity. With $p>0$, it proves a lower bound $\lambda$ for the associated [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md), attained by $y=u$ when $u$ is admissible. For singular endpoints, establish the identity on compact subintervals and check the boundary limits; this is essential for the [Bessel differential equation](bessel-differential-equation.md) with $p=w=x$, $q=k^2/x$.
