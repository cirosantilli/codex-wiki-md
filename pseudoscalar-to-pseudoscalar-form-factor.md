# Pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar form factor

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)

Between spin-zero [pseudoscalars](pseudoscalar.md), [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md) decomposes a [vector current](vector-current.md) [matrix element](matrix-element.md) into $(p+k)^\mu f_+(q^2)+(p-k)^\mu f_-(q^2)$, with $q=p-k$. [Strong interaction](strong-interaction.md) [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) makes the corresponding [axial current](axial-current.md) matrix element vanish.
