# Stationary bending warp of a Kerr disk

↑ **Parent:** [Inviscid warp equations of a nearly Keplerian disk](inviscid-warp-equations-of-a-nearly-keplerian-disk.md)

For $a>0$, $\Sigma\propto R^{-3/4}$, constant [disk aspect ratio](disk-aspect-ratio.md) $\epsilon=H/R$, and $r=Rc^2/(GM)$, the inviscid steady [disk warp](disk-warp.md) equation reduces to $W_{xx}+W=0$ in the displayed coordinate. The vanishing inner torque gives $W_x(x_{\mathrm{in}})=0$; the outer tilt gives $W(0)=W_\infty$. The displayed solution holds when $\cos x_{\mathrm{in}}\ne0$. If $x_{\mathrm{in}}=\pi$, it is $W=W_\infty\cos x$, with an aligned node at $r=2^{4/5}r_{\mathrm{in}}$ and opposite signed inner tilt. For negative spin the corresponding equation is hyperbolic, giving $W=W_\infty\cosh(x_{\mathrm{in}}-x)/\cosh x_{\mathrm{in}}$ with $x=4\sqrt{24|a|}\,r^{-5/4}/(5\epsilon)$.
