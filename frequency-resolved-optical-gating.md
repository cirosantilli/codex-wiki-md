# Frequency-resolved optical gating

↑ **Parent:** [Optical pulse](optical-pulse.md)

Measure the spectrum of a nonlinear optical gate signal as its relative delay is varied. A retrieval algorithm seeks a complex pulse consistent with the resulting two-dimensional [intensity](intensity-physics.md) record and the particular gate law. Different gate laws have different ambiguities and response limitations; a small fitting residual alone does not guarantee stable pulses or correct calibration.
