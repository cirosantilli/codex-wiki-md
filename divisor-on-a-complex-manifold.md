# Divisor on a complex manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Complex analytic hypersurface](complex-analytic-hypersurface.md)

A divisor on a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) is a locally finite formal sum

$$
D=\sum_Yn_YY,
$$

where the $Y$ are irreducible complex analytic hypersurfaces and $n_Y\in\mathbb Z$. Products of powers of [local defining functions](local-defining-function-of-a-complex-analytic-hypersurface.md) give local meromorphic equations $f_\alpha$ for $D$ whose ratios $f_\alpha/f_\beta$ are nowhere-vanishing holomorphic functions.
