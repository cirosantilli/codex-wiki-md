# GLUT1

↑ **Parent:** [Cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md)

[GLUT1](glut1.md) is a facilitative glucose transporter expressed in many tissues. Human beta cells can rely predominantly on GLUT1 rather than sharing the high GLUT2 abundance of rodent beta cells.
