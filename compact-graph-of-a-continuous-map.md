# Compact graph of a continuous map

↑ **Parent:** [Graph of a function](graph-of-a-function.md)

The map $x\mapsto(x,f(x))$ is continuous into the [product topology](product-topology.md). It maps a [compact space](compact-space.md) $X$ onto the [graph of a function](graph-of-a-function.md) of a continuous $f$. Hence that graph is compact, whether or not the codomain is a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md). Projection to $X$ is its continuous inverse.
