# Decoherence factor

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum decoherence](quantum-decoherence.md)

If a joint pure state is $\alpha|0\rangle|E_0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle|E_1\rangle$, the off-diagonal entry of the system's [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) is multiplied by the environmental overlap $\langle E_1|E_0\rangle$. Independent repeated interactions multiply these overlaps.
