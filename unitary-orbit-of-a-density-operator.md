# Unitary orbit of a density operator

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix.md)

The unitary orbit consists exactly of [density operators](density-matrix.md) with the same spectrum. If distinct eigenvalues have multiplicities $n_j$, its stabilizer is $\prod_jU(n_j)$ and its real dimension is $N^2-\sum_jn_j^2$. A reachable compact group with [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$ is transitive on this orbit exactly when $\dim\mathfrak g-\dim(\mathfrak g\cap\{X:[X,\rho]=0\})$ equals the orbit dimension. Global phases act trivially by conjugation.
