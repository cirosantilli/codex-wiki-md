# Quadratic sign-vector diameter formula

↑ **Parent:** [Kahn-Kalai counterexample to the Borsuk conjecture](kahn-kalai-counterexample-to-the-borsuk-conjecture.md)

Take $N=4p-1$, $|A|=|B|=2p-1$ and sign [vectors](vector.md) $u_A=2\mathbf1_A-\mathbf1$. The [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md) of two outer products is $(u_A\cdot u_B)^2$, proving the formula. The squared [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) is largest precisely when $|A\cap B|=p-1$, where the inner product is minus one. The common diagonal can be deleted, leaving an isometric representation in dimension $\binom N2$ if off-diagonal coordinates are multiplied by $\sqrt2$. The [Frankl-Wilson theorem](frankl-wilson-theorem.md) then bounds each smaller-[diameter](diameter.md) class.
