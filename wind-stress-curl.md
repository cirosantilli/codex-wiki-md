# Wind stress curl

↑ **Parent:** [Wind stress](wind-stress.md)

The vertical [curl](curl.md) of surface [wind stress](wind-stress.md) is $(\nabla_h\times\boldsymbol\tau)_z=\tau_{y,x}-\tau_{x,y}$. In a layer of density $\rho_0$ and depth $h$, its normalized [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) source is $W=(\nabla_h\times\boldsymbol\tau)_z/(\rho_0h)$. It drives [Sverdrup balance](sverdrup-balance.md) in the weakly nonlinear steady ocean interior.
