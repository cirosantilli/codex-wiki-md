# Balanced shallow-water energy as a signed potential-vorticity pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic adjustment](geostrophic-adjustment.md)

With constant density divided out, a decaying or periodic [geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md) on an [f-plane](f-plane.md) has

$$
E_b=\frac12\int(H|\mathbf u_g|^2+g\eta^2)\,dA=-\frac{gH}{2f}\int\eta q\,dA,
$$

where $q=\zeta-f\eta/H$ is the signed [linearized shallow-water potential-vorticity anomaly](linearized-shallow-water-potential-vorticity-anomaly.md). The stationary height equation and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) prove the identity. Replacing $q$ by $|q|$ destroys it: an odd height paired with symmetric opposite-sign sheets would then misleadingly give zero.
