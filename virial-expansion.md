# Virial expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virial_expansion)

The virial expansion expresses the free energy or pressure of a dilute system as a power series in number density whose coefficients encode few-body interactions.
