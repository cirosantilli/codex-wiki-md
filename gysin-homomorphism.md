# Gysin homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gysin_homomorphism)

For an oriented proper smooth map $f:M^m\to N^n$ of oriented [manifolds](topological-manifold.md), the [Gysin homomorphism](gysin-homomorphism.md) is a degree-shifting pushforward $f_!:H^k(M)\to H^{k+n-m}(N)$, defined using [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md) and the appropriate pushforward on homology. For an oriented sphere bundle it is integration along the fiber and participates in the [Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md). Oriented embeddings give the corresponding Thom-class construction.
