# Predictable process

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predictable_process)

In discrete time, a process $A_n$ is predictable or previsible when $A_n$ is $\mathcal F_{n-1}$-measurable for every $n\geq1$. In continuous time, predictability means measurability with respect to the [predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md).
