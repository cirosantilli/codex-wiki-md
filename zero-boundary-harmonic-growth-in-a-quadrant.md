# Zero-boundary harmonic growth in a quadrant

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Poisson integral in a quadrant](dirichlet-poisson-integral-in-a-quadrant.md)

The [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $v(x,y)=xy$ vanishes on both axes but is nonzero in the quadrant, and $v_z=-iz/2$. More generally $\operatorname{Im}z^{2m}$, for a positive integer $m$, vanishes on both axes and is harmonic. Adding these unbounded functions leaves all edge [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) unchanged. Thus decay of the boundary data does not imply decay of every solution, and an edge-data-only formula cannot determine an arbitrary solution's [Wirtinger derivative](wirtinger-derivatives.md). A boundedness or suitable growth condition must select the solution.
