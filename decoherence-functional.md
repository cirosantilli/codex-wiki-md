# Decoherence functional

↑ **Parent:** [Consistent histories](consistent-histories.md)

For alternatives represented by [projection operators](projection-linear-algebra.md) at successive times, set $C_\alpha=P_{\alpha_n}(t_n)\cdots P_{\alpha_1}(t_1)$ in the [Heisenberg picture](heisenberg-picture.md). The displayed functional uses the initial [density operator](density-matrix-split.md) $\rho$. Combining two histories gives $D(\alpha+\beta,\alpha+\beta)=D(\alpha,\alpha)+D(\beta,\beta)+2\operatorname{Re}D(\alpha,\beta)$. Thus vanishing real cross-terms gives the [probability](probability.md) sum rule; the stronger commonly used condition $D(\alpha,\beta)=0$ for distinct histories is sufficient as well. Its diagonal is nonnegative because each $C_\alpha\rho C_\alpha^\dagger$ is positive.
