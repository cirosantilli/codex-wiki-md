# Axis of a hyperbolic-space isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic space](hyperbolic-space.md)

An isometry of [hyperbolic space](hyperbolic-space.md) with positive attained [translation length](translation-length.md) has an invariant complete geodesic joining its two ideal fixed points. This is its axis. It translates along the axis by its translation length; in three dimensions it may additionally rotate around it. In upper half-space, $(z,t)\mapsto(kz,|k|t)$ with $|k|\ne1$ has the vertical axis through zero and length $|\log|k||$. Every connecting path has length at least the change in $\log t$, and a vertical segment attains the bound.
