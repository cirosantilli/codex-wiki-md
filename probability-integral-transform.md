# Probability integral transform

↑ **Parent:** [Cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_integral_transform)

If $X$ has a continuous [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F$, then $F(X)$ has the [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(0,1)$. Combined with [inverse transform sampling](inverse-transform-sampling.md), this constructs the [monotone rearrangement](monotone-rearrangement.md) $G^{-1}(F(X))$ to a target with [quantile function](quantile-function.md) $G^{-1}$.
