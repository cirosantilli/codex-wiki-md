# Magnetohydrodynamic wave polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic wave](magnetohydrodynamic-wave.md)

A polarization specifies the direction or trajectory of the oscillating [velocity](velocity.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) in a [magnetohydrodynamic wave](magnetohydrodynamic-wave.md). The linear [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) has velocity perpendicular to the plane of the background [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) and [wavevector](wavevector.md); the [magnetosonic waves](magnetosonic-wave.md) have velocity in that plane. A real fixed polarization vector gives a linear oscillation. Two orthogonal components of equal amplitude in quadrature give a circular trajectory. For propagation parallel to a uniform field, combining the two degenerate transverse linear polarizations produces circular polarization; at finite amplitude its constant transverse magnetic-field magnitude removes the longitudinal [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) gradient.
