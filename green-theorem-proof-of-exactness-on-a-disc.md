# Green-theorem proof of exactness on a disc

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md)

On a star-shaped planar domain containing zero, let $\eta$ be a smooth [closed differential one-form](closed-differential-one-form.md) and set $f(x)=\int_{[0,x]}\eta$. [Green theorem](green-theorem.md) on the triangle with vertices $0,x,x+h$ gives $f(x+h)-f(x)=\int_{[x,x+h]}\eta$. Dividing by a displacement and taking its [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) proves $df=\eta$. Thus every such form is an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md), a degree-one version of the [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) which uses only the planar integral theorem.
