# Cartesian number state of the three-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Three-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator](three-dimensional-isotropic-harmonic-oscillator.md)

From the normalized [ground state](ground-state.md) annihilated by every $A_i$, the normalized Cartesian number states are

$$
|n_1,n_2,n_3\rangle
=\prod_{i=1}^3\frac{(A_i^\dagger)^{n_i}}{\sqrt{n_i!}}|0\rangle.
$$

They have energy $\hbar\omega(n_1+n_2+n_3+3/2)$.
