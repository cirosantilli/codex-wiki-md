<h1 id="kraft-mcmillan-inequality">Kraft–McMillan inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Prefix code](prefix-code.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraft–McMillan_inequality)

The lengths of every decipherable binary code satisfy

$$
\sum_i2^{-l_i}\leq1.
$$

Conversely, any positive integer lengths satisfying this bound can be realized by a [prefix code](prefix-code.md).
