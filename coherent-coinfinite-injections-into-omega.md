# Coherent coinfinite injections into omega

↑ **Parent:** [Injective function](injective-function.md)

There are [injections](injective-function.md) $e_\alpha:\alpha\to\omega$ for $\alpha<\omega_1$, each with coinfinite range, with $e_\alpha\upharpoonright\beta$ equal to $e_\beta$ outside a [finite set](finite-set.md). At countable limits, extend partial [functions](function-split.md) along a cofinal sequence, repair finitely many collisions, and reserve a fresh omitted natural number at each step. The reserved sequence makes the union coinfinite and permits all later successor steps.
