# Brownian first-passage time

↑ **Parent:** [First-passage time](first-passage-time.md)

For standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) and $a>0$, $T_a=\inf\{t\geq0:B_t\geq a\}$ is finite [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) and

$$
\mathbb P(T_a\leq t)=2\left(1-\Phi\left(\frac a{\sqrt t}\right)\right),\qquad t>0.
$$

The [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) gives this [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md). The [Brownian scaling](brownian-scaling.md) identity $T_{ca}\overset d=c^2T_a$ and the [Strong Markov property](strong-markov-property.md) show its [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) is a [strictly stable distribution](strictly-stable-distribution.md) with index $1/2$.
