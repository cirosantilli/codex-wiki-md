# Tetrahydropyranyl protecting group

↑ **Parent:** [Protecting group](protecting-group.md)

A cyclic [acetal](acetal.md) [protecting group](protecting-group.md) made from an [alcohol](alcohol.md) and dihydropyran. It protects the original [alcohol](alcohol.md) [oxygen](oxygen.md) and can be removed under suitable acidic [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) conditions.
