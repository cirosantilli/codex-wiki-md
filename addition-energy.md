# Addition energy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum dot](quantum-dot.md)

The difference between successive electron-addition chemical potentials. In a [constant interaction model](constant-interaction-model.md) it is $E_{\rm add}=e^2/C_\Sigma+\epsilon_{N+1}-\epsilon_N$, separating electrostatic charging from orbital level spacing. Spin, exchange and correlation can change the orbital contribution.
