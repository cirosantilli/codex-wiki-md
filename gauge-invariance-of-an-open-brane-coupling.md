# Gauge invariance of an open-brane coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Wess-Zumino brane coupling](wess-zumino-brane-coupling.md)

For an oriented [brane](brane.md) worldvolume $W$, a [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) $C\mapsto C+d\Lambda$ changes the [Wess-Zumino brane coupling](wess-zumino-brane-coupling.md) by $q\int_{\partial W}X^*\Lambda$, by the [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md). If the brane boundary lies on a manifold carrying a form $B$ of one lower degree, adding the displayed boundary term and transforming $B\mapsto B-\Lambda$ cancels this variation. The sign uses the induced boundary orientation. A closed worldvolume needs no boundary term; noncompact cases require appropriate support or decay conditions.
