# Full-sky cubic bispectrum estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced CMB bispectrum](reduced-cmb-bispectrum.md)

For an isotropic, full-sky temperature map with a fixed bispectrum template $b=f_{\mathrm{NL}}b^1$, the inverse-variance-weighted cubic statistic has normalization $F=\frac16\sum (b^1)^2\mathcal G^2/(C_1C_2C_3)$ over ordered triples. It is unbiased under the linear template relation and has Gaussian [cosmic variance](cosmic-variance.md) $1/F$. The factor $1/6$ counts the six cross-triple [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md). A removed monopole cancels the nine internal-pairing terms; incomplete sky coverage or anisotropic noise generally require a linear correction.
