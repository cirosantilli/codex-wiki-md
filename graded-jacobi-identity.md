# Graded Jacobi identity

↑ **Parent:** [Lie superalgebra](lie-superalgebra.md)

The [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) of a [Lie superalgebra](lie-superalgebra.md) includes parity signs. In particular, for odd $c$ and even $\phi$ it gives $[c,[c,\phi]]=\tfrac12[[c,c],\phi]$.
