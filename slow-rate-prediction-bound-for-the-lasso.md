# Slow-rate prediction bound for the Lasso

↑ **Parent:** [Basic inequality for the Lasso](basic-inequality-for-the-lasso.md)

For $Y=X\beta^0+\eta$, the [Lasso](lasso.md) objective $\|Y-X\beta\|_2^2/(2n)+\lambda\|\beta\|_1$ has this prediction bound whenever $\|X^T\eta\|_\infty/n\leq\lambda$. Compare its value at $\widehat\beta$ and $\beta^0$, apply [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) to the score, and use the triangle inequality. No [restricted eigenvalue condition](restricted-eigenvalue-condition.md) is required.
