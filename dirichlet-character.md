# Dirichlet character

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_character)

A Dirichlet character modulo $m$ is a homomorphism $(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times\to\mathbb C^\times$, extended periodically to integers by zero on numbers not coprime to $m$.
