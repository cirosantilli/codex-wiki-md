# Significand

↑ **Parent:** [Floating-point representation](floating-point-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Significand)

The significant-digit factor $m$ in a [floating-point representation](floating-point-representation.md) $(-1)^s m\,\beta^e$, where $s$ is the sign [bit](bit.md), $\beta$ the radix and $e$ the [exponent](exponent.md). For a normal positive binary [significand](significand.md), $1\leq m<2$, and the leading one need not be stored. In a [subnormal number](subnormal-number.md), the leading digit is zero at the minimum normal [exponent](exponent.md) scale. The [significand](significand.md) is a numerical factor, distinct from the stored trailing fraction [bits](bit.md) that encode part of it.
