# Image charges for a neutral conducting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Image charge for a grounded conducting sphere](image-charge-for-a-grounded-conducting-sphere.md)

A real charge $q$ at $d>a$ outside a spherical conductor of radius $a$ has the usual grounded-sphere [image charge](image-charge.md) $q'=-aq/d$ at $x'=a^2/d$. Adding a central image $q_0=-q'$ makes the total image charge zero, representing an isolated neutral conductor. The off-centre pair has zero potential on $r=a$; the central image therefore sets the constant conductor potential to $q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0d)$. Its induced [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) is $p_x=q'x'=-qa^3/d^2$. Keeping the applied central field $\mathbf E_0=-q\widehat{\mathbf x}/(4\pi\varepsilon_0d^2)$ fixed gives $\mathbf p=4\pi\varepsilon_0a^3\mathbf E_0$. As $d\to\infty$, the external field becomes uniform near the sphere; its divergent constant potential can be removed without affecting the [electric field](electric-field-split.md).
