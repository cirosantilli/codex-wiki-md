# Equivalence of partial functions modulo finite changes

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md)

Two [functions](function-split.md) $f:A\to X$ and $g:B\to X$, whose domains lie in one ambient [set](set-split.md), are equivalent modulo finite changes when $A\mathbin\triangle B$ is a [finite set](finite-set.md) and the [functions](function-split.md) disagree at only finitely many points of $A\cap B$. The [symmetric difference](symmetric-difference.md) inclusion $A\mathbin\triangle C\subseteq(A\mathbin\triangle B)\cup(B\mathbin\triangle C)$ handles domains in a transitive chain. An exceptional [set](set-split.md) for values must also include points of $A\cap C$ absent from the middle domain $B$; these lie in the finite difference $A\setminus B$.
