<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose $f=1$, so that $R_f=R$ and $f\notin\mathfrak p$. For an [integral domain](../../../../../integral-domain.md), the canonical [ring homomorphism](../../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) $R\to R_{\mathfrak p}$ is [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md): if $a/1=0$, the [vanishing criterion in a module localization](../../../../../vanishing-criterion-in-a-module-localization.md) gives $sa=0$ for some $s\notin\mathfrak p$. Such an $s$ is nonzero, so the absence of [zero divisors](../../../../../zero-divisor.md) gives $a=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{f=1\text{ works whenever }R\text{ is an integral domain}.}
$$
