<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [ideal](../../../../ideal.md) $\mathfrak q$ is $\mathfrak p$-[primary](../../../../primary-ideal.md) if it is a proper [primary ideal](../../../../primary-ideal.md) whose [radical of an ideal](../../../../radical-of-an-ideal.md) is $\mathfrak p$. Explicitly,

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{\mathfrak q}=\mathfrak p,\qquad ab\in\mathfrak q,\ a\notin\mathfrak q\ \Longrightarrow\ b^n\in\mathfrak q\text{ for some }n\geq1.}
$$

Equivalently, $ab\in\mathfrak q$ and $b\notin\mathfrak p$ imply $a\in\mathfrak q$: multiplication by every element outside $\mathfrak p$ is [injective](../../../../injective-function.md) on the [quotient ring](../../../../quotient-ring.md) $R/\mathfrak q$.
