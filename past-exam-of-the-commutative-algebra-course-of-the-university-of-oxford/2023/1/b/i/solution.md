A [minimal primary decomposition](../../../../../minimal-primary-decomposition.md) is a finite expression

$$
\boxed{I=\bigcap_{j=1}^t\mathfrak q_j}
$$

in proper [primary ideals](../../../../../primary-ideal.md) such that the [prime ideals](../../../../../prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak p_j=\sqrt{\mathfrak q_j}$ are pairwise distinct and no [primary ideal](../../../../../primary-ideal.md) can be omitted. More explicitly, for every $j$,

$$
\bigcap_{i\ne j}\mathfrak q_i\not\subseteq\mathfrak q_j.
$$

Thus minimality means both irredundancy and distinct [radicals of ideals](../../../../../radical-of-an-ideal.md); it does not assert that the [primary ideals](../../../../../primary-ideal.md) themselves are uniquely determined. For $I=R$, one may use the empty intersection convention.
