<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the terminology of a [minimal primary decomposition](../../../../../minimal-primary-decomposition.md), an [associated prime of a primary decomposition](../../../../../associated-prime-of-a-primary-decomposition.md) of $I$ is a [prime ideal](../../../../../prime-ideal.md) occurring as $\sqrt{\mathfrak q_j}$ in such a decomposition. The [first uniqueness theorem for primary decomposition](../../../../../first-uniqueness-theorem-for-primary-decomposition.md) says that this collection is independent of the chosen [minimal primary decomposition](../../../../../minimal-primary-decomposition.md). Among these [prime ideals](../../../../../prime-ideal.md), an [isolated prime of a primary decomposition](../../../../../isolated-prime-of-a-primary-decomposition.md) is minimal under inclusion, equivalently a [minimal prime over an ideal](../../../../../minimal-prime-over-an-ideal.md) $I$; an embedded [prime ideal](../../../../../prime-ideal.md) strictly contains another member of the collection. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{isolated}=\text{minimal over }I,\qquad\text{embedded}=\text{not minimal over }I.}
$$

When $R$ is a [Noetherian ring](../../../../../noetherian-ring.md), these are precisely the [associated primes of a module](../../../../../associated-prime-of-a-module.md) $R/I$. In that case the equivalent definition is

$$
\mathfrak p=\operatorname{Ann}_R(x+I)=(I:x)
\quad\text{for some }x\notin I,
$$

where $(I:x)$ is a [colon ideal](../../../../../colon-ideal.md). The decomposition terminology applies when a [minimal primary decomposition](../../../../../minimal-primary-decomposition.md) exists; a general [ring](../../../../../ring.md) need not provide one for every [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md).
