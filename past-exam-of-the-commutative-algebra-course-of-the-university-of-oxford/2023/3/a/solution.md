↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [ring](../../../../ring.md) is [reduced](../../../../reduced-ring.md) if its only [nilpotent element](../../../../nilpotent.md) is zero. Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{x^n=0\text{ for some }n\geq1\ \Longrightarrow\ x=0,\qquad\text{or }\sqrt{(0)}=0.}
$$

The second expression says that its [nilradical](../../../../nilradical.md) vanishes. A [reduced ring](../../../../reduced-ring.md) may have [zero divisors](../../../../zero-divisor.md); for example, the [direct product of rings](../../../../direct-product-of-rings.md) $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$ is [reduced](../../../../reduced-ring.md) but $(1,0)(0,1)=0$.
