<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Set $L=M/\mathfrak aM$, a [quotient module](../../../../quotient-module.md). The [exactness of localization](../../../../exactness-of-localization.md) gives, for every [maximal ideal](../../../../maximal-ideal.md) $\mathfrak m$,

$$
L_{\mathfrak m}\cong M_{\mathfrak m}/\mathfrak aR_{\mathfrak m}M_{\mathfrak m}.
$$

If $\mathfrak m\supseteq\mathfrak a$, the hypothesis $M_{\mathfrak m}=0$ makes this [quotient module](../../../../quotient-module.md) zero. If $\mathfrak m\not\supseteq\mathfrak a$, choose $s\in\mathfrak a\setminus\mathfrak m$. Its image in $R_{\mathfrak m}$ is a [unit](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md), so $\mathfrak aR_{\mathfrak m}=R_{\mathfrak m}$ and the displayed [quotient module](../../../../quotient-module.md) is again zero. Thus

$$
L_{\mathfrak m}=0\qquad\text{for every maximal ideal }\mathfrak m.
$$

For completeness, if $0\ne\ell\in L$, its [annihilator](../../../../annihilator-ring-theory.md) is a proper [ideal](../../../../ideal.md), and hence lies in a [maximal ideal](../../../../maximal-ideal.md) $\mathfrak m$. The [vanishing criterion in a module localization](../../../../vanishing-criterion-in-a-module-localization.md) shows $\ell/1\ne0$ in $L_{\mathfrak m}$: any element annihilating $\ell$ lies inside $\mathfrak m$. This contradicts the vanishing just proved. Hence $L=0$, and

$$
\boxed{M/\mathfrak aM=0,\qquad\mathfrak aM=M.}
$$

The use of [localization detects zero elements](../../../../localization-detects-zero-elements.md) is valid for every [module](../../../../module-mathematics.md); neither finite generation nor a [Noetherian](../../../../noetherian-ring.md) hypothesis is needed.
