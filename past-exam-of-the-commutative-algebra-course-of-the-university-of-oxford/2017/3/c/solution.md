<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppose, for a contradiction, that $P_2\cap\mathbb Z=(0)$. Then $P_1\cap\mathbb Z=(0)$ too. Both [prime ideals](../../../../prime-ideal.md) are disjoint from the [multiplicatively closed set](../../../../multiplicatively-closed-set.md) $Y=\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$. The [localization](../../../../localization-of-a-ring.md) is

$$
Y^{-1}\mathbb Z[t]=\mathbb Q[t]:
$$

each fraction on the left has rational coefficients, and every [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) with rational coefficients has a common nonzero integer denominator.

By [b](../b.md), the extended [prime ideals](../../../../prime-ideal.md) form a strict inclusion

$$
\mathbb Q[t]P_1\subsetneq\mathbb Q[t]P_2.
$$

The first is nonzero, because $P_1$ contains a nonzero [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) and the map $\mathbb Z[t]\to\mathbb Q[t]$ is [injective](../../../../injective-function.md).

The [polynomial ring](../../../../polynomial-ring.md) $\mathbb Q[t]$ is a [principal ideal domain](../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md): in any nonzero [ideal](../../../../ideal.md), a nonzero [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) of least [polynomial degree](../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) generates the [ideal](../../../../ideal.md), since the [polynomial division algorithm](../../../../polynomial-division.md) gives a remainder of smaller [polynomial degree](../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md), which must be zero. By [a](../a.md), every nonzero [prime ideal](../../../../prime-ideal.md) of this [principal ideal domain](../../../../principal-ideal-domain.md) is maximal. The strict inclusion of extended [prime ideals](../../../../prime-ideal.md) is therefore impossible. We obtain

$$
\boxed{P_2\cap\mathbb Z\ne(0).}
$$
