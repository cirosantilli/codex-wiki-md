# Ideal-sheaf vanishing for a coherent submodule of a trivial bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomological criterion for affineness](cohomological-criterion-for-affineness.md)

If $H^1(X,\mathcal I)=0$ for every [coherent ideal sheaf](coherent-ideal-sheaf.md), projection to the last coordinate makes any coherent $\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal O_X^r$ an extension of a coherent submodule of $\mathcal O_X^{r-1}$ by an ideal sheaf. The [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) proves the assertion by [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) on $r$.
