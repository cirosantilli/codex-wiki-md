# Poincare-index obstruction to a periodic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Index of a planar periodic orbit](index-of-a-planar-periodic-orbit.md)

A simply connected region containing no equilibrium, or containing equilibria whose total [Poincaré index](poincare-index.md) is not $+1$, cannot contain a [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) that encloses exactly those equilibria.
