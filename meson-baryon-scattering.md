# Meson-baryon scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Strong interaction](strong-interaction.md)

Scattering with one [meson](meson.md) and one [baryon](baryon.md) in the initial and final states. The [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md) conserves [electric charge](electric-charge.md), [baryon number](baryon-number.md), net [strangeness](strangeness.md), four-momentum, total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), and parity. Approximate [isospin](isospin.md) symmetry imposes additional relations among charge-channel amplitudes.
