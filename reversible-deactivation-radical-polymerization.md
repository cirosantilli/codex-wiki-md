# Reversible-deactivation radical polymerization

↑ **Parent:** [Radical polymerization](radical-polymerization.md)

[Radical polymerization](radical-polymerization.md) in which growing chain ends reversibly exchange between active [radicals](radical-chemistry.md) and dormant species. Keeping the active-radical concentration small can reduce bimolecular termination and narrow the [molar mass distribution](molar-mass-distribution.md). Termination and [chain transfer](chain-transfer-in-chain-polymerization.md) may still occur, so reversible deactivation alone does not guarantee ideal [living polymerization](living-polymerization.md).
