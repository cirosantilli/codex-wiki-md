<h1 id="earthquake-depth-origin-time-trade-off">Earthquake depth–origin-time trade-off</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake location by arrival times](earthquake-location-by-arrival-times.md)

For surface stations at similar distances, the travel-time [derivatives](derivative.md) with respect to depth can be small or nearly equal. Their arrival-time shifts are then difficult to distinguish from a common change in [earthquake origin time](earthquake-origin-time.md). A deeper source can be paired with an earlier origin. Nearby stations, broad distance coverage, and depth-sensitive phases help separate the two parameters.
