# Binomial series

↑ **Parent:** [Power series](power-series.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binomial_series)

For $|x|<1$, the binomial series is

$$
(1+x)^\alpha=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\binom{\alpha}{n}x^n,
\qquad
\binom{\alpha}{n}=\frac{\alpha(\alpha-1)\cdots(\alpha-n+1)}{n!}.
$$

For a [complex number](complex-number.md) $\alpha$, set $\binom\alpha0=1$ and $\binom\alpha n=\alpha(\alpha-1)\cdots(\alpha-n+1)/n!$ for $n\geq1$. The displayed [power series](power-series.md) represents $\exp(\alpha\operatorname{Log}(1+z))$ on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), using the [branch of the complex logarithm](branch-of-the-complex-logarithm.md) with value zero at $1$. It terminates for a [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) $\alpha$. Otherwise the [ratio test](ratio-test.md) gives absolute convergence for $|z|<1$. Termwise [differentiation](differentiation.md) gives $(1+z)F'(z)=\alpha F(z)$ and $F(0)=1$, which determine that [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md). With $\alpha=-1/2$ and argument $-z$, its coefficients are the [normalized central binomial coefficients](normalized-central-binomial-coefficients.md).
