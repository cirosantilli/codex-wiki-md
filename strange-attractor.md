# Strange attractor

↑ **Parent:** [Attractor](attractor.md)

Under the geometric convention, a [strange attractor](strange-attractor.md) is an attracting [invariant set](invariant-set-of-a-measure-preserving-transformation.md) with irregular, typically [fractal geometry](fractal-geometry.md) rather than a smooth point, cycle or [torus](torus.md). Geometry and orbit instability are separate properties. A [strange nonchaotic attractor](strange-nonchaotic-attractor.md) can be geometrically strange without positive exponential separation. Some authors build [chaos](chaos.md) into the term instead, so that convention must be distinguished when using it.
