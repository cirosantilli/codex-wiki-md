# Planetary migration

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary system dynamics](planetary-system-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planetary_migration)

Planetary migration changes a [planet](planet.md)'s orbital semimajor axis through torques from a disc or exchanges of energy and angular momentum with other bodies, often followed by [tidal dissipation](tidal-dissipation.md). Short-period giant planets, eccentric progenitors, resonant architectures, and [stellar obliquity](stellar-obliquity.md) provide complementary constraints rather than one unique migration diagnostic.
