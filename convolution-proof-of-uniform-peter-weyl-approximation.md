# Convolution proof of uniform Peter-Weyl approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Peter-Weyl theorem](peter-weyl-theorem.md)

A continuous symmetric approximate identity on a [compact group](compact-group.md) gives a compact self-adjoint [convolution](convolution.md) operator $T$ on $L^2(G)$ which maps into $C(G)$ and commutes with [left translations](left-and-right-translation-on-a-lie-group.md). Its nonzero [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md) are finite-dimensional invariant spaces of [continuous functions](continuous-function.md), hence consist of [matrix coefficients](matrix-coefficient.md) of finite-dimensional [unitary representations](unitary-representation.md). If $S_N$ projects onto increasing finite sums of these [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md), $S_NTf\to Tf$ in $L^2$, and the bound $\|Th\|_\infty\leq\|k\|_2\|h\|_2$ gives $TS_NTf\to T^2f$ uniformly. Choosing the approximate identity narrow makes $T^2f$ uniformly close to $f$. This supplies uniform approximation without confusing an $L^2$ limit with a uniform limit.
