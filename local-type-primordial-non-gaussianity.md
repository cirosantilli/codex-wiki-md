# Local-type primordial non-Gaussianity

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial non-Gaussianity](primordial-non-gaussianity.md)

A local quadratic departure from a [Gaussian random field](gaussian-random-field.md) is

$$
\varphi(\mathbf x)=\varphi_G(\mathbf x)+f_{\rm NL}\bigl(\varphi_G(\mathbf x)^2-\langle\varphi_G^2\rangle\bigr).
$$

The subtraction removes the homogeneous mean. At leading order, [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md) gives the [primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md) $B_\varphi=2f_{\rm NL}[P_\varphi(k_1)P_\varphi(k_2)+\text{cyclic terms}]$. The numerical convention depends on which potential or [comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md) is used.
