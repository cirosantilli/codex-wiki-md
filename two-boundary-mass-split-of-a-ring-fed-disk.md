# Two-boundary mass split of a ring-fed disk

↑ **Parent:** [General steady viscous flux in a Keplerian disk](general-steady-viscous-flux-in-a-keplerian-disk.md)

For steady injection into a [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md) between two zero-[torque](torque.md) boundaries, [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) gives $\dot M=\dot M_{\rm in}+\dot M_{\rm out}$. [Conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $\dot M h_0=\dot M_{\rm in}h_{\rm in}+\dot M_{\rm out}h_{\rm out}$, where $h(R)=\sqrt{GMR}$. Eliminating the outward mass flux gives the displayed inward fraction. This fraction does not depend on the viscosity law: the two boundary fluxes must carry away the injected mass and angular momentum. Equivalently, continuity of the [viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) joins $\nu\Sigma\sqrt R=\dot M_{\rm in}(\sqrt R-\sqrt{R_{\rm in}})/(3\pi)$ inside the source to $\dot M_{\rm out}(\sqrt{R_{\rm out}}-\sqrt R)/(3\pi)$ outside.
