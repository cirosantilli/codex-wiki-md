# Bouwmeester quantum teleportation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum teleportation](quantum-teleportation.md)

[Dik Bouwmeester](dirk-bouwmeester.md) and colleagues demonstrated selected-outcome photon-polarization [quantum teleportation](quantum-teleportation.md). An ultraviolet pump traversed a nonlinear crystal twice: one passage supplied an entangled pair, the other the input [photon](photon.md) and its trigger partner. The input and one resource [photon](photon.md) interfered at a beam splitter; output-detector coincidences selected their antisymmetric [Bell state](bell-state-split.md). Narrow filters made their coherence time exceed the pump duration, preserving temporal indistinguishability.

The reported pump pulses were $200\,\mathrm{fs}$ long at $76\,\mathrm{MHz}$; $788\,\mathrm{nm}$ [photons](photon.md) filtered to $4\,\mathrm{nm}$ bandwidth had a quoted $520\,\mathrm{fs}$ coherence time. A [polarization](polarization-waves.md) analyzer measured the remote output. Trigger-conditioned fourfold coincidences excluded a double-pair background without subtraction. The selected Bell result had ideal [probability](probability.md) $1/4$; this demonstration did not distinguish and actively correct all four outcomes.[pp. 576–578, Figures 1, 4 and 5: source, filters, Bell analyzer and background control](experimental-quantum-teleportation-by-dik-bouwmeester-and-others.md)
