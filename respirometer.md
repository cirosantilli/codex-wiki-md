# Respirometer

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respirometer)

A [respirometer](respirometer.md) measures gas changes associated with [cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md). In the absorber-based [pea seed respirometry](pea-seed-respirometry.md) apparatus, the changing gas inventory is inferred from a liquid boundary's displacement. A [respirometer](respirometer.md) is therefore a physical measurement coupled to a biological process, not a direct [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) counter.
