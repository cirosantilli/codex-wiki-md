# Block of an Artinian algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

A block of an [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) with a finite decomposition of its identity is a two-sided [direct summand](direct-summand.md) $Ae$ corresponding to a [primitive central idempotent](primitive-central-idempotent.md) $e$. Its identity is $e$, and it cannot be split into two nonzero [algebras](algebra-split.md) by [central idempotents](central-idempotent.md). For a [group algebra](group-algebra.md) of a finite [group](group-split.md), this agrees with a [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md). Orthogonal [primitive central idempotents](primitive-central-idempotent.md) give $A=\bigoplus_iAe_i$.
