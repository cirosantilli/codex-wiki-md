# Self-sorting

↑ **Parent:** [Supramolecular chemistry](supramolecular-chemistry.md)

Selective distribution of components among preferred assemblies from a mixture. Competition, concentration, solvent and [protonation](protonation.md) determine the distribution; a ranking of individual affinities does not imply perfectly pure species.
