# Uniformly magnetized ring with a narrow gap

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic circuit](magnetic-circuit.md)

A rod of magnetic path length $l$ and tangential [magnetization](magnetization.md) $M$ closed by a gap of width $a$ has constant flux in the one-dimensional [magnetic circuit](magnetic-circuit.md) approximation. Equal cross sections and negligible leakage give $H_g=H_r+M$, while absence of free current gives $lH_r+aH_g=0$. Their solution is the displayed pair. Gap fringing or an unspecified cross-section change requires a more detailed field model.
