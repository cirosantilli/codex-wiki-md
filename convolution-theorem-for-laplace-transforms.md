# Convolution theorem for Laplace transforms

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md)

For functions whose [Laplace transforms](laplace-transform.md) converge on a common right half-plane, the causal [convolution](convolution.md) $(f*g)(t)=\int_0^tf(s)g(t-s)ds$ satisfies $\mathcal L(f*g)=\mathcal L(f)\mathcal L(g)$. This converts a transformed boundary forcing into a known time-domain [integral representation](integral-representation.md).
