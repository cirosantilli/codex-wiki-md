# Irreducible representation of a finite abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Complex representation](complex-representation.md)

Every irreducible complex representation of a [finite abelian group](finite-abelian-group.md) is one-dimensional. After making the representation unitary, the commuting representing matrices are simultaneously diagonalizable; any common eigenspace is invariant, so irreducibility leaves only one dimension.
