# Time-dependent vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field](vector-field.md)

A smooth family $Y_t$ assigns a tangent vector at each space-time point. Its [flow maps](flow-map.md) solve $\partial_t\rho_t=Y_t\circ\rho_t$, with $\rho_0$ the identity. For a varying [differential form](differential-form-split.md), differentiation gives $\partial_t(\rho_t^*\alpha_t)=\rho_t^*(\dot\alpha_t+\mathcal L_{Y_t}\alpha_t)$.
