# Trace-adapted orthonormal basis of two-by-two matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md)

For the real two-by-two [matrix](matrix.md) space, the [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md) is $\operatorname{tr}(A^tB)=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}b_{ij}$. The displayed four matrices form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). The first spans the identity direction, the next two span the traceless [symmetric](symmetric-relation.md) matrices, and the last spans the antisymmetric matrices. [Symmetric](symmetric-relation.md) and antisymmetric matrices are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) because transposition changes the sign of their [trace](matrix-trace.md) pairing; pairing with the identity gives the [trace](matrix-trace.md). This [basis](basis.md) therefore displays the [orthogonal direct sum](orthogonal-direct-sum.md) into these three natural pieces.
