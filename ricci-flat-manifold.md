# Ricci-flat manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudo-Riemannian manifold](pseudo-riemannian-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricci-flat_manifold)

A [Ricci-flat manifold](ricci-flat-manifold.md) has vanishing [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md). This is weaker than vanishing [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md): the latter describes complete local flatness. A [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md) supplies a [Lorentzian manifold](lorentzian-manifold.md) that is [Ricci flat](ricci-flat-manifold.md) in its vacuum region but has nonzero [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md).
