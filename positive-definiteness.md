# Positive definiteness

↑ **Parent:** [Positive-definite bilinear form](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md)

The strict positivity of a [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) on every nonzero [vector](vector.md). A real symmetric [matrix](matrix.md) has positive definiteness exactly when its associated [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) does, making it a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md). A [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md) is positive definite when its value on $(v,v)$ is real and strictly positive for every nonzero vector.
