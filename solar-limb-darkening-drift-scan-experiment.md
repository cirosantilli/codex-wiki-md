# Solar limb-darkening drift-scan experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Limb darkening](limb-darkening.md)

A motivating measurement used the McMath solar [optical telescope](optical-telescope.md) at Kitt Peak. The [Sun](sun.md)'s image drifted across a $10\,\mathrm{mm}$-high, $0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$-wide [optical slit](optical-slit.md); a double-pass [spectrograph](spectrograph.md) selected [wavelength](wavelength.md) intervals from $0.0015\,\mathrm{nm}$ wide at $303\,\mathrm{nm}$ to $0.007\,\mathrm{nm}$ at $1099\,\mathrm{nm}$. An EMI 9750 QB [photodetector](photodetector.md) or [silicon](silicon.md) [photodiode](photodiode.md) supplied a digitized signal: $1\,\mathrm{kHz}$ samples were summed into 4096 positions per crossing, including dark and off-limb sky readings. [§2, p. 93, instrumental compilation: slit dimensions, double pass, detectors, sampling and drift-curve layout; p. 113, acknowledgements identifying the telescope](solar-limb-darkening-1986-1990-lambda-lambda-303-to-1099-nm-by-heinz-neckel-and-dietrich-labs.md)

Controls included daily realignment of [optical telescope](optical-telescope.md) mirrors, [wavelength](wavelength.md) settings between [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) insensitive to the rotational [Doppler shift](doppler-effect.md), selection of detectors without detectable [hysteresis](hysteresis.md), and measured electronic nonlinearity corrections below $0.1\%$ of maximum signal. Limb-position calibration combined centres of gravity of the edge [derivative](derivative.md) profiles with almanac semidiameters; the preliminary edge shifts were consistent with [astronomical seeing](astronomical-seeing.md) of approximately $5$ [arcseconds](arcsecond.md) [full width at half maximum](full-width-at-half-maximum.md). [§2, pp. 93–94, precautions (1), (6)–(8); §3.1, p. 94, two-step limb positions and seeing estimate](solar-limb-darkening-1986-1990-lambda-lambda-303-to-1099-nm-by-heinz-neckel-and-dietrich-labs.md)

The decreasing centre-to-limb readout gives a [specific intensity](spectral-radiance.md) profile, constraining stratification of the [photosphere](photosphere.md) under a [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md) model. [Astronomical seeing](astronomical-seeing.md), instrumental [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md), and [solar granulation](solar-granulation.md) limit this inference; brightness alone does not uniquely determine [temperature](temperature-split.md) versus geometric height. These observations need not follow the stipulated [linear limb-darkening law](linear-limb-darkening-law.md).
