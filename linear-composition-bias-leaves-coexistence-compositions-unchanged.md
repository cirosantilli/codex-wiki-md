# Linear composition bias leaves coexistence compositions unchanged

↑ **Parent:** [Common-tangent construction for phase coexistence](common-tangent-construction-for-phase-coexistence.md)

Adding $d\phi$ to a [free-energy density](free-energy-density.md) adds a constant at fixed total [compositional order parameter](compositional-order-parameter.md). In the [common-tangent construction for phase coexistence](common-tangent-construction-for-phase-coexistence.md), it shifts the tangent slope by $d$ while preserving its points of contact. Thus coexistence compositions are unchanged, whereas their [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) shifts. This does not assert unchanged equilibrium composition at a fixed externally imposed [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md).
