# Photon subtraction

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md)

Ideal conditional application of an [annihilation operator](annihilation-operator.md), defined only for positive initial mean [photon number](photon-number.md). Number-dependent weighting means that the conditional mean need not fall. Vacuum input gives the zero vector and therefore no successful conditional state.
