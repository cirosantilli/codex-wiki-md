# Axisymmetric settling box model

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md)

An axisymmetric [gravity-current box model](gravity-current-box-model.md) with no entrainment and negligible suspended-particle volume loss has constant bulk volume $\mathcal V$, uniform height $h$, and uniform particle volume fraction $c$. For an absorbing horizontal bed and downward [settling velocity](settling-velocity.md) $w_s>0$, particle [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $\dot c=-w_sc/h$. A constant front [Froude number](froude-number.md) closes spreading by $\dot R=\operatorname{Fr}\sqrt{g_0ch}$, where $g_0=g(\rho_p-\rho_a)/\rho_a$ is buoyancy per unit volume fraction. This model describes the mixed bulk and its head closure, not the initial inertial blast or a resolved head shape.
