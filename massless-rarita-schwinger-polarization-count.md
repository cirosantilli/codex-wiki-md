# Massless Rarita-Schwinger polarization count

↑ **Parent:** [Rarita-Schwinger field](rarita-schwinger-field.md)

Gauge equivalence $\psi_\mu\sim\psi_\mu+\partial_\mu\epsilon$ permits gamma-trace gauge. The [Rarita-Schwinger field](rarita-schwinger-field.md) equation then gives transversality and a massless [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) for each component. For momentum along the third axis, residual gauge removes the temporal and longitudinal components. The remaining constraint is $\psi_2=\gamma^1\gamma^2\psi_1$, with $\psi_1$ in the two-dimensional kernel of $\not p$. Spinor helicity $\pm1/2$ is paired with vector helicity of the same sign, leaving only $\pm3/2$. A [Majorana spinor](majorana-spinor.md) reality condition identifies antiparticles; a Dirac vector-spinor has separate antiparticle modes.
