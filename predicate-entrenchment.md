# Predicate entrenchment

↑ **Parent:** [Projectibility](projectibility.md)

In [Nelson Goodman](nelson-goodman.md)'s account, the standing a predicate acquires through a history of actual projections. It concerns the use of the predicate in inference, rather than merely the number of past objects satisfying it. Entrenchment is revisable and is not a proof of future reliability.[Chapter IV, sections 2–4](fact-fiction-and-forecast-by-nelson-goodman.md)
