<h1 id="original-and-weak-continuity-of-linear-maps-between-frechet-spaces">Original and weak continuity of linear maps between Fréchet spaces</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Weak topology](weak-topology-split.md)

For [Fréchet spaces](frechet-space.md), the two continuity conditions for a [linear map](linear-map.md) are equivalent. Original continuity makes every target [continuous linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) compose to a source [continuous linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md), proving weak continuity. Conversely, weak continuity makes these compositions continuous in the original source [topology](topology-split.md). They separate target points, so limits in the [graph of a linear operator](graph-of-a-linear-operator.md) remain in the graph. The [closed graph theorem for Fréchet spaces](closed-graph-theorem-for-frechet-spaces.md) finishes the proof.

Without the hypotheses, equal [continuous dual spaces](continuous-dual-space-split.md) do not imply equal [topologies](topology-split.md). The identity from an infinite-dimensional [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) with its [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md) to its norm [topology](topology-split.md) is weak-to-weak continuous but not continuous in those original [topologies](topology-split.md).
