# Sequential probability integral transform

↑ **Parent:** [Probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md)

For correct continuous conditional forecasts, the conditional [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md) is uniform given the entire past. Iterating conditional probabilities makes the transformed sequence independent and uniform. Uniformity and serial checks assess absolute predictive calibration using only the forecast distributions and observations.
