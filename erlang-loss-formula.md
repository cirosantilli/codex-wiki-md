# Erlang loss formula

↑ **Parent:** [Loss network](loss-network.md)

For capacity $C$ and [offered traffic](offered-traffic.md) $a$, the probability of a full single-resource loss system is $E(a,C)=(a^C/C!)/(\sum_{k=0}^Ca^k/k!)$. The stable recursion is $E(a,0)=1$, $E(a,k)=aE(a,k-1)/(k+aE(a,k-1))$.
