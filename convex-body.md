# Convex body

↑ **Parent:** [Convex geometry](convex-geometry.md)

A [convex body](convex-body.md) in $\mathbb R^n$ is a [compact](compact-space.md) [convex set](convex-set-split.md) with nonempty [interior](interior-topology.md) in the ambient [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md). It consequently has positive finite [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). A filled [rectangle](rectangle.md) with positive side [lengths](length.md) and a closed positive-radius [Euclidean disk](disk-mathematics.md) are planar [convex bodies](convex-body.md). A [line segment](line-segment.md) in $\mathbb R^2$ has empty ambient [interior](interior-topology.md) and is not a [convex body](convex-body.md) under this convention. This is the convention used in the [Brunn-Minkowski theorem](brunn-minkowski-theorem.md). [Gardner (2002), introduction](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/jaw/COURSES/520s/523/HO.523.20/Gardner.BullAMS.02.pdf)
