# Symplectic dynamical symmetry in quantum control

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical Lie algebra](dynamical-lie-algebra.md)

For a nonsingular skew-symmetric unitary $J$, if each skew-Hermitian control generator obeys $X^TJ+JX=0$, then so do their real linear combinations and [commutators](commutator.md). Differentiating $U^TJU$ shows that it stays equal to $J$. The reachable group is therefore contained in a unitary conjugate of the [compact symplectic group](compact-symplectic-group.md). This is preservation of an alternating bilinear form, not commutation with $J$.
