# Thermodynamic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermodynamic_potential)

A [state function](state-function.md) used to describe a [thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md) with a chosen set of natural variables. [Internal energy](internal-energy-split.md), [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md), [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md), and [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) use different variables and serve different environmental constraints.
