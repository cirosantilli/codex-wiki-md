# Target-space Noether charges of a string

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu-Goto phase-space action](nambu-goto-phase-space-action.md)

Translations and [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md) give [Noether charges](noether-charge.md) $\mathcal P_m=\int P_m\,d\sigma$ and $\mathcal J_{mn}=\int(X_mP_n-X_nP_m)\,d\sigma$. Their time derivatives are boundary fluxes, which vanish for a [closed string](closed-string.md) or a free-ended [open string](open-string.md).
