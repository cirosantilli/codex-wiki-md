# Rigid-motion-projected thermal covariance of a free filament

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal covariance of an elastic filament](thermal-covariance-of-an-elastic-filament.md)

Free bending has two [zero-energy filament modes](zero-energy-filament-mode.md), so it does not define an unrestricted normalizable thermal height distribution. Fix the mean displacement and linear tilt, or project out that affine kernel. On the positive complement, the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md) gives independent Gaussian mode [variances](variance-split.md) $k_BT/(Ak_n^4)$ for normalized modes. The resulting [covariance](covariance.md) is the bending operator pseudoinverse multiplied by $k_BT$. It depends on how the rigid motion is fixed.
