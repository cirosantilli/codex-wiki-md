# Translation invariance of Lebesgue outer measure

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue outer measure](lebesgue-outer-measure.md)

Translating every [interval](interval-mathematics.md) of a countable cover of $A\subseteq\mathbb R$ by $a$ produces a cover of $A+a$ with the same lengths. Taking the [infimum](infimum.md) of cover lengths gives $m^*(A+a)\leq m^*(A)$. Translating back by $-a$ gives the reverse inequality. This proves [translation invariance](translation-invariance.md) for [Lebesgue outer measure](lebesgue-outer-measure.md) on every [subset](subset.md), without first assuming the set is [Lebesgue measurable](lebesgue-measurable-set.md).
