<h1 id="finite-dimensional-interpolation-form-of-goldstine-s-theorem">Finite-dimensional interpolation form of Goldstine's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Goldstine theorem](goldstine-theorem.md)

For a finite-dimensional [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $E\subseteq X^*$ and $x^{**}\in B_{X^{**}}$, exact interpolation on $E$ is possible by an $x\in X$ of [norm](norm.md) less than $1+\varepsilon$. The restriction map $X\to E^*$ is onto and open. Separation of the image of the radius-$1+\varepsilon$ open ball would contradict $|x^{**}(e)|\leq\|e\|$. Scaling the interpolant slightly back into the closed [unit ball](unit-ball.md) proves the [Goldstine theorem](goldstine-theorem.md).
