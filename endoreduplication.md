# Endoreduplication

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)

Replication of nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) without a corresponding normal mitotic division, producing increased [genome](genome.md) copy number. It illustrates that replication and cell division can be developmentally uncoupled.
