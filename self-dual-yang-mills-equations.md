# Self-dual Yang-Mills equations

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills instanton](yang-mills-instanton.md)

The self-dual [Yang-Mills equations](yang-mills-equations.md) require a [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) two-form to have positive [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) eigenvalue on an oriented Riemannian four-manifold. By the [Bianchi identity](bianchi-identity.md), they imply the full second-order [Yang-Mills equations](yang-mills-equations.md). Smooth finite-action solutions saturate the [Yang-Mills instanton Bogomolny bound](yang-mills-instanton-bogomolny-bound.md). Reversing orientation exchanges this equation with the [Anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations](anti-self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md).
