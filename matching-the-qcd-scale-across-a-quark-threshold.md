# Matching the QCD scale across a quark threshold

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop running of the strong coupling](one-loop-running-of-the-strong-coupling.md)

At a heavy-quark mass $m$, leading-order continuity gives $\beta_- \ln(m/\Lambda_-)=\beta_+\ln(m/\Lambda_+)$. Therefore $\boxed{\Lambda_+=m(\Lambda_-/m)^{\beta_-/\beta_+}}$. This is a change of the [strong-coupling scale](strong-coupling-scale.md) parametrization across a change of active flavour number, not a discontinuity of the matched [QCD coupling](strong-coupling-constant.md). For four to five active flavours in $SU(3)$, $\beta_-=25/3$ and $\beta_+=23/3$, hence $\Lambda_5=\Lambda_4(m_b/\Lambda_4)^{-2/23}$.
