# Age ordering of dust universes at fixed Hubble parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Age of a dust universe without a cosmological constant](age-of-a-dust-universe-without-a-cosmological-constant.md)

For an expanding [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) universe with zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), fixed current [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) $H_0$ and current matter [cosmological density parameter](cosmological-density-parameter.md) $\Omega>0$, the [age of a dust universe without a cosmological constant](age-of-a-dust-universe-without-a-cosmological-constant.md) is $H_0t_0=\int_0^1\sqrt{x}/\sqrt{\Omega+(1-\Omega)x}\,dx$. For every $0<x<1$ the integrand decreases strictly as $\Omega$ increases. Thus open models with $0<\Omega<1$ are older than the flat model, and closed expanding models with $\Omega>1$ are younger. The flat value is $2/3$ and the empty [Milne universe](milne-model.md) limit is $1$. Specifying what is held fixed matters: at fixed present [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) and [energy density](energy-density.md), the integral $t_0=\int_0^{a_0}da/\sqrt{C/a-k}$ instead increases with $k$, provided all three models reach $a_0$ on their expanding branches.
