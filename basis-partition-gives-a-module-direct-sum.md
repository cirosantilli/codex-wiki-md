# Basis partition gives a module direct sum

↑ **Parent:** [Free module](free-module.md)

Partition a [basis of a module](basis-of-a-module.md) $E$ into disjoint subsets $E',E''$. Splitting the finite expression of each element gives $M=\langle E'\rangle+\langle E''\rangle$. An element in both generated [submodules](submodule.md) would give, by subtraction, a relation among the disjoint parts of $E$; [linear independence in a module](linear-independence-in-a-module.md) forces every coefficient to vanish. The intersection is zero, proving the [direct sum of modules](direct-sum-of-modules.md) decomposition. Each part is itself a [basis of a module](basis-of-a-module.md) for the [submodule](submodule.md) it generates.
