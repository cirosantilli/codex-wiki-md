# Weak topology of a CW complex

↑ **Parent:** [CW complex](cw-complex.md)

The weak topology of a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) is determined by its closed cells: a subset is closed if and only if its inverse image under every characteristic map from a closed cell is closed. A map out of the complex is continuous when its restrictions along those characteristic maps are continuous. This is distinct from the [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md) of a vector space. Taking the product with a locally finite [CW complex](cw-complex.md), such as a closed interval with its finite cell structure, retains the corresponding CW weak topology.
