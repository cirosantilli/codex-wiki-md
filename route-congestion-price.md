# Route congestion price

↑ **Parent:** [Resource congestion price](resource-congestion-price.md)

The total price seen by a route is the sum of [resource congestion prices](resource-congestion-price.md) on its links. With [weighted logarithmic utility](weighted-logarithmic-utility.md) $w_r\log x_r$, maximizing utility minus linear expenditure gives the demand $x_r=w_r/p_r$. This relation links user decisions to [multiplicative resource-price dynamics](multiplicative-resource-price-dynamics.md).
