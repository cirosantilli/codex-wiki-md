# Phosphodegron

↑ **Parent:** [Degron](degron.md)

A [degron](degron.md) whose recognition depends on [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md). Phosphorylation can thereby couple protein turnover to a regulatory signal; not every phosphorylated motif is a degradation signal.
