# Scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_field)

A scalar field assigns one scalar to each point of [spacetime](spacetime.md). Under a change of coordinates, its value at a physical point is unchanged.
