# Scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_field)

A [scalar field](scalar-field.md) assigns one scalar to each point of a specified space, such as [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) or [spacetime](spacetime.md). For example, $F(x,y)=x^2+2y^2$ is a [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) on a plane, and its change along a moving observation is given by the [derivative along a curve](derivative-along-a-curve.md). Under a change of coordinates, a scalar field's value at the same physical point is unchanged.
