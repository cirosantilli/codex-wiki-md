# Harmonic capacity from infinity in the upper half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md)

This capacity measures the asymptotic probability that [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) hits a [compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md) before the real axis. It scales linearly with length and is distinct from [half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md). A vertical slit of height $h$ has capacity $2h$; a half-disc of radius $r$ has capacity $4r$. Monotonicity of Brownian hitting probabilities gives $\operatorname{cap}(K)\le4\operatorname{rad}(K)$, where the radius is that of the least real-centred enclosing half-disc. For finite slit hulls the capacity is the length of the image of the two-sided absorbing boundary under the [mapping-out function](mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md).
