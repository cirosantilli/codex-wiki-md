# Harmonic-oscillator recoil matrix element

↑ **Parent:** [Trapped-ion internal and motional states](trapped-ion-internal-and-motional-states.md)

For normalized oscillator states and $x=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}(a+a^\dagger)$, define $\eta=k\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}$. Normal ordering the displacement operator gives the displayed [matrix element](matrix-element.md). A sudden [momentum](momentum-split.md) kick therefore creates a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) of oscillator occupations with mean $\eta^2$. Its mean added motional [energy](energy-split.md) is $\eta^2\hbar\omega=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$, although most events retain $n=0$ when $\eta\ll1$.
