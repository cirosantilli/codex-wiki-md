# Optic axis

↑ **Parent:** [Uniaxial optical medium](uniaxial-optical-medium.md)

The symmetry direction of a [uniaxial optical medium](uniaxial-optical-medium.md). For a [wavevector](wavevector.md) along it, the two [refractive indices](refractive-index.md) coincide: there is no [birefringence](birefringence.md) for that propagation direction, even though $n_e\ne n_o$.
