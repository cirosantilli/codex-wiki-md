# S-matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S-matrix)

The [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) maps incoming asymptotic states to outgoing asymptotic states. Its connected transition matrix elements contain an overall energy-momentum [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) and an invariant [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md). In [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md), the [LSZ reduction formula](lsz-reduction-formula.md) obtains these elements from amputated field correlators.
