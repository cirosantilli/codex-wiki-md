# Brownian motion conditioned to exit at a boundary point

↑ **Parent:** [Doob h-transform](doob-h-transform.md)

Killed [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) conditioned to exit at a smooth boundary point $a$ is the [Doob h-transform](doob-h-transform.md) by its positive [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md), normalized at the starting point. Its generator is $\tfrac12\Delta+\nabla\log h\cdot\nabla$. Conditioning on shrinking boundary arcs yields the same localized laws and specifies a canonical version of an otherwise probability-zero conditioning event.
