# Matter-era forced radiation response

↑ **Parent:** [Subhorizon radiation acoustic oscillations](subhorizon-radiation-acoustic-oscillations.md)

Let $\delta_c=C_g\tau^2+C_d\tau^{-3}$ be the [matter-era growing and decaying density modes](matter-era-growing-and-decaying-density-modes.md), with radiation neglected in the background gravity. The radiation [density contrast](density-contrast.md) is

$$
\delta_r=C_1\cos(\omega\tau)+C_2\sin(\omega\tau)+\frac{8C_g}{3\omega^2}
+\frac{16C_d}{\omega}\int_{\tau_*}^{\tau}s^{-5}\sin[\omega(\tau-s)]\,ds.
$$

The formula follows by the [Green's function](green-s-function.md) of the forced [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md). For $k\tau\gg1$, the slowly varying particular response to the decaying mode begins at $16C_d\tau^{-5}/\omega^2$; lower-endpoint oscillations are absorbed into $C_1,C_2$. The growing mode drives a constant radiation offset, while [cold dark matter](cold-dark-matter.md) continues growing as $\tau^2$.
