# Selberg least-common-multiple weights

↑ **Parent:** [Selberg sieve diagonalization](selberg-sieve-diagonalization.md)

For real coefficients $\rho_1=1$ supported on permitted [divisors](divisor.md) up to $L$, put $\lambda_d=\sum_{[u,v]=d}\rho_u\rho_v$. Then $\sum_{d\mid n}\lambda_d=(\sum_{d\mid n}\rho_d)^2$ is an upper bound for the [indicator function](indicator-function.md) of numbers free of the forbidden [prime factors](prime-factor.md). Its support has $d\leq L^2$. If the coefficients vanish on non-[squarefree integers](squarefree-integer.md) and satisfy $|\rho_d|\leq1$, then $|\lambda_d|\leq3^{\omega(d)}$: each [prime factor](prime-factor.md) of $d$ occurs in $u$, in $v$, or in both.
