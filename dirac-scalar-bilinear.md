# Dirac scalar bilinear

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md)

The [Dirac scalar bilinear](dirac-scalar-bilinear.md) is invariant under the simultaneous spinor and adjoint transformations because $\bar\Psi'\Psi'=\bar\Psi S^{-1}S\Psi$. It is the [mass term](mass-term.md) of the [Dirac action](dirac-action.md). At fixed coordinates it transforms as a [scalar field](scalar-field.md), with an orbital transport term and no spin term.
