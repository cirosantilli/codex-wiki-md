# Bounded convex functions are locally Lipschitz on an open ball

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

Let a real [convex function](convex-function.md) satisfy $|f|\leq M$ on the open unit ball of a [normed space](normed-vector-space.md). For $4r=1-\|x\|$, points $u,v\in B(x,r)$ satisfy $|f(u)-f(v)|\leq(M/r)\|u-v\|$. Extend the segment from $u$ through $v$ to length $2r$; its endpoint still lies in the domain, and [convexity](convex-function.md) bounds the difference by $2M\|u-v\|/(2r)$. Reverse $u,v$ for the opposite inequality. Unlike finite-dimensional local continuity theorems, this argument applies in arbitrary dimension because boundedness on a full neighborhood is assumed.
