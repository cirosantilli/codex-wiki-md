# Heegaard diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Heegaard surface](heegaard-surface.md)

A Heegaard diagram marks the boundaries of compressing disks from the two sides of a [Heegaard surface](heegaard-surface.md). Attaching [two-handles](two-handle.md) along the two collections, and capping resulting spherical boundary components, reconstructs the [three-manifold](3-manifold.md).
