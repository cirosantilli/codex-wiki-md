# Radial profile

↑ **Parent:** [Radial function](radial-function.md)

A radial profile is a one-variable function $\bar u$ representing a [radial function](radial-function.md) $u$ by $u(x)=\bar u(|x|)$. On an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) $a<|x|<b$, with $a>0$, the [spherical coordinate system](spherical-coordinate-system.md) identifies its [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) with the weighted profile [norm](norm.md) through $\int|u|^p=|S^{n-1}|\int_a^b|\bar u(r)|^p r^{n-1}\,dr$. Thus equality of two [radial functions](radial-function.md) [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) identifies their profiles [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) in the ordinary one-dimensional sense.
