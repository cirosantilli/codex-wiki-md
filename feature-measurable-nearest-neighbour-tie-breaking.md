# Feature-measurable nearest-neighbour tie-breaking

↑ **Parent:** [K-nearest neighbors algorithm](k-nearest-neighbors-algorithm.md)

For a fixed query, order training features by their distances and break equal distances by a rule depending only on features, such as original index. If auxiliary uniforms are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of the features, the uniform selected by the resulting index remains uniform conditional on all features: sum over the possible selected indices. Label-dependent tie-breaking can invalidate [Bernoulli coupling by a shared uniform random variable](bernoulli-coupling-by-a-shared-uniform-random-variable.md) and the usual [one-nearest-neighbour asymptotic risk](one-nearest-neighbour-asymptotic-risk.md).
