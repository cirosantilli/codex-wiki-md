# Radon-Nikodym density martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Radon-Nikodym derivative](radon-nikodym-derivative.md)

For an absolutely continuous change of probability measure, restricting its [Radon-Nikodym derivative](radon-nikodym-derivative.md) to the current sigma-algebra gives this nonnegative uniformly integrable [martingale](martingale-split.md). If $Z_T>0$ almost surely, the measures are equivalent on $\mathcal F_T$. This finite-horizon equivalence need not imply equivalence on a larger sigma-algebra.
