# Finite-energy property (percolation)

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation theory](percolation-theory.md)

A [percolation](percolation-theory.md) measure has the finite-energy property if, conditional on all exterior states, every prescribed state of any finite collection of sites or edges has positive [probability](probability.md) almost surely. This permits finite local modifications within events of positive probability. It is a hypothesis on the measure and does not follow merely from invariance under graph symmetries.
