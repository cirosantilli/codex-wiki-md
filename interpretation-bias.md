# Interpretation bias

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive bias](cognitive-bias.md)

A systematic tendency to resolve ambiguous information in a particular direction, such as threat. Task performance, current [anxiety](anxiety.md) and durable clinical vulnerability are distinct variables.[Journal of Abnormal Psychology 109, 602–615; five experiments, ambiguity training, active meaning generation and state anxiety](induced-emotional-interpretation-bias-and-anxiety-by-andrew-mathews-and-bundy-mackintosh.md)
