# Nematic twist

↑ **Parent:** [Distortion free energy density](distortion-free-energy-density.md)

[Nematic twist](nematic-twist.md) rotates the [nematic director](nematic-director.md) around an axis perpendicular to it. The helix $\mathbf n=(\cos qz,\sin qz,0)$ has zero [nematic splay](nematic-splay.md) and [nematic bend](nematic-bend.md), and twist density $K_2q^2/2$.
