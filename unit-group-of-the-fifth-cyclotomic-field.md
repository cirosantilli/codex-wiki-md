# Unit group of the fifth cyclotomic field

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet's unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md)

For a primitive fifth root of unity $\zeta$,

$$
\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q(\zeta)}^\times
=\left\{\mathord\pm\zeta^a
\left(\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\right)^b:a,b\in\mathbb Z\right\}
\cong C_{10}\times\mathbb Z.
$$
