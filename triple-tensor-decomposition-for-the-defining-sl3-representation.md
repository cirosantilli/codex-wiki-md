# Triple tensor decomposition for the defining sl3 representation

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental representations of sl3](fundamental-representations-of-sl3.md)

For the defining representation $V=V(\omega_1)$ of $\mathfrak{sl}_3$,

$$
V\otimes V\otimes V^*
\cong
V(2\omega_1+\omega_2)\oplus
V(2\omega_2)\oplus
V(\omega_1)^{\oplus2}.
$$
