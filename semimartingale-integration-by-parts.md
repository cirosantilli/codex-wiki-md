# Semimartingale integration by parts

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md)

For càdlàg semimartingales, the product rule uses left-limit integrands and quadratic covariation including jump products. For continuous semimartingales it reduces to the [Itô product rule](ito-product-rule.md) without left-limit notation. This rule permits an induction proof of the Itô formula for polynomial functions.
