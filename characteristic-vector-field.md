# Characteristic vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic curve](characteristic-curve.md)

For $au_x+bu_y=c$, the projected [vector field](vector-field.md) $(a,b)$ has the [characteristic projections](characteristic-projection.md) as its integral curves. The lifted [vector field](vector-field.md) is $(a,b,c)$ in $(x,y,u)$ coordinates. A vanishing projected [vector field](vector-field.md) can obstruct a [classical solution](classical-solution.md) even when the lifted [vector field](vector-field.md) does not vanish.
