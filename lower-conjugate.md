# Lower conjugate

↑ **Parent:** [Concave Legendre dual](concave-legendre-dual.md)

The [lower conjugate](lower-conjugate.md) uses an infimum, unlike the supremum defining a [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md). For any $F$, $F_*(\tau)=-(-F)^*(-\tau)$ and $F(e)\le\tau:e-F_*(\tau)$. The inequality is informative only when the [lower conjugate](lower-conjugate.md) is finite. If the infimum is attained uniquely and the dual is differentiable, $DF_*(\tau)$ is its minimizing argument. This sign convention is useful for upper comparison-energy bounds.
