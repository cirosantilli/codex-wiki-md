# Cosine-potential gap in a quantum ring

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic flux spectrum of a quantum ring](magnetic-flux-spectrum-of-a-quantum-ring.md)

In the [plane wave](plane-wave.md) basis of a ring, $V(x)=-\lambda\cos(2\pi x/L)$ has [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) $V_{mn}=-(\lambda/2)(\delta_{m,n+1}+\delta_{m,n-1})$. Near $\alpha=1/2$, [degenerate perturbation theory](degenerate-perturbation-theory.md) on $n=0,1$ gives

$$
E_\pm=E_L[(\alpha-1/2)^2+1/4]\pm\sqrt{E_L^2(\alpha-1/2)^2+\lambda^2/4},
$$

up to corrections from remote states. Thus the [avoided crossing](avoided-crossing.md) gap is $|\lambda|$ to first order when $|\lambda|\ll E_L$.

For $|\lambda|\gg E_L$, a [parabolic approximation](parabolic-approximation.md) about a potential minimum instead gives a local [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) spacing $\sqrt{2|\lambda|E_L}$. A local [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) removes the flux from this approximation. Actual ring levels retain exponentially small flux dependence through [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md), while nonquadratic corrections to their mean energies are algebraic in $\sqrt{E_L/|\lambda|}$.
