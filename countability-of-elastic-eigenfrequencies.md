# Countability of elastic eigenfrequencies

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

Distinct squared traction-free [eigenfrequencies](eigenfrequency.md) in a bounded lossless elastic body have [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) in $L^2(\mathcal D,\rho\,dV)^3$, by the [Betti identity for elastodynamic fields](betti-identity-for-elastodynamic-fields.md). Normalize one field from each [eigenspace](eigenspace.md). This is an [orthonormal set](orthonormal-set.md) in a [separable Hilbert space](separable-hilbert-space.md), so it is countable: disjoint balls of radius less than $1/\sqrt2$ around its unit vectors must contain distinct points of a countable dense set. Positive and negative [frequency](frequency.md) partners add at most a factor of two. This argument proves countability without requiring a separate compact-resolvent proof.
