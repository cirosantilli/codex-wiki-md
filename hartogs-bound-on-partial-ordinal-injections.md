# Hartogs bound on partial ordinal injections

↑ **Parent:** [Hartogs number](hartogs-number.md)

Every [injection](injective-function.md) from an [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) into a [set](set-split.md) $X$ has domain strictly below the [Hartogs number](hartogs-number.md) $h(X)$. Therefore all partial [injections](injective-function.md) with [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) domain and range in $X$ form a [set](set-split.md) of [graphs](graph-of-a-function.md) contained in $\mathcal P(h(X)\times X)$. Under extension, the [union](set-union.md) of a [chain](chain-in-a-partial-order.md) remains a partial [injection](injective-function.md) of this kind. [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) then supplies a maximal one, and extending at an unused range element shows that it must be a [bijection](bijection.md) onto $X$. This gives the [well-ordering theorem](well-ordering-theorem.md) with the set-sizedness of the poset made explicit.
