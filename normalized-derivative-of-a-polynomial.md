# Normalized derivative of a polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Siegel's lemma](siegel-s-lemma.md)

The $j$th normalized derivative is

$$
D_jP(X)=\frac1{j!}\frac{d^jP}{dX^j}(X).
$$

If $P(X)=\sum_ra_rX^r$ has integer coefficients, then $D_jP(X)=\sum_{r\geq j}\binom rj a_rX^{r-j}$ also has integer coefficients. For $\deg P\leq n$, its [naive polynomial height](naive-polynomial-height.md) is at most $2^nH(P)$.
