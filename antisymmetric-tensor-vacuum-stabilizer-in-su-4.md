<h1 id="antisymmetric-tensor-vacuum-stabilizer-in-su-4">Antisymmetric tensor vacuum stabilizer in SU(4)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Two-index antisymmetric representation](two-index-antisymmetric-representation.md)

For a complex [antisymmetric tensor](antisymmetric-tensor.md) $A\in\wedge^2\mathbb C^4$, the [SU(4)](su-4-group.md) action is $A\mapsto UAU^T$. The [unitary skew-diagonalization of an antisymmetric matrix](unitary-skew-diagonalization-of-an-antisymmetric-matrix.md) gives $A=\operatorname{diag}(a\epsilon,b\epsilon)$ in suitable coordinates, where $\epsilon=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$. If $|a|\ne|b|$, preserving $AA^\dagger$ forces a stabilizing [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) to preserve the two coordinate planes. The identity $V\epsilon V^T=(\det V)\epsilon$ then forces both two-by-two blocks to have [determinant](determinant.md) one, using $\det U=1$ also when one of $a,b$ vanishes. The exact [stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md) is $SU(2)\times SU(2)$. If $|a|=|b|\ne0$, the [stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md) is the larger [compact symplectic group](compact-symplectic-group.md) $USp(4)$. If $a=b=0$, it is all of $SU(4)$. The [real structure of the SU4 exterior square](real-structure-of-the-su4-exterior-square.md) imposes $b=\bar a$ in this form, so a nonzero real six-dimensional field has the larger symplectic [stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md), whereas unequal paired [singular values](singular-value.md) are generic for a complex six-dimensional field.
