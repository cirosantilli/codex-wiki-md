# Optical fiber cladding

↑ **Parent:** [Optical fiber](optical-fiber.md)

The optical material surrounding the [optical fiber core](optical-fiber-core.md). In a conventional [step-index optical fiber](step-index-optical-fiber.md), its lower [refractive index](refractive-index.md) permits [total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md); it is distinct from the protective coating.
