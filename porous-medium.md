# Porous medium

↑ **Parent:** [Porous-media flow](porous-media-flow-split.md)

A porous medium is a solid framework containing connected voids in which a [fluid](fluid.md) can reside and flow. Its [porosity](porosity.md) is the void fraction; its [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) measures the ease of pressure-driven flow through the connected pores. The averaged [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) is measured per total cross-sectional area, whereas the pore velocity is that flux divided by porosity. Not every material with voids has connected permeable pores.
