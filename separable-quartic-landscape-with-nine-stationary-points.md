# Separable quartic landscape with nine stationary points

↑ **Parent:** [Critical point](critical-point.md)

The [gradient](gradient.md) of this [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) vanishes exactly when $x=0,\pm\sqrt{a/2}$ and $y=0,\pm\sqrt{b/2}$, giving nine [stationary points](stationary-point.md). Its diagonal [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) has entries $12x^2-2a$ and $2b-12y^2$. The [second-derivative test](second-derivative-test.md) therefore gives two strict [local maxima](local-maximum.md) at $(0,\pm\sqrt{b/2})$, with value $b^2/4$, and two strict [local minima](local-minimum.md) at $(\pm\sqrt{a/2},0)$, with value $-a^2/4$. The origin and the four points $(\pm\sqrt{a/2},\pm\sqrt{b/2})$ are [saddle points of a scalar function](saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md), with values zero and $(b^2-a^2)/4$, respectively. Every [stationary point](stationary-point.md) is nondegenerate.

Completing the squares organizes the [contour plot](contour-plot.md):

$$
F=\frac{b^2-a^2}{4}+\left(x^2-\frac a2\right)^2-\left(y^2-\frac b2\right)^2.
$$

The [level set](level-set.md) through the four outer [saddle points of a scalar function](saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md) factors as

$$
F-\frac{b^2-a^2}{4}=\left(x^2-y^2+\frac{b-a}{2}\right)\left(x^2+y^2-\frac{a+b}{2}\right).
$$

It is a [circle](circle.md) together with a [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) when $a\ne b$; for $a=b$, the latter factor's complementary zero set is the pair of lines $y=\pm x$. Neither the [local maxima](local-maximum.md) nor the [local minima](local-minimum.md) are global, since the restrictions to the two coordinate axes are unbounded in opposite directions. The case $a=1$, $b=4$ gives four outer saddle values $15/4$ and an exact circle-hyperbola contour crossing at all four outer [saddle points of a scalar function](saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md).
