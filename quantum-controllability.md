# Quantum controllability

↑ **Parent:** [Controllability](controllability.md)

[Quantum controllability](quantum-controllability.md) concerns the transformations reachable under specified quantum [control inputs](control-input.md). [Unitary operator controllability](unitary-operator-controllability.md) concerns entire propagators; [density operator controllability](density-operator-controllability.md) concerns conjugation on every spectral orbit; [pure-state controllability](pure-state-controllability.md) concerns normalized state vectors or their physical rays. These notions need not coincide.
