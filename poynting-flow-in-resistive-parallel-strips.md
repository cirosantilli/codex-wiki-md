# Poynting flow in resistive parallel strips

↑ **Parent:** [Static Poynting flow](static-poynting-flow.md)

For a resistive upper strip at $z=d$ with $V(x)=V_0-IRx/l$ and an ideal lower strip at zero potential, the narrow-gap approximation gives $\phi=zV(x)/d$ and $\mathbf H=(0,I/a,0)$. The [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) has both an axial flux and an upward flux into the lossy conductor. Axial [power](power.md) decreases by $I^2R$ along the line, exactly equal to the integrated upper-surface flux. The ideal lower conductor receives no steady dissipation.
