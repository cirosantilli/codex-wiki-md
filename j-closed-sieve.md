# J-closed sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Sieve (category theory)](sieve-category-theory.md)

A sieve is J-closed when membership is local with respect to the [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md) $J$. Pullback preserves J-closedness. Their presheaf is the [subobject classifier](subobject-classifier.md) of the sheaf topos: a section of a sheaf is sent to the sieve of arrows on which it belongs to the chosen subsheaf.
