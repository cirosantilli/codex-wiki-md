# Modular Specht module

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

Over a field of characteristic $p$, the invariant tabloid form on $S^\lambda$ may be degenerate. Its radical is $S^\lambda\cap(S^\lambda)^\perp$.
