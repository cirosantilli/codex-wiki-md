# Adjacent products of independent Gaussian variables

↑ **Parent:** [Uncorrelated random variables](uncorrelated-random-variables.md)

For independent [standard normal random variables](standard-normal-random-variable.md) $\eta_1,\ldots,\eta_n$, define the displayed errors for $i\ge2$. Each has [mean](expected-value-split.md) zero and [variance](variance-split.md) one. Nonadjacent errors depend on disjoint sets of independent variables. Adjacent [covariances](covariance.md) vanish because their product contains an independent centered factor. Thus their [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) is $I_n$.

They are nevertheless dependent: the [Gaussian fourth moment](gaussian-fourth-moment.md) gives $\mathbb E[\epsilon_i^2\epsilon_{i+1}^2]=3$, and hence $\operatorname{Cov}(\epsilon_i^2,\epsilon_{i+1}^2)=2$. Constant [variance](variance-split.md) and zero pairwise [covariance](covariance.md) can conceal dependence in magnitudes. This is useful when interpreting a [residual plot](residual-plot.md) in [linear regression](linear-regression-split.md).
