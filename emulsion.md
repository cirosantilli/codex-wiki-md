# Emulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

A dispersion of droplets of one liquid in another immiscible liquid. Its interfacial [energy](energy-split.md) and kinetic persistence depend on droplet size, [interfacial tension](interfacial-tension.md) and stabilizing species.
