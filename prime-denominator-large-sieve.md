# Prime-denominator large sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Local-multiplicity large sieve](local-multiplicity-large-sieve.md)

When $N\leq P$,

$$
\sum_{\substack{P\leq p\leq2P\\p\text{ prime}}}\sum_{a=1}^{p-1}\left|\sum_{M<n\leq M+N}a_ne(an/p)\right|^2\ll\frac{P^2}{\log P}\sum|a_n|^2.
$$

An arc of length $1/P$ contains at most three points of each grid of denominator $p\leq2P$. The [Chebyshev estimate](chebyshev-estimate.md) gives $O(P/\log P)$ such [primes](prime-number.md). Apply the [local-multiplicity large sieve](local-multiplicity-large-sieve.md). Using only the separation of distinct [reduced fractions](reduced-fraction.md) gives the weaker $O(P^2)\sum|a_n|^2$.
