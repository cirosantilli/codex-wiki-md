# Hyperbolic three-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

A complete hyperbolic three-manifold is a complete [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) of dimension three with constant [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) $-1$. Its [universal cover](universal-cover.md) is [hyperbolic space](hyperbolic-space.md) $\mathbb H^3$ and its deck group acts freely and discretely by [Riemannian isometries](riemannian-isometry.md). In the orientable case it is a torsion-free [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md).
