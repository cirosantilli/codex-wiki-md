# Isostatic depth partition of an interfacial current

↑ **Parent:** [Interfacial viscous gravity current](interfacial-viscous-gravity-current.md)

[Hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) continuity across both interfaces fixes the partition of current thickness. The modified current pressure is $\Delta\rho gh$, with $\Delta\rho=(\rho_0-\rho_1)(\rho_2-\rho_0)/(\rho_2-\rho_1)$. The equivalent radial [body force](body-force.md) is $-\Delta\rho g h_r\mathbf e_r$.
