<h1 id="jorgensen-s-inequality">Jørgensen's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jørgensen's_inequality)

For a non-elementary [discrete subgroup](discrete-subgroup.md) generated by $A,B\in\operatorname{SL}(2,\mathbb C)$, the displayed inequality is a necessary trace constraint. Failure of it proves nondiscreteness when non-elementarity has independently been checked. It is not a sufficient condition for discreteness and must not be applied to an elementary pair without its hypothesis.
