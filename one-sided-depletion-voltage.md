# One-sided depletion voltage

↑ **Parent:** [Depletion region](depletion-region.md)

For a uniformly doped [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md) whose depleted thickness $d$ lies almost entirely on the donor side, [Poisson's equation](poisson-s-equation.md) gives $dE/dx=eN_D/\epsilon$. The minimum field obeys $E(d)=0$. Integrating the triangular [electric field](electric-field-split.md) profile gives $V_{\mathrm{dep}}=eN_Dd^2/(2\epsilon)$. The external reverse bias is this total voltage minus the built-in [junction potential](built-in-potential.md).
