# Isoperimetric constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md)

An isoperimetric constraint fixes the value of an [integral](integral-split.md) while optimizing another integral over functions. A constant [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) incorporates the constraint into the variational integrand, and the resulting [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) must be supplemented by the appropriate [natural boundary conditions for a free endpoint](natural-boundary-conditions-for-a-free-endpoint.md). The name includes integral constraints more general than prescribed geometric length.
