# Magnetic domain wall

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic domain](magnetic-domain.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_wall_(magnetism))

A [magnetic domain wall](magnetic-domain-wall.md) separates differently oriented [magnetic domains](magnetic-domain.md). Its motion changes their [volumes](volume.md) and average [magnetization](magnetization.md). It is a magnetic [domain wall](domain-wall.md).

An original two-[magnetic domain](magnetic-domain.md) model makes the collective change explicit. Suppose opposite regions have [magnetizations](magnetization.md) $+M_s$ and $-M_s$ along the measuring axis, where $M_s$ is the local [saturation magnetization](saturation-magnetization.md). If a [magnetic domain wall](magnetic-domain-wall.md) sweeps [volume](volume.md) $\Delta V$ from the negative region into the positive one, in a specimen of fixed [volume](volume.md) $V$, then

$$
\Delta m=2M_s\Delta V,\qquad
\Delta M=\frac{2M_s\Delta V}{V}.
$$

The factor two comes from replacing $-M_s$ by $+M_s$. The local magnitude $M_s$ stays fixed while the average [magnetization](magnetization.md) changes; the event size depends on the swept [volume](volume.md), rather than a universal single-[spin](spin.md) increment.
