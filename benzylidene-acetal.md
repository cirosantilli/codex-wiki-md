# Benzylidene acetal

↑ **Parent:** [Acetal](acetal.md)

An [acetal](acetal.md) formed with [benzaldehyde](benzaldehyde.md), containing a Ph–CH unit attached to two oxygens. It can protect a diol and is acid-labile.
