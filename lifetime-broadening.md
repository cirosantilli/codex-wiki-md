# Lifetime broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line](spectral-line.md)

In the absence of additional dephasing, a transition whose excited-state population has lifetime $T_1$ has a field-coherence decay time $2T_1$. Its [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md) has angular-frequency half-width $1/(2T_1)$ and full width $1/T_1$. Population decay time, amplitude decay time and linewidth conventions must therefore be distinguished. Extra dephasing shortens the [optical coherence time](optical-coherence-time.md) without necessarily changing the population lifetime.
