# Zeros and poles

↑ **Parent:** [Isolated singularity](isolated-singularity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeros_and_poles)

[Zeros and poles](zeros-and-poles.md) describe the positive and negative local orders of a meromorphic function. Locally $f(z)=(z-a)^m g(z)$ with $g$ holomorphic and nonzero at $a$: $m>0$ is a zero, $m<0$ is a pole, and $m=0$ is a regular nonzero value. A [simple pole](simple-pole.md) has $m=-1$, and the [order of vanishing](order-of-vanishing.md) records $m$.
