# Prime-power fraction decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md)

For a positive integer $b=\prod_i p_i^{n_i}$, every rational number $a/b$ has a unique representation $a/b=n+\sum_iq_i/p_i^{n_i}$ with $n\in\mathbb Z$ and $0\leq q_i<p_i^{n_i}$. With $B_i=b/p_i^{n_i}$, the coefficients satisfy $q_i\equiv aB_i^{-1}\pmod{p_i^{n_i}}$. Their existence and uniqueness use [modular multiplicative inverses](modular-multiplicative-inverse.md) and the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md).
