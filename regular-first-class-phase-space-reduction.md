# Regular first-class phase-space reduction

↑ **Parent:** [First-class constraint](first-class-constraint.md)

For $n$ independent regular [first-class constraints](first-class-constraint.md) in a $2N$-dimensional phase space, imposing the equations removes $n$ dimensions and quotienting independent gauge orbits removes another $n$. Reducibility, singular strata or vanishing gauge directions can invalidate that elementary count. Closure of the [constraint algebra](constraint-algebra.md) is necessary but does not by itself establish regularity.
