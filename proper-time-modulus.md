# Proper-time modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Worldline einbein](worldline-einbein.md)

The constant mode of the [worldline einbein](worldline-einbein.md) is not removed by [gauge transformations](gauge-transformation.md) whose parameters vanish at the ends of a fixed interval. This Schwinger proper-time parameter remains integrated over after [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md). For $\varphi=(p^2+M^2)/2$, the geometric on-shell proper length is $M\int e\,dt$.
