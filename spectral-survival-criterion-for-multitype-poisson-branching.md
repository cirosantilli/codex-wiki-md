# Spectral survival criterion for multitype Poisson branching

↑ **Parent:** [Multitype Poisson branching process](multitype-poisson-branching-process.md)

This criterion holds even for reducible [nonnegative matrices](nonnegative-matrix.md). If a [fixed point](fixed-point.md) $p\ne0$ exists, $1-e^{-z}<z$ for $z>0$ gives $(\Lambda p)_i>p_i$ on its support. Some $\alpha>1$ therefore satisfies $\Lambda p\ge\alpha p$, forcing the [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md) above one by the [Perron–Frobenius theorem](perron-frobenius-theorem.md). Conversely take $v\ge0$, $v\ne0$, with $\Lambda v=\rho v$ and $\rho>1$. For sufficiently small $\eta>0$, $1-e^{-\eta\rho v_i}\ge\eta v_i$. Thus $\eta v$ is a subsolution; monotone iteration under $T$ gives a positive [fixed point](fixed-point.md) below $\mathbf1$. Strictness of $1-e^{-z}<z$ excludes survival at the critical value for this Poisson offspring law.
