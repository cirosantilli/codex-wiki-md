# Cartesian lifting of a binary operation to lists

↑ **Parent:** [List comprehension](list-comprehension.md)

For a [binary operation](binary-operation.md) $\oplus$, its Cartesian lifting sends [lists](list-abstract-data-type.md) $xs,ys$ to the [list comprehension](list-comprehension.md) $[x\oplus y\mid x\leftarrow xs; y\leftarrow ys]$, with the right generator varying fastest. On finite [lists](list-abstract-data-type.md) this preserves [associativity](associative-property.md) but need not preserve [commutativity](commutativity.md) or [idempotence](idempotence.md), because [list](list-abstract-data-type.md) order and repeated entries matter.
