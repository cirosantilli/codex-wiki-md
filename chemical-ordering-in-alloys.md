# Chemical ordering in alloys

↑ **Parent:** [Alloy](alloy.md)

Preferential occupation of distinct crystal sublattices by different chemical elements. Favourable unlike-neighbour bonding can stabilize a periodic [alloy](alloy.md) arrangement despite its lower [configurational entropy](configurational-entropy.md) than a random [alloy](alloy.md).
