# Scallop theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic reversibility of Stokes flow](kinematic-reversibility-of-stokes-flow.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scallop_theorem)

A [force-free](force-free.md) swimmer with a reciprocal shape stroke cannot achieve net locomotion in an inertia-free [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md). A single real shape coordinate that retraces an interval gives a reciprocal cycle. The conclusion concerns free swimming; externally imposed translations can instead force and pump the fluid.
