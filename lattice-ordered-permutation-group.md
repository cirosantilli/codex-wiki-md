# Lattice-ordered permutation group

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md)

A [group](group-split.md) of [order automorphisms](order-automorphism.md) of a [totally ordered set](totally-ordered-set.md) that is closed under pointwise [maximum](maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) and [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md). Products act on the right when writing $\alpha f$; then $\alpha(fg)=(\alpha f)g$. The pointwise lattice operations make it a [lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md).
