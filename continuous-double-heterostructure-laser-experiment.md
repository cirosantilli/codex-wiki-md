# Continuous double heterostructure laser experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Double heterostructure laser](double-heterostructure-laser.md)

The experimental distinction is concrete. A 1962 [gallium arsenide](gallium-arsenide.md) [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md) [laser](laser-split.md) used top and bottom contacts, parallel polished faces, and current pulses with the device in liquid nitrogen. An infrared image converter recorded the radiation pattern; a spectrometer measured narrowing above [laser threshold](laser-threshold.md). Comparing the same device below and above [laser threshold](laser-threshold.md) supplied a control. Pulsed, cooled operation did not demonstrate continuous operation at room [temperature](temperature-split.md).[pp. 366–367, Figs. 1–2](coherent-light-emission-from-gaas-junctions-by-r-n-hall-and-others.md)

[Zhores I. Alferov](zhores-ivanovich-alferov.md)'s 1970 team photolithographically patterned stripe [double heterostructure lasers](double-heterostructure-laser.md) on silver-covered copper plates. He reports sustained [laser](laser-split.md) emission at room [temperature](temperature-split.md); Fig. 4 shows the device structure. Independently, [Izuo Hayashi](izuo-hayashi.md), [Morton B. Panish](morton-b-panish.md) and collaborators demonstrated continuous [double heterostructure laser](double-heterostructure-laser.md) operation with diamond heat sinks.[§II, p. 770, Fig. 4](nobel-lecture-the-double-heterostructure-concept-and-its-applications-in-physics-electronics-and-technology-by-zhores-ivanovich-alferov.md)

The below-threshold comparison tests the onset of [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md); continuous operation additionally requires sustained removal of waste [heat](heat-split.md). Neither observation alone measures the separate [band offsets](band-offset.md), optical overlap, or every loss mechanism. The schematic above therefore explains a design principle rather than reconstructing the historical apparatus.
