# Radial momentum operator

↑ **Parent:** [Radial Schrodinger equation for the hydrogen atom](radial-schrodinger-equation-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md)

For three-dimensional radial [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md) with measure $r^2dr$, this formal symmetric [momentum](momentum-split.md) expression obeys $[f(r),p_r]=i\hbar f'(r)$ on appropriate test functions and has square $-\hbar^2(\partial_r^2+2\partial_r/r)$. It is useful in separating [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) as $p^2=p_r^2+L^2/r^2$. Formal [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) and these identities do not establish a self-adjoint [momentum](momentum-split.md) observable on the half-line; domains and origin conditions must be specified when taking adjoints or defining products.
