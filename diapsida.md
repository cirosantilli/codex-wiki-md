# Diapsida

↑ **Parent:** [Sauropsida](sauropsida.md)

A reptile lineage with a two-opening temporal [skull](skull.md) organization inferred in its ancestry. Modified descendants can lose or close these openings; [turtles](turtle.md) illustrate why present-day hole counts do not determine ancestry.
