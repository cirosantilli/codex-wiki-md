# Mehler formula for the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup](ornstein-uhlenbeck-semigroup.md)

Here $Z$ has the standard [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). Applied to the generating function $e^{sx-s^2/2}$, the [Gaussian moment-generating function](moment-generating-function-of-a-normal-distribution.md) replaces $s$ by $e^{-t}s$, proving the Hermite [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) identity. [Positivity](positivity-linear-maps.md) and preservation of [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md) then give the $L^2$ contraction and identify the full [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup](ornstein-uhlenbeck-semigroup.md) by polynomial density.
