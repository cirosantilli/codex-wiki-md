# Spectral zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral geometry](spectral-geometry.md)

For a nonnegative elliptic operator $L$ on a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md), sum its positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) with multiplicity. The defining series converges in a right half-plane. If $n_0=\dim\ker L$ and $K(t)=\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-tL})$ is the [heat trace](heat-trace.md), then

$$
\zeta_L(s)=\frac1{\Gamma(s)}\int_0^\infty t^{s-1}(K(t)-n_0)\,dt.
$$

Subtracting finitely many terms of the short-time [heat kernel expansion](heat-kernel-expansion.md) gives a [meromorphic continuation](meromorphic-continuation.md). Zero eigenvalues must be excluded: assigning them a nonexistent inverse power does not define the spectral zeta function.
