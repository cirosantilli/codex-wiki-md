<h1 id="1-3-dioxane">1,3-Dioxane</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Heterocyclic compound](heterocyclic-compound.md)

[1,3-Dioxane](1-3-dioxane.md) is a six-membered ring with [oxygen](oxygen.md) at positions 1 and 3. It commonly adopts [chair conformations](chair-conformation.md). Axial substituent penalties differ sharply between C2 and C5 because the relevant 1,3 contacts involve different ring atoms.
