# Primorial

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primorial)

For real $X\geq1$, the primorial up to $X$ is

$$
P(X)=\prod_{p\leq X}p,
$$

where the product ranges over primes.
