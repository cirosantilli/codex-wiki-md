# Square-integrable martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

A [martingale](martingale-split.md) is square integrable when its value at every time belongs to [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md). This implies ordinary integrability by [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). The condition is separate from a uniform bound on these second moments over an infinite horizon. An [Itô integral](ito-integral.md) is square integrable on a finite interval when its predictable integrand has finite expected integrated square, by [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md).
