# Creep compliance

↑ **Parent:** [Linear viscoelastic fluid](linear-viscoelastic-fluid.md)

A small step of [shear stress](shear-stress.md) $s_0H(t)$ produces shear strain $\gamma(t)=s_0J(t)$ from a relaxed state. This time-dependent deformation under held stress is creep. The causal constitutive convolution gives $\widehat s=p\widehat G\widehat\gamma$, proving the displayed [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) relation. For a [Maxwell fluid](linear-maxwell-fluid.md), $J(t)=1/G_0+t/(G_0\tau)$: an elastic jump followed by viscous flow.
