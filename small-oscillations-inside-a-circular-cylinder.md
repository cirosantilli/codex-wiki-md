# Small oscillations inside a circular cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling inside a fixed circular cylinder](rolling-inside-a-fixed-circular-cylinder.md)

For [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) inside a stationary circular cylinder, take the inner body's axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) to be $I=\gamma mr^2$. Its [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) follows a circle of radius $d=R-r$, and the reduced [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is $L=m(1+\gamma)d^2\dot\theta^2/2-mgd(1-\cos\theta)$. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives $(1+\gamma)d\ddot\theta+g\sin\theta=0$. Linearizing near the lowest position yields the displayed [small oscillation](small-oscillation.md) frequency. A uniform solid cylinder has $\gamma=1/2$. This reduction requires sustained contact and enough [static friction](static-friction.md) to prevent slipping.
