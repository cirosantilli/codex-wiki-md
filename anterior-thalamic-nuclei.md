# Anterior thalamic nuclei

↑ **Parent:** [Thalamus](thalamus.md)

Thalamic nuclei connected with the [mammillary bodies](mammillary-body.md), hippocampal formation and cingulate regions. Their dysfunction can compromise spatial and event-related memory.
