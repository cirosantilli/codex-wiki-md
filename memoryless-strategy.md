# Memoryless strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Strategy in an infinite game](strategy-in-an-infinite-game.md)

A strategy choosing an action from the current full game state rather than from the entire prior history. In a finite [reachability game](reachability-game.md), the first-entry rank in the winning [fixed-point iteration](fixed-point-iteration.md) gives such a strategy by choosing a successor of smaller rank. The state must include all information affecting legal moves, such as ownership of reversible assignments and the player to move.
