# Finite-dimensional Hilbert sampling reconstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Oblique projection](oblique-projection.md)

For equal finite-dimensional [closed subspaces of a Hilbert space](closed-subspace-of-a-hilbert-space.md) $T,S$, positivity of $\cos\theta_{T,S}$ makes $P_S|_T$ invertible. The displayed reconstruction is the unique element of $T$ whose [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto $S$ matches that of $f$. It is the [oblique projection](oblique-projection.md) onto $T$ along $S^\perp$. Its [operator norm](operator-norm.md) is the value of the [secant function](secant-trigonometry.md) at the [directed subspace angle](directed-subspace-angle.md), and its error is at most that [secant function](secant-trigonometry.md) value times the best [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) error. The lower error bound follows from the [Pythagorean identity](pythagorean-theorem-in-an-inner-product-space.md). Zero-dimensional cases are handled directly.
