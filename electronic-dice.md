# Electronic dice

↑ **Parent:** [Sequential logic](sequential-logic.md)

An electronic display cycling through the six die faces while a switch is pressed. A [modulo-six counter](modulo-six-counter.md) and [combinational logic](combinational-logic.md) can drive seven lights arranged as die pips. The apparent unpredictability comes from uncertainty in release timing relative to a rapid [clock signal](clock-signal.md); deterministic cycling alone does not establish equal outcome probabilities.
