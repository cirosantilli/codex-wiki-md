# Minkowski addition

↑ **Parent:** [Convex geometry](convex-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_addition)

The [Minkowski sum](minkowski-addition.md) of subsets $A,B$ of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is $A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,b\in B\}$. [Minkowski addition](minkowski-addition.md) also makes sense for nonconvex sets; translations and positive dilations commute with it.
