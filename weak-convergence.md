# Weak convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Weak topology](weak-topology-split.md)

A sequence $x_n$ converges weakly to $x$ when $f(x_n)\to f(x)$ for every $f$ in the [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md) $X^*$.
