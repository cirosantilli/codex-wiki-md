# CKM parameter counting

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md)

An $N\times N$ [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) has $N^2$ real parameters. Its real rotation subgroup has $N(N-1)/2$ mixing angles, leaving $N(N+1)/2$ phases. The $N$ up-type and $N$ down-type [quark fields](quark-field.md) allow $2N$ [quantum field rephasings](quantum-field-rephasing.md), but their common phase acts trivially on the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md). Removing $2N-1$ phases therefore leaves

$$
N_{\rm angles}=\frac{N(N-1)}2,\qquad
N_{\rm phases}=\frac{(N-1)(N-2)}2.
$$

For two [quark generations](quark-generation.md), there is one mixing angle and no physical [CP-violating phase](cp-violating-phase.md). For three [quark generations](quark-generation.md), there are three mixing angles and one physical [CP-violating phase](cp-violating-phase.md). The count describes generic, nondegenerate [quark masses](quark-mass.md) and generic mixing; a vanishing mixing angle can make the remaining phase unobservable, and mass degeneracies enlarge the allowed basis changes. Three [quark generations](quark-generation.md) permit [CP violation](cp-violation.md) but do not force it.
