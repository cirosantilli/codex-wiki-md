# Specific heat capacity at constant pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md)

The [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md) at fixed [pressure](pressure.md) and composition is $c_P=(\partial h/\partial T)_P$, where $h$ is [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md). Equivalently, it is [heat capacity at constant pressure](heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) divided by [mass](mass.md). Under the process assumptions in [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), it includes expansion [work](work.md) as well as the [internal energy](internal-energy.md) change. The [sensible heat](sensible-heat.md) example uses this response separately for [ice](ice.md) and [water](water.md).
