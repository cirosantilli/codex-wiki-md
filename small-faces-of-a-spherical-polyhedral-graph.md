# Small faces of a spherical polyhedral graph

↑ **Parent:** [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md)

For a connected cellular spherical [planar graph](planar-graph.md) with vertex degrees at least three and face sizes at least three, degree/face double counting and the [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md) give $\sum_n(6-n)F_n=12+\sum_m(2m-6)V_m\ge12$. The left side is at most $3(F_3+F_4+F_5)$, proving the displayed bound. In particular a convex polyhedron has at least four faces of size at most five.
