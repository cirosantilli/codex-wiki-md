# Schwarzschild-Tangherlini metric

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)

For $D\geq4$, the static spherical vacuum metric $ds^2=-fdt^2+f^{-1}dr^2+r^2d\Omega_{D-2}^2$ has an [event horizon](event-horizon.md) at $r_h=\mu^{1/(D-3)}$. With time normalized at infinity its [ADM mass](arnowitt-deser-misner-mass.md) is $M=(D-2)\Omega_{D-2}\mu/(16\pi G_D)$, where $\Omega_{D-2}$ is the unit-sphere area. For $D=5$ and $\mu=2m$, $r_h=\sqrt{2m}$ and $M=3\pi m/(4G_5)$. The spherical orbits are three-spheres, even though the radial-time [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md) has the same causal pattern as its four-dimensional counterpart.
