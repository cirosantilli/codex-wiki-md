# Renormalization scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

A choice of [renormalization conditions](renormalization-condition.md) and finite [counterterms](counterterm.md) defines a renormalization scheme. The [minimal subtraction scheme](minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) removes only regulator poles; the [modified minimal subtraction scheme](modified-minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) also removes conventional universal constants. Different schemes can assign different values to renormalized couplings while describing the same physical observables after parameters are consistently matched.
