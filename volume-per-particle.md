# Volume per particle

↑ **Parent:** [Volume (thermodynamics)](volume-thermodynamics.md)

The [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) divided by the positive number of particles $N$. The [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md) uses this convention for $v$, whereas [specific volume](specific-volume.md) divides by [mass](mass.md) and [molar volume](molar-volume.md) divides by the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md). With the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) $N_A$, the [molar volume](molar-volume.md) is $v_m=N_Av$.
