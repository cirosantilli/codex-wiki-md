# Wilsonian rescaling of a scalar coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md)

After shell integration and $x^\prime=bx$, restore the [kinetic term](kinetic-term.md) by $\phi^\prime=b^{-(d-2)/2}\sqrt{1+\Delta Z}\,\phi$. An operator with $n$ scalar fields and $m$ derivatives scales by $b^{m+n(d-2)/2-d}$. In four dimensions this gives the displayed factor with $m+n-4$; the general exponent is $m+n(d-2)/2-d$. The coupling shift and [wavefunction renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) arise before the rescaling.
