# Charge stabilization of colloids

↑ **Parent:** [DLVO theory](dlvo-theory.md)

Like-charged [colloids](colloid.md) can resist aggregation because bringing their [electric double layers](electric-double-layer.md) together costs [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md). In [DLVO theory](dlvo-theory.md), screened electrostatic repulsion competes with attractive [Van der Waals forces](van-der-waals-force.md). A repulsive barrier much larger than $k_BT$ suppresses thermally activated aggregation, although the dispersion can remain metastable. Lower [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md) generally lengthens the [Debye–Hückel screening length](debye-huckel-screening-length.md), while multivalent [counterions](counterion.md) or added salt shorten it and can lower the barrier. The surface charge, charge regulation and solvent-dependent attraction also matter; screening length alone does not guarantee stability.
