# Constant-stress Reynolds-averaged wall flow

↑ **Parent:** [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md)

For a stationary, horizontally homogeneous mean flow without a horizontal pressure gradient, the averaged horizontal [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md) is $\nu U_{zz}-(\overline{u'w'})_z=0$. Integrating gives the displayed physical [shear stress](shear-stress.md). The derivative of the Reynolds covariance belongs in the stress-divergence equation, not inside the stress itself. The kinematic stress is $\tau_d/\rho$, which must be used when an [eddy viscosity](eddy-viscosity.md) with units of kinematic viscosity multiplies the mean gradient.
