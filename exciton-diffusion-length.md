# Exciton diffusion length

↑ **Parent:** [Exciton](exciton.md)

A characteristic transport length before [exciton](exciton.md) decay, commonly $L_D=\sqrt{D_X\tau_X}$ in a diffusion-decay convention. A root-mean-square displacement over a fixed time has dimension-dependent factors and is not automatically this same convention.
