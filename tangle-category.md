# Tangle category

↑ **Parent:** [Tangle](tangle.md)

Objects are finite words of strand orientations. Morphisms are boundary-fixed ambient-isotopy classes of oriented [tangles](tangle.md) in a slab, composed by stacking compatible boundary words; horizontal juxtaposition is the [tensor product](tensor-product.md). Cups, caps, identity strands and crossings generate the category. Ordinary unframed isotopy includes all three [Reidemeister moves](reidemeister-move.md); framed variants impose different first-move relations.
