# Rodrigues' formula

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigues'_formula)

A Rodrigues formula constructs an [orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md) by differentiating a weight multiplied by a power. The [Rodrigues formula for Legendre polynomials](rodrigues-formula-for-legendre-polynomials.md) uses a derivative of $(x^2-1)^l$. For [Generalized Laguerre polynomials](generalized-laguerre-polynomial.md),

$$
L_n^{(\alpha)}(x)
=\frac{x^{-\alpha}e^x}{n!}\frac{d^n}{dx^n}
\left(e^{-x}x^{n+\alpha}\right).
$$
