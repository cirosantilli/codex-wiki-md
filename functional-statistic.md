# Functional statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical functional](statistical-functional.md)

A functional statistic evaluates a [statistical functional](statistical-functional.md) $T$ at the [empirical distribution](type-information-theory.md) $F_n=n^{-1}\sum_i\delta_{X_i}$. The map $T$ acts on distributions, whereas $T(F_n)$ is a [statistic](statistic.md). If $T$ has an [asymptotic linear representation](asymptotic-linear-representation.md), then

$$
T(F_n)-T(F)=\frac1n\sum_i\operatorname{IF}(X_i;T,F)+o_p(n^{-1/2}).
$$

A square-integrable [influence function](influence-function.md) with mean zero consequently gives [asymptotic variance](asymptotic-variance.md) $n^{-1}\int\operatorname{IF}(x;T,F)^2\,dF(x)$ by the [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md).
