# Midpoint symmetry of a viscous Burgers step

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous Burgers step solution with negative flux](viscous-burgers-step-solution-with-negative-flux.md)

The [viscous Burgers step solution with negative flux](viscous-burgers-step-solution-with-negative-flux.md) is symmetric around $\theta=-Uz/2$ after subtracting $U/2$. Its Gaussian-tail ratio equals one precisely at this midpoint, giving $f=U/2$ for either sign of $U$. For $U>0$ this point becomes the entropy-shock center in the [vanishing viscosity approximation](vanishing-viscosity-approximation.md); for $U<0$ it is the center of a [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md). The same trajectory therefore does not by itself identify a [shock](shock-wave.md).
