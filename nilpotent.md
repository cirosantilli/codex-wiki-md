# Nilpotent

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilpotent)

An element $x$ of a ring is nilpotent when $x^n=0$ for some positive integer $n$. A field has no nonzero nilpotent elements.
