# Separability of the continuous dual implies separability

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md)

If the [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md) $X'$ of a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md) is norm separable, then $X$ is [separable](separable-topological-space.md). Choose a dense sequence $f_n$ in the dual unit ball and unit-ball points $x_n$ with $|f_n(x_n)|\geq\|f_n\|/2$. If their closed [linear span](linear-span.md) were proper, the [Hahn-Banach distance formula](hahn-banach-distance-formula.md) would give a norm-one functional $f$ vanishing on it. An $f_n$ within $1/4$ of $f$ would simultaneously satisfy $|f_n(x_n)|<1/4$ and $|f_n(x_n)|>3/8$, a contradiction. Rational linear combinations give a countable dense subset of $X$.

The converse fails by [duality of l1 and l infinity](duality-of-l1-and-l-infinity.md): $\ell^1$ is separable, whereas $\ell^\infty$ contains uncountably many zero-one sequences at mutual [distance](distance.md) one.
