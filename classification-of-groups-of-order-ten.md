# Classification of groups of order ten

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group theory](finite-group-theory-split.md)

A [group](group-split.md) of order ten is either a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) or the [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of the pentagon. [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md) and the fact that a group of exponent two has power-of-two order force an element of order five or ten. In the latter case the group is cyclic. In the former case its order-five [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md) is normal by index two; an outside involution acts on it either trivially or by inversion. The trivial action gives $C_5\times C_2\cong C_{10}$, and inversion gives $D_{10}$.
