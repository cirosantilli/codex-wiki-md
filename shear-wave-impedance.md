# Shear-wave impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic impedance](acoustic-impedance.md)

The product $Z_s=\rho V_s$ of bulk [density](density.md) and [S wave](s-wave.md) speed. At a normal-incidence boundary it controls the corresponding stress-amplitude [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md).
