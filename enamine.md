# Enamine

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enamine)

An [enamine](enamine.md) contains an amino nitrogen bonded to an alkene carbon. Delocalization between the nitrogen lone pair and the alkene makes its carbon nucleophilic; carbon alkylation can give an [iminium ion](iminium.md) that hydrolyses to a carbonyl compound.
