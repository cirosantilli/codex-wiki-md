# Thylakoid

↑ **Parent:** [Chloroplast](chloroplast.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thylakoid)

A [thylakoid](thylakoid.md) is a membrane-enclosed sac; its [thylakoid membrane](thylakoid-membrane.md) contains [chlorophyll](chlorophyll.md) and [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md) machinery. A stack is a [granum](granum.md).[Biology 2e, 8.1, Basic Photosynthetic Structures, Figure 8.6](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/8-1-overview-of-photosynthesis.md)

The membrane and its enclosed fluid answer different location questions. A soluble [protein](protein-split.md) in [thylakoid lumen](thylakoid-lumen.md) is inside the sac; a [membrane protein](membrane-protein.md) is embedded in its boundary and can expose different parts on the two sides. [Membrane topology](membrane-topology.md) and [organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md) provide the reusable language for those distinctions.
