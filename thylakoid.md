# Thylakoid

↑ **Parent:** [Chloroplast](chloroplast.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thylakoid)

A [thylakoid](thylakoid.md) is a flattened membrane-bounded compartment. In a [chloroplast](chloroplast.md), its membrane contains the machinery for [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md), including [chlorophyll](chlorophyll.md). Stacks of [thylakoids](thylakoid.md) pack membrane area into a small space within the [chloroplast](chloroplast.md); the [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md) schematic simplifies this architecture.
