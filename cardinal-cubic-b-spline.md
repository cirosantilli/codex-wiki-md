# Cardinal cubic B-spline

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal B-spline](cardinal-b-spline.md)

A cardinal cubic B-spline is a degree-three [Cardinal B-spline](cardinal-b-spline.md). At the three interior integer knots, its nonzero normalized values are $1/6$, $4/6$, and $1/6$.
