<h1 id="kink-antikink-attraction-from-the-stress-tensor">Kink–antikink attraction from the stress tensor</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Phi-four kink](phi-four-kink.md)

For $V(\phi)=\lambda(m^2-\phi^2)^2$ with $\lambda,m>0$, set $\kappa=\sqrt{2\lambda}m$. A well-separated [kink](scalar-field-kink.md)–[antikink](antikink.md) pair at $-d/2,d/2$ is approximated by $\phi=m\tanh\kappa(x+d/2)-m\tanh\kappa(x-d/2)-m$. At its midpoint, $\phi_x=0$ and $\phi=m-4m e^{-\kappa d}+O(e^{-2\kappa d})$. The static [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) component $T^{11}=\phi_x^2/2-V$ is therefore negative there. [Momentum](momentum.md) conservation gives the force on the left half-line as $T^{11}(-\infty)-T^{11}(0)$, which is positive. Its leading magnitude is $F\sim32m^2\kappa^2e^{-2\kappa d}$. Thus the pair attracts. This is a controlled leading-tail estimate at large separation, not an exact superposed solution or a theorem about all subsequent collision outcomes.
