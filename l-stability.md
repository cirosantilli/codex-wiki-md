# L-stability

↑ **Parent:** [A-stability](a-stability.md)

An A-stable one-step method is L-stable when its [stability function](stability-function.md) also satisfies $R(z)\to0$ as $|z|\to\infty$ within the left half-plane. It damps modes whose decay time is far shorter than the numerical step.
