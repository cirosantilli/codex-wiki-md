# Critical closed matter-cosmological-constant universe

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann effective potential for pressureless matter](friedmann-effective-potential-for-pressureless-matter.md)

For positive spatial curvature and positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), the effective potential has its maximum at

$$
a_E=\left(\frac{3\mu}{2\Lambda}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

The critical case $V(a_E)=-1$ has

$$
\Lambda=\frac{4}{9\mu^2},
\qquad
a_E=\frac{3\mu}{2}=\Lambda^{-1/2},
$$

and contains the [Einstein static universe](einstein-static-universe.md).
