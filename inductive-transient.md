# Inductive transient

↑ **Parent:** [Inductor](inductor.md)

Following a change in applied [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md), a series [inductor](inductor.md) and [resistor](resistor.md) obey $L\dot I+RI=\mathcal E(t)$. For a constant applied [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md), the difference from the final [electric current](electric-current.md) decays as $e^{-tR/L}$. Neglecting [self-inductance](self-inductance.md) is justified only when the forcing varies slowly compared with the [time constant](time-constant.md) $L/R$, or when considering a steady state.
