# Linear independence

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_independence)

Vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ are linearly independent when

$$
a_1v_1+\cdots+a_nv_n=0
$$

implies $a_1=\cdots=a_n=0$.
