# Tarski-Vaught test

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary substructure](elementary-substructure.md)

A substructure $M\subseteq N$ is elementary if and only if, whenever $N\models\exists x\,\varphi(x,\bar a)$ with parameters $\bar a$ from $M$, some witness $b\in M$ satisfies $N\models\varphi(b,\bar a)$.

The witness criterion characterizes an [elementary substructure](elementary-substructure.md), and hence preservation of all first-order formulas with parameters from the substructure.
