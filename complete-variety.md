# Complete variety

↑ **Parent:** [Proper morphism](proper-morphism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_variety)

A [variety](algebraic-variety.md) over a field $k$ is complete when its map to $\operatorname{Spec}k$ is [universally closed](universally-closed-morphism.md): after any base change, projection sends closed subsets to closed subsets. For a separated finite-type [variety](algebraic-variety.md), this is equivalent to being [proper](proper-morphism.md). In the classical algebraically closed setting one may test projections $X\times Y\to Y$ for arbitrary [varieties](algebraic-variety.md). Images of rational points over a non-algebraically-closed field must not replace geometric images.
