# Pattern-mixture sensitivity analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Sensitivity analysis](sensitivity-analysis.md)

Specify distributions of measurements separately by observation pattern and vary assumptions about unseen values that the observed data do not identify. For example, shift the imputed binary-response [log odds](log-odds.md) for nonresponders away from its [missing at random](missing-at-random.md) prediction and refit the substantive analysis. This investigates [missing not at random](missing-not-at-random.md) departures rather than testing them from observed data.
