# Region of absolute convergence of a Laplace transform

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md)

The [set](set-split.md) of [complex numbers](complex-number.md) $s$ for which the displayed [integral](integral-split.md) is finite is the region of absolute convergence of a [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md). It depends only on the [real part](real-part.md) $\sigma=\operatorname{Re}s$. Once the condition holds for one $\sigma$, it holds for every larger [real part](real-part.md). For the [exponential function](exponential-function.md) $e^{-2t}$ it is exactly $\operatorname{Re}s>-2$; in general, convergence on the boundary must be checked separately. An [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) outside this [set](set-split.md) need not equal a convergent defining [integral](integral-split.md).
