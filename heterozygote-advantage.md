# Heterozygote advantage

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics.md)

A [heterozygote](heterozygote.md) has greater [genotype fitness](genotype-fitness.md) than either corresponding [homozygote](homozygote.md). In the deterministic random-mating two-allele viability-selection model this gives an attracting interior [allele frequency](allele-frequency.md), unlike the repelling interior frequency produced by [underdominance](underdominance.md).
