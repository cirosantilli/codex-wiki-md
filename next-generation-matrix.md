# Next-generation matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Basic reproduction number](basic-reproduction-number.md)

The entry $K_{ij}$ is the expected number of newly infected type-$i$ individuals produced by a newly infected type-$j$ individual while the population is disease-free. In a compartmental [linearization](linearization.md), $F$ describes new-infection production and $V$ transitions and removal. Provided the infection-lifetime subsystem is transient, its integrated occupation [matrix](matrix.md) is $V^{-1}$, yielding $K=FV^{-1}$. The [basic reproduction number](basic-reproduction-number.md) is its [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md). This construction is described in [the original next-generation-matrix derivation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19892718/).
