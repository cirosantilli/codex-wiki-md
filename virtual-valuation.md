# Virtual valuation

↑ **Parent:** [Single-parameter mechanism](single-parameter-mechanism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_valuation)

For an absolutely continuous valuation distribution with density $f(v)>0$, its virtual valuation is $v-(1-F(v))/f(v)$. The [virtual-surplus revenue identity](virtual-surplus-revenue-identity.md) converts expected incentive-compatible payments into expected allocations weighted by virtual valuations. A [regular prior](regular-distribution-economics.md) makes this quantity nondecreasing.
