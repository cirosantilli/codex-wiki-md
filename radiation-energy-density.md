# Radiation energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Black-body radiation](black-body-radiation.md)

The spectral radiation [energy density](energy-density.md) $u(\omega)$ is defined per unit [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) and physical volume. Thermal [Planck law](planck-s-law.md) convention excludes the vacuum zero-point term. A density per ordinary frequency instead obeys $u_\omega=u_\nu/(2\pi)$, evaluated at $\nu=\omega/(2\pi)$; [Einstein coefficients](einstein-coefficients.md) multiplying these densities must be converted correspondingly.
