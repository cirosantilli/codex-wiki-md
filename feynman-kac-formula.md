# Feynman-Kac formula

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov backward equation](kolmogorov-backward-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feynman-Kac_formula)

The Feynman-Kac formula represents solutions of certain parabolic [partial differential equations](partial-differential-equation-split.md) as conditional expectations of functionals of a diffusion. It follows by applying [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) to the solution along the diffusion and taking expectations.
