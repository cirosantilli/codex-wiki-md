# Galactic foreground

↑ **Parent:** [Milky Way](milky-way.md)

Emission or absorption from the [Milky Way](milky-way.md) contributes a foreground when observing sources beyond it. For [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) measurements, important components include thermal [interstellar dust](interstellar-dust.md), [synchrotron radiation](synchrotron-radiation.md) and [free-free emission](free-free-emission.md). Their spectral and spatial behavior helps distinguish them from a near-isotropic [blackbody](blackbody.md) background.
