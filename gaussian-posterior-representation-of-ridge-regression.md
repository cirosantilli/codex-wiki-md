# Gaussian posterior representation of ridge regression

↑ **Parent:** [Ridge regression](ridge-regression.md)

For likelihood $Y\mid\beta\sim N(X\beta,\sigma^2I)$ and independent prior $\beta\sim N(0,\tau^2I)$, the [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) is Gaussian with covariance $V$ as displayed and mean $(X^TX+\lambda I)^{-1}X^TY$. Its mean and mode therefore coincide with the [ridge regression](ridge-regression.md) coefficient estimate. A Gaussian prior alone does not imply this conclusion without the stated likelihood.
