<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the central [classically forbidden region](../../../../../classically-forbidden-region.md), define the positive decay [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $\chi(x)=\sqrt{2m[V(x)-E]}$ and the [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) [action integral](../../../../../action-integral.md) $K(x)=\hbar^{-1}\int_x^{x_1}\chi(y)\,dy$. The general leading [WKB approximation](../../../../../wkb-approximation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\psi_{\rm I}(x)\simeq\frac{A e^{K(x)}+B e^{-K(x)}}{\sqrt{\chi(x)}},\qquad 0<x<x_1.}
$$

Both coefficients are allowed: this interval has finite length, so the exponential that grows towards the barrier centre cannot be discarded by a condition at infinity. The [WKB validity condition](../../../../../wkb-validity-condition.md) holds away from the [classical turning point](../../../../../classical-turning-point.md) $x_1$; a local [Airy function](../../../../../airy-function.md) solution supplies its continuation there.
