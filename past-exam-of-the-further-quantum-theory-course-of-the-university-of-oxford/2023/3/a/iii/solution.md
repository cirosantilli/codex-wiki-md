<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Beyond the outer [classical turning point](../../../../../classical-turning-point.md), let $\chi(x)=\sqrt{2m[V(x)-E]}$ and $U(x)=\hbar^{-1}\int_{x_2}^x\chi(y)\,dy$. The [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md) condition removes the growing branch at positive infinity, leaving

$$
\boxed{\psi_{\rm III}(x)\simeq\frac{F e^{-U(x)}}{\sqrt{\chi(x)}},\qquad x>x_2.}
$$

Here $F$ is an arbitrary common amplitude pending the matching and [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md). For the confining [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) in the native diagram, the decaying branch gives the required exterior [square-integrable function](../../../../../square-integrable-function.md).
