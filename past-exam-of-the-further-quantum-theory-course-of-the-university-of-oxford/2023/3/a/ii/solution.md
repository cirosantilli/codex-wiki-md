<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the right-hand [classically allowed region](../../../../../classically-allowed-region.md), put $p(x)=\sqrt{2m[E-V(x)]}$ and $s(x)=\hbar^{-1}\int_{x_1}^xp(y)\,dy$. A convenient form of the general [WKB approximation](../../../../../wkb-approximation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\psi_{\rm II}(x)\simeq\frac{C\cos(s(x)+\pi/4)+D\sin(s(x)+\pi/4)}{\sqrt{p(x)}},\qquad x_1<x<x_2.}
$$

The phase origin $\pi/4$ is a choice of [basis](../../../../../basis.md) for the two oscillatory solutions, anticipating the [Airy turning-point connection formula](../../../../../airy-turning-point-connection-formula.md). Equivalently, one may use independent $e^{is}$ and $e^{-is}$ branches. The coefficients have not yet been related to those in either forbidden region.
