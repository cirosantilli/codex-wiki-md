<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the ordered product [basis](../../../../basis.md) $(|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle,|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle,|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle,|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle)$, the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) has [matrix](../../../../matrix.md)

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}
\alpha/4&0&0&0\\
0&-\alpha/4+\beta&\alpha/2&0\\
0&\alpha/2&-\alpha/4-\beta&0\\
0&0&0&\alpha/4
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The middle block has [characteristic polynomial](../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) $(E+\alpha/4)^2-\beta^2-\alpha^2/4$. Hence the exact [energy eigenvalues](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\frac\alpha4\quad\text{(twice)},\qquad E_\pm=-\frac\alpha4\pm\sqrt{\beta^2+\frac{\alpha^2}{4}}.}
$$

This is the [Two-spin Heisenberg Hamiltonian in an opposing longitudinal field](../../../../two-spin-heisenberg-hamiltonian-in-an-opposing-longitudinal-field.md), with its interaction and field parameters rescaled to the paper's units. For positive $\beta$ and $|\alpha|\ll\beta$, the [Taylor expansion](../../../../taylor-expansion.md) gives

$$
E_\pm=-\frac\alpha4\pm\left(\beta+\frac{\alpha^2}{8\beta}+O(\alpha^4/\beta^3)\right),
$$

which agrees with the first-order result of part (c). For positive $\alpha$ and $|\beta|\ll\alpha$,

$$
E_+=\frac\alpha4+\frac{\beta^2}{\alpha}+O(\beta^4/\alpha^3),\qquad E_-=-\frac{3\alpha}{4}-\frac{\beta^2}{\alpha}+O(\beta^4/\alpha^3).
$$

These agree with the second-order triplet and singlet results. If $\alpha<0$, their association with the signs $\pm$ reverses; if $\beta<0$, the two antiparallel product-state labels reverse. The unordered exact [spectrum](../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md) and the perturbative expressions with their signed denominators remain valid.
