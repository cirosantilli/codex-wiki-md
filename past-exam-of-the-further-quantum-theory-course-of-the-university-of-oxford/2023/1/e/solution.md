<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $X$ exchange the two equal-[spin](../../../../spin.md) factors, so $X^\dagger=X=X^{-1}$. For the unperturbed [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), [addition of angular momentum](../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md) gives

$$
E_J^{(0)}=\frac\alpha2\bigl[J(J+1)-2j(j+1)\bigr],\qquad J=0,1,\ldots,2j.
$$

For nonzero $\alpha$, different $J$ have distinct [energy eigenvalues](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md), while each [eigenspace](../../../../eigenspace.md) has the $2J+1$ values of $M$. The stated symmetry of the [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](../../../../clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) gives the [exchange parity of two coupled identical spins](../../../../exchange-parity-of-two-coupled-identical-spins.md):

$$
X|J,M\rangle=(-1)^{2j-J}|J,M\rangle.
$$

However, $D=(S_3^{(1)}-S_3^{(2)})/\hbar$ changes sign: $XDX=-D$. For any two [basis](../../../../basis.md) vectors in the same $J$ [eigenspace](../../../../eigenspace.md), insertion of $X^2=I$ therefore gives

$$
\langle J,M|D|J,M'\rangle=(-1)^{2(2j-J)}\langle J,M|XDX|J,M'\rangle=-\langle J,M|D|J,M'\rangle=0.
$$

Thus the entire projected perturbation $P_J\beta DP_J$ is zero, including its off-diagonal [matrix elements](../../../../matrix-element.md). [Degenerate perturbation theory](../../../../degenerate-perturbation-theory.md) now proves that every first-order-in-$\beta$ [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) correction vanishes. This is [first-order cancellation for an exchange-odd perturbation](../../../../first-order-cancellation-for-an-exchange-odd-perturbation.md); checking only the individual diagonal expectations would not by itself settle a degenerate problem.
