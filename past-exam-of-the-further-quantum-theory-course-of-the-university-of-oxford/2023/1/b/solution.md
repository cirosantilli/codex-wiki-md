<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On the [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md) of spins $j_1$ and $j_2$, the combined [angular momentum operator](../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md) is $\mathbf J=\mathbf S^{(1)}\otimes I+I\otimes\mathbf S^{(2)}$. The [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition](../../../../clebsch-gordan-decomposition.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H_{j_1}\otimes\mathcal H_{j_2}\cong\bigoplus_{J=|j_1-j_2|}^{j_1+j_2}\mathcal H_J,}
$$

where $J$ increases by one and each [irreducible spin representation](../../../../irreducible-spin-representation.md) occurs once. The [dimensions](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) agree: $\sum_J(2J+1)=(2j_1+1)(2j_2+1)$.

The uncoupled [orthonormal basis](../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) diagonalizes $S_3^{(1)},S_3^{(2)}$; the coupled [orthonormal basis](../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) diagonalizes $\mathbf J^2,J_3$. The [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](../../../../clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) are their change-of-[basis](../../../../basis.md) entries, with convention

$$
|J,M\rangle=\sum_{m_1,m_2}C_{j_1,j_2}(J,M;m_1,m_2)\,|j_1,m_1\rangle\otimes|j_2,m_2\rangle.
$$

They vanish unless $m_1+m_2=M$. Since both [bases](../../../../basis.md) are orthonormal, this change-of-[basis](../../../../basis.md) [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) obeys [unitarity](../../../../unitary-operator.md). Overall phases of the coupled [basis](../../../../basis.md) vectors are fixed by a convention and do not change the [energy eigenvalues](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md).
