<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Continue the [transmission amplitude](../../../../transmission-amplitude.md) to complex [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md) $q=i\kappa$ with $\kappa>0$, equivalently complex [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) $k=i\hbar\kappa$. Then $E=-\hbar^2\kappa^2/(2m)<0$. In the left exterior region the $A$ term grows at negative infinity and the $B$ term decays; in the right exterior region the $D$ term grows and the $C$ term decays. A [bound state](../../../../bound-state.md) thus requires $A=D=0$, with nonzero $B,C$. The [transfer matrix](../../../../transfer-matrix.md) relation gives $M_{22}(i\kappa)=0$. Equivalently, a zero of the incoming coefficient is a [bound-state pole](../../../../bound-state-pole-of-the-scattering-amplitude.md) of $t(q)$, whose numerator is nonzero at $q=i\kappa$.

The [denominator](../../../../denominator.md) found in part (c) gives

$$
0=(i\kappa+i\mu)^2+\mu^2e^{-4a\kappa}=-(\kappa+\mu)^2+\mu^2e^{-4a\kappa}.
$$

Factorization produces two branches. To identify their [parity](../../../../parity.md), use an even interior [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) $A\cosh(\kappa x)$ or an odd interior [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) $A\sinh(\kappa x)$ for $|x|<a$, joined to a decaying exterior [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) at $a$. Continuity and the derivative jump give, respectively,

$$
-\kappa\bigl[1+\tanh(\kappa a)\bigr]=2\mu,\qquad -\kappa\bigl[1+\coth(\kappa a)\bigr]=2\mu.
$$

Rearrangement gives the implicit [energy eigenvalue](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) equations

$$
\boxed{\kappa_{\rm even}=-\mu(1+e^{-2a\kappa_{\rm even}}),\qquad \kappa_{\rm odd}=-\mu(1-e^{-2a\kappa_{\rm odd}}),\qquad E_{\rm even,odd}=-\frac{\hbar^2\kappa_{\rm even,odd}^2}{2m}.}
$$

Only strictly positive [equation roots](../../../../root-of-an-equation.md) $\kappa$ are retained. With $\nu=-\mu>0$, the even [equation root](../../../../root-of-an-equation.md) lies above $\nu$ and the odd [equation root](../../../../root-of-an-equation.md), when it exists, lies below $\nu$, so the even [bound state](../../../../bound-state.md) has lower [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). The formal zero [equation root](../../../../root-of-an-equation.md) in the odd equation does not itself establish a [normalizable wavefunction](../../../../normalizable-wavefunction.md).
