# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the further quantum theory course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [Further quantum theory course of the University of Oxford](../further-quantum-theory-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, held on 29 May 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 268–271; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=268). In question 2 the paper defines $k=\sqrt{2mE}$, a [momentum](../momentum-split.md); the corresponding [wavenumber](../wavenumber.md) is $q=k/\hbar$. Keeping these distinct retains the paper's convention without setting the [reduced Planck constant](../reduced-planck-constant.md) to one.
