# Ramanujan congruence modulo 691

↑ **Parent:** [Ramanujan tau function](ramanujan-tau-function.md)

The [Fourier expansion of a normalized Eisenstein series](fourier-expansion-of-a-normalized-eisenstein-series.md) and the one-dimensional weight-twelve [cusp form](cusp-form.md) space give $691E_{12}=691E_4^3-432000\Delta$. The coefficient of $q^n$, for $n\geq1$, reduces modulo $691$ to $566\sigma_{11}(n)=566\tau(n)$. Since $566$ is invertible modulo $691$, this proves the congruence. Integrality follows from $E_4=1+240\sum\sigma_3(n)q^n$ and the product $\Delta=q\prod_{n\geq1}(1-q^n)^{24}$.
