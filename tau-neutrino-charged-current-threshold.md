# Tau-neutrino charged-current threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Tau neutrino](tau-neutrino.md)

For a nearly massless incident [tau neutrino](tau-neutrino.md) and stationary target of [mass](mass.md) $M_T$, the initial [Mandelstam variables](mandelstam-variables.md) give $s=M_T^2+2M_TE_\nu$. At threshold the final [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) and the lightest allowed recoil system have no relative [momentum](momentum-split.md), so $s=(m_\tau+M_X)^2$. This proves the formula. A free [neutron](neutron-split.md) recoil channel gives approximately $3.45\,\mathrm{GeV}$; an [electron](electron.md) target has a different recoil and a threshold near $3.09\,\mathrm{TeV}$.
