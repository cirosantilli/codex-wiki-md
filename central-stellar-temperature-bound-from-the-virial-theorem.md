# Central stellar temperature bound from the virial theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar virial theorem](stellar-virial-theorem.md)

For spherical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) with negligible surface [pressure](pressure-split.md), [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) support and constant [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md) $\mu$, the [stellar virial theorem](stellar-virial-theorem.md) gives $3k_B\int T\,dm/(\mu m_p)=\int Gm\,dm/r$. Since $r<R$ throughout the interior, the right side exceeds $GM^2/(2R)$. Division by $M$ gives the stated mass-weighted [temperature](temperature-split.md) bound. The central bound additionally assumes that the central [temperature](temperature-split.md) is maximal. A central [pressure](pressure-split.md) lower bound alone is insufficient without controlling central [mass density](density.md).
