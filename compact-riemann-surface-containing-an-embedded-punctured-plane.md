# Compact Riemann surface containing an embedded punctured plane

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformization theorem](uniformization-theorem.md)

If $\mathbb C^*$ embeds holomorphically in a compact Riemann surface $R$, the embedding extends across zero and infinity to a degree-one holomorphic map from the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) to $R$. Hence $R$ is conformally the sphere.
