# Starobinsky inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starobinsky_inflation)

Starobinsky inflation is an inflationary model whose Einstein-frame scalar potential can be written

$$
V(\phi)=V_0\left(1-e^{-\sqrt{2/3}\,\phi/M_{\rm Pl}}\right)^2.
$$

The exponentially flat large-field plateau supports [slow-roll inflation](slow-roll-approximation.md), while the potential is quadratic near its minimum.
