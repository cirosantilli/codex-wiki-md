# Sobolev multiplication by a smooth cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

Multiplication by a [test function](test-function.md) is bounded on every real-index [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md). Its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) acts by convolution with a [Schwartz function](schwartz-function.md); frequency-weight estimates and [Young's convolution inequality](young-s-convolution-inequality.md) give the norm bound.
