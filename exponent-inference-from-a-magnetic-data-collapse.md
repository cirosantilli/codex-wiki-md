# Exponent inference from a magnetic data collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling relation for critical exponents](scaling-relation-for-critical-exponents.md)

If magnetic data collapse with independently specified [critical exponents](critical-exponent.md) $\beta,\gamma$, the ordinary scaling identities infer $\Delta=\beta+\gamma$, $\delta=1+\gamma/\beta$, and the displayed exponents. The [Fisher scaling relation](fisher-scaling-relation.md) then gives $\eta=2-\gamma/\nu$. These are conditional inferences, not five additional independent measurements. [Hyperscaling](hyperscaling-relation.md) is needed for the spatial exponents, and subtraction can make small inferred $\alpha$ or $\eta$ unusually sensitive to uncertainty. A slope on linear axes is not a fitted logarithmic exponent.
