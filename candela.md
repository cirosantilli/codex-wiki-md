# Candela

↑ **Parent:** [Luminous intensity](luminous-intensity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candela)

At exactly $540\,\mathrm{THz}$, [radiant intensity](radiant-intensity.md) $1/683\,\mathrm{W\,sr^{-1}}$ corresponds to [luminous intensity](luminous-intensity.md) $1\,\mathrm{cd}$. This convention specifies a direction, rather than the source's total [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md). [SI base unit: candela (cd)](si-base-unit-candela-cd.md)
