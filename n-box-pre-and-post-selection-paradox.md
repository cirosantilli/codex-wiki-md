<h1 id="n-box-pre-and-post-selection-paradox">N-box pre- and post-selection paradox</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Context dependence of pre- and post-selected measurements](context-dependence-of-pre-and-post-selected-measurements.md)

For a uniform prestate in $N+1$ dimensions and poststate proportional to $\sum_{i=1}^N|i\rangle-(N-1)|N+1\rangle$, each binary question $P_i$ versus $I-P_i$ has conditional probability one for $i\leq N$. A fully resolved [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) instead assigns $1/D$ to each of the first $N$ outcomes and $(N-1)^2/D$ to the last. The [context dependence of pre- and post-selected measurements](context-dependence-of-pre-and-post-selected-measurements.md) explains why these alternative certainties do not imply simultaneous properties.
