# Delay-time distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar population](stellar-population.md)

The event rate per unit initial stellar mass and per unit delay after [star formation](star-formation.md). An extended [star formation history](star-formation-history.md) produces a rate $R(t)=\int\mathrm{SFR}(t-\tau)\Psi(\tau)d\tau$. Measuring rates versus age in old populations constrains progenitor channels only after accounting for stellar lifetimes, age spreads and selection.
