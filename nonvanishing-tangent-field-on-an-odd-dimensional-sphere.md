# Nonvanishing tangent field on an odd-dimensional sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Nowhere-zero section](nowhere-zero-section.md)

Identify $\mathbb R^{2m+2}$ with $\mathbb C^{m+1}$ and let $J$ be multiplication by $i$. On the unit sphere $S^{2m+1}$, the [vector field](vector-field.md) $V(x)=Jx$ has norm one and satisfies $\langle x,Jx\rangle=0$. It is therefore a continuous tangent [nowhere-zero section](nowhere-zero-section.md), including on $S^1$.
