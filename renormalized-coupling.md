# Renormalized coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization condition](renormalization-condition.md)

A [renormalized coupling](renormalized-coupling.md) is an interaction parameter defined by a specified finite [renormalization condition](renormalization-condition.md) or subtraction scheme at a scale $\mu$. Observables remain independent of that arbitrary scale when its running and the loop corrections are included consistently. The coupling itself, and higher-order coefficients in its [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md), can depend on the scheme; they should not be identified without qualification with a regulator-independent bare coefficient.
