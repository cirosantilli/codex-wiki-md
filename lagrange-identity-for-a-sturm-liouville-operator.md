# Lagrange identity for a Sturm-Liouville operator

↑ **Parent:** [Sturm-Liouville operator](sturm-liouville-operator.md)

For $L(v)=(pv')'+av$, direct differentiation gives $uL(v)-vL(u)=[p(uv'-vu')]'$. The potential terms cancel. Integrating with common homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) eliminates the endpoint term. If $L(u)=0$ and $L(v)=f$, this gives $\int uf=0$, a necessary [solvability condition](solvability-condition.md) for the inhomogeneous equation.
