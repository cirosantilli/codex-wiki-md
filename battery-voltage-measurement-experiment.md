# Battery voltage measurement experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Electric battery](electric-battery.md)

The institutional handout [Measurement of Emf Using a Potentiometer, Oklahoma Christian University laboratory](measurement-of-emf-using-a-potentiometer-oklahoma-christian-university-laboratory.md) (Experimental Activity: calibration procedure, measuring a test cell, and measuring internal resistance of a cell) compares unloaded and loaded [battery](electric-battery.md) readings. Its apparatus uses a slidewire [potentiometer](potentiometer.md), calibrated resistance coils, a reference cell, a [galvanometer](galvanometer.md), a nominal $1.5\,\mathrm V$ flashlight cell, a [voltmeter](voltmeter.md), and a switched variable [resistor](resistor.md) branch with an [ammeter](ammeter.md). Calibration uses the reference cell's tagged [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md); zero [galvanometer](galvanometer.md) deflection establishes a [null measurement](null-measurement.md). With the load branch open, the null reading draws no test-cell [electric current](electric-current.md). The protocol compares newer and older cells using both instruments, then records simultaneous loaded terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) and [electric current](electric-current.md). It prescribes brief measurements, opening the load switch between readings, and periodic calibration rechecks. These are documented procedures, not run data.[Experimental Activity, calibration procedure and measuring a test cell; measuring internal resistance, side branch K-A-R and simultaneous readings](measurement-of-emf-using-a-potentiometer-oklahoma-christian-university-laboratory.md)

For the [battery internal resistance](battery-internal-resistance.md) model, $V_L=\mathcal E-rI$. The handout's analysis estimates [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) from the intercept and $r$ from the negative slope, then compares the intercept with the independent null reading.[Measuring internal resistance of a cell, final analysis paragraphs: line intercept/slope and comparison with the earlier emf measurement](measurement-of-emf-using-a-potentiometer-oklahoma-christian-university-laboratory.md)

Additional replication controls are to repeat the open-load reading after the sweep, return to an earlier load setting to detect drift, and record [temperature](temperature-split.md), elapsed time, instrument resolution and [measurement error](measurement-error.md). Include [ammeter](ammeter.md) and contact [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) in the external path; allow for [voltmeter loading](voltmeter-loading.md) if a [voltmeter](voltmeter.md) replaces the null instrument. The [Thévenin measurement experiment](thevenin-measurement-experiment.md) gives the corresponding meter-loading correction. Curvature or time-dependent readings can reject the constant-$r$ approximation, but a terminal curve alone does not identify which internal transport or [electrode](electrode.md) process caused the deviation. These extra controls and interpretations are model-based extensions, not reported outcomes of the handout.
