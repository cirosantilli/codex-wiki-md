# Rational polynomial differential forms

↑ **Parent:** [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md)

For the standard $n$-simplex use $\Omega_n=\mathbb Q[t_0,\ldots,t_n,dt_0,\ldots,dt_n]/(\sum t_i-1,\sum dt_i)$, with $|t_i|=0$, $|dt_i|=1$, and $d(t_i)=dt_i$. Compatible families of these forms on the simplices of a simplicial model of $X$ form the commutative [differential graded algebra](differential-graded-algebra.md) $A_{\mathrm{PL}}(X)$. Its [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) is $H^*(X;\mathbb Q)$: polynomial contraction proves the simplex Poincaré lemma, and extension across faces gives the simplicial de Rham comparison. The contravariant functor takes simplicial pushouts to pullbacks of algebras.
