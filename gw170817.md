# GW170817

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron star merger](neutron-star-merger.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GW170817)

The 17 August 2017 binary-neutron-star merger detected through [gravitational waves](gravitational-wave-split.md), with a short [gamma-ray burst](gamma-ray-burst.md) and a multiwavelength counterpart including a [kilonova](kilonova.md). Its joint observations connected compact-binary inspiral, relativistic outflows and heavy-element-rich ejecta.
