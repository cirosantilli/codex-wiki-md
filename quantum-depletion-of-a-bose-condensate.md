# Quantum depletion of a Bose condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md)

The [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) vacuum contains physical particles at nonzero momentum because the [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) mixes creation and [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md). Their occupation is $v_k^2=(A_k/E_k-1)/2$, and the depletion density is its momentum sum divided by the volume. At nonzero temperature an additional $(A_k/E_k)n_B(E_k)$ contribution is required; quantum depletion alone is a zero-temperature expression.
