# Centrosome orientation checkpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)

A mechanism delaying mitotic entry when interphase centrosomes are misoriented relative to the niche in fly male [germline stem cells](germline-stem-cell.md). It couples geometry to the timing of an oriented division.
