# Unramified division torsors at good primes

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer exact sequence of an elliptic curve](kummer-exact-sequence-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

At a prime of [good reduction of an elliptic curve](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) with residue characteristic not dividing $m$, multiplication by $m$ extends to a finite étale map of the smooth proper elliptic group scheme over the valuation ring. A local rational point extends to a section by properness. Its division fibre is therefore a finite étale torsor, so the [Kummer map of an elliptic curve](kummer-map-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) gives an unramified cohomology class. This bounds ramification in the [Weak Mordell-Weil theorem](weak-mordell-weil-theorem.md).

A direct proof describes the coordinates, not only the cohomology class. Reduce $P$ by properness and choose a point $\overline Q$ with $[m]\overline Q=\widetilde P$ over a finite extension of the [residue field](residue-field.md). Over the corresponding [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of the complete [local field](local-field.md), lift $\overline Q$ by the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). The error $[m]Q_0-P$ is in the [kernel of reduction of an elliptic curve](kernel-of-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md); [prime-to-residue-characteristic multiplication on a formal group](prime-to-residue-characteristic-multiplication-on-a-formal-group.md) corrects it uniquely. There is no $m$-torsion in that kernel, even after a ramified field extension, so every division point with that reduction equals this unramified lift. Consequently all [division points of an elliptic curve](division-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) above $P$ have unramified coordinate fields. The torsion case is discussed in [J. S. Milne's Elliptic Curves, Chapter V, Proposition 8.1](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf).
