# Adjoint of an integration operator anchored at zero

↑ **Parent:** [Volterra operator](volterra-operator.md)

On $L^2(-a,a)$, the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) is $T^*g(s)=\int_s^ag(t)dt$ for $s>0$, and $T^*g(s)=-\int_{-a}^sg(t)dt$ for $s<0$. These formulas follow by reversing the triangular integration order separately on the two half-intervals. Consequently $(T+T^*)g$ is constant on each half, with values $\int_0^ag$ and $-\int_{-a}^0g$. Applying this adjoint to a real weight transfers a weighted integral of an anchored function to an integral against its derivative.
