# Simply periodic function

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic function](periodic-function.md)

A nonconstant [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) on the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) is simply periodic when its group of periods is generated by one nonzero complex number. It is distinct from a doubly periodic [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md), whose [period lattice](period-lattice.md) has two real-linearly independent generators. Single periodicity alone imposes no algebraic addition law.
