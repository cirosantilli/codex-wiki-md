# Optimal time and gain of a Reynolds-scaled triangular model

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional triangular model of transient growth](two-dimensional-triangular-model-of-transient-growth.md)

For $L=\begin{pmatrix}-1/R&1\\0&-1/(4R)\end{pmatrix}$ with $R>1$, put $s=\sqrt{1-R^{-2}}$ and $q=(5-3s)/(5+3s)$. The [optimal energy amplification of a linear system](optimal-energy-amplification-of-a-linear-system.md) reaches its maximum at

$$
T^*=-\frac{4R}{3}\log q,\qquad G_{\max}=\frac{1+s}{1-s}q^{5/3}.
$$

The maximizing initial orientation is $\rho^+$ and the final orientation is $\rho^-$ from the [orientation interval for transient energy growth](orientation-interval-for-transient-energy-growth.md). Taking $R\to\infty$ gives the displayed viscous-time and Reynolds-squared asymptotics. These constants belong to this specified triangular model, not to every [non-normal matrix](non-normal-matrix.md).
