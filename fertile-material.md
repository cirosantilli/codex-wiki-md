# Fertile material

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fertile_material)

Material convertible into a [fissile material](fissile-material.md) by [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md) followed by nuclear transformations. A central example is [uranium-238](uranium-238.md) capturing a neutron and undergoing two [beta-minus decays](beta-minus-decay.md) to [plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md). Fertile does not mean that thermal-neutron capture immediately causes fission.
