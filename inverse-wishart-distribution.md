# Inverse-Wishart distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Wishart distribution](wishart-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse-Wishart_distribution)

If $W\sim W_p(\nu,R^{-1})$ has the [Wishart distribution](wishart-distribution.md), then $W^{-1}$ has the inverse-Wishart distribution. For $\nu>p+1$, its [expected value](expected-value.md) is $R/(\nu-p-1)$; below this threshold the matrix mean does not exist. It commonly models a [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md), but couples the prior behavior of variances and correlations.
