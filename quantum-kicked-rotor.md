# Quantum kicked rotor

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum rotor](quantum-rotor.md)

A [particle](particle-physics.md) with a periodic angular coordinate undergoing free rotational motion interrupted by impulsive angular potentials. A single-period unitary combines free propagation and a position-dependent kick. Its momentum-space [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) can give resonant ballistic growth or other transport regimes.
