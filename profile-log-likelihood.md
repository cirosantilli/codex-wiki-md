# Profile log-likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Profile likelihood](profile-likelihood.md)

The logarithm of the [profile likelihood](profile-likelihood.md) is the full [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md) evaluated at the nuisance fit constrained to the specified parameter of interest. It preserves [likelihood ratios](likelihood-ratio.md) and maximizers, but is not generally a normalized sampling log-density. Conditioning and integrating out [nuisance parameters](nuisance-parameter.md) are different operations.
