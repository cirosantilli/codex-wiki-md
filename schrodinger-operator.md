<h1 id="schrodinger-operator">Schrödinger operator</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md)

A Schrödinger operator is a differential operator of the form

$$
H=-a\Delta+V,
\qquad a>0,
$$

where $\Delta$ is the [Laplace operator](laplace-operator.md) and $V$ is a [potential function](potential-function.md). Its [eigenvalue problem](eigenvalue-problem.md) is the [Time-independent Schrödinger equation](time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md).

A [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) of this form with $a=\hbar^2/(2m)$ supplies the [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) of a particle in the potential $V$.
