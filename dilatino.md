# Dilatino

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

A dilatino is a spin-one-half fermionic partner in a [supergravity](supergravity.md) theory containing a [dilaton](dilaton.md). Type IIA and type IIB theories each contain two Majorana-Weyl dilatini with eight physical polarizations apiece. Their chirality assignments differ between the two theories.
