# Fitting subgroup is self-centralizing in soluble groups

↑ **Parent:** [Fitting subgroup](fitting-subgroup.md)

In a finite [soluble group](solvable-group.md), $C_G(F(G))\le F(G)$. Put $C=C_G(F)$ and $D=C\cap F=Z(F)$. If $C>D$, choose an elementary abelian [minimal normal subgroup](minimal-normal-subgroup.md) of $G/D$ inside $C/D$, and let $K$ be its preimage. Then $K'\le D\le Z(K)$, so $K$ is a [nilpotent normal subgroup](nilpotent-normal-subgroup.md), contradicting $K\not\le F$.
