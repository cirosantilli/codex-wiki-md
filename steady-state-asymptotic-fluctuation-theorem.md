# Steady-state asymptotic fluctuation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Fluctuation theorem](fluctuation-theorem.md)

A [steady state](steady-state.md) does not by itself give every fluctuating observable an [entropy production](entropy-production.md) symmetry. If the dimensionless total [entropy production](entropy-production.md) $\Sigma_t$ has the same-law finite relation and a [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) with [rate function](rate-function.md) $I$ for $\Sigma_t/t$, then, on its common domain,

$$
p_t(\Sigma_t/t\approx s)\asymp e^{-tI(s)},\qquad
I(-s)-I(s)=s.
$$

The first expression denotes leading exponential scaling; the second follows by taking the scaled [logarithm](logarithm.md) of the probability ratio. A finite-state, autonomous [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) with time-even states, positive bidirectional rates and a positive [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) supplies a standard setting for a finite total-[entropy production](entropy-production.md) relation. The stochastic system [entropy](entropy.md) is then bounded, so its endpoint contribution does not change the long-time [rate function](rate-function.md) of bath [entropy production](entropy-production.md). [Seifert, 2012, §3.3.2, p. 22, Eq. (65); §4.4.3, p. 28, Eqs. (93)–(95): finite stationary total entropy and endpoint-conditioned medium entropy](stochastic-thermodynamics-fluctuation-theorems-and-molecular-machines-by-udo-seifert.md)

Unbounded state spaces can have endpoint fluctuations on the scale relevant to a [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md). Dropping system [entropy](entropy.md), changing the measured observable to [heat](heat.md), or changing the reverse preparation therefore requires a fresh argument. The exact finite [integral fluctuation theorem](integral-fluctuation-theorem.md), a same-law finite relation, and an asymptotic [rate function](rate-function.md) symmetry are distinct claims.
