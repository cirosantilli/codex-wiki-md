# Jacobi integral

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

For a mechanical system whose imposed motion is uniform rotation with [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega$ about a fixed axis, a conserved rotating-frame [energy](energy-split.md) can exist even when the inertial [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) $E$ changes. With [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $L_z$ about that axis, it is $J=E-\omega L_z$. For a bead constrained to rotate with $\dot\theta=\omega$, this becomes

$$
J=\frac m2(\dot r^2+\dot z^2)+V-\frac m2\omega^2r^2.
$$

For $z=r^2/(2a)$ in uniform [gravity](gravity.md), division by [mass](mass.md) yields

$$
\frac Jm=\frac12\left(1+\frac{r^2}{a^2}\right)\dot r^2+\frac12\left(\frac ga-\omega^2\right)r^2.
$$

This is the [first integral](first-integral.md) that controls the bead's radial motion. Different normalizations and signs are used for the related Jacobi constant in celestial mechanics.
