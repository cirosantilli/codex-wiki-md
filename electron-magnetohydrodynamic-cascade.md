# Electron-magnetohydrodynamic cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic turbulence](magnetohydrodynamic-turbulence.md)

An electron-magnetohydrodynamic cascade transfers magnetic fluctuations through the nonlinear [electron magnetohydrodynamics](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md) induction equation. Writing $b_l=\delta B_l/B_0$, its perpendicular nonlinear interaction time is $\tau_{\mathrm{nl}}\sim l_\perp^2/(v_Ad_i b_l)$. The anisotropic [whistler wave](whistler-wave.md) time is $\tau_w\sim l_\parallel l_\perp/(v_Ad_i)$. Scaling predictions depend on locality, phase decorrelation and the assumed balance of these times.
