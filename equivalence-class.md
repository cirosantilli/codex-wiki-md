# Equivalence class

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivalence_class)

The equivalence class of an element $x$ under an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) $R$ is the [set](set-split.md) of all elements related to $x$. Two such [sets](set-split.md) are equal or disjoint, and together they partition the original [set](set-split.md).
