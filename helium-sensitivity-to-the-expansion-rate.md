# Helium sensitivity to the expansion rate

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron-limited helium synthesis](neutron-limited-helium-synthesis.md)

During radiation domination, the weak conversion rate scales as $T^5$ whereas the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) scales as $\sqrt{Gg_*}\,T^2$. Their crossing fixes the [cosmological weak freeze-out](cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md) temperature. A faster expansion raises it, leaving a larger [neutron-to-proton ratio](neutron-to-proton-ratio.md), and shortens the interval for [free-neutron decay after freeze-out](free-neutron-decay-after-freeze-out.md). Both effects increase the helium-4 mass fraction if the extra component does not independently change reaction rates or inject decay products.
