# Fault shear stress from a plate-integrated force

↑ **Parent:** [Shear stress](shear-stress.md)

A horizontally compressive [force](force-split.md) per width $F$, carried uniformly through thickness $a$, gives additional horizontal [stress](stress.md) $F/a$. Resolving its [traction](traction.md) on a [fault](geological-fault.md) dipping at angle $\delta$ gives the displayed mean [shear stress](shear-stress.md). An isotropic background [pressure](pressure-split.md) adds only normal [traction](traction.md). The integrated [force](force-split.md) alone does not determine the actual depth-dependent [stress](stress.md) distribution or whether a [fault](geological-fault.md) fails.
