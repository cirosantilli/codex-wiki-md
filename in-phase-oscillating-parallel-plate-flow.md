# In-phase oscillating parallel-plate flow

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)

For incompressible unidirectional flow between $y=\pm d$ with both walls moving at $U\cos\Omega t$ and no pressure gradient, the [Navier-Stokes equations](navier-stokes-equation.md) reduce to $\rho u_t=\mu u_{yy}$. The periodic complex amplitude satisfies $f''=i\rho\Omega f/\mu$, $f(\pm d)=1$, giving $f=\cosh(qy)/\cosh(qd)$ with $q=(1+i)\sqrt{\rho\Omega/(2\mu)}$. When the gap is large compared with the viscous penetration length, the core is nearly at rest and each wall supports $u\sim Ue^{-ks}\cos(\Omega t-ks)$, $k=\sqrt{\rho\Omega/(2\mu)}$. Without initial data this specifies the periodic response; decaying zero-wall transients may be superposed.
