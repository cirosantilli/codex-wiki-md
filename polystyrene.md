# Polystyrene

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polystyrene)

[Polystyrene](polystyrene.md) is a [polymer](polymer-split.md) used as a calibration-standard family in organic-solvent [size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md). Its [hydrodynamic size](hydrodynamic-size.md) in a specified [solvent](solvent.md) supplies a conditional [SEC molar mass calibration](sec-molar-mass-calibration.md) for other dissolved chains.
