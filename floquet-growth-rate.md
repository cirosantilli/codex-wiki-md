# Floquet growth rate

↑ **Parent:** [Floquet theory](floquet-theory.md)

The maximal asymptotic growth rate of a finite-dimensional periodic [linear differential equation](linear-differential-equation.md) is $P^{-1}\log\rho(N)$, where $P$ is the coefficient period and $N$ the [monodromy matrix of a periodic linear system](monodromy-matrix-of-a-periodic-linear-system.md). The [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md) selects the dominant [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md). Generic initial data attain this rate; data lying entirely in other invariant subspaces can grow or decay at different rates.
