# Kolmogorov equations

↑ **Parent:** [Transition semigroup of a continuous-time Markov chain](transition-semigroup-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolmogorov_equations)

For a finite-state [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) with [Q-matrix](transition-rate-matrix.md) $Q$, the backward and forward equations are $P'(t)=QP(t)$ and $P'(t)=P(t)Q$, respectively.
