# Suspension isomorphism for top de Rham cohomology of spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Mayer--Vietoris sequence for de Rham cohomology](mayer-vietoris-sequence-for-de-rham-cohomology.md)

Cover the [sphere](sphere.md) by complements of its two poles. Each open set is contractible, and their intersection deformation retracts to the equatorial [sphere](sphere.md). The [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) and [homotopy invariance of de Rham cohomology](homotopy-invariance-of-de-rham-cohomology.md) make the two neighboring groups in the [Mayer--Vietoris sequence for de Rham cohomology](mayer-vietoris-sequence-for-de-rham-cohomology.md) zero, so its connecting map is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). Its inverse takes a top-degree form to the difference of its local primitives on the equator, up to the connecting-map sign convention.
