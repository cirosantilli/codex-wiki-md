# Compound Atwood machine

↑ **Parent:** [Atwood machine](atwood-machine.md)

A [compound Atwood machine](compound-atwood-machine.md) replaces one hanging [mass](mass.md) by another [pulley](pulley.md) carrying two [masses](mass.md). Let the upper [mechanical string](mechanical-string.md) carry [mass](mass.md) $M$ opposite the moving [pulley](pulley.md), and let the lower [mechanical string](mechanical-string.md) carry [masses](mass.md) $a,b$ at upward heights $x,y$. The length constraints on the [mechanical strings](mechanical-string.md) give the height of $M$ as $-(x+y)/2$ up to a constant. Thus its [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is

$$
\mathcal L=\frac M8(\dot x+\dot y)^2+\frac a2\dot x^2+\frac b2\dot y^2-g\left[\left(a-\frac M2\right)x+\left(b-\frac M2\right)y\right].
$$

This follows by adding the three [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md) and subtracting their [gravitational potential energies](gravitational-energy.md). Constant coordinate shifts annihilating the linear [potential energy](potential-energy.md) give conserved [generalized momentum](generalized-momentum.md) components by [Noether's theorem](noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md).
