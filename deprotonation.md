# Deprotonation

↑ **Parent:** [Acid-base chemistry](acid-base-chemistry-split.md)

[Deprotonation](deprotonation.md) removes a [proton](proton.md) from a [chemical species](chemical-species.md), changing its [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) by $-1$. It can expose a donor [lone pair](lone-pair.md) or change donor charge without changing the metal [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md).
