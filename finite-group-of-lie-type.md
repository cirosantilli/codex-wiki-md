# Finite group of Lie type

↑ **Parent:** [Reductive group](reductive-group.md)

A finite group of Lie type is obtained from rational points of a connected [reductive algebraic group](reductive-group.md) with a suitable [Frobenius endomorphism of an algebraic group](frobenius-endomorphism-of-an-algebraic-group.md), often followed by central quotient or passage to a derived subgroup. Its complex representations combine finite-group [character theory](character-theory.md) with the geometry of [Borel subgroups](borel-subgroup.md), [maximal algebraic tori](maximal-algebraic-torus.md), and the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md).
