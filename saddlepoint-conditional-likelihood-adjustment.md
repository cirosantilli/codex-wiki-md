# Saddlepoint conditional likelihood adjustment

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional likelihood](conditional-likelihood.md)

For a regular two-parameter [exponential family](exponential-family-split.md), condition on the [sample mean](sample-mean.md) of the nuisance canonical statistic. [Exponential tilting](exponential-tilting.md) to its observed value and the [saddlepoint density approximation](saddlepoint-density-approximation.md) give a nuisance-statistic density proportional to $d_{\lambda\lambda}^{-1/2}$ times its large-deviation exponential. Dividing the joint [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) by this density gives the [profile log-likelihood](profile-log-likelihood.md) plus the displayed adjustment, up to a data-only constant. The sign is positive. This construction assumes an interior saddle with positive curvature and a sufficiently regular nonlattice density; conditioning on a lattice statistic requires the corresponding mass approximation.
