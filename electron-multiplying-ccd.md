# Electron-multiplying CCD

↑ **Parent:** [Charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md)

An electron-multiplying CCD adds a high-field multiplication register before the output amplifier. [Impact ionization](impact-ionization.md) generates a large mean gain, reducing input-referred [read noise](read-noise.md) but adding [multiplication excess noise](multiplication-excess-noise.md).
