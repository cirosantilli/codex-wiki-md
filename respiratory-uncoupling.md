# Respiratory uncoupling

↑ **Parent:** [Oxidative phosphorylation](oxidative-phosphorylation.md)

[Respiratory uncoupling](respiratory-uncoupling.md) allows [proton](proton.md) return to bypass [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md), dissipating the [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md). [Electron](electron.md) transport and [oxygen](oxygen.md) consumption can continue while less [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) is recovered.[Chapter 14, Electron-Transport Chains and Their Proton Pumps, H+ Ionophores Uncouple Electron Transport from ATP Synthesis](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

In the thermodynamic example under [chemiosmosis](chemiosmosis.md), reducing both the activity ratio to one and the [electric potential](electric-potential.md) difference to zero removes the driving contribution. The hypothetical [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md)-forming conversion then costs $+45\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. Continued donor [oxidation](oxidation.md) would not restore coupling unless it rebuilt a usable [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md). This distinction makes [respiratory uncoupling](respiratory-uncoupling.md) useful conceptually: abundant [oxygen](oxygen.md) consumption can coexist with poor recovery of [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md).
