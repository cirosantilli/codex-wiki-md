# Induced magnetization

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md)

The [magnetization](magnetization.md) caused by an applied [magnetic field strength](magnetic-field-strength.md). The displayed scalar linear law assumes [isotropic](isotropy.md) susceptibility and neglects magnetic self-interaction; [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md) is an additional component that need not align with the present field.
