# Normal shock wave

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

A normal shock is a discontinuity perpendicular to the flow direction across which mass, momentum, and total energy fluxes are conserved.

Property ratios for this discontinuity are tabulated in [normal shock tables](normal-shock-tables.md).
