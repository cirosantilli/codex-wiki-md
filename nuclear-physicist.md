## William Bertozzi

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physicist](nuclear-physicist.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Physicist](physicist.md)

An experimental nuclear physicist whose [electron](physics.md#electron) speed-and-energy measurement provides a direct test of relativistic kinematics. [Bertozzi's electron speed-energy experiment](special-relativity.md#bertozzi-s-electron-speed-energy-experiment) measures flight time and calorimetric [energy](energy.md) independently.

Official profile: [https://physics.mit.edu/faculty/william-bertozzi/](https://physics.mit.edu/faculty/william-bertozzi/)

## Lise Meitner

🏷️ **Tags:** [Physicist](physicist.md)

[Lise Meitner](#lise-meitner) and [Otto Robert Frisch](#otto-robert-frisch) interpreted the energy release associated with the chemical findings of [Otto Hahn](chemistry.md#otto-hahn) and [Fritz Strassmann](chemistry.md#fritz-strassmann).
