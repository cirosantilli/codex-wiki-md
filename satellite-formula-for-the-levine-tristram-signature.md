# Satellite formula for the Levine-Tristram signature

↑ **Parent:** [Satellite knot](satellite-knot.md)

For a pattern of winding number $w$,

$$
\sigma_\omega(P(K))=\sigma_\omega(P(U))+\sigma_{\omega^w}(K)
$$

whenever the relevant [signatures](levine-tristram-signature.md) are defined by nonsingular forms. The usual averaged convention extends the identity through roots.
