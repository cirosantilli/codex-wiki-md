# Phosphine oxide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

An $\mathrm{R_3PO}$ compound with approximately tetrahedral [phosphorus](phosphorus.md). Its short, strongly polar P–O bond can be described using ionic contributions and [oxygen](oxygen.md) lone-pair donation into P–R antibonding orbitals, without requiring large [phosphorus](phosphorus.md) 3d participation.
