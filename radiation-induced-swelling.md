# Radiation-induced swelling

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation damage in materials](radiation-damage-in-materials.md)

Volume increase caused by irradiation-induced defects and microstructure changes. Accumulating [helium](helium.md) and defect clusters can contribute; [radiation-induced swelling](radiation-induced-swelling.md) is not identical to [radiation-induced amorphization](radiation-induced-amorphization.md).
