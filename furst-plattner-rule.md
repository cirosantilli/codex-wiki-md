<h1 id="furst-plattner-rule">Fürst-Plattner rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Diastereoselectivity](diastereoselectivity.md)

The [Fürst-Plattner rule](furst-plattner-rule.md) describes a preference for trans-diaxial opening of suitable bridged intermediates on six-membered rings. A chair-like pathway generally avoids the unfavorable geometry of a competing twist/boat pathway; substrate fusion and substitution determine which route is accessible.
