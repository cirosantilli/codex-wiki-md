# Linear independence of nonzero orthogonal vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal vectors](orthogonal-vectors.md)

A finite collection of nonzero pairwise [orthogonal vectors](orthogonal-vectors.md) is [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). Given $\sum_i a_iq_i=0$, take the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) with $q_j$ to obtain $a_j\lVert q_j\rVert^2=0$. Positive definiteness and $q_j\ne0$ force $a_j=0$ for every index. The nonzero condition matters: adjoining a [zero vector](zero-vector.md) preserves pairwise orthogonality but destroys [linear independence](linear-independence.md).
