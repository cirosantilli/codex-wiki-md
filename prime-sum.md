# Prime sum

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic number theory](analytic-number-theory-split.md)

A prime sum is a [summation](summation.md) indexed by [prime numbers](prime-number.md), for example $\sum_{p\leq x}1/p$. The indexing restriction distinguishes it from a sum over all [positive integers](positive-integer.md).
