# Score equation for a phase-averaged linkage likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Phase averaging in a linkage likelihood](phase-averaging-in-a-linkage-likelihood.md)

For a known-phase contribution $L_1(\theta)\propto\theta^{r_1}(1-\theta)^{m_1-r_1}$ and an independent phase-averaged contribution $L_2(\theta)$, an interior maximum satisfies

$$
[r_1-m_1\theta]L_2(\theta)+\theta(1-\theta)L_2'(\theta)=0.
$$

This follows by differentiating $\log L_1+\log L_2$ and multiplying by $\theta(1-\theta)L_2$. The constrained [maximum-likelihood estimate](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) on $[0,1/2]$ must also be compared with the endpoints. An interior root cannot be asserted without the observed pedigree counts; complete nonrecombinant data, for example, have their maximum at zero.
