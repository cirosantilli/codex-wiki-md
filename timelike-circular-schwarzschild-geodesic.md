# Timelike circular Schwarzschild geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md)

For the [Schwarzschild metric](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md) with $\mu=GM/c^2$, let $k=(1-2\mu/r)\dot t$ and $h=r^2\dot\phi$ on an equatorial [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md). The normalization of the [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) gives $\dot r^2+V(r)=c^2(k^2-1)$, where $V(r)=(1-2\mu/r)h^2/r^2-2\mu c^2/r$. A circular [geodesic](geodesic.md) obeys $V'(R)=0$, giving the displayed constants and requiring $R>3\mu$. Its coordinate [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) is $\Omega^2=GM/R^3$, and its proper clock rate is $d\tau/dt=\sqrt{1-3\mu/R}$. Differentiating $V$ twice at the circular solution gives stability for $R>6\mu$.
