# Divisor line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

On an [integral scheme](integral-scheme.md), for a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) locally represented by $a_i\in K^*$, its divisor line bundle is the subsheaf of rational functions locally equal to $a_i^{-1}\mathcal O_X$. Unit ratios glue these free rank-one modules. Given a nonzero [rational section of a line bundle](rational-section-of-a-line-bundle.md) $s=a_ie_i$, the maps $e_i\mapsto a_i^{-1}$ identify that [invertible sheaf](line-bundle.md) with $\mathcal O_X(D)$. Replacing $s$ by a nonzero rational multiple changes $D$ by a [principal Cartier divisor](principal-cartier-divisor.md).
