# Minimum-mass solar nebula

↑ **Parent:** [Planet formation](planet-formation.md)

The minimum-mass solar nebula estimates a primordial gas-and-solid [protoplanetary disk](protoplanetary-disk.md) by spreading planetary material into annuli and restoring a solar mixture. A common idealized [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) is proportional to $r^{-3/2}$. This reconstruction is a model of minimum material requirements, not a unique history of the Solar System. Extrapolating it far beyond the planetary region need not give a physically consistent disk.
