# James submodule theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

Let $U$ be a submodule of the Young permutation module $M^\lambda$ over any field. Then either

$$
S^\lambda\subseteq U
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
U\subseteq(S^\lambda)^\perp
$$

for the [tabloid bilinear form](tabloid-bilinear-form.md). The key identity is $b_tu=\langle u,e(t)\rangle e(t)$: if one pairing is nonzero, the cyclic generator $e(t)$ and hence the whole Specht module lies in $U$.
