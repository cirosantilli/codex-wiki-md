# Elastic stress concentration in a pressurized thick cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Plane strain](plane-strain.md)

For an isotropic elastic [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) $a<r<b$ with internal excess pressure $P$ and zero outer gauge pressure, radial [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) gives $u=Cr+D/r$. Its [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) has $\tau_{rr}=2(\lambda+\mu)C-2\mu D/r^2$ and $\tau_{\theta\theta}=2(\lambda+\mu)C+2\mu D/r^2$. Enforcing $\tau_{rr}(a)=-P$, $\tau_{rr}(b)=0$ gives $2\mu D=Pa^2b^2/(b^2-a^2)$, so the maximum difference occurs at the bore and equals the displayed expression. Once an appropriate [yield criterion](yield-criterion.md) is reached, [stress](stress.md) must be related to [elastic strain](elastic-strain.md) while total [strain](strain.md) includes a plastic contribution. The elastic formula alone cannot describe further irreversible deformation.
