# Topology determines norm equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalent norms](equivalent-norms.md)

Two [norms](norm.md) $p,q$ on a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) give the same [topology](topology-split.md) if and only if $c p(x)\le q(x)\le C p(x)$ for some positive constants. Indeed, continuity of the identity at zero gives $p(x)<\delta\Rightarrow q(x)<1$. For $x\ne0$, apply this to $\delta x/(2p(x))$ to obtain $q(x)<2p(x)/\delta$. Reverse the roles of the [norms](norm.md) for the lower bound. Conversely the two inequalities compare every translated [open ball](open-ball.md). This proof uses [absolute homogeneity of a norm](absolute-homogeneity-of-a-norm.md), not finite dimension.
