# Tree rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Binary tree](binary-tree.md)

A local change of root and child pointers that preserves inorder order. A [right rotation](right-tree-rotation.md) promotes a left child, moves that child's right [subtree](subtree.md) to the old root's left position, and makes the old root its right child. The five inorder pieces remain in the same order; a left rotation reverses this transformation. Rotation does not by itself require search-tree key ordering.
