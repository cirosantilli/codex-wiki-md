# Topological generator of the additive group of p-adic integers

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic integer](p-adic-integer.md)

An element $\alpha\in\mathbb Z_p$ topologically generates the additive group of $\mathbb Z_p$ exactly when $\alpha\not\equiv0\pmod p$, equivalently when $\alpha$ is a [p-adic unit](p-adic-unit.md). In that case its image generates every cyclic quotient $\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z$.
