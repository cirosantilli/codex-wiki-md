# Rotating-flow pressure potential

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating-frame effective potential](rotating-frame-effective-potential.md)

For a uniformly rotating incompressible fluid of constant [mass density](density.md), [pressure](pressure.md) and conservative body forces combine as the gradient of this scalar. The centrifugal term has a negative sign. [Pressure](pressure.md) enforces [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), whereas the velocity-dependent [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) remains explicit and cannot be included in a scalar position potential. Adding a spatially constant function of time to $\psi$ does not change the motion.
