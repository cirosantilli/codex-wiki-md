# Filter (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filter_(mathematics))

A filter is a nonempty upward-closed [subset](subset.md) $G$ of a [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md) such that every two members have a common lower bound in $G$. With [standard notation for forcing](standard-notation-for-forcing.md), lower means stronger, so these are exactly the directed filters used to define a [generic filter](generic-filter.md). The [projection of a product-generic filter](projection-of-a-product-generic-filter.md) uses these properties before genericity of its factor filters is proved.
