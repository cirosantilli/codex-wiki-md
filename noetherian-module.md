# Noetherian module

↑ **Parent:** [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noetherian_module)

An $R$-module is Noetherian when every [submodule](submodule.md) is finitely generated, equivalently when every ascending chain of submodules stabilizes. Quotients and finite direct sums of Noetherian modules are Noetherian.
