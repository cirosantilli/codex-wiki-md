# Symmetric stochastic attrition process

↑ **Parent:** [Markov jump process](markov-jump-process.md)

This finite-state [Markov jump process](markov-jump-process.md) models two opposing populations, each suffering losses at a rate proportional to the surviving opposing population. Both coordinate axes are absorbing. Its embedded [jump chain](jump-chain.md) removes one individual at each step, with probabilities $b/(a+b)$ and $a/(a+b)$. The corresponding deterministic equations $\dot a=-\kappa b$, $\dot b=-\kappa a$ conserve $a^2-b^2$; finite stochastic populations satisfy a near-invariant instead.
