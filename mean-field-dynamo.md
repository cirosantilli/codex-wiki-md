# Mean-field dynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamo action](dynamo-action.md)

A mean-field dynamo evolves a slowly varying spatially averaged [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). Correlations of fluctuating velocity and magnetic field supply a [mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md). Its local linear response includes the [alpha effect](alpha-effect.md); gradients of the mean field can contribute additional transport terms. The distinction between a uniform test field used to measure a response coefficient and a spatially varying mean field used to obtain growth is essential.
