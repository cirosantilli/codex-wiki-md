# Left-endpoint approximation of a continuous semimartingale integral

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md)

For a left-continuous locally bounded [adapted process](adapted-process.md) $H$ and a [continuous semimartingale](continuous-semimartingale.md) $X$, the elementary predictable left-endpoint approximations tend pointwise to $H$. Decompose $X$ into a [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) and a continuous [finite-variation process](finite-variation-process.md). After localization, dominated convergence with respect to the variation measure handles the finite-variation integral. The [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) and [Doob L2 maximal inequality](doob-l2-maximal-inequality.md) handle the martingale integral. The omitted partial last interval is bounded by the local bound on $H$ times the modulus of continuity of $X$. This proves [uniform convergence on compacts in probability](uniform-convergence-on-compacts-in-probability.md) and is the grid-sum foundation of the [Itô product rule](ito-product-rule.md).
