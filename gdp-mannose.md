# GDP-mannose

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide sugar](nucleotide-sugar.md)

[GDP-mannose](gdp-mannose.md) is an activated donor containing a mannose residue linked to [GDP](guanosine-diphosphate.md). Transfer requires an appropriate [glycosyltransferase](glycosyltransferase.md) and acceptor; access to the correct compartment can be limiting.
