# Iterated sumset

↑ **Parent:** [Sumset](sumset.md)

For a [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) $m$, the iterated sumset $mA$ is the sum of $m$ copies of $A$, with $0A=\{0\}$. Repetitions are allowed, so its elements are sums $a_1+\cdots+a_m$ with each $a_i\in A$.
