# Planck-suppressed corrections to inflaton potentials

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md)

In an [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md), higher-power terms in an [inflaton](inflaton.md) [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) can be important when $\phi/M_{\rm Pl}$ is large, even if $V/M_{\rm Pl}^4$ is small. An assumed simple [monomial inflation potential](monomial-inflation-potential.md) over such a field range needs a [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) or [ultraviolet completion](ultraviolet-completion.md) controlling these operators and [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md). A large field value alone does not establish Planckian [energy density](energy-density.md) or invalidate every possible completion.
