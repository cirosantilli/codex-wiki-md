# Pauli matrix completeness identity

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli matrix multiplication law](pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md)

The identity follows because the identity matrix and the three [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) form an orthogonal basis of two-by-two matrices under the trace inner product. It rewrites a spin-vector bilinear square as a scalar four-fermion expression, with the Grassmann signs supplied when fields are reordered. This is the algebra behind a [spin-vector Hubbard–Stratonovich decoupling](spin-vector-hubbard-stratonovich-decoupling.md).
