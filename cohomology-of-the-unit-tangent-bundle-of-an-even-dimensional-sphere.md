# Cohomology of the unit tangent bundle of an even-dimensional sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Unit tangent bundle](unit-tangent-bundle.md)

The Euler class of $TS^{2n}$ evaluates to $2$. Its [Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) gives

$$
H^q(STS^{2n};\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&q=0,4n-1,\\
\mathbb Z/2,&q=2n,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

All products of positive-degree classes vanish.
