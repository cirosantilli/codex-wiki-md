# Doppler dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md)

An observer moving through isotropic [blackbody radiation](black-body-radiation.md) sees a direction-dependent [temperature](temperature-split.md). With the sky angle measured toward the velocity apex in the observer frame, $T(\theta)=T_0/[\gamma(1-\beta\cos\theta)]$, where $\beta=v/c$ and $\gamma=(1-\beta^2)^{-1/2}$. First-order expansion gives the displayed dipole. The forward sky is hotter. The [cosmic microwave background dipole](cosmic-microwave-background-dipole.md) is an application.
