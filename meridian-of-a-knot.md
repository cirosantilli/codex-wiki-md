# Meridian of a knot

↑ **Parent:** [Knot exterior](knot-exterior.md)

A meridian is an oriented simple closed curve on the boundary of a [knot exterior](knot-exterior.md) that bounds a disk in the removed tubular neighborhood and has [linking number](linking-number.md) one with the knot.

Viewed inside the removed tubular neighborhood, this is a [meridian of a solid torus](meridian-of-a-solid-torus.md).
