# Particle-physics control region

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

An event sample designed to constrain a background contribution to a [signal region](signal-region.md), while having little expected signal contamination. Changed particle-identification, kinematic or tagging requirements can isolate a process. The model must account for its relation to the signal sample, shared uncertainties and any signal leakage. A separate validation sample can test extrapolation without tuning the discovery selection.
