# Ray-plane intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)

Intersection of $o+\lambda r$ with the [plane](plane.md) $n\cdot(p-a)=0$, when $n\cdot r\ne0$. For a [ray](ray-geometry.md), require $\lambda\geq0$. A zero denominator requires separate parallel or coplanar handling.
