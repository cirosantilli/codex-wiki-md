# Laurent polynomial ring

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md)

A Laurent polynomial ring is the [localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md) $k[X_1,\ldots,X_r]$ obtained by inverting all variables. Its elements are finite sums of [Laurent polynomials](laurent-polynomial.md) with exponent vectors in $\mathbb Z^r$. It is a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) by the [Hilbert basis theorem](hilbert-basis-theorem.md) and [Localization of a Noetherian ring](localization-of-a-noetherian-ring.md).
