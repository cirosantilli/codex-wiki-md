# Geiger-Marsden experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutherford_scattering_experiments)

The Geiger-Marsden experiment probes the interior of a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) by counting [alpha particles](alpha-particle.md) deflected by metal foils. The surprising observation was a small population returning towards the incident side. [Hans Geiger](hans-geiger.md) and [Ernest Marsden](ernest-marsden.md) performed the experiments under [Ernest Rutherford](ernest-rutherford.md)'s direction; [Ernest Rutherford](ernest-rutherford.md) developed the [Rutherford model](rutherford-model.md) in 1911. Observation and interpretation were distinct contributions.[Dictionary of New Zealand Biography; Biography, paragraphs on the 1909 undergraduate experiment and subsequent interpretation](dictionary-of-new-zealand-biography/marsden-ernest-by-ross-galbreath.md)

In 1909, a [radon](radon.md) source and its [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) products in a conical glass tube supplied [alpha particles](alpha-particle.md) through a [mica](mica.md) window towards a reflector. Lead shielded a fixed [zinc sulfide](zinc-sulfide.md) screen from direct hits; an [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) resolved individual [particle scintillations](scintillation-physics.md). Reflectors occupied the same position. Reflector-out counts tested air scattering; stacked [gold](gold.md) foils and bare glass tested thickness and support contributions.[On a Diffuse Reflection of the α-Particles, printed pp. 495–498, Fig. 1 and experiments I–II](on-a-diffuse-reflection-of-the-alpha-particles-by-hans-geiger-and-ernest-marsden.md)

Nearby source geometry blurred incidence [angles](angle.md). Extrapolating [platinum](platinum.md) counts uniformly over a [hemisphere](hemisphere.md) was an assumption, not a universal reflection probability.[Printed pp. 497, 499–500, angular limitation and experiment III](on-a-diffuse-reflection-of-the-alpha-particles-by-hans-geiger-and-ernest-marsden.md)
