# Group velocity from a period-dependent phase velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Group velocity](group-velocity.md)

Since $k=\omega/c(\omega)$, [differentiation](differentiation.md) gives $u=(dk/d\omega)^{-1}=c/[1-(\omega/c)\,dc/d\omega]$. Using the [period](period-of-a-function.md) $P=2\pi/\omega$ gives the displayed formula. A positive [period](period-of-a-function.md) slope makes the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) smaller than the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) on this branch. A slope read from a graph produces an approximate local result, not a globally fitted [dispersion](wave-dispersion.md) law.
