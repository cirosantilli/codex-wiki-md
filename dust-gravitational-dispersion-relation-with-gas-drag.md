# Dust gravitational dispersion relation with gas drag

↑ **Parent:** [Secular gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk](secular-gravitational-instability-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md)

The local axisymmetric density/velocity system of a pressured dust layer with linear [gas drag](gas-drag.md) and a fixed [Keplerian shearing sheet](keplerian-shearing-sheet.md) gas flow has this cubic [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). The $\Omega/2$ azimuthal velocity coefficient includes advection of the [Keplerian shear](keplerian-shear.md). The [razor-thin disk Poisson kernel](razor-thin-disk-poisson-kernel.md) supplies the $|k|$ self-gravity term. Keeping the full amplitude [determinant](determinant.md) retains the zero-frequency branch and avoids dividing out the secular mode.
