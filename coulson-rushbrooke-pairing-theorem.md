# Coulson-Rushbrooke pairing theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hückel method](huckel-method.md)

For a bipartite pi system with a common diagonal energy $\alpha$, the orbital spectrum is paired about $\alpha$. If $H=\alpha I+K$ and $P$ changes the sign on one sublattice, then $PKP=-K$; an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of energy $\alpha+\epsilon$ is mapped to one of energy $\alpha-\epsilon$. Their site-probability magnitudes are equal.
