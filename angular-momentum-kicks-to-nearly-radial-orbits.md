# Angular-momentum kicks to nearly radial orbits

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary scattering](planetary-scattering.md)

For a nearly radial [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md), $q\simeq h^2/(2GM)$, so modest changes in [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) can strongly change [pericentre](periapsis.md). A planetary [torque](torque.md) acting for a reference orbital time gives $\delta h\sim(m_p/M)\sqrt{GMa_p}$. Coherent kicks change $q$ by order itself after $N\sim(M/m_p)\sqrt{q/a_p}$ orbital-scale encounters, whereas uncorrelated kicks require an order-$N^2$ random-walk count. Both estimates are conditional on encounter geometry: a close encounter with a smaller [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) can give a much larger kick.
