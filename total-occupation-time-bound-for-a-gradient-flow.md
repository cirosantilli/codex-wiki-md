# Total occupation-time bound for a gradient flow

↑ **Parent:** [Gradient-flow dissipation identity](gradient-flow-dissipation-identity.md)

If $f$ is bounded along a downward [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md), the total time spent where $\|\nabla_g f\|\geq\varepsilon$ is at most $(\sup f-\inf f)/\varepsilon^2$. The [gradient-flow dissipation identity](gradient-flow-dissipation-identity.md) proves this by integrating the indicator of that region. It bounds all visits together, without requiring the visited region to be connected.
