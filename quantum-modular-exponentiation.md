# Quantum modular exponentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum order finding](quantum-order-finding.md)

Quantum modular exponentiation is the [reversible computation](reversible-computation.md) that maps

$$
|x\rangle|0\rangle\longmapsto|x\rangle|a^x\bmod N\rangle.
$$

It can be implemented in [polynomial time](polynomial-time.md) in the bit lengths of $x$ and $N$ by [repeated squaring](exponentiation-by-squaring.md) and controlled modular multiplication.
