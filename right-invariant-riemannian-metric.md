# Right-invariant Riemannian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

A [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) on a [Lie group](lie-group.md) is right-invariant when every right translation is an [isometry](isometry.md). It is determined by a positive-definite [inner product](inner-product.md) at the identity, extended with a coframe of [right-invariant differential forms](right-invariant-differential-form.md). Its translation-generated [Killing vector fields](killing-vector-field.md) are left-invariant, rather than the right-invariant vector fields dual to that coframe.
