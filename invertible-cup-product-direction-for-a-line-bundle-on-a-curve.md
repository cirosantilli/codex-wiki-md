# Invertible cup-product direction for a line bundle on a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant description of the theta divisor](determinant-description-of-the-theta-divisor.md)

Suppose a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $L$ of degree $g-1$ on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) has $h^0(L)=h^1(L)=r$. Choose $r$ distinct points where evaluation is invertible for both $H^0(L)$ and $H^0(K_X\otimes L^{-1})$. Such choices exist simultaneously: each [determinant](determinant.md) defines a nonempty open subset of $X^r$, since independent sections cannot vanish at every point. Let $\xi\in H^1(\mathcal O_X)=H^0(K_X)^*$ be a sum of nonzero weighted evaluation functionals at these points. The [Serre duality](serre-duality.md) [matrix](matrix.md) of $\xi\smile-$ factors as the product of the two invertible evaluation [matrices](matrix.md) and an invertible diagonal [matrix](matrix.md). Its [determinant](determinant.md) is nonzero. This gives the nonzero leading [determinant](determinant.md) needed for the [Riemann singularity theorem](riemann-singularity-theorem.md).
