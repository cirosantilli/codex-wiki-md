# Compact acoustic quadrupole Mach-number scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic quadrupole](acoustic-quadrupole.md)

For a low-[Mach number](mach-number.md) source with stress $O(\rho_0U^2)$ and advective time $\ell/U$, the three-dimensional compact [acoustic quadrupole](acoustic-quadrupole.md) has density amplitude $O(\rho_0M^4\ell/r)$, where $M=U/c_0$. The power depends on these source-time and stress hypotheses, not only on the quadrupole label.
