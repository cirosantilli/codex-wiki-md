# Small-mass-ratio collinear Lagrange points

↑ **Parent:** [Collinear Lagrange point](collinear-lagrange-point.md)

In barycentric coordinates with unit primary separation, a small secondary mass fraction $\alpha$ gives distances $(\alpha/3)^{1/3}$ from that secondary to the two neighboring [Lagrange points](lagrange-point.md) at leading order. This is the dimensionless [Hill radius](hill-radius.md). The opposite exterior [L3 Lagrange point](l3-lagrange-point.md) has coordinate $-1-5\alpha/12+O(\alpha^2)$.
