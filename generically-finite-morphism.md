# Generically finite morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md)

A dominant [morphism of varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) is generically finite when its extension of [function fields](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) is finite. Its degree is the degree of this field extension. A dominant morphism between integral varieties of equal dimension is generically finite; over a suitable nonempty open subset of the target its fibers are finite.
