<h1 id="equality-in-the-three-dimensional-loomis-whitney-inequality">Equality in the three-dimensional Loomis--Whitney inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Loomis--Whitney inequality](loomis-whitney-inequality.md)

Equality in the three-dimensional [Loomis--Whitney inequality](loomis-whitney-inequality.md) forces a measurable body to agree up to a null set with a Cartesian product $E_1\times E_2\times E_3$. This follows from the equality conditions in the two [Cauchy-Schwarz inequalities](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) used in its proof. If the body is connected and is a finite union of positive-volume [axis-parallel boxes](axis-parallel-box.md), each $E_i$ is an interval and equality up to a null set upgrades to exact equality with one box.
