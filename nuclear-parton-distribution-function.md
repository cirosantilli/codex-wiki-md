# Nuclear parton distribution function

↑ **Parent:** [Parton distribution function](parton-distribution-function.md)

A nuclear [parton](parton.md) distribution describes [parton](parton.md) [momentum](momentum-split.md) fractions in a bound nuclear target. Its [normalization](normalization.md) must specify whether it is per [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) or per [nucleon](nucleon.md) and how $x$ is defined. Nuclear binding, shadowing and other [nuclear modifications](nuclear-parton-distribution-function.md) mean it need not equal a simple sum of free-nucleon distributions.
