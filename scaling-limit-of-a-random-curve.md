# Scaling limit of a random curve

↑ **Parent:** [Weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md)

A [scaling limit](scaling-limit-of-a-random-curve.md) of random curves is a [weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) after spatial rescaling, usually modulo monotone reparameterization. Tightness specifies the topology and yields subsequential limits; identification of their laws gives a unique continuum model. [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) is a central family of such limiting curve laws.
