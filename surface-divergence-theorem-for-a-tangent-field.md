# Surface divergence theorem for a tangent field

↑ **Parent:** [Surface divergence](surface-divergence.md)

For a smooth tangent [vector field](vector-field.md) $v$ on a compact [orientable surface](orientable-surface.md) with smooth boundary and [outward conormal of a surface boundary](outward-conormal-of-a-surface-boundary.md), the integral of its [surface divergence](surface-divergence.md) is its boundary [flux](flux.md). Extend the unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) to a unit field $m$. The [curl of a cross product](curl-of-a-cross-product.md) gives $m\cdot\nabla\times(m\times v)=\nabla\cdot v-m_im_j\partial_jv_i$ on the surface: $m\cdot v=0$ there and $m\cdot\partial_jm=0$ by unit length. Apply [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) and use $(m\times v)\cdot d\mathbf r=v\cdot(d\mathbf r\times m)$ to obtain the formula.
