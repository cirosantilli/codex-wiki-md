# Hard-wall condensate healing profile

↑ **Parent:** [Healing length](healing-length.md)

A repulsive condensate next to an impenetrable planar wall has [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) $\psi(0)=0$. Writing $\psi=\sqrt{n_0}f$ reduces its stationary [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) to $\ell_h^2f''+(1-f^2)f=0$. The bulk conditions $f\to1,f'\to0$ give $\ell_h^2(f')^2=(1-f^2)^2/2$ and hence the displayed [number density](number-density.md). Omitting $n_0$ describes normalized [number density](number-density.md), not dimensional [number density](number-density.md).
