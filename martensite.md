# Martensite

↑ **Parent:** [Steel microstructure](steel-microstructure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martensite)

A product of a [displacive transformation](displacive-transformation.md). In carbon steel, rapid cooling can trap [carbon](carbon.md) in a distorted iron lattice, producing high [hardness](hardness.md) and low as-quenched [toughness](toughness.md).
