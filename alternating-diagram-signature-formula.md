# Alternating diagram signature formula

↑ **Parent:** [Signature of a knot](signature-of-a-knot.md)

For a reduced connected [alternating knot diagram](alternating-knot-diagram.md), its [knot signature](signature-of-a-knot.md) is $s_A(D)-c_+(D)-1$, where $s_A$ counts circles in the all-$A$ [bracket smoothing state](bracket-smoothing-state.md) and $c_+$ counts positive crossings. The convention is that the $A$-smoothing at a positive braid crossing is the [oriented smoothing](oriented-smoothing.md), and a positive [trefoil knot](trefoil-knot.md) has [knot signature](signature-of-a-knot.md) $-2$. Reversing the global [knot signature](signature-of-a-knot.md) convention negates the formula.
