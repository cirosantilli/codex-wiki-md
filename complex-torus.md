# Complex torus

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_torus)

A complex torus is a quotient $\mathbb C^n/\Lambda$ by a discrete subgroup $\Lambda\cong\mathbb Z^{2n}$ spanning $\mathbb C^n$ over $\mathbb R$. Translation-invariant [differential forms](differential-form-split.md) descend to the quotient.
