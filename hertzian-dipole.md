# Hertzian dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Dipole antenna](dipole-antenna.md)

A [Hertzian dipole](hertzian-dipole.md) is an ideal electrically short oscillating [electric dipole](electric-dipole.md) used to model radiation from a localized current element. In [vacuum](vacuum.md) its far-field [electric dipole radiation](electric-dipole-radiation.md) has $\sin^2\theta$ angular power distribution and total [power](power.md) $P=\mu_0|\ddot{\mathbf p}|^2/(6\pi c)$, evaluated at the [retarded time](retarded-time.md). The [electric-dipole approximation for radiation](electric-dipole-approximation-for-radiation.md) requires a source much smaller than its radiation [wavelength](wavelength.md).
