# Quantum harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_harmonic_oscillator)

The [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) describes a particle of [mass](mass.md) $m>0$ in a quadratic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) with [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega>0$. In the position representation its [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is

$$
H=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2}{dx^2}+\frac{m\omega^2x^2}2.
$$

The model is useful far beyond a literal spring: a [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) about a stable equilibrium with positive potential curvature has this quadratic term as its leading nonconstant approximation. The pure [quartic oscillator](quartic-oscillator.md) has zero curvature at its minimum, so that particular approximation does not apply. [Allan Adams, MIT 8.04 Lecture 8, Quantum Harmonic Oscillator, pp. 3–4](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-04-quantum-physics-i-spring-2013/808334d09369e3a726f6f20d82315c20_MIT8_04S13_Lec08.pdf).

In units with $\hbar=1$, [creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) give

$$
H=\omega\left(N+\frac12\right),
\qquad N=A^\dagger A.
$$

Here $N$ is the [number operator](number-operator.md). Restoring the reduced [Planck constant](planck-constant.md), the [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) are $E_n=\hbar\omega(n+1/2)$ for $n=0,1,2,\ldots$: neighboring levels are separated by $\hbar\omega$, and the [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md) is the [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) $\hbar\omega/2$.

With length scale $\ell=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$ and dimensionless coordinate $u=x/\ell$, the [ground state](ground-state.md) is the normalized [Gaussian wave packet](gaussian-wave-packet.md)

$$
\phi_0(x)=\frac1{\pi^{1/4}\sqrt\ell}e^{-u^2/2},\qquad \langle x^2\rangle_0=\frac{\ell^2}2.
$$

For $\hbar=m=\omega=1$, this is precisely the $a=1$ trial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) in the [quantum variational principle](quantum-variational-principle.md). The balance of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) therefore recovers the exact [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md) using only [Gaussian integrals](gaussian-integral.md).
