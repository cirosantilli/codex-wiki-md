<h1 id="wavelet-coefficient-decay-for-holder-functions">Wavelet coefficient decay for Hölder functions</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder-Taylor remainder bound](holder-taylor-remainder-bound.md)

Localized [wavelets](wavelet.md) of support diameter $O(2^{-j})$ and $L^1$ [norm](norm.md) $O(2^{-j/2})$, with enough [vanishing moments](vanishing-moment.md), satisfy

$$
|\langle f,\psi_{j,n}\rangle|\le C\|f\|_{C^\alpha}2^{-j(\alpha+1/2)}.
$$

Subtract a [Taylor polynomial](taylor-polynomial.md) annihilated by the [wavelet](wavelet.md), apply the [Hölder-Taylor remainder bound](holder-taylor-remainder-bound.md) on its [support of a function](support.md), and multiply by its $L^1$ [norm](norm.md). The same argument applies to localized [boundary wavelets](boundary-wavelet.md) with the same cancellation; finitely many coarse [scaling functions](scaling-function.md) are handled separately.
