# Square triangular number

↑ **Parent:** [Triangular number](triangular-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_triangular_number)

A [square triangular number](square-triangular-number.md) is simultaneously a [triangular number](triangular-number.md) and a [perfect square](square-number.md). For example, $36=T_8=6^2$. This differs from a [squared triangular number](squared-triangular-number.md): $T_4^2=100$ is not itself triangular, by the [recognition criterion for triangular numbers](recognition-criterion-for-triangular-numbers.md). Writing $T_n=m^2$ gives $(2n+1)^2-8m^2=1$, a [Pell equation](pell-equation.md) that explains why this intersection of two counting patterns has additional arithmetic structure.
