# Sigmoidal delayed ventilation threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Delayed carbon-dioxide ventilation feedback](delayed-carbon-dioxide-ventilation-feedback.md)

For $K\dot p=M-pV(p(t-\tau))$ and a positive steady state, [linearization](linearization.md) gives $\dot z=-Az-Bz(t-\tau)$, where $A=V(p^*)/K$ and $B=p^*V'(p^*)/K$. Splitting the [characteristic equation of a delay differential equation](characteristic-equation-of-a-delay-differential-equation.md) at an imaginary root yields $\omega^2=B^2-A^2$ and the displayed first critical delay. The root crosses into the unstable half-plane with real derivative $\omega^2/[(1+A\tau_c)^2+\omega^2\tau_c^2]>0$. If $A\ll B$, the period near onset is approximately $4\tau_c$. For a [Hill equation](hill-equation.md) response, $B/A=n\theta^n/(\theta^n+(p^*)^n)$ identifies whether delayed gain can exceed instantaneous damping.

A longer circulation time increases the delay between gas exchange and its sensed feedback. The American Thoracic Society's documented case relates a change in breathing pattern during one night to a longer estimated circulation time; the simple scalar model isolates a possible feedback mechanism rather than representing every determinant of that observation.[American Thoracic Society, Circulation Time and Sleep-Disordered Breathing: case discussion and circulation-time/volume/cardiac-output relation](https://www.thoracic.org/professionals/clinical-resources/sleep/sleep-fragments/circulation-time-and-sleep-disordered-breathing.php)
