# Born effective charge

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization density](polarization-density.md)

A [tensor](tensor.md) derivative of polarization with respect to a sublattice displacement at specified macroscopic electrical conditions. It includes electronic rearrangement and need not equal a formal ionic charge.
