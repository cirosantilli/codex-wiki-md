<h1 id="seven-bridges-of-konigsberg">Seven Bridges of Königsberg</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Eulerian graph](eulerian-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Bridges_of_Königsberg)

Model the four land regions by [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) $A,B,C,D$ and the seven bridges by distinct [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md): two $AB$, two $AC$, and one each of $AD,BD,CD$. Their [vertex degrees](degree-graph-theory.md) are $(5,3,3,3)$. [Leonhard Euler](leonhard-euler.md)'s paper records these connections in §6 and the obstruction in §9; §20 states the zero-or-two odd-region rule. Four odd [vertex degrees](degree-graph-theory.md) exclude an [Eulerian trail](eulerian-trail.md), even if returning to the starting [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) is optional.[§§6, 9, 20](solutio-problematis-ad-geometriam-situs-pertinentis-by-leonhard-euler.md)
