# Quaternionic tautological line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Tautological bundle](tautological-bundle.md)

Its fibre over a point of [quaternionic projective space](quaternionic-projective-space.md) is the quaternionic line represented by that point. As a real vector bundle it has rank four, and its unit [sphere bundle](sphere-bundle.md) is $S^{4n+3}\to\mathbb{HP}^n$. Right multiplication by a chosen imaginary unit equips it with complex rank two. Under the [complex inclusion into quaternionic projective space](complex-inclusion-into-quaternionic-projective-space.md), it restricts to $L\oplus\overline L$, where $L$ is the complex [tautological bundle](tautological-bundle.md). Thus its [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md) restricts to $-c_1(L^*)^2$.
