# Surface divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Surface covariant derivative](surface-covariant-derivative.md)

The surface divergence of an ambient [vector field](vector-field.md) is the tangential [trace](matrix-trace.md) $\nabla_s\cdot\mathbf v=(\delta_{ij}-n_in_j)\partial_jv_i$. For a tangent [vector field](vector-field.md) it is the intrinsic [divergence](divergence.md). For $\mathbf v=\mathbf n$ it gives the sum of the two principal [curvatures](curvature.md); a sphere with outward normal has $\nabla_s\cdot\mathbf n=2/a$.
