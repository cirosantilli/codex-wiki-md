<h1 id="wolstenholme-s-theorem">Wolstenholme's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Integer congruence](integer-congruence.md)

For every [prime](prime-number-split.md) $p\ge5$, $\binom{2p-1}{p-1}\equiv1\pmod{p^3}$. Equivalently, $\sum_{k=1}^{p-1}1/k\equiv0\pmod{p^2}$, with denominators interpreted through [modular inverses](modular-multiplicative-inverse.md).
