# Mantle potential temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Earth mantle](earth-mantle.md)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) a parcel of [mantle](planetary-mantle.md) material would attain after a reversible [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) bringing it to a specified reference pressure, without changing its composition or phase assemblage. It removes the compressional contribution when comparing sources at different depths. Within one phase and with depth $z$ positive downward, the [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) gives the gradient is $dT/dz=\alpha gT/c_p$, so

$$
T_p=T(z)\exp\left(-\int_0^z\frac{\alpha(s)g(s)}{c_p(s)}\,ds\right).
$$

Absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md) is used inside this formula. Recovering a source $T_p$ from erupted [magma](magma.md) also requires accounting for melting, [latent heat](latent-heat.md), ascent and [fractional crystallization](fractional-crystallization.md); an eruption temperature is not automatically a [mantle potential temperature](mantle-potential-temperature.md).
