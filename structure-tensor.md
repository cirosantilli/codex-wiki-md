# Structure tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Image processing](image-processing.md)

For a smoothed [image signal](image-signal.md) $u_\sigma$, average the [gradient](gradient.md) outer product as $J_\rho=G_\rho*(\nabla u_\sigma\nabla u_\sigma^T)$. The dominant [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) estimates the normal to a coherent [image edge](image-edge.md), and [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) contrast [measures](measure.md) local directional structure. Two distinct scales separate differentiation from orientation averaging.
