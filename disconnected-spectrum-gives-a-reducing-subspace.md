# Disconnected spectrum gives a reducing subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous functional calculus](continuous-functional-calculus.md)

For a [normal operator](normal-operator.md) with disconnected [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) $K=K_1\sqcup K_2$, the [indicator function](indicator-function.md) of a nonempty proper clopen component union is continuous on $K$. The [continuous functional calculus](continuous-functional-calculus.md) makes it a nonzero proper self-adjoint [linear projection](projection-linear-algebra.md) commuting with $T,T^*$. Its range is therefore a nontrivial closed reducing subspace. No [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) is needed.
