# Quadratic energy minimization by the parallelogram law

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet principle](dirichlet-principle.md)

Let $V$ be a closed [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) of a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), and let $Q(v)=a(v,v)$ for a symmetric [bounded bilinear form](bounded-bilinear-form.md) that is nonnegative on the ambient space and coercive on $V$. Then $Q$ has a unique minimizer on every closed [affine subspace](affine-subspace.md) $g+V$. If $Q(v_j)\to m=\inf_{g+V}Q$, the midpoint belongs to $g+V$ and the [parallelogram law](parallelogram-law.md) gives $Q(v_j-v_k)\leq2Q(v_j)+2Q(v_k)-4m\to0$. Coercivity on $V$ makes the [minimizing sequence](minimizing-sequence.md) Cauchy; completeness and continuity give a minimizer. The vanishing [first variation](first-variation.md) is $a(u,v)=0$ for all $v\in V$. Two minimizers differ by $w\in V$ with $Q(w)=0$, so coercivity gives uniqueness. This proves the symmetric existence theorem directly, without invoking the [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md).
