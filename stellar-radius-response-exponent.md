# Stellar radius response exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical stability of binary mass transfer](dynamical-stability-of-binary-mass-transfer.md)

The stellar radius response exponent measures how a star's radius changes as it loses mass. The adiabatic exponent $\zeta_{\rm ad}$ describes loss too rapid for thermal adjustment; the equilibrium exponent $\zeta_{\rm eq}$ follows a sequence of thermally relaxed stars. Compare $\zeta_{\rm ad}$ with the [Roche-lobe radius response exponent](roche-lobe-radius-response-exponent.md) to test dynamical stability.
