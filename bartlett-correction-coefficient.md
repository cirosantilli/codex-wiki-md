# Bartlett correction coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Bartlett correction](bartlett-correction.md)

The coefficient $b$ is the leading relative null-mean discrepancy of a regular [likelihood-ratio test statistic](likelihood-ratio-test-statistic.md). It is distinct from the finite-sample divisor $1+b/n$ used in a [Bartlett correction](bartlett-correction.md). Different conventions call either of these a correction factor, so the normalization should always be stated explicitly.
