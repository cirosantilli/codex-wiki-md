# Skew Laurent polynomial ring

↑ **Parent:** [Skew polynomial ring of an automorphism](skew-polynomial-ring-of-an-automorphism.md)

Adjoining an inverse of $t$ to the [skew polynomial ring of an automorphism](skew-polynomial-ring-of-an-automorphism.md) allows finite sums $\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}a_jt^j$, with $ta=\sigma(a)t$. Every element can be moved into the polynomial subring by multiplying by a sufficiently large power of $t$. Consequently, for a [right ideal](right-ideal.md) $I$ of this [ring](ring.md), $I$ is generated by its intersection with the polynomial subring: if $ft^N$ lies there, then $f=(ft^N)t^{-N}$. This proves transfer of right [Noetherianity](noetherian-ring.md) from the polynomial subring, and multiplication on the left proves the left-handed version.
