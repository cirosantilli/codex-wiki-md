# S-wave superconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductor](superconductor.md)

A superconductor with an s-wave orbital pairing component: isotropic zero orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) in the rotationally invariant model, or the corresponding fully symmetric orbital representation in a crystal. Crystal anisotropy can still make the gap magnitude angle dependent. In the usual centrosymmetric spin-singlet model its orbital parity is even.
