# Radial Dirichlet modes of a spherical cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md)

A bounded spherically symmetric solution of the [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md) in a ball with zero value at radius $a$ satisfies $-(r^2\psi')'=k^2r^2\psi$. The substitution $u=r\psi$ gives $u''+k^2u=0$ with $u(0)=u(a)=0$, so the displayed modes have $n\geq1$; the apparent singularity at zero is removable. Their [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) is $\int_0^ar^2\psi'^2dr/\int_0^ar^2\psi^2dr$. The trial profile $1-r^2/a^2$ gives the upper bound $21/(2a^2)$, compared with the exact lowest value $\pi^2/a^2$. Boundedness at zero eliminates the $1/r$ solution and makes the endpoint term in the [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md) vanish.
