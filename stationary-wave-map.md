# Stationary wave map

↑ **Parent:** [Wave map](wave-map.md)

A time-independent [wave map](wave-map.md) is a [harmonic map](harmonic-map.md) of its spatial domain into the target. For target $S^2$, its equation is $\Delta Q+|\nabla Q|^2Q=0$. In two dimensions inverse [stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md) gives a smooth nonconstant example of finite [wave map energy](wave-map-energy.md) $4\pi$.
