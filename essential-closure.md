# Essential closure

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md)

For a [Lebesgue measurable set](lebesgue-measurable-set.md) $A$ in an [open set](open-set.md) $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, its essential closure consists of the points whose every sufficiently small ball has positive [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) intersection with $A$. Equivalently,

$$
\operatorname{esscl}_\Omega A=\operatorname{supp}\mathbf1_A,
$$

where the right side is the [support of a distribution](support-of-a-distribution.md). To see the equivalence, a ball on which $A$ is null gives zero pairings with all [test functions](test-function.md). If $A$ has positive measure in a ball, cover that ball by smaller compactly contained balls and choose a nonnegative [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) positive on one meeting $A$ in positive measure; its pairing is positive. Thus essential closure is relatively closed and unchanged by modifying $A$ on a [Lebesgue null set](lebesgue-null-set.md). For an [open set](open-set.md) $A$ it equals its relative closure.
