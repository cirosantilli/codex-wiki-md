# Landau amplitude equation

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md)

The cubic Landau amplitude equation

$$
\dot A=\mu A-gA^3
$$

with $g>0$ describes saturation through a supercritical [pitchfork bifurcation](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md) when a reflection symmetry permits both signs of $A$.
