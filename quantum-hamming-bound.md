# Quantum Hamming bound

↑ **Parent:** [Nondegenerate quantum error-correcting code](nondegenerate-quantum-error-correcting-code.md)

For unknown-location [Pauli operators](pauli-operator.md) of weight at most $t$, there are $N(t)=\sum_{j=0}^t3^j\binom nj$ physically distinct errors modulo phase, including the identity. A [nondegenerate quantum code](nondegenerate-quantum-error-correcting-code.md) assigns them mutually orthogonal error-image subspaces, each of dimension $2^k$, within the $2^n$-dimensional physical [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). Hence $N(t)2^k\leq2^n$. The hypothesis of nondegeneracy is essential to this dimension count.
