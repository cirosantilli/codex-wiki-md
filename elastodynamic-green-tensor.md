# Elastodynamic Green tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave](elastic-wave.md)

The causal [Green function](green-s-function.md) for isotropic [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) is tensor-valued: column $k$ is the displacement caused by a point impulsive [body force](body-force.md) in direction $k$. Its radiative terms are longitudinal [P waves](p-wave.md) at $t=R/\alpha$ and transverse [S waves](s-wave.md) at $t=R/\beta$, proportional to $R^{-1}$. A term supported between the arrivals carries the intermediate/near-field response. Convolution in time and space constructs the displacement for a general source.
