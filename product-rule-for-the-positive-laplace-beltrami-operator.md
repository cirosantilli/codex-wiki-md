# Product rule for the positive Laplace-Beltrami operator

↑ **Parent:** [Positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md)

For a function $f$ and one-form $\alpha$, the [codifferential](codifferential.md) obeys $\delta(f\alpha)=f\delta\alpha-\langle df,\alpha\rangle_g$. This follows from the [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) and the definition of the [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md). Applying it to $d(fh)=f\,dh+h\,df$ gives the product formula. In particular $\Delta_+(f^2)=2f\Delta_+f-2|df|_g^2$. The cross-term sign reverses for the opposite [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) convention.
