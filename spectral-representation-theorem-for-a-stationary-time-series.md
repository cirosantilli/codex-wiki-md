# Spectral representation theorem for a stationary time series

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md)

A centered [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) has a representation $X_t=\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}e^{2\pi it\omega}\,dZ(\omega)$ with orthogonal random increments. Their [variance](variance-split.md) measure is the spectral measure. When it has density $f$, the [autocovariance](autocovariance.md) is $\gamma(h)=\int e^{2\pi ih\omega}f(\omega)\,d\omega$.
