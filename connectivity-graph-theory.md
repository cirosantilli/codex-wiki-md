# Connectivity (graph theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connectivity_(graph_theory))

Connectivity studies the existence of paths between [vertices of a graph](vertex-graph-theory.md) and the persistence of paths after vertex or edge deletions. A [connected graph](connected-graph.md) is the basic case in which every pair of vertices is linked by a path.
