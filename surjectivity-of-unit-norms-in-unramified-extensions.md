# Surjectivity of unit norms in unramified extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Norm subgroup of a local field extension](norm-subgroup-of-a-local-field-extension.md)

For a finite [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of non-Archimedean [local fields](local-field.md), the [field norm](field-norm.md) on residue units is surjective: on a cyclic group of order $q^n-1$ it is exponentiation by $(q^n-1)/(q-1)$ and has image of order $q-1$. For a common [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\pi$ and $r\geq1$, expansion of the conjugate product gives

$$
N_{L/K}(1+\pi^r a)\equiv1+\pi^r\operatorname{Tr}_{k_L/k_K}(\bar a)\pmod{\pi^{r+1}}.
$$

The [field trace](field-trace.md) of a finite [finite field](finite-field.md) extension is onto: its polynomial $X+X^q+\cdots+X^{q^{n-1}}$ is nonzero of degree below $q^n$, so defines a nonzero linear map to the base field. Lift a residue norm and correct successively with elements of $1+\pi^r\mathcal O_L$. The infinite product converges by [completeness](completeness.md), and its norm is the prescribed unit. This argument also works in positive characteristic and when $p$ divides $n$.
