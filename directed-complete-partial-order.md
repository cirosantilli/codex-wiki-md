# Directed-complete partial order

↑ **Parent:** [Complete partial order](complete-partial-order.md)

A [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) is directed-complete if every [directed set](directed-set.md) in it has a [least upper bound](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). A [complete lattice](complete-lattice.md) is directed-complete. Conversely, directed completeness together with all finite [joins](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md), including the empty join, gives arbitrary [joins](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md): take the [directed set](directed-set.md) of joins of finite subsets.
