# Quartic residue

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicative group of a finite field](multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field.md)

A nonzero element $a$ of a [finite field](finite-field.md) is a quartic residue when $a=b^4$ for some nonzero $b$. If $q\equiv1\pmod4$, the [multiplicative group of a finite field](multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field.md) is cyclic of order divisible by four, so the [quartic residues](quartic-residue.md) form a subgroup of index four; equivalently $a^{(q-1)/4}=1$.
