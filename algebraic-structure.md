# Algebraic structure

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_structure)

An algebraic structure is a [set](set-split.md) equipped with specified operations satisfying selected axioms. [Groups](group-split.md), [rings](ring.md) and [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) are examples. Its [homomorphisms](homomorphism.md) preserve the operations belonging to that structure.
