# Removed compartment

↑ **Parent:** [SIR model](sir-model.md)

The [removed compartment](removed-compartment.md) consists of people permanently excluded from both acquiring and passing on [infection](infection.md) in the [SIR model](sir-model.md). Its count is $R(t)$ and its [fraction](fraction.md) is $r(t)=R(t)/N$. Removal can represent regained health with lasting [immunity](immunity-medical.md), or another permanent exclusion from acquiring or passing on [infection](infection.md); it need not mean being healthy again. Here there are no deaths. In a variant that counts deaths within $R$, [conservation of population](conservation-of-population.md) would concern the original cohort, rather than the number currently alive.[David Tong, Mathematical Biology, section 1.5.1](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/mathbio/mathbio1.pdf)
