# Grace period

↑ **Parent:** [Read-copy-update](read-copy-update.md)

An interval sufficient to establish that every reader that might retain a preexisting reference has left its protected read-side region. Completion permits the corresponding old object to undergo [memory reclamation](memory-reclamation.md).
