# Digital counter

↑ **Parent:** [Synchronous sequential circuit](synchronous-sequential-circuit.md)

A [digital counter](digital-counter.md) is a finite-state circuit that increments its stored value on each qualifying clock or event. A binary $w$-bit implementation naturally has modulus $2^w$. Dividing counted ticks by a known clock frequency gives a time interval, subject to wrap ambiguity and sampling accuracy.
