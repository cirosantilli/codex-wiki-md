# Osmotic concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Osmosis](osmosis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmotic_concentration)

In the dilute ideal particle-count description, [osmolarity](osmotic-concentration.md) is the total [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of independently dissolved [solute](solute.md) particles per [volume](volume.md) of [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md): $c_{\mathrm{osm}}=\sum_j n_j/V$. It includes both membrane-permeating and retained [solutes](solute.md); specifying a particular membrane is unnecessary to count particles. A neutral undissociated [solute](solute.md) contributes one particle per [molecule](molecule-split.md), whereas separated [ions](ion.md) are counted individually. Common units are $\mathrm{osmol\,L^{-1}}$ or $\mathrm{mOsm\,L^{-1}}$, with one osmole denoting one mole of the counted particles. [Osmolality](osmolality.md) uses mass of [solvent](solvent.md) instead of [volume](volume.md) of [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md); the two numerical values should not be identified without the appropriate mass-to-volume conversion.[Cell Membranes—Structure and Transport, Osmosis: paragraphs beginning Earlier this semester and Osmolarity is a useful term; total particle counting](osmosis-cell-membranes-structure-and-transport-byu-idaho.md)

This ideal counting definition states its scope: interactions can require a [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) description of [osmotic pressure](osmotic-pressure.md). [Tonicity](tonicity.md) asks a different question, namely how that surrounding [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) changes a specified [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)'s [volume](volume.md) through its particular [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md).
