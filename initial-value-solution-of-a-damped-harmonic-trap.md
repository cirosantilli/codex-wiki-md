# Initial-value solution of a damped harmonic trap

↑ **Parent:** [Damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For distinct roots $r_\pm=(-\gamma\pm\sqrt{\gamma^2-4mk})/(2m)$, the deterministic response is $X=(v_0-r_-x_0)e^{r_+t}/(r_+-r_-)+(r_+x_0-v_0)e^{r_-t}/(r_+-r_-)$. It satisfies $X(0)=x_0$ and $\dot X(0)=v_0$. With zero-[mean](expected-value-split.md) future noise and prescribed initial data it is also the conditional ensemble [mean](expected-value-split.md), not a full stochastic trajectory.
