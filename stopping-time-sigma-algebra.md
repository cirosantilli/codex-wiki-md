# Stopping-time sigma-algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping time](stopping-time.md)

The information available at a [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $T$ is the [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md)

$$
\mathcal F_T=\{A\in\mathcal F:A\cap\{T\leq t\}\in\mathcal F_t\text{ for every }t\geq0\}.
$$

In discrete time, $A\cap\{T=k\}\in\mathcal F_k$ for $A\in\mathcal F_T$. A [bounded stopping time](bounded-stopping-time.md) permits conditional forms of the [optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) with respect to this [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md).
