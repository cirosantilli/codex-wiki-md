# Saturated amplifier with exclusive upper-to-lower decay

↑ **Parent:** [Optical gain](optical-gain.md)

An open pumped [two-level system](two-level-system.md) amplifier with fixed upper-state source $R_2$, upper [spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md) only into the lower [energy level](energy-level.md), and lower-state removal rate $N_1/\tau_1$. Let $r=g_2/g_1$, $W=\sigma_{21}I/(\hbar\omega)$ and $D=N_2-rN_1$. Steady [rate equations](rate-equation.md) give $N_1=R_2\tau_1$ and $D=R_2(1-rA_{21}\tau_1)/(A_{21}+W)$. Consequently the [optical gain](optical-gain.md) is $\alpha=\alpha_0/(1+I/I_s)$ with $\alpha_0=\sigma_{21}R_2(A_{21}^{-1}-r\tau_1)$. Lower-state removal controls whether [population inversion](population-inversion.md) is possible, while its lifetime cancels from this model's [saturation intensity](saturation-intensity.md). Different upper-state branching or a nonconstant pump generally changes that conclusion.
