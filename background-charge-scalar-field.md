# Background-charge scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Linear dilaton conformal field theory](linear-dilaton-conformal-field-theory.md)

A background-charge scalar field is a free scalar coupled linearly to the worldsheet Ricci scalar. This curvature coupling produces the improved stress tensor of a [linear dilaton conformal field theory](linear-dilaton-conformal-field-theory.md).
