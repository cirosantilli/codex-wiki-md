# Sol

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

A fluid [colloidal](colloid.md) system. A solid-in-liquid example is fine [silicon dioxide](silicon-dioxide.md) dispersed in [water](water.md). The term can also include other fluid [colloidal](colloid.md) systems, so the dispersed and continuous constituents should be specified.
