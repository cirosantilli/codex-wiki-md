# Finite-test-mass dynamical friction coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Chandrasekhar dynamical friction](chandrasekhar-dynamical-friction.md)

For a test [mass](mass.md) $M$ and isotropic background particles of [mass](mass.md) $m$, the hyperbolic [two-body problem](two-body-problem.md) uses the relative deflection scale $b_{90}=G(M+m)/w^2$. The test body's longitudinal [velocity](velocity.md) change in one encounter is $-2mw/[(M+m)(1+b^2/b_{90}^2)]$. Integrating the encounter rate over [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) gives a logarithm; replacing its velocity-dependent value by a common [Coulomb logarithm in stellar dynamics](coulomb-logarithm-in-stellar-dynamics.md) gives the displayed systematic drag. The approximation $M+m\simeq M$ requires $M\gg m$, not merely $M>m$. [Velocity](velocity.md) diffusion and exact cutoff dependence are distinct effects omitted by this leading-log drag model.
