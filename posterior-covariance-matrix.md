# Posterior covariance matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Posterior variance](posterior-variance.md)

For a vector parameter with finite second moments and [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) $m_y$, the posterior covariance matrix records its uncertainty and linear dependence after conditioning on the observations. Its diagonal entries are [posterior variances](posterior-variance.md). In a regular locally flat-prior [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) approximation it is the inverse observed information. A [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) has posterior expectation equal to the form at the posterior mean plus the trace of its matrix times this covariance.
