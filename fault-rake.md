# Fault rake

↑ **Parent:** [Fault plane](fault-plane.md)

The [fault rake](fault-rake.md) specifies the slip direction within a [fault plane](fault-plane.md). With an along-strike unit [vector](vector.md) $\mathbf s$ and a down-dip unit [vector](vector.md) $\mathbf d$, one explicit convention is $\mathbf b/b=\cos\lambda\,\mathbf s-\sin\lambda\,\mathbf d$. Positive $90^\circ$ then denotes up-dip slip. A convention must accompany a signed [fault rake](fault-rake.md).
