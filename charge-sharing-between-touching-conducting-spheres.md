# Charge sharing between touching conducting spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Conducting sphere](conducting-sphere.md)

For externally tangent [conducting spheres](conducting-sphere.md) of radii $a,b$, centre separation $D=a+b$, and common [electric potential](electric-potential.md) $V$, the [method of images](method-of-images.md) gives

$$
Q_a=4\pi\epsilon_0Vab\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac1{b+nD}-\frac1{(n+1)D}\right),\quad
Q_b=4\pi\epsilon_0Vab\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac1{a+nD}-\frac1{(n+1)D}\right).
$$

Alternating spherical image reflections cancel the nonconstant boundary [electric potential](electric-potential.md). Pairing terms makes each series convergent. The ratio is generally not $a:b$: that rule uses isolated [capacitances](capacitance.md) and applies to widely separated [conducting spheres](conducting-sphere.md) connected by a wire. For $b=2a$, the smaller sphere carries the fraction $1/2-\pi/(6\sqrt3\ln3)\simeq0.22483$ of the total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md).
