# Quadratic wave interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly nonlinear expansion](weakly-nonlinear-expansion.md)

A quadratic product of [plane waves](plane-wave.md) generates sums and differences of their [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md) and [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md). A component drives a [secular term](secular-term.md) when its generated pair also satisfies the linear [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). A [solvability condition in the method of multiple scales](solvability-condition-in-the-method-of-multiple-scales.md) then determines the coupled [amplitude equations](amplitude-equation.md) on a [slow time](slow-time.md).
