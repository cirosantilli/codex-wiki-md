# Base stacking

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleic acid double helix](nucleic-acid-double-helix.md)

[Base stacking](base-stacking.md) is the close arrangement and interaction of neighbouring [nucleobases](nucleotide-base.md) with their approximately planar faces above one another. Along a [nucleic acid double helix](nucleic-acid-double-helix.md), neighbouring [base pairs](base-pair.md) stack successively; [base pairing](base-pairing.md) instead associates partners across the two strands. Both affect duplex stability, and the interactions depend on the identities and arrangement of neighbouring [nucleobases](nucleotide-base.md). See [Brown, Genomes: base pairing and base stacking](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21134/).
