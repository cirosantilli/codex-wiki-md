# Dirac valleys in graphene

↑ **Parent:** [Nearest-neighbour model of graphene](nearest-neighbour-model-of-graphene.md)

At either displayed [wave vector](wavevector.md), the three complex hopping amplitudes add to zero: $1+2\cos(2\pi/3)=0$. Therefore the [energy bands](energy-band.md) touch. There are two inequivalent [electronic valleys](electronic-valley.md); other equivalent corners are related by reciprocal-lattice translations. Setting $\mathbf k=\mathbf K_\tau+\mathbf q$ and retaining first-order terms gives

$$
H_\tau(\mathbf q)\simeq\hbar v_F(\tau q_x\sigma_x+q_y\sigma_y),\qquad
v_F=\frac{3tb}{2\hbar},\qquad E_\pm\simeq\pm\hbar v_F|\mathbf q|,
$$

where $\sigma_x,\sigma_y$ are [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md). For example, at $\mathbf K_+$, $f\simeq-3b(q_x-iq_y)/2$. The matrix has the form of a massless two-dimensional [Dirac Hamiltonian](dirac-hamiltonian.md). Here $v_F$ is a material-model [velocity](velocity.md), rather than the vacuum [speed of light](speed-of-light.md); this description does not change an [electron](electron.md)'s vacuum [rest mass](invariant-mass.md). It describes the [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) of electronic excitations near a band crossing.
