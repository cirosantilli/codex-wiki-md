# Max-product belief propagation

↑ **Parent:** [Belief propagation](belief-propagation.md)

The max-product form of [belief propagation](belief-propagation.md) replaces summation by maximization. On a tree, storing maximizing states while passing messages and then backtracking gives an exact [maximum a posteriori estimate](maximum-a-posteriori-estimate.md).
