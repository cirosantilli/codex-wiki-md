# Integer lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Affine space](affine-space.md)

The points with [integer](integer.md) coordinates in a real d-dimensional [affine space](affine-space.md) with a chosen origin and coordinate basis. [Translations](translation-geometry.md) by [integer](integer.md) [vectors](vector.md) and dilations by [positive integers](positive-integer.md) preserve this set. The [Gallai theorem for an integer lattice](gallai-theorem-for-an-integer-lattice.md) concerns [finite colourings](finite-coloring.md) of these points and [homothetic copies](homothetic-copy-of-a-finite-configuration.md) of finite configurations of them.
