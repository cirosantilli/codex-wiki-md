# Cohomological proof of Riemann-Roch for curves

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md)

On a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md), the exact sequence $0\to\mathcal O(D-P)\to\mathcal O(D)\to k(P)\to0$ increases the Euler characteristic by one. Iteration for positive and negative coefficients gives $\chi(\mathcal O(D))=\deg D+1-g$. [Serre duality](serre-duality.md) identifies $h^1(\mathcal O(D))$ with $h^0(\mathcal O(K-D))$, proving $\ell(D)-\ell(K-D)=\deg D+1-g$.
