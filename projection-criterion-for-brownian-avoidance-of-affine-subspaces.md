# Projection criterion for Brownian avoidance of affine subspaces

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

A [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) starting outside a fixed affine subspace of codimension at least two almost surely never hits it. Choose a two-dimensional orthogonal projection annihilating the subspace's direction and with nonzero projected initial displacement. The projected process is planar [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) starting away from zero, and a hit of the subspace would hit zero in that projection. The [polar point for planar Brownian motion](polar-point-for-planar-brownian-motion.md) result excludes this. In three dimensions this shows avoidance of every fixed line when started outside it.
