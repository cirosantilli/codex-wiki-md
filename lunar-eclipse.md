# Lunar eclipse

↑ **Parent:** [Moon](moon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_eclipse)

A [lunar eclipse](lunar-eclipse.md) occurs when [Earth](earth.md) blocks some or all of the direct sunlight reaching the [Moon](moon.md). It requires alignment near full Moon; the [orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md) usually carries the [Moon](moon.md) clear of [Earth](earth.md)'s shadow. Ordinary [lunar phases](lunar-phase.md) instead arise from viewing different amounts of the sunlit hemisphere. [NASA: lunar eclipse geometry](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/eclipses/).
