# Exact ground state of two coordinate-coupled oscillators

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

For identical [masses](mass.md) and bare [frequencies](frequency.md) with coupling $kxy$, coordinates $(x\pm y)/\sqrt2$ diagonalize the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). A stable normalizable [ground state](ground-state.md) requires $|k|<m\omega^2$. Its wave function is the product of Gaussians of [frequencies](frequency.md) $\omega_\pm$ in these [normal coordinates](normal-coordinates.md); a two-mode squeeze of the original bare oscillators alone is generally insufficient.
