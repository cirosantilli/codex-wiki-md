# Gaussian white noise

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md)

Gaussian white noise is a generalized Gaussian process with zero mean and covariance proportional to a [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) in time, and possibly in space. It is the formal time derivative of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) and has independent fluctuations on disjoint time intervals.
