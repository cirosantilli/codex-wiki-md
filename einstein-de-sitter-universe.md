# Einstein-de Sitter universe

↑ **Parent:** [Large-scale structure of the universe](large-scale-structure-of-the-universe-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein–de_Sitter_universe)

An Einstein-de Sitter universe is spatially flat and contains only pressureless matter. In conformal time, $a\propto\tau^2$ and $\mathcal H=a'/a=2/\tau$.
