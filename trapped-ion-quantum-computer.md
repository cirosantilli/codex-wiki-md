# Trapped-ion quantum computer

↑ **Parent:** [Trapped-ion quantum control](trapped-ion-quantum-control.md)

A quantum processor encoding [qubits](qubit.md) in long-lived internal states of confined [ions](ion.md). Coherent drives implement single-qubit rotations; shared vibrational modes can mediate [entangling](entangled-state.md) gates. Individual [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) [measurements](quantum-measurement-split.md) distinguish bright and dark [qubit](qubit.md) levels. Mode crowding and residual spin-motion entanglement complicate gates as a single chain grows.
