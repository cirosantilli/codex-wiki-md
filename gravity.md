# Gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity)

Gravity governs falling bodies and [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md). [Newtonian gravity](gravitational-acceleration.md) describes it through a [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) acting on masses; [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) instead relates matter and radiation to the geometry of [spacetime](spacetime.md) through the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations-split.md).
