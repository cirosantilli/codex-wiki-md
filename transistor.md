# Transistor

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transistor)

A [transistor](transistor.md) is a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) device in which an input controls an output [electric current](electric-current.md). Used with [biasing](biasing.md) and an [electrical load](electrical-load.md), it can form an [electronic amplifier](electronic-amplifier.md) or a switch. Its [power gain](power-gain.md) comes from an external supply: the input [signal](signal.md) controls the conversion of supplied [energy](energy-split.md) into output [signal](signal.md) [power](power.md).

The [point-contact transistor](point-contact-transistor.md) makes the idea tangible: two nearby contacts interact through one piece of [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md). A [bipolar junction transistor](bipolar-junction-transistor.md) instead uses two [p-n junctions](p-n-junction.md) and a thin intervening region. The distinction matters both for the history and for choosing a physical model.
