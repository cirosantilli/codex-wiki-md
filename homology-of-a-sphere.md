# Homology of a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)

For $n>0$,

$$
H_i(S^n;\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&i=0,n,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

Writing $S^n$ as two contractible hemispheres whose intersection deformation retracts to $S^{n-1}$ gives the calculation inductively through the [Mayer–Vietoris theorem](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md).
