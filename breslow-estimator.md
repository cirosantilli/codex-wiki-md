# Breslow estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Cox proportional-hazards model](cox-proportional-hazards-model.md)

The Breslow estimator of the baseline cumulative hazard in a [Cox proportional-hazards model](cox-proportional-hazards-model.md) adds the observed number of events at each event time divided by the sum of fitted relative risks in its [risk set](risk-set.md).
