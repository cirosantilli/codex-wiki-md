# Local maximum of Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

The times of [local maxima](local-maximum.md) of a [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) path are dense in the half-line [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), and every such [local maximum](local-maximum.md) is a [strict local maximum](strict-local-maximum.md). Density follows from [nowhere monotonicity of Brownian motion](nowhere-monotonicity-of-brownian-motion.md) and the [extreme value theorem](extreme-value-theorem.md). Strictness follows by checking that maxima over separated rational compact time intervals are unequal; the increment across their gap supplies an independent nondegenerate [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md).
