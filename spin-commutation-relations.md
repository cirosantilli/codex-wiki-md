# Spin commutation relations

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

The components of [spin angular momentum](spin.md) satisfy $[S_i,S_j]=i\hbar\epsilon_{ijk}S_k$. In units $\hbar=1$, the [spin ladder operators](spin-ladder-operator.md) obey $[S^+,S^-]=2S^z$ and $[S^z,S^\pm]=\pm S^\pm$.
