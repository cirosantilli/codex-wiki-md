# Correspondence theorem for ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)

For a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$ and an [ideal](ideal.md) $I$, the maps $J\mapsto J/I$ and $H\mapsto q^{-1}(H)$ give inverse inclusion-preserving bijections between [ideals](ideal.md) $J\supseteq I$ of $R$ and [ideals](ideal.md) $H$ of the [quotient ring](quotient-ring.md) $R/I$, where $q:R\to R/I$ is the quotient map. The preimage is an [ideal](ideal.md) containing the kernel $I$. Conversely, $q^{-1}(q(J))=J$ for $J\supseteq I$, and surjectivity of $q$ gives $q(q^{-1}(H))=H$. These identities prove the claim.
