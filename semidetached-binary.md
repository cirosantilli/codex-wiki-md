# Semidetached binary

↑ **Parent:** [Binary star](binary-star.md)

A semidetached binary has one component filling its [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md) while the other remains inside its own lobe. [Algol binaries](algol-binary.md) and most ordinary [cataclysmic variables](cataclysmic-variable.md) are examples.
