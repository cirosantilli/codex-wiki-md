# Age of an FLRW universe

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

The age is the proper cosmic time elapsed from the scale-factor singularity $a=0$ to the present epoch.

The [age of the universe](age-of-the-universe.md) is the cosmological quantity; an FLRW model computes it by integrating proper cosmic time from its initial scale-factor singularity.
