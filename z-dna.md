# Z-DNA

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

[Z-DNA](z-dna.md) is a left-handed duplex family with a zigzag backbone and alternating nucleotide conformations. Alternating purine–pyrimidine sequences can favour it; local torsional stress and binding partners influence its occurrence. Left-handed nucleosome wrapping is a different structural level.
