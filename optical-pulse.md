# Optical pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Light](light.md)

An optical pulse is a finite-duration envelope of [light](light.md). A nearly monochromatic pulse has many carrier oscillations within its duration $\tau$: in units with light speed one, its [wavelength](wavelength.md) obeys $\lambda\ll\tau$. A slowly varying propagation-time map stretches the pulse duration by its derivative and divides the observed [frequency](frequency.md) by the same factor.
