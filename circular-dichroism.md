# Circular dichroism

↑ **Parent:** [Optical activity](optical-activity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_dichroism)

[Circular dichroism](circular-dichroism.md) is unequal absorption of opposite [circular polarizations](circular-polarization.md). It can produce [elliptical polarization](elliptical-polarization.md); it is the absorptive response related to [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md), rather than the same measured quantity.
