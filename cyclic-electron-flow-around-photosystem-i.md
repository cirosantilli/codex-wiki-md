# Cyclic electron flow around photosystem I

↑ **Parent:** [Photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md)

[Cyclic electron flow around photosystem I](cyclic-electron-flow-around-photosystem-i.md) returns photosystem-I-derived electrons to the intersystem electron-transport chain. It can support proton-gradient formation without net NADPH production, helping adjust ATP supply and photoprotection.
