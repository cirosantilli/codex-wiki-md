# Extremally disconnected space

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is extremally disconnected when the closure of every [open set](open-set.md) is open. This concerns open sets, not arbitrary subsets. In particular, a nonisolated singleton in a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) is closed but not open, so its closure gives a counterexample to that stronger condition.
