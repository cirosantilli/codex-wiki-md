# Polyurethane

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer.md)

A [polyurethane](polyurethane.md) contains repeated connections of the form $\mathrm{{-}O{-}C(=O){-}NH{-}}$. Suitable [hydroxy groups](hydroxy-group.md) and [isocyanate](isocyanate.md) groups can produce those connections by [polyaddition](polyaddition.md); exactly two suitable groups per feed [molecule](molecule.md) give the bifunctional linear model only when competing reactions and ring closure are excluded.
