# Clebsch-Gordan splitting of a defining module and its cubic symmetric power

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md)

For the defining [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md) module with basis $x,y$, put $A_k=x\otimes x^{3-k}y^k$ and $B_k=y\otimes x^{3-k}y^k$. The raising operator has $eA_k=kA_{k-1}$ and $eB_k=A_k+kB_{k-1}$; the lowering operator has $fA_k=B_k+(3-k)A_{k+1}$ and $fB_k=(3-k)B_{k+1}$. The [highest-weight vectors](highest-weight-vector.md) $A_0$ and $B_0-A_1$ generate irreducible submodules of highest weights four and two. Bases are respectively $(A_0,3A_1+B_0,A_2+B_1,A_3+3B_2,B_3)$ and $(B_0-A_1,B_1-A_2,B_2-A_3)$. Their vectors are independent in every [weight space](weight-space.md), proving the displayed [direct sum](direct-sum.md) without only comparing dimensions.
