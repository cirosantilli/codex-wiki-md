# Planar vorticity velocity kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Vorticity](vorticity.md)

The [velocity](velocity.md) induced by planar [vorticity](vorticity.md) is a [convolution](convolution.md) with this kernel, whose sign depends on the [stream function](stream-function.md) and vorticity conventions. Its magnitude is $(2\pi|z|)^{-1}$ and its derivative is bounded by $C|z|^{-2}$. Splitting the convolution at radius $R$ gives $\|K*\omega\|_\infty\leq R\|\omega\|_\infty+\|\omega\|_1/(2\pi R)$. The same singularity yields a [log-Lipschitz modulus](log-lipschitz-modulus.md), enough for a [Yudovich characteristic flow](yudovich-characteristic-flow.md).
