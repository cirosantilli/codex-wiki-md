# Harnack inequality on the unit disk

↑ **Parent:** [Harnack inequality for harmonic functions](harnack-inequality-for-harmonic-functions.md)

For a nonnegative [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), the displayed inequalities are sharp. Apply the [Poisson integral on the unit disk](poisson-integral-on-the-unit-disk.md) on radius $R>|z|$, bound its positive kernel between $(R-|z|)/(R+|z|)$ and its reciprocal, and use the [mean value property for harmonic functions](mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md). Letting $R\uparrow1$ proves the assertion. Positive [Poisson kernels](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md) with one boundary pole give equality in the appropriate radial directions.
