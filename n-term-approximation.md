# N-term approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md)

An N-term approximation in an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) uses at most $N$ basis functions. For a chosen index set $\Lambda$, its optimal coefficients are the [inner products](inner-product.md) with those functions, and its squared error is the sum of the omitted squared coefficients by the [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md).
