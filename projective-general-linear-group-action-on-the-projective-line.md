# Projective general linear group action on the projective line

↑ **Parent:** [General linear group over a finite field](general-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md)

The group $PGL_2(F)=GL_2(F)/Z$ acts faithfully on

$$
\mathbb P^1(F)=F\cup\{\infty\}
$$

by [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md). For $|F|=q$, this embeds $PGL_2(F)$ into the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) $S_{q+1}$.
