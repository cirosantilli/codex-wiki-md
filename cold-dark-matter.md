# Cold dark matter

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter](dark-matter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_dark_matter)

Cold dark matter is nonrelativistic early enough that its primordial free-streaming length is small compared with the cosmological structures of interest.
