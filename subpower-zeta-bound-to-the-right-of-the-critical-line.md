# Subpower zeta bound to the right of the critical line

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann hypothesis](riemann-hypothesis.md)

Under [Riemann hypothesis](riemann-hypothesis.md), integrate a smoothed explicit formula from $1/2+\delta$ to two, taking $x=\log|t|$. The [prime](prime-number.md) contribution is $O_\delta(x^{1-2\delta})$, while the local zero count bounds the zero contribution by $O_\delta(x^{-\delta}\log|t|/\log x)$. Both are $o(\log|t|)$ for fixed $0<\delta<1/4$, yielding the displayed bound; other fixed positive shifts follow by standard strip bounds and the [Euler product](euler-product.md).
