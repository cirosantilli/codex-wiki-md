# Noetherian induction

↑ **Parent:** [Ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md)

To prove a property of all elements of a partially ordered set satisfying the [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md), assume a counterexample exists and take a maximal counterexample. All strictly larger elements satisfy the property, allowing an inductive contradiction. Applied to [ideals](ideal.md), this often replaces induction on a numerical size.
