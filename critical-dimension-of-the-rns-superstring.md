# Critical dimension of the RNS superstring

↑ **Parent:** [Critical dimension of string theory](critical-dimension-of-string-theory.md)

Each of $D$ embedding [bosons](boson.md) contributes one to the chiral [central charge](central-charge.md), and each real [worldsheet Majorana fermion](worldsheet-majorana-fermion.md) contributes one half. The reparameterization [bc system](bc-system.md) contributes $-26$, and the [superconformal ghosts](superconformal-ghost.md) contribute $+11$. Vanishing total [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) therefore requires $3D/2-26+11=0$, or $D=10$. This differs from the critical dimension 26 of the bosonic string, which has no worldsheet [fermions](fermion.md) or superconformal ghosts.
