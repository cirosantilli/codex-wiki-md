# Tamely ramified extension

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification group](ramification-group.md)

A finite extension of local fields is tamely ramified when its residue-field extension is separable and its [ramification index](ramification-index.md) is coprime to the residue characteristic. For a finite Galois extension this is equivalent to trivial [wild inertia group](wild-inertia-group.md) $G_1$.
