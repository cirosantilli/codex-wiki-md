# Monotonic photon cooling during pair annihilation

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-positron entropy transfer to photons](electron-positron-entropy-transfer-to-photons.md)

Electromagnetic comoving [entropy](entropy-split.md) conservation gives $a^3g_{*s}(T_\gamma)T_\gamma^3$ constant. Differentiation proves the displayed relation. During equilibrium electron-positron annihilation, $g_{*s}$ increases with [temperature](temperature-split.md), so the absolute [photon](photon.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md) still falls as space expands. The comoving [temperature](temperature-split.md) $aT_\gamma$ rises, and the asymptotic relativistic-to-pair-free [entropy](entropy-split.md) count heats [photons](photon.md) relative to decoupled [neutrinos](neutrino.md) by $(11/4)^{1/3}$. This relative heating must not be mistaken for an absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md) rise along the smooth expanding history.
