# miR-34 family

↑ **Parent:** [MicroRNA](microrna.md)

A [microRNA](microrna.md) family with members inducible by [p53](p53.md) in appropriate contexts. Forced expression and endogenous requirement are distinct: complete family deletion did not abolish the tested [p53](p53.md) responses in one mouse study.[Triple-family deletion](intact-p53-dependent-responses-in-mir-34-deficient-mice-by-carla-p-concepcion-and-others.md)
