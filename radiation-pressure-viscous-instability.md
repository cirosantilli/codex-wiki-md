# Radiation-pressure viscous instability

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous instability of an accretion disk](viscous-instability-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

In a characteristic vertical model of a radiation-pressure-dominated [alpha disk](alpha-disk.md) with constant [opacity](opacity.md), $\mu\sim\alpha p/\Omega\sim c/\kappa$ and $p\sim\Sigma\Omega^2H$ imply $H\propto\Sigma^{-1}$ and $\bar\nu\Sigma\propto\Sigma^{-1}$. The resulting $d(\bar\nu\Sigma)/d\Sigma<0$ gives a [viscous instability of an accretion disk](viscous-instability-of-an-accretion-disk.md), distinct from a [thermal instability of a radiation-pressure-dominated alpha disk](thermal-instability-of-a-radiation-pressure-dominated-alpha-disk.md).
