**[Obsessive-compulsive disorder](../../../obsessive-compulsive-disorder.md) and [severe health anxiety](../../../severe-health-anxiety.md) can share a cycle of threatening interpretation, distress and short-lived relief from [safety behavior](../../../safety-behavior.md), while differing in the feared meaning and the function of the response. Effective treatment therefore needs both a shared [learning](../../../learning.md) framework and an individualized [clinical formulation](../../../clinical-formulation.md).** Similar-looking checking is insufficient to distinguish the problems: checking whether one has an illness and checking to prevent oneself causing harm can involve different predictions, even when both concern health.

In [obsessive-compulsive disorder](../../../obsessive-compulsive-disorder.md), [obsessions](../../../obsession.md) are recurrent intrusive thoughts, images or urges, and [compulsions](../../../compulsion.md) are repeated actions or mental acts intended to neutralize distress or avert a feared outcome. [Paul Salkovskis](../../../paul-salkovskis.md)'s cognitive analysis located a maintaining problem in the appraisal of intrusive material, particularly [inflated responsibility](../../../inflated-responsibility.md), rather than in the mere occurrence of an unwanted thought. An intrusion can become significant because the person treats having it as evidence of danger or an obligation to act. Covert neutralization matters as well as visible washing or checking. This is a psychological account to test, not a claim that responsibility explains every presentation.[1985; proposed responsibility appraisal and neutralization cycle](../../../obsessional-compulsive-problems-a-cognitive-behavioural-analysis-by-paul-salkovskis.md)

[Health anxiety](../../../health-anxiety.md) centers on perceived illness and the interpretation of bodily changes or health information. The cognitive account proposed by [Hilary Warwick](../../../hilary-warwick.md) and colleagues links threatening illness interpretations to selective [attention](../../../attention.md), [anxiety](../../../anxiety.md) and attempts to obtain certainty. A real sensation can be amplified by monitoring, and the resulting experience can seem to confirm the original interpretation. Medical reassurance may produce temporary relief without changing the underlying rule that uncertainty is intolerable. The target is the meaning and maintaining process, not an assertion that [symptoms](../../../symptom.md) are imaginary.[1990; proposed cognitive-behavioral account of development and maintenance](../../../hypochondriasis-by-hilary-warwick-and-others.md)

Both formulations predict [negative reinforcement](../../../negative-reinforcement.md). If a ritual or request for reassurance reduces distress immediately, repetition becomes more likely even when the long-term inference is unhelpful. Avoiding a situation can also prevent [learning](../../../learning.md) what would happen without the precaution. The functional question is whether the person attributes safety to the behavior and therefore fails to update the feared prediction. Objectively useful precautions and proportionate medical care are different: their function and evidence must be assessed rather than classifying every precaution as pathological.

The differences are clinically consequential. [Obsessive-compulsive disorder](../../../obsessive-compulsive-disorder.md) can involve contamination, accidental harm, morality, symmetry or incompleteness, not just illness. The central concern may be causing harm through responsibility, whereas [health anxiety](../../../health-anxiety.md) may involve already having a disease that others have missed. Insight varies in both, so an apparently strongly held conviction does not alone establish the distinction. Likewise, unwanted thoughts can occur in [health anxiety](../../../health-anxiety.md) and [reassurance seeking](../../../reassurance-seeking.md) can be a [compulsion](../../../compulsion.md). Assessment needs the sequence of trigger, interpretation, response and consequence, with [comorbidity](../../../comorbidity.md) considered rather than forcing every person into a mutually exclusive category.

For [obsessive-compulsive disorder](../../../obsessive-compulsive-disorder.md), [exposure and response prevention](../../../exposure-and-response-prevention.md) makes the feared cue available while reducing the ritual that prevents informative [learning](../../../learning.md). The aim is to test predictions and develop a different relationship to uncertainty, not simply to maximize distress or demand certainty that harm is impossible. Cognitive work can clarify responsibility assumptions and identify mental rituals that would otherwise remain unnoticed. Outcome measures should assess time, interference and distress as well as the number of visible acts; suppressing one ritual while replacing it with covert neutralization would be an inadequate success criterion.

[Edna B. Foa](../../../edna-b-foa.md) and colleagues' adult randomized trial compared [exposure and response prevention](../../../exposure-and-response-prevention.md), [clomipramine](../../../clomipramine.md), their combination and pill placebo. The exposure-containing conditions provided strong evidence of benefit, with the combination not establishing a clear advantage over [exposure and response prevention](../../../exposure-and-response-prevention.md) alone. This supports a treatment package, not proof that one proposed cognitive change is its exclusive active mechanism. Medication effects also need not establish that the disorder is caused by a simple [neurotransmitter](../../../neurotransmitter.md) deficiency.[2005; randomized exposure/ritual prevention, clomipramine, combined and placebo conditions](../../../randomized-placebo-controlled-trial-of-exposure-and-ritual-prevention-clomipramine-and-their-combination-in-the-treatment-of-obsessive-compulsive-disorder-by-edna-b-foa-and-others.md)

For [health anxiety](../../../health-anxiety.md), [cognitive behavioral therapy](../../../cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md) examines competing explanations of [symptoms](../../../symptom.md), patterns of [attention](../../../attention.md) and the consequences of checking, avoidance and repeated reassurance. [Behavioral experiments](../../../behavioral-experiment.md) can compare a threat interpretation with an alternative maintenance explanation. A coordinated medical plan matters because genuine illness and [health anxiety](../../../health-anxiety.md) can coexist; reducing repetitive certainty seeking does not imply disregarding new clinically relevant information. A collaborative explanation respects the reality of the distress while making the maintenance account open to revision.

[Peter Tyrer](../../../peter-tyrer.md) and colleagues' CHAMP study randomized 444 medical outpatients to adapted [cognitive behavioral therapy](../../../cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md) or standard care. Concurrent physical illness was not an exclusion, and the follow-up report found an advantage in [health anxiety](../../../health-anxiety.md) [symptoms](../../../symptom.md) sustained over five years. This strengthens applicability beyond a purely psychiatric clinic. Nevertheless, improvement in the [health anxiety](../../../health-anxiety.md) outcome did not imply cost-effectiveness on every economic or quality-of-life endpoint, and comparison with standard care does not isolate each [treatment component](../../../treatment-component.md).[2017; Abstract, eligibility, health anxiety follow-up and economic outcomes](../../../cognitive-behaviour-therapy-for-health-anxiety-in-medical-patients-champ-a-randomised-controlled-trial-with-outcomes-to-5-years-by-peter-tyrer-and-others.md)

Medication evidence further discourages an absolute psychological-versus-biological division. [Brian A. Fallon](../../../brian-a-fallon.md) and colleagues randomized 195 people with hypochondriasis to [fluoxetine](../../../fluoxetine.md), [cognitive behavioral therapy](../../../cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md), both or placebo. Individual active-treatment contrasts against placebo were not significant on the primary responder definition, although secondary continuous analyses favored [fluoxetine](../../../fluoxetine.md). Thus it would be misleading either to omit the medication signal or to describe every endpoint as proving superiority. This pattern also illustrates why a positive trial abstract needs to be read alongside its prespecified outcome.[2017; primary responder analysis versus secondary continuous outcomes at 24 weeks](../../../a-randomized-controlled-trial-of-medication-and-cognitive-behavioral-therapy-for-hypochondriasis-by-brian-a-fallon-and-others.md)

The shared components are collaborative formulation, clarification of predictions, modification of maintaining [safety behaviors](../../../safety-behavior.md) and evaluation of [learning](../../../learning.md) in daily life. Their content differs with the feared catastrophe, timescale and interpretation of responsibility or illness. **Treating the functional cycle is more useful than treating checking as a uniform [symptom](../../../symptom.md), while diagnosis still helps organize assessment and select evidence-supported options.** Neither the overlap nor the treatment response establishes that the two problems are identical, and neither diagnosis removes the need for appropriate assessment of physical health and individual circumstances.
