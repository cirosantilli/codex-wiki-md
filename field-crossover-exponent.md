# Field crossover exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Critical exponent](critical-exponent.md)

The [field crossover exponent](field-crossover-exponent.md) describes the field scale $h\sim|t|^\Delta$ at which the zero-field critical regime changes. In the two-variable [scaling hypothesis for critical phenomena](scaling-hypothesis-for-critical-phenomena.md), its value is the ratio $y_h/y_t$ of magnetic and thermal [scaling dimensions](scaling-dimension.md). It is distinct from either $y_h$ or the [scaling dimension](scaling-dimension.md) of the [order parameter](order-parameter.md). The [Widom scaling relation](widom-scaling-relation.md) and differentiation of the homogeneous [free-energy density](free-energy-density.md) give the displayed identities when ordinary scaling applies.
