# Blast wave

↑ **Parent:** [Shock wave](shock-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blast_wave)

A [blast wave](blast-wave.md) is the expanding disturbance produced by localized rapid release of [energy](energy-split.md), initially led by a strong [shock wave](shock-wave.md). An energy-conserving [self-similar blast wave](self-similar-blast-wave.md) ties front speed to the mass swept up. Once the front is only weakly supersonic, a strong-shock approximation ceases to apply; a subsequent [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) is not excluded by that transition.
