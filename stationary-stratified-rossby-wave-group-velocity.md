# Stationary stratified Rossby-wave group velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Baroclinic Rossby wave](baroclinic-rossby-wave.md)

For [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega=Uk-\beta k/(k^2+\alpha^2)$ with fixed $\alpha^2\geq0$, nonzero zonal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) and stationary phase imply $U=\beta/(k^2+\alpha^2)$. Differentiating at fixed $\alpha$ yields $c_{g,x}=U+\beta(k^2-\alpha^2)/(k^2+\alpha^2)^2$, which reduces to the displayed positive value for $\beta>0$. Thus a stationary [Rossby wave](rossby-wave.md) can transport [energy](energy-split.md) eastward even though its crests stay fixed.
