# Extraembryonic ectoderm

↑ **Parent:** [Trophectoderm](trophectoderm.md)

A trophoblast-derived tissue adjacent to the early mouse [epiblast](epiblast.md). Its signalling interactions help organize [gastrulation](gastrulation.md), while it is not itself an [epiblast](epiblast.md)-derived [germ layer](germ-layer.md).
