# Rigid-motion zero modes of a spring framework

↑ **Parent:** [Zero mode](zero-mode.md)

For unstressed central Hookean [springs](spring.md), the quadratic elastic [energy](energy-split.md) is $\tfrac12\sum_{ij}k_{ij}[\mathbf n_{ij}\cdot(\mathbf u_i-\mathbf u_j)]^2$, with unit edge direction $\mathbf n_{ij}$. A common [translation](translation-geometry.md) gives zero [displacement](displacement.md) difference. An infinitesimal [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) $\mathbf u_i=\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf r_i$ also gives zero extension because a [vector](vector.md) is perpendicular to its [cross product](cross-product.md) with $\boldsymbol\Omega$. Thus a noncollinear three-dimensional free framework has three translational and three rotational [zero modes](zero-mode.md). Extra mechanisms can give additional [zero modes](zero-mode.md); six is a lower bound until rigidity is established.
