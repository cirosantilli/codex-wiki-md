<h1 id="mean-drift-from-a-fokker-planck-equation">Mean drift from a Fokker--Planck equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion approximation of a birth-death process](diffusion-approximation-of-a-birth-death-process.md)

If boundary fluxes decay sufficiently fast, two integrations by parts give

$$
\frac d{dt}\mathbb{E} X_t=\mathbb{E}[u(X_t)],
$$

while the diffusion term contributes zero to the first moment.
