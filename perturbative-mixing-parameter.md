# Perturbative mixing parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Nondegenerate perturbation theory](nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md)

The ratio $|\langle k|V|i\rangle/(E_i-E_k)|$ measures a leading admixture between unperturbed eigenstates. Small [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) compared with relevant gaps justify a nondegenerate expansion locally. A small overall perturbation does not suffice near a coupled degeneracy, where the nearly degenerate block needs diagonalization.
