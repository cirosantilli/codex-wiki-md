# Characteristic plane

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic hypersurface](characteristic-hypersurface.md)

A characteristic plane is a planar [characteristic hypersurface](characteristic-hypersurface.md). For $u_{zz}-m^2u_{xx}=0$, the planes $x\pm mz=\text{constant}$ carry the two travelling-wave families.
