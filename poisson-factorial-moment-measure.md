# Poisson factorial moment measure

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md)

For a [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md) with [intensity measure](intensity-measure-of-a-point-process.md) $\mu$, ordered tuples of distinct atoms have factorial moment [measure](measure.md) $\mu^{\otimes k}$. Thus for any nonnegative [measurable](measurability.md) function $g$,

$$
\mathbb E\sum_{z_1,\ldots,z_k\text{ distinct}}g(z_1,\ldots,z_k)=\int g\,d\mu^{\otimes k}.
$$

For $k=2$, separating diagonal terms from distinct pairs gives the second moment of a [Poisson](poisson-distribution.md) sum. Dividing by two instead counts unordered pairs, which is essential for geometric intersection intensities.
