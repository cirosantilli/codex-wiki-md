# Norming subspace of a dual space

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md)

A [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $Z\subseteq X^*$ is norming if some $c>0$ satisfies the displayed inequality. It retains enough continuous [linear functionals](linear-functional.md) to control the [norm](norm.md) of every vector. Every such [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) is dense for the [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md): otherwise a nonzero evaluation [linear functional](linear-functional.md) would annihilate its weak-star closure, contradicting the inequality. In the real case its symmetric [unit ball](unit-ball.md) makes the absolute-value and signed supremum versions equivalent.
