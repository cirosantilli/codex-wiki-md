# Ion-temperature-gradient instability

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

A drift-scale [plasma](plasma-physics.md) instability fed by an [ion temperature gradient](ion-temperature-gradient.md). In common electrostatic limits, [Boltzmann electron response](boltzmann-electron-response.md), [ion](ion.md) temperature evolution, magnetic drifts and [polarization drift](polarization-drift.md) determine the dynamics. Unfavorable [magnetic field-line curvature](magnetic-field-line-curvature.md) can drive a toroidal branch; straight-field branches can involve parallel compression. The threshold depends on the equilibrium gradients and geometry.
