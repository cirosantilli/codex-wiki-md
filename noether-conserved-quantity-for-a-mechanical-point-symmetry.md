# Noether conserved quantity for a mechanical point symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Noether theorem](noether-theorem.md)

If a time-independent [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) $L(q,\dot q)$ is invariant under a one-parameter point transformation with infinitesimal generator $\xi_i=\partial_sQ_i|_{s=0}$, then every Euler-Lagrange trajectory conserves

$$
J=\sum_i\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_i}\xi_i.
$$
