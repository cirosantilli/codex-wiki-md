# Totally ramified extension

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification index](ramification-index.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_ramified_extension)

A finite extension of local fields is totally ramified when its [residue-field degree](residue-field-degree.md) is one, equivalently when its [ramification index](ramification-index.md) equals its field degree.
