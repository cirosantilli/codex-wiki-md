# Terminal differentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular differentiation](cellular-differentiation.md)

[Terminal differentiation](terminal-differentiation.md) is advanced specialization at the end of a developmental lineage. It often includes withdrawal from the [cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md); the term does not mean that every nuclear [gene](gene.md) needed for another [cell fate](cell-fate.md) has been deleted. A mature mammalian [red blood cell](red-blood-cell.md) is an especially restrictive example because it lacks a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md); the [intestinal nucleus transfer experiment](intestinal-nucleus-transfer-experiment.md) used nucleated donors.
