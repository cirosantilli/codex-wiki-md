# Saturated elementary extension theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Saturated model](saturated-model.md)

For every infinite regular [cardinal](cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$, every [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) has a $\kappa$-saturated [elementary extension](elementary-extension.md). At one stage use the [compactness theorem](compactness-theorem.md) with the [elementary diagram](elementary-diagram-of-a-structure.md) to realize all [complete types](complete-type.md) over [subsets](subset.md) of the stage having size less than $\kappa$. There is a [set](set-split.md) of such types; finite [subsets](subset.md) of the enlarged theory are satisfiable because their finitely many witnesses are independent. Iterate for $\kappa$ stages, taking elementary [unions](set-union.md) at limits. Regularity puts any parameter [set](set-split.md) of size less than $\kappa$ inside one stage, and its type is realized at the next one. Passing to a larger [regular cardinal](regular-cardinal.md) covers singular requested degrees of saturation as well.
