# Adrenergic adipocyte assay

↑ **Parent:** [Adrenaline](adrenaline.md)

In the 1981 study, investigators incubated human [adipocytes](adipocyte.md) or their [cell membranes](cell-membrane-split.md) from fat near the abdominal lining with [epinephrine](adrenaline.md). They combined detection of bound [ligands](ligand-biochemistry.md) by [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) with biochemical readouts: [adenylyl cyclase](adenylyl-cyclase.md) activity, [cAMP](cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) and released [glycerol](glycerol.md). Blocking [alpha-2 adrenergic receptors](alpha-2-adrenergic-receptor.md) increased the [epinephrine](adrenaline.md) response; blocking [alpha-1 adrenergic receptors](alpha-1-adrenergic-receptor.md) did not. A separate labeled-[phosphate](phosphate.md) incorporation readout in a [phospholipid](phospholipid.md) showed the reverse blocker sensitivity. Thus the comparisons separated response branches.[Abstract, first through fourth paragraphs: human properitoneal preparations, radioligand binding, biochemical readouts; yohimbine versus prazosin comparisons](pharmacological-characterizations-of-adrenergic-receptors-in-human-adipocytes-by-thomas-w-burns-and-others.md)

The [glycerol](glycerol.md) readout indicates [lipolysis](lipolysis.md), rather than directly measuring whole-body fuel use. Isolated [adipocytes](adipocyte.md) omit circulation and nervous input; blocker selectivity also limits the inference. These comparisons support receptor-specific responses in that preparation, not identical behavior in all [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md).
