# Sampling without replacement need not be independent

↑ **Parent:** [Independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md)

Draw two different labels uniformly from $\{1,2,3,4\}$ using [sampling without replacement](sampling-without-replacement.md). Each position has a [uniform distribution on a finite set](discrete-uniform-distribution.md), but $\mathbb P(X_1=X_2)=0$. Two [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) with those same [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md) would match with probability $1/4$. Equal marginal laws therefore do not make this sample an [IID sample](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md). Mark some of those four labels as successes. Their count in the two draws has a [hypergeometric distribution](hypergeometric-distribution.md); independent [Bernoulli trials](bernoulli-trial.md) with a common success probability give a [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md).
