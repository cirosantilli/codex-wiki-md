# Principal-axis reduction of a quadric

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md)

Write a real quadratic polynomial as $x^THx+2q^Tx+c$, with $H$ symmetric. If $H$ is invertible, translation by $x_0=-H^{-1}q$ removes the linear term. An orthonormal eigenbasis then makes the remaining [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) diagonal. Eigenvalue signs and the translated constant determine the real geometry, such as an ellipsoid or a [one-sheet hyperboloid](one-sheet-hyperboloid.md). A singular $H$ requires separate treatment of linear terms in its null directions; a center need not exist.
