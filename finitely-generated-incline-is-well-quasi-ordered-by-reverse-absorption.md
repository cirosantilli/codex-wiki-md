# Finitely generated incline is well-quasi-ordered by reverse absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Incline](incline.md)

Expand every term in finitely many generators as a finite nonempty sum of [monomials](monomial.md), and associate its exponent support with the upward-closed [subset](subset.md) of $\mathbb N^r$ that it generates. The [reverse-inclusion well-quasi-ordering of monomial ideals](reverse-inclusion-well-quasi-ordering-of-monomial-ideals.md) supplies an earlier support upset containing a later one. Every later [monomial](monomial.md) is then a multiple of an earlier [monomial](monomial.md), hence is absorbed by it under $a+ab=a$. The earlier sum absorbs the later sum. Thus every infinite term [sequence](sequence.md) has an earlier element preceding a later one in the reverse natural order. Reflexivity, antisymmetry and transitivity follow directly from commutative idempotent addition.
