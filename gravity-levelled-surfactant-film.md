# Gravity-levelled surfactant film

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity smoothing of a Marangoni front](gravity-smoothing-of-a-marangoni-front.md)

After [gravity smoothing of a Marangoni front](gravity-smoothing-of-a-marangoni-front.md) reaches the whole spreading pool, the film approaches uniform depth while surface transport continues. A small height gradient creates a [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) return flow that almost cancels the net [Marangoni stress](marangoni-effect.md) flux. Setting $q\simeq0$ in the [thin-film equations with insoluble surfactant](thin-film-equations-with-insoluble-surfactant.md) gives $h_x\simeq-3AC_x/(2\rho gh_0)$ and fractional depth variation $O(AM/(\rho gh_0^2x_N))\ll1$. The depressed region under the [surfactant](surfactant.md) therefore becomes progressively shallower.
