# Circular-orbit energy ratio in a power-law potential

↑ **Parent:** [Circular orbit](circular-orbit.md)

A [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) in a [central potential](central-potential.md) $U(r)=Ar^s$ requires inward [force](force-split.md) $U'(r)=mv^2/r>0$. Thus $2K=rU'(r)=sU$, giving $K/U=s/2$ for the specified zero of potential. The ratio depends on that choice of zero; adding a constant to $U$ leaves the orbit unchanged but changes $K/U$. This is the circular special case of the [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md).
