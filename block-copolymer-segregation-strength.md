# Block copolymer segregation strength

↑ **Parent:** [Diblock copolymer](diblock-copolymer.md)

The product $\chi N$ of the [Flory interaction parameter](flory-interaction-parameter.md) and [degree of polymerization](degree-of-polymerization.md), using compatible reference-segment conventions. It compares unlike-contact avoidance with chain entropy; solvent dilution need not change the chemical chain length.
