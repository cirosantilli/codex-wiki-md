# Zero section of a vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

The [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) sends each base point to the zero vector in its fiber. It is a smooth [section of a vector bundle](section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) and an embedded copy of the base. The [Liouville one-form](canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) vanishes as an ambient covector precisely along the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) of a [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md).
