# Thermal equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_equilibrium)

Systems are in thermal equilibrium when no net heat flows between them; their temperatures are equal.
