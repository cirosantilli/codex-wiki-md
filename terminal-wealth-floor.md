# Terminal wealth floor

↑ **Parent:** [Expected utility maximization](expected-utility-maximization.md)

A terminal [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md) floor is a prescribed lower bound, possibly a [random variable](random-variable-split.md), on terminal portfolio [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md). In a [complete market](complete-market.md), its [state-price density](state-price-density.md) cost cannot exceed initial [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md). Nonnegative admissibility replaces a possibly negative floor by its positive part.
