# Shock speed in a symmetric cloud collision

↑ **Parent:** [Shock wave](shock-wave.md)

Two identical cold clouds approach at speeds $\pm V$ in their center-of-momentum frame. [Reflection symmetry](reflection-symmetry.md) makes the uniformly compressed gas stationary. A shock moving outward at $S$ sees upstream speed $V+S$ and downstream speed $S$; their ratio equals the strong adiabatic compression $(\gamma+1)/(\gamma-1)$. Solving gives the displayed speed. For a [monatomic gas](monatomic-gas.md) $S=V/3$. Finite-cloud breakout ends the steady upstream supply and requires a subsequent boundary-release calculation.
