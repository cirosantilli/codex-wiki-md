# Reciprocal spiral with its endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Path-connected space](path-connected-space.md)

The map $F(s)=se^{2\pi i/s}$ for $s>0$, $F(0)=0$, is [continuous](continuous-function.md) on $[0,1]$, since $|F(s)|=s$. Its image is the displayed spiral together with its endpoint. The image is [compact](compact-space.md) and [path-connected](path-connected-space.md): between images of $s_0,s_1$ the explicit path is $F((1-u)s_0+us_1)$.
