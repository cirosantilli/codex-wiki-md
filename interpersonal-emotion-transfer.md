# Interpersonal emotion transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Emotion](emotion.md)

A change in one person's [emotion](emotion.md) attributable to information or influence from another person. It includes matching and complementary responses. Evidence must distinguish such influence from two people independently reacting to a shared event.
