<h1 id="first-lubin-tate-torsion-fields-for-the-p-adic-numbers">First Lubin–Tate torsion fields for the p-adic numbers</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lubin–Tate torsion](lubin-tate-torsion.md)

The two Lubin–Tate series $(1+X)^p-1$ and $X^p+pX$ for $\mathbb Q_p$ have respective nonzero $p$-torsion points $\zeta_p-1$ and the roots of $X^{p-1}=-p$. A [Lubin–Tate change of series](lubin-tate-change-of-series.md) identifies their torsion fields, so

$$
\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_p)=\mathbb Q_p(\sqrt[p-1]{-p}).
$$
