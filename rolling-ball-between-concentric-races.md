# Rolling ball between concentric races

↑ **Parent:** [Ball bearing](ball-bearing.md)

If an inner race of radius $R$ rotates at [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega$ and an outer race is stationary, a ball of radius $r$ rolling at both contacts has centre speed $R\omega/2$ and spin $-R\omega/(2r)$. Its centre follows a circle of radius $R+r$. The [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) about the common race axis includes both orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and spin.
