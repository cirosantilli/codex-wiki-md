# Surface roughness

↑ **Parent:** [Material perception](material-perception.md)

Variation of surface geometry over a specified range of spatial scales. Its optical effects depend on scale relative to [wavelength](wavelength.md) and imaging resolution, material and illumination; perceived roughness is not a direct measurement of one universal roughness parameter.
