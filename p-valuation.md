# p-valuation

↑ **Parent:** [Filtration on a group](filtration-on-a-group.md)

For a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$, a p-valuation is a separated [filtration on a group](filtration-on-a-group.md) such that every $x\ne1$ satisfies

$$
\omega(x)>\frac1{p-1},
\qquad
\omega(x^p)=\omega(x)+1.
$$

A group equipped with one is called a p-valued group.
