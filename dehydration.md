# Dehydration

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Net loss of [water](water.md). A [nitroaldol reaction](henry-reaction.md) can first give an [alcohol](alcohol.md); eliminating water from that product is a separate reaction rather than an automatic part of the addition mechanism.
