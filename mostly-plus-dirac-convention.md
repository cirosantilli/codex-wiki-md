# Mostly-plus Dirac convention

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md)

One consistent [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) convention uses $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$, $\{\gamma^a,\gamma^b\}=-2\eta^{ab}$, and $(i\gamma^a\partial_a+m)\psi=0$. Taking the negatives of the standard mostly-minus [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md), and defining the [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md) with the new $\gamma^0$, gives the same physical [Dirac action](dirac-action.md). The positive-frequency plane wave is $e^{ip\cdot x}u(p)$ with $p^0>0$, and its equation is $(\not p-m)u=0$. The [mass shell](mass-shell.md) is $p^2=-m^2$. These signs must be translated together when using a formula stated in another convention.
