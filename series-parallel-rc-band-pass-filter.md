# Series-parallel RC band-pass filter

↑ **Parent:** [Band-pass filter](band-pass-filter.md)

A series [resistor](resistor.md) $R$ and [capacitor](capacitor.md) $C$ feed a parallel pair with the same values. Measuring the parallel pair's [voltage](voltage.md) relative to the return gives this unloaded [transfer function](transfer-function.md) for [phasors](phasor.md) proportional to $e^{i\omega t}$. The [voltage divider](voltage-divider.md) applied to $Z_s=R+1/(i\omega C)$ and $Z_p=R/(1+i\omega RC)$ proves the formula. The [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) peaks at $\omega=1/(RC)$ with ratio $1/3$ and zero relative [phase](phase-waves.md). At low and high positive [frequencies](frequency.md), the [transfer function](transfer-function.md) tends respectively to $i\omega RC$ and $-i/(\omega RC)$. Reversing output polarity changes its sign, while loading changes the response itself.
