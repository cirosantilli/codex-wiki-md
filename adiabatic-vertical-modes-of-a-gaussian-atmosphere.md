# Adiabatic vertical modes of a Gaussian atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md)

A [plane-parallel atmosphere](plane-parallel-atmosphere.md) with [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md) $c_s$ in gravity $-\Omega^2z$ has [mass density](density.md) $\rho_0e^{-z^2/(2H^2)}$, $H=c_s/\Omega$. Above a rigid base, vertically directed [adiabatic fluid perturbations](adiabatic-fluid-perturbation.md) satisfy $w''-zw'/H^2+(\sigma^2/\Omega^2-1)w/(\gamma H^2)=0$ and $w(0)=0$. Set $x=z/H$ and $\lambda=(\sigma^2/\Omega^2-1)/\gamma$. The substitution $y=e^{-x^2/4}w$ converts finite Gaussian-weighted [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) into square integrability for $-y''+x^2y/4=(\lambda+1/2)y$. Odd extension across the base and the oscillator lowering operator force $\lambda$ to be an odd positive integer. Thus $w_n$ is proportional to the [Probabilists' Hermite polynomial](probabilists-hermite-polynomial.md) $\mathrm{He}_{2n+1}(z/H)$. These are vertical [acoustic waves](acoustic-wave.md); a general stably stratified atmosphere also admits [internal gravity waves](internal-wave.md) with horizontal structure.
