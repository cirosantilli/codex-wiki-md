# Strictly copositive matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Copositive matrix](copositive-matrix.md)

A real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) whose [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) is positive at every nonzero [vector](vector.md) in the [nonnegative orthant](nonnegative-orthant.md). By [compactness](compact-space.md) of the nonnegative [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md), this is equivalent to a positive uniform lower bound $x^TAx\geq\delta\|x\|_2^2$ on that orthant. It need not be a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md).
