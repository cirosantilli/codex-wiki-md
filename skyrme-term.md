# Skyrme term

↑ **Parent:** [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md)

The particular quartic-derivative interaction in the [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md), with $L_\mu=U^\dagger\partial_\mu U$. Its static energy is positive and scales inversely with soliton size, opposing collapse under [Derrick scaling](derrick-scaling.md). It is one choice of four-derivative mesonic interaction, not the whole general fourth-order [chiral perturbation theory](chiral-perturbation-theory.md) Lagrangian.
