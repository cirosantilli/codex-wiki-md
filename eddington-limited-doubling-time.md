# Eddington-limited doubling time

↑ **Parent:** [Salpeter time](salpeter-time.md)

At constant [radiative efficiency of black-hole accretion](radiative-efficiency-of-black-hole-accretion.md) $\eta$ and the [Eddington luminosity](eddington-luminosity.md), a [black hole](black-hole-split.md) grows exponentially with the [Salpeter time](salpeter-time.md) $t_S=\eta t_E/(1-\eta)$, where $t_E=\sigma_Tc/(4\pi Gm_p)$. Its doubling time is $t_S\log2$. The factor $1-\eta$ accounts for the supplied [rest energy](rest-energy.md) that escapes as [radiation](radiation.md) instead of increasing the hole's [mass](mass.md).
