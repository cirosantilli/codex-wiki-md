# Light-second

↑ **Parent:** [Speed of light](speed-of-light.md)

A [light-second](light-second.md) is a unit of length: the distance vacuum [light](light.md) travels in one second. It is useful in a [spacetime diagram](spacetime-diagram.md) because a time of one second corresponds to a $ct$ coordinate of one [light-second](light-second.md), making the slopes $x=\pm ct$ of the [light cone](light-cone.md) particularly easy to read. The unit is a distance, rather than an elapsed time.
