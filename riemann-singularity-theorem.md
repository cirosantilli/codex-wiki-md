# Riemann singularity theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Theta divisor](theta-divisor.md)

For a [compact Riemann surface](compact-riemann-surface.md) of genus $g\ge1$, the [theta divisor](theta-divisor.md) in $\operatorname{Pic}^{g-1}(X)$ has the displayed multiplicity. A local [determinant description of the theta divisor](determinant-description-of-the-theta-divisor.md) reduces to an $r$ by $r$ [matrix](matrix.md) vanishing at $L$, where $r=h^0(L)$. Its linear term is cup product with a tangent class in $H^1(\mathcal O_X)$. The [invertible cup-product direction for a line bundle on a curve](invertible-cup-product-direction-for-a-line-bundle-on-a-curve.md) shows that its [determinant](determinant.md) has order exactly $r$. Consequently the [singular locus](singular-locus.md) consists precisely of classes with at least two independent [global sections](global-section.md).
