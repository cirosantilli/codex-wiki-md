# Placental mammal

↑ **Parent:** [Eutheria](eutheria.md)

A member of the [crown group](crown-group.md) containing all living placental lineages. The presence of a placenta alone is not a sufficient phylogenetic definition: forms of placentation also occur in other [mammals](mammal.md).
