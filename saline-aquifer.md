# Saline aquifer

↑ **Parent:** [Aquifer](aquifer.md)

An [aquifer](aquifer.md) containing saline rather than fresh water. A deep [saline aquifer](saline-aquifer.md) can provide pore space for [carbon capture and storage](carbon-capture-and-storage.md), but safe retention depends on reservoir connectivity, continuous [caprock](caprock.md) and pressure management.
