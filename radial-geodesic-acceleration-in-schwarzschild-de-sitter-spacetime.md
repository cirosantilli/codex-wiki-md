# Radial geodesic acceleration in Schwarzschild-de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild-de Sitter spacetime](schwarzschild-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

For $ds^2=A\,dt^2-dr^2/A-r^2d\Omega^2$, $A=1-2GM/r-\Lambda r^2/3$, and $c=1$, a radial timelike [geodesic](geodesic.md) has $\dot r^2=k^2-A$. The radial [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) gives $\ddot r=-A'/2=-GM/r^2+\Lambda r/3$, including at a [turning point](turning-point.md). This is a second [derivative](derivative.md) of the [areal radius](areal-radius.md) with respect to [proper time](proper-time.md); the [geodesic](geodesic.md)'s [four-acceleration](four-acceleration.md) still vanishes. In the slow-motion weak-field limit, restoring units gives $d^2r/dt^2=-GM/r^2+\Lambda c^2r/3$.
