# Wilsonian coarse-grained statistical Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum-shell renormalization group](momentum-shell-renormalization-group.md)

Integrate field modes with momenta between a fixed microscopic [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md) $\Lambda_0$ and a lower cutoff $\Lambda$, retaining the modes below $\Lambda$ as backgrounds. The exact [statistical Hamiltonian](statistical-hamiltonian.md) includes all generated interactions and field-independent constants. Integrating its retained modes reproduces the original [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md), so long-distance observables do not depend on the arbitrary intermediate cutoff. With sources retained, the corresponding coarse observables reproduce long-distance response and [connected correlation functions](connected-correlation-function.md). A local [gradient expansion](gradient-expansion.md) or a finite-coupling truncation is an additional approximation, rather than a consequence of cutoff independence. The momentum-shell construction is also explained in [the Cambridge statistical field theory lecture notes](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/sft/sfthtml/S3.html).
