# Fixed-chemical-potential Sommerfeld density increase

↑ **Parent:** [Sommerfeld expansion](sommerfeld-expansion.md)

For a three-dimensional parabolic band and fixed positive $\mu-E_c$, the [Sommerfeld expansion](sommerfeld-expansion.md) gives $n(T)=n(0)[1+\pi^2(k_BT/(\mu-E_c))^2/8+O(T^4)]$. This increase differs from the usual fixed-density calculation, in which the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) decreases with [temperature](temperature-split.md). The exact density is proportional to $T^{3/2}F_{1/2}[(\mu-E_c)/(k_BT)]$.
