# Square of a standard Laplace variable

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace distribution](laplace-distribution.md)

For a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ with [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $e^{-|x|}/2$, the event $X^2\leq y$ is $-\sqrt y\leq X\leq\sqrt y$ for $y\geq0$. Integrating the symmetric density gives the displayed [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md); it is zero for negative $y$. The density on $y>0$ is $e^{-\sqrt y}/(2\sqrt y)$. Its integrable singularity at zero does not create an atom, because the [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is continuous there.
