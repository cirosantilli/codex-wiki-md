# Energy uniqueness for traction-driven elasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave](elastic-wave.md)

The difference of two solutions with the same [body force](body-force.md), boundary [traction](traction.md) and initial data satisfies a homogeneous elastic equation with zero boundary [work](work.md). Multiplication by its [velocity](velocity.md) and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) conserve the displayed positive [energy](energy.md). Zero initial [energy](energy.md) forces zero [velocity](velocity.md) for all times, and zero initial [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) then forces zero [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md). Rigid zero-strain motions do not cause nonuniqueness after initial data are fixed. This assumes positive [mass density](density.md), stable real elastic moduli and sufficient regularity for the [energy](energy.md) identity.
