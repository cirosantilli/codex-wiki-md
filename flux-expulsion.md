# Flux expulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive magnetohydrodynamics](resistive-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

Flux expulsion is redistribution of [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) out of the interiors of persistent circulating flows into their edges and surrounding regions. In two-dimensional [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), a magnetic flux function obeys $\partial_t A+\mathbf u\cdot\nabla A=\eta\nabla^2 A$. At large but finite [magnetic Reynolds number](magnetic-reynolds-number.md), circulation stretches gradients of $A$, enabling resistive smoothing and weak interior [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) with strong field near the circulation boundary. Finite [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) is essential to the eventual rearrangement; exact ideal [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md) alone does not justify a flux-free steady cell. Magnetic back reaction can limit the process.
