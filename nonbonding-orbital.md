# Nonbonding orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md)

A [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) with little or no net bonding interaction between the atoms in the stated orbital model. A symmetry-isolated [atomic orbital](atomic-orbital.md) can be exactly nonbonding in a minimal basis: the out-of-plane nitrogen $2p_z$ orbital in planar [ammonia](ammonia.md) has no matching hydrogen [symmetry orbital](symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md). A mainly localized [lone pair](lone-pair.md) can acquire bonding admixture when a distortion permits additional mixing.
