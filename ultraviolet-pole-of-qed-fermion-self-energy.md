# Ultraviolet pole of QED fermion self-energy

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion self-energy](fermion-self-energy.md)

In [Feynman gauge](feynman-gauge.md), with insertion $-i\Sigma(p)$ and $d=4-2\epsilon$, the one-loop [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) result is $\Sigma_{\rm UV}=e^2(-\not p+4m)/(16\pi^2\epsilon)$. The coefficient of $\not p$ is the electron kinetic [counterterm](counterterm.md) $\delta_2=-e^2/(16\pi^2\epsilon)$ when its insertion is $i\not p\delta_2$. The scalar part fixes a separate [mass counterterm](mass-counterterm.md) in the chosen normalization convention.
