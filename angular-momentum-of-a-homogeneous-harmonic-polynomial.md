# Angular momentum of a homogeneous harmonic polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md)

For a homogeneous [harmonic polynomial](harmonic-polynomial.md) $H_l$ of degree $l$ in three dimensions, the [Euler theorem for homogeneous functions](euler-theorem-for-homogeneous-functions.md) gives $DH_l=lH_l$, where $D=\mathbf x\cdot\nabla$. The [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) identity $\hat L^2=-\hbar^2(r^2\Delta-D^2-D)$ therefore gives $\hat L^2H_l=l(l+1)\hbar^2H_l$. This constructs [angular momentum eigenstates](angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) directly from polynomials.
