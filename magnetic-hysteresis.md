# Magnetic hysteresis

↑ **Parent:** [Hysteresis](hysteresis.md)

[Magnetic hysteresis](magnetic-hysteresis.md) means that a material's [magnetization](magnetization.md) depends on its field history as well as the present applied field. A closed quasistatic magnetic cycle dissipates [energy](energy-split.md) per unit volume equal to its $H$–$B$ loop area. It is relevant to remanence in [permanent magnets](permanent-magnet.md) and core losses in [electrical transformers](transformer-electrical-engineering.md).
