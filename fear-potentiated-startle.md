# Fear-potentiated startle

↑ **Parent:** [Fear conditioning](fear-conditioning.md)

Enhancement of the [acoustic startle reflex](acoustic-startle-reflex.md) in the presence of a cue associated with threat. It operationalizes a defensive response rather than directly reading subjective [fear](fear.md).
