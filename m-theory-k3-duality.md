# M-theory K3 duality

↑ **Parent:** [M-theory](m-theory.md)

[M-theory](m-theory.md) on a [K3 surface](k3-surface.md) and the [heterotic string](heterotic-string.md) on a three-torus have matching seven-dimensional moduli and charge lattices. The [K3 intersection lattice](k3-intersection-lattice.md) supplies twenty-two vector charges; an [M5-brane](m5-brane.md) wrapped on K3 becomes the heterotic [fundamental string](fundamental-string.md). The strong-coupling interpretation and low-energy matching are derived in section 4.7 of [String Theory Dynamics in Various Dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9503124).
