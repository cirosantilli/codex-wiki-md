# Four-layer CIP sheet resistance

↑ **Parent:** [CIP thickness-averaged two-current model](cip-thickness-averaged-two-current-model.md)

Let $D=t_1+t_2$, $a=t_1\rho_L+t_2\rho_N$ and $b=t_1\rho_H+t_2\rho_N$. A square of four layers has total thickness $2D$. For parallel moments its channel [sheet resistances](sheet-resistance.md) are $a/(2D^2)$ and $b/(2D^2)$; for antiparallel moments both are $(a+b)/(4D^2)$. Adding parallel spin conductances gives the title. The factor of four-layer thickness is essential; changing the normalization changes the prefactors. The nonmagnetic bulk resistivity would be $\rho_N/2$ when $\rho_N$ is this model's per-spin value.
