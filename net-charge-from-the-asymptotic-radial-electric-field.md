# Net charge from the asymptotic radial electric field

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)

For a spherically symmetric [electric field](electric-field-split.md) in [vacuum](vacuum.md), integrate [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) over a sphere. Its [electric flux](electric-flux.md) is $4\pi r^2E_r(r)$, so taking the enclosing [radius](radius.md) to infinity gives the displayed total charge, whenever that limit and total charge exist. In particular, a radial field decaying faster than $r^{-2}$ can be nonzero locally while its total source charge is zero. The origin must also be checked: a point charge contributes a nonzero limiting flux as $r\to0$.
