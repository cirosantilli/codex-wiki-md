# Pareto highest density interval

↑ **Parent:** [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md)

For the [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md) of shape $a>0$ and lower bound $b>0$, the density $ab^ax^{-a-1}$ is strictly decreasing on $[b,\infty)$. Its highest-density interval of probability $1-\alpha$, $0<\alpha<1$, must begin at $b$. The tail probability $\mathbb P(X>u)=(b/u)^a$ makes the upper endpoint $u=b\alpha^{-1/a}$. If this is a [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md), the result is its [highest posterior density interval](highest-posterior-density-interval.md). In particular, [Uniform-Pareto conjugacy](uniform-pareto-conjugacy.md) supplies posterior shape $a+n$ and lower bound $\max(b,\max_i y_i)$.
