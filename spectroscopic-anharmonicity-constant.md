# Spectroscopic anharmonicity constant

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)

In the quadratic vibrational term expression $E_v/(hc)=\widetilde\omega_e(v+1/2)-\widetilde\omega_ex_e(v+1/2)^2$, the constant $x_e$ is dimensionless, while $\widetilde\omega_ex_e$ has units of [spectroscopic wavenumber](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md). A positive value makes adjacent level spacings decrease. For an exact [Morse oscillator](morse-oscillator.md), $D_e/(hc)=\widetilde\omega_e/(4x_e)$. Using only two low-lying transitions to extrapolate this identity to a real molecule assumes the higher-order anharmonic terms are negligible all the way to dissociation.
