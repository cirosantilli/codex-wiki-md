# Fermion mass

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md)

The rest [mass](mass.md) of a [fermion](fermion.md). In the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md), charged-fermion [masses](mass.md) arise from couplings to the [Higgs field](higgs-field.md); at [energies](energy-split.md) much greater than these [masses](mass.md), [helicity](helicity.md) and [chirality](chirality-physics.md) can be related with small mass corrections.
