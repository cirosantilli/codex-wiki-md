# Superhedging

↑ **Parent:** [Claim replication](claim-replication.md)

An admissible [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) superhedges a [contingent claim](contingent-claim.md) when its terminal wealth is at least the claim payoff [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). The least permitted initial cost is the [superhedging price](superhedging-price.md). Exact [claim replication](claim-replication.md) requires equality.
