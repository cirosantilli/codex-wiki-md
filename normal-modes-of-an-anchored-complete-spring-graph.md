# Normal modes of an anchored complete spring graph

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate normal modes from permutation symmetry](degenerate-normal-modes-from-permutation-symmetry.md)

Connect every pair among $n$ moving scalar coordinates and one fixed coordinate by identical [springs](spring.md) of stiffness $k$. The coupling pattern is the [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $K_{n+1}$ with one vertex fixed. Its [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is $k\sum_{i<j}(X_i-X_j)^2/2$. The moving-coordinate [stiffness matrix](stiffness-matrix.md) is the principal submatrix displayed in the header. For equal [masses](mass.md) $m$, the uniform [normal mode](normal-mode.md) has squared [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $k/m$, while every sum-zero vector has squared angular frequency $(n+1)k/m$. There are $n-1$ such degenerate modes. This follows directly from the [Laplacian spectrum of a complete graph](laplacian-spectrum-of-a-complete-graph.md) and its rank-one matrix form. Constant gravitational forces shift the [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) without changing the stiffness.
