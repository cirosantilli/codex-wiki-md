# Thrust

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

A propulsive [force](force-split.md) exerted on a body by an engine or another momentum-transfer mechanism. In a vehicle moving through a fluid, its direction need not coincide with the vehicle's actual [velocity](velocity.md), and a transverse component can steer the motion. The component along the velocity contributes mechanical [power](power.md) $\mathbf T\cdot\mathbf v$; a purely transverse component changes direction without doing instantaneous work. [Rocket thrust](rocket-thrust.md) is one particular mechanism and includes momentum carried by expelled material.
