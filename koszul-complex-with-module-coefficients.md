# Koszul complex with module coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Koszul complex](koszul-complex.md)

Tensor the finite free [Koszul complex](koszul-complex.md) on $f_1,\ldots,f_r$ with the module $M$. For a [regular sequence on a module](regular-sequence-on-a-module.md), its positive [Koszul homology](koszul-homology.md) vanishes. If the sequence is regular on $R$, this complex instead computes $\operatorname{Tor}^R(R/(f),M)$ for arbitrary $M$.
