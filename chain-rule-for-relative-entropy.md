# Chain rule for relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md)

For [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md) $P$ and $Q$ on a product space, the [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) decomposes into the divergence of a marginal and the expected divergence of the corresponding conditional distributions. Iteration gives

$$
D(Q\Vert P)=\sum_i\mathbb E_QD\bigl(Q_i(\mathord\cdot\mid X_{<i})\Vert P_i(\mathord\cdot\mid X_{<i})\bigr).
$$
