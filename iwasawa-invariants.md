# Iwasawa invariants

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic ideal](characteristic-ideal.md)

The invariants $\mu,\lambda$ of a finitely generated torsion [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md) measure its $p$-power elementary factors and distinguished-polynomial factors. Vanishing of $\mu$ means that the module is finitely generated over $\mathbb Z_p$, up to finite error. It does not mean that the module is finite: a factor $\Lambda/(T)$ has $\mu=0$ and is $\mathbb Z_p$.
