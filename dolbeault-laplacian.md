# Dolbeault Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler identities](kahler-identities.md)

The Dolbeault Laplacian is $\Delta_{\bar\partial}=\bar\partial\bar\partial^*+\bar\partial^*\bar\partial$. On a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), the [Kähler identities](kahler-identities.md) imply

$$
\Delta_d=2\Delta_\partial=2\Delta_{\bar\partial}.
$$
