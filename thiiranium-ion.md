# Thiiranium ion

↑ **Parent:** [Sulfonium ion](sulfonium-ion.md)

A three-membered ring containing a positively charged [sulfur](sulfur.md) atom and two [carbon](carbon.md) atoms. Nucleophilic opening at [carbon](carbon.md) normally involves [backside attack](backside-attack.md) and cleavage of one [sulfur](sulfur.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) bond.
