# Recombinase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A [recombinase](recombinase.md) is an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that catalyzes a genetic recombination reaction. The [RecA](reca.md) family performs homology-dependent [DNA strand exchange](dna-strand-exchange.md); the term also includes enzymes with other recombination mechanisms.
