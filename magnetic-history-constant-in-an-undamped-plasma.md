# Magnetic-history constant in an undamped plasma

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect conductor](perfect-conductor.md)

The linear carrier acceleration equation $\dot{\mathbf j}=\epsilon_0\omega_p^2\mathbf E$ and [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) imply this conserved local quantity. Setting its initial value to zero gives the second [London equation](london-equations.md) and [London screening](london-screening.md). An undamped response alone does not select that initial value, so it must not be used to infer expulsion of a pre-existing [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) for every magnetic history.[pp. 71–74: acceleration theory and the additional superconducting relation](the-electromagnetic-equations-of-the-supraconductor-by-fritz-london-and-heinz-london.md)
