# Bass-Serre tree

↑ **Parent:** [Bass-Serre theory](bass-serre-theory.md)

For $G=A*_CB$, the Bass-Serre tree has vertices $G/A\sqcup G/B$ and edges $G/C$, with $gC$ joining $gA$ to $gB$. Vertex degrees are the indices of the corresponding images of $C$.
