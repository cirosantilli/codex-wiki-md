# Union of three incomparable nonprime ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)

In $\mathbb F_2[u,v]/(u,v)^2$, the [ideals](ideal.md) $(u)$, $(v)$ and $(u+v)$ are pairwise incomparable and nonprime. Their union is the [ideal](ideal.md) $(u,v)$: the three lines cover its two-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) over $\mathbb F_2$. This shows why a primality hypothesis matters in [prime avoidance](prime-avoidance.md).
