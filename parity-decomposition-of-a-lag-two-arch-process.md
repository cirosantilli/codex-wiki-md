# Parity decomposition of a lag-two ARCH process

↑ **Parent:** [Lag-two ARCH process](lag-two-arch-process.md)

The stationary squared process has the positive series $X_t^2=\alpha_0\sum_{k\geq0}\alpha_2^k\prod_{j=0}^k\varepsilon_{t-2j}^2$. Even and odd observations therefore use disjoint families of [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md). This explains zero lag-one [covariance](covariance.md) of the squares despite dependence at lag two.
