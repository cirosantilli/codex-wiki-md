# Chiral active Brownian displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

A planar self-propelled particle with translational [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$, propulsion speed $v$, rotational diffusion $D_r$ and constant angular drift $\omega$ has orientation correlation $e^{-D_r|t|}\cos(\omega t)$. Integrating this correlation twice gives the displayed mean-square displacement. For $D_r>0$, its long-time [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) is $D+v^2D_r/[2(D_r^2+\omega^2)]$. The full position-angle process has the [Markov property](markov-property.md), whereas position alone generally retains orientational memory. The angular second moment refers to an unwrapped angle.
