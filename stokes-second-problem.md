# Stokes' second problem

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)

[Stokes' second problem](stokes-second-problem.md) describes the oscillatory shear flow generated by a tangentially oscillating flat plate in a semi-infinite [viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md). The [velocity](velocity.md) satisfies $u_t=\nu u_{xx}$, with $u(0,t)=U_0\cos\omega t$ and decay away from the plate. For $\omega>0$, the displayed time-periodic solution follows by taking the real part of $U_0e^{i\omega t-(1+i)x/\delta}$. The length $\delta$ is the penetration depth: both amplitude attenuation and phase lag grow with $x/\delta$. Boundary data alone also allow homogeneous transients; the periodic solution describes the established oscillation after those transients decay.
