# Two-sided top-hat line plume

↑ **Parent:** [Line plume](line-plume.md)

For a [top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md) with full width $w$, upward speed $U$, [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $b$, and edge [entrainment coefficient](entrainment-coefficient.md) $e$, define [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit span $V=wU$, kinematic [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) $M=wU^2$, and [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) $B=wUb$. A uniform ambient and two exposed edges give $V'=2eU$, $M'=wb=B/U$, and $B'=0$. A [pure plume](pure-plume.md) with $V(0)=0$ has

$$
U=\left(\frac B{2e}\right)^{1/3},\qquad w=2ez,\qquad V=(2e)^{2/3}B^{1/3}z.
$$

Indeed $M=UV$ with constant $U$ reduces the momentum balance to $2eU^3=B$. If width means half-width, its growth coefficient is $e$, not $2e$; profile shape factors also alter the coefficients.
