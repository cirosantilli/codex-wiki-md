# Glycine

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid composition](amino-acid-composition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glycine)

[Glycine](glycine.md) is an [amino acid](amino-acid.md), represented by Gly or G for an incorporated [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md). Its [amino acid side chain](amino-acid-side-chain.md) is a [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). It was identified at one [insulin](insulin.md) [N-terminus](n-terminus.md) in [Sanger's insulin sequencing experiment](sanger-s-insulin-sequencing-experiment.md). See [IUPAC Blue Book, P-103.1: amino-acid names, symbols and structures](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/P10.html).
