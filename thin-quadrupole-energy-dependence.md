# Thin-quadrupole energy dependence

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrupole magnet](quadrupole-magnet.md)

At fixed gradient and magnet length, the signed thin-lens [focal length](focal-length.md) is proportional to particle [momentum](momentum-split.md). For total [energy](energy-split.md) error $\delta=(E-E_0)/E_0$, differentiating $p^2c^2=E^2-m^2c^4$ gives $d\ln p/d\ln E=1/\beta^2$. The ultrarelativistic result is $f=f_0(1+\delta)$ to first order. If energy means kinetic energy instead, the coefficient is $(K+mc^2)/(K+2mc^2)$.
