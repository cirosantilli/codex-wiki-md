# Grue

↑ **Parent:** [New riddle of induction](new-riddle-of-induction.md)

For a fixed cutoff $t$, let $E_t(x)$ mean examined before $t$. [Nelson Goodman](nelson-goodman.md)'s predicate is $G_t(x)\iff(E_t(x)\land\operatorname{green}(x))\lor(\neg E_t(x)\land\operatorname{blue}(x))$. An already examined green object remains grue after $t$ without a colour change. The conflicting projection concerns objects not examined before $t$.
