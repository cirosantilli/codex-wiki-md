# Character inversion identity

↑ **Parent:** [Character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md)

For a [complex representation](complex-representation.md) of a [finite group](finite-group.md), the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of each represented element are [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md). They have [absolute value](absolute-value.md) one, so inversion equals [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) on each [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). Taking [traces](matrix-trace.md) gives the identity.
