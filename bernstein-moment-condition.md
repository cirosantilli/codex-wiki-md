# Bernstein moment condition

↑ **Parent:** [Subexponential distribution (light-tailed)](subexponential-distribution-light-tailed.md)

A centered [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ with $\mathbb E[X^2]=\nu$ satisfies the Bernstein moment condition with parameter $b$ when

$$
|\mathbb E[X^k]|\leq\frac{k!}{2}\nu b^{k-2}
$$

for every integer $k\geq2$. Summing its moment-generating series shows that it is sub-exponential with parameters $(2\nu,2b)$.
