# Incidence equivalence in a set family

↑ **Parent:** [Set family](set-family.md)

Two ground elements are incidence-equivalent when they belong to exactly the same members of a [set family](set-family.md). Their incidence columns are identical. For the [set basis problem](set-basis-problem.md), deleting one of two such elements preserves the answer. A basis before deletion can be restricted to the smaller ground [set](set-split.md). Conversely, if $x$ remains and $y$ was deleted, lift each reduced basis [set](set-split.md) $A$ to $A\cup\{y\}$ when $x\in A$, and leave it unchanged otherwise. In every [union](set-union.md) of lifted basis [sets](set-split.md), $y$ appears exactly when $x$ appears, which restores precisely the original input [sets](set-split.md). Repeated deletion thus leaves one representative of every incidence class.
