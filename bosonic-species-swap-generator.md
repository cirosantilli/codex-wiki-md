# Bosonic species-swap generator

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md)

For two equal-energy bosonic species with [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md) $a,b$, set $c_-=(a-b)/\sqrt2$ and let $N_-$ be its [number operator](number-operator.md). Since $[N_-,c_-]=-c_-$, conjugation by $e^{i\pi N_-}$ reverses the antisymmetric combination while fixing $(a+b)/\sqrt2$, and therefore exchanges $a,b$ and their [creation operators](creation-operator.md). For the particle and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) modes of a [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) this realizes [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md), fixes the [Fock vacuum](fock-vacuum.md), and squares to one. The normal-ordered generator $\int(a^\dagger-b^\dagger)(a-b)$ equals $2N_-$. Writing the annihilation factors first adds an infinite constant in the continuum, which must be subtracted or regulated before defining its exponential.
