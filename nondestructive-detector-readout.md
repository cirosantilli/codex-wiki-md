# Nondestructive detector readout

↑ **Parent:** [Hybrid infrared detector](hybrid-infrared-detector.md)

A nondestructive read samples a pixel’s accumulated signal without resetting it. Repeated samples permit [Fowler sampling](fowler-sampling.md) and [up-the-ramp sampling](up-the-ramp-sampling.md), and can help identify saturation and cosmic-ray charge steps.
