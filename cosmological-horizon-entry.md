# Cosmological horizon entry

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological horizon crossing](cosmological-horizon-crossing.md)

During a decelerating expansion the [comoving Hubble radius](comoving-hubble-radius.md) grows. A fixed comoving [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) then passes from a [superhorizon scale](superhorizon-scale.md) to a shorter scale when $k\simeq aH$. In [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md), $aH=1/\tau$, so this occurs at $k\tau\simeq1$. Here horizon entry refers to [Hubble radius](hubble-radius.md) crossing; a true causal horizon instead depends on a conformal-time integral.
