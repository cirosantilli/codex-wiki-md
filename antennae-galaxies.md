# Antennae Galaxies

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy merger](galaxy-merger.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antennae_Galaxies)

An interacting pair of [spiral galaxies](spiral-galaxy.md) undergoing a [galaxy merger](galaxy-merger.md). Their long [tidal tails](tidal-tail.md) contain [stars](star.md) displaced by the encounter. They are also a nearby example of a [starburst galaxy](starburst-galaxy.md), with vigorous [star formation](star-formation.md) accompanying the interaction.[Description of the interacting pair, extended tails and young star clusters](caldwell-60-and-61-by-nasa.md)

The [Hubble Space Telescope](hubble-space-telescope.md) image below combines observations from its [Wide Field Camera 3](wide-field-camera-3.md) and [Advanced Camera for Surveys](advanced-camera-for-surveys.md). Bright young [stars](star.md), emitting regions of the [interstellar medium](interstellar-medium.md) and obscuring [interstellar dust](interstellar-dust.md) reveal different aspects of the encounter. The close view emphasizes the interacting central regions; wider views show the long outer [tidal tails](tidal-tail.md).[Image potw1345a, observing instruments, image description and field of view](antennae-galaxies-reloaded-by-esa-and-nasa.md)
