# Locally compact group

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact_group)

A locally compact group is a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) whose underlying space is a [locally compact space](locally-compact-space.md). In [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) and [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) theory one normally also assumes it is a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md). Local compactness supplies compact neighbourhoods, not just compact sets which algebraically generate the group.
