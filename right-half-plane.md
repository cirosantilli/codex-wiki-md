# Right half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Complex analysis](complex-analysis-split.md)

The [right half-plane](right-half-plane.md) consists of [complex numbers](complex-number.md) with positive [real part](real-part.md). Its [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) is the imaginary axis. For example, $1+i$ is inside, $i$ is on the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md), and $-1+i$ is outside. Rotation $z\mapsto iz$ takes the [right half-plane](right-half-plane.md) onto the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md); the [Cayley transform between the half-plane and disk](cayley-transform-between-the-half-plane-and-disk.md) uses $R(z)=(z-1)/(z+1)$ for this convention.
