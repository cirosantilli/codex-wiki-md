# Quenched intrinsic curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic curvature of an elastic filament](intrinsic-curvature-of-an-elastic-filament.md)

Quenched [intrinsic curvature of an elastic filament](intrinsic-curvature-of-an-elastic-filament.md) is fixed during a mechanical experiment, although different filaments can have different random intrinsic shapes. Ensemble averaging is then over these shapes after mechanical minimization. It differs from averaging over time-dependent thermal bending fluctuations in a [worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md).
