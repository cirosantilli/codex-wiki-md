# Projection formula for the K-theory transfer

↑ **Parent:** [K-theory transfer of a finite covering](k-theory-transfer-of-a-finite-covering.md)

The [K-theory transfer of a finite covering](k-theory-transfer-of-a-finite-covering.md) distributes over a pulled-back [tensor product of vector bundles](tensor-product-of-vector-bundles.md):

$$
p_!(p^*a\cdot b)=a\cdot p_!b.
$$

For an $n$-sheeted [covering map](covering-space.md), $p_!(1)$ is the permutation [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) of rank $n$. Its difference from $n$ is a [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md) by [nilpotence of rank-zero K-theory classes](nilpotence-of-rank-zero-k-theory-classes.md), so it is a unit after inverting $n$. Thus pullback on [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md) becomes injective after this [localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md).
