# Angular quotient functions belong to Sobolev spaces below the dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

The bounded angular [function](function-split.md) has ordinary derivatives $D_ju=\delta_{1j}/r-x_1x_j/r^3$ away from zero. Their magnitudes are at most $2/r$, whose $p$-th power is integrable on the unit ball exactly below the dimension threshold in this estimate. To verify the [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md) across zero, integrate by parts outside a ball of radius $\varepsilon$ against a compactly supported smooth test. The boundary term has magnitude at most $C\varepsilon^{n-1}$, tending to zero for $n>1$, while the derivative volume term converges by integrability. This proves the stated Sobolev membership with those [weak derivatives](weak-derivative.md). The dimensional condition distinguishes it from the one-dimensional sign [function](function-split.md), whose jump produces a Dirac derivative.
