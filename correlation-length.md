# Correlation length

↑ **Parent:** [Correlation function](correlation-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correlation_length)

The correlation length is the characteristic distance over which a connected [correlation function](correlation-function.md) decays. It diverges at an ordinary continuous critical point as $\xi\sim|t|^{-\nu}$.
