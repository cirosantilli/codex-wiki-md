# Vapor-density molar mass measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Molar mass](molar-mass.md)

An [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md) measurement obtains [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) without knowing the [chemical formula](chemical-formula.md) in advance:

$$
n=\frac{pV}{RT},\qquad M_{\rm inferred}=\frac{mRT}{pV}.
$$

Here $p$ is vapor [pressure](pressure-split.md), $V$ is its occupied [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md), $T$ is absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md), $R$ is the [molar gas constant](molar-gas-constant.md), and $m$ is the [mass](mass.md) of vapor occupying that [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) at those conditions.

Santa Monica College's teaching experiment uses a $125\,\mathrm{mL}$ [Erlenmeyer flask](erlenmeyer-flask.md) with a pinhole foil cap, a $600\,\mathrm{mL}$ water bath on a hot plate, a [thermometer](thermometer.md), and a barometer. Excess volatile liquid displaces air. A dissolved dye loses its colour and deposits when the liquid evaporates; three more minutes of heating follow. Cooling condenses retained vapor. Subtracting the dry assembly [mass](mass.md) from the cooled assembly [mass](mass.md) supplies $m$. The flask [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) comes from brim-full [water](water.md) [mass](mass.md) and [mass density](density.md) at its measured [temperature](temperature-split.md). Controls include a dry exterior, excluding bath [water](water.md), and three trials.[Santa Monica College, 11B, Experimental Set-up steps 1–6; Trials 1–3 steps 1–4: apparatus, dye endpoint, heating, drying and repetition](11b-the-dumas-method-experiment-by-santa-monica-college.md)

The inference assumes vapor [pressure](pressure-split.md) equals atmospheric [pressure](pressure-split.md), vapor and bath reach [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md), the retained vapor [mass](mass.md) is recovered on cooling, and the vapor obeys the [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md). Residual air, [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md) of the flask, and vapor [temperature](temperature-split.md) below bath [temperature](temperature-split.md) are explicit error questions in the teaching experiment. Repeating trials assesses repeatability; it does not remove a shared bias.[Lab Report, questions 1–4: residual air, glass expansion, nonideal vapor and incomplete thermal equilibration](11b-the-dumas-method-experiment-by-santa-monica-college.md)
