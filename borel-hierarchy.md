# Borel hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Borel set](borel-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel_hierarchy)

For a topological space, $\Sigma^0_1$ is the class of open sets and $\Pi^0_\alpha$ consists of complements of $\Sigma^0_\alpha$ sets. For countable $\alpha>1$, $\Sigma^0_\alpha$ consists of countable unions of sets in $\bigcup_{\beta<\alpha}\Pi^0_\beta$. These countable levels contain every [Borel set](borel-set.md). Continuous inverse images preserve each level, by induction using openness, complementation and countable unions.
