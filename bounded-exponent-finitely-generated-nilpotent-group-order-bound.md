# Bounded-exponent finitely generated nilpotent group order bound

↑ **Parent:** [Lower central series](lower-central-series.md)

Let $G$ be a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md) of class at most $s$ in which every element has order at most $r$. A subgroup generated by $k$ elements has order at most

$$
r^{sk^s}.
$$

Indeed, collection by the [lower central series](lower-central-series.md) expresses every element as an ordered product of simple [group commutators](group-commutator.md) in the generators of weights at most $s$. There are at most $k+k^2+\cdots+k^s\leq sk^s$ such commutators, and each exponent may be reduced to one of at most $r$ values.
