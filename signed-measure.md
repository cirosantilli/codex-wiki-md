# Signed measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signed_measure)

A signed measure is a countably additive real-valued set function, allowing negative values. A finite signed measure has a [variation measure](variation-measure.md), whose total mass is its [total variation norm of a measure](total-variation-norm-of-a-measure.md).
