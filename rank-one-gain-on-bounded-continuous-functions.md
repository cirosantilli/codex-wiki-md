# Rank-one gain on bounded continuous functions

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Boltzmann collision operator](linear-boltzmann-collision-operator.md)

A bounded continuous factor $k_1$ and an integrable factor $k_2$ give a bounded velocity-integral gain on [bounded continuous functions](bounded-continuous-functions.md), with bound $\|Kf\|_\infty\leq\|k_1\|_\infty\|k_2\|_1\|f\|_\infty$. The [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) proves joint continuity of the velocity [integral](integral.md). Combined with damped transport, the [factorial bound for a Volterra iterate](factorial-bound-for-a-volterra-iterate.md) proves existence on arbitrary finite time slabs.
