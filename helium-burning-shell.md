# Helium-burning shell

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md)

A helium-burning shell converts [helium](helium.md) into [carbon](carbon.md) and [oxygen](oxygen.md) outside a helium-exhausted stellar core. Thin-shell burning on the [Asymptotic giant branch](asymptotic-giant-branch.md) can develop a [thermal pulse of an asymptotic-giant-branch star](thermal-pulse-of-an-asymptotic-giant-branch-star.md).
