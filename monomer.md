# Monomer

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

A [monomer](monomer.md) is a [chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md) whose [molecules](molecule-split.md) can undergo [polymerization](polymerization.md) and contribute units to the essential structure of a [polymer](polymer-split.md). A [monomer molecule](monomer-molecule.md) is the individual reactant; its incorporated [monomeric unit](monomeric-unit.md) is the portion identifiable as derived from that reactant. Incorporation can change [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) and release small byproducts, so the unit in the chain need not have the complete [molecular formula](molecular-formula.md) of the feed [molecule](molecule-split.md). See [IUPAC: monomer molecule](https://old.goldbook.iupac.org/html/M/M04019.html) and [IUPAC, A brief guide to polymer terminology: monomeric units](https://doi.org/10.1515/pac-2023-0304).

Three different counts answer three different questions. [Monomer molecules](monomer-molecule.md) describe the feed; [monomeric units](monomeric-unit.md) trace that feed's contributions to the product; a [constitutional repeating unit](repeat-unit.md) describes the smallest chemical pattern repeating along a regular chain. In [Nylon 6,10](nylon-6-10.md), two different [monomers](monomer.md) supply one interior [constitutional repeating unit](repeat-unit.md),

$$
\mathrm{{-}NH{-}(CH_2)_6{-}NH{-}C(=O){-}(CH_2)_8{-}C(=O){-}}.
$$

Its [molecular formula](molecular-formula.md) is $\mathrm{C_{16}H_{30}N_2O_2}$. The diamine feed is $\mathrm{C_6H_{16}N_2}$, and the diacid-chloride feed is $\mathrm{C_{10}H_{16}Cl_2O_2}$; subtracting two $\mathrm{HCl}$ gives the interior-repeat budget. For a finite linear chain made from $n$ of each reactant, there are $2n-1$ joining connections, hence $2n-1$ formal $\mathrm{HCl}$ eliminations, rather than $2n$. One [amine](amine.md) end and one acid-chloride end remain in that ideal unmodified chain. This is a [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) check on the depicted reaction, not a claim that the washed experimental thread retains those ends. The reacting end-group chemistry is documented in [Hammond, lecture 5, p. 4](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/10-569-synthesis-of-polymers-fall-2006/d9f9ac7021b0d0f418d3d3c90dbfb94b_lec05_09152006.pdf); the specified [Nylon 6,10](nylon-6-10.md) reactant pair is reported in [Polymer Day, nylon demonstration](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jchemed.6b00767).

[Ethene](ethylene.md) shows the reverse counting mismatch. One $\mathrm{CH_2{=}CH_2}$ [monomer molecule](monomer-molecule.md) contributes a two-[carbon](carbon.md) $\mathrm{{-}CH_2{-}CH_2{-}}$ [monomeric unit](monomeric-unit.md) to ideal linear [polyethylene](polyethylene.md), without a small byproduct in that growth step. But the smallest [constitutional repeating unit](repeat-unit.md) of its uniform backbone is just $\mathrm{{-}CH_2{-}}$: a chain containing $m$ incorporated [ethene](ethylene.md) [monomeric units](monomeric-unit.md) contains $2m$ such interior one-[carbon](carbon.md) repeats. End groups are additional bookkeeping. The frequently drawn $[\mathrm{{-}CH_2{-}CH_2{-}}]_m$ is useful for tracing the feed, although it is not the smallest constitutional repeat. The minimum-pattern definition is given in [IUPAC: constitutional repeating unit](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/C01286/plain); the [ethene](ethylene.md) growth chemistry is described by [McMurry, Organic Chemistry, 31.1](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/31-1-chain-growth-polymers.md).

A segment of a [freely jointed chain](ideal-chain.md) or [Gaussian chain](gaussian-chain.md) instead belongs to a statistical model. It can represent many [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) and several [constitutional repeating units](repeat-unit.md); its length and the segment count are chosen to describe chain conformation at the intended scale. The three-repeats-per-segment drawing in [Figure 3. "From two liquid layers to a Nylon 6,10 thread: monomers, a constitutional repeating unit, and statistical segments"](polymerization.md#polymerization-monomers-interface-units) is a deliberately arbitrary coarse-graining example, not a measured [Kuhn length](kuhn-length.md). Always state the counting convention before substituting a segment count for a [degree of polymerization](degree-of-polymerization.md). This separation lets chemistry track which [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) entered the material while statistical models track how the material bends and extends.
