# Linear equivalence of Cartier divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

Two [Cartier divisors](cartier-divisor-split.md) are linearly equivalent when their difference is a [principal Cartier divisor](principal-cartier-divisor.md). Their associated [line bundles](line-bundle.md) are then isomorphic, so their [global section](global-section.md) spaces have the same dimension. This definition works in every dimension.
