# Projected subgradient method

↑ **Parent:** [Projected gradient descent](projected-gradient-descent.md)

The projected subgradient method minimizes a possibly nonsmooth [convex function](convex-function.md) over a closed [convex set](convex-set.md) $C$ by iterating $x_{i+1}=\Pi_C(x_i-t_i g_i)$ with $g_i\in\partial f(x_i)$. Nonexpansiveness of [Euclidean projection onto a convex set](euclidean-projection-onto-a-convex-set.md) and the [subgradient inequality](subgradient-inequality.md) give its basic convergence bound.
