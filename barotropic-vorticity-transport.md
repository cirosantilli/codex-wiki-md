# Barotropic vorticity transport

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md)

For an inviscid [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md) with only potential body forces, the [vorticity](vorticity.md) $\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf u$ obeys

$$
\partial_t\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times(\mathbf u\times\boldsymbol\omega).
$$

The pressure force is a [gradient](gradient.md) because $\rho^{-1}\nabla p=\nabla\int^\rho p'(s)\,ds/s$. A spatially uniform [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md) makes a fixed-composition fluid barotropic. Merely having $Ds/Dt=0$ does not exclude [baroclinic vorticity generation](baroclinic-vorticity-generation.md) from spatial entropy gradients.
