# Local formula for the Chern connection on a vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Chern connection](chern-connection.md)

In a [holomorphic local frame](holomorphic-local-trivialization.md) of a Hermitian [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md), let $H$ be the matrix of its [Hermitian metric on a holomorphic vector bundle](hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md). With the convention $\nabla e=eA$, the [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) has

$$
A=H^{-1}\partial H.
$$

The condition $\nabla^{0,1}=\bar\partial_E$ forces $A$ to have type $(1,0)$, while [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) forces this formula, proving both existence and uniqueness.
