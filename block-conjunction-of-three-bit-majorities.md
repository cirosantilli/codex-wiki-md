# Block conjunction of three-bit majorities

↑ **Parent:** [Majority function](majority-function.md)

Its $n$ disjoint triples give a product of degree-three [multilinear polynomials](multilinear-polynomial.md), with all-variable coefficient $(-2)^n$. Hence its degree is $3n$ and its [exact quantum query complexity](exact-quantum-query-complexity.md) is at least $\lceil3n/2\rceil$. At the input consisting of repeated $110$ triples, each of the $2n$ one bits is a disjoint sensitive singleton. Thus [block sensitivity](block-sensitivity.md) is at least $2n$, implying a bounded-error [quantum query complexity](quantum-query-complexity.md) lower bound $\Omega(\sqrt n)$.
