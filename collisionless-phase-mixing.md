# Collisionless phase mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Landau damping](landau-damping.md)

For freely streaming particles, a spatial Fourier perturbation of a [velocity distribution](velocity-distribution.md) evolves with factors $e^{-ikvt}$. Contributions from different velocities acquire different phases and can cancel in a density or field moment even while the fine-grained distribution retains structure. In a self-consistent plasma, resonant field–particle exchange gives [Landau damping](landau-damping.md). This is distinct from collisional thermalization and from orbital phase mixing in a planetary ensemble.
