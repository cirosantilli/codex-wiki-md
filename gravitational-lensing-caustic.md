# Gravitational-lensing caustic

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational-lensing critical curve](gravitational-lensing-critical-curve.md)

A caustic is the source-plane image of a [gravitational-lensing critical curve](gravitational-lensing-critical-curve.md). Crossing a generic fold caustic creates or destroys a pair of images of opposite parity. For a circular lens, a tangential critical circle maps to an aligned-source point, while a radial critical circle can map to a finite source-plane circle. Critical curves and caustics consequently occupy different planes and must not be identified as the same angular locus.
