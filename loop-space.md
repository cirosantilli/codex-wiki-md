# Loop space

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy](homotopy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_space)

The loop space $\Omega X$ is the space of based loops in $X$. It is the homotopy fiber of the inclusion of the base point into $X$.
