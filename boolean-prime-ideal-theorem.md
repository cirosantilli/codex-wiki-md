# Boolean prime ideal theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_prime_ideal_theorem)

Every proper [lattice ideal](lattice-ideal.md) of a [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md) extends to a prime [lattice ideal](lattice-ideal.md). More generally, an ideal disjoint from a [lattice filter](lattice-filter.md) extends to a prime ideal still disjoint from that filter. This Boolean case of the [Stone prime filter theorem](stone-prime-filter-theorem.md) is equivalent to the [ultrafilter lemma](ultrafilter-lemma.md) over [Zermelo–Fraenkel set theory](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) and is weaker than the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md).
