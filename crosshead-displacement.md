# Crosshead displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Tensile testing](tensile-testing.md)

[Crosshead displacement](crosshead-displacement.md) is the measured travel of a testing machine's moving crosshead. Depending on the readout and apparatus, it includes deformation outside the specimen segment measured by an [extensometer](extensometer.md). It is affected by [machine compliance](machine-compliance.md).
