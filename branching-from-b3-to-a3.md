# Branching from B3 to A3

↑ **Parent:** [Branching rule](branching-rule.md)

For [B3 root system](b3-root-system.md) [simple roots](simple-root.md) $e_1-e_2,e_2-e_3,e_3$, choose the [D3 root system](d3-root-system.md) [simple roots](simple-root.md) in chain order $e_2-e_3,e_1-e_2,e_2+e_3$. The [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) project by $(a,b,c)\mapsto(b,a,b+c)$. This separates the vector [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) $\{0,\pm e_i\}$ into the six-dimensional [exterior square](exterior-square.md) of the defining [SU(4)](su-4-group.md) [representation](group-representation.md) and a [singlet representation](singlet-representation.md). The spinor cube vertices $\tfrac12(\pm e_1\pm e_2\pm e_3)$ split into its defining [representation](group-representation.md) and its [dual representation](dual-representation.md).
