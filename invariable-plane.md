# Invariable plane

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invariable_plane)

The invariable plane is perpendicular to a system's total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). For an isolated planetary system, it is fixed while individual orbital planes precess.
