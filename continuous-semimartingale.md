# Continuous semimartingale

↑ **Parent:** [Semimartingale](semimartingale.md)

A continuous [semimartingale](semimartingale.md) is a continuous adapted process admitting a decomposition $X=X_0+N+V$, with $N$ a zero-starting [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) and $V$ continuous adapted [finite variation](total-variation-of-a-function.md). The decomposition is unique because a continuous finite-variation local [martingale](martingale-split.md) starting at zero vanishes. Its [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) is $[X]=[N]$.
