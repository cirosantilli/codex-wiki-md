# Chemotactic instability on a rectangular domain

↑ **Parent:** [Keller--Segel model](keller-segel-model.md)

For [cell diffusion](diffusion.md), self-produced [chemotactic agent](chemotaxis.md) and [logistic proliferation](logistic-differential-equation.md), [Neumann cosine modes](neumann-boundary-condition.md) reduce [linear stability](linear-stability.md) of the uniform state to a two-by-two [matrix](matrix.md) indexed by $s=\pi^2(n^2/L_1^2+m^2/L_2^2)$. Its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is negative and its [determinant](determinant.md) is $D(s)=\alpha s^2+(1+\alpha\kappa-\beta\kappa\eta)s+\kappa$. [Chemotaxis](chemotaxis.md) can destabilize a mode precisely when $D(s)<0$, despite [stability](lyapunov-stability.md) of the spatially uniform reaction system.
