# Vandermonde determinant invariance under monic basis change

↑ **Parent:** [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md)

Evaluating arbitrary [monic polynomials](monic-polynomial.md) of successive degrees $0,\ldots,m-1$ gives the same [determinant](determinant.md) as evaluating the monomials of those degrees. The basis-change [matrix](matrix.md) is triangular with diagonal entries one, so its [determinant](determinant.md) is one. This permits an [orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial.md) basis to replace the monomials in [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) densities.
