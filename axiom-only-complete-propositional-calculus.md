# Axiom-only complete propositional calculus

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert proof system](hilbert-proof-system.md)

Take precisely the [propositional tautologies](tautology.md) as [axioms](axiom.md) and supply no [inference rules](inference-rule.md). A one-line [proof](formal-proof.md) then establishes exactly the valid [propositional formulas](propositional-formula.md), so this calculus is sound and complete for [theoremhood](formal-proof.md). Its [axiom set](axiom.md) is decidable by finite [truth tables](truth-table.md). This construction does not by itself give strong completeness for consequence from arbitrary assumptions.
