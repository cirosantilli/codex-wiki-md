# Orbit-averaged drag work

↑ **Parent:** [Specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md)

A weak perturbing relative acceleration $\mathbf f$ changes [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) at rate $\dot\varepsilon=\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf f$. Since $\varepsilon=-\mu/(2a)$ for a [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md),

$$
\dot a=\frac{2a^2}{\mu}\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf f.
$$

For a circular [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) under $\mathbf f=-\kappa\mathbf v+\mathbf g$ with constant $\mathbf g$, the constant-force term averages to zero over an unperturbed orbit. Thus $\langle\dot a\rangle=-2\kappa a$. This is a leading-order orbital average; a strong constant force can distort the orbit instead.
