# Time of flight

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_of_flight)

The elapsed time for a [particle](particle-physics.md) to traverse a specified path. For constant [speed](speed.md) $v$ and known path [length](length.md) $L$, $t=L/v$; varying [speed](speed.md) instead requires integrating $dt=ds/v(s)$.
