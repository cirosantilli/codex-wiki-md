# Space of orthogonal complex structures

↑ **Parent:** [Complex structure](complex-structure.md)

On a Euclidean real space of dimension $2n$, an orthogonal [complex structure](complex-structure.md) is an orthogonal endomorphism $J$ with $J^2=-I$. Orthogonal changes of basis act transitively on these structures, and the stabilizer of the standard structure is $U(n)$. The compact [homogeneous space](homogeneous-space.md) has real dimension $n(n-1)$.
