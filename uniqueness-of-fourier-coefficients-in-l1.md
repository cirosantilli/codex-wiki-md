# Uniqueness of Fourier coefficients in L1

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md)

If a periodic integrable function has all [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) zero, it vanishes almost everywhere. Its [Fejér sums](fejer-sum.md) vanish, whereas the [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md) is an approximate identity and converges to that function in the integrable norm. Applied to a periodized squared [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md), this detects orthogonality of integer translates.
