# Quotient characterization of bounded characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded characteristic](bounded-characteristic.md)

A [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) has [bounded characteristic](bounded-characteristic.md) if and only if it has this representation. Its pole [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md) $B$ makes $g=Bf$ analytic with bounded positive logarithmic means. The [least harmonic majorant by expanding disk lifts](least-harmonic-majorant-by-expanding-disk-lifts.md) gives $H\ge\log^+|g|$. A [harmonic conjugate](harmonic-conjugate.md) gives an analytic $A$ with $\operatorname{Re}A=H$, so $u=ge^{-A}$ and $v=Be^{-A}$ are bounded. Conversely the [Nevanlinna first main theorem](nevanlinna-first-main-theorem.md) bounds the characteristic of $u/v$ by that of $u$ and $1/v$, hence by a constant.
