# Slip-enhanced swimming speed of a transverse sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Navier-slip Taylor swimming sheet](navier-slip-taylor-swimming-sheet.md)

At second order, the displaced-boundary [Navier slip boundary condition](navier-slip-boundary-condition.md) gives $(\psi_2)_Y-\delta[(\psi_2)_{YY}-(\psi_2)_{XX}]=(1+2\delta)\sin^2(X-\tau)$ at $Y=0$. Its mean shear is zero in a bounded half-space flow, so the [Navier-slip Taylor swimming sheet](navier-slip-taylor-swimming-sheet.md) dimensionless speed is $U^*=\epsilon^2(1+2\delta)/2+O(\epsilon^4)$. The sheet travels opposite to the wave. This fixed-slip [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) predicts a speed enhancement factor $1+2\delta$.
