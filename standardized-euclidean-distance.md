# Standardized Euclidean distance

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md)

With fixed positive scale estimates $s_j$, this is [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) in standardized coordinates. It removes marginal units but ignores correlations. Small estimated [variances](variance-split.md) can greatly amplify measurement noise; a zero scale must be handled separately.
