# Chain sliding over a table edge

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

An inextensible uniform chain of length L and [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\rho$ links a [mass](mass.md) m sliding on a frictionless table to a hanging [mass](mass.md) M. If the initial hanging chain length is zero and x is its subsequent hanging length, all moving material has speed $\dot x$. The [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $(M+m+\rho L)\dot x^2/2$, while the [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) is $-Mgx-\rho gx^2/2$. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives the displayed equation until a mass hits the floor or reaches the edge. Increasing hanging chain weight produces a [hyperbolic cosine](hyperbolic-cosine.md) displacement from rest, rather than uniform acceleration.
