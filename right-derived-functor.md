# Right derived functor

↑ **Parent:** [Homological algebra](homological-algebra.md)

For an additive covariant [left-exact functor](finite-limit-preserving-functor.md) on module categories, choose an [injective resolution](injective-resolution.md) $M\to I^\bullet$ and set $R^iF(M)=H^i(F(I^\bullet))$. For a contravariant functor, use a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) instead, as in [contravariant right derived functor](contravariant-right-derived-functor.md). Resolution comparison and homotopies give independence and functoriality; short exact sequences give long exact derived-functor sequences. The zeroth functor is naturally $F$.
