# Operational amplifier

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic amplifier](electronic-amplifier.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operational_amplifier)

An [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md) is a differential [electronic amplifier](electronic-amplifier.md) whose unloaded [voltage](voltage.md) is modeled in its linear regime by $v_o=A(s)(v_+-v_-)$. Its large [open-loop gain](open-loop-gain.md), large [input impedance](input-impedance.md) and small [output impedance](output-impedance.md) make [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md) useful for setting a reproducible [transfer function](transfer-function.md) with external components. Supply voltages bound the available output swing; the linear model does not apply after saturation.
