# Preservation obstructions to extending an adjoint chain

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint functors](adjoint-functors.md)

A [functor](functor.md) admitting a further [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) must itself be a [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) and preserve all existing [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md). A functor admitting a further [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) must preserve all existing [colimits](colimit.md). Failure to preserve a single [terminal object](terminal-object.md), [equalizer](equaliser.md), [initial object](initial-object.md) or [coproduct](coproduct.md) therefore rules out the corresponding extension. In the [adjoint chain for the objects of a category](adjoint-chain-for-the-objects-of-a-category.md), connected components fail an equalizer and the [indiscrete category](indiscrete-category.md) functor fails a binary coproduct.
