# Hypergeometric sine identity

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary specialization of a hypergeometric solution](elementary-specialization-of-a-hypergeometric-solution.md)

For $k\ne0$ and compatible local branches near $x=0$,

$$
F\left(\frac{1+k}{2},\frac{1-k}{2};\frac32;\sin^2x\right)
=\frac{\sin(kx)}{k\sin x}.
$$

This is obtained from the [second local hypergeometric solution](second-local-hypergeometric-solution.md) at zero and its normalization.
