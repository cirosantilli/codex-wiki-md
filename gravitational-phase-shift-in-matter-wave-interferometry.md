# Gravitational phase shift in matter-wave interferometry

↑ **Parent:** [Matter-wave interference](matter-wave-interference.md)

For equal-speed nonrelativistic matter waves in an interferometer with signed enclosed vertical area $A$, a uniform [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) gives the displayed leading-order phase difference. In a rectangular device, the horizontal paths differ in [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) by $mg h$ for a time $\ell/v$, so their phase difference has magnitude $mg h\ell/(\hbar v)$. Assumptions include negligible fractional changes of speed, matched unperturbed optical paths and a consistent treatment of the beam splitters. The [Colella–Overhauser–Werner experiment](colella-overhauser-werner-experiment.md) uses neutron interference to test this gravitational phase.
