# Large deviations of a fixed sum of geometric random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Large deviations of a scaled geometric random variable](large-deviations-of-a-scaled-geometric-random-variable.md)

For fixed finitely many independent [geometric random variables](geometric-distribution.md), the [product large-deviation principle](product-large-deviation-principle.md) gives rate $\sum_i a_ix_i$ on the nonnegative orthant for their scaled vector, where $a_i=-\log(1-p_i)$. The [contraction principle for large deviations](contraction-principle-for-large-deviations.md) under addition minimizes this over $\sum_ix_i=s$. The answer is $s\min_i a_i$, achieved by assigning all scaled excess to a coordinate with the slowest geometric tail. The rate is infinite for negative $s$. Deterministic coordinates with $p_i=1$ contribute zero on this scale; if all coordinates are deterministic, only zero has finite rate.
