# Counterterm

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterterm)

A counterterm is a local term added to a regulated [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) to cancel ultraviolet divergences and impose chosen [renormalization conditions](renormalization-condition.md). Field-strength, mass and coupling counterterms respectively adjust propagator normalization, pole position and interaction strength.
