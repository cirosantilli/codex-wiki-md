# Electron shell

↑ **Parent:** [Electron configuration](electron-configuration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron_shell)

An [electron shell](electron-shell.md) collects the allowed electronic [quantum states](quantum-state.md) sharing a [principal quantum number](principal-quantum-number.md) $n$. Its occupancy is the number of [electrons](electron.md) assigned to those states in an [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md). An [electron shell](electron-shell.md) is not a material surface or a prescribed orbit.
