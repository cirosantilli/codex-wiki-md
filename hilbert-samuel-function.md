<h1 id="hilbert-samuel-function">Hilbert–Samuel function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Samuel polynomial](hilbert-samuel-polynomial.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert–Samuel_function)

For an [ideal of definition](ideal-of-definition.md) $I$ of a [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md) and a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) $M$, the Hilbert–Samuel function is

$$
\chi(M,I;n)=\operatorname{length}(M/I^nM).
$$

For sufficiently large $n$, it equals the [Hilbert-Samuel polynomial](hilbert-samuel-polynomial.md).
