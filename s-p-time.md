<h1 id="s-p-time">S–P time</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake location from interphase delays](earthquake-location-from-interphase-delays.md)

The delay between corresponding [S wave](s-wave.md) and [P wave](p-wave.md) arrivals. For a common uniform path of length $r$, $\Delta t=r(1/V_s-1/V_p)$, cancelling [earthquake origin time](earthquake-origin-time.md). Matched phase identification and an appropriate [velocity model](velocity-model.md) are needed to infer distance.
