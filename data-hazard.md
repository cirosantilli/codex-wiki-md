# Data hazard

↑ **Parent:** [Instruction pipelining](instruction-pipelining.md)

An execution-timing conflict arising from the ordering of accesses to a value. A [read-after-write hazard](read-after-write-hazard.md) occurs when a consumer would read before its required producer's value becomes available.
