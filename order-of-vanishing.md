# Order of vanishing

↑ **Parent:** [Rational function](rational-function.md)

The order of vanishing of a nonzero [rational function](rational-function.md) $f$ at a point $p$ is the exponent of a local parameter in its local factorization. A negative order is the order of a pole.

The local multiplicity in [zeros and poles](zeros-and-poles.md) is positive for a zero and negative for a pole; it is zero at a regular nonvanishing point.
