# Rational homotopy group

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy group](homotopy-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_homotopy_group)

The rational homotopy group is obtained by tensoring a homotopy group with the [rational numbers](rational-number.md). It retains the free part and discards torsion.
