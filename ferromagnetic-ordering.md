# Ferromagnetic ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md)

Formation of [magnetic order](magnetic-order.md) with a spontaneous uniform [magnetization](magnetization.md). A large positive [Pauli paramagnetism](pauli-paramagnetism.md) alone does not establish this: an instability caused by interactions is needed for spontaneous [magnetization](magnetization.md) in zero applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).
