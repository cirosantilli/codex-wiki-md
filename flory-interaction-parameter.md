# Flory interaction parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md)

The Flory interaction parameter measures the excess free-energy cost of unlike contacts in [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md). For a simple lattice of coordination number $z_c$ and contact energies $\epsilon_{ps}$, $\epsilon_{pp}$ and $\epsilon_{ss}$,

$$
\chi=\frac{z_c}{k_BT}\left[\epsilon_{ps}-\frac{\epsilon_{pp}+\epsilon_{ss}}2\right].
$$

Real effective parameters may also contain entropic contributions and composition dependence. With site volume $v_0$ and composition-independent $\chi$, the effective two-segment [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md) is $v_{\mathrm{eff}}=v_0(1-2\chi)$. Positive, zero and negative $v_{\mathrm{eff}}$ correspond respectively to a [good solvent](good-solvent.md), the [theta temperature](theta-temperature.md) condition and a [poor solvent](poor-solvent.md). In particular, $\chi=1/2$ means cancellation of leading repulsive and attractive contributions; it does not mean that every molecular interaction vanishes.
