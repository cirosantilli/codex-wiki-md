<h1 id="torsional-alfven-wave">Torsional Alfvén wave</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md)

A torsional [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) couples azimuthal [velocity](velocity.md) to a [toroidal magnetic field](toroidal-magnetic-field.md) around a fixed [poloidal magnetic field](poloidal-magnetic-field.md). Axisymmetric induction gives $\partial_tB_\phi=R\mathbf B_p\cdot\nabla\Omega$ and magnetic tension gives $\rho R^2\mu_0\partial_t\Omega=\mathbf B_p\cdot\nabla(RB_\phi)$. Their combination is the displayed wave equation. Its local [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) is $\omega^2=(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf B_p)^2/(\mu_0\rho)$.
