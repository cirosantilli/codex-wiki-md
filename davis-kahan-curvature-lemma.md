# Davis-Kahan curvature lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Davis-Kahan theorem](davis-kahan-theorem.md)

If a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $H$ has largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_1$ separated from $\lambda_2$ by $\gamma>0$, and $P=vv^\top$ is its leading rank-one [orthogonal projection matrix](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md), then every unit vector $u$, with $Q=uu^\top$, satisfies the displayed inequality. Expanding $u$ in an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) gives $\lambda_1-u^\top Hu\geq\gamma(1-|u^\top v|^2)$, while $\|P-Q\|_F^2=2(1-|u^\top v|^2)$. The analogous statement for a smallest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) follows by replacing $H$ by $-H$.
