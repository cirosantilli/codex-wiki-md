# Metric compatibility in Killing identities

↑ **Parent:** [Killing equation](killing-equation.md)

For a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md), the [Lie derivative](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) of a [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) obeys

$$
(\mathcal L_Kg)_{ab}=2g_{c(a}\nabla_{b)}K^c+K^c\nabla_cg_{ab}
=2\nabla_{(a}K_{b)}+K^c\nabla_cg_{ab}-2(\nabla_{(a}g_{b)c})K^c.
$$

Thus the abbreviated formula $\mathcal L_Kg=2\nabla_{(a}K_{b)}$ requires [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md). The familiar [Killing equation](killing-equation.md) and [second covariant derivative of a Killing vector](second-covariant-derivative-of-a-killing-vector.md) use the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), which has both vanishing [torsion tensor](torsion-tensor.md) and [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md). On [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md) with only $\Gamma^0{}_{00}=1$ supplies a counterexample: $K=\partial_0$ is a [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field-split.md) for the constant [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md), but $\nabla_0K_0=1$.
