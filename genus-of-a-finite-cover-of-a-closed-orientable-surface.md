# Genus of a finite cover of a closed orientable surface

↑ **Parent:** [Closed orientable surface](closed-orientable-surface.md)

A connected $n$-sheeted [covering space](covering-space.md) of a [closed orientable surface](closed-orientable-surface.md) of genus $g$ has genus $1+n(g-1)$. Lifting a finite cell decomposition gives $n$ cells above every base cell, so its [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) is $n(2-2g)$. Equating this with $2-2g'$ gives the formula. For $g\ge1$, every positive $n$ occurs: send one handle generator of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) to $1$ in $\mathbb Z/n$ and all remaining generators to zero, then use the [classification of connected covering spaces](classification-of-connected-covering-spaces.md) for the kernel of that surjection.
