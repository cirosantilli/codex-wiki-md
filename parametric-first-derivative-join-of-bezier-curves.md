<h1 id="parametric-first-derivative-join-of-bezier-curves">Parametric first-derivative join of Bézier curves</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bézier derivative control polygon](bezier-derivative-control-polygon.md)

Two adjacent [Bézier curves](bezier-curve.md) of degrees $n,m$ and interval lengths $h,k$ have a $C^1$ join precisely when their endpoint positions and their [derivatives](derivative.md) in the common parameter agree. Parallel endpoint polygon edges alone give tangent-direction agreement; their scaled vectors must be equal for parametric first-derivative [continuity](continuous-function.md).
