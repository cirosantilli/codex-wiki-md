# Cold-atom collapse and revival after a lattice quench

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum quench](quantum-quench.md)

A concrete [quantum quench](quantum-quench.md) experiment loaded a [Bose-Einstein condensate](bose-einstein-condensate.md) of $^{87}\mathrm{Rb}$ [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) from a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) trap into an [optical lattice](optical-lattice.md): three orthogonal $838\,\mathrm{nm}$ [laser](laser.md) [standing waves](standing-wave.md), with approximately $125\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ [beam waists](beam-waist.md). An $80\,\mathrm{ms}$ loading ramp preceded a $50\,\mathrm{\mu s}$ increase in lattice depth. This suppressed [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) while retaining each well's vibrational [ground state](ground-state.md). The switch preserved atom-number populations rather than freezing every motional degree of freedom.[Greiner and others, manuscript pp. 3–4: preparation and switching; p. 7, Methods: wavelength, beam waists and lattice geometry](collapse-and-revival-of-the-matter-wave-field-of-a-bose-einstein-condensate-by-markus-greiner-and-others.md)

After variable hold times, releasing the traps and taking [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) images after $16\,\mathrm{ms}$ of flight revealed disappearance and return of [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) peaks. Background-subtracted peak counts supplied a coherent fraction. Varying final depth tested the predicted revival period $h/U$; varying initial depth tested the collapse's sensitivity to atom-number statistics. Peak broadening outside counting windows and spatial variation of $U$ limited later signals.[Manuscript p. 4 and figure 2 caption on p. 11: flight and image extraction; figure 3 caption on p. 12: readout limitations; figures 4–5 captions on pp. 13–14: final-depth and initial-depth comparisons](collapse-and-revival-of-the-matter-wave-field-of-a-bose-einstein-condensate-by-markus-greiner-and-others.md)

Here $U$ is the on-site interaction [energy](energy.md). The approximate isolated-well [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H=U\hat n(\hat n-1)/2$, with [number operator](number-operator.md) $\hat n$, gives different [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md) to its [number states](number-state.md), which later rephase. This is a many-level realization of the distinction between fixed populations and changing [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md).[Manuscript pp. 2–3, equations (1)–(2): isolated-well Hamiltonian and number-state phase evolution](collapse-and-revival-of-the-matter-wave-field-of-a-bose-einstein-condensate-by-markus-greiner-and-others.md)
