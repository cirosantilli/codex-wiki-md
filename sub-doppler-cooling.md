# Sub-Doppler cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Laser cooling](laser-cooling.md)

Real atoms have multiple internal [quantum states](quantum-state.md). [Optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) and spatially varying [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) can remove [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) by additional mechanisms, producing [sub-Doppler cooling](sub-doppler-cooling.md) below the ideal [Doppler cooling limit](doppler-cooling-limit.md). In [polarization-gradient cooling](polarization-gradient-cooling.md), state-dependent energy shifts from the [Stark effect](stark-effect.md) and delayed [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) make atoms repeatedly climb [potential energy](potential-energy.md) hills. This mechanism needs the internal-state structure omitted by the independent-beam [two-level system](two-level-system.md) above. [William D. Phillips, Nobel Lecture: Laser cooling and trapping of neutral atoms, pp. 733–734 and Fig. 18: multilevel polarization-gradient cooling](nobel-lecture-laser-cooling-and-trapping-of-neutral-atoms-by-william-d-phillips.md).

[William D. Phillips](william-d-phillips.md)'s team tested unexpectedly cold [sodium](sodium.md) molasses with [time-of-flight atom detection](time-of-flight-atom-detection.md). A probe above the cloud initially saw no atoms; deliberately heating them verified that the detector worked. Moving the probe below the cloud produced arrival signals consistent with [temperatures](temperature-split.md) below the [Doppler cooling limit](doppler-cooling-limit.md). Independent release/recapture checks used sharply apertured beams and included [gravity](gravity.md); uncertain cloud and probe geometry still limited the inferred [temperature](temperature-split.md). These controls tested the thermometer as well as the cooling theory. [William D. Phillips, Nobel Lecture: Laser cooling and trapping of neutral atoms, pp. 731–732, Figs. 15–16: probe reversal, deliberate heating, geometric uncertainties and independent thermometry](nobel-lecture-laser-cooling-and-trapping-of-neutral-atoms-by-william-d-phillips.md).
