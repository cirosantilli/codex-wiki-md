# Virial coefficients of the van der Waals gas

↑ **Parent:** [Virial expansion](virial-expansion.md)

Expanding $p=nk_BT/(1-nb)-an^2$ at $nb\ll1$ gives the [second virial coefficient](second-virial-coefficient.md) $B_2=b-a/(k_BT)$ and the displayed higher density-series coefficients. These are the [Van der Waals gas](van-der-waals-gas.md) predictions, not exact coefficients for every fluid. The vanishing of $B_2$ at the [Boyle temperature](boyle-temperature.md) removes only the leading density correction.
