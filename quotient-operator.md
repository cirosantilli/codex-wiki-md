# Quotient operator

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md)

A quotient operator is a [surjective](surjective-function.md) [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) that is an [open map](open-map.md). It is equivalent to requiring a ball about zero in the [image](image-of-a-function.md) of the open unit ball. Translation and scaling then prove being an [open map](open-map.md) at every point. For a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) domain, a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) is a quotient operator exactly when its [Banach-space adjoint](transpose-of-a-bounded-linear-operator.md) is an [isomorphic embedding](isomorphic-embedding.md): a lower bound on the adjoint gives approximate ball-surjectivity by [Hahn-Banach separation](hahn-banach-separation-theorem.md), and [successive approximation for quotient operators](successive-approximation-for-quotient-operators.md) gives exact preimages.
