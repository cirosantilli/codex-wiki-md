# Disformation tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Nonmetricity tensor](nonmetricity-tensor.md)

With derivative-last connection coefficients and $N_{ab c}=\nabla_cg_{ab}$, the displayed [tensor](tensor.md) is the symmetric-in-$a,b$ correction associated with the [nonmetricity tensor](nonmetricity-tensor.md). Raising its final slot gives the correction to connection coefficients. It vanishes for [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md). Reversing the sign used to define nonmetricity reverses this formula.
