# Two-descent on an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer map of an elliptic curve](kummer-map-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

When an elliptic curve has full rational [2-torsion](2-torsion.md), its Kummer map embeds $E(\mathbb Q)/2E(\mathbb Q)$ into a product of square-class groups. Valuation and local-solubility conditions reduce the image to finitely many classes and thereby bound the rank of the [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md).
