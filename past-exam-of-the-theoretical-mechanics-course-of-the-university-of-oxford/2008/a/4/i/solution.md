<h1 id="a/4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $X=x_{\rm dim}/L$, $Y=y/h_0$, scale horizontal and vertical velocities by $U$ and $Uh_0/L$, and define $P=(p_{\rm dim}-p_0)h_0^2/(\mu UL)$. The small [aspect ratio](../../../../../aspect-ratio.md) makes $P$ independent of $Y$ at leading order. The second small parameter, $Uh_0^2/(\nu L)$, makes [inertia](../../../../../inertia.md) negligible compared with transverse viscous stress. Thus the [lubrication equations](../../../../../lubrication-theory.md) are $u_{YY}=P_X$ and $u_X+V_Y=0$.

[No slip](../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) gives $u(X,0)=1$, $u(X,h)=0$, so

$$
\boxed{u=1-\frac Yh+\frac{P_X}{2}Y(Y-h),\qquad q=\int_0^hu\,dY=\frac h2-\frac{h^3P_X}{12}.}
$$

[No penetration](../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md) at the two solid walls and continuity make $q$ constant. Therefore, writing the [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) variables again as $x,p$,

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dx}(h^3p'-6h)=0,\qquad p(0)=p(1)=0.}
$$

The [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) scale is set by balancing the longitudinal [pressure gradient](../../../../../pressure-gradient.md) with shear across the thin gap, not by the inertial dynamic-[pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) scale.
