<h1 id="b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Incompressibility](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) and $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$ give $\boxed{\nabla^2\phi=0}$. The permitted vector identity, with zero [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md), gives $(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=\nabla(|\mathbf u|^2/2)$. Substituting into Euler's equation yields

$$
\nabla\left(\rho\phi_t+\frac\rho2|\nabla\phi|^2+p+\rho gz\right)=0.
$$

The bracket is spatially constant in a connected fluid region, so

$$
\boxed{\rho\phi_t+\frac\rho2|\nabla\phi|^2+p+\rho gz=F(t).}
$$

Adding a function of time to the potential changes only $F(t)$ and leaves the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) unchanged.
