<h1 id="b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For positive depth put $c=\sqrt{gh}$. The equations become

$$
u_t+uu_x+2cc_x=0,\qquad c_t+uc_x+\frac c2u_x=0.
$$

Adding or subtracting twice the second equation to the first gives

$$
\boxed{(\partial_t+(u\pm c)\partial_x)(u\pm2c)=0.}
$$

Thus the two [characteristic speeds](../../../../../characteristic-speed.md) are $\boxed{dx/dt=u\pm c}$ and the corresponding conserved [Riemann invariants](../../../../../riemann-invariant.md) are $\boxed{Q_\pm=u\pm2c}$. At a dry front $c=0$ the two speeds coincide; the positive-depth formulas are applied up to that limit.
