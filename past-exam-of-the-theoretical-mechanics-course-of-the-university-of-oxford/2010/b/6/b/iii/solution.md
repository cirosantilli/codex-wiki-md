<h1 id="b/6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The head of the rarefaction reaches the wall at $(-a,t_0)$ with $t_0=a/c_0$. The right-running characteristic through that event is obtained by setting $\tau=t_0$ in the preceding formula:

$$
\boxed{x_b(t)=2c_0t-3a(c_0t/a)^{1/3}.}
$$

It bounds the region reached by the first wall-induced change in $Q_+$. To its right the characteristic information remains the original dam-break information, so

$$
\boxed{u=\frac23(c_0+x/t)\quad\text{for }x_b(t)\leq x<2c_0t.}
$$

Behind it, the impermeable wall must keep $u(-a,t)=0$. Continuing the original fan there would instead give $2(c_0-a/t)/3>0$ for $t>t_0$. Reflected characteristic data therefore change $Q_+$ and invalidate the simple fan formula. The shaded region in the characteristic diagram represents this changed [domain of dependence](../../../../../../domain-of-dependence.md); it includes $0<x<x_b(t)$ once that interval becomes nonempty. Its first positive extent occurs when $t/t_0>(3/2)^{3/2}$.
