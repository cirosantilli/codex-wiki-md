<h1 id="b/4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Rigid impermeable walls require zero normal fluid [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md), so the potential satisfies a homogeneous Neumann condition. In the cylinder,

$$
\boxed{\phi_r(a,\theta,z,t)=0,\qquad
\phi_z(r,\theta,0,t)=\phi_z(r,\theta,h,t)=0.}
$$

It is also single-valued and periodic in $\theta$, and regular on the axis. No tangential [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) is imposed on this inviscid acoustic potential.
