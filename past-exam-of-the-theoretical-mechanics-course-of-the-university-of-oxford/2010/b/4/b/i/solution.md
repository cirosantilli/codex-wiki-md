<h1 id="b/4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Linearization](../../../../../../linearization.md) about the uniform resting state gives

$$
\rho'_t+\rho_0\nabla\cdot\mathbf u'=0,\qquad
\rho_0\mathbf u'_t=-\nabla p',\qquad
p'_t=-\gamma p_0\nabla\cdot\mathbf u'.
$$

For irrotational motion write $\mathbf u'=\nabla\phi$. The momentum equation integrates to $p'=-\rho_0\phi_t$ after choosing the time-dependent potential gauge. Substituting into the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) equation yields

$$
\boxed{\phi_{tt}=c_0^2\nabla^2\phi,\qquad
c_0=\sqrt{\gamma p_0/\rho_0}.}
$$

The constant is the adiabatic [sound speed](../../../../../../speed-of-sound.md), also given by $(\partial p/\partial\rho)_S^{1/2}$ at the base state.
