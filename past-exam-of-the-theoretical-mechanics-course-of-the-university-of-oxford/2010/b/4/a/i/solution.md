<h1 id="b/4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [material derivative](../../../../../../material-derivative.md) $\boxed{D/Dt=\partial_t+\mathbf u\cdot\nabla}$ measures change while following a moving gas parcel. The four equations respectively express **[mass conservation](../../../../../../mass-conservation.md)**, **[momentum conservation](../../../../../../momentum-conservation.md)**, **internal-energy balance**, and the **[ideal gas law](../../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md)**. The first accounts for [density](../../../../../../density.md) change under expansion or compression. The second is Newton's law with [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) as the only force. In the third the specific internal energy is $c_vT$ and [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) work $-p\nabla\cdot\mathbf u$ heats compression and cools expansion. The fourth relates thermodynamic [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md), [density](../../../../../../density.md) and temperature; $R$ is the gas constant per unit mass.
