<h1 id="b/4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Conservation of the parcel's [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) makes $p/\rho^\gamma$ constant along its trajectory. Its initial value is $p_0/\rho_0^\gamma$, so

$$
\boxed{p=p_0\left(\frac\rho{\rho_0}\right)^\gamma.}
$$

This is a relation following the specified parcel; parcels with different initial [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) can have different constants.
