<h1 id="a/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppress hats for this calculation. Transverse [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) uniformity and zero surface [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) give $p=0$ throughout the film. Thus $u_{zz}=-S$. Integrating twice, using $u_z(h)=0$ and $u(0)=-1$, gives

$$
\boxed{u(z)=-1+S(hz-z^2/2).}
$$

The flux is $q=\int_0^h u\,dz=-h+Sh^3/3$. Integrate continuity across the layer and apply the wall and kinematic conditions to obtain $h_t+q_x=0$. Restoring hats yields

$$
\boxed{\widehat h_{\widehat t}+(S\widehat h^2-1)\widehat h_{\widehat x}=0.}
$$

The cubic flux contains downward [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md)-driven transport and upward wall entrainment.
