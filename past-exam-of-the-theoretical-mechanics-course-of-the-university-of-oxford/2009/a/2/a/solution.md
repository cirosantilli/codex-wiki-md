<h1 id="a/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\delta=Re^{-1/2}$ and use the inner variables $\boxed{Y=y/\delta=Re^{1/2}y,\quad V=v/\delta=Re^{1/2}v}$. Continuity balances $u_x$ with $V_Y$. In the tangential momentum balance, advection is order one while the normal viscous term is $Re^{-1}\delta^{-2}u_{YY}=u_{YY}$. Streamwise [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) is smaller by $Re^{-1}$. The normal momentum balance forces the leading [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) to be independent of $Y$. Thus

$$
\boxed{u_x+V_Y=0,\qquad uu_x+Vu_Y=-p_x+u_{YY},\qquad p_Y=0.}
$$

On the stationary impermeable plate, [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) and [no-penetration boundary condition](../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md) give $u=V=0$ at $Y=0$, $x>0$. Matching to the uniform outer stream gives $u\to1$ as $Y\to\infty$, and the leading-edge/upstream data are $u\to1$ as $x\downarrow0$ at fixed $Y>0$. There is no requirement that the scaled transverse [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $V$ vanish at infinity; its physical value $v=\delta V$ matches a smaller outer correction. The outer streamwise Euler equation with constant speed one gives $p_x=0$, and $p_Y=0$ carries this [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) gradient throughout the layer.
