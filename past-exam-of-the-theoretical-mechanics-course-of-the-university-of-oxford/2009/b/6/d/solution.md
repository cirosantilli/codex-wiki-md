<h1 id="b/6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $U<0$ the same positive algebraic root remains real and satisfies $0<V<c_0$, because $V^2-c_0^2=(\gamma+1)UV/2<0$. The jump relations then give a [density](../../../../../density.md) decrease, $\rho_1/\rho_0=V/(V-U)<1$, and a [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) decrease. Where both states remain thermodynamically positive, this is an expansion [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md): its upstream [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md)-frame flow is subsonic and its [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) change has the forbidden sign. At sufficiently large negative $U$ the formula even predicts nonpositive [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), an additional reason it cannot represent a physical gas state.

The observed outgoing wave is instead a continuous isentropic [rarefaction wave](../../../../../rarefaction-wave.md). Its plus-family rays spread from the initial piston corner rather than coalescing. The fan head enters the undisturbed gas at $x=c_0t$; for moderate recession the tail is $x=(U+c_1)t$, with the uniform gas next to the piston moving at $U$ and $c_1=c_0+(\gamma-1)U/2>0$. The appropriate [Riemann invariant](../../../../../riemann-invariant.md) is $u-2c/(\gamma-1)=-2c_0/(\gamma-1)$, so inside the fan, with $\xi=x/t$,

$$
u=\frac{2(\xi-c_0)}{\gamma+1},\qquad c=\frac{2c_0+(\gamma-1)\xi}{\gamma+1}.
$$

These expressions show the spreading characteristics in the right panel of the preceding figure. If $U\leq-2c_0/(\gamma-1)$, the gas cannot follow the piston with positive [sound speed](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md); a [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) gap forms, bounded by the limiting [rarefaction wave](../../../../../rarefaction-wave.md) edge. Thus the fan description also identifies the strong-recession limit.
