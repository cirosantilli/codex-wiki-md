<h1 id="b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Integrate each steady one-dimensional conservation law across a thin region enclosing the [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md). [Mass conservation](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) gives constant [mass flux](../../../../../mass-flux.md) $j=\rho u$. [Momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) includes both advected momentum and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) force, so $p+\rho u^2$ is constant. The total energy flux is $u(\rho e+p)=j(e+p/\rho)$, where $e=c_vT+u^2/2$. For a genuine [shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md) $j\ne0$, division by the [mass flux](../../../../../mass-flux.md) and the equation of state $c_vT=p/[(\gamma-1)\rho]$ give the conserved stagnation enthalpy

$$
e+p/\rho=\frac{u^2}2+\frac{\gamma p}{(\gamma-1)\rho}.
$$

Thus the [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions for a perfect gas](../../../../../rankine-hugoniot-conditions-for-a-perfect-gas.md) are

$$
\boxed{[\rho u]=0,\quad[p+\rho u^2]=0,\quad\left[\frac{u^2}2+\frac{\gamma p}{(\gamma-1)\rho}\right]=0.}
$$

The velocities here are measured in the stationary [shock frame](../../../../../shock-frame.md); a moving-[shock](../../../../../shock-wave.md) problem must first be transformed to that frame.
