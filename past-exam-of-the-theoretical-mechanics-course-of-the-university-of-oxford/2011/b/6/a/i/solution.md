<h1 id="b/6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [weak solution](../../../../../../weak-solution.md) is a field $\mathbf u$ for which the composed densities $\mathbf P(x,t,\mathbf u)$ and fluxes $\mathbf Q(x,t,\mathbf u)$ are locally integrable and

$$
\boxed{\iint\bigl(\mathbf P\cdot\partial_t\boldsymbol\varphi+\mathbf Q\cdot\partial_x\boldsymbol\varphi\bigr)\,dx\,dt=0}
$$

for every smooth compactly supported [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) $\boldsymbol\varphi$ in the spacetime domain. This is the distributional form of the [conservation law](../../../../../../conservation-law.md), obtained by moving [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) onto the [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md). If tests are allowed to meet an initial boundary $t=0$, the specified initial [density](../../../../../../density.md) contributes the additional term $\int\mathbf P(x,0,\mathbf u_0)\cdot\boldsymbol\varphi(x,0)dx$ in the usual initial-value formulation.
