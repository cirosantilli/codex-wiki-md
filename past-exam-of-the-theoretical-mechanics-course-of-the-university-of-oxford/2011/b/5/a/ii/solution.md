<h1 id="b/5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a negative second [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md), the corresponding local normal form is $\psi=\psi(k_\star)-s^2/2$. The oscillatory Gaussian [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) is the complex conjugate of the positive-curvature one, so the phase shift changes sign. Thus

$$
\boxed{I(t)=f(k_\star)e^{it\psi(k_\star)-i\pi/4}\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{t|\psi''(k_\star)|}}+o(t^{-1/2}).}
$$

When the leading [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) is nonzero, the equality may again be written as an asymptotic equivalence. Both signs are summarized by the factor $e^{i\pi\operatorname{sgn}(\psi'')/4}$.
