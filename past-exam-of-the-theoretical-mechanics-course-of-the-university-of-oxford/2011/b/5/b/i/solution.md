<h1 id="b/5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Irrotationality permits a [velocity potential](../../../../../../velocity-potential.md) with $\mathbf u=(\phi_x,\phi_z)$. [Incompressibility](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) gives [Laplace's equation](../../../../../../laplace-equation.md) in the moving domain $z<\eta(x,t)$. Neglecting surface tension and taking the free-surface [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) as constant atmospheric [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md), the full surface conditions are

$$
\boxed{\phi_{xx}+\phi_{zz}=0\quad(z<\eta),\qquad
\eta_t+\phi_x\eta_x=\phi_z\quad(z=\eta),\qquad
\phi_t+\tfrac12(\phi_x^2+\phi_z^2)+g\eta=0\quad(z=\eta).}
$$

The kinematic condition makes the [free surface](../../../../../../free-surface.md) material; the dynamic condition is [Bernoulli's equation](../../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) on that surface. A time-dependent additive gauge in $\phi$ has absorbed the spatially uniform Bernoulli constant. Deep below the surface, impose $\nabla\phi\to0$ as $z\to-\infty$, together with suitable horizontal decay. Initially $\eta(x,0)=\eta_0(x)$ and the [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) is at rest, so $\nabla\phi(x,z,0)=0$; its initial constant may be chosen as zero.
