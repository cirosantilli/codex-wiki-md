<h1 id="a/2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use [autonomous third-order reduction using the dependent variable](../../../../../../autonomous-third-order-reduction-using-the-dependent-variable.md). On a monotone branch where $f$ can serve as the independent variable, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
f''=GG_f,\qquad f'''=G(G_f^2+GG_{ff}).
$$

Therefore the reduced second-order equation is

$$
\boxed{G^2G_{ff}+G(G_f)^2+\frac\alpha2 fGG_f+\alpha(A^2-G^2)=0.}
$$

The wall and matching conditions become

$$
\boxed{G(0)=0,\qquad G(f)\longrightarrow A\text{ as }f\to\operatorname{sgn}(A)\infty.}
$$

For the usual positive outer flow this is the limit $f\to+\infty$. The reduced equation is singular at $G=0$: if $f''(0)=b\ne0$, then $f\sim b\eta^2/2$ and $G\sim b\eta$, so $G$ has square-root behavior as a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) of $f$ near the wall. A finite value for $G_f(0)$ should not be imposed as an extra [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md).
