<h1 id="a/3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [flux average for a translating lubrication gap](../../../../../../flux-average-for-a-translating-lubrication-gap.md) follows by integrating the [pressure gradient](../../../../../../pressure-gradient.md) between the endpoints:

$$
0=6\int_{-1}^1\frac{\widehat h-2Q}{\widehat h^3}\,d\widehat x.
$$

If $2Q$ were smaller than the minimum height, the integrand would be strictly positive everywhere, contradicting zero total [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) change. If it were larger than the maximum, the integrand would be strictly negative. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\min\widehat h\leq2Q\leq\max\widehat h.}
$$

Equivalently,

$$
2Q=\frac{\int_{-1}^1\widehat h^{-2}\,d\widehat x}{\int_{-1}^1\widehat h^{-3}\,d\widehat x}
$$

is a weighted average of the height with strictly positive weight $\widehat h^{-3}$. This also identifies the constant [flux](../../../../../../flux.md) uniquely.
