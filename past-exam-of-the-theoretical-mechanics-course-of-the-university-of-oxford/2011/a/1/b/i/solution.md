<h1 id="a/1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The only [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) component is axial, so [incompressibility](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) gives $\partial_zw=0$. Thus $w=w(x,y)$, and the steady convective [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) is $w\partial_z(w\mathbf k)=0$. The transverse [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) equations give $p_x=p_y=0$, so $p=p(z)$. The axial [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) equation is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mu(w_{xx}+w_{yy})=p'(z).}
$$

Its left side depends only on $x,y$, while its right side depends only on $z$. Equality throughout the pipe [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) both to be one constant, say $G$. Consequently **the axial [pressure gradient](../../../../../../pressure-gradient.md) is constant**, and $p(z)=Gz+p_*$. This is absence of dependence on a coordinate, rather than probabilistic independence.
