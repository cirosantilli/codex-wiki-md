# 2011

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the theoretical mechanics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source release: [Oxford examination archive release thread](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), Part B bundle pages 284–290. The cover identifies Theoretical Mechanics, paper B6, Friday 17 June 2011. Section A concerns viscous flow and section B [waves](../wave-split.md) and compressible flow.

Answer availability checked on 28 October 2026: bounded official Oxford searches by exact year, title, module and formal code found no full-paper or selected-part solution advertisement. No release policy for later years was assumed to cover 2011.
