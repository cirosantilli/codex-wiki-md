# Scaling symmetry of the sine-Gordon equation

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon equation](sine-gordon-equation.md)

The transformations

$$
(X,T,u)\longmapsto(e^sX,e^{-s}T,u)
$$

form a one-parameter [Lie point symmetry](lie-point-symmetry.md) of $u_{XT}=\sin u$. The product $z=XT$ is a [similarity variable](similarity-variable.md), and a [group-invariant solution](group-invariant-solution.md) $u=F(z)$ satisfies

$$
zF''+F'=\sin F.
$$
