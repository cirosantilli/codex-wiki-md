# Plain Kolmogorov complexity

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov complexity](kolmogorov-complexity.md)

For a fixed [optimal description machine](optimal-description-machine.md) $U$, the [plain Kolmogorov complexity](plain-kolmogorov-complexity.md) of a [binary string](binary-string.md) is the minimum length of a finite program producing it. The admissible programs need not satisfy a prefix restriction. There are at most $2^n-1$ descriptions shorter than $n$, which is the counting fact behind the existence of [incompressible strings](incompressible-string.md).
