<h1 id="hopf-s-formula">Hopf's formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schur multiplier](schur-multiplier.md)

For a [free presentation](free-presentation.md) $G\cong F/R$, [Hopf's formula](hopf-s-formula.md) is

$$
H_2(G,\mathbb Z)\cong\frac{R\cap[F,F]}{[F,R]}.
$$
