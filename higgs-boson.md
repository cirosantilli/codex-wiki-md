# Higgs boson

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs field](higgs-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higgs_boson)

The Higgs boson is the neutral radial excitation of the Higgs field about its vacuum expectation value.

In the single-doublet [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) this physical excitation is a [spin](spin.md) zero excitation of a [scalar field](scalar-field.md). Its mass depends on the [Higgs potential](higgs-field-potential.md), so the theory's [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md) alone does not specify where to search. The [Higgs boson discovery in 2012](higgs-boson-discovery-in-2012.md) tested the existence of a new [particle resonance](particle-resonance.md); establishing its detailed identity requires testing its interactions and [spin](spin.md) as well.
