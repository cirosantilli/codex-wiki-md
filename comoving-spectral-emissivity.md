# Comoving spectral emissivity

↑ **Parent:** [Luminosity density](luminosity-density.md)

[Comoving spectral emissivity](comoving-spectral-emissivity.md) is emitted [spectral luminosity](spectral-luminosity.md) per [comoving volume](comoving-volume.md), measured using source-rest-frame time and [frequency](frequency.md). For identical sources with [comoving number density](comoving-number-density.md) $n_c$, it is $\epsilon_\nu=n_cL_\nu$. For a population with a [luminosity function](luminosity-function-astronomy.md), integrate its rest-frame [spectral luminosity](spectral-luminosity.md) over that distribution. Multiplying a proper-volume emissivity by $a^3$ gives this comoving convention for dimensionless [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) $a$.
