# Newtonian limit of Nordstrom gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Nordström's theory of gravitation](nordstrom-s-theory-of-gravitation.md)

For a weak, nearly static [conformal factor](conformal-factor.md) in [Nordstrom theory of gravitation](nordstrom-s-theory-of-gravitation.md), the trace of the [dust stress-energy tensor](dust-stress-energy-tensor.md) is negative in signature $(-+++)$, so its scalar field equation reduces to the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) for the [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md). The [geodesic equation for a conformally flat metric](geodesic-equation-for-a-conformally-flat-metric.md) gives $\ddot{\boldsymbol x}=-\boldsymbol\nabla\Phi$ at small speeds. The constant one in $\phi$ has no effect on the force.
