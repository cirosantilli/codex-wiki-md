# The Global Schmidt Law in Star Forming Galaxies by Robert C. Kennicutt Jr.

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Robert C. Kennicutt Jr.](work-by-robert-c-kennicutt-jr.md)

This primary observational study combines disk-averaged star-formation and gas tracers for normal spirals and infrared-selected starbursts. Its fitted power-law index is $1.4\pm0.15$; the tracer selection, dust correction and gas conversion factors matter to interpretation.

[https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9712213](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9712213)

[https://doi.org/10.1086/305588](https://doi.org/10.1086/305588)
