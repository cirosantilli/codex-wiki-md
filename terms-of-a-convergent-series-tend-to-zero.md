# Terms of a convergent series tend to zero

↑ **Parent:** [Convergent series](convergent-series.md)

If the [partial sums](partial-sum.md) $S_n=\sum_{j=1}^nu_j$ tend to a finite limit $S$, then $u_n=S_n-S_{n-1}\to S-S=0$. Thus failure of the terms to tend to zero proves divergence. The converse is false: the [harmonic series](harmonic-series.md) has terms tending to zero but diverges.
