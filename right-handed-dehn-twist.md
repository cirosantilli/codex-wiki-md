# Right-handed Dehn twist

↑ **Parent:** [Dehn twist](dehn-twist.md)

In orientation-preserving annular coordinates $(\theta,r)$ with orientation $d\theta\wedge dr$, the twist is $(\theta,r)\mapsto(\theta+2\pi f(r),r)$, where $f$ increases from zero to one. The map is the identity outside this [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) and near its boundary. The orientation of the ambient [topological surface](topological-surface.md), rather than the orientation assigned to the curve, fixes the handedness.
