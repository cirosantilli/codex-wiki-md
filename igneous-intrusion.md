# Igneous intrusion

↑ **Parent:** [Magma](magma.md)

A body formed by emplacement and solidification of [magma](magma.md) within pre-existing [rock](rock-geology.md). [Dikes](dike.md) and deeper intrusive bodies add material and [heat](heat-split.md) to [crust](planetary-crust.md) without requiring surface eruption.
