# Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space

↑ **Parent:** [Frenet-Serret formulas](frenet-serret-formulas.md)

A Euclidean motion has the form $E(x)=Ax+b$ with $A\in O(3)$. It preserves [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md), angles, and all extrinsic geometric quantities transformed with their orientations.

This is the dimension-three case $E(3)=O(3)\ltimes\mathbb R^3$ of the [Euclidean group](euclidean-group.md).
