# Nambu spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md)

A [Nambu spinor](nambu-spinor.md) groups an [annihilation operator](annihilation-operator.md) with an opposite-momentum [creation operator](creation-operator.md) to express pairing in a quadratic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) as a matrix problem. Its components are not independent: the spinor at $-k$ is related to conjugated components at $k$. This redundancy must be accounted for when summing energies and [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) constants. Component phases are a convention and change the displayed off-diagonal matrix entries.
