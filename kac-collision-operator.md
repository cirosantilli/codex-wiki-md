# Kac collision operator

↑ **Parent:** [Kac model](kac-model.md)

For $N\geq2$, $U_{ij,\theta}F=F\circ R_{ij,\theta}$ uses the genuine two-coordinate rotation $(v_i,v_j)\mapsto(v_i\cos\theta+v_j\sin\theta,-v_i\sin\theta+v_j\cos\theta)$. Average it uniformly over angles and pairs to obtain $Q$. Rotation invariance of [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) makes $Q$ a bounded [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) of [norm](norm.md) one on $L^2(\mathbb R^N)$. Each pair average is an [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md), but their full average is not in general a projection.
