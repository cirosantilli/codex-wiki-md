# Chern number

↑ **Parent:** [Chern class](chern-class.md)

On a compact oriented $2r$-manifold without boundary, an integral product of [Chern classes](chern-class.md) of total complex degree $r$ pairs with the [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) to give a [Chern number](chern-number.md). A [unitary connection](unitary-connection.md) supplies [Chern-Weil theory](chern-weil-homomorphism.md) representatives, but the integer is independent of that connection. The [Second Chern number](second-chern-number.md) on a four-manifold is one important example; the integral of the [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md) over a closed surface is another.
