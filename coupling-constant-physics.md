# Coupling constant (physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Interaction term](interaction-term.md)

A coefficient multiplying an interaction term controls its strength relative to the chosen field normalization. Its [engineering dimension](engineering-dimension.md) determines whether it is dimensionless, relevant or irrelevant. A renormalized [coupling constant](coupling-constant-physics.md) constant is specified at a [renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md); its change with that scale is described by the [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md).
