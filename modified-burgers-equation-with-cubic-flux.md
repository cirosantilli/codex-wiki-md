# Modified Burgers equation with cubic flux

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md)

The cubic-flux variant replaces the quadratic conservative flux of [Burgers' equation](burgers-equation.md) by $-q^3/3$. Its inviscid [characteristic speed](characteristic-speed.md) is $-q^2$, while positive diffusivity regularizes [shock waves](shock-wave.md). It is not linearized by the usual [Cole-Hopf transformation](cole-hopf-transformation.md).
