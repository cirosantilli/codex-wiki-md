# Bessel threshold for self-buckling of a vertical rod

↑ **Parent:** [Self-buckling of a vertical rod](self-buckling-of-a-vertical-rod.md)

For a uniform [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md) clamped below and free above, its own weight gives [filament tension](filament-tension.md) $T(z)=-\lambda g(L-z)$. With [filament bending modulus](filament-bending-modulus.md) $B=EI$, the static slope $u$ satisfies $Bu''+\lambda g(L-z)u=0$, $u(0)=0$, and $u'(L)=0$. Setting $\eta=(2/3)\sqrt{\lambda g/B}(L-z)^{3/2}$ gives $u=\eta^{1/3}J_{-1/3}(\eta)$ up to amplitude; the $J_{1/3}$ branch violates the zero-moment condition. The first positive zero $j_{-1/3,1}$ therefore yields $\lambda gL_c^3/B\simeq7.83735$. This is a [self-buckling of a vertical rod](self-buckling-of-a-vertical-rod.md) threshold, not a constant-end-load Euler threshold.
