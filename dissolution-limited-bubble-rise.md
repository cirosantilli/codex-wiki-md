# Dissolution-limited bubble rise

↑ **Parent:** [Clean-bubble Stokes drag](clean-bubble-stokes-drag.md)

A spherical clean bubble with constant gas [mass density](density.md) and prescribed dissolution law $\dot M=-4\pi aD\rho_a$ obeys $\dot a=-D/a$. With negligible gas [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md), instantaneous [terminal velocity](terminal-velocity.md) is $\dot z=\Delta\rho ga^2/(3\mu)$. Eliminating time yields the displayed radius-height relation and maximum rise $z_{\mathrm{diss}}=\Delta\rho ga_0^4/(12\mu D)$. This uses an instantaneous force balance; a literally constant speed during shrinkage instead gives $a^2$ linear in height. The [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) must remain small for the [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md) drag model to apply.
