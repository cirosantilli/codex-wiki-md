# Fubini-Study metric

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fubini–Study_metric)

The Fubini-Study metric is the standard unitary-invariant [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md) on [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md), induced from the unit sphere by the [Hopf fibration](hopf-fibration.md). Its associated [Kähler form](kahler-form.md) is the [Fubini-Study form](fubini-study-form.md); scaling conventions determine the area of a projective line.
