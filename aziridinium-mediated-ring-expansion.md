# Aziridinium-mediated ring expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

A neighbouring [tertiary amine](tertiary-amine.md) displaces a leaving group to form a three-membered ammonium ring; attack at the original ring [carbon](carbon.md) cleaves its old C–N bond and incorporates the side-chain [carbon](carbon.md) into an expanded [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) ring.
