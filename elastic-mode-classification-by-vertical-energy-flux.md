# Elastic mode classification by vertical energy flux

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic energy velocity](elastic-energy-velocity.md)

For a mode $u=a e^{i\omega(p_\alpha x_\alpha+qx_3-t)}$ with $x_3$ positive downwards, the reduced [traction](traction.md) is $(Tq+R)a$. Its mean vertical [acoustic energy flux](acoustic-energy-flux.md) is the displayed expression. Propagating modes are downgoing for positive flux and upgoing for negative flux; phase-slowness sign need not agree in [anisotropy](anisotropy.md). Depth-decaying evanescent modes have $\operatorname{Im}q>0$. At grazing or repeated roots use limiting radiation modes.
