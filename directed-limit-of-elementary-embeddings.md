# Directed limit of elementary embeddings

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary chain theorem](elementary-chain-theorem.md)

A directed system of [first-order structures](first-order-structure.md) and coherent [elementary embeddings](elementary-embedding.md) has a direct limit into which every member embeds elementarily. Represent an element by a pair consisting of a stage and an element at that stage; identify two representatives when their images agree at a common later stage. Interpret [functions](function-split.md) and relations at a common stage. [Mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) on [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) proves elementarity: for an existential [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) true at the limit, its witness and parameters occur at a common stage, and elementarity pulls truth back to the stage containing the original parameters. This generalizes the [elementary chain theorem](elementary-chain-theorem.md) beyond linearly ordered diagrams.
