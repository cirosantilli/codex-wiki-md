# Seismic transmission coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic reflection and transmission at an interface](acoustic-reflection-and-transmission-at-an-interface.md)

The transmitted-to-incident amplitude ratio at an interface, with physical variable specified. At normal incidence, stress amplitudes give $T_\sigma=2Z_b/(Z_a+Z_b)=1+R_\sigma$, while particle velocity gives $T_v=2Z_a/(Z_a+Z_b)=1-R_\sigma$. The transmitted energy fraction is $4Z_aZ_b/(Z_a+Z_b)^2$; amplitude greater than one therefore does not by itself violate [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md).
