# Disjoint cylinder decomposition of open subsets of Cantor space

↑ **Parent:** [Cantor space](cantor-space.md)

Every [open set](open-set.md) in [Cantor space](cantor-space.md) is a countable disjoint union of prefix [cylinder sets](cylinder-set.md). Select all finite words whose cylinder lies in the open set but whose immediate predecessor's cylinder does not. These words are prefix-free, so their cylinders are disjoint. Every point of the open set has a shortest qualifying prefix. The whole space is represented by the empty prefix.
