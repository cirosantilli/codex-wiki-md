# Basic measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Projective measurement](projective-measurement.md)

A rank-one [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) associated with an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $\{|b_j\rangle\}_j$. For a [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) $\rho$, outcome $j$ has [probability](probability.md) $\langle b_j|\rho|b_j\rangle$ and leaves the measured system in the [pure state](pure-state.md) $|b_j\rangle$ whenever that [probability](probability.md) is nonzero. It can be implemented by mapping this [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) to the [computational basis](computational-basis.md) with a [unitary transformation](unitary-operator.md) and performing a [computational-basis measurement](quantum-measurement-in-the-computational-basis.md).[§2.3, Definition 2.34; specialize the measurement effects to rank-one orthogonal projections](the-theory-of-quantum-information-by-john-watrous/basic-notions-of-quantum-information.md)
