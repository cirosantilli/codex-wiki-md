# Shortest-path distance

↑ **Parent:** [Shortest path problem](shortest-path-problem-split.md)

The infimum of total [edge weights](edge-weight.md) over allowed walks from a source to a target. The value is $+\infty$ when no route exists. A [negative cycle](negative-cycle.md) reachable from the source and able to reach the target makes it $-\infty$. In a finite graph without such a cycle, a finite distance is attained by a [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md), after removing non-improving repetitions.
