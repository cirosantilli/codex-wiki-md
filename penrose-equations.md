# Penrose equations

↑ **Parent:** [Moore-Penrose inverse](moore-penrose-inverse.md)

The Moore--Penrose inverse is characterized by

$$
AA^\dagger A=A,
\quad
A^\dagger AA^\dagger=A^\dagger,
\quad
(AA^\dagger)^*=AA^\dagger,
\quad
(A^\dagger A)^*=A^\dagger A.
$$
