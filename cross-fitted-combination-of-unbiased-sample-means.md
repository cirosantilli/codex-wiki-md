# Cross-fitted combination of unbiased sample means

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum-variance combination of unbiased sample means](minimum-variance-combination-of-unbiased-sample-means.md)

Split independent observational blocks into two folds. Use one fold to estimate the combination weight for the two [sample means](sample-mean.md) in the other fold, and conversely. Each held-out combination is an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) conditionally on its training fold because its two held-out [sample means](sample-mean.md) have the same [expectation](expected-value.md). A deterministic average of the two combinations remains unbiased. Under consistency of the moment estimates and balanced fold sizes, the weights approach those of the [minimum-variance combination of unbiased sample means](minimum-variance-combination-of-unbiased-sample-means.md). Paired observations must stay together when splitting or [bootstrapping](bootstrapping-statistics.md) so their [covariance](covariance.md) is preserved.
