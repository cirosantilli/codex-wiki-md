# Deviance residual

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized linear model](generalized-linear-model.md)

A deviance residual is the signed square root of an observation's contribution $d_i$ to fitted-model deviance. For a [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.md), $d_i=2[y_i\log(y_i/\widehat\mu_i)-(y_i-\widehat\mu_i)]$, with $0\log0=0$. Its squared sum is the [Poisson deviance](poisson-deviance.md). Leverage and estimated dispersion can further standardize these residuals; a normal [quantile-quantile plot](q-q-plot.md) is a diagnostic approximation, not a requirement that count errors have a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md).
