# Uniform ring condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensate](bose-einstein-condensate.md)

A one-dimensional condensate on a ring of circumference $2\pi R$ has a uniform winding state $\psi=\sqrt{N/(2\pi R)}e^{in\theta}$ when the order parameter is normalized to atom number. Its [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) per atom is $n\hbar$, while the flow contribution to its total [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is $\hbar^2n^2/(2mR^2)$.
