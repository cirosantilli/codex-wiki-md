# Uniqueness for a strongly monotone variational inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Variational inequality](variational-inequality.md)

Let $K$ be a [convex set](convex-set-split.md) in a real [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), and let $A:K\to X^*$ be a [strongly monotone operator](strongly-monotone-operator.md). If $u,w\in K$ both satisfy the displayed [variational inequality](variational-inequality.md), testing with $v=w$ and $v=u$ respectively and adding yields $\langle Au-Aw,u-w\rangle\le0$. Strong monotonicity gives $m\|u-w\|^2\le0$, hence $u=w$. This proves uniqueness whenever a solution exists.
