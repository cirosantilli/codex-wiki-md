# Compatible almost complex structure

↑ **Parent:** [Almost complex manifold](almost-complex-manifold.md)

An [almost complex structure](almost-complex-manifold.md) $J$ on a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) $(M,\omega)$ is compatible with $\omega$ when

$$
\omega(Ju,Jv)=\omega(u,v)
$$

and $g_J(u,v)=\omega(u,Jv)$ is a positive-definite [inner product](inner-product.md). Every symplectic vector bundle admits a compatible almost complex structure.
