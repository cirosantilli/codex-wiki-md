# Hyperfine multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md)

The set of [hyperfine levels](hyperfine-level.md) for fixed nuclear spin I and electronic J. Its total angular momenta run from F=|I-J| to F=I+J; each zero-field F level contains 2F+1 magnetic substates. The multiplet is the collection, rather than a synonym for one member level.
