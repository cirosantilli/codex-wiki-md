# Identity element

↑ **Parent:** [Binary operation](binary-operation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_element)

An [identity element](identity-element.md) $e$ for a [binary operation](binary-operation.md) satisfies $e\ast a=a\ast e=a$ for every element $a$ of the underlying [set](set-split.md). It is a two-sided identity: merely satisfying one of the two equations is insufficient. It is unique when it exists, since two candidates satisfy $e=e\ast e'=e'$. This uniqueness does not require the [associative property](associative-property.md).

Zero is the [identity element](identity-element.md) for [addition](addition.md) of [integers](integer.md), one for [multiplication](multiplication.md) of [integers](integer.md), and the [identity function](identity-function.md) for [function composition](function-composition.md) of [self-maps](self-map.md). The [empty word](empty-word.md) is the [identity element](identity-element.md) for [concatenation](concatenation.md). An [associative operation](associative-property.md) with an [identity element](identity-element.md) makes a [monoid](monoid.md); [inverse elements](inverse-element.md) are an additional requirement for a [group](group-split.md).
