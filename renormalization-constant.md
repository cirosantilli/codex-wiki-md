# Renormalization constant

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

A [renormalization constant](renormalization-constant.md) relates bare parameters or fields to renormalized ones, for example $\phi_0=Z_\phi^{1/2}\phi$. Its regulator-dependent terms cancel [ultraviolet divergences](ultraviolet-divergence.md); its finite part depends on the [renormalization scheme](renormalization-scheme.md). Field and vertex constants may depend on the chosen [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) even when physical observables do not.
