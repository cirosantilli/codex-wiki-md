# Multiplicity descent in toric desingularization

↑ **Parent:** [Toric resolution of singularities](toric-resolution-of-singularities.md)

First make a [toric fan](fan-in-toric-geometry.md) simplicial by compatible rational subdivisions. In a nonregular simplicial cone, choose a nonregular face minimal under inclusion. Its half-open fundamental parallelepiped contains a nonzero point of the [toric lattice](lattice-in-toric-geometry.md) $w=\sum_i\alpha_iv_i$, with every $0<\alpha_i<1$: a zero coefficient would put it in a regular proper face and force all coefficients to be integers. A [star subdivision](star-subdivision.md) at the primitive point on this ray replaces a generator by $w$ and multiplies the affected lattice determinant by a number strictly between zero and one. The maximum multiplicity and the number of maximal cones realizing it decrease lexicographically, giving a finite [toric resolution of singularities](toric-resolution-of-singularities.md).
