<h1 id="a-stability-of-near-diagonal-exponential-pade-approximants">A-stability of near-diagonal exponential Padé approximants</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A-stability](a-stability.md)

For nonnegative degrees $m,n$ with $n-2\le m\le n$, the $[m/n]$ [Padé approximant](pade-approximant.md) $R$ to the [exponential function](exponential-function.md) has no poles in the left half-plane and satisfies $|R(z)|\le1$ for $\operatorname{Re}z\le0$. Consequently a numerical method having that [stability function](stability-function.md) is [A-stable](a-stability.md). In particular, the near-diagonal choice $[s-1/s]$ gives the [Radau IIA method](radau-iia-method.md) stability function, while $[s/s]$ corresponds to the [Gauss collocation method](gauss-legendre-method.md). The theorem concerns these exponential approximants, not arbitrary rational approximations with the same degrees.
