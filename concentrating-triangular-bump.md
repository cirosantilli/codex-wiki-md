# Concentrating triangular bump

↑ **Parent:** [Pointwise convergence](pointwise-convergence.md)

On $[0,1]$, for $n\geq2$, these [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) have support $[0,2/n]$, height $n$, and integral one. Their [pointwise limit](pointwise-limit.md) is zero: at zero every value vanishes, while every fixed positive argument eventually leaves the support. Thus [pointwise convergence](pointwise-convergence.md) of [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) on a [compact interval](compact-interval.md) does not suffice for convergence of their integrals. The mass becomes more concentrated as its height grows.
