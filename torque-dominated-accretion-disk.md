# Torque-dominated accretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Steady Keplerian accretion disk with an inner torque](steady-keplerian-accretion-disk-with-an-inner-torque.md)

When the applied inner torque dominates the advective [angular-momentum flux](angular-momentum-flux.md), the constant-[kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) inner profile resembles a [decretion disk](decretion-disk.md). This resemblance does not alone imply outward mass transport.
