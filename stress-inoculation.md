# Stress inoculation

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological resilience](psychological-resilience.md)

A proposed protective effect of manageable prior challenges on later responses. A curved association between lifetime [adversity](adversity.md) and current functioning is not sufficient to establish that effect causally or identify a safe beneficial [exposure](exposure.md) for an individual. Human observational and animal experimental evidence answer different questions.[2010, nonmonotonic longitudinal associations](whatever-does-not-kill-us-by-mark-d-seery-and-others.md)
