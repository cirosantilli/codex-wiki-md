# Initial velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Initial condition](initial-condition.md)

Initial velocity specifies the time derivative of the [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) at the starting time. A second-order [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) requires it independently of [initial displacement](initial-displacement.md). Both vanish for a causal scattered field before an incident pulse reaches its scatterer.
