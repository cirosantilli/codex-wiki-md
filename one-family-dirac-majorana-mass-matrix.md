# One-family Dirac-Majorana mass matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Seesaw mechanism](seesaw-mechanism.md)

In an all-left-handed Weyl [basis](basis.md) $(\nu_L,\nu_R^c)$, the symmetric [matrix](matrix.md) has left and right Majorana entries $M_L,M_R$ and off-diagonal Dirac entry $m$. Real [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) can have signs; physical Majorana [masses](mass.md) are nonnegative after appropriate field rephasings.
