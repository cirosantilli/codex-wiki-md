# Cis effect

↑ **Parent:** [Dissociative substitution](dissociative-substitution.md)

The carbonyl [cis effect](cis-effect.md) is preferential labilization of a ligand cis to another ligand in many six-coordinate metal carbonyls. Site preference in five-coordinate intermediates and transition states matters. Isotopic exchange can distinguish departure sites more directly than substitution-product geometry alone.
