# Matter parity

↑ **Parent:** [R-parity](r-parity.md)

[Matter parity](matter-parity.md) assigns odd parity to the quark and lepton [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md) and even parity to the [Higgs chiral doublets](higgs-chiral-doublet.md) and gauge [superfields](superfield.md). It is related to component [R-parity](r-parity.md) by $R_p=P_M(-1)^{2s}$. The Lorentz-invariant action contains an even number of fermions, so conserved matter parity is equivalent to conserved [R-parity](r-parity.md). Products of four matter [superfields](superfield.md) can be even while violating [baryon number](baryon-number.md); matter parity alone does not exclude higher-dimensional [proton-decay operators](proton-decay-operator.md).
