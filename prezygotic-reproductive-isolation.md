# Prezygotic reproductive isolation

↑ **Parent:** [Reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md)

Barriers to gene exchange acting before a zygote forms. Habitat separation, differing breeding times, behavioral discrimination and mechanical mismatch can reduce mating or gamete transfer; gametic incompatibility can act after mating while still preventing [fertilization](fertilization.md). Thus [prezygotic isolation](prezygotic-reproductive-isolation.md) is broader than [premating isolation](premating-isolation.md) and distinct from [postzygotic isolation](postzygotic-isolation.md). The time and biological stage measured by an assay determine which category it tests.
