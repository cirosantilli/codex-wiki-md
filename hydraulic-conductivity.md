# Hydraulic conductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy law](darcy-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulic_conductivity)

[Hydraulic conductivity](hydraulic-conductivity.md) is the coefficient in $\mathbf q=-K\nabla h$, with units $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Because the gradient of [hydraulic head](hydraulic-head.md) is dimensionless, [hydraulic conductivity](hydraulic-conductivity.md) has the dimensions of [velocity](velocity.md), but it is not the actual [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) unless the driving gradient has magnitude one. Intrinsic [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) $k$ has units $\mathrm{m^2}$ and describes pore geometry; [hydraulic conductivity](hydraulic-conductivity.md) also depends on [mass density](density.md), [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) and [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md).[https://books.gw-project.org/hydrogeologic-properties-of-earth-materials-and-principles-of-groundwater-flow/chapter/hydraulic-conductivity/](https://books.gw-project.org/hydrogeologic-properties-of-earth-materials-and-principles-of-groundwater-flow/chapter/hydraulic-conductivity/)

For $k=10^{-12}\,\mathrm{m^2}$, $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ and $\mu=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Pa\,s}$, [hydraulic conductivity](hydraulic-conductivity.md) is $K=9.81\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Doubling [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) while keeping pore geometry, [mass density](density.md) and [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) fixed halves [hydraulic conductivity](hydraulic-conductivity.md) without changing intrinsic [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md). Thus a value quoted as a material's [hydraulic conductivity](hydraulic-conductivity.md) needs its [fluid](fluid.md) conditions.
