# Coulomb integral

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chemistry](quantum-chemistry.md)

In [atomic units](atomic-units.md), the displayed integral is the direct electrostatic contribution between two normalized [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) $a,b$. It enters a two-electron [energy](energy-split.md) expectation together with the [exchange integral](exchange-integral.md).
