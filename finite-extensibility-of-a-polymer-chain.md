# Finite extensibility of a polymer chain

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer contour length](polymer-contour-length.md)

The [end-to-end distance of a polymer chain](end-to-end-distance-of-a-polymer-chain.md) with $N$ fixed-length links of [length](length.md) $a$ cannot exceed its [polymer contour length](polymer-contour-length.md) $Na$. A [Gaussian chain](gaussian-chain.md) [spring](spring.md) has no such bound, so it eventually becomes an unphysical extrapolation. For a three-dimensional [freely jointed chain](ideal-chain.md) under [force](force-split.md) $f$, the mean extension along the [force](force-split.md) is $Na[\coth x-1/x]$, where $x=fa/(k_BT)$. Its small-$x$ limit is $Nfa^2/(3k_BT)$, and its large-$x$ limit approaches $Na$ from below.
