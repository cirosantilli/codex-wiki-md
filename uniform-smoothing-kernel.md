# Uniform smoothing kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel for nonparametric regression](kernel-for-nonparametric-regression.md)

The [uniform smoothing kernel](uniform-smoothing-kernel.md) assigns equal weight inside the window $[x-h,x+h]$ and zero weight outside. Its [Nadaraya–Watson estimator](nadaraya-watson-estimator.md) is the average of observations in that window.
