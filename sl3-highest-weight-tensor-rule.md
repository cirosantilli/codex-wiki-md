# sl3 highest-weight tensor rule

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of Lie algebra representations](tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md)

For the complex [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sl}_3$, tensoring an irreducible [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md) by the defining representation gives the displayed sum, omitting terms with negative [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md). It follows by multiplying its [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md) by $x_1+x_2+x_3$. In particular $\Gamma_{2,1}\otimes\Gamma_{1,0}=\Gamma_{3,1}\oplus\Gamma_{1,2}\oplus\Gamma_{2,0}$, with dimensions $24+15+6=45$.
