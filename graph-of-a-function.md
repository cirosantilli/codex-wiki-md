# Graph of a function

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_of_a_function)

For a [function](function-split.md) $f:A\to B$, its graph is the subset $\{(x,f(x)):x\in A\}$ of $A\times B$. It differs from a [graph](graph-split.md) in [graph theory](graph-theory-split.md). A solution curve of a scalar differential equation can be a graph over its independent coordinate.
