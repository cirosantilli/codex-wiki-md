# Gyrokinetics

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyrokinetics)

A low-[frequency](frequency.md) kinetic reduction averaging over fast [cyclotron motion](gyromotion.md) while allowing perpendicular [wavelengths](wavelength.md) comparable with the [gyroradius](gyroradius.md). It retains [finite Larmor radius effects](finite-larmor-radius-effect.md) absent from the leading long-[wavelength](wavelength.md) [drift-kinetic equation](drift-kinetic-equation.md).
