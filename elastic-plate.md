# Elastic plate

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elastic_plate)

An elastic plate is a thin elastic body whose leading deformation is bending of its middle surface.
