# Composition of relations

↑ **Parent:** [Binary relation](binary-relation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Composition_of_relations)

This convention composes paths in their written order; some sources instead reverse that convention. [Composition of relations](composition-of-relations.md) is associative. Taking inverse relations reverses the order: $(R\circ S)^{-1}=S^{-1}\circ R^{-1}$.
