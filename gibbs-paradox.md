# Gibbs paradox

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md)

Counting permutations of identical classical [particles](particle-physics.md) as different states produces a spurious [entropy of mixing](entropy-of-mixing.md) when otherwise identical gases are combined. The dilute [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) $Z_N=Z_1^N/N!$ removes that label multiplicity and gives an extensive [entropy](entropy-split.md) in the thermodynamic limit. For different species, genuinely distinct composition information still produces a [entropy of mixing](entropy-of-mixing.md). [quantum statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics-split.md) supplies the underlying identical-particle counting.
