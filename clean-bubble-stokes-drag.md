# Clean-bubble Stokes drag

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes's law](stokes-s-law.md)

A spherical clean bubble with negligible internal [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) has zero tangential [traction](traction.md) and permits tangential slip. In the creeping-flow, negligible-deformation limit its drag is $-4\pi\mu a\mathbf U$, versus $-6\pi\mu a\mathbf U$ for a no-slip rigid sphere.
