# Mechanical configuration

↑ **Parent:** [Mechanical configuration space](mechanical-configuration-space.md)

A mechanical configuration specifies the [positions](position.md) and [orientations](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) of all parts of a system, subject to its constraints. It is a point of the [mechanical configuration space](mechanical-configuration-space.md) $Q$; [generalized coordinates](generalized-coordinate.md) label such points locally. A configuration does not by itself specify [velocities](velocity.md), so it generally does not determine the system's full dynamical state. For a bead on a fixed circular hoop, its [angle](angle.md) specifies its configuration, while its angular velocity is additional data.
