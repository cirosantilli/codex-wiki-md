# Factorial weighted right shift

↑ **Parent:** [Unilateral shift operator](unilateral-shift-operator.md)

On the [absolutely summable sequence space](absolutely-summable-sequence-space.md), this weighted shift has $T^me_j=e_{j+m}/[j(j+1)\cdots(j+m-1)]$, so $\|T^m\|=1/m!$. The [spectral radius formula](spectral-radius-formula.md) gives radius zero. Its spectrum is exactly $\{0\}$ because it is not onto, while it has no eigenvalues: zero is excluded by injectivity, and any nonzero eigenvalue equation forces all coordinates to vanish starting at the first. Thus it is a nonzero [quasinilpotent element](quasinilpotent-element.md) of the operator algebra.
