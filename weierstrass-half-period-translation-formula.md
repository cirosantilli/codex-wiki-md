# Weierstrass half-period translation formula

↑ **Parent:** [Half-period values of the Weierstrass elliptic function](half-period-values-of-the-weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

Let $h_i$ represent a nonzero [two-torsion point of a complex torus](two-torsion-point-of-a-complex-torus.md), and write $e_i=\wp(h_i)$. The product $(\wp(z+h_i)-e_i)(\wp(z)-e_i)$ is an [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md) whose apparent [poles](pole.md) cancel against double [zeros](zero-of-a-function.md). It is therefore constant by [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md). Its value at $z=0$ is $\wp''(h_i)/2$. The [Weierstrass elliptic differential equation](weierstrass-elliptic-differential-equation.md) gives $\wp''(h_i)/2=3e_i^2-g_2/4=(e_i-e_j)(e_i-e_l)$, proving the formula.
