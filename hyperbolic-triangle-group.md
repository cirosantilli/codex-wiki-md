# Hyperbolic triangle group

↑ **Parent:** [Fuchsian group](fuchsian-group.md)

When $1/p+1/q+1/r<1$, the orientation-preserving hyperbolic triangle group has presentation

$$
\Delta(p,q,r)=\langle a,b,c\mid a^p=b^q=c^r=abc=1\rangle
$$

and is a non-elementary Fuchsian group.

This is the orientation-preserving hyperbolic member of the broader [triangle group](triangle-group.md) construction.
