# Scattering s-channel

↑ **Parent:** [Mandelstam variables](mandelstam-variables.md)

The direct channel combines the two incoming [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) vectors. A one-particle [pole](pole.md) in this channel has the intermediate particle's squared [mass](mass.md) equal to $s$. Imaginary [rapidity](rapidity.md) differences can satisfy this [mass](mass.md) relation for a [bound state](bound-state.md) even though there is no [pole](pole.md) at real physical scattering [rapidity](rapidity.md).
