# Mean energy of a two-dimensional Fermi gas

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional free-electron density of states](two-dimensional-free-electron-density-of-states.md)

A quadratic-dispersion [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md) in two dimensions has constant [density of states](density-of-states.md) $g$. At zero [temperature](temperature-split.md), $N=gE_F$ and $U=gE_F^2/2$. Hence the mean [energy](energy-split.md) is half the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md), independent of a common spin-degeneracy factor.
