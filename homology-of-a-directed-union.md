# Homology of a directed union

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)

Suppose $X=\bigcup_aX_a$ is directed by inclusion and every compact subset of $X$ lies in some $X_a$. Every finite [singular chain](singular-chain.md) then lies in one $X_a$, so $C_*(X)=\varinjlim_aC_*(X_a)$. Exactness of [filtered colimits](filtered-colimit-of-modules.md) gives

$$
H_i(X)\cong\varinjlim_aH_i(X_a).
$$
