# Vanishing moment

↑ **Parent:** [Wavelet](wavelet.md)

A [wavelet](wavelet.md) has $q$ vanishing moments when these integrals vanish for $0\le r<q$. For [compact support](compact-support.md), this is equivalent to a zero of order at least $q$ at the origin of its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md). Such a [wavelet](wavelet.md) annihilates [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of degree less than $q$, and a [Taylor polynomial](taylor-polynomial.md) bounds coefficients on smooth regions.
