<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A further field derivative of the [magnetization](../../../../../magnetization.md) scaling form gives

$$
\chi=\partial_hm=s^{2-\alpha-2\Delta}g'_{m,\pm}(x).
$$

At zero field, on a branch with a finite nonzero susceptibility [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md), comparison with $\chi\sim s^{-\gamma}$ yields

$$
\boxed{\gamma=2\Delta-2+\alpha=\Delta-\beta.}
$$

Consequently $\alpha+2\beta+\gamma=2$ and $\gamma=\beta(\delta-1)$, the Rushbrooke and [Widom scaling relations](../../../../../widom-scaling-relation.md). Regular analytic terms do not change the leading divergent susceptibility.
