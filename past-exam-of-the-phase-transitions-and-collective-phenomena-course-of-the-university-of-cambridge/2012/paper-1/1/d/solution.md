<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Hyperscaling](../../../../../hyperscaling-relation.md) adds the physical assumption that the singular [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) associated with one [correlation volume](../../../../../correlation-volume.md) is of order $k_BT_c$:

$$
f_s\xi^d\sim k_BT_c,\qquad f_s\sim\xi^{-d}.
$$

At fixed scaled field $x=h/s^\Delta$, this predicts $f_s\sim s^{d\nu}$, whereas the given thermodynamic form has power $2-\alpha$. Hence

$$
\boxed{d\nu=2-\alpha.}
$$

The product $g_f(x)[g_\xi(x)]^d$ can be a finite dimensionless function of fixed $x$; it need not be constant. The homogeneity of $\xi$ alone is insufficient: the correlation-volume free-energy assumption supplies the connection. Above an [upper critical dimension](../../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md), a [dangerously irrelevant coupling](../../../../../dangerously-irrelevant-coupling.md) can invalidate this [argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md); at a marginal dimension logarithms may qualify pure [powers](../../../../../power.md).
