<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $s=|t|$, $x=h/s^\Delta$ and separate scaling functions above and below the transition. Up to a nonsingular conversion between physical and dimensionless field, [magnetization scaling-function matching](../../../../../magnetization-scaling-function-matching.md) gives

$$
m=-\partial_h f_s=s^{2-\alpha-\Delta}g_{m,\pm}(x),\qquad g_{m,\pm}=-g'_{f,\pm}.
$$

On the ordered side take $h\to0^+$ after the [thermodynamic limit](../../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md), selecting one [pure thermodynamic phase](../../../../../pure-thermodynamic-phase.md). A finite nonzero $g_{m,-}(0^+)$ implies

$$
\boxed{\beta=2-\alpha-\Delta.}
$$

The critical isotherm must have a finite nonzero limit at fixed small $h$ as $s\to0$. Thus $g_m(x)\sim C\operatorname{sgn}(x)|x|^{1/\delta}$ at large $|x|$. Cancellation of the residual power of $s$ requires

$$
2-\alpha-\Delta-\frac\Delta\delta=0,\qquad
\boxed{\delta=\frac{\Delta}{2-\alpha-\Delta},\qquad\Delta=\beta\delta.}
$$

The absolute value and branch choice matter: spontaneous zero-field [magnetization](../../../../../magnetization.md) is below $T_c$, whereas the symmetry-unbroken [thermodynamic phase](../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md) has $m(t,0)=0$. The [field crossover exponent](../../../../../field-crossover-exponent.md) is not the [scaling dimension](../../../../../scaling-dimension.md) of the [magnetization](../../../../../magnetization.md) field.
