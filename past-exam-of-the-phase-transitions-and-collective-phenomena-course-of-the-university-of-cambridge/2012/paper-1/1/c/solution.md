<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At fixed zero field, $\partial_T=T_c^{-1}\partial_t$. Therefore, away from $t=0$ on either branch,

$$
c_{h,s}=-T\left(\frac{\partial^2 f_s}{\partial T^2}\right)_h
=-\frac{T}{T_c^2}(2-\alpha)(1-\alpha)g_{f,\pm}(0)s^{-\alpha}.
$$

Thus **the singular [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) has exponent $\alpha$**. An arbitrary analytic background must be added to obtain the full [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md). Vanishing branch [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) or the marginal case $\alpha=0$ need care: a finite jump is compatible with this power counting, while a logarithmic singularity requires a logarithmic refinement of the assumed homogeneous form.
