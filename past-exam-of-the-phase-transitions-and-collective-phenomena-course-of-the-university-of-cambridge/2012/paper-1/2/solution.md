↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

[Spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) means that a [pure thermodynamic phase](../../../../pure-thermodynamic-phase.md) is less symmetric than the [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) even when the selecting external field is removed. For example,

$$
\mathbf m_0=\lim_{\mathbf h\to0^+}\lim_{V\to\infty}\langle\mathbf m\rangle_{V,\mathbf h}\ne0.
$$

The direction of the infinitesimal field is held fixed in this limit. Reversing the limits in a finite symmetric sample normally gives zero. For the [XY model](../../../../xy-model.md), an ordered state chooses one direction from a circle of energetically equivalent directions.

A [Goldstone mode](../../../../goldstone-boson.md) is a long-wavelength fluctuation along this continuously degenerate family. A uniform angle rotation costs no [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); a slowly varying one costs only [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). In the stated static phase-only model the quadratic kernel is $\bar K q^2$, tending to zero as $q\to0$. **There is one independent soft [order-parameter phase](../../../../order-parameter-phase.md) coordinate for broken $O(2)$ rotation.** The static [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) does not specify a dynamical dispersion $\omega(q)$; that requires a dynamical law. Magnitude fluctuations instead have a restoring local curvature and have been excluded from this low-energy model.
