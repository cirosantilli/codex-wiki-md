# Geometric logic

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

The fragment uses finite conjunction, set-indexed disjunction and existential quantification, with equality and atomic relations. It is preserved under [inverse image functors of geometric morphisms](inverse-image-functor-of-a-geometric-morphism.md). The universal closure of an axiom is expressed by its variable context, not an unrestricted universal-quantifier constructor.
