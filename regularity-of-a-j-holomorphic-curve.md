# Regularity of a J-holomorphic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy–Riemann operator](cauchy-riemann-operator.md)

A [J-holomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md) is regular when the linearized [Cauchy–Riemann operator](cauchy-riemann-operator.md) is [surjective](surjective-function.md). Regularity only for simple curves is weaker than regularity for all maps: multiple covers of an exceptional sphere can fail regularity even when that simple sphere is regular.
