# Topological current

↑ **Parent:** [Topological charge](topological-charge.md)

A local current whose conservation follows from field geometry rather than requiring the field equation. For [Skyrmions](skyrmion.md), pulling back the closed volume form on the target three-sphere gives the current associated with [topological baryon number in the Skyrme model](topological-baryon-number-in-the-skyrme-model.md). Its spatial integral is a [topological charge](topological-charge.md), unchanged by smooth evolution with the prescribed boundary behaviour.
