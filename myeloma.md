# Myeloma

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)

A [myeloma](myeloma.md) is a malignancy of the plasma-cell lineage. Selected cultured myeloma lines provide an immortal fusion partner in hybridoma production; their metabolic selection properties depend on the particular line.
