# Salt rejection

↑ **Parent:** [Saline Stefan problem](saline-stefan-problem.md)

When salt-free [ice](ice.md) forms from a saline liquid, the moving [phase boundary](phase-boundary.md) excludes the dissolved salt. With equal phase [mass densities](density.md), no bulk flow, liquid salt [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$, and interface [concentration](concentration.md) $C_i$, salt conservation gives the displayed condition, where $x$ increases from solid to liquid. Positive freezing speed $\dot a$ therefore requires a negative liquid [concentration](concentration.md) [gradient](gradient.md). During melting the same condition gives dilution. Coupling [salt rejection](salt-rejection.md), the [liquidus](liquidus.md), and the [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) produces the [saline Stefan problem](saline-stefan-problem.md).
