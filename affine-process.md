# Affine process

↑ **Parent:** [Markov process](markov-process-split.md)

An affine process is a time-homogeneous [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) whose conditional [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is exponential-affine in its starting state. In discrete time one writes $\log\mathbb E_x e^{\theta X_1}=A(\theta)x+B(\theta)$ on the finite [exponential moment](exponential-moment.md) domain. Continuous-time definitions allow the functions to depend also on elapsed time.
