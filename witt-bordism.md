# Witt bordism

↑ **Parent:** [Cobordism](cobordism.md)

Two closed oriented rational [Witt spaces](witt-space.md) are Witt bordant if their signed disjoint union is the collared boundary of a compact oriented [Witt space with collared boundary](witt-space-with-collared-boundary.md). Disjoint union gives the bordism group $\Omega_n^{\mathrm{Witt}}$.
