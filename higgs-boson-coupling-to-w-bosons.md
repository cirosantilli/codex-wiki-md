# Higgs boson coupling to W bosons

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md)

Expanding the [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md) kinetic term about its [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) gives $\mathcal L\supset(2m_W^2/v)hW_\mu^+W^{-\mu}$. Its vertex is proportional to $g_{\mu\nu}$, so scalar exchange contracts the external [particle polarization](particle-polarization.md) vectors.
