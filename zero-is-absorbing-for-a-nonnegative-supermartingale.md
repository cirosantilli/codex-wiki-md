# Zero is absorbing for a nonnegative supermartingale

↑ **Parent:** [Supermartingale](supermartingale.md)

If a nonnegative [supermartingale](supermartingale.md) reaches zero at a discrete [stopping time](stopping-time.md), its later nonnegative values have conditional [expectation](expected-value.md) at most zero and must also be zero [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md).
