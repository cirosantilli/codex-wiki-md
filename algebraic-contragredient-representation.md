# Algebraic contragredient representation

↑ **Parent:** [Dual representation](dual-representation.md)

The algebraic contragredient acts on the entire algebraic [dual space](dual-space.md). It need not be a [smooth representation](smooth-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md), even if the original representation is smooth. Taking the inverse in this formula is necessary to obtain a group action, and inverts a one-dimensional [linear character](linear-character.md).
