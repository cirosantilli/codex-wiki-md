# Trifurcation vertex in percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Uniqueness of the infinite percolation cluster](uniqueness-of-the-infinite-percolation-cluster.md)

A trifurcation [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) is a [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) of an infinite [percolation cluster](percolation-cluster.md) whose deletion separates that cluster into at least three infinite [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md). In a finite modification argument one may make it have exactly three open incident [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) leading to three different infinite components. On a translation-invariant lattice, a positive [probability](probability.md) of trifurcation at one [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) gives a positive expected density of trifurcations.
