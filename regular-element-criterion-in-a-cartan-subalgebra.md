# Regular element criterion in a Cartan subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Regular element of a semisimple Lie algebra](regular-element-of-a-semisimple-lie-algebra.md)

For $t$ in a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak t$, the [root-space decomposition](root-space-decomposition.md) gives

$$
\mathfrak z_{\mathfrak g}(t)=\mathfrak t\oplus\bigoplus_{\alpha(t)=0}\mathfrak g_\alpha.
$$

Thus $t$ is regular exactly when $\alpha(t)\ne0$ for every root $\alpha$.
