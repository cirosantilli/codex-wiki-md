# Independence of the Abelian group axioms

↑ **Parent:** [Independent axiom set](independent-axiom-set.md)

The usual associative, identity, two-sided inverse and [commutativity](commutativity.md) [axioms](axiom.md) form an [independent axiom set](independent-axiom-set.md). A three-element commutative unital operation with self-inverses can fail [associativity](associative-property.md); a constant-zero operation can satisfy the other three [axioms](axiom.md) while failing the identity condition; natural-number [addition](addition.md) fails the inverse condition; and a noncommutative [group](group-split.md) fails [commutativity](commutativity.md). Each example must be checked against all three retained [axioms](axiom.md).
