# Brownian occupation time

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

The occupation time of a measurable set $A$ through time $t$ is $\int_0^t\mathbf1_{\{B_s\in A\}}ds$. The [occupation-times formula](occupation-times-formula.md) expresses such integrals through [local time of a semimartingale](local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md). Recurrence and the [Strong Markov property](strong-markov-property.md) imply that one-dimensional Brownian motion spends an unbounded total time in every nonempty open interval.
