# Inverting RC high-pass amplifier

↑ **Parent:** [Inverting amplifier](inverting-amplifier.md)

An ideal [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md) with a series input [resistor](resistor.md) and [capacitor](capacitor.md), resistive feedback and a grounded noninverting terminal realizes the displayed [high-pass filter](high-pass-filter.md). [Virtual ground](virtual-ground.md) and [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) give $H=-R_f/[R_i+1/(i\omega C)]$. Its [phase](phase-waves.md) is $-\pi/2-\tan^{-1}(\omega R_iC)$ in the $e^{i\omega t}$ convention. Equal resistances do not make its [phase](phase-waves.md) [frequency](frequency.md) independent.
