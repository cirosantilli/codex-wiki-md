# Euclidean de Sitter action and entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Static patch of de Sitter spacetime](static-patch-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

In units $G=\hbar=c=k_B=1$, a smooth [Wick rotation](wick-rotation.md) of the four-dimensional [static patch of de Sitter spacetime](static-patch-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md) has Euclidean time period $2\pi\sqrt{3/\Lambda}$. Its compact geometry is a round four-sphere of radius $\sqrt{3/\Lambda}$. The [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) give $R=4\Lambda$, and direct integration gives volume $24\pi^2/\Lambda^2$, hence gravitational [Euclidean action](euclidean-action.md) $I_E=-3\pi/\Lambda$. With zero assigned internal energy, $I_E=\beta E-S$ gives [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md) $S=3\pi/\Lambda=A/4$.
