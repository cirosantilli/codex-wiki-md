# Holomorphic cubic differential

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md)

A section of the cube of the [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md), locally $c=\phi(z)\,dz^3$ with [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) coefficient. The [flat coordinates](natural-coordinate-of-a-holomorphic-differential.md) obtained by integrating cube roots have transitions $w\mapsto\zeta w+b$, $\zeta^3=1$; general real-linear deformations do not preserve these as [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) transitions.
