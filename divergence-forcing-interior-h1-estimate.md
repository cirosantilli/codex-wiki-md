# Divergence-forcing interior H1 estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md)

For $\Delta v=\operatorname{div}F+G$ and $U\subset\subset V$, a [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) energy estimate gives $\|v\|_{H^1(U)}\leq C(\|v\|_{L^2(V)}+\|F\|_{L^2(V)}+\|G\|_{L^2(V)})$. One derivative of forcing is permitted because [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) places it on the [test function](test-function.md). This estimate allows a mollified distributional solution initially in $L^2$ to gain its first [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md).
