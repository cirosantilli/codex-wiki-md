# Sugawara construction

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugawara_construction)

For a restricted representation of an affine current algebra with $[J_m^a,J_n^b]=if^{abc}J_{m+n}^c+km\delta^{ab}\delta_{m+n,0}$ and $f^{abc}f^{abd}=Q\delta^{cd}$, the displayed [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) construction is defined when $2k+Q\ne0$. Moving the currents past each other gives $[L_m,J_n^a]=-nJ_{m+n}^a$; the additional $Q$ in the denominator is the adjoint-Casimir reordering term. The resulting [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md) has [central charge](central-charge.md) $c=k\dim\mathfrak g/(k+Q/2)$. On a compact unitary positive-energy module, $L_m^\dagger=L_{-m}$.
