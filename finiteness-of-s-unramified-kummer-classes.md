# Finiteness of S-unramified Kummer classes

↑ **Parent:** [S-unramified power class group](s-unramified-power-class-group.md)

The [S-unramified power class group](s-unramified-power-class-group.md) of a [number field](number-field.md) is finite. For $[a]\in K(S,n)$, the fractional ideal of $a$ away from $S$ is an $n$th power $\mathfrak a^n$. Sending $[a]$ to $[\mathfrak a]$ gives an exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{K,S}^{\times}/(\mathcal O_{K,S}^{\times})^n\longrightarrow K(S,n)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_{K,S})[n]\longrightarrow0.
$$

The [S-unit group](s-unit-group.md) is finitely generated, and the localized [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md) is a quotient of the finite ordinary ideal class group. Both end groups are finite. This is the arithmetic finiteness needed in the [Kummer-theoretic proof of the weak Mordell-Weil theorem](kummer-theoretic-proof-of-the-weak-mordell-weil-theorem.md).
