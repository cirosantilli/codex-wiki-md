# Fundamental theorem of Riemannian geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md)

A [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) determines a unique [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md) that is a [metric connection](metric-connection.md). Metric compatibility and the torsion identity imply the [Koszul formula](koszul-formula.md), which proves uniqueness. Conversely the right-hand side of the [Koszul formula](koszul-formula.md) is a smooth one-form in its last vector-field argument. The [musical isomorphism](musical-isomorphism.md) defines $D_XY$ from it; expanding the [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) verifies the connection rules, metric compatibility and zero torsion. Thus it constructs the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) intrinsically.
