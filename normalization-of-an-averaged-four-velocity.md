# Normalization of an averaged four-velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Four-velocity](four-velocity.md)

The average of future-directed normalized [four-velocities](four-velocity.md) is generally timelike but is not normalized. For an isotropic ensemble with common [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma$, the mean is $(\gamma c,0,0,0)$ and has squared norm $-\gamma^2c^2$. A normalized bulk velocity instead equals $c\langle v\rangle/\sqrt{-g(\langle v\rangle,\langle v\rangle)}$. In a [perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) its rest-frame energy eigenvector is the natural bulk choice; the rest-frame [energy density](energy-density.md) includes random kinetic energy.
