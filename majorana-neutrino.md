# Majorana neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)

A [Majorana neutrino](majorana-neutrino.md) is its own [antiparticle](antiparticle.md). A corresponding mass term can violate conventional [lepton number](lepton-number.md) by two. Observing a wrong-helicity charged-current process is nevertheless suppressed by the very small [neutrino mass](neutrino-mass.md) relative to its energy, rather than having the ordinary charge-conjugate branching fraction.
