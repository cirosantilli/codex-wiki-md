# Kalb-Ramond field strength

↑ **Parent:** [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md)

The three-form field strength of the [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md) is $H=dB$. Its components are $H_{mnp}=\partial_mB_{np}+\partial_nB_{pm}+\partial_pB_{mn}$. In flat spacetime the free equation is $\partial^mH_{mnp}=0$.
