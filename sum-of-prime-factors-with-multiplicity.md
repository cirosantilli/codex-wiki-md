# Sum of prime factors with multiplicity

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental theorem of arithmetic](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md)

For an [integer](integer-split.md) $n>1$ with [prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) $n=\prod_{j=1}^r p_j^{e_j}$, this is the sum of its [prime factors](prime-factor.md) with each repeated $e_j$ times. For example, $s(18)=2+3+3=8$. It differs from the sum of distinct [prime factors](prime-factor.md).
