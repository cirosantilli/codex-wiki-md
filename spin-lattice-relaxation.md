# Spin-lattice relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Spin relaxation](spin-relaxation.md)

Exchange of [energy](energy-split.md) between magnetic [spin](spin.md) degrees of freedom and the surrounding lattice, allowing their [temperatures](temperature-split.md) to approach [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md). It permits [adiabatic demagnetization](adiabatic-demagnetization.md) to cool a solid and its attached load.
