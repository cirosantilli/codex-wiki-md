# Fluorescence emission spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md)

A [fluorescence emission spectrum](fluorescence-emission-spectrum.md) resolves emitted [photons](photon.md) by [wavelength](wavelength.md) at fixed excitation settings. Subtract a matched sample-container and solvent background, and correct the wavelength-dependent throughput and [photodetector](photodetector.md) sensitivity when estimating the emitted distribution. A camera image through one [fluorescence emission filter](fluorescence-emission-filter.md) instead measures a weighted band [integral](integral-split.md), not a whole [fluorescence emission spectrum](fluorescence-emission-spectrum.md). A [fluorescence lifetime](fluorescence-lifetime.md) resolves delay after excitation; its horizontal axis is [time](time-in-physics.md), not [wavelength](wavelength.md).
