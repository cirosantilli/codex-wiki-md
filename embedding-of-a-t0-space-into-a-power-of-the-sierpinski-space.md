<h1 id="embedding-of-a-t0-space-into-a-power-of-the-sierpinski-space">Embedding of a T0 space into a power of the Sierpiński space</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sierpiński space](sierpinski-space.md)

For a [T0 space](kolmogorov-space.md) $X$, evaluation against all continuous maps $g:X\to S$ is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) onto its image under the map

$$
x\longmapsto(g(x))_g
$$

into a power of the [Sierpiński space](sierpinski-space.md). The $T_0$ axiom makes it injective, and the coordinate inverse images of $\{1\}$ recover every open subset of $X$.
