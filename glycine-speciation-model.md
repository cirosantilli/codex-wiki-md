# Glycine speciation model

↑ **Parent:** [Glycine protonation](glycine-protonation.md)

In an ideal aqueous [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), set $h=[\mathrm H_3\mathrm O^+]/c^\circ=10^{-\mathrm{pH}}$, with $c^\circ=1\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$, and approximate [activity coefficients](activity-coefficient.md) by one. Put $K_1=10^{-2.35}$ and $K_2=10^{-9.78}$ for the stipulated [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) [acid dissociation constants](acid-dissociation-constant.md). The [Henderson-Hasselbalch equation](henderson-hasselbalch-equation.md) gives the adjacent-state [ratios](ratio.md)

$$
\frac{[G^0]}{[G^+]}=\frac{K_1}{h},\qquad\frac{[G^-]}{[G^0]}=\frac{K_2}{h}.
$$

With [analytical concentration](analytical-concentration.md) $C_T=[G^+]+[G^0]+[G^-]$, normalization gives the three fractions and mean [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) in units of the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md):

$$
D=h^2+K_1h+K_1K_2,\qquad
\alpha_+=\frac{h^2}{D},\quad\alpha_0=\frac{K_1h}{D},\quad\alpha_-=\frac{K_1K_2}{D},\qquad
\bar z=\alpha_+-\alpha_-.
$$

All three fractions are nonnegative and sum to one. At [pH](ph.md) $7$, the model gives about $99.832\%$ [zwitterion](zwitterion.md), $0.00224\%$ [cation](cation.md) and $0.1657\%$ [anion](anion.md); $\bar z\approx-0.00163$. Predominantly [zwitterionic](zwitterion.md) therefore need not mean exactly at the [isoelectric point](isoelectric-point.md).

Setting $\bar z=0$ requires $h^2=K_1K_2$. Taking the [logarithm](logarithm.md) yields

$$
\mathrm{pI}=\frac{\mathrm pK_{a1}+\mathrm pK_{a2}}2=\frac{2.35+9.78}2=6.065.
$$

At this model [isoelectric point](isoelectric-point.md), $\alpha_+=\alpha_-\approx0.0001927$ and $\alpha_0\approx0.9996146$: about $0.0193\%$ of each charged form remains. At either [pKa](pka.md), the relevant adjacent fractions are equal, but the third fraction is not mathematically zero. This three-state model neglects the minor uncharged form $\mathrm{H_2N{-}CH_2{-}COOH}$; macroscopic [pKa](pka.md) values alone do not determine its fraction separately from the [zwitterion](zwitterion.md).
