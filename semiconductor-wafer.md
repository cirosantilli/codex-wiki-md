# Semiconductor wafer

↑ **Parent:** [Integrated circuit](integrated-circuit.md)

A thin piece of [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) processed to form devices or [integrated circuits](integrated-circuit.md), often many [semiconductor dies](semiconductor-die.md) together. Dividing wafer [area](surface-area.md) by die [area](surface-area.md) gives an ideal count before edge losses and spaces used to separate [semiconductor dies](semiconductor-die.md).
