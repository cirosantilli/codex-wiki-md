# Bessel density modes of a polytropic expanding universe

↑ **Parent:** [Linear density modes with polytropic pressure](linear-density-modes-with-polytropic-pressure.md)

For a flat Newtonian expanding background $R\propto t^{2/3}$ with [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md) $p=K\rho^\gamma$, a comoving [density contrast](density-contrast.md) satisfies $\ddot\delta+4\dot\delta/(3t)+[\Lambda^2t^{-2\nu-2}-2/(3t^2)]\delta=0$. Here $\Lambda=kc(t_0)t_0^{\gamma-1/3}$ and $\nu=\gamma-4/3$. For $\nu>0$, substitution $z=\Lambda/(\nu t^\nu)$ and $\delta=t^{-1/6}y(z)$ gives the [Bessel equation](bessel-differential-equation.md) of order $\lambda$. A universally valid basis uses the [Bessel function of the first kind](bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md) and [Bessel function of the second kind](bessel-function-of-the-second-kind.md), $t^{-1/6}J_\lambda(z)$ and $t^{-1/6}Y_\lambda(z)$. When $\lambda$ is not an integer, $J_{-\lambda}$ can replace $Y_\lambda$. For $4/3<\gamma<5/3$, the early oscillatory envelope is $t^{(3\gamma-5)/6}$; late modes have leading powers $t^{2/3}$ and $t^{-1}$. Late growth requires a nonzero coefficient of the growing branch.
