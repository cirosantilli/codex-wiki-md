# Categorical diagonal

↑ **Parent:** [Product (category theory)](product-category-theory.md)

For a binary [product in a category](product-category-theory.md), the diagonal $\Delta_A:A\to A\times A$ is the unique map whose two projection composites are $1_A$. It is a [split monomorphism](split-monomorphism.md), since either projection is a left inverse. The diagonal converts equality of two maps into a lifting problem: $\langle u,v\rangle$ factors through $\Delta_A$ exactly when $u=v$.
