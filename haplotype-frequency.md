# Haplotype frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Haplotype](haplotype.md)

A [haplotype frequency](haplotype-frequency.md) is the proportion of chromosome copies carrying specified [alleles](allele.md) together across chosen [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md). Its [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md) give [allele frequencies](allele-frequency.md); its departures from products of those frequencies are [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md).
