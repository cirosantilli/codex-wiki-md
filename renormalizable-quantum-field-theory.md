# Renormalizable quantum field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization](renormalization.md)

A renormalizable [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md) permits ultraviolet divergences to be absorbed into a fixed finite set of parameters and field normalizations. In ordinary four-dimensional perturbative [power counting in quantum field theory](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md), its interaction coefficients have nonnegative [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md).
