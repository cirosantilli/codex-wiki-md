# Quark doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Model representation](standard-model-representation.md)

In one [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) generation, the left-handed up and down [quark fields](quark-field.md) form a weak $SU(2)$ doublet of [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) $1/6$. Each weak component has three colours. The same field therefore counts as three doublets for weak gauge-loop contributions and two colour fundamentals for strong gauge-loop contributions. Right-handed up and down quarks are separate weak singlets.
