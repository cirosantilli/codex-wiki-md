# Contour potential for an anti-self-dual Maxwell field

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian Ward splitting by Laurent series](abelian-ward-splitting-by-laurent-series.md)

The [Abelian Ward splitting by Laurent series](abelian-ward-splitting-by-laurent-series.md) gives this [gauge potential](gauge-field.md) through the [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md), with $f_{,B}=\partial f/\partial\omega^B$ evaluated on the [twistor line](twistor-line.md). The integrand has total projective degree zero. Holding the contour fixed locally and differentiating gives $F_{AA'BB'}=\epsilon_{A'B'}(2\pi i)^{-1}\oint_\Gamma f_{,AB}\,\pi_{D'}d\pi^{D'}$, using $\pi_{A'}\iota_{B'}-\pi_{B'}\iota_{A'}=\epsilon_{A'B'}(\iota_{C'}\pi^{C'})$. Its unprimed [two-component spinor](two-component-spinor.md) is symmetric, so the primed [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) vanishes and the field obeys the [anti-self-dual Maxwell equations](anti-self-dual-maxwell-equations.md). These signs use $\epsilon_{0'1'}=1$, $\epsilon^{0'1'}=-1$ and raising by left multiplication; other consistent raising conventions can reverse both displayed overall signs.
