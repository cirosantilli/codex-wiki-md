# Order-one growth of the Riemann xi function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann xi function](riemann-xi-function.md)

The [pole-subtracted theta integral for the completed zeta function](pole-subtracted-theta-integral-for-the-completed-zeta-function.md) bounds the [Riemann xi function](riemann-xi-function.md) on $|s|\leq R$ by a polynomial in $R$ times $\int_1^\infty e^{-\pi t}t^{(R+1)/2}\,dt$. The [Stirling formula](stirling-formula.md) gives logarithmic growth $O(R\log R)$. On the positive real axis, the definition and $\zeta(R)\geq1$ give $\log\xi(R)=\frac R2\log R+O(R)$. Thus its [order of an entire function](order-of-an-entire-function.md) is exactly one.
