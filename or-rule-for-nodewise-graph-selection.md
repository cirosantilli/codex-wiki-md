# OR rule for nodewise graph selection

↑ **Parent:** [Nodewise regression](nodewise-regression.md)

The union rule retains an edge whenever either of the two [nodewise regression](nodewise-regression.md) fits selects the other variable. It is more inclusive than the [AND rule for nodewise graph selection](and-rule-for-nodewise-graph-selection.md) and makes an undirected edge decision from potentially asymmetric estimated neighbourhoods.
