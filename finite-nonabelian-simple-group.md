# Finite nonabelian simple group

↑ **Parent:** [Simple group](simple-group.md)

A finite nonabelian simple group is a [finite group](finite-group.md) that is a [nonabelian group](non-abelian-group.md) and a [simple group](simple-group.md). Every such group is a [perfect group](perfect-group.md) because its [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is a nontrivial normal subgroup.
