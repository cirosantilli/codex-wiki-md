# Diatomic-chain dispersion relation with equal springs

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon dispersion relation](phonon-dispersion-relation.md)

For alternating [masses](mass.md) $m,M$ at spacing $d$, equal nearest-neighbour [spring constants](spring-constant.md) $K$, and cell length $2d$, a two-component [normal mode](normal-mode.md) gives

$$
\omega_\pm^2=K\left(\frac1m+\frac1M\right)\pm K\sqrt{\left(\frac1m+\frac1M\right)^2-\frac4{mM}\sin^2(kd)},\qquad |k|\leq\frac\pi{2d}.
$$

The minus sign is the [acoustic phonon branch](acoustic-phonon-branch.md) and the plus sign is the [optical phonon branch](optical-phonon-branch.md). For $M>m$ the edge frequencies are $\sqrt{2K/M}$ and $\sqrt{2K/m}$, leaving a gap. At $k=0$ they are zero and $\sqrt{2K(1/m+1/M)}$.
