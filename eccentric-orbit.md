# Eccentric orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md)

An orbit with nonzero [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md). A bound [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) with $0<e<1$ is a noncircular [ellipse](ellipse.md); $e=1$ and $e>1$ describe the parabolic and hyperbolic unbound cases. A [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) fixed by [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md) need not equal the time average of the orbital radius.
