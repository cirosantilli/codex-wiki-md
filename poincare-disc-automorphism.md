# Poincare disc automorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré disk model](poincare-disk-model.md)

For $|a|<1$, the [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $f_a(z)=(z-a)/(1-\overline az)$ is an [isometry](isometry.md) of the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md) sending $a$ to zero. Rotations combined with these transformations describe its orientation-preserving [isometries](isometry.md).
