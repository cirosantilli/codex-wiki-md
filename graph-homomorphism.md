# Graph homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_homomorphism)

A graph homomorphism from $H$ to $G$ is a [function](function-split.md) $f:V(H)\to V(G)$ that sends every edge $uv$ of $H$ to an edge $f(u)f(v)$ of $G$.
