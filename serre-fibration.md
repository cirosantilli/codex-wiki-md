# Serre fibration

↑ **Parent:** [Serre spectral sequence](serre-spectral-sequence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serre_fibration)

A Serre fibration is a map having the [homotopy lifting property](homotopy-lifting-property.md) with respect to every disc, equivalently every CW complex.
