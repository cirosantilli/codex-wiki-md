<h1 id="perron-s-formula">Perron's formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perron's_formula)

Perron's formula recovers a summatory arithmetic function from its [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md) by the inverse Mellin integral

$$
\sum_{n\leq x}a_n
=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{c-i\infty}^{c+i\infty}
\left(\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{a_n}{n^s}\right)\frac{x^s}{s}\,ds,
$$

with the usual convergence and endpoint conventions.
