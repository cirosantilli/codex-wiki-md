# Alexander module of a knot

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander polynomial](alexander-polynomial.md)

The Alexander module is the first integral homology of the [infinite cyclic cover of a knot exterior](infinite-cyclic-cover-of-a-knot-exterior.md). Its deck transformation makes it a module over $\mathbb Z[t^{\pm1}]$, and its first elementary ideal determines the [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md).
