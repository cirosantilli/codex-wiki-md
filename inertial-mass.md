# Inertial mass

↑ **Parent:** [Mass](mass.md)

In [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics-split.md), inertial [mass](mass.md) is the coefficient in $\mathbf F=m_{\rm i}\mathbf a$, measuring a body's response to a net [force](force-split.md). The [weak equivalence principle](weak-equivalence-principle.md) makes its ratio to passive [gravitational mass](gravitational-mass.md) universal; choosing compatible units then sets the two equal.
