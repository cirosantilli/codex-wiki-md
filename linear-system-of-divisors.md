# Linear system of divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Weil divisor](weil-divisor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_system_of_divisors)

A [linear system of divisors](linear-system-of-divisors.md) is a projective family of effective [Weil divisors](weil-divisor.md) linearly equivalent to a fixed divisor. A [complete linear system of a divisor](complete-linear-system-of-a-divisor.md) contains every effective representative of that class; a projective subspace gives a subsystem. For a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md), the family is obtained from global sections of its associated [line bundle](line-bundle.md). The [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) case recovers the usual moving family of effective divisors.
