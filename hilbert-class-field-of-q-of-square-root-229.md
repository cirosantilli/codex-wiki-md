# Hilbert class field of Q of square root 229

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md)

The polynomial is irreducible with [discriminant](discriminant.md) $229$, so its splitting field has [Galois group](galois-group.md) $S_3$ and quadratic subfield $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{229})$. The splitting field is cyclic cubic over that subfield. It is unramified outside 229, and at 229 its inertia is a transposition, meeting the alternating cubic subgroup trivially. All roots are real. It is therefore everywhere [unramified](unramified-extension.md) over the quadratic field. The [ideal class group of Q of square root 229](ideal-class-group-of-q-of-square-root-229.md) has order three, so this extension is exactly the [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md).
