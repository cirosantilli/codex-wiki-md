<h1 id="cochran-s-q-statistic">Cochran's Q statistic</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Between-study heterogeneity](between-study-heterogeneity.md)

With fixed inverse-variance weights and weighted mean $\widehat\mu$, $Q=\sum_iw_i(y_i-\widehat\mu)^2$. Under a common-effect normal model with $m$ independent estimates and known variances it has a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $m-1$ degrees of freedom. Estimated variances make this calibration approximate.
