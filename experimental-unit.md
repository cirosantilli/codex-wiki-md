# Experimental unit

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments-split.md)

An [experimental unit](experimental-unit.md) is a unit to which a [treatment](treatment.md) is assigned under the experimental allocation. A whole orchard can be an [experimental unit](experimental-unit.md) even when responses are recorded separately on its trees. Different factors in a [split-plot design](split-plot-design.md) can have different [experimental units](experimental-unit.md).
