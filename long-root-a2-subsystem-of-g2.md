# Long-root A2 subsystem of G2

↑ **Parent:** [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md)

For short and long simple roots $\alpha,\beta$ of $G_2$, the long roots form an $A_2$ root system with simple roots $\delta_1=\beta$ and $\delta_2=3\alpha+\beta$. If $\omega_1,\omega_2$ are the corresponding $A_2$ [fundamental weights](fundamental-weight.md), then $\alpha=\omega_2-\omega_1$ and $\alpha^\vee=\delta_2^\vee-\delta_1^\vee$.
