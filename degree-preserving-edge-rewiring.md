# Degree-preserving edge rewiring

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

[Degree-preserving edge rewiring](degree-preserving-edge-rewiring.md) replaces two [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) $(u,v)$ and $(x,y)$ by $(u,y)$ and $(x,v)$, preserving every endpoint's [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md). On [simple graphs](simple-graph.md), reject proposed [self-loops](loop-graph-theory.md) and duplicate edges. Symmetric random proposals with rejected moves retained preserve a uniform distribution on the reachable realizations; adequate mixing remains a separate requirement. Connectivity can change unless explicitly constrained.
