# Boundary Rossby wave

↑ **Parent:** [Eady model](eady-model.md)

A boundary Rossby wave is supported by a [buoyancy perturbation](buoyancy-perturbation.md) or potential-temperature anomaly on a rigid horizontal boundary. Its intrinsic propagation depends on the boundary orientation and basic buoyancy gradient; the upper and lower boundary waves of the [Eady model](eady-model.md) propagate oppositely relative to their local background flows.
