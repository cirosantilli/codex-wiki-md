# Autism

↑ **Parent:** [Neurodevelopmental disorder](neurodevelopmental-disorder.md)

A heterogeneous neurodevelopmental condition involving characteristic differences in [social communication](social-communication.md) and restricted or repetitive patterns of behaviour or interests. Sensory differences and varying support needs matter; an [autistic](autism.md) individual’s [empathy](empathy.md), [language](language.md) or mental health cannot be inferred from the label alone.
