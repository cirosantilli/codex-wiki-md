# Monod chemostat equilibrium and relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemostat](chemostat.md)

For a dimensionless [chemostat](chemostat.md) with [Monod equation](monod-equation.md) kinetics,

$$
n'=\alpha\frac{cn}{1+c}-n,\qquad c'=\beta-c-\frac{cn}{1+c},
$$

the total nutrient equivalent $z=c+n/\alpha$ satisfies $z'=\beta-z$. The positive [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is $c_*=1/(\alpha-1)$, $n_*=\alpha(\beta-c_*)$, provided $\alpha>1$ and $\beta>c_*$. The [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $-1$ and $-n_* /(1+c_*)^2$, so the positive [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is [locally asymptotically stable](asymptotic-stability.md).
