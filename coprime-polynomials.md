# Coprime polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

Two [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) over a [field](field.md) are coprime if their only common factors are nonzero constants. Equivalently, their [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) in the [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md). In one variable this is equivalent to a [Bezout identity](bezout-identity.md) $Af+Bg=1$ with polynomial coefficients. Independent members of a pencil spanned by [coprime polynomials](coprime-polynomials.md) are themselves coprime.
