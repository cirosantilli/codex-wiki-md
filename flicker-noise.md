# Flicker noise

↑ **Parent:** [Noise (signal processing)](noise-signal-processing.md)

Low-frequency [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) with an approximately inverse-[frequency](frequency.md) [power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md) over a specified range. It can dominate the flat spectrum of a weakly confined mechanical coordinate or an electronic readout at low [frequencies](frequency.md). A $1/f$ law cannot hold with finite total [variance](variance-split.md) over unlimited frequency range, so its measured bandwidth and low-frequency cutoff matter. An observed excess alone does not uniquely identify the microscopic source.
