# Bell theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Foundations of quantum mechanics](foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_theorem)

Bell's theorem shows that no theory assigning outcomes through local hidden variables can reproduce every quantum correlation.

The force of the [Bell theorem](bell-theorem.md) is that a disagreement about a [local hidden-variable theory](local-hidden-variable-theory.md) becomes a constraint on frequencies in a [Bell test](bell-test.md). [Quantum entanglement](entangled-state.md) alone does not guarantee a violation for every [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md): the [quantum state](quantum-state.md), analyzer settings and treatment of missed events all matter. A violation of the [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md) tests the conjunction of [Bell local causality](bell-local-causality.md), [measurement independence](measurement-independence.md) and the stated outcome and sampling assumptions; it remains compatible with [quantum no-signalling](quantum-no-signalling.md).
