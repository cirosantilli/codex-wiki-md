# Sober space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sober_space)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is sober when every nonempty [irreducible closed subset](irreducible-closed-subset.md) is the closure of a unique point. Every [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) is sober. In contrast, an infinite set with the [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md) has the whole space irreducible and closed, while every singleton is closed, so it is not sober.
