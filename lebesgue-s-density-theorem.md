<h1 id="lebesgue-s-density-theorem">Lebesgue's density theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Measure density](measure-density.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue's_density_theorem)

For every [Lebesgue measurable set](lebesgue-measurable-set.md) $A\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, its [Lebesgue density](measure-density.md) is $1$ at almost every point of $A$ and $0$ at almost every point of its complement.
