# Treewidth

↑ **Parent:** [Tree decomposition](tree-decomposition.md)

The minimum width of a [tree decomposition](tree-decomposition.md). A [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md) has width at most one, while a [clique](clique-graph-theory.md) of $k$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) forces width at least $k-1$. Every pair of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in the [clique](clique-graph-theory.md) shares a bag; their connected bag [connected subgraphs of a tree](connected-subgraph-of-a-tree.md) have a common intersection by the [Helly property](helly-property.md) of connected [connected subgraphs of a tree](connected-subgraph-of-a-tree.md), placing the whole [clique](clique-graph-theory.md) in one bag. Small [treewidth](treewidth.md) is useful for [dynamic programming](dynamic-programming.md) on finite-domain [constraint satisfaction problems](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md).
