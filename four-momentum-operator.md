# Four-momentum operator

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum operator](momentum-operator.md)

The generators of spacetime translations in a [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md). With the mostly-minus [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md), $P\cdot a=Ha^0-\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf a$. A [momentum eigenstate](momentum-eigenstate.md) satisfies $P^\mu|p\rangle=p^\mu|p\rangle$, where $p^\mu$ is its [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). After subtracting the [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md), a translation-invariant [vacuum state](vacuum-state.md) has eigenvalue zero.
