# Product of exponentiable objects is exponentiable

↑ **Parent:** [Exponentiable object](exponentiable-object.md)

If $A$ and $B$ are [exponentiable objects](exponentiable-object.md), then

$$
-\times(A\times B)\cong(-\times A)\times B
$$

has the composite of their exponential functors as a right adjoint. The [terminal object](terminal-object.md) supplies the empty product, so exponentiable objects are closed under finite products.
