# Probability model

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)

A mathematical account of random outcomes using a [probability space](probability-space-split.md) and specified [random variables](random-variable-split.md). It determines [probabilities](probability.md) for events or observed data. A [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md) commonly supplies a family of such [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md) with unknown parameters; choosing a model and estimating its parameters are distinct tasks.
