# Degenerate-plasma dielectric response

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatic plasma wave](electrostatic-plasma-wave.md)

For an isotropic completely degenerate nonrelativistic [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md) in classical-trajectory collisionless [kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md), the parallel [marginal distribution](marginal-distribution.md) is $F(u)=3n(v_F^2-u^2)\mathbf1_{|u|<v_F}/(4v_F^3)$. Integrating $F'(u)/(u-\omega/k)$ yields the electron [dielectric function](dielectric-function.md) in the title for fixed ions. Its real roots outside the occupied interval have no [Landau damping](landau-damping.md). Quantum recoil, collisions and finite-temperature tails require an enlarged model.
