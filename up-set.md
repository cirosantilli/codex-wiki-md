# Up-set

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean lattice](boolean-lattice.md)

An up-set is a family $\mathcal U$ such that $A\in\mathcal U$ and $A\subseteq B$ imply $B\in\mathcal U$.

An up-set is upward closed under inclusion: if $A$ belongs and $A\subseteq B$, then $B$ belongs. This is the Boolean-lattice case of [upper and lower sets](upper-and-lower-sets.md).
