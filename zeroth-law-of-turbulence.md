# Zeroth law of turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Energy cascade](energy-cascade.md)

At fixed outer [velocity](velocity.md) and [integral scale of turbulence](integral-scale-of-turbulence.md), the mean [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) per unit mass approaches a positive finite value as the [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) becomes large. The dimensionless coefficient $C_\epsilon=\epsilon\ell/u^3$ is then of order one. This is a phenomenological property of established three-dimensional [turbulence](turbulence-split.md), not a consequence of taking [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) to zero in an arbitrary smooth flow.
