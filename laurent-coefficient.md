# Laurent coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent series](laurent-series.md)

A Laurent coefficient is a coefficient $a_n$ in the [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) $f(z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}a_n(z-z_0)^n$ of a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on an annulus. Integration around a circle in the annulus gives the displayed formula, including negative indices. The coefficient $a_{-1}$ is the [residue](residue.md).
