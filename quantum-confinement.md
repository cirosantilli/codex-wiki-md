# Quantum confinement

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

[Quantum confinement](quantum-confinement.md) restricts a particle's wavefunction over a length comparable with its characteristic quantum length scales, altering the allowed [energy levels](energy-level.md) relative to a bulk material. For a parabolic band confined over width $L$, a characteristic spacing is of order $\hbar^2/(m^*L^2)$, with boundary-dependent numerical factors. A [quantum dot](quantum-dot.md) confines all three directions; confinement in only one direction can leave continuous motion in the other two. Finite barrier height and interactions modify the simplest infinite-wall estimates.
