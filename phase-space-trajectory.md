# Phase-space trajectory

↑ **Parent:** [Phase space](phase-space.md)

An [integral curve of a vector field](integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) in phase space, representing the positions and momenta of a moving system. A periodic one-dimensional Hamiltonian orbit encloses the area used to define the [action variable](action-variable.md).
