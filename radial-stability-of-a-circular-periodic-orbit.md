# Radial stability of a circular periodic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital stability](orbital-stability.md)

For $\dot r=r(a-g(r))$, $\dot\theta=1$, a positive [root](root-of-a-polynomial.md) $a=g(r_0)$ gives a [circular periodic orbit](limit-cycle.md) of period $2\pi$. The radial [exponent](eigenvalue.md) is $-r_0g\prime(r_0)$, so $g\prime(r_0)>0$ gives [orbital asymptotic stability](orbital-stability.md) and $g\prime(r_0)<0$ gives [instability](lyapunov-stability.md). The phase direction is neutral; a [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) is not an [asymptotically stable](asymptotic-stability.md) individual point of the flow.
