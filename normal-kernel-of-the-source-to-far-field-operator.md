# Normal kernel of the source-to-far-field operator

↑ **Parent:** [Source-to-far-field operator at fixed frequency](source-to-far-field-operator-at-fixed-frequency.md)

The [adjoint source-to-far-field operator](adjoint-source-to-far-field-operator.md) composed with the [source-to-far-field operator at fixed frequency](source-to-far-field-operator-at-fixed-frequency.md) has volume kernel $K(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')=(4\pi)^{-1}\operatorname{sinc}(k|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|)$, where $\operatorname{sinc}(s)=\sin(s)/s$ and $\operatorname{sinc}(0)=1$. This follows from $\int_{S_1}e^{ik\boldsymbol\theta\cdot\mathbf d}dS=4\pi\operatorname{sinc}(k|\mathbf d|)$. Its smoothing action helps explain the unstable [normal equation for a linear inverse problem](normal-equation-for-a-linear-inverse-problem.md).
