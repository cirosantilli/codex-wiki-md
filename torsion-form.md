# Torsion form

↑ **Parent:** [Solder form](solder-form.md)

The torsion form of a connection on $TX$ is the covariant exterior derivative of the [solder form](solder-form.md). It satisfies

$$
T(U,V)=\nabla_UV-\nabla_VU-[U,V].
$$
