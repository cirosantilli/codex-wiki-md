# Risk-neutral measure for the Black-Scholes model

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md)

For $S_t=S_0e^{\mu t+\sigma W_t}$, shifting Brownian drift by

$$
\theta=\frac{\mu+\sigma^2/2-r}{\sigma}
$$

makes $S_t=S_0e^{(r-\sigma^2/2)t+\sigma W_t^Q}$ under an [equivalent martingale measure](risk-neutral-measure.md) $Q$. The discounted stock is then a [martingale](martingale-split.md).
