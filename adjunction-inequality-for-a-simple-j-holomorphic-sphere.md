# Adjunction inequality for a simple J-holomorphic sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Simple J-holomorphic sphere](simple-j-holomorphic-sphere.md)

The singular-curve adjunction formula in a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) of real dimension four gives $c_1(A)=A^2+2-2\delta$ for a [simple J-holomorphic sphere](simple-j-holomorphic-sphere.md), with a nonnegative singularity count $\delta$. Hence the displayed inequality holds. This is the version needed when the sphere need not be embedded.
