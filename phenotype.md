# Phenotype

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenotype)

A phenotype is an observable characteristic of an individual, arising from its [genotype](genotype.md), environment and other influences. In a [pedigree likelihood](pedigree-likelihood.md), the conditional phenotype probability is specified given the individual's underlying genotype.
