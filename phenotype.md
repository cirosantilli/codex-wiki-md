# Phenotype

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenotype)

A [phenotype](phenotype.md) is an observable characteristic of an [organism](organism-split.md), arising from its [genotype](genotype.md), environment and other influences. Seed shape is a [phenotype](phenotype.md); $Aa$ is a [genotype](genotype.md). The distinction prevents appearance from being mistaken for a complete inherited description. See [NHGRI: phenotype](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Phenotype).

For a specified binary [phenotype](phenotype.md) $T$, let its [penetrance](penetrance.md) in [genotype](genotype.md) $g$ be $r_g=P(T\mid g)$. The [law of total probability](law-of-total-probability.md) gives $P(T)=\sum_g r_gP(g)$. Thus the ideal $Aa\times Aa$ example has $P(T)=r_{AA}/4+r_{Aa}/2+r_{aa}/4$: the familiar $3/4$ follows only when these [penetrances](penetrance.md) are $1,1,0$. A [pedigree likelihood](pedigree-likelihood.md) keeps inheritance and the conditional [phenotype](phenotype.md) model as separate factors.
