# Born series

↑ **Parent:** [Born approximation for scalar wave scattering](born-approximation-for-scalar-wave-scattering.md)

The Born series is the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md) expansion of the [Lippmann-Schwinger equation](lippmann-schwinger-equation.md). Its terms describe successive orders of [wave scattering](wave-scattering.md); convergence is ensured, for example, by a scattering [operator norm](operator-norm.md) smaller than one.
