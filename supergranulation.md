# Supergranulation

↑ **Parent:** [Sun](sun.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergranulation)

A larger-scale horizontal cellular flow pattern on the [Sun](sun.md)'s surface. Transport by these flows helps arrange small magnetic elements into a [solar magnetic network](solar-magnetic-network.md) at cell boundaries. The observed pattern does not establish a unique thermal driving depth or an exact hierarchy of isolated steady eddies.
