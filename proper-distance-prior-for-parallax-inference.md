# Proper distance prior for parallax inference

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar parallax](stellar-parallax.md)

For a [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) measured [stellar parallax](stellar-parallax.md), the [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) at distance $r$ approaches a nonzero constant as $r\to\infty$. An unbounded spatial-volume [prior](prior-probability.md) proportional to $r^2$ therefore gives an improper [posterior](bayesian-posterior.md). A finite survey/population model or a decaying [density](density.md) is needed. The illustrative proper [prior](prior-probability.md) displayed has normalizing constant $1/(2L^3)$; its scale is a model choice requiring justification, not a new measured datum. [prior](prior-probability.md) sensitivity is especially important at low parallax signal-to-noise.
