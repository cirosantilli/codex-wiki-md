# Comoving observer

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving coordinate](comoving-coordinate.md)

A comoving observer has constant [comoving coordinates](comoving-coordinate.md) in a homogeneous expanding universe. Its physical separation from another comoving observer grows in proportion to the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md), giving relative recession governed by the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md). Light exchanged between these observers undergoes [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md).
