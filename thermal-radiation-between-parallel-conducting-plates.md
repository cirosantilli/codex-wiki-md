# Thermal radiation between parallel conducting plates

↑ **Parent:** [Black-body radiation](black-body-radiation.md)

Two wide perfect-conductor plates separated by $d$ support a gapless [transverse electromagnetic mode](transverse-electromagnetic-mode.md) whose [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is normal to the plates. At $\omega\ll c/d$, multiply the [single-polarization two-dimensional photon density of states](single-polarization-two-dimensional-photon-density-of-states.md) by $\hbar\omega$ and the [Bose-Einstein distribution](bose-einstein-distribution.md) to get the title's spectrum. This is a low-frequency statement; it need not describe the full thermal spectrum if thermally populated higher branches cross their cutoffs.
