# Ramond zero-mode Clifford algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md)

The zero-mode anticommutator $\{d_0^I,d_0^J\}_+=\delta^{IJ}$ becomes the Euclidean [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) after $\gamma^I=\sqrt2d_0^I$. It acts within the oscillator-vacuum space. Eight transverse Gamma matrices give a minimal complex representation of dimension sixteen, split into two eight-dimensional [chirality](chirality-physics.md) sectors.
