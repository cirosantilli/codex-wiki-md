# Ramification groups of the splitting field of T3 minus 3T plus 3 over Q3

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups](uniformizer-criterion-for-lower-ramification-groups.md)

For the splitting field $M/\mathbb Q_3$ of $T^3-3T+3$, the cubic is [Eisenstein](eisenstein-criterion.md) and its discriminant is $-135$. The quadratic subfield is $\mathbb Q_3(\sqrt3)$, and the degree-six extension is totally ramified. If $\alpha$ is a root and $s=\sqrt3$, then $\pi=\alpha^2/s$ is a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md). Transpositions have $v_M(\sigma\pi-\pi)=1$, while three-cycles have value $2$, giving the displayed [ramification groups](ramification-group.md). Its [different exponent from ramification groups](different-exponent-from-ramification-groups.md) is $7$.
