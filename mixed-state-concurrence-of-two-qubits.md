# Mixed-state concurrence of two qubits

↑ **Parent:** [Concurrence](concurrence.md)

For a normalized two-[qubit](qubit.md) [pure state](pure-state.md) with coefficient matrix $M$, [concurrence](concurrence.md) is $2|\det M|$. The displayed [convex roof extension](convex-roof-extension.md) defines its mixed-state version. Combining decompositions proves convexity: $C(\sum_jr_j\rho_j)\leq\sum_jr_jC(\rho_j)$. Every [separable quantum state](separable-quantum-state.md) has a decomposition into product vectors, each with zero determinant, so its concurrence is zero.
