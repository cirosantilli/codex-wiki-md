# Cyclic Sylow subgroup at the least prime gives a normal complement

↑ **Parent:** [Burnside transfer theorem](burnside-transfer-theorem.md)

Let $p$ be the smallest prime dividing the order of a [finite group](finite-group.md), and suppose its [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) $P$ is cyclic of order $p^a$. [Conjugation](conjugation.md) embeds $N_G(P)/C_G(P)$ into $\operatorname{Aut}(P)$, of order $p^{a-1}(p-1)$. Since $P\subseteq C_G(P)$, this quotient has order prime to $p$. Its remaining possible prime divisors are smaller than $p$ and cannot divide $|G|$. Thus $N_G(P)=C_G(P)$, and the [Burnside transfer theorem](burnside-transfer-theorem.md) gives a [normal p-complement](normal-p-complement.md).
