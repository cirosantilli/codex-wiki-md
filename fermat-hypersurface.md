# Fermat hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic equivalence of smooth projective hypersurfaces](symplectic-equivalence-of-smooth-projective-hypersurfaces.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_hypersurface)

The degree-$d$ Fermat hypersurface in $\mathbb{CP}^n$ is

$$
z_0^d+\cdots+z_n^d=0.
$$
