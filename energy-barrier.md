# Energy barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Energy landscape](energy-landscape.md)

An [energy barrier](energy-barrier.md) is an increase in [energy](energy-split.md) that must be overcome along an allowed route between states. Its value depends on which paths or single-step moves are allowed; specifying only the energies of the endpoints does not determine the barrier.
