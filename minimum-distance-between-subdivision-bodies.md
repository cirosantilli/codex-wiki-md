# Minimum distance between subdivision bodies

↑ **Parent:** [Subdivision surface interrogation](subdivision-surface-interrogation.md)

For disjoint compact bodies, the minimum occurs on their boundaries. Patch [bounding volumes](bounding-volume.md) give certified lower bounds and evaluated limit-point pairs give upper bounds. A [branch and bound](branch-and-bound.md) search refines promising patch pairs until the global bounds differ by the tolerance. Boundary distance alone cannot detect containment; touching, crossing or nested solid bodies have ordinary set distance zero. Negative overlap values require an additional convention such as [translational penetration depth](translational-penetration-depth.md).
