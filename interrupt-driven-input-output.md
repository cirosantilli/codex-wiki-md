# Interrupt-driven input-output

↑ **Parent:** [Interrupt](interrupt.md)

A device signals an [interrupt](interrupt.md) when it needs service, allowing the processor to perform unrelated work until then. Delivery, entry and completion have costs, so very high event rates can favor [polled input-output](polled-input-output.md) or a combined scheme.
