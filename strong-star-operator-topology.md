# Strong-star operator topology

↑ **Parent:** [Strong operator topology](strong-operator-topology.md)

This topology on [bounded operators](continuous-linear-operator.md) is generated by the seminorms $(\|T\xi\|^2+\|T^*\xi\|^2)^{1/2}$. A net converges exactly when both it and its adjoint net converge in the [strong operator topology](strong-operator-topology.md). It is the topology used in the full-unit-ball version of the [Kaplansky density theorem](kaplansky-density-theorem.md).
