# Autoradiography

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoradiography)

[Autoradiography](autoradiography.md) localizes a radioactive label through its effect on a recording medium. In [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md), developed photographic grains are compared with specimen structures; their positions have finite localization precision.
