# Solvable lambda term

↑ **Parent:** [Head normal form](head-normal-form.md)

A [lambda term](lambda-term.md) is solvable if its closure by [lambda abstractions](lambda-abstraction.md) over its [free variables](free-variable.md) can be applied to finitely many closed [lambda terms](lambda-term.md) to obtain $I=\lambda x.x$ in [beta equivalence](beta-equivalence.md). This is equivalent to having a [head normal form](head-normal-form.md). A head variable in a [head normal form](head-normal-form.md) can be replaced by a projection returning $I$; conversely [standardization theorem for beta reduction](standardization-theorem-for-beta-reduction.md) traces any successful head computation back to a head variable of the original term.
