# Antireflection coating

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film interference](thin-film-interference.md)

An optical coating designed to reduce reflection through refractive-index choice and [thin-film interference](thin-film-interference.md). A quarter-wave design is a single-wavelength approximation, not broadband perfection.
