<h1 id="fjortoft-theorem">Fjørtoft theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md)

For a smooth inviscid parallel shear profile with an exponentially growing mode, let $U_s$ be its velocity at an inflection point. The real and imaginary parts of the integrated [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md) imply $\int U''(U-U_s)|\psi|^2/|U-c|^2=-\int(|\psi'|^2+k^2|\psi|^2)<0$. Consequently $U''(U-U_s)<0$ somewhere in the channel. This strengthens [Rayleigh's inflection-point theorem](rayleigh-s-inflection-point-theorem.md) but is still only a necessary condition, not a sufficient instability criterion.
