# Irradiation void

↑ **Parent:** [Void swelling](void-swelling.md)

A vacancy-rich cavity generated under irradiation. Its growth is governed by the balance of [vacancy](vacancy.md) and interstitial fluxes and differs from a strongly gas-pressure-stabilised bubble.
