# Partial derivatives do not imply continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Partial derivative](partial-derivative.md)

Define $f(x,y)=xy/(x^2+y^2)$ away from the origin and zero at the origin. Both [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) exist everywhere and are zero at the origin, yet along $x=y$ the function is $1/2$, so it is not [continuous](continuous-function.md) there. Coordinate-axis derivative tests do not control all neighboring directions.
