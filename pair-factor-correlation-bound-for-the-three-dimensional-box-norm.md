# Pair-factor correlation bound for the three-dimensional box norm

↑ **Parent:** [Three-dimensional box norm](three-dimensional-box-norm.md)

For a real [function](function-split.md) $g$ on $X\times Y\times Z$ and [functions](function-split.md) $a(y,z),b(x,z),c(x,y)$ bounded in absolute value by one,

$$
|\mathbb E_{x,y,z}g(x,y,z)a(y,z)b(x,z)c(x,y)|\leq\|g\|_{\square^3}.
$$

First apply [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) in $(y,z)$ to remove $a$, duplicating $x$. Apply it in $(x_0,x_1,z)$ to remove $b(x_0,z)b(x_1,z)$, duplicating $y$. Finally apply it in $(x_0,x_1,y_0,y_1)$ to remove the four $c$ factors, duplicating $z$. The resulting eighth-power bound is exactly the eight-factor average defining the [three-dimensional box norm](three-dimensional-box-norm.md). [Functions](function-split.md) depending on just one coordinate, or constants, can be incorporated in any of the three pair factors.
