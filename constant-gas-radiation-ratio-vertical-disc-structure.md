# Constant gas-radiation ratio vertical disc structure

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical structure of an astrophysical disk](vertical-structure-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md)

For constant positive $\lambda=p_r/p_g$ and molecular gas constant $\mathcal R=k/(\mu_m m_p)$, the gas–radiation [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) gives $\rho=4\sigma T^3/(3c\lambda\mathcal R)$. Vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) in harmonic central gravity therefore gives $T=T_0(1-z^2/H^2)$ with $T_0=\Omega^2H^2/[8(1+\lambda)\mathcal R]$. The [mass density](density.md) and [pressure](pressure.md) powers are three and four. Integrating both faces yields $\Sigma=(32/35)\rho_0H$ and $\int p\,dz=\Sigma\Omega^2H^2/9$. With constant [opacity](opacity.md), [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) produces a linear flux and height-independent [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) $\mu=4c\lambda/[9\kappa(1+\lambda)]$. This is an interior formal profile; its low-density surface requires a photospheric treatment beyond diffusion.
