# Trace curvature transgression

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature difference formula](curvature-difference-formula.md)

The difference of two [connections on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) is a global endomorphism-valued one-form. Taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the [curvature difference formula](curvature-difference-formula.md) cancels both the commutator terms and the trace of the square of that one-form. Consequently the trace curvatures represent the same [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) class.
