# Transmitted power

↑ **Parent:** [Power transmission coefficient](power-transmission-coefficient.md)

The [energy flux](energy-flux.md) carried into a transmitted wave, integrated over the relevant cross section. The [power transmission coefficient](power-transmission-coefficient.md) compares this with incident [power](power.md). A displacement or field-amplitude [transmission coefficient](transmission-coefficient.md) must be converted with the appropriate wave impedances; its squared modulus alone need not be the power fraction. This distinction applies both to [electromagnetic waves](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) and transverse string waves.
