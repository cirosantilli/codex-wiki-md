# Ultraviolet pole of QED vacuum polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Photon vacuum polarization](photon-vacuum-polarization.md)

For one unit-charge [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) in $d=4-2\epsilon$, define the insertion as $i\Pi_{\mu\nu}=i(q^2g_{\mu\nu}-q_\mu q_\nu)\Pi$. The one-loop [ultraviolet pole](ultraviolet-pole.md) is $\Pi_{\rm UV}=-e^2/(12\pi^2\epsilon)$. The [Ward identity](ward-identity.md) cancels any apparent divergent vector [mass](mass.md) term. With photon [counterterm](counterterm.md) insertion $-i(q^2g_{\mu\nu}-q_\mu q_\nu)\delta_3$, this gives $\delta_3=-e^2/(12\pi^2\epsilon)$ in the [minimal subtraction scheme](minimal-subtraction-scheme.md).
