# Z-pinch

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical magnetostatic equilibrium](cylindrical-magnetostatic-equilibrium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z-pinch)

In a Z-pinch, an axial [electric current](electric-current.md) produces a [toroidal magnetic field](toroidal-magnetic-field.md) whose inward [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) confines a plasma. A cylindrical [magnetostatic equilibrium](magnetostatic-equilibrium.md) balances the resulting [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) against a [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md).
