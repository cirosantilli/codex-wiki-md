# Fluorescence anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md)

[fluorescence anisotropy](fluorescence-anisotropy.md) measures the polarization dependence of emitted [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) after polarized excitation. It can constrain rotational freedom on the fluorescence-lifetime timescale, but probe orientation, lifetime and partitioning also affect the signal.
