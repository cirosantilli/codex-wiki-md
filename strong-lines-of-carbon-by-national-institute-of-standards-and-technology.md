# Strong Lines of Carbon by National Institute of Standards and Technology

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by National Institute of Standards and Technology](work-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)

[https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/carbontable2.htm](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/carbontable2.htm)

The institutional [carbon](carbon.md) [spectral line](spectral-line.md) table explicitly separates vacuum and air [wavelengths](wavelength.md). Its $193.0906\,\mathrm{nm}$ C I line is a vacuum [wavelength](wavelength.md), while its $247.8561\,\mathrm{nm}$ C I line is an air [wavelength](wavelength.md). Their differing conventions matter when converting line positions into atomic [energy](energy-split.md) differences.
