# Amide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

Here an [amide](amide.md) means a [carbonyl group](carbonyl-group.md) whose [carbon](carbon.md) is bonded directly to [nitrogen](nitrogen.md); each of $R$, $R'$ and $R''$ may be [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) or a group attached through [carbon](carbon.md). A [peptide bond](peptide-bond.md) contains this linkage. [Chemical resonance](resonance-chemistry.md) distinguishes its [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) from that of an [amine](amine.md). [McMurry: amide resonance](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/24-3-basicity-of-amines) and [Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, 20.4: amide and peptide connectivity](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/20-4-amines-and-amides)
