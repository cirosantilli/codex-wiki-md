# Quantum oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Landau level](landau-level.md)

A [quantum oscillation](quantum-oscillation.md) is an oscillatory contribution to a metal's response as a magnetic field moves quantized orbital levels past the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md). For a closed extremal [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) cross-section of wavevector-space area $A_{\rm ext}$, [Onsager quantization](onsager-quantization.md) gives inverse-field period $\Delta(1/B)=2\pi e/(\hbar A_{\rm ext})$. Oscillations of [magnetization](magnetization.md) and [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md) are respectively the [de Haas-van Alphen effect](de-haas-van-alphen-effect.md) and [Shubnikov-de Haas effect](shubnikov-de-haas-effect.md). Low [temperature](temperature-split.md) and long [quantum lifetime](quantum-lifetime.md) are needed so thermal smearing and disorder do not wash out the [Landau levels](landau-level.md).
