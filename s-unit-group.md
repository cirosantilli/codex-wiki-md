# S-unit group

↑ **Parent:** [Unit group](unit-group.md)

For a [number field](number-field.md) $K$ and a finite set $S$ of finite [primes](prime-number.md), an S-unit is an element of $K^\times$ whose [discrete valuation](discrete-valuation.md) is zero at every [prime](prime-number.md) outside $S$. Its group is the unit group of the ring obtained by allowing denominators at $S$. The [Dirichlet unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md) and the [valuation](valuation.md) homomorphism to $\mathbb Z^S$ show that this group is a [finitely generated abelian group](finitely-generated-abelian-group.md).
