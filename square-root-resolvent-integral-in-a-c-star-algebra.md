# Square-root resolvent integral in a C-star algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous functional calculus](continuous-functional-calculus.md)

For a [Positive element of a C-star algebra](positive-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) $x$, the displayed improper integral converges in norm. Near zero its integrand has norm at most $t^{-1/2}$; near infinity it has norm at most $\|x\|t^{-3/2}$. The scalar identity follows by setting $t=su^2$ for $s>0$ and using $\int_0^\infty2/(1+u^2)\,du=\pi$; for $s=0$ both sides vanish. The bounds are uniform on the compact [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md), so [continuous functional calculus](continuous-functional-calculus.md) gives the operator identity.
