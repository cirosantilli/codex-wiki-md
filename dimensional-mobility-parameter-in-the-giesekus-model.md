# Dimensional mobility parameter in the Giesekus model

↑ **Parent:** [Giesekus model](giesekus-model.md)

When $\sigma=nHC-nk_BT I$ and the bead [hydrodynamic mobility matrix](hydrodynamic-mobility-matrix.md) is $(I+\alpha\sigma)/\zeta$, $\alpha$ has inverse-[stress](stress.md) units. With $p_T=nk_BT$, $A=HC/(k_BT)$ and $b=\alpha p_T$, the dimensionless mobility is $M=(1-b)I+bA$. Thus $0<b<1$ and a positive-definite [conformation tensor](conformation-tensor.md) ensure a positive-definite mobility. The closed [constitutive equation](constitutive-equation.md) is $\lambda\sigma^{\nabla}+\sigma+\alpha\sigma^2=\mu(G+G^T)$, with $\lambda=\zeta/(4H)$ and $\mu=\lambda p_T$. This dimensional $\alpha$ differs from conventions in which the symbol denotes $b$ itself.
