# Stochastic order

↑ **Parent:** [Boundedness in probability](boundedness-in-probability.md)

The notation $Z_n=O_{\mathbb P}(a_n)$ means that $Z_n/a_n$ is [bounded in probability](boundedness-in-probability.md) asymptotically: for every $\varepsilon>0$ some $C$ gives $\limsup_n\mathbb P(|Z_n|>Ca_n)\leq\varepsilon$. A tail bound $\mathbb P(|Z_n|>za_n)\leq C_0/z^2$ is sufficient. In statements involving bandwidths, specify whether the constants must be uniform over bandwidth sequences.
