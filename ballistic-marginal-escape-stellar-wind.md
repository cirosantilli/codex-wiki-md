# Ballistic marginal-escape stellar wind

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar wind](stellar-wind.md)

A steady outward radial flow with negligible pressure force conserves $v^2/2-GM/r$. If its speed at a reference radius equals the mechanical [escape velocity](escape-velocity.md), this constant is zero and $v\propto r^{-1/2}$. [Conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) gives $\rho\propto r^{-3/2}$. An adiabatically evolving [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) carried along that leading flow has $p\propto r^{-3\gamma/2}$ and $T\propto r^{-3(\gamma-1)/2}$.

Finite [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) changes the dynamical problem: the full conserved [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) is $v^2/2+h-GM/r=B$. At the same boundary mechanical escape speed, $B=h_e$ rather than zero. A warm wind therefore needs its enthalpy and equation of state specified before the explicit ballistic velocity can be replaced by a consistent pressure-driven solution.
