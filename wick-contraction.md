# Wick contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Wick's theorem](wick-s-theorem.md)

A Wick contraction replaces a selected pair of free fields by their two-point function. Summing pairings computes Gaussian correlation functions and supplies the propagator lines and combinatorial factors of a [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md).
