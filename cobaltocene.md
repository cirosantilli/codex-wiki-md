# Cobaltocene

↑ **Parent:** [Metallocene](metallocene.md)

The neutral sandwich compound $\mathrm{Co(\eta^5-C_5H_5)_2}$, conventionally Co(II), $d^7$, with 19 valence [electrons](electron.md). One-electron [oxidation](oxidation.md) gives the 18-electron [cobaltocenium](cobaltocenium.md) [cation](cation.md).
