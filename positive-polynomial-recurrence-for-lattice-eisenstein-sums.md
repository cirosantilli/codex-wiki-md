# Positive polynomial recurrence for lattice Eisenstein sums

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent coefficients of the Weierstrass elliptic function](laurent-coefficients-of-the-weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

Write the [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) as $\wp(z)=z^{-2}+\sum_{n\geq1}c_nz^{2n}$, with $c_1=3G_4$, $c_2=5G_6$ and $c_n=(2n+1)G_{2n+2}$. Differentiate the [Weierstrass elliptic differential equation](weierstrass-elliptic-differential-equation.md) to obtain $\wp''=6\wp^2-g_2/2$. Comparing coefficients of $z^{2n-2}$ gives the displayed recurrence for $n\geq3$. Every denominator is positive, so induction expresses every higher [lattice Eisenstein series](lattice-eisenstein-sum.md) as a polynomial in $G_4,G_6$ with positive rational coefficients on its occurring monomials. In particular $G_8=3G_4^2/7$ and $G_{10}=5G_4G_6/11$.
