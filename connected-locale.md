# Connected locale

↑ **Parent:** [Locale](locale.md)

Under classical logic a positive [locale](locale.md) is connected when it cannot be written as the union of two disjoint nonzero opens. Equivalently its only complemented opens are $0$ and $1$. An open sublocale uses this criterion relative to its own top element. Positivity of the whole locale excludes the empty locale. This definition is stated with its logical convention; replacing positivity by negated nonemptiness is not a constructive step.
