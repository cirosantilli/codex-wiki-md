# Classification of a real complex-circle equation

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

For real $A,C$ and complex $B$, if $A\ne0$, [completing the square](completing-the-square.md) gives $|z+B/A|^2=(|B|^2-AC)/A^2$. A positive right side gives a [circle](circle.md); zero gives a single point; a negative value gives the [empty set](empty-set.md). If $A=0$ and $B\ne0$, the equation is the [straight line](straight-line.md) $2\operatorname{Re}(\overline Bz)+C=0$. If $A=B=0$, it describes the whole [complex plane](complex-plane.md) when $C=0$, and the [empty set](empty-set.md) otherwise. Thus unrestricted coefficients require both the single-point and whole-plane degeneracies.
