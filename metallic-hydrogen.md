# Metallic hydrogen

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen](hydrogen.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallic_hydrogen)

Metallic hydrogen is a high-pressure conducting state of [hydrogen](hydrogen.md). Its fluid form in the deep interiors of [gas giants](gas-giant.md) supplies a conducting medium for a [planetary dynamo](planetary-dynamo.md), unlike the iron-alloy conducting cores of [terrestrial planets](terrestrial-planet.md).
