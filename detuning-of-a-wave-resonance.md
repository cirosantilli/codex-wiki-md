# Detuning of a wave resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic wave interaction](quadratic-wave-interaction.md)

Detuning measures the small mismatch from a [resonance condition](resonance-condition.md). When the mismatch and nonlinear forcing are both $O(\epsilon)$, they enter the same [slow time](slow-time.md) equation. A small shift of the [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) can therefore balance a quadratic interaction and permit a locked periodic [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md).
