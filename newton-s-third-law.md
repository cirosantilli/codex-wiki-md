<h1 id="newton-s-third-law">Newton's third law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's laws of motion](newton-s-laws-of-motion.md)

For an isolated pair of particles, the force of particle 1 on particle 2 is equal and opposite to the force of particle 2 on particle 1.
