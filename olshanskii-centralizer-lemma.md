# Olshanskii centralizer lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Jucys–Murphy element](jucys-murphy-element.md)

For the standard inclusion $S_{n-1}\subset S_n$, over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md),

$$
C_{\mathbb C S_n}(\mathbb C S_{n-1})=\operatorname{alg}(Z(\mathbb C S_{n-1}),X_n).
$$

Here $X_n$ is a [Young–Jucys–Murphy element](jucys-murphy-element.md). One way to see generation is to use [multiplicity-free restriction](multiplicity-free-restriction.md): a central idempotent of $S_{n-1}$ selects a preceding shape, and the distinct contents of its [addable nodes of a Young diagram](addable-node-of-a-young-diagram.md) distinguish all possible succeeding shapes. Polynomial interpolation in $X_n$ supplies every diagonal projection in the [centralizer of a subalgebra](centralizer-of-a-subalgebra.md).
