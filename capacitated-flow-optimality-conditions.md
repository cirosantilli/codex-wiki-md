# Capacitated flow optimality conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum-cost flow problem](minimum-cost-flow-problem.md)

For positive capacities and a feasible flow, optimality is equivalent to [network dual potentials](network-dual-potential.md) for which the [network reduced cost](network-reduced-cost.md) is nonnegative at a lower bound, zero in the interior, and nonpositive at an upper bound. These signs make every feasible displacement nonnegative in total cost. They are also equivalent to absence of negative-cost cycles in the [residual network](residual-network.md). A zero-capacity edge is fixed and imposes no reduced-cost sign condition.
