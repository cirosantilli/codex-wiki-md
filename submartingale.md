# Submartingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submartingale)

An integrable adapted process $(X_n)$ is a submartingale when

$$
\mathbb E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\geq X_n.
$$
