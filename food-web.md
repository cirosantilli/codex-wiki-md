# Food web

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)

A [food web](food-web.md) records resource-consumer interactions among species. A linear [food chain](food-chain.md) is a special case. Positive interaction coefficients alone do not imply persistence: the per-capita growth balances must admit feasible positive long-term [mean](expected-value.md) abundances.
