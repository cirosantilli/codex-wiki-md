# Double-null focusing identity

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical null expansions in double-null coordinates](spherical-null-expansions-in-double-null-coordinates.md)

The [Null Raychaudhuri equation](null-raychaudhuri-equation.md) and [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) give $\partial_U(f^{-1}r_{,U})=-rR_{UU}/(2f)=-4\pi rT_{UU}/f$, with an exchanged-coordinate identity in $V$. Under the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md), each rescaled radial derivative is nonincreasing along its corresponding future null direction.
