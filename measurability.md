# Measurability

↑ **Parent:** [Measurable function](measurable-function.md)

Measurability is the property that inverse images of measurable target sets are measurable in the source space. For a [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md), joint measurability in time and sample is a different condition from the separate adaptedness condition at each fixed time. The [predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md) and [progressive measurability](progressive-measurability.md) encode particular joint measurability requirements.
