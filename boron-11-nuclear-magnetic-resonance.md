# Boron-11 nuclear magnetic resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

[Boron-11 nuclear magnetic resonance](boron-11-nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) probes boron environments. Rapid exchange can average inequivalent sites into one resonance; slow exchange reveals distinct sites. Quadrupolar relaxation and line broadening can complicate integration.
