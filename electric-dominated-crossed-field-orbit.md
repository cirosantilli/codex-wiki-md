# Electric-dominated crossed-field orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-field relativistic Lorentz-force solution](constant-field-relativistic-lorentz-force-solution.md)

Uniform perpendicular fields with $E^2>c^2B^2$ admit a subluminal [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) along $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$ that eliminates the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The transformed motion has constant transverse [momentum](momentum-split.md) and hyperbolic [acceleration](acceleration.md) in the electric direction. An initially stationary laboratory particle generally has nonzero transverse [velocity](velocity.md) in this adapted frame; dropping it gives the wrong laboratory trajectory.
