# Greenhouse effect

↑ **Parent:** [Irradiated planetary atmosphere](irradiated-planetary-atmosphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhouse_effect)

An atmosphere that absorbs and reemits thermal radiation can reduce outgoing energy at a given surface temperature, requiring a warmer surface to balance absorbed stellar energy. The [planetary equilibrium temperature](planetary-equilibrium-temperature.md) refers to the radiation budget, not the resulting surface temperature. [Water](water.md), [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) and clouds can alter this balance.
