<h1 id="le-cam-s-first-lemma">Le Cam's first lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Contiguity of probability measures](contiguity-of-probability-measures.md)

Let $L_n$ be the [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) of the absolutely continuous part of $Q_n$ relative to $P_n$. If $L_n$ converges in distribution under $P_n$ to $L>0$ almost surely with $\mathbb EL=1$, then $P_n,Q_n$ are [mutually contiguous](mutual-contiguity.md). Indeed, $\mathbb E L_n\le1$ and convergence to a mean-one limit imply [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md) and vanishing singular mass, giving forward [contiguity](contiguity-of-probability-measures.md). For reverse [contiguity](contiguity-of-probability-measures.md), $P_n(A_n)\le P_n(L_n\le c)+Q_n(A_n)/c$, and one first takes $n\to\infty$ and then $c\downarrow0$.
