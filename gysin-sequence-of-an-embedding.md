# Gysin sequence of an embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Thom isomorphism theorem](thom-isomorphism-theorem.md)

For a closed smooth submanifold $M\subseteq N$ of codimension $c>0$, a [tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md) and the homological [Thom isomorphism theorem](thom-isomorphism-theorem.md) identify

$$
H_j(N,N\setminus M;\mathbb F_2)\cong H_{j-c}(M;\mathbb F_2).
$$

Substitution in the [long exact sequence in relative homology](long-exact-sequence-in-relative-homology.md) gives the displayed embedding Gysin sequence. It does not require orientability over $\mathbb F_2$.
