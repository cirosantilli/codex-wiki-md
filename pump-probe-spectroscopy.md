# Pump-probe spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Spectroscopic measurement of hydration dynamics](spectroscopic-measurement-of-hydration-dynamics.md)

[Pump-probe spectroscopy](pump-probe-spectroscopy.md) measures a sample's response to a probe pulse at a controlled delay after an exciting pump pulse. Varying delay follows dynamics; selecting [frequency](frequency.md) and [wave polarization](polarization-waves.md) can distinguish different contributions. [Spectroscopic measurement of hydration dynamics](spectroscopic-measurement-of-hydration-dynamics.md) is an application to [water](water.md) surrounding [ions](ion.md).
