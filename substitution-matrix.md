# Substitution matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence alignment](sequence-alignment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_matrix)

A [substitution matrix](substitution-matrix.md) assigns a score to aligning each pair of sequence symbols. Scores based on [logarithms](logarithm.md) of observed-to-background pair frequencies turn products of relative support into additive alignment scores.
