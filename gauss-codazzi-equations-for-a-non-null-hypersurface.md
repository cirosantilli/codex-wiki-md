<h1 id="gauss-codazzi-equations-for-a-non-null-hypersurface">Gauss–Codazzi equations for a non-null hypersurface</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss–Codazzi equations](gauss-codazzi-equations.md)

For a [hypersurface](hypersurface.md) with unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) $n$, put $\varepsilon=g(n,n)=\pm1$, $P=1-\varepsilon n\otimes n^\flat$, and $K(X,Y)=g(\nabla_Xn,Y)$ for tangent vectors. The [induced metric](induced-metric.md) is nondegenerate precisely when the normal is non-null. With $R(X,Y)Z=[\nabla_X,\nabla_Y]Z-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z$ and $R_{abcd}=g(e_a,R(e_c,e_d)e_b)$, the [Gauss formula](gauss-formula.md) is $\nabla_XY=D_XY-\varepsilon K(X,Y)n$. Substituting twice into the ambient [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) and projecting tangentially gives the displayed [Gauss equation](gauss-equation.md). Its normal projection gives $g(n,R(X,Y)Z)=-(D_XK)(Y,Z)+(D_YK)(X,Z)$, the curved-ambient [Codazzi equation](codazzi-equation.md). A null hypersurface needs additional geometric data because its induced metric is degenerate.
