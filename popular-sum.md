# Popular sum

↑ **Parent:** [Additive energy](additive-energy.md)

A popular sum of a finite set $A$ is an element having at least a specified number $u$ of ordered representations as $a+b$, with $a,b\in A$. Popular sums define a dense [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) when [additive energy](additive-energy.md) is large. Since the total representation count is $|A|^2$, there are at most $|A|^2/u$ popular sums.
