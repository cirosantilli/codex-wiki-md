# Radiation-supported vertical disk

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md)

A [radiation-supported vertical disk](radiation-supported-vertical-disk.md) has vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) supplied mainly by [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md). In a constant-[opacity](opacity.md), optically thick layer in gravity $-\Omega^2z$, [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) gives $F=c\Omega^2z/\kappa$. This statement applies at a fixed radius and does not resolve the optically thin surface.
