# ATP-dependent chromatin remodelling complex

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatin remodelling](chromatin-remodelling.md)

An [ATP-dependent chromatin remodelling complex](atp-dependent-chromatin-remodelling-complex.md) uses ATP-dependent activity to alter nucleosome positioning, composition or organization. It changes access to DNA without needing to change the DNA sequence.
