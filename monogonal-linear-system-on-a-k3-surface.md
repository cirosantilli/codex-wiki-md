# Monogonal linear system on a K3 surface

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic pencil on a K3 surface](elliptic-pencil-on-a-k3-surface.md)

For a nef big divisor, the monogonal case is the fixed-section system $|D|=\Gamma+|mE|$, where $E$ is an [elliptic pencil on a K3 surface](elliptic-pencil-on-a-k3-surface.md) and $\Gamma$ is a section. Thus $D\cdot E=1$. The fixed part persists even though $D$ is nef; the general divisor is reducible. [Riemann–Roch theorem for algebraic surfaces](riemann-roch-theorem-for-algebraic-surfaces.md) gives $h^0(D)=m+1=h^0(mE)$, proving the equality of systems.
