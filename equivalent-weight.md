# Equivalent weight

↑ **Parent:** [Stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md)

The [mass](mass.md) corresponding to one specified reaction equivalent. The value depends on the [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md); older equivalent formulas must be interpreted together with the adopted [atomic weights](relative-atomic-mass.md).
