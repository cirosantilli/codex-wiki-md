# Weak equivalence principle

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md)

Freely falling [test particles](test-particle.md) with identical initial position and velocity follow the same trajectory independently of their material composition. The principle neglects appreciable self-gravity and nongravitational forces. The [Einstein equivalence principle](einstein-equivalence-principle.md) extends the local statement to nongravitational experiments; the [strong equivalence principle](strong-equivalence-principle.md) also includes gravitational experiments and self-gravitating bodies.
