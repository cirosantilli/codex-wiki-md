# Norm attainment on the vanishing sequence space

↑ **Parent:** [Space of sequences converging to zero](space-of-sequences-converging-to-zero.md)

The [continuous linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) $f_a(x)=\sum a_nx_n$ on the real [space of sequences converging to zero](space-of-sequences-converging-to-zero.md) corresponds to $a\in\ell_1$ and has [operator norm](operator-norm.md) $\sum|a_n|$. Equality $f_a(x)=\|f_a\|$ on the closed unit ball forces $x_n=\operatorname{sgn}(a_n)$ on the support of $a$, since every term $|a_n|-a_nx_n$ is nonnegative. Such an $x$ can tend to zero only if that support is finite. Conversely, the finite sign sequence attains equality for every [finitely supported sequence](finitely-supported-sequence.md) $a$.
