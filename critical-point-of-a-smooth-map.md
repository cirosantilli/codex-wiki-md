# Critical point of a smooth map

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)

For a [smooth map](smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) $f:X\to Y$, a critical point is a point where its [differential](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) is not surjective. This is relative to the target [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md): it is not in general equivalent to a [derivative](derivative.md) being zero.
