# O-acylation of an enolate

↑ **Parent:** [Enolate](enolate.md)

Oxygen-centred attack of an [enolate](enolate.md) on an acylating reagent forms an enol ester or carbonate. Carbon acylation gives a different connectivity, so the ambident nucleophile's two resonance representations should not be confused with two equivalent products.
