# Gas-phase metallicity

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic metallicity](galactic-metallicity.md)

Gas-phase metallicity is the heavy-element abundance of interstellar gas. In star-forming galaxies it is commonly inferred from nebular emission lines in [H II regions](h-ii-region.md), often through oxygen abundance $12+\log_{10}(\mathrm O/\mathrm H)$.
