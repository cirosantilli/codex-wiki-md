# Gravitational binding energy from strip density

↑ **Parent:** [Strip density of a spherical system](strip-density-of-a-spherical-system.md)

For an isolated spherical distribution, shell assembly gives [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) $W=-\int_0^\infty GM(r)r^{-1}\,dM(r)$. Use $S'=-2\pi r\rho$ and $M'=-2rS'$ to write $W=2G\int MS'\,dr=-2G\int SM'\,dr=4G\int rSS'\,dr$. A second [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives the displayed negative quadratic functional. The boundary terms $MS$ and $rS^2$ must vanish; finite-mass regular profiles with finite binding energy satisfy the usual sufficient decay conditions.
