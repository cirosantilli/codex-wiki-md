# Finite-dimensional weak and norm topologies coincide

↑ **Parent:** [Weak topology](weak-topology-split.md)

On a [finite-dimensional vector space](finite-dimensional-vector-space.md) equipped with a [norm](norm.md), the [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md) and [norm topology](norm-topology.md) coincide. The weak topology is no finer because every element of the dual is norm-continuous. Conversely, finitely many coordinate functionals in a basis control the norm, so every sufficiently small basic weak neighbourhood lies in a prescribed norm ball.
