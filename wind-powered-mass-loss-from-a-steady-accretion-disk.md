# Wind-powered mass loss from a steady accretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion disk](accretion-disk.md)

For a [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md), let $S$ be the total loss through both faces per unit projected area and $\dot M>0$ the inward [accretion rate](accretion-rate.md). Annular [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $\dot M'=2\pi RS$. A circular orbit has [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) $-GM/(2R)$, so a marginally escaping wind requires additional energy $GM/(2R)$ per unit lost mass. Equating this sink to a fraction $f$ of the two-face [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) $(9/4)\nu\Sigma\Omega^2$ gives the displayed relation. If the wind carries the local [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $h=\sqrt{GMR}$, [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $(\dot Mh-\mathcal G)'=h\dot M'$, with [viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) $\mathcal G=3\pi\nu\Sigma h$. Eliminating the torque yields $R^2\dot M''+\tfrac32R\dot M'=\tfrac32f\dot M$.
