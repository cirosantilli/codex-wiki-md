# Thurston elliptization conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thurston_elliptization_conjecture)

Every connected closed orientable [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) with finite [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) admits spherical geometry. With cyclic [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md), it is a [lens space](lens-space.md).
