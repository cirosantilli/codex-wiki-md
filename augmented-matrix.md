# Augmented matrix

↑ **Parent:** [System of linear equations](system-of-linear-equations.md)

The augmented [matrix](matrix.md) of a [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md) $Ax=b$ appends the right-hand side as one extra column. Elementary row operations preserve its solution set. The [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md) is consistent exactly when the coefficient [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) equals the augmented [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md), because this is equivalent to $b$ lying in the span of the columns of $A$. A larger augmented [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) produces a zero coefficient row with a nonzero right-hand side during [Gaussian elimination](gaussian-elimination.md).
