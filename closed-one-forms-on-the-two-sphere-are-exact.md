# Closed one-forms on the two-sphere are exact

↑ **Parent:** [Closed differential one-form](closed-differential-one-form.md)

Remove the north pole and south pole separately. Each resulting chart is diffeomorphic to the plane, so the [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) supplies local primitives of a closed one-form. Their difference has zero derivative on the connected overlap and is constant. Adjust one primitive by that constant and glue. This proves exactness without assuming a prior computation of [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md).
