# Holomorphic stereographic atlas of the sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md)

On the unit [sphere](sphere.md), use $\zeta=(x+iy)/(1-z)$ away from the north pole and $\eta=(x-iy)/(1+z)$ away from the south pole. Their overlap relation $\eta=1/\zeta$ is a [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md), giving a [holomorphic atlas](holomorphic-atlas.md) for the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md). The opposite sign of $iy$ in the second [manifold chart](manifold-chart.md) ensures a holomorphic rather than antiholomorphic transition.

Writing the sphere coordinates as $(X,Y,Z)$ avoids confusing their height with the [complex coordinate](complex-coordinate.md) $z$. The two [manifold charts](manifold-chart.md) cover the whole [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md):

$$
z=\frac{X+iY}{1-Z}\quad\text{on }S^2\setminus\{N\},\qquad w=\frac{X-iY}{1+Z}\quad\text{on }S^2\setminus\{S\}.
$$

On their overlap, $X^2+Y^2=1-Z^2$ gives

$$
zw=\frac{X^2+Y^2}{(1-Z)(1+Z)}=1,\qquad w=\frac1z,\qquad \frac{dw}{dz}=-\frac1{z^2}\ne0.
$$

The [coordinate transformation](smooth-transition-map.md) and its inverse are [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md), so these [manifold charts](manifold-chart.md) make the [sphere](sphere.md) a [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces-split.md). The sign in $X-iY$ is essential. Geometric projection from the south pole with the same planar axis labels would give $v=(X+iY)/(1+Z)=1/\overline z$. The [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) in that transition prevents it from being [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md). For $z=i$, the correct second coordinate is $w=-i$: positive $Y$ corresponds to negative [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) in this [manifold chart](manifold-chart.md).
