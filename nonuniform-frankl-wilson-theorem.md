# Nonuniform Frankl-Wilson theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Frankl-Wilson theorem](frankl-wilson-theorem.md)

Let $p$ be a [prime number](prime-number.md) and $L\subseteq\mathbb F_p$ have $s$ elements. A [set family](set-family.md) whose member sizes modulo $p$ avoid $L$, and whose distinct pairwise intersection sizes modulo $p$ belong to $L$, has at most $\sum_{j=0}^{\min(s,n)}\binom nj$ members. The [intersection polynomials](intersection-polynomial.md) $\prod_{\ell\in L}(\sum_{i\in A}x_i-\ell)$ have a diagonal, nonzero evaluation matrix on the family's [characteristic vectors of sets](characteristic-vector-of-a-set.md). [Multilinear reduction on the Boolean cube](multilinear-reduction-on-the-boolean-cube.md) places these [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) functions in the space spanned by square-free [monomials](monomial.md) of degree at most $s$.
