# Zero-curvature condition

↑ **Parent:** [Lax pair](lax-pair.md)

For $\Phi_x+U\Phi=0$ and $\Phi_y+V\Phi=0$, compatibility is $V_x-U_y+[U,V]=0$.

For the auxiliary equations $\psi_x=U\psi$ and $\psi_t=V\psi$, equality of mixed derivatives gives $U_t-V_x+[U,V]=0$. This is the compatibility condition for a [Lax pair](lax-pair.md).
