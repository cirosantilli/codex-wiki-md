# Fully mixed hydrogen-burning evolutionary track

↑ **Parent:** [Composition-dependent CNO homology](composition-dependent-cno-homology.md)

With negligible heavy-element contribution to the [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md), $\mu=4/(3+5X)$ and $\kappa\propto1+X$. At fixed [mass](mass.md), [composition-dependent CNO homology](composition-dependent-cno-homology.md) gives $L\propto(1+X)^{-1}(3+5X)^{-4}$ and $R\propto[X(1+X)]^{1/16}(3+5X)^{-9/16}$. The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) then gives $T_e\propto X^{-1/32}(1+X)^{-9/32}(3+5X)^{-23/32}$. From the formal $X=1$ reference, declining $X$ initially increases [luminosity](luminosity.md) and [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md), unlike the usual redward main-sequence turn-off. A trace CNO catalyst abundance is implicit in the burning coefficient; an exactly metal-free pure-hydrogen star cannot burn through that cycle.
