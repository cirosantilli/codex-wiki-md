# Spring-stabilized chain over a table edge

↑ **Parent:** [Chain sliding over a table edge](chain-sliding-over-a-table-edge.md)

Attach the table-end mass to a linear [spring](spring.md) of [spring constant](spring-constant.md) k. Adding spring [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $k(e_0+x)^2/2$ gives restoring curvature $k-\rho g$ about equilibrium. The positive gravitational feedback from increasing hanging chain length subtracts from the spring stiffness. Stable [oscillations](oscillation.md) require $k>\rho g$; equality is neutral in this ideal linear model, while $k<\rho g$ gives exponential departure. The frequency is independent of the preload, although the equilibrium position is not.
