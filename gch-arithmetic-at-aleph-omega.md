# GCH arithmetic at aleph omega

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md)

Put $\kappa=\aleph_\omega$. [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) gives $\kappa^\kappa=2^\kappa$. König's theorem and the shift of product coordinates give $\kappa<\prod_n\aleph_n\le\kappa^{\aleph_0}\le2^\kappa$. The single instance $2^\kappa=\kappa^+$ of the [generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md) therefore fixes all three displayed values. The strict lower bound explains why [countable](countable-set.md) exponentiation cannot leave this [singular cardinal](singular-cardinal.md) unchanged.
