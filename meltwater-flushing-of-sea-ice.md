# Meltwater flushing of sea ice

↑ **Parent:** [Brine rejection](brine-rejection.md)

Surface meltwater with a positive hydraulic head can percolate through permeable [sea ice](sea-ice.md), displacing brine into the ocean. Flushing can efficiently desalinate warm summer ice, but requires meltwater and connected flow paths.
