# Continuous cohomology of a profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

For a [profinite group](profinite-group.md) and a discrete module with continuous action, continuous cohomology is computed by continuous cochains. On a compact domain these cochains have finite image. Positive-degree classes become zero on a sufficiently small open subgroup and hence are torsion by restriction followed by corestriction.
