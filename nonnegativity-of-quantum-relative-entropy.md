# Nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md)

For [density operators](density-matrix.md), [Klein's inequality](klein-s-inequality.md) gives $D(\rho\|\sigma)\geq0$, with equality exactly when $\rho=\sigma$. If the [support of a positive operator](support-of-a-positive-operator.md) $\rho$ is not contained in that of $\sigma$, the [quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md) is $+\infty$.
