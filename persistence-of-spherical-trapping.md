# Persistence of spherical trapping

↑ **Parent:** [Double-null focusing identity](double-null-focusing-identity.md)

In a regular double-null region satisfying the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md), an initially negative $r_{,U}$ on a [Cauchy hypersurface](cauchy-surface.md) remains negative in its [future domain of dependence](future-domain-of-dependence.md). If both spherical [null expansions](null-expansion.md) are negative at one sphere, the exchanged-coordinate focusing inequality preserves the other sign along its future outgoing null direction. Every later sphere that still exists there remains a [trapped surface](trapped-surface.md).
