# System of equations

↑ **Parent:** [Equation](equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_of_equations)

A [system of equations](system-of-equations.md) requires one choice of unknowns to satisfy several [equations](equation.md) at once. Its solution set is the intersection of the solution sets of the individual [equations](equation.md). For example, $x^2+y^2=1$ and $y=(x-1)^2$ ask for intersections of a [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) and a [parabola](parabola.md). A [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md) is the special case in which every [equation](equation.md) is linear in the unknowns.
