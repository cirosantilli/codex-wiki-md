# Datalog

↑ **Parent:** [Logic programming](logic-programming.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datalog)

A function-free rule language based on definite [first-order Horn clauses](first-order-horn-clause.md), normally with safety conditions on rule variables. Positive [Datalog](datalog.md) has a [least Herbrand model](least-herbrand-model.md) obtained by [forward chaining](forward-chaining.md).
