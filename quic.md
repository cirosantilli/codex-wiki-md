# QUIC

↑ **Parent:** [Transport protocol](transport-protocol.md)

[QUIC](quic.md) is a [transport protocol](transport-protocol.md) designed over [User Datagram Protocol](user-datagram-protocol.md) with multiple streams and cryptographic protection. At the 17 January 2020 examination date, the cited November 2019 design was an Internet-Draft and work in progress. This historical description does not treat a later standard as already available.[4 November 2019, §1 and Status of This Memo](quic-a-udp-based-multiplexed-and-secure-transport-internet-draft-24.md)
