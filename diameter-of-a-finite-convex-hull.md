# Diameter of a finite convex hull

↑ **Parent:** [Diameter](diameter.md)

In a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md), the [diameter](diameter.md) of the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of finitely many points equals their greatest pairwise [distance](distance.md). For $x=\sum_i\lambda_i v_i$ and $y=\sum_j\mu_jv_j$, with nonnegative coefficients each summing to one,

$$
x-y=\sum_{i,j}\lambda_i\mu_j(v_i-v_j).
$$

The [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) gives $\|x-y\|\leq\sum_{i,j}\lambda_i\mu_j\|v_i-v_j\|\leq\max_{i,j}\|v_i-v_j\|$. The reverse inequality holds because all the vertices belong to the [convex hull](convex-hull.md). Thus a continuum of [distances](distance.md) inside a [convex polygon](convex-polygon.md) can be controlled by finitely many [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) pairs.
