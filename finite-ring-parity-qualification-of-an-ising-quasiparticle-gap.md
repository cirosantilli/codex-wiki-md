# Finite-ring parity qualification of an Ising quasiparticle gap

↑ **Parent:** [Ising-chain quasiparticle dispersion](ising-chain-quasiparticle-dispersion.md)

A bulk [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) energy is not automatically the first excitation gap of a finite periodic [spin](spin.md) chain. The [Jordan–Wigner transformation](jordan-wigner-transformation.md) fixes a boundary condition through [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) and restricts occupations to that same parity. At zero transverse field an Ising domain wall costs 2J, but periodic spin configurations contain an even number of walls; the first wall-pair excitation costs 4J. An ordered finite chain also has a distinct small symmetry-sector splitting at nonzero field. The thermodynamic dispersion and these finite-system scales answer different questions.
