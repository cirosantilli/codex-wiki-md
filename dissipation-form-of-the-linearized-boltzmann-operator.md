# Dissipation form of the linearized Boltzmann operator

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear Boltzmann collision operator](nonlinear-boltzmann-collision-operator.md)

Write $f=M(1+h)$ around a fixed Maxwellian and expand the [nonlinear Boltzmann collision operator](nonlinear-boltzmann-collision-operator.md) to first order. The [weak Boltzmann collision identity](weak-boltzmann-collision-identity.md) gives the nonnegative dissipation form in $L^2(M\,dv)$, and polarization proves symmetry on an appropriate common domain. Its zero modes are the [collision invariants](collision-invariant.md) when the scattering kernel is nondegenerate. A [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md), and self-adjointness of an unbounded realization, require additional domain and kernel hypotheses.
