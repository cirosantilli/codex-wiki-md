# Normed division algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Normed algebra](normed-algebra.md)

A normed division algebra is a nonzero unital [normed algebra](normed-algebra.md) in which every nonzero element has a two-sided multiplicative inverse. The complex [Gelfand-Mazur theorem](gelfand-mazur-theorem.md) forces such an algebra to be $\mathbb C$, even when completeness is not initially assumed: embed it in its [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) completion and use [nonemptiness of the Banach-algebra spectrum](nonemptiness-of-the-banach-algebra-spectrum.md).
