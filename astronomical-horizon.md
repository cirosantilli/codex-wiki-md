# Astronomical horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Horizontal coordinate system](horizontal-coordinate-system.md)

The astronomical horizon is the [great circle](great-circle.md) perpendicular to the local vertical. It is a geometric horizon, distinct from a terrain boundary or an apparent refracted horizon.
