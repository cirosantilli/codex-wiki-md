# Fundamental sequence of a limit ordinal

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal arithmetic](ordinal-arithmetic.md)

For a countable limit [ordinal](ordinal.md), a fundamental sequence is an increasing cofinal sequence of smaller ordinals. Effective choices allow a [fast-growing hierarchy](fast-growing-hierarchy.md) to turn limit recursion into recursion at a finite input. Below [epsilon zero](epsilon-zero.md), write the final term as $\gamma+\omega^\beta$. For successor $\beta=\delta+1$, take $\gamma+\omega^\delta(n+1)$; for nonzero limit $\beta$, take $\gamma+\omega^{\beta[n]}$. Earlier terms in the [Cantor normal form](cantor-normal-form.md) remain fixed.
