# Mantle thermal budget

↑ **Parent:** [Mantle convection](mantle-convection.md)

An [energy balance](energy-balance.md) for heat stored in the [mantle](planetary-mantle.md). Here $C_m$ is its effective [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), $H_m$ internally generated radioactive power, $Q_{\rm cmb}$ power entering across the [core-mantle boundary](core-mantle-boundary.md) and $Q_m$ power leaving the mantle. The sign of the right-hand side determines warming or [secular cooling](secular-cooling.md) in this reduced model. Different reservoirs and [latent heat](latent-heat.md) may require additional terms. Surface heat loss includes crustal contributions and should not be substituted for $Q_m$ without specifying the partition.
