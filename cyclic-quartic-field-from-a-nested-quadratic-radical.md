# Cyclic quartic field from a nested quadratic radical

↑ **Parent:** [Finite Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md)

For a prime $p=n^2+1$, this real [number field](number-field.md) is a [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of degree four with cyclic [Galois group](galois-group.md). If $\theta=\sqrt{p+\sqrt p}$, its [minimal polynomial of an algebraic element](minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) is $X^4-2pX^2+p^2-p$, irreducible by [Eisenstein's criterion](eisenstein-criterion.md) at $p$. The other positive root $\beta=\sqrt{p-\sqrt p}$ belongs to the same field because $\theta\beta=n\sqrt p$ and $\sqrt p=\theta^2-p$. Thus all four roots lie in the field, proving normality. The [Galois automorphism](automorphism-of-a-field-extension.md) $\theta\mapsto\beta$ sends $\sqrt p$ to its negative and consequently sends $\beta$ to $-\theta$. It has order four, so the group is $C_4$, rather than a product of two order-two groups.

The prime condition is used for irreducibility, while the square condition on $p-1$ supplies the missing conjugate inside the field. The example $p=2$ yields $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{2+\sqrt2})$, the real subfield of the sixteenth [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md).
