# Komar mass normalization in higher dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Komar mass](komar-mass.md)

For future static-slice normal $u$ and outward radial normal $n$, take $d\Sigma_{ab}=(n_au_b-n_bu_a)dA$, with both antisymmetric components summed. For the [Schwarzschild-Tangherlini metric](schwarzschild-tangherlini-metric.md) the flux is $-\Omega_{D-2}r^{D-2}f'=-(D-3)\Omega_{D-2}\mu$. The displayed dimension-dependent coefficient then reproduces the [ADM mass](arnowitt-deser-misner-mass.md). Reversing orientation reverses the flux; including a factor of one-half in the binormal convention also changes its numerical value. A four-dimensional normalization cannot be carried unchanged into other dimensions.
