# Cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)

Cohomology is the contravariant counterpart of [homology](homology-split.md), formed from cochains and coboundaries. A coefficient ring equips it with the [cup product](cup-product.md) and hence with a graded ring structure.
