# Bouquet graph

↑ **Parent:** [Multigraph](multigraph.md)

A bouquet graph consists of one vertex and $j$ [self-loops](loop-graph-theory.md). Its vertex has degree $2j$, and its [graph excess](graph-excess.md) is $j-1$. The two-loop bouquet is one of the three possible kernels of a [bicyclic graph](bicyclic-graph.md); in a simple subdivision, each loop must expand to a cycle of length at least three.
