# Damping of coupled secular eigenmodes

↑ **Parent:** [Eccentricity damping](eccentricity-damping.md)

Linear [eccentricity damping](eccentricity-damping.md) on one planet changes $\dot z=iAz$ to $\dot z=i[A+i\operatorname{diag}(\tau^{-1},0)]z$. For a nondegenerate, weakly damped two-planet matrix, let $D=\sqrt{(A_{11}-A_{22})^2+4A_{12}A_{21}}$. The modal damping rates are $g_{p,m}=[1\pm(A_{11}-A_{22})/D]/(2\tau)>0$. Both [secular eigenmodes](secular-eigenmode.md) damp even though only one planet is directly affected; the coupling shares the dissipation.
