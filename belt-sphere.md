# Belt sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md)

The belt sphere of an index-$h$ handle is $\{0\}\times S^{n-h-1}$ in the boundary after attachment. It is the [attaching sphere](attaching-sphere.md) of the dual index-$(n-h)$ handle. Its intersections with the [attaching spheres](attaching-sphere.md) of later handles detect passages through this handle.
