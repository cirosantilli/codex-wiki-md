# Fixed-vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth representation of a locally profinite group](smooth-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md)

The [fixed-vector space](fixed-vector-space.md) is $V^K=\{v:\pi(k)v=v\text{ for all }k\in K\}$. Conjugation gives an isomorphism $V^K\to V^{gKg^{-1}}$, $v\mapsto\pi(g)v$. If $K\subseteq L$, then $V^L\subseteq V^K$.
