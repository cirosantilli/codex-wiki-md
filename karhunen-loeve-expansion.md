<h1 id="karhunen-loeve-expansion">Karhunen–Loève expansion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Functional principal component analysis](functional-principal-component-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karhunen–Loève_expansion)

The Karhunen–Loève expansion writes a centered square-integrable random function as $X=\sum_k\xi_k\phi_k$ in mean square, where the $\phi_k$ are [principal component functions](principal-component-function.md) and the uncorrelated scores satisfy $\mathbb E\xi_k^2=\lambda_k$.
