# Magnetic declination

↑ **Parent:** [Geomagnetic field](geomagnetic-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_declination)

The horizontal angle from geographic north to magnetic north, conventionally positive eastward. A survey profile direction and the inducing field's [magnetic declination](magnetic-declination.md) together determine the in-profile horizontal field component.
