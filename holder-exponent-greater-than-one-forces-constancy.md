<h1 id="holder-exponent-greater-than-one-forces-constancy">Hölder exponent greater than one forces constancy</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder condition](holder-condition.md)

If a real function on an interval obeys $|f(x)-f(y)|\leq C|x-y|^\alpha$ for finite $C$ and $\alpha>1$, its difference quotients tend to zero everywhere. The [mean value theorem](mean-value-theorem.md) makes it constant. Equivalently, divide a segment into $N$ pieces to obtain $|f(x)-f(y)|\leq C|x-y|^\alpha N^{1-\alpha}\to0$.
