# Minimum spanning forest

↑ **Parent:** [Forest](forest.md)

A [forest](forest.md) containing one [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md) of each [connected component of a graph](component-graph-theory.md) of a finite undirected [weighted graph](weighted-graph.md), including isolated [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). For disconnected input, [Kruskal's algorithm](kruskal-s-algorithm.md) returns a [minimum spanning forest](minimum-spanning-forest.md).
