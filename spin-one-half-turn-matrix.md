# Spin-one half-turn matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible spin representation](irreducible-spin-representation.md)

In the ordered spin-one basis $(m=1,0,-1)$, the second [angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator.md) satisfies $J_2^3=J_2$. Therefore its [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) reduces to $I-i\sin\theta J_2+(\cos\theta-1)J_2^2$. A half-turn exchanges the two outer weights and negates the middle weight. Applied to the [pion](pion.md) [isospin](isospin.md) triplet, this fixes the rotation factor in [G parity](g-parity.md).
