# Steady fields in two leaky dielectric layers

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric](dielectric.md)

For two planar homogeneous layers in series, [charge conservation](charge-conservation.md) makes the steady normal [current density](current-density.md) the same in both layers. Combining [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) with $E_1d_1+E_2d_2=V$ determines the fields when both conductivities are positive. The free interfacial [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) is $\epsilon_2E_2-\epsilon_1E_1$, whereas total free-plus-bound charge is $\epsilon_0(E_2-E_1)$. The two notions of charge are distinct. This DC state can differ from the immediate response to switching the voltage; interfacial free charge accumulates during the transient.
