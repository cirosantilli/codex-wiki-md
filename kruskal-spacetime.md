# Kruskal spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

The [Kruskal spacetime](kruskal-spacetime.md) is the maximal analytic extension of the positive-mass vacuum [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md). It contains two exterior regions, a [black hole](black-hole.md), and a [white hole](white-hole.md). [Kruskal–Szekeres coordinates](kruskal-szekeres-coordinates.md) remove both horizon coordinate singularities, while $r=0$ remains a [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md).
