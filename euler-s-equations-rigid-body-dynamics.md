↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

In principal body coordinates, [Euler's equations](euler-s-equations-rigid-body-dynamics.md) express [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) balance as $I\dot\omega+\omega\times(I\omega)=\tau$, with applied [torque](torque.md) $\tau$. Setting the torque to zero gives the following torque-free specialization.
