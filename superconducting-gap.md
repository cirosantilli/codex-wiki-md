# Superconducting gap

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

The gap feature in a superconducting single-particle excitation spectrum. In a simple BCS model, excitations have $E_{\mathbf k}=\sqrt{\xi_{\mathbf k}^2+|\Delta(\mathbf k)|^2}$. The [superconducting gap function](superconducting-gap-function.md) specifies the pairing amplitude and its phase; its magnitude need not be constant, and nodes can leave gapless excitations.
