# Fluid limit of the SIRS Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [SIRS Markov chain](sirs-markov-chain.md)

For convergent initial proportions, the scaled [SIRS Markov chain](sirs-markov-chain.md) converges uniformly in probability on each fixed finite interval to the solution of these equations. The [Poisson time-change representation of a Markov chain](poisson-time-change-representation-of-a-markov-chain.md) writes it as its integrated drift plus centered Poisson errors. Bounded rates, Poisson concentration and the [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) control the error. This finite-time limit does not by itself justify an approximation over time intervals growing with the population size.
