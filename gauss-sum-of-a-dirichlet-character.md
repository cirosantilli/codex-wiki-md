# Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md)

Extend a [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) by zero on nonunits. Its Gauss sum is $g(\chi)=\sum_{a\bmod N}\chi(a)e^{2\pi ia/N}$, and its finite Fourier transform at $r$ is $S_\chi(r)=\sum_{a\bmod N}\chi(a)e^{2\pi ira/N}$.
