# Pair mass versus collision energy

↑ **Parent:** [Richter-Ting independent J/psi discovery experiments](richter-ting-independent-j-psi-discovery-experiments.md)

For a reconstructed [electron](electron.md)–[Positron](positron.md) pair, [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) gives

$$
M_{ee}^2c^4=(E_++E_-)^2-c^2|\mathbf p_++\mathbf p_-|^2.
$$

Neglecting the [electron mass](electron-mass.md) compared with the daughter [energies](energy-split.md), and calling the opening angle $\theta$, this becomes

$$
M_{ee}^2c^4\simeq2E_+E_-(1-\cos\theta).
$$

As an original example, choose $E_+=2\,\mathrm{GeV}$, $E_-=3\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $\theta=60^\circ$. Then $M_{ee}c^2=\sqrt6\,\mathrm{GeV}\simeq2.449\,\mathrm{GeV}$, although the pair's laboratory [energy](energy-split.md) is $5\,\mathrm{GeV}$. The pair carries net [momentum](momentum-split.md). Neither its laboratory [energy](energy-split.md) nor the original [proton](proton.md) beam [energy](energy-split.md) is its [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md); unobserved products carry additional [four-momentum](four-momentum.md).

For equal, oppositely directed incoming beams with equal total [energy](energy-split.md) $E_b$, their total [momentum](momentum-split.md) vanishes. Thus the incoming [center-of-mass energy](center-of-mass-energy.md) is

$$
E_{\rm cm}=\sqrt{(E_b+E_b)^2-c^2|\mathbf p+(-\mathbf p)|^2}=2E_b.
$$

Stipulated $1.50\,\mathrm{GeV}$ beams therefore provide $3.00\,\mathrm{GeV}$ of incoming [center-of-mass energy](center-of-mass-energy.md). This identity is exact for the stated equal-beam geometry. Radiation before annihilation can reduce the [energy](energy-split.md) forming the final state; the apparatus also has a distribution of beam [energies](energy-split.md). A nominal machine setting is therefore not an exact event-by-event final-state [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md).
