# Investment-consumption problem

↑ **Parent:** [Expected utility maximization](expected-utility-maximization.md)

An investment-consumption problem chooses portfolio holdings and consumption to maximize discounted [expected utility maximization](expected-utility-maximization.md) subject to a [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) wealth equation and admissibility constraints. A [Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation](hamilton-jacobi-bellman-equation.md) or [utility duality with martingale deflators](utility-duality-with-martingale-deflators.md) characterizes the optimum when the value is finite.
