# Parahippocampal cortex

↑ **Parent:** [Medial temporal lobe](medial-temporal-lobe.md)

Cortex adjoining the [hippocampus](hippocampus.md), implicated in processing contextual and spatial information. Its relation to rodent [postrhinal cortex](postrhinal-cortex.md) does not justify treating every cross-species task or anatomical boundary as identical.
