# Lasso sign recovery from cone invertibility

↑ **Parent:** [Compatibility condition for the Lasso](compatibility-condition-for-the-lasso.md)

Suppose every $\delta$ in the [Lasso cone condition](lasso-cone-condition.md) satisfies $\psi\|\delta_S\|_\infty\leq\|\widehat\Sigma\delta\|_\infty$. On the usual score event, the [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) give $\|\widehat\Sigma(\widehat\beta-\beta^0)\|_\infty\leq3\lambda/2$. Hence every active-coordinate error is at most $3\lambda/(2\psi)$, and a larger minimum nonzero signal guarantees coordinatewise sign recovery.
