# Domain of convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Power series](power-series.md)

The domain of convergence of a [power series](power-series.md) is the [set](set-split.md) of inputs where the [series](series-mathematics.md) has a finite sum. Inside its [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) the [series](series-mathematics.md) has [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md), and outside it diverges. At a finite positive radius the real domain may include either, both, or neither endpoint; the [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) alone does not decide endpoint convergence.
