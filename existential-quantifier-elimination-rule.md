# Existential quantifier elimination rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

From $\exists x\,A(x)$, reason under a [temporary assumption](temporary-assumption.md) $A(u)$ for a fresh witness $u$. If this yields a conclusion $B$ not containing $u$ free, close the witness subproof and infer $B$. The [eigenvariable condition](eigenvariable-condition.md) also requires that $u$ not be free in other undischarged assumptions. The rule does not authorize selecting one fixed named object as a witness or allowing the witness to escape its scope.
