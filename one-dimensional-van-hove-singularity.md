# One-dimensional van Hove singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Van Hove singularity](van-hove-singularity.md)

For a one-dimensional subband $E=E_0+\alpha k^2$ with $\alpha>0$, the [density of states](density-of-states.md) varies as $(E-E_0)^{-1/2}$ just above its edge. Optical peaks also depend on the [joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md), selection rules and [excitons](exciton.md).
