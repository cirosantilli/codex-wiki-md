# Pontryagin product on loop-space homology

↑ **Parent:** [Pontryagin ring](pontryagin-ring.md)

Apply the [homology cross product](homology-cross-product.md) and then the map induced by loop concatenation. This degree-additive bilinear product supplies the [Pontryagin ring](pontryagin-ring.md). The [Bott–Samelson theorem](bott-samelson-theorem.md) computes it for looped suspensions with free [homology](homology-split.md).
