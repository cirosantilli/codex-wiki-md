# Selberg trace formula

↑ **Parent:** [Length spectrum](length-spectrum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selberg_trace_formula)

For a compact [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md), the Selberg trace formula equates a sum over [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) to an area term and a sum over primitive closed [geodesics](geodesic.md) and their iterates. It proves that the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) and the unmarked [length spectrum](length-spectrum.md), with multiplicities, determine each other.
