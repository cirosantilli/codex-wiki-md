# Boltzmann scattering kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear Boltzmann collision operator](nonlinear-boltzmann-collision-operator.md)

The nonnegative weight describing the rate and angular dependence of binary [elastic collisions](elastic-collision.md) in the [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation-split.md). In a scattering-angle representation with solid-angle measure $d\Omega$, it contains relative [speed](speed.md) $g$ times differential [scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md). A parametrization by collision normals has a different Jacobian. Any symmetry factor from integrating over a hemisphere instead of a sphere must be included consistently.
