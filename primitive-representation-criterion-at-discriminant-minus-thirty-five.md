# Primitive representation criterion at discriminant minus thirty-five

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced positive definite binary quadratic forms of discriminant minus thirty-five](reduced-positive-definite-binary-quadratic-forms-of-discriminant-minus-thirty-five.md)

For odd $n$ prime to 35, proper representation by a positive definite [binary quadratic form](binary-quadratic-form.md) of discriminant $-35$ is equivalent to $-35$ being a square modulo every prime divisor of $n$. A primitive representing vector extends to a determinant-one integer matrix, producing a form with leading coefficient $n$ and middle coefficient $b$ satisfying $b^2\equiv-35\pmod{4n}$. Conversely this congruence constructs such a form. Its local roots lift to prime powers and combine by the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md). Every class reduces to one of $x^2+xy+9y^2$ and $3x^2+xy+3y^2$. The condition is local at each prime divisor; a product [Jacobi symbol](jacobi-symbol.md) equal to one alone is insufficient.
