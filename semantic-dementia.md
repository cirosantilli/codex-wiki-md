# Semantic dementia

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive neurology](cognitive-neurology.md)

A progressive syndrome with deterioration of [conceptual knowledge](conceptual-knowledge.md), often accompanied by fluent [speech](speech.md), [anomia](anomia.md) and poor word comprehension. Nonverbal semantic tests are important for distinguishing broad conceptual impairment from an isolated language-output problem.[2000, evidence beyond verbal tests](non-verbal-semantic-impairment-in-semantic-dementia-by-sasha-bozeat-and-others.md)
