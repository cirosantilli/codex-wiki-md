# Convergence in distribution to a constant implies convergence in probability

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence in probability](convergence-in-probability.md)

If $X_n\xrightarrow d c$ for fixed real $c$, convergence of the [distribution functions](cumulative-distribution-function.md) at $c-\varepsilon$ and $c+\varepsilon$ shows $\mathbb P(|X_n-c|>\varepsilon)\to0$. Thus [convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md) to a deterministic limit implies [convergence in probability](convergence-in-probability.md) to that limit, even though the implication fails for nonconstant limits on a specified [probability space](probability-space.md).
