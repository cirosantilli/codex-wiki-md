<h1 id="serre-polynomial-generators-for-mod-two-eilenberg-maclane-cohomology">Serre polynomial generators for mod-two Eilenberg–MacLane cohomology</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Eilenberg–MacLane space](eilenberg-maclane-space.md)

For $n\geq1$, the mod-two cohomology of $K(\mathbb Z/2,n)$ is polynomial on the admissible operations in the displayed strict-excess range, including the empty operation. It is obtained from path-space transgressions and compatibility with [Steenrod squares](steenrod-square.md). Products must still be included when listing low-degree vector-space bases.
