# Galvin theorem for bipartite list edge coloring

↑ **Parent:** [List-chromatic index](list-chromatic-index.md)

A [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) has equal [list-chromatic index](list-chromatic-index.md), [chromatic index](chromatic-index.md) and [maximum degree of a graph](maximum-degree-of-a-graph.md) $\Delta$. Start with a proper $\Delta$-[edge coloring](edge-coloring.md). Orient its [line graph](line-graph.md) toward smaller colors at one part and toward larger colors at the other. An edge of color $c$ has at most $(c-1)+(\Delta-c)=\Delta-1$ outgoing neighbours. Every induced orientation has a [digraph kernel](kernel-of-a-directed-graph.md) obtained from a [stable matching in a bipartite graph](stable-matching-in-a-bipartite-graph.md) with those preferences. Apply the [kernel list-coloring lemma](kernel-list-coloring-lemma.md).
