# Reaction mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reaction_mechanism)

A [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md) proposes the sequence of [elementary reactions](elementary-reaction.md) that produces an overall [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md). Its predicted [rate law](rate-law.md) must agree with observations; matching one [rate law](rate-law.md) need not uniquely identify a [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md). See [Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, §12.6: reaction mechanisms](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/12-6-reaction-mechanisms).
