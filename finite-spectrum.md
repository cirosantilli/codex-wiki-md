# Finite spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Cell spectrum](cell-spectrum.md)

A [finite spectrum](finite-spectrum.md) is a [topological spectrum](spectrum-topology.md) equivalent to a [cell spectrum](cell-spectrum.md) with finitely many cells. Such an object is compact in the [stable homotopy category](stable-homotopy-category.md) and has a [Spanier-Whitehead dual](spanier-whitehead-dual.md). Compactness means maps out of it commute with filtered [homotopy colimits of spectra](homotopy-colimit-of-spectra.md).
