# Leptogenesis

↑ **Parent:** [Baryogenesis](baryogenesis.md)

A mechanism in which an initial [lepton](lepton.md) asymmetry is generated and then partly converted into a [baryon](baryon.md) asymmetry, commonly through electroweak anomalous processes. Heavy [Majorana neutrino](majorana-neutrino.md) decays offer one possible realization; a [seesaw mechanism](seesaw-mechanism.md) by itself does not guarantee the required asymmetry.
