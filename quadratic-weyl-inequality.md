# Quadratic Weyl inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic exponential sum](quadratic-exponential-sum.md)

For [coprime integers](coprime-integers.md) $a,q$ with $1\le q\le Q$ and $|\alpha-a/q|\le1/(qQ)$, expand the squared [quadratic exponential sum](quadratic-exponential-sum.md) by its difference $h$. The [exponential geometric sum bound](exponential-geometric-sum-bound.md) gives $|S|^2\ll N+\sum_{h\le N}\min(N+1,\|2\alpha h\|^{-1})$. In each block of length at most $q/4$, the rotations $2\alpha h$ are separated by at least $1/(2q)$: the reduced denominator of $2a/q$ is at least $q/2$, and the approximation error between two block points is at most $1/(2q)$. The [separated reciprocal-distance sum](separated-reciprocal-distance-sum.md) bounds each block by $O(N+q\log(2N))$. There are $O(N/q+1)$ blocks, giving the displayed inequality. For bounded $q$ the trivial estimate supplies the same conclusion.
