# Percolation one-arm decay rate

↑ **Parent:** [One-arm probability](one-arm-probability.md)

For [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on the [square lattice](square-lattice.md), the [one-arm probability](one-arm-probability.md) $g_n=\mathbb P_p(0\leftrightarrow\partial[-n,n]^2)$ has a root [limit of a sequence](limit-of-a-sequence.md) $\gamma(p)\in[p,1]$. The [BK boundary-splitting estimate](bk-boundary-splitting-estimate.md) implies that $32n^2g_n$ is submultiplicative for positive integers. Applying the [Fekete lemma](fekete-s-lemma.md) to its logarithm proves existence when $p>0$; at $p=0$ the rate is zero. The same rate is the root limit of the [percolation two-point connection probability](percolation-two-point-connection-probability.md) along a coordinate axis, by the [reflection lower bound for two-point percolation](reflection-lower-bound-for-two-point-percolation.md).
