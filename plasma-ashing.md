# Plasma ashing

↑ **Parent:** [Etching](etching.md)

Chemical stripping of organic material, commonly [photoresist](photoresist.md), by reactive species produced in a plasma. [oxygen](oxygen.md) radicals convert suitable organic material into volatile [oxidation](oxidation.md) products. A remote source reduces direct energetic [ion](ion.md) bombardment, but exposed materials can still oxidize and inorganic residue can remain.
