# Saturation of harmonic-trap excited states

↑ **Parent:** [Bose condensation in a three-dimensional harmonic trap](bose-condensation-in-a-three-dimensional-harmonic-trap.md)

At ground-relative [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) zero, the leading semiclassical excited population is $N_{\rm ex}=\zeta(3)(k_BT/\hbar\omega)^3$. Additional particles occupy the [ground state](ground-state.md) once this capacity is reached. The continuum description requires many excited trap levels to be thermally accessible.
