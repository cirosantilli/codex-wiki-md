# Nuclear DNA content

↑ **Parent:** [Ploidy](ploidy.md)

[Nuclear DNA content](nuclear-dna-content.md) is the amount of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) in a specified [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md). Define $1C$ relative to one unreplicated [haploid](haploid.md) set of the chosen [species](species.md). It includes coding and [non-coding DNA](non-coding-dna.md) and excludes [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) outside the [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), such as a [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md)'s [genome](genome.md). $C$ is an amount, whereas $n$ is a [chromosome](chromosome.md) count; neither fixes the other across different [species](species.md).

For an original numerical model, assign the three [chromosome](chromosome.md) types unreplicated [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amounts of 3, 2 and 1 arbitrary units. Then $C=3+2+1=6$ units: [diploid](diploid.md) [G1 phase](g1-phase.md) contains 12 units, [G2 phase](g2-phase.md) 24, each completed [meiosis I](meiosis-i.md) product 12, and each completed [meiosis II](meiosis-ii.md) product 6. The totals $2\times12=24$ and $4\times6=24$ check conservation after copying. Equal [chromosome](chromosome.md) counts alone would not justify equal [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amounts if the sets had different sequence lengths.

How can an experiment measure the amount independently of [chromosome](chromosome.md) counting? UCL's [Cell Cycle Analysis by Propidium Iodide Staining](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/child-health/sites/child_health/files/facilities-flowcyt-pi_cell_cycle.pdf_.pdf), pp. 1–2, specifies a [flow cytometric DNA-content assay](flow-cytometric-dna-content-assay.md). Washed [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) receive cold 70% [ethanol](ethanol.md) dropwise while vortexing, then at least 30 minutes on ice. After centrifugation and two washes, [ribonuclease A](ribonuclease-a.md) removes [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) that would also bind the dye. [Propidium iodide](propidium-iodide.md) stains the preparation. A [flow cytometry](flow-cytometry.md) instrument such as the listed BD LSR II records individual-event [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) on a linear scale. Signal area versus height or width excludes doublets and clumps; acquisition is below 400 events per second, retaining at least 10,000 single-cell events. These are protocol settings, not measured results.[pp. 1–2, Materials, Equipment, Procedure steps 1–9, and Flow analysis](cell-cycle-analysis-by-propidium-iodide-staining-by-ucl-institute-of-child-health-flow-cytometry-core-facility.md)

For a [ploidy](ploidy.md) comparison, add a known [diploid](diploid.md) [G1 phase](g1-phase.md) reference processed with the same staining and detector settings, and an unstained background control. These are proposed comparison controls, beyond the protocol's clump-exclusion procedure. Proportional [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) then estimates relative [Nuclear DNA content](nuclear-dna-content.md); it does not count [chromosomes](chromosome.md). A $2C$ event could be a [diploid](diploid.md) [G1 phase](g1-phase.md) product or a replicated [haploid](haploid.md) product; $4C$ could be replicated [diploid](diploid.md) material or an unreplicated four-set [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md). Peaks alone therefore cannot establish [ploidy](ploidy.md) or [cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md) stage without a reference and biological context. Uneven staining, residual [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) and unresolved doublets limit this inference; two $2C$ particles can mimic one $4C$ event.
