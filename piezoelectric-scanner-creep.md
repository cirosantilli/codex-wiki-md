# Piezoelectric scanner creep

↑ **Parent:** [Piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md)

Slow displacement relaxation following a change in piezoelectric scanner drive, even after the commanded voltage is held fixed. Settling and position-sensor [closed-loop control](closed-loop-control.md) reduce its effect on image coordinates.
