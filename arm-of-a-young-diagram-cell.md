# Arm of a Young-diagram cell

↑ **Parent:** [Hook of a Young diagram](hook-of-a-young-diagram.md)

The arm consists of the cells strictly to the right of $(i,j)$ in its row of the [Young diagram](young-diagram.md); its length is $a_\lambda(i,j)=\lambda_i-j$. It excludes the anchor cell. At $(1,2)$ in $(3,2)$ the arm contains only $(1,3)$, so its length is one. Together with the [leg of a Young-diagram cell](leg-of-a-young-diagram-cell.md) and the anchor, it forms the [hook of a Young diagram](hook-of-a-young-diagram.md).
