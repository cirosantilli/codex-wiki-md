# Comma-to-semicolon prescription

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md)

The prescription replaces partial derivatives in flat-spacetime matter equations by the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) and the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) by the curved [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). It implements minimal gravitational coupling and reproduces flat-spacetime physics at a point in a [local inertial frame](local-inertial-frame.md). It is not a unique rule for rearranged higher-derivative equations: commuting partial derivatives before replacement can conceal a [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) term afterwards. The [derivative-order ambiguity in covariant Maxwell potential equations](derivative-order-ambiguity-in-covariant-maxwell-potential-equations.md) is an explicit example. The [Equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md) removes uniform gravitational acceleration locally but does not erase tidal curvature over a finite experiment.
