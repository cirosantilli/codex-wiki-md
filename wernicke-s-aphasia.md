<h1 id="wernicke-s-aphasia">Wernicke's aphasia</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Aphasia](aphasia.md)

A fluent aphasic syndrome in which comprehension, repetition and informative [speech](speech.md) can all be impaired. Acoustic–[phonological analysis](phonological-analysis.md), [semantic representations](semantic-representation.md) and [semantic control](semantic-control.md) must be distinguished; receptive difficulty does not imply preserved production.[2012, case-series comparison](wernicke-s-aphasia-reflects-a-combination-of-acoustic-phonological-and-semantic-control-deficits-by-holly-robson-and-others.md)
