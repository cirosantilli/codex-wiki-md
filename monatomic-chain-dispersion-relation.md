# Monatomic-chain dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon dispersion relation](phonon-dispersion-relation.md)

For equal [masses](mass.md) $m$ at spacing $d$ joined by nearest-neighbour [springs](spring.md) of [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $K$, [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) gives $m\ddot u_j=K(u_{j+1}+u_{j-1}-2u_j)$. A [normal mode](normal-mode.md) $u_j=u_0e^{i(kjd-\omega t)}$ gives

$$
\omega(k)=2\sqrt{K/m}\,|\sin(kd/2)|,\qquad |k|\leq\pi/d.
$$

This single [acoustic phonon branch](acoustic-phonon-branch.md) is linear at small [wavevector](wavevector.md) and has zero [group velocity](group-velocity.md) at the first [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md) edge.
