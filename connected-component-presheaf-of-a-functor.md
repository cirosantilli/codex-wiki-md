# Connected-component presheaf of a functor

↑ **Parent:** [Final-discrete-fibration factorization](final-discrete-fibration-factorization.md)

For a [functor](functor.md) $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$, precomposition by $u:B\to B'$ induces $(B'\downarrow F)\to(B\downarrow F)$, hence a map of [categorical connected components](connected-component-of-a-category.md) in the opposite direction to $u$. This defines a [presheaf](presheaf-of-sets-on-a-topological-space.md) on $\mathcal D$. Its [category of elements](category-of-elements.md) is the intermediate [category](category-split.md) in the [final-discrete-fibration factorization](final-discrete-fibration-factorization.md).
