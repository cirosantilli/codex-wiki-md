# Seiche

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow-water approximation](shallow-water-approximation.md)

A [seiche](seiche.md) is a standing oscillation of a body of water. Its [natural frequencies](natural-frequency.md) depend on the basin geometry, [water depth](water-depth.md) and the boundary conditions at its closed walls or open connections.
