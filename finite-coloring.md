# Finite coloring

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey theory](ramsey-theory-split.md)

A finite coloring of a set $X$ is a [function](function-split.md) $c:X\to C$ into a [finite set](finite-set.md) $C$ of colors. A subset is monochromatic when the restriction of $c$ to it is [constant](constant-function.md).
