# Gauss-Wantzel theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible regular polygon](constructible-regular-polygon.md)

For $N\geq3$, a [regular polygon](regular-polygon.md) with $N$ sides is a [constructible regular polygon](constructible-regular-polygon.md) exactly when $N=2^a p_1\cdots p_r$, where $a$ is a [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) and the $p_j$ are distinct [Fermat primes](fermat-prime.md); the product may be empty. Thus seventeen and fifteen qualify, while nine does not. [Carl Friedrich Gauss](carl-friedrich-gauss.md) established sufficiency, and [Pierre Wantzel](pierre-wantzel.md) proved necessity in 1837.[slide 18](primality-testing-then-and-now-by-carl-pomerance.md)
