# Seismic reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

The return of an [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md) from a contrast in material properties. At normal incidence in an isotropic medium, the conventional stress-amplitude [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) is $(Z_2-Z_1)/(Z_2+Z_1)$ for the relevant [P wave](p-wave.md) or [shear-wave impedance](shear-wave-impedance.md).
