# Landau contour

↑ **Parent:** [Landau damping](landau-damping.md)

The velocity-integration contour implementing causal continuation of a kinetic response from the upper complex [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) half-plane. As a pole approaches the real axis, the contour passes below it. With denominator $v-u$, the real-axis limit is $\operatorname{PV}\int f_0'(v)/(v-u)\,dv+i\pi f_0'(u)$. Continuing the pole below the axis keeps this causal prescription rather than simply replacing the contour by an undeformed real-axis integral.
