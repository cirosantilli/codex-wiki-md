# Magnetic structure factor

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic neutron diffraction](magnetic-neutron-diffraction.md)

The [structure factor](structure-factor.md) for [magnetic neutron diffraction](magnetic-neutron-diffraction.md) sums the [magnetic form factor](magnetic-form-factor.md) and the [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) component perpendicular to the [scattering vector](scattering-vector.md) over the magnetic [atomic basis](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md). For unpolarized [neutrons](neutron-split.md), the magnetic contribution to the coherent [diffraction](diffraction.md) [wave intensity](intensity-physics.md) is proportional to $|\mathbf F_M|^2$. A periodic change of sign of [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) can shift its allowed peaks away from the nuclear [reciprocal lattice vectors](reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) without changing nuclear positions.
