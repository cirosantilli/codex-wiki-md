# Spherical reaction-diffusion growth threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction-diffusion spectral decay threshold](reaction-diffusion-spectral-decay-threshold.md)

For $n_t=D\Delta n+\gamma n$ in a sphere of radius $a$, with zero surface density and regularity at the origin, the lowest spherically symmetric [normal mode](normal-mode.md) is $n(r,t)=n_c\sin(\pi r/a)/(\pi r/a)\exp[(\gamma-\pi^2D/a^2)t]$. Substitution of $R=f/r$ in the radial [Laplacian](laplacian.md) gives $f''+k^2f=0$, and regularity removes the cosine solution. The [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) gives $k=m\pi/a$; a radial mode with no interior zeros requires $m=1$. Thus [diffusion](diffusion.md) leakage suppresses growth below the displayed threshold, while the mode is stationary at equality. The expression at $r=0$ uses its continuous limit. In a linear [neutron](neutron-split.md) multiplication model this is the competition between bulk production and surface loss.
