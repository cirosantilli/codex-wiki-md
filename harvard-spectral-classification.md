# Harvard spectral classification

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral classification](spectral-classification.md)

The stellar temperature sequence O, B, A, F, G, K, M, with numerical subclasses approximately from hotter 0 to cooler 9 within each letter class. Spectral features follow temperature-dependent excitation, ionization and molecule survival, not a corresponding change in the bulk [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) abundance. A separate [luminosity class](luminosity-class.md) describes gravity-sensitive spectral differences.
