# Moment hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic chemical kinetics](stochastic-chemical-kinetics.md)

A moment hierarchy is the coupled family of equations obtained by applying a [Markov jump-process generator](markov-jump-process-generator.md) to powers of the state. Nonlinear [reaction propensity functions](reaction-propensity-function.md) make a moment of one order depend on higher-order moments, so the hierarchy generally does not close after finitely many equations.
