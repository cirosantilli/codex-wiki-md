# Catenary light ray in a linear refractive index

↑ **Parent:** [Mirage](mirage.md)

For height $x$ and horizontal position $y$, [Fermat principle](fermat-principle.md) in a positive index profile $n(x)=1+\beta x$, $\beta>0$, conserves $n(x)y'/\sqrt{1+(y')^2}$ on a graph $y(x)$. For positive magnitude $c$ of the horizontal optical momentum, integration gives the displayed [catenary](catenary.md). Its minimum height is $x_0=(c-1)/\beta$, at $y=y_0$. Thus $c>1$ produces a smooth turning ray wholly above a road at $x=0$. The two graph branches have opposite signs of $dy/dx$; $x(y)$ continues smoothly through their common horizontal tangent. For a signed negative graph constant, use $|c|$ in the prefactor; $c=0$ gives a vertical ray separately.

The [refractive index](refractive-index.md) increases with height, and the [light ray](light-ray.md) turns at $X=c-1>0$.
