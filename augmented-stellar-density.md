# Augmented stellar density

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md)

An augmented density $\rho(r,\psi)$ treats radius and [relative potential](relative-potential.md) as independent variables and reduces to the physical density on $\psi=\psi(r)$. Its extension away from that curve contains additional orbital-anisotropy information. Different extensions can give the same physical density; physical realizability additionally requires a nonnegative [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md).
