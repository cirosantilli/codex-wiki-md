# Horizontally invariant magnetized shearing-sheet equations

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetorotational instability](magnetorotational-instability.md)

For [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md) at constant [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, a [shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md) with velocity $-q\Omega x\mathbf e_y+\mathbf v_h(z,t)$ and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) $\mathbf B_h(z,t)+B_z\mathbf e_z$ obeys

$$
\partial_tv_x-2\Omega v_y=\frac{B_z}{\mu_0\rho}\partial_zB_x,\qquad
\partial_tv_y+(2-q)\Omega v_x=\frac{B_z}{\mu_0\rho}\partial_zB_y,
$$



$$
\partial_tB_x=B_z\partial_zv_x,\qquad
\partial_tB_y+q\Omega B_x=B_z\partial_zv_y.
$$

Horizontal invariance and vanishing vertical velocity eliminate nonlinear [advection](advection.md). These equations retain [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md), orbital shear and [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md). The vertical pressure adjusts to balance gravity and [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md).
