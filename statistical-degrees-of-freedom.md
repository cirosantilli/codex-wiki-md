# Statistical degrees of freedom

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

The number of free dimensions remaining after specified constraints often determines a reference [sampling distribution](sampling-distribution.md). In a full-rank [normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md) with $n$ observations and $p$ mean coefficients, the residual space has dimension $n-p$. A regular nested [likelihood-ratio test](likelihood-ratio-test.md) instead uses the difference in free [statistical parameter](statistical-parameter.md) dimensions. Boundary constraints may invalidate this regular calibration.
