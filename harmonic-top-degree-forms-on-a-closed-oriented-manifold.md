# Harmonic top-degree forms on a closed oriented manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic differential form](harmonic-differential-form.md)

On a connected compact oriented [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) without boundary, a top-degree form is $f\operatorname{vol}_g$. Its exterior derivative is automatically zero, and the Hodge codifferential vanishes exactly when $df=0$. Thus it is harmonic exactly when $f$ is constant. Equivalently the [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) identifies this space with harmonic functions. Without connectedness the dimension is the number of connected components.
