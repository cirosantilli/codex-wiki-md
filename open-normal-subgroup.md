# Open normal subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Open subgroup](open-subgroup.md)

An [open normal subgroup](open-normal-subgroup.md) $N$ of a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) is simultaneously open in its [topology](topology-split.md) and invariant under [conjugation](conjugation.md). For a [profinite group](profinite-group.md), the quotient $G/N$ is a finite discrete [group](group-split.md): its [cosets](coset.md) form a disjoint [open cover](open-cover.md), so [compactness](compact-space.md) gives finitely many. Conversely the [group homomorphism kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) of a continuous [homomorphism](homomorphism.md) to a finite discrete [group](group-split.md) is open and normal. Such [subgroups](subgroup.md) form an identity-neighbourhood [basis](basis.md) of every [profinite group](profinite-group.md).
