# Reflexive Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Reflexive space](reflexive-space.md)

A Banach space $X$ is reflexive when its canonical embedding $J_X:X\to X^{**}$ is surjective. Equivalently, its closed unit ball is [weakly compact](weakly-compact-set.md).
