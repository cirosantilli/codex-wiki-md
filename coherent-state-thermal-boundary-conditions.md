# Coherent-state thermal boundary conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent-state time slicing](coherent-state-time-slicing.md)

The thermal trace closes bosonic coherent-state paths periodically and fermionic coherent-state paths antiperiodically: $\psi(\beta)=\zeta\psi(0)$ and $\bar\psi(\beta)=\zeta\bar\psi(0)$, with $\zeta=1$ for bosons and $-1$ for fermions. The minus sign follows from the [fermionic coherent-state trace](fermionic-coherent-state-trace.md), not from choosing a different Hamiltonian. These boundary conditions determine the allowed [Matsubara frequencies](matsubara-frequency.md).
