# Conjunctive normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md)

A [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md) is in conjunctive normal form when it is a conjunction of [Boolean clauses](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md). It is true precisely when every clause is true; an empty conjunction is true. [3-SAT](3-sat.md) restricts each clause to at most three [Boolean literals](boolean-literal.md).
