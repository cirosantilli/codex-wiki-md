# Titanocene methylidene

↑ **Parent:** [Organometallic compound](organometallic-compound.md)

The reactive early-metal alkylidene $\mathrm{Cp_2Ti{=}CH_2}$. [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) methylenation proceeds through an [oxatitanacyclobutane](oxatitanacyclobutane.md); [alkene](alkene.md) capture gives a [titanacyclobutane](titanacyclobutane.md). Its [oxygen](oxygen.md) affinity makes [ester](ester.md) methylenation feasible.
