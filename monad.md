# Monad

↑ **Parent:** [Category theory](category-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monad_(category_theory))

A monad is an endofunctor $T$ with unit and multiplication satisfying associativity and unit laws.
