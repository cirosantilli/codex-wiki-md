# D5 root system

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

The [root system](root-system.md) $D_5$ consists of the forty [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md) $\pm e_i\pm e_j$, $1\le i<j\le5$, in an orthonormal [basis](basis.md). One choice of [simple roots](simple-root.md) is $e_1-e_2,e_2-e_3,e_3-e_4,e_4-e_5,e_4+e_5$. The [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) has a fork at node $3$ with ends $4$ and $5$. Its [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) permutes coordinates and changes an even number of signs. The associated complex [simple Lie algebra](simple-lie-algebra.md) is $\mathfrak{so}(10,\mathbb C)$. Its two [half-spin representations](half-spin-representation.md) have dimension $16$, with [highest weights](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\tfrac12(e_1+e_2+e_3+e_4\mp e_5)$.
