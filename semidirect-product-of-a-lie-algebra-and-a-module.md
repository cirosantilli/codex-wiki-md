# Semidirect product of a Lie algebra and a module

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

For a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) of $\mathfrak g$ on $V$, the semidirect product $\mathfrak g\ltimes V$ is the vector space $\mathfrak g\oplus V$ with bracket

$$
[(x,v),(y,w)]=([x,y],xw-yv).
$$

The subspace $V$ is an abelian [ideal of a Lie algebra](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
