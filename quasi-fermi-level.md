# Quasi-Fermi level

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md)

A [quasi-Fermi level](quasi-fermi-level.md) is a local electron or hole population parameter in a semiconductor driven out of equilibrium, assuming each population is internally close enough to thermal equilibrium to use a [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md). Separate electron and hole parameters replace one common equilibrium [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md). Their spatial profiles depend on transport, generation and recombination as well as electrostatics. For ideal ohmic contacts with the p voltage $V$ relative to n, the contact electron [electrochemical potentials](electrochemical-potential.md) obey $\mu_p-\mu_n=-eV$.
