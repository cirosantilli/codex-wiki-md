# Asymptotic-pair obstruction to an invariant metric

↑ **Parent:** [Proximality](proximality.md)

If distinct points $x,y$ of a [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md) on a compact [metric space](metric-space.md) satisfy $d(T^nx,T^ny)\to0$ in one [compatible metric](compatible-metric.md), no [compatible metric](compatible-metric.md) can make $T$ an [isometry](isometry.md). On a compact space, all [compatible metrics](compatible-metric.md) give the same asymptotic-pair property by [uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md), whereas an [isometry](isometry.md) preserves the strictly positive distance between distinct points. In a [full shift](full-shift.md), a constant [sequence](sequence.md) and a [sequence](sequence.md) differing at just one coordinate converge to each other under forward shifts, proving this obstruction directly in the [product topology](product-topology.md).
