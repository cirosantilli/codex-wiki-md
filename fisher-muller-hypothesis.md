# Fisher-Muller hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md)

The proposal that [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md) can accelerate adaptation by combining beneficial [alleles](allele.md) that arise in different lineages. Recombination also produces other combinations, so the benefit depends on the inherited associations and ensuing [natural selection](natural-selection.md).
