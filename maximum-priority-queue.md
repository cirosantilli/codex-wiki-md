# Maximum priority queue

↑ **Parent:** [Priority queue](priority-queue.md)

A [priority queue](priority-queue.md) whose distinguished entry has largest key. A [max-heap](max-heap.md) implements maximum inspection in [constant time](constant-time.md), and insertion, extraction of a maximum and increasing the key of a located entry in $O(\log n)$ [running time](running-time.md). An entry handle needs its current heap index to support that last bound; searching by an arbitrary key can take [linear time](linear-time.md). Multiple entries can have equal keys without requiring a particular tie order.
