# Planetary magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary science](planetary-science-split.md)

A planetary magnetic field may include an intrinsic field maintained by an internal [planetary dynamo](planetary-dynamo.md), local crustal [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md), and externally driven fields associated with the [solar wind](solar-wind.md). Separating these components requires spatial and temporal observations. A global internally sourced field, a localized crustal anomaly, and an [induced magnetosphere](induced-magnetosphere.md) are different evidence about the planet's present and past interior.
