<h1 id="fisher-s-inequality-for-block-designs">Fisher's inequality for block designs</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Balanced incomplete block design](balanced-incomplete-block-design.md)

For a [balanced incomplete block design](balanced-incomplete-block-design.md) with $r>\lambda$, $NN^T$ has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $r-\lambda>0$ on the zero-sum subspace and $r+(t-1)\lambda>0$ on the constant vector. Thus its [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md) is $t$. As $N$ is $t\times b$, $t=\operatorname{rank}(NN^T)\leq\operatorname{rank}(N)\leq b$.
