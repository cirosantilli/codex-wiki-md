# Dual of a sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md)

The dual is the [sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md) whose sections on $U$ are module-sheaf morphisms $\mathcal F|_U\to\mathcal O_U$. For a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md) of finite rank, the dual is locally free of the same rank and the natural double-dual map is an isomorphism. General [coherent sheaves](coherent-sheaf.md) need not have this property: a skyscraper at a point of positive-dimensional projective space has zero ordinary sheaf dual.
