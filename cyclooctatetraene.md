# Cyclooctatetraene

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

An eight-membered cyclic [conjugated system](conjugated-system.md) with eight $\pi$ [electrons](electron.md). A planar bond-alternating model has the [D4h molecular point group](d4h-molecular-point-group.md). Constraints on planarity matter when discussing its [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md) and electronic stability.
