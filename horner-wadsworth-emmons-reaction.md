# Horner-Wadsworth-Emmons reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Organic chemistry](organic-chemistry.md)

Olefination of a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) compound by a stabilized phosphonate carbanion, with phosphate elimination. Many common conditions favor an E alkene.
