<h1 id="so-3-as-real-projective-three-space">SO(3) as real projective three-space</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SO(3) group](so-3-group.md)

The [Adjoint double cover from SU(2) to SO(3)](adjoint-double-cover-from-su-2-to-so-3.md) has kernel $\{I,-I\}$. Under [SU(2) as the three-sphere](su-2-as-the-three-sphere.md), multiplication by $-I$ is the antipodal map, so the quotient is [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) $\mathbb{RP}^3$. Equivalently, an axis-angle ball of radius $\pi$ has opposite boundary points identified. The [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is $\mathbb Z_2$.
