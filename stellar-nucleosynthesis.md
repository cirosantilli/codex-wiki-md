# Stellar nucleosynthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleosynthesis](nucleosynthesis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_nucleosynthesis)

[Stellar nucleosynthesis](stellar-nucleosynthesis.md) encompasses nuclear production during stellar evolution and explosions, including [hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md), [core helium burning](core-helium-burning.md), advanced burning and neutron-capture processes. Stellar mass, composition, mixing and mass loss govern which nuclei form and which eventually escape. [Slow neutron-capture process](s-process.md) and [rapid neutron-capture process](r-process.md) build many nuclei beyond the iron group.
