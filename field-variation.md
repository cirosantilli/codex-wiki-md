# Field variation

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory-split.md)

A fixed-coordinate [field variation](field-variation.md) compares two [fields](field.md) at the same [spacetime](spacetime.md) point. If a transformation also changes coordinates by $\delta x^\mu$, its total variation and fixed-coordinate variation differ by $\delta x^\mu\partial_\mu\phi$. Keeping these separate is essential when applying the [Noether current for a spacetime symmetry](noether-current-for-a-spacetime-symmetry.md).
