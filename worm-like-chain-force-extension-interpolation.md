# Worm-like chain force-extension interpolation

↑ **Parent:** [Worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md)

The interpolation links chain extension to entropic tensile [force](force-split.md), with contour length $L$ and [persistence length](persistence-length.md) $L_p$. Small extension gives spring constant $3k_BT/(2L_pL)$; near $x=L$ the force stiffens as $(1-x/L)^{-2}$. The divergence assumes an inextensible backbone and is not a physical infinite tensile force. A branch fit estimates available contour and flexibility, not an unfolding activation barrier by itself.
