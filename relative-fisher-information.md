# Relative Fisher information

↑ **Parent:** [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md)

For sufficiently regular positive [probability densities](probability-density.md), this compares their spatial logarithmic derivatives. It is the dissipation functional for [relative entropy](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) under suitable diffusion equations. For standard Gaussian $g=\gamma$, it becomes $\int|\nabla\log f+v|^2f$, and in one dimension equals $\int|f'|^2/f+\int v^2f-2$ at unit [mass](mass.md).
