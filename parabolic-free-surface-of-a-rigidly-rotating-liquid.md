# Parabolic free surface of a rigidly rotating liquid

↑ **Parent:** [Effective gravity in a rotating frame](effective-gravity-in-a-rotating-frame.md)

A liquid in rigid [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) about a vertical axis is at rest in its [rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md). Constant surface [pressure](pressure-split.md) makes $gz-\Omega^2r^2/2$ constant along the [free surface](free-surface.md), giving the displayed paraboloid. Comparing with $z-z_0=r^2/(4f)$ gives the focal length $f$. The result assumes uniform [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md), a vertical rotation axis and negligible surface-tension corrections. It explains how a rotating liquid can form a reflecting surface or a mould for a mirror.
