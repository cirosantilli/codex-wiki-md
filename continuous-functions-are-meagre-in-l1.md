# Continuous functions are meagre in L1

↑ **Parent:** [Space of continuous functions on a compact space](space-of-continuous-functions-on-a-compact-space.md)

The image of $C([0,1])$ in the [L1 space](l1-space.md) $L^1(0,1)$ is a [meagre set](meagre-set.md). The canonical map $J$ is a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) from the [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $C([0,1])$ with its [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md), since $\|Jf\|_1\leq\|f\|_\infty$. It is not onto: the indicator of $(1/2,1)$ has no [continuous function](continuous-function.md) representative. Continuity would force values zero throughout $(0,1/2)$ and one throughout $(1/2,1)$, contradicting continuity at $1/2$. The [operator range category dichotomy](operator-range-category-dichotomy.md) therefore proves the claim. Continuous ramps $f_n(t)=\min(1,\max(0,n(t-1/2)))$ also show directly that this image is not closed, since their [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) distance to that indicator is $1/(2n)$ for $n\geq2$.
