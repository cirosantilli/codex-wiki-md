# Chebyshev series

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev polynomial](chebyshev-polynomial.md)

A Chebyshev series expands a [function](function-split.md) in [Chebyshev polynomials](chebyshev-polynomial.md) $T_j$. If $|c_j|\leq Mq^j$ for $0<q<1$, then $|T_j(x)|\leq1$ on $[-1,1]$ gives [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) and the tail bound $\|f-\sum_{j=0}^dc_jT_j\|_\infty\leq Mq^{d+1}/(1-q)$ by the [geometric series](geometric-series.md).
