# Colour-preserving gluon vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Color charge](color-charge.md)

A [quark](quark.md)-[gluon](gluon.md) [Feynman vertex](interaction-vertex.md) whose initial and final [quark](quark.md) have the same chosen [color charge](color-charge.md) basis label. Such vertices exist: the diagonal [Gell-Mann matrices](gell-mann-matrices.md) act nontrivially on the red basis vector. Their [gluons](gluon.md) are traceless colour-octet combinations, not [colour singlets](colour-singlet.md). A ninth singlet generator proportional to the identity is absent from [SU(3)](su-3-group.md).
