# Casimir splitting of a trivial quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md)

In a finite-dimensional [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) $0\to N\to E\to\mathbb C\to0$ of representations of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), the generalized zero [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) of the [Casimir operator](casimir-element.md) maps onto the trivial quotient. All its irreducible [composition factors](composition-factor.md) have zero [Casimir eigenvalue](casimir-eigenvalue.md), so they are trivial. The action is therefore strictly upper triangular and has solvable image; because a [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) is a [perfect Lie algebra](perfect-lie-algebra.md), that image is zero. Every lift in this generalized zero [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) is invariant, giving a [split short exact sequence](split-short-exact-sequence.md).
