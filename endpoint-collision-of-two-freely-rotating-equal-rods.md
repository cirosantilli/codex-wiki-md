# Endpoint collision of two freely rotating equal rods

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics.md)

Each massless rod has endpoint masses $m$ at distance $\ell$ from its centre, hence total mass $2m$ and [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $2m\ell^2$. In a symmetric opposite-end collision with centres approaching at speed $V$, a horizontal [impulse](impulse.md) $J$ changes each centre speed by $J/(2m)$ and each contact-point speed by $J/m$. The [coefficient of restitution](coefficient-of-restitution.md) gives $2J/m-2V=2eV$. Both rods rotate in the same sense for the staggered vertical configuration; their centre speeds after impact are $V(3-e)/4$.
