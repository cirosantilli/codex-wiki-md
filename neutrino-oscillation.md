# Neutrino oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutrino_oscillation)

[Neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md) is coherent conversion between [neutrino flavors](neutrino-flavor.md): different [mass eigenstates](mass-eigenstate.md) acquire different propagation phases. It measures squared-[mass](mass.md) differences and mixing, rather than an absolute [neutrino mass](neutrino-mass.md). The [Super-Kamiokande atmospheric-neutrino experiment](super-kamiokande-atmospheric-neutrino-experiment.md) tests path-dependent disappearance; the [Sudbury Neutrino Observatory](sudbury-neutrino-observatory.md) compares reactions with complementary [neutrino flavor](neutrino-flavor.md) sensitivity. [Solar neutrino](solar-neutrino.md) propagation also requires matter effects; the [two-flavour vacuum survival model](two-flavour-vacuum-survival-model.md) is only an illustration.
