# Acetylcholine

↑ **Parent:** [Neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acetylcholine)

[Acetylcholine](acetylcholine.md) is a [neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md) that acts through nicotinic [ionotropic receptors](ionotropic-receptor.md) and muscarinic [metabotropic receptors](metabotropic-receptor.md). The [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) and the target cell's machinery determine the response; [acetylcholine](acetylcholine.md) itself does not specify whether coupling is direct or mediated by intracellular [signal transduction](signal-transduction-split.md).
