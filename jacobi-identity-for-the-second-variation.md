# Jacobi identity for the second variation

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi equation](jacobi-equation.md)

If $w$ is a nonvanishing solution of $-(Pw')'+Qw=0$, expanding the right-hand side gives the displayed identity. Integrating expresses a scalar [second variation](second-variation.md) as a square plus a boundary term. A simple zero of $w$ at a fixed endpoint causes no difficulty for [Lipschitz](lipschitz-continuity.md) endpoint-vanishing variations, since $\varphi=O(x-a)$ makes the boundary term tend to zero. This identity is the scalar form of a [ground-state transform for a weighted Dirichlet energy](ground-state-transform-for-a-weighted-dirichlet-energy.md).
