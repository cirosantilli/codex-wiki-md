# Photonic band structure

↑ **Parent:** [Photonic crystal](photonic-crystal.md)

The frequencies $\omega_n(\mathbf k)$ of electromagnetic [Bloch modes](bloch-s-theorem-split.md) form allowed bands in a periodic [dielectric](dielectric.md). Their gaps and curvature determine forbidden propagation, confinement and [group velocity](group-velocity.md).
