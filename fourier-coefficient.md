# Fourier coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

For an integrable [function](function-split.md) of [period](period-of-a-function.md) $L>0$, its complex [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) measure its [inner product](inner-product-split.md) with each [complex exponential](complex-exponential-function.md) mode:

$$
\widehat f_n=\frac1L\int_{x_0}^{x_0+L}f(x)e^{-2\pi inx/L}\,dx.
$$

For a real [function](function-split.md) of period $2\pi$, the real coefficient convention of [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) gives $\widehat f_0=a_0/2$ and $\widehat f_n=(a_n-ib_n)/2$ for $n\geq1$, with $\widehat f_{-n}=\overline{\widehat f_n}$. For $f(x)=1/2+\cos x+\tfrac12\sin(2x)$, the only nonzero complex [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) are $\widehat f_0=1/2$, $\widehat f_{\pm1}=1/2$, $\widehat f_2=-i/4$, and $\widehat f_{-2}=i/4$. This explains why the real and complex lists look different while representing the same [function](function-split.md).
