# Fourier coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

For a function of period $L$, its complex Fourier coefficients are

$$
\widehat f_n=\frac1L\int_{x_0}^{x_0+L}f(x)e^{-2\pi inx/L}\,dx.
$$
