# Weak Robin problem for a uniformly elliptic operator

↑ **Parent:** [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md)

For $Lu=-\partial_j(a^{ij}\partial_i u)+b^i\partial_i u+cu$ with smooth coefficients on a bounded smooth domain, the [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md) of $(L+\lambda)u=f$ and conormal [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md) $a^{ij}\partial_i u\,\nu_j+\beta u=0$ is

$$
\int_U\bigl(a^{ij}\partial_i u\partial_j v+b^i\partial_i u\,v+(c+\lambda)uv\bigr)
+\int_{\partial U}\beta TuTv=\int_Ufv\qquad(v\in H^1(U)).
$$

The [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) is bounded by the [Sobolev trace theorem](sobolev-trace-theorem.md). If $a^{ij}$ satisfy the positive [uniformly elliptic operator](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) inequality, the [multiplicative trace inequality on a bounded smooth domain](multiplicative-trace-inequality-on-a-bounded-smooth-domain.md) and the [Young inequality](young-s-inequality-for-products.md) show that the form is [coercive](coercive-bilinear-form.md) when $\lambda$ is sufficiently large, even if $\beta$ and $c$ have negative parts. The [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) then gives a unique [weak solution](weak-solution.md) for every $f\in L^2(U)$.
