# Positive-root data for Cn

↑ **Parent:** [Cn root system](cn-root-system.md)

Choose positive [roots of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md) $\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j$, $\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j$ for $i<j$, and $2\varepsilon_i$. The [simple roots](simple-root.md) are $\alpha_i=\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_{i+1}$ for $i<n$ and $\alpha_n=2\varepsilon_n$. Their [coroots](coroot.md) are $\alpha_i^\vee=\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_{i+1}$ and $\alpha_n^\vee=\varepsilon_n$. Therefore the [fundamental weights](fundamental-weight.md) and [Weyl vector](half-sum-of-positive-roots.md) are

$$
\omega_i=\sum_{j=1}^i\varepsilon_j,\qquad \rho=\sum_{j=1}^n(n-j+1)\varepsilon_j.
$$

The [highest root](highest-root.md) is $2\varepsilon_1=2\alpha_1+\cdots+2\alpha_{n-1}+\alpha_n$. These formulas include rank one.
