# Theta series of integer squares

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi theta function](jacobi-theta-function.md)

This convention for the [Jacobi theta function](jacobi-theta-function.md) is the usual theta constant evaluated at twice the argument. The [Poisson summation formula](poisson-summation-formula.md) gives $\theta(-1/(4\tau))=\sqrt{-2i\tau}\,\theta(\tau)$, with the square root holomorphic on the half-plane and positive on the imaginary axis after evaluating its positive real argument.
