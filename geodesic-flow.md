# Geodesic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential map (Riemannian geometry)](exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_flow)

The geodesic flow sends a tangent vector $v$ to the velocity at time $t$ of the unique geodesic with initial velocity $v$. Smooth dependence of solutions of the [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) makes it a smooth flow on the tangent bundle wherever defined.
