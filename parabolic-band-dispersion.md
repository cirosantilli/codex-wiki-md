# Parabolic band dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md)

An approximation $E(k)=E_0+\hbar^2k^2/(2m^*)$ defining an [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) near a band extremum. A fitted positive curvature gives positive [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md).
