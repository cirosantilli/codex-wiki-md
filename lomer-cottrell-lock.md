# Lomer-Cottrell lock

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation junction](dislocation-junction.md)

A junction produced when suitable [partial dislocations](partial-dislocation.md) on intersecting FCC slip planes react to form a low-energy sessile stair-rod segment. It obstructs further slip and can promote [work hardening](work-hardening.md). Frank energetic favorability alone is not a complete calculation of junction geometry or mobility.
