# Semantic aphasia

↑ **Parent:** [Aphasia](aphasia.md)

A pattern of impaired semantic processing often investigated after [stroke](stroke.md), in which regulating the use of knowledge can be especially difficult. The label does not imply that every patient has the same [brain lesion](brain-lesion.md) or precisely the same mechanism.
