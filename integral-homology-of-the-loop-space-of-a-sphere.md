# Integral homology of the loop space of a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Loop space](loop-space.md)

For $r\geq3$, the path-loop [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md) has contractible total space. The [relative homology of a fibration over a sphere](relative-homology-of-a-fibration-over-a-sphere.md) gives $H_j(\Omega S^r)\cong H_{j-r+1}(\Omega S^r)$ for $j>0$, with negative [homology](homology-split.md) zero and $H_0=\mathbb Z$. This proves the displayed groups. The unit map $S^{r-1}\to\Omega S^r$, adjoint to the identity of $S^r$, represents the generator of the first nonzero [homotopy group](homotopy-group.md). Naturality of the [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) shows that it induces an integral [homology](homology-split.md) isomorphism in all degrees through $2r-3$.
