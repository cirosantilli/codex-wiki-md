# Closure characterization of continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous map](continuous-map.md)

A [function](function-split.md) $f:X\to Y$ between [topological spaces](topological-space.md) is [continuous](continuous-function.md) if and only if $f(\overline A)\subseteq\overline{f(A)}$ for every [subset](subset.md) $A\subseteq X$, where the bars denote [closure](closure-topology.md). For a [continuous](continuous-function.md) $f$, pull back an [open neighbourhood](open-neighbourhood.md) of $f(x)$ for $x\in\overline A$; its [preimage](preimage.md) meets $A$, so the neighbourhood meets $f(A)$. Conversely, apply the inclusion to $A=f^{-1}(C)$ with $C$ [closed](closed-set.md). Then $f(\overline A)\subseteq C$ implies $\overline A\subseteq A$, so the [preimage](preimage.md) of every [closed subset](closed-set.md) is [closed](closed-set.md) and $f$ is [continuous](continuous-function.md).
