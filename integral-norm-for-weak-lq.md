# Integral norm for weak Lq

↑ **Parent:** [Weak Lq space](weak-lq-space.md)

The integral expression for the [weak Lq space](weak-lq-space.md) is homogeneous, positive definite up to equality almost everywhere, and obeys the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) by integrating $|f+g|\leq|f|+|g|$ over each set. Its exponent is $-1/q'$, not $-1/q$. The sharp comparison constant with the distribution-function quasi-norm is $q'$; the power function $x^{-1/q}$ on $(0,\infty)$ attains that ratio on initial intervals.
