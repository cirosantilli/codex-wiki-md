# Scalar-field matter projections

↑ **Parent:** [3+1 decomposition of the stress-energy tensor](3-plus-1-decomposition-of-the-stress-energy-tensor.md)

For signature $(+---)$ and future normal $n$, write $\Pi=n^\mu\partial_\mu\phi$ and use the positive induced metric $\gamma_{ij}$ for $s^2=\gamma^{ij}D_i\phi D_j\phi$. The [Klein-Gordon scalar stress-energy tensor](klein-gordon-scalar-stress-energy-tensor.md) has normal-frame density $\rho=(\Pi^2+s^2)/2+V$, momentum $J_i=-\Pi D_i\phi$ and spatial stress $S_{ij}=D_i\phi D_j\phi+\gamma_{ij}[(\Pi^2-s^2)/2-V]$. The spatial index convention and normal orientation must be specified.
