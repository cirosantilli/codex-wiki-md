# Fundamental theorem of Dehn surgery

↑ **Parent:** [Dehn filling](dehn-filling.md)

Every closed connected orientable [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) is obtained by integer [Dehn surgery](dehn-surgery.md) on a finite [framed link](framed-link.md) in the [three-sphere](three-sphere.md). Compare a [Heegaard splitting](heegaard-splitting.md) with a standard splitting of the sphere, factor their gluing difference using the [Lickorish-Dehn theorem](lickorish-dehn-theorem.md), and realize each twist by unit surgery on a distinct level of a collar of the splitting surface.
