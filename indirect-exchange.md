# Indirect exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md)

Magnetic moments can interact through intermediate orbitals or itinerant carriers rather than direct overlap alone. [Superexchange](superexchange.md) uses virtual charge transfer, the [RKKY interaction](rkky-interaction.md) uses conduction-electron spin response, and [double exchange](double-exchange-mechanism.md) gains carrier kinetic energy through spin-dependent hopping. More than one mechanism can operate in the same solid, with different signs and ranges.
