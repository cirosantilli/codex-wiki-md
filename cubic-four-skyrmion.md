# Cubic four-Skyrmion

↑ **Parent:** [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md)

The familiar low-energy $B=4$ [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md) has a cubic baryon-density shell with six face-hole directions and full density symmetry $O_h$, the [symmetry group of a cube](symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md). The proper subgroup is $O\cong S_4$, the [rotational symmetry group of a cube](rotational-symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md); its field invariance includes compensating [isorotations](isorotation.md). A useful [rational map approximation for Skyrmions](rational-map-approximation-for-skyrmions.md) has $R(z)=(z^4+2i\sqrt3z^2+1)/(z^4-2i\sqrt3z^2+1)$. The relation $R(iz)=1/R(z)$ pairs a spatial quarter-turn with a target half-turn. Its branch directions are $z=0,\infty,\pm1,\pm i$, corresponding to the six coordinate-axis directions. The map gives an approximate field, not an exact analytic energy-minimizing solution.
