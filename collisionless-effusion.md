# Collisionless effusion

↑ **Parent:** [Effusion](effusion.md)

During [collisionless effusion](collisionless-effusion.md), selective loss changes the distribution of surviving particle velocities without collision-driven relaxation to a new [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md). The loss law depends on flight paths and on the wall-reflection rule. Speed-dependent depletion and, for specular walls, directional structure can remain.
