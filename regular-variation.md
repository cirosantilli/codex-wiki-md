# Regular variation

↑ **Parent:** [Real analysis](real-analysis-split.md)

A positive measurable function $q$ has [regular variation](regular-variation.md) at infinity with index $\rho$ if $q(tu)/q(t)\to u^\rho$ for every $u>0$. Equivalently, $q(t)=t^\rho\ell(t)$ with $\ell$ a [slowly varying function](slowly-varying-function.md).
