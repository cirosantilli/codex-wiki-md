# Steady conical ice cap with a linear accumulation gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-driven ice flow on a conical slope](gravity-driven-ice-flow-on-a-conical-slope.md)

For [ice accumulation](ice-accumulation.md) $a=A(1-x/\ell)$, regular zero flux at the apex gives

$$
h_s^3=\frac AD\left(\frac x2-\frac{x^2}{3\ell}\right),\qquad 0<x<\frac32\ell.
$$

The steady zero-thickness terminus is at $3\ell/2$, below the [snowline](snowline.md) $x=\ell$. Accumulation above the snowline exactly balances [ice ablation](ice-ablation.md) below it.
