# Generalized Cantor normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Cantor normal form](cantor-normal-form.md)

For every [ordinal](ordinal.md) base $\rho>1$, a nonzero ordinal has a unique finite [ordinal addition](ordinal-addition.md) expansion with strictly decreasing exponents $\beta_i$ and coefficients $0<\xi_i<\rho$. Choose the largest power at most the remaining ordinal and apply the [ordinal division algorithm](ordinal-division-algorithm.md). The next remainder has smaller leading exponent, so [well-foundedness](well-founded-relation.md) forces termination. Each lower tail is smaller than its preceding power; uniqueness of division consequently fixes the exponent, coefficient and tail successively. For $\rho=\omega$ this is [Cantor normal form](cantor-normal-form.md).
