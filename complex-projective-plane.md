# Complex projective plane

↑ **Parent:** [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_projective_plane)

The complex projective plane is the two-dimensional [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md). Its second homology is generated by the class $H$ of a [projective line](projective-line.md), with $H^2=1$.
