# Cubic and quartic Higgs couplings to electroweak gauge bosons

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)

The [gauge-covariant kinetic term](gauge-covariant-kinetic-term.md) of the [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md) gives $M_W^2(1+H/v)^2W^+_\mu W^{-\mu}+M_Z^2(1+H/v)^2Z_\mu Z^\mu/2$. Differentiation with respect to the external fields yields [Feynman rules](feynman-rule.md) $2iM_V^2g_{\mu\nu}/v$ for one [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) and two matching massive [electroweak gauge bosons](electroweak-gauge-boson.md), and $2iM_V^2g_{\mu\nu}/v^2$ for two [Higgs bosons](higgs-boson.md) and the same pair. Identical neutral fields supply combinatorial factors. The neutral radial [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) has no corresponding elementary coupling to [photons](photon.md) at tree level.
