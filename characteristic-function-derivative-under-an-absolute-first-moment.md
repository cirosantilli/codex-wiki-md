# Characteristic-function derivative under an absolute first moment

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic function](characteristic-function.md)

If $\mathbb E|X|<\infty$, the [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) $\phi(u)=\mathbb E e^{iuX}$ satisfies $\phi'(0)=i\mathbb E X$. The bound $|(e^{iuX}-1)/u|\leq|X|$ and the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) justify differentiation at zero. For mean zero, $\psi(u)=|1-\phi(u)|/|u|$ extends continuously by $\psi(0)=0$, and $|1-\phi(u)^n|\leq n|u|\psi(u)$ by a finite [geometric series](geometric-series.md). This supplies a direct characteristic-function proof of the [weak law of large numbers](weak-law-of-large-numbers.md).
