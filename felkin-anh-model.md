# Felkin-Anh model

↑ **Parent:** [Diastereoselectivity](diastereoselectivity.md)

The [Felkin-Anh model](felkin-anh-model.md) predicts facial preference in nucleophilic addition beside a stereogenic centre under suitable open, non-chelating conditions. Orient the large alpha substituent approximately perpendicular to C=O, then favour approach opposite it and past the small substituent. It is a predictive model rather than a universal guarantee.
