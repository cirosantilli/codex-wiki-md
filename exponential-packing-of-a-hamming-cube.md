# Exponential packing of a Hamming cube

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming cube](hamming-cube.md)

An [inclusion-maximal separated set](inclusion-maximal-separated-set.md) of separation $an$ covers the [Hamming cube](hamming-cube.md) by strict-radius $an$ balls. The [Hoeffding lower-tail bound for Hamming balls](hoeffding-lower-tail-bound-for-hamming-balls.md) then gives at least $e^{2n(1/2-a)^2}$ selected vertices. At $a=1/8$ this yields $e^{9n/32}$ vertices, all separated by at least $n/8$.
