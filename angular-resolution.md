# Angular resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Observational astronomy](observational-astronomy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angular_resolution)

[Angular resolution](angular-resolution.md) describes the smallest angular separation or structure an observation can distinguish. A physical scale $r$ at distance $D$ subtends approximately $r/D$ radians when $r\ll D$. Resolving a [black-hole sphere of influence](black-hole-sphere-of-influence.md) therefore involves distance as well as its physical radius, and useful kinematics also require adequate [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md).
