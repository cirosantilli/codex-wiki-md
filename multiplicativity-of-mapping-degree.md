# Multiplicativity of mapping degree

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md)

For maps of oriented connected [closed manifolds](closed-manifold.md) of the same dimension, apply functoriality of [homology](homology-split.md) to $f_*[M]=(\deg f)[N]$ and $g_*[N]=(\deg g)[L]$. The composite coefficient is their product. This recovers the multiplication of signed sheet counts for compositions of finite [covering maps](covering-space.md).
