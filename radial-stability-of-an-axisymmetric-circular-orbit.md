# Radial stability of an axisymmetric circular orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md)

At fixed [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $j$, perturb the [effective potential](effective-potential.md) $\Phi(r)+j^2/(2r^2)$ about a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md). Linear radial motion has $\ddot x+\kappa^2x=0$, where $\kappa^2=\Phi^{\prime\prime}+3\Phi^\prime/r=r^{-3}d(j_c^2)/dr$. Positive $\kappa^2$ gives stable oscillations; axial symmetry alone does not force that sign.
