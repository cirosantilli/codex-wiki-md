# Legendre expansion of thin-disk gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md)

For a thin axisymmetric [astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md), angular integration of the Newtonian kernel eliminates odd [Legendre polynomials](legendre-polynomial.md). Define $I_n=\int_0^R\Sigma(s)s^{n+1}ds$ and $J_n=\int_R^\infty\Sigma(s)s^{-n}ds$. The even coefficient is $\alpha_n=\pi[n!/(2^n((n/2)!)^2)]^2$. In differentiating the paired inner and outer contributions, their moving-boundary terms cancel. The resulting [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md) includes an inward inner-ring contribution and an outward exterior-ring contribution. For smooth [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md), the force at the disk is interpreted with a symmetric [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) or a vanishing-thickness regularization.
