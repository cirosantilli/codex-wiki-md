# Unit normal

↑ **Parent:** [Normal vector](normal-vector.md)

A unit normal is a [normal vector](normal-vector.md) of [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) one. Given a nonzero [normal vector](normal-vector.md) $n_0$, normalization gives $n=n_0/\|n_0\|$. This makes a [dot product](dot-product.md) $w\cdot n$ measure the signed length of the [normal component](normal-component.md), without an extra factor from the length of $n_0$. On a [hypersurface](hypersurface.md), the two opposite choices $n$ and $-n$ correspond to opposite normal orientations; a continuous choice on an [orientable surface](orientable-surface.md) fixes its [orientation of a surface](orientation-of-a-surface.md).
