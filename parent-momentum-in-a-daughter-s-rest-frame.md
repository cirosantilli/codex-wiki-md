<h1 id="parent-momentum-in-a-daughter-s-rest-frame">Parent momentum in a daughter's rest frame</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic two-body decay](relativistic-two-body-decay.md)

For $C\to B+t$ with massless $t$, use $p_C=p_B+p_t$ in the B [rest frame](rest-frame.md). The mass-shell identity gives $E_t=(m_C^2-m_B^2)/(2m_B)$ and $\mathbf p_C=\mathbf p_t$. The denominator differs from the familiar daughter-momentum formula in the parent's [rest frame](rest-frame.md). Units here have $c=1$.
