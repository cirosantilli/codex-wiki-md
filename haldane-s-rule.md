<h1 id="haldane-s-rule">Haldane's rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md)

When one sex of first-generation [hybrids](hybrid-biology.md) is absent, rare or sterile, it tends to be the [heterogametic sex](heterogametic-sex.md). This predicts a sex bias within crosses, rather than a universal difference in isolation between birds and mammals.[Journal of Genetics 12, pp. 101–109](sex-ratio-and-unisexual-sterility-in-hybrid-animals-by-j-b-s-haldane.md)
