# Stevedore knot

↑ **Parent:** [Knot theory](knot-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevedore_knot_(mathematics))

The Stevedore knot $6_1$ is a ribbon knot with Alexander polynomial $2t^2-5t+2$ up to a unit.
