# Large lifted product from a coset progression

↑ **Parent:** [Large lower-step product in a torsion-free nilpotent approximate group](large-lower-step-product-in-a-torsion-free-nilpotent-approximate-group.md)

Let $\pi:G\to G/[G,G]$ be the [abelianization](abelianization.md) map and let $A$ be a finite $K$-[approximate group](approximate-group.md). Suppose a coset progression $HP(x_1,\ldots,x_r;L_1,\ldots,L_r)$ lies in $\pi(A^4)$ and has size at least $\delta|\pi(A)|$. Then

$$
\left|
\bigl(A^{16}\cap\pi^{-1}(H)\bigr)
\prod_{i=1}^r\bigl(A^{22}\cap\pi^{-1}(\langle x_i\rangle)\bigr)
\right|\geq\delta|A|.
$$

A section of $\pi$ is multiplicative up to a bounded power of $A$ inside the [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md); successively separating the subgroup and progression coordinates proves the inclusion needed for this estimate.
