# Inverted falling film

↑ **Parent:** [Falling film flow](falling-film-flow.md)

An [inverted falling film](inverted-falling-film.md) flows on the underside of an inclined solid surface. Taking the normal coordinate from the solid into the liquid reverses the normal component of [gravity](gravity.md) relative to an upper-surface film. In the [Benney equation](benney-equation.md), the hydrostatic contribution to the [volume flux per unit width](volume-flux-per-unit-width.md) therefore changes from $-2\varepsilon h^3h_x\cot\alpha/3$ to its negative. Normal [gravity](gravity.md) then amplifies disturbances of long [wavelengths](wavelength.md), while [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) resists short [wavelengths](wavelength.md).
