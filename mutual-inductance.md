# Mutual inductance

↑ **Parent:** [Inductance](inductance.md)

For two fixed linear coils, [mutual inductance](mutual-inductance.md) $M$ relates [magnetic flux linkage](magnetic-flux-linkage.md) in one coil to [electric current](electric-current.md) in the other: $\Lambda_{21}=MI_1$. The induced [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) is $\mathcal E_2=-M\,dI_1/dt$ with consistent winding orientations. For reciprocal coils, $|M|=k\sqrt{L_1L_2}$, where $L_1,L_2$ are [self-inductances](self-inductance.md) and $0\le k\le1$ measures magnetic coupling. Its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is the [henry](henry-unit.md).
