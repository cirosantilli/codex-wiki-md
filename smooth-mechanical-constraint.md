# Smooth mechanical constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint (mechanics)](constraint-mechanics.md)

An ideal contact [mechanical constraint](constraint-mechanics.md) supplying a [normal reaction](normal-force.md) and no tangential [friction](friction.md). For a stationary constraining [surface](topological-surface-split.md), the permitted [velocity](velocity.md) is a [tangent vector](tangent-vector-split.md), so the [normal force](normal-force.md) has zero [power](power.md) and does no [work](work.md). For a moving [surface](topological-surface-split.md), normality alone does not imply zero [power](power.md) in the laboratory frame.
