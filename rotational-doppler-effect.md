# Rotational Doppler effect

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md)

A [frequency](frequency.md) change associated with rotation and exchange of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). If an optical element rotates at angular velocity $\Omega$ about a chosen axis and gains [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $\Delta L$ from a [photon](photon.md), its mechanical [energy](energy-split.md) gain is $\Omega\Delta L$ in the prescribed slow-rotation model. [Energy](energy-split.md) conservation then gives [photon](photon.md) [frequency](frequency.md) change $\Delta\omega=-\Omega\Delta L/\hbar$. The [rotating half-wave plate frequency shift](rotating-half-wave-plate-frequency-shift.md) is a spin-polarization example.
