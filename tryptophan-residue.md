# Tryptophan residue

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md)

A [tryptophan residue](tryptophan-residue.md) is tryptophan incorporated in a polypeptide. Its aromatic group contributes to ultraviolet absorption and intrinsic protein fluorescence, with optical behaviour dependent on the surrounding structure.
