# Frobenius kernel theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius kernel](frobenius-kernel.md)

For a [Frobenius complement](frobenius-complement.md) $H$, a class function $\theta$ with $\theta(1)=0$ satisfies $(\theta^G)_H=\theta$. For each nontrivial $\eta\in\operatorname{Irr}(H)$ of degree $d$, the [virtual character](virtual-character.md) $(\eta-d1_H)^G+d1_G$ has norm one and positive degree by [Frobenius reciprocity](frobenius-reciprocity.md). It is therefore irreducible, restricts to $\eta$ and is identically $d$ on the candidate kernel $N$. The intersection of the kernels of these characters is exactly $N$: outside $N$, separation follows from the character of the [regular representation](regular-representation.md) of $H$. This proves normality; $N\cap H=1$ and $|N|=[G:H]$ then give the semidirect product.
