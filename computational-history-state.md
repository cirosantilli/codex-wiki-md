# Computational history state

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md)

A [computational history state](computational-history-state.md) coherently records a circuit's intermediate states with orthogonal clock labels: $(T+1)^{-1/2}\sum_{t=0}^T|\psi_t\rangle|c_t\rangle$. A propagation term penalizes a mismatch between adjacent time labels and the corresponding gate. With correct propagation, uniform coefficients form a zero-energy [ground state](ground-state.md) within the history subspace.
