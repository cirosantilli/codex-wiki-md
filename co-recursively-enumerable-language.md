# Co-recursively enumerable language

↑ **Parent:** [Recursively enumerable language](recursively-enumerable-language.md)

A [formal language](formal-language.md) whose [complement of a set](complement-of-a-set.md), relative to the [set](set-split.md) of all input [words](string.md), is a [recursively enumerable language](recursively-enumerable-language.md). A recognizer for the complement detects every negative instance eventually and need not halt on positive instances. A [formal language](formal-language.md) is a [decidable language](recursive-language.md) exactly when it and its complement are both [recursively enumerable languages](recursively-enumerable-language.md).
