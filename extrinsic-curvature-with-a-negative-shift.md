# Extrinsic curvature with a negative shift

↑ **Parent:** [Extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface](extrinsic-curvature-of-a-spatial-hypersurface.md)

For $ds^2=-N^2dt^2+h_{ij}(dx^i-N^idt)(dx^j-N^jdt)$ and the future unit normal, the convention $K_{ij}=-n_{i;j}$ gives $K_{ij}=-(\dot h_{ij}+D_iN_j+D_jN_i)/(2N)$. Lower the [shift vector](shift-vector.md) with $h$. The sign of the shift terms changes when the opposite threading convention is used.
