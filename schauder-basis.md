# Schauder basis

↑ **Parent:** [Functional analysis](functional-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schauder_basis)

A Schauder basis of a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $X$ is a sequence $(e_n)$ such that every $x\in X$ has a unique norm-convergent expansion $x=\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_ne_n$.
