# Cosmological Lagrangian displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Zeldovich approximation](zeldovich-approximation.md)

The cosmological Lagrangian displacement sends an initially labelled matter element from its [Lagrangian coordinate](lagrangian-coordinate.md) $\mathbf q$ to $\mathbf x(\mathbf q,t)=\mathbf q+\boldsymbol\psi(\mathbf q,t)$. Its [time derivative](derivative.md) determines the [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md) through $\mathbf v=a\dot{\boldsymbol\psi}$.
