# Wilson-line gauge transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Wilson line](wilson-line.md)

Expanding a short [Wilson line](wilson-line.md) determines the inhomogeneous gauge-potential transformation. For $V=1+i\alpha$ and Hermitian generators,

$$
A_\mu\mapsto A_\mu+\frac1g\partial_\mu\alpha+i[\alpha,A_\mu].
$$
