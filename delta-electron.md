# Delta electron

↑ **Parent:** [Ionization](ionization.md)

An energetic secondary [electron](electron.md) knocked out by a charged particle. It can radiate above a [Cherenkov radiation](cherenkov-radiation.md) threshold even when the primary particle is below threshold.
