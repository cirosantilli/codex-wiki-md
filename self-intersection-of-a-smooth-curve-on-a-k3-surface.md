# Self-intersection of a smooth curve on a K3 surface

↑ **Parent:** [K3 surface](k3-surface.md)

If a smooth curve $C$ of genus $g$ lies on a [K3 surface](k3-surface.md), the [adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md) and $K_X=0$ give

$$
C^2=2g-2.
$$
