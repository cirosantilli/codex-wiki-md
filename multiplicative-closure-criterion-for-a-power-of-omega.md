# Multiplicative closure criterion for a power of omega

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicatively closed ordinal](multiplicatively-closed-ordinal.md)

For nonzero $\lambda$, the ordinal $\omega^\lambda$ is [multiplicatively closed](multiplicatively-closed-ordinal.md) exactly when $\lambda$ is [additively closed](additively-closed-ordinal.md). One direction follows from

$$
\omega^\rho\omega^\sigma=\omega^{\rho+\sigma}.
$$

For the other, the [leading exponent of an ordinal product](leading-exponent-of-an-ordinal-product.md) shows that products of ordinals below $\omega^\lambda$ still have leading exponent below $\lambda$.
