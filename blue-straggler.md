# Blue straggler

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_straggler)

A [blue straggler](blue-straggler.md) lies above and blueward of the normal [main-sequence turnoff](main-sequence-turnoff.md) of its associated old [stellar population](stellar-population.md). Binary mass transfer and stellar mergers can give an old system a rejuvenated, more massive hydrogen-burning [star](star.md). Its position therefore need not imply recent ordinary [star formation](star-formation.md).
