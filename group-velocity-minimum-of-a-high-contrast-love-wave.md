# Group-velocity minimum of a high-contrast Love wave

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid-base approximation to Love waves](rigid-base-approximation-to-love-waves.md)

With $\epsilon=\beta'/\beta\ll1$, $\eta=\mu'/\mu=O(\epsilon^2)$ and a fixed mode index, put $y=qh$, $W=\omega h/\beta'$ and $K=kh$. The exact [Love wave](love-wave.md) branch has $W=y\sqrt{1+\eta^2\tan^2y}/\sqrt{1-\epsilon^2}$ and $K=y\sqrt{\epsilon^2+\eta^2\tan^2y}/\sqrt{1-\epsilon^2}$. Its minimum [group velocity](group-velocity.md) occurs near $y=(n+1/2)\pi$, with transition width $W-(n+1/2)\pi$ of order $(\eta^2(n+1/2)\pi)^{1/3}$. Well above that width, the [rigid-base approximation to Love waves](rigid-base-approximation-to-love-waves.md) captures both velocities. Density ratios and the mode index must be controlled in this estimate.
