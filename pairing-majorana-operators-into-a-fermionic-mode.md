# Pairing Majorana operators into a fermionic mode

↑ **Parent:** [Majorana fermion operator](majorana-fermion-operator.md)

Two distinct [Majorana fermion operators](majorana-fermion-operator.md) with $\{\gamma_i,\gamma_j\}=2\delta_{ij}$ define the displayed [fermionic annihilation operator](fermionic-annihilation-operator.md). Direct multiplication gives $f^2=0$ and $\{f,f^\dagger\}=1$. Its [number operator](number-operator.md) is a [projection operator](projection-linear-algebra.md) with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) zero and one. An individual Hermitian Majorana operator has $\gamma_i^\dagger\gamma_i=\gamma_i^2=1$, so that expression is not a nontrivial occupation observable. The occupation belongs to a chosen pair. A quadratic coupling $iJ\gamma_a\gamma_b=J(2f^\dagger f-1)$ splits the pair's two occupations by $2|J|$.
