# Weak type with a normed domain

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-type operator](weak-type-operator.md)

For a [normed space](normed-vector-space.md) $E$ and $0<q<\infty$, an operator $T:E\to L^0$ is of weak type $(E,q)$ when the displayed distribution bound holds uniformly over $f\in E$ and $a>0$. No completeness of $E$ is needed. A [sublinear operator](sublinear-operator.md) has pointwise triangle domination and absolute homogeneity; its outputs may be understood modulo [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) equality.
