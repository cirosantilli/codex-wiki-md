# Spectral responsivity

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

For a [photodiode](photodiode.md) in its linear regime, [spectral responsivity](spectral-responsivity.md) is the dark-subtracted [electric current](electric-current.md) per incident [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) in a narrow band at [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda$, measured in $\mathrm{A\,W^{-1}}$. More generally a [photodetector](photodetector.md)'s readout can be electrical voltage or another quantity, with corresponding units. A broad-band readout weights the incoming [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) by the detector's [spectral responsivity](spectral-responsivity.md); it is not the unweighted total incident [power](power.md). [NIST, Spectral responsivity measurements: wavelength-dependent sensitivity and spatial characterization](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/spectral-responsivity-measurements-and-spatial-spectral-power-responsivity).
