# I-adic topology

↑ **Parent:** [Filtration of a module](filtration-of-a-module.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I-adic_topology)

The $I$-adic topology on a [module](module-mathematics.md) $M$ has the powers $I^nM$ as a neighbourhood basis of zero. It is generated by the [I-adic filtration](i-adic-filtration.md); separatedness means $\bigcap_n I^nM=0$.
