# Within-family Mendelian randomization

↑ **Parent:** [Mendelian randomization](mendelian-randomization.md)

Within-family [Mendelian randomization](mendelian-randomization.md) uses genetic contrasts within families, such as differences between siblings. Conditioning on shared parental background can reduce [population stratification](population-stratification.md) and family-level sources of [confounding](confounding.md), although the [exclusion restriction](exclusion-restriction.md) and sufficient [instrument relevance](instrument-relevance.md) remain necessary.
