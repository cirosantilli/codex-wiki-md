# Untruncated Eddington inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Eddington inversion](eddington-inversion.md)

If the [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) is not restricted to positive [relative energy](relative-energy.md), isotropic [velocity](velocity.md) integration gives $\rho(\psi)=4\pi\sqrt2\int_{-\infty}^{\psi}f(E)\sqrt{\psi-E}\,dE$. The same [Abel transform](abel-transform.md) argument as [Eddington inversion](eddington-inversion.md) yields the displayed formula, with lower limit $-\infty$. For $\rho=\rho_0e^{\psi/\sigma_0^2}$, substitution $\psi=E-\sigma_0^2s^2$ gives $f(E)=\rho_0(2\pi\sigma_0^2)^{-3/2}e^{E/\sigma_0^2}$. An escape-energy truncation changes both the density law and the velocity tails.
