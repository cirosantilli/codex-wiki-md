# Surface-fed growth of an accreting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Variable-mass system](variable-mass-system.md)

For a [sphere](sphere.md) with constant [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ collecting [mass](mass.md) at rate $\beta$ per unit surface [area](surface-area.md), $\dot m=4\pi r^2\beta$ and $m=4\pi\rho r^3/3$ give $\dot r=\beta/\rho$. If it falls through stationary material under constant [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $g$ without [drag](drag-physics.md), its [velocity](velocity.md) satisfies $\dot v+3\dot r\,v/r=g$. With $v(0)=0$, an [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) $r^3$ gives $v=g[r-r_0^4/r^3]/(4\dot r)$.
