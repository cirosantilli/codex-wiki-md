# Compactness of normalized weak elliptic solutions

↑ **Parent:** [De Giorgi-Nash-Moser theorem](de-giorgi-nash-moser-theorem.md)

For a fixed bounded measurable [uniformly elliptic operator](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) in divergence form, solutions bounded by one have uniform local [Caccioppoli inequalities](caccioppoli-inequality.md) and [Hölder continuity](holder-condition.md) estimates. A [diagonal subsequence argument](diagonal-subsequence-argument.md) yields a local weak $W^{1,2}$ limit, while the [Arzelà-Ascoli theorem](arzela-ascoli-theorem.md) gives [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) on a fixed smaller closed [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md). The weak equation passes to the limit because the coefficients are fixed and bounded. A global supremum normalization does not force the interior limit to be nonzero.
