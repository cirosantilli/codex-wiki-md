# Stationarity of ordinals of prescribed cofinality

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary set](stationary-set.md)

For a regular infinite $\theta<\kappa$, with $\kappa$ a regular uncountable [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md), $S_\theta^\kappa=\{\delta<\kappa:\operatorname{cf}(\delta)=\theta\}$ is stationary. Build a strictly increasing continuous $\theta$-sequence in any [club set](club-set.md), and take its supremum. Closure places it in that club and the [cofinality of an increasing ordinal supremum](cofinality-of-an-increasing-ordinal-supremum.md) gives [cofinality](cofinality.md) $\theta$. In particular the two disjoint sets $S_\omega^{\omega_2}$ and $S_{\omega_1}^{\omega_2}$ show that the [club filter](club-filter.md) on $\omega_2$ is not an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md).
