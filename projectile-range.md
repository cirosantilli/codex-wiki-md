# Projectile range

↑ **Parent:** [Projectile motion](projectile-motion.md)

The horizontal displacement between launch and landing in a [projectile motion](projectile-motion.md) model. With equal launch and landing heights, uniform [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $g$, no [air resistance](air-resistance.md), launch speed $u$ and angle $\theta$, it is $R=u^2\sin(2\theta)/g$. Different landing heights or forces change the result.
