# Susceptible compartment

↑ **Parent:** [SIR model](sir-model.md)

The [susceptible compartment](susceptible-compartment.md) consists of people who can acquire [infection](infection.md). In the closed [SIR model](sir-model.md), $S(t)$ is their count and $s(t)=S(t)/N$ their [fraction](fraction.md) of the fixed total $N$. Membership expresses the assumptions of the [mathematical model](mathematical-model.md), rather than measuring the strength of an individual's [immunity](immunity-medical.md).
