# Bernoulli invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md)

In a steady adiabatic inviscid fluid with a conservative body force, the [Bernoulli invariant](bernoulli-invariant.md) is the displayed specific energy, constant along each [streamline](streamline.md). Dotting the [Euler equations](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) with velocity and using the [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) identity gives $\mathbf u\cdot\nabla b=0$. In [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md), magnetic work need not vanish; the [planar ideal-MHD Bernoulli invariant](planar-ideal-mhd-bernoulli-invariant.md) includes the corresponding electromagnetic correction.
