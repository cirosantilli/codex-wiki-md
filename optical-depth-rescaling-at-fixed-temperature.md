# Optical-depth rescaling at fixed temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md)

Multiplying an absorber abundance by $a$ with fixed cross sections and physical [temperature](temperature-split.md) profile multiplies [optical depth](optical-depth.md) by $a$. The [Planck function](planck-function.md) as a function of the new coordinate must therefore be composed with division by $a$. A term proportional to $\tau^n$ acquires coefficient $a^{-n}$. Holding its old optical-depth coefficient fixed would instead change the physical temperature profile. This is the immediate radiative comparison before thermal adjustment.
