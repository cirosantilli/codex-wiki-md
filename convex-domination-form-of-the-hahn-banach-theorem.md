# Convex domination form of the Hahn-Banach theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md)

A [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $f$ dominated by a finite real [convex function](convex-function.md) $\phi$ on a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $W$ extends with the same domination to the whole real [vector space](vector-space-split.md). To add $v\notin W$, choose $g(v)$ between the supremum of $(f(w)-\phi(w-tv))/t$ and the infimum of $(\phi(w+sv)-f(w))/s$, over $w\in W$ and $s,t>0$. Convexity, applied to $(s(w_1-tv)+t(w_2+sv))/(s+t)$, makes every lower candidate at most every upper candidate. The candidates at $w=0,s=t=1$ bound the interval by finite numbers. Chain unions preserve domination, so [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) gives a full extension. No homogeneity of $\phi$ is assumed.
