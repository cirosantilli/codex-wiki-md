# Caudate nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Striatum](striatum.md)

A striatal nucleus participating in [corticostriatal circuits](corticostriatal-circuit.md). Reduced volume in affected members of the KE family correlates with aspects of speech-related performance, without isolating a single causal mediator of the [phenotype](phenotype.md).[Brain 125, 465–478; affected/unaffected KE-family members, matched controls, VBM and caudate volumetry](mri-analysis-of-an-inherited-speech-and-language-disorder-structural-brain-abnormalities-by-kate-watkins-and-others.md)
