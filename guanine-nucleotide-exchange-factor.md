# Guanine nucleotide exchange factor

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

A factor accelerating release of bound guanine nucleotide from a target protein, permitting reloading from the free pool. It does not phosphorylate bound [GDP](guanosine-diphosphate.md) into [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md).
