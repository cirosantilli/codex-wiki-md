# Multiplicative inverse

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplication](multiplication.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiplicative_inverse)

A multiplicative inverse of $x$ is an element $x^{-1}$ satisfying $xx^{-1}=x^{-1}x=1$.
