# Chord error bound

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

If a twice differentiable [parametric curve](parametric-curve.md) $R:[a,b]\to\mathbb R^m$ satisfies $\lVert R''\rVert\le M$, its deviation from the linearly parameterized chord is at most $M(b-a)^2/8$. This follows from the Green function for the second derivative with zero endpoint values. It bounds the [Hausdorff distance](hausdorff-distance.md) between the arc and its chord.
