# Residence time distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical engineering](chemical-engineering.md)

The probability density $E(t)$ of times spent in a flowing system. The surviving fraction of a first-order decaying solute is $\int_0^\infty E(t)e^{-\lambda t}\,dt$; it is generally not the [exponential function](exponential-function.md) evaluated at the mean [residence time](residence-time.md).
