# Bose condensation with power-law dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md)

For a homogeneous ideal gas in $d$ dimensions with $A,\alpha>0$, the [density of states](density-of-states.md) scales as $\epsilon^{d/\alpha-1}$. At limiting chemical potential zero, the [Bose-Einstein distribution](bose-einstein-distribution.md) is proportional to $1/\epsilon$ near zero energy. The excited-particle integral is finite exactly when $d/\alpha>1$. Thus positive-temperature condensation at fixed nonzero density is possible precisely for $0<\alpha<d$. A nonconserved particle number, as for ordinary equilibrium photons, removes the fixed-number saturation mechanism even when the integral is finite.
