# NADPH oxidase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An enzyme transferring electrons from NADPH to oxygen. Plant respiratory-burst oxidase homologues can generate extracellular reactive oxygen species participating in [redox signalling](redox-signalling.md).
