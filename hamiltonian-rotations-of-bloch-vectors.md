# Hamiltonian rotations of Bloch vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Bloch representation](generalized-bloch-representation.md)

In a Hilbert-Schmidt orthonormal operator basis, the generator $-i[H,\cdot]$ is represented on the traceless Hermitian subspace by a real [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md). Its propagator is therefore in the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md) and preserves the [purity of a density operator](purity-of-a-density-operator.md). For $N>2$, these rotations form the adjoint image of the accessible unitary group, generally a proper subgroup of the full Euclidean rotation group; preservation of the full [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) of the [density operator](density-matrix.md) imposes further constraints.
