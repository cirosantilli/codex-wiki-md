# Sub-Gamma random variable in the right tail

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration inequality](concentration-inequality.md)

A centered [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ is sub-Gamma in the right tail with variance parameter $v$ and scale parameter $c$ when

$$
\log\mathbb E e^{\lambda X}\leq\frac{v\lambda^2}{2(1-c\lambda)}
$$

for $0\leq\lambda<c^{-1}$.
