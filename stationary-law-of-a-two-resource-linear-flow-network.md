# Stationary law of a two-resource linear flow network

↑ **Parent:** [Two-resource linear flow network](two-resource-linear-flow-network.md)

For route offered loads $\rho_1,\rho_2,\rho_0$, the [balance function of a flow-level network](balance-function-of-a-flow-level-network.md) $\Psi(a,b,c)=\binom{a+b+c}{c}$ gives the [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md)

$$
\pi(a,b,c)=B\binom{a+b+c}{c}\rho_1^a\rho_2^b\rho_0^c.
$$

The [negative binomial series](negative-binomial-series.md) sums over $c$, and two geometric sums give $B=(1-\rho_0-\rho_1)(1-\rho_0-\rho_2)/(1-\rho_0)$ under the [stability conditions for a two-resource linear flow network](stability-conditions-for-a-two-resource-linear-flow-network.md).
