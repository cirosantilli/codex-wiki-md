# Triangle identities for an adjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Unit and counit of an adjunction](unit-and-counit-of-an-adjunction.md)

For $F\dashv G$ with [adjunction unit](unit-of-an-adjunction.md) $\eta$ and [adjunction counit](counit-of-an-adjunction.md) $\varepsilon$, the identities are $\varepsilon_{FC}F\eta_C=1_{FC}$ and $G\varepsilon_D\eta_{GD}=1_{GD}$. They make the transpose maps $f\mapsto Gf\eta$ and $g\mapsto\varepsilon Fg$ inverse.
