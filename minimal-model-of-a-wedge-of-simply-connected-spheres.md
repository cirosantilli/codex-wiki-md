# Minimal model of a wedge of simply connected spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md)

For a finite [wedge sum](wedge-sum.md) of spheres of dimensions $n_i\ge2$, let $L=\mathbb L(e_i)$ with $|e_i|=n_i-1$ and zero differential. Its [Chevalley–Eilenberg cochains of a graded Lie algebra](chevalley-eilenberg-cochains-of-a-graded-lie-algebra.md) are a minimal commutative [differential graded algebra](differential-graded-algebra.md): one generator dual to each Lie [basis](basis.md) word has degree one more than that word, and its differential is dual to the Lie bracket. Its [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) is $\mathbb Q\oplus\bigoplus_i\mathbb Q a_{n_i}$ with all positive products zero. One way to compute this is $U(L)=T(e_i)$; the augmentation module has a length-one free resolution, so the corresponding Ext has only the unit and the dual generating classes.
