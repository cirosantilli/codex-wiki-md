# Proton-motive force

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md)

A [proton-motive force](proton-motive-force.md) is a [proton](proton.md) [electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md) difference per unit [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). It combines the [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) and the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) difference across a [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md); the direction and sign convention must be specified.
