# Laplacian eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Laplacian eigenfunction](laplacian-eigenfunction.md)

A scalar $\lambda$ for which a nonzero [Laplacian eigenfunction](laplacian-eigenfunction.md) satisfies this equation, with specified boundary conditions or on a closed [manifold](topological-manifold.md), is an eigenvalue of the positive [Laplacian](laplacian.md). A [Dirichlet Laplacian eigenvalue](dirichlet-laplacian-eigenvalue.md) imposes zero boundary values; other boundary conditions generally change the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md).
