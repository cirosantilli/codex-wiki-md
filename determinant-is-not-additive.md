# Determinant is not additive

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)

For $A=B=I_2$, $\det(A+B)=\det(2I_2)=4$, while $\det A+\det B=2$. The [determinant](determinant.md) is multilinear in its separate columns, rather than a linear function of the matrix as a whole. Expanding the columns of a sum introduces mixed terms that the proposed additive identity omits.
