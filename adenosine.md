# Adenosine

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleoside](nucleoside.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adenosine)

[Adenosine](adenosine.md) consists of [adenine](adenine.md) joined to [ribose](ribose.md). Adding one, two, or three [phosphate](phosphate.md) groups at the [ribose](ribose.md)'s $5'$ position gives [AMP](adenosine-monophosphate.md), [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md), or [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md), respectively. See [Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell: the components of nucleotides](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26883/).
