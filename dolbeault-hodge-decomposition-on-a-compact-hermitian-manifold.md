# Dolbeault Hodge decomposition on a compact Hermitian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md)

For a compact [Hermitian manifold](hermitian-manifold.md), elliptic theory gives the orthogonal decomposition

$$
\Omega^{p,q}
=\mathcal H^{p,q}_{\bar\partial}
\oplus\bar\partial\Omega^{p,q-1}
\oplus\bar\partial^*\Omega^{p,q+1}.
$$

The harmonic space $\mathcal H^{p,q}_{\bar\partial}=\ker\Delta_{\bar\partial}$ is finite-dimensional and represents [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md).
