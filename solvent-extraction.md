# Solvent extraction

↑ **Parent:** [Separation process](separation-process.md)

Transfer of a solute between immiscible liquid phases according to its relative chemical affinities. [complexation](complexation.md) and [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) can change the distribution; selective back-extraction is often called stripping. Extraction efficiency alone does not establish separation from a second solute.
