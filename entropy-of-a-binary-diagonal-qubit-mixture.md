# Entropy of a binary diagonal qubit mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md)

A mixture of two orthogonal pure qubit states has [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) equal to the [binary entropy](binary-entropy.md) of its weights. Its entropy difference from the first [pure state](pure-state.md) is exactly $h_2(u)$ and its [trace distance](trace-distance.md) from that state is $u$. The elementary bound $-(1-u)\ln(1-u)\leq u$ gives $h_2(u)\leq u\log_2(e/u)$; entropy also never exceeds one bit. For small $u$, the leading term is $u\log_2(1/u)$.
