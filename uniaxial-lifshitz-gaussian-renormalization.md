# Uniaxial Lifshitz Gaussian renormalization

↑ **Parent:** [Lifshitz point](lifshitz-point.md)

For kernel $\kappa^{-1}p_\perp^2+\mu^{-1}p_\parallel^4+m^2$ with positive stiffnesses, preserve both derivative coefficients by $q_\perp=bp_\perp$ and $q_\parallel=b^{1/2}p_\parallel$. The Fourier field multiplier is $\widetilde Z=b^{(2D+3)/4}$, the mass becomes $bm$, and a uniform source becomes $b^{(2D+3)/4}h$. The [anisotropic effective dimension](anisotropic-effective-dimension.md) is $D-1/2$ and the Gaussian exponents are $\alpha=9/4-D/2$, $\Delta=(2D+3)/8$.
