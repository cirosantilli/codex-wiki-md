# Polarization-resolved broadband absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Angular-frequency Einstein coefficients](angular-frequency-einstein-coefficients.md)

Weak incoherent frequency components contribute probabilities. The long-time sinc-squared kernel gives the displayed [stimulated absorption](stimulated-absorption.md) rate for a fixed polarization and spectral energy density per [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). Isotropic unpolarized radiation instead contributes $|\mathbf d_{21}|^2/3$. Converting to density per ordinary frequency changes the coefficient by $2\pi$.
