# Sanger-Nicklen-Coulson sequencing experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Sanger sequencing](sanger-sequencing.md)

In 1977, [Frederick Sanger](frederick-sanger.md), [S. Nicklen](s-nicklen.md) and [Alan Coulson](alan-coulson.md) tested chain termination on [Phi X 174](phi-x-174.md) [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). They isolated [restriction enzyme](restriction-enzyme.md) fragments as primers, heated them with template to $100^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ for three minutes and annealed at $67^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ for thirty minutes. Four extension mixtures used the [Klenow fragment](klenow-fragment.md) of [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md) and radioactive dATP. Their Figure 1 used ddGTP, ddATP, ddTTP and araCTP, an alternative terminating substrate; Figure 2 used all four dideoxy types. This distinction matters when relating the original experiments to an all-ddNTP teaching model.[Sanger, Nicklen and Coulson, DNA sequencing with chain-terminating inhibitors, PNAS 74 (1977), pp. 5463–5465, Sequencing Procedure and Figures 1–2](dna-sequencing-with-chain-terminating-inhibitors-by-frederick-sanger-and-others.md)

Figure 1 used a 12% acrylamide gel at $40\,\mathrm{mA}$ for fourteen hours, read by [autoradiography](autoradiography.md). Omitting the unlabeled dATP chase caused unwanted A stops across samples: a chemistry [scientific control](scientific-control.md). Ordered bands supported local [nucleotide sequences](nucleic-acid-sequence.md); comparisons could revise provisional assignments. Weak T bands, unresolved long runs and structure-dependent migration limited interpretation. The paper's observations are evidence from particular preparations, not a guarantee that every endpoint is resolved.[Sanger, Nicklen and Coulson (1977), p. 5464, chase-omission observation and Figure 1 caption; p. 5466, Results: weak T bands, unresolved runs, sequence comparisons and structure-dependent migration](dna-sequencing-with-chain-terminating-inhibitors-by-frederick-sanger-and-others.md)
