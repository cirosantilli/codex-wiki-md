# Pressure work

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure-split.md)

Mechanical [work](work.md) transferred by a [pressure](pressure-split.md) acting through a displaced [volume](volume.md). In steady [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), the [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) contains $p/\rho$ as flow work per unit [mass](mass.md); it is not an independently conserved internal energy of an isolated parcel.
