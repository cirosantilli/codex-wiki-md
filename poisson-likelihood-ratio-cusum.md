# Poisson likelihood-ratio CUSUM

↑ **Parent:** [CUSUM](cusum.md)

For independent [Poisson](poisson-distribution.md) observations, comparing mean $\lambda_{0t}$ with $\theta\lambda_{0t}$ gives the displayed [log-likelihood ratio](log-likelihood-ratio.md). Updating a [CUSUM](cusum.md) with these scores targets the specified multiplicative increase. Its expected score is negative under the null and positive under the alternative when $\theta>1$, so resetting at zero discards earlier evidence against an increase.
