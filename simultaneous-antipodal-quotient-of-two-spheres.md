# Simultaneous antipodal quotient of two spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Product of two spheres](product-of-two-spheres.md)

For positive $p,q$, quotient $S^p\times S^q$ by $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y)$. This is a free double [covering space](covering-space.md) action. Its [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) quotient is orientable exactly when $p+q$ is even, because the product [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) is $(-1)^{p+q+2}$. If $p,q\ge2$, the product is [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) and the quotient has [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) $\mathbb Z/2$. For $p=q=2$, the quotient has integral [cohomology groups](cohomology-group.md) $\mathbb Z$ in degrees $0,4$, $\mathbb Z/2$ in degrees $2,3$, and zero elsewhere. Every positive-degree [cup product](cup-product.md) is zero. These conclusions follow from [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md), the [Euler characteristic under a finite covering](euler-characteristic-under-a-finite-covering.md), and the [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md).
