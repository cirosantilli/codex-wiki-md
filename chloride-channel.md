# Chloride channel

↑ **Parent:** [Ion channel](ion-channel.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloride_channel)

An [ion channel](ion-channel.md) that conducts chloride and, for some family members, other anions. Members differ in voltage dependence and physiological role; an anion channel is distinct from a coupled anion exchanger.
