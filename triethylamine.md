# Triethylamine

↑ **Parent:** [Amine](amine.md)

[Triethylamine](triethylamine.md) is a tertiary [amine](amine.md), $\mathrm{NEt_3}$, often used to capture acid generated during substitution. Protonation gives triethylammonium.
