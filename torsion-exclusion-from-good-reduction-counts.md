# Torsion exclusion from good reduction counts

↑ **Parent:** [Reduction of torsion points on an elliptic curve](reduction-of-torsion-points-on-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) over $\mathbb Q$, reduction at a good prime $p$ is injective on the $\ell$-primary [torsion subgroup](torsion-subgroup.md) when $\ell\ne p$: its kernel is a [formal group](formal-group-law.md), where multiplication by $\ell$ is bijective. Hence a rational point of order $\ell$ forces $\ell\mid\#\widetilde E(\mathbb F_p)$. If for every possible prime $\ell$ some good $p\ne\ell$ has a point count not divisible by $\ell$, rational torsion is trivial. In particular two distinct good primes $p,r$ with coprime counts, $p\nmid\#\widetilde E(\mathbb F_r)$ and $r\nmid\#\widetilde E(\mathbb F_p)$ suffice: the coprime counts exclude other primes, and the two cross conditions exclude $p,r$. This argument does not incorrectly apply prime-to-$p$ injectivity to $p$-power torsion.
