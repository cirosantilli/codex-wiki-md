# Obstruction to norm-minus-one units from a prime congruent to three modulo four

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental unit (number theory)](fundamental-unit-number-theory.md)

If a squarefree positive $d$ has a prime divisor $p\equiv3\pmod4$, a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) of norm $-1$ in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$ would give $a^2-db^2=-4$ for integers $a,b$. Modulo $p$ this makes $-1$ a [quadratic residue](quadratic-residue.md), which is impossible. The obstruction is sufficient, not a full criterion for negative-norm units.
