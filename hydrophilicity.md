# Hydrophilicity

↑ **Parent:** [Physical chemistry](physical-chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrophilicity)

[Hydrophilicity](hydrophilicity.md) describes affinity for [water](water.md). Regions with [chemical polarity](chemical-polarity.md) or [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) can interact favorably with [water](water.md), including through [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md) where suitable groups are present. [Hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md) regions of membrane-forming [phospholipids](phospholipid.md) face the [water](water.md) on either side of a [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md). Such affinity does not mean forming [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md) with every neighboring substance, nor does it require a net negative [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). See [Alberts et al.: the chemical components of a cell](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26883/).
