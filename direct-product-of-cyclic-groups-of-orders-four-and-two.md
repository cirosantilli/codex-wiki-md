# Direct product of cyclic groups of orders four and two

↑ **Parent:** [Direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md)

This [abelian group](abelian-group.md) has eight elements $c^ra^s$, with $r$ taken modulo four and $s$ modulo two. Its [group operation](group-operation.md) adds these exponent pairs separately. The elements of order two are $c^2,a,c^2a$, and the other four nonidentity elements $c,c^3,ca,c^3a$ have order four. It is a [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) rather than a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md), since no element has order eight.
