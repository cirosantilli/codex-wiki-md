# Brittle fracture

↑ **Parent:** [Elasticity (physics)](elasticity-physics-split.md)

Failure by crack formation or propagation with limited distributed permanent deformation. Shear and opening fractures have different strength conditions; a frictional [failure envelope](failure-envelope.md) should not be extrapolated indefinitely into tension.
