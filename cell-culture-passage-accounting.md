# Cell culture passage accounting

↑ **Parent:** [Subculture](subculture.md)

In an original constructed example, seed $300\,000$ [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) in $6\,\mathrm{mL}$ of [cell culture medium](cell-culture-medium.md). Suppose the recoverable count becomes $1\,200\,000$ before the next [subculture](subculture.md). Transfer a well-mixed quarter of that harvest into a new vessel and bring its final [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) to $6\,\mathrm{mL}$. Assume complete recovery, no loss during transfer and no division during handling. The selected portion contains

$$
N_{\mathrm{seed}}=\frac14(1\,200\,000)=300\,000,\qquad c_{\mathrm{seed}}=\frac{300\,000}{6\,\mathrm{mL}}=50\,000\,\mathrm{mL}^{-1}.
$$

The other $900\,000$ [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) remain outside the tracked vessel. The lower count after transfer is selection of a portion, not evidence of death. Adding fresh [cell culture medium](cell-culture-medium.md) lowers the [number density](number-density.md) further without changing the selected count.

**[Figure 2](#image-one-subculture-transfer-can-follow-two-cell-line-population-doublings). One subculture transfer can follow two cell line population doublings**. Original constructed count ledger, not an observed [HeLa](hela.md) growth curve. Each growth interval multiplies the recoverable count by four; each transfer retains one quarter. The tracked lineage gains four net [cell line population doublings](cell-line-population-doublings.md) across two intervals although both seed counts are $300\,000$. Boxes describe whole cultured populations, not persistent individual [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md).

If the next interval again changes $300\,000$ to $1\,200\,000$, the two growth intervals contribute $2+2=4$ net [cell line population doublings](cell-line-population-doublings.md). At the second harvest there has been just one intervening passage. Another quarter-transfer would add another passage without adding a growth interval. Thus neither passage number nor the [logarithm](logarithm.md) of the final count divided by the very first seed count records all growth when portions are removed.
