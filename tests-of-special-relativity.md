# Tests of special relativity

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tests_of_special_relativity)

[Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) connects measurable clock rates, [length contraction](length-contraction.md), light propagation, and [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) through the [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md). Tests probe particular predictions rather than measuring a coordinate transformation in isolation. Examples include [cosmic-ray muon time-dilation experiments](cosmic-ray-muon-time-dilation-experiment.md), [muon storage-ring time-dilation experiments](muon-storage-ring-time-dilation-experiment.md), the [Michelson-Morley experiment](michelson-morley-experiment.md), the [Fizeau experiment](fizeau-experiment.md), and particle-collision energy and momentum balances.
