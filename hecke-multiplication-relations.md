# Hecke multiplication relations

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md)

For level-one weight-$k$ [modular forms](modular-form.md), $T_mT_n=\sum_{d\mid\gcd(m,n)}d^{k-1}T_{mn/d^2}$. Thus coprime indices multiply directly, and $T_pT_{p^r}=T_{p^{r+1}}+p^{k-1}T_{p^{r-1}}$. The [Fourier coefficients of a composite-index Hecke operator](fourier-coefficients-of-a-composite-index-hecke-operator.md) prove these relations by regrouping divisors. They show that the [Hecke algebra of modular forms](integral-hecke-algebra-of-level-one-cusp-forms.md) is commutative and generated by prime operators.
