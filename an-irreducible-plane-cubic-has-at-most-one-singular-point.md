# An irreducible plane cubic has at most one singular point

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md)

Two distinct singular points would impose at least two zeros each on the restriction of the cubic to their joining line. A nonzero homogeneous polynomial of degree three cannot have total zero multiplicity four, so that restriction vanishes identically. The line would be a component, contradicting the [irreducible polynomial](irreducible-polynomial.md) hypothesis. This argument works in every characteristic.
