# Scan statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test.md)

A scan statistic searches a family of candidate locations or subsets and reports the largest local [statistic](statistic.md). Under a [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md), a [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) converts a tail estimate for each candidate into a bound for the maximum; no [independence](independent-random-variables.md) between local [statistics](statistic.md) is required. Structured alternatives often permit a [chi-squared testing lower bound](chi-squared-testing-lower-bound.md) using the overlap geometry of two candidates.
