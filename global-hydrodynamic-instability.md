# Global hydrodynamic instability

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

A spatially nonuniform system is globally unstable when a [normal mode](normal-mode.md) satisfying its global spatial [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) has positive temporal [growth rate](growth-rate.md). This differs from purely local temporal amplification or [convective wave-packet instability](convective-wave-packet-instability.md). A locally absolutely unstable region can be necessary without being sufficient for a growing global mode of finite spatial extent.
