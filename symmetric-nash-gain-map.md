# Symmetric Nash gain map

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric equilibrium](symmetric-equilibrium.md)

The continuous gain map takes a [probability simplex](probability-simplex.md) to itself. The [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) supplies a fixed point, whose positive gains would contradict the zero strategy-weighted average payoff excess. Thus all gains vanish, giving a strategy that is a [best response](best-response.md) to itself. In a [symmetric bimatrix game](symmetric-bimatrix-game.md), this produces a [symmetric equilibrium](symmetric-equilibrium.md).
