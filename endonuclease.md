# Endonuclease

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A [nuclease](nuclease.md) that cuts internal bonds of a nucleic-acid chain. For an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) substrate, internal cleavage can create new ends for further degradation.
