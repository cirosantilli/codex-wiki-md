# Translation reinitiation

↑ **Parent:** [Translation initiation](translation-initiation.md)

Initiation after a preceding [biological translation](translation-biology.md) event on the same [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md), often involving retention of a small subunit and reacquisition of required factors. Upstream reading-frame effects depend on these kinetics.
