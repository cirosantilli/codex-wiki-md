# Absolute-error loss

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical decision theory](statistical-decision-theory.md)

The loss $|a-\theta|$ for estimating a real parameter $\theta$ by $a$. Its [risk of a decision rule](risk-of-a-decision-rule.md) is the corresponding [expected value](expected-value.md). Unlike [squared-error loss](squared-error-loss.md), its pointwise size is a [metric](metric.md), so the triangle inequality gives a direct overlap version of the [Le Cam two-point lemma](le-cam-two-point-lemma.md).
