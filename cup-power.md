# Cup power

↑ **Parent:** [Cup product](cup-product.md)

For a [cohomology class](cohomology-class.md) $a$, its rth cup power is its r-fold product in the [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md). Naturality gives $f^*(a^r)=(f^*a)^r$. A nilpotent class has some positive cup power zero; the nilpotence order is preserved by ring isomorphisms and can obstruct a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md) or a nonzero [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md).
