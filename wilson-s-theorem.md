<h1 id="wilson-s-theorem">Wilson's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilson's_theorem)

For a prime $p$,

$$
(p-1)!\equiv-1\pmod p.
$$

Every nonzero residue other than $1$ and $-1$ pairs with a distinct multiplicative inverse, leaving only $1(-1)$ in the product.
