# Fredholm determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Trace-class operator](trace-class-operator.md)

For a trace-class operator $K$ on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), the Fredholm determinant is the convergent product over its eigenvalues, counted with algebraic multiplicity. For self-adjoint $K$, [spectral theorem for compact self-adjoint operators](spectral-theorem-for-compact-hermitian-operators.md) reduces the product to a diagonal calculation. For $\|K\|<1$, $\log\det(I+K)=\sum_{n\geq1}(-1)^{n+1}\operatorname{tr}(K^n)/n$. This identity exposes the successive [cumulants](cumulant.md) in a [Gaussian quadratic exponential moment](gaussian-quadratic-exponential-moment.md).
