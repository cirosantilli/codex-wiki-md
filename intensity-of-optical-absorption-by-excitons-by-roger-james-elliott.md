# Intensity of Optical Absorption by Excitons by Roger James Elliott

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

[https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.108.1384](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.108.1384)

The primary theory treats near-edge optical absorption using the effective-mass exciton model. It distinguishes direct transitions from phonon-assisted indirect ones and predicts bound lines below the free-pair continuum.
