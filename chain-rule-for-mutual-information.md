# Chain rule for mutual information

↑ **Parent:** [Mutual information](mutual-information.md)

The information supplied by two observations decomposes as

$$
I(X;Y_1,Y_2)=I(X;Y_1)+I(X;Y_2\mid Y_1).
$$
