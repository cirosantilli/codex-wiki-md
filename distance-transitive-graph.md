# Distance-transitive graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distance-transitive_graph)

An automorphism group is transitive on ordered pairs of vertices at every fixed distance. [Grassmann graphs](grassmann-graph.md) provide examples with the finite general linear group as a distance-transitive automorphism group.
