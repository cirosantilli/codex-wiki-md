# Weyl field

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md)

A spacetime field valued in one irreducible chiral [spinor](spinor.md) representation. A free massless left-handed [Weyl field](weyl-field.md) obeys $i(\partial_t-\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\boldsymbol\nabla)\chi_L=0$. The [left-handed massless plane-wave spinor](left-handed-massless-plane-wave-spinor.md) and [helicity content of a quantized Weyl field](helicity-content-of-a-quantized-weyl-field.md) describe its modes and particle states. Field chirality fixes one spinor representation; it does not give the same particle and antiparticle helicity.
