# Compact parabolic trial bound for the quantum harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md)

For the dimensionless [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) $H=-d^2/dx^2+x^2$, the normalized compact trial function is $\psi_a=\sqrt{15/(16a^5)}(a^2-x^2)$ on $|x|\leq a$ and zero elsewhere. Its kinetic and potential contributions to the energy [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) are $5/(2a^2)$ and $a^2/7$. Minimizing their sum gives $a^4=35/2$ and the displayed upper bound, approximately $1.19523$, compared with exact [ground state](ground-state.md) eigenvalue $1$. The [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md) is square-integrable because the trial is continuous at its endpoints. Jumps in the ordinary first derivative do not invalidate the [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md): it requires the energy form domain, rather than the smaller [operator domain](operator-domain.md).
