# Rapidly rotating saddle confinement

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating saddle trap](rotating-saddle-trap.md)

In rapid rotation, both rotating-frame modes are nearly circular and counter-rotate relative to the axes at [frequency](frequency.md) near $|\Omega|$. Transforming back to the laboratory leaves a slow nearly harmonic oscillation and small rapid micromotion. For positive rotation the slow ellipse also has higher-order prograde precession at rate $\omega^4/(8\Omega^3)$. These follow by expanding $\sqrt{\Omega^2\pm\omega^2}$ and combining the two laboratory circular components.[preprint pp. 2 and 6–7, model distinction and equations (12)–(18)](rotating-saddle-trap-as-foucault-s-pendulum-by-oleg-n-kirillov-and-mark-levi.md)
