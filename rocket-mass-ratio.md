# Rocket mass ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

The [rocket mass ratio](rocket-mass-ratio.md) compares the initial and final total rocket [masses](mass.md) for a burn. It is [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md), and the ideal [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) becomes $\Delta v=u\ln R$. For the same fixed [rocket exhaust speed](rocket-exhaust-speed.md), two successive burns with [rocket mass ratios](rocket-mass-ratio.md) $R_1$ and $R_2$ have combined [rocket mass ratio](rocket-mass-ratio.md) $R_1R_2$ and total [velocity](velocity.md) gain $u\ln R_1+u\ln R_2$.
