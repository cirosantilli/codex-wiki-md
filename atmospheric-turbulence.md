# Atmospheric turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric optics](atmospheric-optics.md)

Atmospheric turbulence produces changing [refractive index](refractive-index.md) fluctuations. These alter [optical path lengths](optical-path-length.md), distorting [wavefronts](wavefront.md) and causing [astronomical seeing](astronomical-seeing.md) and [atmospheric scintillation](scintillation-astronomy.md).
