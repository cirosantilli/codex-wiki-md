# Invariant primitive under a finite group action

↑ **Parent:** [Antipodal map](antipodal-map.md)

Let a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$ act smoothly on a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), and suppose that a $G$-invariant [differential form](differential-form-split.md) $\omega$ is an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md), say $\omega=d\eta$. Averaging gives the invariant primitive

$$
\bar\eta=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}g^*\eta,
\qquad d\bar\eta=\omega.
$$

If the action is free, invariant forms descend uniquely through the resulting [covering map](covering-space.md).
