# Planetary equilibrium temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Irradiated planetary atmosphere](irradiated-planetary-atmosphere.md)

For a planet of [Bond albedo](bond-albedo.md) $A_B$ orbiting a [blackbody](blackbody.md) star of radius $R_*$ and temperature $T_*$, complete global redistribution and negligible internal heat give

$$
T_{\rm eq}^4=(1-A_B)T_*^4\frac{R_*^2}{4a^2}.
$$

Uniform dayside-only reradiation replaces $4a^2$ by $2a^2$. The [planetary equilibrium temperature](planetary-equilibrium-temperature.md) describes absorbed stellar power; including an intrinsic cooling flux gives a total [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md) $T_{\rm eff}^4=T_{\rm int}^4+T_{\rm eq}^4$, distinct from $T_{\rm eq}$. Neither quantity is necessarily the greenhouse-warmed surface temperature.
