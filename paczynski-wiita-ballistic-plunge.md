<h1 id="paczynski-wiita-ballistic-plunge">Paczyński-Wiita ballistic plunge</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Paczyński-Wiita circular orbit](paczynski-wiita-circular-orbit.md)

The limiting inward departure from the marginal [Paczyński-Wiita circular orbit](paczynski-wiita-circular-orbit.md) at $r=3a$ conserves the displayed [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) and [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md). In the [Paczyński-Wiita potential](paczynski-wiita-potential.md), its inward speed satisfies $v_r^2=GM(3a-r)^3/[4ar^2(r-a)]$. The exactly marginal circular orbit does not depart without a perturbation; the formula describes the separatrix limit of inward releases.
