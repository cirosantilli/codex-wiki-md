# Integrability

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md)

A real or complex [measurable function](measurable-function.md) is integrable for a [measure](measure.md) $\mu$ when its absolute value has finite integral. For a [random variable](random-variable-split.md), this is integrability with respect to its [probability measure](probability-measure.md), and makes its [expectation](expected-value.md) finite.
