# Rotor radius

↑ **Parent:** [Centrifuge rotor](centrifuge-rotor.md)

The [rotor radius](rotor-radius.md) $r$ is the perpendicular distance from the rotation axis to a specified sample position, not the tube's length. A filled tube spans a range $r_{\min}\leq r\leq r_{\max}$. Consequently, one rotational speed produces different local [centrifugal accelerations](centrifugal-acceleration.md) along that range. A reported [relative centrifugal force](relative-centrifugal-force.md) needs a stated reference position, such as the outermost sample radius.
