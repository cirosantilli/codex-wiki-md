# Quadratic-map conjugacy to the full tent map

↑ **Parent:** [Real quadratic iteration map](real-quadratic-iteration-map.md)

The [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) $H(u)=-2\cos(\pi u)$ maps $[0,1]$ to $[-2,2]$ and satisfies $H\circ T=f\circ H$, where $T$ is the full [tent map](tent-map.md) and $f(x)=2-x^2$. This transfers [dense periodic points](dense-periodic-points.md), [topological mixing](topological-mixing.md) and [sensitivity](butterfly-effect.md) to the [quadratic interval map](real-quadratic-iteration-map.md). The related circle [map](discrete-dynamical-system-split.md) $h(y)=-2\cos(2\pi y)$ only gives a [semiconjugacy](semiconjugacy-of-dynamical-systems.md) from doubling, because $h(y)=h(1-y)$ and is not [injective](injective-function.md).
