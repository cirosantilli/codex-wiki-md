<h1 id="meszaros-equation">Mészáros equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mészáros effect](meszaros-effect.md)

In a flat universe containing [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) and smooth [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md), put $y=a/a_{\rm eq}$ and $C=H_0^2\Omega_{m,0}^2/\Omega_{r,0}$. The [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $\mathcal H^2=C(1+y)/y^2$, so $y'^2=C(1+y)$ and $y''=C/2$. The subhorizon [cosmological Poisson equation](cosmological-poisson-equation.md) gives $\delta''+\mathcal H\delta'=3C\delta/(2y)$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) produces the displayed equation. Smoothing radiation neglects its rapid acoustic forcing after horizon entry; it is not an exact treatment of all radiation perturbations.
