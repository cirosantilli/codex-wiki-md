# Gaussian phase averaging

↑ **Parent:** [Random phase screen](random-phase-screen.md)

For a centered real [Gaussian random variable](gaussian-random-variable.md) $\phi$, its [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) gives $\mathbb E e^{i\phi}=\exp[-\operatorname{Var}(\phi)/2]$. Applied to a [random phase screen](random-phase-screen.md), this controls the [coherent and diffuse wave fields](coherent-and-diffuse-wave-fields.md).
