# Blackbody ionizing photon function

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen-ionizing photon production rate](hydrogen-ionizing-photon-production-rate.md)

A spherical [blackbody](blackbody.md) star emits $Q(E_0)=8\pi^2R_s^2(kT_s)^3F(E_0/kT_s)/(h^3c^2)$ photons per time above threshold $E_0$. Integrating [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) over the outward hemisphere gives flux $\pi B_\nu$; dividing the resulting [spectral luminosity](spectral-luminosity.md) by photon energy and substituting $x=h\nu/kT_s$ proves the formula. The convergent series $F(y)=\sum_{m\geq1}e^{-my}(y^2/m+2y/m^2+2/m^3)$ follows by integrating the geometric-series expansion termwise.
