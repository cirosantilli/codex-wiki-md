# U-statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Estimator](estimator.md)

A [U-statistic](u-statistic.md) averages a symmetric function of $m$ distinct sample observations over all unordered $m$-tuples. For [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) observations it is an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of $\mathbb E H(X_1,\ldots,X_m)$. Omitting repeated indices can remove diagonal contributions in quadratic plug-in [estimators](estimator.md).
