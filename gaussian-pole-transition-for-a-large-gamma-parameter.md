# Gaussian pole transition for a large gamma parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Error-function smoothing of a Stokes multiplier](error-function-smoothing-of-a-stokes-multiplier.md)

For the upper-pole integral $I=\int_{\mathcal C_+}t^{\gamma-1}e^{\lambda(1-t)}(1-t)^{-1}dt$, let $\gamma\to\infty$ and $\lambda=\gamma+i\mu\sqrt\gamma+O(1)$. Scaling $t=1+s/\sqrt\gamma$ gives $-\int_{\mathcal C_+}e^{-s^2/2-i\mu s}ds/s$ at leading order. At $\mu=0$ its indentation is $i\pi$. Differentiation with respect to $\mu$ gives $i\sqrt{2\pi}e^{-\mu^2/2}$, so integration gives the displayed [error function](error-function.md). This is an [optimal truncation](optimal-truncation.md) transition; the underlying analytic integral has no literal discontinuity. The general mechanism is discussed in [the DLMF account of Stokes smoothing](https://dlmf.nist.gov/2.11#iv).
