# Elastic Bragg scattering condition

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal scattering](crystal-scattering.md)

For elastic scattering with $k-k'=q\in\Lambda^*$ and $|k|=|k'|=k$,

$$
2k\cdot q=|q|^2,
\qquad
|q|=2k\sin\frac\theta2.
$$

The smallest nonzero diffraction angle is determined by the shortest reciprocal-lattice vector.

This reciprocal-lattice condition is equivalent to [Bragg's law](bragg-s-law.md), expressed in terms of the lattice-plane spacing and the wavelength.
