# Gompertz distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Survival analysis](survival-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gompertz_distribution)

A positive, exponentially increasing [hazard function](hazard-function.md) $h(t)=qe^{\beta t}$ gives the [survivor function](survival-function.md)

$$
S(t)=\exp\!\left[-\frac q\beta(e^{\beta t}-1)\right],\qquad q,\beta>0.
$$

Its mean is $\beta^{-1}e^{q/\beta}E_1(q/\beta)$, where $E_1$ is the [exponential integral](exponential-integral.md). Unlike the [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md), its future mean waiting time is not the reciprocal of its current [hazard function](hazard-function.md).
