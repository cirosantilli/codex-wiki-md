# Inversion in a circle

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized circle under a Möbius transformation](generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md)

[Inversion in a circle](inversion-in-a-circle.md) of centre $a$ and radius $R$ is an orientation-reversing involution of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) fixing that circle pointwise and exchanging its interior and exterior. It is an anti-[Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md). Reflection in a line is the corresponding inversion for a [generalized circle](generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md) through infinity. A composition of two such inversions is a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md).
