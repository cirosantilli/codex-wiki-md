# Carbon-oxygen core

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

A carbon-oxygen core is the product of [core helium burning](core-helium-burning.md). After central helium exhaustion it has little [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) or [helium](helium.md), while helium-rich and hydrogen-rich layers surround it.
