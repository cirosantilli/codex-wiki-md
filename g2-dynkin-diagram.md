# G2 Dynkin diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md)

The finite [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) $G_2$ has two vertices joined by a triple bond, with the arrow toward the [short root](short-root.md). The squared root-length ratio is three. It represents the [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md).
