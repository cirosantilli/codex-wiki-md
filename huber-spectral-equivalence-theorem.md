# Huber spectral equivalence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Length spectrum](length-spectrum.md)

For closed [hyperbolic surfaces](hyperbolic-surface.md) of curvature $-1$, equality of the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) [spectra](spectrum-functional-analysis.md), including multiplicities, is equivalent to equality of the unmarked [length spectra](length-spectrum.md) of closed [geodesics](geodesic.md). The [Selberg trace formula](selberg-trace-formula.md) proves the equivalence. Primitive lengths with multiplicities and the multiset including their iterates determine one another, provided the same orientation convention is used.
