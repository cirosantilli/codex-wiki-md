# Kinetic term

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_term)

A kinetic term contains derivatives of a field and determines its free propagation. Its quadratic differential operator becomes the inverse [quantum field theory propagator](propagator.md).
