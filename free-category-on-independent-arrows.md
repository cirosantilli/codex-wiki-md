# Free category on independent arrows

↑ **Parent:** [Category of small categories](category-of-small-categories.md)

Take two distinct objects and one nonidentity arrow for each element of $S$, without arrows between different copies. A [functor](functor.md) from this category to $\mathcal C$ is exactly a choice of one arbitrary arrow of $\mathcal C$ for each element of $S$. Consequently this construction is [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) to the functor taking the set of all arrows of a small category. Its free object has three arrows per generator, including the two identity arrows.
