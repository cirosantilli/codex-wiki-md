# K-theory Euler class

↑ **Parent:** [K-theory Thom class](k-theory-thom-class.md)

Pulling the K-theory Thom class back along the zero section gives

$$
e^K(E)=\Lambda_{-1}(\overline E)
=\sum_j(-1)^j[\Lambda^j\overline E].
$$
