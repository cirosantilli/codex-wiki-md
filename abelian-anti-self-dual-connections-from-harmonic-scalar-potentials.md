# Abelian anti-self-dual connections from harmonic scalar potentials

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-self-dual connection](anti-self-dual-connection.md)

Locally a [U(1) connection](u-1-connection.md) whose [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) has no $(2,0)$ or $(0,2)$ part can be written $A=\partial u+\bar\partial v$ by the [Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma](dolbeault-poincare-lemma.md). Unitarity permits $v=-\bar u$. Writing $u=p+iq$, a [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) removes $i\,dq$ and leaves $A=\partial p-\bar\partial p$. Setting $f=-2p$ gives the displayed [unitary](unitary-connection.md) form, whose [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) is $\partial\bar\partial f$. Its remaining [anti-self-duality of gauge curvature](anti-self-duality-of-gauge-curvature.md) condition is the scalar [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md). Alternatively a complex [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) by $e^{-u}$ gives $A=\bar\partial(v-u)$; this second gauge generally does not preserve unitarity.
