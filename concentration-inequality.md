# Concentration inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Probability inequality](probability-inequality-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concentration_inequality)

A concentration inequality bounds the probability that a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) differs substantially from a typical value such as its [expected value](expected-value.md) or [median](median.md).
