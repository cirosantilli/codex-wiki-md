# Exponential convergence of a Morse gradient flow

↑ **Parent:** [Morse function](morse-function.md)

The [Morse lemma](morse-lemma.md) gives coordinates $f=f(z)-|u|^2+|v|^2$. Choose a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) Euclidean in these coordinates. A downward [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md) converging to $z$ has $u=0$ eventually, while $v(t)=e^{-2(t-t_0)}v(t_0)$. Thus its [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md) from $z$ decays exponentially. A [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) makes this a global metric choice; changing the metric may change the trajectories.
