# Pressure-truncated singular isothermal gas sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md)

For $p=a_T^2\rho$, spherical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) admits $\rho=a_T^2/(2\pi Gr^2)$ with $M(r)=2a_T^2r/G$. Matching an external [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p_0$ fixes $r_s=a_T^2/\sqrt{2\pi Gp_0}$. The central [density](density.md) is singular although the enclosed [mass](mass.md) tends to zero; this is one branch, not every finite-centred isothermal cloud.
