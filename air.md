# Air

↑ **Parent:** [Mixture](mixture-split.md)

The atmospheric gas [mixture](mixture-split.md), principally [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) and [oxygen](oxygen.md) near the Earth's surface, with variable [water vapour](water-vapor.md) and smaller amounts of other components. Gas [relative density](relative-density.md) comparisons conventionally specify dry air as the reference.
