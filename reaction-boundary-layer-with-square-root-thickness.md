# Reaction boundary layer with square-root thickness

↑ **Parent:** [Matched asymptotic expansion](matched-asymptotic-expansion.md)

For a positive small parameter $\epsilon$, an order-one mismatch between the reduced solution $y_0=f(x)$ and a prescribed right endpoint value $y(1)=b$ creates a right [boundary layer](boundary-layer.md). Balancing $\epsilon y''$ against the order-one reaction term gives width $\sqrt\epsilon$. In the [inner variable](inner-variable.md) $X=(1-x)/\sqrt\epsilon$, the leading equation is $Y_0''=Y_0-f(1)$. Its boundary and matching conditions are $Y_0(0)=b$ and $Y_0\to f(1)$ as $X\to\infty$, so

$$
Y_0(X)=f(1)+(b-f(1))e^{-X}.
$$

The leading [additive composite expansion](additive-composite-expansion.md) is $f(x)+(b-f(1))e^{-(1-x)/\sqrt\epsilon}$, obtained by subtracting the common overlap value. For the nonlinear perturbation $\epsilon y''=y-x^2+\epsilon(2+y^2)$ with zero endpoint values, the first two [outer expansion](outer-expansion.md) terms are $x^2-\epsilon x^4$, and the leading right-layer term is $1-e^{-X}$.
