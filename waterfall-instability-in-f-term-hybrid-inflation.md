# Waterfall instability in F-term hybrid inflation

↑ **Parent:** [F-term hybrid inflation](f-term-hybrid-inflation.md)

In the small-field global approximation, the waterfall fluctuations on $u=v=0$ have the displayed masses. One becomes negative when $|S|^2<|\zeta|$, driving the fields towards the [supersymmetric vacuum](supersymmetric-vacuum.md) branch. In the canonical supergravity model, the exact masses follow from expanding the exponential and Kähler-covariant derivatives; their Planck-suppressed corrections should not be discarded unless the corresponding field and symmetry-breaking scales are small.
