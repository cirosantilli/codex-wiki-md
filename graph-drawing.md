# Graph drawing

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

A [graph drawing](graph-drawing.md) represents [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) by distinct points in the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md) and [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) by curves joining their endpoints. A crossing of [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) is not an extra [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) unless we explicitly change the [graph](graph-split.md) to make it one. Curves may bend: the [graph](graph-split.md) records connections, not coordinates.
