# Complex plane is not conformally equivalent to the upper half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal equivalence](conformal-equivalence.md)

Composing a hypothetical conformal equivalence $\mathbb C\to\mathbb H$ with a [Cayley transform](cayley-transform-hyperbolic-geometry.md) $\mathbb H\to\mathbb D$ would give a nonconstant bounded [entire function](entire-function.md), contradicting the [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md).
