# Planetary thermal mass

↑ **Parent:** [Type I migration](type-i-migration.md)

The scale $M_{\rm th}=h^3M_\star$ makes the Bondi [radius](radius.md) comparable with the [disk scale height](disk-scale-height.md). It organizes when [planet](planet.md)-induced disturbances become nonlinear; it is not identical to an exact universal gap-opening [mass](mass.md).
