# X-ray transform

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)

The X-ray transform records integrals of a function or tensor evaluation over a specified family of curves. The curve family, parametrization and any weights are part of the definition. Straight lines, [geodesics](geodesic.md) and [magnetic flows](magnetic-flow-on-a-riemannian-surface.md) give different versions; their injectivity and unavoidable potential kernels depend on that geometry.
