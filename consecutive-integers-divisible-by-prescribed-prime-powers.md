# Consecutive integers divisible by prescribed prime powers

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md)

Let $q_1,\ldots,q_r$ be distinct [prime numbers](prime-number-split.md) and $a_1,\ldots,a_r$ positive [integers](integer-split.md). There are infinitely many positive [integers](integer-split.md) $n$ with $q_i^{a_i}\mid n+i$ for every $i$. Indeed the moduli $q_i^{a_i}$ are pairwise [coprime](coprime-integers.md), so the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) solves $n\equiv-i\pmod{q_i^{a_i}}$ simultaneously. Adding multiples of their product gives arbitrarily large positive solutions. This allows long runs in which every [integer](integer-split.md) has a large prescribed [prime power](prime-power.md) divisor, even though neighboring [integers](integer-split.md) are [coprime](coprime-integers.md).
