# Minimal electromagnetic coupling of a Dirac field

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)

Replace the derivative in the [Dirac action](dirac-action.md) by the [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md) $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+iqA_\mu$. The interaction is then $-qA_\mu\bar\Psi\gamma^\mu\Psi$. With $\Psi\mapsto e^{-iq\alpha}\Psi$ and $A_\mu\mapsto A_\mu+\partial_\mu\alpha$, the covariant derivative transforms in the same way as the spinor, proving local [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md).
