# Dephlogisticated air

↑ **Parent:** [Oxygen](oxygen.md)

[Joseph Priestley](joseph-priestley.md)'s historical name for the unusually combustion-supporting air now associated with [oxygen](oxygen.md). The name expressed a [phlogiston theory](phlogiston-theory.md) interpretation, rather than the modern account of [chemical composition](chemical-composition.md).
