# Sparse injectivity

↑ **Parent:** [Compressed sensing](compressed-sensing.md)

A [linear map](linear-map.md) is injective on the class of [sparse vectors](sparse-vector.md) of order $s$ exactly when its [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) contains no nonzero [vector](vector.md) with at most $2s$ nonzero entries. Differences of two sparse [vectors](vector.md) have at most $2s$ active coordinates. Conversely, splitting the [support of a vector](support-of-a-vector.md) of a $2s$-sparse null [vector](vector.md) into two parts constructs two distinct $s$-sparse [vectors](vector.md) with the same image. The strict [null space property](nullspace-property.md) implies sparse injectivity by applying it to both parts of such a split.
