# Periodic domain

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)

A [periodic domain](periodic-domain.md) identifies opposite faces of a fundamental cell, producing a [flat torus](flat-torus.md). On an [interval](interval-mathematics.md) $[0,L]$, smooth [periodic boundary conditions](periodic-boundary-conditions.md) require $u(0,t)=u(L,t)$ and matching spatial [derivatives](derivative.md). These endpoints are one location, rather than two independently controlled walls. Periodic [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) has no boundary term, and zero-mean [functions](function-split.md) have no zero [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md).
