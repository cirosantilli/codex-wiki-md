# Infinite-aperture spectral normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral measure of a stationary random field](spectral-measure-of-a-stationary-random-field.md)

For the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) $\widehat E_L(\nu)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{-L/2}^{L/2}E(z)e^{-i\nu z}dz$ of a stationary random field, the periodogram measure $(2\pi/L)\mathbb E|\widehat E_L(\nu)|^2d\nu$ converges weakly to the [spectral measure of a stationary random field](spectral-measure-of-a-stationary-random-field.md). The expected squared modulus is $(4\pi^2)^{-1}\int_{-L}^L(L-|\zeta|)C(\zeta)e^{-i\nu\zeta}d\zeta$. The factor $2\pi/L$ follows from [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md), and preserves the total power per unit length. It also avoids meaningless squares of [Dirac delta distributions](dirac-delta-function.md) for coherent plane-wave components.
