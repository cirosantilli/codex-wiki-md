# Adsorption site

↑ **Parent:** [Adsorption](adsorption.md)

An [adsorption site](adsorption-site.md) is a position or local environment at which a [chemical species](chemical-species.md) can be retained at a boundary. Treating all [adsorption sites](adsorption-site.md) as identical is an assumption about their binding properties, not a definition of [adsorption](adsorption.md).
