# Carrier-envelope phase

↑ **Parent:** [Optical pulse](optical-pulse.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrier-envelope_phase)

The [carrier-envelope phase](carrier-envelope-phase.md) locates the carrier oscillation relative to the envelope of an [optical pulse](optical-pulse.md). In $E_{\rm real}(t)=F(t)\cos(\omega_0t+\phi_{\rm CE})$ with envelope maximum at zero, it is the displayed phase modulo $2\pi$. Few-cycle dynamics can depend on it even when cycle-averaged [light intensity](light-intensity.md) is unchanged. A pulse train's phase increment is related to its [carrier-envelope offset frequency](carrier-envelope-offset-frequency.md); signs depend on the complex-field convention.
