# Isothermal compressibility

↑ **Parent:** [Compressibility](compressibility.md)

The [compressibility](compressibility.md) measured at fixed [temperature](temperature.md). For a mechanically stable equilibrium it is positive; its reciprocal is the isothermal bulk modulus.
