# Automorphisms of the symmetric group

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric group](symmetric-group.md)

An automorphism preserving the transposition class is inner: commuting relations recover the vertex stars of the complete graph, with a small-case adjustment in degrees three and four. An involution class has the same size as the transposition class only in degree six, when triple transpositions also have size fifteen. Hence [outer automorphism groups](outer-automorphism-group.md) of symmetric groups are trivial outside degree six and have size at most two in degree six.
