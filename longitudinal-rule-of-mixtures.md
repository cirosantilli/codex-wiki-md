# Longitudinal rule of mixtures

↑ **Parent:** [Long-fibre composite](long-fibre-composite.md)

For continuous, parallel, perfectly bonded fibres and uniform axial [strain](strain.md), $E_L=V_fE_f+(1-V_f)E_m$. This idealization is the axial [Voigt elastic approximation](voigt-elastic-approximation.md); voids, fibre waviness and poor load transfer reduce stiffness.
