# Fixed vectors in a hook Specht module in characteristic two

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

Let $n\geq4$ and let the [coefficient](coefficient.md) [field](field.md) have [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) two. The [subgroup](subgroup.md) fixing one permutes the standard [hook](hook-of-a-young-diagram.md) [basis](basis.md) $e_{1ij}$ indexed by unordered pairs from $\{2,\ldots,n\}$. Its fixed space is the line generated by $v=\sum_{2\leq i<j\leq n}e_{1ij}$. Record a [hook](hook-of-a-young-diagram.md) [tabloid](tabloid.md) by its second- and third-row entries. In $v$, every recorded ordered pair avoiding one occurs once, and every pair involving one occurs $n-2$ times: a [polytabloid](polytabloid.md) is the sum of the six ordered pairs from its three column entries. For odd $n$, $v$ is therefore the sum of all [tabloids](tabloid.md) and is fixed by the full [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md). For even $n$, it is the sum of the [tabloids](tabloid.md) avoiding one and is moved by $(1,2)$. No other full-group fixed vector is possible because the point-stabilizer fixed space is one-dimensional. In [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two, swapping $i,j$ negates the $e_{1ij}$ [coefficient](coefficient.md) and forces it to vanish, so even the point-stabilizer fixed space is zero.
