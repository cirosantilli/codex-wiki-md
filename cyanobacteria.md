# Cyanobacteria

↑ **Parent:** [Bacteria](bacteria.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyanobacteria)

[Cyanobacteria](cyanobacteria.md) are [bacteria](bacteria.md) that perform [oxygenic photosynthesis](oxygenic-photosynthesis.md): [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md) release [oxygen](oxygen.md) from [water](water.md). Their photosynthetic machinery operates on [thylakoid](thylakoid.md) membranes without a [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) or a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md). This is a particularly useful counterexample to equating [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md) with being a [plant](plant.md). [Chloroplasts](chloroplast.md) descend from an ancestral cyanobacterial partner through [endosymbiosis](endosymbiosis.md); present-day [cyanobacteria](cyanobacteria.md) are independent descendants, rather than [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md) waiting to become [plants](plant.md). See [Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell: chloroplasts and cyanobacteria](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26819/).
