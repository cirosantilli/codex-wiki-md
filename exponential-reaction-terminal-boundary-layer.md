# Exponential-reaction terminal boundary layer

↑ **Parent:** [Outflow boundary layer](outflow-boundary-layer.md)

For $\epsilon y''-y'+e^y=0$ with $y(0)=A<0$ and $y(1)=0$, the [outer solution](outer-expansion.md) is $-\log(e^{-A}-x)$. The right-end [boundary layer](boundary-layer.md) has width $\epsilon$ and leading correction $\log(e^{-A}-1)e^{(x-1)/\epsilon}$. This follows by setting $X=(x-1)/\epsilon$, solving $Y''-Y'=0$, and matching its constant part. The leading correction changes sign at $A=-\log2$.
