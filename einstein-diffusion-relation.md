# Einstein diffusion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion](diffusion.md)

For translational drag coefficient $\zeta$ and [mobility](order-parameter-mobility.md) $1/\zeta$, [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) requires $D=k_BT/\zeta$. This connects random displacement to dissipative response and is a form of the [fluctuation-dissipation theorem](fluctuation-dissipation-theorem.md). Combining it with [Stokes drag law](stokes-s-law.md) gives the [Stokes–Einstein relation](stokes-einstein-relation.md).
