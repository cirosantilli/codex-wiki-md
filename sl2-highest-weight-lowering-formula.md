# sl2 highest-weight lowering formula

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md)

In the [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md), if $Xv=0$ and $Hv=mv$, then the [commutator](commutator.md) relations give $HY^jv=(m-2j)Y^jv$ and $XY^{k+1}v=(k+1)(m-k)Y^kv$. Induct using $XY=YX+H$. This controls the [raising operator](raising-operator.md) on a string formed by the [lowering operator](lowering-operator.md) and yields the [classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md).
