# Mitotic daughter chromosome accounting

↑ **Parent:** [Mitosis](mitosis.md)

Count the same hypothetical [diploid](diploid.md) [animal](animal.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) in three ways: [chromosomes](chromosome.md), double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md), and nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount. Assume one continuous double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md) per unreplicated [chromosome](chromosome.md) or replicated [chromatid](chromatid.md), complete [S phase](s-phase.md), and no losses or [mutations](mutation.md). Exclude [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md) [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md). Here $n=2$ is the size of one [haploid](haploid.md) set, and $C$ is its unreplicated nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount, as defined in [cell-cycle DNA content](cell-cycle-dna-content.md). A double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md) counts as one, despite containing two strands.

| Stage and compartment | [Chromosomes](chromosome.md) | Double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) | Nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One [G1 phase](g1-phase.md) parent | 4 unreplicated | 4 | $2C$ |
| One [prophase](prophase.md) or [metaphase](metaphase.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) | 4 replicated | 8 in 4 pairs of [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) | $4C$ |
| Whole undivided [anaphase](anaphase.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) | 8 separated daughter [chromosomes](chromosome.md) | 8 | $4C$ total |
| One [anaphase](anaphase.md) pole's allocation | 4 daughter [chromosomes](chromosome.md) | 4 | $2C$ allocated |
| Each daughter after [mitosis](mitosis.md) and [cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md) | 4 unreplicated | 4 | $2C$ each |

**[Figure 3](#image-four-replicated-chromosomes-eight-dna-molecules-then-four-of-each-per-daughter). Four replicated chromosomes, eight DNA molecules, then four of each per daughter**. Original accounting diagram for $2n=4$, counting nuclear [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) only. Each colored stroke represents one double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md); the small bridge in a replicated pair indicates association, not a new [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md). The eight separate strokes in the whole [anaphase](anaphase.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) are already partitioning into two groups. The final row states counts per daughter, not their sum.

The allocation can be checked copy by copy. [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) turns $A$ into $A_1,A_2$, $a$ into $a_1,a_2$, and similarly for $B,b$. One daughter receives $A_1,a_1,B_1,b_1$ and the other $A_2,a_2,B_2,b_2$. Each therefore inherits the same four [chromosome](chromosome.md) identities and the same [gene](gene.md) and [allele](allele.md) content as the unreplicated parent, under the stated assumptions. Across both daughters there are $4+4=8$ [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) and $2C+2C=4C$: partition conserves the material present after [S phase](s-phase.md).

The transient whole-cell count of eight does not mean that each daughter's [ploidy](ploidy.md) has doubled. Each completed daughter is [diploid](diploid.md), containing both homologues of each [homologous chromosome](homologous-chromosome.md) pair. This differs from separating [homologous chromosomes](homologous-chromosome.md) during [meiosis I](meiosis-i.md). Faithful [mitosis](mitosis.md) preserves a nuclear [genome](genome.md) through growth and renewal; equal inherited [genes](gene.md) need not imply equal [gene expression](gene-expression.md) or equal [cell fate](cell-fate.md). [Mutations](mutation.md) or [chromosome segregation](chromosome-segregation.md) errors can break the ideal inheritance pattern.
