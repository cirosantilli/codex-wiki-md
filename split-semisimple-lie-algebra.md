# Split semisimple Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)

A [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) over a field of [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md) is split if it has a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) whose adjoint action is simultaneously diagonalizable over that field. Its [root-space decomposition](root-space-decomposition.md) then needs no extension of scalars. The [sl2R Lie algebra](sl2r-lie-algebra.md) is split, with the real diagonal [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md). The real [special unitary Lie algebra](special-unitary-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ is not: its adjoint operators are skew-adjoint for the positive definite [inner product](inner-product.md) obtained by negating its [Killing form](killing-form.md). Thus a split adjoint operator would have to be zero.
