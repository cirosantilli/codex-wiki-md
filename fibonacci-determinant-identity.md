# Fibonacci determinant identity

↑ **Parent:** [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md)

For nonnegative $n,m,l$,

$$
F_{n+l}F_{n+m}-F_nF_{n+m+l}
=(-1)^nF_mF_l.
$$

Cassini's identity is the adjacent-index special case.
