# Accretion stream

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion flow](accretion-flow.md)

A spatially concentrated flow of transferred matter toward an accretor. In a binary it can be fed by overflow from a donor and can form an accretion-disk stream-impact hot spot when it encounters the disk.
