# Electron-positron annihilation into electron neutrinos

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-positron annihilation](electron-positron-annihilation.md)

The leading [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) gauge amplitudes are s-channel [Z boson](z-boson.md) exchange and t-channel [W boson](w-boson.md) exchange. They interfere. At collision energies well below the weak scale the [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) scales as $G_F^2s$. In an inclusive high-energy Born calculation, the forward W-exchange region gives a nonzero limiting scale of order $g^4/m_W^2$, unlike a fixed-angle estimate. Angular cuts and beam polarization can change comparisons with other annihilation channels.
