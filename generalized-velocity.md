# Generalized velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized coordinate](generalized-coordinate.md)

The [time derivative](time-derivative.md) of a [generalized coordinate](generalized-coordinate.md). It is an independent velocity argument of a mechanical [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md), but need not have the units or direction of a Cartesian [velocity](velocity.md): for an angular coordinate it is an [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md). A regular [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md) replaces these variables with the corresponding [canonical momenta](canonical-momentum.md).
