# Blackbody limit of an isothermal atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Formal solution of the radiative transfer equation](formal-solution-of-the-radiative-transfer-equation.md)

For a thermalized [radiative transfer source function](radiative-transfer-source-function.md) $S_\lambda=B_\lambda(T)$ constant along a ray, the [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) after optical-depth interval $\Delta\tau$ is $I_\lambda=B_\lambda+(I_{\rm in}-B_\lambda)e^{-\Delta\tau/\mu}$ for $\mu>0$. As $\Delta\tau\to\infty$, it tends to the [Planck function](planck-function.md), independently of a bounded boundary input. A temperature gradient is unnecessary for [blackbody](blackbody.md) emission. In a scattering medium, [local thermodynamic equilibrium](local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) alone does not guarantee $S_\lambda=B_\lambda$; thermalization must be justified.
