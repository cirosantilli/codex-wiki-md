<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Euler-Lagrange field equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is $F'(\phi)-\partial_t^2\phi+\partial_x^2\phi=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\partial_t^2\phi-\partial_x^2\phi=f(\phi).}
$$

Put $c=\cosh\beta$, $s=\sinh\beta$, $X=cx+st$ and $T=ct+sx$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\partial_t=s\partial_X+c\partial_T$ and $\partial_x=c\partial_X+s\partial_T$. Cross terms cancel and $c^2-s^2=1$, so

$$
(\partial_t^2-\partial_x^2)\phi(X,T)
=(\partial_T^2-\partial_X^2)\phi(X,T)=f(\phi(X,T)).
$$

Thus **the boosted [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) is another solution** for any constant [rapidity](../../../../../rapidity.md). The nonlinear term in the [nonlinear Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../../nonlinear-klein-gordon-equation.md) is unchanged because it is a local function of the scalar itself.
