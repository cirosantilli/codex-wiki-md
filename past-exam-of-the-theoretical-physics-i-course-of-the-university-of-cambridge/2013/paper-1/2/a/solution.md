<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the magnetic-coupling convention of the supplied [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md); electromagnetic unit factors are absorbed into the stated $e$ and $B$. Let $\alpha=m_1/M$ and $\beta=m_2/M$, so

$$
\mathbf r_1=\mathbf R-\beta\mathbf r,\qquad
\mathbf r_2=\mathbf R+\alpha\mathbf r.
$$

The [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) separates into $M\dot{\mathbf R}^2/2+\mu\dot{\mathbf r}^2/2$. The [symmetric gauge](../../../../../symmetric-gauge.md) coupling $\sum_i e_i\dot{\mathbf r}_i\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf r_i)/2$ becomes

$$
-\frac e2[\dot{\mathbf R}\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf r)
+\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf R)]
-e^*\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf r),\quad
e^*=\frac{e(m_1-m_2)}{2M}.
$$

The [scalar triple product](../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) identity gives

$$
\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf R)
=\frac d{dt}[\mathbf r\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf R)]
+\dot{\mathbf R}\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf r).
$$

Discarding $-e/2$ times that [total derivative](../../../../../total-derivative.md), which changes endpoint terms and [canonical momenta](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) but not the trajectories, leaves

$$
\boxed{L=\frac M2\dot{\mathbf R}^2+\frac\mu2\dot{\mathbf r}^2+\frac{e^2}{r}
-e^*\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf r)
-e\dot{\mathbf R}\cdot(\mathbf B\times\mathbf r).}
$$
