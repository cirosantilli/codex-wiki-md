<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For this [neutral Coulomb pair in a uniform magnetic field](../../../../../neutral-coulomb-pair-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md), the reduced [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) gives the [canonical momenta](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md)

$$
\mathbf P=M\dot{\mathbf R}-e\mathbf B\times\mathbf r,\qquad
\mathbf p=\mu\dot{\mathbf r}-e^*\mathbf B\times\mathbf r.
$$

Invert these relations and take the [Legendre transform](../../../../../convex-conjugate.md). The velocity-linear terms cancel against their [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) contributions, giving

$$
\boxed{H=\frac{|\mathbf P+e\mathbf B\times\mathbf r|^2}{2M}
+\frac{|\mathbf p+e^*\mathbf B\times\mathbf r|^2}{2\mu}-\frac{e^2}{r}.}
$$

There is no explicit time dependence, so $dH/dt=0$. There is no dependence on $\mathbf R$, so $\dot{\mathbf P}=0$. The distinction between [kinetic momentum and magnetic pseudomomentum](../../../../../kinetic-momentum-and-magnetic-pseudomomentum.md) matters: **the conserved [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is $\mathbf P$, not generally $M\dot{\mathbf R}$**. The mechanical centre-of-mass [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $M\dot{\mathbf R}=\mathbf P+e\mathbf B\times\mathbf r$ generally varies as the separation changes. A neutral pair can therefore have accelerated centre-of-mass motion in a [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md).
