<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Equilibrium requires $h=am+bm^3$. On the critical isotherm $a=0$, so

$$
\boxed{h=bm^3,\qquad m=\operatorname{sgn}(h)|h/b|^{1/3},\qquad\delta=3.}
$$

Here $\delta$ is defined by $h\propto\operatorname{sgn}(m)|m|^\delta$ at the [critical temperature](../../../../../critical-temperature.md), or equivalently $|m|\propto|h|^{1/\delta}$. The positive quartic coefficient selects a stable equilibrium, including the flat but stable zero-field [thermodynamic critical point](../../../../../thermodynamic-critical-point.md).
