<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The expansion is a local phenomenological description in a small scalar [order parameter](../../../../../order-parameter.md) near a transition. Its coefficients summarize microscopic physics and generally depend smoothly on [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). At zero field, spin-reversal [Z2 symmetry](../../../../../z2-symmetry.md) requires even powers of [magnetization](../../../../../magnetization.md); the conjugate field supplies the term $-hm$. The additive $F_0$ does not affect minimization with respect to $m$.

For the ordinary continuous-transition quartic model, **$b>0$ ensures stability**, and the quadratic coefficient changes sign, $a=a_0t+O(t^2)$ with $a_0>0$. If $b$ vanishes or is negative, stabilizing higher powers are required and the phase structure can change. Treating one uniform [magnetization](../../../../../magnetization.md) neglects spatial gradients, fluctuations and domains; the resulting exponents are predictions of [mean-field theory](../../../../../mean-field-theory.md).
