<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $G=g/\ell$ and $s=\kappa/m$. Applying the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) to the [quadratic Lagrangian](../../../../../quadratic-lagrangian.md) gives

$$
\boxed{2\ddot\theta+\ddot\phi+(2G-s)\theta+s\phi=0,\qquad
\ddot\theta+\ddot\phi+s\theta+(G-s)\phi=0.}
$$

A nonzero motion satisfying the specified equal-and-opposite initial displacements can have one [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) only if $(1,-1)$ is a normal-mode [vector](../../../../../vector.md). Its acceleration terms are proportional to $(1,0)$, while the restoring terms are proportional to $(2G-2s,2s-G)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{s=G/2,\qquad \kappa=\frac{mg}{2\ell},\qquad \omega^2=G,}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\theta(t)=\xi\cos(\sqrt{g/\ell}\,t),\qquad
\phi(t)=-\xi\cos(\sqrt{g/\ell}\,t).}
$$

The rods swing oppositely. The hinge [mass](../../../../../mass.md) moves sideways, while the final [mass](../../../../../mass.md) has no first-order displacement: the two horizontal contributions cancel and its vertical change is quadratic in angle. In the corresponding geometric ansatz it moves vertically at twice the small-oscillation [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) to leading nonzero order, but the displayed single-frequency solution is a linearized result. The trivial case $\xi=0$ imposes no parameter condition.
