<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Measure both [angles](../../../../../angle.md) from the downward vertical, as in the PDF. With the support as origin and downward coordinate $z$,

$$
\mathbf r_B=\ell(\sin\theta,\cos\theta),\qquad
\mathbf r_C=\ell(\sin\theta+\sin\phi,\cos\theta+\cos\phi).
$$

Thus $|AC|^2=2\ell^2[1+\cos(\theta-\phi)]$. The requested expression implicitly uses a **[zero-rest-length spring](../../../../../zero-rest-length-spring.md)**: its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $\kappa|AC|^2/2$. The exact [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) for equal [masses](../../../../../mass.md) is

$$
L=m\ell^2\dot\theta^2+\frac{m\ell^2}{2}\dot\phi^2
+m\ell^2\dot\theta\dot\phi\cos(\theta-\phi)
+mg\ell(2\cos\theta+\cos\phi)
-\kappa\ell^2[1+\cos(\theta-\phi)].
$$

Expand the [cosines](../../../../../cosine.md) and count [angles](../../../../../angle.md) and [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) as first order. The cosine correction multiplying $\dot\theta\dot\phi$ is fourth order and is dropped. After removing constants,

$$
\boxed{L_2=\frac{m\ell^2}{2}(2\dot\theta^2+2\dot\theta\dot\phi+\dot\phi^2)
-\frac{mg\ell}{2}(2\theta^2+\phi^2)
+\frac{\kappa\ell^2}{2}(\theta-\phi)^2.}
$$

The positive [spring](../../../../../spring.md) term in $L_2$ means the [spring](../../../../../spring.md) lowers its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) when the straight assembly bends. If its natural length were $s_0$, this term would instead be $\kappa\ell(2\ell-s_0)(\theta-\phi)^2/4$. An unspecified ordinary natural length cannot simply be ignored; the displayed target fixes the zero-length idealization.
