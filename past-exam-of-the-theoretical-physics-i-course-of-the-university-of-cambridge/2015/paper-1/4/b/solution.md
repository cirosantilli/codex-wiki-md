<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Adding a constant to $\psi_y$ changes neither its time nor space derivatives, so the [action](../../../../../action.md) is invariant. The associated Noether density and flux are

$$
j^0_y=\rho\dot\psi_y,\qquad j^x_y=-F\psi_y'.
$$

The [wave equation](../../../../../wave-equation-split.md) gives $\partial_tj^0_y+\partial_xj^x_y=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P_y=\int\rho\dot\psi_y\,dx,\qquad
\frac{dP_y}{dt}=F[\psi_y']_{\rm left}^{\rm right}.}
$$

This is total physical transverse momentum. It is conserved for a whole isolated infinite string with vanishing boundary flux, periodic [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md), or endpoints exerting no net transverse force. For a finite subsystem momentum can flow through its ends, and fixed supports can exchange momentum with the string. Thus the local [conservation law](../../../../../conservation-law.md) always holds in this model, while constancy of the integrated charge requires the stated [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md).
