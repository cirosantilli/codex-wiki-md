<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [canonical momenta](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) are

$$
p_r=m\dot r,\qquad p_\phi=mr^2\dot\phi+\frac{qBr^2}{2}.
$$

The [Legendre transform](../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) cancels the velocity-linear magnetic term, giving the energy expressed in velocities,

$$
\boxed{H=\frac m2\dot r^2+\frac{mr^2}{2}\dot\phi^2+2\pi^2k(r-r_0)^2.}
$$

But [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) differentiate with respect to canonical coordinates and momenta, not velocities. In its proper [canonical variables](../../../../../canonical-variables.md) the same [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is

$$
\boxed{H(r,\phi,p_r,p_\phi)
=\frac{p_r^2}{2m}+
\frac{(p_\phi-qBr^2/2)^2}{2mr^2}
+2\pi^2k(r-r_0)^2.}
$$

It explicitly contains $B$ because canonical [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) differs from mechanical [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md). This reconciles magnetic forces with the absence of magnetic work: the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) can exchange radial and tangential motion without changing the total [mechanical energy](../../../../../mechanical-energy.md).
