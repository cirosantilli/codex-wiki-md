<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Time-translation symmetry conserves the energy $H$, and axial rotational symmetry makes $\phi$ a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md), conserving

$$
\boxed{J=p_\phi=mr^2\dot\phi+\frac{qBr^2}{2}.}
$$

The radial [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) before elimination is

$$
m\ddot r=mr\dot\phi^2+qBr\dot\phi-4\pi^2k(r-r_0).
$$

Insert $\dot\phi=J/(mr^2)-qB/(2m)$. The cross terms linear in $JqB$ cancel, leaving

$$
\boxed{m\ddot r=\frac{J^2}{mr^3}-\frac{q^2B^2}{4m}r-4\pi^2k(r-r_0).}
$$

Equivalently use the reduced radial potential

$$
U_J(r)=\frac{J^2}{2mr^2}+\frac{q^2B^2r^2}{8m}
+2\pi^2k(r-r_0)^2-\frac{JqB}{2m}.
$$

The last term is constant at fixed $J$ and has no radial derivative, but is part of the full conserved energy. Conservation of $J$ is stated in the rotationally [symmetric gauge](../../../../../symmetric-gauge.md); the conserved mechanical combination can be written in terms of [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) in a gauge-independent way.
