<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In [mean-field theory](../../../../../mean-field-theory.md) all sites have the same normalized single-site distribution $e^{x\cos\theta}/Z_1(x)$. Consequently

$$
\langle S_{kz}\rangle=\frac{d\log Z_1}{dx}
=\coth x-\frac1x\equiv\mathcal L(x),
$$

where $\mathcal L$ is the [Langevin function](../../../../../langevin-function.md). Requiring the average used to construct the field to equal the average in that field gives

$$
\boxed{S=\coth(\beta z_cJS)-\frac1{\beta z_cJS}.}
$$

Set

$$
\boxed{x=\beta z_cJS=\frac{4JS}{k_BT},\qquad
\tau=\frac1{\beta z_cJ}=\frac{k_BT}{4J}.}
$$

Since $S=\tau x$, the [self-consistency equation](../../../../../self-consistency-equation.md) is exactly **$\tau x=\coth x-1/x$**. It also follows by stationarity of $F_{\rm MF}/N=z_cJS^2/2-k_BT\log Z_1(\beta z_cJS)$. This independent route checks the constant term and all neighbor-counting factors in the [partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md).
