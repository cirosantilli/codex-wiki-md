<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take positive $\theta$ toward positive $x$ from the downward vertical, as in the source diagram. The mass has position and velocity

$$
\mathbf r=(a_x+l\sin\theta,\ a_y-l\cos\theta,0),\qquad
\dot{\mathbf r}=(\dot a_x+l\dot\theta\cos\theta,\ \dot a_y+l\dot\theta\sin\theta,0).
$$

Thus $T=m|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2/2$ and $V=mg(a_y-l\cos\theta)$. Expanding the squared speed gives

$$
\boxed{L=\frac m2\left[\dot a_x^2+\dot a_y^2+l^2\dot\theta^2
+2l\dot\theta(\dot a_x\cos\theta+\dot a_y\sin\theta)\right]
-mg(a_y-l\cos\theta).}
$$

The prescribed support coordinates are functions of time, rather than additional dynamical variables. Terms depending only on time do not affect the [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) for $\theta$, but have been retained here to obtain the inertial-frame kinetic and [potential energies](../../../../../potential-energy.md) directly.
