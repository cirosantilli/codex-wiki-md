<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $H=p\cosh(2q)$,

$$
\boxed{\dot q=\cosh(2q),\qquad \dot p=-2p\sinh(2q).}
$$

The derivatives of $O=e^{qp}$ are $O_q=pe^{qp}$ and $O_p=qe^{qp}$. Its [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) with $H$ is therefore

$$
\boxed{\dot O=\{O,H\}=p e^{qp}\left[\cosh(2q)-2q\sinh(2q)\right].}
$$

Direct differentiation, $\dot O=e^{qp}(p\dot q+q\dot p)$, gives the same expression and checks the bracket sign.
