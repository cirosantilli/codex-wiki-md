<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Along [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) motion,

$$
\frac{dO}{dt}=\frac{\partial O}{\partial t}
+\sum_i\left(\frac{\partial O}{\partial q_i}\dot q_i
+\frac{\partial O}{\partial p_i}\dot p_i\right).
$$

Substituting $\dot q_i=\partial H/\partial p_i$ and $\dot p_i=-\partial H/\partial q_i$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{dO}{dt}=\frac{\partial O}{\partial t}+\{O,H\}.}
$$

For the printed $O(q,p)$ there is no explicit time term. In particular, an autonomous [observable](../../../../../../observable.md) is conserved when its [Poisson bracket](../../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) with $H$ vanishes. Taking $O=q_i$ or $O=p_i$ recovers [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md).
