↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For canonical coordinates $(q_i,p_i)$, the [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) is

$$
\boxed{\{F,G\}=\sum_i\left(
\frac{\partial F}{\partial q_i}\frac{\partial G}{\partial p_i}
-\frac{\partial F}{\partial p_i}\frac{\partial G}{\partial q_i}\right).}
$$

It is bilinear and antisymmetric, obeys the [Leibniz rule](../../../../../leibniz-rule.md) in either argument, and satisfies the [Jacobi identity](../../../../../jacobi-identity.md). These properties encode the symplectic structure of [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md). The two printed inline applications are treated separately below.
