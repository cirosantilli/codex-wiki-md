<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Apply the linear transformation of the [scalar fields](../../../../scalar-field.md) to either time- or space-derivative product. Its coefficients become $\alpha\gamma$ multiplying the square of the first derivative, $\beta\delta$ multiplying the square of the second, and $\alpha\delta+\beta\gamma$ multiplying their cross product. Invariance for arbitrary fields therefore requires

$$
\alpha\gamma=0,\qquad\beta\delta=0,\qquad\alpha\delta+\beta\gamma=1.
$$

The complete real solution is

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&\beta\\\gamma&\delta\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}r&0\\0&r^{-1}\end{pmatrix}\quad\text{or}\quad\begin{pmatrix}0&r\\r^{-1}&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad r\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}.}
$$

There are four [connected components](../../../../connected-component.md), distinguished by the diagonal/antidiagonal choice and the sign of $r$. Only the positive diagonal family is in the [identity component](../../../../identity-component.md); write $r=e^s$ to obtain $\phi_1\mapsto e^s\phi_1$, $\phi_2\mapsto e^{-s}\phi_2$. This is the [reciprocal rescaling symmetry of two cross-coupled scalar fields](../../../../reciprocal-rescaling-symmetry-of-two-cross-coupled-scalar-fields.md). The antidiagonal transformations exchange the fields as well as rescale them.
