<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the angular coordinate $\theta$ for the position on the ring. The [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $T=ma^2\dot\theta^2/2$. The [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) is constant because the ring is horizontal, and the ideal constraint force has no tangential component. Thus the [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) has no dependence on $\theta$ itself: it is a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md).

The [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives

$$
\frac d{dt}(ma^2\dot\theta)=0,\qquad\boxed{\dot\theta=\text{constant}.}
$$

Equivalently, the conserved [conjugate momentum](../../../../canonical-momentum.md) $p_\theta=ma^2\dot\theta$ is the vertical [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md), as required by [rotational symmetry](../../../../rotational-symmetry.md).
