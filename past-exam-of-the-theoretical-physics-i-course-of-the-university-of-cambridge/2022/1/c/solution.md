<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two continuous, time-independent [symmetries](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) are time translation and a common [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) of both angles. Their [Noether charges](../../../../noether-charge.md) are respectively the total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and vertical [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md):

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12I_1\dot\theta^2+\frac12I_2\dot\varphi^2+\frac k2[a^2+A^2+h^2-2aA\cos(\theta-\varphi)],}
$$



$$
\boxed{J=I_1\dot\theta+I_2\dot\varphi.}
$$

For the second symmetry, $\theta\mapsto\theta+\epsilon$ and $\varphi\mapsto\varphi+\epsilon$, so the [Noether theorem](../../../../noether-theorem.md) gives the sum of their conjugate momenta. Independent [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) are not [symmetries](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of the spring coupling.

There are also [discrete symmetries](../../../../discrete-symmetry.md): simultaneous reflection $(\theta,\varphi)\mapsto(-\theta,-\varphi)$, and [time reversal in classical mechanics](../../../../time-reversal-in-classical-mechanics.md), which reverses both angular velocities. These leave the dynamics invariant but provide no additional infinitesimal [Noether charges](../../../../noether-charge.md). Combined with common rotations, the reflections give the full planar orthogonal symmetry of the angular geometry. If $I_1=I_2$, interchange of the two angular coordinates is an additional [discrete symmetry](../../../../discrete-symmetry.md) of the reduced dynamics.

There is a further useful time-dependent [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md), up to a [total derivative](../../../../total-derivative.md), on a continuous lift of the angles: adding the same constant [angular velocity](../../../../angular-velocity.md), $(\theta,\varphi)\mapsto(\theta+vt,\varphi+vt)$. It changes $L$ by

$$
\frac d{dt}\left[v(I_1\theta+I_2\varphi)+\frac12(I_1+I_2)v^2t\right].
$$

Its conserved quantity is $\boxed{I_1\theta+I_2\varphi-tJ}$. It is the integration constant for free common rotation, rather than a new globally single-valued angle charge on the configuration torus. The globally defined continuous charges are $E$ and $J$ above.
