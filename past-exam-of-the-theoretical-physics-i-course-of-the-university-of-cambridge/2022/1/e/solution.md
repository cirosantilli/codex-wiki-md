<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $\delta_0=\theta_0-\varphi_0$ be $0$ or $\pi$, and write the angular displacements as $\eta_1,\eta_2$. To quadratic order the restoring [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) is $\kappa\cos\delta_0(\eta_1-\eta_2)^2/2$. The linearized [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) equation is

$$
\left[\kappa\cos\delta_0\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}-\omega^2\begin{pmatrix}I_1&0\\0&I_2\end{pmatrix}\right]\begin{pmatrix}\eta_1\\\eta_2\end{pmatrix}=0.
$$

The [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) gives $\omega^2[\omega^2I_1I_2-\kappa\cos\delta_0(I_1+I_2)]=0$. Thus the [normal modes of two spring-coupled coaxial rotors](../../../../normal-modes-of-two-spring-coupled-coaxial-rotors.md) are

$$
\boxed{\omega_0^2=0,\qquad(\eta_1,\eta_2)\propto(1,1),}
$$



$$
\boxed{\omega_{\mathrm{rel}}^2=\kappa\left(\frac1{I_1}+\frac1{I_2}\right)\cos\delta_0,\qquad(\eta_1,\eta_2)\propto(I_2,-I_1).}
$$

For the aligned family, the relative mode oscillates with positive frequency

$$
\Omega=\sqrt{kaA\left(\frac1{ma^2}+\frac1{MA^2}\right)}.
$$

For the antipodal family, $\omega_{\mathrm{rel}}^2=-\Omega^2$, so that displacement has solutions $e^{\pm\Omega t}$ and the equilibrium is unstable.

The common [zero mode](../../../../zero-mode.md) has $\eta_1=\eta_2=C+Dt$. It describes a neutral displacement or uniform common [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), not a restoring oscillation. Thus the aligned family is stable with respect to relative motion and has [orbital stability](../../../../orbital-stability.md) modulo common rotations. A particular static point is not strictly [Lyapunov stable](../../../../lyapunov-stability.md) against unrestricted small velocity perturbations: arbitrarily small nonzero $J$ eventually moves the common angle far from that point. With $J=0$, the common angle is fixed and the aligned relative [equilibrium point](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is stable. This distinguishes the symmetry-induced drift from the exponential instability of the antipodal family.
