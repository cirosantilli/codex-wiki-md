<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The angular [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) are

$$
I_1\ddot\theta=-\kappa\sin(\theta-\varphi),\qquad I_2\ddot\varphi=\kappa\sin(\theta-\varphi).
$$

A static [equilibrium point](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) has both angular velocities zero and $\sin(\theta-\varphi)=0$. Hence the complete equilibrium families, with angles taken modulo $2\pi$, are

$$
\boxed{(\theta,\varphi)=(q,q)\quad\text{or}\quad(\theta,\varphi)=(q+\pi,q),\qquad q\in\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z).}
$$

The first family is aligned in azimuth and minimizes the spring length; the second is antipodal and maximizes it. Their potentials are $k[(a-A)^2+h^2]/2$ and $k[(a+A)^2+h^2]/2$, respectively. Common rotations generate a whole family rather than isolated equilibria.
