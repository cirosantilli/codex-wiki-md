<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For this density the [Euler-Lagrange field equation with second derivatives](../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation-with-second-derivatives.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\rho\phi_{tt}+\alpha\phi_{xx}+\beta\phi_{xxxx}=0.}
$$

Insert a [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) $\phi=Ae^{i(kx-\omega t)}$. The second and fourth spatial [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md) give $-k^2$ and $k^4$, respectively, so

$$
-\rho\omega^2-\alpha k^2+\beta k^4=0,\qquad\boxed{\omega^2=\frac{\beta k^4-\alpha k^2}{\rho}.}
$$

For propagating modes, $k^2>\alpha/\beta$ and the [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) has magnitude $|\omega/k|=\sqrt{(\beta k^2-\alpha)/\rho}$, which depends on the [wave number](../../../../wavenumber.md). Thus the waves are dispersive. For $0<k^2<\alpha/\beta$, the squared frequency is negative: the corresponding solutions grow or decay exponentially rather than propagate with a real frequency. The uniform $k=0$ displacement is a zero-frequency shift mode.
