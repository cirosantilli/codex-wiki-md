<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Vary the [action](../../../../action.md) $S=\int dt\,dx\,\mathcal L$ with compactly supported $\delta\phi$, or impose vanishing $\delta\phi$ and its first derivatives on the spacetime boundary. The [first variation](../../../../first-variation.md) is

$$
\delta S=\int dt\,dx\left[\mathcal L_\phi\delta\phi+\mathcal L_{\phi_{,\mu}}\partial_\mu\delta\phi+\mathcal L_{\phi_{,\mu\nu}}\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\delta\phi\right].
$$

One [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) on the first-derivative term and two on the second-derivative term remove all [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md) from $\delta\phi$. The boundary terms vanish, leaving

$$
\delta S=\int dt\,dx\left[\mathcal L_\phi-\partial_\mu\mathcal L_{\phi_{,\mu}}+\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\mathcal L_{\phi_{,\mu\nu}}\right]\delta\phi.
$$

The [fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations](../../../../fundamental-lemma-of-the-calculus-of-variations.md) gives the [Euler-Lagrange field equation with second derivatives](../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation-with-second-derivatives.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\phi}-\partial_\mu\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu\phi)}+\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\phi)}=0.}
$$

Repeated indices run over time and space. The coefficient of the symmetric second derivative is defined by the displayed variation; if $\phi_{tx}$ is instead treated as a single independent mixed derivative variable, it is counted once rather than twice.
