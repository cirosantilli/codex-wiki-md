<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [self-consistency equation](../../../../self-consistency-equation.md) requires that the mean spin computed using the single-site [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) equals the spin $S$ used to form the effective field. With $h=4JS+B$,

$$
\langle s\rangle_h=\frac{\sum_{s=-1,0,1}s e^{\beta hs}}{\sum_{s=-1,0,1}e^{\beta hs}}=\frac{e^{\beta h}-e^{-\beta h}}{1+e^{\beta h}+e^{-\beta h}}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{S=\frac{2\sinh[\beta(4JS+B)]}{1+2\cosh[\beta(4JS+B)]}.}
$$

Equivalently, differentiating part (b) gives $N^{-1}\partial F_{\rm MF}/\partial S=4J[S-\langle s\rangle_h]$, so stationarity of the [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) in the [mean-field approximation](../../../../mean-field-approximation.md) is exactly this condition. Among multiple [stationary points](../../../../stationary-point.md) one must select the stable [minimum](../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md).
