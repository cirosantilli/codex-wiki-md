<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The small-argument [Taylor series](../../../../taylor-series.md) of the single-site response is

$$
\frac{2\sinh x}{1+2\cosh x}=\frac23x-\frac19x^3+O(x^5).
$$

Let $K=4\beta J$. For a small nonzero [order parameter](../../../../order-parameter.md) at zero field, the [self-consistency equation](../../../../self-consistency-equation.md) becomes

$$
1=\frac{2K}{3}-\frac{K^3}{9}S^2+O(S^4).
$$

Since $K_c=3/2$ and $K=K_c/(1+t)$, where $t=T/T_c-1$, this gives

$$
0=-t-\frac38S^2+O(t^2,tS^2,S^4).
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{S_\pm=\pm\sqrt{\frac83}\,(-t)^{1/2}+O((-t)^{3/2}),\qquad t\to0^-.}
$$

The positive branch is the expression printed in the question, while its negative partner is required by [spin inversion symmetry](../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md). The square-root behavior gives the [mean-field critical exponent](../../../../mean-field-critical-exponent.md) $1/2$ for the [spontaneous magnetization](../../../../spontaneous-magnetization.md).
