<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [square lattice](../../../../square-lattice.md) has [coordination number of a lattice](../../../../coordination-number-of-a-lattice.md) $z=4$. The mean-field decoupling is

$$
S_iS_j\simeq S(S_i+S_j)-S^2.
$$

Accounting for the factor $1/2$ in the directed-neighbour sum gives the [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) in the [mean-field approximation](../../../../mean-field-approximation.md):

$$
H_{\mathrm{MF}}=2JNS^2-(4JS+B)\sum_iS_i.
$$

The constant term is necessary to correct the double counting of the interaction. Let $h=4JS+B$. The one-site [partition function](../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is

$$
Z_1=\sum_{s=-1,0,1}e^{\beta hs}=1+2\cosh(\beta h),
$$

so $Z_{\rm MF}=e^{-2\beta JNS^2}Z_1^N$. The [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{F_{\mathrm{MF}}(S,T,B)=N\left[2JS^2-\frac1\beta\log\{1+2\cosh[\beta(4JS+B)]\}\right].}
$$

The equilibrium [mean-field approximation](../../../../mean-field-approximation.md) is obtained by minimizing this expression over the allowed $S$. The $s=0$ state supplies the extra $1$ in $Z_1$; omitting it would instead describe the two-state [Ising model](../../../../ising-model.md). This is the [Blume-Capel mean-field transition at zero single-ion anisotropy](../../../../blume-capel-mean-field-transition-at-zero-single-ion-anisotropy.md).
