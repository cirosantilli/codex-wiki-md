<h1 id="3/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

For a homogeneous [saddle-point approximation](../../../../saddle-point-approximation.md) define $r=a(T-T_c)>0$. With the cubic source coupling, minimizing the [Landau free energy](../../../../landau-free-energy.md) gives

$$
2rm+2bm^3-B^3=0.
$$

Its curvature $2r+6bm^2$ is positive, so there is a unique [local minimum](../../../../local-minimum.md) and [global minimum](../../../../global-minimum.md). The solution near $B=0$ has

$$
m(B)=\frac{B^3}{2r}+O(B^9),
$$

because substituting this leading term makes the cubic self-interaction of order $B^9$. Therefore the [cubic source coupling and third-order susceptibility](../../../../cubic-source-coupling-and-third-order-susceptibility.md) give

$$
\boxed{\chi^{(1)}=\chi^{(2)}=0,\qquad\chi^{(3)}=\frac{3!}{2r}=\frac3{a(T-T_c)}.}
$$

Thus the first nonzero response diverges like $(T-T_c)^{-1}$ from above. The factor $3!$ is essential: the coefficient of $B^3$ is not itself the third [derivative](../../../../derivative.md). This is susceptibility to the specified cubic source variable, rather than the usual third-order response to a linearly coupled field.
