<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the supplied [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md), variation with respect to $\phi^*$ uses

$$
\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu\phi^*)}
=\partial^\mu\phi+ieA^\mu\phi,
\qquad
\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\phi^*}
=-m^2\phi-ieA_\mu\partial^\mu\phi+f.
$$

Thus $\Box\phi+2ieA^\mu\partial_\mu\phi+ie(\partial_\mu A^\mu)\phi+m^2\phi=f$. In the [Lorenz gauge](../../../../../lorenz-gauge-condition.md),

$$
\boxed{(\Box+2ieA^\mu\partial_\mu+m^2)\phi=f(t).}
$$

For real external fields and driving, the conjugate equation has $-2ieA^\mu\partial_\mu$ acting on $\phi^*$. The supplied model has no $e^2A^2|\phi|^2$ term; one must not insert such a term from a different minimally coupled [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md).
