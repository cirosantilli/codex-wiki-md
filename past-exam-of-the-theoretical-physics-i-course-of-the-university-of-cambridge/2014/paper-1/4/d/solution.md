<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Under the combined local transformation,

$$
D'_\mu\phi'=(\partial_\mu+iqA_\mu+iq\partial_\mu\alpha)
(e^{-iq\alpha}\phi)=e^{-iq\alpha}D_\mu\phi.
$$

The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the phase cancels the added gauge potential. The conjugate [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) gains the opposite phase, so their product and $|\phi|^2$ are invariant. Also

$$
F'_{\mu\nu}=F_{\mu\nu}+\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\alpha
-\partial_\nu\partial_\mu\alpha=F_{\mu\nu}.
$$

**Every term of the interacting [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is therefore invariant.**
