<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An infinitesimal constant phase change has $\delta\phi=-iq\alpha\phi$ and $\delta\phi^*=iq\alpha\phi^*$. Both the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) product and the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) term are invariant. [Noether's theorem](../../../../../noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) gives, per unit $\alpha$,

$$
j^\mu=(\partial^\mu\phi^*)(-iq\phi)
+(\partial^\mu\phi)(iq\phi^*)
=iq(\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-\phi\partial^\mu\phi^*).
$$

Thus **the global phase symmetry supplies the conserved current in part a**.
