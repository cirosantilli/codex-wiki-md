<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\psi$ be the small hoop's absolute spin angle with the same angular sign convention. Equating the two contact-point velocities cancels the large hoop centre's [translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md). The remaining tangential equality is

$$
a\dot\phi+a\dot\psi=2a\dot\theta,
\qquad\boxed{\dot\psi=2\dot\theta-\dot\phi.}
$$

This [rolling constraint inside a moving circular track](../../../../../rolling-constraint-inside-a-moving-circular-track.md) adds small-hoop rotational [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $Ma^2(2\dot\theta-\dot\phi)^2/2$. At quadratic order its cross term cancels the translational cross term in part a. The potential is unchanged, so

$$
\boxed{L_2=\frac12Ma^2(16\dot\theta^2+2\dot\phi^2)
-\frac12Mga(4\theta^2+\phi^2).}
$$

Static friction enforces rolling; ideal no-slip contact dissipates no [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
