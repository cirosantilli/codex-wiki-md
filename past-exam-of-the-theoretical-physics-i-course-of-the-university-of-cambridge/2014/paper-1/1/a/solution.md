<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Measure both angles from the downward vertical, as in the source diagram. With upward $y$, the cylinder centres are

$$
\mathbf R=(2a\sin\theta,-2a\cos\theta),\qquad
\mathbf r=\mathbf R+(a\sin\phi,-a\cos\phi).
$$

The large hoop has central inertia $4Ma^2$ and [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) $\dot\theta$, while the small hoop has no spin in this part. [Translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) and [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) therefore give

$$
T=\frac12Ma^2[12\dot\theta^2+\dot\phi^2+
4\cos(\theta-\phi)\dot\theta\dot\phi],
\qquad V=-Mga(4\cos\theta+\cos\phi).
$$

Thus $L=T-V$. Counting each angle and [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) as first order, replacing the cosine in the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) product by one changes only fourth-order terms. Expanding the potential and dropping its constant gives

$$
\boxed{L_2=\frac12Ma^2(12\dot\theta^2+\dot\phi^2+4\dot\theta\dot\phi)
-\frac12Mga(4\theta^2+\phi^2).}
$$
