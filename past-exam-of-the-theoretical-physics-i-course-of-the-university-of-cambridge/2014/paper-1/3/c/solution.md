<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

When the Hamiltonians agree, exactness of the one-form gives

$$
\sum_i p_i\,dq_i-\sum_iP_i\,dQ_i=dG,
\qquad L-\mathcal L=\frac{dG}{dt}.
$$

The [action](../../../../../action.md) argument of part b then proves canonicity. Equivalently, exterior differentiation preserves the symplectic two-form. For a time-dependent transformation, **the [exact differential](../../../../../exact-differential.md) must supply the full [action](../../../../../action.md) identity**; a fixed-time check alone would not establish that the new [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) equals the old one without a time-dependent correction.
