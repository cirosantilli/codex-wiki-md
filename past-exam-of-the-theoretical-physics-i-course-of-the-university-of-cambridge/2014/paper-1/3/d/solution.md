<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The general [action](../../../../../action.md) identity is

$$
dG=\sum_i p_i\,dq_i-\sum_iP_i\,dQ_i+(\mathcal H-H)dt.
$$

Treating $q,Q,t$ as independent and comparing coefficients gives

$$
\boxed{p_i=G_{q_i},\qquad P_i=-G_{Q_i},\qquad
\mathcal H=H+G_t.}
$$

Choose a [complete integral of the Hamilton-Jacobi equation](../../../../../complete-integral-of-the-hamilton-jacobi-equation.md),

$$
\boxed{G_t+H(q,G_q,t)=0.}
$$

Then $\mathcal H=0$, and [Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) become $\dot Q_i=\dot P_i=0$. Completeness and local invertibility are needed: a solution with too few independent parameters cannot generate an arbitrary trajectory.
