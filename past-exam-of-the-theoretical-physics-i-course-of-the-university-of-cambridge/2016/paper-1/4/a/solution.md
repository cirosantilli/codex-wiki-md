<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the isotropic zero-field [classical Heisenberg model](../../../../../classical-heisenberg-model.md) of a [ferromagnet](../../../../../ferromagnet.md), the [analytic vector Landau expansion](../../../../../analytic-vector-landau-expansion.md) uses the Cartesian components of the [vector order parameter](../../../../../vector-order-parameter.md) near $\mathbf m=0$. [rotational symmetry](../../../../../rotational-symmetry.md) makes its scalar terms functions of $\mathbf m\cdot\mathbf m$. Global [spin inversion symmetry](../../../../../spin-inversion-symmetry.md) also requires $f(\mathbf m)=f(-\mathbf m)$. Through fourth order this gives

$$
\boxed{a=c=0,\qquad f=bm^2+dm^4,\qquad d>0.}
$$

A positive quartic coefficient stabilizes the unconstrained truncated [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). The disordered phase has $b>0$ and its minimum at $m=0$; the aligned phase has $b<0$ and a minimum at nonzero $m$. At the continuous transition $b=0$. The attractive alignment interaction supplies an energetic tendency toward negative quadratic curvature; orientational [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) favors the disordered phase at high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), so the sign of $b$ is a free-energy question rather than a consequence of the pair [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) alone.

The analyticity assumption matters: the variable printed as $m$ is a nonnegative magnitude, so reversing $\mathbf m$ does not send that scalar to $-m$. Terms proportional to $|\mathbf m|$ or $|\mathbf m|^3$ would be rotationally invariant but nonanalytic at the [vector](../../../../../vector.md) origin. Standard analytic [Landau theory](../../../../../landau-theory.md) excludes them. Without that assumption, the stated pair [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) alone would not exclude odd powers of the magnitude.[§1.2, small-order-parameter expansion; §4.2, rotationally invariant vector free energy](../../../../../statistical-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
