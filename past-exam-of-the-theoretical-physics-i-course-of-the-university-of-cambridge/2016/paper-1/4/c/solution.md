<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [independent head-tail reversal symmetry](../../../../../independent-head-tail-reversal-symmetry.md) means that the squared [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) interaction [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is unchanged when any individual $\mathbf s_i$ is replaced by $-\mathbf s_i$. All configurations $\mathbf s_i=\sigma_i\mathbf n$, with $\sigma_i=\pm1$ independently, attain the aligned ground-state pair [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). There is consequently no energetic preference for either end of a molecular axis. Averaging over these equally weighted signs gives $\langle\mathbf s_i\rangle=0$ and **$\langle\mathbf m\rangle=0$ even in the nematically ordered phase**. The surviving order is the unoriented axis, represented by a [nematic director](../../../../../nematic-director.md) with $\mathbf n\sim-\mathbf n$. A second-moment tensor can detect this order while a polar [vector](../../../../../vector.md) cannot.[§4.3, printed pp.10–11, head-tail equivalence and the tensor order parameter](../../../../../lectures-on-landau-theory-of-phase-transitions-by-peter-d-olmsted.md)

The word “always” needs the [statistical ensemble](../../../../../statistical-ensemble.md) or apolar interpretation: it is not an identity for each finite configuration. For example, choosing all $\sigma_i=+1$ is also a ground-state representative and gives $\mathbf m=\mathbf n$. In an unbiased sign [statistical ensemble](../../../../../statistical-ensemble.md) the typical residual polar [vector](../../../../../vector.md) has size of order $N^{-1/2}$, because $\langle|\mathbf m|^2\rangle=1/N$ for these independent ground-state signs. This does not make polar order an equilibrium [nematic order parameter](../../../../../nematic-order-parameter.md). The tensor below is unchanged by each sign flip and thus removes the arbitrary labeling of rod ends.
