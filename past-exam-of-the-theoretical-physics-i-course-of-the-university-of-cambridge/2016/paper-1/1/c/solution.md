<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The continuous transformation $\theta\mapsto\theta+\epsilon$, $\theta_B\mapsto\theta_B+\epsilon$, $z\mapsto z+b\epsilon$, $r\mapsto r$ moves both endpoints by the same screw motion and preserves the fixed [helix](../../../../../helix.md). Both $\Delta$ and $u$ are unchanged, so $\delta L=0$. [Noether theorem](../../../../../noether-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal Q=p_\theta+p_{\theta_B}+bp_z=mr^2\dot\theta+M(a^2+b^2)\dot\theta_B+bm\dot z=\text{constant}.}
$$

This is the [Noether conserved quantity for a mechanical point symmetry](../../../../../noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) applied to [helical symmetry](../../../../../helical-symmetry.md). In physical Cartesian terms it equals **total $z$ [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) plus $b$ times total $z$ [linear momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md)**: the bead contributes $Ma^2\dot\theta_B+b(Mb\dot\theta_B)$. Neither pure axial [translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md) nor pure axial [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) preserves the fixed coil separately. Their conserved combination is a consequence of the [helical symmetry](../../../../../helical-symmetry.md), not two independently conserved momenta. Substitution of the four equations of motion verifies $\dot{\mathcal Q}=-kar\sin\Delta+k(ar\sin\Delta+bu)-bku=0$.[online chapter 2, §2.4.1, equations (2.76)–(2.79): continuous coordinate symmetry and its conserved momentum](../../../../../classical-dynamics-by-david-tong.md)
