<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the bead position in [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) as $\mathbf x_B=(a\cos\theta_B,a\sin\theta_B,b\theta_B)$. Differentiation gives $|\dot{\mathbf x}_B|^2=(a^2+b^2)\dot\theta_B^2$, while the free particle has $|\dot{\mathbf x}|^2=\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2+\dot z^2$. Thus the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is the sum of these squared [speeds](../../../../../speed.md) weighted by half the respective [mass](../../../../../mass.md). The squared [spring](../../../../../spring.md) length is

$$
\ell^2=|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_B|^2=r^2+a^2-2ar\cos(\theta-\theta_B)+(z-b\theta_B)^2.
$$

A [spring](../../../../../spring.md) obeying [Hooke's law](../../../../../hooke-s-law.md) with zero natural length has [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) $k\ell^2/2$. Consequently **$L=T-k\ell^2/2$**, and deleting the additive constant $-ka^2/2$ gives

$$
L=\frac m2(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2+\dot z^2)+\frac{M(a^2+b^2)}2\dot\theta_B^2-\frac k2\left[r^2-2ar\cos(\theta-\theta_B)+(z-b\theta_B)^2\right].
$$

The [spring](../../../../../spring.md) length is the straight separation of its endpoints, not distance measured along the [helix](../../../../../helix.md). The fixed frictionless constraint is already incorporated by using the [generalized coordinate](../../../../../generalized-coordinate.md) $\theta_B$; it supplies no extra [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md).
