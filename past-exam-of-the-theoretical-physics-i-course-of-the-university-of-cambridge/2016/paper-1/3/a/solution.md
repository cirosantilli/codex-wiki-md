<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Treat $\phi$ and $\phi^*$ as independent variables during [variation](../../../../../variation.md). With [Minkowski metric](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $(+---)$, differentiation with respect to $\phi^*$ gives

$$
\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu\phi^*)}=\partial^\mu\phi+ieA^\mu\phi,\qquad \frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\phi^*}=-m^2\phi-ieA^\mu\partial_\mu\phi+f(t).
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange field equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is therefore $\Box\phi+2ieA^\mu\partial_\mu\phi+ie(\partial_\mu A^\mu)\phi+m^2\phi=f$. The static purely temporal potential has $\partial_\mu A^\mu=\partial_tV=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{(\partial_t^2-\nabla^2+2ieV(\mathbf r)\partial_t+m^2)\phi=f(t).}
$$

[variation](../../../../../variation.md) with respect to $\phi$ gives $(\Box-2ieA^\mu\partial_\mu+m^2)\phi^*=f(t)$. This is the [complex conjugate](../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) equation when the driving [force](../../../../../force-split.md) is real, as is required for the supplied [lagrangian density](../../../../../lagrangian-density.md) to be real. For a complex driving amplitude a real [source](../../../../../source-quantum-field-theory.md) term would instead require conjugate coefficients. The interaction supplied here is linear in the external potential: adding the $e^2A_\mu A^\mu|\phi|^2$ term from full [minimal coupling](../../../../../minimal-coupling.md) would change the problem and its [equation of motion](../../../../../equation-of-motion.md).
