<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md) and the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $g_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$, with $A^\mu=(\Phi,\mathbf A)$ and $A_\mu=(\Phi,-\mathbf A)$. Then $F_{0i}=E_i$, $F^{i0}=E_i$, and $F^{ij}=-\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$. The [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) is an [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md), which gives gives

$$
\delta\mathcal L=-\tfrac12F^{\mu\nu}\delta F_{\mu\nu}=-F^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\delta A_\nu.
$$

[integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) in the [action](../../../../../action.md), with the [variation](../../../../../variation.md) vanishing on its boundary, yields $\delta S=\int d^4x\,(\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu})\delta A_\nu$. Independent [variations](../../../../../variation.md) of the four components therefore give **$\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=0$**. The time component is $\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=0$; the spatial components are $\nabla\times\mathbf B-\partial_t\mathbf E=0$. These are the two source-free [Maxwell equations](../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) obtained dynamically. The other two, $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0$ and $\partial_t\mathbf B+\nabla\times\mathbf E=0$, follow identically from $F=dA$, through the [Electromagnetic Bianchi identity](../../../../../electromagnetic-bianchi-identity.md). They are not additional [Euler-Lagrange field equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md).[chapter 1, §§1.1 and 1.1.3: potential conventions and the Maxwell variation](../../../../../quantum-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
