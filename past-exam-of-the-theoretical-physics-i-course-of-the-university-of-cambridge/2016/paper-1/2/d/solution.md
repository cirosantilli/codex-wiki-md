<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the constant-parameter subgroup of the local transformation: $\delta\phi=-iq\epsilon\phi$, $\delta\phi^*=iq\epsilon\phi^*$, $\delta A_\mu=0$. The momenta conjugate to the [complex scalar field](../../../../../complex-scalar-field.md) [gradients](../../../../../gradient.md) are $\Pi_\phi^\mu=(D^\mu\phi)^*$ and $\Pi_{\phi^*}^\mu=D^\mu\phi$. [Noether theorem](../../../../../noether-theorem.md) gives

$$
j^\mu=\Pi_\phi^\mu\frac{\delta\phi}{\epsilon}+\Pi_{\phi^*}^\mu\frac{\delta\phi^*}{\epsilon}=iq[\phi^*D^\mu\phi-\phi(D^\mu\phi)^*]=J^\mu.
$$

The [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) equations are $D_\mu D^\mu\phi+m^2\phi=0$ and their [complex conjugate](../../../../../complex-conjugate.md). Applying the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md), the gauge-potential terms cancel to give

$$
\partial_\mu J^\mu=iq\left[\phi^*D_\mu D^\mu\phi-\phi(D_\mu D^\mu\phi)^*\right]=iq[-m^2|\phi|^2+m^2|\phi|^2]=\boxed{0}.
$$

This establishes conservation of the [Noether current of scalar electrodynamics](../../../../../noether-current-of-scalar-electrodynamics.md) [on shell](../../../../../on-shell.md), and conservation of its integrated [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) when there is no boundary flux. An arbitrary function $\alpha(x)$ describes [gauge redundancy](../../../../../gauge-redundancy.md); the associated [Noether second theorem](../../../../../noether-second-theorem.md) supplies an identity among [Euler-Lagrange field equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md), rather than a distinct physical charge for each function. Indeed its on-shell current can be written $j^\mu[\alpha]=\alpha J^\mu-F^{\mu\nu}\partial_\nu\alpha=-\partial_\nu(\alpha F^{\mu\nu})$. The [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md) makes its [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) vanish. Independently, differentiating the Maxwell equation also gives $\partial_\nu J^\nu=\partial_\nu\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=0$, confirming compatibility with the matter derivation.[§1.3.4, internal phase symmetry; §6.1, gauge symmetry and its constant subgroup](../../../../../quantum-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
