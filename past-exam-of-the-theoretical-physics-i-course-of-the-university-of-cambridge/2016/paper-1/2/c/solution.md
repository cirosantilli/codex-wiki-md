<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Hold $\phi,\phi^*$ fixed while varying $A_\nu$. With the specified convention $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+iqA_\mu$, the two matter [variations](../../../../../variation.md) are $\delta(D_\mu\phi)=iq\phi\,\delta A_\mu$ and $\delta(D_\mu\phi)^*=-iq\phi^*\delta A_\mu$. Thus

$$
\delta\mathcal L_{\rm matter}=iq\left[\phi(D^\nu\phi)^*-\phi^*D^\nu\phi\right]\delta A_\nu=-J^\nu\delta A_\nu.
$$

Adding the Maxwell [variation](../../../../../variation.md) from part (a) gives $\delta S=\int d^4x\,[\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu}-J^\nu]\delta A_\nu$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=J^\nu,\qquad J^\mu=iq\left[\phi^*D^\mu\phi-\phi(D^\mu\phi)^*\right].}
$$

Expanding the [gauge covariant derivatives](../../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative.md) supplies a useful convention check:

$$
J^\mu=iq(\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-\phi\partial^\mu\phi^*)-2q^2A^\mu|\phi|^2.
$$

In particular, the last sign is negative for the specified plus sign in $D_\mu$. Reversing that convention requires consistently changing the phase transformation and current conventions as well. This current is the matter [source](../../../../../source-quantum-field-theory.md) in [scalar electrodynamics](../../../../../scalar-electrodynamics.md); its $A$ dependence must not be dropped.
