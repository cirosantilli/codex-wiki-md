<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At [phase coexistence](../../../../phase-coexistence.md), the finite ordered [global minimum](../../../../global-minimum.md) has the same [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) as $m=0$. With $x=m^2>0$ and $f(0)=0$, stationarity and equal values give

$$
r+bx+cx^2=0,\qquad r+\frac b2x+\frac c3x^2=0.
$$

Subtracting eliminates $r$:

$$
\frac b2x+\frac{2c}{3}x^2=0,\qquad x_*=-\frac{3b}{4c}>0.
$$

Substitute back to get $r_*=3b^2/(16c)$, so

$$
\boxed{T_c^*=T_c+\frac{3b^2}{16ac},\qquad
m_{\rm jump}=\sqrt{-\frac{3b}{4c}}\quad(b<0).}
$$

The three degenerate equilibrium values at this temperature are $0$ and $\pm m_{\rm jump}$. A useful global check, avoiding confusion with a [spinodal point](../../../../spinodal-point.md), is the exact factorization

$$
\left.f(m)\right|_{r=r_*}=\frac c3m^2\left(m^2+\frac{3b}{4c}\right)^2\geq0.
$$

All three zeros are therefore [global minima](../../../../global-minimum.md). Their curvatures are positive: $f''(0)=3b^2/(8c)$ and $f''(\pm m_{\rm jump})=3b^2/(2c)$.

The ordered local minimum first appears on cooling at $r=b^2/(4c)$, where the nonzero stationary roots merge. It becomes globally stable only at $r_*$; the disordered minimum remains [metastable](../../../../metastability.md) down to $r=0$. These different temperatures distinguish [phase coexistence](../../../../phase-coexistence.md) from loss of local stability.
