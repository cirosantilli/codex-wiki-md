<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [Landau free energy](../../../../landau-free-energy.md) is a coarse-grained thermodynamic potential expressed as a function of a proposed macroscopic [order parameter](../../../../order-parameter.md). Its coefficients include microscopic and thermal effects already averaged over. At fixed external thermodynamic conditions, equilibrium selects its [global minimum](../../../../global-minimum.md); other [local minima](../../../../local-minimum.md) can represent [metastable](../../../../metastability.md) states. It is not the internal energy of a single microscopic magnetic configuration. Here the real scalar $m$ represents a selected component of [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md): zero describes the disordered state, while nonzero values distinguish two oppositely ordered states.

Every power is even, so the internal [discrete symmetry](../../../../discrete-symmetry.md) is

$$
\boxed{m\longmapsto-m,\qquad \mathbb Z_2.}
$$

For a magnetic [order parameter](../../../../order-parameter.md), this represents reversal of the magnetization, as under [time reversal](../../../../t-symmetry.md) in zero applied [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). A nonzero field would generally add an odd term and break this symmetry explicitly. This scalar model has no continuous internal rotation symmetry; physical rotational symmetry of a vector magnet is not implied by treating one real component. The condition $c>0$ makes the polynomial bounded below at large $|m|$.

For the requested ordered phase on cooling, take the usual implicit assumption $a>0$, and write $r=a(T-T_c)$. If $a<0$ the temperature directions reverse; if $a=0$ this expansion has no temperature-driven transition. The source explicitly states only $c>0$.
