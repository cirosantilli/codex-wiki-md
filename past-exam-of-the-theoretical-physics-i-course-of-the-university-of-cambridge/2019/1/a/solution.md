<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $g_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$ and units with the [speed of light](../../../../speed-of-light.md) equal to one. Thus $u^\mu u_\mu=-1$, and $h_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+u_\mu u_\nu$ is the [spatial projector of a timelike congruence](../../../../spatial-projector-of-a-timelike-congruence.md). Keep the medium velocity fixed when varying the [electromagnetic four-potential](../../../../electromagnetic-four-potential.md). Since the [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) is $F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu$, its variation gives

$$
\delta_A S=\int d^4x\,\frac12(M^{\mu\nu}-F^{\mu\nu})(\partial_\mu\delta A_\nu-\partial_\nu\delta A_\mu)
=\int d^4x\,(M^{\mu\nu}-F^{\mu\nu})\partial_\mu\delta A_\nu.
$$

Both [tensors](../../../../tensor.md) are antisymmetric. [Integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md), with variations vanishing at the boundary, therefore gives

$$
\delta_A S=\int d^4x\,\partial_\mu(F^{\mu\nu}-M^{\mu\nu})\delta A_\nu.
$$

The [Euler-Lagrange field equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is $\partial_\mu(F^{\mu\nu}-M^{\mu\nu})=0$. Renaming the free index and using antisymmetry gives

$$
\boxed{\partial_\nu F^{\mu\nu}=\partial_\nu M^{\mu\nu}.}
$$

These are the inhomogeneous [Maxwell equations](../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) with the [bound charge and bound current](../../../../bound-charge-and-bound-current.md) encoded by the [polarization-magnetization tensor](../../../../polarization-magnetization-tensor.md).
