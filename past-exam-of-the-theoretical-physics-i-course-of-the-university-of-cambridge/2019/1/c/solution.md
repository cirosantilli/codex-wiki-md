<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose $A^\mu=(\Phi,\mathbf A)$, so that $F_{i0}=E_i$ and $F_{ij}=\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$, with $\epsilon_{123}=1$. The definitions of the [polarization-magnetization tensor](../../../../polarization-magnetization-tensor.md) imply $M^{0i}=-P_i$ and $M^{ij}=\epsilon_{ijk}M_k$. Separating temporal and spatial indices,

$$
\frac12M^{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}=M^{i0}F_{i0}+\frac12M^{ij}F_{ij}
=\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf E+\frac12\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{ij\ell}M_kB_\ell.
$$

The [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) contraction equals $2\delta_{k\ell}$, giving

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_{\rm int}=\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf E+\mathbf M\cdot\mathbf B.}
$$

Thus $\mathbf P$ is the [electric dipole moment](../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md) per volume, or [electric polarization](../../../../polarization-density.md), and $\mathbf M$ is the [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) per volume, or [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md). The signs are those of dipole interactions in a [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md); the associated fixed-dipole potential energies have the opposite signs. Consistently, the equation in part (a) has components

$$
\nabla\cdot(\mathbf E+\mathbf P)=0,\qquad
\nabla\times(\mathbf B-\mathbf M)=\partial_t(\mathbf E+\mathbf P),
$$

so $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$ and $\mathbf J_b=\partial_t\mathbf P+\nabla\times\mathbf M$ are the [bound charge and bound current](../../../../bound-charge-and-bound-current.md).
