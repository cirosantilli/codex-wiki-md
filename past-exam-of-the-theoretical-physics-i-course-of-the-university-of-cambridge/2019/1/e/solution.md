<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The constants specify isotropic, nondispersive [linear response](../../../../linear-response-theory.md): $\kappa$ controls [electric polarization](../../../../polarization-density.md), and $\chi$ controls [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md). Their numerical relation to conventional [electric susceptibility](../../../../electric-susceptibility.md) and [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md) depends on the signs and field normalization in the action. In the rationalized units used here, the printed action gives the [constitutive relations](../../../../constitutive-relations.md)

$$
\mathbf D=\mathbf E+\mathbf P=(1-\kappa)\mathbf E,\qquad
\mathbf H=\mathbf B-\mathbf M=(1+\chi)\mathbf B.
$$

Thus its relative [permittivity](../../../../permittivity-split.md) and [magnetic permeability](../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md) are

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon_r=1-\kappa,\qquad \mu_r=\frac1{1+\chi},\qquad \chi_e=-\kappa,\qquad \chi_m=-\frac{\chi}{1+\chi}.}
$$

Here conventional $\chi_m$ is defined by $\mathbf M=\chi_m\mathbf H$, not by $\mathbf M=\chi_m\mathbf B$. With the alternative repaired action that yields the PDF's target equation, $\varepsilon_r$ is unchanged but $\mu_r=(1-\chi)^{-1}$ and $\chi_m=\chi/(1-\chi)$. In particular, a positive conventional [electric susceptibility](../../../../electric-susceptibility.md) corresponds to negative $\kappa$ in the printed conventions; the problem does not specify signs for these constants.
