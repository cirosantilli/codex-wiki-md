<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Treat $\psi,\psi^*$ independently in the [Legendre transform in mechanics](../../../../legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md). Their [canonical momenta](../../../../canonical-momentum.md) are

$$
\pi_\psi=\frac{i\hbar}{2}\psi^*,\qquad \pi_{\psi^*}=-\frac{i\hbar}{2}\psi.
$$

The time-derivative terms cancel in $\pi_\psi\dot\psi+\pi_{\psi^*}\dot\psi^*-\mathcal L$, giving the [Hamiltonian density](../../../../hamiltonian-density.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H=\frac{\hbar^2}{2M}|\nabla\psi|^2-\mu|\psi|^2+\frac g2|\psi|^4.}
$$

The first-order time dependence makes the momenta constrained; this direct calculation still gives the canonical density. In physical terms this is the [grand-canonical Hamiltonian of a Bose field](../../../../grand-canonical-hamiltonian-of-a-bose-field.md), $K=H-\mu N$. The physical energy density is obtained by adding $\mu|\psi|^2$ back. A stationary field in this chemical-potential frame has the usual factor $e^{-i\mu t/\hbar}$ in the physical frame.

Complete the square:

$$
\mathcal H=\frac{\hbar^2}{2M}|\nabla\psi|^2+\frac g2\left(|\psi|^2-\frac\mu g\right)^2-\frac{\mu^2}{2g}.
$$

For uniform or periodic boundary conditions, or in the homogeneous bulk, both nonconstant terms are nonnegative and vanish simultaneously at

$$
\boxed{\psi_0=\sqrt{\frac\mu g}\,e^{i\theta_0},\qquad \mathcal H_{\min}=-\frac{\mu^2}{2g}.}
$$

The [ground states](../../../../ground-state.md) form a circle. A particular nonzero $\psi_0$ is changed by every nontrivial phase rotation, although the full family is invariant. Selecting one such [mean-field theory](../../../../mean-field-theory.md) state therefore exhibits [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) of $U(1)$. This describes phase selection in the thermodynamic limit; an exact finite-system particle-number [eigenstate](../../../../eigenstate.md) need not itself have a nonzero field expectation.
