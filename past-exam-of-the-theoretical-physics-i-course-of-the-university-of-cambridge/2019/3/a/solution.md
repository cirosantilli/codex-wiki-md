<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under $\psi\mapsto e^{i\alpha}\psi$ with constant real $\alpha$, the field and its [complex conjugate](../../../../complex-conjugate.md) acquire opposite phases. These cancel in $|\psi|^2$, $|\psi|^4$, $\nabla\psi^*\cdot\nabla\psi$, and $\psi^*\dot\psi-\psi\dot\psi^*$. The [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) therefore has the [particle-number phase symmetry](../../../../particle-number-phase-symmetry.md), a global [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md) $U(1)$.

For a spacetime-dependent real $\alpha(\mathbf r,t)$,

$$
\dot\psi'=e^{i\alpha}(\dot\psi+i\dot\alpha\psi),\qquad
\nabla\psi'=e^{i\alpha}(\nabla\psi+i\psi\nabla\alpha).
$$

Writing $n=|\psi|^2$, the change in the [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) is

$$
\Delta\mathcal L=-\hbar n\dot\alpha-\frac{\hbar^2}{2M}\left[
i(\psi\nabla\psi^*-\psi^*\nabla\psi)\cdot\nabla\alpha+n|\nabla\alpha|^2\right].
$$

This is not generally zero or a [boundary term](../../../../boundary-term.md). Thus **the symmetry is global, with no arbitrary local phase symmetry**. A compensating [gauge field](../../../../gauge-field.md) and [gauge-covariant derivative of a charged scalar field](../../../../gauge-covariant-derivative-of-a-charged-scalar-field.md) would be required to promote it to a local redundancy.
