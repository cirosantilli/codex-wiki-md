<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

At long wavelength,

$$
\boxed{\omega(k)=c_s k+O(k^3),\qquad c_s=\sqrt{\frac\mu M},\qquad \omega(0)=0.}
$$

This is the sound-like [Goldstone mode](../../../../goldstone-boson.md) of the broken global $U(1)$ [continuous symmetry](../../../../continuous-symmetry.md). A spatially uniform infinitesimal phase change is $\delta\psi=i\psi_0\delta\theta$, entirely in the $\chi_2$ direction, and costs no potential energy. Slow spatial variations acquire gradient energy and propagate as sound. Thus the [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) realizes [Goldstone's theorem](../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) through a gapless excitation; it is not a massive phase oscillation.

The amplitude and phase are canonically paired by the first-order time derivative in the [quadratic condensate fluctuation Lagrangian](../../../../quadratic-condensate-fluctuation-lagrangian.md). There is one positive-frequency branch, rather than an additional independent amplitude branch of a relativistic second-order-time theory. At high momentum,

$$
\hbar\omega=\epsilon_k+\mu+O(\mu^2/\epsilon_k),
$$

so the same branch becomes particle-like. The [healing length](../../../../healing-length.md) $\xi=\hbar/\sqrt{2M\mu}$ sets the crossover scale.
