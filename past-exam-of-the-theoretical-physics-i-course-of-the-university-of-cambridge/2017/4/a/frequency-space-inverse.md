<h1 id="4/a/frequency-space-inverse">Frequency-space inverse</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

Use exactly the supplied inverse [Fourier transform](../../../../fourier-transform-split.md) convention $e^{-i\omega\tau}$, with $\tau=t-t'$. Then $\partial_t\mapsto-i\omega$ and $\partial_t^3\mapsto i\omega^3$. The transformed equation is

$$
(1-\omega^2-i\varepsilon\omega^3)x(\omega)=f(\omega),\qquad
\boxed{G(\omega)=\frac1{1-\omega^2-i\varepsilon\omega^3}}.
$$

This algebraic inverse still needs a [pole](../../../../pole.md) prescription to select the time-domain solution, particularly when a [pole](../../../../pole.md) is on the real integration contour.
