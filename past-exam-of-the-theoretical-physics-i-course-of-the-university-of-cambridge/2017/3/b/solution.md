<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $v=\sqrt{-\ln\lambda/\lambda}>0$ and write $\phi=v+\chi$. Then

$$
|\phi|^2=v^2+v(\chi+\chi^*)+\chi^*\chi.
$$

Since $V'(v^2)=0$ and $V''(v^2)=\lambda$, the quadratic potential is

$$
V=V_{\min}+\frac\lambda2v^2(\chi+\chi^*)^2+O(\chi^3).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L^{(2)}=\partial_\mu\chi^*\partial^\mu\chi-\frac{\lambda v^2}{2}(\chi+\chi^*)^2-V_{\min}}.
$$

The constant can be dropped from the fluctuation equations. For canonical real fields, use $\chi=(h+i\pi)/\sqrt2$:

$$
\mathcal L^{(2)}=\frac12(\partial h)^2+\frac12(\partial\pi)^2-\frac12m_h^2h^2,
\qquad
\boxed{m_h^2=2\lambda v^2=-2\ln\lambda>0,\quad m_\pi^2=0}.
$$

These are the [radial and angular masses of a phase-invariant scalar potential](../../../../radial-and-angular-masses-of-a-phase-invariant-scalar-potential.md). The radial [Higgs mode](../../../../higgs-mode.md) changes the field magnitude and costs [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) to second order. The angular [Goldstone boson](../../../../goldstone-boson.md) changes the [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) along the vacuum circle without a quadratic restoring [force](../../../../force-split.md). Its linear dispersion is $\omega^2=|\mathbf k|^2$, whereas the radial mode has $\omega^2=|\mathbf k|^2+m_h^2$. The zero angular [mass](../../../../mass.md) follows the continuous broken global [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md), not from an accidental cancellation in a particular coordinate choice.
