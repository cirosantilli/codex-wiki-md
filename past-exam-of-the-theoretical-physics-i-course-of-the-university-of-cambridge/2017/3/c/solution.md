<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $\phi=(v+h/\sqrt2)e^{i\vartheta}$. The covariant kinetic term contains

$$
(v+h/\sqrt2)^2(\partial_\mu\vartheta+eA_\mu)
(\partial^\mu\vartheta+eA^\mu).
$$

A local [U(1) gauge symmetry](../../../../u-1-gauge-symmetry.md) can remove $\vartheta$ in [unitary gauge](../../../../unitary-gauge.md). The angular fluctuation no longer appears as an independent physical massless [Goldstone boson](../../../../goldstone-boson.md); its degree of freedom supplies the longitudinal polarization of the massive vector field. At quadratic order,

$$
\mathcal L^{(2)}\supset-\frac14F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}+e^2v^2A_\mu A^\mu
=-\frac14F^2+\frac12m_A^2A^2,\qquad
\boxed{m_A^2=2e^2v^2}.
$$

The factor of two follows from the convention that $v$ is the complex field's vacuum value. This [Higgs mechanism](../../../../higgs-mechanism.md) changes a two-polarization massless electromagnetic field into a three-polarization massive [Proca field](../../../../proca-field.md); the remaining radial scalar keeps $m_h^2=2\lambda v^2$. Static fields in the homogeneous broken [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) are screened on a scale of order $m_A^{-1}$ in these units. The gauge [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) is a redundancy of the description; the physical statement is the changed spectrum and disappearance of an independent massless angular mode.
