<h1 id="1/b/uniform-field-shift">Uniform field shift</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

The transformation $\psi\mapsto\psi+b$ leaves both derivatives unchanged, so the [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) and [action](../../../../action.md) are invariant. For infinitesimal unit field variation, the [Noether current for a first-derivative scalar field](../../../../noether-current-for-a-first-derivative-scalar-field.md) is the [shift current of an elastic rod](../../../../shift-current-of-an-elastic-rod.md),

$$
j_1^0=A\rho\dot\psi=\Pi,\qquad j_1^z=-AK\psi',\qquad
\boxed{Q_1=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\Pi\,dz}.
$$

Its divergence vanishes by the [equation of motion](../../../../equation-of-motion.md). The charge is the total longitudinal mechanical [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) to this order in the displacement field.
