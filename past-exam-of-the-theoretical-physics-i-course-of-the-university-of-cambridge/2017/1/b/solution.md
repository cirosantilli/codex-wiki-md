↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Both [Noether charges](../../../../noether-charge.md) require boundary conditions that admit the stated transformations and no net boundary-current flux. Take a sufficiently localized field on the whole rod, with the weighted integrals below finite. Fixed endpoints at finite positions generally spoil the waveform-dilation [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md).
