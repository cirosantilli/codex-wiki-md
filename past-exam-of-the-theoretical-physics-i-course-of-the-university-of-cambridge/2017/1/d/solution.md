**Position-dependent density.** The density $\mathcal L_1$ remains independent of $\psi$ itself, so the shift [Noether charge](../../../../noether-charge.md) survives with its altered [canonical momentum](../../../../canonical-momentum.md):

$$
\boxed{Q_{1,1}=A\int\rho(z)\dot\psi\,dz}.
$$

A prescribed generic function $\rho(z)$ supplies a fixed spatial scale and breaks [scale invariance](../../../../scale-invariance.md). For the dilation-current candidate with the corresponding $\mathcal H_1$ and $\Pi_1$, the [scale-breaking sources in derivative field theories](../../../../scale-breaking-sources-in-derivative-field-theories.md) give

$$
\partial_t j_{D,1}^0+\partial_z j_{D,1}^z=-\frac A2z\rho'(z)\dot\psi^2.
$$

Thus **$Q_2$ is generally not conserved**. Constant density recovers the earlier case; specially scale-invariant profiles and suitably invariant domains would require separate treatment.

**Quartic strain.** The extra term $-\alpha(\psi')^4$ in $\mathcal L_2$ again preserves a uniform field shift. Its time [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is unchanged, while its spatial current becomes $-AK\psi'-4\alpha(\psi')^3$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{Q_{1,2}=A\rho\int\dot\psi\,dz=Q_1}.
$$

The new [Hamiltonian density](../../../../hamiltonian-density.md) is $\mathcal H_2=\mathcal H_0+\alpha(\psi')^4$. Under waveform dilation, the quartic term scales with $b^4$ rather than $b^2$, and

$$
\partial_\mu j_{D,2}^\mu=-2\alpha(\psi')^4.
$$

Hence **there is no corresponding conserved $Q_2$ for nonzero generic $\alpha$**, even if the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) in its candidate expression is updated.

**Field-dependent quadratic stiffness.** In $\mathcal L_3$, the term $-\beta\psi^2(\psi')^2$ breaks the uniform field shift:

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\int\Pi\,dz=-2\beta\int\psi(\psi')^2dz
$$

when spatial flux vanishes. Thus **$Q_1$ is not generally conserved**. However all terms still contain exactly two derivatives in total, with a field of zero dilation weight. The factor $\psi^2$ gains no extra power of $b$, so waveform dilation remains an [action](../../../../action.md) [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md). Its charge is

$$
\boxed{Q_{2,3}=\int\left[t\left(\mathcal H_0+\beta\psi^2(\psi')^2\right)+z\Pi\psi'\right]dz}.
$$

Here $\Pi=A\rho\dot\psi$ as before. Relative to the original charge, the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) term changes; the [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) survives despite the nonlinear field dependence. These statements concern [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) conservation, separately from any restrictions on parameters needed for positive elastic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).
