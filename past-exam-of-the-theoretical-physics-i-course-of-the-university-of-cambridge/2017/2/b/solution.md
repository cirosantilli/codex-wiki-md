<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The azimuth is a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md), so its [canonical momentum](../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is conserved:

$$
\boxed{p_\theta=\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot\theta}=\gamma m\rho^2\dot\theta+q\rho A_\theta},\qquad
\gamma=\frac1{\sqrt{1-\mathbf v^2/c^2}}.
$$

Initially the [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) is parallel to the axis in a field-free region, so $p_\theta=0$. Conservation of [canonical angular momentum across a solenoid fringe](../../../../canonical-angular-momentum-across-a-solenoid-fringe.md) therefore gives, inside the uniform field,

$$
\boxed{\dot\theta=-\frac{qB}{2\gamma m}\equiv-\Omega}.
$$

This holds away from the coordinate singularity $\rho=0$; the Cartesian orbit will be smooth there.

Time independence also conserves the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md),

$$
E=\sum_i\dot q_i\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_i}-L
=\boxed{\gamma mc^2}.
$$

The velocity-linear magnetic term cancels in this [Legendre transform](../../../../convex-conjugate.md). Thus $\gamma=\gamma_v=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$ is constant, as expected because a static magnetic [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) does no work. Mechanical angular [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) can change even though $p_\theta$ and the total speed are conserved.
