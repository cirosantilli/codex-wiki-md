<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With constant $\nu$, the bulk [action](../../../../action.md) is invariant under time translations, translations in the two spatial coordinates, and rotations or reflections within the substrate plane. The [Laplacian](../../../../laplacian.md) has no preferred direction, giving the planar $O(2)$ symmetry. It also has [scalar-field shift symmetry](../../../../scalar-field-shift-symmetry.md) $h\mapsto h+a$, expressing the arbitrary origin of height, and the discrete symmetry $h\mapsto-h$ because its residual is squared.

There is an additional exact tilt symmetry $h\mapsto h+\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf x$: the added slope is time independent and has zero [Laplacian](../../../../laplacian.md). More generally, adding any function $f$ satisfying $f_t=\nu\nabla^2f$ leaves the residual unchanged. Such bulk transformations need not preserve prescribed boundary conditions or a restricted physical interpretation of the height.

In two spatial dimensions the [action](../../../../action.md) also has the anisotropic [scale invariance](../../../../scale-invariance.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x\mapsto b\mathbf x,\qquad t\mapsto b^2t,\qquad h\mapsto h\quad(b>0).}
$$

The spacetime measure scales as $b^4$ and the squared residual as $b^{-4}$. The time and length scales are therefore related diffusively, rather than by a Lorentz transformation.

Ordinary [time reversal](../../../../t-symmetry.md) needs a boundary qualification. The identity

$$
(h_t-\nu\nabla^2h)^2=h_t^2+\nu^2(\nabla^2h)^2+\nu\partial_t|\nabla h|^2-2\nu\nabla\cdot(h_t\nabla h)
$$

shows that the density is time-reversal invariant up to a [total divergence](../../../../total-divergence.md). Thus the bulk stationary equation $h_{tt}-\nu^2\nabla^4h=0$ is invariant under $t\mapsto-t$, although the printed density is not pointwise unchanged. The [time reversal of the squared diffusion-residual action](../../../../time-reversal-of-the-squared-diffusion-residual-action.md) exchanges the growing and decaying branches; the selected first-order [diffusion equation](../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md) alone is not time-reversal invariant at fixed positive $\nu$.
