<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With no [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md), the power relation from part (a) gives

$$
\frac d{dt}(\gamma mc^2)=e\mathbf v\cdot(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)=0.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\gamma(t)=\gamma_0,\qquad |\mathbf v(t)|=|\mathbf v_0|.}
$$

The magnetic part of the [relativistic Lorentz force](../../../../relativistic-lorentz-force.md) changes the direction of [mechanical momentum](../../../../mechanical-momentum.md), but performs no work. This conclusion does not require the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) to be spatially uniform: it follows from the local orthogonality of $\mathbf v$ and $\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$.
