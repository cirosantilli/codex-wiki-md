<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The derivatives of the [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) are $\mathcal L_\phi=0$, $\mathcal L_{\phi_t}=\phi_t$, and $\mathcal L_{\phi_x}=-\phi_x$. The [Euler-Lagrange field equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\phi_{tt}-\phi_{xx}=0.}
$$

This is the [one-dimensional wave equation](../../../../one-dimensional-wave-equation.md) with [wave speed](../../../../wave-speed.md) $c_s=\sqrt{\kappa/\rho}=1$. Both signs of propagation are allowed: $\phi(x,t)=F(x-t)+H(x+t)$ for sufficiently regular profiles.
