<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A one-degree-of-freedom [canonical transformation](../../../../../canonical-transformation.md) requires the [Poisson bracket](../../../../../poisson-bracket.md) $\{x,p\}_{X,P}=1$. To first order,

$$
\{x,p\}_{X,P}
=(1+2\alpha_1X+2\alpha_2P)(1+2\beta_2X+2\beta_3P)+O(\alpha\beta)
=1+2(\alpha_1+\beta_2)X+2(\alpha_2+\beta_3)P+O(2).
$$

The product of the off-diagonal [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) contributes only at second order. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\beta_2=-\alpha_1,\qquad\beta_3=-\alpha_2,}
$$

with $\alpha_3$ and $\beta_1$ unconstrained at this order. The self-brackets vanish identically. This is perturbative preservation of the [symplectic form](../../../../../symplectic-form.md), not exact canonicity of the truncated polynomial map.
