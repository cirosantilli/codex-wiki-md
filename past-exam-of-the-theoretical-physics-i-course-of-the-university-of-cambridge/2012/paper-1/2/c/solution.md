<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Hamilton's equations](../../../../../hamilton-s-equations.md) give $\dot X=P/m+O(\lambda^2)$ and $\dot P=-m\omega^2X+O(\lambda^2)$. Hence, with constants $A\geq0$ and $\delta$ fixed by transformed [initial data](../../../../../initial-data-in-general-relativity.md),

$$
\boxed{X(t)=A\cos\vartheta+O(\lambda^2),\qquad
P(t)=-m\omega A\sin\vartheta+O(\lambda^2),\qquad
\vartheta=\omega t+\delta.}
$$

An equivalent form uses $X(0)\cos\omega t+[P(0)/(m\omega)]\sin\omega t$ for $X(t)$. The indicated errors are a fixed-time perturbative statement; a higher-order [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) correction can accumulate [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) on longer times.
