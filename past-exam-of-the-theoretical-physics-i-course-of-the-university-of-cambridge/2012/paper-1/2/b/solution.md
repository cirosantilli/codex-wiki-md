<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Treat all transformation coefficients as $O(\lambda)$, so products of them with $\lambda$ are second order. Substitution into the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) gives its harmonic part plus

$$
(m\omega^2\alpha_1+\lambda)X^3+
(2m\omega^2\alpha_2+\beta_1/m)X^2P+
(m\omega^2\alpha_3+2\beta_2/m)XP^2+
(\beta_3/m)P^3.
$$

Set these four coefficients to zero and impose the canonical constraints. The result is

$$
\boxed{\alpha_1=-\frac\lambda{m\omega^2},\quad
\alpha_2=0,\quad\alpha_3=-\frac{2\lambda}{m^3\omega^4},\quad
\beta_1=0,\quad\beta_2=\frac\lambda{m\omega^2},\quad\beta_3=0.}
$$

Thus the [first-order canonical removal of a cubic oscillator perturbation](../../../../../first-order-canonical-removal-of-a-cubic-oscillator-perturbation.md) is

$$
\boxed{x=X-\frac\lambda{m\omega^2}X^2-\frac{2\lambda}{m^3\omega^4}P^2,
\qquad p=P+\frac{2\lambda}{m\omega^2}XP,}
$$

up to $O(\lambda^2)$. It yields $K=P^2/(2m)+m\omega^2X^2/2+O(\lambda^2)$. The inverse map, if old [initial data](../../../../../initial-data-in-general-relativity.md) are specified, is $X=x+\lambda x^2/(m\omega^2)+2\lambda p^2/(m^3\omega^4)$ and $P=p-2\lambda xp/(m\omega^2)$ to the same order. There is no time-dependent generating term because the map is autonomous.
