<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the figure's upward-positive vertical coordinate. The support is at $(x,0)$ and the bob at $(x+l\sin\phi,-l\cos\phi)$. Thus

$$
v_2^2=(\dot x+l\dot\phi\cos\phi)^2+(l\dot\phi\sin\phi)^2
=\dot x^2+2l\dot x\dot\phi\cos\phi+l^2\dot\phi^2.
$$

The potential is $V=-m_2gl\cos\phi$, up to an irrelevant constant. Combining both [kinetic energies](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) gives

$$
\boxed{L=\frac12(m_1+m_2)\dot x^2+
\frac12m_2l^2\dot\phi^2+m_2l\dot x\dot\phi\cos\phi+m_2gl\cos\phi.}
$$

The cross term expresses the moving support's contribution to the bob [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md); it would be lost by treating the pivot as fixed.
