<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate with respect to the generalized [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md):

$$
\boxed{p_x=(m_1+m_2)\dot x+m_2l\dot\phi\cos\phi,\qquad
p_\phi=m_2l^2\dot\phi+m_2l\dot x\cos\phi.}
$$

The [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) has no explicit $x$, so $\dot p_x=\partial L/\partial x=0$. This [canonical momentum](../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is also the system's total horizontal mechanical [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md). **Horizontal [translation symmetry](../../../../../translational-symmetry.md) conserves $p_x$** even though the support and bob separately accelerate.
