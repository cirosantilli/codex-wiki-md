<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) function $E=\sum_i\dot q_i\partial L/\partial\dot q_i-L$ is

$$
\boxed{E=\frac m2(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2+\dot z^2)+e\lambda z^2.}
$$

The velocity-linear magnetic term cancels. Since $L$ has no explicit time dependence, $dE/dt=-\partial_tL=0$. Equivalently, magnetic [force](../../../../../force-split.md) does no [work](../../../../../work.md), and electric [work](../../../../../work.md) is exactly minus the change of $e\lambda z^2$. [Energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) here is defined in the supplied time-independent gauge and equals physical kinetic plus [electrostatic potential](../../../../../electric-potential.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
