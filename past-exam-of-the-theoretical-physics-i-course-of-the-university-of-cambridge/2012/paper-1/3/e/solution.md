<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the specified parameter relation,

$$
\boxed{\Omega_z^2=\frac{2e\lambda}{m}
=\left(\frac{2e\mu}{m}\right)^2n^2,
\qquad\Omega_z=|n|\Omega_c.}
$$

Thus there are $|n|$ axial [oscillations](../../../../../oscillation.md) per transverse revolution, and the nondegenerate three-dimensional trajectory closes after $2\pi/\Omega_c$. Since $\lambda>0$, the integer $n=0$ is excluded; changing its sign changes nothing. More generally [commensurate cyclotron and axial oscillations](../../../../../commensurate-cyclotron-and-axial-oscillations.md) close for any rational [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) ratio, whereas irrational ratios give [quasiperiodic motion](../../../../../quasiperiodic-motion.md) when both components are present. An integer ratio is sufficient, not the only closure condition. The same [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) argument also applies to transverse cyclotron circles centred away from the chosen cylindrical origin.
