<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For constant $r=R>0$, the radial equation requires $\dot\theta(\dot\theta+2e\mu/m)=0$. Select the stipulated nonzero [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) and define the positive [cyclotron frequency](../../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md) $\Omega_c=2e\mu/m$. Then

$$
\boxed{\theta(t)=\theta_0-\Omega_ct,\qquad
z(t)=Z\cos(\Omega_zt+\delta),\qquad
\Omega_z=\sqrt{2e\lambda/m}.}
$$

This is a circle in the transverse plane combined with an independent axial [oscillation](../../../../../oscillation.md), as in [uniform magnetic field with axial harmonic confinement](../../../../../uniform-magnetic-field-with-axial-harmonic-confinement.md). Viewed from positive $z$, positive charge rotates clockwise. Its three-dimensional path lies on the cylinder $r=R$ and reverses its vertical motion; **it is generally not a constant-pitch helix**. For $Z=0$ it is a horizontal circle. This orbit has $p_\theta=-e\mu R^2$.
