<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The coordinate $\theta$ is cyclic. Its conjugate [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) is therefore a second [conserved quantity](../../../../../conserved-quantity.md),

$$
\boxed{p_\theta=mr^2\dot\theta+e\mu r^2=\text{constant}.}
$$

It differs from mechanical [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $mr^2\dot\theta$. The magnetic contribution is essential to the rotational [Noether charge](../../../../../noether-charge.md) in this axisymmetric gauge.
