# Hyperbolic circle

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md)

A hyperbolic circle of centre $o$ and radius $R>0$ is the [level set](level-set.md) $\{x:d_{\mathbb H}(o,x)=R\}$ for the [hyperbolic distance](hyperbolic-distance.md). Equally spaced directions from its centre give vertices of a regular geodesic polygon.
