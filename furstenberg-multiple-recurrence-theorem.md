# Furstenberg multiple recurrence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré recurrence theorem](poincare-recurrence-theorem.md)

For a probability [measure-preserving system](measure-preserving-system.md), every measurable $B$ of positive measure and every integer $k\geq2$ admit an integer $n\geq1$ with $\mu(\bigcap_{j=0}^{k-1}T^{-jn}B)>0$. The sets are preimages; neither invertibility nor an [ergodic transformation](ergodicity.md) is required. The [Furstenberg correspondence principle](furstenberg-correspondence-principle.md) converts this simultaneous return into [arithmetic progressions](arithmetic-progression.md) and yields the [Szemerédi theorem](szemeredi-s-theorem.md). The case $k=2$ follows from the [Poincaré recurrence theorem](poincare-recurrence-theorem.md).
