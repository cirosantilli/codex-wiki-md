<h1 id="maschke-s-theorem">Maschke's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Representation theory](representation-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maschke's_theorem)

Every [group representation](group-representation.md) of a [finite group](finite-group.md) over a [field](field.md) whose [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) does not divide the number of elements of the group is [semisimple](semisimple-representation.md). Given an invariant subspace, averaging any projection onto it over the group produces an equivariant projection, whose kernel is an invariant complement.
