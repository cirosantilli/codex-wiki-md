# Maximal right ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Right ideal](right-ideal.md)

A proper [right ideal](right-ideal.md) $L$ is maximal if no proper [right ideal](right-ideal.md) strictly contains it. Equivalently, $A/L$ is a [simple module](irreducible-module.md). The [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) is the intersection of all [maximal right ideals](maximal-right-ideal.md).
