# Poisson equation for differential forms

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md)

This [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) uses the [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md) on [differential forms](differential-form-split.md). On a closed [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), the obstruction to solving it is the component of the source along [harmonic differential forms](harmonic-differential-form.md). The [Green operator of the Hodge Laplacian](green-operator-of-the-hodge-laplacian.md) supplies a solution when that component vanishes.
