# Theory of infinitely many singleton and doubleton equivalence classes

↑ **Parent:** [First-order theory](first-order-theory.md)

In the language containing one binary [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) $E$, require all classes to have size at most two and infinitely many classes of each of the two sizes. This is a [complete theory](complete-theory.md): every countable model has countably many singleton classes and countably many doubleton classes, giving a class-by-class isomorphism; [Downward Lowenheim-Skolem theorem](downward-lowenheim-skolem-theorem.md) then rules out a sentence separating arbitrary models. It is also an [aleph-zero-categorical theory](omega-categorical-theory.md).

It is not finitely axiomatizable. Any finite fragment of the explicit infinitude schemes has a finite model with sufficiently many classes of each size; [compactness theorem](compactness-theorem.md) would make such a finite fragment suffice if any finite axiomatization existed. It is not [model-complete](model-complete-theory.md): adjoining a new singleton preserves the theory, and subsequently giving that new point a partner also preserves the theory, while changing the existential partner formula at that point. Naming the singleton-class predicate removes this particular obstruction to elementary embeddings.
