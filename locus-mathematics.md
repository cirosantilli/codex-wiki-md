# Locus (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locus_(mathematics))

A locus is the [set](set-split.md) of [points](point-geometry.md) satisfying specified geometric conditions. A [circle](circle.md) is the locus at fixed [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) from a point; an [ellipse](ellipse.md) is the locus with fixed sum of distances to its [foci of a conic](focus-of-a-conic.md).
