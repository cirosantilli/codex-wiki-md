# Parton factorization scale

↑ **Parent:** [Parton distribution function](parton-distribution-function.md)

The scale at which collinear contributions are assigned to [parton distribution functions](parton-distribution-function.md) rather than the hard scattering coefficient. The separate factors depend on this scale, while a consistently calculated physical [differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md) does not.
