# BQP error reduction

↑ **Parent:** [BQP](bqp.md)

Independent runs of a [BQP](bqp.md) algorithm with fresh [ancilla qubits](ancilla-qubit.md) can be combined by a classical threshold rule. If completeness exceeds soundness by $\gamma$, a [Hoeffding inequality](hoeffding-inequality.md) bounds the error after $r$ runs by $e^{-r\gamma^2/2}$. Polynomial repetition handles inverse-polynomial gaps. This does not automatically preserve the restricted gate and measurement rules of a [stoquastic circuit](stoquastic-circuit.md).
