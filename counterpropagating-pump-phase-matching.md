# Counterpropagating-pump phase matching

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate four-wave mixing](degenerate-four-wave-mixing.md)

For opposite equal pump [wave vectors](wavevector.md), a conjugate mixing source has [wave vector](wavevector.md) $-\mathbf k_3$. In an isotropic nondispersive medium this exactly matches a free backward wave at the generated [frequency](frequency.md) only in the degenerate case. Away from degeneracy the longitudinal mismatch has magnitude $2n|\omega_P-\omega_3|/c$; a finite interaction length supplies a tolerance, not exact vector matching.
