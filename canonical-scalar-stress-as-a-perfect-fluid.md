# Canonical scalar stress as a perfect fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md)

For a canonical scalar with timelike gradient, its [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) has perfect-fluid form. In a homogeneous background, $\rho=\dot\phi^2/2+V$ and $p=\dot\phi^2/2-V$. Potential domination gives negative pressure, while the scalar's physical rest-frame [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) is one. A constant negative background $w$ therefore does not force its perturbations to obey the adiabatic barotropic closure $\delta p=w\delta\rho$.
