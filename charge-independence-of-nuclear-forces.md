# Charge independence of nuclear forces

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md)

The approximate invariance of the strong [nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md) under [isospin](isospin.md) rotations. It compares channels with the same [spin](spin.md), orbital and total-isospin quantum numbers; it does not identify the spin-triplet isospin-zero [deuteron](deuteron.md) channel with the spin-singlet isospin-one neutron-neutron channel. Electromagnetic effects and [quark](quark.md) [mass](mass.md) differences break the approximation.
