# Octet representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

An eight-dimensional representation. For SU(3), the adjoint [octet](octet-representation.md) is realized by traceless matrices, and decomposing $\bar3\otimes3$ separates this traceless part from the scalar [singlet](singlet-representation.md).
