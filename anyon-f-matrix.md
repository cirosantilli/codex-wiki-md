# Anyon F-matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon fusion tree](anyon-fusion-tree.md)

The [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) relating the [anyon fusion trees](anyon-fusion-tree.md) $((ab)_u c)_t$ and $(a(bc)_v)_t$. Fix the convention $|((ab)_u c)_t\rangle=\sum_v[F^{abc}_t]_{uv}|(a(bc)_v)_t\rangle$. An inverse change of parentheses uses the adjoint [matrix](matrix.md), so its entries are complex conjugates with exchanged indices.
