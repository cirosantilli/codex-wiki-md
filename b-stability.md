# B-stability

↑ **Parent:** [A-stability](a-stability.md)

A Runge--Kutta method is B-stable when it does not increase distances between numerical solutions of a dissipative differential equation. [Algebraic stability of a Runge-Kutta method](algebraic-stability-of-a-runge-kutta-method.md) is a standard sufficient condition.
