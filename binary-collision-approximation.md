# Binary collision approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation-split.md)

Treat each interaction as an isolated two-particle scattering event, neglecting simultaneous interactions involving three or more particles. For a dilute short-range [gas](gas.md), the interaction range is much smaller than the [mean free path](mean-free-path.md), making this approximation compatible with a local [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation-split.md). It is distinct from [molecular chaos for the Boltzmann equation](molecular-chaos-for-the-boltzmann-equation.md), which is the statistical factorization of incoming pair distributions.
