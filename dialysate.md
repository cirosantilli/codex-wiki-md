# Dialysate

↑ **Parent:** [Dialysis (biochemistry)](dialysis-biochemistry.md)

The external [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) on the other side of a [dialysis](dialysis-biochemistry.md) [semipermeable membrane](semipermeable-membrane.md). Its composition supplies the boundary condition for exchange: it can receive unwanted small [solutes](solute.md) or supply desired small [solutes](solute.md). A bath replacement discards the old [dialysate](dialysate.md) and introduces a new reservoir with a specified composition.
