# Finite normalization of an energy-only Kepler distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Delaunay phase-volume proof of the thermal eccentricity distribution](delaunay-phase-volume-proof-of-the-thermal-eccentricity-distribution.md)

For bound [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md) with gravitational parameter $\mu$, integrating the [Delaunay variables](delaunay-variables.md) over $0<G<L$, $-G<H<G$ and their three full angle periods gives the displayed population. A globally defined [thermal eccentricity distribution](thermal-eccentricity-distribution.md) requires this integral to converge, or an explicit finite action range. Merely having finite $F(E)$ at every energy does not suffice: $F=1$ yields an infinite integral at large $L$. At each fixed energy, or on every finite normalized action band, the cumulative eccentricity fraction remains $e^2$.
