# Single-particle tracking PALM

↑ **Parent:** [Photoactivated localization microscopy](photoactivated-localization-microscopy.md)

[Sparse photoactivation](sparse-photoactivation.md) allows successive positions of individual labelled molecules to be linked into trajectories; activation of fresh subsets yields many tracks in living cells. This extends [photoactivated localization microscopy](photoactivated-localization-microscopy.md) from accumulated positions to mobility. For free two-dimensional [diffusion](diffusion.md) with independent per-coordinate [fluorescence localization precision](fluorescence-localization-precision.md) $\sigma$, instantaneous frames give mean squared displacement $4D\Delta t+4\sigma^2$. [Motion blur](motion-blur.md), tracking errors, confinement and photobleaching must be accounted for before assigning mobility classes.[Abstract and pages 155–156: combining sparse activation with single-particle trajectories](high-density-mapping-of-single-molecule-trajectories-with-photoactivated-localization-microscopy-by-suliana-manley-and-others.md)
