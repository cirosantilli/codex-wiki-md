<h1 id="1-3-diaxial-interaction">1,3-Diaxial interaction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Steric hindrance](steric-hindrance.md)

A [1,3-Diaxial interaction](1-3-diaxial-interaction.md) is the close nonbonded contact between axial groups separated by one ring atom in a chair. Replacing a [carbon](carbon.md)-bearing axial [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) by an [oxygen](oxygen.md) changes this contact, so cyclohexane substituent preferences cannot be transferred unchanged to dioxanes.
