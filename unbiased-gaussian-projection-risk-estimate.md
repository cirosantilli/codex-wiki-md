# Unbiased Gaussian projection risk estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-vector prediction risk](mean-vector-prediction-risk.md)

For $Y\sim N_n(\mu,\sigma^2I)$ and a fixed [orthogonal projection matrix](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) $P$ of rank $k$, the displayed expression is an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of the [mean-vector prediction risk](mean-vector-prediction-risk.md) whenever $\widehat\sigma^2$ is an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of $\sigma^2$. [Independence](independent-random-variables.md) of its two terms is unnecessary. Comparing fixed models yields the [Mallows Cp](mallows-s-cp.md) penalty, but minimizing unbiased estimates does not preserve unbiasedness after selection.
