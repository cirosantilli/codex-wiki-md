# Horizontal strip

↑ **Parent:** [Skew Young diagram](skew-young-diagram.md)

A horizontal strip is a [skew Young diagram](skew-young-diagram.md) containing at most one cell in each column. Equivalently, $\lambda/\mu$ is a horizontal strip when $\lambda_i\geq\mu_i\geq\lambda_{i+1}$ for all $i$. The multiplicity of the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) in the associated [skew representation of a symmetric group](skew-representation-of-a-symmetric-group.md) is one for horizontal strips and zero otherwise. A column with two cells supplies a cyclic tableau vector on which an adjacent [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) acts by $-1$, excluding invariants.
