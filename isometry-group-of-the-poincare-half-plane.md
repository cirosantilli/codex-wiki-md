# Isometry group of the Poincare half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [PSL2(R)](psl2-r.md)

Every orientation-preserving isometry of the [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md) lies in $PSL_2(\mathbb R)$. Every orientation-reversing isometry is the composition of one of these with the [hyperbolic reflection](hyperbolic-reflection.md) $z\mapsto-\overline z$ in the imaginary axis. Equivalently, the full isometry group consists of real Möbius and anti-Möbius transformations, modulo nonzero scalar matrices.
