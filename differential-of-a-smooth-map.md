# Differential of a smooth map

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth map between manifolds](smooth-map-between-manifolds.md)

For a [smooth map between manifolds](smooth-map-between-manifolds.md) $f:M\to N$, the [differential](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) $df_x:T_xM\to T_{f(x)}N$ is the linear map obtained by differentiating in charts. Intrinsically it sends the tangent vector of a curve $\gamma$ through $x$ to the tangent vector of $f\circ\gamma$. The chain rule makes it independent of charts and gives $d(g\circ f)_x=dg_{f(x)}\circ df_x$.
