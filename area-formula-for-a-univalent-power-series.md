# Area formula for a univalent power series

↑ **Parent:** [Univalent function](univalent-function.md)

For an [injective](injective-function.md) [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $f(z)=\sum_{n\geq0}c_nz^n$ on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), its real [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $|f'(z)|^2$. [Change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) gives image [area](surface-area.md) $\int_{|z|<r}|f'(z)|^2dA$. Angular [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) removes cross terms of the differentiated [power series](power-series.md); integrating $2\pi n^2|c_n|^2\rho^{2n-1}$ radially gives the title formula. For $r<1$, uniform convergence on a slightly larger [closed disk](closed-disc.md) justifies termwise operations. Injectivity is essential for this to be image [area](surface-area.md) rather than [multiplicity of a parametrized surface integral](multiplicity-of-a-parametrized-surface-integral.md).
