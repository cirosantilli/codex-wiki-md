# Greenhouse gas radiative efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Greenhouse gas](greenhouse-gas.md)

The incremental [radiative forcing](radiative-forcing.md) per increment of a specified atmospheric gas burden. State whether the abundance unit is emitted [mass](mass.md), mole fraction or ppb, and specify the radiation-adjustment convention. For equal emitted masses in a fixed atmospheric air inventory, a per-ppb coefficient must be divided by the gas [molar mass](molar-mass.md) before comparing efficiencies. Spectral overlap with existing absorption makes efficiency depend on the background state.
