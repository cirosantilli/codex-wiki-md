# Accretion-powered effective-temperature scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-radius accretion energy budget](constant-radius-accretion-energy-budget.md)

For constant supply, negligible seed [mass](mass.md) and fixed emitting radius, $M=\dot Mt$ and $L_{\rm acc}\propto t$. Thermal emission with $L=4\pi r_0^2\sigma_{\rm SB}T_{\rm eff}^4$ therefore gives the quarter-power law. It describes an effective surface [temperature](temperature-split.md). A homologous ideal-gas interior at the same fixed radius instead has [virial temperature](virial-temperature.md) proportional to [mass](mass.md) and hence to time; its absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md) requires a structural model.
