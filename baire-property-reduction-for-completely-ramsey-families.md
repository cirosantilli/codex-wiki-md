# Baire-property reduction for completely Ramsey families

↑ **Parent:** [Ellentuck Borel sets are completely Ramsey](ellentuck-borel-sets-are-completely-ramsey.md)

In the [Ellentuck topology](ellentuck-topology.md), suppose every [open set](open-set.md) is [completely Ramsey](completely-ramsey-set.md) and every [meagre set](meagre-set.md) is [completely Ramsey-null](completely-ramsey-null-set.md). If $E\triangle U$ is contained in a meagre set for some open $U$, first refine a basic [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) without changing its stem to avoid that exceptional set, and then refine again to be homogeneous for $U$. The resulting neighbourhood is homogeneous for $E$. Sets with such an open representative form a [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md): countable unions collect the meagre errors, and complements replace $U^c$ by its interior, differing only on the nowhere-dense boundary of $U$. This proves that all star-[Borel sets](borel-set.md) are completely Ramsey.
