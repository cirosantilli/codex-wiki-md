# Covering graph

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covering_graph)

A covering graph is a graph map that induces a bijection on the incident oriented edges at every vertex. Connected based covers of a graph correspond to subgroups of its fundamental group.
