# Proximity labeling

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

[proximity labeling](proximity-labeling.md) uses a localized reactive system to mark nearby [molecules](molecule-split.md) before extraction. The recovered label integrates access, distance, labeling rate and residence time; proximity alone does not demonstrate a direct [protein-protein interaction](protein-protein-interaction.md).
