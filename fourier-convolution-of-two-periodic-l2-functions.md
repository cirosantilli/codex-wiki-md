# Fourier convolution of two periodic L2 functions

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic convolution operator](periodic-convolution-operator.md)

With $e_n(s)=(2\pi)^{-1/2}e^{ins}$, periodic $L^2$ functions have the displayed convolution identity for every real $t$. Apply the polarized [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md) to $f$ and $\overline{g(t-\cdot)}$; a change of variable gives the latter function's pairing with $e_n$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) bounds the sum of absolute coefficient products by $\|f\|_2\|g\|_2$, so the resulting series converges absolutely and uniformly. This proves [continuity](continuous-function.md) of the convolution even though neither original [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) need converge [pointwise](pointwise-convergence.md).
