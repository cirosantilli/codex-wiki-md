# Exponential limit

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential function](exponential-function.md)

For fixed real $x$, the base $1+x/n$ is positive for sufficiently large $n$. Applying [Taylor's theorem](taylor-s-theorem.md) to the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md) at one gives $n\log(1+x/n)=x+O(n^{-1})$. Continuity of the [exponential function](exponential-function.md) then proves the displayed [limit](limit-of-a-function.md). In particular $(1-z/n)^n\to e^{-z}$ for fixed $z\geq0$, a basic source of [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) limits in [probability theory](probability-theory-split.md).
