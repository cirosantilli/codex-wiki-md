# Virtual intermediate state in two-photon absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Two-photon absorption](two-photon-absorption.md)

An intermediate contribution to a second-order transition amplitude need not be an occupied, on-shell stationary atomic state. Both time orderings must be summed with their [energy](energy-split.md) denominators. Large detuning can suppress real intermediate occupation while preserving a coherent two-photon coupling. Near resonance, finite linewidth, saturation and sequential population transfer must be treated explicitly.
