# Schinzel-Tijdeman exponent theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine equation](diophantine-equation.md)

For a fixed [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $f\in\mathbb Q[X]$ with at least two distinct complex roots, [integer](integer.md) solutions with $m\ge2$ and $|y|>1$ have an effectively bounded exponent. Factorization over a [splitting field](splitting-field.md) and the fixed differences between distinct roots control common [ideal](ideal.md) [divisors](divisor.md). Outside a fixed set of primes, each factor's valuation is divisible by $m/\gcd(m,e_i)$, where $e_i$ is its root [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md). The generalized power-equation estimates from [linear forms in logarithms of algebraic numbers](linear-form-in-logarithms-of-algebraic-numbers.md), including [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) and exceptional-prime factors, give the exponent bound. A single-root [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) and the values $y=0,\pm1$ cannot be included: $4^m=(2^m)^2$, and $1^m=f(0)$ for $f(X)=X(X-1)+1$, give infinite exponent families.
