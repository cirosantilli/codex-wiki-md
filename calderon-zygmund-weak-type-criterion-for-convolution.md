<h1 id="calderon-zygmund-weak-type-criterion-for-convolution">Calderón–Zygmund weak type criterion for convolution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hörmander integral kernel condition](hormander-integral-kernel-condition.md)

Suppose a [convolution](convolution.md) operator has $L^2$ operator norm at most $A$, and its kernel satisfies the [Hörmander integral kernel condition](hormander-integral-kernel-condition.md) with constant $C$. Under hypotheses making its integrals well-defined, the [Calderón–Zygmund decomposition](calderon-zygmund-decomposition.md) gives [weak type (1,1)](weak-type-1-1.md). Indeed, the bounded part has squared $L^2$ norm at most $2^d\lambda\|f\|_1$. The bad cubes occupy at most $\|f\|_1/\lambda$, while outside their $4\sqrt d$ dilations the [cancellation estimate outside a dilated cube](cancellation-estimate-outside-a-dilated-cube.md) bounds the bad image in $L^1$. Applying distributional estimates to these three regions gives $|\{|Tf|>\lambda\}|\leq((4\sqrt d)^d+2^{d+2}A^2+4C)\|f\|_1/\lambda$.
