# Carbon cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Climate change](climate-change.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_cycle)

Carbon is exchanged among atmospheric, oceanic, biological and geological reservoirs. A model that only redistributes carbon among closed reservoirs cannot permanently remove its total excess burden.
