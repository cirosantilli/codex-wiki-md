# Hydraulic control over a smooth hump

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md)

A slowly varying, steady, inviscid channel flow has constant discharge $Q=uh$ per unit width and the displayed [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md). The bed-height function of depth has its maximum at $h_c=(Q^2/g)^{1/3}$, where the [Froude number](froude-number.md) is one. At a nondegenerate critical crest, a differentiable profile changes between the [subcritical flow](subcritical-flow.md) and [supercritical flow](supercritical-flow.md) branches. With subcritical upstream squared Froude number $F<1$ and upstream depth $h_1$, the shock-free supercritical downstream depth is $h_1(F+\sqrt{F^2+8F})/4$.
