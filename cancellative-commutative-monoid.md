# Cancellative commutative monoid

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative monoid](commutative-monoid.md)

A [commutative monoid](commutative-monoid.md) is cancellative when $a+c=b+c$ implies $a=b$. Cancellation makes the natural map into its [Grothendieck group](grothendieck-group.md) injective.
