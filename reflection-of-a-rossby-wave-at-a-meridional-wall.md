# Reflection of a Rossby wave at a meridional wall

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow-water Rossby-wave dispersion relation](shallow-water-rossby-wave-dispersion-relation.md)

A stationary straight meridional wall preserves frequency and tangential wavenumber $l$. The two normal wavenumbers obey

$$
k_i k_r=l^2+a^2,\qquad k_i+k_r=-\frac\beta\omega.
$$

Incidence and reflection are distinguished by the normal [group velocity](group-velocity.md). In the leading [quasi-geostrophic approximation](quasi-geostrophic-approximation.md), a nonzero $l$ and the [impermeability condition](no-penetration-boundary-condition.md) give reflected height amplitude $A_r=-A_i$. The $l=0$ no-normal-flow condition is degenerate in that reduced model; exact ageostrophic transport generally changes the amplitude ratio.
