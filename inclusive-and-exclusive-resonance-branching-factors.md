# Inclusive and exclusive resonance branching factors

↑ **Parent:** [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md)

Inclusive formation sums over all resonance decays and has exit [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md) one. Selecting a particular final channel instead multiplies by that channel's branching fraction. Thus resonant elastic scattering contains the square of the entrance branching fraction, while inclusive formation contains only one power.
