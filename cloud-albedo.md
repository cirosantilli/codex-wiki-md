# Cloud albedo

↑ **Parent:** [Albedo](albedo.md)

The fraction of incident radiation reflected by a [cloud](cloud.md) under a specified spectral and angular convention. For the approximate shortwave relation $A=\tau/(\tau+7)$, increasing [optical depth](optical-depth.md) raises [albedo](albedo.md) with diminishing sensitivity near $A=1$. Transmitted and absorbed radiation must also be accounted for in the [energy](energy-split.md) budget.
