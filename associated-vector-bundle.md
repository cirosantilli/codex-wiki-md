# Associated vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Principal bundle](principal-bundle.md)

A linear representation $\rho:G\to GL(V)$ of a [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md)'s structure group defines the quotient of $P\times V$ by $(p,v)\sim(pg,\rho(g)^{-1}v)$. It is a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) over the same base. A [principal connection](connection-principal-bundle.md) induces [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) and a local [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) $d+\rho_*(A)$ on its sections.
