# Colour-magnitude diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)

A plot of stellar magnitude against a colour index. It is an observational counterpart of the [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md), but converting colour to [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md) and magnitude to [luminosity](luminosity.md) requires calibration, extinction and distance information. A coeval population's [main-sequence turnoff](main-sequence-turnoff.md) helps estimate its [stellar age](stellar-age.md).
