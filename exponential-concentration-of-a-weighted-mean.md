# Exponential concentration of a weighted mean

↑ **Parent:** [Limit of a function](limit-of-a-function.md)

For finitely many real numbers $a_1,\ldots,a_m$, let $M=\max_ja_j$. Divide numerator and denominator of their exponentially weighted mean by $e^{M/\tau}$. Every term with $a_j<M$ vanishes as $\tau\downarrow0$, while each maximizer contributes $M$ to the numerator and one to the denominator. Their quotient therefore tends to $M$, even when the maximum is negative or attained more than once. The finiteness of the list is used to pass the [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) through each sum.
