# Linear basal drag law

↑ **Parent:** [Basal sliding](basal-sliding.md)

A linear basal drag law has resisting traction $-\mu\beta u_b$, with $\beta>0$ of dimension inverse length. It idealizes lubrication by [subglacial till](subglacial-till.md) and is equivalent to a [Navier slip boundary condition](navier-slip-boundary-condition.md) of [slip length](slip-length.md) $1/\beta$ when the ice is a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md). It is a constitutive idealization, not a universal sliding law.
