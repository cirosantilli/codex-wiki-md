# Vector-fluid momentum dilution

↑ **Parent:** [Vector cosmological perturbation](vector-cosmological-perturbation.md)

In [linear cosmological perturbation theory](linear-cosmological-perturbation-theory-split.md), an unsourced [vector cosmological perturbation](vector-cosmological-perturbation.md) of a [perfect fluid](perfect-fluid.md) in [synchronous gauge](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md) obeys [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) in the form $\mathbf q'+4\mathcal H\mathbf q=0$, where $\mathbf q=(\bar\rho+\bar P)\mathbf v_\perp$ is the [momentum density](momentum-density.md), $\mathcal H$ the [conformal Hubble parameter](conformal-hubble-parameter.md), and the prime a [conformal time](conformal-time.md) [derivative](derivative.md). Its conserved quantity is $a^4\mathbf q$. For constant [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w\ne-1$, the background [cosmological continuity equation](cosmological-continuity-equation.md) gives $\mathbf v_\perp\propto a^{3w-1}$. Thus a [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) vector velocity falls as $a^{-1}$, while a radiation vector velocity is constant. This statement assumes vanishing vector [anisotropic stress](anisotropic-stress.md) and no momentum exchange with another component.
