# Nematic hedgehog defect

↑ **Parent:** [Topological defect](topological-defect.md)

A three-dimensional point defect characterized by how the surrounding [nematic director](nematic-director.md) covers orientation space. Its hedgehog charge is not the same quantity as a planar disclination winding strength.
