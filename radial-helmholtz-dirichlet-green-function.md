# Radial Helmholtz Dirichlet Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function.md)

For $L=d^2/dx^2+(2/x)d/dx+k^2$ on $0<x<1$, impose finiteness at zero and zero value at one. Set $H=xG$; then $H''+k^2H=\xi\delta(x-\xi)$ with ordinary zero-endpoint conditions. The displayed [Green function](green-s-function.md) follows from the left/right sine solutions, where $x_<=\min(x,\xi)$ and $x_>=\max(x,\xi)$. Its derivative jumps by one. It exists for $k\notin\pi\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$; at zero take the removable limit $G=-\xi x_<(1-x_>)/x$. At nonzero integer multiples of pi the radial problem has a nonzero homogeneous solution and a [Fredholm solvability condition](fredholm-solvability-condition.md). Integrating against the constant forcing gives $y=k^{-2}[1-\sin(kx)/(x\sin k)]$, with $y=(x^2-1)/6$ at $k=0$.
