# Injective resolution of sheaves

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md)

An injective resolution is an [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) starting with a [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) and continuing with [injective sheaves](injective-sheaf.md). It is constructed by successively embedding each quotient in an injective sheaf. Taking [global sections](global-section.md) and then cohomology computes [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md); in particular an injective sheaf has zero higher cohomology.
