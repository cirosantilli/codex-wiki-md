# Diode reverse breakdown

↑ **Parent:** [Diode](diode.md)

A sufficiently large reverse [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) can produce a rapidly increasing reverse [electric current](electric-current.md). Both the [ideal diode model](ideal-diode-model.md) and the unextended [Shockley diode equation](shockley-diode-equation.md) omit this behavior.[The Junction Diode, §1.7, printed p. 17: breakdown after the small reverse-current region](the-junction-diode-by-w-marshall-leach-jr.md)
