# Roots of unity at the tame ramification boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Valuation of a primitive p-power root of unity](valuation-of-a-primitive-p-power-root-of-unity.md)

For odd $p$, both possibilities occur at $v(p)=p-1$: $\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_p)$ contains $p$-torsion, while $\mathbb Q_p(\sqrt[p-1]{p})$ does not. Both extensions are totally ramified of degree $p-1$ by the [Eisenstein criterion](eisenstein-criterion.md). In the second field, if $\zeta_p=1+u\pi$ with $\pi^{p-1}=p$, the cyclotomic equation reduced after division by $p$ would give $1+\bar u^{p-1}=0$ in $\mathbb F_p$, contradicting $\bar u^{p-1}=1$. For $p=2$, $v(2)=1$ always permits the root $-1$, so the torsion exponent is exactly one and the zero-exponent alternative is impossible.
