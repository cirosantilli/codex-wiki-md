# Power-law spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation spectrum](radiation-spectrum.md)

A [radiation spectrum](radiation-spectrum.md) whose [spectral flux density](spectral-flux-density.md) follows a [power law](power-law.md) over a stated [frequency](frequency.md) range. For $F_\nu\propto\nu^{-\alpha}$, the [spectral energy distribution](spectral-energy-distribution.md) satisfies $\nu F_\nu\propto\nu^{1-\alpha}$. Thus $\alpha=1$ gives equal [energy](energy-split.md) flux in equal logarithmic [frequency](frequency.md) intervals. A spectral break invalidates extrapolation with a single index.
