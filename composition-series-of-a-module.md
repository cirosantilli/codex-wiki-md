# Composition series of a module

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

A composition series of a [module](module-mathematics.md) is a finite chain of [submodules](submodule.md) whose nonzero successive [quotient modules](quotient-module.md) are [simple modules](irreducible-module.md). Every finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) has such a chain, viewed as a [module](module-mathematics.md) over the [universal enveloping algebra](universal-enveloping-algebra.md).
