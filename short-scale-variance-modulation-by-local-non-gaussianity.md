# Short-scale variance modulation by local non-Gaussianity

↑ **Parent:** [Local-type primordial non-Gaussianity](local-type-primordial-non-gaussianity.md)

For a long potential approximately constant over the short-scale region, [local-type primordial non-Gaussianity](local-type-primordial-non-gaussianity.md) gives $\varphi_s=(1+2f_{\rm NL}\varphi_{G,L})\varphi_{G,s}$, to first order in the long mode and ignoring short-mode squares. A linear [cosmological Poisson equation](cosmological-poisson-equation.md) therefore gives

$$
\sigma_s^2=\sigma_{G,s}^2(1+4f_{\rm NL}\varphi_{G,L})
$$

to the same order. The [variance](variance-split.md) is conditional on the long mode.
