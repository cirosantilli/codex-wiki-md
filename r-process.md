# r-process

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar nucleosynthesis](stellar-nucleosynthesis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/r-process)

At sufficiently large neutron density, successive [neutron captures](neutron-capture.md) outrun relevant beta decays and produce very neutron-rich nuclei. Decay toward stability afterward yields many heavy isotopes, including some actinides. Neutron-rich ejecta from [neutron star mergers](neutron-star-merger.md) provide an established site; particular magnetically driven collapse outflows are additional candidates. This does not imply that every ordinary [core-collapse supernova](core-collapse-supernova.md) produces a complete heavy-element pattern.
