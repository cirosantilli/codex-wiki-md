# Euler theorem on four squares in arithmetic progression

↑ **Parent:** [Mordell-Weil group of y squared equals x times x plus one times x plus four](mordell-weil-group-of-y-squared-equals-x-times-x-plus-one-times-x-plus-four.md)

There is no nonconstant four-term [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) of rational square numbers. If $r^2,s^2,1,t^2$ formed one, then $(-2s^2,2rst)$ would lie on $y^2=x(x+1)(x+4)$; its explicitly known [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md) forces $s^2=1$ and the common difference to vanish.
