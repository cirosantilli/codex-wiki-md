# Apparent retrograde motion

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary system dynamics](planetary-system-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apparent_retrograde_motion)

A temporary reversal in a planet's apparent direction across the background sky, arising from the observer's changing [position](position.md). For an outer planet observed from the [Earth](earth.md), the [Earth](earth.md) overtakes it along its smaller, faster [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md). Different apparent reversals for two planets can produce three apparent [conjunctions](conjunction-astronomy.md) around one [Sun](sun.md)-centred encounter, even when both follow prograde [circular orbits](circular-orbit.md).
