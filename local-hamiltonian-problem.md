# Local Hamiltonian problem

↑ **Parent:** [Local Hamiltonian](local-hamiltonian.md)

Given a polynomial list of fixed-locality Hermitian terms and thresholds $a<b$ separated by at least an inverse polynomial, distinguish whether the minimum [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of their sum is at most $a$ or at least $b$. Terms may be normalized to $0\leq h_j\leq I$ with polynomial rescaling. This [promise problem](promise-problem.md) is in [QMA](qma.md) by [local-energy measurement verification](local-energy-measurement-verification.md).
