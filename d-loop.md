# D-loop

↑ **Parent:** [DNA repair](dna-repair.md)

A [D-loop](d-loop.md) is a local strand-invasion structure in which an incoming strand pairs with one strand of a duplex and displaces the other. It is a useful intermediate and assay product in [homologous recombination](homologous-recombination.md).
