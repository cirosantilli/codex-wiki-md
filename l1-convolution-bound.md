# L1 convolution bound

↑ **Parent:** [Convolution](convolution.md)

[Tonelli's theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) gives the product [integral](integral-split.md) of $|g(x-y)h(y)|$ as $\|g\|_1\|h\|_1$. [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) then defines the [convolution](convolution.md) [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) and gives an integrable result with [norm](norm.md) at most that product. [Multiplication](multiplication.md) by a [complex exponential](complex-exponential-function.md) of [modulus](modulus.md) one preserves [absolute integrability](integrability.md) and permits the usual [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) [convolution](convolution.md) identity.
