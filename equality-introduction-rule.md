# Equality introduction rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

The reflexivity rule for [first-order equality](logical-equality.md): any well-formed term $t$ satisfies $t=t$ without additional premises. Together with the [equality elimination rule](equality-elimination-rule.md), it derives symmetry: from $s=t$ and $s=s$, substitute in the first designated argument position of $u=s$ to obtain $t=s$. Transitivity follows from $s=t$ and $t=u$ by substitution in the second designated argument position of $s=v$.
