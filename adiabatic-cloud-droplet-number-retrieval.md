# Adiabatic cloud droplet number retrieval

↑ **Parent:** [Cloud albedo](cloud-albedo.md)

A shallow liquid cloud with $w=c_wh$ has [liquid-water path](liquid-water-path.md) $W=c_wH^2/2$. If $N$, extinction efficiency $Q$ and shape factor $k$ are constant with height, integrating droplet extinction gives $\tau_c=(3/5)\pi Qk^{1/3}[3c_w/(4\pi\rho_w)]^{2/3}N^{1/3}H^{5/3}$. Eliminating $H$ yields $N=[250\sqrt2/(243\pi Q^3k)]\rho_w^2c_w^{1/2}\tau_c^3/W^{5/2}$. Nonadiabatic water profiles and biased water paths strongly affect the retrieval.
