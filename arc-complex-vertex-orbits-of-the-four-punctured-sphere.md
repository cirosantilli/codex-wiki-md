# Arc-complex vertex orbits of the four-punctured sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Arc complex](arc-complex.md)

The mapping class group of the four-punctured sphere has two orbits on vertices of its arc complex: arcs whose endpoints are distinct and arcs whose endpoints coincide. Each orbit is infinite, and [Dehn twists](dehn-twist.md) exhibit infinitely many classes of the second type based at any fixed puncture.
