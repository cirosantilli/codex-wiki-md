# Poisson large-service overflow asymptotic

↑ **Parent:** [Bufferless queue](bufferless-queue.md)

For fixed [Poisson](poisson-distribution.md) mean $\lambda>0$ and capacity $c\to\infty$, set $m=\lfloor c\rfloor+1$. The optimized [Chernoff bound](chernoff-bound.md) gives $\log\mathbb P(X\ge m)\le-I_P(m)$. The lower bound by $\mathbb P(X=m)$ and [Stirling's formula](stirling-formula.md) differs by only $O(\log m)$. Rounding $m$ to $c$ changes the cost by $O(\log c)$, proving the displayed formula and the leading scale $-c\log c$.
