# Nonlinear acoustics

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_acoustics)

[Nonlinear acoustics](nonlinear-acoustics.md) retains finite-amplitude effects in [sound waves](acoustic-wave.md), including amplitude-dependent propagation [speed](speed.md), distortion and possible [shock waves](shock-wave.md). For a one-dimensional [polytropic flow](polytropic-flow.md) with $p\propto\rho^\gamma$, a rightgoing simple wave has characteristic [speed](speed.md) $u+c=c_0+(\gamma+1)u/2$ relative to a quiescent reference. Thus small-amplitude [linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md) need not be uniform when a background [velocity](velocity.md) nearly cancels the [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md).
