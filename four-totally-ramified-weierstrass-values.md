# Four totally ramified Weierstrass values

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

The [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) has degree two on its period torus. Its three distinct half-period values $e_j$ have double local preimages, and its [pole](pole.md) is double, so these four values are totally ramified. This also follows from $\wp'^2=4\prod_j(\wp-e_j)$ and the principal part at the [pole](pole.md). A [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) with [pole](pole.md) away from these values moves all four to finite values without changing local degrees.
