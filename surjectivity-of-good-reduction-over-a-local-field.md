# Surjectivity of good reduction over a local field

↑ **Parent:** [Good reduction of an elliptic curve](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

If an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) has [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) over a [local field](local-field.md) $K$, its integral projective model is smooth. Every point over the [residue field](residue-field.md) lifts by the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md), applied in a chart with a unit [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md). Thus reduction gives an exact sequence $0\to E_1(K)\to E(K)\to\widetilde E(k)\to0$. The [kernel of reduction of an elliptic curve](kernel-of-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) is the [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) of the [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md), with the [formal group of an elliptic curve](formal-group-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) as its [group operation](group-operation.md).
