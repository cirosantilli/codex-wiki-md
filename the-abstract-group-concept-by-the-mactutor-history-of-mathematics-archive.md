# The abstract group concept by the MacTutor History of Mathematics Archive

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)

Archive exposition based on a 19 March 2001 lecture by [Peter Michael Neumann](peter-m-neumann.md). Its account compares concrete substitutions, abstract definitions and [Heinrich Weber](heinrich-martin-weber.md)'s finite cancellation axioms. The byline explains the relation between the lecture and the written notes.
