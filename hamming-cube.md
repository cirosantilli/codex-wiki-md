# Hamming cube

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md)

The [Hamming cube](hamming-cube.md) is $\{-1,1\}^n$, or equivalently $\{0,1\}^n$, equipped with [Hamming distance](hamming-distance.md). Under its uniform [probability measure](probability-measure.md), the distance from a fixed vertex has the [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) with parameters $n,1/2$. This converts geometric ball sizes into probability tails.
