# Free streamline

↑ **Parent:** [Streamline](streamline.md)

A [free streamline](free-streamline.md) is a [streamline](streamline.md) that forms an unknown [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of a [fluid](fluid.md) region, typically against a region of prescribed pressure. In steady inviscid [potential flow](potential-flow.md) with gravity and surface tension neglected, the [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) makes its [speed](speed.md) constant when its pressure is constant. It is simultaneously a level set of the [stream function](stream-function.md). These two [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) take particularly simple forms in the [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) and the [hodograph plane](hodograph-plane.md).
