# Convex subgroups of a finite-rank ordered Abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Linearly ordered group](linearly-ordered-group.md)

For a [torsion-free abelian group](torsion-free-abelian-group.md) of finite rational rank $r$, each [convex subgroup of an ordered group](convex-subgroup-of-an-ordered-group.md) is pure: if $ng\in C$ for a positive integer $n$, then $g$ lies between zero and $ng$ or their negatives and hence belongs to $C$. A proper inclusion of convex subgroups therefore increases rational rank. They form a chain, so there are at most $r+1$, including zero and the whole group. A lexicographically ordered copy of $\mathbb Z^r$ attains the bound.
