# Adjoint Dirac equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md)

Varying the [Dirac action](dirac-action.md) with respect to the spinor, rather than its [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md), gives the left-acting field equation after integration by parts. Together with the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) it proves conservation of the vector current.
