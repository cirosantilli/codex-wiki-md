# Cohomology sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md)

For a [cochain complex](cochain-complex.md) of [sheaves](sheaf-mathematics.md) $E$, its $i$th [cohomology sheaf](cohomology-sheaf.md) is $\mathcal H^i(E)=\ker(d^i)/\operatorname{im}(d^{i-1})$, where the image and quotient are taken in [sheaves](sheaf-mathematics.md). Its stalk at $x$ is the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of the stalk complex, because taking stalks is exact.
