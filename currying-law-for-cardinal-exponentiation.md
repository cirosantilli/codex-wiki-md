# Currying law for cardinal exponentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite cardinal arithmetic](infinite-cardinal-arithmetic.md)

There is a natural [bijection](bijection.md) between functions $M\to K^L$ and functions $M\times L\to K$, obtained by sending $f$ to $(m,l)\mapsto f(m)(l)$. Therefore

$$
(\kappa^\lambda)^\mu=\kappa^{\lambda\mu}.
$$
