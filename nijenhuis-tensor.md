# Nijenhuis tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Almost complex manifold](almost-complex-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nijenhuis_tensor)

The Nijenhuis tensor of an [almost complex manifold](almost-complex-manifold.md) is

$$
N_J(U,V)=[JU,JV]-J[JU,V]-J[U,JV]-[U,V].
$$

It measures the failure of the $(0,1)$ tangent distribution to be closed under the [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md).
