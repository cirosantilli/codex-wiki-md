# Rocket dry mass

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket propellant](rocket-propellant.md)

In this ideal [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) model, [rocket dry mass](rocket-dry-mass.md) is the [mass](mass.md) remaining after all usable [rocket propellant](rocket-propellant.md) has been expelled: structure, engines and payload. It is the final [mass](mass.md) in the worked example; if [rocket propellant](rocket-propellant.md) remains at shutdown, the final [mass](mass.md) is larger than the [rocket dry mass](rocket-dry-mass.md).
