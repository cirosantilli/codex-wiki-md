# One-sided bound criterion for a martingale clock

↑ **Parent:** [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md)

A continuous [local martingale](local-martingale.md) bounded above pathwise throughout a stochastic time interval cannot have infinite [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) at that interval's endpoint. The [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) represents it as Brownian motion at its quadratic-variation clock; an infinite clock would force unbounded oscillations. Its clock therefore has a finite limit, and so does the martingale. A bound below gives the same conclusion by changing sign. The bound may be random; it must hold over the entire interval.
