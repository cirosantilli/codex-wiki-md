# Marginal posterior weights of a Poisson count change point

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson count change-point model with gamma priors](poisson-count-change-point-model-with-gamma-priors.md)

Integrating the two rates out of the [Poisson count change-point model with gamma priors](poisson-count-change-point-model-with-gamma-priors.md) gives these weights up to a common factor, by the defining [Gamma function](gamma-function.md) integral. Normalizing across the finitely many possible indices gives the exact marginal [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) of $m$, providing a check on [Markov chain Monte Carlo](markov-chain-monte-carlo.md) estimates. One can also draw $m$ from these weights and then draw both rates from their conditional [Gamma distributions](gamma-distribution.md), producing independent exact joint posterior samples.
