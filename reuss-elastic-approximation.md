# Reuss elastic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Effective elastic energy](effective-elastic-energy.md)

The [Reuss elastic approximation](reuss-elastic-approximation.md) assumes uniform [stress](stress.md). The local [strain](strain.md) is the derivative of the [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md) $W^*(S,x)$; its mean is $D\langle W^*\rangle(S)$. Under invertible differentiable duality, this mean determines $S=DW_R(E)$. For positive [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md), $C_R=\langle C^{-1}\rangle^{-1}$. Uniform [stress](stress.md) is generally not accompanied by a compatible local [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md), so this approximation is distinguished from an exact heterogeneous solution.
