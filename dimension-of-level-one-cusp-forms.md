# Dimension of level-one cusp forms

↑ **Parent:** [Cusp form](cusp-form.md)

For even $k\geq12$,

$$
\dim S_k(\Gamma(1))=
\begin{cases}
\lfloor k/12\rfloor-1,&k\equiv2\pmod{12},\\
\lfloor k/12\rfloor,&k\not\equiv2\pmod{12}.
\end{cases}
$$

The dimension is zero for odd $k$ and for $k<12$.
