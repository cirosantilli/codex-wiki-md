# Lower-dimensional sphere maps are null-homotopic

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial approximation theorem](simplicial-approximation-theorem.md)

If $n<m$, every continuous map $S^n\to S^m$ is [null-homotopic](null-homotopic-map.md). After simplicial approximation, its image lies in the $n$-skeleton of a triangulation of $S^m$, hence omits a point. The punctured sphere is homeomorphic to $\mathbb R^m$ and is therefore contractible.
