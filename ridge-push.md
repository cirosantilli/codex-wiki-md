# Ridge push

↑ **Parent:** [Plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridge_push)

Gravitational sliding of lithospheric material away from elevated spreading regions. It supplies a driving contribution to [plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md) together with [slab pull](slab-pull.md) and mantle stresses.
