# Antibody phage display

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)

[Antibody phage display](antibody-phage-display.md) displays antibody fragments on phage particles carrying their encoding sequences. Binding selection enriches candidates while maintaining genotype–phenotype linkage, followed by testing in soluble and intended therapeutic formats.
