# Thermal radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_radiation)

Thermal radiation is [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md) emitted by matter because of its temperature. Matter in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) emits a spectrum governed by its absorptivity, approaching the [Planck law](planck-s-law.md) for a [blackbody](blackbody.md).
