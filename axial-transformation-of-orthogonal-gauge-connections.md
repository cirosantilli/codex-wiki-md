# Axial transformation of orthogonal gauge connections

↑ **Parent:** [SO(3) vector Higgs model](so-3-vector-higgs-model.md)

For an [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) gauge transformation $R\in O(3)$, a scalar vector transforms as $\phi\mapsto R\phi$. The [gauge connection](connection-vector-bundle.md) belongs to the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), identified with axial vectors: the displayed cross-product identity makes its homogeneous transformation $A\mapsto(\det R)RA$. The matrix connection also has the usual inhomogeneous derivative term. Thus $D\phi$ transforms as an ordinary vector, including under improper orthogonal transformations. Treating both fields as ordinary vectors would fail because a [cross product](cross-product.md) changes sign under an orientation reversal. A nonzero vector vacuum has [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) $O(2)$; its connected part is $SO(2)$.
