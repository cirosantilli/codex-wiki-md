# Kernel pair

↑ **Parent:** [Pullback (category theory)](pullback-category-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kernel_pair)

The kernel pair of a morphism $f:A\to B$ is the [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md) $A\times_BA$ with its two projections to $A$. It is the universal parallel pair equalized by $f$.
