# Light-cone Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic particle phase-space action](relativistic-particle-phase-space-action.md)

With $ds^2=-2dx^+dx^-+dx_i^2$, $x^+=t$, and $p_-\ne0$, the [mass-shell condition](string-mass-shell-condition.md) gives $H=-p_+=-(p_i^2+\mu^2)/(2p_-)$. In the future-directed sector $p^+=-p_->0$, this is $(p_i^2+\mu^2)/(2p^+)$. Its [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) is equivalent to the [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) after multiplication by $2p_-$.
