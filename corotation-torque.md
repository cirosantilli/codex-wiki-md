# Corotation torque

↑ **Parent:** [Type I migration](type-i-migration.md)

The orbital torque associated with disk material near the [planet](planet.md)'s corotation [radius](radius.md). Vortensity and entropy gradients matter because [gas](gas.md) exchanged across the orbit carries different conserved properties. Horseshoe turns can make the response nonlinear even while the [planet](planet.md) remains in the low-[mass](mass.md) migration regime.
