# Polypropylene

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

[Polypropylene](polypropylene.md) is formed from [propene](propene.md). Head-to-tail insertion gives the repeat connectivity $\mathrm{{-}CH_2{-}CH(Me){-}}$. The stereochemical sequence of its substituted carbons determines its [tacticity](tacticity.md).
