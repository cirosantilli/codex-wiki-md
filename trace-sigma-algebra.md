# Trace sigma-algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md)

For a [measurable space](measurable-space.md) $(\Omega,\mathcal F)$ and any [subset](subset.md) $E\subseteq\Omega$, the [trace sigma-algebra](trace-sigma-algebra.md) is the displayed family of [subsets](subset.md) of $E$. It contains $E=E\cap\Omega$, is closed under relative [complements](complement-of-a-set.md) since $E\setminus(E\cap A)=E\cap(\Omega\setminus A)$, and is closed under [countable unions](countable-union.md). If $E\in\mathcal F$, it equals $\{B\in\mathcal F:B\subseteq E\}$. Without that measurability assumption, the traces need not be [measurable sets](measurable-set.md) in the original [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md).
