# Three-isogeny descent

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer map of an elliptic curve](kummer-map-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For a degree-three isogeny whose kernel becomes $\mu_3$ under the [Weil pairing](weil-pairing.md), the Kummer connecting map embeds the quotient by the isogeny into $K^\times/(K^\times)^3$. Valuations outside the primes of bad reduction restrict its image to an [S-unramified power class group](s-unramified-power-class-group.md).
