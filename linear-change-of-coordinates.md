# Linear change of coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Linear map](linear-map.md)

An invertible [linear transformation](linear-map.md) replaces one system of [coordinates](coordinate-linear-algebra.md) by another. If $x=Pu$ in a [linear differential equation](linear-differential-equation.md) $\dot x=Ax$, then $\dot u=P^{-1}APu$. The [similar matrices](matrix-similarity.md) $A$ and $P^{-1}AP$ have the same [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) and [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), although their coordinate descriptions of the [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) differ. Such a change can turn a planar linear system with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\alpha\pm i\beta$ into $\dot u=\alpha u-\beta v$, $\dot v=\beta u+\alpha v$.
