# Mechanistic dehumanization

↑ **Parent:** [Dehumanization](dehumanization.md)

Representing a person primarily as an impersonal mechanism lacking a recognized subjective perspective or agency. A scientific biological explanation does not logically require [mechanistic dehumanization](mechanistic-dehumanization.md); how an explanation is framed and used is an empirical question.
