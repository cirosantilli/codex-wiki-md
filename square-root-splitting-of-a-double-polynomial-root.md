# Square-root splitting of a double polynomial root

↑ **Parent:** [Puiseux series](puiseux-series.md)

Suppose a perturbed [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) has the local form $A(x-x_0)^2+\epsilon B+O((x-x_0)^3,\epsilon(x-x_0),\epsilon^2)$ with $AB\ne0$. [Dominant balance](dominant-balance.md) gives $x-x_0=\epsilon^{1/2}z$ and $Az^2+B=0$. The two resulting [Puiseux series](puiseux-series.md) split a [double root](double-root.md); their leading displacements can be real or a [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) pair. Ordinary [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) in $\epsilon$ cannot describe this splitting.
