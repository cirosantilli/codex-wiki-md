# Regular solid harmonic

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md)

A [regular solid harmonic](regular-solid-harmonic.md) is $r^lY_l^m(\theta,\phi)$: it is homogeneous of degree $l$ and solves the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md). Thus $\mathbf r\cdot\nabla F=lF$ and $\nabla^2F=0$. These identities turn angular [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) expansions of displacement, density and [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) into radial equations.
