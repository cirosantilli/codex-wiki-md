# Minimum pairwise dot-product sum of three unit vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md)

For three [unit vectors](unit-vector.md), the squared [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) of their sum is $3+2S$, proving the displayed bound; equality holds exactly when $u+v+w=0$. For fixed unit vectors $u,v$ with $u+v\ne0$, the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives the unique minimizing third vector $w=-(u+v)/\|u+v\|$, and the minimum is $u\cdot v-\sqrt{2+2u\cdot v}$. Three coplanar directions separated by $120$ degrees attain the global bound.
