# Discriminant valuation obstruction to good reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Bad reduction of an elliptic curve](bad-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Discriminants of isomorphic [Weierstrass equations of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) differ by a twelfth power of the scale parameter, so their [valuations](valuation.md) differ by a multiple of twelve. An integral model with [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) has discriminant valuation zero. Thus a displayed integral model with discriminant valuation not divisible by twelve proves intrinsic [bad reduction](bad-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). More generally, a displayed discriminant valuation between zero and eleven is already minimal, since any smaller integral-model valuation would be nonnegative and differ by twelve. Positive valuations divisible by twelve alone do not establish bad reduction; rescaling may remove them.
