# Drift velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Charge carrier](charge-carrier.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drift_velocity)

The [drift velocity](drift-velocity.md) is a [charge carrier](charge-carrier.md) population's mean [velocity](velocity.md) relative to its material's rest frame. It describes the net transport superposed on microscopic motion. For a single species, [current density](current-density.md) is $\mathbf J=nq\mathbf v_d$; for [Electrons](electron.md), $q=-e$ reverses the direction. It is distinct from the propagation speed of an electrical disturbance. [Ling, Sanny and Moebs, §9.2: drift and transport](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/9-2-model-of-conduction-in-metals).
