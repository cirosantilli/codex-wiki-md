# Perfect conductor

↑ **Parent:** [Plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_conductor)

At the surface of a perfect conductor, the tangential [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and normal [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) vanish. An incident [plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) is therefore accompanied by a reflected wave whose tangential electric field cancels the incident field at the surface.
