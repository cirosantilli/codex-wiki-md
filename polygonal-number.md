# Polygonal number

↑ **Parent:** [Figurate number](figurate-number.md)

For [integers](integer-split.md) $s\ge3$ and $n\ge1$, the $n$th $s$-gonal number is $P_{s,n}=((s-2)n^2-(s-4)n)/2$. Its successive differences form an [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md). The cases $s=3,4,5,6$ give [triangular numbers](triangular-number.md), [square numbers](square-number.md), [pentagonal numbers](pentagonal-number.md), and [hexagonal numbers](hexagonal-number.md).
