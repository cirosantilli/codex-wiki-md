# Cosmological caustic

↑ **Parent:** [Shell crossing](shell-crossing.md)

A cosmological caustic is the image of a singular point of the Lagrangian map. The ideal collisionless density diverges there, although velocity dispersion and other small-scale physics regularize the singularity.
