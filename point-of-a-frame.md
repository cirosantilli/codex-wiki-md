# Point of a frame

↑ **Parent:** [Complete Heyting algebra](complete-heyting-algebra.md)

A point is a [frame homomorphism](frame-homomorphism.md) to the two-element [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) $\mathbf 2=\{0<1\}$. Its inverse image of $1$ is a completely prime filter: membership of an arbitrary join forces membership of one term. The point space $P(A)$ has opens $U_a=\{p:p(a)=1\}$. Indeed $U_{a\wedge b}=U_a\cap U_b$ and $U_{\bigvee_i a_i}=\bigcup_iU_{a_i}$, so these sets already form a [topology](topology-split.md).
