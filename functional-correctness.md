# Functional correctness

↑ **Parent:** [Algorithm correctness](algorithm-correctness.md)

Conformance of an implementation's behaviour to its specified functional requirements. The exact obligations come from the [formal specification](formal-specification.md): correct delivery of an [IPC](inter-process-communication.md) payload and correct computation of a [file](computer-file.md) read are different requirements. Neither requirement by itself supplies a numerical response-time bound.
