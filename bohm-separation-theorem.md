<h1 id="bohm-separation-theorem">Böhm separation theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Böhm-out lemma](bohm-out-lemma.md)

Two distinct closed [lambda terms](lambda-term.md) in normal form for both [beta reduction](beta-reduction.md) and [eta conversion](eta-conversion.md) admit a separating context that maps them respectively to the two [Church Booleans](church-boolean.md). Finite [Böhm trees](bohm-tree.md) and the [Böhm-out lemma](bohm-out-lemma.md) isolate their first genuine head disagreement. The qualification about [eta conversion](eta-conversion.md) matters: distinct beta-normal forms that are eta-equivalent cannot be separated this way.
