<h1 id="cech-differential">Čech differential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Čech cochain complex](cech-cochain-complex.md)

The Čech differential sends a cochain on $(p+1)$-fold intersections to the alternating sum of its restrictions to $(p+2)$-fold intersections. Each pair of omitted indices occurs twice with opposite signs in the composite, so $\delta^2=0$. It defines the [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md) of the cover.
