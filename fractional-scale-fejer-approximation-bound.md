<h1 id="fractional-scale-fejer-approximation-bound">Fractional-scale Fejér approximation bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fejér first-moment approximation bound](fejer-first-moment-approximation-bound.md)

The positive normalized [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md) has a $1/(nt^2)$ tail. Split the error integral at $\delta=n^{-\alpha}$ and use the chaining inequality $\omega(f,t)\leq(1+t/\delta)\omega(f,\delta)$. Its tail contributes at most a constant times $n^{\alpha-1}(1+\alpha\log n)\omega(f,\delta)$. The factor is uniformly bounded for each fixed $\alpha<1$, proving the displayed estimate. Its constant need not remain bounded as $\alpha$ tends to one.
