# Moment map for rotation of the complex projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Moment map](moment-map.md)

For the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) $\omega=i\,dz\wedge d\bar z/(1+|z|^2)^2$, the [circle group](circle-group.md) rotates $z$ by $e^{i\theta}$. Its real generator is $K=iz\partial_z-i\bar z\partial_{\bar z}$. The contraction is $\iota_K\omega=-dH$, so $H$ is a [Hamiltonian function](hamiltonian-function.md) in this sign convention. In the other chart $w=1/z$, $H=1/(1+|w|^2)$, proving smoothness at infinity. Its image is $[0,1]$ with the endpoints at the fixed poles. The [symplectic area](symplectic-area.md) is $2\pi$; rescaling to the round area $4\pi$ would double this moment function.
