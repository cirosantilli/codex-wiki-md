# Minimization problem

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

A minimization problem seeks a feasible point with smallest objective value. Its infimum can be finite without being attained, or equal to $-\infty$ when unbounded below. Negating the objective converts it to a [maximization problem](maximization-problem.md).
