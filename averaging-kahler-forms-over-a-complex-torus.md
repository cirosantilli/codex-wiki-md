<h1 id="averaging-kahler-forms-over-a-complex-torus">Averaging Kähler forms over a complex torus</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Complex torus](complex-torus.md)

Averaging a [Kähler form](kahler-form.md) over translations with normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) gives an invariant [Kähler form](kahler-form.md) in the same [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) class. Positivity is preserved by averaging positive values on $(v,Jv)$, and the class is unchanged because translations are homotopic to the identity. Thus any [Hodge metric](hodge-metric.md) on a [complex torus](complex-torus.md) can be replaced by an invariant one.
