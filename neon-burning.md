# Neon burning

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md)

High-temperature [photodisintegration](nuclear-photodisintegration.md) of neon-20 releases an alpha particle and oxygen-16. Capture of that alpha particle by another neon-20 nucleus produces magnesium-24. The breakup consumes energy, but the combined reactions can release net energy. Other captures modify the ashes in a real network.
