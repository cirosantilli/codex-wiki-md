# Viscous drag coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md)

The proportionality between drag [force](force-split.md) and relative [velocity](velocity.md) in a linear low-speed regime. Its translational units are $\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$; thermal equilibrium gives the [Einstein diffusion relation](einstein-diffusion-relation.md) $D=k_BT/\gamma$.
