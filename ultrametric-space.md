# Ultrametric space

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrametric inequality](ultrametric-inequality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrametric_space)

An ultrametric space is a metric space satisfying the ultrametric inequality. Its open balls are clopen, and any two balls are either disjoint or one contains the other.
