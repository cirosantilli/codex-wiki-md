# Pauli equation

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md)

For a nonrelativistic spin-one-half electron of charge $-e$, with $e>0$, the Pauli Hamiltonian is

$$
H_P=\frac1{2m}(-i\hbar\nabla+eA)^2+\frac{e\hbar}{2m}\sigma\cdot B-e\phi.
$$

The wavefunction has two spin components and $B=\nabla\times A$. If $\pi=-i\hbar\nabla+eA$, then $[\pi_i,\pi_j]=-ie\hbar\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$; the [Pauli matrix multiplication law](pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md) gives $(\sigma\cdot\pi)^2=\pi^2+e\hbar\sigma\cdot B$, explaining the spin-coupling sign. Thus $i\hbar\partial_t\Psi=H_P\Psi$ extends the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) to include the electron's magnetic moment.
