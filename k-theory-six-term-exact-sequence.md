# K-theory six-term exact sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md)

For a pair $(X,A)$ of [CW complexes](cw-complex.md), [relative topological K-theory](relative-topological-k-theory.md) and [Bott periodicity](bott-isomorphism.md) give the cyclic [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md)

$$
K^0(X,A)\to K^0(X)\to K^0(A)\to K^{-1}(X,A)\to K^{-1}(X)\to K^{-1}(A)\to K^0(X,A).
$$
