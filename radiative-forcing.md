# Radiative forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Climate change](climate-change.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiative_forcing)

Radiative forcing is a change in the net downward [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) caused by an imposed perturbation, evaluated with a specified adjustment convention before the surface climate responds. State the reference period and whether the diagnostic is at the tropopause or top of the atmosphere. A local seasonal [ocean heat flux](ocean-heat-flux.md) is not directly interchangeable with global annual radiative forcing.
