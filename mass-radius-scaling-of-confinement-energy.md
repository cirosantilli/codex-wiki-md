# Mass-radius scaling of confinement energy

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md)

If [mass](mass.md) m, confinement radius R and [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) h are the only dimensional inputs to a nonrelativistic [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), matching [mass](mass.md), length and time dimensions gives $K=Ch^2/(mR^2)$. The dimensionless coefficient C depends on the [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) and state. Ratios of energies therefore scale as inverse [mass](mass.md) and inverse radius squared only when that dimensionless physics is unchanged. This separates a robust scaling law from a claim about an unspecified confinement spectrum.
