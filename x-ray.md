# X-ray

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md)

X-rays are high-frequency [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md), conventionally with wavelengths of roughly $0.01$ to $10\,\mathrm{nm}$. The precise wavelength convention varies with context. Hot accreting gas and energetic electronic processes can emit X-rays, whose short wavelengths make them useful probes of compact objects and atomic structure.
