# Polar-frame parallel spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Parallel spinor](parallel-spinor.md)

In flat three-dimensional spacetime with polar spatial [orthonormal coframe](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) $(dT,d\rho,\rho d\phi)$, the [spin connection](spin-connection.md) has $\omega_{12}=-d\phi$. Thus every [parallel spinor](parallel-spinor.md) has the displayed components. Since $(\gamma^1\gamma^2)^2=-1$, these components change sign under $\phi\mapsto\phi+2\pi$. This is the lift of a full rotation to the [spin structure](spin-structure.md) extending over the disk; the polar frame itself does not extend over the origin. In a Cartesian frame the same [parallel spinors](parallel-spinor.md) have constant components.
