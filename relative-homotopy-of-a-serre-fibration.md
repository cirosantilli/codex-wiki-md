# Relative homotopy of a Serre fibration

↑ **Parent:** [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md)

For a based [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md) with fibre $F$ over the basepoint, lift a based cube in $B$ from its constant bottom face, keeping the side faces fixed. Its final face lies in $F$, giving surjectivity of the relative map. [Homotopies](homotopy.md) in $B$ lift; two lifts of the same cube are relatively homotopic by lifting with both side liftings prescribed. This gives injectivity. For $n\ge2$ this is a [group](group-split.md) [isomorphism](isomorphism.md); at $n=1$ it is initially a bijection of [pointed sets](pointed-set.md), unless the relative set is endowed with the [group](group-split.md) law transported through the fibration.
