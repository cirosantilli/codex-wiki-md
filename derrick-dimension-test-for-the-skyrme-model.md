# Derrick dimension test for the Skyrme model

↑ **Parent:** [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md)

For a spatially dilated [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md) field $U_L(x)=U(x/L)$, the quadratic and quartic derivative energies scale as shown. A static stationary field must satisfy $(d-2)E_2+(d-4)E_4=0$. In three spatial dimensions this permits $E_2=E_4$ and a positive second variation along scale. In two dimensions it requires $E_4=0$, and in four or more it forces a constant finite-energy smooth field. In one dimension the commutator term vanishes identically. [Derrick scaling](derrick-scaling.md) tests a necessary variational condition, not existence or stability under all deformations.
