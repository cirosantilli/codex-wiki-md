# Small radial oscillations in a power-law potential

↑ **Parent:** [Circular-orbit stability for a power-law central force](circular-orbit-stability-for-a-power-law-central-force.md)

For a [central potential](central-potential.md) $V=\beta r^k$ and fixed nonzero [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $L$, the [effective potential](effective-potential.md) $L^2/(2mr^2)+\beta r^k$ has a nondegenerate stable [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) when $\beta k>0$ and $k>-2$. Its radius obeys $r_0^{k+2}=L^2/(m\beta k)$. The radial [small oscillation](small-oscillation.md) frequency satisfies $\omega_r^2=(k+2)\Omega^2$, where $\Omega=|L|/(mr_0^2)$. Rational frequency ratios close the linearized near-circular sketch, but generally not the exact finite-amplitude orbit; [Bertrand's theorem](bertrand-s-theorem.md) distinguishes the exceptional harmonic and Kepler potentials.
