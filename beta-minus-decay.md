# Beta-minus decay

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)

An energetically allowed weak transition converts a neutron into a proton, emitting an electron and an electron antineutrino. Free [neutron](neutron-split.md) decay is an example; in nuclei, the nuclear binding energy determines whether the transition is possible.
