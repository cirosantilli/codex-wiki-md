# Critical surface

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization-group fixed point](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md)

The critical surface is the stable manifold of a critical [renormalization-group fixed point](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md). Tuning every relevant direction places a theory on this surface.
