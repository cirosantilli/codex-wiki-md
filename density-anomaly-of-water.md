# Density anomaly of water

↑ **Parent:** [Penetrative convection](penetrative-convection.md)

Fresh water reaches maximum [mass density](density.md) near $4\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ at atmospheric pressure. A useful local [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) is

$$
\rho=\rho_m\left[1-\alpha(T-T_m)^2\right],
$$

so water becomes less dense when its temperature moves either above or below $T_m$.
