# Adiabatic suppression of fragmentation

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md)

For adiabatic monatomic gas, $T\propto\rho^{2/3}$ and the [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md) increases as $\rho^{1/2}$. Once cooling fails and collapse becomes adiabatic, further fragmentation is suppressed.
