# Two-reservoir carbon adjustment

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon cycle](carbon-cycle.md)

A coupled atmosphere–ocean [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) model can have a fast atmospheric [exponential relaxation time](exponential-relaxation-time.md) and a much slower ocean-carbon time. For atmospheric inventory coefficient $I$, exchange coefficient $h$, ocean [water](water.md) mass $M_o$, removal coefficient $b$ and linear surface-[pressure](pressure-split.md) relation $p_s=C/K$, atmospheric balance gives $p\simeq C/K+(v-A_LW)/h$. Substitution into the ocean equation cancels the weathering and exchange terms, giving $(M_o/b)\dot C=v/(M_{\mathrm{CO}_2}b)-C$. Sustained extra emissions therefore first raise [pressure](pressure-split.md) at nearly fixed ocean [concentration](concentration.md) and then increase both reservoirs over the ocean removal time.
