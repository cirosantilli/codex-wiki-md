# Galactic angular-momentum catastrophe

↑ **Parent:** [Hierarchical galaxy formation](hierarchical-galaxy-formation.md)

The galactic angular-momentum catastrophe is the excessive loss or redistribution of baryonic [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) in early simulated galaxy formation, producing overly compact disks or large central concentrations. Premature cooling, gravitational torques, interactions, and resolution contributed. Improved [stellar feedback](stellar-feedback.md) and gas accretion histories can mitigate it; it is a historical simulation problem rather than a theorem against [cold dark matter](cold-dark-matter.md).
