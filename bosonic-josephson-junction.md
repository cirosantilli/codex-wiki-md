# Bosonic Josephson junction

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluidity](superfluidity.md)

Two weakly linked Bose-Einstein condensates form a [bosonic Josephson junction](bosonic-josephson-junction.md). Their relative phase controls a particle current through the weak link, analogous to the pair current of a superconducting [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md). For neutral reservoirs the phase evolution follows a chemical-potential difference rather than an electric voltage. Interactions can make the tunneling dynamics nonlinear, including self-trapping at sufficiently large imbalance.
