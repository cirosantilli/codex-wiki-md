# Weakly null continuous functions converge in L1

↑ **Parent:** [Space of continuous functions on a compact space](space-of-continuous-functions-on-a-compact-space.md)

[Weak convergence](weak-convergence.md) to zero gives pointwise convergence by the continuous evaluation functionals and uniform boundedness in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) by the [weakly bounded set](weakly-bounded-set.md) criterion. The [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) for Lebesgue measure then proves the displayed implication. The finite measure of the interval and uniform norm bound are the relevant hypotheses.
