# Hereditary abelian category with enough projectives

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian category](abelian-category.md)

An [abelian category](abelian-category.md) with enough projectives is hereditary when every [subobject](subobject.md) of a projective object is projective. For $0\to K\to P\to A\to0$ with $P$ projective, dimension shifting gives $L_2F(A)\cong L_1F(K)$. Hence hereditaryness is equivalent to vanishing of $L_2F$ for every additive [right exact functor](right-exact-additive-functor.md) into any abelian [category](category-split.md), by [projectivity detected by all first left derived functors](projectivity-detected-by-all-first-left-derived-functors.md). It is also equivalent to left exactness of every $L_1F$: the derived long exact sequence gives injectivity of $L_1F(K)\to L_1F(P)$ when $L_2F$ vanishes, and applying left exactness to the displayed sequence proves the converse.
