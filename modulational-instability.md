# Modulational instability

↑ **Parent:** [Spatiotemporal wave-packet stability](spatiotemporal-wave-packet-stability.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modulational_instability)

Modulational instability is growth of long-wavelength [perturbations](perturbation.md) of a uniform or periodic wave due to coupling between its modulation and nonlinear dynamics. In the [focusing nonlinear Schrodinger equation](focusing-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md), the amplitude and phase perturbations can have imaginary modulation frequencies even though the unperturbed wave is an exact solution.
