# Open cluster

↑ **Parent:** [Star cluster](star-cluster.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_cluster)

An [open cluster](open-cluster.md) is a relatively low-mass group of [stars](star.md) formed together, typically in a [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md). [Two-body relaxation](two-body-relaxation.md), [stellar evaporation](stellar-evaporation.md), and external tidal disturbances disperse many [open clusters](open-cluster.md) on much shorter times than the lifetimes of their low-mass member [stars](star.md).
