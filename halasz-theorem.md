<h1 id="halasz-theorem">Halász theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Pretentious distance](pretentious-distance.md)

Halász's theorem bounds the mean of a bounded [multiplicative arithmetic function](multiplicative-function.md) in terms of its least [pretentious distance](pretentious-distance.md) from an [Archimedean character](archimedean-character.md). A large mean can occur only when this distance is small.
