# Coherent wave field

↑ **Parent:** [Wave propagation in a random medium](wave-propagation-in-a-random-medium.md)

In [wave propagation in a random medium](wave-propagation-in-a-random-medium.md), the coherent field is the [expectation](expected-value.md) of the complex wave amplitude, $m(\mathbf r)=\mathbb E[E(\mathbf r)]$. The remaining field $E-m$ has zero [expectation](expected-value.md). This is the [coherent and diffuse wave fields](coherent-and-diffuse-wave-fields.md) decomposition. [Statistical homogeneity](statistical-homogeneity.md) of the medium does not make $m$ spatially constant when the incident field is localized: translation symmetry applies to the response together with its incident data. The coherent component can attenuate while the total [ensemble-averaged wave intensity](ensemble-averaged-wave-intensity.md) remains substantial.
