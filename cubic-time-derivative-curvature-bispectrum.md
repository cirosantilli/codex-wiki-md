# Cubic time-derivative curvature bispectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md)

Put $C=\epsilon+1-c_s^2$. For constant $H,\epsilon,c_s>0$ and cubic interaction $H_{\rm int}=-M_{\rm Pl}^2\int d^3x\,a^3\epsilon C\dot\zeta^3/(Hc_s^2)$, the [Bunch-Davies vacuum](bunch-davies-vacuum.md) modes $u_k=H(1+ikc_s\tau)e^{-ikc_s\tau}/\sqrt{4\epsilon M_{\rm Pl}^2c_sk^3}$ give

$$
B(k_1,k_2,k_3)=\frac{3H^4C}{8\epsilon^2M_{\rm Pl}^4c_s^2k_1k_2k_3(k_1+k_2+k_3)^3}.
$$

To derive this [primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md), transform $dt=a\,d\tau$, $\dot\zeta=\zeta'/a$, use the six connected [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md), and integrate $\int_{-\infty(1-i0)}^0\tau^2e^{ic_s(k_1+k_2+k_3)\tau}\,d\tau=2i/[c_s(k_1+k_2+k_3)]^3$ in the [in-in formalism](keldysh-formalism.md). The normalized equilateral amplitude is $B(k,k,k)/P(k)^2=2C/9$; raw inverse powers of $c_s$ must not be confused with normalized [primordial non-Gaussianity](primordial-non-gaussianity.md).
