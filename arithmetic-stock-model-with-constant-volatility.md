# Arithmetic stock model with constant volatility

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)

This additive price model has constant absolute volatility and can take negative [stock](stock.md) values. With constant cash interest rate $r$, its [market price of risk](market-price-of-risk.md) is $(\mu-rS_t)/\sigma$. Under an equivalent [risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md), its drift becomes $rS_t$, so its conditional law is [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) with mean $e^{r(T-t)}S_t$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2(e^{2r(T-t)}-1)/(2r)$. It is distinct from a multiplicative Black–Scholes diffusion.
