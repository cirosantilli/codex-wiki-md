# Shape-two Weibull scale likelihood-ratio test

↑ **Parent:** [Neyman-Pearson lemma](neyman-pearson-lemma.md)

For independent [Weibull distribution](weibull-distribution.md) observations of known shape two, the likelihood ratio between any scale $\lambda_1>\lambda_0$ and the null scale $\lambda_0$ increases with $\sum_iX_i^2$. Under the null, $2\sum_iX_i^2/\lambda_0^2$ has a chi-squared distribution with $2n$ degrees of freedom. The same upper-tail critical region is therefore most powerful for every larger scale, giving a uniformly most powerful size-$\alpha$ test.
