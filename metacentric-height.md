# Metacentric height

↑ **Parent:** [Floating equilibrium](floating-equilibrium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacentric_height)

For a small heel of a floating body in a uniform [fluid](fluid.md), the metacentre is the intersection of the new vertical [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) line with the original vertical through the [centre of buoyancy](centre-of-buoyancy.md). With the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) a signed distance $BG$ above the original [centre of buoyancy](centre-of-buoyancy.md), the [metacentric height](metacentric-height.md) is $GM=I_{\rm wp}/V_d-BG$. Here $I_{\rm wp}$ is the waterplane [second moment of area](second-moment-of-area.md) about the heel axis and $V_d$ the displaced [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). Positive $GM$ gives a restoring small-angle [torque](torque.md) and negative $GM$ gives instability. The formula describes local static stability, not all large-angle behaviour or effects of free [liquid](liquid.md) surfaces inside the body.
