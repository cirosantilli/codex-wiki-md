# Parallel-bond ray with different site and bond thresholds

↑ **Parent:** [Directed percolation](directed-percolation.md)

Take a [directed graph](directed-graph.md) with vertices $v_0,v_1,\ldots$ and $n+1$ parallel bonds from $v_n$ to $v_{n+1}$. This is a [locally finite graph](locally-finite-graph.md), although the incoming degrees are unbounded. For [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md), $\theta_b(p)=\prod_{n\ge0}(1-(1-p)^{n+1})>0$ whenever $p>0$: the failure [probabilities](probability.md) are summable, so the [infinite product](infinite-product.md) is positive. For [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md), survival requires every vertex of the ray to be open, which has [probability](probability.md) zero for $p<1$. Thus the [survival and susceptibility thresholds for directed percolation](survival-and-susceptibility-thresholds-for-directed-percolation.md) satisfy $p_H^b=0$ and $p_H^s=1$.
