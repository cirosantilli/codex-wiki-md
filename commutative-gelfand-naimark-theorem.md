<h1 id="commutative-gelfand-naimark-theorem">Commutative Gelfand--Naimark theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md)

Every commutative unital C-star algebra $A$ is isometrically star-isomorphic to $C(\Phi_A)$ through its [Gelfand transform](gelfand-representation.md), where $\Phi_A$ is its [character space of an algebra](character-space-of-an-algebra.md). Thus abstract algebra elements can be treated as continuous functions on a compact Hausdorff space.

This is the isometric commutative C-star specialization of the [Gelfand representation](gelfand-representation.md).
