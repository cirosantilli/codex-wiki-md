# Oblate tensor-virial shape factor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md)

For a finite stationary axisymmetric self-gravitating system with similar oblate density surfaces, let $q=b/a$, $s=\sqrt{1-q^2}$, $A_1=q\arccos(q)/s^3-q^2/s^2$, and $A_3=2/s^2-2q\arccos(q)/s^3$. Its [stellar potential-energy tensor](stellar-potential-energy-tensor.md) has $Q=A_1/(q^2A_3)$; the common radial integral cancels. The spherical limit has $A_1=A_3=2/3$ and $Q=1$. With mass-weighted in-plane [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) $\sigma_x$ and constant azimuthal streaming $v$, the [tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md) gives $v^2/\sigma_x^2=2[(1-\beta)Q-1]$, where $\sigma_z^2=(1-\beta)\sigma_x^2$. Neither $Q=1/q$ nor the omission of the Cartesian streaming factor is exact.
