# Optimal sinusoidal waveform for viscous snail locomotion

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication model of a crawling snail](lubrication-model-of-a-crawling-snail.md)

For $h=h_0[1-A\sin(2\pi x/L)]$, the crawling speed is $U/V=3A^2/(1+2A^2)$. At prescribed $U$, eliminate $V$ to obtain $\Phi=2\mu LU^2(1+2A^2)/(3h_0A^2\sqrt{1-A^2})$. The derivative in $A^2$ vanishes only at $A^2=1/2$, and the dissipation diverges at both endpoints. The displayed optimum still dissipates $8\sqrt2/3$ times the simple [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) dissipation of an externally driven flat foot moving at speed $U$ across a uniform film of thickness $h_0$.
