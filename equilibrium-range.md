# Equilibrium range

↑ **Parent:** [Energy cascade](energy-cascade.md)

The [equilibrium range](equilibrium-range.md) consists of scales below the energy-containing motions whose statistics can adjust rapidly to the local energy supply. It includes the [inertial range](inertial-range.md) and dissipative scales. Approximate equilibrium concerns mean transfer and [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md), not absence of intermittent spatial fluctuations.
