# Homoscedasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical model](statistical-model-split.md)

Constancy of the error [variance](variance-split.md) across observations or conditional covariate values. In [fixed-design nonparametric regression](fixed-design-nonparametric-regression.md), $Y_i=m(x_i)+\sigma\epsilon_i$ with $\operatorname{Var}(\epsilon_i)=1$ has $\operatorname{Var}(Y_i)=\sigma^2$ for every design point.
