# Collapsing a contractible cofibration

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy extension property](homotopy-extension-property.md)

If $A\subseteq X$ is contractible and $(X,A)$ has the homotopy extension property, the quotient map

$$
q:X\to X/A
$$

is a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md). Extend a contraction of $A$ to $X$; its endpoint is constant on $A$ and therefore factors through $q$ to supply a homotopy inverse.
