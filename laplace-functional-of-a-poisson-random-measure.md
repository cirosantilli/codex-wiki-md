# Laplace functional of a Poisson random measure

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md)

For nonnegative measurable $f$, independent Poisson counts first prove this formula for simple functions on disjoint measurable sets. Monotone approximation gives the general formula, allowing infinite integrals with $e^{-\infty}=0$. Conversely, the formula evaluated at $f=\sum_ju_j\mathbf1_{A_j}$ for disjoint finite-intensity sets gives the joint Laplace transform of independent Poisson counts, so characterizes the law of a [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md).
