# Nonlinear sigma model

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_sigma_model)

A nonlinear sigma model has fields valued in a curved target space such as a [vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md) $G/H$. Its leading action is quadratic in spacetime derivatives but nonlinear in any unconstrained coordinates used for the target.
