# Cumulative coherence

↑ **Parent:** [Coherence of a normalized matrix](coherence-of-a-normalized-matrix.md)

For unit [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) columns, cumulative coherence measures the largest sum of $k$ correlations with one other column. Its domain is $0\le k\le N-1$, with $\mu_1(0)=0$ by the empty-sum convention. It is nondecreasing, $\mu_1(1)=\mu$ for $N\ge2$, and $\mu_1(k)\le k\mu$, where $\mu$ is the [mutual coherence](coherence-of-a-normalized-matrix.md). Keeping a sum of actual correlations can be substantially sharper than replacing each summand by the largest one.
