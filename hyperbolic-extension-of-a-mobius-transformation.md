<h1 id="hyperbolic-extension-of-a-mobius-transformation">Hyperbolic extension of a Möbius transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare ball model](poincare-ball-model.md)

A [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) of the ideal two-sphere extends uniquely to an orientation-preserving [isometry](isometry.md) of three-dimensional [hyperbolic space](hyperbolic-space.md). In upper half-space, translations, complex similarities, and $z\mapsto-1/z$ extend respectively to $(z,t)\mapsto(z+b,t)$, $(\lambda z,|\lambda|t)$, and $(-\overline z,t)/(|z|^2+t^2)$. Each preserves $(|dz|^2+dt^2)/t^2$; composing these extensions constructs the general case. Ball-origin stabilizers are ordinary rotations, represented on the boundary by $SU(2)$ modulo its central signs.
