# Acoustically rigid boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md)

An acoustically rigid boundary has zero perturbation [normal velocity](normal-velocity.md). For a reflected [acoustic plane wave](acoustic-plane-wave.md), [pressure](pressure.md) amplitudes add in phase and the [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) is $+1$. It is the infinite-[surface acoustic impedance](surface-acoustic-impedance.md) limit. A [pressure-release boundary](pressure-release-boundary.md) instead fixes the [pressure](pressure.md) perturbation to zero and reflects with coefficient $-1$.
