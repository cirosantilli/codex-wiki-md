# Finite-depth Rayleigh-Taylor dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Taylor instability](rayleigh-taylor-instability.md)

For two resting incompressible inviscid layers between impermeable horizontal walls, the upper layer has density $\rho_1$ and depth $L_1$, and the lower layer has $\rho_2<\rho_1$ and depth $L_2$. Solving the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md) for the [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) and imposing the linearized kinematic and capillary pressure conditions gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). With positive [surface tension](surface-tension.md) $\gamma$, exponential [Rayleigh-Taylor instability](rayleigh-taylor-instability.md) occurs for $0<k<k_c$, where $k_c=\sqrt{g(\rho_1-\rho_2)/\gamma}$. The depths change the growth rate, but not this cutoff.
