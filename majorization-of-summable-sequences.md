# Majorization of summable sequences

↑ **Parent:** [Majorization](majorization.md)

For decreasing positive sequences in $\ell^1$, write $x\prec y$ if every initial [partial sum](partial-sum.md) of $x$ is at most the corresponding [partial sum](partial-sum.md) of $y$ and the finite total sums agree. The finiteness requirement matters: equality of two divergent totals interpreted as infinity is too weak to guarantee a [doubly stochastic realization of summable-sequence majorization](doubly-stochastic-realization-of-summable-sequence-majorization.md).
