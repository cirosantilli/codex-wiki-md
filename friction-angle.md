# Friction angle

↑ **Parent:** [Static friction](static-friction.md)

The limiting angle between a contact resultant and the compressive [normal force](normal-force.md) when [static friction](static-friction.md) obeys $|F_t|\le\mu N$. Resolving the resultant gives $\tan\varphi=|F_t|/N$, so admissible contact forces lie within a cone of half-angle $\arctan\mu$ about the inward normal. A contact-force magnitude is generally $\sqrt{N^2+F_t^2}$, distinct from N.
