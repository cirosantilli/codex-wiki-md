# Noncommutative formal power series

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative formal power series ring](noncommutative-formal-power-series-ring.md)

A noncommutative formal power series is an element of a [noncommutative formal power series ring](noncommutative-formal-power-series-ring.md). Its [constant term](constant-term.md) is the coefficient of the empty [word](string.md). Formal sums are interpreted coefficient by coefficient; analytic convergence is not required.
