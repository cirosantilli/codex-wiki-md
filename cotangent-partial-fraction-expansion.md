# Cotangent partial-fraction expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent](cotangent.md)

Away from integer [poles](pole.md),

$$
\pi\cot\pi z=\frac1z+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{2z}{z^2-n^2}.
$$

The series is locally uniformly convergent. Integrate $\pi\cot(\pi\zeta)/(\zeta^2-z^2)$ around the square with sides $\operatorname{Re}\zeta,\operatorname{Im}\zeta=\pm(N+1/2)$. The [cotangent](cotangent.md) is uniformly bounded there, so the integral tends to zero. The [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md) then gives $\pi\cot(\pi z)/z+\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}(n^2-z^2)^{-1}=0$, which is the expansion. Differentiation generates higher integer-lattice [pole](pole.md) sums. Setting $w=e^{2\pi iz}$ gives $\pi\cot\pi z=\pi i(w+1)/(w-1)$. On $0<|z|<1$, expansion of each paired summand gives $\pi\cot\pi z=z^{-1}-2\sum_{m\ge0}\zeta(2m+2)z^{2m+1}$, relating this [trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md) to the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md).
