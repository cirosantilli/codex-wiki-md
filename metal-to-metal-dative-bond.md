# Metal-to-metal dative bond

↑ **Parent:** [Coordinate covalent bond](coordinate-covalent-bond.md)

An electron-pair interaction represented as donation from a metal-base fragment into an orbital of a metal-acid fragment. [electron counting](electron-counting.md) must avoid adding a new pair twice to an already occupied donor orbital.
