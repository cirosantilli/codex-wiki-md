# Tait graph

↑ **Parent:** [Link diagram](link-diagram.md)

Choose a checkerboard coloring of the complementary regions of a [link diagram](link-diagram.md). The Tait graph has one vertex for each black region and one edge through each crossing joining its adjacent black regions. The other coloring gives its planar dual. For a connected [alternating link diagram](alternating-link-diagram.md), all-A and all-B smoothing [circles](circle.md) correspond to the regions of its two checkerboard colors; one-circle smoothing states correspond to [spanning trees](spanning-tree.md).
