# Equality in Sperner theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Sperner's theorem](sperner-s-theorem.md)

The maximum [antichains](antichain.md) in the [Boolean lattice](boolean-lattice.md) are exactly its full middle levels. For even $n$ there is one such level, and for odd $n$ either middle level works. The [LYM inequality](lubell-yamamoto-meshalkin-inequality.md) first confines a maximum antichain to those levels; connectedness of the regular inclusion [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) rules out a mixture of the two odd middle levels.
