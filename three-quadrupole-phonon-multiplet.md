# Three-quadrupole-phonon multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear quadrupole vibration](nuclear-quadrupole-vibration.md)

Three identical quadrupole quanta occupy the third [symmetric power](symmetric-power.md) of the five-dimensional spin-two space. Its dimension is $\binom73=35$, and its [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) have $J=0,2,3,4,6$, each once. All have positive [parity](parity.md). This counts allowed angular states in the ideal harmonic [nuclear quadrupole vibration](nuclear-quadrupole-vibration.md); anharmonic interactions can split their energies.
