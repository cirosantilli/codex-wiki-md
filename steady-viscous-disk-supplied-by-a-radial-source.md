# Steady viscous disk supplied by a radial source

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

For a [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md) with evolution $\partial_t\Sigma=(2\pi r)^{-1}\partial_r\mathcal F+S$ and $\mathcal F=6\pi r^{1/2}\partial_r(r^{1/2}\bar\nu\Sigma)$, take zero viscous [torque](torque.md) at $r_1>0$ and zero flux outside the source region at $r_2>r_1$. The [steady state](steady-state.md) gives $\mathcal F'=-2\pi rS$, and its outer condition gives the displayed flux. Integrating once more from the [zero-torque inner boundary condition](zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) yields

$$
\bar\nu(r)\Sigma(r)=\frac{1}{3\sqrt r}\int_{r_1}^{r}\frac{du}{\sqrt u}\int_u^{r_2}vS(v)\,dv.
$$

The equation determines the viscosity-density product, so a thermodynamic viscosity closure can be imposed afterward. For nonnegative integrable $S$, it is nonnegative and the inner [accretion rate](accretion-rate.md) is exactly the total supplied mass per unit time.
