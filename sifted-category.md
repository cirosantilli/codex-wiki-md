# Sifted category

↑ **Parent:** [Colimit](colimit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sifted_category)

A small category $\mathcal J$ is sifted when it is connected and its diagonal functor $\mathcal J\to\mathcal J\times\mathcal J$ is a [final functor](final-functor.md). Equivalently, colimits of shape $\mathcal J$ in the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md) preserve finite products.
