# Lorenz-gauge potentials for a circularly polarized plane wave

↑ **Parent:** [Lorenz gauge condition](lorenz-gauge-condition.md)

For a transverse monochromatic [electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) with angular [frequency](frequency.md) $\omega\ne0$, $\partial_t^2\mathbf E=-\omega^2\mathbf E$. The displayed [electric potential](electric-potential.md) and [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) therefore give $-\nabla\Phi-\partial_t\mathbf A=\mathbf E$. Transversality gives $\nabla\cdot\mathbf A=0$, so the [Lorenz gauge](lorenz-gauge-condition.md) holds. For $\mathbf E=E_0(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0)$ and $\theta=\omega t-Kz$, taking a [curl](curl.md) yields $\mathbf B=(K/\omega)\widehat{\mathbf z}\times\mathbf E$. The vacuum [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations-split.md) require $K^2=\omega^2/c^2$. The field has [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md), with $|\mathbf B|=|\mathbf E|/c$ and perpendicular electric and magnetic directions.
