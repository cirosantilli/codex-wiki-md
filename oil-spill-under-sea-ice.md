# Oil spill under sea ice

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

An oil spill beneath [sea ice](sea-ice.md) is transported by a rising [buoyant plume](buoyant-plume.md), lateral currents and the evolving ice--water interface. A slick can be spread, trapped in ridges, incorporated into growing ice or released through brine channels and melt ponds.
