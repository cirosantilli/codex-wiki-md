# Renormalized momentum of a condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Gross–Pitaevskii solitary wave](gross-pitaevskii-solitary-wave.md)

For a condensate with uniform bulk complex field one, its convergent localized [momentum](momentum.md) is

$$
\mathbf p=\frac1{2i}\int[(\psi^*-1)\nabla\psi-(\psi-1)\nabla\psi^*]d^d x.
$$

The subtraction removes the uniform-background phase-gradient contribution. If $\psi-1=O(r^{-1})$ and $\nabla\psi=O(r^{-2})$, its integrand is $O(r^{-3})$, integrable in two dimensions. Under decaying variations its [functional derivative](functional-derivative.md) is $\delta p_j/\delta\psi^*=-i\partial_j\psi$; the subtraction is a boundary correction and does not change the bulk equation.
