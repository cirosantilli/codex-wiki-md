# Inverse-density Abelian Higgs model

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian Higgs model](abelian-higgs-model.md)

This planar [Abelian Higgs model](abelian-higgs-model.md) replaces the usual magnetic energy by $B^2/h$. Use $D_i=\partial_i-iA_i$ and $B=\partial_1A_2-\partial_2A_1$. The magnetic term is singular at a zero of the [Higgs field](higgs-field.md), so the admissible class must have finite energy; formal field equations are first derived where $h>0$. Its positive-flux [Bogomolny equations](bogomolny-equations.md) are $(D_1+iD_2)\Phi=0$ and $B=h(1-h)/2$. On those solutions $B/h=(1-h)/2$ extends regularly across zeros.
