# Charge selection rule for complex scalar correlators

↑ **Parent:** [Global phase symmetry of a complex scalar field](global-phase-symmetry-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md)

In a phase-invariant [vacuum](vacuum.md), a correlator with $r$ insertions of $\phi$ and $s$ of $\phi^*$ gains the phase $e^{i\alpha(r-s)}$. Its invariance for all real $\alpha$ forces it to vanish unless $r=s$. Balanced local interactions and the absence of same-charge free contractions give the same rule order by order in [Wick's theorem](wick-s-theorem.md). A symmetry-breaking vacuum is an essential exception to the vacuum-invariance premise.
