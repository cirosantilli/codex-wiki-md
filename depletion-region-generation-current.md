# Depletion-region generation current

↑ **Parent:** [Depletion region](depletion-region.md)

Thermal generation of [electron-hole pairs](electron-hole-pair.md) within a reverse-biased [depletion region](depletion-region.md) can supply a leakage [electric current](electric-current.md) in addition to neutral-region diffusion. For a simple midgap trap with equal electron and hole lifetime parameters, its scale is $e\mathcal A Wn_i/(2\tau_g)$, where $W$ is depletion width and $\tau_g$ the lifetime parameter. The [electric field](electric-field-split.md) separates generated carriers. Unlike ideal [diode minority-carrier diffusion current](diode-minority-carrier-diffusion-current.md), this contribution scales approximately with $n_i$ rather than $n_i^2$ and can vary with reverse voltage through $W$. Consequently a measured activation energy need not equal the full [band gap](band-gap.md).
