# Sensitivity index

↑ **Parent:** [Signal detection theory](signal-detection-theory.md)

Under an equal-variance Gaussian evidence model, $d'$ is separation of signal and noise means in units of their common [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md). A yes/no task gives $d'=\Phi^{-1}(H)-\Phi^{-1}(F)$, where $H$ and $F$ are hit and false-alarm [probabilities](probability.md). This formula assumes the model; it does not define every possible sensory-discrimination measure.
