# Exponentially coupled Ising inverse kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Ising model](ising-model.md)

For the infinite one-dimensional coupling [matrix](matrix.md) $J_{ij}=Je^{-\kappa|i-j|}$ with $J,\kappa>0$, its Fourier symbol is $J\sinh\kappa/(\cosh\kappa-\cos q)$. Its inverse is nearest-neighbor even though the original interaction is not. The inverse quadratic form equals $[2J\sinh\kappa]^{-1}\sum_j(m_j-m_{j+1})^2+[\tanh(\kappa/2)/J]\sum_jm_j^2$. Finite open chains add endpoint corrections; a periodized coupling gives the same translation-invariant formula.
