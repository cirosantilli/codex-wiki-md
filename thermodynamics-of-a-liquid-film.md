# Thermodynamics of a liquid film

↑ **Parent:** [Surface tension](surface-tension-split.md)

For a film described by $dU=T\,dS+\gamma\,dA$, with material content fixed, its [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) obeys $dF=-S\,dT+\gamma\,dA$. Equality of mixed [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) gives the [Maxwell relation](maxwell-relations.md) $(\partial S/\partial A)_T=-(\partial\gamma/\partial T)_A$. If $\gamma$ depends only on [temperature](temperature-split.md), write $\gamma'=d\gamma/dT$; then

$$
dS=\frac{C_A}{T}\,dT-\gamma'\,dA,
\qquad dU=C_A\,dT+(\gamma-T\gamma')\,dA.
$$

Here $C_A$ is the [heat capacity at constant area](heat-capacity-at-constant-area.md). For $C_A>0$ and $\gamma'<0$, reversible adiabatic stretching cools the film, since $dT/dA=T\gamma'/C_A<0$, although positive mechanical input can increase its total [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md). If $\gamma=\gamma_0-\beta T$ with constant positive $\beta$ and constant $C_A$, the [isentropic process](isentropic-process.md) satisfies $C_A\ln(T/T_*)+\beta A=\mathrm{constant}$, where $T_*$ is an arbitrary fixed reference [temperature](temperature-split.md). The [work](work.md) convention uses the total [area](surface-area.md) conjugate to $\gamma$; a two-surface film described by projected [area](surface-area.md) requires the corresponding factor of two consistently throughout.
