# Effective amorphization volume per decay

↑ **Parent:** [Direct-impact amorphization model](direct-impact-amorphization-model.md)

The effective volume converted to an amorphous state by one independent [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) event in a direct-impact model. The subscript alpha can index the event without identifying the emitted [alpha particle](alpha-particle.md) as the dominant source of [ballistic radiation damage](ballistic-radiation-damage.md).
