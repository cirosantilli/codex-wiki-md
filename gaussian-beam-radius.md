# Gaussian beam radius

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md)

For a circular [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md) with intensity proportional to $e^{-2r^2/w(z)^2}$, the displayed $w(z)$ is the intensity $1/e^2$ radius. Its minimum $w_0$ is the [beam waist](beam-waist.md) radius, while the larger radius at a cavity mirror is not itself a waist. The [Rayleigh range](rayleigh-length.md) is $z_R=\pi w_0^2/\lambda$.
