# General steady viscous flux in a Keplerian disk

↑ **Parent:** [Keplerian viscous diffusion equation](keplerian-viscous-diffusion-equation.md)

Two integrations of the steady [Keplerian viscous diffusion equation](keplerian-viscous-diffusion-equation.md) give this general transported quantity. $A$ fixes the [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md), while $B$ fixes the additive [angular-momentum flux](angular-momentum-flux.md) or inner [torque](torque.md). A [zero-torque inner boundary condition](zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) gives $B=-A\sqrt{r_{\rm in}}$; a [nonaccreting constant-torque disk](nonaccreting-constant-torque-disk.md) has $A=0$.
