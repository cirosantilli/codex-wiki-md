# Level-two modular curve

↑ **Parent:** [Modular curve](modular-curve.md)

The quotient $\mathbb H/\Gamma(2)$ is a [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-zero [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md) with three cusps and no [elliptic Möbius transformations](elliptic-element-of-psl2-r.md), hence is [biholomorphic](biholomorphism.md) to $\mathbb C\setminus\{0,1\}$. The integer level-two [principal congruence subgroup](principal-congruence-subgroup.md) has projective index six; reduction modulo two gives three cusp classes.
