# Rapid-rotation mean gyrotactic orientation

↑ **Parent:** [Gyrotactic orientation Fokker-Planck equation](gyrotactic-orientation-fokker-planck-equation.md)

For rigid fluid rotation with [vorticity](vorticity.md) $-2\Omega\mathbf e_2$, $B\Omega\gg1$, and $BD_R$ of order one, the normalized steady density is $f=(4\pi)^{-1}[1-2p_1/(B\Omega)]+O((B\Omega)^{-2})$. The result follows by acting with the rotation generator on $p_1$ and using $\int p_i p_jd\Omega=4\pi\delta_{ij}/3$. Reversing the imposed rotation reverses the mean horizontal swimming direction.
