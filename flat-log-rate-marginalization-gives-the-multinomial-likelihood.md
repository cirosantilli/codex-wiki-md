# Flat log-rate marginalization gives the multinomial likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson trick](poisson-trick.md)

For positive group totals, the [improper prior](improper-prior.md) $du/u$ on an independent Poisson baseline yields the [multinomial likelihood](multinomial-likelihood.md) kernel after integration. This prior is flat in $\log u$. A proper finite-variance log-rate prior generally introduces an additional coefficient-dependent factor.
