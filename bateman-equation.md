# Bateman equation

↑ **Parent:** [Sequential radioactive decay](sequential-radioactive-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bateman_equation)

The Bateman equation gives the populations in a sequential [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) chain. For a parent with population $N_1(0)$ and an initially absent daughter, distinct decay constants give $N_2(t)=N_1(0)\lambda_1(e^{-\lambda_1t}-e^{-\lambda_2t})/(\lambda_2-\lambda_1)$; the first population satisfies $N_1'=-\lambda_1N_1$.
