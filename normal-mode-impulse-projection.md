# Normal-mode impulse projection

↑ **Parent:** [Mass-weighted elastic normal modes](mass-weighted-elastic-normal-modes.md)

For an [impulse](impulse.md) vector $\mathbf J=M\Delta\dot{\mathbf q}$ delivered at an unstrained [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), project the [velocity](velocity.md) jump onto mass-orthogonal [normal modes](normal-mode.md). Multiplying $M\Delta\dot{\mathbf q}$ by $\mathbf u_j^T$ gives modal [velocity](velocity.md) coefficient $\mathbf u_j^T\mathbf J/(\mathbf u_j^TM\mathbf u_j)$. Its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is the displayed expression, including a translational zero mode if one is present. A single endpoint kick in an equal-mass [free three-mass spring chain](free-three-mass-spring-chain.md) puts fractions $1/3,1/2,1/6$ into translation and the two internal modes.
