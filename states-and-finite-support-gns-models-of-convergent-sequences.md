# States and finite-support GNS models of convergent sequences

↑ **Parent:** [Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction](gelfand-naimark-segal-construction.md)

On the [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) of convergent complex sequences, a functional in the displayed coordinate representation is a [state on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md) exactly when all its weights are nonnegative and sum to one. Coordinate projections test positivity of $w_j$ at finite indices. Positive sequences with the first $N$ terms zero and the rest one test $w_\infty$ after passage to the limit. The known dual isometry makes the [norm](norm.md) the sum of the absolute weights, proving necessity and sufficiency.

If only finitely many weights are positive, the [GNS construction](gelfand-naimark-segal-construction.md) identifies the quotient by $\{\alpha:F(\alpha^*\alpha)=0\}$ with the values at precisely those supporting indices. Its [inner product](inner-product-split.md) is the weighted coordinate [inner product](inner-product-split.md). Passing to the standard Hilbert [norm](norm.md) gives diagonal evaluation operators and cyclic vector whose entries are the square roots of the weights. Arbitrary finite coordinate values and the limiting value can be assigned independently by a convergent sequence, which proves cyclicity. For weights $1/2$ at infinity and $1/4$ at each of the first two indices, the space is $\mathbb C^3$ and the cyclic vector is $(1/\sqrt2,1/2,1/2)$.
