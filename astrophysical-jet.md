# Astrophysical jet

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md)

A collimated [plasma](plasma-physics.md) outflow from an astrophysical engine, often associated with accretion. Magnetic stresses, rotation, pressure and interaction with an external medium shape its acceleration and confinement. [Jets](jet-particle-physics.md) can carry particle [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), thermal energy and electromagnetic energy far beyond the compact launch region.
