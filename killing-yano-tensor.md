# Killing-Yano tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md)

A Killing-Yano tensor is a [differential form](differential-form-split.md) satisfying the displayed equation for the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md). Its [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) is totally antisymmetric. Contraction with the velocity of an affinely parametrized [geodesic](geodesic.md) gives a form that is carried by [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) along that [geodesic](geodesic.md). This differs from a [Killing tensor](killing-tensor.md), which is symmetric.
