# Brightness

↑ **Parent:** [Color appearance](color-appearance.md)

Perceived amount or [intensity](intensity-physics.md) of [light](light.md) from a stimulus. It is distinct from physical [luminance](luminance.md) and from [lightness](lightness.md) as a judgment related to surface [reflectance](reflectance.md).
