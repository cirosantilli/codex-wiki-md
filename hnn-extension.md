# HNN extension

↑ **Parent:** [Bass-Serre theory](bass-serre-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HNN_extension)

Given isomorphic subgroups $C,D\leq A$ and an isomorphism $\phi:C\to D$, the HNN extension is

$$
\langle A,t\mid tct^{-1}=\phi(c)\text{ for every }c\in C\rangle.
$$

Its stable letter $t$ records the loop edge in the associated graph of groups.
