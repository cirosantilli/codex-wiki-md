# Hall magnetohydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

[Hall magnetohydrodynamics](hall-magnetohydrodynamics.md) retains bulk [ion](ion.md) inertia but freezes [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) into the [Electron](electron.md) velocity $\mathbf u_e=\mathbf u-\mathbf j/(en)$. With constant density, negligible [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md) and [Electron](electron.md) inertia, [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md) give $\mathbf j=c\nabla\times\mathbf B/(4\pi)$ and $\partial_t\mathbf B=\nabla\times[(\mathbf u-c\nabla\times\mathbf B/(4\pi en))\times\mathbf B]$. The [ion](ion.md) velocity still satisfies the [magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation](magnetohydrodynamic-momentum-equation.md). Neglecting that velocity gives [electron magnetohydrodynamics](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md), a further approximation rather than a synonym for the complete Hall system.
