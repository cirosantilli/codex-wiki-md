# Club of closure points for countable set-valued functions

↑ **Parent:** [Club set](club-set.md)

For a regular uncountable [cardinal](cardinal-number.md) $\lambda$ and $B:\lambda\to[\lambda]^\omega$, the [limit ordinals](limit-ordinal.md) closed under all earlier values of $B$ form a [club set](club-set.md). Countably iterate bounds for $\bigcup_{\alpha<\gamma}B(\alpha)$ to obtain unboundedly many closure points. Regularity keeps each bound and the countable supremum below $\lambda$. Limits of closure points remain closure points.
