# Weighted norm

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md)

A positive measurable weight $w$ defines the displayed [norm](norm.md) on the [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) for the [measure](measure-split.md) $w(x)\,dx$, with functions identified when equal almost everywhere. This is the [norm](norm.md) induced by the [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md). Changing $w$ changes the contribution of each region to approximation error. If $p_0,\ldots,p_N$ are nonzero mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) real functions, completing squares shows that the unique best approximation to real $f$ in their [linear span](linear-span.md) has coefficients $a_j=\langle f,p_j\rangle_w/\|p_j\|_w^2$.
