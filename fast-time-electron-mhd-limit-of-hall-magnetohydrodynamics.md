# Fast-time electron-MHD limit of Hall magnetohydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Hall magnetohydrodynamics](hall-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

For field amplitudes of order the guide field, the [ion](ion.md) Lorentz-force response over time $\tau$ is $\Delta u/v_A=O(v_A\tau/l)\ll1$. The electron-current velocity is of order $v_Ad_i/l$, larger than a bulk velocity of order $v_A$. Thus the leading [Hall magnetohydrodynamics](hall-magnetohydrodynamics.md) induction equation becomes the closed [electron magnetohydrodynamics](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md) equation, while [ions](ion.md) remain fixed to leading order on this fast scale. The first nonzero [ion](ion.md) response need not have zero acceleration. This limit retains the fast branch of [incompressible Hall-MHD wave dispersion](incompressible-hall-mhd-wave-dispersion.md), not the slow branch; very oblique modes must separately satisfy the fast-time ordering.
