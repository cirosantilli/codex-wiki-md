# Stochastic heating

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

Increase of particle random [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) due to decorrelating electromagnetic kicks. In the simple unscreened electrostatic [white noise](white-noise.md) limit it is [velocity-space diffusion](velocity-space-diffusion.md) with constant coefficient and heating rate $mnD$. In other [plasma](plasma-physics.md) settings the same term can refer to orbit stochasticity produced by spatial structure or overlapping [resonances](resonance.md); its precise mechanism must be stated.
