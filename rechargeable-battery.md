# Rechargeable battery

↑ **Parent:** [Electric battery](electric-battery.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rechargeable_battery)

A [battery](electric-battery.md) designed to recover usable chemical energy by electrical charging that reverses its discharge reactions. Reversibility, cycle life and parasitic reactions distinguish a practical secondary cell from a merely thermodynamically reversible equation.
