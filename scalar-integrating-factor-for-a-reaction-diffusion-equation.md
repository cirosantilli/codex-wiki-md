# Scalar integrating factor for a reaction-diffusion equation

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md)

A spatially uniform time-dependent reaction coefficient can be removed from a linear [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) by a scalar [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md). If $A'(t)=a(t)$ and $v=e^{A(t)}u$, substitution gives $u_t=u_{xx}$. Initial data and boundary values must also be divided by $e^{A(t)}$. A nonzero boundary profile can then be removed by [boundary lifting](boundary-lifting.md) before applying [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md). This reduction distinguishes growth of the whole amplitude from decay of the spatial diffusion modes.
