# Killing spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

A [Killing spinor](killing-spinor.md) is a nonzero [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) parameter that makes the fermionic transformations vanish on a bosonic background. In undeformed [minimal four-dimensional supergravity](minimal-four-dimensional-supergravity.md) it is parallel, $\nabla_\mu\epsilon=0$; in its cosmological deformation the equation uses $\mathcal D_\mu=\nabla_\mu+(m/2)\gamma_\mu$. Its commuting [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) is a [Killing vector](killing-vector-field.md), since the spinor equation makes the symmetric [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) of the current vanish. General [supergravity](supergravity.md) theories also impose the vanishing of matter-fermion transformations.
