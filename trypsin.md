# Trypsin

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trypsin)

[Trypsin](trypsin.md) is a [serine protease](serine-protease.md): an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that performs [proteolysis](proteolysis.md) by catalysing [peptide bond](peptide-bond.md) [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md). It preferentially cuts on the [C-terminus](c-terminus.md) side of [lysine](lysine.md) and [arginine](arginine.md) [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md), leaving the selected [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md) in the preceding fragment.[Trypsin from bovine pancreas, DPCC Treated, T1005 product information sheet, p. 1: Product Description, serine-protease family and C-terminal cleavage specificity](trypsin-from-bovine-pancreas-dpcc-treated-t1005-product-information-sheet-sigma-aldrich.md)

The bond being broken is the backbone [amide](amide.md) linkage, rather than the [lysine](lysine.md) or [arginine](arginine.md) [amino acid side chain](amino-acid-side-chain.md). A formal connectivity equation for [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) is

$$
\mathrm{R^1{-}C(=O){-}NH{-}R^2}+\mathrm{H_2O}
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{R^1{-}COOH}+\mathrm{H_2N{-}R^2}.
$$

Here $R^1$ and $R^2$ stand for the connected portions of the original [peptide](peptide.md). Actual terminal [protonation](protonation.md) and [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) depend on [pH](ph.md); the equation displays connectivity. [Water](water.md) supplies the elements needed to form the two new ends, while the [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) is regenerated.

Selective cutting turns an unwieldy [protein](protein-split.md) into shorter [peptides](peptide.md), making [peptide mapping](peptide-mapping.md) useful for comparing [proteins](protein-split.md). In [cell culture](cell-culture.md), [trypsin](trypsin.md) also helps detach adherent [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md): the [HeLa culture viability experiment](hela-culture-viability-experiment.md) provides a concrete example of its use and subsequent stopping of [proteolysis](proteolysis.md). The same [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) can therefore serve both chemical analysis and preparation of a [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) suspension.
