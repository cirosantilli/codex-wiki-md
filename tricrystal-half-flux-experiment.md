# Tricrystal half-flux experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-sensitive superconducting gap experiment](phase-sensitive-superconducting-gap-experiment.md)

A ring crossing three appropriately oriented grain-boundary junctions tests a superconducting pairing sign pattern. An odd total intrinsic $\pi$ offset frustrates integer phase matching and can favor a half-flux state. Actual enclosed flux also depends on loop inductance and junction currents; the measurement requires controls against ordinary trapped flux.[1994 primary tricrystal-ring pairing-symmetry experiment](pairing-symmetry-and-flux-quantization-in-a-tricrystal-superconducting-ring-by-c-c-tsuei-and-others.md)
