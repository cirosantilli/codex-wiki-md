# Covering homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md)

A covering homomorphism is a surjective homomorphism of topological groups whose underlying map is a [covering space](covering-space.md). Its discrete kernel is the fiber over the identity.
