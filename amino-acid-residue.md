# Amino acid residue

↑ **Parent:** [Polypeptide](polypeptide.md)

An [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md) is the portion of an [amino acid](amino-acid.md) incorporated into a [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md). For example, the A in the illustrative [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md) is an [alanine](alanine.md) residue, rather than a free [alanine](alanine.md) [molecule](molecule.md) floating beside the chain. Counting [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) counts chain positions; repeated letters are separate positions. See [IUPAC-IUB, section 3AA-16: symbols for amino-acid residues](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/AminoAcid/A1416.html).
