# Period domain

↑ **Parent:** [Polarized Hodge structure](polarized-hodge-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Period_domain)

Fix a lattice, a bilinear polarization and Hodge numbers. The period domain consists of the [Hodge filtrations](hodge-filtration.md) with these dimensions satisfying orthogonality, opposition and positivity. It is an open subset of its [compact dual of a period domain](compact-dual-of-a-period-domain.md), described algebraically by the dimensions and orthogonality conditions alone.
