# Biproducts of complete join-semilattices

↑ **Parent:** [Category of complete join-semilattices](category-of-complete-join-semilattices.md)

In the [category of complete join-semilattices](category-of-complete-join-semilattices.md), hom-sets have pointwise join as addition and the constant-bottom map as zero, making it a [semi-additive category](semi-additive-category.md). For any family $A_i$, the cartesian product with coordinatewise joins is also its coproduct. The injections put an element in one coordinate and bottom in all others. A family of maps $f_i:A_i\to B$ extends uniquely by $(a_i)\mapsto\bigvee_i f_i(a_i)$, since each tuple is the join of its coordinate injections. Thus all set-indexed canonical product-coproduct comparisons are invertible. The two-element chain has distinct identity and zero morphisms, so this category is not trivial and not additive.
