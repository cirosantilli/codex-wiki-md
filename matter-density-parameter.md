# Matter density parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological density parameter](cosmological-density-parameter.md)

The [matter density parameter](matter-density-parameter.md) is the ratio of total nonrelativistic matter density, including baryonic and [dark matter](dark-matter.md), to the [critical density](critical-density.md) $\rho_{\rm crit}=3H^2/(8\pi G)$ at the same epoch. A present-epoch value uses $H_0$ and $\rho_{m,0}$. It is not the total [cosmological density parameter](cosmological-density-parameter.md) when radiation or vacuum energy also contributes.
