# Particle Lagrangian in a shearing sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md)

Expand a unit-mass particle's [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) about an axisymmetric [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) of radius $r_0$ and frequency $\Omega_0$, using $r=r_0+x$ and $\varphi=\Omega_0t+y/r_0$. Circular-orbit balance removes the linear radial term; [total-time-derivative invariance of a Lagrangian](total-time-derivative-invariance-of-a-lagrangian.md) removes $r_0\Omega_0\dot y$. To second order,

$$
L_2=\frac12(\dot x^2+\dot y^2+\dot z^2)+2\Omega_0x\dot y-\Phi_t,
\qquad
\Phi_t=-q\Omega_0^2x^2+\frac12\Omega_z^2z^2.
$$

Here $q$ is the [orbital shear parameter](orbital-shear-parameter.md) and $\Omega_z$ is the [vertical epicyclic frequency](vertical-epicyclic-frequency.md). The [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) retain the local [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) and tidal gravity.
