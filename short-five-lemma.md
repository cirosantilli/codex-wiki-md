# Short five lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md)

The [Five lemma](five-lemma.md) has a short form: in a commutative diagram of short exact sequences, if the maps on both outer terms are isomorphisms, then the map on the middle terms is an isomorphism.
