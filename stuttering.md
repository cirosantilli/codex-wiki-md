# Stuttering

↑ **Parent:** [Speech production](speech-production.md)

A disorder of [speech](speech.md) fluency involving disruptions such as repetitions, prolongations or blocks. Proposed timing and motor-control mechanisms must account for variability across conditions and development; [brain](brain.md) differences measured after onset can include consequences or compensation.
