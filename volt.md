# Volt

↑ **Parent:** [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volt)

The [volt](volt.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [electric potential](electric-potential.md) and [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md): $1\,\mathrm V=1\,\mathrm{J/C}$, one [joule](joule.md) per [coulomb](coulomb.md). A positive [electric charge](electric-charge.md) of $2\,\mathrm C$ moved through a potential increase of $3\,\mathrm V$ gains $\Delta U=q\Delta\Phi=6\,\mathrm J$ of [potential energy](potential-energy.md); the [electric field](electric-field.md) does $-6\,\mathrm J$ of [work](work.md). [The International System of Units (SI), ninth edition, SI Brochure](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md).
