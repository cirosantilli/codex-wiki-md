# Recursive simple envelope by cyclic HNN extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Boone-Higman theorem](boone-higman-theorem.md)

A [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) with soluble [word problem for a group](word-problem-for-groups.md) embeds in a countably generated [simple group](simple-group.md) with a [recursive presentation of a group](recursive-presentation-of-a-group.md). Start with $P_0=G*\langle a\rangle$, retaining decidable membership in $\langle a\rangle$. At stage $n$, add a free infinite cyclic generator $z_n$. For every nonidentity base element $g$, conjugate $[g,z_n]$ to $a$ and conjugate $gz_n$ to $a$; also conjugate $z_n$ to $a$, using distinct [stable letters](stable-letter.md). All associated subgroups are infinite cyclic, with decidable membership by [effective cyclic membership in a free product](effective-cyclic-membership-in-a-free-product.md). [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md) preserves embeddings, decidable word problem and decidable membership in the inherited $\langle a\rangle$.

In the union, any nontrivial normal subgroup contains some nonidentity $g$ and hence $a$, by the commutator-conjugation relation. The other relations force $z_n$ and then every old $g$ into the normal closure of $a$. Every stable letter becomes an old element at a later stage, so the normal closure of $a$ is the whole union. The union is nontrivial and simple. Effective word enumeration at each stage makes all relators recursively enumerable. A [two-generator HNN embedding](two-generator-hnn-embedding.md) followed by the [Higman embedding theorem](higman-s-embedding-theorem.md) embeds this simple envelope in a finitely presented group.
