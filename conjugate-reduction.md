# Conjugate reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

A [conjugate reduction](conjugate-reduction.md) adds a reducing equivalent at the beta position of a conjugated carbonyl system. Hydride delivery followed by protonation can reduce C=C while retaining C=O, unlike direct carbonyl-to-alcohol reduction.
