# Savage-Dickey density ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Bayes factor](bayes-factor.md)

For suitable nested models whose common nuisance parameters have matching priors, the Savage-Dickey density ratio expresses a [Bayes factor](bayes-factor.md) as posterior density divided by prior density at the nested parameter value.
