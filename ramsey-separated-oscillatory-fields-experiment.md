# Ramsey separated oscillatory fields experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey interferometry](ramsey-interferometry.md)

A moving [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) or [molecule](molecule-split.md) can experience the two pulses of [Ramsey interferometry](ramsey-interferometry.md) by flying through two short oscillatory-field regions. Their common [oscillator](oscillator.md) supplies the reference [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md); the flight between them supplies the interrogation time. The long interval need not contain a driving field. This turns a spatial apparatus into a coherent pulse sequence and makes narrow [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) possible without filling the entire flight path with a uniform oscillatory field.

[Norman Foster Ramsey, Jr.](norman-foster-ramsey-jr.md)'s 1950 paper calculates the two-state transition [probability](probability.md) and averages it over molecular [velocities](velocity.md). It is a theoretical investigation, rather than a report of an [atomic clock](atomic-clock.md)'s measured accuracy.[Physical Review 78 (1950), pp. 695–699, abstract: separated interaction regions, transition probability and molecular-velocity average](a-molecular-beam-resonance-method-with-separated-oscillating-fields-by-norman-foster-ramsey-jr.md)

His 1989 lecture contrasts an electric-resonance apparatus photograph with a magnetic-resonance schematic. Coherent drive regions bracket a static-field flight region; state-dependent deflection converts transitions into detector changes, while differing flight times suppress side fringes.[1989 lecture, pp. 553–558, Figs. 1–2, 4–6](experiments-with-separated-oscillatory-fields-and-hydrogen-masers-by-norman-foster-ramsey-jr.md)

The readout infers a change of internal [quantum state](quantum-state.md) through state-dependent transmission, as in the [Rabi molecular beam magnetic resonance experiment](rabi-molecular-beam-magnetic-resonance-experiment.md). A useful control is to block one drive region: a narrow fringe requiring both interactions must disappear. Changing the second region's [oscillation phase](phase-waves.md) should move the fringe, whereas an incoherent background need not move. These are consequences of the model below, not additional historical measurements. A detector dip alone neither proves perfect preparation nor determines all apparatus-induced [frequency](frequency.md) shifts; [cesium-beam clock procedure](cesium-beam-clock-procedure.md) adds the controls needed for a practical [atomic clock](atomic-clock.md).

The publication sequence begins with [Norman Foster Ramsey, Jr.](norman-foster-ramsey-jr.md)'s 1949 article [A New Molecular Beam Resonance Method](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.76.996), Physical Review 76, p. 996, published 1 October; the fuller theoretical paper followed in 1950.[Byline and publication record](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.76.996)

NIST-7 supplied a practical realization: [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) moved [caesium](caesium.md) populations into the $F=3$ manifold, without isolating $m_F=0$. Two separated [microwave](microwave.md) interactions probed the clock transition; [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) detected transferred $F=4$ atoms. A synthesized reference, tied to a [hydrogen maser](hydrogen-maser.md), sampled opposite sides of resonance for [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md). Beam reversal tested cavity [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) bias; independent [velocity](velocity.md) determinations checked motion corrections. The observed central fringe resolved the transition, but power-dependent velocity weighting and light shifts still required evaluation.[pp. 50–52, §§2.2–2.2.4, Fig. 2](primary-atomic-frequency-standards-at-nist-by-d-b-sullivan-and-others.md)
