# Hysteresis spinodals of a scalar quartic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Spinodal point](spinodal-point.md)

For the [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) $V(M)=rM^2/2+uM^4/4-hM$ with $r<0$ and $u>0$, stationary branches satisfy $h=rM+uM^3$. Their local stability requires $r+3uM^2>0$. At a [spinodal point](spinodal-point.md), $M_{\rm sp}=\pm\sqrt{|r|/(3u)}$ and $h_{\rm sp}=(2r/3)M_{\rm sp}$, giving the displayed field magnitude. Following local minima without [nucleation](nucleation.md) produces a [hysteresis](hysteresis.md) loop switching at these limits. Equilibrium instead switches between the two global minima at $h=0$.
