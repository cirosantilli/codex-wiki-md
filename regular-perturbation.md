# Regular perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

A regular perturbation admits an [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) about the limiting problem without changing its relevant scale or losing solutions or conditions. A [regular perturbation of a simple algebraic root](regular-perturbation-of-a-simple-algebraic-root.md) follows from the [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md). A full problem can still be a [singular perturbation](singular-perturbation.md) even when some individual solution branches are regular.
