# Uniform stability of a shifted two-level advection scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Power boundedness of a two-level Fourier scheme](power-boundedness-of-a-two-level-fourier-scheme.md)

For $U_m^{n+1}=(1-2\mu)(U_m^n-U_{m+1}^n)+U_{m+1}^{n-1}$, write an [amplification root](amplification-root.md) as $G=e^{i\theta/2}\lambda$. Its equation is $\lambda^2+2i(1-2\mu)\sin(\theta/2)\lambda-1=0$. The roots have modulus one and a positive frequency-uniform separation exactly for $0<\mu<1$, giving [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) [stability](stability-of-a-numerical-method.md) through a uniformly bounded diagonalization. At either endpoint the Nyquist symbol has a repeated unit root and a [Jordan block](jordan-block.md), producing linear growth. The normalized [local truncation error](local-truncation-error.md) is generally second order; at $\mu=1/2$ and $1$ exactly sampled characteristics solve the recurrence, although the latter parameter is unstable for arbitrary perturbations.
