# Wall-shear and pressure-drop balance in a tube

↑ **Parent:** [Nearly occluding sphere in a cylindrical tube](nearly-occluding-sphere-in-a-cylindrical-tube.md)

An axial force balance on fluid between two remote cross-sections of a cylindrical tube gives

$$
a\int_{x_1}^{x_2}\int_0^{2\pi}\sigma_{rx}(a,\theta,x)\,d\theta\,dx
=\pi a^2[p(x_2)-p(x_1)]
$$

when every immersed body between the sections is [force-free](force-free.md).
