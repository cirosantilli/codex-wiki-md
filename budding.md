# Budding

↑ **Parent:** [Reproduction](reproduction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budding)

[Budding](budding.md) produces a new individual from an outgrowth of its parent. In [Saccharomyces cerevisiae](saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md), the daughter [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) grows while attached to the mother and later separates; see the Burgess, Powers and Mell account cited there. [Budding](budding.md) describes [reproduction](reproduction-split.md), not [fermentation](fermentation.md).
