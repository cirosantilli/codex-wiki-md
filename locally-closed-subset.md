# Locally closed subset

↑ **Parent:** [Subspace topology](subspace-topology.md)

A subset $B$ of a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$ is locally closed if $B=U\cap F$ for an [open set](open-set.md) $U$ and a [closed set](closed-set.md) $F$. Equivalently, $B$ is open in its [closure](closure-topology.md) $\overline B$, or $\overline B\setminus B$ is closed in $X$. For the equivalence, $B\subseteq F$ implies $\overline B\subseteq F$, so $B=U\cap\overline B$. Relative complementation proves the second condition implies the third. Conversely, if $C=\overline B\setminus B$ is closed, then $B=(X\setminus C)\cap\overline B$ is such an intersection.
