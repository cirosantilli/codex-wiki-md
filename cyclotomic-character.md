# Cyclotomic character

↑ **Parent:** [Galois representation](galois-representation.md)

The cyclotomic character is defined by $g(\zeta)=\zeta^{\kappa(g)}$ on compatible $p$-power [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md). On the full cyclotomic tower of $\mathbb Q$, it identifies the [Galois group](galois-group.md) with $\mathbb Z_p^\times$. Its finite-order part is the [Teichmüller character](teichmuller-character.md) and its pro-$p$ part acts through $1+p\mathbb Z_p$, or $1+4\mathbb Z_2$.
