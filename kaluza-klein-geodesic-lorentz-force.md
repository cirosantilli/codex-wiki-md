# Kaluza-Klein geodesic Lorentz force

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein decomposition along a Killing field](kaluza-klein-decomposition-along-a-killing-field.md)

For $ds_5^2=\gamma_{ij}dx^idx^j+(dz+A_idx^i)^2$ with $z$-independent coefficients, the [geodesic Lagrangian](geodesic-lagrangian.md) makes $k=\dot z+A_i\dot x^i$ constant. Its remaining [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) give the displayed [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md), where $F=dA$ and $D$ uses the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) of $\gamma$. For a particle of five-dimensional mass $M$ with proper-time parameter $s$, the conserved momentum is $p_z=Mk$ and the four-dimensional rest mass is $m=M\sqrt{1+k^2}$. Reparametrizing by four-dimensional proper time gives $Du^i/d\tau=(p_z/m)F^i{}_ju^j$.
