# Rashba effect

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashba_effect)

The [Rashba effect](rashba-effect.md), named for [Emmanuel Rashba](emmanuel-rashba.md), is a [momentum](momentum-split.md)-dependent [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md) associated with broken spatial [inversion symmetry](inversion-symmetry.md). In an isotropic two-dimensional model, choose $\lambda>0$ and write the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H=\hbar^2k^2I/(2m)+\lambda(\hat{\mathbf z}\times\mathbf k)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$, where $I$ is the [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md) and $\boldsymbol\sigma$ is the vector of [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md). The [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the spin term give $E_\pm(k)=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)\pm\lambda k$. Define $k_R=m\lambda/\hbar^2$ and $E_R=\hbar^2k_R^2/(2m)$. The lower branch is $E_-(k)=\hbar^2(k-k_R)^2/(2m)-E_R$ and has a ring of [minima](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) at $k=k_R$. A negative $\lambda$ reverses the [spin](spin.md) directions; ordering the two [energies](energy-split.md) still uses $|\lambda|$. The alternative parameter $q_R=2k_R$ must not be mistaken for the minimum radius.
