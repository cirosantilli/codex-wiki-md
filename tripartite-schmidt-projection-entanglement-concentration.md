# Tripartite Schmidt projection entanglement concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt projection entanglement concentration](schmidt-projection-entanglement-concentration.md)

For $\sqrt p\,|000\rangle+\sqrt{1-p}\,|111\rangle$, a [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) of the number of ones in one party's $n$ local [qubits](qubit.md) gives a common type class of size $d_k=\binom nk$. The conditional [pure state](pure-state.md) is $d_k^{-1/2}\sum_{|x|=k}|x,x,x\rangle$. It can be converted exactly and deterministically to a uniform common-label state of any dimension $m\leq d_k$: one party measures an $m$-element subset $S$ with [Kraus operator](kraus-operator.md) $\Pi_S/\sqrt{\binom{d_k-1}{m-1}}$, and all parties relabel their common retained labels after receiving $S$. Choosing $m=2^f$ gives $f$ independent [GHZ states](greenberger-horne-zeilinger-state.md). [Stirling's formula](stirling-formula.md) and the [weak law of large numbers](weak-law-of-large-numbers.md) give $f/n\to h_2(p)$ in probability. Measuring every local bit separately would instead destroy the coherence and leave a [product state](product-state.md).
