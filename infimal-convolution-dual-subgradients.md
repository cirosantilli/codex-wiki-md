# Infimal-convolution dual subgradients

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-valued infimal convolution](finite-valued-infimal-convolution.md)

Under the finite-valued assumptions, [subgradients](subgradient.md) of an [infimal convolution](infimal-convolution.md) are obtained as its attained dual optimizers. Equivalently $z\in\partial(h^*+k^*)(p)$. Splitting the latter [subdifferential](subdifferential.md) into two separate ones needs a [subdifferential sum rule](subdifferential-sum-rule.md) qualification; solving the aggregate dual objective avoids that extra assumption.
