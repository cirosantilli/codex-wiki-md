# Immigration fraction among upward steps

↑ **Parent:** [Birth-death chain](birth-death-chain.md)

In a positive recurrent [birth-death-immigration jump chain](birth-death-immigration-jump-chain.md), let $I_n$ and $A_n$ count immigration events and upward steps before time $n$. The centered immigration indicators form bounded [martingale differences](martingale-difference.md); the [strong law for martingales with bounded increments](strong-law-for-martingales-with-bounded-increments.md) and the [countable-state Markov chain ergodic theorem](countable-state-markov-chain-ergodic-theorem.md) give $I_n/n\to\sum_k\pi_k\theta/(k+\theta)$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). The analogous argument for upward steps gives $A_n/n\to\sum_k\pi_ku_k$. Summing [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) gives $\sum_k\pi_ku_k=\sum_k\pi_kd_k=1/2$, since every step is upward or downward. Therefore $I_n/A_n$ converges to the displayed fraction. For this chain the explicit [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) reduces the fraction to $1-p/(1-p)$.
