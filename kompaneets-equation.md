# Kompaneets equation

↑ **Parent:** [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kompaneets_equation)

The [Kompaneets equation](kompaneets-equation.md) describes frequency diffusion of a mean [photon](photon.md) [occupation number](occupation-number.md) $n$ through repeated nonrelativistic [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md). For thermal [Electrons](electron.md), with $x_e=h\nu/(k_BT_e)$ and $dy=(k_BT_e/m_ec^2)n_e\sigma_Tc\,dt$, its full homogeneous form is $\partial_y n=x_e^{-2}\partial_{x_e}[x_e^4(\partial_{x_e}n+n+n^2)]$. The drift and stimulated terms permit a thermal stationary spectrum. In the hot-electron limit $T_e\gg T_\gamma$, with low photon energies, they can be neglected relative to the diffusion term; any fixed linear frequency variable $x$ then gives $\partial_y n=x^{-2}\partial_x(x^4\partial_xn)$. The quantity $n$ is not the frequency-integrated [photon number density](photon-number-density.md).
