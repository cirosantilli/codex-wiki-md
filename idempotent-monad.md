# Idempotent monad

↑ **Parent:** [Monad](monad.md)

A monad is idempotent when its multiplication is invertible. Then $T\eta=\eta T=\mu^{-1}$. If $(A,a)$ is an [algebra for a monad](algebra-for-a-monad.md), its unit law and naturality give $a\eta_A=1_A$ and $\eta_Aa=Ta\,\eta_{TA}=T(a\eta_A)=1_{TA}$. Thus every algebra is isomorphic to a free algebra, and the [Kleisli comparison functor](kleisli-comparison-functor.md) into the [Eilenberg-Moore category](eilenberg-moore-category.md) is an equivalence.
