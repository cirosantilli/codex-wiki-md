# Thermal instability from negative hydrogen ion opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal stability of an accretion disk](thermal-stability-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

For a gas-supported [alpha disk](alpha-disk.md) with $\kappa\propto\rho^{1/3}T^{10}$, compare rates at fixed [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md), radius, $\alpha$ and composition on the [thermal timescale of an accretion disk](thermal-timescale-of-an-accretion-disk.md). Assume that the [vertical dynamical timescale of a disk](vertical-dynamical-timescale-of-a-disk.md) is shorter than that thermal time, and that the [viscous timescale](viscous-timescale.md) is longer; the separation is controlled for $\alpha\ll1$ and small [disk aspect ratio](disk-aspect-ratio.md). Vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $H\propto T^{1/2}$, $\rho\propto T^{-1/2}$, so $\kappa\propto T^{59/6}$. Viscous heating satisfies $Q^+\propto T$ and [radiative cooling](radiative-cooling.md) satisfies $Q^-\propto T^4/(\kappa\Sigma)\propto T^{-35/6}$. At equilibrium a positive [temperature](temperature.md) perturbation changes the net heating by $(41/6)(Q_0/T)\delta T>0$. With positive effective [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), it grows rather than relaxes. This is a local thermal statement and uses the fixed-column, rather than fixed-volume-density, comparison.
