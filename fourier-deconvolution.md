# Fourier deconvolution

↑ **Parent:** [Nonparametric statistics](nonparametric-statistics-split.md)

Independent additive noise $Y=X+\epsilon$ multiplies [characteristic functions](characteristic-function.md): $\varphi_Y=\varphi_X\varphi_\epsilon$. When the noise [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) is nonzero, division recovers $\varphi_X$. A smoothing kernel limits the frequencies used, controlling the amplification of sampling error where $|\varphi_\epsilon|$ is small.
