# Borel measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel_measure)

A Borel measure on a [topological space](topological-space.md) is a [measure](measure.md) defined on the [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) generated by the [open sets](open-set.md), equivalently on the [Borel sets](borel-set.md).
