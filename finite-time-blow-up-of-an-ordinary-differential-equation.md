# Finite-time blow-up of an ordinary differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

A solution of an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) has finite-time blow-up when its maximal interval of existence has a finite endpoint and its [norm](norm.md) becomes unbounded on approach to that endpoint.
