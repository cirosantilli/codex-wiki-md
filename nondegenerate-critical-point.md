# Nondegenerate critical point

↑ **Parent:** [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md)

A [critical point](critical-point.md) of a real [function](function-split.md) with continuous second [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) is nondegenerate when its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md) there, equivalently when none of its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) is zero. A [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) gives a strict [local minimum](local-minimum.md), a [negative definite](negative-definite-matrix.md) [Hessian](hessian-matrix.md) gives a strict [local maximum](local-maximum.md), and opposite-sign [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) give a [saddle point of a scalar function](saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md). A nondegenerate [critical point](critical-point.md) need not be an extremum: $x^2-y^2$ has [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) $\operatorname{diag}(2,-2)$, which is an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md).

Such critical points are the local building blocks of [Morse theory](morse-theory.md).
