# Nondegenerate critical point

↑ **Parent:** [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md)

A critical point of a twice differentiable function is nondegenerate when its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is invertible there. A positive-definite Hessian gives a strict local minimum, a negative-definite Hessian gives a strict local maximum, and an indefinite Hessian gives a saddle point.

Such critical points are the local building blocks of [Morse theory](morse-theory.md).
