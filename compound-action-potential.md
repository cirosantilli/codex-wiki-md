# Compound action potential

↑ **Parent:** [Action potential](action-potential.md)

A [compound action potential](compound-action-potential.md) is an extracellularly recorded sum of activity from multiple [axons](axon.md). Amplitude and waveform depend on recruitment and relative arrival times. The documented [neuronal paired-pulse recovery](neuronal-paired-pulse-recovery.md) experiment illustrates why recovery of a summed response is not recovery of one [neuron](neuron.md).
