# Hardy inequality on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Hardy averaging inequality](hardy-averaging-inequality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardy_inequality_on_an_interval)

If $u\in W^{1,p}(0,1)$ has zero [trace](sobolev-trace-theorem.md) at zero, then

$$
\left\|\frac ux\right\|_{L^p(0,1)}
\leq\frac p{p-1}\|u'\|_{L^p(0,1)}.
$$

Indeed, the [one-dimensional Sobolev representative](one-dimensional-sobolev-representative.md) satisfies $u(x)=\int_0^xu'(t)\,dt$, so the claim is the [Hardy averaging inequality](hardy-averaging-inequality.md) applied to $u'$.
