# Seismic migration

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic reflection survey](seismic-reflection-survey.md)

Processing that moves recorded reflection energy to its inferred subsurface positions using a [velocity model](velocity-model.md). It corrects apparent positions of dipping reflectors and collapses suitable diffraction events; insufficient spatial sampling and incorrect velocities impair the result.
