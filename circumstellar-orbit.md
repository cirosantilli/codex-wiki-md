# Circumstellar orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)

An [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) about a [star](star.md). Dust on such an [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) can experience [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) and [Poynting–Robertson drag](poynting-robertson-drag.md), in addition to gravitational perturbations.
