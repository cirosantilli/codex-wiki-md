# Reststrahlen effect

↑ **Parent:** [Optical phonon](optical-phonon.md)

In a polar insulating crystal, a band with negative real [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) can occur between the transverse and longitudinal optical resonances. A lossless bulk transverse wave is evanescent there, producing high normal-incidence [reflectance](reflectance.md). Damping rounds the edges and permits absorption. The reflection minimum beyond the band can occur where the refractive index approaches one; it is not exactly the zero of permittivity.
