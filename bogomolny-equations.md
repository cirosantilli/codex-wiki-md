# Bogomolny equations

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogomolny_equations)

Bogomolny equations are first-order field equations obtained by setting every nonnegative square in a [Bogomolny bound](bogomolny-bound.md) to zero. Their solutions automatically satisfy the corresponding second-order Euler-Lagrange equations.
