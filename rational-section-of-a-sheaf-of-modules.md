# Rational section of a sheaf of modules

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md)

A rational section is a section on a dense [open subset](open-set.md), with two representatives identified when they agree on a common dense [open subset](open-set.md). Intersecting representatives' domains defines addition. Multiplication by a similarly represented [rational function on an algebraic variety](rational-function-on-an-algebraic-variety.md) makes these classes a module over the ring $\operatorname{Rat}(X)$. For an [irreducible variety](irreducible-variety.md), this ring is its [function field](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md).
