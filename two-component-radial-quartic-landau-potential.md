# Two-component radial quartic Landau potential

↑ **Parent:** [Landau-Ginzburg theory](landau-ginzburg-theory.md)

With positive $g$, the quadratic coefficients select the component with the smaller negative coefficient. Equal negative coefficients give a circle of minima in the [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md); the negative diagonal is a [spin-flop transition](spin-flop-transition.md), ending at a [bicritical point](bicritical-point.md) at the origin.
