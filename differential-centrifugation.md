# Differential centrifugation

↑ **Parent:** [Centrifugation](centrifugation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_centrifugation)

[Differential centrifugation](differential-centrifugation.md) successively separates [centrifugation pellets](centrifugation-pellet.md) from [supernatants](supernatant.md) by differences in settling rate. A first [centrifugation pellet](centrifugation-pellet.md) can enrich faster-settling material; a later [centrifugation pellet](centrifugation-pellet.md) from its [supernatant](supernatant.md) can enrich slower-settling material. The fractions overlap because particles have different starting positions and can become trapped. [Differential centrifugation](differential-centrifugation.md) is consequently an enrichment method, not automatic identification or complete purification.[Particle Separation: differential pelleting, smaller particles initially near the tube bottom, and incomplete separation](https://www.beckman.com/resources/technologies/centrifugation/principles/particle-separation/)
