# Radial deformation of an elastic solid

↑ **Parent:** [Finite elasticity](finite-elasticity.md)

A spherical radial deformation is $y(x)=r(R)x/R$, with $R=|x|$ and $\boldsymbol\theta=x/R$. Differentiation gives the displayed symmetric gradient. Its radial eigenvalue is $r'$ and its two tangential eigenvalues are $r/R$; when these are positive, they are the [principal stretches](principal-stretch.md). The determinant is $r'(r/R)^2$. Positivity distinguishes an orientation-preserving radial elastic deformation from a map that collapses an interior sphere.
