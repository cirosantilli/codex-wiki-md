# Unitization of the vanishing-at-zero ideal on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Unitization of a C-star algebra](unitization-of-a-c-star-algebra.md)

Evaluation at zero is a bounded homomorphism on $C[0,1]$. Its kernel $I$ is a closed self-adjoint ideal and hence a [C-star subalgebra](c-star-subalgebra.md). It has no identity: applying a proposed identity to $f(t)=t$ would force that identity to equal one at every positive point, contradicting continuity and its value zero at zero. The map $(f,\lambda)\mapsto f+\lambda\mathbf1$ from the [unitization of a C-star algebra](unitization-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) is a bijective star homomorphism, with inverse $h\mapsto(h-h(0),h(0))$. Pulling back the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) verifies the [C-star identity](c-star-identity.md), so this is the prescribed [unitization](unitization-of-an-algebra.md) [norm](norm.md). Thus adjoining the missing constant direction recovers all [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) on the interval.
