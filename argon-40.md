# Argon-40

↑ **Parent:** [Argon](argon.md)

[Argon-40](argon-40.md) is a stable [isotope](isotope.md) with 18 [protons](proton.md) and 22 [neutrons](neutron-split.md). Radiogenic [argon-40](argon-40.md) accumulated from [potassium-40](potassium-40.md) is a daughter product used in [potassium-argon dating](potassium-argon-dating.md).
