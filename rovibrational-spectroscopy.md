# Rovibrational spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotational–vibrational_spectroscopy)

Spectroscopy of simultaneous changes of vibrational and rotational state. The transition wavenumbers combine vibrational energy differences with [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md) terms. The allowed rotational changes depend on the molecule and the transition type.
