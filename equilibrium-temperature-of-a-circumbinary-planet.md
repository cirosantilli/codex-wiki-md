# Equilibrium temperature of a circumbinary planet

↑ **Parent:** [Circumbinary planet](circumbinary-planet.md)

For [blackbody](blackbody.md) stars of radii $R_A,R_B$ and [temperatures](temperature.md) $T_A,T_B$, a distant [circumbinary planet](circumbinary-planet.md) with [Bond albedo](bond-albedo.md) $A_B$ and uniform global reradiation has [planetary equilibrium temperature](planetary-equilibrium-temperature.md)

$$
T_{\rm eq}^4=\frac{1-A_B}{4a^2}(R_A^2T_A^4+R_B^2T_B^4).
$$

This neglects internal cooling, eccentricity, and corrections from finite binary separation. The stars heat the planet through the sum of their absorbed bolometric fluxes, not an average of their [temperatures](temperature.md).
