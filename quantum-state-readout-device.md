# Quantum state readout device

↑ **Parent:** [Local quantum state under objective collapse](local-quantum-state-under-objective-collapse.md)

A quantum state readout device at $x$ hypothetically outputs a classical description of the [local quantum state under objective collapse](local-quantum-state-under-objective-collapse.md) without changing that state. Its output can respond to a remote collapse only after the collapse enters the past light cone of $x$, so the device preserves [relativistic causality](relativistic-causality.md).
