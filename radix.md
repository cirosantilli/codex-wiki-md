# Radix

↑ **Parent:** [Positional notation](positional-notation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radix)

The [radix](radix.md) is the base of [positional notation](positional-notation.md). In the ordinary integer-base case it is an [integer](integer-split.md) $b\geq2$, and successive [place values](place-value.md) differ by a factor of $b$.
