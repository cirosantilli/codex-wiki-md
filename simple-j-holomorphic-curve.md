# Simple J-holomorphic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoholomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md)

A [J-holomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md) is simple if it does not factor through a holomorphic branched covering of degree greater than one. For a nonconstant curve this is equivalent to having a point where its derivative is nonzero and its image has exactly one preimage.
