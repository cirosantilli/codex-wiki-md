# Twin

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin)

Twins are two offspring from the same gestation. [Monozygotic twins](monozygotic-twin.md) arise from one fertilized egg, while [dizygotic twins](dizygotic-twin.md) arise from two. The distinction supplies the biological premise for [twin studies](twin-study.md).
