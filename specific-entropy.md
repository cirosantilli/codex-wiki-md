# Specific entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy](entropy.md)

Specific entropy is [entropy](entropy.md) per unit mass. For a [perfect gas](ideal-gas.md) with constant [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md) $c_v$ at constant volume and [specific-heat ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma$, differences obey

$$
s_2-s_1=c_v\log\left[\frac{p_2}{p_1}\left(\frac{\rho_1}{\rho_2}\right)^\gamma\right],
$$

where $p$ is [pressure](pressure.md) and $\rho$ is [mass density](density.md). Thus a [homentropic flow](homentropic-flow.md) has constant $p/\rho^\gamma$.
