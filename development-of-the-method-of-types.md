# Development of the method of types

↑ **Parent:** [Method of types](method-of-types.md)

In his 1998 retrospective, [Imre Csiszár](imre-csiszar.md) describes the [method of types](method-of-types.md) as a 1970s refinement of the [typical set](typical-set.md) approach, with older counting ingredients. He credits his research group with turning those ingredients into a general tool, while explicitly declining to give a complete discovery history.[§I, printed pp. 2505–2506: the refinement in the 1970s and the author's qualified historical account](the-method-of-types-by-imre-csiszar.md) The useful shift is from an approximately typical collection to exact frequency classes: the latter retain enough structure to study how fast error [probabilities](probability.md) decay.
