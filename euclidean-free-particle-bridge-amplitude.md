# Euclidean free-particle bridge amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Free-particle propagator](free-particle-propagator.md)

[Wick rotation](wick-rotation.md) of the free-line propagator gives the displayed coincident-endpoint kernel for positive Euclidean duration $T$. Equivalently it is the [path integral](path-integral.md) over Dirichlet fluctuations about the straight classical path, with its time-sliced [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md) included. It is a propagator density, not a full thermal [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md): that [trace](matrix-trace.md) additionally integrates position and, on a multiply connected configuration space, sums the winding sectors. For an angular coordinate replace $M$ by [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md).
