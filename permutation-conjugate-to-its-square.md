# Permutation conjugate to its square

↑ **Parent:** [Cycle type](cycle-type.md)

Squaring an odd-length [permutation cycle](permutation-cycle.md) retains its length, whereas an even-length cycle splits into two cycles of half its length. Thus if any even cycle is present, the number of cycles strictly increases, so the [cycle type](cycle-type.md) changes. Since [conjugate permutations](conjugate-permutation.md) in a [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) have the same cycle type, a permutation is conjugate to its square precisely when all its cycle lengths are odd, equivalently when its [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) is odd.
