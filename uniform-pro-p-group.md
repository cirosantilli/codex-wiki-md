# Uniform pro-p group

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)

A finitely generated [powerful p-group](powerful-p-group.md) in the category of [pro-p groups](pro-p-group.md) is uniform if all successive indices in its [lower p-series](lower-p-series.md) are equal. Equivalently it is torsion-free. If its generator number is $d$, the successive indices are $p^d$, the power map carries $G^{p^n}$ bijectively onto $G^{p^{n+1}}$, and an ordered minimal generating set gives unique coordinates $g_1^{\lambda_1}\cdots g_d^{\lambda_d}$ with $\lambda_i$ in the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md). For $p=2$, powerfulness means $[G,G]\leq G^4$.
