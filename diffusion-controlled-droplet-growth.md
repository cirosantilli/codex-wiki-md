# Diffusion-controlled droplet growth

↑ **Parent:** [Ostwald ripening](ostwald-ripening.md)

For a three-dimensional droplet with concentration jump $\Delta\phi$, constant [mobility](order-parameter-mobility.md) $M$, and quasi-static exterior [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md),

$$
\mu(r)=\mu_\infty+(\mu_s-\mu_\infty)\frac Rr,
\qquad
\dot R=-\frac{M(\mu_s-\mu_\infty)}{\Delta\phi\,R}.
$$

The first formula solves the exterior [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md). The second follows from conservation: the total outward flux $4\pi MR(\mu_s-\mu_\infty)$ removes excess composition at the rate $-4\pi R^2\Delta\phi\,\dot R$. For a symmetric mixture, $\Delta\phi=2\phi_B$ and the [Gibbs--Thomson relation](gibbs-thomson-relation.md) gives $\mu_s=\gamma/(\phi_B R)$.
