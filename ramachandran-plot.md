# Ramachandran plot

↑ **Parent:** [Protein structure](protein-structure.md)

A [Ramachandran plot](ramachandran-plot.md) displays backbone $\phi,\psi$ angles. [glycine](glycine.md) has a broader sterically allowed region because its side chain is [hydrogen](hydrogen.md), whereas [proline](proline.md) ring closure strongly restricts $\phi$.
