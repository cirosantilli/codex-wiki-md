# Inverse bremsstrahlung

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

Collisional absorption of electromagnetic wave energy by charged particles scattering from other charges. In a weakly collisional unmagnetized [Drude model](drude-model.md), with $X=\omega_p^2/\omega^2<1$, the intensity absorption coefficient is $\alpha_I\simeq(\nu_{ei}/c)X/\sqrt{1-X}$. The corresponding electric-field amplitude decays at half that rate.
