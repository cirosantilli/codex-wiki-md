# Holomorphy of a Dirichlet series from bounded partial sums

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md)

Writing $B(x)=\sum_{n\le x}b_n$, the [Abel summation formula](abel-s-summation-formula.md) gives $\sum b_n n^{-s}=s\int_1^\infty B(x)x^{-s-1}\,dx$. If $B$ is bounded, the integral and its differentiated integrands converge uniformly on compact subsets of $\operatorname{Re}s>0$, giving a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md). A nonprincipal [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) has bounded partial sums because it is periodic and its sum over a period is zero.
