# Typed lambda term

↑ **Parent:** [Simply typed lambda calculus](simply-typed-lambda-calculus.md)

A [typed lambda term](typed-lambda-term.md) carries a type derivation under a context of typed free variables. The rules for variables, abstraction, and application correspond to assumptions, the [implication introduction rule](implication-introduction-rule.md), and the [implication elimination rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) under the [Implicational Curry-Howard correspondence](implicational-curry-howard-correspondence.md). General [fixed-point combinators](fixed-point-combinator.md) belong to the [untyped lambda calculus](untyped-lambda-calculus.md); self-application cannot be given a finite simple type.
