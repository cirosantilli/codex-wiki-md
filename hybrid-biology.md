# Hybrid (biology)

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_(biology))

An offspring or descendant combining ancestry from distinguishable parental lineages. A [hybrid](hybrid-biology.md) can be viable and fertile; its existence alone neither proves nor disproves strong [reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md) in natural [biological populations](population-biology.md).
