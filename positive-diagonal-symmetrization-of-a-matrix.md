# Positive diagonal symmetrization of a matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Similarity transformation](similarity-transformation.md)

If a real [matrix](matrix.md) $B$ has a positive [diagonal matrix](diagonal-matrix.md) $W$ such that $WB$ is a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md), then $H=W^{1/2}BW^{-1/2}$ is a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) related to $B$ by a [similarity transformation](similarity-transformation.md): $B^TW=WB$ gives $H^T=H$. Thus $B$ is a [diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md) with real [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). If $\mathbf z^TWB\mathbf z\le0$ for all real [vectors](vector.md) $\mathbf z$, its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are nonpositive. This converts symmetry in a weighted [inner product](inner-product.md) into Euclidean symmetry.
