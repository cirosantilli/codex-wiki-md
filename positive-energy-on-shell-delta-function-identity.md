# Positive-energy on-shell delta function identity

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure](lorentz-invariant-phase-space-measure.md)

For $E>0$ and the positive-energy branch, the root Jacobian of the [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) gives this identity. In a [massless collinear parton approximation](massless-collinear-parton-approximation.md), it produces $\delta(x-\xi)/(P\cdot q)$.
