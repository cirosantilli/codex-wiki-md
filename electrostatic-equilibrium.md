# Electrostatic equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics.md)

A state with a stationary [electric charge](electric-charge.md) distribution and no net drift of mobile charge driven by the [electric field](electric-field.md). A [conductor in electrostatic equilibrium](conductor-in-electrostatic-equilibrium.md) has zero interior [electric field](electric-field.md), while bound charges in an insulating material need not move to eliminate its interior field.
