# Pendulum with a uniform rod and an endpoint mass

↑ **Parent:** [Physical pendulum](physical-pendulum.md)

A uniform rigid rod of length $l$ and [mass](mass.md) $M_r$, pivoted at one end with a [point mass](point-mass.md) $m$ at the other, has pivot [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I=l^2(m+M_r/3)$. Its gravitational [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is $-gl(m+M_r/2)\cos\theta$. The planar [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) is $I\ddot\theta+gl(m+M_r/2)\sin\theta=0$, giving the displayed small-amplitude [oscillation period](period-of-an-oscillation.md). The limit $M_r=0$ gives a [simple pendulum](simple-pendulum-split.md), whereas $m=0$ gives the uniform-rod [physical pendulum](physical-pendulum.md). Thinness does not specify a rod's mass, so both contributions must be stated or explicitly neglected.
