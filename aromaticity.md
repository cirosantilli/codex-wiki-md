# Aromaticity

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aromaticity)

[Aromaticity](aromaticity.md) describes the distinctive stability and associated electronic properties of suitable cyclic delocalized systems. In simple planar conjugated rings, orbital occupancy determines whether delocalization is stabilizing; merely drawing a ring is insufficient.
