# Cantor Bernoulli measure

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli shift](bernoulli-shift.md)

The law of $\sum_{j\geq1}2\omega_j3^{-j}$, for independent fair binary digits $\omega_j$, is supported on the [Cantor set](cantor-set.md). It is invariant and ergodic under $T_3$, as the image of a [Bernoulli shift](bernoulli-shift.md). Each permitted ternary cylinder of length $n$ has measure $2^{-n}$, giving entropy rate $\log2$. The [Host equidistribution theorem](host-equidistribution-theorem.md) makes almost every point a [normal number](normal-number.md) in base $2$, while its ternary digits omit $1$.
