# Automatic continuity of characters

↑ **Parent:** [Character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md)

For a [character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md) $\varphi$ on a complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), $\varphi(1)=1$ and $\varphi(a)\in\sigma(a)$, so $|\varphi(a)|\leq\lVert a\rVert$ without any prior continuity assumption. Under the usual normalization $\lVert1\rVert=1$, this gives $\lVert\varphi\rVert=1$; for an unnormalized [algebra norm](algebra-norm.md), only the upper bound is automatic.
