# Tracking an exponentially moving equilibrium without oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Forced harmonic oscillator](forced-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For $\ddot x+\gamma\dot x+\omega_0^2(x-x_0)=0$, the particular path $x=x_0$ is a solution precisely when $\ddot x_0+\gamma\dot x_0=0$. An exponentially moving equilibrium $x_0=d e^{-\gamma t}$ satisfies this condition. If the initial position and [velocity](velocity.md) match it, uniqueness removes all homogeneous oscillations even when the natural system is underdamped. The motion is sustained dynamically by [linear drag](linear-drag.md), with zero instantaneous spring extension.
