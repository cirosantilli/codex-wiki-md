# Artinian modules are co-Hopfian

↑ **Parent:** [Artinian module](artinian-module.md)

For an injective [module endomorphism](module-endomorphism.md) $f:M\to M$ of an [Artinian module](artinian-module.md), the chain $M\supseteq fM\supseteq f^2M\supseteq\cdots$ stabilizes. If $f^nM=f^{n+1}M$, then every $x$ satisfies $f^nx=f^{n+1}y$. Injectivity of $f^n$ gives $x=fy$, proving surjectivity. No finite-length or Noetherian hypothesis is needed.
