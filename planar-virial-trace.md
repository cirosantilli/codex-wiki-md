# Planar virial trace

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md)

The planar trace of the [stellar pressure-energy tensor](stellar-pressure-energy-tensor.md) is the sum of its two Cartesian in-plane components. For an axisymmetric nonstreaming population it equals $\int\rho(\langle v_R^2\rangle+\langle v_\phi^2\rangle)d^3x$, not merely the cylindrical radial second moment. A spherical isotropic population has $\Pi_\perp=2\Pi_{zz}$.
