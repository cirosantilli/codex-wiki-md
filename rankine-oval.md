# Rankine oval

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

A uniform planar stream combined with an equal source and sink produces a closed dividing [streamline](streamline.md) enclosing them. Outside it the flow approaches the uniform stream; inside it streamlines connect the source and sink. For positive $U,m$ and separation one, the [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) is $U-m/[2\pi z(z-1)]$ and the stagnation points are $[1\pm\sqrt{1+2m/(\pi U)}]/2$. Both lie on the dividing streamline, which has zero streamfunction with consistent logarithm branches.
