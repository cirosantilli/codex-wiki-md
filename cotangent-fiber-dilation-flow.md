# Cotangent fiber-dilation flow

↑ **Parent:** [Liouville vector field](liouville-vector-field.md)

The vertical [Liouville vector field](liouville-vector-field.md) on a [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md) has complete flow $D_s(x,p)=(x,e^sp)$. Its pullbacks scale both the [Liouville one-form](canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) and the canonical [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) by $e^s$. As $s\to-\infty$, each orbit tends to its base point on the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md). This contraction proves [rigidity of cotangent Liouville-form preservation](rigidity-of-cotangent-liouville-form-preservation.md).
