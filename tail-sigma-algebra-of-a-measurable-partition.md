# Tail sigma-algebra of a measurable partition

↑ **Parent:** [K-mixing](k-mixing.md)

The tail sigma-algebra of a finite partition $\xi$ is

$$
\mathcal T(\xi)=\bigcap_{n\geq0}\bigvee_{k=n}^\infty T^{-k}\xi.
$$

By the [reverse martingale convergence theorem](reverse-martingale-convergence-theorem.md), K-mixing is equivalent to triviality of $\mathcal T(\xi)$ for every finite partition $\xi$.
