# Cell-free extract

↑ **Parent:** [Biochemistry](biochemistry-split.md)

A [cell-free extract](cell-free-extract.md) is material obtained from disrupted [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) after removing intact [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). Its biochemical activity can be tested separately from the whole [organism](organism-split.md). It can contain many [enzymes](enzyme-split.md) and [substrates](substrate-biochemistry.md), together with [cofactor](cofactor-biochemistry.md) components; cell removal is different from purification of one component.
