# Adjugate identity

↑ **Parent:** [Adjugate matrix](adjugate-matrix.md)

Every square matrix $M$ over a commutative ring satisfies

$$
M\operatorname{adj}(M)=\operatorname{adj}(M)M=\det(M)I.
$$
