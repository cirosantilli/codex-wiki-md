# Tomita operator

↑ **Parent:** [Separating vector for an operator algebra](separating-vector-for-an-operator-algebra.md)

For a [Von Neumann algebra](von-neumann-algebra.md) with a cyclic separating [vector](vector.md), this is a densely defined antilinear operator. It is closable: the commutant orbit lies in the domain of its antilinear adjoint and is dense. Write $S$ for its [closure](closure-topology.md) and $S=J\Delta^{1/2}$ for its polar decomposition. In finite dimension it is everywhere defined and invertible. For a tracial [vector](vector.md), $S_0$ is isometric and extends to an antiunitary involution.
