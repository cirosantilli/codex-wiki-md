# Twistor incidence relation

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor space](twistor-space.md)

For a fixed affine spacetime point $x$, this linear relation cuts out a two-dimensional subspace $W_x=\{(ix\pi,\pi)\}$ of [twistor space](twistor-space.md). Projectivizing gives its [twistor line](twistor-line.md). For a fixed projective twistor with $\pi\ne0$, its solutions are $x=x_0+u^A\pi^{A'}$, a totally null affine [alpha-plane](alpha-plane.md). The compact description replaces affine solutions by the two-planes containing the fixed twistor line in the vector space.
