# Transformation of a random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Random variable](random-variable-split.md)

A [measurable function](measurable-function.md) $g$ applied to a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ defines a new [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $Y=g(X)$. Its [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) is the [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md) under $g$.
