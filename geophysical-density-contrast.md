# Geophysical density contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md)

The absolute [density](density.md) difference between a subsurface body and the material it replaces, commonly in $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. It is the source term in local [gravity inversion](gravity-inversion.md), unlike the dimensionless relative density contrast used in [cosmology](cosmology-split.md).
