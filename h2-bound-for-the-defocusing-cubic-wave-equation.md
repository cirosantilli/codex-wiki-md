# H2 bound for the defocusing cubic wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Defocusing semilinear wave equation](defocusing-semilinear-wave-equation.md)

For $u_{tt}-\Delta u+u^3=0$ in three spatial dimensions, conserved positive [wave energy](wave-energy.md) $E$ controls $\|\nabla u\|_2$. The [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md) gives $\|u^2\nabla u\|_2\lesssim\|\nabla u\|_2^2\|D^2u\|_2\lesssim E\|D^2u\|_2$. Differentiated [wave energy estimates](wave-energy-estimate.md) and the [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) then give $\|u(t)\|_{\dot H^2}+\|u_t(t)\|_{\dot H^1}\leq C D e^{C E t}$, where $D$ bounds the corresponding initial [Sobolev norms](sobolev-norm.md).
