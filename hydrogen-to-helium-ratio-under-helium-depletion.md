# Hydrogen-to-helium ratio under helium depletion

↑ **Parent:** [Saturn](saturn.md)

Removing [helium](helium.md) from a hydrogen-rich envelope lowers He/H and raises its reciprocal H/He. A helium-rain explanation of atmospheric depletion must keep those two ratio conventions distinct. Settling helium-rich material can release [gravitational energy](gravitational-energy.md) while changing atmospheric composition; excess [luminosity](luminosity.md) alone does not uniquely determine the interior process.
