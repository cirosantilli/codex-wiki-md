# Forward-backward asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Angular distribution](angular-distribution.md)

The normalized difference of forward and backward [cross sections](cross-section-physics.md) relative to a specified incoming-particle axis. For $d\sigma/dc=K[(A+B)(1+c^2)+2(A-B)c]$ with $c=\cos\theta$, integration gives $\sigma_F-\sigma_B=2K(A-B)$ and $\sigma_F+\sigma_B=8K(A+B)/3$, hence $A_{\rm FB}=3(A-B)/(4(A+B))$. The [pole forward-backward asymmetry for charged leptons](pole-forward-backward-asymmetry-for-charged-leptons.md) is its specialized [Z boson](z-boson.md) result.
