# Parallelogram

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrilateral](quadrilateral.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallelogram)

A parallelogram is the [convex set](convex-set.md) with vertices $p,p+u,p+v,p+u+v$ for two linearly independent planar vectors $u,v$. Its opposite edges are parallel and its centre $p+(u+v)/2$ gives central symmetry. Its area is $|\det[u,v]|$, so an invertible linear map changes its area by the absolute value of that map's [determinant](determinant.md). These facts make parallelograms convenient convex bodies for two-dimensional applications of the [Minkowski convex body theorem](minkowski-s-theorem.md).
