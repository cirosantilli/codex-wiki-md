# Near-contact van der Waals attraction of spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

For two equal spheres of radius $a$ separated by a surface gap $h\ll a$, a positive [Hamaker constant](hamaker-constant.md) $A_H$ gives the nonretarded [Van der Waals force](van-der-waals-force.md) approximation $U=-A_Ha/(12h)$ and $F=-dU/dh=-A_Ha/(12h^2)$. The [Derjaguin approximation](derjaguin-approximation.md) derives the geometry factor from the planar interaction energy per area $W=-A_H/(12\pi h^2)$ and effective radius $a/2$. A sphere and plane instead have effective radius $a$, doubling the force at the same gap. Molecular repulsion, roughness and retardation limit these continuum formulas.
