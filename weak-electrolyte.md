# Weak electrolyte

↑ **Parent:** [Electrolyte](electrolyte.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_electrolyte)

A [weak electrolyte](weak-electrolyte.md) yields only a fraction of [ions](ion.md) from its dissolved substance under the specified conditions, as illustrated by [acetic acid](acetic-acid.md) in [water](water.md).

For a neutral [weak acid](weak-acid.md), unconverted [molecules](molecule-split.md) coexist with [hydronium](hydronium.md) and the [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) at [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md). The [weak acid dissociation fraction](weak-acid-dissociation-fraction.md) describes the partition of the dissolved inventory; the [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md) describes the [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) at fixed [solvent](solvent.md), [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [standard states](standard-state.md). A single fraction is not a permanent label for every [concentration](concentration.md): [dilution](dilution.md) can increase that fraction while the [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md) stays fixed. Calling a substance a [weak electrolyte](weak-electrolyte.md) does not mean its [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) must be dilute.[Chemistry 2e, §14.3, Acid and Base Ionization Constants, percent-ionization discussion preceding Example 14.7](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/14-3-relative-strengths-of-acids-and-bases.md)

The following [ideal weak electrolyte dilution law](ideal-weak-electrolyte-dilution-law.md) makes the distinction quantitative for a [monoprotic acid](monoprotic-acid.md). Other [weak electrolytes](weak-electrolyte.md), mixed [chemical solutions](solution-chemistry.md) and additional proton-transfer steps require their own [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) and inventory balances.
