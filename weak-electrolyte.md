# Weak electrolyte

↑ **Parent:** [Electrolyte](electrolyte.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_electrolyte)

A [weak electrolyte](weak-electrolyte.md) yields only a fraction of [ions](ion.md) from its dissolved substance under the specified conditions, as illustrated by [acetic acid](acetic-acid.md) in [water](water.md).
