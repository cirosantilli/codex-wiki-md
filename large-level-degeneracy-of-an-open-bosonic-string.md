# Large-level degeneracy of an open bosonic string

↑ **Parent:** [Open-string oscillator degeneracy](open-string-oscillator-degeneracy.md)

The [Dedekind eta function](dedekind-eta-function.md) modular transformation gives $F(e^{-t})\sim(t/2\pi)^{12}\exp(4\pi^2/t-t)$. The [saddle-point approximation](saddle-point-approximation.md) for coefficient extraction has $t_0=2\pi/\sqrt{p-1}$, phase $4\pi\sqrt{p-1}$ and curvature $(p-1)^{3/2}/\pi$. Its prefactor $t_0^{12}$ supplies $(p-1)^{-6}$ and the Gaussian width supplies $(p-1)^{-3/4}/\sqrt2$. This explains both the exponential growth and the power $-27/4$.
