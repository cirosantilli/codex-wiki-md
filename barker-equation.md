# Barker equation

↑ **Parent:** [Parabolic trajectory](parabolic-trajectory.md)

For a [parabolic trajectory](parabolic-trajectory.md) with [periapsis](periapsis.md) radius $r_p$, put $D=\tan(f/2)$, where $f$ is [true anomaly](true-anomaly.md). The Barker equation is $t-t_p=\sqrt{2r_p^3/(GM)}(D+D^3/3)$. Since $r=r_p(1+D^2)$, it is equivalent to the [radius-time relation for a parabolic Kepler orbit](radius-time-relation-for-a-parabolic-kepler-orbit.md). The sign of $D$ distinguishes approach from recession; $t_p$ is the [periapsis](periapsis.md) time.
