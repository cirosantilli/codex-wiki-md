# Double complex

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)

A double complex has modules $C_{p,q}$ and differentials lowering either index, each squaring to zero and anticommuting with the other. The total [chain complex](chain-complex.md) has term $\bigoplus_{p+q=i}C_{p,q}$ and differential the sum of the two differentials. For the tensor product of two [chain complexes](chain-complex.md), a sign $(-1)^p$ on the second differential ensures anticommutation. If the double complex is in the first quadrant and one direction has [homology](homology-split.md) only in degree zero, its total [homology](homology-split.md) is computed by the surviving degree-zero complex. Applying this twice to two [free resolutions](free-resolution.md) proves the balanced calculation of the [Tor functor](tor-functor.md).
