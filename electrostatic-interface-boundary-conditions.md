# Electrostatic interface boundary conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics.md)

Across a charged dielectric interface the tangential [electric field](electric-field.md) is continuous and the normal displacement jump is the fixed [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md). Without a dipole sheet the [electrostatic potential](electric-potential.md) is continuous. For a charge sheet with equal [permittivity](permittivity.md) on both sides, its normal potential derivative has jump $-\sigma_{\rm fixed}/\varepsilon$. Setting the field to zero on one side is an additional physical boundary assumption, as in a conductor or a negligible-exterior-admittance idealization.
