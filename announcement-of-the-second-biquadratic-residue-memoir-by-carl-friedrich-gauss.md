# Announcement of the second biquadratic-residue memoir by Carl Friedrich Gauss

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Carl Friedrich Gauss](work-by-carl-friedrich-gauss.md)

German self-announcement in Göttingische gelehrte Anzeigen. The digital transcription reproduces the argument about terminology, [Gaussian integers](gaussian-integer.md) and the [complex plane](complex-plane.md). Its concluding discussion is the source of the wording quoted in the exam; this is distinct from the Latin memoir printed in 1832.
