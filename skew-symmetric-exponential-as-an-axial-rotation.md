# Skew-symmetric exponential as an axial rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md)

For a nonzero real three-dimensional [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md), choose real orthogonal vectors $u,v$ of equal length with $Au=\theta v$, $Av=-\theta u$ and $\theta>0$, and a real unit vector $e_3$ in its kernel. The [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) acts by $e^Au=u\cos\theta+v\sin\theta$, $e^Av=v\cos\theta-u\sin\theta$ and $e^Ae_3=e_3$. Its matrix in this [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) is a planar [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) with an unchanged axis. A purely imaginary nonzero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) and its complex [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) give $u,v$ by taking real and imaginary parts; antisymmetry proves their equal norms and orthogonality.
