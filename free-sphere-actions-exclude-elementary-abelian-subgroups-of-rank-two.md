# Free sphere actions exclude elementary abelian subgroups of rank two

↑ **Parent:** [Free group action](free-group-action.md)

Suppose a finite [group](group-split.md) $G$ acts freely on $S^n$, $n>1$. The quotient has [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) $G$ and higher [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) equal to those of $S^n$. Attach one $(n+1)$-cell killing a generator of $\pi_n\cong\mathbb Z$, then attach higher cells successively to kill all remaining higher [homotopy](homotopy.md). This constructs a [Eilenberg–MacLane space](eilenberg-maclane-space.md) $K(G,1)$ with only one $(n+1)$-cell. The [one-cell bound on next-degree cohomology](one-cell-bound-on-next-degree-cohomology.md) gives dimension at most one in degree $n+1$. But if $G=C_p\times C_p$, the [mod-p cohomology ring of a cyclic group](mod-p-cohomology-ring-of-a-cyclic-group.md) and [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) give dimension $n+2$ there. This contradiction excludes that group, and also excludes such a subgroup of any group acting freely on a sphere.
