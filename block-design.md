# Block design

↑ **Parent:** [Blocks in experimental design](blocks-in-experimental-design.md)

A [block design](block-design.md) assigns [treatments](treatment.md) to [experimental units](experimental-unit.md) partitioned into blocks. Its incidence counts $n_{ij}$ record how often [treatment](treatment.md) $i$ occurs in block $j$. Balance and connectedness determine which [treatment contrasts](treatment-contrast.md) are estimable and how much adjustment for blocks costs.
