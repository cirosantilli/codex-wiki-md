<h1 id="prufer-multiplicities-for-a-full-rooted-binary-tree">Prüfer multiplicities for a full rooted binary tree</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Prüfer sequence](prufer-sequence.md)

A tree on $2m+1$ labels has one root of degree two, $m-1$ other interior vertices of degree three, and $m+1$ leaves precisely when its [Prüfer code](prufer-sequence.md) contains one label once, $m-1$ labels twice, and all other labels zero times. The [Prüfer degree formula](prufer-degree-formula.md) gives both directions, and identifies the root as the uniquely once-occurring label. Children are unordered unless a plane embedding is additional data.
