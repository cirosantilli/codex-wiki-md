# Isothermal free-energy change of a Van der Waals gas

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals gas](van-der-waals-gas.md)

For the one-mole [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md) with constant parameters, integrate $dF=-p\,dV$ along an isotherm to obtain the displayed difference. The attractive contribution has a positive change on expansion, whereas the translational term lowers free [energy](energy-split.md). Thermodynamic consistency independently gives $\Delta U=a(1/V_1-1/V_2)$ and $\Delta S=R\ln[(V_2-b)/(V_1-b)]$. These homogeneous-branch expressions are not a substitute for equilibrium convexification through a subcritical coexistence region.
