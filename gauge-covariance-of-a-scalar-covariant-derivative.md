# Gauge covariance of a scalar covariant derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md)

With $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+R(A_\mu)$ and $\delta_X A_\mu=-\epsilon\partial_\mu X+\epsilon[X,A_\mu]$, the derivative terms in $\delta_X(D_\mu\phi)$ cancel. The remaining terms combine using $R([X,A_\mu])=[R(X),R(A_\mu)]$, so the covariant derivative transforms in the same [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) as the scalar. This convention absorbs the gauge coupling into the connection.
