# Weakly typical sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Typical set](typical-set.md)

For an [IID random variable](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) source with [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) $p$, a word $x^n$ is weakly typical when $|-(1/n)\log_2p(x^n)-H(X)|\leq\varepsilon$. It has probability on the scale $2^{-nH(X)}$. The [weak law of large numbers](weak-law-of-large-numbers.md) makes the [typical set](typical-set.md) probable, but does not make every word in it representative: for a uniform source every possible word is weakly typical.
