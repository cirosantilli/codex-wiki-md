# Radial orbit time transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Celestial mechanics](celestial-mechanics.md)

For a central-force radial [energy](energy.md) equation, choose a monotone radius $q=q(r)$ and clock $\tau$ such that $d\tau/dt=(q/r)dq/dr$. Then $q'=(r/q)\dot r$, and multiplying the old [energy](energy.md) equation by $r^2/q^2$ preserves the centrifugal form $L^2/(2q^2)$. A split of the remaining terms into constant [energy](energy.md) and a new [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) produces a transformed radial orbit.
