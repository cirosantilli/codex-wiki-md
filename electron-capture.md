# Electron capture

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)

A nucleus can capture an electron when energetically allowed, converting a proton into a neutron and emitting an electron [neutrino](neutrino.md). Beryllium-7 [electron capture](electron-capture.md) participates in one of the [proton-proton chain branches](proton-proton-chain-branches.md). Stellar capture rates depend on ionization, [mass density](density.md) and electron phase space.
