# Separating transcendence basis

↑ **Parent:** [Transcendence basis](transcendence-basis.md)

A [transcendence basis](transcendence-basis.md) $T$ for $L/k$ is separating if $L/k(T)$ is a [separable field extension](separable-extension.md). In this case the $dt$, $t\in T$, form a basis of the [Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md). In characteristic zero every transcendence basis is separating. In positive characteristic the condition on the chosen basis is essential.
