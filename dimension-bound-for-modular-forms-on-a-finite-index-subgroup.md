# Dimension bound for modular forms on a finite-index subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md)

For nonnegative integer $k$ and a finite-index subgroup $\Gamma$, a nonzero [modular form](modular-form.md) has order at infinity, measured in a genuine periodic cusp parameter, at most $k[SL_2(\mathbb Z):\Gamma]/12$. The product of its slash translates over the left cosets is a nonzero level-one form; the translates along the infinity-cusp orbit contribute its full local order to this product. The [valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md) gives the bound. Initial coefficients through that bound therefore give an injective linear map.
