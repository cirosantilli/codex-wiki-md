# Spiral arm

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic disk](galactic-disk.md)

An elongated overdensity in a [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md), commonly traced by gas, young stars and [star formation](star-formation.md). An arm pattern need not rotate at the same rate as individual stars.
