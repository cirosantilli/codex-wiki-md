# Charged-current neutrino-electron threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)

For a massless incident [neutrino](neutrino.md) and a stationary target [electron](electron.md), the invariant squared center-of-mass [energy](energy-split.md) is $s=m_e^2+2m_eE_\nu$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). Production of a charged [lepton](lepton.md) of mass $m_\ell$ and a massless neutrino requires $s\ge m_\ell^2$, giving the displayed threshold. For the electron flavour the threshold is zero; for heavier charged leptons it is enhanced by the small stationary-target mass.
