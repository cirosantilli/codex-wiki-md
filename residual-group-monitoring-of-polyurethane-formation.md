# Residual-group monitoring of polyurethane formation

↑ **Parent:** [Polyaddition](polyaddition.md)

An experimental comparison joined a two-ended [diol](diol.md) chain, HOPCLOH, with a bifunctional [isocyanate](isocyanate.md), at reported [isocyanate](isocyanate.md)-to-[diol](diol.md) ratio $1.14:1$. The researchers used oven-dried $25\,\mathrm{mL}$ round-bottom flasks and an IKA RET magnetic hotplate with a metal carrier. They varied [solvent](solvent.md), [concentration](concentration.md), reaction [temperature](temperature.md) ($50$ or $61\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$), and duration ($1$ or $8\,\mathrm h$).[sections 2.1 and 2.3: flask, heating apparatus and varied conditions](experimental-design-2%E2%81%B4-to-improve-the-reaction-conditions-of-non-segmented-poly-ester-urethanes-peus-derived-from-alpha-omega-hydroxy-telechelic-poly-epsilon-caprolactone-hopcloh-by-jaime-maldonado-estudillo-and-others.md)

A Bruker $500\,\mathrm{MHz}$ [NMR spectroscopy](nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) instrument measured residual [hydroxy group](hydroxy-group.md)-adjacent signal relative to an [ester](ester.md) signal. Independently, [size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md) used ResiPore columns at $70\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, a refractive-index detector, and polystyrene calibration. Figure 8 reports sharply increasing apparent number-average [molar mass](molar-mass.md) beyond the reported conversion $0.95$. A deliberately water-exposed [isocyanate](isocyanate.md) comparison reproduced a $1625\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ [infrared spectroscopy](infrared-spectroscopy.md) band assigned to [urea](urea.md)-containing products. The authors warn that tiny residual [NMR spectroscopy](nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) signals reduce integration precision.[sections 3.2–3.3; Figure 3 discussion of the water comparison; section 5.1 and Figure 8: independent conversion and GPC readouts and low-signal limitation](experimental-design-2%E2%81%B4-to-improve-the-reaction-conditions-of-non-segmented-poly-ester-urethanes-peus-derived-from-alpha-omega-hydroxy-telechelic-poly-epsilon-caprolactone-hopcloh-by-jaime-maldonado-estudillo-and-others.md)

The inference is increasing chain size alongside depletion of reactive groups, consistent with [step-growth polymerization](step-growth-polymerization.md). It is not an exact measurement of $X_n$ in the toy model. The feed contains already extended [diol](diol.md) chains: counting their constituent repeat units differs from counting each feed [molecule](molecule.md) as one unit. Their varying [molar masses](molar-mass.md), calibration against a different [polymer](polymer.md), residual starting material, and competing [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction.md) further obstruct a direct conversion-to-$X_n$ substitution. Report which [functional groups](functional-group.md) the conversion readout measures before comparing it with [stoichiometric imbalance in step growth](stoichiometric-imbalance-in-step-growth.md).

Useful _proposed controls_, distinct from the reported water comparison, are pre-reaction spectra and matched mixtures omitting each joining partner in turn. Keep [solvent](solvent.md), heating, and signal normalization matched. Disappearance of an [isocyanate](isocyanate.md) signal in the omitted-[diol](diol.md) control would reveal competing consumption rather than establish chain joining. Agreement between residual-group and independently calibrated chain-size readouts is stronger evidence than calculating both quantities from the same [Carothers equation](carothers-equation.md).
