# Current clamp

↑ **Parent:** [Patch clamp](patch-clamp.md)

[Current clamp](current-clamp.md) prescribes injected [electric current](electric-current.md) while recording [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md). A constant command does not force [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md) to remain constant: a [neuron](neuron.md) can produce [action potentials](action-potential.md) or [synaptic potentials](synaptic-potential.md) while that command is unchanged. At zero commanded [electric current](electric-current.md), the amplifier still measures [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md); intrinsic ionic [electric currents](electric-current.md) continue. The name concerns current supplied by the instrument, not the cancellation of all currents in the [biological cell](biological-cell.md).

In [whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md), the measured pipette voltage during a current pulse includes the [patch-clamp series resistance](patch-clamp-series-resistance.md) drop. A modern authored methods article explicitly describes current-pulse protocols and this distortion.[Intracellular Current Injection; Step 5: Whole-Cell Configuration](multiple-two-photon-targeted-whole-cell-patch-clamp-recordings-from-monosynaptically-connected-neurons-in-vivo-by-jean-sebastien-jouhanneau-and-others.md) Check the access contribution with small pulses and distinguish the immediate pipette-voltage jump from the slower [membrane capacitance](membrane-capacitance.md) response. A zero-input interval measures a baseline, not proof that the instrument has no voltage offset. The passive example below explains these distinctions without asserting any new physiological measurements.
