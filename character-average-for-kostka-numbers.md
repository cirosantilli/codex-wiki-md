# Character average for Kostka numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Kostka number](kostka-number.md)

Over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), the [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md) of $S^\mu$ in the [Young permutation module](young-permutation-module.md) $M^\lambda$ equals $K_{\mu\lambda}$. Its [character](character-of-a-representation.md) is $\operatorname{Ind}_{S_\lambda}^{S_n}\mathbf1$, so [Frobenius reciprocity](frobenius-reciprocity.md) expresses this [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md) as the average of the restricted [irreducible character](irreducible-character.md) over the [Young subgroup](young-subgroup.md). For $\lambda=(2,1^{n-2})$ that [subgroup](subgroup.md) contains only the identity and one [transposition](transposition-permutation.md), giving $(\chi_\mu(1)+\chi_\mu((12)))/2$. For $\lambda=(1^n)$ it is trivial, giving the [character](character-of-a-representation.md) degree. This supplies a calculation from [ordinary character](ordinary-character.md) values without enumerating [semistandard tableaux](semistandard-young-tableau.md). An indexing convention writing the [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md) as $m_{\lambda,\mu}$ reverses the conventional Kostka indices.
