# Relative extension of a compatible almost complex structure

↑ **Parent:** [Compatible almost complex structure](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md)

If a compatible almost complex structure is prescribed along a closed [symplectic submanifold](symplectic-submanifold.md) $N\subset M$ and preserves $TN$, it extends to one on $M$. Choose a compatible structure separately on $TN$ and its [symplectic normal bundle](symplectic-normal-bundle.md), use the associated metric along $N$, extend that metric to $M$, and apply the [metric construction of a compatible almost complex structure](metric-construction-of-a-compatible-almost-complex-structure.md).
