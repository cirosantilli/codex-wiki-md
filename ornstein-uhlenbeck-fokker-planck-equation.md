# Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Fokker-Planck equation

↑ **Parent:** [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md)

The probability-density equation for the [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md) with drift $-v$ and diffusion coefficient $\sqrt2$. The unit-covariance [Gaussian density](multivariate-normal-density.md) is stationary because its [Fokker-Planck probability current](fokker-planck-probability-current.md) vanishes. This normalization makes [mean velocity](mean-velocity-of-a-kinetic-distribution.md) decay at rate one and [energy](energy.md) excess decay at rate two.
