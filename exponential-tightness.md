# Exponential tightness

↑ **Parent:** [Large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md)

A family with [large-deviation speed](large-deviation-speed.md) $a_N$ is exponentially tight if for every $M>0$ there is a [compact set](compact-space.md) $K_M$ such that

$$
\limsup_N a_N^{-1}\log\mathbb P(X_N\notin K_M)\leq-M.
$$

Local upper bounds then imply the closed-set upper bound of a [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md): intersect the closed set with $K_M$, cover that compact intersection by finitely many neighborhoods on which the local bounds hold, and use a finite union bound. The remaining probability has exponent at most $-M$; let $M\to\infty$.
