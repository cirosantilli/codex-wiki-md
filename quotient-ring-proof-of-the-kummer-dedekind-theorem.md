# Quotient-ring proof of the Kummer-Dedekind theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer-Dedekind theorem](kummer-dedekind-theorem.md)

For an integral generator $\alpha$ of a [number field](number-field.md), let $A=\mathbb Z[\alpha]$ and suppose $p$ is coprime to $[\mathcal O_K:A]$. Localizing at $p$ makes $A$ and the [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) equal, so reduction gives the displayed [quotient ring](quotient-ring.md). If $\overline m=\prod g_i^{e_i}$ with distinct monic [irreducible polynomials](irreducible-polynomial.md), the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) gives local factors $\mathbb F_p[X]/(g_i^{e_i})$. Their [residue fields](residue-field.md) have degrees $\deg g_i$ and their [maximal ideals](maximal-ideal.md) have [nilpotence indices of an ideal](nilpotence-index-of-an-ideal.md) $e_i$. Localizing the [Dedekind domain](dedekind-domain.md) $\mathcal O_K$ at the corresponding [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md) gives [discrete valuation rings](discrete-valuation-ring.md), where this same [nilpotence index of an ideal](nilpotence-index-of-an-ideal.md) is the [ramification index of a prime ideal](ramification-index-of-a-prime-ideal.md). Thus $(p)=\prod(p,g_i(\alpha))^{e_i}$ and each [residue degree](residue-degree.md) is $\deg g_i$.
