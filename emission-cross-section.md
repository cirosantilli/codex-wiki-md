# Emission cross section

↑ **Parent:** [Stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md)

The effective area converting incident photon flux into a [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) rate for a specified transition, [frequency](frequency.md), [polarization](polarization-waves.md) and sublevel distribution. For statistically populated [energy levels](energy-level.md) of [statistical weights](statistical-weight.md) $g_1,g_2$, the [Einstein coefficients](einstein-coefficients.md) imply $g_1\sigma_{12}=g_2\sigma_{21}$. The related [absorption cross section](absorption-cross-section.md) therefore differs when the [statistical weights](statistical-weight.md) differ.
