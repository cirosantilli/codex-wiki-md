# Biotin

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[biotin](biotin.md) is a small biological [molecule](molecule-split.md) used as a covalent tag in several [protein](protein-split.md) labeling methods. Strong capture of biotinylated material enables enrichment before [mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md).
