# Hologram

↑ **Parent:** [Holography](holography.md)

A [hologram](hologram.md) is the recorded [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) pattern used for [holographic wavefront reconstruction](holographic-wavefront-reconstruction.md). The pattern need not resemble the object. A [photographic emulsion](photographic-emulsion.md) stores a material change produced by exposure; a digital intensity record instead requires numerical reconstruction or a device that displays the corresponding optical modulation.
