# Curl product rule for a scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Curl](curl.md)

For a continuously differentiable [scalar field](scalar-field.md) $\phi$ and [vector field](vector-field.md) $\mathbf F$, the [curl](curl.md) satisfies

$$
\nabla\times(\phi\mathbf F)=\phi\nabla\times\mathbf F+\nabla\phi\times\mathbf F.
$$

Indeed, with fixed Cartesian [basis vectors](basis-vector.md) $\mathbf e_i$, the Cartesian expression for the [curl](curl.md) and the [product rule](product-rule.md) give

$$
\sum_i\mathbf e_i\times\partial_i(\phi\mathbf F)
=\sum_i(\partial_i\phi)\mathbf e_i\times\mathbf F
+\phi\sum_i\mathbf e_i\times\partial_i\mathbf F.
$$

The first sum is the [cross product](cross-product.md) of the [gradient](gradient.md) of $\phi$ with $\mathbf F$; the second is $\phi$ times the [curl](curl.md) of $\mathbf F$. The formula is useful when applying the [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) to a weighted [vector field](vector-field.md).
