# Sophie Germain identity

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

The [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) identity $a^4+4b^4=(a^2-2ab+2b^2)(a^2+2ab+2b^2)$ follows by writing the left side as $(a^2+2b^2)^2-(2ab)^2$ and taking a difference of squares.
