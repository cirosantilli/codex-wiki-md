# Three-dimensional lens space as a circle bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md)

The diagonal quotient $L(p)=S^3/(\mathbb Z/p)$ is the unit [circle bundle](circle-bundle.md) of a complex line bundle over $S^2$ whose [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md) is $p$ times a generator. Its [Gysin sequence](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) gives

$$
H^j(L(p);\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&j=0,3,\\
\mathbb Z/p,&j=2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
$$

while $H^j(L(p);\mathbb F_p)\cong\mathbb F_p$ for $0\leq j\leq3$.
