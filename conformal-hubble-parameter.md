# Conformal Hubble parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md)

For [conformal time](conformal-time.md) $d\tau=dt/a$, the conformal Hubble parameter is $\mathcal H=a'/a=aH=\dot a$, where primes and dots denote conformal and [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md) derivatives respectively. Its inverse, in units $c=1$, is the [comoving Hubble radius](comoving-hubble-radius.md). It differs from the ordinary [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) by one factor of the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md).
