# End-driven two-bead string

↑ **Parent:** [Coupled oscillator](coupled-oscillator.md)

Place two equal [masses](mass.md) $M$ on a massless [mechanical string](mechanical-string.md) with spans $L,2L,L$ and uniform [tension](tension.md) $T$. Fix one end and drive the other with displacement $h(t)$. Linear transverse force balance gives $\ddot y_1+\omega_0^2(3y_1-y_2)=0$ and $\ddot y_2+\omega_0^2(3y_2-y_1)=2\omega_0^2h$. The mass-normalized stiffness matrix has symmetric and antisymmetric [normal modes](normal-mode.md) with squared angular frequencies $2\omega_0^2$ and $4\omega_0^2$. The mode coordinates $(y_1+y_2)/2$ and $(y_1-y_2)/2$ separate the forced equations.
