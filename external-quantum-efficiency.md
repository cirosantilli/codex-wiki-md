# External quantum efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Light-emitting diode efficiency](light-emitting-diode-efficiency.md)

The rate of escaping [photons](photon.md) divided by the electrical pair-supply rate $I/q$ in a [light-emitting diode](light-emitting-diode.md). This counts [photons](photon.md) rather than comparing optical and electrical [power](power.md).
