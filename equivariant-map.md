# Equivariant map

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant_map)

For $G$-sets $X$ and $Y$, a map $f:X\to Y$ is equivariant when $f(gx)=gf(x)$ for every $g\in G$ and $x\in X$.
