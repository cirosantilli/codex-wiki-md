# Inflationary scalar Fourier mode

↑ **Parent:** [Inflaton action in an expanding universe](inflaton-action-in-an-expanding-universe.md)

For nearly constant inflaton potential and constant $H_{\rm inf}$, the spatial [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) obeys

$$
\ddot{\widehat\phi}_{\mathbf k}
+3H_{\rm inf}\dot{\widehat\phi}_{\mathbf k}
+\frac{c^2k^2}{a^2}\widehat\phi_{\mathbf k}=0.
$$
