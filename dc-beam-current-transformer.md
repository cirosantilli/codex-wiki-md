# DC beam current transformer

↑ **Parent:** [Beam diagnostics](beam-diagnostics.md)

A nonintercepting circulating-beam current monitor that senses the beam's direct-current component through a magnetic core and feedback electronics. It is suitable for total stored intensity and [beam lifetime](beam-lifetime.md) measurements; zero-offset drift and calibration must be controlled. Bunch-resolved signals instead require an appropriate time-dependent pickup or current-transformer bandwidth.
