# Rectangular membrane normal mode

↑ **Parent:** [Vibrating membrane](vibrating-membrane.md)

For a rectangle of sides a and b with fixed edges, [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) gives $\psi=A\sin(p\pi x/a)\sin(q\pi y/b)\cos(\omega t+\phi)$ with positive integers p and q. Its [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is $\omega=c\pi\sqrt{p^2/a^2+q^2/b^2}$. Zero integers produce the trivial displacement, not a lower [mode](mode-statistics.md). The p−1 and q−1 interior nodal lines divide alternating-sign lobes.
