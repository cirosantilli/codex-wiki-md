# Axial-current divergence for a pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Axial current](axial-current.md)

For $\mathcal L_{\rm int}=-g\phi\bar\psi\gamma^5\psi$, the variational [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) and [adjoint Dirac equation](adjoint-dirac-equation.md) are $(i\not\partial-m-g\phi\gamma^5)\psi=0$ and $i(\partial_\mu\bar\psi)\gamma^\mu+m\bar\psi+g\phi\bar\psi\gamma^5=0$. Substitute these into the [derivative](derivative.md) of $j_5^\mu=\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi$. Anticommuting the chirality matrix past $\gamma^\mu$ gives two identical mass terms and two identical interaction terms, proving the displayed identity. These are classical field equations; the formula does not assert an unrenormalized quantum composite-operator identity. With Hermitian coupling $g=i g_P$, the second term is $-2g_P\phi\bar\psi\psi$.
