<h1 id="lubin-tate-extension">Lubin–Tate extension</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lubin–Tate torsion](lubin-tate-torsion.md)

Fix a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\pi$ of a [local field](local-field.md) $K$ and a [Lubin–Tate formal group](lubin-tate-formal-group.md) $\mathcal F$. Its nth torsion field is generated by all its $\pi^n$-torsion points. A primitive point $\lambda_n$ generates the [Lubin–Tate torsion](lubin-tate-torsion.md) as a free rank-one $\mathcal O_K/\pi^n$-[module](module-mathematics.md); therefore $K_{\pi,n}=K(\lambda_n)$. If the residue field has size $q$, the [Eisenstein layers of Lubin–Tate torsion](eisenstein-layers-of-lubin-tate-torsion.md) give $[K_{\pi,n}:K]=(q-1)q^{n-1}$ and total ramification.
