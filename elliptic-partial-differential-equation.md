# Elliptic partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order partial differential equation](second-order-partial-differential-equation.md)

In two independent variables, a scalar second-order [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) with a definite [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md). It has no real characteristic direction. [Laplace's equation](laplace-equation.md) is the basic example.
