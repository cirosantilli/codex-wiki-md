# Osculating-element evolution under isotropic mass loss

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic stellar mass loss](isotropic-stellar-mass-loss.md)

For [isotropic stellar mass loss](isotropic-stellar-mass-loss.md) the [eccentricity vector](eccentricity-vector.md) obeys $\dot{\mathbf e}=-(\dot\mu/\mu)(\mathbf e+\widehat{\mathbf r})$. With [true anomaly](true-anomaly.md) $f$ and $e>0$, this gives $\dot e=-(\dot\mu/\mu)(e+\cos f)$ and $\dot\varpi=-(\dot\mu/\mu)\sin f/e$. The osculating [pericentre](periapsis.md) satisfies $\dot q/q=-(\dot\mu/\mu)(1-\cos f)/(1+e)$, hence cannot decrease during [mass](mass.md) loss and is instantaneously stationary at [pericentre](periapsis.md). The vector form is nonsingular at a circular orbit.
