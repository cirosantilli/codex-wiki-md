# Light-neutrino counting from the Z width

↑ **Parent:** [Massless leptonic Z decay width](massless-leptonic-z-decay-width.md)

Each effectively massless active [neutrino](neutrino.md) species contributes the same invisible [Z boson](z-boson.md) partial [decay width](decay-width.md). Subtracting calculated or measured visible widths from the total width therefore measures the displayed number, assuming no additional invisible channels. The inference concerns particles lighter than $M_Z/2$ with ordinary weak couplings. It does not exclude heavier neutrinos or [right-handed neutrinos](right-handed-neutrino.md) that are [gauge singlets](gauge-singlet.md). Early LEP resonance measurements were consistent with three light active species, with statistical uncertainty. See the [OPAL report on its 1989 data](https://inspirehep.net/files/afa87f449c4b3cb87894b53955b564da) for the original resonance-width comparison.
