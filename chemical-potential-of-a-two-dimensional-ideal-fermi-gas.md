# Chemical potential of a two-dimensional ideal Fermi gas

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional free-electron density of states](two-dimensional-free-electron-density-of-states.md)

For a constant total [density of states](density-of-states.md) $D$ above a zero-energy band bottom, integrating the Fermi-Dirac occupation gives $N=Dk_BT\ln(1+e^{\mu/(k_BT)})$. With $\epsilon_F=N/D$, inversion gives the displayed [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md). It approaches $\epsilon_F$ from below with an exponentially small correction at low [temperature](temperature-split.md), rather than the usual three-dimensional quadratic correction. This formula assumes an ideal parabolic band extending to arbitrarily high [energy](energy-split.md).
