# Energy selectivity for evaporation to increase phase-space density

↑ **Parent:** [Evaporative cooling energy balance](evaporative-cooling-energy-balance.md)

For a classical three-dimensional harmonic trap of fixed [frequencies](frequency.md), $E=3Nk_BT$ and $\mathcal D\propto NT^{-3}$. Combining $dE=\epsilon_{\rm ev}dN$ with these equations gives $d\ln\mathcal D/d\ln N=4-\epsilon_{\rm ev}/(k_BT)$. Since evaporation decreases $N$, [phase-space density of an atomic gas](phase-space-density-of-an-atomic-gas.md) increases only when the removed mean [energy](energy-split.md) exceeds $4k_BT$. Merely lowering [temperature](temperature-split.md) requires only $\epsilon_{\rm ev}>3k_BT$. Changing confinement adds trap work and changes these formulas.
