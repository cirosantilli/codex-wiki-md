# Magnetosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary science](planetary-science-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetosphere)

A magnetosphere is the region around a body in which the body's magnetic interaction controls charged-particle motion and its response to the surrounding plasma. A planet with an intrinsic [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) can support a compressed dayside and an extended magnetotail in the [solar wind](solar-wind.md). Magnetospheric currents must be distinguished from the internal field when inferring a [planetary dynamo](planetary-dynamo.md).
