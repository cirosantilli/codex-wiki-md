# Elliptic stabilizer obstruction to a hyperbolic manifold quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md)

A nontrivial finite-order [elliptic Möbius transformation](elliptic-element-of-psl2-r.md) fixes an axis in [hyperbolic space](hyperbolic-space.md). The quotient metric has cone angle $2\pi/m$ along that axis for a stabilizer of order $m$, so it is locally an [orbifold](orbifold.md) metric rather than a smooth hyperbolic-manifold metric. The underlying topological quotient may nevertheless be a manifold.
