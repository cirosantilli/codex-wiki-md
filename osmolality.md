# Osmolality

↑ **Parent:** [Regulated physiological variable](regulated-physiological-variable.md)

[Osmolality](osmolality.md) counts dissolved particles per mass of [solvent](solvent.md); it characterizes a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md), rather than the total amount of [water](water.md) in an organism.
