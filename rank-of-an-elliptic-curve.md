# Rank of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md)

For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) over a [number field](number-field.md) $K$, the [Mordell-Weil theorem](mordell-weil-group.md) writes $E(K)$ as a finite [torsion subgroup](torsion-subgroup.md) plus a free [abelian group](abelian-group.md) of finite [rank of an abelian group](rank-of-an-abelian-group.md) $r$. This integer is the rank of the elliptic curve over $K$. It can increase under field extension. A [two-isogeny descent](two-isogeny-descent.md) supplies rank bounds from rational square-class images; the [canonical height pairing](canonical-height-pairing.md) can certify independence of rational points.
