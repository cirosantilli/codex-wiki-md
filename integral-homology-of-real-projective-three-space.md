# Integral homology of real projective three-space

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md)

The integral homology groups are

$$
H_i(\mathbb{RP}^3;\mathbb Z)=
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&i=0,3,\\
\mathbb Z/2,&i=1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
