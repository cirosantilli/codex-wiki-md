# Most unstable softened disk wavenumber

↑ **Parent:** [Softened Toomre dispersion relation](softened-toomre-dispersion-relation.md)

With $s=Q c_sk/\kappa$ and $\delta=\kappa\epsilon/(Q c_s)$, maximize $F(s)=2se^{-\delta s}-s^2$. Its unique positive critical point satisfies the displayed equation. For positive [gravitational softening](gravitational-softening.md), $e^{-\delta s_*}<1$ and $1-\delta s_*>0$, giving the strict bound. Unsoftened gravity has $s_*=1$.
