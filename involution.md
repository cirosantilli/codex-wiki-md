# Involution

↑ **Parent:** [Order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Involution)

An involution is a group element $s$ satisfying $s^2=1$. Some conventions require $s\ne1$.
