# Polarization selection of a rotational Stark doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Dipole moment from a rotational Stark doublet](dipole-moment-from-a-rotational-stark-doublet.md)

Radiation polarized along the static field drives the zero-projection change, while transverse [polarization](polarization-waves.md) supplies the two circular components with projection changes plus or minus one. The opposite signs of the excited [magnetic quantum number](magnetic-quantum-number.md) remain degenerate in a pure static [electric field](electric-field-split.md); they need not be separate spectral lines.
