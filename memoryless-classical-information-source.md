# Memoryless classical information source

↑ **Parent:** [Information theory](information-theory-split.md)

A memoryless classical source emits [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) from a fixed finite alphabet. Its block [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) factors as shown, and its information per symbol is the [Shannon entropy](information-entropy.md) $H(U)=-\sum_up(u)\log_2p(u)$. Zero-probability symbols never occur and contribute zero to the entropy. The [typical sequence theorem](typical-sequence-theorem.md) describes the size and probability of the high-probability block set used for reliable compression.
