# Hyperbolic triangle inradius bound

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic triangle](hyperbolic-triangle.md)

For curvature minus one, no [hyperbolic triangle](hyperbolic-triangle.md) contains an open hyperbolic disc of radius greater than $\tfrac12\log3$. Move the disc centre to zero in the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md) and enclose the triangle in an ideal triangle with the same radial vertex directions. One gap between ideal directions is at least $2\pi/3$. Its opposite geodesic is at distance at most $2\operatorname{artanh}(2-\sqrt3)=\tfrac12\log3$ from zero. The symmetric [ideal hyperbolic triangle](ideal-triangle.md) attains the bound as its inradius.
