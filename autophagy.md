# Autophagy

↑ **Parent:** [Lysosome](lysosome.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autophagy)

[Autophagy](autophagy.md) delivers a [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)'s own material for digestion and recycling in [lysosomes](lysosome.md). [Macroautophagy](macroautophagy.md) is the enclosing-carrier route illustrated here; other forms deliver cargo differently. The cargo's internal origin distinguishes this route from uptake by [endocytosis](endocytosis.md).[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Lysosomal Proteolysis, Figure 7.41 and subsequent selective-uptake paragraph: enclosed cargo and alternative uptake](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/protein-degradation.md)
