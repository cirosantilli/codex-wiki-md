# Saddlepoint density of a Laplace sample mean

↑ **Parent:** [Saddlepoint density approximation](saddlepoint-density-approximation.md)

For independent unit-scale [Laplace distributions](laplace-distribution.md), the [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md) is $K(t)=-\log(1-t^2)$ for $|t|<1$. The [saddlepoint density approximation](saddlepoint-density-approximation.md) solves $K'(t)=y$, giving $t=y/(1+\sqrt{1+y^2})$. At this tilt, $K''(t)=D(1+D)$ and $ty=D-1$, giving the displayed [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) approximation for the [sample mean](sample-mean.md). Its value at zero is $\sqrt{n/(4\pi)}$; it need not integrate to one before normalization.
