# Dense monomorphism classifier

↑ **Parent:** [Lawvere-Tierney topology](lawvere-tierney-topology.md)

The pullback of the truth point along a [local operator](lawvere-tierney-topology.md) $j$ is the [subobject](subobject.md) $J\hookrightarrow\Omega$. A [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) with characteristic map $\chi$ is [j-dense](j-dense-monomorphism.md) exactly when $\chi$ factors through $J$. Its internal order is inherited from the [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra.md) of truth values. Having a least global element is an internal condition over all parameter objects, not just a condition on global truth values.
