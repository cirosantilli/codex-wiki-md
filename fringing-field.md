# Fringing field

↑ **Parent:** [Electric field](electric-field-split.md)

The nonuniform [electric field](electric-field-split.md) near the edges of finite electrodes, where [electric field lines](electric-field-line.md) bend away from an ideal uniform or one-dimensional configuration. A [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md) with separation much smaller than its lateral size has nearly uniform interior field, but its edge field contributes a correction to [capacitance](capacitance.md).
