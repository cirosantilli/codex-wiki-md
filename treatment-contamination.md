# Treatment contamination

↑ **Parent:** [Intention-to-treat analysis](intention-to-treat-analysis.md)

Treatment contamination occurs when participants in one arm of a [randomized controlled trial](randomized-controlled-trial.md) receive an intervention assigned to another arm. This is distinct from [contamination](contamination-statistics.md) in robust estimation. The [intention-to-treat analysis](intention-to-treat-analysis.md) still compares randomized assignments, but intervention receipt becomes less separated between the arms, often diluting the assignment effect. Receipt-based comparisons can introduce [confounding](confounding.md) because receipt need not be randomized.
