# Stereochemistry

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereochemistry)

[Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md) studies the spatial arrangement of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) in [molecules](molecule-split.md). A [Fischer projection](fischer-projection.md) records this information at successive [stereocenters](stereocenter.md), whereas a [molecular formula](molecular-formula.md) only counts [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). See [McMurry: representing carbohydrate stereochemistry](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/25-2-representing-carbohydrate-stereochemistry-fischer-projections).
