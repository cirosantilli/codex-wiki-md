# Reflection holonomy along a closed projective geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Holonomy](holonomy.md)

In the round [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md), project the great-circle arc $(\cos t,\sin t,0)$ for $0\le t\le\pi$. Its endpoint and velocity match after antipodal identification, so it is a closed [geodesic](geodesic.md). On the sphere its tangent and the constant vector $(0,0,1)$ are parallel. Endpoint identification fixes the returned tangent and reverses the returned transverse vector. Its [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) is therefore a reflection. Nonorientability permits determinant-minus-one holonomy.
