# Hyperbolicity

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md)

For a one-dimensional first-order system $v_t+A(v,x,t)v_x=s$, hyperbolicity means that the coefficient [matrix](matrix.md) has real [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) and a complete set of [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md). Distinct real eigenvalues give strict hyperbolicity and separate [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md). Along a curve of speed $\lambda$, a left [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) $\ell A=\lambda\ell$ gives the compatibility equation $\ell\,dv/dt=\ell s$.
