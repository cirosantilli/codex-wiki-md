<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose [normal coordinates](../../../../normal-coordinates.md) $\xi^\alpha$ centered at an event $p$. At $p$, the [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) in these coordinates is the constant [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta_{\alpha\beta}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$, with $\xi^0$ measured in length units, and its first [partial derivatives](../../../../partial-derivative.md) vanish. In arbitrary coordinates $x^\mu$, the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) for the [line element](../../../../line-element.md) gives

$$
ds^2\big|_p=\eta_{\alpha\beta}\,d\xi^\alpha d\xi^\beta
=g_{\mu\nu}(p)\,dx^\mu dx^\nu,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{g_{\mu\nu}(p)=\left.
\eta_{\alpha\beta}\frac{\partial\xi^\alpha}{\partial x^\mu}
\frac{\partial\xi^\beta}{\partial x^\nu}\right|_p.}
$$

This construction is pointwise. At nearby events, the [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) in the chosen [normal coordinates](../../../../normal-coordinates.md) is $g^{(\xi)}_{\alpha\beta}(\xi)=\eta_{\alpha\beta}+O(|\xi|^2)$, with the quadratic corrections determined by the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md). Replacing $g^{(\xi)}$ by one constant [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md) throughout an open region would make that region flat, so it cannot describe general curved [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md) exactly.
