<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a contravariant [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) $A^\lambda$, the [covariant derivative](../../../../covariant-derivative.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla_\nu A^\lambda=A^\lambda{}_{;\nu}
=\partial_\nu A^\lambda+\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}A^\mu.}
$$

The [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) is the unique [torsion-free connection](../../../../torsion-free-connection.md) satisfying [metric compatibility](../../../../metric-compatibility.md). Its [Christoffel symbols](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) in a [coordinate basis](../../../../coordinate-basis.md) are

$$
\boxed{\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}
=\frac12g^{\lambda\sigma}
\left(\partial_\mu g_{\sigma\nu}
+\partial_\nu g_{\sigma\mu}-\partial_\sigma g_{\mu\nu}\right),
\qquad\Gamma^\lambda_{\mu\nu}=\Gamma^\lambda_{\nu\mu}.}
$$

There is generally no symmetry interchanging the upper index with a lower one. The [Christoffel symbols](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) themselves are not the components of a [tensor](../../../../tensor.md).

An ordinary [partial derivative](../../../../partial-derivative.md) of the components of a [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) includes the apparent change caused by the varying [coordinate basis](../../../../coordinate-basis.md). The connection term corrects for that change, so $\nabla_\nu A^\lambda$ obeys the [tensor component transformation law](../../../../tensor-component-transformation-law.md). It gives coordinate-independent ways to express [parallel transport](../../../../parallel-transport.md), [geodesics](../../../../geodesic.md) and [stress-energy conservation](../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) in curved [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md).
