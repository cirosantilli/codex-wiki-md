↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../levi-civita-connection.md), the all-lowered [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md) has the algebraic symmetries

$$
\boxed{R_{\lambda\mu\nu\kappa}=-R_{\mu\lambda\nu\kappa}
=-R_{\lambda\mu\kappa\nu},\qquad
R_{\lambda\mu\nu\kappa}=R_{\nu\kappa\lambda\mu}.}
$$

The [first Bianchi identity](../../../../first-bianchi-identity.md) is the cyclic identity

$$
\boxed{R_{\lambda\mu\nu\kappa}+R_{\lambda\nu\kappa\mu}+R_{\lambda\kappa\mu\nu}=0.}
$$

These statements concern the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md) at each point and are unchanged by reversing its overall [curvature sign convention](../../../../curvature-sign-convention.md).
