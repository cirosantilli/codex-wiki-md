<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the [Newtonian gravitational potential](../../../../newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) $\Phi$, the weak static [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) has $g_{00}\simeq-1-2\Phi/c^2$. The [Newtonian limit](../../../../newtonian-limit.md) derived above consequently gives

$$
R_{00}\simeq-\frac1{c^2}\nabla^2\Phi
=\frac12C\rho c^2,
\qquad
\nabla^2\Phi=-\frac12C c^4\rho.
$$

Matching the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](../../../../poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md), $\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho$, fixes

$$
\boxed{C=-\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}.}
$$

Here $G$ is the [gravitational constant](../../../../gravitational-constant.md) and $c$ is the [speed of light](../../../../speed-of-light.md). This negative coupling agrees with the source formula sheet. The familiar positive coupling uses the opposite [curvature sign convention](../../../../curvature-sign-convention.md) for the [Ricci tensor](../../../../ricci-tensor.md); the physical [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](../../../../poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) is the same.
