<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take the [tensor contraction](../../../../tensor-contraction.md) of the [Einstein field equations](../../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md) with the [inverse metric](../../../../inverse-metric.md) $g^{\mu\nu}$. In four-dimensional [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md), $g^{\mu\nu}g_{\mu\nu}=4$, hence

$$
R-\frac12(4)R=CT,\qquad T=g^{\mu\nu}T_{\mu\nu},\qquad R=-CT.
$$

Here $T$ is the [tensor contraction](../../../../tensor-contraction.md) of the [stress-energy tensor](../../../../stress-energy-tensor-split.md). Substituting this result back into the [Einstein field equations](../../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md), while keeping both free indices lowered, gives the [trace-reversed stress-energy tensor](../../../../trace-reversed-stress-energy-tensor.md):

$$
\boxed{R_{\mu\nu}=C\left(T_{\mu\nu}-\frac12g_{\mu\nu}T\right)
=CS_{\mu\nu}.}
$$

The factor $1/2$ uses the dimension four: in dimension $n>2$, the [Trace-reversed Einstein field equations in D dimensions](../../../../trace-reversed-einstein-field-equations-in-d-dimensions.md) have $1/(n-2)$ in place of $1/2$.
