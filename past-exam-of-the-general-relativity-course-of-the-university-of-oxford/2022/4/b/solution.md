<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Earth](../../../../earth.md)-[Moon](../../../../moon.md) pair is a [gravitationally bound system](../../../../gravitationally-bound-system.md), so its separation does not obey $\dot r=H_0r$. The [Hubble flow](../../../../hubble-flow.md) describes the motion of the approximately homogeneous cosmological matter distribution; it is not a compulsory extra velocity assigned to the members of every local orbit. Thus the numerical proximity of the counterfactual rate to $3.8\,\mathrm{cm\,yr^{-1}}$ is not evidence for that explanation.

The observed recession arises principally from [tidal dissipation](../../../../tidal-dissipation.md). The [Earth](../../../../earth.md) rotates faster than the [Moon](../../../../moon.md) orbits, so the displaced tidal response transfers [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) from the [Earth](../../../../earth.md)'s spin into the lunar orbit. For a nearly circular orbit, increasing orbital [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) increases its size. A model of this effect must include the appropriate tidal response, rather than add a universal [Hubble flow](../../../../hubble-flow.md) recession.

A residual [local cosmological tidal acceleration](../../../../local-cosmological-tidal-acceleration.md) can exist, but its background scale is $|(\ddot a/a)r|\sim H_0^2r$, not an imposed first-order secular drift $H_0r$. At $r=4\times10^8\,\mathrm m$,

$$
H_0^2r\sim2\times10^{-27}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}},
\qquad
\frac{G(M_E+M_M)}{r^2}\sim2.5\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}},
$$

using $G(M_E+M_M)\simeq4\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-2}}$. The cosmological scale is about $10^{-24}$ of the mutual [gravitational acceleration](../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md). Its detailed coefficient depends on how the local system is embedded in the cosmological background. **Cosmological expansion can be neglected for the lunar recession calculation at the stated precision**; this conclusion follows from binding and the tiny residual [local cosmological tidal acceleration](../../../../local-cosmological-tidal-acceleration.md), rather than from comparing two recession speeds.
