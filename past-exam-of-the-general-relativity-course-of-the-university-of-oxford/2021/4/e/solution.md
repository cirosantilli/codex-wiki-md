<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The actual large-scale expansion is well described by the [Lambda-CDM model](../../../../lambda-cdm-model.md): [radiation in cosmology](../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md), ordinary baryonic [matter](../../../../matter.md), [cold dark matter](../../../../cold-dark-matter.md) and late-time [dark energy](../../../../dark-energy.md) coexist. A component becomes dominant through continuous changes in their density ratios, rather than every previous component disappearing at an instantaneous phase boundary. Matter [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) is small in its nonrelativistic regime, but [radiation](../../../../radiation.md) persists, and massive [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) interpolate between relativistic and nonrelativistic behavior.

There is also a late accelerated era, absent from a model ending permanently in S3. It is consistent with nearly constant [vacuum energy](../../../../vacuum-energy.md), whose negative [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) eventually dominates because [pressureless matter](../../../../pressureless-matter.md) dilutes as $R^{-3}$. The [scale factor](../../../../scale-factor-cosmology.md) then ceases to follow the pure $t^{2/3}$ [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md) law.

An early phase of [cosmic inflation](../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) is a well-motivated model rather than a directly observed exact $w=-1$ phase. Successful [cosmic inflation](../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) must end and transfer its [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) to matter and [radiation](../../../../radiation.md) through [reheating](../../../../reheating.md); realistic models generally have slowly varying $H$ and $w$ close to, rather than exactly equal to, $-1$. Extrapolating a strictly constant $H$ to arbitrarily small $R$ is not a measurement of the universe's past duration. Finally, [spatial homogeneity](../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md) and [cosmological isotropy](../../../../cosmological-isotropy.md) apply statistically on large scales: galaxies, [gravitational waves](../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) and other perturbations are absent from the exact [FLRW metric](../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), and small spatial [curvature](../../../../curvature.md) remains an observational possibility.
