<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the [equation of state](../../../../equation-of-state.md) as $P=w\rho c^2$. Integrating the [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) for constant $w$ gives $\rho\propto R^{-3(1+w)}$. With spatial flatness and the geometric [cosmological constant](../../../../cosmological-constant.md) set to zero, the [Friedmann equation](../../../../friedmann-equations.md) is $H^2=8\pi G\rho/3$.

In S1, $w=-1$ describes [vacuum energy](../../../../vacuum-energy.md): $T_{\mu\nu}=-\rho c^2g_{\mu\nu}$ and $\rho$ is constant. Its negative [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) drives accelerated expansion, $\ddot R/R=8\pi G\rho/3=H_1^2$, and the expanding solution is $R\propto e^{H_1t}$. This is the ideal [de Sitter spacetime](../../../../de-sitter-spacetime.md) approximation to [cosmic inflation](../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md). An exactly constant vacuum component cannot end its phase unaided; a phase transition or [reheating](../../../../reheating.md) mechanism is required for the assumed change to S2.

In S2, $w=1/3$ describes an isotropic bath of ultrarelativistic [particles](../../../../particle-physics.md), such as [photons](../../../../photon.md), with negligible [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) relative to their [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). The number per physical [volume](../../../../volume.md) falls as $R^{-3}$, while the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) per particle redshifts as $R^{-1}$, so $\rho\propto R^{-4}$. This [radiation-dominated universe](../../../../radiation-dominated-universe.md) has $R\propto(t-t_*)^{1/2}$ on its expanding branch.

In S3, $w=0$ describes [pressureless matter](../../../../pressureless-matter.md), or [relativistic dust](../../../../relativistic-dust.md), with negligible random [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md). Conserved particle number and approximately fixed [rest energy](../../../../rest-energy.md) give $\rho\propto R^{-3}$. A flat universe containing only this component is the [Einstein-de Sitter universe](../../../../einstein-de-sitter-universe.md), with $R\propto(t-t_*)^{2/3}$. The constants $t_*$ are integration offsets in the individual phases; they need not represent one common start time when phases are matched.
