↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**No.** A motionless isolated [point mass](../../../../../point-mass.md) produces a static [gravitational field](../../../../../gravitational-field.md). All its [mass multipole moments](../../../../../mass-multipole-moment.md) are time independent, so there is no outgoing [gravitational radiation](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md). In the [quadrupole formula](../../../../../quadrupole-formula.md), $h_{ij}^{\rm TT}\propto d^2Q_{ij}^{\rm TT}/dt^2=0$.
