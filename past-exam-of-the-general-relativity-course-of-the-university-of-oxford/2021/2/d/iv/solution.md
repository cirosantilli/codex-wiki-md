[Gravitational waves](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) also exhibit [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md): the [geometrical optics](../../../../../geometrical-optics-split.md) ray description fails when a propagation structure or lens delay is not large compared with the wave's [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) or period. For a compact lens of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $M_L$, a useful [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) parameter is $w=4GM_L\omega_{\rm gw}/c^3$; $w$ of order one signals potentially appreciable wave effects, with cosmological lens redshift supplying an additional factor $1+z_L$ when needed. Astrophysical [gravitational lensing](../../../../../gravitational-lensing-split.md) can therefore produce [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) and frequency-dependent amplification. Ordinary objects are poor opaque screens for [gravitational waves](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md), so everyday slit-and-screen demonstrations are implausible despite the large [wavelengths](../../../../../wavelength.md), which are about $3000$ km at $100$ Hz.
