↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Gravitational waves](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) can exhibit [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md). In [linearized gravity](../../../../../linearized-gravity-split.md), overlapping strains add, so coherent contributions can enhance or cancel the measured response. Independent astrophysical sources generally have unrelated phases and average to a stochastic background rather than stable fringes. More plausible coherent paths arise when [gravitational lensing](../../../../../gravitational-lensing-split.md) sends different parts of the same signal to the detector with different delays; fringes require sufficient overlap and coherence. The relevant quadratic radiative [energy flux](../../../../../energy-flux.md) contains the cross terms even though the leading wave equation is linear.
