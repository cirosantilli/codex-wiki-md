[Gravitational radiation](../../../../../gravitational-wave-split.md) admits [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md): [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md) has two transverse tensor modes, [plus polarization](../../../../../plus-polarization.md) and [cross polarization](../../../../../cross-polarization.md), whose relative amplitudes and phase give linear, elliptical or [circular gravitational-wave polarization](../../../../../circular-gravitational-wave-polarization.md). Their axes transform through twice a rotation of the transverse coordinate frame, unlike the vector [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) of [electromagnetic radiation](../../../../../electromagnetic-radiation-split.md). Measuring these modes is physically plausible with several differently oriented detectors; a single detector generally measures only one projected combination.
