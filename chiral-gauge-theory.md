# Chiral gauge theory

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge theory](gauge-theory.md)

A [gauge theory](gauge-theory.md) whose left- and right-chiral [fermions](fermion.md) transform differently under its [gauge group](gauge-group.md). The [weak charged current](charged-current.md) gives a physical example. A chiral representation assignment must satisfy the relevant [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md) cancellation conditions to define a consistent quantum theory.
