# Square-root trick for positively associated events

↑ **Parent:** [Harris-FKG inequality](harris-fkg-inequality.md)

If [increasing events](increasing-event.md) $A_1,\ldots,A_k$ have a common probability, applying the [Harris-FKG inequality](harris-fkg-inequality.md) to their decreasing complements gives $\mathbb P(A_1)\geq1-(1-\mathbb P(\bigcup_iA_i))^{1/k}$.
