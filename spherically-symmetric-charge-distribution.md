# Spherically symmetric charge distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

A [charge distribution](charge-distribution.md) invariant under [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) about one center. For its [spherically symmetric potential](spherically-symmetric-potential.md), [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) gives the radial [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $\mathbf E=Q(r)\mathbf e_r/(4\pi\epsilon_0r^2)$, where $Q(r)$ is the enclosed [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md).
