# Cholesterol

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid](lipid.md)

[Cholesterol](cholesterol.md) is a [lipid](lipid.md) with four fused rings and a small [hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md) region. It helps regulate the fluidity of animal [cell membranes](cell-membrane-split.md); it is structurally distinct from a [phospholipid](phospholipid.md). See [Biology 2e: cholesterol](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-3-lipids).
