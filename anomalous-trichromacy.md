# Anomalous trichromacy

↑ **Parent:** [Trichromacy](trichromacy.md)

[Color vision](color-vision.md) with three independent receptor classes whose [spectral sensitivities](spectral-sensitivity.md) differ from a reference trichromat's, often reducing separation of long- and middle-wave signals. A shifted sensitivity, fewer receptors and loss of an entire independent class are different mechanisms and should not be conflated.
