# Chirp requirement for longitudinal bunch compression

↑ **Parent:** [Beam optics](beam-optics.md)

A magnetic chicane's momentum-dependent [path length](arc-length.md) does not specify a compression factor from initial length and marginal [energy](energy-split.md) spread alone. A suitable longitudinal position-energy correlation is required. With zero correlation the [variance](variance-split.md) above is minimized at $R_{56}=0$, so such a chicane cannot shorten that ideal uncorrelated bunch. An imposed chirp and its uncorrelated residual determine the achievable minimum.
