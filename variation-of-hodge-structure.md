# Variation of Hodge structure

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge structure](hodge-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variation_of_Hodge_structure)

A pure integral variation consists of a [local system](local-system.md) of free abelian groups, holomorphic filtration subbundles of its associated flat bundle, and fibrewise [Hodge structures](hodge-structure.md) of fixed weight and Hodge numbers. Its flat connection satisfies [Griffiths transversality](griffiths-transversality.md). A polarized variation additionally has a flat integral bilinear form polarizing every fibre.
