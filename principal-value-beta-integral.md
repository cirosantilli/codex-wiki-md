# Principal-value beta integral

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md)

For $0<s<a$,

$$
\operatorname{PV}\int_0^\infty\frac{x^{s-1}}{1-x^a}dx=\frac\pi a\cot\frac{\pi s}{a}.
$$
