# Hadamard squared-distance inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Hadamard space](hadamard-space.md)

For the constant-speed [geodesic](geodesic.md) $\gamma$ from $x$ to $y$ in a [Hadamard space](hadamard-space.md), the displayed inequality follows by repeatedly applying the midpoint inequality and passing from dyadic parameters to all $t\in[0,1]$. Applying it on two sides of a triangle gives $d(x_s,y_t)^2\leq s^2A^2+t^2B^2-st(A^2+B^2-C^2)$, where $A=d(a,x)$, $B=d(a,y)$ and $C=d(x,y)$. The right side is the squared comparison distance by the Euclidean cosine rule.
