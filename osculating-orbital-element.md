# Osculating orbital element

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital element](orbital-element.md)

An [orbital element](orbital-element.md) of the [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) having the same instantaneous position and [velocity](velocity.md) as a perturbed trajectory. Its elements vary as the perturbing [force](force.md) acts. Their interpretation depends on the central [gravitational parameter](standard-gravitational-parameter.md) used, for example whether static [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) is included in the unperturbed attraction.
