# Gaussian Gram matrix concentration on a fixed subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian empirical Gram matrix](gaussian-empirical-gram-matrix.md)

For $G$ with $n$ [independent](independent-random-variables.md) standard Gaussian rows in dimension $k$, combine a constant-radius sphere [metric net](metric-net.md), the [quadratic form net bound](quadratic-form-net-bound.md), the [chi-squared concentration inequality](chi-squared-concentration-inequality.md), and a [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md). This gives $P(\|G^TG/n-I_k\|_{\mathrm{op}}>1/2)\leq2\exp(k\log B-n/1024)$ for a numerical $B$. Taking $n$ larger than a constant times $k\log p$ gives exponential decay in $k\log p$. The subspace is fixed, so no union over supports is needed.
