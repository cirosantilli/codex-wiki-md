# Green-Kubo formula for diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md)

For a stationary zero-[mean](expected-value-split.md) [velocity](velocity.md) with an absolutely integrable [velocity autocorrelation function](velocity-autocorrelation-function.md) $\chi$, the [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) is its time [integral](integral-split.md). Indeed, [differentiation](differentiation.md) of the [mean squared displacement](mean-squared-displacement.md) identity gives $\tfrac12\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb E[(X(t)-X(0))^2]=\int_0^t\chi(u)\,du$, and the right side tends to the displayed [integral](integral-split.md). In the equilibrium [generalized Langevin equation](generalized-langevin-equation.md), this gives $D=\Theta/\widetilde\kappa(0)$ whenever stable relaxation and the required [integrability](integrability.md) hold. The formula is named after [Melville S. Green](melville-s-green.md) and [Ryogo Kubo](ryogo-kubo.md).
