# Square-root stock claim

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent claim](contingent-claim.md)

The payout $\sqrt{S_T}$ is a concave stock payoff. Its conditional expected value has a discount-like dependence on future variance. The [square-root stock supermartingale](square-root-stock-supermartingale.md) explains the negative variance drift.
