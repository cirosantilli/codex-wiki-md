# Cartesian vortices

↑ **Parent:** [History of physics](history-of-physics.md)

A material circulation used in [René Descartes](rene-descartes.md)'s natural philosophy to account for celestial motions. [Isaac Newton](isaac-newton.md)'s criticism concerned the compatibility of such circulation with observed orbital periods and cometary paths, not the impossibility of every modern fluid [vortex](phase-vortex.md).[opening discussion of the vortex hypothesis](general-scholium-by-isaac-newton.md)
