# Gradient in cylindrical coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical coordinate system](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md)

For a differentiable [scalar field](scalar-field.md) and [cylindrical coordinates](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) with $r>0$, the [gradient](gradient.md) has the displayed components in the local [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) applied to $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ and a local angle $\theta$ gives $r_x=\cos\theta$, $r_y=\sin\theta$, $\theta_x=-\sin\theta/r$, and $\theta_y=\cos\theta/r$. Substitution in $\nabla\phi=\phi_x\mathbf e_x+\phi_y\mathbf e_y+\phi_z\mathbf e_z$ proves the formula. The angular factor $1/r$ appears because an angular change $d\theta$ sweeps [arc length](arc-length.md) $r\,d\theta$.
