# Prey

↑ **Parent:** [Predation](predation.md)

[Prey](prey.md) is an [organism](organism.md) consumed by a [predator](predator.md) in [predation](predation.md). The name describes the receiving end of that interaction: a small [fish](fish.md) can be [prey](prey.md) for a large [fish](fish.md) while being a [predator](predator.md) of [zooplankton](zooplankton.md). In a [food web](food-web.md) drawn with resource-to-[consumer](consumer-food-chain.md) arrows, a feeding arrow goes outward from the [prey](prey.md) toward its [predator](predator.md). This convention makes the route of food and [energy](energy.md) easy to follow.
