# Fock vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Fock state](fock-state.md)

The oscillator vacuum is annihilated by all [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md). Acting with [creation operators](creation-operator.md) constructs [Fock states](fock-state.md). In single-string quantization this is the unexcited string, not the empty spacetime state.
