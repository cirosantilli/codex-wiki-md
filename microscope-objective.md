# Microscope objective

↑ **Parent:** [Optical microscope](optical-microscope.md)

The specimen-facing [optical lens](lens-optics.md) assembly of an [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md). A [microscope objective](microscope-objective.md) collects [light](light.md) from the specimen and sets the object-side [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md). In a [finite-conjugate microscope](finite-conjugate-microscope.md) it forms the [intermediate microscope image](intermediate-microscope-image.md) directly; in an [infinity-corrected microscope](infinity-corrected-microscope.md) a [tube lens](tube-lens.md) completes that [image formation](image-formation.md). Its label alone does not calibrate a specimen [length](length.md).
