# Folded site frequency spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)

The [folded site frequency spectrum](folded-site-frequency-spectrum.md) counts variable sites by the less frequent allele count, without identifying which allele is ancestral. With four sampled sequences, counts one and three both contribute to the $3:1$ class, whereas count two gives the $2:2$ class.
