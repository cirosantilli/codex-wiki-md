# Nondegeneracy of a bimatrix game

↑ **Parent:** [Bimatrix game](bimatrix-game.md)

A [bimatrix game](bimatrix-game.md) is nondegenerate if every [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) with $k$ positive coordinates has at most $k$ pure [best responses](best-response.md) for the other player. This condition prevents ties in the leaving-variable choice along the usual [Lemke-Howson algorithm](lemke-howson-algorithm.md) path and allows unique continuation once the dropped label has been selected.
