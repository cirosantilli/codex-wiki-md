# Continuity of distributional differentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md)

If $u_j\to u$ in [weak convergence of distributions](weak-convergence-of-distributions.md), then every fixed [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) converges too. For each [test function](test-function.md) $\phi$, its derivative $D^\alpha\phi$ is another [test function](test-function.md), and

$$
\langle D^\alpha u_j,\phi\rangle=(-1)^{|\alpha|}\langle u_j,D^\alpha\phi\rangle
\longrightarrow(-1)^{|\alpha|}\langle u,D^\alpha\phi\rangle.
$$

Thus a series converging as [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) can be differentiated term by term in that topology. For example, [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) on [compact sets](compact-space.md) implies convergence of the associated [regular distributions](regular-distribution.md), even when the ordinary series of [derivatives](derivative.md) does not converge pointwise.
