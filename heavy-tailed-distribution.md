# Heavy-tailed distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy-tailed_distribution)

In the right-tail convention, every positive exponential [moment](moment.md) is infinite. This is a tail property, not the assertion that the [variance](variance-split.md) or all power moments must be infinite. A [heavy-tailed subexponential distribution](subexponential-distribution-heavy-tailed.md) has the additional property that a sum's far tail is dominated by one large summand.
