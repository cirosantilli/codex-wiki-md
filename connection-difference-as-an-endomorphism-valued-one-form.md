# Connection difference as an endomorphism-valued one-form

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

The [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) terms of two connections cancel, making their difference $C^\infty$-linear in the section. It is therefore an element of $A^1(\operatorname{End}E)$. Conversely adding any such form to a connection gives another connection, making the space of connections an [affine space](affine-space.md).
