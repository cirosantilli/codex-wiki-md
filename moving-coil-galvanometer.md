# Moving-coil galvanometer

↑ **Parent:** [Galvanometer](galvanometer.md)

A [moving-coil galvanometer](moving-coil-galvanometer.md) has a winding that rotates about an axis, a restoring spring, and a pointer or optical readout. In the radial-field construction, a cylindrical iron core and shaped magnetic poles keep the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) approximately in the coil's plane as it turns. The resulting [moving-coil galvanometer torque](moving-coil-galvanometer-torque.md) is nearly independent of the deflection [angle](angle.md); the spring then converts [electric current](electric-current.md) into a proportional settled deflection. [Physics Part I, Class XII, §4.10, printed pp. 129–130, Figure 4.20 and equation (4.26): radial field, iron core, spring and pointer](physics-part-i-class-xii/moving-charges-and-magnetism.md)
