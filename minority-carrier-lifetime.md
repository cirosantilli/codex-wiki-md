# Minority carrier lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-hole recombination](electron-hole-recombination.md)

In a weak-injection regime where the majority population is nearly fixed, a small excess minority [charge carrier](charge-carrier.md) population often relaxes exponentially with time constant $\tau$. This lifetime describes carrier removal by recombination. It differs from the [momentum relaxation time](momentum-relaxation-time.md) governing [charge carrier mobility](charge-carrier-mobility.md): many momentum-scattering events can occur before a carrier recombines. Traps and spatial transport can make a single exponential inadequate.
