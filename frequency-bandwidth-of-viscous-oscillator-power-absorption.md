# Frequency bandwidth of viscous oscillator power absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Forced harmonic oscillator](forced-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For a [forced harmonic oscillator](forced-harmonic-oscillator.md) with constant viscous damping coefficient $m\gamma>0$, the [average power](average-power.md) divided by its maximum is $\gamma^2\omega^2/[(\omega_0^2-\omega^2)^2+\gamma^2\omega^2]$. It peaks at $\omega_0=\sqrt{k/m}$. Its two positive half-power [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) are $\omega_{1,2}=(\sqrt{4\omega_0^2+\gamma^2}\mp\gamma)/2$. Hence their separation is exactly $\gamma$, and their product is $\omega_0^2$. The narrow-resonance approximation $\omega_{1,2}\simeq\omega_0\mp\gamma/2$ is needed for symmetry about the peak, not for the exact bandwidth in angular-frequency units.
