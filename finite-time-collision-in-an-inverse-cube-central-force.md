# Finite-time collision in an inverse-cube central force

↑ **Parent:** [Central force](central-force.md)

For [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $\Phi(r)=-mk/r^2$, the inward [central force](central-force.md) has magnitude $2mk/r^3$. With conserved specific [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $h=r^2\dot\theta$, the radial equation is $\ddot r=(h^2-2k)/r^3$. At the critical value $h^2=2k$, this reduces to $\ddot r=0$. An inward initial radial [velocity](velocity.md) $-v$, with $v>0$ and $r(0)=a$, therefore gives $r=a-vt$ until collision at $t_*=a/v$.

For $h>0$, integration of $\dot\theta=h/(a-vt)^2$ gives $\theta-\theta(0)=(h/v)(1/r-1/a)$. Thus the [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) is a [hyperbolic spiral](hyperbolic-spiral.md) and the [particle](particle-physics.md) completes infinitely many turns before the finite collision time. The tangential [speed](speed.md) $h/r$ diverges. The [potential energy](potential-energy.md) and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) individually diverge with opposite signs, while their sum remains the finite conserved [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) $mv^2/2$.
