# Solenoid turn density

↑ **Parent:** [Solenoid](solenoid.md)

The [solenoid turn density](solenoid-turn-density.md) $n$ counts turns per unit axial length in a uniformly wound [solenoid](solenoid.md). The total turn count $N$ is dimensionless, whereas $n$ has units $\mathrm{m^{-1}}$. A $1000$-turn coil of length $0.50\,\mathrm m$ has $n=2000\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}$; an axial interval of length $s=0.010\,\mathrm m$ then contains $ns=20$ turns. The effective circumferential [surface current density](surface-current-density.md) in the closely spaced circular-turn model is $K=nI$, with units $\mathrm{A/m}$. [MIT 8.02 course notes, §9.4, equations 9.4.3–9.4.4: turns per length and surface current](https://web.mit.edu/8.02t/www/test/materials/StudyGuide/guide09_revised.pdf).

For a single-layer [helix](helix.md), the axial advance per turn is approximately $1/n$. The circular-turn model neglects this advance and the associated axial component of [electric current](electric-current.md), as well as the detailed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) close to individual wires. The number of turns around the whole coil is always $N=n\ell$ here; it must not be substituted for $n$ in $B=\mu_0nI$.
