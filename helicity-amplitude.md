# Helicity amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md)

A [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) evaluated with definite external [helicities](helicity.md). Rotational covariance relates its angular dependence to a [Wigner D-matrix](wigner-d-matrix.md). Summing final [helicities](helicity.md) and averaging an unpolarised initial mixture yields a spin-averaged squared [matrix element](matrix-element.md).
