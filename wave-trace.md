# Wave trace

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral geometry](spectral-geometry.md)

For the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) $P$ on a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md), the [wave trace](wave-trace.md) is the [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) $\sum_j e^{it\sqrt{\lambda_j}}$, counting [multiplicities](multiplicity-mathematics.md). The series is interpreted after testing against smooth compactly supported functions, rather than as an ordinarily convergent sum. Its singularities relate the spectrum to the [length spectrum](length-spectrum.md) of [closed geodesics](closed-geodesic.md), through the [Duistermaat-Guillemin trace formula](duistermaat-guillemin-trace-formula.md).
