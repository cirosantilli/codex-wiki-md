# Translation subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean isometry](euclidean-isometry.md)

The translation subgroup of a group of [Euclidean isometries](euclidean-isometry.md) consists of its [translations](translation-geometry.md). It is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md), because conjugation by $x\mapsto Ax+b$ takes translation by $v$ to translation by $Av$. Its vectors form an additive subgroup of Euclidean space; for a [plane crystallographic group](wallpaper-group.md) they form a rank-two [translation lattice](translation-lattice.md).
