# Completely split prime

↑ **Parent:** [Splitting of prime ideals in Galois extensions](splitting-of-prime-ideals-in-galois-extensions.md)

A [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) of a [number field](number-field.md) splits completely in a finite extension of degree $n$ when it factors into $n$ distinct [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md), each of [residue-field degree](residue-field-degree.md) one. In a [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md), an unramified [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) splits completely exactly when its [Frobenius conjugacy class](frobenius-conjugacy-class.md) is the identity class. The [Chebotarev density theorem](chebotarev-density-theorem.md) then gives these primes [Dirichlet density](dirichlet-density.md) $1/n$.
