# Natural kind

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of science](philosophy-of-science.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_kind)

A classification whose usefulness in prediction and explanation is grounded in relevant natural structure, rather than solely in a convenient label. Exact membership conditions can help identify a [natural kind](natural-kind.md) but do not alone demonstrate its explanatory significance.
