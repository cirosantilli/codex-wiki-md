<h1 id="godel-numbering">Gödel numbering</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [First-order theory](first-order-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel_numbering)

A Gödel numbering assigns [natural numbers](natural-number.md) effectively to symbols, formulas, and finite proofs. Syntactic predicates such as “$p$ codes a proof in $T$ of the formula with code $q$” can then be represented by arithmetical formulas.
