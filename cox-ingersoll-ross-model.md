<h1 id="cox-ingersoll-ross-model">Cox–Ingersoll–Ross model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Short rate](short-rate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cox–Ingersoll–Ross_model)

A nonnegative mean-reverting short-rate diffusion with positive parameters. Its mean-reversion level is $a/b$ and its diffusion size is proportional to $\sqrt r$. The [Feller positivity condition for the CIR model](feller-positivity-condition-for-the-cir-model.md) controls whether zero is reachable. Its stationary law is a [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) and its [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) prices are exponential-affine.
