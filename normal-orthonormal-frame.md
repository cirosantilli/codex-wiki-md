# Normal orthonormal frame

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian orthonormal frame](riemannian-orthonormal-frame.md)

At any point of a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), choose [geodesic normal coordinates](geodesic-normal-coordinates.md) and orthonormalize their coordinate fields smoothly. At the point, the metric is the identity and its first derivatives vanish, so the orthonormalization coefficients have zero first derivatives. The [Christoffel symbols](christoffel-symbol.md) also vanish there. This gives a local [Riemannian orthonormal frame](riemannian-orthonormal-frame.md) with the displayed property, useful for invariant identities evaluated at one point.
