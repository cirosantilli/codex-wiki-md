# Disrupted magnetic braking model

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic braking of a binary star](magnetic-braking-of-a-binary-star.md)

In this model, strong [magnetic braking of a binary star](magnetic-braking-of-a-binary-star.md) inflates the mass-losing donor above the [cataclysmic-variable period gap](cataclysmic-variable-period-gap.md). A reduction in the torque near the [fully convective star](fully-convective-star.md) transition allows thermal contraction and detachment. [Gravitational-wave emission from a binary system](gravitational-wave-emission-from-a-binary-system.md) continues to shrink the orbit until contact returns below the gap. The model requires a braking decrease and a previously inflated donor, not disappearance of stellar magnetism. [Knigge's review](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.4716) describes this evolutionary picture and observational tests.
