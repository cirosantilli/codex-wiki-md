# Typical-set cardinality bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Typical set](typical-set.md)

For a [typical set](typical-set.md) of probability at least $1-\delta$,

$$
(1-\delta)2^{n(H-\varepsilon)}\leq|T_\varepsilon^{(n)}|\leq2^{n(H+\varepsilon)}.
$$

The upper bound sums the lower probability bound for each word; the lower bound sums the upper probability bound and uses the set's total probability. These bounds turn the [asymptotic equipartition property](asymptotic-equipartition-property.md) into exponential estimates of source-coding dimension.
