# Potential of a uniform spherical source

↑ **Parent:** [Origin-regular spherical Poisson flux law](origin-regular-spherical-poisson-flux-law.md)

For a uniform spherical source of radius $a$, the origin-regular radial [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) gives $\nabla f=\rho_0x/3$ inside and $\nabla f=\rho_0a^3x/(3|x|^3)$ outside. Integrating radially, imposing $f\to0$ at infinity and continuity at $|x|=a$, gives

$$
f(x)=\begin{cases}\rho_0(|x|^2-3a^2)/6,&|x|\le a,\\-\rho_0a^3/(3|x|),&|x|\ge a.\end{cases}
$$

The potential and its normal derivative are continuous at the interface, so its distributional [Laplacian](laplacian.md) has no surface delta contribution. The classical equation holds off the interface and the source equation holds almost everywhere and distributionally.
