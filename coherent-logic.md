# Coherent logic

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

Coherent logic uses atomic formulas and equality, finite [logical conjunctions](logical-conjunction.md) and [logical disjunctions](logical-disjunction.md), truth, falsity and [existential quantification](existential-quantification.md). Sequents are universally interpreted in a finite variable context. Its categorical semantics is provided by [coherent categories](coherent-category.md), where conjunction is intersection, disjunction is finite union and existential quantification is image.
