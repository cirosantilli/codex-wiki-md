# Cortisol

↑ **Parent:** [Steroid hormone](steroid-hormone.md)

A steroid hormone involved in physiological regulation and [psychological stress](psychological-stress.md) responses. Interpretation depends on sampling time, baseline and the challenge being studied; a lower concentration is not automatically a measure of better psychological adaptation.
