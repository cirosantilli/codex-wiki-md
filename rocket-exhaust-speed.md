# Rocket exhaust speed

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

The [rocket exhaust speed](rocket-exhaust-speed.md) is the magnitude of the emitted material's [velocity](velocity.md) relative to the instantaneous rocket. In the ideal rearward-ejection convention of the [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md), a rocket with inertial [velocity](velocity.md) $v$ emits material with inertial [velocity](velocity.md) $v-u$ to first order. A constant $u$ does not make $v-u$ constant.
