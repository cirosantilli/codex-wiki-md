# Case-insensitive comparison

↑ **Parent:** [String](string.md)

A comparison of [strings](string.md) that disregards a chosen distinction between uppercase and lowercase characters. A simple convention compares characterwise lowercase forms. Full Unicode case folding is a distinct normalization operation and can change character counts.
