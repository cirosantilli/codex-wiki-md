# Direct CP asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [CP violation](cp-violation.md)

A [direct CP asymmetry](direct-cp-asymmetry.md) compares [decay widths](decay-width.md) for a process and its [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md) conjugate. Let two [decay amplitudes](decay-amplitude.md) contribute with real nonnegative magnitudes $a_1,a_2$, [absorptive phases](absorptive-phase-of-a-decay-amplitude.md) $\delta_i$ and [weak phases](weak-phase-cp-violation.md) $\phi_i$. Then $\mathcal A=a_1e^{i(\delta_1+\phi_1)}+a_2e^{i(\delta_2+\phi_2)}$ and $\overline{\mathcal A}=a_1e^{i(\delta_1-\phi_1)}+a_2e^{i(\delta_2-\phi_2)}$. Expanding the absolute squares gives

$$
|\mathcal A|^2-|\overline{\mathcal A}|^2=-4a_1a_2\sin(\delta_1-\delta_2)\sin(\phi_1-\phi_2).
$$

Thus two nonzero contributions and both kinds of relative phase are required in this example. Equal total [decay widths](decay-width.md) under [CPT symmetry](cpt-symmetry.md) constrain the sum over channels and do not forbid individual channel asymmetries.
