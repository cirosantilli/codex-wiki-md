# Achromatic doublet power balance

↑ **Parent:** [Achromatic lens](achromatic-lens.md)

For two thin lenses in contact, achromatism requires $\Phi_1/V_1+\Phi_2/V_2=0$, where $V_i$ are their [Abbe numbers](abbe-number.md). With total power $\Phi=1/f$, the component powers are $\Phi_1=\Phi V_1/(V_1-V_2)$ and $\Phi_2=-\Phi V_2/(V_1-V_2)$. A positive doublet using a larger-$V$ [crown glass](crown-glass-optics.md) element and smaller-$V$ [flint glass](flint-glass.md) element therefore uses a converging crown and a diverging flint.
