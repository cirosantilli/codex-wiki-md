# Maser history

↑ **Parent:** [Maser](maser-split.md)

The 1955 paper by [James Power Gordon](james-power-gordon.md), [Herbert Jack Zeiger](herbert-jack-zeiger.md) and [Charles Hard Townes](charles-hard-townes.md) acknowledged [Nikolay Basov](nicolay-gennadiyevich-basov.md) and [Aleksandr Prokhorov](aleksandr-mikhailovich-prokhorov.md)'s independent proposal.[p. 1264, Introduction and reference 2](the-maser-new-type-of-microwave-amplifier-frequency-standard-and-spectrometer-by-james-power-gordon-and-others.md) The latter authors' theory paper states that they presented the molecular-generator possibility in May 1952, cites their 1954 publication, and separately cites the Gordon–Zeiger–Townes experimental realization. These are contributions from independent groups.[p. 426, historical footnote and references 3–4; manuscript submitted December 10, 1954, English publication October 1956](theory-of-the-molecular-generator-and-molecular-power-amplifier-by-nicolay-gennadiyevich-basov-and-aleksandr-mikhailovich-prokhorov.md)
