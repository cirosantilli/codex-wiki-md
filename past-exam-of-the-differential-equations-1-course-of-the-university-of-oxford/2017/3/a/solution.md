<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The principal coefficients are $A=-xy$, $B=0$, $C=1$, in the convention $Au_{xx}+2Bu_{xy}+Cu_{yy}$. Their [discriminant](../../../../discriminant.md) is **$B^2-AC=xy$**. Thus it is a [hyperbolic partial differential equation](../../../../hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) where $xy>0$ and an [elliptic partial differential equation](../../../../elliptic-partial-differential-equation.md) where $xy<0$. Its principal part has [parabolic type](../../../../parabolic-partial-differential-equation.md) where $xy=0$; however the full equation contains $u_y/y$ and is undefined on $y=0$. On its actual domain $y\ne0$, the parabolic locus is $x=0$.

The [characteristic equation of a partial differential equation](../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{A(dy)^2-2B\,dx\,dy+C(dx)^2=0,\qquad (dx)^2-xy(dy)^2=0.}
$$

In a hyperbolic region this gives two distinct real [characteristic directions](../../../../characteristic-direction.md). The singular lower-order coefficient should not be mistaken for an ordinary parabolic point of the full equation on $y=0$.
