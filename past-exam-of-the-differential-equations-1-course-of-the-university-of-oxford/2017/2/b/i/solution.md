<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Where $y\ne-1$, **$y'=-\sqrt{|1+y|}<0$**. By the [mean value theorem](../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md), the solution is strictly decreasing on every interval where it avoids $-1$. At $y=-1$, the derivative is zero, so a constant segment there is possible. Thus all solutions are nonincreasing, while strict decrease need not persist through an interval spent at that equilibrium.
