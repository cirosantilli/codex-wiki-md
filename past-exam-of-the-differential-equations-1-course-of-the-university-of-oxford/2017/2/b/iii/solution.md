<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Two solutions for $x\geq2$ are

$$
\boxed{y_1(x)=-1,\qquad y_2(x)=-1-\frac{(x-2)^2}{4}.}
$$

For the second, $y_2'=-(x-2)/2=-\sqrt{|1+y_2|}$ because $x\geq2$; both have value $-1$ and derivative zero at two. They are distinct for every $x>2$. Local [Lipschitz continuity](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) fails at the equilibrium, so the existence of these two continuations is compatible with the [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](../../../../../picard-lindelof-theorem.md).
