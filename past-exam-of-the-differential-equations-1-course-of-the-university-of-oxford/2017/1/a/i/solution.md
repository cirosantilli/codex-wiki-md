The [autonomous planar system](../../../../../autonomous-planar-system.md) has two real state variables, so its [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md) is a subset of $\mathbb R^2$. It is autonomous because its [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) $(X(x,y),Y(x,y))$ has no explicit time dependence. The [nullclines](../../../../../nullcline.md) are $X(x,y)=0$ and $Y(x,y)=0$, on which the respective coordinate's instantaneous rate of change vanishes. A [critical point of a dynamical system](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is an intersection where **$X=Y=0$**; the constant trajectory at that point is an [equilibrium of an autonomous differential equation](../../../../../equilibrium-of-an-autonomous-differential-equation.md). A nullcline alone need not consist of equilibria.
