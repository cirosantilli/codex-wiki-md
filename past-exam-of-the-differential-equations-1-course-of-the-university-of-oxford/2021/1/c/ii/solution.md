<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\beta=0$, the equation for the [characteristic curves](../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) is

$$
x(dy)^2-2y\,dx\,dy=dy\,(x\,dy-2y\,dx)=0.
$$

The two families are therefore $y=\text{constant}$ and the [characteristic projections](../../../../../characteristic-projection.md) from part (a), $y=Kx^2$, together with the vertical characteristic $x=0$. On either region $y>0$ or $y<0$, the [hyperbolic partial differential equation](../../../../../hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) admits the [characteristic coordinates](../../../../../characteristic-coordinate.md)

$$
\boxed{\varphi=y,\qquad \psi=\frac{x}{\sqrt{|y|}}.}
$$

Indeed $\varphi$ is constant along the first family, while $(x\partial_x+2y\partial_y)\psi=0$. Their [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is

$$
\frac{\partial(\varphi,\psi)}{\partial(x,y)}=-\frac1{\sqrt{|y|}}\ne0.
$$

The coefficients of the two pure second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) in these coordinates vanish, and the coefficient of the mixed [partial derivative](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) is $2y/\sqrt{|y|}\ne0$. Hence they give the hyperbolic [characteristic normal form](../../../../../characteristic-normal-form.md). They also remain regular at $x=0$, provided $y\ne0$.

The alternative $\varphi=y$, $\psi=y/x^2$ is valid on patches with $xy\ne0$. On $y=0$, it is a [parabolic partial differential equation](../../../../../parabolic-partial-differential-equation.md) away from the fully degenerate origin, so two independent hyperbolic [characteristic coordinates](../../../../../characteristic-coordinate.md) are unavailable.
