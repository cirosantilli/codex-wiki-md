<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using $x=-u$, $y=-v$ in the original [autonomous planar system](../../../../../autonomous-planar-system.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\dot u=v+1,\qquad \dot v=1-u^2.}
$$

The map $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y)$ is a [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) by $\pi$, preserving time and the clockwise or counterclockwise orientation of trajectories. Hence the given [centre equilibrium](../../../../../center-equilibrium.md) at $(-1,1)$ becomes a **clockwise centre at $(1,-1)$**, and the given [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md) at $(1,1)$ becomes a **saddle at $(-1,-1)$**. These are the only [equilibrium points](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), because [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) is invertible.
