↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Applying [time reversal of an autonomous differential equation](../../../../../time-reversal-of-an-autonomous-differential-equation.md) to the system from part (i), and renaming its coordinates $(x,y)$, gives

$$
\boxed{\widetilde X(x,y)=-(y+1),\qquad \widetilde Y(x,y)=x^2-1.}
$$

The [equilibrium points](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) remain $(1,-1)$ and $(-1,-1)$. [Time reversal of an autonomous differential equation](../../../../../time-reversal-of-an-autonomous-differential-equation.md) preserves a [saddle equilibrium](../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md), interchanging its stable and unstable directions; it also reverses the direction around a [centre equilibrium](../../../../../center-equilibrium.md). Therefore this system has precisely the required **counterclockwise centre at $(1,-1)$ and saddle at $(-1,-1)$**.
