<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Set $z=y-\beta$. Then the [autonomous planar system](../../../../../autonomous-planar-system.md) becomes

$$
\dot x=z,\qquad \dot z=x^3-x,
$$

which has no $\beta$ dependence. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{changing }\beta\text{ translates the entire phase portrait vertically}.}
$$

More precisely, replacing $\beta$ by $\beta+\delta$ sends every trajectory $(x(t),y(t))$ to $(x(t),y(t)+\delta)$, with the same time parametrization. The [equilibrium points](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), [nullclines](../../../../../nullcline.md), [periodic orbits](../../../../../periodic-orbit.md) and [separatrices](../../../../../separatrix.md) all undergo this same [translation](../../../../../translation-geometry.md). Their types and orientations are independent of $\beta$, so varying $\beta$ produces no [bifurcation](../../../../../bifurcation.md).
