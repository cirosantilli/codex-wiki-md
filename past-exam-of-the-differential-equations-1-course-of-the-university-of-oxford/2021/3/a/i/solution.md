<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [equilibrium points](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) satisfy $y=\beta$ and $x(x^2-1)=0$, so they are

$$
\boxed{(-1,\beta),\qquad(0,\beta),\qquad(1,\beta).}
$$

The [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) and its [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) equation are

$$
J(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\3x^2-1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad \lambda^2=3x^2-1.
$$

At $x=\pm1$ the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $\pm\sqrt2$, so both points are [saddle equilibria](../../../../../saddle-equilibrium.md). Their stable and unstable tangent directions have slopes $-\sqrt2$ and $\sqrt2$, respectively.

At $x=0$ the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $\pm i$, giving a centre for the [linearized equation](../../../../../linearized-equation.md). Purely imaginary [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) alone do not classify the nonlinear system. The [first integral](../../../../../first-integral.md) established in part (ii) proves that $(0,\beta)$ is in fact a [centre equilibrium](../../../../../center-equilibrium.md). It is clockwise: immediately to its right, with $0<x<1$ and $y=\beta$, the [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) points downwards. Thus the final nonlinear classification is

$$
\boxed{(-1,\beta),(1,\beta)\text{ are saddles};\quad(0,\beta)\text{ is a clockwise centre}.}
$$
