<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fix any $t_0\geq0$ and compare $y=R$ with $y=0$. The quotient required in a global [Lipschitz condition](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) in the dependent variable is

$$
\frac{|f(t_0,R)-f(t_0,0)|}{|R-0|}=|R-a(t_0)|\longrightarrow\infty
\qquad(R\longrightarrow\infty).
$$

Consequently,

$$
\boxed{f\text{ is not globally Lipschitz in }y.}
$$

It is nevertheless [locally Lipschitz](../../../../../locally-lipschitz-function.md) in $y$: for $|y|,|v|\leq R$,

$$
|f(t,y)-f(t,v)|=|y-v|\,|y+v-a(t)|\leq(2R+C_2)|y-v|.
$$

Thus the [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](../../../../../picard-lindelof-theorem.md) gives local existence and uniqueness for the [initial value problem](../../../../../initial-value-problem.md); the remaining work establishes existence on the entire half-line for the specified small data.
