<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $B=|b|>0$ and $\gamma=4B/C_1<1$. Initially $M_0=B<2B$. If $M_n<2B$, the preceding [supremum norm](../../../../../supremum-norm.md) estimate gives

$$
M_{n+1}\leq B+\frac{M_n^2}{C_1}<B+\frac{4B^2}{C_1}=B(1+\gamma)<2B.
$$

Hence [mathematical induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\sup_{t\geq0}|y_n(t)|<2|b|\quad\text{for every }n\geq0.}
$$
