<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [parabolic boundary](../../../../../parabolic-boundary.md) values are zero. An exact solution satisfies the preceding [differential inequality](../../../../../differential-inequality.md), so its maximum estimate with $A=0$ gives $u\leq0$. The function $v=-u$ satisfies the same equation because both the linear differential operator and the cubic term change sign. Applying the same [maximum principle](../../../../../maximum-principle.md) to $v$ gives $-u\leq0$. Hence the complete solution set is

$$
\boxed{\{u\equiv0\}.}
$$

Substitution verifies existence, and the two inequalities prove uniqueness among the assumed smooth solutions for every continuous choice of the drift coefficients.
