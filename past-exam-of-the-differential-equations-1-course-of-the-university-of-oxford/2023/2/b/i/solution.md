<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [characteristic curve](../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) in $(x,y,z)$ follows the [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) defining the first-order [partial differential equation](../../../../../partial-differential-equation-split.md). Along such a curve the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) turns the equation into

$$
\frac{dx}{ds}=xy,\qquad \frac{dy}{ds}=y^2,\qquad
\frac{dz}{ds}=-\frac13z^4.
$$

Its [characteristic projection](../../../../../characteristic-projection.md) is the curve $(x(s),y(s))$ in the independent-variable plane.

Writing $x(0)=\xi$, $y(0)=\eta$, $z(0)=\zeta$, [separation of variables](../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives

$$
\boxed{x(s)=\frac{\xi}{1-\eta s},\qquad
y(s)=\frac{\eta}{1-\eta s},\qquad
z(s)=\frac{\zeta}{(1+\zeta^3s)^{1/3}}.}
$$

These formulas hold on the maximal interval through $s=0$ where the denominators remain nonzero, using the real [cube root](../../../../../cube-root.md). They include $\eta=0$ (a stationary [characteristic projection](../../../../../characteristic-projection.md)) and $\zeta=0$ (the zero height). For $\zeta\ne0$, the last formula follows from $d(z^{-3})/ds=1$. The alternative zero solution must be retained when deriving this by division by $z^4$.
