<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [equilibrium point](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $p=(x_0,y_0)$ of an [autonomous planar system](../../../../../autonomous-planar-system.md) is a point where its [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) vanishes:

$$
\boxed{X(x_0,y_0)=Y(x_0,y_0)=0.}
$$

For a locally well-posed [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md), [Lyapunov stability](../../../../../lyapunov-stability.md) means that every prescribed neighbourhood can be protected by choosing a sufficiently small initial neighbourhood. More precisely, for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $\delta>0$ such that every trajectory starting with $\|p(0)-p\|<\delta$ exists for all $t\geq0$ and satisfies

$$
\boxed{\|p(t)-p\|<\varepsilon\quad\text{for all }t\geq0.}
$$

The number $\delta$ may depend on $\varepsilon$ but not on the particular starting point. [Asymptotic stability](../../../../../asymptotic-stability.md) additionally requires convergence to the [equilibrium point](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) for all sufficiently close starting points.
