# Quasiparticle residue

↑ **Parent:** [Landau quasiparticle](landau-quasiparticle.md)

The [quasiparticle residue](quasiparticle-residue.md) is the coherent pole weight of the single-particle [Green's function](green-s-function-split.md). Its coherent spectral term $Z\delta(E-\xi_p)$ contributes $Z\Theta(-\xi_p)$ to the zero-[temperature](temperature-split.md) [momentum](momentum-split.md) occupation. A smooth remainder leaves a jump $Z$ at the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md); [Landau quasiparticle](landau-quasiparticle.md) occupations have a unit step.[p.70, equations (4)–(6); integrate the pole spectral weight](on-the-theory-of-the-fermi-liquid-by-lev-davidovich-landau.md)

For the local illustrative distribution in the figure, put $x=(p-p_F)/p_F$ and

$$
n_{\rm particle}(x)=0.65\Theta(-x)+0.35\left[\frac12-0.2\tanh(2x)\right].
$$

$\Theta$ is the [Heaviside step function](heaviside-step-function.md). Its one-sided limits are $0.825$ and $0.175$, giving $Z=0.65$. The smooth term is stipulated; this local curve does not specify a normalized distribution over all momenta. In particular, $Z$ is neither the fraction of atoms above the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) nor $m/m^*$.
