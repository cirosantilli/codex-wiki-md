# Self-similar blast wave

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

A [self-similar blast wave](self-similar-blast-wave.md) is an expanding [shock wave](shock-wave.md) whose post-shock profiles retain their shape in a rescaled radius or distance. A fixed explosion energy, ambient [mass density](density.md) and geometry set the front scaling through conservation of total energy. The dimensionless coefficient requires the profiles and an energy-normalization convention.
