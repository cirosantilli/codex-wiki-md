# Spectrum of an all-ones matrix

↑ **Parent:** [All-ones matrix](all-ones-matrix.md)

For $u=(1,\ldots,1)^T$, $J_n=uu^T$ maps $v$ to $(\sum_iv_i)u$. Therefore $u$ is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $n$, while the zero-sum [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) is its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). For $n\geq2$ a [basis](basis.md) of the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $e_1-e_n,\ldots,e_{n-1}-e_n$. These vectors and $u$ form an [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md), proving [diagonalization](diagonalization-of-a-matrix.md) and [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) $t^{n-1}(t-n)$. Both [algebraic multiplicity](algebraic-multiplicity.md) and [geometric multiplicity](geometric-multiplicity.md) are one at $n$ and $n-1$ at zero. At $n=1$ the sole [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is 1; the zero-sum space has dimension zero and contributes no additional root.
