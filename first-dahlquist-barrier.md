# First Dahlquist barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Linear multistep method](linear-multistep-method.md)

For a consistent [zero-stable](zero-stability.md) real linear $k$-step method with constant coefficients and only first-derivative evaluations, the attainable order is at most $k+1$ for odd $k$ and $k+2$ for even $k$. For an explicit method the bound is $k$. These are order barriers under the [root condition for a multistep method](root-condition-for-a-multistep-method.md), not bounds on multiderivative methods or on arbitrary variable-step formulations. The [Second Dahlquist barrier](second-dahlquist-barrier.md) adds the stronger bound of order two for irreducible [A-stable](a-stability.md) methods.
