# Fixed-endpoint harmonic-oscillator action

↑ **Parent:** [Simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md)

For $S=\tfrac12\int_0^T(\dot x^2-\omega^2x^2)dt$ and fixed endpoint values $a,b$, the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) is $\ddot x+\omega^2x=0$. When $\sin\omega T\ne0$, solve the endpoint equations for the unique trajectory and use $\dot x^2-\omega^2x^2=(x\dot x)'$ on that trajectory to obtain the displayed [action](action.md). For $\omega\ne0$, at $\omega T=k\pi$ a solution exists only if $b=(-1)^ka$; then there is a family of solutions and their action is zero. At $\omega=0$ the nonresonant formula has the limit $(b-a)^2/(2T)$.
