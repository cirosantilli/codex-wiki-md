# Constant viscosity in radiation-supported vertical balance

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation-supported vertical disk](radiation-supported-vertical-disk.md)

Combining $F=c\Omega^2z/\kappa$ with viscous heating $dF/dz=(9/4)\mu\Omega^2$ gives a height-independent [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) $\mu=4c/(9\kappa)$ under constant [opacity](opacity.md). This exact limiting relation differs from an approximate vertically averaged [alpha disk](alpha-disk.md) closure.
