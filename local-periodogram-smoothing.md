# Local periodogram smoothing

↑ **Parent:** [Periodogram](periodogram.md)

Average neighboring [periodogram](periodogram.md) ordinates with nonnegative weights summing to one. Let the number $L_T$ of averaged ordinates tend to infinity while their frequency span, of order $L_T/T$, tends to zero. For a smooth [spectral density](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) and suitable short-memory or fourth-order dependence conditions, the bias tends to zero and variance is of order $1/L_T$. Smooth second-order spectral density alone does not ensure this: a time-constant random scale multiplying independent [white noise](white-noise.md) has a smooth unconditional spectrum but a nonergodic random limiting local power.
