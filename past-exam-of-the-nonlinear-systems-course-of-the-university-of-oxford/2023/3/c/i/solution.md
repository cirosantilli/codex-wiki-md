<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Solving $\mu x(1-x)=1$ by the [quadratic formula](../../../../../quadratic-formula.md) gives

$$
x_\pm=\frac12\pm\sqrt{\frac14-\frac1\mu}.
$$

For $\mu>4$, $0<x_-<1/2<x_+<1$. On $L=[0,x_-]$ the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $f'(x)=\mu(1-2x)$ is strictly positive, while on $R=[x_+,1]$ it is strictly negative. Thus the restrictions are strictly [monotonic functions](../../../../../monotonic-function.md). Their endpoint values are

$$
f(0)=0,\quad f(x_-)=1,\qquad f(x_+)=1,\quad f(1)=0.
$$

By [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) and strict [monotonicity](../../../../../monotonic-function.md), **each restriction maps its interval bijectively onto $[0,1]$**, with opposite orientations. The corresponding [inverse functions](../../../../../inverse-function.md) are

$$
\boxed{g_L(v)=\frac{1-\sqrt{1-4v/\mu}}2,\qquad
g_R(v)=\frac{1+\sqrt{1-4v/\mu}}2,\qquad 0\leq v\leq1.}
$$
