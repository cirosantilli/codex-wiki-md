<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [fixed point](../../../../fixed-point.md) equations give $x=\mu y$ and

$$
0=-\mu x+\mu y+y^3=y\bigl(y^2+\mu-\mu^2\bigr).
$$

Thus the origin exists for every real $\mu$. The nonzero [fixed points](../../../../fixed-point.md) are

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=\left(\pm\mu\sqrt{\mu(\mu-1)},\ \pm\sqrt{\mu(\mu-1)}\right),\qquad \mu<0\ \text{or}\ \mu>1,}
$$

with the same sign in both positions. At $\mu=0$ or $\mu=1$ these expressions coalesce with the origin and do not give additional [fixed points](../../../../fixed-point.md). There are no nonzero real [fixed points](../../../../fixed-point.md) for $0<\mu<1$.
