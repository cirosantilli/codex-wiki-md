# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the nonlinear systems course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all three questions of the [nonlinear systems course of the University of Oxford](../nonlinear-systems-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, held on 30 May 2023. Source: [released examination PDF, bundle pages 236–238; paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=236). Numbering follows the original paper. In Question 1 the displayed [centre manifold](../center-manifold.md) dynamics are a truncated local approximation; the neglected terms are specified below. At Question 2's parameter value $\mu=-1$, the [fixed-point multipliers](../multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) have a [strong resonance of a planar map](../strong-resonance-of-a-planar-map.md).
