# Protoplanetary disk dispersal

↑ **Parent:** [Protoplanetary disk](protoplanetary-disk.md)

Removal of disk gas by [accretion](accretion.md) onto the host [star](star.md), winds and [photoevaporation](photoevaporation.md). The finite availability of gas limits which solid cores can acquire massive envelopes. The disk's evolutionary state matters more than a single universal dispersal time.
