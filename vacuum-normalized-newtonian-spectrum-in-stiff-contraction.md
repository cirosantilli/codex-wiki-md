# Vacuum-normalized Newtonian spectrum in stiff contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian-potential Hankel modes in stiff contraction](newtonian-potential-hankel-modes-in-stiff-contraction.md)

The momentum constraint relates the incoming scalar mode $a^{-1}\sqrt{\hbar/(2k)}e^{-ik\tau}$ to the [Newtonian-potential Hankel modes in stiff contraction](newtonian-potential-hankel-modes-in-stiff-contraction.md). Large-argument Hankel asymptotics give $|N_k|^2=3\pi\hbar/(32k^2)$. The small-argument behavior $H_1^{(1)}(z)\sim-2i/(\pi z)$ then gives the displayed spectrum. Its dimensional power is $k^{-4}$ and its power per logarithmic interval is proportional to $k^{-1}$. These are Newtonian-potential spectra; a curvature-perturbation spectrum need not have the same exponent.
