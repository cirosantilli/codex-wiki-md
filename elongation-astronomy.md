# Elongation (astronomy)

↑ **Parent:** [Lunar phase](lunar-phase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elongation_(astronomy))

[Elongation](elongation-astronomy.md) is the apparent angular separation of a celestial body from the [Sun](sun.md), measured at the observer. For a [lunar phase](lunar-phase.md), the [elongation](elongation-astronomy.md) is the [Sun](sun.md)–[Earth](earth.md)–[Moon](moon.md) [angle](angle.md); in the distant-[Sun](sun.md) approximation it is supplementary to the [phase angle](phase-angle-astronomy.md). A given unsigned [elongation](elongation-astronomy.md) can occur on either side of the [Sun](sun.md) in the sky.
