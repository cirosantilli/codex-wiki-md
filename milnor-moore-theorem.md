<h1 id="milnor-moore-theorem">Milnor–Moore theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Pontryagin ring](pontryagin-ring.md)

For [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) $Y$, rational [Hurewicz homomorphism](hurewicz-homomorphism.md) identifies $\pi_*(\Omega Y)\otimes\mathbb Q$ with the [primitive elements of a Hopf algebra](primitive-element-of-a-hopf-algebra.md) of $H_*(\Omega Y;\mathbb Q)$ and induces an isomorphism $U(\pi_*(\Omega Y)\otimes\mathbb Q)\cong H_*(\Omega Y;\mathbb Q)$ of graded Hopf algebras. The bracket on [homotopy](homotopy.md) is the [Samelson product](samelson-product.md). The underlying algebra theorem says that a connected graded cocommutative [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md) over [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md) is the [universal enveloping algebra](universal-enveloping-algebra.md) of its primitive [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). These statements let a free [tensor algebra](tensor-algebra.md) of loop-space [homology](homology-split.md) detect a [free graded Lie algebra](free-graded-lie-algebra.md) of [homotopy](homotopy.md).
