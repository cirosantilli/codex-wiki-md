# Relative Moser theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Moser's trick](moser-s-trick.md)

Two closed [symplectic forms](symplectic-form.md) agreeing as ambient bilinear forms along a compact embedded submanifold are equivalent near it by a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) fixing it pointwise. Shrink until their affine interpolation is [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md). A relative homotopy primitive $\eta$ for their difference vanishes as an ambient covector on the submanifold. Solving $\iota_{Z_t}\omega_t=-\eta$ gives a [vector field](vector-field.md) vanishing there; its local flow fixes the submanifold and makes $\omega_t$ constant under pullback.
