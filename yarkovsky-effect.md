# Yarkovsky effect

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary system dynamics](planetary-system-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarkovsky_effect)

Anisotropic [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md) produces recoil that changes the [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) of a rotating solid body. Finite [thermal inertia](thermal-inertia.md) provides the temperature lag. Large bodies have small acceleration per unit mass, while very small bodies become nearly isothermal. The [Yarkovsky effect](yarkovsky-effect.md) can supply bodies to dynamical escape regions.
