# Compressed sensing

↑ **Parent:** [Sparse optimization](sparse-optimization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compressed_sensing)

Compressed sensing reconstructs a [sparse vector](sparse-vector.md), or an approximately sparse one, from fewer linear measurements than its ambient dimension. [Basis pursuit](basis-pursuit.md) replaces counting nonzero coordinates by [convex](convex-function.md) $\ell^1$ minimization. A [null space property](nullspace-property.md) characterizes exact uniform recovery, while a [robust null space property](robust-null-space-property.md) controls errors from noise and nonsparse tails.
