# Bounded stopping time

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping time](stopping-time.md)

A [bounded stopping time](bounded-stopping-time.md) is a [stopping time](stopping-time.md) bounded [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) by a deterministic finite constant. In discrete time, a bound $T\leq N$ makes $M_T$ integrable whenever $M_0,\ldots,M_N$ are [integrable random variables](integrable-random-variable.md), and the [optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) applies to a [martingale](martingale-split.md) without additional limiting hypotheses. Almost-sure finiteness alone is weaker than boundedness.
