# Photodiode

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photodiode)

A photodiode is a [semiconductor](semiconductor.md) diode that converts absorbed photons into electrical carriers. A reverse-biased detector separates the carriers and collects charge; its [quantum efficiency](quantum-efficiency.md) and [dark current](dark-current-physics.md) determine sensitivity.
