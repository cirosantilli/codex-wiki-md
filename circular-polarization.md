# Circular polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_polarization)

An electromagnetic wave has circular polarization when its transverse [electric field](electric-field-split.md) has constant magnitude and rotates once during each period. In complex-amplitude notation, two orthogonal components have equal magnitude and a relative phase of one quarter-cycle.
