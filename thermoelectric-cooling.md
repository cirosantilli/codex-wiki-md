# Thermoelectric cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Peltier effect](peltier-effect.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermoelectric_cooling)

[Thermoelectric cooling](thermoelectric-cooling.md) uses the [Peltier effect](peltier-effect.md) to remove [heat](heat-split.md) from a load using electrical [work](work.md). A typical [thermoelectric module](thermoelectric-module.md) connects many pairs of different [semiconductors](semiconductor-split.md) electrically in a [series circuit](series-circuit.md) and thermally in parallel between two faces. For an idealized pair with $S_p>S_n$, positive [conventional current](conventional-current.md) crosses n to p at the cold bridge and p to n at the hot return. With common bridge material and isothermal contacts, the intervening metal contributions telescope: the net pair coefficient is $S_p-S_n$. For $N$ identical pairs define the module [Seebeck coefficient](seebeck-coefficient.md) $\alpha=N(S_p-S_n)>0$.

Choose fixed face [temperatures](temperature-split.md) $T_h\ge T_c>0$, $\Delta T=T_h-T_c$, module [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R>0$ and open-circuit [thermal conductance](thermal-conductance.md) $K>0$. All three module parameters $\alpha,R,K$ are constants over the chosen operating range. Positive $I$ is the orientation absorbing [Peltier effect](peltier-effect.md) [heat](heat-split.md) at c. Define $Q_c$ as net [heat](heat-split.md) extracted from the cold reservoir per unit time, $Q_h$ as net [heat](heat-split.md) delivered to the hot reservoir per unit time, and $P_{\rm in}$ as electrical [power](power.md) entering the module. Despite the conventional letters $Q_c,Q_h$, both denote [power](power.md) in [watts](watt.md), not accumulated [energy](energy-split.md). Ferrotec gives the corresponding single-stage equations and distinguishes module performance from bypass leaks.[§11.4(a–f), single-stage equations; §11.7, surrounding air and clamping-screw heat leaks](mathematical-modeling-of-modules-by-ferrotec.md)
