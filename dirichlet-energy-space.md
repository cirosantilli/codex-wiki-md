# Dirichlet energy space

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet inner product](dirichlet-inner-product.md)

For planar [Gaussian free field](gaussian-free-field.md) theory, this is the completion of real [test functions](test-function.md) $C_c^\infty(D)$ in the norm induced by the [Dirichlet inner product](dirichlet-inner-product.md), $(f,g)_\nabla=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_D\nabla f\cdot\nabla g$. On a general unbounded domain this convention must be distinguished from completion in the inhomogeneous $H^1$ norm. A [conformal map](conformal-map.md) identifies its energy norm with that on the unit disc by conformal invariance.
