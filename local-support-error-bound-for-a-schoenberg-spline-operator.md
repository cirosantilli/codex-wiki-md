# Local-support error bound for a Schoenberg spline operator

↑ **Parent:** [Schoenberg spline operator](schoenberg-spline-operator.md)

For nonnegative partition-normalized order-$k$ [B-splines](b-spline.md), let $Vf(t)=\sum_i f(\tau_i)N_i(t)$ with each sample point inside $[t_i,t_{i+k}]$. If $N_i(t)$ is nonzero, both $t$ and $\tau_i$ lie in an interval of length at most $k|\Delta|$. Their function values differ by at most the [modulus of continuity](modulus-of-continuity.md) at that length. Summing with nonnegative weights of total one proves the displayed bound. With fixed order and vanishing maximum knot gap, [uniform continuity](uniform-continuity.md) gives [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md). The same argument works for bounded piecewise-continuous outputs if full interior knot multiplicities are allowed; their individual continuity should not then be asserted.
