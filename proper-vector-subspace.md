# Proper vector subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Vector subspace](vector-subspace.md)

A proper [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is one strictly smaller than the ambient space. In a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), this is equivalent to having smaller [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md): a [basis](basis.md) of the subspace can be extended to a [basis](basis.md) of the ambient space, and strict inclusion requires at least one additional [vector](vector.md).
