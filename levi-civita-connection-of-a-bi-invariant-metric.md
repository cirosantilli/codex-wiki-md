# Levi-Civita connection of a bi-invariant metric

↑ **Parent:** [Bi-invariant Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-riemannian-metric.md)

For [left-invariant vector fields](left-invariant-vector-field.md), the [Koszul formula](koszul-formula.md) and the adjoint invariance of a [bi-invariant Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-riemannian-metric.md) give the displayed [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md). In particular $\nabla_XX=0$.
