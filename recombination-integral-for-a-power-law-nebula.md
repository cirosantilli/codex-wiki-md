# Recombination integral for a power-law nebula

↑ **Parent:** [Photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md)

For fully ionized pure hydrogen of density $n(r)=n_0(r/r_0)^{-\beta}$ and constant [Case B recombination](case-b-recombination.md) coefficient $\alpha$, the total recombination rate is

$$
\mathcal R(R;r_{\rm in})=4\pi\alpha n_0^2r_0^{2\beta}\int_{r_{\rm in}}^R r^{2-2\beta}\,dr.
$$

The central integral converges only for $\beta<3/2$; an envelope extending to infinity has finite recombination capacity only for $\beta>3/2$. At $\beta=3/2$, each logarithmic radius interval contributes equally. A [cored power-law density profile](cored-power-law-density-profile.md) removes the central singularity.
