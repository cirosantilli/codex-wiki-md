# Equatorial wave velocity polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial shallow-water dispersion relation](equatorial-shallow-water-dispersion-relation.md)

In dimensionless [equatorial wave](equatorial-wave.md) units with [gravity wave](gravity-wave-split.md) speed and [beta plane](beta-plane.md) coefficient one, a Fourier mode $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$ obeys $\omega u-k\eta=iyv$ and $\omega\eta-ku=-iv'$. Inverting this two-by-two [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md) gives the displayed [wave polarization](polarization-waves.md), provided $\omega^2\ne k^2$. Inserting it in the meridional momentum equation gives the [Hermite differential equation](hermite-differential-equation.md) after a Gaussian factor is removed. At singular values the original equations, rather than these quotient formulae, determine whether a trapped mode exists.
