# Vertical surface in a Seifert fibered space

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert fibered space](seifert-fibered-space.md)

A vertical surface is a union of [Seifert fibers](seifert-fiber.md) over an arc or a closed curve in the base [orbifold](orbifold.md). Typical connected examples are an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) and a [torus](torus.md).
