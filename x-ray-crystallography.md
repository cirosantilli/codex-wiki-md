# X-ray crystallography

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallography](crystallography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray_crystallography)

[X-ray crystallography](x-ray-crystallography.md) infers a [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) from measured [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md). Peak positions constrain the lattice geometry, while intensities constrain the [atomic basis of a crystal](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md); the inference also needs an experimental model and does not directly observe every atomic position.
