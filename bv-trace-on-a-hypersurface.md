# BV trace on a hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

A [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) function has two one-sided approximate mean [BV traces on a hypersurface](bv-trace-on-a-hypersurface.md) at almost every point of an oriented countably rectifiable hypersurface, with respect to [Hausdorff measure](hausdorff-measure.md). They agree outside its [approximate discontinuity set](approximate-discontinuity-set.md). At an [approximate jump point](approximate-jump-point.md), they are the jump values, up to the choice of orientation.
