# Structure factor

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal scattering](crystal-scattering.md)

The coherent [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) associated with a specified spatial arrangement, obtained by summing the site amplitudes with their relative [wave phases](phase-waves.md). Distinguish the [crystal lattice structure factor](crystal-lattice-structure-factor.md), which sums over lattice sites, from the [crystal basis structure factor](crystal-basis-structure-factor.md), which sums the [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) inside a [unit cell](unit-cell.md). In a periodic [crystal](crystal.md) the two factors multiply. A basis factor can vanish even at an allowed [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector, causing an extinct reflection.
