# Effective mass of a rolling rigid body

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

For [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) with contact radius $r$, substitute $\omega=v/r$ into translational plus rotational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md):

$$
T=\frac12Mv^2+\frac12I\omega^2=\frac12\left(M+\frac I{r^2}\right)v^2.
$$

The coefficient acts as an effective inertia for a translation coordinate. It is not additional gravitational [mass](mass.md). A quadratic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $Kx^2/2$ then gives small-oscillation frequency $\omega_0=\sqrt{K/M_{\rm eff}}$ when this coefficient is constant.
