The solutions use [natural units](../natural-units.md) $\hbar=c=1$ and the [Minkowski metric](../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$. In question 1, $\gamma^5=\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3$ is exactly the paper's convention: it contains no factor $i$. The qualifications in [iii](2025/2/c/iii.md), [d](2025/2/d.md) and [d](2025/3/d.md) distinguish the printed expressions from the results of [charge conservation](../charge-conservation.md), [identical final-state symmetry factors](../identical-particle-factor-in-a-final-state-phase-space-integral.md) and [fermionic time ordering](../fermionic-time-ordering.md).
