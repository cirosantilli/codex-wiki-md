<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In $2+1$ spacetime dimensions, the dimensionless [action](../../../../action.md) gives $[\mathcal L]=3$ and $[\partial_\mu]=1$. The scalar [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) requires $2[\phi]+2=3$, whereas the fermion [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) requires $2[\psi]+1=3$. Thus the [engineering dimensions of relativistic fields](../../../../engineering-dimensions-of-relativistic-fields.md) are

$$
[\phi]=\frac12,\qquad[\psi]=[\bar\psi]=1.
$$

Matching each interaction to [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) three gives

$$
\boxed{[g_1]=3-(2[\phi]+2[\psi])=0,\quad[g_2]=3-4[\phi]=1,\quad[g_3]=3-6[\phi]=0.}
$$

Here the brackets mean exponents of mass in [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md). The coefficients $g_1,g_3$ are [marginal couplings](../../../../marginal-coupling.md) by classical [power counting](../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md), and $g_2$ is a [relevant coupling](../../../../relevant-coupling.md).
