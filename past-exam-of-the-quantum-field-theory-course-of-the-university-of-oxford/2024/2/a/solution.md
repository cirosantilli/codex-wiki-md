<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using [Feynman slash notation](../../../../feynman-slash-notation.md), the symmetric tensor $p_\mu p_\nu$ kills the antisymmetric [commutator](../../../../commutator.md) of the [gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md). The [Clifford algebra](../../../../clifford-algebra.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\not p\,\not p=\tfrac12p_\mu p_\nu\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=p^2I.}
$$

Consequently $(\not p)^{2r}=(p^2)^rI$ and $(\not p)^{2r+1}=(p^2)^r\not p$. For a scalar-coefficient [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) $F(z)=\sum_nc_nz^n$, split its even and odd powers:

$$
\boxed{F(\not p)=A(p^2)\not p+B(p^2)I,\quad A(z)=\sum_rc_{2r+1}z^r,\quad B(z)=\sum_rc_{2r}z^r.}
$$

This reduction assumes scalar coefficients; arbitrary noncommuting matrix coefficients would not give the stated scalar functions $A,B$.
