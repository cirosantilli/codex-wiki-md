<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Both a [particle](../../../../particle-physics.md) and its [antiparticle](../../../../antiparticle.md) carry the [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) of their mode label. Thus the [normal-ordered](../../../../normal-ordering.md) total [momentum operator](../../../../momentum-operator.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf P=\int_{\mathbf p}\mathbf p\left(a_{\mathbf p}^\dagger a_{\mathbf p}+b_{\mathbf p}^\dagger b_{\mathbf p}\right).}
$$

The [canonical commutation relations](../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) give

$$
[a_{\mathbf p},\mathbf P]=\mathbf p\,a_{\mathbf p},\qquad
[b_{-\mathbf p}^\dagger,\mathbf P]=-(-\mathbf p)b_{-\mathbf p}^\dagger=\mathbf p\,b_{-\mathbf p}^\dagger.
$$

The minus sign in the [antiparticle](../../../../antiparticle.md) mode label is essential: both terms in the printed [mode expansion of a free field](../../../../mode-expansion-of-a-free-field.md) contain $e^{i\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf x}$. Hence

$$
[\phi,\mathbf P]=\int_{\mathbf p}\frac{\mathbf p}{\sqrt{2E_p}}\left(b_{-\mathbf p}^\dagger e^{iE_pt}+a_{\mathbf p}e^{-iE_pt}\right)e^{i\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf x}
=\boxed{-i\nabla\phi.}
$$
