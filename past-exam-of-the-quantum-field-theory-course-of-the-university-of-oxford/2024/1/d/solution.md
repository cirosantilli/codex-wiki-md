<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose the zero of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) so that the translation-invariant [vacuum state](../../../../vacuum-state.md) has $P^\mu|\Omega\rangle=0$. A [one-particle state](../../../../one-particle-state.md) of [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) $m_\psi$ obeys the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{p^\mu=(E_{\mathbf p},\mathbf p),\qquad E_{\mathbf p}=\sqrt{m_\psi^2+|\mathbf p|^2},\qquad p^2=m_\psi^2.}
$$

The [translation covariance of a quantum field](../../../../translation-covariance-of-a-quantum-field.md) in [c](../c.md) remains valid for the full interacting [four-momentum operator](../../../../four-momentum-operator.md). Acting its [exponential functions](../../../../exponential-function.md) on the two [momentum eigenstates](../../../../momentum-eigenstate.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\Omega|\phi(x)|\psi,\mathbf p\rangle
&=\langle\Omega|e^{iP\cdot x}\phi(0)e^{-iP\cdot x}|\psi,\mathbf p\rangle\\
&=\boxed{e^{-ip\cdot x}\langle\Omega|\phi(0)|\psi,\mathbf p\rangle.}
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $p\cdot x=E_{\mathbf p}t-\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf x$. A different additive [vacuum energy](../../../../vacuum-energy.md) must be subtracted consistently from both [energy eigenvalues](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) before identifying the physical $p^0$.
