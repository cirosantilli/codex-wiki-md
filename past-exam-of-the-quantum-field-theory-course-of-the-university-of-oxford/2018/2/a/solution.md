<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md) with $[x]=-1$, $[\partial_\mu]=1$ and dimensionless [action](../../../../action.md). The [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) in [Euclidean space](../../../../euclidean-norm.md) has dimension $D$, and the [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) fixes $2+2[\phi_0]=D$. Thus

$$
[\phi_0]=\frac{D-2}{2},\qquad
\boxed{[\lambda_{2k}]=D-k(D-2)=2k-(k-1)D\equiv d_k.}
$$

A dimensionless [coupling constant](../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{g_{2k}(\mu)=\mu^{-d_k}\lambda_{2k},\qquad
\lambda_{2k}=\mu^{d_k}g_{2k}.}
$$

Here $\mu$ is a positive [regularization](../../../../regularization.md) scale with [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) one. The interaction is marginal at its [upper critical dimension](../../../../upper-critical-dimension.md) $D_c=2k/(k-1)$, relevant below it and irrelevant above it by this engineering-dimension test. In particular, $d_2=4-D$, while $d_k=4-2k+2(k-1)\epsilon$ near $D=4-2\epsilon$. At this stage $g_{2k}$ is the dimensionless version of the displayed bare coupling; after introducing [counterterms](../../../../counterterm.md) one may use the same scale factor for the [renormalized coupling](../../../../renormalized-coupling.md).
