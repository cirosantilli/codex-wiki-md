<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [phi-fourth theory](../../../../quartic-interaction.md) interaction has [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) $4-D$, so its bare coefficient must contain a [renormalization scale](../../../../renormalization-scale.md) factor $\mu^{4-D}=(\mu^2)^{2-D/2}$ when expressed through a dimensionless [renormalized coupling](../../../../renormalized-coupling.md). The tree-level four-leg [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) is linear in that coupling. At the next order, two quartic vertices form the three one-loop [bubble diagram](../../../../bubble-diagram.md) graphs shown in [c](../../2/c.md), with external pairings $12|34$, $13|24$ and $14|23$.

For each pairing there are two identical internal lines and [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) 2. The loop integral has the large-[momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) behaviour

$$
\int\frac{d^Dq}{(2\pi)^D}\frac1{q^2(q+P)^2}
\sim\int^\infty dq\,q^{D-5}.
$$

It is logarithmically divergent in four dimensions. [Dimensional regularization](../../../../dimensional-regularization.md) represents that local divergence by a simple pole proportional to $1/(D-4)$. Its residue is independent of external [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md), so a local quartic [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) cancels it. The three graphs sum to one constant coefficient; this argument establishes its structure without requiring its value.

The relation consequently has the form

$$
\boxed{\lambda_0=\mu^{4-D}\left[g+\frac{Cg^2}{D-4}+O(g^3)\right].}
$$

The sign of $C$ incorporates the chosen pole denominator and vertex convention. In particular, a negative $C$ here corresponds to a positive coefficient of $1/(4-D)$ in the bare coupling. Finite pieces depend on the [renormalization scheme](../../../../renormalization-scheme.md); a pure-pole relation is natural in [minimal subtraction scheme](../../../../minimal-subtraction-scheme.md). One-loop two-point [tadpole diagrams](../../../../tadpole-diagram.md) contribute [mass](../../../../mass.md) [renormalization](../../../../renormalization.md), but not the momentum-dependent [field renormalization](../../../../wave-function-renormalization.md) that could change this coupling relation at order $g^2$. The four-point object relevant here is the amputated [one-particle-irreducible vertex](../../../../one-particle-irreducible-vertex.md); external-leg [self-energy](../../../../self-energy.md) insertions are dealt with separately.
