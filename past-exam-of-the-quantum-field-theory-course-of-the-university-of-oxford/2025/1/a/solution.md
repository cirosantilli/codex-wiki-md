<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Distinct [gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) anticommute, and $(\gamma^0)^2=I$, $(\gamma^j)^2=-I$ for $j=1,2,3$. Moving a particular $\gamma^\mu$ through the other three factors in $\gamma^5$ gives three minus signs. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\{\gamma^5,\gamma^\mu\}=0.}
$$

Pairing equal factors in the square involves $3+2+1=6$ interchanges. The [Clifford algebra](../../../../clifford-algebra.md) consequently gives

$$
\boxed{(\gamma^5)^2=(-1)^6\prod_{\mu=0}^3(\gamma^\mu)^2=-I.}
$$

In particular $(\gamma^5)^{-1}=-\gamma^5$; replacing it by the conventional [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) matrix would change this answer.

The [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) is unchanged by a [similarity transformation](../../../../similarity-transformation.md). Since $(\gamma^0)^{-1}=\gamma^0$ and $\gamma^0\gamma^5\gamma^0=-\gamma^5$, [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) gives $\operatorname{tr}\gamma^5=-\operatorname{tr}\gamma^5$. Also

$$
\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^\mu)=\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^5)=-\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^\mu).
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}\gamma^5=\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^5\gamma^\mu)=0.}
$$
