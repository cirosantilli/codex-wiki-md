<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md) are $[\phi]=1$, $[\psi]=3/2$, $[g]=0$. Up to [total derivatives](../../../../total-derivative.md), [power counting](../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) allows local [counterterms](../../../../counterterm.md) of [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) at most four. A convenient basis for the [counterterm Lagrangian](../../../../counterterm-lagrangian.md) compatible with the actual [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) and [Dirac fermion number conservation](../../../../dirac-fermion-number-conservation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{\rm ct}^{(+)}={}&-\delta\Lambda+\delta Z_\psi\bar\psi i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\psi-\delta M\bar\psi\psi
+\tfrac12\delta Z_\phi\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi-\tfrac12\delta m^2\phi^2\\
&-\delta g\,\phi\bar\psi\gamma^5\psi-\frac{\delta\lambda}{4!}\phi^4.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The optional constant $\delta\Lambda$ renormalizes the [vacuum energy density](../../../../vacuum-energy-density.md). The [wavefunction renormalization](../../../../wave-function-renormalization.md), [mass counterterms](../../../../mass-counterterm.md) and [Yukawa coupling](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) renormalization correct the terms already present. The [quartic interaction](../../../../quartic-interaction.md) must also be included: the [fermion loop](../../../../fermion-loop.md) with four external bosonic legs is logarithmically divergent, even if the [renormalized](../../../../renormalization.md) quartic coupling is set to zero at one chosen [renormalization scale](../../../../renormalization-scale.md). The displayed original interactions alone do not form a closed set of [counterterms](../../../../counterterm.md).

If “most general” is interpreted before imposing the [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) examined in [d](../d.md), [Lorentz invariance](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md), a [Hermitian](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) [action](../../../../action.md) and [Dirac fermion number conservation](../../../../dirac-fermion-number-conservation.md) also allow

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{\rm ct}^{(-)}={}&-\delta t\,\phi-\frac{\delta\kappa}{3!}\phi^3-\delta y\,\phi\bar\psi\psi-\delta M_5\bar\psi\gamma^5\psi
+\delta Z_5\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\partial_\mu\psi.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here all coefficients can be real in the paper's [gamma matrix](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) convention; the last term is Hermitian in the [action](../../../../action.md) up to a [total derivative](../../../../total-derivative.md). It is an axial kinetic normalization, which could alternatively be removed by a suitable perturbative field redefinition. Every term in $\mathcal L_{\rm ct}^{(-)}$ is odd under the actual [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](../../../../parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md), so its coefficient vanishes in a symmetry-preserving [renormalization scheme](../../../../renormalization-scheme.md). Listing these terms makes explicit which assumption excludes a [tadpole counterterm](../../../../tadpole-counterterm.md), a cubic scalar term, a scalar [Yukawa interaction](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md), a pseudoscalar [Dirac mass term](../../../../dirac-mass-term.md) and an axial kinetic term.
