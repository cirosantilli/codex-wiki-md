<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) give $\{a_1^\dagger,a_2^\dagger\}=0$, where each label includes both [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and [spin](../../../../spin.md). Therefore

$$
|2,1\rangle=a_2^\dagger a_1^\dagger|0\rangle=-a_1^\dagger a_2^\dagger|0\rangle=-|1,2\rangle.
$$

Thus **exchanging the complete labels of the two identical [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) changes the sign of their state**. If the labels coincide, $(a_1^\dagger)^2=0$; the state vanishes, which is the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). A negative state vector describes the same isolated physical ray, but its relative sign matters when exchange alternatives interfere.
