For the [complex scalar fields](../../complex-scalar-field.md), use the conventional [particle](../../particle-physics.md) names: $\phi$ annihilates a light $\phi$ particle and creates a light [antiparticle](../../antiparticle.md) $\bar\phi$; $\Phi$ annihilates a heavy $\Phi$ particle and creates $\bar\Phi$. These choices make the distinction between [particle](../../particle-physics.md) labels and field symbols explicit throughout [ii](2/c/ii.md) and [iii](2/c/iii.md). The couplings $g,h$ are real as required by the printed Hermitian [Lagrangian density](../../lagrangian-density.md).
