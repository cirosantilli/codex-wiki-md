<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Independent [global phase symmetries](../../../../global-phase-symmetry.md) would rotate $\phi\mapsto e^{-i\alpha}\phi$ and $\Phi\mapsto e^{-i\beta}\Phi$. The cubic [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) preserves only $2\alpha+\beta=0$ modulo $2\pi$. The continuous [internal symmetry of a classical field theory](../../../../internal-symmetry-of-a-classical-field-theory.md) is therefore the [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md)

$$
\boxed{U(1):\quad\phi\mapsto e^{-i\alpha}\phi,\qquad\Phi\mapsto e^{2i\alpha}\Phi.}
$$

Normalize the [Noether charge](../../../../noether-charge.md) so that $q_\phi=1$. Then $q_\Phi=-2$, $q_{\bar\phi}=-1$ and $q_{\bar\Phi}=2$. The [Noether current](../../../../noether-current.md) is

$$
\boxed{j^\mu=i\phi^\dagger\overleftrightarrow{\partial^\mu}\phi-2i\Phi^\dagger\overleftrightarrow{\partial^\mu}\Phi,\qquad\partial_\mu j^\mu=0,}
$$

where $A\overleftrightarrow\partial B=A\partial B-(\partial A)B$. For example, the [Euler-Lagrange field equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) give

$$
\partial_\mu(i\phi^\dagger\overleftrightarrow{\partial^\mu}\phi)=ih(\phi^2\Phi-\phi^{\dagger2}\Phi^\dagger),\qquad
\partial_\mu(i\Phi^\dagger\overleftrightarrow{\partial^\mu}\Phi)=\frac{ih}{2}(\phi^2\Phi-\phi^{\dagger2}\Phi^\dagger),
$$

which cancel with the relative coefficient $-2$. The quartic [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) separately preserves both original phase rotations.

There is also a discrete [charge conjugation](../../../../charge-conjugation.md) symmetry $(\phi,\Phi)\mapsto(\phi^\dagger,\Phi^\dagger)$ for these real couplings. Including it, the internal symmetry is $U(1)\rtimes\mathbb Z_2\cong O(2)$, with [charge conjugation](../../../../charge-conjugation.md) reversing the [Noether charge](../../../../noether-charge.md). The connected symmetry and its [conserved current](../../../../conserved-current.md) are the $U(1)$ above.
