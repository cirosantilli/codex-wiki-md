<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) basis of [a](../a.md), the two rows of the [Dirac Hamiltonian](../../../../dirac-hamiltonian.md) equation give

$$
\boxed{i(\partial_t+\partial_x)\psi_R=m\psi_L,\qquad
i(\partial_t-\partial_x)\psi_L=m\psi_R.}
$$

The [Dirac mass term](../../../../dirac-mass-term.md) thus couples the two opposite [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) components. In particular, the [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) is

$$
\mathcal L=i\psi_R^\dagger(\partial_t+\partial_x)\psi_R
+i\psi_L^\dagger(\partial_t-\partial_x)\psi_L
-m(\psi_L^\dagger\psi_R+\psi_R^\dagger\psi_L).
$$

For $m=0$, the coupling disappears and the [massless Dirac equation](../../../../massless-dirac-equation.md) splits into the independent transport equations

$$
\boxed{(\partial_t+\partial_x)\psi_R=0,\qquad
(\partial_t-\partial_x)\psi_L=0.}
$$

These are the two [chiral transport equations in two spacetime dimensions](../../../../chiral-transport-equations-in-two-spacetime-dimensions.md). The [chiral projectors](../../../../chiral-projector.md) are $P_R=(1+\gamma^5)/2$ and $P_L=(1-\gamma^5)/2$; in this basis they simply select the upper and lower components.
