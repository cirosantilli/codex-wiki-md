<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Continue with $m=0$. Along a characteristic $x=x_0+t$, the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\psi_R(t,x_0+t)=(\partial_t+\partial_x)\psi_R=0.
$$

Thus the initial [wave packet](../../../../wave-packet.md) $\psi_R(0,x)=f(x)$ evolves as $\psi_R(t,x)=f(x-t)$. Similarly, along $x=x_0-t$, the left component is constant, giving

$$
\boxed{\psi_R(t,x)=f(x-t),\qquad \psi_L(t,x)=h(x+t).}
$$

A feature initially at $x_0$ therefore appears at $x_0+t$ for the right component and $x_0-t$ for the left component. The profiles translate without changing shape at the [speed of light](../../../../speed-of-light.md), which is 1 in these units. For positive-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) [plane waves](../../../../plane-wave.md), the right component has $E=p>0$ and the left component has $E=-p>0$, another way to identify their propagation directions.

The conclusion concerns the [massless Dirac equation](../../../../massless-dirac-equation.md). For $m\ne0$, the components couple as in [c](../c.md), and a positive-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) packet has [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) $p/\sqrt{p^2+m^2}$, rather than a universal speed $\pm1$. For example, a massive positive-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) rest solution has $p=0$ and $\psi_R=\psi_L$, so neither component alone describes an independently moving light-speed packet.
