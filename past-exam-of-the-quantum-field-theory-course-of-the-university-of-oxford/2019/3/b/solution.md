<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Variation of the [action](../../../../action.md) with respect to the independent [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) gives $(i\gamma^0\partial_t+i\gamma^1\partial_x-m)\psi=0$. The [gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) chosen in [a](../a.md) make the [Dirac equation](../../../../dirac-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}
-m&i(\partial_t-\partial_x)\\
i(\partial_t+\partial_x)&-m
\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}\psi_R\\\psi_L\end{pmatrix}=0.}
$$

Multiplication by $\gamma^0$ gives the equivalent [Dirac Hamiltonian](../../../../dirac-hamiltonian.md) form

$$
i\partial_t\psi=(-i\sigma_3\partial_x+m\sigma_1)\psi.
$$

For a [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) $\psi=u\,e^{-iEt+ipx}$, where $p$ is the signed one-dimensional [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), the equation is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}E-p&-m\\-m&E+p\end{pmatrix}u=0.
$$

A nonzero [spinor](../../../../spinor.md) exists precisely when the [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) vanishes:

$$
\boxed{E^2-p^2-m^2=0,\qquad E=\pm\sqrt{p^2+m^2}.}
$$

For $m\ne0$, one possible unnormalized [spinor](../../../../spinor.md) solution is $u=(E+p,m)^{\mathsf T}$. Alternatively, $(p\sigma_3+m\sigma_1)^2=(p^2+m^2)1$ follows from the [Pauli matrix multiplication law](../../../../pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md), proving the same [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md). Acting on the [Dirac equation](../../../../dirac-equation.md) with $i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu+m$ also shows that each component satisfies $(\partial_t^2-\partial_x^2+m^2)\psi=0$, the [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md).
