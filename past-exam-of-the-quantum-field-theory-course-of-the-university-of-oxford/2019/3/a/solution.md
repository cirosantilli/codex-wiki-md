<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$, the [Clifford algebra](../../../../clifford-algebra.md) gives $(\gamma^0)^2=1$, $(\gamma^1)^2=-1$, and $\gamma^0\gamma^1=-\gamma^1\gamma^0$. Hence

$$
\gamma^5\gamma^0=-\gamma^1,\quad \gamma^0\gamma^5=\gamma^1,\qquad
\gamma^5\gamma^1=-\gamma^0,\quad \gamma^1\gamma^5=\gamma^0,
$$

so $\{\gamma^5,\gamma^0\}=\{\gamma^5,\gamma^1\}=0$. Its square is

$$
(\gamma^5)^2=\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^0\gamma^1
=-(\gamma^0)^2(\gamma^1)^2=1.
$$

Choose the following [gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md), expressed using the [Pauli matrices](../../../../pauli-matrices.md):

$$
\boxed{\gamma^0=\sigma_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\gamma^1=-i\sigma_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\gamma^5=\sigma_3=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

These satisfy $(\gamma^0)^\dagger=\gamma^0$, $(\gamma^1)^\dagger=-\gamma^1$ and $(\gamma^5)^\dagger=\gamma^5$. The [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are therefore $\boxed{+1,-1}$, and we order the [spinor](../../../../spinor.md) as $\psi=(\psi_R,\psi_L)^{\mathsf T}$ throughout. Its [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) is $\bar\psi=(\psi_L^\dagger,\psi_R^\dagger)$. No additional factor of $i$ multiplies $\gamma^0\gamma^1$ in this spacetime dimension. This realizes a [Dirac field in two spacetime dimensions](../../../../dirac-field-in-two-spacetime-dimensions.md).
