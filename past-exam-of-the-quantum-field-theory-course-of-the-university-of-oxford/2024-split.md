Use [natural units](../natural-units.md) $\hbar=c=1$ and the mostly-minus [Minkowski metric](../minkowski-metric.md). Question 3 has two spatial dimensions: its [gamma matrices](../gamma-matrices.md) are the specified two-by-two matrices. The printed $\sigma^\mu$ in its [kinetic term](../kinetic-term.md) is interpreted as those $\gamma^\mu$, rather than as three unmodified [Pauli matrices](../pauli-matrices.md). All [spin sums](../spin-sum.md) in question 2 instead use four-component [Dirac spinors](../dirac-spinor.md) in four spacetime dimensions.
