<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two [complex scalar fields](../../../../complex-scalar-field.md) have [scalar propagators](../../../../scalar-propagator.md)

$$
\langle T\phi\phi^\dagger\rangle(p)=\frac{i}{p^2-m^2+i\epsilon},\qquad
\langle T\Phi\Phi^\dagger\rangle(p)=\frac{i}{p^2-M^2+i\epsilon};
$$

contractions of two unconjugated fields or two conjugated fields vanish. Draw an arrow on each line in the direction of positive [conserved charge](../../../../conserved-charge.md) flow: a solid line carries one unit and a dashed line two units. A particle arrow follows its physical [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md); an [antiparticle](../../../../antiparticle.md) arrow opposes it.

There are two conjugate three-leg [interaction vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md), both with [Feynman rule](../../../../feynman-rule.md) $-ig$: one has a charge-two arrow entering and two charge-one arrows leaving, and the other reverses every arrow. These describe the two conjugate terms $\phi^2\Phi^\dagger$ and $\phi^{\dagger2}\Phi$. The two identical $\phi$ attachments cancel the $1/2$ in the [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md). All crossed assignments are obtained by moving external lines between initial and final states while maintaining the arrows. There is no [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) with three equal-sign charges.

Each [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) conserves [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md), supplying $(2\pi)^4\delta^4(\sum p_j)$ with all momenta entering. Integrate each independent [loop momentum](../../../../loop-momentum.md) with $d^4k/(2\pi)^4$ and divide by the [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). No minus sign is assigned to a closed scalar loop. These rules are for $i\mathcal M$ after amputating external lines and removing the overall [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) factor.
