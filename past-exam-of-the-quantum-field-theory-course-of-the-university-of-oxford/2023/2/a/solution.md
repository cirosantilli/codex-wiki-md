<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For nonzero real $g$, the continuous [internal symmetry of a classical field theory](../../../../internal-symmetry-of-a-classical-field-theory.md) is the [global U(1) symmetry](../../../../global-u-1-symmetry.md)

$$
\phi\longmapsto e^{-i\alpha}\phi,\qquad
\Phi\longmapsto e^{-2i\alpha}\Phi,
\qquad \alpha\text{ constant}.
$$

The [kinetic terms](../../../../kinetic-term.md) and mass terms are invariant separately, while the phases in $\phi^2\Phi^\dagger$ cancel. Independent phase rotations would instead require $2\alpha_\phi-\alpha_\Phi=0$ modulo $2\pi$, leaving one connected circle. Simultaneous [complex conjugation](../../../../complex-conjugation.md) is also a discrete symmetry, reversing the continuous charge; including it gives $U(1)\rtimes\mathbb Z_2\cong O(2)$ for generic parameters. At $g=0$ the continuous symmetry enlarges to $U(1)\times U(1)$.

The [Noether current](../../../../noether-current.md) with particles assigned positive charges is

$$
\boxed{j^\mu=i\bigl(\phi^\dagger\partial^\mu\phi-(\partial^\mu\phi^\dagger)\phi\bigr)
+2i\bigl(\Phi^\dagger\partial^\mu\Phi-(\partial^\mu\Phi^\dagger)\Phi\bigr).}
$$

For example, put $A=\phi^2\Phi^\dagger$ and $B=\phi^{\dagger2}\Phi$. The [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) give $\partial_\mu j_\phi^\mu=ig(A-B)$ and $\partial_\mu j_\Phi^\mu=ig(B-A)$, with the factor $2$ already included in $j_\Phi^\mu$. Their sum vanishes. The [Noether charge](../../../../noether-charge.md) $Q=\int d^3x\,j^0$ is conserved and, after [normal ordering](../../../../normal-ordering.md), counts

$$
Q=N_\phi-N_{\bar\phi}+2(N_\Phi-N_{\bar\Phi}).
$$

Thus a $\Phi$ particle carries two units of the same [conserved charge](../../../../conserved-charge.md) carried by a $\phi$ particle; their [antiparticles](../../../../antiparticle.md) carry the negatives. The interaction permits conversion of one $\Phi$ particle into two $\phi$ particles when allowed by [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md), while preserving this weighted number.
