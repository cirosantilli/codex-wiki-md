<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Label the incoming [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md) $p_1,p_2$ and the outgoing ones $p_3,p_4$. Define the [Mandelstam variables](../../../../../mandelstam-variables.md) $s=(p_1+p_2)^2$, $t=(p_1-p_3)^2$, $u=(p_1-p_4)^2$. Two incoming $\phi$ particles have [conserved charge](../../../../../conserved-charge.md) $+2$, so the only [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) is an $s$-channel $\Phi$ exchange, shown in panel (b) of [b](../../../1/b.md)'s figure. Each end converts two charge-one lines into one charge-two line. There are no $t$- or $u$-channel exchanges: an incoming and an outgoing particle at such an end would transfer zero charge, for which this theory has no cubic [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md).

$$
i\mathcal M_{\phi\phi}=(-ig)^2\frac{i}{s-M^2+i\epsilon},
\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal M_{\phi\phi}=-\frac{g^2}{s-M^2+i\epsilon}.}
$$

The single [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) is already symmetric under exchanging the two outgoing particles. This does not add another copy of the [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md); the [identical final-state symmetry factor](../../../../../identical-particle-factor-in-a-final-state-phase-space-integral.md) enters the integrated [scattering cross-section](../../../../../scattering-cross-section.md) instead.
