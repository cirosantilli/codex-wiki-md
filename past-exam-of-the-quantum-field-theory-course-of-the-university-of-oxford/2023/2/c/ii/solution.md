<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take $\phi(p_1)+\bar\phi(p_2)\to\phi(p_3)+\bar\phi(p_4)$ with the [Mandelstam variables](../../../../../mandelstam-variables.md) of [i](../i.md). The allowed [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) is the crossed $u$-channel $\Phi$ exchange in panel (c) of [b](../../../1/b.md)'s figure. At one end the incoming particle and outgoing [antiparticle](../../../../../antiparticle.md) transfer charge $+2$; the other end absorbs that charge. The zero-charge $s$- and $t$-channel assignments are forbidden by the [interaction vertices](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M_{\phi\bar\phi}=-\frac{g^2}{u-M^2+i\epsilon}.}
$$

In the [center-of-mass frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) with $|\mathbf p|\ll m$, $s=4m^2+O(|\mathbf p|^2)$ and $u=O(|\mathbf p|^2)$. For $M\gg m$, both [scattering amplitudes](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) approach $g^2/M^2$. The elastic two-body [Lorentz-invariant phase space](../../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) and incident flux agree for the two experiments. Using

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}=\frac{|\mathcal M|^2}{64\pi^2s}
$$

for distinguishable final particles, and an extra factor $1/2!$ when integrating the identical-particle final state over the whole sphere, gives

$$
\sigma_{\phi\phi}\simeq\frac{g^4}{32\pi sM^4},\qquad
\sigma_{\phi\bar\phi}\simeq\frac{g^4}{16\pi sM^4},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{\sigma_{\phi\phi}}{\sigma_{\phi\bar\phi}}\longrightarrow\frac12.}
$$

There is no additional division for identical incoming particles in the definition of a beam [scattering cross-section](../../../../../scattering-cross-section.md). Before taking $M/m\to\infty$, the zero-[momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) ratio is $\tfrac12[M^2/(M^2-4m^2)]^2$, provided the threshold is away from the heavy pole.

Near $s=M^2$, the bare $s$-channel [scalar propagator](../../../../../scalar-propagator.md) makes fixed-order [perturbation theory](../../../../../perturbation-theory.md) fail: repeated [self-energy](../../../../../self-energy.md) insertions contain powers of $\Sigma_\Phi(s)/(s-M^2)$. The $\Phi\to\phi\phi$ channel is open for $M>2m$, giving the leading [decay width](../../../../../decay-width.md)

$$
\Gamma_\Phi=\frac{g^2}{32\pi M}\sqrt{1-\frac{4m^2}{M^2}}.
$$

Resumming the [self-energy](../../../../../self-energy.md) gives a finite [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](../../../../../breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md), with denominator $s-M_{\rm pole}^2+iM_{\rm pole}\Gamma_\Phi$ near the pole, rather than an infinite [scattering cross-section](../../../../../scattering-cross-section.md). Also, $s\simeq M^2$ with $M\gg m$ lies outside the assumed nonrelativistic incoming kinematics; the ratio $1/2$ is not a resonance prediction.
