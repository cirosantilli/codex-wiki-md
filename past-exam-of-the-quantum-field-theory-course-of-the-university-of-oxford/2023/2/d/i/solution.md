<h1 id="2/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Four external $\Phi$ lines require four cubic [interaction vertices](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md), since each such [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) contains only one heavy field. Their other eight endpoints must form four light internal lines. A [connected Feynman diagram](../../../../../connected-feynman-diagram.md) therefore has $V=I=4$ and $L=1$: the leading contribution is a one-loop [box diagram](../../../../../box-diagram.md), of order $g^4$, with four $\phi$ [scalar propagators](../../../../../scalar-propagator.md). Charge-two incoming and outgoing heavy lines attach alternately around the box, as shown in panel (d) of [b](../../../1/b.md)'s figure. Each incoming heavy line supplies the two outgoing light-charge arrows at its corner, and each outgoing heavy line absorbs them. With external labels fixed there is one such box and its [symmetry factor](../../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) is one.

For $|\mathbf p|\ll M\ll m$, the external energies are also small compared with $m$, so expand the loop integrand in all external [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md). Its leading term is

$$
i\mathcal M_{\Phi\Phi}=g^4\int\frac{d^4k}{(2\pi)^4}\frac1{(k^2-m^2+i\epsilon)^4}
+O\left(\frac{i g^4M^2}{m^6}\right).
$$

A [Wick rotation](../../../../../wick-rotation.md) and the [Euclidean massive loop integral](../../../../../euclidean-massive-loop-integral.md) give

$$
\int\frac{d^4k}{(2\pi)^4}\frac1{(k^2-m^2+i\epsilon)^4}
=i\int\frac{d^4k_E}{(2\pi)^4}\frac1{(k_E^2+m^2)^4}
=\frac{i}{96\pi^2m^4}.
$$

Hence, more precisely than just its requested scaling,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M_{\Phi\Phi}=\frac{g^4}{96\pi^2m^4}
\left[1+O\left(\frac{M^2}{m^2},\frac{|\mathbf p|^2}{m^2}\right)\right].}
$$

The [mass dimension](../../../../../mass-dimension.md) $[g]=1$ makes $g^4/m^4$ dimensionless, as a four-dimensional [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) should be. The leading amplitude has no extra identical-particle multiplicity.
