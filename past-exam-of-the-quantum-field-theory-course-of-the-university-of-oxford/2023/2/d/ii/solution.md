<h1 id="2/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Cross the second incoming heavy particle and one outgoing heavy particle in [i](../i.md) to obtain $\Phi\bar\Phi\to\Phi\bar\Phi$. Panel (e) of [b](../../../1/b.md)'s figure shows the resulting [box diagram](../../../../../box-diagram.md). Its four light-charge arrows again alternate source and sink corners; it is the only connected order-$g^4$ topology. There is no tree exchange because every cubic [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) has only one heavy leg. With $|\mathbf p|\ll M\ll m$, crossing does not affect the zero-external-[momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) loop integral, so

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M_{\Phi\bar\Phi}=\frac{g^4}{96\pi^2m^4}
\left[1+O\left(\frac{M^2}{m^2},\frac{|\mathbf p|^2}{m^2}\right)\right].}
$$

An independent check is to integrate out the [complex scalar field](../../../../../complex-scalar-field.md) $\phi$ at constant $\Phi$. Its two real-component squared masses are $m^2\pm g|\Phi|$. The one-loop [quantum effective potential](../../../../../quantum-effective-potential.md) is therefore

$$
V_1(\Phi)=\frac12\int\frac{d^4k_E}{(2\pi)^4}
\left[\log(k_E^2+m^2+g|\Phi|)+\log(k_E^2+m^2-g|\Phi|)\right].
$$

Expanding the logarithms to fourth order in $\Phi$ gives

$$
V_1\supset-\frac{g^4|\Phi|^4}{4}\int\frac{d^4k_E}{(2\pi)^4}\frac1{(k_E^2+m^2)^4}
=-\frac{g^4}{384\pi^2m^4}(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)^2.
$$

Thus the [effective Lagrangian](../../../../../effective-lagrangian.md) has the positive local coefficient $g^4/(384\pi^2m^4)$. Its $2!2!$ external attachments give the common [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) $g^4/(96\pi^2m^4)$ in both channels. This also checks the box multiplicity and sign.
