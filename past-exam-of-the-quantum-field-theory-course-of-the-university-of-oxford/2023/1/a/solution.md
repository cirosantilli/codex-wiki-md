<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $\hbar=c=1$, the [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$, and $p^2=(p^0)^2-(p^1)^2$. The momentum-space [Feynman rules](../../../../feynman-rules.md) for the [real scalar field](../../../../real-scalar-field.md) are

$$
\boxed{\text{internal line: }\frac{i}{p^2-m^2+i\epsilon},\qquad
\text{four-leg vertex: }-ig_4.}
$$

At each [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) impose two-dimensional [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md); before removing the overall conservation factor this is $(2\pi)^2\delta^{(2)}(\sum_jp_j)$. Integrate each independent [loop momentum](../../../../loop-momentum.md) with $d^2k/(2\pi)^2$, and divide a [Feynman diagram](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) by its [symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). External lines carry the same [scalar propagator](../../../../scalar-propagator.md) in an unamputated [two-point correlation function](../../../../two-point-correlation-function.md); they are removed in an amputated [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md).

The quadratic [action](../../../../action.md) is inverted with the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](../../../../feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) to obtain the [scalar propagator](../../../../scalar-propagator.md). Expanding the [Dyson series](../../../../dyson-series.md) gives $-ig_4/4!$ per insertion; the $4!$ attachments in the [Wick theorem](../../../../wick-s-theorem.md) cancel the factorial. Fourier integration over the insertion point supplies the [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) factor. Automorphisms that leave the labelled external attachments fixed account for the remaining [symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). In two spacetime dimensions the [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md) are $[\phi]=0$ and $[g_4]=2$.
