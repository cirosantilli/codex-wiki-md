<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [real scalar field](../../../../real-scalar-field.md), the two derivatives entering the [Euler-Lagrange field equation](../../../../euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) are

$$
\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu\phi)}=\partial^\mu\phi,
\qquad
\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\phi}=-m^2\phi-\frac{\lambda}{6}\phi^3.
$$

Consequently the [equation of motion](../../../../equation-of-motion.md) is

$$
\boxed{(\Box+m^2)\phi+\frac{\lambda}{6}\phi^3=0.}
$$

The [quartic interaction](../../../../quartic-interaction.md) therefore adds a local cubic term to the free [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md). Below, write $F=(\Box+m^2)\phi+\lambda\phi^3/6$; thus $F=0$ means that the [field](../../../../field.md) is [on shell](../../../../on-shell.md).
