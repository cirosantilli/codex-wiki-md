<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Because the quartic [coupling constant](../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md) has [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) $\epsilon=4-D$, introduce a [renormalization scale](../../../../renormalization-scale.md) $\mu$ and define the dimensionless [renormalized coupling](../../../../renormalized-coupling.md) $g$ by [minimal subtraction scheme](../../../../minimal-subtraction-scheme.md):

$$
\boxed{\lambda_0=\mu^\epsilon\left[g+\frac{3g^2}{16\pi^2\epsilon}+O(g^3)\right].}
$$

Here $\lambda_0$ is the fixed bare [coupling constant](../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md). Equivalently the tree-level quartic term is $-\mu^\epsilon g\phi^4/4!$, and its one-loop [counterterm](../../../../counterterm.md) is $-\mu^\epsilon3g^2\phi^4/(16\pi^2\epsilon\,4!)$. This states explicitly the scale convention implicit in [solution](../c/solution.md).

Put $a=3/(16\pi^2)$. Differentiating the bare relation at fixed $\lambda_0$ gives

$$
0=\epsilon\left(g+\frac{ag^2}{\epsilon}\right)
+\widehat\beta(g)\left(1+\frac{2ag}{\epsilon}\right)+O(g^3).
$$

Write $\widehat\beta=-\epsilon g+b g^2+O(g^3)$. The coefficient of $g^2$ in the last identity is $a+b-2a$, so $b=a$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\widehat\beta(g)=-\epsilon g+\frac{3g^2}{16\pi^2}+O(g^3).}
$$

The first term is the [engineering term in a dimensionally continued beta function](../../../../engineering-term-in-a-dimensionally-continued-beta-function.md). In four dimensions it disappears, leaving the positive [one-loop quartic scalar beta function](../../../../one-loop-quartic-scalar-beta-function.md) $3g^2/(16\pi^2)$. Thus the [renormalized coupling](../../../../renormalized-coupling.md) increases with increasing [renormalization scale](../../../../renormalization-scale.md) near weak positive coupling. This quantum running is the obstruction to extending the classical [scale invariance](../../../../scale-invariance.md) in [solution](../../1/d/solution.md) to the interacting quantum theory.
