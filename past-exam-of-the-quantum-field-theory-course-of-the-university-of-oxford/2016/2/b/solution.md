<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The unit-normalized [Fock vacuum](../../../../fock-vacuum.md) is characterized by

$$
a_{\mathbf p}|0\rangle=0\quad\hbox{for every }\mathbf p,
\qquad \langle0|0\rangle=1.
$$

Choose the [relativistic normalization of a one-particle state](../../../../relativistic-normalization-of-a-one-particle-state.md) from the outset:

$$
|\mathbf p\rangle=\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf p}}\,a^\dagger_{\mathbf p}|0\rangle,
\qquad
|\mathbf p_1,\ldots,\mathbf p_n\rangle
=\prod_{r=1}^n\bigl(\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf p_r}}\,a^\dagger_{\mathbf p_r}\bigr)|0\rangle.
$$

Since any two [creation operators](../../../../creation-operator.md) commute, any [permutation](../../../../permutation.md) of their order gives exactly the same [state vector](../../../../state-vector.md). Thus these [multiparticle quantum states](../../../../multiparticle-quantum-state.md) are symmetric under exchange and describe [bosons](../../../../boson.md), without a minus sign or any additional exchange phase.

The [Hilbert space](../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) is the [bosonic Fock space](../../../../bosonic-fock-space.md), the direct sum of the symmetric $n$-particle [Hilbert spaces](../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), including the zero-particle sector. Sharp-[momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) kets are distributionally normalized; square-integrable [wave packets](../../../../wave-packet.md) provide ordinary [Hilbert space](../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) vectors. For repeated occupation of a discrete mode, a unit-normalized [number state](../../../../number-state.md) uses $(a^\dagger)^n|0\rangle/\sqrt{n!}$. That factorial does not belong in the above labelled continuum-ket convention.
