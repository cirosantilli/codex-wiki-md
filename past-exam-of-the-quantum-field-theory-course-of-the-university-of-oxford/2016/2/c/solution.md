<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [normal ordering](../../../../normal-ordering.md) of both [linear maps](../../../../linear-map.md) puts every [annihilation operator](../../../../annihilation-operator.md) to the right, so

$$
H|0\rangle=0,\qquad\mathbf P|0\rangle=\mathbf0.
$$

In particular the stated [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) has already subtracted the free [vacuum energy](../../../../vacuum-energy.md). For a [one-particle state](../../../../one-particle-state.md), evaluate the [commutators](../../../../commutator.md) using $[AB,C]=A[B,C]+[A,C]B$:

$$
[H,a^\dagger_{\mathbf k}]
=\int_{\mathbf p} E_{\mathbf p}a^\dagger_{\mathbf p}(2\pi)^3\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf k)
=E_{\mathbf k}a^\dagger_{\mathbf k},
\qquad
[\mathbf P,a^\dagger_{\mathbf k}]=\mathbf k\,a^\dagger_{\mathbf k}.
$$

Acting on the [Fock vacuum](../../../../fock-vacuum.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{H|\mathbf k\rangle=E_{\mathbf k}|\mathbf k\rangle,
\qquad\mathbf P|\mathbf k\rangle=\mathbf k|\mathbf k\rangle.}
$$

The extra factor $\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf k}}$ in the chosen [relativistic normalization of a one-particle state](../../../../relativistic-normalization-of-a-one-particle-state.md) is a scalar multiplier and does not change either [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md). Applying the same argument to a [multiparticle quantum state](../../../../multiparticle-quantum-state.md) gives additive total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md).
