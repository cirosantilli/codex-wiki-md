<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Fock vacuum](../../../../fock-vacuum.md) condition and the [creation operator](../../../../creation-operator.md) [commutator](../../../../commutator.md) give

$$
\langle\mathbf p|\mathbf q\rangle
=\sqrt{2E_{\mathbf p}\,2E_{\mathbf q}}\,
\langle0|a_{\mathbf p}a^\dagger_{\mathbf q}|0\rangle
=\boxed{2E_{\mathbf p}(2\pi)^3\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)}.
$$

Equality of the square-root product and $2E_{\mathbf p}$ holds on the support of the [Dirac delta function](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md).

The positive-energy [mass shell](../../../../mass-shell.md) has the [Lorentz invariant](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) measure

$$
d^4p\,\theta(p^0)\delta(p^2-m^2)=\frac{d^3p}{2E_{\mathbf p}}.
$$

Here both $p^2$ and the four-dimensional [volume element](../../../../volume-element.md) are invariant, and positive [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is preserved by a proper orthochronous [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md). Consequently the three-dimensional [Jacobian determinant](../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is $J=E_{\boldsymbol\Lambda p}/E_{\mathbf p}$. Transforming the [Dirac delta function](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) yields

$$
\delta^3(\boldsymbol\Lambda p-\boldsymbol\Lambda q)
=\frac{E_{\mathbf p}}{E_{\boldsymbol\Lambda p}}\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q),
$$

so its factor exactly cancels that of $2E_{\boldsymbol\Lambda p}$. Thus the displayed [inner product](../../../../inner-product-split.md) is invariant under simultaneous transformation of both [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md). This is also why the one-particle completeness measure is $d^3p/((2\pi)^3 2E_{\mathbf p})$.[Section 2.4.1, relativistic normalization and the positive-energy mass-shell measure](../../../../quantum-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
