<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let brackets denote [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md). In [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md), $[x]=-1$ and $[\partial_\mu]=1$, so a dimensionless [action](../../../../action.md) requires $[\mathcal L]=D$. The [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) fixes

$$
2+2[\phi]=D,
\qquad
\boxed{[\phi]=\frac{D-2}{2}.}
$$

The [cubic scalar field theory](../../../../phi-cubed-theory.md) interaction then requires $[\lambda]+3[\phi]=D$, giving

$$
\boxed{[\lambda]=\frac{6-D}{2},\qquad D_c=6,\qquad[\phi]_{D_c}=2.}
$$

The [coupling constant](../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md) is dimensionless at this [critical dimension of a monomial scalar interaction](../../../../critical-dimension-of-a-monomial-scalar-interaction.md). For $D<6$ it has positive [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md), while for $D>6$ it has negative [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md), the usual [power counting](../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md) distinction between super-renormalizable and non-renormalizable cubic interactions.
