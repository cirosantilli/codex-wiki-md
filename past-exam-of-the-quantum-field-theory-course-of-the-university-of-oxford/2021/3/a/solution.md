Here three means the total spacetime dimension. A dimensionless [action](../../../../action.md) requires $[\mathcal L]=3$ in [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md). The [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) gives $2+2[\phi]=3$, so $[\phi]=1/2$. Matching the [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md) of both [field interaction terms](../../../../field-interaction-term.md) gives

$$
\boxed{[g_1]=3-4[\phi]=1,\qquad [g_2]=3-6[\phi]=0.}
$$

Thus $g_1$ has units of [mass](../../../../mass.md) and $g_2$ is dimensionless. The quartic interaction is superrenormalizable by [power counting](../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md), and $g_2$ is a [marginal coupling](../../../../marginal-coupling.md) by [power counting](../../../../power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md).
