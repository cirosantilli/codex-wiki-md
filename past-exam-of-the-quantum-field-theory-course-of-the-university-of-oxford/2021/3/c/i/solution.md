<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With a single quartic [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md), the two unused line ends close into one [tadpole diagram](../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md). With a single sextic [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md), the four unused ends pair into two [tadpole diagrams](../../../../../tadpole-diagram.md). These are the only one-vertex [one-particle irreducible](../../../../../one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md) [Feynman diagrams](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) for the [two-point function](../../../../../two-point-correlation-function.md).

Define the Minkowski [Feynman integrals](../../../../../feynman-integral.md)

$$
D(k)=\frac{i}{k^2-m^2+i0},\qquad
T_M=\int\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}D(k).
$$

The amputated [two-point function](../../../../../two-point-correlation-function.md) contributions are

$$
\boxed{C_4(-ig_1)T_M,\qquad C_6(-ig_2)T_M^2,}
$$

where $C_4,C_6$ are the reciprocals of the corresponding [Feynman-diagram symmetry factors](../../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). Both are independent of external [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), and hence affect the [mass counterterm](../../../../../mass-counterterm.md) rather than [wave-function renormalization](../../../../../wave-function-renormalization.md). No numerical [Feynman-diagram symmetry factors](../../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) are needed here.
