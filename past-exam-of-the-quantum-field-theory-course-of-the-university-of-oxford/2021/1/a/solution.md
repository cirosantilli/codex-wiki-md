<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [momentum space](../../../../momentum-space.md) [Feynman rules](../../../../feynman-rules.md) use the [scalar propagator](../../../../scalar-propagator.md) and two [Dirac propagators](../../../../dirac-propagator.md)

$$
\boxed{\Delta_\phi(p)=\frac{i}{p^2-m^2+i0},\qquad
S_\chi(p)=\frac{i(\not p+m)}{p^2-m^2+i0},\qquad
S_\Psi(p)=\frac{i(\not p+M)}{p^2-M^2+i0}.}
$$

The cubic [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md) [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) is $-i\lambda$. Each of the two flavour-changing [Yukawa coupling](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) [interaction vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md), $\phi\bar\Psi\chi$ and $\phi\bar\chi\Psi$, has factor $-ig\,\delta_{ab}$ in [Dirac spinor](../../../../dirac-spinor.md) indices. A [fermion flow](../../../../fermion-flow.md) arrow continues through each [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md), but its species changes. There is no $\phi\bar\Psi\Psi$ or $\phi\bar\chi\chi$ [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md).

Every [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) carries $(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(\sum p)$ with inward [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md). Integrate each independent [loop momentum](../../../../loop-momentum.md) with $d^4\ell/(2\pi)^4$, divide by the [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md), and include a minus sign and a [gamma-matrix trace](../../../../gamma-matrix-trace.md) for each closed [fermion loop](../../../../fermion-loop.md). The $i0$ is the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](../../../../feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md).

For an amputated [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md), incoming [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) supply $u^s(p)$ and outgoing [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) supply $\bar u^s(p)$; incoming [antifermions](../../../../antifermion.md) supply $\bar v^s(p)$ and outgoing [antifermions](../../../../antifermion.md) supply $v^s(p)$. An external [real scalar field](../../../../real-scalar-field.md) particle supplies $1$. Use the standard [relativistic normalization of a one-particle state](../../../../relativistic-normalization-of-a-one-particle-state.md); the external factors $1/\sqrt{2E}$ belong to that normalization and the [Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space-measure.md), rather than an additional factor in the invariant [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md).
