<h1 id="1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $\Psi(p_1)\to\chi(p_2)+\phi(k_1)+\phi(k_2)$, the sole [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) has a [Yukawa coupling](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) producing an off-shell [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) particle of [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) $q=p_1-p_2=k_1+k_2$, followed by its cubic [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md). Its invariant [decay amplitude](../../../../../decay-amplitude.md) is

$$
\boxed{i\mathcal M=(-ig)(-i\lambda)\,
\bar u_\chi(p_2)u_\Psi(p_1)\,
\frac{i}{(k_1+k_2)^2-m^2+i0}.}
$$

Two successive [Yukawa coupling](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) [interaction vertices](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) would change the [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) species twice and leave a $\Psi$, so they cannot produce this final state. Interchanging the two identical outgoing [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) does not create another [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md); the cubic factorial is already included in the [Feynman rule](../../../../../feynman-rule.md). The integrated [decay width](../../../../../decay-width.md) instead includes the [identical final-state symmetry factor](../../../../../identical-particle-factor-in-a-final-state-phase-space-integral.md) $1/2!$.
