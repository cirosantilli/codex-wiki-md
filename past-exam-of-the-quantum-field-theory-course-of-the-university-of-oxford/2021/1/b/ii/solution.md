<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\Psi(p_1)+\phi(k_1)\to\Psi(p_2)+\phi(k_2)$, there are two [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md), both with an internal $\chi$ [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md). Their internal [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md) are $p_1+k_1$ and $p_1-k_2$. The [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) is

$$
i\mathcal M=(-ig)^2\bar u_\Psi(p_2)
\left[S_\chi(p_1+k_1)+S_\chi(p_1-k_2)\right]u_\Psi(p_1).
$$

These are the two possible absorption/emission orderings along the [fermion flow](../../../../../fermion-flow.md). There is no third [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) using the cubic [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md), since attaching its remaining [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) line to the external $\Psi$ line would require a forbidden $\phi\bar\Psi\Psi$ [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md).
