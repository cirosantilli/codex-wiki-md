<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $\Psi(p_1)\to\chi(p_2)+\phi(p_3)$, the sole [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) is a single flavour-changing [Yukawa coupling](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md), with $p_1=p_2+p_3$. Its invariant [decay amplitude](../../../../../decay-amplitude.md) is

$$
\boxed{i\mathcal M_{s_1s_2}=-ig\,\bar u_\chi^{s_2}(p_2)u_\Psi^{s_1}(p_1).}
$$

The solid arrow passes from the incoming $\Psi$ to the outgoing $\chi$; the dashed outgoing line carries $p_3$.
