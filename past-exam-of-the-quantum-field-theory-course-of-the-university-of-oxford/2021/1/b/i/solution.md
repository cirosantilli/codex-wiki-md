<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Label the external [four-momenta](../../../../../four-momentum.md) by $\Psi(p_1)+\chi(p_2)\to\Psi(p_3)+\chi(p_4)$. The only [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) exchanges a [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) particle between the transitions $\Psi(p_1)\to\chi(p_4)$ and $\chi(p_2)\to\Psi(p_3)$. Its internal [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) is $q=p_1-p_4=p_3-p_2$. Thus its [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md), with a consistent external-state ordering, is

$$
i\mathcal M=(-ig)^2\,[\bar u_\chi(p_4)u_\Psi(p_1)]\,
\frac{i}{(p_1-p_4)^2-m^2+i0}\,
[\bar u_\Psi(p_3)u_\chi(p_2)].
$$

It is the crossed, or $u$-channel, exchange with these labels. A direct $t$-channel [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) exchange would need a flavour-preserving [Yukawa coupling](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md), which the [Lagrangian density](../../../../../lagrangian-density.md) does not contain. There is no annihilation [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) for two incoming [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md).
