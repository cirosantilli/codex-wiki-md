<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the convention of [iii](../b/iii.md), the invariant [decay amplitude](../../../../decay-amplitude.md) is $\mathcal M_{s_1s_2}=-g\bar u_\chi^{s_2}(p_2)u_\Psi^{s_1}(p_1)$. Including the common overall $i$ in the definition changes no squared [decay amplitude](../../../../decay-amplitude.md). An unpolarized ensemble is an incoherent equal mixture of the two initial [spin](../../../../spin.md) states; hence take a [spin average](../../../../spin-average.md) over $s_1$ and a [spin sum](../../../../spin-sum.md) over the unobserved final $s_2$. Different final [spin](../../../../spin.md) states are orthogonal outcomes, so their probabilities, rather than their [decay amplitudes](../../../../decay-amplitude.md), add.

Using the [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) and the [fermion spin sum](../../../../fermion-spin-sum.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}
&=\frac{g^2}{2}\sum_{s_1,s_2}
(\bar u_2u_1)(\bar u_1u_2)\\
&=\frac{g^2}{2}\operatorname{tr}[(\not p_2+m)(\not p_1+M)]
=2g^2(p_1\cdot p_2+mM).
\end{aligned}
$$

The [gamma-matrix trace](../../../../gamma-matrix-trace.md) identities used here are $\operatorname{tr}\gamma^\mu=0$ and $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)=4\eta^{\mu\nu}$. The [mass-shell conditions](../../../../string-mass-shell-condition.md) $p_1^2=M^2$ and $p_2^2=p_3^2=m^2$, together with [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md), imply

$$
m^2=(p_1-p_2)^2=M^2+m^2-2p_1\cdot p_2,
\qquad p_1\cdot p_2=\frac{M^2}{2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=g^2M(M+2m).}
$$

In the parent's rest frame this also says $E_2=E_3=M/2$.
