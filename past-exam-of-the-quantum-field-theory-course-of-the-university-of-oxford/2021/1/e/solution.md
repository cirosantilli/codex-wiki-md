<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Put $Q=M-3m$. For $Q<0$ the [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) is zero by [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md). Just above the three-particle [decay threshold](../../../../decay-threshold.md), all three daughters are nonrelativistic. The [decay amplitude](../../../../decay-amplitude.md) of [iv](../b/iv.md) remains finite: the internal [scalar propagator](../../../../scalar-propagator.md) denominator approaches $(2m)^2-m^2=3m^2$, and the [Dirac scalar bilinear](../../../../dirac-scalar-bilinear.md) does not vanish at rest. Thus, for nonzero fixed $g$ and $\lambda$, the leading suppression comes entirely from [three-body threshold phase space](../../../../three-body-threshold-phase-space.md).

Use the spatial [Dirac delta function](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) to eliminate one daughter [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md). The remaining integral has six independent [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) coordinates and one [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) constraint,

$$
\int d^3p\,d^3q\,
\delta\!\left(Q-\frac{p^2+q^2+(\mathbf p+\mathbf q)^2}{2m}\right).
$$

Rescaling both [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) by $\sqrt{mQ}$ gives $Q^3$ from the measure and $Q^{-1}$ from the [Dirac delta function](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md). The smooth factors $1/(2E)$ do not change the power. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\Gamma=C(M,g,\lambda)\,(M-3m)^2[1+O((M-3m)/M)]\quad\text{as }m\uparrow M/3,}
$$

with a finite positive leading coefficient when $g\lambda\ne0$; $\Gamma=0$ for $m\geq M/3$. The $1/2!$ for the identical [bosons](../../../../boson.md) changes only this coefficient. Equivalently, at fixed $M$ the opening is quadratic in $M/3-m$. If $g\lambda=0$, this leading [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) vanishes, so the stated leading coefficient is zero.
