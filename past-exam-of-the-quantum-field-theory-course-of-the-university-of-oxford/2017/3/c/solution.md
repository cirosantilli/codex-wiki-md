<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [propagator](../../../../propagator.md) carries a real-species label rather than a charge-flow arrow:

$$
\boxed{D_{ab}(p)=\frac{i\delta_{ab}}{p^2-m_0^2+i0}.}
$$

Differentiating the interaction with respect to its four external real [fields](../../../../field.md) gives the compact [tensor](../../../../tensor.md) rule

$$
\boxed{v_{abcd}=-\frac{i\lambda_0}{2}
(\delta_{ab}\delta_{cd}+\delta_{ac}\delta_{bd}+\delta_{ad}\delta_{bc}).}
$$

Thus four $\phi_1$ legs or four $\phi_2$ legs give $-3i\lambda_0/2$, while two of each give $-i\lambda_0/2$. Vertices with an odd number of either species vanish. For example, $4!(\lambda_0/16)=3\lambda_0/2$, whereas $2!2!(2\lambda_0/16)=\lambda_0/2$. These factors already account for assignments at one vertex; graph [symmetry factors](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) must still be included separately. [Momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) conservation and loop measures are the same as in part a.
