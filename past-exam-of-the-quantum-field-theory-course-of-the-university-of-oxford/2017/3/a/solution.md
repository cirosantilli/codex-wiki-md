<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A free contraction must connect one $\phi$ and one $\phi^*$ [field](../../../../field.md). In [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) space,

$$
\boxed{\langle T\phi(p)\phi^*(-p)\rangle_0=\frac{i}{p^2-m_0^2+i0},\qquad
\langle T\phi\phi\rangle_0=\langle T\phi^*\phi^*\rangle_0=0.}
$$

Represent the nonzero [propagator](../../../../propagator.md) by a directed line so the two conjugate ends remain distinguishable; an undirected line alone would incorrectly allow same-charge contractions. Choose one consistent arrow convention, for example pointing from the starred end to the unstarred end.

The interaction contains two identical $\phi$ [fields](../../../../field.md) and two identical $\phi^*$ [fields](../../../../field.md). Their $2!2!$ assignments cancel the coefficient $1/4$, giving

$$
\boxed{v_{\phi\phi\phi^*\phi^*}=-i\lambda_0.}
$$

The vertex has two incoming and two outgoing arrows. Include $(2\pi)^d\delta^d(\sum p_i)$ at every vertex, integrate $d^dk/(2\pi)^d$ for each independent loop, and divide by the oriented graph's [symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). There is no fermion-loop sign.
