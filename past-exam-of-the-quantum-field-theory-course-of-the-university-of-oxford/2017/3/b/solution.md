<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two real [fields](../../../../field.md) have canonical normalization because

$$
\phi^*\phi=\frac{\phi_1^2+\phi_2^2}{2},\qquad
\partial_\mu\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi=\frac12\sum_{a=1}^2\partial_\mu\phi_a\partial^\mu\phi_a.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L=\frac12\sum_{a=1}^2\left[(\partial\phi_a)^2-m_0^2\phi_a^2\right]
-\frac{\lambda_0}{16}\left(\phi_1^4+2\phi_1^2\phi_2^2+\phi_2^4\right).}
$$

The interaction is invariant under $O(2)$ rotations and reflections of $(\phi_1,\phi_2)$. The continuous rotations are the original complex-field $U(1)$ [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md). The factor $1/16$ follows from both the original $1/4$ and the square of the $1/2$ in $\phi^*\phi$.
