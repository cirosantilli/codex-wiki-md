<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At fixed external [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md), fixed regulator and fixed bare [coupling](../../../../coupling.md), define the total scale derivative

$$
\mathcal D=\mu\frac{\partial}{\partial\mu}+\beta(g)\frac{\partial}{\partial g}.
$$

The bare vertex has no independent dependence on the arbitrary [renormalization scale](../../../../renormalization-scale.md). Differentiating $\Gamma^{(n)}=Z_\phi^{n/2}\Gamma_0^{(n)}$ therefore gives

$$
\mathcal D\Gamma^{(n)}=\frac n2\left(\mu\frac{d\log Z_\phi}{d\mu}\right)_{\mathrm{bare}}\Gamma^{(n)}.
$$

Defining the two scale-response functions as in the source yields

$$
\boxed{\left[\mu\partial_\mu+\beta(g)\partial_g-\frac n2\gamma(g)\right]\Gamma^{(n)}=0.}
$$

This is the [Callan-Symanzik equation](../../../../callan-symanzik-equation.md). Its minus sign and factor $n/2$ follow from the specified $Z_\phi^{n/2}$ multiplying the bare vertex. In particular, the source's $\gamma=\mu\,d\log Z_\phi/d\mu$ is twice the [field](../../../../field.md) [anomalous dimension](../../../../anomalous-dimension.md) defined using $\log Z_\phi^{1/2}$. A convention change must be made consistently in both definitions and equation.
