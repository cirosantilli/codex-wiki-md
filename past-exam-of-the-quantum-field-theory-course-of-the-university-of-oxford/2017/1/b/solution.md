<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a constant real parameter, $\partial_\mu(e^{i\alpha}\phi)=e^{i\alpha}\partial_\mu\phi$. The phases therefore cancel separately in the [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) and in $\phi^*\phi$, leaving any $f(\phi^*\phi)$ unchanged. This is the [global phase symmetry of a complex scalar field](../../../../global-phase-symmetry-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md), with [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md) $U(1)$.

Use infinitesimal variations per unit parameter, $\delta\phi=i\phi$, $\delta\phi^*=-i\phi^*$. The [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) is exactly invariant, so no total-derivative correction is needed in [Noether's theorem](../../../../noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md). The [Noether current](../../../../noether-current.md) is

$$
\boxed{J^\mu=i(\partial^\mu\phi^*)\phi-i\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi.}
$$

It depends only on derivatives of the [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) with respect to [field](../../../../field.md) derivatives, so the derivative-free [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md) cannot alter its form. Direct differentiation cancels the mixed first-derivative terms:

$$
\partial_\mu J^\mu=i(\Box\phi^*)\phi-i\phi^*\Box\phi
=-if'(s)\phi^*\phi+if'(s)\phi^*\phi=0.
$$

Thus it is conserved on solutions of the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md). Its spatial integral is a conserved [Noether charge](../../../../noether-charge.md) when the [flux](../../../../flux.md) through the boundary vanishes. Its overall sign is fixed here by $\delta\phi=+i\phi$; reversing the orientation of the phase transformation reverses the current.
