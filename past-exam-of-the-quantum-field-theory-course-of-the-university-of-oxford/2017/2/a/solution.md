<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In $d$ [spacetime](../../../../spacetime.md) dimensions, $[d^dx]=-d$ and a dimensionless [action](../../../../action.md) requires $[\mathcal L]=d$. Since $[\partial_\mu]=1$, the canonical [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) fixes $2+2[\phi]=d$. The cubic and quartic terms then give

$$
\boxed{[\phi]=\frac{d-2}{2},\qquad[\kappa]=\frac{6-d}{2},\qquad[\lambda]=4-d.}
$$

These brackets denote [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md). The cubic [coupling](../../../../coupling.md) is dimensionless at $d=6$, whereas the quartic [coupling](../../../../coupling.md) is dimensionless at $d=4$. Hence there is **no single dimension** in which both nonzero [couplings](../../../../coupling.md) are dimensionless. For example, at $d=4$ the cubic [coupling](../../../../coupling.md) still has [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) one.[Section 3.1, dimensional analysis of scalar interactions](../../../../quantum-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
