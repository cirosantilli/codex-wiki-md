<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is invariant under the constant [global phase symmetry](../../../../global-phase-symmetry.md) $\psi\mapsto e^{i\theta}\psi$, $\bar\psi\mapsto\bar\psi e^{-i\theta}$. The [Noether current](../../../../noether-current.md) of this [unitary group](../../../../unitary-group.md) $U(1)$ is the [Dirac current](../../../../dirac-current.md) obtained in [b](../b.md). For fields with vanishing flux at infinity, or periodic boundary conditions, the [divergence theorem](../../../../divergence-theorem.md) and that [continuity equation](../../../../continuity-equation.md) imply

$$
\boxed{Q=\int d^3x\,:\!\psi^\dagger\psi\!:\ ,\qquad [Q,H]=0,\qquad \frac{dQ}{dt}=0.}
$$

The [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) also give $[Q,\psi]=-\psi$ and $[Q,\psi^\dagger]=\psi^\dagger$, hence $e^{i\theta Q}\psi e^{-i\theta Q}=e^{-i\theta}\psi$. Reversing the parameter sign recovers the stated phase convention. Thus $Q$ generates the [global phase symmetry](../../../../global-phase-symmetry.md). With [normal ordering](../../../../normal-ordering.md), $Q$ counts [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) minus [antiparticles](../../../../antiparticle.md), rather than the sum of both populations. Its [conservation law](../../../../conservation-law.md) is [Dirac fermion number conservation](../../../../dirac-fermion-number-conservation.md). A position-dependent phase produces derivative terms in this free [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md); the symmetry under discussion is global.
