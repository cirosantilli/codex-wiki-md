<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Combine the two equal-[mass](../../../../mass.md) [Dirac fields](../../../../dirac-field.md) into $\Psi=(\psi^1,\psi^2)^T$. A constant [unitary matrix](../../../../unitary-matrix.md) $U$ acting on this flavour index commutes with the common [Dirac Hamiltonian](../../../../dirac-hamiltonian.md), so $\Psi\mapsto U\Psi$ gives the [unitary flavour symmetry of degenerate Dirac fields](../../../../unitary-flavour-symmetry-of-degenerate-dirac-fields.md) $U(2)$. Its real [Lie algebra](../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) has dimension four. A convenient Hermitian generator basis is $T^0=\mathbf1_2/2$, $T^a=\tau^a/2$, $a=1,2,3$, where $\tau^a$ are the [Pauli matrices](../../../../pauli-matrices.md).

For each generator the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) give

$$
\rho_A=\Psi^\dagger T^A\Psi,\qquad
j_A^k=\bar\Psi\gamma^kT^A\Psi,\qquad
[\rho_A(\mathbf x),H]=-i\partial_k j_A^k(\mathbf x).
$$

The calculation is exactly the one in [b](../b.md), since $T^A$ commutes with both the [Dirac gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) and the common [fermion mass matrix](../../../../fermion-mass-matrix.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{Four independent number-preserving vector currents:}\qquad
j_A^\mu=\bar\Psi\gamma^\mu T^A\Psi,\quad A=0,1,2,3.}
$$

Explicitly their densities are

$$
\rho_0=\tfrac12(\psi^{1\dagger}\psi^1+\psi^{2\dagger}\psi^2),\qquad
\rho_3=\tfrac12(\psi^{1\dagger}\psi^1-\psi^{2\dagger}\psi^2),
$$



$$
\rho_1=\tfrac12(\psi^{1\dagger}\psi^2+\psi^{2\dagger}\psi^1),\qquad
\rho_2=\tfrac12(-i\psi^{1\dagger}\psi^2+i\psi^{2\dagger}\psi^1).
$$

The two off-diagonal [conserved currents](../../../../conserved-current.md) are why counting only the individual [fermion numbers](../../../../fermion-number.md) misses two generators. The singlet generates $U(1)$; the other three generate [special unitary group](../../../../special-unitary-group.md) $SU(2)$. Unequal [masses](../../../../mass.md) would break the off-diagonal [conservation laws](../../../../conservation-law.md).

**Scope of the count.** Four is the count of currents of the displayed $\bar\psi^A\gamma^\mu T_{AB}\psi^B$ form. The completely free system also has an [enhanced internal symmetry of free Dirac fields](../../../../enhanced-internal-symmetry-of-free-dirac-fields.md) if mixing with [charge-conjugate fields](../../../../charge-conjugate-field.md) is admitted. For $m\ne0$, write its two [Dirac fields](../../../../dirac-field.md) as four equal-[mass](../../../../mass.md) [Majorana spinors](../../../../majorana-spinor.md) $\chi^I$. Their [orthogonal group](../../../../orthogonal-group.md) $O(4)$ rotations have six continuous generators, with [conserved currents](../../../../conserved-current.md) proportional to $i\bar\chi^I\gamma^\mu\chi^J$ for $I<J$; the four $U(2)$ currents form a subgroup. This larger count is relevant if “similar” means _all_ local internal currents of the free system, without preserving a chosen [fermion number](../../../../fermion-number.md). If $m=0$, the usual number-preserving [chiral flavour symmetry of massless Dirac fields](../../../../chiral-flavour-symmetry-of-massless-dirac-fields.md) is $U(2)_L\times U(2)_R$ with eight currents; allowing [charge conjugation](../../../../charge-conjugation.md) mixing enlarges it to $U(4)$ with sixteen. These counts concern internal currents, excluding spacetime [Noether currents](../../../../noether-current.md).
