<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In [momentum space](../../../../momentum-space.md), the [Feynman rules](../../../../feynman-rules.md) for the [real scalar field](../../../../real-scalar-field.md) are

$$
\boxed{\text{internal line: }\frac{i}{p^2-m^2+i0},\qquad
\text{cubic vertex: }-ig_1,\qquad
\text{quartic vertex: }-ig_2.}
$$

Each [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) supplies $(2\pi)^6\delta^{(6)}(\sum p)$, with all its [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) counted inward. Impose [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md), integrate each independent [loop momentum](../../../../loop-momentum.md) with $d^6p/(2\pi)^6$, and divide by the [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). For a connected amputated [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md), remove the external [scalar propagators](../../../../scalar-propagator.md) and retain a single overall [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) factor. These signs follow by expanding $\exp(i\int\mathcal L_{\mathrm{int}})$; the factorials in the [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) cancel the corresponding attachments in the [Wick theorem](../../../../wick-s-theorem.md). The [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](../../../../feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) specifies the [scalar propagator](../../../../scalar-propagator.md) poles.
