<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A dimensionless [action](../../../../action.md) in six spacetime dimensions requires the [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) to have [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md) six. The [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md) then fixes $2+2[\phi]=6$, and each [field interaction term](../../../../field-interaction-term.md) must have the same [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{[\phi]=2,\qquad [g_1]=0,\qquad [g_2]=-2.}
$$

Equivalently, in powers of a length unit $L$, $\phi$ has units $L^{-2}$, $g_1$ has units $L^0$, and $g_2$ has units $L^2$. Thus literal _length exponents_ would be $(-2,0,2)$; the $d$'s in the supplied [superficial degree of divergence](../../../../superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) formula must instead mean [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md), namely $(2,0,-2)$. This distinction resolves the wording's convention mismatch.

To check the convention independently, let $I,V_3,V_4,\ell$ count internal lines, cubic [interaction vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md), quartic [interaction vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md), and independent [loop momenta](../../../../loop-momentum.md). A connected [Feynman diagram](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) obeys $2I+n=3V_3+4V_4$ and $\ell=I-V_3-V_4+1$. Hence its [superficial degree of divergence](../../../../superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) is $6\ell-2I=6-2n+2V_4$, agreeing with the supplied formula only with the [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md) above.
