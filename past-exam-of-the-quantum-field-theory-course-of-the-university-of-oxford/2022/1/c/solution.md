<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Set $g=g_1$ and $g_2=0$. The [superficial degree of divergence](../../../../superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) is then $\omega=6-2n$, independently of the number of cubic [interaction vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md). After subtracting [ultraviolet subdivergences](../../../../ultraviolet-subdivergence.md), the divergent part of a [one-particle irreducible](../../../../one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md) [Feynman diagram](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) is a local [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) in external [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) of degree at most $\omega$. [Lorentz invariance](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) and [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) leave the following possibilities:

- $n=0$: a constant [vacuum energy density](../../../../vacuum-energy-density.md).
- $n=1$: a linear [tadpole counterterm](../../../../tadpole-counterterm.md). The single external [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is zero, so derivatives cannot generate further independent terms.
- $n=2$: a [mass counterterm](../../../../mass-counterterm.md) and a [wave-function renormalization](../../../../wave-function-renormalization.md), since the [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) has the form $A+Bp^2$.
- $n=3$: a constant cubic [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md).
- $n\geq4$: no overall [ultraviolet divergence](../../../../ultraviolet-divergence.md), although [ultraviolet subdivergences](../../../../ultraviolet-subdivergence.md) still require their lower-point [counterterms](../../../../counterterm.md).

Thus a finite set of [counterterms](../../../../counterterm.md) closes under [perturbation theory](../../../../perturbation-theory.md). In terms of renormalized coefficients, write

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L+\mathcal L_{\mathrm{ct}}
=\frac{1+\delta_Z}{2}(\partial\phi)^2
-\frac{m^2+\delta_m}{2}\phi^2
-\frac{g+\delta_g}{3!}\phi^3-\delta_h\phi-\delta_\mathcal E.}
$$

Here $\delta_m$ is a coefficient of squared [mass](../../../../mass.md), rather than a correction to $m$ itself. The [tadpole counterterm](../../../../tadpole-counterterm.md) fixes the chosen expansion point, for example $\langle\phi\rangle=0$; it cannot be omitted merely because the original [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) lacks a linear term. The [vacuum energy density](../../../../vacuum-energy-density.md) term cancels out of normalized [Green functions](../../../../green-s-function-split.md), but is required if the unnormalized [vacuum functional](../../../../vacuum-functional.md) is also to be renormalized. This establishes perturbative [renormalizability](../../../../renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md); the unbounded [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md) of a real cubic [scalar field theory](../../../../scalar-field-theory-split.md) does not by itself define a stable nonperturbative [vacuum state](../../../../vacuum-state.md).
