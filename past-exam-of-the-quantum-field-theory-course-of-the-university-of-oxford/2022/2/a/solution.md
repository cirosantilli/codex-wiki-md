<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [auxiliary field](../../../../auxiliary-field.md) $\sigma$ has quadratic kernel $-1$ and no [kinetic term](../../../../kinetic-term.md). Inverting that kernel, rather than introducing an artificial [scalar mass](../../../../scalar-mass.md), gives the [momentum space](../../../../momentum-space.md) [Feynman rules](../../../../feynman-rules.md)

$$
\boxed{\sigma\text{ line: }-i,\qquad
\psi\text{ line: }\frac{i(\not p+m)}{p^2-m^2+i0},\qquad
\sigma\bar\psi\psi\text{ vertex: }ig.}
$$

The [auxiliary-field propagator](../../../../auxiliary-field-propagator.md) is independent of [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md); in position space it is $-i\delta^{(4)}(x-y)$. At each [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) attach $(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(\sum p)$ for [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md). Integrate independent [loop momenta](../../../../loop-momentum.md) with $d^4p/(2\pi)^4$, respect [fermion flow](../../../../fermion-flow.md), include the [closed fermion loop sign](../../../../closed-fermion-loop-sign.md) $-1$, and use the appropriate [symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). Incoming and outgoing [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) carry $u_s(p)$ and $\bar u_s(p)$; incoming and outgoing [antiparticles](../../../../antiparticle.md) carry $\bar v_s(p)$ and $v_s(p)$. Exchanging external [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) introduces the [fermionic sign](../../../../fermionic-sign.md). Here $\not p=\gamma^\mu p_\mu$ is [Feynman slash notation](../../../../feynman-slash-notation.md).

For a dimensional check, $[\psi]=3/2$, $[\sigma]=2$, $[g]=-1$ in [mass dimensions](../../../../mass-dimension.md). Eliminating $\sigma$ consequently produces a [four-fermion interaction](../../../../four-fermion-interaction.md) of negative coupling [mass dimension](../../../../mass-dimension.md), not a propagating scalar degree of freedom.
