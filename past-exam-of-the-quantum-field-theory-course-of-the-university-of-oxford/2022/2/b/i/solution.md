↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Label the incoming [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) [momenta](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $p_1,p_2$ and outgoing [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) [momenta](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $p_3,p_4$, with $p_1+p_2=p_3+p_4$. There are two [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md): the $t$ channel connects $1$ to $3$ and $2$ to $4$, with exchanged [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $q_t=p_1-p_3=p_4-p_2$; the $u$ channel connects $1$ to $4$ and $2$ to $3$, with $q_u=p_1-p_4=p_3-p_2$. Both internal lines are [auxiliary-field propagators](../../../../../auxiliary-field-propagator.md). The upper row in the figure gives these labelled connections; the outgoing labels are exchanged in its second panel to avoid a visually unnecessary crossing.

Each [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) contains two factors $ig$ and one factor $-i$, giving $ig^2$. The exchange of the two identical outgoing [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) gives the relative [fermionic sign](../../../../../fermionic-sign.md). Up to a common external-state phase, the corresponding [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M_{ff}=g^2\left[(\bar u_3u_1)(\bar u_4u_2)-(\bar u_4u_1)(\bar u_3u_2)\right].}
$$

There is no annihilation channel: both incoming particles carry the same [fermion number](../../../../../fermion-number.md), whereas a $\sigma\bar\psi\psi$ [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) couples one [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) to one [antiparticle](../../../../../antiparticle.md) when both are incoming.
