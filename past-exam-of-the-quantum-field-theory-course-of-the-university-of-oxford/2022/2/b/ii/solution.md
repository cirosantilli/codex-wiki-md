<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use incoming [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) $p_1$ and [antiparticle](../../../../../antiparticle.md) $p_2$, outgoing [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) $p_3$ and [antiparticle](../../../../../antiparticle.md) $p_4$. The two [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) are the $t$ channel, with $q_t=p_1-p_3=p_4-p_2$, and the $s$ channel, with $q_s=p_1+p_2=p_3+p_4$. They occupy the lower row of the figure. In the $t$ channel the [fermion flow](../../../../../fermion-flow.md) follows $1\to3$ and $4\to2$; in the $s$ channel it follows $1\to2$ at the incoming [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) and $4\to3$ at the outgoing [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md).

The two contractions again have a relative [fermionic sign](../../../../../fermionic-sign.md). With the same harmless common-phase convention as [i](../i.md), their sum is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M_{f\bar f}=g^2\left[(\bar u_3u_1)(\bar v_2v_4)-(\bar v_2u_1)(\bar u_3v_4)\right].}
$$

The [auxiliary-field propagator](../../../../../auxiliary-field-propagator.md) is the same constant $-i$ in both channels. There is no $u$ channel preserving the external [fermion number](../../../../../fermion-number.md) assignments.
