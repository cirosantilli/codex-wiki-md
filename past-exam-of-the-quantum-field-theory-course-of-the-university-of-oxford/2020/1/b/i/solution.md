<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Label the initial species-1 and species-2 [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) by $(p_1,s_1)$ and $(p_2,s_2)$, and the final species-1 and species-2 [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) by $(p_3,s_3)$ and $(p_4,s_4)$. There is exactly one [tree-level Feynman diagram](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md): the $t$-channel [scalar propagator](../../../../../scalar-propagator.md) joins the continuous species-1 line $1\to3$ to the continuous species-2 line $2\to4$. Its exchanged [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) $q_t$ determines $t$, in the notation of [Mandelstam variables](../../../../../mandelstam-variables.md):

$$
\boxed{q_t=p_1-p_3=p_4-p_2,\qquad t=q_t^2.}
$$

The top row of the figure displays this [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md). A crossed attachment $1\to4$ would change species at an [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) and is forbidden. There is no initial [antifermion](../../../../../antifermion.md) for an annihilation channel.
