<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $p_1,p_3$ label the incoming and outgoing [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md), and $p_2,p_4$ the incoming and outgoing [antifermion](../../../../../antifermion.md). The bottom row of the figure gives the two [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md):

- The $t$ channel has an open [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) line $1\to3$ and an open [antifermion](../../../../../antifermion.md) line with [fermion flow](../../../../../fermion-flow.md) $4\to2$. The [scalar propagator](../../../../../scalar-propagator.md) carries $q_t=p_1-p_3=p_4-p_2$.
- The $s$ channel has initial [particle-antiparticle annihilation](../../../../../annihilation.md) into a [scalar propagator](../../../../../scalar-propagator.md) with $q_s=p_1+p_2=p_3+p_4$, followed by creation of the final pair.

Thus the [Mandelstam variables](../../../../../mandelstam-variables.md) in the two denominators are

$$
\boxed{t=(p_1-p_3)^2,\qquad s=(p_1+p_2)^2.}
$$

The two [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) terms have a relative [fermionic sign](../../../../../fermionic-sign.md) of minus when written with spinor products $(\bar u_3u_1)(\bar v_2v_4)$ and $(\bar v_2u_1)(\bar u_3v_4)$. The overall phase depends on the chosen ordering of the external states. No $u$ channel joins an incoming [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) to an outgoing [antifermion](../../../../../antifermion.md) through a species-preserving [Yukawa interaction](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md).
