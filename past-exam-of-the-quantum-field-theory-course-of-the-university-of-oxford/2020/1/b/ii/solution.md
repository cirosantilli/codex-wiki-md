<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With all four external [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) of species 1, both final attachments are possible. The middle row of the figure gives the two [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md):

- The $t$ channel has lines $1\to3$ and $2\to4$, joined by a [scalar propagator](../../../../../scalar-propagator.md) with $q_t=p_1-p_3=p_4-p_2$.
- The $u$ channel has lines $1\to4$ and $2\to3$, joined by a [scalar propagator](../../../../../scalar-propagator.md) with $q_u=p_1-p_4=p_3-p_2$.

For a fixed ordering of external [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) states, the [fermionic sign](../../../../../fermionic-sign.md) from exchanging the final identical [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal M=-g^2\left[
\frac{(\bar u_3u_1)(\bar u_4u_2)}{t-m^2+i0}
-\frac{(\bar u_4u_1)(\bar u_3u_2)}{u-m^2+i0}
\right],\qquad u=q_u^2.}
$$

Here $u_j=u_{s_j}(p_j)$ are [Dirac spinors](../../../../../dirac-spinor.md). In particular, the [scattering amplitude](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) changes sign when the complete final labels $3$ and $4$ are exchanged, as required for identical [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md).
