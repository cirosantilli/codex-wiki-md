<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) $k$ flowing along the [fermion flow](../../../../fermion-flow.md), the [momentum space](../../../../momentum-space.md) [Feynman rules](../../../../feynman-rules.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\text{scalar line: }&\frac{i}{k^2-m^2+i0},\\
\text{fermion line of species }i\text{ to }j:\quad&\delta_{ij}\frac{i(\not k+m)}{k^2-m^2+i0},\\
\phi\bar\psi_i\psi_j\text{ vertex: }&-ig\delta_{ij}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The [scalar propagator](../../../../scalar-propagator.md) and [Dirac propagator](../../../../dirac-propagator.md) invert their respective quadratic kernels. The [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) follows by expanding $\exp(i\int\mathcal L_{\rm int})$, fixing its sign. Species is conserved along each open [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) line; there is no vertex changing species 1 into species 2.

An [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) carries $(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(\sum k)$ with inward [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md). Impose [momentum conservation](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) and integrate each independent [loop momentum](../../../../loop-momentum.md) with $d^4k/(2\pi)^4$. Include the [closed fermion loop sign](../../../../closed-fermion-loop-sign.md) $-1$ for every [fermion loop](../../../../fermion-loop.md), the relevant [fermionic signs](../../../../fermionic-sign.md) when rearranging external states, and the [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](../../../../feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). External incoming and outgoing [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) carry $u_s(p)$ and $\bar u_s(p)$; incoming and outgoing [antifermions](../../../../antifermion.md) carry $\bar v_s(p)$ and $v_s(p)$. An external [real scalar field](../../../../real-scalar-field.md) leg has factor 1 in an amputated [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md). The [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](../../../../feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) specifies the poles.
