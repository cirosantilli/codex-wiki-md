# Phase angle (astronomy)

↑ **Parent:** [Lunar phase](lunar-phase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_angle_(astronomy))

The astronomical [phase angle](phase-angle-astronomy.md) is the [angle](angle.md) at an illuminated body between the directions to its light source and to its observer. For a [lunar phase](lunar-phase.md) it is the [Sun](sun.md)–[Moon](moon.md)–observer [angle](angle.md) at the [Moon](moon.md), rather than the [elongation](elongation-astronomy.md) measured at [Earth](earth.md). A small [phase angle](phase-angle-astronomy.md) gives a nearly full apparent disk in the spherical model.
