# Hippocampal sharp-wave ripple

↑ **Parent:** [Hippocampus](hippocampus.md)

A brief coordinated hippocampal population event with a sharp-wave component and fast ripple oscillation. Such events occur in quiet waking as well as sleep, so effects of their disruption cannot automatically be assigned to sleep alone.
