# Classical ground states of an XXZ spin chain

↑ **Parent:** [XXZ spin chain](xxz-spin-chain.md)

For an even periodic classical chain with [spin](spin.md) length $S$ and $J>0$, each bond attains the negative largest [singular value](singular-value.md) of $\operatorname{diag}(1,1,\Delta)$. For $\Delta>1$ the two minimizing configurations are alternating $z$ [spins](spin.md). For $|\Delta|<1$ they are planar [Néel states](neel-state.md) with arbitrary in-plane angle. For $\Delta<-1$ the two configurations have uniform $z$ [spins](spin.md). At $\Delta=1$ the Néel direction is arbitrary on a sphere; at $\Delta=-1$ a staggered transverse component and uniform longitudinal component parameterize another spherical degeneracy. These are classical configuration manifolds, not a claim about finite quantum-chain degeneracy.
