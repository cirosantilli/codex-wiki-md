# Clausius gas

↑ **Parent:** [Equation of state](equation-of-state.md)

A Clausius gas with covolume parameter $b$ obeys $P(V-Nb)=Nk_BT$. It is the repulsive-volume part of a [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md) without the attractive term.
