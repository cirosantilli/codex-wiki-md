# Merit function

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

A merit function is a scalar [objective function](objective-function.md) used by an iterative constrained [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) to balance objective reduction and feasibility. It permits a [line search](line-search.md) or other acceptance test even when the original [objective function](objective-function.md) alone is insufficient to judge progress. [Quadratic penalty methods](quadratic-penalty-method.md) and the [augmented Lagrangian method](augmented-lagrangian-method.md) give two constructions with different treatments of multiplier information.
