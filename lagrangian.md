# Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Functional](functional.md)

A Lagrangian is a function or density whose stationary integral encodes an extremization problem. In [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md) its action gives the equations of motion; in geometry it describes geodesics and minimal surfaces.

In [Lagrangian](lagrangian-field-theory.md), the action integrates such a density over spacetime.

A finite-dimensional [optimization Lagrangian](optimization-lagrangian.md) combines an [objective function](objective-function.md) with [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) terms for its constraints.
