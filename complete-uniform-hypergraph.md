# Complete uniform hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md)

The complete $\ell$-uniform hypergraph on $r$ vertices contains every $\ell$-subset of its [vertex set](vertex-set.md). A hypergraph clique here means a restriction to a vertex subset of this form, rather than merely a clique in the pairwise shadow. For $\ell=2$, it is an ordinary [complete graph](complete-graph.md).
