# Circle log-mean of a holomorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md)

Under the hypotheses of [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md), the circle log-mean is the angular average of the natural [logarithm](logarithm.md) of the [complex modulus](complex-modulus.md). The factor $1/(2\pi)$ normalizes the [integral](integral-split.md) to a mean. It averages values at equally weighted angles on the [circle](circle.md), and [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md) computes it from $|f(0)|$ and the interior [zeros of a function](zero-of-a-function.md).
