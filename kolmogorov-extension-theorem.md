# Kolmogorov extension theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-dimensional distribution](finite-dimensional-distribution.md)

Consistent [probability distributions](probability-distribution-split.md) on finite collections of coordinates in [standard Borel spaces](standard-borel-space.md) determine a unique [probability measure](probability-measure.md) on the [product sigma-algebra](product-sigma-algebra.md). Consistency means that discarding coordinates by [marginalization](marginalization.md) agrees with the law already assigned to the smaller collection, and relabeling coordinates relabels the law. The theorem turns finite recipes into a complete [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md). In particular, products of one fixed [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) construct an [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) sequence; putting one common [random variable](random-variable-split.md) in every coordinate constructs the perfectly shared process above. The theorem supplies a law on the coordinate space, but does not by itself ensure [continuous](continuous-function.md) [sample paths](sample-path.md).
