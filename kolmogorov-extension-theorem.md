# Kolmogorov extension theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-dimensional distribution](finite-dimensional-distribution.md)

Consistent probability distributions on finite collections of coordinates in standard Borel spaces determine a unique probability measure on the product cylinder $\sigma$-algebra. In particular repeated products of a fixed distribution construct an [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) sequence.
