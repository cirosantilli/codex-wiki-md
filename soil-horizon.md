# Soil horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Stratigraphy](stratigraphy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soil_horizon)

A distinguishable layer within a soil profile formed by soil processes. A buried horizon can mark a stable former surface and help separate phases of deposition and [fault slip](fault-slip.md).
