# Optical scattering force

↑ **Parent:** [Optical tweezers](optical-tweezers.md)

An optical scattering force transfers [momentum](momentum.md) from [light](light.md) to a particle through [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md). In a usual single-beam [optical trap](optical-tweezers.md), its axial contribution pushes along beam propagation; the [optical gradient force](optical-gradient-force.md) must balance it for axial confinement. This balance can place the stable [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) away from the beam focus.
