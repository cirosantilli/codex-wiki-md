# Graded shift

↑ **Parent:** [Graded module](graded-module.md)

The graded shift $M(-a)$ is the same underlying [module](module-mathematics.md) with $M(-a)_d=M_{d-a}$. Thus $S(-a)$ has a free generator in degree $a$. Its [Hilbert series](hilbert-series.md) is $t^aH_M(t)$, and multiplication by a homogeneous element of degree $a$ becomes a degree-preserving map $M(-a)\to M$.
