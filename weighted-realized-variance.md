# Weighted realized variance

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md)

For observations of an [Itô integral](ito-integral.md) on a [partition of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md), the statistic $\sum_i g(t_{i-1})(X_{t_i}-X_{t_{i-1}})^2$ estimates $\int g(t)\sigma(t)^2\,dt$. With a deterministic noise coefficient the squared increments are [independent](independent-random-variables.md), and the fluctuation has [variance](variance-split.md) $2\sum_i g(t_{i-1})^2(\int_{t_{i-1}}^{t_i}\sigma^2)^2$.
