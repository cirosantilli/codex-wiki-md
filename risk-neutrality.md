# Risk neutrality

↑ **Parent:** [Utility function](utility-function-split.md)

Risk neutrality means evaluating uncertain monetary gains by their expected value. For auction payment identities, [quasilinear utility](quasilinear-utility.md) gives a bidder's expected utility as its value times its winning probability minus expected payment.
