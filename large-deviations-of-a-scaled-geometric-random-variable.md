# Large deviations of a scaled geometric random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md)

For a [geometric random variable](geometric-distribution.md) $X$ with $\mathbb P(X=k)=p(1-p)^{k-1}$, $0<p<1$, the family $X/L$ has [large-deviation speed](large-deviation-speed.md) $L$ and [good rate function](good-rate-function.md) $I(x)=ax$ for $x\geq0$, infinite otherwise, where $a=-\log(1-p)$. The exact tail $\mathbb P(X/L\geq x)=(1-p)^{\lceil Lx\rceil-1}$ gives the upper bound. A lattice point tending to any positive $x$ supplies the open-neighborhood lower bound; neighborhoods of zero have probability tending to one. Finite sublevel sets are compact intervals.
