# RC charging circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor](capacitor.md)

An ideal constant-voltage source $V$ charging an initially uncharged [capacitor](capacitor.md) through a series [resistor](resistor.md) gives $Q(t)=CV(1-e^{-t/(RC)})$ and $I(t)=(V/R)e^{-t/(RC)}$. These follow from [Kirchhoff's voltage law](kirchhoff-cycle-law.md) and $I=dQ/dt$. A smaller [time constant](time-constant.md) means faster fractional settling; it does not fix the initial current or final charge unless the separate values of $R$ and $C$ are specified.
