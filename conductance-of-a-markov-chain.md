# Conductance of a Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Reversible Markov chain](reversible-markov-chain.md)

For a [reversible Markov chain](reversible-markov-chain.md) with stationary flow $Q(x,y)=\pi(x)P(x,y)$, the conductance of a set $A$ is

$$
\Phi(A)=\frac{Q(A,A^c)}{\pi(A)}.
$$

Its [Cheeger constant](cheeger-constant.md) minimizes the boundary flow divided by the smaller stationary mass of the two sides of the cut.
