# Rayleigh-Plesset equation

↑ **Parent:** [Spherically symmetric incompressible radial flow](spherically-symmetric-incompressible-radial-flow.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayleigh–Plesset_equation)

The [Rayleigh-Plesset equation](rayleigh-plesset-equation.md) balances the radial inertia of an incompressible liquid around a spherical bubble against internal and ambient [pressure](pressure.md), [surface tension](surface-tension.md) and [viscous stress](viscous-stress-tensor.md). Removing the last two effects and setting the bubble pressure to zero gives the following vacuum-collapse case.
