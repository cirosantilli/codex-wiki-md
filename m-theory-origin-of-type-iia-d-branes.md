# M-theory origin of type IIA D-branes

↑ **Parent:** [M-theory circle duality](m-theory-circle-duality.md)

[D0-branes](d0-brane.md) are circle momentum states, [D2-branes](d2-brane.md) are unwrapped [M2-branes](m2-brane.md), [D4-branes](d4-brane.md) are circle-wrapped [M5-branes](m5-brane.md), and [D6-branes](d6-brane.md) arise from [Kaluza-Klein monopoles](kaluza-klein-monopole.md) with the M-circle as fibre. Wrapped M2-branes give [fundamental strings](fundamental-string.md), and unwrapped M5-branes give [NS5-branes](ns5-brane.md). The [D8-brane](d8-brane.md) is a source for [Romans mass](romans-mass.md) and is outside this ordinary massless-circle dictionary. Tension matching uses $R_{11}=g_s\ell_s$ and $\ell_p^3=g_s\ell_s^3$.
