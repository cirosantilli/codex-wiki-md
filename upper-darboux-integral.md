# Upper Darboux integral

↑ **Parent:** [Upper and lower Darboux integrals](upper-and-lower-darboux-integrals.md)

The upper Darboux integral of a bounded real function on a closed interval is the [infimum](infimum.md) of its [upper Darboux sums](upper-darboux-sum.md) over all [partitions of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md). Refinement decreases an [upper Darboux sum](upper-darboux-sum.md), and the function is [Riemann integrable](riemann-integrable-function.md) precisely when this infimum agrees with its [lower Darboux integral](lower-darboux-integral.md).
