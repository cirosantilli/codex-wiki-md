# Centrifugation pellet

↑ **Parent:** [Centrifugation](centrifugation.md)

A [centrifugation pellet](centrifugation-pellet.md) is material accumulated against the vessel wall by [centrifugation](centrifugation.md). Its existence establishes collection, not purity; other suspended material can be trapped or co-sediment.
