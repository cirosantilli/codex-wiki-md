# Left Kan extension as a comma-category colimit

↑ **Parent:** [Left Kan extension](left-kan-extension.md)

For [small categories](small-category.md) and a set-valued [functor](functor.md) $Q$, each displayed [colimit](colimit.md) exists in the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md). A representative $(c,u:Fc\to d,x\in Qc)$ maps under a [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) $\eta:Q\to GF$ to $G(u)\eta_c(x)$. The [colimit](colimit.md) identifications make this well-defined, proving the [adjunction](adjoint-functors.md) between [left Kan extension](left-kan-extension.md) and precomposition.
