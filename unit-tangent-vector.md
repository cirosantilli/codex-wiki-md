# Unit tangent vector

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent vector](tangent-vector.md)

The unit tangent vector of a regular parametrized curve $\mathbf r(t)$ is

$$
T(t)=\frac{\mathbf r'(t)}{|\mathbf r'(t)|}.
$$
