# Fundamental group of a special cube complex

↑ **Parent:** [Special cube complex](special-cube-complex.md)

The local isometry from a connected special cube complex to a [Salvetti complex](salvetti-complex.md) induces an injection of fundamental groups. Hence its fundamental group is a subgroup of a [Right-angled Artin group](right-angled-artin-group.md).
