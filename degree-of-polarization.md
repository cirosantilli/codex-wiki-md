# Degree of polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization (waves)](polarization-waves.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_of_polarization)

The [degree of polarization](degree-of-polarization.md) quantifies how strongly a transverse optical field departs from an unpolarized mixture. Here $\mu_1\geq\mu_2\geq0$ are the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of its [coherency matrix](coherency-matrix.md). For two mutually uncorrelated orthogonal linear components of intensities $I_1,I_2$, $\mathcal P=|I_1-I_2|/(I_1+I_2)$. It is undefined when the total [light intensity](light-intensity.md) is zero. A signed difference is a separate convention that also identifies a preferred polarization direction.
