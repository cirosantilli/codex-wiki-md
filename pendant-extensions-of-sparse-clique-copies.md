# Pendant extensions of sparse clique copies

↑ **Parent:** [Sparse clique-count concentration](sparse-clique-count-concentration.md)

If $H$ is formed by attaching one pendant [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) to a fixed [clique](clique-graph-theory.md), each copy has a unique clique core. Its number of extensions is the number of ambient [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) from that core to outside [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). At a scale where the core count is tight and its expected extension count diverges sufficiently rapidly, a [Chernoff bound](chernoff-bound.md) uniformly over all possible cores shows that the total count, divided by the extension [mean](expected-value.md), has the same limiting law as the core count. Non-induced copies are intended; extra ambient [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) are not forbidden.
