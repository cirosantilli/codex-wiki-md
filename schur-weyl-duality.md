<h1 id="schur-weyl-duality">Schur–Weyl duality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schur algebra](schur-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schur–Weyl_duality)

The commuting actions of the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) and the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md) on a [tensor power](tensor-power.md) are mutual [commutants](centralizer.md). The displayed sum ranges over partitions with at most $\dim V$ rows. The [Specht modules](specht-module.md) and [Schur modules](schur-module.md) are the simple factors for the two actions. In particular a primitive [Young symmetrizer](young-symmetrizer.md) selects one copy of the matching Schur [module](module-mathematics.md).
