# Dismantling trial

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical trial](clinical-trial.md)

A [randomised](randomization.md) comparison that varies a specified treatment component while keeping the surrounding intervention appropriately similar. It is designed to separate component effects that cannot be inferred from efficacy of a multicomponent package.
