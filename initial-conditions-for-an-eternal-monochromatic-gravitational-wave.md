# Initial conditions for an eternal monochromatic gravitational wave

↑ **Parent:** [TT coordinate separation and measured separation](tt-coordinate-separation-and-measured-separation.md)

At first order, [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md) gives $\ddot\xi_i=\ddot h_{ij}X^j/2$ for a small detector in a [plane gravitational wave in linearized gravity](plane-gravitational-wave-in-linearized-gravity.md). For physical initial displacement zero and initial [velocity](velocity.md) $V_i$, integration gives $\xi_i=[h_{ij}(t)-h_{ij}(0)]X^j/2+[V_i-\dot h_{ij}(0)X^j/2]t$. A bounded periodic response selects $V_i=\dot h_{ij}(0)X^j/2$. Prescribed positions alone do not select that response when the wave has no wave-free past.
