# Method factor

↑ **Parent:** [Factor analysis](factor-analysis.md)

A [latent variable](latent-variable-split.md) introduced to represent [covariance](covariance.md) related to a measurement method. Its separation from trait factors depends on indicators and identifying restrictions; labeling a factor as methodological does not establish that interpretation by itself.
