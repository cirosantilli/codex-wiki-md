# Linearized vacuum Einstein equations

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized Einstein equations](linearized-einstein-equations.md)

The source-free [Linearized Einstein equations](linearized-einstein-equations.md). For a [metric perturbation](linearized-gravity-split.md) about the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md), imposing the [Lorenz gauge in linearized gravity](lorenz-gauge-in-linearized-gravity.md) reduces them to $\Box h_{\mu\nu}=0$ together with $\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0$, where $\bar h$ is the [trace-reversed metric perturbation](trace-reversed-metric-perturbation.md). A [linearized coordinate gauge transformation](linearized-coordinate-gauge-transformation.md) preserves $G^{(1)}[h]=0$, while preserving the imposed gauge additionally requires its generator to satisfy the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md).
