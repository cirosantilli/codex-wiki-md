# Haldane phase

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry-protected topological phase](symmetry-protected-topological-phase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haldane_phase)

The $S=1$ [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md) chain realizes the [Haldane phase](haldane-phase.md): a gapped [symmetry-protected topological phase](symmetry-protected-topological-phase.md) with effective [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) degrees of freedom at open ends. [Time-reversal symmetry](t-symmetry.md) is one possible protecting symmetry. Integer [spin](spin.md) alone does not imply the same protected class: odd- and even-integer chains differ. The broader gap distinction is the [Haldane conjecture](haldane-conjecture.md).[Haldane, individual Nobel lecture, p. 040502-9, Fig. 8 and odd-integer versus even-integer distinction](nobel-lecture-topological-quantum-matter-by-f-duncan-m-haldane.md)
