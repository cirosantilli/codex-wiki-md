# Distortion free energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic director](nematic-director.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distortion_free_energy_density)

For a unit [nematic director](nematic-director.md), the bulk elastic density is $[K_1(\nabla\cdot\mathbf n)^2+K_2(\mathbf n\cdot\nabla\times\mathbf n)^2+K_3|\mathbf n\times\nabla\times\mathbf n|^2]/2$. The three positive moduli penalize [nematic splay](nematic-splay.md), [nematic twist](nematic-twist.md), and [nematic bend](nematic-bend.md). Boundary elastic terms, chirality, or varying nematic order require additional contributions.
