# Signed group velocity of a magnetic-gradient doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Double-well oscillator scale in a linear magnetic field](double-well-oscillator-scale-in-a-linear-magnetic-field.md)

Differentiating $E_k\simeq\sqrt{\hbar^3q\alpha k/2}/m$ gives the displayed [group velocity](group-velocity.md) when $q\alpha k>0$. Its sign is the sign of $q\alpha$, equivalently of k on that branch. Writing only a positive square root of $q\alpha/k$ would lose this sign. Exactly, the [Hellmann–Feynman theorem](hellmann-feynman-theorem.md) gives $v_y=\langle\hbar k-q\alpha x^2/2\rangle/m$. Evaluating this small drift from a localized wavefunction requires enough anharmonic accuracy: a leading centred Gaussian alone need not reproduce it.
