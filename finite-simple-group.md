# Finite simple group

↑ **Parent:** [Simple group](simple-group.md)

A nontrivial [finite group](finite-group.md) whose only [normal subgroups](normal-subgroup.md) are the [trivial group](trivial-group.md) and the whole group. A [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) of [prime number](prime-number.md) order gives an abelian example; $A_5$ gives a nonabelian [alternating group](alternating-group.md) example. Finite versions of [classical groups](classical-group.md) can produce further examples after taking suitable subgroups and quotients; small parameters require separate checks.
