# Wave

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave)

A wave is a propagating or standing disturbance that can carry [energy](energy.md) and momentum. Its oscillations are described by quantities such as [wavelength](wavelength.md), [frequency](frequency.md), and [wave speed](wave-speed.md).
