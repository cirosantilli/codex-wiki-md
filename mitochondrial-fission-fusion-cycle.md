# Mitochondrial fission-fusion cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Mitochondria](mitochondria.md)

Dynamic remodeling of a mitochondrial population through division and merger. Fusion permits content mixing, while fission supports distribution and can segregate portions for [mitophagy](mitophagy.md).
