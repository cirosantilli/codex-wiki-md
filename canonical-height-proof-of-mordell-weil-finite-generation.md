# Canonical-height proof of Mordell-Weil finite generation

↑ **Parent:** [Height descent lemma](height-descent-lemma.md)

For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) over a [number field](number-field.md), its nonnegative [canonical height of an elliptic curve](canonical-height-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) has finite bounded subsets by the [Northcott theorem](northcott-theorem.md) and its bounded difference from half the naive logarithmic height. By the [Weak Mordell-Weil theorem](weak-mordell-weil-theorem.md), choose representatives $R_i$ modulo $2E(K)$ and put $H=\max_i\widehat h(R_i)$. If $P=2Q+R_i$, the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) for the height pairing gives $\widehat h(Q)\leq(\widehat h(P)+H)/2$. Iterating brings every point into the finite set of height at most $H+1$. This set and the $R_i$ generate $E(K)$, proving the [Mordell-Weil theorem](mordell-weil-group.md) without assuming finite generation when constructing the height.
