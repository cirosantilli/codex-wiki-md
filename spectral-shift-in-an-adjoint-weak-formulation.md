# Spectral shift in an adjoint weak formulation

↑ **Parent:** [Weak formulation](weak-formulation.md)

The weak form of $(L^*-\lambda)u=f$ includes the same [scalar](scalar.md) shift as the [differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md). The unshifted identity $B^*[u,v]=(f,v)$ instead represents $L^*u=f$ and cannot imply the shifted equation for nonzero $\lambda$ unless $u=0$. On a smooth [bounded](boundedness.md) [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) choose any nonzero compactly supported smooth $u$ and take $L=-\Delta+1$, $f=Lu$ for a direct counterexample. For constant drift coefficients, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) reverses the drift sign in $L^*$ without changing this spectral term.
