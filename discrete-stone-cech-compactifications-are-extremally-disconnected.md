<h1 id="discrete-stone-cech-compactifications-are-extremally-disconnected">Discrete Stone-Čech compactifications are extremally disconnected</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md)

For a [discrete space](discrete-space.md) $X$ and $D\subseteq X$, the continuous extension of $\mathbf1_D$ to $\beta X$ remains idempotent by density, so it takes values in $\{0,1\}$. Its one-set is the clopen closure of $D$. If $O$ is any [open set](open-set.md) in $\beta X$, density gives $\overline O=\overline{O\cap X}$, so this closure is clopen as well. Thus $\beta X$ is an [extremally disconnected space](extremally-disconnected-space.md). For infinite $X$, this does not imply that every subset of $\beta X$ has open closure.
