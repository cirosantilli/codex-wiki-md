# Hydrogen ionization fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Recombination (cosmology)](recombination-cosmology.md)

In hydrogen-only cosmology, charge neutrality gives $n_e=n_p$ and baryon conservation gives $n_b=n_p+n_H$. The fraction $X_e$ lies between zero and one, with $n_H=(1-X_e)n_b$. With helium present, [Electron](electron.md) abundance per [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) nucleus and per total baryon are different conventions and must be specified. The [hydrogen-only Saha equation](hydrogen-only-saha-equation.md) gives the equilibrium fraction.
