# Hartree-Fock method

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chemistry](quantum-chemistry.md)

A variational approximation restricting a many-electron [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) to one [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md). Minimizing its energy gives self-consistent one-electron equations with Coulomb and exchange operators. It includes exchange from antisymmetry but omits correlation beyond the [determinant](determinant.md).
