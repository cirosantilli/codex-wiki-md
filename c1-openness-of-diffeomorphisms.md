# C1 openness of diffeomorphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md)

On a compact [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) without boundary, every smooth self-map sufficiently $C^1$-close to a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) is a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md). Reducing to a map near the identity, invertibility of its differential follows uniformly by compactness. It is then a proper local [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md), hence a finite [covering map](covering-space.md). Sufficient $C^0$ closeness gives a [homotopy](homotopy.md) to the identity on each component; its induced map on the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is surjective. The covering therefore has one sheet on each component, proving the assertion. This lets a nearby [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) transverse to cotangent fibers be represented by a single global [differential one-form](one-form.md) graph.
