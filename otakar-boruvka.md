<h1 id="otakar-boruvka">Otakar Borůvka</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [List of mathematicians](list-of-mathematicians.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otakar_Borůvka)

A [mathematician](mathematician.md) whose 1926 work introduced a component-merging method for the [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md) problem, now called [Borůvka's algorithm](boruvka-s-algorithm.md).[Original article, 1926, pp. 37–58](o-jistem-problemu-minimalnim-by-otakar-boruvka.md)
