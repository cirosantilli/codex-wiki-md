# Third law of black-hole mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole thermodynamics](black-hole-thermodynamics.md)

The third law of black-hole mechanics says that no finite physical process can reduce the [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) of a regular black-hole horizon to zero.
