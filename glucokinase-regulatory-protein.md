# Glucokinase regulatory protein

↑ **Parent:** [Hexokinase](hexokinase.md)

[Glucokinase regulatory protein](glucokinase-regulatory-protein.md) binds and regulates hepatic glucokinase, including its sequestration in the nucleus under suitable conditions. Glucose and fructose-phosphate metabolites alter this interaction, coordinating enzyme availability with nutrient context.
