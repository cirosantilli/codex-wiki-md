# Extremal hypergraph theory

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

Extremal hypergraph theory studies the largest or smallest possible size of a [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) under prescribed forbidden-subhypergraph or covering conditions. It generalizes [extremal graph theory](extremal-graph-theory.md). The [Turán density](turan-density.md) describes the asymptotic maximum edge proportion for a fixed uniform forbidden hypergraph.
