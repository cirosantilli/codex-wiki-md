# Investment with fixed debt service

↑ **Parent:** [Investment-consumption problem](investment-consumption-problem.md)

A fixed loan principal contributes a constant interest outflow $h$. If the objective stops when available [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md) reaches zero, the [value function](value-function.md) has an absorbing boundary $V(0)=0$. This differs from requiring the portfolio to finance the debt service forever.
