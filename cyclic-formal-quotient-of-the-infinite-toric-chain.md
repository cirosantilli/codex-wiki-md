# Cyclic formal quotient of the infinite toric chain

↑ **Parent:** [Semistable infinite chain from a toric fan](semistable-infinite-chain-from-a-toric-fan.md)

The lattice shear $(a,b)\mapsto(a+mb,b)$ shifts the chain charts by $m$ and preserves $q$. On the dense torus it acts as $(t,q)\mapsto(q^mt,q)$. For $m\geq3$, translates of each chart of the [formal completion of a scheme](formal-completion-of-a-scheme.md) along $q=0$ are disjoint. Glue $m$ representative formal charts cyclically by the adjacent-chart formulas to obtain a [formal scheme](formal-scheme.md) quotient. The two branch opens of each chart are disjoint because $D(x_i)\cap D(y_i)=D(q)$ is empty on the formal support. The quotient fibre is an $m$-gon of [projective lines](projective-line.md), and the quotient map is locally an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) on each translated chart. Invariant morphisms descend uniquely through this gluing.
