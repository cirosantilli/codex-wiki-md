# Massless particle little group

↑ **Parent:** [Little group](little-group.md)

The stabilizer of a nonzero null [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) has connected group $ISO(D-2)$. For ordinary finite-polarization particles its translation part acts trivially, leaving a representation of $SO(D-2)$.
