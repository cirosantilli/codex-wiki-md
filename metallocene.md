# Metallocene

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

A complex in which a [metal](metal.md) is bound between two cyclopentadienyl-type rings, commonly with [hapticity](hapticity.md) $\eta^5$ at each ring. In ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md), each unsubstituted cyclopentadienyl [anion](anion.md) donates six [electrons](electron.md).
