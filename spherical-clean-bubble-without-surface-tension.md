# Spherical clean bubble without surface tension

↑ **Parent:** [Clean-bubble Stokes drag](clean-bubble-stokes-drag.md)

A buoyantly rising inviscid bubble in otherwise quiescent unbounded liquid has an exterior [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) with $F=4\pi\mu aU=4\pi\rho ga^3/3$. Its tangential traction vanishes on the [sphere](sphere.md). The dynamic normal traction is $-3F\cos\theta/(4\pi a^2)$, while the hydrostatic normal-traction variation is $\rho ga\cos\theta$. Their dipoles cancel at the terminal [velocity](velocity.md), leaving a constant total normal stress compatible with uniform gas [pressure](pressure.md) even when [surface tension](surface-tension.md) is zero. This is an exact spherical solution in the stated Stokes model, not a proof of shape stability under arbitrary perturbations.
