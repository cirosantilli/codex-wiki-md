# Hyperbolic cotangent generating function for Bernoulli numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli number](bernoulli-number.md)

The defining generating function of the [Bernoulli numbers](bernoulli-number.md), with $B_1=-1/2$, gives this identity of formal [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) by $\coth z=1+2/(e^{2z}-1)$. Therefore $a\coth(az)-\coth z$ is regular and odd. Integrating it gives the even formal logarithm of $\sinh(az)/(a\sinh z)$. Its even derivatives of order $k$ at zero are $2^k(a^k-1)B_k/k$, while the odd derivatives vanish. This computes the [cyclotomic higher logarithmic derivatives](cyclotomic-higher-logarithmic-derivative.md) of symmetric [cyclotomic units](cyclotomic-unit.md) without introducing a spurious linear term.
