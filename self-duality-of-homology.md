# Self-duality of homology

↑ **Parent:** [Homology object](homology-object.md)

Let $q:C_n\to\operatorname{coker}d_{n+1}$. Since $d_nd_{n+1}=0$, there is an induced $\bar d_n:\operatorname{coker}d_{n+1}\to C_{n-1}$. In an [abelian category](abelian-category.md),

$$
\operatorname{coker}\bigl(\operatorname{im}d_{n+1}\to\ker d_n\bigr)
\cong
\ker\bar d_n.
$$

The left side is the usual homology construction and the right side is its construction in the [opposite category](opposite-category.md), so the definition of homology is self-dual.
