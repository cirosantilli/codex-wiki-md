# Airy resolvent kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Airy equation](airy-equation.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}p>0$, put $\lambda=p^{1/3}$ using the [principal cube root](principal-cube-root.md) and $r_{1,2}=\lambda e^{\pm i\pi/3}$. The decaying [Green function](green-s-function.md) for $\partial_s^3+p$ on the real line is

$$
R_p(s)=\begin{cases}e^{-\lambda s}/(3\lambda^2),&s\geq0,\\-\sum_{j=1}^2e^{r_js}/(3r_j^2),&s<0.\end{cases}
$$

The identities among the three [characteristic roots](characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) show that $R_p$ and $R_p'$ are continuous, while $R_p''$ has jump one. Its [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) therefore satisfies $(\partial_s^3+p)R_p=\delta_0$. This is the kernel of the [resolvent operator](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) $(p+\partial_s^3)^{-1}$, obtained by the time [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) of the [Airy equation](airy-equation.md).
