# Derivative energy constant for a smooth sieve cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth divisor-square sieve asymptotic](smooth-divisor-square-sieve-asymptotic.md)

Write $e^u\phi(u)=\int\psi(t)e^{-iut}\,dt$. The double integral $\iint\psi(t)\psi(t')(1+it)(1+it')/(2+i(t+t'))\,dt\,dt'$ equals the displayed constant. Express the denominator as a Laplace integral and each differentiated Fourier factor as $-\phi'(u)$, then use [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md). For a real cutoff the resulting square is positive even though the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) factors have no [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md). The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and $\phi(0)=1$, $\phi(1/3)=0$ give $c_\phi\geq3$.
