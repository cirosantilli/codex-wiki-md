# Continuous maps from products of cofinite spaces to Hausdorff spaces are constant

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous maps from an infinite cofinite space to a Hausdorff space are constant](continuous-maps-from-an-infinite-cofinite-space-to-a-hausdorff-space-are-constant.md)

A [continuous map](continuous-map.md) from a nonempty finite [product space](product-space.md) of infinite [sets](set-split.md) with their [cofinite topologies](cofinite-topology.md) to a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) is a [constant function](constant-function.md). Fixing all but one coordinate gives a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) from an infinite [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md), so changing that coordinate does not change the value. Any two points in a finite [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) can be joined by finitely many one-coordinate changes. This proves the assertion. In particular every real-valued [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on $Z\times Z$ is constant when $Z$ is the [real line](real-line.md) equipped with the [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md).
