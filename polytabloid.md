# Polytabloid

↑ **Parent:** [Tabloid](tabloid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytabloid)

For a tableau $t$, its polytabloid is

$$
e(t)=\sum_{\pi\in C(t)}\operatorname{sgn}(\pi)\{\pi t\}.
$$
