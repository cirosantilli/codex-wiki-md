# Self-gravitating Kramers power-law envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative envelope](radiative-envelope.md)

For a constant-luminosity [radiative envelope](radiative-envelope.md) with ideal-gas [pressure](pressure.md) and [Kramers' opacity law](kramers-opacity-law.md), impose [power laws](power-law.md) on [enclosed mass](enclosed-mass.md), density, [pressure](pressure.md) and [temperature](temperature.md) while retaining $M_r'=4\pi r^2\rho$. If their exponents are $m,b,p,d$, respectively, mass conservation gives $m=b+3$, hydrostatic balance gives $d=m-1$, the gas law gives $d=p-b$, and diffusion gives $d-1=2b-2-13d/2$. Solving gives $(m,b,p,d)=(1,-32,-42,-10)/11$. In particular $R/r_c=(T_c/T_R)^{11/10}$. Neglecting envelope self-gravity instead sets $M_r$ approximately constant and changes the exponents to $d=-1,b=-13/4,p=-17/4$; that approximation is not an exact solution of mass conservation with nonzero density.
