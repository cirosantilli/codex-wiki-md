# Constructible-universe obstruction to a uniform inner-model proof of failure of choice

↑ **Parent:** [Inner model](inner-model.md)

Every [inner model](inner-model.md) $N$ of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) contains the ambient [constructible universe](constructible-universe.md) $L$. By induction, $L_\alpha^N=L_\alpha$: at successor stages, satisfaction in the same [set](set-split.md) structure and definability with parameters are absolute; at limits both constructions take the same [union](set-union.md). Thus, if the ambient universe satisfies $V=L$, its only inner model of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) is itself, and it satisfies the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md). A procedure that must produce a choice-failing inner model from every model of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) is therefore impossible. This does not assert that arbitrary inner models of arbitrary choice universes satisfy choice; the obstruction concerns a uniform construction.
