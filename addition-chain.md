# Addition chain

↑ **Parent:** [Exponentiation](exponentiation.md)

An [addition chain](addition-chain.md) for a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $k$ is $1=a_0<a_1<\cdots<a_r=k$, where each $a_t$ is the [sum](sum.md) of two earlier entries, which may coincide. It computes $x^k$ in $r$ [multiplications](multiplication.md) by reusing previously computed powers. For example, $1,2,3,6,12,15$ computes $x^{15}$ in five [multiplications](multiplication.md).
