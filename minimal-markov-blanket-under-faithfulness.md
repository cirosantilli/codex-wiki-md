# Minimal Markov blanket under faithfulness

↑ **Parent:** [Markov blanket](markov-blanket.md)

Under [faithfulness of a directed acyclic graph](faithfulness-of-a-directed-acyclic-graph.md), every conditioning set screening a vertex from all remaining vertices contains its graphical [Markov blanket](markov-blanket.md). A missing adjacent vertex leaves a one-edge active path; a missing other parent of a child leaves the active path $k\to h\leftarrow u$, since the child $h$ is necessarily conditioned. Thus the graphical blanket is the unique smallest screening set.
