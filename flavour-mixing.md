# Flavour mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md)

The interaction-labelled fields of several particle species can be linear combinations of the propagation or mass eigenfields. A [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) relates orthonormal bases with canonical kinetic normalization. [Quark mixing](quark-mixing.md) is described by the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md); the [lepton mixing matrix](lepton-mixing-matrix.md) describes the corresponding mismatch for leptons. A [mixing angle](mixing-angle.md) parametrizes part of this matrix once ordering and phase conventions are fixed.
