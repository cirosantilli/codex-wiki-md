# Counting finite group actions by transitive orbit types

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action-split.md)

For a finite [group](group-split.md) $G$, transitive finite [group actions](group-action-split.md) are classified up to equivalence by conjugacy classes of [subgroups](subgroup.md): the class of $H$ gives the [coset action](coset-action.md) on $G/H$. Every finite action decomposes uniquely as a disjoint union of its [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md), so its equivalence type is a multiset of these transitive types. Consequently the coefficient of $t^n$ in the displayed product, with one factor for every subgroup conjugacy class, counts actions on $n$ points up to relabeling. Each factor expands as a [geometric series](geometric-series.md) recording any multiplicity of that orbit type. For both $C_6$ and $S_3$ there is exactly one transitive type of each size $1,2,3,6$; their coefficient at degree six is $4+2+1+1=8$.
