# Pythagorean triple

↑ **Parent:** [Pythagorean theorem](pythagorean-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pythagorean_triple)

A Pythagorean triple consists of three positive [integers](integer.md) satisfying $x^2+y^2=z^2$. It is primitive when their [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) is one. Reducing their [square numbers](square-number.md) modulo $3$ shows that at least one leg is divisible by $3$; reducing modulo $4$ shows that both legs cannot be odd.
