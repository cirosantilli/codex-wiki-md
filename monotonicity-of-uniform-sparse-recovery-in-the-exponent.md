# Monotonicity of uniform sparse recovery in the exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Lq null space property](lq-null-space-property.md)

If the [Lq null space property](lq-null-space-property.md) holds at $q\le1$, it holds at every $0<p<q$. Order a nonzero null [vector](vector.md)'s magnitudes $a_1\ge\cdots\ge a_N$ and put $t=a_s>0$. Since $p-q<0$, the top-$s$ $p$-power sum is at most $t^{p-q}$ times its $q$-power sum, while the tail $p$-power sum is at least that multiple of the tail $q$-power sum. The strict $q$ inequality therefore gives the strict $p$ inequality. The largest $s$ magnitudes are the worst [support of a vector](support-of-a-vector.md), so all other supports satisfy it too.
