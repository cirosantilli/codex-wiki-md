# Centered moment comparison for additive arithmetic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Erdős-Kac theorem](erdos-kac-theorem.md)

If a [strongly additive arithmetic function](strongly-additive-arithmetic-function.md) $g$ is supported on [primes](prime-number.md) up to $y$, its $j$th uniform [central moment](central-moment.md) differs from the independent [Bernoulli random variables](bernoulli-distribution.md) model with probabilities $1/p$ by $O(jy^j(\sum_{p\le y}|g(p)|)^j/N)$. Expansion of divisibility [indicator functions](indicator-function.md) reduces the comparison to $\lfloor N/d\rfloor/N=1/d+O(1/N)$.
