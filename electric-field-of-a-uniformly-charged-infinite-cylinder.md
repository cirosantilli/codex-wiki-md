# Electric field of a uniformly charged infinite cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)

For an infinite insulating cylinder of [radius](radius.md) $R$ and uniform fixed [charge density](charge-density.md) $\rho$, [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) gives interior [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $\rho\mathbf r/(2\epsilon_0)$ and exterior [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $\rho R^2\mathbf r/(2\epsilon_0|\mathbf r|^2)$, where $\mathbf r$ is transverse displacement from its axis. The [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is continuous at $|\mathbf r|=R$ when there is no separate [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md). The interior linear dependence makes displaced-cylinder [superposition](superposition-principle.md) particularly simple.
