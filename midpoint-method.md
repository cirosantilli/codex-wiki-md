# Midpoint method

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midpoint_method)

The [midpoint method](midpoint-method.md) advances an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) using a derivative evaluated at a temporal midpoint. Its explicit form predicts that midpoint from the initial derivative, while the [implicit midpoint rule](implicit-midpoint-rule.md) solves for the derivative at the mean of the endpoint states.
