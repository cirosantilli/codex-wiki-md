# Binary digit

↑ **Parent:** [Binary numeral system](binary-numeral-system.md)

A [binary digit](binary-digit.md) is a zero or one in the base-two positional representation of an [integer](integer.md). The sum $s_2(n)=\sum_jb_j$ determines the [Thue–Morse sequence](thue-morse-sequence.md) and is the [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md) of the binary representation.
