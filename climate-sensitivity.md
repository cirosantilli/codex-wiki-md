# Climate sensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Climate model](climate-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_sensitivity)

[Climate sensitivity](climate-sensitivity.md) quantifies the equilibrium [temperature](temperature-split.md) response to a specified change in forcing. Distinguish the response to doubled [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) from the gain per unit [radiative forcing](radiative-forcing.md): for a linear net restoring coefficient $\lambda>0$ and constant forcing $F_0$, the gain is $1/\lambda$, whereas the equilibrium warming is $\Delta T_{\rm eq}=F_0/\lambda$. The forcing convention and the [climate feedbacks](climate-feedback.md) permitted to adjust are part of the definition. A transient warming before the [ocean heat content](ocean-heat-content.md) has adjusted is a different quantity.
