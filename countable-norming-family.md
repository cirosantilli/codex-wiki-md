# Countable norming family

↑ **Parent:** [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md)

A real [separable Banach space](separable-banach-space.md) has a sequence $(\varphi_n)$ in its dual unit ball with $\lVert x\rVert=\sup_n\varphi_n(x)$ for every $x$. Choose a dense sequence $(u_n)$ in the unit sphere and apply the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) to obtain $\varphi_n(u_n)=1$. In the complex case use $\sup_n\operatorname{Re}\varphi_n(x)$.
