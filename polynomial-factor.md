# Polynomial factor

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

A polynomial factor $g$ of a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $f$ satisfies $f=gh$ for another polynomial $h$. Common polynomial factors are cancelled when expressing a [rational function](rational-function.md) in reduced form; its [degree of a rational map of the Riemann sphere](degree-of-a-rational-map-of-the-riemann-sphere.md) is determined after this cancellation.
