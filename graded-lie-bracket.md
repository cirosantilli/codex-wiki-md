# Graded Lie bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Graded Lie algebra](graded-lie-algebra.md)

The bracket on a [graded Lie algebra](graded-lie-algebra.md) is a bilinear operation satisfying graded antisymmetry and the graded [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md). For homogeneous elements of degrees $r,s$, $[u,v]=-(-1)^{rs}[v,u]$. A [Gerstenhaber bracket](gerstenhaber-bracket.md) uses this convention on the shifted degrees $r=|u|-1$ and $s=|v|-1$.
