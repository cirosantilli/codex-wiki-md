# Ester

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

The [carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md)-derived [ester](ester.md) linkages in a [triglyceride](triglyceride.md) or typical [glycerophospholipid](glycerophospholipid.md) have the form $\mathrm{R{-}C(=O){-}O{-}R'}$. Here the [acyl group](acyl-group.md) is attached through [oxygen](oxygen.md) to the [glycerol](glycerol.md)-derived part. See [IUPAC Blue Book, P-65.6: esters](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BlueBook/P6.html).
