<h1 id="buhlmann-straub-credibility-factor">Bühlmann–Straub credibility factor</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bühlmann–Straub credibility estimate](buhlmann-straub-credibility-estimate.md)

The experience weight is $Z=Wa/(Wa+v)$, where $W$ is total observed exposure, $a$ the [variance of hypothetical means](variance-of-hypothetical-means.md), and $v$ the [expected process variance](expected-process-variance.md). With positive $a$, it is $W/(W+v/a)$. More exposure raises the weight; larger process noise lowers it. If $a=0$, the unknown conditional mean is constant and one can set $Z=0$.
