# Exponential isomorphism between the additive and multiplicative formal groups

↑ **Parent:** [Formal multiplicative group](formal-multiplicative-group.md)

Over an [algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md) whose scalar field is $\mathbb Q$, the series $\exp(X)-1$ defines an isomorphism from the [formal additive group](formal-additive-group.md) to the [formal multiplicative group](formal-multiplicative-group.md), with inverse $\log(1+X)$.
