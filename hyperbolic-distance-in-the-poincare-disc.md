# Hyperbolic distance in the Poincare disc

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré disk model](poincare-disk-model.md)

For $z_1,z_2$ in the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md), put

$$
\rho=\left|\frac{z_2-z_1}{1-\overline{z_1}z_2}\right|.
$$

Their [hyperbolic distance](hyperbolic-distance.md) is

$$
d(z_1,z_2)=2\operatorname{artanh}\rho
=\log\frac{1+\rho}{1-\rho}.
$$
