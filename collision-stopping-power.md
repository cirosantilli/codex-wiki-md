# Collision stopping power

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping power](stopping-power.md)

The positive mean loss of projectile [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) per path length through electronic excitation and [ionization](ionization.md), conventionally $S_{\rm col}=-dT/dx$. Dividing by absorber [density](density.md) gives mass stopping power.
