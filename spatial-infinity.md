# Spatial infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary condition](boundary-condition.md)

A [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) at [spatial infinity](spatial-infinity.md) specifies limiting behaviour as the spatial coordinate leaves every bounded set, such as $\lim_{x\to+\infty}u(x,t)=g(t)$ on a half-line. A pointwise limit for each time is different from uniform control over a time interval. Maximum-principle and uniqueness proofs on unbounded domains must use a suitable uniform boundedness, growth restriction or barrier hypothesis to justify passage from finite domains to infinity.
