# Subduction trench

↑ **Parent:** [Subduction](subduction.md)

A convergent margin where one plate descends beneath another. In a simple junction-kinematic model the trench normal motion is tied to the [overriding plate](overriding-plate.md); rollback or distributed deformation can modify that rule.
