<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On a [probability space](../../../../../probability-space-split.md) $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)$, a standard one-dimensional [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) is a real-valued [stochastic process](../../../../../stochastic-process-split.md) $B=(B_t)_{t\geq0}$ with $B_0=0$ [almost surely](../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md), continuous [sample paths](../../../../../sample-path.md) [almost surely](../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md), and [independent increments](../../../../../independent-increments.md), such that

$$
\boxed{B_t-B_s\sim N(0,t-s)\qquad(0\leq s<t).}
$$

Here the second parameter is the [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md). Thus its [Brownian increments](../../../../../brownian-increment.md) have the [normal distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) with [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) zero and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) equal to elapsed time. Equivalently, relative to a specified [filtration](../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_t)$, require $B$ to be an [adapted process](../../../../../adapted-process.md) and $B_t-B_s$ to be [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of $\mathcal F_s$ with this [normal distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md). Taking the [natural Brownian filtration](../../../../../natural-brownian-filtration.md) recovers the first definition.
