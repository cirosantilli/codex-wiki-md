# Endomorphism of a finite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Endomorphism](endomorphism.md)

Every [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) of the additive [finite cyclic group](finite-cyclic-group.md) $C_n=\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$, for a positive [integer](integer-split.md) $n$, has the form $f_a(k)=ak\pmod n$, determined by $a=f_a(1)$. This is the self-map case of [homomorphism from a finite cyclic group](homomorphism-from-a-finite-cyclic-group.md): repeated [addition](addition.md) forces the value at every element. Conversely, each choice of $a$ respects [addition](addition.md) and gives a well-defined map on [congruence classes](congruence-class.md).

Let $d=\gcd(n,a)$, the [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md). The [image of a group homomorphism](image-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is the [cyclic subgroup](cyclic-subgroup.md) generated by $a$, with size $n/d$ by [order of an element of a finite cyclic group](order-of-an-element-of-a-finite-cyclic-group.md). The [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is

$$
\ker f_a=\{0,n/d,2n/d,\ldots,(d-1)n/d\},\qquad |\ker f_a|=d.
$$

Indeed, $ak=0\pmod n$ is equivalent to $n/d$ dividing $k$, after cancelling $d$ and using [coprimality](coprime-integers.md). Consequently $f_a$ is a [group automorphism](group-automorphism.md) exactly when $a$ and $n$ are [coprime](coprime-integers.md). In that case a [modular inverse](modular-multiplicative-inverse.md) $b$ of $a$ gives $f_a^{-1}=f_b$. This connects reversible self-maps directly to the [generators of a finite cyclic group](generators-of-a-finite-cyclic-group.md).[Chapter 3, Automorphisms of cyclic groups](group-theory-v4-01-by-james-s-milne.md)
