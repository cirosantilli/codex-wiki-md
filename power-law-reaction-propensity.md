# Power-law reaction propensity

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction propensity function](reaction-propensity-function.md)

A power-law propensity uses the same reactant monomial as a deterministic [law of mass action](law-of-mass-action.md), scaled by reactor volume. For example, a bimolecular channel may be assigned $a(x)=\alpha x^2/V$. This convention differs at small copy number from the combinatorial propensity $\alpha x(x-1)/V$ and must therefore be stated explicitly.
