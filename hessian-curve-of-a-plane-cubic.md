# Hessian curve of a plane cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Hessian criterion for a flex](hessian-criterion-for-a-flex.md)

For a cubic [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) $F$, its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) has linear entries, so its determinant is a cubic [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md), possibly zero. On a [nonsingular plane cubic](nonsingular-plane-cubic.md) in characteristic different from two, its zeros are exactly the [flexes](inflection-point-of-an-algebraic-plane-curve.md), by the [Hessian criterion for a flex](hessian-criterion-for-a-flex.md). If the determinant is nonzero, [Bézout's theorem](bezout-s-theorem.md) supplies intersections; if it is zero, the local criterion itself supplies flexes.
