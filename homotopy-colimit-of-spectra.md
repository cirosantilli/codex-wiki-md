# Homotopy colimit of spectra

↑ **Parent:** [Stable homotopy theory](stable-homotopy-theory.md)

A [homotopy colimit of spectra](homotopy-colimit-of-spectra.md) is the derived [colimit](colimit.md) of a diagram of [topological spectra](spectrum-topology.md). For a sequential diagram $T_0\to T_1\to\cdots$, it is the [cofiber of spectra](cofiber-of-spectra.md) of $1-s:\bigvee_jT_j\to\bigvee_jT_j$, where $s$ uses the bonding maps into the next summands.
