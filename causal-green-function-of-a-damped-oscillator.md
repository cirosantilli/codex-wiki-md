# Causal Green function of a damped oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For $L=D_t^2+2kD_t+k^2+\omega^2$, the causal [Green function](green-s-function.md) satisfies $LG=\delta(t)$. It is continuous at zero, with a unit jump in its first derivative. Its smooth positive-time kernel $g$ obeys $g(0)=0,g'(0)=1$. The zero-frequency limit is $H(t)te^{-kt}$. Shifting $G$ gives the zero-initial-data response to a delayed [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md).
