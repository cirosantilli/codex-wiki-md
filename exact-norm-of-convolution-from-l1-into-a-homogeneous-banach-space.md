# Exact norm of convolution from L1 into a homogeneous Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous Banach space on the circle](homogeneous-banach-space-on-the-circle.md)

The [convolution](convolution.md) estimate gives the upper bound. Apply the operator to the [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md), whose $L^1$ norm is one. The [summability kernel on the circle](summability-kernel-on-the-circle.md) property implies $f*K_n\to f$ in $B$, giving the reverse bound. This identifies the operator norm even when no single input attains it.
