# Quadratic hindered-settling flux

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic sedimentation](kinematic-sedimentation.md)

For downward settling speed $u_s(1-\phi)$ in upward coordinate $z$, particle flux is $j=-u_s\phi(1-\phi)$. The concentration-characteristic speed is $j^{\prime}=u_s(2\phi-1)$ and differs from particle speed. Since $j^{\prime\prime}=2u_s>0$, decreasing concentration with height creates a compressive [shock](shock-wave.md), while increasing concentration creates a [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md).
