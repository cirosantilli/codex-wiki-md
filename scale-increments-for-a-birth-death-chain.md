# Scale increments for a birth-death chain

↑ **Parent:** [Birth-death chain](birth-death-chain.md)

The interior equation for a [harmonic function for a Markov chain](harmonic-function-for-a-markov-chain.md) on the integers gives $p_k(h(k+1)-h(k))=q_k(h(k)-h(k-1))$. Positive scale increments satisfy $w_{k+1}=(q_k/p_k)w_k$. For absorbing boundaries $L<U$, the lower exit probability from $x$ is

$$
\frac{\sum_{j=x+1}^{U}w_j}{\sum_{j=L+1}^{U}w_j}.
$$

This satisfies the harmonic equation and the boundary values and therefore is the [hitting probability](hitting-probability.md). For parity-dependent probabilities, $w_{j+2}=\rho w_j$ with $\rho=q_0q_1/(p_0p_1)$; geometric sums distinguish rightward, balanced and leftward escape.
