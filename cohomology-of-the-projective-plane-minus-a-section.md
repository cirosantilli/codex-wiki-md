# Cohomology of the projective plane minus a section

↑ **Parent:** [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md)

For a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$, remove the section $[1:0:0]$ from $\mathbb P_R^2$. The two remaining standard charts have [coordinate rings](coordinate-ring.md) $R[a,b]$ and $R[a/b,b^{-1}]$ inside the [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md) $R[a,b,b^{-1}]$. Their intersection consists of constants: their [monomial](monomial.md) exponent regions are $i\geq0,j\geq0$ and $i\geq0,j\leq-i$, respectively. The [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md) in degree one is free over $R$ on the missing [monomials](monomial.md) $a^ib^{-k}$ with $1\leq k<i$. Equivalently its [basis](basis.md) consists of the classes of $x_0^{m+n}/(x_1^mx_2^n)$ for $m,n\geq1$. Degree zero is $R$ and every degree at least two is zero. The charts and their intersection are [affine schemes](affine-scheme.md), so this computes [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md). If $R$ is nonzero, the first [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) is nonzero and this [scheme](scheme.md) is not an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md).
