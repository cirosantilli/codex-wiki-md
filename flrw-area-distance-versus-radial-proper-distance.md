# FLRW area distance versus radial proper distance

↑ **Parent:** [Luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md)

In curvature coordinates the present sphere area is $4\pi(R_0r)^2$. Its transverse area distance is $R_0r$, whereas radial proper distance is $R_0\int_0^rdr/\sqrt{1-Kr^2}$. Confusing them invalidates a flat-space distance identity in curved geometry.
