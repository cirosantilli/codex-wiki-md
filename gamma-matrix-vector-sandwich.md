# Gamma-matrix vector sandwich

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md)

For [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) with $\{\gamma^a,\gamma^b\}=2\eta^{ab}$ and $\gamma^{ab}=[\gamma^a,\gamma^b]/2$, contracted [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) gives $\gamma_a\gamma^m\gamma^a=(2-D)\gamma^m$. Expanding the two antisymmetrized factors gives $\gamma_a\gamma_b\gamma^m\gamma^a\gamma^b-D\gamma^m$. The five-gamma product equals $[2D-(D-2)^2]\gamma^m$, by moving the middle contracted gamma through and using the three-gamma contraction twice. Subtraction yields the displayed identity. A convention without the factor one-half in each bivector multiplies the result by four.
