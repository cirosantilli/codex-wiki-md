# Phase plate

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-contrast microscopy](phase-contrast-microscopy.md)

An [optical filter](optical-filter.md) that changes selected light's [phase](phase-waves.md) relative to other transmitted light. In [phase-contrast microscopy](phase-contrast-microscopy.md), its ring can also attenuate the direct reference; the [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) transmission and [light intensity](light-intensity.md) transmission must be distinguished. An amplitude transmission $a=1/2$ gives an intensity transmission $a^2=1/4$.
