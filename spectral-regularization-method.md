# Spectral regularization method

↑ **Parent:** [Regularization of an inverse problem](regularization-of-an-inverse-problem.md)

A spectral regularization method applies a bounded scalar filter to the spectrum of $A^*A$. In a [singular system of a compact operator](singular-system-of-a-compact-operator.md), it has the form

$$
R_\alpha f=\sum_jg_\alpha(\sigma_j^2)\sigma_j\langle f,u_j\rangle v_j,
$$

where the filters approximate $1/\lambda$ while controlling amplification near $\lambda=0$.
