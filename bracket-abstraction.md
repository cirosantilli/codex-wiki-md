# Bracket abstraction

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatory logic](combinatory-logic.md)

Bracket abstraction removes a free variable from a [combinatory logic term](combinatory-logic-term.md). The displayed clauses are applied in that order, giving $FV([x]T)=FV(T)\setminus\{x\}$ and $([x]T)A\to^*T[x:=A]$. Under the standard lambda expansions of $S,K,I$, the result is beta-equivalent to $\lambda x.T$. The recursive translation of lambda terms therefore preserves variables and application and reflects weak equivalence, but need not preserve all beta equivalences.
