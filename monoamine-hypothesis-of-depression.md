# Monoamine hypothesis of depression

↑ **Parent:** [Major depressive disorder](major-depressive-disorder.md)

A family of proposals relating depression to reduced or dysregulated [monoamine](monoamine.md) signalling. A simple universal deficiency account is stronger than the claim that a monoaminergic manipulation can alter symptoms. Drug-target engagement, therapeutic mediation and original disease cause are separate causal claims.
