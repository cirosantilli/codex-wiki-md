# Fermi-Dirac statistics

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion](fermion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi-Dirac_statistics)

The antisymmetric exchange statistics of identical [fermions](fermion.md). Interchanging two one-particle labels reverses the total state, and fermionic [creation operators](creation-operator.md) anticommute. Occupation of a one-particle mode is therefore zero or one, giving the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md); thermal equilibrium leads to the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md). Color, flavor, spin and orbital factors must together have the required antisymmetry, even if one factor is symmetric.
