# Oriented site percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Directed percolation](directed-percolation.md)

Independently open vertices of a [directed graph](directed-graph.md) with common [probability](probability.md) $p$, and require every vertex of an infinite directed path to be open, including its root. Conditioning the root to be open changes the survival probability by a factor $p$ and does not change the critical probability. This model gives an independent comparison process in the [independent oriented-percolation comparison for the contact process](independent-oriented-percolation-comparison-for-the-contact-process.md).
