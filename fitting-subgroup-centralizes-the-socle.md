# Fitting subgroup centralizes the socle

↑ **Parent:** [Fitting subgroup](fitting-subgroup.md)

For a [finite group](finite-group.md), $F(G)$ centralizes its [group socle](socle-of-a-finite-group.md). A nonabelian [minimal normal subgroup](minimal-normal-subgroup.md) meets $F(G)$ trivially: otherwise it is nilpotent, and its nontrivial characteristic center makes it abelian. Their [group commutators](group-commutator.md) therefore vanish. An abelian [minimal normal subgroup](minimal-normal-subgroup.md) $M$ is an [elementary abelian p-group](elementary-abelian-group.md); $M\le O_p(G)$ and $M\cap Z(O_p(G))\ne1$ by the fixed-point counting of a [finite p-group](finite-p-group.md) on $M$. Minimal normality makes $M\le Z(O_p(G))$, and other [p-cores](p-core.md) commute with $M$.
