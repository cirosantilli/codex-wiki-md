# Imperfect soft Duffing-van der Pol oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)

The [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) are $0$ and $-\varepsilon\pm\sqrt{\varepsilon^2+\lambda}$. Their linear [trace](matrix-trace.md) is $\kappa-v_*^2$ and [determinant](determinant.md) $\lambda-4\varepsilon v_*-3v_*^2$. For nonzero $\varepsilon$ the symmetry-protected [pitchfork bifurcation](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md) splits into a [transcritical bifurcation](transcritical-bifurcation.md) at $\lambda=0$ and a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md) at $\lambda=-\varepsilon^2$. The [energy](energy.md) $H=\dot v^2/2+\lambda v^2/2-2\varepsilon v^3/3-v^4/4$ obeys $\dot H=(\kappa-v^2)\dot v^2$. For positive $\lambda$, the two outer [saddle equilibria](saddle-equilibrium.md) have unequal barrier heights, so the symmetric [heteroclinic cycle](heteroclinic-cycle.md) unfolds into separated one-way connections and a [homoclinic orbit](homoclinic-orbit.md) termination of the central stable [limit cycle](limit-cycle.md).
