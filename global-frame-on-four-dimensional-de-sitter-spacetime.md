# Global frame on four-dimensional de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Oriented frame bundle of de Sitter spacetime](oriented-frame-bundle-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

In global coordinates the [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md) metric is $-d\tau^2+\cosh^2\tau\,g_{S^3}$. A global [frame of a vector bundle](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) orthonormal for the round metric $Y_1,Y_2,Y_3$ on $S^3$ gives the displayed global [pseudo-orthonormal frame](pseudo-orthonormal-frame.md). For example, identify $S^3$ with the [unit quaternions](unit-quaternion.md) and use $qi,qj,qk$. Choosing the spatial orientation and the future direction consistently gives a [section of a fiber bundle](section-fiber-bundle.md) for the oriented, time-oriented frame bundle. The [principal bundle trivialization by a global section](principal-bundle-trivialization-by-a-global-section.md) then proves that bundle is trivial.
