# Associator

↑ **Parent:** [Monoidal category](monoidal-category.md)

The natural isomorphism $\alpha_{X,Y,Z}:(X\otimes Y)\otimes Z\to X\otimes(Y\otimes Z)$ in a [monoidal category](monoidal-category.md). It satisfies the pentagon axiom.
