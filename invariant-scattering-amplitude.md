# Invariant scattering amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md)

With relativistically normalized external states, $\mathcal M$ is the reduced [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) element after removing the [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) distribution. A [relativistic scattering cross-section](relativistic-scattering-cross-section.md) combines $|\mathcal M|^2$ with the [Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure](lorentz-invariant-phase-space-measure.md) and the [invariant flux factor](invariant-flux-factor.md). Identical-leg factorials in [Feynman vertices](interaction-vertex.md) and the [identical final-state symmetry factor](identical-particle-factor-in-a-final-state-phase-space-integral.md) in the event integral have different roles.
