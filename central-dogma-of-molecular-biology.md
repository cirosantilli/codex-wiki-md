# Central dogma of molecular biology

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_dogma_of_molecular_biology)

Sequence information may pass among [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) and from [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) to [protein](protein-split.md); the dogma excludes a general transfer from protein sequence back into nucleic-acid sequence. [Reverse transcription](reverse-transcription.md) does not contradict it.
