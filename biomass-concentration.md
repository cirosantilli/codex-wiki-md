# Biomass concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Chemostat](chemostat.md)

The [biomass concentration](biomass-concentration.md) is the mass of biological material per unit culture volume; here it is measured as dry cell mass in $\mathrm{g}\,\mathrm{L}^{-1}$. A [biomass concentration](biomass-concentration.md) differs from a cell count per unit volume because cell sizes can change. In the ideal [chemostat](chemostat.md), biomass leaves the vessel at rate $Qx$, giving a removal term $-Dx$ in the [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) for $x$.
