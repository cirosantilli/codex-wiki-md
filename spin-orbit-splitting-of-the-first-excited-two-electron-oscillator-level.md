# Spin-orbit splitting of the first excited two-electron oscillator level

↑ **Parent:** [Two-electron states in the first two shells of an isotropic oscillator](two-electron-states-in-the-first-two-shells-of-an-isotropic-oscillator.md)

For the interaction $\lambda\mathbf S\cdot\mathbf L$ built from the total two-electron [spin](spin.md) and [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md), set $\mathbf J=\mathbf L+\mathbf S$. The [spin-dot-product eigenvalue](spin-dot-product-eigenvalue.md) identity then gives the shift in the title. In the first excited two-electron oscillator manifold, the singlet has $L=1,S=0,J=1$ and zero shift with multiplicity three. The triplet has $L=S=1$, with $J=0,1,2$ shifts $-2\lambda\hbar^2,-\lambda\hbar^2,+\lambda\hbar^2$ and multiplicities $1,3,5$.

The multiplicities sum to twelve, and their weighted shift sums to zero. The specified interaction preserves both $L^2$ and $S^2$; it is distinct from summing separate single-electron spin-orbit interactions. Its shifts are exact within this model's invariant oscillator manifold, with weak coupling ensuring the original level ordering remains useful.
