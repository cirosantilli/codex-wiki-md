# Von Neumann double commutant theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Commutant of an operator algebra](commutant-of-an-operator-algebra.md)

For a unital star-subalgebra $A$ of the [bounded operators](continuous-linear-operator.md) on a complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), its [strong operator topology](strong-operator-topology.md) and [weak operator topology](weak-operator-topology.md) closures equal its double [commutant of an operator algebra](commutant-of-an-operator-algebra.md). To approximate $T\in A''$ on finitely many vectors, take their tuple in $H^m$. The projection onto its diagonal-$A$ cyclic subspace has matrix entries in $A'$, so it commutes with the diagonal action of $T$. Therefore the image tuple under $T$ lies in that cyclic subspace and can be approximated by a single $a\in A$.
