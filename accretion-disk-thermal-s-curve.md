# Accretion-disk thermal S-curve

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen-ionization disk instability](hydrogen-ionization-disk-instability.md)

At fixed disk radius, balancing local heating and cooling gives thermal equilibria parameterized by [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) and [temperature](temperature.md). In the [hydrogen-ionization disk instability](hydrogen-ionization-disk-instability.md), this locus has cold and hot stable branches joined by unstable equilibria. Crossing the turning points drives the state changes of a [dwarf nova](dwarf-nova.md). [Thermal stability of an accretion disk](thermal-stability-of-an-accretion-disk.md) tests the local heating and cooling response, while [viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md) changes the surface density.
