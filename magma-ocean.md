# Magma ocean

↑ **Parent:** [Magma](magma.md)

A large, potentially global region of extensive silicate melting in a young or strongly heated rocky body. Its crystallization involves [latent heat](latent-heat.md), crystal settling or flotation, and chemical fractionation. An early [magma ocean](magma-ocean.md) is distinct from the later, mostly solid [mantle convection](mantle-convection.md) of a planet. The Moon's proposed example is the [lunar magma ocean](lunar-magma-ocean.md).
