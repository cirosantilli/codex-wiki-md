# Straightedge and compass construction

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Straightedge_and_compass_construction)

A [compass-and-straightedge construction](straightedge-and-compass-construction-split.md) uses an unmarked straightedge to draw a [Euclidean line](straight-line.md) through two already constructed points, and a compass to draw a [circle](circle.md) with a constructed centre and radius. New points arise from intersections of these lines and circles. The allowed operations matter: an accurate numerical drawing need not supply such a construction.
