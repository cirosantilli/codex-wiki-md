<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [rational map](../../../../../rational-map-complex-analysis.md) from [irreducible](../../../../../irreducible-topological-space.md) $X$ to $Y$ is an equivalence class of [morphisms](../../../../../morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) $F_U:U\to Y$ defined on nonempty [open subsets](../../../../../open-set.md) $U\subseteq X$. Two representatives are equivalent if they agree on a nonempty [open subset](../../../../../open-set.md) of their [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md).

[Irreducibility](../../../../../irreducible-topological-space.md) makes all these nonempty opens [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) and ensures their [intersections](../../../../../set-intersection.md) are nonempty, so the equivalence expresses agreement at general [points](../../../../../point-geometry.md). A [rational map](../../../../../rational-map-complex-analysis.md) need not extend across all of $X$, and it need not be dominant. An [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md) [homomorphism](../../../../../homomorphism.md) of [function fields](../../../../../function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) additionally requires a [dominant morphism of affine varieties](../../../../../dominant-morphism-of-affine-varieties.md), or the corresponding condition on a [rational map](../../../../../rational-map-complex-analysis.md).
