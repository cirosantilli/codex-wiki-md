<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [morphism](../../../../../morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) $F:X\to Y$ is a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) map in the Zariski topologies such that for every open $V\subseteq Y$ and every [regular function](../../../../../regular-function.md) $h$ on $V$, the [pullback](../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) $h\circ F$ is regular on $F^{-1}(V)$.

Equivalently, near every [point](../../../../../point-geometry.md), choose [affine charts](../../../../../affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) of source and target; the target coordinates of $F$ must then be [regular functions](../../../../../regular-function.md) of the source coordinates. These local conditions agree on overlaps. In particular a [morphism](../../../../../morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) is defined at every [point](../../../../../point-geometry.md) of its source, not merely on a [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) [open subset](../../../../../open-set.md).
