<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [regular function](../../../../regular-function.md) on $U$ is a function $f:U\to k$ locally representable, in an [affine chart](../../../../affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) of the ambient [projective space](../../../../projective-space-split.md), as a quotient $a/b$ of [polynomial functions](../../../../polynomial-function.md) with denominator nonzero on that [neighbourhood](../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md). Equivalently it is locally a quotient of homogeneous forms of the same degree. The equal-degree condition makes the quotient independent of the chosen projective representative.

The [ring](../../../../ring.md) $\mathcal O_{X,p}$ is the [ring](../../../../ring.md) of [germs](../../../../germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) of such functions near $p$. Two representatives $(U,f)$ and $(V,g)$ define the same [germ](../../../../germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) when they agree on some smaller [neighbourhood](../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $p$. Addition and multiplication use a common [neighbourhood](../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md). It is a [local ring](../../../../local-ring.md): its unique [maximal ideal](../../../../maximal-ideal.md) consists of [germs](../../../../germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) vanishing at $p$, and any [germ](../../../../germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) nonzero at $p$ has a regular reciprocal on a smaller [neighbourhood](../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md).

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak m_p=\{[f]_p:f(p)=0\},\qquad\mathcal O_{X,p}/\mathfrak m_p\cong k.}
$$
