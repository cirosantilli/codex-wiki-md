<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [multiplicatively closed set](../../../../multiplicatively-closed-set.md) $S$ containing one, the [localization of a ring](../../../../localization-of-a-ring.md) $S^{-1}R$ consists of fractions $r/s$ with $r\in R,s\in S$, under the equivalence

$$
\boxed{\frac rs=\frac{r'}{s'}\iff\exists u\in S:\ u(rs'-r's)=0.}
$$

The factor $u$ is needed when the [ring](../../../../ring.md) has [zero divisors](../../../../zero-divisor.md). Addition and multiplication are

$$
\frac rs+\frac{r'}{s'}=\frac{rs'+r's}{ss'},\qquad
\frac rs\frac{r'}{s'}=\frac{rr'}{ss'}.
$$

The canonical map sends $r$ to $r/1$ and makes every element of $S$ a [unit](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md). It is universal with this property: a [ring homomorphism](../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) $\phi:R\to T$ taking $S$ into [units](../../../../unit-in-a-ring.md) extends uniquely by $r/s\mapsto\phi(r)\phi(s)^{-1}$. Usually zero is excluded from $S$; if it is included, this construction is the [zero ring](../../../../zero-ring.md).
