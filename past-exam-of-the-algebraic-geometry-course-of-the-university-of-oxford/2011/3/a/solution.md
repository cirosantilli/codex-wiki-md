A [quasi-projective variety](../../../../quasi-projective-variety.md) is a locally [closed subset](../../../../closed-set.md) of [projective space](../../../../projective-space-split.md): equivalently an [open subset](../../../../open-set.md) of some [projective variety](../../../../projective-variety.md), with the induced [algebraic variety](../../../../algebraic-variety-split.md) structure. [Affine varieties](../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md) are examples, via the standard [affine chart](../../../../affine-chart-of-a-variety.md) in [projective space](../../../../projective-space-split.md).

It is [irreducible](../../../../irreducible-topological-space.md) if it is nonempty and cannot be written as the [union](../../../../set-union.md) of two proper [closed subsets](../../../../closed-set.md). Equivalently, every two nonempty [open subsets](../../../../open-set.md) meet, or every nonempty [open subset](../../../../open-set.md) is [dense](../../../../dense-set.md). Closed and open are interpreted in the induced [Zariski topology](../../../../zariski-topology.md) of $X$.
