<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [radical of an ideal](../../../../radical-of-an-ideal.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sqrt I=\{f\in R:f^N\in I\text{ for some integer }N\geq1\}.}
$$

The [ideal](../../../../ideal.md) is [radical](../../../../radical-chemistry.md) if $I=\sqrt I$. Its [zero set](../../../../zero-set.md) is $V(I)=\{a\in k^n:f(a)=0\text{ for every }f\in I\}$, an [affine variety](../../../../affine-algebraic-set.md) by definition.

Every $f\in I$ vanishes at every [point](../../../../point-geometry.md) of $V(I)$, so $I\subseteq I(V(I))$. If $g^N\in I(V(I))$, then $g(a)^N=0$ in the [field](../../../../field.md) $k$ for every $a\in V(I)$, so $g(a)=0$ for each such [point](../../../../point-geometry.md). Hence $g\in I(V(I))$ and

$$
\boxed{I(V(I))\text{ is a radical ideal containing }I.}
$$
