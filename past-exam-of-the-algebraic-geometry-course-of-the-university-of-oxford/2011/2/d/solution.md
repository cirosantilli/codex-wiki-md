<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $S=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ and let $B=S/I(X)$ be the [homogeneous coordinate ring](../../../../homogeneous-coordinate-ring.md) of a nonempty [projective variety](../../../../projective-variety.md). Its [Hilbert function](../../../../hilbert-function.md) is $h_X(t)=\dim_k B_t$. For all sufficiently large integers $t$, this function agrees with a [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) $P_X(t)$, the [Hilbert polynomial](../../../../hilbert-polynomial.md).

The [dimension](../../../../dimension-of-an-algebraic-set.md) $r$ is the largest length of a strict chain of nonempty [irreducible](../../../../irreducible-topological-space.md) closed [subvarieties](../../../../closed-subvariety.md) of $X$. Equivalently $r=\dim B-1=\deg P_X$. The degree of the embedded [variety](../../../../algebraic-variety-split.md) is the integer specified by the leading term

$$
\boxed{P_X(t)=\frac{\deg X}{r!}t^r+\text{lower-degree terms}.}
$$

Geometrically it is the number of [intersection](../../../../set-intersection.md) [points](../../../../point-geometry.md) with a general [linear subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md) of codimension $r$, counted with [intersection multiplicities](../../../../intersection-multiplicity.md). The [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md)'s degree is the [dimension](../../../../dimension-of-an-algebraic-set.md) $r$; the [variety](../../../../algebraic-variety-split.md)'s degree is the [leading coefficient](../../../../leading-coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md) multiplied by $r!$. Keeping those two meanings separate is important in the next computation.
