# Black-hole spin measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole spin](black-hole-spin.md)

An inference of black-hole [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), usually quoted through $a_*$. Accretion-based techniques use the dependence of the stable inner orbit and relativistic [radiation](radiation.md) transfer on spin in the [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md) model. Disk truncation, inclination, illumination and atmosphere assumptions can imitate a change of spin and must enter the uncertainty model.
