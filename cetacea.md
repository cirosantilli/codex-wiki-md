# Cetacea

↑ **Parent:** [Artiodactyla](artiodactyla.md)

The lineage of whales, dolphins and porpoises. Its [Eocene](eocene.md) [fossils](fossil.md) document a sequence from terrestrial or amphibious relatives to fully aquatic forms; living members comprise [Odontoceti](odontoceti.md) and [Mysticeti](mysticeti.md).
