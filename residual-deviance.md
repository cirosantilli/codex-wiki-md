# Residual deviance

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis of deviance for nested generalized linear models](analysis-of-deviance-for-nested-generalized-linear-models.md)

The unscaled residual deviance of an [exponential dispersion family](exponential-dispersion-model.md) compares its fitted mean with the [saturated statistical model](saturated-statistical-model.md) at a common [dispersion parameter](dispersion-parameter.md). Its normalization removes the factor $1/\phi$ in the [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md). In [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.md) and [binomial regression](binomial-regression.md), $\phi=1$, so it is twice the saturated-minus-fitted [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md). Under suitable regularity, $D/\phi$ has approximately a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with the [residual degrees of freedom](residual-degrees-of-freedom.md); this is not an automatic accurate approximation for sparse responses. Nested deviance reductions with estimated dispersion lead to approximate [F-tests](f-test.md) after scaling by the dispersion estimate.
