# Countability of pairwise disjoint open disks

↑ **Parent:** [Countable set](countable-set.md)

Every family of pairwise disjoint nonempty open disks in the plane is countable. The [density of the rational numbers](density-of-the-rational-numbers.md) gives each disk a point of the [countable set](countable-set.md) $\mathbb Q^2$. Fixing an enumeration and taking the first point in each disk yields an [injection](injective-function.md) from disks to labels. This argument requires no countable choice of representatives and is useful when geometric separation would otherwise produce an uncountable family.
