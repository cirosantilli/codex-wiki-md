# Eikonal equation for a variable-speed wave

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics.md)

For the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) $\varphi_{tt}-\nabla\cdot(c^2\nabla\varphi)=0$ with slow variables and phase $e^{i\theta/\varepsilon}$, define $\omega=-\theta_T$ and $\mathbf k=\nabla\theta$. The leading balance is $\omega^2=c^2|\mathbf k|^2$. Its positive-frequency [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) is $\Omega=c|\mathbf k|$, and the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $c\mathbf k/|\mathbf k|$.
