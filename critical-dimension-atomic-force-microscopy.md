# Critical-dimension atomic force microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

An AFM approach using calibrated side-sensitive tip geometry and position control to measure steep or reentrant features. A flared tip can reach surfaces that an ordinary tapered probe cannot; calibration must account for its effective width.[Flared probes, lateral sensing and effective tip width](effects-of-lateral-tip-control-in-cd-afm-width-metrology-by-ronald-g-dixson-and-others.md)
