# Nested Monte Carlo

↑ **Parent:** [Monte Carlo method](monte-carlo-method.md)

[Nested Monte Carlo](nested-monte-carlo.md) approximates an outer [expected value](expected-value.md) whose integrand contains another [expected value](expected-value.md). A nonlinear transformation, such as a [logarithm](logarithm.md), of an inner sample average usually introduces finite-sample bias. Increasing both sample sizes requires suitable integrability controls to establish consistency; an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of the inner quantity does not make its [logarithm](logarithm.md) unbiased.
