# Copositive reformulation of an orthant Rayleigh minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Copositive optimization](copositive-optimization.md)

Let $\alpha=\min\{x^TQx:x\geq0,\ \|x\|_2=1\}$ for a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $Q$. Compactness gives attainment. Homogeneity shows $Q-\lambda I$ is a [copositive matrix](copositive-matrix.md) exactly when $\lambda\leq\alpha$, proving

$$
\alpha=\max\{\lambda:Q-\lambda I\in\operatorname{COP}_n\}.
$$

Its [conic program](conic-optimization.md) dual is the trace-normalized [completely positive optimization](completely-positive-optimization.md) problem. A minimizing [vector](vector.md) gives $X=xx^T$, which certifies equality and dual attainment directly. In general $\alpha$ differs from the unrestricted smallest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md).
