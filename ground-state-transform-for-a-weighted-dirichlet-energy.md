# Ground-state transform for a weighted Dirichlet energy

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted Poincare inequality](weighted-poincare-inequality.md)

For $w=e^{-\Phi}$ and $g=he^{-\Phi/2}$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\int|h'|^2e^{-\Phi}
=\int|g'|^2+\int\left(\frac{|\Phi'|^2}{4}-\frac{\Phi''}{2}\right)|g|^2.
$$

This conjugates the weighted Dirichlet energy to a [Schrödinger operator](schrodinger-operator.md) with effective potential $|\Phi'|^2/4-\Phi''/2$.
