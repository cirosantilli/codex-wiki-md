# Lorentz transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorentz_transformation)

A Lorentz transformation preserves the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md). A boost of speed $u$ along the $x$-axis uses $\beta=u/c$ and

$$
ct'=\gamma(ct-\beta x),\qquad
x'=\gamma(x-\beta ct).
$$
