# Spinor decomposition of gauge curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md)

In complexified four-dimensional space-time, antisymmetry gives

$$
F_{AA'BB'}=\epsilon_{AB}\varphi_{A'B'}+\epsilon_{A'B'}\varphi_{AB},
$$

where both [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) [two-component spinors](two-component-spinor.md) are symmetric. The two summands are the two duality components. In the convention where the primed symmetric part is self-dual, the [Anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations](anti-self-dual-yang-mills-equations.md) are $\varphi_{A'B'}=0$. Equivalently $\pi^{A'}\pi^{B'}F_{AA'BB'}=0$ for every primed [two-component spinor](two-component-spinor.md) $\pi$, since a quadratic polynomial vanishing for every [two-component spinor](two-component-spinor.md) has all symmetric coefficients zero.
