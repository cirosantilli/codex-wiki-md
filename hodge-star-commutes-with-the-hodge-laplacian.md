# Hodge star commutes with the Hodge Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md)

On an oriented [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), the [codifferential](codifferential.md) convention $\delta=(-1)^{n(p+1)+1}*d*$ and $*^2=(-1)^{p(n-p)}$ give $d*\alpha=(-1)^p*\delta\alpha$ and $\delta*\alpha=(-1)^{p+1}*d\alpha$. Applying these twice proves the displayed identity. Thus the [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) preserves [harmonic differential forms](harmonic-differential-form.md) even without compactness.
