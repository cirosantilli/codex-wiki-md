# Brinkman swimming sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor swimming sheet](taylor-swimming-sheet.md)

A small transverse [Taylor swimming sheet](taylor-swimming-sheet.md) in a Brinkman medium obeys $(\nabla^2-A^2)\nabla^2\psi=0$ with $A=\alpha/k$. Its no-slip material [velocity](velocity.md) is imposed on the moving sheet. The matrix's preferred frame and the mean [force-free](force-free.md) condition must be included when finding the second-order swimming speed.
