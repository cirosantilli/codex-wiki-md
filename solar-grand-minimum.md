# Solar grand minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Solar cycle](solar-cycle.md)

A solar grand minimum is a prolonged interval, lasting several ordinary cycles, of strongly reduced sunspot activity. Historical records identify the [Maunder minimum](maunder-minimum.md); cosmogenic carbon-14 and beryllium-10 provide longer indirect records because stronger heliospheric magnetic shielding generally reduces cosmic-ray production of these isotopes. Weak activity does not imply a zero [solar magnetic field](solar-magnetic-field.md). Proposed causes include stochastic weakening of poloidal-field generation, nonlinear dynamo modulation, and intermittency near an excitation threshold.
