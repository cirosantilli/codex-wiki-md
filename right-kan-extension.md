# Right Kan extension

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint functors](adjoint-functors.md)

The right Kan extension along $K:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ is the right adjoint to precomposition by $K$ between suitable [functor categories](functor-category.md).

This is the right-adjoint form of [Kan extension](kan-extension.md); left Kan extension is its left-adjoint counterpart.
