# Diagonal monomial action of a second-order Euler operator

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate polynomial](multivariate-polynomial.md)

The [monomial](monomial.md) $x^iy^j$ is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of the differential [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $x^2\partial_x^2+y^2\partial_y^2$, with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $i(i-1)+j(j-1)$. Over the real or complex numbers this vanishes exactly for $i,j\in\{0,1\}$. On [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of bounded [total degree](total-degree-of-a-polynomial.md), the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is therefore spanned by the permitted members of $1,x,y,xy$, while the [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) is spanned by all remaining [monomials](monomial.md). The [trace](matrix-trace.md) is the sum of those diagonal [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md).
