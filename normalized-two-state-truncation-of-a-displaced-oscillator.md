# Normalized two-state truncation of a displaced oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Electric-field displacement of a quantum harmonic oscillator](electric-field-displacement-of-a-quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)

Let $x_{\rm zp}=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}$ and $\eta=x_0/(2x_{\rm zp})$. The normalized first-order truncation is $(|0\rangle+\eta|1\rangle)/\sqrt{1+\eta^2}$. Its mean displacement is $2\eta x_{\rm zp}/(1+\eta^2)$, agreeing with $x_0$ only at small $|\eta|$. The exact translated [ground state](ground-state.md) is a [coherent state](coherent-state.md) whose expansion contains all [number states](number-state.md) and has mean occupation $\eta^2$. Correct linear response does not justify extrapolating a truncated state to large fields.
