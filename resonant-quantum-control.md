# Resonant quantum control

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian engineering](hamiltonian-engineering.md)

A near-resonant drive selects a transition and, in an appropriate rotating frame and [rotating-wave approximation](rotating-wave-approximation.md), controls a two-level effective [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). If $H_{\mathrm{eff}}=\Omega(t)\sigma_x/2$ with fixed axis, then $U(T)=\exp[-i\sigma_x\int_0^T\Omega(t)dt/2]$. Pulse area fixes the rotation angle; drive phase chooses a transverse axis. Selectivity and neglect of rapidly oscillating terms require the appropriate detuning and drive-strength regime.
