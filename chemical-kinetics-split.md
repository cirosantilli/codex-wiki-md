# Chemical kinetics

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_kinetics)

[Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md) studies how quickly [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md) proceed and how their [reaction rates](reaction-rate.md) depend on composition and conditions. It connects a measured change in [concentration](concentration.md) to a [rate law](rate-law.md), then uses a [rate equation](rate-equation.md) to predict future [concentrations](concentration.md). This makes it useful for choosing how long to wait for conversion and for testing a proposed [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md). Changing [temperature](temperature-split.md), [reactant](reactant.md) [concentrations](concentration.md), or the presence of a [catalyst](catalyst.md) can change [reaction rates](reaction-rate.md); see [Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, §12.2: factors affecting reaction rates](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/12-2-factors-affecting-reaction-rates).

[Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) specifies the eventual balance under given constraints; [chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md) determines how rapidly it is approached. At fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md), a decrease in [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) favours conversion but does not guarantee a fast [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md): reaching a [transition state](transition-state.md) can require crossing a substantial barrier. Thus a mixture can change imperceptibly over an observation period without being at [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md). The distinction separates which direction is favoured from how long conversion takes. See [12.5 Collision Theory](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/12-5-collision-theory.md) (§12.5: collision theory and reaction barriers) and [Chemistry 2e, §16.1: spontaneity and speed](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/16-1-spontaneity).
