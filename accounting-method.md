# Accounting method

↑ **Parent:** [Amortized analysis](amortized-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accounting_method)

The [accounting method](accounting-method.md) charges operations enough to pay for their work and save credits for later work. A nonnegative balance from an initially empty account makes every prefix's total charge an upper bound on its actual cost. Choosing the balance to be $\Phi$ gives the [potential method](potential-method.md); the ledger for [dynamic array doubling](dynamic-array-doubling.md) illustrates this equivalence.[Robert Endre Tarjan, Amortized Computational Complexity, section 2, pp. 307–308: banker's credits and equivalence with the potential viewpoint](https://www.math.u-szeged.hu/~hajnal/courses/MSc_AlgoBony/2021/Tarjan.pdf)
