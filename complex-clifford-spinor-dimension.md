# Complex Clifford spinor dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md)

After complexifying a nondegenerate metric in $D=2k$ dimensions, pair [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) into $k$ fermionic creation and annihilation operators. Their binary occupation states span $2^k$ dimensions, and the [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) generate all endomorphisms of this space. The Dirac module is therefore irreducible as a Clifford module. The even algebra preserves the chirality grading, splitting its Lorentz-spinor representation into two irreducible Weyl modules of dimension $2^{k-1}$. In $D=2k+1$, adjoining the old [chirality matrix](chirality-matrix.md) as the extra gamma gives an irreducible $2^k$-dimensional [Spin group](spin-group.md) representation with no Weyl splitting. The two possible signs of that extra gamma distinguish Clifford modules but give equivalent [Spin group](spin-group.md) modules.
