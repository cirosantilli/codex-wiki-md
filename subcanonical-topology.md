# Subcanonical topology

↑ **Parent:** [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md)

A Grothendieck topology is subcanonical when every [representable functor](representable-functor.md) is a [sheaf on a site](sheaf-on-a-site.md). It is also called standard in some topos-theory terminology. A nontrivial covering quotient can fail this condition by identifying distinct sections of a representable.
