<h1 id="levy-continuity-theorem">Lévy continuity theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic function](characteristic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lévy_continuity_theorem)

If the [characteristic functions](characteristic-function.md) $\varphi_n$ of [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md) $\mu_n$ converge pointwise to a function $\varphi$ that is continuous at zero, then $\varphi$ is a characteristic function and $\mu_n$ [converges in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md) to its distribution. Conversely, convergence in distribution implies pointwise convergence of the characteristic functions.
