# Biophotovoltaic device

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemistry](electrochemistry-split.md)

A [biophotovoltaic device](biophotovoltaic-device.md) couples photosynthetic biological activity to an external electrical circuit. Measured current depends on intracellular electron supply, export, electrode reactions and abiotic background.
