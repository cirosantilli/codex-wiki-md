# Feigenbaum geometric potential

↑ **Parent:** [Feigenbaum period-doubling map](feigenbaum-period-doubling-map.md)

The geometric symbolic potential for the [Feigenbaum period-doubling map](feigenbaum-period-doubling-map.md) has uniformly negative bounded values and exponentially decreasing variations. Potential sums describe logarithmic lengths of odd-address period-doubling intervals up to a uniform additive error. This is a geometric scaling potential, distinct from an unbounded derivative potential at a critical point. Its pressure is continuous, strictly decreasing and convex by the [almost-additive partition-function limit](almost-additive-partition-function-limit.md) and finite-volume inequalities.
