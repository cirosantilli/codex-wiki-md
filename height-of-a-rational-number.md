# Height of a rational number

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute multiplicative Weil height](absolute-multiplicative-weil-height.md)

For coprime integers $p,q$ with $q>0$,

$$
H(p/q)=\max\{|p|,q\}.
$$

This follows directly from the real and [p-adic absolute values](p-adic-absolute-value.md), or from the [height-Mahler measure formula](height-mahler-measure-formula.md) for the primitive polynomial $qX-p$.
