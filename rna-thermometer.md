# RNA thermometer

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

An [RNA thermometer](rna-thermometer.md) uses a temperature-dependent RNA structural equilibrium to regulate expression, often by masking or exposing a translation-initiation region. Demonstration requires a relevant temperature range and sequence-dependent structural controls.
