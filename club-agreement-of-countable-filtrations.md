# Club agreement of countable filtrations

↑ **Parent:** [Kappa-filtration](kappa-filtration.md)

For two [kappa-filtrations](kappa-filtration.md) at $\kappa=\omega_1$ exhausting sets of size $\aleph_1$, any bijection between the sets carries corresponding stages onto one another on a [club set](club-set.md) of indices. Bound both forward and inverse images at each countable stage by a function $g:\omega_1\to\omega_1$. The limit ordinals closed under all earlier $g$-values form a club; continuity gives equality at those ordinals.
