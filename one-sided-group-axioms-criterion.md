# One-sided group axioms criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Group axioms](group-axioms.md)

An [associative](associative-property.md) [binary operation](binary-operation.md) $*$ on a nonempty [set](set-split.md) $X$ makes $X$ a [group](group-split.md) if there is $e\in X$ with $x*e=x$ for every $x$, and every $x$ has some $z$ with $x*z=e$. No left [identity element](identity-element.md) or left [inverse element](inverse-element.md) assumption is needed. First, an [idempotent element of a semigroup](idempotent-element-of-a-semigroup.md) $y$ satisfies $y=e$: choose $z$ with $y*z=e$ and compute $y=y*e=y*(y*z)=(y*y)*z=y*z=e$. If $x*z=e$, then $(z*x)*(z*x)=(z*(x*z))*x=(z*e)*x=z*x$, so $z*x=e$ by the previous observation. Finally, $e*x=(x*z)*x=x*(z*x)=x*e=x$. Thus all [group axioms](group-axioms.md) follow.
