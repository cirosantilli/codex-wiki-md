# Lie derivative of a tensor field

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor derivation](tensor-derivation.md)

For a smooth [vector field](vector-field.md) $X$, the Lie derivative is the [tensor derivation](tensor-derivation.md) determined by $\mathcal L_Xf=X(f)$ and $\mathcal L_XY=[X,Y]$. The identity $[X,fY]=f[X,Y]+X(f)Y$ permits its unique extension. On a [differential one-form](one-form.md),

$$
(\mathcal L_X\omega)(Y)=X(\omega(Y))-\omega([X,Y]).
$$

On [differential forms](differential-form-split.md) it agrees with the usual [Lie derivative of a differential form](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) and [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md).
