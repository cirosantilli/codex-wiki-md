# Promoter (genetics)

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promoter_(genetics))

A [gene promoter](promoter-genetics.md) is a [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) region involved in initiating [transcription](transcription-biology.md). Moving a coding sequence into a nuclear [genome](genome.md) does not guarantee that its original [gene promoter](promoter-genetics.md) can drive nuclear [gene expression](gene-expression.md).
