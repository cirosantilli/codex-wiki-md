# L2-bounded martingale convergence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md)

An $L^2$-bounded real [martingale](martingale-split.md) has a square-integrable terminal value, converges to it almost surely and in $L^2$, and satisfies $M_t=\mathbb E[M_\infty\mid\mathcal F_t]$. The $L^2$ Cauchy property follows from orthogonality of increments and monotonicity of $\mathbb EM_t^2$. This terminal representation permits optional sampling at finite unbounded [stopping times](stopping-time.md) and uniform-integrability control of stopped squares by conditional Jensen.
