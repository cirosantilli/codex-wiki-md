# Low-energy Fermi-liquid quasiparticle lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Landau quasiparticle](landau-quasiparticle.md)

The [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) restricts final states available to a low-energy [Landau quasiparticle](landau-quasiparticle.md) and the particle–[hole](electron-hole.md) pair it excites. In a regular three-dimensional [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md), this phase-space restriction makes the decay rate quadratic in excitation [energy](energy-split.md) or [temperature](temperature-split.md). The excitation's width divided by its [energy](energy-split.md) therefore vanishes at the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md), allowing an asymptotically sharp quasiparticle despite interactions.
