# Geometric-offspring critical branching process

↑ **Parent:** [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md)

The offspring [probabilities](probability.md) $P(K=j)=2^{-j-1}$ for $j\geq0$ have [expected value](expected-value.md) one. For one initial individual, the [branching-process generating-function iteration](branching-process-generating-function-iteration.md) gives

$$
G_n(s)=\frac{n-(n-1)s}{n+1-ns},\quad P(X_n=0)=\frac n{n+1},\quad
P(X_n=j)=\frac1{(n+1)^2}\left(\frac n{n+1}\right)^{j-1}\ (j\geq1).
$$

For $n\geq1$ the [PGF](probability-generating-function.md) disk is $|s|<1+1/n$; $G_0(s)=s$ separately. Conditional on survival, the population is positive geometric with success [probability](probability.md) $1/(n+1)$, leading to the [exponential limit of surviving geometric branching](exponential-limit-of-surviving-geometric-branching.md).
