# Dark energy

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_energy)

Dark energy is a smooth component with sufficiently negative pressure to drive accelerated cosmic expansion. The [supernova discovery of accelerating expansion](supernova-discovery-of-accelerating-expansion.md) measures an expansion history through [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md); interpreting that history as a component of the stress tensor additionally assumes [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md). A [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) is one possibility, whereas [dynamical dark energy](dynamical-dark-energy.md) permits a changing [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md).
