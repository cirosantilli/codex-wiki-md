# Inverse quantum circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)

The inverse of a quantum circuit applies the adjoints of its gates in reverse temporal order. The [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md), [Pauli Z gate](pauli-z-gate.md), and [controlled-NOT gate](controlled-not-gate.md) are each self-inverse.
