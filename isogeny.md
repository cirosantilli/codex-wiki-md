# Isogeny

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic group](algebraic-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isogeny)

An isogeny is a surjective [morphism](morphism.md) of [algebraic groups](algebraic-group.md) with finite kernel. For [abelian varieties](abelian-variety-split.md) it is a finite surjective group morphism. An [isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md) is its one-dimensional abelian-variety specialization.
