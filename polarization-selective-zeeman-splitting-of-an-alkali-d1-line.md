# Polarization-selective Zeeman splitting of an alkali D1 line

↑ **Parent:** [Anomalous Zeeman effect](anomalous-zeeman-effect.md)

For the electronic $^2S_{1/2}\to{}^2P_{1/2}$ transition, neglect [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) and use weak-field [LS coupling](ls-coupling.md). The [Landé g-factors](lande-g-factor.md) are $g_g=2$ and $g_e=2/3$, so a transition from $m_g$ to $m_e$ shifts by $(\mu_BB/h)(g_em_e-g_gm_g)$.

With propagation perpendicular to the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) parallel to the field drives the $\pi$ [electric-dipole selection rule](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md) $\Delta m=0$. The two shifts are $\pm2\mu_BB/(3h)$. Perpendicular linear polarization is an equal-amplitude combination of the two field-axis circular components, driving $\sigma^\pm$ transitions with $\Delta m=\pm1$ and shifts $\pm4\mu_BB/(3h)$. Each geometry therefore selects a doublet, with twice the separation in the perpendicular-polarization case. Equal line heights within a pair require equal lower-state populations and an unsaturating probe; line positions do not require that extra population assumption.
