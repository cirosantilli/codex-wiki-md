# Cut norm

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)

For a [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) $H$ on a nonempty finite [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) $X\times Y$, its normalized cut norm is the maximum of $|\mathbb E_{x,y}H(x,y)1_A(x)1_B(y)|$ over $A\subseteq X,B\subseteq Y$. It measures all rectangular subset discrepancies. The convention using arbitrary test functions bounded by one is equivalent within a factor of four, by decomposing their positive and negative parts into [indicator functions](indicator-function.md).
