# Fundamental plane (elliptical galaxies)

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptical galaxy](elliptical-galaxy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_plane_(elliptical_galaxies))

The fundamental plane relates effective radius, stellar [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md), and mean [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) of [elliptical galaxies](elliptical-galaxy.md). Homologous [virial equilibrium](virial-equilibrium.md) with constant [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md) predicts $R_e\propto\sigma^2/I_e$; empirical exponents differ. The two-variable [Faber-Jackson relation](faber-jackson-relation.md) is a projection, not the entire plane.
