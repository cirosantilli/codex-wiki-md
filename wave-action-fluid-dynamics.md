# Wave action (fluid dynamics)

↑ **Parent:** [Wave activity](wave-activity.md)

For a small-amplitude conservative wave packet in a slowly varying [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md), its intrinsic [energy](energy.md) divided by [intrinsic frequency](intrinsic-frequency.md) is transported along its [group velocity](group-velocity.md), with the basic [advection](advection.md) added in the laboratory frame. A stationary background conserves laboratory [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), but a changing mean [velocity](velocity.md) changes the intrinsic one through the [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md); intrinsic [energy](energy.md) alone need not be conserved. Geometrical spreading and dissipation enter the action-flux balance.
