# Covariance of a field equation does not fix homogeneous solutions

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md)

If a [differential operator](differential-operator.md) $L$ is a [linear map](linear-map.md) and has nonzero [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), the equation $LA=J$ determines $A$ only up to a [homogeneous solution](homogeneous-solution.md). [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md) of $L$ and $J$ therefore does not force independently selected solutions in different [inertial frames](inertial-frame.md) to obey a particular [tensor](tensor.md) transformation law. A covariant [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) and consistently transformed homogeneous data do provide such a choice. For the [four-potential](electromagnetic-four-potential.md) in [Lorenz gauge](lorenz-gauge-condition.md), $\Box A^\mu=-\mu_0J^\mu$ still permits $A^\mu\mapsto A^\mu+\partial^\mu\chi$ with $\Box\chi=0$, a [residual Lorenz gauge symmetry](residual-lorenz-gauge-symmetry.md). Constant spatial potentials for zero sources are a concrete example.
