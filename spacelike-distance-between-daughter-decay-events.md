# Spacelike distance between daughter decay events

↑ **Parent:** [Spacelike separation](spacelike-separation.md)

For a parent of mass $M$ decaying into two freely propagating daughters of equal mass $m$, whose proper survival times are $\tau_1,\tau_2$, the separation of their decay events has the displayed spacelike squared length when positive. It follows from their invariant relative four-velocity contraction. The distance in a simultaneous inertial frame is therefore independent of the laboratory boost of the parent; when the expression is not positive such a simultaneous frame does not exist.
