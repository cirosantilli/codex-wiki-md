# Entropy of a finite measurable partition

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodic theory](ergodic-theory.md)

For a finite measurable partition $\xi$, its entropy is

$$
H_\mu(\xi)=-\sum_{A\in\xi}\mu(A)\log\mu(A),
$$

with $0\log0=0$.
