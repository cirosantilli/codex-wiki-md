# Complex cosine

↑ **Parent:** [Cosine](cosine.md)

The extension of the [cosine](cosine.md) to the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) is $\cos z=(e^{iz}+e^{-iz})/2$. Splitting the [complex exponential function](complex-exponential-function.md) into its [real part](real-part.md) and [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) gives the displayed formula in terms of [hyperbolic cosine](hyperbolic-cosine.md) and [hyperbolic sine](hyperbolic-sine.md).
