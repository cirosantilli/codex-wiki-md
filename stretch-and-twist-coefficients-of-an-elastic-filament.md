# Stretch and twist coefficients of an elastic filament

↑ **Parent:** [Worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md)

For an [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md) with reference arclength $s$, curvature $c$, axial [strain](strain.md) $\epsilon$ and excess twist rate $\Omega$, a harmonic [energy](energy-split.md) [density](density.md) is $(K_bc^2+S\epsilon^2+K_t\Omega^2)/2$. Bending and torsional rigidities have units of [energy](energy-split.md) times [length](length.md); the stretch modulus has units of [force](force-split.md). Factoring out $k_BT$ therefore makes $A,C$ [lengths](length.md) but $B$ an inverse [length](length.md). Axial [strain](strain.md) under [tension](tension.md) is approximately $F/S$. Twist is controlled by [torque](torque.md) and rotational [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), not [force](force-split.md) alone. A three-dimensional thermally fluctuating [worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md) has [persistence length](persistence-length.md) $K_b/(k_BT)$.
