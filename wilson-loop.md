# Wilson loop

↑ **Parent:** [Wilson line](wilson-line.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilson_loop)

With [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md) $D=d+A$, a Wilson loop is the [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) in a specified [group representation](group-representation.md) of [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) around a closed curve. The [holonomy of a connection](holonomy.md) changes by conjugation under [gauge equivalence of principal connections](gauge-equivalence-of-principal-connections.md), so its trace is gauge-invariant. It detects global connection data even when the local [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) is zero.
