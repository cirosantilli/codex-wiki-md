# Electrical double-layer capacitance

↑ **Parent:** [Supercapacitor](supercapacitor.md)

Charge-storage response of separated ionic and electronic charge at an electrode-electrolyte interface. The differential capacitance is $C=dQ/dV$; it can vary with [electrode](electrode.md) potential, [ion](ion.md) packing and surface structure.
