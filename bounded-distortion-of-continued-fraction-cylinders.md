# Bounded distortion of continued-fraction cylinders

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss continued-fraction map](gauss-continued-fraction-map.md)

The inverse branch of the $n$th iterate of the [Gauss continued-fraction map](gauss-continued-fraction-map.md) on a fixed digit cylinder has the displayed form, with [continued fraction convergents](continued-fraction-convergent.md) $p_j/q_j$. Its derivative has absolute value $(q_n+q_{n-1}y)^{-2}$. Since $q_{n-1}\leq q_n$, the maximum-to-minimum derivative ratio is at most four. The density of the [Gauss measure](gauss-measure.md) changes this ratio by at most a further factor two. The cylinders shrink to irrational points, so this uniform distortion bound and the [Lebesgue density theorem](lebesgue-s-density-theorem.md) prove ergodicity of the map.
