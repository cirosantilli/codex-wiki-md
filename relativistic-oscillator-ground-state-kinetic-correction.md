# Relativistic oscillator ground-state kinetic correction

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

The expansion of the [relativistic kinetic energy](relativistic-kinetic-energy.md) at fixed [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) gives the perturbation $-p^4/(8m^3c^2)$. In the harmonic-oscillator [ground state](ground-state.md), $\langle p^4\rangle=3(m\hbar\omega/2)^2$, proving the displayed [first-order energy correction](first-order-energy-correction.md). Its small parameter is $\hbar\omega/(mc^2)$. This correction lowers the nonrelativistic [energy](energy-split.md) and does not include other relativistic interactions.
