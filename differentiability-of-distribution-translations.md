# Differentiability of distribution translations

↑ **Parent:** [Translation of a distribution](translation-of-a-distribution.md)

With [translation of a distribution](translation-of-a-distribution.md) defined by $\langle\tau_hu,\varphi\rangle=\langle u,\varphi(\mathord\cdot+h)\rangle$, differentiation of the [test function](test-function.md) gives $\frac d{dh}\tau_hu=-\tau_hu'$ in [weak convergence of distributions](weak-convergence-of-distributions.md). The [Taylor theorem](taylor-theorem.md) shows convergence in the [space of test functions](space-of-test-functions.md), including a common [compact support](compact-support.md). Thus $(\tau_{-h}u-u)/h\to u'$; fixing the translation convention prevents a sign error.
