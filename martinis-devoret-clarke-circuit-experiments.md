# Martinis-Devoret-Clarke circuit experiments

↑ **Parent:** [Current-biased Josephson phase dynamics](current-biased-josephson-phase-dynamics.md)

The 1985 spectroscopy experiment used [niobium](niobium.md)-based tunnel [Josephson junctions](josephson-junction.md) on [silicon](silicon.md), thermally anchored with final bias [low-pass filters](low-pass-filter.md) to a [dilution refrigerator](dilution-refrigerator.md). Calibrated [germanium](germanium.md) and [cobalt](cobalt.md) thermometers measured [temperature](temperature-split.md). A filtered [coaxial cable](coaxial-cable.md) injected [microwaves](microwave.md); repeated switches to nonzero [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) measured escape with irradiation on and off. Bias scans at fixed [microwave](microwave.md) [frequency](frequency.md) resolved separate enhancements matching calculated unequal [energy level](energy-level.md) spacings. Raising [temperature](temperature-split.md) merged the structure toward classical response. Junction parameters were calibrated in the classical regime; reducing the [superconducting critical current](superconducting-critical-current.md) with a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) checked thermal readout. Parameter uncertainties limited absolute resonance positions.[Original paper, pp.1543–1545, apparatus and Figs.2–4; p.1546, numerical boundary condition](energy-level-quantization-in-the-zero-voltage-state-of-a-current-biased-josephson-junction-by-john-m-martinis-and-others.md)

The companion escape experiment mounted a tunnel [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md) on an attenuating [coaxial cable](coaxial-cable.md), cold-anchored its bias filters, ramped the [electric current](electric-current.md), and counted switches to nonzero [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). Rapid current shutoff limited heating. Classical escape and [microwave](microwave.md) resonance calibrated the [superconducting critical current](superconducting-critical-current.md), [capacitance](capacitance.md) and environmental loading. Low-temperature escape agreed with the quantum prediction using these calibrated parameters. A [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) reduced the [superconducting critical current](superconducting-critical-current.md) to keep the same apparatus thermally activated at lower [temperature](temperature-split.md), testing excess noise. Frequency-dependent loading contributed uncertainty; the predicted [damping](damping.md) correction was smaller than combined errors, so the experiment did not resolve that correction.[Original paper, pp.1909–1910, calibration, Fig.2 noise control and dissipation limit](measurements-of-macroscopic-quantum-tunneling-out-of-the-zero-voltage-state-of-a-current-biased-josephson-junction-by-michel-h-devoret-and-others.md)

Together these tests connect spectral structure and escape of the same kind of collective coordinate. They do not constitute a demonstration of coherent gate operations on a [qubit](qubit.md), or prove that every macroscopic coordinate retains quantum behavior under arbitrary environmental coupling. The attraction is more concrete: a fabricated [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) admits both an independently measurable classical model and quantitative quantum tests of its phase dynamics.
