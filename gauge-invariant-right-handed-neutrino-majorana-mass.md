# Gauge-invariant right-handed neutrino Majorana mass

↑ **Parent:** [Right-handed neutrino](right-handed-neutrino.md)

A [right-handed neutrino](right-handed-neutrino.md) is a singlet of the entire [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) gauge group. Its same-chirality bilinear with the field obtained by [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) therefore admits the displayed renormalizable [Majorana mass term](majorana-mass-term.md), in addition to a Higgs-induced [Dirac mass term](dirac-mass-term.md). It violates [lepton number](lepton-number.md) by two units. Analogous terms for electrically charged Standard Model fermions carry nonzero [electric charge](electric-charge.md); quarks also have nontrivial [color charge](color-charge.md). The presence of a right-handed field permits mass but does not force a nonzero mass parameter.
