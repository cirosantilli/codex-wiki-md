# Moon

↑ **Parent:** [Natural satellite](natural-satellite.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon)

The [Moon](moon.md) is [Earth](earth.md)'s [natural satellite](natural-satellite.md). Its [synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md) keeps approximately the same [near side of the Moon](near-side-of-the-moon.md) facing [Earth](earth.md), while both the [near side of the Moon](near-side-of-the-moon.md) and the [far side of the Moon](far-side-of-the-moon.md) experience sunlight and darkness. See [illumination of a synchronously rotating moon](illumination-of-a-synchronously-rotating-moon.md) for the geometry. [NASA: Moon facts](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/).
