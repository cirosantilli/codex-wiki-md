# Mirror of a link

↑ **Parent:** [Link isotopy](link-isotopy.md)

Apply an orientation-reversing [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) of $S^3$ to a [link](link.md) and transport its component orientations. Reflection through the projection plane produces a [link diagram](link-diagram.md) by switching every crossing.
