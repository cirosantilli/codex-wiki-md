# Exact factor pricing without idiosyncratic risk

↑ **Parent:** [Arbitrage pricing theory](arbitrage-pricing-theory.md)

For returns $r_i=a_i+\sum_jb_{ji}f_j$, fund a risky position $v$ by a [risk-free asset](risk-free-asset.md). Its zero-cost payoff is $(a-R\mathbf1)^Tv+\sum_j(Bv)_jf_j$. If $Bv=0$, a nonzero constant term would create an [arbitrage](arbitrage.md) after choosing the sign of $v$. Hence $a-R\mathbf1$ annihilates $\ker B$ and lies in the row span of $B$. Taking [expectations](expected-value.md) gives exact factor-premium pricing. Full row [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md) makes the coefficients unique; if exposures have smaller [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md), only the effective spanned factors are identified.
