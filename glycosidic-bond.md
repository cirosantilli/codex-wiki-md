# Glycosidic bond

↑ **Parent:** [Covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md)

A [glycosidic bond](glycosidic-bond.md) links a sugar to another group through its anomeric centre. In a nucleotide, rotation about the base–sugar linkage contributes to anti and syn conformations; it is distinct from the phosphate links joining successive nucleotides.
