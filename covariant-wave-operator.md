# Covariant wave operator

↑ **Parent:** [Klein-Gordon field](klein-gordon-field.md)

On a scalar, the [covariant wave operator](covariant-wave-operator.md) is $\Box\phi=|g|^{-1/2}\partial_a(|g|^{1/2}g^{ab}\partial_b\phi)$. For the [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-+++)$ it has the flat-space form $-\partial_t^2+\nabla^2$. This divergence expression is convenient for [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) and for integrating the [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) by parts.
