# EXPTIME

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential time](exponential-time.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EXPTIME)

The [complexity class](complexity-class.md) of [formal languages](formal-language.md) decidable in deterministic [exponential time](exponential-time.md). It contains [NP](np-complexity.md): enumerate the finitely many choices of a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md)-length [nondeterministic computation](nondeterministic-turing-machine.md) and simulate each branch with a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) time cutoff. There are at most $b^{p(n)}$ branches for fixed branching bound $b$ and [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $p$.
