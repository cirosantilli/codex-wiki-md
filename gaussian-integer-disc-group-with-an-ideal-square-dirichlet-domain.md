# Gaussian-integer disc group with an ideal-square Dirichlet domain

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet domain](dirichlet-domain.md)

Consider determinant-one [matrices](matrix.md) $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}u&v\\\overline v&\overline u\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ over [Gaussian integers](gaussian-integer.md) with $u+v\equiv1\pmod{2\mathbb Z[i]}$, modulo their common sign. They act on the [Poincare disc model](poincare-disk-model.md) by fractional linear maps. Their origin-centered [Dirichlet domain](dirichlet-domain.md) is

$$
D=\{x+iy: x^2+y^2<1,\ 2(|x|+|y|)\le1+x^2+y^2\}.
$$

For a nonidentity [matrix](matrix.md) set $N=|v|^2\ge1$ and $uv=m+in$. The [group orbit](orbit-of-a-group-action.md) point is $(m+in)/(N+1)$ and its bisector inequality is $2(mx+ny)\le N(1+x^2+y^2)$. Since $m^2+n^2=N(N+1)$, integrality gives $|m|,|n|\le N$, so the four inequalities defining $D$ imply all of them. They are necessary because the [matrices](matrix.md) with $u=1\pm i$, $v=\pm i$ realize the four combinations $m,n=\pm1$. The four sides are arcs of the unit-radius circles centered at $\pm1\pm i$, and the ideal vertices are $1,i,-1,-i$.
