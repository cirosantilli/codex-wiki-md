# Arterial pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Circulatory system](circulatory-system.md)

A travelling [pressure](pressure-split.md) disturbance produced by cardiac ejection in the coupled [blood](blood.md) and arterial wall. Its propagation is distinct from the transport of individual blood cells.
