# Bernstein filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl algebra](weyl-algebra.md)

Give both position and differentiation generators of a [Weyl algebra](weyl-algebra.md) degree one. The ordered [monomial](monomial.md) [basis](basis.md) gives $\dim F_jA_n=\binom{j+2n}{2n}$ and $\operatorname{gr}_F A_n$ equal to a polynomial ring in $2n$ variables. Commuting a filtered element with any generator lowers its degree by at least one.
