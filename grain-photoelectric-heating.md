# Grain photoelectric heating

↑ **Parent:** [Photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md)

Ultraviolet [photons](photon.md) can eject electrons from dust grains in interstellar gas. The electron's excess [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) heats the gas through collisions. The efficiency depends on the grain population and electric charge, so absorbed ultraviolet power is not automatically equal to gas-heating power.
