# Model-theoretic ultralimit

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrapower](ultrapower.md)

An ultralimit is the direct limit of a chain of structures formed by taking successive [ultrapowers](ultrapower.md) along their diagonal [elementary embeddings](elementary-embedding.md). At limit stages use the [elementary chain theorem](elementary-chain-theorem.md). This is a different concept from a topological [ultrafilter limit](ultralimit.md). Alternating relative ultrapower embeddings constructs isomorphic ultralimits of any two elementarily equivalent structures, without requiring the stronger single-ultrapower isomorphism theorem.
