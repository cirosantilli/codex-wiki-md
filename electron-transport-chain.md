# Electron transport chain

↑ **Parent:** [Energy metabolism](energy-metabolism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron_transport_chain)

An [electron transport chain](electron-transport-chain.md) transfers [electrons](electron.md) from an [electron donor](electron-donor.md) through [electron carriers](electron-carrier.md) to a terminal [electron acceptor](electron-acceptor.md). In the [mitochondrial respiratory chain](mitochondrial-respiratory-chain.md), some transfers drive [protons](proton.md) across the [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md); their return through [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) can drive [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) formation.[Chapter 14, Electron-Transport Chains and Their Proton Pumps, Summary: carrier sequence, outward proton transport and ATP synthase](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

The useful separation is between an [electron](electron.md) route and a [proton](proton.md) route. The former ends in a [redox](redox.md) [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md); the latter changes an [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) across a membrane. [Energy coupling](energy-coupling.md) connects them without making [electrons](electron.md) and [protons](proton.md) interchangeable. An [oxygen](oxygen.md) readout consequently need not be an [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) readout.

This article focuses on the [mitochondrial respiratory chain](mitochondrial-respiratory-chain.md). [Bacteria](bacteria.md) can use other terminal [electron acceptors](electron-acceptor.md) in [anaerobic respiration](anaerobic-respiration.md). In [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md), light supplies [energy](energy-split.md) and linear [electron](electron.md) transport connects [water](water.md) [oxidation](oxidation.md) to [NADP+](nadp-plus.md) [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md), releasing [oxygen](oxygen.md). Thus neither [oxygen](oxygen.md) consumption nor the mitochondrial arrangement defines every [electron transport chain](electron-transport-chain.md).[§7.5, Anaerobic Cellular Respiration; §8.2, Generating an Energy Carrier: linear transport from water to NADP+](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)
