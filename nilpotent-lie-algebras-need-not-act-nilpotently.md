# Nilpotent Lie algebras need not act nilpotently

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

The [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md) $kI\subseteq\operatorname{End}(V)$, for $V\ne0$, is a [nilpotent Lie algebra](nilpotent-lie-algebra.md) but contains the nonnilpotent [identity map](identity-function.md). Thus nilpotence of the abstract [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) does not imply that a particular [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) consists of [nilpotent endomorphisms](nilpotent-linear-map.md). [Engel theorem](engel-s-theorem.md) requires nilpotence of every represented endomorphism to obtain strict upper triangularity.
