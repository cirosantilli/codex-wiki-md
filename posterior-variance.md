# Posterior variance

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md)

A [posterior variance](posterior-variance.md) is the [variance](variance-split.md) of a parameter under its [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md), namely $\mathbb E[(\theta-\mathbb E[\theta\mid y])^2\mid y]$ when finite. It describes remaining uncertainty after observing data and is distinct from the repeated-sampling variance of a fitted estimator.
