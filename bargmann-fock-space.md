# Bargmann-Fock space

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md)

The Bargmann-Fock space is a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) of entire [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) with Gaussian-weighted area inner product. If $f(z)=\sum a_nz^n$, angular integration and the [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) give $\|f\|^2=\sum n!|a_n|^2$. Conversely any such square-summable coefficient sequence defines an entire function, since $\sum|a_nz^n|\leq(\sum n!|a_n|^2)^{1/2}e^{|z|^2/2}$. This proves completeness and shows that $z^n/\sqrt{n!}$ is an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). The same bound shows boundedness of evaluation and gives reproducing kernel $e^{z\bar w}$.
