# Telecommunication

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunication)

Telecommunication conveys information between separated places by physical [signals](signal.md). [Radio communication](radio-communication.md) uses [radio waves](radio-wave.md); [telegraphy](telegraphy.md) communicates discrete written symbols. The engineering problem includes choosing a source, a channel and a readable [output](output-computing.md), then distinguishing that output from [noise](noise-signal-processing.md).
