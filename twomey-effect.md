# Twomey effect

↑ **Parent:** [Aerosol-cloud interaction](aerosol-cloud-interaction.md)

Increasing [cloud droplet](cloud-droplet.md) number at fixed liquid-water content and [cloud](cloud.md) thickness reduces mean droplet size and increases total extinction area, raising shortwave [optical depth](optical-depth.md) and [cloud albedo](cloud-albedo.md). This idealized response produces negative shortwave [radiative forcing](radiative-forcing.md). Changes in [cloud](cloud.md) amount, [water](water.md) content and dynamics are separate adjustments.[Fixed-water-content droplet-number and [albedo](albedo.md) response](the-influence-of-pollution-on-the-shortwave-albedo-of-clouds-by-s-twomey.md)
