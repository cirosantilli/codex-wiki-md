<h1 id="strong-collision-ion-neutral-alfven-modes">Strong-collision ion-neutral Alfvén modes</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Equal-density ion-neutral Alfvén dispersion relation](equal-density-ion-neutral-alfven-dispersion-relation.md)

For $|a|\ll\mu$, the equal-density ion-neutral cubic has a rapidly damped mode $\omega_d=-2i\mu+ia^2/(4\mu)+O(a^4/\mu^3)$ and two propagating modes $\omega_\pm=\pm a/\sqrt2-ia^2/(8\mu)+O(a^3/\mu^2)$. The reduced [phase speed](phase-speed.md) uses the total [density](density.md) $\rho_i+\rho_n=2\rho_i$. The exact [displacement](displacement.md) ratio is $\xi_n/\xi_i=\mu/(\mu-i\omega)$. It is nearly $-1$ for the rapidly damped counterflow and nearly $1\pm ia/(\sqrt2\mu)$ for the propagating modes. Thus the latter have small ion-neutral slippage of relative size $|a|/\mu$, responsible for [ambipolar damping](ambipolar-damping.md).
