# Gaussian surface

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_surface)

A Gaussian surface is a closed surface used with the integral form of [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) to relate electric flux to enclosed charge.
