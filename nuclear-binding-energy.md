# Nuclear binding energy

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_binding_energy)

The positive energy needed to separate a bound [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) into its constituent [protons](proton.md) and [neutrons](neutron-split.md). It is the mass defect times $c^2$. In a [Skyrmion model of a nucleus](skyrmion-model-of-a-nucleus.md), both separated nucleon masses and the multi-soliton state require a consistent quantization and calibration.
