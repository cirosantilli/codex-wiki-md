# Universal property

↑ **Parent:** [Category theory](category-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_property)

A universal property characterizes an object by a prescribed family of [morphisms](morphism.md) and a unique factorization of every competing family. It determines that object up to a unique compatible [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). Examples include the [pushout in a category](pushout-in-a-category.md) and the [universal property of the tensor product of modules](universal-property-of-the-tensor-product-of-modules.md).
