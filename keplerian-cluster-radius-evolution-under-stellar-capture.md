# Keplerian cluster radius evolution under stellar capture

↑ **Parent:** [Loss cone](loss-cone.md)

For a homologous [black hole](black-hole.md)-dominated cluster, write $E=-bGM_HNm/(2R)$, and let a captured star's specific orbital energy be $\epsilon_{\rm cap}=\xi E/(Nm)$. Removing negative orbital energy raises the remaining cluster energy, while growth $\dot M_H=m\Gamma$ deepens its potential. The energy budget gives the displayed radius response. Preferentially removing strongly bound stars can drive expansion; representative-energy removal instead gives the adiabatic contraction due to black-hole growth. The sign is not fixed by the [loss cone](loss-cone.md) angle alone.
