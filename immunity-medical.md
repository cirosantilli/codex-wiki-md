# Immunity (medical)

↑ **Parent:** [Medicine](medicine.md)

In infection modelling, [immunity](immunity-medical.md) is protection against infection or disease, with its strength and duration represented by the chosen [host](host-biology.md) states. A [SIR model](sir-model.md) has a permanently protected recovered state, whereas a [SIS model](sis-model.md) has no lasting post-recovery protection.
