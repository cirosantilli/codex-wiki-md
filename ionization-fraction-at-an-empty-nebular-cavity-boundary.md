# Ionization fraction at an empty nebular cavity boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md)

For pure hydrogen illuminated by an isotropic central source with [hydrogen-ionizing photon production rate](hydrogen-ionizing-photon-production-rate.md) $Q$, an empty cavity of physical radius $r_{\rm in}$ gives [photoionization rate](photoionization-rate.md) $q=Q\langle\sigma\rangle/(4\pi r_{\rm in}^2)$ per neutral atom. Local [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md) is $(1-\zeta)q=\alpha_Bn_H\zeta^2$, so $\Theta=\alpha_Bn_H/q$ and the physical [quadratic equation](quadratic-equation.md) root is the displayed rationalized expression. If an unextinguished total recombination-line [observed radiative flux](observed-radiative-flux.md) $F_\ell$ has photon yield $y_\ell$, then $Q=4\pi D^2F_\ell\lambda_\ell/(y_\ell hc)$ and $\Theta=y_\ell\alpha_Bn_Hhc(r_{\rm in}/D)^2/(\langle\sigma\rangle F_\ell\lambda_\ell)$. The ratio $r_{\rm in}/D$, rather than a product of lengths, makes $\Theta$ dimensionless.
