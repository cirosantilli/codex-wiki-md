# Divisibility by a prime

↑ **Parent:** [Divisible group](divisible-group.md)

An abelian group $A$ is $p$-divisible when multiplication by $p$ is surjective, equivalently $A/pA=0$. This does not mean it has no $p$-torsion: $\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ is divisible and has elements of every finite order. The [Kummer cohomology divisibility criterion](kummer-cohomology-divisibility-criterion.md) relates this property to [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md).
