# Catastrophic interference

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical learning](statistical-learning-split.md)

A severe loss of previously learned performance when updating a shared representation on new material. Overlapping parameters allow transfer but also let a new objective change computations needed for older examples. Interleaving and representation design can reduce the problem; the mere presence of a [neural network](neural-network.md) does not imply inevitable catastrophic forgetting.
