# Least-norm dual certificate

↑ **Parent:** [Strict dual certificate for basis pursuit](strict-dual-certificate-for-basis-pursuit.md)

When $A_S$ is [injective](injective-function.md), this [vector](vector.md) solves $A_S^*h_0=\operatorname{sgn}(x_S)$. Any other solution differs by a [vector](vector.md) in $\ker A_S^*$, orthogonal to the range of $A_S$ containing $h_0$. The [Pythagorean identity](pythagorean-theorem-in-an-inner-product-space.md) therefore proves that $h_0$ has the smallest [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md). It is a [strict dual certificate for basis pursuit](strict-dual-certificate-for-basis-pursuit.md) exactly when each inactive coordinate of $A^*h_0$ has magnitude less than one. Failure of that test does not rule out another certificate: a [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) component of $A_S^*$ can alter those inactive coordinates.
