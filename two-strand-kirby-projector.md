# Two-strand Kirby projector

↑ **Parent:** [Kirby killing property](kirby-killing-property.md)

An encircling [normalized Kirby color](normalized-kirby-color.md) loop around two equally oriented [anyon](anyon.md) strands is $\mathcal D$ times the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto their total [vacuum anyon](vacuum-anyon.md) charge. In the [isotopy normalization of anyon diagrams](isotopy-normalization-of-anyon-diagrams.md), erasing the vacuum edge writes this as $\delta_{b,\bar a}(\mathcal D/d_a)$ times the cup-cap diagram. The cup-cap squared is $d_a$ times the cup-cap, so $d_a^{-1}$ is essential.
