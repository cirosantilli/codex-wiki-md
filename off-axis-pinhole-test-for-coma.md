# Off-axis pinhole test for coma

↑ **Parent:** [Coma (optics)](coma-optics.md)

The institution-issued [AY 105 Lab Experiment 3: Optical aberrations by California Institute of Technology](ay-105-lab-experiment-3-optical-aberrations-by-california-institute-of-technology.md) (Spring 2011 offering, Part A, pp. 2–3, 7–8) specifies a concrete [optical bench](optical-bench.md): a lamp, opal diffuser and condensing [optical lens](lens-optics.md) illuminate a $25$ or $30\,\mu\mathrm m$ [optical pinhole](optical-pinhole.md); a photographic telephoto [optical lens](lens-optics.md) acts as a [collimator](collimator.md), producing an approximately $75\,\mathrm{mm}$ beam. A test [optical lens](lens-optics.md) sits on a rotation stage, followed by an adjustable [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md). The procedure establishes the on-axis image, then rotates the test [optical lens](lens-optics.md) a few degrees and examines the off-axis image through [optical focus](focus-optics.md). It compares this readout with through-focus [ray tracing](ray-tracing.md) at the corresponding field [angles](angle.md) and offers a [Ronchi ruling](ronchi-ruling.md) to calibrate the measuring eyepiece.

Part B (pp. 9–10) substitutes a spherical mirror of $1\,\mathrm m$ radius of [curvature](curvature.md), with the [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) beside the incoming beam so the image is necessarily off-axis. It calls for measuring the field [angle](angle.md), calibrating the eyepiece and recording the image's length and width at minimum size. These are specified apparatus dimensions and instructions, not supplied measurement results or dimensions of the normalized figures.

A controlled extension would record images at zero and equal positive and negative field [angles](angle.md), repeat zero afterwards, and scan through [optical focus](focus-optics.md) at every setting. Keep the illuminated [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) and illumination fixed, recenter the [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) without clipping the bundle, and use its calibrated scale to separate a mere image displacement from a change in blur shape. The pure [Seidel coma pupil mapping](seidel-coma-pupil-mapping.md) predicts reversal and linear growth; a stationary asymmetric central image instead calls for checking alignment. A focus-dependent change between perpendicular elongations suggests an [astigmatism](astigmatism-optical-systems.md) contribution. These comparisons test the model rather than assuming every off-axis flare is pure [coma](coma-optics.md).

The [optical pinhole](optical-pinhole.md) has finite angular size, approximately its [diameter](diameter.md) divided by the [collimator](collimator.md)'s [focal length](focal-length.md); the [collimator](collimator.md) and observing [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md) also contribute their own [optical aberrations](optical-aberration.md). A broadband lamp can mix [chromatic aberration](chromatic-aberration.md) with the geometric blur, and [diffraction](diffraction.md) limits the smallest visible structure. A narrow spectral band, repeated field settings and a reference image help separate these effects. Consequently a measured length and width are useful checks on off-axis [image formation](image-formation.md), but they cannot by themselves determine a unique [coma](coma-optics.md) coefficient or recover the full [point spread function](point-spread-function-split.md).
