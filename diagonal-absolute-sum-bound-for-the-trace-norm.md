# Diagonal absolute-sum bound for the trace norm

↑ **Parent:** [Trace-norm variational principle for Hermitian operators](trace-norm-variational-principle-for-hermitian-operators.md)

For any [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) and [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md), $\sum_i|\langle\psi_i|X|\psi_i\rangle|\leq\|X\|_1$. In the [trace-norm variational principle for Hermitian operators](trace-norm-variational-principle-for-hermitian-operators.md), choose the diagonal $T$ whose entries are the signs of these diagonal entries. This bounds the total distinguishability visible in one fixed measurement basis.
