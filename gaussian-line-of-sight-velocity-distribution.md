# Gaussian line-of-sight velocity distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal collisionless tracer](isothermal-collisionless-tracer.md)

For an [isothermal collisionless tracer](isothermal-collisionless-tracer.md) without streaming or an escape cutoff, integrate its three-dimensional [Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-velocity-distribution.md) over the two transverse [velocities](velocity.md). The remaining marginal is the displayed [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). Its width and mean do not depend on position. Integrating along the [line of sight](line-of-sight.md) therefore only multiplies the marginal by the projected [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md); the normalized observed velocity distribution remains the same Gaussian at every projected radius.
