# Bradley-Terry score equation

↑ **Parent:** [Bradley-Terry model](bradley-terry-model.md)

Let $W_i$ count observed wins and $n_{ij}$ count comparisons of players $i,j$. Differentiating the [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md) with respect to $\log\theta_i$ gives observed wins minus model-expected wins. Vanishing derivatives therefore give the displayed equations. One equation is redundant because abilities are identifiable only up to common scaling.
