<h1 id="cox-snell-residual">Cox–Snell residual</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Survival analysis](survival-analysis-split.md)

A [Cox–Snell residual](cox-snell-residual.md) is the fitted [cumulative hazard function](cumulative-hazard-function.md) evaluated at an observed event or censoring time. Under a correct model, complete transformed event times are unit exponential; censored transformed times retain their event indicators. A logarithmic [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md) survivor plot should be close to a line of slope minus one.
