# Projective model structure on nonnegative chain complexes

↑ **Parent:** [Model category](model-category.md)

The [model category](model-category.md) of nonnegative complexes of left modules has [quasi-isomorphisms](quasi-isomorphism.md) as [model weak equivalences](model-weak-equivalence.md), positive-degree surjections as [model fibrations](model-fibration.md), and degreewise injections with projective cokernel as [model cofibrations](model-cofibration.md). All objects are fibrant; cofibrant objects are degreewise projective.
