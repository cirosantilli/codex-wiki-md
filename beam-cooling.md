# Beam cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Particle accelerator](particle-accelerator-split.md)

[Beam cooling](beam-cooling.md) reduces random deviations around a beam's reference motion, such as its [momentum](momentum-split.md) spread and transverse spread. A highly energetic beam can be cool in this sense: its large common directed [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is distinct from the spread. Concentrating the occupied [phase space](phase-space.md) helps accumulated [particles](particle-physics.md) fit the next machine's acceptance. [Stochastic cooling](stochastic-cooling.md) achieves this through measured beam fluctuations and [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md).
