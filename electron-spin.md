# Electron spin

↑ **Parent:** [Spin one-half](spin-one-half.md)

An [electron](electron.md) has [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md). Its intrinsic [spin angular momentum](spin.md) has two possible components, $\pm\hbar/2$, along a specified [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf n$. These are the two [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the corresponding [spin measurement](spin-measurement.md); the [spin](spin.md) is distinct from the electron's [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md).
