# Crystallographic point group

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal symmetry](crystal-symmetry.md)

The finite orthogonal group obtained by quotienting a [crystal](crystal.md) [space group](space-group.md) by its [translation subgroup](translation-subgroup.md). In three dimensions there are 32 crystallographic point-group types, constrained by preservation of a lattice. It describes macroscopic rotational and [reflection symmetry](reflection-symmetry.md), not the fractional translations of the full [space group](space-group.md).
