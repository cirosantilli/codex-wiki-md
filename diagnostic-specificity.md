# Diagnostic specificity

↑ **Parent:** [Binary classification](binary-classification.md)

[Diagnostic specificity](diagnostic-specificity.md) is the probability that a test is negative in someone without the target condition. For an antibody test, relevant negative controls can include samples with antibodies to related pathogens to assess cross-reactivity. With true positive fraction $p$, observed positivity is $Se\,p+(1-Sp)(1-p)$.
