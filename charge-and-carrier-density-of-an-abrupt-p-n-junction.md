# Charge and carrier density of an abrupt p-n junction

↑ **Parent:** [Depletion approximation](depletion-approximation.md)

For a zero-bias abrupt [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md), choose the p side at $x<0$ and the n side at $x>0$. Ionized acceptors and donors remain fixed while mobile [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md) diffuse and establish a built-in [electric field](electric-field-split.md). In the [depletion approximation](depletion-approximation.md), mobile carriers are neglected on $-x_p<x<x_n$ and the net [charge density](charge-density.md) is

$$
\rho(x)=\begin{cases}-eN_A,&-x_p<x<0,\\+eN_D,&0<x<x_n,\\0,&\text{outside the depletion region}.
\end{cases}
$$

Charge neutrality gives $N_Ax_p=N_Dx_n$. The quasi-neutral bulk can have a large mobile carrier density while its net [charge density](charge-density.md) is nearly zero, because fixed dopant charge compensates it. With complete ionization and nondegenerate carriers, equilibrium also gives $np=n_i^2$; real carrier profiles vary smoothly rather than forming exact step functions at depletion edges.
