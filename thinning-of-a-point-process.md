# Thinning of a point process

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)

Thinning removes points from a [point process](point-process.md) according to a retention rule. Independent retention with probability $p(x)$ changes the intensity measure to $p(x)\mu(dx)$. For a [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md), the retained and removed point processes are independent Poisson processes.
