# Octahedral splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

In an ideal [octahedral molecular geometry](octahedral-molecular-geometry.md), the metal $d$ orbitals divide into lower $t_{2g}$ and higher $e_g$ sets. Their separation is $\Delta_o$. Whether [electrons](electron.md) pair in the lower set depends on that separation relative to pairing costs; observed many-electron transition energies need not equal $\Delta_o$ exactly.
