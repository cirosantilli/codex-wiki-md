# MPhil in evidence-based social intervention and policy evaluation course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

21 months full time. Official programme title: MPhil in Evidence-Based Social Intervention and Policy Evaluation. Open for 2027-28 entry; expected start October 2027. Includes taught components and a thesis. Included for its health and social intervention methods; no general social-policy expansion.

Official course page: [https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/mphil-social-intervention-and-policy-evaluation](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/mphil-social-intervention-and-policy-evaluation)
