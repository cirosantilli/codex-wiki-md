# Stern-Gerlach spin measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Stern-Gerlach experiment](stern-gerlach-experiment.md)

The ideal [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) of a spin-one-half component along a unit axis $\mathbf n$. The two outcomes have [Born rule](born-rule.md) [probabilities](probability.md) $\langle\psi|P_\pm|\psi\rangle$. For an input with [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) $\mathbf r$, they are $(1\pm\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf r)/2$. This model distinguishes a mathematical [spin](spin.md) measurement from engineering a literal free-electron beam splitter.
