<h1 id="beta-gamma-identity">Beta--gamma identity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Beta function](beta-function.md)

The sum-and-ratio change of variables in a product of gamma integrals gives

$$
B(p,q)=\frac{\Gamma(p)\Gamma(q)}{\Gamma(p+q)}.
$$
