# Diagonalization of a cyclic shift over a field

↑ **Parent:** [Diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md)

Let $S$ send successive standard [basis vectors](basis-vector.md) of $K^n$ to the next one, wrapping the last to the first. Its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) is $X^n-1$: this polynomial annihilates it, and the cyclic vectors $e_1,Se_1,\ldots,S^{n-1}e_1$ form a [basis](basis.md), excluding any annihilator of smaller degree. The [diagonalizability criterion using the minimal polynomial](diagonalizability-criterion-using-the-minimal-polynomial.md) gives the displayed equivalence. A splitting polynomial is square-free exactly when the [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md) does not divide $n$, as follows by taking its [derivative](derivative.md) $nX^{n-1}$ and checking common roots. Splitting over the specified field is a separate requirement. For $n=3$, the shift is diagonalizable over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md) and $\mathbb F_7$, but not over $\mathbb F_2$ or $\mathbb F_3$.
