# Clinical psychology

↑ **Parent:** [Psychology](psychology-split.md)

The scientific study, assessment and psychological treatment of distress, impairment and [mental disorders](mental-disorder.md). A [clinical formulation](clinical-formulation.md) proposes processes relevant to a particular presentation, while a diagnosis groups observed features; the two serve different purposes.
