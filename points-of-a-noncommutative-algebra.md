# Points of a noncommutative algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra homomorphism over a field](algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md)

An $R$-point of a unital [algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md) $A$ is a unital [algebra homomorphism over a field](algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md) $A\to R$. For the [quantum plane](quantum-plane.md) over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), scalar points lie on the two coordinate axes when $q\ne1$, while matrix-valued points are pairs $X,Y$ with $YX=qXY$.
