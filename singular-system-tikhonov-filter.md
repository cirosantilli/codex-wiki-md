# Singular-system Tikhonov filter

↑ **Parent:** [Tikhonov regularization](tikhonov-regularization.md)

For a [singular system of a compact operator](singular-system-of-a-compact-operator.md) with $Av_i=\sigma_i u_i$, [Tikhonov regularization](tikhonov-regularization.md) gives $x_\alpha=\sum_i g_\alpha(\sigma_i)(y,u_i)v_i$, where $g_\alpha(\sigma)=\sigma/(\sigma^2+\alpha)$ and the [inner product](inner-product.md) is linear in the first argument. The bounded gain yields a norm-convergent reconstruction; components in $\ker A^*$ are discarded and no component in $\ker A$ is introduced.
