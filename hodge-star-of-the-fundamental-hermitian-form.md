# Hodge star of the fundamental Hermitian form

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental form of a Hermitian manifold](fundamental-form-of-a-hermitian-manifold.md)

In an adapted real orthonormal coframe, the [fundamental form of a Hermitian manifold](fundamental-form-of-a-hermitian-manifold.md) is $\omega=\sum_j e^j\wedge f^j$ and the [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md) is $\omega^n/n!$. The complex-linear [Hodge star](hodge-star-operator.md) sends each summand to the product of all the other oriented two-dimensional factors. Summation gives the displayed formula, valid for every [Hermitian manifold](hermitian-manifold.md), without a [Kähler](kahler-manifold.md) assumption.
