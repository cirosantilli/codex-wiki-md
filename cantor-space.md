# Cantor space

↑ **Parent:** [Product topology](product-topology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor_space)

Cantor space is the binary sequence space with the [product topology](product-topology.md) of discrete two-point factors. Here the Bernoulli-space synonym refers to this [topology](topology-split.md), without selecting a particular [probability measure](probability-measure.md). The [metric](metric.md) $d(x,y)=\sum_{j\ge1}2^{-j}|x_j-y_j|$ induces the [topology](topology-split.md). A diagonal subsequence argument gives [compactness](compact-space.md). Fixing a finite prefix gives a [clopen](clopen-set.md) [cylinder set](cylinder-set.md). The map $x\mapsto\sum_j2x_j3^{-j}$ is a homeomorphism to the usual [Cantor set](cantor-set.md).
