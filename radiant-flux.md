# Radiant flux

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometry](radiometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiant_flux)

[Radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) is the [power](power.md) carried by [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md): radiation [energy](energy.md) transferred per unit [time](time-in-physics.md), measured in [watts](watt.md), $\mathrm W=\mathrm{J\,s^{-1}}$. Specify what is counted: all radiation emitted by a source, radiation within one beam, or radiation intercepted by a [photodetector](photodetector.md). Writing $Q(t)$ for the cumulative [energy](energy.md) crossing the specified boundary gives

$$
\Phi_e(t)=\frac{dQ}{dt},\qquad
\overline\Phi_e=\frac{Q(t_2)-Q(t_1)}{t_2-t_1},\qquad
Q(t_2)-Q(t_1)=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\Phi_e(t)\,dt.
$$

The first expression is an instantaneous [derivative](derivative.md); the second is an average over the stated [time](time-in-physics.md) interval. If $0.020\,\mathrm J$ arrives during $0.010\,\mathrm s$, the mean [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) is $2\,\mathrm W$. The [photodetector](photodetector.md)'s absorbed [power](power.md) equals this incident [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) only if it absorbs all of the incident radiation.

Keeping [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) fixed while widening a beam reduces its [irradiance](irradiance.md), the incident [power](power.md) per receiving [area](surface-area.md). [Radiance](radiance.md) additionally resolves the propagation direction and has units $\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}\,sr^{-1}}$; an [optical camera](optical-camera.md) can collect different [radiant fluxes](radiant-flux.md) from sources with the same [radiance](radiance.md) but different apparent [solid angles](solid-angle.md). [Luminous flux](luminous-flux.md), measured in lumens, weights the [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) by the human eye's standardized response instead of counting [energy](energy.md) equally at every [wavelength](wavelength.md). Equal [radiant fluxes](radiant-flux.md) need not give equal [luminous fluxes](luminous-flux.md).[Radiometry and Photometry: Review for Vision Optics](osa-handbook-of-optics-volume-iii-visual-optics-and-vision/radiometry-and-photometry-review-for-vision-optics.md)

[Radiant power](radiant-flux.md) also differs from the [optical power](optical-power.md) of an [optical lens](lens-optics.md), which is reciprocal [focal length](focal-length.md) in [dioptres](dioptre.md). Likewise, astronomical [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) is [power](power.md) per [area](surface-area.md). Stating the units prevents these shared words from hiding different physical quantities.
