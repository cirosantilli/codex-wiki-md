# Non-geminal substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophilic substitution](nucleophilic-substitution.md)

[Non-geminal substitution](non-geminal-substitution.md) puts incoming groups on different central atoms. On a [phosphazene](phosphazene.md) ring, it still permits cis/trans relative configurations and does not by itself specify one stereoisomer.
