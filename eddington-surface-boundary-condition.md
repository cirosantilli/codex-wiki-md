# Eddington surface boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md)

For no incident radiation and outward intensity approximated as direction independent, the surface angular moments are $F=\pi I_0$ and $P_{\rm rad}(0)=2\pi I_0/(3c)$. Integrating the radiation momentum equation with the [Eddington closure approximation](eddington-closure-approximation.md) yields the [grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md) law $T^4=3T_{\rm eff}^4(\tau+2/3)/4$. This is an approximate angular boundary condition, not isotropy across both hemispheres.
