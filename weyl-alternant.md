# Weyl alternant

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md)

For a [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) acting on a [weight lattice](weight-lattice.md), the displayed element of its [group ring](group-ring.md) is alternating: applying $s\in W$ multiplies it by $\det(s)$, by reindexing the sum. It vanishes when $\nu$ is fixed by a root reflection. For the [Weyl vector](half-sum-of-positive-roots.md) $\rho$, it is the [Weyl denominator](weyl-denominator.md). The [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md) is the ratio $A_{\lambda+\rho}/A_\rho$. These general root-system alternants specialize to monomial determinants in type A but are not restricted to that type.
