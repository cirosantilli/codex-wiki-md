# Homogeneous broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line broadening](spectral-line-broadening.md)

Broadening for which all participating emitters share the same spectral response, as in a uniform lifetime-limited line. A resonant field can saturate the common transition response. This differs from a distribution of transition frequencies across independently resonant subensembles.
