# Stellar relaxation time

↑ **Parent:** [Two-body relaxation](two-body-relaxation.md)

The [stellar relaxation time](stellar-relaxation-time.md) is the [time](time-in-physics.md) on which random gravitational encounters change a [star](star.md)'s [velocity](velocity.md) by an amount comparable to its characteristic random [velocity](velocity.md). A weak straight-line encounter at relative [speed](speed.md) $u$ and [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $b$ gives a kick $2Gm/(bu)$. Three-dimensional encounter counting gives $d\langle|\Delta\mathbf v|^2\rangle/dt=8\pi G^2m^2n\log(b_{\max}/b_{\min})/u$. Definitions of relaxation, [velocity](velocity.md) averaging and system structure alter order-one coefficients; the robust spherical scaling is $t_{\rm rel}\sim v^3/(G^2m^2n\log\Lambda)\sim(N/\log N)t_{\rm dyn}$. Collisionless simulation requirements depend on the duration and permitted fractional relaxation, not a universal minimum particle number.
