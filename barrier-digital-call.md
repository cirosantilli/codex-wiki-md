# Barrier digital call

↑ **Parent:** [Binary option](binary-option.md)

A cash-at-maturity upper-barrier [digital option](binary-option.md) pays a fixed amount if the stock touches an upper barrier by maturity. In the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md), write $a=\log(c/S_0)/\sigma$ and $b=-(r-\sigma^2/2)/\sigma$. Its unit-payoff price is $e^{-rT}$ times the [linear-boundary Brownian first-passage distribution](linear-boundary-brownian-first-passage-distribution.md) at $T$. This differs from paying at the hitting time, when the discount factor is random, and from an ordinary terminal-price [digital option](binary-option.md).
