# Positive-energy linear diameter growth

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

For an isolated Newtonian point-mass system, $\ddot I=T+E\geq E>0$. Twice integrating gives a quadratic lower bound on $I$. The [maximum-separation inertia bounds](maximum-separation-inertia-bounds.md) then give at-least-linear asymptotic growth of the diameter $R$ for a trajectory existing for arbitrarily large times. The bound does not assert instantaneous monotonicity or escape of every particle.
