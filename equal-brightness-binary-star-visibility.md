# Equal-brightness binary-star visibility

↑ **Parent:** [Michelson stellar interferometer](michelson-stellar-interferometer.md)

Let identical spatially incoherent stellar brightness profiles of equal integrated [flux](flux.md) be displaced by projected angles $\pm\beta/2$ along an interferometer baseline. The Fourier shift property makes their normalized signed coherence $g(Y)=g_\star(Y)\cos(\pi\beta Y/\lambda)$. The observed visibility is its absolute value. The slow single-star envelope depends on each star's brightness profile, while the oscillatory factor records projected separation. Unequal [flux](flux.md) prevents complete cancellation at the binary minima.
