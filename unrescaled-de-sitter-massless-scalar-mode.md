# Unrescaled de Sitter massless scalar mode

↑ **Parent:** [Minimally coupled massless scalar field](minimally-coupled-massless-scalar-field.md)

In the flat slicing of [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md), $a\propto-1/\eta$, $\eta<0$, a [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) of a [minimally coupled massless scalar field](minimally-coupled-massless-scalar-field.md) obeys $f_k''-2f_k'/\eta+c^2k^2f_k=0$. Substitution verifies the displayed positive-frequency branch. The [Canonically rescaled de Sitter scalar mode](canonically-rescaled-de-sitter-scalar-mode.md) is $u_k=af_k$, a different variable satisfying $u_k''+(c^2k^2-2/\eta^2)u_k=0$. For nonzero $k$, the opposite-frequency branch completes the time solution; at $k=0$ the solutions are $1$ and $\eta^3$. This separation avoids losing the homogeneous decaying mode by taking a restricted ansatz literally at zero wave number.
