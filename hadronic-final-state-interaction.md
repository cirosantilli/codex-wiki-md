# Hadronic final-state interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Strong interaction](strong-interaction.md)

An interaction among outgoing [hadrons](hadron.md) that changes the observed channel amplitude or its phase after the short-distance production vertex. Rescattering can mix channels with the same conserved quantum numbers. Consequently identical CKM suppression orders do not imply equal [branching fractions](branching-fraction.md), and a free-quark spectator diagram need not determine the ordering of exclusive hadronic [decay rates](decay-width.md).
