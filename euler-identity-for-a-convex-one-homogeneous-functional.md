# Euler identity for a convex one-homogeneous functional

↑ **Parent:** [Absolutely one-homogeneous functional](absolutely-one-homogeneous-functional.md)

For an [absolutely one-homogeneous functional](absolutely-one-homogeneous-functional.md) $J$ that is convex and $p\in\partial J(u)$,

$$
J(u)=\langle p,u\rangle.
$$

The [subgradient inequality](subgradient-inequality.md) at zero gives $\langle p,u\rangle\geq J(u)$, and at $2u$ gives the reverse inequality. This is an Euler identity that needs no differentiability.
