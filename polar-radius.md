# Polar radius

↑ **Parent:** [Polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md)

In planar [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) $(r,\theta)$, the polar radius $r\geq0$ is the [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) to the origin. It satisfies $x=r\cos\theta$ and $y=r\sin\theta$. For a differentiable [plane curve](plane-curve.md) with parameters $r(t),\theta(t)$, the orthogonality of radial and angular directions gives squared [speed](speed.md) $\dot r^2+r^2\dot\theta^2$.
