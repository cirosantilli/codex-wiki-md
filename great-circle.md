# Great circle

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_circle)

A great circle is the intersection of a [sphere](sphere.md) with a plane through its centre. Its shorter arcs are [geodesics](geodesic.md) of the round [sphere](sphere.md); celestial meridians and the equator are examples.
