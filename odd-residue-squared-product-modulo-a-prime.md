# Odd-residue squared product modulo a prime

↑ **Parent:** [Wilson's theorem](wilson-s-theorem.md)

For an odd prime $p$, the even residues are the negatives of the odd residues. If $O=1\cdot3\cdots(p-2)$, then $(p-1)!\equiv(-1)^{(p-1)/2}O^2$. Apply [Wilson theorem](wilson-s-theorem.md) to obtain the displayed congruence. The empty product for $p=3$ is one.
