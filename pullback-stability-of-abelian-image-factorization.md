# Pullback stability of abelian image factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Image factorization in an abelian category](image-factorization-in-an-abelian-category.md)

Pull back the two arrows of an [image factorization in an abelian category](image-factorization-in-an-abelian-category.md). [Monomorphisms](monomorphism.md) are preserved by pullback, and [pullback stability of epimorphisms in an abelian category](pullback-stability-of-epimorphisms-in-an-abelian-category.md) preserves its epic part. Pasting the squares gives the pulled-back composite. The resulting epi-mono factorization is therefore its image factorization by uniqueness.
