# Shapiro time delay

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

Extra propagation time of a light or gravitational-wave signal through a [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) compared with an appropriate flat-space reference. In a weak static field with [Newtonian potential of a point mass](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) $\Phi_N<0$, the leading delay along an approximately straight ray is $\Delta t=-2\int\Phi_N\,dl/c^3>0$. The local [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) remains $c$; the integrated geometry changes the relation between emission and reception times.
