# Bandwidth decorrelation of a point source

↑ **Parent:** [Field-dependent averaging losses in radio interferometry](field-dependent-averaging-losses-in-radio-interferometry.md)

A [point source](point-source.md) away from the tracked direction has residual [geometric delay](geometric-delay.md) $\tau$. Averaging its frequency-dependent phase over a top-hat band gives the displayed sinc attenuation times its central-frequency complex phase. The first [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md) null is complete cancellation, not a small-loss operating limit. Fine [frequency](frequency.md) channels preserve the information needed for later frequency-aware imaging.
