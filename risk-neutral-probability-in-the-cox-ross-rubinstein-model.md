<h1 id="risk-neutral-probability-in-the-cox-ross-rubinstein-model">Risk-neutral probability in the Cox--Ross--Rubinstein model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cox--Ross--Rubinstein model](cox-ross-rubinstein-model.md)

The unique equivalent martingale measure in the one-stock Cox--Ross--Rubinstein model assigns each up move probability

$$
q=\frac{R-D}{U-D}=\frac{1-d}{u-d}
$$

and each down move probability $1-q$. Under this measure the discounted stock is a [martingale](martingale-split.md).
