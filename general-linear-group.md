# General linear group

↑ **Parent:** [Group theory](group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_linear_group)

The general linear group $GL(V)$ is the [group](group-split.md) of invertible [linear maps](linear-map.md) from a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $V$ to itself, with composition as its operation.
