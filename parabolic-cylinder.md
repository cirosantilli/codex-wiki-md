# Parabolic cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Quadric surface](quadric-surface.md)

A [quadric surface](quadric-surface.md) obtained by extending a [parabola](parabola.md) along a fixed direction outside its plane. For example, $x=c-y^2/(4c)$ with arbitrary $z$ is a parabolic cylinder parallel to the $z$ axis. Each cross-section of fixed $z$ has vertex $(c,0)$ and focus $(0,0)$ in its $xy$ plane. It occurs as a coordinate surface in [parabolic cylindrical coordinates](parabolic-cylindrical-coordinates.md).
