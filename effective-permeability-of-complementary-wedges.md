# Effective permeability of complementary wedges

↑ **Parent:** [Effective permeability](effective-permeability.md)

In a slender layer, two parallel sublayers of fractions $x/L$ and $1-x/L$ have local effective permeability $K(x)=k_1x/L+k_2(1-x/L)$. Integrating their successive hydraulic resistance gives the logarithmic mean displayed above. A leading [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) uniform across the thickness does not make parcel paths horizontal; a small transverse velocity preserves [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) as the cross-sectional conductance changes.
