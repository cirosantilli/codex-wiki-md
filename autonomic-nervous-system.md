# Autonomic nervous system

↑ **Parent:** [Nervous system](nervous-system.md)

The part of the [nervous system](nervous-system.md) regulating organs, glands and related internal functions. It includes the [sympathetic nervous system](sympathetic-nervous-system.md) and [parasympathetic nervous system](parasympathetic-nervous-system.md). Its activity supports bodily regulation during [emotion](emotion.md), exercise and many nonemotional states; no single recording is a direct readout of a private feeling.
