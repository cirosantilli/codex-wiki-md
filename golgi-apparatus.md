# Golgi apparatus

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle](organelle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golgi_apparatus)

The [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md) modifies and sorts cargo arriving from the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) for delivery to other cellular destinations. The eponym refers to [Camillo Golgi](camillo-golgi.md).

Its usual stacked organization is polarized: cargo enters on the cis side, encounters medial processing regions, and leaves through the trans side and [trans-Golgi network](trans-golgi-network.md). [Golgi cisternae](golgi-cisterna.md) enclose a [Golgi lumen](golgi-lumen.md) separated from the surrounding [cytosol](cytosol.md). Processing changes cargo; sorting selects its destination.[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Organization of the Golgi, Figures 9.22–9.23; Protein Sorting and Export from the Golgi Apparatus](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/the-golgi-apparatus.md)

This division of labor explains why reaching the [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md) does not imply being secreted: cargo can take different onward routes, while resident [enzymes](enzyme-split.md) continue working there. The conventional route discussed here is part of [secretory protein routing](secretory-protein-routing.md); many [cytosol](cytosol.md) [proteins](protein-split.md) never enter it.
