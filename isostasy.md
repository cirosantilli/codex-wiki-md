# Isostasy

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

Approximate gravitational compensation of loads by subsurface [mass](mass.md) redistribution. Airy and Pratt models use local column balances; flexural support spreads the load laterally. Real [surface topography](surface-topography.md) can also be maintained by dynamic [stresses](stress.md).
