# Linear matter density equation in Newtonian gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological Euler equation](cosmological-euler-equation.md)

For [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) in [Newtonian gauge in cosmology](newtonian-gauge.md) with a common metric potential $\Phi$, let $\theta=\nabla\cdot\mathbf v$. The linear equations $\delta_m'=-\theta+3\Phi'$ and $\theta'=-\mathcal H\theta-\nabla^2\Phi$, where primes denote [conformal time](conformal-time.md), yield

$$
\delta_m''+\mathcal H\delta_m'=\nabla^2\Phi+3(\Phi''+\mathcal H\Phi').
$$

This follows by differentiating the first equation and eliminating $\theta$. On deeply subhorizon scales with a slowly varying matter potential, the metric time derivatives are small compared with its spatial [Laplacian](laplacian.md).
