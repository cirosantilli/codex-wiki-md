# Hermite vertical mode of an isothermal disk

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal vertical structure in stellar gravity](isothermal-vertical-structure-in-stellar-gravity.md)

In an inviscid locally isothermal [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md), a radial [normal mode](normal-mode.md) of [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ has pressure variable $\xi$ proportional to a [Probabilists' Hermite polynomial](probabilists-hermite-polynomial.md) in $x=z/H$. Gaussian-weighted finite energy excludes the exponentially growing vertical branch. Polynomial termination gives $n=0,1,\ldots$ and the local dispersion relation $(\widehat\omega^2-n\Omega_K^2)(\widehat\omega^2-\Omega_K^2)=k^2c_s^2\widehat\omega^2$, where $\widehat\omega=\omega-m\Omega_K$. At singular values introduced by eliminating velocity components, the original equations must also be checked. The $n=0$ propagating branch is an [inertial-acoustic wave](inertial-acoustic-wave.md); $n=1$ has vertically uniform vertical velocity and odd pressure perturbation, permitting bending disturbances.
