# CM elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Complex multiplication](complex-multiplication.md)

An [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) with [complex multiplication](complex-multiplication.md) is called a CM elliptic curve. Distinguish geometric [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) from [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) defined over the base [field](field.md). In characteristic zero, adjoining the [imaginary quadratic field](imaginary-quadratic-field.md) selected by the tangent action makes all geometric [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) defined over the base. Good split primes have [ordinary reduction](ordinary-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md); good inert primes have [supersingular reduction](supersingular-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md).
