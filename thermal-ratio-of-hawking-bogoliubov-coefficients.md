# Thermal ratio of Hawking Bogoliubov coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Hawking exponential ray map](hawking-exponential-ray-map.md)

For the [Hawking exponential ray map](hawking-exponential-ray-map.md), regulated integrals $\int_0^\infty x^{ia}e^{-(\epsilon\pm i\omega')x}\,dx=\Gamma(1+ia)(\epsilon\pm i\omega')^{-1-ia}$ have squared-modulus ratio $e^{-2\pi a}$ as $\epsilon\downarrow0$, where $a=\omega/\kappa$. Combining this with the [canonical identities for a bosonic Bogoliubov transformation](canonical-identities-for-a-bosonic-bogoliubov-transformation.md) gives the bosonic occupation $[e^{2\pi\omega/\kappa}-1]^{-1}$. Continuum modes require wave-packet normalization to interpret finite particle counts.
