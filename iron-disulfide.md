# Iron disulfide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

The compound with formula $\mathrm{FeS_2}$. Its conversion with metallic [lithium](lithium.md) consumes four Li per formula unit and can form [iron](iron.md) and two [lithium](lithium.md) sulfide units; that [electron](electron.md) inventory sets an ideal [battery](electric-battery.md) capacity.
