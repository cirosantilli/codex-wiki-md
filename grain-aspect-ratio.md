# Grain aspect ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Grain in a polycrystal](grain-in-a-polycrystal.md)

A measure of [crystal grain](grain-in-a-polycrystal.md) elongation. It changes directional boundary spacings and can influence anisotropic defect transport independently of average [grain size](grain-size.md).
