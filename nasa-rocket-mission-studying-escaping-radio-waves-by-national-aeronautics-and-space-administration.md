# NASA Rocket Mission Studying Escaping Radio Waves by National Aeronautics and Space Administration

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by National Aeronautics and Space Administration](work-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)

Official VIPER mission description: [https://www.nasa.gov/missions/sounding-rockets/nasa-rocket-mission-studying-escaping-radio-waves/](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/sounding-rockets/nasa-rocket-mission-studying-escaping-radio-waves/)

The mission account describes low-frequency ionospheric absorption/[reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) and condition-dependent leakage into the [magnetosphere](magnetosphere.md), with measurements below, within and above the relevant layers. It supports a propagation qualification, not a universal claim that all low-frequency electromagnetic signals are blocked.
