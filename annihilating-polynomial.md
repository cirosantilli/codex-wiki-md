# Annihilating polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Operator theory](linear-operator-theory-split.md)

An [annihilating polynomial](annihilating-polynomial.md) of a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) is a nonzero polynomial $p$ for which the displayed operator is zero. The [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) is its unique monic annihilating polynomial of least degree and divides every other annihilating polynomial. For a diagonalizable operator with distinct eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_s$, the product $\prod_j(t-\lambda_j)$ annihilates it. For an individual vector it suffices to annihilate the eigenvalues contributing to that vector; this gives exact finite-step termination bounds for a [Krylov subspace method](krylov-subspace-method.md).
