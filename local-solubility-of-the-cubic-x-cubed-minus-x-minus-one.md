# Local solubility of the cubic x cubed minus x minus one

↑ **Parent:** [Reduction of an elliptic curve](reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Modulo three the right side is always minus one, a nonsquare. At two, $x=-2$ gives the square [2-adic integer](2-adic-integer.md) $-7\equiv1\pmod8$. At twenty-three the same value is a nonzero square modulo the [prime](prime-number-split.md) and lifts by [Hensel's lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). At every other [prime](prime-number-split.md) at least five, the model has [good reduction of an elliptic curve](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) and the [Hasse bound](hasse-s-theorem-on-elliptic-curves.md) gives more than its single point at infinity, hence an affine smooth point. Lifting that point gives [p-adic integer](p-adic-integer.md) coordinates.
