# De Bruijn graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

For an alphabet of size $q$, the order-$n$ directed [graph](graph-split.md) has words of length $n-1$ as [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and words of length $n$ as [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). An [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) goes from its prefix of length $n-1$ to its suffix of that length. An [Eulerian cycle](eulerian-cycle.md) therefore lists every length-$n$ word once as a cyclic window, producing a [De Bruijn sequence](de-bruijn-sequence.md).
