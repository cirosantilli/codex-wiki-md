# Phase mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Relative permeability](relative-permeability.md)

Relative permeability divided by the phase's [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md). In the phase [Darcy law](darcy-law.md), $q_i=-K\lambda_i\nabla p_i$ without body forces. Ratios of mobilities determine [fractional flow](fractional-flow.md) when the phases have a common pressure gradient.
