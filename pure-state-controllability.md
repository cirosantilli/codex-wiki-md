# Pure-state controllability

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum controllability](quantum-controllability.md)

For freely chosen control duration in a finite-dimensional closed system, pure-state controllability means the reachable group acts transitively on normalized states or their rays. Up to unitary conjugation, the possible [Dynamical Lie algebras](dynamical-lie-algebra.md) are $\mathfrak u(N)$, $\mathfrak{su}(N)$, and for even $N=2n$ the standard [compact symplectic Lie algebra](compact-symplectic-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sp}(n)$, with or without $\mathbb R iI$. For $N\ge4$, the symplectic possibilities are pure-state controllable but generally lack [density operator controllability](density-operator-controllability.md).
