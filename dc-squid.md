# DC SQUID

↑ **Parent:** [SQUID](squid.md)

A [SQUID](squid.md) with two [Josephson junctions](josephson-junction.md) in a superconducting loop. For equal junctions, negligible self-inductance and no intrinsic phase offset, adding their sinusoidal currents gives the displayed flux-dependent critical current. An rf SQUID instead has one junction and uses loop inductance.
