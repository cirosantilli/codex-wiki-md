# Multiplicativity of the Legendre symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre symbol](legendre-symbol.md)

For an odd prime $p$,

$$
\left(\frac{ab}{p}\right)=
\left(\frac ap\right)\left(\frac bp\right).
$$

In particular, if $p\nmid a$, then $(a^{-1}/p)=(a/p)$.
