# Isoplanatic angle

↑ **Parent:** [Isoplanatic patch](isoplanatic-patch.md)

The isoplanatic angle is the characteristic angular separation over which a single-direction atmospheric correction remains useful. It depends on the altitude distribution of [atmospheric turbulence](atmospheric-turbulence.md) as well as on [wavelength](wavelength.md).
