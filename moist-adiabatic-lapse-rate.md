# Moist adiabatic lapse rate

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic temperature gradient](adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md)

A saturated ascending air parcel releases [latent heat](latent-heat.md) as [water vapor](water-vapor.md) condenses, offsetting part of its expansion cooling. Its [temperature](temperature-split.md) decreases less steeply than the [dry adiabatic lapse rate](dry-adiabatic-lapse-rate.md), with the gradient depending on [temperature](temperature-split.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md), moisture and whether condensate remains in the parcel. It is not a universal constant equal to the typical environmental lapse rate.
