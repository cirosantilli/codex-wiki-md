# Supercurrent

↑ **Parent:** [Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry](four-dimensional-n-1-supersymmetry.md)

The supercurrent is the spinor-valued [Noether current](noether-current.md) of rigid [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md). Its spatial integral is a [supercharge](supersymmetry-generator.md). Localizing the [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) parameter produces $\partial_\mu\bar\epsilon\,S^\mu$ in the variation of the [action](action.md); a [gravitino](gravitino.md) coupling cancels this term and starts the [Noether gauging procedure](noether-gauging-procedure.md).
