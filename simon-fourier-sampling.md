# Simon Fourier sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Simon's algorithm](simon-s-algorithm.md)

Apply the [Hadamard transform](hadamard-transform.md) to a [Simon coset state](simon-coset-state.md). The [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) of $|z\rangle$ is $2^{-(n+1)/2}(-1)^{x\cdot z}[1+(-1)^{s\cdot z}]$. Destructive [quantum interference](quantum-interference.md) eliminates exactly those $z$ with $z\cdot s=1$. A [computational-basis measurement](quantum-measurement-in-the-computational-basis.md) therefore samples the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) $s^\perp$ uniformly, independently of the representative $x$.
