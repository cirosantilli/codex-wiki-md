# Fluctuation-induced force

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical mechanics](statistical-mechanics.md)

Boundaries or inclusions change the available thermal or [quantum fluctuations](quantum-fluctuation.md) and thereby the [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md). Their [force](force-split.md) follows from $F=-\partial\mathcal F/\partial d$ under specified control conditions. Electromagnetic dispersion, depletion and critical fluctuations are distinct realizations, with signs and ranges set by their physical modes and boundary conditions.
