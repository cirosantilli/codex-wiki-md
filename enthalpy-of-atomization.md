# Enthalpy of atomization

↑ **Parent:** [Thermochemistry](thermochemistry.md)

An [enthalpy of atomization](enthalpy-of-atomization.md) is the [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) change for making one [mole](mole-unit.md) of separated gaseous [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) from a specified starting [chemical substance](chemical-substance.md). For [sodium](sodium.md), the relevant step is [sublimation](sublimation.md) to monatomic [gas](gas.md); for diatomic [chlorine](chlorine.md), it is half the compatible molar [bond-dissociation energy](bond-dissociation-energy.md) expressed as an [enthalpy](enthalpy.md).
