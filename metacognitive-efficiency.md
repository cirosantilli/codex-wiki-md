# Metacognitive efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Meta-d-prime](meta-d-prime.md)

A model-based comparison of [meta-d-prime](meta-d-prime.md) with first-order discrimination, often their ratio. Interpretation becomes unstable when the denominator is close to zero. Efficiency, [metacognitive calibration](metacognitive-calibration.md) and overall confidence are different quantities.
