# Constant Ricci curvature without constant sectional curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein manifold](einstein-manifold.md)

The [product Riemannian metric](product-riemannian-metric.md) of two round unit spheres has [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md) equal to its metric, since the factor Ricci tensors equal their metrics and the product connection splits. Its directional Ricci curvatures are all one. A two-plane tangent to a single sphere has [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) one, while a mixed plane has sectional curvature zero. This four-dimensional [Einstein manifold](einstein-manifold.md) therefore need not have constant sectional curvature.
