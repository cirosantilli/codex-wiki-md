# Nilradical as the intersection of prime ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Nilradical](nilradical.md)

For every [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) $R$,

$$
\operatorname{Nil}(R)=\bigcap_{P\in\operatorname{Spec}R}P.
$$

Every [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md) lies in every [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md). If $a$ is not nilpotent, an [ideal](ideal.md) maximal among those disjoint from $\{1,a,a^2,\ldots\}$ is a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) avoiding $a$, by the [Zorn lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) and the maximality argument.
