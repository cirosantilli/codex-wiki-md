# Reference-buoyancy convention in quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity

↑ **Parent:** [Three-dimensional quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](three-dimensional-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md)

The hydrostatic convention $b=f_0\psi_z$ may refer to full [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) or to its anomaly relative to $N^2z$. Subtracting the uniform reference profile changes $\psi$ by $-N^2z^2/(2f_0)$ and changes $q=f_0+\nabla_h^2\psi+(f_0^2/N^2)\psi_{zz}$ by the uniform constant $-f_0$. Horizontal velocity and [potential-vorticity gradients](potential-vorticity-gradient.md) are unchanged, so the advection and stability dynamics are identical. Stating the convention avoids mistaking this reference constant for a dynamical gradient.
