# Thermal damping of quantum oscillations

↑ **Parent:** [Shubnikov-de Haas effect](shubnikov-de-haas-effect.md)

A finite-temperature measurement averages the oscillatory response over the derivative of the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md). For the fundamental harmonic and a locally constant [cyclotron effective mass](cyclotron-effective-mass.md), this gives $R_T=X/\sinh X$ with $X=2\pi^2k_BT/(\hbar\omega_c)$. The oscillations become small when thermal smearing substantially exceeds their Landau-level spacing.
