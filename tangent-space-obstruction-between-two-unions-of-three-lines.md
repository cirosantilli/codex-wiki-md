# Tangent-space obstruction between two unions of three lines

↑ **Parent:** [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md)

The union of the three coordinate axes in $\mathbb A^3$ and a union of three distinct lines through the origin in $\mathbb A^2$ are not [isomorphic](isomorphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md). Their origins are intrinsically the unique points common to all three [irreducible components](irreducible-component.md), but their [Zariski tangent spaces](zariski-tangent-space.md) there have dimensions three and two respectively.
