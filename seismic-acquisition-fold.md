# Seismic acquisition fold

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic reflection survey](seismic-reflection-survey.md)

The number of source–receiver traces contributing to a midpoint bin or image location under a stated acquisition and processing scheme. Greater useful fold can improve the [signal-to-noise ratio](signal-to-noise-ratio.md), but does not compensate for missing spatial bandwidth or an incorrect [velocity model](velocity-model.md).
