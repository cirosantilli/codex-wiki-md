# Rate ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson exposure model](poisson-exposure-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rate_ratio)

A rate ratio compares event counts per unit exposure between two conditions. In a [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.md) with a [log link](logarithmic-link-function.md) and binary indicator coefficient $\beta$, it equals $e^\beta$. Exponentiating an approximate [Wald confidence interval](wald-confidence-interval.md) for $\beta$ gives an interval for the rate ratio. A rate ratio is distinct from a [relative risk](risk-ratio.md) or an [odds ratio](odds-ratio.md).
