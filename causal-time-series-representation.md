# Causal time-series representation

↑ **Parent:** [Causal time series](causal-time-series.md)

A [causal time-series representation](causal-time-series-representation.md) expresses a [time series](time-series-split.md) using present and past driving [white noise](white-noise.md). Its coefficients vanish at negative lags. Square summability ensures [mean-square convergence](convergence-in-l2.md) for a white-noise input; absolute summability is the stronger stable-filter convention. Causality refers to the particular driving sequence, not merely to stationary existence.
