# Normalization of a probability distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md)

A [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) assigns total [probability](probability.md) one to its [sample space](sample-space.md). For a nonnegative [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) this means that its [integral](integral-split.md) over the full domain equals one; for a [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) the sum of its masses equals one. A nonnegative integrable function with positive finite integral can be divided by that integral to normalize it. A negative function cannot be a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) even if a formal manipulation purports to normalize it.
