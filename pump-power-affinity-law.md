# Pump power affinity law

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md)

If pump [power](power.md) depends only on fluid [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, impeller diameter $D$ and [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega$, [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) fixes $P=C_P\rho\omega^3D^5$, where $C_P$ is dimensionless. Comparing pumps with the same $C_P$ gives the displayed scaling. Geometrical or flow-regime changes can change $C_P$; the law is not a universal comparison of arbitrary pumps.
