# Equal-ratio RC bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)

A bridge driven between top and bottom nodes has equal [resistors](resistor.md) on its left arms, upper-right [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md) $Z=R_x/(1+i\omega R_xC_x)$ and lower-right [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md) $W=R_v+1/(i\omega C)$. A zero detector [electric current](electric-current.md) requires $Z=W$. Equating [real parts](real-part.md) and [imaginary parts](imaginary-part.md) gives $R_v=R_x(1-C_x/C)$ and $\omega_0^{-2}=R_x^2C_x(C-C_x)$, requiring $0<C_x<C$. Its open-circuit right-minus-left junction [voltage](voltage.md) is $V_0(W-Z)/[2(W+Z)]$, whereas a connected ideal [ammeter](ammeter.md) has zero terminal [voltage](voltage.md) at every [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). This distinction separates a null-current measurement from an unloaded voltage measurement.
