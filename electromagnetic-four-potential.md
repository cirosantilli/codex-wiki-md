# Electromagnetic four-potential

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic electromagnetism](relativistic-electromagnetism.md)

The four-potential combines the [electric potential](electric-potential.md) and [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) as $A^\mu=(\phi/c,\mathbf A)$. The electromagnetic four-potential $A_\mu$ transforms under

$$
A_\mu\mapsto A_\mu+\partial_\mu\chi,
$$

while the antisymmetric field tensor $F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu$ is unchanged.
