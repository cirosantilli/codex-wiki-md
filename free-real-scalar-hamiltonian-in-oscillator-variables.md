# Free real scalar Hamiltonian in oscillator variables

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical quantization of a real scalar field](canonical-quantization-of-a-real-scalar-field.md)

Insert the [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) mode expansion into its quadratic [Hamiltonian density](hamiltonian-density.md) and use the [Fourier representation of the Dirac delta function](fourier-representation-of-the-dirac-delta-function.md). The two-annihilator and two-creator terms cancel by $E_{\mathbf p}^2=\mathbf p^2+m^2$. The remaining symmetric expression is $H=\frac12\int d^3p\,E_{\mathbf p}(a^\dagger a+aa^\dagger)/(2\pi)^3$. [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) removes its constant [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md), yielding the displayed operator. The [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) give $[:H:,a_{\mathbf p}]=-E_{\mathbf p}a_{\mathbf p}$ and $[:H:,a^\dagger_{\mathbf p}]=E_{\mathbf p}a^\dagger_{\mathbf p}$.
