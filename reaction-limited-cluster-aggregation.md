# Reaction-limited cluster aggregation

↑ **Parent:** [Particle aggregation](particle-aggregation.md)

In reaction-limited cluster aggregation, encounters occur frequently compared with successful attachment: an activation or interaction barrier gives a small sticking probability. Repeated unsuccessful approaches allow clusters to sample more relative configurations before bonding, commonly producing denser aggregates than [diffusion-limited cluster aggregation](diffusion-limited-cluster-aggregation.md). Typical three-dimensional isotropic irreversible cluster models give [mass fractal dimension](mass-fractal-dimension.md) around $2.1$. This structural contrast does not require previously formed bonds to break and restructure; it can arise from unsuccessful encounters alone. [Universality laws in coagulation: experimental comparison of diffusion-limited and reaction-limited aggregate dimensions](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/016974399180043P)
