# Cosmological perturbation theory

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmological_perturbation_theory)

[Cosmological perturbation theory](cosmological-perturbation-theory.md) studies departures of the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) and matter fields from a homogeneous and isotropic [FRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), order by order. [Linear cosmological perturbation theory](linear-cosmological-perturbation-theory-split.md) keeps only first-order terms. Higher orders describe nonlinear coupling between perturbations. [Nonlinear cosmological perturbation theory](nonlinear-cosmological-perturbation-theory.md) also uses expansions that allow finite amplitudes, such as the [long-wavelength approximation in cosmology](long-wavelength-approximation-in-cosmology.md).
