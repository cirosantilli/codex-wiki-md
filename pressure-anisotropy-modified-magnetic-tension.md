# Pressure-anisotropy-modified magnetic tension

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure anisotropy](pressure-anisotropy.md)

In [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md), the gyrotropic [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md) adds directional [stress](stress.md) to the [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md). A transverse displacement of a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) obeys $\rho\partial_t^2\boldsymbol\xi_\perp=\mathcal T\partial_\parallel^2\boldsymbol\xi_\perp$. Positive $\mathcal T$ gives a restoring [force](force-split.md); negative $\mathcal T$ gives the fluid [firehose instability](firehose-instability.md). This follows by combining $-\nabla\cdot\mathsf P$ with the divergence of the [Maxwell stress tensor](maxwell-stress-tensor.md). In [SI](international-system-of-units.md) replace $B^2/(4\pi)$ by $B^2/\mu_0$.
