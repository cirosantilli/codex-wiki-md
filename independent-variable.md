# Independent variable

↑ **Parent:** [Design of experiments](design-of-experiments-split.md)

A variable whose levels define a comparison or predictor of interest. In an experiment it can be assigned or manipulated; calling an observed predictor an independent variable does not establish a causal effect. The measured outcome is the [response variable](response-variable.md).
