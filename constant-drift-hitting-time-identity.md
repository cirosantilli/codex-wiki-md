# Constant-drift hitting-time identity

↑ **Parent:** [Expected hitting time](expected-hitting-time.md)

For a finite [Markov chain](markov-chain.md), let $T$ be the first [hitting time](first-passage-time.md) of $D$, assume $T<\infty$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), $f=0$ on $D$ and $Pf=f-\delta$ on its complement, with $\delta>0$. The [stopped martingale](stopped-martingale.md) $f(X_{n\wedge T})+\delta(n\wedge T)$ has mean $f(x)$. Boundedness of $f$ and [bounded convergence theorem](bounded-convergence-theorem.md) give $\mathbb E f(X_{n\wedge T})\to0$; [monotone convergence](monotone-convergence-theorem.md) then gives the stated finite [expected hitting time](expected-hitting-time.md). No drift condition is required after entry into $D$.
