<h1 id="schrodinger-equation-from-a-short-time-path-integral">Schrödinger equation from a short-time path integral</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized short-time Schrödinger kernel](normalized-short-time-schrodinger-kernel.md)

Expand a smooth [wavefunction](wave-function.md) and potential in the [normalized short-time Schrödinger kernel](normalized-short-time-schrodinger-kernel.md). Its odd moment vanishes and its second moment is $i\Delta t$, so one step changes the wavefunction by $i\Delta t\,\psi^{\prime\prime}/2-i\Delta tV\psi+O(\Delta t^2)$. Division by the time step gives the [Time-dependent Schrödinger equation](time-dependent-schrodinger-equation.md).
