# Braid group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braid_group)

The [group](group-split.md) of braids on $n$ strands, composed by stacking, with adjacent-crossing generators $\sigma_i$ and the [braid group relations](braid-group-relations.md). It retains over/under crossings and is not a permutation group.
