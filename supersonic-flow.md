# Supersonic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Mach number](mach-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersonic_flow)

A supersonic flow has [Mach number](mach-number.md) greater than one, so the flow speed exceeds the local [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md).

For uniform [gas](gas.md) moving at $U\mathbf e_x$ past a resting body, even an [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) directed against the [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md) has body-frame [velocity](velocity.md) $(U-c)\mathbf e_x$ downstream. More generally, an [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) travelling along a [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf n$ has body-frame [velocity](velocity.md) $U\mathbf e_x+c\mathbf n$; its $x$ component is at least $U-c>0$. All such weak signals move downstream and their possible directions fit inside a [Mach cone](mach-cone.md).
