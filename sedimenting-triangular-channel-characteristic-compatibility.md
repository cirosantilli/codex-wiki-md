# Sedimenting triangular-channel characteristic compatibility

↑ **Parent:** [Prismatic triangular-channel shallow water equations](prismatic-triangular-channel-shallow-water-equations.md)

For the [prismatic triangular-channel shallow water equations](prismatic-triangular-channel-shallow-water-equations.md), $c^2=G\phi h/2$. The [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) are $u,u\pm c$. Along $dx/dt=u$, $d\phi/dt=-2w\phi/h$. Along $dx/dt=u\pm c$, the [hyperbolic system](hyperbolic-system.md) has compatibility relation

$$
du\pm4\,dc\mp\frac{4c}{3\phi}\,d\phi=\mp\frac{4wc}{3h}\,dt.
$$

The [concentration](concentration.md) differential cannot generally be discarded to obtain [Riemann invariants](riemann-invariant.md) $u\pm4c$. Positive depth and [concentration](concentration.md) are required for this nondegenerate form.
