# Squared triangular number

↑ **Parent:** [Triangular number](triangular-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squared_triangular_number)

A [squared triangular number](squared-triangular-number.md) is the [square of a number](square-of-a-number.md) $T_n$, where $T_n$ is a [triangular number](triangular-number.md). Beginning at $n=0$, the values are $0,1,9,36,100,225,441,\ldots$. [Nicomachus's theorem](nicomachus-s-theorem.md) gives them a second description as cumulative sums of [cubes of numbers](cube-of-a-number.md).
