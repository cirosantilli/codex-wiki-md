# Skolemization

↑ **Parent:** [Skolem function](skolem-function.md)

Replacing an existentially quantified variable by a fresh [Skolem function](skolem-function.md) of the preceding universally quantified variables. A witness independent of all universal variables is represented by a fresh constant. The operation preserves satisfiability after expanding the [first-order signature](first-order-signature.md); it is not generally [logical equivalence](logical-equivalence.md) for arbitrary interpretations of the new symbols.
