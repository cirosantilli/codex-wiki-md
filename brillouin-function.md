# Brillouin function

↑ **Parent:** [Brillouin magnetization formula](brillouin-magnetization-formula.md)

The [Brillouin function](brillouin-function.md) gives the normalized [magnetization](magnetization.md) of independent angular-momentum-$J$ moments. It is odd, increasing, has slope $(J+1)/(3J)$ at zero and tends to $\pm1$ at large positive or negative argument. Explicitly,

$$
B_J(x)=\frac{2J+1}{2J}\coth\!\left(\frac{2J+1}{2J}x\right)-\frac1{2J}\coth\!\left(\frac{x}{2J}\right).
$$

It follows by differentiating the logarithm of $\sum_{m=-J}^J\exp(mx/J)$, the one-ion [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md). Its limiting value gives [magnetic saturation](magnetic-saturation.md), and its initial slope gives the [Curie law](curie-s-law.md).
