# Spectral projection

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral measure of a normal operator](spectral-measure-of-a-normal-operator.md)

For a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) $A$, its projection-valued [spectral measure of a normal operator](spectral-measure-of-a-normal-operator.md) assigns each Borel set $S\subseteq\mathbb R$ an [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) $E_A(S)$. For a normalized [quantum state](quantum-state.md) $\psi$, the probability of a measurement result in $S$ is $\langle\psi,E_A(S)\psi\rangle$. In finite dimensions, $E_A(S)$ is the sum of the orthogonal projections onto eigenspaces whose eigenvalues lie in $S$. This generalizes an individual eigenvector probability to degenerate eigenvalues and continuous spectra. The functional-calculus notation $\mathbf1_S(A)$ uses the indicator function of the set.
