# Dimension formula for an algebraic group homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic group](algebraic-group.md)

The kernel is a closed identity fiber. The image is closed because a [constructible subgroup is closed](constructible-subgroup-is-closed.md). Every nonempty fiber is a kernel translate, so the [fiber dimension theorem](fiber-dimension-theorem.md) gives $\dim G=\dim\ker\varphi+\dim\operatorname{im}\varphi$, even for inseparable morphisms.
