# Density of the irrational numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Irrational number](irrational-number.md)

Every nonempty open real interval contains an [irrational number](irrational-number.md). Choose a [rational number](rational-number.md) $q$ strictly inside the interval using [density of the rational numbers](density-of-the-rational-numbers.md); then $q+\sqrt2/N$ remains inside for sufficiently large positive [integers](integer.md) $N$ and is irrational. Thus the irrational numbers form a [dense subset](dense-set.md) of the real line. Together with rational density, this gives approaching [sequences](sequence.md) of both types at every real point, useful for testing [continuity](continuous-function.md) of functions defined differently on the two sets.
