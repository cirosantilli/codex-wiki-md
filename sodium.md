# Sodium

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical element](chemical-element.md)

Sodium is the [chemical element](chemical-element.md) of [atomic number](atomic-number.md) eleven. Carbon-fusion channels in [carbon burning](carbon-burning.md) include ${}^{12}\mathrm C+{}^{12}\mathrm C\longrightarrow{}^{23}\mathrm{Na}+p$, producing sodium-23 and a [proton](proton.md). Its abundance in expelled stellar material depends on later reactions and mixing, rather than on this one production channel alone.
