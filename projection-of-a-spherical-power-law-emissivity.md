# Projection of a spherical power-law emissivity

↑ **Parent:** [Surface brightness](surface-brightness.md)

An optically thin spherical source with volume emissivity $j(r)=j_0r^{-p}$ has [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md)

$$
I(R)=\frac{j_0}{4\pi}R^{1-p}\sqrt\pi\,\frac{\Gamma((p-1)/2)}{\Gamma(p/2)},\qquad p>1.
$$

Substitute $z=Ru$ in the line-of-sight [integral](integral.md) and evaluate $\int_{-\infty}^\infty(1+u^2)^{-p/2}du$ using the [beta function](beta-function.md). The condition $p>1$ is convergence at large distance. A core or finite outer boundary changes the projection near that boundary; the stated scaling applies between those scales.
