# Clebsch-Gordan decomposition of group representations

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md)

A Clebsch-Gordan decomposition expresses a [tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) as a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md), whenever [complete reducibility of compact-group representations](complete-reducibility-of-compact-group-representations.md) or [Maschke's theorem](maschke-s-theorem.md) applies. For a [finite group](finite-group.md) over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), the multiplicity of $V_\lambda$ is the [character inner product](character-inner-product.md) $m_\lambda=\langle\chi_\lambda,\chi_V\chi_W\rangle$. The [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) give the corresponding change of [basis](basis.md).
