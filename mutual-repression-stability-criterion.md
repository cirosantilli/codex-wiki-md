# Mutual repression stability criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Mutual repression](mutual-repression.md)

At an equilibrium $x_*=f(y_*)$, $y_*=g(x_*)$, the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has eigenvalues $-1\pm\sqrt{f'(y_*)g'(x_*)}$. Its product of repression gains is also $(\partial\log f/\partial\log y)(\partial\log g/\partial\log x)$. Product below one gives a stable node, above one a saddle, and equality the threshold for a zero eigenvalue. Robust multiple stable equilibria require an intervening unstable equilibrium; a gain exceeding one alone is not a sufficient global criterion for a particular pair of repression functions.
