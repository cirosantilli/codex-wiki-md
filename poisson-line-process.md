# Poisson line process

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md)

Identify an unoriented line not through the origin by $L(p,\theta)=\{z:z\cdot(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)=p\}$, with $p>0$. A [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md) on this parameter space is a random collection of lines. The [measure](measure.md) $c\,dp\,d\theta$ is invariant under rotations and translations; signed coordinates $t\in\mathbb R$, $0\leq\theta<\pi$ give the same [measure](measure.md) $c\,dt\,d\theta$. The number of lines meeting a disk of radius $r$ is [Poisson](poisson-distribution.md) with [mean](expected-value.md) $2\pi cr$.
