# Vacancy diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion](diffusion.md)

Migration through successive exchanges of atoms or [ions](ion.md) with vacant lattice sites. The diffusivity depends on both defect abundance and jump kinetics; mobile-defect diffusivity and atomic tracer diffusivity are different quantities.
