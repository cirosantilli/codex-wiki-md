# Well-formed weighted projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted projective space](weighted-projective-space.md)

A [weighted projective space](weighted-projective-space.md) with positive integer weights $q_0,\ldots,q_n$ is well-formed when the greatest common divisor of the weights with any one weight omitted is one. The quotient [toric lattice](lattice-in-toric-geometry.md) $N=\mathbb Z^{n+1}/\mathbb Z(q_0,\ldots,q_n)$ is free and the images of the coordinate vectors are primitive [toric rays](ray-of-a-fan.md). Its fan contains the cones on all proper subsets of these rays.
