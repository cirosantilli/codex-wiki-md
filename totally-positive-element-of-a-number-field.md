# Totally positive element of a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Archimedean place](archimedean-place.md)

A nonzero element of a [number field](number-field.md) is totally positive when its image is positive under every real [field embedding](field-embedding.md). In a totally real [number field](number-field.md), the [principal fractional ideals](principal-fractional-ideal.md) having a [totally positive](totally-positive-element-of-a-number-field.md) generator form the subgroup used to define the [narrow ideal class group](narrow-ideal-class-group.md). Signs of different generators are related by the [unit signature map](unit-signature-map.md).
