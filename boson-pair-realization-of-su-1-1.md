<h1 id="boson-pair-realization-of-su-1-1">Boson-pair realization of SU(1,1)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(1,1)](su-1-1.md)

Pair creation and annihilation in two independent oscillator modes obey $[K_-,K_+]=2K_3$ and $[K_3,K_\pm]=\pm K_\pm$. The [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md) components $K_1=(K_++K_-)/2$ and $K_2=(K_+-K_-)/(2i)$ satisfy $[K_1,K_2]=-iK_3$. Multiplying all three [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md) components by $i$ gives the real Lie bracket signs of [SU(1,1)](su-1-1.md). Both occupations change equally, so their difference is conserved. This realizes the algebra used in two-mode squeezed states.
