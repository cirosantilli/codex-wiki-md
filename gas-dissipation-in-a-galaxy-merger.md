# Gas dissipation in a galaxy merger

↑ **Parent:** [Gas-rich galaxy merger](gas-rich-galaxy-merger.md)

Gas can shock and radiate during a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) encounter, allowing it to change orbital energy in ways unavailable to an ideal collisionless stellar component. Gravitational torques can transport its [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and fuel central [star formation](star-formation.md) or [accretion](accretion.md). The outcome depends on orbit, gas content and internal structure.
