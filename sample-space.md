# Sample space

↑ **Parent:** [Probability space](probability-space.md)

The [sample space](sample-space.md) is the set $\Omega$ of possible outcomes underlying a [probability space](probability-space.md) $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)$. Its measurable subsets are [events](event.md), and the whole [sample space](sample-space.md) has probability one.
