# Orbital overlap

↑ **Parent:** [Linear combination of atomic orbitals](linear-combination-of-atomic-orbitals.md)

The [orbital overlap](orbital-overlap.md) of normalized real [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) is their [inner product](inner-product-split.md) $S=\int\phi_A\phi_B$. It is a [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) measure of their common amplitude, not an [electron](electron.md) count. For the [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) toy, increasing $a$ from $1$ to $2$ reduces $S$ from $e^{-1}\approx0.367879$ to $e^{-4}\approx0.018316$. As $S$ tends to zero, both [LCAO](linear-combination-of-atomic-orbitals.md) normalization denominators tend to $\sqrt2$.
