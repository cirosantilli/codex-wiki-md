# Seed dormancy

↑ **Parent:** [Germination](germination.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_dormancy)

A living [seed](seed.md)'s restriction on [germination](germination.md) under otherwise favorable conditions, distinct from insufficient [water](water.md), unsuitable [temperature](temperature-split.md) or lost [seed viability](seed-viability.md). One failed test cannot diagnose [seed dormancy](seed-dormancy.md).[Seed dormancy and the control of germination, §II.2, p. 502: operational definition and why absence of germination is insufficient](seed-dormancy-and-the-control-of-germination-by-william-e-finch-savage-and-gerhard-leubner-metzger.md)
