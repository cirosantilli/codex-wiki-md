# Spot volatility

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic volatility model](stochastic-volatility-model.md)

Spot volatility is the instantaneous diffusion coefficient multiplying relative price increments in a [stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md) for an asset. In a [stochastic volatility model](stochastic-volatility-model.md) it is itself a [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md).
