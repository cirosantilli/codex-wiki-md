# Search depth

↑ **Parent:** [Search tree](search-tree.md)

The number of action edges between a [search node](search-node.md) and the root of its [search tree](search-tree.md). It is zero at the root. When every step costs more than $\epsilon>0$, a positive-depth node satisfies $g(n)>d(n)\epsilon$, connecting depth finiteness to [path cost](path-cost.md) bounds.
