<h1 id="norm-of-a-positive-functional-on-c-k">Norm of a positive functional on C(K)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md)

For a real [positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md) on the [space of continuous functions on a compact space](space-of-continuous-functions-on-a-compact-space.md), $-\|f\|_\infty1\le f\le\|f\|_\infty1$ gives $|\phi(f)|\le\phi(1)\|f\|_\infty$. Evaluation at $1$ gives equality of the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) and $\phi(1)$. Thus positivity alone already implies continuity in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md).
