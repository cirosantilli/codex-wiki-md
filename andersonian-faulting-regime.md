# Andersonian faulting regime

↑ **Parent:** [Geological fault](geological-fault.md)

A local [principal stress](principal-stress.md) model associated with [Ernest Masson Anderson](ernest-masson-anderson.md) in which one [principal stress](principal-stress.md) is vertical, as suggested by negligible [shear stress](shear-stress.md) on a flat free surface. With compression positive and $\sigma_1\geq\sigma_2\geq\sigma_3$, vertical $\sigma_1$ gives [normal faulting](normal-faulting.md), vertical $\sigma_2$ gives [strike-slip faulting](strike-slip-faulting.md), and vertical $\sigma_3$ gives [reverse faulting](reverse-faulting.md). The other two [principal stresses](principal-stress.md) are horizontal. Topography, mechanical layering and local [fault](geological-fault.md) geometry can violate the idealization.
