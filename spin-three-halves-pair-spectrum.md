# Spin-three-halves pair spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-dot-product eigenvalue](spin-dot-product-eigenvalue.md)

For $s_1=s_2=3/2$, [angular momentum addition](angular-momentum-addition.md) gives one copy of $j=0,1,2,3$. The identity $2\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2=\mathbf J^2-\mathbf S_1^2-\mathbf S_2^2$ gives the four [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) in the title, with [state degeneracy](state-degeneracy.md) $1,3,5,7$. These exhaust the $4\times4$ spin space. If the particles are identical [fermions](fermion.md), an appropriate spatial exchange parity accommodates each sector; only a specified spatial restriction can remove sectors.
