# Eventual coupling of planar symmetric random walks

↑ **Parent:** [Coupling](coupling.md)

Two [simple symmetric random walks](simple-symmetric-random-walk.md) on the planar integer lattice starting at sites of the same parity can be coupled to agree after a finite time almost surely. Run them independently until they meet, then give them identical increments. Their difference walk has return probability $p_{2n}(0,0)$ at time $n$; the divergent return sum proves [Markov-chain recurrence](recurrent-markov-chain.md) and makes the meeting time finite.
