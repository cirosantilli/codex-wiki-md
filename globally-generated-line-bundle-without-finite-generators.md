# Globally generated line bundle without finite generators

↑ **Parent:** [Globally generated line bundle](globally-generated-line-bundle.md)

The displayed line bundle is globally generated, but no finite family of [global sections](global-section.md) generates it. A family of $N$ sections restricts to $N$ linear forms on $\mathbb P^m$; for $m\geq N$, these have a common zero. This shows why [finite global generation on a quasi-compact scheme](finite-global-generation-on-a-quasi-compact-scheme.md) requires its finiteness hypothesis.
