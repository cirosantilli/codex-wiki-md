# Calorimeter (particle physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detector](particle-detector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calorimeter_(particle_physics))

A [particle calorimeter](calorimeter-particle-physics.md) infers incident [energy](energy-split.md) from the response to deposited [energy](energy-split.md), often after a [particle shower](particle-shower.md). Segmentation supplies directional information relative to the collision position. A [particle calorimeter](calorimeter-particle-physics.md) does not automatically measure the full [momentum](momentum-split.md) of every species: [muons](muon.md), [neutrinos](neutrino.md), incomplete coverage and unequal material response require separate treatment.
