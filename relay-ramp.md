# Relay ramp

↑ **Parent:** [Normal faulting](normal-faulting.md)

A tilted region transferring displacement between overlapping [normal fault](normal-fault.md) segments. Opposing gradients of [fault throw](fault-throw.md) can produce an unbroken ramp. Continued extension can breach the ramp with a connecting [fault](geological-fault.md), generating a linked segmented trace.
