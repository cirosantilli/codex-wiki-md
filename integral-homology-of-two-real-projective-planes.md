# Integral homology of two real projective planes

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md)

Each [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md) has cells $e_0,e_1,e_2$ with $de_2=2e_1$ and $de_1=0$. Their product [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) has ranks $1,2,3,2,1$. Its degree-two [cycle](permutation-cycle.md) $e_1\times e_1$ has order two in [homology](homology-split.md). The degree-three cycle $e_2\times e_1+e_1\times e_2$ also has order two, because its double is $d(e_2\times e_2)$. Consequently $H_0=\mathbb Z$, $H_1=(\mathbb Z/2)^2$, $H_2=H_3=\mathbb Z/2$ and all other groups vanish. The degree-three torsion is also the [Tor functor](tor-functor.md) term in the [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md).
