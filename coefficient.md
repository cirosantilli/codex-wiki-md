# Coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Linear combination](linear-combination.md)

A [scalar](scalar.md) multiplying a specified term in a [linear combination](linear-combination.md), [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) or [power series](power-series.md). When the term family has [linear independence](linear-independence.md), the [coefficients](coefficient.md) are unique; uniqueness of finite [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md) [coefficients](coefficient.md) follows after multiplying by a sufficiently large power to obtain an ordinary [polynomial](polynomial-split.md).
