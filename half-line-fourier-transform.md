# Half-line Fourier transform

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md)

A [half-line Fourier transform](half-line-fourier-transform.md) separates the contributions supported on the positive and negative real axes. With the displayed sign convention, a bound $y(x)=O(e^{-\gamma_+x})$ as $x\to+\infty$ gives a [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) $Y_+$ in $\operatorname{Im}k>-\gamma_+$; a bound $y(x)=O(e^{-\gamma_-|x|})$ as $x\to-\infty$ gives a [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) $Y_-$ in $\operatorname{Im}k<\gamma_-$. The estimates hold uniformly on compact subsets of these half-planes and remain integrable after multiplication by any fixed power of $x$, which justifies complex differentiation under the [integral](integral-split.md). The overlap strip is the natural domain for a [Wiener-Hopf equation](wiener-hopf-equation.md). [Holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) continuation of a transform beyond its convergence region is a separate question from convergence of the defining [integral](integral-split.md).
