# Simple-cubic sphere packing

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere packing](sphere-packing.md)

Equal spheres of radius $r$ occupy the sites of a cubic lattice of spacing $2r$. A cell contains one sphere, so its [packing fraction](packing-fraction.md) is $(4\pi r^3/3)/(2r)^3=\pi/6$. This is an ideal arranged deposit rather than a claim about all settling suspensions.
