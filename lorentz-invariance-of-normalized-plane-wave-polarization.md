# Lorentz invariance of normalized plane-wave polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md)

For a [vacuum](vacuum.md) [plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md), a [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) multiplies both transverse [Jones vector](jones-vector.md) [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) by the same positive [Doppler factor](doppler-factor.md) when the basis is carried to the aberrated ray. Their ratio and [phase difference](phase-difference.md) therefore remain unchanged. All [Stokes parameters](stokes-parameters.md) gain the same squared factor, leaving normalized parameters, ellipse shape, handedness and [degree of polarization](degree-of-polarization.md) unchanged. This does not mean the propagation direction or the components in an arbitrarily fixed pair of spatial axes remain unchanged.
