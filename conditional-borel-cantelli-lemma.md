# Conditional Borel-Cantelli lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Borel-Cantelli lemmas](borel-cantelli-lemmas.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conditional_Borel-Cantelli_lemma)

For events $B_n\in\mathcal F_n$, put $p_n=\mathbb P(B_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1})$. Then $\sum_n\mathbf1_{B_n}<\infty$ almost surely on $\{\sum_np_n<\infty\}$, while $\sum_n\mathbf1_{B_n}=\infty$ almost surely on $\{\sum_np_n=\infty\}$. The proof applies the [martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md) to compensated sums $\sum_{j\leq n}(\mathbf1_{B_j}-p_j)$ and uses [Kronecker lemma](kronecker-lemma.md) in the divergent case.
