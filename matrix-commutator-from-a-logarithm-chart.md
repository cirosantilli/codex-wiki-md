# Matrix commutator from a logarithm chart

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra of a matrix Lie group](lie-algebra-of-a-matrix-lie-group.md)

If the matrix logarithm identifies a neighborhood of the identity in a [Matrix Lie group](matrix-lie-group.md) with a neighborhood of zero in its tangent subspace, the logarithm of the displayed group commutator belongs to that subspace. Divide it by $t^2$ and take the limit. Finite-dimensional linear subspaces are closed, proving closure under the matrix [commutator](commutator.md) without attempting to give a nonsmooth square-root reparametrization a velocity.
