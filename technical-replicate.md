# Technical replicate

↑ **Parent:** [Replication in experimental design](replication-in-experimental-design.md)

A [technical replicate](technical-replicate.md) repeats a measurement or analytical procedure on the same biological preparation. For example, read the [length](length.md) of the same [leaf](leaf.md) three times to assess [measurement error](measurement-error.md). Measuring three different [leaves](leaf.md) within one pot instead samples variation within the [plant](plant.md); it should be described explicitly rather than silently equated with repeated readings of one [leaf](leaf.md). Neither procedure supplies another pot-level [treatment](treatment.md) [replicate](replicate.md).
