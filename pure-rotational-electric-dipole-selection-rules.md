# Pure rotational electric-dipole selection rules

↑ **Parent:** [Linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md)

For the field-free, spin-free [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) in one fixed internal [quantum state](quantum-state.md), the [electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md) is $-\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E(t)$. A nonzero permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) along the molecular axis allows pure rotational [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) with $\Delta J=+1$ and [photon emission](photon-emission.md) with $\Delta J=-1$. The [matrix element](matrix-element.md) between initial and final [quantum states](quantum-state.md) must be nonzero: the existence of an [energy](energy-split.md) gap alone is insufficient. Relative to a chosen $z$ axis, [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) along $z$ selects $\Delta M=0$; transverse [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) permits $\Delta M=\pm1$.[printed pp. 2–3: dipole transition integrals, polarization and selection rules](lecture-24-supplement-energy-levels-of-a-rigid-rotor-by-dudley-herschbach.md)

[Hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md) and [carbon monoxide](carbon-monoxide.md) are examples with a permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md). By symmetry, [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) and [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) have no permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) in the ideal symmetric internal state. They still rotate and have rotational [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md), but the simple permanent-[electric dipole](electric-dipole.md) spectrum described here is absent. This restriction concerns this interaction and these assumptions, not every possible way of detecting [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md).
