# Long chain under eventual domination

↑ **Parent:** [Eventual domination on a regular cardinal](eventual-domination-on-a-regular-cardinal.md)

For every infinite [regular cardinal](regular-cardinal.md) $\kappa$, there is a strictly eventually increasing sequence of $\kappa^+$ [functions](function-split.md) $\kappa\to\kappa$. At stage $0<\zeta<\kappa^+$ enumerate predecessors by $e:\kappa\to\zeta$ and put $f_\zeta(\delta)=\sup_{\eta<\delta}(f_{e(\eta)}(\delta)+1)$. Regularity bounds each value below $\kappa$, while any predecessor's index is eventually included in the supremum.
