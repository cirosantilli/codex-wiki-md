# Composition in UML

↑ **Parent:** [Class diagram](class-diagram.md)

A strong whole-part relationship in a [UML class diagram](class-diagram.md), conventionally marked by a filled diamond at the whole. It expresses exclusive composite ownership and associated lifetime constraints. A stored reference or observer-registration association alone does not establish composition: the same observer may be registered with several subjects unless the design forbids it.
