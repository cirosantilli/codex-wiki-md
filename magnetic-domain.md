# Magnetic domain

↑ **Parent:** [Ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_domain)

A [magnetic domain](magnetic-domain.md) is a region sharing an ordered magnetic orientation. Different [magnetic domains](magnetic-domain.md) can reduce the total [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) without erasing the local [magnetic order](magnetic-order.md). A zero total [magnetization](magnetization.md) therefore does not by itself establish [antiferromagnetism](antiferromagnetism.md).
