# Pseudo-Riemannian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudo-Riemannian_manifold)

A [pseudo-Riemannian manifold](pseudo-riemannian-manifold.md) is a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) with a smooth [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) that is a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) on each [tangent space](tangent-space.md). Positivity is not required. A [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) is the positive-definite case, while a [Lorentzian manifold](lorentzian-manifold.md) has exactly one temporal direction in its [metric signature](metric-signature.md).
