# Power-law creep

↑ **Parent:** [Creep (deformation)](creep-deformation.md)

A regime in which the steady [material creep](creep-deformation.md) rate varies approximately as a [power](power.md) of [stress](stress.md), $\dot\varepsilon\propto\sigma^n$, with thermally activated coefficients. In ordinary dislocation-creep fields, glide, climb and recovery give an exponent greater than the linear-stress diffusion-creep limit.
