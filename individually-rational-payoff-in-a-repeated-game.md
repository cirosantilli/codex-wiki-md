# Individually rational payoff in a repeated game

↑ **Parent:** [Repeated game](repeated-game.md)

A [payoff](payoff.md) vector giving every player at least that player's [minmax payoff](minmax-payoff.md). It is strictly individually rational if every inequality is strict. A player can reject an equilibrium offering less than the guaranteed amount by choosing a securing strategy instead. These bounds concern repeated-game incentives; they are distinct from participation constraints in [mechanism design](mechanism-design.md).
