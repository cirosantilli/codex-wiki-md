# Principal plane

↑ **Parent:** [Thick lens](thick-lens.md)

The two [principal planes](principal-plane.md) are reference planes that represent a centred imaging system in the [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md). In air on both sides, measure object distance from the front [principal plane](principal-plane.md), image distance from the rear [principal plane](principal-plane.md), and [focal length](focal-length.md) from each [principal plane](principal-plane.md) to its corresponding [optical focus](focus-optics.md). They need not coincide with a surface or mechanical centre. Their signed separation matters in the [Bessel lens displacement method](bessel-lens-displacement-method.md).[Refracting systems (details), definition of principal planes, unit lateral magnification, and final imaging equation](refracting-systems-details-university-of-tennessee-physics-teaching-resource.md)
