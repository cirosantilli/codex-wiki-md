# Euler product for the gamma function

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma function](gamma-function.md)

For $z$ away from the poles, the [gamma function](gamma-function.md) satisfies

$$
\Gamma(z)=\frac1z\prod_{n=1}^\infty(1+1/n)^z(1+z/n)^{-1}.
$$

Its finite product is $m!(m+1)^z/[z(z+1)\cdots(z+m)]$. Splitting even and odd factors gives the [gamma duplication formula](gamma-duplication-formula.md).
