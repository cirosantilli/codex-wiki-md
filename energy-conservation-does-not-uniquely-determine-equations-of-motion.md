# Energy conservation does not uniquely determine equations of motion

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md)

For constant symmetric mass and stiffness matrices, $E=\tfrac12\dot q^{\mathsf T}M\dot q+\tfrac12q^{\mathsf T}Kq$ satisfies $\dot E=\dot q^{\mathsf T}(M\ddot q+Kq)$. Every equation $M\ddot q+Kq=G\dot q$ with antisymmetric $G$ conserves this same [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md), because $\dot q^{\mathsf T}G\dot q=0$. Thus a scalar conservation law alone cannot select the dynamics of several degrees of freedom. The specified conservative-force or [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) structure supplies the missing information.
