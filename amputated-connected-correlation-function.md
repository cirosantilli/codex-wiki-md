# Amputated connected correlation function

↑ **Parent:** [Connected correlation function](connected-correlation-function.md)

An amputated [connected correlation function](connected-correlation-function.md) is obtained by removing external propagators. Amputation using full propagators eliminates external [self-energy](self-energy.md) decorations; internal exchange diagrams remain. It is distinct from a [one-particle-irreducible correlation function](one-particle-irreducible-correlation-function.md). In a scalar theory with vanishing one-point function, at zero momentum the four-point quantity satisfies $\mathcal A_4=-\Gamma^{(4)}+3[\Gamma^{(3)}]^2/\Gamma^{(2)}$ with a convention in which connected diagram vertices are minus effective-action derivatives. For a constant scalar source, the Legendre relation $J=\Gamma'(\phi)$ gives $G''=1/\Gamma''$ and $G''''=-\Gamma''''/(\Gamma'')^4+3(\Gamma''')^2/(\Gamma'')^5$. Dividing by the four full external propagators proves the formula.
