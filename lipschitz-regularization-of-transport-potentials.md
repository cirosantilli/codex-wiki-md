# Lipschitz regularization of transport potentials

↑ **Parent:** [Kantorovich–Rubinstein theorem](kantorovich-rubinstein-theorem.md)

For [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) $f,g$ on a [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md) satisfying $f(x)+g(y)\leq d(x,y)$, set $h(x)=\min_y(d(x,y)-g(y))$. The [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) makes $h$ $1$-[Lipschitz](lipschitz-continuity.md), while the constraint gives $f\leq h$ and evaluation at $y=x$ gives $g\leq-h$. Thus the feasible pair $(h,-h)$ has at least as large an objective against any two [probability measures](probability-measure.md). This explains why [metric](metric.md) [optimal transport](optimal-transport.md) duality reduces to a single $1$-[Lipschitz function](lipschitz-continuity.md).
