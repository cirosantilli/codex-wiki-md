# Scalar action obstructs a cyclic module generator

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic module](cyclic-module.md)

If an [algebra](algebra-split.md) acts on a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) only by scalar multiples of the identity, every vector generates at most a one-dimensional [submodule](submodule.md). A higher-dimensional such [module](module-mathematics.md) is therefore not cyclic. For example upper triangular $n$-by-$n$ matrices act by the first diagonal entry on their first-row [ideal](ideal.md). For $n>1$ this [ideal](ideal.md) is a noncyclic [submodule](submodule.md) of the cyclic regular [module](module-mathematics.md). Thus a [submodule](submodule.md) of a [cyclic module](cyclic-module.md) need not be cyclic, even over a finite-dimensional [algebra](algebra-split.md).
