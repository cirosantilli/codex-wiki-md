# Forward contract

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent claim](contingent-claim.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_contract)

An agreement to exchange an underlying at a fixed delivery price on a specified maturity, without the futures convention of repeated settlement. Its fair delivery quote is $\mathbb E_Q[D_{tT}S_T\mid\mathcal F_t]/\mathbb E_Q[D_{tT}\mid\mathcal F_t]$. It may differ from a [futures contract](futures-contract.md) quote when rates are stochastic.
