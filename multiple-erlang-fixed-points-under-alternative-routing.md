# Multiple Erlang fixed points under alternative routing

↑ **Parent:** [Alternative routing](alternative-routing.md)

A symmetric triangle with direct calls and two-link overflow routes has [Erlang fixed point approximation](erlang-fixed-point-approximation.md) equation $B=E(\lambda[1+2B(1-B)],C)$. For $C=1000$ and $\lambda=950$, this has at least three solutions, certified by the [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) using signs at $B=0,0.01,0.1,0.4$.
