# Algebraic branch point

↑ **Parent:** [Branch point](branch-point.md)

At an algebraic [branch point](branch-point.md), a local solution becomes [meromorphic](meromorphic-function.md) after the finite substitution $z-a=t^k$. The displayed convergent expansion has only finitely many negative terms. A square-root [branch point](branch-point.md) corresponds to $k=2$; an [essential singularity](essential-singularity.md) or [logarithmic singularity](logarithmic-singularity.md) need not become meromorphic under any such finite substitution.
