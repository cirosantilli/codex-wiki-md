# Source condition for quadratic regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Tikhonov regularization](tikhonov-regularization.md)

This range condition puts the exact solution in the [operator range](range-of-a-bounded-linear-operator.md) of the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md). It controls the small-[singular value](singular-value.md) components of the solution and supplies convergence rates for [spectral regularization methods](spectral-regularization-method.md). It is the quadratic-penalty case of a [source condition in variational regularization](source-condition-in-variational-regularization.md).
