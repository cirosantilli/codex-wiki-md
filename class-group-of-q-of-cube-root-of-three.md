# Class group of Q of cube root of three

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski's bound](minkowski-s-bound.md)

For $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{3})$ with $\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[\sqrt[3]{3}]$, the field discriminant is $-243$ and the Minkowski bound is $8\sqrt3/\pi<5$. The prime ideals above $2$ and $3$ are principal, so every ideal class has a principal representative and

$$
\operatorname{Cl}(K)=1.
$$
