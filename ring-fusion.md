# Ring fusion

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md)

A [ring fusion](ring-fusion.md) shares two neighboring atoms and their bond between rings. Cis or trans fusion specifies the relative faces of junction substituents; axial/equatorial labels additionally require a [conformation](conformation.md).
