<h1 id="b/5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $\alpha=\bigcup X$. Every element of $\alpha$ belongs to some [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) in $X$ and is itself an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md). If $u\in v\in\alpha$, choose $\beta\in X$ with $v\in\beta$; transitivity of $\beta$ gives $u\in\beta\subseteq\alpha$. Thus $\alpha$ is a [transitive set](../../../../../../transitive-set.md) of [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md).

Any two [ordinals](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) are comparable by membership or equality, so membership strictly totally orders $\alpha$. Every nonempty subset $A\subseteq\alpha$ has an $\in$-minimal element by the [Axiom of foundation](../../../../../../axiom-of-regularity.md), and totality makes it the least element. Hence **$\bigcup X$ is an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md)**, including $\bigcup\varnothing=0$.
