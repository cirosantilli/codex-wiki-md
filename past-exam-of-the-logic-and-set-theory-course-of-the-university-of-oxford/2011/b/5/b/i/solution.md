A [well-order](../../../../../../well-order.md) is a strict total order in which every nonempty subset has a least element. A [Von Neumann ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) is a [transitive set](../../../../../../transitive-set.md) well-ordered by membership. Thus its predecessors are precisely its members, and its ordering is the restriction of $\in$.
