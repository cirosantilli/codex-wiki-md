<h1 id="b/5/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [transfinite induction](../../../../../../transfinite-induction.md). For any $\beta<\alpha$, the induction hypothesis and strict increase give $\beta\leq F(\beta)<F(\alpha)$. Thus every $\beta<\alpha$ belongs to $F(\alpha)$, so $\alpha\subseteq F(\alpha)$, i.e.

$$
\boxed{\alpha\leq F(\alpha).}
$$

This includes $\alpha=0$ and does not yet use continuity of the [normal function on an ordinal](../../../../../../normal-function-on-an-ordinal.md).
