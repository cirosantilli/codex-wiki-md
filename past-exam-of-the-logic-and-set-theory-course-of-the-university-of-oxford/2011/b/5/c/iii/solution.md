<h1 id="b/5/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Repeat the preceding construction starting at $\alpha_0=\gamma+1$, for any given [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) $\gamma$. A stabilized value or the strictly increasing sequence's supremum is a fixed point at least $\gamma+1$. Thus fixed points occur above every [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md).

If the fixed points formed a set $S$, their union would be an [ordinal](../../../../../../ordinal-split.md) bounding them all. Applying the construction above that bound contradicts it. Hence

$$
\boxed{\{\alpha\in\mathrm{ON}:F(\alpha)=\alpha\}\text{ is a proper class}.}
$$

This is the unboundedness part of the [fixed point of a normal ordinal function](../../../../../../fixed-point-of-a-normal-ordinal-function.md) theorem.
