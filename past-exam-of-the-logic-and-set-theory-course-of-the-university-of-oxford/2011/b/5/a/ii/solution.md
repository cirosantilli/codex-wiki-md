<h1 id="b/5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $a\in b\in\mathcal P(X)$. Since $b\subseteq X$, we have $a\in X$. Transitivity of $X$ gives $a\subseteq X$, so $a\in\mathcal P(X)$. This is exactly the membership criterion for a [transitive set](../../../../../../transitive-set.md), proving **the [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) of a [transitive set](../../../../../../transitive-set.md) is transitive**.
