↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A two-element model of $\phi$ would be a finite model with even size, contrary to the defining property established in part (b). Every model of $\phi$ therefore has size other than two, and the preceding interpretation of $\psi$ gives $\boxed{\{\phi\}\models\psi}$. This includes infinite models, since the antecedent of $\psi$ is false there.
