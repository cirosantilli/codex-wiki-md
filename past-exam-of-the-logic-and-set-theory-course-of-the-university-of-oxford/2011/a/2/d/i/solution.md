<h1 id="a/2/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The antecedent says that the nonempty domain has **at most two elements**: its two witnesses need not be distinct. The consequent says it has exactly one element. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A\models\psi\iff |A|\ne2.}
$$

One-element structures satisfy both sides; domains of size at least three, including infinite domains, make the antecedent false. The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $f$ has no bearing on this condition.
