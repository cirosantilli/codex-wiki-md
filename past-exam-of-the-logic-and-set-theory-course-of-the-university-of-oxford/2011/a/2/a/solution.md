A [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) for this language is $\mathcal A=(A,f^{\mathcal A})$, where $A$ is a **nonempty set** and $f^{\mathcal A}:A\to A$ is a total [function](../../../../../function-split.md). The logical equality symbol is interpreted as actual [equality](../../../../../equality-mathematics.md) on $A$; there are no other nonlogical symbols to interpret.
