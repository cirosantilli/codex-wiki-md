A [logical derivation](../../../../../logical-derivation.md) is a finite proof in the specified calculus, with conclusion $\Gamma\vdash\phi$. Assumptions from $\Gamma$ and instances of A1–A7 are available; the rules are the [modus ponens rule](../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md), [universal generalization rule](../../../../../universal-generalization-rule.md), and [weakening](../../../../../weakening.md).

In judgement form the [universal generalization rule](../../../../../universal-generalization-rule.md) takes $\Delta\vdash\theta$ to $\Delta\vdash\forall x_i\theta$ only if $x_i$ is not free in any undischarged assumption in $\Delta$. [Weakening](../../../../../weakening.md) permits $\Delta\vdash\theta$ to become $\Delta\cup\Sigma\vdash\theta$. All instances of A4 and A5 must meet their stated substitution and [free variable](../../../../../free-variable.md) conditions. **Derivability means existence of such a finite proof with assumptions contained in $\Gamma$.** Equivalently one tracks the finite assumption set used by each proof line.
