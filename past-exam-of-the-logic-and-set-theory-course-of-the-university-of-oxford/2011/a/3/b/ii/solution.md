<h1 id="a/3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take $\phi=\forall x_1\,(x_0=x_1)$. The term $x_2$ is [free for substitution](../../../../../../free-for-substitution.md) for $x_0$: its variable is not bound by the enclosing quantifier. The term $x_1$ is not [free for substitution](../../../../../../free-for-substitution.md) for $x_0$, because insertion under $\forall x_1$ captures that occurrence.

Thus the first substitution gives $\forall x_1(x_2=x_1)$; the second, illegitimate substitution would give $\forall x_1(x_1=x_1)$.
