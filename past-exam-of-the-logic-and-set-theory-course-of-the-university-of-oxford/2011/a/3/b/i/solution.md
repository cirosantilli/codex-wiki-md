The [first-order term](../../../../../../first-order-term.md) $t$ is [free for substitution](../../../../../../free-for-substitution.md) for $x_i$ in $\phi$ if, at every free occurrence of $x_i$ to be replaced, no enclosing quantifier binds a variable occurring in $t$. Equivalently, the substitution introduces no [variable capture](../../../../../../variable-capture.md). Only free occurrences of $x_i$ are replaced.
