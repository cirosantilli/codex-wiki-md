A [logical derivation](../../../../../../logical-derivation.md) of $\alpha$ from $\Gamma$ is a finite sequence $\delta_1,\ldots,\delta_n=\alpha$ in which each line belongs to $\Gamma$, is an instance of one of the three specified axiom schemes, or follows from two earlier lines $\delta_j$ and $\delta_j\to\delta_i$ by the [modus ponens rule](../../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md). **$\Gamma\vdash\alpha$ means that such a finite derivation exists.** In particular, a derivation uses only finitely many assumptions even if $\Gamma$ is infinite.
