A [conjunctive normal form](../../../../../../conjunctive-normal-form.md) is a finite conjunction of clauses, each clause being a finite disjunction of literals. A literal is a [propositional variable](../../../../../../propositional-variable.md) or the negation of one. Thus its shape is

$$
\boxed{\bigwedge_{i=1}^m\left(\bigvee_{j=1}^{n_i}\ell_{ij}\right),\qquad\ell_{ij}\in\{p_k,\neg p_k:k\in\omega\}.}
$$

A single clause or single literal is allowed; the necessary parentheses can be inserted according to the grammar.
