↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Logical equivalence](../../../../../../logical-equivalence.md) means that the two [propositional formulas](../../../../../../propositional-formula.md) receive the same [truth value](../../../../../../truth-value.md) under every [Boolean valuation](../../../../../../boolean-valuation.md) of the [propositional variables](../../../../../../propositional-variable.md). Equivalently, $\alpha\leftrightarrow\beta$ is a [tautology](../../../../../../tautology.md):

$$
\boxed{\forall v\quad v(\alpha)=v(\beta).}
$$
