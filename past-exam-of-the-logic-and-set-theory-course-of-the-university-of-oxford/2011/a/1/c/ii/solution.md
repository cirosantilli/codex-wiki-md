<h1 id="a/1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assign to each [propositional formula](../../../../../../propositional-formula.md) its finite symbol length. The set of lengths of formulas [logically equivalent](../../../../../../logical-equivalence.md) to $\alpha$ is a nonempty subset of the [natural numbers](../../../../../../natural-number.md), because it contains the length of $\alpha$. The [well-ordering principle](../../../../../../well-ordering-principle-for-the-natural-numbers.md) for the [natural numbers](../../../../../../natural-number.md) gives a least such length, and hence a shortest equivalent formula.

**A shortest equivalent formula need not be unique.** For example $(p_0\to p_0)$ and $(p_0\leftrightarrow p_0)$ are distinct [tautologies](../../../../../../tautology.md) of the same minimal length. A shorter formula would contain no binary connective and hence be a variable with some number of negations, which is not a [tautology](../../../../../../tautology.md). This counts each [propositional variable](../../../../../../propositional-variable.md) as one symbol, as in the stated language, and proves that a [shortest equivalent formula need not be unique](../../../../../../shortest-equivalent-formula-need-not-be-unique.md).
