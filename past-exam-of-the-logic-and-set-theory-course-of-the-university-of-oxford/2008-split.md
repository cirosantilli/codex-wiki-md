# 2008

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the logic and set theory course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part B paper is cross-listed for Mathematics, Mathematics and Computer Science, Mathematics and Philosophy, and Mathematics and Statistics. It contains [mathematical logic](../mathematical-logic-split.md) in section A and [set theory](../set-theory-split.md) in section B. [Source release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3) The archive contains the full editable five-page transcription. Bounded official-source searches found no advertised answers for this exact 2008 paper. All source leaves are solved below, including all five optional arguments in A1(e) and the unnumbered preliminary tasks in B5, B6 and B7.
