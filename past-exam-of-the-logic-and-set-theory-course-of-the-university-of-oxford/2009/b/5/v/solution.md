<h1 id="b/5/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The common amplitude $a>0$ is uniquely determined by any entry's [absolute value](../../../../../absolute-value.md). After choosing it, each entry independently has sign $+1$ or $-1$. Thus the correspondence

$$
(a,(\varepsilon_n)_{n<\omega})\longmapsto(a\varepsilon_n)_{n<\omega}
$$

is a [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) from $(0,\infty)\times\{-1,1\}^{\mathbb N}$ onto the [set](../../../../../set-split.md). The positive reals have cardinal $\mathfrak c$, and binary sign [sequences](../../../../../sequence.md) have cardinal $2^{\aleph_0}=\mathfrak c$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak c\cdot\mathfrak c=\mathfrak c.}
$$

Here “independently” refers to unconstrained deterministic choices of coordinates, not probabilistic independence; no probability distribution is being specified.
