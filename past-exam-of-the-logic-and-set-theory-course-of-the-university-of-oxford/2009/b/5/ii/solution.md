<h1 id="b/5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For an infinite cardinal $\kappa$, multiplication by a nonzero [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) cardinal does not change it. Since $|\mathbb R|=\mathfrak c\geq\aleph_0$,

$$
\boxed{|\mathbb R\times\mathbb N|=\mathfrak c\cdot\aleph_0=\mathfrak c.}
$$

There is also an immediate lower bound from $r\mapsto(r,0)$; the standard product identity supplies the matching [upper bound](../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). The discreteness of the second coordinate does not create a new cardinal beyond the continuum.
