↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The notation ${}^{\mathbb R}\mathbb R$ means all functions from the [real numbers](../../../../../real-number.md) to themselves, with no continuity restriction. Its cardinal is

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak c^{\mathfrak c}=(2^{\aleph_0})^{\mathfrak c}=2^{\aleph_0\cdot\mathfrak c}=2^{\mathfrak c}.}
$$

The exponent law follows by viewing a function to a function [set](../../../../../set-split.md) as one function on a [Cartesian product](../../../../../cartesian-product.md). [Cantor's theorem](../../../../../cantor-s-theorem.md) gives $2^{\mathfrak c}>\mathfrak c$, so this is strictly larger than the other continuum-sized examples here. [Characteristic functions](../../../../../characteristic-function-split.md) of [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) of $\mathbb R$ already supply the lower bound $2^{\mathfrak c}$.
