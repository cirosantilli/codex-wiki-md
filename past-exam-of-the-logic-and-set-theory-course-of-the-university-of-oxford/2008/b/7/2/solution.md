<h1 id="b/7/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Map $S\subseteq\omega$ to its [indicator function](../../../../../indicator-function.md) $\mathbf1_S:\omega\to\{0,1\}$. Its inverse maps a [binary sequence](../../../../../bitstream.md) $u$ to $\{n\in\omega:u(n)=1\}$. These maps are mutually inverse, so

$$
\boxed{|{}^{\omega}2|=|\mathcal P(\omega)|=2^{\aleph_0}.}
$$

Here the left superscript denotes the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) from $\omega$ into $2$. It is a space of [binary sequences](../../../../../bitstream.md), not an [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md) square.
