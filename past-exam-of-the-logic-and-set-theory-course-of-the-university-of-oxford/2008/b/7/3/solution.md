<h1 id="b/7/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

There is an [injection](../../../../../injective-function.md) from the [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) of $\omega$ into the [real numbers](../../../../../real-number.md) given by

$$
S\longmapsto x_S=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{2\mathbf1_S(n)}{3^{n+1}}.
$$

If $S$ and $T$ first differ at $k$, the contribution at $k$ has magnitude $2/3^{k+1}$, while the entire later tail has magnitude at most

$$
\sum_{n>k}\frac2{3^{n+1}}=\frac1{3^{k+1}}.
$$

Their real values are therefore different. This proves [injectivity](../../../../../injective-function.md) directly and avoids any ambiguity of numeral expansions.

Conversely, map a [real number](../../../../../real-number.md) $x$ to the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $\{q\in\mathbb Q:q<x\}$. Distinct [real numbers](../../../../../real-number.md) give distinct [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) because the [rational numbers](../../../../../rational-number.md) are dense in the [real line](../../../../../real-line.md). Fixing a [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) between $\omega$ and the [countable set](../../../../../countable-set.md) $\mathbb Q$ turns this into an [injection](../../../../../injective-function.md) $\mathbb R\to\mathcal P(\omega)$. [Cantor-Schröder-Bernstein theorem](../../../../../cantor-schroder-bernstein-theorem.md) now gives

$$
\boxed{|\mathbb R|=2^{\aleph_0}.}
$$
