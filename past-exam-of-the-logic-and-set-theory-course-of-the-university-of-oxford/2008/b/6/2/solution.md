↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**This encoding fails.** Choose two distinct [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) $0$ and $1$, for example the first two finite von Neumann [ordinals](../../../../../ordinal-split.md). The distinct [ordered triples](../../../../../ordered-triple.md) $(0,0,1)$ and $(0,1,1)$ both encode as

$$
\{\{0\},\{0,1\}\}.
$$

Indeed the repeated [singleton](../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md) in the first encoding and the repeated two-element [set](../../../../../set-split.md) in the second disappear when forming an ordinary [set](../../../../../set-split.md). Thus the middle coordinate cannot always be recovered. The issue is loss of multiplicity, not a failure to construct a [set](../../../../../set-split.md).
