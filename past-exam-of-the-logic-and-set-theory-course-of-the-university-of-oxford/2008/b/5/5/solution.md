**False.** Take $\alpha=\beta=1$ and $\gamma=\omega$. Then

$$
(1+1)\omega=2\omega=\omega,
\qquad 1\omega+1\omega=\omega+\omega>\omega.
$$

Thus [ordinal multiplication](../../../../../ordinal-multiplication.md) on the right does not in general distribute over [ordinal addition](../../../../../ordinal-addition.md). The side on which [ordinal multiplication](../../../../../ordinal-multiplication.md) occurs matters; the left-distributive identity in the previous part does not imply this one.
