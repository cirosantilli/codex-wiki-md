**False.** Use $\alpha=2$ and $\beta=\omega$. The recursive definition of [ordinal multiplication](../../../../../ordinal-multiplication.md) gives

$$
2\omega=\sup_{n<\omega}2n=\omega,
\qquad \omega\cdot2=\omega+\omega>\omega.
$$

The first product concatenates countably many finite two-point blocks. The second concatenates two whole copies of the [order type](../../../../../order-type.md) $\omega$, producing a strictly larger [ordinal](../../../../../ordinal-split.md). Hence [ordinal multiplication](../../../../../ordinal-multiplication.md) is not commutative.
