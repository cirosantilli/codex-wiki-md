<h1 id="a/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a countable first-order language, the [Henkin construction](../../../../../henkin-construction.md) introduces only countably many [constant symbols](../../../../../constant-symbol.md): each round needs at most one for each [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md), there are countably many finite strings in a countable alphabet, and countably many rounds suffice. There are therefore at most countably many [closed terms](../../../../../closed-term.md). The [term model](../../../../../term-model.md), and also its [quotient set](../../../../../quotient-set.md) by [equality](../../../../../equality-mathematics.md), has at most countably many elements.

It follows that **every satisfiable theory in a countable first-order language has an at most countable [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md)**. If the theory has no finite [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md), the constructed [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) is countably infinite. More generally the same counting argument gives a [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) at most $\max(|L|,\aleph_0)$ for any satisfiable theory in language $L$.

For the elementary-substructure version of the downward [Löwenheim-Skolem theorem](../../../../../lowenheim-skolem-theorem.md), begin with a [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) $M$ and a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $S$ of designated elements. Close $S$, the named [constant symbols](../../../../../constant-symbol.md) and one initial element under the language's [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) and under chosen witnesses in $M$ for [existential formulas](../../../../../existential-formula.md) with parameters already chosen. Iterate countably often. The resulting [set](../../../../../set-split.md) has [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) at most $\max(|L|,|S|,\aleph_0)$, and its induced substructure $N$ is elementary: induction on [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) proves the truth equivalence, with the existential case supplied by the chosen witnesses. This is the same witness mechanism as the Henkin [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md), now keeping witnesses inside a given [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md).
