Start with the [propositional variables](../../../../../propositional-variable.md) as atomic symbols and close under the two formation operations

$$
p_i\in\operatorname{Form}(L_0),\qquad
\psi\in\operatorname{Form}(L_0)\Longrightarrow\neg\psi\in\operatorname{Form}(L_0),\qquad
\psi,\chi\in\operatorname{Form}(L_0)\Longrightarrow(\psi\to\chi)\in\operatorname{Form}(L_0).
$$

The [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of [propositional formulas](../../../../../propositional-formula.md) is the **smallest** [set](../../../../../set-split.md) satisfying these conditions: every [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) has a finite construction from [logical variables](../../../../../logical-variable.md) by [negation](../../../../../negation.md) and [logical implication](../../../../../logical-implication.md), and no other strings qualify. In particular, parentheses belong to the binary [logical implication](../../../../../logical-implication.md) [formation rule](../../../../../formation-rule.md); they cannot be added or removed arbitrarily. Each symbol $p_i$ is one token, regardless of how its index is printed.
