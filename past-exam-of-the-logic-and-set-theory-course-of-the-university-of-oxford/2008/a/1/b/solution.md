<h1 id="a/1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [structural induction](../../../../../structural-induction.md) on the formation of a [propositional formula](../../../../../propositional-formula.md). A [propositional variable](../../../../../propositional-variable.md) has no parentheses. Applying [negation](../../../../../negation.md) to a [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) adds no parentheses, so preserves [equality](../../../../../equality-mathematics.md) of the two counts. If $\psi$ and $\chi$ each have equally many opening and closing parentheses, the formation of $(\psi\to\chi)$ adds one of each to their combined counts. Thus every [propositional formula](../../../../../propositional-formula.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{\#\text{ opening parentheses}=\#\text{ closing parentheses}.}
$$

This is a necessary condition for being a [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md); it is not sufficient, since the [logical connectives](../../../../../logical-connective.md) and the placement of symbols must also follow the [formation rules](../../../../../formation-rule.md).
