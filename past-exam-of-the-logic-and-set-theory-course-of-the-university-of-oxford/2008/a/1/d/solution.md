↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [unique readability theorem](../../../../../unique-readability-theorem.md) says that every [propositional formula](../../../../../propositional-formula.md) has **exactly one** of the following forms: a unique [propositional variable](../../../../../propositional-variable.md); $\neg\psi$ for a unique [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) $\psi$; or $(\psi\to\chi)$ for a unique [ordered pair](../../../../../ordered-pair.md) of [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) $(\psi,\chi)$. These three alternatives are disjoint. Consequently each [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) has a unique [formula syntax tree](../../../../../syntax-tree-of-a-formula.md) and a uniquely determined principal [logical connective](../../../../../logical-connective.md) whenever it is not a [logical variable](../../../../../logical-variable.md).
