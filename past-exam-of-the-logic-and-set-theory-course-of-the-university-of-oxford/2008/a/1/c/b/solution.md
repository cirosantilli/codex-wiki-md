**Not a [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) of this language.** [Logical disjunction](../../../../../../logical-disjunction.md) is not one of its permitted [logical connectives](../../../../../../logical-connective.md). Even though the displayed expression has an ordinary interpretation in a larger propositional language, membership in the specified [formal language](../../../../../../formal-language.md) is a syntactic question. A logically equivalent [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) using only [negation](../../../../../../negation.md) and [logical implication](../../../../../../logical-implication.md) would have to be written with those symbols instead.
