**A [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md).** Both $p_1$ and $p_2$ are [propositional variables](../../../../../../propositional-variable.md), so $(p_1\to p_2)$ and $(p_2\to p_1)$ are [logical formulas](../../../../../../logical-formula.md). Applying the binary [logical implication](../../../../../../logical-implication.md) [formation rule](../../../../../../formation-rule.md) to this [ordered pair](../../../../../../ordered-pair.md) of [logical formulas](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) gives the displayed whole expression. This supplies a construction of its [formula syntax tree](../../../../../../syntax-tree-of-a-formula.md) rather than merely checking that its parentheses balance.
