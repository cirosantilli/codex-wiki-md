<h1 id="a/1/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [propositional variable](../../../../../../propositional-variable.md) $p_i$ is a single symbol of the alphabet. Its only nonempty substring is that symbol itself. Every [propositional formula](../../../../../../propositional-formula.md) is nonempty, so the only substring that is a [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) is $p_i$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Sub}(p_i)=\{p_i\}.}
$$

The subscript is part of the name of an atomic token; it does not introduce additional symbol positions inside the [logical variable](../../../../../../logical-variable.md).
