<h1 id="a/1/e/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The whole [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) $\neg\psi$ is a [subformula](../../../../../../subformula.md) of itself, and any [subformula](../../../../../../subformula.md) of $\psi$ remains a contiguous substring after placing a [negation](../../../../../../negation.md) symbol in front of $\psi$.

Conversely, a [subformula occurrence](../../../../../../subformula-occurrence.md) starting at the first symbol of $\neg\psi$ is an initial [subformula](../../../../../../subformula.md), so equals the whole [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) by [prefix-freeness of fully parenthesized formulas](../../../../../../prefix-freeness-of-fully-parenthesized-formulas.md). An occurrence starting later lies wholly inside the following string $\psi$, and is therefore a [subformula](../../../../../../subformula.md) of $\psi$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Sub}(\neg\psi)=\{\neg\psi\}\cup\operatorname{Sub}(\psi).}
$$

Here $\operatorname{Sub}$ records distinct [logical formulas](../../../../../../logical-formula.md); multiple occurrences of the same [logical formula](../../../../../../logical-formula.md) do not create different elements of this [set](../../../../../../set-split.md).
