↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**Yes, for [soundness](../../../../../soundness.md) and completeness of [theoremhood](../../../../../theoremhood.md).** An [axiom-only complete propositional calculus](../../../../../axiom-only-complete-propositional-calculus.md) is obtained as follows. Declare every propositional [tautology](../../../../../tautology.md), and only such a [tautology](../../../../../tautology.md), to be an [axiom](../../../../../axiom.md); supply no [inference rules](../../../../../inference-rule.md). A [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) then consists of a single [axiom](../../../../../axiom.md). Every proved [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) is valid, giving [soundness](../../../../../soundness.md), and every valid [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) is already an [axiom](../../../../../axiom.md), giving completeness.

This is not merely an unspecified collection: whether a finite [propositional formula](../../../../../propositional-formula.md) is a [tautology](../../../../../tautology.md) is decidable by its finite [truth table](../../../../../truth-table.md), so the [axiom set](../../../../../axiom-set.md) can be recognized effectively. It is a deliberately uninformative [Hilbert proof system](../../../../../hilbert-proof-system.md), since all the work has been put into [axiom](../../../../../axiom.md) recognition. If one instead asked for strong completeness of derivations from arbitrary additional assumptions, a system whose only [formal proof](../../../../../formal-proof.md) lines are [axioms](../../../../../axiom.md) or assumptions would not suffice; the affirmative construction here concerns $\vdash\phi$ versus $\models\phi$.
