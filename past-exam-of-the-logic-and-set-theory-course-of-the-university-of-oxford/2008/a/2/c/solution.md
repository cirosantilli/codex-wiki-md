<h1 id="a/2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Under any [Boolean valuation](../../../../../boolean-valuation.md) there are two possibilities. If $\neg(\psi\to\chi)$ is false, the outer [logical implication](../../../../../logical-implication.md) is true by [vacuous truth](../../../../../vacuous-truth.md). If it is true, then $\psi\to\chi$ is false, which forces $\psi$ true and $\chi$ false. In that case $\neg\chi$ is true, so the outer [logical implication](../../../../../logical-implication.md) is again true. These possibilities exhaust the [truth values](../../../../../truth-value.md) of its antecedent; therefore

$$
\boxed{\models\neg(\psi\to\chi)\to\neg\chi.}
$$
