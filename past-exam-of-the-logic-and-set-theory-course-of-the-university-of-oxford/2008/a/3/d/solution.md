<h1 id="a/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**No [finite axiomatization](../../../../../finite-axiomatization.md) exists.** Write $\Gamma$ for the [axioms](../../../../../axiom.md) just constructed and suppose a [finite set](../../../../../finite-set.md) $\Delta$ of [first-order sentences](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) had exactly the same [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md). Its [logical conjunction](../../../../../logical-conjunction.md) $\delta$ is a single [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md), and $\Gamma\models\delta$. By the [compactness theorem](../../../../../compactness-theorem.md) there is a finite [subset](../../../../../subset.md) $\Gamma_0\subseteq\Gamma$ with $\Gamma_0\models\delta$: otherwise $\Gamma\cup\{\neg\delta\}$ would be finitely satisfiable and hence satisfiable, a contradiction.

Only finitely many of the integers $n$ in the torsion-free scheme occur in $\Gamma_0$. Choose a [prime number](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) $p$ larger than all of them. The [cyclic group](../../../../../cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$ satisfies all the [group axioms](../../../../../group-axioms.md) and each of these finitely many conditions from the [torsion-free abelian group axiom scheme](../../../../../torsion-free-abelian-group-axiom-scheme.md): [multiplication](../../../../../multiplication.md) by an [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $1\le n<p$ is invertible modulo $p$, so $nx=0$ implies $x=0$. It consequently satisfies $\Gamma_0$, then $\delta$, and therefore $\Delta$.

But its nonzero elements have [finite order](../../../../../finite-order.md) $p$, so it is not a [torsion-free group](../../../../../torsion-free-group.md), contradicting the assumed description of the [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $\Delta$. This establishes the [nonfinite axiomatizability of torsion-free abelian groups](../../../../../nonfinite-axiomatizability-of-torsion-free-abelian-groups.md), ruling out every [finite set](../../../../../finite-set.md) of defining [first-order sentences](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md), not just finite [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) of the particular scheme in part (c).
