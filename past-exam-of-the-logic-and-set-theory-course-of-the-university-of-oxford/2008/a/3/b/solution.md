<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The assertion that $\mathcal A$ is a [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $\Gamma$ means that every [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) belonging to $\Gamma$ is true in the [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A\models\Gamma\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (\forall\gamma\in\Gamma)\ \mathcal A\models\gamma.}
$$

Equivalently each [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) is true under every [assignment in a first-order structure](../../../../../assignment-in-a-first-order-structure.md), since [first-order sentences](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) have no [free variables](../../../../../free-variable.md). The [first-order sentences](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) must all hold in the same interpretation of the language; one cannot select a different [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) for each member of $\Gamma$.
