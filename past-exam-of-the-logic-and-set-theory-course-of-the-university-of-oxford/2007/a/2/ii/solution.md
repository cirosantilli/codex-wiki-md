<h1 id="a/2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose an auxiliary [propositional formula](../../../../../propositional-formula.md) $\beta$. The two instances of L1 are

$$
\alpha\to(\beta\to\alpha),\qquad
\alpha\to(\neg\beta\to\alpha).
$$

The instance of L2 with $\gamma=\alpha$ is

$$
(\alpha\to(\beta\to\alpha))\to
((\alpha\to(\neg\beta\to\alpha))\to(\alpha\to\alpha)).
$$

Apply [modus ponens rule](../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md) first with the first L1 instance and then with the second. This is an assumption-free [logical derivation](../../../../../logical-derivation.md), so

$$
\boxed{\vdash\alpha\to\alpha.}
$$

In particular no semantic completeness theorem or [deduction theorem for propositional logic](../../../../../deduction-theorem-for-propositional-logic.md) has been assumed to obtain this identity theorem.
