<h1 id="a/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An instance of L3 is

$$
(\alpha\to\beta)\to(\alpha\to(\neg\beta\to\gamma)).
$$

Using the first assumed [logical derivation](../../../../../logical-derivation.md) and [modus ponens rule](../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md) gives $\Gamma\vdash\alpha\to(\neg\beta\to\gamma)$. The L2 instance for these same three [propositional formulas](../../../../../propositional-formula.md) is

$$
(\alpha\to(\beta\to\gamma))\to
((\alpha\to(\neg\beta\to\gamma))\to(\alpha\to\gamma)).
$$

Use the other assumed [logical derivation](../../../../../logical-derivation.md) and then the newly obtained [logical derivation](../../../../../logical-derivation.md) in two applications of [modus ponens rule](../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Gamma\vdash\alpha\to\gamma.}
$$

This [conditional modus ponens](../../../../../conditional-modus-ponens.md) construction is the step needed to discharge assumptions from a [modus ponens rule](../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md) line in part (iv).
