The [first-order terms](../../../../../first-order-term.md) are the smallest collection containing every variable and $c$ and containing $F(s,t)$ whenever $s,t$ are [first-order terms](../../../../../first-order-term.md). The [atomic formulas](../../../../../atomic-formula.md) are exactly $s\approx t$ for [first-order terms](../../../../../first-order-term.md) $s,t$. [Logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) are then obtained from [atomic formulas](../../../../../atomic-formula.md) by the [logical connectives](../../../../../logical-connective.md) and [quantifiers](../../../../../quantifier.md): if $\theta,\psi$ are [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md) and $x$ a variable, then $\neg\theta$, $(\theta\wedge\psi)$, $(\theta\vee\psi)$, $(\theta\to\psi)$, $\forall x\theta$ and $\exists x\theta$ are [logical formulas](../../../../../logical-formula.md). These closure rules, with no additional strings admitted, define the syntax; a presentation using fewer primitive connectives treats the others as abbreviations.

[Semantic entailment](../../../../../semantic-entailment.md) is the condition

$$
\boxed{\Gamma\models\phi\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{for every }\mathfrak A\text{ and every assignment }v,
\bigl(\mathfrak A\models\gamma[v]\text{ for all }\gamma\in\Gamma\bigr)
\Longrightarrow\mathfrak A\models\phi[v].}
$$

The same [first-order variable assignment](../../../../../first-order-variable-assignment.md) is used for all premises and the conclusion. This matters when [free variables](../../../../../free-variable.md) occur; for [first-order sentences](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) the [truth value](../../../../../truth-value.md) is independent of the [first-order variable assignment](../../../../../first-order-variable-assignment.md).
