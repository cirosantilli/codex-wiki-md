↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Soundness](../../../../../soundness.md) states that [formal derivability](../../../../../formal-derivability.md) implies [semantic entailment](../../../../../semantic-entailment.md):

$$
\boxed{\Gamma\vdash_K\phi\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma\models\phi.}
$$

For [first-order sentences](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) this means that every [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md) satisfying all assumptions also satisfies every [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) formally derived from them. In the [logical formula](../../../../../logical-formula.md) version one uses the usual restrictions on generalization from assumptions with [free variables](../../../../../free-variable.md).

Strong completeness states the converse:

$$
\boxed{\Gamma\models\phi\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma\vdash_K\phi.}
$$

Equivalently, every syntactically consistent [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of [first-order sentences](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) has a [first-order model](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md). Equality is interpreted as actual identity, and its usual logical [axioms](../../../../../axiom.md) and substitution rules are part of the standard calculus. Both assertions hold for arbitrary [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) of assumptions, rather than only [finite sets](../../../../../finite-set.md).
