<h1 id="b/8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Irrationality of $\sqrt2$ makes the expression $q+r\sqrt2$ unique for rational $q,r$: equality of two such expressions with different $r$ would express $\sqrt2$ as a [rational number](../../../../../rational-number.md). Therefore the rule selecting the coefficient $r$ defines a [function](../../../../../function-split.md) on $A$. Its domain is a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) over $\mathbb Q$, and direct coefficient [addition](../../../../../addition.md) and rational scaling show it is $\mathbb Q$-linear, hence weakly linear. In particular

$$
f(1)=0,\qquad f(\sqrt2)=1.
$$

Use [Zorn's lemma](../../../../../zorn-s-lemma.md) on the nonempty family of weakly [linear extensions](../../../../../linear-extension.md) of this particular [function](../../../../../function-split.md). [Chain](../../../../../chain-in-a-partial-order.md) [unions](../../../../../set-union.md) remain extensions by (a); hence there is a maximal extension $h$. Any further extension in $\mathcal F$ would also contain the original [function](../../../../../function-split.md), so this $h$ is maximal in $\mathcal F$. Part (b) therefore gives $\operatorname{dom}h=\mathbb R$.

A [real-linear map](../../../../../real-linear-map.md) $\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is [multiplication](../../../../../multiplication.md) by its value at $1$. Were $h$ real-linear, it would satisfy

$$
h(\sqrt2)=\sqrt2\,h(1)=0,
$$

contradicting the inherited value $h(\sqrt2)=1$. Thus **a [weakly linear function](../../../../../weakly-linear-partial-function.md) on all of $\mathbb R$ need not be real-linear**. Its [rational homogeneity of an additive function](../../../../../rational-homogeneity-of-an-additive-function.md) is insufficient for real homogeneity. This particular extension is also discontinuous: [rational numbers](../../../../../rational-number.md) approaching $\sqrt2$ have value [zero](../../../../../zero-of-a-function.md), while the value at their [limit](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) is one.
