<h1 id="b/5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Fix $m$ and induct on $n$. At $n=0$,

$$
m^++0=m^+=(m+0)^+=m+0^+.
$$

If $m^++n=m+n^+$, then

$$
m^++n^+=(m^++n)^+=(m+n^+)^+=m+(n^+)^+.
$$

The last expression is the right-hand side required at the successor $n^+$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{m^++n=m+n^+\quad(m,n\in\omega).}
$$

In particular both sides also equal $(m+n)^+$ by the [recursion](../../../../../recursion-computer-science.md) equation for the second argument.
