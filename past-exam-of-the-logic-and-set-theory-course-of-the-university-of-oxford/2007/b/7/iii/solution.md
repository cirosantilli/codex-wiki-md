<h1 id="b/7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $D=\mathbb Q+i\mathbb Q$ is [countable](../../../../../countable-set.md) and dense in $\mathbb C$. Restriction to $D$ is [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md) on [continuous functions](../../../../../continuous-function.md) $\mathbb C\to\mathbb R$: if two such [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) agree there, their equality [set](../../../../../set-split.md) is closed and contains a [dense set](../../../../../dense-set.md), so is all of $\mathbb C$. Equivalently a [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) of points of $D$ converging to any chosen point and [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) determine its value uniquely.

Thus the family has [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) at most

$$
|\mathbb R^D|=\mathfrak c^{\aleph_0}
=(2^{\aleph_0})^{\aleph_0}=2^{\aleph_0}=\mathfrak c.
$$

For the [lower bound](../../../../../lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md), every [real number](../../../../../real-number.md) determines a different constant [continuous function](../../../../../continuous-function.md). Cantor-Schröder-Bernstein yields

$$
\boxed{|C(\mathbb C,\mathbb R)|=\mathfrak c.}
$$

The reduction to countably many values is the feature that separates this answer from unrestricted [functions](../../../../../function-split.md).
