<h1 id="b/7/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The family is a [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of all complex [sequences](../../../../../sequence.md), so its [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) is at most

$$
|\mathbb C^{\mathbb N}|=\mathfrak c^{\aleph_0}=\mathfrak c.
$$

For each $z\in\mathbb C$, the [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) $(z,0,0,\ldots)$ belongs to the family because its displayed [series](../../../../../series-mathematics.md) has just one nonzero [first-order term](../../../../../first-order-term.md). These [sequences](../../../../../sequence.md) are distinct as $z$ varies, giving a [lower bound](../../../../../lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) $\mathfrak c$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{cardinality}=\mathfrak c.}
$$

The source condition is [convergence](../../../../../convergence.md) of $\sum_j a_j^2$, without absolute-value bars. The argument uses exactly that printed condition and makes no assertion that it is equivalent to square summability of complex [sequences](../../../../../sequence.md). The same [upper and lower bounds](../../../../../upper-and-lower-bounds.md) would also work for the stronger absolute-square condition, but no alteration of the source is needed.
