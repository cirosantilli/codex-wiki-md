<h1 id="a/2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $f=\alpha\to(\beta\to\gamma)$ and $h=\alpha\to\beta$. Temporarily assume $f$, $h$, and $\alpha$. [Modus ponens rule](../../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md) first gives $\beta\to\gamma$ from $f$ and $\alpha$, then $\beta$ from $h$ and $\alpha$, and finally $\gamma$.

Apply the [deduction theorem for propositional logic](../../../../../../deduction-theorem-for-propositional-logic.md) successively to discharge $\alpha$, then $h$, then $f$. The result is

$$
\boxed{(\alpha\to(\beta\to\gamma))\to((\alpha\to\beta)\to(\alpha\to\gamma)),}
$$

the required axiom scheme. This derivation uses only assumptions, [modus ponens rule](../../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md), and the added deduction rule; no [negation](../../../../../../negation.md) axiom is involved.
