<h1 id="a/2/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Only one [logical implication](../../../../../../logical-implication.md) of the printed equivalence is justified:

$$
\boxed{\Gamma\vdash_{L'_0}\phi\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma\vdash_{L_0}\phi.}
$$

The new system's first axiom is an old axiom, its replacement axiom is derivable in the old system by part (iii), [modus ponens rule](../../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md) is shared, and its deduction rule is admissible in the old system by the theorem allowed in the source. Induction on a new-system derivation therefore translates it into an old-system derivation with the same assumptions.

The reverse [logical implication](../../../../../../logical-implication.md) is false for the displayed systems. Take $\Gamma=\varnothing$ and let $\phi$ be an instance of the old contraposition axiom, for example with distinct [propositional variables](../../../../../../propositional-variable.md) in place of $\alpha,\beta$. It is an old-system theorem, but the matrix countermodel in part (ii) refutes its derivability in the new system. Thus the requested two-way equivalence cannot be concluded from these printed axioms.
