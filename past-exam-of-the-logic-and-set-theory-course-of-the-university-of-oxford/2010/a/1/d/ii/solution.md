↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**No.** The full language can express $\neg(x_0\dot=x_0)$, which is false in the singleton structure from part (i). Every formula of the reduced language is true in that structure under every [assignment in a first-order structure](../../../../../../assignment-in-a-first-order-structure.md). Therefore no formula of the reduced language can be logically equivalent to this negated equality.

This is a [singleton truth-preservation obstruction to expressive completeness](../../../../../../singleton-truth-preservation-obstruction-to-expressive-completeness.md). Adding both quantifiers to [logical implication](../../../../../../logical-implication.md) does not repair the failure: on this particular nonempty singleton model, quantification cannot create a false formula from true [atomic formulas](../../../../../../atomic-formula.md).
