**False.** A ternary atomic predicate gives a counterexample:

$$
\boxed{\phi=P_0^{(3)}(x_0,x_0,x_0).}
$$

This string contains one opening parenthesis and two commas. Thus its number of opening parentheses is smaller than its number of commas. A high-arity argument list contributes several commas while requiring only one pair of parentheses.
