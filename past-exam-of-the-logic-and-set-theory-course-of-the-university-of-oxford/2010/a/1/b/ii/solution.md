↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** Choose a variable $x_j$ different from $x_i$ and take

$$
\boxed{\phi=\forall x_j\,P_0^{(1)}(x_j).}
$$

The variable $x_i$ does not occur at all, so in particular it is not free. Yet the formula has one universal-quantifier symbol and zero occurrences of $x_i$. The hypothesis on free occurrences of one variable does not control quantifiers binding other variables.
