<h1 id="a/3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The downward [Löwenheim-Skolem theorem](../../../../../../lowenheim-skolem-theorem.md) says that a structure in a [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) language has a [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) [elementary substructure](../../../../../../elementary-substructure.md) containing any specified finite or [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) set of its elements. More generally the size bound is $\max(|\mathcal L|,|C|,\aleph_0)$ for a required parameter set $C$. In particular any satisfiable theory in a [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) language has a model of size at most [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md).

To see the last assertion directly from the completeness construction, add only countably many witness constants in the [Henkin construction](../../../../../../henkin-construction.md). There are then countably many finite [closed terms](../../../../../../closed-term.md), and taking their quotient cannot increase [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md). The resulting term model is therefore [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md). For the elementary-substructure version, perform the witness construction inside the given structure, choosing actual witnesses and closing under the [function symbols](../../../../../../function-symbol.md). The induced substructure is elementary by the [Tarski-Vaught test](../../../../../../tarski-vaught-test.md). This is the same witness mechanism as the completeness construction, with the ambient structure supplying the chosen witnesses.

The upward companion states that a theory with an infinite model has models in every sufficiently large infinite cardinal, at least $|\mathcal L|+\aleph_0$. Add that many pairwise unequal constants, apply compactness, and then the size-bounded construction. The [countable](../../../../../../countable-set.md) consequence is the part needed below.
