↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the [Henkin construction](../../../../../../henkin-construction.md), first extend the consistent sentence set to a complete consistent theory with witnesses in an expanded language containing fresh constants. Its [term model](../../../../../../term-model.md) has [closed terms](../../../../../../closed-term.md) modulo provable equality as elements:

$$
[t]=[u]\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad T\vdash t\dot=u.
$$

Functions are interpreted by applying their symbols to representative terms, and an atomic predicate holds of term classes exactly when the corresponding atomic sentence belongs to $T$. Equality axioms make these definitions independent of representatives. The [Henkin witness property](../../../../../../henkin-witness-property.md) handles the existential step in the truth lemma, so the resulting structure satisfies $T$ and hence the original $\Sigma$.

An arbitrary sentence set need not already have enough [closed terms](../../../../../../closed-term.md) or witnesses. Calling this a term model for $\Sigma$ means a term-model construction of a model satisfying $\Sigma$, usually through that witnessed completion; it does not assert that every arbitrary $\Sigma$ itself already supplies the construction.
