<h1 id="a/3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A set of sentences is [satisfiable](../../../../../../satisfiable-first-order-theory.md) if some nonempty [first-order structure](../../../../../../first-order-structure.md) in its language makes every sentence in the set true. Because the formulas are sentences, their truth is independent of the [assignment in a first-order structure](../../../../../../assignment-in-a-first-order-structure.md).

This is a semantic existence condition. Its equivalence with syntactic consistency is the content of the classical [Gödel's completeness theorem](../../../../../../godel-s-completeness-theorem.md), together with soundness.
