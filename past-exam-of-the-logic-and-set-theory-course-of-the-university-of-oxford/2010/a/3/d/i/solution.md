<h1 id="a/3/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the axioms for a strict [dense linear order without endpoints](../../../../../../dense-linear-order-without-endpoints.md):

$$
\forall x\,\neg(x<x),\qquad
\forall x\forall y\forall z\,((x<y\wedge y<z)\to x<z),
$$



$$
\forall x\forall y\,(x<y\vee x=y\vee y<x),\qquad
\forall x\forall y\,(x<y\to\exists z(x<z\wedge z<y)),
$$



$$
\forall x\exists y\,(y<x),\qquad\forall x\exists y\,(x<y).
$$

The first three give strict linear order; the next gives density; the final two exclude endpoints. Domains are nonempty under the adopted first-order semantics, and these axioms force them to be infinite.
