**Yes.** The induced order is strict and linear and remains dense. For $0<x<1$, the points $x/2$ and $(x+1)/2$ lie in the domain and are strictly below and above $x$. Hence the open interval is a [dense linear order without endpoints](../../../../../../../dense-linear-order-without-endpoints.md).
