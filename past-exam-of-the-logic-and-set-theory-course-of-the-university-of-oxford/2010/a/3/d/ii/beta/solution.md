**Yes.** The usual order on the [real numbers](../../../../../../../real-number.md) is strict and linear, midpoints witness density, and translation by $\pm1$ supplies points on either side. It therefore satisfies the same [dense linear order without endpoints](../../../../../../../dense-linear-order-without-endpoints.md) axioms.
