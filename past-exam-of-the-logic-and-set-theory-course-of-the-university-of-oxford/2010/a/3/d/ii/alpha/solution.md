**Yes.** The usual order on the [rational numbers](../../../../../../../rational-number.md) is strict and linear. Between $a<b$ lies the rational midpoint $(a+b)/2$, and $a-1,a+1$ witness absence of endpoints. Thus it is a [dense linear order without endpoints](../../../../../../../dense-linear-order-without-endpoints.md).
