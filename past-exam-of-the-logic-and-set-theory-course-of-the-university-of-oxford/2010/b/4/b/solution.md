<h1 id="b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**True in ZF.** A function from $M$ into the functions $B\to A$ corresponds bijectively to a function from $B\times M$ into $A$:

$$
\boxed{F\longmapsto f,\qquad f(b,m)=F(m)(b).}
$$

The inverse sends $f$ to the function $m\mapsto(b\mapsto f(b,m))$. These two explicit transformations are inverses, proving the [currying law for cardinal exponentiation](../../../../../currying-law-for-cardinal-exponentiation.md). Empty domains and codomains cause no exception: the same correspondence still applies, including the unique [empty function](../../../../../empty-function.md) when its domain is empty. No selection of representatives or choice functions is involved.
