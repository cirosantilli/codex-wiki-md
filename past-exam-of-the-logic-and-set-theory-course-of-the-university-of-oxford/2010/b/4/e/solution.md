↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**False.** Take $\kappa=2^{\aleph_0}$ and $\lambda=\aleph_0$, both infinite. The explicit pairing [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) between $\mathbb N^2$ and $\mathbb N$ and the [currying law for cardinal exponentiation](../../../../../currying-law-for-cardinal-exponentiation.md) give

$$
\boxed{\kappa^\lambda=(2^{\aleph_0})^{\aleph_0}
=2^{\aleph_0\cdot\aleph_0}=2^{\aleph_0}=\kappa.}
$$

The power-set cardinal need not be assumed well-orderable. Every [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) used in this calculation is explicitly available in ZF, so the counterexample does not use choice.
