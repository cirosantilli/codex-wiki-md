# Coherent ideal sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md)

An [ideal sheaf of a closed subscheme](ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) that is a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) as a [module](module-mathematics.md) over the [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md). On a [Noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md), every [quasi-coherent](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) ideal sheaf is coherent.
