# Linear interpolation

↑ **Parent:** [Piecewise linear function](piecewise-linear-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_interpolation)

Between two prescribed values, [linear interpolation](linear-interpolation.md) assigns $(1-t)f(a)+tf(b)$ at $(1-t)a+tb$, for $0\leq t\leq1$. Joining successive samples produces a [piecewise linear function](piecewise-linear-function.md). For a [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on a compact interval, interpolants along grids with mesh tending to zero converge in the [uniform norm](supremum-norm.md); their error is bounded by the [modulus of continuity](modulus-of-continuity.md) at the mesh size.
