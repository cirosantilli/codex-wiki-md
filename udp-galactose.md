# UDP-galactose

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide sugar](nucleotide-sugar.md)

[UDP-galactose](udp-galactose.md) is an activated donor containing a galactose residue linked to UDP. Transfer requires an appropriate [glycosyltransferase](glycosyltransferase.md) and acceptor; access to the correct compartment can be limiting.
