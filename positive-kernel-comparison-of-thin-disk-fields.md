# Positive-kernel comparison of thin-disk fields

↑ **Parent:** [Razor-thin disk Poisson kernel](razor-thin-disk-poisson-kernel.md)

The vertical field of a disk has kernel $Gz/(|\mathbf R-\mathbf R'|^2+z^2)^{3/2}>0$. The [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) therefore bounds a signed-[surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) field by the field of its absolute [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md). If that [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) is everywhere smaller than a nonnegative comparison [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md), their vertical fields satisfy the displayed strict comparison, provided the integrals exist and the strict bound holds on positive measure. This is useful when comparing [gravity-equivalent magnetic surface density](gravity-equivalent-magnetic-surface-density.md) with actual disk mass.
