# Strongly lacunary polynomial L1-L2 bound

↑ **Parent:** [Lacunary Fourier series](lacunary-fourier-series.md)

Use normalized measure on the circle. If a [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md) has frequency support $\{\pm n_j\}$ with $n_{j+1}>3n_j$, every zero sum of four signed frequencies pairs into opposite equal frequencies: the largest unpaired term would exceed the sum of the other three. Expanding the fourth moment and bounding its three possible pairings gives $\|p\|_4^4\leq3\|p\|_2^4$. The [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) gives $\|p\|_2\leq\|p\|_1^{1/3}\|p\|_4^{2/3}$ and hence the displayed estimate. Therefore an $L^1$ function with only these lacunary frequencies has square-summable coefficients: apply the estimate to its uniformly $L^1$-bounded [Fejér sums](fejer-sum.md) and then pass to finite coefficient blocks. Finitely many extra modes do not affect the conclusion.
