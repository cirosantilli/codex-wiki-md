# Global survival threshold of the contact process

↑ **Parent:** [Survival probability of the contact process](survival-probability-of-the-contact-process.md)

This is the critical infection rate for the [contact process](contact-process.md) started from one infected site to avoid its absorbing empty state forever. It concerns infection somewhere in the [graph](graph-split.md) at all times, rather than repeated infection of a fixed site. On a transitive [graph](graph-split.md) it is independent of the starting site. Infection-arrow inclusion couples different rates monotonically.
