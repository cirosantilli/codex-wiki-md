# Hyperbolic sine

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic function](hyperbolic-function.md)

The [hyperbolic sine](hyperbolic-sine.md) is the [odd function](odd-function.md)

$$
\sinh x=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2.
$$

Its [derivative](derivative.md) is $\cosh x\geq1$, so it increases strictly from $-\infty$ to $\infty$ and is a [bijection](bijection.md) of $\mathbb R$ onto itself. For example, $\sinh0=0$, $\sinh1\approx1.175201$, and $\sinh(-1)\approx-1.175201$. It is the vertical coordinate on the unit [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) parametrized by $(\cosh x,\sinh x)$.

The [Taylor series](taylor-series.md)

$$
\sinh x=x+\frac{x^3}{3!}+\frac{x^5}{5!}+\cdots
$$

shows that $\sinh x\approx x$ near zero. Its [tangent line](tangent-line.md) there is $y=x$; unlike the ordinary [sine](sine.md), the [hyperbolic sine](hyperbolic-sine.md) grows without bound. Its [antiderivative](antiderivative.md) is $\cosh x+C$. In the [catenary](catenary.md) $y=a\cosh(x/a)$, the [hyperbolic sine](hyperbolic-sine.md) gives both the slope, through the [derivative](derivative.md) $y'=\sinh(x/a)$, and the [arc length](arc-length.md) $s(x)=a\sinh(x/a)$ from the lowest point for $x\geq0$.
