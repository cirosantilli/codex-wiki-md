# Facial mimicry

↑ **Parent:** [Facial expression](facial-expression.md)

Facial reactions congruent with another person's observed [facial movements](facial-movement.md). [Facial electromyography](facial-electromyography.md) can measure small congruent responses. Congruent movement alone does not establish [emotional contagion](emotional-contagion.md) or a necessary route to [emotion recognition](emotion-recognition.md).
