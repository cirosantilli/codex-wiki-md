# Cartesian second moment of a solid ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Solid ellipsoid](solid-ellipsoid.md)

For the [solid ellipsoid](solid-ellipsoid.md) $D=\{x^2/a^2+y^2/b^2+z^2/c^2\le1\}$ with positive semi-axes, set $x=au$, $y=bv$, $z=cw$. The [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) of this inverse coordinate map is $abc$, and $D$ becomes the unit ball. Symmetry gives $\int_{B_1}u^2\,dV=\frac13\int_{B_1}r^2\,dV=4\pi/15$, proving the displayed moment. The other two moments follow by interchanging the semi-axes; mixed moments vanish by reflection symmetry.
