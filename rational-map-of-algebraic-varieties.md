# Rational map of algebraic varieties

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md)

A rational map is a [morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) defined on a dense [open subset](open-set.md), with representatives identified when they agree on a smaller dense [open subset](open-set.md). For an integral source and a [separated variety](separated-variety.md) as target, agreement on a dense open determines a morphism uniquely. This definition also permits nonprojective sources.
