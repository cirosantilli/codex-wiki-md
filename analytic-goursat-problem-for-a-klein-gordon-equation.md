<h1 id="analytic-goursat-problem-for-a-klein-gordon-equation">Analytic Goursat problem for a Klein--Gordon equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Kovalevskaya theorem](cauchy-kovalevskaya-theorem.md)

In null coordinates, the characteristic boundary problem

$$
u_{\xi\eta}=-\frac14u,\qquad
u(\xi,0)=a(\xi),\qquad
u(0,\eta)=b(\eta)
$$

is a Volterra integral equation. Compatible analytic boundary data determine a unique analytic solution.
