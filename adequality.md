# Adequality

↑ **Parent:** [History of mathematics](history-of-mathematics.md)

A procedure associated with [Pierre de Fermat](pierre-de-fermat.md) for [extrema](maximum-and-minimum.md) and [tangents](tangent.md). Compare expressions at $a$ and $a+e$, remove common terms, divide by a remaining power of $e$, and suppress terms still containing $e$. Its interpretation must be distinguished from ordinary equality at finite $e$ and from a modern [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) definition.[Fermat's rule for maxima and minima by the Garden of Archimedes](fermat-s-rule-for-maxima-and-minima-by-the-garden-of-archimedes.md)
