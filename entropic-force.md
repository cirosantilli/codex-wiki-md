# Entropic force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropic_force)

An [entropic force](entropic-force.md) is the part of a generalized [force](force-split.md) arising from the dependence of [entropy](entropy-split.md) on a coordinate. At fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), the system's force is $-\partial A/\partial x=-\partial U/\partial x+T\partial S/\partial x$, where $A=U-TS$ is the [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md). If the [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) is independent of the coordinate, the force is entirely entropic. For an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md) whose configurations become less numerous on extension, it acts toward shorter extension. The external [tension](tension.md) holding the chain extended has the opposite sign. Thus the force depends on the statistical number of available configurations, even when the energy of each configuration is unchanged.
