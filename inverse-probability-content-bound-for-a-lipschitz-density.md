# Inverse probability content bound for a Lipschitz density

↑ **Parent:** [Probability content of a nearest neighbour ball](probability-content-of-a-nearest-neighbour-ball.md)

For a strictly positive [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) with [Lipschitz bound](lipschitz-bound.md) $L$, $p_x(r)=\int_{x-r}^{x+r}f(y)\,dy$ satisfies $|p_x(r)-2rf(x)|\leq Lr^2$. If $0<s<1$ and $s\leq f(x)^2/L$, monotonicity gives $p_x^{-1}(s)\leq s/f(x)$ and hence $|2f(x)p_x^{-1}(s)-s|\leq Ls^2/f(x)^2$. The [Lipschitz density height bound](lipschitz-density-height-bound.md) prevents extending this local argument to every $s\in(0,1)$ by assuming $f(x)\geq\sqrt L$ for a strictly positive density.
