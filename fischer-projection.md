# Fischer projection

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fischer_projection)

In a [Fischer projection](fischer-projection.md), each cross represents a [carbon](carbon.md) position: horizontal [chemical bonds](chemical-bond.md) point toward the viewer and vertical [chemical bonds](chemical-bond.md) point away. For the open-chain [aldehyde](aldehyde.md) [sugars](sugar.md) illustrated here, place $\mathrm{CHO}$ at the top and $\mathrm{CH}_2\mathrm{OH}$ at the bottom. This is a convention for [stereochemistry](stereochemistry.md), not a measured molecular shape. See [Moss, IUPAC Recommendations 1996: Fischer projection](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/stereo/FM.html#Fischer).

In these [Fischer projections](fischer-projection.md), D specifies that the [hydroxy group](hydroxy-group.md) at the lowest [stereocenter](stereocenter.md) lies on the right: C4 for [ribose](ribose.md) and [deoxyribose](deoxyribose.md), C5 for [glucose](glucose.md). D does not specify every other [stereocenter](stereocenter.md), and it does not specify the direction of rotation of polarized [light](light.md). See [McMurry: the meaning of D and L](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/25-3-d-l-sugars).
