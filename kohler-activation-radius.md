<h1 id="kohler-activation-radius">Köhler activation radius</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Critical supersaturation](critical-supersaturation.md)

In the dilute radius form $\ln S_{\rm eq}=A/r-B/r^3$, the curvature coefficient A has length units and B has volume units. Setting its derivative to zero gives the displayed maximum. Ambient supersaturation above it permits activation into a growing [cloud droplet](cloud-droplet.md). Below it, a sufficiently small wet equilibrium can be stable, while the larger-radius intersection is unstable. The approximation should not be extrapolated into the dry core.
