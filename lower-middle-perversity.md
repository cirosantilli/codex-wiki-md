# Lower middle perversity

↑ **Parent:** [Perversity](perversity.md)

The [lower middle perversity](lower-middle-perversity.md) is $\bar m(k)=\lfloor(k-2)/2\rfloor$. The complementary [upper middle perversity](upper-middle-perversity.md) is $\bar n(k)=\lceil(k-2)/2\rceil$, and $\bar m(k)+\bar n(k)=k-2$. They agree at even codimensions.
