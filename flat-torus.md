# Flat torus

↑ **Parent:** [Flat manifold](flat-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat_torus)

A flat torus is $\mathbb R^d/\Lambda$ with its quotient [Euclidean metric](euclidean-metric.md), where $\Lambda$ is a full-rank [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md). Its volume is the lattice covolume. The [dual lattice](dual-lattice.md) indexes its complete [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) of [Laplacian eigenfunctions](laplacian-eigenfunction.md).
