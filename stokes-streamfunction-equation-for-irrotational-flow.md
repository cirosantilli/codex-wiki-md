# Stokes streamfunction equation for irrotational flow

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes streamfunction](stokes-streamfunction.md)

For an [axisymmetric flow](axisymmetric-flow.md) without azimuthal [velocity](velocity.md), the spherical [Stokes streamfunction](stokes-streamfunction.md) gives $u_r=\Psi_\theta/(r^2\sin\theta)$ and $u_\theta=-\Psi_r/(r\sin\theta)$. These formulas enforce [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md). The only possibly nonzero component of [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) is

$$
(\nabla\times\mathbf u)_\varphi=-\frac{r^2\Psi_{rr}+\Psi_{\theta\theta}-\cot\theta\,\Psi_\theta}{r^3\sin\theta}.
$$

Thus the displayed equation is precisely the condition for [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md), away from coordinate poles, with limits interpreted through regularity of the physical [velocity field](velocity-field.md). It differs from the scalar spherical [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md): $\Psi$ is a [Stokes streamfunction](stokes-streamfunction.md), not a [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md).
