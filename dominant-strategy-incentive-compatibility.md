# Dominant-strategy incentive compatibility

↑ **Parent:** [Incentive compatibility](incentive-compatibility.md)

Dominant-strategy incentive compatibility means truthful reporting maximizes a player's utility for every fixed profile of the other reports. It is the monetary-mechanism version of [strategyproofness](strategyproofness.md) and implies [Bayesian incentive compatibility](bayesian-incentive-compatibility.md) under any independent prior.
