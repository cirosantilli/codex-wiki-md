# Binary palindrome

↑ **Parent:** [Palindromic number](palindromic-number.md)

A [palindromic number](palindromic-number.md) in [binary representation](binary-number.md), with no leading zeros. Every positive [binary palindrome](binary-palindrome.md) is an [odd number](odd-number.md), because its first and last [numerical digits](numerical-digit.md) must both be one.
