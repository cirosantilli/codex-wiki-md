# Linearized tangent cone

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent tangent cone](contingent-tangent-cone.md)

For smooth inequalities $c_j\geq0$, the displayed intersection of half-spaces contains the [contingent tangent cone](contingent-tangent-cone.md). This follows by differentiating each active feasibility inequality along a feasible tangent sequence. The [linear independence constraint qualification](linear-independence-constraint-qualification.md), and more generally the [Mangasarian–Fromovitz constraint qualification](mangasarian-fromovitz-constraint-qualification.md), ensure equality in the inequality-only setting. Without a qualification the inclusion can be strict: for $c(x)=-x^2\geq0$, the feasible set is $\{0\}$ and its tangent cone is $\{0\}$, while $c'(0)=0$ makes the linearized cone the whole real line.
