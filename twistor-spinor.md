# Twistor spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor covariant derivative](spinor-covariant-derivative.md)

A [twistor spinor](twistor-spinor.md) has vanishing gamma-trace-free part of its [spinor covariant derivative](spinor-covariant-derivative.md). In $n$ dimensions the displayed equation uses [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) satisfying $\{\gamma_a,\gamma_b\}=2g_{ab}$. It is weaker than being a [parallel spinor](parallel-spinor.md). In four dimensions it implies $[\gamma_a\nabla_b+\gamma_b\nabla_a-g_{ab}\gamma^c\nabla_c/2]\epsilon=0$.
