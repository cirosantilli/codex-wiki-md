# Cardinal directions of color space

↑ **Parent:** [Opponent color processing](opponent-color-processing.md)

Stimulus directions distinguished by selective adaptation or physiological response nulls under specified conditions, commonly associated with an achromatic-like combination and two cone-opponent combinations. Their usefulness near a particular background does not make them identical to unique-hue directions or sufficient for every suprathreshold appearance judgment.
