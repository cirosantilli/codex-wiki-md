# Cotangent bundle orientation

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md)

Every [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md) $T^*M$ is an [orientable smooth manifold](orientable-smooth-manifold.md) even when its base is not. The intrinsic [canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle](canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) $\lambda_{(p,\alpha)}(W)=\alpha(d\pi W)$ has $d\lambda=\sum_i da_i\wedge dx^i$. Its $n$th wedge power is the nowhere-zero form $n!\,da_1\wedge dx^1\wedge\cdots\wedge da_n\wedge dx^n$, which defines an [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) of the $2n$-dimensional total space. Using $-d\lambda$ gives another standard sign convention; either is a [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md).
