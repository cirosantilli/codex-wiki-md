# Finite faithful representation from point-separating representations

↑ **Parent:** [Peter-Weyl theorem](peter-weyl-theorem.md)

Suppose finite-dimensional [continuous](continuous-function.md) [group representations](group-representation.md) separate points of a [compact Lie group](compact-lie-group.md). Choose an identity neighborhood $U$ containing no nontrivial subgroup, using [Lie groups have no small subgroups](lie-groups-have-no-small-subgroups.md). For each $g\notin U$, choose a representation whose matrix at $g$ is not the identity. The corresponding open nonidentity sets cover the compact complement of $U$, so finitely many representations suffice there. Their [direct sum](direct-sum.md) has kernel inside $U$ and therefore trivial kernel. [Unitarization of a compact-group representation](unitarization-of-a-compact-group-representation.md) makes it a finite-dimensional faithful [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md).
