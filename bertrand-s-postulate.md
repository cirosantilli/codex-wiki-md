<h1 id="bertrand-s-postulate">Bertrand's postulate</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number.md)

For every integer $n>1$, there is a [prime number](prime-number.md) strictly between $n$ and $2n$. This supplies cyclic prime moduli comparable to an integer interval's length in [additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md).
