# Decomposable positive map

↑ **Parent:** [Positive linear map](positive-linear-map.md)

A decomposable positive map between complex matrix algebras has the form $\Phi=\Phi_1+\Phi_2\circ T$, where $\Phi_1,\Phi_2$ are [completely positive maps](completely-positive-map.md) and $T$ is the [matrix transpose](transpose.md). Such a map cannot detect a [positive partial transpose](positive-partial-transpose.md) entangled state: applying it to one subsystem gives a sum of two positive operators.
