# Beltrami coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiconformal mapping](quasiconformal-mapping.md)

For an [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md)-preserving [quasiconformal map](quasiconformal-mapping.md), the almost-everywhere coefficient $\mu_f=f_{\bar z}/f_z$. It is a coordinate-dependent representative of a tensor; the modulus and the resulting [maximal dilatation](maximal-dilatation.md) are coordinate-independent.
