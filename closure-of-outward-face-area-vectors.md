# Closure of outward face area vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Vector surface area element](vector-surface-area-element.md)

For a closed [polyhedron](polyhedron.md), let $A_F$ be a face area and $\mathbf n_F$ its outward [unit normal vector](unit-normal.md). The sum of the face area [vectors](vector.md) is zero. To prove it, fix any constant [vector](vector.md) $\mathbf q$ and apply the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) to the constant [vector field](vector-field.md) $\mathbf q$: its [divergence](divergence.md) vanishes, while its outward flux is $\mathbf q\cdot\sum_F A_F\mathbf n_F$. This is zero for every $\mathbf q$, so the sum vanishes. For a [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md), the same identity follows directly by expanding the four oriented face [cross products](cross-product.md); multiplying every face [vector](vector.md) by two leaves the identity intact.
