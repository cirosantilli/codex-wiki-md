# Location-scale form of the logistic distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Logistic distribution](logistic-distribution.md)

For a real location parameter $\mu$ and positive scale parameter $s$, the [logistic distribution](logistic-distribution.md) has [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) and [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md)

$$
f(x)=\frac1{4s}\operatorname{sech}^2\!\left(\frac{x-\mu}{2s}\right),\qquad
F(x)=\frac12\left[1+\tanh\!\left(\frac{x-\mu}{2s}\right)\right].
$$

Differentiating $F$ recovers $f$, and its limits at infinity show [normalization of a probability distribution](normalization-of-a-probability-distribution.md). The density is symmetric about $\mu$ with exponentially decaying tails, so its [expected value](expected-value-split.md) exists and equals $\mu$. Allowing $s<0$ in the displayed expression makes $f$ negative and its integral $-1$; a signed scale must instead be replaced by $|s|$ in the prefactor to define the same valid density.
