# Redox equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Redox](redox.md)

A [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) involving coupled oxidation and reduction. Its position depends on activities and can be shifted by [complexation](complexation.md), [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) or [precipitation](precipitation-chemistry.md), because these change the relative free energies of [oxidation states](oxidation-state.md).
