# Venous valve

↑ **Parent:** [Vein](vein.md)

A structure within a [vein](vein.md) that helps oppose backward movement of [blood](blood.md). [William Harvey](william-harvey.md) used observations of these structures and experiments with ligatures in his argument for [blood circulation](blood-circulation.md). [chapter XIII](on-the-motion-of-the-heart-and-blood-in-animals-by-william-harvey.md)
