# Mesa solution

↑ **Parent:** [Front solution](front-solution.md)

A mesa is a localized broad plateau joined to its surrounding state by two separated [fronts](front-solution.md). Its leading spatial average equals the plateau fraction times the plateau value; the transition layers give corrections proportional to their width divided by the domain length. A conserved mean can determine the fraction occupied by the plateau, as in the [coexistence fraction of a conserved-field amplitude mesa](coexistence-fraction-of-a-conserved-field-amplitude-mesa.md).
