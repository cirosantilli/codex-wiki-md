# Sudden vector-potential pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Electric field](electric-field-split.md)

In [temporal gauge](temporal-gauge.md), a spatially uniform step $\mathbf A(t)=\mathbf A_0\Theta(t)$ gives $\mathbf E=-\mathbf A_0\delta(t)$. A charged scalar Fourier mode retains its canonical [wavevector](wavevector.md) while its [kinetic momentum](kinetic-momentum.md) changes from $\hbar\mathbf k$ to $\hbar\mathbf k-e\mathbf A_0$. The resulting [frequency](frequency.md) jump mixes positive- and negative-frequency modes.
