# Maximal additivity algebra of a regular outer measure

↑ **Parent:** [Regular outer measure](regular-outer-measure.md)

For a [regular outer measure](regular-outer-measure.md), its [Caratheodory measurability criterion](caratheodory-s-criterion.md) defines a maximal [algebra of sets](algebra-of-sets.md) on which that [outer measure](outer-measure.md) is finitely additive. Indeed, suppose an additive algebra contains all [measurable sets](measurable-set.md) and also $A$. Given $E$ of finite [outer measure](outer-measure.md), take a measurable hull $H\supseteq E$ of equal mass. [Finite additivity](finite-additivity-of-a-set-function.md) splits $H$ by $A$, and [monotonicity](monotonic-function.md) gives $\mu^*(E)\geq\mu^*(E\cap A)+\mu^*(E\setminus A)$. Outer subadditivity gives equality. For infinite mass that equality already follows from outer subadditivity. Thus $A$ was measurable. No [sigma-finite measure](sigma-finite-measure.md) assumption is necessary.
