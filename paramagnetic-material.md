# Paramagnetic material

↑ **Parent:** [Paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md)

A material exhibiting [paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md). Its induced [magnetization](magnetization.md) points along the applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) in the linear-response regime, giving positive [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md). Unlike a [ferromagnet](ferromagnet.md), it has no spontaneous ordered moment in that phase. The inverse-temperature response of independent localized moments is one model, rather than a definition that includes all microscopic mechanisms of [paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md).
