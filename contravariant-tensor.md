# Contravariant tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor](tensor.md)

A [contravariant tensor](contravariant-tensor.md) transforms with one forward coordinate Jacobian for each upper index. A [Lorentz four-vector](four-vector.md) is the rank-one example; the [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) with two upper indices is a rank-two example. Lower-index [covariant tensors](covariant-tensor.md) transform with inverse Jacobians, and full contractions yield scalars. Index position specifies a transformation law rather than implying that the numerical components are invariant.
