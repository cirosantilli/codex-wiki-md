# Interaction energy

↑ **Parent:** [Interaction potential](interaction-potential.md)

The factor $1/2$ counts unordered pairs once. For a symmetric [interaction potential](interaction-potential.md), this energy is unchanged by translating the entire [probability measure](probability-measure.md). It therefore cannot have positive uniform [displacement convexity](displacement-convexity.md) on the unrestricted space containing translations. If $D^2W\geq\lambda I$, integrate the pointwise strong [convexity](convex-function.md) inequality along an optimal [transport plan](transport-plan.md) and its independent copy. The [variance identity for independent copies](variance-identity-for-independent-copies.md) then gives $\lambda$-[displacement convexity](displacement-convexity.md) on every class with fixed [barycenter of a measure](barycenter-of-a-measure-on-a-banach-space.md), whenever the integrals are finite.
