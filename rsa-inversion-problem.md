# RSA inversion problem

↑ **Parent:** [RSA cryptosystem](rsa-cryptosystem.md)

Recover a [plaintext](plaintext.md) from an [RSA cryptosystem](rsa-cryptosystem.md) [ciphertext](ciphertext.md) and the [public key](public-key.md), without a supplied [private key](private-key.md). [Integer factorization](integer-factorization.md) of the modulus suffices; the existence of an efficient general reduction in the reverse direction is not known.
