# Elastic eccentric impact of a thin disc

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

Let a nonrotating disc initially move downwards at [speed](speed.md) $u$ and strike a stationary point support offset $d$ from its centre. If the [impulse](impulse.md) is vertical and the normal [coefficient of restitution](coefficient-of-restitution.md) is one, its upward [impulse](impulse.md) satisfies $J(1/M+d^2/I)=2u$. The signed upward centre [velocity](velocity.md) is therefore the title formula, where $I$ is the [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about the horizontal diameter perpendicular to the impact lever arm. At $d=a/2$ for a thin circular disc, $I=Ma^2/4=Md^2$, so its centre instantaneously stops and all initial [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) becomes rotation. A fixed support can absorb [momentum](momentum-split.md) without doing work.
