# Sidereus Nuncius by Galileo Galilei

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Galileo Galilei](work-by-galileo-galilei.md)

[https://library.si.edu/digital-library/book/sidereusnuncius00gali](https://library.si.edu/digital-library/book/sidereusnuncius00gali)  
[https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/galileo/galileo.sid.html](https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/galileo/galileo.sid.html)

Original edition and Latin transcription. English translation by Albert Van Helden: [https://www.reed.edu/math-stats/wieting/mathematics537/SideriusNuncius.pdf](https://www.reed.edu/math-stats/wieting/mathematics537/SideriusNuncius.pdf)

The observations concern lunar relief, stars in the Milky Way and bodies accompanying Jupiter. Later observations of Venus's phases are not part of the March 1610 publication.
