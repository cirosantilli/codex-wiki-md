# Pauli group

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pauli_group)

The $n$-qubit Pauli group consists of tensor products of $I,X,Y,Z$ multiplied by phases $\pm1$ or $\pm i$.
