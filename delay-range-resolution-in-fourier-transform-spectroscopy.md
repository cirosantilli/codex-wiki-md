# Delay-range resolution in Fourier transform spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform spectroscopy](fourier-transform-spectroscopy.md)

A [Michelson interferometer](michelson-interferometer.md) mirror displacement $x$ changes round-trip [optical path difference](optical-path-difference.md) by $2x$, giving delay $\tau=2x/c$. An interferogram recorded over a finite delay range is multiplied by a window; the recovered spectrum is the true spectrum convolved with that window's [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md). Its characteristic [frequency](frequency.md) resolution scales inversely with delay range. Consequently a mirror travel range $L$ gives $\delta\nu\propto c/L$ and $\delta\lambda\simeq(\lambda^2/c)\delta\nu\propto\lambda^2/L$. Numerical width factors depend on the scan definition, window and resolution criterion; the inverse-travel scaling does not.
