# Sum of two squares

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)

A representation $n=a^2+b^2$ with [integers](integer-split.md) $a,b$. Signs, ordering, and whether zero is allowed must be specified when counting representations. The [sum of two squares theorem](sum-of-two-squares-theorem.md) characterizes which [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md) have such a representation.
