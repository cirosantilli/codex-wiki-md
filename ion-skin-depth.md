# Ion skin depth

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

For a singly charged ion species in [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md), $\omega_{pi}=(4\pi ne^2/m_i)^{1/2}$ is its [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md) and $d_i=c\sqrt{m_i/(4\pi ne^2)}$. The ion skin depth marks a characteristic scale below which ion and electron motion can separate in a magnetized [plasma](plasma-physics.md). Together with the [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md), it satisfies $v_Ad_i=cB_0/(4\pi en)$, the coefficient of guide-field propagation in [electron magnetohydrodynamics](electron-magnetohydrodynamics.md).
