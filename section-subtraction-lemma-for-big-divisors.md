# Section subtraction lemma for big divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Big divisor](big-divisor.md)

If $D$ is a [big divisor](big-divisor.md) and $F$ an [effective Cartier divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md), infinitely many $m$ have $H^0(X,mD-F)\ne0$. The [divisor restriction exact sequence](divisor-restriction-exact-sequence.md) bounds the dimension lost upon restriction to $F$ by $O(m^{n-1})$, using the [polynomial bound for sections of a fixed divisor](polynomial-bound-for-sections-of-a-fixed-divisor.md); this cannot exhaust the $c m^n$ sections along the infinite growth sequence.
