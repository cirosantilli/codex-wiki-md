# Modular-addition quantum oracle

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)

For $h:\mathbb Z_N\to\mathbb Z_M$, its modular-addition oracle is the [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md)

$$
U_h|x,y\rangle=|x,y+h(x)\bmod M\rangle.
$$

For each fixed $x$, [addition](addition.md) by $h(x)$ permutes the answer register's [computational basis](computational-basis.md). This preserves [inner products](inner-product.md), even when $h$ is not injective.
