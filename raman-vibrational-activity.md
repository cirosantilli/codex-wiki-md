# Raman vibrational activity

↑ **Parent:** [Raman scattering](raman-scattering.md)

The simplest off-resonant explanation is a vibrating [polarizability](electric-polarizability.md). Let $Q(t)=Q_0\cos(2\pi\nu_vt)$ be a small [normal mode](normal-mode.md) coordinate of [molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md), and use one scalar component of [polarizability](electric-polarizability.md), $\alpha(Q)\simeq\alpha_0+\alpha'_0Q$. For incident [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $E(t)=E_0\cos(2\pi\nu_0t)$, the induced [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) is

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(t)&=\alpha(Q(t))E(t)\\
&=\alpha_0E_0\cos(2\pi\nu_0t)\\
&\quad+\frac{\alpha'_0Q_0E_0}{2}
\left[\cos\bigl(2\pi(\nu_0-\nu_v)t\bigr)
+\cos\bigl(2\pi(\nu_0+\nu_v)t\bigr)\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

The [product-to-sum formula](product-to-sum-formula.md) produces the two displaced [frequencies](frequency.md). A nonzero derivative $\alpha'_0$ gives vibrational [Raman scattering](raman-scattering.md) in this linear model. Real [polarizability](electric-polarizability.md) is generally a [tensor](tensor.md), and the selected components depend on incident and observed [wave polarization](polarization-waves.md). This condition differs from [vibrational infrared activity](vibrational-infrared-activity.md), which requires a changing molecular [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) without an applied optical [electric field](electric-field-split.md). A vibration can therefore appear in one measurement and be absent in the other.

This classical modulation argument explains line locations but supplies equal sideband [wave amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md); it does not explain their thermal asymmetry or the nonzero [Stokes Raman scattering](stokes-raman-scattering.md) from a vibrational [ground state](ground-state.md). Those require the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) population and transition strengths. The far-off-resonance drawing uses dashed virtual intermediate levels: they represent terms in a scattering [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md), not a long-lived occupied electronic [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md). Near an electronic resonance, a more detailed treatment of the intermediate response is needed.
