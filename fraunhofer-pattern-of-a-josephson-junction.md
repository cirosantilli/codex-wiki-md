# Fraunhofer pattern of a Josephson junction

↑ **Parent:** [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md)

A spatially uniform [Josephson junction](josephson-junction.md) in a uniform transverse in-plane [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) has a linear gauge-invariant phase profile across its width. Integrating its sinusoidal local [current-phase relation of a superconducting weak link](current-phase-relation-of-a-superconducting-weak-link.md) gives a signed sinc coefficient multiplying the sine of the central phase. Maximizing over that phase gives the displayed nonnegative critical-current envelope. Zeros occur at nonzero integer multiples of the [superconducting flux quantum](superconducting-flux-quantum.md). The relevant magnetic thickness includes electrode penetration when appreciable, and self-fields or nonuniform local critical current density alter the simple pattern.
