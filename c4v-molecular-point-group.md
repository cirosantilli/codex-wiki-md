# C4v molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The eight-operation [molecular point group](molecular-point-group.md) of an ideal square pyramid. Its polar-vector components transform as $A_1$ for the principal-axis coordinate and $E$ for the transverse pair. Symmetric quadratic functions also contain $B_1$ and $B_2$, allowing [Raman spectroscopy](raman-spectroscopy.md) activity beyond the [infrared](infrared.md) modes.
