# Cosmological surface-brightness dimming

↑ **Parent:** [Surface brightness](surface-brightness.md)

For freely propagating radiation with the usual [distance duality relation](distance-duality-relation.md), a fixed emitted bolometric [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) is observed smaller by $(1+z)^4$. One factor each comes from [photon energy](photon-energy.md) and arrival rate, and two from the relation of projected angular area to [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md). In a fixed observed [photometric passband](photometric-passband.md), spectral shifts add a band-dependent correction; the bolometric power is not automatically the monochromatic one. A distortion created by scattering the local [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) has a different redshift comparison, as in the [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md).
