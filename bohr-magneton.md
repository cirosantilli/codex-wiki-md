# Bohr magneton

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohr_magneton)

The Bohr magneton,

$$
\mu_B=\frac{e\hbar}{2m_e},
$$

is the natural unit of an electron's orbital and spin magnetic moment.
