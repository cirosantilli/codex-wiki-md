# Elastic cavitation

↑ **Parent:** [Finite elasticity](finite-elasticity.md)

The opening of an internal cavity under tensile loading in a solid. For [spherically symmetric deformation](spherically-symmetric-deformation.md) of an [isotropic](isotropy.md) [hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md) with [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md), and an empty cavity without [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md), radial [static elastic equilibrium](static-elastic-equilibrium.md) gives the displayed critical signed external [pressure](pressure-split.md), where $h(\lambda)=W(\lambda^{-2},\lambda,\lambda)$ and positive $P$ denotes compression. Indeed, the [principal stress formula for incompressible hyperelasticity](principal-stress-formula-for-incompressible-hyperelasticity.md) gives $dt_r/d\lambda=h'(\lambda)/(1-\lambda^3)$. Integration between the free inner surface and the outer surface, followed by the vanishing-initial-hole and vanishing-current-cavity limits, gives the formula. For a [neo-Hookean solid](neo-hookean-solid.md) with [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) $\mu>0$, the tensile magnitude is $-P_{\mathrm{crit}}=5\mu/2$.
