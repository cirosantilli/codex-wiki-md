# Superconducting critical current

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md)

A [superconducting critical current](superconducting-critical-current.md) $I_c$ is the limiting [electric current](electric-current.md), for a specified sample, [temperature](temperature-split.md), external [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and measurement criterion, beyond which the chosen state of [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md) no longer sustains dissipation-free transport. A weak connection can limit a loop before the rest of its material does. A [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) generated by the [electric current](electric-current.md) itself also contributes to the sample's conditions. A limit on the initial [superconducting persistent current](persistent-current-superconductivity.md) and a bound on its subsequent [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) answer different experimental questions.
