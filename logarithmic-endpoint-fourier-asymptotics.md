# Logarithmic endpoint Fourier asymptotics

↑ **Parent:** [Watson's lemma](watson-s-lemma.md)

For real $x\to+\infty$, deformation into the upper half-plane separates a logarithmically singular endpoint at zero from the smooth endpoint at one. The first contributes the exact logarithmic term displayed. The second yields its factorial [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) by [Watson's lemma](watson-s-lemma.md). The two endpoints must both be retained; repeated [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) without treating the logarithmic singularity loses its leading contribution.
