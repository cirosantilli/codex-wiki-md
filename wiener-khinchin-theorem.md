# Wiener-Khinchin theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiener–Khinchin_theorem)

The [Wiener-Khinchin theorem](wiener-khinchin-theorem.md) relates the autocorrelation of a [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) to its power spectral density by a [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md). The displayed convention is appropriate when the optical correlation is $\Gamma(\tau)=\langle f^*(t)f(t+\tau)\rangle$ and the analytic field contains $e^{-i\omega t}$. A [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md) of half-width $\kappa$ gives exponential correlation decay $e^{-\kappa|\tau|}$, including the carrier factor $e^{-i\omega\tau}$ when the full field is correlated.
