# Equicorrelation covariance matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md)

This unit-variance [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) has [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $1+(p-1)r$ on the constant-vector direction and [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $1-r$ on its orthogonal complement. It is positive semidefinite exactly when $-1/(p-1)\le r\le1$. For positive $r$, the first [population principal component](population-principal-component.md) is the normalized sum of the centered variables.
