# Display calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Colorimetry](colorimetry.md)

Measurement and adjustment of a display's spectral, [intensity](intensity-physics.md) and temporal output relative to control settings. The emitted primary spectra and their nonlinear control functions matter; RGB numbers alone do not specify retinal stimulation.
