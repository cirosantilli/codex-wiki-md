# Local Rayleigh-number closure

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md)

A local Rayleigh-number closure estimates the thickness $\delta$ of a turbulent [thermal boundary layer](thermal-boundary-layer.md) by setting

$$
\operatorname{Ra}_\delta
=\frac{g\Delta\rho\,\delta^3}{\rho\nu\kappa}
=\operatorname{Ra}_c,
$$

where $\operatorname{Ra}_c$ is an order-one or geometry-dependent critical value. Combining this with [Fourier's law](fourier-s-law.md) converts the boundary-layer temperature and density differences into a [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) law.
