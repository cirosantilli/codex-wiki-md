# Pairwise additivity of soliton shifts

↑ **Parent:** [Soliton time delay](soliton-time-delay.md)

A many-[soliton](soliton.md) spatial shift is pairwise additive when $\Delta x_i=\sum_{j\ne i}\Delta x_{i|j}$. In the all-[kink](scalar-field-kink.md) sector of [Sine-Gordon theory](sine-gordon-theory.md), $\Delta x_{i|j}=-\operatorname{sgn}(v_i-v_j)\log\tanh^2[(\theta_i-\theta_j)/2]/\cosh\theta_i$. Dominant spectator exponentials in the [Sine-Gordon multisoliton tau representation](sine-gordon-multisoliton-tau-representation.md) multiply the effective exponential of [kink](scalar-field-kink.md) $i$, so their logarithms add. This is a classical signature of [factorized scattering](factorized-scattering.md), with no independent many-body contribution to the asymptotic shift.
