The study models false positives and detection recovery to infer [exoplanet occurrence rates](exoplanet-occurrence-rate.md). Section VI and Figure 7 discuss average numbers per star; these must not be confused with the fraction of stars hosting at least one planet. It is used as independent methodological evidence, not an unverified attribution of the exam's figure.
