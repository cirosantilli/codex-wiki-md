# Nitrous oxide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitrous_oxide)

A nitrogen oxide produced substantially by microbial nitrogen cycling on modern [Earth](earth.md). Its atmospheric abundance and lifetime make it a candidate [atmospheric biosignature gas](atmospheric-biosignature-gas.md), with context-dependent abiotic alternatives.
