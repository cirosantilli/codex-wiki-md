<h1 id="kahler-form">Kähler form</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md)

For a [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md) $g$ with [complex structure](complex-structure.md) $J$, the associated real two-form is $\omega(u,v)=g(Ju,v)$. It has type $(1,1)$, is positive in the sense $\omega(v,Jv)=g(v,v)>0$ for $v\ne0$, and is closed. Conversely a real closed positive $(1,1)$ form determines a [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md). A local [geometric Kähler potential](kahler-potential-complex-geometry.md) writes it as $i\partial\bar\partial\varphi$.
