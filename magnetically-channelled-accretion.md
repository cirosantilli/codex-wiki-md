# Magnetically channelled accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion flow](accretion-flow.md)

In magnetically channelled accretion, a strong [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) guides an [accretion flow](accretion-flow.md) along [flux tubes](flux-tube.md). The component of the [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) parallel to the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) vanishes, so the longitudinal flow can be treated hydrodynamically while the field controls the transverse geometry. A [magnetic dipole field](magnetic-dipole-field.md) near a [neutron star](neutron-star.md) channels matter toward small polar caps.
