# Stratospheric aerosol injection

↑ **Parent:** [Climate change](climate-change.md)

Proposed climate intervention that adds material to the [stratosphere](stratosphere.md) to increase reflection of sunlight. Sulfur-dioxide injection relies on conversion into a sufficiently persistent [sulfate](sulfate.md) particle population. A global-mean shortwave forcing target does not uniquely specify regional [temperature](temperature-split.md), rainfall or [ozone](ozone.md) responses.
