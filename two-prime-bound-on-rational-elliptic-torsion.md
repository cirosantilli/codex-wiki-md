# Two-prime bound on rational elliptic torsion

↑ **Parent:** [Reduction of torsion points on an elliptic curve](reduction-of-torsion-points-on-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Let an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) over $\mathbb Q$ have [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) at distinct primes $p,r$, with point counts $N_p,N_r$. For a torsion point, its $\ell$-primary part injects into every reduction whose residue characteristic differs from $\ell$. Consequently its $\ell$-power order has exponent at most $\min(v_\ell(N_p),v_\ell(N_r))$ when $\ell\ne p,r$, at most $v_p(N_r)$ when $\ell=p$, and at most $v_r(N_p)$ when $\ell=r$. This precise form avoids assuming injectivity on residue-characteristic torsion. Counts five at two and seven at three force trivial rational torsion.
