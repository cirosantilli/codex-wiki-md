# Elliptical-bar torsion and first yield

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion stress function for a simply connected bar](torsion-stress-function-for-a-simply-connected-bar.md)

On $x^2/a^2+y^2/b^2<1$, the [torsion stress function for a simply connected bar](torsion-stress-function-for-a-simply-connected-bar.md) is $\phi=\mu\Omega a^2b^2(1-x^2/a^2-y^2/b^2)/(a^2+b^2)$. Its Laplacian is $-2\mu\Omega$ and its boundary value is zero, proving the formula by uniqueness of the [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) with Dirichlet data. Integration gives $M=\pi\mu\Omega a^3b^3/(a^2+b^2)$, hence the displayed stiffness $R=M/\Omega$.

The three [principal stresses](principal-stress.md) are $0,\pm|\nabla\phi|$. With $\tau_Y$ defined as the shear yield stress, the [Tresca yield criterion](tresca-yield-criterion.md) is $|\nabla\phi|\leq\tau_Y$. For $a>b$ its maximum is $2|C|/b$ at $(0,\pm b)$, where $C=\mu\Omega a^2b^2/(a^2+b^2)$. Equating that maximum to $\tau_Y$ gives the displayed first-yield twist. The ends of the minor axis yield first; geometric distance from the bar axis alone does not determine stress in a noncircular section.
