# Random Lipschitz constant in a finite net bound

↑ **Parent:** [Finite net](finite-net.md)

Suppose $|Z(u)-Z(v)|\leq L\|u-v\|$ with a random finite $L$. On $L\leq\ell$, a [finite net](finite-net.md) with radius $\eta$ bounds the full [supremum](supremum.md) by the grid maximum plus $\ell\eta$. The [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) controls the exceptional event using $\mathbb EL$, and a [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) controls the grid maximum. Choosing the net spacing balances these two costs.
