# Polarization drift

↑ **Parent:** [Drift kinetics](drift-kinetics.md)

The inertial correction to the electric drift $\mathbf u_E=c\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/B^2$ in a slowly varying [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). In the simplest uniform-field electrostatic case it is $\mathbf v_{\rm pol}=m_sc^2\partial_t\mathbf E_\perp/(q_sB^2)$. Its species-dependent [current density](current-density.md) supplies the perpendicular polarization response needed in many slow [plasma](plasma-physics.md) instabilities.
