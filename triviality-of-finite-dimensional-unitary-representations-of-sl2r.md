# Triviality of finite-dimensional unitary representations of SL2R

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary representation](unitary-representation.md)

The diagonal element $\operatorname{diag}(m,m^{-1})$ conjugates $U(t)$ to $U(m^2t)=U(t)^{m^2}$. In a finite-dimensional [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md), conjugacy therefore makes its eigenvalue multiset invariant under every square power. [Square-power rigidity of a finite nonzero spectrum](square-power-rigidity-of-a-finite-nonzero-spectrum.md) makes the image of every upper unipotent the identity. Its kernel is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md); [elementary unipotent generators of SL2R](elementary-unipotent-generators-of-sl2r.md) then force the entire [group representation](group-representation.md) to be trivial. This algebraic argument does not require continuity.
