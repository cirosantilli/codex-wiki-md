# n-Selmer group

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer exact sequence of an elliptic curve](kummer-exact-sequence-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

The $n$-Selmer group consists of the classes in $H^1(K,E[n])$ whose restriction at every place lies in the image of the local [Kummer map of an elliptic curve](kummer-map-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). It is finite and contains $E(K)/nE(K)$.
