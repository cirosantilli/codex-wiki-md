# Algebra of sets

↑ **Parent:** [Sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md)

A nonempty collection of subsets of a fixed universe closed under complements and finite unions, hence also finite intersections. It generates a [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) by allowing countable set operations. A [countable generating algebra](countable-generating-algebra.md) provides one simultaneous family of test indicators for measure-theoretic arguments.
