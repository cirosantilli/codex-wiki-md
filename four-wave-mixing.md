# Four-wave mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md)

A third-order [nonlinear optical susceptibility](nonlinear-optical-susceptibility.md) produces a polarization term proportional to $E_1E_2E_3^*$. Its temporal and spatial phases give the displayed output [frequency](frequency.md) and the source [wave vector](wavevector.md) $\mathbf k_1+\mathbf k_2-\mathbf k_3$. Efficient emission also requires that this source match a propagating optical mode. The pump waves supply the generated [energy](energy-split.md).
