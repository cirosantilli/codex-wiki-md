# Cubic counterterm from a Yukawa fermion triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa fermion triangle](yukawa-fermion-triangle.md)

For interactions $-G\bar\psi\psi\Phi-g\bar\psi\psi\phi$, the two [Yukawa fermion triangle](yukawa-fermion-triangle.md) orientations generate a local divergent three-scalar vertex proportional to $mGg^2$. In $d=4-2\epsilon$, with $S-1=i\mathcal M$ convention, its loop pole is $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{div}}=-24mGg^2/(16\pi^2\bar\epsilon)$. A $\Phi\phi^2$ [counterterm](counterterm.md) cancels it. The finite renormalized coefficient is additional data, fixed by a [renormalization condition](renormalization-condition.md). Declaring this cubic absent from the displayed bare interaction is not by itself a prescription for its loop-generated finite value.
