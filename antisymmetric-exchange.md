# Antisymmetric exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Spin model](spin-model-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisymmetric_exchange)

An antisymmetric exchange interaction between [spins](spin.md), proportional to $\mathbf D_{ij}\cdot(\mathbf S_i\times\mathbf S_j)$ with a bond-dependent vector $\mathbf D_{ij}$. The overall sign is conventional and must be specified. It favors a handed twist rather than simple parallel or antiparallel alignment. For a uniform chain term $-D(\mathbf S_j\times\mathbf S_{j+1})_z$, common [spin](spin.md) rotations about $z$ and [time reversal](t-symmetry.md) remain [symmetries](symmetry-physics.md), although reversing the bond orientation changes the sign of $D$.
