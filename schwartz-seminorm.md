# Schwartz seminorm

↑ **Parent:** [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md)

These [seminorms](seminorm.md) measure all polynomially weighted [derivatives](derivative.md) and generate the Fréchet topology of the [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md). Finitely many such bounds control each weighted [derivative](derivative.md)'s integrable norm by adding a spatial decay factor stronger than $\langle x\rangle^{-n}$. [Continuity](continuous-function.md) of the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) follows by applying those bounds after differentiation and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). Equivalent families use powers of $\langle x\rangle$ instead of individual monomials.
