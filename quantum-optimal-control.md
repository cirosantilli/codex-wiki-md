# Quantum optimal control

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal control](optimal-control.md)

[Quantum optimal control](quantum-optimal-control.md) minimizes a state-transfer, observable, or gate error together with resource costs, subject to a controlled [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) or [Lindblad equation](lindblad-equation.md). Forward propagation of the state and backward propagation of a [costate](costate.md) compute the [gradient](gradient.md) efficiently. The optimum depends on the admissible time-dependent fields and laboratory constraints.
