# Substitution (algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)

A substitution replaces a [variable](variable-mathematics.md) or subexpression by another [expression](expression-mathematics.md). In an [equation](equation.md) or [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md), introducing a new [variable](variable-mathematics.md) can expose a simpler form; any division or inverse transformation must retain its domain restrictions and separately check discarded branches.
