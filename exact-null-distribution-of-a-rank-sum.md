# Exact null distribution of a rank sum

↑ **Parent:** [Mann–Whitney U test](mann-whitney-u-test.md)

Under the continuous common-distribution null of the [Wilcoxon rank sum test](mann-whitney-u-test.md), pooled labels are exchangeable. The product records every choice of ranks: choosing $zt^k$ assigns rank $k$ to the first sample. Its indicated coefficient counts subsets of size $m$ and rank sum $w$. Divide by $\binom{m+n}{m}$ and sum the lower tail for the one-sided [P-value](p-value.md). Equivalently the counts satisfy $a_{k,h,w}=a_{k-1,h,w}+a_{k-1,h-1,w-k}$ with $a_{0,0,0}=1$, allowing an exact integer computation.
