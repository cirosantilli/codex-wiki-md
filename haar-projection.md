# Haar projection

↑ **Parent:** [Haar wavelet](haar-wavelet.md)

The [Haar projection](haar-projection.md) at level $J$ is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto the [functions](function-split.md) constant on level-$J$ [dyadic intervals](dyadic-interval.md). It replaces a [function](function-split.md) by its mean on each cell. It can be expressed using level-$J$ [Haar scaling functions](haar-scaling-function.md), or using the constant [function](function-split.md) and [Haar wavelets](haar-wavelet.md) at levels below $J$.
