# Sequence term

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

A [sequence term](sequence-term.md) is an entry of a [sequence](sequence.md) at a specified [sequence index](sequence-index.md). The [sequence index](sequence-index.md) distinguishes the position even when different entries have the same value.
