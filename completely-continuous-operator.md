# Completely continuous operator

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md)

A [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) between [Banach spaces](banach-space-split.md) is completely continuous when it maps weakly convergent sequences to norm-convergent sequences. Equivalently, it maps weakly compact sets to norm-compact sets: the [Eberlein-Šmulian theorem](eberlein-smulian-theorem.md) gives the forward direction, while a weakly convergent sequence together with its limit is weakly compact and gives the reverse direction. The inclusion $C(\mathbb T)\to L^2(\mathbb T)$ for finite [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) is completely continuous by pointwise convergence, the [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md) and the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md), but is not a [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md) because of the orthogonal Fourier modes.
