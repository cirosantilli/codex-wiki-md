# Trace of an idempotent equals its rank

↑ **Parent:** [Projection (linear algebra)](projection-linear-algebra.md)

For a finite-dimensional real or complex [linear projection](projection-linear-algebra.md) $P$, every vector splits as $Px+(I-P)x$ in the [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) and the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), respectively. Their intersection is zero, so a basis adapted to this [direct sum](direct-sum.md) represents $P$ as $\operatorname{diag}(I_r,0)$. Its [trace](matrix-trace.md) and [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md) both equal $r$. In particular, a projection's [determinant](determinant.md) is zero unless it is the identity. Hermiticity is unnecessary for this algebraic identity; a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) projection is additionally orthogonal.
