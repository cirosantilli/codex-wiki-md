# Band curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Band effective mass](band-effective-mass.md)

[Band curvature](band-curvature.md) is the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) of an [energy band](energy-band.md) as a function of [wavevector](wavevector.md). Dividing it by $\hbar^2$ gives the inverse [band effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) tensor. At a one-dimensional band minimum the curvature is positive, whereas at a maximum it is negative. Near a weak two-wave [avoided crossing](avoided-crossing.md) with reciprocal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $G$ and coupling magnitude $U$, the two edge curvatures are $E_\pm''=\hbar^2/m\pm\hbar^4G^2/(4m^2U)$. This is a crystal response coefficient, not a change in the bare [electron mass](electron-mass.md).
