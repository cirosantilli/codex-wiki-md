# Formal viscous inspiral time in Schwarzschild spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-torque inner boundary condition](zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md)

For constant [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu$, use the phenomenological [viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md), [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $h=rc\sqrt{m/(r-3m)}$, and $h_0=\sqrt{12}mc$ at the [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md). Neglect radial kinetic motion in the clock relation and neglect differential light travel time. The [Schwarzschild time](schwarzschild-time.md) to drift formally from $r_1\geq6m$ to $6m$ is the displayed expression, with

$$
\mathcal T(X)=3X^2+6X+6\log(X-1)-8(X-1)^{3/2}-24\sqrt{X-1}+8.
$$

Indeed $\mathcal T(2)=0$ and $\mathcal T'(X)=6X[X-2\sqrt{X-1}]/(X-1)\geq0$ for $X\geq2$. The [zero-torque inner boundary condition](zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) makes the formal drift speed diverge as $r\downarrow6m$, so the nearly circular approximation must give way to the [plunging region of a black-hole accretion disk](plunging-region-of-a-black-hole-accretion-disk.md) sufficiently close to that orbit. This formula describes the stipulated approximation, not an exact relativistic flow solution.
