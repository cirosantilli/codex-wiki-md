# Mixed-normalization spline Gram matrix

↑ **Parent:** [B-spline](b-spline.md)

For partition-normalized [B-splines](b-spline.md) $N_i$ and integral-normalized $M_i=kN_i/(t_{i+k}-t_i)$, let $H_{ij}=(N_i,N_j)$ and $D_{ii}=(t_{i+k}-t_i)/k$. The mixed matrix $G_{ij}=(M_i,N_j)$ equals $D^{-1}H$. It is invertible whenever the splines form a basis and their support widths are positive. It need not be symmetric, unlike the ordinary [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md).
