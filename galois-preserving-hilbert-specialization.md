# Galois-preserving Hilbert specialization

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert's irreducibility theorem](hilbert-s-irreducibility-theorem.md)

For a [Finite Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) $E/K(T)$, take a primitive element and exclude all ramification and denominator problems. A [Hilbert subset](hilbert-subset.md) keeping its minimal [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) irreducible has residue degree $[E:K(T)]$. The residue group is an unramified decomposition subgroup of the generic [Galois group](galois-group.md) with the same order, so it is the whole group. This specializes a regular Galois realization over $K(T)$ to realizations over $K$ when $K$ is [Hilbertian](hilbertian-field.md).
