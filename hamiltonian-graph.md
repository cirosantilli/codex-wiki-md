# Hamiltonian graph

↑ **Parent:** [Hamilton cycle](hamilton-cycle.md)

A [Hamiltonian graph](hamiltonian-graph.md) is a [graph](graph-split.md) containing a [Hamilton cycle](hamilton-cycle.md). This describes existence of a spanning [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md), rather than requiring every [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) of the [graph](graph-split.md) to belong to that [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md). In the finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md) convention, $K_n$ is a [Hamiltonian graph](hamiltonian-graph.md) for $n\geq3$, because any ordering of its [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) closes using available [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md).
