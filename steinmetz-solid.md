# Steinmetz solid

↑ **Parent:** [Solid geometry](solid-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinmetz_solid)

The common region inside equal-radius [circular cylinders](circular-cylinder.md) whose axes meet orthogonally. Two cylinders give a [bicylinder](bicylinder.md); three give a [tricylinder](tricylinder.md).
