# Waiting-time distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md)

The [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) of a nonnegative duration until an event. For a continuous duration with [survival function](survival-function.md) $S(t)=P(T>t)$, its [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is $p(t)=-S'(t)$. A constant hazard gives an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md); successive independent first-order waits give a convolution, as in the [two-step catalytic waiting time](two-step-catalytic-waiting-time.md).
