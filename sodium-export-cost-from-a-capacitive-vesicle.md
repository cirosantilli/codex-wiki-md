# Sodium export cost from a capacitive vesicle

↑ **Parent:** [Sodium-motive force](sodium-motive-force.md)

Start at sodium electrochemical equilibrium with $n_0$ internal ions. Exporting $n$ ions changes the [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) by $-ne/C$ and the internal number density to $(n_0-n)/V$. Subtracting the initially balanced chemical and electrical contributions gives the displayed incremental cost. An initial zero potential further implies $n_0=c_oV$ for an ideal fixed external reservoir. Nonzero initial equilibrium potential permits unequal concentrations, so that latter equality is an additional assumption.
