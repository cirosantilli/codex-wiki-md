# Lang map

↑ **Parent:** [Lang theorem for algebraic groups](lang-theorem-for-algebraic-groups.md)

For the [Lang map](lang-map.md), $\mathcal L_F(gh)=h^{-1}\mathcal L_F(g)F(h)$. This twisted right-translation identity determines which subgroups act on its inverse images. Fibres are left $G^F$-cosets, by the [Lang theorem for algebraic groups](lang-theorem-for-algebraic-groups.md).
