# Stagnation enthalpy

↑ **Parent:** [Enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md)

The sum of specific [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) and macroscopic [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) per unit [mass](mass.md). In a steady adiabatic flow without shaft work, its change is determined by the change in external [potential energy](potential-energy.md). At essentially constant external [potential energy](potential-energy.md), it is conserved through a stationary nonradiating [shock wave](shock-wave.md) even though [entropy](entropy-split.md) increases.
