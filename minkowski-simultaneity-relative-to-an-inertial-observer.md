# Minkowski simultaneity relative to an inertial observer

↑ **Parent:** [Relativity of simultaneity](relativity-of-simultaneity.md)

In [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) of [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, let $u$ be a constant future-directed [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) with $\eta(u,u)=-c^2$. The time difference assigned to two [spacetime events](event-relativity.md) is $\Delta t_u=-\eta(u,Q-P)/c^2$. Thus the observer's simultaneous events form an affine [hyperplane](hyperplane.md) orthogonal to $u$. This is an invariant relation of the three arguments $u,P,Q$; the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) alone does not single out one $u$. For fixed $u$, it defines an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) and the hyperplanes form a [foliation](foliation.md). Changing $u$ changes this [foliation](foliation.md).
