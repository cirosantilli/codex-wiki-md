# Rational Dehn surgery

↑ **Parent:** [Dehn filling](dehn-filling.md)

For a null-homologous [knot](knot.md), choose a [meridian of a knot](meridian-of-a-knot.md) $\mu$ and [Seifert longitude](seifert-longitude.md) $\lambda$. Rational surgery with relatively prime numerator $p$ and denominator $q$ fills the [knot exterior](knot-exterior.md) along $p\mu+q\lambda$. A negative [continued fraction](continued-fraction.md) for $p/q$ replaces this operation by integer surgery on a chain of meridians, using [slam-dunk moves](slam-dunk-move.md).
