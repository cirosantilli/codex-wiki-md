# Open strong-decay thresholds for baryons

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)

A [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md) decay must conserve quark flavour and have final rest energy below the initial [mass](mass.md). An excited light baryon can often decay to a lighter baryon plus a [pion](pion.md). A ground-state strange baryon may lack any such open flavour-conserving channel even when its spin permits one. In particular, the [Omega baryon](omega-baryon.md) cannot decay strongly to a [Xi baryon](xi-baryon.md) plus a [pion](pion.md) because that changes [strangeness](strangeness.md); its flavour-conserving Xi-kaon channel is closed by the mass threshold. A spin-three-half assignment alone does not imply a strong decay.
