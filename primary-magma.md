# Primary magma

↑ **Parent:** [Magma](magma.md)

A melt composition in equilibrium with its source before substantial post-segregation [fractional crystallization](fractional-crystallization.md), mixing or crustal contamination. Reconstructing it from an erupted [basalt](basalt.md) commonly requires restoring lost crystals and checking mineral–melt equilibrium. A crystal-rich erupted rock is not automatically a high-temperature primary liquid merely because its bulk Mg content is high.
