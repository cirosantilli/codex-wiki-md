# Hilbert-space construction of dominated measure densities

↑ **Parent:** [Radon-Nikodym theorem](radon-nikodym-theorem.md)

For finite [positive measures](positive-measure.md) $\mu,\nu$, put $\rho=\mu+\nu$. The [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $g\mapsto\int g\,d\nu$ is bounded on real $L^2(\rho)$ by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). The [Riesz representation theorem](riesz-representation-theorem.md) gives a measurable $h$ with $\nu(A)=\int_Ah\,d\rho$. Testing indicators of its negative and greater-than-one level sets shows $0\le h\le1$ almost everywhere. Subtraction gives $\mu(A)=\int_A(1-h)\,d\rho$. This constructs the densities without assuming the [Radon-Nikodym theorem](radon-nikodym-theorem.md) as a prior result.
