# Hartree energy

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic units](atomic-units.md)

The atomic unit of energy, $E_h=\hbar^2/(m_ea_0^2)$, approximately $27.2$ eV. The hydrogen-atom ground-state energy is $-E_h/2$.
