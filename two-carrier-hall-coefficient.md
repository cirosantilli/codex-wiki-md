# Two-carrier Hall coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md)

For isotropic [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md) with positive mobilities, adding their weak-field [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) tensors and imposing zero transverse total current gives

$$
R_H=\frac{p\mu_h^2-n\mu_e^2}{e(p\mu_h+n\mu_e)^2},\qquad
\sigma=e(n\mu_e+p\mu_h).
$$

The [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md) changes sign at $p\mu_h^2=n\mu_e^2$. Carrier abundance alone does not fix the sign; mobilities enter quadratically. The formula assumes $\mu_e B\ll1$, $\mu_h B\ll1$, independent semiclassical carriers, and no anomalous Hall contribution.
