# Cuban prime

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number-split.md)

A [prime number](prime-number-split.md) expressible as $(x^3-y^3)/(x-y)=x^2+xy+y^2$ for specified consecutive or nearly consecutive [positive integers](positive-integer.md). The first kind has $x=y+1$ and therefore the form $3y^2+3y+1$; the second kind has $x=y+2$ and therefore the form $3y^2+6y+4$. The first kind arises when a [difference of two cubes](difference-of-two-cubes.md) is itself [prime](prime-number-split.md): the factorization $x^3-y^3=(x-y)(x^2+xy+y^2)$ forces $x-y=1$ for $x>y>0$.
