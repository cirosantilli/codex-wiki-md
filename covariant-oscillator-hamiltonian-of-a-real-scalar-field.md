# Covariant oscillator Hamiltonian of a real scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical quantization of a real scalar field](canonical-quantization-of-a-real-scalar-field.md)

With $d\Pi_p=d^3p/[(2\pi)^3 2E_p]$, a free [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) has [mode expansion of a free field](mode-expansion-of-a-free-field.md) $\phi=\int d\Pi_p(ae^{-ipx}+a^\dagger e^{ipx})$ and [commutator](commutator.md) $[a(p),a^\dagger(q)]=(2\pi)^3 2E_p\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$. Substitution into the quadratic [Hamiltonian density](hamiltonian-density.md) gives $H=\frac12\int d\Pi_p E_p(a^\dagger a+aa^\dagger)$. The terms with two [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md) or two [creation operators](creation-operator.md) cancel by $E_p^2=\mathbf p^2+m^2$. [Normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) removes the constant [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md). The [commutators](commutator.md) $[H,a(p)]=-E_pa(p)$ and $[H,a^\dagger(p)]=E_pa^\dagger(p)$ give the [boson](boson.md) energy without changing under this subtraction. Replacing $a(p)$ by $\sqrt{2E_p}\,c(p)$ recovers the noncovariant normalization $[c(p),c^\dagger(q)]=(2\pi)^3\delta^3(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$.
