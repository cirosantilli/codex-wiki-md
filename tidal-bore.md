# Tidal bore

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_bore)

An incoming tide can form a moving surge that propagates upstream against a river's current. A discontinuous idealization is a [shock wave](shock-wave.md) of the [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md), while weaker or dispersive bores can have an undular wave train. A boundary overtaking upstream-propagating [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) warns that a smoothly attached quasi-steady description may fail; the appropriate discontinuity must satisfy the conservative jump conditions of the chosen model.
