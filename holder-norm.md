<h1 id="holder-norm">Hölder norm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder space](holder-space.md)

The full norm is $|u|_{k,\alpha;U}=|u|_{k;U}+[D^k u]_{\alpha;U}$, combining the [derivative supremum norm](derivative-supremum-norm.md) with the top-derivative [Hölder seminorm](holder-seminorm.md). At order zero it is the sum of the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) and the [Hölder seminorm](holder-seminorm.md).
