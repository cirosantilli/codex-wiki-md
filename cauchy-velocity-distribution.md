# Cauchy velocity distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Collisionless Jeans dispersion relation](collisionless-jeans-dispersion-relation.md)

The isotropic three-dimensional [Cauchy velocity distribution](cauchy-velocity-distribution.md) $f_0=\rho_0b/[\pi^2(v^2+b^2)^2]$, $b>0$, has density $\rho_0$ and one-dimensional marginal $F(u)=\rho_0b/[\pi(u^2+b^2)]$. Its second [velocity](velocity.md) moment diverges, so $b$ is a scale rather than a finite [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md). At zero phase [speed](speed.md), $\int F'(u)/u\,du=-\rho_0/b^2$, giving $k_J=\sqrt{4\pi G\rho_0}/b$. In the upper-half frequency [plane](plane.md), the marginal response is $\rho_0/(\omega/k+ib)^2$; the growing branch has $\omega=i(\sqrt{4\pi G\rho_0}-kb)$ for $0<k<k_J$.
