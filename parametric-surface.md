# Parametric surface

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth surface](smooth-surface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parametric_surface)

A [parametric surface](parametric-surface.md) is represented by a map $(u,v)\mapsto P(u,v)$. A regular patch has $P_u\times P_v\ne0$; this [cross product](cross-product.md) supplies its [normal vector](normal-vector.md).
