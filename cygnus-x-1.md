# Cygnus X-1

↑ **Parent:** [Wind-fed black-hole binary](wind-fed-black-hole-binary.md)

A stellar black-hole binary with a massive stellar companion. A 2011 dynamical analysis combined optical photometry and spectroscopy with a [distance](distance.md) constraint and inferred $14.8\pm1.0$ [solar masses](solar-mass.md) for the compact object. This is that study's historical estimate, not a claim about the latest measurement.[abstract and dynamical-model constraints](the-mass-of-the-black-hole-in-cygnus-x-1-by-jerome-a-orosz-and-others.md)
