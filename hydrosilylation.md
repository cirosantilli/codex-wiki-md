# Hydrosilylation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

Addition of Si–H across an unsaturated bond. For an [alkene](alkene.md), the net products acquire one C–H and one C–Si bond. Alternative catalytic mechanisms can insert the [alkene](alkene.md) into either a [metal](metal.md)–[hydride](hydride.md) or a [metal](metal.md)–silyl bond; these alternatives should not be conflated.
