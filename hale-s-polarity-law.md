<h1 id="hale-s-polarity-law">Hale's polarity law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solar cycle](solar-cycle.md)

Bipolar solar active regions preferentially have one leading magnetic polarity in a given hemisphere and cycle, the opposite leading polarity in the other hemisphere, and reversed assignments in the next cycle. These statistical polarity rules reveal the ordered toroidal component of the [solar magnetic field](solar-magnetic-field.md). Individual active regions can violate the preferred pattern, so the law is a statistical regularity rather than an exact rule for every spot pair.
