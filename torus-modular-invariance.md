# Torus modular invariance

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet moduli](worldsheet-moduli.md)

The torus worldsheet path integral is invariant under the [modular group](modular-group.md) acting on its complex parameter $\tau$. Integrating over the [standard fundamental domain of the modular group](standard-fundamental-domain-of-the-modular-group.md) counts each inequivalent torus once and excludes the short-proper-time ultraviolet region.
