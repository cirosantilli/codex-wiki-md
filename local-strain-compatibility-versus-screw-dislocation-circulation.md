# Local strain compatibility versus screw-dislocation circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Screw dislocation](screw-dislocation.md)

The antiplane field $w=b\arg(x+iy)/(2\pi)$ has a smooth local [gradient](gradient.md) on the [punctured plane](punctured-complex-plane.md) and satisfies the differential [strain](strain.md) compatibility condition there. Its [circulation](circulation-physics.md) around the defect axis is nevertheless $b\ne0$, excluding a globally single-valued [displacement](displacement.md) on that domain. A radial cut permits a branch with an axial jump $b$ between the two cut faces; joining faces with that mismatch is the Volterra construction of a [screw dislocation](screw-dislocation.md) with [Burgers vector](burgers-vector.md) parallel to the axis. The [stress](stress.md) has a singular core where [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) requires microscopic or plastic regularization. This example separates a local differential integrability test from global closure around a hole.
