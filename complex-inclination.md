# Complex inclination

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Lagrange secular theory](laplace-lagrange-secular-theory.md)

Complex inclination packages small [orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md) $I$ and [longitude of ascending node](longitude-of-ascending-node.md) $\Omega$. It differs from [complex eccentricity](complex-eccentricity.md). Linear [Laplace-Lagrange secular theory](laplace-lagrange-secular-theory.md) gives $\dot{\mathbf y}=iB\mathbf y$, whose [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) solution decomposes into nodal [secular eigenmodes](secular-eigenmode.md).
