# Poincare inequality for a reversible Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet form of a Markov chain](dirichlet-form-of-a-markov-chain.md)

For a finite [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md) with at least two states satisfying [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md), its [Dirichlet form of a Markov chain](dirichlet-form-of-a-markov-chain.md) obeys

$$
\gamma=1-\lambda_2=\inf_{\operatorname{Var}_\pi(f)>0}\frac{\mathcal E(f,f)}{\operatorname{Var}_\pi(f)}.
$$

Expand $f-\pi(f)$ in an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). The denominator is $\sum_{j\geq2}a_j^2$, and the numerator is $\sum_{j\geq2}(1-\lambda_j)a_j^2$. Their ratio is at least $1-\lambda_2$ and equality holds for the second [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md). Thus the optimal [Poincare inequality for a reversible Markov chain](poincare-inequality-for-a-reversible-markov-chain.md) constant is $1/\gamma$, for the ordinary rather than absolute [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md).
