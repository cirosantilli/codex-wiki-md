# hunchback messenger RNA

↑ **Parent:** [Messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md)

A [Drosophila](drosophila.md) transcript with distinct maternal and zygotic contributions. [Nanos](nanos.md)-dependent regulation of the maternal [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) involves response elements in its [3′ untranslated region](3%E2%80%B2-untranslated-region.md) and shapes the posterior [Hunchback](hunchback.md) [protein](protein-split.md) distribution.
