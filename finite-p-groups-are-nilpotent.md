# Finite p-groups are nilpotent

↑ **Parent:** [Finite p-group](finite-p-group.md)

Induct on the order of the finite [p-group](p-group.md). Its [center of a group](center-of-a-group.md) is nontrivial unless the group itself is trivial. Quotient by the center to obtain a smaller finite p-group and lift a [central series](central-series.md) supplied by induction. Prepending the center gives a central series for the original group: the first term is central, and the remaining commutator-containment conditions are exactly the lifted quotient conditions. Hence every finite p-group is a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md). Since central factors are Abelian, every nilpotent group is also a [soluble group](solvable-group.md).
