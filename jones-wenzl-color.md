# Jones-Wenzl color

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced Temperley-Lieb skein theory](reduced-temperley-lieb-skein-theory.md)

A surviving color denotes $j$ parallel framed strands equipped with the [Jones-Wenzl idempotent](jones-wenzl-idempotent.md) $f_j$. Its closed trace is its [quantum dimension](quantum-dimension.md). The [semisimplicity of the reduced Temperley-Lieb category](semisimplicity-of-the-reduced-temperley-lieb-category.md) identifies these colors with the simple objects; their [tensor products](tensor-product.md) decompose according to [fusion of Jones-Wenzl colors](fusion-of-jones-wenzl-colors.md).
