# Planck constant

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planck_constant)

The reduced Planck constant is $\hbar=h/(2\pi)$ and sets the scale of quantum commutators and phases.
