# Volume of a Euclidean unit sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md)

The $(n-1)$-dimensional [volume](volume.md) of the Euclidean unit sphere equals $n$ times the $n$-dimensional volume of its unit ball. If $R=\sum_i x^i\partial_i$ is the radial vector field and $\omega=dx^1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx^n$, then $d(\iota_R\omega)=n\omega$, and the [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md) proves the formula.
