# Fixed-pressure planar Darcy displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy law](darcy-law.md)

For two incompressible fluids separated by a planar material front $X(t)$, the [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) is uniform and the pressure drop sums the resistances of the two layers: $U=k\Delta P/[\mu X+\mu_h(L-X)]$. The front moves at the [pore velocity](pore-velocity.md) $X'=U/\phi$. Integrating from $X(0)=0$ yields the displayed relation. The formula holds up to breakthrough $X=L$, with either sign of the viscosity difference; equal viscosities give constant speed.
