# Twistor Hermitian form

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor space](twistor-space.md)

For $Z=(\omega,\pi)$ and $W=(\eta,\rho)$ the displayed [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md) has signature $(2,2)$. It is zero on every pair of vectors in $W_x=\{(ix\pi,\pi)\}$ when $x$ is Hermitian, that is, Lorentzian real. Conversely a transverse totally isotropic two-plane is such a graph with $x$ Hermitian. This gives the [Lorentzian reality structure on twistor space](lorentzian-reality-structure-on-twistor-space.md).
