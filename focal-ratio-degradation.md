# Focal-ratio degradation

↑ **Parent:** [Optical fiber](optical-fiber.md)

Focal-ratio degradation is the broadening of the output angular cone of an [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md) relative to its input cone. It can make a spectrograph collimator lose transmitted light or require larger optics. Stress, bends, and mode mixing in the fiber can contribute.
