# Rotational spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotational_spectroscopy)

[Rotational spectroscopy](rotational-spectroscopy.md) probes transitions between [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md) [quantum states](quantum-state.md). In the simple [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md), line spacing determines a [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md), while an [isotope shift of a linear rotor](isotope-shift-of-a-linear-rotor.md) tests the mass dependence. More general [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md) requires several principal [moments of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) and more complicated assignments. The combination makes spectra sensitive fingerprints of both composition and shape.
