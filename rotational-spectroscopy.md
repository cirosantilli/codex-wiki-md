# Rotational spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotational_spectroscopy)

[Rotational spectroscopy](rotational-spectroscopy.md) measures transitions between [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md) [quantum states](quantum-state.md), usually in low-pressure gaseous [molecules](molecule-split.md). The measured [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) become a molecular fingerprint only after [rotational spectral line assignment](rotational-spectral-line-assignment.md): identify which transitions belong together, determine their [quantum numbers](quantum-number.md), fit a rotational [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), and test additional predictions. Line positions constrain [moments of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md), while [rotational state populations](rotational-state-populations.md) and transition coupling determine which lines can be detected.

The simplest ordinary microwave absorption ladder belongs to a polar [linear molecule](linear-molecule.md) obeying [pure rotational electric-dipole selection rules](pure-rotational-electric-dipole-selection-rules.md). A [homonuclear diatomic molecule](homonuclear-diatomic-molecule.md) has no permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) in its symmetric internal state, but [rotational Raman spectroscopy](rotational-raman-spectroscopy.md) can probe its rotation through anisotropic [electric polarizability](electric-polarizability.md). The coupling mechanism must therefore be specified before interpreting a missing spectrum.

The existing [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md), [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md), [isotope shift of a linear rotor](isotope-shift-of-a-linear-rotor.md), and [centrifugal distortion](centrifugal-distortion.md) explain the forward model. [Molecular structure from rotational constants](molecular-structure-from-rotational-constants.md) asks the reverse question: which arrangements of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) could produce the fitted constants? The [NIST trace-gas FTMW validation experiment](nist-trace-gas-ftmw-validation-experiment.md) makes the measurement chain concrete and separates identifying a species from measuring its [concentration](concentration.md).
