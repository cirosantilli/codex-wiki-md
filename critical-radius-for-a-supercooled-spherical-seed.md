# Critical radius for a supercooled spherical seed

↑ **Parent:** [Quasistatic spherical Stefan problem with capillarity](quasistatic-spherical-stefan-problem-with-capillarity.md)

The [Gibbs--Thomson relation](gibbs-thomson-relation.md) depresses a spherical interface's equilibrium temperature by $\Gamma/a$. For $a>a_c$, its interface is warmer than the supercooled ambient and heat loss drives growth. For $a<a_c$, its interface is colder than the ambient and heat gain drives melting. The equilibrium radius is unstable because the derivative of the quasistatic radius velocity at $a_c$ is positive.
