# Parametric plane curve

↑ **Parent:** [Parametric curve](parametric-curve.md)

A [parametric curve](parametric-curve.md) in the plane, given by two coordinate functions $x(t),y(t)$. Its speed is $\sqrt{x'(t)^2+y'(t)^2}$ wherever these derivatives exist, giving its [arc length](arc-length.md) by [integration](integral-split.md).
