# Univariate polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

A [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) in one [indeterminate](indeterminate.md); its [coefficients](coefficient.md) lie in a specified [ring](ring.md). For example, $3x^2+1$ is univariate, whereas $x+y$ involves two [indeterminates](indeterminate.md).
