<h1 id="finite-levy-collapse-to-omega-one">Finite Lévy collapse to omega-one</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-condition Lévy collapse](finite-condition-levy-collapse.md)

With $\kappa$ an uncountable [regular cardinal](regular-cardinal.md), finite [partial functions](partial-function.md) on $\kappa\times\omega$ assigning a value below the first coordinate collapse every ground [ordinal](ordinal.md) below $\kappa$ to countable size. The [Delta-system lemma](delta-system-lemma.md) gives the $\kappa$ [chain in a partial order](chain-in-a-partial-order.md) condition; the [possible-values lemma for chain-condition forcing](possible-values-lemma-for-chain-condition-forcing.md) then preserves the regularity of $\kappa$. Therefore $\kappa$ becomes the extension $\omega_1$. Strong inaccessibility is enough but is not needed for this identification.
