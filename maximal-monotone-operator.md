# Maximal monotone operator

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone operator](monotone-operator.md)

A [monotone operator](monotone-operator.md) is maximal when its graph has no proper enlargement that is still monotone. In Euclidean or Hilbert space, its [resolvent of a monotone operator](resolvent-of-a-monotone-operator.md) is single-valued and everywhere defined for every positive step. Subdifferentials of proper [lower semicontinuous](lower-semicontinuity.md) [convex functions](convex-function.md) are fundamental examples.
