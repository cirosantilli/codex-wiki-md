# Upper-truncated Poisson distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md)

A [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) of parameter $a$ conditioned to lie in $\{0,\ldots,C\}$. It is the occupancy law of a unit-resource [loss network](loss-network.md). Its mean is the [carried load of an Erlang loss resource](carried-load-of-an-erlang-loss-resource.md), $a[1-E(C,a)]$. Differentiating the mean with respect to $\log a$ gives its [variance](variance-split.md), which is positive for $a>0$ and $C\geq1$; this proves strict monotonicity of the carried load.
