# Nonaffineness of a punctured affine plane

↑ **Parent:** [Punctured affine plane](punctured-affine-plane.md)

The [punctured affine plane](punctured-affine-plane.md) $U=\mathbb A_k^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}$ is not an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md). On its cover $D(x)\cup D(y)$, [global sections](global-section.md) are $k[x,y]_x\cap k[x,y]_y=k[x,y]$ inside the [field of fractions](field-of-fractions.md). Coprimality of $x$ and $y$ proves this equality. If $U$ were affine, its inclusion into $\mathbb A_k^2$ would be an isomorphism by [affine scheme reconstruction from global sections](affine-scheme-reconstruction-from-global-sections.md), contradicting the omitted point.
