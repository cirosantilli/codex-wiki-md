# Band-gap extraction from intrinsic carrier activation

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic carrier concentration](intrinsic-carrier-concentration.md)

For fixed [band effective masses](band-effective-mass.md) and [band gap](band-gap.md) $E_g$, the [intrinsic carrier concentration](intrinsic-carrier-concentration.md) obeys $n_i=C T^{3/2}e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}$. Thus

$$
\frac{d\ln(n_i/T^{3/2})}{d(1/T)}=-\frac{E_g}{2k_B}.
$$

A base-ten plot against $x=1000\,\mathrm K/T$ instead has slope $-E_g/(2000\,k_B\mathrm K\ln10)$ after the prefactor correction. The intrinsic branch must be used, rather than the extrinsic plateau or [dopant freeze-out](dopant-freeze-out.md) branch. A changing gap or effective mass makes this an activation-energy estimate, and a small gap uncertainty can produce a much larger multiplicative uncertainty in a room-[temperature](temperature-split.md) minority density.
