# Commuting coefficients of a Poisson pencil Casimir

↑ **Parent:** [Lenard-Magri recursion](lenard-magri-recursion.md)

For a [Lenard-Magri recursion](lenard-magri-recursion.md), antisymmetry shifts an index: $\{H_i,H_j\}_1=\{H_{i-1},H_{j+1}\}_1$ when $i\geq1$. Repeating reaches $\{H_0,H_{i+j}\}_1=0$, because $H_0$ is a [Casimir function](casimir-function-of-a-poisson-manifold.md). The recursion then gives $\{H_i,H_j\}_2=-\{H_{i+1},H_j\}_1=0$. This proof applies to every finite initial portion for the indices that can be shifted inside it, and to all pairs in an infinite sequence.
