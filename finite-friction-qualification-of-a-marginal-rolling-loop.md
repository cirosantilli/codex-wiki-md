# Finite-friction qualification of a marginal rolling loop

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling-cylinder loop contact threshold](rolling-cylinder-loop-contact-threshold.md)

At the limiting release height for a solid cylinder, the loop [normal force](normal-force.md) is $N=(7mg/3)(1+\cos\theta)$ and the required static-friction magnitude is $mg|\sin\theta|/3$. Thus $|f|/N=|\sin\theta|/[7(1+\cos\theta)]$ diverges near the top, even though both [forces](force-split.md) vanish there. A finite [friction](friction.md) coefficient therefore requires a height above the ideal contact threshold to maintain no-slip rolling throughout. A question imposing rolling but giving no coefficient determines the ideal limiting bound, not a realistic friction-dependent minimum.
