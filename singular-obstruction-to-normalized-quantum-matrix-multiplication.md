# Singular obstruction to normalized quantum matrix multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum spectral filtering](quantum-spectral-filtering.md)

If $|b\rangle$ lies in the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $A$, then $A|b\rangle=0$ has no normalized [quantum state](quantum-state.md). A request to produce that normalized vector with positive [probability](probability.md) is consequently undefined. For example, $A=\operatorname{diag}(0,1/2)$ on one [qubit](qubit.md) has distinct dyadic [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), but annihilates $|0\rangle$. The corrected filtering assertion requires $A|b\rangle\neq0$; it cannot be repaired by assigning a positive success probability to this input.
