# Photoionization

↑ **Parent:** [Ionization](ionization.md)

[Photoionization](photoionization.md) removes a bound electron when an atom or ion absorbs a sufficiently energetic photon. The threshold is its [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md). The excess photon energy becomes kinetic energy of the products; subsequent collisions can heat the gas or cause secondary ionizations. A [photoionization](photoionization.md) rate and a [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md) are distinct concepts: equilibrium also requires specified recombination and other reaction rates.
