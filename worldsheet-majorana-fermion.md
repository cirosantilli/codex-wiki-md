# Worldsheet Majorana fermion

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md)

Each real chiral fermion has [conformal weight](conformal-weight.md) $1/2$ and contributes $1/2$ to the [central charge](central-charge.md). The [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md) and [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md) differ by periodic versus antiperiodic boundary conditions.
