# Surface boundary condition for a power-law-opacity grey atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Grey-atmosphere pressure with power-law opacity](grey-atmosphere-pressure-with-power-law-opacity.md)

For a [plane-parallel atmosphere](plane-parallel-atmosphere.md) in [radiative equilibrium](radiative-equilibrium.md), constant [surface gravity of a star](surface-gravity-of-a-star.md) $g$ and [opacity](opacity.md) $\kappa=\kappa_0P^{\alpha-1}T^{4-4\beta}$, match the [stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md) interior where the [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) satisfies $F=\sigma T^4$. The [grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md) law then puts the surface at [optical depth](optical-depth.md) $\tau=2/3$ with $T^4=2T_0^4$. Integrating [hydrostatic equilibrium in optical depth](hydrostatic-equilibrium-in-optical-depth.md) from $P(0)=0$ gives $P^\alpha=2\alpha g(T^{4\beta}-T_0^{4\beta})/(3\beta\kappa_0T_0^4)$. Multiplication by $\kappa_0T^{4-4\beta}/g$ proves the displayed boundary condition for $\alpha>0$, $\beta\ne0$. At $\beta=0$ the integral is logarithmic and the continuous [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) is $P\kappa/g=4\alpha\log2/3$. The relation assumes negligible [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) and describes the [Eddington closure approximation](eddington-closure-approximation.md), rather than exact angular transfer.
