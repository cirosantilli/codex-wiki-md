# Asymptotic mean integrated squared error

↑ **Parent:** [Integrated mean squared error](integrated-mean-squared-error.md)

For a second-order [kernel density estimator](kernel-density-estimation.md), write $R(q)=\int q^2$ and $\mu_2(K)=\int u^2K(u)\,du$. Under the usual smoothness and shrinking-bandwidth assumptions, the leading [mean integrated squared error](integrated-mean-squared-error.md) is the displayed sum of integrated [variance](variance-split.md) and squared [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md). Its minimizer is $h=[R(K)/(n\mu_2(K)^2R(f''))]^{1/5}$, giving order $n^{-4/5}$. An added $O(n^{-1})$ integrated covariance term is lower order at this bandwidth and does not change its leading constant.
