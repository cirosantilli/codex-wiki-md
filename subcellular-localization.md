# Subcellular localization

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)

[subcellular localization](subcellular-localization.md) is the distribution of a component among cellular regions or compartments. Changing localization can change a [protein](protein-split.md)'s partners even when its total abundance is constant.
