# Section of a vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

This is a [section of a fiber bundle](section-fiber-bundle.md) whose fibers are [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md).

A section of a vector bundle $\pi:E\to B$ is a map $s:B\to E$ satisfying $\pi\circ s=\operatorname{id}_B$.
