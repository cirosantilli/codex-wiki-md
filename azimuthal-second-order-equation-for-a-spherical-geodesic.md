# Azimuthal second-order equation for a spherical geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical-geodesic first integral](spherical-geodesic-first-integral.md)

Differentiating the [spherical-geodesic first integral](spherical-geodesic-first-integral.md) gives

$$
\theta''+\sin\theta\cos\theta-
\frac{2\sin^3\theta\cos\theta}{a^2h^2}=0,
\qquad{}'=d/d\phi.
$$

The same equation follows from the affine [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) after eliminating the arc-length derivatives. The squared first-derivative equation also fixes the normalization constant; a second-order solution must retain that first integral.
