# Allostery

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[allostery](allostery.md) is coupling in which a perturbation at one region of a [protein](protein-split.md) changes a functional property elsewhere. It can act through altered [conformational ensemble](conformational-ensemble.md) populations and need not require a large change in the mean structure.
