# Lorentz-force equation from the worldline action

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic charged-particle action](relativistic-charged-particle-action.md)

Varying the [relativistic charged-particle action](relativistic-charged-particle-action.md) with fixed endpoints gives

$$
m\frac{du_\mu}{d\tau}=qF_{\mu\nu}u^\nu,
$$

or the equivalent raised-index equation.
