# Completion preserves ramification and residue data

↑ **Parent:** [Completion of a number field at a prime ideal](completion-of-a-number-field-at-a-prime-ideal.md)

For a prime $\mathfrak q$ of a finite separable extension $L/K$ above $\mathfrak p$, completion preserves the [ramification index](ramification-index.md) and [residue-field extension](residue-field-extension.md):

$$
e(L_{\mathfrak q}/K_{\mathfrak p})=e_{\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p},
\qquad
k_{L_{\mathfrak q}}=k_{\mathfrak q}.
$$
