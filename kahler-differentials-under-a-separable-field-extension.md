<h1 id="kahler-differentials-under-a-separable-field-extension">Kähler differentials under a separable field extension</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler differential](kahler-differential.md)

For finite separable $L/F$, every $k$-[derivation](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) on $F$ into an $L$-module extends uniquely to $L$. Differentiate the minimal polynomial of each algebraic generator and divide by its nonzero derivative. Consequently the [Transitivity exact sequence for Kähler differentials](transitivity-exact-sequence-for-kahler-differentials.md) identifies $\Omega_{L/k}$ with $L\otimes_F\Omega_{F/k}$, and $\Omega_{L/F}=0$.
