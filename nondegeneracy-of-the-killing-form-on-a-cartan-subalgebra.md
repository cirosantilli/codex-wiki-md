# Nondegeneracy of the Killing form on a Cartan subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md)

For a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), the [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) is orthogonal under the [Killing form](killing-form.md) to every nonzero [root space](root-space.md). A vector in the Cartan subalgebra orthogonal to that whole subalgebra is therefore orthogonal to the entire Lie algebra and must vanish. On the Cartan subalgebra the form is $\kappa(h,h')=\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi}\alpha(h)\alpha(h')$.
