# Massive two-form field

↑ **Parent:** [Classical field theory](classical-field-theory.md)

An antisymmetric tensor $A_{mn}$ with $F=dA$ obeys $\partial^mF_{mnp}=\mu^2A_{np}$ for $\mu\ne0$. Taking a divergence yields $\partial^mA_{mn}=0$, and the remaining equation is the [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) for each component. The physical [particle polarizations](particle-polarization.md) form the [exterior square](exterior-square.md) of the massive [little group](little-group.md) vector representation, of dimension $(D-1)(D-2)/2$. Unlike a [two-form gauge field](two-form-gauge-field.md), it has physical longitudinal polarizations.
