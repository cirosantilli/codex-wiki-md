# Variable projection

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted least squares](weighted-least-squares.md)

In [variable projection](variable-projection.md), a prediction has the form $\mathbf G(\mathbf q)\mathbf m$, nonlinear in $\mathbf q$ but [linear](linearity.md) in $\mathbf m$. For each trial $\mathbf q$, solve the inner [weighted least squares](weighted-least-squares.md) problem for $\mathbf m$, then optimize the resulting residual over $\mathbf q$. This reduces the nonlinear dimension without pretending that the whole [inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md) is [linear](linearity.md).
