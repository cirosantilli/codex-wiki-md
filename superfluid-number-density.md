# Superfluid number density

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluidity](superfluidity.md)

The particle-number density associated with the [superfluid](superfluid.md) response. In two dimensions it is a number per unit area and gives the [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md) $\rho_s=\hbar^2n_s/m$ for particles of mass $m$. It differs from total density and need not equal condensate density.
