# Quantum money

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum cryptography](quantum-cryptography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_money)

A verification scheme using [quantum states](quantum-state.md) as part of a banknote or token. Secret-basis [qubits](qubit.md) can make extra copies hard to produce because their encodings are nonorthogonal. Security claims must specify the adversary's information, access to original tokens and the verification procedure; the [no-cloning theorem](no-cloning-theorem.md) alone does not fix every forging [probability](probability.md).
