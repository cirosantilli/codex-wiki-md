# Fourfold coincidence counting

↑ **Parent:** [Coincidence counting](coincidence-counting.md)

Retaining events with a detection in each of four channels within a specified timing window. For exactly four incident [photons](photon.md) and ideal [photodetectors](photodetector.md), a click in every channel requires one photon per channel. More photons, losses and detector timing introduce additional qualifications.
