# Parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical model](mathematical-model.md)

A [parameter](parameter.md) specifies a member of a family of mathematical models and is held fixed while a particular solution evolves. Varying it can change equilibrium existence, stability or long-term dynamics, producing a [bifurcation](bifurcation.md). A model [parameter](parameter.md) differs from its evolving state variables and from an initial condition, even though all may be numerical inputs to a computation.
