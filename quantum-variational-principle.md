# Quantum variational principle

↑ **Parent:** [Ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md)

For every normalized trial state $\psi$ in the domain of a [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H$ that is bounded below,

$$
E_0\leq\langle\psi,H\psi\rangle,
$$

where $E_0$ is the [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md). Equality holds precisely when the trial state lies in the ground-state eigenspace.
