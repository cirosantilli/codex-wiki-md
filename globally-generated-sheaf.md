# Globally generated sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md)

A sheaf of modules is globally generated when the evaluation map from its [global sections](global-section.md) is surjective. Equivalently, the germs of global sections span each stalk as a module over the local ring. For a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) on a [projective variety](projective-variety.md), every sufficiently positive twist is globally generated: embed the variety in projective space, present its direct-image sheaf as a quotient of a finite sum of $\mathcal O(-a)$, twist until those line bundles are globally generated, and restrict their generating sections to the variety.
