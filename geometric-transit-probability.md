# Geometric transit probability

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md)

The probability that an isotropically oriented [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) produces an [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md), assuming $a>R_\star+R_p$. Uniformity of $|\cos i|$ gives the displayed result. [Orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md), a restriction to full rather than grazing crossings, or a non-isotropic orientation distribution changes the expression.
