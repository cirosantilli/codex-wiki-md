# Positron emission tomography

↑ **Parent:** [Tomography](tomography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positron_emission_tomography)

[Tomography](tomography.md) using approximately coincident annihilation-photon detections to constrain an internal emission distribution. A calibrated model accounts for propagation, detector response, unrelated coincidences and scattering.
