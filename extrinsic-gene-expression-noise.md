# Extrinsic gene-expression noise

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

Expression fluctuations caused by shared variation in cellular conditions, such as expression machinery or growth state. Matched reporters tend to co-vary. For mean-normalized reporters whose intrinsic components are conditionally independent, the squared extrinsic noise equals $\langle xy\rangle-1$.
