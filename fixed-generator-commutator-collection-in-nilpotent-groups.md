# Fixed-generator commutator collection in nilpotent groups

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md)

If a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md) is generated by $a_1,\ldots,a_d$, every element of its [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is a product $[x_1,a_1]\cdots[x_d,a_d]$. The sets in the displayed formula need not be [subgroups](subgroup.md). Induct on the nilpotency class $c$. Collection in $G/\gamma_c(G)$ gives the desired product with a central error. Since $\gamma_c(G)=[\gamma_{c-1}(G),G]$, that error is a product $[y_1,a_1]\cdots[y_d,a_d]$ with $y_i\in\gamma_{c-1}(G)$. These factors are central, and $\gamma_{c-1}(G)$ centralizes $G\prime$, so replacing $x_i$ by $x_iy_i$ absorbs the error. For a finitely generated [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md), applying this formula in all finite quotients and using compactness makes its algebraically generated [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) closed.
