# Static Killing field is a Ricci eigenvector

↑ **Parent:** [Static spacetime](static-spacetime.md)

A timelike [Killing vector](killing-vector-field.md) with [hypersurface orthogonality](hypersurface-orthogonality.md) admits local coordinates with $k=\partial_t$ and $ds^2=N(\mathbf x)^2dt^2-h_{ij}(\mathbf x)dx^idx^j$. The only connection coefficients with time indices that can be nonzero are $\Gamma^0_{0i}=\partial_i\log N$ and $\Gamma^i_{00}=Nh^{ij}\partial_jN$. Substitution into the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) gives $R_{0i}=0$. Thus $R_{ab}k^b$ has only its time component and is proportional to $k_a$. Equivalently $k_dR^d{}_{[a}k_{b]}=0$. This local statement does not require the [Vacuum Einstein equations](vacuum-einstein-equations.md) and is independent of the overall curvature-sign convention.
