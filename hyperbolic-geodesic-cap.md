# Hyperbolic geodesic cap

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic distance in the Poincare disc](hyperbolic-distance-in-the-poincare-disc.md)

For a proper arc $I$ of the boundary of the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), $Q(I)$ is the side of the [hyperbolic geodesic](geodesic-in-the-poincare-half-plane-model.md) between its endpoints adjacent to $I$. For short arcs this is a curved boundary cap. For arcs longer than a semicircle it contains the center. With endpoints $e^{\pm i\alpha}$ and $0<\alpha<\pi/2$, the geodesic is the circle with center $\sec\alpha$ and radius $\tan\alpha$, orthogonal to the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md). It is a diameter at $\alpha=\pi/2$.
