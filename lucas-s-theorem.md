<h1 id="lucas-s-theorem">Lucas's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas's_theorem)

For a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$, the [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md) modulo $p$ factors into the corresponding digit [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md) in the [base-p expansions](base-p-expansion.md) of the nonnegative [integers](integer.md) $n,k$. Pad both expansions to the same length and use $\binom ab=0$ when $b>a$. The identity follows by factoring $(1+x)^n$ into digit powers and using the [Frobenius endomorphism](frobenius-endomorphism.md); digit uniqueness identifies each coefficient.
