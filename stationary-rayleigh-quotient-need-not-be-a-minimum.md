# Stationary Rayleigh quotient need not be a minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md)

For a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) with two normalized orthogonal [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) $u_0,u_1$ and $E_0<E_1$, the [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) of $\psi_t=u_1+tu_0$ is $(E_1+t^2E_0)/(1+t^2)$. It is stationary at $t=0$ but has second derivative $2(E_0-E_1)<0$ there. Thus an exact excited [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) can be a maximum within a trial family. A constrained minimum requires exclusion of lower states, as in an [excited-state variational constraint](excited-state-variational-constraint.md).
