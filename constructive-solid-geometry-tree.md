# Constructive solid geometry tree

↑ **Parent:** [Constructive solid geometry](constructive-solid-geometry.md)

A [rooted tree](rooted-tree.md) whose leaves describe primitive regions and whose internal nodes describe Boolean operations. A [half-space](half-space.md) leaf can hold an [implicit equation](implicit-equation.md) and an inside-sign convention. Evaluate membership from the leaves toward the root; [interval arithmetic](interval-arithmetic.md) can propagate conservative inside/outside bounds over a box instead of a single [point](point-geometry.md).
