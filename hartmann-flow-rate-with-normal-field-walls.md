# Hartmann flow rate with normal-field walls

↑ **Parent:** [Hartmann flow](hartmann-flow.md)

For a streamwise pressure gradient $-\rho G$ and walls at $z=\pm L$ with zero tangential [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), the [volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit span is

$$
Q(H)=\frac{2GL^3}{\nu}\frac{H\coth H-1}{H^2}.
$$

Its zero-field value is $2GL^3/(3\nu)$; it decreases strictly for $H>0$ and is asymptotic to $2GL^3/(\nu H)$. The [partial-fraction expansion of the hyperbolic cotangent](partial-fraction-expansion-of-the-hyperbolic-cotangent.md) proves monotonicity directly.
