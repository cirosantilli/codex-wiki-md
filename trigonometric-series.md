# Trigonometric series

↑ **Parent:** [Series (mathematics)](series-mathematics.md)

A [series](series-mathematics.md) whose terms are multiples of [sines](sine.md) and [cosines](cosine.md) of integer multiples of a variable. A [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) is the special case with [coefficients](coefficient.md) extracted from a specified [function](function-split.md) by [integration](integral-split.md).
