# Perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Perturbation theory](perturbation-theory.md)

A perturbation is a small change in a model, operator, state or parameter. It can be organized as a family $F_\varepsilon=F_0+\varepsilon G+o(\varepsilon)$ in a specified norm. If $F_\varepsilon(z_\varepsilon)=0$ and $DF_0(z_0)$ is invertible, differentiating at zero gives $z'_0=-[DF_0(z_0)]^{-1}G(z_0)$. The [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md) justifies this continued solution locally. Singular derivatives at a bifurcation instead require an unfolding or reduced equation; smallness of the change alone does not preserve a degenerate bifurcation.
