# Finite-variation process

↑ **Parent:** [Semimartingale](semimartingale.md)

A process $A$ has finite variation on compact intervals when every sample path has finite [total variation of a function](total-variation-of-a-function.md) there. Such a process can be integrated pathwise by the Lebesgue-Stieltjes integral.
