# Cowling anti-dynamo theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-dynamo theorem](anti-dynamo-theorem.md)

An isolated axisymmetric [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) cannot be sustained by ordinary resistive [dynamo action](dynamo-action.md) with scalar positive [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md). Its [poloidal magnetic flux function](poloidal-magnetic-flux-function.md) satisfies a homogeneous scalar advection-diffusion equation with no toroidal-to-poloidal source. The maximum principle excludes regeneration of that flux; after it decays the toroidal field has no lasting source either. This constrains the symmetry of the field. An [axisymmetric flow](axisymmetric-flow.md) may still generate a nonaxisymmetric field, and a turbulent mean-field electromotive force is not covered by the unmodified scalar-induction proof.
