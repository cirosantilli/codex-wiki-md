# Strong-coupling scale

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md)

The strong-coupling scale of an asymptotically free gauge theory is the renormalization-group invariant scale at which the one-loop running coupling formally diverges. It replaces a dimensionless ultraviolet coupling through [dimensional transmutation](dimensional-transmutation.md).
