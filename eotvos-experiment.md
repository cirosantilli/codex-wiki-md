<h1 id="eotvos-experiment">Eötvös experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Weak equivalence principle](weak-equivalence-principle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_experiment)

A torsion-balance comparison of the gravitational acceleration of bodies of different composition, testing equality of inertial and passive gravitational mass up to a universal proportionality factor. A universal acceleration law independent of test-body mass and composition agrees with this [weak equivalence principle](weak-equivalence-principle.md) test. It does not by itself establish the [strong equivalence principle](strong-equivalence-principle.md) for self-gravitating bodies.
