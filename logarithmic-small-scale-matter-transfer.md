# Logarithmic small-scale matter transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Cold-dark-matter transfer function](cold-dark-matter-transfer-function.md)

Modes with $k\gg k_{\rm eq}$ enter the horizon during [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md). Their matter perturbations grow only logarithmically until [matter-radiation equality](matter-radiation-equality.md), rather than as $a$. Matching the radiation-era logarithmic growth onto the matter-era growing mode gives the large-$k$ [cosmological transfer function](cosmological-transfer-function.md) scaling shown, up to constants and the matching scale inside the logarithm. For a primordial [scalar spectral index](scalar-spectral-index.md) $n_s$, the late-time linear [cosmological density power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md) consequently changes from $P(k)\propto k^{n_s}$ at small $k$ to $P(k)\propto k^{n_s-4}\log^2(k/k_{\rm eq})$ at large $k$. These scalings omit baryonic acoustic structure and nonlinear evolution.

Here the [cold-dark-matter transfer function](cold-dark-matter-transfer-function.md) is normalized so that $\delta_m(k,a)=A(a)k^2T(k)\mathcal R(k)$, where $\mathcal R$ is the primordial [comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md) and $A(a)$ is independent of $k$. During [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md), horizon entry occurs at $a_{\rm ent}\propto k^{-1}$. An adiabatic entry amplitude of order $\mathcal R$ then acquires a factor $\log(a_{\rm eq}/a_{\rm ent})\sim\log(k/k_{\rm eq})$ by [matter-radiation equality](matter-radiation-equality.md). Subsequent [matter domination](matter-domination.md) supplies a common growth factor; division by the conventional $k^2$ prefactor therefore gives $T(k)\propto k^{-2}\log(k/k_{\rm eq})$.
