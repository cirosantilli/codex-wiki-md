# Parallel LC circuit

↑ **Parent:** [LC circuit](lc-circuit.md)

An ideal [inductor](inductor.md) and [capacitor](capacitor.md) in parallel have [electrical admittance](electrical-admittance.md) $1/Z=1/(j\omega L)+j\omega C$ for [phasors](phasor.md) with time dependence $e^{j\omega t}$. The branch currents cancel at [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega_0=1/\sqrt{LC}$, giving zero total [electrical admittance](electrical-admittance.md) and an infinite limiting [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md). Below this [resonance](resonance.md) its [electrical reactance](electrical-reactance.md) is positive; above it the [electrical reactance](electrical-reactance.md) is negative. Losses make the peak finite.
