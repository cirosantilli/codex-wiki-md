# Beta strand

↑ **Parent:** [Beta sheet](beta-sheet.md)

A [beta strand](beta-strand.md) is an extended segment of [protein backbone](protein-backbone.md) in the geometry characteristic of a [beta sheet](beta-sheet.md). Several [beta strands](beta-strand.md) can associate through [backbone hydrogen bonds](backbone-hydrogen-bond.md). A [beta strand](beta-strand.md) need not be an entire [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md).
