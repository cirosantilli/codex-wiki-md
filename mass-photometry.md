# Mass photometry

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)

Measurement of single-particle mass through its contribution to an optical scattering signal. Unlike [size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md), its principal size readout is mass rather than [hydrodynamic size](hydrodynamic-size.md).
