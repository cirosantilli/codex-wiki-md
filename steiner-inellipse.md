# Steiner inellipse

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipse](ellipse.md)

The Steiner inellipse of a nondegenerate [triangle](triangle.md) is the unique [ellipse](ellipse.md) of largest [area](surface-area.md) contained in the [triangle](triangle.md). Its center is the [centroid](centroid.md), and it touches each side at its midpoint.
