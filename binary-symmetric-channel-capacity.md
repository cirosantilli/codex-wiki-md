# Binary symmetric channel capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly symmetric channel capacity](weakly-symmetric-channel-capacity.md)

A binary symmetric channel with crossover probability $p$ has capacity

$$
C=1-h_2(p)
$$

bits per channel use.
