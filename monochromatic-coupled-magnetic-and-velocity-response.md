# Monochromatic coupled magnetic and velocity response

↑ **Parent:** [First-order smoothing approximation](first-order-smoothing-approximation.md)

For a steady forced [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) with $|\mathbf q|=k$, coupled viscous and magnetic response gives $\widehat{\mathbf u}=[\eta k^2\widehat{\mathbf f}+i(\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf q)\widehat{\mathbf g}/(\mu_0\rho)]/D$ and $\widehat{\mathbf b}=[\nu k^2\widehat{\mathbf g}+i(\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf q)\widehat{\mathbf f}]/D$. Their [mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md) contains both forcing helicities and a possible weighted cross-forcing correlation. Jointly isotropic forcing removes the latter; its vanishing at zero mean field alone is insufficient for anisotropic forcing.
