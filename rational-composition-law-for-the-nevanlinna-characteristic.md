# Rational composition law for the Nevanlinna characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Nevanlinna characteristic](nevanlinna-characteristic.md)

For a degree-$d$ [rational function](rational-function.md) $U$ of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), choose relatively prime homogeneous [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) $(P,Q)$ of degree $d$. Their joint norm on the unit three-sphere has positive lower and finite upper bounds. Thus $\log\|(P,Q)(v)\|-d\log\|v\|$ is bounded. Averaging over a circle using a nonvanishing entire homogeneous lift of a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) proves the displayed law for the spherical characteristic. [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md) and $0\le\tfrac12\log(1+x^2)-\log^+x\le\tfrac12\log2$ show that it also holds for the ordinary [Nevanlinna characteristic](nevanlinna-characteristic.md).
