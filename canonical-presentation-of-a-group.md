# Canonical presentation of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Group presentation](group-presentation.md)

The [canonical presentation of a group](canonical-presentation-of-a-group.md) uses one symbol for each [group element](group-element.md) and all words that evaluate to the [identity element](identity-element.md) as [relators](relator.md). Evaluation is a [surjective](surjective-function.md) [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from the [free group](free-group.md) on the underlying [set](set-split.md); the [first isomorphism theorem](first-isomorphism-theorem.md) proves that the resulting [group presentation](group-presentation.md) recovers the original [group](group-split.md). Equivalently the multiplication-table [relators](relator.md) $s_gs_hs_{gh}^{-1}$ normally generate its kernel: they reduce every word to the symbol for its value. [Topology and Groups, Definition V.8 and preceding construction, printed p. 44](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/lackenby/tg11o17.pdf#page=44)
