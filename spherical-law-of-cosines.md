# Spherical law of cosines

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical trigonometry](spherical-trigonometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_law_of_cosines)

For a convex minor [spherical triangle](spherical-triangle.md) with opposite side-angle pairs $(a,A),(b,B),(c,C)$, the displayed identity is the [spherical law of cosines](spherical-law-of-cosines.md). At a vertex with unit position [vector](vector.md) $u$, the two outgoing unit tangent [vectors](vector.md) are $(v-(u\cdot v)u)/\sin c$ and $(w-(u\cdot w)u)/\sin b$. Taking their [dot product](dot-product.md) gives the identity.
