# Weyl theorem for bounded compact perturbations

↑ **Parent:** [Essential spectrum of a closed operator](essential-spectrum-of-a-closed-operator.md)

For bounded $L$ and compact $K$, use the Fredholm convention $\Sigma_{\mathrm e}(L)=\{z:L-zI\text{ is not Fredholm}\}$. Applying [compact perturbation invariance of Fredholm operators](compact-perturbation-invariance-of-fredholm-operators.md) to every shift proves the equality. This general theorem needs no self-adjointness; other definitions of [essential spectrum](essential-spectrum-of-a-closed-operator.md) for nonnormal operators must be distinguished.
