# Polarization of environmental dielectric reflections

↑ **Parent:** [Brewster's angle](brewster-s-angle.md)

At a transparent nonmagnetic [dielectric](dielectric.md) interface, the Fresnel [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) coefficients differ for s and p [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md). At Brewster incidence the reflected p [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) vanishes and reflected [unpolarized light](unpolarized-light.md) becomes s polarized. For a horizontal reflecting surface this is horizontal glare. Away from Brewster incidence it is generally partial polarization. Refraction and internal [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) in droplets also select the two components differently, explaining polarization of rainbow [light](light.md) without a biological mechanism.
