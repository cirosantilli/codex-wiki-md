# Uniform electron-gas exchange energy

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock electron gas](hartree-fock-electron-gas.md)

In a three-dimensional unpolarized gas, exchange [energy](energy-split.md) per particle is $-3e^2k_F/(16\pi^2\varepsilon)$, with Coulomb convention $e^2/(4\pi\varepsilon r)$. It scales as minus the inverse dimensionless spacing radius, while kinetic [energy](energy-split.md) scales as its inverse square. Exchange acts only between equal-spin occupied orbitals.
