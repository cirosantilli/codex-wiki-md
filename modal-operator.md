# Modal operator

↑ **Parent:** [Modal logic](modal-logic.md)

A [modal operator](modal-operator.md) forms a new formula from a formula, such as $A\mapsto\Box A$. In [provability logic](provability-logic.md), it represents an assertion that a proof of $A$ exists, rather than an assertion that $A$ is true. A box is not interchangeable with an unboxed formula.
