# Shifted-exponential Poisson mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson mixture](poisson-mixture.md)

When the random intensity is $\alpha+Y$ with $Y$ having an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of rate $\beta$, the mixed count is the sum of independent [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) and support-zero [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) counts. The Poisson parameter is $\alpha$ and the geometric success probability is $\beta/(\beta+1)$. At $\alpha=0$ the law is geometric.
