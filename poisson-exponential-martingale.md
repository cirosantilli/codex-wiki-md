# Poisson exponential martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson process](poisson-process.md)

For a unit-rate [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) and real $\theta$, the displayed process is a nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md) with initial value one. Independent increments give $\mathbb E e^{\theta(N_t-N_s)}=\exp((t-s)(e^\theta-1))$, proving its conditional mean property. The [Ville inequality](ville-inequality.md) turns this exponential moment into a maximal tail bound.
