# Infinite quadratic variation forces unbounded oscillations

↑ **Parent:** [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md)

For a [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) with infinite terminal [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md), the [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) represents $M_t-M_0=W_{[M]_t}$. Its continuous clock traverses all nonnegative times. Standard one-dimensional [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) has unbounded positive and negative oscillations, by the [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) or [recurrence of one-dimensional Brownian motion](recurrence-of-one-dimensional-brownian-motion.md). Continuity bounds every path on compact time intervals, so these large values occur at arbitrarily late times. Pulling them back through the clock proves the displayed conclusion [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), and in particular $\limsup_{t\to\infty}|M_t|=\infty$.
