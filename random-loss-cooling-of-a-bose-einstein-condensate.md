# Random-loss cooling of a Bose-Einstein condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md)

For a partially condensed equilibrium ideal [Bose gas](bose-gas.md) in a fixed three-dimensional box, the [energy](energy-split.md) scales as $T^{5/2}$. Unbiased random particle removal keeps mean [energy](energy-split.md) per particle fixed, so re-equilibration gives $T\propto N^{2/5}$ until condensation disappears. Removal of an excited particle can be followed by partial replenishment from the zero-energy condensate. This differs from selective [evaporative cooling of atoms](evaporative-cooling-atomic-physics.md), which preferentially removes high-energy atoms and reduces mean [energy](energy-split.md) per particle.
