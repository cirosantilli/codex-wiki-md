# Dingle factor

↑ **Parent:** [Shubnikov-de Haas effect](shubnikov-de-haas-effect.md)

The [Dingle factor](dingle-factor.md) describes the damping of quantum oscillations by Landau-level broadening. For a Lorentzian half-width $\hbar/(2\tau_q)$, the fundamental harmonic has $R_D=\exp[-\pi/(\omega_c\tau_q)]$. Since $\omega_c$ decreases with field, the amplitude decays at large inverse field. The lifetime here is the [quantum lifetime](quantum-lifetime.md), not automatically the mobility-derived transport time.
