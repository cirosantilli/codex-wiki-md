# Hermitian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermitian_manifold)

A Hermitian manifold is a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) whose tangent bundle carries a smoothly varying positive-definite [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md). Its real part is a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) invariant under the [complex structure](complex-structure.md) $J$.
