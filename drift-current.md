# Drift current

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)

A [drift current](drift-current.md) is an [electric current](electric-current.md) caused by the response of mobile charge carriers to an [electric field](electric-field-split.md). It is distinct from the [diffusion](diffusion.md) contribution due to concentration gradients. At equilibrium in a [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md), the two contributions cancel although each can be nonzero.
