# Hamiltonian of a charged particle in an electromagnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Charged-particle electromagnetic Lagrangian](charged-particle-electromagnetic-lagrangian.md)

The [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md) of the [charged-particle electromagnetic Lagrangian](charged-particle-electromagnetic-lagrangian.md) is

$$
H(\mathbf x,\mathbf p,t)=\frac{[\mathbf p-q\mathbf A(\mathbf x,t)]^2}{2m}+q\phi(\mathbf x,t).
$$
