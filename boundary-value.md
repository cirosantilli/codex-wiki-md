# Boundary value

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary condition](boundary-condition.md)

The value assigned to or approached by a [function](function-split.md) at a point of the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of its domain. Prescribing a [boundary value](boundary-value.md) is a [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md).
