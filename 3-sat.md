# 3-SAT

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md)

3-SAT is the [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md) restricted to [Boolean formulas](boolean-formula.md) in [conjunctive normal form](conjunctive-normal-form.md) with at most three [Boolean literals](boolean-literal.md) per [Boolean clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md). It is [NP-complete](np-completeness.md). A nonempty short [Boolean clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) can be padded to three positions by repeating a [Boolean literal](boolean-literal.md), without changing satisfiability.
