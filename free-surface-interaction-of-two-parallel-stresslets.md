# Free-surface interaction of two parallel stresslets

↑ **Parent:** [Free-surface image of a force dipole](free-surface-image-of-a-force-dipole.md)

For equal [stresslets](force-dipole-flow.md) with height $h$ and axial separation $\ell\gg h$, the neighbour image doubles the leading axial repulsion. Its normal contribution is smaller than own-image attraction by order $(h/\ell)^3$, so leading height dynamics are unchanged. The approximation also neglects weak neighbour-induced orientation corrections.
