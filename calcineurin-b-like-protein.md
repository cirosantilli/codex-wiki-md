# Calcineurin B-like protein

↑ **Parent:** [Calcium signaling](calcium-signaling.md)

A plant calcium sensor, often abbreviated CBL, that works with a [CBL-interacting protein kinase](cbl-interacting-protein-kinase.md). Sensor localization and partner selection help confer response specificity.
