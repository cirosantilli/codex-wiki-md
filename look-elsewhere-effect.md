# Look-elsewhere effect

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical significance](statistical-significance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Look-elsewhere_effect)

Searching many possible signal locations increases the chance of finding an apparently unusual fluctuation somewhere.
