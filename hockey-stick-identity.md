# Hockey-stick identity

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md)

For nonnegative [integers](integer.md) $n,m$, the [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md) satisfy

$$
\sum_{k=0}^m\binom{n+k}k=\binom{n+m+1}m.
$$

The identity follows by [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md): adding the next term combines two adjacent [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md) through [Pascal's identity](pascal-s-rule.md). An equivalent form is $\sum_{r=j}^N\binom rj=\binom{N+1}{j+1}$, a diagonal sum in [Pascal's triangle](pascal-s-triangle.md). It provides closed forms for sums of [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md) without expanding individual [factorials](factorial.md).
