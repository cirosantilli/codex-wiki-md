# Iitaka dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Complete linear system of a divisor](complete-linear-system-of-a-divisor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iitaka_dimension)

The Iitaka dimension of a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$ is the maximum dimension of the images of the rational maps given by its nonempty multiples $|mD|$. It is $-\infty$ if all positive multiples have no sections. For an integral $n$-dimensional projective variety it lies between zero and $n$ when nonnegative. The [Kodaira dimension](kodaira-dimension.md) of the variety is the special case $D=K_X$.
