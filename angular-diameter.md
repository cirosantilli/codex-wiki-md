# Angular diameter

↑ **Parent:** [Angle](angle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angular_diameter)

The [angle](angle.md) subtended by an object at an observer. For a circular object of diameter $D$ viewed face-on at a distance $d$ much larger than $D$, its angular diameter is approximately $D/d$ in [radians](radian.md).
