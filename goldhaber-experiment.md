# Goldhaber experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Parity violation](parity-violation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldhaber_experiment)

The experiment infers [neutrino helicity](neutrino-helicity.md) from nuclear [nuclear resonance fluorescence](nuclear-resonance-fluorescence.md) and [photon helicity](photon-helicity.md). A spin-zero nucleus undergoes [electron capture](electron-capture.md) to a spin-one daughter, which emits a nuclear [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) to a spin-zero ground state. Selecting emission along the daughter recoil relates the normalized daughter, photon and neutrino helicities. Reversing magnetized iron measures the circular polarization of the selected radiation.[Pages 1015–1017, selection principle and helicity measurement](helicity-of-neutrinos-by-maurice-goldhaber-and-others.md)
