# Boyer-Lindquist coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyer-Lindquist_coordinates)

Boyer-Lindquist coordinates adapt the Kerr metric to its stationary and axial symmetries. The horizon radii are the roots $r_\pm=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-a^2}$ of $\Delta=r^2-2Mr+a^2$.
