# Pullback of an epimorphism is a pushout in an abelian category

↑ **Parent:** [Pullback stability of epimorphisms in an abelian category](pullback-stability-of-epimorphisms-in-an-abelian-category.md)

In an [abelian category](abelian-category.md), suppose a commutative square $gf=kh$ is a [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md) and $g$ is an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md). Then $(f,h):A\to B\oplus C$ is the [categorical kernel](kernel-in-a-category.md) of the epimorphism $[g,-k]$. Since an epimorphism is the cokernel of its kernel, any compatible pair $u:B\to X$, $v:C\to X$ induces a unique map $D\to X$ by factoring $[u,-v]$. Hence the square is also a [pushout in a category](pushout-in-a-category.md).
