# Curvilinear reduction to the one-dimensional wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic normal form](characteristic-normal-form.md)

In $x,y>0$, the change of variables in the title reduces

$$
-xyu_{xx}+u_{yy}=\frac12\left(yu_x+\frac{u_y}{y}\right)
$$

to the [one-dimensional wave equation](one-dimensional-wave-equation.md) $U_{ss}-U_{rr}=0$. Indeed $u_{xx}=U_{rr}/(4x)-U_r/(4x^{3/2})$ and $u_{yy}=yU_{ss}/4+U_s/(4\sqrt y)$; the first-derivative terms cancel exactly against the right-hand side. The independent [characteristic coordinates](characteristic-coordinate.md) $\xi=r+s$, $\eta=r-s$ then give $V_{\xi\eta}=0$ and $u=g(\xi)+h(\eta)$. Two characteristic families alone generally reduce only the principal part; the cancellation of lower-order terms is the extra feature that gives this simple exact solution.
