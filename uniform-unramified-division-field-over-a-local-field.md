# Uniform unramified division field over a local field

↑ **Parent:** [Division field of an elliptic curve](division-field-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Let $E/K$ have [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) over a [local field](local-field.md) of residue characteristic $p$, with $p\nmid n$. All solutions of $nQ=P$, for all $P\in E(K)$ together, lie in a single finite [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of degree at most $nf$, where $f=[K(E[n]):K]$. First lift a solution over the algebraic closure of the [residue field](residue-field.md) to a finite [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md); the [multiplication isomorphism of a formal group law](multiplication-isomorphism-of-a-formal-group-law.md) uniquely corrects the error in $E_1$. This also puts $E[n]$ in the [maximal unramified extension](maximal-unramified-extension.md). If $\sigma$ is its Frobenius generator, $\sigma^f$ fixes $E[n]$. For $nQ=P$, set $T=\sigma^fQ-Q\in E[n]$. Then $\sigma^{nf}Q=Q+nT=Q$, uniformly in $P$.
