# Constraint hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md)

The [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) whose [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) are the [variables](variable-mathematics.md) of a [constraint satisfaction problem](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md) and whose [hypergraph edges](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) are its constraint scopes. Several constraints can have the same scope. An incidence drawing uses extra scope nodes for display; these do not become extra [variables](variable-mathematics.md) of the original problem. Ordinary [treewidth](treewidth.md) of a [constraint hypergraph](constraint-hypergraph.md) refers to [treewidth](treewidth.md) of its [primal graph](primal-graph.md), equivalently to a [tree decomposition](tree-decomposition.md) covering each whole scope, not to generalized hypergraph width measures.
