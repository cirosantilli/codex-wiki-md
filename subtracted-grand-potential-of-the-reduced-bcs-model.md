# Subtracted grand potential of the reduced BCS model

↑ **Parent:** [BCS pairing-field effective action](bcs-pairing-field-effective-action.md)

Subtracting the normal-state [grand potential](grand-potential.md) fixes the reference constant in the uniform [BCS pairing-field effective action](bcs-pairing-field-effective-action.md). Here $\omega_n=(2n+1)\pi/\beta$ has energy units and $E_k^2=\xi_k^2+|\Delta|^2$. The ratio cancels a field-independent determinant phase and produces a convergent high-frequency sum under the same momentum regulator. Differentiation gives the [BCS gap equation](bcs-gap-equation.md). For a fixed attractive pairing shell, this is a finite regulated model; an unrestricted continuum contact interaction additionally needs ultraviolet renormalization. Its weak-coupling condensation density $-\nu_F\Delta^2/2$ uses a single-spin [density of states](density-of-states.md) per volume.
