# Binding affinity

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand binding](ligand-binding.md)

The equilibrium strength of association under specified conditions. For simple one-to-one [ligand binding](ligand-binding.md), a smaller [dissociation constant](dissociation-constant.md) corresponds to stronger affinity; a predicted bound pose does not itself determine this constant.
