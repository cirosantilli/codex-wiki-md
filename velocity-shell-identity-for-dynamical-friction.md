# Velocity-shell identity for dynamical friction

↑ **Parent:** [Chandrasekhar dynamical friction](chandrasekhar-dynamical-friction.md)

For an isotropic, integrable background [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md), [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md) makes the integral radial in [velocity](velocity.md) space. Its divergence with respect to $\mathbf V$ is $4\pi f(V)$ by the [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md) identity for the divergence of the inverse-square [vector field](vector-field.md). Integrating over a velocity-space sphere and using the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) proves the displayed identity. Exterior [velocity](velocity.md) shells have zero contribution, just as in the [spherical shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md). This explains why the leading-log [Chandrasekhar dynamical friction](chandrasekhar-dynamical-friction.md) integral contains only particles slower than the test body.
