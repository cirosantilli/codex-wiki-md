# Seismic ray theory

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

[Seismic ray theory](seismic-ray-theory.md) approximates high-frequency propagation through smoothly varying media. The travel-time field satisfies the [eikonal equation](eikonal-equation.md), and its characteristics are paths of stationary travel time according to [Fermat's principle](fermat-principle.md). Diffraction and caustics require refinements beyond a simple geometrical-ray approximation.
