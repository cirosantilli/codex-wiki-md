# RNA export

↑ **Parent:** [Post-transcriptional regulation](post-transcriptional-regulation.md)

Movement of [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) from the nucleus to the cytoplasm in the relevant eukaryotic pathway. Processing, recognition and quality control can be coupled to export; export is distinct from transcription or subsequent translation.
