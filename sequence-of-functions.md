# Sequence of functions

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

A [sequence](sequence.md) whose terms are [functions](function-split.md) on a common [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md). Its [pointwise convergence](pointwise-convergence.md) tests each argument separately; its [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) controls the error throughout the domain with one bound.
