# Boundary component of a surface

↑ **Parent:** [Surface with boundary](surface-with-boundary.md)

A boundary component of a [surface with boundary](surface-with-boundary.md) is a [connected component](connected-component.md) of its [manifold boundary](manifold-boundary.md). For a [compact](compact-space.md) [surface with boundary](surface-with-boundary.md), these are finitely many [circles](circle.md). Their number $b$ enters the [classification of compact surfaces](classification-of-compact-surfaces.md): $\chi=2-2g-b$ in the [orientable](orientable-surface.md) case and $\chi=2-k-b$ in the [nonorientable surface](non-orientable-surface.md) case, where $k$ is the [nonorientable genus](nonorientable-genus.md).
