# Slant optical depth of an isothermal atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric scale height](atmospheric-scale-height.md)

For an isothermal thin atmosphere with [number density](number-density.md) $n(z)=n_0e^{-z/H}$ and extinction cross-section $\sigma_\lambda$, a tangent ray near radius $R_p+z$ has height $z+\ell^2/(2R_p)$ at distance $\ell$ along the ray. The resulting [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) gives slant [optical depth](optical-depth.md)

$$
\tau_\lambda(z)\simeq\sigma_\lambda n(z)\sqrt{2\pi R_pH}.
$$

The effective transit height is near $\tau_\lambda\sim1$, so $z(\lambda)=H\log\sigma_\lambda+\mathrm{constant}$ if composition is constant. This explains the [scattering slope of a transmission spectrum](scattering-slope-of-a-transmission-spectrum.md).
