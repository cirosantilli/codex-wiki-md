# Generalized fluctuation-dissipation relation

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Langevin equation](generalized-langevin-equation.md)

For the equilibrium [generalized Langevin equation](generalized-langevin-equation.md) with unit mass, the random force has zero [mean](expected-value-split.md) and the displayed [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md), where $\Theta$ is the equilibrium [velocity](velocity.md) [variance](variance-split.md). If the deterministic relaxation $g$ is stable, $g(0)=1$ and $g'=-\kappa*g$, then the stationary [velocity autocorrelation function](velocity-autocorrelation-function.md) is $\chi=\Theta g$, equivalently

$$
\widetilde\chi(s)=\frac{\Theta}{s+\widetilde\kappa(s)}.
$$

One proof uses the [power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md). The even force [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md) has spectrum $2\Theta\operatorname{Re}\widetilde\kappa(i\omega)$. With transfer function $H(i\omega)=[i\omega+\widetilde\kappa(i\omega)]^{-1}$, the [velocity](velocity.md) spectrum is $2\Theta|H|^2\operatorname{Re}\widetilde\kappa=2\Theta\operatorname{Re}H$, the spectrum of the even extension of $\Theta g$. Stability removes initial transients with finite second [moment](moment.md). A valid force [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md) must have nonnegative [power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md); an arbitrary proposed [memory kernel](memory-kernel.md) does not automatically satisfy this condition.
