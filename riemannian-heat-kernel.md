# Riemannian heat kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

For the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md), the kernel solving $(\partial_t+\Delta_x)H=0$ with initial [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md) on the diagonal represents the [heat semigroup](heat-semigroup.md). On a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) with a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) it is smooth for $t>0$, symmetric, nonnegative, preserves constants, and obeys the [semigroup property](semigroup-property.md).
