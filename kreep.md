# KREEP

↑ **Parent:** [Moon](moon.md)

Lunar material enriched in potassium, rare-earth elements and phosphorus, together with other incompatible elements. It is associated with evolved residual liquids from lunar differentiation; accompanying uranium and thorium provide [radiogenic heating](radiogenic-heating.md). [KREEP](kreep.md) is a geochemical designation, not itself a synonym for all mare [basalt](basalt.md) or for a unique physical crustal layer.
