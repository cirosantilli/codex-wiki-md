# Mammal

↑ **Parent:** [Mammaliaform](mammaliaform.md)

For a crown-based usage, a member of the [clade](clade.md) comprising the [last common ancestor](last-common-ancestor.md) of living [monotremes](monotreme.md) and [therians](theria.md) and all its descendants. [hair](hair.md) and [lactation](lactation.md) characterize living members, but recognition of many early [fossils](fossil.md) depends on jaw and dental characters.
