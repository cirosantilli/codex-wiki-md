# Wind Accretion and State Transitions in Cygnus X-1 by D. R. Gies and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by D. R. Gies](work-by-d-r-gies.md)

Astrophysical Journal 583, 424–436. [https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0206253](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0206253)

[https://doi.org/10.1086/345345](https://doi.org/10.1086/345345)

The primary spectroscopic monitoring study compares H-alpha wind emission with [X-ray](x-ray.md) state changes. The wind-to-accretion interpretation depends on wind velocity and ionization as well as [mass](mass.md) loss; its abstract reports that a stronger H-alpha wind need not yield a brighter soft [X-ray](x-ray.md) state.
