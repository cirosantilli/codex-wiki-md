# Ramification polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md)

The ramification [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) records the normalised differences of conjugates of a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md), including the zero difference from the identity automorphism. Its quotient by $X$ is the [reduced ramification polynomial](reduced-ramification-polynomial.md). In the usual coefficient-index convention, the finite slopes of its lower [Newton polygon](newton-polygon.md) are negatives of the [valuations](valuation.md) of its nonzero roots; the coefficient at the zero root is treated as having infinite [valuation](valuation.md).
