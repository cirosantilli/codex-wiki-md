# Log-Lipschitz modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Modulus of continuity](modulus-of-continuity.md)

Set $\mu(0)=0$ and extend $\mu(r)=1$ for $r\geq1$. This positive, nondecreasing [modulus of continuity](modulus-of-continuity.md) is weaker than a linear [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md) bound but still has $\int_0^1dr/\mu(r)=\infty$. At small distances it is equivalent to $r\log(1/r)$. A signed $r\log r$ cannot be an upper modulus at distances less than one. The [planar vorticity velocity kernel](planar-vorticity-velocity-kernel.md) maps $L^1\cap L^\infty$ into velocity fields with this modulus.
