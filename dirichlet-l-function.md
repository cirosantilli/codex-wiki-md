# Dirichlet L-function

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_L-function)

For $\operatorname{Re}s>1$,

$$
L(s,\chi)=\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{\chi(n)}{n^s}
=\prod_p(1-\chi(p)p^{-s})^{-1}.
$$
