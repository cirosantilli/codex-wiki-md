# Parity action on canonical massive spin states

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic parity](intrinsic-parity.md)

Choose canonical spin states by applying the rotationless [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) $L(p)$ to rest-frame spin states. Spatial inversion $\Pi=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$ commutes with rest-frame rotations, so [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) makes its action on one irreducible rest spin multiplet a phase $\eta_P$. Since $\Pi L(p)\Pi=L(\Pi p)$, the formula follows. Canonical spin labels are unchanged; [helicity](helicity.md) reverses. With $P^2=I$, the [intrinsic parity](intrinsic-parity.md) is $\eta_P=\pm1$.
