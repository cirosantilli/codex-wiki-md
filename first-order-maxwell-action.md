# First-order Maxwell action

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell action](maxwell-action.md)

Vary the antisymmetric auxiliary [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) and the [electromagnetic four-potential](electromagnetic-four-potential.md) independently. The first variation sets $F_{ab}=\nabla_aA_b-\nabla_bA_a$ algebraically, and variation of the potential gives $\nabla_bF^{ab}=0$. Substitution of the algebraic equation reduces the first-order action to the usual negative quadratic [Maxwell action](maxwell-action.md). Metric variation gives the same stress tensor after eliminating the auxiliary field, because the chain-rule term multiplying its variational equation then vanishes.
