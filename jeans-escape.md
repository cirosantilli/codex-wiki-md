# Jeans escape

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric escape](atmospheric-escape.md)

Jeans escape is collisionless [atmospheric escape](atmospheric-escape.md) by particles in the high-speed tail of a thermal distribution at the [exobase](exobase.md). The [Jeans escape parameter](jeans-escape-parameter.md) $\lambda=GMm/(k_BTr_e)$ controls the exponential suppression, and the [Jeans escape flux](jeans-escape-flux.md) is proportional to $(1+\lambda)e^{-\lambda}$. There is no exact positive-temperature threshold below which the tail vanishes. For $\lambda$ of order a few or smaller, a purely hydrostatic tail model can fail and [hydrodynamic atmospheric escape](hydrodynamic-escape.md) becomes important.
