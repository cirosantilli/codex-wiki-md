# Laplacian eigenspace

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenspace](eigenspace.md)

The [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of functions satisfying $\Delta f=\lambda f$ for a specified [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md). On a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) its dimension is finite and is the spectral multiplicity of $\lambda$. A finite isometric [group action](group-action.md) preserves this space; invariant elements are the [Laplacian eigenfunctions](laplacian-eigenfunction.md) on the corresponding smooth quotient.
