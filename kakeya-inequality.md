# Kakeya inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier restriction theory](fourier-restriction-theory.md)

Kakeya inequalities bound overlaps of long thin tubes with controlled directions.

These tube estimates are used to study [Kakeya sets](kakeya-set.md) and their dimension.
