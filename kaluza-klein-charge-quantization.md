# Kaluza-Klein charge quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein mode](kaluza-klein-mode.md)

For a periodic scalar on a circle of circumference $2\pi R$, its [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) has modes $\psi_n(x)e^{inz/R}$. In the fixed-radius metric $ds_5^2=\gamma_{ij}dx^idx^j+(dz+A_idx^i)^2$, the five-dimensional [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md) becomes $(D_iD^i-M^2-n^2/R^2)\psi_n=0$ with $D_i=\nabla_i-inA_i/R$. Thus the geometric gauge charge is quantized in units $1/R$ in units $\hbar=1$, or the conserved compact momentum in units $\hbar/R$. A rescaling to a canonically normalized electromagnetic potential changes the corresponding numerical unit of physical electric charge.
