# Three-body phase space with one massive daughter

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz-invariant phase space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md)

For one daughter of [mass](mass.md) $m$ and two massless daughters, set $r=m^2/M^2$, where $M$ is the parent [mass](mass.md). In [natural units](natural-units.md), integrating over all directions and all kinematically allowed final [momenta](momentum-split.md) gives

$$
\Phi_3=\frac1{128\pi^3M^2}\int_{m^2}^{M^2}\frac{(M^2-s)(s-m^2)}s\,ds
=\frac{M^2}{256\pi^3}(1-r^2+2r\ln r).
$$

This follows by splitting the final state into a massless spectator and a two-particle cluster of invariant squared [mass](mass.md) $s$, and using $d\Phi_3=d\Phi_2(M;\sqrt{s},0)d\Phi_2(\sqrt{s};m,0)ds/(2\pi)$. The expression counts [phase space](phase-space.md) alone; a physical [decay rate](decay-width.md) must weight it with the squared [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md). Near threshold the dimensionless factor vanishes as $(1-r)^3/3$.
