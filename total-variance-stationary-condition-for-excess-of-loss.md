# Total variance stationary condition for excess of loss

↑ **Parent:** [Excess of loss reinsurance](excess-of-loss-reinsurance.md)

For positive claim sizes with finite [second moment](second-moment.md) in a [compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md) aggregate of parameter $\lambda$, the two [excess of loss reinsurance](excess-of-loss-reinsurance.md) payouts are $\min(X,M)$ and $(X-M)_+$. The derivative of their total aggregate [variance](variance-split.md) is $2\lambda(M\overline F(M)-\mathbb E[(X-M)_+])$. Thus the displayed condition characterizes stationarity. When the tail probability is positive it says that the retention equals the [mean residual life](mean-residual-life.md). Global minimality requires an additional sign or comparison argument.
