# Lebesgue point

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue differentiation theorem](lebesgue-differentiation-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue_point)

A point $x\in\mathbb R^n$ is a Lebesgue point of a locally [Lebesgue integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) $f$ when

$$
\lim_{r\downarrow0}\frac1{|B_r(x)|}
\int_{B_r(x)}|f(y)-f(x)|\,dy=0.
$$

The [Lebesgue differentiation theorem](lebesgue-differentiation-theorem.md) says that almost every point is a Lebesgue point.
