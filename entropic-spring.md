# Entropic spring

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal chain](ideal-chain.md)

For an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md) of $N$ independent statistical segments of length $a$, the end-to-end [probability density](probability-density.md) is proportional to $\exp[-3R^2/(2Na^2)]$. Its constrained [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) has extension-dependent part $3k_BTR^2/(2Na^2)$, so the restoring force is $-3k_BT\mathbf R/(Na^2)$. This is an entropic spring because extension removes available chain configurations, and its stiffness grows with [temperature](temperature-split.md). The Gaussian law applies at extensions small compared with the contour length $Na$; near full extension, finite extensibility matters.
