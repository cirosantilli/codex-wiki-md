# Planar graph edge bound

↑ **Parent:** [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md)

A finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md) that is a [connected graph](connected-graph.md) and a [planar graph](planar-graph.md), with $n\geq3$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and $m$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md), satisfies $m\leq3n-6$. Choose a [plane graph](plane-graph.md): each [facial boundary walk](facial-boundary-walk.md) has length at least three, so $3f\leq2m$. Substitute $f=2-n+m$ from the [Euler formula for a connected planar graph](euler-formula-for-a-connected-planar-graph.md).[Mathematics for Computer Science, §12.4, Lemma 12.4.2 and Theorem 12.4.3, printed pp. 485–486](mathematics-for-computer-science-by-eric-lehman-and-others.md)

The [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $K_4$ attains equality: $6=3\cdot4-6$. The bound also holds for a disconnected finite [simple graph](simple-graph.md) that is a [planar graph](planar-graph.md) with $n\geq3$: place its [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md) in disjoint regions and join them with new [bridges in a graph](bridge-graph-theory.md), then apply the connected bound.
