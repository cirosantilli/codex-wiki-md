# Semimartingale decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Semimartingale](semimartingale.md)

A semimartingale has a decomposition $X=X_0+M+A$ into a [local martingale](local-martingale.md) $M$ and an adapted [finite-variation process](finite-variation-process.md) $A$. Under standard normalizations the decomposition is unique.
