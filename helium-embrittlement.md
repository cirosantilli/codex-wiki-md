# Helium embrittlement

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation damage in materials](radiation-damage-in-materials.md)

Loss of mechanical integrity associated with retained [helium](helium.md), including the growth and coalescence of gas-containing cavities at [grain boundaries](grain-boundary.md). Gas location, trapping, [temperature](temperature-split.md), [stress](stress.md) and the initial [microstructure](microstructure.md) influence the result. It can occur in austenitic [steel](steel.md) without the ferritic [ductile-to-brittle transition](ductile-to-brittle-transition.md).[Abstract: helium production and boundary-bubble interpretation of high-temperature ductility loss](high-temperature-embrittlement-of-stainless-steel-irradiated-in-fast-fluxes-by-a-s-fraser-and-others.md)
