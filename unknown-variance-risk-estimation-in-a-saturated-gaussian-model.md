# Unknown-variance risk estimation in a saturated Gaussian model

↑ **Parent:** [Unbiased Gaussian projection risk estimate](unbiased-gaussian-projection-risk-estimate.md)

For a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) $N_n(\mu,\sigma^2I)$ with unrestricted $\mu\in\mathbb R^n$ and unknown $\sigma^2$, an integrable data-only [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of the [mean-vector prediction risk](mean-vector-prediction-risk.md) of a fixed rank-$k$ [orthogonal projection matrix](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) exists exactly when $2k=n$. To prove necessity, randomize the mean by [independent](independent-random-variables.md) Gaussian [variance](variance-split.md) $v$: conditioning adds $(n-k)v$ to the squared bias term, whereas the marginal [variance](variance-split.md) identity would add $kv$. When $2k=n$, the [residual sum of squares](residual-sum-of-squares.md) itself is unbiased.
