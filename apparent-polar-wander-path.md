# Apparent polar wander path

↑ **Parent:** [Paleomagnetism](paleomagnetism.md)

A time-ordered sequence of ancient magnetic pole positions inferred from dated [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md) in a continental block. The apparent motion includes the block's movement relative to the rotation axis and can include [true polar wander](true-polar-wander.md). Consistent paths after a fixed relative rotation support an interval of shared motion; conventional [paleomagnetism](paleomagnetism.md) does not independently determine absolute longitude.
