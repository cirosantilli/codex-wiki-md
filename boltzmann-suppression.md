# Boltzmann suppression

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md)

[Boltzmann suppression](boltzmann-suppression.md) is the exponential reduction of an equilibrium population when creating it costs energy much greater than the temperature. For a nonrelativistic species with negligible [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md), the leading factor is $e^{-m/T}$, multiplied by its momentum phase-space volume. It concerns the equilibrium abundance; freeze-out can preserve a larger population than the instantaneous equilibrium value at later times.
