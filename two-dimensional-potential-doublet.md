# Two-dimensional potential doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md)

A [two-dimensional potential doublet](two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md) is a localized [potential flow](potential-flow.md) singularity with [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) $W=M/(z-b)$, where $M=me^{i\alpha}$ specifies strength and orientation. It is the limiting field of an equal [point source](point-source.md) and sink as their separation tends to zero with strength times separation fixed, with the sign absorbed in $M$. Its [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) is $-M/(z-b)^2$, so the [velocity](velocity.md) decays as inverse distance squared. The total [volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md) around the singularity is zero. Its two-dimensional decay differs from that of the three-dimensional [source dipole](source-dipole.md).
