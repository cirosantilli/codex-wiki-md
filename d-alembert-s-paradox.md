<h1 id="d-alembert-s-paradox">D'Alembert's paradox</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Alembert's_paradox)

Ideal [steady flow](steady-flow.md) with [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), [inviscid flow](inviscid-flow.md) and [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) can predict zero [drag](drag-physics.md) on a body that has nonzero [drag](drag-physics.md) in a real [fluid](fluid.md). The zero-[circulation](circulation-physics.md) cylinder [pressure coefficient](pressure-coefficient.md) distribution gives an explicit cancellation example; [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) and [flow separation](flow-separation.md) are absent from that model.
