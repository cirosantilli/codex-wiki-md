# Location-scale family

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical model](statistical-model-split.md)

In a location-scale family, $Y=\mu+\sigma Z$ for a fixed base [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) of $Z$, with $\mu\in\mathbb R$ and $\sigma>0$. Location translates observations, and scale multiplies their deviations. For an independent sample with a base density and at least two observations, the normalized residual vector $(Y_i-\bar Y)/s$, where $s^2=n^{-1}\sum_i(Y_i-\bar Y)^2$, is an [ancillary statistic](ancillary-statistic.md). Indeed, $\bar Y=\mu+\sigma\bar Z$ and $s=\sigma s_Z$, making the residual vector a function of the base sample alone. This algebraic result needs no population moments.
