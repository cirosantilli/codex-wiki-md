# Demagnetizing factor

↑ **Parent:** [Demagnetizing field](demagnetizing-field.md)

A [demagnetizing factor](demagnetizing-factor.md) relates a component of [demagnetizing field](demagnetizing-field.md) to [magnetization](magnetization.md). The three principal factors of a uniformly magnetized [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) sum to one in the [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md). Factors quoted in a different unit convention need conversion; state the convention and measurement geometry before applying them.
