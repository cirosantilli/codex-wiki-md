# Entrainment velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md)

The mean speed, measured normal to a chosen plume or jet boundary, at which ambient fluid enters the turbulent region. The boundary and averaging convention must be specified: this speed is not the plume's axial velocity. In the [Batchelor entrainment hypothesis](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md) for a rising [Boussinesq](boussinesq-approximation.md) plume, $u_e=\alpha w$, where $\alpha$ is the dimensionless [entrainment coefficient](entrainment-coefficient.md) and $w$ the characteristic axial speed. Multiplying by the boundary perimeter gives the entrained [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) per axial length.
