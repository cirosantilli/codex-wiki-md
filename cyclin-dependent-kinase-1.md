# Cyclin-dependent kinase 1

↑ **Parent:** [Cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclin-dependent_kinase_1)

[Cyclin-dependent kinase 1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md), abbreviated [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) and historically called [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md), is the catalytic [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) in the [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md)–[cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) assembly. [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) transfers a [phosphate](phosphate.md) group from [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) to a [protein](protein-split.md) [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md); [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) supplies a regulatory binding partner. Binding and [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) regulate catalytic competence, so detecting [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) [protein](protein-split.md) alone does not establish activity. The distinction makes a durable catalytic component reusable as the [cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md) changes its regulatory inputs.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition, Components of the Cell-Cycle Control System: “The Cell-Cycle Control System Is Based on Cyclically Activated Protein Kinases”, Figures 17-15–17-16](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/components-of-the-cell-cycle-control-system.md)

The [cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md) uses [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) activity as both an output and a regulatory input: activity promotes [Cdc25](cdc25.md) activation and suppresses [Wee1](wee1.md), reinforcing release from inhibitory [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md). The already described [cell-cycle hysteresis experiment](cell-cycle-hysteresis-experiment.md) tests how that output depends on history at matched [cyclin B](cyclin-b.md) input; the [histone H1 kinase assay](histone-h1-kinase-assay.md) below explains what its output measures. A binding diagram alone cannot establish [hysteresis](hysteresis.md).
