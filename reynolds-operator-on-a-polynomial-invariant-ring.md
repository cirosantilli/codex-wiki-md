# Reynolds operator on a polynomial invariant ring

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md)

In characteristic zero, averaging over a compact group, or over a Zariski-dense compact form of a complex reductive group, gives a degree-preserving projection onto the [polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md). Its module property is $\mathcal R(af)=a\mathcal R(f)$ for invariant $a$. This property turns finite ideal generation in the ambient polynomial ring into finite algebra generation of the invariant subring.
