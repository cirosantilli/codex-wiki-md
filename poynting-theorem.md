# Poynting theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md)

The [Poynting theorem](poynting-theorem.md) is the local [energy](energy-split.md) balance of classical [electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md). For vacuum field laws, with [current density](current-density.md) $\mathbf J$, the [electromagnetic energy](electromagnetic-energy.md) per unit volume is

$$
u=\frac{\epsilon_0|\mathbf E|^2}{2}+\frac{|\mathbf B|^2}{2\mu_0},
$$

and

$$
\partial_tu+\nabla\cdot\mathbf S=-\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E.
$$

Thus a decrease in local [electromagnetic energy](electromagnetic-energy.md) can supply outward [energy flux](energy-flux.md) or [work](work.md) on [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). The term $\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E$ is [power](power.md) transferred to matter per unit volume. Its sign matters: positive values remove field [energy](energy-split.md), while negative values describe matter supplying the field. The magnetic part of the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) supplies no work term.

For a fixed volume $V$, the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) gives the directly measurable balance

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\int_Vu\,dV
=-\int_{\partial V}\mathbf S\cdot\mathbf n\,dA
-\int_V\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E\,dV.
$$

For example, $2.0\,\mathrm W$ entering a region with $0.50\,\mathrm W$ leaving and $1.20\,\mathrm W$ transferred to matter means stored [electromagnetic energy](electromagnetic-energy.md) increases at $0.30\,\mathrm W$. In source-free vacuum only the boundary flux changes the stored [energy](energy-split.md); a nonzero local [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) can still have zero net closed-surface flux.

A brief proof takes the [dot product](dot-product.md) of the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) with $\mathbf E/\mu_0$ and uses [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) with $\mathbf B/\mu_0$. The identity $\nabla\cdot(\mathbf E\times\mathbf B)=\mathbf B\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf E)-\mathbf E\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf B)$ turns the remaining terms into the [divergence](divergence.md) of the [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md). [Feynman lectures, II.27.3: local field-energy conservation](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/II_27.html#Ch27-S3).
