# Poincare inequality with a partial Dirichlet boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md)

If $U$ is bounded and connected and a boundary portion $\Gamma_D$ has positive surface measure, then

$$
\lVert v\rVert_{L^2(U)}\leq C\lVert\nabla v\rVert_{L^2(U)}
$$

for every $v\in H^1(U)$ whose [trace](sobolev-trace-theorem.md) vanishes on $\Gamma_D$. Otherwise a normalized counterexample sequence, the [Rellich-Kondrashov compactness theorem](rellich-kondrashov-compactness-theorem-for-h01.md), and continuity of the trace would produce a nonzero constant with zero trace on $\Gamma_D$.
