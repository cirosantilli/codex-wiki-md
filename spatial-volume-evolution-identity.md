# Spatial volume evolution identity

↑ **Parent:** [Extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface](extrinsic-curvature-of-a-spatial-hypersurface.md)

In a [3+1 decomposition of spacetime](3-plus-1-decomposition-of-spacetime.md) with $K_{ij}=-(\partial_t\gamma_{ij}-\mathcal L_\beta\gamma_{ij})/(2\alpha)$, contract the metric evolution with $\gamma^{ij}$ and use the [Jacobi determinant derivative formula](jacobi-determinant-derivative-formula.md). Here $\gamma=\det\gamma_{ij}$, $K=\gamma^{ij}K_{ij}$, $\alpha$ is the [lapse function](lapse-function.md), and $\beta$ is the [shift vector](shift-vector.md). The identity separates spatial transport and coordinate compression from the geometric volume change caused by [extrinsic curvature](extrinsic-curvature.md).
