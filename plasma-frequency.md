# Plasma frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

The plasma frequency is the characteristic electrostatic oscillation frequency of a charged species displaced against a neutralizing background. For number density $n_s$, charge $q_s$ and mass $m_s$, the displayed formula uses SI units; in [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md) it is $\omega_{ps}=\sqrt{4\pi n_sq_s^2/m_s}$. The [ion skin depth](ion-skin-depth.md) is $c/\omega_{pi}$ for the ion species. Different species have different plasma frequencies.
