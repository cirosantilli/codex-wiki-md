# Intermembrane space

↑ **Parent:** [Mitochondria](mitochondria.md)

In a [mitochondrion](mitochondria.md), the [intermembrane space](intermembrane-space.md) lies between the [outer mitochondrial membrane](outer-mitochondrial-membrane.md) and [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md), extending into the interiors of [cristae](crista.md). It includes the side of the [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md) from which [protons](proton.md) return through [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) to the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md).

A useful sidedness check is to follow a single arrow: outward pumping goes from green to yellow in the diagram, while coupled return goes from yellow to green. These are opposing movements of [protons](proton.md), not a circuit in which [electrons](electron.md) return through [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md). The [mitochondrial respiratory chain](mitochondrial-respiratory-chain.md) and [chemiosmosis](chemiosmosis.md) articles distinguish the two ledgers.
