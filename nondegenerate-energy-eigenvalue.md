# Nondegenerate energy eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Time-independent perturbation theory](time-independent-perturbation-theory.md)

An [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue.md) is nondegenerate when its [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) is one-dimensional. Its normalized [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) is then unique up to a complex phase.
