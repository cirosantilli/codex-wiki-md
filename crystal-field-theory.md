# Crystal field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

[Crystal field theory](crystal-field-theory.md) models the effect of surrounding ligand charges or dipoles on [metal](metal.md) [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md). Directional repulsion splits the $d$ levels. Unlike [molecular orbital theory](molecular-orbital-theory.md), the simplest model does not explicitly construct covalent [metal](metal.md)–ligand bonds.
