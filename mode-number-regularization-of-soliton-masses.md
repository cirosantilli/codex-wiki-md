# Mode-number regularization of soliton masses

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop soliton mass correction](one-loop-soliton-mass-correction.md)

Compare the same total number of finite-box modes in the [soliton](soliton.md) and vacuum sectors before removing the [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md). A localized [bound state](bound-state.md) replaces a continuum mode, and its contribution must be retained. For the [Sine-Gordon kink fluctuation operator](sine-gordon-kink-fluctuation-operator.md) and the odd phase branch $\delta(k)=2\arctan(1/k)$, the bound translation mode replaces the free $k=0$ oscillator. Thus the complete oscillator correction is $-m/2-(m/2)\int dk\,\delta(k)k/[2\pi\sqrt{k^2+1}]$ in this convention. The continuum integral alone misses the finite $-m/2$ contribution.
