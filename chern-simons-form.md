# Chern-Simons form

↑ **Parent:** [Chern-Weil homomorphism](chern-weil-homomorphism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern–Simons_form)

A [Chern-Simons form](chern-simons-form.md) is an odd-degree transgression form whose [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) is an invariant polynomial in the [curvature form](curvature-form.md). For the trace polynomial of degree $k$, $d\omega_{2k-1}=\operatorname{Tr}(F^k)$; the [Chern-Simons 3-form](chern-simons-3-form.md) is the degree-three case.
