# Weighted gradient circulation identity

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md)

For sufficiently smooth scalar fields near an oriented [surface](topological-surface-split.md), the product rule for the [curl](curl.md) gives $\nabla\times(\phi\nabla\psi)=\phi\nabla\times\nabla\psi+\nabla\phi\times\nabla\psi=\nabla\phi\times\nabla\psi$. [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) then proves the displayed identity with consistent boundary orientation. A scalar multiple of a gradient need not itself be a gradient; the cross product of the two gradients detects its local circulation.
