# Affine exponential map

↑ **Parent:** [Affine connection](affine-connection.md)

For an [affine connection](affine-connection.md), let $\gamma_X$ be the affinely parametrized [geodesic](geodesic.md) starting at $p$ with velocity $X$. Define the exponential map by the displayed formula for initial velocities whose [geodesics](geodesic.md) exist until parameter one. It is defined near zero, has differential equal to the identity there, and obeys $\exp_p(tX)=\gamma_X(t)$ wherever defined. This construction requires no metric and extends the metric [exponential map](exponential-map-riemannian-geometry.md).
