# Open neighbourhood

↑ **Parent:** [Neighbourhood (mathematics)](neighbourhood-mathematics.md)

An [open neighbourhood](open-neighbourhood.md) of a point $x$ in a [topological space](topological-space.md) $(X,\mathcal T)$ is an [open set](open-set.md) $U$ containing $x$. A general [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) need only contain an [open neighbourhood](open-neighbourhood.md); it need not itself be [open](open-set.md). A finite [intersection](set-intersection.md) of [open neighbourhoods](open-neighbourhood.md) of the same point is again an [open neighbourhood](open-neighbourhood.md), by the [topology axioms](topology-axiom.md).
