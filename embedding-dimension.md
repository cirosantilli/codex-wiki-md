# Embedding dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent space of a local ring](cotangent-space-of-a-local-ring.md)

The embedding dimension of a [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md) $(R,\mathfrak m)$ is $\dim_{R/\mathfrak m}(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2)$. It counts the smallest number of local generators needed for the [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md). It is at least the [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md); equality characterizes a [regular local ring](regular-local-ring.md).
