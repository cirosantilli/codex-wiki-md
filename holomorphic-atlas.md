# Holomorphic atlas

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

An atlas of charts into $\mathbb C^n$ whose transition maps are [biholomorphic](biholomorphism.md). Their real [differentials](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) commute with multiplication by $i$, giving an [almost complex structure induced by a complex atlas](almost-complex-structure-induced-by-a-complex-atlas.md).
