# Magnetization of the quantum XX chain

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum XX chain](quantum-xx-chain.md)

For exchange $J>0$, the [Jordan–Wigner transformation](jordan-wigner-transformation.md) gives a band $\epsilon_k=h-J\cos k$. At zero [temperature](temperature-split.md), negative-energy states form a filled interval $|k|<\arccos(h/J)$. Since each [fermion](fermion.md) decreases total [magnetization](magnetization.md) by one, the thermodynamic [magnetization](magnetization.md) per site is $1/2-\arccos(h/J)/\pi$. It saturates at $1/2$ for $h\ge J$. A finite ring instead has discrete level crossings and parity-dependent [momentum](momentum-split.md) grids.
