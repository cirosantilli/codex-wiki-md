# Copernican principle

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological principle](cosmological-principle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copernican_principle)

Our observer location is not specially privileged. In cosmology this assumption allows [cosmological isotropy](cosmological-isotropy.md) observed around us to be interpreted statistically as [cosmological isotropy](cosmological-isotropy.md) around other typical observers. It is an assumption about typicality, not a deduction from one observer's sky or from the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) alone.
