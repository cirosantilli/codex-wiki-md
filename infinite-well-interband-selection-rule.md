# Infinite-well interband selection rule

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum well](quantum-well.md)

In an [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md), [electron](electron.md) and hole envelopes are the same normalized [sine](sine.md) functions even when their [effective masses](band-effective-mass.md) differ. In the electric dipole and envelope approximations, their overlap is $\int F_{n^{\prime}}^*(z)F_n(z)\,dz=\delta_{n^{\prime}n}$. The same-index rule is an ideal-envelope rule, separate from Bloch-state [polarization](polarization-waves.md) selection rules.
