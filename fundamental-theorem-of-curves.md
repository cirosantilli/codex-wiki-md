# Fundamental theorem of curves

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_theorem_of_curves)

Suitable curvature functions determine a regular Frenet curve up to a Euclidean rigid motion. In the plane one signed curvature suffices; in three dimensions positive curvature and torsion determine a unit-speed curve, as in the [fundamental theorem of regular space curves](fundamental-theorem-of-regular-space-curves.md). The result is local on the parameter interval and assumes the regularity needed by the frame equations.
