# Rashba spin texture

↑ **Parent:** [Rashba effect](rashba-effect.md)

For $H_R=\lambda(k_x\sigma_y-k_y\sigma_x)$ with $\lambda>0$, the lower- and upper-band [spinors](spinor.md) of unit [norm](norm.md) can be chosen as

$$
u_-\!=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}(k_y+ik_x)/k\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad u_+\!=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}-(k_y+ik_x)/k\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad k>0.
$$

Their [spin expectation values](spin-expectation-value.md) are $\langle\mathbf S\rangle_\pm=\pm(\hbar/2)(-k_y,k_x,0)/k$. They are tangential to [circles](circle.md) of constant [momentum](momentum-split.md) magnitude, opposite between branches, and reversed at opposite momenta as required by [time-reversal symmetry](t-symmetry.md). When $E_F>0$, the outer contour belongs to the lower branch and the inner one to the upper branch. For $E_F<0$ the two [Fermi contours](fermi-contour.md) both belong to the lower branch and have the same tangential [spin](spin.md) orientation at a given angle in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md). At $k=0$ the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) has [quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md) in [spin](spin.md), and no unique [spin texture](spin-texture.md) direction is selected.
