# Isotropic spin-wave vacuum energy comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md)

The polarized [ground state](ground-state.md) of an isotropic [Heisenberg model](heisenberg-model.md) with ferromagnetic exchange is an exact state. Its normal-ordered harmonic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) has no pair-creation terms, no vacuum magnon occupation and no additional zero-point shift. An antiferromagnetic [Néel state](neel-state.md) instead generates anomalous terms, a squeezed [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) vacuum, a negative energy correction and reduced staggered magnetization. Neither statement licenses omitting normal-ordering constants from an arbitrary spin-wave model with anisotropy or a different reference state.
