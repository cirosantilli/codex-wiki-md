# Overdispersion of nondegenerate mixed Poisson counts

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed Poisson distribution](mixed-poisson-distribution.md)

If $N\mid L$ has Poisson mean $L$, [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) and the [law of total variance](law-of-total-variance.md) give the display. A positive [variance](variance-split.md) of the random mean prevents the unconditional count from being Poisson. For Poisson parents of density $\lambda$ and offspring mean $\mu$, the count in $A$ has excess [variance](variance-split.md) $\lambda\mu^2\int\kappa(A-z)^2dz$, which is positive for a bounded positive-area set and a nontrivial model.
