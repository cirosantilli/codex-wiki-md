# Infinite-dimensional Hamiltonian integrability

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable systems](integrable-systems-split.md)

For a field evolving by a [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md), infinite-dimensional integrability combines sufficiently rich commuting conservation laws with a mechanism such as the [inverse scattering transform](inverse-scattering-transform.md) or a spectral coordinate system that linearizes evolution. [Lax equations](isospectral-lax-equation.md) supply spectral invariants; [Lenard-Magri recursions](lenard-magri-recursion.md) supply commuting [Hamiltonians](hamiltonian.md) when their solvability conditions hold. Infinite many [conserved quantities](conserved-quantity.md) alone do not prove completeness or an infinite-dimensional action-angle theorem: the [function](function-split.md) space, [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) and analytic independence all matter.
