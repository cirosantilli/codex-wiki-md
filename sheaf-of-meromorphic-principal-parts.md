# Sheaf of meromorphic principal parts

↑ **Parent:** [Principal part of a meromorphic function](principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md)

On a [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md), the quotient sheaf $\mathcal P=\mathcal M_X/\mathcal O_X$ records the finite negative [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) tails of meromorphic germs. At $x$, a local coordinate $t$ identifies its stalk with $\bigoplus_{m\geq1}\mathbb C t^{-m}$. As a sheaf it is the [direct sum of sheaves](direct-sum-of-sheaves.md) $\bigoplus_x(i_x)_*\mathcal P_x$. Global sections prescribe locally finite families of principal parts, possibly infinite on a noncompact surface. The [connecting homomorphism](connecting-homomorphism.md) to $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)$ is exactly the obstruction to a global meromorphic function with those parts and no extra poles.
