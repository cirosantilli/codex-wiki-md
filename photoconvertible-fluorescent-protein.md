# Photoconvertible fluorescent protein

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescent protein](fluorescent-protein.md)

A [fluorescent protein](fluorescent-protein.md) whose emission can be changed by controlled illumination. Conversion of one imaged progenitor enables independent spatial [lineage tracing](lineage-tracing.md), limited by dilution, turnover and inadvertent conversion of neighbouring [cells](biological-cell-split.md).
