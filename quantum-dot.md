# Quantum dot

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

A [quantum dot](quantum-dot.md) confines [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md) in all three spatial directions, producing discrete [energy levels](energy-level.md). A [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) island surrounded by a larger-[band gap](band-gap.md) material is one realization. Its optical excitation and [electron-hole recombination](electron-hole-recombination.md) depend on confinement, [Coulomb interactions](coulomb-interaction.md) and [electric-dipole selection rules](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md).
