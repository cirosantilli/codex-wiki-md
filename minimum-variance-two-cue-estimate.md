# Minimum-variance two-cue estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Reliability-weighted cue integration](reliability-weighted-cue-integration.md)

Let unbiased independent estimates $X_1,X_2$ have [variances](variance-split.md) $a,b>0$. The [variance](variance-split.md) of $wX_1+(1-w)X_2$ is $w^2a+(1-w)^2b$. Its derivative is $2w(a+b)-2b$ and its second derivative is $2(a+b)>0$, proving the unique optimum $w=b/(a+b)$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $ab/(a+b)$. Independent Gaussian likelihoods give the same estimate by differentiating $(x_1-x)^2/a+(x_2-x)^2/b$. With [covariance](covariance.md) $c$, the [variance](variance-split.md) becomes $w^2a+(1-w)^2b+2w(1-w)c$, and the unconstrained optimum is $(b-c)/(a+b-2c)$ when the denominator is positive; it need not lie between zero and one.
