# Dichlorophenolindophenol

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dichlorophenolindophenol)

[DCPIP](dichlorophenolindophenol.md) is an artificial [electron acceptor](electron-acceptor.md) used in the [chloroplast dye-reduction experiment](chloroplast-dye-reduction-experiment.md). Under its assay conditions, the oxidized dye is blue and its [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) removes that blue color.
