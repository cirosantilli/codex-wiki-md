# Symmetrization postulate

↑ **Parent:** [Indistinguishable particles](indistinguishable-particles.md)

The complete state of identical [bosons](boson.md) is symmetric under exchange, while that of identical [fermions](fermion.md) is antisymmetric. The condition includes [spin](spin.md) and other internal variables as well as spatial coordinates.
