# Two-block extremisers for the cross-Sperner inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-Sperner inequality](cross-sperner-inequality.md)

Choose disjoint [subsets](subset.md) $K_1,K_2\subseteq[n]$ of size $k\geq1$ with $2k\leq n$. Let $\mathcal A$ consist of [subsets](subset.md) containing all of $K_1$ and none of $K_2$; let $\mathcal B$ consist of [subsets](subset.md) omitting at least one point of $K_1$ and containing at least one point of $K_2$. These form a [cross-Sperner family](cross-sperner-family.md) pair, with sizes $2^{n-2k}$ and $(2^k-1)^2 2^{n-2k}$. Their square roots sum to $2^{n/2}$, proving sharpness of the [Cross-Sperner inequality](cross-sperner-inequality.md). The free coordinates outside the two blocks account for the factor $2^{n-2k}$.
