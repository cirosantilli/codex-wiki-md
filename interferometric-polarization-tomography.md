# Interferometric polarization tomography

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state tomography](quantum-state-tomography.md)

A [polarizing beam splitter](polarizing-beam-splitter.md) converts a [photon polarization](photon-polarization.md) [qubit](qubit.md) to two paths. Rotating one arm's [photon polarization](photon-polarization.md) to match the other and recombining at a balanced [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md) gives $P_1(\theta)=1/2+\operatorname{Re}(\alpha^*\beta e^{i\theta})$. Measurements at phases zero and $\pi/2$ recover the transverse [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) components; direct counts in the two separated arms recover the population difference. All three components, rather than a fringe alone, determine an arbitrary [photon polarization](photon-polarization.md) [density matrix](density-matrix.md).
