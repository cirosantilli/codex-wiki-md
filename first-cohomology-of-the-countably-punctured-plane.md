# First cohomology of the countably punctured plane

↑ **Parent:** [Universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md)

The connected open set $X=\mathbb R^2\setminus\{(n,0):n\geq1\}$ retracts onto a locally finite graph having one independent loop around each puncture. Hence $H_1(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\bigoplus_{n\geq1}\mathbb Z$, while the [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) gives

$$
H^1(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\operatorname{Hom}\left(\bigoplus_{n\geq1}\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z\right)\cong\prod_{n\geq1}\mathbb Z,
$$

an uncountable group.
