# Orientifold

↑ **Parent:** [String theory](string-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientifold)

A string construction combining worldsheet orientation reversal with a suitable spacetime involution. It projects the spectrum and introduces fixed-locus sources whose charges must be treated together with branes and fluxes. In compactifications, such projections can reduce [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) and modify the gauge and matter spectrum. Charge cancellation is part of consistency.
