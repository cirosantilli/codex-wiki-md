# Common ingroup identity model

↑ **Parent:** [Recategorization](recategorization.md)

An account of [prejudice](prejudice.md) reduction in which former [outgroup](social-outgroup.md) members become included in a common [ingroup](ingroup.md). The proposed mechanism extends favorable [ingroup](ingroup.md) responses across a previous boundary. Whether the common identity recognizes or suppresses subgroup identity is a substantive boundary condition.
