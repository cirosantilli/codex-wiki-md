# Euler angles

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_angles)

[Euler angles](euler-angles.md) describe the orientation of a [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics.md) through three successive angular rotations. Their convention specifies the axes and order of the rotations. For a symmetric top, a common convention takes $\theta$ as the inclination, $\phi$ as precession and $\psi$ as spin about the body axis.
