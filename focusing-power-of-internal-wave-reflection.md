# Focusing power of internal-wave reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Reflection of an internal-wave ray](reflection-of-an-internal-wave-ray.md)

For an [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) ray at angle $\theta$ to the horizontal reflecting from a slope at angle $\alpha$, conservation of tangential [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) gives

$$
\gamma=\frac{k_{\rm reflected}}{k_{\rm incident}}
=\frac{\sin(\theta+\alpha)}{\sin(\theta-\alpha)}.
$$

At a [subcritical internal-wave reflection](subcritical-internal-wave-reflection.md), $\gamma>1$: the reflected wavelength shortens and its wavelength-averaged [energy density](energy-density.md) increases by $\gamma^2$.
