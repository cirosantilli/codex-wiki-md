# Homogeneous ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)

An [ideal](ideal.md) in a [graded ring](graded-ring.md) is homogeneous when it is generated by [homogeneous elements](homogeneous-polynomial.md), equivalently when every homogeneous component of each of its elements also belongs to the ideal.
