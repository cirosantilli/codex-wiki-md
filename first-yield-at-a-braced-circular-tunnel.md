# First yield at a braced circular tunnel

↑ **Parent:** [Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion](mohr-coulomb-failure-criterion.md)

For a circular tunnel of radius $a$ in a plane-strain elastic medium, isotropic far compression $p_\infty$ and wall bracing pressure $P$ give $\tau_{rr}=-p_\infty-(P-p_\infty)a^2/r^2$ and $\tau_{\theta\theta}=-p_\infty+(P-p_\infty)a^2/r^2$. The [Mohr circle](mohr-s-circle.md) center is $-p_\infty$ and its radius is $|P-p_\infty|a^2/r^2$. Its maximum is at the wall, proving that first yield occurs there. Cohesionless frictional tangency requires this radius to equal $p_\infty\sin\phi$. Lowering $P$ from hydrostatic confinement yields the displayed threshold and stress ratio $\tau_{\theta\theta}/\tau_{rr}=(1+\sin\phi)/(1-\sin\phi)$. Increasing bracing instead reaches $P=p_\infty(1+\sin\phi)$ with the reciprocal ratio. The two cases must not be confused when identifying the first loading threshold.
