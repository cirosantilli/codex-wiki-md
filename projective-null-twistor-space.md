# Projective null twistor space

↑ **Parent:** [Null twistor](null-twistor.md)

The five-real-dimensional space of projective [null twistors](null-twistor.md). A projective line lies entirely in it precisely when its corresponding two-plane is totally isotropic for the [twistor Hermitian form](twistor-hermitian-form.md). Such lines represent real points of [Minkowski conformal compactification](minkowski-conformal-compactification.md). Two independent null twistors lie on one such line exactly when $h(X,Y)=0$.
