# Ground term

↑ **Parent:** [First-order term](first-order-term.md)

A [first-order term](first-order-term.md) containing no variables. Constant symbols are [ground terms](ground-term.md); applying a function symbol to [ground terms](ground-term.md) produces another [ground term](ground-term.md).
