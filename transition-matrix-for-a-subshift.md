# Transition matrix for a subshift

↑ **Parent:** [Subshift of finite type](subshift-of-finite-type.md)

The [adjacency matrix of a directed graph](adjacency-matrix-of-a-directed-graph.md) whose vertices are symbols and whose directed edges are the permitted consecutive pairs. Its entries indicate permission. In comparison, the [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) of a [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) records transition probabilities.
