<h1 id="schreier-s-lemma">Schreier's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-index subgroup quasi-isometry](finite-index-subgroup-quasi-isometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schreier's_lemma)

If a group $G$ is generated by $S$, a subgroup $H$ has a set $T$ of right-coset representatives, and $\overline{ts}$ denotes the representative of $Hts$, then $H$ is generated by the Schreier generators

$$
ts\overline{ts}^{-1}
\qquad(t\in T, s\in S).
$$

In particular, every finite-index subgroup of a finitely generated group is finitely generated.
