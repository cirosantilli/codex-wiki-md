# Marginal ice zone

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marginal_ice_zone)

The marginal ice zone is the transition between open ocean and compact [sea ice](sea-ice.md), where interacting [ice floes](ice-floe.md) and [surface gravity waves](surface-gravity-wave.md) can dominate the mechanical evolution.
