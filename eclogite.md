# Eclogite

↑ **Parent:** [Petrology](petrology.md)

High-[pressure](pressure-split.md) metamorphic rock with a major assemblage of [garnet](garnet.md) and omphacitic clinopyroxene, generally lacking [plagioclase](plagioclase.md). It can derive from mafic [crust](planetary-crust.md) by several burial mechanisms.
