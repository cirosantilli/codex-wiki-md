# Electron-donating group

↑ **Parent:** [Substituent](substituent.md)

A [substituent](substituent.md) that increases [electron](electron.md) density in a specified molecular region through induction or [chemical resonance](resonance-chemistry.md). Donation into an [arene](arene.md) can affect an attached [metal](metal.md) and its other [coordination ligands](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md).
