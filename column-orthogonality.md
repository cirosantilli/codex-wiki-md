# Column orthogonality

↑ **Parent:** [Character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md)

For a [finite group](finite-group.md), the sum of products of [irreducible character](irreducible-character.md) values is zero when $g,h$ are not conjugate, and equals $|C_G(g)|$ when $g,h$ are conjugate. To prove this from [row orthogonality relations for a character table](row-orthogonality-relations-for-a-character-table.md), choose representatives $g_j$ of its [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) and form $U_{ij}=\sqrt{|g_j^G|/|G|}\,\chi_i(g_j)$. Row orthogonality says $UU^*=I$. There are equally many [irreducible characters](irreducible-character.md) and [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md), so this is a square [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) and $U^*U=I$ as well. Its column equations give the result, using $|G|/|g_j^G|=|C_G(g_j)|$.
