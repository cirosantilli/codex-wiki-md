<h1 id="kahler-differentials-of-a-polynomial-algebra">Kähler differentials of a polynomial algebra</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler differential](kahler-differential.md)

The [Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md) of a [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) are free on the differentials of its variables. Assigning arbitrary values to these generators uniquely defines a [derivation](derivation-of-an-algebra.md). Applying the [Conormal exact sequence for Kähler differentials](conormal-exact-sequence-for-kahler-differentials.md) to a quotient gives its presentation by the formal derivatives of its defining equations.
