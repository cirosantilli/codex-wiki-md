# Nested hydrogen-helium ionization zones

↑ **Parent:** [Strömgren sphere](stromgren-sphere.md)

In a uniform sharp-front approximation, an inner $\mathrm{He}^{++}$ zone of radius $R_2$ lies inside a $\mathrm{He}^{+}$ shell ending at $R_1$, within the hydrogen ionization front $R_H$. For nuclear densities $n_H,n_{He}$, the [Electron](electron.md) densities are $n_H+2n_{He}$, $n_H+n_{He}$ and $n_H$ in the three zones. Thus the hydrogen recombination integral is $(4\pi/3)\beta_Hn_H[n_HR_H^3+n_{He}(R_1^3+R_2^3)]$. The helium integrals are $(4\pi/3)\beta_{HeI}n_{He}(n_H+n_{He})(R_1^3-R_2^3)$ and $(4\pi/3)\beta_{HeII}n_{He}(n_H+2n_{He})R_2^3$. These are balanced against species-specific effective photon budgets, not three independently reused cumulative stellar photon counts.
