# Global Markov property of a directed acyclic graph

↑ **Parent:** [D-separation](d-separation.md)

The global Markov property of a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md) says that d-separation of vertex sets $A$ and $B$ by $S$ implies conditional independence of the corresponding random vectors given the variables at $S$.
