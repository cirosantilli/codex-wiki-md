# Random k-out graph

↑ **Parent:** [Random graph](random-graph.md)

Each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) independently chooses $k$ distinct other [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) uniformly, and an undirected [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) exists when either endpoint chooses the other. This has dependent undirected [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and is not a [binomial random graph](binomial-random-graph.md). Coupling the choices as the first $k$ entries of [independent](independent-random-variables.md) random permutations makes the [graphs](graph-split.md) increasing in $k$.
