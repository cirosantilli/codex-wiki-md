<h1 id="clark-wells-model-of-social-anxiety-disorder">Clark–Wells model of social anxiety disorder</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Social anxiety disorder](social-anxiety-disorder.md)

A cognitive account in which feared evaluation promotes [self-focused attention](self-focused-attention.md) and an internally generated threatening impression of the self. [Safety behaviors](safety-behavior.md) can distort an interaction and prevent disconfirmation; anticipatory and [post-event processing](post-event-processing.md) can maintain the threat. Developed by [David M. Clark](david-m-clark.md) and [Adrian Wells](adrian-wells.md), the account generates separate experimental questions about [attention](attention.md), behaviour and interpretation. [Adolescent](adolescent.md) applicability requires developmentally valid measures, rather than assuming an adult [self-report](self-report.md) vocabulary.[Clinical Child and Family Psychology Review 21, 388–414; cognitive model and developmental qualifications](understanding-social-anxiety-disorder-in-adolescents-and-improving-treatment-outcomes-applying-the-cognitive-model-of-clark-and-wells-1995-by-eleanor-leigh-and-david-m-clark.md)
