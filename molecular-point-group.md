# Molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The [point group](point-group.md) of orthogonal spatial transformations that preserve a fixed [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md), including the positions and chemical identities of all nuclei. Changing a conformation can change the [molecular point group](molecular-point-group.md) even when the molecular connectivity is unchanged. Its [group representations](group-representation.md) on [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) depend on orbital signs and rotations as well as the permutation of centres.
