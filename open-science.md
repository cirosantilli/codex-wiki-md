# Open science

↑ **Parent:** [Science](science.md)

Practices making research plans, materials, analyses and outputs inspectable and reusable where ethically appropriate. [Preregistration](preregistration.md), transparent reporting and executable analysis support distinct aspects of openness. Protecting confidentiality can require restricted data access rather than unrestricted publication.
