# Direct sum of group representations

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

For [group representations](group-representation.md) $R:G\to GL(V)$ and $S:G\to GL(W)$, their direct sum acts on the [direct sum of vector spaces](direct-sum.md) by $(R\oplus S)(g)(v,w)=(R(g)v,S(g)w)$. Its [character](character-of-a-representation.md) and [Dynkin index](dynkin-index.md), when defined, are the sums of those of the two summands.
