# Maximum-separation inertia bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Pairwise moment-of-inertia identity](pairwise-moment-of-inertia-identity.md)

Let $R$ be the actual largest pairwise separation of positive [masses](mass.md) in their [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) frame. The [pairwise moment-of-inertia identity](pairwise-moment-of-inertia-identity.md) gives $m_{(1)}m_{(2)}R^2/(2M)\leq I\leq R^2\sum_{j<k}m_jm_k/(2M)$. Consequently the scalar inertia is comparable to $R^2$, independently of configuration.
