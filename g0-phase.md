# G0 phase

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G0_phase)

[G0 phase](g0-phase.md) describes withdrawal from the division cycle. Some [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) re-enter [G1 phase](g1-phase.md); others remain nondividing. [Metabolism](metabolism.md) and [gene expression](gene-expression.md) can continue. [Biology 2e, §10.2, G0 Phase](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/the-cell-cycle.md) In the ordinary [diploid](diploid.md) case, $2C$ alone cannot distinguish [G0 phase](g0-phase.md) from [G1 phase](g1-phase.md).
