# Pseudotensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor](tensor.md)

Under an orthogonal coordinate transformation $R$, a Cartesian pseudotensor has the usual [tensor](tensor.md) transformation law multiplied by $\det R$. Thus proper [rotation matrices](rotation-matrix.md) act as on ordinary tensors, while an orientation-reversing change contributes an extra minus sign. Interpreted as a rank-three pseudotensor, the [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md) has the same numerical components in every orthonormal frame, because its ordinary three-index transformation already contributes a factor $\det R$.
