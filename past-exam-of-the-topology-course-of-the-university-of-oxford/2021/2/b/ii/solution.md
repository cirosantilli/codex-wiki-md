<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

**False**, with “[disjoint union](../../../../../disjoint-union.md)” understood as a [topological disjoint union](../../../../../topological-disjoint-union.md). Take the [rational numbers](../../../../../rational-number.md) $\mathbb Q$ with the usual [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) from the [real line](../../../../../real-line.md).

Every [connected subset](../../../../../connected-subset.md) of $\mathbb Q$ has at most one point. Indeed, if it contained $r<s$, choose an [irrational number](../../../../../irrational-number.md) $t\in(r,s)$. The two relatively [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md)

$$
\mathbb Q\cap(-\infty,t),\qquad \mathbb Q\cap(t,\infty)
$$

partition $\mathbb Q$ and separate any [subset](../../../../../subset.md) containing both $r$ and $s$. Hence the [connected components](../../../../../connected-component.md) of $\mathbb Q$ are [singletons](../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md).

If a [topological disjoint union](../../../../../topological-disjoint-union.md) of nonempty [connected spaces](../../../../../connected-space.md) were [homeomorphic](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) to $\mathbb Q$, the [image](../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of each summand would be [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md), hence a [singleton](../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md). Every summand would therefore be a one-point [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md). Their [topological disjoint union](../../../../../topological-disjoint-union.md) is a [discrete space](../../../../../discrete-space.md), since each summand is [open](../../../../../open-set.md). But $\mathbb Q$ is not a [discrete space](../../../../../discrete-space.md): for each $r\in\mathbb Q$, the distinct [rational numbers](../../../../../rational-number.md) $r+1/n$ approach $r$, so $r$ is not an [isolated point](../../../../../isolated-point.md). This is a contradiction. The [set](../../../../../set-split.md) decomposition into [connected components](../../../../../connected-component.md) always exists; giving those pieces the [topological disjoint union](../../../../../topological-disjoint-union.md) topology need not recover the original [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md).
