<h1 id="2/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Let $W=(\mathbb N\times S^1)/A$, and choose in each [circle](../../../../../circle.md) the point opposite $o$:

$$
z_n=(0,2/n)\in C_n,\qquad B=\{z_n:n\geq1\}\subseteq X.
$$

In the [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) from the [Euclidean plane](../../../../../euclidean-plane.md), the [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) $z_n$ converges to $o$, which does not belong to $B$. Thus $B$ is not [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in $X$.

On the other hand, let $D=\bar f^{-1}(B)\subseteq W$. With the [quotient map](../../../../../quotient-map.md) $q$ from part (i),

$$
q^{-1}(D)=\mathbb N\times\{(0,1)\}.
$$

This is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in $\mathbb N\times S^1$: its [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) is the [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $\mathbb N\times(S^1\setminus\{(0,1)\})$. The definition of the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md), applied to [complements](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md), says that $D$ is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in $W$. A [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) would carry this [closed set](../../../../../closed-set.md) to the [closed set](../../../../../closed-set.md) $B$, a contradiction. Therefore **$\bar f$ is not a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md)**.

The obstruction occurs at the common point. The [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $W\setminus D$ contains the collapsed point, but its [image](../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) $X\setminus B$ contains no [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $o$, since $z_n\to o$. The [wedge sum](../../../../../wedge-sum.md) topology allows a separate small [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) on each [circle](../../../../../circle.md); a [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $o$ in the [Hawaiian earring](../../../../../hawaiian-earring.md) must contain all sufficiently small whole [circles](../../../../../circle.md).
