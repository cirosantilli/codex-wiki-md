↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

For the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) model of the [real projective plane](../../../../../real-projective-plane.md), let

$$
S^2=\{u\in\mathbb R^3:\|u\|=1\},\qquad u\sim_S v\Longleftrightarrow v=u\text{ or }v=-u.
$$

Then $\mathbb{RP}^2=S^2/{\sim_S}$ with the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md): each [equivalence class](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) is an [antipodal pair](../../../../../antipodal-pair.md).

For the [closed disc](../../../../../closed-disc.md) model, let

$$
D^2=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:x^2+y^2\leq1\},\qquad u\sim_D v\Longleftrightarrow u=v\text{ or }(u,v\in\partial D^2\text{ and }v=-u).
$$

Give $D^2/{\sim_D}$ the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md). **Only [antipodal pairs](../../../../../antipodal-pair.md) on the [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) [circle](../../../../../circle.md) are identified in the [closed disc](../../../../../closed-disc.md) model; interior points remain distinct.**
