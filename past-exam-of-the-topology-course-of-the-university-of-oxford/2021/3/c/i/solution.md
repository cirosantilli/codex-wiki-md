<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

A complete pairing of the six [polygon edges](../../../../../side-of-a-polygon.md) leaves three edges and one face as [CW cells](../../../../../topological-cell.md), together with the vertex [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md). If $V$ denotes their number, then $V\geq1$ and the [polygonal-schema Euler count](../../../../../polygonal-schema-euler-count.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\chi=V-3+1=V-2\geq-1.}
$$

A [closed orientable surface](../../../../../closed-orientable-surface.md) of [genus](../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) two has [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) $2-2\cdot2=-2$. Since the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) is a [topological invariant](../../../../../topological-invariant.md), **no complete pairing of a [hexagon](../../../../../hexagon.md) can give that [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md)**. Equivalently, the [polygonal-schema Euler count](../../../../../polygonal-schema-euler-count.md) bounds the [genus](../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) of an [orientable](../../../../../orientable-surface.md) result by $2g\leq3$, so $g\leq1$.
