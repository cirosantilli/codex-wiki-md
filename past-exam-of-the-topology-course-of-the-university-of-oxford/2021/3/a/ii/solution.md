<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Let $o=(0,0)$ be the common point of the two [circles](../../../../../circle.md). For sufficiently small $\varepsilon>0$, the [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md)

$$
W=X\cap B_\varepsilon(o)
$$

has the following local structure: $W\setminus\{o\}$ consists of four disjoint [open arcs](../../../../../open-arc.md), an upper and a lower branch from each [circle](../../../../../circle.md). These are its four [connected components](../../../../../connected-component.md), each [open](../../../../../open-set.md) and [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) relative to $W\setminus\{o\}$.

Suppose a [coordinate chart](../../../../../manifold-chart.md) of some [dimension of a manifold](../../../../../dimension-of-a-manifold.md) $n$ existed at $o$. The case $n=0$ would make $o$ an [isolated point](../../../../../isolated-point.md), which it is not. For $n\geq1$, shrink the chart inside $W$ so that its [image](../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) is an [open ball](../../../../../open-ball.md) centred at the [image](../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of $o$. Its [domain of a function](../../../../../domain-of-a-function.md) $V$ is an [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $o$, so it meets all four arms of $W$. Intersecting those arms with $V\setminus\{o\}$ gives four disjoint nonempty [clopen sets](../../../../../clopen-set.md) partitioning $V\setminus\{o\}$; hence there are at least four [connected components](../../../../../connected-component.md).

But a [punctured ball](../../../../../punctured-ball.md) in $\mathbb R$ has exactly two [connected components](../../../../../connected-component.md), and a [punctured ball](../../../../../punctured-ball.md) in $\mathbb R^n$ for $n\geq2$ is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md). A [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) preserves [connected components](../../../../../connected-component.md), giving a contradiction in every [dimension of a manifold](../../../../../dimension-of-a-manifold.md). Thus **$X$ is not a [topological manifold](../../../../../topological-manifold-split.md)**. The argument uses arbitrarily small [coordinate charts](../../../../../manifold-chart.md); the fact that the whole $X\setminus\{o\}$ has two pieces alone would not be a local obstruction.
