<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) and [second countable](../../../../../second-countable-space.md), since both properties pass to [topological subspaces](../../../../../topological-subspace.md) of the [Euclidean plane](../../../../../euclidean-plane.md). To exhibit its local [coordinate charts](../../../../../manifold-chart.md), consider the four [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [semicircles](../../../../../semicircle.md)

$$
U_{y,+}=S^1\cap\{y>0\},\quad U_{y,-}=S^1\cap\{y<0\},\quad U_{x,+}=S^1\cap\{x>0\},\quad U_{x,-}=S^1\cap\{x<0\}.
$$

They cover $S^1$. On $U_{y,\pm}$, the [projection map](../../../../../projection-map.md) $(x,y)\mapsto x$ is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) onto the [open interval](../../../../../open-interval.md) $(-1,1)$, with [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) [inverse function](../../../../../inverse-function.md)

$$
t\longmapsto(t,\pm\sqrt{1-t^2}).
$$

On $U_{x,\pm}$, use $(x,y)\mapsto y$, with [inverse function](../../../../../inverse-function.md) $t\mapsto(\pm\sqrt{1-t^2},t)$. These [coordinate charts](../../../../../manifold-chart.md) show that **$S^1$ is a one-dimensional [topological manifold](../../../../../topological-manifold-split.md) with empty [manifold boundary](../../../../../manifold-boundary.md).**
