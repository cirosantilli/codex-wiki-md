<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Label the [pentagon](../../../../../pentagon.md) [polygon vertices](../../../../../vertex-of-a-polygon.md) clockwise from the upper left as

$$
v_0=(0,2),\quad v_1=(2,2),\quad v_2=(2,0),\quad v_3=(1,0),\quad v_4=(0,1).
$$

Use $a$ for the upward single arrows and $b$ for the rightward double arrows in the original diagram. Their endpoint identifications are

$$
\begin{aligned}
a:&\quad v_4\sim v_2,\quad v_0\sim v_1,\\
b:&\quad v_0\sim v_3,\quad v_1\sim v_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

All five [polygon vertices](../../../../../vertex-of-a-polygon.md) are therefore identified. The remaining diagonal [polygon edge](../../../../../side-of-a-polygon.md), from $v_3$ to $v_4$, has its endpoints identified and is the only [boundary component of a surface](../../../../../boundary-component-of-a-surface.md).

The paired [polygon edges](../../../../../side-of-a-polygon.md) join half-[disc](../../../../../topological-disc.md) [neighbourhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) into [disc](../../../../../topological-disc.md) [neighbourhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md). At the common vertex, the five corner sectors join in one chain whose two free ends lie on the unpaired [polygon edge](../../../../../side-of-a-polygon.md): its [vertex link in a polygonal schema](../../../../../vertex-link-in-a-polygonal-schema.md) is a [closed interval](../../../../../closed-real-interval.md). Hence that point has a half-[disc](../../../../../topological-disc.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md). This verifies that the [quotient space](../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) is a [surface with boundary](../../../../../surface-with-boundary.md), rather than merely counting its [CW cells](../../../../../topological-cell.md). The paired [polygon edges](../../../../../side-of-a-polygon.md) are oppositely traversed around the [polygon](../../../../../polygon.md), so this [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) is [orientable](../../../../../orientable-surface.md).

Its [CW cells](../../../../../topological-cell.md) comprise one vertex, three edges (the $a$ pair, the $b$ pair, and the free [polygon edge](../../../../../side-of-a-polygon.md)), and one face. Therefore

$$
\chi=1-3+1=-1,\qquad b=1,\qquad 2-2g-b=-1\Longrightarrow g=1.
$$

The [classification of compact surfaces](../../../../../classification-of-compact-surfaces.md) identifies it as **a [torus](../../../../../torus.md) with an [open disc](../../../../../open-disc.md) removed**.
