<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Label the [hexagon](../../../../../hexagon.md) [polygon vertices](../../../../../vertex-of-a-polygon.md) $v_0,\ldots,v_5$ in the direction in which its boundary word is $abca^{-1}b^{-1}c^{-1}$, with the first side running from $v_0$ to $v_1$. Matching the endpoints according to the arrows gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
a:&\quad v_0\sim v_4,\quad v_1\sim v_3,\\
b:&\quad v_1\sim v_5,\quad v_2\sim v_4,\\
c:&\quad v_2\sim v_0,\quad v_3\sim v_5.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the two vertex [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) are $\{v_0,v_2,v_4\}$ and $\{v_1,v_3,v_5\}$.

The [polygonal schema](../../../../../polygonal-schema.md) gives a [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) with empty [manifold boundary](../../../../../manifold-boundary.md). Interior points have [disc](../../../../../topological-disc.md) [neighbourhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md); a paired [polygon edge](../../../../../side-of-a-polygon.md) glues two half-[disc](../../../../../topological-disc.md) [neighbourhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) into a [disc](../../../../../topological-disc.md). At each of the two vertex [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md), its three corner sectors join cyclically, so the [vertex link in a polygonal schema](../../../../../vertex-link-in-a-polygonal-schema.md) is a [circle](../../../../../circle.md), again giving a [disc](../../../../../topological-disc.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md). Pairing every label once positively and once negatively makes these gluings consistent with an [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md), so the resulting [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) is [orientable](../../../../../orientable-surface.md).

The resulting [CW complex](../../../../../cw-complex.md) has [CW cells](../../../../../topological-cell.md) comprising two vertices, three edges and one face. Its [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) is

$$
\chi=V-E+F=2-3+1=0.
$$

By the [classification of compact surfaces](../../../../../classification-of-compact-surfaces.md), a [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) [closed orientable surface](../../../../../closed-orientable-surface.md) with $\chi=0$ has [genus](../../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$ satisfying $2-2g=0$, hence $g=1$. **The [quotient space](../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) is a [torus](../../../../../torus.md).**
