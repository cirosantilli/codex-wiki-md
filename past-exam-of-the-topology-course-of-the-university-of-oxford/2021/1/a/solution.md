<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [a](../a.md)

The two conditions for a [basis of a topology](../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) both hold. First, every [real number](../../../../real-number.md) $x$ belongs to $[x,x+1)$, so the proposed basic [intervals](../../../../interval-mathematics.md) cover the [real line](../../../../real-line.md). Second, if

$$
x\in[a,b)\cap[c,d),
$$

then $\max(a,c)\leq x<\min(b,d)$, and

$$
[a,b)\cap[c,d)=[\max(a,c),\min(b,d))
$$

is itself one of the proposed basic [intervals](../../../../interval-mathematics.md). Thus every point in an [intersection](../../../../set-intersection.md) of two basic [sets](../../../../set-split.md) has a basic [neighbourhood](../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) contained in that [intersection](../../../../set-intersection.md).

Consequently the arbitrary [unions](../../../../set-union.md) of members of $\mathcal B$, including the [empty union](../../../../empty-union.md), form a [topology](../../../../topology-split.md) $\mathcal T$. The [basis of a topology](../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) conditions give closure under finite [intersections](../../../../set-intersection.md); arbitrary [unions](../../../../set-union.md) are built into the definition. **This is the [lower limit topology](../../../../lower-limit-topology.md), also called the [Sorgenfrey line](../../../../lower-limit-topology.md).**
