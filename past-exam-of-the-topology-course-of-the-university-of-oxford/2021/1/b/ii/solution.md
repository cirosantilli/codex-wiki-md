↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

**No, $[0,1]$ is not [open](../../../../../open-set.md).** Every basic [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $[a,b)$ of $1$ has $b>1$, so it contains points larger than $1$. Therefore $1$ has no [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) contained in $[0,1]$.
