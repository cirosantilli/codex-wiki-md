↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

The [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $[0,1)$ is a basic [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) contained in $[0,1]$. The remaining point $1$ is not an [interior](../../../../../interior-topology.md) point, by the preceding argument. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{int}_{\mathcal T}[0,1]=[0,1).}
$$
