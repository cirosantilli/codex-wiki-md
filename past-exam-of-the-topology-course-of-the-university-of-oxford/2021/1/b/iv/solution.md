<h1 id="1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iv](../iv.md)

**The only [isolated point](../../../../../isolated-point.md) of the [subset](../../../../../subset.md) $[0,1]$ is $1$.** Indeed,

$$
[1,2)\cap[0,1]=\{1\},
$$

so $\{1\}$ is [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in the [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md). If $0\leq x<1$, every basic [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $[a,b)$ containing $x$ also contains a point strictly between $x$ and $\min(b,1)$, hence another point of $[0,1]$. Such an $x$ is not an [isolated point](../../../../../isolated-point.md) of the [subset](../../../../../subset.md). Here isolation refers to $[0,1]$ with its [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md); $1$ is not an [isolated point](../../../../../isolated-point.md) of the ambient [Sorgenfrey line](../../../../../lower-limit-topology.md).
