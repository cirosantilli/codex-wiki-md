<h1 id="1/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

**$X$ is not [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md).** In the [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $X=[0,1]$, consider the [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md)

$$
\mathcal U=\{[0,1-1/n):n\geq2\}\cup\{\{1\}\}.
$$

The [intervals](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) are basic [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) in the ambient [Sorgenfrey line](../../../../../lower-limit-topology.md), and $\{1\}=X\cap[1,2)$ is [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in $X$. Every $x<1$ belongs to one of those [intervals](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md), while the [singleton](../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md) covers $1$.

A [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md) would have to include $\{1\}$. Among its finitely many other members, let $N$ be the largest index. Their [union](../../../../../set-union.md) is $[0,1-1/N)$, leaving $1-1/N$ uncovered. Selecting no such member also fails to cover $0$. Hence no [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md) exists.
