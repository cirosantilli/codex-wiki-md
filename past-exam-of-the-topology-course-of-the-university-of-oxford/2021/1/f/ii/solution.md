<h1 id="1/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Take

$$
\boxed{g(x)=-x.}
$$

It is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) for the [Euclidean metric](../../../../../euclidean-metric.md), since $|g(x)-g(y)|=|x-y|$. But the basic [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $[0,1)$ of the target [Sorgenfrey line](../../../../../lower-limit-topology.md) has [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md)

$$
g^{-1}([0,1))=(-1,0].
$$

This is not [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in the source [Sorgenfrey line](../../../../../lower-limit-topology.md): every basic [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $0$ contains positive points. Thus $g$ is not [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) for the [lower limit topology](../../../../../lower-limit-topology.md).
