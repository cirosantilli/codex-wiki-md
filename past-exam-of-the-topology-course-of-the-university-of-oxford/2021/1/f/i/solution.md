<h1 id="1/f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Use the [Heaviside step function](../../../../../heaviside-step-function.md) with the value at the jump chosen as follows:

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\begin{cases}0,&x<0,\\1,&x\geq0.\end{cases}}
$$

For any [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) in the target [Sorgenfrey line](../../../../../lower-limit-topology.md), its [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md) is one of $\varnothing$, $\mathbb R$, $(-\infty,0)$, and $[0,\infty)$. All four are [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in the source [Sorgenfrey line](../../../../../lower-limit-topology.md), so $f$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md). In the [Euclidean topology](../../../../../euclidean-topology.md), the [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md) of $(1/2,3/2)$ is $[0,\infty)$, which is not [open](../../../../../open-set.md). Hence the same [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is not [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) as a usual real-to-real [map](../../../../../function-class.md).
