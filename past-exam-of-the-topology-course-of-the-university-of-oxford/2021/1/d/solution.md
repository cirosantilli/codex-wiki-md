<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [d](../d.md)

The usual [real line](../../../../real-line.md) is [connected](../../../../connected-space.md). In contrast,

$$
\mathbb R_{\mathcal T}=(-\infty,0)\sqcup[0,\infty)
$$

is a [separation of a topological space](../../../../separation-of-a-topological-space.md). Both pieces are nonempty and disjoint; the first is [open](../../../../open-set.md) by part (c), and the second is the [open](../../../../open-set.md) [union](../../../../set-union.md) $\bigcup_{n\geq1}[0,n)$. Thus the [Sorgenfrey line](../../../../lower-limit-topology.md) is not [connected](../../../../connected-space.md). Since a [homeomorphism](../../../../homeomorphism.md) preserves [connectedness](../../../../connected-space.md), **the two [topological spaces](../../../../topological-space.md) are not [homeomorphic](../../../../homeomorphism.md).** Merely showing that their [topologies](../../../../topology-split.md) differ would not by itself exclude some other [homeomorphism](../../../../homeomorphism.md) between them.
