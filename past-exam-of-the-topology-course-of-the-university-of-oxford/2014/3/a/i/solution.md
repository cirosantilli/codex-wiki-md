↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [abstract simplicial complex](../../../../../simplicial-complex.md) $K=(V,\Sigma)$ consists of a [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) set $V$ and a collection $\Sigma$ of finite nonempty subsets containing every singleton and every nonempty subset of each member. A member with $n+1$ vertices is an $n$-simplex.

Its [topological realization](../../../../../geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) glues a standard geometric [simplex](../../../../../simplex.md) for each member along common faces. A concrete model is

$$
|K|=\left\{(t_v)_{v\in V}:t_v\geq0,\ \sum_vt_v=1,\ \{v:t_v>0\}\in\Sigma\right\},
$$

with finite support and the [weak topology of a simplicial realization](../../../../../weak-topology-of-a-simplicial-realization.md): a set is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) precisely when its [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) with every [simplex](../../../../../simplex.md) is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md). This agrees with the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md) of the gluing construction. Specifying this [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) matters for complexes with infinitely many vertices.
