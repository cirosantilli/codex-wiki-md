<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [open star in a simplicial complex](../../../../../open-star-in-a-simplicial-complex.md) of a [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) $v$ is the [union](../../../../../set-union.md) of the relative interiors of the simplices containing $v$. In barycentric coordinates it is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{st}(v)=\{p\in|K|:t_v(p)>0\}.}
$$

Let $L$ be the [subcomplex](../../../../../simplicial-subcomplex.md) consisting of all simplices not containing $v$. The [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) of the star is exactly $|L|$: the positive-coordinate support of a point misses $v$ precisely when it is a [simplex](../../../../../simplex.md) of $L$. By the allowed closed-subcomplex theorem, $|L|$ is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in $|K|$. Hence the star is [open](../../../../../open-set.md).

This is the open-star convention required by the question. The [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) star, which also includes the opposite faces, is a different subset and need not be [open](../../../../../open-set.md).
