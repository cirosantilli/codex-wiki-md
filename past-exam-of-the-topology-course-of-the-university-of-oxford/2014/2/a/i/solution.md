The [product topology](../../../../../product-topology.md) has [basis](../../../../../basis.md)

$$
\boxed{\{U\times V:U\subseteq X\text{ open},\ V\subseteq Y\text{ open}\}.}
$$

[Open](../../../../../open-set.md) subsets of the product are arbitrary unions of these rectangles. The rectangles cover the product and their finite intersections are rectangles, so they satisfy the [basis](../../../../../basis.md) axioms. Equivalently, this is the smallest [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) making both coordinate projections [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md).
