<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [quotient set](../../../../../quotient-set.md) $X/\mathcal R$ consists of the [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md). Let $p(x)=[x]$. Equip that set with the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md)

$$
\boxed{U\subseteq X/\mathcal R\text{ open}\iff p^{-1}(U)\text{ open in }X.}
$$

Preimages preserve the empty set, whole set, arbitrary unions and finite intersections, so this is a [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md). The canonical projection is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) and [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md), and the displayed equivalence specifies the [quotient space](../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) completely.
