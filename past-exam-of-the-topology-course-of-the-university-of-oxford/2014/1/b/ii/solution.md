<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because $f$ is [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md), $f^{-1}(B)$ is infinite whenever $B$ is infinite; a finite [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md) could have only finitely many [image](../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) points. By part (i), its [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) is $X$. Apply part (a)(ii):

$$
Y=f(X)=f\bigl(\overline{f^{-1}(B)}\bigr)
\subseteq\overline{f(f^{-1}(B))}=\overline B.
$$

The other inclusion is automatic, so

$$
\boxed{\overline B=Y\quad\text{for every infinite }B\subseteq Y.}
$$

Both [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) and [surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md) enter the argument.
