<h1 id="1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Any two nonempty [open](../../../../../open-set.md) sets of $Y$ are cofinite by part (iii). Their complements have [finite union](../../../../../finite-union.md), so the two sets must intersect because $Y$ is infinite. Thus $Y$ is a [hyperconnected space](../../../../../hyperconnected-space.md).

A [Hausdorff space](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) with at least two points has disjoint nonempty [open](../../../../../open-set.md) neighbourhoods of two distinct points. This is impossible here. Therefore **$Y$ is not [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md)**.
