<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Any proper [closed subset](../../../../../closed-set.md) $F$ of $Y$ must be finite: if it were infinite, part (ii) would give $F=\overline F=Y$. Therefore every nonempty [open subset](../../../../../open-set.md) of $Y$, being the [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) of a proper [closed set](../../../../../closed-set.md), is cofinite.

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T_Y\subseteq\mathcal T_{\mathrm{cofinite}}.}
$$

The empty set belongs to both topologies. Inclusion need not be equality; the target may have fewer [open](../../../../../open-set.md) sets.
