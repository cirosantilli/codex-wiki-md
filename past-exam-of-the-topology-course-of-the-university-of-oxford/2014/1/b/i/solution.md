↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A proper [closed subset](../../../../../closed-set.md) in the [cofinite topology](../../../../../cofinite-topology.md) is finite, so cannot contain the infinite subset $A$. Its only [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) superset is therefore the whole space:

$$
\boxed{\overline A=X.}
$$

In particular, every infinite subset is [dense](../../../../../dense-set.md) in this [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md).
