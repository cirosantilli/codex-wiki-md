<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) $\overline A$ is the [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) of every [closed subset](../../../../../closed-set.md) of $X$ containing $A$. Thus it is the smallest such [closed subset](../../../../../closed-set.md). Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{x\in\overline A\iff\text{every open neighbourhood of }x\text{ meets }A.}
$$

For equivalence, if an [open neighbourhood](../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) misses $A$, its [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) is a [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) superset of $A$ missing $x$. Conversely, a [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) superset missing $x$ has an [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) that is a neighbourhood of $x$ disjoint from $A$.
