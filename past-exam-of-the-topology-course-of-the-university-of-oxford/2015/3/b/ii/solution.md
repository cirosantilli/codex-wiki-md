<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [classification theorem for surfaces](../../../../../classification-theorem-for-surfaces.md) states that every connected [closed combinatorial surface](../../../../../closed-combinatorial-surface.md) is [homeomorphic](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) to exactly one of:

- the [two-sphere](../../../../../two-sphere.md);
- an [orientable surface](../../../../../orientable-surface.md) of [orientable genus](../../../../../orientable-genus.md) $g\geq1$, the [connected sum](../../../../../connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) of $g$ [tori](../../../../../torus.md);
- a [nonorientable surface](../../../../../non-orientable-surface.md) of [crosscap number](../../../../../crosscap-number.md) $h\geq1$, the [connected sum](../../../../../connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) of $h$ [real projective planes](../../../../../real-projective-plane.md).

The orientable family has [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md) $2-2g$; the nonorientable family has Euler characteristic $2-h$. Orientability together with Euler characteristic distinguishes the entries.

Boundary words giving the required [polygonal schemas](../../../../../polygonal-schema.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
S^2:&\quad aa^{-1},\\
\Sigma_g:&\quad a_1b_1a_1^{-1}b_1^{-1}\cdots a_gb_ga_g^{-1}b_g^{-1},\\
N_h:&\quad a_1a_1a_2a_2\cdots a_ha_h.
\end{aligned}}
$$

A two-sided polygon here means a disk with its boundary split into two arcs; subdivision produces an ordinary polygon with more sides without changing the quotient. Alternatively the square word $aa^{-1}bb^{-1}$ represents the sphere. Each four-letter block in the orientable word adds a torus handle; each repeated-letter block in the nonorientable word adds a projective-plane summand.
