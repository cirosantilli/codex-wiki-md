An $n$-dimensional [topological manifold](../../../../../topological-manifold-split.md) is a [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md), [second countable](../../../../../second-countable-space.md) topological space in which every point has an open neighbourhood [homeomorphic](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) to an open subset of $\mathbb R^n$. Equivalently, charts can be chosen onto open balls. This is the without-boundary convention.

A [topological surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) is a two-dimensional topological manifold. When connectedness is included in the convention for a surface, impose it as well; in the classification statement below the surface is explicitly connected. A [closed surface](../../../../../closed-surface.md) means a compact surface without boundary, rather than merely a closed subset of another space.
