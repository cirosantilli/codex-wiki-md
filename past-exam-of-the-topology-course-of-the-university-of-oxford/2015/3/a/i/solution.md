The [standard simplex](../../../../../standard-simplex.md) of dimension $n$ is

$$
\boxed{\Delta^n=\{(t_0,\ldots,t_n)\in\mathbb R^{n+1}:t_j\geq0,\ \sum_jt_j=1\}.}
$$

It is the [convex hull](../../../../../convex-hull.md) of the coordinate vectors, and its [faces of a simplex](../../../../../face-of-a-simplex.md) arise by setting selected coordinates to zero.

An [abstract simplicial complex](../../../../../simplicial-complex.md) is a pair $(V,\Sigma)$ where $V$ is the vertex set and $\Sigma$ consists of finite nonempty subsets of $V$, contains every singleton, and contains every nonempty subset of each member. A member with $n+1$ vertices is an $n$-[simplex](../../../../../simplex.md).

Its [geometric realization](../../../../../geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) is obtained by gluing one [standard simplex](../../../../../standard-simplex.md) for each member along the faces specified by common vertex subsets. Equivalently, its points have [barycentric coordinates](../../../../../barycentric-coordinate-system.md) $(t_v)_{v\in V}$ with finite support, nonnegative entries summing to one, and support a simplex of $K$. Use the topology induced by this gluing, equivalently the [weak topology of a simplicial realization](../../../../../weak-topology-of-a-simplicial-realization.md).
