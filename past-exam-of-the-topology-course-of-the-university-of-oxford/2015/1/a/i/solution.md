<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md) is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) if it has no [separation](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md): two disjoint nonempty [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) whose union is the whole space. Equivalently, its only [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) that are both [open](../../../../../open-set.md) and [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) are the [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md) and the whole space.

A [subset](../../../../../subset.md) is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) when its [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) has this property. Thus the sets in a proposed [separation](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md) of a subset need only be relatively [open](../../../../../open-set.md), rather than [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in the ambient space.

$$
\boxed{A\text{ connected}\iff A\text{ has no separation in its subspace topology}.}
$$
