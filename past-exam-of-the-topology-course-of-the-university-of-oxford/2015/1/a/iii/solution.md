<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $f(A)=U\cup V$ were a [separation](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md), then

$$
A\cap f^{-1}(U),\qquad A\cap f^{-1}(V)
$$

would be disjoint nonempty relatively [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) covering $A$. They are relatively [open](../../../../../open-set.md) by [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the restriction $f|_A:A\to f(A)$. This contradicts [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md) of $A$.

$$
\boxed{A\text{ connected},\ f\text{ continuous}\ \Longrightarrow\ f(A)\text{ connected}.}
$$

This is the [continuous image of a connected space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-connected-space.md) theorem; no separation property of the target is required.
