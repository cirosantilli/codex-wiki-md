↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md) is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) if every [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) has a [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md):

$$
\boxed{X=\bigcup_{i\in I}U_i,\ U_i\text{ open}\ \Longrightarrow\ X=U_{i_1}\cup\cdots\cup U_{i_n}\text{ for finitely many indices}.}
$$

There is no [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) requirement in this definition.
