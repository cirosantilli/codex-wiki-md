<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $(V_i)$ be an [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) of $Y$. By [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md), the [inverse images](../../../../../preimage.md) $f^{-1}(V_i)$ are [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in $X$; by [surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md), they cover $X$. [Compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md) supplies finitely many covering $X$. Applying $f$ and using $f(X)=Y$ shows that the corresponding finitely many $V_i$ cover $Y$.

Thus **a continuous [surjective function](../../../../../surjective-function.md) from a [compact space](../../../../../compact-space.md) has [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) target**. This is the [continuous image of a compact space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md) theorem.
