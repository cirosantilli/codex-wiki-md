<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $g$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md), then so is $g\circ p$, as a [composition of continuous functions](../../../../../composition-of-continuous-functions.md). Conversely, suppose $g\circ p$ is continuous. For an [open subset](../../../../../open-set.md) $W\subseteq Z$,

$$
p^{-1}(g^{-1}(W))=(g\circ p)^{-1}(W)
$$

is open in $X$. The [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md) criterion therefore says $g^{-1}(W)$ is open in $X/\mathcal R$. This proves continuity of $g$.

$$
\boxed{g\text{ continuous}\iff g\circ p\text{ continuous}.}
$$

This is the [universal property of the quotient topology](../../../../../universal-property-of-the-quotient-topology.md).
