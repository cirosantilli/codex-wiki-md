For the [canonical projection of a quotient set](../../../../../canonical-projection-of-a-quotient-set.md) $p$, define

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T_q=\{U\subseteq X/\mathcal R:p^{-1}(U)\in\mathcal T\}.}
$$

The [inverse images](../../../../../preimage.md) of the [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md) and whole target are $\varnothing$ and $X$. Inverse images commute with arbitrary [unions](../../../../../set-union.md) and finite [intersections](../../../../../set-intersection.md), so the topology axioms follow from those of $\mathcal T$. This is the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md), and it makes $p$ [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) by definition.

The crucial criterion is an equivalence: $U$ is open precisely when $p^{-1}(U)$ is open. This is stronger than continuity alone and is what makes the next part work.
