# 2021

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the topology course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part A [topology](../topology-split.md) examination, sat on 25 June 2021. Source: [officially released examination PDF, parent PDF pages 94–96; printed paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_part_a_examinati/response/2820674/attach/3/FOI%20202410%20944%20Data.pdf#page=94). All 25 answer parts are covered, including every alternative. Only the best two questions counted towards the examination mark.
