<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [subset](../../../../../subset.md) $K$ is **[compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) if every [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) has a [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md)**. Explicitly, if $U_i$ are [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) of $X$ and $K\subseteq\bigcup_{i\in I}U_i$, there are finitely many indices $i_1,\ldots,i_m$ such that

$$
K\subseteq U_{i_1}\cup\cdots\cup U_{i_m}.
$$

Equivalently, the [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) makes $K$ a [compact space](../../../../../compact-space.md). An [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) in this [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) is $K\cap U$ for an ambient [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $U$, which proves equivalence of the two [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) formulations. The [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md) is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md): the empty selection is a [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md).
