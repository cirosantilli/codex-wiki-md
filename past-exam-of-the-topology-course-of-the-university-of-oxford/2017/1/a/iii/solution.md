<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $F$ be a [closed subset](../../../../../closed-set.md) of the [compact space](../../../../../compact-space.md) $X$. Express an arbitrary [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) of $F$ in its [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) as $\{F\cap U_i:i\in I\}$, where the $U_i$ are [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) of $X$. Since $X\setminus F$ is [open](../../../../../open-set.md), the family

$$
\{U_i:i\in I\}\cup\{X\setminus F\}
$$

is an [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) of $X$. Take a [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md) and discard $X\setminus F$ if it occurs. The remaining finitely many $U_i$ still cover $F$, since the discarded [set](../../../../../set-split.md) contains no point of $F$. Their traces on $F$ give the required [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md). Hence **every [closed subset of a compact space](../../../../../closed-subset-of-a-compact-space.md) is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md)**, with no [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) assumption.
