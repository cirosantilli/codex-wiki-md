↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [abstract simplicial complex](../../../../../simplicial-complex.md) is a pair $K=(V,\Sigma)$, where $V$ is a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of vertices and $\Sigma$ is a family of finite nonempty [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) of $V$ satisfying two conditions: every singleton $\{v\}$ is in $\Sigma$, and every nonempty subset of a member of $\Sigma$ is again in $\Sigma$. Its members are the [simplexes](../../../../../simplex.md); a face with $n+1$ vertices has dimension $n$.

To form its [topological realization](../../../../../geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md), take a disjoint copy of the standard geometric simplex $\Delta^{|\sigma|-1}$ for every $\sigma\in\Sigma$, label its vertices by $\sigma$, and identify shared labelled faces using affine face inclusions. Give the resulting space $|K|$ the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md) of this disjoint union. Equivalently, its points have finite-support [barycentric coordinates](../../../../../barycentric-coordinate-system.md) $\lambda_v\geq0$ with $\sum_v\lambda_v=1$ and positive support belonging to $\Sigma$.

The topology is part of the definition: $U\subseteq|K|$ is open exactly when $U\cap|\sigma|$ is open in each closed geometric simplex. This is the [weak topology of a simplicial realization](../../../../../weak-topology-of-a-simplicial-realization.md), used below for the infinite complex. For finite complexes it coincides with the usual Euclidean realization topology.[Definitions I.17–I.18, printed pp. 8–9: abstract complexes and face-gluing realization](../../../../../topology-and-groups-by-marc-lackenby.md)
