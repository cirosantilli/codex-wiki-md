<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) is a [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) for which both the [function](../../../../../function-split.md) and its inverse are [continuous maps](../../../../../continuous-map.md).

Let $f:X\to Y$ satisfy the given assumptions. For a [closed subset](../../../../../closed-set.md) $F$ of $X$, the [closed subset of a compact space](../../../../../closed-subset-of-a-compact-space.md) theorem makes $F$ [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md). The [continuous image of a compact space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md) theorem makes $f(F)$ [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md). Because $Y$ is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md), its [compact subset](../../../../../compact-space.md) $f(F)$ is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md). Thus $f$ is a [closed map](../../../../../closed-map.md).

For $g=f^{-1}:Y\to X$, the [inverse image](../../../../../preimage.md) of a [closed set](../../../../../closed-set.md) $F\subseteq X$ is

$$
g^{-1}(F)=f(F),
$$

which is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in $Y$. The [closed-set criterion for continuity](../../../../../closed-set-criterion-for-continuity.md) therefore makes $g$ [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md). Hence **$f$ is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md)**, proving the [compact-to-Hausdorff continuous bijection theorem](../../../../../compact-to-hausdorff-continuous-bijection-theorem.md).
