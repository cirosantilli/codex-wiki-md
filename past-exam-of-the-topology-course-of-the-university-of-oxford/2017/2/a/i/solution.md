<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md) is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) when any two distinct points have [disjoint](../../../../../disjoint-sets.md) [open neighbourhoods](../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md).

Any two nonempty [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) of the infinite [cofinite topology](../../../../../cofinite-topology.md) intersect: their [complements](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) are [finite sets](../../../../../finite-set.md), so the [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) of their [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) is a [finite union](../../../../../finite-union.md) of [finite sets](../../../../../finite-set.md) and cannot be all of $Z$. Thus $Z$ is a [hyperconnected space](../../../../../hyperconnected-space.md).

If the [continuous map](../../../../../continuous-map.md) $f$ took distinct values at $s,t\in Z$, choose [disjoint](../../../../../disjoint-sets.md) [open neighbourhoods](../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) $G,H$ of $f(s),f(t)$ in the [Hausdorff space](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) $Y$. By [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md), $f^{-1}(G)$ and $f^{-1}(H)$ would be nonempty [disjoint](../../../../../disjoint-sets.md) [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) of $Z$, a contradiction. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f\text{ is constant}.}
$$

The proof establishes the general fact that [continuous maps from an infinite cofinite space to a Hausdorff space are constant](../../../../../continuous-maps-from-an-infinite-cofinite-space-to-a-hausdorff-space-are-constant.md).
