<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [product topology](../../../../../product-topology.md) on the [Cartesian product](../../../../../cartesian-product.md) $X\times Y$ has a [basis of a topology](../../../../../basis-of-a-topology.md) consisting of all rectangles $U\times V$, where $U$ is [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in $X$ and $V$ is [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in $Y$. Equivalently, it is the smallest [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) making the two [coordinate projections](../../../../../coordinate-projection.md) [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md).

Take distinct points $(x_1,y_1)$ and $(x_2,y_2)$. If $x_1\ne x_2$, use the [Hausdorff condition](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) in $X$ to find [disjoint](../../../../../disjoint-sets.md) [open neighbourhoods](../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) $U_1,U_2$. Then $U_1\times Y$ and $U_2\times Y$ are [disjoint](../../../../../disjoint-sets.md) [open neighbourhoods](../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) of the two points. If $x_1=x_2$, then $y_1\ne y_2$; [disjoint](../../../../../disjoint-sets.md) [open neighbourhoods](../../../../../open-neighbourhood.md) $V_1,V_2$ in $Y$ give $X\times V_1$ and $X\times V_2$. Thus **$X\times Y$ is [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md)**, as established by the forward implication of the [Hausdorff property of a nonempty product](../../../../../hausdorff-property-of-a-nonempty-product.md). An empty [Cartesian product](../../../../../cartesian-product.md) is automatically [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md).
