↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the topology course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part A [topology](../topology-split.md) examination, sat on 16 June 2023. Source: [officially released examination PDF, parent PDF pages 101–103; printed paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_part_a_examinati/response/2820674/attach/3/FOI%20202410%20944%20Data.pdf#page=101). The wording in 1(a)(v) requires an interpretation explained in its solution. The classification reference in 3(b)(iii) should point to 3(a)(iv).
