Give the nonempty [subset](../../../../../subset.md) $A\subseteq X$ its [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md). It is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) if every [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) admits a [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md). Equivalently, whenever $A\subseteq\bigcup_{j\in J}U_j$ with each $U_j$ [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in $X$, there are $j_1,\ldots,j_m$ such that $A\subseteq U_{j_1}\cup\cdots\cup U_{j_m}$.

It is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) if it has no [separation of a topological space](../../../../../separation-of-a-topological-space.md): there are no disjoint nonempty [open subsets](../../../../../open-set.md) $B,C$ of $A$ with $A=B\cup C$. Here openness is relative to the [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md); equivalently, the only [clopen sets](../../../../../clopen-set.md) of $A$ are $\varnothing$ and $A$.
