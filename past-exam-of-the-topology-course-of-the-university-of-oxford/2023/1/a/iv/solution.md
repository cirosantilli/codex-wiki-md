<h1 id="1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use the [Sierpiński space](../../../../../sierpinski-space.md) $X=\{0,1\}$ with [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) $\{\varnothing,\{1\},X\}$. The [singleton](../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md) $A=\{1\}$ is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md): from any [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md), choose one member containing $1$. But $X\setminus A=\{0\}$ is not [open](../../../../../open-set.md), so $A$ is not [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md). This also exhibits why the [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) hypothesis in the preceding proof matters.
