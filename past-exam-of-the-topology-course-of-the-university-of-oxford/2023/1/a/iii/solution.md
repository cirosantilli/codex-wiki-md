<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $K\subseteq X$ be [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md), with $X$ a [Hausdorff space](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md). The [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md) is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md), so suppose $K\neq\varnothing$. Fix $x\in X\setminus K$. For each $y\in K$, the [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) property supplies disjoint [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $U_y$ of $y$ and $V_y$ of $x$.

The $U_y$ form an [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) of $K$. Choose a [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md) $U_{y_1},\ldots,U_{y_m}$ and put $V=\bigcap_{r=1}^m V_{y_r}$. This is an [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $x$. If a point of $V$ belonged to $K$, it would belong to some $U_{y_r}$ as well as $V_{y_r}$, a contradiction. Hence $V\subseteq X\setminus K$. Every point of the complement has such an [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md), so $X\setminus K$ is [open](../../../../../open-set.md) and $K$ is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md).
