<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $(V_j)_{j\in J}$ be an [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) of $f(K)$ by [open subsets](../../../../../open-set.md) of $Y$. The [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of $f$ makes each [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md) $f^{-1}(V_j)$ [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in $X$, and these [preimages](../../../../../preimage.md) cover $K$. By [compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md), choose $j_1,\ldots,j_m$ with

$$
K\subseteq\bigcup_{r=1}^m f^{-1}(V_{j_r}).
$$

Applying $f$ gives $f(K)\subseteq\bigcup_{r=1}^m V_{j_r}$. Thus every [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md) of $f(K)$ has a [finite subcover](../../../../../finite-subcover.md), proving the [continuous image of a compact space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md) theorem. A cover by sets [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in $f(K)$ gives the same argument after expressing them as intersections with ambient [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md).
