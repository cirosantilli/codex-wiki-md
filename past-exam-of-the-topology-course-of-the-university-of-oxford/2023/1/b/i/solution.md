<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Suppose $K\setminus\{a\}$ were [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md). By the [compact subset of a Hausdorff space](../../../../../compact-subset-of-a-hausdorff-space.md) theorem, it would be [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in $X$, hence [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in $K$. The [singleton](../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md) $\{a\}$ is also [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in the [Hausdorff space](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md) $X$: each other point has an [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) avoiding $a$.

Consequently both $\{a\}$ and $K\setminus\{a\}$ are [clopen](../../../../../clopen-set.md) in $K$. They are disjoint, nonempty, and cover $K$, giving a [separation of a topological space](../../../../../separation-of-a-topological-space.md) in contradiction to the [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md) of $K$. Thus $K\setminus\{a\}$ is not [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md).
