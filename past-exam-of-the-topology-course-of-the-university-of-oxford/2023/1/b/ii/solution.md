<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Removing $1/2$ from the [closed interval](../../../../../closed-real-interval.md) $I=[0,1]$ leaves the [separation of a topological space](../../../../../separation-of-a-topological-space.md)

$$
I\setminus\{1/2\}=[0,1/2)\sqcup(1/2,1].
$$

Thus $1/2$ is a [cut point](../../../../../cut-point.md) of $I$.

In contrast, removing any point $p$ from the [square](../../../../../square.md) $Q=[0,1]^2$ leaves a [path-connected space](../../../../../path-connected-space.md). To prove this, take distinct $u,v\in Q\setminus\{p\}$. Choose an interior point $w$ outside the two [lines](../../../../../straight-line.md) through $(u,p)$ and $(v,p)$. Such a choice exists: a small [circle](../../../../../circle.md) in the interior meets each of those [lines](../../../../../straight-line.md) in at most two points. Since $Q$ is a [convex set](../../../../../convex-set-split.md), the [line segments](../../../../../line-segment.md) $[u,w]$ and $[w,v]$ stay in $Q$; our choice of $w$ ensures that both avoid $p$. Their concatenation is a [continuous path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) from $u$ to $v$ in $Q\setminus\{p\}$.

The [product topology](../../../../../product-topology.md) on $I\times I$ agrees with its usual [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) in the [Euclidean plane](../../../../../euclidean-plane.md). If $h:Q\to I$ were a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md), its [restriction](../../../../../restriction-mathematics.md) would give a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md)

$$
Q\setminus\{h^{-1}(1/2)\}\longrightarrow I\setminus\{1/2\}.
$$

The domain is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md), since [path connectedness](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) implies [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md), while the target is not [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md). This contradicts the preservation of [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md) by a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md).
