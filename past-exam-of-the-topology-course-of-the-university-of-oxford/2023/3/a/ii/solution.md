<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Fix a [vertex of a simplicial complex](../../../../../vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) $v$, and let $R$ be the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of vertices reachable from $v$ by an [edge path](../../../../../edge-path.md). If a [simplex](../../../../../simplex.md) contains a vertex of $R$, every vertex of that [simplex](../../../../../simplex.md) lies in $R$: append its [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) from the reachable vertex to the existing [edge path](../../../../../edge-path.md). Thus every [simplex](../../../../../simplex.md) has either all its vertices in $R$ or all outside $R$.

Partition the [geometric realization](../../../../../geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) as $|K|=A\sqcup B$, according to whether the [support](../../../../../support.md) in the [barycentric coordinate system](../../../../../barycentric-coordinate-system.md) lies in $R$ or outside $R$. On each geometric [simplex](../../../../../simplex.md), the intersection with $A$ is either the whole [simplex](../../../../../simplex.md) or the [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md), and the same holds for $B$. The defining realization [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) therefore makes both $A$ and $B$ [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md), hence also [open](../../../../../open-set.md) because they are complements.

The vertex $v$ belongs to $A$. If any vertex were unreachable, it would belong to the nonempty set $B$, giving a [separation of a topological space](../../../../../separation-of-a-topological-space.md) of $|K|$. This contradicts [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md). Therefore every vertex is reachable from $v$, and any two vertices can be joined by an [edge path](../../../../../edge-path.md).
