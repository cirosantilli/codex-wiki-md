<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [classification theorem for closed combinatorial surfaces](../../../../../classification-theorem-for-closed-combinatorial-surfaces.md) states that the [geometric realization](../../../../../geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) of every [closed combinatorial surface](../../../../../closed-combinatorial-surface.md) is [homeomorphic](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) to exactly one of the following:

- The [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) $S^2$.
- An [orientable](../../../../../orientable-surface.md) [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) $\Sigma_g$ formed as a [connected sum of surfaces](../../../../../connected-sum-of-surfaces.md) of $g\geq1$ copies of the [torus](../../../../../torus.md) $T^2$.
- A [nonorientable surface](../../../../../non-orientable-surface.md) $N_k$ formed as a [connected sum of surfaces](../../../../../connected-sum-of-surfaces.md) of $k\geq1$ copies of the [real projective plane](../../../../../real-projective-plane.md) $\mathbb{RP}^2$.

With $\Sigma_0=S^2$, the complete distinguishing data are [orientability](../../../../../orientability.md) and the [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md):

$$
\chi(\Sigma_g)=2-2g,\qquad \chi(N_k)=2-k.
$$

Here $\chi(K)=V-E+F$, where $V,E,F$ count the [vertices of a simplicial complex](../../../../../vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md), [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md), and triangles of a finite [triangulation](../../../../../triangulation.md). Thus two such [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) closed [surfaces](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) are [homeomorphic](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) if and only if they have the same [orientability](../../../../../orientability.md) and [Euler characteristic](../../../../../euler-characteristic.md). In particular $N_1$ is the [real projective plane](../../../../../real-projective-plane.md) and $N_2$ is the [Klein bottle](../../../../../klein-bottle.md). Every listed type admits a finite [triangulation](../../../../../triangulation.md). For disconnected finite [closed combinatorial surfaces](../../../../../closed-combinatorial-surface.md), apply the theorem to each of the finitely many [connected components](../../../../../connected-component.md); the resulting unordered collection of types determines the [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) type.
