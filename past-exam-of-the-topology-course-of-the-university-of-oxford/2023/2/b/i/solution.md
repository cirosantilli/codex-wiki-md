<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** The [canonical projection of a quotient set](../../../../../canonical-projection-of-a-quotient-set.md) $p:X\to X/{\sim}$ is a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) [surjection](../../../../../surjective-function.md). Its image is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) by the [continuous image of a compact space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-compact-space.md) theorem.

For [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md), if $U,V$ formed a [separation of a topological space](../../../../../separation-of-a-topological-space.md) of $X/{\sim}$, then the [preimages](../../../../../preimage.md) $p^{-1}(U),p^{-1}(V)$ would be disjoint nonempty [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) covering $X$, contradicting its [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md). This proves the [continuous image of a connected space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-connected-space.md) theorem in this case. Hence the [quotient space](../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) is both [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md) and [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md).
