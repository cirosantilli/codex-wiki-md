<h1 id="2/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**False.** Take the [metric space](../../../../../metric-space.md) $X=\mathbb R\times\{0,1\}$ with the [Euclidean distance](../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) and [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) inherited from $\mathbb R^2$. For every $t\neq0$, identify $(t,0)$ with $(t,1)$; leave the two points $(0,0)$ and $(0,1)$ in separate [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md). All [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) have one or two points, so all are [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in this [metric space](../../../../../metric-space.md).

Let $o_0,o_1$ be the two distinct images of the origins. If $U_0,U_1$ are any [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $o_0,o_1$ in the [quotient space](../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md), their [preimages](../../../../../preimage.md) contain respectively

$$
(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\times\{0\},\qquad(-\delta,\delta)\times\{1\}
$$

for some $\varepsilon,\delta>0$. Choose $0<t<\min(\varepsilon,\delta)$. The common image $p(t,0)=p(t,1)$ belongs to both $U_0$ and $U_1$. Therefore the origins have no disjoint [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhoods](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) and the [quotient space](../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) is not [Hausdorff](../../../../../hausdorff-space.md). This is the [line with two origins](../../../../../line-with-two-origins.md).
