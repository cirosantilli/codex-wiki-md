<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** Choose the [irrational number](../../../../../irrational-number.md) $\alpha=\sqrt2$, and partition the [rational numbers](../../../../../rational-number.md) into

$$
C_n=\mathbb Q\cap(n+\alpha,n+1+\alpha),\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.
$$

No endpoint is [rational](../../../../../rational-number.md), so these disjoint sets cover $\mathbb Q$. Each is nonempty by the [density of the rational numbers](../../../../../density-of-the-rational-numbers.md), and each is [clopen](../../../../../clopen-set.md) in the [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $\mathbb Q$.

Let two [rational numbers](../../../../../rational-number.md) be equivalent exactly when they belong to the same $C_n$. The [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md)

$$
h:\mathbb Q/{\sim}\longrightarrow\mathbb Z,\qquad h([q])=n\text{ when }q\in C_n
$$

is well-defined. For every [subset](../../../../../subset.md) $B\subseteq\mathbb Z$, the [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md) of $h^{-1}(B)$ under the [canonical projection of a quotient set](../../../../../canonical-projection-of-a-quotient-set.md) is $\bigcup_{n\in B}C_n$, which is [open](../../../../../open-set.md) in $\mathbb Q$. Thus every [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of $\mathbb Q/{\sim}$ is [open](../../../../../open-set.md): its [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md) is the [discrete topology](../../../../../discrete-space.md). The usual [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) on the [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md) is also the [discrete topology](../../../../../discrete-space.md), because $\{n\}=\mathbb Z\cap(n-1/2,n+1/2)$. Consequently $h$ is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md).
