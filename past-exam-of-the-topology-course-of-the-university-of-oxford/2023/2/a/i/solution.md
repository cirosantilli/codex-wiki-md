<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [quotient set](../../../../../quotient-set.md) $X/{\sim}$ consists of the [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) $[x]=\{y\in X:y\sim x\}$. Its collapsing map is the [canonical projection of a quotient set](../../../../../canonical-projection-of-a-quotient-set.md)

$$
p:X\longrightarrow X/{\sim},\qquad p(x)=[x].
$$

Equip this [set](../../../../../set-split.md) with the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md):

$$
U\subseteq X/{\sim}\text{ is open}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad p^{-1}(U)\text{ is open in }X.
$$

This is a [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) because [preimages](../../../../../preimage.md) preserve arbitrary [unions](../../../../../set-union.md), finite [intersections](../../../../../set-intersection.md), the [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md), and the whole [set](../../../../../set-split.md). The resulting [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md) is the [quotient space](../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md). The displayed rule immediately implies that the [preimage](../../../../../preimage.md) of every [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) is [open](../../../../../open-set.md), so $p$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md); it is also [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md).
