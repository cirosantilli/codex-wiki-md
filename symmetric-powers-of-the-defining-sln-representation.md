# Symmetric powers of the defining sln representation

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md)

For $n\geq2$ and $m\geq0$, the representation $\operatorname{Sym}^m\mathbb C^n$ of $\mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbb C)$ is irreducible with [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $m\omega_1$. Its monomial basis supplies weights by adding $m$ defining-representation weights, with repeated weights counted separately.
