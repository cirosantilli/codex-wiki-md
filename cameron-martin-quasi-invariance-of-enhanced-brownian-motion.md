# Cameron-Martin quasi-invariance of enhanced Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Rough path translation](rough-path-translation.md)

For a deterministic path $h$ in the [Cameron-Martin space of Wiener measure](cameron-martin-space-of-wiener-measure.md), the law of $T_h\mathbf B$ is equivalent to the law of [enhanced Brownian motion](enhanced-brownian-motion.md). The [Cameron-Martin theorem](cameron-martin-theorem.md) gives equivalence for the underlying Brownian paths; the canonical enhancement of the translated path is exactly the rough-path translation, including its mixed area terms. Continuity of this translation transfers small-ball positivity at the identity to every control signature.
