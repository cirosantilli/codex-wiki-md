# Pure polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization (waves)](polarization-waves.md)

[Pure polarization](pure-polarization.md) means that the transverse components of a light field have a fixed complex amplitude ratio. A single [Jones vector](jones-vector.md) describes the state; its [coherency matrix](coherency-matrix.md) has rank one. Removing overall intensity and a common phase leaves two real parameters, which determine its [polarization ellipse](polarization-ellipse.md).
