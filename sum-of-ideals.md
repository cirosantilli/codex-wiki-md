# Sum of ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)

The sum of two [ideals](ideal.md) $I,J$ of a [ring](ring.md) is

$$
I+J=\{a+b:a\in I,\ b\in J\}.
$$

It is the smallest [ideal](ideal.md) containing both $I$ and $J$: it is closed under subtraction and multiplication by ring elements, and any [ideal](ideal.md) containing $I$ and $J$ contains every such sum. The [ideals](ideal.md) are [comaximal ideals](comaximal-ideals.md) when $I+J=R$, equivalently when $1=a+b$ for some $a\in I$ and $b\in J$.
