# Magnetic bending in an incompressible disk

↑ **Parent:** [Horizontally invariant magnetized shearing-sheet equations](horizontally-invariant-magnetized-shearing-sheet-equations.md)

In the [steady state](steady-state.md) of the [horizontally invariant magnetized shearing-sheet equations](horizontally-invariant-magnetized-shearing-sheet-equations.md), let $B_x(\pm H)=\pm B_+$ and $B_y(\pm H)=0$. For $q>0$ and $B_z\ne0$, the symmetric equilibrium has

$$
B_x=B_+\frac{\sin(Kz)}{\sin(KH)},\quad B_y=v_x=0,\quad
v_y=-\frac{B_z}{2\mu_0\rho\Omega}\frac{dB_x}{dz},\qquad
K^2=\frac{2q\mu_0\rho\Omega^2}{B_z^2}.
$$

This follows by combining azimuthal induction with radial [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) balance to obtain $B_x''+K^2B_x=0$. Nonzero $B_+$ requires $\sin(KH)\ne0$; even homogeneous additions at $\cos(KH)=0$ are excluded by imposing midplane symmetry.
