# Uniform Stoner decoupling of the Hubbard interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Hubbard model](hubbard-model.md)

For spin imbalance $s=n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow$ per site, the uniform mean-field interaction energy is $U(n^2-s^2)/4$. With noninteracting [density of states](density-of-states.md) $D$ per spin per site, the kinetic cost is $s^2/(4D)$. The quadratic coefficient changes sign at $UD=1$, producing the Stoner mean-field instability. This does not describe every strong-coupling Hubbard phase.
