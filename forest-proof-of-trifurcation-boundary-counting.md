# Forest proof of trifurcation boundary counting

↑ **Parent:** [Trifurcation boundary-counting lemma](trifurcation-boundary-counting-lemma.md)

Delete a finite set of degree-three [trifurcation vertices in percolation](trifurcation-vertex-in-percolation.md) in each [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md) and contract the remaining components to nodes. The incidence graph is a [tree](tree-graph-theory.md): a cycle would bypass an original [trifurcation vertex in percolation](trifurcation-vertex-in-percolation.md). All leaf components are infinite, otherwise a [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) would have a finite branch after deletion. The [tree](tree-graph-theory.md) degree identity gives at least two more infinite leaves than degree-three [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). Distinct infinite components escape a containing box through distinct outer boundary [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). Hence the number of counted trifurcations is at most the outer boundary size.
