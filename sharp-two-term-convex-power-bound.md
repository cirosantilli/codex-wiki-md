# Sharp two-term convex power bound

↑ **Parent:** [Jensen's inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md)

Apply [Jensen's inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) to the two equally weighted values $a,b$ and the [convex function](convex-function.md) $t\mapsto t^p$. Equality for $a=b>0$ proves that $2^{p-1}$ is the smallest possible constant. For $p>1$, strict convexity makes equality equivalent to $a=b$; for $p=1$, equality holds for every pair.
