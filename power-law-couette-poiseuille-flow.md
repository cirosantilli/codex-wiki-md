# Power-law Couette-Poiseuille flow

↑ **Parent:** [Power-law fluid](power-law-fluid.md)

For plates at y=+-h moving at velocities +-U and pressure gradient -G, [force balance](force-balance.md) fixes the displayed stress. The [power-law fluid](power-law-fluid.md) law gives $u_y=(Gh/k)^{1/n}\operatorname{sgn}(\alpha-y/h)|\alpha-y/h|^{1/n}$. Integrating from the lower no-slip wall yields $u=-U+hn(Gh/k)^{1/n}[|\alpha+1|^{1+1/n}-|\alpha-y/h|^{1+1/n}]/(n+1)$. The upper wall fixes alpha uniquely for k,n,G,U\>0. Absolute values are essential when the shear reverses inside the gap.
