# Protonophore

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)

A substance facilitating proton transfer across a membrane and dissipating the proton electrochemical gradient. It can uncouple respiration from ATP synthesis without directly inhibiting every electron-transfer reaction.
