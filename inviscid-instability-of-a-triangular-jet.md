# Inviscid instability of a triangular jet

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md)

Decaying [normal modes](normal-mode.md) of this continuous piecewise-linear profile satisfy $\phi''-k^2\phi=0$ between the corners. [Pressure matching at a piecewise-linear shear interface](pressure-matching-at-a-piecewise-linear-shear-interface.md) yields an odd [varicose mode of a planar jet](varicose-mode-of-a-planar-jet.md) with real speed $c=(1-e^{-2k})/(2k)$ and an even [sinuous mode of a planar jet](sinuous-mode-of-a-planar-jet.md) whose speeds satisfy

$$
2k^2c^2+k(1-2k-e^{-2k})c-[1-k-(1+k)e^{-2k}]=0.
$$

Its reduced [discriminant](discriminant.md) factors as $(2k-3-e^{-2k}-4e^{-k})(2k-3-e^{-2k}+4e^{-k})$. The second factor is positive for $k>0$, while the first is strictly increasing and crosses zero once. Thus exactly the long-wave even branch is exponentially unstable, for $0<k<k_c$, where $2k_c-3-e^{-2k_c}-4e^{-k_c}=0$.
