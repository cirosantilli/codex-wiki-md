# Very weakly dominated strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Dominated strategy](dominated-strategy.md)

A [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md) is [very weakly dominated](very-weakly-dominated-strategy.md) by another strategy when its [payoff](payoff.md) is no greater against every opposing pure [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md), with no strict advantage required anywhere. The definition permits [payoff-equivalent strategies](payoff-equivalent-strategies.md) and even domination by the original strategy itself. A [weakly dominated strategy](weakly-dominated-strategy.md) instead has a strict inequality for at least one opposing [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md).

In a [binary game](binary-game.md), suppose $\sigma_i$ very weakly dominates $s_i$ and assigns positive [probability](probability.md) to a different pure action. At every opposing [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md) where $s_i$ pays one, all actions in the [support of a mixed strategy](support-of-a-mixed-strategy.md) must also pay one. Where $s_i$ pays zero, their [payoffs](payoff.md) are automatically no smaller. Therefore every distinct supported action very weakly dominates $s_i$. If the mixed domination is weak with a strict improvement somewhere, choose a supported action paying one at a profile where $s_i$ pays zero; that action also supplies a strict improvement. The requirement of nontrivial support is indispensable for the very-weak version.
