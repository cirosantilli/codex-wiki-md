# Linear hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) is linear when two distinct [hypergraph edges](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) intersect in at most one [vertex of a hypergraph](vertex-of-a-hypergraph.md). Equivalently, every pair of distinct [vertices of a hypergraph](vertex-of-a-hypergraph.md) has [hypergraph codegree](hypergraph-codegree.md) at most one. This restricts local overlap without bounding the [hypergraph chromatic number](hypergraph-chromatic-number.md): the [random alteration method](random-alteration-method.md) gives linear three-uniform [hypergraphs](hypergraph-split.md) of arbitrarily large [hypergraph chromatic number](hypergraph-chromatic-number.md).
