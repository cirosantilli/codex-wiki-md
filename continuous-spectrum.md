# Continuous spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (functional analysis)](spectrum-functional-analysis.md)

For a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) $T$, a real number $\lambda$ lies in the continuous [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) when $T-\lambda I$ is [injective](injective-function.md) with dense range but is not surjective. Such spectral values need not be [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of normalizable states. The free-particle [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) on the real line has a continuous energy [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md).
