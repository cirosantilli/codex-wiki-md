<h1 id="frohlich-hamiltonian">Fröhlich Hamiltonian</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-phonon interaction](electron-phonon-interaction.md)

A second-quantized electron-phonon Hamiltonian with free [electrons](electron.md), harmonic [phonons](phonon.md) and terms linear in [phonon](phonon.md) displacement, $M_qc_{k+q,\sigma}^{\dagger}c_{k,\sigma}(b_q+b_{-q}^{\dagger})$. The name is used for both general linear electron-phonon models and the more specific long-range coupling to polar optical modes; the intended dispersion and [matrix element](matrix-element.md) must be stated.
