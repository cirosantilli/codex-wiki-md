# Hofer nondegeneracy from rational Lagrangian displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Hofer metric](hofer-metric.md)

Assume the lower bound $e(L)\geq\gamma(L)/2$ for a closed [rational Lagrangian submanifold](rational-lagrangian-submanifold.md), with matching energy convention. A nonidentity Hamiltonian map displaces a sufficiently small ball about some moved point. Inside that ball place a [Lagrangian torus](lagrangian-torus.md) formed from equal-area circles. Its [least positive Liouville period](least-positive-liouville-period.md) is positive, so the map's norm is at least half this period. This proves positivity of the distance between different maps without appealing to nondegeneracy itself.
