# Two-electron exchange integral

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock method](hartree-fock-method.md)

For real [spatial orbitals](spatial-orbital.md), $K_{pq}=\iint\phi_p(1)\phi_q(1)r_{12}^{-1}\phi_q(2)\phi_p(2)\,d1\,d2$ in [atomic units](atomic-units.md). It enters the energy of a [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md) through antisymmetry and becomes small for well-separated localized orbitals.
