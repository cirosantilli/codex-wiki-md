# Mapping-out function of a compact H-hull

↑ **Parent:** [Compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md)

The mapping-out function is the unique [conformal map](conformal-map.md) $g_A:\mathbb H\setminus A\to\mathbb H$ with [hydrodynamic normalization at infinity](hydrodynamic-normalization-at-infinity.md)

$$
g_A(z)=z+\frac{a}{z}+O(|z|^{-2}).
$$
