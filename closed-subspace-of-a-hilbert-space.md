# Closed subspace of a Hilbert space

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md)

A [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) closed in the [norm topology](norm-topology.md) of a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) is complete in the inherited [norm](norm.md) and [inner product](inner-product.md). Its [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) gives a decomposition of the ambient [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) into an [orthogonal direct sum](orthogonal-direct-sum.md). This closedness permits [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) and ensures limits used in range arguments remain in the subspace. Every finite-dimensional [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) is closed.
