# One-reflection path through an equilateral prism

↑ **Parent:** [Prism (optics)](prism-optics.md)

For the lower-face-first ray path through an equilateral [optical prism](prism-optics.md), take external entry angle $\theta$, internal refraction angle $r$, base incidence $\alpha-r$ and one [total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md) at the horizontal base. At the other sloped face the internal incidence equals $r$, so [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) gives exit angle $\theta$ again. Its counterclockwise, oriented direction change is the displayed expression with $\alpha=60^\circ$. It is independent of [refractive index](refractive-index.md) for this route, but whether the base reflection is total is not. Under [normal dispersion](normal-dispersion.md), a setting at the green critical threshold sends shorter wavelengths along the total-reflection route while longer wavelengths can leak through the base.
