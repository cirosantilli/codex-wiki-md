# Join (graph theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Join_(graph_theory))

The join $\Gamma_1*\Gamma_2$ is obtained from the [disjoint union of graphs](disjoint-union-of-graphs.md) $\Gamma_1\sqcup\Gamma_2$ by adding every edge between a vertex of $\Gamma_1$ and a vertex of $\Gamma_2$.
