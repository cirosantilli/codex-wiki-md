# Guanidinium

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

[Guanidinium](guanidinium.md) is the resonance-stabilized cationic group found in protonated [arginine](arginine.md) side chains. Its N–H donors can make paired [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md) to the O6 and N7 acceptors on [guanine](guanine.md).
