# Brane

↑ **Parent:** [String theory](string-theory-split.md)

A brane is an extended object with $p$ spatial dimensions. Its history is a $(p+1)$-dimensional [worldvolume](worldvolume.md) embedded in target [spacetime](spacetime.md). A point particle has $p=0$, a string has $p=1$, and a membrane has $p=2$. Geometric brane actions depend on the [induced worldvolume metric](induced-worldvolume-metric.md); [D-branes](d-brane.md) also carry open-string endpoint and gauge-field data.
