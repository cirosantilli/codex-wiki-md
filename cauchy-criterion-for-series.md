# Cauchy criterion for series

↑ **Parent:** [Convergent series](convergent-series.md)

A [series](series-mathematics.md) over the [real numbers](real-number.md) or [complex numbers](complex-number.md) converges if and only if its tails satisfy: for every $\varepsilon>0$, some $N$ makes $|\sum_{k=n+1}^m a_k|<\varepsilon$ whenever $m>n\geq N$. This says exactly that its [partial sums](partial-sum.md) form a [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md); sufficiency uses [completeness](completeness.md).
