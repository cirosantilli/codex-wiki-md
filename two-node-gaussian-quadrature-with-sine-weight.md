# Two-node Gaussian quadrature with sine weight

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md)

For integration against $\sin x$ on $[0,\pi]$, the monic quadratic [orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial.md) is $x^2-\pi x+2$. Its two roots are the [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md) nodes $c_{1,2}=\pi/2\mp\sqrt{\pi^2/4-2}$, with weights one. The rule integrates every cubic exactly: divide it by the orthogonal quadratic and integrate the degree-one remainder.
