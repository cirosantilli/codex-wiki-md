# Cohort study

↑ **Parent:** [Longitudinal study](longitudinal-study.md)

A study following a defined group to relate earlier characteristics or [exposures](exposure.md) to later outcomes. Prospective measurement improves temporal ordering but does not automatically remove [confounding](confounding.md), selective attrition or [exposure](exposure.md) misclassification.
