# Integral scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced scheme](reduced-scheme.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_scheme)

An integral scheme is a nonempty [scheme](scheme.md) that is both [reduced](reduced-scheme.md) and [irreducible](irreducible-topological-space.md). An affine scheme $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is integral exactly when $A$ is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md).
