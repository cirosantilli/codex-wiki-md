# Stress-energy quadrupole identity

↑ **Parent:** [Mass quadrupole moment](mass-quadrupole-moment.md)

For a symmetric [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) with [compact support](compact-support.md) and $\partial_bT^{ab}=0$ in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), let $I^{ij}=\int T^{00}x^ix^j\,d^3x$. Two integrations by parts give $\dot I^{ij}=\int(T^{0i}x^j+T^{0j}x^i)\,d^3x$ and the displayed identity. Compact support removes the boundary fluxes. The support should stay bounded over the time interval used when differentiating the integrals.
