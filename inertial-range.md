# Inertial range

↑ **Parent:** [Energy cascade](energy-cascade.md)

The [inertial range](inertial-range.md) lies between the energy-containing and viscous scales. Direct forcing and [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) are small there, and nonlinear transfer carries an approximately constant mean [energy flux](energy-flux.md) in a three-dimensional forward cascade. Scale similarity and universality are additional statistical hypotheses.
