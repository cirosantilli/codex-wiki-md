# Diatomic molecule

↑ **Parent:** [Linear molecule](linear-molecule.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diatomic_molecule)

A [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md) contains two [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md); [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md) and [carbon monoxide](carbon-monoxide.md) are examples. Their fixed-separation [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) is $I=\mu r^2$ in the [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) model.
