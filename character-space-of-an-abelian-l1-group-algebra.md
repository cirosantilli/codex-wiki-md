# Character space of an Abelian L1 group algebra

↑ **Parent:** [L1 convolution algebra](l1-convolution-algebra.md)

For a locally compact Hausdorff Abelian group, each [continuous unitary character](continuous-unitary-character.md) defines an [algebra character](character-of-an-algebra.md) by $f\mapsto\int f(x)\chi(x)\,dx$. Conversely, if $\Phi$ is a nonzero [algebra character](character-of-an-algebra.md) and $\Phi(g)\ne0$, the ratio $\Phi(T_xg)/\Phi(g)$ is independent of $g$, continuous and multiplicative. Translation [isometries](isometry.md) bound all its integer powers, so its modulus is one. The [convolution](convolution.md) formula $f*g=\int f(x)T_xg\,dx$ recovers $\Phi(f)=\int f\chi$. The [Gelfand topology](gelfand-topology.md) corresponds to the [compact-open topology](compact-open-topology.md) on the [Pontryagin dual group](pontryagin-dual-group.md); conjugating the characters gives the alternative Fourier sign convention.
