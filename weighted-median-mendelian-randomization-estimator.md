# Weighted-median Mendelian randomization estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Mendelian randomization](mendelian-randomization.md)

This weighted median of genetic variant ratio estimates consistently estimates a common [causal effect](causal-effect.md) when more than half of its asymptotic weight comes from valid [instrumental variables](instrumental-variable.md), subject to the usual sampling regularity conditions. It is a [sensitivity analysis](sensitivity-analysis.md) for [horizontal pleiotropy](horizontal-pleiotropy.md), not an unconditional guarantee of validity.
