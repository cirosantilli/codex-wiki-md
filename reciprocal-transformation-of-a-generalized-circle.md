# Reciprocal transformation of a generalized circle

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized circle under a Möbius transformation](generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md)

Write a [generalized circle](generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md) equation as $a|z|^2+b\bar z+\bar bz+c=0$, where $a,c$ are real. Under the reciprocal [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) $z=1/w$, with $w\ne0$, multiply by $|w|^2$ to obtain

$$
c|w|^2+\bar b\bar w+bw+a=0.
$$

Thus the coefficient triple transforms as $(a,b,c)\mapsto(c,\bar b,a)$. A nondegenerate [circle](circle.md) through zero has $c=0$ and becomes a [straight line](straight-line.md); a [straight line](straight-line.md) missing zero becomes a [circle](circle.md) through zero on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md). When working only with finite nonzero coordinates, retain the excluded point $w=0$ even if it satisfies the multiplied equation.

This reciprocal transformation is different from [inversion in a circle](inversion-in-a-circle.md), whose unit-circle formula is $z\mapsto1/\bar z$. The latter reverses [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md); the reciprocal transformation is holomorphic away from its pole.
