# Direct versus indirect dark-matter detection

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter](dark-matter.md)

Direct detection measures scattering or absorption of incident halo particles in a target. Indirect detection searches for secondary products from annihilation or decay. Recording those secondary particles directly does not turn an indirect search into a direct halo-scattering measurement.
