# Harmonic-oscillator number-state second moments

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

For a [number operator](number-operator.md) [eigenstate](eigenstate.md) $|n\rangle$, the [annihilation operator](annihilation-operator.md) and [creation operator](creation-operator.md) satisfy $\langle a\rangle=\langle a^\dagger\rangle=\langle a^2\rangle=\langle(a^\dagger)^2\rangle=0$, $\langle a^\dagger a\rangle=n$ and $\langle aa^\dagger\rangle=n+1$. Inserting $x=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}(a+a^\dagger)$ and $p=i\sqrt{m\hbar\omega/2}(a^\dagger-a)$ gives the displayed second moments and zero means.

Thus the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) each have mean $\hbar\omega(n+1/2)/2$, and the uncertainty product is $\Delta x\Delta p=\hbar(n+1/2)$. Among number states only the ground state saturates the [Heisenberg uncertainty relation](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md). A [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md) can have nonzero position and momentum means despite each constituent number state's zero means.
