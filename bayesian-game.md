# Bayesian game

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

A Bayesian game gives players private information about their types, a probability model for that information, and strategies depending on the player's own type. A [private-value auction](private-value-auction.md) with uncertain other valuations is a Bayesian game.
