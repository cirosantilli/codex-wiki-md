# Pulley coupled to a rolling disc

↑ **Parent:** [Effective mass of a rolling rigid body](effective-mass-of-a-rolling-rigid-body.md)

A hanging [mass](mass.md) $m$ pulls the centre of a uniform disc of [mass](mass.md) $M$ up an incline of angle $\theta$, using a light string and light frictionless pulley. If the disc rolls without slipping and the string is parallel to the slope, its effective translational [mass](mass.md) is $M+I/a^2=3M/2$. With downward hanging [acceleration](acceleration.md) $q$, $q=g(m-M\sin\theta)/(m+3M/2)$. [Static friction](static-friction.md) has signed downslope component $Mq/2$ and supplies the rolling [torque](torque.md). The [acceleration](acceleration.md) threshold is unchanged from a sliding block, although rotational inertia reduces the magnitude.
