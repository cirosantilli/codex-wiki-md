# Open dust distance-redshift relation

↑ **Parent:** [Luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md)

For an open dust-only [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) with zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) and $0<\Omega<1$, $H(z)=H_0(1+z)\sqrt{1+\Omega z}$. The present radial [cosmological proper distance](proper-distance-in-cosmology.md) is $d_S=\int_0^z dz'/H(z')$, and the areal radial coordinate satisfies $a_0r=\sinh[H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega}\,d_S]/[H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega}]$. Integrating with $v=\sqrt{1+\Omega z}$ gives the displayed relation. It has $r\sim z/(a_0H_0)$ near the observer and $r\to2/(a_0H_0\Omega)$ as $z\to\infty$. The [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md) is $(1+z)a_0r$, not the radial [cosmological proper distance](proper-distance-in-cosmology.md).
