# Connected-sum gluing of functions

↑ **Parent:** [Connected sum of oriented manifolds](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md)

Remove small [disks](disk-mathematics.md) around a nondegenerate maximum of a [smooth function](smooth-function.md) on one closed [surface](topological-surface.md) and a nondegenerate minimum on another. Rescale their values so the boundary values agree with the two ends of a neck. Their regular level collars allow a strictly monotone function on the neck, with nonzero derivative, matching smoothly to both sides. The resulting [connected sum](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) has all the old [critical points](critical-point.md) except the deleted maximum and minimum. This permits one degenerate [torus](torus.md) saddle to be retained while adding ordinary [handles](handle.md) for higher genus.
