<h1 id="lubin-tate-cyclotomic-example">Lubin–Tate cyclotomic example</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lubin–Tate extension](lubin-tate-extension.md)

For $K=\mathbb Q_p$ and $\pi=p$, choose the [Lubin–Tate series](lubin-tate-series.md) $f(T)=(1+T)^p-1$. The associated [formal group law](formal-group-law.md) is the [formal multiplicative group](formal-multiplicative-group.md). Its torsion points are $\zeta-1$ for $\zeta^{p^n}=1$, so its [Lubin–Tate extension](lubin-tate-extension.md) is the [cyclotomic extension of a p-adic field](cyclotomic-extension-of-a-p-adic-field.md) $\mathbb Q_p(\mu_{p^n})$. The Galois action $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^a$ gives $(\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z)^{\times}$, including the trivial first layer when $p=2$.
