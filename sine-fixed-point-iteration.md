# Sine fixed-point iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Iterated function](iterated-function.md)

The [sine fixed-point iteration](sine-fixed-point-iteration.md) converges to zero from every real starting value. After one step, $|x_n|\leq1$, and $|x_{n+1}|=\sin|x_n|$. Since $0<\sin t<t$ for $0<t\leq1$, the absolute values form a bounded [monotone sequence](monotone-sequence.md). Its limit $a$ satisfies $a=\sin a$ by [continuity](continuous-function.md), forcing $a=0$. This proves convergence even though the [sine](sine.md) is not a [contraction mapping](contraction-mapping.md) on any [interval](interval-mathematics.md) containing zero in its interior.
