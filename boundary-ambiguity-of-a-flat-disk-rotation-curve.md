# Boundary ambiguity of a flat disk rotation curve

↑ **Parent:** [Mestel disk potential-density pair](mestel-disk-potential-density-pair.md)

The midplane [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md) fixes the radial derivative of the [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md), not every exterior boundary condition. Adding $a|z|$ leaves that radial derivative unchanged but adds a uniform sheet of [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $a/(2\pi G)$. The pure scale-free [Mestel disk](mestel-disk.md) excludes such extra sources.
