# Parabolic basin

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed Fatou component classification](fixed-fatou-component-classification.md)

A parabolic basin consists of [Fatou components](fatou-component.md) whose iterates approach a parabolic periodic orbit.

A parabolic basin consists of points whose iterates converge to a parabolic cycle along a specified attracting direction. Each connected basin component lies in the Fatou set, and its boundary lies in the Julia set.
