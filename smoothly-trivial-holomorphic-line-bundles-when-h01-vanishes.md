# Smoothly trivial holomorphic line bundles when H01 vanishes

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md)

A nowhere-zero smooth section $s$ of a [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md) writes its [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md) as $\bar\partial_Ls=a\otimes s$. Its integrability gives $\bar\partial a=0$. If $H^{0,1}(X)=0$, then $a=\bar\partial f$ globally. The section $e^{-f}s$ is nowhere zero and [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md). This argument requires no [compactness](compact-space.md) and explicitly constructs the [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) trivialization.
