# Stationary trapped Brownian displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md)

For overdamped [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) in potential $\kappa x^2/2$, the [relaxation time](relaxation-time.md) is $\tau=\zeta/\kappa$ and the equilibrium [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) is $(k_BT/\kappa)e^{-|t|/\tau}$. Expanding the squared displacement between two equilibrium samples gives the title expression. Initializing the particle exactly at the centre instead gives [variance](variance-split.md) $(k_BT/\kappa)(1-e^{-2t/\tau})$. Both are valid but concern different initial ensembles; their saturation values differ by a factor of two. These formulas neglect inertia and require times long compared with [momentum](momentum-split.md) relaxation.
