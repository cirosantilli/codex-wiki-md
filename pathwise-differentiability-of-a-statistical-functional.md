# Pathwise differentiability of a statistical functional

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical functional](statistical-functional.md)

A statistical functional is pathwise differentiable relative to chosen [statistical paths](statistical-path.md) if its [derivative](derivative.md) along every path depends only on that path's [score function](informant-function.md) and defines a [bounded linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) on their [statistical tangent space](statistical-tangent-space.md). The [Riesz representation theorem](riesz-representation-theorem.md) expresses this [derivative](derivative.md) as an [L2 inner product](l2-inner-product.md) with a unique element of the [statistical tangent space](statistical-tangent-space.md), the [canonical gradient](canonical-gradient.md). A path family and the associated [derivative](derivative.md) remainder conditions must both be specified; a formal [derivative](derivative.md) along one convenient family does not establish differentiability along all paths.
