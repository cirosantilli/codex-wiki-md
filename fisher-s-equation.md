<h1 id="fisher-s-equation">Fisher's equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling wave](travelling-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fisher's_equation)

[Fisher's equation](fisher-s-equation.md) is a [reaction-diffusion equation](reaction-diffusion-system.md) combining diffusion with logistic growth. For positive $D,r,K$, one-dimensional fronts joining $K$ to zero have minimum speed $2\sqrt{Dr}$. The [phase-plane construction of a Fisher travelling front](phase-plane-construction-of-a-fisher-travelling-front.md) and the linearized leading edge explain this threshold.
