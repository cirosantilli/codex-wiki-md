# Two hinged rods with both ends sliding on a floor

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics.md)

Two identical uniform [rigid bodies](rigid-body-dynamics.md) of rod shape, each of length $2l$ and mass $M$, form a symmetric arch with a free common hinge and both outer ends sliding without friction on a horizontal floor. At angle $\theta$, each center has [speed](speed.md) $l|\dot\theta|$, giving total [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $4Ml^2\dot\theta^2/3$. The total gravitational [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is $2Mgl\sin\theta$. [Conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) from rest at $\alpha$ gives the displayed hinge impact [speed](speed.md). Unlike fixing one outer endpoint, allowing both endpoints to slide keeps the arch center horizontally fixed. The floor reaction on each rod is $Mg[1+9\sin^2\theta-6\sin\alpha\sin\theta]/4$, nonnegative throughout this fall.
