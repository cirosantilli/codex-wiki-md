# Determinant-one cyclic quotient of the affine plane

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md)

The action $(x,y)\mapsto(\zeta x,\zeta^{-1}y)$ has invariant [monomials](monomial.md) exactly when their two exponents differ by a multiple of $n$. Every such [monomial](monomial.md) is a product of $x^n,y^n,xy$. Reducing by $uv=w^n$ leaves the [monomials](monomial.md) $w^j$, $u^iw^j$ and $v^iw^j$ with $i\ge1$; their images are distinct [monomials](monomial.md) in $x,y$, proving that this is the only relation. The map $(x,y)\mapsto(x^n,y^n,xy)$ is finite and its fibres are the finite-group orbits, so the hypersurface is the [geometric quotient](geometric-quotient.md).
