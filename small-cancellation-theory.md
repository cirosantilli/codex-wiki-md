# Small cancellation theory

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_cancellation_theory)

Small cancellation theory controls overlap between [relators](relator.md) or attaching paths of polygonal two-cells. Requiring overlaps to be small compared with cell perimeters gives strong [disc diagram](disc-diagram.md) structure, shortening algorithms and geometric restrictions on subgroups. The [metric small cancellation condition](metric-small-cancellation-condition.md) $C'(1/6)$ is a central example.
