# Schwarzschild static observer

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md)

An observer at fixed exterior Schwarzschild spatial coordinates. Its [proper time](proper-time.md) is $dT=\sqrt f\,dt$, with $f=1-2GM/(rc^2)>0$. It is accelerated, not freely falling; no such static timelike [worldline](world-line.md) exists on or inside the [event horizon](event-horizon.md).
