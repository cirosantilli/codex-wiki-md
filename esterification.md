# Esterification

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophilic acyl substitution](nucleophilic-acyl-substitution.md)

Formation of an [ester](ester.md) from an acyl component and an [alcohol](alcohol.md). Intramolecular [esterification](esterification.md) of a hydroxy acid makes a [lactone](lactone.md). [Fischer esterification](fischer-esterification.md) is the acid-catalysed carboxylic-acid/[alcohol](alcohol.md) case.
