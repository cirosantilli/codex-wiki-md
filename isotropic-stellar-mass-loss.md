# Isotropic stellar mass loss

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary system dynamics](planetary-system-dynamics.md)

[Isotropic stellar mass loss](isotropic-stellar-mass-loss.md) changes the central gravitational parameter $\mu(t)$ while preserving the central direction of the force, provided the escaping material causes no recoil or drag on the orbiting body. The relative equation is $\ddot{\mathbf r}=-\mu(t)\mathbf r/r^3$. [Specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) remains exactly constant; [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) obeys $\dot C=-\dot\mu/r$. Orbital expansion is adiabatic only when the [mass](mass.md)-loss law varies slowly on the [orbital period](orbital-period.md).
