# Constant relative risk aversion utility

↑ **Parent:** [Utility function](utility-function-split.md)

A constant relative risk aversion utility has $-cU^{\prime\prime}(c)/U^\prime(c)=R>0$. Up to a positive scale and an additive constant, it is $U(c)=c^{1-R}/(1-R)$ for $R\ne1$ and $U(c)=\log c$ for $R=1$. Power utility gives multiplicative [homogeneity](homogeneity.md) of an investment value; logarithmic utility gives additive scaling.
