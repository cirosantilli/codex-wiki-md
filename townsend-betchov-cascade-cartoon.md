# Townsend-Betchov cascade cartoon

↑ **Parent:** [Energy cascade](energy-cascade.md)

The [Townsend-Betchov cascade cartoon](townsend-betchov-cascade-cartoon.md) organizes [inertial range](inertial-range.md) [vorticity](vorticity.md) into sheets by [bi-axial strain](bi-axial-strain.md), then uses sheet instability and roll-up to generate finer [vortex tubes](vortex-tube.md). Dissipative tube cores balance stretching with [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md). Such differing mechanisms across scales challenge literal identical-step, memoryless cascade models without contradicting a mean [energy flux](energy-flux.md).
