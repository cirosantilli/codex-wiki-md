# Hypergraph isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A hypergraph isomorphism is a [bijection](bijection.md) of the [vertex sets](vertex-set.md) such that a subset is a [hyperedge](hyperedge.md) of the first [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) if and only if its image is a [hyperedge](hyperedge.md) of the second. The condition preserves both edges and nonedges. In the two-uniform case it is the usual relation of [isomorphic graphs](graph-isomorphism.md).
