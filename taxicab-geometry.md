# Taxicab geometry

↑ **Parent:** [L1 norm](l1-norm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxicab_geometry)

Taxicab geometry equips Euclidean coordinate space with the distance induced by the [L1 norm](l1-norm.md), so the distance between two points is the sum of their coordinatewise absolute differences.
