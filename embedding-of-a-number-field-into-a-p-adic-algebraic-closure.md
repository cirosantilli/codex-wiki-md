# Embedding of a number field into a p-adic algebraic closure

↑ **Parent:** [Place of a number field](place-of-a-number-field.md)

Such a [field embedding](field-embedding.md) pulls the [p-adic absolute value](p-adic-absolute-value.md) back to a finite [place of a number field](place-of-a-number-field.md) above $p$. It extends to an embedding of the corresponding completion, whose image is a finite extension of the [P-adic numbers](p-adic-number.md).
