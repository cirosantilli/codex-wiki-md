# Potentiometric titration

↑ **Parent:** [Titration curve](titration-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potentiometric_titration)

A [potentiometric titration](potentiometric-titration.md) monitors an [electrode](electrode.md) [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) as [titrant](titrant.md) is added. A calibrated [glass electrode](glass-electrode.md) can report [pH](ph.md) during an [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md).
