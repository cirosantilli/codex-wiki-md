# Vortex sheet in a symmetric rigid channel

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin-Helmholtz instability](kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md)

Consider an equal-density [vortex sheet](vortex-sheet.md) halfway between rigid walls, with equal layer depths $h$, base [velocities](velocity.md) $U_1,U_2$ and no restoring interfacial force. The irrotational perturbations have vertical factors $\cosh(k(y-h))$ above and $\cosh(k(y+h))$ below. Their [kinematic boundary conditions](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) and [pressure continuity](pressure-continuity.md) give

$$
\coth(|k|h)\left[(\omega-kU_1)^2+(\omega-kU_2)^2\right]=0.
$$

The common depth factor cancels, yielding the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) roots and [Kelvin-Helmholtz instability](kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md) for nonzero shear. The real [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) of the pattern is $(U_1+U_2)/2$ and the growing branch has [temporal growth rate](growth-rate.md) $|k(U_1-U_2)|/2$. This exact depth cancellation depends on equal depths and equal [mass densities](density.md); a single nearby wall or unequal layers changes the inertia factors.
