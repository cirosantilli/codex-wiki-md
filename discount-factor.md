# Discount factor

↑ **Parent:** [Interest rate](interest-rate.md)

A multiplicative factor converting a value into units of a chosen initial account. With the [continuous-time bank account](continuous-time-bank-account.md) $B$, $D_t=1/B_t$; discounted traded asset prices are [martingales](martingale-split.md) under the corresponding [equivalent martingale measure](risk-neutral-measure.md).
