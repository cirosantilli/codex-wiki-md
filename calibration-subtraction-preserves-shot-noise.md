# Calibration subtraction preserves shot noise

↑ **Parent:** [Photon shot noise](photon-shot-noise.md)

Subtracting a reproducible sky, dark or bias mean removes its offset but does not undo random arrivals or electronic fluctuations already present in a science exposure. An independently estimated [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) adds its own uncertainty. Longer integration reduces relative independent counting [noise](noise-signal-processing.md); correlated backgrounds and systematic residuals need separate models and may set an accuracy floor.
