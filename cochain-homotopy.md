# Cochain homotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Chain homotopy](chain-homotopy.md)

For cochain maps $f,g:C^\bullet\to D^\bullet$, a cochain homotopy is a family $K:C^q\to D^{q-1}$ satisfying

$$
f-g=dK+Kd.
$$

Cochain-homotopic maps induce the same map on [cohomology](cohomology-split.md).
