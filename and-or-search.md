# AND-OR search

↑ **Parent:** [Search algorithm](search-algorithm.md)

A [search algorithm](search-algorithm.md) whose OR nodes represent controllable choices and whose AND nodes require solutions for every possible outcome of a chosen action. In [nondeterministic planning](nondeterministic-planning.md), a successful OR choice needs successful continuations for all its AND children. In [contingent planning](contingent-planning.md), those children can represent different observations and updated [belief states](belief-state.md). Cycle handling must match the required solution concept: ignoring a repeated state is not a proof that the goal will inevitably be reached.
