# Conformal optical metric for scalar perturbations

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gauge](newtonian-gauge.md)

Rescale the perturbed cosmological metric by $a^{-2}(1+2\phi)$. To first order, its spatial part is Euclidean and only its lapse is perturbed. The unparameterized [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) are unchanged. The nonzero linear connection coefficients are $\widehat\Gamma^0_{00}=2\partial_\eta\Psi$, $\widehat\Gamma^0_{0i}=2\partial_i\Psi$ and $\widehat\Gamma^i_{00}=2\partial^i\Psi$. The affine parameter is that of the rescaled metric, not necessarily that of the original spacetime.
