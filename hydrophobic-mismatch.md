# Hydrophobic mismatch

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane protein](membrane-protein.md)

[hydrophobic mismatch](hydrophobic-mismatch.md) is an energetic incompatibility between a membrane protein's hydrophobic span and the hydrophobic thickness of its surrounding [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md). Local deformation, altered conformation or changed association can partly accommodate it.
