# Blind reverse fault

↑ **Parent:** [Reverse faulting](reverse-faulting.md)

A [fault](geological-fault.md) with reverse slip whose upper tip remains beneath the ground surface. It can deform the overburden through [folding](folding.md), localized [fold scarps](fold-scarp.md), and secondary faults.
