# Pierolapithecus

↑ **Parent:** [Great ape](great-ape.md)

A [Miocene](miocene.md) fossil ape with a mosaic of trunk and hand characters. Its informative anatomy does not uniquely identify it as the [Pan](pan.md)–[Homo](homo.md) [last common ancestor](last-common-ancestor.md). [Science 306, pp. 1339–1344; orthograde trunk and contrasting hand morphology](pierolapithecus-catalaunicus-a-new-middle-miocene-great-ape-from-spain-by-salvador-moya-sola-and-others.md)
