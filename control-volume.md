# Control volume

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A [control volume](control-volume.md) is a chosen region over which fluid [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) and momentum balance are integrated. It may be fixed or move with a prescribed [velocity](velocity.md). Boundary fluxes must use [velocity](velocity.md) relative to the moving boundary. In [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md), negligible inertia reduces the momentum balance to the sum of body [forces](force.md) and boundary tractions, making a carefully chosen [control volume](control-volume.md) useful for finding drag without integrating directly over a curved body.
