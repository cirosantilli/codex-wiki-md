# Turing degree

↑ **Parent:** [Turing reduction](turing-reduction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_degree)

The Turing degree of a set is its equivalence class under mutual [Turing reducibility](turing-reduction.md). Degrees inherit the partial order induced by Turing reducibility. The [Friedberg–Muchnik theorem](friedberg-muchnik-theorem.md) gives incomparable degrees represented by [computably enumerable sets](recursively-enumerable-set.md).
