# Asymptotic cold-dark-matter power spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Cold-dark-matter transfer function](cold-dark-matter-transfer-function.md)

The dimensional [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md) contains a factor $k^{n_s}$, rather than $k^{n_s+3}$. The [cosmological transfer function](cosmological-transfer-function.md) tends to one below the [matter-radiation equality scale](matter-radiation-equality-scale.md) and to $\ln(k/k_{\rm eq})/(k/k_{\rm eq})^2$ above it. The smooth spectrum therefore scales as $k^{n_s}$ at small wavenumber and $k^{n_s-4}\ln^2(k/k_{\rm eq})$ at large wavenumber. The logarithmic factor records slow cold-matter growth during [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md). Multiplying by $k^3$ instead produces dimensionless matter power.
