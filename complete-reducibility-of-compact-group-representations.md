# Complete reducibility of compact-group representations

↑ **Parent:** [Unitarization of a compact-group representation](unitarization-of-a-compact-group-representation.md)

A finite-dimensional [complex representation](complex-representation.md) of a [compact group](compact-group.md) is a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md). Average a positive [Hermitian inner product](hermitian-form.md) over normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md). The [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) of an [invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md) is then invariant, so induction on [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) gives the decomposition. The same argument works for finite-dimensional [real representations](real-representation.md) with a positive real [inner product](inner-product.md).
