# Invariant volume form on a Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Left-invariant differential form](left-invariant-differential-form.md)

Choose a nonzero element of $\Lambda^nT_e^*G$ for an $n$-dimensional [Lie group](lie-group.md) and transport it by inverse left translations. This gives a smooth nowhere-zero [left-invariant differential form](left-invariant-differential-form.md) of top degree, hence an [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md). The identity $L_{(hg)^{-1}}L_h=L_{g^{-1}}$ proves invariance. The space of invariant top forms is one-dimensional, since evaluation at the identity identifies it with $\Lambda^nT_e^*G$.
