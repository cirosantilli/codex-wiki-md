# Cloning failure bound from trace-distance contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum cloning](quantum-cloning.md)

For two [pure states](pure-state.md) of overlap $0<c<1$, a [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) attempts to turn $M$ copies into $N>M$ copies. Let $e_i$ be the failure probability of the all-copies projector test on input $i$. The [pure-state trace distance and fidelity identity](pure-state-trace-distance-and-fidelity-identity.md), [trace-distance contraction under quantum channels](trace-distance-contraction-under-quantum-channels.md), [pure-target upper bound on trace distance](pure-target-upper-bound-on-trace-distance.md) and [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) give the displayed strictly positive gap $\Delta$. Hence $\max_i e_i\geq\Delta^2/4$, and for positive priors $\pi_0+\pi_1=1$, weighted [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\pi_0e_0+\pi_1e_1\geq\pi_0\pi_1\Delta^2$. No positive state-by-state bound holds: preparing one candidate perfectly makes its own conditional failure probability zero.
