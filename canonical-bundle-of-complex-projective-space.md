# Canonical bundle of complex projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Euler sequence on complex projective space](euler-sequence-on-complex-projective-space.md)

Dualizing the determinant identity in the [Euler sequence on complex projective space](euler-sequence-on-complex-projective-space.md) gives

$$
K_{\mathbb{CP}^n}\cong\mathcal O(-n-1)\cong[-(n+1)H],
$$

where $H$ is a [hyperplane divisor](hyperplane-divisor.md).
