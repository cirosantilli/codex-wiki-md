# Bond polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Electronegativity](electronegativity.md)

[Bond polarization](bond-polarization.md) is unequal sharing of [electron density](electron-density.md) across a [chemical bond](chemical-bond.md). An [electronegativity](electronegativity.md) difference is a heuristic for its direction and tendency, not a unique [partial atomic charge](partial-atomic-charge.md).
