# Matching covering maximum-degree vertices in a bipartite graph

↑ **Parent:** [Matching (graph theory)](matching-graph-theory-split.md)

A finite [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md) of positive [maximum degree](maximum-degree.md) $\Delta$ has a [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory-split.md) covering every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) of [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) $\Delta$. To cover the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) of [maximum degree](maximum-degree.md) in one side $A$, apply [Hall's marriage theorem](hall-s-marriage-theorem.md) to that [subset](subset.md): for each [subset](subset.md) $S$ of it, [double counting](double-counting-proof-technique.md) gives $\Delta|S|\leq\Delta|N(S)|$, where $N(S)$ is its [graph neighbourhood](graph-neighbourhood.md). Construct the corresponding [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory-split.md) for the other side, then use the [union of two matchings](union-of-two-matchings.md) to cover both specified [subsets](subset.md) simultaneously.
