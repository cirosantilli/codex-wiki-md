# Hydrodynamic limit of the Boltzmann equation

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann equation](boltzmann-equation.md)

In a scaling $\partial_tf+v\cdot\nabla_xf=\varepsilon^{-1}Q(f,f)$ with small [Knudsen number](knudsen-number.md), the leading collision balance formally forces a [local Maxwellian](local-maxwellian.md). The conserved moments then give the [Euler equations](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) with a monatomic ideal-gas closure. The next nonequilibrium correction produces viscous and heat-conduction terms. These substitutions are formal: rigorous limits need uniform estimates, initial and boundary conditions, and sufficient convergence.
