# First-order canonical removal of a cubic oscillator perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical transformation](canonical-transformation.md)

The accompanying [momentum](momentum-split.md) map is $p=P+2\lambda XP/(m\omega^2)$. Its [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md) is one up to second-order corrections. Substitution into $p^2/(2m)+m\omega^2x^2/2+\lambda x^3$ cancels all terms linear in $\lambda$, leaving a harmonic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) to that order. The map is local and perturbative, not an exact global removal of an unbounded cubic potential.
