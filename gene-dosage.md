# Gene dosage

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)

The number of copies of a [gene](gene.md) available in the specified [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) or [genotype](genotype.md). A dosage change can alter an outcome even before duplicated copies diverge in function.
