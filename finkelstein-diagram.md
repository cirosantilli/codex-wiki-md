# Finkelstein diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md)

A [Finkelstein diagram](finkelstein-diagram.md) plots [areal radius](areal-radius.md) $r$ against $T=v-r$ in [Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](ingoing-eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md). Its radial [null directions](null-directions.md) show explicitly that both future light-ray families decrease $r$ inside a [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md). It is not a compactified [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md).
