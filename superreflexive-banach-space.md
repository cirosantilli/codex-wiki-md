# Superreflexive Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Finite representability of a Banach space](finite-representability-of-a-banach-space.md)

A Banach space is superreflexive when every Banach space finitely representable in it is a [reflexive Banach space](reflexive-banach-space.md). Equivalently, it admits an equivalent uniformly convex norm, or every one of its ultrapowers is reflexive.
