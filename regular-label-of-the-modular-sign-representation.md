# Regular label of the modular sign representation

↑ **Parent:** [Regular partition](regular-partition.md)

Write $n=q(p-1)+r$, $0\le r<p-1$. The [regular partition](regular-partition.md) labelling the [sign representation](sign-representation.md) in characteristic $p>0$ is $\lambda=((q+1)^r,q^{p-1-r})$, with zero parts removed. Its [conjugate partition](conjugate-partition.md) is $((p-1)^q,r)$, which satisfies the [invariant vector criterion for a Specht module](invariant-vector-criterion-for-a-specht-module.md). The sign-twisted duality of [Specht modules](specht-module.md) turns this invariant line into a sign quotient of $S^\lambda$, necessarily its unique simple [head](head-of-a-module.md). Conversely, regularity says consecutive column heights differ by less than $p$; combining this with the invariant congruences forces these heights to be $p-1$ except possibly the last. For $p=2$, the label is $(n)$ because sign equals the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md).
