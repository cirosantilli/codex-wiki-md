# Transitive closure is Delta-one definable

↑ **Parent:** [Transitive closure](transitive-closure.md)

The [transitive closure](transitive-closure.md) is obtained by $x_0=x$, $x_{n+1}=\bigcup x_n$, $t=\bigcup_{n<\omega}x_n$. This is transitive, contains $x$ as a subset, and is contained in any other [transitive set](transitive-set.md) containing $x$, by induction. Its graph has a [Sigma-one formula in set theory](sigma-one-formula-in-set-theory.md) saying there exist $\omega$, a sequence with this recursion, and its range. The [bounded definition of omega](bounded-definition-of-omega.md), function and union predicates keep the matrix bounded. Existence and uniqueness in [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) express failure of $y=\operatorname{TC}(x)$ by the existential assertion that the construction produces some $z\ne y$. Thus the graph is a [Delta-one formula modulo ZF](delta-one-formula-modulo-zf.md), and [Delta-one absoluteness](delta-one-absoluteness.md) makes [transitive closure](transitive-closure.md) absolute between nested [transitive models](transitive-model.md) of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md).
