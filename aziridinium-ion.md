# Aziridinium ion

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

A positively charged three-membered [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) heterocycle. Intramolecular formation from a tertiary amino [alcohol](alcohol.md) with an activated hydroxyl can be followed by regioselective nucleophilic ring opening and net ring expansion.
