<h1 id="o-n-model">O(N) model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md)

The $O(N)$ model has an $N$-component real field and an [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) symmetry. Its standard Landau-Ginzburg interaction is $g(\boldsymbol\phi\cdot\boldsymbol\phi)^2$; below the upper critical dimension its continuous transition is governed by an $O(N)$ [Wilson-Fisher fixed point](wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md).
