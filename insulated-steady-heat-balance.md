# Insulated steady heat balance

↑ **Parent:** [Heat balance with volumetric heat generation](heat-balance-with-volumetric-heat-generation.md)

For a [steady state](steady-state.md) [heat equation](heat-equation.md) $\nabla\cdot(k\nabla T)+H=0$ in a bounded region with zero outward [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md), integration and the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) give the necessary compatibility condition

$$
\int_RH\,dV=0.
$$

In particular, a strictly positive [PDE source term](source-term-in-a-partial-differential-equation.md) throughout a region of positive [volume](volume.md) makes a [steady state](steady-state.md) impossible. The time-dependent [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) law instead gives $dE/dt=\int_RH\,dV>0$: stored thermal [energy](energy-split.md) must grow. This is a compatibility condition on the total source, not a restriction requiring the source to vanish pointwise.
