# Bounded-increment martingale convergence-or-oscillation dichotomy

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded increments](bounded-increments.md)

A discrete-time [martingale](martingale-split.md) with a common deterministic bound on all increments either converges finitely or has $\liminf X_n=-\infty$ and $\limsup X_n=+\infty$, [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). Stopping on first crossing below $-A$ gives a lower bound $-A-M$ on the overshoot. Adding $A+M+X_0^-$ produces a nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md), so the [martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md) forces finite convergence on the event of never crossing. Apply the same argument to $-X$.
