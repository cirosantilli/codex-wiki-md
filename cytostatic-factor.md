# Cytostatic factor

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)

[Cytostatic factor](cytostatic-factor.md) denotes activity maintaining the meiotic arrest of an unfertilized vertebrate egg. Calcium-dependent release of arrest permits the regulated destruction of mitotic cyclins and completion of meiosis.
