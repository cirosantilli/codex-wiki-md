# Rest frame

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial frame](inertial-frame.md)

The rest frame of a massive particle is an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) in which its spatial [momentum](momentum-split.md) is zero. Its [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) is then $(mc,\mathbf0)$ and its energy is $mc^2$. Such a frame exists for a future-directed timelike four-momentum: the [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) with boost velocity $\mathbf v=c^2\mathbf p/E$ makes the spatial momentum zero, and the [mass shell](mass-shell.md) relation implies $|\mathbf v|<c$. A massless particle has no rest frame, because $E=c|\mathbf p|$ would require a boost with speed $c$.
