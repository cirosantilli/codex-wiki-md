# Cross-lagged panel model

↑ **Parent:** [Longitudinal study](longitudinal-study.md)

A model relating repeated measures of variables across occasions. Conventional cross-lagged paths can mix stable between-person differences with [within-person change](within-person-change.md) and do not automatically identify [causal effects](causal-effect.md).
