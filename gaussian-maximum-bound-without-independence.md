# Gaussian maximum bound without independence

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian maximum](gaussian-maximum.md)

For $N$ variables with the [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md), the expected maximum of their absolute values is at most $\sqrt{2\log(2N)}$, even when they are dependent. Bound the exponential of the maximum by the sum of the $2N$ signed exponentials, use the normal [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md), then apply [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) and minimize over the exponential parameter. The resulting logarithmic growth is useful in simultaneous estimation.
