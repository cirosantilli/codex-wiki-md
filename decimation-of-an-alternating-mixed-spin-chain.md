# Decimation of an alternating mixed-spin chain

↑ **Parent:** [Transfer matrix for a classical spin chain](transfer-matrix-for-a-classical-spin-chain.md)

For [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) $s,t=\pm1$ separated by a decorating [spin](spin.md) $\tau=0,\pm1$, summing its [Boltzmann weights](boltzmann-factor.md) removes that [spin](spin.md) exactly. If its two [exchange interactions](exchange-interaction.md) are $-\tau(J_1s+J_2t)$, the effective [transfer matrix](transfer-matrix.md) has entries $1+2\cosh[\beta(J_1s+J_2t)]$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\lambda_+=2+4\cosh(\beta J_1)\cosh(\beta J_2)$ and $\lambda_-=4\sinh(\beta J_1)\sinh(\beta J_2)$. A transfer step contains two physical sites, so the limiting [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) per site is $-(2\beta)^{-1}\log\lambda_+$.
