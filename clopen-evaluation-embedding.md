# Clopen evaluation embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-dimensional space](zero-dimensional-space.md)

The [characteristic functions](characteristic-function-split.md) of a [clopen](clopen-set.md) [topological basis](basis-of-a-topology.md) give a [topological embedding](topological-embedding.md) of a zero-dimensional $T_0$ [topological space](topological-space.md) into a product of discrete two-point [topological spaces](topological-space.md). The [separation](axiom-schema-of-specification.md) axiom makes those [functions](function-split.md) distinguish points, and their coordinate preimages recover the original [topological basis](basis-of-a-topology.md).
