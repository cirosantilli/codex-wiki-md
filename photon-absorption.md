# Photon absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Photon](photon.md)

[Photon](photon.md) absorption removes a [photon](photon.md) and transfers its [energy](energy.md) and [momentum](momentum.md) to matter. It contributes a negative photon-number collision source. It differs from [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md), which redistributes a [photon](photon.md)'s [energy](energy.md) and direction while preserving the count. The integrated source can vanish if [photon emission](photon-emission.md) and absorption balance.
