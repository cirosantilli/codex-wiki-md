<h1 id="levy-identity-for-brownian-motion">Lévy identity for Brownian motion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian running maximum](brownian-running-maximum.md)

For a standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) $B$, its [Brownian running maximum](brownian-running-maximum.md) $M$, and its [local time of a semimartingale](local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md) $L^0$ at zero, the process pairs satisfy

$$
(M_t-B_t,M_t)_{t\geq0}
\overset d=
(|B_t|,L_t^0)_{t\geq0}.
$$

In particular, $M-B$ and $|B|$ are both [Reflected Brownian motion](reflected-brownian-motion.md) and have the same law as processes.
