# Recursive procedure

↑ **Parent:** [Recursion (computer science)](recursion-computer-science.md)

A procedure whose definition invokes itself directly or indirectly. This is a property of its [programming language syntax](programming-language-syntax.md). It does not determine whether execution accumulates unfinished operations.[Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs, second edition, §1.2.1: paragraph beginning “In contrasting iteration and recursion”](structure-and-interpretation-of-computer-programs-by-harold-abelson-and-others.md)
