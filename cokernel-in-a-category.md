# Cokernel in a category

↑ **Parent:** [Pointed category](pointed-category.md)

The cokernel of $f:A\to B$ is the universal morphism $q:B\to Q$ satisfying $qf=0$. It is the [coequalizer](coequalizer.md) of $f$ and the zero morphism.
