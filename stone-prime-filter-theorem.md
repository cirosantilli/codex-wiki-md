# Stone prime filter theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Prime filter of a distributive lattice](prime-filter-of-a-distributive-lattice.md)

If a [lattice filter](lattice-filter.md) $F$ and [lattice ideal](lattice-ideal.md) $I$ in a [distributive lattice](distributive-lattice.md) are disjoint, there is a [prime filter of a distributive lattice](prime-filter-of-a-distributive-lattice.md) containing $F$ and disjoint from $I$.

The [Boolean prime ideal theorem](boolean-prime-ideal-theorem.md) is the Boolean-algebra version; distributivity gives this corresponding filter-extension statement.
