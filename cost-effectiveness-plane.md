# Cost-effectiveness plane

↑ **Parent:** [Cost-effectiveness analysis](cost-effectiveness-analysis.md)

The [cost-effectiveness plane](cost-effectiveness-plane.md) plots an effect increment horizontally and a cost increment vertically. The southeast quadrant represents greater effectiveness at lower cost, while the northwest quadrant represents lower effectiveness at greater cost. A threshold $\lambda$ corresponds to the line $\Delta C=\lambda\Delta E$; points below the line have positive [incremental net monetary benefit](incremental-net-monetary-benefit.md), in every quadrant. A joint confidence region or paired [bootstrap](bootstrapping-statistics.md) cloud reveals uncertainty and preserves the dependence between cost and effect estimates.
