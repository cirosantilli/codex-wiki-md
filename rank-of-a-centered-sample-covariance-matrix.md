# Rank of a centered sample covariance matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Sample covariance matrix](sample-covariance-matrix.md)

If $H=I_n-\mathbf1\mathbf1^T/n$ and $S=X^THX/n$, then $\operatorname{rank}S=\operatorname{rank}(HX)\leq\min(p,n-1)$. In particular [full column rank](full-column-rank.md) of raw $X$ does not ensure a nonsingular centered [sample covariance matrix](sample-covariance-matrix.md). When $S$ is singular, its Gaussian precision [profile likelihood](profile-likelihood.md) has no finite maximizer: increase precision along a nonzero null vector.
