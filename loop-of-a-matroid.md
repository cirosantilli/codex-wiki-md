# Loop of a matroid

↑ **Parent:** [Matroid](matroid.md)

An element $x$ is a [loop of a matroid](loop-of-a-matroid.md) when its [singleton](singleton-mathematics.md) is not a [matroid independent set](independent-set-of-a-matroid.md). By heredity, no [matroid independent set](independent-set-of-a-matroid.md) contains it. Equivalently its [singleton](singleton-mathematics.md) has [rank of a matroid](rank-of-a-matroid.md) zero, or it belongs to the [closure of a matroid](closure-of-a-matroid.md) of the [empty set](empty-set.md). In a [graphic matroid](graphic-matroid.md), a [self-loop](loop-graph-theory.md) is a [loop of a matroid](loop-of-a-matroid.md).
