# Eigenvalue equation for heat conduction in two joined slabs

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

For two length-$L$ slabs with zero exterior [temperatures](temperature-split.md) and perfect [thermal contact](thermal-contact.md), put $\alpha_i=\sqrt{\rho_ic_i/k_i}$, $u=\omega\alpha_1L$, and $v=\omega\alpha_2L$. A [separated solution](separated-solution.md) with time factor $e^{-\omega^2t}$ and $\omega\ne0$ exists precisely when the displayed interface [determinant](determinant.md) vanishes. Dividing by $\cos u\cos v$ gives a tangent [characteristic equation](characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) only away from its poles. Common zeros of both cosines are legitimate [normal modes](normal-mode.md) and must be retained. The zero-frequency case gives only the trivial [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) with these positive material parameters and exterior [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
