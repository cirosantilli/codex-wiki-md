# One-way speed of light

↑ **Parent:** [Speed of light](speed-of-light.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-way_speed_of_light)

A one-way [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) measurement assigns a travel time between spatially separated emission and reception events and therefore requires a distant-clock synchronization convention. In an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md), [Einstein synchronisation](einstein-synchronisation.md) assigns the vacuum value $c$ in either direction. A completed-loop comparison such as the [Sagnac effect](sagnac-effect.md) is a different observable.
