# Ultrametric

↑ **Parent:** [Metric](metric.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrametric)

An ultrametric is a [metric](metric.md) satisfying the strong triangle inequality

$$
d(x,z)\leq\max\{d(x,y),d(y,z)\}.
$$
