# Nuclear envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_envelope)

The [nuclear envelope](nuclear-envelope.md) is the double-membrane boundary around a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md). Regulated passage connects nuclear processes to the surrounding [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md): [transcription](transcription-biology.md) produces [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) in the [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), while exported [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) can undergo [biological translation](translation-biology.md) on [ribosomes](ribosome.md) outside it. See [NHGRI: nuclear membrane](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Nuclear-Membrane).
