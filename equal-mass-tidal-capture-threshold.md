# Equal-mass tidal-capture threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal capture of galaxies](tidal-capture-of-galaxies.md)

Two equal galaxies each receive the [tidal impulse heating of a spherical galaxy](tidal-impulse-heating-of-a-spherical-galaxy.md), while their relative orbital energy at infinity is $m_gv^2/4$. Transfer of more internal energy than that orbital energy gives $pv<[32G^2m_g^2\langle r^2\rangle/3]^{1/4}$. Slow encounters require [adiabatic shielding of tidal encounters](adiabatic-shielding-of-tidal-encounters.md), so the impulsive inequality does not apply there.
