# Vector-particle polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Little group](little-group.md)

A massive vector in $D$ spacetime dimensions has $D-1$ polarizations perpendicular to its timelike [momentum](momentum.md). A massless vector has transverse polarizations modulo its null [longitudinal polarization](longitudinal-polarization.md), leaving $D-2$ physical [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md).
