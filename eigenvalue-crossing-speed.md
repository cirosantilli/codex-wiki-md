# Eigenvalue crossing speed

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md)

The [eigenvalue crossing speed](eigenvalue-crossing-speed.md) is the derivative of the real part of a parameter-dependent eigenvalue at a stability boundary. A nonzero speed for an imaginary pair is the transversality condition in a generic Hopf bifurcation.
