# Descending membership sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Axiom of regularity](axiom-of-regularity.md)

A [descending membership sequence](descending-membership-sequence.md) is a [function](function-split.md) on the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) with each next value a member of the preceding value. [Foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md) excludes it: the range is a nonempty [set](set-split.md) with no member disjoint from that range.
