# Instantaneous power

↑ **Parent:** [Power](power.md)

[Instantaneous power](instantaneous-power.md) is [power](power.md) at a particular moment. Consider a [point mass](point-mass.md) with [mass](mass.md) $m=2\,\mathrm{kg}$, initially at rest, acted on by a constant net [force](force.md) $F=3\,\mathrm N$ along a straight path. [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) gives constant [acceleration](acceleration.md) $a=F/m=1.5\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$, hence [velocity](velocity.md) $v(t)=at$ and

$$
P(t)=Fat=(4.5\,\mathrm{W/s})t.
$$

The [instantaneous power](instantaneous-power.md) starts at zero even though the [force](force.md) is nonzero, because the initial [velocity](velocity.md) is zero. At distance $4\,\mathrm m$, the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) example below gives [speed](speed.md) $\sqrt{12}\,\mathrm{m/s}$, so the final [instantaneous power](instantaneous-power.md) is $3\sqrt{12}\,\mathrm W\approx10.3923\,\mathrm W$. Negative [instantaneous power](instantaneous-power.md) means the selected [force](force.md) is removing [energy](energy.md) at that moment.
