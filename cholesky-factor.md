# Cholesky factor

↑ **Parent:** [Cholesky decomposition](cholesky-decomposition.md)

For a real [symmetric positive-definite matrix](symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md), the lower triangular matrix $C$ with positive diagonal in its [Cholesky decomposition](cholesky-decomposition.md) is its lower [Cholesky factor](cholesky-factor.md); $C^T$ is the corresponding upper factor. If a full-column-rank matrix $B$ has a reduced [QR decomposition](qr-decomposition.md) $B=QR$ with positive diagonal of $R$, then $B^TB=R^TR$, so $R$ is the upper [Cholesky factor](cholesky-factor.md) of $B^TB$.
