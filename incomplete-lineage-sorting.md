# Incomplete lineage sorting

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)

Persistence of ancestral variation through successive lineage splits, allowing the ancestry of one genomic region to disagree with the population branching history. Such disagreement is not by itself evidence of [introgression](introgression.md).
