# Global causal step in the Penrose theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose singularity theorem](penrose-singularity-theorem.md)

Assume future [null geodesic completeness](null-geodesic-completeness.md). The [compactness of the future horismos of a trapped surface](compactness-of-the-future-horismos-of-a-trapped-surface.md) makes $E^+(S)$ a nonempty [compact](compact-space.md) [achronal boundary](achronal-boundary.md) without edge. Projection along a complete [timelike vector field](timelike-vector-field.md) into the [Cauchy hypersurface](cauchy-surface.md) is injective, open by [invariance of domain](invariance-of-domain.md), and closed by [compactness](compact-space.md). Connectedness forces surjectivity, contradicting the noncompact [Cauchy hypersurface](cauchy-surface.md). This global step completes the contradiction.[p. 59, compactness and noncompactness contradiction](gravitational-collapse-and-space-time-singularities-by-roger-penrose.md)
