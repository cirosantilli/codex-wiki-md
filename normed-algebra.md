# Normed algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)

A normed algebra is an [algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md) with a [norm](norm.md) satisfying $\|ab\|\le\|a\|\|b\|$. Its completion is a [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), since multiplication extends continuously. Completeness is additional structure, rather than part of the definition of a normed algebra.
