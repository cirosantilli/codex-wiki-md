# Ward identity for QED renormalization constants

↑ **Parent:** [Proper-vertex Ward-Takahashi identity in QED](proper-vertex-ward-takahashi-identity-in-qed.md)

In a gauge-preserving regularization and consistent [renormalization](renormalization.md) convention, the [proper-vertex Ward-Takahashi identity in QED](proper-vertex-ward-takahashi-identity-in-qed.md) equates the vertex and electron [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) constants. With $\psi_0=Z_2^{1/2}\psi$ and $A_0=Z_3^{1/2}A$, the vertex coefficient gives $e_0=\mu_R^\epsilon Z_1e/(Z_2\sqrt{Z_3})$, proving the displayed charge relation in $d=4-2\epsilon$. [Gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) restricts [counterterms](counterterm.md) but does not forbid charge running.
