# Electric part of the Weyl tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl tensor](weyl-tensor.md)

Relative to a timelike [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $n$, the electric part is $E_{ab}=C_{acbd}n^cn^d$. It describes the tidal component of the [Weyl tensor](weyl-tensor.md) measured by that observer. Antisymmetry in each curvature pair makes it spatial in both slots; pair symmetry and vanishing traces make it symmetric and trace-free.
