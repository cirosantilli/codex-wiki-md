# Gaussian filtering Fourier multiplier

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian blur](gaussian-blur.md)

The [Fourier transform of a Gaussian](fourier-transform-of-a-gaussian.md) and the [convolution theorem](convolution-theorem.md) give this multiplier with the angular-frequency convention $e^{-ix\cdot\xi}$. In cycles-per-length [frequencies](frequency.md) $k$, it is $e^{-2\pi^2\sigma^2|k|^2}$. Each coordinate has variance $\sigma^2$, and higher [frequencies](frequency.md) receive stronger attenuation.
