# Heath-Jarrow-Morton model

↑ **Parent:** [Interest rate](interest-rate.md)

A model evolving the entire [instantaneous forward rate](instantaneous-forward-rate.md) curve. In the displayed one-factor [risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md) dynamics the [drift](drift-coefficient.md) restriction makes every suitably integrable discounted [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) price a [martingale](martingale-split.md). The multi-factor form replaces the product of volatilities by their [dot product](dot-product.md).
