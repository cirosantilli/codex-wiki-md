# Size-biased geometric distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Size-biased distribution](size-biased-distribution.md)

For the positive-support [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) with success probability $p$, [size bias](size-bias.md) gives $\mathbb P(\widetilde Y=k)=kp^2(1-p)^{k-1}$. The sum of two independent copies has $k$ positive-integer decompositions at value $k+1$, so its shifted probability is exactly this expression. Its [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) is $p^2s/(1-(1-p)s)^2$, also obtained by the [size-biased probability generating function](size-biased-probability-generating-function.md) identity.
