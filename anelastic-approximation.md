# Anelastic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

An anelastic model filters acoustic density fluctuations while retaining a prescribed stratified reference [mass density](density.md). For $\rho_0\propto e^{-z/H}$, continuity is $u_x+v_y+w_z-w/H=0$. Momentum and buoyancy conventions must be consistent: with reduced pressure $\Phi=p'/\rho_0$, a pressure acceleration $-\nabla\Phi$ contains a vertical density-gradient term that is absent from $-\rho_0^{-1}\nabla p'$. The two expressions cannot be interchanged when deriving waves in a stratified atmosphere.
