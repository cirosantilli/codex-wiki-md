# Kerr ISCO spin limits

↑ **Parent:** [Innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md)

For equatorial test-particle circular orbits in the extremal-spin limit, the prograde [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md) [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md) has Boyer-Lindquist radius tending to $GM/c^2$ and specific [energy](energy-split.md) tending to $1/\sqrt3$. The retrograde limit has radius $9GM/c^2$ and specific [energy](energy-split.md) $5/(3\sqrt3)$. The prograde radius approaching the horizon coordinate does not make the massive limiting orbit identical to the null horizon generator.[§II, Eqs. 2.20–2.23, pp. 351–353](rotating-black-holes-locally-nonrotating-frames-energy-extraction-and-scalar-synchrotron-radiation-by-james-m-bardeen-and-others.md)
