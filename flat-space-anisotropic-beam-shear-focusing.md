# Flat-space anisotropic beam shear focusing

↑ **Parent:** [Area-square-root optical focusing equation](area-square-root-optical-focusing-equation.md)

In flat spacetime take a twist-free beam's two screen Jacobi factors to be $1+\lambda$ and $1-\lambda$. They solve the zero-curvature [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md) equation. Their product is the displayed area, and subtracting the trace from their optical matrix gives the displayed nonnegative shear. Direct differentiation yields $(A^{1/2})''=-(1-\lambda^2)^{-3/2}=-\sigma^2A^{1/2}$, an explicit check of the focusing sign.
