# Uniform minimum variance conditionally unbiased estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Conditionally unbiased estimator](conditionally-unbiased-estimator.md)

A UMVCUE has minimum conditional [variance](variance-split.md) among all [estimators](estimator.md) unbiased in the selected experiment for every parameter. For independent Gaussian stage estimates $A,V$ with information $I_1,J$, continuation $A\geq c$, total information $I_2=I_1+J$ and pooled estimate $T$, [Rao-Blackwellization](rao-blackwellization.md) of the fresh estimate gives $T-(I_1s/J)\phi((T-c)/s)/\Phi((T-c)/s)$, where $s^2=1/I_1-1/I_2$. The conditional family has [complete sufficient statistic](complete-sufficient-statistic.md) $T$, so the [Lehmann–Scheffé theorem](lehmann-scheffe-theorem.md) establishes the optimum. A conditional-[likelihood](likelihood-function.md) bias correction does not automatically have this unbiasedness property.
