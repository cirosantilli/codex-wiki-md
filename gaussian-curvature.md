↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_curvature)

Gaussian curvature is the determinant of the shape operator:

$$
K=\frac{eg-f^2}{EG-F^2}.
$$
