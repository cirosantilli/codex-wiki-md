# Finite-part normal trace for mixed quarter-power boundary data

↑ **Parent:** [Wiener-Hopf method](wiener-hopf-method.md)

In the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), the displayed [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) has [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) value $x^{-1/4}$ on the positive axis, zero [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) value on the negative axis, and decay at infinity. Its negative trace is $\sqrt2|x|^{-1/4}$, but its positive normal [derivative](derivative.md) is $x^{-5/4}/4$. The latter requires a [Hadamard finite-part integral](hadamard-finite-part-integral.md) in a [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md). For the $e^{ikx}$ convention the transformed traces are $\sqrt2\Gamma(3/4)(ik)^{-3/4}$ below and $-\Gamma(3/4)(-ik)^{1/4}$ above. The homogeneous corner mode $r^{-1/2}\sin(\theta/2)$ produces the additional lower [integral transform](integral-transform-split.md) proportional to $k^{-1/2}$. Higher singular corner modes require a larger [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) class than a locally integrable negative trace.
