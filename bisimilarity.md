# Bisimilarity

↑ **Parent:** [Bisimulation](bisimulation.md)

Two [rooted directed graphs](rooted-directed-graph.md) are bisimilar if some [bisimulation](bisimulation.md) relates their roots. A root with one terminal successor is bisimilar to a root with two terminal successors, although the graphs have different sizes.
