# Ring of rational functions on a reduced variety

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md)

For a possibly reducible [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) $X$, take pairs $(U,f)$ with $U$ a dense open subset and $f$ a [regular function](regular-function.md) on $U$. Identify pairs agreeing on a common dense open subset. Addition and multiplication on intersections make these classes a [ring](ring.md). For an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) with reduced [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) $A$, this ring is its [total quotient ring](total-ring-of-fractions.md). For an [irreducible variety](irreducible-variety.md), it is the [function field](function-field-of-an-algebraic-variety.md). An [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) of dense open subsets induces an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) of these rings.
