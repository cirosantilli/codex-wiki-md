<h1 id="flory-huggins-solution-theory">Flory–Huggins solution theory</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer solution](polymer-solution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flory–Huggins_solution_theory)

[Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md) is an incompressible lattice description of mixing a [polymer](polymer-split.md) with a [solvent](solvent.md). A chain occupies $N$ connected sites, while a solvent molecule occupies one; the displayed mixing [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is per site. The first two terms are translational [entropy of mixing](entropy-of-mixing.md) contributions and the last is a mean-field excess contact interaction. A positive interaction parameter $\chi$ penalizes polymer–solvent contacts. If the site volume is $v_0$, the physical free-energy density is $f/v_0$ and the polymer [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md) gives monomer [number density](number-density.md) $\phi/v_0$. Composition-dependent interaction parameters require extra derivative terms in stability calculations.
