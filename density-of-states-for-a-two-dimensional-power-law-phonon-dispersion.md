# Density of states for a two-dimensional power-law phonon dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon dispersion relation](phonon-dispersion-relation.md)

For two polarizations in area $A$ and $\omega=C|k|^\alpha$, counting [wavevector](wavevector.md) states in a disk gives

$$
g(\omega)=\frac{A}{\pi\alpha C^{2/\alpha}}
\omega^{2/\alpha-1}.
$$
