# Oriented connector crossing count

↑ **Parent:** [Hookean dumbbell model](hookean-dumbbell-model.md)

For a locally uniform distribution of labelled connectors, a connector crosses a small surface when its midpoint lies in a slab of thickness $|\mathbf R\cdot\mathbf n|$. This gives the displayed unsigned count per unit area. Omitting the absolute value gives a signed count, or requires a restriction to one orientation. The signed spring [force](force-split.md) transmitted from the positive side to the negative side is $H\mathbf R\operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf R\cdot\mathbf n)$, so multiplication by the unsigned count yields [traction](traction.md) $nH\langle\mathbf R\mathbf R^T\rangle\mathbf n$. Each labelled dumbbell is counted once.
