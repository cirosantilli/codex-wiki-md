# Preadditive category

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preadditive_category)

A [category](category-split.md) is preadditive if every hom-set is an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) and composition is additive in each variable. Zero morphisms and differences of parallel morphisms are therefore defined. A [zero object](zero-object.md) or finite [biproducts](biproduct.md) need not exist; these are extra requirements in an [additive category](additive-category.md).
