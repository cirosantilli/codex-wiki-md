# Breslow approximation for tied event times

↑ **Parent:** [Cox partial likelihood](cox-partial-likelihood.md)

When $d_j$ events occur at the same recorded time, the Breslow approximation to [Cox partial likelihood](cox-partial-likelihood.md) uses the factor $\exp(\sum_{i\in D_j}x_i^T\beta)/(\sum_{i\in R_j}e^{x_i^T\beta})^{d_j}$, where $R_j$ is the [risk set](risk-set.md) and $D_j$ the event set. It approximates the ordering of tied events rather than treating their within-tie order as known.
