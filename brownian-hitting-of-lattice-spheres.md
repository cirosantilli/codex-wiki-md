# Brownian hitting of lattice spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

For three-dimensional [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), every radius $r>0$ and every initial point satisfy

$$
\mathbb P_x\bigl(\exists t\geq0,\ z\in\mathbb Z^3:\ |B_t+z|=r\bigr)=1.
$$

If the initial point is on one of the [spheres](sphere.md), it is already a hit at time zero. If it is inside a lattice-centered [open ball](open-ball.md), its eventual exit hits that [sphere](sphere.md). Otherwise choose at each integer time a nearest lattice center. The current displacement from it has norm at most $\sqrt3/2$, so the [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) of the next unit increment gives a uniform positive chance of entering its radius-$r/2$ [open ball](open-ball.md). The [geometric tail bound from a uniform escape probability](geometric-tail-bound-from-a-uniform-escape-probability.md) makes eventual entry certain, and continuity forces a boundary crossing. This periodic-family recurrence does not contradict the [Brownian sphere-hitting probability in dimension three](brownian-sphere-hitting-probability-in-dimension-three.md) for one fixed [sphere](sphere.md).
