# Runaway planetesimal growth

↑ **Parent:** [Core accretion](core-accretion.md)

A growth regime in which larger [planetesimals](planetesimal.md) have shorter fractional growth times. In a dispersion-dominated population with nearly mass-independent random speed and strong [gravitational focusing](gravitational-focusing.md), fixed material density gives $\dot M\propto M^{4/3}$. The displayed scaling is an illustrative limit, not a universal law once large bodies control the random velocities.
