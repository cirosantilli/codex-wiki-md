# Nuclear fusion

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)

Fusion combines nuclear reactants into heavier compound nuclei or products. A net decrease in rest mass releases energy; heavier fusion reactions can instead require energy. [Stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md) uses exothermic networks to power stars, and [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) through the [Coulomb barrier](coulomb-barrier.md) permits many charged-particle reactions at stellar temperatures.
