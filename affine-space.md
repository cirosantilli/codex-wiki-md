# Affine space

↑ **Parent:** [Affine geometry](affine-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_space)

A nonempty set $A$ equipped with a [group action](group-action-split.md) of the additive group of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) $V$, such that for every $x,y\in A$ there is a unique $v\in V$ with $x+v=y$. There is no distinguished origin. Choosing one identifies $A$ with $V$; changing it gives a [translation](translation-geometry.md).
