# Density-gradient flotation

↑ **Parent:** [Density-gradient centrifugation](density-gradient-centrifugation.md)

Separation in which a particle's buoyant properties cause it to move toward a lower-density part of a gradient. A [liposome](liposome.md) fraction can thereby be separated from unassociated soluble [proteins](protein-split.md); copurification alone does not demonstrate correct [membrane topology](membrane-topology.md).
