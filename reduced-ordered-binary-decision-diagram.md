# Reduced ordered binary decision diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Ordered binary decision diagram](ordered-binary-decision-diagram.md)

An ordered [binary decision diagram](binary-decision-diagram.md) with shared equal [terminal nodes](terminal-node.md), no [decision node](decision-node.md) having identical zero and one children, and no two [decision nodes](decision-node.md) having the same tested [Boolean variable](boolean-variable.md) and the same ordered pair of children. For a fixed [variable ordering of a binary decision diagram](variable-ordering-of-a-binary-decision-diagram.md), it is unique up to labelled [graph isomorphism](graph-isomorphism.md): each nonconstant remaining [Boolean cofactor](boolean-cofactor.md) has a uniquely determined first essential [Boolean variable](boolean-variable.md), and its two children are recursively determined by its zero and one [Boolean cofactors](boolean-cofactor.md). This uniqueness does not compare different orderings.
