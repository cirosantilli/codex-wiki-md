# K-theory transfer of a finite covering

↑ **Parent:** [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md)

For a finite [covering map](covering-space.md) $p:Y\to X$, define the fiber of the transferred [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) by $(p_!V)_x=\bigoplus_{y\in p^{-1}(x)}V_y$. Local trivializations of the [covering map](covering-space.md) make this a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md), and compatibility with [direct sums](direct-sum.md) extends it to [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md).
