# Autochthonous fossil assemblage

↑ **Parent:** [Fossil assemblage](fossil-assemblage.md)

An assemblage whose organisms are preserved where they lived. Local origin does not eliminate [taphonomic bias](taphonomic-bias.md): soft tissues can disappear and resistant parts can accumulate through time.
