# Metric Tietze two-thirds approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Tietze extension theorem](tietze-extension-theorem.md)

For a real function $g$ in the [bounded continuous functions](bounded-continuous-functions.md) of norm $M$ on a closed subset $Y$ of a [metric space](metric-space.md), separate its closed level sets $A=\{g\le-M/3\}$ and $B=\{g\ge M/3\}$ by $u=(M/3)(d(\cdot,A)-d(\cdot,B))/(d(\cdot,A)+d(\cdot,B))$. The [distance to a set](distance-to-a-set.md) functions make $u$ continuous with the displayed bounds. Empty level sets are handled by the constants $M/3$, $-M/3$ or $0$. Iterating on the residual yields a uniformly convergent continuous extension with no increase in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md).
