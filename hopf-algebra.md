# Hopf algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopf_algebra)

A Hopf algebra is a compatible algebra and coalgebra equipped with a counit and antipode. Coordinate rings of [affine algebraic groups](linear-algebraic-group.md) are commutative Hopf algebras.
