# Exponential family

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponential_family)

A density $h(y)\exp\{\theta T(y)-A(\theta)\}$ has sufficient statistic $T$ and cumulant function $A$.
