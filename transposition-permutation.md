# Transposition (permutation)

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

A transposition is a [permutation](permutation.md) that exchanges two elements and fixes every other element. Every transposition has order two.

A transposition is a [cyclic permutation](cyclic-permutation.md) of length two.
