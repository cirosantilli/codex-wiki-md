# Coble creep

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion creep](diffusion-creep.md)

Diffusional [material creep](creep-deformation.md) dominated by grain-boundary transport. An ideal scaling is $\dot\varepsilon\sim C\delta D_{GB}\Omega\sigma/(k_BT d^3)$. Its stronger grain-size dependence and typically lower transport [activation energy](activation-energy.md) distinguish it from bulk-diffusion [material creep](creep-deformation.md).
