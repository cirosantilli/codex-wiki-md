# Orthogonality of Sturm-Liouville eigenfunctions

↑ **Parent:** [Sturm-Liouville theory](sturm-liouville-theory-split.md)

For a regular [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md) with positive weight $w$ and self-adjoint boundary conditions, [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) with distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) in the weighted [inner product](inner-product-split.md) $\int w\overline uv$. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) proves this by equating $\int\overline u Lv$ and $\int\overline{Lu}v$.
