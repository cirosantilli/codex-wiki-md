# Arbuzov reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Reaction of a trialkyl phosphite with an alkyl halide to give a phosphonate and an alkyl halide byproduct. With a haloacetate it prepares a phosphonoacetate used in the [Horner-Wadsworth-Emmons reaction](horner-wadsworth-emmons-reaction.md).
