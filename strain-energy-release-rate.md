# Strain energy release rate

↑ **Parent:** [Fracture toughness](fracture-toughness.md)

The reduction in a mechanical system's potential energy per incremental crack area under specified loading conditions. In small-scale-yielding linear elasticity, mode-I [plane strain](plane-strain.md) gives $G=K_I^2/E'$ with $E'=E/(1-\nu^2)$. The critical value includes the dissipative processes associated with crack advance.
