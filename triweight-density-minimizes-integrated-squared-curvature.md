# Triweight density minimizes integrated squared curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Triweight kernel](triweight-kernel.md)

Let $f_0$ be the [triweight kernel](triweight-kernel.md) and h a twice continuously differentiable density with matching zero mean and variance. Put $e=h-f_0$ and $c=35/32$. Two integrations by parts over its support give $\int e''f_0''=48c[h(1)+h(-1)]+c\int_{|x|>1}(360x^2-72)h(x)dx\geq0$, using the zero mass and second moment of e. Therefore $R(h'')=R(f_0'')+R(e'')+2\int e''f_0''\geq R(f_0'')$. The endpoint terms matter because the third derivative jumps there.
