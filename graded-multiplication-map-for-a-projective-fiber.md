# Graded multiplication map for a projective fiber

↑ **Parent:** [Projective Nullstellensatz](projective-nullstellensatz.md)

For homogeneous fiber equations $f_i$ of fixed degrees $d_i$ in $S=k[y_0,\ldots,y_n]$, put $S_j=0$ for $j<0$. The [linear map](linear-map.md) $\mu_N((g_i)_i)=\sum_i g_if_i$ has image the degree-$N$ component of their [homogeneous ideal](homogeneous-ideal.md). Hence it is surjective exactly when that ideal contains $(y_0,\ldots,y_n)^N$: the degree-$N$ [monomials](monomial.md) generate this power. In a family, the [matrix](matrix.md) entries are regular functions on the base. Failure of surjectivity is therefore a closed [determinantal variety](determinantal-variety.md), cut out by the maximal-size [matrix minors](minor-linear-algebra.md).
