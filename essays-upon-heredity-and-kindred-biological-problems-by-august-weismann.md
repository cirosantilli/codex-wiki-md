# Essays upon Heredity and Kindred Biological Problems by August Weismann

↑ **Parent:** [Ecology and evolution bibliography](ecology-and-evolution-bibliography-split.md)

[https://www.gutenberg.org/files/48132/48132-h/48132-h.htm](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/48132/48132-h/48132-h.htm)

Primary essays translated into English. The discussion of acquired characters and note 224 explicitly distinguishes old miniature-organism [preformationism](preformationism.md) from a renewed material account, and contrasts these with [epigenesis](epigenesis-biology.md). The collection's preface warns that the essays preserve stages in the author's changing views.
