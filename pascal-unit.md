# Pascal (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal_(unit))

The [pascal](pascal-unit.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [pressure](pressure.md):

$$
1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-2}}=1\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}\,s^{-2}}.
$$

For a uniform normal [force](force.md) of $10\,\mathrm N$ over [area](surface-area.md) $0.02\,\mathrm{m^2}$, the [pressure](pressure.md) is $500\,\mathrm{Pa}$. The unit expresses a [force](force.md) per [area](surface-area.md), rather than the total [force](force.md) alone. [The International System of Units (SI), ninth edition, SI Brochure](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md).
