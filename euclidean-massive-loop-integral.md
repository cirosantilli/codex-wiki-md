# Euclidean massive loop integral

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md)

For $m^2>0$ and $n>d/2$, [Schwinger parameterization](schwinger-parameterization.md) and a [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) give $I_{d,n}(m)=\int d^dk\,(2\pi)^{-d}(k^2+m^2)^{-n}=\Gamma(n-d/2)(m^2)^{d/2-n}/[(4\pi)^{d/2}\Gamma(n)]$. Its meromorphic continuation supplies [dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md) poles.
