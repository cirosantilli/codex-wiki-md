# Radiation-dominated universe

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md)

A [radiation-dominated universe](radiation-dominated-universe.md) has expansion controlled mainly by [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md). When spatial curvature and the [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) are negligible, the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) and density scaling $\rho\propto a^{-4}$ give the displayed [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md), with [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) $H=1/(2t)$.
