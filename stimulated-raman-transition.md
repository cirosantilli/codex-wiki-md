# Stimulated Raman transition

↑ **Parent:** [Raman transition](raman-transition.md)

Coherent [absorption](absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) from one applied beam and [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) into another, transferring an atom between two long-lived states. The [energy](energy-split.md) transfer uses the [frequency](frequency.md) difference and the [momentum](momentum-split.md) transfer the [wavevector](wavevector.md) difference. It is distinct from an absorption-absorption process.
