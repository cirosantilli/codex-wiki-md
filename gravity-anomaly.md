# Gravity anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

A difference between observed [gravity](gravity.md) and a specified reference field after stated reductions. Free-air, Bouguer and isostatic anomalies remove different modeled effects; they are not interchangeable raw measurements.
