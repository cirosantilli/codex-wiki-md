# Cayley transform between the half-plane and disk

↑ **Parent:** [Complex analysis](complex-analysis-split.md)

The fractional linear maps

$$
w=\frac{z-1}{z+1},
\qquad
z=\frac{1+w}{1-w}
$$

map the right half-plane and unit disk conformally onto one another.
