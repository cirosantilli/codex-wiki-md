# Kinetic equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md)

Momentum-exchange collisions maintain a common [temperature](temperature-split.md) and an equilibrium-shaped momentum [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md). Particle abundances and [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) need not have their [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) values: elastic scattering alone conserves particle number. Thus kinetic equilibrium does not by itself imply either zero [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) or conserved comoving [entropy](entropy-split.md).
