# Flexural gravity admittance

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-topography admittance](gravity-topography-admittance.md)

For a surface load above a uniform-density [crust](planetary-crust.md) over a denser substrate, a simple [lithospheric flexure](lithospheric-flexure.md) model gives the indicated [gravity-topography admittance](gravity-topography-admittance.md), with $\ell_f^4=D/[g(\rho_m-\rho_c)]$. The surface mass contributes positive gravity; downward displacement of the [density](density.md) interface contributes a compensating negative signal attenuated by $e^{-kt}$. Hence $Z\to0$ at long [wavelength](wavelength.md) and $Z\to2\pi G\rho_c$ at short [wavelength](wavelength.md). Here $t$ is the interface depth, not automatically the [effective elastic thickness](effective-elastic-thickness.md).
