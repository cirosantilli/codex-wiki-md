# Exchange interaction in a double quantum dot

↑ **Parent:** [Double quantum dot](double-quantum-dot.md)

An effective coupling that separates the two-electron [singlet state](singlet-state.md) and [triplet states](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) by energy $J$. For a symmetric two-site Hubbard model with weak tunnelling $t_c$ and charging cost $U$, $J\simeq4t_c^2/U$. Gate-controlled detuning and tunnelling change the interaction; the simple expression does not hold at arbitrary detuning or strong charge hybridization.
