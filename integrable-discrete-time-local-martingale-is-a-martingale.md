# Integrable discrete-time local martingale is a martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Local martingale](local-martingale.md)

An integrable discrete-time [local martingale](local-martingale.md) is a true [martingale](martingale-split.md). Its stopped increments have the form $\mathbf1_{\{\tau\geq t\}}(X_t-X_{t-1})$, and the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) removes a [localizing sequence](localizing-sequence.md) from the conditional increment identity.
