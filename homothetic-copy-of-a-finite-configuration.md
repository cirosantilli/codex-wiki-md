# Homothetic copy of a finite configuration

↑ **Parent:** [Affine space](affine-space.md)

A [translation](translation-geometry.md) and positive uniform scaling of a finite configuration $F$ in a [real vector space](real-vector-space.md). It preserves the ratios and directions of differences between its points. In an [integer lattice](integer-lattice.md), the [Gallai theorem for an integer lattice](gallai-theorem-for-an-integer-lattice.md) guarantees a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) [homothetic copy](homothetic-copy-of-a-finite-configuration.md) of every finite pattern in any [finite colouring](finite-coloring.md), with [positive integer](positive-integer.md) scaling factor.
