# Barycentric coordinate system

↑ **Parent:** [Simplex](simplex.md)

A [barycentric coordinate system](barycentric-coordinate-system.md) assigns to a point in the affine span of the vertices of a nondegenerate [simplex](simplex.md) its unique vertex weights $\lambda_0,\ldots,\lambda_d$, constrained to sum to one. Uniqueness follows because $v_1-v_0,\ldots,v_d-v_0$ form a [basis](basis.md) of the translation space: expand $x-v_0$ in that basis and set $\lambda_0=1-\sum_{i=1}^d\lambda_i$. A point lies in the closed simplex exactly when every weight is nonnegative. An [affine function](affine-function.md) satisfies $f(x)=\sum_i\lambda_i f(v_i)$, which makes these coordinates useful for interpolation and the [integral of an affine function over a simplex](integral-of-an-affine-function-over-a-simplex.md).
