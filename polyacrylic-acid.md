# Polyacrylic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyacrylic_acid)

A carboxylic-acid-bearing [polymer](polymer-split.md) whose charge and conformation depend on ionization and solution conditions. Single-chain pulling can distinguish low-force configurational compliance from high-force backbone stretching.
