# Nitrogen monoxide

↑ **Parent:** [Nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md)

The atmospheric [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) oxide $\mathrm{NO}$. It consumes [ozone](ozone.md) when converted to [nitrogen dioxide](nitrogen-dioxide.md) by $\mathrm{NO+O_3\to NO_2+O_2}$, but its oxidation by [peroxy radicals](peroxy-radical.md) instead enables net [photochemical ozone production](photochemical-ozone-production.md).
