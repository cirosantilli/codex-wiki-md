# SCAN disk scheduling

↑ **Parent:** [Disk scheduling](disk-scheduling.md)

Sweep in one direction, servicing requests encountered in track order, continue to the physical endpoint, and then reverse. A calculation must state the initial direction and whether movement after the final requested service is included. [LOOK disk scheduling](look-disk-scheduling.md) instead turns around at the last pending request in that direction.
