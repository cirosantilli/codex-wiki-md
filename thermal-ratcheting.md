# Thermal ratcheting

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal cycling growth](thermal-cycling-growth.md)

Progressive residual deformation over thermal cycles when thermal mismatch or constraints are accommodated irreversibly in a noncancelling way. [plasticity](plasticity-physics.md), [creep](creep-deformation.md) or damage may contribute; not every cyclic plastic response ratchets indefinitely.
