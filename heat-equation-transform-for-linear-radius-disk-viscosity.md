# Heat-equation transform for linear-radius disk viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Square-root-radius diffusion transform](square-root-radius-diffusion-transform.md)

For source-free [Keplerian viscous diffusion equation](keplerian-viscous-diffusion-equation.md), the variables $y=\sqrt r$ and $g=r^{3/2}\Sigma$ give $g_t=(3\nu_0/4)g_{yy}$ when $\bar\nu=\nu_0r$. Rescaling y gives the displayed [heat equation](heat-equation.md). Initial and radial boundary conditions must also be transformed; the reduction does not specify them.
