<h1 id="section-b/b8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A rapidly spinning [gyroscope](../../../../../../gyroscope.md) approximately has [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $\mathbf L=I_3\omega_3\hat{\mathbf e}$ along its axle. With no [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) in an [inertial frame](../../../../../../inertial-frame.md), this vector retains its direction. A transverse [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) changes its direction according to $d\mathbf L/dt=\boldsymbol\tau$; it does not initially push the axle in the direction of the applied [force](../../../../../../force-split.md).

For a fixed pivot, [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) $l\hat{\mathbf e}$ from it and gravity $-Mg\hat{\mathbf z}$, the [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) is $Mgl\hat{\mathbf z}\times\hat{\mathbf e}$. In slow steady [precession](../../../../../../precession.md) about the vertical, $d\mathbf L/dt=\Omega\hat{\mathbf z}\times\mathbf L$, giving

$$
\boxed{\Omega\simeq\frac{Mgl}{I_3\omega_3}.}
$$

The factor $\sin\theta$ appears both in [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) and in the [radius](../../../../../../radius.md) traced by the angular-momentum tip, so it cancels for a nonvertical axle. Reversing [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) reverses this [precession](../../../../../../precession.md). The full symmetric-top steady condition is $I_1\cos\theta\,\Omega^2-I_3\omega_3\Omega+Mgl=0$; the approximation neglects its quadratic term. Sudden release can also produce [nutation](../../../../../../nutation.md), so large [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) alone does not guarantee a steady sweep.

A wheel-on-pivot experiment can compare [precession](../../../../../../precession.md) periods at different measured [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) [speeds](../../../../../../speed.md) and reverse the [spin](../../../../../../spin.md). Keep the pivot and [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) fixed; bearing [friction](../../../../../../friction.md), [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) decay, an initial push and support motion can mimic departures from the predicted inverse-spin relation. A freely gimballed direction reference and a gravity-loaded precessing wheel test different [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) conditions.[Equation11.12 and torque/precession geometry](../../../../../../university-physics-volume-1-by-william-moebs-and-others/11-4-precession-of-a-gyroscope.md)
