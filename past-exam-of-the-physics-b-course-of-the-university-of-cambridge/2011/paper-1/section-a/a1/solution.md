<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

For an outward radial [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md), [Gauss's law](../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives the enclosed charge

$$
Q(r)=4\pi\epsilon_0r^2 E_r=4\pi\epsilon_0\alpha r e^{-\lambda r}.
$$

Taking the enclosing sphere to infinity gives **$Q_{\rm total}=0$**. A nonzero local field is compatible with zero net charge. Indeed, for $r>0$,

$$
\rho(r)=\epsilon_0\nabla\cdot\mathbf E
=\frac{\epsilon_0\alpha e^{-\lambda r}}{r^2}(1-\lambda r).
$$

Positive inner charge is balanced by negative outer charge. There is no point charge at the origin, since $\lim_{r\to0}4\pi\epsilon_0r^2 E_r=0$. The field's magnitude does not specify its inward/outward sign, but reversing a globally radial direction leaves the zero total unchanged. This is [net charge from the asymptotic radial electric field](../../../../../net-charge-from-the-asymptotic-radial-electric-field.md).
