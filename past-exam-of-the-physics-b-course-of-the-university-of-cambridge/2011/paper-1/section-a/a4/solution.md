<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

For a nonabsorbing interface at [normal incidence](../../../../../normal-incidence.md), the [Fresnel equations](../../../../../fresnel-equations.md) give the [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) [reflectance](../../../../../reflectance.md)

$$
\mathcal R=\left(\frac{n-1}{n+1}\right)^2=0.04.
$$

The two air–glass interfaces each transmit a fraction $1-\mathcal R$ of their incident power. Omitting all internally reflected paths,

$$
\boxed{\frac{I_{\rm transmitted}}{I_{\rm incident}}=(1-0.04)^2=0.9216,\qquad\text{reduction}=7.84\%\simeq8\%.}
$$

The power-transmission factors, rather than electric-field [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) alone, are multiplied. Absorption, coatings and interference are excluded by this approximation.
