<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

Take $f$ as the tensile [force](../../../../../force-split.md) exerted on the band, so reversible stretching supplies work $f\,dx$. At fixed other mechanical variables,

$$
dU=T\,dS+f\,dx,\qquad dF=-S\,dT+f\,dx.
$$

Equality of mixed [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) of the [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) yields the [thermoelastic Maxwell relation for a stretched band](../../../../../thermoelastic-maxwell-relation-for-a-stretched-band.md)

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial x}\right)_T=-\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial T}\right)_x.}
$$

For entropy-dominated [rubber elasticity](../../../../../rubber-elasticity.md), increasing extension reduces [configurational entropy](../../../../../configurational-entropy.md), consistently giving a tensile [force](../../../../../force-split.md) that increases with [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) at fixed extension. A [force](../../../../../force-split.md) defined as the band's restoring [force](../../../../../force-split.md) on its support has the opposite sign.
