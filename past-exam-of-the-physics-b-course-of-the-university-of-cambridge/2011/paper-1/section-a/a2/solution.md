<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

In steady state the incoming [volumetric flow rate](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) equals the discharge. [Bernoulli's principle](../../../../../bernoulli-s-principle.md) between a nearly stationary free surface and the atmospheric outlet gives the ideal [Torricelli's law](../../../../../torricelli-s-law.md) [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $\sqrt{2gh}$. The specified [discharge coefficient](../../../../../discharge-coefficient.md) multiplies the opening-flow rate, so

$$
Q=\frac{10\times10^{-3}}{60}=1.6667\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-1}},\qquad
Q=C_d A\sqrt{2gh}.
$$

Thus, with $A=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$,

$$
\boxed{h=\frac1{2g}\left(\frac{Q}{0.62A}\right)^2=0.368\,\mathrm m.}
$$

Neglect surface-speed and capillary corrections. The coefficient includes contraction and loss effects; it should not be applied a second time to the calculated height.
