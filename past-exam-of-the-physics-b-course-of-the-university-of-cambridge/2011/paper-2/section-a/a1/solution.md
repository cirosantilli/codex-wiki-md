<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

For the [entropy balance of a thermostatted resistor](../../../../../entropy-balance-of-a-thermostatted-resistor.md), electrical work becomes [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) $Q=I^2Rt=2.0\times10^7\,\mathrm J$. At fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) the [resistor](../../../../../resistor.md) has no net change of [thermodynamic state](../../../../../thermodynamic-state.md); it exports this [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) to its thermostat. Treating the electrical supply as a source of work,

$$
\boxed{\Delta S_{\rm resistor}=0,\qquad \Delta S_{\rm universe}=\frac{I^2Rt}{T}=6.67\times10^4\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.}
$$

[Entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) is produced by [Joule heating](../../../../../joule-heating.md) even though it does not accumulate in the [resistor](../../../../../resistor.md). Additional [entropy production](../../../../../entropy-production.md) inside a nonideal [power](../../../../../power.md) supply is outside the specified model.
