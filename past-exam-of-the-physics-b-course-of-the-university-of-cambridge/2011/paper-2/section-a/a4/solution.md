<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

For a monatomic [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), $\gamma=C_P/C_V=5/3$. If the expansion is reversible adiabatic, $TV^{\gamma-1}$ is constant, so

$$
\boxed{T_f=300\times8^{-2/3}=75\,\mathrm K\quad\text{(isentropic expansion).}}
$$

Adiabatic isolation alone does not imply this [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) law. For example, [free expansion](../../../../../free-expansion.md) into vacuum has $Q=W=0$, so an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) remains at $300\,\mathrm K$. Thus $75\,\mathrm K$ is the usual intended answer with the additional isentropic assumption; the literal adjective “adiabatic” does not uniquely select it.[“Isothermal and Adiabatic Expansion”, Eqs. (6.53) and (6.61); “Free Expansion of Gas”, Eqs. (6.144)–(6.148)](../../../../../thermodynamics-and-statistical-mechanics-by-richard-fitzpatrick.md)
