<h1 id="section-b/b6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the vacuum half-space keep the actual charge $Q$ at $(a,0,0)$ and put its image at the reflected point $(-a,0,0)$. Then

$$
\boxed{\phi_+(x,y,z)=\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left[\frac1{r_+}-\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+1}\frac1{r_-}\right]\quad(x>0).}
$$

The reflected singularity lies outside this region. The real singularity has the correct charge strength, and the interface coefficients obey the two matching equations in the parent Solution at every $y,z$. With decay at infinity, [uniqueness of an electrostatic dielectric boundary-value problem](../../../../../../uniqueness-of-an-electrostatic-dielectric-boundary-value-problem.md) establishes the construction throughout both half-spaces.
