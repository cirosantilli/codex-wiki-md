<h1 id="section-b/b6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [dielectric](../../../../../../dielectric.md) half-space the two formal sources coincide at $(a,0,0)$, outside the region being represented. Their sum is $Q+Q'=2\epsilon_rQ/(1+\epsilon_r)$ in the [dielectric](../../../../../../dielectric.md) Coulomb kernel, hence

$$
\boxed{\phi_-(x,y,z)=\frac{2Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0(1+\epsilon_r)r_+}\quad(x<0).}
$$

There is no charge singularity inside this half-space. The expression satisfies [Laplace's equation](../../../../../../laplace-equation.md) there, decays at infinity, and matches the vacuum solution's tangential [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) and normal electric displacement. The coincident image is a mathematical representation, not an extra physical charge in the vacuum region.
