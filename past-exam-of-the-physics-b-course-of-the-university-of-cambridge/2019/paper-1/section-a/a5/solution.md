<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

For a reversible [adiabatic process](../../../../../adiabatic-process.md), an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) obeys $TV^{\gamma-1}=\text{constant}$. A [monatomic gas](../../../../../monatomic-gas.md) has [heat capacity ratio](../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma=5/3$. Taking the stated expansion factor to mean $V_f=9V_i$,

$$
\boxed{T_f=320\left(\frac19\right)^{2/3}\ \mathrm K
\simeq74.0\ \mathrm K.}
$$

This assumes the usual quasistatic [isentropic process](../../../../../isentropic-process.md). Heat insulation alone does not determine the final [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md): an ideal-gas [free expansion](../../../../../free-expansion.md) is also adiabatic but leaves the temperature unchanged. The numerical answer uses nine times the original volume, the conventional intended meaning of the source's “nine times larger”.
