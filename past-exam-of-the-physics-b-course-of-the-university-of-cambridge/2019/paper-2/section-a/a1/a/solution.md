<h1 id="section-a/a1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The units supplied for 130 indicate [specific heat capacity](../../../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md), so the block's total [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) is $C=mc=0.5(130)=65\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. Treat it as constant, and the lake as a [thermal reservoir](../../../../../../heat-bath.md) at $T_0=283.15\,\mathrm K$. The initial block [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) is $T_i=373.15\,\mathrm K$.

Compute the block's [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) change using a reversible comparison path, even though the actual cooling is irreversible:

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{block}}=\int_{T_i}^{T_0}\frac{C\,dT}{T}
=C\ln\frac{T_0}{T_i}=-17.940\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.
$$

The lake receives $Q=C(T_i-T_0)=5850\,\mathrm J$ at constant [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), so

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{lake}}=\frac Q{T_0}=20.660\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta S_{\mathrm{Universe}}=2.72\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.}
$$

This is [entropy generated by cooling a finite body in a reservoir](../../../../../../entropy-generated-by-cooling-a-finite-body-in-a-reservoir.md). The positive total is consistent with the [Second law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md), although the cooling block's own [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) decreases.
