<h1 id="section-b/b8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A spatially uniform external [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md) moves a body's [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) without stretching it. [Tidal forces](../../../../../../tidal-force.md) arise from the variation of that [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) across the body. Let a distant [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) $M$ lie at distance $D$ along [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) $\mathbf n$, and let $|\mathbf r|\ll D$ denote position relative to the body's centre. Subtract the centre's [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md). To first order,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a_{\mathrm{tidal}}=\frac{GM}{D^3}
\left[3(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf r)\mathbf n-\mathbf r\right].}
$$

The [tidal tensor](../../../../../../tidal-tensor.md) is $T_{ij}=GM(3n_in_j-\delta_{ij})/D^3$. It stretches along the line to the source with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $2GM/D^3$ and compresses in the two perpendicular directions with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $-GM/D^3$. Its [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) vanishes outside the source, consistently with [Laplace's equation](../../../../../../laplace-equation.md) for the [gravitational potential](../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md).

After removal of the constant and uniform-acceleration terms, the tidal potential is

$$
\Phi_{\mathrm{tidal}}=-\frac{GM}{2D^3}
\left[3(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf r)^2-r^2\right].
$$

An ideal equilibrium ocean on a [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) of radius $R$ follows an equipotential of the body's gravity plus this perturbation. For surface gravity $g$, the leading [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md) is

$$
\delta h(\psi)=-\frac{\Phi_{\mathrm{tidal}}(R,\psi)}g
=\frac{GMR^2}{2gD^3}(3\cos^2\psi-1),
$$

up to a constant fixing total [water](../../../../../../water.md) [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md). The quadrupolar form produces bulges on both the near and far sides; the far-side bulge follows from differential [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md), without postulating a repulsive gravitational [force](../../../../../../force-split.md).

The Moon and Sun both raise terrestrial [tides](../../../../../../tide.md), with relative strength set mainly by $M/D^3$, rather than by the total gravitational pull $M/D^2$. Their contributions reinforce near new and full Moon to produce [spring tides](../../../../../../spring-tide.md), and partly cancel near quadrature to produce [neap tides](../../../../../../neap-tide.md). Earth's [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) relative to the tidal pattern gives a strong semidiurnal component in the simplest geometry. Real [tide](../../../../../../tide.md) heights and timing depend on ocean depth, coastlines, basin resonances, [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md), friction and the declination of the forcing bodies. [Tidal dissipation](../../../../../../tidal-dissipation.md) produces phase lags and [torques](../../../../../../torque.md), exchanging rotational and [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) and gradually altering the [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) and orbit. The equilibrium bulge is therefore a useful starting model, not an exact local [tide](../../../../../../tide.md) forecast.
