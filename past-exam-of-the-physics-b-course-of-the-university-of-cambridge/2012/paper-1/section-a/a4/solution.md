<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](../../../../../stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives solar [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) $L_\odot=4\pi R_\odot^2\sigma T_\odot^4$. At orbital distance $r$, the incident [irradiance](../../../../../irradiance.md) is $\sigma T_\odot^4R_\odot^2/r^2$. A spherical absorber intercepts a disc of [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) $\pi R_M^2$ but radiates over $4\pi R_M^2$. Thus the uniform-temperature [radiative equilibrium](../../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md) estimate is

$$
\pi R_M^2\sigma T_\odot^4\frac{R_\odot^2}{r^2}=4\pi R_M^2\sigma T_M^4,
\qquad
\boxed{T_M=T_\odot\sqrt{\frac{R_\odot}{2r}}\simeq245\,\mathrm K.}
$$

The planetary [radius](../../../../../radius.md) cancels. This is a [black-body radiation](../../../../../black-body-radiation.md) estimate with zero reflected fraction and complete redistribution of absorbed [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md). More generally the balance fixes $\langle T^4\rangle^{1/4}$, rather than the arithmetic mean of a nonuniform surface [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). It excludes atmospheric heating and thermal lag; no measured surface [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is inferred.
