<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

Choose the plate polarity so that the gap [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) points along $+z$ when $V>0$. Away from the plate edges, the [quasistatic electromagnetic approximation](../../../../../quasistatic-electromagnetic-approximation.md) gives $E_z=V_0\sin\omega t/d$. A circular [Ampere-Maxwell law](../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) contour of [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $r<a$ then gives

$$
2\pi r B_\phi=\mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{d}{dt}\int_0^r E_z\,2\pi s\,ds,
\qquad
\boxed{\mathbf B(r,t)=\frac{\mu_0\epsilon_0\omega V_0}{2d}\,r\cos\omega t\,\widehat{\boldsymbol\phi}\quad(0\leq r<a).}
$$

It is [displacement current](../../../../../displacement-current.md), rather than a [conduction current](../../../../../conduction-current.md) across the air, that supplies the enclosed current term. The direction follows the [right-hand rule](../../../../../right-hand-rule.md) for increasing positive [electric flux](../../../../../electric-flux.md); it reverses every half cycle. In the ideal extension that sets the gap [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) to zero outside the plate footprint, $B_\phi=\mu_0\epsilon_0\omega V_0a^2\cos\omega t/(2dr)$ for $r>a$. That extension is an edge approximation, not an exact exterior solution for finite plates. Neglect [fringing field of a capacitor](../../../../../fringing-field-of-a-capacitor.md) and retardation, requiring $d\ll a$ and $\omega a/c\ll1$.
