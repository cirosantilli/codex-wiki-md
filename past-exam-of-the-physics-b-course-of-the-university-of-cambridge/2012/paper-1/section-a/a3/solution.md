<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

For a hollow, air-filled [rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](../../../../../rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md) with conducting walls, transverse confinement gives

$$
f_{mn}=\frac c2\sqrt{\left(\frac m w\right)^2+\left(\frac n h\right)^2}.
$$

A [transverse electric mode of a rectangular waveguide](../../../../../transverse-electric-mode-of-a-rectangular-waveguide.md) permits one zero index, but not two; a [transverse magnetic mode of a rectangular waveguide](../../../../../transverse-magnetic-mode-of-a-rectangular-waveguide.md) requires both indices positive. Take $w=20\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $h=10\,\mathrm{mm}$. The dominant mode is $\mathrm{TE}_{10}$, with

$$
\boxed{f_c=\frac{c}{2w}=7.49\,\mathrm{GHz}\simeq7.5\,\mathrm{GHz}.}
$$

The [waveguide cutoff frequency](../../../../../waveguide-cutoff-frequency.md) is the limiting value: positive axial [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) and propagating power require $f>f_c$. There is no [transverse electromagnetic mode](../../../../../transverse-electromagnetic-mode.md) in this single hollow conducting pipe; otherwise a zero cutoff would incorrectly follow.
