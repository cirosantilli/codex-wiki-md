<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

For the [inertia tensor of a uniform cube](../../../../../inertia-tensor-of-a-uniform-cube.md), reflection [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) makes every off-diagonal element of the central [inertia tensor](../../../../../inertia-tensor.md) vanish. Permutation of the three axes makes its diagonal elements equal. Since $M=8\rho a^3$ and the mean of each squared coordinate in the [cube](../../../../../cube.md) is $a^2/3$,

$$
I_{xx}=\int(y^2+z^2)\,dm=\frac23Ma^2,\qquad
\mathsf I=\frac23Ma^2\mathsf1.
$$

Every central axis is therefore a [principal axis](../../../../../principal-axis.md), including the body diagonal. The [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is parallel to the [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md), and its magnitude is

$$
\boxed{J=\frac{16}{3}\rho a^5\omega.}
$$
