<h1 id="section-c/c11/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a repulsive [inverse-square force](../../../../../../inverse-square-force.md), $V(r)=+\kappa/r$. The [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md) is positive and decreases from infinity to zero, so a trajectory reaching a finite [radius](../../../../../../radius.md) has $E>0$. For nonzero [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md), it is the accessible branch of a **[hyperbola](../../../../../../hyperbola.md)**, with $e^2=1+2EJ^2/(m\kappa^2)>1$. There are no bound [ellipses](../../../../../../ellipse.md) or finite-distance zero-energy [parabolas](../../../../../../parabola.md). At zero [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md), the motion is straight inward followed by straight outward after reflection at a radial turning point.
