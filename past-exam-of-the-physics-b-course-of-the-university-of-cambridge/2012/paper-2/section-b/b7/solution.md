<h1 id="section-b/b7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B7](../b7.md)

In microscopic SI form, [Maxwell's equations](../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) couple charge [density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho$ and current [density](../../../../../density.md) $\mathbf J$ to electric and [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md):

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\frac\rho{\epsilon_0},\qquad
\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0,\qquad
\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t\mathbf B,\qquad
\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J+\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t\mathbf E.
$$

Gauss's electric law expresses electric flux from charge and reduces to the inverse-square Coulomb field for a point charge. Gauss's magnetic law gives zero net [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) through every closed surface; [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) lines do not begin or end on magnetic monopoles in this theory.

Faraday's induction law says changing [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) produces a circulating [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md). For a fixed loop, its circulation is minus the rate of change of enclosed [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md), explaining the sign of induction and [Lenz's law](../../../../../lenz-s-law.md). For example, changing a primary coil's [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) changes the flux through a stationary secondary coil and induces a [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md). A moving wire also has the magnetic part of the [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) on its charges; the fixed-loop field equation and the full motional-emf calculation must be distinguished.

The Ampère-Maxwell law relates magnetic circulation to conduction current and [displacement current](../../../../../displacement-current.md). The latter is essential around a charging capacitor: a surface cutting the lead and another passing through the gap must predict the same circulation. Taking the divergence gives

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf J+\partial_t\rho=0,
$$

using Gauss's electric law. [Charge conservation](../../../../../charge-conservation.md) therefore fixes the consistency of the time-dependent equations. In matter, the equivalent macroscopic equations use $\mathbf D$ and $\mathbf H$, free sources and constitutive relations. The [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho\mathbf E+\mathbf J\times\mathbf B$ describes exchange of [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) with charges.

In source-free vacuum, take the curl of Faraday's law and use $\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf E)=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf E)-\nabla^2\mathbf E$. Both fields satisfy

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\mathbf E=\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t^2\mathbf E,\qquad
\nabla^2\mathbf B=\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t^2\mathbf B,
\qquad c=\frac1{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}}.}
$$

For a [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md), $\omega=ck$, $\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf E=\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf B=0$ and $\mathbf B=\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf E/c$. The fields are transverse, mutually perpendicular and in phase. Their [energy density](../../../../../energy-density.md) and [energy flux](../../../../../energy-flux.md) are

$$
u=\frac{\epsilon_0E^2}{2}+\frac{B^2}{2\mu_0},\qquad
\mathbf S=\frac{\mathbf E\times\mathbf B}{\mu_0},\qquad
\partial_tu+\nabla\cdot\mathbf S=-\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E.
$$

This is [Poynting's theorem](../../../../../poynting-theorem.md): field [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is transported and can do [work](../../../../../work.md) on matter. **[Electromagnetic radiation](../../../../../electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) is a propagating, energy-carrying solution of the coupled electric and [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) equations.**
