<h1 id="section-b/b8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A small [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md) $\mathbf u$ in a homogeneous isotropic elastic solid obeys [linear elasticity](../../../../../../linear-elasticity.md). With Lamé constants $\lambda,\mu$ and [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) [density](../../../../../../density.md) $\rho$, [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) balance gives

$$
\rho\partial_t^2\mathbf u=(\lambda+\mu)\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf u)+\mu\nabla^2\mathbf u.
$$

A longitudinal [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) has [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md) parallel to its propagation direction and changes local volume. A [transverse wave](../../../../../../transverse-wave.md) has perpendicular [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md) and produces shear. Substitution gives

$$
\boxed{c_L=\sqrt{\frac{\lambda+2\mu}{\rho}},\qquad
c_T=\sqrt{\frac\mu\rho}.}
$$

An ideal fluid has no static [shear modulus](../../../../../../shear-modulus.md) and cannot support this bulk transverse [elastic wave](../../../../../../elastic-wave.md). A thin rod with free lateral surfaces instead supports an extensional long-wavelength mode with speed $\sqrt{E/\rho}$; its transverse contraction changes the effective stiffness, so it should not be identified with the unbounded-solid longitudinal mode.

[Stress](../../../../../../stress.md) and [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md) boundary conditions produce reflection, transmission, mode conversion and guided [waves](../../../../../../wave-split.md). [Viscoelasticity](../../../../../../viscoelasticity.md) and scattering can attenuate [waves](../../../../../../wave-split.md); finite geometry can make guided modes dispersive even when the bulk elastic constants are frequency independent. **[Wave](../../../../../../wave-split.md) speed depends on both material stiffness and the allowed deformation.**
