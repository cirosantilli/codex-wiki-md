<h1 id="section-b/b8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For [conservative forces](../../../../../../conservative-force.md) and ideal constraints that do no [work](../../../../../../work.md), [mechanical energy](../../../../../../mechanical-energy.md) $E=T+U$ is constant. Work-energy balance remains useful with nonconservative [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md): their power gives $dE/dt$. In one dimension,

$$
\tfrac12m\dot q^2+U(q)=E
$$

locates accessible regions and turning points, and reduces the trajectory to a quadrature $dt=dq/\sqrt{2(E-U)/m}$. A local minimum of potential is a stable equilibrium; a local maximum is unstable. Near a nondegenerate minimum, $U=U_0+K(q-q_0)^2/2+\cdots$ gives $\omega^2=K/m$.

For a [central force](../../../../../../central-force.md), conserved [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) reduces radial motion to an [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md) $U_{\rm eff}=U(r)+L^2/(2mr^2)$. Its extrema determine circular orbits and their radial stability. In several coordinates, [small oscillations](../../../../../../small-oscillation.md) follow from the kinetic-energy [mass matrix](../../../../../../mass-matrix.md) $M$ and potential Hessian $K$ through $\det(K-\omega^2M)=0$.

[Energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is also central to the [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) $\mathcal L=T-U$ and the [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md). A time-independent natural mechanical system has conserved [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md); [cyclic coordinates](../../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md) yield additional momenta. [Energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) alone does not normally determine every coordinate of a multi-degree-of-freedom motion. **Its value is to expose allowed motion, stability and reductions before solving the full differential equations.**
