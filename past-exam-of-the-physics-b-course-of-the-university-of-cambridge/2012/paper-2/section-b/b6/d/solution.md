<h1 id="section-b/b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use a cold, unmagnetized, collisionless [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) plasma with immobile ions. For an oscillating field proportional to $e^{-i\omega t}$, the [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md) follows $-m_e\omega^2\mathbf x=-e\mathbf E$. Hence $\mathbf P=-n_ee\mathbf x=-n_ee^2\mathbf E/(m_e\omega^2)$ and

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_r(\omega)=1-\frac{\omega_p^2}{\omega^2},\qquad
\omega_p^2=\frac{n_ee^2}{\epsilon_0m_e}.}
$$

The induced polarization opposes the field because [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) inertia changes the phase of its [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md). [Maxwell's equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) give transverse electromagnetic [dispersion](../../../../../../wave-dispersion.md)

$$
\omega^2=\omega_p^2+c^2k^2.
$$

Thus [transverse waves](../../../../../../transverse-wave.md) propagate above the [plasma frequency](../../../../../../plasma-frequency.md), while below it the wavenumber is imaginary and a semi-infinite lossless plasma reflects an incident [wave](../../../../../../wave-split.md) with an evanescent interior field. In the cold electrostatic model, a longitudinal [density](../../../../../../density.md) oscillation instead has frequency $\omega_p$.

Collisions at rate $\nu$ give $\epsilon_r=1-\omega_p^2/[\omega(\omega+i\nu)]$ for this time convention, introducing absorption. A background [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) makes the response anisotropic, and thermal motion supplies additional [dispersion](../../../../../../wave-dispersion.md) and static screening. The divergent zero-frequency cold collisionless formula should not be mistaken for a complete description of static Debye screening. **The simple [dielectric](../../../../../../dielectric.md) function is a dynamical electron-inertia response under stated assumptions.**
