<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

For inner radius $a$ and outer conductor's inner radius $b$, [Gauss's law](../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives $E(s)=\lambda/(2\pi\epsilon_0\epsilon_rs)$. Integrating across the [dielectric](../../../../../dielectric.md) gives [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) $V=\lambda\ln(b/a)/(2\pi\epsilon_0\epsilon_r)$. Thus the [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md) per unit length is

$$
\boxed{C'=\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0\epsilon_r}{\ln(b/a)}
=\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0}{\ln2}\simeq1.61\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{F\,m^{-1}}
=161\,\mathrm{pF\,m^{-1}}.}
$$

The stated outer radius is interpreted as the radius of the dielectric-facing surface. [Magnetic permeability](../../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md) affects [inductance](../../../../../inductance.md), but does not enter this electrostatic [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md).
