<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

[Kepler's third law](../../../../../kepler-s-third-law.md) for negligible planet [mass](../../../../../mass.md) gives $a_P/a_E=(P_P/P_E)^{2/3}$. Therefore $a_P=1.5\times10^8(248)^{2/3}\simeq5.92\times10^9\,\mathrm{km}$. The furthest point is [apoapsis](../../../../../apoapsis.md), so

$$
\boxed{r_{\max}=a_P(1+e)\simeq7.40\times10^9\,\mathrm{km}.}
$$

The supplied orbital period and [eccentricity](../../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) are sufficient; no present-day ephemeris is needed.
