<h1 id="section-c/c12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) rises linearly with elastic twist, so the stored [elastic energy](../../../../../../elastic-energy.md) is the work integral

$$
U=\int_0^\phi\frac{GJ_p}{L}\phi'\,d\phi'
=\frac12C\phi.
$$

Using the transmitted torque and end twist,

$$
\boxed{U\simeq\frac12(133.3)(1.243\times10^{-3})\ \mathrm J
=8.29\times10^{-2}\ \mathrm J.}
$$

This is energy associated with deformation, distinct from the much larger kinetic energy of the whole spinning shaft.
