<h1 id="section-c/c12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Power is transmitted at rate $P=C\Omega$, so

$$
C=\frac{4.0\times10^4}{300}\ \mathrm{N\,m}
=133.3\ \mathrm{N\,m}.
$$

For $a=0.040\ \mathrm m$, $L=3.0\ \mathrm m$ and $G=80\times10^9\ \mathrm{Pa}$, the [polar second moment of area](../../../../../../polar-second-moment-of-area.md) is $J_p=4.021\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^4}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\phi=\frac{CL}{GJ_p}
\simeq1.24\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{rad}
=0.0712^\circ.}
$$

The density does not enter this static torsional deformation.
