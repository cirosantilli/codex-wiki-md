<h1 id="section-c/c12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the uniform solid cylinder, the axial [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) is

$$
I_z=\int r^2\,dm=\rho LJ_p
=(8000)(3.0)(4.021\times10^{-6})
\simeq0.0965\ \mathrm{kg\,m^2}.
$$

The rotational [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) at $\Omega=300\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$ is

$$
\boxed{K=\frac12I_z\Omega^2
\simeq4.34\times10^3\ \mathrm J.}
$$

This includes the shaft's bulk steady rotation; the small twist does not appreciably alter its mass moment at this order.
