<h1 id="section-c/c11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take a transverse offset $\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf R=0$. Its distance from the source changes only at second order, but the direction of the gravitational force changes at first order:

$$
\mathbf g(\mathbf R+\mathbf b)
=-\frac{GM}{R^3}(\mathbf R+\mathbf b)+O(GMb^2/R^4)
$$

in its first-order vector expansion. Subtracting the reference acceleration gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf T(\mathbf b)\simeq-\frac{GM}{R^3}\mathbf b.}
$$

It compresses the transverse separation. Together with the radial result, this is [point-mass tidal stretching and squeezing](../../../../../../point-mass-tidal-stretching-and-squeezing.md), with tensor eigenvalues $(2,-1,-1)GM/R^3$.
