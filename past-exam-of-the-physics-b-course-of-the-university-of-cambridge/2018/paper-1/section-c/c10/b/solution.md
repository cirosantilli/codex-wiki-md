<h1 id="section-c/c10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $I_1=I_2=I_\perp$, with $I_3\ne I_\perp$. Then

$$
\boxed{p^2=-\Omega^2\frac{(I_3-I_\perp)^2}{I_\perp^2}<0,\qquad
\nu=|\Omega|\frac{|I_3-I_\perp|}{I_\perp}.}
$$

Rotation about the symmetry axis is stable whether that axial moment is larger or smaller than the transverse moment. In fact $\omega_3$ and $\omega_1^2+\omega_2^2$ are exactly constant for a torque-free axisymmetric body: the transverse components rotate into one another at the stated body-frame frequency. Stability here means bounded wobble, not relaxation back to perfect axial rotation.
