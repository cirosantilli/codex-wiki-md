<h1 id="section-c/c10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For three distinct positive [principal moments of inertia](../../../../../../principal-moment-of-inertia.md), the denominator of $p^2$ is positive. If $I_3$ is the largest moment, the two numerator factors have opposite signs, so $p^2<0$. If it is the smallest, they again have opposite signs. If $I_3$ is intermediate, the two factors have the same sign, so $p^2>0$. Thus the [intermediate axis theorem](../../../../../../intermediate-axis-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\text{largest or smallest principal moment: stable;\qquad
intermediate principal moment: unstable}.}
$$

For the stable cases the body-frame wobble frequency is

$$
\nu=|\Omega|\sqrt{\frac{|(I_3-I_1)(I_3-I_2)|}{I_1I_2}}.
$$

The perturbation does not decay in the torque-free conservative model. For the intermediate moment, a generic small impulse excites the growing branch and eventually leaves the linear regime.
