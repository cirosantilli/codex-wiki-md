↑ **Parent:** [B7](../b7.md)

**The field law.** In the [Biot-Savart law](../../../../../biot-savart-law.md), $d\mathbf B$ is the contribution to the [magnetic flux density](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) at the observation point from a directed wire element $d\boldsymbol\ell$ carrying steady conventional [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) $I$. The vector $\mathbf R$ points from that element to the observation point, $R=|\mathbf R|$, and $\widehat{\mathbf R}=\mathbf R/R$. The constant $\mu_0$ is the [vacuum permeability](../../../../../vacuum-permeability.md); the [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) sets the direction by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../right-hand-rule.md). Integrating the elements gives the total field, in teslas.

**The moment of the shown loop.** The arrows in the original PDF run counterclockwise viewed from $+z$. Thus the [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf m=I\Delta x\Delta y\,\widehat{\mathbf z}.}
$$

Its units are $\mathrm{A\,m^2}$. Reversing the current reverses both this moment and the field.
