<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At the hemispherical surface $r=a$, the outward normal from the conductor into the gap is $\widehat{\mathbf r}$. The tangential field is zero there and

$$
E_n=E_r(a,\theta)=-3E_0\cos\theta.
$$

The [electrostatic interface boundary conditions](../../../../../../electrostatic-interface-boundary-conditions.md) give [surface charge density](../../../../../../surface-charge-density.md) $\sigma=\varepsilon_0E_n$, because the interior [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) is zero. Hence

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\rm hemi}=-\frac{3\varepsilon_0V}{d}\cos\theta.}
$$

It is negative for a positive upper-plate voltage, greatest in magnitude at the apex, and zero at the equator. Its units are coulombs per square metre.
