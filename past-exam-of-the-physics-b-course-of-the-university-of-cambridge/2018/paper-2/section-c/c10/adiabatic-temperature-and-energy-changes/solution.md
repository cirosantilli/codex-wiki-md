<h1 id="section-c/c10/adiabatic-temperature-and-energy-changes/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic temperature and energy changes](../adiabatic-temperature-and-energy-changes.md)

For the specified increment,

$$
\frac{\beta\Delta A}{C_A}
=\frac{(1.3\times10^{-4})(10^{-4})}{0.070}
\simeq1.86\times10^{-7}.
$$

Using the adiabat, or its first-order [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md),

$$
\Delta T=330\left(e^{-1.86\times10^{-7}}-1\right)\,\mathrm K
\simeq-330(1.86\times10^{-7})\,\mathrm K,
\qquad
\boxed{\Delta T\simeq-6.1\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm K.}
$$

This is a very small cooling, about $61\,\mathrm{\mu K}$. There is no [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) exchange, and the film is stretched against positive [surface tension](../../../../../../surface-tension-split.md), so the [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) **increases**, with

$$
\boxed{\Delta U=W_{\rm on}=\int_{A_i}^{A_f}\gamma(T(A))\,dA
\simeq2.2\,\mathrm{\mu J}>0.}
$$

The cooling does not contradict this increase. In the linear model, $\gamma-T\gamma'=\gamma_0\simeq0.065\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-2}}$, so

$$
\Delta U=C_A\Delta T+\gamma_0\Delta A
\simeq-4.3\,\mathrm{\mu J}+6.5\,\mathrm{\mu J}.
$$

Creation of surface raises the [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) more than the cooling lowers its temperature-dependent part. The change in [surface tension](../../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) during this tiny [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) fall changes the [work](../../../../../../work.md) only at a negligible higher order.
