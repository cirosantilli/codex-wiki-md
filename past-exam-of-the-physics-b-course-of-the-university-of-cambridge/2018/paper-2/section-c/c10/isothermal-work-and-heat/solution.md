<h1 id="section-c/c10/isothermal-work-and-heat/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal work and heat](../isothermal-work-and-heat.md)

Reading the original PDF graph gives approximately

$$
\gamma(330\,\mathrm K)\simeq22\,\mathrm{mN\,m^{-1}},\qquad
\gamma'\simeq-0.13\,\mathrm{mN\,m^{-1}K^{-1}}
=-1.3\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-2}K^{-1}}.
$$

For example, the points near $330\,\mathrm K$ and $450\,\mathrm K$ are about $22$ and $6.5\,\mathrm{mN\,m^{-1}}$, respectively, giving this slope. The graph supports approximate values rather than exact fitted constants. The [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) increment is $\Delta A=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m^2}$.

At fixed [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), the [surface tension](../../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) is constant during this stretch. The [work](../../../../../../work.md) done _on_ the film is positive:

$$
W_{\rm on}=\int\gamma\,dA\simeq(22\times10^{-3})(10^{-4}),
\qquad \boxed{W_{\rm on}\simeq2.2\,\mathrm{\mu J}.}
$$

For a reversible [isothermal process](../../../../../../isothermal-process.md), the [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) supplied is $Q=T\Delta S=-T\gamma'\Delta A$. Thus

$$
\boxed{Q\simeq(330)(1.3\times10^{-4})(10^{-4})
=4.3\,\mathrm{\mu J}.}
$$

The film absorbs [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) while its [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) stays constant. The first law gives the useful consistency check $\Delta U=Q+W_{\rm on}\simeq6.5\,\mathrm{\mu J}$, agreeing with $(\gamma-T\gamma')\Delta A$.
