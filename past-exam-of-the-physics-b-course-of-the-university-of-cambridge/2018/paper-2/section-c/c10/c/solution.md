<h1 id="section-c/c10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Substitute the [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) differential into the fundamental relation:

$$
dU=T\left(\frac{C_A}{T}\,dT-\gamma'\,dA\right)+\gamma\,dA.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{dU=C_A\,dT+\left(\gamma-T\frac{d\gamma}{dT}\right)dA.}
$$

The coefficient of $dA$ is the change of [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) per added [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) at fixed [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), whereas $\gamma$ is the change of [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) per added [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md). Their difference $-T\gamma'$ is the [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) absorbed per added [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) in a reversible isothermal stretch. Both $\gamma$ and $T\gamma'$ have units $\mathrm{J\,m^{-2}}=\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$.
