<h1 id="section-c/c10/free-energy-identity/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Free-energy identity](../free-energy-identity.md)

Treat the film at fixed material content, with [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) $A$ the coordinate conjugate to the [surface tension](../../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) in the stated [work](../../../../../../work.md) term. The [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) is a [Legendre transform](../../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) of the [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md):

$$
F=U-TS,\qquad dF=dU-T\,dS-S\,dT=-S\,dT+\gamma\,dA.
$$

Its natural independent variables are therefore $T,A$, so

$$
S=-\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial T}\right)_A,
\qquad \gamma=\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial A}\right)_T.
$$

Equality of mixed second [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) gives the [Maxwell relation](../../../../../../maxwell-relations.md) for the [thermodynamics of a liquid film](../../../../../../thermodynamics-of-a-liquid-film.md):

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial\gamma}{\partial T}\right)_A
=-\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial A}\right)_T.}
$$

The [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) convention matters: the supplied fundamental relation already specifies the coefficient of $dA$. There is no additional factor of two unless one changes from total interfacial [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) to a projected film [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) and changes the [work](../../../../../../work.md) term consistently.
