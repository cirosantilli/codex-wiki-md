<h1 id="section-c/c10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The definition of [heat capacity at constant area](../../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-area.md) and the [Maxwell relation](../../../../../../maxwell-relations.md) give the [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) differential

$$
dS=\frac{C_A}{T}\,dT-\gamma'\,dA.
$$

For a reversible [adiabatic process](../../../../../../adiabatic-process.md), $dS=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{dT}{dA}\right)_S=\frac{T\gamma'}{C_A}<0}
$$

for positive [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) $C_A$. **Reversible adiabatic stretching cools the film.** Reversibility, or negligible [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) production, is essential to this inference. Adiabatic isolation alone only sets the [heat transfer](../../../../../../heat-transfer.md) to zero; an irreversible stretch can generate [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md), so its [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) change is not fixed by this isentropic [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md).
