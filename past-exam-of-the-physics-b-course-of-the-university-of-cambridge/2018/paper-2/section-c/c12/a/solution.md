<h1 id="section-c/c12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Distinguish electrical [current density](../../../../../../current-density.md) $\mathbf J_e$ from [heat flux](../../../../../../heat-flux-density.md) $\mathbf J_Q$. Uniform current across the circular section gives

$$
J_e=\frac{I}{\pi a^2},\qquad E=\frac{J_e}{\sigma}
$$

by [Ohm's law](../../../../../../ohm-s-law.md). The [local Joule heating rate](../../../../../../local-joule-heating-rate.md) is electrical [work](../../../../../../work.md) per unit [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) and time:

$$
\boxed{H=\mathbf J_e\cdot\mathbf E=\frac{J_e^2}{\sigma}
=\frac{I^2}{\sigma\pi^2a^4}.}
$$

For a segment of length $\ell$, integration gives $H\pi a^2\ell=I^2\ell/(\sigma\pi a^2)=I^2R$, confirming the usual resistor [power](../../../../../../power.md). The model takes [electrical conductivity](../../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) constant and ignores temperature-induced current redistribution.
