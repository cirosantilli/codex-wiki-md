<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

Model air as a dilute [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) of [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md) described by the [hard sphere](../../../../../hard-sphere.md) model. The [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) and [mean free path](../../../../../mean-free-path.md) are

$$
n=\frac{p}{k_BT},\qquad \lambda=\frac{1}{\sqrt2\,n\pi d^2}
=\frac{k_BT}{\sqrt2\,\pi d^2p}.
$$

The factor $\sqrt2$ accounts for the relative motion of the colliding [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md). A molecular [collision](../../../../../collision.md) diameter has to be estimated because none is supplied. Taking $d\simeq0.37\,\mathrm{nm}$, appropriate as an order-of-magnitude size for the main constituents of air, gives

$$
n=\frac{10^5}{(1.380649\times10^{-23})(298.15)}
\simeq2.43\times10^{25}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}},
\qquad
\lambda\simeq6.8\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm m.
$$

Thus **the [mean free path](../../../../../mean-free-path.md) is about $7\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm m$, or $70\,\mathrm{nm}$**. A slightly smaller assumed diameter $0.35\,\mathrm{nm}$ gives about $76\,\mathrm{nm}$, illustrating the intended precision of the estimate.
