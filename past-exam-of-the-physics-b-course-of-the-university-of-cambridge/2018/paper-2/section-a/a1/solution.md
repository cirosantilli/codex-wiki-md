<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

For the usual hollow [rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](../../../../../rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md), let the propagation axis be $z$ and the interior be $0<x<a$, $0<y<b$. A [transverse magnetic polarization](../../../../../transverse-magnetic-polarization.md) has $B_z=0$ and $E_z\ne0$. At a [perfect conductor](../../../../../perfect-conductor.md) wall the tangential [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) vanishes. Since $E_z$ is tangential to all four side walls, it satisfies a homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) around the rectangle.

Separating the transverse [Helmholtz equation](../../../../../helmholtz-equation.md) therefore gives the [transverse magnetic mode of a rectangular waveguide](../../../../../transverse-magnetic-mode-of-a-rectangular-waveguide.md)

$$
E_z=E_0\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{a}\right)\sin\left(\frac{m\pi y}{b}\right)e^{i(k_z z-\omega t)},
\qquad n,m=1,2,\ldots.
$$

A zero value of either index makes $E_z$ identically zero, rather than producing a nonzero field uniform along that direction. The transverse electric and [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) of this TM family are obtained from transverse [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) of $E_z$, so they also vanish. Thus **both TM indices must be nonzero: neither $\mathrm{TM}_{n0}$ nor $\mathrm{TM}_{0m}$ exists** in this rectangular hollow-guide model. In contrast, transverse electric modes obey a [Neumann boundary condition](../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md) on $B_z$ and may have one zero index. The claim is about this [waveguide](../../../../../waveguide.md) geometry, not all possible guides.
