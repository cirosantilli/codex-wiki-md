<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

Use absolute [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_c=278.15\,\mathrm K$ and $T_h=298.15\,\mathrm K$. Let $\dot Q_c$ be the [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) extracted from the ground, $\dot Q_h$ the [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) delivered indoors, and $P$ the electrical input [power](../../../../../power.md). For a reversible [heat pump](../../../../../heat-pump.md), the [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) transferred per unit time balances:

$$
\frac{\dot Q_h}{T_h}=\frac{\dot Q_c}{T_c},
\qquad \dot Q_h=\dot Q_c+P.
$$

Consequently the [coefficient of performance](../../../../../coefficient-of-performance.md) is

$$
\mathrm{COP}_{\rm heating}=\frac{\dot Q_h}{P}
=\frac{T_h}{T_h-T_c}=\frac{298.15}{20}=14.9075.
$$

The requested [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) delivery is a rate, since the duration is unspecified:

$$
\boxed{\dot Q_h\simeq14.9\,\mathrm{kW}.}
$$

Of this, $13.9\,\mathrm{kW}$ comes from the ground and $1.0\,\mathrm{kW}$ from the electricity. A [coefficient of performance](../../../../../coefficient-of-performance.md) greater than one is consistent with [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) because the [heat pump](../../../../../heat-pump.md) transports [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) as well as converting electrical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
