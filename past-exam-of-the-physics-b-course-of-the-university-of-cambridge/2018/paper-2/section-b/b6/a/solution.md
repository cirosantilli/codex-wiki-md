**[Magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and [force](../../../../../../force-split.md).** A steady [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) produces a [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). In [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) the [Biot-Savart law](../../../../../../biot-savart-law.md) gives

$$
\mathbf B_1(\mathbf r)=\frac{\mu_0I_1}{4\pi}
\int_{C_1}\frac{d\boldsymbol\ell_1\times(\mathbf r-\mathbf r_1)}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_1|^3}.
$$

The [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) on the charge carriers transfers [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) to the material carrying them. The resulting [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) on a current element of another circuit is $d\mathbf F_2=I_2\,d\boldsymbol\ell_2\times\mathbf B_1$, or, for a continuous current distribution, the magnetic [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) per unit [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) is $\mathbf f=\mathbf J\times\mathbf B$.

For two long parallel straight wires a distance $r$ apart, [Ampère's law](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) gives $B_1=\mu_0 I_1/(2\pi r)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{F}{L}=\frac{\mu_0 I_1I_2}{2\pi r}.}
$$

The [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) is **attractive for parallel currents and repulsive for opposite currents**, by the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) rule. Its units are $\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$. This approximation neglects wire thickness and end effects; a complete circuit includes its return current. Element-by-element [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) need not form an isolated Newton's-third-law pair, but complete stationary circuits have balanced mutual [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) when the entire system is included.

**[Energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) description.** For two linear coupled circuits the [magnetic energy](../../../../../../magnetic-energy.md) is

$$
W_B=\frac12L_1I_1^2+\frac12L_2I_2^2+M(q)I_1I_2.
$$

Here $q$ is a mechanical coordinate and $M$ the signed [mutual inductance](../../../../../../mutual-inductance.md). At prescribed currents, the generalized [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) is $I_1I_2\,\partial M/\partial q$ if the self-inductances do not vary. This positive [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is obtained from [magnetic coenergy](../../../../../../magnetic-coenergy.md), or by including the electrical sources' [work](../../../../../../work.md); differentiating $W_B$ with a minus sign at fixed currents would omit that source [work](../../../../../../work.md). At prescribed flux linkages the [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) instead equals the negative [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of stored [magnetic energy](../../../../../../magnetic-energy.md) with those linkages fixed. The geometry thus determines both [inductance](../../../../../../inductance.md) and mechanical [force](../../../../../../force-split.md).
