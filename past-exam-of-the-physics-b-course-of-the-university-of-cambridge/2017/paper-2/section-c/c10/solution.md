<h1 id="section-c/c10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C10](../c10.md)

A [Carnot cycle](../../../../../carnot-cycle.md) consists of four reversible stages: isothermal expansion at the hot-reservoir [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_h$, adiabatic expansion to $T_c$, isothermal compression at $T_c$, and adiabatic compression back to the starting state. The adiabatic stages exchange no [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md). For one mole of an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), the two isothermal stages have heats $Q_h=RT_h\ln(V_B/V_A)$ and $Q_c=RT_c\ln(V_C/V_D)$ in magnitude. The reversible adiabatic relations ensure $V_B/V_A=V_C/V_D$.

More generally, the total [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) change over any reversible cycle is zero. The two [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) exchanges therefore satisfy

$$
\frac{Q_h}{T_h}-\frac{Q_c}{T_c}=0.
$$

With [work](../../../../../work.md) output $W=Q_h-Q_c$, its [thermal efficiency](../../../../../thermal-efficiency.md) is

$$
\boxed{\eta_C=\frac W{Q_h}=1-\frac{T_c}{T_h}.}
$$

The [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) must be absolute. The second law makes this the maximum efficiency for an engine exchanging [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) only with those two reservoirs; irreversible [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) production lowers the efficiency.
