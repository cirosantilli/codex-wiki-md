<h1 id="section-c/c10/state-temperatures/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [State temperatures](../state-temperatures.md)

For one mole, $pV=RT$, and constant [heat capacities](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) satisfy $C_p-C_V=R$. The specified diatomic-gas model has

$$
\gamma=1.4=\frac75,\qquad C_V=\frac{R}{\gamma-1}=\frac52R,
\qquad C_p=\frac72R.
$$

A reversible [adiabatic process](../../../../../../adiabatic-process.md) satisfies $TV^{\gamma-1}=\mathrm{constant}$. Constant-pressure expansion satisfies $T\propto V$. Applying these in order gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
T_2&=T_1\left(\frac{V_1}{V_2}\right)^{\gamma-1},\\
T_3&=T_2\frac{V_3}{V_2}
=T_1\left(\frac{V_1}{V_2}\right)^{\gamma-1}\frac{V_3}{V_2},\\
T_4&=T_3\left(\frac{V_3}{V_1}\right)^{\gamma-1}
=T_1\left(\frac{V_3}{V_2}\right)^\gamma,\\
T_{\rm final}&=T_1.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Every [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) ratio is dimensionless, so the expressions have units of [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). For the intended [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) ordering, the minimum [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) is $T_1$ and the maximum is $T_3$.
