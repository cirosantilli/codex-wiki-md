<h1 id="section-c/c12/wall-arrival-rate/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Wall arrival rate](../wall-arrival-rate.md)

Choose a flat wall and let $\theta$ measure the angle from the direction towards it. [Particles](../../../../../../particle-physics.md) of [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) $v$ in [solid angle](../../../../../../solid-angle.md) $d\Omega$ occupy fraction $f(v)dv\,d\Omega/(4\pi)$ of an isotropic [gas](../../../../../../gas.md). In [time](../../../../../../time-in-physics.md) $dt$, those within normal distance $v\cos\theta\,dt$ reach the wall. Their number per [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) per [time](../../../../../../time-in-physics.md) is therefore $n f(v)dv\,v\cos\theta\,d\Omega/(4\pi)$.

Only the incoming [hemisphere](../../../../../../hemisphere.md) contributes. Integrating gives the one-way [collision](../../../../../../collision.md) rate per unit [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md),

$$
J_N=n\int_0^\infty vf(v)\,dv\,
\frac1{4\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos\theta\sin\theta\,d\theta
=\boxed{\frac14n\overline v.}
$$

Its units are $\mathrm{m^{-2}s^{-1}}$. This is not $n\overline v/2$: selecting the incoming half of the [particles](../../../../../../particle-physics.md) must also account for their [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) normal [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md). For [effusion](../../../../../../effusion.md) through a thin hole whose diameter is much less than the [mean free path](../../../../../../mean-free-path.md), the same one-way rate applies because [collisions](../../../../../../collision.md) within the opening can be neglected.
