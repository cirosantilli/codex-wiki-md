<h1 id="section-c/c11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a reversible adiabatic change, $dS=0$, equivalently $C_VdT=-p\,dV$. Substitute $p=RT/(V-b)$:

$$
C_V\frac{dT}{T}+R\frac{dV}{V-b}=0.
$$

Integration, with fixed positive reference values $T_*,W_*$, gives

$$
C_V\ln\frac{T}{T_*}+R\ln\frac{V-b}{W_*}=\mathrm{constant},
\qquad T(V-b)^{R/C_V}=\mathrm{constant}.
$$

Since $T=p(V-b)/R$ and $\gamma=1+R/C_V$,

$$
\boxed{p(V-b)^\gamma=\mathrm{constant}.}
$$

The relation applies to the quasistatic reversible [adiabat](../../../../../../adiabat.md). Adiabatic [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) isolation alone does not enforce it for an irreversible process which generates [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md). The shifted [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) $V-b$, rather than $V$ itself, is the appropriate coordinate in this model.
