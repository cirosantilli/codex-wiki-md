<h1 id="section-c/c11/equations-of-state-and-transitions/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Equations of state and transitions](../equations-of-state-and-transitions.md)

An [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) relates equilibrium state variables such as [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md), [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md), [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and material amount. At fixed composition, it reduces the number of independent thermodynamic variables. It does not by itself specify all caloric properties: information such as [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) or [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) is also needed.

At a liquid-gas first-order transition, an equilibrium [isotherm](../../../../../../isotherm.md) can contain a constant-pressure interval in which changing total [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) changes the phase fractions. A [metastable](../../../../../../metastability.md) single-phase extrapolation can instead show a region with $(\partial p/\partial V)_T>0$; that region has negative [isothermal compressibility](../../../../../../isothermal-compressibility.md) and is mechanically unstable. [Thermodynamic phase](../../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md) coexistence requires equal [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) and [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md), and a consistent free-energy description replaces the unstable region by coexistence. For an appropriate analytic [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md), a [liquid-gas critical point](../../../../../../liquid-gas-critical-point.md) occurs where $(\partial p/\partial V)_T=(\partial^2p/\partial V^2)_T=0$.

These tests diagnose the phase behaviour represented by that [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md). They do not guarantee detection of every possible transition involving an additional [order parameter](../../../../../../order-parameter.md) which the mechanical equation omits.
