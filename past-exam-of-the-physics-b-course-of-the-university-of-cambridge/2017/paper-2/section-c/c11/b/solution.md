<h1 id="section-c/c11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) $H=U+pV$. The [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) gives $pV=RT+pb$, so

$$
H=C_VT+U_*+RT+pb.
$$

At fixed [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md), $pb$ is constant. Therefore

$$
\boxed{C_p=\left(\frac{\partial H}{\partial T}\right)_p=C_V+R,
\qquad \gamma=\frac{C_p}{C_V}=1+\frac R{C_V}.}
$$

Since $C_V$ and $R$ are constants, this ratio is independent of both [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md). Although the [gas](../../../../../../gas.md) is not mechanically ideal when $b\ne0$, its constant-pressure/constant-volume heat-capacity difference remains $R$ in this model.
