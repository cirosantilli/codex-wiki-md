<h1 id="section-c/c11/phase-transition-test/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-transition test](../phase-transition-test.md)

At every $T>0$, $V>b$,

$$
\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial V}\right)_T=-\frac{RT}{(V-b)^2}<0,
\qquad
\left(\frac{\partial^2p}{\partial V^2}\right)_T=\frac{2RT}{(V-b)^3}>0.
$$

There is no constant-pressure coexistence interval, unstable loop or finite-temperature critical inflection. The [isothermal compressibility](../../../../../../isothermal-compressibility.md) is positive:

$$
\kappa_T=-\frac1V\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial p}\right)_T
=\frac{V-b}{pV}>0.
$$

Thus **this [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md) predicts no liquid-gas [phase transition](../../../../../../phase-transition.md) in its physical domain**. Its [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) at the excluded-volume boundary is a repulsive [constraint](../../../../../../constraint-mechanics.md), not a liquid-gas coexistence mechanism. This conclusion concerns the stated model and cannot rule out an ordering transition requiring additional physics or an additional thermodynamic variable. Extrapolation to [absolute zero](../../../../../../absolute-zero.md) is also outside the classical [gas](../../../../../../gas.md) model's reliable regime.
