<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

Assume a hollow square [electromagnetic waveguide](../../../../../electromagnetic-waveguide.md) with perfectly conducting walls, filled with [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) or air. For side $a$, the [waveguide cutoff frequency](../../../../../waveguide-cutoff-frequency.md) is

$$
f_{c,mn}=\frac{c}{2}\sqrt{\left(\frac m a\right)^2+\left(\frac n a\right)^2}.
$$

A transverse electric mode can have one zero index, but not both. A transverse magnetic mode requires both indices positive. Thus the lowest cutoff belongs to the two degenerate transverse electric modes $\mathrm{TE}_{10}$ and $\mathrm{TE}_{01}$, with

$$
\boxed{f_c=\frac{c}{2a}=\frac{2.9979\times10^8}{2\times10^{-2}}\,\mathrm{Hz}
\simeq15.0\,\mathrm{GHz}.}
$$

The corresponding cutoff [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) is $2a=2.0\,\mathrm{cm}$. Strictly, a travelling mode carrying [power](../../../../../power.md) along the guide needs $f>f_c$: at the cutoff itself, the axial [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md) and [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) are zero. The number above is therefore the lower [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) threshold, not a propagating mode with nonzero longitudinal [energy flux](../../../../../energy-flux.md) exactly at that [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md). A [dielectric](../../../../../dielectric.md) filling would replace $c$ by the wave [speed](../../../../../speed.md) in that medium.
