<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

The [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) is [electromagnetic reciprocity](../../../../../reciprocity-electromagnetism.md) applied to [mutual inductance](../../../../../mutual-inductance.md). For thin closed circuits in [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md), the magnetic [vector](../../../../../vector.md) potential generated by circuit B is

$$
\mathbf A_B(\mathbf r)=\frac{\mu_0I_B}{4\pi}\oint_B\frac{d\boldsymbol\ell_B}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_B|}.
$$

Stokes theorem converts the [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) through circuit A into its magnetic [vector](../../../../../vector.md) potential [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md). Dividing by $I_B$ gives [Neumann's mutual-inductance formula](../../../../../neumann-s-mutual-inductance-formula.md),

$$
M_{AB}=\frac{\Phi_{A\leftarrow B}}{I_B}
=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\oint_A\oint_B
\frac{d\boldsymbol\ell_A\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell_B}{|\mathbf r_A-\mathbf r_B|}.
$$

The [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) and separation are unchanged by interchanging A and B, so

$$
\boxed{M_{AB}=M_{BA}.}
$$

Each has units $\mathrm{Wb\,A^{-1}}=\mathrm H$. The winding orientations must be used consistently to determine the common sign. The same reciprocity holds in ordinary linear reciprocal magnetic media; it is not an unrestricted statement about biased nonreciprocal materials. Equivalently, a reversible [magnetic energy](../../../../../magnetic-energy.md) function has equal mixed [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) with respect to the two currents.
