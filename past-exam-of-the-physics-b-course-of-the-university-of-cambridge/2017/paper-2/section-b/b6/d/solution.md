<h1 id="section-b/b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) is the [electromagnetic energy](../../../../../../electromagnetic-energy.md) flux density,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf S=\mathbf E\times\mathbf H,\qquad [\mathbf S]=\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}.}
$$

In [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) it equals $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/\mu_0$. For a linear nondispersive medium with time-independent constitutive coefficients, [Maxwell's equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) and the identity $\nabla\cdot(\mathbf E\times\mathbf H)=\mathbf H\cdot\nabla\times\mathbf E-\mathbf E\cdot\nabla\times\mathbf H$ give [Poynting's theorem](../../../../../../poynting-theorem.md),

$$
\frac{\partial u_{\rm EM}}{\partial t}+\nabla\cdot\mathbf S=-\mathbf J_f\cdot\mathbf E,
\qquad
u_{\rm EM}=\frac12\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf D+\frac12\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf H.
$$

The source term is [work](../../../../../../work.md) done by the fields on free charges; for an Ohmic conductor it is the positive [local Joule heating rate](../../../../../../local-joule-heating-rate.md) $\sigma E^2$. Integrating over a [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) states that loss of stored field [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) plus outward [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) flow equals [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) transferred to matter. In dispersive media, such as a [plasma](../../../../../../plasma-physics.md), material motion contributes to stored [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and the simple nondispersive expression cannot be used indiscriminately.

For a [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md), $B=E/c$ and $\mathbf E$, $\mathbf H$ and the propagation direction form a right-handed triad. With real peak field $E_0$, the time-averaged [wave intensity](../../../../../../intensity-physics.md) is

$$
\langle S\rangle=\frac{E_0^2}{2\mu_0c}=\frac12\epsilon_0cE_0^2.
$$

More generally, complex peak amplitudes give $\langle\mathbf S\rangle=\tfrac12\operatorname{Re}(\mathbf E_0\times\mathbf H_0^*)$. Along a [transmission line](../../../../../../transmission-line.md), the cross-section [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) of the longitudinal [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) is the transmitted electrical [power](../../../../../../power.md) $VI$. [Energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) flows through the electromagnetic fields in the surrounding [dielectric](../../../../../../dielectric.md), even though the conductors guide the currents.
