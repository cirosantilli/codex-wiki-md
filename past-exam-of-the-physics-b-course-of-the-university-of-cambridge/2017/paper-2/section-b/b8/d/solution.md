<h1 id="section-b/b8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Archimedes' principle](../../../../../../archimedes-principle.md) states that the resultant [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) on a body equals the weight of the [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) displaced, directed opposite to gravity. Let $V_d$ be the displaced [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) region, with outward normal $\widehat{\mathbf n}$ from the body into the [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md). Since the [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) is inward,

$$
\mathbf F_b=-\oint_{\partial V_d}p\widehat{\mathbf n}\,dA
=-\int_{V_d}\nabla p\,dV.
$$

Hydrostatic balance is $\nabla p=\rho_f\mathbf g$, with downward [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md) $\mathbf g$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F_b=-\int_{V_d}\rho_f\mathbf g\,dV
=-\rho_fV_d\mathbf g}
$$

for uniform [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) density and gravity. Uniform atmospheric [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) gives no net [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) on a closed surface. For a floating body, the same displaced-volume construction includes the free surface; any significant air [buoyancy](../../../../../../buoyancy.md) can be included as a second displaced [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md).

A freely floating body of [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) $m$ satisfies $mg=\rho_f gV_d$, so its displaced [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) is $m/\rho_f$. A homogeneous body of density $\rho_b<\rho_f$ floats with submerged fraction $\rho_b/\rho_f$; if $\rho_b>\rho_f$, hydrostatic [buoyancy](../../../../../../buoyancy.md) alone cannot support it. The [buoyant force](../../../../../../buoyancy.md) acts through the [centre of buoyancy](../../../../../../centre-of-buoyancy.md), the [centroid](../../../../../../centroid.md) of the displaced [volume](../../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) for constant density, while weight acts through the [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md). Their relative positions determine rotational equilibrium and stability. For a fully submerged body, a [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) below the [centre of buoyancy](../../../../../../centre-of-buoyancy.md) gives a restoring [torque](../../../../../../torque.md); a floating body's changing displaced shape additionally requires the [metacentric height](../../../../../../metacentric-height.md) criterion. [Surface tension](../../../../../../surface-tension-split.md), externally driven flow and contact [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) can alter the simple [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) balance.
