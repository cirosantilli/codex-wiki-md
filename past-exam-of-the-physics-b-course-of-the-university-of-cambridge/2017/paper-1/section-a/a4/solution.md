<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

First estimate the excess [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) as weight divided by contact area:

$$
\Delta p=\frac{mg}{S}
=\frac{50(9.81)}{10^{-4}}\ \mathrm{Pa}
\simeq4.91\times10^6\ \mathrm{Pa}.
$$

For the equilibrium estimate, use the [Clapeyron equation](../../../../../clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) for [melting](../../../../../melting.md). The [specific volume](../../../../../specific-volume.md) change on melting is $\Delta v=1/\rho_w-1/\rho_i$, and the [latent heat of fusion](../../../../../latent-heat-of-fusion.md) $L$ gives $\Delta s=L/T_m$. Thus the [common-pressure melting-point slope](../../../../../common-pressure-melting-point-slope.md) is

$$
\frac{dT_m}{dp}=\frac{T_m}{L}
\left(\frac1{\rho_w}-\frac1{\rho_i}\right).
$$

Take $T_m\simeq273.15\ \mathrm K$ and the usual rounded [water](../../../../../water.md) density $\rho_w\simeq1000\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, in addition to the supplied $\rho_i=920\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and $L=3.3\times10^5\ \mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}}$. Then

$$
\frac{dT_m}{dp}\simeq-7.20\times10^{-8}\ \mathrm{K\,Pa^{-1}},
\qquad \Delta T_m\simeq-0.353\ \mathrm K,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{T_m\simeq272.80\ \mathrm K\simeq-0.35\,{}^\circ\mathrm C.}
$$

The sign is negative because [ice](../../../../../ice.md) contracts on melting. This is a linear equilibrium estimate treating the load as a common local [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) on coexisting ice and water. The actual contact [stress](../../../../../stress.md) is nonuniform, and frictional heating, deformation and drainage also affect skating; the estimate is not a complete account of the lubricating water film.
