<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At an interface with unit normal $\hat{\mathbf n}$ pointing from medium 1 to medium 2, the magnetic [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) are

$$
\boxed{\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot(\mathbf B_2-\mathbf B_1)=0,\qquad\hat{\mathbf n}\times(\mathbf H_2-\mathbf H_1)=\mathbf K_f.}
$$

The first follows from a pillbox applied to $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0$. The [tangential magnetic field jump from a free surface current](../../../../../../tangential-magnetic-field-jump-from-a-free-surface-current.md) follows from a shrinking rectangular Ampère loop; $\mathbf K_f$ is the free surface current. With no free sheet current, normal $B$ and tangential $H$ are continuous. In linear isotropic media $\mathbf B=\mu\mathbf H$, so neither all components of $B$ nor all components of $H$ generally remain continuous.

For [magnetic field-line refraction at a permeability interface](../../../../../../magnetic-field-line-refraction-at-a-permeability-interface.md), with angles measured from the normal, these conditions give $\tan\theta_2/\tan\theta_1=\mu_2/\mu_1$. They determine the bending of [magnetic flux](../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) at a material interface. In a slender core electromagnet, flux continuity and [Ampère's law](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) lead to the magnetic-circuit estimate

$$
\Phi\simeq\frac{NI}{\sum_j l_j/(\mu_jA_j)}.
$$

An air gap can dominate the reluctance even when the iron core has high [magnetic permeability](../../../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md). [Boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) also determine [demagnetizing fields](../../../../../../demagnetizing-field.md); fringing, [saturation](../../../../../../homogeneity-plus-realization-of-empty-set-types-implies-saturation.md) and [hysteresis](../../../../../../hysteresis.md) limit the simple linear circuit model. A high-[magnetic permeability](../../../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md) material redirects flux, whereas a perfect-conductor boundary is a different physical assumption.[Chapter 7: magnetic fields in matter and interface conditions](../../../../../../electromagnetism-by-david-tong.md)
