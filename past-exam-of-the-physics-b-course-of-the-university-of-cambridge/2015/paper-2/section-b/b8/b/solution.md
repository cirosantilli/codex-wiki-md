<h1 id="section-b/b8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For generalized coordinates $q_i$ with [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) $L(q,\dot q,t)$, the [conjugate momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is

$$
\boxed{p_i=\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot q_i},\qquad\dot p_i=\frac{\partial L}{\partial q_i}.}
$$

A coordinate absent from $L$ is cyclic, and its [conjugate momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) is conserved. More generally, [Noether's theorem](../../../../../../noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md) connects continuous symmetries of the action to conserved quantities: spatial translations give linear momentum and rotations give [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md). If $L$ has no explicit time dependence, the conserved energy function is

$$
E=\sum_i p_i\dot q_i-L,\qquad\frac{dE}{dt}=-\frac{\partial L}{\partial t}=0.
$$

The momentum conjugate to an angle is an [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md), not a linear velocity. [Canonical momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) can also differ from [mechanical momentum](../../../../../../mechanical-momentum.md): for $L=\tfrac12m v^2+q\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf v-q\phi$, **$\mathbf p=m\mathbf v+q\mathbf A$**. Thus a conserved [conjugate momentum](../../../../../../canonical-momentum.md) must be identified from the actual [Lagrangian](../../../../../../lagrangian.md) and its symmetry, rather than assumed to equal $m\mathbf v$. Gauge-dependent canonical momenta require the corresponding gauge choice or gauge-covariant symmetry interpretation.[Chapters 2 and 4: conservation laws, conjugate momentum and the Hamiltonian](../../../../../../classical-dynamics-by-david-tong.md)
