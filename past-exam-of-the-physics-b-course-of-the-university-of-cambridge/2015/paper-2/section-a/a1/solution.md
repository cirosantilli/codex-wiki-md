<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

For [entropy gain from impact and thermal equilibration](../../../../../entropy-gain-from-impact-and-thermal-equilibration.md), let the large lake be a reservoir at an initially unspecified [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_L$, in kelvin, and take the final meteorite [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) to be $T_L$. Its initial [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is $T_i=1273.15\,\mathrm K$. The meteorite loses thermal energy $mc(T_i-T_L)$, and its [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $mv^2/2$ is also dissipated into the lake. Thus

$$
\Delta S_{\rm met}=mc\log(T_L/T_i),\qquad\Delta S_{\rm lake}=\frac{mc(T_i-T_L)+mv^2/2}{T_L},
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\Delta S_{\rm universe}=mc\left[\log\frac{T_L}{T_i}+\frac{T_i}{T_L}-1\right]+\frac{mv^2}{2T_L}.}
$$

Both [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) equilibration and dissipation contribute positively. For the requested estimate, assume a lake [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_L=300\,\mathrm K$. This gives **$\Delta S_{\rm universe}\simeq2.93\times10^3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$**. Taking $273$–$300\,\mathrm K$ instead gives about $3.3$–$2.9\,\mathrm{kJ\,K^{-1}}$; this shows the estimate's sensitivity without asserting an unprovided lake [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). The boxed expression retains the full [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence. The model uses the stated constant [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md), no phase change of the meteorite, and a lake large enough that its [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) change is negligible.[Section 4.3.3: entropy changes and thermal reservoirs](../../../../../classical-thermodynamics-by-david-tong.md)
