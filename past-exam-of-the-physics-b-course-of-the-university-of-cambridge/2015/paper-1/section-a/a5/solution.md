<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

After the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has thermalized, its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E_\gamma=hc/\lambda$ belongs to the macroscopic body's internal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). The body's [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) makes the resulting [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) change negligible. Its equilibrium [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) therefore increases by

$$
\boxed{\Delta S\simeq\frac{hc}{\lambda T}.}
$$

The [Boltzmann entropy](../../../../../boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md) $S=k_B\ln\Omega$ then gives the [microstate multiplier from photon absorption](../../../../../microstate-multiplier-from-photon-absorption.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Omega_f}{\Omega_i}=\exp(\Delta S/k_B)\simeq
\exp\!\left(\frac{hc}{\lambda k_BT}\right).}
$$

For scale, explicitly choosing a representative visible [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda=500\ \mathrm{nm}$ and room [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T=300\ \mathrm K$ gives $\Delta S=1.32\times10^{-21}\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$, or $\Delta S/k_B=95.9$, and **a multiplier approximately $4.5\times10^{41}$**. These are illustrative choices, not additional supplied data: the exact multiplier depends exponentially on the particular visible [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). The exact [Planck constant](../../../../../planck-constant.md), [speed of light](../../../../../speed-of-light.md), and [Boltzmann constant](../../../../../boltzmann-constant.md) fix the conversion.[2022 CODATA adjustment, Planck constant, speed of light in vacuum, and Boltzmann constant entries](../../../../../fundamental-physical-constants-complete-listing-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology-and-others.md)
