<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

For a smooth isolated [conducting sphere](../../../../../conducting-sphere.md), [Gauss's law](../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives the surface [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) $E_s=Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0a^2)$ and [electric potential](../../../../../electric-potential.md) $V=Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0a)=aE_s$. At the specified [dielectric strength](../../../../../dielectric-strength.md), $V_{\max}=3.0\times10^3\ \mathrm V$. The [capacitance of an isolated conducting sphere](../../../../../capacitance-of-an-isolated-conducting-sphere.md) is $C=4\pi\epsilon_0a$, so its [capacitor energy](../../../../../capacitor-energy.md) is

$$
U_{\max}=\frac12 C V_{\max}^2=2\pi\epsilon_0a^3E_s^2
=\boxed{5.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm J\simeq0.50\ \mu\mathrm J.}
$$

This is the ideal [breakdown-limited energy of a conducting sphere](../../../../../breakdown-limited-energy-of-a-conducting-sphere.md), taking air's [permittivity](../../../../../permittivity-split.md) approximately equal to [vacuum permittivity](../../../../../vacuum-permittivity.md) and neglecting nearby objects and surface irregularities. The [vacuum permittivity](../../../../../vacuum-permittivity.md) used numerically is $8.8542\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm{F\,m^{-1}}$.[2022 CODATA adjustment, vacuum electric permittivity entry](../../../../../fundamental-physical-constants-complete-listing-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology-and-others.md)
