<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

The varying [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) through the ring induces an [electromotive force](../../../../../electromotive-force.md) by [Faraday's law of induction](../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md). Its direction follows [Lenz's law](../../../../../lenz-s-law.md). With solenoid current $I_s$, ring current $I_r$, ring [self-inductance](../../../../../self-inductance.md) $L_r$, [electrical resistance](../../../../../electrical-resistance.md) $R$, and [mutual inductance](../../../../../mutual-inductance.md) $M(z)$,

$$
L_r\dot I_r+RI_r=-M\dot I_s,\qquad F_z=I_sI_r\frac{dM}{dz}.
$$

The ring sits in a nonuniform [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md): coupling decreases above the solenoid, so $M>0$ and $M'<0$ with a common orientation for the two windings. For $I_s=I_0\cos\omega t$, the in-phase part of the induced ring current is negative:

$$
\langle I_sI_r\rangle=-\frac{I_0^2\omega^2ML_r}{2(R^2+\omega^2L_r^2)},\qquad
\boxed{\langle F_z\rangle=-\frac{I_0^2\omega^2ML_rM'}{2(R^2+\omega^2L_r^2)}>0.}
$$

Thus [alternating-current induction levitation](../../../../../alternating-current-induction-levitation.md) can balance the ring's weight when $\langle F_z\rangle=mg$. A falling mean lifting [force](../../../../../force-split.md) with height supplies vertical [stability](../../../../../stability-of-a-numerical-method.md). In the strongly inductive limit the two currents are almost opposite: the ring repels the solenoid during both half cycles. Finite [self-inductance](../../../../../self-inductance.md) matters; an ideal purely resistive loop would carry a current exactly in quadrature with $I_s$ and give zero average [force](../../../../../force-split.md) in this model. The source supplies the [Joule heating](../../../../../joule-heating.md) in the real ring, and any mechanical work while it rises.
