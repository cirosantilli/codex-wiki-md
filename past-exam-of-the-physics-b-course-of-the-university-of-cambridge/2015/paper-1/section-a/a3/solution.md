<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

The [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) measures the transport of tangential [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) between adjacent layers. The [kinetic estimates of gas transport coefficients](../../../../../kinetic-estimates-of-gas-transport-coefficients.md) give

$$
\eta\sim\frac13nm\overline v\lambda,\qquad
\lambda\sim\frac1{\sqrt2n\sigma_c},\qquad
\overline v\propto\sqrt{\frac{k_BT}{m}},
$$

where $n$ is number density, $m$ the molecular [mass](../../../../../mass.md), $\lambda$ the [mean free path](../../../../../mean-free-path.md), and $\sigma_c$ a collision [cross section](../../../../../cross-section-physics.md). At fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), raising the [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) increases the number of momentum carriers but shortens their [mean free path](../../../../../mean-free-path.md) by the same factor. Consequently **the [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) of a dilute gas is approximately independent of [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md)**.

For a temperature-independent collision [cross section](../../../../../cross-section-physics.md), the same estimate gives $\eta\propto\sqrt{mk_BT}/\sigma_c$, so **$\eta\propto T^{1/2}$ in the hard-sphere model**. Faster molecules carry [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) across layers more rapidly. Real intermolecular interactions make the effective collision [cross section](../../../../../cross-section-physics.md) temperature-dependent, changing this simple exponent. The pressure-independence also fails when wall collisions, rather than molecule–molecule collisions, set the transport distance; the continuum estimate assumes a small [Knudsen number](../../../../../knudsen-number.md).
