<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

For the [tangent-axis inertia of a spherical shell](../../../../../tangent-axis-inertia-of-a-spherical-shell.md), subtract the missing solid core from a solid sphere:

$$
M=\frac{4\pi\rho}{3}(R_2^3-R_1^3),\qquad
I_c=\frac{8\pi\rho}{15}(R_2^5-R_1^5).
$$

The [parallel axis theorem](../../../../../parallel-axis-theorem.md) shifts the axis a distance $R_2$:

$$
\boxed{I_{\rm tangent}=\frac{8\pi\rho}{15}(R_2^5-R_1^5)
+\frac{4\pi\rho}{3}(R_2^3-R_1^3)R_2^2.}
$$

It tends to $7MR_2^2/5$ for a solid sphere and $5MR_2^2/3$ for a thin [spherical shell](../../../../../spherical-shell.md).
