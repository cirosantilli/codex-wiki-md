<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

Let absorbed solar flux per unit surface area be $F_\odot=\sigma T_0^4$. The [single-layer greenhouse model](../../../../../single-layer-greenhouse-model.md) emits infrared upward and downward. Top-of-atmosphere balance gives $\sigma T_a^4=F_\odot$, hence $T_a=T_0$. Surface balance is $\sigma T_s^4=F_\odot+\sigma T_a^4$, so

$$
\boxed{T_s=2^{1/4}T_0\simeq333\,\mathrm K.}
$$

The layer simultaneously satisfies $\sigma T_s^4=2\sigma T_a^4$. This ideal radiative model excludes convection, spectral windows and atmospheric [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) structure; it is not a quantitative prediction for the real atmosphere.
