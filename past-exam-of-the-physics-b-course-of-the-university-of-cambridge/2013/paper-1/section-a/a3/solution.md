<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

With the standard liquid-water approximation $\rho\simeq10^3\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, each portion has [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $100\,\mathrm{kg}$. The equal [heat capacities](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) give final [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_f=310\,\mathrm K$ by [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation. For an insulated mixing process with no other changes,

$$
\boxed{\Delta S_{\rm universe}=mc\ln\frac{T_f}{280}
+mc\ln\frac{T_f}{340}
=4.20\times10^5\ln\frac{310^2}{280\times340}
\simeq3.95\times10^3\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.}
$$

The reversible integrals $mc\int dT/T$ evaluate [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) differences between [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) states; the actual [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) transfer is irreversible. These are two portions of the same substance, so there is no additional ideal-mixture compositional [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md).
