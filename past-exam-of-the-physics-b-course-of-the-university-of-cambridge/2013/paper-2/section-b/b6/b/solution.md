<h1 id="section-b/b6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An applied [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) displaces [bound charges](../../../../../../bound-charge.md) or reorients permanent dipoles, producing [electric polarization](../../../../../../polarization-density.md) $\mathbf P$, the dipole moment per unit volume. It gives [bound charges](../../../../../../bound-charge.md) $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$ and $\sigma_b=\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf n$. Define

$$
\mathbf D=\epsilon_0\mathbf E+\mathbf P,\qquad
\mathbf P=\epsilon_0\chi_e\mathbf E,\qquad
\epsilon=\epsilon_0(1+\chi_e)
$$

for a **linear isotropic [dielectric](../../../../../../dielectric.md)**. Then $\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\rho_f$ separates free charge from the bound-charge response. Electronic, ionic and orientational [electric polarization](../../../../../../polarization-density.md) respond on different timescales; real [permittivity](../../../../../../permittivity-split.md) is [frequency](../../../../../../frequency.md) dependent, and its dissipative part describes [dielectric loss](../../../../../../dielectric-loss.md).

A uniform [dielectric](../../../../../../dielectric.md) filling a [parallel-plate capacitor](../../../../../../parallel-plate-capacitor.md) raises its [capacitance](../../../../../../capacitance.md) to $\epsilon A/d$. At fixed charge this reduces field [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) $Q^2/(2C)$; at fixed [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) the stored [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) $CV^2/2$ increases because the [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) source supplies charge and [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md). Insulation and [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) storage are useful applications, limited by loss and [dielectric breakdown](../../../../../../dielectric-breakdown.md). Static interface conditions are continuous tangential $\mathbf E$ and a jump in normal $\mathbf D$ equal to free surface charge.
