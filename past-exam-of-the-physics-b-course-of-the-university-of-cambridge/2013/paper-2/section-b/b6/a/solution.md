<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In vacuum, **[displacement current](../../../../../../displacement-current.md) contributes the additional term** $\epsilon_0\partial\mathbf E/\partial t$:

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J+\mu_0\epsilon_0\frac{\partial\mathbf E}{\partial t}.
$$

Taking the divergence and using [Gauss law](../../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) gives $\nabla\cdot\mathbf J+\partial\rho/\partial t=0$, the local conservation of charge. Without [displacement current](../../../../../../displacement-current.md) the curl equation would incorrectly require a divergence-free current even during charge accumulation.

For a charging [capacitor](../../../../../../capacitor.md), a surface cutting the wire carries conduction current while a surface passing through the gap carries changing [electric flux](../../../../../../electric-flux.md). The [displacement current](../../../../../../displacement-current.md) term makes the circulation independent of the spanning surface. It is not transport of free charge across the vacuum gap. In matter the macroscopic equation is $\nabla\times\mathbf H=\mathbf J_f+\partial\mathbf D/\partial t$, with [electric polarization](../../../../../../polarization-density.md) included in $\mathbf D$. Together with [Faraday's law of induction](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md), the vacuum equations give [electromagnetic waves](../../../../../../electromagnetic-wave-split.md) of [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) $1/\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}$.
