<h1 id="section-b/b8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) transfers [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) between adjacent fluid layers. A [Newtonian fluid](../../../../../../newtonian-fluid.md) has [shear stress](../../../../../../shear-stress.md) proportional to [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) rate: for simple shear, $\boxed{\tau_{xy}=\eta\,\partial u_x/\partial y}$. Thus parallel plates of [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) $A$, separation $d$ and relative [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) $U$ require force magnitude $\eta AU/d$ in a steady linear profile.

For an incompressible isotropic [Newtonian fluid](../../../../../../newtonian-fluid.md),

$$
\sigma_{ij}=-p\delta_{ij}+\eta(\partial_i u_j+\partial_j u_i),\qquad
\rho\frac{D\mathbf u}{Dt}=-\nabla p+\eta\nabla^2\mathbf u+\rho\mathbf g
$$

for constant [viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md). With [strain-rate tensor](../../../../../../strain-rate-tensor.md) $e_{ij}$, the [viscous dissipation](../../../../../../viscous-dissipation.md) rate per unit volume is $2\eta e_{ij}e_{ij}\geq0$. [Kinematic viscosity](../../../../../../kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu=\eta/\rho$ is the [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) diffusivity: a disturbance spreads over distance of order $\sqrt{\nu t}$.

The [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md) $\rho UL/\eta$ compares inertial and viscous forces. Small [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md) supports a creeping-flow approximation, while large values permit thin viscous [boundary layers](../../../../../../boundary-layer.md) and possible [turbulence](../../../../../../turbulence-split.md). It does not mean molecular [viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) has disappeared. Gas [viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) results from molecular [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) transport; a liquid's microscopic transport depends strongly on intermolecular interactions. Non-Newtonian materials require a more general constitutive law.
