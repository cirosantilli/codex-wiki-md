<h1 id="section-c/c12/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A source much deeper than the annual penetration depth has $T_S\simeq288.15$ K. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{COP}_{\rm ground}=\frac{293.15}{5}=58.63,\quad
\frac{W_{\rm ground}}{W_{\rm direct}}=\frac5{293.15}=0.0171.}
$$

The ideal coldest-day electrical-input ratios are **direct : air-source : deep-ground = 1 : 0.0853 : 0.0171**. The deep source needs one fifth of the ideal air-source [work](../../../../../../work.md). These ratios compare equal useful heating, and do not include excavation, circulation losses or an assumed real compressor efficiency.
