<h1 id="section-c/c12/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The coldest day has outside [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) $-5^\circ\mathrm C$, so the indoor–outdoor [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference is 25 K. If $K$ is the building's heat-loss coefficient, the required heating rate is $\dot Q_H=25K$. For direct electrical heating,

$$
\boxed{\dot W_{\rm direct}=\dot Q_H.}
$$

This sets the reference electrical cost for the same delivered [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) and tariff.
