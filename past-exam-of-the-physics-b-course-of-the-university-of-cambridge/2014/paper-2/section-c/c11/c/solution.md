<h1 id="section-c/c11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Substitute the [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) differential into the given first law:

$$
dU=T\left(\frac{C_A}{T}dT-\gamma'dA\right)+\gamma dA,
\qquad
\boxed{dU=C_A\,dT+(\gamma-T\gamma')\,dA.}
$$
