<h1 id="section-c/c11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At fixed [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md), $(\partial S/\partial T)_A=C_A/T$. Therefore

$$
dS=\frac{C_A}{T}\,dT-\gamma'\,dA.
$$

For reversible adiabatic stretching, $dS=0$ and

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{dT}{dA}\right)_S=\frac{T\gamma'}{C_A}<0.}
$$

The film **cools**, assuming positive [heat capacity](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md). Adiabaticity alone would not ensure constant [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) if the deformation were irreversible.
