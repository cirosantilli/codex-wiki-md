<h1 id="section-c/c11/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C11](../c11.md)

With $F=U-TS$, the [thermodynamics of a liquid film](../../../../../thermodynamics-of-a-liquid-film.md) gives

$$
\boxed{dF=-S\,dT+\gamma\,dA.}
$$

Equality of mixed derivatives of $F(T,A)$ gives the [Maxwell relation](../../../../../maxwell-relations.md)

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial A}\right)_T
=-\left(\frac{\partial\gamma}{\partial T}\right)_A.}
$$

Use the [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) convention already specified by $\gamma\,dA$; do not insert an additional factor for two film faces.
