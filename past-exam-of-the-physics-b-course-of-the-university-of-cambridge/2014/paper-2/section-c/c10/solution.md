<h1 id="section-c/c10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C10](../c10.md)

A [heat engine](../../../../../heat-engine.md) operates cyclically, absorbing [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) $Q_h$ from a hotter source, rejecting [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) $Q_c$ to a colder sink and delivering net [work](../../../../../work.md) $W=Q_h-Q_c$. Its efficiency is $\eta=W/Q_h$. The working substance returns to its original state; the reservoirs and surroundings need not.
