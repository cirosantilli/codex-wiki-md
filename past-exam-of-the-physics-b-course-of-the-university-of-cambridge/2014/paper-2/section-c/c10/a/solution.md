<h1 id="section-c/c10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Suppose an engine were more efficient than a reversible Carnot engine between the same reservoirs. Couple it to a reversed Carnot engine, scaling the cycles to have equal [work](../../../../../../work.md). With no net external [work](../../../../../../work.md), their combined effect would be to transfer [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) from the colder reservoir to the hotter one, contradicting the [Second law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md). **No such engine can exceed $\eta_C=1-T_c/T_h$.** Equivalently, the cyclic [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) inequality gives $-Q_h/T_h+Q_c/T_c\ge0$, hence the same bound.
