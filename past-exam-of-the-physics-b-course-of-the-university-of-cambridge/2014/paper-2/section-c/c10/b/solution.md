<h1 id="section-c/c10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The reversible working substance changes [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) by $Q_h/T_h$ on its hot isotherm and $-Q_c/T_c$ on its cold isotherm, with zero change on the two reversible adiabats. Closure requires $Q_h/T_h=Q_c/T_c$. The substance has zero [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) change over a cycle, while the reservoirs have changes $-Q_h/T_h$ and $Q_c/T_c$. Thus **$\Delta S_{\rm universe}=0$** for the complete [Carnot cycle](../../../../../../carnot-cycle.md).
