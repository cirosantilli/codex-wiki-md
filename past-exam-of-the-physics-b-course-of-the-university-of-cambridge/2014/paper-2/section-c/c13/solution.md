↑ **Parent:** [C13](../c13.md)

**Molecules.** In a dilute classical gas, collisions establish a [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md). The normalized [speed](../../../../../speed.md) distribution and its first two moments are

$$
f(v)=4\pi\left(\frac{m}{2\pi k_BT}\right)^{3/2}v^2e^{-mv^2/(2k_BT)},
\quad \overline v=\sqrt{\frac{8k_BT}{\pi m}},\quad
\overline{v^2}=\frac{3k_BT}{m}.
$$

[Momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) transfer to a wall gives $p=nm\overline{v^2}/3=nk_BT$. Translational [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) is $U=3Nk_BT/2$; rotational and vibrational degrees of freedom add [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) when thermally populated, without changing the dilute ideal-gas [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) law. Classical [equipartition](../../../../../equipartition-theorem.md) gives $k_BT/2$ per quadratic contribution, but quantum level spacing can freeze out modes.

Only the molecules moving towards a surface contribute to its one-sided number flux:

$$
J=\frac14n\overline v=\frac{p}{\sqrt{2\pi mk_BT}}.
$$

For an [effusive molecular energy flux](../../../../../effusive-molecular-energy-flux.md), faster molecules are preferentially sampled, giving mean translational [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $2k_BT$ per crossing molecule and $F_E=2k_BTJ$. This differs from multiplying $J$ by the bulk mean $3k_BT/2$. Collisions and finite molecular interactions produce transport and departures from the ideal-gas [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md).

**[Photons](../../../../../photon.md).** All [photons](../../../../../photon.md) travel at $c$ in vacuum, so their thermal distribution is over [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), not over a range of speeds. The two polarizations and Bose-Einstein occupation with zero [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) give

$$
u_\nu\,d\nu=\frac{8\pi h\nu^3}{c^3}
\frac{d\nu}{e^{h\nu/(k_BT)}-1},\qquad
u\equiv\int_0^\infty u_\nu\,d\nu=a_RT^4,
\quad a_R=\frac{8\pi^5k_B^4}{15h^3c^3}.
$$

Here the spectral quantity is $u_\nu$ and the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) is $u$. [Momentum flux](../../../../../momentum-flux.md) gives $p=u/3$, while isotropic angular averaging gives the one-sided [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) flux $F_E=cu/4=\sigma_{\rm SB}T^4$ through a small aperture of a black-body cavity. The [photon](../../../../../photon.md) number flux is similarly $cn_\gamma/4$.

**Both pressures arise from [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) transfer, but their [equations of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md) differ:** a classical molecular gas has $pV=Nk_BT$ with fixed molecule number, whereas a [photon gas](../../../../../photon-gas.md) has $pV=U/3$, $U=a_RVT^4$ and an equilibrium [photon](../../../../../photon.md) number that changes with [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). [Photon](../../../../../photon.md) creation and absorption by the walls are essential to the zero-chemical-potential equilibrium description.
