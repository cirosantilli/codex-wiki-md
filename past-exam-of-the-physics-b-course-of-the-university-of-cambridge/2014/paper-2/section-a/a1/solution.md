<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

Treat the vapour as an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), neglect the liquid's [molar volume](../../../../../molar-volume.md), and take the molar [latent heat](../../../../../latent-heat.md) as constant across this small interval. Integrating the [Clausius-Clapeyron equation](../../../../../clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) gives

$$
\ln\frac{p_2}{p_1}=\frac{L_m}{R}\left(\frac1{T_1}-\frac1{T_2}\right),
\qquad
\boxed{L_m=\frac{R\ln(1.1)}{1/230.7-1/233.4}
\simeq15.8\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.}
$$

Only the [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) ratio enters, so conversion from atmospheres to pascals is unnecessary.
