<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

The [Hohmann transfer orbit](../../../../../hohmann-transfer-orbit.md) has [periapsis](../../../../../periapsis.md) $r$ and [apoapsis](../../../../../apoapsis.md) $2r$, so its [semi-major axis](../../../../../semi-major-axis.md) is $3r/2$. [Kepler's third law](../../../../../kepler-s-third-law.md) gives a period $(3/2)^{3/2}$ times the original [orbital period](../../../../../orbital-period.md). Only half the [ellipse](../../../../../ellipse.md) is traversed:

$$
\boxed{t=\frac{T_0}{2}\left(\frac32\right)^{3/2}
\simeq22.0\,\mathrm h,\qquad T_0\simeq24\,\mathrm h.}
$$

The relevant radii are measured from [Earth](../../../../../earth.md)'s centre, not its surface.
