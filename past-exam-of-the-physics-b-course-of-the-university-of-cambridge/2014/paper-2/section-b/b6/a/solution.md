<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Biot-Savart law](../../../../../../biot-savart-law.md) gives the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) of a steady thin-wire [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) in vacuum:

$$
\mathbf B(\mathbf r)=\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi}
\int\frac{d\boldsymbol\ell'\times(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')}
{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|^3}.
$$

The cross product fixes the circulation direction by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md); fields from different [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) elements superpose. Integration gives $B=\mu_0I/(2\pi r)$ outside a straight infinite wire and $B=\mu_0I/(2a)$ at the centre of a circular loop of radius $a$. A distributed [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) replaces $I\,d\boldsymbol\ell'$ by $\mathbf J\,dV'$. **This is a magnetostatic law:** arbitrary time-dependent [electric currents](../../../../../../electric-current.md) require retarded electromagnetic fields, not instantaneous substitution.
