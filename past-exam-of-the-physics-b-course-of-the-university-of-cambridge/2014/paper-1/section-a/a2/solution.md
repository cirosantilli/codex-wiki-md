<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

The [Maxwell speed distribution](../../../../../maxwell-speed-distribution.md) is proportional to $v^2e^{-mv^2/(2k_BT)}$. Setting its logarithmic derivative to zero gives the most probable [speed](../../../../../speed.md):

$$
\boxed{v_{\rm mp}=\sqrt{\frac{2k_BT}{m}}=\sqrt{\frac{2RT}{M}}
\simeq3.95\times10^2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

Use molecular [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md), with $M\simeq0.032\,\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}$, and $T=300\,\mathrm K$.
