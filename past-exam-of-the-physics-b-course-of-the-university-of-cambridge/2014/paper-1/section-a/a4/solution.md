<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

For isotropic planar motion, the [planar effusion flux](../../../../../planar-effusion-flux.md) gives an escape rate through a gap of width $w$ is $\Gamma=w(N/\mathcal A)\langle v\rangle/\pi$, where $\mathcal A$ is tray [area](../../../../../surface-area.md). At fixed equilibrium [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), or fixed shape of the scaled [speed distribution](../../../../../speed-distribution.md), $v\propto m^{-1/2}$. Hence **halving the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) increases the rate by $\sqrt2$**. This planar [effusion](../../../../../effusion.md) result assumes the same spatial [density](../../../../../density.md) and an unrestricted small opening. Strictly, average [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) alone fixes $\langle v^2\rangle$, not $\langle v\rangle$; an arbitrary change of distribution shape would need more information.
