<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

For steady thin beams a distance $d$ apart, let $\lambda_1,\lambda_2$ be their laboratory charge per [length](../../../../../length.md). Their current magnitudes are $|\lambda_j|v$, giving [force](../../../../../force-split.md) magnitudes per [length](../../../../../length.md)

$$
f_E=\frac{|\lambda_1\lambda_2|}{2\pi\epsilon_0d},\qquad
f_B=\frac{\mu_0|\lambda_1\lambda_2|v^2}{2\pi d},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{f_E}{f_B}=\frac{c^2}{v^2}.}
$$

The [electric force](../../../../../electric-force.md) is repulsive and the magnetic [force](../../../../../force-split.md) attractive for the parallel electron [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md). The net [force](../../../../../force-split.md) remains repulsive for $v<c$.
