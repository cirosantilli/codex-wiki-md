<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

For [rolling without slipping](../../../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) through height $h$, [conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) gives

$$
mgh=\tfrac12mv^2+\tfrac12I(v/R)^2,\qquad
\boxed{v^2=\frac{2gh}{1+I/(mR^2)}.}
$$

A uniform solid sphere has $I/(mR^2)=2/5$, a solid cylinder $1/2$, and a pipe $(1+r_{\rm in}^2/R^2)/2>1/2$. Thus **the ball is fastest, then the solid cylinder, then the pipe**. A smaller [moment of inertia](../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) leaves more [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) in translation. This assumes release from rest through the same height, adequate [static friction](../../../../../static-friction.md) and negligible rolling resistance; a thin pipe has $v^2=gh$.
