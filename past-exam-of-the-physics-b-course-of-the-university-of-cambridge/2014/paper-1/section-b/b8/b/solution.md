<h1 id="section-b/b8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At a perfect conductor, the reflected [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) cancels the incident tangential field, while their [magnetic fields](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) add. Thus $E_y(0)=0$ and $H_z(0)$ has peak amplitude $2A/Z_v$. Taking the zero-penetration limit, including its surface [force](../../../../../../force-split.md), gives

$$
\boxed{P_{\rm refl}=\epsilon_0A^2=\frac{2\overline S_{\rm inc}}c.}
$$

Reversal of the normal momentum doubles the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) compared with absorption at the same incident Poynting flux.
