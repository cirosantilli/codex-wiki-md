<h1 id="section-b/b8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B8](../b8.md)

The macroscopic [Maxwell equations](../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md), with free charge and current, are

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\rho_f,\quad \nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0,\quad
\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t\mathbf B,\quad
\nabla\times\mathbf H=\mathbf J_f+\partial_t\mathbf D.
$$

Inside this body $\mathbf D=\epsilon_0\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf H$. The wave [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) lies along $z$; its sign follows the local wave component. Faraday's and Ampère's laws give

$$
\partial_xE_y=-\mu_0\partial_tH_z,\qquad
\boxed{\mathbf J=(0,-\partial_xH_z-\epsilon_0\partial_tE_y,0).}
$$

Static background fields are excluded from this wave solution. The magnetic [force](../../../../../force-split.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) is along $x$, with $f_x=\mu_0J_yH_z$. For a steady periodic state, $\overline{H_z\partial_tE_y}=-\overline{E_y\partial_tH_z}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\overline f_x=-\partial_x\left(\tfrac12\mu_0\overline{H_z^2}+\tfrac12\epsilon_0\overline{E_y^2}\right).}
$$

The result gives [radiation-force density without Ohm's law](../../../../../radiation-force-density-without-ohm-s-law.md). Integrating through the body gives its [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) as the boundary value of the averaged [electromagnetic energy](../../../../../electromagnetic-energy.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) minus its value deep inside. In both limiting cases below the latter is zero. Take $A$ as the incident [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md)'s peak amplitude and write $Z_v=\sqrt{\mu_0/\epsilon_0}$.
