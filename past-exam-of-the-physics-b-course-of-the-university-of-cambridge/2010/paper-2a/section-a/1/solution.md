↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a reversible [isothermal expansion](../../../../../isothermal-expansion.md) of one mole of an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md), [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) depends only on [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), so $\Delta U=0$. The [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) gives

$$
Q=W=\int_{V_i}^{2V_i}\frac{RT_0}{V}\,dV=\boxed{RT_0\ln2.}
$$

This uses quasistatic expansion against the gas pressure. Specifying only the initial and final volumes and constant [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) would not fix heat for an arbitrary irreversible process: an insulated ideal-gas free expansion has $Q=W=0$.
