↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

An empty perfectly conducting rectangular [electromagnetic waveguide](../../../../../electromagnetic-waveguide.md) has

$$
\omega^2=c^2\left[k_z^2+\pi^2\left(\frac{m^2}{a^2}+\frac{n^2}{b^2}\right)\right].
$$

The lowest [transverse electric mode](../../../../../transverse-electric-mode.md) has indices $(1,0)$ or $(0,1)$. A simply connected hollow metallic guide has no [transverse electromagnetic mode](../../../../../transverse-electromagnetic-mode.md). With $a=b=10^{-2}\,\mathrm m$, the two lowest modes are degenerate and their [cutoff frequency](../../../../../cutoff-frequency.md) is

$$
\boxed{f_c=\frac{c}{2a}\simeq15.0\,\mathrm{GHz}.}
$$

Propagation with nonzero axial energy transport requires $f>f_c$; the equality is the lower limiting frequency, with zero axial [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md). A filling medium would replace $c$ by $1/\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$.
