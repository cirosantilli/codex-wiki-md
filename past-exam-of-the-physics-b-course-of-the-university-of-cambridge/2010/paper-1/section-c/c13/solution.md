<h1 id="section-c/c13/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C13](../c13.md)

The [zeroth law of thermodynamics](../../../../../zeroth-law-of-thermodynamics.md) states the transitivity of [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md): if two systems each equilibrate thermally with a third, they equilibrate with one another. A reproducible thermometric property can therefore label their [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). Its empirical numerical scale is not itself the complete thermodynamic content; reversible heat-engine relations establish an absolute [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) scale.

The [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) expresses [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation. For a [closed system](../../../../../closed-system.md), $dU=\delta Q-\delta W$ when $\delta W$ is work done by the system. [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) is a state function, while [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) and work depend on the path. In reversible simple compressible motion, $\delta W=p\,dV$ and $dU=T\,dS-p\,dV$; an open system additionally needs the energies carried by transferred matter. An [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) can expand freely with $Q=W=0$ and unchanged [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md). The first law alone does not decide whether the reverse spontaneous process occurs.

The [Second law of thermodynamics](../../../../../second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) introduces [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) and restricts direction. For a reversible path, $dS=\delta Q_{\rm rev}/T$; for an irreversible process the [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) balance includes nonnegative production. The [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) of an isolated system cannot decrease. In a cycle, the [Clausius inequality](../../../../../clausius-inequality.md) is $\oint\delta Q/T\leq0$, using the appropriate boundary/reservoir [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md). The Kelvin–Planck statement forbids a cyclic device whose sole effect is extracting [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) from one reservoir and turning all of it into work; the Clausius statement forbids moving [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) from cold to hot with no compensating effect. A [refrigerator](../../../../../refrigerator.md) is allowed because it consumes work. A two-reservoir engine has $\eta\leq1-T_c/T_h$, with equality in the reversible limit. Work may be fully dissipated as [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) without contradicting this one-way restriction.

The [third law of thermodynamics](../../../../../third-law-of-thermodynamics.md) constrains low-temperature [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md). An equilibrium perfect crystal with a nondegenerate [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) approaches zero [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) as $T\to0$, fixing the conventional absolute [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) reference. Degenerate [ground states](../../../../../ground-state.md) or frozen nonequilibrium disorder require care when discussing [residual entropy](../../../../../residual-entropy.md). Under regular equilibrium conditions the [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) must decrease sufficiently for $\int C/T\,dT$ to remain finite; a constant positive classical [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) cannot be extrapolated to zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). The unattainability formulation states that absolute zero cannot be reached by a finite sequence of admissible cooling operations. These statements concern the limiting thermodynamic regime, rather than predicting that every substance stays in one classical phase during cooling.

The laws work together: conservation sets a device's [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) budget, [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) production restricts the attainable budget, and constitutive equations give the properties of its working material. For fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [volume](../../../../../volume.md), [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) decreases toward equilibrium; at fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) the corresponding potential is [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md). Stable equilibrium, [phase coexistence](../../../../../phase-coexistence.md) and [Maxwell relations](../../../../../maxwell-relations.md) follow from these potentials with appropriate constraints. Calorimetry, mechanical-work measurements, thermometry and reversible-path comparisons test different parts of this framework. None of the laws by itself supplies a particular gas [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md) or microscopic [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md).
