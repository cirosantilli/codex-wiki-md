<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Let $Q_c$ be [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) extracted from the cold reservoir, $Q_h$ the [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) delivered to the surroundings and $W$ the supplied work. The [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) inequality for a cyclic [refrigerator](../../../../../refrigerator.md) is

$$
\frac{Q_h}{T_h}-\frac{Q_c}{T_c}\geq0.
$$

Equality is the reversible [Carnot refrigerator](../../../../../carnot-refrigerator.md) limit. Therefore

$$
\boxed{Q_{h,\min}=\frac{300}{4}\times1\,\mathrm J=75\,\mathrm J.}
$$

[energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation gives $W_{\min}=Q_h-Q_c=74\,\mathrm J$. The requested rejected [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) is **75 J**, while **74 J** is the minimum work; both have to be distinguished. Any [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) production increases the required rejection at these fixed reservoir [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md).
