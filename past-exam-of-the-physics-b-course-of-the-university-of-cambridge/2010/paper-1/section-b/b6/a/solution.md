<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[displacement current](../../../../../../displacement-current.md) completes Ampère's law:

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf H=\mathbf J_{\rm free}+\frac{\partial\mathbf D}{\partial t},\qquad
I_d=\frac{d}{dt}\int_S\mathbf D\cdot d\mathbf A.
$$

In [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) $\mathbf D=\epsilon_0\mathbf E$. Taking the [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) and using $\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\rho_{\rm free}$ gives $\nabla\cdot\mathbf J_{\rm free}+\partial_t\rho_{\rm free}=0$, exactly the [continuity equation](../../../../../../continuity-equation.md). Without the added term a time-dependent charge distribution would contradict the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) equation.

A charging parallel-plate capacitor illustrates the physics. For plate [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) $A$, $Q=\epsilon_0AE$, so the gap [displacement current](../../../../../../displacement-current.md) is $I_d=\epsilon_0A\dot E=\dot Q$, equal to the conduction current in its leads. An Ampère contour therefore gives the same magnetic [circulation](../../../../../../circulation-physics.md) whether its spanning surface crosses the wire or the gap. No electrons have to cross an ideal [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) gap. In matter, $\partial_t\mathbf D$ includes the polarization response as well as the [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) term. Combined with Faraday induction, the [displacement](../../../../../../displacement.md) term permits source-free electromagnetic waves.
