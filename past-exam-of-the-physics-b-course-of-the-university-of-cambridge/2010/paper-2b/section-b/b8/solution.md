The [parallel-plate transmission line](../../../../../parallel-plate-transmission-line.md) has per-length $C=\epsilon b/a$ and $L=\mu a/b$. The lossless [telegrapher's equations](../../../../../telegrapher-s-equations.md) imply wave speed $1/\sqrt{LC}=1/\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$, independent of the much slower [electron drift velocity](../../../../../electron-drift-velocity.md).
