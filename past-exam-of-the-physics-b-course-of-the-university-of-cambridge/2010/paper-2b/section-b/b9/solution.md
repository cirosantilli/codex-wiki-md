↑ **Parent:** [B9](../b9.md)

The [image charge for a grounded conducting sphere](../../../../../image-charge-for-a-grounded-conducting-sphere.md) is $-qa/d$ at radius $a^2/d$. With symmetric real charges, their exact force-balance equation has a solution close to $d/a=8$. Holding potential $V$ adds force $qaV/d^2$ to the grounded result, approximately $qV/(64a)$ at that separation.

Full derivation: [solution](../../../paper-2a/section-b/b9/solution.md).
