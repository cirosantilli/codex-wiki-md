↑ **Parent:** [C13](../c13.md)

Stable circular power-law orbits require $nB>0$ and $n>-2$, excluding $n=0$. [Bertrand's theorem](../../../../../bertrand-s-theorem.md) selects $n=-1,2$ when every bound orbit must close. For $n=2+\epsilon$, the near-circular ellipse orientation precesses at $-\epsilon\Omega/8$ to first order.
