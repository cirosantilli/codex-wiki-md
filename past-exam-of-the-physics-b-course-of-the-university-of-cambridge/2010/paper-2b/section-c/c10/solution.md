↑ **Parent:** [C10](../c10.md)

[Bernoulli's theorem](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) and [conservation of mass](../../../../../mass-conservation.md) give $t=r_1^2/(2r)$. Ignoring weight, the stated stagnant-rear-pressure model gives zero resultant at $r_2=\sqrt2r_1$. Including the leading film-weight estimate gives a cancellation radius near $2gr_1^2/v^2$. The printed inequality does not establish negligible force at every larger radius; the shared derivation explains this source qualification.
