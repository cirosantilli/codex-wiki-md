The [torsional wave equation in a circular shaft](../../../../../torsional-wave-equation-in-a-circular-shaft.md) has speed $\sqrt{G/\rho}$. The given shaft has torque $100\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, twist $9.95\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$, strain energy $0.497\,\mathrm J$ and kinetic energy $181\,\mathrm J$. The respective transit-time estimates are $786\,\mathrm W$ and $286\,\mathrm{kW}$; these must not be confused with independent bounds on the actual $30\,\mathrm{kW}$ stress-work flux.
