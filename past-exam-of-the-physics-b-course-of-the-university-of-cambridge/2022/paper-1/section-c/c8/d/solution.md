<h1 id="section-c/c8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) along $+z$, and use [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md). A [uniform current in a straight homogeneous wire](../../../../../../uniform-current-in-a-straight-homogeneous-wire.md) has

$$
\mathbf J=\frac I{\pi a^2}\widehat{\mathbf z}.
$$

The local [Ohm's law](../../../../../../ohm-s-law.md) $\mathbf J=\sigma\mathbf E$ gives the longitudinal [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md)

$$
\mathbf E=\frac I{\sigma\pi a^2}\widehat{\mathbf z}.
$$

For a circular [Amperian loop](../../../../../../amperian-loop.md) of radius $r\le a$, the enclosed [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) is $I_{\rm enc}=Ir^2/a^2$. The [magnetostatic Ampère law](../../../../../../magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) gives $2\pi rH_\phi=I_{\rm enc}$, hence

$$
\mathbf H=\frac{Ir}{2\pi a^2}\widehat{\boldsymbol\phi}.
$$

Since $\widehat{\mathbf z}\times\widehat{\boldsymbol\phi}=-\widehat{\mathbf r}$, the [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf N(r)=-\frac{I^2r}{2\sigma\pi^2a^4}\widehat{\mathbf r},\qquad0\le r\le a.}
$$

It is zero at the centre and points radially inward elsewhere. Its units are $\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$. These are the interior fields in the long-wire approximation, neglecting end and contact effects.
