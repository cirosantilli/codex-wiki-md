<h1 id="section-c/c8/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [Ohmic heating](../../../../../../joule-heating.md) per unit volume is $\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf J=J^2/\sigma$, uniform in this wire. Multiplying by its volume gives the dissipated [power](../../../../../../power.md)

$$
\boxed{P_{\rm loss}=\frac1\sigma\left(\frac I{\pi a^2}\right)^2\pi a^2L
=\frac{I^2L}{\sigma\pi a^2}=I^2R_{\rm wire},}
$$

where the [electrical resistance](../../../../../../electrical-resistance.md) is $R_{\rm wire}=L/(\sigma\pi a^2)$. The result is in watts; over a time interval $\Delta t$ the dissipated [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is $P_{\rm loss}\Delta t$.
