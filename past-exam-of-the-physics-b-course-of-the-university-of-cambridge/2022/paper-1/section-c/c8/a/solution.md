<h1 id="section-c/c8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) is the local [electromagnetic energy](../../../../../../electromagnetic-energy.md) flux density:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf N=\mathbf E\times\mathbf H.}
$$

Here $\mathbf E$ is the [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) and $\mathbf H$ the [magnetic field intensity](../../../../../../magnetic-field-intensity.md). For an oriented area element $d\mathbf S$, $\mathbf N\cdot d\mathbf S$ is the electromagnetic [power](../../../../../../power.md) crossing that element in its positive normal direction. Thus $\int_S\mathbf N\cdot d\mathbf S$ is outward [power](../../../../../../power.md) through a closed [surface](../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md), in watts. The [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) has units $\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$, and its direction describes local [electromagnetic energy](../../../../../../electromagnetic-energy.md) transport.
