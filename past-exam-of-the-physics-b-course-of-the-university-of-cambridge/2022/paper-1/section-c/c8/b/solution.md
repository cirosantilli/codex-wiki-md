<h1 id="section-c/c8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The unit [relative permittivity](../../../../../../relative-permittivity.md) and vacuum-valued [magnetic permeability](../../../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md) imply $\mathbf D=\epsilon_0\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf H$. The [electromagnetic energy](../../../../../../electromagnetic-energy.md) per unit volume is

$$
\boxed{U=\frac12\epsilon_0\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf E+\frac12\mu_0\mathbf H\cdot\mathbf H,\qquad[U]=\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}.}
$$

The two contributions are the stored electric and magnetic [energies](../../../../../../energy-split.md). Although called $U$ in this paper, this quantity is an [energy density](../../../../../../energy-density.md); the total [electromagnetic energy](../../../../../../electromagnetic-energy.md) is $\int_VU\,dV$.
