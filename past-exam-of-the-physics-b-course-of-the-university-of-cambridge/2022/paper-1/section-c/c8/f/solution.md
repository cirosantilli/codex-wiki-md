<h1 id="section-c/c8/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

On the curved wire [surface](../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md), the outward normal is $\widehat{\mathbf r}$ and the [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) has the constant radial value $N_r(a)=-I^2/(2\sigma\pi^2a^3)$. Its outward [flux](../../../../../../flux.md) is

$$
\Phi_N=\int_{\rm curved}\mathbf N\cdot d\mathbf S
=N_r(a)(2\pi aL)
=\boxed{-\frac{I^2L}{\sigma\pi a^2}=-P_{\rm loss}.}
$$

The [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) is radial, so it has zero normal component on the two end faces in this approximation. This is therefore also the outward [flux](../../../../../../flux.md) through the complete wire [surface](../../../../../../topological-surface-split.md). Equivalently, positive electromagnetic [power](../../../../../../power.md) $P_{\rm loss}$ flows inward.

The fields are steady, so the stored [electromagnetic energy](../../../../../../electromagnetic-energy.md) does not change. [Poynting's theorem](../../../../../../poynting-theorem.md) consequently requires the incoming [power](../../../../../../power.md) to equal the [Ohmic heating](../../../../../../joule-heating.md) in part (e), exactly as found. The energy reaches the wire through the surrounding [electromagnetic field](../../../../../../electromagnetic-field.md). A local check is $\nabla\cdot\mathbf N=r^{-1}\partial_r(rN_r)=-I^2/(\sigma\pi^2a^4)=-\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf J$.
