<h1 id="section-c/c9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the closed untapered torus, apply the [magnetostatic Ampère law](../../../../../../magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) to a circular path through the core. It links all $N$ turns, each carrying [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) $I$:

$$
\oint\mathbf H\cdot d\mathbf l=NI.
$$

Since $L\gg R$, variation of the path length and of the tangential [magnetic field intensity](../../../../../../magnetic-field-intensity.md) across the section is negligible. Thus $H_{\rm in}L\simeq NI$, giving, within this thin-torus approximation,

$$
\boxed{H_{\rm in}=\frac{NI}{L}.}
$$

The units are $\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$. The core [magnetic flux density](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is $B_{\rm in}=\mu_0\mu NI/L$ in the linear material model.
