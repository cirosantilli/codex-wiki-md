<h1 id="section-c/c9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With uniform section and negligible [magnetic fringing](../../../../../../magnetic-fringing.md), constant [magnetic flux](../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) gives equal [magnetic flux densities](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) in the core and gap:

$$
\mu_0\mu H_{\rm in}=\mu_0H_{\rm gap},\qquad H_{\rm in}=H_{\rm gap}/\mu.
$$

Here $L$ is the core arc length and $h$ the additional gap length. Applying the [magnetostatic Ampère law](../../../../../../magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) around the complete [magnetic circuit](../../../../../../magnetic-circuit.md),

$$
H_{\rm in}L+H_{\rm gap}h=NI,
\qquad
H_{\rm gap}\left(\frac L\mu+h\right)=NI.
$$

Consequently,

$$
\boxed{H_{\rm gap}=\frac{\mu NI}{\mu h+L}.}
$$

This tends to $NI/h$ when the gap dominates the [magnetic reluctance](../../../../../../magnetic-reluctance.md), $\mu h\gg L$, and reduces to the no-gap result as $h\to0$. The gap [magnetic flux density](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is $B_{\rm gap}=\mu_0H_{\rm gap}$.
