<h1 id="section-c/c9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let the unit normal $\mathbf n$ point from material 1 to material 2, and let their relative [magnetic permeabilities](../../../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md) be $\mu_1,\mu_2$, so $\mathbf B_i=\mu_0\mu_i\mathbf H_i$ for linear isotropic media. Apply [Gauss's law for magnetism](../../../../../../gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) to an infinitesimal [Gaussian pillbox](../../../../../../gaussian-pillbox.md) straddling the interface. The side contribution vanishes as its height tends to zero, leaving

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf B_2-\mathbf B_1)=0.}
$$

The normal [magnetic flux density](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is continuous.

Next use a narrow rectangular [Amperian loop](../../../../../../amperian-loop.md) crossing the interface. The two short-side contributions vanish in the zero-height limit. The [magnetostatic Ampère law](../../../../../../magnetostatic-ampere-law.md) for [magnetic field intensity](../../../../../../magnetic-field-intensity.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n\times(\mathbf H_2-\mathbf H_1)=\mathbf K_f,}
$$

where $\mathbf K_f$ is any free [surface current density](../../../../../../surface-current-density.md). Thus, if there is no free [surface current density](../../../../../../surface-current-density.md) on the interface, the tangential [magnetic field intensity](../../../../../../magnetic-field-intensity.md) is continuous. In that case the component relations are

$$
\boxed{B_{1n}=B_{2n},\qquad H_{1t}=H_{2t},\qquad
\mu_1H_{1n}=\mu_2H_{2n},\qquad
\frac{B_{1t}}{\mu_1}=\frac{B_{2t}}{\mu_2}.}
$$

The distinction between free and bound [electric currents](../../../../../../electric-current.md) matters: bound currents due to [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md) are already incorporated in the definition of [magnetic field intensity](../../../../../../magnetic-field-intensity.md). A change of [magnetic permeability](../../../../../../permeability-electromagnetism.md) alone does not make the tangential [magnetic flux density](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) continuous.
