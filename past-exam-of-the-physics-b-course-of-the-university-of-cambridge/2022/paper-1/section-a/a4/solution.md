<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

The quoted [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md) is that of the filled [parallel-plate capacitor](../../../../../parallel-plate-capacitor.md), $C_i=1.0\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm F$. Disconnecting the source imposes [charge conservation](../../../../../charge-conservation.md) on the isolated plates:

$$
Q=C_iV_i=(1.0\times10^{-6})(2500)=2.50\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm C.
$$

On removing the [dielectric](../../../../../dielectric.md), the [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md) falls by its [relative permittivity](../../../../../relative-permittivity.md), so $C_f=C_i/5=2.0\times10^{-7}\ \mathrm F$. The [capacitor energy](../../../../../capacitor-energy.md) at fixed [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) is $U=Q^2/(2C)$. Therefore

$$
U_i=\frac12C_iV_i^2=3.125\ \mathrm J,\qquad
U_f=\frac{Q^2}{2C_f}=5U_i=15.625\ \mathrm J,
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\Delta U=U_f-U_i=+12.5\ \mathrm J.}
$$

The increase is supplied by the external [work](../../../../../work.md) needed to withdraw the [dielectric](../../../../../dielectric.md) against its attraction into the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md), in the lossless quasistatic limit. The final [electric potential difference](../../../../../electric-potential-difference.md) is $Q/C_f=12500\ \mathrm V$, not the original charging value.
