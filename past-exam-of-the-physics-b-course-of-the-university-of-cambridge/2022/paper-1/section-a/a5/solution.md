<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

For the propagating mode of an air-filled or evacuated [rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](../../../../../rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md), the longitudinal [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) is

$$
\omega^2=c^2k_z^2+\omega_c^2.
$$

The [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) and [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) along the guide are consequently

$$
v_p=\frac{\omega}{k_z}=\frac{c}{\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}},\qquad
v_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk_z}=c\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}.
$$

With [waveguide cutoff frequency](../../../../../waveguide-cutoff-frequency.md) $f_c=10\ \mathrm{GHz}$ and carrier [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) $f=30\ \mathrm{GHz}$, $\sqrt{1-(f_c/f)^2}=\sqrt8/3$. Thus

$$
\boxed{v_p=\frac{3c}{\sqrt8}=3.18\times10^8\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},\qquad
v_g=\frac{\sqrt8\,c}{3}=2.83\times10^8\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The pulse envelope travels at the [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) for a sufficiently narrow range of [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md). The [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md) exceeding the [speed of light](../../../../../speed-of-light.md) does not describe superluminal signal transport. The check $v_pv_g=c^2$ follows directly from the [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md). A different uniform filling medium would require replacing $c$ by its electromagnetic propagation speed; the stated numerical answer uses the usual empty-guide assumption.
