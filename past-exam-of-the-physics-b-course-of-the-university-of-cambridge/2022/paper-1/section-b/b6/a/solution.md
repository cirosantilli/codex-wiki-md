<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use orthonormal basis [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf e_i(t)$ fixed in the [rotating reference frame](../../../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md), and expand the [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) as $\mathbf J=\sum_iJ_i\mathbf e_i$. Over time $dt$, the basis undergoes infinitesimal [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) $\boldsymbol\Omega\,dt$, so

$$
\left(\frac{d\mathbf e_i}{dt}\right)_{S_0}=\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf e_i.
$$

The [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) gives the [rotating-frame derivative formula](../../../../../../rotating-frame-derivative-formula.md):

$$
\left(\frac{d\mathbf J}{dt}\right)_{S_0}
=\sum_i\dot J_i\mathbf e_i+\sum_iJ_i\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf e_i
=\left(\frac{d\mathbf J}{dt}\right)_S+\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf J.
$$

Since the applied [couple](../../../../../../couple-mechanics.md) is the rate of change of [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) in the [inertial frame](../../../../../../inertial-frame.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbf G=\left(\frac{d\mathbf J}{dt}\right)_S+\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf J.}
$$

The second term accounts for the changing basis, even when the components of [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) are constant in the [rotating reference frame](../../../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md).
