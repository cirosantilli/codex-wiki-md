<h1 id="section-b/b6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For this [gyrocompass](../../../../../../gyrocompass.md), let $\mathbf n,\mathbf w,\mathbf z$ be local north, west and upward unit [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md), forming a right-handed basis. [Earth's rotation](../../../../../../earth-s-rotation.md) has local components

$$
\boldsymbol\Omega_E=\Omega_E\cos\lambda\,\mathbf n+\Omega_E\sin\lambda\,\mathbf z.
$$

Let the spin axis point along $\mathbf u=\cos\theta\,\mathbf n+\sin\theta\,\mathbf w$, and write its dominant [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) as $J_s\mathbf u$, where $J_s=I_1\omega>0$. In [mechanical equilibrium](../../../../../../mechanical-equilibrium.md) relative to [Earth](../../../../../../earth.md), this leading [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is fixed in the terrestrial [rotating reference frame](../../../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md). The bearings can exert horizontal [torques](../../../../../../torque.md) to keep the axle horizontal, but free rotation about the vertical implies $G_z=0$. The vertical component of the [rotating-frame derivative formula](../../../../../../rotating-frame-derivative-formula.md) therefore requires

$$
0=(\boldsymbol\Omega_E\times J_s\mathbf u)\cdot\mathbf z
=\Omega_EJ_s\cos\lambda\sin\theta.
$$

Away from either geographical pole, $\cos\lambda\ne0$, hence

$$
\boxed{\theta=0\ \text{or}\ \pi:\quad\text{the axle lies north--south}.}
$$

This is a necessary [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) condition; the next part distinguishes its stable and unstable spin orientations. The rapid-spin approximation neglects [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) terms of order $I_2\Omega_E$ compared with $I_1\omega$. At either pole the horizontal component of [Earth's rotation](../../../../../../earth-s-rotation.md) vanishes, and this north-seeking mechanism gives no preferred horizontal direction. The paper explicitly excludes $90^\circ$; if latitude is signed, $-90^\circ$ must likewise be excluded.
