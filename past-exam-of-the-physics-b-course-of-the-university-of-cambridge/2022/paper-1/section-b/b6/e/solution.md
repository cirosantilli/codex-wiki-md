<h1 id="section-b/b6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For a thin [gyrocompass](../../../../../../gyrocompass.md) flywheel with rotational [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md), the [perpendicular axis theorem](../../../../../../perpendicular-axis-theorem.md) gives $I_1=I_x+I_y=2I_2$. This does not require the mass to be concentrated at the rim. Convert the specified spin to [angular velocity](../../../../../../angular-velocity.md) and use one [sidereal day](../../../../../../sidereal-day.md) for [Earth's rotation](../../../../../../earth-s-rotation.md):

$$
\omega=5000\frac{2\pi}{60}=523.6\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}},\qquad
\Omega_E=\frac{2\pi}{86164\ \mathrm s}=7.2921\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}.
$$

At the given latitude,

$$
\omega_{\rm shm}=\sqrt{2(523.6)(7.2921\times10^{-5})\cos52.2^\circ},\qquad
\boxed{\omega_{\rm shm}\simeq0.216\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The associated [oscillation period](../../../../../../period-of-an-oscillation.md) is $T=2\pi/\omega_{\rm shm}\simeq29.0\ \mathrm s$. This uses the stated neglect of the support assembly's contribution to the [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md); a substantial additional vertical [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) would lengthen the [oscillation period](../../../../../../period-of-an-oscillation.md).
