<h1 id="section-b/b7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [bending moment](../../../../../../bending-moment.md) $B(x)$ is the internal [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) transmitted across a cut through an [elastic beam](../../../../../../elastic-beam.md), about the transverse bending axis. It balances the moments of the external [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) on the isolated portion on one side of the cut. Its sign depends on the chosen convention, and its units are $\mathrm{N\,m}$.

The relevant “moment of area” is the [second moment of area](../../../../../../second-moment-of-area.md) of the cross-section about its [neutral axis](../../../../../../neutral-axis.md):

$$
\boxed{I=\int_A z^2\,dA,}
$$

where $z$ is perpendicular distance from that axis within the section. It has units $\mathrm{m^4}$ and is a geometrical quantity, unlike a mass [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md). For a homogeneous beam under pure bending, the [neutral axis](../../../../../../neutral-axis.md) passes through the section's [centroid](../../../../../../centroid.md), and its longitudinal [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) is zero.
