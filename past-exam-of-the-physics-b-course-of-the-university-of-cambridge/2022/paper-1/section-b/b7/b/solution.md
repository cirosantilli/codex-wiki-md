<h1 id="section-b/b7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume a slender homogeneous [elastic beam](../../../../../../elastic-beam.md) obeying [linear elasticity](../../../../../../linear-elasticity.md), with plane sections remaining plane and no resultant longitudinal [force](../../../../../../force-split.md). A length element of its [neutral axis](../../../../../../neutral-axis.md) subtending a small [angle](../../../../../../angle.md) $d\phi$ has length $R\,d\phi$. A neighbouring fibre at distance $z$ from that axis has a length change of magnitude $z\,d\phi$, giving longitudinal [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) $\varepsilon=z/R$ with signs chosen consistently for tensile and compressed fibres. By [Hooke's law](../../../../../../hooke-s-law.md), its longitudinal [stress](../../../../../../stress.md) is $\sigma=E\varepsilon=Ez/R$.

The [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) of these longitudinal [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) about the [neutral axis](../../../../../../neutral-axis.md) is therefore

$$
B=\int_A z\sigma\,dA=\frac E R\int_Az^2\,dA=\frac{EI}{R},
\qquad
\boxed{R=\frac{EI}{B}.}
$$

Here $E$ is [Young's modulus](../../../../../../young-s-modulus.md) and $I$ the [second moment of area](../../../../../../second-moment-of-area.md). The displayed relation uses a signed radius and [bending moment](../../../../../../bending-moment.md); an unsigned geometrical radius is $|R|=EI/|B|$. For consistency with the negative [bending moments](../../../../../../bending-moment.md) in the later parts, take the deflection $y$ positive downward and the signed [curvature](../../../../../../curvature.md) as $1/R\simeq y''$. Then the small-slope [Euler-Bernoulli beam equation](../../../../../../euler-bernoulli-beam-equation.md) reads $EIy''=B$. Reversing the vertical coordinate reverses the corresponding sign relation but leaves the physical deflection unchanged.
