<h1 id="section-b/b7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The uniform weight per unit length is $q=\rho abg$, where $\rho$ is the [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) and $g$ the [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md). The total weight is $qL$, so each support provides an upward [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) $qL/2$. The weight on the portion from $0$ to $x$ is $qx$ acting at that portion's midpoint, a distance $x/2$ from the cut. In the same [bending moment](../../../../../../bending-moment.md) convention as part (c),

$$
B(x)=-\frac{qL}{2}x+(qx)\frac x2,
\qquad
\boxed{B(x)=-\frac12\rho abg\,x(L-x).}
$$

The units are $(\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}})\mathrm{m^2}=\mathrm{N\,m}$. Its greatest magnitude occurs at $x=L/2$, where $B=-qL^2/8$.
