<h1 id="section-c/c9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A cubic reflecting cavity supports [standing waves](../../../../../../standing-wave.md) with positive integer mode indices and [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) components $k_i=\pi n_i/L$. A cell in mode space has [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) $(\pi/L)^3$. In the continuum approximation, a spherical shell of radius $k$ and thickness $dk$ has one-eighth of its [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) in the positive octant. There are two independent transverse [photon polarizations](../../../../../../photon-polarization.md) for a generic [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md). Hence

$$
dN=2\times\frac18\times4\pi k^2dk\left(\frac L\pi\right)^3=\frac{L^3k^2}{\pi^2}\,dk.
$$

The [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is $\epsilon=\hbar\omega=\hbar ck$, so $dk=d\epsilon/(\hbar c)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{dN=\frac{L^3\epsilon^2}{\pi^2\hbar^3c^3}\,d\epsilon.}
$$

This is the leading bulk [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md), valid when the cavity is large compared with the relevant [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md) and many modes lie in the counting interval. A finite cavity has a discrete mode spectrum, with boundary corrections beyond this approximation. Modes with vanishing indices require separate boundary counting but do not change the leading $L^3$ term. Here $\hbar$ is the [reduced Planck constant](../../../../../../reduced-planck-constant.md), as printed in the original PDF; replacing it by $h$ would introduce an erroneous factor of $(2\pi)^3$.
