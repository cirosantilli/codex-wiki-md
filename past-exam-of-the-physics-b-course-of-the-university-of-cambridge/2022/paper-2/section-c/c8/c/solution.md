<h1 id="section-c/c8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The complete cycle must be a [reversible thermodynamic process](../../../../../../reversible-thermodynamic-process.md): no [friction](../../../../../../friction.md), viscous dissipation, unrestrained expansion, or other source of [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md). Each moving boundary must be arbitrarily close to [mechanical equilibrium](../../../../../../mechanical-equilibrium.md). [Heat transfer](../../../../../../heat-transfer.md) to or from a bath must occur across an infinitesimal [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference, and the insulated stages must be genuinely [adiabatic processes](../../../../../../adiabatic-process.md). There must also be no direct [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) leak between the [heat baths](../../../../../../heat-bath.md).

For an engine exchanging [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) only with these two [heat baths](../../../../../../heat-bath.md), the [Second law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) gives

$$
S_{\rm gen}=\frac{Q_c}{T_c}-\frac{Q_h}{T_h}\geq0,
$$

and thus

$$
\eta=1-\frac{T_c}{T_h}-\frac{T_cS_{\rm gen}}{Q_h}\leq\eta_{\rm Carnot}.
$$

Practical engines generate [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) through finite [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) differences, [friction](../../../../../../friction.md) and other dissipative effects. They therefore fall below the [Carnot cycle](../../../../../../carnot-cycle.md) limit. Approaching reversible [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) exchange generally requires vanishingly slow operation for a finite heat-transfer apparatus, limiting useful [power](../../../../../../power.md).
