<h1 id="section-c/c8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Because the working substance returns to its original [thermodynamic state](../../../../../../thermodynamic-state.md), its net [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) change is zero. There is no [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) change on either [reversible ideal-gas adiabat](../../../../../../reversible-ideal-gas-adiabat.md); on each [isothermal process](../../../../../../isothermal-process.md) the [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) transfer is the exchanged [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) divided by the bath's absolute [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). Hence

$$
\frac{Q_h}{T_h}-\frac{Q_c}{T_c}=0,\qquad \frac{Q_c}{Q_h}=\frac{T_c}{T_h}.
$$

The [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) over a complete cycle gives $W=Q_h-Q_c$. The [thermal efficiency](../../../../../../thermal-efficiency.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\eta_{\rm Carnot}=\frac{W}{Q_h}=1-\frac{Q_c}{Q_h}=1-\frac{T_c}{T_h}=1-\frac{T_1}{T_2}.}
$$

The use of kelvins is essential. This result does not depend on the working substance. For the [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) illustration, the two [reversible ideal-gas adiabats](../../../../../../reversible-ideal-gas-adiabat.md) imply $V_C/V_B=V_D/V_A=(T_h/T_c)^{1/(\gamma-1)}$, so $\ln(V_B/V_A)=\ln(V_C/V_D)$; the explicit [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) expressions in (a) give the same ratio.
