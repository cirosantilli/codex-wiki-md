<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

Let $N_u,N_l$ be the upper- and lower-level populations and $g_u,g_l$ their [degeneracy factors](../../../../../degeneracy-factor.md). At [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md), the [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) gives

$$
\frac{N_u}{N_l}=\frac{g_u}{g_l}\exp\left(-\frac{\Delta E}{k_BT}\right),\qquad
\frac{\Delta E}{k_B}=\frac{27\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{eV}}{8.617333262\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{eV\,K^{-1}}}=0.313322\,\mathrm K.
$$

No [degeneracy factors](../../../../../degeneracy-factor.md) are supplied, so the numerical answers use equal [degeneracy factors](../../../../../degeneracy-factor.md). Stating the ratio as upper population divided by lower population removes an otherwise arbitrary inversion.

**(a)** At $T=500\,\mathrm K$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{N_u}{N_l}=e^{-0.000626644}=0.999374.}
$$

The two populations are nearly equal because the level separation is much smaller than the thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $k_BT$.

**(b)** At $T=2\,\mathrm{mK}=0.002\,\mathrm K$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{N_u}{N_l}=e^{-156.661}=9.18\times10^{-69}.}
$$

Almost every atom occupies the lower level. If the levels have unequal [degeneracy factors](../../../../../degeneracy-factor.md), each boxed ratio must be multiplied by $g_u/g_l$; their individual values cannot be inferred from the paper.
