<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

**(a)** Treat the lake as a [heat bath](../../../../../heat-bath.md) at fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_0=288.15\,\mathrm K$ and the block's [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) as constant. Its initial [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is $T_i=473.15\,\mathrm K$, and $C=mc=355\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. Although the cooling is irreversible, [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) is a [state function](../../../../../state-function.md), so evaluate the block's change along an imagined [reversible thermodynamic process](../../../../../reversible-thermodynamic-process.md):

$$
\Delta S_{\rm block}=\int_{T_i}^{T_0}\frac{C\,dT}{T}=C\ln\frac{T_0}{T_i}=-176.1\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.
$$

The lake receives [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) $Q=C(T_i-T_0)=65675\,\mathrm J$, and therefore

$$
\Delta S_{\rm lake}=\frac{Q}{T_0}=227.9\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.
$$

Adding the two contributions gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta S_{\rm Universe}=C\left[\ln\frac{T_0}{T_i}+\frac{T_i-T_0}{T_0}\right]=51.9\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.}
$$

The positive result is consistent with the [Second law of thermodynamics](../../../../../second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) for [heat transfer](../../../../../heat-transfer.md) across a finite [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference. The calculation uses $c=710\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ exactly as printed in both the original PDF and the transcription; it does not replace the supplied value by a tabulated value for lead.

**(b)** The block starts and eventually finishes at the lake's [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), so its net [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) change is approximately zero. Neglecting buoyancy, air resistance and the final immersion depth, the lost [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../gravitational-energy.md) $mgh$ becomes [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) in the lake after the motion and waves have dissipated. The lake remains at $T_0$, giving

$$
\boxed{\Delta S_{\rm Universe}\simeq\frac{mgh}{T_0}=\frac{(0.5\,\mathrm{kg})(9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}})(200\,\mathrm m)}{288.15\,\mathrm K}=3.40\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}.}
$$

Temporary heating of the block or temporary storage in [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) does not change this final [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) balance once [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md) is restored.
