<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

Interpret the applied [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) as a uniform increase of external [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) on all six faces, and approximate the rock by an [isotropic](../../../../../isotropy.md) material obeying [linear elasticity](../../../../../linear-elasticity.md). Taking tensile [stress](../../../../../stress.md) as positive, $\sigma_x=\sigma_y=\sigma_z=-p$. By [Hooke's law](../../../../../hooke-s-law.md), the [strain](../../../../../strain.md) along any one axis is

$$
\epsilon_x=\frac{\sigma_x-\nu(\sigma_y+\sigma_z)}{E}=-\frac{(1-2\nu)p}{E}.
$$

To first order in the small [strain](../../../../../strain.md), the fractional [volume](../../../../../volume.md) change is the sum of the three axial [strains](../../../../../strain.md):

$$
\frac{\Delta V}{V}=\epsilon_x+\epsilon_y+\epsilon_z=-\frac{3(1-2\nu)p}{E}=-\frac pK,
\qquad K=\frac{E}{3(1-2\nu)}.
$$

Thus the [bulk modulus](../../../../../bulk-modulus.md) is $K=40\,\mathrm{GPa}$. Using the specified [Young's modulus](../../../../../young-s-modulus.md), [Poisson's ratio](../../../../../poisson-s-ratio.md), $V=1\,\mathrm{m^3}$ and $p=101325\,\mathrm{Pa}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta V=-2.53\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3}=-2.53\,\mathrm{cm^3}.}
$$

The sign indicates contraction. This is the change relative to the unloaded reference state; simply maintaining a specimen already equilibrated at this same [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) would produce no further change.
