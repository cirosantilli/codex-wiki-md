<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

For a fixed amount of a [simple compressible system](../../../../../simple-compressible-system.md), write the [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) as $U(S,V)$. Its exact [differential](../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) is

$$
dU=T\,dS-p\,dV,\qquad T=\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial S}\right)_V,\qquad -p=\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_S.
$$

Assuming the [thermodynamic potential](../../../../../thermodynamic-potential-split.md) has continuous second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md), equality of its [mixed partial derivatives](../../../../../mixed-partial-derivative.md) yields the first [Maxwell relation](../../../../../maxwell-relations.md):

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial V}\right)_S=\frac{\partial^2U}{\partial V\partial S}=-\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial S}\right)_V.}
$$

For the second [Maxwell relation](../../../../../maxwell-relations.md), use the [Legendre transform](../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) $H=U+pV$, the [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md). Its natural variables are $S,p$ since

$$
dH=dU+p\,dV+V\,dp=T\,dS+V\,dp.
$$

Thus $T=(\partial H/\partial S)_p$ and $V=(\partial H/\partial p)_S$. Equality of the [mixed partial derivatives](../../../../../mixed-partial-derivative.md) now gives

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_S=\frac{\partial^2H}{\partial p\partial S}=\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial S}\right)_p.}
$$
