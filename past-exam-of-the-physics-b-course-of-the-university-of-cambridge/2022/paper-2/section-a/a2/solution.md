<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

Use the pipe-diameter convention for the [Reynolds number](../../../../../reynolds-number.md), with mean [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $\bar v$, [density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho$ and [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) $\mu$:

$$
\mathrm{Re}=\frac{\rho\bar vD}{\mu},\qquad
D_{\rm crit}=\frac{\mathrm{Re}_{\rm crit}\mu}{\rho\bar v}.
$$

The [Reynolds number](../../../../../reynolds-number.md) compares inertial and viscous effects in the [fluid flow](../../../../../fluid-flow.md). A conventional estimate for the onset of [turbulence](../../../../../turbulence-split.md) in a pipe is $\mathrm{Re}_{\rm crit}\simeq2000$, giving

$$
D_{\rm crit}=\frac{2000(3.16\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{Pa\,s})}{(789\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}})(0.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}})}=1.60\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm m.
$$

Hence the usual onset estimate is $\boxed{D\gtrsim1.6\,\mathrm{mm}}$. Using the also common transition criterion $\mathrm{Re}_{\rm crit}=2300$ gives $1.84\,\mathrm{mm}$, whereas $\mathrm{Re}\gtrsim4000$, used for established [turbulence](../../../../../turbulence-split.md), gives $D\gtrsim3.2\,\mathrm{mm}$. There is no universal sharp minimum: inlet disturbances and pipe conditions affect the transition from [laminar flow](../../../../../laminar-flow.md), so the threshold convention must accompany the answer.
