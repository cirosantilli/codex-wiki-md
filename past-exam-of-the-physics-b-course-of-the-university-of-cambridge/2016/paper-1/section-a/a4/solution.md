<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

To obtain the [maximum work from two finite heat reservoirs](../../../../../maximum-work-from-two-finite-heat-reservoirs.md), operate a [reversible heat engine](../../../../../reversible-heat-engine.md) between the bodies until their [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) coincide. Include the [work](../../../../../work.md) store in the isolated total system: no [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) enters from outside, while the bodies can transfer [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to that store. With the stated constant [heat capacities](../../../../../heat-capacity.md), reversibility requires

$$
C_1\ln\frac{T_f}{T_1}+C_2\ln\frac{T_f}{T_2}=0,
\qquad T_f=T_1^{C_1/(C_1+C_2)}T_2^{C_2/(C_1+C_2)}.
$$

Numerically, $T_f=450^{5/8}273^{3/8}=373.094\,\mathrm K$. The decrease of the bodies' [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) is

$$
W_{\max}=C_1T_1+C_2T_2-(C_1+C_2)T_f=\boxed{2.1061\times10^4\,\mathrm J\simeq21.1\,\mathrm{kJ}.}
$$

Direct thermal equilibration without an engine would instead reach the arithmetic heat-capacity-weighted [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $383.625\,\mathrm K$ and deliver no [work](../../../../../work.md). [entropy production](../../../../../entropy-production.md) can only raise the final common [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) above the value for zero [entropy production](../../../../../entropy-production.md) and reduce extractable [work](../../../../../work.md).
