<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

Use a cold, unmagnetized, collisionless [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) with a sharp interface, and take the incident medium to be vacuum. Its [relative permittivity](../../../../../relative-permittivity.md) is $\epsilon_r=1-\omega_p^2/\omega^2$, so at the stated [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md) $n=\sqrt{1-(10/12)^2}=\sqrt{11}/6$. Conservation of tangential [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md) gives

$$
k_{z,\rm plasma}^2=\frac{\omega^2}{c^2}\left(\cos^2\theta_i-\frac{\omega_p^2}{\omega^2}\right).
$$

For [oblique total reflection at an unmagnetized plasma](../../../../../oblique-total-reflection-at-an-unmagnetized-plasma.md), require $k_{z,\rm plasma}^2<0$, or $\cos\theta_i<5/6$. Thus **total reflection occurs for $\theta_i>33.56^\circ$ measured from the normal**, up to grazing incidence. At the ideal critical [angle](../../../../../angle.md) $\theta_c=\cos^{-1}(5/6)$ the transmitted normal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) flux also vanishes, so the lossless reflectance reaches unity there. Collisions or an imposed [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) change this simple criterion. The constitutive response used here follows the electron-motion derivation in the cited university notes.[Section 9.7, equations 9.27–9.30, unmagnetized-plasma dispersion and refractive index](../../../../../oscillations-and-waves-by-richard-fitzpatrick.md)
