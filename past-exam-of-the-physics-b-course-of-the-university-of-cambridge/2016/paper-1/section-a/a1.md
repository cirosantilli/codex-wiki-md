<h1 id="section-a/a1">A1</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Section A](../section-a.md)

Take a long strip along $y$, occupying $-w/2<x<w/2$ in $z=0$, with uniform surface current $K=I/w$. Above the strip's centre, the contributions odd in $x$ cancel, while the [Biot-Savart law](../../../../biot-savart-law.md) gives the [magnetic field of a uniformly conducting strip](../../../../magnetic-field-of-a-uniformly-conducting-strip.md)

$$
B_x(0,z)=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi w}\int_{-w/2}^{w/2}\frac{z\,dx'}{x'^2+z^2}=\frac{\mu_0I}{\pi w}\tan^{-1}\frac{w}{2z},\qquad z>0.
$$

The field reverses below the strip. Its field lines wrap around the longitudinal current: nearly parallel to the faces near the centre, then curving round the edges and approaching circles at large [distances](../../../../distance.md). The sketch is an original field integration; the conductor thickness is idealized as negligible.
