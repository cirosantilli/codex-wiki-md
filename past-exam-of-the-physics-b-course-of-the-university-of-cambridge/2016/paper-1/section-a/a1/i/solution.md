<h1 id="section-a/a1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At [distance](../../../../../../distance.md) $r\gg w$, the strip is indistinguishable to leading order from a straight line current. [Ampère's law](../../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) on a large circular contour gives **$B\simeq\mu_0I/(2\pi r)$**, directed tangentially around the strip's longitudinal axis by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md). On the perpendicular centreline this also follows by expanding $\tan^{-1}(w/(2z))\simeq w/(2z)$.
