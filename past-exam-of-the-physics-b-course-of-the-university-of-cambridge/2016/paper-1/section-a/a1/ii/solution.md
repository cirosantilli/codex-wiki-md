<h1 id="section-a/a1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At perpendicular [distance](../../../../../../distance.md) $z\ll w$ from the middle of a face, but outside the conductor, the strip approximates an infinite current sheet. The tangential field jump is $\mu_0K$ and symmetry gives equal opposite fields on the two sides. Hence **$B\simeq\mu_0I/(2w)$** on either side. The centreline integral gives the same limit as $\tan^{-1}(w/(2z))\to\pi/2$. Uniform current across the width and [distance](../../../../../../distance.md) from the strip edges are implicit in this sheet approximation; a nonuniform surface current would require its local value instead.
