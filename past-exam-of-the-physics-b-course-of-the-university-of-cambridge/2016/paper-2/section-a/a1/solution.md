↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

The [Boltzmann entropy](../../../../../boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md) is

$$
S=k_B\ln\Omega=k_B\ln\Omega_0+\frac{5N}{2}k_B\ln(E/E_0).
$$

The thermodynamic definition of [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) gives

$$
\frac1T=\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial E}\right)_{N,\ldots}=\frac{5Nk_B}{2E},\qquad \boxed{T=\frac{2E}{5Nk_B}.}
$$

Holding the other system parameters fixed, $E=(5N/2)k_BT$, so its [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) is **$C=5Nk_B/2$**. The constants $\Omega_0$ and $E_0$ set an additive reference for [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md); neither affects this derivative or the resulting [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md).
