<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

At fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), equilibrium [blackbody radiation](../../../../../black-body-radiation.md) has fixed [energy density](../../../../../energy-density.md) $u$, with [radiation pressure](../../../../../radiation-pressure.md) $p=u/3$. Doubling the [volume](../../../../../volume-thermodynamics.md) therefore doubles its internal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), so $\Delta U=U_0$. The [work](../../../../../work.md) done by the radiation is

$$
W=\int_{V_0}^{2V_0}p\,dV=\frac u3V_0=\frac{U_0}{3}.
$$

The [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md), with positive [work](../../../../../work.md) done by the radiation, gives

$$
\boxed{Q=\Delta U+W=\frac43U_0=1000\ \mu\mathrm J=1.00\ \mathrm{mJ}.}
$$

Thus **$1.00\ \mathrm{mJ}$ is absorbed from the walls**. The walls must replace the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) used to do [work](../../../../../work.md) as well as supply the extra stored [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md); using only $\Delta U=750\ \mu\mathrm J$ would omit that work. This is a [reversible isothermal expansion of a photon gas](../../../../../reversible-isothermal-expansion-of-a-photon-gas.md), not an adiabatic expansion.
