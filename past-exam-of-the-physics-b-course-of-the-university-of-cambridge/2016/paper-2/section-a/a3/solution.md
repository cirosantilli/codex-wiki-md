<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

Separate a ball's orbital motion from its spin. For a [rolling ball between concentric races](../../../../../rolling-ball-between-concentric-races.md), the ball centre lies $2a$ from the axle axis. Let its tangential centre [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) be $v$ in the direction of axle motion and let its signed spin [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) be $\Omega$, positive in the same sense as $\omega$. [Rolling without slipping](../../../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) at the moving inner contact and stationary outer contact requires

$$
v-a\Omega=a\omega,\qquad v+a\Omega=0.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{v=\frac{a\omega}{2},\qquad \Omega=-\frac\omega2,\qquad \omega_{\rm orbit}=\frac{v}{2a}=\frac\omega4.}
$$

The spin is opposite to the axle's rotation, while the centre orbits in the same sense. The total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) about the axle is the sum of its orbital [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and its [angular momentum about the centre of mass](../../../../../angular-momentum-about-the-centre-of-mass.md):

$$
L_z=(2a)mv+I_{\rm cm}\Omega=ma^2\omega-\frac12I_{\rm cm}\omega.
$$

For a uniform solid spherical ball, $I_{\rm cm}=2ma^2/5$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf L=\frac45ma^2\boldsymbol\omega.}
$$

The solid-ball assumption supplies the usual [moment of inertia](../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md); the preceding expression in terms of $I_{\rm cm}$ also states the answer for a different internal [mass distribution](../../../../../mass-distribution.md).
