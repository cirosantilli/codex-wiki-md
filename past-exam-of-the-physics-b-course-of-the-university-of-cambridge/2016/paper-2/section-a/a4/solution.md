<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

In the thin-gap [Couette flow](../../../../../couette-flow.md) approximation, the tangential [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) varies linearly through the gap. At radius $r$, its difference between the two discs is $r\omega$, so a [Newtonian fluid](../../../../../newtonian-fluid.md) exerts [shear stress](../../../../../shear-stress.md)

$$
\tau_{\theta z}=\eta\frac{r\omega}{d}.
$$

An annulus has area $2\pi r\,dr$, and its tangential [force](../../../../../force-split.md) acts at lever arm $r$. Integrating its [torque](../../../../../torque.md) gives

$$
\tau_{\rm drive}=\int_0^a r\frac{\eta r\omega}{d}\,2\pi r\,dr,\qquad \boxed{\tau_{\rm drive}=\frac{\pi\eta\omega a^4}{2d}.}
$$

The driving [torque](../../../../../torque.md) is in the direction of rotation; the liquid's resisting [torque](../../../../../torque.md) has the opposite sign. This [parallel-disc viscometer](../../../../../parallel-disc-viscometer.md) result assumes laminar shear with no slip, negligible edge effects and negligible secondary circulation. Its fourth-power radius dependence comes from the combined radial dependence of speed, annular area and lever arm.
