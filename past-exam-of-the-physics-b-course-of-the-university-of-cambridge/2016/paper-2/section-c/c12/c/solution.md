<h1 id="section-c/c12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The energy average must be taken over the atoms that actually arrive, not over the bulk population. Weighting the collision flux by $mv^2/2$ gives

$$
\langle K\rangle_{\rm hit}=\frac{(m/2)nA\pi I_5}{nA\pi I_3}=\frac m2\frac{I_5}{I_3},\qquad \boxed{\langle K\rangle_{\rm hit}=2k_BT.}
$$

The [wall-impact speed distribution](../../../../../../wall-impact-speed-distribution.md) is proportional to $v^3e^{-\alpha v^2}$ rather than the bulk $v^2e^{-\alpha v^2}$. Faster atoms are overrepresented among arrivals. Equivalently, the mean normal [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) of the arriving flux is $k_BT$, while each tangential component still contributes $k_BT/2$.
