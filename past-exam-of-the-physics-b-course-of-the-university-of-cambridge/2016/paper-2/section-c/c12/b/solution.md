<h1 id="section-c/c12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An elastic reflection transfers normal [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) $2mv\cos\theta$ to the wall. Multiplying this impulse by the collision flux and integrating gives

$$
p=2mnA I_4\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin\theta\cos^2\theta\,d\theta\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi=\frac{4\pi}{3}mnAI_4.
$$

Using the radial integral and $\alpha=m/(2k_BT)$ yields

$$
\boxed{p=\frac{nm}{2\alpha}=nk_BT.}
$$

This agrees with the ideal-gas [equation of state](../../../../../../equation-of-state.md). The calculation assumes a stationary wall and elastic normal momentum reversal, appropriate to a gas in equilibrium with it.
