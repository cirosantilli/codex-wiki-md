<h1 id="section-b/b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Magnus effect](../../../../../../magnus-effect.md) is the transverse [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) produced when a spinning body moves relative to a [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md). Rotation changes the flow and [circulation](../../../../../../circulation-physics.md) around the body; the resulting asymmetric [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) and wake deflect the [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md), with the opposite transverse [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) on the body. Examples include the curved flight of spinning balls and the lift of rotating-cylinder sails.

A useful quantitative model is steady two-dimensional [incompressible flow](../../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) past a cylinder of radius $a$, with [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf U=U\hat{\mathbf x}$ far away. Let [circulation](../../../../../../circulation-physics.md) $\Gamma$ be positive anticlockwise as viewed from $+z$. The potential-flow [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) at the cylinder surface is

$$
v_\theta(a,\theta)=-2U\sin\theta+\frac{\Gamma}{2\pi a}.
$$

The [Bernoulli equation](../../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) outside the [boundary layer](../../../../../../boundary-layer.md) gives

$$
p(\theta)=p_\infty+\frac\rho2\left(U^2-v_\theta^2\right).
$$

Integrating the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) around the cylinder, per unit span, yields

$$
F_y'=-a\int_0^{2\pi}p(\theta)\sin\theta\,d\theta=-\rho U\Gamma.
$$

Thus the [Kutta–Joukowski theorem](../../../../../../kutta-joukowski-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F'=\rho\mathbf U\times(\Gamma\hat{\mathbf z}),\qquad |\mathbf F'|=\rho U|\Gamma|.}
$$

The sign here refers to the [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) relative to the cylinder. For a body moving through otherwise stationary [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md), that relative [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) points opposite to the body's translational [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md).

Real [viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) and the [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) transmit the surface rotation to the [fluid](../../../../../../fluid.md) and help set the [circulation](../../../../../../circulation-physics.md) and separation pattern. Pure inviscid flow with only an impermeability condition does not determine $\Gamma$ from the cylinder's [angular speed](../../../../../../angular-speed.md). Therefore the [circulation](../../../../../../circulation-physics.md) must not be assigned a universal spin-rate formula without additional assumptions. The cylinder result illustrates the lift mechanism; quantitative [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) on a finite spinning ball also depend on its geometry, [Reynolds number](../../../../../../reynolds-number.md), rotation rate, surface roughness and wake.
