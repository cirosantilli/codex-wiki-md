<h1 id="section-c/c8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For liquid-vapor equilibrium, use molar quantities. At low vapor [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) the gas is dilute, approximately ideal, and has a much larger [molar volume](../../../../../../molar-volume.md) than the liquid. Hence

$$
\Delta v_m=v_{m,g}-v_{m,l}\simeq v_{m,g}\simeq\frac{RT}{p}.
$$

Substitution into the [Clapeyron equation](../../../../../../clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) gives

$$
\frac{dp}{dT}\simeq\frac{Lp}{RT^2},\qquad
\frac{d\ln p}{dT}\simeq\frac{L}{RT^2}.
$$

If the molar [latent heat](../../../../../../latent-heat.md) varies sufficiently slowly to be treated as constant over the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) interval, integration gives

$$
\ln p=\text{constant}-\frac{L}{RT},\qquad
\boxed{p=p_0\exp\left(-\frac{L}{RT}\right).}
$$

The positive constant $p_0$ has [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) units and is an integration constant. It is not necessarily a real infinite-temperature [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md), since the approximations fail before that limit.

For [mercury](../../../../../../mercury-element.md) between $300$ and $500\,\mathrm K$, the original plot places this range above the [triple point](../../../../../../triple-point.md) and below the normal boiling and critical [temperatures](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). Its vapor [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) is low relative to the critical [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md), making a dilute-gas description reasonable; the vapor's [molar volume](../../../../../../molar-volume.md) greatly exceeds that of the dense liquid. The interval is also far from the critical region where $L$ tends to zero, so a roughly constant [latent heat](../../../../../../latent-heat.md) is a useful first approximation. The approximations are not exact: [Kirchhoff's law of thermochemistry](../../../../../../kirchhoff-s-law-of-thermochemistry.md) gives, at fixed [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md), the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of a transformation enthalpy as the difference between the two molar [heat capacities](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md), so a precise fit over a wide interval would allow $L$ to vary.
