<h1 id="section-c/c9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) is the canonical mean of the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md):

$$
U_{\rm sys}=\sum_j\epsilon_j\,\frac{g_je^{-\beta\epsilon_j}}Z.
$$

Holding the external mechanical parameters fixed, so that the spectrum and degeneracies do not change when differentiating with respect to $\beta$, gives

$$
\frac{\partial Z}{\partial\beta}=-\sum_jg_j\epsilon_je^{-\beta\epsilon_j}.
$$

Comparison yields

$$
\boxed{U_{\rm sys}=-\frac1Z\frac{\partial Z}{\partial\beta}=-\frac{\partial\ln Z}{\partial\beta},\qquad \beta=\frac1{k_BT}.}
$$

An explicitly temperature-dependent effective Hamiltonian would require care: differentiating its partition sum would also differentiate the assigned [energy levels](../../../../../../energy-level.md). The stated result uses the fixed physical spectrum of the canonical system.
