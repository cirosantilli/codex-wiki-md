<h1 id="section-c/c9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The expression is the [canonical partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) for a system in [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md) with a large [heat bath](../../../../../../heat-bath.md) at fixed [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T$. The system exchanges [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) with the bath, while its particle number and external mechanical parameters, such as [volume](../../../../../../volume.md), are fixed. The bath is large enough that its [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) is not appreciably changed by this exchange.

The system has allowed [energy levels](../../../../../../energy-level.md) $\epsilon_j$. The index $j$ labels distinct [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) values, and the integer [degeneracy factor](../../../../../../degeneracy-factor.md) $g_j$ counts distinct [quantum states](../../../../../../quantum-state.md) with that same [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md). $k_B$ is the [Boltzmann constant](../../../../../../boltzmann-constant.md), and $\exp[-\epsilon_j/(k_BT)]$ is a state's [Boltzmann factor](../../../../../../boltzmann-factor.md). The dimensionless sum $Z$ normalizes the probabilities:

$$
\boxed{P(\text{one state at }\epsilon_j)=\frac{e^{-\epsilon_j/(k_BT)}}Z,\qquad
P(\text{level }j)=\frac{g_je^{-\epsilon_j/(k_BT)}}Z.}
$$

Thus the degeneracy multiplies the probability of occupying the level, not the probability assigned to each individual state within it.

The canonical model is not restricted to a classical [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md). It applies to a quantum rotor, for example, provided the sum counts all of that system's allowed states with their proper degeneracies. For a many-particle system, $\epsilon_j$ would be the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) of a many-particle state, with any quantum-statistical restrictions already built into the state count.
