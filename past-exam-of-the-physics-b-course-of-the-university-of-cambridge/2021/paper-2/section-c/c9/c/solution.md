<h1 id="section-c/c9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the supplied [linear rigid rotor](../../../../../../linear-rigid-rotor.md) spectrum, sum over every nonnegative integer $J$ and include all $2J+1$ magnetic substates:

$$
\boxed{Z_{\rm rot}=\sum_{J=0}^{\infty}(2J+1)
\exp\left[-\frac{\hbar^2J(J+1)}{2Ik_BT}\right].}
$$

It is convenient to introduce the [rotational temperature](../../../../../../rotational-temperature.md)

$$
\theta_r=\frac{\hbar^2}{2Ik_B},
$$

so that the first terms are $Z_{\rm rot}=1+3e^{-2\theta_r/T}+5e^{-6\theta_r/T}+\cdots$. Here $I$ is the [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) and $\hbar$ is the [reduced Planck constant](../../../../../../reduced-planck-constant.md), as confirmed in the original PDF. The stipulated degeneracies are used as given, without adding nuclear-spin weights or omitting selected $J$ values.
