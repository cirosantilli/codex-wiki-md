<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

This is the one-mole, or equivalently [molar volume](../../../../../molar-volume.md), form of the [Van der Waals equation](../../../../../van-der-waals-equation.md), an approximate [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md) for a [real gas](../../../../../real-gas.md):

$$
p=\frac{RT}{V-b}-\frac{a}{V^2}.
$$

Here $p$ is the gas [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), $T$ its absolute [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), $R$ the [molar gas constant](../../../../../molar-gas-constant.md), and $V$ the [volume](../../../../../volume.md) occupied by one mole. If $V$ denotes [molar volume](../../../../../molar-volume.md) explicitly, its units are $\mathrm{m^3\,mol^{-1}}$.

The positive parameter $b$ represents [excluded volume](../../../../../excluded-volume.md) arising from the finite size and short-range repulsion of the [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md). The [volume](../../../../../volume.md) available for translational motion is reduced from $V$ to $V-b$. This is an effective [excluded volume](../../../../../excluded-volume.md), not simply the sum of the [molecules](../../../../../molecule-split.md)' geometric [volumes](../../../../../volume.md). Its molar units are $\mathrm{m^3\,mol^{-1}}$.

The positive parameter $a$ measures intermolecular attraction in a [mean-field approximation](../../../../../mean-field-approximation.md). Attractions reduce the [force](../../../../../force-split.md) exerted on the walls, lowering the measured [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) by $a/V^2$ relative to the repulsion-only expression. Thus $p+a/V^2$ is the [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) corrected for this attraction. The squared inverse-volume dependence reflects a contribution proportional to the square of the molecular [number density](../../../../../number-density.md). The molar units of $a$ are $\mathrm{Pa\,m^6\,mol^{-2}}$.

For an arbitrary amount $n$ measured in moles, with total container [volume](../../../../../volume.md) $V_{\rm tot}$, the corresponding formula is

$$
\boxed{\left(p+\frac{an^2}{V_{\rm tot}^2}\right)(V_{\rm tot}-nb)=nRT.}
$$

At low [density](../../../../../density.md), when $b\ll V$ and $a/V^2\ll RT/V$, it reduces to the [ideal gas law](../../../../../ideal-gas-law.md). The model captures qualitative attraction, repulsion and liquid-gas behavior, but does not supply an exact [equation of state](../../../../../equation-of-state.md) for every material.
