<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

For [charge sharing between distant connected spheres](../../../../../charge-sharing-between-distant-connected-spheres.md), use the standard approximation that the spheres are sufficiently far apart for mutual electrostatic effects to be negligible, and that the wire adds negligible [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md). The [capacitance of an isolated conducting sphere](../../../../../capacitance-of-an-isolated-conducting-sphere.md) is $C=4\pi\epsilon_0r$. Thus

$$
C_1=5.56\,\mathrm{pF},\qquad C_2=11.1\,\mathrm{pF}=2C_1.
$$

The initial charges are $Q_i=C_iV_i$. Connecting the spheres allows charge to flow until their potentials are equal. [Charge conservation](../../../../../charge-conservation.md) gives

$$
V_f=\frac{C_1V_1+C_2V_2}{C_1+C_2}
=\frac{(2\,\mathrm{kV})+2(4\,\mathrm{kV})}{3}
=3.333\,\mathrm{kV}.
$$

The initial and final [electrostatic energies](../../../../../electrostatic-energy.md) are $U_i=\tfrac12C_1V_1^2+\tfrac12C_2V_2^2$ and $U_f=\tfrac12(C_1+C_2)V_f^2$. Their difference becomes [Joule heating](../../../../../joule-heating.md) in the resistive wire:

$$
Q_{\rm heat}=U_i-U_f=\frac12\frac{C_1C_2}{C_1+C_2}(V_1-V_2)^2.
$$

Numerically,

$$
\boxed{Q_{\rm heat}=\frac12\left(3.71\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm F\right)(2000\,\mathrm V)^2=7.42\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm J=7.42\,\mu\mathrm J.}
$$

The resistance determines how fast this happens, not the final [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) difference. Sufficient damping suppresses sustained charge-current oscillations; the calculation also neglects [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) radiated away. At finite sphere separation the [capacitance matrix](../../../../../capacitance-matrix.md) would be needed, and the radii and initial potentials alone would not specify an exact correction.
