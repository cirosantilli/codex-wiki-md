<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

Assume a hollow, perfectly conducting [rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](../../../../../rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md) filled with air approximated by vacuum. Let its wider side be $a=0.040\,\mathrm m$ and its narrower side $b=0.020\,\mathrm m$. The longitudinal propagation constant of a mode is

$$
k_z^2=\frac{\omega^2}{c^2}-\left(\frac{m\pi}{a}\right)^2-\left(\frac{n\pi}{b}\right)^2,
$$

so the [waveguide cutoff frequency](../../../../../waveguide-cutoff-frequency.md) is

$$
f_{c,mn}=\frac c2\sqrt{\left(\frac ma\right)^2+\left(\frac nb\right)^2}.
$$

For a mode with [transverse electric polarization](../../../../../transverse-electric-polarization.md), one of $m,n$ may vanish, but they cannot both vanish. For a mode with [transverse magnetic polarization](../../../../../transverse-magnetic-polarization.md), both must be positive. A single hollow conducting guide has no [transverse electromagnetic mode](../../../../../transverse-electromagnetic-mode.md) with zero cutoff.

The lowest cutoff therefore belongs to the $\mathrm{TE}_{10}$ mode, varying across the wider side:

$$
\boxed{f_c=\frac{c}{2a}=\frac{2.998\times10^8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}{0.080\,\mathrm m}=3.75\times10^9\,\mathrm{Hz}\simeq3.75\,\mathrm{GHz}.}
$$

Strictly, a traveling mode transporting [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) down the guide requires $f>f_c$. At exactly $f_c$, $k_z=0$ and the axial [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) vanishes. Below cutoff the field is evanescent. A different [dielectric](../../../../../dielectric.md) filling would replace $c$ by the [wave speed](../../../../../wave-speed.md) in that material.
