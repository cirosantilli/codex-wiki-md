<h1 id="section-b/b7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Set $q=\ell_1/\ell_2$ and use [normal mode](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) amplitudes $\phi_j=A_je^{i\omega t}$, with real physical displacements obtained at the end. In units of $\omega_0^2$, the stiffness matrix is

$$
\mathsf K=\begin{pmatrix}1+q&-q\\-q&q\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Writing $\lambda=\omega^2/\omega_0^2$, the [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) condition is

$$
\det(\mathsf K-\lambda\mathsf 1)=(1+q-\lambda)(q-\lambda)-q^2
=\lambda^2-(1+2q)\lambda+q=0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\omega_\pm^2=\omega_0^2\left[\frac12+\frac{\ell_1}{\ell_2}
\pm\sqrt{\frac14+\left(\frac{\ell_1}{\ell_2}\right)^2}\right].}
$$

For finite positive fibre lengths both [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are positive. The corresponding amplitude ratio is

$$
\frac{A_2}{A_1}=\frac q{q-\lambda}=\frac1{1-R\lambda},\qquad R=\frac{\ell_2}{\ell_1}.
$$

The lower [normal mode](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) has the spheres twisting in the same sense, and the higher one has opposite signs of angular displacement.
