<h1 id="section-b/b7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $I$ be each sphere's [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) about the fibre, and let $K_t$ be the common fibre [filament torsional stiffness](../../../../../../filament-torsional-stiffness.md), so a length $\ell$ exerts restoring [torque](../../../../../../torque.md) $-K_t\phi/\ell$. The single-sphere [torsion pendulum](../../../../../../torsion-pendulum.md) obeys

$$
I\ddot\phi=-\frac{K_t}{\ell_1}\phi,\qquad
\omega_0^2=\frac{K_t}{I\ell_1}.
$$

For the two [coupled torsional pendulums](../../../../../../coupled-torsional-pendulums.md), the upper fibre has twist $\phi_1$ and the joining fibre has relative twist $\phi_2-\phi_1$. The joining fibre exerts equal and opposite [torques](../../../../../../torque.md) on the two spheres:

$$
I\ddot\phi_1=-\frac{K_t}{\ell_1}\phi_1+\frac{K_t}{\ell_2}(\phi_2-\phi_1),\qquad
I\ddot\phi_2=-\frac{K_t}{\ell_2}(\phi_2-\phi_1).
$$

Using the measured $\omega_0$ gives

$$
\boxed{\ddot\phi_1=-\omega_0^2\left[\phi_1-\frac{\ell_1}{\ell_2}(\phi_2-\phi_1)\right],\qquad
\ddot\phi_2=-\omega_0^2\frac{\ell_1}{\ell_2}(\phi_2-\phi_1).}
$$

The fibre's own rotational inertia and damping are neglected, so its twists supply elastic [torques](../../../../../../torque.md) without additional dynamic coordinates.
