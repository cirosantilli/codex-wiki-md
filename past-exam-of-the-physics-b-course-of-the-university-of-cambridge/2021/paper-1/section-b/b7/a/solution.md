↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [normal mode](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) of a linearized mechanical system is a motion in which every generalized displacement oscillates at a single common [angular frequency](../../../../../../angular-frequency.md), with fixed relative amplitudes and phases. In coordinates $q_j$, an undamped oscillatory mode may be written

$$
\boxed{q_j(t)=A_j\cos(\omega t+\delta),}
$$

where the amplitude [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $(A_1,\ldots,A_n)$ is fixed; negative components describe relative phase $\pi$. An arbitrary small motion is a [linear combination](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of the [normal modes](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) when the mode vectors form a complete [basis](../../../../../../basis.md).
