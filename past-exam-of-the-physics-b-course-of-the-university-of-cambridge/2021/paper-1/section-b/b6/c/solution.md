<h1 id="section-b/b6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Eliminate $v_p,v_a$ using the [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) expressions from part (b):

$$
\frac{J^2}{2mr_p^2}-\frac{GMm}{r_p}
=\frac{J^2}{2mr_a^2}-\frac{GMm}{r_a}.
$$

Hence

$$
\frac{J^2}{2m}\left(\frac1{r_p^2}-\frac1{r_a^2}\right)
=GMm\left(\frac1{r_p}-\frac1{r_a}\right).
$$

For distinct turning radii, factor the difference of squares and cancel the common factor to obtain

$$
\boxed{J^2=\frac{2GMm^2}{1/r_p+1/r_a}
=\frac{2GMm^2r_pr_a}{r_p+r_a}.}
$$

The [circular Kepler orbit](../../../../../../circular-kepler-orbit.md) case is recovered continuously by setting $r_p=r_a=r$, giving $J^2=GMm^2r$. Combining this with the energy expression also gives $E=-GMm/(r_p+r_a)$ for the transfer [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md).
