<h1 id="section-b/b6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose zero gravitational [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) at infinite separation. The spacecraft's conserved total [orbital energy](../../../../../../orbital-energy.md) is

$$
\boxed{E=\frac12mv_p^2-\frac{GMm}{r_p}
=\frac12mv_a^2-\frac{GMm}{r_a}.}
$$

At [periapsis](../../../../../../periapsis.md) and [apoapsis](../../../../../../apoapsis.md), the radial [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) vanishes, so the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is perpendicular to the radius. The conserved [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) magnitude is therefore

$$
\boxed{J=mr_pv_p=mr_av_a.}
$$

The [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) vector is normal to the fixed orbital plane. The respective units are joules and $\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$. These expressions use Newtonian [gravity](../../../../../../gravity.md) and the approximation $M\gg m$.
