<h1 id="section-c/c9/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For a linear induced response, reversing $I$ reverses $H_0$ and the sample [magnetization](../../../../../../magnetization.md), while also reversing the field gradient. Their product in $F_z=\mu_0V\chi_{\rm eff}H_0H'_0$ therefore keeps the same sign and magnitude:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F(-I)=\mathbf F(I).}
$$

This is also explicit in the $I^2$ dependence in part (d), for both the shape-consistent and printed alternatives. The conclusion assumes no independent permanent moment, remanence or [hysteresis](../../../../../../hysteresis.md); those would require a different response model.
