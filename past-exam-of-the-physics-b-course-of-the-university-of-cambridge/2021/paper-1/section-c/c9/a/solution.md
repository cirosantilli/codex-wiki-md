↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a steady filamentary [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) $I$ in vacuum, the [Biot-Savart law](../../../../../../biot-savart-law.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B(\mathbf r)=\frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi}
\int\frac{d\boldsymbol\ell'\times(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|^3}.}
$$

The line element points with conventional [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md), and the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) fixes the [magnetic flux density](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) direction by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md).

As an application, an infinitely long straight wire has circular symmetry about its axis. At perpendicular distance $s$,

$$
B_\phi(s)=\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi}\int_{-\infty}^\infty
\frac{s\,dz'}{(s^2+z'^2)^{3/2}}
=\boxed{\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi s}.}
$$

Its direction follows the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md). The following part applies the same law to a circular current loop.
