<h1 id="section-c/c9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the small-body approximation, write $\chi_{\rm eff}=\chi/(1+N_d\chi)$, with $\chi=\mu-1$. The [induced magnetic dipole](../../../../../../induced-magnetic-dipole.md) moment is $\mathbf m=VM\widehat{\mathbf z}=V\chi_{\rm eff}H_0\widehat{\mathbf z}$. Its effective interaction [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) at fixed source current is

$$
U_{\rm eff}(z)=-\frac12\mu_0V\chi_{\rm eff}H_0(z)^2.
$$

The factor $1/2$ accounts for inducing the moment: differentiating $-m(z)B_0(z)$ as if the moment were permanent would double the force. Equivalently, the instantaneous dipole force is $F_z=m\,dB_0/dz$. Thus the [force on a small linearly magnetizable body](../../../../../../force-on-a-small-linearly-magnetizable-body.md) is

$$
F_z=\mu_0V\chi_{\rm eff}H_0\frac{dH_0}{dz}.
$$

From part (b), $dH_0/dz=-3a^2Iz/[2(a^2+z^2)^{5/2}]$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=-\frac{3\mu_0V\chi_{\rm eff}a^4I^2z}{4(a^2+z^2)^4}\widehat{\mathbf z}.}
$$

For the ideal slender rod, $\chi_{\rm eff}\simeq\mu-1$. If the source's printed magnetisation is imposed instead, its corresponding conditional answer is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F_{\rm printed}=-\frac{3\mu_0V(\mu-1)a^4I^2z}
{4\mu(a^2+z^2)^4}\widehat{\mathbf z}.}
$$

For $\mu>1$ and $z>0$, both expressions point toward the loop, where the applied [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is stronger; for $z<0$ the direction reverses toward the loop plane. A material with [diamagnetism](../../../../../../diamagnetism.md), $\chi_{\rm eff}<0$, reverses this attraction. The force is in newtons, since a [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) times a field gradient has units $\mathrm{A\,m^2\,T\,m^{-1}}$. Both formulas assume [linear response](../../../../../../linear-response-theory.md), negligible saturation and approximately uniform applied field across the sample.
