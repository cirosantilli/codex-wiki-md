<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

Under a uniform compressive [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) $p$, each normal [stress](../../../../../stress.md) is $-p$. The isotropic [Hooke's law](../../../../../hooke-s-law.md) gives each longitudinal [strain](../../../../../strain.md) as

$$
\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_y=\varepsilon_z
=\frac{-p-\sigma(-p-p)}E=-\frac{p(1-2\sigma)}E.
$$

Thus $\Delta V/V=-3p(1-2\sigma)/E$. By the definition of [bulk modulus](../../../../../bulk-modulus.md), $B=-p/(\Delta V/V)$, so the [isotropic elastic modulus relation](../../../../../isotropic-elastic-modulus-relation.md) is

$$
\boxed{B=\frac E{3(1-2\sigma)},\qquad E=3B(1-2\sigma).}
$$

Positive finite [Young's modulus](../../../../../young-s-modulus.md) and [bulk modulus](../../../../../bulk-modulus.md) require

$$
\boxed{\sigma<\frac12.}
$$

The incompressible limit is $\sigma\to1/2$ with $B\to\infty$ at finite $E$. If positivity of the [shear modulus](../../../../../shear-modulus.md) $G_s=E/[2(1+\sigma)]$ is also imposed, the full stable isotropic range is $-1<\sigma<1/2$. Negative [Poisson's ratio](../../../../../poisson-s-ratio.md) is not ruled out; the displayed bulk-modulus relation alone supplies the upper bound.
