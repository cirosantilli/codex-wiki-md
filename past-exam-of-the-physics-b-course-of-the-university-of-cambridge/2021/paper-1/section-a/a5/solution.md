<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

For the intended reversible [adiabatic process](../../../../../adiabatic-process.md), an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) obeys $TV^{\gamma-1}=\text{constant}$. A [monatomic gas](../../../../../monatomic-gas.md) has [heat capacity ratio](../../../../../heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma=5/3$, so

$$
T_f=T_i\left(\frac{V_i}{V_f}\right)^{\gamma-1}
=400\left(\frac18\right)^{2/3},\qquad
\boxed{T_f=100\ \mathrm K.}
$$

The required extra assumption is that “adiabatic” denotes the usual quasistatic [isentropic process](../../../../../isentropic-process.md). Absence of heat transfer alone would not fix the final [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md): an [ideal gas](../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) undergoing [free expansion](../../../../../free-expansion.md) into vacuum is also adiabatic but leaves its [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) unchanged.
