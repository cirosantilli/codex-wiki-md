<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

For the intended ideal [Venturi effect](../../../../../venturi-effect.md), take [steady flow](../../../../../steady-flow.md), [incompressible flow](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md), negligible viscous losses, and the same height at the two stations shown. The [Bernoulli equation](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) along a [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md) gives

$$
p_1+\frac12\rho v_1^2=p_2+\frac12\rho v_2^2.
$$

Using water [mass density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho\simeq1000\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$,

$$
\boxed{p_2-p_1=\frac12(1000)(2^2-6^2)=-1.60\times10^4\ \mathrm{Pa}.}
$$

Thus the outlet [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) is lower by $16\ \mathrm{kPa}$. The increased [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) per unit volume comes from the drop in [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md).

The source's assumption of [laminar flow](../../../../../laminar-flow.md) alone does not imply negligible [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) or losses. The numerical result uses the usual lossless [Bernoulli equation](../../../../../bernoulli-equation.md) approximation, with the quoted speeds interpreted as representative streamline speeds. A real tube with significant viscous losses requires additional data; in a uniform-velocity energy balance its pressure drop would include an extra positive loss term.
