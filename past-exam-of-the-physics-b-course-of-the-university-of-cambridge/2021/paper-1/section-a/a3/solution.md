<h1 id="section-a/a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

Use local east, north and upward [unit vectors](../../../../../unit-vector.md) at the equator, with [Earth's rotation](../../../../../earth-s-rotation.md) $\boldsymbol\Omega=\Omega_E\widehat{\mathbf n}$. The parcel starts at rest relative to the ground. To first order in $\Omega_E$, its unperturbed vertical [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is $\dot z=-gt$ and its fall time from height $h$ is

$$
t_f=\sqrt{\frac{2h}{g}}.
$$

The [Coriolis acceleration](../../../../../coriolis-acceleration.md) is $-2\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf v$. Since north crossed with downward points west, its negative points east:

$$
\ddot x_E=-2\Omega_E\dot z=2\Omega_Egt.
$$

Integrating with $x_E(0)=\dot x_E(0)=0$ gives the [equatorial Coriolis deflection in free fall](../../../../../equatorial-coriolis-deflection-in-free-fall.md),

$$
x_E(t)=\frac13\Omega_Egt^3,\qquad
\boxed{x_E(t_f)=\frac{\Omega_E}{3}\frac{(2h)^{3/2}}{\sqrt g}.}
$$

For $h=200\ \mathrm m$, $g=9.81\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ and $\Omega_E=2\pi/(86164\ \mathrm s)=7.2921\times10^{-5}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$, $t_f=6.39\ \mathrm s$ and

$$
\boxed{\text{miss distance}\simeq0.0621\ \mathrm m=6.2\ \mathrm{cm},\quad\text{eastward}.}
$$

This neglects air resistance, variation of $g$ over the fall, and terms of order $\Omega_E^2$. The measured local $g$ already includes the background centrifugal correction. “Stationary” is interpreted as hovering above a fixed ground location, not being stationary in an inertial frame.
