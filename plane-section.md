# Plane section

↑ **Parent:** [Projective hypersurface](projective-hypersurface.md)

The [scheme-theoretic intersection](scheme-theoretic-intersection.md) of a [projective hypersurface](projective-hypersurface.md) with a projective plane. If the plane is not contained in the hypersurface, restricting the defining [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) gives a [projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md) of the same degree, with possible repeated components.
