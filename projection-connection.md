# Projection connection

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

A smooth self-adjoint [projection](projection-linear-algebra.md) $p:M\to M_N(\mathbb C)$ of locally constant rank defines the [Hermitian vector bundle](hermitian-vector-bundle.md) $\operatorname{im}p$ inside the trivial bundle. Projecting the flat derivative gives the [unitary connection](unitary-connection.md) $\nabla s=p\,ds$ for $ps=s$. Differentiating $p^2=p$ gives $p\,dp=dp(1-p)$ and $p\,dp\,p=0$. For a section $s$, $\nabla^2s=p\,dp\wedge ds=p(dp)^2s$, proving the curvature formula. Relative to $\operatorname{im}p\oplus\ker p$, $dp$ is off-diagonal and $(dp)^2$ is diagonal. Therefore $d\operatorname{tr}(p(dp)^{2k})=\operatorname{tr}(dp)^{2k+1}=0$. These are explicit closed representatives of the [Chern character](chern-character.md).
