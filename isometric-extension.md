# Isometric extension

↑ **Parent:** [Isometric embedding](isometric-embedding.md)

An [isometric extension](isometric-extension.md) of a connected metric manifold is a proper embedding onto an open subset of a larger connected manifold of the same dimension, preserving the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). Smooth complete connected [Riemannian manifolds](riemannian-manifold.md) admit no such proper extension; Lorentzian geodesic completeness has different subtleties.
