# Exterior harmonic potential with no flux through a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md)

An axisymmetric harmonic potential outside a sphere of radius $r_0$, with far field $v_0r\cos\theta$ and zero radial derivative on the sphere, is

$$
U=v_0(r+r_0^3/(2r^2))\cos\theta.
$$

The regular spherical expansion in [Legendre polynomials](legendre-polynomial.md) fixes all coefficients. A spatial constant remains arbitrary if only the far-field gradient is specified, and is fixed by prescribing the potential difference at infinity.
