# Stability of a numerical method

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

For a numerical evolution, stability means that perturbations of the initial data and forcing remain bounded over a fixed time interval, with a bound independent of the discretization parameters. For a linear homogeneous recurrence, a mesh-uniform bound on powers of its update [matrix](matrix.md) provides this estimate. [von Neumann stability analysis](von-neumann-stability-analysis.md) and the [energy method](energy-method.md) are two complementary ways to establish it.
