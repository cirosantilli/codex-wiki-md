# Piezoelectricity

↑ **Parent:** [Current-distance test of vacuum tunnelling](current-distance-test-of-vacuum-tunnelling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piezoelectricity)

The converse effect of [piezoelectricity](piezoelectricity.md) converts an applied [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) into a small mechanical deformation, permitting fine control of a [tunnel junction](tunnel-junction.md)'s separation.
