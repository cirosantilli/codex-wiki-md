# Chain rule for information entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Information entropy](information-entropy.md)

For discrete random variables,

$$
H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y\mid X).
$$

Iterating gives $H(X_1,\ldots,X_n)=\sum_iH(X_i\mid X_1,\ldots,X_{i-1})$.
