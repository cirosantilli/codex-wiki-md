# Queueing delay

↑ **Parent:** [Queue (queueing theory)](queue-queueing-theory.md)

The [queueing delay](queueing-delay.md) is the time between a customer's arrival and the start of its service, excluding its own [service time](service-time.md). Under [first come first served](first-come-first-served.md) with service rate $C$, its [queueing delay](queueing-delay.md) is the [queue workload](workload-of-a-queue.md) ahead of it divided by $C$. The [customer sojourn time](customer-sojourn-time.md) also includes the customer's own service. In a preemptive [priority queue with a shared buffer](priority-queue-with-a-shared-buffer.md), a voice customer's initial waiting time depends only on the voice [queue workload](workload-of-a-queue.md) already present.
