# Holomorphic need for two Higgs chiral doublets

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal supersymmetric Standard Model](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md)

A renormalizable [MSSM](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md) [superpotential](superpotential.md) is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) of [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md) and cannot use their conjugates. Gauge-invariant up-type [Yukawa couplings](yukawa-interaction.md) require $H_u$ of [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) $+1/2$, while down-type and charged-lepton [Yukawa couplings](yukawa-interaction.md) require $H_d$ of [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) $-1/2$. A single doublet and its conjugate cannot play both roles in the [superpotential](superpotential.md). This is distinct from [higgsino anomaly cancellation](higgsino-anomaly-cancellation.md).
