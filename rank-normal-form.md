# Rank normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix equivalence](matrix-equivalence.md)

Every matrix of rank $r$ is equivalent to a block matrix with $I_r$ in its upper-left corner and zeros elsewhere. This follows by choosing bases adapted to the kernel and image of the represented [linear map](linear-map.md).
