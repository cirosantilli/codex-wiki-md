# Practical number

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)

A [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$ such that every [positive integer](positive-integer.md) at most $n$ is a sum of distinct [divisors](divisor.md) of $n$. For example, $6$ is practical because its [divisors](divisor.md) $1,2,3,6$ represent every value from $1$ through $6$.
