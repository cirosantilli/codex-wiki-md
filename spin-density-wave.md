# Spin density wave

↑ **Parent:** [Condensed matter physics](condensed-matter-physics.md)

A spatially modulated spin density, distinct from a [charge density wave](charge-density-wave.md). Opposite modulations of spin-up and spin-down occupations can give a periodically varying [magnetization](magnetization.md) while their total [charge density](charge-density.md) stays uniform.
