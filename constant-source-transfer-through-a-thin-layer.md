# Constant source transfer through a thin layer

↑ **Parent:** [Formal solution of the radiative transfer equation](formal-solution-of-the-radiative-transfer-equation.md)

Along a radial ray through a layer with constant [source function](radiative-transfer-source-function.md), the incident [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) is attenuated by $e^{-\tau}$ and the layer adds $S(1-e^{-\tau})$. At small [optical depth](optical-depth.md), $I_{\rm out}=I_{\rm in}+(S-I_{\rm in})\tau+O(\tau^2)$. Whether the source exceeds the incident [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) determines the first-order emission or absorption contrast.
