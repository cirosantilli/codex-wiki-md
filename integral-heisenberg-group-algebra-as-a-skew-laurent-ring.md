# Integral Heisenberg group algebra as a skew Laurent ring

↑ **Parent:** [Skew Laurent polynomial ring](skew-laurent-polynomial-ring.md)

For the [Integer Heisenberg group](integer-heisenberg-group.md), choose $x=I+E_{12}$, $y=I+E_{23}$ and central $z=I+E_{13}$. Every element has a unique form $y^cz^bx^a$, and $xy=zyx$. The [group algebra](group-algebra.md) is therefore the displayed [skew Laurent polynomial ring](skew-laurent-polynomial-ring.md), with $\sigma(z)=z$. The base is a commutative [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md) and an [integral domain](integral-domain.md); the [skew Hilbert basis theorem](skew-hilbert-basis-theorem.md) gives both-sided Noetherianity and the [leading coefficient](leading-coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md) of a product proves that there are no zero divisors.
