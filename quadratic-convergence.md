# Quadratic convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence of a numerical method](convergence-of-a-numerical-method.md)

An iteration converges at least quadratically to a limit if its errors eventually satisfy the displayed bound with a fixed finite $C>0$. If $Ce_N<1$, induction gives $|e_{N+k}|\le C^{-1}(C|e_N|)^{2^k}$. Exact asymptotic quadratic convergence has $|e_{n+1}|/|e_n|^2\to c\in(0,\infty)$. [Newton root-finding iteration](newton-root-finding-iteration.md) at a simple scalar root has this order when the second derivative at the root is nonzero; if that derivative vanishes, convergence can be faster.
