# Integrated Compton parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Compton y parameter](compton-y-parameter.md)

For a source at [angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md) $D_A$,

$$
Y=\frac{\sigma_T}{m_ec^2D_A^2}\int P_e\,dV.
$$

The quantity $YD_A^2$ measures volume-integrated [electron](electron.md) [pressure](pressure-split.md), hence thermal [energy](energy-split.md) up to a known ideal-gas factor. The redshift independence of the thermal SZ distortion's [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) does not make $Y$ distance independent. Angular integration region, beam convolution, [temperature](temperature-split.md) structure and cluster geometry matter when comparing measurements and mass proxies.
