# Graph exhaustion

↑ **Parent:** [Locally finite graph](locally-finite-graph.md)

An exhaustion of a countable [graph](graph-split.md) is an increasing [sequence](sequence.md) of finite connected [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) sets whose union is the whole [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) set. In a [locally finite graph](locally-finite-graph.md), every prescribed finite [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) together with all [graph neighbours](neighbour-of-a-vertex.md) of its visited [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) lies in a sufficiently large exhaustion set. This permits coupling finite-volume and infinite-volume [simple random walks](simple-random-walk.md) up to a finite stopping time.
