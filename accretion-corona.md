# Accretion corona

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion disk](accretion-disk.md)

A hot, comparatively optically thin [plasma](plasma-physics.md) region associated with an [accretion](accretion.md) flow. Thermal or nonthermal electrons can transfer [energy](energy-split.md) to softer seed [photons](photon.md) through [inverse Compton scattering](inverse-compton-scattering.md), producing a harder radiation component. Its geometry, heating and coupling to the cooler disc are model dependent; a hard [radiation spectrum](radiation-spectrum.md) alone does not uniquely determine these properties.
