# Completed partially directed acyclic graph

↑ **Parent:** [Markov equivalence of directed acyclic graphs](markov-equivalence-of-directed-acyclic-graphs.md)

The completed partially directed acyclic graph of a [Markov equivalence of directed acyclic graphs](markov-equivalence-of-directed-acyclic-graphs.md) class has their common [skeleton of a directed graph](skeleton-of-a-directed-graph.md). An edge is directed exactly when its orientation agrees in every member of the class, and is otherwise undirected. It can be constructed by enumerating all acyclic orientations with the prescribed [unshielded colliders](unshielded-collider.md) and retaining only the common directions; practical [PC algorithms](pc-algorithm.md) use orientation propagation instead of enumeration.
