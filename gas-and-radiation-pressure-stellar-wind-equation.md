# Gas and radiation pressure stellar wind equation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar wind](stellar-wind.md)

In an optically thick steady spherical [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md) of constant [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md) $\mu_{\rm mol}$, let $v$ be the outward speed and $a_g^2=k_BT/(\mu_{\rm mol}m_u)$ the [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md) squared. Mass conservation gives $\rho'/\rho=-2/r-v'/v$, where a prime denotes differentiation with respect to radius $r$. Use $P_{\rm gas}=\rho a_g^2$ and [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) to write $P_{\rm rad}'/\rho=-g\Gamma$, where $g=GM_r/r^2$ and $\Gamma=L_r/L_{\rm Edd}$ uses the local [Eddington luminosity](eddington-luminosity.md) $L_{\rm Edd}=4\pi cGM_r/\kappa$ and [opacity](opacity.md) $\kappa$. Substituting into the momentum equation yields the displayed wind equation. Smooth [sonic point](sonic-point.md) crossing requires both its denominator and numerator to vanish. The radiation-pressure gradient already supplies radiative acceleration; adding that force separately would count it twice.
