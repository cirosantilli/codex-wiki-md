# Integer factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integer_factorization)

Finding the [prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) of a positive integer. It is distinct from [primality testing](primality-testing.md): deciding that an integer is composite does not itself supply its factors. For a semiprime, knowledge of a nontrivial square root of unity gives a factor through a [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) calculation.
