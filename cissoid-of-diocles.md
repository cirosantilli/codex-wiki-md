# Cissoid of Diocles

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cissoid_of_Diocles)

For $a>0$, this [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) can be constructed using a [circle](circle.md) of [radius](radius.md) $a$ centred at $(a,0)$ and its [tangent line](tangent-line.md) $x=2a$. On a ray from the origin, subtract the distance to its nonzero circle intersection from the distance to the line. The resulting radial distance is $r=2a\sin^2\theta/\cos\theta$, for $-\pi/2<\theta<\pi/2$. Thus a [parametric curve](parametric-curve.md) for it is

$$
x=\frac{2at^2}{1+t^2},\qquad y=\frac{2at^3}{1+t^2},\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
$$

Eliminating $t$ gives the displayed cubic equation. It has a [cusp](cusp-algebraic-geometry.md) at the origin, horizontal tangent direction there, and vertical [asymptote](asymptote.md) $x=2a$. Its name honours [Diocles](diocles-mathematician.md).
