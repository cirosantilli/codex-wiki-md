<h1 id="9/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under the usual piecewise smoothness and exponential-order conditions, [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) in the [Laplace transform](../../../../laplace-transform.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L[x'](p)=p\bar x(p)-x(0).}
$$

The boundary term at infinity vanishes in the transform's half-plane of convergence. The value at zero supplies the initial-data term.
