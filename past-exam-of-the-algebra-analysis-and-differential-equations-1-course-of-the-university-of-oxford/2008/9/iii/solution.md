<h1 id="9/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Using $\sin t=(e^{it}-e^{-it})/(2i)$ and integrating the two exponentials for $\operatorname{Re}p>0$,

$$
\mathcal L[\sin t](p)=\frac1{2i}
\left(\frac1{p-i}-\frac1{p+i}\right)
=\boxed{\frac1{p^2+1}.}
$$

This also follows directly by two integrations by parts in the [Laplace transform](../../../../laplace-transform.md).
