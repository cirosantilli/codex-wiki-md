<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [self-adjoint operator](../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) on a finite-dimensional complex [inner product space](../../../../inner-product-space.md), every eigenvalue is real and eigenspaces for distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal. The [spectral theorem](../../../../spectral-theorem.md) gives an orthonormal eigenbasis. Accordingly

$$
\boxed{V=V_1\mathbin{\perp}\cdots\mathbin{\perp}V_r,}
$$

and the $E_i$ are orthogonal projections satisfying $E_i^*=E_i$. Since $T$ is nonsingular, none of its eigenvalues is zero. Its representing matrix in the orthonormal eigenbasis is real diagonal.
