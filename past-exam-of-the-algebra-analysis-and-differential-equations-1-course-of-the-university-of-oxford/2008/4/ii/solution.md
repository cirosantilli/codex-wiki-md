<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The reciprocal map is holomorphic for $z\ne0$, and the [complex exponential](../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) is entire. Its denominator vanishes exactly when

$$
e^{2\pi i/z}=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad z^{-1}\in\mathbb Z.
$$

All these points are excluded from $U$. The set $U$ is open: it is the inverse image of $\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Z$ under the reciprocal map on $\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}$. A reciprocal of a nonvanishing holomorphic function is holomorphic. Therefore **$f$ is holomorphic throughout $U$**.
