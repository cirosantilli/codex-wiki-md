<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $w=1/(z+1)$ and invert to $z=1/w-1$. The [pole](../../../../pole.md) $z=-1$ lies on the excluded boundary, so it is not a point of the original region. For $w\neq0$ the first inequality becomes

$$
|z|>1
\iff|1-w|>|w|
\iff1-2\operatorname{Re}w>0.
$$

The second becomes

$$
|z-1|<2
\iff|1-2w|<2|w|
\iff1-4\operatorname{Re}w<0.
$$

Thus the image is contained in the vertical strip $1/4<\operatorname{Re}w<1/2$. Conversely every point in that strip is nonzero; its inverse satisfies both original inequalities, so no points are missing. Therefore

$$
\boxed{w(\mathcal R_1)=\{w:\tfrac14<\operatorname{Re}w<\tfrac12\}.}
$$

The two circles pass through the [pole](../../../../pole.md) of the [Möbius transformation](../../../../mobius-transformation.md), which is why their images are straight lines. Direct inequality transformation fixes which side of each line is required.
