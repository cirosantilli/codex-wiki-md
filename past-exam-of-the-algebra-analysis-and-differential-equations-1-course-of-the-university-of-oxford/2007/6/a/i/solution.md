<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Parametrize the vertical line as $z=a+it$, $t\in\mathbb R$. Then $e^z=e^a(\cos t+i\sin t)$, whose modulus is $e^a$ and whose argument takes every value. The image is

$$
\boxed{\{w:|w|=e^a\}.}
$$

Increasing $t$ traverses the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) counterclockwise, repeating with period $2\pi$. There is no injectivity on the full line.
