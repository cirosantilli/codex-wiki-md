<h1 id="6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Parametrize the horizontal line as $z=t+ib$, $t\in\mathbb R$. Then $e^z=e^t e^{ib}$, and $e^t$ takes every positive real value exactly once. The image is

$$
\boxed{\{re^{ib}:r>0\}.}
$$

This is the [ray](../../../../../ray-geometry.md) with argument $b$ modulo $2\pi$, excluding its endpoint zero. As $t$ increases, the image moves outward from arbitrarily small to arbitrarily large radius.
