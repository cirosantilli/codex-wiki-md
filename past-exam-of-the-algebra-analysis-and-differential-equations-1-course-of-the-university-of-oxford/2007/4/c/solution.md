<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Because the image is integer-valued, the open interval $(n-\tfrac12,n+\tfrac12)$ contains exactly one possible image value, namely $n$. Thus

$$
f^{-1}(\{n\})=f^{-1}\bigl((n-\tfrac12,n+\tfrac12)\bigr).
$$

The right side is open in $A$ by [continuity](../../../../continuous-function.md), so

$$
\boxed{f^{-1}(\{n\})\text{ is open in }A.}
$$

This includes the possibility of an empty [fiber](../../../../fiber-of-a-function.md). The source notation $f^{-1}(n)$ is understood as the [preimage](../../../../preimage.md) of the singleton $\{n\}$.
