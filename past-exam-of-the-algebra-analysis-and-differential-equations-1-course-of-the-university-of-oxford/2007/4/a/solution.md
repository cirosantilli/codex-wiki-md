<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The topology on $A$ is the [subspace topology](../../../../subspace-topology.md) inherited from the [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md). A subset $B$ is open in $A$ if $B=A\cap U$ for some [open subset](../../../../open-set.md) $U\subseteq\mathbb C$. Equivalently, every $b\in B$ has a radius $\epsilon>0$ such that $A\cap\{z:|z-b|<\epsilon\}\subseteq B$.

A subset $B$ is closed in $A$ if $A\setminus B$ is open in $A$, equivalently if $B=A\cap F$ for a [closed subset](../../../../closed-set.md) $F$ of the [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md). These are relative properties: the same subset need not be open or closed in the ambient plane.
