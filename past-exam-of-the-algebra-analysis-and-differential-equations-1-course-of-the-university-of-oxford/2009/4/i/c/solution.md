For [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) in $\mathbb C$, the answers are **$X_1$: no; $X_2$: no; $X_3$: yes**. Every complex disk centered at a real point contains nonreal points, so a nonempty subset of the real axis cannot be open in the complex plane. The empty set remains open.
