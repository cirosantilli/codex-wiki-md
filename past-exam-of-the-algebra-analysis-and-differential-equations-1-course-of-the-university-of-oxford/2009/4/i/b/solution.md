For [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) in $\mathbb R$, the answers are **$X_1$: yes; $X_2$: no; $X_3$: yes**. Every point of the positive ray has a small interval contained in it, while zero is not an interior point of the nonnegative ray. The empty set is open by the topology axioms.
