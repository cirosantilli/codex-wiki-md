↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**Yes.** In $\mathbb F_7$, the [minimal polynomial](../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) splits as

$$
X^3-1=(X-1)(X-2)(X-4).
$$

The three factors are distinct, so the [minimal polynomial criterion for diagonalizability](../../../../diagonalizability-criterion-using-the-minimal-polynomial.md) applies. For example the [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md) $v_\lambda=(1,\lambda^2,\lambda)^{\mathsf T}$ for $\lambda=1,2,4$ give an [eigenbasis](../../../../eigenbasis.md) over this [finite field](../../../../finite-field.md). The contrast with characteristic three is that the shift polynomial now splits without repeated roots.
