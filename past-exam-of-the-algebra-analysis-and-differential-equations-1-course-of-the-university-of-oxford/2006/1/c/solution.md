**No.** In [characteristic three](../../../../characteristic-three.md), $X^3-1=(X-1)^3$. This is still the [minimal polynomial](../../../../minimal-polynomial.md), because the three cyclic [basis vectors](../../../../basis-vector.md) remain independent. Its repeated factor violates the [minimal polynomial criterion for diagonalizability](../../../../diagonalizability-criterion-using-the-minimal-polynomial.md). Equivalently, the only possible [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) is one; a diagonalizable matrix with that sole [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) would be the [identity matrix](../../../../identity-matrix.md), whereas this matrix is not the identity.
