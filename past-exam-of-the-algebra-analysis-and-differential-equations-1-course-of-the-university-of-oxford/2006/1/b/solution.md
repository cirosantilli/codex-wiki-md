**No.** The same cyclic-vector argument gives [minimal polynomial](../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) $X^3-1=(X-1)(X^2+X+1)$ over the [finite field](../../../../finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_2$. The quadratic has no root in $\mathbb F_2$: its value is one at both zero and one. Thus the [minimal polynomial](../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) does not split into linear factors over the specified [field](../../../../field.md), violating the [minimal polynomial criterion for diagonalizability](../../../../diagonalizability-criterion-using-the-minimal-polynomial.md). It becomes diagonalizable over a suitable larger [finite field](../../../../finite-field.md), but that is a different question.
