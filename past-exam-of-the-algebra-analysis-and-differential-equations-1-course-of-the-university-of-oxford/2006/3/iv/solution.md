Here $J_4=(X^2+1)$ is a [principal ideal](../../../../principal-ideal.md). The [evaluation homomorphism](../../../../evaluation-homomorphism.md) $p\mapsto p(i)$ from $\mathbb R[X]$ onto $\mathbb C$ has this [kernel of a ring homomorphism](../../../../kernel-of-a-ring-homomorphism.md): division by $X^2+1$ leaves a remainder $a+bX$, and $a+bi=0$ with real $a,b$ forces both to vanish. Surjectivity follows because every complex number is $a+bi$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbb R[X]/J_4\cong\mathbb C,\qquad J_4\text{ is maximal}.}
$$

Equivalently, $X^2+1$ is an [irreducible polynomial](../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) over the [real numbers](../../../../real-number.md).
