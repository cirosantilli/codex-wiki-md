<h1 id="3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [evaluation homomorphism](../../../../evaluation-homomorphism.md) $\mathbb R[X]\to\mathbb R$, $p\mapsto p(0)$, is surjective and has [kernel of a ring homomorphism](../../../../kernel-of-a-ring-homomorphism.md) $J_1=(X)$. Hence $J_1$ is an [ideal](../../../../ideal.md), and the [first isomorphism theorem for rings](../../../../first-isomorphism-theorem-for-rings.md) gives $\boxed{\mathbb R[X]/J_1\cong\mathbb R}$. Since this quotient is a [field](../../../../field.md), $\boxed{J_1\text{ is maximal}}$.
