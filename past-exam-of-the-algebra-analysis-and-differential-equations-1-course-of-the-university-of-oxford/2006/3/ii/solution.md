<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**It is not an ideal.** The constant [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) one belongs to $J_2$, but its product with $X$ does not: the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) of $X$ at zero is one. Thus $J_2$ fails closure under multiplication by arbitrary elements of $\mathbb R[X]$, a required [ideal](../../../../ideal.md) property. Closure under addition alone would not suffice.
