<h1 id="4/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The rational cut is **both open and closed in $\mathbb Q$**. It is relatively open as $\mathbb Q\cap(-\infty,\sqrt2)$. Since $\sqrt2$ is irrational, its relative complement is $\mathbb Q\cap(\sqrt2,\infty)$, also relatively open. Thus this cut is a [clopen set](../../../../../clopen-set.md) in the [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) of the rationals.
