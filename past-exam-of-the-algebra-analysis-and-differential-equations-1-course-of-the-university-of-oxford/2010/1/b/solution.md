↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the adapted basis from part (a), the matrix has $r$ diagonal entries equal to one and $N-r$ equal to zero. With the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) convention $\chi_E(X)=\det(XI-E)$,

$$
\boxed{\chi_E(X)=X^{N-r}(X-1)^r.}
$$

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is independent of the choice of basis.
