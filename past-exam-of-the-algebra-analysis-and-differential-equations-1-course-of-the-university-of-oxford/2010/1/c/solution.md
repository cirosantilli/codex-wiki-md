<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $Ev=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$, applying $E^2=E$ gives $\lambda^2v=\lambda v$, hence $\lambda\in\{0,1\}$. The kernel is nonzero since $E\ne I$, so zero always occurs. One occurs exactly when the image is nonzero. Thus the [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) set is **$\{0\}$ if $E=0$, and $\{0,1\}$ otherwise**. Their algebraic multiplicities are $N-r$ and $r$ respectively.
