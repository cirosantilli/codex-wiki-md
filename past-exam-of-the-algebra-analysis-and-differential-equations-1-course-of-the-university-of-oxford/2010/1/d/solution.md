<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The polynomial $X(X-1)$ annihilates $E$ by idempotence, so the [minimal polynomial](../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) divides it. If $E=0$, its monic polynomial of smallest positive degree is $X$. If $E\ne0$, both eigenvalues zero and one occur, so both factors must divide every annihilating polynomial. Therefore

$$
\boxed{m_E(X)=\begin{cases}X,&E=0,\\X(X-1),&E\ne0\quad(E\ne I).\end{cases}}
$$
