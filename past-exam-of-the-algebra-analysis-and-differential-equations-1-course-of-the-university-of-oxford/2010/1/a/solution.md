<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Every vector decomposes as $v=Ev+(v-Ev)$, where $Ev\in\operatorname{im}E$ and $E(v-Ev)=0$. If $w$ lies in both the image and kernel, write $w=Eu$ to obtain $w=E^2u=Ew=0$. Thus

$$
V=\operatorname{im}E\oplus\ker E,
$$

and $E$ is the [projection](../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) onto its [image](../../../../image-of-a-function.md) along its [kernel](../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). It is the identity on its image and zero on its kernel. Let $N=\dim V$ and $r=\operatorname{rank}E$. A basis adapted to this [direct sum](../../../../direct-sum.md) gives matrix $\operatorname{diag}(I_r,0_{N-r})$. Since $E$ is not the identity, $N-r>0$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{\det E=0.}
$$
