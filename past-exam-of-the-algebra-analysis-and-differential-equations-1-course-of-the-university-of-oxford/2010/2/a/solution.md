<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For real scalars $\alpha,\beta$ and matrices $A,C,B$, distributivity of [matrix multiplication](../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) and linearity of the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) give

$$
\varphi(\alpha A+\beta C,B)
=\operatorname{tr}(\alpha AB+\beta CB)
=\alpha\varphi(A,B)+\beta\varphi(C,B).
$$

Thus **$\varphi$ is linear in its first argument**.
