<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $E_{ji}$ be the [matrix unit](../../../../matrix-unit.md) having its only nonzero entry, one, in position $(j,i)$. Direct multiplication gives $\operatorname{tr}(AE_{ji})=a_{ij}$. If $A\in R$, every such pairing vanishes, forcing every entry $a_{ij}$ to be zero. Conversely, zero pairs to zero with every matrix. Thus

$$
\boxed{R=\{0\}.}
$$

The [matrix trace pairing](../../../../matrix-trace-pairing.md) is therefore a [nondegenerate bilinear form](../../../../nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md), despite its failure to be positive definite when $n\geq2$.
