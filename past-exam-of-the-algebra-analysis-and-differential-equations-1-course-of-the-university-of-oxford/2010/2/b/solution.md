<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expand the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) by matrix entries:

$$
\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\sum_i\sum_j a_{ij}b_{ji}
=\sum_j\sum_i b_{ji}a_{ij}=\operatorname{tr}(BA).
$$

Hence **$\varphi(A,B)=\varphi(B,A)$**. Combined with part (a), this makes $\varphi$ a [symmetric bilinear form](../../../../symmetric-bilinear-form.md).
