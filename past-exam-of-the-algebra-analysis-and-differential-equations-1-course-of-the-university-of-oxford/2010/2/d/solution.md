<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Interpret diagonalizability over the specified underlying field $\mathbb R$. Write $A=P\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)P^{-1}$ with real eigenvalues. Invariance of the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) under [similarity](../../../../matrix-similarity.md) gives

$$
\varphi(A,A)=\operatorname{tr}(A^2)=\sum_i\lambda_i^2.
$$

If $A\ne0$ is diagonalizable, at least one eigenvalue is nonzero, proving **$\varphi(A,A)>0$**. Real diagonalizability is essential: the rotation block from part (c) is diagonalizable over $\mathbb C$ but has eigenvalues $i,-i$ and negative trace of its square.
