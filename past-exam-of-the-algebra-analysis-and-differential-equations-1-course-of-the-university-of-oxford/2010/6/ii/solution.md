<h1 id="6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the circle, the integrand in the [Cauchy derivative formula](../../../../cauchy-derivative-formula.md) has modulus at most $M/r^{k+1}$, and the circle has length $2\pi r$. The [ML inequality](../../../../estimation-lemma.md) therefore gives

$$
|f^{(k)}(z_0)|\leq\frac{k!}{2\pi}(2\pi r)\frac M{r^{k+1}}
=\boxed{\frac{k!M}{r^k}.}
$$

The maximum $M$ exists because the modulus of the continuous function $f$ attains a maximum on the compact circle. These are the [Cauchy estimate](../../../../cauchy-estimate.md).
