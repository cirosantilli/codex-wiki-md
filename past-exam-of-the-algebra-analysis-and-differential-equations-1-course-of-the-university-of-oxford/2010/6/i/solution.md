<h1 id="6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the positively oriented circle $C=\{z:|z-z_0|=r\}$, the [Cauchy derivative formula](../../../../cauchy-derivative-formula.md) states

$$
\boxed{f^{(k)}(z_0)=\frac{k!}{2\pi i}\int_C\frac{f(z)}{(z-z_0)^{k+1}}dz,\qquad k=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$

The hypothesis that the closed disk lies in the open holomorphy domain ensures the formula applies on and inside this circle.
