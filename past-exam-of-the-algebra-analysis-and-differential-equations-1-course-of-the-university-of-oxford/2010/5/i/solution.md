<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [complex line integral](../../../../contour-integral.md) along the smooth parametrized curve is

$$
\int_\gamma f(z)dz:=\int_a^b f(\gamma(t))\gamma'(t)dt.
$$

For the reversed curve, the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\widetilde\gamma'(t)=-\gamma'(a+b-t)$. Substitution $s=a+b-t$ yields

$$
\int_{\widetilde\gamma}f(z)dz
=-\int_a^b f(\gamma(a+b-t))\gamma'(a+b-t)dt
=-\int_a^b f(\gamma(s))\gamma'(s)ds.
$$

Consequently **reversing orientation negates the complex line integral**.
