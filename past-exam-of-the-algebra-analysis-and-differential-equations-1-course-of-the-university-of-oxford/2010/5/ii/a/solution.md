<h1 id="5/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An entire [antiderivative](../../../../../antiderivative.md) is $F(z)=z\sin z+\cos z$, since differentiation cancels the sine terms and leaves $F'(z)=z\cos z$. The curve runs from $-i$ to $i$. Both terms of $F$ are even, so $F(i)=F(-i)$, and the [complex line integral](../../../../../contour-integral.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int_\gamma z\cos z\,dz=0.}
$$
