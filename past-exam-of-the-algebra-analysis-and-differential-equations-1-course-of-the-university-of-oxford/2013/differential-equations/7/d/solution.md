<h1 id="differential-equations/7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [Lipschitz condition](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) here is uniform in the independent variable: there is a finite constant $L\geq0$ such that, whenever both $(x,Z)$ and $(x,W)$ belong to the cube,

$$
\boxed{\|F(x,Z)-F(x,W)\|_\infty\leq L\|Z-W\|_\infty.}
$$

The same $L$ must work for every allowed $x,Z,W$. Part (c) proves this with $L=1$. This controls changes in the dependent variables; no Lipschitz condition in $x$ is required for the [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](../../../../../picard-lindelof-theorem.md).
