<h1 id="differential-equations/7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Collect the two dependent variables in a column vector. The [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) and initial vector are

$$
\boxed{F(x,Z)=\begin{pmatrix}(|z_2|+x^2+1)^{-1}\\(|z_1|+1)^{-1}\end{pmatrix},
\qquad B=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The given [system of ordinary differential equations](../../../../../system-of-ordinary-differential-equations.md) is then $Y'=F(x,Y)$ with $Y(0)=B$. Both components of $F$ are continuous on all of $\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^2$, since their denominators are at least one.
