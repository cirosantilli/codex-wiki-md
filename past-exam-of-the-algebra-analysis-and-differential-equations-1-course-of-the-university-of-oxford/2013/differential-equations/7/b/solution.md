<h1 id="differential-equations/7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Each denominator is at least one and positive, so each component of $F$ lies in $(0,1]$. Taking the [maximum norm](../../../../../supremum-norm.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\|F(x,Z)\|_\infty=\max\!\left\{\frac1{|z_2|+x^2+1},\frac1{|z_1|+1}\right\}\leq1.}
$$

This is a global bound, so in particular the constant $M=1$ is valid throughout the specified cube.
