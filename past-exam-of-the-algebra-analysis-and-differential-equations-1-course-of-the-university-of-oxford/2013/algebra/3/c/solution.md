<h1 id="algebra/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [Gaussian integers](../../../../../gaussian-integer.md), use the [Gaussian norm](../../../../../gaussian-integer-norm.md)

$$
\boxed{\delta(a+bi)=a^2+b^2\quad(a+bi\neq0).}
$$

To identify the [ideal](../../../../../ideal.md), note that $a^2+b^2$ is even if and only if $a,b$ have the same parity. Also

$$
\frac{a+bi}{1+i}=\frac{a+b}{2}+\frac{b-a}{2}i.
$$

This quotient is a [Gaussian integer](../../../../../gaussian-integer.md) precisely when $a,b$ have the same parity. Therefore the prescribed set consists exactly of the multiples of $1+i$, giving

$$
\boxed{I=\mathbb Z[i](1+i),\qquad d=1+i.}
$$

Any associate of $1+i$ would generate the same [principal ideal](../../../../../principal-ideal.md).
