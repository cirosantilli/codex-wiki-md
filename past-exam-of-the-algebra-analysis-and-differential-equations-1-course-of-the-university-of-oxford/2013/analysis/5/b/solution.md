<h1 id="analysis/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose the continuous argument with values in $(-\pi/2,3\pi/2)$ and define

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Log}z=\log|z|+i\operatorname{Arg}z,\qquad -\frac\pi2<\operatorname{Arg}z<\frac{3\pi}2.}
$$

Its [branch cut](../../../../../branch-cut.md) is the nonpositive imaginary axis, including zero, exactly the removed ray. Locally away from that ray, the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) or the local inverse of the exponential show that it is a [holomorphic logarithm](../../../../../holomorphic-logarithm.md), with [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $1/z$ and $e^{\operatorname{Log}z}=z$. The chosen interval fixes the additive ambiguity by integer multiples of $2\pi i$.
