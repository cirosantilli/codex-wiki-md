<h1 id="analysis/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a piecewise continuously differentiable [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) $\gamma:[a,b]\to U$, the [contour integral](../../../../../contour-integral.md) is defined by

$$
\boxed{\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz=\int_a^b f(\gamma(t))\gamma'(t)\,dt.}
$$

If the contour has corners, integrate on its finitely many differentiable pieces and sum the results. The integral on the right is taken componentwise as an ordinary real-parameter integral of a complex-valued function. The factor $\gamma'(t)$ encodes both the speed and orientation of the [contour](../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md).
