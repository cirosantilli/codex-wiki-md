<h1 id="analysis/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $z=x+iy$. Both moduli are nonnegative, so squaring preserves their strict inequality:

$$
|z|<|z-i|\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x^2+y^2<x^2+(y-1)^2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 2y<1.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{c=\frac12.}
$$

The boundary is the perpendicular bisector of $0$ and $i$ in the [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md); the strict inequality selects the half-plane containing $0$.
