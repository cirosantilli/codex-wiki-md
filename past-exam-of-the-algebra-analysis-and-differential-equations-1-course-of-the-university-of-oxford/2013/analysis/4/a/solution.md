<h1 id="analysis/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under the assumed continuous real differentiability, the necessary and sufficient conditions are the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) at every point of $U$:

$$
\boxed{u_x=v_y,\qquad u_y=-v_x.}
$$

Necessity follows by computing the complex [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) along real and imaginary increments. For sufficiency, real differentiability gives the first-order expansion

$$
f(z+h)-f(z)=(u_x+iv_x)\operatorname{Re}h+(u_y+iv_y)\operatorname{Im}h+o(|h|).
$$

The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) turn the linear part into $(u_x+iv_x)h$, so the complex [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) exists and equals $u_x+iv_x$. Thus $f$ is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on $U$.
