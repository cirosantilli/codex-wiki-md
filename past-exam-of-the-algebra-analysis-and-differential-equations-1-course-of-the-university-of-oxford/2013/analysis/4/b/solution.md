<h1 id="analysis/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At any $z\neq0$, take sufficiently small $h$ so $z+h\neq0$. The complex difference quotient is

$$
\frac{(z+h)^{-1}-z^{-1}}h=-\frac1{z(z+h)}\longrightarrow-\frac1{z^2}.
$$

Hence the complex [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) exists at every point of the punctured plane, and

$$
\boxed{f'(z)=-z^{-2},\qquad f\text{ is holomorphic on }\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}.}
$$

The excluded point is essential to the domain statement: the reciprocal has a [simple pole](../../../../../simple-pole.md) there.
