<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take the unit circle $\gamma(t)=e^{it}$, $0\le t\le2\pi$, in the [punctured complex plane](../../../../punctured-complex-plane.md). Direct parameter integration gives

$$
\int_\gamma\frac{dz}{z}=\int_0^{2\pi}e^{-it}ie^{it}\,dt=2\pi i\ne0.
$$

A global [holomorphic antiderivative](../../../../holomorphic-primitive.md) would make this integral zero by the previous part. Thus **there is no such antiderivative on the punctured plane**. Local primitives can exist without combining into one single-valued function on this domain.
