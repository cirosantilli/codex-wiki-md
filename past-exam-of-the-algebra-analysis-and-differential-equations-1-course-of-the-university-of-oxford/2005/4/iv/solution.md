<h1 id="4/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

If a [holomorphic function](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) $g$ on the [punctured complex plane](../../../../punctured-complex-plane.md) satisfied $e^{g(z)}=z$, the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) would give $e^{g(z)}g'(z)=1$, hence $g'(z)=1/z$. This contradicts the previous part. Therefore **there is no global holomorphic logarithm on the punctured plane**. A [branch of the complex logarithm](../../../../branch-of-the-complex-logarithm.md) requires a suitable smaller domain.
