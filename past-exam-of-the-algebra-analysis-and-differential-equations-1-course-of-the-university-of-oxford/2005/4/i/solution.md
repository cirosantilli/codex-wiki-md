↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [smooth curve](../../../../smooth-curve.md) $\gamma:[a,b]\to\mathbb C$ and continuous $f$ on its image, the [contour integral](../../../../contour-integral.md) is defined by

$$
\boxed{\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz=\int_a^b f(\gamma(t))\gamma'(t)\,dt.}
$$

The derivative carries the direction and speed of traversal; the complex-valued real-parameter integral is defined by integrating its real and imaginary parts.
