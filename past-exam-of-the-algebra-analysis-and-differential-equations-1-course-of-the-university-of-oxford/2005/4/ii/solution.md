<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $dF(\gamma(t))/dt=F'(\gamma(t))\gamma'(t)=f(\gamma(t))\gamma'(t)$. The [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) therefore yields

$$
\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz=F(\gamma(b))-F(\gamma(a))=\boxed0,
$$

because the [closed curve](../../../../closed-curve.md) has equal endpoints. The existence of a single-valued [holomorphic antiderivative](../../../../holomorphic-primitive.md) on the whole neighborhood is the decisive hypothesis.
