↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the [subspace topology](../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $S$, a subset $A$ is [open](../../../../open-set.md) in $S$ precisely when $A=S\cap O$ for some [open set](../../../../open-set.md) $O\subseteq\mathbb C$. A subset $B$ is [closed](../../../../closed-set.md) in $S$ precisely when $S\setminus B$ is open in $S$, equivalently $B=S\cap F$ for some [closed set](../../../../closed-set.md) $F\subseteq\mathbb C$.

A [compact space](../../../../compact-space.md) is one for which every [open cover](../../../../open-cover.md) has a finite [subcover](../../../../subcover.md). Here the cover may use relatively open subsets of $S$, or equivalently ambient open subsets covering it. The [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md) states that

$$
\boxed{S\subseteq\mathbb C\text{ is compact}\iff S\text{ is closed and bounded}.}
$$

Closedness and boundedness here refer to the ambient [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md), not merely closedness in $S$ itself.
