<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The disc is a [closed set](../../../../closed-set.md) and is bounded in the [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md), so it is **compact** by the [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md). It is [convex](../../../../convex-function.md): the [triangle inequality](../../../../triangle-inequality.md) gives $|(1-t)z_0+tz_1|\leq2$ for all $t\in[0,1]$ when both endpoints lie in it. Straight segments therefore supply paths, so it is [path-connected](../../../../path-connected-space.md) and hence **connected**.
