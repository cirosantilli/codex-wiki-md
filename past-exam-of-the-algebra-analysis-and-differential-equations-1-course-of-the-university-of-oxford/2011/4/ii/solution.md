<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [connected space](../../../../connected-space.md) $S$ cannot be expressed as $S=A\cup B$ with $A,B$ disjoint nonempty relatively [open sets](../../../../open-set.md). Equivalently, the only subsets simultaneously relatively [open](../../../../open-set.md) and [closed](../../../../closed-set.md) are $\varnothing$ and $S$. A decomposition into such $A,B$ is a separation of the [topological space](../../../../topological-space.md).
