<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [integers](../../../../integer-split.md) are unbounded in the [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md), so the [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md) shows they are **not compact**. They are also **not connected**: $\{0\}$ and $\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$ are disjoint nonempty relatively [open sets](../../../../open-set.md) covering $\mathbb Z$. Each integer is isolated, so the induced [subspace topology](../../../../subspace-topology.md) is discrete.
