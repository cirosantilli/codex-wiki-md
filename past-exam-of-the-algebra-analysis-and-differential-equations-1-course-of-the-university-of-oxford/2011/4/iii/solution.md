<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [path-connected space](../../../../path-connected-space.md) $S$ has, for every pair $z_0,z_1\in S$, a [continuous](../../../../continuous-function.md) path $\gamma:[0,1]\to S$ with $\gamma(0)=z_0$ and $\gamma(1)=z_1$. Every [path-connected space](../../../../path-connected-space.md) is a [connected space](../../../../connected-space.md), because a separation of the target would pull a joining path apart and contradict [connectedness of an interval](../../../../connectedness-of-an-interval.md).
