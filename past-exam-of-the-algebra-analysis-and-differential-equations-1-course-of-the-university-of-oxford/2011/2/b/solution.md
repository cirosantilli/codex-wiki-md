<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A real [inner product](../../../../inner-product-split.md) must be symmetric. Here $\beta_a(e_1,e_2)=-2$ and $\beta_a(e_2,e_1)=-a$, forcing $a=2$. For this value,

$$
\beta_2(x,x)=4x_1^2-4x_1x_2+3x_2^2
=4(x_1-x_2/2)^2+2x_2^2.
$$

This is strictly positive for every nonzero [vector](../../../../vector.md). Bilinearity is already given, so symmetry and [positive definiteness](../../../../positive-definiteness.md) prove

$$
\boxed{a=2\text{ is the unique admissible value}.}
$$
