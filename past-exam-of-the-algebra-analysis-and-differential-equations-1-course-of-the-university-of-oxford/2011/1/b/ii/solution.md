↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [span](../../../../../linear-span.md) $U$ is the coordinate plane $\{(x_1,x_2,0,0)^T\}$. Consequently its [annihilator of a vector subspace](../../../../../annihilator-of-a-vector-subspace.md) is $U^\circ=\operatorname{span}\{\varepsilon_3,\varepsilon_4\}$. The [dual map](../../../../../transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) is composition with $T$, so

$$
T'\varepsilon_3=\varepsilon_3,\qquad T'\varepsilon_4=0.
$$

It follows that

$$
\boxed{T'(U^\circ)=\operatorname{span}\{\varepsilon_3\}.}
$$

Equivalently, $T'(a\varepsilon_3+b\varepsilon_4)$ sends $x$ to $ax_3$.
