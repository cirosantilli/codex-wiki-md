<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $e_1,\ldots,e_n$, define the [dual basis](../../../../../dual-basis.md) by

$$
\boxed{e_i'(e_j)=\delta_{ij},\qquad e_i'\Bigl(\sum_j x_je_j\Bigr)=x_i.}
$$

Uniqueness of the [coordinates](../../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md) makes each $e_i'$ well-defined and linear. If $\sum_i a_ie_i'=0$, evaluation at $e_j$ gives $a_j=0$, so these [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md) are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md). For any $\lambda\in V'$, evaluation on each [basis vector](../../../../../basis-vector.md) gives

$$
\lambda=\sum_i\lambda(e_i)e_i'.
$$

Both sides agree on a [basis](../../../../../basis.md), hence on every [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md). Thus they also [span](../../../../../linear-span.md) the [dual space](../../../../../dual-space-split.md) and are a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of it.
