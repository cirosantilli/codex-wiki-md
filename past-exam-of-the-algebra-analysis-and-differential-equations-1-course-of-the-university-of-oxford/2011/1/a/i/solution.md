The [annihilator of a vector subspace](../../../../../annihilator-of-a-vector-subspace.md) is the [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of the [dual space](../../../../../dual-space-split.md) consisting of the [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md) that vanish on $U$:

$$
\boxed{U^\circ=\{\lambda\in V':\lambda(u)=0\text{ for every }u\in U\}.}
$$

It is a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) because every [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) of functionals vanishing on $U$ still vanishes there.
