<h1 id="5/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [pole](../../../../pole.md) of order $m\geq1$ at $a$ means

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\frac{g(z)}{(z-a)^m},\qquad g\text{ holomorphic near }a,\quad g(a)\ne0.}
$$

Equivalently the lowest nonzero power in its [Laurent series](../../../../laurent-series.md) is $(z-a)^{-m}$. In particular, $(z-a)^mf(z)$ tends to a finite nonzero limit.
