↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [isolated singularity](../../../../isolated-singularity-split.md) at $a$ means that the function is [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on some punctured disc $0<|z-a|<r$, while it is undefined or fails to be holomorphic at $a$. The punctured neighbourhood contains no other singularities; this isolation is needed before applying the usual removable/pole/essential classification.
