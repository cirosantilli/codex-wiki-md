↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [removable singularity](../../../../removable-singularity.md) at $a$ is an [isolated singularity](../../../../isolated-singularity-split.md) for which one can assign a value at $a$ to obtain a [holomorphic function](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the whole disc. Equivalently its [Laurent series](../../../../laurent-series.md) has no negative powers. A finite limit $L$ supplies the extension value $f(a)=L$.
