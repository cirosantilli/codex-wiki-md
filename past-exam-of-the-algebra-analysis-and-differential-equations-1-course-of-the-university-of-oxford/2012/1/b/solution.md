<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [annihilator of a vector subspace](../../../../annihilator-of-a-vector-subspace.md) consists of the [linear functionals](../../../../linear-functional.md) that vanish on every element of that [vector subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md):

$$
\boxed{W^0=\{\lambda\in V':\lambda(w)=0\text{ for every }w\in W\},\qquad
\dim W^0=\dim V-\dim W.}
$$

For the [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) identity, choose a [basis](../../../../basis.md) $w_1,\ldots,w_k$ of $W$ and use [basis extension](../../../../basis-extension.md) to obtain $w_1,\ldots,w_n$ for $V$. A [linear functional](../../../../linear-functional.md) is determined by its values on these $n$ [basis vectors](../../../../basis-vector.md). Belonging to the [annihilator of a vector subspace](../../../../annihilator-of-a-vector-subspace.md) sets its first $k$ values to zero and leaves its last $n-k$ values arbitrary. Equivalently the last $n-k$ elements of the [dual basis](../../../../dual-basis.md) form a [basis](../../../../basis.md) of $W^0$.
