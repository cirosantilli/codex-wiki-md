<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [dual space](../../../../dual-space-split.md) consists of the [linear functionals](../../../../linear-functional.md) on the original [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md), with addition and scalar multiplication defined pointwise:

$$
\boxed{V'=\operatorname{Hom}_F(V,F).}
$$

The [dual map](../../../../transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) acts by [function composition](../../../../function-composition.md) with $T$:

$$
\boxed{T':V'\longrightarrow U',\qquad T'(\lambda)=\lambda\circ T.}
$$

For $u\in U$, its value is $(T'\lambda)(u)=\lambda(Tu)$. The composite is a [linear functional](../../../../linear-functional.md) because both $T$ and $\lambda$ are [linear maps](../../../../linear-map.md). Moreover $T'(a\lambda+b\mu)=aT'\lambda+bT'\mu$, so the [dual map](../../../../transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) is itself a [linear map](../../../../linear-map.md). Its direction is reversed because a measurement on the output of $T$ becomes a measurement on its input.
