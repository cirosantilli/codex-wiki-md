<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [diagonalizable matrix](../../../../diagonalizable-matrix.md) over the [field](../../../../field.md) $F$ admits an [invertible matrix](../../../../invertible-matrix.md) $P$ with entries in $F$ such that

$$
\boxed{P^{-1}AP=D\quad\text{with }D\text{ a diagonal matrix over }F.}
$$

Equivalently, the [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) $F^n$ has a [basis](../../../../basis.md) of [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md) of $A$. Indeed, $AP=PD$ says that each column of $P$ is an [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) with the corresponding diagonal entry as its [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md). Requiring the [basis](../../../../basis.md) and [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) to lie in $F$ makes the choice of [field](../../../../field.md) essential.
