<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}p>0$, the [complex exponential](../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) $e^{-pT}$ tends to zero, since its modulus is $e^{-T\operatorname{Re}p}$. Therefore direct [integration](../../../../integral-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L\{1\}(p)=\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{1-e^{-pT}}p=\frac1p.}
$$

The condition on the real part also gives absolute convergence of this [improper integral](../../../../improper-integral.md).
