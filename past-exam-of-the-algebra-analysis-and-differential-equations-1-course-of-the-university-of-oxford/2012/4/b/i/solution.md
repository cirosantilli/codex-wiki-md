<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [distance to a set](../../../../../distance-to-a-set.md) is nonnegative and finite here: any fixed $y_0\in F$ gives $0\leq f(x)\leq|x-y_0|$. For every $y\in F$, the [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) gives

$$
|x-y|\leq|x-x'|+|x'-y|.
$$

Taking the [infimum](../../../../../infimum.md) over $y\in F$ yields $f(x)\leq|x-x'|+f(x')$. Interchanging $x$ and $x'$ proves

$$
\boxed{|f(x)-f(x')|\leq|x-x'|.}
$$

Thus $f$ is a [Lipschitz function](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) with [Lipschitz constant](../../../../../lipschitz-constant.md) one, so it is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md). In the epsilon-delta definition of [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md), choose $\delta=\varepsilon$.
