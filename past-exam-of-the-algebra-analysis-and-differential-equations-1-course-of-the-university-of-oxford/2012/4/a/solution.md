↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A subset $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is an [open set](../../../../open-set.md) if every $x\in\Omega$ admits a radius $r>0$ such that the [open ball](../../../../open-ball.md)

$$
B(x,r)=\{y\in\mathbb R^n:|y-x|<r\}
$$

is contained in $\Omega$. A subset $F\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is a [closed set](../../../../closed-set.md) if its [complement](../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) $\mathbb R^n\setminus F$ is an [open set](../../../../open-set.md). Equivalently, $F$ contains the [limit](../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) of every [convergent sequence](../../../../convergent-sequence.md) of its points.
