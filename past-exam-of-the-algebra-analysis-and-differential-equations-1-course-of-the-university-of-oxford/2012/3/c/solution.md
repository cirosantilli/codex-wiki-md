<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Set $X=0$ to define a surjective [ring homomorphism](../../../../ring-homomorphism.md)

$$
E:\mathbb Q[X,Y]\longrightarrow\mathbb Q[Y],\qquad E(P)=P(0,Y).
$$

Its [kernel of a ring homomorphism](../../../../kernel-of-a-ring-homomorphism.md) is exactly $(X)$: the constant coefficient in $X$ must vanish, so every remaining monomial is divisible by $X$. The [first isomorphism theorem for rings](../../../../first-isomorphism-theorem-for-rings.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb Q[X,Y]/(X)\cong\mathbb Q[Y].}
$$

The [polynomial ring](../../../../polynomial-ring.md) $\mathbb Q[Y]$ is an [integral domain](../../../../integral-domain.md), because the product of two nonzero [polynomials](../../../../polynomial-split.md) has nonzero leading coefficient. It is not a [field](../../../../field.md): $Y$ has no polynomial [multiplicative inverse](../../../../multiplicative-inverse.md), since a nonzero product $YQ(Y)$ has positive [degree of a polynomial](../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md). Consequently **$(X)$ is prime and is not maximal**. Concretely, $(X)\subsetneq(X,Y)\subsetneq\mathbb Q[X,Y]$ is a strict chain of [ideals](../../../../ideal.md).
