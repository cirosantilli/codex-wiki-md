<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [continuously differentiable function](../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md) $z\bar z=x^2+y^2$ is a particular solution, since

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial\bar z}(x^2+y^2)
=\frac12(2x+i,2y)=z.
$$

If $f$ is any solution, set $h(z)=f(z)-z\bar z$. Then $h$ is [continuously differentiable](../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md) and its [Wirtinger derivative](../../../../../wirtinger-derivatives.md) vanishes. By the preceding part and the sufficient [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) criterion, $h$ is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on all of $\mathbb C$, hence an [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md).

Conversely, every [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md) $h$ has vanishing [Wirtinger derivative](../../../../../wirtinger-derivatives.md), so addition of $h$ preserves the prescribed derivative. Therefore all solutions are

$$
\boxed{f(z)=|z|^2+h(z),\qquad h\text{ any entire function}.}
$$

This is an [inhomogeneous Wirtinger equation with holomorphic forcing](../../../../../inhomogeneous-wirtinger-equation-with-holomorphic-forcing.md): a particular solution plus an arbitrary element of the homogeneous solution space.
