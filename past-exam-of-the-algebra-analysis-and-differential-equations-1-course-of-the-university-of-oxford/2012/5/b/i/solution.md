<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [continuously differentiable function](../../../../../continuously-differentiable-function.md) $f=u+iv$, the specified [Wirtinger derivative](../../../../../wirtinger-derivatives.md) is

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial\bar z}
=\frac12\bigl(u_x+iv_x+i(u_y+iv_y)\bigr)
=\frac12\bigl((u_x-v_y)+i(v_x+u_y)\bigr).
$$

A [complex number](../../../../../complex-number.md) vanishes exactly when both real and imaginary parts vanish. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial f}{\partial\bar z}=0
\iff u_x=v_y\text{ and }u_y=-v_x.}
$$

Thus the two real [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) are one complex equation expressed by the [Wirtinger derivative](../../../../../wirtinger-derivatives.md).
