**$\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z$ is a [field](../../../../../field.md), and consequently also a [ring](../../../../../ring.md).** Its nonzero elements are $\overline1$ and $\overline2$, with inverses $\overline1$ and $\overline2$ respectively. The [quotient ring](../../../../../quotient-ring.md) axioms are inherited as in the preceding part.
