A [ring](../../../../../ring.md) is a set with an abelian-group [addition](../../../../../addition.md), associative [multiplication](../../../../../multiplication.md) and both distributive laws; here we use [rings](../../../../../ring.md) with multiplicative identity. A [field](../../../../../field.md) is a nonzero [commutative ring](../../../../../commutative-ring.md) in which every nonzero element has a multiplicative inverse.

**The [positive integers](../../../../../positive-integer.md) form neither a [ring](../../../../../ring.md) nor a [field](../../../../../field.md).** They do not contain an [additive identity](../../../../../additive-identity.md) zero, and they also lack [additive inverses](../../../../../additive-inverse.md).
