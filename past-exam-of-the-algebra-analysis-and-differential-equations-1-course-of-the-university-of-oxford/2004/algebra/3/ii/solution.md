<h1 id="algebra/3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**$\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$ is a [ring](../../../../../ring.md) but not a [field](../../../../../field.md).** Quotient operations inherit associativity and distributivity from the integers, and residues have [additive inverses](../../../../../additive-inverse.md). However the nonzero residue $\overline2$ satisfies $\overline2\,\overline2=\overline0$, so it cannot be invertible. A [field](../../../../../field.md) has no nonzero [zero divisors](../../../../../zero-divisor.md).
