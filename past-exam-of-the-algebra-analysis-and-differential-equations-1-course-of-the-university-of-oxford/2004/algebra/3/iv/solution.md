↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

This is the [Gaussian integer](../../../../../gaussian-integer.md) [ring](../../../../../ring.md) $\mathbb Z[i]$. It contains zero and one and is closed under [addition](../../../../../addition.md), [additive inverses](../../../../../additive-inverse.md) and [multiplication](../../../../../multiplication.md), since

$$
(a+bi)(c+di)=(ac-bd)+(ad+bc)i.
$$

Its operations inherit the [ring](../../../../../ring.md) axioms from $\mathbb C$. It is **a [ring](../../../../../ring.md) but not a [field](../../../../../field.md)**: the nonzero element 2 has complex inverse $1/2$, which is not a [Gaussian integer](../../../../../gaussian-integer.md).
