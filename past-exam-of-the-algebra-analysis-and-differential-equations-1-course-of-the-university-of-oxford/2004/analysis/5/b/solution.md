<h1 id="analysis/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [pole](../../../../../pole.md) of order $m\geq1$ means that $(z-a)^mf(z)$ extends holomorphically to $a$ and has a nonzero value there. Equivalently, the lowest nonzero power in the [Laurent expansion](../../../../../laurent-series.md) is $(z-a)^{-m}$.
