<h1 id="analysis/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [removable singularity](../../../../../removable-singularity.md) at $a$ means that there is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the whole disc which agrees with $f$ away from $a$. Equivalently, the [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) has no negative powers. The value at $a$ is filled in by its finite limit.
