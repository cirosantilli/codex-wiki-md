# Matrix trace pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md)

On square matrices over a field, the trace of a product is a [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md). Pairing with the matrix unit $E_{ji}$ reads off entry $a_{ij}$, proving nondegeneracy. For real matrices of size at least two it is not [positive-definite](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md): a planar rotation block squares to $-I_2$ and has self-pairing $-2$. On real diagonalizable matrices its self-pairing is the sum of the squares of the real eigenvalues. This matrix pairing differs from the [trace pairing](trace-pairing.md) of a field extension.
