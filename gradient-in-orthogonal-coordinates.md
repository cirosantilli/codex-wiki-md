# Gradient in orthogonal coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal coordinates](orthogonal-coordinates.md)

For a regular chart of [orthogonal coordinates](orthogonal-coordinates.md) $\mathbf r(q)$, define $h_i=|\partial_{q_i}\mathbf r|>0$ and $\mathbf e_i=h_i^{-1}\partial_{q_i}\mathbf r$. The tangent [unit vectors](unit-vector.md) form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). Matching $d\Phi=\nabla\Phi\cdot d\mathbf r$ with $d\mathbf r=\sum_i h_i\mathbf e_i dq_i$ proves the formula. Dividing by a scale factor converts a derivative per coordinate increment into a derivative per physical distance in that direction.
