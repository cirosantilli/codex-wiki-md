# Degenerate relic neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino degeneracy parameter](neutrino-degeneracy-parameter.md)

A degenerate relic neutrino has $\xi_\nu\gg1$. In the ultrarelativistic limit its [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) approaches a filled momentum sphere, giving for one internal state

$$
\rho_\nu\simeq\frac{\mu_\nu^4}{8\pi^2},
$$

while the antineutrino density is exponentially suppressed.
