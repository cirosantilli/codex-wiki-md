# Tuning relation between Lasso and square-root Lasso

↑ **Parent:** [Square-root Lasso](square-root-lasso.md)

A [Lasso](lasso.md) minimizer with positive residual scale $\widehat\sigma_\lambda=\|Y-X\widehat\beta_\lambda\|_2/\sqrt n$ also minimizes the [Square-root Lasso](square-root-lasso.md) objective at the displayed tuning parameter. Divide its [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) by the residual scale and use the [subgradient optimality condition](subgradient-optimality-condition.md).
